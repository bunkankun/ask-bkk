# KR2h 史鈔類 *Shǐchāo lèi* — History Excerpts

## Scope and scholarly tradition
KR2h gathers works the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù* 四庫全書總目 places under 史鈔類 (also written 史抄類): compilations that excerpt, abridge, or topically re-arrange one or more of the Standard Histories rather than producing fresh historical narrative of their own. The *Sìkù* editors' own programmatic statement — that *shǐchāo* descends from Confucius' editorial reduction of the *Documents* — is a polite way of giving a low-status genre an august pedigree, and Wilkinson (§49.6.1) classes the category accordingly as a useful but undervalued route into the historical corpus for readers who could not face the bulk of the *Standard Histories* whole. Two formal types are represented in the WYG selection: the **catchword-and-quotation** *shǐchāo*, which excerpts striking phrases or incidents from one or two parent histories under brief lemmata while preserving the parent's chapter order — the line that runs from [[KR2h0001]] in the early Sòng to [[KR2h0003]] in the early Qīng; and the **topical re-arrangement** of an existing history, modeled on the *Cèfǔ yuánguī* 冊府元龜, represented by [[KR2h0002]], which redistributes Sīmǎ Guāng's [[司馬光]] *Zīzhì tōngjiàn* 資治通鑑 [[KR2b0011]] across 271 thematic sections (*mén* 門).

The division is small — three works survive in the WYG selection, spanning seven centuries — but methodologically coherent: each is a finder-aid for a literary user, not a critical edition for the historian. The principal scholarly interest of the category lies in (a) its source-critical value, since *shǐchāo* of this sort occasionally preserve commentary readings that the parent woodblock tradition has lost (the *Sìkù tíyào* on [[KR2h0001]] makes this point sharply, citing the lost "Sì Hào" 四皓 entry of Yán Shīgǔ 顏師古 [[顏師古]]); and (b) its documentation of late-imperial reading practice, particularly the late-Sòng vogue for *Tōngjiàn*-derivatives of which [[KR2h0002]] is one of three principal exemplars.

## Important texts and text clusters
The three works in the WYG witness fall into two clusters separated by genealogy of method, not by date:

- The **catchword/quotation *shǐchāo*** of the Standard Histories
  - *Liǎng Hàn bówén* 兩漢博聞 [[KR2h0001]] — Yáng Kǎn's [[楊侃]] early Sòng (989–1032) excerpt-book of *Hànshū* (7 *juàn*) and *Hòu Hànshū* (5 *juàn*), reproducing each excerpt with the standard commentary of Yán Shīgǔ [[顏師古]] or Lǐ Xián [[李賢]]. The earliest surviving Sòng *shǐchāo* of the two Hàn histories, and the explicit methodological model for [[KR2h0003]].
  - *Nán shǐ shíxiǎo lù* 南史識小錄 (with *Běi shǐ shíxiǎo lù* 北史識小錄) [[KR2h0003]] — Shěn Míngsūn [[沈名蓀]] and Zhū Kūntián [[朱昆田]]'s 1690 Qīng pair-volume on Lǐ Yánshòu's [[李延壽]] *Nánshǐ* 南史 and *Běishǐ* 北史 [[KR2a0006]] [[KR2a0007]], transferring the *Liǎng Hàn bówén* method onto the Táng-period summary histories of the Northern and Southern Dynasties. A chrestomathy for poets and prose-stylists rather than a work of historical scholarship; the catalog title reflects only the *Nánshǐ* half of what is in fact a 16-*juàn* whole.
- The **topical re-arrangement** of a parent history
  - *Tōngjiàn zǒnglèi* 通鑑總類 [[KR2h0002]] — Shěn Shū's [[沈樞]] late Southern Sòng (*ca.* 1180–1208) topical compendium that breaks up Sīmǎ Guāng's *Zīzhì tōngjiàn* and redistributes its events across 271 thematic sections in chronological order within each section, preserving Sīmǎ Guāng's editorial *yuē* 曰 verdicts. Together with Yuán Shū's [[袁樞]] *Tōngjiàn jìshì běnmò* 通鑑紀事本末 (1175, narrative-by-event) and Zhū Xī's *Tōngjiàn gāngmù* 通鑑綱目 [[KR2b0014]] (moral-historical), this work is the third of the three canonical Southern Sòng re-arrangements of the *Tōngjiàn*, and the only one to organize its parent's material by *content category*.

## Important persons
- [[楊侃]] (Yáng Kǎn, 964–1032; later Yáng Dàyǎ 楊大雅 after the Zhēnzōng taboo) — Qiántáng 錢塘 native, *jìnshì* of Duāngǒng 2 (989), Academician of the Hall of Worthies (集賢院學士). The earliest *shǐchāo* compiler represented in the division; established the catchword-and-commentary format that [[KR2h0003]] explicitly reproduces. (The catalog meta's "d. 521" is a conflation with the Northern Wèi homonym; see [[KR2h0001]].)
- [[沈樞]] (Shěn Shū, *zì* Chíyào 持要, posth. Xiànmǐn 憲敏) — Déqīng 德清 native, Shàoxīng-era *jìnshì*, retired Tàizǐ zhānshì 太子詹事 / Guānglù qīng 光祿卿. Compiled [[KR2h0002]] in retirement, reportedly working on it past his ninetieth year, and so the division's exemplar of the topical-rearrangement type.
- [[沈名蓀]] (Shěn Míngsūn, *zì* Jiànfāng 澗芳) — Qiántáng native of early Kāngxī Zhèjiāng literary circles; co-compiler of [[KR2h0003]]. Lifedates are not securely known.
- [[朱昆田]] (Zhū Kūntián, *zì* Wén'àng 文盎, 1652–1699) — Xiùshuǐ 秀水 native, son of Zhū Yízūn 朱彝尊 [[朱彝尊]], and Shěn Míngsūn's co-compiler on [[KR2h0003]]; brings the early-Qīng Pùshū tíng 曝書亭 milieu into the division.
- [[樓鑰]] (Lóu Yuè, 1137–1213) — wrote the 1208 preface to [[KR2h0002]] when its Cháoyáng 潮陽 print was being prepared by Shěn Shū's youngest son. Lóu's preface is the firmest *terminus ante quem* for the work and is the principal external testimony to Shěn Shū's life and method.

## Topics
- **The *shǐchāo* as method**: catchword + parent passage + commentary, with no editorial voice, no topical re-ordering ([[KR2h0001]], [[KR2h0003]]) versus systematic content-categorial re-distribution of an entire parent history ([[KR2h0002]]). The division is the cleanest place in the *Sìkù* to watch the two procedures side by side.
- **The literary function of history-excerpts.** All three works are addressed at least as much to the literary user — the poet looking for an allusion, the prose-stylist gathering well-cut diction — as to the historian. The *Sìkù tíyào* on [[KR2h0003]] makes the defense explicit, framing *shǐchāo* as the discipline that prizes the stylistic excess (especially Lǐ Yánshòu's notorious fondness for *jīxiáng* 機祥 / auspicia) that orthodox historiographic critique deplores.
- **The late-Sòng *Tōngjiàn*-derivatives boom.** [[KR2h0002]] stands in a defined family with Yuán Shū's *jìshì běnmò* and Zhū Xī's *gāngmù* [[KR2b0014]]; the division therefore documents one third of the late-Sòng reaction to the sheer bulk of [[KR2b0011]].
- **Source-critical value.** The *Sìkù tíyào* on [[KR2h0001]] singles out the *Sì Hào* entry to argue that *shǐchāo* can preserve commentary readings lost in the parent textual tradition — a point that justifies a low-prestige genre on philological rather than literary grounds.
- **Status of the genre.** The *Sìkù* editors place *shǐchāo* low in the hierarchy of 史部 categories (only three texts admitted, against many dozens for 編年 or 別史), yet open their category-preface with the Confucian Documents-editing pedigree. Wilkinson's §49.6.1 treats this stance even-handedly: the genre is minor, but the abbreviations it produced were a major actual route into historical knowledge for non-specialists.

## Timeline
- 989–1032 — *Liǎng Hàn bówén* 兩漢博聞 [[KR2h0001]] — earliest surviving Sòng *shǐchāo* of the two Hàn histories; establishes the catchword-and-commentary format that the Qīng compilers of [[KR2h0003]] adopt verbatim.
- *ca.* 1180–1208 — *Tōngjiàn zǒnglèi* 通鑑總類 [[KR2h0002]] — Southern Sòng topical re-arrangement of [[KR2b0011]] across 271 thematic sections; the division's sole representative of the *Cèfǔ yuánguī*-style method.
- 1690 — *Nán shǐ shíxiǎo lù* 南史識小錄 (with *Běi shǐ shíxiǎo lù*) [[KR2h0003]] — early-Kāngxī Qīng chrestomathy on Lǐ Yánshòu's two Táng-period dynastic histories; the latest entry in the WYG selection and the only Qīng work in the division.
