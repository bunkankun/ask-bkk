# Liǎng Hàn kānwù bǔyí 兩漢刋誤補遺
*Supplements to the Errata of the Two Han Histories*
by [[吳仁傑]] (Wú Rénjié, b. 1137)

## About the work

A *kǎozhèng* monograph in 10 *juǎn* (8 on the *Hànshū*, 2 on the *Hòu Hànshū*) supplementing and correcting the *Hàn shū kānwù* 漢書刊誤 (1 *j.*) and *Hòu Hàn shū kānwù* 後漢書刊誤 (1 *j.*) of Liú Bīn 劉攽 (1023–1089) — and by extension also incorporating the *Hàn shū biāozhù* 漢書標注 (the so-called "Three Liús" tradition of Liú Chǎng 劉敞, Liú Bīn 劉攽, and Liú Fèngshì 劉奉世). Wú Rénjié's preface (by Zēng Jiàng 曾絳) is dated *Chúnxī* 16 (1189); the *Qìngyuán* 5 (1199) postface (Lín Yíng 林瀛) records a recutting at the Quánzhōu prefectural press by Chén Qiányīng 陳虔英.

### Tiyao

By Wú Rénjié of the Sòng. Rénjié authored the *Yì tú shuō* 易圖說, already catalogued. The work has a preface by Zēng Jiàng 曾絳 dated *Chúnxī jǐyǒu* (1189) saying that Rénjié, when serving as magistrate of Luótián, had the work cut at his own expense; a postface by Lín Yíng 林瀛 dated *Qìngyuán jǐwèi* (1199) says Chén Qiányīng had it recut at the Quánzhōu prefectural press — perhaps the first cutting was begun but not completed, or perhaps Chén recut it. The old impression is long lost; the present text was copied by Zhū Yítzōn's son Kūntián 朱昆田 from the Lǐ Kāixiān 李開先 family in Shāndōng, and circulated thence.

By its title-line the work was meant to follow Liú Bīn's *Liǎng Hàn shū kānwù*, but in fact it also supplements and corrects Liú Chǎng and Liú Fèngshì. (The *tíyào* then untangles the bibliographic history: Liú Bīn first wrote separate *Xī Hàn kānwù* and *Dōng Hàn kānwù* in 1 *juǎn* each, as Zhào Xībiàn's 趙希弁 *Dúshū fùzhì* records; later Bīn's notes were combined with the senior Liú Chǎng and his nephew Liú Fèngshì's *Hànshū biāozhù* 6 *juǎn* into a single edition under the name *Sān Liú Hànshū*, as Xú Dù's 徐度 *Quèsǎo biān* records; thereafter another recension was made with Bīn's *kānwù* as the principal text and the Liú Chǎng / Liú Fèngshì notes as appendices, retaining the title *kānwù*, as Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí* records; and eventually the *Dōng Hàn kānwù* was attached, becoming the *Liǎng Hàn kānwù*. Wú Rénjié supplements the latest of these recensions but did not change the title.

The *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* lists the work in 17 *juǎn*; the *Sòng zhì* lists it in 10. The present text has at most 14 leaves per *juǎn* and at minimum 12 — too short to admit a 17–10 disparity by simple subdivision; rather, by the time the *Sòng shǐ* was compiled, 7 *juǎn* must already have been lost, with the incomplete 10 catalogued. Of the present 10 *juǎn*, 8 supplement the *Qián Hàn shū* and only 2 the *Hòu Hàn*; the disparity is too sharp.

The Liús' work corrected much of the older text, but their stroke-by-stroke notes never analysed the reasoning. Wú Rénjié alone draws on copious citations and detailed *kǎozhèng*, getting to roots and origins, and refusing to leave a point until it is clear beyond doubt. His scholarship is in fact deeper than that of the original. There are minor lapses (his treatment of *lín zhǐ* 麟止 = *qílín zhǐ* 驎趾 is somewhat strained), but on the whole his style and substance are perfectly orderly — one flaw against a hundred merits. Zēng Jiàng's preface quotes Zhōu Bìdà's 周必大 description of him as "a man of broad knowledge and wide learning" — this is no exaggeration.

### Abstract

Wú Rénjié's *Liǎng Hàn kānwù bǔyí* is one of the major Southern Sòng *kǎozhèng* monographs on the Han histories, and the principal continuation and refinement of the great *Sān Liú Hànshū* tradition of the Northern Sòng. Composition window: between Wú's appointment as Luótián 羅田 magistrate (where he undertook the printing) and Lín Yíng's *Qìngyuán* 5 (1199) postface; Zēng Jiàng's preface of *Chúnxī* 16 (1189) gives a firm *terminus ante quem* for substantial completion. The work is therefore best dated 1180–1199.

The 7 *juǎn* of supplements lost by Yuán times have not been recovered. The standard surviving 10-*juǎn* text was preserved through the Yuán-Ming period in private libraries (the *Lǐ Kāixiān* family of Shāndōng), copied by Zhū Kūntián 朱昆田 (1652–1699), son of the great Qing bibliophile [[朱彝尊]], and brought into general circulation in the late Kāngxī.

The work belongs to the formative tradition of Chinese textual *kǎozhèng* on the *zhèngshǐ* — together with Liú Bīn's earlier *Liǎng Hàn shū kānwù*, the *Sān Liú Hàn shū* compilation, Wáng Yīngzhì 王應麟's *Hànyì wénzhì kǎozhèng* 漢藝文志考證, and Hóng Yìxuǎn 洪邁's *Róngzhāi suíbǐ* 容齋隨筆 — that culminated in the Qing in Wáng Xiānqiān's 王先謙 *Hàn shū bǔzhù* 漢書補注 (1900) and *Hòu Hàn shū jí jiě* 後漢書集解 (1915). Wú Rénjié is also the author of the *Yì tú shuō* ([[KR1a0066]], on the *Yìjīng* diagrams), the *Lí sāo cǎo mù shū* 離騷草木疏 (a botanical commentary on the *Lí sāo*; [[KR4a0068]]), and the *Liǎng Hàn càizhuàn cǎomù shù* 兩漢菜傳草木述.

### Translations and research

No translation. Cited extensively in Wáng Xiānqiān 王先謙, *Hàn shū bǔzhù* (1900) and *Hòu Hàn shū jí jiě* (1915); discussed in Yáng Shùdá 楊樹達, *Hàn shū kuīguǎn* 漢書窺管 (Shanghai Gǔjí, 1984). The work has not received a separate critical edition; the standard text remains the WYG. On Wú Rénjié generally: Tom Mazanec, "Wu Renjie and the Botanical Commentary in the Southern Song," *Bulletin of Sung-Yuan Studies* (forthcoming); Hé Wěn 何文, "Wú Rénjié *Liǎng Hàn kānwù bǔyí* yánjiū", *Wénxiàn* 2017.3.
