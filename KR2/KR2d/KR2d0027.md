# Shíqī Shǐ Méngqiú 十七史蒙求
*Meng Qiu on the Seventeen Histories*
by [[王令]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Shíqī Shǐ Méngqiú* 十七史蒙求 is a Northern Sòng historical primer in the tradition of Lǐ Hàn's 李瀚 Táng-dynasty *Méngqiú* 蒙求 (cf. [[KR3k0010]]). Where Lǐ Hàn's work draws on a broad sweep of historical figures and anecdotes, Wáng Lìng's 王令 primer focuses specifically on the "seventeen standard histories" (*shíqī shǐ* 十七史) — the canonical corpus from the *Shǐjì* 史記 through the *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* 新五代史 — as its source base. The format follows the Méngqiú model: paired four-character phrases name two historical figures with their most celebrated attributes (e.g., *Sòng Jǐng dì yī, Lǐ Guǎng wú shuāng* 宋璟第一、李廣無雙 — "Sòng Jǐng was first; Lǐ Guǎng was without equal"), followed by prose passages drawn from the relevant histories explaining the allusion. The text served as a primer for historical knowledge and as a reference tool for parallel-prose (*piánwén* 駢文) composition.

The Kanripo file (KR2d0027, 858 lines) preserves only a portion of the full work. Internal cross-references within the text point to other juan ("見第三卷畤子可誇注", "見第八卷「父子二傳」注", "見第十一卷注中") confirming that the complete edition comprised multiple juan, of which only a portion survives in this transmission.

### Abstract

The *Shíqī Shǐ Méngqiú* 十七史蒙求 was compiled by Wáng Lìng 王令 (1032–1059, *zì* Féngyuán 逢原), the short-lived Northern Sòng poet and close friend and protégé of Wáng Ānshí 王安石. Though Wáng Lìng is primarily known as a poet (his collection *Guǎnglíng jí* 廣陵集 [[KR4d0075]] survives in 31 *juǎn*), he also compiled this historical primer in the early part of his brief adult life. Wilkinson (*Chinese History*, §24.2.4) identifies it as "a widely circulated historical primer" and provides the example phrases that match the opening of the Kanripo text exactly: "宋璟第一, 李廣無雙; 燕許手筆, 李杜文章" (Song Jing was number one, Li Guang without equal; Yan and Xu excelled in writing style, Li and Du in literary achievement).

The work is modelled on the *Méngqiú* 蒙求 by Lǐ Hàn 李瀚 (Táng, early 8th c.), which is preserved in the *Méngqiú jízhù* 蒙求集注 [[KR3k0010]] annotated by Xú Zǐguāng 徐子光 (Southern Sòng). Unlike the *Méngqiú jízhù*, which is a commentary on an earlier text, the *Shíqī Shǐ Méngqiú* is an independent composition structured around paired anecdotes drawn from the standard histories up to and including the Wǔdài (Five Dynasties) period. The historical scope extends from the Zhànguó 戰國 period to the Wǔdài, drawing on the *Shǐjì* 史記, *Hànshū* 漢書, *Hòu Hànshū* 後漢書, *Sānguó zhì* 三國志, *Jìnshū* 晉書, *Nánshǐ* 南史, *Běishǐ* 北史, *Wèishū* 魏書, *Xīn Táng shū* 新唐書, *Wǔdài shǐ* (i.e., *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* 新五代史 by Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修), and other sources including the *Zhànguó cè* 戰國策 and *Xīn xù* 新序.

Thematic groupings in the preserved portion include: loyalty and integrity (*shuāngzhōng* 雙忠), literary talent and wit (*wéncái* 文才), clever judgment (*míngcháo* 明察), and many other virtues and vices drawn from across dynasties. The paired-phrase format makes the work especially useful as a composition aid: each paired phrase signals a historical parallel that could be deployed in formal prose or examination writing.

The *Shíqī Shǐ Méngqiú* was listed in Sòng bibliographic catalogs. A Mǎshā print edition (*Mǎshā běn* 麻沙本) dated 1169 (Southern Sòng) is the earliest known printing; a modern critical edition was published by Yuèlù shūshè 岳麓書社 (Changsha, 1986). It is placed in the *biéshǐ* 別史 category of Kanripo's KR2 division, following the *Sìkù* pattern of assigning miscellaneous history-related primers to that section.

**Extent note:** The Kanripo file is a single merged text (858 lines) without juan headings. Cross-references within the text confirm the full work extended across multiple juan; only a portion is preserved here.
