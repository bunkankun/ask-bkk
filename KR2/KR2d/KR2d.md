## Scope and scholarly tradition

*Biéshǐ* 別史 (lit. "other / separate histories") is a residual bibliographical category, not a genre. The term first appears in Chén Zhènsūn's 陳振孫 *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* 直齋書錄解題 (13th c.), where *biéshǐ* are characterised as more serious works than *záshǐ* 雜史 yet not weighty enough to rank as *zhèngshǐ* 正史 — they lie "somewhere between *zhèngshǐ* on the one hand and *záshǐ* on the other" (Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §52.1). The Qīng Sìkù 四庫 editors made *biéshǐ* the fourth subcategory in the History branch (《史部》), absorbing into it many works that earlier catalogs had placed in *záshǐ*; the original Sìkù tally is 20 *biéshǐ* in the body of the catalog and 36 more in the *cúnmù* 存目 sections (Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §52.1, Table 73). The Kanripo `KR2d` division reproduces the Sìkù listing and supplements it with SBCK *Sānbiān* additions — privately compiled Míng and early-Qīng works, most of them once proscribed, that were impossible to admit into a Qiánlóng-era imperial catalog and that re-entered the bibliographic record only in the early twentieth century.

The works collected here span more than two millennia. The earliest is the *Yì Zhōushū* 逸周書 ([[KR2d0001]]), a heterogeneous Warring-States compilation framed as the residue of the pre-canonical *Shū* corpus; the latest are early- and mid-Qīng products — Wàn Sītóng's 萬斯同 supplementary tables ([[KR2d0021]]), Yáo Zhīyīn's 姚之駰 reconstitutions of the lost Eastern-Hàn histories ([[KR2d0022]]), and Lǐ Kǎi's 李鍇 hundred-*juǎn* recasting of the pre-imperial historical record ([[KR2d0024]]). The category covers, among other forms, *jìzhuàn* 紀傳 recastings of received histories (蘇轍, 蕭常, 郝經, 李鍇), institutional encyclopaedias of the *Sāntōng* 三通 tradition (鄭樵's *Tōngzhì* and the Qiánlóng *Xù tōngzhì*), private dynastic histories (王稱, 查繼佐), histories of alien dynasties written from the Hàn point of view (Liáo, Jīn, the Mongol *Yuáncháo bìshǐ*), mythohistorical compendia of pre-imperial antiquity (羅泌, 蘇轍), and Qīng-era recoveries (*jíyì* 輯佚) of lost histories. A reader navigating this division must keep in mind that the unifying feature is *not* form but *bibliographic placement* — these are works that the cataloguer had nowhere else to put.

## Important texts and text clusters

- **The *Sāntōng* universal-history tradition**: Zhèng Qiáo 鄭樵's *Tōngzhì* 通志 ([[KR2d0006]]) — the second of the three institutional encyclopaedias (after Dù Yòu's *Tōngdiǎn* and before Mǎ Duānlín's *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo*) — and its Qiánlóng-era continuation *Qīndìng xù tōngzhì* 欽定續通志 ([[KR2d0020]], in the catalog meta but not yet cataloged here; see Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §51.2). Both are placed in *biéshǐ* by the Sìkù editors because their inclusion of biographies disqualifies them from the *zhèngshū* 政書 category proper.

- **Shǔ-Hàn 蜀漢 *zhèngtǒng* recastings of the Sānguó zhì**: Xiāo Cháng 蕭常's *Xiāoshì Xù Hòu-Hànshū* 蕭氏續後漢書 ([[KR2d0012]], Southern Sòng, 1200) and Hǎo Jīng 郝經's independently-conceived *Hǎoshì Xù Hòu-Hànshū* 郝氏續後漢書 ([[KR2d0013]], composed in captivity at Yízhēn, 1260–1273). Both reorganise Chén Shòu's 陳壽 *Sānguó zhì* 三國志 ([[KR2a0010]]) so as to recognise Liú Bèi's Shǔ-Hàn rather than Cáo Wèi as the legitimate inheritor of the Hàn — a doctrinal commitment that Zhū Xī 朱熹 had recently codified in the *Tōngjiàn gāngmù* 通鑑綱目.

- **Hàn-history recoveries**: the *Dōngguān Hànjì* 東觀漢記 ([[KR2d0002]]) — the first dynastic compilation undertaken after the *Shǐjì*, one of the *Sānshǐ* 三史 for five centuries until displaced by Fàn Yè's *Hòu-Hànshū*, lost by the Yuán, and reconstructed by the Sìkù editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* — and Yáo Zhīyīn 姚之駰's *Hòu-Hànshū bǔyì* 後漢書補逸 ([[KR2d0022]], 1713), which provided the foundation for the later *Dōngguān* recovery by collecting fragments of eight further lost Eastern-Hàn histories. Together these constitute the principal Qīng-era *jíyì* 輯佚 ("recovery of lost texts") effort on the Hàn historiographical canon.

- **Alien-dynasty companion histories**: Yè Lónglǐ 葉隆禮's *Qìdān guózhì* 契丹國志 ([[KR2d0009]], 1180) on the Liáo and the attributed Yǔwén Màozhāo 宇文懋昭 *Dà-Jīn guózhì* 大金國志 ([[KR2d0010]], dated 1234 but partially later interpolation) on the Jīn — both surviving in *chóngdìng* 重訂 (imperially revised) recensions produced by the Qiánlóng-era Sìkù editors. Companion to these is the *Yuáncháo bìshǐ* 元朝秘史 ([[KR2d0014]]) — the Hàn-character phonetic transcription of the *Mongqol-un niuča tobča'an*, *Secret History of the Mongols*, the most important single source for early-Mongol history and one of the foundational documents of Mongolic historical linguistics.

- **Pre-imperial mythohistorical compendia**: Sū Zhé 蘇轍's *Gǔshǐ* 古史 ([[KR2d0005]], 1100–1112) — a 60-*juǎn* recasting of the *Shǐjì* in deliberately classicising form; Luó Bì 羅泌's *Lùshǐ* 路史 ([[KR2d0008]], 1170) — a syncretic synthesis drawing on the Hàn weft-texts, Daoist sources, and the standard canon; Lǐ Kǎi 李鍇's *Shàngshǐ* 尚史 ([[KR2d0024]], 1730–1745) — built by direct quotation-citation from Mǎ Sù's *Yìshǐ* and the original sources. Closely related are two Kāngxī-era *Chūnqiū-Zhànguó*-period miscellanies: Chén Hòuyào 陳厚耀's *Chūnqiū Zhànguó yìcí* 春秋戰國異辭 ([[KR2d0023]]) and Xuē Yújī 薛虞畿's late-Míng *Chūnqiū bièdiǎn* 春秋別典 ([[KR2d0015]]).

- **Sòng private dynastic histories**: the *Lóngpíng jí* 隆平集 ([[KR2d0004]], attributed to Zēng Gǒng 曾鞏 but probably later), Wáng Chèn 王稱's *Dōngdū shìlüè* 東都事略 ([[KR2d0007]], 1180–1195) — long regarded as one of the three great Sòng private histories of the lost Northern Sòng (with Lǐ Tāo and Lǐ Xīnchuán) — and Huáng Zhèn 黃震's *Gǔjīn jìyào* 古今紀要 ([[KR2d0011]], 1256–1276), one of the earliest substantial textbook implementations of the Zhū Xī *gāngmù* doctrine of *zhèngtǒng*.

- **Late-Míng / early-Qīng proscribed private histories** (the SBCK *Sānbiān* contribution to `KR2d`): Zhuāng Tínglóng 莊廷鑨's *Míngshǐ chāolüè cán* 明史鈔略殘 ([[KR2d0016]]), the surviving fragments of the work that triggered the great *Míngshǐ àn* 明史案 literary inquisition of 1663; and Zhā Jìzuǒ 查繼佐's two sister-works composed in secret over twenty-five years and concealed in his Hǎiníng wall — the comprehensive *Zuìwéi lù* 罪惟錄 ([[KR2d0017]]) and the regionally-organised Southern-Míng record *Dōngshān guóyǔ* 東山國語 ([[KR2d0018]]).

- **Imperial and supplementary chronologies**: the *Yùdìng lìdài jìshì niánbiǎo* 御定歷代紀事年表 ([[KR2d0019]], Kāngxī commission, 1707–1712) — a 100-*juǎn* synoptic table of 3,725 years of Chinese history modelled on the *Shǐjì* and *Tōngjiàn mùlù* — and Wàn Sītóng 萬斯同's *Bǔ lìdài shǐbiǎo* 補歷代史表 ([[KR2d0021]], 1678–1692), which supplied the chronological tables missing from the standard histories and effectively founded the Qīng *bǔbiǎo* 補表 ("supplementary tables") tradition.

- **Survivors from earlier strata**: at the head of the division stand the *Yì Zhōushū* 逸周書 ([[KR2d0001]]) with Kǒng Cháo 孔晁's Jìn-era commentary, and at one remove from it Xǔ Sōng 許嵩's *Jiànkāng shílù* 建康實錄 ([[KR2d0003]], 756–762), the principal annalistic synthesis of the Six Dynasties (Wú through Chén) with their shared capital at Jiànkāng.

## Important persons

- [[鄭樵]] — Pútián polymath (1104–1162); author of the *Tōngzhì* and the architect of the *Èrshí lüè* 二十略, the twenty topical treatises whose methodology shaped subsequent institutional historiography.
- [[蘇轍]] — Northern-Sòng prose-master (1039–1112); reconceived the *Shǐjì* in classicising *jìzhuàn* form as the *Gǔshǐ*.
- [[羅泌]] — Southern-Sòng Lúlíng antiquarian (ca. 1131–after 1189); compiler of the syncretic *Lùshǐ*.
- [[黃震]] — Southern-Sòng Zhū Xī school Confucian (1213–1281); his *Gǔjīn jìyào* implements Zhū Xī's *gāngmù* programme in textbook form.
- [[蕭常]] — Southern-Sòng Lúlíng *jìnshì* (12th c.); completed his father Xiāo Shòupéng's project of recasting the *Sānguó zhì* with Shǔ-Hàn *zhèngtǒng*.
- [[郝經]] — Yuán scholar-diplomat (1223–1275); composed his independently-conceived *Xù Hòu-Hànshū* over sixteen years of detention at the Sòng envoy lodgings.
- [[葉隆禮]] — Southern-Sòng official (*jìnshì* 1247); imperially commissioned compiler of the *Qìdān guózhì*.
- [[王稱]] — Southern-Sòng historian of Méizhōu (fl. 1180s–1195); private historian of the lost Northern Sòng.
- [[萬斯同]] — early-Qīng Zhè-dōng-school historian (1638–1702); compiler of the *Bǔ lìdài shǐbiǎo* and founder of the Qīng *bǔbiǎo* tradition.
- [[查繼佐]] — Hǎiníng Míng-loyalist (1601–1677); composer of the *Zuìwéi lù* and the *Dōngshān guóyǔ* under Qīng proscription, both rediscovered in the twentieth century.
- [[莊廷鑨]] — wealthy late-Míng Nánxún literatus (d. ca. 1660); commissioner of the *Míngshǐ chāolüè*, the work whose proscription triggered the *Míngshǐ àn* of 1663.
- [[姚之駰]] — Kāngxī-era Qiántáng scholar (fl. 1713); first systematic recoverer of the lost Eastern-Hàn histories.
- [[陳厚耀]] — Kāngxī-era evidential historian and mathematical astronomer (1648–1722); compiler of the *Chūnqiū Zhànguó yìcí*.
- [[李鍇]] — Hàn-bannerman scholar (b. 1686, d. 1755); compiler of the *Shàngshǐ* by the technique of direct citation-anthology.
- [[孔晁]] — Jìn-era commentator (late 3rd c. CE); the only continuous early commentary on the *Yì Zhōushū*.

## Topics

- **The *zhèngtǒng* 正統 problem.** More than any other concern, the question of dynastic legitimacy organises this division. The most conspicuous instance is the Sòng-and-after rehabilitation of Shǔ-Hàn against Cáo Wèi (Xiāo Cháng [[KR2d0012]], Hǎo Jīng [[KR2d0013]], Huáng Zhèn [[KR2d0011]]) — a doctrinal commitment following Zhū Xī's reformulation of Xí Zuòchǐ 習鑿齒's *Hàn-Jìn chūnqiū*. Equally important is the Sòng-vs-Liáo/Jīn legitimacy dispute, which the Qiánlóng-era Sìkù editors arbitrated retrospectively in their *chóngdìng* revisions of the *Qìdān guózhì* and *Dà-Jīn guózhì* ([[KR2d0009]], [[KR2d0010]]) — silently rearranging the Sòng-Liáo-Jīn reign-year orderings of the original Southern-Sòng compilers.

- **Recasting of received histories.** Several works in `KR2d` are not new histories but reorganisations of the *zhèngshǐ*: Sū Zhé's *Gǔshǐ* rewrites the *Shǐjì* in classicising form; Xiāo Cháng and Hǎo Jīng both rewrite the *Sānguó zhì* to reverse its legitimacy line; Lǐ Kǎi's *Shàngshǐ* recasts the entire pre-imperial record from the materials Mǎ Sù had collected as the *Yìshǐ*. The form is in each case classical *jìzhuàn*; the contribution is hermeneutic.

- **Recovery (*jíyì* 輯佚) of lost histories.** A signature mid-and-late-Qīng activity: Yáo Zhīyīn's recovery of eight Eastern-Hàn histories ([[KR2d0022]]) provides the foundation for the Sìkù editors' 24-*juǎn* reconstruction of the *Dōngguān Hànjì* ([[KR2d0002]]), itself drawn from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*, the Lǐ Xián commentary on the *Hòu-Hànshū*, the Liú Zhāo commentary on the *Xù-Hànshū* treatises, and the great Táng *lèishū* (*Běitáng shūchāo*, *Yìwén lèijù*, *Chūxué jì*). The Sìkù-era recovery of the *Hǎoshì Xù Hòu-Hànshū* ([[KR2d0013]]) from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* belongs to the same effort.

- **The *Sāntōng* / 三通 tradition.** Zhèng Qiáo's *Tōngzhì* and its Qiánlóng-era continuation establish the institutional-encyclopaedia line that, with Dù Yòu's *Tōngdiǎn* and Mǎ Duānlín's *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo*, constitutes the foundational triad of Chinese institutional historiography (Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §51.2). The Sìkù editors classified the *Tōngzhì* lineage as *biéshǐ* — rather than as *zhèngshū* 政書 — because the works contain biographies.

- **Pre-imperial mythohistory and the *Chūnqiū* outside the Three Commentaries.** Sòng and Qīng *biéshǐ* show a sustained effort to handle the antiquity that lay outside or beneath the canonical histories: Luó Bì's *Lùshǐ* draws on the Hàn weft-texts and Daoist sources; Sū Zhé's *Gǔshǐ* polemically suppresses what its author judges legendary in the *Shǐjì*; and Xuē Yújī's *Chūnqiū bièdiǎn* ([[KR2d0015]]) and Chén Hòuyào's *Chūnqiū Zhànguó yìcí* ([[KR2d0023]]) together gather precisely the *Chūnqiū*-period evidence that lies outside the *Sānzhuàn*.

- **Private vs imperial historiography, and the Qīng literary inquisitions.** The contrast between officially commissioned works (Wáng Zhīshū's *Yùdìng lìdài jìshì niánbiǎo* [[KR2d0019]], the Sìkù-era *chóngdìng* recensions of [[KR2d0009]] and [[KR2d0010]], Jī Huáng's *Xù tōngzhì*) and clandestinely composed private histories (Zhuāng Tínglóng [[KR2d0016]], Zhā Jìzuǒ [[KR2d0017]], [[KR2d0018]]) runs through the whole division. The *Míngshǐ àn* 明史案 of 1663 — the most severe of the early-Qīng literary inquisitions — is the political event that explains why several of these private works survive only in twentieth-century rediscovery and entered the bibliographic record outside the Sìkù line.

- **Alien-dynasty histories and Mongol historiography.** The Liáo–Jīn–Mongol horizon produces three of the most important works in `KR2d`: the *Qìdān guózhì*, the *Dà-Jīn guózhì*, and above all the *Yuáncháo bìshǐ* ([[KR2d0014]]), whose Hàn-character transcription is the principal surviving witness to the Mongolian original.

- **Supplementary tables (*bǔbiǎo* 補表).** Wàn Sītóng's *Bǔ lìdài shǐbiǎo* opens a tradition of methodical reconstruction of the chronological tables the standard histories omit — a Zhè-dōng-school complement to the Wú-school *kǎojù* 考據 evidential scholarship of the same period.

## Timeline

- c. 300 BCE – 280 CE — *Yì Zhōushū* 逸周書 [[KR2d0001]] — heterogeneous Warring-States compilation with Kǒng Cháo's Jìn-era commentary; the earliest stratum in the division.
- 62–225 CE — *Dōngguān Hànjì* 東觀漢記 [[KR2d0002]] — the first imperial *guóshǐ* compilation after the *Shǐjì*; lost by the Yuán, present recension a Sìkù-era reconstruction.
- 756–762 — *Jiànkāng shílù* 建康實錄 [[KR2d0003]] — Six-Dynasties annalistic synthesis from Sūn Quán to the fall of Chén.
- 1081–1142 — *Lóngpíng jí* 隆平集 [[KR2d0004]] — Northern-Sòng *huìyào* attributed to Zēng Gǒng; the 1142 preface fixes the latest date.
- 1050–1059 — *Shíqī shǐ méngqiú* 十七史蒙求 [[KR2d0027]] — Wáng Lìng 王令's historical primer modelled on Lǐ Hàn's *Méngqiú*, covering the seventeen standard histories from *Shǐjì* through *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ*; only a partial transmission survives in the KR corpus.
- 1100–1112 — *Gǔshǐ* 古史 [[KR2d0005]] — Sū Zhé's classicising recasting of the *Shǐjì*.
- 1149–1162 — *Tōngzhì* 通志 [[KR2d0006]] — Zhèng Qiáo's universal history; second of the *Sāntōng*.
- 1170 — *Lùshǐ* 路史 [[KR2d0008]] — Luó Bì's mythohistorical synthesis; dated 1170 by colophon.
- 1180 — *Qìdān guózhì* 契丹國志 [[KR2d0009]] — Yè Lónglǐ's history of Liáo, in the Sìkù *chóngdìng* recension.
- 1180–1195 — *Dōngdū shìlüè* 東都事略 [[KR2d0007]] — Wáng Chèn's private history of the lost Northern Sòng.
- 1180–1200 — *Xiāoshì Xù Hòu-Hànshū* 蕭氏續後漢書 [[KR2d0012]] — Xiāo Cháng's Shǔ-Hàn-*zhèngtǒng* recasting of the *Sānguó zhì*; presented 1200.
- 1228–1264 — *Yuáncháo bìshǐ* 元朝秘史 [[KR2d0014]] — Mongolian *Secret History*, traditional dates of composition (jiǎzǐ 1228, 1240, or 1252); surviving Hàn-character transcription early Míng.
- 1234–1340 — *Qīndìng chóngdìng Dà-Jīn guózhì* 欽定重訂大金國志 [[KR2d0010]] — attributed presentation 1234 but with substantial later (Yuán-period) interpolations; Sìkù *chóngdìng* recension 1784.
- 1256–1276 — *Gǔjīn jìyào* 古今紀要 [[KR2d0011]] — Huáng Zhèn's late-Sòng Zhū-Xī-school *gāngmù* textbook.
- 1260–1275 — *Hǎoshì Xù Hòu-Hànshū* 郝氏續後漢書 [[KR2d0013]] — Hǎo Jīng's independently-conceived recasting of the *Sānguó zhì*, composed in captivity at Yízhēn.
- 1550–1610 — *Chūnqiū bièdiǎn* 春秋別典 [[KR2d0015]] — Xuē Yújī's mid-Míng Chūnqiū-period miscellany, completed by Xuē Yúbīn.
- 1648–1672 — *Zuìwéi lù* 罪惟錄 [[KR2d0017]] — Zhā Jìzuǒ's clandestine private history of the Míng and Southern Míng.
- 1648–1677 — *Dōngshān guóyǔ* 東山國語 [[KR2d0018]] — Zhā Jìzuǒ's regionally-organised companion record of the Míng-loyalist resistance.
- 1657–1660 — *Míngshǐ chāolüè cán* 明史鈔略殘 [[KR2d0016]] — Zhuāng Tínglóng's proscribed Míng history, in its surviving SBCK fragments.
- 1678–1692 — *Bǔ lìdài shǐbiǎo* 補歷代史表 [[KR2d0021]] — Wàn Sītóng's *bǔbiǎo*, with Huáng Zōngxī's 1692 preface.
- 1707–1712 — *Yùdìng lìdài jìshì niánbiǎo* 御定歷代紀事年表 [[KR2d0019]] — Kāngxī-commissioned 100-*juǎn* synoptic chronology, ed. Wáng Zhīshū and Zhōu Qīngyuán.
- 1701–1722 — *Chūnqiū Zhànguó yìcí* 春秋戰國異辭 [[KR2d0023]] — Chén Hòuyào's evidential gathering of variant *Chūnqiū-Zhànguó* materials.
- 1713 — *Hòu-Hànshū bǔyì* 後漢書補逸 [[KR2d0022]] — Yáo Zhīyīn's recovery of eight lost Eastern-Hàn histories at the Lùdí zhāi studio in Qiántáng.
- 1730–1745 — *Shàngshǐ* 尚史 [[KR2d0024]] — Lǐ Kǎi's 107-*juǎn* recasting of the pre-imperial record by direct citation, building on Mǎ Sù's *Yìshǐ*.
- 1780–1817 — *Shìběn* (Wáng Mó *jíběn*) 世本（王謨輯本）[[KR2d0025]] — Qīng-era reconstruction of the ancient genealogical text by Wáng Mó 王謨; one of the eight compilations in the *Shìběn bāzhǒng* 世本八種.
- 1767 — *Qīndìng xù tōngzhì* 欽定續通志 ([[KR2d0020]], in `data/catalogs/meta/KR2d.yaml` but not yet cataloged in `kb/KR2d/`) — Qiánlóng-era institutional continuation of Zhèng Qiáo's *Tōngzhì*, placed in *biéshǐ* by the Sìkù editors because it contains biographies (Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §51.2).
