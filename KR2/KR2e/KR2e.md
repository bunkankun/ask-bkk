# Biéshǐ lèi 別史類
*Other Histories*

## Scope and scholarly tradition

*Biéshǐ* 別史 ("other / separate histories") is a residual bibliographical category, not a literary genre. The term was coined in Chén Zhènsūn's 陳振孫 *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* 直齋書錄解題 in the thirteenth century to designate works "more serious than *zashi* 雜史, lying somewhere between *zhèngshǐ* 正史 on the one hand and *zashi* on the other," and was adopted by the Sìkù 四庫 editors as the fourth subdivision of the History branch in place of the *zashi* of earlier catalogs. As Endymion Wilkinson notes in §52 (*Miscellaneous Histories*) of *Chinese History: A New Manual*, the category gathers works of various kinds — both privately and officially compiled — that the editors judged worthy of preservation alongside the dynastic standard histories without being assimilable to them, and is therefore best rendered in English as "other histories." The Sìkù *zhèngběn* contains twenty *biéshǐ* and the *Sìkù zǒngmù* lists a further thirty-six in the *cúnmù* 存目 sections.

The twenty-two works gathered here in the Kanripo's KR2e span almost two millennia, from the late–Warring-States *Guóyǔ* 國語 [[KR2e0001]] through the Qiānlóng-court 1777 Chinese translation of a 1662 Mongol chronicle [[KR2e0022]]. Internally the corpus falls into three families: (i) pre-imperial historiographical compendia together with their post-classical commentarial traditions — the *Guóyǔ* and the *Zhànguócè* 戰國策 [[KR2e0003]] clusters; (ii) Táng–Yuán supplementary, eyewitness, and topically-organized records that fill gaps left by the official histories, including a substantial body of SòngJīn captivity literature and works recovered by the Sìkù compilers from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* 永樂大典; and (iii) MíngQīng *kǎozhèng* 考證 critique of the dynastic historiographical record together with the imperial-translation programme that brought non-Hàn historical traditions into the canonical corpus.

## Important texts and text clusters

**Pre-imperial historiographical classics.** The *Guóyǔ* 國語 [[KR2e0001]], the late–Warring-States state-by-state speech compendium taken in Hàn convention as the *wàizhuàn* 外傳 (outer commentary) to the *Chūnqiū* 春秋, is here printed in Wéi Zhāo's 韋昭 21-*juǎn* recension — the only continuous classical commentary on the work to survive. Sòng Xiáng's 宋庠 *Guóyǔ bǔyīn* 國語補音 [[KR2e0002]] reworks an inherited Táng anonymous *yīn* 音 into the only Sòng phonological apparatus to the *Guóyǔ* still extant.

**The *Zhànguócè* cluster.** Three of the Sìkù's principal recensions of Liú Xiàng's 劉向 redaction of the late–pre-imperial intriguers' (*zòngheng* 縱橫) speeches sit side-by-side: the HànSòng received text with Gāo Yòu 高誘's truncated annotation supplemented by Yáo Hóng 姚宏's *xùzhù* 續注 [[KR2e0003]]; Bào Biāo 鮑彪's 1147 Sòng-Confucian re-arrangement of the work into 10 chronological *juǎn* with a brand-new continuous commentary [[KR2e0004]]; and Wú Shīdào 吳師道's Yuán critical re-annotation in *bǔ* 補 / *zhèng* 正 interlinear tags correcting Bào against Yáo Hóng [[KR2e0005]]. Together these three works are the principal pre-modern editorial layers through which the *Zhànguócè* is read.

**Mirror-of-princes and Táng historiography.** *Zhēnguān zhèngyào* 貞觀政要 [[KR2e0006]], Wú Jīng's 吳兢 thematically arranged compendium of dialogues between Táng Tàizōng 唐太宗 and his court (here in Gē Zhí 戈直's 1333 Yuán *jíping* recension), is the East-Asian classic of the genre, repeatedly drawn on by the Sòng *jīngyán* 經筵 imperial lectures and by Hēian, Tokugawa, and Chosŏn court ideology. The late-Táng regional history *Zhǔgōng jiùshì* 渚宮舊事 [[KR2e0007]] (Yú Zhīgǔ 余知古, focused on JīngChǔ 荊楚 from Yùxióng 鬻熊 through the Táng) survives in only the first 5 of its original 10 *juǎn*, while Péi Tíngyù's 裴庭裕 *Dōngguān zòujì* 東觀奏記 [[KR2e0008]] preserves the otherwise lost record of the Xuānzōng 宣宗 reign (846–859) reconstructed from oral testimony after the *qǐjū zhù* 起居注 perished.

**Five-Dynasties supplements.** *Wǔdài shǐ quèwén* 五代史闕文 [[KR2e0009]] by Wáng Yǔchēng 王禹偁 (998–1001) and *Wǔdài shǐ bǔ* 五代史補 [[KR2e0010]] by Táo Yuè 陶岳 (1012) together supplement the early-Sòng official *Jiù Wǔdài shǐ* 舊五代史 of Xuē Jūzhèng 薛居正; both were drawn on by Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修's *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* 新五代史 [[KR2b0024]] and by Sīmǎ Guāng's *Zīzhì tōngjiàn* 資治通鑑.

**SòngJīn eyewitness historiography.** A tightly grouped cluster of first-hand records of the Jìngkāng 靖康 catastrophe and the SòngJīn diplomatic and prisoner-of-war regime: *Běi shòu jiànwén lù* 北狩見聞錄 [[KR2e0011]] by Cáo Xūn 曹勛, principal eyewitness to the abdicated Huīzōng 徽宗's removal to the Jīn camp; *Sōngmò jìwén* 松漠紀聞 [[KR2e0012]] by Hóng Hào 洪皓, reconstructed from memory after his fifteen-year Jīn captivity; and *Dà Jīn diàofá lù* 大金弔伐錄 [[KR2e0016]], an anonymous Jīn-period documentary archive of the formal state correspondence between Jīn and Sòng (and the Jīn-installed Chǔ 楚 / Qí 齊 puppets), recovered by the Sìkù editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*.

**Sòng institutional and topical compendia.** *Yānyì yímóu lù* 燕翼詒謀錄 [[KR2e0013]] by Wáng Yǒng 王栐 (1227) gathers 162 founding institutional precedents of the early Northern Sòng from the official archive, drawing explicitly *not* on *bài guān xiǎoshuō* 稗官小說. *Tàipíng zhìjī tǒnglèi* 太平治迹統類 [[KR2e0014]] by Péng Bǎichuān 彭百川 organizes Northern-Sòng governmental affairs by 88 *mén* 門 (categories), and is the most rigorous of the late-Sòng "categorized institutional digest" tradition that includes Jiāng Shǎoyú's *Huángcháo shìshí lèiyuàn* and Lǐ Xīnchuán's *Jiànyán yǐlái cháoyě záji*.

**Late-Sòng / Jīn / Yuán transition records.** *Xiánchún yíshì* 咸淳遺事 [[KR2e0015]] (anonymous, recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*) is the documentary record of the Xiánchún 咸淳 reign of Sòng Dùzōng 度宗 (1265–1272 only — the final two years are missing). *Rǔnán yíshì* 汝南遺事 [[KR2e0017]] is Wáng È 王鶚's day-by-day eyewitness diary of the Jīn collapse at Cài 蔡州 in 1234, in 107 dated *gāngmù* 綱目 entries and a principal source for the *Jīnshǐ* "Āizōng běnjì." *Qiántáng yíshì* 錢塘遺事 [[KR2e0018]] is Liú Yīqīng 劉一清's heavy compilation centred on a critique of Jiǎ Sìdào 賈似道's chancellorship. *Píng Sòng lù* 平宋錄 [[KR2e0019]] is Liú Mǐnzhōng 劉敏中's contemporary record of the 1276 Yuán conquest of Lín'ān 臨安, restored to its true author by the Sìkù editors against the long-standing misattribution to Píng Qìng'ān 平慶安.

**Míng *kǎozhèng* and Qīng translation.** *Yǎnshāntáng biéjí* 弇山堂別集 [[KR2e0020]] by Wáng Shìzhēn 王世貞 (100 *juǎn*) — combining narrative compendia of remarkable Míng affairs, the foundational *Shǐchéng kǎowù* 史乘考誤 critique of the Míng *shílù*, sixty-seven sub-categories of biographical tables, and sixteen institutional treatises — is the largest single work in the division and one of the founding documents of late-imperial evidential scholarship's approach to the Míng historiographical record. *Géchú yìshǐ* 革除逸史 [[KR2e0021]] by Zhū Mùzhì 朱睦㮮 is the most balanced of the Míng treatments of the Jiànwén 建文 reign that Yǒnglè 永樂 had "erased" from the dynastic chronology. *Qīndìng Měnggǔ yuánliú* 欽定蒙古源流 [[KR2e0022]] is the Qiánlóng court's 1777 Chinese translation of Saghang Sechen Hong-tayiji 薩囊徹辰鴻台吉's 1662 Ordos-Mongol *Erdeni-yin tobchi* — the principal indigenous-Mongol historical synthesis of the post-Yuán steppe and a Qing imperial corrective to centuries of garbled Sino-centric transcription of Mongol names.

## Important persons

- [[韋昭]] — late-Wú scholar whose 21-*juǎn* annotation is the only continuous classical commentary on the *Guóyǔ* still extant.
- [[宋庠]] — early Northern-Sòng senior statesman, compiler of the only Sòng phonological apparatus on the *Guóyǔ*.
- [[高誘]] — Hàn-period commentator on the *Zhànguócè*, the partial survival of whose annotation provided the foundation on which the Sòng recensions were built.
- [[姚宏]] — Southern-Sòng continuator of Gāo Yòu's *Zhànguócè* commentary; his *xùzhù* 續注 is the Sìkù base text.
- [[鮑彪]] — Southern-Sòng scholar who rearranged the *Zhànguócè* chronologically into 10 *juǎn* and produced the first thoroughly Sòng-Confucian re-reading of the work.
- [[吳師道]] — Yuán critical annotator who collated Bào Biāo against Yáo Hóng using *bǔ* / *zhèng* interlinear tags.
- [[吳兢]] — Táng court historian, compiler of the *Zhēnguān zhèngyào* — the East-Asian classic of the mirror-of-princes genre.
- [[戈直]] — Yuán *jíping* 集評 editor whose 1333 recension of the *Zhēnguān zhèngyào*, gathering the comments of twenty-two SòngYuán scholars, is the Sìkù base text.
- [[裴庭裕]] — late-Táng *shǐguǎn* 史館 historian, the principal recorder of the otherwise lost Xuānzōng reign.
- [[王禹偁]] — Northern-Sòng critic and Hànlín Academician, the foundational source-critical supplementer of the old *Wǔdài shǐ*.
- [[陶岳]] — early-Sòng official whose 1012 *Wǔdài shǐ bǔ* is the principal narrative supplement on which Ōuyáng Xiū and Sīmǎ Guāng drew.
- [[曹勛]] — Sòng official and principal eyewitness recorder of Huīzōng's removal into Jīn captivity.
- [[洪皓]] — Sòng envoy and fifteen-year Jīn captive whose ethnographic record of the Jīn court is the most circumstantial Sòng-period firsthand account of the northern empire.
- [[王栐]] — Sòng-loyalist compiler of early-Sòng founding institutional precedents, the most authoritative compilation of its kind among the *biéshǐ*.
- [[彭百川]] — late-Southern-Sòng Méishān 眉山 historian, leading exponent of the topically-organized "categorized institutional digest" of Northern-Sòng affairs.
- [[王鶚]] — Jīn-loyalist eyewitness to the dynasty's collapse and (later) early-Yuán Confucian official under Khubilai.
- [[劉敏中]] — early-Yuán Confucian official, contemporary recorder of the Yuán conquest of the Southern Sòng.
- [[王世貞]] — leading Hòu Qī Zǐ 後七子 literary figure and the foremost late-Míng *shǐxué* 史學 historian; foundational figure for late-imperial *kǎozhèng* of the Míng *shílù*.
- [[朱睦㮮]] — Míng imperial-clan scholar; the most balanced historian of the "erased" Jiànwén reign.
- [[薩囊徹辰]] — Ordos Mongol Hong-tayiji and historian, author of the seventeenth-century *Erdeni-yin tobchi* synthesis of Mongol-Tibetan historiographical tradition.

## Topics

- **The residual character of the category.** Following Chén Zhènsūn's original definition and Wilkinson §52, *biéshǐ* is not a genre but a placement between *zhèngshǐ* and *zashi*. The internal heterogeneity of the works — pre-imperial speech compendia next to Qīng translations of Mongol chronicles — is the structural condition of the category, not a flaw in its assembly.
- **Editorial layering of pre-imperial classics.** The Sìkù preserves multiple successive scholarly recensions of the same root text rather than a single recension. The *Zhànguócè* cluster [[KR2e0003]] / [[KR2e0004]] / [[KR2e0005]] is the principal site at which the choice between Liú Xiàng's state-by-state arrangement and Bào Biāo's chronological re-arrangement was negotiated.
- **Eyewitness historiography of dynastic catastrophe.** The SòngJīn captivity literature ([[KR2e0011]], [[KR2e0012]], [[KR2e0017]]) and the SòngYuán transition records ([[KR2e0015]], [[KR2e0019]]) constitute one of the largest concentrations of first-person testimony in the entire Sìkù History branch.
- **The *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* recovery programme.** Several of the works ([[KR2e0015]], [[KR2e0016]]) survived only because the Sìkù compilers extracted them from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. Their inclusion in the *biéshǐ* category reflects the bibliographic logic of late-eighteenth-century recovery rather than any classical pedigree.
- **Supplementing the standard histories.** The Five-Dynasties supplement cluster ([[KR2e0009]], [[KR2e0010]]) reflects the early-Sòng response to perceived deficiencies of the official *Jiù Wǔdài shǐ*, and remained in circulation even after Ōuyáng Xiū's *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* superseded it.
- **Mirror-of-princes literature.** *Zhēnguān zhèngyào* [[KR2e0006]] and its commentarial afterlife are the principal vehicle through which the *biéshǐ* category enters the *jīngyán* curriculum and the East-Asian political-theological lexicon.
- **Topical/categorized historiography.** [[KR2e0013]], [[KR2e0014]], and [[KR2e0020]] represent the *biéshǐ* category's hospitality to topically — as against annalistically — organized historical writing, a structural alternative to the *jìzhuàn* 紀傳 mould of the standard histories.
- **Erasure and rehabilitation.** The Jiànwén historiographical problem ([[KR2e0021]]) and Wáng Shìzhēn's *Shǐchéng kǎowù* attack on the *Tàizǔ shílù* ([[KR2e0020]]) make the division a focal site for late-Míng / early-Qīng questions about official memory and its corruption.
- **Non-Hàn historiography in Chinese translation.** [[KR2e0022]] uniquely integrates an indigenous Mongol historical tradition into the imperial corpus via Qiánlóng-court translation, exemplifying the late-Qing classification practice of treating non-Hàn dynastic histories as authoritative.

## Timeline

- c. 400 BCE – 280 CE — *Guóyǔ* 國語 [[KR2e0001]] — pre-imperial speech compendium printed with Wéi Zhāo's late-Wú commentary; the earliest stratum of the division.
- c. 100 BCE – 1146 CE — *Zhànguócè* 戰國策 [[KR2e0003]] — Hàn redaction by Liú Xiàng transmitted with Gāo Yòu's Hàn-period annotation and Yáo Hóng's twelfth-century *xùzhù*.
- c. 720 – 1333 — *Zhēnguān zhèngyào* 貞觀政要 [[KR2e0006]] — Wú Jīng's Táng compilation in Gē Zhí's Yuán *jíping* edition.
- c. 830–859 — *Zhǔgōng jiùshì* 渚宮舊事 [[KR2e0007]] — late-Táng regional history of JīngChǔ.
- 890–893 — *Dōngguān zòujì* 東觀奏記 [[KR2e0008]] — Péi Tíngyù's draft of the Xuānzōng reign.
- 998–1001 — *Wǔdài shǐ quèwén* 五代史闕文 [[KR2e0009]] — Wáng Yǔchēng's seventeen-item supplement to the old *Wǔdài shǐ*.
- 1012 — *Wǔdài shǐ bǔ* 五代史補 [[KR2e0010]] — Táo Yuè's 5-*juǎn* supplement; the principal narrative supplement to Xuē Jūzhèng.
- 1030–1066 — *Guóyǔ bǔyīn* 國語補音 [[KR2e0002]] — Sòng Xiáng's phonological apparatus to the *Guóyǔ*.
- 1127–1175 — *Dà Jīn diàofá lù* 大金弔伐錄 [[KR2e0016]] — anonymous Jīn-period archive of JīnSòng state correspondence.
- 1128 — *Běi shòu jiànwén lù* 北狩見聞錄 [[KR2e0011]] — Cáo Xūn's eyewitness record of Huīzōng's captivity.
- 1140–1147 — *Bào shì Zhànguó cè zhù* 鮑氏戰國策注 [[KR2e0004]] — Bào Biāo's Southern-Sòng re-annotation and chronological re-arrangement.
- 1143–1155 — *Sōngmò jìwén* 松漠紀聞 [[KR2e0012]] — Hóng Hào's ethnography of the Jīn empire, reconstructed from memory after his return from captivity.
- c. 1190–1230 — *Tàipíng zhìjī tǒnglèi* 太平治迹統類 [[KR2e0014]] — Péng Bǎichuān's topical digest of Northern-Sòng affairs.
- 1227 — *Yānyì yímóu lù* 燕翼詒謀錄 [[KR2e0013]] — Wáng Yǒng's compilation of founding Sòng institutional precedents.
- 1234 — *Rǔnán yíshì* 汝南遺事 [[KR2e0017]] — Wáng È's day-by-day record of the Jīn collapse at Cài.
- 1276–1318 — *Píng Sòng lù* 平宋錄 [[KR2e0019]] — Liú Mǐnzhōng's record of the Yuán conquest of the Southern Sòng.
- c. 1280–1320 — *Xiánchún yíshì* 咸淳遺事 [[KR2e0015]] — anonymous Sòng-loyalist record of the Xiánchún reign, recovered by the Sìkù from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*.
- c. 1290–1330 — *Qiántáng yíshì* 錢塘遺事 [[KR2e0018]] — Liú Yīqīng's critical record of the late Southern Sòng.
- 1320–1344 — *Zhànguó cè jiàozhù* 戰國策校注 [[KR2e0005]] — Wú Shīdào's Yuán critical re-annotation of Bào Biāo.
- 1570–1583 — *Géchú yìshǐ* 革除逸史 [[KR2e0021]] — Zhū Mùzhì's record of the "erased" Jiànwén reign.
- 1570–1590 — *Yǎnshāntáng biéjí* 弇山堂別集 [[KR2e0020]] — Wáng Shìzhēn's 100-*juǎn* compendium of Míng institutional history and *shǐchéng kǎowù*.
