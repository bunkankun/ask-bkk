# Qídì jì 齊地記
*Records of the Qi Region*

Anonymous

## About the work

An extremely short fragmentary geographic text covering the Shandong region. Only a single passage survives in the Kanripo text: the explanation of the name Bùyè Chéng 不夜城 ("Sleepless City" or "Nightless City") in the eastern Shandong peninsula. Author and date unknown. The work is distinct from the *Sān Qí lüèjì* ([[KR2k0162]]) by Fú Chén, though overlapping in geographic coverage.

### Abstract

The sole surviving passage explains the name of Bùyè Chéng 不夜城, located southeast of Yángjué 陽建 county in eastern Shandong: "In antiquity the sun appeared at night in this city; it was named 'Sleepless City' to mark its strangeness." The legend, also preserved in the *Hànshū* 漢書 geographical commentary and in *Shènjì* 神記-type texts, associates the anomaly with the Lái 萊 people of the eastern Shandong peninsula; the *Hànshū* commentary specifies that after witnessing the nighttime solar apparition, the Lái lord established a city at that spot and named it "Bùyè." The fragment's brevity makes author attribution and dating impossible; the text is consistent with a Wei-Jin or Southern Dynasties compilation of regional legendary geography.
