## Scope and scholarly tradition

`KR2p` 出土文獻 (*Chūtǔ wénxiàn*, "Excavated Documents") is a Kanripo extension category that sits formally under `KR2` 史部 (Histories) but in practice lies outside the traditional Sìkù taxonomy: the Sìkù subdivisions of 史部 stop at `KR2o` 史評類 and have no place for texts recovered by archaeology in the 20th and 21st centuries. The works collected here are pre-imperial or early-imperial manuscripts retrieved from tombs — silk rolls and bamboo strips of the late Wǎrring States (late 4th c. BCE) and early Western Hàn (sealed 168 BCE) — and they are heterogeneous in genre: a Mǎwángduī 馬王堆 bedchamber-arts silk text alongside two Guōdiàn 郭店 Confucian philosophical bamboo manuscripts. They are filed under 史部 because, from a transmission-history point of view, they are primarily *documents* (sources for the intellectual and material history of the period) rather than works of any one of the Four Categories — none of the three is part of the received canon, none has a continuous transmission history, and each survives in exactly one physical witness.

The relevant scholarly tradition is therefore not classical exegesis but modern Chinese palaeography and the philosophy-of-history reading of excavated texts. Wilkinson, *Chinese History: A New Manual*, treats this corpus in §59.7 (Excavated Documents — bamboo and silk manuscripts) and in the bibliographic chapters surrounding it (the *Lǎozǐ* Guōdiàn finds at §6 of the excavated-texts bibliography, the Mǎwángduī medical and philosophical caches in the Hàn manuscript sections); the field has its own *editiones principes* (the 1985 Wénwù chūbǎnshè reconstruction of the Mǎwángduī silks; the 1998 Wénwù *Guōdiàn Chǔmù zhújiǎn* volume), its own running critical apparatus (Liú Zhāo's 《郭店楚簡校釋》, Lǐ Líng's *Jiào dú jì*, Qiú Xīguī's 2014 *Chángshā Mǎwángduī Hànmù jiǎnbó jíchéng*), and its own meta-debates about strip ordering, modern editorial titles, and the relationship of the manuscripts to the transmitted canon. A reader approaching `KR2p` must therefore keep in mind that the *titles* under which these works appear are modern editorial assignments (from the opening graphs of the first strip, or by analogy with a transmitted text of the same name), and that the *texts* themselves are reconstructed from physically broken manuscripts whose strip order is, in places, still contested.

## Important texts and text clusters

The division currently holds three texts. They divide into two physical-archaeological clusters:

**Mǎwángduī 馬王堆 silk-manuscript cluster (Hàn tomb M3, sealed 168 BCE).** From the medical-technical silk cache, one text is at present catalogued in `KR2p`:

- *Hé yīnyáng* 合陰陽 [[KR2p0015]] — short anonymous treatise on heterosexual technique within an early-Hàn medical-cosmological framework; the locus classicus for the "Ten Movements / Ten Postures / Eight Movements" catalogue of *fángzhōng* 房中 practice and for the ejaculation-restraint scheme later reused in the *Sùnǚ jīng* tradition.

**Guōdiàn 郭店 bamboo-manuscript cluster (Chǔ tomb 1, Jǐngmén, sealed c. 300 BCE).** From the Guōdiàn Confucian-text group, two texts are catalogued here:

- *Wǔxíng* 五行 [[KR2p0021]] — short Confucian treatise defining *rényìlǐzhìshèng* 仁義禮智聖 as inner virtues "formed within" (*xíng yú nèi* 形於內), structured around *shèn dú* 慎獨; the principal physical evidence for Páng Pú's identification of the so-called SīMèng 思孟 school attacked in *Xúnzǐ* 荀子 *Fēi shí'èr zǐ* 非十二子, and the earliest witness of a *Wǔxíng* text whose Mǎwángduī counterpart adds a *shuō* 說 commentary layer.
- *Chéng zhī wén zhī* 成之聞之 [[KR2p0024]] — short Confucian discourse on rulership by personal exemplification (*shēn* 身), arguing that *jūnzǐ* governance requires *qiú zhū jǐ* 求諸己; thematically adjacent to *Lǐjì* 禮記 *Zī yī* 緇衣 and *Dàxué* 大學, and central to the Guōdiàn editorial debate over strip division (Lǐ Líng's redivision under the title 教 *Jiào* + 天常 *Tiān cháng*).

A natural further cluster — the Mǎwángduī companion *fángzhōng* texts (e.g. *Tiānxià zhìdào tán* 天下至道談, *Shíwèn* 十問) cited in [[KR2p0015]]'s Abstract, and the wider Guōdiàn Confucian set (*Zī yī*, *Liù dé*, *Xìng zì mìng chū*, *Zūn dé yì*, *Tángyú zhī dào*) cited in [[KR2p0024]]'s Abstract — is at present only partially represented in the division. As further excavated texts are added to the Kanripo corpus, the cluster structure of `KR2p` will deepen along the lines laid out in these two abstracts.

## Important persons

All three works in the division are **anonymous excavated manuscripts** without attributable authors, and consequently their per-text frontmatter `persons:` lists are empty. The persons whose work has shaped this division are therefore modern: the *Mǎwángduī Hànmù bóshū zhěnglǐ xiǎozǔ* 馬王堆漢墓帛書整理小組 (the original collation team), the Jīngmén Municipal Museum 荊門市博物館 (Guōdiàn *editio princeps* team), and individual scholars — Páng Pú 龐樸 (Sī-Mèng-school identification of *Wǔxíng*), Lǐ Líng 李零 (palaeographic redivisions of the Guōdiàn Confucian cluster and of *Chéng zhī wén zhī*), Qiú Xīguī 裘錫圭 (lead editor of both the 1998 Guōdiàn volume's annotations and the 2014 *Chángshā Mǎwángduī Hànmù jiǎnbó jíchéng*), Liú Zhāo 劉釗 (current standard Chinese critical apparatus), and Donald Harper (standard English critical edition of the Mǎwángduī medical silks). These figures are named in the Abstracts of the individual texts ([[KR2p0015]], [[KR2p0021]], [[KR2p0024]]); they are not catalogued as authors and have no Person notes in `kb/Persons/`.

## Topics

Three intertwined topics run through the division.

**Excavation, reconstruction, and editorial title.** Every text in `KR2p` is a physical artefact — broken silk, scattered bamboo strips — whose modern *form* (numbered sections, running order, even title) is an editorial reconstruction. *Hé yīnyáng* is named from its opening *fán jiāng hé yīnyáng zhī fāng* 凡將合陰陽之方; *Chéng zhī wén zhī* takes its title from the opening words of what is itself a contested strip-ordering; *Wǔxíng* alone has a title attested in the transmitted record (in *Xúnzǐ* *Fēi shí'èr zǐ*). Any use of these texts must therefore engage with the editorial apparatus, not just the words on the strip.

**Pre-Qín / early-Hàn Confucian moral psychology.** The two Guōdiàn texts ([[KR2p0021]], [[KR2p0024]]) supply the missing-link evidence between the *Lúnyǔ* and *Mèngzǐ* on the inner-versus-outer reading of virtue. *Wǔxíng*'s interiority criterion (*xíng yú nèi* / *xíng yú wài*) and *Chéng zhī wén zhī*'s *qiú zhū jǐ* programme have together reshaped the post-1993 scholarly account of how 仁-義-禮-智-聖 and the doctrine of *shèn dú* 慎獨 developed before being codified in the *Zhōngyōng* and *Dàxué*.

**Early Chinese medical and *yǎngshēng* technique.** Distinct from but contemporary with the Confucian moralisation of virtue, the Mǎwángduī medical silks ([[KR2p0015]] and its M3 companions) show a parallel pre-religious idiom of bodily regulation: intercourse as controlled exchange of *qì*, ejaculation timing as a longevity technique, the HuángLǎo "live as long as Heaven and Earth" closing formula. The division as it currently stands therefore juxtaposes the two great strands of late-Wǎrring-States / early-Hàn elite self-cultivation — Confucian moral training and *fángzhōng* / *yǎngshēng* technical training — as physically contemporary documents.

A fourth running issue — the **status of the SīMèng 思孟 school** and the long Qīng-era dispute over what *wǔxíng* meant in *Xúnzǐ Fēi shí'èr zǐ* — is decisively addressed by the Guōdiàn evidence in [[KR2p0021]] and remains the single most-cited contribution of this corpus to Confucian intellectual history.

## Timeline

- c. 350–300 BCE — *Wǔxíng* 五行 [[KR2p0021]] — earliest witness of the text; physical strips from a tomb sealed c. 300 BCE, composition placed in the second half of the 4th c. BCE.
- c. 350–300 BCE — *Chéng zhī wén zhī* 成之聞之 [[KR2p0024]] — same Guōdiàn cache; pre-Mèngzǐ Confucian discourse on rulership by self-cultivation.
- c. 300 BCE – 168 BCE — *Hé yīnyáng* 合陰陽 [[KR2p0015]] — Mǎwángduī silk roll, sealed in M3 in 168 BCE; on palaeographic grounds composed late Wǎrring States to very early Western Hàn; here placed at the date of the received silk recension consistent with the project's dating policy.
