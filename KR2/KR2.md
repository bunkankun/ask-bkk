# KR2 史部

KR2:  the Histories division.

- [[KR2a]] 正史類 *Zhèngshǐ lèi* — Histories — Standard Histories. Dynastic histories, their core commentaries, supplements, tables, and corrections. 39 texts.
- [[KR2b]] 編年 *Biānnián* — Histories — Annals. Year-by-year chronicle histories in the *biānnián* tradition. 40 texts.
- [[KR2c]] 紀事本末類 *Jìshì běnmò lèi* — Histories — Topically Arranged Histories. Event-centered narratives tracing historical affairs from beginning to end. 23 texts.
- [[KR2d]] 別史類 *Biéshǐ lèi* — Histories — Other Histories. Separate histories outside the official standard-history canon. 25 texts.
- [[KR2e]] 雜史類 *Záshǐ lèi* — Histories — Miscellaneous Histories. Informal, supplementary, and residual historical writings. 22 texts.
- [[KR2f]] 詔令奏議類 *Zhàolìng zòuyì lèi* — Histories — Imperial Edicts and Memorials. Documentary collections of commands, policy memorials, and court statecraft prose. 41 texts.
- [[KR2g]] 傳記類 *Zhuànjì lèi* — Histories — Biographical Writings. Biographies, exemplary lives, local worthies, and biographical compilations. 61 texts.
- [[KR2h]] 史鈔類 *Shǐchāo lèi* — Histories — History Excerpts. Abridgments, extracts, and rearrangements of earlier historical works. 3 texts.
- [[KR2i]] 載記類 *Zǎijì lèi* — Histories — Annals of Non-Legitimate Regimes. Records of rival, regional, and non-orthodox regimes. 23 texts.
- [[KR2j]] 時令類 *Shílìng lèi* — Histories — Seasonal Ordinances. Calendar, festivals, phenology, and monthly ritual-administrative order. 2 texts.
- [[KR2k]] 地理類 *Dìlǐ lèi* — Histories — Geography. Gazetteers, geographical treatises, waterways, administrative geography, and travel knowledge. 201 texts.
- [[KR2l]] 職官類 *Zhíguān lèi* — Histories — Official Posts. Bureaucratic offices, institutions, administrative titles, and official systems. 22 texts.
- [[KR2m]] 政書類 *Zhèngshū lèi* — Histories — Works Relating to Government Administration. Statecraft, institutions, fiscal systems, law, rites, and administrative compendia. 57 texts.
- [[KR2n]] 目錄類 *Mùlù lèi* — Histories — Bibliographic and Epigraphic Catalogues. Catalogues, bibliography, epigraphy, collection records, and textual inventories. 47 texts.
- [[KR2o]] 史評類 *Shǐpíng lèi* — Histories — Historical Criticism. Evaluations of historical writing, historical judgement, and historiographical method. 25 texts.
- [[KR2p]] 出土文獻 *Chūtǔ wénxiàn* — Histories — Excavated Manuscripts. Excavated and manuscript materials outside the traditional Sìkù taxonomy. 158 texts.
