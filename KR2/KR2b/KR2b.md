## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR2b 編年 (*Biānnián*, "Annals") collects works composed in the *biānnián tǐ* 編年體 — the year-by-year chronicle form modelled ultimately on the *Chūnqiū* 春秋 and *Zuǒ zhuàn* 左傳 — within `KR2` 史部 (*Shǐ bù*, the Histories branch of the Sìkù quánshū). It is one of the two principal historiographical genres of the imperial period, standing alongside the *jìzhuàn tǐ* 紀傳體 ("annals-biography" form) of the official *Histories* collected in [[KR2a]] 正史. Wilkinson (§48) traces the genre from the earliest surviving examples — the *Chūnqiū* itself and the *Zhúshū jìnián* 竹書紀年 ([[KR2b0001]]) — through the Hàn-period dynastic annals of [[荀悅]] ([[KR2b0003]]) and [[袁宏]] ([[KR2b0004]]), an extended hiatus during which the official *jìzhuàn* form crowded out private annalistic composition, and the Northern-Sòng restoration of the form by [[司馬光]] in the *Zī zhì tōng jiàn* 資治通鑑 ([[KR2b0007]]). After Sīmǎ Guāng the *biānnián* mode became "once again on a par with the annals-biography" (Wilkinson §48.3) and dominated late-imperial reading habits: from the Sòng to the twentieth century, what most educated people read of Chinese history was the *Tōng jiàn* and Zhū Xī's *Gāngmù* 綱目 (catalogued in `KR2a`), not the dynastic *Histories*.

The chronological span of the works gathered here runs from the late second century CE (Xún Yuè's *Qián Hàn jì*, 198–200 CE) to the Qiánlóng court compilations of the 1770s and 1780s ([[KR2b0037]], [[KR2b0038]], [[KR2b0039]]). The corpus is unusually concentrated: of the forty cataloged works, twenty-four are tied directly to the *Tōng jiàn* — as its apparatus, commentary, continuation, pre-history, abridgment, or *Gāngmù*-form recasting. KR2b is therefore in effect a working library for reading and extending one book.

## Important texts and text clusters

**The two surviving Hàn-Jìn dynastic annals.** The genre's earliest extant exemplars after the *Chūnqiū*-tradition Classics are *Qián Hàn jì* 前漢紀 [[KR2b0003]] of [[荀悅]] (commissioned by Hàn Xiàndì 漢獻帝 and presented in 200 CE) and *Hòu Hàn jì* 後漢紀 [[KR2b0004]] of [[袁宏]] (mid-fourth century). Wilkinson §48.2 notes that these are the only two of many Hàn-Táng annalistic histories to survive intact, the rest having been crowded out by the official *jìzhuàn* *Histories* and then by Sīmǎ Guāng's *Tōng jiàn*.

**Pre-imperial annalistic survivals.** *Zhúshū jìnián* 竹書紀年 [[KR2b0001]] survives in the WYG only as the late-Míng "current text" (*jīnběn* 今本) recension of c. 1570, the genuine 281 CE Jíxiàn 汲縣 tomb-recovery having been reduced to fragments by the Southern Sòng. *Zhúshū tǒngjiān* 竹書統箋 [[KR2b0002]] of [[徐文靖]] is the standard Qīng commentary on the *jīnběn*.

**The *Tōng jiàn* core.** *Zī zhì tōng jiàn* 資治通鑑 [[KR2b0007]] of [[司馬光]], the foundational text of the division and the model for every subsequent universal chronicle, presented in 1084 in 294 *juǎn* and covering 403 BCE to 959 CE. Composed alongside it are the three apparatus works also written by Sīmǎ Guāng: *Zī zhì tōng jiàn kǎo yì* 資治通鑑考異 [[KR2b0008]] (the source-critical collation in 30 *juǎn*), *Zī zhì tōng jiàn mù lù* 資治通鑑目錄 [[KR2b0010]] (the analytical index with synchronised calendrical and astronomical tables), and *Zī zhì tōng jiàn shì lì* 資治通鑑釋例 [[KR2b0009]] (the editorial-conventions handbook). To these belongs also *Jīgǔ lù* 稽古錄 [[KR2b0014]], Sīmǎ Guāng's own short universal outline from Fúxī to 1067, presented in 1086 as a portable companion to the *Tōng jiàn*. The standard premodern commentary, the *yīnzhù* 音注 of [[胡三省]] completed in 1295, is woven throughout the WYG transmission of the base text.

**The *Tōng jiàn* commentary and apparatus tradition.** *Zī zhì tōng jiàn wài jì* 資治通鑑外紀 [[KR2b0015]] of [[劉恕]] (1078) supplies the pre-403-BCE prequel that Liú Shù had originally been commissioned to write for the *Tōng jiàn* but that did not enter the main work. *Zī zhì tōng jiàn shì wén* 資治通鑑釋文 [[KR2b0016]] of [[史炤]] (mid-twelfth century) is the earliest substantial phonetic-and-gloss commentary, later corrected by Hú Sānxǐng's *Tōng jiàn shì wén biàn wù* 通鑑釋文辨誤 [[KR2b0012]] (1295). *Tōngjiàn dìlǐ tōngshì* 通鑑地理通釋 [[KR2b0011]] of [[王應麟]] (late thirteenth century) is the standard geographical reference. *Tōngjiàn Hú zhù jǔ zhèng* 通鑑胡注擧正 [[KR2b0013]] of [[陳景雲]] is the Qīng *kǎozhèng* correction of Hú Sānxǐng's geographical errors.

**The *Tōng jiàn* pre-history and continuations.** *Zī zhì tōng jiàn qián biān* 資治通鑑前編 [[KR2b0033]] of [[金履祥]] (late thirteenth century) extends the chronicle backwards from 403 BCE to the legendary high-antiquity rulers. *Xù Zī zhì tōng jiàn cháng biān* 續資治通鑑長編 [[KR2b0019]] of [[李燾]] (presented in instalments 1163–1183) is the most ambitious Northern-Sòng continuation, in 520 (originally 980) *juǎn*. *Tōng jiàn xù biān* 通鑑續編 [[KR2b0034]] of [[陳桱]] (late Yuán) supplies a Sòng-Yuán continuation. *Zī zhì tōng jiàn hòu biān* 資治通鑑後編 [[KR2b0040]] of [[徐乾學]] (presented 1694) is the leading early-Qīng continuation, covering 960–1367.

**The Sòng private annalistic histories.** A distinct cluster, written without imperial commission, fills out the late-Sòng-to-Yuán record where the *Tōng jiàn* stops. *Jiànyán yǐlái xìnián yào lù* 建炎以來繫年要錄 [[KR2b0024]] of [[李心傳]] (early thirteenth century) is the indispensable source for the Southern-Sòng founding under Gāozōng 高宗, an extension of Lǐ Tāo's project. *Zhōngxīng xiǎo jì* 中興小紀 [[KR2b0018]] of [[熊克]] is the parallel popular abridgment. *Jiǔcháo biānnián bèi yào* 九朝編年備要 [[KR2b0025]] of [[陳均]] and *Liǎngcháo gāngmù bèi yào* 兩朝綱目備要 [[KR2b0030]] (anon.) cover the rest of the Northern and middle Southern Sòng. *Xù Sòng biānnián zī zhì tōng jiàn* 續宋編年資治通鑑 [[KR2b0026]] of [[劉時擧]] gives the late-Sòng narrative. *Sòng shǐ quán wén* 宋史全文 [[KR2b0032]] (anon., late Sòng–Yuán) is the comprehensive single-dynasty annalistic Sòng record. *Sòng jì sān cháo zhèng yào* 宋季三朝政要 [[KR2b0031]] (anon. Yuán) and *Jìngkāng yào lù* 靖康要錄 [[KR2b0029]] (anon. Southern Sòng) document the dynasty's final crises. *Xī Hàn nián jì* 西漢年紀* [[KR2b0028]] of [[王益之]] applies the form to the Western Hàn, and *Dà shì jì* 大事記 [[KR2b0023]] of [[呂祖謙]] is a learned annalistic *kǎozhèng* through 481 BCE, continued in [[王禕]]'s *Dà shì jì xù biān* 大事記續編 [[KR2b0035]] of the early Míng.

**The *Gāngmù* 綱目 supplementary tradition.** Zhū Xī's own *Tōng jiàn gāngmù* is catalogued in `KR2a`; KR2b holds the Míng-Qīng continuations, critiques, and corrections. *Gāngmù xù lín* 綱目續麟 [[KR2b0020]] of [[張自勳]] (1643) and *Gāngmù fēnzhù shí yí* 綱目分註拾遺 [[KR2b0021]] of [[芮長恤]] are critical supplements; *Gāngmù dìng wù* 綱目訂誤 [[KR2b0022]] of [[陳景雲]] corrects errors in the standard text. The Qīng imperial projects close the division: *Yù pī lìdài tōng jiàn jí lǎn* 御批歷代通鑑輯覽 [[KR2b0037]] (1767, with Qiánlóng's personal interlinear comments), *Yù dìng zī zhì tōng jiàn gāngmù sān biān* 御定資治通鑑綱目三編 [[KR2b0038]] (1746/1775, the Míng-history *Gāngmù* continuation), and *Huáng Qīng kāiguó fānglüè* 皇清開國方略 [[KR2b0039]] (1786, the official annalistic record of the Qīng founding).

**Outliers.** *Yuán jīng* 元經 [[KR2b0005]] is a pseudepigraphic Northern-Sòng fabrication ascribed to the Suí thinker [[王通]] but actually compiled by [[阮逸]] (c. 1027–1063), still preserved in the WYG under the older attribution. *Táng chuàngyè qǐjūzhù* 唐創業起居注 [[KR2b0006]] of [[溫大雅]] is the only Táng court diary (*qǐjūzhù* 起居注) to survive, an eye-witness chronicle of the 617–618 Lǐyuán uprising. *Biānnián tōng zǎi* 編年通載 (殘) [[KR2b0027]] of [[章衡]] (1074), now a 4-juan fragment of an originally 10-juan work, is the most condensed universal chronicle of the Northern Sòng — exactly contemporary with Sīmǎ Guāng's project but independent of it. *Huángwáng dà jì* 皇王大紀 [[KR2b0017]] of [[胡宏]] is the Southern-Sòng *dàoxué* annalistic treatment of the sage-king age. *Yuán shǐ xù biān* 元史續編 [[KR2b0036]] of [[胡粹中]] (early Míng) supplements the post-Sòng Yuán in *Gāngmù* form where the official *Yuán shǐ* is thin.

## Important persons

- [[司馬光]] (1019–1086) — the central figure of the division; author of *Zī zhì tōng jiàn* and its three companion apparatus works ([[KR2b0007]]–[[KR2b0010]]), of the parallel *Jīgǔ lù* [[KR2b0014]], and the architect of the *cóngmù* 叢目 / *chángbiān* 長編 / *zhèngwén* 正文 three-stage compositional method that became the model for all subsequent universal chronicles.
- [[劉恕]] (1032–1078) — one of Sīmǎ Guāng's three principal collaborators on the *Tōng jiàn* (responsible for the Three Kingdoms through Northern-and-Southern Dynasties drafts), and on his own the author of the pre-history *Wài jì* [[KR2b0015]]. Sīmǎ Guāng judged him the most original of the three collaborators.
- [[胡三省]] (1230–1302) — author of the standard premodern *Tōng jiàn* commentary ([[KR2b0007]]) and the *Biàn wù* [[KR2b0012]] that corrects Shǐ Zhào's [[KR2b0016]] earlier glosses. Wrote in secret under Yuán rule and survived the *Jǐchǒu* (1289) coastal raids by sealing his manuscript in a cellar.
- [[李燾]] (1115–1184) — author of the *Xù Zī zhì tōng jiàn cháng biān* [[KR2b0019]], the indispensable source for Northern Sòng history.
- [[李心傳]] (1167–1244) — author of *Jiànyán yǐlái xìnián yào lù* [[KR2b0024]], the corresponding indispensable source for the Southern-Sòng founding under Gāozōng.
- [[呂祖謙]] (1137–1181) — author of *Dà shì jì* [[KR2b0023]]; major dàoxué historian whose annalistic *kǎozhèng* method influenced later commentators on the *Gāngmù*.
- [[王應麟]] (1223–1296) — author of *Tōngjiàn dìlǐ tōngshì* [[KR2b0011]], the standard geographical reference for the *Tōng jiàn*; the leading polymath of late Southern Sòng *kǎozhèng*.
- [[金履祥]] (1232–1303) — author of *Tōng jiàn qián biān* [[KR2b0033]]; a leading Jīn-Huá-school disciple of Wáng Bǎi 王柏 who extended the *Tōng jiàn* backwards into the legendary period in Zhū Xī's *Gāngmù* form.
- [[徐乾學]] (1631–1694) — author of *Tōng jiàn hòu biān* [[KR2b0040]] (compiled with Wàn Sītóng 萬斯同); the leading early-Qīng patron of *Tōng jiàn*-tradition scholarship.
- [[陳景雲]] (1670–1747) — author of two corrective Qīng-period sub-commentaries, *Tōngjiàn Hú zhù jǔ zhèng* [[KR2b0013]] and *Gāngmù dìng wù* [[KR2b0022]]; representative of Qīng *kǎozhèng* applied to the *Tōng jiàn* tradition.
- [[徐文靖]] (1668–1756) — author of *Zhúshū tǒngjiān* [[KR2b0002]]; the most ambitious Qīng commentator on the *Zhúshū jìnián* before Wáng Guówéi.
- [[高宗弘曆]] (Qiánlóng, 1711–1799) — imperial patron and active editor of *Yù pī lìdài tōng jiàn jí lǎn* [[KR2b0037]]; supplied a substantial body of his own marginal comments (over 150,000 characters) and decisively shaped Qīng-period orthodox views of legitimate dynastic succession.
- [[荀悅]] (148–209) and [[袁宏]] (328–376) — authors of the two surviving Hàn-Jìn annalistic dynastic histories ([[KR2b0003]], [[KR2b0004]]); together they preserved the *biānnián* form across the *jìzhuàn* eight centuries before Sīmǎ Guāng.

## Topics

- ***Biānnián* vs. *jìzhuàn* — the genre's recurring rivalry.** From the *Shǐjì* onwards the composite annals-biography form was the official mode, with the *biānnián* form recovering primacy only in 1084. Sīmǎ Guāng's programmatic complaint that the *jìzhuàn Histories* are "prolix" (*fánróng* 煩冗) and force the reader to chase events across scattered chapters is the founding manifesto of the post-Hàn revival of the chronicle form.
- **The *Tōng jiàn*'s three-stage compositional method.** *Cóngmù* 叢目 (chronological assembly of primary sources) → *chángbiān* 長編 (long-form topical drafts) → *zhèngwén* 正文 (final compression). This is the most influential methodological innovation in the history of Chinese historiography and survives as a model in the term *chángbiān* — preserved in the title of [[KR2b0019]] and used through to twentieth-century compilations of similar scope.
- ***Kǎo yì* 考異 — variant-reading collation as historiographical apparatus.** Sīmǎ Guāng's parallel *Tōng jiàn kǎo yì* [[KR2b0008]], in which contested points are openly discussed and the source-critical decisions defended, is the prototype of every subsequent Chinese *kǎo yì* and a remarkable anticipation of modern critical-edition practice.
- **The *zhèng tǒng* 正統 problem and Zhū Xī's reordering.** Sīmǎ Guāng adopted a pragmatic single-thread succession (Wèi over Shǔ Hàn, the Northern Dynasties over the Southern, the Five Dynasties as legitimate) for narrative convenience. Zhū Xī's *Gāngmù* drastically recast the same material around moralist *zhèng tǒng* criteria, granting legitimacy instead to Shǔ Hàn (against Wèi), the Eastern Jìn and the Southern Dynasties (against the North), and disqualifying the Five Dynasties as a *wútǒng* 無統 interregnum. The reverberations of this dispute run through every Míng-Qīng continuation in this division — most explicitly through the corrective sub-commentaries of [[張自勳]] [[KR2b0020]], [[芮長恤]] [[KR2b0021]], and [[陳景雲]] [[KR2b0022]], and through the imperial reinterpretations of Qiánlóng in [[KR2b0037]] and [[KR2b0038]].
- **Imperial patronage in the Qīng.** Three of the four longest works in the division ([[KR2b0037]], [[KR2b0038]], [[KR2b0039]]) are court-commissioned compilations executed under direct Qiánlóng-era oversight. They mark the late-imperial recapture of the *biānnián* tradition by the state and supply the dominant pedagogical historiography of the nineteenth century.
- **The *Zhúshū* problem.** The *jīnběn Zhúshū jìnián* ([[KR2b0001]]) preserved in the WYG is, by the Qīng *kǎozhèng* consensus codified by [[王應麟]]'s successor scholars and definitively by Wáng Guówéi (1916), a late-Míng reconstitution rather than the genuine 281 CE recovery. The Sìkù editors themselves denounced it, in one of the most painstaking textual demonstrations in the entire *tíyào*; modern revisionist work (Shào Dōngfāng, Nivison, Shaughnessy) has rehabilitated parts of its chronology against the Qīng verdict. The case is the locus classicus for the problem of received recension versus authentic source in the early-annalistic tradition.
- **Geographic and philological apparatus.** The *Tōng jiàn* tradition is unusually rich in dedicated geographical and phonetic reference works ([[KR2b0011]], [[KR2b0016]], [[KR2b0012]]) — the form's exhaustive coverage of pre-Sòng institutional and toponymic detail demanded specialised companion volumes.

## Timeline

- 198–200 — *Qián Hàn jì* 前漢紀 [[KR2b0003]] — Xún Yuè's commissioned annalistic recasting of Bān Gù's *Hàn shū*, the first surviving annalistic dynastic history after the *Chūnqiū*-tradition Classics.
- 360–372 — *Hòu Hàn jì* 後漢紀 [[KR2b0004]] — Yuán Hóng's parallel annalistic account of the Later Hàn, the second surviving Hàn-Jìn dynastic annal.
- 618–626 — *Táng chuàngyè qǐjūzhù* 唐創業起居注 [[KR2b0006]] — sole extant Táng-period *qǐjūzhù*, an eyewitness chronicle of Lǐ Yuān's 617–618 founding.
- c. 1027–1063 — *Yuán jīng* 元經 [[KR2b0005]] — Northern-Sòng pseudepigraphic chronicle of the 290–589 disunion period, compiled by Ruǎn Yì under the older attribution to Wáng Tōng.
- 1052–1074 — *Biānnián tōng zǎi* (殘) 編年通載 [[KR2b0027]] — Zhāng Héng's surviving 4-juan fragment of a 10-juan universal chronicle, presented to Shénzōng in 1074, the principal Northern-Sòng predecessor of Sīmǎ Guāng.
- 1066–1084 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn* 資治通鑑 [[KR2b0007]] — Sīmǎ Guāng's 294-juan universal annalistic chronicle covering 403 BCE–959 CE, the foundational text of the division.
- 1066–1084 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn kǎo yì* 資治通鑑考異 [[KR2b0008]] — Sīmǎ Guāng's parallel 30-juan variant-readings apparatus.
- 1066–1084 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn mù lù* 資治通鑑目錄 [[KR2b0010]] — Sīmǎ Guāng's 30-juan analytical index with calendrical-astronomical tables.
- c. 1066–1166 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn shì lì* 資治通鑑釋例 [[KR2b0009]] — Sīmǎ Guāng's editorial-conventions handbook, transmitted with later additions.
- 1066–1086 — *Jīgǔ lù* 稽古錄 [[KR2b0014]] — Sīmǎ Guāng's short universal outline from Fúxī to 1067, presented to the throne in 1086.
- 1077–1078 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn wài jì* 資治通鑑外紀 [[KR2b0015]] — Liú Shù's pre-403-BCE "outer chronicle," dictated to his son on his deathbed.
- 1130–1160 — *Huángwáng dà jì* 皇王大紀 [[KR2b0017]] — Hú Hóng's Southern-Sòng *dàoxué* chronicle of the sage-king age.
- 1144–1184 — *Xù Zī zhì tōng jiàn cháng biān* 續資治通鑑長編 [[KR2b0019]] — Lǐ Tāo's vast Northern-Sòng continuation, presented in successive instalments.
- c. 1150–1160 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn shì wén* 資治通鑑釋文 [[KR2b0016]] — Shǐ Zhào's early phonetic-and-gloss commentary, later corrected by Hú Sānxǐng.
- c. 1162–1190 — *Zhōngxīng xiǎo jì* 中興小紀 [[KR2b0018]] — Xióng Kè's popular Southern-Sòng restoration chronicle.
- c. 1165–1200 — *Jìngkāng yào lù* 靖康要錄 [[KR2b0029]] — anonymous Southern-Sòng documentary record of the 1126–1127 *Jìngkāng* catastrophe.
- 1180–1181 — *Dà shì jì* 大事記 [[KR2b0023]] — Lǚ Zǔqiān's annalistic *kǎozhèng* of major events through 481 BCE.
- c. 1180–1200 — *Xī Hàn nián jì* 西漢年紀 [[KR2b0028]] — Wáng Yìzhī's annalistic account of the Western Hàn.
- 1202–1208 — *Jiànyán yǐlái xìnián yào lù* 建炎以來繫年要錄 [[KR2b0024]] — Lǐ Xīnchuán's indispensable 200-juan record of the Southern-Sòng founding, formally extending Lǐ Tāo's *Cháng biān*.
- c. 1220–1229 — *Jiǔcháo biānnián bèi yào* 九朝編年備要 [[KR2b0025]] — Chén Jūn's compendious Northern-Sòng abridgment.
- c. 1224–1280 — *Liǎngcháo gāngmù bèi yào* 兩朝綱目備要 [[KR2b0030]] — anonymous *Gāngmù*-form continuation covering Guāngzōng and Níngzōng of the Southern Sòng.
- c. 1240–1264 — *Xù Sòng biānnián zī zhì tōng jiàn* 續宋編年資治通鑑 [[KR2b0026]] — Liú Shíjǔ's annalistic continuation of the *Tōng jiàn* through the Sòng.
- 1280–1295 — *Tōngjiàn shìwén biànwù* 通鑑釋文辨誤 [[KR2b0012]] — Hú Sānxǐng's correction of Shǐ Zhào's earlier glosses, completed alongside the main *yīnzhù*.
- 1280–1296 — *Tōngjiàn dìlǐ tōngshì* 通鑑地理通釋 [[KR2b0011]] — Wáng Yīnglín's geographical reference companion.
- c. 1280–1300 — *Sòng shǐ quán wén* 宋史全文 [[KR2b0032]] — anonymous Sòng-Yuán transition annalistic record of the entire Sòng dynasty.
- c. 1280–1303 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn qián biān* 資治通鑑前編 [[KR2b0033]] — Jīn Lǚxiáng's *Gāngmù*-form pre-history extending the *Tōng jiàn* back to high antiquity.
- c. 1280–1310 — *Sòng jì sān cháo zhèng yào* 宋季三朝政要 [[KR2b0031]] — anonymous Yuán-period political record of the last three Sòng reigns.
- c. 1340–1370 — *Tōng jiàn xù biān* 通鑑續編 [[KR2b0034]] — Chén Jǐng's late-Yuán Sòng-Yuán universal-history continuation.
- 1365–1373 — *Dà shì jì xù biān* 大事記續編 [[KR2b0035]] — Wáng Yī's early-Míng continuation of Lǚ Zǔqiān.
- 1402–1424 — *Yuán shǐ xù biān* 元史續編 [[KR2b0036]] — Hú Cuìzhōng's *Yǒnglè*-era supplement to the official *Yuán shǐ*.
- c. 1550–1580 — *Zhúshū jìnián* 竹書紀年 [[KR2b0001]] — late-Míng *jīnběn* reconstitution at the Tiānyī gé of Fàn Qīn, the received recension printed in the WYG.
- 1643 — *Gāngmù xù lín* 綱目續麟 [[KR2b0020]] — Zhāng Zìxūn's late-Míng critique of the *Gāngmù*.
- c. 1644–1690 — *Gāngmù fēnzhù shí yí* 綱目分註拾遺 [[KR2b0021]] — Ruì Chángxù's early-Qīng supplement to the *Gāngmù* sub-commentaries.
- 1690–1694 — *Zī zhì tōng jiàn hòu biān* 資治通鑑後編 [[KR2b0040]] — Xú Qiánxué's 184-juan Sòng-Yuán continuation, the leading early-Qīng extension of the *Tōng jiàn*.
- c. 1700–1747 — *Tōngjiàn Hú zhù jǔ zhèng* 通鑑胡注擧正 [[KR2b0013]] — Chén Jǐngyún's *kǎozhèng* correction of Hú Sānxǐng's geographical errors.
- c. 1700–1747 — *Gāngmù dìng wù* 綱目訂誤 [[KR2b0022]] — Chén Jǐngyún's companion correction of errors in the standard *Gāngmù* text.
- c. 1730–1750 — *Zhúshū tǒngjiān* 竹書統箋 [[KR2b0002]] — Xú Wénjìng's full lemma-by-lemma Qīng commentary on the *jīnběn Zhúshū jìnián*.
- 1746–1775 — *Yù dìng zī zhì tōng jiàn gāngmù sān biān* 御定資治通鑑綱目三編 [[KR2b0038]] — Zhāng Tíngyù's imperially-set Míng-history continuation of the *Gāngmù*.
- 1767–1768 — *Yù pī lìdài tōng jiàn jí lǎn* 御批歷代通鑑輯覽 [[KR2b0037]] — Fù Héng's Qiánlóng-era universal chronicle from Fúxī to the end of the Míng, with Qiánlóng's own marginal comments.
- 1773–1786 — *Huáng Qīng kāiguó fānglüè* 皇清開國方略 [[KR2b0039]] — Ā Guì and Liáng Guózhì's officially compiled annalistic record of the Qīng founding, closing the division.
