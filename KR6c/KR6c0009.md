# Dà-pǐn jīng yìshū 大品經義疏
*Doctrinal Commentary on the Great-Section Sūtra (Mahāprajñāpāramitā)*
by [[吉藏]] (Jí-zàng, 撰)

## About the work

A nine-fascicle Suí-period line-by-line commentary by Jí-zàng 吉藏 on Kumārajīva's [[KR6c0004|*Móhē bōrě bōluómì jīng* (T223)]]. The contents-list at the head of fascicle 1 shows the structure: fascicle 1 — *Xuán-yì* 玄意 (esoteric-meaning prologue); fascicle 2 — missing in the transmitted text (the head-note explicitly notes 「但欠此卷」 "only this fascicle is missing"); fascicles 3–9 — line-by-line commentary on the chapters of T223 (序品 / 奉鉢品 / etc.).

### Prefaces

The fascicle 1 *Xuán-yì* serves as the prologue. The catalog header attributes the text to Jí-zàng of the Suí; the catalog's dynasty-attribution "胡" is anomalous (the standard attribution is 隋) and likely reflects a transcription error.

### Abstract

X451 is the principal Sān-lùn line-by-line commentary on the *Mahāprajñāpāramitā* in Chinese, complementing the programmatic [[KR6c0007|*Dà-pǐn jīng yóu-yì* (T1696)]] which gives the doctrinal-philosophical core. Together with [[KR6c0007|T1696]], X451 constitutes Jí-zàng's two-tier commentary on T223 — programmatic and detailed — and is foundational for Sān-lùn / Chinese-Mādhyamika *prajñā* exegesis. The work is dateable to Jí-zàng's documented active period (580–623). The transmitted text is partial: fascicle 2 is missing throughout the manuscript transmission.
