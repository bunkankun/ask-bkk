# Rénwáng jīng héshū 仁王經合疏
*Combined Subcommentary on the Rénwáng-jīng*
spoken by [[智顗]] (說), recorded by [[灌頂]] (記), and combined by [[道霈]] (合)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Qīng-era *combined subcommentary* (*hé-shū* 合疏) on the *Rénwáng-jīng* (KR6c0202 = T245), in which the early-Qīng Cáodòng-school master [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi 為霖道霈 (1615–1702) of Gǔshān 鼓山涌泉寺 has combined Tiāntái Zhìyǐ-and-Guàndǐng's earlier *Rénwáng jīng shū* (T1705 = KR6c0204, Suí period) with subsequent commentarial materials and his own connecting glosses to produce an integrated reading. Preserved in the Wàn xù-zàng / *Manji zoku-zō* as X513. Three fascicles.

The genre marker — *hé-shū* "combined subcommentary" — signals an integrated compilation that brings together earlier commentary materials with the editor's own contributions to produce a unified interpretive framework.

### Abstract

X513 is a primary witness to the early-Qīng Cáodòng-school's continuing engagement with the *Rénwáng-jīng* tradition. Doctrinally Dàopèi works within the standard late-Míng / early-Qīng *jiào-zōng héyī* (doctrine-and-Chan unity) framework, integrating the canonical Tiāntái doctrinal apparatus (from T1705) with Cáodòng-school Chan-style readings and the broader Mahāyāna doctrinal synthesis characteristic of the Yuánxián-Dàopèi Gǔshān lineage.

The work's significance lies in its demonstration that the *Rénwáng-jīng* commentary tradition continued actively through the Míng-Qīng transition, even as the imperial state-Buddhist ritual programme that the sūtra had originally supported was entering decline. Dàopèi's reformulation of the Tiāntái commentary in the early-Qīng Cáodòng-school context represents the broader pattern of late-imperial Chinese Buddhism's reception of Suí-Tang doctrinal heritage.

For the wider history, X513 is significant as: (i) one of the few late-imperial *Rénwáng-jīng* commentaries; (ii) a primary witness to Dàopèi's commentarial output (paralleling his Heart Sūtra commentary X563 = KR6c0182); and (iii) a documentation of the Gǔshān Cáodòng-school's continuing scholastic engagement with state-Buddhist scriptural traditions.

Composition date: no internal dating in the catalog. The work belongs to Dàopèi's mature commentarial career at Gǔshān, c. 1640–1686. The bracket *notBefore* 1640 / *notAfter* 1686 reflects this. The catalog meta retains the Suí dynasty designation (reflecting the underlying Tiāntái source) but the *combined* version is decisively early-Qīng.

## Other points of interest

The *combined subcommentary* (*hé-shū*) genre is one of the more interesting late-imperial commentarial formats, presenting the doctrinal continuity from Suí-Tang through Sòng-Yuán to Míng-Qīng as a single integrated reading. Dàopèi's *Rénwáng-jīng héshū* exemplifies this *long-arc* commentary tradition, which would become an important model for early-twentieth-century Buddhist modernist commentary projects.
