# Dà-zhì-dù lùn shū 大智度論疏
*Commentary on the Mahāprajñāpāramitāśāstra*
by [[慧影抄]] (Huì-yǐng, 撰)

## About the work

A seven-fascicle commentary on Kumārajīva's [[KR6c0005|*Dà-zhì-dù lùn* (T1509)]], compiled by Huì-yǐng 慧影 in late Northern-and-Southern Dynasties / early Suí time. The opening contents-list shows that the surviving text is partial: fascicles 1, 6, 9, 10, 13, and 14 (plus a final fragment) survive; fascicles 2–5, 7–8, 11–12 are lost. The extant juan cover the *Xù-pǐn* (*Mahāprajñāpāramitā* introduction), the thirty-seven *bodhipakṣya-dharmas*, the three *samādhi*s, the four *apramāṇa*s, and selected later sections.

### Prefaces

No author-preface survives (lost with fascicles 2–5); only the contents-list of the seven extant fascicles.

### Abstract

T1509 is the most extensive Mahāyāna *śāstra* in the Chinese canon; X791 is the principal pre-Tang commentary on it. The work is *chāo*-form (excerpted-and-compiled commentary) drawing on earlier Sān-lùn 三論 / Mādhyamika exegetical material; the commentary's doctrinal orientation is broadly compatible with the Six-Dynasties Sān-lùn tradition descending through Sēng-zhào 僧肇, Sēng-rúi 僧叡, and Sēng-quán 僧詮. The fragmentary state of transmission has limited the work's modern scholarly attention, but X791 remains the principal pre-Tang commentary on T1509 and a crucial witness for Six-Dynasties Mādhyamika exegesis.
