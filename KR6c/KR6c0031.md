# Jīngāng bōrě bōluómì jīng lùn 金剛般若波羅蜜經論 (T1510b)
*Vajracchedikā-vyākhyā (Asaṅga's Treatise, Three-Fascicle Recension)*
by [[無著菩薩]] (Asaṅga, 造); translated by [[達磨笈多]] (Dharmagupta, 譯)

## About the work

A three-fascicle alternative recension of Asaṅga's *Vajracchedikā-vyākhyā*, the doublet companion of [[KR6c0030|T1510a]]. The Taishō head-note records the alternative title 「亦名金剛能斷般若」 ("also named the *Vajra-Able-to-Cut Prajñā*"). The opening *praṇāma*-verse is identical to T1510a, indicating the same translation in different textual transmission. Signature: 「無著菩薩造／隋南天竺三藏法師達摩岌多譯」 (with the alternative transliteration 達摩岌多 for the translator's name).

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T1510b is a parallel-recension transmission of Dharmagupta's translation of Asaṅga's *Vajracchedikā-vyākhyā*. The two recensions T1510a and T1510b reflect different states of the same Indic source and the same Chinese translation; the three-fascicle T1510b is somewhat more expanded.
