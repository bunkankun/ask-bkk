# Jīn-gāng jīng zhèng-jiě 金剛經正解
*Correct Explanation of the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[龔穊綵]] (註)

## About the work

A two-juan **mid-Qīng (Yōng-zhèng / Qián-lóng-era) lay-Buddhist *Vajracchedikā* commentary** by the layman **Gōng Jì-cǎi** 龔穊綵 (sobriquet **Shèng-xián jū-shì** 剩閒居士), with editorial collation by the layman **Hù Zhèng-zhì** 扈正智 (sobriquet **Lián-fǎng jū-shì** 蓮舫居士). The catalog credit-line *zhù* 註 ("annotation") and the title-character *zhèng-jiě* ("correct explanation") together identify the genre: a verse-by-verse explanatory commentary aimed at lay readers. Preserved as X25 no. 498. The text bears no internal date; *notBefore* / *notAfter* set conservatively to the **Yōng-zhèng / Qián-lóng era** (1722–1795). Catalog dynasty 清.

### Abstract

The text follows the **Liáng Zhāo-míng 32-section division**, with each section opened by a brief introductory note (*fǎ-huì yīn-yóu fēn dì-yī* etc.) followed by sūtra text in larger character and annotation in smaller. The annotation distinguishes a 【註】 layer (basic gloss) from extended explanation (set off by larger interlinear blocks where necessary). The opening is canonical: *rú-shì zhě, zhǐ cǐ jīng suǒ yán zhī fǎ yě* ("'thus' refers to the dharma expounded in this sūtra"); *wǒ. jí jīng-zhě zì-wèi, jí Ā-nán yě* ("'I' is the sūtra-collector's self-reference, namely Ānanda"). The work is one of the more stable lay-Buddhist *zhèng-jiě* productions of the period, serving as a study-edition for the moderately-trained reader without commitments to a particular doctrinal school.
