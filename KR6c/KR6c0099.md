# Jīn-gāng bān-ruò jīng shū-lùn zuǎn-yào kān-dìng-jì huì-biān 金剛般若經疏論纂要刊定記會編
*Combined Edition of the Diamond Sūtra Commentary, Sub-Commentary, and Sub-Sub-Commentary*
by [[宗密]] (述疏), [[子璿]] (錄記), [[行策]] (會編)

## About the work

A ten-juan **early-Qīng (Kāng-xī 3 = 1664) *huì-biān* (combined-edition) recension** by **Zhù-mèng Xíng-cè** 截流行策 (1628–1682) of the central Huá-yán *Vajracchedikā* commentary apparatus: **Guī-fēng Zōng-mì's** 圭峯宗密 (780–841) Táng-period *Jīn-gāng bān-ruò jīng shū-lùn zuǎn-yào* + **Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán's** 長水子璿 (965–1038) Northern-Sòng *Kān-dìng-jì*. Distinct from the parallel *Xù-zàng-jīng* recension [[KR6c0079]] (X25 no. 491) which incorporates a Míng-period *kē-huì* (outline-synthesis) by **Dà-bīn** 大璸 — the present text, preserved in the Jiā-xīng Canon (J31 no. B269), is Xíng-cè's own larger combined-edition without the Dà-bīn layer, more coordinated for monastic-pedagogical use. The author's preface is dated *Kāng-xī jiǎ-chén* zhōng-xià = mid-summer 1664 (康熙甲辰歲仲夏之吉), composed *yú Wǔ-lín* 於武林 (in Hángzhōu). Cf. [[KR6c0080]] for the smaller Xíng-cè condensation. *notBefore* = **824** (substrate Zōng-mì commentary); *notAfter* = **1664** (Xíng-cè's combined edition). Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

The text presents a strictly hierarchical *kē-wén* outline at the head: *zuǎn-yào shū-wén fēn (sān): chū biāo tí-mù (èr): chū jīng shū míng-tí; èr zuò-zhě jiā-hào* 纂要疏文分(三) / 初標題目(二) / 初經疏名題 / 二作者嘉號 — opening with the title-and-author module before proceeding to the sūtra-commentary body. Xíng-cè's editorial method is the *huì-biān* combined-edition: under each phrase of the Kumārajīva *Vajracchedikā* he supplies first Zōng-mì's *zuǎn-yào* commentary, then Zǐ-xuán's *kān-dìng-jì* sub-commentary, in clearly demarcated layers. The 10-juan length accommodates the substantial Sòng *jì* (sub-commentary) layer in full rather than excerpting it, distinguishing this recension from the more compressed [[KR6c0080]]. The work was preserved in the Jiā-xīng Canon (J31 nB269), a textual-witness independent of the *Xù-zàng-jīng* tradition.

## Translations and research

- For Zōng-mì's *Vajracchedikā* commentary in its Huá-yán hermeneutic context see **Peter N. Gregory**, *Tsung-mi and the Sinification of Buddhism* (Princeton, 1991; rep. Hawaii, 2002).
- For Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán's sub-commentary tradition see surveys of Sòng Buddhism, esp. on the Sì-míng / Cháng-shuǐ axis.
- For Xíng-cè's combined-edition project and his Pure-Land patriarchate see modern surveys of Qīng Pure-Land Buddhism.

## Other points of interest

The Jiā-xīng-canon preservation (J31 nB269) of Xíng-cè's full *huì-biān*, distinct from the *Xù-zàng* multi-stratum [[KR6c0079]] (which adds Dà-bīn's outline), gives modern scholars two independent textual witnesses of the same Zōng-mì + Zǐ-xuán combination — a useful redundancy for textual criticism of the Huá-yán *Vajracchedikā* commentary tradition.
