# Jīn-gāng bān-ruò jīng xiá-zhù 金剛般若經挾註
*Interlinear-Annotation Edition of the Diamond Sūtra*
[anonymous]

## About the work

An **anonymous one-juan interlinear-annotation edition** of the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra* recovered from the **Dūnhuáng cache** and preserved as T85 no. 2739. The catalog title-character *xiá-zhù* 挾註 ("escorting / interlinear annotation") identifies the format: the sūtra-text is presented in main-character size, with annotation in small-character interlinear-block running between sūtra-passages — a layout designed for personal-study reading rather than scholastic disputation. Companion to [[KR6c0109]] and [[KR6c0110]] in the cluster of anonymous Dūnhuáng *Vajracchedikā* commentaries. *notBefore* / *notAfter* set very broadly (**600–900**). Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

The surviving fragment exhibits the *xiá-zhù* layout in action: lemma-and-annotation pairs flow with the sūtra. Sample passage (with manuscript lacunae): *jiàn zhū xiāng fēi xiāng, jí jiàn rú-lái* (見諸相非相，即見如來) — annotation in small-character: *(rú-lái fǎ-kōng)* (如來法空, "the Tathāgata is the dharma's emptiness"); *(lí-xiāng ér jiàn yǐ yě)* (離相而見矣也, "departing-from-marks, then there is seeing"). And: *Sù-pú-tí, bái fó yán shì-zūn …* — annotation: *(fán-fū xīn xìn yīn qǔ-xiāng shēng kǒng wén shì lǐ wú chù jì xīn)* (凡夫心信因取相生恐聞是理無處寄心 — "ordinary-being's mind, signal arising from grasping marks; fearing that hearing this principle there is nowhere for the mind to settle"). The annotation register is brisk and elementary, suiting a personal study-aid rather than a learned commentary. The work is a useful witness to the **practical reception of the *Vajracchedikā* in Dūnhuáng monastic and lay circles** during the late-Táng / Five Dynasties period.
