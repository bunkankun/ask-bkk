# Jīngāng bōrě bōluómì jīng luèshū 金剛般若波羅蜜經略疏
*Brief Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[智儼]] (述), the second **Huáyán** patriarch (602–668)

## About the work

A two-juan **Tang Huá-yán-school commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra***, by **Zhì-yǎn** 智儼 (602–668), the **second patriarch of the Huá-yán** 華嚴 school (the foundational systematizer of Huá-yán doctrine, between **Dù-shùn** 杜順 the first patriarch and **Fǎ-zàng** 法藏 the third patriarch). *notBefore* = **640** (Zhì-yǎn's mature scholarly period); *notAfter* = **668** (Zhì-yǎn's death). Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

Zhì-yǎn 智儼 (DILA A001302; **602–668**) was the **principal early Huá-yán-doctrinal systematizer** — establishing the framework of Huá-yán *yuán-jiào* doctrine that **Fǎ-zàng** 法藏 (643–712) would later bring to its mature systematic form. The present *Vajracchedikā* commentary applies early Huá-yán doctrinal categories to the *prajñā* sūtra.
