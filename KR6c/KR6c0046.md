# Jīngāng jīng zhùshū 金剛經註疏
*Annotated Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[慧淨]] (註), the early-Tang **Huìjìng** 慧淨 (578–?)

## About the work

A three-juan **early-Tang commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra***, by **Huì-jìng** 慧淨 (578–after 645), an early-Tang scholar-monk of **Jì-fǎ-sì** 紀國寺 in Cháng'ān. Preserved in the Wàn-zì xù-zàng-jīng at X24 no. 456. *notBefore* = **580**, *notAfter* = **645**. Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

Huìjìng (DILA A001736; lay surname **Fang** 房; native of **Chángshān** 常山; **578 – after 645**) was an early-Tang scholar-monk known particularly for his role in early-Tang Buddhist debates against Daoist polemicists. He produced commentaries on the *Vajracchedikā*, the *Heart Sūtra*, the *Wúliàngshòu jīng*, and other Mahāyāna canonical texts, plus a now-lost large-scale **Záo Mahāyāna** doctrinal compendium.
