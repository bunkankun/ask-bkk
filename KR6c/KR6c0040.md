# Jīngāng jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào (xuǎnlù) 金剛經疏論纂要（選錄「釋金剛經纂要疏科」）
*Selected Headings from the Compendious-Essentials Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra* (extract: "Explication of the Headings of the Diamond Sūtra Compendious-Essentials Commentary")
extracted by [[子璿]] (錄), the Northern-Sòng **Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán** 長水子璿 (965–1038)

## About the work

A single-juan extract of the **outline-headings** (*kē* 科) section from **Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán's** 長水子璿 *Jīn-gāng jīng shū-lùn zuǎn-yào* 金剛經疏論纂要 — the Northern-Sòng **Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán**'s editorial reorganization of **Zōng-mì's** 宗密 (780–841) *Diamond Sūtra* commentary [[KR6c0041]]. The byline reads **長水沙門子璿** ("*Cháng-shuǐ* śramaṇa Zǐ-xuán"). Preserved at P187 no. 1624.

### Abstract

Authorship: by **Zǐxuán** 子璿 (DILA A000085; alternate persona-name **Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán** 長水子璿; **965–1038 CE**), the Northern-Sòng **Huá-yán-school** scholar based at **Chángshuǐyuàn** 長水院. Zǐxuán was the **principal Sòng-period editorial-redactor of Zōngmì's commentarial corpus**, producing the standard Sòng recensions of Zōngmì's *Yuánjué jīng* 圓覺經 (T1795 / T1796), *Huáyán* commentary (with sub-commentary), and the present *Diamond Sūtra* commentary structuring.

*notBefore* = **990** (Zǐxuán's mature scholarly period); *notAfter* = **1038** (Zǐxuán's death). Catalog dynasty 宋.

The work is a structural / outline-extract of the [[KR6c0041]] *Jīngāng jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào* full commentary — providing the **kē** 科 (heading-outline / topic-tree) framework that allows readers to navigate the substantive commentary. As such, it is more a **reading-aid index** than an independent doctrinal work, but is preserved separately in the canonical tradition for its practical-pedagogical utility.
