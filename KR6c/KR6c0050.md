# Jīn-gāng jīng huì-jiě 金剛經會解
*A Combined Explanation of the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[善月]] (述), = **Shàn-yuè** 善月 (1149–1241)

## About the work

A two-juan **Sòng-Tiāntái-school synthesizing commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra***, by **Shàn-yuè** 善月 (1149–1241, *zì* **Guāng-yuǎn** 光遠, sobriquet **Bǎi-sōng** 柏松), a leading late-Sòng Tiāntái master at the **Yán-qìng-sì** 演慶寺 in **Sì-míng** 四明 (modern Níng-bō). The work is a "**combined explanation**" (*huì-jiě* 會解) — synthesizing previous Tiāntái-school *Vajracchedikā* commentary (cf. [[KR6c0037]] of Zhì-yǐ) with Sòng-period developments. Preserved at X24 no. 462. *notBefore* = **1196** (Shàn-yuè's mature scholarly period); *notAfter* = **1241** (Shàn-yuè's death). Catalog dynasty 宋.

### Abstract

**Shàn-yuè** 善月 (DILA A001616) was a senior Sòng Tiāntái master active in the **Tiāntái-school's late-Sòng *Sì-míng-pài* 四明派** — the dominant Sòng-period Tiāntái lineage descending from **Sì-míng Zhī-lǐ** 四明知禮 (960–1028).
