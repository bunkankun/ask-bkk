# Dà-pǐn jīng yóu-yì 大品經遊意
*Wandering-Meaning of the Great-Section Sūtra (Programmatic Exposition of the Mahāprajñāpāramitā)*
by [[吉藏]] (Jí-zàng, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Suí-period programmatic commentary by Jí-zàng 吉藏 (549–623) on Kumārajīva's [[KR6c0004|*Móhē bōrě bōluómì jīng* (T223)]] — the *Pañcaviṃśatisāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā*. The *yóu-yì* 遊意 ("wandering-meaning" / programmatic prefatorial exposition) genre is one of Jí-zàng's signature commentarial forms, presenting the *prajñāpāramitā*-doctrine in its broadest doctrinal sweep before the line-by-line exegesis of the longer commentary.

### Prefaces

The text opens with Jí-zàng's own programmatic prologue (effectively the whole work is preface): "This Way is profound and subtle, deep and far, hard to fathom; without knowing, without illuminating, without name, without form. Principle cuts off the hundred negations; the Way perishes in the four propositions. Speech has nowhere to lay its discourse, perception has nowhere to sink its mentality. Yet though one-mark is no-mark, the ten-thousand uses are not lacking; though utmost-still and utmost-empty, the Way illuminates the *dharma-dhātu*…" — a doctrinal *prajñā*-elegy in the Sān-lùn idiom.

### Abstract

T1696 is one of three Jí-zàng commentaries on T223 included in the canon ([[KR6c0007|T1696]], [[KR6c0009|X451]], and [[KR6c0008|T1697]] — the last by Wǒnhyo, not Jí-zàng — see entries). The single-fascicle *yóu-yì* form gives the doctrinal-philosophical core of Jí-zàng's reading of the *Prajñāpāramitā*; the longer [[KR6c0009|*Dà-pǐn jīng yìshū* (X451)]] gives the line-by-line commentary. The work is dateable to Jí-zàng's documented active commentarial period, c. 580–623 CE. T1696 is one of the principal sources for the formation of Sān-lùn / Mādhyamika *prajñā*-doctrine as a Chinese philosophical system.
