# Jīngāng jīng kǒujué 金剛經口訣
*Oral Decisions on the Diamond Sūtra*
attributed to [[慧能]] (說, attributed), the **Sixth Chán Patriarch Huìnéng** 慧能 (638–713)

## About the work

A single-juan brief **oral-instruction collection** on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra*, attributed to **Huì-néng** 慧能 (638–713), the Sixth Patriarch of Chinese Chán. Like [[KR6c0047]] (the same author's *Jiě-yì*), the Huì-néng attribution is **modern-scholarly disputed** — the text is plausibly a Sòng-period Chán-school composition. Preserved at X24 no. 460. *notBefore* = **700**, *notAfter* = **900**. Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

The "**oral decision**" (*kǒujué* 口訣) format reflects the Chán-school's emphasis on direct master-to-disciple oral transmission — making the work, whether or not authentically Huìnéng's, a documentary witness to Chán-school *Vajracchedikā* commentarial practice in the oral-instruction format.
