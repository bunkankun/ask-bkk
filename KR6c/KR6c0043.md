# Jīngāng bōrě bōluómì jīng zhùjiě 金剛般若波羅蜜經註解
*Annotated Explanation of the Diamond Sūtra*
co-annotated by [[宗泐]] (Zōnglè, 註) and [[如玘]] (Rúqǐ, 註)

## About the work

A single-juan early-Míng Buddhist co-annotated commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra*, by Zōnglè 宗泐 (1318–1391) and Rúqǐ 如玘 (the second character is a rare variant of *qǐ*, sometimes given as 如𥥆). Both were principal early-Míng monastic figures associated with the Hóngwǔ 洪武 (Tàizǔ Zhū Yuánzhāng's) imperial Buddhist establishment. Preserved at T33 no. 1703. *notBefore* = 1368 (the Míng founding); *notAfter* = 1391 (Zōnglè's death).

### Abstract

Authorship: by Zōnglè 宗泐 (1318–1391) and Rúqǐ 如玘 (lifedates not preserved). Zōnglè was a principal early-Míng court monk under Tàizǔ — leading the imperial-court Buddhist scholarly establishment in Nánjīng. The work is one of his canonical-commentary outputs for the early-Míng imperial Buddhist program.
