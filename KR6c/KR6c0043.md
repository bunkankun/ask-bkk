# Jīngāng bōrě bōluómì jīng zhùjiě 金剛般若波羅蜜經註解
*Annotated Explanation of the Diamond Sūtra*
co-annotated by [[宗泐]] (註) and [[如𥥆]] (註)

## About the work

A single-juan early-Míng Buddhist **co-annotated commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra***, by **Zōng-lè** 宗泐 (1318–1391) and **Rú-jué** 如𥥆 (the second character is a rare variant of *jué*, sometimes given as 如𥥆 / 如玘). Both were principal early-Míng monastic figures associated with the **Hóng-wǔ** 洪武 (Tài-zǔ Zhū Yuán-zhāng's) imperial Buddhist establishment. Preserved at T33 no. 1703. *notBefore* = **1368** (the Míng founding); *notAfter* = **1391** (Zōng-lè's death). Catalog dynasty 明.

### Abstract

Authorship: by **Zōnglè** 宗泐 (1318–1391) and **Rújué** 如𥥆 (lifedates not preserved). Zōnglè was a principal early-Míng court monk under Tàizǔ — leading the imperial-court Buddhist scholarly establishment in Nánjīng. The work is one of his canonical-commentary outputs for the early-Míng imperial Buddhist program.
