# Fómǔ bōrě bōluómìduō yuánjí yàoyì shìlùn 佛母般若波羅蜜多圓集要義釋論
*Triratnadāsa's Commentary on Dignāga's Prajñāpāramitā-piṇḍārtha-saṃgraha*
by [[三寶尊菩薩]] (Triratnadāsa, 造) on the root-treatise of [[大域龍菩薩]] (Dignāga, 造本論); translated by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 等譯)

## About the work

A four-fascicle commentary by Triratnadāsa 三寶尊菩薩 on the verse-summary *Prajñāpāramitā-piṇḍārtha-saṃgraha* of Dignāga 大域龍菩薩 (the root-treatise of which is preserved separately as [[KR6c0018|T1518]]). Translated by Dānapāla 施護 in the Northern-Sòng *Yì-jīng-yuàn*. Signature: 「三寶尊菩薩造／大域龍菩薩造本論／西天譯經三藏…賜紫沙門臣施護等奉詔譯」. The Taishō header cross-references T1518 — the root-treatise — with which T1517 forms a verse-and-commentary pair.

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T1517 / T1518 together represent the Indic Yogācāra-school verse-summary and exegesis of the *Prajñāpāramitā* tradition: Dignāga's verse-compendium of the essential topics of the *Aṣṭasāhasrikā*, with Triratnadāsa's prose-commentary. The translation falls within Dānapāla's documented Northern-Sòng activity (980–1018). The works are doctrinally important as showing the integration of *Prajñāpāramitā* doctrine within the Indic Yogācāra epistemological tradition.
