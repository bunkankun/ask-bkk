# Jīn-gāng jīng shū 金剛經疏 (T85 no. 2737)
*Diamond Sūtra Commentary*
[anonymous]

## About the work

An **anonymous one-juan *Vajracchedikā* commentary** recovered from the **Dūnhuáng cache** and preserved as T85 no. 2737. Authorship is not preserved either in the manuscript itself or in catalog tradition; the catalog meta accordingly lists no `persons` field. The text breaks off mid-passage at the surviving fragment's boundaries, suggesting it is a partial witness of a longer original work. *notBefore* / *notAfter* set very broadly (**600–900**) since neither author nor date is established; Sui-Táng provenance is generally assumed for Dūnhuáng *Vajracchedikā* commentary manuscripts. Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

The surviving fragment opens in the middle of the commentary on the **second-fruit (Skt. *sakṛdāgāmin*)** passage of the sūtra: *Sù-pú-tí, yú yì yún hé, sī-tuó-hán néng zuò shì niàn, wǒ dé sī-tuó-hán guǒ bù?* (Section 9 of the Liáng Zhāo-míng division). The commentator parses the sūtra as a sequence of *Sù-pú-tí*'s questions and the Buddha's responses, with each *fruit* (*xū-tuó-huán*, *sī-tuó-hán*, *ā-nuó-hán*, *ā-luó-hàn*) treated separately. The annotation is structural-doctrinal in the standard mid-Táng manner, using the *Sì-fēn lǜ* schema for the four fruits and indexing each to the corresponding *fán-nǎo* (afflictions) cut by it (*sī-tuó-hán* = *bó tān-chēn* 薄貪嗔, the diminishing of greed-anger, etc.). The work is one of three undated and anonymous Dūnhuáng *Jīn-gāng jīng shū* in T85 (cf. [[KR6c0110]] T85 no. 2738; [[KR6c0111]] T85 no. 2739, the latter an *interlinear annotation* edition).
