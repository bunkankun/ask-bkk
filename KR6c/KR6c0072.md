# Jīn-gāng jīng dà-yì 金剛經大意
*Main Idea of the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[王起隆]] (述)

## About the work

A one-juan **early-Qīng (Shùn-zhì-era) *Vajracchedikā* essay** in epistolary form by the **Xiù-shuǐ** 秀水 layman **Wáng Qǐ-lóng** 王起隆 (sobriquet **Zhǐ-ān** 止菴, 載生居士). The text takes the form of a reply-letter — *dá Tú Xī-ān dú Jīn-gāng-jīng dà-yì shū* 答屠息庵讀金剛經大意書 ("Reply to Tú Xī-ān concerning Reading the Main Idea of the Diamond Sūtra") — addressed to a fellow layman, **Tú Xī-ān** 屠息庵 (cf. Tú Yín 屠垠 of [[KR6c0058]]; whether identical is uncertain), who had circulated his own *Vajracchedikā* commentary draft. Composed in **Shùn-zhì 12 = 1655** per DILA. Preserved as X25 no. 484. *notBefore* / *notAfter* = **1655**. Catalog dynasty 清.

### Abstract

The opening of the letter offers an unusually substantive **doxographic survey** of *Vajracchedikā* commentary: the sūtra has 5,146 characters in Kumārajīva's translation, divided variously by **Liáng Zhāo-míng** (32 sections), **Bodhiruci** (12 sections), **Asaṅga** (18 abodes), **Vasubandhu** (27 doubts), **Maitreya** (80 verses), with additional treatises by **Guṇadā Funct.** 功德施菩薩 and verse-summaries by **Fù Dà-shì** 傅大士. Wáng catalogues the major Chinese commentaries — Tiāntái Zhì-yǐ's [[KR6c0037]] *shū*, Guī-fēng Zōng-mì's *zuǎn*, Yáng Guī's *jí-jiě* (the substrate of [[KR6c0056]]), Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán's *Kān-dìng-jì*, **Zhōng-fēng Míng-běn's** *Lüè-yì*, **Zōng-lè's** annotation, and "the various commentaries of the present age, also accumulated to fill a roof-beam" (亦既充棟). The famous *Cì-shuǐ xuán-zhū* 赤水玄珠 image (from *Zhuāng-zǐ*) is invoked to underscore the difficulty of locating the genuine pearl among so many candidates: *shéi dìng wéi xiàng-wǎng hū* 誰定為象罔乎 ("who can be the *Image-Negation* who alone retrieves it?").

The substantive doctrinal exposition then divides the sūtra into a **dà-tóu-lú** (大頭顱, "great head") and **dà-zhī-gàn** (大支幹, "great limbs"). The "great head" is identified as the *zhēn-rú-xīn* 真如心 (Tathatā-mind), shared by Buddha and sentient beings, *bù biàn suí yuán* / *suí yuán bù biàn* (unchanging-yet-following-conditions / following-conditions-yet-unchanging) — the standard Sòng-Míng *xìng-zōng* doctrinal formula descending through Yán-shòu's *Zōng-jìng-lù*. The "great limbs" are the conditionally-arisen phenomena, illustrated by the sūtra's six similes (*dream-illusion-bubble-shadow-dew-lightning*) and assigned to the *shēng-miè mén* (生滅門) of the *Awakening of Faith*'s *one-mind-two-gates* schema. The letter is concise, scholarly in tone, and sketched-but-confident — a model of mid-seventeenth-century lay-Buddhist literary exegesis.

## Other points of interest

Wáng's invocation of the *yī-xīn èr-mén* 一心二門 (one-mind, two-gates) frame from the *Dà-shèng qǐ-xìn lùn* 大乘起信論 ("Awakening of Mahāyāna Faith") shows the deep Sòng-Míng *xìng-zōng* (nature-school) habit of reading *all* Mahāyāna sūtras through the *Qǐ-xìn* schema — a trans-period continuity that is particularly pronounced in the lay-Buddhist literary commentary line from Sòng (Wáng Rì-xiū) through late-Míng (Hán Yán, Chéng Zhōng-mào of [[KR6c0057]]; Lín Zhào-ēn of [[KR6c0064]]) to early-Qīng (Wáng Qǐ-lóng here). Wáng's other works are the *Huáng-Míng Jīn-gāng xīn-yì lù* 皇明金剛新異錄 (a *Vajracchedikā* miracle-tale anthology) and the *Bān-ruò xīn-jīng dà-yì* (X26 — composed 1679), placing him in the orbit of literati Buddhist anthological-historical writing characteristic of the dynastic transition decades.
