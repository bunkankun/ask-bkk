# Jīngāng jīng zuǎnyào kāndìng jì 金剛經纂要刊定記
*The Established-Cutting Record of the Compendious-Essentials Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[子璿]] (錄)

## About the work

A seven-juan **Northern Sòng sub-commentary** by **Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán** 長水子璿 (965–1038) on **Zōngmì's** 宗密 (780–841) **Jīngāng jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào** 金剛般若經疏論纂要 ([[KR6c0041]]). The work provides extensive sub-commentarial elucidation of Zōngmì's Tang-period *Vajracchedikā* commentary, with particular attention to the textual-critical "**establishment-and-cutting**" (*kāndìng* 刊定) of variant readings and editorial decisions. Preserved at T33 no. 1702.

### Abstract

Authorship: by **Zǐxuán** 子璿 (965–1038), the principal Sòng-period editorial-redactor of Zōngmì's commentarial corpus. *notBefore* = **990**, *notAfter* = **1038**. Catalog dynasty 宋.

The work is the **substantive sub-commentary** companion to Zǐxuán's structural-extract [[KR6c0040]] and his editorial-redaction work on Zōngmì's *Vajracchedikā* commentary [[KR6c0041]]. Together the three works constitute Zǐxuán's complete editorial-and-commentarial intervention in the Zōngmì *Vajracchedikā* tradition.
