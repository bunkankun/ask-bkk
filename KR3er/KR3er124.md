# Yī-xué yào-zé 醫學要則
*The Principles of Medical Learning*
by [[沈懋言]] Shěn Mào-yán (Qīng-era physician, dates uncertain).

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* mid-Qīng pedagogical primer in 24 *zé* 則 ("principles"), composed in 1743 and first printed in *Qián-lóng* 59 / 1794. The work covers: (i) *Nèi-jīng* doctrinal principles; (ii) materia medica properties (*běn-cǎo yào-xìng* 本草藥性); (iii) pulse-diagnosis and channel-doctrine (*mài-jīng jīng-luò* 脈經經絡); (iv) treatment of miscellaneous disorders (*zá-bìng zhì-fǎ* 雜病治法). Each *zé* anthologizes selected citations from the *Nèi-jīng* and post-Hàn medical authors with the compiler's interlinear annotations.

The work is a typical mid-Qīng didactic primer aimed at students entering medical practice. Later bibliographers have judged the arrangement uneven and the doctrinal emphasis diffuse — a characteristic limitation of the *yào-zé* / *yào-yán* primer genre, which tended toward eclectic anthology rather than systematic exposition.

The author Shěn Mào-yán 沈懋言 may be related to (but is distinct from) the Qián-lóng-era Shěn Mào-fā 沈懋發 ([[KR3er093]] *Kuài-cán piān*, c. 1777) — the two names share the *Mào* 懋 generation-character but the relationship is not securely established.

### Abstract

The 1743 composition / 1794 print dating is from the Douban-recorded *Qián-lóng* 59 imprint (*Qīng Qián-lóng wǔ-shí-jiǔ nián Yī-xué yào-zé*). The composition window of 1743–1794 is adopted here. Shěn Mào-yán is poorly documented; not in CBDB.
