# Yī-jīng huì-jiě 醫經會解
*An Integrative Explication of the Medical Classics*
by [[鄧景儀]] Dèng Jǐng-yí (*zì* Yún-lǚ 雲侶, late-Míng physician of Xīn-chéng 新城, Lí-chuān 黎川, Jiāng-xī).

## About the work

A short late-Míng exposition of the *Huáng-dì nèi-jīng* by Dèng Jǐng-yí of the Xū-jiāng 旴江 medical lineage (Jiāng-xī). The work transmits the oral teachings of **Jiāng Méi** 江梅 on the *Nèi-jīng* and was first printed in *Chóng-zhēn* 6 / 1633; an alternative title *Yī-jīng yì-yǔ* 醫經臆語 ("Personal Reflections on the Medical Classics") is attested in some catalogs. The work is one of the principal extant Xū-jiāng-school *Nèi-jīng* exegetical texts and is distinctive for preserving a Jiāng-xī regional reading of the *Sù-wèn* / *Líng-shū* in a period otherwise dominated by Zhū Dān-xī's followers and the later Lǐ Zhōng-zǐ Sōng-jiāng school.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_001.txt` (no `_000.txt`) is header-only — only the title-bar is preserved in the surviving digital transcription. The 1633 dating and the connection to Jiāng Méi must be reconstructed from external bibliography.

### Abstract

Dèng Jǐng-yí's dates are not securely fixed; the 1633 *editio princeps* date is the only firm anchor. The Xū-jiāng 旴江 medical lineage to which Dèng belonged was the dominant Jiāng-xī regional school of the late Míng, distinct from both the Jiāng-nán Dān-xī tradition and the later Sōng-jiāng / Sū-zhōu schools. Not in CBDB.

The work was preserved through Japanese imprint and repatriated via the hxwd series.
