# Dān-tái yù àn 丹臺玉案
*The Jade-Tablet Cases of the Cinnabar Terrace*
by [[孫文胤]] Sūn Wén-yìn (*zì* Duì-wēi 對薇, *hào* Zài-xiá 在峽 / Zài-péng 在蓬, late-Míng).

## About the work

A six-*juǎn* late-Míng clinical compendium covering internal medicine, gynaecology, pediatrics, ophthalmology, and external medicine. The title metaphor *dān-tái yù-àn* — "the jade-tablet cases of the cinnabar terrace" — frames the work as a Daoist-resonant clinical reference (the *dān-tái* is the alchemical altar of the inner-cultivation Daoist tradition; *yù-àn* are the immortal's record-tablets). The work is methodologically distinctive in its self-conscious synthesis of the Sòng-Jīn-Yuán four-master tradition with the Lǐng-shū / Sù-wèn canonical literature, the *Huáng-tíng nèi-jǐng* 黃庭內景 Daoist physiological tradition, and the *Mài-jīng* 脈經 of Wáng Shū-hé. Sūn Wén-yìn's clinical orientation reflects the late-Míng warming-tonifying ascendancy: characteristic formulae include the *Bǔ-zhōng yì-qì tāng* and the *Shēng-mài sǎn*, and the work consistently emphasises *yuán-qì* 元氣 (primordial-qì) preservation as the therapeutic foundation.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` opens with a preface by an official who befriended Sūn Wén-yìn during a stationing at Dāng-hú 當湖 in *yǐ-chǒu* 乙丑 winter (= winter 1625). The preface narrates that Sūn was a Xīn-ān 新安 (Hī-zhōu) native who had moved to Běi-gù 北固 (Zhèn-jiāng) and become the most-sought-after physician of the lower-Yáng-zǐ region; the prefacer met him at Dāng-hú and "from his conversation, every word was striking; from his clinical tests, every test was effective" (與語語輒奇，試技技輒效). The prefacer further reports that Sūn's training came from his careful study of the *Líng-shū*, *Huáng-tíng nèi-jǐng*, *Mài-jīng* of Wáng Shū-hé, and the four Jīn–Yuán masters; he urges Sūn to write down his clinical doctrine, against the objection of the dictum *"yī, yán yì yě"* 醫，言意也 (medicine is intention, not amenable to verbal transmission). The work was therefore composed in response to this prefatory urging and brought to print in the late *Wàn-lì* / *Chóng-zhēn* era.

### Abstract

Sūn Wén-yìn (Duì-wēi) was a Xīn-ān-born late-Míng physician active in the Zhèn-jiāng 鎮江 region; his clinical practice spanned the lower Yangtze, with frequent consultative travel to Sūzhōu and Hángzhōu. The composition of *Dān-tái yù-àn* is conventionally bracketed at *Tiān-qǐ* / early *Chóng-zhēn* era (c. 1625–1636); the *yǐ-chǒu* (1625) preface date provides the secure *terminus post quem* and the standard Chinese-medicine reference works place the work's composition in this Wàn-lì-Chóng-zhēn transitional window. The work was repeatedly reprinted through the Qīng and was particularly influential in the Hangzhou-Suzhou medical milieu. Preserved in the *Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī gǔ-jí cóng-shū* through Japanese collections.
