# Gǔ-jīn yī jiàn (chū-kān běn) 古今醫鑑（初刊本）
*The Medical Mirror, Ancient and Modern (First-Print Edition)*
by [[龔信]] Gōng Xìn (Xī-yuán 西園), edited by his son [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíng-xián (Yún-lín 雲林).

## About the work

The **eight-*juǎn* first-printed recension** of the *Gǔ-jīn yī-jiàn* — a more compact early-print form of the work that is otherwise transmitted in the sixteen-*juǎn* expanded recension preserved as [[KR3er037]]. The two recensions are textually distinct: the *chū-kān běn* preserves the original *Wàn-lì* 4 (1576) version completed by Gōng Xìn before his son Gōng Tíng-xián undertook the further editorial expansion that produced the sixteen-*juǎn* *Wàn-lì* 17 (1589) edition. The eight-*juǎn* recension is therefore of considerable textual-critical value as a witness to the earlier compositional state of the work, before the son's substantive expansion. The two recensions share the same three-fold *mài-zhèng-fāng* organisational template but the eight-*juǎn* recension omits material added in the sixteen-*juǎn* expansion, particularly in the gynaecology and pediatrics sections.

### Abstract

The hxwd transmission preserves both the *chū-kān běn* (eight *juǎn*) and the expanded recension ([[KR3er037]], sixteen *juǎn*) as separate entries, an unusual and helpful editorial decision that allows the two textual states to be compared. The *chū-kān běn* is preserved in Japanese collections (specifically through the Japanese Edo-period reprints) and is preserved nowhere in the standard Chinese medical-textual tradition; its return through the *Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī gǔ-jí cóng-shū* repatriation series is therefore a substantive textual-critical contribution. The *notBefore* / *notAfter* fields reflect the composition window of the parent work, between Gōng Xìn's 1576 completion of the original and the 1589 publication of the expanded recension; the *chū-kān běn* itself was probably printed in the early 1580s on the basis of Gōng Xìn's posthumous manuscript.
