# Líng shā dà dān mì jué 靈砂大丹祕訣

*Secret Instructions on the Great Elixir of Numinous Cinnabar*

Anonymous; Southern Sòng

A Southern-Sòng **alchemical oral-instruction text** on the **great elixir of numinous cinnabar** (*líng shā dà dān* 靈砂大丹). Preserved as **DZ 897** / CT 897 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
