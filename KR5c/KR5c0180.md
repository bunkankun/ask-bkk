# Yōng chéng jí xiān lù 墉城集仙錄

*Collected Records of Immortals of the Walled City*

by [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933)

A major **late-Táng / Five-Dynasties hagiographic compilation** of **female Daoist immortals** by [[杜光庭]] Dù Guāngtíng. Originally in 10 *juàn*; preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 783** / CT 783 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類) in 7 *juàn* (partially incomplete).

## About the work

### Distinctive character

The *Yōng chéng jí xiān lù* is one of the most important pre-modern Chinese **hagiographies of women Daoist immortals** — a relatively rare genre in the Chinese hagiographic corpus. The title *Yōng chéng* 墉城 ("Walled City") refers to the paradise-palace of **Xī Wáng Mǔ** 西王母 (Queen Mother of the West) in Daoist mythology — thus the "Walled City of Immortals" is where female immortals gather.

### Contents

The compilation gathers biographies / hagiographies of:

1. **Xī Wáng Mǔ** 西王母 — the Queen Mother of the West, supreme female Daoist deity.
2. Her celestial **maidens-in-waiting** and attendants.
3. **Historical female Daoists** from the classical past through the late Táng.
4. **Major female immortals** of the Daoist hagiographic tradition.

### Author

[[杜光庭]] Dù Guāngtíng (850–933) — see [[KR5c0114]] for his major *Dàodé jīng* commentary and [[杜光庭]] for his biography. The *Yōng chéng jí xiān lù* is one of his most important hagiographic works, alongside ***Xiān zhuàn shí yí*** 仙傳拾遺 (a general hagiographic supplement).

### Abstract

A major source for the study of **women in classical Chinese religion** and one of the foundational documents of the Chinese female-immortal hagiographic tradition.

**Dating.** Within Dù Guāngtíng's mature period (900–933). Dynasty: 唐-五代.
