# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng jīng zhù 太上老君說常清靜經註 (Dù Guāngtíng)

*Commentary on the Cháng qīng jìng jīng* — Dù Guāngtíng's commentary

by [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng; 850–933) — the monumental late-Táng / Five-Dynasties Daoist master

The **earliest surviving commentary** on the **Cháng qīng jìng jīng** 常清靜經 ([[DZ 620]]) — by the great Táng-Wǔdài Daoist scholar **Dù Guāngtíng** 杜光庭 (see [[杜光庭]]). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 759** / CT 759 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類).

## About the work

### Dù Guāngtíng's broader output

This short commentary is one of Dù Guāngtíng's many scriptural commentaries — alongside his monumental [[KR5c0114|*Dàodé zhēn jīng guǎng shèng yì*]] (DZ 725, 50 *juàn*, 901), various hagiographic works, and ritual manuals. The *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* commentary is short (1 *juàn*) but authoritative.

### Importance as "first commentary"

Dù Guāngtíng's commentary is the **first known commentary on the *Cháng qīng jìng jīng*** — the scripture itself being an early-medieval Daoist meditation-text that Dù Guāngtíng's work helped canonise as a major object of scholarly engagement. Subsequent Sòng-Yuán commentators (Lǐ Dàochún [[KR5c0151]], Wáng Yuánhuī [[KR5c0153]], Hóu Shànyuān [[KR5c0154]], and the anonymous [[KR5c0152]]) build on Dù Guāngtíng's foundational reading.

### Philosophical character

Dù Guāngtíng reads the scripture through the mature late-Táng *Chóngxuán* 重玄 interpretive framework — the same framework that governs his *Dàodé jīng guǎng shèng yì*.

### Abstract

The foundational commentary on the *Cháng qīng jìng jīng*, establishing the interpretive framework within which all subsequent commentators worked.

**Dating.** Within Dù Guāngtíng's mature period, c. 900–933. Frontmatter gives **900–933** as composition window. Dynasty: 唐-五代.
