# Dà Míng Xuán tiān shàng dì ruì yìng tú lù 大明玄天上帝瑞應圖錄

*Illustrated Record of the Auspicious Responses of the Dark-Heaven Supreme Thearch of the Great Míng*

Anonymous; Míng (Yǒng lè reign)

A Míng **illustrated propaganda-record** documenting the **auspicious responses** (*ruì yìng* 瑞應) of **Xuán tiān shàng dì** 玄天上帝 (Zhēn wǔ 真武) to the new Míng dynasty — specifically legitimizing the Yǒng lè emperor's (Míng Chéng zǔ) violent usurpation as backed by Zhēn wǔ's patronage. The Yǒng lè court massively patronised the Zhēn wǔ cult and Wǔ dāng shān, erecting a vast new Daoist complex there. Preserved as **DZ 959** / CT 959 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Yǒng lè reign (1403–1424). Dynasty: 明.
