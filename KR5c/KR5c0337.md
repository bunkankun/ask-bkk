# Dà huán dān jīn hǔ bái lóng lùn 大還丹金虎白龍論

*Discourse on the Gold-Tiger and White-Dragon of the Great Return-Elixir*

by [[還陽子]] (Huán yáng zǐ)

A late-Táng / Five-Dynasties **alchemical discourse** on the **gold-tiger** (*jīn hǔ* 金虎 = lead, *yīn*) and **white-dragon** (*bái lóng* 白龍 = mercury, *yáng*) pair in the cycling of the great return-elixir. Preserved as **DZ 937** / CT 937 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 9th–10th century. Dynasty: 唐-五代.
