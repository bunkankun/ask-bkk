# Tài shàng wèi líng shén huà jiǔ zhuǎn dān shā fǎ 太上衛靈神化九轉丹砂法

*Most High Dharma of the Numinous-Guard Divine-Transformation Nine-Cycle Cinnabar*

Anonymous; probably Táng (7th–8th cent.)

A **Táng alchemical text** on a **nine-cycle cinnabar elixir** (*jiǔ zhuǎn dān shā* 九轉丹砂) invoking the *wèi líng* 衛靈 ("numinous guardians") and divine transformation (*shén huà* 神化) of the substance. Preserved as **DZ 892** / CT 892 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Probably 7th–8th century. Dynasty: 唐.
