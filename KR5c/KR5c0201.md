# Dòng shén sān huáng qī shí èr jūn zhāi fāng chàn yí 洞神三皇七十二君齋方懺儀

*Confession-Repentance Liturgy of the Seventy-Two Lords of the Three Sovereigns in the Cavern-Spirit*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng *Dòng shén* 洞神 ritual-liturgy of **confession and repentance** (*chàn* 懺) addressed to the **seventy-two lords** (*qī shí èr jūn* 七十二君) of the Three Sovereigns revelation. Preserved as **DZ 804** / CT 804 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
