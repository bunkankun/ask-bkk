# Dà dòng liàn zhēn bǎo jīng jiǔ huán jīn dān miào jué 大洞鍊真寶經九還金丹妙訣

*Wondrous Instructions on the Nine-Reversion Gold-Elixir according to the Great-Cavern Precious Scripture of Refining the Perfected*

by [[陳少衛]] (Chén Shàowèi, *fl.* 712–734) — mid-Táng Daoist alchemist

Companion-text to [[KR5c0288]]: Chén Shàowèi's instructions on the **nine-reversion gold-elixir** (*jiǔ huán jīn dān* 九還金丹). Composed in the Kāi yuán reign (712–734). Preserved as **DZ 891** / CT 891 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 712–734. Dynasty: 唐.
