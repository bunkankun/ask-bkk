# Zhì chuān zhēn rén jiào zhèng shù 稚川真人校證術

*Perfected Zhìchuān's Collated-and-Verified Techniques*

attributed to [[葛洪]] (Gě Hóng, *zì* Zhì chuān 稚川)

A Sòng **alchemical-technical text** attributed to the Eastern-Jìn alchemical patriarch Gě Hóng (Bào pǔ zǐ), whose courtesy name was Zhì chuān 稚川. The attribution is pseudepigraphic — the received text appears to be a Sòng compilation circulating under Gě's name. Preserved as **DZ 902** / CT 902 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
