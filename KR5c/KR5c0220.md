# Zhuāng Zhōu qì jué jiě 莊周氣訣解

*Commentary on Zhuāng Zhōu's Pneumatic Instructions*

Anonymous (attributed, falsely, to **Zhuāng Zhōu** [[莊周]]); Táng-era composition

A short **pseudepigraphic** *qì* 氣 cultivation manual attributed to Zhuāng Zhōu but actually a Táng-era composition — reflecting the Táng practice of attributing Daoist cultivation texts to revered classical figures. Preserved as **DZ 823** / CT 823.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
