# Tài shàng shuō xuán tiān dà shèng Zhēn wǔ běn zhuàn shén zhòu miào jīng (alternate recension) 太上說玄天大聖真武本傳神咒妙經

*Sacred Scripture Spoken by the Most High on the Origin and Spells of the Great Sage Zhēn wǔ of the Mysterious Heaven* — alternate 1-juàn recension

A shorter alternate recension of the [[KR5c0150|Zhēn wǔ scripture]], preserved in the Daozang *Pǔ lù* 譜錄 section (DZ 775) in 1 *juàn*. The header notes "三經同卷" — three scriptures in a single juàn — so this may combine multiple related short Zhēn-wǔ-cycle texts in one fascicle.

## About the work

The scripture cluster preserved here (3 short scriptures in one *juàn*) reflects the **Zhēn wǔ** 真武 cult's proliferation of associated scriptures in the Sòng-Yuán period — brief devotional-ritual texts that circulated alongside the main [[KR5c0150|DZ 754 *Běn zhuàn shén zhòu miào jīng*]]. See [[KR5c0150]] for broader treatment of the Zhēn wǔ cult.

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán period. Dynasty: 宋-元.
