# Zhǐ guī jí 指歸集

*Collection of Pointing to the Return*

by [[吳悞]] (Wú Wù) — Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

Wú Wù's companion-anthology to his practical manual [[KR5c0298]] *Dān fáng xū zhī* 丹房須知 — a collection of alchemical sayings, aphorisms, and exegetical pointers. Dated c. 1163. Preserved as **DZ 921** / CT 921 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** c. 1163. Dynasty: 南宋.
