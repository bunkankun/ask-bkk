# Táng Sōng gāo shān Qǐ mǔ miào bēi míng 唐嵩高山啟母廟碑銘

*Táng Stele-Inscription for the Mother-of-Qǐ Temple at Sōng-gāo Mountain*

by [[崔融]] (Cuī Róng, 653–706)

A Táng **stele-inscription** for the **Qǐ mǔ** 啟母 Temple at **Sōng shān** 嵩山 (Central Sacred Peak, Hé nán) — honouring the mother of **Xià Qǐ** 夏啟 (son and successor of the culture-hero Great Yǔ 禹). Preserved as **DZ 971** / CT 971 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類). (Note: there is no DZ 970 in the canonical sequence as received; CT numbering proceeds 969 → 971.)

**Dating.** Within Cuī Róng's lifetime (653–706). Dynasty: 唐.
