# Jiǔ zhuǎn líng shā dà dān 九轉靈砂大丹

*The Nine-Cycle Numinous-Cinnabar Great Elixir*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng Daoist **alchemical text** on the **nine-cycle numinous-cinnabar** (*jiǔ zhuǎn líng shā* 九轉靈砂) great elixir. Preserved as **DZ 893** / CT 893 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
