# Shén xiān shí qì jīn guì miào lù 神仙食氣金櫃妙錄

*Marvelous Golden-Cabinet Records of the Divine Immortals' Consumption of Pneuma*

attributed to [[京里先生]] (Jīng lǐ xiān shēng, "Master Capital-Village"); Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties **pneuma-consumption** (*shí qì* 食氣) manual attributed to the otherwise unknown **Jīng lǐ xiān shēng** 京里先生 ("Master Capital-Village"). Preserved as **DZ 836** / CT 836.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
