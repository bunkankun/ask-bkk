# Tài qīng shí bì jì 太清石壁記

*Great-Clarity Record of the Stone Wall*

compiled by [[楚澤先生]] (Chǔ zé xiān sheng) — Táng pseudonymous compiler

A **mid-Táng alchemical compendium** (758 or 759) compiled by the pseudonymous "Chǔ zé xiān sheng" 楚澤先生, collecting alchemical recipes (*wài dān* 外丹) — particularly for mercuric and arsenical elixirs — purportedly derived from inscriptions on a "stone wall" (*shí bì* 石壁). An important source for mid-Táng alchemical practice. Preserved as **DZ 881** / CT 881 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類) in 3 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Compiled 758 or 759. Dynasty: 唐.
