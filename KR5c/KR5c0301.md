# Chún yáng Lǚ zhēn rén yào shí zhì 純陽呂真人藥石製

*Perfected Pure-Yáng Lǚ's Preparations of Drugs and Minerals*

attributed to [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòngbīn, Chún yáng zǐ)

An **alchemical-pharmaceutical compendium** pseudepigraphically attributed to the Daoist immortal Lǚ Dòngbīn, covering the preparation (*zhì* 製) of *yào shí* 藥石 — medicinal drugs and alchemical minerals. The received text is late (14th–15th century). Preserved as **DZ 903** / CT 903 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 14th–15th century. Dynasty: 元-明.
