# Tiān tán Wáng wū shān shèng jì jì 天壇王屋山聖跡記

*Record of the Sage-Traces at the Heavenly Altar of Wángwū Mountain*

by [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933) — late-Táng / Five-Dynasties Daoist polymath

A gazetteer-hagiography of **Wáng wū shān** 王屋山 (in Hé nán) — one of the ten great grottoes (*shí dà dòng tiān* 十大洞天) of Daoist sacred geography, with its **Tiān tán** 天壇 (Heavenly Altar) on the mountain's summit. Compiled by the great Daoist polymath Dù Guāngtíng. Preserved as **DZ 969** / CT 969 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Late Táng to early Five Dynasties, within Dù's lifetime. Dynasty: 唐-五代.
