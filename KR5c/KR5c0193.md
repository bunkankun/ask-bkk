# Tài shàng sān wǔ Zhèng yī méng wēi yuè lù jiào yí 太上三五正一盟威閱籙醮儀

*Offering Liturgy for the Examination of the Three-Five Zhèng yī Alliance-Awe Registers*

compiled by [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933)

A Zhèng yī ritual-liturgy text for the **examination of registers** (*yuè lù* 閱籙) — a key Daoist ritual of confirming one's ordination-registers with heaven. Compiled by **Dù Guāngtíng** 杜光庭 in his mature period. Preserved as **DZ 796** / CT 796 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

### *Sān wǔ zhèng yī méng wēi*

The "**Three-Five Zhèng yī Alliance-Awe**" (*Sān wǔ Zhèng yī méng wēi* 三五正一盟威) is the canonical **senior register-tradition** of the Celestial Master lineage — a comprehensive system of celestial registers (*lù* 籙) that the ordained Daoist carries as a mandate of his spiritual authority. The *yuè lù* ritual is the periodic re-examination and renewal of these registers.

### Dù Guāngtíng's editorial work

Dù Guāngtíng was a **major codifier of Táng liturgical tradition** — see [[KR5c0114]] for his monumental *Dàodé jīng guǎng shèng yì*, and [[KR5c0180]] for his female-immortal hagiography. The present liturgy is one of several liturgical-ritual manuals he compiled.

**Dating.** Within Dù Guāngtíng's mature period (900–933). Dynasty: 唐-五代.
