# Yù dòng dà shén dān shā zhēn yào jué 玉洞大神丹砂真要訣

*True Essential Instructions of the Great-Spirit Cinnabar from the Jade Cavern*

attributed to [[張果]] (Zhāng Guǒ) — Táng Daoist alchemist, one of the "Eight Immortals"

A Táng **alchemical instruction-text** on **cinnabar** (*dān shā* 丹砂) attributed to Zhāng Guǒ (*Zhāng Guǒ lǎo* 張果老), the famous Daoist of Táng Xuán zōng's court and one of the legendary Bā xiān 八仙 ("Eight Immortals"). The text is dated to 734 (Kāi yuán 22). Preserved as **DZ 896** / CT 896 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 734. Dynasty: 唐.
