# Běi dǒu zhì fǎ wǔ wēi jīng 北斗治法武威經

*Northern-Dipper Governing-Dharma and Martial-Awe Scripture*

Anonymous

A Daoist scripture integrating the **Northern Dipper cult** with **martial / demon-subduing rituals** (*wǔ wēi* 武威 = "martial awe"). Preserved as **DZ 870** / CT 870.

**Dating.** Undated. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
