# Yù qīng nèi shū 玉清內書

*Inner Book of Jade Clarity*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **inner-esoteric text** associated with the **Yù qīng** 玉清 (Jade Clarity) supernal realm — one of the Three Clarities of Daoist cosmography. Preserved as **DZ 947** / CT 947 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
