# Shén xiān fú shí líng cǎo chāng pú wán fāng zhuàn 神仙服食靈草菖蒲丸方傳

*Recipe-Record of Divine Immortals' Consumption of the Numinous Herb Calamus Pills*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng-era Daoist **herbal-pharmaceutical manual** on the preparation and consumption of pills made from the *chāng pú* 菖蒲 plant (calamus / sweet flag) — one of the classical Daoist longevity-herbs. Preserved as **DZ 844** / CT 844.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
