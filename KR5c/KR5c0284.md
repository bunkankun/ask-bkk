# Jiǔ zhuǎn líng shā dà dān zī shèng xuán jīng 九轉靈砂大丹資聖玄經

*Mysterious Scripture of the Nine-Cycle Numinous-Cinnabar Great-Elixir for Sagely Provision*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **alchemical scripture** on the ***jiǔ zhuǎn dà dān*** 九轉大丹 ("nine-cycle great elixir") — an elixir produced through nine iterative cyclings of **líng shā** 靈砂 (numinous cinnabar, HgS). Preserved as **DZ 886** / CT 886 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
