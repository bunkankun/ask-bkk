# Jīn huá chōng bì dān jīng mì zhǐ zhuàn 金華沖碧丹經秘旨傳

*Transmission of the Secret Purport of the Gold-Flower Soaring-Azure Elixir Scripture*

by [[白玉蟾]] (Bái Yùchán, 1194–1229) — fifth patriarch of the *Nán zōng* 南宗

Bái Yùchán's **transmission-commentary** (*zhuàn* 傳) on the *Jīn huá chōng bì dān jīng* 金華沖碧丹經 — an alchemical scripture in the Southern-Lineage *nèi dān* tradition. Dated by Bái's preface of 1225 (Bǎo qìng 1). Preserved together with the scripture proper ([[KR5c0313]]) as **DZ 914** / CT 914 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 1225. Dynasty: 南宋.
