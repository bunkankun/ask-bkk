# Huán jīn shù 還金述

*Account of the Return-Gold*

by [[陶埴]] (Táo Zhí)

A short **alchemical tract** on the **return-gold** (*huán jīn* 還金) elixir. Preserved as **DZ 922** / CT 922 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Late Táng to Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
