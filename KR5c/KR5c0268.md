# Tài shàng chú sān shī jiǔ chóng bǎo shēng jīng 太上除三尸九蟲保生經

*Most High Scripture for Removing the Three Corpse-Worms and Nine Worms and Preserving Life*

Anonymous; Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties Daoist scripture on the **removal of the Three Corpse-Worms** (*sān shī* 三尸) and the **Nine Worms** (*jiǔ chóng* 九蟲) — demonic parasites believed in Daoist physiological tradition to inhabit the human body and shorten its life. Their removal is a central task of Daoist longevity-cultivation. Preserved as **DZ 871** / CT 871.

## About the work

### The Three Corpse-Worms

The ***sān shī*** 三尸 (Three Corpse-Worms) are demonic entities believed to reside in the three *dān tián* 丹田 (elixir-fields) of the human body. They report on the host's transgressions to celestial authorities and seek to shorten their life. Their removal — through purification-rituals, dietary abstinence (especially grain-abstinence *bì gǔ* 辟穀), and talismanic protection — is a classical Daoist longevity-programme.

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
