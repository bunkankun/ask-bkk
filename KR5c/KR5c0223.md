# Zhū zhēn shèng tāi shén yòng jué 諸真聖胎神用訣

*Divine Use-Instructions of the Sagely Embryo of the Various Perfected*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng compilation of **embryonic-breathing** (*shèng tāi* 聖胎 = "sagely embryo", the mature Daoist *nèi dān* term for the cultivated inner-elixir-embryo) **instructions** from various Daoist Perfected (*zhēn rén* 真人). Preserved as **DZ 826** / CT 826.

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
