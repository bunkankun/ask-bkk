# Gēng dào jí 庚道集

*Collection on the Way of Gēng*

Anonymous; Míng (Zhèng tǒng era)

A Míng **alchemical compendium in 9 *juàn*** collecting *wài dān* 外丹 recipes and discussions. "Gēng" 庚 is the seventh heavenly stem, correlated with metal/white and one of the standard alchemical code-names for gold or silver. The collection's comprehensiveness and Míng date suggest it was compiled specifically for inclusion in the Zhèng tǒng 正統 Dào zàng redaction of 1444-45. Preserved as **DZ 953** / CT 953 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Early Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
