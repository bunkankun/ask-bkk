# Tài shàng xiāo zāi qí fú jiào yí 太上消災祈福醮儀

*Most High Offering Liturgy for Dispelling Calamity and Praying for Blessings*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A **general-purpose offering liturgy** for *xiāo zāi* 消災 (dispelling calamity) and *qí fú* 祈福 (praying for blessings) — the two most common purposes of Daoist community-ritual. Preserved as **DZ 810** / CT 810 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
