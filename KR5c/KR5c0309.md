# Gǎn qì shí liù zhuǎn jīn dān 感氣十六轉金丹

*The Sixteen-Cycle Gold Elixir Responding to the Pneuma*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng Daoist **alchemical text** on the **"sixteen-cycle gold elixir"** (*shí liù zhuǎn jīn dān* 十六轉金丹) produced through stages responsive to the *qì* 氣 cycle. Preserved as **DZ 911** / CT 911 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
