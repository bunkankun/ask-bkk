# Bái yún xiān rén líng cǎo gē 白雲仙人靈草歌

*The White-Cloud Immortal's Songs of Numinous Herbs*

Anonymous; Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties Daoist **verse collection** on **numinous herbs** (*líng cǎo* 靈草) — the botanical ingredients of longevity recipes — attributed to the legendary "White-Cloud Immortal" (*Bái yún xiān rén* 白雲仙人). Preserved as **DZ 932** / CT 932 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
