# Tài gǔ tǔ duì jīng 太古土兌經

*Most-Ancient Scripture of Earth and Duì*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **cosmological-alchemical scripture** invoking the **Tǔ** 土 (Earth) and **Duì** 兌 (the Metal / West / Lake trigram of the *Yì jīng*) — a trigram-pair correlating with the alchemical metal-west phase. The received text (1 *juàn*) is truncated from an original 3 *juàn*. Preserved as **DZ 949** / CT 949 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
