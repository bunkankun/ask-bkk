# Zhōng nán shān zǔ tíng xiān zhēn nèi zhuàn 終南山祖庭仙真內傳

*Inner Biographies of the Immortal Perfected at the Zhōng-nán Ancestral Seat*

by [[李道謙]] (Lǐ Dàoqiān, 1219–1296) — Quán zhēn historian

A major **Yuán Quán zhēn hagiography** covering the immortals and perfected associated with **Zhōng nán shān** 終南山, the ancestral seat (*zǔ tíng* 祖庭) of the **Quán zhēn 全眞** lineage. With [[KR5c0356]] and the *Gān shuǐ xiān yuán lù* 甘水仙源錄, it is one of the cornerstone primary sources for the history of the Quán zhēn school and its early hierarchs. Dated by preface 1284. Preserved as **DZ 955** / CT 955 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類) in 3 *juàn*.

**Dating.** 1284 (preface). Dynasty: 元.
