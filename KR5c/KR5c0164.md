# Tú jīng jí zhù yǎn yì běn cǎo xù lì 圖經集註衍義本草序例

*Preface and General Principles to the Illustrated, Collected-Commentary, Extended-Meaning Materia Medica*

compiled by [[寇宗奭]] (Kòu Zōngshì, fl. 1116); revised by [[許洪]] (Xǔ Hóng, fl. 1208)

A **Sòng-era** Daoist-medical **preface / introduction** to the *Běn cǎo* 本草 (materia medica) tradition, compiled by **Kòu Zōngshì** 寇宗奭 (1116) and revised by **Xǔ Hóng** 許洪 (1208). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 768** / CT 768 (*Dòngshén bù, Líng tú lèi* 洞神部靈圖類).

## About the work

The text is a **general introduction** to the *běn cǎo* 本草 pharmacopoeia tradition, providing principles of classification, usage, and interpretation of medicinal substances. It belongs to the Sòng-era revival of materia medica scholarship under imperial patronage.

### Authors

**Kòu Zōngshì** 寇宗奭 (*fl.* 1116) is the author of the celebrated **Běn cǎo yǎn yì** 本草衍義 (1116) — one of the most important Northern-Sòng *běn cǎo* critical works, which refined and extended the *Jīng shǐ zhèng lèi běi jí běn cǎo* 經史證類備急本草 of Táng Shèn wēi 唐慎微. This DZ 768 text contains the **preface and general principles** of that work.

**Xǔ Hóng** 許洪 (*fl.* 1208) — a Southern-Sòng scholar who revised Kòu Zōngshì's preface.

### Significance for Daoism

The inclusion of a *běn cǎo* preface in the Daoist Canon reflects the **deep integration of medical / pharmacological knowledge into Daoist cultivation tradition** — *yǎng shēng* 養生 practice requires knowledge of medicinal plants, minerals, and animal substances, and the Daoist Canon preserved many such medical works.

### Dating

Kòu Zōngshì's original 1116 preface; Xǔ Hóng's 1208 revision. Frontmatter gives **1116–1208** as the composition window. Dynasty: 宋.
