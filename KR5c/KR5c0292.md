# Jiǔ zhuǎn qīng jīn líng shā dān 九轉青金靈砂丹

*The Nine-Cycle Green-Gold Numinous-Cinnabar Elixir*

Anonymous; Sòng

A short Sòng Daoist **alchemical text** on the **nine-cycle green-gold numinous-cinnabar elixir** (*jiǔ zhuǎn qīng jīn líng shā dān* 九轉青金靈砂丹) — "green gold" (*qīng jīn* 青金) denotes lead in the alchemical lexicon. Preserved as **DZ 894** / CT 894 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
