# Xiū liàn dà dān yào zhǐ 修煉大丹要旨

*Essential Purport of Cultivating the Great Elixir*

Anonymous; Southern Sòng

A Southern-Sòng Daoist **alchemical summary-text** in 2 *juàn* on the essentials of cultivating the **great elixir** (*dà dān* 大丹). Preserved as **DZ 912** / CT 912 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
