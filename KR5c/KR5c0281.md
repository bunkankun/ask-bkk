# Tài qīng jīn Tiān shī kǒu jué 太清經天師口訣

*Oral Instructions of the Celestial Master on the Great-Clarity Scripture*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Daoist alchemical **oral-instruction text** (*kǒu jué* 口訣) purporting to transmit the Celestial Master's (*Tiān shī* 天師, i.e., [[張道陵]]) secret commentary on the **Tài qīng** 太清 alchemical scripture corpus. Preserved as **DZ 883** / CT 883 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
