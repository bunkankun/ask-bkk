# Qiān gǒng jiǎ gēng zhì bǎo jí chéng 鉛汞甲庚至寶集成

*Collected Completion of the Supreme Treasures of Lead-Mercury and JiǎGēng*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **alchemical compendium** in 5 *juàn* on **lead-and-mercury** (*qiān gǒng* 鉛汞 — the two principal yáng/yīn substances of Chinese alchemy) together with *jiǎ gēng* 甲庚 (first and seventh heavenly stems, indicating the correspondence system for firing phases). Preserved as **DZ 919** / CT 919 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
