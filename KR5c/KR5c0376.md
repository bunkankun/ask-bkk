# Tài shàng dòng shén wǔ xīng zàn 太上洞神五星讚

*Most High Dòng-shén Hymns to the Five Planets*

attributed to [[張衡]] (Zhāng Héng)

A short Daoist **hymnal** (*zàn* 讚) to the **Five Planets** (*wǔ xīng* 五星), pseudepigraphically attributed to Zhāng Héng (either the Eastern-Hàn polymath Zhāng Héng 張衡 78–139, or Zhāng Héng the second Celestial Master, son of [[張道陵]]). Preserved as **DZ 976** / CT 976 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
