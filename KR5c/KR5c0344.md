# Yàn mén gōng miào jiě lù 鴈門公妙解錄

*Lord Yànmén's Record of Wondrous Interpretations*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **alchemical text** of "wondrous interpretations" (*miào jiě* 妙解) attributed to the pseudonymous **Lord Yànmén** 鴈門公 (after Yàn mén 鴈門, a frontier district in Shānxī). The received text of the scripture in the Dào zàng truncates the first character — in the source title block appearing as 門公妙解錄. Preserved as **DZ 944** / CT 944 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
