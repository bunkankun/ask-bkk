# Sān yuán yán shòu cān zàn shū 三元延壽參贊書

*Supporting Book for Extending Life through the Three Origins*

by [[李鵬飛]] (Lǐ Péngfēi); Southern Sòng

A Southern-Sòng *yǎng shēng* compendium by **Lǐ Péngfēi** 李鵬飛, integrating the Daoist Three Origins (*sān yuán* 三元) framework with practical life-extension advice. Preserved as **DZ 851** / CT 851 in 5 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
