# Zhèng yī chì tán yí 正一敕壇儀

*Zhèng yī Altar-Consecration Liturgy*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Zhèng yī **altar-consecration** liturgy (*chì tán* 敕壇 = "imperially-commanded altar" — i.e. the formal establishment / consecration of a Daoist ritual altar). Preserved as **DZ 800** / CT 800 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text provides the procedure for the **consecration of a Daoist ritual altar** — an essential preliminary to all major Daoist rituals, establishing the sacred space through invocation of celestial authorities and formal purification.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
