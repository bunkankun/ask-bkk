# Sān huáng nèi wén yí mì 三皇內文遺祕

*Remnant Secrets of the Three Sovereigns' Inner Writings*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties / Táng

Preserved fragments and secrets of the **Three Sovereigns Inner Writings** (*Sān huáng nèi wén* 三皇內文) — the foundational talismanic corpus of the *Dòng shén* 洞神 tradition, originally associated with Bào Jìng 鮑靚 and Gé Hóng 葛洪. Preserved as **DZ 856** / CT 856.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties / Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
