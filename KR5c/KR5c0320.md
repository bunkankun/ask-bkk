# Dān fáng ào lùn 丹房奧論

*Mystery-Discourse on the Elixir Chamber*

by [[程了一]] (Chéng Liǎoyī) — early Northern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

An early Northern-Sòng **alchemical treatise** on the "mysteries" (*ào* 奧) of the elixir-chamber (*dān fáng* 丹房) — a speculative/philosophical companion to the technical-practical *Dān fáng xū zhī* ([[KR5c0298]]). Dated 1020. Preserved as **DZ 920** / CT 920 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 1020. Dynasty: 北宋.
