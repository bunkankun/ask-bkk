# Nán huá zhēn jīng yú shì zá lù 南華真經餘事雜錄

*Miscellaneous Remnant Records on the True Scripture of the Southern Florescence*

by [[陳景元]] (Chén Jǐngyuán, *hào* Bìxūzǐ 碧虛子; 1024–1094)

A **second supplementary apparatus** to Chén Jǐngyuán's *Zhuāngzǐ* scholarly programme, alongside [[KR5c0130|DZ 737 *Yú shì*]]. Preserved as **DZ 738** / CT 738 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類). The *Zá lù* 雜錄 ("miscellaneous records") collects further scholarly-biographical notes, Daoist hagiographic material relevant to the *Zhuāngzǐ* tradition, and occasional philological observations.

## About the work

### Relation to Chén Jǐngyuán's *Zhuāngzǐ* corpus

The DZ 738 *Zá lù* forms part of Chén Jǐngyuán's comprehensive *Zhuāngzǐ* scholarly apparatus together with:

- **[[KR5c0129]] DZ 736 *Nán huá zhēn jīng zhāng jù yīn yì*** — the main philological apparatus (1084).
- **[[KR5c0130]] DZ 737 *Nán huá zhēn jīng zhāng jù yú shì*** — first supplementary apparatus.
- **DZ 738 *Nán huá zhēn jīng yú shì zá lù*** — the present *Zá lù*.

Together these four form a coordinated scholarly toolkit for reading the *Zhuāngzǐ*.

### Contents

The *Zá lù* typically includes:

1. **Hagiographic material** on Zhuāng Zhōu 莊周 and other classical Daoist figures.
2. **Scholarly anecdotes** and records of pre-Sòng commentator-traditions.
3. **Miscellaneous observations** on textual-interpretive issues not covered in the main apparatus.

### Prefaces

No substantive preface survives.

### Abstract

The *Zá lù* is a minor supplementary work that completes Chén Jǐngyuán's extensive scholarly-philological engagement with the *Zhuāngzǐ*. Together with the main *Yīn yì* (DZ 736), the *Yú shì* (DZ 737), and various scattered notes, it demonstrates the depth of late-Northern-Sòng Daoist scholarly engagement with the classical texts.

**Dating.** Undated. Per the project's dating rule, and given its parallel with DZ 737, the frontmatter gives **1080–1094** as a conservative window covering Chén Jǐngyuán's mature-activity period. Dynasty: 宋.
