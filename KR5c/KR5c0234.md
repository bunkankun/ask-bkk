# Zhěn zhōng jì 枕中記

*Record within the Pillow*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng-era **pocket-format cultivation manual** (*zhěn zhōng* 枕中 = "within the pillow", i.e., a text to be kept close at hand for private study). Preserved as **DZ 837** / CT 837.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.

**Disambiguation.** Not to be confused with the famous Táng *chuán qí* 傳奇 tale ***Zhěn zhōng jì*** 枕中記 ("Record within the Pillow") by Shěn Jì jì 沈既濟 (fl. 781) — the classic story of Lǚ Dòngbīn and the rice-porridge dream. The Daoist *Zhěn zhōng jì* preserved as DZ 837 is a different text of the same title.
