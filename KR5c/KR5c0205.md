# Tài shàng sān dòng chuán shòu Dàodé jīng zǐ xū lù bài biǎo yí 太上三洞傳授道德經紫虛籙拜表儀

*Liturgy for Memorial-Prostrations of the Three-Cavern Transmission of the Dàodé jīng and Purple-Void Register*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng ritual-liturgy associated with the **transmission of the *Dàodé jīng*** and the **Purple-Void Register** (*zǐ xū lù* 紫虛籙) — a high-rank Daoist ordination-register. Preserved as **DZ 808** / CT 808 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
