# Zhū jiā shén pǐn dān fǎ 諸家神品丹法

*Divine-Grade Elixir Methods from the Various Authorities*

compiled by [[孟要甫]] (Mèng Yàofǔ) — Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

A Southern-Sòng **alchemical encyclopedia** in 6 *juàn* collecting elixir-methods (*dān fǎ* 丹法) of "divine grade" (*shén pǐn* 神品) from the "various authorities" (*zhū jiā* 諸家) of the preceding alchemical tradition. Dated 1163. Preserved as **DZ 918** / CT 918 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 1163. Dynasty: 南宋.
