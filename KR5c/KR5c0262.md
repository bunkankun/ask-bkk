# Tài qīng yuán jí zhì miào shén zhū yù kē jīng 太清元極至妙神珠玉顆經

*Great-Clarity Primordial-Ultimate Supreme-Marvelous Divine-Pearl Jade-Kernel Scripture*

Anonymous

A Daoist *nèi dān* scripture with elaborate metaphorical title evoking the "**divine pearl**" (*shén zhū* 神珠) and "**jade kernel**" (*yù kē* 玉顆) — mature *nèi dān* metaphors for the inner elixir. Preserved as **DZ 865** / CT 865.

**Dating.** Undated. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
