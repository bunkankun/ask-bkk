# Tài qīng fú qì kǒu jué 太清服氣口訣

*Oral Instructions on Pneumatic-Consumption of the Great Clarity*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties / Táng

A short **orally-transmitted instruction manual** (*kǒu jué* 口訣) on **pneumatic-consumption** (*fú qì* 服氣) — the Daoist practice of "eating *qì*" in lieu of (or supplementing) food, a central element of classical *yǎng shēng* cultivation. Preserved as **DZ 822** / CT 822.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties / Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
