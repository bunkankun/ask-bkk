# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng miào jīng zuǎn tú jiě zhù 太上老君說常清靜妙經纂圖解註

*Commentary with Diagrams on the Marvelous Scripture of the Most High Lord Lǎo on Constant Clarity and Stillness*

by [[王玠]] (Wáng Jiè; early 14th cent.)

An **early-Yuán commentary on the *Cháng qīng jìng jīng*** ([[DZ 620]]) **with illustrative diagrams** (*zuǎn tú* 纂圖), by **Wáng Jiè** 王玠. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 760** / CT 760 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類) in 1 *juàn*.

## About the work

### Distinctive feature: diagrams

The distinctive feature of this commentary is its integration of **illustrative diagrams** (*zuǎn tú* 纂圖) alongside the textual explanation. These visualise:

1. **Cosmological concepts** — the *Tài jí* 太極, *Wú jí* 無極, *yīnyáng*, and related structures.
2. ***Nèi dān*** 內丹 cultivation processes — the flow of *jīng*, *qì*, *shén* through the body.
3. **Meditative stages** — illustrating the scripture's cultivation-sequence.

This diagrammatic approach is characteristic of mature Yuán-era *nèi dān* literature, following the precedent of Chén Tuán's 陳摶 (d. 989) *Tài jí tú* and its subsequent development under Shào Yōng 邵雍 and Zhōu Dūnyí 周敦頤.

**Wáng Jiè** 王玠 — early 14th-century Daoist scholar, otherwise little-documented.
