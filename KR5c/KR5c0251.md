# Tài shàng sān huáng bǎo zhāi shén xiān shàng lù jīng 太上三皇寶齋神仙上錄經

*Scripture of the Supreme-Register Divine Immortals of the Three Sovereigns' Precious Fasting-Retreat*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng-era *Dòng shén* 洞神 (Three Sovereigns revelation tradition) scripture on the ritual-register system for **precious fasting-retreats** (*bǎo zhāi* 寶齋). Preserved as **DZ 854** / CT 854.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
