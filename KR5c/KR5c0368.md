# Lóng jiǎo shān jì 龍角山記

*Record of Dragon-Horn Mountain*

Anonymous; later 12th century

A Southern-Sòng **gazetteer-record** of **Lóng jiǎo shān** 龍角山 ("Dragon-Horn Mountain") — a Daoist sacred site. Preserved as **DZ 968** / CT 968 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Later half of the 12th century. Dynasty: 南宋.
