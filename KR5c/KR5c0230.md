# Shén qì yǎng xíng lùn 神氣養形論

*Treatise on Nourishing the Body through Spirit and Pneuma*

Anonymous; Táng / Sòng

A short Daoist *yǎng shēng* theoretical treatise on **nourishing the body** (*yǎng xíng* 養形) through the coordinated cultivation of **spirit** (*shén* 神) and **pneuma** (*qì* 氣). Preserved as **DZ 833** / CT 833.

**Dating.** Undated. Táng to Sòng range. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
