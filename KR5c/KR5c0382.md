# Tài shàng sān dòng biǎo wén 太上三洞表文

*Most High Three-Cavern Memorial Texts*

Anonymous; early Míng

A Míng **collection of memorial-texts** (*biǎo wén* 表文) for the **Three Caverns** (*sān dòng* 三洞) — the classical tripartite division of the Daoist Canon. Used in ritual contexts where deity-addressed memorials must be dispatched by fire. Preserved as **DZ 982** / CT 982 (*Dòngshén bù, Biǎo zòu lèi* 洞神部表奏類) in 3 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Early Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
