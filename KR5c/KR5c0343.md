# Líng fēi sǎn chuán xìn lù 靈飛散傳信錄

*Reliable Transmission-Record of the Numinous-Flying Powder*

by [[齊推]] (Qí Tuī) — mid-Táng Daoist alchemist

A mid-Táng **transmission-record** of the formula and lineage of the **líng fēi sǎn** 靈飛散 ("numinous-flying powder") — a famous longevity powder. (Title's last character varies between 言 and 信 in editions; both senses yield "transmission record".) Dated 812. Preserved as **DZ 943** / CT 943 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 812. Dynasty: 唐.
