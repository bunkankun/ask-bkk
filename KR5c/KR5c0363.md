# Wǔ dāng jì shèng jí 武當紀勝集

*Collection Recording the Scenic Wonders of Wǔ-dāng*

by [[羅霆震]] (Luó Tíngzhèn) — Yuán Daoist

A Yuán **verse-collection** (*jì shèng jí* 紀勝集) on the scenic and sacred sites of **Wǔ dāng shān** 武當山. Preserved as **DZ 963** / CT 963 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Yuán. Dynasty: 元.
