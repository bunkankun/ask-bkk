# Zhèng yī jiào mù yí 正一醮墓儀

*Zhèng yī Tomb-Offering Liturgy*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Zhèng yī **tomb-offering liturgy** — a *jiào* 醮 offering-ritual performed at an ancestral tomb to settle the deceased, purify the site, and propitiate local spirits. Preserved as **DZ 802** / CT 802 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text provides the procedure for Daoist tomb-ritual — a central Chinese religious observance at the intersection of Daoist cosmology, ancestral ritual, and local religious practice.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
