# Tiān xīn zhèng fǎ xiū zhēn dào chǎng shè jiào yí 天心正法修真道場設醮儀

*Liturgy for Establishing the Offering at a Perfection-Cultivating Dharma-Site of the Tiān xīn Zhèng fǎ*

Anonymous; SòngYuán

A SòngYuán ritual-liturgy of the **Tiān xīn zhèng fǎ** 天心正法 ("Heavenly-Heart Orthodox Dharma") — one of the major Sòng Daoist ritual-exorcistic traditions. Preserved as **DZ 807** / CT 807 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

### The Tiān xīn zhèng fǎ

The ***Tiān xīn zhèng fǎ*** 天心正法 ("Heavenly-Heart Orthodox Dharma") was a major Sòng Daoist ritual-exorcistic tradition, one of the so-called "new ritual methods" (*xīn fǎ* 新法) that proliferated in the Sòng. The tradition was especially prominent in the 11th–13th centuries, associated with the ritual innovations of figures like Lǚ Yuán sù 呂元素.

### Contents

The text provides a procedure for establishing a Daoist offering-ritual (*shè jiào* 設醮) at a dharma-site of the Tiān xīn Zhèng fǎ tradition — covering altar setup, invocation of deities, and petition-submission.

**Dating.** SòngYuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
