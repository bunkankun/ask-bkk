# Dān lùn jué zhǐ xīn jiàn 丹論訣旨心鑑

*Heart-Mirror on the Purport of Elixir Discourses and Instructions*

by [[張玄德]] (Zhāng Xuándé)

A Five-Dynasties Daoist **alchemical summary-text** surveying the purport of prior *dān lùn* 丹論 (elixir discourses) and *dān jué* 丹訣 (elixir instructions) — as a "heart-mirror" (*xīn jiàn* 心鑑) for the aspiring alchemist. Preserved as **DZ 935** / CT 935 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
