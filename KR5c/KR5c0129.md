# Nán huá zhēn jīng zhāng jù yīn yì 南華真經章句音義

*Phonetic and Philological Notes on the Chapters and Phrases of the True Scripture of the Southern Florescence*

by [[陳景元]] (Chén Jǐngyuán, *hào* Bìxūzǐ 碧虛子; 1024–1094) — the major Northern-Sòng philological apparatus to the *Zhuāngzǐ*; dated **1084**

A **14-*juàn* philological-phonetic apparatus** to the [[KR5c0051|*Nán huá zhēn jīng*]] (the *Zhuāngzǐ*) by **Chén Jǐngyuán** 陳景元 (1024–1094). Dated **1084**. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 736** / CT 736 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類). Chén Jǐngyuán's systematic philological treatment of the *Zhuāngzǐ* — providing *fǎnqiè* pronunciations, variant-reading notes, textual-critical comparisons, and a comprehensive list of pre-Sòng commentators — establishes the scholarly baseline for subsequent Sòng-Yuán *Zhuāngzǐ* scholarship.

## About the work

### Complementary role to Chén Jǐngyuán's other works

Chén Jǐngyuán produced a coordinated body of scholarly work on the classical Daoist texts. Alongside his famous *Dàodé jīng* commentary (DZ 714, [[KR5c0103]], 1072), the present DZ 736 constitutes his primary *Zhuāngzǐ* apparatus. Chén also composed:

- **DZ 737 *Nán huá zhēn jīng zhāng jù yú shì*** 南華真經章句餘事 — a supplementary apparatus (*yú shì* 餘事 = "remnant matters").
- Further *Zhuāngzǐ* scholarship mentioned in 18th-c. bibliographies.

Together these works constitute Chén's comprehensive *Zhuāngzǐ* scholarly programme.

### Scope

The DZ 736 covers:

1. **Phonetic glosses** (*yīn* 音) — *fǎnqiè* readings and homophone glosses for difficult characters in the 33 *piān*.
2. **Textual notes** (*zhāng jù* 章句) — chapter-and-phrase explanations, following the textual order of Guō Xiàng's 郭象 received recension.
3. **Variant-reading comparisons** — cross-references between different manuscript and print editions of the *Zhuāngzǐ*.
4. **Bibliographical apparatus** — a list of major pre-Sòng commentators on the *Zhuāngzǐ* (cited in DZ 734 [[KR5c0127]] of Chǔ Bóxiù as the authoritative Northern-Sòng bibliography).

### Abstract

The DZ 736 is the **most important Northern-Sòng philological-critical apparatus** on the *Zhuāngzǐ*. Its systematic treatment of the text's philological-phonetic issues set the standard for subsequent Daoist scholarship. Chǔ Bóxiù's 1265 compilation ([[KR5c0127]]) explicitly bases itself on Chén Jǐngyuán's commentator-list.
