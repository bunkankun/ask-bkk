# Míng zhēn pò wàng zhāng sòng 明真破妄章頌

*Hymn-Stanzas on Illuminating the Perfect and Smashing the Deluded*

by [[張繼先]] (Zhāng Jìxiān, 1092–1127) — Thirtieth Celestial Master

A short **hymnic-didactic text** by the Thirtieth Celestial Master, combining doctrinal stanzas ("illuminating the perfected, smashing delusion") with the ritual verse format. Preserved as **DZ 979** / CT 979 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類).

**Dating.** Within Zhāng Jìxiān's lifetime (1092–1127). Dynasty: 北宋.
