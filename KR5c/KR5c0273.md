# Tài shàng wǔ xīng qī yuán kōng cháng jué 太上五星七元空常訣

*Most High Instructions on the Constant Voidness of the Five Planets and Seven Origins*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Daoist **astral-walking manual** on the *wǔ xīng* 五星 (Five Planets: Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) and the *qī yuán* 七元 (Seven Origins = the Northern Dipper's seven stars), with diagrams of stellar pacing (*bù gāng* 歩罡) — inducing the descent of the five planetary spirits and the seven Dipper gods via ritual pacing of their asterisms. Preserved as **DZ 876** / CT 876 (*Dòngshén bù, Fāngfǎ lèi* 洞神部方法類).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
