# Dà dòng liàn zhēn bǎo jīng xiū fú líng shā miào jué 大洞鍊真寶經修伏靈砂妙訣

*Wondrous Instructions on Subduing the Numinous Cinnabar according to the Great-Cavern Precious Scripture of Refining the Perfected*

by [[陳少衛]] (Chén Shàowèi, *fl.* 712–734) — mid-Táng Daoist alchemist

One of Chén Shàowèi's paired mid-Táng alchemical **oral-instruction texts** on the "subduing" (*xiū fú* 修伏) of **numinous cinnabar** (*líng shā* 靈砂). Composed in the Kāi yuán reign (712–734) of Táng Xuán zōng. Preserved as **DZ 890** / CT 890 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 712–734. Dynasty: 唐.
