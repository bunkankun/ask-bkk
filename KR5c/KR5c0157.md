# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn yuán dào zhēn jīng zhù jiě 太上老君元道真經註解

*Commentary on the True Scripture of the Most High Lord Lǎo's Original Way*

by [[隱芝]] (Yǐn zhī)

A 13th-century **Daoist commentary** on a short Lǎojūn scripture **Yuán dào zhēn jīng** 元道真經 ("True Scripture of the Original Way"), by **Yǐn zhī** 隱芝 (*hào*, meaning "Hidden Mushroom"). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 761** / CT 761 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類).

## About the work

### Author

**Yǐn zhī** 隱芝 is a Daoist pseudonym; the *hào* evokes the *zhī* 芝 (magical mushroom) of Daoist immortality-lore, and "hidden" (*yǐn* 隱) evokes Daoist retreat.

### Scripture

The **Yuán dào zhēn jīng** is a short Lǎojūn-attributed meditation scripture. The commentary is relatively brief (1 *juàn*).

### Abstract

A minor but clean Quán zhēn-style commentary on a little-studied Daoist scripture.

**Dating.** 13th century. Frontmatter gives **1200–1300** as a conservative window. Dynasty: 宋-元.
