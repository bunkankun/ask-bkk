# Jīn bì wǔ xiāng lèi Cān tóng qì 金碧五相類參同契

*Gold-Azure Five-Aspects-Class Cān tóng qì*

attributed to [[陰長生]] (Yīn Chángshēng)

A Five-Dynasties / early-Sòng **alchemical text in the *Cān tóng qì* 參同契 tradition**, organized by the "five aspects" (*wǔ xiāng* 五相) of gold and azure (*jīn bì* 金碧 = the alchemical colors). Pseudepigraphically attributed to Yīn Chángshēng; the received text post-dates 947. Preserved as **DZ 904** / CT 904 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類) in 3 *juàn*.

**Dating.** After 947. Dynasty: 五代-宋.
