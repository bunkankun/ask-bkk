# Zhèng yī zhǐ jiào zhāi yí 正一旨教齋儀

*Zhèng yī Purpose-Teaching Fasting-Retreat Liturgy*

Anonymous; Táng

A Zhèng yī liturgical text for the formal **fasting-retreat** (*zhāi* 齋) — the central Daoist ritual-meditative practice that combines dietary abstinence, meditation, and formal liturgical observance. Preserved as **DZ 798** / CT 798 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
