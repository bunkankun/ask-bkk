# Dà Míng yù zhì xuán jiào yuè zhāng 大明御製玄教樂章

*Imperially-Commissioned Musical Stanzas for the Mysterious Teaching of the Great Míng*

Anonymous (imperially-commissioned); early Míng

An early Míng **collection of ritual hymn-texts** (*yuè zhāng* 樂章) composed under imperial commission for Daoist ceremonies (*xuán jiào* 玄教 = Daoism). Preserved as **DZ 981** / CT 981 (*Dòngshén bù, Biǎo zòu lèi* 洞神部表奏類).

**Dating.** First half 15th century (Yǒng lè / Xuān dé / Zhèng tǒng eras). Dynasty: 明.
