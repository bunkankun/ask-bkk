# Lóng hǔ huán dān jué 龍虎還丹訣

*Instructions on the Dragon-Tiger Return-Elixir*

by [[金陵子]] (Jīn líng zǐ) — early Northern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

A Northern-Sòng **alchemical instruction-text** on the **dragon-tiger return-elixir** (*lóng hǔ huán dān* 龍虎還丹) — the coupling of lead (tiger, *yīn*) and mercury (dragon, *yáng*). Dated 1042 (Qìng lì 2). Preserved as **DZ 909** / CT 909 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類) in 2 *juàn*.

**Dating.** 1042. Dynasty: 北宋.
