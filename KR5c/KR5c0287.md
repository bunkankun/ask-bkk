# Tài jí zhēn rén jiǔ zhuǎn huán dān jīng yào jué 太極真人九轉還丹經要訣

*Essential Instructions of the Tàijí Perfected on the Nine-Cycle Return-Elixir Scripture*

Anonymous; early Six Dynasties

An early Six-Dynasties **alchemical instruction-text** on the *jiǔ zhuǎn huán dān* 九轉還丹 (nine-cycle return elixir), attributed to the Tài jí zhēn rén 太極眞人 (Perfected of the Great Ultimate). Preserved as **DZ 889** / CT 889 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Early Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝初期.
