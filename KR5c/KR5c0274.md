# Jìn Yán shòu chì shū biǎo 進延壽赤書表

*Memorial Submitting the Life-Prolonging Red Book*

by [[裴鉉]] (Péi Xuàn) — Táng Zhōngnánshān Daoist

The Táng Daoist Péi Xuàn's **memorial submitting** the *Yán shòu chì shū* 延壽赤書 ("Red Book for Prolonging Life") to the throne. The memorial is preserved together with the scripture proper (see [[KR5c0275]]) as the "a" and "b" parts of **DZ 877**. Péi styles himself "Zhōngnánshān mountain-forest subject" (*Zhōngnán shān lín chén* 終南山林臣).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
