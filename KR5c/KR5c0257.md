# Shàng qīng zhèn yuán róng líng jīng 上清鎭元榮靈經

*Shàng qīng Scripture for Suppressing Origins and Glorifying Spirits*

Anonymous; Northern Sòng

A Northern-Sòng Shàng qīng 上清 ritual scripture for suppressing demonic origins (*zhèn yuán* 鎮元) and glorifying the spirits (*róng líng* 榮靈). Preserved as **DZ 860** / CT 860.

**Dating.** Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋.
