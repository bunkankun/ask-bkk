# Tāi xí bào yī gē 胎息抱一歌

*Song of Embryonic-Breathing and Embracing the One*

Anonymous; Northern Sòng

A **verse-format** (*gē* 歌 "song") instruction on embryonic breathing and the *Lǎozǐ*-derived meditation of "embracing the One" (*bào yī* 抱一). Preserved as **DZ 827** / CT 827.

**Dating.** Northern Sòng (960–1127). Dynasty: 北宋.
