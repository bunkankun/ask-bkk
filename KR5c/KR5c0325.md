# Dān fāng jiàn yuán 丹方鑑源

*Mirror of the Sources of Elixir Recipes*

by [[獨孤滔]] (Dúgū Tāo) — mid-Táng Daoist alchemist

A mid-Táng **alchemical-pharmacological lexicon** cataloguing the sources and synonyms of ingredients used in *dān fāng* 丹方 (elixir recipes). A companion-work to Méi Biāo's [[KR5c0299]] *Shí yào ěr yǎ* (806) in the line of Táng alchemical reference works. Dated 836. Preserved as **DZ 925** / CT 925 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 836. Dynasty: 唐.
