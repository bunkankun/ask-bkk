# Sī yìn qì jué fǎ 思印氣訣法

*Dharma of Meditative Mind, Seal, and Breath Instructions*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **exorcistic-ritual text** combining meditation (*sī* 思), ritual seal (*yìn* 印), and breath (*qì* 氣) techniques. The **Yellow God Yuè Seal** (*Huáng shén yuè zhāng* 黃神越章) already mentioned by [[葛洪]] Gé Hóng in the *Bào pǔ zǐ* 17 is central here. Preserved as **DZ 869** / CT 869.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
