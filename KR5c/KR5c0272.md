# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn dà cún sī tú zhù jué 太上老君大存思圖注訣

*Illustrated and Annotated Instructions for the Great Visualization Practice of the Most High Lord Lǎo*

Anonymous; early Táng

A major **early Táng meditation-manual** with illustrations and prose annotations, teaching the visualization of the **Five Viscera** (*wǔ zàng* 五藏), the **Five Sacred Peaks** (*wǔ yuè* 五嶽), the **Five Planets** (*wǔ xīng* 五星), and the **Five Thearchs** (*wǔ dì* 五帝) — with the golden-radiance (*jīn yìng* 金映) and five-colored aureole (*yuán guāng* 圓光) surrounding the body. Preserved as **DZ 875** / CT 875 (*Dòngshén bù, Fāngfǎ lèi* 洞神部方法類).

**Dating.** Early Táng. Dynasty: 唐初期.
