# Tiān lǎo shén guāng jīng 天老神光經

*Scripture of Heavenly-Elder Divine-Light*

attributed to [[李靖]] (Lǐ Jìng, 571–649) — the famous Táng general

A short Daoist scripture on cosmic divine-light, attributed (pseudepigraphically) to **Lǐ Jìng** 李靖 (571–649) — the celebrated Táng general and statesman who was also later canonised as a Daoist deity in the Tuō tǎ Lǐ Tiānwáng 托塔李天王 cult. Preserved as **DZ 866** / CT 866.

**Dating.** Lǐ Jìng's lifetime (571–649). Dynasty: 隋-唐.
