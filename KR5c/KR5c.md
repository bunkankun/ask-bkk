# KR5c 洞神部 *Dòngshén bù* — Daoist Canon → Cavern of Divinity

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR5c 洞神部 (*Dòngshén bù*, "Cavern of Divinity") collects 386 works (DZ 620 onward in the Schipper / Verellen *Concordance du Tao-tsang* numbering) — the third of the *sāndòng* 三洞 ("Three Caverns") under which the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (1445) is arranged, following [[KR5a]] *Dòngzhēn bù* (洞真部, Shàngqīng-flagged) and [[KR5b]] *Dòngxuán bù* (洞玄部, Língbǎo-flagged). Notionally the *Dòngshén bù* is the canonical home of the **Sānhuáng 三皇** ("Three Sovereigns") tradition — the third of the medieval revelation-cycles, codified around the lost *Sānhuáng wén* 三皇文 talisman-corpus. In practice almost no Sānhuáng material survived the proscriptions of the early Táng (640 imperial edict) and the subsequent Sòng editorial waves, and what the Míng compilers had at their disposal when they came to fill the third Cavern was something else entirely: the **Lǎozǐ corpus and its commentaries** — a textual archive that the *sāndòng* scheme had no proper place for, but which the canon could not omit. Wilkinson §29.4.4 notes the same drift across the canon: the *sāndòng* are *schools* (not genres), the *shí'èr lèi* 十二類 ("twelve sections": 本文, 神符, 玉訣, 靈圖, 譜錄, 戒律, 威儀, 方法, 衆術, 記傳, 讚頌, 表奏) are a cross-cutting genre axis, and by the Míng the two axes had ceased to cohere. In *Dòngshén bù* the dislocation is at its extreme: the *Dàodéjīng* 道德經 [[KR5c0045]] sits at DZ 664, the *Zhuāngzǐ* / *Nánhuá zhēn jīng* 南華真經 [[KR5c0051]] at DZ 670, the *Lièzǐ* / *Chōngxū zhìdé zhēn jīng* 沖虛至德真經 [[KR5c0049]] at DZ 668, the *Gēngsāngzǐ* / *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* 洞靈真經 [[KR5c0050]] at DZ 669, and almost half the entire division (some 175 titles in the 玉訣 *jade-instructions* section) is given over to commentaries on the *Lǎozǐ*. The 洞神部本文類 of the Míng canon is therefore in effect a **LǎoZhuāng canonical block with attached commentary archipelago**, framed by an opening cluster of Táng–Sòng short revealed scriptures, a substantial Tiānshī 天師-precepts layer in the 戒律 section, a 太清 *Tàiqīng* breath-and-alchemy stratum in 衆術, and a hagiographical anchor in *Shén xiān zhuàn* 神仙傳 [[KR5c0317]] and *Yǒngchéng jíxiān lù* 墉城集仙錄 [[KR5c0180]].

The internal section distribution registers this reorganization. The 本文 (original scriptures, [[KR5c0001]]–0058, ~51 titles) and the 玉訣 (commentary, ~90 titles by *source*-line count) jointly account for nearly half the division — the *běnwén* layer holds the *Dàodéjīng*, the *Zhuāngzǐ*, the *Lièzǐ*, the *Wénzǐ* [[KR5c0118]], the *Gēngsāngzǐ*, the *Wén shǐ zhēn jīng* 文始真經 [[KR5c0048]], the *Xī shēng jīng* 西昇經 [[KR5c0047]], plus the opening cluster of short Táng–Sòng *Tàishàng Lǎojūn shuō…* scriptures ([[KR5c0001]]–0044), and the *yùjué* layer holds the Hàn-through-Qīng commentary tradition on these same texts. The 戒律 (precepts, ~90 titles) preserves the largest concentration of early-Tiānshī code material in the canon — *Lǎojūn yīnsòng jiè jīng* 老君音誦戒經 [[KR5c0182]] by [[寇謙之]] (425–448), *Zhèngyī fǎwén tiānshī jiào jiè kē jīng* 正一法文天師教戒科經 [[KR5c0186]] (mid-third-century Hànzhōng Tiānshī material), *Nǚqīng guǐlǜ* 女青鬼律 [[KR5c0187]] (third-to-fourth-century). The 衆術 (various arts, ~75 titles) collects the 太清 breath / *qi* / alchemy short treatises ([[KR5c0214]]–0220 and following) together with the [[葛洪]]- and [[魏伯陽]]-attributed neidan literature; 記傳 (records and hagiography, ~20 titles) holds the *Shén xiān zhuàn*, *Yǒngchéng jíxiān lù*, *Hùnyuán shèngjì* 混元聖紀 [[KR5c0166]], and *Yóu lóng zhuàn* 猶龍傳 [[KR5c0170]]. Strikingly, the 威儀 (liturgy) section is empty in the Kanripo redaction — all properly liturgical material has been folded into the [[KR5b]] *Dòngxuán bù*.

The chronological span runs from the *Dàodéjīng* itself (c. 4th c. BCE, [[KR5c0045]]) and the *Zhuāngzǐ* (c. 4th c. BCE – early 4th c. CE in its received Guō Xiàng recension, [[KR5c0051]] [[KR5c0138]]) through to Guō Qìngfān's 郭慶藩 (1844–1896) *Zhuāngzǐ jí shì* 莊子集釋 [[KR5c0386]] (1894) — a span of more than two millennia, the widest of any KR5 sub-division. Date distribution by composition midpoint is sharply multimodal: a small pre-Hàn / Hàn–Wèi nucleus (5 works, the classics themselves and a HànWèi divinatory residue); a thin Six-Dynasties stratum (~25 works, principally Tiānshī precepts and early *Tàiqīng* breath texts); a thick **mid-to-late Táng peak (~85 works at 750–849)** anchored by [[李隆基]] (Táng Xuánzōng, 685–762) and [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933); a Northern-Sòng surge under [[徽宗]] (Sòng Huīzōng, 1082–1135) and [[陳景元]] (Chén Jǐngyuán, 1024–1094); a dominant **SòngYuán commentarial mass (~115 works at 1100–1349)** that includes the *Wùzhēn piān*-flavoured nèidān reading of the *Lǎozǐ* in [[葛長庚]] (Bái Yùchán) and [[李道純]] and the Confucian-Daoist synthesis of [[蘇轍]], [[林希逸]], and [[吳澄]]; and a sparse but consequential Míng–Qīng tail of imperial and literati commentaries running through to 1894.

## Important texts and text clusters

**The foundational Daoist classics (本文類).**
- *Dàodé zhēn jīng* 道德真經 [[KR5c0045]] — the 81-chapter *Lǎozǐ*, given the elevated title *Dàodé zhēn jīng* under Táng Xuánzōng (8th c.). This is the *Dàozàng*'s received Wáng Bì-tradition recension and is the textual centre of the entire division — every commentary in 玉訣類 attaches to it.
- *Nánhuá zhēn jīng* 南華真經 [[KR5c0051]] — the *Zhuāngzǐ* in the 33-chapter Guō Xiàng recension, elevated to *Nánhuá zhēn jīng* by Táng Xuánzōng in 742.
- *Chōngxū zhìdé zhēn jīng* 沖虛至德真經 [[KR5c0049]] — the *Lièzǐ* under its Táng elevated title (the received Zhāng Zhàn 張湛 recension, late 4th c.).
- *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* 洞靈真經 [[KR5c0050]] — the *Gēngsāngzǐ* / *Kāngsāngzǐ* 庚桑楚, fabricated 742–756 under [[李隆基]]'s patronage to round out the canonical four (and ascribed to a minor figure from *Zhuāngzǐ* ch. 23).
- *Wénzǐ* 文子 [[KR5c0118]] (also at [[KR5c0119]]) and the *Wén shǐ zhēn jīng* 文始真經 [[KR5c0048]] (the *Guān Yǐnzǐ* 關尹子 retake, c. 1100–1167, ascribed to Lǎozǐ's gate-keeper [[尹喜]]) — two further classics in the Lǎozǐ orbit.
- *Lièzǐ (Shì wén)* 列子 (釋文) [[KR5c0125]] — the *Lièzǐ* with [[殷敬順]]'s Táng philological glosses, transmitted alongside the [[張湛]] commentary.

**The opening cluster of Táng–Sòng short revealed scriptures ([[KR5c0001]]–0044).**
- *Tàishàng Lǎojūn shuō cháng qīngjìng miào jīng* 太上老君說常清靜妙經 [[KR5c0001]] — the *Qīngjìng jīng*, the most widely recited short scripture in late-imperial Daoism, terminus ante quem 785 (Huáisù autograph); the daily-recitation core of Quánzhēn monastic practice.
- The **Dipper-scripture cycle** [[KR5c0002]]–[[KR5c0010]]: *Dǒumǔ xīnjīng* (Dipper Mother), *Běidǒu* (Northern Dipper, [[KR5c0003]] [[KR5c0004]]), *Nándǒu* [[KR5c0005]], *Dōngdǒu* [[KR5c0006]], *Xīdǒu* [[KR5c0007]], *Zhōngdǒu* [[KR5c0008]] [[KR5c0009]] — the canonical Five-Dipper / Dipper-Mother programme of SòngYuán astral protection ritual, all classified 本文.
- The **Lǎojūn short scriptures** [[KR5c0012]] [[KR5c0015]] [[KR5c0016]] [[KR5c0022]] [[KR5c0023]] [[KR5c0024]] [[KR5c0031]] [[KR5c0033]] — Táng *Tàishàng Lǎojūn shuō…* miniatures running on cosmology (*Nèiguān jīng*), longevity (*Chángshēng yìsuàn*), filial piety (*Bào fùmǔ ēn zhòng*), and apotropaic charms.
- *Xī shēng jīng* 西昇經 [[KR5c0047]] in the [[徽宗]]-prefaced recension — Lǎozǐ's "Westward Ascent" address to [[尹喜]] at the pass.

**The *Lǎozǐ* commentary cluster — the densest exegetical archipelago in the canon.** The 玉訣 section of *Dòngshén bù* is essentially a Lǎozǐ commentary library. Robinet (1977) counted over 350 extant Lǎozǐ commentaries; a major proportion of the surviving pre-Míng ones are preserved here. Grouped historically:

- *Pre-Táng foundational commentaries:* *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* 道德真經註 (Héshànggōng 河上公 commentary) [[KR5c0065]] — Hàn, the standard "longevity/cultivation" reading (Robinet's type 2); *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* (王弼) [[KR5c0073]] — the Wèi philosopher [[王弼]] (226–249), the foundational "philosophical" reading (type 3); *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhǐguī* 道德真經指歸 [[KR5c0078]] — [[嚴遵]] (Yán Zūn)'s Western-Hàn commentary, preserved only here in the *Dàozàng*.
- *Táng court and Chóngxuán commentaries:* *Táng Xuánzōng yùzhù Dàodé zhēn jīng* 唐玄宗御註道德真經 [[KR5c0059]], *yùzhì shū* 御製疏 [[KR5c0060]] [[KR5c0061]] [[KR5c0062]] — the imperial commentary of [[李隆基]] (732–756), the first comprehensive court reading and the textual occasion for the title-elevation to *zhēn jīng*; *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* (李榮) [[KR5c0111]] — the Chóngxuán 重玄 (Twofold-Mystery) reading of [[李榮]] (fl. 658–663); *Dàodé zhēn jīng shū yì* (趙志堅) [[KR5c0108]] — another mid-Táng Chóngxuán reading.
- *Dù Guāngtíng's late-Táng / Former Shǔ synthesis:* *Dàodé zhēn jīng guǎng shèng yì* 道德真經廣聖義 [[KR5c0114]] — [[杜光庭]]'s 50-juan summary commentary on Xuánzōng's *yùzhù*; the most comprehensive late-Táng treatment of the *Lǎozǐ*.
- *Northern-Sòng imperial and scholarly commentaries:* *Sòng Huīzōng yùjiě Dàodé zhēn jīng* 宋徽宗御解道德真經 [[KR5c0063]] and its derivative *jiěyì* [[KR5c0064]] (commentary by [[章安]]) — [[徽宗]]'s 1111–1118 imperial recension; *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* (蘇轍) [[KR5c0074]] — [[蘇轍]] (1039–1112)'s Confucian-tinged reading; *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí jiě* (司馬光) and other Sòng entries; *Dàodé zhēn jīng cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān* 藏室纂微篇 [[KR5c0103]] — [[陳景元]] (1024–1094), the principal Northern-Sòng Daoist reading, with the same author's [[KR5c0102]] *kāi tí* preface, [[KR5c0104]] *kē wén shū* by [[薛致玄]] (1249), and [[KR5c0105]] *shǒu chāo* by Xuē Zhìxuán again.
- *Southern-Sòng, Jīn, and Quánzhēn / nèidān commentaries:* *Dàodé bǎo zhāng* 道德寶章 [[KR5c0076]] — [[葛長庚]] (Bái Yùchán, c. 1194–1229), the principal Southern-Lineage nèidān reading; *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí jiě* (趙秉文) [[KR5c0080]] — [[趙秉文]] (1159–1232), the Jīn-dynasty Confucian reading; *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí zhù* 道德真經集註 [[KR5c0095]], *Shì wén* [[KR5c0096]], *Zá shuō* [[KR5c0097]] — [[彭耜]] (fl. 1229)'s three-part Southern Sòng compendium; *Dàodé zhēn jīng kǒu yì* 道德真經口義 [[KR5c0088]] — [[林希逸]] (1193–1271), the most influential late-Sòng Confucian-Daoist reading.
- *Yuán syntheses:* *Dàodé huì yuán* 道德會元 [[KR5c0086]] — [[李道純]] (fl. 1290), the Quánzhēn-Southern syncretic reading; *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* (吳澄) [[KR5c0091]] — [[吳澄]] (1249–1333), the standard Yuán Confucian reading; *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí yì dà zhǐ* [[KR5c0112]] — [[劉惟永]] / [[丁易東]], a comprehensive Yuán *jí yì* (~1300).
- *Ming–Qing tail:* *Yùdìng Dàodé jīng zhù* 御定道德經註 [[KR5c0384]] — the Shùnzhì 順治 imperial commentary (1656); *Lǎozǐ shuōlüè* 老子說略 [[KR5c0385]] (1669); *Dàodé jīng zhù* [[KR5c0077]] — [[徐大椿]] (1760).

**The *Zhuāngzǐ* commentary cluster.**
- *Zhuāngzǐ zhù* 莊子注 [[KR5c0138]] — [[郭象]] (Guō Xiàng, c. 252–312)'s foundational WèiJìn commentary, the basis of every subsequent received text.
- *Nánhuá zhēn jīng zhù shū* 南華真經註疏 [[KR5c0139]] — [[成玄英]] (Chéng Xuányīng, fl. 630–660), the central Chóngxuán Táng sub-commentary on Guō Xiàng.
- *Nánhuá miǎo* 南華邈 [[KR5c0133]] and *Nánhuá zhēn jīng zhí yīn* 南華真經直音 [[KR5c0132]] (1086) — phonetic and short philological guides.
- *Zhuāngzǐ jí shì* 莊子集釋 [[KR5c0386]] — [[郭慶藩]] (1894), the modern critical-edition standard, integrating Guō Xiàng, Chéng Xuányīng, Lù Démíng's *Shì wén*, and Qing philological emendations; the base text for every twentieth-century *Zhuāngzǐ* translation (Watson, Graham, Ziporyn).
- SòngYuán philosophical commentaries: [[王雱]] (Wáng Pāng, 1044–1076, Wáng Ānshí's son); [[林希逸]]'s *kǒu yì*; [[呂惠卿]]'s *Zhuāngzǐ yì* — all scattered in the 玉訣 section.

**The *Lǎozǐ* hagiography and the deified-Lǎozǐ literature.**
- *Hùnyuán shèngjì* 混元聖紀 [[KR5c0166]] — [[謝守灝]] (1134–1212), 9 juan, 1191–1193: the systematic narrative of the *successive incarnations* (*lìshì huàshēn*) of the deified Lǎozǐ as Hùnyuán huángdì 混元皇帝, the foundational Daoist hagiography for the imperial cult of the *Tàiqīng gōng* 太清宮 sponsored from the Táng Xuánzōng era forward.
- *Yóu lóng zhuàn* 猶龍傳 [[KR5c0170]] — [[賈善翔]] (fl. 1086–1100), 6 juan: the earlier Sòng counterpart, drawing the title from Confucius' purported description of Lǎozǐ as "like a dragon" (*Shǐ jì* 63).
- *Lǎojūn jīnshū nèi xù* 太上老君金書內序 [[KR5c0168]] and a series of Lǎozǐ-hagiographic short tractates in the 記傳 section.

**The Sānhuáng / 三皇 ritual remnant (戒律類).**
- *Tàishàng dòngshén sānhuáng yí* 太上洞神三皇儀 [[KR5c0200]] — Táng, the one substantial ritual codification of the Three Sovereigns liturgy to survive the 640 proscription; the principal evidence for the lost *Sānhuáng wén* tradition that gives the Cavern its name.
- *Sānhuáng nèiwén yí mì* 三皇內文遺祕 [[KR5c0253]] — fragmentary *Tàiqīng* / *Sānhuáng* talismanic material in 衆術類.

**The early Tiānshī precept cluster (戒律類).** Together the most important concentration of pre-Six-Dynasties Celestial-Master code material in any surviving collection:
- *Tàishàng Lǎojūn jīng lǜ* 太上老君經律 [[KR5c0183]] — third-to-fourth-century Tiānshī precepts attached to the *Xiǎngěr* commentary tradition.
- *Lǎojūn yīnsòng jiè jīng* 老君音誦戒經 [[KR5c0182]] — [[寇謙之]] (Kòu Qiānzhī, 365–448)'s Northern Wèi reform of the Tiānshī liturgy; the principal source for the "New Tiānshī" reorganization at Sōngshān.
- *Zhèngyī fǎwén tiānshī jiào jiè kē jīng* 正一法文天師教戒科經 [[KR5c0186]] — mid-third-century Hànzhōng Tiānshī community materials, with the *Dà dào jiā lìng jiè* 大道家令誡 admonition of 255 CE addressed to the second Celestial Master Zhāng Hèng's 張衡 community.
- *Nǚqīng guǐlǜ* 女青鬼律 [[KR5c0187]] — third-to-fourth-century demon-prevention code, the principal exorcistic-ritual source for early Tiānshī apotropaic practice.

**The 太清 *Tàiqīng* breath-and-alchemy cluster (衆術類).**
- *Tàiqīng zhōnghuáng zhēnjīng* 太清中黃真經 [[KR5c0214]] — Six-Dynasties *Tàiqīng* breath-cultivation classic.
- *Tàiqīng dǎoyǐn yǎngshēng jīng* 太清導引養生經 [[KR5c0215]], *Tàishàng yǎngshēng tāixí qìjīng* [[KR5c0216]], *Tàiqīng tiáo qì jīng* [[KR5c0217]], *Tàishàng Lǎojūn yǎngshēng jué* [[KR5c0218]], *Tàiqīng fúqì kǒujué* [[KR5c0219]] — the canonical Six-Dynasties / early-Táng *Tàiqīng* breath-practice corpus.

**The Sūn Sīmiǎo medical-Daoist cluster.**
- *Cún shén liàn qì míng* 存神鍊氣銘 [[KR5c0231]], *Bǎoshēng míng* 保生銘 [[KR5c0232]], *Sūn zhēnrén shèyǎng lùn* 孫真人攝養論 [[KR5c0238]] — short tractates ascribed to [[孫思邈]] (581–682), the great Táng physician-Daoist; the bridge between *yǎngshēng* literature and Daoist breath cultivation.

**The alchemy and *Cāntóng qì* tradition (衆術類).** Although the *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì* itself is not housed here, the *Dòngshén* alchemical section preserves the principal pseudo-archaic readings of the tradition:
- *Bàopǔzǐ shénxiān jīnzhuó jīng* 抱朴子神仙金汋經 [[KR5c0316]] — Six-Dynasties [[葛洪]]-attributed alchemy.
- *Zhìchuān zhēnrén jiào zhèng shù* 稚川真人校證術 [[KR5c0300]] — Sòng [[葛洪]]-attributed material.
- *Wèi Bóyáng qī fǎn dānshā jué* 魏伯陽七返丹砂訣 [[KR5c0286]] — Táng [[魏伯陽]]-attributed.
- *Jīn bì wǔ xiàng lèi cāntóng qì* 金碧五相類參同契 [[KR5c0302]], *Cāntóng qì wǔ xiàng lèi mì yào* [[KR5c0303]], *Yīn zhēnjūn jīn shí wǔ xiàng lèi* [[KR5c0304]] — late-Táng to Sòng [[陰長生]]-attributed alchemical readings of the *Cāntóng qì*.
- *Tàiqīng jīnyè shén dān jīng* 太清金液神丹經 [[KR5c0278]] — the *Jīnyè* gold-elixir scripture, central to the Six-Dynasties Mao-Shān alchemical tradition.

**The Daoist hagiographies (記傳類).**
- *Shén xiān zhuàn* 神仙傳 [[KR5c0317]] — [[葛洪]] (Gě Hóng, 283–343)'s 10-juan *vitae* of 84 transcendents, the foundational Eastern-Jìn hagiographic source on figures from the HànWèi period (English translation: Campany 2002).
- *Yǒngchéng jíxiān lù* 墉城集仙錄 [[KR5c0180]] — [[杜光庭]] (850–933), 913 CE, the lives of 84 goddesses, matriarchs, and Daoist priestesses, including 18 Táng *nüguān* 女冠 (English translation: Cahill 2006).
- *Zhōngnán shān shuō jīng tái lìdài zhēn xiān bēi jì* 終南山說經臺歷代真仙碑記 [[KR5c0356]] — Yuán Quánzhēn epigraphic record of the Lóumáo Guàn 樓觀, the Tang court-affiliated Lǎozǐ shrine.
- *Tiāntán Wángwūshān shèng jì jì* 天壇王屋山聖跡記 [[KR5c0369]] — gazetteer of the central Daoist cult-mountain Wángwūshān, compiled by [[杜光庭]].

## Important persons

- [[老子]] (Lǎozǐ, attributed) — pseudo-historical author of the *Dàodé zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0045]]; the symbolic and devotional centre of the entire division and the deified protagonist of the *Hùnyuán shèngjì* / *Yóu lóng zhuàn* hagiographic complex.
- [[莊周]] (Zhuāng Zhōu, c. 369 – c. 286 BCE) — author of the *Zhuāngzǐ* / *Nánhuá zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0051]]; the second philosophical foundation of the division.
- [[列禦寇]] (Liè Yùkòu, attributed) and [[庚桑楚]] (Gēngsāngzǐ, attributed) — pseudo-authorial figures for the *Lièzǐ* [[KR5c0049]] [[KR5c0124]] and *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0050]] respectively; the canonical "three further classics" alongside the *Lǎozǐ* and *Zhuāngzǐ*.
- [[辛鈃]] (Xīn Jìng, attributed) — pseudo-author of the *Wénzǐ* [[KR5c0118]].
- [[尹喜]] (Yǐn Xǐ, attributed) — Lǎozǐ's gate-keeper at the Hangu Pass; pseudo-author of the *Wén shǐ zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0048]] and the named recipient of the *Dàodéjīng*.
- [[河上公]] (Héshànggōng, pseudo-Hàn) — pseudonymous compiler of the longevity-cultivation *Lǎozǐ zhāng jù* [[KR5c0065]], the most influential pre-Wáng-Bì commentary.
- [[王弼]] (Wáng Bì, 226–249) — Wèi philosopher; author of the standard "philosophical" Lǎozǐ commentary [[KR5c0073]], the textual basis for the *Daozang*'s received text.
- [[嚴遵]] (Yán Zūn, c. 83 BCE – c. 6 CE) — author of the *Lǎozǐ zhǐguī* [[KR5c0078]] (Robinet's "political-morality" type), preserved only in the *Dàozàng*.
- [[郭象]] (Guō Xiàng, c. 252 – 312) — author of the foundational *Zhuāngzǐ zhù* [[KR5c0138]]; the editor whose redaction reduced the *Zhuāngzǐ* from 52 to the received 33 chapters.
- [[成玄英]] (Chéng Xuányīng, fl. 630–660) — Táng Chóngxuán philosopher; author of the *Nánhuá zhēn jīng zhù shū* [[KR5c0139]] and a counterpart *Dàodé jīng* sub-commentary; the most important post-Guō-Xiàng reader of both classics.
- [[李隆基]] (Táng Xuánzōng 唐玄宗, 685–762, r. 712–756) — Táng emperor whose imperial commentary [[KR5c0059]] and *yùzhì shū* [[KR5c0060]]–[[KR5c0062]] reorganized the *Lǎozǐ* as canonical scripture and gave the work its received title *Dàodé zhēn jīng*; also the patron who elevated the *Zhuāngzǐ*, *Lièzǐ*, and *Gēngsāngzǐ* to *zhēn jīng* status.
- [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933) — the late-Táng / Former Shǔ polymath; the single most prolific author in *Dòngshén bù* (six titles), including the great Lǎozǐ summary [[KR5c0114]] *Guǎng shèng yì*, the *Qīngjìng jīng zhù* [[KR5c0155]], the hagiography *Yǒngchéng jíxiān lù* [[KR5c0180]], the *Tiāntán Wángwūshān shèng jì jì* [[KR5c0369]], and a Zhèngyī ritual cluster [[KR5c0193]]–[[KR5c0194]].
- [[徽宗]] (Sòng Huīzōng 宋徽宗, 1082–1135, r. 1100–1126) — the Daoist-imperial patron whose 1111–1118 commentaries on the *Dàodéjīng* [[KR5c0063]] and *Xī shēng jīng* [[KR5c0047]] reorganize the Northern-Sòng imperial reading of the corpus, with a derivative commentary by [[章安]] [[KR5c0064]] and the parallel sub-commentary by [[江澂]] [[KR5c0079]] [[KR5c0081]].
- [[陳景元]] (Chén Jǐngyuán, 1024–1094) — Northern-Sòng Daoist commentator; author of the *Cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān* [[KR5c0103]] on the *Dàodéjīng* and the *Xī shēng jīng jí zhù* [[KR5c0115]]; the most important pre-Huīzōng Daoist commentator on the Lǎozǐ.
- [[蘇轍]] (Sū Zhé, 1039–1112) — Northern-Sòng Confucian official; his *Lǎozǐ jiě* [[KR5c0074]] is the principal Confucian-Daoist bridge commentary of the era.
- [[葛長庚]] (Gě Chángēng = Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾, c. 1194–1229) — fourth Patriarch of the Southern Lineage (南宗); author of the *Dàodé bǎo zhāng* [[KR5c0076]], the principal nèidān reading of the *Lǎozǐ*.
- [[林希逸]] (Lín Xīyì, 1193–1271) — Southern Sòng Confucian; his *Dàodé zhēn jīng kǒu yì* [[KR5c0088]] and parallel *Zhuāngzǐ kǒu yì* are the most influential late-Sòng Confucian readings of the classical Daoist canon.
- [[李道純]] (Lǐ Dàochún, fl. 1290) — Yuán Quánzhēn-Southern syncretist; his *Dàodé huì yuán* [[KR5c0086]] integrates Three Teachings (*sānjiào* 三教) hermeneutics with internal alchemy.
- [[吳澄]] (Wú Chéng, 1249–1333) — Yuán Confucian; the *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* [[KR5c0091]] is the canonical late-Yuán Cheng-Zhu-school reading of the *Lǎozǐ*.
- [[杜道堅]] (Dù Dàojiān, 1237–1318) — Yuán Daoist; author of [[KR5c0104]] *Kē wén shū* and parallel *Lǎozǐ* exegesis from the SòngYuán transition.
- [[彭耜]] (Péng Sì, fl. 1229) — Southern Sòng compiler; author of the three-part *Lǎozǐ* compendium [[KR5c0095]] *Jí zhù*, [[KR5c0096]] *Shì wén*, [[KR5c0097]] *Zá shuō*.
- [[葛洪]] (Gě Hóng, 283–343) — Eastern-Jìn alchemist, author of the *Bàopǔzǐ*; in *Dòngshén bù* his presence is through six attributed alchemy texts ([[KR5c0239]] [[KR5c0300]] [[KR5c0316]] and others) and the foundational hagiography *Shén xiān zhuàn* [[KR5c0317]].
- [[寇謙之]] (Kòu Qiānzhī, 365–448) — Northern-Wèi Tiānshī reformer; author of *Lǎojūn yīnsòng jiè jīng* [[KR5c0182]], the principal Northern-Tiānshī liturgical reform document.
- [[孫思邈]] (Sūn Sīmiǎo, 581–682) — Táng physician and Daoist; the *Cún shén liàn qì míng* [[KR5c0231]], *Bǎoshēng míng* [[KR5c0232]], and *Shèyǎng lùn* [[KR5c0238]] tractates anchor the medical-Daoist *yǎngshēng* tradition in the division.
- [[魏伯陽]] (Wèi Bóyáng, attributed, Eastern Hàn) and [[陰長生]] (Yīn Chángshēng, attributed, Hàn) — pseudo-archaic alchemical authorities for the [[KR5c0286]] / [[KR5c0302]]–[[KR5c0304]] *Cāntóng qì*-tradition texts.
- [[謝守灝]] (Xiè Shǒuhào, 1134–1212) — Sòng hagiographer; compiler of *Hùnyuán shèngjì* [[KR5c0166]] and several short tractates on the deified Lǎozǐ.
- [[賈善翔]] (Jiǎ Shànxiáng, fl. 1086) — Northern Sòng hagiographer; compiler of *Yóu lóng zhuàn* [[KR5c0170]].
- [[傅奕]] (Fù Yì, 555–639) — early-Táng Daoist scholar; editor of [[KR5c0046]] *Dàodé jīng gǔběn piān*, the "Old-Text" recension drawn from a Hàn-tomb manuscript unearthed in 574 CE.
- [[郭慶藩]] (Guō Qìngfān, 1844–1896) — late-Qīng philologist; author of *Zhuāngzǐ jí shì* [[KR5c0386]] (1894), the standard modern critical edition of the *Zhuāngzǐ*.

## Topics

- **The Sānhuáng / Cavern-of-Divinity problem.** The third Cavern of the *sāndòng* was notionally the home of the *Sānhuáng wén* 三皇文 — the third major medieval revelation, alongside Shàngqīng and Língbǎo. But the bulk of the Sānhuáng corpus was destroyed in the 640 proscription, and the *Dòngshén bù* of the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* preserves only the merest residue ([[KR5c0200]] *Sānhuáng yí*, [[KR5c0253]] *Sānhuáng nèiwén yí mì*). The Míng compilers filled the vacated Cavern with what they had: the Lǎozǐ corpus, the *Zhuāngzǐ*, the *Lièzǐ*, and the great Hàn-through-Sòng commentary archive on these texts. The dislocation between section title and section content is sharper here than anywhere else in the canon, and the historical question — *why does the Lǎozǐ live in the Cavern of Divinity?* — is the central interpretive problem of the division. The answer Schipper & Verellen propose (*Taoist Canon* 1: 30–40) is that the Lǎozǐ was the senior text of the *Tàixuán bù* 太玄部 (the Supplement to the Cavern of Perfection) but that the *Tàixuán* and *Dòngshén* sections were merged in the Sòng-era catalog reform.

- **Robinet's four types of *Lǎozǐ* commentary.** The Lǎozǐ commentary library in 玉訣類 illustrates with unusual completeness the four traditional Lǎozǐ-commentary types identified by Robinet (1977) and Wilkinson §29.4.2.2: (1) **philological** (the [[傅奕]] *gǔběn*, [[KR5c0096]] *Shì wén*); (2) **longevity and cultivation** (the [[河上公]] commentary [[KR5c0065]], the *Xiǎngěr* tradition, the Quánzhēn / Southern-Lineage nèidān readings of [[葛長庚]] and [[李道純]]); (3) **political morality** ([[嚴遵]] [[KR5c0078]], the imperial commentaries of [[李隆基]] and [[徽宗]], the Confucian readings of [[蘇轍]] [[吳澄]] [[林希逸]]); and (4) **philosophical / metaphysical** ([[王弼]] [[KR5c0073]], the Chóngxuán readings of [[李榮]], [[趙志堅]], and [[成玄英]]). The division therefore functions as a near-complete diachronic museum of *Lǎozǐ* hermeneutics from the Western Hàn to the Qīng.

- **The Chóng-xuán 重玄 ("Twofold Mystery") movement.** A distinctive mid-Táng philosophical Daoism, drawing on Mādhyamika Buddhist śūnyatā dialectic to read the *Lǎozǐ* as a programme of double negation. The *Dòngshén bù* preserves the principal Chóng-xuán readings — [[李榮]] on the *Lǎozǐ* [[KR5c0111]], [[趙志堅]] [[KR5c0108]], [[成玄英]] on the *Zhuāngzǐ* [[KR5c0139]] and parallel *Lǎozǐ* sub-commentary — and traces the movement's Sòng afterlife through [[陳景元]]'s *Cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān* [[KR5c0103]]. The *Qīngjìng jīng* [[KR5c0001]] and its commentary corpus are likewise *Chóng-xuán*-flavoured Táng products.

- **Imperial commentary and the Lǎozǐ as political-Daoist scripture.** Three major imperial commentaries on the *Lǎozǐ* are preserved here — the Táng [[李隆基]] [[KR5c0059]]–[[KR5c0062]], the Northern Sòng [[徽宗]] [[KR5c0063]]–[[KR5c0064]], and the early Qīng Shùnzhì *Yùdìng Dàodé jīng zhù* [[KR5c0384]]. Each marks a moment of court-sponsored Daoist resurgence and each rereads the text as political-moral instruction (Robinet's type 3). The Northern Sòng Huīzōng era in particular generates the densest single cluster in the division ([[KR5c0063]]–0081, ~20 titles).

- **Internal alchemy and the *Lǎozǐ* as nèidān manual.** From the late twelfth century onward the *Lǎozǐ* is increasingly read through the lens of [[張伯端]]'s *Wùzhēn piān* and the Southern Lineage's symbolic alchemy of *jīngqìshén* refinement. The principal exemplars are [[葛長庚]] [[KR5c0076]] and [[李道純]] [[KR5c0086]]; the Quánzhēn afterlife of this reading runs through the *Qīngjìng jīng* tradition [[KR5c0001]] and the Quánzhēn daily-recitation curriculum.

- **The Lǎozǐ hagiography and the deification of the philosopher.** A continuous tradition from [[葛洪]]'s *Shén xiān zhuàn* [[KR5c0317]] (where Lǎozǐ is already an immortal) through the Sòng synthesis of [[賈善翔]] *Yóu lóng zhuàn* [[KR5c0170]] and [[謝守灝]] *Hùnyuán shèngjì* [[KR5c0166]], which works out the doctrine of Lǎozǐ's successive incarnations (*lìshì huàshēn*) and provides the textual basis for the *Tàiqīng gōng* 太清宮 imperial cult. The Lǎozǐ is the principal "deity" of *Dòngshén bù* both as scripture and as god.

- **The Tiānshī precept corpus.** The 戒律 section is the densest concentration of early Celestial-Master code material in any extant collection. Together with the *Xiǎngěr* Lǎozǐ commentary tradition, these materials are the principal sources for the historical reconstruction of the Hànzhōng community (c. 142–215 CE) and its Northern reformer [[寇謙之]]. *Tàishàng Lǎojūn jīng lǜ* [[KR5c0183]], *Lǎojūn yīnsòng jiè jīng* [[KR5c0182]], *Zhèngyī fǎwén tiānshī jiào jiè kē jīng* [[KR5c0186]], and *Nǚqīng guǐlǜ* [[KR5c0187]] together cover the third-to-fifth-century Tiānshī liturgical reform.

- **The *Tàiqīng* breath tradition.** Although the Tàiqīng formally has its own supplemental Cavern, the *Tàiqīng* breath-cultivation classics ([[KR5c0214]]–[[KR5c0220]]) are bound into *Dòngshén bù* as the canonical pre-Sòng *qì*-practice literature. They feed directly into the Táng *yǎngshēng* tradition exemplified by [[孫思邈]] ([[KR5c0231]] [[KR5c0232]] [[KR5c0238]]) and beyond into SòngYuán nèidān.

- **Daoist hagiography and the institutional cult.** [[葛洪]]'s *Shén xiān zhuàn* [[KR5c0317]] and [[杜光庭]]'s *Yǒngchéng jíxiān lù* [[KR5c0180]] are jointly the two foundational pre-Sòng Daoist hagiographies (the earlier *Liè xiān zhuàn* lives in [[KR5a]]). The *Yǒngchéng* is unusually significant for women's Daoism, as the principal Táng source on female *zhēn rén* and *nǚguān*; the *Shén xiān zhuàn* is the principal source for the alchemical-immortal biographies that fund the early Bàopǔzǐ tradition.

## Timeline

- **c. 400–250 BCE (Warring States)** — The *Lǎozǐ* / *Dàodé zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0045]] takes shape in successive recensions (Guōdiàn fragments c. 300 BCE; Mǎwángduī recensions c. 200 BCE; received Wáng Bì-line text by the Hàn); the *Zhuāngzǐ* [[KR5c0051]] [[KR5c0126]] accretes around the writings of [[莊周]] (c. 369–286 BCE).
- **c. 300 BCE – 100 CE** — The *Wénzǐ* [[KR5c0118]] takes its layered form (a Warring-States core, much late-Hàn / Eastern Hàn expansion); pseudepigraphic HànWèi alchemy and divination accumulates (e.g. [[KR5c0163]] *Tàishàng tōnglíng bā shǐ shèngwén zhēnxíng tú*).
- **c. 25 – 220 (Eastern Hàn)** — The [[河上公]] *Lǎozǐ zhāng jù* [[KR5c0065]] is composed; the Hànzhōng Tiānshī community materials underlying [[KR5c0186]] *Zhèngyī fǎwén tiānshī jiào jiè kē jīng* begin to form.
- **c. 83 BCE – 6 CE** — [[嚴遵]] composes the *Lǎozǐ zhǐguī* [[KR5c0078]].
- **226–249 (Wèi)** — [[王弼]] composes the foundational *Lǎozǐ zhù* [[KR5c0073]], the basis of the *Dàozàng*'s received text.
- **c. 252 – 312 (Wèi–Jìn)** — [[郭象]] composes the *Zhuāngzǐ zhù* [[KR5c0138]] and fixes the received 33-chapter recension.
- **c. 300 – 370 (Eastern Jìn)** — [[張湛]] (Zhāng Zhàn) compiles the received *Lièzǐ* [[KR5c0049]] [[KR5c0124]]; [[葛洪]] (283–343) composes *Shén xiān zhuàn* [[KR5c0317]] and the Bàopǔzǐ-attributed alchemy texts [[KR5c0316]].
- **425–448 (Northern Wèi)** — [[寇謙之]] composes *Lǎojūn yīnsòng jiè jīng* [[KR5c0182]] as the foundational document of the Northern-Tiānshī reform at Sōngshān.
- **c. 500 – 700** — The *Tàiqīng* breath-cultivation corpus [[KR5c0214]]–[[KR5c0220]] takes shape; *Nǚqīng guǐlǜ* [[KR5c0187]] receives its received form; the [[KR5c0021]] *Dòngshén bā dì miào jīng* is composed within the surviving Sānhuáng tradition.
- **574 (Northern Zhōu)** — A Hàn-tomb manuscript of the *Lǎozǐ* is unearthed; [[傅奕]] (555–639) later (after 618) publishes it as *Dàodé jīng gǔběn piān* [[KR5c0046]].
- **630–660 (early Táng)** — [[成玄英]] composes the *Nánhuá zhēn jīng zhù shū* [[KR5c0139]] and parallel *Lǎozǐ* sub-commentary, the central Chóngxuán reading of both classics.
- **640 (Táng)** — Imperial proscription of Sānhuáng materials; the bulk of the original *Cavern of Divinity* corpus is destroyed.
- **649–730 (Táng)** — Mid-Táng Chóngxuán Daoism flourishes: [[李榮]]'s *Lǎozǐ zhù* [[KR5c0111]], [[趙志堅]]'s *Shū yì* [[KR5c0108]]; the *Tàishàng dòngshén sānhuáng yí* [[KR5c0200]] preserves a recensional remnant of the proscribed tradition.
- **581–682 (Táng)** — [[孫思邈]]'s longevity tractates [[KR5c0231]] [[KR5c0232]] [[KR5c0238]] bridge medicine and Daoist *yǎngshēng*.
- **732–756 (Táng)** — [[李隆基]] composes the imperial *Lǎozǐ* commentary [[KR5c0059]] and *yùzhì shū* [[KR5c0060]]–[[KR5c0062]]; elevates the *Lǎozǐ*, *Zhuāngzǐ*, *Lièzǐ*, and *Gēngsāngzǐ* to *zhēn jīng* status; commissions the new *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0050]] to round out the canonical four.
- **c. 700 – 800 (mid Táng)** — The *Qīngjìng jīng* [[KR5c0001]] is composed; the *Tàishàng Lǎojūn shuō…* short-scripture cluster [[KR5c0001]]–[[KR5c0044]] takes shape; numerous Táng alchemical pseudepigrapha enter the *zhòngshù* section ([[KR5c0286]] [[KR5c0270]] [[KR5c0271]]).
- **850–933 (late Táng / Former Shǔ)** — [[杜光庭]]'s great synthesis: *Dàodé zhēn jīng guǎng shèng yì* [[KR5c0114]] (the 50-juan summary of Xuánzōng's commentary), *Qīngjìng jīng zhù* [[KR5c0155]], *Yǒngchéng jíxiān lù* [[KR5c0180]] (913), *Tiāntán Wángwūshān shèng jì jì* [[KR5c0369]], and a Zhèngyī liturgical cluster [[KR5c0193]]–[[KR5c0194]].
- **1024–1094 (N. Sòng)** — [[陳景元]] composes *Dàodé zhēn jīng cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān* [[KR5c0103]] and *Xī shēng jīng jí zhù* [[KR5c0115]] — the principal Northern-Sòng Daoist exegesis on the *Lǎozǐ* corpus.
- **1086 (N. Sòng)** — [[賈善翔]] compiles *Yóu lóng zhuàn* [[KR5c0170]]; *Nánhuá zhēn jīng zhí yīn* [[KR5c0132]] is compiled.
- **1100–1108 (N. Sòng)** — [[蘇轍]] composes his *Lǎozǐ jiě* [[KR5c0074]]; *Wén shǐ zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0048]] takes its received form.
- **1111–1125 (N. Sòng, Huīzōng era)** — [[徽宗]]'s imperial *Lǎozǐ* commentary [[KR5c0063]], *Xī shēng jīng* commentary [[KR5c0047]], and the surrounding sub-commentary cluster ([[KR5c0064]] [[KR5c0079]] [[KR5c0081]]).
- **1191–1193 (S. Sòng)** — [[謝守灝]] compiles *Hùnyuán shèngjì* [[KR5c0166]], the systematic Lǎozǐ-incarnation hagiography.
- **c. 1194–1229 (S. Sòng)** — [[葛長庚]] (Bái Yùchán) composes *Dàodé bǎo zhāng* [[KR5c0076]] — the principal nèidān reading of the *Lǎozǐ*.
- **1217–1232 (Jīn)** — [[趙秉文]] composes *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí jiě* [[KR5c0080]].
- **1229 (S. Sòng)** — [[彭耜]] compiles his three-part *Lǎozǐ* compendium [[KR5c0095]]–[[KR5c0097]].
- **1249 (S. Sòng)** — [[薛致玄]] composes *Cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān kāi tí kē wén shū* [[KR5c0104]] and *shǒu chāo* [[KR5c0105]] on Chén Jǐngyuán's commentary.
- **1261 (S. Sòng)** — [[林希逸]] composes *Dàodé zhēn jīng kǒu yì* [[KR5c0088]] (and parallel *Zhuāngzǐ kǒu yì*).
- **1280–1333 (Yuán)** — [[吳澄]]'s *Dàodé zhēn jīng zhù* [[KR5c0091]], the canonical Yuán Confucian reading.
- **c. 1290 (Yuán)** — [[李道純]]'s *Dàodé huì yuán* [[KR5c0086]] integrates Quánzhēn-Southern syncretic hermeneutics.
- **1298–1300 (Yuán)** — [[鄧錡]]'s *Dàodé zhēn jīng sān jiě* [[KR5c0070]]; [[劉惟永]] / [[丁易東]]'s *Jí yì dà zhǐ* [[KR5c0112]].
- **1296 (Yuán)** — *Wén shǐ zhēn jīng zhù* [[KR5c0116]].
- **c. 1280 – 1330 (Yuán)** — [[杜道堅]] active; *Zhōngnán shān shuō jīng tái lìdài zhēn xiān bēi jì* [[KR5c0356]] compiled (c. 1280–1300).
- **1368–1644 (Míng)** — Late additions and recompilations: [[KR5c0044]] *Xuántiān shàngdì shuō bào fùmǔ ēn zhòng jīng* (c. 1350–1382, [[趙宜真]]); *Tàiqīng dàodé xiǎnhuà yí* [[KR5c0190]]; the *Sānhuáng nèiwén yí mì* [[KR5c0253]] recension.
- **1656 (early Qīng)** — *Yùdìng Dàodé jīng zhù* [[KR5c0384]] — Shùnzhì imperial commentary.
- **1669, 1760 (Qīng)** — [[KR5c0385]] *Lǎozǐ shuōlüè*; [[徐大椿]] *Dàodé jīng zhù* [[KR5c0077]] (1760).
- **1894 (late Qīng)** — [[郭慶藩]] *Zhuāngzǐ jí shì* [[KR5c0386]] — the modern standard critical edition, terminating the division at the latest possible date.
