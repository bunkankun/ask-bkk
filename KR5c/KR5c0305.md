# Jīn shí bù jiǔ wǔ shù jué 金石簿九五數訣

*Instructions on the Nine-Five Enumerations of the Metals-and-Minerals Register*

Anonymous; Táng (dated 686)

A Táng **alchemical lexicon-and-instruction text** (title on the file cover gives 五九, in the source 九五) cataloguing metals and minerals by a "nine-five" numerological schema. Dated 686 (Chuí gǒng 2 of Wǔ Zé tiān). Preserved as **DZ 907** / CT 907 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 686. Dynasty: 唐.
