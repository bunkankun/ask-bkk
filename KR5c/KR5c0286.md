# Wèi Bóyáng qī fǎn dān shā jué 魏伯陽七返丹砂訣

*Wèi Bóyáng's Instructions on the Seven-Reversion Cinnabar*

attributed to [[魏伯陽]] (Wèi Bóyáng); annotated by [[黃童君]] (Huáng tóng jūn)

A Táng **alchemical instruction-text** on the **"seven-reversion cinnabar"** (*qī fǎn dān shā* 七返丹砂) — an elixir produced by seven iterative reversions of cinnabar — attributed to the *Cān tóng qì* 參同契 patriarch [[魏伯陽]] and annotated by the Táng Daoist Huáng tóng jūn. The attribution to Wèi Bóyáng is pseudepigraphic. Preserved as **DZ 888** / CT 888 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
