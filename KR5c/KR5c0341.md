# Hóng qiān rù hēi qiān jué 紅鉛入黑鉛訣

*Instructions on Inserting Red Lead into Black Lead*

Anonymous; Táng

A short Táng **alchemical instruction-text** on the reaction of **red lead** (*hóng qiān* 紅鉛 = lead tetroxide, Pb₃O₄) into **black lead** (*hēi qiān* 黑鉛 = metallic or sulfide lead). Preserved as **DZ 941** / CT 941 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
