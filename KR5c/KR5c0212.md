# Běi jí Zhēn wǔ pǔ cí dù shì fǎ chàn 北極真武普慈度世法懺

*North-Polar Zhēn wǔ Universally-Compassionate World-Salvation Dharma Repentance*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

Another Zhēn wǔ repentance-liturgy, emphasising Zhēn wǔ's North-Polar cosmic function and his **universal compassion** (*pǔ cí* 普慈) and **world-salvation** (*dù shì* 度世) role. Preserved as **DZ 815** / CT 815. Related to [[KR5c0150]], [[KR5c0171]], [[KR5c0172]], [[KR5c0211]].

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
