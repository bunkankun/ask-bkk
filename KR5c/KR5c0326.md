# Dà huán dān zhào jiàn 大還丹照鑑

*Illumining Mirror of the Great Return-Elixir*

Anonymous; early Northern Sòng

A short **alchemical summary-text** on the **great return-elixir** (*dà huán dān* 大還丹), dated by its preface to 962 (Jiàn lóng 3, Sòng Tài zǔ). Preserved as **DZ 926** / CT 926 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 962 (preface). Dynasty: 北宋.
