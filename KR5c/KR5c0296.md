# Bì yù zhū shā hán lín yù shù guì 碧玉朱砂寒林玉樹匱

*Coffer of the Azure-Jade Cinnabar and Cold-Grove Jade-Tree*

by [[陳大師]] (Chén dà shī) — Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

A Southern-Sòng **alchemical text** by the Daoist "Master Chén" on **azure-jade cinnabar** and the **cold-grove jade-tree** — poetic names for specific alchemical reactants and products. (Note the source file gives the title's last character as 匣 *xiá* rather than 匱 *guì*; both would signify "coffer.") Preserved as **DZ 898** / CT 898 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
