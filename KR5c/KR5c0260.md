# Shén xiān liàn dān diǎn zhù sān yuán bǎo zhào fǎ 神仙鍊丹點鑄三元寶照法

*Dharma of Divine Immortals' Refining, Founding, and Casting the Three Origins' Precious Mirrors*

Anonymous

A Daoist **alchemical-ritual text** on the making of **"precious mirrors"** (*bǎo zhào* 寶照) — ritual implements used in Daoist exorcism and divination. Preserved as **DZ 863** / CT 863.

**Dating.** Undated. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
