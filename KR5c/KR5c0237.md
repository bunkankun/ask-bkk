# Péng Zǔ shè shēng yǎng xìng lùn 彭祖攝生養性論

*Treatise on Regulating Life and Nourishing Nature, by Péng Zǔ*

attributed to [[彭祖]] (Péng Zǔ — legendary Chinese Methuselah figure); Táng composition

A Táng *yǎng shēng* treatise pseudepigraphically attributed to **Péng Zǔ** 彭祖 — the legendary Chinese immortal said to have lived 800 years. Preserved as **DZ 840** / CT 840.

## About the work

### Péng Zǔ

**Péng Zǔ** 彭祖 is the Chinese legendary figure of supreme longevity — according to tradition, a Shāng-dynasty figure who lived 800 years through the perfect cultivation of *yǎng shēng* 養生 practice. His name became a standard allusion for extreme longevity in Chinese literature. The attribution of longevity-manuals to Péng Zǔ is a standard pseudepigraphic convention.

**Dating.** Táng composition. Dynasty: 唐.
