# Tài shàng hùn yuán zhēn lù 太上混元真錄

*Most High Record of the Perfected Mixed-Origin*

Anonymous; Táng

A short **hagiographical record** on the **Hùn yuán** 混元 ("Mixed Origin" = Lǎo jūn, the deified Lǎo zǐ as cosmic origin). Preserved as **DZ 954** / CT 954 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
