# Tài qīng yù bēi zi 太清玉碑子

*Jade-Stele of Great Clarity*

Anonymous; TángSòng

A **Tài qīng 太清** alchemical text in the form of a "jade stele" (*yù bēi* 玉碑) — i.e., short aphoristic sections as if inscribed on stone. Preserved as **DZ 927** / CT 927 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** TángSòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
