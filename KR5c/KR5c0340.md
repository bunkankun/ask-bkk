# Jīn mù wàn líng lùn 金木萬靈論

*Discourse on the Ten-Thousand Numens of Metal and Wood*

attributed to [[葛洪]] (Gě Hóng)

A Táng **alchemical discourse** on the "ten-thousand numens" of **metal and wood** (*jīn mù* 金木) — the Five-Phases correlates paired in alchemical theory — pseudepigraphically attributed to Gě Hóng. Preserved as **DZ 940** / CT 940 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
