# Dà huán xīn jiàn 大還心鑑

*Heart-Mirror of the Great Reversion*

Anonymous; Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties **alchemical summary-text** on the **great-reversion elixir** (*dà huán* 大還 = *dà huán dān* 大還丹), framed as a "heart-mirror" (*xīn jiàn* 心鑑). Preserved as **DZ 936** / CT 936 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
