# Tài shàng Zhèng yī yuè lù yí 太上正一閱籙儀

*Zhèng yī Liturgy for the Examination of Registers*

compiled by [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933)

A simpler Zhèng yī **register-examination liturgy** compiled by Dù Guāngtíng — companion to [[KR5c0193|DZ 796]]. Preserved as **DZ 797** / CT 797 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

A brief register-examination ritual, similar in function to [[KR5c0193]] but shorter. Part of Dù Guāngtíng's systematic compilation of Zhèng yī liturgical-ritual apparatus.

**Dating.** Within Dù Guāngtíng's mature period (900–933). Dynasty: 唐-五代.
