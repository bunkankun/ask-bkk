# Tōng yōu jué 通幽訣

*Instructions for Communicating with the Obscure*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **alchemical-esoteric instruction-text** on "communicating with the obscure" (*tōng yōu* 通幽) — i.e., accessing the hidden ingredients and reactions of the alchemical art. Preserved as **DZ 913** / CT 913 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
