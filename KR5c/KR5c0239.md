# Bào pǔ zǐ yǎng shēng lùn 抱朴子養生論

*Master Bào pǔ's Treatise on Nourishing Life*

pseudepigraphically attributed to [[葛洪]] (Gé Hóng, 283–343) under his *hào* Bào pǔ zǐ 抱朴子; Táng composition

A Táng-era *yǎng shēng* treatise pseudepigraphically attributed to **Gé Hóng** 葛洪 (283–343) — the foundational Eastern-Jìn Daoist-alchemical theorist, author of the celebrated *Bào pǔ zǐ* 抱朴子. Preserved as **DZ 842** / CT 842.

## About the work

### Gé Hóng and his *Bào pǔ zǐ*

**Gé Hóng** 葛洪 (*zì* Zhì chuān 稚川, *hào* Bào pǔ zǐ 抱朴子) — **283–343** — is the foundational theorist of Chinese **outer alchemy** (*wài dān* 外丹) and of the classical **Tài qīng** 太清 Daoist tradition. His *Bào pǔ zǐ* (inner chapters and outer chapters) is one of the most important early medieval Chinese Daoist works.

### Pseudepigraphy

The present short Táng-era *yǎng shēng* treatise is **not an authentic work of Gé Hóng** but a Táng composition under his prestigious authorial attribution — a common practice in the Daoist tradition.

**Dating.** Táng composition. Dynasty: 唐.
