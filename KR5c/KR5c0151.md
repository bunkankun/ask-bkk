# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng jīng zhù 太上老君說常清靜經註 (Lǐ Dàochún)

*Commentary on the Scripture Spoken by the Most High Lord Lǎo on Constant Clarity and Stillness* — Lǐ Dàochún's commentary

by [[李道純]] (Lǐ Dàochún, *hào* Qīng ān 清菴 / Yīng chán zǐ 瑩蟾子; d. 1306)

A **Yuán-era Quán zhēn commentary** on the foundational Daoist meditation-scripture **Cháng qīng jìng jīng** 常清靜經 (DZ 620), by **Lǐ Dàochún** 李道純 (d. 1306). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 755** / CT 755 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類), and in the Qīng *Dàozàng jíyào* as JY055.

## About the work

### The Cháng qīng jìng jīng

The ***Cháng qīng jìng jīng*** 常清靜經 ("Scripture of Constant Clarity and Stillness") is one of the **most widely-recited Daoist meditation-scriptures** — a short scripture (c. 600 characters) attributed to Lǎojūn that teaches the cultivation of interior calm through the contemplation of "empty nature" (*kōng xìng* 空性). The scripture has multiple traditional commentators (see the catalog disambiguator "(一)" distinguishing Lǐ Dàochún's from other *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* commentaries).

### Lǐ Dàochún's commentary

Lǐ Dàochún's commentary is consistent with his broader scholarly programme on the Daoist classics (see [[KR5c0086]] for his *Dàodé huì yuán* and [[李道純]] for his biography). Philosophical features:

1. **Three-level exposition** — characteristic of Lǐ's pedagogical style.
2. ***Nèi dān* 內丹 framework** — reading the scripture through inner-alchemical lenses.
3. **Chán-Buddhist terminology** — integrating Chán *kōng* 空 (emptiness) concepts.
4. **Three Teachings synthesis** — explicit integration of Confucian and Buddhist categories.

### Abstract

The commentary is a **characteristic Yuán Quán zhēn reading** of the foundational Daoist meditation-scripture, preserving Lǐ Dàochún's distinctive synthesis of *nèi dān* alchemy and Chán Buddhist meditation.

**Dating.** Lǐ Dàochún's active period: 1280s–1306. Per the project's dating rule, the frontmatter gives **1290–1306** as a composition window. Dynasty: 元.
