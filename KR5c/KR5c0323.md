# Dà dān qiān gǒng lùn 大丹鉛汞論

*Discourse on the Lead-and-Mercury of the Great Elixir*

by [[金竹坡]] (Jīn Zhúpō) — Sòng Daoist alchemist

A Sòng **alchemical discourse** on **lead and mercury** (*qiān gǒng* 鉛汞) as the twin substrates of the great elixir. Preserved as **DZ 923** / CT 923 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 11th century or later. Dynasty: 宋.
