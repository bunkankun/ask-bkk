# Mì cáng tōng xuán biàn huà liù yīn dòng wēi dùn jiǎ zhēn jīng 祕藏通玄變化六陰洞微遁甲真經

*Secret-Treasury Mystery-Communicating Transformation Six-Yīn Cavern-Subtle Dùn jiǎ True Scripture*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng Daoist scripture of the ***dùn jiǎ*** 遁甲 ("concealment by the *jiǎ*") **divinatory-military tradition** — a major pre-modern Chinese system of military-astrological divination. Preserved as **DZ 857** / CT 857.

## About the work

The **Dùn jiǎ** 遁甲 tradition is one of the three main classical Chinese divinatory-astrological systems (alongside *Tài yǐ* 太乙 and *Liù rén* 六壬). It is used for **military strategy and timing of campaigns** via astrological-cosmological calculation. The Daoist engagement with *dùn jiǎ* in scriptures like DZ 857 represents the integration of military-divinatory technique with Daoist ritual-cosmological framework.

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
