# Fó miè dù hòu guān liǎn zàng sòng jīng 佛滅度後棺斂葬送經
*The Sūtra on the Coffin, Cremation, and Funeral After the Buddha's Parinirvāṇa*
by anonymous translator

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text treats the funerary arrangements for the Buddha after his *parinirvāṇa* — the placement in the coffin, the cremation, and the funeral procession. Together with [[KR6g0036|T0391]] it is part of the *post-Niè-pán* ritual-narrative cycle of Chinese Buddhist texts.
