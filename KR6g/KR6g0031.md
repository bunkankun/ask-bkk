# Zhōng yīn jīng 中陰經
*The Sūtra on the Intermediate State (Antarābhava)*
by [[竺佛念]] (Zhú Fóniàn, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[竺佛念]] Zhú Fóniàn treats the Buddhist doctrine of the *zhōng yīn* 中陰 (Skt. *antarābhava*) — the *intermediate state* between death and rebirth, a controversial topic in Indian Buddhist abhidharma. The Mahāyāna *Zhōng yīn jīng* presents the bodhisattva-perspective on this transitional state.
