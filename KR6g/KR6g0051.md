# Niè pán lùn 涅槃論
*Treatise on Nirvāṇa*
by [[婆藪槃豆]] (Vasubandhu, 作) and [[達磨菩提]] (Dharmabodhi, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the *Nirvāṇa-śāstra* attributed to Vasubandhu, translated by [[達磨菩提]] Dharmabodhi during his Northern-Wèi activity. The work is one of the principal Indian *śāstra*-commentaries on the Mahāyāna *parinirvāṇa* doctrine.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Dharmabodhi's Northern-Wèi period (508 – 535 CE).

The Taishō text (T1527) is established on the standard apparatus.
