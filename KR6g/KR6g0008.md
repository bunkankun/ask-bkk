# Niè pán jīng yóu yì 涅槃經遊意
*Roving Reflections on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[吉藏]] (Jízàng, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[吉藏]] Jí-zàng's compact "roving reflections" (*yóu yì*) on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]], paralleling his [[KR6e0002|*Huāyán yóu yì*]] (T1731) and similar *yóu yì* works on other major Mahāyāna sūtras. The genre is one of Jí-zàng's distinctive literary contributions.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (595 – 623) reflects Jízàng's mature Suí period.
