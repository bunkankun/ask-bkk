# Niè pán jīng yóu yì 涅槃經遊意
*Roving Reflections on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[吉藏]] (Jízàng, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[吉藏]] Jízàng's compact "roving reflections" (*yóu yì*) on *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]), paralleling his *Huāyán yóu yì* 華嚴遊意 ([[KR6e0002]], T1731) and similar *yóu yì* works on other major Mahāyāna sūtras. The genre is one of Jízàng's distinctive literary contributions.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (595–623) reflects Jízàng's mature Suí period.
