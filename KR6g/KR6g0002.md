# Dà bān niè pán jīng yì jì 大般涅槃經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[慧遠]] (Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn / Suí Yuǎn, 述)

## About the work

This 10-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] is by [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn* (523–592). One of the most substantial Suí-period commentaries on the *Niè-pán jīng* and a foundational document of mature Chinese Buddha-nature doctrine.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 592) reflects [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn*'s mature Suí period.

The Taishō text (T1764) is established on the standard apparatus.
