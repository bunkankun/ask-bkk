# Niè pán jīng yì jì 涅槃經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng-preserved text is a commentary on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]]. Preserved in Taishō volume 85 (the *Dūnhuáng yíshū*).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500 – 900) reflects the broad pre-Tang to Tang period of Dūnhuáng-fragment composition.

The Taishō text (T2764A) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript.
