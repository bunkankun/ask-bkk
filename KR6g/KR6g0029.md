# Mó hē mó yē jīng 摩訶摩耶經
*The Mahāmāyā Sūtra*
by [[曇景]] (Tánjǐng, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[曇景]] Tán-jǐng is the *Mahāmāyā-sūtra* — the Mahāyāna sūtra concerning the Buddha's mother *Mahāmāyā* 摩訶摩耶 (Mahā Māyā), narrating the events surrounding the Buddha's parinirvāṇa from Mahāmāyā's perspective in the Trāyastriṃśa heaven and including a famous *prophecy of the decline of the Dharma* (the standard *mò fǎ* 末法 doctrine).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[曇景]] Tánjǐng's XiāoQí period (479 – 502 CE).

The Taishō text (T0383) is established on the standard apparatus.
