# Yí jiào jīng jiě 遺教經解
*Explication of the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra*
by [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the [[KR6g0043|*Yí jiào jīng* T0389]] is by [[智旭]] *Ǒuyì Zhìxù* (1599–1655), one of the *Four Great Masters of the Late Míng*.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1620 – 1655) reflects [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù's mature scholarly period.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X666).
