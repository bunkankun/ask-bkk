# Yí jiào jīng lùn zhù fǎ jì 遺教經論住法記
*Notes on the Abiding-Dharma of the Yí-jiào Sūtra Treatise*
by [[元照]] (Yuánzhào, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle Northern-Sòng sub-commentary on [[KR6g0044|the *Yí jiào jīng lùn* T1529]] is by [[元照]] Yuánzhào (1048–1116), the great Northern-Sòng Vinaya-school master.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1080 – 1116) reflects Yuánzhào's mature scholarly period.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X845).
