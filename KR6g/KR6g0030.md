# Pútísà cóng dōu shù tiān jiàng shén mǔ tāi shuō guǎng pǔ jīng 菩薩從兜術天降神母胎說廣普經
*The Sūtra of the Bodhisattva's Descent from Tuṣita and Entry into the Mother's Womb, with the Universal Discourse*
by [[竺佛念]] (Zhú Fóniàn, 譯)

## About the work

This 7-fascicle text by [[竺佛念]] Zhú Fóniàn is a substantial Mahāyāna sūtra on the bodhisattva's *Tuṣita-descent* and *womb-entry* — the cosmological prelude to the Buddha's birth — combined with a *universal discourse* (*guǎng pǔ* 廣普) on the bodhisattva path.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[竺佛念]] Zhú Fóniàn's Yáo-Qín period (365 – 411 CE).

The Taishō text (T0384) is established on the standard apparatus.
