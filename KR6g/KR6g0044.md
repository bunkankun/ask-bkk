# Yí jiào jīng lùn 遺教經論
*Treatise on the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra*
by [[天親菩薩]] (Vasubandhu, 造) and [[真諦]] (Paramārtha, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the *Vasubandhu* commentary on the [[KR6g0043|*Yí jiào jīng* T0389]], translated by [[真諦]] Paramārtha during his Chén-period activity. The work is the principal Indian śāstra-commentary on the Buddha's *bequeathed teaching*.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[真諦]] Paramārtha's Chén period (557 – 569 CE).

The Taishō text (T1529) is established on the standard apparatus.
