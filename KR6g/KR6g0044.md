# Yí jiào jīng lùn 遺教經論
*Treatise on the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra*
by [[天親菩薩]] (Vasubandhu, 造) and [[真諦]] (Paramārtha, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the [[天親菩薩]] Vasubandhu commentary on the *Yí jiào jīng* 遺教經 ([[KR6g0043]], T0389), translated by [[真諦]] Paramārtha during his Chén-period activity. The work is the principal Indian śāstra-commentary on the Buddha's *bequeathed teaching*.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Paramārtha's Chén period (557–569 CE).
