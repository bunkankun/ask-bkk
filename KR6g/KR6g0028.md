# Jí yī qiè fú dé sānmèi jīng 集一切福德三昧經
*The Sūtra on the Samādhi of Gathering All Merit (Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi)*
by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text by [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva is the second Chinese version of the *Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi-sūtra*, parallel to [[KR6g0027|Zhú Fǎhù's earlier T0381]].

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān period (402 – 412 CE). The Taishō text (T0382) is established on the standard apparatus.
