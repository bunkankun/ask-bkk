# Niè pán jīng huì shū jiě 涅槃經會疏解
*Explication of the Combined Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[師正]] (Shī-zhèng, 分科) and 圓澄 (Yuán-chéng, 會疏)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle Yuán-period composite work combines [[師正]] Shī-zhèng's sectional outline with 圓澄 Yuán-chéng's *huì shū* commentary. The "圓澄" here is most likely the late-Míng / Qīng Yúnmén-school Chán master *Hānshān Yuán-chéng* 雲門圓澄 (1561–1626) or his Japanese namesake; the precise identification is uncertain.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1280 – 1368) reflects the Yuán dynasty period.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X664).
