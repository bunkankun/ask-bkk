# Jiā yè fù fó bān niè pán jīng 迦葉赴佛般涅槃經
*The Sūtra of Mahākāśyapa Hastening to the Buddha's Parinirvāṇa*
by [[竺曇無蘭]] (Zhú Tánwúlán, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺曇無蘭]] Zhú Tán-wú-lán treats the famous narrative of *Mahākāśyapa* hastening from his retreat to the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa*-site (the *Kuśinagara*) — a foundational episode of the post-*parinirvāṇa* sangha-history.
