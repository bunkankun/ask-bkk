# Fó chuí bān niè pán lüè shuō jiào jiè jīng 佛垂般涅槃略說教誡經
*The Sūtra of the Buddha's Brief Teaching-Admonition Just Before Parinirvāṇa* (commonly known as *Yí jiào jīng* 遺教經, "Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra")
by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

The *Yí jiào jīng* in 1 fascicle is one of the most beloved short Buddhist texts in the East Asian tradition: the Buddha's final teaching-admonition (*jiào jiè* 教誡) delivered immediately before his *parinirvāṇa*, gathering together the essential disciplinary instructions for the *saṅgha*. The work was widely chanted in monastic establishments and was the subject of numerous commentaries, including Vasubandhu's [[KR6g0044|*Yí jiào jīng lùn*]] (T1529).

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān period (402 – 412 CE).

The Taishō text (T0389) is established on the standard apparatus.

## Other points of interest

- The *Yí jiào jīng* is one of the few short Buddhist texts to be regularly recited in daily monastic liturgy across East Asian Buddhism, alongside the *Heart Sūtra*, the *Pǔ-xián xíng-yuàn pǐn*, and the *Smaller Sukhāvatī*.
