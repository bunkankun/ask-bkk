# Fó yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào 佛遺教經論疏節要
*Essential Abridgement of the Commentary on the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra Treatise*
by [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 節要) and [[袾宏]] (Zhū-hóng, 補註)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle composite work combines [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's Northern-Sòng abridgement (*jié yào* 節要) of [[KR6g0044|the *Yí jiào jīng lùn* commentarial tradition]] with [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhū-hóng's late-Míng supplementary annotation (*bǔ zhù* 補註).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067 – 1615) reflects the period from Jìngyuán's Northern-Sòng activity to Zhū-hóng's late-Míng supplementary work.

The Taishō text (T1820) is established on the standard apparatus.
