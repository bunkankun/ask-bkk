# Dà bān niè pán jīng jí jiě 大般涅槃經集解
*Compiled Commentary on the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[寶亮]] (Bǎo-liàng *et al.*, 等集)

## About the work

The *Jí jiě* in 71 fascicles is the great Liáng-period compiled commentary on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]], drawing together the work of multiple earlier *Niè-pán*-school authorities into a single comprehensive commentary. The 71-fascicle scope makes it one of the largest single commentaries in pre-Tang Chinese Buddhism.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (488 – 509) reflects [[寶亮]] Bǎo-liàng's mature Liáng-period activity. The work is preserved in the standard apparatus including the *yuán* 原, *jiǎ* 甲, and *Shèng* 聖 witnesses.
