# Fó yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào 佛遺教經論疏節要 (Yongle Northern Canon ed.)
*Essential Abridgement of the Commentary on the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra Treatise* — Yǒnglè Northern-Canon edition
by [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the Northern-Yǒnglè-canon edition of [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's *Yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào*, paralleling [[KR6g0045|the Taishō version]] T1820 but with editorial differences. Both versions document the Northern-Sòng-onwards commentarial tradition on the *Yí jiào jīng*.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067 – 1088) reflects [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's mature scholarly activity.

The work is preserved in the Northern Yǒnglè edition of the Buddhist canon (B1590).
