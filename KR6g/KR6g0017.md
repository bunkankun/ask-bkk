# Niè pán jīng shū sī jì 涅槃經疏私記 (Xíng-mǎn)
*Private Notes on the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[行滿]] (Xíng-mǎn, 集)

## About the work

This 12-fascicle Tang Tiāntái-school sub-commentary on [[灌頂]] Guàn-dǐng's [[KR6g0007|*Niè-pán jīng shū*]] (T1767) is by [[行滿]] Xíng-mǎn. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X660).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (766 – 800) reflects Xíng-mǎn's mid-Tang activity. The work parallels [[KR6g0018|Dào-xiān's *Sī jì*]] (X661).
