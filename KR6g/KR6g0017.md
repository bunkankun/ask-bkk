# Niè pán jīng shū sī jì 涅槃經疏私記 (Xíngmǎn)
*Private Notes on the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[行滿]] (Xíngmǎn, 集)

## About the work

This 12-fascicle Tang Tiāntái-school sub-commentary on [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's [[KR6g0007|*Nièpán jīng shū*]] (T1767) is by [[行滿]] Xíngmǎn. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X660).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (766 – 800) reflects Xíngmǎn's mid-Tang activity. The work parallels [[KR6g0018|Dàoxiān's *Sī jì*]] (X661).
