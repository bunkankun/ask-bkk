# Dà niè pán jīng yì jì juàn dì sì 大涅槃經義記卷第四
*Notes on the Meaning of the Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra, Fascicle Four* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng-preserved fragment preserves only the fourth fascicle of a larger lost commentary on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]]. Companion to T2764A.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500 – 900) reflects the broad Dūnhuáng-fragment composition period.

The Taishō text (T2764B) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript.
