# Niè pán jīng xuán yì wén jù 涅槃經玄義文句
*Profound Meaning and Sectional Notes on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàn-dǐng, 撰), [[道暹]] (Dào-xiān, 撰), [[守篤]] (Shutoku, 分會)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle composite work combines [[灌頂]] Guàn-dǐng's *Xuán yì* (paralleling [[KR6g0005|T1765]]) with [[道暹]] Dào-xiān's Tang-period *Wén jù*, edited and combined by the Japanese scholar [[守篤]] Shutoku. The work documents the Tiāntái-school *Niè-pán* commentary tradition across three centuries and three countries.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (597 – 1700) reflects the maximum window from Guàn-dǐng's mature period to the Edo-Japanese editorial work.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X656).
