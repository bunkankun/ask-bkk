# Děng jí zhòng dé sānmèi jīng 等集眾德三昧經
*The Sūtra on the Samādhi of Equal Gathering of Many Virtues*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù, 譯)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle Mahāyāna *samādhi*-sūtra by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù treats the meditative state of *gathering many virtues equally* (*děng jí zhòng dé* 等集眾德 — the *Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi*).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE). The work is paralleled by [[KR6g0028|Kumārajīva's *Jí yī qiè fú dé sānmèi jīng*]] (T0382).

The Taishō text (T0381) is established on the standard apparatus.
