# Niè pán zōng yào 涅槃宗要
*The Essential Doctrinal Lineage of the Nirvāṇa [Sūtra]*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle compendium by [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo treats the doctrinal essence (*zōng yào*) of the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] — paralleling Wǒnhyo's [[KR6f0067|*Liǎng juàn wú liàng shòu jīng zōng yào*]] (T1747) and other *zōng yào* compendia.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (660 – 686) reflects Wǒnhyo's mature Silla period.

The Taishō text (T1769) is established on the standard apparatus.
