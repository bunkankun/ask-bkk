# Yí jiào jīng bǔ zhù 遺教經補註
*Supplementarily-Annotated Yí-jiào Sūtra*
by [[守遂]] (Shǒu-suì, 註) and [[了童]] (Liǎo-tóng, 補註)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle composite text combines [[守遂]] Shǒu-suì's Sòng-period annotation of the [[KR6g0043|*Yí jiào jīng* T0389]] with [[了童]] Liǎo-tóng's Míng-period supplementary annotation.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1100 – 1644) reflects the maximum window from Shǒu-suì's Sòng activity to Liǎo-tóng's Míng supplementary work.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X665).
