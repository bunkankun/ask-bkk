# Yí jiào jīng bǔ zhù 遺教經補註
*Supplementarily-Annotated Yí-jiào Sūtra*
by [[守遂]] (Shǒusuì, 註) and [[了童]] (Liǎotóng, 補註)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle composite text combines [[守遂]] Shǒusuì's Sòng-period annotation of the [[KR6g0043|*Yí jiào jīng* T0389]] with [[了童]] Liǎotóng's Míng-period supplementary annotation.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1100 – 1644) reflects the maximum window from Shǒusuì's Sòng activity to Liǎotóng's Míng supplementary work.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X665).
