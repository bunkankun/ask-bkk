# Dà bān niè pán jīng hòu fēn 大般涅槃經後分
*The Latter Section of the Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[若那跋陀羅]] (Jñānabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[若那跋陀羅]] Jñānabhadra is the *latter section* of the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* — additional Indic material continuing the narrative of the Buddha's last discourses beyond what is contained in [[KR6g0001|Dharmakṣema's *Northern Recension*]] (T0374). The text provides further material on the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* and the disposition of his relics.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Jñānabhadra's Tang activity (660 – 700 CE).

The Taishō text (T0377) is established on the standard apparatus.
