# Niè pán jīng huì shū 涅槃經會疏
*Combined Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàn-dǐng, 撰), [[湛然]] (Zhànrán, 再治), [[本純]] (Honjun, 分會)

## About the work

This 36-fascicle composite work combines [[灌頂]] Guàn-dǐng's [[KR6g0007|*Niè-pán jīng shū*]] with [[湛然]] Zhànrán's Tang-period editorial revision (*zài zhì* 再治) and the Japanese editorial *fēn huì* of Honjun. The 36-fascicle scope matches the [[KR6g0003|*Southern Recension*]] of the parent sūtra and provides line-by-line interlinear commentary.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (597 – 1700) reflects the maximum window from Guàn-dǐng's mature period to the Edo-Japanese editorial work.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X659).
