# Fó shuō jiù hù shēn mìng jīng 佛說救護身命經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Saving and Protecting Bodily Life*
by [[比丘道真]] (Bǐqiū Dàozhēn, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[比丘道真]] Dàozhēn is preserved in the Fángshān stone-canon. The work is a brief Buddhist devotional text on bodily protection.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (600 – 1000) reflects the broad period of Fángshān stone-canon production.

The work is preserved in the *Fángshān shí jīng* 房山石經 collection (F248).
