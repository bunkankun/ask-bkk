# Niè pán jīng běn yǒu jīn wú jì lùn 涅槃經本有今無偈論
*Treatise on the "Originally-Existed-Now-Non-Existent" Verse of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[天親菩薩]] (Vasubandhu, 造) and [[真諦]] (Paramārtha, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the Vasubandhu commentary on the famous *běn yǒu jīn wú* 本有今無 verse of the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] — the doctrinal crux concerning the relation of *original existence* (*běn yǒu*) and *present non-existence* (*jīn wú*) of phenomenal beings, central to mature Buddha-nature doctrine. Translated by [[真諦]] Paramārtha during his Chén-period activity.
