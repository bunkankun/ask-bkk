# Bān ní huán hòu guàn là jīng 般泥洹後灌臘經
*The Sūtra on the Ablution-and-Sacrifice After the Buddha's Parinirvāṇa*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù treats the post-*parinirvāṇa* ritual practices of *guàn là* 灌臘 — the ablution and sacrificial offerings to the Buddha's relics. The work is one of the early Chinese Buddhist sources for relic-cult practice.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE).

The Taishō text (T0391) is established on the standard apparatus.
