# Niè pán jīng zhì dìng shū kē 涅槃經治定疏科
*Editorially-Established Sectional Outline of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra Commentary*
by [[智圓]] (Zhì-yuán, 撰)

## About the work

This 10-fascicle text by [[智圓]] Zhì-yuán is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) for [[灌頂]] Guàn-dǐng's [[KR6g0007|*Niè-pán jīng shū*]] (T1767). At 10 fascicles, the *kē* itself is one of the largest in the East Asian Buddhist tradition, reflecting the parent commentary's substantial scope.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (990 – 1022) reflects [[智圓]] Zhì-yuán's mature scholarly period.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X657).
