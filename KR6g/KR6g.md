# Niè-pán bù 涅槃部 — Nirvāṇa Section

## Scope and scholarly tradition

`KR6g` is the Kanripo mirror of the Taishō *Niè-pán bù* 涅槃部 — the canonical division that gathers the Mahāyāna *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* (MPNS) corpus in its full transmissional and exegetical extent. In CANWWW the division is `div19`; the Taishō core runs from `T12N0374` (Dharmakṣema's 40-fascicle Northern Recension) through `T12N0396` and is preceded in the canon's architecture by the *Bǎojī bù* 寶積部 ([[KR6f]], `div03`). `KR6g` extends the Taishō *Niè-pán bù* by absorbing the entire commentarial superstructure attached to the *Niè-pán jīng* and to its short companion-text the *Yí jiào jīng* 遺教經: the *jīng-shū bù* Tiāntái cluster from Taishō volumes 37–38 (T1763–T1769), the Dūnhuáng MPNS-commentary fragments from volume 85 (T2764A, T2764B, T2765), the Indian *śāstra* layer on the *Yí jiào jīng* and on the *Niè-pán jīng*'s doctrinal cruxes from volume 26 (T1527–T1529), the *Yí-jiào-jīng-lùn shū* tradition from volume 40 (T1820), the *Manji Xù-zàng-jīng* commentaries on the MPNS and on the *Yí jiào jīng* (X656–X666, X845–X846), and a Northern Yongle-canon witness — 52 works in total.

The chronological span runs from Dharmarakṣa's 竺法護 late-third-century Western-Jìn translations of pre-MPNS parinirvāṇa material ([[KR6g0024]] *Fāng-děng bān ní huán jīng* of c. 266–313, [[KR6g0036]] *Bān ní huán hòu guàn là jīng*) through to the mid-seventeenth-century late-Ming commentary of Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù 蕅益智旭 ([[KR6g0048]] *Yí-jiào-jīng jiě* of 1620–1655), with the active Indic-translation phase concentrated in the fifth century (Dharmakṣema, Fǎxiǎn, the Huì-yán / Huì-guān editorial team) and the active East-Asian commentarial phase running from the Liáng compiled commentary of Bǎo-liàng 寶亮 ([[KR6g0004]] of c. 488–509) through the late-Míng synthesis. The principal transmissional traditions a reader must keep in mind are five: (i) the late-third- / early-fourth-century pre-MPNS translation lineage of Dharmarakṣa 竺法護 and Zhú Fóniàn 竺佛念 in Cháng-ān, whose short *bān-ní-huán* texts established the basic Chinese terminology for the Buddha's death-scene; (ii) the early-fifth-century Northern Liáng / Eastern Jìn translation lineage of Dharmakṣema 曇無讖 and Fǎxiǎn 法顯, which produced the two definitive Chinese MPNS recensions (long 40-fasc. and short 6-fasc.) within the same decade; (iii) the Liú-Sòng editorial lineage of Huì-yán 慧嚴 and Huì-guān 慧觀 at Jiànkāng, which produced the harmonised *Southern Recension* (南本 *Nán-běn*) [[KR6g0003]] that became the dominant text in southern monastic establishments; (iv) the Suí–Táng Tiāntái commentarial lineage of Guàn-dǐng 灌頂 ([[KR6g0005]], [[KR6g0007]]) → Dào-xiān 道暹 / Xíng-mǎn 行滿 ([[KR6g0017]]–[[KR6g0018]]) → Zhàn-rán 湛然 ([[KR6g0016]]) → Zhì-yuán 智圓 ([[KR6g0006]], [[KR6g0014]], [[KR6g0019]]), which constituted the canonical scholastic apparatus on the *Niè-pán jīng*; and (v) the Sòng–Míng *Yí jiào jīng* commentarial tradition of Jìng-yuán 淨源, Shǒu-suì 守遂, Yuán-zhào 元照, Guān-fù 觀復 and Zhì-xù 智旭, which made Kumārajīva's [[KR6g0043]] one-fascicle *Yí jiào jīng* one of the most-commented short Buddhist texts in East Asia. The division is therefore the principal canonical arena for the Mahāyāna *tathāgatagarbha* / Buddha-nature (*fó-xìng* 佛性) doctrine and for the literature of the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* in all its scriptural, narrative and ritual dimensions.

## Important texts and text clusters

### The Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra and its recensions

- *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 [[KR6g0001]] — Dharmakṣema 曇無讖's 40-fascicle ***Northern Recension*** (*Běi-běn* 北本, T0374, 414–421); the great Mahāyāna *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* and the principal scriptural source for the doctrine of universal Buddha-nature (*fó xìng* 佛性), including the controversial inclusion of *icchantikas* (一闡提 *yī-chǎn-tí*). Together with the *Avataṃsaka* ([[KR6e0001]]) and the *Lotus*, it is one of the three foundational *yuán-jiào* / "complete teaching" sūtras of East Asian Mahāyāna.
- *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 (*Southern Recension*) [[KR6g0003]] — Huì-yán 慧嚴, Huì-guān 慧觀 *et al.*'s 36-fascicle ***Southern Recension*** (*Nán-běn* 南本, T0375, 433–443); the Liú-Sòng editorial revision of Dharmakṣema's text, harmonised with Fǎxiǎn / Buddhabhadra's earlier short translation and re-divided into 25 chapters. The *Nán-běn* / *Běi-běn* doublet became the canonical East-Asian MPNS corpus, with the *Nán-běn* preferred in the south and the *Běi-běn* in the north.
- *Fó shuō dà bān ní huán jīng* 佛說大般泥洹經 [[KR6g0022]] — Fǎxiǎn 法顯's 6-fascicle ***short / pre-MPNS recension*** (T0376, 416–418), brought back from India by Fǎxiǎn on his great pilgrimage and translated in collaboration with Buddhabhadra at the Dào-chǎng-sì 道場寺 in Jiànkāng. Predating the full Dharmakṣema translation by a few years, it was the textual basis for Dào-shēng 道生's pre-emptive *Buddha-nature* doctrinal stance.
- *Dà bān niè pán jīng hòu fēn* 大般涅槃經後分 [[KR6g0023]] — Jñānabhadra 若那跋陀羅's 2-fascicle ***Latter Section*** (T0377, 660–700); the Táng supplementary continuation of the MPNS narrating the Buddha's actual *parinirvāṇa* and post-cremation events, the canonical capstone of the corpus.
- *Fó shuō fāng-děng bān ní huán jīng* 佛說方等般泥洹經 [[KR6g0024]] (Dharmarakṣa 竺法護, W. Jìn) and *Bān ní huán hòu guàn là jīng* 般泥洹後灌臘經 [[KR6g0036]] (Dharmarakṣa) — the **earliest Chinese parinirvāṇa material**, predating the full MPNS tradition; provide the basic vocabulary (*bān ní huán* 般泥洹 / *bān niè pán* 般涅槃) and the ritual frame (*guàn là* — ablution-and-sacrifice) for the later corpus.

### Major MPNS commentaries (Taishō *jīng-shū bù*)

- *Dà bān niè pán jīng jí jiě* 大般涅槃經集解 [[KR6g0004]] — Bǎo-liàng 寶亮 *et al.*'s 71-fascicle Liáng-period **compiled commentary** (T1763, c. 488–509), gathering the work of multiple Niè-pán-school authorities into a single comprehensive apparatus; one of the largest single commentaries in pre-Táng Chinese Buddhism.
- *Dà bān niè pán jīng yì jì* 大般涅槃經義記 [[KR6g0002]] — Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn 淨影慧遠's 10-fascicle Suí commentary (T1764, 580–592); the foundational *Dìlùn*-school reading and one of the principal Suí scholastic statements of Buddha-nature doctrine.
- **Tiāntái MPNS commentaries by Guàn-dǐng 灌頂** — [[KR6g0005]] *Xuán yì* 玄義 (2 fasc., T1765, doctrinal preamble) and [[KR6g0007]] *Shū* 疏 (33 fasc., T1767, full sectional commentary) of c. 597–632; the canonical Tiāntái apparatus on the *Niè-pán jīng*, paralleling Zhì-yǐ's *Xuán-yì* / *Wén-jù* / *Zhǐ-guān* triad on the *Lotus*.
- *Niè pán jīng yóu yì* 涅槃經遊意 [[KR6g0008]] — Jí-zàng 吉藏's 1-fascicle Sānlùn-school "roving reflections" (T1768, 595–623); the Sānlùn alternative to the Tiāntái synthesis.
- *Niè pán zōng yào* 涅槃宗要 [[KR6g0009]] — Wǒnhyo 元曉's Sillan compendium on the doctrinal essence of the MPNS (T1769, 660–686); paralleling his [[KR6f0067]] *Zōng-yào* on the *Larger Sukhāvatī*.
- *Niè pán xuán yì fā yuán jī yào* 涅槃玄義發源機要 [[KR6g0006]] — Zhì-yuán 智圓's Northern-Sòng sub-commentary on Guàn-dǐng's *Xuán yì* (T1766, 990–1022); the Sòng *Shānwài* 山外 Tiāntái reading.
- **Dūnhuáng MPNS-commentary fragments** — [[KR6g0010]] *Niè-pán-jīng yì jì* (T2764A), [[KR6g0011]] *Dà-niè-pán-jīng yì jì juàn dì sì* (T2764B), [[KR6g0012]] *Niè-pán-jīng shū* (T2765); the Táng-period anonymous Dūnhuáng witnesses to the otherwise-lost early MPNS-commentary tradition.

### MPNS commentaries in the *Manji Xù-zàng-jīng*

- *Niè pán jīng huì shū* 涅槃經會疏 [[KR6g0016]] — the 36-fascicle composite "combined commentary" assembling Guàn-dǐng's *Shū* with Zhàn-rán's 湛然 Táng editorial revision and Honjun's 本純 Edo-Japanese editorial *fēn huì* (X659); the standard Japanese-Tendai working text on the MPNS. Its prolegomenon [[KR6g0013]] *Xuán yì wén jù* (X656) and its outline [[KR6g0015]] *Tiáo gě* (X658) form a single editorial complex.
- **Sòng Tiāntái sub-commentaries on Guàn-dǐng's *Shū*** — [[KR6g0014]] *Zhì-dìng shū kē* 治定疏科 (10 fasc., X657, structural outline), [[KR6g0019]] *Sān dé zhǐ guī* 三德指歸 (19 fasc., X662, doctrinal sub-commentary) — both by Zhì-yuán 智圓 of the Northern Sòng. Together they constitute the great *Shānwài*-school 山外 apparatus on the MPNS-commentary tradition.
- **Táng Tiāntái sub-commentaries (*sī jì* 私記)** — [[KR6g0017]] *Sī jì* of Xíng-mǎn 行滿 (12 fasc., X660) and [[KR6g0018]] *Sī jì* of Dào-xiān 道暹 (9 fasc., X661); the mid-Táng sub-commentarial layer between Guàn-dǐng and Zhàn-rán.
- **Yuán editorial works on the *Nán-běn*** — [[KR6g0020]] *Kē Nán-běn Niè-pán-jīng* 科南本涅槃經 (Shī-zhèng 師正, X663) and [[KR6g0021]] *Niè-pán jīng huì shū jiě* 涅槃經會疏解 (Shī-zhèng + Yuán-chéng 圓澄, X664); structural-outline and explication of the Southern Recension and its commentary tradition.

### Related Mahāyāna sūtras (T12 0379–0388)

The Taishō editors grouped a cluster of independent Mahāyāna sūtras around the MPNS on grounds of doctrinal and narrative affinity — *samādhi*-doctrine, Mahāmāyā / Buddha-mother literature, *mò-fǎ* prophecy, *antarābhava* (intermediate state):

- *Sì tóng zǐ sānmèi jīng* 四童子三昧經 [[KR6g0025]] — Jñānagupta 闍那崛多 (Suí, T0379), Mahāyāna *samādhi*-sūtra.
- *Dà bēi jīng* 大悲經 [[KR6g0026]] — Narendrayaśas 那連提耶舍 (N. Qí, T0380), the *Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka-sūtra*.
- *Děng jí zhòng dé sānmèi jīng* 等集眾德三昧經 [[KR6g0027]] — Dharmarakṣa (W. Jìn, T0381), another *samādhi* sūtra; paralleled by [[KR6g0028]] *Jí yī-qiè fú-dé sānmèi jīng* (Kumārajīva 鳩摩羅什, Yáo-Qín, T0382, *Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi*).
- *Mó hē mó yē jīng* 摩訶摩耶經 [[KR6g0029]] — Tán-jǐng 曇景 (Xiāo-Qí, T0383), the *Mahāmāyā-sūtra* narrating the Buddha's parinirvāṇa from the perspective of his mother Mahā-māyā in the Trāyastriṃśa heaven; contains the standard *mò-fǎ* 末法 prophecy of the decline of the Dharma.
- *Pú-sà cóng dōu-shù-tiān jiàng shén mǔ-tāi shuō guǎng pǔ jīng* 菩薩從兜術天降神母胎說廣普經 [[KR6g0030]] — Zhú Fóniàn 竺佛念 (Yáo-Qín, T0384), the "descent from Tuṣita" sūtra. Paralleled by [[KR6g0031]] *Zhōng yīn jīng* 中陰經 (Zhú Fóniàn, T0385), the principal Chinese sūtra on the *antarābhava* / intermediate state.
- *Lián huā miàn jīng* 蓮華面經 [[KR6g0032]] — Narendrayaśas (Suí, T0386), prophecy of the destruction of the Dharma by King Mihirakula.
- *Dà fāng-děng wú-xiǎng jīng* 大方等無想經 [[KR6g0033]] — Dharmakṣema (N. Liáng, T0387) — the famous *Dà yún jīng* 大雲經 / *Mahāmegha-sūtra*, politically consequential in Táng history: a re-translation / forgery of it was used by Empress Wǔ Zé-tiān 武則天 in 690 to justify her usurpation, on the basis that the *Dà yún jīng* prophesies the appearance of a female cakravartin queen. Followed by the fragmentary [[KR6g0034]] *Dà yún wú xiǎng jīng juàn dì jiǔ* (T0388).

### Buddha-parinirvāṇa narrative and ritual short sūtras (T390–T396)

A cluster of short anonymous and attributed sūtras transmitting the narrative and ritual surround of the Buddha's death-scene; the textual basis for monastic memorial liturgy:

- *Fó lín niè pán jì fǎ zhù jīng* 佛臨涅槃記法住經 [[KR6g0035]] — Xuán-zàng 玄奘 (Táng, T0390); on the *abiding of the Dharma* (*fǎ zhù*) after the Buddha's death.
- *Fó miè dù hòu guān liǎn zàng sòng jīng* 佛滅度後棺斂葬送經 [[KR6g0037]] (anonymous, T0392) — on the coffining, cremation and funeral.
- *Jiā-yè fù fó bān niè pán jīng* 迦葉赴佛般涅槃經 [[KR6g0038]] — Zhú Tán-wú-lán 竺曇無蘭 (E. Jìn, T0393), Mahākāśyapa's hastening to the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa*.
- *Fó rù niè pán mì-jì jīn-gāng lì-shì āi-liàn jīng* 佛入涅槃密迹金剛力士哀戀經 [[KR6g0039]] (anonymous, T0394) — Vajrapāṇi's lamentation.
- *Fó shuō dāng lái biàn jīng* 佛說當來變經 [[KR6g0040]] — Dharmarakṣa (W. Jìn, T0395), prophecy of coming changes.
- *Fó shuō fǎ miè jìn jīng* 佛說法滅盡經 [[KR6g0041]] (anonymous, T0396) — prophecy of the extinction of the Dharma; together with [[KR6g0029]] and [[KR6g0032]] one of the principal *mò-fǎ* scriptural sources.
- *Fó shuō jiù hù shēn mìng jīng* 佛說救護身命經 [[KR6g0042]] — Bǐ-qiū Dào-zhēn 比丘道真 (Fáng-shān 房山 *Shíjīng*) — a Fáng-shān stone-canon witness to a protective-incantation text grouped here on narrative grounds.

### The *Yí jiào jīng* cluster

The *Yí jiào jīng* 遺教經 — Kumārajīva's one-fascicle rendering of the Buddha's last admonition — is the single most-chanted short Buddhist text in East Asian monastic life, and the principal site of *vinaya*-themed exhortation outside the *Vinaya-piṭaka* proper:

- *Fó chuí bān niè pán lüè shuō jiào jiè jīng* 佛垂般涅槃略說教誡經 [[KR6g0043]] — Kumārajīva 鳩摩羅什 (Yáo-Qín, T0389, 402–412); the foundational *Yí jiào jīng*. The Buddha's final disciplinary admonition delivered immediately before *parinirvāṇa*, gathering the essential instructions for the *saṅgha*.
- *Yí jiào jīng lùn* 遺教經論 [[KR6g0044]] — Vasubandhu 天親 / Paramārtha 真諦 (Chén, T1529, 557–569); the Indian *śāstra*-commentary on the *Yí jiào jīng*.
- **Sòng–Míng *Yí-jiào-jīng-lùn* commentaries** — [[KR6g0045]] Jìng-yuán 淨源's *Lùn shū jié yào* 論疏節要 (T1820) with Zhū-hóng 袾宏's Míng supplementary annotation; [[KR6g0046]] Jìng-yuán's Yongle-canon recension of the same work; [[KR6g0049]] Yuán-zhào 元照's *Zhù fǎ jì* 住法記 (X845); [[KR6g0050]] Guān-fù 觀復's *Lùn jì* 論記 (X846). Together they constitute the Sòng-period *Yí-jiào-jīng-lùn* sub-commentarial corpus.
- **Sòng–Míng commentaries on the *Yí jiào jīng* itself** — [[KR6g0047]] *Bǔ zhù* 補註 of Shǒu-suì 守遂 with Liǎo-tóng 了童's Míng supplementary annotation (X665); [[KR6g0048]] *Yí jiào jīng jiě* 遺教經解 of Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù 蕅益智旭 (X666, 1620–1655), the canonical late-Míng commentary by one of the *Four Great Masters of the Late Míng*.

### *Śāstras* on the *Niè-pán jīng* doctrine

- *Niè pán lùn* 涅槃論 [[KR6g0051]] — Vasubandhu 婆藪槃豆 / Dharmabodhi 達磨菩提 (N. Wèi, T1527, 508–535); the Indian *Nirvāṇa-śāstra* on Mahāyāna *parinirvāṇa* doctrine.
- *Niè pán jīng běn yǒu jīn wú jì lùn* 涅槃經本有今無偈論 [[KR6g0052]] — Vasubandhu / Paramārtha (Chén, T1528, 557–569); the Indian commentary on the famous *běn yǒu jīn wú* 本有今無 ("originally existed, now non-existent") verse of the MPNS, doctrinally central to mature Buddha-nature theology.

## Important persons

- [[曇無讖]] (Dharmakṣema, 385–433) — Central-Indian master at the Northern-Liáng court of Gū-zàng 姑臧; translator of [[KR6g0001]] the 40-fascicle *Northern Recension* MPNS (414–421), the foundational text of the division and one of the most consequential Mahāyāna scriptures in East Asia. Also responsible for [[KR6g0033]] the politically-consequential *Dà yún jīng*. Killed at the orders of Mèngxùn 蒙遜 in 433.
- [[法顯]] (Fǎxiǎn, 337–422) — Eastern-Jìn pilgrim-monk, the first Chinese Buddhist to bring back an Indian text of the MPNS; translator (with Buddhabhadra) of [[KR6g0022]] the short pre-MPNS recension at Jiànkāng's Dào-chǎng-sì 道場寺. His *Fó-guó jì* 佛國記 is the foundational pilgrimage record of Chinese Buddhism.
- [[慧嚴]] (Huì-yán, 363–443) — Liú-Sòng monk; principal editor (with Huì-guān 慧觀) of [[KR6g0003]] the 36-fascicle *Southern Recension* MPNS, the harmonised Liú-Sòng working text that became dominant in southern monastic establishments.
- [[寶亮]] (Bǎo-liàng, 444–509) — Liáng-period master; principal compiler of [[KR6g0004]] the 71-fascicle *Niè-pán-jīng jí jiě*, the great Liáng compiled commentary that gathered the work of multiple earlier Niè-pán-school authorities.
- [[慧遠]] (Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn 淨影慧遠, 523–592) — Suí *Dìlùn*-school exegete (not to be confused with the Lúshān Huì-yuǎn of the Eastern Jìn); author of [[KR6g0002]] *Dà bān niè pán jīng yì jì*, the foundational Suí scholastic reading of the MPNS, alongside his commentaries on the *Larger Sukhāvatī* ([[KR6f0065]]), *Visualization Sūtra* ([[KR6f0072]]) and *Śrīmālā* ([[KR6f0057]]) in `KR6f`.
- [[灌頂]] (Guàn-dǐng / Zhāng-ān 章安, 561–632) — principal disciple of Zhì-yǐ 智顗 and second patriarch of the Tiāntái 天台 school; author of the canonical Tiāntái MPNS apparatus [[KR6g0005]] *Xuán yì*, [[KR6g0007]] *Shū* (33 fasc.) and the composite [[KR6g0013]] / [[KR6g0016]] works. His commentaries are the doctrinal pivot of the Tiāntái reading of the *Niè-pán jīng*.
- [[吉藏]] (Jí-zàng, 549–623) — Sānlùn 三論 patriarch; author of [[KR6g0008]] *Niè pán jīng yóu yì*, the Sānlùn-school alternative to the Tiāntái synthesis. The *yóu yì* genre is one of his distinctive contributions across the Mahāyāna sūtra corpus.
- [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 617–686) — Sillan master; author of [[KR6g0009]] *Niè pán zōng yào*, parallel to his *Wú-liàng-shòu-jīng zōng yào* ([[KR6f0067]]) and other *zōng yào* compendia — the principal Sillan contribution to the division.
- [[湛然]] (Zhàn-rán / Jīng-xī 荊溪, 711–782) — sixth Tiāntái patriarch and the great Táng restorer of the school; editorial revisor of Guàn-dǐng's *Shū* in [[KR6g0016]] the *Huì shū* 會疏 (X659), the standard Tiāntái working text of the MPNS-commentary tradition.
- [[行滿]] (Xíng-mǎn, c. 8th–9th c.) and [[道暹]] (Dào-xiān, c. 8th–9th c.) — mid-Táng Tiāntái sub-commentators; authors of the twin *Sī jì* 私記 [[KR6g0017]] and [[KR6g0018]] on Guàn-dǐng's *Shū*. Together they document the mid-Táng Tiāntái sub-commentarial layer between Guàn-dǐng and Zhàn-rán.
- [[智圓]] (Zhì-yuán, 976–1022) — Northern-Sòng Tiāntái master of the *Shānwài* 山外 (Off-Mountain) branch; author of [[KR6g0006]] *Niè pán xuán yì fā yuán jī yào*, [[KR6g0014]] *Zhì-dìng shū kē* (10 fasc.) and [[KR6g0019]] *Sān dé zhǐ guī* (19 fasc.) — the largest single attribution in the division's commentarial tradition and the foundational *Shānwài* apparatus on the MPNS.
- [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 344–413) — Yáo-Qín Cháng-ān translator; translator of [[KR6g0043]] the *Yí jiào jīng*, the most widely-chanted short Buddhist text in East Asia, and of [[KR6g0028]] the *Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi* sūtra.
- [[真諦]] (Paramārtha / Zhēn-dì, 499–569) — Chén-period translator at Jiànkāng; translator of [[KR6g0044]] *Yí jiào jīng lùn* (Vasubandhu's *śāstra*) and [[KR6g0052]] *Běn yǒu jīn wú jì lùn* (Vasubandhu's commentary on the MPNS's central verse). The principal vector of Indian *śāstra* commentary on the division into Chinese.
- [[天親菩薩]] (Vasubandhu / Tiān-qīn / Pó-sǒu-pán-dòu 婆藪槃豆) — Indian *śāstra*-author; the three principal *śāstras* on the division's doctrine ([[KR6g0044]] *Yí jiào jīng lùn*, [[KR6g0051]] *Niè pán lùn*, [[KR6g0052]] *Běn yǒu jīn wú jì lùn*) are attributed to him in their Chinese transmission. The traditional Indian-school authorial unity is questioned in modern scholarship but preserved as canonical.
- [[達磨菩提]] (Dharmabodhi) — Northern-Wèi translator; translator of [[KR6g0051]] *Niè pán lùn*, the principal Indian *śāstra* on the MPNS doctrine.
- [[玄奘]] (Xuán-zàng, 602–664) — Táng pilgrim-translator; translator of [[KR6g0035]] *Fó lín niè pán jì fǎ zhù jīng*, his short Táng-period rendering of one of the parinirvāṇa-narrative texts.
- [[竺法護]] (Dharmarakṣa / Zhú Fǎ-hù, c. 230–c. 316) — Western-Jìn Yuèzhī-descent translator at Dūnhuáng and Cháng-ān; translator of the earliest Chinese parinirvāṇa material in the division: [[KR6g0024]], [[KR6g0027]], [[KR6g0036]], [[KR6g0040]]. The principal pre-MPNS vector of *bān ní huán* literature into Chinese.
- [[竺佛念]] (Zhú Fó-niàn, Yáo-Qín, fl. late 4th c.) — Liáng-zhōu-descent Cháng-ān translator; translator of [[KR6g0030]] *Pú-sà cóng dōu-shù-tiān jīng*, [[KR6g0031]] *Zhōng yīn jīng* and [[KR6g0034]] *Dà yún wú xiǎng jīng juàn dì jiǔ* — the Yáo-Qín-period entry of Mahāyāna narrative material into the division.
- [[若那跋陀羅]] (Jñānabhadra, Táng) — translator of [[KR6g0023]] *Dà bān niè pán jīng hòu fēn*, the canonical Táng supplement to the MPNS.
- [[那連提耶舍]] (Narendrayaśas, 517–589) — North-Indian translator active under the Northern Qí and Suí; translator of [[KR6g0026]] *Dà bēi jīng* (*Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka*) and [[KR6g0032]] *Lián huā miàn jīng*.
- [[闍那崛多]] (Jñānagupta, 523–600) — Gandhāran translator at the Suí court; translator of [[KR6g0025]] *Sì tóng zǐ sānmèi jīng*.
- [[淨源]] (Jìng-yuán, 1011–1088) — Northern-Sòng Huá-yán master; author of [[KR6g0045]] / [[KR6g0046]] *Yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào* — the principal Sòng-period Huá-yán contribution to the *Yí jiào jīng* commentarial tradition.
- [[守遂]] (Shǒu-suì, fl. Sòng) and [[了童]] (Liǎo-tóng, fl. Míng) — joint authors of [[KR6g0047]] *Yí jiào jīng bǔ zhù* (X665); the Sòng–Míng annotation-and-supplementary-annotation tradition on the *Yí jiào jīng*.
- [[智旭]] (Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù 蕅益智旭, 1599–1655) — one of the *Four Great Masters of the Late Míng*; author of [[KR6g0048]] *Yí jiào jīng jiě* (X666), the canonical late-Míng commentary on the *Yí jiào jīng*. Parallel to his late-Míng commentary on the *Smaller Sukhāvatī* ([[KR6f0093]]).
- [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 1048–1116) and [[觀復]] (Guān-fù, c. Northern Sòng) — Sòng Vinaya / Pure-Land masters; authors of [[KR6g0049]] *Yí jiào jīng lùn zhù fǎ jì* and [[KR6g0050]] *Yí jiào jīng lùn jì*, the Sòng *Yí jiào jīng lùn* sub-commentarial cluster.
- [[竺道生]] (Dào-shēng, c. 360–434) — Liú-Sòng monk; not represented by a per-text in `KR6g` but the central historical figure of the division's doctrinal history. His pre-emptive insistence that even *icchantikas* possess Buddha-nature — based on Fǎxiǎn's short [[KR6g0022]] recension and vindicated upon the arrival of Dharmakṣema's [[KR6g0001]] full recension — became the foundational narrative of Chinese Buddha-nature doctrine.

## Topics

- ***Foxing* 佛性 / Buddha-nature.** The central doctrine of the division. The MPNS articulates the position that all sentient beings — including *icchantikas* (一闡提 *yī-chǎn-tí*), the class traditionally regarded as forever excluded from Buddhahood — possess Buddha-nature and are capable of attaining Buddhahood. This universalising claim is the foundation of mature East-Asian *tathāgatagarbha* doctrine (paralleling but distinct from its other principal scriptural vehicle the *Śrīmālā-sūtra* [[KR6f0045]]) and the conceptual underpinning of subsequent Chinese Buddhist soteriology through Chán, Huá-yán and the *Awakening of Faith*.
- **The *Northern Recension* / *Southern Recension* doublet.** Dharmakṣema's 40-fascicle [[KR6g0001]] *Běi-běn* and Huì-yán *et al.*'s 36-fascicle [[KR6g0003]] *Nán-běn* coexist in the canon as the two parallel canonical texts of the same sūtra — the *Nán-běn* re-organised into 25 chapters and harmonised with Fǎxiǎn's short [[KR6g0022]] recension, the *Běi-běn* preserving the original Dharmakṣema chapter division. The doublet became one of the foundational data-points of Liú-Sòng-period Buddhist intellectual history, and the parallel-text method (reading the two against each other) became one of the canonical East-Asian exegetical techniques.
- **The Dào-shēng 道生 controversy and the doctrine of universal Buddha-nature.** [[竺道生]] Dào-shēng's pre-emptive insistence that even *icchantikas* possess Buddha-nature — formulated from Fǎxiǎn's [[KR6g0022]] short recension before Dharmakṣema's [[KR6g0001]] full recension was available to confirm it — became the most-told story of Chinese Buddhist intellectual history. The controversy was the great theological debate of fifth-century Chinese Buddhism; its resolution in favour of Dào-shēng's universalism shaped all subsequent East-Asian Buddhist soteriology.
- **The *Niè-pán jīng* as one of the three foundational *yuán-jiào* 圓教 sūtras.** Together with the [[KR6e0001|*Avataṃsaka*]] and the *Lotus*, the *Niè-pán jīng* is one of the three sūtras canonically classified as *yuán-jiào* / "complete teaching" in Tiāntái doctrinal classification (*pàn-jiào* 判教); the canonical Tiāntái apparatus of Guàn-dǐng [[KR6g0005]] / [[KR6g0007]] and Zhàn-rán [[KR6g0016]] makes the *Niè-pán jīng* the consummation of the Buddha's teaching career, doctrinally subordinate only to the *Lotus*.
- **The *běn yǒu jīn wú* 本有今無 verse and the metaphysics of Buddha-nature.** The famous verse of the MPNS — "originally existing, now non-existing; originally non-existing, now existing" — is the doctrinal crux of mature Buddha-nature theology, addressing the relation between the eternally-abiding Buddha-nature and the temporally-conditioned phenomenal world. Vasubandhu's [[KR6g0052]] *Běn yǒu jīn wú jì lùn* makes this verse the explicit subject of its own *śāstra*.
- ***Mò-fǎ* 末法 / decline-of-the-Dharma prophecy.** Several texts in the division — [[KR6g0029]] *Mahāmāyā-sūtra*, [[KR6g0032]] *Lián huā miàn jīng*, [[KR6g0040]] *Dāng-lái biàn jīng*, [[KR6g0041]] *Fǎ miè jìn jīng* — articulate the standard Buddhist prophecy of the gradual decline and eventual extinction of the Dharma after the Buddha's parinirvāṇa. The *mò-fǎ* doctrine becomes politically and devotionally consequential in Suí–Táng Buddhism (Sānjiē-jiào 三階教, Pure-Land soteriology, Buddhist apocalyptic literature) and is one of the principal canonical sources for those traditions.
- **The Tiāntái commentarial succession on the *Niè-pán jīng*.** Guàn-dǐng (Suí) → Dào-xiān / Xíng-mǎn (mid-Táng) → Zhàn-rán (Táng) → Zhì-yuán (N. Sòng) constitutes one of the most genealogically explicit commentarial lineages in East Asian Buddhism: each generation rewrites and supplements the previous, the canonical [[KR6g0007]] *Shū* of Guàn-dǐng becoming successively the subject of [[KR6g0017]]–[[KR6g0018]] *sī jì*, then [[KR6g0016]] *huì shū*, then [[KR6g0014]] *kē* and [[KR6g0019]] *zhǐ guī*. The division is therefore the principal canonical site for studying the structural evolution of Chinese Buddhist commentary across half a millennium.
- **The *Yí jiào jīng* as the canonical short monastic exhortation.** Kumārajīva's [[KR6g0043]] *Yí jiào jīng* — at one fascicle, the shortest principal text in the division — is one of the most widely-chanted Buddhist texts in East Asian monastic life, and the principal site of *vinaya*-themed exhortation outside the *Vinaya-piṭaka* proper. The *Yí jiào jīng* commentarial tradition runs from Vasubandhu's [[KR6g0044]] *Yí jiào jīng lùn* through the Sòng commentaries of Jìng-yuán, Yuán-zhào, and Guān-fù, the joint Sòng–Míng *Bǔ zhù* of Shǒu-suì / Liǎo-tóng [[KR6g0047]], and Zhì-xù's late-Míng [[KR6g0048]] *Yí jiào jīng jiě*.
- ***Antarābhava* 中陰 / the intermediate state.** Zhú Fó-niàn's [[KR6g0031]] *Zhōng yīn jīng* is one of the principal Chinese canonical sources for the doctrine of an intermediate-state existence between death and rebirth, a doctrine variously affirmed and denied across Buddhist schools and one of the principal Indian Abhidharma controversies entering East-Asian Buddhism through the *Niè-pán bù*.
- **Political theology of the *Dà yún jīng*.** Dharmakṣema's [[KR6g0033]] *Dà fāng-děng wú-xiǎng jīng* / *Dà yún jīng* is one of the most politically consequential Mahāyāna scriptures in East Asian history: a Táng re-translation / forgery of it was used in 690 to justify Empress Wǔ Zé-tiān 武則天's usurpation of the Táng throne, on the basis that the sūtra prophesies the appearance of a female cakravartin queen. The text became one of the foundational scriptures of imperial Buddhist political theology in China.
- **The Buddha's death-scene as a literary corpus.** The short sūtras T0390–T0396 ([[KR6g0035]]–[[KR6g0041]]) constitute a small but coherent literature of the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* as narrative and ritual event — the coffining, cremation, funeral, Mahākāśyapa's arrival, Vajrapāṇi's lamentation, the *abiding* of the Dharma. The corpus is the textual basis for Buddhist memorial liturgy and for the parinirvāṇa-iconography of East-Asian Buddhist art.
- **Pseudepigraphy and attribution problems.** The Indian-school authorial unity of Vasubandhu across [[KR6g0044]], [[KR6g0051]], [[KR6g0052]] is questioned in modern scholarship but preserved as canonical here. The Táng "re-translation" of the *Dà yún jīng* used by Wǔ Zé-tiān is widely regarded as a Chinese forgery rather than a genuine retranslation. Several short anonymous parinirvāṇa-narrative sūtras (T0392, T0394, T0396) are likely Chinese compositions rather than Indian translations.
