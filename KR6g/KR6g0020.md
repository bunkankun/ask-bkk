# Kē nán běn niè pán jīng 科南本涅槃經
*Sectional Outline of the Southern Recension of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[師正]] (Shī-zhèng, 排科) and [[可度]] (Kě-dù, 重訂)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle Yuán-period text is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) for [[KR6g0003|the Southern Recension of the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] (T0375), arranged by [[師正]] Shī-zhèng and re-edited by [[可度]] Kě-dù.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1280 – 1368) reflects the Yuán dynasty period of the work's compilation.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X663).
