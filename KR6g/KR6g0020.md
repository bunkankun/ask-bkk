# Kē nán běn niè pán jīng 科南本涅槃經
*Sectional Outline of the Southern Recension of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[師正]] (Shīzhèng, 排科) and [[可度]] (Kědù, 重訂)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle Yuán-period text is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) for the Southern Recension of the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* (*Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 [[KR6g0003]], T0375), arranged by [[師正]] Shīzhèng and re-edited by [[可度]] Kědù.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1280–1368) reflects the Yuán dynasty period of the work's compilation.
