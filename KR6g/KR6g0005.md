# Dà bān niè pán jīng xuán yì 大般涅槃經玄義
*The Profound Meaning of the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 撰)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] is by [[灌頂]] Guàn-dǐng (561–632), the principal disciple of [[智顗]] Zhì-yǐ and the second patriarch of the Tiāntái school. The *xuán yì* "profound meaning" format provides a doctrinal preamble to the larger sūtra, in the standard Tiāntái scholastic genre.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (597 – 632) reflects Guàndǐng's mature Tiāntái scholarly activity, beginning with Zhìyǐ's death.

The Taishō text (T1765) is established on the standard apparatus.
