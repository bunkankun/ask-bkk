# Dà bān niè pán jīng shū 大般涅槃經疏
*Commentary on the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàn-dǐng, 撰)

## About the work

The *Niè-pán jīng shū* in 33 fascicles is the great Suí-period Tiāntái commentary on the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] by [[灌頂]] Guàn-dǐng. Together with [[KR6g0005|the *Xuán yì*]] (T1765), it constitutes the canonical Tiāntái-school doctrinal apparatus on the *Niè-pán jīng*. The 33-fascicle scope makes it one of the most substantial single Tiāntái-school commentaries.
