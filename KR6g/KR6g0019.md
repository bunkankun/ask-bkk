# Niè pán jīng shū sān dé zhǐ guī 涅槃經疏三德指歸
*The Three-Virtue Direction-Pointing of the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[智圓]] (Zhì-yuán, 述)

## About the work

This 19-fascicle Northern-Sòng sub-commentary on Guàn-dǐng's [[KR6g0007|*Niè-pán jīng shū*]] is by [[智圓]] Zhì-yuán, the *Shānwài* Tiāntái-school master. The "three virtues" (*sān dé* 三德) of the title refers to the *Niè-pán jīng*'s threefold doctrine of *fǎ shēn* / *bān ruò* / *jiě tuō* (Dharma-body, wisdom, liberation).

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (990 – 1022) reflects Zhì-yuán's mature scholarly activity. The 19-fascicle scope makes this one of the largest Sòng-period sub-commentaries.

The work is preserved in both the Taishō (cross-listed at 【大】) and the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X662).
