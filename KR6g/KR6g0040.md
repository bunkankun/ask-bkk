# Fó shuō dāng lái biàn jīng 佛說當來變經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Coming Changes*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù is a *mò fǎ*-genre (decline-of-Dharma) sūtra prophesying the changes (*biàn* 變) that will come in the latter days of the Dharma. The work is one of the foundational *mò fǎ* texts in the Chinese canon.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE).

The Taishō text (T0395) is established on the standard apparatus.
