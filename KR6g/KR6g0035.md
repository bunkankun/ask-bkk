# Fó lín niè pán jì fǎ zhù jīng 佛臨涅槃記法住經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha at His Parinirvāṇa Recording the Abiding of the Dharma*
by [[玄奘]] (Xuánzàng, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng records the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* discourse on the *fǎ zhù* 法住 (the abiding of the Dharma in the world after his death). The work is one of Xuánzàng's smaller post-India translations.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Xuánzàng's Cháng'ān period (645–664 CE).
