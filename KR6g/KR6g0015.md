# Niè pán jīng huì shū tiáo gě 涅槃經會疏條箇
*Combined-Commentary Item-Outline of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (anonymous)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 3-fascicle anonymous text is a *huì shū tiáo gě* — a *combined-commentary item-by-item outline* — of the [[KR6g0001|*Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*]] commentaries. The format provides item-by-item navigation through the *huì běn* (combined edition) of the *Niè-pán* commentaries.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The work is anonymous and undated; conventionally placed in the broad late-Tang to Qīng period of *huì běn* publishing activity.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X658).
