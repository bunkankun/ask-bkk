# Niè pán jīng shū sī jì 涅槃經疏私記 (Dàoxiān)
*Private Notes on the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[道暹]] (Dàoxiān, 述)

## About the work

This 9-fascicle Tang Tiāntái-school sub-commentary on Guàndǐng's [[KR6g0007|*Nièpán jīng shū*]] is by [[道暹]] Dàoxiān, paralleling [[KR6g0017|Xíngmǎn's parallel *Sī jì*]] (X660). Together the two works document the mid-Tang Tiāntái-school *Nièpán* commentarial tradition.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (766 – 800) reflects Dàoxiān's mid-Tang activity per the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn*. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X661).
