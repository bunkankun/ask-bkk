# Niè pán xuán yì fā yuán jī yào 涅槃玄義發源機要
*Source-Essential Sub-Commentary on the Profound Meaning of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[智圓]] (Zhìyuán, 述)

## About the work

This 4-fascicle Northern-Sòng sub-commentary on [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Dà bān niè pán jīng xuán yì* 大般涅槃經玄義 ([[KR6g0005]], T1765) is by [[智圓]] Zhìyuán (976–1022), the *Shānwài* Tiāntái-school master.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (990–1022) reflects Zhìyuán's mature Northern-Sòng scholarly activity.
