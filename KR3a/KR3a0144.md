# Jiǎngzǐ wànjī lùn 蔣子萬機論
*Master Jiǎng's Discourse on the Ten Thousand Affairs of State*
by [[蔣濟]] (Jiǎng Jì, 188–249, 魏)

## About the work

A lost CáoWèi 曹魏 political-philosophical treatise by the senior Wèi minister Jiǎng Jì 蔣濟. Reconstructed from quotations in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, 《北堂書鈔》, 《初學記》, 《禮記·小功章》 sub-commentary, and from Péi Sōngzhī's *Sānguó zhì* commentary on the *Shǔ zhì·Xǔ Mí Sūn Jiǎn Yīn Qín zhuàn* 蜀志·許麋孫簡伊秦傳評. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1467).

### Abstract

The *Wànjī lùn* — "On the ten thousand levers of governance" — is one of the most substantively political *zǐ*-house treatises of the Wèi period, presented as a series of *lùn* on the discrete questions a ruling minister must address: ritual order, the calendar of mourning, the discrimination of talent, the deployment of *xíng* 刑 and *dé* 德, the management of military expenditure, the proper use of office, and the moral grounding of imperial authority. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists it in 10 *juàn*; only fragments survive. The work is closely tied to Jiǎng Jì's career as one of the dominant policy voices of the Wèi court — chief military adviser to Cáo Cāo, Cáo Pī and Cáo Ruì in turn — and the surviving fragments preserve the same hardened pragmatism that animates his memorials in the *Sānguó zhì*. The composition window is bracketed by his Wèi-period career (220–249); the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
