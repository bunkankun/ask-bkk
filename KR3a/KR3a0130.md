# Yuánzǐ zhènglùn 袁子正論
*The Correct Discourses of Master Yuán*
by [[袁準]] (Yuán Zhǔn, *fl.* mid–late 3rd century, 晉)

## About the work

The second of Yuán Zhǔn's 袁準 two surviving *zǐ*-house works — the companion to [[KR3a0124]] *Yuánzǐ zhèngshū*. Reconstructed from a wide net of citations: 《公羊》 commentary, 《初學記》, 《北堂書鈔》, 《周禮》 sub-commentary, 《國語》, 《左傳》, 《大傳》 and many further leishu. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1388). At ~2,000 lines of citation it is the longest jíyì reconstruction in this KR3a *zǐ*-house cluster.

### Abstract

The *Zhènglùn* is the more substantive and the more politically ambitious of Yuán Zhǔn's two works. It is structured as a series of *lùn* 論 on specific topics — *Zhèng* 政 (administration), *Lǐ* 禮 (ritual), *Xíng* 刑 (punishment), and historical-classical exegesis on the *Zhōu guān* 周官, the *Chūnqiū* 春秋, the *Gōngyáng* 公羊 reading, and the *Zuǒshì zhuàn* 左氏傳. A substantial portion of the surviving fragments engages directly with the *Zhōu lǐ* / *Zhōu guān* offices, making the work an important early Jìn witness to the *gǔwén* 古文 reception of the *Zhōu guān*. Its programmatic claim — that the *Zhōu guān* is the authoritative framework for institutional administration — anticipates later Tang and Sòng *Zhōu lǐ* statecraft. The author's identity, dating, and his relation to his father Yuán Huàn 袁渙 are treated under [[袁準]]. The composition window is bracketed by the Western Jìn period. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.

## Translations and research

*No substantial dedicated secondary literature located* in Western languages. The fullest fragment-compilation is 嚴可均 *Quán Jìn wén* 全晉文 j. 54. Chinese scholarship on the early reception of the *Zhōu guān* (e.g., 楊向奎, 沈文倬) cites the *Zhènglùn* as one of the earliest substantive engagements.
