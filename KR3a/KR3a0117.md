# Sōng-yáng chāo cún 松陽鈔存
*Selections from Sōng-yáng, Preserved*
parent material by [[陸隴其]] (Lù Lǒngqí, 1630–1692, 清, 撰); compiled-and-annotated by [[楊開基]] (Yáng Kāijī, 清, 編注)

## About the work

A 2-juan compilation of materials from Lù Lǒngqí's tenure as Magistrate of Sōng-yáng 松陽 (Zhè-jiāng, 1675), edited by his pupil Yáng Kāijī. The work documents Lù's administrative-and-pedagogical practice in his model magistrate's role at Sōng-yáng — a tenure that became canonical in the late-imperial *qīng guān* (upright officer) tradition. Substantively the work blends *zhèng* (administrative) and *xué* (scholarly) materials, providing the practical complement to Lù's purely-philosophical *Dú Zhū suí bǐ* (KR3a0115) and *Sān-yú-táng shèng yán* (KR3a0116).

### Abstract

The *Sōng-yáng chāo cún* is the most institutionally-grounded of Lù Lǒngqí's three SKQS *Rújiā* works. Composition window: bracketed by Lù's Sōng-yáng tenure beginning 1675 and his death in 1692. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1675–1692.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Rújiā lèi*.
