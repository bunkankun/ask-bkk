# Dōng-xī rì tán lù 東溪日談錄
*Daily Discussions at Dōng-xī*
by [[周琦]] (Zhōu Qí, *zì* Tíngxǐ 廷璽, 明)

## About the work

An 18-juan classical-philosophical *biji* by Zhōu Qí of Mǎ-píng (Guǎng-xī), in 13 thematic categories: *Xìng dào tán* 性道談 (2 juan), *Lǐ qì tán* 理氣談 (1), *Jì sì tán* 祭祀談 (2), *Xué shù tán* 學術談 (1), *Chū chǔ tán* 出處談 (1), *Wù lǐ tán* 物理談 (1), *Jīng zhuàn tán* 經傳談 (3), *Zhù shù tán* 著述談 (1), *Shǐ xì tán* 史系談 (2), *Rú zhèng tán* 儒正談 (1), *Wén cí tán* 文詞談 (1), *Yì duān tán* 異端談 (1), *Pì yì tán* 闢異談 (1). The work derives doctrinally from the *Hé-Dōng xué-pài* of Xuē Xuān (KR3a0079 author). The *Guǎng-xī tōng zhì* records a separate *Rú zhèng piān* discussing Xuē Hé-dōng's learning; the SKQS *tíyào* identifies this as juan 15 of the present work — not a separate composition. First printed in *Jiā-jìng dīng-yǒu* (1537) by Lǚ Jǐng-méng 呂景蒙; the SKQS-base appears to be a manuscript transcription, the original woodblocks having been lost.

### Tiyao

We respectfully submit that the *Dōng-xī rì tán lù* in 18 juan was composed by Zhōu Qí of the Míng. Qí, *zì* Tíngxǐ, was a man of Mǎ-píng. *Jìnshì* of *Chéng-huà xīn-chǒu* (1481); rose in office to *Nán-jīng Hù-bù yuán-wài láng*. Qí's learning issued from Hé-dōng Xuē Xuān. This compilation records his heart-attainments, divided into 13 categories: 2 juan *Xìng dào tán*; 1 juan *Lǐ qì tán*; 2 juan *Jì sì tán*; 1 juan *Xué shù tán*; 1 juan *Chū chǔ tán*; 1 juan *Wù lǐ tán*; 3 juan *Jīng zhuàn tán*; 1 juan *Zhù shù tán*; 2 juan *Shǐ xì tán*; 1 juan *Rú zhèng tán*; 1 juan *Wén cí tán*; 1 juan *Yì duān tán*; 1 juan *Pì yì tán*.

The *Guǎng-xī tōng zhì* records his *Rì tán lù* in 18 juan; further records he composed *Rú zhèng piān* discussing Xuē Hé-dōng's learning. Now examining: the *Rú zhèng* is the 15th juan of this book, not a separate work.

Before is Lǚ Jǐng-méng's preface saying the book was printed in *Jiā-jìng dīng-yǒu* (1537). But this present text is in transmitted-handwriting form — perhaps the printing-blocks were already lost, and later hands recorded and preserved it. Qí's character was upright, careful, and sincere — esteemed in his home district. His book also takes the *Lián-Luò* line as basis, no shame to him.

### Abstract

The *Dōng-xī rì tán lù* is a useful Mid-Míng *Lǐxué*-orthodox *biji* in the *Hé-Dōng xué-pài* line of Xuē Xuān. Composition window: bracketed by Zhōu Qí's working life from his 1481 *jìnshì*; the work was published posthumously in 1537. The frontmatter brackets to 1481–1537.

The substantive content — 13 thematic categories spanning metaphysics, ritual, learning, ethics, classics, history, and heterodoxy — is methodologically conventional Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* but provides a useful prosopographical window into the *Hé-Dōng* tradition's reach into the Guǎng-xī periphery. The juan on Xuē Xuān (j. 15, *Rú zhèng tán*) is the principal substantive contribution: a Mid-Míng student's evaluation of his master.

The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; *Wén-yuān-gé shū-mù*; *Guǎng-xī tōng zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Rújiā lèi*.

## Other points of interest

The *Hé-Dōng xué-pài* lineage's reach into Guǎng-xī through Zhōu Qí is one of the Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* geographical-extension cases — comparable to the parallel reach of Wáng Yáng-míng's *xīn-xué* through subsequent generations to the southwest. Zhōu's solid orthodox positioning provides a useful counter-witness to the more famous Yáng-míng-school spread.
