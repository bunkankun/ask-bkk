# Gānzǐ 干子
*Master Gān*
by [[干寶]] (Gān Bǎo, *fl.* 317–336, 晉)

## About the work

A lost Eastern Jìn 東晉 *zǐbù* treatise (also cited in the early bibliographic record as *Zì-rán lùn* 自然論) by Gān Bǎo 干寶, the historian and anomaly-anthologist of the early Eastern Jìn. Reconstructed from quotations in 《埤雅·釋蟲》, 《埤雅·釋魚》, 《埤雅·釋鳥》, 《太平御覽》, 《荊楚歲時記》 and the *Biàn-huà·xù* 變化序. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1480).

### Abstract

The *Gānzǐ* is one of two Gān Bǎo *zǐ*-house works recorded in the early bibliography — the better-known being his *Sōushén jì* 搜神記 (cf. [[KR3l0099]]). The *Gānzǐ* is on natural philosophy and the cosmology of *biàn-huà* 變化 (transformation), in keeping with Gān Bǎo's wider interest in the empirical demonstration of the supernatural that informs his *Sōushén jì*. The surviving fragments are concentrated in Lù Diàn's 陸佃 Sòng-period *Pí yǎ* 埤雅 (a natural-history commentary on the *Ěr yǎ*), which preserves Gān Bǎo's observations on insects, fish, and birds — and on the *Biàn-huà* preface, which preserves his programmatic account of transformations between species. This combination of natural-historical observation and cosmological speculation gives the *Gānzǐ* a distinctive register, closer in spirit to Zhāng Huá's 張華 *Bówù zhì* 博物志 than to the political *zǐ*-house tradition. The composition window is bracketed by Gān Bǎo's documented Eastern Jìn floruit (317–336); the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*. Background on Gān Bǎo is given on [[干寶]] and [[KR3l0099]].

## Translations and research

*No substantial dedicated secondary literature located* on the *Gānzǐ* specifically; the wider Gān Bǎo bibliography concentrates on the *Sōushén jì*. See Kenneth DeWoskin and J. I. Crump, *In Search of the Supernatural: The Written Record* (Stanford, 1996, translation of *Sōushén jì*). Standard fragment-compilation: 嚴可均 *Quán Jìn wén* 全晉文 j. 127.
