# Péi Shǐ xīnyán 裴氏新言
*The New Sayings of Master Péi*
by [[裴玄]] (Péi Xuán, *fl.* mid 3rd century, 吳)

## About the work

A lost Sūn-Wú 孫吳 *zǐbù* treatise by Péi Xuán 裴玄 (sometimes spelled 裴元 in Sòng catalogs, under the Sòng *yuán* 玄→元 taboo). Reconstructed from quotations in 《北堂書鈔》, 《埤雅》, 《太平御覽》, 《淮南子》 sub-commentary, 《藝文類聚》, and Lǐ Shàn's commentary on Yáng Hù's 《讓開府表》 in *Wén xuǎn*. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1471).

### Abstract

Péi Xuán was a Wú-period official from Pèi-guó 沛國 — described in *Sānguó zhì* commentary as the father of Péi Jùn 裴儁 — who served under Sūn Quán. The *Xīnyán* (also cited as *Péi-shǐ xīnshū* 裴氏新書 in some early Táng sources) survives as a small body of fragments — natural-history observations, classical aphorisms, and short reflective pieces. The work is most often encountered in the *Yìlín* and *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* tradition. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists the *Xīnyán* under the Wú period. The composition window follows Péi Xuán's Wú-period career; the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
