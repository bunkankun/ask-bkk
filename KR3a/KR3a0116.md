# Sān-yú-táng shèng yán 三魚堂賸言
*Surplus Words from the Three-Fish Hall*
parent words by [[陸隴其]] (Lù Lǒngqí, *hào* Sānyú 三魚, 1630–1692, 清, 撰); compiled by [[陳濟]] (Chén Jì, 1665–1718, 清, 編)

## About the work

A 12-juan posthumous *yǔ-lù* of Lù Lǒngqí, compiled by his pupil Chén Jì. The title *sān-yú-táng* is from Lù's hall-name (his *hào* Sānyú "Three Fish"). The work consolidates Lù's *jiǎng-xué* materials into a thematic anthology, complementing the *Dú Zhū suí bǐ* (KR3a0115, his close-reading notes on Zhū-zǐ) and *Sōng-yáng chāo cún* (KR3a0117, his administrative-period reflections from Sōng-yáng).

### Abstract

The *Sān-yú-táng shèng yán* is the principal *yǔ-lù* of Lù Lǒngqí. Composition window: Lù's working life (1670–1692) plus Chén Jì's editorial work (post-1692, completed during Chén's life span 1665–1718). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1670–1700.

The substantive position is uniformly Zhū-Xī-loyalist, providing the thematic-anthology counterpart to the *Dú Zhū suí bǐ*'s reading-notes form.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Rújiā lèi*.
