# Dǔ lùn 篤論
*Sincere Discourse*
by [[杜恕]] (Dù Shù, c. 198–252, 魏)

## About the work

The companion *zǐ*-house treatise to Dù Shù's 杜恕 [[KR3a0120]] *Tǐlùn* 體論. Reconstructed from quotations in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, 《藝文類聚》 and from Péi Sōngzhī's *Sānguó zhì* commentary (*Wèi-zhì běn-zhuàn*). Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1469). At over a thousand lines of citation it is one of the larger *jíyì* reconstructions in this KR3a *zǐ*-house cluster.

### Abstract

The *Dǔlùn* is a substantial Wèi-period political-philosophical treatise of the same idiom and orientation as the *Tǐlùn*: a series of *lùn* on specific topics — the conduct of office, the proper deployment of *xíng* 刑 and *dé* 德, the discrimination of talent, the moral basis of military command, and the management of the *xíng-shì* 形勢 of empire. The longest surviving block of fragments comes from the *Yìlín* of Mǎ Zǒng 馬總. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists the *Dǔlùn* in 4 *juàn*; only fragments survive. The two works (*Tǐlùn* and *Dǔlùn*) are sometimes conflated in early Sòng catalogs, and a small number of fragments are claimed by both reconstructions — but the modern *jíyì* compilations keep them separate. The author's identity, dating, and his lineage (son of Dù Jī, father of Dù Yù) are treated under [[杜恕]]; the composition window is bracketed by his Wèi career. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.

## Translations and research

*No substantial dedicated secondary literature located* in Western languages. The standard fragment-compilation is 嚴可均 *Quán sān guó wén* 全三國文 j. 42, which preserves the largest single block of Dù Shù prose.
