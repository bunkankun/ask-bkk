# Yì yì shuō 易翼說
*Discourse on the Wings of the Yì*
by [[晏斯盛]]

## About the work
The Wing-commentary member of [[晏斯盛]] Yàn Sīshèng's three-part *Yì*-corpus, in eight juàn — the third volume after [[KR1a0147]] (the *Xué Yì chū jīn* methodological framework) and [[KR1a0148]] (the *Yì yì zōng* canonical commentary). The work fully glosses all Ten Wings of the *Zhōuyì*, but in an unusual order: *Xìcí* — *Shuōguà* — *Xùguà* — *Záguà* — *Tuàn* — *Wényán* — *Xiàng*. This is neither the canonical *gǔ Yì* sequence (*Tuàn shàng* — *Tuàn xià* — *Xiàng shàng* — *Xiàng xià* — *Xìcí shàng* — *Xìcí xià* — *Wényán* — *Shuōguà* — *Xùguà* — *Záguà*) nor the *jīn Yì* arrangement; the *Sìkù* editors note "non-ancient, non-modern; one does not know what edition is the basis."

The *Sìkù* editors' joint assessment of all three works (in the *Xué Yì chū jīn* tíyào, [[KR1a0147]]) applies: methodologically distinctive but well-grounded in canonical text; substantively avoiding both the technical-numerology and the metaphysical-talk extremes; "still substantive-and-near-reason among recent *Yì*-houses."

### Abstract
Composition is bracketed by Yàn's mid-Qiánlóng career; see [[KR1a0147]] for biographical details. The *Yì yì shuō* is the Wing-commentary final member of Yàn's three-part *Yì*-corpus.

The work's distinctive feature is the unusual sequence of Wing-glossing. Beginning with the *Xìcí* (rather than the *Tuàn*) suggests Yàn took the *Xìcí*'s methodological reflections as the conceptual foundation for understanding the rest; ending with the *Xiàng* makes the symbol-and-image readings the application of the conceptual foundation. This is a non-standard but coherent methodological architecture.

## Other points of interest
The unusual Wing-glossing sequence — beginning with the most theoretical Wing (*Xìcí*) and ending with the most applied (*Xiàng*) — is one of the more methodologically interesting Qing-period reorderings of the canonical Wings. It deserves separate study as a small case in the philosophy of canonical sequencing.
