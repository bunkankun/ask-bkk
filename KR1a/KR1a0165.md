# Yì wěi biàn zhōng bèi 易緯辨終備
*The Apocryphal Yì on Discriminating-the-End and Preparedness*
nominally by [[鄭玄]] (註)

## About the work
The third of the eight *Yì wěi* 易緯 texts gathered as appendix to the *Yì*-class of the *Sìkù quánshū*; see [[KR1a0163]] for the broader contextualization of the *Yì wěi* tradition. Recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. The work treats the *biàn zhōng bèi* — discriminating-the-end and preparedness — as a divinatory-cosmographic apparatus complementary to the *Jī lǎn tú* ([[KR1a0164]]). One juàn.

### Tiyao
*See [[KR1a0163]] for the joint contextualization of the eight* Yì wěi *texts. The* Sìkù tíyào *for the* Biàn zhōng bèi *is brief and chiefly notes its place within the recovered* Yǒnglè dàdiǎn *Yì-wěi corpus.*

### Abstract
The work is among the more compact members of the eight-text *Yì wěi* corpus. As with the rest of the corpus, the substantive content concerns Hàn-period divinatory-cosmographic apparatus drawn on the *Yìjīng*; the attribution to Zhèng Xuán's commentary is conventional rather than secure.
