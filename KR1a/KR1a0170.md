# Yì wěi Kūn líng tú 易緯坤靈圖
*The Apocryphal Yì on the Spirit-Charts of Kūn*
nominally by [[鄭玄]] (註)

## About the work
The eighth and final of the eight *Yì wěi* 易緯 texts gathered as appendix to the *Yì*-class of the *Sìkù quánshū*; see [[KR1a0163]] for the broader contextualization of the *Yì wěi* tradition. One juàn. The work treats the spirit-charts (*líng tú* 靈圖) of *Kūn* as a divinatory-cosmographic apparatus complementary to the *Qián záo dù* / *Kūn záo dù* pair (the *Záo dù* tradition is methodologically focused on the chiseling-open of *Qián-Kūn*; the *Líng tú* on the spirit-mediated charts that derive from *Kūn*).

The *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* recovery brings the *Sìkù*'s eight-text *Yì wěi* corpus to completion: the *Záo dù* pair ([[KR1a0163]] *Qián Kūn záo dù* + [[KR1a0166]] *Zhōuyì Qián záo dù*); the calendrical-divinatory pair ([[KR1a0164]] *Jī lǎn tú* + [[KR1a0167]] *Tōng guà yàn*); the cosmological pair ([[KR1a0168]] *Qián yuán xù zhì jì* + [[KR1a0170]] *Kūn líng tú*); and two miscellaneous works ([[KR1a0165]] *Biàn zhōng bèi* + [[KR1a0169]] *Shì lèi móu*).

### Tiyao
*See [[KR1a0163]] for the joint contextualization of the eight* Yì wěi *texts.*

### Abstract
The work is the closing member of the *Sìkù*-recovered *Yì wěi* corpus, a compact one-juàn document on the spirit-mediated charts of *Kūn*. As with the broader corpus, the substantive content concerns Hàn-period (or post-Hàn-but-medieval) divinatory-cosmographic apparatus; the Zhèng Xuán-commentary attribution is conventional and not secure.
