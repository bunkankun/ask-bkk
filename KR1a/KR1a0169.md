# Yì wěi shì lèi móu 易緯是類謀
*The Apocryphal Yì on Such-And-Such-Class Stratagems*
nominally by [[鄭玄]] (註)

## About the work
The seventh of the eight *Yì wěi* 易緯 texts gathered as appendix to the *Yì*-class of the *Sìkù quánshū*; see [[KR1a0163]] for the broader contextualization. One juàn. The work treats *shì lèi móu* — divinatory stratagem-applications classified by-category — as a divinatory-cosmographic apparatus complementary to the *Tōng guà yàn* ([[KR1a0167]]) and the *Jī lǎn tú* ([[KR1a0164]]).

### Tiyao
*See [[KR1a0163]] for the joint contextualization of the eight* Yì wěi *texts.*

### Abstract
The work is one of the more compact members of the *Yì wěi* corpus. The substantive content concerns divinatory-stratagem categorization. The Zhèng Xuán-commentary attribution is conventional and not secure.
