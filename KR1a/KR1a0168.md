# Yì wěi Qián yuán xù zhì jì 易緯乾元序制記
*The Apocryphal Yì on the Sequencing-and-Setting Records of Qián's Source*
nominally by [[鄭玄]] (註)

## About the work
The sixth of the eight *Yì wěi* 易緯 texts gathered as appendix to the *Yì*-class of the *Sìkù quánshū*; see [[KR1a0163]] for the broader contextualization. One juàn. The work treats the cosmographic-cosmological sequencing-and-setting (*xù zhì* 序制) of phenomena ordered from the source-of-*Qián* (*Qián yuán* 乾元), drawing on the *Yìjīng*'s opening hexagram as the source-point of cosmological generation.

### Tiyao
*See [[KR1a0163]] for the joint contextualization of the eight* Yì wěi *texts.*

### Abstract
The work is a compact one-juàn member of the *Yì wěi* corpus, focusing on cosmological-cosmographic sequencing through the *Qián* hexagram. The Zhèng Xuán-commentary attribution is conventional and not secure.
