# KR3em — Wǔ-guān kē 五官科

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3em is the *wǔ-guān kē* 五官科 ("five sense-organs") sub-division of `KR3e` 醫家類 (Medicine), itself the principal medical sub-section of `KR3` 子部 (Masters). It collects late-imperial and early-Republican Chinese specialty monographs on diseases of the **eye** (*yǎn-kē* 眼科), the **throat and larynx** (*hóu-kē* 喉科), and the **mouth and teeth** (*kǒu-chǐ* 口齒). The selection criterion is editorial rather than canonical: every one of the 39 cataloged entries carries the source code `hxwd`, i.e. the *Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī shàn-běn gǔ-jí cóng-shū* 海外回流中醫善本古籍叢書 ("Returned-from-Overseas Collectanea of Chinese Medical Rare Books and Old Editions"), a modern reprint series of rare medical books — many of them recovered from Japanese and Korean libraries — that fall outside the *Sìkù quánshū* canon. There is no WYG-edition material in this sub-division and no *tíyào* sections in the per-text notes; per project convention the front-matter section is *Prefaces* instead.

Chronologically the collection spans the Yuán-Míng transition (the *Yuán-jī qǐ-wēi* 原機啟微 of 1370–1391, [[KR3em001]]) to the final years of the Qīng and the early Republic (the *Hóu-kē jiā-xùn* 喉科家訓 of 1918–1924, [[KR3em038]]; the *Hóu-kē jīn-yuè quán-shū* 喉科金鑰全書 of *Xuān-tǒng* 3 / 1911, [[KR3em034]]). The bulk of the corpus is Qīng (28 of 39 entries). Three doctrinal currents organise the field: (1) the Indo-Buddhist couching-for-cataract surgical tradition repackaged in the *Lóng-mù lùn* 龍木論 group of pseudo-Sūn Sī-miǎo eye texts; (2) the Jīn-Yuán *qì-xuè / zàng-fǔ* aetiological synthesis of the Dān-xī school as applied to ophthalmology by [[倪維德]] Ní Wéi-dé and re-canonised in the imperial *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* 醫宗金鑑 ([[KR3em007]]); and (3) the family-and-region transmissions of throat medicine that crystallised in the Yóu 尤, Zhāng 張, Jiāo 焦, and Zhèng 鄭 lineages of the Jiāng-Nán *qī-shí-èr zhǒng yān-hóu* (72-throat-disorder) genre, and which were forced into a programmatic recasting by the late-nineteenth-century diphtheria (*bái-hóu* 白喉) epidemics. Wilkinson (Section 41, "Medicine"; §41.3) treats specialty disciplines like ophthalmology and laryngology as a late development of the secular medical bibliography, paralleling the rise of clan-transmitted *jiā-chuán* 家傳 medical practice in Míng-Qīng Jiāng-Nán; Hinrichs and Barnes (2013, pp. 78, 109–113, 196, 343–345) supply the scholarly anchor for the Indo-Buddhist surgical strand and for the late-Qīng diphtheria literature.

## Important texts and text clusters

**Ophthalmology — Jīn-Yuán aetiological synthesis.**
- *Yuán-jī qǐ-wēi* 原機啟微 [[KR3em001]] (Yuán-Míng; 倪維德, 1370–1391) — earliest fully extant Chinese ophthalmology monograph organised aetiologically rather than symptomatically, the principal carrier of the Dān-xī school in eye medicine.
- *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Yǎn-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué* 醫宗金鑑·眼科心法要訣 [[KR3em007]] (Qīng; 吳謙 et al., 1742–1749) — the imperial *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* ophthalmology section in verse-mnemonic form, the standard Qīng curriculum text.

**Ophthalmology — *Lóng-mù lùn* pseudo-Sūn / Nāgārjuna cluster.** A four-text complex transmitting the Indo-Buddhist couching-surgery tradition under Sūn Sī-miǎo's borrowed prestige:
- *Mì-chuán yǎn-kē Lóng-mù lùn* 秘傳眼科龍木論 [[KR3em003]] (Míng print; attributed to [[龍樹]] Nāgārjuna and [[葆光道人]]) — the core *Lóng-mù* text;
- *Yín-hǎi jīng-wēi* 銀海精微 [[KR3em011]] (attrib. [[孫思邈]], received form Sòng-Míng) — the 81-disorder companion (a parallel WYG recension is at `KR3e0014`);
- *Míng-mù zhì-bǎo* 明目至寶 [[KR3em002]] (Míng) and *Yǎn-kē mì-jué* 眼科秘訣 [[KR3em004]] (early Qīng) — affiliated 72-disorder formularies.

**Ophthalmology — surgical and Wú-region.** *Shěn-shì yáo-hán* 審視瑤函 [[KR3em010]] (late Míng; 傅仁宇) is the most operatively oriented late-Míng eye treatise, preserving golden-needle cataract couching procedure in detail.

**Ophthalmology — Qīng synthesis and pedagogy.** *Mù-jīng dà-chéng* 目經大成 [[KR3em006]] (黃庭鏡, 1741–1805) and *Yín-hǎi zhǐ-nán* 銀海指南 [[KR3em012]] (顧錫, 1807–1810) are eighteenth- and nineteenth-century syntheses of the inherited doctrine for clinical use.

**Ophthalmology — Japanese kanpō outlier.** *Yǎn-kē jǐn-náng* 眼科錦囊 [[KR3em005]] (本荘普一 / Honjō Fuichi, 1831–1837) is the only late-Edo *kanpō* 漢方 work in the subdivision.

**Laryngology — Xīn'ān 新安 / Chóng-lóu 重樓 tradition.**
- *Chóng-lóu yù-yuè* 重樓玉鑰 [[KR3em017]] (Qīng; 鄭宏綱, 1750–1787) — founding work of the *yǎng-yīn qīng-fèi* 養陰清肺 doctrine of throat treatment;
- *Chóng-lóu yù-yuè xù-biān* 重樓玉鑰續編 [[KR3em018]] (Qīng; 方成培) — the continuation that consolidated the doctrine.

**Laryngology — family-and-clan transmissions.**
- Yóu-clan 尤氏: *Yóu-shì hóu-zhèng zhǐ-nán* 尤氏喉症指南 [[KR3em021]] (Míng; 尤仲仁) and *Yóu-shì hóu-kē mì-shū* 尤氏喉科秘書 [[KR3em022]] (Qīng; 尤乘) — the Sūzhōu Yóu lineage;
- Jiāo-clan 焦氏: *Jiāo-shì hóu-kē zhěn-mì* 焦氏喉科枕秘 [[KR3em023]] — canonical 72-disorder framework;
- Bāo-clan 包氏: *Bāo-shì hóu-zhèng jiā-bǎo* 包氏喉證家寶 [[KR3em025]] — family-manual reception of the same framework;
- Zhāng Zōng-liáng's *Hóu-kē zhǐ-zhǎng* 喉科指掌 [[KR3em024]] (1700–1757) — the most-reprinted Qīng laryngology, with *liù-wèi tāng* 六味湯 as its base prescription, and its companion *Yān-hóu mì-jí* 咽喉秘集 [[KR3em026]].

**Laryngology — *bái-hóu* 白喉 (diphtheria) cluster.** A late-Qīng epidemic-response corpus, recognised in the secondary literature (Hinrichs and Barnes 2013, pp. 343–345) as the principal indigenous response to nineteenth-century diphtheria outbreaks:
- *Bái-hóu tiáo-biàn* 白喉條辨 [[KR3em020]] (陳葆善, 1893–1909) — Wēnzhōu *wēn-bìng*-framework treatise;
- *Bái-hóu quán-shēng jí* 白喉全生集 [[KR3em027]] (李紀方, 1860–1882) — landmark Húnán manual, exported as the "Chinese specific" reported in the Australian medical press in 1874;
- *Hóu-kē jí-yè* 喉科集腋 [[KR3em028]] (沈青芝) — the lung-as-metal aetiology of diphtheria;
- *Bái-hóu jié-yào hé-biān* 白喉捷要合編 [[KR3em031]] (1867–1907) — combined essentials;
- *Bái-hóu biàn-zhèng* 白喉辨證 [[KR3em032]] (王裕慶, 1880–1902) — polemical Húnán anti-cooling text in explicit dialogue with [[KR3em027]].

**Laryngology — late Qīng / Republican syntheses and survivors.** *Hóu-kē dà-chéng* 喉科大成 [[KR3em033]] (馬渭齡), *Hóu-kē jīn-yuè quán-shū* 喉科金鑰全書 [[KR3em034]] (袁仁賢, 1911), *Hóu-kē xīn-fǎ* 喉科心法 [[KR3em036]], and *Hóu-kē jiā-xùn* 喉科家訓 [[KR3em038]] (刁步忠, 1918–1924). *Chóng-dìng náng-mì hóu-shū* 重訂囊秘喉書 [[KR3em030]] and *Yān-hóu mài-zhèng tōng-lùn* 咽喉脈證通論 [[KR3em029]] (校刊 by [[許槤]]) preserve earlier anonymous material recovered in the Qīng.

**Mouth and teeth (口齒).** *Kǒu-chǐ lèi-yào* 口齒類要 [[KR3em039]] (Míng; 薛己, 1528–1559) is the canonical late-imperial treatise and the only major one in the corpus; *Zǒu-mǎ jí-gān zhēn-fāng* 走馬急疳真方 [[KR3em040]] (pseudo-Sòng) is a short pseudepigraphic paediatric-cancrum-oris formulary. *Hóu-shé bèi-yào mì-zhǐ* 喉舌備要秘旨 [[KR3em041]] is a hybrid throat-and-tongue work.

**Cough as a throat-adjacent topic.** *Ké-lùn jīng-zhǐ* 咳論經旨 [[KR3em037]] (凌德, late Qīng) is filed under throat medicine but is doctrinally a *xū-sǔn* / *tán-yǐn* digest synthesising *Wēn-bìng* and *Jīn-guì* sources.

## Important persons

- [[倪維德]] Ní Wéi-dé (1303–1377): Wú-zhōng physician of the Yuán-Míng transition; carrier of the Dān-xī school's ophthalmological line; author of [[KR3em001]] and the foundational figure for aetiological eye medicine.
- [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sī-miǎo (581–682): the Táng *yào-wáng* whose authorship is borrowed by the *Lóng-mù* eye-text cluster ([[KR3em002]], [[KR3em004]], [[KR3em011]]); historically present in the division only as a pseudepigraphic anchor.
- [[龍樹]] Lóng-shù (Nāgārjuna): Indo-Buddhist eponymous "author" of [[KR3em003]], standing for the Indian couching-surgery tradition that entered China via Buddhist translations.
- [[傅仁宇]] Fù Rén-yǔ (late Míng): Nán-jīng surgical ophthalmologist; author of [[KR3em010]].
- [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān (1689–1748): chief compiler of the imperial *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*; under his name the ophthalmology section [[KR3em007]] entered the standard Qīng medical curriculum.
- [[黃庭鏡]] Huáng Tíng-jìng (1741–1805): author of the eighteenth-century ophthalmological synthesis [[KR3em006]].
- [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (1487–1559): the great Jiā-jìng-era Sūzhōu *wēn-bǔ* physician; author of [[KR3em039]] (the canonical mouth-and-teeth treatise) and re-editor of [[KR3em001]].
- [[鄭宏綱]] Zhèng Hóng-gāng (Méi-jiàn 梅澗, 1727–1787): Xīn'ān physician; founder of the *yǎng-yīn qīng-fèi* doctrine in throat medicine through [[KR3em017]].
- [[方成培]] Fāng Chéng-péi: continuator of the *Chóng-lóu* tradition with [[KR3em018]].
- [[尤仲仁]] Yóu Zhòng-rén (Míng) and [[尤乘]] Yóu Chéng (Qīng): founders and continuators of the Sūzhōu Yóu-clan throat-medicine line ([[KR3em021]], [[KR3em022]]).
- [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōng-liáng (mid-Qīng): author of [[KR3em024]], the most-circulated Qīng laryngology, and probable redactor of [[KR3em026]].
- [[李紀方]] Lǐ Jì-fāng (mid-late Qīng): Húnán physician; author of the landmark anti-diphtheria *Bái-hóu quán-shēng jí* [[KR3em027]].
- [[陳葆善]] Chén Bǎo-shàn (late Qīng / Republican): Wēnzhōu physician; author of the *wēn-bìng*-framework diphtheria treatise [[KR3em020]].
- [[許槤]] Xǔ Lián (1787–1862): philologist-official and editor; recovered and printed the anonymous *Yān-hóu mài-zhèng tōng-lùn* [[KR3em029]].
- [[本荘普一]] Honjō Fuichi (early-19th-c. Japan): late-Edo *kanpō* ophthalmologist; author of [[KR3em005]] and the division's only Japanese contributor.

## Topics

- **Indigenous vs. Indo-Buddhist ophthalmology.** The *wǔ-lún bā-kuò* 五輪八廓 ("five wheels and eight quadrants") topographic symbolism of the *Lóng-mù* tradition versus the Jīn-Yuán *zàng-fǔ / qì-xuè* aetiology promoted by [[KR3em001]] and canonised by [[KR3em007]]. The struggle is explicit in the editorial framing of [[KR3em001]]: "the eye is the chief manifestation of the qì and blood of the five viscera and six bowels — surely it cannot be confined to the vulgar *wǔ-lún bā-kuò* schema alone."
- **Indo-Buddhist couching for cataract.** Operative needle-couching (*jīn-zhēn bō-zhàng* 金針撥障) for internal cataract enters the Chinese literate medical record through the Buddhist-translation channel of the Táng; by the late Míng the *Yín-hǎi jīng-wēi* [[KR3em011]], the *Lóng-mù lùn* [[KR3em003]], and the *Yuán-jī qǐ-wēi* [[KR3em001]] all describe the procedure in physician-controlled language. Discussed in Hinrichs and Barnes (2013), pp. 78 and 196.
- **The 72-throat-disorder genre (*qī-shí-èr zhǒng yān-hóu zhèng* 七十二種咽喉症).** A taxonomy that organises [[KR3em023]], [[KR3em024]], [[KR3em025]], [[KR3em026]], and their relatives, with *liù-wèi tāng* 六味湯 as the universal opening prescription. The genre is a vehicle of family- and region-transmitted clinical knowledge rather than a state-sanctioned curriculum.
- **The *bái-hóu* / *wēn-bìng* controversy.** Late-Qīng diphtheria epidemics force a doctrinal split between cooling/yīn-nourishing *yǎng-yīn qīng-fèi* (descended from [[KR3em017]] and developed in [[KR3em027]] and [[KR3em020]]) and warming/expelling readings ([[KR3em032]]). The corpus is a textbook case of indigenous epidemic doctrine evolving under pressure from a specific bacterial pathogen, decades before its identification by Klebs and Loeffler.
- **Family transmission (*jiā-chuán* 家傳) and regional schools.** Throat medicine in particular is structured by lineage: the Yóu in Sūzhōu, the Bāo and Jiāo, the Diāo, the Húnán Lǐ line, the Xīn'ān Zhèng-Fāng line. Wilkinson §41 treats this as the dominant social form of specialty medicine in the late empire.
- **Pseudepigraphy and prestige-borrowing.** Sūn Sī-miǎo, Nāgārjuna, and "the Yellow Perfected" (*Huáng zhēn-rén* 黃真人, [[KR3em019]]) serve as borrowed names for technically expert but socially marginal traditions. Editorial recovery work — Xǔ Lián for [[KR3em029]], Yáng Lóng-jiǔ for [[KR3em030]] — frequently restores anonymous Yuán-Míng material to scholarly circulation through Qīng prefaces.
- **Pedagogical verse and mnemonic.** Both [[KR3em007]] and many of the throat manuals encapsulate their doctrine in *yào-jué* 要訣 (rhymed mnemonic formulae), a pedagogical genre Wilkinson (§41.3.1, §29.4.9.1) treats as central to the transmission of imperial-period technical knowledge.
- **Cross-divisional reception.** [[KR3em007]] is the *yǎn-kē* fascicle of the imperial *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* whose body resides at `KR3e0090`; [[KR3em011]] has a parallel WYG recension at `KR3e0014`; [[KR3em039]] is the *kǒu-chǐ* monograph excerpted from [[薛己]]'s collected medical works at `KR3e0070`. The subdivision functions as a specialty annex to the main `KR3e` medical canon, not as a free-standing tradition.

## Timeline

- 1086–1541 — *Yín-hǎi jīng-wēi* 銀海精微 [[KR3em011]] — 81-disorder pseudo-Sūn eye compendium; the broad bracket reflects its received-recension status in the *Lóng-mù* tradition.
