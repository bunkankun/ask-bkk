# Yān-hóu mì-jí 咽喉秘集
*Secret Collection on Throat and Larynx*
attributed to "Zhāng Sòng-liáng" 張宋良 (almost certainly a typographic slip for [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōng-liáng of [[KR3em024]] *Hóu-kē zhǐ-zhǎng*) with a "Mistress / Master Wú" 吳氏 named jointly in the catalog.

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* Qīng laryngology compendium covering the standard mid-Qīng "Zhāng-school" doctrinal architecture: a *zǒng-lùn* 總論 (general theory) restating the *yī-yīn yī-yáng jié ér wéi bì* 一陰一陽結而為痹 framework from the *Nèi-jīng*, a six-pulse diagnostic schema mapping the laryngology to the *cùn-guān-chǐ* 寸關尺 pulse-positions on both wrists, a *fēn-jīng* 分經 anatomical division of the throat into channels, and the systematic application of *liù-wèi tāng* 六味湯 加減 as universal base prescription. This is followed by a clinical typology of named conditions (single, double, rosary-string *rǔ-é* 乳蛾, *chán-hóu fēng* 纏喉風, *hóu-bì* 喉閉, etc.) and a formulary of insufflation powders, decoctions, and external dressings — including the *jié-dú zǐ-jīn dān* 結毒紫金丹, *bó-hé tāng* 薄荷湯, *huí-xuě fāng* 迴雪方, and the *yá-gān fāng* 牙疳方.

### Prefaces

The hxwd witness preserves no preface; the text opens directly with the *zǒng-lùn*.

### Abstract

The catalog meta records two authors: 張宋良 and 吳氏 ("Mistress / Master Wú", anonymous within a clan). The orthography 張宋良 is preserved in the present frontmatter per cataloging convention, but is almost certainly a Qīng-period typographic slip for the well-attested [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōngliáng (*zì* Liú-xiān 留仙) of Sūzhōu, author of [[KR3em024]] *Hóu-kē zhǐ-zhǎng* (preface 1757). The internal doctrinal apparatus of the present text — the six-flavour-decoction architecture (六味湯加減), the right-hand-pulse mapping to *fèi-fēng* 肺風 / *fèi-fú-hán* 肺伏寒, the left-hand-pulse mapping to *xīn-huǒ* 心火 / *shèn-xū huǒ-yán* 腎虛火炎, the explicit *liù-wèi tāng* universal-base strategy — is essentially identical to that of *Hóu-kē zhǐ-zhǎng*, and modern Chinese specialist scholarship (cf. 耿鑒庭 et al.) treats the two works as products of the same author and lineage. The Wú-clan 吳氏 attribution may reflect a co-transmitter or family relative responsible for the redaction of the present recension; no individual biographical detail attaches to this co-attribution and, following standard convention, no separate person note has been created for the anonymous 吳氏 collaborator.

The composition window is set to 1700–1800 — broadly congruent with the 1757 preface of [[KR3em024]] and allowing for the *Yān-hóu mì-jí*'s recension to have been finalized within the same Qián-lóng-era working life of Zhāng Zōngliáng. The catalog meta gives only "清" without finer dating.

## Other points of interest

The doctrinal twinning of *Yān-hóu mì-jí* with [[KR3em024]] *Hóu-kē zhǐ-zhǎng* — same author (under orthographic variant), same therapeutic system, same characteristic phraseology — makes the pair an instructive specimen of late-imperial intra-lineage textual proliferation: one major systematic monograph (*Hóu-kē zhǐ-zhǎng*) and a one-*juǎn* compact handbook (*Yān-hóu mì-jí*) co-existing for different clinical and pedagogical uses within the same family practice.
