# Hóushé bèiyào mìzhǐ 喉舌備要秘旨
*Secret Essentials for Throat-and-Tongue Disorders*
Anonymous (late-Qīng compilation).

## About the work

A late-Qīng anonymous laryngology compilation in one *juǎn* covering throat-medicine (*hóubù* 喉部), oral medicine (*kǒubù* 口部), and dental medicine (*yábù* 牙部), with a closing appendix of three popular-medicine items: an anti-opium-addiction formula (*Zhì yāpiàn yānyǐn qíxiào fāng* 治鴉片煙飲奇效方) credited to [[屠燮臣]] Tú Xièchén of Huánggāng 黃岡 (Húběi) and dated *Tóngzhì* 2 (1863), an emergency anti-poisoning formula (*Jíjiù fúdú liángfāng* 急救服毒良方), and a *gǔgěng fúzhòu* 骨鯁符咒 (bone-impaction talisman-incantation). The work is a heterogeneous compilation rather than a single-author monograph.

The substantive throat-medicine portion of the work is an abridgement-with-modifications of *Zhāngshì hóukē qīshíèr zhèng fāmíng* 張氏喉科七十二症發明 of [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōngliáng (cf. [[KR3em024]]) — the same forty-six-syndrome laryngology nomenclature is given (the *shuāngsōngluó* 雙松虆 / *dānsōngluó* 單松虆 / *shuāngyànzǐ* 雙燕子 / *dānyànzǐ* 單燕子 / *shuāngé* 雙鵝 / *dāné* 單鵝 / *shuāngchán* 雙纏 / *dānchán* 單纏 series and so on), along with the *biàn yánghóu* 辨陽喉 / *biàn yīnhóu* 辨陰喉 / *kàn hóuzhōng biàn zàngsè jué* 看喉中辨臟色訣 doctrinal section. The doctrinal-overview opening ("*Hóubù lùn hóuzhèng*" 喉部論喉症) is, however, identifiably excerpted from [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn's *Jǐngyuè quánshū* 景岳全書 ([[KR3e0061]]) — the discussion of *xūhuǒ* 虛火 vs *shíhuǒ* 實火, of *zhēnyīn* 真陰 vs *géyáng* 格陽 throat-occlusion patterns, the case-records from *Xiāngyáng* 襄陽 and so on, are *verbatim* from the *Jǐngyuè* *hóubì* section. The work is therefore an explicitly synthetic late-Qīng laryngology compendium combining the *Jǐngyuè* throat-medicine doctrine with the *Zhāng Zōngliáng* forty-six-syndrome clinical typology.

### Abstract

The work is unsigned and undated; no preface or postface attributes authorship. The included anti-opium formula is dated 1863 (the *Tóngzhì guǐhài* 同治二年癸亥 colophon of [[屠燮臣]] Tú Xièchén) and is reported by the secondary postscript-note to have been published in [[屠道和]] Tú Dàohé's *Pǔjì liángfāng* 普濟良方 — establishing 1863 as the *terminus a quo* for the received recension. The text's *báihóu* 白喉 section ("*治白喉*" 治白喉, with the *Shíèrwèi xuánqiào yǐn* 十二味玄翹飲) shows the influence of the late-Qīng *báihóu* literature that crystallised around [[張紹修]] Zhāng Shànwú's *Báihóu zhìfǎ juéwēi* 白喉治法抉微 (1864) and the *Báihóu jiābǎo* 白喉家寶 tradition, supporting a *post*-1864 date for the received compilation. The terminus *ante quem* cannot be more closely fixed than the late-Qīng (the work is preserved in a late-Qīng / Republican manuscript tradition). The composition window adopted here is therefore 1863–1900.

The work is one of several late-Qīng provincial *hóukē* compilations that crystallised the *Zhāngshì hóukē* 張氏喉科 forty-six-syndrome nomenclature (the late-eighteenth-century work of [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōngliáng of Sūzhōu) into a more accessible clinical handbook by combining it with the *Jǐngyuè* doctrinal framework and an explicit pharmacopoeia. The opening doctrinal section's insistence that *hóubì* 喉痹 is not simply a *xiānghuǒ* 相火 ("ministerial fire") syndrome to be treated with bitter-cold cooling, but must be sub-classified by *xū / shí* 虛實, *zhēnyīn* 真陰 vs *géyáng* 格陽, and treated with warming and tonifying medicine when indicated, is faithful to the *Jǐngyuè* tradition and aligns the work with the broader late-Qīng *wēnbǔ* 溫補 reaction against the bitter-cold attacking-treatment orthodoxy of mid-Qīng *Wēnbìng xué* 溫病學 throat-medicine. The closing pharmacopoeia is substantial — *Zǐpáo sǎn* 紫袍散, *Yùlóng sǎn* 玉龍散, *Jīn-yào-shi* 金鑰匙, *Bīngpéng sǎn* 冰硼散, *Sānhuáng sǎn* 三黃散, *Hóngbǎo dān* 洪寶丹, *Yángdú sǎn* 陽毒散, *Chōnghé sǎn* 沖和散, *Huánglóng sǎn* 黃龍散, *Liǎngyí sǎn* 兩宜散, *Xīguāshuò* 西瓜霜 with detailed compounding instructions, and an unusual *bǎishé qífāng* 白喉奇方 of duck-dung-on-tile recipe.

The work is heterogeneous in genre (clinical doctrine + syndrome-typology + pharmacopoeia + emergency formulary + incantation + anti-addiction formula) and is best understood as a late-Qīng pragmatic clinical handbook for the *hóukē* generalist rather than a unified treatise; this is characteristic of the regional Qīng *hóukē* tradition preserved in the *jicheng.tw* 漢學文典 digital corpus.
