# Hóu-shé bèi-yào mì-zhǐ 喉舌備要秘旨
*Secret Essentials for Throat-and-Tongue Disorders*
Anonymous (late-Qīng compilation).

## About the work

A late-Qīng anonymous laryngology compilation in one *juǎn* covering throat-medicine (*hóu-bù* 喉部), oral medicine (*kǒu-bù* 口部), and dental medicine (*yá-bù* 牙部), with a closing appendix of three popular-medicine items: an anti-opium-addiction formula (*Zhì yā-piàn yān-yǐn qí-xiào fāng* 治鴉片煙飲奇效方) credited to *Tú Xiè-chén* 屠燮臣 of *Huáng-gāng* 黃岡 (Húběi) and dated *Tóng-zhì* 2 (1863), an emergency anti-poisoning formula (*Jí-jiù fú-dú liáng-fāng* 急救服毒良方), and a *gǔ-gěng fú-zhòu* 骨鯁符咒 (bone-impaction talisman-incantation). The work is a heterogeneous compilation rather than a single-author monograph.

The substantive throat-medicine portion of the work is an abridgement-with-modifications of [[KR3em009]] *Zhāng-shì hóu-kē qī-shí-èr zhèng fā-míng* 張氏喉科七十二症發明 of *Zhāng Zōng-liáng* 張宗良 — the same forty-six-syndrome laryngology nomenclature is given (the *shuāng-sōng-luó* 雙松虆 / *dān-sōng-luó* 單松虆 / *shuāng-yàn-zǐ* 雙燕子 / *dān-yàn-zǐ* 單燕子 / *shuāng-é* 雙鵝 / *dān-é* 單鵝 / *shuāng-chán* 雙纏 / *dān-chán* 單纏 series and so on), along with the *biàn yáng-hóu* 辨陽喉 / *biàn yīn-hóu* 辨陰喉 / *kàn hóu-zhōng biàn zàng-sè jué* 看喉中辨臟色訣 doctrinal section. The doctrinal-overview opening ("*Hóu-bù lùn hóu-zhèng*" 喉部論喉症) is, however, identifiably excerpted from [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jiè-bīn's *Jǐng-yuè quán-shū* 景岳全書 ([[KR3e0061]]) — the discussion of *xū-huǒ* 虛火 vs *shí-huǒ* 實火, of *zhēn-yīn* 真陰 vs *gé-yáng* 格陽 throat-occlusion patterns, the case-records from *Xiāng-yáng* 襄陽 and so on, are *verbatim* from the *Jǐng-yuè* *hóu-bì* section. The work is therefore an explicitly synthetic late-Qīng laryngology compendium combining the *Jǐng-yuè* throat-medicine doctrine with the *Zhāng Zōng-liáng* forty-six-syndrome clinical typology.

### Abstract

The work is unsigned and undated; no preface or postface attributes authorship. The included anti-opium formula is dated 1863 (the *Tóng-zhì guǐ-hài* 同治二年癸亥 colophon of *Tú Xiè-chén* 屠燮臣) and is reported by the secondary postscript-note to have been published in *Tú Dào-hé* 屠道和's *Pǔ-jì liáng-fāng* 普濟良方 — establishing 1863 as the *terminus a quo* for the received recension. The text's *bái-hóu* 白喉 section ("*治白喉*" 治白喉, with the *Shí-èr-wèi xuán-qiào yǐn* 十二味玄翹飲) shows the influence of the late-Qīng *bái-hóu* literature that crystallised around *Zhāng Shàn-wú* 張善吾's *Bái-hóu zhì-fǎ chàng-yán* 白喉治法抉微 (1864) and the *Bái-hóu jiā-bǎo* 白喉家寶 tradition, supporting a *post*-1864 date for the received compilation. The terminus *ante quem* cannot be more closely fixed than the late-Qīng (the work is preserved in a late-Qīng / Republican manuscript tradition reproduced in the hxwd collection). The composition window adopted here is therefore 1863–1900.

The work is one of several late-Qīng provincial *hóu-kē* compilations that crystallised the *Zhāng-shì hóu-kē* 張氏喉科 forty-six-syndrome nomenclature (the late-eighteenth-century work of *Zhāng Zōng-liáng* 張宗良 of Sū-zhōu) into a more accessible clinical handbook by combining it with the *Jǐng-yuè* doctrinal framework and an explicit pharmacopoeia. The opening doctrinal section's insistence that *hóu-bì* 喉痹 is not simply a *xiāng-huǒ* 相火 ("ministerial fire") syndrome to be treated with bitter-cold cooling, but must be sub-classified by *xū / shí* 虛實, *zhēn-yīn* 真陰 vs *gé-yáng* 格陽, and treated with warming and tonifying medicine when indicated, is faithful to the *Jǐng-yuè* tradition and aligns the work with the broader late-Qīng *wēn-bǔ* 溫補 reaction against the bitter-cold attacking-treatment orthodoxy of mid-Qīng *Wēn-bìng xué* 溫病學 throat-medicine. The closing pharmacopoeia is substantial — *Zǐ-páo sǎn* 紫袍散, *Yù-lóng sǎn* 玉龍散, *Jīn-yào-shi* 金鑰匙, *Bīng-péng sǎn* 冰硼散, *Sān-huáng sǎn* 三黃散, *Hóng-bǎo dān* 洪寶丹, *Yáng-dú sǎn* 陽毒散, *Chōng-hé sǎn* 沖和散, *Huáng-lóng sǎn* 黃龍散, *Liǎng-yí sǎn* 兩宜散, *Xī-guā-shuò* 西瓜霜 with detailed compounding instructions, and an unusual *bǎi-shé qí-fāng* 白喉奇方 of duck-dung-on-tile recipe.

The work is heterogeneous in genre (clinical doctrine + syndrome-typology + pharmacopoeia + emergency formulary + incantation + anti-addiction formula) and is best understood as a late-Qīng pragmatic clinical handbook for the *hóu-kē* generalist rather than a unified treatise; this is characteristic of the regional Qīng *hóu-kē* tradition preserved in the hxwd-collection.
