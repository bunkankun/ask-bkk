# Jiǎoqì zhìfǎ zǒngyào 腳氣治法總要
*The Essentials of Treating Foot-Qi*
by [[董汲]] (Dǒng Jí, *zì* Jíyuán 及之, Northern Sòng, *fl.* 1080s–1090s)

## About the work

One of the earliest specialised monographs on *jiǎoqì* 腳氣 ("foot-qi" — a syndrome cluster historically encompassing what modern biomedicine would call beriberi, but in Sòng practice a wider category of damp/heat-induced lower-limb disorders), in 2 *juǎn* (上下) divided into nineteen *mén* 門 by the author's preface. Composition probably 1093 (*Yuányòu* 8). The Sòngshǐ Yìwénzhì lists the work; the dating bracket 1085–1100 here brackets the Sòng compositional context.

### Abstract

Dǒng Jí — better known as the principal Sòng biographer of [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ — was a Northern Sòng physician who, by his own preface, suffered *jiǎoqì* for ten-odd years from his youth. The *Jiǎoqì zhìfǎ zǒngyào* aggregates from across the HànSòng medical canon: the *Sùwèn*, *Jiǔlíng*, *Língshū*, *Jiǎyǐ jīng*, *Tàisù*, Cháo Yuánfāng (《Cháoshì bìngyuán》), *Qiānjīn yàofāng*, *Wàitái mìyào*, *Shèngyào fāng*, *Xiǎopǐn fāng*, *Shānfán*, *Jīnguì yùhán*, materia medica, Sū Gōng's pharmacological notes, and earlier pulse books, and adds tested therapies (acupuncture and moxa, pulse-diagnosis, dietetics, *dǎoyǐn* 導引 calisthenics, fumigation/steaming, emergency formulas).

The work was largely lost in the late Qing transmission and was partially reconstructed from quotations in *Shèngjì zǒnglù* 聖濟總錄, *Pǔjǐ fāng* 普濟方 etc.; the WYG / collectanea text in front of us is therefore a *jíyì* 輯佚 reconstruction rather than a pure Sòng witness. This source-critical caveat is important for any citation that depends on the wording of specific passages.
