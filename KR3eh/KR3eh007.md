# Zázhèng huìxīn lù 雜症會心錄
*Notes on the Heart-Met Miscellaneous Syndromes*
by [[汪文綺]] (Wāng Wénqǐ, *zì* Yùngǔ 蘊谷, *fl.* mid-18th c.)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* internal-medicine handbook by the Wùyuán 婺源 (Huīzhōu 徽州) physician Wāng Wénqǐ, completed *c.* 1754. The text comprises three *zǒng-lùn* 總論 essays of general medical theory followed by some fifty-plus disease-specific essays covering internal-medicine, gynaecology, and miscellaneous *zhèng* — each accompanied by selected prescriptions and brief case-records. Doctrinally aligned with Zhāng Jièbīn's 張景岳 *Jǐng-yuè quán-shū* tradition, with strong emphasis on *fú-yáng yǎng-xīn* 扶陽養心 (supporting yáng, nourishing the heart) — characteristic of the Xīn'ān 新安 medical school in this period. The title is Wāng's reflective figure: the cases and discussions represent diseases he has, over a long clinical practice, "met with the heart" — i.e. truly understood — rather than merely read about.

### Abstract

Composed at the height of Wāng Wénqǐ's clinical practice in Wùyuán and printed shortly after, the *Zázhèng huìxīn lù* is an exemplar of the Xīn'ān-school 新安 mid-Qiánlóng *zábìng* genre — Huīzhōu being one of the great regional centres of Qing literate medicine, with hereditary medical families closely intertwined with the merchant-literati elite. Two prefaces fix the date of completion: Chéng Shìfǎ 程世法's preface dated *Qiánlóng jiǎxū* = 1754 and Wāng's nephew Wú Yǐzhèn 吳以鎮's preface dated *Qiánlóng yǐhài* = 1755. The composition is the work of Wāng's later years, integrating decades of clinical experience inherited from his father Shízhōu 十洲 and elder brother Guǎngqī 廣期.

The most-cited section of the work is the *Fèi-yōng lùn* 肺癰論 (lung-abscess essay), which sets out a three-stage diagnostic and therapeutic protocol — opening stage *gān-jié tāng* 甘桔湯 + *hēi-dòu tāng* 黑豆湯 (clearing the latent toxin and inducing eruption); suppurative stage *bǎi-hé gù-jīn tāng* 百合固金湯 加減 (nourishing yīn and clearing metal/lung); post-rupture *liù-wèi tāng* 六味湯 加減 (water-supplementing and lung-protecting). The framing of the diagnostic challenge — that lung-abscess "始萌之時最易惑人，極難識認" — has been widely cited in subsequent Lǐngnán and Jiāngnán *fèi-bìng* literature.

The work was reprinted in the Republican-period *Zhēn-běn yī-shū jí-chéng* 珍本醫書集成 (Shanghai: Shìjiè shūjú, 1936), the principal modern source.
