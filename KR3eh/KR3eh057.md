# Jiē-nüè lùn shū 痎瘧論疏
*An Annotated Discourse on the Periodic Malaria Diseases*
by [[盧之頤]] (Lú Zhīyí, *zì* Zǐyóu 子繇)

## About the work

A 1-*juǎn* late-Míng commentary on the *Sùwèn*'s "*Nüè lùn*" 瘧論 and "*Cì nüè*" 刺瘧 chapters by the Qián-táng 錢塘 physician Lú Zhīyí. This is the hxwd (*Hǎiwài huíguī zhōngyī gǔjí cóng-shū*) transmission of the same work that is cataloged from its WYG (*Wén-yuān gé Sì-kù quán-shū*) recension at [[KR3e0088]]. The hxwd source directory for KR3eh057 contains only an empty header file; for the full *tíyào* translation, Abstract, doctrinal exposition, and bibliographical apparatus, see the principal entry at **[[KR3e0088]]**. The catalog meta gives the surname 廬 (with the *yǎn* radical) where the correct form is 盧; the slip is documented at the parent entry.

In summary: the work covers four key doctrinal points — *yáng-yīn* polarity (*nüè* = *yáng*, *jiē* = *yīn*); the day-cycle / *yīn-yáng* correlation; the unification of the *wēn / hán / dān / pìn* sub-categories under *jiē-nüè*; and the adoption of Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* ([[KR3e0078]]) as the principal prescriptive source.
