# Zhì-nüè jī-yào 治瘧機要
*Essentials of Malaria Treatment*
by [[劉裁吾]] (Liú Cáiwú, *fl.* 1912)

## About the work

A specialised treatise on *nüè* 瘧 (malaria-like intermittent fever syndromes), in 4 *juǎn*. Liú Cáiwú's central argument is that all the dominant schools — Yú Chāng 喻昌 / Xú Dàchūn 徐大椿 (*zhuān-zhǔ Shào-yáng* 專主少陽 / Bupleurum-centred therapy), Yè Tiānshì 葉天士 (no Bupleurum), Wáng Shìxióng 王士雄, Fèi Bóxióng 費伯雄, and Hán Shànzhēng 韓善徵 — fail to grasp that *nüè* is fundamentally a disorder of *yíng-wèi* 榮衛 (nutritive and defensive *qì*) rather than a six-channel or single-channel disease. He acknowledges Lú Zhīyí 盧之頤 (*Jiè-nüè lùn shū* 痎瘧論疏 [[KR3eh057]]) as the closest predecessor.

### Abstract

The catalog records the work as anonymous; this is incorrect. Three prefaces dated *Mín-guó* 1 = 1912 — by Liú himself, by Chén Bǎo 陳寶 (*zì* Yuán-qīn 原欽), and by Xiè Yìnán 謝邑南 (*zì* Hóng-yí 洪嶷), all of the same locality — identify Liú as the author. The composition window of 1912 here reflects this firm dating.

The work integrates *Sù-wèn* "Nüè lùn" / "Cì-nüè piān" with the *Líng-shū* and the *Jīn-guì*, then engages Western quinine ("guī-ní-dīng" 規尼丁) and iron tonics, finding both inadequate. The treatise is therefore one of the documentary witnesses to the early-Republican Chinese-medical engagement with biomedical disease categories and pharmacotherapy — a project of *zhōng-xī huì-tōng* 中西匯通 in the more confident, Republican-era register of [[唐宗海]] Táng Zōnghǎi's late-Qing forerunner-tradition.

The work is anomalous in a Kanripo *zá-bìng* collection of mostly classical works; it is included here because of its classical-Chinese register and its sustained engagement with the canonical *nüè* literature.
