# Shì mó hē yǎn lùn zàn xuán shū 釋摩訶衍論贊玄疏
*Commentary Praising the Profound Doctrine of the Shi Moheyan Lun*
by [[法悟]] (Fǎ-wù, 撰)

## About the work

A five-*juǎn* sub-commentary on the [[KR6o0084|*Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* 釋摩訶衍論]] by the **Khitan / Liáo** monk [[法悟]] (Fǎ-wù, fl. 1080s–1090s) — though the catalog meta classes the dynasty as 宋, the imperial preface attributing the work to the Liáo emperor Tiān-yòu 天佑 (the personal name of Liáo Dào-zōng 道宗, r. 1055–1101) firmly places it in the Liáo. The work is preserved both in the Sòng *Qī-shā* edition 磧砂藏 and in the *Manji-zokuzōkyō* 卍續藏經 (X45). The Khitan / Liáo court patronised the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* as a foundational doctrinal treatise; this *Zàn-xuán shū* was the most important and most extensive of the Liáo sub-commentaries.

### Structural Division

CANWWW does not record an internal sub-division. The text is divided only by *juǎn* (1–5).

### Abstract

The text opens with a long imperial preface, "釋摩訶衍論贊玄疏引文 / 貞亮翼贊同德致理功臣開府儀同三司守太保兼中書令監修國史知樞密院事上柱國燕國公食邑七千五百戶實封... 臣耶律孝傑奉勑撰" — the preface is by the Liáo Khitan official **Yēlǜ Xiào-jié** 耶律孝傑 (Yelü Xiaojie), one of the principal officials of the Liáo court, who composed the preface at the order of Tiān-yòu Huáng-dì (Liáo Dào-zōng). The preface places the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* in lineage with Aśvaghoṣa's [[KR6o0078|*Awakening of Faith*]] and Nāgārjuna's commentary, and praises Fǎ-wù's commentary as the latest and finest in this lineage. The work is registered in the Liáo *Liáo-zàng* canonical lists and was carried via the Khitan canon to Korean and Japanese transmission.

The author Fǎ-wù is otherwise poorly attested but should be understood as a major Liáo Buddhist scholastic of the late eleventh century, working at one of the principal Khitan-court patronised monasteries. His sub-commentary represents the high point of Liáo Buddhist scholasticism on the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* and was textually authoritative for subsequent Korean Goryeo and early Japanese Shingon readings.

## Other points of interest

The text is the most extensive and most authoritatively-attested of the seven Chinese / Liao sub-commentaries on the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* (KR6o0085–0090); it is the only one with an imperial preface and the only one preserved in both the Sòng *Qī-shā* edition and the *Manji-zokuzōkyō*. Its preface by Yēlǜ Xiào-jié is also of historical interest as one of the few Khitan-court Buddhist documents preserved in the canon.
