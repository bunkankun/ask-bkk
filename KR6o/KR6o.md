### Scope and scholarly tradition

KR6o corresponds to the *Lùnjí bù* 論集部 of the Taishō canon (vol. 32, nos. 1628–1692), together with the commentarial overflow in vol. 44 (nos. 1839–1850) and a small group of Dūnhuáng-recovered manuscripts in vol. 85 (nos. 2813–2816). The division is the canon's general miscellany of doctrinal treatises (*lùn* 論, śāstra) and the verse-collections (*sòng* 頌, *zàn* 讚, *gāthā* 伽他) attached to them. Unlike the dedicated divisions for Mādhyamaka and Yogācāra commentary on specific sūtras (`KR6m` 釋經論部, `KR6n` 毘曇部, 瑜伽部), KR6o gathers freestanding śāstras that do not belong to a single school or sūtra-commentary lineage: hetuvidyā (yīnmíng 因明) logic, Madhyamaka and Sautrāntika doctrinal works, the *Satyasiddhi-śāstra* of the 成實宗 school, *pratītyasamutpāda* tracts, *bodhicitta* literature, the *Mahāyāna-śraddhotpāda* (*Awakening of Faith*) corpus, the Sino-Buddhist *Milindapañha* (*Nā xiān bǐqiū jīng*), and an extensive stotra/*gāthā* section. 

Chronologically the division spans c. 150 CE (the Indian source-strata of treatises by Nāgārjuna, Āryadeva, Dignāga) through 1911 (a late-Qīng vernacular gloss on *Dà zōngdì xuánwén běn lùn*), with the densest layers in (i) the fifth-/sixth-century translation projects of Kumārajīva, Paramārtha and Bodhiruci, (ii) the Táng Xuánzàng–Yìjìng era, (iii) the Northern Sòng translation bureau under Tiānxīsāi 天息災, Dānapāla 施護 and Dharmadeva 法天 (c. 982–1000), and (iv) the Heian–Kamakura and late-Míng waves of native East-Asian commentary on *yīnmíng* and *Qǐxìnlùn*. The principal transmissional issue throughout is the apocryphal or pseudepigraphic status of several of its most influential texts — *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn*, *Shì móhēyǎn lùn*, *Zhāng suǒzhī lùn*, the Sino-Tibetan-Mongol *Bāsībā* tract — for which East-Asian and modern scholarship has reached very different conclusions.

### Important texts and text clusters

- **Yīnmíng 因明 (Buddhist logic) cluster** — *Yīnmíng zhènglǐmén lùn běn* 因明正理門論本 [[KR6o0001]] (Dignāga 大域龍, tr. Xuánzàng), *Yīnmíng rù zhènglǐ lùn* 因明入正理論 [[KR6o0003]] (Śaṅkarasvāmin 商羯羅主, tr. Xuánzàng), and a parallel re-translation by Yìjìng [[KR6o0002]] form the doctrinal core of East-Asian Buddhist logic; the commentarial chain runs through Shéntài 神泰 [[KR6o0007]], Kuījī 窺基's *Yīnmíng dàshū* 因明大疏 [[KR6o0008]], Wǒnhyo's *Pànbǐliàng lùn* 判比量論 [[KR6o0034]], the Heian *Yīnmíng lùnshū míngdēng chāo* 因明論疏明燈抄 [[KR6o0009]] of Zenju 善珠, and the Edo *Yīnmíng dàshū róngguàn chāo* [[KR6o0011]] of Hōtan 鳳潭.
- **Nāgārjuna's logical-Madhyamaka treatises** — *Huízhèng lùn* 迴諍論 [[KR6o0004]] (*Vigrahavyāvartanī*), *Fāngbiàn xīn lùn* 方便心論 [[KR6o0005]] (*Upāyahṛdaya*), and *Rúshí lùn* 如實論 [[KR6o0006]] (*Tarkaśāstra*, tr. Paramārtha) preserve the earliest layer of Indian Buddhist epistemology.
- **Satyasiddhi-śāstra** — *Chéngshí lùn* 成實論 [[KR6o0050]] (Harivarman 訶梨跋摩, tr. Kumārajīva 411–412); the foundational text of the short-lived Chinese 成實宗 school and a major bridge between Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma and emerging Mahāyāna.
- **Doctrinal tracts of the Paramārtha and Bodhiruci circles** — *Suíxiāng lùn* 隨相論 [[KR6o0045]], *Sìdì lùn* 四諦論 [[KR6o0051]], *Lìshì āpítán lùn* 立世阿毘曇論 [[KR6o0048]], *Shíèr yīnyuán lùn* 十二因緣論 [[KR6o0055]], *Típó púsà pò lèngqié jīng zhōng wàidào xiǎochéng sìzōng lùn* 提婆菩薩破楞伽經中外道小乘四宗論 [[KR6o0043]] and its companion 涅槃論 [[KR6o0044]]; together they constitute the sixth-century North Indian doxographic and ontological strata in Chinese translation.
- **Bodhicitta cluster** — Centred on the *Pútí xíng jīng* 菩提行經 [[KR6o0067]] (Śāntideva's *Bodhicaryāvatāra* in Tiānxīzāi's Sòng-period rendering), with *Pútí zīliáng lùn* 菩提資糧論 [[KR6o0065]], *Guǎng shì pútíxīn lùn* 廣釋菩提心論 [[KR6o0069]], and the Vajrayāna *Jīngāngdǐng yújiā zhōng fā ānòuduōluó sānmiǎo sānpútí xīn lùn* 金剛頂瑜伽中發阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心論 [[KR6o0070]] (attr. Nāgārjuna, tr. Amoghavajra 不空), which became the principal *bodhicitta* text of Shingon and attracted a dense Heian–Kamakura commentarial layer ([[KR6o0071]]–[[KR6o0075]]).
- **Awakening of Faith corpus** — *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn* 大乘起信論 attr. Aśvaghoṣa 馬鳴菩薩, in the Paramārtha [[KR6o0078]] and Śikṣānanda 實叉難陀 [[KR6o0079]] recensions; the single most consequential text of the division and arguably of East-Asian Buddhism, generating an enormous commentarial tradition represented here by Huìyuǎn 慧遠 of Jìngyǐng [[KR6o0100]], Wǒnhyo 元曉 [[KR6o0103]]/[[KR6o0104]], Fǎzàng 法藏 [[KR6o0105]]/[[KR6o0106]], Zǐxuán 子璿 [[KR6o0107]], Taehyŏn 太賢 [[KR6o0108]], Zhìxù 智旭 [[KR6o0109]], and late-Míng/Qīng glosses [[KR6o0114]]–[[KR6o0120]]; plus the Dūnhuáng-recovered Tánkuàng 曇曠 layer [[KR6o0121]]/[[KR6o0122]] and an anonymous *Qǐxìnlùn zhù* [[KR6o0123]] preserved as T2814.
- **Shì móhēyǎn lùn 釋摩訶衍論 sub-cluster** — *Shì móhēyǎn lùn* [[KR6o0084]], attributed to Nāgārjuna and a translator (Vṛddhimati 筏提摩多) for whom no other works survive; almost certainly a late-eighth-century Sino-Korean apocryphon, but enormously influential in Japanese Shingon and Tendai exegesis, with thirteen commentaries here ([[KR6o0085]]–[[KR6o0097]]).
- **Apocryphal and pseudepigraphic foundational treatises** — *Dà zōngdì xuánwén běn lùn* 大宗地玄文本論 [[KR6o0098]] attr. Mǎmíng / tr. Paramārtha (very probably a Sui-Tang Chinese composition); *Zhāng suǒzhī lùn* 彰所知論 [[KR6o0049]] composed in Chinese by ʼPhags-pa Blo-gros rgyal-mtshan 八思巴 in 1278 for Qubilai's heir Zhēnjīn 真金.
- **Nā xiān bǐqiū jīng 那先比丘經 (Milindapañha)** — Two parallel early Chinese recensions [[KR6o0124]] and [[KR6o0125]], the principal Chinese witness to the Theravāda *Milindapañha* tradition.
- **Stotra / verse-praise (zàn 讚 / sòng 頌 / gāthā 伽他) cluster** — Three Chinese translations of Nāgārjuna's *Suhṛllekha* (Letter to King Gautamīputra): *Lóngshù púsà wèi Chántuójiā wáng shuō fǎyào jì* 龍樹菩薩為禪陀迦王說法要偈 [[KR6o0127]], *Quànfā zhū wáng yào jì* 勸發諸王要偈 [[KR6o0128]], and *Lóngshù púsà quànjiè wáng sòng* 龍樹菩薩勸誡王頌 [[KR6o0129]]; plus *Yī bǎi wǔshí zàn fó sòng* 一百五十讚佛頌 [[KR6o0135]] (Mātṛceṭa, tr. Yìjìng), *Zàn fǎjiè sòng* 讚法界頌 [[KR6o0130]], *Fú gài zhèngxíng suǒjí jīng* 福蓋正行所集經 [[KR6o0126]], and the Sòng-period block of brief hymns [[KR6o0130]]–[[KR6o0143]].

### Important persons

- [[鳩摩羅什]] — translator of *Chéngshí lùn* (411–412); single most influential Latter-Qín translator represented in the division.
- [[真諦]] (Paramārtha) — chief translator of the sixth-century South Indian doctrinal layer: *Rúshí lùn*, *Suíxiāng lùn*, *Sìdì lùn*, *Lìshì āpítán lùn*, *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn* (KR6o0078), *Dà zōngdì xuánwén běn lùn*.
- [[玄奘]] — translator of the *yīnmíng* core (Dignāga's *Nyāyamukha*, Śaṅkarasvāmin's *Nyāyapraveśa*) and of doctrinal compendia (*Yuánshēng lùn*, *Pútí zīliáng lùn*), founder of the East-Asian *yīnmíng* tradition.
- [[義淨]] — re-translator of the *Nyāyamukha* [[KR6o0002]], the *Suhṛllekha* [[KR6o0129]], the *Bǎo xíng wáng zhèng lùn* [[KR6o0061]], and of several treatises (*Shǒuzhàng lùn* [[KR6o0062]], *Zhǐguān mén lùn sòng* [[KR6o0060]]) reflecting late-seventh-century Indian doctrinal style.
- [[施護]] (Dānapāla), [[法天]] (Dharmadeva, later [[法賢]]), [[天息災]] (Tiānxīzāi) — the three principal translators of the Northern Sòng 譯經院, responsible for the bulk of the *bodhicitta* and stotra texts dated c. 982–1017.
- [[龍樹菩薩]] (Nāgārjuna) — attributed author of *Huízhèng lùn*, *Bǎo xíng wáng zhèng lùn*, *Shì móhēyǎn lùn* (pseudepigraphically), the *Suhṛllekha*, and *Quànjiè wáng sòng*; the structural anchor of the division's Indian Madhyamaka layer.
- [[大域龍菩薩]] (Dignāga) and [[商羯羅主菩薩]] (Śaṅkarasvāmin) — Indian founders of Buddhist *pramāṇa* logic, whose texts head the division.
- [[馬鳴菩薩]] (Aśvaghoṣa, attributed) — pseudepigraphic author of the *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn* and *Dà zōngdì xuánwén běn lùn*.
- [[訶梨跋摩]] (Harivarman) — author of the *Chéngshí lùn*, eponym of the 成實宗.
- [[提婆]] (Āryadeva) — attributed author of the two *Lèngqié-jīng wàidào sìzōng / nièpán* treatises [[KR6o0043]]/[[KR6o0044]].
- [[窺基]] (Kuījī) — author of the *Yīnmíng rù zhènglǐ lùn shū* 因明大疏 [[KR6o0008]], standard East-Asian *yīnmíng* commentary.
- [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo) — Silla scholar; author of *Pànbǐliàng lùn* and two foundational *Qǐxìnlùn* commentaries [[KR6o0103]]/[[KR6o0104]].
- [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng) — Huáyán patriarch; author of the *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn yìjì* [[KR6o0105]], for centuries the dominant Chinese reading of the text.
- [[慧遠]] (Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn) — author of the earliest extant *Qǐxìnlùn yíshū* [[KR6o0100]].
- [[慧沼]] (Huìzhǎo), [[神泰]], [[文軌]] — Tang *yīnmíng* commentators whose works survive in part in T1839–1842 [[KR6o0007]]/[[KR6o0018]]/[[KR6o0019]]/[[KR6o0037]].
- [[曇曠]] (Tánkuàng) — eighth-century Dūnhuáng exegete; the *Qǐxìnlùn lüèshù* [[KR6o0121]] and *Guǎngshì* [[KR6o0122]] are the principal Dunhuang witnesses to the division.
- [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù) — late-Míng author of three works represented here: *Yīnmíng rù zhènglǐ lùn zhíjiě* [[KR6o0029]], *Liè wǎng shū* on the *Qǐxìnlùn* [[KR6o0109]], and *Yīnmíng zuǎn yào lüèjì*.
- [[八思巴]] (ʼPhags-pa) — Sa-skya hierarch and Yuán imperial preceptor, author of *Zhāng suǒzhī lùn* [[KR6o0049]] (1278).
- [[善珠]] (Zenju) and [[鳳潭]] (Hōtan) — Heian and Edo Japanese systematizers of East-Asian *yīnmíng*.

### Topics

- **Buddhist logic (yīnmíng 因明 / hetuvidyā)** — the structure of the three-membered syllogism (三支比量), the analysis of the nine reasons (九句因) and the four kinds of pseudo-reason (四相違), the boundary between perception (現量) and inference (比量); a topic that runs from Dignāga's *Nyāyamukha* [[KR6o0001]] through Kuījī's *Dàshū* [[KR6o0008]] and the Japanese *Sìxiāngwéi* sub-commentaries [[KR6o0014]]–[[KR6o0017]] to late-Míng synthetic restatements [[KR6o0026]]–[[KR6o0029]].
- **The status of the *Awakening of Faith* and the *Shì móhēyǎn lùn*** — whether they are translations of lost Indian originals or East-Asian (Chinese, Korean, or possibly Central-Asian) compositions; the textual history of the doubled *Qǐxìnlùn* translation under Paramārtha and Śikṣānanda; the philological independence (or not) of the *Shì móhēyǎn lùn* from the *Qǐxìnlùn* itself. This is the principal modern philological debate in the division.
- **Pseudepigraphy and translator-attribution forgery** — *Dà zōngdì xuánwén běn lùn* and *Shì móhēyǎn lùn* travel in the canon under unverifiable translator names (Paramārtha, Vṛddhimati 筏提摩多), allowing comparison of the formal traits by which East-Asian apocrypha were inserted into a canon of putative Indian śāstras.
- **The *bodhicitta* tradition's bifurcation** — between the exoteric, ethical reading represented by Śāntideva's *Bodhicaryāvatāra* [[KR6o0067]] and the esoteric, ritual reading represented by the Vajraśekhara *Pútíxīn lùn* [[KR6o0070]]; the latter's commentarial chain in Heian Japan is the densest sub-cluster in the division.
- **The 成實宗 question** — whether Harivarman's *Chéngshí lùn* belongs to Sautrāntika, to a transitional position between Sthaviravāda and Mahāyāna, or constitutes an independent doctrinal lineage; the disappearance of the Chinese 成實宗 school in the early Táng.
- **The fivefold Pratītyasamutpāda corpus** — five short treatises ([[KR6o0055]]–[[KR6o0059]]) translating overlapping Indian materials on dependent origination, of unusual value for comparative study because they preserve parallel Chinese renderings of related Sanskrit verse-cores.
- **The transmission of Buddhist verse genres into Chinese** — how *gāthā* / *stotra* / *sòng* forms were rendered, including the three-translation problem of Nāgārjuna's *Suhṛllekha* [[KR6o0127]]–[[KR6o0129]] and the metrical conventions of the Sòng translation bureau ([[KR6o0130]]–[[KR6o0143]]).
- **The Dūnhuáng *Qǐxìnlùn* layer** — the unique witness of Tánkuàng's mid-eighth-century *Qǐxìnlùn* lectures preserved only at Dunhuang (T2813–T2814 = [[KR6o0121]]/[[KR6o0122]]/[[KR6o0123]]), evidence for a North-West-Chinese reading tradition independent of the central Táng commentaries.

### Timeline

- c. 150–250 — *Yīnyuán xīn lùn sòng / shì* 因緣心論頌因緣心論釋 中文 [[KR6o0058]] — earliest stratum; attributed to Nāgārjuna in a short Indian verse-commentary form.
- 317–420 — *Nā xiān bǐqiū jīng* 那先比丘經 中文 [[KR6o0124]] / [[KR6o0125]] — Eastern Jìn anonymous translations of the *Milindapañha*; the earliest Chinese witness to the dialogue.
- c. 350–431 — *Sānmídǐbù lùn* 三彌底部論 中文 [[KR6o0053]] and *Pìzhīfó yīnyuán lùn* 辟支佛因緣論 中文 [[KR6o0054]] — anonymous Sixteen-Kingdoms translations of Sthaviravāda doctrinal tracts.
- 401–413 — *Fā pútíxīn jīng lùn* 發菩提心經論 中文 [[KR6o0064]] — Latter-Qín; attributed to the Kumārajīva circle.
- 411–412 — *Chéngshí lùn* 成實論 中文 [[KR6o0050]] — Kumārajīva's translation of Harivarman; foundational for the 成實宗.
- 426–437 — *Rù dàshèng lùn* 入大乘論 中文 [[KR6o0038]] — Liú-Sòng-period rendering attributed to Daoyao 道泰.
- 431 — *Lóngshù púsà wèi Chántuójiā wáng shuō fǎyào jì* 龍樹菩薩為禪陀迦王說法要偈 中文 [[KR6o0127]] — Guṇavarman 求那跋摩's translation of the *Suhṛllekha*.
- 434–442 — *Quànfā zhū wáng yào jì* 勸發諸王要偈 中文 [[KR6o0128]] — Saṅghavarman 僧伽跋摩's second *Suhṛllekha* translation.
- 435–468 — *Bīntóulú tūluóshé wèi Yōutuóyán wáng shuō fǎ jīng* 賓頭盧突羅闍為優陀延王說法經 中文 [[KR6o0145]] — early Sòng-period *Piṇḍolabhāradvāja* tract.
- 457 — *Qǐng bīntóulú fǎ* 請賓頭盧法 中文 [[KR6o0144]] — Huìjiǎn 慧簡; a ritual instruction for inviting the arhat.
- 472 — *Fāngbiàn xīn lùn* 方便心論 中文 [[KR6o0005]] — Jíjiāyè 吉迦夜's translation of the *Upāyahṛdaya*; early witness to Indian dialectic.
- 505–520 — *Jiětuō dào lùn* 解脫道論 中文 [[KR6o0052]] — Saṅghapāla 僧伽婆羅's Chinese *Vimuttimagga*; closely parallels the *Visuddhimagga*.
- 508–535 — *Típó púsà pò / shì lèngqié-jīng zhōng wàidào xiǎochéng sìzōng / nièpán lùn* 中文 [[KR6o0043]] / [[KR6o0044]], *Shíèr yīnyuán lùn* 十二因緣論 中文 [[KR6o0055]] — Bodhiruci 菩提流支 layer at Luòyáng.
- 541 — *Huízhèng lùn* 迴諍論 中文 [[KR6o0004]] — Pímùzhìxiān 毘目智仙 and Gautama Prajñāruci 瞿曇流支's Northern-Wèi rendering of Nāgārjuna's *Vigrahavyāvartanī*.
- 550–569 — *Rúshí lùn* 如實論 中文 [[KR6o0006]], *Suíxiāng lùn* 隨相論 中文 [[KR6o0045]], *Sìdì lùn* 四諦論 中文 [[KR6o0051]], *Lìshì āpítán lùn* 立世阿毘曇論 中文 [[KR6o0048]], *Bǎo xíng wáng zhèng lùn* 寶行王正論 中文 [[KR6o0061]] — Paramārtha 真諦's Liáng/Chén-period programme.
- c. 550–553 — *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn* 大乘起信論 中文 [[KR6o0078]] — traditionally Paramārtha's translation; modern philology dates the received Chinese text to mid-sixth-century China.
- 557–569 — *Dà zōngdì xuánwén běn lùn* 大宗地玄文本論 中文 [[KR6o0098]] — likely a Northern-Zhou or Suí-period Chinese composition transmitted under Paramārtha's name.
- 580–592 — *Dà shèng qǐxìn lùn yìshū* 大乘起信論義疏 中文 [[KR6o0100]] (Huìyuǎn of Jìngyǐng) — earliest extant *Qǐxìnlùn* commentary.
- 605–616 — *Yuánshēng lùn* 緣生論 中文 [[KR6o0056]], *Pútí zīliáng lùn* 菩提資糧論 中文 [[KR6o0065]] — Suí Dharmagupta 達摩笈多.
- 647–650 — *Yīnmíng rù zhènglǐ lùn* 因明入正理論 中文 [[KR6o0003]], *Yīnmíng zhènglǐmén lùn běn* 因明正理門論本 中文 [[KR6o0001]] — Xuánzàng's *yīnmíng* renderings, beginning Chinese Buddhist logic.
- 650–712 — Tang *Qǐxìnlùn* commentaries: Wǒnhyo 元曉 [[KR6o0103]]/[[KR6o0104]], Fǎzàng 法藏 [[KR6o0105]]/[[KR6o0106]].
- 660–686 — *Pànbǐliàng lùn* 判比量論 中文 [[KR6o0034]] (Wǒnhyo) and Kuījī 窺基's *Yīnmíng dàshū* [[KR6o0008]] — the apex of Sinitic *yīnmíng*.
- 671 — *Pànbǐliàng lùn* dated witness from Wǒnhyo's circle.
- 700–712 — Yìjìng 義淨's *yīnmíng* re-translation [[KR6o0002]], *Yī bǎi wǔshí zàn fó sòng* 一百五十讚佛頌 中文 [[KR6o0135]], *Lóngshù púsà quànjiè wáng sòng* 龍樹菩薩勸誡王頌 中文 [[KR6o0129]], *Zhǐguān mén lùn sòng* 止觀門論頌 中文 [[KR6o0060]], *Shǒuzhàng lùn* 手杖論 中文 [[KR6o0062]].
- c. 700–800 — *Shì móhēyǎn lùn* 釋摩訶衍論 中文 [[KR6o0084]] — the Sino-Korean apocryphal commentary, whose dating is the principal philological problem of the division's mid-Táng layer.
- 746–774 — Amoghavajra 不空 (《大乘緣生論》 *Dà shèng yuánshēng lùn* [[KR6o0057]], *Jīngāngdǐng pútíxīn lùn* 金剛頂菩提心論 [[KR6o0070]]) — Vajrayāna additions.
- 750–797 — *Yīnmíng lùnshū míngdēng chāo* 因明論疏明燈抄 中文 [[KR6o0009]] (Zenju 善珠) — first major Japanese *yīnmíng* commentary.
