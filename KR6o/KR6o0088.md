# Shì mó hē yǎn lùn kē 釋摩訶衍論科
*Topical Outline of the Shi Moheyan Lun*
by [[普觀]] (Pǔguān, 治定)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* topical outline (*kē* 科 — a structural diagram of the parent text) for the [[KR6o0084|*Shì Móhēyǎn lùn* 釋摩訶衍論]], compiled by the Sòng monk [[普觀]] (Pǔguān, also Pǔjì 普覺?) of *Yuánzhèngguāngyán wújì dàshī* 圓證光嚴無際大師 honorific. The work is preserved only in the *Manji-zokuzōkyō* 卍續藏經 (X46). A *kē* (科文) is the standard East Asian Buddhist genre of analytical structural outline — a hierarchical break-down of the parent text's argumentative structure used for memorisation and pedagogical exposition. It is conceptually parallel to a Western scholastic *summa* outline.

### Structural Division

The text *is* itself a structural outline; CANWWW does not record sub-divisions.

### Abstract

The text opens "釋摩訶衍論科卷下 / 皇宋圓證光嚴無際大師普觀治定" and proceeds with a hierarchical outline using the standard Sòng-period typography (○, *chū* 初, *èr* 二, *sān* 三, etc.). The author Pǔguān is also the compiler of [[KR6o0089]] (a longer prose record on the same parent text); the two works are paired (the *kē* is the structural outline, the *jì* is the running commentary). Both are preserved together in the *Manji-zokuzōkyō*. Pǔguān is otherwise poorly attested in standard biographical sources (*Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* 宋高僧傳); internal evidence places him loosely in the Northern or Southern Sòng period. His honorific *Yuánzhèngguāngyán wújì dàshī* suggests he was a Buddhist scholastic of some standing at one of the major Sòng monastic complexes.

## Translations and research

- Plassen, Jörg. Recent works on the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* commentarial tradition.

## Other points of interest

This is one of two paired Sòng works by the same author on the *Shì Móhēyǎn lùn* (the other being [[KR6o0089]]). Together they constitute the principal Northern Sòng commentary on the parent treatise.
