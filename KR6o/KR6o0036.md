# Zhēn wéishí liàng lüè jiě 真唯識量略解
*Brief Explanation of the True Consciousness-Only Syllogism*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 略解)

## About the work

A short late-Míng / early-Qīng one-*juǎn* commentary by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒuyì 蕅益, 1599–1655) on [[玄奘]] (Xuánzàng)'s famous *Zhēn-wéishí liàng* 真唯識量 — the syllogism by which Xuánzàng is said to have established the Yogācāra thesis at the eighteen-day council of Kanyākubja under Harṣa. Like Míngyù's *Sān zhī bǐ liàng yì chāo* [[KR6o0035]], Zhìxù starts from the passage in Yánshòu's *Zōngjìng lù* and offers a careful philosophical exposition; but he goes further than Míngyù in providing a structural outline (序述, 正明, 結歎) and treating the syllogism as an exemplar of the entire Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng *yīnmíng* method.

### Abstract

The colophon "唐奘師真唯識量略解 / 宋永明壽禪師宗鏡錄中節出 / 明藕益釋智旭略解" identifies the three layers: Xuánzàng's original (Táng), Yánshòu's *Zōngjìng lù* excerpt (Sòng), and Zhìxù's commentary (Míng). The structure is in three parts: (1) 敘述 (narrative — the historical setting at Kanyākubja); (2) 正明 (proper exposition — the syllogism analysed as 宗-因-喻); (3) 結歎 (concluding praise). Zhìxù treats the syllogism as a paradigm case of how the Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng method must use the very precise restrictions on *parārthaanumāna* (other-directed inference), and especially the *zìxǔ* 自許 (self-conceded) and *biǎnshì* 遍是 conventions, to prevent the syllogism from collapsing into self-contradiction. Composition is in Zhìxù's mature period at Línshān 靈山 in Zhèjiāng, c. 1635–1655. The work is a much shorter and more accessible companion to his *Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn zhí jiě* [[KR6o0029]] and was used as introductory teaching material in early-Qīng Buddhist seminaries.
