# Qǐxìn lùn chāochū 起信論抄出
*Extracted Digest of the Awakening-of-Faith Treatise*
by [[尊辨]] (Zūnbiàn / Sonben, 撰)

## About the work

A two-fascicle question-and-answer extracted digest by the medieval Japanese Hossō scholar [[尊辨]] (Sonben), of [[KR6o0078]] *Dà-chéng qǐ-xìn-lùn* 大乘起信論 (T1666), the *Awakening of Faith in the Mahāyāna* attributed to **Aśvaghoṣa** 馬鳴 and translated by [[真諦]] (Paramārtha). Preserved in Taishō vol. 69 (no. 2283). The Japanese title is *Kishin-ron shōshutsu*. The work also engages closely with the *Yìjì* 義記 commentary of [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng) ([[KR6o0105]]).

### Prefaces

The work has no formal authorial preface. It opens directly with the first scholastic question, framing the central tension between *nirvikalpa-jñāna* (the non-discriminative wisdom) and the *dharmas* it cognises:

> 問。無分別智分別法相可云耶 答。爾也  
> 付之。無分別智者。照如理一味。何云分別法相耶。是以説法之用預後得智。故報化二身説法。不論法身説法之義。若無分別智云分別法相者。可云法身説法也。如何  
> 答。凡任高祖所釋。無分別智分別法相云事。實如御難。雖可云後得智ト無分別智是其本也。

"Q: May one say that the *nirvikalpa-jñāna* (non-discriminative wisdom) discriminates the *dharma-lakṣaṇas*? A: It may. — But: the non-discriminative wisdom illuminates suchness with a uniform flavour, how can it discriminate the *dharma-lakṣaṇas*? Hence the function of preaching the Dharma is assigned to the *pṛṣṭha-labdha-jñāna* (subsequently-attained wisdom); therefore the *saṃbhoga-kāya* and *nirmāṇa-kāya* preach the Dharma, while the *dharma-kāya* is not said to preach. If we say that the non-discriminative wisdom discriminates the *dharma-lakṣaṇas*, we would have to say that the *dharma-kāya* preaches. How is this to be resolved?"

The opening *kana*-style interlinear marks (ト, ヌレハ, ヲ) are characteristic of late-Heian Hossō scholastic annotation and locate the work in that period.

### Abstract

Sonben's *Shōshutsu* approaches the *Awakening of Faith* from the Hossō perspective: the central scholastic question is the relation between *nirvikalpa-jñāna* and *pṛṣṭha-labdha-jñāna* — the two-fold wisdom of the bodhisattva-path as developed in the Yogācāra-school *Mahāyāna-sūtrālaṃkāra* / *Madhyāntavibhāga* tradition. The *Qǐ-xìn-lùn*'s doctrine of *one-mind-two-gates* (一心二門) is recast through this Hossō lens, with the *suchness* gate identified with the *nirvikalpa* wisdom and the *birth-and-death* gate with the *pṛṣṭha-labdha* function.

This reading is methodologically distinct from the Huayan-Kegon reading of [[KR6o0105]] *Yìjì* by [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng), which Sonben cites by short title as "高祖所釋" ("the lofty patriarch's exposition"). Sonben does not reject Fǎzàng but augments him with the Hossō *bùfēnzhì* / *hòudézhì* taxonomy, producing a synthesis characteristic of late-Heian Kōfuku-ji scholastic method.

Composition window: c. 1100–1180, late Heian period.

## Other points of interest

The *Shōshutsu*'s identification of the *Qǐ-xìn-lùn*'s "one mind two gates" with the Hossō two-fold wisdom is an important documentary witness to late-Heian Hossō engagement with the *Awakening of Faith*. The Hossō school had a longstanding doctrinal tension with the *Qǐ-xìn-lùn* (whose *suchness-conditioned-arising* doctrine sits uneasily with the Hossō *ālaya-vijñāna* doctrine), and the *Shōshutsu*'s attempt to incorporate the *Qǐ-xìn-lùn* doctrines into the Hossō framework is a notable instance of inter-school doctrinal accommodation.
