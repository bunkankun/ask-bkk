# Jīnlù shíhuí dùrén wǎncháo kāishōu yí 金籙十迴度人晚朝開收儀
*Late-Audience Opening-and-Closing Liturgy for the Ten-Cycle Salvation of Humanity in the Golden-Register Fast*

## About the work

Third and concluding piece of the *shí-huí dù-rén kāi-shōu* triad ([[KR5b0186]], [[KR5b0187]], the present text), under 三儀同卷軆九.

### Abstract

The evening *kāi-shōu* handles the final two *huí* (9 and 10) of the recitation cycle plus the cumulative *shōu-jīng* of the whole day's ten-fold sequence. The dedication paragraphs of the evening *huí* are correspondingly the longest and most elaborate, addressing the cosmic salvation of the dead in all ten regions, the *qī-zǔ* 七祖 (seven generations of ancestors), and the protection of the *bāng-jiā* 邦家 (the realm). Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 996–997, John Lagerwey) underline the function of the *kāi-shōu* set as the structural skeleton of the *Jīnlù*'s scriptural-recitation phase, complementing the recitation rites proper of [[KR5b0189]]–[[KR5b0191]].
