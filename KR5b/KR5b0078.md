# Tàiqīng tàiyī jīnquè yùxǐ jīnzhēn jì 太清太一金闕玉璽金真紀
*Annals of the Golden Zhēnrén of the Jade Seal of the Golden Portal of the Supreme One, of the Great Clarity*

(The catalog reads 太清; the source file title reads 上清. The *Taoist Canon* follows the source-file *Shàngqīng* reading.)

## About the work

An eight-folio Táng Shàngqīng apocryphon, transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* with DZ 392 and DZ 393. The text as a whole is an amalgam of fragments.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with the sub-titled *Dùmìng fēixiān fú* 度命飛仙符 ("Talisman of the Flying Immortal for Delivering Destiny") and its cognates, and carries no author preface or transmission colophon.

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Robinet (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 613–614, DZ 394). The title is inspired by a title in the biography of Péi jūn 裴君 in *Yúnjí qīqiān* 105.9b. The present text is quoted under a simplified title *Tàiyī jīnzhēn jì* 太一金真紀 in *Sāndòng zhūnáng* 三洞珠囊 5.4a and 3b, and in DZ 1132 *Shàngqīng dào lèishì xiàng* 2.1b; a passage on 5a is cited in *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽 679.7b. The text therefore circulated under the Táng.

The greater part of the text consists of quotations from the *Péi jūn* biography mentioned above, together with references to texts outside the Shàngqīng revelation; one long passage (4b) is even entirely alien to the Shàngqīng tradition. Robinet's characterisation as "apocryphal" reflects this patchwork composition.
