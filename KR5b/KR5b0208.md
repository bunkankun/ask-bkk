# Yùlù dàzhāi dìsānrì wǔcháo yí 玉籙大齋第三日午朝儀
*Noon-Audience Liturgy of the Third Day of the Great Jade-Register Fast*

## About the work

Last of the eight-part **Yùlù dàzhāi** programme (DZ 505 a–h). The noon audience of day 3 is the final regular audience of the fast; the closing *wǎn-cháo* function is performed not by a parallel *wǎncháo yí* but by the dedicated *jì-yōu pàn-hú* 濟幽判斛 salvation rite that follows in DZ 506 ([[KR5b0209]]). Thus the three-day, nine-audience programme is structurally 3 + 3 + 2 ordinary audiences plus one specialised closing salvation, totalling nine offices.

### Abstract

The noon rite of day 3 carries the culminating dedication: the merit of the preceding eight audiences is brought to a focus on the *Dōng-jí Qīng-xuán shàng-dì* 東極青玄上帝 and the *Tài-yī jiù-kǔ tiān-zūn* 太乙救苦天尊, the salvific deities who will receive the deceased in the eastern paradise. The body of the fascicle includes the *jiǔ-zhuǎn shēng-shén* 九轉生神 (nine-fold regeneration of the spirit), the *tōu-jiǎn* 投簡 (tablet-deposit) addressed to the celestial *zhēng-yīn* 徵引 (summoners), and the *kāi-mén* 開門 (opening of the celestial gates) by which the released souls ascend. The final *huí-xiàng* anticipates the immediately following *jì-yōu pàn-hú* rite, in which the salvific deliverance is concretely effected.
