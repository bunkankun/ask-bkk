# Yùlù dàzhāi dìèrrì wǎncháo yí 玉籙大齋第二日晚朝儀
*Evening-Audience Liturgy of the Second Day of the Great Jade-Register Fast*

## About the work

Sixth of the eight-part **Yùlù dàzhāi** programme (DZ 505 a–h). The evening audience of day 2 stands at the structural pivot of the three-day fast — the close of the central day, after which the rite turns toward the concluding *jì-yōu* salvation phase.

### Abstract

The evening rite closes the day with the final ninth *huí* of the central scripture-recitation cycle, plus a transitional *huí-xiàng* anticipating the next day's *jì-yōu pàn-hú* salvation rite ([[KR5b0209]]). The text retains the standard *wǎncháo* dedications to the *yīn-fǔ* deities and the *gū-yōu* of the *jiǔ-yōu*. Following the dedication, the rite includes the special evening *xiè-tiān-dì* 謝天地 (thanksgiving to Heaven and Earth) which seals the salvific *huí* of the day. The structural symmetry of the *Yùlù* programme — three days × three audiences × three incense-pinches — makes this fascicle the deepest interior point of the rite, the one furthest from both the opening *sùqǐ* and the closing *yán-gōng* 言功.
