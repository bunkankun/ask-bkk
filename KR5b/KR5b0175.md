# Jīnlù jiětán yí 金籙解壇儀
*Altar-Dismissal Liturgy of the Golden-Register Fast*

## About the work

The ninth of the Dù Guāngtíng **Jīnlù** 金籙 cycle ([[KR5b0167]]–[[KR5b0181]]); the *jiětán yí* 解壇儀 ("altar-dismissal liturgy") closes the multi-day *Jīnlù* rite by formally dissolving the ritual demarcation of the altar space. The text is transmitted in a single fascicle (三儀同卷軆五, *Tǐ* 5) together with [[KR5b0176]] *shèjiào yí* and [[KR5b0177]] *fàngshēng yí* — the offering, the release-of-life, and the altar-dismissal — which together close the rite.

### Abstract

Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 996, entry by John Lagerwey). The *jiětán* sequence: *xièzhēn* 謝真 (thanks to the descended deities); formal *sòngshèng* 送聖 dismissal; dispersal of the altar implements; lifting of the *jiéjiè* spatial boundary; and the *bǔ* 卜 divination to verify the rite's success. The procedure was already articulated in the Six Dynasties Lingbao *zhāi* tradition but receives its standard articulation here in the Dù Guāngtíng synthesis.
