# Yùlù dàzhāi dìsānrì zǎocháo yí 玉籙大齋第三日早朝儀
*Morning-Audience Liturgy of the Third Day of the Great Jade-Register Fast*

## About the work

Seventh of the eight-part **Yùlù dàzhāi** programme (DZ 505 a–h). The morning audience of day 3 opens the concluding day of the fast, the day on which the cumulative merit of the preceding two days of *zhuǎn-jīng* is dedicated to the deceased and the rite is concluded with the *jì-yōu pàn-hú* 濟幽判斛 closing salvation ([[KR5b0209]]).

### Abstract

The third-day morning audience follows the established frame (*fā-lú*, *qǐng-chēng fǎ-wèi*, three *niǎn-xiāng*) but with significant new ritual elements directed to the closing of the rite: the *xuān-bǎng* 宣牓 (public proclamation of the rite's accomplishment), the *zhāo-zhào* 召召 summoning of the named *gū-yōu* by name, and a preparatory *kāi-yù* 開獄 (opening of the underworld prisons) sequence that anticipates the final *pàn-hú* judgment. The merit of the day is dedicated specifically to the release of the named souls and to the imperial *zōng-tiào*; the orientation is by this point fully toward salvific deliverance.
