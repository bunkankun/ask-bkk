# Jīnlù wǎncháo yí 金籙晚朝儀
*Evening-Audience Liturgy of the Golden-Register Fast*

## About the work

The seventh of the Dù Guāngtíng **Jīnlù** 金籙 cycle ([[KR5b0167]]–[[KR5b0181]]); the evening audience of the *Jīnlù*-fast daily *cháo* sequence, transmitted with [[KR5b0171]] and [[KR5b0172]] as 三儀同卷. The opening hymn *Shénguāng lǎngyào* 神光朗曜 ("Divine light shines forth, auspicious vapour wreathes round, the Winnowing-and-Dipper purified in luminance, *zhīyǔ* tranquil; jade-eye and golden-perfected, the treasure-altar pure Qián") is closely parallel to that of [[KR5b0156]] *Hóng'ēn Língjì wǎncháo* — evidence that the early-Míng *Língjì* cycle inherited its *cháo* diction from this Tang prototype.

### Abstract

The evening audience presents the *wǎnzhāng* 晚章 memorial to the celestial bureau at the *yǒu* (5–7 p.m.) hour. Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 994–996, entry by John Lagerwey). The text closes with the *yǐnshēng* 引聲 dismissal and the *huíxiàng* dedication of the day's accumulated merit toward the imperial-state purposes of the *Jīnlù* rite.
