# Língbǎo jìng-míng huáng-sù shū shì-yì mì-jué 靈寶淨明黃素書釋義祕訣
*Secret Formulae for the Exegesis of the Língbǎo Jìng-míng Yellow-Silk Book*
by [[周方文]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juǎn doctrinal supplement to the *Língbǎo jìng-míng huáng-sù shū* [[KR5b0259]] (DZ 555), compiled by the Jìng-míng disciple [[周方文]] (*Zhōu zhēn-gōng* 周真公). Where the parent scripture is structured as cosmogonic revelation with [[傅飛卿]]'s exegetical commentary, this *mì-jué* is structured as a question-and-answer between Fāngwén and his Perfected Master, in which the former lays out the technical doctrinal puzzles of the *Huáng-sù shū* and the latter resolves them.

### Abstract

The text opens "*Dìzǐ Fāng-wén jǐn jī-shǒu, zhēn-shī gǎn-wèn Huáng-sù shū shù-shì, fú-wàng yù-zhǐ zhēn-zhǐ gēn-yuán fēn-míng chuí-jiào*…" 弟子方文謹稽首，真師敢問黃素書數事，伏望玉旨真指根源分明垂教 ("Your disciple Fāngwén respectfully prostrates; he ventures to ask the Perfected Master about several matters in the *Huáng-sù shū*, hoping that the master's jade utterance and true designation will clearly indicate their roots and bestow instruction"). The questions then range across the *Huáng-sù shū*'s key technical formulae: *wǔ-qì cháo-yuán sān-yáng jù-dǐng* 五炁朝元三陽聚頂 ("the five qì come to court at the Origin; the three yáng assemble at the crown"), pondered against the Zhōnglí-Lǚ formula *sān-huā jù-dǐng* 三花聚頂; the formula *dāng sī yī-shēn hé suǒ cóng lái* 當思一身何所從來 ("contemplate whence this single body has come"); and so on through the major alchemical heads. The master's answers harmonise the *Huáng-sù* formulae with the *Zhōng-Lǚ* alchemical lore.

Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1094, John Lagerwey) treat the work as the most explicit *Jìng-míng* doctrinal complement to the parent scripture. The text is one of the principal sources for understanding how the Sòng-Yuán *Jìng-míng* tradition absorbed and re-narrated the *Zhōng-Lǚ* (Zhōnglí Quán + Lǚ Dòngbīn) *nèi-dān* alchemy.
