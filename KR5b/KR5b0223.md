# Tàishàng língbǎo yùguì míngzhēn dàzhāi chànfāng yí 太上靈寶玉匱明真大齋懺方儀
*Liturgy of Confession-by-Direction in the Great-High Língbǎo Jade-Casket Luminous-Perfected Great Fast*

by [[杜光庭]] (修)

## About the work

The "great-fast" (*dà-zhāi* 大齋) expansion of the *Yùguì míngzhēn chànfāng yí* (cf. [[KR5b0222]]), again attributed to **Dù Guāngtíng** 杜光庭. In the canonical bundling the two share one physical fascicle (*二儀同卷化三*). Where [[KR5b0222]] gives the abbreviated everyday version, the present work is the full-scale directional confession for the climactic *dà-zhāi* of the *Yùguì míngzhēn* programme.

### Abstract

The work is organised by direction: *dōng-fāng* 東方, *nán-fāng* 南方, *xī-fāng* 西方, *běi-fāng* 北方, *shàng-fāng* 上方, *xià-fāng* 下方, *dōng-běi*, *dōng-nán*, *xī-nán*, *xī-běi* — the ten directions of the Daoist mandala. For each direction the rite invokes by name and title the local infinite-Tàishàng-Língbǎo tiān-zūn, the local *yǐ-dé-dào dà-shèng-zhòng zhì-zhēn zūn-shén* 巳得道大聖衆至眞尊神, and a long enumerated pantheon of subordinate deities — beginning with the *Tài-qīng Xuán-yuán wú-shàng sān-tiān wú-jí dà-dào Wú-shàng Xuán-yuán lǎo-jūn* 太清玄元無上三天無極大道無上玄元老君, the *Tài-shàng zhàng-rén* 太上丈人, the *Huáng-shàng lǎo-jūn* 皇上老君, the *Huáng-shàng zhàng-rén* 皇上丈人, the *Qīng-líng shàng-zhēn tiān-dì-jūn* 青靈上眞天帝君, etc. — and offers the confessional declaration. The east-facing first section names the *Hòu-shèng Dì-jūn* 後聖帝君, the *Qīng-tóng-jūn* 青童君, the *Jīn-quē hòu-shèng Dì-jūn* 金闕後聖帝君, and the *Língbǎo jiǔ-xiān-jūn* 靈寳九仙君 among others — a roster characteristic of Dù's *Língbǎo* synthesis. Per Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1083, John Lagerwey, DZ 520), the work is structurally the largest and ritually most elaborate of the three *Yùguì míngzhēn* liturgies.
