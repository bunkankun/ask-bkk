# Língbǎo jìng-míng Tiānshū-yuàn dū-sī fǎ-yuàn xū-zhī fǎ-wén 靈寶淨明天樞院都司法院須知法文
*Method-Text to Be Known by the Law-Court of the Director-General's Office of the Língbǎo Jìng-míng Heavenly-Pivot Bureau*

## About the work

A short single-juǎn ritual handbook concluding the four-text *Tiānshū-yuàn* cluster of *Jìng-míng* 淨明 manuals (DZ 549 / [[KR5b0253]] – DZ 553). Where the preceding *Lìng* ([[KR5b0255]]) and *Gé* ([[KR5b0256]]) fix the Bureau's administrative statutes and penal regulations, this manual gives the practical *fǎ-wén* 法文 — the methodical text of the rite — by which an officer of the *Dū-sī* internally cultivates his office.

### Abstract

The opening section, *Tiānshū dū-lù xíng-chí fǎ* 天樞都録行持法 ("Procedure for the Maintenance of the Office, by the Director-Registrar of the Heavenly-Pivot"), declares the bureaucratic ethos of the Bureau: as *Tiān-mén-sī yuàn* 天門司院, "Court-Office of the Heaven-Gate", it speaks for Heaven (*shàng-tiān hóu-shé* 上天喉舌), superintends the seventy-three sub-offices plus the seven *lù-shì* (so "seventy-four offices" *qī-shí-sì sī* 七十四司), and audits the *yīn-yáng* good-and-evil records of the Three Worlds. The text then turns to the internal-cultivation methods proper: the *yǎng-shén fǎ* 養神法 (sunrise visualisation of the sun's red qì entering the crown and pairing with the moon's white qì in the breast to form an inner cinnabar pellet), the *bì-qì* 閉炁 visualisations involving the Western Queen Mother and the *Yín-hàn* 銀漢 (Silver River = Milky Way), and so on.

The text shares with [[KR5b0254]] (DZ 550) the *Shàngqīng* inner-anatomy vocabulary — *ní-wán* 泥丸, *jiàng-gōng* 絳宮 — and provides what Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1092–1093, John Lagerwey) describe as the technical *liàn-jīng* 鍊精 underpinning of the Bureau's executive office. The whole forms one of the most explicit accounts of how a Sòng-Yuán *fǎ-shī* 法師 was expected to internalise the celestial bureaucratic role in his own body.
