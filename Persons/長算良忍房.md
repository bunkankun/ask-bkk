# 長算良忍房

Late-Muromachi Japanese Shingon copyist; produced a *Bunmei*-era 文明 (1484, *jiǎchén* year) manuscript copy of Kūkai's *Rén-wáng-jīng kāití* 仁王經開題 ([[KR6c0218]], T56n2200) at age 22. The transmission witness preserved in the Taishō derives from his hand. No further biographical record beyond the colophon.
