Chéng Zhì 程智 (1602–1651), late-Míng / early-Qīng *Yìjīng* speculative theorist. His doctrine that the *Xìcí*'s *Dì chū hū Zhèn* 帝出乎震 preserves the substance of the lost *Xià*-dynasty *Lián shān* 連山 and *Kūn yǐ zàng zhī* 坤以藏之 preserves the substance of the *Yīn*-dynasty *Guī cáng* 歸藏 was adopted in [[趙繼序]] Zhào Jìxù's [[KR1a0161]] and rejected by the *Sìkù* editors as forced.
