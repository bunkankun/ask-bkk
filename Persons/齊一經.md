# 齊一經

Qí Yījīng of Běihǎi 北海 (Shāndōng), *jìnshì* of 1541, mid-Míng official (Vice Surveillance Commissioner of Hénán etc.). Wrote the preface to the earliest known printed edition of [[KR3em011]] *Yínhǎi jīngwēi* 銀海精微, explicitly admitting the work's authorship is unknown. CBDB c_personid 132089.
