**Lǐ Ruò-shuǐ** 李若水 (1092–1126), *zì* Qīng-qīng 清卿, of Qū-zhōu 洺州 Qū-zhōu 曲周 (Hé-běi). *Jìnshì* of *Xuān-hé* 5 / 1123. Held office as *Tài-xué bó-shì*, then *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* under Qīnzōng. Famous *Jìng-kāng* loyalist martyr: when Kāi-fēng fell to the Jurchen in 1126 and Huīzōng and Qīnzōng were taken north, Lǐ accompanied Qīnzōng into Jin custody, refused to acknowledge Jin sovereignty, and was killed for cursing the Jin invaders to their faces. Posthumously canonised *Zhōng-mǐn* 忠愍 (Loyal-and-Lamented) under Gāozōng. Author of *Zhōng-mǐn jí* 忠愍集 [[KR4d0133]] in 3 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 446 (Zhōng-yì 1). Lifedates 1092–1126 confirmed by CBDB.
