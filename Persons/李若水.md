**Lǐ Ruòshuǐ** 李若水 (1092–1126), *zì* Qīngqīng 清卿, of Qūzhōu 洺州 Qūzhōu 曲周 (Héběi). *Jìnshì* of *Xuānhé* 5 / 1123. Held office as *Tàixué bóshì*, then *Lǐbù shàngshū* under Qīnzōng. Famous *Jìngkāng* loyalist martyr: when Kāifēng fell to the Jurchen in 1126 and Huīzōng and Qīnzōng were taken north, Lǐ accompanied Qīnzōng into Jin custody, refused to acknowledge Jin sovereignty, and was killed for cursing the Jin invaders to their faces. Posthumously canonised *Zhōngmǐn* 忠愍 (Loyal-and-Lamented) under Gāozōng. Author of *Zhōngmǐn jí* 忠愍集 [[KR4d0133]] in 3 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 446 (Zhōngyì 1). Lifedates 1092–1126 confirmed by CBDB.
