**Yuán Miàozōng** 元妙宗 was a late-Northern-Sòng ritualist, author of [[KR5g0036]] ***Tài shàng zhù guó jiù mín zǒng zhēn bì yào*** 太上助國救民總真祕要 (preface 1116). Yuán Miàozōng's ritual-compendium was sponsored by the Huī-zōng court's program of Daoist institutional consolidation.
