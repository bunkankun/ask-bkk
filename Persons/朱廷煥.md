Zhū Tínghuàn 朱廷煥 (*fl. Chóngzhēn*; *jìnshì* 1634), *zì* Zhōngbái 中白, native of Shànxiàn 單縣 in Shāndōng. Late-Míng official; *Chóngzhēn jiǎxū* (= 1634) *jìnshì*. Held office as *Gōngbù zhǔshì* (Ministry-of-Works Section-chief) in charge of the Hángzhōu transit-tax station, during which time (ca. 1634–1644) he produced the *Zēngbǔ Wǔlín jiùshì* 增補武林舊事 ([[KR2k0120]]) — a supplemented and re-organised 8-juan edition of Zhōu Mì's *Wǔlín jiùshì*, drawing additional material from the *Xīhú zhì*, *Hèlín yùlù*, *Róngzhāi suíbǐ*, *Chuōgēng lù*, and *Guǐxīn záshí*. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 127266 (the second CBDB hit, 350670, is a Qīng-period homonym).
