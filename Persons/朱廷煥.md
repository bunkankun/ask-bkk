Zhū Tíng-huàn 朱廷煥 (*fl. Chóngzhēn*; *jìnshì* 1634), *zì* Zhōng-bái 中白, native of Shàn-xiàn 單縣 in Shāndōng. Late-Míng official; *Chóngzhēn jiǎ-xū* (= 1634) *jìnshì*. Held office as *Gōng-bù zhǔ-shì* (Ministry-of-Works Section-chief) in charge of the Hángzhōu transit-tax station, during which time (ca. 1634–1644) he produced the *Zēngbǔ Wǔlín jiùshì* 增補武林舊事 ([[KR2k0120]]) — a supplemented and re-organised 8-juan edition of Zhōu Mì's *Wǔlín jiùshì*, drawing additional material from the *Xī-hú zhì*, *Hè-lín yù-lù*, *Róng-zhāi suí-bǐ*, *Chuō-gēng lù*, and *Guǐxīn zá-shí*. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 127266 (the second CBDB hit, 350670, is a Qīng-period homonym).
