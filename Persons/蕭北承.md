# Xiāo Běichéng 蕭北承

Late-Qīng *xiàolián* 孝廉 (provincial graduate); head of the Wǔchāng yīguǎn 武昌醫館 under [[柯逢時]] Kē Féngshí. Edited the 1910 critical reprint of the anonymous Sòng paediatric encyclopaedia *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* 小兒衛生總微論方 ([[KR3ej017]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
