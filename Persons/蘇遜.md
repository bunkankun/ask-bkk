Sū Xùn 蘇遜, son of Sū Zhé 蘇轍 (1039–1112; see [[蘇轍]]). Known almost exclusively for the supplementary notes (*fùzhù* 附註) attached to his father's *Gǔshǐ* 古史 ([[KR2d0005]]); the Sìkù editors identified him as the author of these notes (which the old editions had carried anonymously) on the strength of Yè Dàqìng 葉大慶's *Kǎogǔ zhìyí* 考古質疑. CBDB id 19181 (no dates). Otherwise unrecorded in the historical record beyond his father's *Luánchéng* circle.
