# Zhuāng Guǎng 莊廣

Late-Míng lay-Buddhist scholar, *hào* **Huánrú lǎorén** 還如老人 ("Old Man of Returning-As-Such") and **Xīfāng jūshì** 西方居士 ("Layman of the West"). His exact lifedates are not preserved; he was active in the late Wànlì (萬曆) reign and was a serious lay disciple in the Yúnqī Zhūhóng [[袾宏]] circle. His major surviving work is the **《淨土資糧全集》** *Jìngtǔ zīliáng quánjí* [[KR6p0065]] (X1162, 6 *juǎn*), a comprehensive Pure Land anthology compiled by Zhuāng Guǎng (under his sobriquet Huánrú) and edited / collated by [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng. The work was completed in the mid-Wànlì period and is one of the principal lay-compiled Pure Land anthologies of the late Míng. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
