束皙, styled 廣微, was a Western Jin scholar from 陽平元城. He served as an official in the imperial library and was tasked with transcribing and editing the bamboo-strip texts discovered in 279 CE at the tomb of King Xiang of Wei in Jizhong (汲郡). He compiled the 汲冢書鈔 (*Jizhong shu chao*), a summary of the excavated texts, and also authored a preface to the 穆天子傳. He is also known for his *fù* rhapsodies and other literary works.
