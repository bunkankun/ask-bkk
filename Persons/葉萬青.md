Yè Wànqīng 葉萬青, *fl.* early nineteenth century, *yuánsūn* 元孫 (great-great-grandson) of [[葉桂]] Yè Guì (Yè Tiānshì, 1666–1745). Sūzhōu physician, custodian of the Yè-family manuscript-archive. His 1832 editorial compilation [[KR3ep079]] *Yèshì yīàn cúnzhēn* preserves Yè Tiānshì cases not included in Huá Xiùyún's 1764 *Línzhèng zhǐnán yīàn* ([[KR3ep010]]), plus appended cases of Yè's Sūzhōu contemporaries Mǎ Yuányí 馬元儀, Qí Zhèngmíng 祁正明, and Wáng Jìnsān 王晉三 (i.e. [[王子接]]). No further transmitted biographical record.
