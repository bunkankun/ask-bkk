Yè Wàn-qīng 葉萬青, *fl.* early nineteenth century, *yuán-sūn* 元孫 (great-great-grandson) of [[葉桂]] Yè Guì (Yè Tiān-shì, 1666–1745). Sūzhōu physician, custodian of the Yè-family manuscript-archive. His 1832 editorial compilation [[KR3ep079]] *Yè-shì yī-àn cún-zhēn* preserves Yè Tiān-shì cases not included in Huá Xiù-yún's 1764 *Lín-zhèng zhǐ-nán yī-àn* ([[KR3ep010]]), plus appended cases of Yè's Sūzhōu contemporaries Mǎ Yuán-yí 馬元儀, Qí Zhèng-míng 祁正明, and Wáng Jìn-sān 王晉三 (i.e. [[王子接]]). No further transmitted biographical record.
