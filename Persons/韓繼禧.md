# 韓繼禧 Hán Jìxǐ

**Hán Jìxǐ** 韓繼禧 (Korean: **Han Kye-hŭi** 한계희, 1423–1482), Chosŏn-period royal scholar, ennobled as Prince of Sŏp'yŏng 西平君; wrote the 1474 preface to the Korean reprint of [[陳會]] Chén Huì's *Shényìng jīng* 神應經 ([[KR3ee029]]) at the court of King Sŏngjong.
