Xú Qiáo 徐僑 (c. 1160–1237), *zì* Chóngfǔ 崇甫, *hào* Yìzhāi 毅齋, of Yìwū 義烏. *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī* 14 (1187); disciple of [[呂祖謙]] Lǚ Zǔqiān and later of [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī. Held a series of regional posts; rose to *Gōngbù shìláng* 工部侍郎 and *Bǎowénge xuéshì* 寶文閣學士. Composed *Dú Yì jì* 讀易記 and *Shàngshū kuòzhǐ* 尚書括旨. *Sòngshǐ* biography in juan 422. One of the candidate-identifications offered by the Sìkù editors for the unnamed "Xú Zǒnggàn" author of *Yì chuán dēng* ([[KR1a0044]]), though no positive evidence resolves the attribution.
