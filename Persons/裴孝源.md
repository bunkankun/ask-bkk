# Péi Xiàoyuán 裴孝源

Native-place not securely recorded. Active in the Zhēn-guān (627-649) era of Tang Tài-zōng. Held the office of *Zhōng-shū shè-rén* (Secretariat Drafter). Birth and death years not securely recorded.

The author of the *Zhēn-guān gōng-sī huà-shǐ* 貞觀公私畫史 ([[KR3h0004]]) — the foundational Tang inventory of paintings in imperial-and-private collections — composed at the request of Hàn-wáng Lǐ Yuánchāng 漢王李元昌 in Zhēn-guān 13 (639). Per the Sìkù 提要, Péi Xiàoyuán also composed a separate *Huà-pǐn lù* (lost; cited by Zhāng Yànyuǎn's [[KR3h0009]] *Lì-dài míng-huà jì*) in the systematic-criticism style of [[KR3h0001]] *Gǔ-huà pǐn-lù*.
