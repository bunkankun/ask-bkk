# Péi Xiàoyuán 裴孝源

Native-place not securely recorded. Active in the Zhēnguān (627-649) era of Tang Tàizōng. Held the office of *Zhōngshū shèrén* (Secretariat Drafter). Birth and death years not securely recorded.

The author of the *Zhēnguān gōngsī huàshǐ* 貞觀公私畫史 ([[KR3h0004]]) — the foundational Tang inventory of paintings in imperial-and-private collections — composed at the request of Hànwáng Lǐ Yuánchāng 漢王李元昌 in Zhēnguān 13 (639). Per the Sìkù 提要, Péi Xiàoyuán also composed a separate *Huàpǐn lù* (lost; cited by Zhāng Yànyuǎn's [[KR3h0009]] *Lìdài mínghuà jì*) in the systematic-criticism style of [[KR3h0001]] *Gǔhuà pǐnlù*.
