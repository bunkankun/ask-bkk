**Zhāng Shòukǒng** 張受孔 (*fl.* late Ming). Late-Ming official whose principal documented activity is the final compilation and annotation of the *Yībiàn* 醫便 ([[KR3ed043]]) practical-clinical recipe handbook. The work descended through a multi-generational chain (Wáng *Shìyù* on Shǎnxī tour → Liú *Cān-zhī* on Wú-xìng prefectship → Chén Mín-lù *Zhí-zhǐ* on Qīngxī → Xú Yìngdēng's family → Zhāng Shòukǒng) and the *tí-gāng* "summary outline" apparatus that converts the work into a quick-reference tool is Zhāng's contribution. Not in CBDB; no biographical record outside the *Yībiàn* attribution.
