# Shàomíng 紹明

Nānpo Jōmyō 南浦紹明 (1235–1308), Japanese Rinzai monk who studied in China under Xūtáng Zhìyú; founder, with his disciple Shūhō Myōchō (Daitō Kokushi) and Kanzan Egen, of the Ōtōkan 応燈関 line that carried koan-practice Rinzai Zen forward in Japan.
