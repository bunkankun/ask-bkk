Late-Qīng physician of **Shān-yīn** 山陰 (modern Shào-xīng 紹興 in Zhèjiāng), active around the turn of the 20th century. Author of *Wēn-bìng zhǐ-nán* 溫病指南 ([[KR3eg007]]), preface dated Guāng-xù *guǐ-mǎo* (1903), composed during a long residence in Hé-nán as a clinical handbook for physicians of the inland provinces still using *Shāng-hán*-style therapy on warm disease. His pupil Xiāo Jí-fǔ 蕭吉甫 assisted in the editorial work. Lóu is doctrinally an heir to the southern *wēn-bìng* line of Wú Jūtōng ([[KR3eg010]]) — the *Wēn-bìng zhǐ-nán* explicitly cites Wú's *sān-jiāo* differentiation as its framework. No detailed biographical information preserved; CBDB entry 598805 lacks dates. The reading "Shān-yīn" is sometimes mistakenly given as "Jiāng-sū" in catalog meta — Shān-yīn is in fact in Zhèjiāng.
