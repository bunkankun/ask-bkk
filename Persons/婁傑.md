Late-Qīng physician of **Shānyīn** 山陰 (modern Shàoxīng 紹興 in Zhèjiāng), active around the turn of the 20th century. Author of *Wēnbìng zhǐnán* 溫病指南 ([[KR3eg007]]), preface dated Guāngxù *guǐmǎo* (1903), composed during a long residence in Hénán as a clinical handbook for physicians of the inland provinces still using *Shānghán*-style therapy on warm disease. His pupil Xiāo Jífǔ 蕭吉甫 assisted in the editorial work. Lóu is doctrinally an heir to the southern *wēnbìng* line of Wú Jūtōng ([[KR3eg010]]) — the *Wēnbìng zhǐnán* explicitly cites Wú's *sānjiāo* differentiation as its framework. No detailed biographical information preserved; CBDB entry 598805 lacks dates. The reading "Shānyīn" is sometimes mistakenly given as "Jiāngsū" in catalog meta — Shānyīn is in fact in Zhèjiāng.
