# 徐正卿 Xú Zhèngqīng

**Xú Zhèngqīng** 徐正卿, Southern-Sòng literatus and acquaintance of [[王執中]] Wáng Zhízhōng; sponsored the first printing of the *Zhēnjiǔ zīshēng jīng* 針灸資生經 ([[KR3ee020]]) and wrote its 1220 (Jiādìng 13) preface.
