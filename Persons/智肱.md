Zhìgōng 智肱 (active 11th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; the dharma-grandson of *Chángshuǐ Zǐjùn* 長水子濬 (the Northern-Sòng Tiāntái-Huáyán synthesist) and author of the [[KR6e0151|*Huáyán Qīngliáng guóshī lǐ zàn wén* 華嚴清涼國師禮讚文]] (X1471) — a verse-eulogy on [[澄觀]] Chéngguān (the *Qīngliáng guóshī* 清涼國師) for liturgical use. Birth and death dates not preserved.
