**Shǐ Diǎn** 史典 (*zì* Jìnchén 搢臣, *fl.* mid-Qīng, late Qiánlóng — Jiāqìng period), Qīng-period physician, original author of the *Yuàntǐ yīhuà* 願體醫話 ([[KR3eq064]]) bedside-clinical compendium. The work was subsequently edited by Yú Shìguì 俞世貴 (mid-Dào-guāng) and given final printing supervision by Wáng Shìxióng 王士雄 (1853). Sparse independent biographical record. Not in CBDB.
