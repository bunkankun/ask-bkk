**Shǐ Diǎn** 史典 (*zì* Jìn-chén 搢臣, *fl.* mid-Qīng, late Qián-lóng — Jiā-qìng period), Qīng-period physician, original author of the *Yuàn-tǐ yī-huà* 願體醫話 ([[KR3eq064]]) bedside-clinical compendium. The work was subsequently edited by Yú Shì-guì 俞世貴 (mid-Dào-guāng) and given final printing supervision by Wáng Shì-xióng 王士雄 (1853). Sparse independent biographical record. Not in CBDB.
