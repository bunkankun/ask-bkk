# Zhāng Jìngmǎn 張淨滿

Early-Táng layman named in the appended hagiographic materials of the Zōngbǎo recension of the *Tánjīng* as the agent of the Korean monk [[金大悲]] Jīn Dàbēi in the 722 attempt to steal Huìnéng's mummified head from Cáoxī.
