**Chéng Yún-lái** 程雲來 (also given as 程林 Chéng Lín, *fl.* *Shùn-zhì* 順治 – early *Kāng-xī* 康熙, Xīn-ān 新安 / Shè-xiàn 歙縣) — early-Qīng physician of the Xīn-ān medical tradition; a member of the [[程文囿]] Chéng-clan of Shè-xiàn (his exact relation to Chéng Wén-yòu uncertain but probably an ancestor of the same lineage). After the 1645 Yáng-zhōu massacre, Chéng was among the first physicians to re-establish medical practice in Yáng-zhōu, where the Xīn-ān medical tradition became an enduring presence. Active as a editor-physician based variously in Yáng-zhōu and Hangzhou (Qián-táng 錢塘). Compiler / editor of (i) the *Jīn-guì yào-lüè zhí-jiě* 金匱要略直解 (preface 1653); (ii) the *Shèng-jì zǒng-lù zuǎn-yào* 聖濟總錄纂要; and (iii) the re-edition of [[虞摶]] Yú Tuán's *Cāng-shēng sī-mìng* ([[KR3er078]], printed 1677 under [[李錦]] Lǐ Jǐn's patronage).
