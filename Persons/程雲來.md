**Chéng Yúnlái** 程雲來 (also given as 程林 Chéng Lín, *fl.* *Shùnzhì* 順治 – early *Kāngxī* 康熙, Xīnān 新安 / Shèxiàn 歙縣) — early-Qīng physician of the Xīnān medical tradition; a member of the [[程文囿]] Chéng-clan of Shèxiàn (his exact relation to Chéng Wényòu uncertain but probably an ancestor of the same lineage). After the 1645 Yángzhōu massacre, Chéng was among the first physicians to re-establish medical practice in Yángzhōu, where the Xīnān medical tradition became an enduring presence. Active as a editor-physician based variously in Yángzhōu and Hangzhou (Qiántáng 錢塘). Compiler / editor of (i) the *Jīnguì yàolüè zhíjiě* 金匱要略直解 (preface 1653); (ii) the *Shèngjì zǒnglù zuǎnyào* 聖濟總錄纂要; and (iii) the re-edition of [[虞摶]] Yú Tuán's *Cāngshēng sīmìng* ([[KR3er078]], printed 1677 under [[李錦]] Lǐ Jǐn's patronage).
