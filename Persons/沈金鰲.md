Mid-Qiánlóng-period Wúxī 無錫 polymath and physician; *zì* Zhīwēi 芷薇, *hào* Zùnshēng zǐ 尊生子 (also Jímén 汲門). Lifedates 1717–1776 are conventional. *Jǔrén* of the 1750 cohort; turned to medicine after exam-track frustrations. Author of the *Shěn shì zùnshēng shū* 沈氏尊生書 (1773) — a seventy-juan medical *cóngshū* covering the entire mainstream of Qing medical curriculum, including: [[KR3eb027]] *Mài xiàng tǒng lèi*, [[KR3eb028]] *Zhū mài zhǔ bìng shī*, *Zá bìng yuán liú xī zhú* 雜病源流犀燭 (the most widely used component), *Shānghán lùn gāng mù* 傷寒論綱目, *Fù kē yù chǐ* 婦科玉尺, *Yòu kē shì mí* 幼科釋謎, *Mài zhèng zhì fǎ qún xìng pǔ* 脈症治法群醒譜, *Yào pǐn biàn yì* 藥品辨義. No CBDB record. The *Zùnshēng shū* was widely reprinted in the late Qing and early Republic as a standard medical textbook.
