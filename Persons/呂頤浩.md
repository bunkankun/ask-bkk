Lǚ Yíhào 呂頤浩 (1071–1139), *zì* Yuánzhí 元直, ancestral seat Lèlíng 樂陵 (Shāndōng), settled in Qízhōu 齊州. *Jìnshì* graduate; under Huīzōng held office to *Héběi dūzhuǎnyùnshǐ* 河北都轉運使. After the southward crossing he served Gāozōng twice as *Tóng zhōngshū ménxià píngzhāngshì* 同中書門下平章事 (Co-Director-General). Eventually retired as *Shàofù, Lǐquánguān shǐ* 少傅、醴泉觀使. Posthumously honoured *Tàishī, Qínguógōng, shì Zhōngmù* 太師、秦國公、諡忠穆. Career in *Sòng shǐ* j. 362.

Lǚ was a key figure in the suppression of the 1129 Miáo Fǔ 苗傅 / Liú Zhèngyàn 劉正彥 *Jiànyán* mutiny: with Zhāng Jùn 張浚 he raised the loyalist forces and stabilised Gāozōng's throne. As prime minister he was *zhuānsì zìyòng* (autocratic and self-willed) — energetically suppressing the *Yuányòu* faction (Lǐ Gāng [[李綱]], Lǐ Guāng) and creating the *yuèzhuāngqián* 月樁錢 fiscal levy that became a long-term burden on the Southeast.

Catalog meta says "d. 1139"; CBDB (id 1310) confirms 1071–1139, followed here. The catalog is silent on birthdate; CBDB and *Sòng shǐ* both supply 1071.

His literary collection is *Zhōngmù jí* 忠穆集 [[KR4d0160]] in 8 *juǎn* (Sìkù *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* reconstruction; originally 15 *juǎn*).
