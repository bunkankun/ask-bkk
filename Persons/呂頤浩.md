Lǚ Yí-hào 呂頤浩 (1071–1139), *zì* Yuán-zhí 元直, ancestral seat Lè-líng 樂陵 (Shān-dōng), settled in Qí-zhōu 齊州. *Jìn-shì* graduate; under Huīzōng held office to *Hé-běi dū-zhuǎn-yùn-shǐ* 河北都轉運使. After the southward crossing he served Gāozōng twice as *Tóng zhōng-shū mén-xià píng-zhāng-shì* 同中書門下平章事 (Co-Director-General). Eventually retired as *Shào-fù, Lǐ-quán-guān shǐ* 少傅、醴泉觀使. Posthumously honoured *Tài-shī, Qín-guó-gōng, shì Zhōng-mù* 太師、秦國公、諡忠穆. Career in *Sòng shǐ* j. 362.

Lǚ was a key figure in the suppression of the 1129 Miáo Fǔ 苗傅 / Liú Zhèng-yàn 劉正彥 *Jiàn-yán* mutiny: with Zhāng Jùn 張浚 he raised the loyalist forces and stabilised Gāozōng's throne. As prime minister he was *zhuān-sì zì-yòng* (autocratic and self-willed) — energetically suppressing the *Yuán-yòu* faction (Lǐ Gāng [[李綱]], Lǐ Guāng) and creating the *yuè-zhuāng-qián* 月樁錢 fiscal levy that became a long-term burden on the Southeast.

Catalog meta says "d. 1139"; CBDB (id 1310) confirms 1071–1139, followed here. The catalog is silent on birthdate; CBDB and *Sòng shǐ* both supply 1071.

His literary collection is *Zhōng-mù jí* 忠穆集 [[KR4d0160]] in 8 *juǎn* (Sì-kù *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* reconstruction; originally 15 *juǎn*).
