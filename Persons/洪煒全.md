**Hóng Wěi-quán** 洪煒全 (fl. mid-18th c.), *zì* Jí'ān 緝庵 (some editions give 緝菴). Mid-Qīng physician of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). Co-compiler with Yán Jié [[嚴潔]] and Shī Wén [[施雯]] of the *Dé pèi běncǎo* 得配本草 ([[KR3ec058]], 1761), the principal Qīng pharmacopoeia of drug *pèi-wǔ* 配伍 (combinatorial pairing). Not in CBDB.
