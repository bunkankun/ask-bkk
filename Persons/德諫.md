Déjiàn 德諫 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* mid-13th c.), Yuán-dynasty Cáo-dòng Chán master; *zì* **Xuě-táng** 雪堂. Dharma-heir of the great Yuán Cáo-dòng master **Wàn-sōng Xíng-xiù** 萬松行秀 (1166–1246), whose *Cóng-róng lù* 從容錄 commentary on Hóng-zhì's *Sòng-gǔ* 頌古 collection is the principal Yuán Cáo-dòng commentarial work. Resident at **Yān-jīng Dà Wàn-shòu-sì** 燕京大萬壽寺 (the Mongol capital).

He composed the prose annotation (*zhù* 注) to the **《禪苑蒙求瑤林》** *Chán-yuàn méng-qiú yáo-lín* ([[KR6r0097]], X87 no. 1614) — the Chán *méng-qiú* by the Jīn Cáo-dòng master [[志明]] Zhìmíng. Déjiàn's annotation glosses the brief paired-couplet entries of the original with prose elaborations identifying the persons, events, and texts referenced, making the compendium accessible to novice readers. The annotation is dated by reference to Wàn-sōng's death-date (1246) as terminus a quo. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001694.
