# Liǎozhēn 了貞

Late-Qīng Chán monk; co-compiler (輯) of the *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* 正源略集 ([[KR6q0032]]) together with Jìyuán 際源. Active in the early nineteenth century; exact lifedates not transmitted.
