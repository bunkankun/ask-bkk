# Yímò 宜默

Gimoku 宜默, eighteenth-century Japanese Rinzai monk; wrote the preface (dated Genbun 4.3.8 = 8 March 1739) to the *Jūnzhōu Dòngshān Wùběn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0066]]), and also compiled the parallel *Fǔzhōu Cáoshān Běnjì chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0069]]) under his alternate name Genkei 玄契.
