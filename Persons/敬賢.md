# Jìngxián 敬賢

Jìngxián 敬賢 (660–723), Northern-School Chán master, one of [[神秀|Shénxiù]]'s four major dharma-heirs (alongside [[普寂|Pǔjì]], [[義福|Yìfú]], and [[玄賾|Xuánzé]]). Listed in [[淨覺|Jìngjué]]'s *Lèngjiā shī zī jì* [[KR6q0109]] as part of the eighth-generation patriarchal succession.
