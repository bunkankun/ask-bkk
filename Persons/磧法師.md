Qī fǎ-shī 磧法師 ("Master Qī"; lifedates unknown), Suí-period Chinese Buddhist monk of the Sān-lùn 三論 lineage, named in the colophon of [[KR6m0035]] *Sān-lùn yóu-yì yì* 三論遊意義 (T1855) as the author of that one-fascicle Sān-lùn-school doctrinal-systematic treatise. The bare-name attribution "Master Qī" 碩法師 is unusual; no secure biographical context is preserved. The name appears nowhere else in the standard Sino-Japanese Buddhist hagiographic record (*Gāosēng zhuàn*, *Xù gāosēng zhuàn*, *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn*, etc.) and the *Sān-lùn lineage-tradition records* (preserved in Eichō's 永超 *Tōiki dentōroku* 東域傳燈目錄 and successor catalogs) likewise do not supply biographical detail.

The work's doctrinal content (covering the Sān-lùn programme, the Madhyamaka *zōng* 宗, and the *wú-fāng wèn-nán* 無方問難 polemical method) is consistent with a mid-Suí (c. 580–610) Sān-lùn author working in the orbit of [[吉藏]] 吉藏 (549–623) or one of his immediate teachers. Some scholars (Hirai 1976) have proposed that 磧 may be a graphic variant or scribal abbreviation of a longer monastic name, but no secure identification has been made.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6m0035]] *Sān-lùn yóu-yì yì* 三論遊意義 (T1855).
