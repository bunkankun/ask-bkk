Zhāng Dàchāng 張大昌 (*zì* Wéijìng 為靖; 1926–1995) was a Héběi physician, grandson of [[張偓南]], who inherited the manuscript scroll of the *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]). After the original was destroyed in 1966 he reconstructed the text from memory; a separate transcript made by a disciple also survived. In 1974 he forwarded his reconstruction to the Zhōngguó Zhōngyī yánjiū yuàn 中國中醫研究院, where [[馬繼興]] and others vetted it.
