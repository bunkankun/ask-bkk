# Fànqīng 梵清

Japanese (Edo-period) monk; copied the single-juan supplement to the [[KR6q0072]] *Tiāntóng Rújìng chánshī xù yǔlù* that was kept secret at the Dānzhōu Déyún quarters and rediscovered in 1715 by [[卍山道白]] Manzan Dōhaku.
