# Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟

Mìyún Yuánwù (1566–1642), late-Míng Línjì-line Chán master, abbot of Tiāntóngshān 天童山, and one of the leading revivalist figures of late-imperial Línjì Chán. Teacher of Fèiyǐn [[通容]] Tōngróng and of several other prominent disciples.
