Dù Wénxiè 杜文燮 (*zì* Tiānjiéng 天倩), *fl.* Wànlì era, native of Xīnān 新安 / Húīzhōu 徽州 (modern Ānhuī). Húīzhōu commercial-medical physician embedded in the late-Míng publishing network. Author of the *Yào jiàn* 藥鑑 ([[KR3ec030]]), a 2-*juǎn* pharmacological textbook published in the late Wànlì era (c. 1598–1613). No further biographical record beyond Húīzhōu gazetteer mentions.
