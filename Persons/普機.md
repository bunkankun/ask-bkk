**Fuki** 普機 (also written 普幾) — Late-Heian or Kamakura-period Japanese Buddhist monk, identifiable only as the author of *Huā-yán-zōng yī-chéng kāi-xīn lùn* 華嚴宗一乘開心論 ([[KR6t0022]], T72n2326), a six-fascicle Kegon doctrinal treatise that uniquely applies the *Hetuvidyā* 因明 (Buddhist logic) inferential apparatus to the doctrine of the One Vehicle. The text presents "eighty-one inferences" with accompanying *圓印量圖* (circular-seal inference diagrams), now lost from the canon's reproduction.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001233. No biographical detail beyond the work's canonical attribution is preserved.
