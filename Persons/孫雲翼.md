Sūn Yúnyì 孫雲翼 (Míng), of *Dānyáng* 丹陽 (modern Jiāngsū). Annotated [[KR4d0288]] *Júshān sìliù* in *Wànlì dīngwèi* (1607); preface dated *Wànlì dīngwèi xià wǔ* (5th month). Sūn Yúnyì's grandfather *Qūshuǐ wēng* 曲水翁 was a book-collector who owned the manuscript of the *Júshān sìliù*. Sūn responded to the *gòng* examination in the capital in *jiǎshēn* (1604) and gradually annotated the manuscript during his subsequent posting in the south. The *Sìkù* editors found his annotation extensive but unhelpful (*wúzá*). CBDB id 600878.
