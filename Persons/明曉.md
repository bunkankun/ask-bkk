Míngxiǎo 明曉 was a Korean (Silla) monk active in Chángān at the end of the seventh century. He is recorded only as the petitioner who requested the lay translator Lǐ Wúchǎn ([[李無諂]]) of Lampāka (嵐波國) to render the *Amoghapāśa-dhāraṇī-sūtra* into Chinese at the **Fóshòujìsì** 佛授記寺 (Buddha-Prophecy Monastery) Translation Cloister in Shènglì 3 (700) — the resulting text is *Bùkōng juànsuǒ tuóluóní jīng* 不空羂索陀羅尼經 ([[KR6j0304]], T20n1096). No further biographical record survives.
