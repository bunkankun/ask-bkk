# Niàndōng Gāo xiānshēng 念東高先生

An early-Qīng lay Buddhist scholar and friend of [[龍其]] Lóngqí; encouraged Lóngqí's work on Pure Land practice that ultimately led to the publication of [[續法]] Xùfǎ's *Shìzhì yuántōng zhāng shūchāo* ([[KR6j0719]]).
