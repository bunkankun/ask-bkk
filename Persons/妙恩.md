# Miàoēn 妙恩

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì (Pòān *pài*) Chán master, *hào* Duànyá 斷崖 ("Cut-Cliff"); posthumously styled Guǎngmíng Tōnghuì Pǔjì chánshī 廣明通慧普濟禪師. Principal dharma-heir of [[可湘]] Juéàn Kěxiāng (1206–1290); abbot of the Kāiyuán chánsì 開元禪寺 at Quánzhōu in Fújiàn. Principal editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0317]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000471). Also listed as co-editor of the *Xīsǒu Shàotán chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]), suggesting a broader role in late-Sòng Línjì textual circulation.
