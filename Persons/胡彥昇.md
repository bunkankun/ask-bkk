Hú Yànshēng 胡彥昇, *zì* Zhúxuān 竹軒. Native of Déqīng 德清 (modern Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng *gēngxū* 雍正庚戌 = 1730. Held office as Magistrate of Dìngtáo 定陶縣 (modern Shāndōng). His chief surviving work is the *Yuèlǜ biǎowēi* 樂律表微 ([[KR1i0019]]) in 8 *juàn*, an early-Qián-lóng music treatise notable for its careful treatment of *xuángōng* without resort to the speculative *biànlǜ* (derived pitches) tradition.

CBDB id 66887 gives birth year 1696; death year unknown. The 1730 *jìnshì* date and conventional government career bracket place his floruit in the 1730s–1750s.
