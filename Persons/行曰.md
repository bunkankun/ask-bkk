Xíngrì 行曰 (DILA-standardized **行日** Xíngrì; the catalog meta of [[KR6q0505]] uses the visually-similar form 行曰), *zì* **Dúlǎng** 獨朗 ("Solitary Bright"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) — same teacher as [[覺]] Zìxián Xíngjué (cf. [[KR6q0469]]) and [[行聞]] Fógǔ Xíngwén (cf. [[KR6q0501]]). *Shìshòu* **66**, *sēnglà* **43**. Native of **Xuānchéng Jiànpíng** 宣城建平; lay surname **Xǔ** 許. Lifedates not exactly recovered.

**Formation.** Tonsured at **Nányuè** 南嶽 under **Héng shī** 恒師; full precepts in Fújiàn (*Mǐnzōng* 閩宗). Final transmission from **Línyě Tōngqí** at **Tōngxuánsì** 通玄寺.

**Two abbacies.** **Yáojiāng Jīqìng** 姚江積慶; **Tiāntái Tōngxuánsì** 天台通玄寺 (the death-seat). Stūpa at Tōngxuán's western precincts.

**Work.** [[KR6q0505]] 《天台通玄寺獨朗禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B368, compiled by *fǎsì* **Chāoxīn** [[超心]] + **Chāoxiāng** [[超香]] *děng biān*; juan 2 *tǎmíng*.

Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 79.
