Xíng-rì 行曰 (DILA-standardized **行日** Xíng-rì; the catalog meta of [[KR6q0505]] uses the visually-similar form 行曰), *zì* **Dú-lǎng** 獨朗 ("Solitary Bright"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) — same teacher as [[覺]] Zì-xián Xíng-jué (cf. [[KR6q0469]]) and [[行聞]] Fó-gǔ Xíng-wén (cf. [[KR6q0501]]). *Shì-shòu* **66**, *sēng-là* **43**. Native of **Xuān-chéng Jiàn-píng** 宣城建平; lay surname **Xǔ** 許. Lifedates not exactly recovered.

**Formation.** Tonsured at **Nán-yuè** 南嶽 under **Héng shī** 恒師; full precepts in Fú-jiàn (*Mǐn-zōng* 閩宗). Final transmission from **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** at **Tōng-xuán-sì** 通玄寺.

**Two abbacies.** **Yáo-jiāng Jī-qìng** 姚江積慶; **Tiān-tái Tōng-xuán-sì** 天台通玄寺 (the death-seat). Stūpa at Tōng-xuán's western precincts.

**Work.** [[KR6q0505]] 《天台通玄寺獨朗禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B368, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chāo-xīn** [[超心]] + **Chāo-xiāng** [[超香]] *děng biān*; juan 2 *tǎ-míng*.

Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 79.
