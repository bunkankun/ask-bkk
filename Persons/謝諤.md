Xiè È 謝諤 (1121–1194), *zì* Chāngguó 昌國, *hào* Gènzhāi 艮齋 and Guìshān lǎorén 桂山老人. Native of Xīn-gàn 新淦, Lú-líng 廬陵 prefecture (mod. Jiāng-xī). *Jìn-shì* of *Shào-xīng* 27 (1157). Successively held posts as *Yǒng-fēng* magistrate, *Hú-běi* and *Jiāng-xī* judicial intendant, censor (*Yù-shǐ zhōng-chéng* 御史中丞), and finally *Yǐ-zhèng-diàn xué-shì* 議政殿學士. A close associate of [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī and an active member of the late-twelfth-century *dào-xué* circle; his writings include the *Gènzhāi jí* 艮齋集 and the *Xìng-shàn-tángyǔ-lù* 性善堂語錄. He was one of the four Jiāng-xī literati friends of [[曾敏行]] Zēng Mǐnxíng and wrote the postface to [[曾敏行]]'s *Dúxǐng zázhì* [[KR3l0073]]. Posthumous title *Wén-yì* 文懿.
