Xiè È 謝諤 (1121–1194), *zì* Chāngguó 昌國, *hào* Gènzhāi 艮齋 and Guìshān lǎorén 桂山老人. Native of Xīngàn 新淦, Lúlíng 廬陵 prefecture (mod. Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Shàoxīng* 27 (1157). Successively held posts as *Yǒngfēng* magistrate, *Húběi* and *Jiāngxī* judicial intendant, censor (*Yùshǐ zhōngchéng* 御史中丞), and finally *Yǐzhèngdiàn xuéshì* 議政殿學士. A close associate of [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī and an active member of the late-twelfth-century *dàoxué* circle; his writings include the *Gènzhāi jí* 艮齋集 and the *Xìngshàntángyǔlù* 性善堂語錄. He was one of the four Jiāngxī literati friends of [[曾敏行]] Zēng Mǐnxíng and wrote the postface to [[曾敏行]]'s *Dúxǐng zázhì* [[KR3l0073]]. Posthumous title *Wényì* 文懿.
