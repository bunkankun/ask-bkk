Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk, sobriquet **Duàn-méi Xíng-mǐn** 斷眉行敏 ("Severed-Brow Xíng-mǐn"). Lifedates unrecorded. **Dharma-heir of Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661), styled *fǎ-lún shā-mén wén-fó qī-shí-yī shì sūn* 法輪沙門文佛七十一世孫 ("monk of the Dharma-wheel, seventy-first-generation descendant of Wén-fó"). Author of two *Vajracchedikā* commentaries — *Jīn-gāng jīng rú-shì jīng-yì* 金剛經如是經義 ([[KR6c0089]], X25 no. 501) and *Jīn-gāng jīng zhù-jiǎng* 金剛經註講 ([[KR6c0090]], X25 no. 502) — and parallel pair on the *Heart Sūtra*: *Bān-ruò xīn-jīng zhù-jiǎng* and *Bān-ruò xīn-jīng rú-shì jīng-yì*. The *rú-shì jīng-yì* genre (signaled by *rú-shì* 如是 in the title) places the work in dialogue with [[KR6c0073]] *Jīn-gāng jīng rú-shì jiě* of [[無是道人]].

Distinguish from: **Yú Xíng-mǐn** 俞行敏 (Bǎo-lián dào-rén 寶蓮道人), an early-Qīng layman of Táng-qī-zhèn, separate person — DILA disambiguates.
