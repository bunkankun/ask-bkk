Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk, sobriquet **Duànméi Xíngmǐn** 斷眉行敏 ("Severed-Brow Xíngmǐn"). Lifedates unrecorded. **Dharma-heir of Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661), styled *fǎlún shāmén wénfó qīshíyī shì sūn* 法輪沙門文佛七十一世孫 ("monk of the Dharma-wheel, seventy-first-generation descendant of Wénfó"). Author of two *Vajracchedikā* commentaries — *Jīngāng jīng rúshì jīngyì* 金剛經如是經義 ([[KR6c0089]], X25 no. 501) and *Jīngāng jīng zhùjiǎng* 金剛經註講 ([[KR6c0090]], X25 no. 502) — and parallel pair on the *Heart Sūtra*: *Bānruò xīnjīng zhùjiǎng* and *Bānruò xīnjīng rúshì jīngyì*. The *rúshì jīngyì* genre (signaled by *rúshì* 如是 in the title) places the work in dialogue with [[KR6c0073]] *Jīngāng jīng rúshì jiě* of [[無是道人]].

Distinguish from: **Yú Xíngmǐn** 俞行敏 (Bǎolián dàorén 寶蓮道人), an early-Qīng layman of Tángqīzhèn, separate person — DILA disambiguates.
