Chuán-huì 傳慧, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Pò-fēng Pǔ-zhòng** [[普重]] 破峰普重 at **Dōng-shān** 東山. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《東山破峰重禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0492]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
