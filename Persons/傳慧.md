Chuánhuì 傳慧, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Pòfēng Pǔzhòng** [[普重]] 破峰普重 at **Dōngshān** 東山. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《東山破峰重禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0492]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
