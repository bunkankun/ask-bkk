Early-Qīng Bannerman administrator and connoisseur of painting; *zì* Xīng-jù 星聚. Member of the Hàn-jūn 漢軍 *Xiāng-hóng-qí* 鑲紅旗 (Bordered-Red Banner of the Chinese Martial). His self-preface identifies his origin as Sān-Hán 三韓, a Liáodōng / Yǒngníng bannerman seat. Rose through the provincial judicial circuit to *Hú-guǎng àn-chá shǐ* 湖廣按察使 (Provincial Judicial Commissioner of Húguǎng); his administrative reputation as a *lì-cái* 吏才 is noted in Chén Dǐng's 陳鼎 *Liú-xī wài-zhuàn*. Author of [[KR3h0065]] *Huì-shì bèi-kǎo* 繪事備考 (1691), an 8-(in fact 10-)*juàn* chronological dictionary of Chinese painters from the Yellow Emperor through the early Míng, completed during his Húguǎng tenure. No precise lifedates available in CBDB or the standard sources; activity is fixed firmly in 1691.
