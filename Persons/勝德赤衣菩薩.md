Shèng-dé Chì-yī púsà 勝德赤衣菩薩 ("Bodhisattva Triumphant-Virtue Red-Robed"; Indic name uncertain — possibly \*Triratnadāsa or one of the *Vimuktisena*-class *Prajñāpāramitā*-commentators; lifedates unknown), Indic Buddhist *bodhisattva*-class commentator, named in the Chinese tradition as the author of [[KR6c0016|*Shèng fó-mǔ bōrě bōluómìduō jiǔ-sòng jīngyì lùn* 聖佛母般若波羅蜜多九頌精義論 (T1516)]] — a Sòng-period Chinese translation of a *Prajñāpāramitā* nine-verse commentary. The Indic identification of this *bodhisattva*-author is uncertain; modern scholarship has tentatively identified him with one of the Pāla-period *Prajñāpāramitā*-school masters, possibly *Triratnadāsa* (a known author of a *Mahāyāna-sūtrālaṃkāra* commentary).

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6c0016]] *Shèng fó-mǔ bōrě bōluómìduō jiǔ-sòng jīngyì lùn* (T1516).
