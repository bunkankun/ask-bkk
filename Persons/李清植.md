# Lǐ Qīngzhí 李清植

Mid-Qīng court scholar and editor, 1690–1744. Native place per CBDB. *Jìnshì* and Hànlín 翰林 court appointee; attached as editor to the editorial committee of the *Shísān jīng zhùshū* imperial reissue under Qiánlóng (1747, Qiánlóng 12). Co-author with [[朱良裘]] (Zhū Liángqiú) and [[陳浩]] (Chén Hào) of the *kǎozhèng* 考證 (textual-critical) apparatus appended to the *Zhōuyì zhèngyì* in the WYG / Sìkù recension (= part of [[KR1a0007]]).

The death-date 1744 indicates that the *kǎozhèng* apparatus must have been substantially complete by that year, even though the imperial reissue formally appeared only in 1747; the apparatus circulated in MS form for a few years before the print run.
