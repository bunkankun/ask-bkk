Chéng-qiān 承遷 (active 11th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; the annotator of [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's *Jīn shī zǐ zhāng* 金師子章 ("Treatise on the Golden Lion") in the *Huáyán jīng jīn shī zǐ zhāng zhù* 華嚴經金師子章註 ([[KR6e0098|T1881]]). His birth and death dates are not preserved. He is conventionally placed in the late-eleventh-century Sòng Buddhist intellectual scene, contemporary with [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's broader Huáyán-school revival project at Hángzhōu.
