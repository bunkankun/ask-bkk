# Kè-míng 克明

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[文煥]] and [[宗成]]) of *juan* 5 (*sòng-gǔ* + *zàn-yǔ*) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
