Guāntāo Dàqí 觀濤大奇 (1625/26–1678), *zì* **Guāntāo** 觀濤. Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáo-dòng Chán master of the **Shòu-chāng** 壽昌 sub-branch; dharma-heir of **Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (1593–1659). Native of **Wèi-chuān-lǐ** 渭川里 in **Yún-lín** 雲林, Fǔ-zhōu 撫州 (Jiāngxī), lay surname **Péng** 彭. At nineteen he tired of the world and sought monastic formation under **Xiàng-shān** 項山; then trained with **Tiě-suǒ lǎo-rén** of **Ráo-shān** 瀘陽饒山鐵索老人, and took the full precepts at the **Lóng-hú** 龍湖 ordination-altar in Shào-wǔ 邵武. He shut himself in closed-retreat at **Jué-xiān-fēng** 覺僊峰 (Xū 旴), investigating the *Liù-zǔ tán-jīng* 六祖壇經 phrase *"bù-sī shàn, bù-sī è"* 不思善不思惡 / *"nà-gè shì Míng shàng-zuò běn-lái miàn-mù"* 那個是明上座本來面目, and had a major opening. After a further pilgrimage to various masters he met **Dào-shèng** at Tiān-jiè and received formal dharma-transmission. His principal abbacies were at **Chóng-xiān Xiǎn-xiào-sì** 崇先顯孝寺 at **Gāo-tíng-shān** 皋亭山 (Hángzhōu) — whence he figures in catalogue signatures as *Chóng-xiān Dàqí* — and at **Xīn-chéng Shòu-chāng-sì** 新城壽昌寺 (the ancestral seat of the Shòu-chāng branch). He died on Kāngxī 17.7.10 (26 August 1678), aged 54, with 35 dharma-years. His dharma-heir was **Xīng-quán** 興泉 of Qí-yuán 淇園 at Hángzhōu Gāo-tíng-shān Xiǎn-xiào-sì. He is listed as co-editor (*děng biān* 等編) of his master's *Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Shèng chán-shī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0399]], J25 B174) with the senior heir **Dàchéng** [[大成]]. His own *yǔlù* is preserved as [[KR6q0499]] 《觀濤奇禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B362, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xīng-shū** [[興舒]] *děng biān*; juan 6 includes the *Shòu-zàng-míng* 壽藏銘 that gives his lifedates.
