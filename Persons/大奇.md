Guāntāo Dàqí 觀濤大奇 (1625/26–1678), *zì* **Guāntāo** 觀濤. Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng Chán master of the **Shòuchāng** 壽昌 sub-branch; dharma-heir of **Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (1593–1659). Native of **Wèichuānlǐ** 渭川里 in **Yúnlín** 雲林, Fǔzhōu 撫州 (Jiāngxī), lay surname **Péng** 彭. At nineteen he tired of the world and sought monastic formation under **Xiàngshān** 項山; then trained with **Tiěsuǒ lǎorén** of **Ráoshān** 瀘陽饒山鐵索老人, and took the full precepts at the **Lónghú** 龍湖 ordination-altar in Shàowǔ 邵武. He shut himself in closed-retreat at **Juéxiānfēng** 覺僊峰 (Xū 旴), investigating the *Liùzǔ tánjīng* 六祖壇經 phrase *"bùsī shàn, bùsī è"* 不思善不思惡 / *"nàgè shì Míng shàngzuò běnlái miànmù"* 那個是明上座本來面目, and had a major opening. After a further pilgrimage to various masters he met **Dàoshèng** at Tiānjiè and received formal dharma-transmission. His principal abbacies were at **Chóngxiān Xiǎnxiàosì** 崇先顯孝寺 at **Gāotíngshān** 皋亭山 (Hángzhōu) — whence he figures in catalogue signatures as *Chóngxiān Dàqí* — and at **Xīnchéng Shòuchāngsì** 新城壽昌寺 (the ancestral seat of the Shòuchāng branch). He died on Kāngxī 17.7.10 (26 August 1678), aged 54, with 35 dharma-years. His dharma-heir was **Xīngquán** 興泉 of Qíyuán 淇園 at Hángzhōu Gāotíngshān Xiǎnxiàosì. He is listed as co-editor (*děng biān* 等編) of his master's *Tiānjiè Juélàng Shèng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0399]], J25 B174) with the senior heir **Dàchéng** [[大成]]. His own *yǔlù* is preserved as [[KR6q0499]] 《觀濤奇禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B362, compiled by *fǎsì* **Xīngshū** [[興舒]] *děng biān*; juan 6 includes the *Shòuzàngmíng* 壽藏銘 that gives his lifedates.
