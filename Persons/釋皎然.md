# Shì Jiǎorán 釋皎然 (*ca.* 720–798)

The principal *shī-sēng* 詩僧 (monk-poet) of the *Dà-lì* and early *Zhēn-yuán* periods. *Zì* Qīng-zhòu 清晝; lay surname Xiè 謝, *fǎ-míng* (dharma-name) Jiǎo-rán 皎然 ("Bright"). Native of *Wú-xìng* 吳興 (modern *Hú-zhōu*, Zhèjiāng); abbot of the *Miào-xǐ sì* 妙喜寺 at *Zhù-shān* 杼山, in the *Tāi-hú* 太湖 region. He claimed direct descent from the *Liú-Sòng* poet Xiè Líng-yùn 謝靈運 (385–433), the founding figure of the Chinese *shān-shuǐ* tradition; his lay biographer Yú Dí 于頔 calls him a "tenth-generation descendant of [Xiè] Líng-yùn" and reads his poetry as the literary inheritor of the Xiè family lineage in the *Wú-yuè* 吳越 monastic context.

A senior figure of the late-Tang *Niú-tóu* 牛頭 *Chán* lineage, and the principal *Wú-yuè* literary patron of his generation. Closely associated with Yán Zhēn-qīng [[顏真卿]] during Yán's *Wú-xìng* governorship (768–771; Jiǎo-rán was a contributor to Yán's collaborative philological project the *Yùn-hǎi jìng-yuán* 韻海鏡源), with Wéi Yìng-wù [[韋應物]] during Wéi's *Sū-zhōu* governorship, and through them with the broader *Dà-lì* literary world.

His extant corpus comprises (a) the *Zhù-shān jí* [[KR4c0031]] in 10 *juǎn* — his secular *bié-jí*, deposited in the *Jí-xián diàn yù-shū-yuàn* 集賢殿御書院 by imperial order in *Zhēn-yuán* 8 (792); and (b) the *Shī shì* 詩式 (transmitted separately, in 5 *juǎn*) — his poetry-critical treatise, foundational for late-Tang *shī-huà* 詩話 theory. His theoretical innovation in the *Shī shì* — including the doctrine of *jìng* 境 (scene-realm), the formal analysis of *fēng-gé* 風骨, and the doctrine of poetic transcendence (*chū yán wài, wàng yán nèi* 出言外忘言內) — makes him one of the principal mediating figures between High-Tang practice and late-Tang theory.
