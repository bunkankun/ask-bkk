# Shì Jiǎorán 釋皎然 (*ca.* 720–798)

The principal *shīsēng* 詩僧 (monk-poet) of the *Dàlì* and early *Zhēnyuán* periods. *Zì* Qīngzhòu 清晝; lay surname Xiè 謝, *fǎmíng* (dharma-name) Jiǎorán 皎然 ("Bright"). Native of *Wúxìng* 吳興 (modern *Húzhōu*, Zhèjiāng); abbot of the *Miàoxǐ sì* 妙喜寺 at *Zhùshān* 杼山, in the *Tāihú* 太湖 region. He claimed direct descent from the *LiúSòng* poet Xiè Língyùn 謝靈運 (385–433), the founding figure of the Chinese *shānshuǐ* tradition; his lay biographer Yú Dí 于頔 calls him a "tenth-generation descendant of [Xiè] Língyùn" and reads his poetry as the literary inheritor of the Xiè family lineage in the *Wúyuè* 吳越 monastic context.

A senior figure of the late-Tang *Niútóu* 牛頭 *Chán* lineage, and the principal *Wúyuè* literary patron of his generation. Closely associated with Yán Zhēnqīng [[顏真卿]] during Yán's *Wúxìng* governorship (768–771; Jiǎorán was a contributor to Yán's collaborative philological project the *Yùnhǎi jìngyuán* 韻海鏡源), with Wéi Yìngwù [[韋應物]] during Wéi's *Sūzhōu* governorship, and through them with the broader *Dàlì* literary world.

His extant corpus comprises (a) the *Zhùshān jí* [[KR4c0031]] in 10 *juǎn* — his secular *biéjí*, deposited in the *Jíxián diàn yùshūyuàn* 集賢殿御書院 by imperial order in *Zhēnyuán* 8 (792); and (b) the *Shī shì* 詩式 (transmitted separately, in 5 *juǎn*) — his poetry-critical treatise, foundational for late-Tang *shīhuà* 詩話 theory. His theoretical innovation in the *Shī shì* — including the doctrine of *jìng* 境 (scene-realm), the formal analysis of *fēnggé* 風骨, and the doctrine of poetic transcendence (*chū yán wài, wàng yán nèi* 出言外忘言內) — makes him one of the principal mediating figures between High-Tang practice and late-Tang theory.
