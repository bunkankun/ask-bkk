**Hǎo Dàtōng** 郝大通 (1140–1212), *zì* Tài gǔ 太古, *hào* Guǎng níng zǐ 廣寧子, was **one of the Seven Perfected of Quán zhēn**, founder of the **Huá shān** 華山 branch. Particularly devoted to cosmological-diagrammatic speculation on the *Yì jīng*. Author of [[KR5e0063]] *Tài gǔ jí* 太古集.
