**Wáng Sǔnyǎn** 王損庵, late-Míng / early-Qīng physician whose dysentery writings are charged by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]] with having identified the disease but missed its root. Frequently paired in the dysentery literature with [[李士材]] Lǐ Shìcái as a *záběng*-school theorist whose framework Wú rejects. Further biographical detail not recovered.
