Pǔ-zhòng 普重, *hào* **Pò-fēng** 破峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huì-jué Zhào-yī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Listed as the first of Zhào-yī's six heirs in DILA. Lifedates not preserved.

**Abbacy.** **Dōng-shān** 東山 (whence the title-form 東山破峰重).

**Work.** [[KR6q0492]] 《東山破峰重禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B352, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chuán-huì** [[傳慧]] *děng biān*.
