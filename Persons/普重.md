Pǔzhòng 普重, *hào* **Pòfēng** 破峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huìjué Zhàoyī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) in the Pòshān-Sìchuān sub-line. Listed as the first of Zhàoyī's six heirs in DILA. Lifedates not preserved.

**Abbacy.** **Dōngshān** 東山 (whence the title-form 東山破峰重).

**Work.** [[KR6q0492]] 《東山破峰重禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B352, compiled by *fǎsì* **Chuánhuì** [[傳慧]] *děng biān*.
