# Guō Níngzhī 郭凝之

Late-Míng / early-Qīng lay Buddhist compiler. Principal editor (*huìbiān* 彙編) of the paired lay-Chán biographical works *Xiānjué zōngshèng* 先覺宗乘 ([[KR6q0050]]) and *Yōupóyí zhì* 優婆夷志 ([[KR6q0051]]), with editorial supervision by the monk Yuánxìn 圓信. Active in the mid-seventeenth century; exact lifedates and native place not transmitted.
