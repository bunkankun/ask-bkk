**Wáng Shūxiào** 王舒嘯 (mid-Qiánlóng era, 清), author of the principal preface to [[劉奎]] Liú Kuí's *Sōngfēng shuō yì* ([[KR3eg039]]), dated Qiánlóng 50 *yǐsì* (1785). No further biographical information located.
