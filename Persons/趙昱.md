Zhào Yù 趙昱 (1689–1747, *zì* Gōng-qiān 功千, *hào* Gǔ-lín 谷林), of Rén-hé 仁和 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Brother of [[趙信]] Zhào Xìn. Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōng-zhèng Háng-zhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È; contributed 100 poems to the collaborative *Nán-Sòng zá-shì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]]. Owner with his brother of the *Xiǎo-shān-táng* 小山堂 — a celebrated Háng-zhōu private library. Remained at *zhū-shēng* level. Produced the *Aì-rì-táng* poetry collection (*Aì-rì-táng yín-cǎo* 愛日堂吟草).
