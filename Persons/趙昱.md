Zhào Yù 趙昱 (1689–1747, *zì* Gōngqiān 功千, *hào* Gǔlín 谷林), of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Brother of [[趙信]] Zhào Xìn. Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng Hángzhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È; contributed 100 poems to the collaborative *Nán Sòng záshì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]]. Owner with his brother of the *Xiǎoshāntáng* 小山堂 — a celebrated Hángzhōu private library. Remained at *zhūshēng* level. Produced the *Aìrìtáng* poetry collection (*Aìrìtáng yíncǎo* 愛日堂吟草).
