# Shíyuàn 時願

Early-Qīng layman, grandson of the late-Míng compiler [[郭凝之]] Guō Níngzhī; per the Kāngxī 4 (1665) colophon to the *Dìwáng wèndào lù* selection of *Xiānjué zōngshèng* ([[KR6q0052]]) he donated the printing blocks of the fascicle to the Gǔméi'ān 古梅菴 at Jìngshān 徑山 for inclusion in the Jiāxīng canon.
