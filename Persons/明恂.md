Míngxún 明恂 (active c. 670s), Táng-dynasty Chinese monk of the Chán-lín sì 禪林寺 in Xī-jīng 西京 (Chāng'ān), who in Yí-fèng 2 (677) recorded the Chán-teaching Q-and-A session between himself and the visiting Kashmīrī monk Buddhapālita ([[覺愛]] Juéài / *Fóduópōlì* 佛陀波利), mediated through the Indian monk Huìzhì's 慧智 oral translation. The session is preserved as the *Xiū chán yào jué* 修禪要訣 ([[KR6q0116]], X63 n1222). No independent biographical material survives; no DILA authority entry.
