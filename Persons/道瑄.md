# Dàoxuān 道瑄

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán dharma-heir (*sìfǎ bǐqiū* 嗣法比丘) of [[無慍]] Shùzhōng Wúyùn (1309–1386); abbot of Chǔzhōufǔ Nánmíng chánsì 處州府南明禪寺. Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0349]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
