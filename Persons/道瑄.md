# Dào-xuān 道瑄

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán dharma-heir (*sì-fǎ bǐ-qiū* 嗣法比丘) of [[無慍]] Shù-zhōng Wú-yùn (1309–1386); abbot of Chǔ-zhōu-fǔ Nán-míng chán-sì 處州府南明禪寺. Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0349]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
