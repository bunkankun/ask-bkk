# Xuán-xìng 旋性

Late-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shì-zhě* 侍者 (attendant) to [[印|Fǎ-xǐ-yìn]] 法璽印 during Fǎ-xǐ-yìn's **Léng-yán-sì** 楞嚴寺 abbacy at Sū-zhōu (1676–1678). Head recorder (*jì-lù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0435]] 《楞嚴法璽印禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B221).

The 旋-generation character aligns with the same cohort as the dharma-brothers **Xuán-jī** 旋璣, **Xuán-zhé** 旋哲, **Xuán-xián** 旋賢 (co-editors of [[KR6q0434]]) — i.e., the 32nd-generation Línjì tier in the Xiào-yán / Hàn-shān / Guān-héng sub-line lineage-poem. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0435]] juan-head signature line "*shì-zhě Xuán-xìng jì-lù* 侍者旋性記錄".
