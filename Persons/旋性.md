# Xuánxìng 旋性

Late-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shìzhě* 侍者 (attendant) to [[印|Fǎxǐyìn]] 法璽印 during Fǎxǐyìn's **Léngyánsì** 楞嚴寺 abbacy at Sūzhōu (1676–1678). Head recorder (*jìlù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0435]] 《楞嚴法璽印禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B221).

The 旋-generation character aligns with the same cohort as the dharma-brothers **Xuánjī** 旋璣, **Xuánzhé** 旋哲, **Xuánxián** 旋賢 (co-editors of [[KR6q0434]]) — i.e., the 32nd-generation Línjì tier in the Xiàoyán / Hànshān / Guānhéng sub-line lineage-poem. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0435]] juan-head signature line "*shìzhě Xuánxìng jìlù* 侍者旋性記錄".
