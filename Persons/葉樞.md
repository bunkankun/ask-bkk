Yè Shū 葉樞, Northern Sòng Daoist of Kuàijī 會稽 (modern Shàoxīng 紹興, Zhèjiāng), active during the Zhènghé 政和 era. He is credited as the redactor of the *Lóngruìguàn Yǔxué Yángmíng dòngtiān tújīng* 龍瑞觀禹穴陽明洞天圖經 [[KR5b0309]] in 1114, building on an earlier (pre-1012) recension by the Hànlín xuéshì [[李宗諤]]. CBDB has a Sòng-period entry (id 548898) under this name without dates; biographical details are otherwise unrecorded. (CBDB also has a separate Míng-period 葉樞, id 565801, who is not to be confused with the Northern Sòng Daoist.)
