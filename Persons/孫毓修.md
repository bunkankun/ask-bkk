Late-Qīng / Republican-era bibliographer and editor, *zì* Xīng-rú 星如, of *Wú-xī* 無錫. Joined the *Shāng-wù yìn-shū-guǎn* 商務印書館 (Commercial Press) in 1907 and became one of the central editors of its *Sì-bù cóng-kān* 四部叢刊 project — the great Republican-era reproduction of canonical Sòng-Yuán-Míng witnesses. Did extensive 校補 work on Northern-Sòng *bié-jí* and Yuán *cóng-shū* (including the *Hé-nán Mù-gōng jí* [[KR4d0015]]). Also a pioneer of modern Chinese children's literature; his *Tóng-huà* 童話 series for the Commercial Press introduced Western fairy tales in literary Chinese to a generation of educated readers and influenced May-Fourth-era children's literature. Author of *Zhōng-guó diāo-bǎn yuán-liú kǎo* 中國雕版源流考 (1916), one of the foundational Chinese-language histories of woodblock printing. CBDB id 76212.
