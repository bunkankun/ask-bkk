Late-Qīng / Republican-era bibliographer and editor, *zì* Xīngrú 星如, of *Wúxī* 無錫. Joined the *Shāngwù yìnshūguǎn* 商務印書館 (Commercial Press) in 1907 and became one of the central editors of its *Sìbù cóngkān* 四部叢刊 project — the great Republican-era reproduction of canonical SòngYuánMíng witnesses. Did extensive 校補 work on Northern-Sòng *biéjí* and Yuán *cóngshū* (including the *Hénán Mùgōng jí* [[KR4d0015]]). Also a pioneer of modern Chinese children's literature; his *Tónghuà* 童話 series for the Commercial Press introduced Western fairy tales in literary Chinese to a generation of educated readers and influenced May-Fourth-era children's literature. Author of *Zhōngguó diāobǎn yuánliú kǎo* 中國雕版源流考 (1916), one of the foundational Chinese-language histories of woodblock printing. CBDB id 76212.
