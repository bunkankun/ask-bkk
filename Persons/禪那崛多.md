# Chánnà Juéduō 禪那崛多 / Jñānagupta (c. 523–600)

A major **Suí-dynasty translator** of Mahāyāna Buddhist scriptures into Chinese, conventionally known under the variant transliterations **Chánnà Juéduō** 禪那崛多, **Shénajuéduō** 闍那崛多, and **Shénajíduō** 闍那笈多 — all rendering the Sanskrit *Jñānagupta*. Born in **Gandhāra** 犍陀羅 c. 523 to a brahmin family that had converted to Buddhism; took monastic ordination in his youth and trained in the great northwestern Indian Buddhist scholastic centres of the period. He travelled to China c. 559 with a group of monastic colleagues, served at the Northern Zhōu and then Suí courts, and became one of the principal Buddhist translation figures of the Suí establishment under **Wéndì** 文帝 (r. 581–604).

His translation corpus is substantial — more than thirty works survive in the Chinese canon under his name, including the *Tiān-pǐn miào-fǎ lián-huá jīng* 添品妙法蓮華經 (T264, the supplemented version of the *Lotus Sūtra*) co-translated with Dharmagupta in 601, and a number of short Pure Land texts. The **《十二禮》** *Shí-èr lǐ* [[KR6p0080]] (X057, 1 *juǎn*) — a sequence of twelve devotional verses ascribed to **Lóngshù** 龍樹 / Nāgārjuna and described as Jñānagupta's "**别譯**" *bié-yì* (alternate translation) — is preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* and is one of the principal Pure Land devotional texts of his corpus.

He died c. 600 at age 78. DILA Authority A005010.
