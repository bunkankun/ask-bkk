Early- to mid-Qīng Buddhist scholar-monk. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded; the *floruit* given here (c. 1660–1740) reflects the late-Qīng commentarial period in which his attested work belongs. His sole extant work in the Kanripo corpus is the *[[KR6i0374|Yúlánpén jīng lüèshū 盂蘭盆經略疏]]* (X379), a brief Qīng-dynasty *Yúlánpén* commentary in the *lüèshū* 略疏 ("abbreviated commentary") genre. The verb 書 ("written") in the colophon (rather than the more usual 撰 / 述 / 疏) suggests a teaching-text rather than a fully-developed scholarly commentary.
