Fāng Shìjì 方式濟 (1676–1717), *zì* Wūyuán 屋源, *hào* Yāoyuán 訞園; native of Tóngchéng 桐城 in Ānhuī. Son of the *Tóngchéng* school scholar Fāng Dēngyì 方登嶧 (1659–1725) and nephew of Fāng Bāo 方苞. Early-Qīng official and *fēngtǔ* author. *Kāngxī jǐchǒu jìnshì* (1709); held office as *Nèi gé Zhōngshū shèrén*. In 1713 his father was exiled to Qíqíhāěr 齊齊哈爾 (Tsitsihar, in Hēilóngjiāng) in connection with the *Nánshānjí* 南山集 literary case (the political prosecution of Fāng Bāo's friend Dài Míngshì 戴名世). Fāng Shìjì accompanied his father in the exile, and during their joint exile composed the *Lóngshā jìlüè* 龍沙紀略 ([[KR2k0130]]) — the first systematic Chinese-language monograph on Manchuria based on first-hand observation. Died at Qíqíhāěr in 1717 without returning. CBDB id 56397.
