Fāng Shì-jì 方式濟 (1676–1717), *zì* Wū-yuán 屋源, *hào* Yāo-yuán 訞園; native of Tóng-chéng 桐城 in Ān-huī. Son of the *Tóng-chéng* school scholar Fāng Dēng-yì 方登嶧 (1659–1725) and nephew of Fāng Bāo 方苞. Early-Qīng official and *fēng-tǔ* author. *Kāng-xī jǐ-chǒu jìnshì* (1709); held office as *Nèi gé Zhōng-shū shè-rén*. In 1713 his father was exiled to Qí-qí-hā-ěr 齊齊哈爾 (Tsitsihar, in Hēi-lóng-jiāng) in connection with the *Nán-shān-jí* 南山集 literary case (the political prosecution of Fāng Bāo's friend Dài Míng-shì 戴名世). Fāng Shì-jì accompanied his father in the exile, and during their joint exile composed the *Lóng-shā jì-lüè* 龍沙紀略 ([[KR2k0130]]) — the first systematic Chinese-language monograph on Manchuria based on first-hand observation. Died at Qí-qí-hā-ěr in 1717 without returning. CBDB id 56397.
