**Hé Shèngshān** 何聲山 (Republican period, 民國), son of [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén; in 1934 published his father's posthumous *Chóngdìng tōngsú Shānghán lùn* 重訂通俗傷寒論 ([[KR3ef036]]) through the *Shàoxīng yīyào xué bào* 紹興醫藥學報 press.
