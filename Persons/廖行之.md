Liào Xíngzhī 廖行之 (1137–1189, *zì* Tiānmín 天民, *hào* Shěngzhāi 省齋), of *Héngzhōu* 衡州 (modern Héngyáng, Húnán); ancestrally of *Yánpíng* 延平 in *Mǐn* (Fújiàn), the family migrated south during the Five Dynasties. *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī jiǎchén* (11th year, 1184). Held office as *Yuèzhōu Bālíng-wèi*; resigned to support his elderly parents; was reassigned *Níngxiāng zhǔbù* but died before taking up the post. Played a documented role in the *Shígǔ shūyuàn* 石鼓書院 (Stone-Drum Academy) endowment at *Héngzhōu*. CBDB id 27711. Author of [[KR4d0283]] *Shěngzhāi jí*. Note: this is one of two Sòng figures with the *hào* *Shěngzhāi*; the other is Zhōu Bìdà 周必大 (1126–1204), and the *Sìkù* editors note historical confusion between their *biǎo-qǐ*.
