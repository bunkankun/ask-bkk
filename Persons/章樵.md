# Zhāng Qiáo 章樵 (fl. 1232)

*Zì* Shēng-dào 升道, of Wǔ-lín 武林 (Hángzhōu). A Southern-Sòng *cháo-fèng láng* 朝奉郎 who held the post of *Zhī Píng-jiāng-fǔ Wú-xiàn-shì* (Magistrate of Wú-county under Píng-jiāng prefecture, i.e. Sūzhōu). His principal scholarly contribution is the annotated edition of the *Gǔwén yuàn* 古文苑 [[KR4h0021]], expanded from the earlier Hán Yuán-jí 韓元吉 nine-*juǎn* arrangement of *Chún-xī* 6 (1179) into a 21-*juǎn* annotated form completed in *Shào-dìng rén-chén* (1232). Zhāng's preface to that edition is the principal biographical document; nothing else of his career is recoverable. No CBDB entry.
