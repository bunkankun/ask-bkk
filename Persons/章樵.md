# Zhāng Qiáo 章樵 (fl. 1232)

*Zì* Shēngdào 升道, of Wǔlín 武林 (Hángzhōu). A Southern-Sòng *cháofèng láng* 朝奉郎 who held the post of *Zhī Píngjiāngfǔ Wúxiànshì* (Magistrate of Wú-county under Píngjiāng prefecture, i.e. Sūzhōu). His principal scholarly contribution is the annotated edition of the *Gǔwén yuàn* 古文苑 [[KR4h0021]], expanded from the earlier Hán Yuánjí 韓元吉 nine-*juǎn* arrangement of *Chúnxī* 6 (1179) into a 21-*juǎn* annotated form completed in *Shàodìng rénchén* (1232). Zhāng's preface to that edition is the principal biographical document; nothing else of his career is recoverable. No CBDB entry.
