**Luó Liángbì** 羅良弼 (fl. early 12th century), of Jiāngxī 江西. Disciple and editor of Liú Yǎn 劉弇 [[劉弇]]; compiled the master's *Lóngyún jí* 龍雲集 [[KR4d0107]] in 32 *juǎn* after Liú's death in 1102, providing the principal channel of transmission. Held minor office; precise dates and biography not securely established beyond his role as student-editor.
