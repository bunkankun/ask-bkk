**Yángdiǎn** 陽典 (*fl.* late Qīng), Qīng popular-medical prefacer-editor signing the preface to the *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē gēkuò* 傅青主女科歌括 ([[KR3ei021]]). The preface narrates the transmission history of the [[傅山]] Fù Shān *Nǚkē* corpus from manuscript circulation in Shānxī through its first *Jiāqìng* imprint at Píngyángjùn 平陽郡 to the present versified redaction. No further biographical identification located.
