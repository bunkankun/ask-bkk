# 房融

Fáng Róng (Táng minister and Buddhist devotee; d. ca. 705). Served at the imperial court under Wǔ Zétiān and was later exiled to Guǎngzhōu, where he is traditionally said to have participated in **Pāramiti** 般剌蜜帝's 705 translation of the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]], T19n0945) as the scribe-recorder (筆受). His literary polish has frequently been cited in arguments that the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* is a Chinese apocryphal composition.
