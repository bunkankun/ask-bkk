**Zhōu Wéncǎi** 周文採 (*fl.* late 15th c., precise lifedates unrecorded). Mid-Ming Tàiyī yuàn physician seconded to the Xīngxiàn Prince Zhū Yòuyuán 朱祐杬 (father of the future Jiājìng emperor) at the princely court at Anlu 安陸 (modern Húběi). Compiler of the *Yīfāng xuǎnyào* 醫方選要 ([[KR3ed046]]) in 10 *juǎn*, published 1495 (*hóngzhì* 8) at the Xīngxiàn princely court. The work's postface is by the Xīngxiàn Prince himself under the pseudonym *Chúnyī dàorén* 純一道人. Not in CBDB; no biographical record outside this princely-court attribution.
