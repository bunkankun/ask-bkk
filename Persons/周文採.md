**Zhōu Wéncǎi** 周文採 (*fl.* late 15th c., precise lifedates unrecorded). Mid-Ming Tài-yī yuàn physician seconded to the Xīng-xiàn Prince Zhū Yòu-yuán 朱祐杬 (father of the future Jiā-jìng emperor) at the princely court at Anlu 安陸 (modern Húběi). Compiler of the *Yīfāng xuǎnyào* 醫方選要 ([[KR3ed046]]) in 10 *juǎn*, published 1495 (*hóng-zhì* 8) at the Xīng-xiàn princely court. The work's postface is by the Xīng-xiàn Prince himself under the pseudonym *Chún-yī dàorén* 純一道人. Not in CBDB; no biographical record outside this princely-court attribution.
