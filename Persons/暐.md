Wěi 暐 (catalog form; full reference **Dǒu-nán Wěi** 斗南暐), *zì* **Dǒu-nán** 斗南 ("Big Dipper South"); often referred to as **Dōng-ōu Dǒu-nán** 東甌斗南 (Dōng-ōu = Wēn-zhōu region). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bié-ān Xìng-tǒng** 別庵性統 (1661–1717, of Pǔ-tuó-shān) — thus a co-disciple of **Cuì-yá Bì** [[必]] (cf. [[KR6q0595]]). Lifedates unknown.

**Abbacies (two).** **Wēn-zhōu Jiē-dài chán-sì** 溫州接待禪寺; **Wú-xī Cí-yún chán-sì** 無錫慈雲禪寺.

**Disciples / recorders.** **Pǔ-rùn** [[普潤]] (lead recorder), **Fǎ-quán** 法權 (co-recorder).

**Work.** [[KR6q0596]] 《斗南暐禪師語錄》 3 juan, J40 B488, recorded by **Pǔ-rùn** [[普潤]] *děng lù*.

**Sources.** 《斗南暐禪師語錄》 vol. 1–3; 《普陀洛迦新志》.
