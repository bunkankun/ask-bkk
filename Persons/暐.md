Wěi 暐 (catalog form; full reference **Dǒunán Wěi** 斗南暐), *zì* **Dǒunán** 斗南 ("Big Dipper South"); often referred to as **Dōngōu Dǒunán** 東甌斗南 (Dōngōu = Wēnzhōu region). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Biéān Xìngtǒng** 別庵性統 (1661–1717, of Pǔtuóshān) — thus a co-disciple of **Cuìyá Bì** [[必]] (cf. [[KR6q0595]]). Lifedates unknown.

**Abbacies (two).** **Wēnzhōu Jiēdài chánsì** 溫州接待禪寺; **Wúxī Cíyún chánsì** 無錫慈雲禪寺.

**Disciples / recorders.** **Pǔrùn** [[普潤]] (lead recorder), **Fǎquán** 法權 (co-recorder).

**Work.** [[KR6q0596]] 《斗南暐禪師語錄》 3 juan, J40 B488, recorded by **Pǔrùn** [[普潤]] *děng lù*.

**Sources.** 《斗南暐禪師語錄》 vol. 1–3; 《普陀洛迦新志》.
