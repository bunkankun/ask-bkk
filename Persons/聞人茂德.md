# Wénrén Màodé 聞人茂德

Early-Southern-Sòng Confucian of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (modern Zhèjiāng), counted among the circle of [[李侗]] Lǐ Tóng's students. Together with [[喻樗]] Yù Chū, the principal teacher of [[程迥]] Chéng Jiǒng (*jìnshì* 1163), the *Yì*-divinatory scholar through whom [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī inherited a strand of the formative-Sòng *Yì*-tradition (see *Zhōuyì gǔ zhàn fǎ* 周易古占法, [[KR1a0030]]). His own writings have not survived; he is documented chiefly in the *Sòngshǐ Rúlín zhuàn* notice of Chéng Jiǒng and in the Sìkù *tíyào* on *Zhōuyì gǔ zhàn fǎ*. Lifedates not on record.
