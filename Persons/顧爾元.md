Late-Qián-lóng / mid-Jiā-qìng-era Qīng physician of Liángxī 梁溪 (= Wúxī 無錫, in the Sūzhōu region), the seventh generation (*qīshì jiāyī* 七世家醫) of a Wúxī hereditary medical lineage active from the late Míng. His sole documented work is [[KR3er097]] *Yī zhōng yī dé* 醫中一得 (*Jiāqìng* 8 / 1803), composed at age 74. The work refines [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì's *wēnbìng* doctrine of *shǔyì* 暑邪 (summerheat as a *wúxíng zhī qì* 無形之氣 upper-burner illness) with a specific *shēngmá*-based formula (*Gěshēng tāng* 葛升湯) for *dānnüè* 癉瘧. Birth-year derived from his stated age in 1803; death-year undocumented. Not in CBDB.
