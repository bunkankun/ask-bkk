Late-Qián-lóng / mid-Jiā-qìng-era Qīng physician of Liáng-xī 梁溪 (= Wú-xī 無錫, in the Sū-zhōu region), the seventh generation (*qī-shì jiā-yī* 七世家醫) of a Wú-xī hereditary medical lineage active from the late Míng. His sole documented work is [[KR3er097]] *Yī zhōng yī dé* 醫中一得 (*Jiā-qìng* 8 / 1803), composed at age 74. The work refines [[葉桂]] Yè Tiān-shì's *wēn-bìng* doctrine of *shǔ-yì* 暑邪 (summerheat as a *wú-xíng zhī qì* 無形之氣 upper-burner illness) with a specific *shēng-má*-based formula (*Gě-shēng tāng* 葛升湯) for *dān-nüè* 癉瘧. Birth-year derived from his stated age in 1803; death-year undocumented. Not in CBDB.
