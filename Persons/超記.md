Chāojì 超記 (lifedates unrecorded), Qīng-dynasty Chán monk, disciple of [[行盛|Běnchōng Xíngshèng]] 本充行盛 (d. 1671/2) at the Huìyún sì 慧雲寺. Named recorder (*lù* 錄) of Xíngshèng's *Chánlín bǎoxùn niān sòng* 禪林寶訓拈頌 ([[KR6q0155]], X64 n1264).
