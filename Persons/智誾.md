# Zhìyín 智誾 (Xuěguān Zhìyín 雪關智誾)

Late-Wàn-lì / Chóng-zhēn-era **Cáodòng** 曹洞 Chán master, **31st-generation Cáodòng** in the **Shòuchāng Wúmíng Huìjīng** 壽昌無明慧經 sub-line. Principal dharma-heir of [[元來|Bóshān Wúyì Yuánlái]] 博山無異元來 (1575–1630) and his immediate successor at the Bóshān seat from 1632. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Zhìyín** 智誾, changed to **Dàoyín** 道誾 in 1632 on pagoda-sweeping at Wúmíng Huìjīng's Shòuchāngsì tomb, to formally succeed the Shòuchāng lineage. *Hào* **Xuěguān** 雪關 ("Snow-Pass", from a *Xuěguān gē* 雪關歌 composed during his six-year *bìguān* 閉關). Lay surname **Fù** 傅 (in the text variant-written 傳), of **Shàngráo** 上饒 (Jiāngxī). Lifedates **萬曆 13 乙酉 9.1 – 崇禎 10 丁丑 10.11** = **~20 September 1585 – 26 November 1637**, age **53 *suì***. DILA A011738.

**Early life.** Father **Fù Chóngjiǎn** 傅崇儉 died when Zhìyín was 8; widowed mother **Zhèngshì** 鄭氏 sent him to **Jǐngdésì** 景德寺 as a *tóngzǐ* under an elder named Fùgōng. Vision of Guānyīn touching his head strengthened him. Questioned the *Platform Sutra*'s *huǒ shāo hǎidǐ* 火燒海底 line; went to **Bóshān Yuánlái** for Chán training. First awakening on the *chuánzǐ cángshēn* 船子藏身 *gōngàn* on seeing a loose millstone grinding-nose, with verse "*zhíxià xiāngféng chù, yóulái jué fùcáng* 直下相逢處由來絕覆藏". Six-year *bìguān* sitting on a fire-box with a *fǎncān* 返參 mirror at Yúnqīsì. Composed the *Xuěguān gē* 雪關歌 from which the *hào* derives. Appointed *shǒuzuò* 首座 by Yuánlái.

**Six abbacies.** (1) **Yíngshān** 瀛山 (1629, famously took up a ruined seat and composed the *Pòyuàn gē* 破院歌, receiving a response-poem from the grand-secretary **Zhèng Fāngshuǐ** 鄭方水). (2) **Xīnzhōu Bóshān Néngrénsì** 信州博山能仁寺 (succeeded Yuánlái after the 1630 death; formally entered the seat Chóngzhēn 5.4.1 = **19 May 1632**). (3) **Fúzhōu Gǔshān Yǒngquánsì** 福州鼓山湧泉禪寺 (1632, petitioned by the Fújiàn bibliographer **Cáo Xuéquán** 曹學佺; pagoda-sweeping of Wúmíng Huìjīng at Shòuchāng and name-change to Dàoyín). Zhìyín's 1632 Gǔshān abbacy is the **founding event of the Gǔshān Chán school** whose subsequent (more famous) tenure by Yǒngjué Yuánxián secured the sub-lineage's institutional base. (4) **Hang-zhōu Hǔpáosì** 虎跑寺 (*bǐngzǐ* 1636 spring, petitioned by *zhōngchéng* **Yú Dàchéng** 余大成 and Hang-zhōu *tuīguān* **Huáng Duānbó** 黃端伯). (5) **Hang-zhōu Miàoxíngsì** 妙行寺 (re-founded 1636, Great Hall completed 1637). (6) Final return to **Yíngshān** in 1637 autumn due to illness. Died at Yíngshān 1637.10.11 (= 26 November 1637); body returned to Bóshān for burial at the western ridge of Lotus-Flower Peak.

**Signature genre.** The **《禪鏡語》** 禪鏡語 ("Chán Mirror-Words", at juan 7 of the *yǔlù*) is a pedagogical parallel to Yuánlái's *Chán jǐngyǔ* 禪警語 ([[KR6q0417]] juan 4) — the "mirror" (*jìng* 鏡) being Zhìyín's signature *fǎncān* training-device during the 六年雪 *bìguān*. Also the *Chánjìng fāyǐn* 禪淨發隱 ("Disclosing the Hidden in Chán and Pure Land") at juan 4 is an unusually explicit Chán–Pure Land syncretic essay for a Shòu-chāng-line Cáodòng master.

Work: [[KR6q0418]] 《雪關禪師語錄》 (13 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J27 B198, compiled by attendant [[成巒|Chéngluán Chuánshàn]] and dharma-grandson [[開詗|Kāixuàn]]). Biographical apparatus at juan 13: *zhuàn* by **Cáo Xuéquán** 曹學佺 (1574–1646, the Fújiàn bibliographer) + *tǎmíng* by **Huáng Duānbó** 黃端伯 (c. 1585–1646, the Jiāngyīn loyalist-martyr — whose preserved signed prose pieces are few).
