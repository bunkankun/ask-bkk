# Zhì-yín 智誾 (Xuě-guān Zhì-yín 雪關智誾)

Late-Wàn-lì / Chóng-zhēn-era **Cáo-dòng** 曹洞 Chán master, **31st-generation Cáo-dòng** in the **Shòu-chāng Wú-míng Huì-jīng** 壽昌無明慧經 sub-line. Principal dharma-heir of [[元來|Bó-shān Wú-yì Yuán-lái]] 博山無異元來 (1575–1630) and his immediate successor at the Bó-shān seat from 1632. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Zhì-yín** 智誾, changed to **Dào-yín** 道誾 in 1632 on pagoda-sweeping at Wú-míng Huì-jīng's Shòu-chāng-sì tomb, to formally succeed the Shòu-chāng lineage. *Hào* **Xuě-guān** 雪關 ("Snow-Pass", from a *Xuě-guān gē* 雪關歌 composed during his six-year *bì-guān* 閉關). Lay surname **Fù** 傅 (in the text variant-written 傳), of **Shàng-ráo** 上饒 (Jiāng-xī). Lifedates **萬曆 13 乙酉 9.1 – 崇禎 10 丁丑 10.11** = **~20 September 1585 – 26 November 1637**, age **53 *suì***. DILA A011738.

**Early life.** Father **Fù Chóng-jiǎn** 傅崇儉 died when Zhì-yín was 8; widowed mother **Zhèng-shì** 鄭氏 sent him to **Jǐng-dé-sì** 景德寺 as a *tóng-zǐ* under an elder named Fù-gōng. Vision of Guān-yīn touching his head strengthened him. Questioned the *Platform Sutra*'s *huǒ shāo hǎi-dǐ* 火燒海底 line; went to **Bó-shān Yuán-lái** for Chán training. First awakening on the *chuán-zǐ cáng-shēn* 船子藏身 *gōng-àn* on seeing a loose millstone grinding-nose, with verse "*zhí-xià xiāng-féng chù, yóu-lái jué fù-cáng* 直下相逢處由來絕覆藏". Six-year *bì-guān* sitting on a fire-box with a *fǎn-cān* 返參 mirror at Yún-qī-sì. Composed the *Xuě-guān gē* 雪關歌 from which the *hào* derives. Appointed *shǒu-zuò* 首座 by Yuán-lái.

**Six abbacies.** (1) **Yíng-shān** 瀛山 (1629, famously took up a ruined seat and composed the *Pò-yuàn gē* 破院歌, receiving a response-poem from the grand-secretary **Zhèng Fāng-shuǐ** 鄭方水). (2) **Xīn-zhōu Bó-shān Néng-rén-sì** 信州博山能仁寺 (succeeded Yuán-lái after the 1630 death; formally entered the seat Chóng-zhēn 5.4.1 = **19 May 1632**). (3) **Fú-zhōu Gǔ-shān Yǒng-quán-sì** 福州鼓山湧泉禪寺 (1632, petitioned by the Fú-jiàn bibliographer **Cáo Xué-quán** 曹學佺; pagoda-sweeping of Wú-míng Huì-jīng at Shòu-chāng and name-change to Dào-yín). Zhì-yín's 1632 Gǔ-shān abbacy is the **founding event of the Gǔ-shān Chán school** whose subsequent (more famous) tenure by Yǒng-jué Yuán-xián secured the sub-lineage's institutional base. (4) **Hang-zhōu Hǔ-páo-sì** 虎跑寺 (*bǐng-zǐ* 1636 spring, petitioned by *zhōng-chéng* **Yú Dà-chéng** 余大成 and Hang-zhōu *tuī-guān* **Huáng Duān-bó** 黃端伯). (5) **Hang-zhōu Miào-xíng-sì** 妙行寺 (re-founded 1636, Great Hall completed 1637). (6) Final return to **Yíng-shān** in 1637 autumn due to illness. Died at Yíng-shān 1637.10.11 (= 26 November 1637); body returned to Bó-shān for burial at the western ridge of Lotus-Flower Peak.

**Signature genre.** The **《禪鏡語》** 禪鏡語 ("Chán Mirror-Words", at juan 7 of the *yǔlù*) is a pedagogical parallel to Yuán-lái's *Chán jǐng-yǔ* 禪警語 ([[KR6q0417]] juan 4) — the "mirror" (*jìng* 鏡) being Zhì-yín's signature *fǎn-cān* training-device during the 六年雪 *bì-guān*. Also the *Chán-jìng fā-yǐn* 禪淨發隱 ("Disclosing the Hidden in Chán and Pure Land") at juan 4 is an unusually explicit Chán–Pure Land syncretic essay for a Shòu-chāng-line Cáo-dòng master.

Work: [[KR6q0418]] 《雪關禪師語錄》 (13 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J27 B198, compiled by attendant [[成巒|Chéng-luán Chuán-shàn]] and dharma-grandson [[開詗|Kāi-xuàn]]). Biographical apparatus at juan 13: *zhuàn* by **Cáo Xué-quán** 曹學佺 (1574–1646, the Fú-jiàn bibliographer) + *tǎ-míng* by **Huáng Duān-bó** 黃端伯 (c. 1585–1646, the Jiāng-yīn loyalist-martyr — whose preserved signed prose pieces are few).
