**Hán Mào** 韓懋 (*zì* Tiānjué 天爵, *hào* Fēixiázǐ 飛霞子, c. 1441 – after 1522, mid-Míng), physician of Sìchuān Lúzhōu 瀘州. Hán's clinical training was hereditary; he succeeded his father, whose collection of clinical recipes (effective formulae) Hán framed in his own preface as the empirical basis for his medical study. His principal work is the *Hánshì yītōng* 韓氏醫通 ([[KR3er005]], completed *Jiājìng* 1 = 1522), a short two-*juǎn* aphoristic medical compendium in nine chapters and ninety-five entries that became one of the most-cited mid-Míng didactic handbooks. Hán was an early articulator of the **diagnostic-by-questioning** programme (*wènzhěn* 問診) that was later canonised by [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn's *shíwèn* 十問 doctrine. CBDB 320758 places him at *c_index_year* 1525, consistent with the dated preface. The *Fēixiázǐ* literary cover-name became associated with the work, which was sometimes catalogued under the alternative title *Fēixiázǐ yītōng*.
