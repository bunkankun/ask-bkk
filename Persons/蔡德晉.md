Cài Déjìn 蔡德晉 (Qīng, *fl.* 1726–1737, CBDB id 125397 has *fl. earliest* 1737, *fl. latest* 1766; the catalog meta gives the work-completion date 1726). *Zì* Jìngzhāi 敬齋. Native of Wúxī 無錫 (modern Wúxī, Jiāngsū). Held minor office as a *Gōngbù sīwù* 工部司務 (Junior Officer of the Board of Works), recommended for the *Sānlǐ* commission ([[KR1d0018]] [[KR1d0037]] [[KR1d0068]]) under early Qiánlóng.

Author of [[KR1d0044]] *Lǐjīng běnyì* 禮經本義 in 17 juan — a commentary on the *Yílǐ* ([[KR1d0025]]) drawing extensively on Sòng-Yuán-Míng commentary tradition cross-referenced with the *zhùshū*. The seventeenth juan appends Wú Chéng's [[KR1d0034]] eight-chapter *Yílǐ yìjīng* reconstruction. The Sìkù *tíyào* judges the work as having both lapses (notably his uncritical adoption of Wàn Sīdà's [[KR1d0039]] *kuētóu* reading) and substantial contributions (notably his careful distinction between the hair-securing *jī* and the cap-securing *jī*, against Áo Jìgōng's reading).
