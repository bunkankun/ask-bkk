**Dōng-xuān jūshì** 東軒居士 ("Master of the Eastern Pavilion"), pseudonymous Southern-Sòng (pre-Xiào-zōng or contemporary, *fl.* before / during 1170, 南宋) author of the ***Wèi-jì bǎo-shū*** 衛濟寶書 ([[KR3e0035]]) — a specialist surgical-medical treatise on furuncular abscesses (癰疽) in 2 juan, with 22 *piān* of disease-discussion and illustrations. The pseudonym is the only authorial signature; the SKQS editors note that no further identification is possible. The work was preserved in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* (where the SKQS editors recovered it) and through the household manuscript tradition of the Wāng 汪 family of Méi-gēn 梅根, from which a Dǒng Lián 董璉 son-in-law obtained it; Dǒng's preface (preserved in the *Yǒnglè* recovery) is dated to Qián-dào geng-yín (1170) under Sòng Xiào-zōng. So the *Dōng-xuān jūshì* must antedate 1170 and is a Southern-Sòng (pre-1170) figure.
