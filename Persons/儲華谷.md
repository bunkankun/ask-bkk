**Chǔ Huágǔ** 儲華谷 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemical commentator, author of [[KR5d0027]] *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì zhù* 周易參同契註 — the fifth of the Dào zàng's recensional commentaries on the *Cān tóng qì*.
