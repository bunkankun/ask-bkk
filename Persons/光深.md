Guāngshēn 光深, *zì* **Xīngrán** 惺然 ("Awakened Thus"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Bànshuǐ Fāyuán** [[發元]] (?–1691). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù* / *děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0539]] 《三峰半水元禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B420, prefaced 1691.

The *yǔlù*'s preface relates that Guāngshēn first met 華嚴 [[德玉]] in winter **辛未** = **1691** during the Huáyán *jiézhì* retreat, where his rough-edged Chán confidence earned him "a vigorous beating" from the elder; later, repenting, he presented his teacher's draft *yǔlù* to Déyù for vetting and a preface.
