Guāng-shēn 光深, *zì* **Xīng-rán** 惺然 ("Awakened Thus"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bàn-shuǐ Fā-yuán** [[發元]] (?–1691). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù* / *děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0539]] 《三峰半水元禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B420, prefaced 1691.

The *yǔ-lù*'s preface relates that Guāng-shēn first met 華嚴 [[德玉]] in winter **辛未** = **1691** during the Huá-yán *jié-zhì* retreat, where his rough-edged Chán confidence earned him "a vigorous beating" from the elder; later, repenting, he presented his teacher's draft *yǔ-lù* to Dé-yù for vetting and a preface.
