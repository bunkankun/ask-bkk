**Wú Mín** 吳旻 (*fl.* mid-Ming, precise lifedates and biographical detail unrecorded). Mid-Ming physician. Author of the *Fúshòu jīngfāng* 扶壽精方 ([[KR3ed045]]), a practical-medical recipe handbook of 2 *juǎn*. The work was already circulating before 1553 (the date of the earliest extant preface, by the *Sānguān bīngbèi* Zhào Wénháo) and was reprinted in 1573 (the *Wànlì* year 1) by the *Héngyáng shānrén* Shī Dǔchén. Wú Mín is not in CBDB; no biographical record survives outside his attribution on the work. Several distinct mid-Ming physicians named Wú Mín exist in popular medical anthologies; this Wú Mín is distinguished only by the *Fúshòu jīngfāng* attribution.
