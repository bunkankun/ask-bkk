**Wú Wù** 吳悞 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0298]] *Dān fáng xū zhī* 丹房須知 ("What Must Be Known about the Elixir Chamber") — a practical manual on alchemical laboratory procedure, equipment, and ingredients, dated by its 1163 preface. The *Dān fáng xū zhī* is one of the most important technical alchemical texts from the Southern Sòng, extensively illustrated in the received Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng edition.
