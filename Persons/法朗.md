# 法朗
Fǎlǎng (507–581)

Chén-dynasty Sānlùn 三論 master, known as Xīnghuáng Fǎlǎng 興皇法朗 from his residence at the Xīnghuáng monastery 興皇寺 in Jiànkāng under imperial patronage. A direct disciple of [[僧詮]] Sēngquán of Shèshān 攝山, he transmitted the Madhyamaka exegetical tradition descended from [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva via [[僧肇]] Sēngzhào, and was the master of [[吉藏]] Jízàng, the systematizer of the Sānlùn school. Lived 507–581 CE.
