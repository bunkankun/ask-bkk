Chén Mó 陳謨 (Sòng), *zì* Zhōngxíng 中行, was a Southern Sòng official of Yǒng-chūn 永春 (Quán-zhōu 泉州, Fú-jiàn). *Jìnshì* of Qìng-yuán 2 (1196). Held a sequence of imperial-library posts: appointed *Mì-shū-shěng zhèng-zì* in Jiā-tài 2 (1202), with concurrent *Guó-shǐ-yuàn biān-xiū* from Jiā-tài 3 (1203); appointed *Shí-lù-yuàn jiǎn-tǎo guān* in Kāi-xǐ 3 (1207); appointed *Jiào-shū láng* in Jiā-dìng 2 (1209) with continuing *jiǎn-tǎo* duty. His career outline is in the *Guǎn-gé xù-lù*. His major surviving work is the *Dōng-gōng bèi-lǎn* 東宮備覽 (KR3a0068), submitted to the throne during his *Mì-shū-shěng zhèng-zì* tenure (Jiā-tài 2 / 1202) for the heir-apparent's instruction. (Note: the Kanripo catalog meta gives the author's name as 陳模 — apparently a typographical slip 模 for 謨, as both pronounced *mó*. The form 陳謨 is preserved in the SKQS WYG source itself; the *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì* records this work under 陳謨, and the form 陳謨 is followed here. The 陳模 form circulates in some modern catalogs.)
