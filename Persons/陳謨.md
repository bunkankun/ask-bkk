Chén Mó 陳謨 (Sòng), *zì* Zhōngxíng 中行, was a Southern Sòng official of Yǒngchūn 永春 (Quánzhōu 泉州, Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of Qìngyuán 2 (1196). Held a sequence of imperial-library posts: appointed *Mìshūshěng zhèngzì* in Jiātài 2 (1202), with concurrent *Guóshǐyuàn biānxiū* from Jiātài 3 (1203); appointed *Shílùyuàn jiǎntǎo guān* in Kāixǐ 3 (1207); appointed *Jiàoshū láng* in Jiādìng 2 (1209) with continuing *jiǎntǎo* duty. His career outline is in the *Guǎngé xùlù*. His major surviving work is the *Dōnggōng bèilǎn* 東宮備覽 ([[KR3a0068]]), submitted to the throne during his *Mìshūshěng zhèngzì* tenure (Jiātài 2 / 1202) for the heir-apparent's instruction. (Note: the Kanripo catalog meta gives the author's name as 陳模 — apparently a typographical slip 模 for 謨, as both pronounced *mó*. The form 陳謨 is preserved in the SKQS WYG source itself; the *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì* records this work under 陳謨, and the form 陳謨 is followed here. The 陳模 form circulates in some modern catalogs.)
