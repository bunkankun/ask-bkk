**Wáng Xuánhé** 王懸河 (*fl.* late 7th / early 8th century) was a Táng Daoist scholar, compiler of [[KR5e0034]] ***Shàng qīng dào lèi shì xiāng*** 上清道類事相 and other Shàng qīng 上清 topical reference works.
