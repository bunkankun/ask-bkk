# Xīnyuè 心月

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Shíxī 石溪 ("Stone-Brook"), also Chuányī 傳衣 ("Robe-Transmitter"). Posthumously styled **Fóhǎi chánshī 佛海禪師**. Per DILA: died Bǎoyòu 2.6.9 (24 July 1254). Native of Méizhōu 眉州 in Shǔ. Dharma-heir in the Sōngyuán → Yùn'ān or parallel branch (precise master debated). Abbot of Jìngshān in his maturity (whence Jìngshān Xīnyuè). Recorded sayings: *Shíxī Xīnyuè chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]], 3 juan) and the miscellany *Shíxī Xīnyuè chánshī zálù* ([[KR6q0339]], 1 juan).

A separate Qīng monk also named **Xīnyuè** 心月: Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** [[超見]] (1614–1688) at **Pánshān** 盤山. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Chèfán** [[徹凡]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0586]] 《盤山了宗禪師語錄》 4 juan, J40 B474.
