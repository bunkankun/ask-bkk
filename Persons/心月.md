# Xīn-yuè 心月

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Shí-xī 石溪 ("Stone-Brook"), also Chuán-yī 傳衣 ("Robe-Transmitter"). Posthumously styled **Fó-hǎi chánshī 佛海禪師**. Per DILA: died Bǎoyòu 2.6.9 (24 July 1254). Native of Méi-zhōu 眉州 in Shǔ. Dharma-heir in the Sōngyuán → Yùn'ān or parallel branch (precise master debated). Abbot of Jìng-shān in his maturity (whence Jìng-shān Xīn-yuè). Recorded sayings: *Shí-xī Xīn-yuè chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]], 3 juan) and the miscellany *Shí-xī Xīn-yuè chánshī zá-lù* ([[KR6q0339]], 1 juan).
