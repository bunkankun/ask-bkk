# Mèng Xiàn-zhōng 孟獻忠

A mid-Tang official-Buddhist devotee, lifedates not preserved; identified in his own colophons as **梓州司馬** ("Sī-mǎ [Adjutant] of Zǐ-zhōu" — modern Sān-tái 三台 county, Sìchuān). His dating is fixed by his own preface to the **《金剛般若經集驗記》** [[KR6r0174]] *Jīn-gāng bō-rě jīng jí-yàn jì*, dated **大唐開元六年 = 718**, and by an internal reference to his earlier post at **Shēn-zhōu** 申州 in **長安三載 = 703**. His career is therefore datable to the late 7th and early 8th century — the great age of Tang Diamond-Sūtra cultic devotion. The mid-Tang local-administrative position (*sī-mǎ* of a regional *zhōu*) places him squarely within the Tang **scholar-official lay-Buddhist class** that produced much of the Tang miracle-tale anthological literature.

His sole surviving work is the **《金剛般若經集驗記》** *Jīn-gāng bō-rě jīng jí-yàn jì* (X1629, 3 *juǎn*), the earliest **specialised** *Vajracchedikā*-miracle anthology — a thematic predecessor of the much larger Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng *Jīn-gāng-jīng* miracle-anthological tradition.
