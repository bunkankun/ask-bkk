# 田元愷

Tián Yuánkǎi 田元愷 (字 Huáchén 華臣), of Western Qín (Shǎnxī). Early-Qīng *guānfēng* (circuit-inspector) of the Hǎiyī / Yúnjiān district; patron of the physician [[李修之]] Lǐ Xiūzhī, who conferred the studio-name *Jiùdétáng* 舊德堂 and supplied the principal preface to [[KR3ep098]].
