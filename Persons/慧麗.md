Huìlì 慧麗 (1586 – **1650-11-03**), the third-listed *fǎsì* of **Jùyún Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]] in the canonical 《續燈正統》 roster, alongside **Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] and **Sānmù Huìzhī** 萬松三目慧芝. *Hào* **Tiěméi** 鐵眉 ("Iron-Brow", from Guǎngzhēn's transmission-verse *zhèng kàn Tiěméi rèn-me lái* 正看鐵眉恁麼來), also **Sānbāzhǎng** 三巴掌 ("Three-Slap"; both *hào* bestowed by Guǎngzhēn at transmission). Native of **Bǎixiāng** 柏鄉 (Zhílì, Héběi) — exceptional in the otherwise Sì-chuān-centered Jùyúnpài. Death notice: **Shùnzhì 7 庚寅 10/10** = **1650-11-03**, age **65** (per 《續燈正統》 j. 17).

**Formation.** Tall, broad-browed, full-bearded with screw-curl hair (*xū rú luójié* 鬚如螺結). Originally a glaze-tile artisan at the **Liúlíchǎng** 琉璃廠 (Bǎixiāng locality); tonsured in middle age under **Tiāntái Wújìn chánshī** 天台無盡禪師. **Chóngzhēn 6 = 1633**: traveled to **Éméishān** 峨眉山 to feed monks; the company was attacked by bandits, only Huìlì surviving. Crossing through Zhōngzhōu, **Mǎ Shàoyóu** 馬少游 (a *sīmǎ*) sent him on to Guǎngzhēn's seat. Guǎngzhēn at first sight: *wǒjiā rén lái le* 我家人來了 ("our man has come"). Trained as *hángtáng sǎsǎo* 行堂灑掃 servant; further work under **Tiěbì Yǐnshī** [[慧機]] 鐵壁隱師 (his fellow-disciple *xiōngdì*); breakthrough on the *wànfǎ guīyī* 萬法歸一 *gōngàn* in the 25th *qī* (175 days continuous *zuòchán*) with *qī zhù xiāng bù xià chántà* 七炷香不下禪塌. Pre-transmission interview: when Guǎngzhēn forbade him each gestural reply (clapping, foot-stamp, teeth-clicking), Huìlì stood erect and offered the *cāndé sān bāzhǎng* 參得三巴掌 *jì*; transmission ratified.

**Abbacies.** *Chūshì hòu kāitáng sì cì* 出世後開堂四次 — four formal abbacies: (i) **Kuízhōufǔ Shízhù Niúnánshān Huáyánsì** 夔州府石砫牛南山華嚴寺 (entry **崇禎辛巳 10/15** = **1641 winter**); (ii) **Wànxiàn Bǎofēngshān Yúnláisì** 萬縣寶峰山雲來寺 — the seat from which his title-form **寶峰三巴掌** derives; (iii) **Wànxiàn Dàfósì** 萬縣大佛寺; and (iv) **Shīwèi** 施衛 in **甲申秋 = 1644 autumn** with the lay-patron **Xióng Yuèyá** 熊月崖 (= [[KR6q0448]]'s preface-writer Xióng Rǔxué 熊汝學). Subsequent flight south to **Nánbīn Fèngwèi** 南濱鳳衛 at the request of **Móugōng** 牟公 (the late-Míng grand secretary 文安之 Wén Ānzhī, who composed the 巴掌歌 panegyric *Chóngzhēn jiǎshēn qiū qián 5 rì* = **1644 late summer**). Died at Nánbīn passing the bowl-and-robe to dharma-heir **Xióngěrān** 熊耳菴; stūpa at **Guānyīnshān** 觀音山.

**Work.** [[KR6q0452]] 《鐵眉三巴掌禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B242, compiled by *fǎsūn* 性養 + 性亮.

**Catalog form.** The Jiāxīng catalog meta gives the author-name as the unusual compound **鐵眉號三巴掌** "Tiěméi (*hào*) Sānbāzhǎng" — the dharma-name 慧麗 itself does not appear in the title or catalog and was recovered only via the 1644 行狀 closing the *yǔlù* and the 《續燈正統》 j. 17 cross-reference.

Sources: [[KR6q0452]] 行狀 (anonymous, post-1650 closing biography); 《續燈正統》 j. 17; 《南宋元明禪林僧寶傳》 j. 15; 文安之 〈巴掌歌〉 1644.
