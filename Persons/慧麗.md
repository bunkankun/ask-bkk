Huì-lì 慧麗 (1586 – **1650-11-03**), the third-listed *fǎ-sì* of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]] in the canonical 《續燈正統》 roster, alongside **Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] and **Sān-mù Huì-zhī** 萬松三目慧芝. *Hào* **Tiě-méi** 鐵眉 ("Iron-Brow", from Guǎng-zhēn's transmission-verse *zhèng kàn Tiě-méi rèn-me lái* 正看鐵眉恁麼來), also **Sān-bā-zhǎng** 三巴掌 ("Three-Slap"; both *hào* bestowed by Guǎng-zhēn at transmission). Native of **Bǎi-xiāng** 柏鄉 (Zhí-lì, Hé-běi) — exceptional in the otherwise Sì-chuān-centered Jù-yún-pài. Death notice: **Shùn-zhì 7 庚寅 10/10** = **1650-11-03**, age **65** (per 《續燈正統》 j. 17).

**Formation.** Tall, broad-browed, full-bearded with screw-curl hair (*xū rú luó-jié* 鬚如螺結). Originally a glaze-tile artisan at the **Liú-lí-chǎng** 琉璃廠 (Bǎi-xiāng locality); tonsured in middle age under **Tiān-tái Wú-jìn chán-shī** 天台無盡禪師. **Chóng-zhēn 6 = 1633**: traveled to **Éméi-shān** 峨眉山 to feed monks; the company was attacked by bandits, only Huì-lì surviving. Crossing through Zhōng-zhōu, **Mǎ Shào-yóu** 馬少游 (a *sī-mǎ*) sent him on to Guǎng-zhēn's seat. Guǎng-zhēn at first sight: *wǒ-jiā rén lái le* 我家人來了 ("our man has come"). Trained as *háng-táng sǎ-sǎo* 行堂灑掃 servant; further work under **Tiě-bì Yǐn-shī** [[慧機]] 鐵壁隱師 (his fellow-disciple *xiōng-dì*); breakthrough on the *wàn-fǎ guī-yī* 萬法歸一 *gōng-àn* in the 25th *qī* (175 days continuous *zuò-chán*) with *qī zhù xiāng bù xià chán-tà* 七炷香不下禪塌. Pre-transmission interview: when Guǎng-zhēn forbade him each gestural reply (clapping, foot-stamp, teeth-clicking), Huì-lì stood erect and offered the *cān-dé sān bā-zhǎng* 參得三巴掌 *jì*; transmission ratified.

**Abbacies.** *Chū-shì hòu kāi-táng sì cì* 出世後開堂四次 — four formal abbacies: (i) **Kuí-zhōu-fǔ Shí-zhù Niú-nán-shān Huá-yán-sì** 夔州府石砫牛南山華嚴寺 (entry **崇禎辛巳 10/15** = **1641 winter**); (ii) **Wàn-xiàn Bǎo-fēng-shān Yún-lái-sì** 萬縣寶峰山雲來寺 — the seat from which his title-form **寶峰三巴掌** derives; (iii) **Wàn-xiàn Dà-fó-sì** 萬縣大佛寺; and (iv) **Shī-wèi** 施衛 in **甲申秋 = 1644 autumn** with the lay-patron **Xióng Yuè-yá** 熊月崖 (= [[KR6q0448]]'s preface-writer Xióng Rǔ-xué 熊汝學). Subsequent flight south to **Nán-bīn Fèng-wèi** 南濱鳳衛 at the request of **Móu-gōng** 牟公 (the late-Míng grand secretary 文安之 Wén Ān-zhī, who composed the 巴掌歌 panegyric *Chóng-zhēn jiǎ-shēn qiū qián 5 rì* = **1644 late summer**). Died at Nán-bīn passing the bowl-and-robe to dharma-heir **Xióng-ěr-ān** 熊耳菴; stūpa at **Guān-yīn-shān** 觀音山.

**Work.** [[KR6q0452]] 《鐵眉三巴掌禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B242, compiled by *fǎ-sūn* 性養 + 性亮.

**Catalog form.** The Jiā-xīng catalog meta gives the author-name as the unusual compound **鐵眉號三巴掌** "Tiě-méi (*hào*) Sān-bā-zhǎng" — the dharma-name 慧麗 itself does not appear in the title or catalog and was recovered only via the 1644 行狀 closing the *yǔ-lù* and the 《續燈正統》 j. 17 cross-reference.

Sources: [[KR6q0452]] 行狀 (anonymous, post-1650 closing biography); 《續燈正統》 j. 17; 《南宋元明禪林僧寶傳》 j. 15; 文安之 〈巴掌歌〉 1644.
