**Jiǎng Xuězhōu** 蔣雪洲 (*fl.* late 17th c., 清), son of [[蔣介繁]]. After his father's death he raised the funds and saw the *Běncǎo zéyào gāngmù* 本草擇要綱目 ([[KR3ec057]]) through the press in 康熙己未 (1679), composing the editorial *fánlì* and explaining that he was completing the project to fulfil filial obligation ("一以完先人仁壽之懷"). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
