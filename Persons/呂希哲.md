Lǚ Xī-zhé 呂希哲 (*fl. c.* 1089 — early 12th century; lifedates uncertain), *zì* Yuán-míng 原明. Of the great Sòng *Lǚ-shì* family of Shòu-zhōu 壽州 (originally of Lái-zhōu 萊州). Son of Lǚ Gōng-zhù 呂公著 (1018–1089, *Sī-tú* chancellor); great-grandson of Lǚ Yí-jiǎn 呂夷簡 (978–1043, *Tài-shī chéng-xiàng*) — three generations of Lǚ-shì prime ministers. Refused office while his father was prime minister; entered service after his father's death (1089). Career: *Bīng-bù yuán wài-láng*, *Chóng-zhèng diàn shuō-shū*, *Mì-gé jiào-lǐ*, *Zhī Huái-zhōu*; *Guāng-lù shǎo-qīng* under Hui-zōng; *Zhī Cáo-zhōu*; banished under the *Yuán-yòu dǎng* purge of 1102; restored to *Zhī Xiāng-zhōu* and *Zhī Xíng-zhōu*; ended as *Fèng-cí* sinecure-holder. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 336. The principal example of late-Northern-Sòng intellectual eclecticism — studied under Jiāo Qiān-zhī 焦千之, Sūn Fù 孫復, Shí Jiè 石介 (the *Tài-xué* tradition), the two Chéng brothers (the *Luò* school), Zhāng Zǎi 張載 (the *Guān* school), and the Wáng Ān-shí father-and-son (the *Xī-níng* reform). Author of the *Lǚ-shì zá jì* 呂氏雜記 ([[KR3j0099]]).
