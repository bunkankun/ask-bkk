Lǚ Xīzhé 呂希哲 (*fl. c.* 1089 — early 12th century; lifedates uncertain), *zì* Yuánmíng 原明. Of the great Sòng *Lǚshì* family of Shòuzhōu 壽州 (originally of Láizhōu 萊州). Son of Lǚ Gōngzhù 呂公著 (1018–1089, *Sītú* chancellor); great-grandson of Lǚ Yíjiǎn 呂夷簡 (978–1043, *Tàishī chéngxiàng*) — three generations of Lǚshì prime ministers. Refused office while his father was prime minister; entered service after his father's death (1089). Career: *Bīngbù yuán wàiláng*, *Chóngzhèng diàn shuōshū*, *Mìgé jiàolǐ*, *Zhī Huáizhōu*; *Guānglù shǎoqīng* under Hui-zōng; *Zhī Cáozhōu*; banished under the *Yuányòu dǎng* purge of 1102; restored to *Zhī Xiāngzhōu* and *Zhī Xíngzhōu*; ended as *Fèngcí* sinecure-holder. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 336. The principal example of late-Northern-Sòng intellectual eclecticism — studied under Jiāo Qiānzhī 焦千之, Sūn Fù 孫復, Shí Jiè 石介 (the *Tàixué* tradition), the two Chéng brothers (the *Luò* school), Zhāng Zǎi 張載 (the *Guān* school), and the Wáng Ānshí father-and-son (the *Xīníng* reform). Author of the *Lǚshì zá jì* 呂氏雜記 ([[KR3j0099]]).
