**Yǒushān** 友珊 (*hào*), Qīng-period figure, brother-in-law of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng (Mèngyīng); inheritor of [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng's manuscript *Nǚkē jíyào* ([[KR3ei010]]) after the death of [[徐虹橋]] Xú Hóngqiáo. The manuscript was passed from Yǒushān to Wáng for the 1850 printed edition.
