# Wèi Shěn 衛沈

Eastern-Hàn (legendary?) paediatrician; conventionally identified by Sòng-Qīng bibliographers as the original author / transmitter of the early paediatric canon *Lúxìn jīng* 顱顖經 ([[KR3ej023]]), said by [[柯逢時]] Kē Féngshí's 1910 collation note to *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* ([[KR3ej017]]) to have "originated" the work, which was then brought to completion by [[錢乙]] Qián Zhòngyáng of the Sòng. Historicity uncertain; some modern philologists treat the attribution as legendary.
