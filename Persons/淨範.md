# Jìng-fàn 淨範 (Wéi-zé / Zhè-ān Jìng-fàn 為則 / 蔗菴淨範)

Cáo-dòng-school *chán-shī* of the 淨-generation (= dharma-grandson of **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄 through [[明盂|Sān-yí Míng-yú]] 三宜明盂, 1599–1665). Born **1620**; died **康熙31 = 1692**, age **73**. Native of **Wú-jiāng** 吳江 (Sū-zhōu prefecture); lay surname **Jī** 嵇. *Zì* **Wéi-zé** 為則 ("To-Be Pattern"), *bié-hào* **Zhè-ān** 蔗菴 ("Sugarcane Hermit"). DILA-disambiguated as A016471: 淨範 = 為則 = 蔗菴 = same person.

**Formation.** **15** out of household; **18** tonsured at **Tiáo-xī Shuāng-lín Zhōng-ān** 苕谿雙林中菴. **20** studied *wéi-shí* under **Xuě-háng fǎ-shī** 雪航法師. Pilgrimage to **Biàn-shān** 弁山 to consult **Mò-yīn** 默音; *xǐng* on thunder-clap during *pǔ-qǐng*. **21** full precepts at **Gū-sū Ruì-guāng** 姑蘇瑞光. Pilgrimage to **Zhí-shí**, **Xuě-jiāo**, **Yú-ān** 愚菴 (= Sān-yí Míng-yú), **Tiān-tóng**. **26** returned to Yún-mén as *jì-shì*. **己丑 = 1649** (age 30) formal *fù-fǎ* from Yú-ān.

**Five abbacies.** **Zuì-lǐ Zī-shèng** 檇李資聖, **Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng** 雲門顯聖 (Cáo-dòng flagship — succession-seat after Míng-yú's 1665 death), **Cáo-shān Hù-shēng** 曹山護生, **Dōng-tǎ Guǎng-fú** 東塔廣福, **Dōng-guān Tiān-huá** 東關天華.

**Listed first** among Míng-yú's thirty *fǎ-sì*. Lone heir: **Hú-ān Lǎn-wēng Yù** 安定關華藏嬾翁遇.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0411]] 《三宜盂禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J27 B189) — head compiler of his master's *yǔ-lù* (signed *zhù Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng-sì mén-rén Jìng-fàn biān* 住雲門顯聖寺門人淨範編). After Míng-yú's death 1665, Jìng-fàn led the post-mortem custody of the master's body and was installed as abbot of Xiǎn-shèng. He and his Cáo-dòng-line dharma-brother **Tǐng-liáng-tíng** 挺俍亭 (= [[淨挺]]) each brought an independent *xíng-zhuàng* to **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** at Tiān-tóng requesting the *tǎ-míng* that was then added to the re-integrated c. 1666–67 cutting.
- [[KR6q0506]] 《蔗菴範禪師語錄》 30 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B369 — his own collected sermons, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-zhāng** [[智璋]] *děng lù*. Juan 30 *zì-xù*.

Calligraphy was a noted skill (*gōng shū* 工書). Sources: [[KR6q0411]] juan-head signature line + juan 11 *tǎ-míng*; [[KR6q0506]] juan 30; 《正源略集》 j. 13; *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* j. 63; 《疑年錄》 451.
