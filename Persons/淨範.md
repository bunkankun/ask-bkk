# Jìngfàn 淨範 (Wéizé / Zhèān Jìngfàn 為則 / 蔗菴淨範)

Cáo-dòng-school *chánshī* of the 淨-generation (= dharma-grandson of **Zhànrán Yuánchéng** 湛然圓澄 through [[明盂|Sānyí Míngyú]] 三宜明盂, 1599–1665). Born **1620**; died **康熙31 = 1692**, age **73**. Native of **Wújiāng** 吳江 (Sūzhōu prefecture); lay surname **Jī** 嵇. *Zì* **Wéizé** 為則 ("To-Be Pattern"), *biéhào* **Zhèān** 蔗菴 ("Sugarcane Hermit"). DILA-disambiguated as A016471: 淨範 = 為則 = 蔗菴 = same person.

**Formation.** **15** out of household; **18** tonsured at **Tiáoxī Shuānglín Zhōngān** 苕谿雙林中菴. **20** studied *wéishí* under **Xuěháng fǎshī** 雪航法師. Pilgrimage to **Biànshān** 弁山 to consult **Mòyīn** 默音; *xǐng* on thunder-clap during *pǔqǐng*. **21** full precepts at **Gūsū Ruìguāng** 姑蘇瑞光. Pilgrimage to **Zhíshí**, **Xuějiāo**, **Yúān** 愚菴 (= Sānyí Míngyú), **Tiāntóng**. **26** returned to Yúnmén as *jìshì*. **己丑 = 1649** (age 30) formal *fùfǎ* from Yúān.

**Five abbacies.** **Zuìlǐ Zīshèng** 檇李資聖, **Yúnmén Xiǎnshèng** 雲門顯聖 (Cáodòng flagship — succession-seat after Míngyú's 1665 death), **Cáoshān Hùshēng** 曹山護生, **Dōngtǎ Guǎngfú** 東塔廣福, **Dōngguān Tiānhuá** 東關天華.

**Listed first** among Míngyú's thirty *fǎsì*. Lone heir: **Húān Lǎnwēng Yù** 安定關華藏嬾翁遇.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0411]] 《三宜盂禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J27 B189) — head compiler of his master's *yǔlù* (signed *zhù Yúnmén Xiǎnshèngsì ménrén Jìngfàn biān* 住雲門顯聖寺門人淨範編). After Míngyú's death 1665, Jìngfàn led the post-mortem custody of the master's body and was installed as abbot of Xiǎnshèng. He and his Cáo-dòng-line dharma-brother **Tǐngliángtíng** 挺俍亭 (= [[淨挺]]) each brought an independent *xíngzhuàng* to **Mùchén Dàomǐn** at Tiāntóng requesting the *tǎmíng* that was then added to the re-integrated c. 1666–67 cutting.
- [[KR6q0506]] 《蔗菴範禪師語錄》 30 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B369 — his own collected sermons, recorded by *fǎsì* **Zhìzhāng** [[智璋]] *děng lù*. Juan 30 *zìxù*.

Calligraphy was a noted skill (*gōng shū* 工書). Sources: [[KR6q0411]] juan-head signature line + juan 11 *tǎmíng*; [[KR6q0506]] juan 30; 《正源略集》 j. 13; *Wǔdēng quánshū* j. 63; 《疑年錄》 451.
