**Cháng Pǔlánxī** 常普蘭奚 (*fl.* early-to-mid Yuán, *Tiān-lì* era), Yuán Imperial Diary Attendant who authored the presentation memorial (*jìn-shū biǎo* 進書表) to Hū Sī-huì's *Yǐn-shàn zhèng-yào* 飲膳正要 ([[KR3eo040]]) on its presentation to the throne in *Tiān-lì* 3 = 1330. The non-Hàn name (likely Mongol or Central-Asian transliteration) suggests a multi-ethnic Yuán official. No further biographical data available. No CBDB record.
