**Cháng Pǔlánxī** 常普蘭奚 (*fl.* early-to-mid Yuán, *Tiānlì* era), Yuán Imperial Diary Attendant who authored the presentation memorial (*jìnshū biǎo* 進書表) to Hū Sīhuì's *Yǐnshàn zhèngyào* 飲膳正要 ([[KR3eo040]]) on its presentation to the throne in *Tiānlì* 3 = 1330. The non-Hàn name (likely Mongol or Central-Asian transliteration) suggests a multi-ethnic Yuán official. No further biographical data available. No CBDB record.
