Jìn Fǔ 靳輔 (1633–1692), zì *Zǐ-yuán* 紫垣, posthumous *Wén-xiāng* 文襄, was the principal Qīng *Zǒng-dū Hé-dào* 總督河道 (Yellow River Commissioner) of the *Kāng-xī* period and the practical implementer of Pān Jì-xùn 潘季馴's late-Míng *shù-shuǐ gōng-shā* hydraulic doctrine for the early Qīng. From the *Xiāng-hóng* 鑲紅 Banner of the *Hàn-jūn* (Chinese-bannered) division, he was originally given a *Hàn-lín-yuàn xiū-zhuàn* 翰林院修撰 appointment, then transferred to provincial-administration. He was elevated from *Ān-huī xún-fǔ* to *Zǒng-dū Hé-dào* in *Kāng-xī* 16 (1677) at the height of the Yellow-River crisis when the river had broken through *Gāo-jiā yàn* 高家堰 and the lower Yellow River and Huái converged eastward into the sea, abandoning the historical channel.

Jìn served three terms as *Zǒng-dū Hé-dào* (1677–88, with brief intermissions), in close partnership with the deputy Chén Huáng 陳潢 — the actual hydraulic theorist of the team. The Jìn-Chén program was Pān Jì-xùn's principle made operational: confine the Yellow River to a single channel between high embankments, scour silt by raising channel velocity, integrate Yellow River and Grand Canal management. Their construction works included: the *Zhōng-hé* 中河 (Middle Canal) and *Zào-hé* 皂河 — major new channels providing the critical *Yùn-hé* (Grand Canal) bypass of the unstable lower Yellow River; the *Yáo-dī* 遥堤 and *Lǚ-dī* 縷堤 (multi-tier dyke system); the rebuilt *Gāo-jiā yàn* and the *Zhái-bà* 翟壩 closure works; and the six *Jiǎn-shuǐ* (water-reduction) overflow gates.

The Sì-kù tíyào gives the canonical summary of his hydraulic doctrine: "*zhù dī yá, shū xià-liú, sāi jué-kǒu — yǒu xiān-hòu ér wú huǎn-jí*" (build embankment-cliffs, clear the lower-flow, plug the breaches — there is order of priority but no relaxation). The works produced the *Kāng-xī*-era stabilization of the Yellow River — the foundation of the eighteenth-century consolidation.

His memorials are preserved in the [[KR2f0035]] *Wén-xiāng zòu-shū* 文襄奏疏 in 8 *juàn* (with the appended *Fǔ Wǎn tí-gǎo* 撫皖題藁 of one fasc.), edited by his son Jìn Zhì-yù 靳治豫. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 279 has his biography. CBDB id 57335 (1633–1692).
