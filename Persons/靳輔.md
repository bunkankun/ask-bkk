Jìn Fǔ 靳輔 (1633–1692), zì *Zǐyuán* 紫垣, posthumous *Wénxiāng* 文襄, was the principal Qīng *Zǒngdū Hédào* 總督河道 (Yellow River Commissioner) of the *Kāngxī* period and the practical implementer of Pān Jìxùn 潘季馴's late-Míng *shùshuǐ gōngshā* hydraulic doctrine for the early Qīng. From the *Xiānghóng* 鑲紅 Banner of the *Hànjūn* (Chinese-bannered) division, he was originally given a *Hànlínyuàn xiūzhuàn* 翰林院修撰 appointment, then transferred to provincial-administration. He was elevated from *Ānhuī xúnfǔ* to *Zǒngdū Hédào* in *Kāngxī* 16 (1677) at the height of the Yellow-River crisis when the river had broken through *Gāojiā yàn* 高家堰 and the lower Yellow River and Huái converged eastward into the sea, abandoning the historical channel.

Jìn served three terms as *Zǒngdū Hédào* (1677–88, with brief intermissions), in close partnership with the deputy Chén Huáng 陳潢 — the actual hydraulic theorist of the team. The Jìn-Chén program was Pān Jìxùn's principle made operational: confine the Yellow River to a single channel between high embankments, scour silt by raising channel velocity, integrate Yellow River and Grand Canal management. Their construction works included: the *Zhōnghé* 中河 (Middle Canal) and *Zàohé* 皂河 — major new channels providing the critical *Yùnhé* (Grand Canal) bypass of the unstable lower Yellow River; the *Yáodī* 遥堤 and *Lǚdī* 縷堤 (multi-tier dyke system); the rebuilt *Gāojiā yàn* and the *Zháibà* 翟壩 closure works; and the six *Jiǎnshuǐ* (water-reduction) overflow gates.

The Sìkù tíyào gives the canonical summary of his hydraulic doctrine: "*zhù dī yá, shū xiàliú, sāi juékǒu — yǒu xiānhòu ér wú huǎnjí*" (build embankment-cliffs, clear the lower-flow, plug the breaches — there is order of priority but no relaxation). The works produced the *Kāngxī*-era stabilization of the Yellow River — the foundation of the eighteenth-century consolidation.

His memorials are preserved in the [[KR2f0035]] *Wénxiāng zòushū* 文襄奏疏 in 8 *juàn* (with the appended *Fǔ Wǎn tígǎo* 撫皖題藁 of one fasc.), edited by his son Jìn Zhìyù 靳治豫. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 279 has his biography. CBDB id 57335 (1633–1692).
