**Yú Liú** 虞流 (*fl.* Northern Sòng), obstetrical compiler whose now-lost *Bèichǎn jìyòng fāng* 備產濟用方 is preserved substantively only within *juǎn* 6 of [[朱端章]] Zhū Duānzhāng's *Wèishēng jiābǎo chǎnkē bèiyào* ([[KR3ei056]], c. 1180). Together with [[李師聖]] Lǐ Shīshèng's *Chǎnyù bǎoqìng jí* ([[KR3ei076]]), Yú's work forms the substantive Northern-Sòng obstetrical-formulary core of Zhū Duānzhāng's compilation. No independent transmission of Yú's text survives; no biographical detail is recorded in standard sources. Not in CBDB.
