# Yǐnkōng 隱空

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Fújiàn Buddhist monk, resident of **Guǎngjìsì** 廣濟寺. Lifedates not preserved.

Documented in [[項謙]] Xiàng Qiān's 1652 preface to the *Zhǔntí fénxiū xīdì chànhuǐ xuánwén* ([[KR6j0760]]) as the monk who showed Xiàng both the *Cuṇḍā* repentance liturgy and the set of protector-deity images transmitted from the Míng-dynasty visionary lay practitioner [[夏道人]] Xià Dàorén. Yǐnkōng's transmission served as the proximate channel for the 1652 Jiā-hé re-printing of the *xuánwén*.
