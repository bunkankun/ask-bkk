Zōng-wèi 宗位. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sūn* (dharma-grandson) of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] (1611–1680), and dharma-heir of **Zhuó-fēng Xìng-jué** [[性玨]]. Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler of two *yǔ-lù* in J39: [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》 (J39 B438, prefaced 1695–1696, his dharma-grandfather's *yǔ-lù*) and 《卓峰玨禪師語錄》 (J39 B444, his teacher's *yǔ-lù*). Both signed "嗣法門人宗位編".

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Korean Sŏn monk **Chŏngam Chongwi** 청암종위 / 青嵓宗位 (DILA A032016), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hŏwŏl Munryŏng** 虛月文䝺.
