Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master and exegete; *zì* **Tán-wǔ** 曇武, sobriquet **Zhā-ān** 樝菴 (or 樝庵, "Hawthorn Hermitage"). DILA Authority A000383. Lay surname **Hú** 胡 (one source: 何); native of **Líng-hǎi** 臨海 in Tāizhōu 台州 (modern Zhèjiāng). Born 1021, died on the day of *fū-zuò* (cross-legged seated meditation) in early summer of Jiànzhōng-jìngguó 建中靖國 1 (= April–May 1101 CE), aged 81, with 68 years of monastic seniority.

Took monastic vows at six under a master at the Língjiù 靈鷲 of Tiāntái-shān; received full ordination at fourteen. Reading Yǒngmíng Yánshòu's 永明延壽 (904–975) *Xīn-fù* 心賦 occasioned an awakening; he subsequently travelled to the Néngrén-sì 能仁寺 to study under **Shénzhào Běnrú** 神照本如 (982–1051), the dharma-heir of Sìmíng Zhīlǐ 四明知禮, and there mastered the Tiāntái doctrines of *yī-xīn sān-guān* 一心三觀 ("threefold contemplation in a single mind") and *Fǎhuá sānmèi* 法華三昧 ("Lotus Sūtra *samādhi*"). He served first as abbot of the Wú-xiàng Huì-yīn-sì 無相慧因寺, then was transferred to the Chìchéng Chóngshàn-sì 赤城崇善寺, where his teaching attracted large numbers for several decades.

In Shàoshèng 紹聖 (1094–1098) the prefecture invited him to be abbot of Néngrén-sì; he declined and retired to the eastern peak of Dōngyè-shān 東掖山, where he built a thatched hut next to a hawthorn tree (whence his sobriquet *Zhā-ān*) and devoted himself for some twenty years to Pure Land practice (*ān-yǎng* 安養 / Sukhāvatī).

Major works: *Xuán-qiān bèi-jiǎn* 玄籤備檢 ([[KR6d0009]], X28n588, the supplementary check on Zhànrán's *Xuán-yì shìqiān*), *Wén-jù jiān-nán* 文句箋難 (subcommentary on Zhìyǐ's *Fǎhuá wén-jù*), *Zhǐguān zhù-lǎn* 止觀助覽 (a study aid for the *Móhē zhǐguān*), *Ān-lè-xíng zhù* 安樂行注 (commentary on Huìsī's *Lotus Sūtra Course of Ease and Bliss*), *Lóng-wáng fǎ-yìn jīng shū* 龍王法印經疏, *Ē-mítuó lǐ-wén* 阿彌陀禮文 (Pure Land liturgy), and *Huò-duì* 或對. Disciples include **Fǎlín** 法麟 and **Yīngtōng** 應通.

Sources: *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀 (T2035) *juan* 13 and 24; *Shìmén zhèngtǒng* 釋門正統 *juan* 6; DILA A000383.
