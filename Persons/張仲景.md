# 張仲景 Zhāng Zhòngjǐng

**Zhāng Zhòngjǐng** 張仲景 (given name **Zhāng Jī** 張機, c. 150 – c. 219, late 東漢), of Nányáng 南陽 (modern Hénán). The canonical author of the *Shānghán zábìng lùn* 傷寒雜病論, foundational physician of the *shānghán* febrile-disease tradition and one of the four great masters (*sì dà yīshèng*) of Chinese medicine. The transmitted recensions of his work — *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 and *Jīnguì yàolüè* 金匱要略 — survive through the Northern Sòng editorial reconstitution by [[林億]] Lín Yì and colleagues. Together they form the core of the *fāngjì* tradition that organizes Chinese clinical pharmacy down to the present.
