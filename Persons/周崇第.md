Zhōu Chóngdì 周崇第 (*hào* Yīlì zhǔrén 一慄主人; fl. mid-19th c.) was the *Tóngzhì*-era publisher of [[郭楚賢]] Guō Chǔxián's *Diānkuáng tiáobiàn* ([[KR3eh048]]); his 1863 preface attests Guō's authorship.
