Shèng Yǐjìn 盛以進 (late 16th–early 17th c.), *Línqīngzhōu zhīzhōu* in the early Wàn-lì-late period. In **Wànlì rénzǐ** (1612) he obtained the **Prince of Zhào**'s old edition of **Xiè Zhēn**'s *Sìmíng jí* [[KR4e0216]] and produced the supplemented-and-fixed 10-*juǎn* cutting that became the WYG-base text.
