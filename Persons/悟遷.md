# Wùqiān 悟遷

Southern-Sòng monk (*bǐqiū* 比丘) responsible for a later reprinting of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]); the colophon *bǐqiū Wùqiān kāibǎn* 比丘悟遷開板 records a *wùwǔ*-year cutting, most plausibly Qìngyuán 4 (1198).
