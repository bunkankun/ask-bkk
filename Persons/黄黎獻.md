Northern-Sòng *Yì*-numerologist active under Rénzōng (r. 1023–1063), a follower of Liú Mù [[劉牧]] and developer of his chart-tradition. He authored *Lüèlì yǐnjué* 畧例隱訣, a now-lost work elaborating the *Yìshù gōuyǐn tú* 易數鉤隱圖 ([[KR1a0011]]) chart-corpus. His school is referenced in the *Sìkù* *tíyào* to the *Gōuyǐn tú* as one of three Sòng works (with Wú Mì [[吳秘]]'s *Tōngshén* and Chéng Dàchāng [[程大昌]]'s *Yìyuán*) that developed Liú Mù's system.
