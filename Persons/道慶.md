**Dàoqìng** 道慶 (南朝·齊). Late-5th-century figure named in the genealogy of [[KR3ek040]] *Liú Juānzǐ guǐyí fāng*: an older relative of the editor [[龔慶宣]] who, on his deathbed, entrusted the manuscript to Gōng. No further documentation.
