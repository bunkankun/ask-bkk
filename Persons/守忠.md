# Shǒu-zhōng 守忠

Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Tán-fāng 曇芳. Native of Dū-chāng 都昌 (Nán-kāng, Jiāngxī), lay surname Huáng 黃; born on the 1st of the 10th month of Zhìyuán 12 (28 October 1275), died at Dà-lóng-xiáng Jí-qìng-sì on the 28th of the 10th month of Zhìzhèng 8 (27 November 1348), *shì-shòu* 74, *sēng-là* 56. Tonsured at eleven under [[德珍]] Yù-shān Dé-zhēn at Yún-jū-shān 雲居山, and a dharma-heir of the same; thus, via Dé-zhēn, dharma-grandson of Xū-zhōu Pǔ-dù 虛舟普度 ([[KR6q0340]]). Trained also, in his wandering years, under Tiě-shān Qióng 鐵山瓊, Měng-shān Yì 蒙山異 at Xiū-xiū-ān 休休菴, [[淨伏]] Hǔ-yán Jìng-fú at Jìng-shān (as 綱維 for a year), Dōng-yán Rì 東巖日 at Āyù-wáng, and Yě-wēng Tóng 野翁同 at Xuě-dòu; attained awakening under Dé-zhēn at Líng-yǐn on the 庭前柏樹子 *huàtóu* and received formal *yìn-kě*.

He held five major abbacies — Jiàn-kāng Chóng-yīn chán-sì 崇因禪寺 (from Dàdé 9 / 1305, fifteen years), Jiǎng-shān Tài-píng Xīng-guó 蔣山太平興國 (from Zhìzhì 1 / 1321), the newly-founded Jiǎng-shān Dà-chóng-xǐ Wàn-shòu 大崇禧萬壽寺 (established in Tàidìng 3 / 1326 on his recommendation, after a visit by the future Wén-zōng 文宗 to the Bǎo-zhū peak 寶珠峰), Jìng-shān Xīng-shèng Wàn-shòu 徑山興聖萬壽 (from Zhìzhèng 2 / 1342, as the 49th abbot), and finally Dà-lóng-xiáng Jí-qìng-sì 大龍翔集慶寺 (from Zhìzhèng 5 / 1345). The five-abbacy span gave his *yǔlù* its alternate title *Wǔ-huì lù* 五會錄 ([[KR6q0343]]). His imperial titles accumulated in parallel: Fó-hǎi Pǔ-yìn dà-chánshī 佛海普印大禪師 (1328, from Wén-zōng on accession), Dà-zhōng dà-fū Guǎng-cí Yuán-wù dà-chánshī 大中大夫廣慈圓悟大禪師 (1329), a gold-brocade *jiā-shā* at the Dà-míng-diàn in 1331, and continuing patronage from Shùndì and the Bái-yán 伯顏 / Tuō-tuō 脫脫 governments into the Zhìzhèng era.

Per the *tǎ-míng* of Ōuyáng Xuán 歐陽玄 (Zhìzhèng 9 / 1349), his named dharma-heirs include [[繼祖]] Jì-zǔ of Chángzhōu Shàn-quán, Lán-jiāng Qīng-chǔ 蘭江清濋 (DILA A020031), Shòu-yán Chūn 壽巖椿, Xī-yuán Dá 西源達 (DILA A009716), Yuán-zōng Xìng 圓宗性, and Xī-yǐn Zǔ 西隱祖. Principal sources: the biographical apparatus appended to [[KR6q0343]] (Kè-xīn's *xíng-yè-jì* and Ōuyáng Xuán's *tǎ-míng*); *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 6; *Xù-dēng zhèng-tǒng* *juan* 24; *Nán-Sòng Yuán-Míng chán-lín sēng-bǎo zhuàn* *juan* 13; *Jìng-shān zhì* (identifies him as the 49th-generation 列祖 of Jìng-shān).
