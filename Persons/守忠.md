# Shǒuzhōng 守忠

Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Tánfāng 曇芳. Native of Dūchāng 都昌 (Nánkāng, Jiāngxī), lay surname Huáng 黃; born on the 1st of the 10th month of Zhìyuán 12 (28 October 1275), died at Dàlóngxiáng Jíqìngsì on the 28th of the 10th month of Zhìzhèng 8 (27 November 1348), *shìshòu* 74, *sēnglà* 56. Tonsured at eleven under [[德珍]] Yùshān Dézhēn at Yúnjūshān 雲居山, and a dharma-heir of the same; thus, via Dézhēn, dharma-grandson of Xūzhōu Pǔdù 虛舟普度 ([[KR6q0340]]). Trained also, in his wandering years, under Tiěshān Qióng 鐵山瓊, Měngshān Yì 蒙山異 at Xiūxiūān 休休菴, [[淨伏]] Hǔyán Jìngfú at Jìngshān (as 綱維 for a year), Dōngyán Rì 東巖日 at Āyùwáng, and Yěwēng Tóng 野翁同 at Xuědòu; attained awakening under Dézhēn at Língyǐn on the 庭前柏樹子 *huàtóu* and received formal *yìnkě*.

He held five major abbacies — Jiànkāng Chóngyīn chánsì 崇因禪寺 (from Dàdé 9 / 1305, fifteen years), Jiǎngshān Tàipíng Xīngguó 蔣山太平興國 (from Zhìzhì 1 / 1321), the newly-founded Jiǎngshān Dàchóngxǐ Wànshòu 大崇禧萬壽寺 (established in Tàidìng 3 / 1326 on his recommendation, after a visit by the future Wénzōng 文宗 to the Bǎozhū peak 寶珠峰), Jìngshān Xīngshèng Wànshòu 徑山興聖萬壽 (from Zhìzhèng 2 / 1342, as the 49th abbot), and finally Dàlóngxiáng Jíqìngsì 大龍翔集慶寺 (from Zhìzhèng 5 / 1345). The five-abbacy span gave his *yǔlù* its alternate title *Wǔhuì lù* 五會錄 ([[KR6q0343]]). His imperial titles accumulated in parallel: Fóhǎi Pǔyìn dàchánshī 佛海普印大禪師 (1328, from Wénzōng on accession), Dàzhōng dàfū Guǎngcí Yuánwù dàchánshī 大中大夫廣慈圓悟大禪師 (1329), a gold-brocade *jiāshā* at the Dàmíngdiàn in 1331, and continuing patronage from Shùndì and the Báiyán 伯顏 / Tuōtuō 脫脫 governments into the Zhìzhèng era.

Per the *tǎmíng* of Ōuyáng Xuán 歐陽玄 (Zhìzhèng 9 / 1349), his named dharma-heirs include [[繼祖]] Jìzǔ of Chángzhōu Shànquán, Lánjiāng Qīngchǔ 蘭江清濋 (DILA A020031), Shòuyán Chūn 壽巖椿, Xīyuán Dá 西源達 (DILA A009716), Yuánzōng Xìng 圓宗性, and Xīyǐn Zǔ 西隱祖. Principal sources: the biographical apparatus appended to [[KR6q0343]] (Kèxīn's *xíngyèjì* and Ōuyáng Xuán's *tǎmíng*); *Zēngjí Xùchuándēnglù* *juan* 6; *Xùdēng zhèngtǒng* *juan* 24; *NánSòng YuánMíng chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* *juan* 13; *Jìngshān zhì* (identifies him as the 49th-generation 列祖 of Jìngshān).
