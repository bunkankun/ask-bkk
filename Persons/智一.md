Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; *zì* **Xuě-dùn** 雪墩 (also referenced as **Zhì-yī Xuě-dùn** 智一雪墩). DILA Authority A001237. Lifedates unrecorded; died at age 73. Took ordination at age nine; became dharma-disciple of [[佛閑]] **Fó-xián Xù-yī héshàng** 佛閑勗伊和尚 of Pǔ-dé-sì 普德寺 in Nán-jīng. At forty he stationed his staff at the **Jǐ-míng-sì** 鳮鳴寺 ancestral garden; subsequently took residence at Pǔ-dé-sì where he lectured on the *Śūraṃgama*, the *Vimalakīrti*, and other major sūtras.

Editorial role: provided *shí-yí* 拾遺 ("gathering of remnants" / supplementary editing) and *chóng-dìng* 重訂 ("re-redaction") on his master Fó-xián's three-text Lotus Sūtra apparatus ([[KR6d0092]], [[KR6d0093]], [[KR6d0094]]); his preface to the front-matter is dated Kāngxī *yǐ-yǒu* 康熙乙酉 (= 1705 CE). Two named dharma-disciples: **Dǔ-chū** 篤初 and **Kǔ-xíng** 苦行.

Note: distinct from the Liú-Sòng *gǎn-tōng* monk **Zhì-yī** of Línglóng-sì in Qián-táng (DILA A007466), who is the celebrated "white-ape Brahma-call" (白猿梵召) hagiographical figure of the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn*; and from the late-Míng *Niàn-chún Zhì-yī* 念純智一 (1587–1659).

Sources: *Fómíng* 1014; *Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí xuán-tán juàn-shǒu* preface (1705); DILA A001237.
