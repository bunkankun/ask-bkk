Mídì 彌第 (lifedates unknown), king of the Central-Asian polity of Qiánbù 前部國 (the Turfan-region Anterior Jūshī 車師 kingdom) at the time when [[因提麗|Yīntílì 因提麗]] introduced [[婆素跋陀|Vasubhadra]]'s *Tridharmaka* abstract to the country. According to [[道安|Dàoān]]'s preface to [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (T1505), Mídì sought out the concealed text and committed it to memory, after which it began to circulate. No further biographical record survives.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (named in the transmission account).
