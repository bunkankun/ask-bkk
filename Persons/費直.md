# Fèi Zhí 費直

*Zì* Zhǎngwēng 長翁. Western Hàn 西漢 *Yì* master, native of Dōnglái 東萊 (modern Shāndōng). Founder of the "Fèi school" *Yì* — an *ancient-script* (*gǔwén* 古文) line of transmission that stood apart from the four orthodox imperial-academy schools ([[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ, Shī Chóu 施讎, Liáng Qiū Hè 梁丘賀, [[京房]] Jīng Fáng). The Fèi *Yì* used the Wings (*shíyì* 十翼) to interpret the canonical text rather than relying on the *xiàngshù* 象數 calendrical correlations of the Mèng-Jīng school. The Fèi tradition was continued by Chén Yuán 陳元, [[馬融]] Mǎ Róng, and [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán, and through them was the principal line that fed into [[王弼]] Wáng Bì's *yìlǐ* 義理 reading. The original commentary is lost; *Hànshū* (juan 88, *Rúlín zhuàn*) gives him a brief notice.
