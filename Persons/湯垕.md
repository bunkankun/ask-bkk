Early Yuán painting connoisseur; *zì* Jūnzài 君載, *hào* Cǎizhēnzǐ 采真子, of DōngChǔ 東楚 (the Huái river region). Active in *Yánjīng* 燕京 (Khanbaliq, the Yuán capital) in the 1320s; contemporary and friend of [[柯九思]] (Kē Jiǔsī, *Jiànhuà bóshì* under Wénzōng from *Tiānlì* 1 = 1328). Author of [[KR3h0037]] *Huàjiàn* 畫鑒, the principal Yuán connoisseur's manual of painting (the surviving text in fact a posthumous re-editing by an unnamed hand, as the Sìkù *tíyào* establishes). The work's theoretical position — that painting connoisseurship begins from *qìyùn* 氣韻 and not from textual evidential investigation — represents the early-Yuán literati-school refinement of Northern Sòng connoisseurship in the line of Mǐ Fú [[KR3h0021]].
