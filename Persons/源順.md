**Minamoto no Shitagō** 源順 (Chinese: Yuán Shùn, 911–983), mid-Heian Japanese scholar, poet, and lexicographer. Best known for the *Wamyō ruijushō* 和名類聚抄 (c. 938), the foundational Sino-Japanese terminological encyclopaedia organising vocabulary by semantic category — a general-purpose counterpart to the slightly earlier and pharmacologically focused *Honzō wamyō* 本草和名 ([[KR3ec072]]) of [[深江輔仁]] Fukae Sukehito. Japanese scholar; not in CBDB.
