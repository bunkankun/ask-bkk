# Zōngzhuó 宗卓

Sōtaku 拙孫宗卓 ("the clumsy grandson Sōtaku"), Japanese dharma-descendant in the line of Xūtáng Zhìyú via Nānpo Jōmyō; in Shōwa 3 (1313), at Ryūshō-ji 龍翔寺, he gathered further fragments and re-cut the blocks of the *Xūtáng héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0065]]), producing the Japanese editorial layer preserved in the Taishō witness.
