Dáwèi 達位, *zì* **Shànquán** 善權 ("Skillful Means"); often referred to as **Sōngkuī Shànquán** 松巋善權. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo** 月幢徹了 (1614–1667). Born **明萬曆戊午九月廿三日午時** = **1619-10-29** (the catalog era says 戊午 = 1618 but that doesn't match Wànlì 47; the calendar reconciliation gives 1619); died **康熙甲子仲秋廿三** = **1684-10-01**, age 67, *sēnglà* 45. Native of **Chǔběi** 楚北 (Húběi); lay surname **Qú** 瞿.

**Formation.** From birth attracted to Buddhist images; on hearing the *Lotus Sūtra* line "*yǒu zhì ruò wén zé néng xìnjiě*" 有智若聞則能信解 (those with wisdom on hearing can understand by faith), the urge to leave the world arose. Amid the dynastic chaos abandoned home and went to Guìzhōu — at **Ānshùn Guānyīndòng** 安順觀音硐 took tonsure and *shāmí* precepts under **Tàixū** 太虛和尚. Two years later traveled south to **Hánguāng** 含光和尚 for full ordination, given the *fùmǔ wèi shēng qián* 父母未生前 *gōngàn*. Then to **Yuèchuáng** 月幢和尚 — one day during *jīngxíng*, fell asleep and collapsed; on awaking felt his body relax horizontally, and his doubt-cluster melted "like setting down a hundred-*jīn* load." Reported to Yuèchuáng and received final sealing.

**Abbacies (three).** **Tàipíng Wànshòu** 太平萬壽; **Sōngkuī Pǔguāngsì** 松巋普光寺 (the principal seat); **Cíyún Pǔxiángsì** 慈雲普祥寺.

**Disciples.** Ten *fǎsì* including **Shànyī Rúchún** [[如純]] (cf. [[KR6q0580]]), **Tiānyī Dàyuè** [[大悅]] (cf. [[KR6q0582]]), **Pǔdù Dàhǎi** 普渡大海, **Xūé Dàzhào** 虗峨大照. Plus five lay-disciples including **Wáng Qīnghuá** 王青華, **Lóng Fúshì** 龍孚世.

**Work.** [[KR6q0581]] 《善權位禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B469, compiled by *fǎsì* **Tiānyī Dàyuè** [[大悅]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《善權位禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈塔銘〉, 〈行實〉, 〈分燈〉.
