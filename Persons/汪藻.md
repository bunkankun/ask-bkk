**Wāng Zǎo** 汪藻 (1079–1154), *zì* Yànzhāng 彥章, *hào* Fúxī xiānshēng 浮溪先生, of Ráozhōu 饒州 Déxīng 德興 (Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì*. The cardinal early-Southern-Sòng *címìng* (court drafter) — celebrated as the *zhōngxìng címìng dìyī* (chief court-drafter of the dynasty's resurgence). Compared by contemporaries to Lù Zhì 陸贄 of the Táng. Drafted the *Yuányòu tàihòu shǒushū* (handwritten edict of *Yuányòu* Empress-Dowager) — establishing Gāozōng's accession; the *Sòng Qíyú zécí* (rebuke of Sòng Qíyú); and the *Zhāng Bāngchāng zécí* (rebuke of Zhāng Bāngchāng) — all canonical early-Southern-Sòng political documents in formal-prose preserved through Hóng Mài 洪邁's citation. Author of *Fúxī jí* 浮溪集 [[KR4d0145]] originally in 60 *juǎn*; *Sìkù* reconstruction is 32 *juǎn*. The *Fúxī wéncuì* 浮溪文粹 [[KR4d0146]] is a Míng-period *zhāicí* (extracts) compiled by Hú Yáochén 胡堯臣 from the lost original. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 445 (Wényuán). Lifedates 1079–1154 confirmed.
