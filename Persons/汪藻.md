**Wāng Zǎo** 汪藻 (1079–1154), *zì* Yàn-zhāng 彥章, *hào* Fú-xī xiānshēng 浮溪先生, of Ráo-zhōu 饒州 Dé-xīng 德興 (Jiāng-xī). *Jìnshì*. The cardinal early-Southern-Sòng *cí-mìng* (court drafter) — celebrated as the *zhōng-xìng cí-mìng dì-yī* (chief court-drafter of the dynasty's resurgence). Compared by contemporaries to Lù Zhì 陸贄 of the Táng. Drafted the *Yuán-yòu tài-hòu shǒu-shū* (handwritten edict of *Yuán-yòu* Empress-Dowager) — establishing Gāozōng's accession; the *Sòng Qí-yú zé-cí* (rebuke of Sòng Qí-yú); and the *Zhāng Bāng-chāng zé-cí* (rebuke of Zhāng Bāng-chāng) — all canonical early-Southern-Sòng political documents in formal-prose preserved through Hóng Mài 洪邁's citation. Author of *Fú-xī jí* 浮溪集 [[KR4d0145]] originally in 60 *juǎn*; *Sìkù* reconstruction is 32 *juǎn*. The *Fú-xī wén-cuì* 浮溪文粹 [[KR4d0146]] is a Míng-period *zhāi-cí* (extracts) compiled by Hú Yáo-chén 胡堯臣 from the lost original. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 445 (Wén-yuán). Lifedates 1079–1154 confirmed.
