Lǐ Qǐ 李杞 (lifedates not securely recorded; fl. 1203, per the firmly-fixed auto-preface date of his *Yòng Yì xiáng jiě*), *zì* Zǐ-cái 子才, *hào* Qiān-zhāi 謙齋, of Méi-shān 眉山 in Sìchuān (modern Méi-shān prefecture, central Sìchuān).

Distinct from two other Sòng-period *Lǐ Qǐ*'s (per the Sìkù *tiyao*'s explicit disambiguation):
- The Northern-Sòng *Lǐ Qǐ* (CBDB id 981, d. 1080), *Dà-lǐ-sì chéng* and Sū Shì *Wū-tái shī àn* circle figure.
- The Zhū-Xī disciple *Lǐ Qǐ* (*zì* Liáng-zhòng 良仲, of Píng-jiāng), recorder of the *Jiǎ-yín wèn-dá* 甲寅問答.

CBDB has multiple Sòng-period *Lǐ Qǐ* entries without dates (id 40866, 40869, 40874, 478059); the present author is most plausibly id 40866 (or 40869), both Sòng-period without further detail.

Career-detail unknown beyond the auto-preface's signature *Qiān-zhāi jūshì* 謙齋居士 ("Layman Qiān-zhāi"), suggesting a non-office-holding scholar status, possibly a member of the local Méi-shān gentry.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0059]] *Yòng Yì xiáng jiě* 用易詳解 (originally 20 juan; recovered by the Sìkù editors from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* citations as 16 juan, with 7 hexagrams' material plus 4 lines of *Jìn* missing). Composed in 1203 (*Jiā-tài guǐ-hài*, 6th-month full-moon-day).

Methodologically distinctive for combining (a) the historical-case method that runs from Zhèng Xuán through Lǐ Guāng to Yáng Wànlǐ ([[KR1a0040]]) — citing concrete historical events for each line-statement — with (b) extensive *Lǎo-Zhuāng* citation, continuing the *Yì-Lǎo* synthesis line of Yè Mèng-dé and Chéng Dàchāng. Title invokes Wáng Tōng (*Wén-zhōng-zǐ*)'s formula "*the* Yì *is the sage's coming-into-action.*"
