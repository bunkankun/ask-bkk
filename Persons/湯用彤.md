# 湯用彤

Tāng Yòngtóng (1893–1964), modern Chinese historian of Chinese Buddhism and philosophy. Author of *Hàn-Wèi Liǎngjìn Nánběicháo Fójiào shǐ* 漢魏兩晉南北朝佛教史 (1938), a foundational history of pre-Táng Chinese Buddhism.
