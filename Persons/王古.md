Northern-Sòng official and lay Buddhist literatus, *zì* Mǐnzhòng 敏仲, of *Dōngdū* 東都 (Kāifēng) family. *Jìnshì* of *Jiāyòu* / *Xīníng*; entered court as *shìcóng* 侍從 in *Yuányòu*. His political career was tied to Sū Shì 蘇軾 and the *Yuányòu* faction; held *Lǐbù shìláng*, *Hùbù shìláng*; demoted under the *Shàoshèng* anti-*Yuányòu* purges to a southern post and died in exile. CBDB id 1840 (no birth/death year); fl. ca. 1080–1100. A leading figure in the late-Northern-Sòng lay Pure-Land Buddhist circle: edited the *Dàoyuàn jí yào* [[KR4d0008]] from his predecessor Cháo Jiǒng's [[晁迥]] *Chán*-syncretist notebooks, and authored the *Zhízhǐ jìngtǔ juéyí jí* 直指淨土決疑集 — a foundational Northern-Sòng Pure-Land lay text. Ally of Yáng Jié 楊傑 [[楊傑]] in the lay Pure-Land devotional movement.
