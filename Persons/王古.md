Northern-Sòng official and lay Buddhist literatus, *zì* Mǐn-zhòng 敏仲, of *Dōng-dū* 東都 (Kāi-fēng) family. *Jìn-shì* of *Jiā-yòu* / *Xī-níng*; entered court as *shì-cóng* 侍從 in *Yuán-yòu*. His political career was tied to Sū Shì 蘇軾 and the *Yuán-yòu* faction; held *Lǐ-bù shì-láng*, *Hù-bù shì-láng*; demoted under the *Shào-shèng* anti-*Yuán-yòu* purges to a southern post and died in exile. CBDB id 1840 (no birth/death year); fl. ca. 1080–1100. A leading figure in the late-Northern-Sòng lay Pure-Land Buddhist circle: edited the *Dào-yuàn jí yào* [[KR4d0008]] from his predecessor Cháo Jiǒng's [[晁迥]] *Chán*-syncretist notebooks, and authored the *Zhí-zhǐ jìng-tǔ jué-yí jí* 直指淨土決疑集 — a foundational Northern-Sòng Pure-Land lay text. Ally of Yáng Jié 楊傑 [[楊傑]] in the lay Pure-Land devotional movement.
