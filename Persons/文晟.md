# Wénshèng 文晟

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[梵琦]] Chǔshí Fànqí (1296–1370); co-editor of his master's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
