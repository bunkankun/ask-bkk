Fù Sōng-qīng 傅崧卿 (fl. 1111–1130s), *zì* Zǐ-jùn 子駿, native of Shān-yīn 山陰 (Yuè-zhōu 越州, modern Shào-xīng, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of the late Northern Sòng (Yuán-yòu / Zhèng-hé period); reached *Jǐ-shì-zhōng* 給事中 (Supervising Censor). His principal scholarly work is the *Xià xiǎo zhèng Dàishì zhuàn* 夏小正戴氏傳 [[KR1d0077]] in 4 juan — the foundational Sòng monograph on the *Xià xiǎo zhèng* chapter of the *Dà-Dài Lǐjì*, which separated ancient *jīng* core from later *zhuàn* commentary on the model of Dù Yù's *Chūnqiū-Zuǒ-zhuàn* edition. The work was the basis for Zhū Xī's [[KR1d0085]] subsequent re-editing of the text and for Jīn Lǚ-xiáng's *Tōng-jiàn qián-biān* annotation. CBDB id 503389; precise dates not preserved.
