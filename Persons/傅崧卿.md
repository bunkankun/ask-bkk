Fù Sōngqīng 傅崧卿 (fl. 1111–1130s), *zì* Zǐjùn 子駿, native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Yuèzhōu 越州, modern Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of the late Northern Sòng (Yuányòu / Zhènghé period); reached *Jǐshìzhōng* 給事中 (Supervising Censor). His principal scholarly work is the *Xià xiǎo zhèng Dàishì zhuàn* 夏小正戴氏傳 [[KR1d0077]] in 4 juan — the foundational Sòng monograph on the *Xià xiǎo zhèng* chapter of the *DàDài Lǐjì*, which separated ancient *jīng* core from later *zhuàn* commentary on the model of Dù Yù's *ChūnqiūZuǒzhuàn* edition. The work was the basis for Zhū Xī's [[KR1d0085]] subsequent re-editing of the text and for Jīn Lǚxiáng's *Tōngjiàn qiánbiān* annotation. CBDB id 503389; precise dates not preserved.
