Late-Míng Buddhist layman of **Sān-shān** 三山 (Fú-zhōu, Fú-jiàn), sobriquet **Hè-xuān** 鶴軒 (Crane-Pavilion). Lifedates unrecorded; only certain literary act is the *jí-jiě* compilation underlying *Jīn-gāng jīng bǔ-zhù* 金剛經補註 ([[KR6c0057]], X24 no. 469), a two-juan annotated *Vajracchedikā* commentary completed for circulation by **天啟 *bǐng-yín* 丙寅 = 1626** (per the preface of **Zhā Yìng-guāng** 查應光). Hán's contribution is the *jí-jiě* layer (gathered explanations) which **Chéng Zhōng-mào** [[程衷懋]] subsequently expanded with *bǔ-zhù* (supplementary annotation).
