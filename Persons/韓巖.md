Late-Míng Buddhist layman of **Sānshān** 三山 (Fúzhōu, Fújiàn), sobriquet **Hèxuān** 鶴軒 (Crane-Pavilion). Lifedates unrecorded; only certain literary act is the *jíjiě* compilation underlying *Jīngāng jīng bǔzhù* 金剛經補註 ([[KR6c0057]], X24 no. 469), a two-juan annotated *Vajracchedikā* commentary completed for circulation by **天啟 *bǐngyín* 丙寅 = 1626** (per the preface of **Zhā Yìngguāng** 查應光). Hán's contribution is the *jíjiě* layer (gathered explanations) which **Chéng Zhōngmào** [[程衷懋]] subsequently expanded with *bǔzhù* (supplementary annotation).
