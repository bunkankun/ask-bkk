Zhāng Zhòngshēn 張仲深 (Yuán; the *zhòngshēn* is the given name, *zǐyuān* the *zì*). The *Sìkù tíyào* deduces the identity from the *Suìjìn shī* line "*zhào wǒ xiāngguān mèng — xiāngsuí dào Màochéng*" (Màochéng being the old name for Yínxiàn) + Nàyán [[納延]]'s *Huái Míngzhōu Zhāng Zǐyuān* exchange-poem → therefore a Qìngyuánlù (Yínxiàn / Míngzhōu) man, *zì* Zǐyuān. Same poetic exchange-circle as Nàyán, Yáng Wéizhēn 楊維禎, Zhāng Yǔ [[張雨]], Wēi Sù 危素, Yuán Huá 袁華, Zhōu Huànwén 周煥文, Hán Xìng 韓性, the brothers Wū Běnliáng 烏本良 and Sīdào 斯道. The collection [[KR4d0535]] was lost; the *Sìkùguǎn* reconstructed it from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* into 6 *juǎn*. *Mùbài* with Mèi Zhòngshēn 暨仲深 (per *Tiěfǔ zhōng lián shī* small-note) — i.e., a homophone different person — is to be distinguished.
