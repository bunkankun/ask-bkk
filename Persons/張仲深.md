Zhāng Zhòng-shēn 張仲深 (Yuán; the *zhòng-shēn* is the given name, *zǐ-yuān* the *zì*). The *Sìkù tíyào* deduces the identity from the *Suì-jìn shī* line "*zhào wǒ xiāng-guān mèng — xiāng-suí dào Mào-chéng*" (Mào-chéng being the old name for Yín-xiàn) + Nà-yán [[納延]]'s *Huái Míng-zhōu Zhāng Zǐ-yuān* exchange-poem → therefore a Qìng-yuán-lù (Yín-xiàn / Míng-zhōu) man, *zì* Zǐ-yuān. Same poetic exchange-circle as Nà-yán, Yáng Wéi-zhēn 楊維禎, Zhāng Yǔ [[張雨]], Wēi Sù 危素, Yuán Huá 袁華, Zhōu Huàn-wén 周煥文, Hán Xìng 韓性, the brothers Wū Běn-liáng 烏本良 and Sī-dào 斯道. The collection [[KR4d0535]] was lost; the *Sìkù-guǎn* reconstructed it from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* into 6 *juǎn*. *Mù-bài* with Mèi Zhòng-shēn 暨仲深 (per *Tiě-fǔ zhōng lián shī* small-note) — i.e., a homophone different person — is to be distinguished.
