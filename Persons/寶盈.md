# Bǎo-yíng 寶盈

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yú-ān Zhì-jí (1311–1378); *hào* Xùn-zhōng 巽中 (the name appears in *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 1 as "Xùn-zhōng Bǎo-yíng *zàng-zhǔ*" 巽中寶盈藏主). Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
