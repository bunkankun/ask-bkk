# Bǎoyíng 寶盈

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yúān Zhìjí (1311–1378); *hào* Xùnzhōng 巽中 (the name appears in *Zēngjí Xùchuándēnglù* *juan* 1 as "Xùnzhōng Bǎoyíng *zàngzhǔ*" 巽中寶盈藏主). Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
