**Akiyoshi Tadashi** 秋吉質 (Edo-period, dates undetermined), Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician. Author of *Wēnyì lùn shīpíng* / *On'eki ron shihyō* 溫疫論私評 ([[KR3eg022]]), a commentary on Wú Yǒuxìng's *Wēnyì lùn* ([[KR3eg004]]). The text was transmitted to China and entered the late-Qīng *Huáng Hàn yīxué cóngshū* 皇漢醫學叢書 channel (1936); it is one of the substantial Edo-period Japanese contributions to the *wēnyì* commentary tradition. No detailed biographical material located.
