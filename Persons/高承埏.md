# Gāo Chéngyán 高承埏

Late-Míng official and lay Buddhist practitioner. *Jìnshì* of Chóngzhēn era. Died in the Míng-Qīng transition (lifedates 1601–1645). Substantial lay patron of late-Míng Buddhist projects.

Supplementary editor (補) of the *Bāshíbā zǔ dàoyǐng zhuàn zàn* 八十八祖道影傳贊 ([[KR6q0045]]), adding patriarchal entries to Hānshān Déqīng 憨山德清's original compilation.
