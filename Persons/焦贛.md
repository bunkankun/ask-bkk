**Jiāo Gòng** 焦贛 (also **Jiāo Yánshòu** 焦延壽) was a Western-Hàn *Yì jīng* specialist, traditional author of the ***Yì lín*** 易林 ([[KR5h0044]]) — a vast Hàn *Yì*-based divination manual giving 4,096 four-line divinatory verses for every pair of hexagrams. Teacher of Jīng Fáng 京房.
