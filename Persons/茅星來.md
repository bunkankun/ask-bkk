Máo Xīnglái 茅星來 (1678–1748), *zì* Qǐsù 豈宿, was an early-Qīng Confucian classicist of Guīān 歸安 (Húzhōu 湖州, Zhèjiāng). Best known for the *Jìnsī lù jízhù* 近思錄集註 ([[KR3a0043]]), the Qīng-period replacement for Yè Cǎi's Sòng-period *jíjiě* on the *Jìnsī lù*. The work was composed under his father's instruction, completed in Kāngxī *xīnchǒu* (1721), and supplemented by a second preface in Qiánlóng *bǐngchén* (1736) — fifteen years after the body of the work — making it the labour of his lifetime. He is methodologically distinctive among early-Qīng *Lǐxué* commentators for arguing (in his *hòuxù*) that the practice of post-Sòng *Lǐxué* must include the HànTáng glossographic-classical tradition (Mǎ Róng, Zhèng Xuán, Jiǎ Yì, Kǒng Yǐngdá) — a position that distinguishes him from purist *Lǐxué* loyalists.
