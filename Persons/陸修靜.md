Lù Xiūjìng 陸修靜 (*zì* Yuándé 元德; posthumous sobriquet Jiǎnjì xiānshēng 簡寂先生), native of Dōngqiān 東遷 in Wúxīng 吳興 (modern Zhèjiāng), was the most consequential Daoist systematiser of the Liú-Sòng 劉宋 (420–479) dynasty. A scion of the Wú-Zhèjiāng gentry, he studied widely in both the Shàngqīng 上清 and Língbǎo 靈寶 traditions before withdrawing as a recluse to Lúshān 廬山, where he occupied the Jiǎnjì guǎn 簡寂觀.

Lù received imperial patronage from the Liú-Sòng emperor Míng 明帝 (r. 465–472), who constructed the Chóngxū guǎn 崇虛館 at the capital Jiànkāng for him and convened at court a celebrated dispute with the Buddhist Tánzàn 曇贊. Lù's lasting contributions are:

- **The Daoist canon.** His *Sāndòng jīngshū mùlù* 三洞經書目錄, presented to the throne in 471, first organised the received corpus into the three sections (*sāndòng* 三洞) that have structured the *Dàozàng* ever since. This followed on his earlier 437 *Língbǎo jīngmù* 靈寶經目 cataloguing the new Língbǎo revelations.
- **The Língbǎo Retreat liturgy.** He codified the nine-fold system of Língbǎo Retreats (*zhāi* 齋) and left a dozen liturgical works, including the *Fǎzhú jīng* [[KR5b0033]] (DZ 349), *Guāng zhújiè fǎdēng zhūyuàn yí* (DZ 524), *Wǔgǎn wén* 五感文 (DZ 1278), and the *Dàomén kēlüè* 道門科略.
- **The Celestial Master reform.** His *Lùjiā dàomén kēlüè* 陸家道門科略 reorganised the post-Northern-Liáng rump of the Zhèngyī 正一 (Celestial Master) tradition for southern conditions.

Lù's vocabulary and classificatory schemata — *sāndòng* 三洞, the nine *zhāi*, the integrated *sùqǐ* 宿啟 / *yánqǐ* 言啟 preparatory rites — became normative for all subsequent medieval and late-imperial Daoist liturgy. No CBDB record exists (CBDB coverage of pre-Táng Daoist clerics is sparse); lifedates are from the *Sòngshū* 宋書 and *Mào shān zhì* 茅山志 traditions and are uncontested in modern scholarship. Standard studies: Ōfuchi Ninji, *Dōkyōshi no kenkyū* (1964); Fukui Kōjun, *Dōkyō no kisoteki kenkyū* (1952); Kobayashi Masayoshi, *Rikuchō dōkyōshi kenkyū* (1990).
