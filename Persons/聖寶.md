# 聖寶

Shōbō (Chinese: Shèngbǎo, 832–909), early-Heian Sanron-Esoteric master, founder of Daigo-ji 醍醐寺 in 874. Second figure in the transmission lineage of [[願曉]] Gangyō's *Konkōmyō saishōōkyō genzū* ([[KR6i0318]]).
