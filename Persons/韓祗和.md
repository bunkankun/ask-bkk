**Hán Zhīhé** 韓祗和 (*fl.* Yuányòu reign, 1086, 北宋), Northern-Sòng specialist on cold-damage (*Shānghán*) medicine and author of the ***Shānghán wēizhǐ lùn*** 傷寒微旨論 ([[KR3e0025]]) — the principal Sòng-period theoretical-clinical treatise on the *Shānghán lùn*. The original work's preface (lost in independent transmission) was dated by Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí* to Yuányòu bǐngyín 元祐丙寅 (1086), placing Hán in the reign of Sòng Zhézōng. The *Sòng shǐ Fāngjì zhuàn* does not preserve his career-record. The Yuán physician Dài Liáng's *Jiǔ líng shānfáng jí* names him as one of the *Shānghán*-line transmitters from Zhāng Jī through Wáng Shūhé, Chéng Wúyǐ, Páng Ānshí, Zhū Hóng, Xǔ Shūwēi, Hán Zhīhé, and Wáng Bīnzhī — confirming his standing as a major Sòng-period *Shānghán* specialist.
