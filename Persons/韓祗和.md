**Hán Zhīhé** 韓祗和 (*fl.* Yuán-yòu reign, 1086, 北宋), Northern-Sòng specialist on cold-damage (*Shānghán*) medicine and author of the ***Shānghán wēi-zhǐ lùn*** 傷寒微旨論 ([[KR3e0025]]) — the principal Sòng-period theoretical-clinical treatise on the *Shānghán lùn*. The original work's preface (lost in independent transmission) was dated by Chén Zhèn-sūn's *Shū-lù jiětí* to Yuán-yòu bǐng-yín 元祐丙寅 (1086), placing Hán in the reign of Sòng Zhézōng. The *Sòng shǐ Fāng-jì zhuàn* does not preserve his career-record. The Yuán physician Dài Liáng's *Jiǔ líng shānfáng jí* names him as one of the *Shānghán*-line transmitters from Zhāng Jī through Wáng Shū-hé, Chéng Wúyǐ, Páng Ān-shí, Zhū Hóng, Xǔ Shū-wēi, Hán Zhīhé, and Wáng Bīn-zhī — confirming his standing as a major Sòng-period *Shānghán* specialist.
