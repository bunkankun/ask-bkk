Xiè Dàochéng 謝道承 (b. unknown, d. Qiánlóng 6 = 1741), *zì* Yòushào 又紹, *hào* Gǔméi 古梅, native of Mǐnxiàn 閩縣 (Fúzhōu prefecture, modern Fúzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 60 (1721); entered the Hanlin and rose to *biānxiū* 編修 and *shìjiǎngxuéshì* 侍講學士. A leading Fújiàn literatus of the early Qiánlóng era, recognised for his poetry; principal *biānzuǎn* of the *Fújiàn tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0045]]) under Hǎo Yùlín. His own *Xiǎo lántíng zhái jí* 小蘭亭齋集 (8 *juan*) survives. CBDB id 90620; death year per *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú niánbiǎo* (#23577); native place index_addr 7770 (Mǐnxiàn) confirmed.
