**Dúgū Tāo** 獨孤滔 was a mid-Táng Daoist alchemist of non-Hàn (probably Xiānbēi) ancestry — *Dúgū* 獨孤 being a well-known Xiānbēi clan name. Author of [[KR5c0325]] *Dān fāng jiàn yuán* 丹方鑑源 ("Mirror of the Sources of Elixir Recipes"), an alchemical-pharmacological lexicon dated 836 (Kāi chéng 1, under Táng Wén zōng).
