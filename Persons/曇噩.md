# Tán-è 曇噩

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Wú-mèng 無夢 (also 覺夢), *hào* Mèng-táng 夢堂 (also 酉庵 Yǒu-ān); imperial title Fó-zhēn Wén-yì chánshī 佛真文懿禪師 (conferred by Yuán Shùndì). Native of Cí-xī 慈溪 (Míng-zhōu), lay surname Wáng 王; 1285 – 14 March 1373 (lunar 洪武 6.2.12). Contemporary with [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí (also Míng-zhōu-born; both trained under [[行端]] Yuán-sǒu Xíng-duān).

Widely read in the hundred schools from childhood; his discovery of the *Platform Sūtra* at the Yán-qìng lecture-hall prompted him to seek ordination under Liáng Shàng-rén 良上人 of Fèng-huà Guǎng-fǎ-yuàn 奉化廣法院. At twenty-three received the full precepts at Cháng-lú from Xuě-tíng Chuán 雪庭傳; attained awakening at Líng-yǐn / Zhōng-tiān-zhú under Xíng-duān and was named his dharma-heir. Served as *sī-jì* 司記 at Jìng-shān. From Zhì-yuán 5 (1339) held abbacies at Sì-míng Xián-shèng-sì 四明咸聖寺, Cí-xī Kāi-shòu-sì 開壽寺, Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì 天台國清寺, Xiàng-shān Ruì-lóng-yuàn 瑞龍院 — where at near seventy his "hearing and vision were still sharp and he expounded the school's doctrine with vigour." In Zhì-zhèng 17 (1357) founded the Ruì-guāng-yuàn 瑞光院. In Hóng-wǔ 3 (1370) the newly-enthroned Míng emperor held audience with Buddhist elders and placed Tán-è at the head; from there he became abbot of Nán-jīng Tiān-jiè-sì 天界寺.

Co-editor of Xíng-duān's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0352]]). His own most consequential work is the 30-juan **Xīn-xiū kē-fēn liù-xué sēng-zhuàn** 新修科分六學僧傳 — a late-imperial re-organization of the Buddhist biographical tradition under the rubric of the six disciplines, surviving as X77 no. 1522 and a major source for Chinese Buddhist historiography. He also compiled the **《定應大師布袋和尚傳》** *Dìng-yìng dà-shī Bù-dài hé-shàng zhuàn* ([[KR6r0107]], X1597, 2 juan) — the principal Yuán-Míng-transition source on the canonical Maitreya-incarnation Bù-dài 布袋. Died aged 89, *sēng-là* unrecorded; stupa at Guó-qīng-sì. Named dharma-heirs include Zhì-yán 智巖 and Jìng-shān Dài-zōng Xīn-tài 徑山岱宗心泰.
