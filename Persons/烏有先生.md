"Wūyǒu Xiānsheng" 烏有先生 (Mr. Nobody) is the pen name used by the anonymous Qīng-dynasty author of [[KR4k0263]] *Xiù Xié Jì* 繡鞋記. The name is a literary allusion to the fictional interlocutor "Wūyǒu Xiānsheng" in Sīmǎ Xiāngrú's 司馬相如 *Zǐxū Fù* 子虛賦 (a figure whose name literally means "there is no such person"), signalling deliberate authorial anonymity in the classical tradition. No historical individual behind the pseudonym has been identified.
