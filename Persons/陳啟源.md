# Chén Qǐyuán 陳啟源

*Zì* Chángfā 長發. Native of Wújiāng 吳江 (modern Jiāngsū). CBDB index year 1626; death year 1689. Senior fellow-townsman and lifelong scholarly collaborator of Zhū Hè-líng 朱鶴齡 ([[朱鶴齡]]).

Author of:

- *Máo Shī jīgǔ biān* 毛詩稽古編 in 30 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0049]]) — completed *Kāngxī* 26, *dīng-mǎo* (1687) over fourteen years and three drafts, with a preface by his pupil Zhào Jiājī 趙嘉稷 dated *Kāngxī* 40, *xīn-sì* (1701). The work is the principal early-Qīng systematic *Hàn-xué* (Hàn-school) reading of the *Shī jīng*: *xùngǔ* per *Ěryǎ*, *piān-yì* per *xiǎo xù*, *quānjīng-zhǐ* per *Máo zhuàn* with *Zhèng jiān* as auxiliary, *míngwù* per Lù Jī's *Cǎo mù chóng yú shū*. The work systematically attacks the Sòng-school readings, principally targeting Liú Jǐn's *Shī zhuàn tōngshì* ([[KR1c0028]]) and Fǔ Guǎng's *Shī tóngzǐ wèn* ([[KR1c0021]]).

His name is sometimes recorded as *Chén Qǐ-yuán* with the *yuán* character 源, sometimes 嫄 or other variants in older catalogs; the standard form is 啟源 / 啟源.
