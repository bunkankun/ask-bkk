**Jitsugu** 實弘 — Medieval Japanese **Kegon** 華嚴 (Avataṃsaka) monk, identified only through his sole canonical work, *Huā-yán-zōng dà-yào chāo* 華嚴宗大要抄 ([[KR6t0030]], T72n2334), a single-fascicle schematic reference-manual covering the principal Kegon numerical schemes — the ten Buddha-bodies, six characteristics, ten profound doors, and ten meanings. The work's reliance on Fa-zang's *Wǔ jiào zhāng* and Chéng-guān's *Avataṃsaka* commentary places him in the mainstream medieval Tōdaiji-Kegon scholastic curriculum.
