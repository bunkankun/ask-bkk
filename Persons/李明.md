# 李明

Lǐ Míng, mid-Qīng colleague (同學兄) of [[黃庭鏡]] Huáng Tíngjìng who contributed a preface to [[KR3em006]] *Mùjīng dàchéng* 目經大成 noting Huáng's earlier engagement with classical *gǔwéncí* before his turn to medicine.

Note: 李明 is a very common name; this entry refers specifically to the Qīng prefacer of *Mùjīng dàchéng*. If a homonymous figure is later cataloged, append below a dotted divider.
