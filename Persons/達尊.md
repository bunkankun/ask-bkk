# Dá-zūn 達尊 (Shí-shuāng Ěr-zhān Dá-zūn 石霜爾瞻達尊)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, **the post-Míng-Qīng-transition restorer of the ancient Shí-shuāng-shān seat** 瀏陽石霜山 in Liú-yáng, Hú-nán — the Chán ancestral seat of **Shí-shuāng Qìng-zhū** 石霜慶諸 (807–888) and **Cí-míng Shí-shuāng Chǔ-yuán** 慈明石霜楚圓 (986–1039). *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Dá-zūn** 達尊, *hào* **Ěr-zhān** 爾瞻 (from a *Shī-jīng* allusion). Lay surname **Táng** 唐, of **Níng-bō Yín-xiàn** 寧波鄞縣. Lifedates **萬曆 36 – 康熙 2 癸卯 9月** = **1608 – October-November 1663**, age **56 *suì*** / *sēng-là* **31** per the Běn-xiù *tǎ-míng* at [[KR6q0420]] juan 2. Principal dharma-heir of **Dùn-sǒu Zhì-jì** 鈍叟智際 at **Dōng-míng Lǜ-luó-ān** 東明綠蘿菴 on Héng-shān — a lesser-documented Mìyún Yuán-wù sub-line master. Mù-chén Dào-mǐn's preface identifies Dá-zūn as "*Lóng-chí Huàn lǎo-rén xià xíng-zì fǎ-sì zhōng dì-yī qiáo-chǔ* 龍池幻老人下行字法嗣中第一翹楚" — first-rank among the **行-generation** dharma-heirs of **Huàn-yǒu Zhèng-chuán** 幻有正傳 (1549–1614, Mìyún's teacher).

**Life.** Family near **Pǔ-tuó-sì** 補陀寺 (Níng-bō). One day found an abandoned *pú-tuán* + *jiè-yī* set at the roadside; took it to **Fú-quán-shān** 福泉山 and requested tonsure from **Yuán-míng-shī** 圜明師. Served Yuán-míng three years. **Chóng-zhēn 乙亥 winter** (= **1635 winter**): received full precepts from **Mìyún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 at Tiān-tóng-sì at age 28. Subsequent consultation-tour: **Jīn-sù Chéng-gōng** 乘公, **Biàn-shān Xuě-gōng** 雪公, **Nán-jiàn Cí-wèn** 南澗慈問 (1580–1659). Final sealing at **Dōng-míng Lǜ-luó-ān** 東明綠蘿菴 by **Dùn-sǒu Zhì-jì** 際公 on the *Jiā-shān bō-chén jiàn Fó* 夾山撥塵見佛 *gōng-àn* and the subsequent *yuān-yāng xiù* 鴛鴦繡 transmission-verse. Five-year attendance at Héng-shān with Zhì-jì. **乙酉** 乙酉 (1645): Zhì-jì fled the transition-warfare to Tán-zhōu and died there; **Nán-yuán-shī** 南源師 brought the body back to Héng-yuè for burial.

**Shí-shuāng abbacy (1646–1663).** **順治 3 丙戌** (= **1646**): accepted invitation from **Liú-yáng Shí-shuāng Běn-huō** 瀏陽石霜本豁 and other patrons to take the Shí-shuāng abbacy. **18-year tenure** restoring the ancient *zǔ-tíng*, with a brief **1658** monastic-dispute withdrawal followed by a **1659** return. **1660**: restored Shí-shuāng Qìng-zhū's ancestor-pagoda and petitioned **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** 木陳道忞 at Tiān-tóng for the restoration-*jì* 記. **1663**: built his own pre-mortem *shòu-tǎ* 壽塔 adjacent to Qìng-zhū's ancestor-pagoda; illness followed immediately. Died sitting cross-legged at 亥時 on an unspecified night in the 9th lunar month of Kāng-xī 2 = October/November 1663. 7-day post-death *samādhi*; rain-cessation on cremation-day; ashes interred **10月9日** (= early November 1663).

**Historical significance.** Dá-zūn's 18-year Shí-shuāng restoration is the single most consequential Qīng-era revival of a Línjì-tradition *zǔ-tíng* seat in Hú-nán after the Zhāng-Xiàn-zhōng devastation. His parallels are [[珠|Dà-xiū Zhū]] at Cáo-xī Nán-huá (1654–56, at [[KR6q0413]]) and [[通醉|Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì]] at Chéng-dū Zhāo-jué (from 1663, at [[KR6q0415]]) — three near-simultaneous post-1644 **Chán *zǔ-tíng* restoration-programs** at three different historically-central sites.

Work: [[KR6q0420]] 《石霜爾瞻尊禪師語錄》 (2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J27 B200, compiled by *shì-zhě* [[本開]] Běn-kāi; preface by Lǐ Wén-yìn 1666-11 + preface by Mù-chén Dào-mǐn c. 1665–66 + *tǎ-míng* by dharma-brother [[本秀]] Běn-xiù dated 1666-04-04).

Sources: [[KR6q0420]] juan 2 *tǎ-míng* by **Běn-xiù** 本秀 (1666-04-04) and juan 1 prefaces by **Lǐ Wén-yìn** (1666-11) and **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** (c. 1665–66); *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書; *Shí-shuāng-sì zhì* 石霜寺志.

**Editorial role**: Lead compiler (*děng biān-cì*, with co-disciple [[達謙]] **Dá-qiān**) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0608]] 《山茨際禪師語錄》 4 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1649. Note that DILA's record of Tōng-jì's *fǎ-sì* lists "**爾瞻尊**" 爾瞻尊 — i.e. *zì* + *míng* — and the variant "**鈍叟智際**" used in the existing description above is a name-form variant for **山茨通際** (DILA primary form), with shared *bié-hào* **Dùn-sǒu** 鈍叟 confirming identity.
