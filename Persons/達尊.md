# Dázūn 達尊 (Shíshuāng Ěrzhān Dázūn 石霜爾瞻達尊)

Early-Qīng LínjìYángqí Chán master, **the post-Míng-Qīng-transition restorer of the ancient Shíshuāngshān seat** 瀏陽石霜山 in Liúyáng, Húnán — the Chán ancestral seat of **Shíshuāng Qìngzhū** 石霜慶諸 (807–888) and **Címíng Shíshuāng Chǔyuán** 慈明石霜楚圓 (986–1039). *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Dázūn** 達尊, *hào* **Ěrzhān** 爾瞻 (from a *Shījīng* allusion). Lay surname **Táng** 唐, of **Níngbō Yínxiàn** 寧波鄞縣. Lifedates **萬曆 36 – 康熙 2 癸卯 9月** = **1608 – October-November 1663**, age **56 *suì*** / *sēnglà* **31** per the Běnxiù *tǎmíng* at [[KR6q0420]] juan 2. Principal dharma-heir of **Dùnsǒu Zhìjì** 鈍叟智際 at **Dōngmíng Lǜluóān** 東明綠蘿菴 on Héngshān — a lesser-documented Mìyún Yuánwù sub-line master. Mùchén Dàomǐn's preface identifies Dázūn as "*Lóngchí Huàn lǎorén xià xíngzì fǎsì zhōng dìyī qiáochǔ* 龍池幻老人下行字法嗣中第一翹楚" — first-rank among the **行-generation** dharma-heirs of **Huànyǒu Zhèngchuán** 幻有正傳 (1549–1614, Mìyún's teacher).

**Life.** Family near **Pǔtuósì** 補陀寺 (Níngbō). One day found an abandoned *pútuán* + *jièyī* set at the roadside; took it to **Fúquánshān** 福泉山 and requested tonsure from **Yuánmíngshī** 圜明師. Served Yuánmíng three years. **Chóngzhēn 乙亥 winter** (= **1635 winter**): received full precepts from **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 at Tiāntóngsì at age 28. Subsequent consultation-tour: **Jīnsù Chénggōng** 乘公, **Biànshān Xuěgōng** 雪公, **Nánjiàn Cíwèn** 南澗慈問 (1580–1659). Final sealing at **Dōngmíng Lǜluóān** 東明綠蘿菴 by **Dùnsǒu Zhìjì** 際公 on the *Jiāshān bōchén jiàn Fó* 夾山撥塵見佛 *gōngàn* and the subsequent *yuānyāng xiù* 鴛鴦繡 transmission-verse. Five-year attendance at Héngshān with Zhìjì. **乙酉** 乙酉 (1645): Zhìjì fled the transition-warfare to Tánzhōu and died there; **Nányuánshī** 南源師 brought the body back to Héngyuè for burial.

**Shíshuāng abbacy (1646–1663).** **順治 3 丙戌** (= **1646**): accepted invitation from **Liúyáng Shíshuāng Běnhuō** 瀏陽石霜本豁 and other patrons to take the Shíshuāng abbacy. **18-year tenure** restoring the ancient *zǔtíng*, with a brief **1658** monastic-dispute withdrawal followed by a **1659** return. **1660**: restored Shíshuāng Qìngzhū's ancestor-pagoda and petitioned **Mùchén Dàomǐn** 木陳道忞 at Tiāntóng for the restoration-*jì* 記. **1663**: built his own pre-mortem *shòutǎ* 壽塔 adjacent to Qìngzhū's ancestor-pagoda; illness followed immediately. Died sitting cross-legged at 亥時 on an unspecified night in the 9th lunar month of Kāngxī 2 = October/November 1663. 7-day post-death *samādhi*; rain-cessation on cremation-day; ashes interred **10月9日** (= early November 1663).

**Historical significance.** Dázūn's 18-year Shíshuāng restoration is the single most consequential Qīng-era revival of a Línjì-tradition *zǔtíng* seat in Húnán after the ZhāngXiànzhōng devastation. His parallels are [[珠|Dàxiū Zhū]] at Cáoxī Nánhuá (1654–56, at [[KR6q0413]]) and [[通醉|Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì]] at Chéngdū Zhāojué (from 1663, at [[KR6q0415]]) — three near-simultaneous post-1644 **Chán *zǔtíng* restoration-programs** at three different historically-central sites.

Work: [[KR6q0420]] 《石霜爾瞻尊禪師語錄》 (2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J27 B200, compiled by *shìzhě* [[本開]] Běnkāi; preface by Lǐ Wényìn 1666-11 + preface by Mùchén Dàomǐn c. 1665–66 + *tǎmíng* by dharma-brother [[本秀]] Běnxiù dated 1666-04-04).

Sources: [[KR6q0420]] juan 2 *tǎmíng* by **Běnxiù** 本秀 (1666-04-04) and juan 1 prefaces by **Lǐ Wényìn** (1666-11) and **Mùchén Dàomǐn** (c. 1665–66); *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書; *Shíshuāngsì zhì* 石霜寺志.

**Editorial role**: Lead compiler (*děng biāncì*, with co-disciple [[達謙]] **Dáqiān**) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0608]] 《山茨際禪師語錄》 4 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1649. Note that DILA's record of Tōngjì's *fǎsì* lists "**爾瞻尊**" 爾瞻尊 — i.e. *zì* + *míng* — and the variant "**鈍叟智際**" used in the existing description above is a name-form variant for **山茨通際** (DILA primary form), with shared *biéhào* **Dùnsǒu** 鈍叟 confirming identity.
