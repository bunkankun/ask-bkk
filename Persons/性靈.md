Xìnglíng 性靈, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí (Pòshān → 不會) dharma-heir of **Bùhuì Fǎtōng** [[法通]] 不會法通 (b. 1621) at **Qīngguǒ Chányuàn** 清果禪院 in **Shèjiāng** 射江 (Sìchuān). Lead compiler of the 10-juan 《不會禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0462]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Xìnglíng děng biān* 性靈等編. Named in the DILA authority A019261 entry as one of the principal heirs alongside **Tōngdā** 通耷, **Cíkūn Fǎyù** 慈昆法玉, and **Qìngfǎ** 慶法.
