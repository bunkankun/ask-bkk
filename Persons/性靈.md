Xìng-líng 性靈, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí (Pòshān → 不會) dharma-heir of **Bù-huì Fǎ-tōng** [[法通]] 不會法通 (b. 1621) at **Qīng-guǒ Chán-yuàn** 清果禪院 in **Shè-jiāng** 射江 (Sì-chuān). Lead compiler of the 10-juan 《不會禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0462]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Xìng-líng děng biān* 性靈等編. Named in the DILA authority A019261 entry as one of the principal heirs alongside **Tōng-dā** 通耷, **Cí-kūn Fǎ-yù** 慈昆法玉, and **Qìng-fǎ** 慶法.
