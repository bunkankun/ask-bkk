Northern-Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk active in the third quarter of the eleventh century at the Pǔjìng Monastery 普靜寺 at Zhá-xī 霅谿 (the area around the Tài-hú lake in modern Zhèjiāng). The colophon of his [[KR6i0371|*Lán-pén shū-chāo yú-yì* 蘭盆疏鈔餘義]] (X376) describes him as "門人霅谿普靜寺賜紫日新隨聽次錄" — "the disciple Rì-xīn, granted purple, of Pǔjìng Monastery at Zhá-xī, recorded these notes in the order in which he heard them lectured." The grant of the purple robe (*cì zǐ* 賜紫) marks him as a Sòng-imperial-recognized scholar-monk; "*suí-tīng cì lù*" 隨聽次錄 ("recorded as heard, in lecture order") gives the original genre of his work as auditor's lecture-notes.

[[日新]]'s preserved text is dated by its own colophon to **Xī-níng 1 (1068)** — "時熙寧元年歲次戊申六月望日啟講，七月十有二日徹廗" ("on the full-moon day of the sixth month of the *wù-shēn* year, Xī-níng 1, the lectures were inaugurated, and on the twelfth day of the seventh month they were concluded"). The work is a *yú-yì* 餘義 ("residual matters") sub-commentary on the *Yúlán-pén* commentarial cluster.
