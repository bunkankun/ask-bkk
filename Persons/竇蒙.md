Mid-Táng official and calligraphy commentator; *zì* Zǐquán 子全, elder brother of [[竇臮]] (Dòu Jì). He served as *Shì Guózǐ-sīyè* 試國子司業 concurrently *xiànlìng* of Tàiyuán 太原縣令, and (according to a self-reference embedded in the annotations to the *Shùshū fù*) as *sīyì-láng* 司議郎 and *Ānnán dūhù* 安南都護. Active in the *Tiānbǎo* and post-rebellion decades (the CBDB florescence range gives 773–782 for activity in the bureaucracy). Author of the annotations on his brother's [[KR3h0007]] *Shùshū fù*, which include the *Shùshū fù yǔlì* 述書賦語例 (terminological apparatus) and supply offices, dates and stylistic notes for the 198 calligraphers named in the *fù*. The Sìkù editors flagged a self-referential note under his own entry as a possible indication that the annotations may in fact be Jì's, but retained the traditional attribution on the strength of Zhāng Yànyuǎn's *Fǎshū yàolù*.
