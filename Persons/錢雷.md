Late-Míng physician of Sì-míng 四明 (Níng-bō prefecture, Zhè-jiāng); *zì* Yù-zhāi 豫齋. Active in the late Wàn-lì era (c. 1580–1620). Best known for his supplementary editing in *Wàn-lì* 36 / 1608 of the originally-anonymous 8-*juǎn* *Zàng-fǔ zhèng-zhì tú-shuō Rén-jìng jí* 臟腑證治圖說人鏡集, to which he added his own 2-*juǎn* *Rén-jìng jīng fù-lù* 人鏡經附錄; the combined work circulated as [[KR3er118]] *Rén-jìng jīng fù-lù quán-shū* 人鏡經附錄全書. His acupuncture-doctrinal contribution — particularly the *shí-sì jīng jīng-luò* doctrine, the *biàn-zhèng lùn-zhì* protocols, the *sì-hǎi bā-huì* 四海八會 framework, and the *xiān-tiān-jīng-luò* integration — is one of the principal late-Míng theoretical contributions to acupuncture. Early-Qīng Zhāng Jùn-yīng 張俊英 produced a further continuation, the *Rén-jìng jīng xù-lù* 人鏡經續錄. Not in CBDB.
