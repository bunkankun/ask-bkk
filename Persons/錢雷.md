Late-Míng physician of Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō prefecture, Zhèjiāng); *zì* Yùzhāi 豫齋. Active in the late Wànlì era (c. 1580–1620). Best known for his supplementary editing in *Wànlì* 36 / 1608 of the originally-anonymous 8-*juǎn* *Zàngfǔ zhèngzhì túshuō Rénjìng jí* 臟腑證治圖說人鏡集, to which he added his own 2-*juǎn* *Rénjìng jīng fùlù* 人鏡經附錄; the combined work circulated as [[KR3er118]] *Rénjìng jīng fùlù quánshū* 人鏡經附錄全書. His acupuncture-doctrinal contribution — particularly the *shísì jīng jīngluò* doctrine, the *biànzhèng lùnzhì* protocols, the *sìhǎi bāhuì* 四海八會 framework, and the *xiāntiānjīngluò* integration — is one of the principal late-Míng theoretical contributions to acupuncture. Early-Qīng Zhāng Jùnyīng 張俊英 produced a further continuation, the *Rénjìng jīng xùlù* 人鏡經續錄. Not in CBDB.
