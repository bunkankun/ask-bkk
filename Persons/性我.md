Kamakura-period (late 12th century) Japanese monk recorded in the 1189 colophon to [[明覺]] Myōkaku's *Dàfódǐng rúlái fàngguāng xīdátā bōdáluō tuóluóní kānzhù* ([[KR6j0183]]). Made a transcript of the *kanchū* at Kamakura on the 10th day of the 10th month of Bunji 5 (1189 CE).
