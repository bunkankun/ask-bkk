# Xiāo Zǐxiǎn 蕭子顯

*Zì* Jǐngyáng 景陽. Native of Lánlíng 蘭陵 (the ancestral home of the QíLiáng Xiāo imperial lineage; modern Wǔjìn 武進, Jiāngsū). Grandson of Qí Gāodì 齊高帝 Xiāo Dàochéng 蕭道成 and son of Yǔzhāng wáng 豫章王 Xiāo Yí 蕭嶷.

Career: served the Liáng dynasty (502–557) that displaced his ancestral Qí. Held *Tàizǐ shěrén* 太子舍人, *Hànlín xuéshì*, and ultimately *Wúxīng tàishǒu* 吳興太守, dying *in officio* in 537 at age 49. He was a member of the Liáng Wǔdì 梁武帝 Xiāo Yǎn's inner circle (Wǔdì being his cousin in the third degree) and a fixture of the literary salons of the Crown Prince Xiāo Tǒng 蕭統 (compiler of the *Wén xuǎn*).

Principal works: (1) the *Nán Qí shū* 南齊書 ([[KR2a0017]]), the dynastic history of his own grandfather's house, in 60 *juǎn* (now 59); presented to the Liáng court ca. 514–537. (2) The *Hòu Hàn shū* 後漢書 in 100 *juǎn* (lost); (3) the *Pǔtōng běifá jì* 普通北伐記 in 5 *juǎn* (lost); (4) substantial *fù* and *shī*, partly preserved in the *Wén xuǎn* and *Yìwén lèijù*.

His biographies are in *Liáng shū* 35 ([[KR2a0018]]) and *Nán shǐ* 42 ([[KR2a0024]]). His brother Xiāo Zǐkè 蕭子恪 has the principal Xiāo-clan biography, with Xiāo Zǐxiǎn appended.
