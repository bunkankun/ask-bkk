# 崔丞相 Cuī chéngxiàng

A Koryŏ-dynasty Korean prime minister (姓崔) who wrote to the Southern-Sòng Tiāntái master [[法照]] Huìyán Fǎzhào requesting Buddhist instruction, demonstrating the broad East-Asian reception of Fǎzhào's teaching even before the formal printing of his *Fǎhuá jīng sāndàbù dújiào jì* ([[KR6d0062]]) in 1256. Identity beyond the surname Cuī 崔 and ministerial office is not firmly established.
