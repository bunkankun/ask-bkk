# 徐蔭庭

Xú Yìntíng 徐蔭庭, of the Hánjiāng 邗江 region (Yángzhōu). Manuscript-holder from whom [[嚴敦益]] Yán Dūnyì obtained one of the manuscripts used in his 1923 recovery-edition of [[王九峰]] Wáng Jiǔfēng's case-records ([[KR3ep076]], [[KR3ep077]]).
