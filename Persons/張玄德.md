**Zhāng Xuándé** 張玄德 was a Five-Dynasties Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0335]] *Dān lùn jué zhǐ xīn jiàn* 丹論訣旨心鑑 ("Heart-Mirror on the Purport of Elixir Discourses and Instructions"). Biographical details are unrecorded.
