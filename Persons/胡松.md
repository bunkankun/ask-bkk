Mid-Míng official and educational official. **CBDB id 68290, dates 1490–1572**. *Zì* **Mào-zhōng** 茂中. Native of **Chú-yáng** 滁陽 (modern Anhuī's Chú-zhōu). Note: the catalog meta of [[KR4h0111]] confused him with an earlier Yuán-era 胡松 with dates 1274–1357 (CBDB id 104865); the Míng anthologist is the Tóng-chéng / Chú-yáng man of the Jiā-jìng era. *Jìnshì* of **Jiā-jìng 1521** (according to standard sources).

Served as **Shān-xī *àn-chá-sī tí-xué fù-shǐ*** (Vice Education Commissioner of Shān-xī) at the time of compiling the **[[KR4h0111]]** *Táng-Sòng-Yuán míng-biǎo* 唐宋元名表 (5 *juǎn*, prefaced **Jiā-jìng *rén-yín* / 1542**). The anthology served as an examination-preparation model for second-stage civil-service candidates' *biǎo* compositions. His other major work, the *Chú-zhōu zhì* (Chú-zhōu gazetteer), is separately catalogued.
