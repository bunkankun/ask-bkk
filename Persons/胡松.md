Mid-Míng official and educational official. **CBDB id 68290, dates 1490–1572**. *Zì* **Màozhōng** 茂中. Native of **Chúyáng** 滁陽 (modern Anhuī's Chúzhōu). Note: the catalog meta of [[KR4h0111]] confused him with an earlier Yuán-era 胡松 with dates 1274–1357 (CBDB id 104865); the Míng anthologist is the Tóngchéng / Chúyáng man of the Jiājìng era. *Jìnshì* of **Jiājìng 1521** (according to standard sources).

Served as **Shānxī *ànchásī tíxué fùshǐ*** (Vice Education Commissioner of Shānxī) at the time of compiling the **[[KR4h0111]]** *Táng-Sòng-Yuán míngbiǎo* 唐宋元名表 (5 *juǎn*, prefaced **Jiājìng *rényín* / 1542**). The anthology served as an examination-preparation model for second-stage civil-service candidates' *biǎo* compositions. His other major work, the *Chúzhōu zhì* (Chúzhōu gazetteer), is separately catalogued.
