# Tàiquán 太泉

Early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán *shūjì* 書記 under [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi (1615–1702) at Fúzhōu Gǔshān 鼓山; recorder (*lù* 錄) of the two-juan *Wèilín Dàopèi chánshī cānxiāng lù* ([[KR6q0369]]) during Dàopèi's semi-retirement period of Kāngxī 3–6 (1664–1667), when despite the master's attempts to withdraw from public preaching he was repeatedly drawn back into it at the insistence of lay donors. Lifedates unrecorded.
