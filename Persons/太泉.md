# Tài-quán 太泉

Early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán *shū-jì* 書記 under [[道霈]] Wèi-lín Dào-pèi (1615–1702) at Fú-zhōu Gǔ-shān 鼓山; recorder (*lù* 錄) of the two-juan *Wèi-lín Dào-pèi chánshī cān-xiāng lù* ([[KR6q0369]]) during Dào-pèi's semi-retirement period of Kāng-xī 3–6 (1664–1667), when despite the master's attempts to withdraw from public preaching he was repeatedly drawn back into it at the insistence of lay donors. Lifedates unrecorded.
