# Yòng-sūn 用孫

Northern-Sòng Chán disciple (*cān xué* 參學) of Láng-yá Huì-jué 瑯琊慧覺. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Preface-writer for his master's *yǔlù*, preserved in the Kanripo corpus as [[KR6q0275]] (preface-only preservation).
