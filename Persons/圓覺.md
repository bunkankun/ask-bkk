Yuán-jué 圓覺 (active 14th c.), Yuán Buddhist scholar-monk; author of [[KR6e0107|*Huáyán yuán rén lùn jiě* 華嚴原人論解]] (X1032, 3 fasc.) and [[KR6e0108|*Huáyán yuán rén lùn hé jiě* 華嚴原人論合解]] (X1033, 2 fasc.) — both Yuán-period commentaries on [[宗密]] Zōngmì's [[KR6e0105|*Yuán rén lùn*]] (T1886). The two works represent successive stages of Yuán Buddhist commentarial activity on Zōngmì's *Yuán rén lùn*. Birth and death dates not preserved.
