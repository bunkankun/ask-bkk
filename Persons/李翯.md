**Lǐ Hè** 李翯 (Yi Hwang, traditional attribution). Korean *yǎngshēng* figure whose tradition is associated with the *Huórén xīnfǎ* eight-figure illustrated sequence that [[鐵峰居士]] Tiěfēng Jūshì incorporated into the *Bǎoshēng xīnjiàn* 保生心鑑 ([[KR3eo041]]).
