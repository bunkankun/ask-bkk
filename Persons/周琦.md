Zhōu Qí 周琦 (Míng), *zì* Tíngxǐ 廷璽, was a Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* official of Mǎ-píng 馬平 (Liǔ-zhōu region, Guǎng-xī). *Jìnshì* of *Chéng-huà xīn-chǒu* (1481); rose to *Nán-jīng Hù-bù yuán-wài láng* 南京戶部員外郎. His learning derived from the *Hé-Dōng xué-pài* of Xuē Xuān (KR3a0079 author). His major work is the *Dōng-xī rì tán lù* 東溪日談錄 (KR3a0083), an 18-juan *biji* of his classical-philosophical reflections, printed in *Jiā-jìng dīng-yǒu* (1537) by Lǚ Jǐng-méng 呂景蒙. Famed in his home district for *duān-zhí jǐn-hòu* (uprightness, rigour, sincerity). The *Guǎng-xī tōng zhì* records the *Rì tán lù* in 18 juan and a separate *Rú zhèng piān* — the SKQS *tíyào* identifies the *Rú zhèng piān* as juan 15 of the *Rì tán lù* rather than a separate work.
