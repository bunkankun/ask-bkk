Zhōu Qí 周琦 (Míng), *zì* Tíngxǐ 廷璽, was a Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* official of Mǎpíng 馬平 (Liǔzhōu region, Guǎngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Chénghuà xīnchǒu* (1481); rose to *Nánjīng Hùbù yuánwài láng* 南京戶部員外郎. His learning derived from the *HéDōng xuépài* of Xuē Xuān ([[KR3a0079]] author). His major work is the *Dōngxī rì tán lù* 東溪日談錄 ([[KR3a0083]]), an 18-juan *biji* of his classical-philosophical reflections, printed in *Jiājìng dīngyǒu* (1537) by Lǚ Jǐngméng 呂景蒙. Famed in his home district for *duānzhí jǐnhòu* (uprightness, rigour, sincerity). The *Guǎngxī tōng zhì* records the *Rì tán lù* in 18 juan and a separate *Rú zhèng piān* — the SKQS *tíyào* identifies the *Rú zhèng piān* as juan 15 of the *Rì tán lù* rather than a separate work.
