**Liú Qí** 劉跂 (fl. *Yuán-fēng*–*Zhèng-hé*, c. 1075–1117), *zì* Sī-lì 斯立, *hào* Xué-Yì xiānshēng 學易先生, of Dōng-píng 東平 (Shān-dōng). Eldest son of Liú Zhì 劉摯 (the *Yuán-yòu* chief councillor). *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-fēng* 2 / 1079. Held office to *Cháo-fèng-láng*, *Tài-cháng bó-shì*. Like his father, identified with the *Yuán-yòu* faction; in *Shào-shèng* / *Yuán-fú* his father was demoted and exiled, and Liú Qí spent the *Chóng-níng* / *Dà-guān* period in retirement. *Hào* "Xué-Yì" reflects his deep engagement with the *Yì-jīng*. Author of *Xué-Yì jí* 學易集 [[KR4d0113]] in 8 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 340 (附 in his father Liú Zhì's biography).
