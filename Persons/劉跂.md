**Liú Qí** 劉跂 (fl. *Yuánfēng*–*Zhènghé*, c. 1075–1117), *zì* Sīlì 斯立, *hào* Xué-Yì xiānshēng 學易先生, of Dōngpíng 東平 (Shāndōng). Eldest son of Liú Zhì 劉摯 (the *Yuányòu* chief councillor). *Jìnshì* of *Yuánfēng* 2 / 1079. Held office to *Cháofèngláng*, *Tàicháng bóshì*. Like his father, identified with the *Yuányòu* faction; in *Shàoshèng* / *Yuánfú* his father was demoted and exiled, and Liú Qí spent the *Chóngníng* / *Dàguān* period in retirement. *Hào* "Xué-Yì" reflects his deep engagement with the *Yìjīng*. Author of *Xué-Yì jí* 學易集 [[KR4d0113]] in 8 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 340 (附 in his father Liú Zhì's biography).
