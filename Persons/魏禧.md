# Wèi Xǐ 魏禧

Early-Qīng essayist (1624–1681), *zì* Bīngshū 冰叔, *hào* Sháotíng 勺庭, of Níngdū 寧都 (Jiāngxī). One of the *Yìtáng sān Wèi* 易堂三魏 (Three Wèi of Yìtáng), together with his brothers Wèi Jì 魏際 and Wèi Lǐ 魏禮; an *yímín* 遺民 (Míng-loyalist holdover) who refused service under the Qīng. His *Wèi Sháotíng wénjí* 魏勺庭文集 is a major source for early-Qīng intellectual history. Praised the paediatrician [[程雲鵬]] Chéng Yúnpéng's clinical reasoning as comparable to [[諸葛亮]] Zhūgě Liàng's strategic generalship (*Wǔhóu yòngbīng* 武侯用兵).
