# Huì-zhōng 慧忠

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with earlier Chán masters of the same name (Nán-yáng Huì-zhōng 南陽慧忠 of the Táng, among others).
