# Huìzhōng 慧忠

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with earlier Chán masters of the same name (Nányáng Huìzhōng 南陽慧忠 of the Táng, among others).
