**Xuán chōng shī** 玄沖師, *hào* Xuán xū zǐ 玄虛子, was a Southern-Sòng Daoist, author of [[KR5e0047]] *Xuán xū zǐ míng zhēn jí* 玄虛子鳴真集 (preface 1251).
