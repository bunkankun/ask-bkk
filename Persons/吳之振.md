Wú Zhī-zhèn 吳之振 (1640–1717, *zì* Mèng-jǔ 孟舉, *hào* Huáng-yè-cūn-nóng 黃葉村農), of Shí-mén 石門 (Tóng-xiāng prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Major early-Qīng anthologist; principal editor (with Lǚ Liú-liáng 呂留良 and Wú Zì-mù 吳自牧 in the early phases) of the *Sòng shī chāo* 宋詩鈔 [[KR4h0157]] — the canonical pre-modern Sòng-poetry anthology in 106 *juǎn*, covering 100 individual Sòng poets with brief biographical *xiǎo-zhuàn* in the manner of Yuán Hǎo-wèn's *Zhōng-zhōu jí*. The compilation was undertaken in the late 1660s and printed by Wú himself in 1672 — at a moment when early-Qīng poets were actively rebelling against late-Míng *Qián / Hòu qī-zǐ* imitation by recovering Sòng-poetry style as an alternative. Wú also produced the *Huáng-yè cūn-zhuāng shī jí* 黃葉村莊詩集 (his own collected poetry) — recorded in the *Sìkù tíyào*. The compilation's 1672 first-printing was incomplete (16 of the 100 *bié-jí* had table-of-contents listings but no surviving text, as the woodblocks were never cut); these were supplemented in the early 18th century by Cáo Tíng-dòng's [[曹庭棟]] *Sòng bǎi-jiā shī cún* 宋百家詩存 [[KR4h0167]]. CBDB id 78225; lifedates 1640–1717. Standard biographies in *Qīng-shǐ gǎo* 清史稿 j.484.
