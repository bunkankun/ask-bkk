# 普敬

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Wúmén [[慧開|Huìkāi]] 無門慧開 (1183–1260). Co-recorder of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0289]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
