Xiè Huìlián 謝惠連 (407–433 CE; *zì* Xiǎoshān 小山) was a Liú Sòng 劉宋 poet from the Chéngjùn Xiè 陳郡謝 clan, first cousin of Xiè Língyùn 謝靈運. He died young at age 27. Evaluated in Zhōng Róng's 鍾嶸 *Shīpǐn* 詩品 in the upper grade. Best known for the 〈雪賦〉 (Rhapsody on Snow, *Wénxuǎn* juǎn 13) and his place in the "Three Xies" (*Sān Xiè* 三謝) literary tradition alongside Xie Lingyun and Xiè Tiào 謝朓. Biography in *Sòngshū* 宋書 juǎn 73 and *Nán shǐ* 南史 juǎn 19. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0044]].
