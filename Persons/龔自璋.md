Mid-Qīng (Dàoguāng / Xiánfēng era) physician of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng); *shēngyuán* of his county. Son of Gōng Dǐān 龔砥庵, the third-generation head of a Rénhé hereditary medical family. Best known by his *zì* Yuèchuān 月川. His principal documented work is the compilation of [[KR3er114]] *Jiāyòng liángfāng* 家用良方 (1851), composed in collaboration with the Cantonese *jìnshì* [[黃統]] Huáng Tǒng from 1843 to 1851. The work became one of the most widely distributed late-Qīng household formularies, with multiple reprints into the Tóngzhì and Guāngxù periods. Not in CBDB.
