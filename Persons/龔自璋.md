Mid-Qīng (Dào-guāng / Xián-fēng era) physician of Rén-hé 仁和 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhè-jiāng); *shēng-yuán* of his county. Son of Gōng Dǐ-ān 龔砥庵, the third-generation head of a Rén-hé hereditary medical family. Best known by his *zì* Yuè-chuān 月川. His principal documented work is the compilation of [[KR3er114]] *Jiā-yòng liáng-fāng* 家用良方 (1851), composed in collaboration with the Cantonese *jìn-shì* [[黃統]] Huáng Tǒng from 1843 to 1851. The work became one of the most widely distributed late-Qīng household formularies, with multiple reprints into the Tóng-zhì and Guāng-xù periods. Not in CBDB.
