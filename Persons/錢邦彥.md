Qián Bāng-yàn 錢邦彥 — credited in the Kanripo catalog meta as 撰坿錄 (writer of supplementary records / annotations) on Gù Yánwǔ's *Tiānxià jùn-guó lì-bìng shū* 天下郡國利病書 ([[KR2k0037]]). His exact identity and lifedates are uncertain. CBDB has four entries under this name: a Qīng-era figure (c_personid 86495, 1848–1914) too late to be Gù's annotator; a Míng-era figure (c_personid 133102, b. 1501) probably too early; and two further Míng / Qīng entries with no recorded dates (c_personid 226014, 530931). The SBCK *biān-yìn lì-yán* itself discusses the difficulty of separating annotator-additions from Gù's own hand on the autograph manuscript ("校注之字…其爲亭林手書與否任人以字蹟辨之"). The most plausible inference is that Qián Bāng-yàn was a late-Míng or early-Qīng figure of Gù's circle, perhaps connected with the Kūn-shān or Sū-zhōu literati; a definitive identification has not been made in the modern scholarly literature consulted.

- - - - -

A Qīng-era homonym Qián Bāng-yàn 錢邦彥 (1848–1914), known from the *Qīng-dài rén-wù shēng-zú nián-biǎo* (CBDB c_personid 86495), is unrelated to the *Lì-bìng shū* annotator and belongs to the late Qīng / early Republican period.
