The singular mid-Yuán 元 Hàn-lín literary patriarch and principal *Yù-táng* (Hàn-lín) state-paper composer of the Zhì-zhì and Tiān-lì eras (1321–1330); senior member of the **mid-Yuán four-master quartet** (Yú-Yáng-Fàn-Jiē). *Zì* **Bó-shēng** 伯生; *hào* **Dào-yuán** 道園 ("Dào-Garden"); posthumous *shì* **Wén-jìng** 文靖. Native of **Shǔ-jùn** 蜀郡 (Sì-chuān). 1272–1348 (CBDB 10801).

**Recruitment.** Recovered into Yuán imperial service by **Chéng Jù-fū** [[程鉅夫]] [[KR4d0466]] in the Zhì-yuán 23 (1286) southern-recruitment expedition — Yú was then 14 years old. This recruitment is one of the foundational consequential acts of Yuán imperial Hàn-Confucian formation.

**Career.** Successive Hàn-lín offices spanning four Yuán reigns (Chéng-zōng, Wǔ-zōng, Rén-zōng, Yīng-zōng, Tài-dìng-dì, Wén-zōng, Shùn-dì). Principal Hàn-lín state-paper composer of the Zhì-zhì and Tiān-lì eras: the *cháo-tíng diǎn-cè* (court canonical-investiture documents) and the *gōng-qīng dà-fū bēi-bǎn* (senior officials' steles-and-tablets) all came from his hand.

**Mid-Yuán quartet.** With **Yáng Zài** 楊載 [[KR4d0495]] (1271–1323), **Fàn Pèng** 范梈 [[KR4d0496]] (1272–1330), and **Jiē Xī-sī** 揭傒斯 [[KR4d0497]] (1274–1344) forms the canonical **Yú-Yáng-Fàn-Jiē** mid-Yuán literary quartet.

**Maternal lineage.** Mother **Yáng-shì** 楊氏 was the daughter of the Sòng *Héng-yáng* magistrate **Yáng Wén-zhòng** 楊文仲. The maternal-uncle's-great-grandson **Yáng Chūn** 楊椿 of Méi-shān composed the preface to the *Dào-yuán yí-gǎo* in Zhì-zhèng *jǐ-hài* (1359).
