The singular mid-Yuán 元 Hànlín literary patriarch and principal *Yùtáng* (Hànlín) state-paper composer of the Zhìzhì and Tiānlì eras (1321–1330); senior member of the **mid-Yuán four-master quartet** (YúYángFànJiē). *Zì* **Bóshēng** 伯生; *hào* **Dàoyuán** 道園 ("Dào-Garden"); posthumous *shì* **Wénjìng** 文靖. Native of **Shǔjùn** 蜀郡 (Sìchuān). 1272–1348 (CBDB 10801).

**Recruitment.** Recovered into Yuán imperial service by **Chéng Jùfū** [[程鉅夫]] [[KR4d0466]] in the Zhìyuán 23 (1286) southern-recruitment expedition — Yú was then 14 years old. This recruitment is one of the foundational consequential acts of Yuán imperial Hàn-Confucian formation.

**Career.** Successive Hànlín offices spanning four Yuán reigns (Chéngzōng, Wǔzōng, Rénzōng, Yīngzōng, Tàidìngdì, Wénzōng, Shùndì). Principal Hànlín state-paper composer of the Zhìzhì and Tiānlì eras: the *cháotíng diǎncè* (court canonical-investiture documents) and the *gōngqīng dàfū bēibǎn* (senior officials' steles-and-tablets) all came from his hand.

**Mid-Yuán quartet.** With **Yáng Zài** 楊載 [[KR4d0495]] (1271–1323), **Fàn Pèng** 范梈 [[KR4d0496]] (1272–1330), and **Jiē Xīsī** 揭傒斯 [[KR4d0497]] (1274–1344) forms the canonical **YúYángFànJiē** mid-Yuán literary quartet.

**Maternal lineage.** Mother **Yángshì** 楊氏 was the daughter of the Sòng *Héngyáng* magistrate **Yáng Wénzhòng** 楊文仲. The maternal-uncle's-great-grandson **Yáng Chūn** 楊椿 of Méishān composed the preface to the *Dàoyuán yígǎo* in Zhìzhèng *jǐhài* (1359).

**Within the Kanripo corpus.**
- [[KR4d0493]] *Dàoyuán xuégǔ lù* 道園學古錄 (撰) — the principal 50-*juàn* collection edited by his disciple **Lǐ Běn** 李本
- [[KR4d0494]] *Dàoyuán yígǎo* 道園遺稿 (撰) — supplementary collection compiled by Yú's grand-nephew **Yú Kān** 虞堪
