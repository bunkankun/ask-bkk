# Chéngyù 澄彧

Early Northern-Sòng Tiāntái-affiliated monk active at **Wúshān** 吳山 (in present-day Hángzhōu 杭州). Lifedates not recorded; he is known almost exclusively as the annotator of the *Zhù shíyí lùn* 注十疑論 [[KR6p0051]] — his commentary on the *Jìngtǔ shíyí lùn* 淨土十疑論 attributed to [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ. The text is prefaced by [[贊寧]] Zànníng (919–1001), whose preface is dated **太平興國 8 (983)**, fixing Chéngyù's floruit firmly in the WúYuè / early-Sòng transition. Zànníng's preface refers to him reverentially as *Wúshān Chéngyù shàngrén* 吳山澄彧上人. No further prosopographical information has been recovered from Sòng-period biographical sources.
