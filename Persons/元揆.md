Yuánkuí 元揆, *zì* **Yīkuí** 一揆. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yuǎnān Běntǎn** [[本僼]] 遠菴本僼 (1622–1682). Born **崇禎甲戌 4/27 申時** = **1634-05-23 Gregorian**; alive in **康熙36 = 1697** at age 64. Native of **Shàoxīngfǔ Huìjī** 紹興府會稽; lay surname **Qián** 錢.

**Formation.** Tonsured at 22 (1655) under **Huìshān héshàng** 慧山. Pilgrimage to **Lǐlíng Yúnyán** 醴陵雲巖, **Shíshuāng Ěrzhān** 石霜爾瞻; awakening on **Huángyán** 黃巖 viewing a waterfall. Continued to **Bàoēn Yùlín**, **Gǔnán Mùyún**, **Tiāntóng Hóngjué** etc. Final transmission from **Yuǎnān Běntǎn** at Tiāntóng.

**Five abbacies.** **Yuèzhǎng Shòushèng** 月掌壽聖, **Jīngshān Yǒnglóng** 京山永隆, **Shéndǐng Zīshèng** 神鼎資聖 (the title-form 神鼎一揆), **Míngzhōu Ruìyán** 明州瑞巖 (succession to teacher), **Míngzhōu Tiāntóng** 明州天童.

**Heirs.** **Yùtáng Jiǒng** [[成炯]] 昱堂烱; **Yuètāo Càn** 月濤粲; **Dòngyě Xiān** 洞野先 et al.

**Work.** [[KR6q0513]] 《神鼎一揆禪師語錄》 12 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B388, compiled by *fǎsì* **Chéngjiǒng** [[成炯]] *děng biān*; juan 12 *xíngshí* + *shòutǎmíng*.
