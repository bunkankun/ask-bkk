Yuán-kuí 元揆, *zì* **Yī-kuí** 一揆. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yuǎn-ān Běn-tǎn** [[本僼]] 遠菴本僼 (1622–1682). Born **崇禎甲戌 4/27 申時** = **1634-05-23 Gregorian**; alive in **康熙36 = 1697** at age 64. Native of **Shào-xīng-fǔ Huì-jī** 紹興府會稽; lay surname **Qián** 錢.

**Formation.** Tonsured at 22 (1655) under **Huì-shān héshàng** 慧山. Pilgrimage to **Lǐ-líng Yún-yán** 醴陵雲巖, **Shí-shuāng Ěr-zhān** 石霜爾瞻; awakening on **Huáng-yán** 黃巖 viewing a waterfall. Continued to **Bào-ēn Yù-lín**, **Gǔ-nán Mù-yún**, **Tiān-tóng Hóng-jué** etc. Final transmission from **Yuǎn-ān Běn-tǎn** at Tiān-tóng.

**Five abbacies.** **Yuè-zhǎng Shòu-shèng** 月掌壽聖, **Jīng-shān Yǒng-lóng** 京山永隆, **Shén-dǐng Zī-shèng** 神鼎資聖 (the title-form 神鼎一揆), **Míng-zhōu Ruì-yán** 明州瑞巖 (succession to teacher), **Míng-zhōu Tiān-tóng** 明州天童.

**Heirs.** **Yù-táng Jiǒng** [[成炯]] 昱堂烱; **Yuè-tāo Càn** 月濤粲; **Dòng-yě Xiān** 洞野先 et al.

**Work.** [[KR6q0513]] 《神鼎一揆禪師語錄》 12 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B388, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chéng-jiǒng** [[成炯]] *děng biān*; juan 12 *xíng-shí* + *shòu-tǎ-míng*.
