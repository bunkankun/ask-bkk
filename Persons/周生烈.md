Zhōushēng Liè 周生烈 (compound surname 周生, *míng* Liè; *zì* Wényì 文逸 in some sources) was a classicist of the Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 court, active in the mid-3rd century. His biography survives only in scattered notices: a brief appendix to the *Sānguó zhì* 三國志 j. 13 (王肅傳, Péi Sōngzhī's commentary), where he is described as a Dūn-huáng 燉煌 native who served at the Wèi court and produced commentaries on the *Yì* 易, the *Lúnyǔ* 論語 and the *Lǐjì* 禮記. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records his *Lúnyǔ yìshuō* 論語義說 in 10 *juàn* and a *Yàolùn* 要論. His Wèi-period dates and exact career are otherwise unrecorded; he is conventionally treated as a contemporary of Wáng Sù 王肅 (195–256) and as part of the Wèi anti-Zhèng-Xuán classicist circle.

In the Kanripo corpus he appears as the attributed author of the reconstructed [[KR3a0119]] *Zhōushēngzǐ yàolùn* 周生子要論. CBDB has no confident entry under this name.
