Lǐ Gài 李概 (fl. mid-sixth century), a Northern Qí 北齊 phonologist. *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records his *Yīn pǔ* 音譜 ([[KR1j0112]]) in four *juàn* — a phonological compendium in the *Shēng lèi* / *Yùn jí* lineage that contributed to the development of the rhyme-book tradition immediately preceding the Suí *Qièyùn* (601). The *Yīn pǔ* was lost in the Sòng; surviving fragments are preserved chiefly in Buddhist *yīnyì* literature and in *Guǎngyùn* citations; reconstructed in CHANT. No biography in any standard history.
