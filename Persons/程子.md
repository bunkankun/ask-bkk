# 程子 Chéngzǐ

**Chéngzǐ** 程子, collective honorific of the Northern-Sòng Neo-Confucian brothers [[程顥]] Chéng Hào (1032–1085) and [[程頤]] Chéng Yí (1033–1107) of Luòyáng; founders of the *Luò-school* of Neo-Confucianism. Cited in [[楊繼洲]] Yáng Jìzhōu's *Zhēndào yuánliú* 針道源流 essay (preface to the *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng* [[KR3ee027]]) on the dating of the *Sùwèn* to the end of the Warring States.
