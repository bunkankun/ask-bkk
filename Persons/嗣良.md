Yuán-dynasty Vinaya monk **Sì-liáng** 嗣良 (lifedates not preserved), associate and editorial collaborator of [[省悟]] *Xǐng-wù* (DILA A000860) of the *Yǎn-zhōng lǜ-sì* 演忠律寺 in *Sì-míng* 四明 (Níngbō). Per DILA A001399: Yuán dynasty; monk; sole recorded function as the *cān-dìng* 參訂 ("collator") of Xǐng-wù's *Lǜ-yuàn shì-guī* 律苑事規 ([[KR6k0257]], X1113, 10 *juàn*) — preface dated *Tài-dìng* 泰定 1 (1324) by *Yuán Jué*, postface by Xǐng-wù dated *Tài-dìng* 2 (1325). No further biographical material survives.
