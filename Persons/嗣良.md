Yuán-dynasty Vinaya monk **Sìliáng** 嗣良 (lifedates not preserved), associate and editorial collaborator of [[省悟]] *Xǐngwù* (DILA A000860) of the *Yǎnzhōng lǜsì* 演忠律寺 in *Sìmíng* 四明 (Níngbō). Per DILA A001399: Yuán dynasty; monk; sole recorded function as the *cāndìng* 參訂 ("collator") of Xǐngwù's *Lǜyuàn shìguī* 律苑事規 ([[KR6k0257]], X1113, 10 *juàn*) — preface dated *Tàidìng* 泰定 1 (1324) by *Yuán Jué*, postface by Xǐngwù dated *Tàidìng* 2 (1325). No further biographical material survives.
