Tián Wángsūn 田王孫, Western Hàn *Yì*-master, transmitter of [[田何]] Tián Hé's *Zhōuyì* and teacher of the three principal Hàn *Yì*-lineages: [[施讐]] Shī Chóu, [[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ, and [[梁丘賀]] Liáng Qiūhè.
