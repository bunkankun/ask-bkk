Wǔbǎi Luóhàn 五百羅漢 ("five hundred arhats") is a shortened form of [[五百大阿羅漢]] *Wǔbǎi Dà Āluóhàn*. See that note for the legendary collective attribution behind the Sarvāstivāda *Vibhāṣā* literature ([[KR6l0010]], [[KR6l0011]], [[KR6l0012]]).
