Jìjī 濟機, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 / **Jìqǐ Hóngchǔ** 繼起弘儲 (1605–1672) in the **Hànyuè Fǎzàng** Sānfēng line at **Sūzhōu Língyánsì** 蘇州靈巖寺. Lead compiler of the 10-juan 《南嶽繼起和尚語錄》 ([[KR6q0475]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Jìjī děng biān* 濟機等編. The volume's self-preface by Hóngchǔ dated **壬子九月霜降第一日 = 1672/10** notes that the master himself selected and edited the contents from larger preceding compilations (the *Shùquán Bàocí*, *Jiǎchén Xiāngyúnguǎn sǎnlù*, *Yáofēng hòulù*) just weeks before his 1672/11/16 death; Jìjī's role was the editorial finalization. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
