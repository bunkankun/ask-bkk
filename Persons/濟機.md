Jì-jī 濟機, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 / **Jì-qǐ Hóng-chǔ** 繼起弘儲 (1605–1672) in the **Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** Sān-fēng line at **Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-sì** 蘇州靈巖寺. Lead compiler of the 10-juan 《南嶽繼起和尚語錄》 ([[KR6q0475]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Jì-jī děng biān* 濟機等編. The volume's self-preface by Hóng-chǔ dated **壬子九月霜降第一日 = 1672/10** notes that the master himself selected and edited the contents from larger preceding compilations (the *Shù-quán Bào-cí*, *Jiǎ-chén Xiāng-yún-guǎn sǎn-lù*, *Yáo-fēng hòu-lù*) just weeks before his 1672/11/16 death; Jì-jī's role was the editorial finalization. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
