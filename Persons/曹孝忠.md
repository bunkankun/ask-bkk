**Cáo Xiàozhōng** 曹孝忠 (*fl.* Zhènghé period, 1111–1118, 北宋), Northern-Sòng official-physician with the title **Tíjǔ yīxué** 提舉醫學 (Director of Medical Studies) under Sòng Huīzōng. Cáo's principal contribution is the imperial collation of Táng Shènwēi's ***Zhènglèi běncǎo*** 證類本草 — under Huīzōng's commission of Zhènghé 6 (1116), Cáo's collation produced the recension known as the ***Zhènghé Zhènglèi běncǎo*** 政和證類本草 ([[KR3e0028]] base text), distinguished from the parallel and slightly earlier 1108 ***Dàguān Zhènglèi běncǎo*** 大觀證類本草 (also based on Táng but collated under different editorial leadership at the Dàguān court). Cáo's preface — preserved at the head of the SKQS print — documents the Imperial Eunuch Yáng Jiǎn 楊戩's role in the printing arrangements and Cáo's own collation work. Beyond this work he has no transmitted biographical record.
