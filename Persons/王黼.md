Northern-Sòng grand councillor and antiquarian compiler under the Huīzōng emperor; *zì* Jiāng-míng 將明. Lifedates 1079–1126 (CBDB matches catalog meta's "d. 1126"). Originally named Fǔ 甫; the emperor changed it to Fǔ 黼. Native of Kāifēng 開封. Rose with extraordinary speed through the metropolitan officialdom, reaching *Shàng-shū Zuǒ-pú-yè* and *Tài-fù* (effective grand councillor) under Huīzōng. One of the central political figures of the late Huīzōng court, conventionally listed among the *Liù-zéi* 六賊 (Six Traitors) — Cài Jīng 蔡京, Lǐ Yán 李彥, Tóng Guàn 童貫, Lǐ Bāng-yàn 李邦彥, Liáng Shīchéng 梁師成 and Wáng Fǔ — by the Southern-Sòng historiographical tradition. Executed during the Jurchen sack of Biàn-jīng in 1126. Author of [[KR3h0087]] *Chóng-xiū Xuān-hé bó-gǔ tú* 重修宣和博古圖, the imperial Sòng catalog of bronze antiquities (30 *juàn*, begun *Dà-guān* 1107, completed in Xuān-hé). The Yuán *Zhì-dà* (1308–1311) recutting struck his authorship from the title-page as part of the political damnatio memoriae, leading to the long-current attribution to "Wáng Chǔ" 王楚 — which the Sìkù editors correctly recovered to Wáng Fǔ.
