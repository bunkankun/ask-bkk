# Zǔxiù 祖琇

Southern Sòng Chán monk and historian, active in the *Shàoxīng* and *Lóngxīng* eras (mid-twelfth century). Exact lifedates not transmitted.

Author of two principal works: the *Sēngbǎo zhèngxù zhuàn* 僧寶正續傳 ([[KR6q0041]]), the seven-juan supplement to Huìhóng's *Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn*; and the *Lóngxīng biānnián tōnglùn* 隆興編年通論 (1164), an annalistic Buddhist history covering events from antiquity through the early Southern Sòng. Both are important sources for Northern-to-early-Southern Sòng Chán biographical and historical material.
