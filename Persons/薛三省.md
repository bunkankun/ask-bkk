**Xuē Sān-shěng** 薛三省 (*zì* Lǔ-shū 魯叔, *hào* Fú-fēng 浮峰, c. 1555 – c. 1634), late-Míng official and friend of the Níngbō medical lineage. *Jìn-shì* in *Wàn-lì* 23 (1595); served at the Hàn-lín Academy as 翰林院侍講 and as 右春坊右諭德 in the eastern-palace tutorial establishment of the imperial heir. A native of Dìng-hǎi 定海 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng) and a personal friend of the elder brother of [[趙獻可]] Zhào Xiànkě; in this capacity he wrote the *Yī-wū-lǘ zǐ Yī-guàn xù* 醫巫閭子醫貫序 prefatory introduction to Zhào's *Yī-guàn* ([[KR3er001]]), which became the canonical late-Míng prefatory framing of the *Mìng-mén* school. CBDB (362264) gives *c_index_year* 1571; the slightly later birth-bracket given here follows the consensus of late-Míng official-biography reference works, which place his *jìn-shì* career trajectory in the period 1595–1620.
