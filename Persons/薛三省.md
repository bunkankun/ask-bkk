**Xuē Sānshěng** 薛三省 (*zì* Lǔshū 魯叔, *hào* Fúfēng 浮峰, c. 1555 – c. 1634), late-Míng official and friend of the Níngbō medical lineage. *Jìnshì* in *Wànlì* 23 (1595); served at the Hànlín Academy as 翰林院侍講 and as 右春坊右諭德 in the eastern-palace tutorial establishment of the imperial heir. A native of Dìnghǎi 定海 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng) and a personal friend of the elder brother of [[趙獻可]] Zhào Xiànkě; in this capacity he wrote the *Yīwūlǘ zǐ Yīguàn xù* 醫巫閭子醫貫序 prefatory introduction to Zhào's *Yīguàn* ([[KR3er001]]), which became the canonical late-Míng prefatory framing of the *Mìngmén* school. CBDB (362264) gives *c_index_year* 1571; the slightly later birth-bracket given here follows the consensus of late-Míng official-biography reference works, which place his *jìnshì* career trajectory in the period 1595–1620.
