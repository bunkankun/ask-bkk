Zhāng Wénjiā 張文嘉 (lifedates unrecorded), Qing-dynasty lay-scholar editor. With his brother [[張文憲]] Zhāng Wénxiàn, the Zhāng brothers compiled the *Chánlín bǎoxùn hé zhù* 禪林寶訓合註 ([[KR6q0154]], X64 n1263) — a four-juan combined commentary on the Southern-Sòng [[KR6q0099]] *Chánlín bǎoxùn*. Wénjiā served as *jiào dìng* 較定 (collator and corrector); Wénxiàn served as *cān yuè* 參閱 (editorial reviewer).

Lay-Buddhist status (not monks). No independent biographical material located; no DILA authority entry. Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0154]] only (editorial role).
