# Jikkan 實觀 (Edo-period Japanese Tendai monk, fl. late 17th c.)

A Japanese Tendai-school 天台 / 山家 monk active in the late seventeenth century, known principally as the editor and re-publisher of [[知禮]] Sìmíng Zhīlǐ's *Guān-wúliángshòu fó jīng shū miào-zōng chāo* 觀無量壽佛經疏妙宗鈔 [[KR6p0007]] in *Jōkyō* 貞享 4 (1687). His preface to that edition records that he undertook the re-cutting in order to restore the original 1013 text from the corrupted version produced by the Míng monk Bǎi-sōng / Zhēnjué 百松真覺, who had silently emended the text. Jikkan's *Jōkyō*-4 edition divided the original five-*juǎn* commentary into chapter-and-verse subdivisions (*fēn-huì* 分會) for ease of student reference, and the resulting recension is the basis of the *Xù-zàng-jīng* and Taishō (X22n0407) text.

His lifedates are not preserved. He is identified in the colophon as *shāmén* 沙門 ("śramaṇa") without monastic-lineage specification; his Tendai affiliation is inferred from the doctrinal commitments of the preface. The Japanese name "Jikkan" is the standard rendering; he should not be confused with the Tang Chinese commentator *Shíguān* 實觀 attested in earlier *Lǎn-bō hé-shàng* literature.
