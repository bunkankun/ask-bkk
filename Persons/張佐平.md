# Zhāng Zuǒpíng 張佐平

An early-Qīng lay Buddhist (居士) referred to with the honorific *kāishì* 開士 in the preface to Xùfǎ's *Fódǐng zūnshèng tuóluóní jīng shì* ([[KR6j0733]]); he funded the printing and distribution of that commentary.
