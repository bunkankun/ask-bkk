Zhēnzài 真在, *hào* **Shānduó** 山鐸 ("Mountain-Bell"), *biézì* **Yúnān** 筠庵 ("Bamboo-Shoot Hut"). LínjìYángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jièān Wùjìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673) — thus a co-disciple of **Sāntǎ Zhǔfēng** [[圓法]] (cf. [[KR6q0532]]) and **Huánglián Dōngyán** [[真璨]] (cf. [[KR6q0533]]). Born **明天啟辛酉 11/19** = **1621-12-31** Gregorian; died **康熙壬子 9/15 辰時** = **1672-11-04**, age 52, *sēnglà* 28. Native of **Jiāngzhōu Déhuàxiàn Sāngluò** 江州德化縣桑落 (Jiǔjiāng region, Jiāngxī); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Parents died early. Took tonsure at **Dōnglínsì** 東林寺 (Lúshān) under elder **Zìzhēn** 自真 — the latter, the *qísù* of Dōnglín, was reluctant to tonsure others. While reading the *Gāofēng yǔlù* doubted the "*tuō sǐshī*" 拖死屍 (dragging the corpse) phrase. Wandered through many monasteries. Final entry into the room of **Jièān** Wùjìn — recognized at first sight as if from old acquaintance — broke through after several exchanges and was sealed with the *yuánliú* (lineage chart).

**Abbacies.** **Lúshān Dōnglín Xīnglóngyuàn** 廬山東林興龍禪院 (Jiǔjiāng); **ChǔQí Huáguì Néngrénsì** 楚蘄華桂能仁寺 (Húběi); **Huángān Bìyúnshān Lóngxīng chányuàn** 黃安碧雲山龍興禪院.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* include **Tiānzé Néng** 天則能, **Yǐshān Wén** 以山聞 (also 以山電), **Shíyuán Jīyún** [[機雲]] 石源機雲, **Huìyuè Zhìxù** 晦岳智旭 [[智旭|Huìyuè]] (the Línjì-lineage master, NOT to be confused with the famous Ŏuyì Zhìxù), **Jīnéng** 機能, and others.

**Death.** Predicted his death seven months ahead. On **9/8** entered town to take leave of the lay community and other elders; **9/11** returned to the mountain; **9/12** mounted the dharma-seat and gave final instructions; **9/15** at the *chén* hour bathed, lit incense, sat, wrote a *jì*, and passed.

**Works.**
- 《徑石滴乳集》 [[KR6q0255]] — five-juan Chán-lineage biographical anthology compiled by Zhēnzài and continued (*xù*) by his dharma-heir **Shíyuán Jīyún** [[機雲]].
- [[KR6q0534]] 《山鐸真在禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B414, recorded by his dharma-heirs **Jīyún** [[機雲]] and **Huìyuè Zhìxù** [[智旭]].

**Sources.** 《山鐸真在禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行狀〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 81; 《徑石滴乳集》 vol. 5.
