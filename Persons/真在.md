Zhēn-zài 真在, *hào* **Shān-duó** 山鐸 ("Mountain-Bell"), *bié-zì* **Yún-ān** 筠庵 ("Bamboo-Shoot Hut"). Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673) — thus a co-disciple of **Sān-tǎ Zhǔ-fēng** [[圓法]] (cf. [[KR6q0532]]) and **Huáng-lián Dōng-yán** [[真璨]] (cf. [[KR6q0533]]). Born **明天啟辛酉 11/19** = **1621-12-31** Gregorian; died **康熙壬子 9/15 辰時** = **1672-11-04**, age 52, *sēng-là* 28. Native of **Jiāng-zhōu Dé-huà-xiàn Sāng-luò** 江州德化縣桑落 (Jiǔ-jiāng region, Jiāng-xī); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Parents died early. Took tonsure at **Dōng-lín-sì** 東林寺 (Lú-shān) under elder **Zì-zhēn** 自真 — the latter, the *qí-sù* of Dōng-lín, was reluctant to tonsure others. While reading the *Gāo-fēng yǔ-lù* doubted the "*tuō sǐ-shī*" 拖死屍 (dragging the corpse) phrase. Wandered through many monasteries. Final entry into the room of **Jiè-ān** Wù-jìn — recognized at first sight as if from old acquaintance — broke through after several exchanges and was sealed with the *yuán-liú* (lineage chart).

**Abbacies.** **Lú-shān Dōng-lín Xīng-lóng-yuàn** 廬山東林興龍禪院 (Jiǔ-jiāng); **Chǔ-Qí Huá-guì Néng-rén-sì** 楚蘄華桂能仁寺 (Hú-běi); **Huáng-ān Bì-yún-shān Lóng-xīng chán-yuàn** 黃安碧雲山龍興禪院.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎ-sì* include **Tiān-zé Néng** 天則能, **Yǐ-shān Wén** 以山聞 (also 以山電), **Shí-yuán Jī-yún** [[機雲]] 石源機雲, **Huì-yuè Zhì-xù** 晦岳智旭 [[智旭|Huì-yuè]] (the Línjì-lineage master, NOT to be confused with the famous Ŏuyì Zhìxù), **Jī-néng** 機能, and others.

**Death.** Predicted his death seven months ahead. On **9/8** entered town to take leave of the lay community and other elders; **9/11** returned to the mountain; **9/12** mounted the dharma-seat and gave final instructions; **9/15** at the *chén* hour bathed, lit incense, sat, wrote a *jì*, and passed.

**Works.**
- 《徑石滴乳集》 [[KR6q0255]] — five-juan Chán-lineage biographical anthology compiled by Zhēn-zài and continued (*xù*) by his dharma-heir **Shí-yuán Jī-yún** [[機雲]].
- [[KR6q0534]] 《山鐸真在禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B414, recorded by his dharma-heirs **Jī-yún** [[機雲]] and **Huì-yuè Zhì-xù** [[智旭]].

**Sources.** 《山鐸真在禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行狀〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 81; 《徑石滴乳集》 vol. 5.
