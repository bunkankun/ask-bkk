Northern-Sòng scholar-official and lexicographer, *zì* Yǒng-nián 永年, of Nán-chéng 南城 (Jiāngnán-xī, modern Jiangxi). Granted *jìn-shì* status under Tài-zōng (Tài-píng-xīng-guó 8 / 983), he rose through the Hànlín to senior court positions including *Hànlín xuéshì* and *Lǐ-bù shàngshū*. He is the principal editor of two foundational Northern-Sòng lexicographic projects: the *Chóng-xiū Yùpiān* [[KR1j0022]] / *Dà-guǎng yì-huì Yùpiān* [[KR1j0056]] of Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 6 (1013), thoroughly revising Sūn Qiáng's [[孫強]] Táng recension; and the *Chóng-xiū Guǎng-yùn* [[KR1j0055]] (Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 1, 1008), which converts Lù Fǎ-yán's Suí-period *Qiè-yùn* and Sūn Miǎn's Táng-period *Táng-yùn* into the canonical Sòng rhyme-book — for the next 800 years the standard reference in Chinese phonology. His collaborator Qiū Yōng [[丘雍]] worked alongside him on both projects. CBDB lifedates 961–1017, in agreement with the *Sòng-shǐ* biography (juàn 287).
