Northern-Sòng scholar-official and lexicographer, *zì* Yǒngnián 永年, of Nánchéng 南城 (Jiāngnánxī, modern Jiangxi). Granted *jìnshì* status under Tàizōng (Tàipíngxīngguó 8 / 983), he rose through the Hànlín to senior court positions including *Hànlín xuéshì* and *Lǐbù shàngshū*. He is the principal editor of two foundational Northern-Sòng lexicographic projects: the *Chóngxiū Yùpiān* [[KR1j0022]] / *Dàguǎng yìhuì Yùpiān* [[KR1j0056]] of Dàzhōngxiángfú 6 (1013), thoroughly revising Sūn Qiáng's [[孫強]] Táng recension; and the *Chóngxiū Guǎngyùn* [[KR1j0055]] (Dàzhōngxiángfú 1, 1008), which converts Lù Fǎyán's Suí-period *Qièyùn* and Sūn Miǎn's Táng-period *Tángyùn* into the canonical Sòng rhyme-book — for the next 800 years the standard reference in Chinese phonology. His collaborator Qiū Yōng [[丘雍]] worked alongside him on both projects. CBDB lifedates 961–1017, in agreement with the *Sòngshǐ* biography (juàn 287).
