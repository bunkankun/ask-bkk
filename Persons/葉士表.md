Yè Shìbiǎo 葉士表 (zì Wénshū 文叔; fl. 1161) was a Southern-Sòng commentator on Zhāng Bóduān's 張伯端 *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇. His commentary, dated *Shàoxīng* 31 (1161), is the earliest known commentary on the *Wùzhēn piān*; it is preserved within the *Wùzhēn piān* of [[KR5a0268]] (juan 26–30 of the *Xiūzhēn shíshū*, DZ 263e). The commentary, framed in terms of the *dàyǎn* 大衍 numerical system of the *Xìcí* 繫辭 appendix to the *Yìjīng*, was criticized by Wēng Bǎoguāng 翁葆光 in the latter's preface of 1173 (cf. [[KR5d0141|*Zǐyáng zhēnrén Wùzhēn piān zhùshū*]] preface 8b). Yè's original commentary ended with a series of diagrams entitled *Wùzhēn piān wàizhuàn* 悟真篇外傳 (now incorporated into the present text without authorial attribution). No biographical information is preserved beyond what is given in the prefaces.
