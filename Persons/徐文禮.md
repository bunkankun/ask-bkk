**Xú Wénlǐ** 徐文禮 (*fl.* Qián-dào period, 1165–1173, 宋), Southern-Sòng compiler and titler of [[KR3ek003]] *Wèi-jì bǎo-shū* 衛濟寶書. The work's short *hòu-xù* 後序 explains that Xú was summoned by an unnamed prefect (*tài-shǒu*) to debate the treatment of dorsal *bèi-zhèng* 背證 with the Xīn'ān imperial-attendant Wáng *tài-jiān* 新安王太監; on the strength of the discussion Xú was charged with collating the manuscript and gave it its present title. No further biographical record. Not in CBDB.
