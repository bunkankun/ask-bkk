**Xú Wénlǐ** 徐文禮 (*fl.* Qiándào period, 1165–1173, 宋), Southern-Sòng compiler and titler of [[KR3ek003]] *Wèijì bǎoshū* 衛濟寶書. The work's short *hòuxù* 後序 explains that Xú was summoned by an unnamed prefect (*tàishǒu*) to debate the treatment of dorsal *bèizhèng* 背證 with the Xīn'ān imperial-attendant Wáng *tàijiān* 新安王太監; on the strength of the discussion Xú was charged with collating the manuscript and gave it its present title. No further biographical record. Not in CBDB.
