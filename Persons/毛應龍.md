Máo Yìnglóng 毛應龍 (Yuán, *fl.* Dàdé era ca. 1297–1307; CBDB id 100599 has *fl. earliest* 1302). *Zì* Jièshí 介石. Native of Yùzhāng 豫章 (modern Nánchāng, Jiāngxī). According to Zhāng Xuān's 張萱 *Nèigé shūmù*, he held office as Lǐzhōu jiàoshòu 澧州教授 (Education Officer of Lǐzhōu) during the Dàdé era. Jiāngxī local gazetteers no longer preserve his name; the rest of his career is lost.

Author of [[KR1d0014]] *Zhōuguān jíchuán* 周官集傳 — a major Yuán anthology-commentary on the *Zhōulǐ* (KR1d0001) in 24 juan (16 surviving from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*; *Dìguān* and *Xiàguān* lost). Particularly draws on Zhèng È 鄭鍔, Xú-shì 徐氏 (*yīnbiàn* 音辨), and Ōuyáng Qiānzhī 歐陽謙之. He also wrote a *Zhōuguān huòwèn* 周官或問 in 5 juan, which the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* preserves only in fragments (19 entries on *Tiānguān*, 14 on *Chūnguān*, 1 each on *Qiūguān* and *Kǎogōngjì*) — these survive appended to the corresponding sections of his *Jíchuán*.
