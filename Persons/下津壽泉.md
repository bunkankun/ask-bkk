**Shimotsu Jusen** 下津壽泉 (Chinese reading: Xià-jīn Shòu-quán), Edo-period Japanese paediatrician of Setsuyō 攝陽 (Settsu province, modern Ōsaka region). His paediatric handbook *Yòu-kē zhèng-zhì dà-quán* 幼科證治大全 ([[KR3ej041]]) was compiled at the beginning of the eighteenth century and prefaced *Hōei* 6 *jǐ-chǒu suì dōng shí-yuè jí-dàn* 寶永六年己丑歲冬十月吉旦 = 1709 winter tenth month auspicious dawn at *Sè-yáng* (Setsuyō). The work draws on Chinese paediatric authorities (the *Yī-lín* 醫林, Xuē-shì, the *Yī-tǒng* 醫統, *Dà-quán* 大全, *Hǎo-gǔ* 好古, and others) along with Japanese clinical commentary, including the *Nán-fēng Lǐ-shì* 南豐李氏 and *Dōng-jǐng wēng* 東井翁 — both probably Edo-period Japanese physicians of the *kobō-ha* circle.
