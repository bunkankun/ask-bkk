**Shimotsu Jusen** 下津壽泉 (Chinese reading: Xiàjīn Shòuquán), Edo-period Japanese paediatrician of Setsuyō 攝陽 (Settsu province, modern Ōsaka region). His paediatric handbook *Yòukē zhèngzhì dàquán* 幼科證治大全 ([[KR3ej041]]) was compiled at the beginning of the eighteenth century and prefaced *Hōei* 6 *jǐchǒu suì dōng shíyuè jídàn* 寶永六年己丑歲冬十月吉旦 = 1709 winter tenth month auspicious dawn at *Sèyáng* (Setsuyō). The work draws on Chinese paediatric authorities (the *Yīlín* 醫林, Xuēshì, the *Yītǒng* 醫統, *Dàquán* 大全, *Hǎogǔ* 好古, and others) along with Japanese clinical commentary, including the *Nánfēng Lǐshì* 南豐李氏 and *Dōngjǐng wēng* 東井翁 — both probably Edo-period Japanese physicians of the *kobō-ha* circle.
