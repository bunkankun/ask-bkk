Luó Kāilín 羅開驎 (variant 羅開麟), *hào* **Kōngzhū jūshì** 空諸居士 ("Layman of Emptied All"), monastic name **Zhēnxìng** 真性. Lay dharma-heir of **Jièān Wùjìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673), thus a lay co-disciple of [[圓法]], [[真璨]], [[真在]], [[真雄]]. Native of **Zuìlǐ** 檇李 (Jiāxīng); lifedates unknown.

**Formation.** As a youth attended **Jìngwèiān** 敬畏菴 with his father (*Héngyánggōng* 衡陽公) at a *shàngtáng* by **Shíchē chánshī** 石車禪師, but did not yet credit Chán teaching. Both parents died in **順治丙戌** = **1646**, and he wandered in great hardship; his elder brother-in-law **Shī Yuēān** 施約菴 repeatedly drew him toward the *zōng* tradition. Together they visited **Dōngtǎ Qí** 東塔奇, then **Fúyán Róng** 福嚴容, finally **Jīnmíng Wùjìn** 介菴, from whom he received *fùfǎ*.

**Works.**
- 《華嚴疏鈔》 (a redaction of the *Avataṃsaka* commentarial corpus)
- 《金剛摸像》 (a 《Vajracchedikā》-related work)
- 《宗門拈頌》 (Chán-school *niānsòng* anthology)

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the principal preface to [[KR6q0230]] 《調實居士證源錄》 of his lineage-brother [[陸瑛]] (Kāngxī 30 = **1691** autumn). Author of one of the prefaces to [[KR6q0247]] 《普光坦菴禪師語錄》 (*Pǔguāng Tǎnān*: 機峻說 全弘錄). Author of one of the two prefaces to [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 (with [[機雲]]).

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 81; 《徑石滴乳集》 vol. 5.
