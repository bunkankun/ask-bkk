Yú Gāo 兪皋 (the catalog uses the graph 兪; the standard form is 俞 — the two are interchangeable graphic variants), *zì* Xīnyuǎn 心遠, native of Xīnān 新安 (Huīzhōu 徽州, modern Ānhuī). Yuán dynasty. Lifedates not preserved. He studied with his fellow-villager Zhào Liángjūn 趙良鈞, a *jìnshì* of the late Sòng who taught the *Chūnqiū* in the Xīnān region after refusing Yuán office, and inherited Zhào's scholarly legacy. Wú Chéng [[吳澄]] (1249–1331) wrote the preface to Yú Gāo's *Chūnqiū jízhuàn shìyì dàchéng* 春秋集傳釋義大成 [[KR1e0056]], which dates the work to before Wú's death in 1331. Wú's preface is also the earliest source for the term *sìzhuàn* 四傳 — the *fourth-commentary* status of Hú Ānguó's [[KR1e0036]] *Chūnqiū zhuàn* alongside the canonical three. Yú's only surviving work is the *Jízhuàn shìyì dàchéng*. CBDB has him listed as person 44224 with no biographical details.
