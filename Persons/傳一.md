Chuán-yī 傳一, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Huì-gōng Kāi-xiòng** [[開詗]] 慧弓開詗 (1634–1676/77) at **Nán-hǎi Bǎo-xiàng-lín** 南海寶象林 (Guǎng-dōng). Lead recorder of the 6-juan 《南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0484]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
