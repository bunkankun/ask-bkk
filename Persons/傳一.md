Chuányī 傳一, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Huìgōng Kāixiòng** [[開詗]] 慧弓開詗 (1634–1676/77) at **Nánhǎi Bǎoxiànglín** 南海寶象林 (Guǎngdōng). Lead recorder of the 6-juan 《南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0484]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
