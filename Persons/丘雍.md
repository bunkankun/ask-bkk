Northern-Sòng court philologist, *fl.* 984. Held the office *zhí Jí-xián-yuàn* 直集賢院. Worked with Chén Péng-nián [[陳彭年]] on the imperial revisions of the *Yùpiān* [[KR1j0022]] (Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 6, 1013) and the *Guǎngyùn* [[KR1j0055]] (Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 1, 1008) — two of the foundational Northern-Sòng reference works. Beyond these collaborative editorships no further biographical details are recoverable; CBDB carries one entry (#161) with *fl.* date 984.
