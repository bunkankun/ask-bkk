Kōng-qíng 空情. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* (or *shì-zhě*) of **Dà-xiào Xìng-chóng** [[崇]] (b. 1634) at **Wàn-xiàn Bǎo-jī-sì** 萬縣寶積寺 / **Zhōng-zhōu Gāo-fēng chán-yuàn** 忠州高峰禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0557]] 《大笑崇禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B442, prefaced 1690–1692.
