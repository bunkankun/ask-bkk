Kōngqíng 空情. LínjìYángqí *fǎsì* (or *shìzhě*) of **Dàxiào Xìngchóng** [[崇]] (b. 1634) at **Wànxiàn Bǎojīsì** 萬縣寶積寺 / **Zhōngzhōu Gāofēng chányuàn** 忠州高峰禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*lù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0557]] 《大笑崇禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B442, prefaced 1690–1692.
