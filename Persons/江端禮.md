Jiāng Duānlǐ 江端禮 (1060–1097) was a Northern-Sòng classicist and disciple of Xú Jī 徐積. Native of Mián-zhōu 綿州 (Sì-chuān). Held minor provincial posts; died at thirty-seven. He is the editor of the *Jié-xiào yǔ-lù* (KR3a0035), the *yǔ-lù* of his master Xú Jī, completed during Xú's last years (early 1090s). The work matches the *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* in its one-juan extent, and is the major preservation of Xú Jī's oral teaching. CBDB id 13384, dates 1060–1097.
