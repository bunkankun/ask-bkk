Jǐ Yún 紀昀 (1724–1805), zì Xiǎolán 曉嵐 (also Chūnfān 春帆), hào Shíyún 石雲, posthumous title Wéndá 文達, was a Zhílì 直隸 (Xiànxiàn 獻縣) scholar-official who became chief editor (*zǒngzuǎn guān* 總纂官) of the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 (1773–1782) and principal author of the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào* 四庫全書總目提要. He took the *jìnshì* in 1754, served as Hànlín bachelor and over a long career as Lǐbù shàngshū 禮部尚書 and Xiéban dàxuéshì 協辦大學士. His private *bǐjì* the *Yuèwēi cǎotáng bǐjì* 閱微草堂筆記 is among the most influential Qīng strange-tale collections. Almost every WYG *tíyào* in this knowledgebase was finalized under his supervision. CBDB dates from standard biographies; *Qīngshǐ gǎo* 清史稿 j.320.
