# Qián Yī-ān 錢伊庵

Late-Qīng lay Buddhist scholar. *Zì* Dōngfǔ 東甫; *hào* Yī-ān 伊庵 ("Yi-Hermitage"). Native of Wǔlín 武林 (Hángzhōu). Birth year unrecorded; died Dàoguāng 17 (1837).

A lay practitioner (*fēi sēng* 非僧) with sustained serious engagement in Chán and Pure Land traditions. Studied Chán under Fùchūn Shàn Huá-zàng 富春單華藏, a Zhèjiāng Chán lay teacher. Widely read in both Chán and Pure Land; active in the late-Qīng Buddhist lay-intellectual culture centred around the Jiāngnán printing houses.

Principal works:
- *Zōng fàn* 宗範 [[KR6q0170]] — his 1835-published two-juan systematic Chán practice-manual, later reprinted by [[楊文會|Yáng Wénhuì]] at the Jīnlíng Kè-jīng chù in 1886.
- Preface to the 1820 (Jiāqìng 24) reprint of the *Chè-wù dàshī yíjí* 徹悟大師遺集 — evidencing his participation in the late-Qīng Chán–Pure Land integrative revival tradition of Chè-wù Jìlóng 徹悟際龍 (1742–1810).

The *Zōng fàn*'s late-Qīng reception in Yáng Wénhuì's Jīnlíng Kè-jīng chù printing programme helped establish it as one of the canonical practical-meditative manuals for the early-Republican-era Chinese Buddhist revival.
