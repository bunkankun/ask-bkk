# Fǎxūn 法薰

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Shítián 石田 ("Stone-Field"). Native of Méishān 眉山 in Shǔ. Per the *xíngzhuàng* appended to his *yǔlù*: 1171 – Chúnyòu 5.1.11 (16 February 1245), *shì* 75.

Dharma-heir of [[祖先]] Pòān Zǔxiān (1136–1211); senior brother-in-dharma of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249). Abbacies (in order): Píngjiāng Gāofēng, Píngjiāng Féngqiáo Pǔmíng, Jiànkāng Jiāngshān Tàipíng Xīngguó, Lín'ān Jìngcí (whence Jìngcí Shítián), and Lín'ān Língyǐn (whence Língyǐn Fǎxūn). Recorded sayings survive as *Shítián Fǎxūn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0319]]), compiled c. 1246–1247 by his disciples [[了覺]] and [[師坦]].
