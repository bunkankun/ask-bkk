# Fǎ-xūn 法薰

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Shí-tián 石田 ("Stone-Field"). Native of Méi-shān 眉山 in Shǔ. Per the *xíng-zhuàng* appended to his *yǔlù*: 1171 – Chúnyòu 5.1.11 (16 February 1245), *shì* 75.

Dharma-heir of [[祖先]] Pò-ān Zǔ-xiān (1136–1211); senior brother-in-dharma of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249). Abbacies (in order): Píngjiāng Gāo-fēng, Píngjiāng Féng-qiáo Pǔ-míng, Jiàn-kāng Jiāng-shān Tài-píng Xīng-guó, Lín'ān Jìng-cí (whence Jìng-cí Shí-tián), and Lín'ān Líng-yǐn (whence Líng-yǐn Fǎ-xūn). Recorded sayings survive as *Shí-tián Fǎ-xūn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0319]]), compiled c. 1246–1247 by his disciples [[了覺]] and [[師坦]].
