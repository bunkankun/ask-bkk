**Zhāng Suìchén** 張遂辰 (*zì* Qīngzǐ 卿子, also Xiāngqī 相期; *hào* Xīnóng Lǎorén 西農老人, 1589–1668, late Míng – early Qīng), Hángzhōu 杭州 scholar-physician, a key figure of the early-Qīng Jiāngnán medical scene. He served as a teacher to the next generation of Hángzhōu physicians — most notably [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù (1617–1700), through whom his school dominated the early-Qīng SūzhōuHángzhōu medical curriculum. Zhāng Suìchén is the supplementary annotator of the *Zhāng Qīngzǐ Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef006]]), preserving Chéng Wúyǐ's commentary against the Anhui revisionist line, and the author of a *Sìzhěn fǎ* 四診法 (lost) and a *Zhāngshì yī tōng* 張氏醫通 (not the later *Yī tōng* of Zhāng Lù). He is buried near Xīhú in Hángzhōu. No CBDB record.
