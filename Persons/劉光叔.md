# 劉光叔

Liú Guāngshū 劉光叔, fl. mid-thirteenth-century Sòng. Father-in-law of [[方實孫]] Fāng Shísūn, the *Yì*-scholar who composed the *Cóngshān dú Zhōuyì* ([[KR1a0060]]).
