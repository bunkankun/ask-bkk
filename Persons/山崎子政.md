# 山崎子政 Yamazaki Shisei

**Yamazaki Shisei** 山崎子政 (given name **Yamazaki Zen** 山崎善, *Igaku kyōyu* 醫學教諭 = court physician), Edo-period Japanese physician and cousin of [[原昌克]] Hara Masakatsu; wrote a preface to Hara's *Jīngxué huìjiě* 經穴匯解 ([[KR3ee022]], 1803).
