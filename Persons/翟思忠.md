Yuán-period official and *Rúguān* 儒官 from Xiàpī 下邳. He served at the beginning of the *Zhìshùn* 至順 reign (1330–1333) as Prefect of Chángzhōu 常州 (*Chángzhōu zhīshì* 常州知事). He authored the *Wèi Zhènggōng jiàn xùlù* 魏鄭公諫續錄 in two *juàn* ([[KR2g0012]]), a continuation of the Táng *Jiànlù* of Wèi Zhēng compiled at his Chángzhōu post; the work was first cut in *Yuántǒng* (1333–1335). He also composed an *Yì zhuàn yǎntàixuán* 易傳衍太玄 (an *Yìjīng* commentary in the manner of Yáng Xióng's *Tàixuán*), which has not survived separately. The *Sìkù* editors describe him as "indeed a man of learning fond of the antique."
