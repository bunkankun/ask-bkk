Yuán-period official and *Rú-guān* 儒官 from Xià-pī 下邳. He served at the beginning of the *Zhì-shùn* 至順 reign (1330–1333) as Prefect of Cháng-zhōu 常州 (*Cháng-zhōu zhī-shì* 常州知事). He authored the *Wèi Zhèng-gōng jiàn xù-lù* 魏鄭公諫續錄 in two *juàn* ([[KR2g0012]]), a continuation of the Táng *Jiàn-lù* of Wèi Zhēng compiled at his Cháng-zhōu post; the work was first cut in *Yuán-tǒng* (1333–1335). He also composed an *Yì zhuàn yǎn-tài-xuán* 易傳衍太玄 (an *Yìjīng* commentary in the manner of Yáng Xióng's *Tài-xuán*), which has not survived separately. The *Sì-kù* editors describe him as "indeed a man of learning fond of the antique."
