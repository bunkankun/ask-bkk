Yè Cǎi 葉采 (Sòng), *zì* Zhòngguī 仲圭, *hào* Píngyán 平巖, was a late-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* commentator. Native of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Fújiàn) — the same county as Zhū Xī's intellectual base. *Jìnshì* of Chúnyòu 1 (1241); held the post of *Bìshūjiān* 秘書監 in the imperial library. He is the author of the *Jìnsī lù jíjiě* 近思錄集解 — the standard premodern collected commentary on the *Jìnsī lù* ([[KR3a0042]]), drawing on prior commentary by Yè Mèngdé 葉夢得 (his ancestor) and supplying his own integration. The *jíjiě* was completed in Bǎoyòu 5 (1257). The integrated *Jìnsī lù* + *jíjiě* is the SKQS-base reproduced under [[KR3a0042]]. CBDB id 27447, no dates.
