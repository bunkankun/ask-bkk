Yè Cǎi 葉采 (Sòng), *zì* Zhòngguī 仲圭, *hào* Píngyán 平巖, was a late-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* commentator. Native of Jiàn-yáng 建陽 (Fú-jiàn) — the same county as Zhū Xī's intellectual base. *Jìnshì* of Chún-yòu 1 (1241); held the post of *Bì-shū-jiān* 秘書監 in the imperial library. He is the author of the *Jìn-sī lù jí-jiě* 近思錄集解 — the standard premodern collected commentary on the *Jìn-sī lù* (KR3a0042), drawing on prior commentary by Yè Mèngdé 葉夢得 (his ancestor) and supplying his own integration. The *jí-jiě* was completed in Bǎo-yòu 5 (1257). The integrated *Jìn-sī lù* + *jí-jiě* is the SKQS-base reproduced under KR3a0042. CBDB id 27447, no dates.
