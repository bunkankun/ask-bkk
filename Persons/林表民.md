# Lín Biǎomín 林表民 (fl. 1248)

*Zì* Féngjí 逢吉. Native of Línhǎi 臨海 in Táizhōu (Zhèjiāng); his ancestors had migrated to Línhǎi from Qǔfù in Lǔ (Shāndōng), hence Wú Zǐliáng's description of him as "*DōngLǔ rén*." He was the senior Southern-Sòng compiler of **Táizhōu regional anthologies**, responsible for both:
- the *Tiāntāi qiánjí* 天台前集 [[KR4h0048]] (joint *Tiāntāi* anthology supplementing Lǐ Gēng's earlier collection)
- the *Chìchéng jí* 赤城集 [[KR4h0049]] in 18 *juǎn* (later extended to a 28-*juǎn* form according to Xiè Duó's *Chìchéng xīnzhì*), a prose-and-poetry continuation of Chén Qíqìng's *Chìchéng zhì*. Wú Zǐliáng wrote the preface in *Chúnyòu* 8 (1248), fixing Lín's *floruit*. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
