# Lín Biǎo-mín 林表民 (fl. 1248)

*Zì* Féng-jí 逢吉. Native of Línhǎi 臨海 in Táizhōu (Zhèjiāng); his ancestors had migrated to Línhǎi from Qǔfù in Lǔ (Shāndōng), hence Wú Zǐ-liáng's description of him as "*Dōng-Lǔ rén*." He was the senior Southern-Sòng compiler of **Táizhōu regional anthologies**, responsible for both:
- the *Tiān-tāi qián-jí* 天台前集 [[KR4h0048]] (joint *Tiān-tāi* anthology supplementing Lǐ Gēng's earlier collection)
- the *Chì-chéng jí* 赤城集 [[KR4h0049]] in 18 *juǎn* (later extended to a 28-*juǎn* form according to Xiè Duó's *Chì-chéng xīn-zhì*), a prose-and-poetry continuation of Chén Qí-qìng's *Chì-chéng zhì*. Wú Zǐ-liáng wrote the preface in *Chún-yòu* 8 (1248), fixing Lín's *floruit*. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
