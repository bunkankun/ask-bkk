Xīn Chāngwǔ 辛昌五 (fl. mid-18th c.) was a Lǐngnán colleague of [[何夢瑤]] Hé Mèngyáo who contributed a preface to the 1751 first printing of the *Yī biàn* ([[KR3eh005]]).
