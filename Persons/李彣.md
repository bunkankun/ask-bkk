**Lǐ Wén** 李彣 (*zì* Zhènshēng 振聲, *hào* Xīyuán 西垣, *fl.* late seventeenth century, 清), early-Kāngxī physician. Student of Pān Dēnglín 潘鄧林. Author of the *Jīnguì yào lüè guǎng zhù* 金匱要略廣註 ([[KR3ef087]], 8 juan, dated Kāngxī 21 = 1682), one of the earliest sustained Qīng commentaries on the *Jīnguì yào lüè*, slightly later than [[徐彬]] Xú Bīn's *Lùn zhù* and Chéng Lín 程林's *Zhí jiě*. The "*guǎng zhù*" 廣註 (extensive annotation) refers to Lǐ's broad citation practice — drawing not only on the standard SòngYuánMíng medical authorities (徐之才, 朱肱, 許叔微, 張子和, 朱丹溪, 王履, 張兼善, 趙養葵, 樓全善, 王三陽, 喻嘉言, 郭稽中, 武之望, 陳藏器, 李時珍) but also on the *Yì jīng* 易經, *Shàng shū* 尚書, and the military classic *Hǔ qián jīng* 虎鈐經. The work survives in a single Kāngxī 21 (1682) blockprint and several manuscript copies; it was not widely circulated in the Qīng but was cited by the editors of the *Yī zōng jīn jiàn* 醫宗金鑑 ([[KR3ef086]]) and noted by Tamba no Motokata in his bibliographical survey ([[KR3ef085]]). Detailed lifedates are not preserved.
