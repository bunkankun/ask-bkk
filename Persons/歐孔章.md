**Ōu Kǒngzhāng** 歐孔章 of Hùxī 滬西 — Qīng patron and protégé of [[蔡益亭]] Cài Yìtíng. Commissioned the 1856 (Xiánfēng *bǐngchén*) wood-block edition of [[單南山]] Shàn Nánshān's *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* ([[KR3ei053]]) at his family's Zhōngxiàn dì 中憲第 mansion, with [[陳彩盅]] Chén Cǎizhōng as editor. Not in CBDB.
