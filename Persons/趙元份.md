# 趙元份

Zhào Yuánfèn 趙元份 (969–1005), Northern-Sòng prince Shāngwáng 商王, fourth son of Sòng Tàizōng 宋太宗. Cited in the Kanripo corpus as the seventh-generation ancestor of the imperial-clan *Yì*-scholar [[趙汝楳]] Zhào Rǔméi (author of [[KR1a0058]]).
