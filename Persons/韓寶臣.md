**Hán Bǎochén** 韓寶臣 (*fl.* late Qīng), Qīng *cìshǐ* 刺史 (Prefect); supplier of the manuscript source-text used in the preparation of the *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē gēkuò* 傅青主女科歌括 ([[KR3ei021]]), passed to the prefacer via the editor [[祈爾誠]] Qí Ěrchéng.
