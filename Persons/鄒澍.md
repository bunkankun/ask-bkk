**Zōu Shù** 鄒澍 (1790–1844), *zì* Rùn'ān 潤安 (also written 閏庵), *hào* Shùzhāi 恕齋. Native of Wǔjìn 武進 (Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū) — central figure of the Chángzhōu *jīngfāng* / pharmacology circle of the first half of the 19th century. Lifelong private scholar-physician; never held office. His magnum opus is the *Běnjīng shū zhèng* 本經疏證 ([[KR3ec066]], 12 juan, printed 1837) — a substance-by-substance critical exegesis of the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* read systematically against Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *ShānghánJīnguì* formula usage. It is the most influential 19th-century Chinese pharmacology and remains a standard *jīngfāng* reference. He also wrote the *Běnjīng xù shū* 本經續疏 (6 juan, supplementary cases) and *Běnjīng shū zhèng yī cí* 本經疏證遺辭. Friend and preface-writer for Yáng Shítài's [[楊時泰]] *Běncǎo shù gōuyuán* ([[KR3ec052]]).
