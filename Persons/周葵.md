# Zhōu Kuí 周葵

Zhōu Kuí 周葵 (1098–1174), Southern-Sòng official who served as Participant-in-State-Affairs (*cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事); wrote the pagoda inscription (*tǎmíng* 塔銘) for Hóngzhì Zhèngjué, recorded in the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
