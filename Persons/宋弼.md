Early-mid Qīng official and literary disciple of Wáng Shìzhēn [[王士禎]]; native of Shāndōng. *Zì* Zhòngliáng 仲良; *hào* Méngquán 蒙泉. CBDB (personid 79809) gives lifedates 1703–1768. *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 2 (1737); held a series of provincial posts, ultimately rising to Surveillance Commissioner (按察使 Àncháshǐ) of Gānsù. Known principally for his role in transmitting and supplementing Wáng Shìzhēn's unfinished *Wǔdài shīhuà* 五代詩話 ([[KR4i0064]]): Sòng Bì received Wáng's draft from disciple-circle transmission, supplemented the lacunae, and printed the work. The *Sìkù* editors register that his supplementation was limited by his strict adherence to Wáng's bibliographic horizon ("*fēi suǒ zhù zhī shū bù dú*" — he would not read books his master had not read). His own collected works include the *Méngquán shī chāo* 蒙泉詩鈔 and *Méngquán wén chāo*.
