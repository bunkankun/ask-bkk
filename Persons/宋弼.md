Early-mid Qīng official and literary disciple of Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]]; native of Shāndōng. *Zì* Zhòng-liáng 仲良; *hào* Méng-quán 蒙泉. CBDB (personid 79809) gives lifedates 1703–1768. *Jìnshì* of Qián-lóng 2 (1737); held a series of provincial posts, ultimately rising to Surveillance Commissioner (按察使 Àn-chá-shǐ) of Gānsù. Known principally for his role in transmitting and supplementing Wáng Shì-zhēn's unfinished *Wǔ-dài shī-huà* 五代詩話 ([[KR4i0064]]): Sòng Bì received Wáng's draft from disciple-circle transmission, supplemented the lacunae, and printed the work. The *Sìkù* editors register that his supplementation was limited by his strict adherence to Wáng's bibliographic horizon ("*fēi suǒ zhù zhī shū bù dú*" — he would not read books his master had not read). His own collected works include the *Méng-quán shī chāo* 蒙泉詩鈔 and *Méng-quán wén chāo*.
