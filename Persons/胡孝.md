# Hú Xiào 胡孝

Late-Míng former *jìnshì* (前進士) who, after disengaging from official life, took up *yǒufǎ sēng* 有髮僧 ("haired-monk", i.e. lay-monastic) life at Wǔlín 武林 (Hángzhōu) under the dharma-name [[了幻]] Liǎohuàn. Collator (校) of [[謝觀光]] Xiè Guānguāng's *Bōrě xīnjīng shìyì* 般若心經釋義 ([[KR6c0159]]), Wànlì 15 (1587).
