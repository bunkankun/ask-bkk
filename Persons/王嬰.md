Wáng Yīng 王嬰 is a Jìn-period author known almost exclusively from the title-attribution on his lost *Gǔjīn tōnglùn* 古今通論 (preserved in the *Yìlín* 意林 of Mǎ Zǒng 馬總 and in Qú-tán Xī-dá's 瞿曇悉達 *Kāi-yuán zhàn jīng* 開元占經). No biography survives in the standard histories. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records the title under the Jìn period without further notes. Sòng catalogs occasionally attribute the work to a Liú-Sòng or Qí figure of the same name; the conventional placement under Jìn follows the *Suí shū* and is the placement adopted by the Kanripo title-list. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0131]] *Gǔjīn tōnglùn* 古今通論.
