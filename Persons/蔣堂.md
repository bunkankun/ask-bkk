Northern-Sòng prefect-administrator and minor poet, *zì* Xī-lǔ 希魯, posthumous *Wén-yì* 文懿, of *Yí-xīng* 宜興 (Cháng-zhōu). *Jìn-shì* of Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 5 / 1012. Under Rén-zōng held *Zuǒ-jiàn-yì-dà-fū zhī Sū-zhōu* (twice prefect of Sū-zhōu, where he supervised waterworks and garden-building), then *Jǐ-shì-zhōng* still serving as *zhōu*, then prefect of *Yáng-zhōu*, *Hé-nán*, and *Hú-zhōu*. Retired as *Lǐ-bù shì-láng* and settled in Sū-zhōu. Died in 1054 age 75. *Shén-dào bēi* by Hú Sù [[胡宿]] in his *Wén-gōng jí* [[KR4d0022]]. Original 20-*juǎn* collection lost; surviving 1-*juǎn* gleaning *Chūn-qīng yí-gǎo* [[KR4d0018]] reconstituted in late Míng *Tiān-qǐ* by his 20th-generation descendant Jiǎng Hóng [[蔣鐄]].
