Northern-Sòng prefect-administrator and minor poet, *zì* Xīlǔ 希魯, posthumous *Wényì* 文懿, of *Yíxīng* 宜興 (Chángzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Dàzhōngxiángfú 5 / 1012. Under Rénzōng held *Zuǒjiànyìdàfū zhī Sūzhōu* (twice prefect of Sūzhōu, where he supervised waterworks and garden-building), then *Jǐshìzhōng* still serving as *zhōu*, then prefect of *Yángzhōu*, *Hénán*, and *Húzhōu*. Retired as *Lǐbù shìláng* and settled in Sūzhōu. Died in 1054 age 75. *Shéndào bēi* by Hú Sù [[胡宿]] in his *Wéngōng jí* [[KR4d0022]]. Original 20-*juǎn* collection lost; surviving 1-*juǎn* gleaning *Chūnqīng yígǎo* [[KR4d0018]] reconstituted in late Míng *Tiānqǐ* by his 20th-generation descendant Jiǎng Hóng [[蔣鐄]].
