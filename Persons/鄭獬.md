Mid-Northern-Sòng *Hàn-lín xué-shì*, *zì* Yì-fū 毅夫, of *Ān-zhōu Ān-lù* 安州安陸 (Hú-běi). *Zhuàng-yuán* (top-place *jìn-shì*) of *Huáng-yòu* 5 / 1053. Held *Tōng-pàn Chén-zhōu*, *Zhí Jí-xián-yuàn Zhī-zhì-gào*; under Yīng-zōng repeatedly memorialized; sent out *zhī Jīng-nán*; under Shén-zōng called as *Hàn-lín xué-shì quán zhī Kāi-fēng-fǔ* (1068–1069). Refused to enforce Wáng Ān-shí's *Qīng-miáo* loan policy at Kāi-fēng-fǔ — one of the dramatic *Xī-níng* opposition confrontations of 1069. Demoted to *zhī Hángzhōu*, then *Qīng-zhōu*; pleaded illness for *Tí-jǔ Hóng-qìng-gōng* (sinecure) and died in *Xī-níng* 5 / 1072 age 51. Articulately placed his *gǔ-wén* in the Hán Yù lineage (against Lǐ Áo, Huáng-fǔ Shí, Zhāng Jí) in his correspondence with Liú Chǎng [[劉敞]]. Surviving collection: *Yún-xī jí* [[KR4d0050]] (28 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* against the original 50-*juǎn* recension cut by Qín Hú in *Chún-xī* 1186).
