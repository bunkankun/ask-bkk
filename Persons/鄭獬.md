Mid-Northern-Sòng *Hànlín xuéshì*, *zì* Yìfū 毅夫, of *Ānzhōu Ānlù* 安州安陸 (Húběi). *Zhuàngyuán* (top-place *jìnshì*) of *Huángyòu* 5 / 1053. Held *Tōngpàn Chénzhōu*, *Zhí Jíxiányuàn Zhīzhìgào*; under Yīngzōng repeatedly memorialized; sent out *zhī Jīngnán*; under Shénzōng called as *Hànlín xuéshì quán zhī Kāifēngfǔ* (1068–1069). Refused to enforce Wáng Ānshí's *Qīngmiáo* loan policy at Kāifēngfǔ — one of the dramatic *Xīníng* opposition confrontations of 1069. Demoted to *zhī Hángzhōu*, then *Qīngzhōu*; pleaded illness for *Tíjǔ Hóngqìnggōng* (sinecure) and died in *Xīníng* 5 / 1072 age 51. Articulately placed his *gǔwén* in the Hán Yù lineage (against Lǐ Áo, Huángfǔ Shí, Zhāng Jí) in his correspondence with Liú Chǎng [[劉敞]]. Surviving collection: *Yúnxī jí* [[KR4d0050]] (28 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* against the original 50-*juǎn* recension cut by Qín Hú in *Chúnxī* 1186).
