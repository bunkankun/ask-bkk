Zhèn-wén 振聞, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Qǐ-zhēn Chéng** [[誠]] 啟真誠 at **Jīng-nán Nèi Hù-guó-sì** 荊南內護國寺. Lead compiler of the 4-juan 《荊南內護國寺啟真誠禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0520]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
