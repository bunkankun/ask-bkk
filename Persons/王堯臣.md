# Wáng Yáochén 王堯臣

*Zì* Bóyōng 伯庸. Native of Yúchéng 虞城 (in Yīngtiānfǔ 應天府, modern Shāngqiū 商丘, Henán). *Zhuàngyuán* 狀元 (top of the *jìnshì* list) of the Tiānshèng 5 (1027) examination — the famously elite "*zǎizhí bǎng*" 宰執榜 cohort that included Hán Qí 韓琦 and Wén Yànbó 文彥博.

Rose through Hanlin academician (*Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士), acting Three Departments Commissioner (*Sānsī shǐ* 三司使), and finally Counsellor of State (*Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事) in 1056. Principal compiler, with [[王洙]] and [[歐陽修]], of the imperial library catalogue *Chóngwén zǒngmù* 崇文總目 ([[KR2n0001]], submitted 1041). Biography: *Sòngshǐ* 宋史 j. 292. His epitaph was written by Ōuyáng Xiū.

Lifedates: 1003–1058 per the *Sòngshǐ* and standard Chinese reference works. The project catalog meta gives 1001–1056, but 1003–1058 is the figure used throughout modern Sinophone scholarship and is followed here.
