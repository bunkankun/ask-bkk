**Xǔ Jǐnghéng** 許景衡 (1072–1128), *zì* Shàoyī 少伊, of Wēnzhōu Ruìān 瑞安. *Jìnshì* of *Yuányòu* 9 / 1094 (i.e., *Shàoshèng* 1). *Xuānhé* mid-period summoned as *Jiānchá yùshǐ* (Investigating Censor); promoted *Diànzhōng shìyùshǐ*. Qīnzōng's accession (1126): summoned as *Zuǒ zhèngyán* (Left Adviser of the Right); promoted *Zhōngshū shèrén*. Gāozōng's accession (1127): reached *Shàngshū yòuchéng* (Vice-Chancellor); dismissed as *Zīzhèngdiàn dàxuéshì*, *Tíjǔ Dòngxiāo gōng* (Vice-Director-State-Council Grand Scholar, Inspector of Dòngxiāo Temple); died (1128). Posthumously canonised *Zhōngjiǎn* 忠簡 (Loyal-and-Simple — same canonisation as Zōng Zé). One of the *Yǒngjiā* school's second generation, drawing on the *Sān xiānshēng* (Zhōu Xíngjǐ, Liú Ānjié, Liú Ānshàng) but politically distinct: opposed eunuch power (memorialised against Tóng Guàn), opposed the *huāshí gāng* (flower-and-stone supply train), and opposed the *Jīngkāng* peace-faction. Author of *Héngtáng jí* 橫塘集 [[KR4d0140]] originally in 30 *juǎn* (per *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì* and *Shūlù jiětí*); *Sìkù* reconstruction is 20 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 363. Lifedates 1072–1128 confirmed.
