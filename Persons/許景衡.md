**Xǔ Jǐng-héng** 許景衡 (1072–1128), *zì* Shào-yī 少伊, of Wēn-zhōu Ruì-ān 瑞安. *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-yòu* 9 / 1094 (i.e., *Shào-shèng* 1). *Xuān-hé* mid-period summoned as *Jiān-chá yù-shǐ* (Investigating Censor); promoted *Diàn-zhōng shì-yù-shǐ*. Qīnzōng's accession (1126): summoned as *Zuǒ zhèng-yán* (Left Adviser of the Right); promoted *Zhōng-shū shè-rén*. Gāozōng's accession (1127): reached *Shàng-shū yòu-chéng* (Vice-Chancellor); dismissed as *Zī-zhèng-diàn dà-xué-shì*, *Tí-jǔ Dòng-xiāo gōng* (Vice-Director-State-Council Grand Scholar, Inspector of Dòng-xiāo Temple); died (1128). Posthumously canonised *Zhōng-jiǎn* 忠簡 (Loyal-and-Simple — same canonisation as Zōng Zé). One of the *Yǒng-jiā* school's second generation, drawing on the *Sān xiān-shēng* (Zhōu Xíng-jǐ, Liú Ān-jié, Liú Ān-shàng) but politically distinct: opposed eunuch power (memorialised against Tóng Guàn), opposed the *huā-shí gāng* (flower-and-stone supply train), and opposed the *Jīng-kāng* peace-faction. Author of *Héng-táng jí* 橫塘集 [[KR4d0140]] originally in 30 *juǎn* (per *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén-zhì* and *Shū-lù jiě-tí*); *Sìkù* reconstruction is 20 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 363. Lifedates 1072–1128 confirmed.
