**Xióng Hù** 熊笏 (*zì* Shūlíng 叔陵, *fl.* c. 1810–1830, Qing physician of Ānyì 安義 in Jiāngxī). No CBDB record.

His one preserved work is the *Zhōng-fēng lùn* 中風論 ([[KR3eh035]], 1 *juǎn*) — a focused monograph on wind-stroke (*piān-kū* 偏枯 / *zhōng-fēng* 中風) — composed and prefaced *Dào-guāng xīn-sì* = *Dào-guāng* 1 = 1821 at the Shānyáng 山陽 / Huái'ān 淮安 prefectural office. The work circulated as manuscript only for sixty years and was first printed in *Guāng-xù jiǎ-shēn* = 1884 by Lín Qìng-qí 林慶祺 from a manuscript copy made in 1818 (*wù-yín*) by Guō Qiū-quán 郭秋泉 — a pupil of Chén Xiūyuán's circle who had clandestinely copied it from Xióng's box while the author was out, by his own postface admission. A sister text, *Jí-zhù Nán-jīng* 輯註難經, is mentioned in the prefaces but is otherwise unavailable.
