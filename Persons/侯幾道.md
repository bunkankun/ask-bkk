Hóu Jǐdào 侯幾道 (d. 1645), *zì* **Yúnjù** 雲俱; native of **Jiādìng** 嘉定. Disciple of [[黃淳耀]] (1605–1645) and one of his lifelong study-companions in the *xìngmìng* (nature-and-destiny) tradition, named alongside [[黃淵耀]] (Wěigōng) and Xià Qǐlín 夏啟霖 in [[吳偉業]]'s preface to the *Táoān quánjí* ([[KR4e0244]]) as part of the Jiādìng *dàoxué* circle. Died with master and friends in the *Jiādìng sāntú* of 1645. No CBDB match confirmed.
