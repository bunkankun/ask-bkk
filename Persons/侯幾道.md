Hóu Jǐ-dào 侯幾道 (d. 1645), *zì* **Yún-jù** 雲俱; native of **Jiā-dìng** 嘉定. Disciple of [[黃淳耀]] (1605–1645) and one of his lifelong study-companions in the *xìng-mìng* (nature-and-destiny) tradition, named alongside [[黃淵耀]] (Wěi-gōng) and Xià Qǐ-lín 夏啟霖 in [[吳偉業]]'s preface to the *Táo-ān quán-jí* ([[KR4e0244]]) as part of the Jiā-dìng *dào-xué* circle. Died with master and friends in the *Jiā-dìng sān-tú* of 1645. No CBDB match confirmed.
