"Dànyuán 澹園" (Serene Garden) is the pen name of the anonymous Qīng-dynasty author of [[KR4k0267]] *Yànzi Jiān* 燕子箋. No historical individual behind this pseudonym has been identified. The name follows the common practice of Qīng fiction writers adopting garden-studio names (*zhāihào* 齋號) as author attributions.
