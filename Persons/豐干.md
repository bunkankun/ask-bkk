# Fēnggān 豐干

Táng-era Chán eccentric, traditionally paired with [[寒山|Hánshān]] 寒山 and [[拾得|Shídé]] 拾得 at Mount Tiāntái 天臺 as one of the "Three Sages of Tiāntái" (*Tiāntái sān shèng* 天台三聖). Lifedates uncertain.
