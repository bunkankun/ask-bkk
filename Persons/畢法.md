**Bì Fǎ** 畢法 (*zì* Cānglín 蒼霖, *fl.* mid Qiánlóng – early Dàoguāng, 清), friend and surgical collaborator of [[許克昌]]. Author of an unfinished two-*juǎn* draft titled *Wàikē zhèngzhì* 外科證治, which he was unable to complete before his death (before age thirty, per Xǔ's preface). Xǔ Kèchāng took up the manuscript and, after some twenty-odd years of further clinical testing and expansion, produced the published [[KR3ek001]] *Wàikē zhèngzhì quánshū* in 5 *juǎn* (1831). Bì's contribution is the original *Wàikē zhèngzhì* kernel preserved as the core of *juǎn* 1–3 of the printed text. No further biographical record. Not in CBDB.
