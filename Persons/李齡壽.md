# 李齡壽

Lǐ Língshòu 李齡壽, *zì* Xīntuó 辛坨, of Yánzézhèn 盛澤鎮 in Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū). Late-Qīng physician of the Wújiāng Yè-school medical lineage; teacher of [[凌淦]] Líng Gàn. His case-records were transcribed in ten volumes by [[沈陳麟]] Shěn Chénlín.
