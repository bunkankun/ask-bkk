Chāo-zhí 超直, *hào* **Wèn-shí** 問石, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xū-zhōu Xíng-shěng** [[行省]] 虛舟行省 (1600–1668) at his Cí-xī / Hú-zhōu seats. Lead compiler of the 4-juan 《虛舟省禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0465]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Chāo-zhí děng biān* 超直等編. Per the second preface (Zhāng Wén-jiā), Wèn-shí showed exceptional dedication to the Chán community (*dān-dāng fǎ-mén yǒu qí-jié, yù jí-nán zhī shì zhé yì xíng yú sè* 擔當法門有奇節遇急難之事輒義形於色) and fundraised ardently for the Léng-yán-sì 楞嚴寺 publication (*nǎi jié-lì mù zǐ, cáng bǎn yú Hé-zhōng zhī Léng-yán* 乃竭力募梓藏板於禾中之楞嚴) to ensure the master's *yǔ-lù* circulation after his 1668 death. No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
