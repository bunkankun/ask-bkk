Chāozhí 超直, *hào* **Wènshí** 問石, LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Xūzhōu Xíngshěng** [[行省]] 虛舟行省 (1600–1668) at his Cíxī / Húzhōu seats. Lead compiler of the 4-juan 《虛舟省禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0465]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Chāozhí děng biān* 超直等編. Per the second preface (Zhāng Wénjiā), Wènshí showed exceptional dedication to the Chán community (*dāndāng fǎmén yǒu qíjié, yù jínán zhī shì zhé yì xíng yú sè* 擔當法門有奇節遇急難之事輒義形於色) and fundraised ardently for the Léngyánsì 楞嚴寺 publication (*nǎi jiélì mù zǐ, cáng bǎn yú Hézhōng zhī Léngyán* 乃竭力募梓藏板於禾中之楞嚴) to ensure the master's *yǔlù* circulation after his 1668 death. No independent *yǔlù* located.
