Early-Republican Húnán figure of Xiāngtán 湘潭; *hào* Shùntángshì 順堂氏. Wrote the principal preface (*Xù yī* 序一, autumn 1941) to [[劉本昌]] Liú Běnchāng's *Mài jué xīn biān* 脈訣新編 ([[KR3eb030]]), engaging the early-Republican "abolish-Chinese-medicine" debate and defending the *Nèijīng* vessel-and-channel doctrine. Not in CBDB; no further biographical material located.
