# 趙文龍

Zhào Wénlóng 趙文龍 (*fl.* 300 CE), the scribe (*bǐshòu* 筆受) named in the colophon of the *Xiánjié jīng* 賢劫經 ([[KR6i0001]]) at the close of the eighth fascicle: he recorded [[竺法護]]'s oral rendering of the Bhadrakalpa-samādhi delivered on the 21st day of the seventh month of Yǒngkāng 永康 1 (300 CE). No further biographical detail is preserved.
