**Lǐ Zhāngwǔ** 李彰五 (fl. Guāng-xù period, late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng official-physician active in nearly forty years of medical practice up to 1898. Author of multiple works including the *Bìng-jiā xū-zhī gē-jué* 病家須知歌訣, an annotated *Hán-wēn tiáo-biàn* 寒溫條辨, the *Zhòng-jǐng Shānghán jí-zhù* 仲景傷寒輯注 (30 juan, unpublished), the *Zhòng-jǐng mài-fǎ wài-zhù* 仲景脈法外注 (unpublished), and the *Mài-dù yùn-xíng kǎo* 脈度運行考 ([[KR3ee039]], 1898). No further biographical record survives.
