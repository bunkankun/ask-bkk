**Lǐ Zhāngwǔ** 李彰五 (fl. Guāngxù period, late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng official-physician active in nearly forty years of medical practice up to 1898. Author of multiple works including the *Bìngjiā xūzhī gējué* 病家須知歌訣, an annotated *Hánwēn tiáobiàn* 寒溫條辨, the *Zhòngjǐng Shānghán jízhù* 仲景傷寒輯注 (30 juan, unpublished), the *Zhòngjǐng màifǎ wàizhù* 仲景脈法外注 (unpublished), and the *Màidù yùnxíng kǎo* 脈度運行考 ([[KR3ee039]], 1898). No further biographical record survives.
