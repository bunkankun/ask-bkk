Lín Yáosǒu 林堯叟 (*zì* Tángwēng 唐翁) was a late Sòng *Zuǒzhuàn* commentator. Lifedates not securely attested; not recorded in CBDB. Zhèng Yuè 鄭玥 (cited in Zhū Yízūn's 朱彝尊 *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考) records his *zì* as Tángwēng. He is the author of the *Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn jù jiě* 春秋左傳句解 in 40 *juǎn*, a phrase-by-phrase elementary commentary on the *Zuǒzhuàn* designed for student readers — the standard SòngYuánMíng "primer" on the *Zuǒ*. His annotations are largely paraphrastic and explanatory rather than philological, and the *Sìkù* compilers (in their notice on [[KR1e0092]]) judge them clearly inferior to the precise glosses of Dù Yù 杜預 ([[杜預]]).

In the late Míng (Chóngzhēn 崇禎 reign) the Hangzhōu publisher Wáng Dàokūn 王道焜 ([[王道焜]]) and his fellow-townsman Zhào Rúyuán 趙如源 ([[趙如源]]) produced a composite edition combining Dù Yù's classical *jí jiě* with Lín's vernacular paraphrase, the *Zuǒzhuàn DùLín hé zhù* 左傳杜林合注 ([[KR1e0092]]) in 50 *juǎn*. This composite circulated widely as a working student text throughout the late Míng and Qīng. Subsequent commercial reprints, however, dropped the editors' names and converted the editors' *fánlì* 凡例 into a preface ascribed to Lín himself, generating bibliographic confusion the *Sìkù tíyào* takes pains to correct.
