**Xú Shǒu-zhēn** 徐守真 (*fl.* late Kāng-xī era), Qīng literatus of **Jīn-chuān** 金川 (place-name not uniquely resolvable; possibly the Jīn-chuān district of An-hui or another Jīn-chuān locality). Wrote the preface to the *Jí-jiù xiān-fāng* 急救仙方 obstetric-emergency section of **[[孫偉]]** (Sūn Wěi)'s *Liángpéng huìjí jīngyàn shénfāng* ([[KR3ed147]]). The preface develops a sustained moral-economic argument that standard pregnancy-and-childbirth formularies have failed the rural and poor population because of cumbersome / rare / expensive ingredient requirements. Sparse further biographical record.
