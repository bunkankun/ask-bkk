**Xú Shǒuzhēn** 徐守真 (*fl.* late Kāngxī era), Qīng literatus of **Jīnchuān** 金川 (place-name not uniquely resolvable; possibly the Jīnchuān district of An-hui or another Jīnchuān locality). Wrote the preface to the *Jíjiù xiānfāng* 急救仙方 obstetric-emergency section of **[[孫偉]]** (Sūn Wěi)'s *Liángpéng huìjí jīngyàn shénfāng* ([[KR3ed147]]). The preface develops a sustained moral-economic argument that standard pregnancy-and-childbirth formularies have failed the rural and poor population because of cumbersome / rare / expensive ingredient requirements. Sparse further biographical record.
