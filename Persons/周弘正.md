# Zhōu Hóngzhèng 周弘正

*Zì* Sīxíng 思行. Liáng 梁 / Chén 陳 classicist and philosopher, native of Rǔnán 汝南 (modern Hénán). One of the leading *Xuánxué* 玄學 / classical scholars of the Southern Dynasties; held high office under both the Liáng (*Hàn-lín xuéshì* 翰林學士) and the Chén (*Shàngshū lìng* 尚書令). Wrote on the *Yì*, *Lǎozǐ*, and *Zhuāngzǐ*; his *Yì* commentary is lost but cited in [[史徵]] Shǐ Zhēng's *Zhōuyì kǒujué yì* ([[KR1a0009]]) and in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Chén shū* 陳書 (juan 24) and *Nán shǐ* 南史 give his biography.
