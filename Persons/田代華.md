# Tián Dàihuá 田代華

Contemporary Chinese-medicine philologist; editor of the standard punctuated and annotated edition *Huóyòu xīnshū jiàozhù* 活幼心書校注 ([[KR3ej007]]; Beijing: Rénmín wèishēng chūbǎnshè, 1985) and of many critical editions of classical Chinese medical texts published by Rénmín wèishēng chūbǎnshè.
