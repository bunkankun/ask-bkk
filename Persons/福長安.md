Fú Cháng-ān 福長安 (1760–1817), of the Manchu Plain Yellow Banner Fù-chá 富察 family, *zì* Chéng-zhāi 誠齋. Fourth son of Fù-héng 傅恒 (the great Qiánlóng-era Grand Secretary and Junghar campaigner) and younger brother of the celebrated Fú-kāng-ān 福康安. He served in the late Qiánlóng-era court at the highest ranks: *Yùqián dà-chén* 御前大臣, *Lǐng-shìwèi nèi-dà-chén* 領侍衛內大臣, *Bīng-bù shàng-shū* 兵部尚書, *Hù-bù zuǒ-shì-láng* 戶部左侍郎 (concurrent), and *Nèi-wù-fǔ dà-chén* 內務府大臣. With Hé-shēn 和珅 he was a senior member of the inner *Jūn-jī chù* circle of the late Qiánlóng era and held *zǒng-cái* (chief editorial supervisor) responsibility on the *Qīndìng Shèngjīng tōngzhì* 欽定盛京通志 ([[KR2k0040]]) and other late-Qiánlóng imperial compilations. On Qiánlóng's death in 1799, he was implicated in the prosecution of Hé-shēn but ultimately spared by the Jiā-qìng emperor and continued in court service in lower-ranking positions; he died in 1817.
