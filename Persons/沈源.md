Shěn Yuán 沈源 (*hào* Bào-yuán-zǐ 抱元子) — Cháng-shú 常熟 (Jiāngsū) physician of the late eighteenth century, descended from the *Cháng-shuǐ* 長水 Shěn lineage. He compiled the eight-*juǎn* [[KR3ep008]] *Qí-zhèng huì* 奇症彙 ("Compendium of Strange Symptoms"), one of the principal Qīng anthologies of unusual clinical phenomena. His brother-in-law was the late-Qián-lóng official **Qián Dūn-táng 錢敦堂** of Dāng-hú 當湖, who supplied the work's main preface. The compendium gathers cases of singular presentations from sources as diverse as the *Shǐ-jì* biography of Chún-yú Yì 淳于意, the *Yī-shuō* 醫說 of Zhāng Gǎo 張杲, Jiāng Guàn's *Míng-yī lèi-àn* 名醫類案, and Shěn Yuán's own clinical practice, with editorial commentary explaining each case in the framework of standard medical theory. His clinical lifedates are not confirmed; CBDB has several Shěn Yuán records (33422, 198973, 296685, 331097, etc.) without securely matched dates for this person.
