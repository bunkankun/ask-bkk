Shěn Yuán 沈源 (*hào* Bàoyuánzǐ 抱元子) — Chángshú 常熟 (Jiāngsū) physician of the late eighteenth century, descended from the *Chángshuǐ* 長水 Shěn lineage. He compiled the eight-*juǎn* [[KR3ep008]] *Qízhèng huì* 奇症彙 ("Compendium of Strange Symptoms"), one of the principal Qīng anthologies of unusual clinical phenomena. His brother-in-law was the late-Qián-lóng official **Qián Dūntáng 錢敦堂** of Dānghú 當湖, who supplied the work's main preface. The compendium gathers cases of singular presentations from sources as diverse as the *Shǐjì* biography of Chúnyú Yì 淳于意, the *Yīshuō* 醫說 of Zhāng Gǎo 張杲, Jiāng Guàn's *Míngyī lèiàn* 名醫類案, and Shěn Yuán's own clinical practice, with editorial commentary explaining each case in the framework of standard medical theory. His clinical lifedates are not confirmed; CBDB has several Shěn Yuán records (33422, 198973, 296685, 331097, etc.) without securely matched dates for this person.
