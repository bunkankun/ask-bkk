**Zhāng Qí** 張琦 (*fl.* late 18th c., 清), native of Yánghú 陽湖 (Chángzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). Wrote the 後序 (postface) preserved in the standard edition of [[黃元御]]'s *Chángshā yàojiě* 長沙藥解 ([[KR3ec064]]), explaining the *Běnjīng* / *Shānghán* relationship that motivates the work. The Qiánlóng-era Yánghú Zhāng Qí edition is the principal printing of the *Chángshā yàojiě*. No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record located.
