Northern-Sòng 北宋 *Yìjīng* 易經 numerologist of the Sānqú 三衢 (modern Qúzhōu 衢州, Zhèjiāng) school, *zì* Xiānzhī 先之 (also 長民 Chángmín). With his contemporary **Shào Yōng** 邵雍 (1011–1077), Liú Mù is the only member of the early-Sòng 圖書 ("School of Charts and Writings") *Yì*-numerology lineage whose works have come down to us in complete form. Active under Sòng Rénzōng 宋仁宗 (r. 1023–1064), Liú Mù was a transmitter of a chart-tradition reportedly received from Fàn Èchāng 范諤昌 ← Xǔ Jiān 許堅 ← Lǐ Gài 李漑 ← Chōng Fàng 种放 ← Chén Tuán 陳摶 ([[陳摶]]) — i.e., from the legendary patriarch of the Sòng *Yì*-tú 易圖 tradition.

His principal works are the *Yìshù gōuyǐn tú* 易數鉤隱圖 ([[KR5a0160]], DZ 159 / CT 159; "Charts for Probing the Hidden Meaning of the Figures of the *Book of Changes*"), recorded in the *Jùnzhāi dúshū zhì* 郡齋讀書志 1.33–34 and abstracted in the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào* 四庫全書總目提要 2.1a–b, with an editor's preface in the *Tōngzhìtáng jīngjiě* 通志堂經解 edition that describes the text's Sòng transmission; and a posthumous supplement, the *Yìshù gōuyǐn tú yílùn jiǔshì* 易數鉤隱圖遺論九事 ([[KR5a0161]], DZ 160 / CT 160), which was already incorporated as an appendix in Southern-Sòng editions (*Jùnzhāi dúshū zhì* 1.134). Old editions of the *Yìshù gōuyǐn tú* once carried a preface by Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修 (1007–1072), later excised because considered likely spurious.

Liú Mù's contribution to the Sòng *Yì*-tradition was to produce an integrated chart-commentary on every step of the cosmogonic sequence — *tàijí* 太極 → *liǎngyí* 兩儀 → *sìxiàng* 四象 → *bāguà* 八卦 — anchored in his "fifty-five charts" (the number of the *Hétú* 河圖 + *Luòshū* 洛書 cosmic numerology). His chart-corpus and explanatory technique influenced Zhū Zhèn 朱震 (1072–1138), Zhū Xī 朱熹 (1130–1200), and the entire later *xiàngshù* 象數 tradition. Pseudonymously, his name was attached to several later chart-compilations, including the [[KR5a0159|*Dàyì xiàngshù gōushēn tú*]] (DZ 158).
