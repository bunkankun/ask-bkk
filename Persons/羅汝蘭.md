Late-Qīng physician of **Lián-jiāng** 廉江 county (modern Zhàn-jiāng 湛江 prefecture, Guǎng-dōng). Author of *Shǔ-yì yuē-biān* 鼠疫約編 / *Shǔ-yì huì-biān* 鼠疫汇編 ([[KR3eg041]]), the principal Chinese-medical monograph composed in immediate response to the great **1890–1894 Guǎng-zhōu / Hong Kong plague epidemic** — the start of the *Third Plague Pandemic*, in which bubonic plague that had begun in Yún-nán in the 1850s reached Guǎng-zhōu in 1890–1893 in small annual outbreaks and erupted into a major epidemic in 1894.

The book gives traditional Chinese-medical analysis and prescription for ***shǔ-yì*** 鼠疫 (literally "rat-plague") — a category that, although it has older roots in Sòng-Yuán Lǐng-nán medical writing, became canonically associated with bubonic plague through the *Third Pandemic* epidemics. Luó's text is one of the principal Chinese-language sources for the medical response to those epidemics.

Lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record. Source: Carol Benedict, *Bubonic Plague in Nineteenth-Century China* (Stanford UP, 1996).
