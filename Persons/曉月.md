Xiǎoyuè 曉月 (fl. mid 11th c.), Northern-Song Chán master of the Lètánshān 泐潭山 monastic tradition (in modern Jiāngxī), known by the appellation Lètán Chánshī 泐潭禪師. He is named in the colophon of [[KR6m0042]] *Zhàolùn shū kē* 肇論疏科 (X868) as the editor and structural arranger of the *Zhàolùn shū kēwén* — a *kēwén* 科文 (structural-outline) treatment of the [[KR6m0038]] *Zhào lùn* — and in the colophon of [[KR6m0043]] *Jiākē Zhàolùn xù zhù* 夾科肇論序注 (X869) as the annotator of the same.

Xiǎoyuè's biographical record is fragmentary. He was associated with the Lètánshān Yúnménzōng 雲門宗 lineage of Northern-Song Chán Buddhism and was a contemporary of the great Yúnménzōng masters of the Bāofēng Wéizhāo 寶峰惟照 (1085–1155) generation. The *kēwén* and *zhù* genres in which Xiǎoyuè worked are characteristic of Northern-Song Chán-school doctrinal-commentarial literature: the *kē* breaks the source-text into structural divisions; the *zhù* supplies brief inline glosses. Xiǎoyuè's *Zhàolùn* commentaries are the principal Northern-Song Chán-school engagement with Sēngzhào's tradition.
