Xiǎo-yuè 曉月 (fl. mid 11th c.), Northern-Song Chán master of the Lè-tán-shān 泐潭山 monastic tradition (in modern Jiāngxī), known by the appellation Lè-tán Chán-shī 泐潭禪師. He is named in the colophon of [[KR6m0042]] *Zhào-lùn shū kē* 肇論疏科 (X868) as the editor and structural arranger of the *Zhào-lùn shū kē-wén* — a *kē-wén* 科文 (structural-outline) treatment of the [[KR6m0038]] *Zhào lùn* — and in the colophon of [[KR6m0043]] *Jiā-kē Zhào-lùn xù zhù* 夾科肇論序注 (X869) as the annotator of the same.

Xiǎo-yuè's biographical record is fragmentary. He was associated with the Lè-tán-shān Yún-mén-zōng 雲門宗 lineage of Northern-Song Chán Buddhism and was a contemporary of the great Yún-mén-zōng masters of the Bāo-fēng Wéi-zhāo 寶峰惟照 (1085–1155) generation. The *kē-wén* and *zhù* genres in which Xiǎo-yuè worked are characteristic of Northern-Song Chán-school doctrinal-commentarial literature: the *kē* breaks the source-text into structural divisions; the *zhù* supplies brief inline glosses. Xiǎo-yuè's *Zhào-lùn* commentaries are the principal Northern-Song Chán-school engagement with Sēng-zhào's tradition.
