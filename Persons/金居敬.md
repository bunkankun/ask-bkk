Early-Qīng Hanlin scholar; native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). *Zì* Jǐngfú 景孚. Took the *jìnshì* in *Kāngxī* 9 (1670). Served as Hanlin Compiler (*Biānxiū*); was selected by Kǒng Yùqí 孔毓圻 to head the editorial team for the *Xìnglǔ Shèngdiǎn* 幸魯盛典 ([[KR2m0033]]) in 1685. CBDB gives the birth-year 1639; death-year not transmitted. Active through the late Kāngxī era.
