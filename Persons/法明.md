Fǎmíng 法明 (fl. late seventh c.), Tang monk associated with the Luòyáng translation programme of [[達摩流支]] (Dharmaruci) under Empress [[武則天]] Wǔ Zétiān. According to Antonino Forte's analysis (1976) of the Tunhuang document S.6502 and related sources, Fǎmíng was among the Chinese monks who collaborated with [[薛懷義]] in the composition / interpolation of the prophecy of a female cakravartin into the 693 retranslation of the *[[KR6i0298|Bǎoyǔ jīng (T660)]]*, a key text in Wǔ Zétiān's imperial legitimation programme.
