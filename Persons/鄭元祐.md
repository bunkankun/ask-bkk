Zhèng Yuán-yòu 鄭元祐 (1292–1364), *zì* Míng-dé 明德, *hào* Suì-chāng shān-rén 遂昌山人. Family origin Suì-chāng 遂昌 (Chǔ-zhōu, Zhèjiāng); relocated to Qián-táng 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu) and from there *liú-yù* (in transit-residence) at Píng-jiāng (= Sū-zhōu) for 40 years — hence the collection title *Qiáo-Wú jí* "Sojourning in Wú." Authored *Suì-chāng zá-lù*. Recommended late in life; in Zhì-zhèng-era twice held *xiào-guān* (school-officer) posts; the post-collation *xíng-zhuàng* by Sū Dà-nián 蘇大年 (and Lú Xióng's 盧熊 *Sū-zhōu fǔ-zhì*) state he was only ever a *xiào-guān* and did not hold other posts; yet the collection contains a *Zhāng Dé-cháng shū* with the line "*pú zàn jùn wú-bǔ — cháng yí Léi-lǐ zhī yǔ*" — possibly Zhèng composing for someone else and forgetting to flag the attribution. The collection [[KR4d0545]] *Qiáo-Wú jí* survives in the Míng *Zhāng Xí* 張習 *zhòng-dìng* 1496 *bǐng-chén* recension — Zhèng's original was longer, in *Suì-chāng shān-rén jí* and *Qiáo-Wú jí* with much overlap; Zhāng combined and selected only the "精純者" (essence-pure) as the present 12-*juǎn* version.
