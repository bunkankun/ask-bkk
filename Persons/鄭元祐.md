Zhèng Yuányòu 鄭元祐 (1292–1364), *zì* Míngdé 明德, *hào* Suìchāng shānrén 遂昌山人. Family origin Suìchāng 遂昌 (Chǔzhōu, Zhèjiāng); relocated to Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu) and from there *liúyù* (in transit-residence) at Píngjiāng (= Sūzhōu) for 40 years — hence the collection title *QiáoWú jí* "Sojourning in Wú." Authored *Suìchāng zálù*. Recommended late in life; in Zhì-zhèng-era twice held *xiàoguān* (school-officer) posts; the post-collation *xíngzhuàng* by Sū Dànián 蘇大年 (and Lú Xióng's 盧熊 *Sūzhōu fǔzhì*) state he was only ever a *xiàoguān* and did not hold other posts; yet the collection contains a *Zhāng Décháng shū* with the line "*pú zàn jùn wúbǔ — cháng yí Léilǐ zhī yǔ*" — possibly Zhèng composing for someone else and forgetting to flag the attribution. The collection [[KR4d0545]] *QiáoWú jí* survives in the Míng *Zhāng Xí* 張習 *zhòngdìng* 1496 *bǐngchén* recension — Zhèng's original was longer, in *Suìchāng shānrén jí* and *QiáoWú jí* with much overlap; Zhāng combined and selected only the "精純者" (essence-pure) as the present 12-*juǎn* version.
