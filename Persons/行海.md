# Xínghǎi 行海

Mid-Qīng Chán monk. Lifedates, native place, and monastic lineage all unrecorded. Self-styled *hòu xué shāmén* 後學沙門 ("junior-disciple śramaṇa") in the only surviving attribution.

One surviving work: the *Chán zōng zhǐ zhǎng* 禪宗指掌 [[KR6q0175]], a brief eight-part Chán catechism first printed in 1787 (Qiánlóng 52) at the Tiānníng-sì 天寧寺 of Chángzhōu 常州 (Jiāngsū), and subsequently reprinted in 1893 at Tài-bái-fēng 太白峯 in Yín-xiàn 鄞縣 (near Níngbō).

Distinct from the earlier 大方行海 Dà-fāng Xínghǎi (DILA A012051, 1604–1670), a Míng-Qīng transitional Chán master whose lifedates preclude authorship of the 1787 text.

# Xíng-hǎi 行海 (Dà-fāng Xíng-hǎi 大方行海)

A different Xíng-hǎi: Línjì-Yángqí *chán-shī* of the Mì-yún line. *Hào* **Dà-fāng** 大方. Born **萬曆甲辰 6/7 巳時** = **1604-07-03 Gregorian**; died **庚戌 2/5 子時** = **1670-02-24 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **67**, *fǎ-là* **36**. Native of **Tóng-xiāng** 桐鄉 (Jiā-xīng); lay surname **Qián** 錢.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fú-shí Tōng-xián** [[通賢]] 浮石通賢.

**Formation.** At 20 vegetarian; *jìng-tǔ* at **Yún-qī** 雲棲. At 29 (1632) tonsured at **Jìng-shān Míng-zhū-táng** under **Wú-yì shī** 無異師. Awakening at 30; full precepts under [[圓悟]] **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** at age 31 (1635) at **Tiān-tóng**; *xū-kōng fěn-suì* boundary-experience. At 35 (1639) met **Ěr-mì** at **Xiǎn-shèng**. At 47 (1650) found the *ān-lè* 安樂 under **Fú-shí Tōng-xián**.

**Abbacy.** **康熙八 = 1669**: took up **Jiāng-sū Lóu-jiāng Chán-dēng-ān** 江蘇婁江禪燈菴.

**Work.** [[KR6q0503]] 《大方禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B366, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chāo-míng** [[超鳴]] *biān*; juan 6 *xíng-shí* + *tǎ-míng* preserves lifedates.
