# Xínghǎi 行海

Mid-Qīng Chán monk. Lifedates, native place, and monastic lineage all unrecorded. Self-styled *hòu xué shāmén* 後學沙門 ("junior-disciple śramaṇa") in the only surviving attribution.

One surviving work: the *Chán zōng zhǐ zhǎng* 禪宗指掌 [[KR6q0175]], a brief eight-part Chán catechism first printed in 1787 (Qiánlóng 52) at the Tiānníngsì 天寧寺 of Chángzhōu 常州 (Jiāngsū), and subsequently reprinted in 1893 at Tàibáifēng 太白峯 in Yínxiàn 鄞縣 (near Níngbō).

Distinct from the earlier 大方行海 Dàfāng Xínghǎi (DILA A012051, 1604–1670), a Míng-Qīng transitional Chán master whose lifedates preclude authorship of the 1787 text.

# Xínghǎi 行海 (Dàfāng Xínghǎi 大方行海)

A different Xínghǎi: Línjì-Yángqí *chánshī* of the Mìyún line. *Hào* **Dàfāng** 大方. Born **萬曆甲辰 6/7 巳時** = **1604-07-03 Gregorian**; died **庚戌 2/5 子時** = **1670-02-24 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **67**, *fǎlà* **36**. Native of **Tóngxiāng** 桐鄉 (Jiāxīng); lay surname **Qián** 錢.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fúshí Tōngxián** [[通賢]] 浮石通賢.

**Formation.** At 20 vegetarian; *jìngtǔ* at **Yúnqī** 雲棲. At 29 (1632) tonsured at **Jìngshān Míngzhūtáng** under **Wúyì shī** 無異師. Awakening at 30; full precepts under [[圓悟]] **Mìyún Yuánwù** at age 31 (1635) at **Tiāntóng**; *xūkōng fěnsuì* boundary-experience. At 35 (1639) met **Ěrmì** at **Xiǎnshèng**. At 47 (1650) found the *ānlè* 安樂 under **Fúshí Tōngxián**.

**Abbacy.** **康熙八 = 1669**: took up **Jiāngsū Lóujiāng Chándēngān** 江蘇婁江禪燈菴.

**Work.** [[KR6q0503]] 《大方禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B366, compiled by *fǎsì* **Chāomíng** [[超鳴]] *biān*; juan 6 *xíngshí* + *tǎmíng* preserves lifedates.
