Wáng Shìdiǎn (*zì* Jìzhì 繼志, d. 1359?), a native of Xūjù 須句 in Dōngpíng 東平 (modern Dōngpíng county, Shāndōng), served the Yuán court as *tōngshì shèrén* 通事舍人 (audience usher) of the Court of Imperial Receptions and concurrently as one of the seal-keeping officials of the *Mìshū jiān* 秘書監 (Imperial Library / Bureau of Imperial Manuscripts). CBDB places his floruit between 1330 and 1342, consistent with the Zhìshùn-era prefaces (1330, 1333) to his *Jìnbiǎn* [[KR2k0002]]; Wilkinson's "d. 1359?" reflects a later date of death sometimes cited but not securely documented. He is also the author of the *Mìshū jiān zhì* 秘書監志, the institutional treatise of the Yuán imperial library and one of the two major sources for the genesis and organization of the *Dà-Yuán dà yītǒng zhì* 大元大一統志.
