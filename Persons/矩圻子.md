**Jǔqǐzǐ** 矩圻子 is the literary pseudonym of the anonymous author of *Xǐng Mèng Pián Yán* 醒夢駢言 ([[KR4k0260]]), a moral fiction of the early Qīng dynasty. Project Gutenberg (eBook #27108) describes this author as "active 17th century." The true identity has not been established. The Kanripo catalog records the work as anonymous (*wúmíngshì* 無名氏). No CBDB record identified.
