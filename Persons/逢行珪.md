**Féng Xíngguī** 逢行珪 (also written 逄行珪 in the source) was a Táng minor official who held the post of Magistrate of Zhèng-xiàn in Huá-zhōu 華州鄭縣尉 in the Yǒnghuī 永徽 era of Tang Gāozōng. He is otherwise unknown; his native place is unrecorded in the *Sìkù* tiyao. He is remembered solely as the annotator of the *Yùzǐ* 鬻子 ([[KR3j0001]]), which he presented to the throne with a memorial dated the 26th day of the 11th month of Yǒnghuī 4 (5 January 654 CE).
