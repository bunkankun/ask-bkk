**Lǐ Xùn** 李迅 (*zì* Sìlì 嗣立, *fl.* late Southern Sòng), Sòng-period physician of Quánzhōu 泉州 (Fújiàn). Held the office of *Dàlǐ píngshì* 大理評事 (Court Examiner) and was famous as a physician. Author of the ***Jíyàn bèijū fāng*** 集驗背疽方 ([[KR3e0042]]) — a specialist Southern-Sòng treatise on **back-and-shoulder furuncles** (背疽 *bèijū*, a serious and frequently fatal class of suppurative infection) in 1 juan, with 53 entries each carrying both theoretical discussion and prescription. The Mǎ Duānlín *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* erroneously gives the author's personal name as 逸 ("Yì") rather than the correct 迅 ("Xùn"); the SKQS editors note this and correct on the basis of Guō Yìngxiáng 郭應祥's preface preserved in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* recovery. Beyond the *Jíyàn bèijū fāng* and his Court-Examiner office, Lǐ Xùn has no transmitted biographical record.
