Zhāng Hóng 張鴻, late-Qīng disciple of [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèng-yīng, *fl.* 1850s–1860s. Compiled and edited [[KR3ep084]] *Wáng-shì yī-àn xù-biān* (eight *juǎn*), the principal documentary record of Wáng Mèng-yīng's clinical practice during the Tài-píng war years (1850–1864). No further transmitted biographical record. Not in CBDB.
