Zhāng Hóng 張鴻, late-Qīng disciple of [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèngyīng, *fl.* 1850s–1860s. Compiled and edited [[KR3ep084]] *Wángshì yīàn xùbiān* (eight *juǎn*), the principal documentary record of Wáng Mèngyīng's clinical practice during the Tàipíng war years (1850–1864). No further transmitted biographical record. Not in CBDB.
