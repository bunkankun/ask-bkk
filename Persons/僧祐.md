The principal **founding figure of Chinese Buddhist canonical-bibliographic and historical scholarship**. Lay surname **Yú** 俞, native of **Xià-pī jùn** 下邳郡 (in modern Jiāng-sū). Born **445**; died at **Jiànchū-sì** 建初寺 in the southern capital on **Tiānjiān** 17, **5th month, 26th day** = **21 June 518**, age 74.

He took ordination at age 14 at **Dìng-lín-sì** 定林寺 under **Fǎ-xiàn** 法獻, then deepened his *vinaya* studies under **Fǎ-yǐng** 法穎 — becoming the leading **Sì-fēn-lǜ** 四分律 expert of his generation. Subsequently, at the request of the **Cí-líng-wáng** 竟陵王 (= **Xiāo Zǐ-liáng** 蕅子良, the great Liáng-dynasty Buddhist patron), he opened lectures on the *vinaya*. In **Yǒng-míng** 永明 (483–493) of the Southern Qí, he was imperially commanded to enter the **Wú** region, examine and confirm the five-class monastic ordination, lecture on the *Shí-sòng-lǜ* 十誦律 (*Daśabhāṇavāra-vinaya*), and expound the methods of receiving the precepts.

His scholarly legacy is foundational for Chinese Buddhist studies:

- **Chū sān-zàng jì jí** 出三藏記集 ([[KR6s0084]], T2145, 15 juan) — the foundational Chinese Buddhist canonical bibliography and translator-historical compendium.
- **Hóng-míng jí** 弘明集 (T2102, 14 juan) — the foundational anthology of Chinese Buddhist apologetic literature, gathering anti-Daoist and pro-Buddhist polemical writings from the Hàn through Liáng periods.
- **Shì-jiā pǔ** 釋迦譜 (T2040, 5 juan) — biographical compilation on Śākyamuni and his Indian disciples.
- **Sà-pó-duō-bù xiāng-chéng zhuàn** 薩婆多部相承傳 — Sarvāstivāda lineage tradition.
- **Pú-sà shàn-jiè dì-chí èr-jīng jì** 菩薩善戒地持二經記 (lost).
- **Dà-jí xū-kōng-zàng wú-jìn-yì jīng jì** 大集虛空藏無盡意經記 (lost).
- **Xián-yú jīng jì** 賢愚經記 (lost).

His major disciples include **Zhì-zàng** 智藏 (one of the three great Liáng dharma-masters), **Huì-kuò** 慧廓, **Bǎochàng** 寶唱 (compiler of [[KR6s0001]] *Jīng-lǜ yì-xiàng*), and **the Lín-chuān-wáng Hóng** 臨川王宏 — together with monks-and-laity totaling over 10,000 followers.

Death: at **Jiànchū-sì** 建初寺 in the southern capital on the 26th day of the 5th lunar month of **Tiānjiān** 17 = **21 June 518**, age 74.
