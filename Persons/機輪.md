Jīlún 機輪, *jī*-generation LínjìYángqí (PǔmíngYòng → YǒngzhèngYī → Duànyú line) dharma-disciple of **Tuìān Duànyú Zhì** [[智]] 退菴斷愚智. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《嘉興退菴斷愚智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0455]]), with the catalog meta crediting *Jīlún děng biān* 機輪等編 (Jīlún and others). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
