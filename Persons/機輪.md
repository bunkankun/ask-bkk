Jī-lún 機輪, *jī*-generation Línjì-Yángqí (Pǔ-míng-Yòng → Yǒng-zhèng-Yī → Duàn-yú line) dharma-disciple of **Tuì-ān Duàn-yú Zhì** [[智]] 退菴斷愚智. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《嘉興退菴斷愚智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0455]]), with the catalog meta crediting *Jī-lún děng biān* 機輪等編 (Jī-lún and others). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
