Early-Yuán Daoist master of Lúshān 廬山 (Jiāngxī), *hào* Xiānwēng 仙翁 ("Immortal Elder"), holder of the *Shàngqīng dàdòng bǎolù* 上清大洞寶籙 register and presiding officer of the *Xiānléi tán* 顯雷壇 ("Altar for the Manifestation of Thunder"). Known principally as the author of [[KR5a0093|Yuánshǐ wúliàng dùrén shàngpǐn miàojīng zhùjiě 元始無量度人上品妙經註解]] (DZ 93, presented 1303/1304, postfaces through 1316) — a deliberately accessible commentary on the *Dùrén jīng* intended for religious novices. Xuē was a spiritual heir of [[雷默庵|Léi Mò'ān]] 雷默庵 (on whom see [[KR5a0297|DZ 297 *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn xùbiān* 5.11b–14a]]), from whom he received a dream-revelation at the Thunder Altar in 1308, and who registered his name in the Shénxiāo 神霄 celestial offices as a *Dùrénjīng* exegete "equal in merit" to himself. Xuē was subsequently granted a further revelation by the divine Clerk of the Thunder Clap Xīn Zhōngyì 辛仲義, from whom he received the "heart-seal transmission." No CBDB record exists. Xuē's commentary is the canonical "simple" *Dùrénjīng* commentary — stripped of esoteric and bureaucratic readings — and was subsequently heavily cited by [[陳致虛|Chén Zhìxū]] (DZ 92) and [[張宇初|Zhāng Yǔchū]] (DZ 89).
