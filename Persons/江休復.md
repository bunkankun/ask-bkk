**Jiāng Xiūfù** 江休復 (1005–1060), *zì* Línjī 鄰幾. Native of Kāifēng Chénliú 陳留. *Jìnshì* under *Tiānshèng*. Appointed *Jíxián jiàolǐ* (Editor of the Worthy Treasury). Demoted to *jiān Càizhōu shuì* (Càizhōu tax-supervisor) in the wake of the *Qìnglì* reform's collapse (1045); later restored to office, reaching *Xíngbù lángzhōng* and *Xiū qǐjū zhù* (Reviser of the Imperial Diary). Died in office in *Jiāyòu* 5 (1060). His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 443 (*Wényuàn zhuàn*). CBDB id 10170 records the lifedates 1005–1060.

He was a close member of the literary circle around Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修 and Méi Yáochén 梅堯臣 — Ōuyáng wrote his tomb-inscription. His literary collection in 20 *juàn* is lost; only the [[KR3l0033]] *Jiāyòu zázhì* 嘉祐雜誌 survives (1 *juàn* in the present recension, 3 *juàn* per *Sòng shǐ Yìwén zhì*).
