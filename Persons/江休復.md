**Jiāng Xiū-fù** 江休復 (1005–1060), *zì* Lín-jī 鄰幾. Native of Kāi-fēng Chén-liú 陳留. *Jìnshì* under *Tiān-shèng*. Appointed *Jí-xián jiào-lǐ* (Editor of the Worthy Treasury). Demoted to *jiān Cài-zhōu shuì* (Cài-zhōu tax-supervisor) in the wake of the *Qìng-lì* reform's collapse (1045); later restored to office, reaching *Xíng-bù láng-zhōng* and *Xiū qǐ-jū zhù* (Reviser of the Imperial Diary). Died in office in *Jiā-yòu* 5 (1060). His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 443 (*Wén-yuàn zhuàn*). CBDB id 10170 records the lifedates 1005–1060.

He was a close member of the literary circle around Ōu-yáng Xiū 歐陽修 and Méi Yáo-chén 梅堯臣 — Ōu-yáng wrote his tomb-inscription. His literary collection in 20 *juàn* is lost; only the [[KR3l0033]] *Jiāyòu zázhì* 嘉祐雜誌 survives (1 *juàn* in the present recension, 3 *juàn* per *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén zhì*).
