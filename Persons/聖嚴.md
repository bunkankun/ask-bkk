# 聖嚴

Shèngyán 聖嚴 (Sheng Yen, 1931–2009), modern Taiwanese Chán master, scholar of late-Míng Chinese Buddhism, and founder of Dharma Drum Mountain (Fǎgǔshān 法鼓山). Author of *Mínmò Zhōngguó Fójiào zhī yánjiū* 明末中國佛教之研究 (1987), a standard scholarly study of late-Míng Chinese Buddhism.
