Bōkaku 房覺 (fl. mid-12th c.) was a Japanese Shingon monk active at **Tsubosaka-dera** 壺坂寺 (Minami-Hokke-ji) in Yamato, the great Hossō / Kannon-pilgrimage centre that nonetheless maintained a substantial Shingon esoteric faculty in the late Heian period. His lifedates are unknown, but the colophon of his *Wèi-jué dá-jué* 未決答決 dates the composition to **Hōgen 2 = 1157**, locating him in the mid-12th-century scholastic milieu. He signs himself *Shingon mappa* 眞言末葉 ("a late-leaf [disciple] of the Shingon school"), indicating self-conscious humility before the early masters. His one surviving work systematically answers the Hossō scholar **Tokuitsu** 德一's challenges to the Shingon teachings.

Surviving work in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0165]] *Wèi-jué dá-jué* (1157).
