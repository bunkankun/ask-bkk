# Dàqì 大器

Southern-Sòng official; lay correspondent of Dàhuì [[宗杲]] Zōnggǎo. Letters and exchanges with him are preserved among the literati-correspondence sections of the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0060]]) and the companion *Dàhuì shū* ([[KR6q0024]]).
