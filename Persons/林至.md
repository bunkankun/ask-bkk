Lín Zhì 林至 (lifedates unrecorded; fl. *Chúnxī*-era 1174–1189 onward), *zì* Déjiǔ 德久. Native place either Sōngjiāng 松江 (per the Sìkù *tiyao*) or Zuìlǐ 檇李 (an old name for Jiāxīng / Sūzhōu, per Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*) — the lower-Yangzi delta region; the discrepancy is left unresolved by the Sìkù editors.

*Jìnshì* of the *Chúnxī* era (1174–1189). Held *Mìshūshěng zhèngzì* 秘書省正字 (Imperial Library Orthographer). Direct disciple of Zhū Xī 朱熹: Zhū Xī's *Wén jí* contains a substantial "*Reply to Lín Déjiǔ*" (*Dá Lín Déjiǔ shū* 答林德久書).

CBDB id 33342 records him without lifedates. Biographical references at *Nán Sòng guǎngé xùlù* 8.9b and 9.26b; *Zhūzǐ ménrén* 朱子門人 (the disciple-roster) p. 147.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0045]] *Yì bì zhuàn* 易裨傳 — a 2-juan critical-corrective treatise on Sòng *Yì*-numerology, executing the Zhū-school *xiàngshù*-corrective program in a freestanding format.
