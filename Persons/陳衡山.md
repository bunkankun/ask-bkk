# 陳衡山 Chén Héngshān

**Chén Héngshān** 陳衡山, late-Qīng official in the salt-monopoly administration; rediscovered the Sòng block-print of *Bèijí jiǔfǎ* 備急灸法 ([[KR3ee049]]) in a Japanese book-market and reprinted it in China.
