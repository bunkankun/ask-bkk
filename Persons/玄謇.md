# 玄謇
Xuánjiǎn (fl. early seventh c.)

Early Táng monk who served as *yìyǔ* (translator of speech) in [[波羅頗蜜多羅]] Prabhākaramitra's translation team for the *Bǎoxīng tuóluóní jīng* 寶星陀羅尼經 ([[KR6h0006]]) at Dàxīngshàn monastery in 629–630.
