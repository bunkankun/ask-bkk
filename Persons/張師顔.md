張師顔 Zhāng Shīyán was a Jin-dynasty official (fl. mid-12th century; CBDB id 119954) who served as a court diarist (握筆東觀) and ritual attendant (禮郎). He compiled the *Nán Qiān Lù* 南遷錄 ([[KR4k0019]]), a record of a southward imperial journey, with a preface dated to Dàdìng 大定 14 (1174 CE). The CBDB note references a *Sòngshǐ* biography entry, suggesting possible Song-dynasty service before or alongside Jin service. Nothing further is known of his career or other writings.
