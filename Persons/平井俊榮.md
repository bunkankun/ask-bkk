Píngjǐng Jùnróng 平井俊榮 / Hirai Shun'ei (b. 1935) — modern Japanese Buddhologist; author of *Sanron-kyōgaku no kenkyū* 三論教學の研究 (1990) and a leading specialist on Sanron-school exegesis.
