# 今覞

Jīnyào (dates uncertain), late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng-school 曹洞宗 Chán monk of Léifēng 雷峯 monastery in Pānyú 番禺. A senior dharma-heir of [[圅昰]] Tiānrán Hánshì (1608–1685); named in [[KR6i0351]] as one of the proofreaders (較) of the *Léngqié jīng xīnyìn*.
