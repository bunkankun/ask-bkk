Zhào Xìn 趙信 (b. 1701, *zì* Yìlín 意林, *hào* Xiùlín 秀林), of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Younger brother of [[趙昱]] Zhào Yù. Co-owner with his brother of the *Xiǎoshāntáng* 小山堂 library at Hángzhōu. Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng Hángzhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È; contributed 100 poems to the collaborative *Nán Sòng záshì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]]. Remained at *zhūshēng* level; produced the *Xiùlín* poetry collection. CBDB id 84081; birth-year 1701, death-year not recorded.
