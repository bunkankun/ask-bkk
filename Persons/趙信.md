Zhào Xìn 趙信 (b. 1701, *zì* Yì-lín 意林, *hào* Xiù-lín 秀林), of Rén-hé 仁和 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Younger brother of [[趙昱]] Zhào Yù. Co-owner with his brother of the *Xiǎo-shān-táng* 小山堂 library at Háng-zhōu. Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōng-zhèng Háng-zhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È; contributed 100 poems to the collaborative *Nán-Sòng zá-shì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]]. Remained at *zhū-shēng* level; produced the *Xiù-lín* poetry collection. CBDB id 84081; birth-year 1701, death-year not recorded.
