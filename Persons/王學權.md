Wáng Xuéquán 王學權, Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng-era (mid-to-late 18th to early 19th c.) physician of **Hǎiníng** 海寧 (Zhèjiāng), **great-grandfather** of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng (Mèngyīng, 1808–1868). Founder of the Wáng-family medical lineage that culminates in Wáng Mèngyīng's *Wēnbìng* synthesis. Author of [[KR3eq089]] *Chóngqìngtáng suíbǐ* 重慶堂隨筆 — distinguished by its sustained critical engagement with the Jesuit *Rénshēn túshuō* 人身圖說 and *Rénshēn shuōgài* 人身說概 (Adam Schall / Johann Schreck), as well as with Wáng Qīngrèn's *Yīlín gǎicuò* (1830). The received text is a posthumous *zēngjiào* recension prepared by Wáng Mèngyīng for print in 1855 (Xiánfēng 5). Not in CBDB.
