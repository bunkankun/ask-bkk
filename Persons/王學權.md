Wáng Xué-quán 王學權, Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng-era (mid-to-late 18th to early 19th c.) physician of **Hǎi-níng** 海寧 (Zhèjiāng), **great-grandfather** of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shì-xióng (Mèng-yīng, 1808–1868). Founder of the Wáng-family medical lineage that culminates in Wáng Mèng-yīng's *Wēn-bìng* synthesis. Author of [[KR3eq089]] *Chóng-qìng-táng suí-bǐ* 重慶堂隨筆 — distinguished by its sustained critical engagement with the Jesuit *Rén-shēn tú-shuō* 人身圖說 and *Rén-shēn shuō-gài* 人身說概 (Adam Schall / Johann Schreck), as well as with Wáng Qīng-rèn's *Yī-lín gǎi-cuò* (1830). The received text is a posthumous *zēng-jiào* recension prepared by Wáng Mèng-yīng for print in 1855 (Xián-fēng 5). Not in CBDB.
