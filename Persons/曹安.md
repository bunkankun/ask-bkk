Cáo Ān 曹安 (*fl.* 1444–1487; CBDB id 454868), *zì* Yǐ-níng 以寧, was a mid-Míng literatus of Sōng-jiāng 松江 (Wú-sōng). *Jǔ-rén* of *Zhèng-tǒng jiǎ-zǐ* (1444); served as *Ān-qiū-xiàn jiào-yù* 安邱縣教諭 (Education Officer of Ān-qiū county); was also recruited as a senior compiler (*zǒng-cái guān*) for the *Xiàn-zōng shí-lù* — the imperial *Veritable Records* of the *Chéng-huà* emperor. He composed many poems and prose works (the *Sì-kù* editors note "his composed work is much, but the poetic and prose collections have not been transmitted"). His one surviving work is the *Lán yán cháng yǔ* 讕言長語 ([[KR3j0151]]) in 1 *juàn*, a *bǐjì* of long lifetime hearings and observations, dated *Chéng-huà* 22 = 1486 fifth month. The book's *lùn dú jīng* (discussion of reading the classics) entry is regarded by the *Sì-kù* editors as *qiē zhōng míng-dài sú-xué zhī bì* — "deeply hitting the failings of Míng-period vulgar-learning."
