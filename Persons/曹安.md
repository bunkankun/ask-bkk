Cáo Ān 曹安 (*fl.* 1444–1487; CBDB id 454868), *zì* Yǐníng 以寧, was a mid-Míng literatus of Sōngjiāng 松江 (Wúsōng). *Jǔrén* of *Zhèngtǒng jiǎzǐ* (1444); served as *Ānqiūxiàn jiàoyù* 安邱縣教諭 (Education Officer of Ānqiū county); was also recruited as a senior compiler (*zǒngcái guān*) for the *Xiànzōng shílù* — the imperial *Veritable Records* of the *Chénghuà* emperor. He composed many poems and prose works (the *Sìkù* editors note "his composed work is much, but the poetic and prose collections have not been transmitted"). His one surviving work is the *Lán yán cháng yǔ* 讕言長語 ([[KR3j0151]]) in 1 *juàn*, a *bǐjì* of long lifetime hearings and observations, dated *Chénghuà* 22 = 1486 fifth month. The book's *lùn dú jīng* (discussion of reading the classics) entry is regarded by the *Sìkù* editors as *qiē zhōng míngdài súxué zhī bì* — "deeply hitting the failings of Míng-period vulgar-learning."
