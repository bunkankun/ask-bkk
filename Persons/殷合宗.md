**Yīn Hézōng** 殷合宗 of Lújiāng 廬江, Qīng-era descendant of [[殷宅心]] Yīn Zháixīn (Wáng Kěntáng's pupil); through Yīn Hézōng the manuscript of [[KR3eq046]] *Yīxué qióngyuán jí* came into the hands of [[湯世質]] Tāng Shìzhì who printed it in 1808.
