Qī Guāng 戚光 (*fl.* early 14th c.), Yuán scholar of Jīnlíng 金陵 (modern Nánjīng). His sole known scholarly contribution is the *yīnshì* 音釋 (one-juàn phonetic and textual notes) to [[陸游]] Lù Yóu's *Nán Táng shū* [[KR2i0018]]. The *yīnshì* was printed in *Tiānlì* 天歷 1 (1328) by his student [[程塾]] Chéng Shú of the Jīnlíng *Bóshì* 博士 with a preface by Yuán *Jíxiányuàn xuéshì* 集賢院學士 Zhào Shìyán 趙世延; the edition is the standard transmission of the *Nán Táng shū* and is the basis of the Yuán-period text. Qī Guāng's career is otherwise unrecorded; he had no biography in the *Yuánshǐ*. He may also have worked on a related *Nán Táng shū yīnshì* — Mǎ Duānlín's 馬端臨 *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* and the Sìkù editors mention his name in connection with the alternative title "*Jiāngnán Guǎnzǎi*" (a corruption of *Jiāngnán Yúzǎi* [[KR2i0012]]).
