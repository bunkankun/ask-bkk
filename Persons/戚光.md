Qī Guāng 戚光 (*fl.* early 14th c.), Yuán scholar of Jīn-líng 金陵 (modern Nánjīng). His sole known scholarly contribution is the *yīn-shì* 音釋 (one-juàn phonetic and textual notes) to [[陸游]] Lù Yóu's *Nán-Táng shū* [[KR2i0018]]. The *yīn-shì* was printed in *Tiān-lì* 天歷 1 (1328) by his student [[程塾]] Chéng Shú of the Jīn-líng *Bó-shì* 博士 with a preface by Yuán *Jí-xián-yuàn xué-shì* 集賢院學士 Zhào Shì-yán 趙世延; the edition is the standard transmission of the *Nán-Táng shū* and is the basis of the Yuán-period text. Qī Guāng's career is otherwise unrecorded; he had no biography in the *Yuán-shǐ*. He may also have worked on a related *Nán-Táng shū yīn-shì* — Mǎ Duān-lín's 馬端臨 *Wén-xiàn tōng-kǎo* and the Sìkù editors mention his name in connection with the alternative title "*Jiāng-nán Guǎn-zǎi*" (a corruption of *Jiāng-nán Yú-zǎi* [[KR2i0012]]).
