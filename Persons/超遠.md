Chāo-yuǎn 超遠, *zì* **Zǐ-sù** 子肅. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wén-ruò Xíng-yíng** 文弱行盈 (Lóng-chí line). Born **崇禎辛未 10/18 辰時** = **1631-11-11 Gregorian**; died **己巳 11/4 寅時** = **1689-12-15 Gregorian**, age **59**. Native of **Wú-wéi-zhōu** 無為州; lay surname **Zhào** 趙.

**Formation.** At 6 tonsured at **Sì-miàn-shān Shuǐ-jié-quán Chán-shī** 四面山水潔泉. Subsequent **Liù-ān-shān Mó-yún-ān** 六安山摩雲菴. Full precepts at 22 under **Huì-shān Yuán** 會山元. Studied Vinaya under **Huá-shān Jiàn-yuè** 華山見月. At 34 (1665) consulted **Fú-shān Wén-ruò Xíng-yíng** 浮山文弱行盈 with breakthrough; transmitted as 33rd-generation Línjì.

**Abbacy.** **Huá-zhōu Zhōng-nán-shān Pán-lóng gǔ-sì Zhèng-zhí-diàn** 華州終南山蟠龍古寺正直殿 from **康熙十一年 = 1672/8/19**. Twelve heirs including **Jiè-yuè Huì** 戒月慧, **Xīn-yìn Xǐ** 心印璽, **Kōng-gǔ Yuán** 空谷源, **Xùn-léi Míng-xǐ** 迅雷明喜 (compiler of 《緇門世譜》).

**Work.** [[KR6q0521]] 《終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B396 (also 卍續藏), compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-míng** [[性明]] *biān* + **Hǎi-kuò** [[海闊]] *děng lù*; juan 1 *xíng-shí*.
