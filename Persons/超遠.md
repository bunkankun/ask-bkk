Chāoyuǎn 超遠, *zì* **Zǐsù** 子肅. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wénruò Xíngyíng** 文弱行盈 (Lóngchí line). Born **崇禎辛未 10/18 辰時** = **1631-11-11 Gregorian**; died **己巳 11/4 寅時** = **1689-12-15 Gregorian**, age **59**. Native of **Wúwéizhōu** 無為州; lay surname **Zhào** 趙.

**Formation.** At 6 tonsured at **Sìmiànshān Shuǐjiéquán Chánshī** 四面山水潔泉. Subsequent **Liùānshān Móyúnān** 六安山摩雲菴. Full precepts at 22 under **Huìshān Yuán** 會山元. Studied Vinaya under **Huáshān Jiànyuè** 華山見月. At 34 (1665) consulted **Fúshān Wénruò Xíngyíng** 浮山文弱行盈 with breakthrough; transmitted as 33rd-generation Línjì.

**Abbacy.** **Huázhōu Zhōngnánshān Pánlóng gǔsì Zhèngzhídiàn** 華州終南山蟠龍古寺正直殿 from **康熙十一年 = 1672/8/19**. Twelve heirs including **Jièyuè Huì** 戒月慧, **Xīnyìn Xǐ** 心印璽, **Kōnggǔ Yuán** 空谷源, **Xùnléi Míngxǐ** 迅雷明喜 (compiler of 《緇門世譜》).

**Work.** [[KR6q0521]] 《終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B396 (also 卍續藏), compiled by *fǎsì* **Xìngmíng** [[性明]] *biān* + **Hǎikuò** [[海闊]] *děng lù*; juan 1 *xíngshí*.
