Northern-Sòng court Daoist, *hào* Xūjìng dàshī 虛靖大師 ("Great Master of Void Tranquillity"). Author of [[KR5a0116|Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註]] (DZ 116), a twelve-folio commentary on the *Yīnfú jīng* whose preface narrates the origin-legend of the Queen Mother of the West (*Xī wángmǔ* 西王母) revealing the teaching to the Yellow Emperor.
