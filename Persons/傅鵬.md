Fù Péng 傅鵬. Lay editor of **Bóshān Sùrú Hónghàn** [[弘瀚]] (1630–1706). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0597]] 《博山粟如瀚禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B490.
