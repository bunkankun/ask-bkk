**Máo Xiáng-lín** 毛祥麟 (*hào* **Duì-shān** 對山), Qīng physician of the Dào-guāng — Tóng-zhì era (active 1820s–1870s by internal evidence), principal author of **[[KR3eq013]]** *Duì-shān yī-huà* 對山醫話 and of the parallel *Mò-yú lù* 墨余錄 (Inkstone Leftovers) miscellany of clinical-theoretical jottings. Geographical base unclear from the available evidence (his anecdotes range across Sū-zhōu, Qīng-pǔ, Suǐ-lǐ, and Shàng-hǎi). His work is among the most useful late-Qīng sources for mid-nineteenth-century mainstream physician-anecdote and for the early Chinese reception of Western medical practice. Cáo Bǐng-zhāng 曹炳章 (1878–1956) prepared a *Bǔ-biān* of additional Máo material for the 1936 *Zhōng-guó yī-xué dà-chéng*. Not in CBDB.
