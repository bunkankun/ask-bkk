**Máo Xiánglín** 毛祥麟 (*hào* **Duìshān** 對山), Qīng physician of the Dàoguāng — Tóngzhì era (active 1820s–1870s by internal evidence), principal author of **[[KR3eq013]]** *Duìshān yīhuà* 對山醫話 and of the parallel *Mòyú lù* 墨余錄 (Inkstone Leftovers) miscellany of clinical-theoretical jottings. Geographical base unclear from the available evidence (his anecdotes range across Sūzhōu, Qīngpǔ, Suǐlǐ, and Shànghǎi). His work is among the most useful late-Qīng sources for mid-nineteenth-century mainstream physician-anecdote and for the early Chinese reception of Western medical practice. Cáo Bǐngzhāng 曹炳章 (1878–1956) prepared a *Bǔbiān* of additional Máo material for the 1936 *Zhōngguó yīxué dàchéng*. Not in CBDB.
