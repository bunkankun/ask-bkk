Jiāng Cì 姜垐, late-Kāngxī to early-Yōngzhèng-era Wúxī literati, author of the *Jī Liúshān xiānshēng zhuàn* 嵇留山先生傳 — the principal biography of [[嵇永仁]] Jī Yǒngrén (1637–1676) included as the *fù-lù* opening of the *Bào-dú shān-fáng jí* ([[KR4f0028]]). No independent CBDB record. Jiāng is identified in the *Sìkù tíyào* and the *fù-lù* simply as the author of the biographical sketch, evidently a close friend of the Jī family and active in the post-*Sān fān* recovery of Yǒng-rén's reputation and writings.
