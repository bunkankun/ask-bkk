Bào Biāo 鮑彪 (fl. mid-12th c.), *zì* Wénhǔ 文虎, was a Southern-Sòng scholar from Jìnyún 縉雲 in the Lóngquán 龍泉 area of Chùzhōu 處州 (modern Zhèjiāng). The Sìkù editors infer his *zì* from the citations of his remarks under the rubric "Bào Wénhǔ shuō" 鮑文虎說 in Huáng Hè 黃鶴's *Dù shī bǔzhù* 杜詩補注 and Guō Zhīdá 郭知達's *Jí zhù jiǔ jiā Dù shī* 集注九家杜詩. He held office as a Director (*shàngshū láng* 尚書郎). His one substantial scholarly work is the *Bào shì Zhànguó cè zhù* 鮑氏戰國策注 [[KR2e0004]], a complete re-annotation and chronological rearrangement of the [[KR2e0003]] *Zhànguó cè* completed in 1147 (Shàoxīng 17). Although the Yuán scholar [[吳師道]] (in his *Bǔzhèng* [[KR2e0005]]) and the Sòng-period Zhào Yǔshí 趙與時 (in the *Bīntuì lù*) both criticized Bào Biāo's handling of the Eastern and Western Zhōu chapters, his recension stands as the earliest sustained Sòng-Confucian historicization of the late–Warring-States intriguers' speeches, and his commentary remained the working base for all subsequent annotated *Zhànguó cè* editions through the Yuán and Míng. His birth and death years are not preserved.
