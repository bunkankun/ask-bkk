Late-Yuán Jiāngxī literatus of **Xūjiāng** 旴江 (Jiāngxī); *zì* **Xiùchōng** 秀充. Life-dates not recorded. He is known only as a co-anthologised poet in **[[KR4h0089]]** *Yuányīn yíxiǎng*, in which his verse together with that of [[劉紹]] occupies the final 2 *juǎn* (after the 8 *juǎn* of [[胡布]]). His career is otherwise undocumented; the SKQS editors group him as a Yuán *yílǎo* on internal evidence.
