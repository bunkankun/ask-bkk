**Lǐ Chúnfēng** 李淳風 (602–670) was an early-Táng polymath — court astronomer, mathematician, and calendar-reformer — author of the *Lín dé lì* 麟德曆 calendar (665), commentator on the *Shí bù suàn jīng* 十部算經 (Ten Mathematical Classics), and co-author with Yuán Tiāngāng 袁天綱 of the famous prophetic *Tuī bèi tú* 推背圖. Also credited pseudepigraphically as author of Daoist ritual-divinatory texts, including [[KR5d0035]] *Jīn suǒ liú zhū yǐn* 金鎖流珠引 (29 *juàn*) — a massive ritual-astrological compendium.
