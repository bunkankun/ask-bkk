**Chǔ Yuān** 褚淵 (435–482), great Southern-Qí official (*tàizǎi shìzhōng lùshàngshū gōng* 太宰侍中錄尚書公), brother of [[褚澄]] Chǔ Chéng, the physician to whom [[KR3eq029]] *Chǔshì yíshū* is attributed.
