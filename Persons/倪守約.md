Ní Shǒu-yuē 倪守約 (fl. Xián-chún 1265–1274), *hào* Zhú-quán 竹泉, self-styled *Sōng-shān yǔ-shì* 松山羽士 (Daoist priest of Sōng-shān), was a late-Sòng Daoist priest of the Sōng-shān abbey at Mt. Chì-sōng 赤松山 in Jīn-huá 金華 (Wǔ-zhōu, Zhèjiāng). His preface records that he renounced household and parents in his youth and entered monastic life under a Master at Mt. Chì-sōng, where he had been practising for over forty years at the time of compilation. His sole transmitted work is the *Jīn-huá Chì-sōng-shān zhì* [[KR5b0306]] (DZ 601), a 1-juǎn Daoist mountain-monograph and the foundational documentary source for the cult of Huáng Chū-píng / Huáng Dà-xiān 黃大仙. Composed in the Xián-chún era under Sòng Dù-zōng, shortly before the Mongol conquest. CBDB has no entry under this name.
