Ní Shǒuyuē 倪守約 (fl. Xiánchún 1265–1274), *hào* Zhúquán 竹泉, self-styled *Sōngshān yǔshì* 松山羽士 (Daoist priest of Sōngshān), was a late-Sòng Daoist priest of the Sōngshān abbey at Mt. Chìsōng 赤松山 in Jīnhuá 金華 (Wǔzhōu, Zhèjiāng). His preface records that he renounced household and parents in his youth and entered monastic life under a Master at Mt. Chìsōng, where he had been practising for over forty years at the time of compilation. His sole transmitted work is the *Jīnhuá Chìsōngshān zhì* [[KR5b0306]] (DZ 601), a 1-juǎn Daoist mountain-monograph and the foundational documentary source for the cult of Huáng Chūpíng / Huáng Dàxiān 黃大仙. Composed in the Xiánchún era under Sòng Dùzōng, shortly before the Mongol conquest. CBDB has no entry under this name.
