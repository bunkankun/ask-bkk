# Yuánchéng 圓澄 (Zhànrán Yuánchéng 湛然圓澄)

Late-Míng Cáodòng-lineage Chán master. *Hào* Zhànrán 湛然 ("Still-and-Pure"), Sànmù dàorén 散木道人 ("Daoist of Dispersed Wood"); also called Yúnmén Chéng 雲門澄 (for his Yúnmén-sì 雲門寺 abbacy) and Xiǎnshèng Chéng 顯聖澄 (for his Xiǎnshèng-sì 顯聖寺 abbacy). Lay surname Xià 夏. Native of Kuàijī 會稽 (modern Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). Lifedates 1561/9/23 (Jiājìng 40 / 8/5) – 1627/1/20 (Tiānqǐ bǐng-yín / 12/4, age 66, per the *Zhàn-rán Yuánchéng chánshī yǔlù* 湛然圓澄禪師語錄 juan 8 tomb-inscription).

A central figure in the late-Míng Cáodòng revival. Initially tonsured at Tiānhuāng-shān 天荒山 under Miào-fēng 妙峰; received full precepts from [[袾宏|Yúnqī Zhūhóng]] 雲棲袾宏 (showing the cross-lineage connections typical of late Míng); after a period in closed retreat at Bǎolín 寶林, received dharma-transmission (*dé fǎ* 得法) from Dàjué Niàn 大覺念 (1552–1594) of the Cáodòng line. Abbacies at Yúnmén 雲門 (his main seat in Kuàijī), Jìngshān 徑山, Dōngtǎ 東塔 (in Hé-zhōu 禾州), and Xiǎnshèng 顯聖. Six recorded dharma-heirs: Míng-huái 明懷, Míng-yú 明盂, Míng-fāng 明方, and others.

Major works:
- *Kǎi gǔ lù* 慨古錄 [[KR6q0171]] — 1607 reformist polemic against late-Míng monastic corruption.
- *Zōng mén huò wèn* 宗門或問 — dialogical Chán apologetic.
- *Léng-yán yì shuō* 楞嚴臆說, *Fǎ-huá yì yǔ* 法華意語, *Huì Niè-pán shū* 會涅槃疏 — commentaries on the *Śūraṅgama*, *Lotus*, and *Mahāparinirvāṇa* sūtras respectively.
- *Zhàn-rán Yuánchéng chánshī yǔlù* 湛然圓澄禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0374]], X72 n1444; J28 B208 in the Jiāxīng canon; 8 juan) — his collected Chán sermons, compiled posthumously by his disciple [[明凡]] and edited by the lay literati [[丁元公]] and [[祁駿佳]].

# Yuán-chéng 圓澄

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[慧性]] Wúmíng Huìxìng (1162–1237); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0312]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A021629). Not to be confused with the Míng-era Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng (1561–1627) above.
