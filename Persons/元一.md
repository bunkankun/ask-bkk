Yuányī 元一, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Yúnsǒu Běnzhù** [[住]] 雲叟本住 (1620–1676) at **Tánzhōu Lóngyásì** 潭州龍牙寺. Lead recorder of the 2-juan 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0481]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
