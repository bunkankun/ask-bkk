Yuán-yī 元一, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Yún-sǒu Běn-zhù** [[住]] 雲叟本住 (1620–1676) at **Tán-zhōu Lóng-yá-sì** 潭州龍牙寺. Lead recorder of the 2-juan 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0481]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
