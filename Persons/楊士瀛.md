**Yáng Shìyíng** 楊士瀛 (*zì* Dēngfù 登父, *hào* Rénzhāi 仁齋, *fl.* Jǐng-dìng / Xián-chún reigns, 1264, late 南宋), Southern-Sòng physician of Fú-zhōu 福州 (Fú-jiàn). Author of the comprehensive medical compendium ***Rénzhāi zhí-zhǐ*** 仁齋直指 ([[KR3e0045]], 26 juan in 79 entries, completed Jǐng-dìng jiǎ-zǐ = 1264) — a major synthetic exposition of Sòng medicine drawing on the classical canon and the Three-Causes school of Chén Yán; and the ***Shānghán lèi-shū*** 傷寒類書 (also catalogued as part of [[KR3e0045]] in some recensions, 7 juan, completed before the *Zhí-zhǐ*). His earlier works include the *Huó-rén zǒng-kuò* 活人總括 and the *Yīng'ér zhǐ-yào* 嬰兒指要*. The Míng physician [[朱崇正]] Zhū Chóngzhèng (*zì* Zōngrú 宗儒, of Huī-zhōu) added supplementary entries (附遺 *fù-yí* "appended-supplementaries") in his Jiājìng-period reprint, the SKQS-base recension. Yáng has no further transmitted biographical record.
