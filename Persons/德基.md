Dìngān Déjī 定菴德基 (1634–1700/01) was the third-generation patriarch of the **Bǎohuáshān Lǜzōng** 寶華山律宗 in the early Qīng. Native of **Pútián** 莆田 (Fújiàn). Born Chóngzhēn 7 (1634), 3rd month, 24th day; died Kāngxī *gēngchén* (1700/01), 1st month, 8th day, aged 67. He was the dharma-heir of **Sānmèi Jìguāng** 三昧寂光's principal disciple **Jiànyuè Dútǐ** 見月讀體 ([[讀體]], 1601–1679) and the immediate successor at Bǎohuáshān after Dútǐ's death.

After more than twenty summers of intensive study of the *Sìfēn lǜ* corpus, he produced his major Vinaya commentary, the **Píní guānyào** 毗尼關要 in sixteen *juan* ([[KR6k0152]]) — the principal early-Qīng study manual of *Sìfēn* discipline at Bǎohuáshān — together with the companion glossary **Píní guānyào shìyì** 毗尼關要事義 ([[KR6k0153]]). His Vinaya teaching defined the early-Qīng Bǎohuáshān curriculum and formed the practical complement to Zhìxù's [[KR6k0151]] *Chóngzhì píní shìyì jíyào*.
