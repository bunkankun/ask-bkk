Dìng-ān Dé-jī 定菴德基 (1634–1700/01) was the third-generation patriarch of the **Bǎo-huá-shān Lǜ-zōng** 寶華山律宗 in the early Qīng. Native of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Fú-jiàn). Born Chóngzhēn 7 (1634), 3rd month, 24th day; died Kāngxī *gēng-chén* (1700/01), 1st month, 8th day, aged 67. He was the dharma-heir of **Sān-mèi Jì-guāng** 三昧寂光's principal disciple **Jiàn-yuè Dú-tǐ** 見月讀體 ([[讀體]], 1601–1679) and the immediate successor at Bǎo-huá-shān after Dú-tǐ's death.

After more than twenty summers of intensive study of the *Sìfēn lǜ* corpus, he produced his major Vinaya commentary, the **Pí-ní guān-yào** 毗尼關要 in sixteen *juan* ([[KR6k0152]]) — the principal early-Qīng study manual of *Sìfēn* discipline at Bǎo-huá-shān — together with the companion glossary **Pí-ní guān-yào shì-yì** 毗尼關要事義 ([[KR6k0153]]). His Vinaya teaching defined the early-Qīng Bǎo-huá-shān curriculum and formed the practical complement to Zhìxù's [[KR6k0151]] *Chóng-zhì pí-ní shì-yì jí-yào*.
