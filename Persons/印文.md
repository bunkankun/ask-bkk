Yìn-wén 印文, *zì* **Líng-yǐn** 靈隱 ("Numinous Hermit"); often referred to as **Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén** 靈隱印文. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Lifedates unknown — active in Yún-nán in 1661 when his disciple **Méi-xī Fú-dù** [[福度]] (1637–1701) received transmission from him at age 25. Native of **Kuí-zhōu Gāo-liáng** 夔州高梁 (= 梁山, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** Took tonsure at **Mù-lán-sì** 木蘭寺 under **Dà-qiān** 大千和尚. Resolutely undertook *cān-fǎng*; first under **Huáng-bò** 黃檗 for years of austere practice. Reading the *Chán-zōng zhèng-mài* 禪宗正脉 at the *xiàng-chē bù-xíng dǎ-niú jí shì* 向車不行打牛即是 *gōng-àn*, suddenly broke through "and his body and mind became light and easy." At **Zhōng-lù-sī** 中路司 met **Wàn-fēng Míng** 萬峰明 — received final *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies (across Yún-nán / Guì-zhōu).** **Ān-shùn Jìng-lè** 安順靜樂, **Yún-nán Yún-jí** 雲南雲集, **Dòu-bǎo Guān-yīn** 竇堡觀音 (where in **辛丑冬** = **1661** winter, on the *fó chéng-dào rì*, his disciple Fú-dù received *fù-fǎ*), **Hóng-jiāng Huí-lóng** 洪江迴龍, **Qián-nán Zǐ-zhú** 黔南紫竹.

**Disciples (fourteen).** **Cāng-bì Míng-ruì** 蒼璧明睿, **Dà-tóng Hé-rén** 大同合仁, **Mì-yìn Zhēn-chuán** 密印真傳, **Tiān-rú Qīng-jué** 天如清覺, **Méi-xī Fú-dù** [[福度]] (1637–1701, of Yún-nán-Guì-zhōu), and others.

**Work.** [[KR6q0560]] 《靈隱文禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B446, *fù biān* (re-compiled) by *fǎ-sì* **Méi-xī Fú-dù** [[福度]]; preface dated **康熙戊寅歲季夏月吉旦** = **1698** lunar 6.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 70; 《靈隱文禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈聯芳偈〉.
