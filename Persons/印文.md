Yìnwén 印文, *zì* **Língyǐn** 靈隱 ("Numinous Hermit"); often referred to as **Língyǐn Yìnwén** 靈隱印文. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Lifedates unknown — active in Yúnnán in 1661 when his disciple **Méixī Fúdù** [[福度]] (1637–1701) received transmission from him at age 25. Native of **Kuízhōu Gāoliáng** 夔州高梁 (= 梁山, Sìchuān); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** Took tonsure at **Mùlánsì** 木蘭寺 under **Dàqiān** 大千和尚. Resolutely undertook *cānfǎng*; first under **Huángbò** 黃檗 for years of austere practice. Reading the *Chánzōng zhèngmài* 禪宗正脉 at the *xiàngchē bùxíng dǎniú jí shì* 向車不行打牛即是 *gōngàn*, suddenly broke through "and his body and mind became light and easy." At **Zhōnglùsī** 中路司 met **Wànfēng Míng** 萬峰明 — received final *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies (across Yúnnán / Guìzhōu).** **Ānshùn Jìnglè** 安順靜樂, **Yúnnán Yúnjí** 雲南雲集, **Dòubǎo Guānyīn** 竇堡觀音 (where in **辛丑冬** = **1661** winter, on the *fó chéngdào rì*, his disciple Fúdù received *fùfǎ*), **Hóngjiāng Huílóng** 洪江迴龍, **Qiánnán Zǐzhú** 黔南紫竹.

**Disciples (fourteen).** **Cāngbì Míngruì** 蒼璧明睿, **Dàtóng Hérén** 大同合仁, **Mìyìn Zhēnchuán** 密印真傳, **Tiānrú Qīngjué** 天如清覺, **Méixī Fúdù** [[福度]] (1637–1701, of YúnnánGuìzhōu), and others.

**Work.** [[KR6q0560]] 《靈隱文禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B446, *fù biān* (re-compiled) by *fǎsì* **Méixī Fúdù** [[福度]]; preface dated **康熙戊寅歲季夏月吉旦** = **1698** lunar 6.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 70; 《靈隱文禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈聯芳偈〉.
