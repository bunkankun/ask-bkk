Southern-Sòng statesman, *zì* Zǐgōng 子公, son of [[張根]] Zhāng Gēn. Rose under Xiàozōng to *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 (Vice Privy Councillor). His advancement entailed the posthumous elevation of his father Zhāng Gēn to the rank of *Tàishī* 太師 ("Grand Tutor"), as recorded in the postface to the *Wúyuán Zhōuyì jiě* ([[KR1a0019]]) by his son [[張垓]] Zhāng Gāi.
