Fù Wáng-lù 傅王露 (b. 1678), *zì* Liáng-xī 良谿, of Cí-xī 慈谿 (Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng), was a Kāng-xī 51 (1712) *jìnshì* who served as Hànlín Compiler before retirement. In retirement he was engaged by the Yōngzhèng-era Zhèjiāng Governor-General Lǐ Wèi 李衛 to direct the compilation of the *Xī-hú zhì* in 48 *juan* (Yōng-zhèng era; Shěn Dé-qián served as a sub-redactor at this stage). Subsequently in the Qiánlóng era he co-compiled (with Shěn Dé-qián and Liáng Shīzhèng) the imperial-redacted *Xī-hú zhì-zuǎn* ([[KR2k0089]]) of 1753. CBDB id 90098 confirms birth 1678; death not recorded but presumably in the early Qiánlóng era.
