Fù Wánglù 傅王露 (b. 1678), *zì* Liángxī 良谿, of Cíxī 慈谿 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng), was a Kāngxī 51 (1712) *jìnshì* who served as Hànlín Compiler before retirement. In retirement he was engaged by the Yōngzhèng-era Zhèjiāng Governor-General Lǐ Wèi 李衛 to direct the compilation of the *Xīhú zhì* in 48 *juan* (Yōngzhèng era; Shěn Déqián served as a sub-redactor at this stage). Subsequently in the Qiánlóng era he co-compiled (with Shěn Déqián and Liáng Shīzhèng) the imperial-redacted *Xīhú zhìzuǎn* ([[KR2k0089]]) of 1753. CBDB id 90098 confirms birth 1678; death not recorded but presumably in the early Qiánlóng era.
