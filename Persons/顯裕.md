Xiǎnyù 顯裕, *shìzhě* 侍者 attendant-disciple of **Dàyǔ Xíngtāo** [[行韜]] 大庾行韜 (d. 1652) at **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** 天台國清寺. Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《國清大庾韜禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0477]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
