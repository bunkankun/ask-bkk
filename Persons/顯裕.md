Xiǎn-yù 顯裕, *shì-zhě* 侍者 attendant-disciple of **Dà-yǔ Xíng-tāo** [[行韜]] 大庾行韜 (d. 1652) at **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** 天台國清寺. Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《國清大庾韜禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0477]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
