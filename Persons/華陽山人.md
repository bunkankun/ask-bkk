**Huáyáng shānrén** 華陽山人 ("the Mountain-Hermit of Huá-yáng") is the studio-name (*hào*) of the mid-Qīng physician who taught Zhīyǔ qiáokè 芝嶼樵客, the author of *Ér-kē xǐng* 兒科醒 ([[KR3ej008]]). Huáyáng wrote the preface to that work, identifying himself only as the author's teacher (*yǔ dì-zǐ Zhīyǔ shòu yǔ yì wéi Ér-kē shū* 予弟子芝嶼受予意為兒科書). The personal name has not been securely identified. The *hào* references Mount Huáyáng 華陽山 (one of several places of that name, including those in Jiāng-sū and Sì-chuān) but does not by itself permit identification. Mid-Qīng dating from internal evidence of *Ér-kē xǐng*.
