Pǔ-ruì 普瑞 (active mid-14th c.), Yuán Buddhist scholar-monk; compiler of the [[KR6e0120|*Huáyán xuán tán huì xuán jì* 華嚴懸談會玄記]] (X236, 40 fasc.) — the great 40-fascicle Yuán-period synthesis-commentary on the *xuán tán* sections of [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's [[KR6e0011|*Shū*]] and [[KR6e0012|*Chāo*]]. The work is one of the most ambitious single Yuán Buddhist commentarial projects, drawing together the entire Sòng-period commentary tradition on Chéngguān's preliminary doctrinal essays. Birth and death dates not preserved.
