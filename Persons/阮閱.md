Ruǎn Yuè 阮閱 (fl. 1124–1130s), *zì* Hóngxiū 閎休, native of Shūchéng 舒城 (modern Ānhuī). At *Xuānhé jiǎchén* (1124) Prefect of Chēnzhōu 郴州 (Húnán), where he composed the *Chēnjiāng bǎiyǒng* 郴江百詠 [[KR4d0186]]. *Jiànyán* beginning, Prefect of Yuánzhōu 袁州 (Jiāngxī).

Compiler of the substantial *shīhuà* compilation *Shīhuà zǒngguī* 詩話總龜 (catalog meta KR4h-series). Author of *Sōngjú jí* 松菊集 — now lost.

The *Sìkù* tíyào of [[KR4d0186]] notes Ruǎn's poetry is heavily flavored with *Chán* (Zen) — typical of the Sòng period — but Ruǎn's poetic learning is sufficient that he avoids the *suānfǔ* (sour-pedantic) mode. The *Chēnjiāng bǎiyǒng* preface (dated *Xuānhé jiǎchén* 1124, 2nd month *Zhōnghé* day, by 舒城阮閱序) explains the work as filling-in poetic coverage of Chēnzhōu sites that earlier Tang poets (Dù Fǔ, Hán Yù, Liǔ Zōngyuán, Shěn Quánqí, Dài Shūlún) had only sparsely-treated.
