Ruǎn Yuè 阮閱 (fl. 1124–1130s), *zì* Hóng-xiū 閎休, native of Shū-chéng 舒城 (modern Ān-huī). At *Xuān-hé jiǎ-chén* (1124) Prefect of Chēn-zhōu 郴州 (Hú-nán), where he composed the *Chēn-jiāng bǎi-yǒng* 郴江百詠 [[KR4d0186]]. *Jiàn-yán* beginning, Prefect of Yuán-zhōu 袁州 (Jiāng-xī).

Compiler of the substantial *shī-huà* compilation *Shī-huà zǒng-guī* 詩話總龜 (catalog meta KR4h-series). Author of *Sōng-jú jí* 松菊集 — now lost.

The *Sì-kù* tíyào of [[KR4d0186]] notes Ruǎn's poetry is heavily flavored with *Chán* (Zen) — typical of the Sòng period — but Ruǎn's poetic learning is sufficient that he avoids the *suān-fǔ* (sour-pedantic) mode. The *Chēn-jiāng bǎi-yǒng* preface (dated *Xuān-hé jiǎ-chén* 1124, 2nd month *Zhōng-hé* day, by 舒城阮閱序) explains the work as filling-in poetic coverage of Chēn-zhōu sites that earlier Tang poets (Dù Fǔ, Hán Yù, Liǔ Zōng-yuán, Shěn Quán-qí, Dài Shū-lún) had only sparsely-treated.
