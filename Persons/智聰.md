Southern-Sòng Tiāntái-tradition monk, former abbot (*qián-zhù* 前住) of the Chóng-shàn Jiào-sì 崇善教寺 on Mount Chì-chéng 赤城山 at Tāizhōu 台州 (modern Línhǎi 臨海, Zhèjiāng); author of [[KR6i0565]] *Yuánjué jīng xīn-jìng* 圓覺經心鏡 (X10 no. 254, 6 fascicles). His autograph preface to that work is dated 皇宋寶慶丁亥夏六月 = summer 1227 CE (Sòng Lǐzōng's Bǎo-qìng 3), establishing his active period in the early thirteenth century. His preface relates that he received early monastic training at Tiāntái-shān 天台山 ("早登台嶺") and took up the *Yuánjué jīng* in later life. Lifedates and other biographical details not preserved.

The Mount Chì-chéng region in Tāizhōu, sometimes called the "Northern Gate of Tiāntái-shān", was a major Tiāntái doctrinal-teaching centre throughout the Sòng; the Chóng-shàn Jiào-sì was a recognised Tiāntái *jiào-sì* 教寺.
