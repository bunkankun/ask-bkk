Wú Nè 吳訥 (1372–1457), *zì* Mǐndé 敏德, *hào* Sī'ān 思菴, posthumously *Wénkè* 文恪, was an early-Míng official and *Lǐxué* writer. Native of Chángshú 常熟 (Sūzhōu region). Held office to *Xiàyángxiàn jiàoyù* and *Nánjīng Dūcháyuàn yòu fù dūyùshǐ*. Major works: *Shānzhōng dōng kān shū* 山中冬看書 and *Wénzhāng biàn tǐ* 文章辨體 (genre-classification of Chinese literature). Edited and supplemented Guì Wànróng's *Táng yīn bǐ shì* 棠陰比事 ([[KR3c0008]]) under his *shānbǔ* (cut-and-supplemented) version. CBDB id 66884.
