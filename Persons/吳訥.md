Wú Nè 吳訥 (1372–1457), *zì* Mǐndé 敏德, *hào* Sī'ān 思菴, posthumously *Wénkè* 文恪, was an early-Míng official and *Lǐxué* writer. Native of Cháng-shú 常熟 (Sūzhōu region). Held office to *Xià-yáng-xiàn jiào-yù* and *Nán-jīng Dū-cháyuàn yòu fù dū-yù-shǐ*. Major works: *Shān-zhōng dōng kān shū* 山中冬看書 and *Wén-zhāng biàn tǐ* 文章辨體 (genre-classification of Chinese literature). Edited and supplemented Guì Wànróng's *Táng yīn bǐ shì* 棠陰比事 (KR3c0008) under his *shān-bǔ* (cut-and-supplemented) version. CBDB id 66884.
