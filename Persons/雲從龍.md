# Yún Cónglóng 雲從龍

Yuán-period *àncháshǐ* 按察使 (provincial judicial commissioner) in southern China; patron of [[宗寶]] Zōngbǎo's 1291 editorial project at the Fēngfān Bào'ēn Guāngxiào chánsì in Sháozhōu that produced the received recension of the *Tánjīng*.
