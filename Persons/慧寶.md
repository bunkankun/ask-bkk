Huìbǎo 慧寶, early-Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; lifedates not transmitted; floruit early 11th c. He compiled an extensive **annotated edition** (注 *zhù*) of [[神清]] **Shénqīng's** late-Tang scholarly compendium **《北山錄》** *Běishān lù* ([[KR6r0146]], T2113, 10 juan), preserving much late-Tang and early-Sòng documentary material that would otherwise be lost. The annotated text became a standard Sòng Buddhist scholarly reference, anchoring the **Sòng Tiāntái** establishment's claim of continuity with the late-Tang scholarly tradition. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001742.

(DILA records multiple Huìbǎo entries; the *Bēishān lù* commentator is the A001742 entry.)
