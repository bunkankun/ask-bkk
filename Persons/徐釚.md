Xú Qiú 徐釚 (1636–1708), *zì* Diànfā 電發, *hào* Hóngtíng 虹亭 (and also Júzhuāng 菊莊, after his famous *Júzhuāng yuèfǔ* 菊莊樂府 collection), of Wújiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu region). *Jǔrén* of *Kāngxī yǐwèi* / 1679 *Bóxué hóngcí*; appointed *Hànlínyuàn jiǎntǎo*. Major early-*Kāngxī cí*-poet of the *Yángxiànpài* periphery. Author of the *Júzhuāng yuèfǔ* (his own *cí* collection), the *Nánzhōu cǎotáng cíhuà* 南洲草堂詞話, and — his greatest scholarly work — the *Cíyuàn cóngtán* [[KR4j0085]]: the most learned of all the Qīng *cíhuà*.
