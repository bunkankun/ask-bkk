Xú Qiú 徐釚 (1636–1708), *zì* Diàn-fā 電發, *hào* Hóng-tíng 虹亭 (and also Jú-zhuāng 菊莊, after his famous *Jú-zhuāng yuè-fǔ* 菊莊樂府 collection), of Wú-jiāng 吳江 (Sū-zhōu region). *Jǔ-rén* of *Kāng-xī yǐ-wèi* / 1679 *Bó-xué hóng-cí*; appointed *Hàn-lín-yuàn jiǎn-tǎo*. Major early-*Kāng-xī cí*-poet of the *Yáng-xiàn-pài* periphery. Author of the *Jú-zhuāng yuè-fǔ* (his own *cí* collection), the *Nán-zhōu cǎo-táng cí-huà* 南洲草堂詞話, and — his greatest scholarly work — the *Cí-yuàn cóng-tán* [[KR4j0085]]: the most learned of all the Qīng *cí-huà*.
