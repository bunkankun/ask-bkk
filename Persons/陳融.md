Chén Róng 陳融 (*fl.* 3rd century) is a Sūn-Wú 孫吳 author known almost exclusively from the title-attribution on his lost *Yàoyán* 要言, preserved in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 under the Wú period. No biography survives in the standard histories. The work is recorded as already fragmentary in the Sòng *Chóngwén zǒngmù* 崇文總目. His exact birth and death years are not preserved. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0143]] *Chénzǐ yàoyán* 陳子要言.
