**Zhāng Yù** 張預 (*fl.* late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng literatus. Wrote the 光緒辛丑 (1901) preface to [[陳其瑞]]'s *Běncǎo cuōyào* 本草撮要 ([[KR3ec055]]), praising Chén's clinical effectiveness in his work at the Sūzhōu prefectural medical clinic. No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
