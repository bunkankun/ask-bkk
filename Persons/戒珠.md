Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Yàozhī** 耀之, sobriquet **Fēishān** 飛山 (also referenced as **Fēishān Jièzhū** 飛山戒珠, **Fēishān Mòzǐ** 飛山默子 "Silent-Master of Fēishān"). DILA Authority A000529. Born 985.

Author of the **Biéchuán xīnfǎ yì** 別傳心法議 ([[KR6d0229]], X57n0953, 1 *juan*) — a Northern-Sòng polemical treatise on the doctrine of the "separately-transmitted" (*biéchuán* 別傳) heart-dharma, addressing the standard Chán-school claim to exclusive transmission of the Buddha's awakening.

Source: DILA A000529.
