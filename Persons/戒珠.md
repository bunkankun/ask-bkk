Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Yào-zhī** 耀之, sobriquet **Fēi-shān** 飛山 (also referenced as **Fēi-shān Jiè-zhū** 飛山戒珠, **Fēi-shān Mò-zǐ** 飛山默子 "Silent-Master of Fēi-shān"). DILA Authority A000529. Born 985.

Author of the **Bié-chuán xīn-fǎ yì** 別傳心法議 ([[KR6d0229]], X57n0953, 1 *juan*) — a Northern-Sòng polemical treatise on the doctrine of the "separately-transmitted" (*bié-chuán* 別傳) heart-dharma, addressing the standard Chán-school claim to exclusive transmission of the Buddha's awakening.

Source: DILA A000529.
