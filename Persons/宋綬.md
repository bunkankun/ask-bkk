**Sòng Shòu** 宋綬 (991–1040), *zì* Gōng chuí 公垂, was a Northern-Sòng scholar-official, one of the compilers of the *Tiān shèng biān chì* 天聖編敕 under Sòng Rén zōng, and a noted bibliophile and stylist. Author of [[KR5c0366]] *Sòng Xī Tài yǐ gōng bēi míng* 宋西太乙宮碑銘 (1029) — companion-inscription to Hù Méng's for the Eastern palace.
