Míngzhì 明智, *zì* **Lǚān** 旅菴 ("Travel Hut"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Héyī Chāozì** [[超自]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Míngxiū** [[明修]] and **Mínghuì** [[明惠]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0575]] 《何一自禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B462.

- - - - -

Several other Qīng monks bear the dharma-name **Míngzhì** 明智, including **Xīwéi Míngzhì** 息為明智 (DILA A042255). Not the same person.
