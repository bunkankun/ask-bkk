Míng-zhì 明智, *zì* **Lǚ-ān** 旅菴 ("Travel Hut"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** [[超自]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Míng-xiū** [[明修]] and **Míng-huì** [[明惠]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0575]] 《何一自禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B462.

- - - - -

Several other Qīng monks bear the dharma-name **Míng-zhì** 明智, including **Xī-wéi Míng-zhì** 息為明智 (DILA A042255). Not the same person.
