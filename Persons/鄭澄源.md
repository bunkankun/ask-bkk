# Zhèng Chéngyuán 鄭澄源 (style 學烺 Xuélàng, late Qīng)

A late-Qīng lay-Buddhist (*jūshì* 居士), brother of [[鄭澄德]] Zhèng Chéngdé (style 學川). Together they produced the *Ēmítuó jīng zhù* 阿彌陀經註 [[KR6p0027]] in 咸豐 11 (1861). The two brothers belonged to the *Zōngjìngtáng* 宗鏡堂 lay-Buddhist publishing circle in Hangzhou. Lifedates not preserved; otherwise undocumented in the standard biographical literature.
