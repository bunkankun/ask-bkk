**Guō Pú** 郭璞 (276–324), *zì* Jǐng chún 景純, was an Eastern-Jìn polymath — poet, exegete, philologist, geomancer. The leading *Ěr yǎ* 爾雅 commentator of his age, he also wrote influential commentaries on the *Shān hǎi jīng* 山海經 ([[KR5d0054]]), the *Mù tiān zǐ zhuàn* 穆天子傳, the *Fāng yán* 方言, the *Cǎng Jié piān* 蒼頡篇, and the *Chǔ cí* 楚辭. Executed at 49 in connection with the Wáng Dūn 王敦 rebellion. Guō's *Shān hǎi jīng* commentary is the standard received text.
