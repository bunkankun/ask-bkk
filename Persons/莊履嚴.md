**Zhuāng Lǚyán** 莊履嚴 (*fl.* Qīng dynasty), gynecological physician. Author of the *Fùkē bǎibiàn* 婦科百辨 ([[KR3ei034]]), a one-*juǎn* gynecology in the *bǎibiàn* (one-hundred-discriminations) didactic format. No further biographical detail located in standard reference works; not in CBDB.
