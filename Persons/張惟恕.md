**Zhāng Wéishù** 張惟恕 (*fl.* early-to-mid 16th c., Míng), literatus of Shàng-cài 上蔡 in Hénán. Known principally as the postface-writer (*hòu-xù*) to Wáng Tíngxiāng's *Shè-shēng yào-yì* 攝生要義 ([[KR3eo015]]), in which he identifies the pseudonymous "Hé-bīn zhàng-rén" 河濱丈人 as Wáng Tíngxiāng (then Minister of War). The afterword carries the formula *hòu-xué Shàng-cài Zhāng Wéi-shù shū* 上蔡後學張惟恕書 ("Disciple of the later age, Zhāng Wéi-shù of Shàng-cài, wrote"), signalling a younger contemporary of Wáng. No CBDB record. No other works attributed.
