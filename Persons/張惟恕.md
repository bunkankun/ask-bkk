**Zhāng Wéishù** 張惟恕 (*fl.* early-to-mid 16th c., Míng), literatus of Shàngcài 上蔡 in Hénán. Known principally as the postface-writer (*hòuxù*) to Wáng Tíngxiāng's *Shèshēng yàoyì* 攝生要義 ([[KR3eo015]]), in which he identifies the pseudonymous "Hébīn zhàngrén" 河濱丈人 as Wáng Tíngxiāng (then Minister of War). The afterword carries the formula *hòuxué Shàngcài Zhāng Wéishù shū* 上蔡後學張惟恕書 ("Disciple of the later age, Zhāng Wéishù of Shàngcài, wrote"), signalling a younger contemporary of Wáng. No CBDB record. No other works attributed.
