Hào 昊 (variant 旲), *zì* **Huī-zhōu** 暉洲 (also written 暉州); often referred to as **Huī-zhōu Hào** 暉洲昊 or **Wàn-shàn Huī-zhōu** 萬善暉洲. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pán-shān** 盤山. Lifedates unknown — active **1670s–1699+**. Native of **Luán-zhōu Lè-tíng** 灤州樂亭 (modern **Hébei**, on the road to Manchuria); lay surname **Liú** 劉.

**Formation.** Vegetarian even in the womb; tranquil from birth. At 11 his mother died and his father sent him to **Hán-jiā Dào-xiǎo-kōng** 韓家道曉空 to take tonsure. At 18 he entered *bì-guān* and read the *Lotus Sūtra* devoutly; at the *Ān-lè-xíng-pǐn* 安樂行品 verse "*Fó-zǐ zhù cǐ dì jí shì Fó shòu-yòng*" (the Buddha-son who dwells here is using what the Buddha used) doubt suddenly arose. Went to **Pán-shān** to **Liǎo-zōng** with his question; received the staff. With his doubt deepening he received final *fù-fǎ* and was made *shū-jì* (scribe).

**Abbacies (thirteen — exceptionally large circuit, all in the Hébei / Liáodōng / Manchurian frontier).** **Luán-zhōu Zé-tā Wàn-shàn chán-sì** 灤州澤它萬善禪寺 (the principal seat); **Gāo-shān Bái-dào-zǐ Shí-fó-sì** 高山白道子石佛寺; **Shèng-jīng Fèng-tiān-fǔ Huá-yán-sì** 盛京奉天府華嚴寺 (Mukden); **Jǐn-fǔ Níng-yuǎn-zhōu Dì-zàng-sì** 錦府寧遠州地藏寺; **Liáo-dōng Lá-ér-shān Cháo-yáng-sì** 遼東剌兒山朝陽寺; **Yǒng-píng-fǔ Lè-tíng Shèng-yán-sì** 永平府樂亭聖嚴寺; **Yù-tián-xiàn Yuán-tōng-yuàn** 玉田縣圓通院; **Tiān-jīn-wèi Fàng-shēng-yuàn** 天津衛放生院; **Lè-tíng-xiàn Shèng-yán-sì** 樂亭縣聖嚴寺; **Pán-shān Zhèng-fǎ chán-sì** 盤山正法禪寺; **Yǒng-píng-fǔ Dì-zàng-yuàn** 永平府地藏院; **Tiān-jīn-wèi Bō-rě-yuàn** 天津衛般若院; **Fèng-tiān-fǔ Yáng-jiā-sì** 奉天府楊家寺.

**Disciple.** **Tiān-xǐ Yìn** 天璽印 (sole *fǎ-sì* — *zhī-cáng* 知藏 named in the *fù-zhǔ* sequence in juan 6).

**Work.** [[KR6q0573]] 《灤州萬善暉州昊禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B460, recorded by his attendants **Xìng-zhēn** [[性珍]] and **Jì-bǎo** [[寂寶]] *děng lù*; preface dated **康熙三十八年歲次己卯閏月** = **1699** leap-month (Kāngxī 38).

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 106; 《灤州萬善暉洲昊禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈略狀〉.
