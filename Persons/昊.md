Hào 昊 (variant 旲), *zì* **Huīzhōu** 暉洲 (also written 暉州); often referred to as **Huīzhōu Hào** 暉洲昊 or **Wànshàn Huīzhōu** 萬善暉洲. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pánshān** 盤山. Lifedates unknown — active **1670s–1699+**. Native of **Luánzhōu Lètíng** 灤州樂亭 (modern **Hébei**, on the road to Manchuria); lay surname **Liú** 劉.

**Formation.** Vegetarian even in the womb; tranquil from birth. At 11 his mother died and his father sent him to **Hánjiā Dàoxiǎokōng** 韓家道曉空 to take tonsure. At 18 he entered *bìguān* and read the *Lotus Sūtra* devoutly; at the *Ānlèxíngpǐn* 安樂行品 verse "*Fózǐ zhù cǐ dì jí shì Fó shòuyòng*" (the Buddha-son who dwells here is using what the Buddha used) doubt suddenly arose. Went to **Pánshān** to **Liǎozōng** with his question; received the staff. With his doubt deepening he received final *fùfǎ* and was made *shūjì* (scribe).

**Abbacies (thirteen — exceptionally large circuit, all in the Hébei / Liáodōng / Manchurian frontier).** **Luánzhōu Zétā Wànshàn chánsì** 灤州澤它萬善禪寺 (the principal seat); **Gāoshān Báidàozǐ Shífósì** 高山白道子石佛寺; **Shèngjīng Fèngtiānfǔ Huáyánsì** 盛京奉天府華嚴寺 (Mukden); **Jǐnfǔ Níngyuǎnzhōu Dìzàngsì** 錦府寧遠州地藏寺; **Liáodōng Láérshān Cháoyángsì** 遼東剌兒山朝陽寺; **Yǒngpíngfǔ Lètíng Shèngyánsì** 永平府樂亭聖嚴寺; **Yùtiánxiàn Yuántōngyuàn** 玉田縣圓通院; **Tiānjīnwèi Fàngshēngyuàn** 天津衛放生院; **Lètíngxiàn Shèngyánsì** 樂亭縣聖嚴寺; **Pánshān Zhèngfǎ chánsì** 盤山正法禪寺; **Yǒngpíngfǔ Dìzàngyuàn** 永平府地藏院; **Tiānjīnwèi Bōrěyuàn** 天津衛般若院; **Fèngtiānfǔ Yángjiāsì** 奉天府楊家寺.

**Disciple.** **Tiānxǐ Yìn** 天璽印 (sole *fǎsì* — *zhīcáng* 知藏 named in the *fùzhǔ* sequence in juan 6).

**Work.** [[KR6q0573]] 《灤州萬善暉州昊禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B460, recorded by his attendants **Xìngzhēn** [[性珍]] and **Jìbǎo** [[寂寶]] *děng lù*; preface dated **康熙三十八年歲次己卯閏月** = **1699** leap-month (Kāngxī 38).

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 106; 《灤州萬善暉洲昊禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈略狀〉.
