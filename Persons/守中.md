# Shǒuzhōng 守中

Northern-Sòng Chán monk; reissued the *Fényáng Wúdé chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0054]]) in Jiànzhōng Jìngguó 1 (1101), the printing collated by [[圓璣]] Yuánjī of Lúshān Yuántōng Chóngshèng chányuàn — the recension that represents the stabilised Sòng text of Fényáng Shànzhāo's recorded sayings.
