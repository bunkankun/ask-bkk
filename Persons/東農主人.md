**Dōngnóng zhǔrén** 東農主人 ("the Master of the Eastern-Agriculture Studio") is the studio-name of an unidentified Qīng-period editor of the *Yuáncù yàolùn* 原瘄要論 ([[KR3ej050]]) — the consolidated paediatric *cùzhèng* manuscript of the Yínxiàn region Yuán, Shěn, and Yú family medical lineages. The preface is dated *chángzhì jié hòu sān rì* 長至節後三日 (three days after the summer solstice) without an absolute year. The personal name has not been identified.
