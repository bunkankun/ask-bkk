**Dōng-nóng zhǔ-rén** 東農主人 ("the Master of the Eastern-Agriculture Studio") is the studio-name of an unidentified Qīng-period editor of the *Yuán-cù yào-lùn* 原瘄要論 ([[KR3ej050]]) — the consolidated paediatric *cù-zhèng* manuscript of the Yín-xiàn region Yuán, Shěn, and Yú family medical lineages. The preface is dated *cháng-zhì jié hòu sān rì* 長至節後三日 (three days after the summer solstice) without an absolute year. The personal name has not been identified.
