Ennyo 圓如 (clerical name **Kōyū** 光融, 1491–1521), eldest son of **Jitsunyo** 實如 (the ninth Hongan-ji abbot) and grandson of [[蓮如]] **Rennyo**, was active in the Hongan-ji during the **Eishō / Daiei** period (*c.* 1504–1521). His principal literary contribution is the **compilation of the *Ofumi* anthology** [[KR6t0379]] — the standard five-fascicle edition of Rennyo's pastoral letters — assembled from the manuscripts preserved by his father Jitsunyo and the senior Hongan-ji clergy. He predeceased his father Jitsunyo and never himself held the abbacy; he is nevertheless one of the principal figures in the textual canonization of Rennyo. The DILA authority id is `A001240`.
