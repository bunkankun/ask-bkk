**Kē Qín** 柯琴 (*zì* Yùnbó 韻伯, *hào* Sìfēng 似峰; conventional lifedates ca. 1662–1735, 清), Kāng-xī / Yōng-zhèng period scholar-physician, native of Cíxī 慈溪 (Zhèjiāng), later resident in Yú-shān 虞山 (Cháng-shú, Jiāngsū). One of the most influential Qīng commentators on the *Shānghán lùn*, founder of the "channel-pattern" (以證類證 *yǐ zhèng lèi zhèng*) school of *Shānghán* exegesis, which re-organizes the *Shānghán* materials by clinical syndrome rather than by the Wáng Shū-hé manuscript sequence. Principal works:

- *Shānghán lái sū jí* 傷寒來蘇集 ([[KR3ef013]]) — his 4-juan general commentary on the *Shānghán lùn*.
- *Shānghán lùn zhù lái sū jí* 傷寒論註來蘇集 ([[KR3ef014]]) — companion commentary.
- *Shānghán lùn yì* 傷寒論翼 ([[KR3ef007]]) — 2-juan theoretical companion.
- *Shānghán fù yì* 傷寒附翼 ([[KR3ef033]]) — supplementary essays.
- *Shānghán fǎ zǔ* 傷寒法祖 ([[KR3ef042]]) — posthumous compilation from his Yú-shān manuscripts.

His commentary tradition was central to the late-Qīng *Shānghán* curriculum and remains influential in modern Chinese-medicine pedagogy. No CBDB record. The conventional lifedates 1662–1735 are widely cited but not securely documented; some authorities give 1660s as birth and ca. 1700s as death.
