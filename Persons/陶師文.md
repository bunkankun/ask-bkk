# 陶師文 Táo Shīwén

**Táo Shīwén** 陶師文 (of the Wèijì 未齊 workshop), mid-Míng printer who issued the 1537 edition of [[高武]] Gāo Wǔ's *Zhēnjiǔ Sù Nàn yàozhǐ* 針灸素難要旨 ([[KR3ee021]]).
