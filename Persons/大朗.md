# Dàlǎng 大朗

**Early monastic name of [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] 蕅益智旭** (1599–1655), used in some of his works. Full monastic form *Shì Dàlǎng* 釋大朗. See [[智旭]] for full biographical details.

Particularly relevant in the Kanripo corpus as preface-writer (under the name 大朗) for the *Pì xié jí* 闢邪集 [[KR6q0190]], for which the principal author is also Zhìxù under his lay name [[鍾始聲]] — the preface-writer and the author being the same person.
