# Zōng-mì 宗謐

Early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Cuì-fēng 翠峰. Principal dharma-heir of [[文琇]] Nán-shí Wén-xiù (1345–1418); heads the editorial byline of his master's four-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0355]]). Recorded in *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* and *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* among Wén-xiù's *fǎ-sì* 法嗣. Lifedates unrecorded; active at Sū-zhōu Cuì-fēng-sì 翠峰寺 (the Wú-jùn 吳郡 seat that gives him his *hào*).
