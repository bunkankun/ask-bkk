# Zōngmì 宗謐

Early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Cuìfēng 翠峰. Principal dharma-heir of [[文琇]] Nánshí Wénxiù (1345–1418); heads the editorial byline of his master's four-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0355]]). Recorded in *Zēngjí Xùchuándēnglù* and *Wǔdēng quánshū* among Wénxiù's *fǎsì* 法嗣. Lifedates unrecorded; active at Sūzhōu Cuìfēngsì 翠峰寺 (the Wújùn 吳郡 seat that gives him his *hào*).
