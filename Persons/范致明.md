Fàn Zhì-míng 范致明 (*fl. Yuán-fú* 1098–1100; *jìnshì* 1099, per CBDB 12975 with index year 1070), *zì* Huì-shū 晦叔, native of Jiàn-ān 建安 in Mǐn (modern Fú-jiàn). Northern-Sòng official; *jìnshì* under Zhé-zōng. Subsequently banished under Huī-zōng to Yuè-zhōu 岳州 (Yuè-yáng) where he served as *Xuān-dé láng* with concurrent supervision of commercial taxation; during that exile (early *Chóng-níng* / *Dà-guān*, ca. 1102–1110) he composed the *Yuèyáng fēng-tǔ jì* 岳陽風土記 ([[KR2k0110]]) — a critical *kǎo-zhèng*-style topographical monograph correcting inherited errors in the *Tài-píng huán-yǔ jì*, *Tōng-diǎn*, and *Shuǐ-jīng zhù*. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB has no birth/death years.
