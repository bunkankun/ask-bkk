Fàn Zhìmíng 范致明 (*fl. Yuánfú* 1098–1100; *jìnshì* 1099, per CBDB 12975 with index year 1070), *zì* Huìshū 晦叔, native of Jiànān 建安 in Mǐn (modern Fújiàn). Northern-Sòng official; *jìnshì* under Zhézōng. Subsequently banished under Huīzōng to Yuèzhōu 岳州 (Yuèyáng) where he served as *Xuāndé láng* with concurrent supervision of commercial taxation; during that exile (early *Chóngníng* / *Dàguān*, ca. 1102–1110) he composed the *Yuèyáng fēngtǔ jì* 岳陽風土記 ([[KR2k0110]]) — a critical *kǎozhèng*-style topographical monograph correcting inherited errors in the *Tàipíng huányǔ jì*, *Tōngdiǎn*, and *Shuǐjīng zhù*. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB has no birth/death years.
