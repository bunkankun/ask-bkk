Yuán-dynasty commentator, *hào* Liúxū sǎnrén 六虛散人, a native of Tōngchuān 潼川 (Sichuan). Active in 1291, when he composed a commentary on the *Lǎozǐ* ([[KR5a0723|DZ 723 *Dàodé zhēnjīng jíyì dàzhǐ*]] and [[KR5a0724|DZ 724 *Dàodé zhēnjīng jíyì*]]). Author of [[KR5a0121|Huángdì yīnfú jīng xīnfǎ 黃帝陰符經心法]] (DZ 121), a three-juan *Yīnfú jīng* commentary in Neo-Confucian-inflected mind-cultivation terms (*xiūdào jí xiūxīn* 修道即修心).
