**Mǎ Yù** 馬鈺 (1123–1184), *zì* Xuán bǎo 玄寶, *hào* Dān yáng zǐ 丹陽子 (hence **Mǎ Dān yáng** 馬丹陽), was the **first of the Seven Perfected** and Wáng Chóngyáng's senior disciple. A wealthy Shān dōng landowner who gave up his estate to follow Wáng. Senior patriarch of the **Yù xiān** 遇仙 branch of Quán zhēn. Dào zàng works: [[KR5d0080]] *Dān yáng zhēn rén yǔ lù*, [[KR5e0044]] *Jiàn wù jí*, [[KR5e0051]] *Dòng xuán jīn yù jí*, [[KR5e0052]] *Dān yáng shén guāng càn*.
