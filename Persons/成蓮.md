Late-Qīng (Guāngxù-era) Buddhist monk-editor. Lifedates unknown; the *floruit* given here (1880–1910) reflects his attested editorial activity. The DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority (A017925) records only that he composed a preface to the *Rénwángjīng shū huìběn* (X514 = [[KR6c0210]]) in Guāngxù 26 (1900).

His sole attested work is the preface and editorial assembly of the *Rénwáng jīng shū huìběn* — a *huìběn* (combined-edition) of Tiāntái Zhìyǐ-and-Guàndǐng's *Rénwángjīng* commentary (T1705 = [[KR6c0204]]) integrated with subsequent commentary materials, prepared for late-Qīng monastic study use. The 1900 date places the work at the very end of the Qīng dynasty, in the same period as the late-Qīng Buddhist revival anchored by Yáng Wénhuì 楊文會 and the Jīnlíng Scriptural-Press programme.

He represents the late-Qīng monastic editorial tradition that sought to make canonical Tiāntái commentaries accessible to contemporary practitioners through *huìběn* combined-edition formats.
