*Sī-nóng-sī* 司農司 ("Office of Agricultural Affairs") is the institutional author of the imperially-commissioned *Nóng-sāng jí yào* 農桑輯要 (KR3d0003), composed under Yuán *Zhì-yuán* 23 (1286) by the imperial agricultural department. The work was the official Yuán imperial agricultural manual, distributed for use by local officials. The catalog meta uses the institutional name as author, the standard convention for state-compiled works without named principal compilers.
