# 查應光

Zhā Yìngguāng (sobriquet Tàishēng jūshì 汰生居士)

Late-Míng layman who wrote the 1626 sponsoring preface to the *Jīngāng jīng bǔzhù* ([[KR6c0057]]) by [[韓巖]] Hán Yán and [[程衷懋]] Chéng Zhōngmào.
