Early-Míng Imperial Physician (*Tài-yī yuàn yù-yī* 太醫院御醫) of Sū-zhōu; documented at the *Yǒng-lè* / *Hóng-xī* / *Xuān-dé* court of *Míng Chéng-zǔ* and his successors. Early in his career he had retired to the *Dòng-tíng* 洞庭 lakeside region near Sū-zhōu for study; called to court and rose to become one of the principal early-Míng court physicians. Shèng was associated with the early-Míng court medical-academy curriculum but is not the actual author of the work [[KR3er105]] *Yī-jīng mì-zhǐ* 醫經秘旨, which was falsely attributed to him in the early-Qīng 18th c. as an editorial extraction from [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhī-lán's 1662 [[KR3er103]] *Yī-jīn yī fá*. The Shèng Yín attribution was definitively unmasked by Gāo Sī-qián's 1925 *Sān-sān yī-shū* philological investigation. CBDB c_personid 88775.
