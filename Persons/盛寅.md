Early-Míng Imperial Physician (*Tàiyī yuàn yùyī* 太醫院御醫) of Sūzhōu; documented at the *Yǒnglè* / *Hóngxī* / *Xuāndé* court of *Míng Chéngzǔ* and his successors. Early in his career he had retired to the *Dòngtíng* 洞庭 lakeside region near Sūzhōu for study; called to court and rose to become one of the principal early-Míng court physicians. Shèng was associated with the early-Míng court medical-academy curriculum but is not the actual author of the work [[KR3er105]] *Yījīng mìzhǐ* 醫經秘旨, which was falsely attributed to him in the early-Qīng 18th c. as an editorial extraction from [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhīlán's 1662 [[KR3er103]] *Yījīn yī fá*. The Shèng Yín attribution was definitively unmasked by Gāo Sīqián's 1925 *Sānsān yīshū* philological investigation. CBDB c_personid 88775.
