Mid-Yuán 元 Chán abbot and principal imperial-Buddhist literary figure under **Wén-zōng** and **Shùn-dì**. Religious name **Dà-xīn** 大訢; *zì* **Xiào-yǐn** 笑隱 ("Smiling Recluse"). Lay-surname **Chén** 陳氏. Native of **Nán-chāng** 南昌 (Jiāng-xī). CBDB 28415 records 1284–1344.

**Religious career.**
- Resident at **Fèng-shān** 鳳山 in Háng-zhōu
- Transferred to abbatical position at **Zhōng-tiān-zhú** 中天竺 monastery (one of the major Háng-zhōu Buddhist establishments)
- On Wén-zōng's accession (1328), Wén-zōng converted his pre-imperial princely residence at Jiàn-kāng (Nán-jīng) into the **Jí-qìng-sì** 集慶寺 and specially raised Dà-xīn to its abbatical position
- Conferred imperial honour **Tài-zhōng dà-fū** 太中大夫

**Literary network.** Substantial exchange-correspondence with:
- **Zhào Mèng-fǔ** [[趙孟頫]] — Zhào outsourced two commissioned-temple-stele compositions to Dà-xīn (the *Háng-zhōu-lù Jīn-gāng xiǎn-jiào yuàn jì* and the *Jīn-líng Tiān-xǐ jiǎng-sì Fó-guāng dà-shī Dé-gōng tǎ-míng*)
- **Kē Jiǔ-sī** 柯九思 (the great Yuán imperial-painter)
- **Sà Dū-lā** 薩都拉 [[KR4d0514]] (the Sa'dula poet)
- **Gāo Kè-gōng** 高克恭 (as Gāo Yàn-jìng 高彥敬)
- **Yú Jí** [[虞集]] — composed the principal preface to the *Pú-shì jí*
- **Mǎ Zhēn** [[馬臻]], **Zhāng Zhù** [[KR4d0530]], **Lǐ Xiào-guāng** [[KR4d0531]]

**Documentary contribution.** The *Wáng Kě-yì shàng-shū lì-rèn jì* preserves verifiable cross-check material for the *Yuán-shǐ Wén-zōng běn-jì*; specifically the Zhì-zhì-era exile of Wén-zōng to Hǎi-nán (the *jì* dates to Zhì-zhì 2 = 1322, the *běn-jì* dates to Zhì-zhì 1 = 1321; the Sìkù editors suggest *yī*/*èr* (one/two) miswriting).

**Sìkù-era name-corrections** preserved in this collection:
- *Yǎo-zhù* 咬住 → *Yuē-ěr-zhū* 約爾珠 (Yuán *Zhōng-zhèng-shǐ*)
- *Tuō-huān-chá-ér* 脱歡察児 → *Tuō-huān-chè-ěr* 妥歡徹爾
- *Sà Dū-cì* 薩都剌 → *Sà Dū-lā* 薩都拉

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0478]] *Pú-shì jí* 蒲室集 (撰).
