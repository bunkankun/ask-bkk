Mid-Yuán 元 Chán abbot and principal imperial-Buddhist literary figure under **Wénzōng** and **Shùndì**. Religious name **Dàxīn** 大訢; *zì* **Xiàoyǐn** 笑隱 ("Smiling Recluse"). Lay-surname **Chén** 陳氏. Native of **Nánchāng** 南昌 (Jiāngxī). CBDB 28415 records 1284–1344.

**Religious career.**
- Resident at **Fèngshān** 鳳山 in Hángzhōu
- Transferred to abbatical position at **Zhōngtiānzhú** 中天竺 monastery (one of the major Hángzhōu Buddhist establishments)
- On Wénzōng's accession (1328), Wénzōng converted his pre-imperial princely residence at Jiànkāng (Nánjīng) into the **Jíqìngsì** 集慶寺 and specially raised Dàxīn to its abbatical position
- Conferred imperial honour **Tàizhōng dàfū** 太中大夫

**Literary network.** Substantial exchange-correspondence with:
- **Zhào Mèngfǔ** [[趙孟頫]] — Zhào outsourced two commissioned-temple-stele compositions to Dàxīn (the *Hángzhōulù Jīngāng xiǎnjiào yuàn jì* and the *Jīnlíng Tiānxǐ jiǎngsì Fóguāng dàshī Dégōng tǎmíng*)
- **Kē Jiǔsī** 柯九思 (the great Yuán imperial-painter)
- **Sà Dūlā** 薩都拉 [[KR4d0514]] (the Sa'dula poet)
- **Gāo Kègōng** 高克恭 (as Gāo Yànjìng 高彥敬)
- **Yú Jí** [[虞集]] — composed the principal preface to the *Púshì jí*
- **Mǎ Zhēn** [[馬臻]], **Zhāng Zhù** [[KR4d0530]], **Lǐ Xiàoguāng** [[KR4d0531]]

**Documentary contribution.** The *Wáng Kěyì shàngshū lìrèn jì* preserves verifiable cross-check material for the *Yuánshǐ Wénzōng běnjì*; specifically the Zhì-zhì-era exile of Wénzōng to Hǎinán (the *jì* dates to Zhìzhì 2 = 1322, the *běnjì* dates to Zhìzhì 1 = 1321; the Sìkù editors suggest *yī*/*èr* (one/two) miswriting).

**Sìkù-era name-corrections** preserved in this collection:
- *Yǎozhù* 咬住 → *Yuēěrzhū* 約爾珠 (Yuán *Zhōngzhèngshǐ*)
- *Tuōhuāncháér* 脱歡察児 → *Tuōhuānchèěr* 妥歡徹爾
- *Sà Dūcì* 薩都剌 → *Sà Dūlā* 薩都拉

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0478]] *Púshì jí* 蒲室集 (撰).
