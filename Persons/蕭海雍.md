# 蕭海雍

Xiāo Hǎiyōng, late-Qīng physician; author of a parallel *Báihóu zhènglùn* 白喉證論 cited in the northern preface to [[KR3em031]] *Báihóu jiéyào hébiān* 白喉捷要合編 but not preserved in the combined edition itself.
