Dàozhēn 道真 (active Tang or earlier), Buddhist monk-translator. Per the Fángshān stone-canon attribution, he produced *[[KR6g0042|Fó shuō jiù hù shēn mìng jīng 佛說救護身命經]]* (F248). Birth and death dates not preserved.

(Note: this *Dàozhēn* may or may not be the same as the famous Dūnhuáng monk Dàozhēn 道真 active there in the 10th century; the identification is not certain.)
