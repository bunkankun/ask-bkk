**Xiàng Yù** 項煜 (*fl.* late Wàn-lì 萬曆 – Chóng-zhēn 崇禎, *jìnshì* 進士 of the late Míng) — late-Míng official; held the Bái-xuě-lóu 白雪樓 residence at *Shuò-yuán* 碩園, the venue for considerable late-Míng literary patronage. Author of the *Chóng-zhēn* 15 / 1642 publication-preface to [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōng-zǐ's *Shān-bǔ yí-shēng wēi-lùn* ([[KR3er079]]). CBDB id 344896.
