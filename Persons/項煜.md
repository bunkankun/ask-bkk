**Xiàng Yù** 項煜 (*fl.* late Wànlì 萬曆 – Chóngzhēn 崇禎, *jìnshì* 進士 of the late Míng) — late-Míng official; held the Báixuělóu 白雪樓 residence at *Shuòyuán* 碩園, the venue for considerable late-Míng literary patronage. Author of the *Chóngzhēn* 15 / 1642 publication-preface to [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's *Shānbǔ yíshēng wēilùn* ([[KR3er079]]). CBDB id 344896.
