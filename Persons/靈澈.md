# Língchè 靈澈

Táng Vinaya master of *Huìjī Yúnménsì* 會稽雲門寺. Author of the lost twenty-one-juan *Xíngshì chāo yǐnyuán jì* 行事鈔引源記, the second of the *liùshí jiā* sub-commentaries on [[道宣]] Dàoxuān's *Xíngshì chāo* per [[慧顯]] Huìxiǎn's catalogue ([[KR6k0170]]). Likely identical with the late-eighth-century Tang poet-monk Língchè (746–816) of *Yúnménsì*, though the identification is not certain.
