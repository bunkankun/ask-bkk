**Zhù Dēngyuán** 祝登元, early-to-mid Qīng physician of the Shùnzhì / Kāngxī era (*fl.* 1640s–1700s by internal evidence from the integration of [[喻昌]] / [[李中梓]] Wú-region clinical-doctrinal positions), author of **[[KR3eq032]]** *Xīnyī jí* 心醫集 (2 *juǎn*). The work integrates classical Confucian *xīnxué* 心學 (mind-learning) categories into clinical reasoning. Not in CBDB; no further independent documentation located.
