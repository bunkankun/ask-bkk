# Lǐ Chū 李樗

*Zì* Ruòlín 若林. Native of Mǐnxiàn 閩縣 (modern Fúzhōu 福州). Twelfth-century *Shī* scholar; lifedates not recoverable. The *Sìkù* editors note he was a *xiānggòng* 鄉貢 ("provincial graduate") nominee but no national-level *jìnshì* attested. He was the maternal cousin (*wàixiōng* 外兄) of Lín Zhīqí 林之奇 (per *Shū lù jiětí*) and a pupil of Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 (per Hé Qiáoyuǎn 何喬遠's *Mǐnshū* 閩書).

Author of *Máoshī xiángjiě* 毛詩詳解 in 36 *juǎn*, surviving as the principal layer of the joint compilation [[KR1c0012]] *Máoshī LǐHuáng jíjiě* 毛詩李黃集解 (with [[黃櫄]]). Methodologically he follows Sū Zhé ([[KR1c0010]]) on the *small preface*: only the opening sentence of each *xù* descends from a pre-Hàn teacher (he ascribes it to Máo Gōng), the rest being Wèi Hóng's later supplement. His learning is a synthesis of the Lǚ Běnzhōng line with mid-Northern-Sòng *Shī* scholarship.
