# Míngyuán 明源

Early-Kāngxī Buddhist monk of the Bǎolún 寶輪 monastic establishment. Lifedates not preserved; floruit Kāngxī era.

Author of the **Tiānxī Zhǔntí sānmèi xíngfǎ xù** 天溪准提三昧行法序, preface to [[受登]] Tiānxī Shòudēng's *Zhǔntí sānmèi xíngfǎ* 准提三昧行法 ([[KR6j0759]], X74 no. 1481), dated **Kāngxī *yǐsì* 乙巳, mid-spring = early 1665**. He signs as *Bǎolún shāmén Míngyuán* 寶輪沙門明源.
