# 朱塾

Zhū Shú 朱塾 (1153–1191), eldest son of [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī. He predeceased his father by nine years; his eldest son [[朱鑑]] Zhū Jiàn (1190–1258) was Zhū Xī's direct-line eldest grandson and the compiler of the *Wéngōng Yì shuō* 文公易說 ([[KR1a0056]]).
