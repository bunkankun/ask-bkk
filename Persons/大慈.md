# Dàcí 大慈

Táng Vinaya exegete of the post-[[道宣]] Dàoxuān generation, cited by [[志鴻]] Zhìhóng in [[KR6k0162]] *Sìfēnlǜ sōuxuán lù* among the more than forty earlier *jì* and *chāo* writers whose work the *Sōuxuán lù* digests. Independent biographical data on Dàcí is not preserved; his Vinaya writings are lost and survive only as citations in Táng and Sòng sub-commentaries.
