# Máo Xǐ 毛喜

Chén-dynasty official (516–587), holding the office of *Shàngshūlìng* 尚書令 (Secretariat Director). Named in the editorial framing to the *Xiǎo zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0143]]) as the patron whose request prompted [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ to produce the *Bùdìng zhǐguān* 不定止觀 / *Liùmiào fǎmén* ([[KR6d0145]], T1917) in one *juan*.
