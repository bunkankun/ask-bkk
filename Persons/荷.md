Hé 荷 (full reference form **Lǚyán Hé** 侶巖荷, also **Lóngquán Lǚyán Hé** 龍泉侶巖荷). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānàn Běnshēng** 天岸本昇 (1620–1673) — in the **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 → ... line. Lifedates unknown — active **1675–1685**.

**Abbacies (three).** **Húzhōu Wūshān Lóngquánān** 湖州烏山龍泉菴 (the seat giving him his sobriquet **Lóngquán** 龍泉); **Tiáochéng Shīzǐlín** 苕城師子林; **Ānjízhōu Lóngshān Dōngchánsì** 安吉州龍山東禪寺.

**Work.** [[KR6q0566]] 《侶巖荷禪師語錄》 7 juan, J39 B452, recorded by his attendant **Chéngchún** [[成純]] *děng jìlù*. Two prefaces: by **Tiāntóngshān Xiǎoxī** 天童山曉皙 (= 山曉本晳, dharma-cousin) dated **康熙乙丑菊月** = **1685** lunar 9; by lay disciple **Chén Liàng** 陳量 dated **康熙二十四年佛誕日** = **1685-05-29**.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 94; 《侶巖荷禪師語錄》 vol. 1, 2.
