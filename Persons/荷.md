Hé 荷 (full reference form **Lǚ-yán Hé** 侶巖荷, also **Lóng-quán Lǚ-yán Hé** 龍泉侶巖荷). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-àn Běn-shēng** 天岸本昇 (1620–1673) — in the **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 → ... line. Lifedates unknown — active **1675–1685**.

**Abbacies (three).** **Hú-zhōu Wū-shān Lóng-quán-ān** 湖州烏山龍泉菴 (the seat giving him his sobriquet **Lóng-quán** 龍泉); **Tiáo-chéng Shī-zǐ-lín** 苕城師子林; **Ān-jí-zhōu Lóng-shān Dōng-chán-sì** 安吉州龍山東禪寺.

**Work.** [[KR6q0566]] 《侶巖荷禪師語錄》 7 juan, J39 B452, recorded by his attendant **Chéng-chún** [[成純]] *děng jì-lù*. Two prefaces: by **Tiān-tóng-shān Xiǎo-xī** 天童山曉皙 (= 山曉本晳, dharma-cousin) dated **康熙乙丑菊月** = **1685** lunar 9; by lay disciple **Chén Liàng** 陳量 dated **康熙二十四年佛誕日** = **1685-05-29**.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 94; 《侶巖荷禪師語錄》 vol. 1, 2.
