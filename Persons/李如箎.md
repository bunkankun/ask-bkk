Lǐ Rúchí 李如箎 (*fl.* 1132; CBDB id 33314), *hào* Dōngyuán xiānshēng 東園先生, was a Southern Sòng *bǐjì* writer of Kuòcāng 括蒼 (modern Lìshuǐ 麗水 in Zhèjiāng). His career is incompletely recorded. By his own preface (dated *Shàoxīng rénzǐ* = 1132 — strictly *Shàoxīng* 2; the *Sìkù* editors error toward *Shàoxīng* 1 = 1131) he was then serving as *Tóngxiāng chéng* 桐鄉丞 (subprefect of Tóngxiāng); a *bá* of *Shàoxīng jiǎyín* (1134) printed in Jiànān by Zhōu Tíngyún identifies him as *Dōngyuán xiānshēng*. The *Sìkù* editors flag a number of internal-evidence anomalies that make them suspect post-Sòng tampering of the recension (a reference to Gāozōng's posthumous temple-name; reference to events of *Shàoxīng* 24 = 1154 and *Shàoxīng* 31 = 1161 — both well after the original preface; and apparent reliance on Zhū Xī's *Jí zhù*). Yet the substance of the book is sufficiently valuable to retain: classical exegesis on the *Yì*, *Shī*, *Chūnqiū*, *Lǐ jì*, and *Lùn yǔ*; *kǎozhèng* on Sòng astronomy and *lì shù* (calendar studies); and assorted *bǐjì* notes. His one surviving work is the *Dōng yuán cóng shuō* 東園叢說 ([[KR3j0115]]) in 3 *juàn*.
