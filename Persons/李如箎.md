Lǐ Rú-chí 李如箎 (*fl.* 1132; CBDB id 33314), *hào* Dōng-yuán xiān-shēng 東園先生, was a Southern Sòng *bǐjì* writer of Kuò-cāng 括蒼 (modern Lì-shuǐ 麗水 in Zhè-jiāng). His career is incompletely recorded. By his own preface (dated *Shào-xīng rén-zǐ* = 1132 — strictly *Shào-xīng* 2; the *Sì-kù* editors error toward *Shào-xīng* 1 = 1131) he was then serving as *Tóng-xiāng chéng* 桐鄉丞 (subprefect of Tóng-xiāng); a *bá* of *Shào-xīng jiǎ-yín* (1134) printed in Jiàn-ān by Zhōu Tíng-yún identifies him as *Dōng-yuán xiān-shēng*. The *Sì-kù* editors flag a number of internal-evidence anomalies that make them suspect post-Sòng tampering of the recension (a reference to Gāo-zōng's posthumous temple-name; reference to events of *Shào-xīng* 24 = 1154 and *Shào-xīng* 31 = 1161 — both well after the original preface; and apparent reliance on Zhū Xī's *Jí zhù*). Yet the substance of the book is sufficiently valuable to retain: classical exegesis on the *Yì*, *Shī*, *Chūn-qiū*, *Lǐ jì*, and *Lùn yǔ*; *kǎo-zhèng* on Sòng astronomy and *lì shù* (calendar studies); and assorted *bǐjì* notes. His one surviving work is the *Dōng yuán cóng shuō* 東園叢說 ([[KR3j0115]]) in 3 *juàn*.
