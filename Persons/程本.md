**Chéng Běn** 程本, *zì* Zǐ huá zǐ 子華子, was a Spring-and-Autumn figure mentioned in the *Zhuāng zǐ* as a contemporary of Kǒng zǐ. Nominal author of the ***Zǐ huá zǐ*** 子華子 ([[KR5f0008]]), but the received text is a late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng fabrication (late 11th or early 12th century).
