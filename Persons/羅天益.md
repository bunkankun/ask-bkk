**Luó Tiān-yì** 羅天益 (*zì* Qiān-fǔ 謙甫, *c.* 1220 – *c.* 1290) — physician of Zhēn-dìng 真定 (modern Zhèngdìng 正定, Héběi), the principal direct disciple of [[李杲]] Lǐ Gǎo (Dōng-yuán 東垣, 1180–1251) and the editorial channel through which the Dōng-yuán *Pí-wèi* 脾胃 school was transmitted into the Yuán dynasty. Luó accompanied Lǐ Gǎo at the household of the Mongol prince Bóyán 伯顏 and on the latter's military campaigns; the consultation case-records embedded in his *Wèi-shēng bǎo-jiàn* 衛生寶鑑 ([[KR3er052]], 1281) document patients of high Mongol-court rank. Lǐ Gǎo entrusted Luó with his unprinted writings on his deathbed; Luó subsequently saw the *Nèi-wài shāng biàn-huò lùn* 內外傷辨惑論 ([[KR3e0033]]), *Pí-wèi lùn* 脾胃論 ([[KR3e0034]]) and *Lán-shì mì-cáng* 蘭室秘藏 ([[KR3er062]]) into print. Luó also wrote the *Nèi-jīng lèi-biān* 內經類編 (lost) and contributed a preface to Lǐ Gǎo's *Cǐ-shì nán-zhī* 此事難知 ([[KR3e0032]]) in 1276 (CBDB activity record). CBDB id 10033.
