**Luó Tiānyì** 羅天益 (*zì* Qiānfǔ 謙甫, *c.* 1220 – *c.* 1290) — physician of Zhēndìng 真定 (modern Zhèngdìng 正定, Héběi), the principal direct disciple of [[李杲]] Lǐ Gǎo (Dōngyuán 東垣, 1180–1251) and the editorial channel through which the Dōngyuán *Píwèi* 脾胃 school was transmitted into the Yuán dynasty. Luó accompanied Lǐ Gǎo at the household of the Mongol prince Bóyán 伯顏 and on the latter's military campaigns; the consultation case-records embedded in his *Wèishēng bǎojiàn* 衛生寶鑑 ([[KR3er052]], 1281) document patients of high Mongol-court rank. Lǐ Gǎo entrusted Luó with his unprinted writings on his deathbed; Luó subsequently saw the *Nèiwài shāng biànhuò lùn* 內外傷辨惑論 ([[KR3e0033]]), *Píwèi lùn* 脾胃論 ([[KR3e0034]]) and *Lánshì mìcáng* 蘭室秘藏 ([[KR3er062]]) into print. Luó also wrote the *Nèijīng lèibiān* 內經類編 (lost) and contributed a preface to Lǐ Gǎo's *Cǐshì nánzhī* 此事難知 ([[KR3e0032]]) in 1276 (CBDB activity record). CBDB id 10033.
