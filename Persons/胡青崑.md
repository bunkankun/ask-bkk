**Hú Qīngkūn** 胡青崑 (also written **Hú Qīngkūn** 胡青昆; *hào* Làngfēng 閬風, *fl.* mid-Xián-fēng era, mid-19th c., 清), Qīng-period traumatologist and *shūwū* schoolmaster of **Nányì** 南邑 / **Shàngyì** 上邑 (precise identification of the place unclear; the colophon places him teaching at the **Yǎnglíng shūwū** 養翎書屋 in **Guànchéng** 灌城). He is known from his preface and editorial work on [[KR3el016]] *Diēdǎ sǔnshāng huíshēng jí* 跌打損傷回生集, published in *Xiánfēng* 6 (1856). The manuscript he edited had been transmitted to him by his father **Wèiqīnggōng** 位卿公, who had received it during the *Jiāqìng* era from a great-uncle **Qǐwàngōng** 啟萬公 — Qǐwàngōng having compiled it "across several decades of investigation" earlier in the Qīng. Hú Qīngkūn published the work with the financial backing of his literary friends after years of insufficient funds had prevented earlier printing. His exact lifedates are not preserved.
