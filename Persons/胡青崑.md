**Hú Qīng-kūn** 胡青崑 (also written **Hú Qīng-kūn** 胡青昆; *hào* Làng-fēng 閬風, *fl.* mid-Xián-fēng era, mid-19th c., 清), Qīng-period traumatologist and *shū-wū* schoolmaster of **Nán-yì** 南邑 / **Shàng-yì** 上邑 (precise identification of the place unclear; the colophon places him teaching at the **Yǎng-líng shū-wū** 養翎書屋 in **Guàn-chéng** 灌城). He is known from his preface and editorial work on [[KR3el016]] *Diē-dǎ sǔn-shāng huí-shēng jí* 跌打損傷回生集, published in *Xián-fēng* 6 (1856). The manuscript he edited had been transmitted to him by his father **Wèi-qīng-gōng** 位卿公, who had received it during the *Jiā-qìng* era from a great-uncle **Qǐ-wàn-gōng** 啟萬公 — Qǐ-wàn-gōng having compiled it "across several decades of investigation" earlier in the Qīng. Hú Qīng-kūn published the work with the financial backing of his literary friends after years of insufficient funds had prevented earlier printing. His exact lifedates are not preserved.
