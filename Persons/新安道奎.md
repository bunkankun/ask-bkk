# 新安道奎

Xīn'ān Dàokuí; Sòng- or Yuán-period Buddhist editor of Xīn'ān 新安 (modern Huīzhōu 徽州 area), author of the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn pǐn bié xíng shū chāo chóng kān xù* 普賢行願品別行疏鈔重刊序 ("Preface to the Reprinting of the *Bié xíng Shū Chāo* on the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn pǐn*") preserved at the head of [[KR6e0071|*Huáyán jīng xíng yuàn pǐn shū chāo* 華嚴經行願品疏鈔]] (X229). Lifedates not preserved.
