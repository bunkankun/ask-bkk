# Qìng-yù 慶預

Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, imperial title Huì-zhào chánshī 慧照禪師 (also 慧炤). Dàjiàn-下第 14 世 dharma-heir of [[子淳]] Dān-xiá Zǐ-chún (1066–1119). 1078 – Shào-xīng 10 / 1140, *shì-shòu* 63. Native of Jīng-shān 京山 (Yǐng-zhōu, in Jīng-hú-běi), lay surname Hú 胡.

Tonsured at twenty-four under [[道楷]] Fú-róng Dào-kǎi; took the full precepts; subsequently went to Zǐ-chún and received dharma-transmission. Abbacy sequence: Suí-zhōu Dà-hóng-shān Líng-fēng-sì 大洪山靈峰寺 (succeeding his master at the Dà-hóng seat after Zǐ-chún's death in 1119); Shuǐ-nán Xīng-guó-sì 水南興國寺; Xuě-fēng Chóng-shòu-sì 雪峰崇壽寺 (the "Xuě-fēng Huì-zhào" 雪峰慧照 style derives from this seat). Held steady through the dislocations of the Jìng-kāng 靖康 catastrophe (1126–1127) — "during the Jìng-kāng upheaval the master focused specially on *chán-sòng* 禪誦 in order to settle the minds of the community" (DILA).

Primary editorial achievement: *jiào-kān* 校勘 of Zǐ-chún's two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0358]]; X71 n1425), named at the text's head as "*sì-fǎ xiǎo-shī* 嗣法小師." Named dharma-heirs: Huì-lì Wù 惠力悟, Xuě-fēng Huì-shēn 雪峯慧深, Zhì-mén Yǎ 智門雅, Pǔ-zhào Chōng 普照充, Jiàn-fú Yǎn 薦福演, and one other.
