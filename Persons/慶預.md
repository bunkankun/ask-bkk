# Qìngyù 慶預

Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, imperial title Huìzhào chánshī 慧照禪師 (also 慧炤). Dàjiàn-下第 14 世 dharma-heir of [[子淳]] Dānxiá Zǐchún (1066–1119). 1078 – Shàoxīng 10 / 1140, *shìshòu* 63. Native of Jīngshān 京山 (Yǐngzhōu, in Jīnghúběi), lay surname Hú 胡.

Tonsured at twenty-four under [[道楷]] Fúróng Dàokǎi; took the full precepts; subsequently went to Zǐchún and received dharma-transmission. Abbacy sequence: Suízhōu Dàhóngshān Língfēngsì 大洪山靈峰寺 (succeeding his master at the Dàhóng seat after Zǐchún's death in 1119); Shuǐnán Xīngguósì 水南興國寺; Xuěfēng Chóngshòusì 雪峰崇壽寺 (the "Xuěfēng Huìzhào" 雪峰慧照 style derives from this seat). Held steady through the dislocations of the Jìngkāng 靖康 catastrophe (1126–1127) — "during the Jìngkāng upheaval the master focused specially on *chánsòng* 禪誦 in order to settle the minds of the community" (DILA).

Primary editorial achievement: *jiàokān* 校勘 of Zǐchún's two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0358]]; X71 n1425), named at the text's head as "*sìfǎ xiǎoshī* 嗣法小師." Named dharma-heirs: Huìlì Wù 惠力悟, Xuěfēng Huìshēn 雪峯慧深, Zhìmén Yǎ 智門雅, Pǔzhào Chōng 普照充, Jiànfú Yǎn 薦福演, and one other.
