# Kǒng Yǐngdá 孔穎達

Style names *Zhòngdá* 仲達 and *Chōngyuǎn* 沖遠. Native of Héngshuǐ 衡水 in Jì zhōu 冀州 (modern southern Héběi). Thirty-second-generation lineal descendant of Confucius. Lifedates 574–648 (Suí and early Táng).

Took the *xiùcái* (Suí) and rose through Suí offices (*Guójiāng kāo gōng láng* 國子監考功郎); under the early Táng held a series of court appointments culminating in Director of the Imperial Academy (*Guózǐ jìjiǔ* 國子祭酒, the highest scholarly office in the empire). *Jiù Tángshū* juan 73 and *Xīn Tángshū* juan 198 (*Rúlín*) give substantial biographies; the *Xīn Tángshū* notice in particular emphasizes his role in fixing the imperial-examination orthodoxy.

The defining act of his career is his leadership of the imperial commission that produced the *Wǔ jīng zhèngyì* 五經正義 ("Correct Meanings of the Five Classics") under Tàizōng's edict — official subcommentaries on the *Yì*, *Shū*, *Shī*, *Lǐjì*, and *Zuǒ zhuàn*, first compiled 642 (Zhēnguān 16), revised after Yán Shīgǔ's 顏師古 critiques, and promulgated 653 (Yǒnghuī 4) under Gāozōng. The set determined imperial-examination orthodoxy on the Five Classics for the rest of the Táng and was inherited as the canonical sub-commentary in the Sòng *Shísān jīng zhùshū* tradition.

For the *Yì*, his commission selected Wáng Bì–Hán Kāngbó's *Zhōuyì zhù* ([[KR1a0006]]) as the canonical-note layer (against Zhèng Xuán's [[KR1a0003]] / [[KR1a0004]] line) and produced the sub-commentary that systematically expounds it (= [[KR1a0007]] *Zhōuyì zhèngyì* / *Zhōuyì zhùshū*). For the other classics: *Shàngshū zhèngyì*, *Máoshī zhèngyì*, *Lǐjì zhèngyì*, and *Chūnqiū Zuǒ zhuàn zhèngyì*; all four likewise become the canonical TángSòng sub-commentaries.

Kǒng's editorial decisions — to canonize Wáng Bì for the *Yì*, the older "Confucius–Kǒng Ānguó" pseudo-commentary for the *Shū*, Mǎo Hēng + Zhèng Xuán + the *Máoshī xù* for the *Shī*, Zhèng Xuán for the *Lǐjì*, and Dù Yù for the *Zuǒ zhuàn* — fixed the medieval canon's exegetical complexion for nearly a millennium. The Sìkù *tiyao* on [[KR1a0007]] gives a measured but critical assessment of his *Yì* sub-commentary's partisan character.
