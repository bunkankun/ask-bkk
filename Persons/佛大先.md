Fódàxiān 佛大先 (\*Buddhasena, "Army-of-the-Buddha"), Indian Sarvāstivāda *yogācāra* master of Kashmir, active around the turn of the fifth century. He is identified in the preface to [[KR6i0255|*Dámóduōluó chán jīng* (T618)]] — composed by [[慧遠]] and preserved in [[KR6s0084|*Chū sānzàng jì jí*]] — as one of the two principal Kashmiri masters (with [[達摩多羅|Dharmatrāta]]) whose meditation tradition was transmitted by [[佛陀跋陀羅]] (Buddhabhadra) to Lúshān.
