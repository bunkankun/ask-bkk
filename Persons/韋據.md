# Wéi Jù 韋據

Early-Táng prefect (*cìshǐ* 刺史) of Sháozhōu 韶州, on whose invitation [[惠能]] Huìnéng delivered the dharma-sermon at the Dàfàn sì 大梵寺 recorded in the *Tánjīng* (Platform Sūtra).
