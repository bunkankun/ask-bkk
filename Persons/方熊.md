Fāng Xióng 方熊, early-Qing scholar; lifedates and native place not securely recorded. Compiler of the *bǔzhù* 補注 ("supplementary commentary") to Rèn Fǎng's [[任昉]] *Wénzhāng yuánqǐ* [[KR4i0004]], built on top of the earlier Míng *zhù* of Chén Màorén [[陳懋仁]]; in the *Sìkù* edition each entry headed *zhù* is Chén's and each entry headed *bǔzhù* is Fāng's. The CBDB *Fāng Xióng* at id 72485 (1783–1860) is a different person — a late-Qīng Tóng-chéng-school figure whose dates make him impossible for the supplementary commentary preserved in the early-eighteenth-century *Sìkù* manuscript witness; the *Sìkù tíyào* tags him *guócháo* 國朝 ("of our dynasty") which in late-Qiánlóng usage means anyone of the Qing prior to the 1770s. The Fāng Xióng of the *Wénzhāng yuánqǐ* supplement is therefore plausibly to be placed in the late Shùnzhì / early Kāngxī generation, but no firm anchor is available. *No CBDB id is confidently assigned.*
