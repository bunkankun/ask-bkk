# Zǔqián 祖灊

Late-Yuán Chán *shīzhí bǐqiū* 師姪比丘 (nephew-disciple) of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[海壽]]) of *juan* 9 (*tíbá* 題䟦 + biographical apparatus) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Per Sòng Lián's *Xíngdàojì*, when Qīngyù's Língyán residence was destroyed in the mid-Zhìzhèng wars and the master had nowhere to stay, Zǔqián built for him the Cíyún stupa-hermitage 慈雲塔院 three *lǐ* north of Zuìlǐ 欈李 pavilion in Jiāxīng; upon Qīngyù's death in 1367 Zǔqián oversaw the sealing of the master's relic-box. He was close also to the neighbouring Chǔshí Fànqí 楚石梵琦 circle (as editor of Fànqí's *yǔlù*, X71 no. 1420) and to the Wénkāng line. Lifedates unrecorded.
