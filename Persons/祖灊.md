# Zǔ-qián 祖灊

Late-Yuán Chán *shī-zhí bǐ-qiū* 師姪比丘 (nephew-disciple) of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[海壽]]) of *juan* 9 (*tí-bá* 題䟦 + biographical apparatus) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Per Sòng Lián's *Xíng-dào-jì*, when Qīng-yù's Líng-yán residence was destroyed in the mid-Zhìzhèng wars and the master had nowhere to stay, Zǔ-qián built for him the Cí-yún stupa-hermitage 慈雲塔院 three *lǐ* north of Zuì-lǐ 欈李 pavilion in Jiā-xīng; upon Qīng-yù's death in 1367 Zǔ-qián oversaw the sealing of the master's relic-box. He was close also to the neighbouring Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí 楚石梵琦 circle (as editor of Fàn-qí's *yǔlù*, X71 no. 1420) and to the Wén-kāng line. Lifedates unrecorded.
