Mid-Yuán official of Dōnglǔ 東魯 (eastern Shāndōng); *Tōngyì dàfū Yānnán Héběi dào tíxíng àncháshǐ* 通議大夫燕南河北道提刑按察使 (Pacification Commissioner for Yānnán and Héběi). He wrote the principal preface to [[李杲]] Lǐ Dōngyuán's [[KR3er120]] *Dōngyuán shìxiào fāng* in *Zhìyuán* 17 / 1280, for the Yuán court reprint of the 1266 Luó Tiānyì *editio princeps*. The preface gives one of the most-cited summary statements of Lǐ Dōngyuán's clinical method (*míngjīng* / *biémài* / *shízhèng* / *chǔfāng*).
