Mid-Yuán official of Dōng-lǔ 東魯 (eastern Shān-dōng); *Tōng-yì dà-fū Yān-nán Hé-běi dào tí-xíng àn-chá-shǐ* 通議大夫燕南河北道提刑按察使 (Pacification Commissioner for Yān-nán and Hé-běi). He wrote the principal preface to [[李杲]] Lǐ Dōng-yuán's [[KR3er120]] *Dōng-yuán shì-xiào fāng* in *Zhì-yuán* 17 / 1280, for the Yuán court reprint of the 1266 Luó Tiān-yì *editio princeps*. The preface gives one of the most-cited summary statements of Lǐ Dōng-yuán's clinical method (*míng-jīng* / *bié-mài* / *shí-zhèng* / *chǔ-fāng*).
