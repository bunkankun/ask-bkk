# Míng-kāng 明康

Qīng Chán monk; dharma-nephew (*fǎ zhí* 法姪) of the lay Buddhist [[朱道則|Zhū Dào-zé]] 朱道則. Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of Zhū Dào-zé's *Tiān-rán jūshì huái jìng tǔ shī* 天然居士懷淨土詩 [[KR6q0239]].
