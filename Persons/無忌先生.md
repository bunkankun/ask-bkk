# Wújì xiānshēng 無忌先生

Pen-name ("Master No-Taboo") of an otherwise unidentified Míng-period Confucian scholar; sole nominal author of the paediatric handbook *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* 保幼新編 ([[KR3ej005]]), the original Chinese text of which is lost and which survives only in the late-Joseon Korean recension transmitted by the monk [[正訓]] Zhèngxùn and prefaced in 1785 by [[盧光履]] Lú Guānglǚ. Real name and lifedates unrecorded.
