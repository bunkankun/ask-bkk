Zhèng-chuán 正傳, *zì* **Huàn-yǒu** 幻有 ("Illusory Existence"); often referred to as **Lóng-chí Huàn-yǒu** 龍池幻有 or **Yǔ-mén Huàn-yǒu** 禹門幻有 from his abbacies; alternative *hào* **Yuán-tōng** 圓通, **Yī-xīn** 一心. **The pivotal Línjì-Yángqí lineage-restorer of the late Míng** — the master from whom the entire Mì-yún → Lín-yě / Tiān-yǐn / Wàn-rú late-Míng-early-Qīng establishment descends. Born **1549** (estimated); died **明萬曆甲寅二月十二日** = **1614-03-21**, age 66. Native of **Lì-yáng** 溧陽 (Jiāng-sū).

**Lineage centrality.** *Fǎ-shī* of the entire Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Línjì-Yángqí post-1614 establishment: his three principal *fǎ-sì* (the **三大** *sān-dà*) were **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642), **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū** 天隱圓修 (1575–1635), and **Xuě-jiāo Yuán-xìn** 雪嶖圓信 (1571–1647) — three of the most influential Línjì lineage-builders of the Míng-Qīng transition.

**Abbacies.** **Lì-yáng Lóng-chí Yǔ-mén-shān** 溧陽龍池禹門山 (the principal seat — gives him both his **Lóng-chí** and **Yǔ-mén** sobriquets).

**Work.** [[KR6q0601]] 《幻有傳禪師語錄》 10 juan, J40 (also Qiánlóng Edition Vol. 153 No. 1637, the catalog citation), compiled jointly by his three principal *fǎ-sì* — **Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] (Mì-yún) + **Yuán-xiū** [[圓修]] (Tiān-yǐn) — *děng biān*. The compilation by Mì-yún Yuán-wù makes this *yǔ-lù* the foundational text of the entire late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí revival. The recorded materials include *Nán-lù* (southern records) and *Běi-lù* (northern records) plus his late-life *Xián-tán* 閒談 ("idle conversations") and *Wǎn-huà* 晚話 ("evening talks") and his deathbed *mò-hòu jì bìng yǔ* 末後偈并語.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 64; multiple modern lineage-studies, esp. Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (2008).
