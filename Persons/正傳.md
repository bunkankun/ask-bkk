Zhèngchuán 正傳, *zì* **Huànyǒu** 幻有 ("Illusory Existence"); often referred to as **Lóngchí Huànyǒu** 龍池幻有 or **Yǔmén Huànyǒu** 禹門幻有 from his abbacies; alternative *hào* **Yuántōng** 圓通, **Yīxīn** 一心. **The pivotal Línjì-Yángqí lineage-restorer of the late Míng** — the master from whom the entire Mìyún → Línyě / Tiānyǐn / Wànrú late-Míng-early-Qīng establishment descends. Born **1549** (estimated); died **明萬曆甲寅二月十二日** = **1614-03-21**, age 66. Native of **Lìyáng** 溧陽 (Jiāngsū).

**Lineage centrality.** *Fǎshī* of the entire Mìyún Yuánwù → Línjì-Yángqí post-1614 establishment: his three principal *fǎsì* (the **三大** *sāndà*) were **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642), **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** 天隱圓修 (1575–1635), and **Xuějiāo Yuánxìn** 雪嶖圓信 (1571–1647) — three of the most influential Línjì lineage-builders of the Míng-Qīng transition.

**Abbacies.** **Lìyáng Lóngchí Yǔménshān** 溧陽龍池禹門山 (the principal seat — gives him both his **Lóngchí** and **Yǔmén** sobriquets).

**Work.** [[KR6q0601]] 《幻有傳禪師語錄》 10 juan, J40 (also Qiánlóng Edition Vol. 153 No. 1637, the catalog citation), compiled jointly by his three principal *fǎsì* — **Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] (Mìyún) + **Yuánxiū** [[圓修]] (Tiānyǐn) — *děng biān*. The compilation by Mìyún Yuánwù makes this *yǔlù* the foundational text of the entire late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí revival. The recorded materials include *Nánlù* (southern records) and *Běilù* (northern records) plus his late-life *Xiántán* 閒談 ("idle conversations") and *Wǎnhuà* 晚話 ("evening talks") and his deathbed *mòhòu jì bìng yǔ* 末後偈并語.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 64; multiple modern lineage-studies, esp. Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (2008).
