# Xiàng Jùnqīng 項俊卿

Late-Míng lay-Buddhist of the Xiàng 項 family of Jiāhé 嘉禾 (modern Jiāxīng 嘉興); hào **Lánzhāi** 蘭齋, hence styled *Lánzhāi gōng* 蘭齋公. Son of [[項忠]] Xiàng Zhōng (1421–1502); father of [[項謙]] Xiàng Qiān.

A lifelong *Cuṇḍā* dhāraṇī devotee, he printed a *Zhǔntí yíguǐ* 准提儀軌 and cast 108 *Cuṇḍā* protector-mirrors (*Zhǔntí bǎojìng* 准提寶鏡). Documented in his son Xiàng Qiān's 1652 preface to the *Zhǔntí fénxiū xīdì chànhuǐ xuánwén* ([[KR6j0760]]).
