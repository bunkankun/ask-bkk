# Déqián 德潛

Southern-Sòng monk of the Dōngchán Bào'ēn Guāngxiào chán sì 東禪報恩光孝禪寺 in Fúzhōu; printer of the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0060]]) for inclusion in the Pílú canon 毘盧大藏 in Qiándào 8.1 (January/February 1172).
