Wáng Sēngrú 王僧孺 (465–522 CE; *zì* Sēngrú 僧孺) was a Liáng 梁 court official, literary figure, and bibliographer. He participated in the *Yǒngmíng* era literary salon of Xiāo Zǐliáng 蕭子良, then continued under the Liang dynasty. A distinguished calligrapher and bibliographic compiler, his collected works were listed in twenty-eight *juǎn* in the *Suíshū Jīngjízhì*. Biography in *Liángshū* juǎn 33 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 59. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0056]].
