Dàoyì 道邑 was a Tang-period Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng commentator known principally for his five-fascicle [[KR6n0037]] *Chéng wéishí lùn yìyùn* 成唯識論義蘊 (X49n0814) — a doctrinal-substantive commentary on [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn*. No biographical material survives independently of the work; on internal grounds (his use of [[窺基]]'s *shùjì* but apparent unfamiliarity with [[智周]]'s *yǎn-mì*) he is generally placed in the late seventh century, perhaps a contemporary or slightly junior to [[慧沼]]. The work is preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X49n0814) and not in the Taishō.
