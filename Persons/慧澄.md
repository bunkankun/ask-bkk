# Huìchéng 慧澄

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[子益]] Jiànguān Zǐyì (d. 1267); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0320]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
