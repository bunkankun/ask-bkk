Péi Xuán 裴玄 (*fl.* mid 3rd century), *zì* Yànhuáng 彥黃, was a Sūn-Wú 孫吳 official from Pèiguó 沛國 (modern Ānhuī). He is described in *Sānguó zhì* commentary as the father of Péi Jùn 裴儁 and is mentioned in scattered notices in *Sānguó zhì·Wúshū*. He served under Sūn Quán 孫權. His birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally placed in the mid 3rd century. CBDB has no entry. Sòng catalogs sometimes write the name as 裴元, observing the *yuán* 玄→元 taboo of the Sòng emperor Shènzōng 神宗.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0147]] *Péi Shǐ xīnyán* 裴氏新言.
