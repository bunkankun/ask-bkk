**Asada Shisei** 淺田惟斆 (*zì* Shisei 子誠), early-Meiji Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician of the Asada-ryū 浅田流 school, related to Asada Sōhaku [[淺田惟常]] (probably an adopted son or disciple). Staff member of the Hakusaidō 博濟堂 beriberi hospital from 1878. Named among the institutional co-compilers of [[KR3eh054]] *Kakke gairon* 腳氣概論 (1879). No further biographical material located.
