Jìng-qǐ 淨啟 (d. **康熙甲寅 9/16 = 1674-10-15 Gregorian**), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂. Native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (modern Háng-zhōu); lay surname **Chén** 陳; resident at **Tiáo-xī Fèng-shān** 苕溪鳳山 (Hú-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng). Recorded in some sources as **Duō-fú Jìng-qǐ** 多福淨啟 and **Miào-yè Jìng-qǐ** 妙叶淨啟. Internal preface evidence in [[KR6q0459]] identifies the *zhòng-biān* 重編 hand as a *fǎ-xiōng* called **Qíng-yún** 晴雲 — perhaps Jìng-qǐ's *zì-hào* or alternative monastic-name.

**Work.** [[KR6q0459]] 《明州天童景德禪寺宏智覺禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J32 B272 — the *zhòng-biān* 重編 (re-edited) recension of **Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué** [[正覺]] 宏智正覺 (1091–1157)'s *yǔ-lù*, gathered from scattered sources. Front-preface by **Yì-tíng jū-shì** **Xú Jì-ēn** 逸亭居士徐繼恩 noting that *zì Shào-xīng qì jīn dài shù-bǎi nián, yǔ-jù sǎn-wáng, hǎn dǔ quán-lù; Qíng-yún fǎ-xiōng yú tā jiā zá-jiàn zhě, guǎng wéi sōu-jí, lè chéng yī juan* 自紹興迄今殆數百年語句散亡罕睹全錄晴雲法兄於他家雜見者廣為搜緝勒成一卷 — confirming that the volume is a Qīng-period reconstitution of Sòng-era material.

Sources: DILA authority A016319; 《洞上》 j. 15 p. 614; 《疑年錄》 432; 《道影》 j. 3 p. 194.
