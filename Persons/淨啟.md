Jìngqǐ 淨啟 (d. **康熙甲寅 9/16 = 1674-10-15 Gregorian**), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂. Native of **Qiántáng** 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu); lay surname **Chén** 陳; resident at **Tiáoxī Fèngshān** 苕溪鳳山 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Recorded in some sources as **Duōfú Jìngqǐ** 多福淨啟 and **Miàoyè Jìngqǐ** 妙叶淨啟. Internal preface evidence in [[KR6q0459]] identifies the *zhòngbiān* 重編 hand as a *fǎxiōng* called **Qíngyún** 晴雲 — perhaps Jìngqǐ's *zìhào* or alternative monastic-name.

**Work.** [[KR6q0459]] 《明州天童景德禪寺宏智覺禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J32 B272 — the *zhòngbiān* 重編 (re-edited) recension of **Hóngzhì Zhèngjué** [[正覺]] 宏智正覺 (1091–1157)'s *yǔlù*, gathered from scattered sources. Front-preface by **Yìtíng jūshì** **Xú Jìēn** 逸亭居士徐繼恩 noting that *zì Shàoxīng qì jīn dài shùbǎi nián, yǔjù sǎnwáng, hǎn dǔ quánlù; Qíngyún fǎxiōng yú tā jiā zájiàn zhě, guǎng wéi sōují, lè chéng yī juan* 自紹興迄今殆數百年語句散亡罕睹全錄晴雲法兄於他家雜見者廣為搜緝勒成一卷 — confirming that the volume is a Qīng-period reconstitution of Sòng-era material.
