Zhōu Shènzhāi 周慎齋 (proper name Zhōu Zhīgān 周之干; *c.* 1508–1586) was the principal late-Míng systematiser of the [[李杲]] Lǐ Dōngyuán *bǔtǔ* (補土) spleen-stomach tradition in his Sūzhōu medical circle and the teacher of [[胡慎柔]] Hú Shènróu ([[KR3eh037]]).
