# 耐修子

Nàixiūzǐ, otherwise unidentified pseudonymous late-Qīng author of the anonymous *Báihóu zhìfǎ jìbiǎo juéwēi* 白喉治法忌表抉微, occasioned by the 1888 Beijing diphtheria outbreak. His drug-table is critiqued in detail in [[KR3em020]] *Báihóu tiáobiàn* 白喉條辨.
