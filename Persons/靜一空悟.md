**Jìngyī Kōngwù dàshī** 靜一空悟大師 (Qīng). Buddhist instructor of the Shàolín / *Yìjīn jīng* lineage who transmitted the seated, recumbent, and side-posture figures of the *Yìjīn xǐsuǐ* corpus to Zhōu Shǒurú.
