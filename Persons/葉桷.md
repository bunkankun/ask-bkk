**Yè Jué** 葉桷 (*zì* Bócái 伯材, *fl.* mid-12th c., Southern Sòng) — cousin (*biǎodì* 表弟) of the physician [[陳言]] Chén Yán; addressee and intended publisher of Chén's first six-*juǎn* medical compilation, the *Yīyuán zhǐzhì* 依源指治 (1161, *Shàoxīng* 31), known only through Chén's preface to the later *Sānyīn jíyī bìngzhèng fānglùn* ([[KR3er055]]). Yè was at the Southern-Sòng *xíngzài* 行在 (Línān) court when he received the work and intended to have it printed under noble patronage, but died before he could see it through the press.
