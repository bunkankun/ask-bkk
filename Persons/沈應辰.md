# Shěn Yīngchén 沈應辰

Southern-Sòng layman of Wúxīng 吳興 (modern Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Patron of the *Yuánjué jīng lèijiě* ([[KR6i0563]]) by [[行霆]] Xíngtíng, which he commissioned after obtaining a *jiǎngyì* manuscript from Sìchuān. Lifedates unrecorded.
