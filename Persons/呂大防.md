Northern-Sòng statesman, *zì* Wēizhòng 微仲, of Jīngzhào Lántián 京兆藍田 (modern Shǎnxī). *Jìnshì* of 1049. Rose to *Shàngshū zuǒpúyè* 尚書左僕射 (Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs) under Zhézōng. Elder brother of [[呂大臨]] Lǚ Dàlín; both brothers were students at [[張載]] Zhāng Zài's Héngqú 橫渠 academy and are central figures of the Northern-Sòng Guānxué 關學 milieu.
