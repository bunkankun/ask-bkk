Shǐ Jiǎn 史簡 (active mid-Kāngxī period, *zì* Wén-lìng 文令), of Pó-yáng 鄱陽 (modern Pó-yáng-xiàn, Jiāng-xī). Local scholar; compiler of the *Pó-yáng wǔ-jiā jí* 鄱陽五家集 [[KR4h0165]] — a 15-*juǎn* anthology gathering the *bié-jí* of five Pó-yáng poets from the late Sòng / Yuán transition through the early Míng: **Lí Tíng-ruì** 黎廷瑞 (Sòng *jìnshì* 1271), **Wú Cún** 吳存 (Yuán Yán-yòu 1314 *jǔ-rén*), **Xú Ruì** 徐瑞 (Sòng-Yuán transition), **Yè Lán** 葉蘭 (Yuán-Míng transition; drowned himself rather than serve the Míng), **Liú Bǐng** 劉炳 (early Míng official). The compilation is the **standard pre-modern source for these five regional poets**, several of whose collections survive only through Shǐ Jiǎn's preservation. No CBDB record; standard biographies are thin. Shǐ Jiǎn's work belongs to the Qīng tradition of **regional / local anthological preservation** parallel to Hú Wén-xué's *Yǒng-shàng qí-jiù shī* [[KR4h0162]] and Shěn Jì-yǒu's *Zuì-lǐ shī xì* [[KR4h0163]].
