Shǐ Jiǎn 史簡 (active mid-Kāngxī period, *zì* Wénlìng 文令), of Póyáng 鄱陽 (modern Póyángxiàn, Jiāngxī). Local scholar; compiler of the *Póyáng wǔjiā jí* 鄱陽五家集 [[KR4h0165]] — a 15-*juǎn* anthology gathering the *biéjí* of five Póyáng poets from the late Sòng / Yuán transition through the early Míng: **Lí Tíngruì** 黎廷瑞 (Sòng *jìnshì* 1271), **Wú Cún** 吳存 (Yuán Yányòu 1314 *jǔrén*), **Xú Ruì** 徐瑞 (Sòng-Yuán transition), **Yè Lán** 葉蘭 (Yuán-Míng transition; drowned himself rather than serve the Míng), **Liú Bǐng** 劉炳 (early Míng official). The compilation is the **standard pre-modern source for these five regional poets**, several of whose collections survive only through Shǐ Jiǎn's preservation. No CBDB record; standard biographies are thin. Shǐ Jiǎn's work belongs to the Qīng tradition of **regional / local anthological preservation** parallel to Hú Wénxué's *Yǒngshàng qíjiù shī* [[KR4h0162]] and Shěn Jìyǒu's *Zuìlǐ shī xì* [[KR4h0163]].
