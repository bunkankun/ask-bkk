Hézhé 合哲 (variant 合喆). Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Yànjū Déshēn** [[德申]] (1610–1678). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0590]] 《雲山燕居申禪師語錄》 8 juan, J40 B478.
