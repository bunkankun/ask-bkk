Zuòxiào Shānrén 坐嘯山人 ("Mountain-Man Sitting and Whistling") is the *hào* (pen-name) of an otherwise unidentified late-Qīng physician of the warm-disease tradition, author of the *Zhěnyàn yīfāng gēkuò* 診驗醫方歌括 [[KR3ed091]], a rhymed-verse formulary covering principally the **Wú Jūtōng (Wú Táng) warm-disease formulas** (the *Yínqiào sǎn* 銀翹散, *Sāngjú yǐn* 桑菊飲, etc., from Wú Táng's *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn* 溫病條辨, 1798). The literary-Daoist self-styling *zuò xiào shān rén* (cf. Sū Shì 蘇軾, "*chí jié zuòxiào* 持節坐嘯," and the *Shuǐjīng zhù* tradition of recluse-figures sitting on mountains and whistling) suggests a literatus of the Tóngzhì-Guāngxù generation. No CBDB entry.
