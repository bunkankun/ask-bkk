Wáng Yīrén 王一仁 (fl. mid-20th c.) was a Jiāngnán Chinese-medicine scholar who co-led the 1956 reconstruction of [[姜禮]] Jiāng Lǐ's *Fēngláogǔgé sìdà zhèngzhì* ([[KR3eh038]]).
