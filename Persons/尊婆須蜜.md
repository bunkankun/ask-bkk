Zūn Póxūmì 尊婆須蜜 (Skt. *Vasumitra* "Friend of the Vasus"; DILA Authority A001184) was a Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma master attributed with the *Zūn Póxūmì púsà suǒjí lùn* 尊婆須蜜菩薩所集論 ([[KR6l0014]], T1549). The preface to that text describes him as a bodhisattva predicted to become the future Buddha "Lion Tathāgata" (師子如來), which distinguishes him from the better-known [[世友]] (*Vasumitra*), the master of the Abhidharma "feet" (六足論). Modern scholars (Willemen, Dessein, Cox 1998) treat this *Upaśrī Vasumitra* as a separate historical figure from the Kaśmīra-council Vasumitra, though both bore the same Sanskrit name. Nothing further is known of his biography.
