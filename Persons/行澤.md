Xíngzé 行澤 (1607-09-21 – c. **1654-02-17 / 1655-02-05** = 順治甲午), *hào* **Yúnwài** 雲外, also styled **Shéndǐng** 神鼎 from his late abbacy. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yìnzhōng Tōngshòu** 印中通授 (also **Sōngjì Tōngshòu** 松際通授, and 陽山見授 in the [[KR6q0463]] Wáng Guāngchéng preface) at **Qìngshān** 磬山. Native of **Wùyuán** 婺源 (新安, Xīnān, Huīzhōu); lay surname **Wāng** 汪; lay-name **Yìnglín** 應麟 (mother dreamed of a unicorn-dance, *qí lín wǔ yú tíngwéi* 麟舞於庭維). *Shìshòu* **48**, *sēnglà* **20**.

**Formation.** Father **Zhòngguān** 仲觀, mother **Wángshì** 王氏. Entered school at 6. Tonsured at **25** at **Huángshān Yúngǔyuàn** 黃山雲谷院 under **Wúyì** 無易; full precepts at 26 from **Màolín lǜshī** 茂林律師 in Gūsū. Followed Màolín to **Tiāntóng** 天童 to meet **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] 圓悟; received initial *xǐng* on hearing a door-opening sound at night. Decisive *fùfǎ* under **Tōngshòu** at **Qìngshān** on the *fēngdòng liánmù* 風動簾幙 incident — wind blew a curtain to the ground with a *pūsù* 撲簌 sound, triggering the breakthrough.

**Abbacies and death.** **Qízhōu Lǎozǔsì** 蘄州老祖寺, then **Xiāngyīn Shéndǐngsì** 湘陰神鼎寺 — the latter the seat from which his Hāo *Shéndǐng* derives. Died **順治甲午 = 1654** with the verse *shìfēi hǎi nèi zhǎn quánjī* 是非海內展全機; full-body stūpa at Shéndǐng Zīfúsì right side. **Heirs**: **Hóngfú** [[宏怤]] (compiler of [[KR6q0463]]), **Shēnjí** 深極, **Shēnzhī** 深知, **Shēnhào** 深皜.

**Work.** [[KR6q0463]] 《神鼎雲外澤禪師語錄》 15 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B280, compiled by *cānxué ménrén* **Hóngfú** [[宏怤]] *děng*, with prefaces by **Wáng Guāngchéng Pántán** 王光承槃談 of Yúnjiān (Sōngjiāng) dated **康熙十一年孟春 = 1672 spring** and other prefaces. The original *yǔlù* was praised by the Míng loyalist **Jīn Zhèngxī** 金正希 (= 金聲, 1598–1645) — that earlier preface is also preserved in the volume.

Sources: [[KR6q0463]] juan 15 *Shéndǐng Yúnwài Zé chánshī chuán*; 《五燈全書》 j. 81; 《正源略集》 j. 4; 《五燈全書目錄》 j. 13. DILA A019555.
