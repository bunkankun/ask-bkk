Xíng-zé 行澤 (1607-09-21 – c. **1654-02-17 / 1655-02-05** = 順治甲午), *hào* **Yún-wài** 雲外, also styled **Shén-dǐng** 神鼎 from his late abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yìn-zhōng Tōng-shòu** 印中通授 (also **Sōng-jì Tōng-shòu** 松際通授, and 陽山見授 in the [[KR6q0463]] Wáng Guāng-chéng preface) at **Qìng-shān** 磬山. Native of **Wù-yuán** 婺源 (新安, Xīn-ān, Huī-zhōu); lay surname **Wāng** 汪; lay-name **Yìng-lín** 應麟 (mother dreamed of a unicorn-dance, *qí lín wǔ yú tíng-wéi* 麟舞於庭維). *Shì-shòu* **48**, *sēng-là* **20**.

**Formation.** Father **Zhòng-guān** 仲觀, mother **Wáng-shì** 王氏. Entered school at 6. Tonsured at **25** at **Huáng-shān Yún-gǔ-yuàn** 黃山雲谷院 under **Wú-yì** 無易; full precepts at 26 from **Mào-lín lǜ-shī** 茂林律師 in Gū-sū. Followed Mào-lín to **Tiān-tóng** 天童 to meet **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] 圓悟; received initial *xǐng* on hearing a door-opening sound at night. Decisive *fù-fǎ* under **Tōng-shòu** at **Qìng-shān** on the *fēng-dòng lián-mù* 風動簾幙 incident — wind blew a curtain to the ground with a *pū-sù* 撲簌 sound, triggering the breakthrough.

**Abbacies and death.** **Qí-zhōu Lǎo-zǔ-sì** 蘄州老祖寺, then **Xiāng-yīn Shén-dǐng-sì** 湘陰神鼎寺 — the latter the seat from which his Hāo *Shén-dǐng* derives. Died **順治甲午 = 1654** with the verse *shì-fēi hǎi nèi zhǎn quán-jī* 是非海內展全機; full-body stūpa at Shén-dǐng Zī-fú-sì right side. **Heirs**: **Hóng-fú** [[宏怤]] (compiler of [[KR6q0463]]), **Shēn-jí** 深極, **Shēn-zhī** 深知, **Shēn-hào** 深皜.

**Work.** [[KR6q0463]] 《神鼎雲外澤禪師語錄》 15 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B280, compiled by *cān-xué mén-rén* **Hóng-fú** [[宏怤]] *děng*, with prefaces by **Wáng Guāng-chéng Pán-tán** 王光承槃談 of Yún-jiān (Sōng-jiāng) dated **康熙十一年孟春 = 1672 spring** and other prefaces. The original *yǔ-lù* was praised by the Míng loyalist **Jīn Zhèng-xī** 金正希 (= 金聲, 1598–1645) — that earlier preface is also preserved in the volume.

Sources: [[KR6q0463]] juan 15 *Shén-dǐng Yún-wài Zé chán-shī chuán*; 《五燈全書》 j. 81; 《正源略集》 j. 4; 《五燈全書目錄》 j. 13. DILA A019555.
