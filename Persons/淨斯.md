Jìng-sī 淨斯, *hào* **Bǎi-yú** 百愚 ("Hundred Fool"). Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Ruì-bái Míng-xuě** [[明雪]] 瑞白明雪 (1584–1641). Born **萬曆庚戌 5/21 寅時** = **1610-07-11 Gregorian**; died **康熙乙巳 8/28 卯時** = **1665-10-06 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **56**, *sēng-là* **35**. Native of **Tóng-bǎi** 桐柏 (Nán-yáng); lay surname **Gǔ** 谷.

**Formation.** Tonsured at 21 (1630) at **Dào-lín-sì** 道林寺; full precepts the following year at **Bào-guó-sì** 報國寺. Decisive *xǐng* on a bell-sound under **Ruì-bái Míng-xuě** with formal *jì-bié*.

**Eight abbacies.** **Jiā-dìng Zhāo-qìng** 嘉定昭慶, **Qīng-lóng Lóng-fú** 青龍隆福 (the source of *hào* 青龍百), **Biàn-shān Lóng-huá** 弁山龍華, **Yuè-zhōu Néng-rén** 越州能仁, **Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng** 雲門顯聖 (the Cáo-dòng flagship), **Gāo-tíng Fó-rì** 皋亭佛日, **Guó-shān Shàn-quán** 國山善權, **Jiāng-dū Shàn-qìng** 江都善慶.

**42 named heirs** including **Hán-sōng Cāo** 寒松操, **Zǐ-gǔ Jué** 紫谷覺, **Tiān-jiàn Xiān** 天鑑暹.

**Work.** [[KR6q0498]] 《百愚禪師語錄》 20 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B359, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-cāo** [[智操]] + **Zhì-hǎi** [[智海]] *děng biān-yǔ-lù*. Sources: 《正源略集》 j. 13; 《百愚禪師語錄》 j. 20 *tǎ-zhì-míng*; DILA authority A016277.
