Jìngsī 淨斯, *hào* **Bǎiyú** 百愚 ("Hundred Fool"). Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Ruìbái Míngxuě** [[明雪]] 瑞白明雪 (1584–1641). Born **萬曆庚戌 5/21 寅時** = **1610-07-11 Gregorian**; died **康熙乙巳 8/28 卯時** = **1665-10-06 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **56**, *sēnglà* **35**. Native of **Tóngbǎi** 桐柏 (Nányáng); lay surname **Gǔ** 谷.

**Formation.** Tonsured at 21 (1630) at **Dàolínsì** 道林寺; full precepts the following year at **Bàoguósì** 報國寺. Decisive *xǐng* on a bell-sound under **Ruìbái Míngxuě** with formal *jìbié*.

**Eight abbacies.** **Jiādìng Zhāoqìng** 嘉定昭慶, **Qīnglóng Lóngfú** 青龍隆福 (the source of *hào* 青龍百), **Biànshān Lónghuá** 弁山龍華, **Yuèzhōu Néngrén** 越州能仁, **Yúnmén Xiǎnshèng** 雲門顯聖 (the Cáodòng flagship), **Gāotíng Fórì** 皋亭佛日, **Guóshān Shànquán** 國山善權, **Jiāngdū Shànqìng** 江都善慶.

**42 named heirs** including **Hánsōng Cāo** 寒松操, **Zǐgǔ Jué** 紫谷覺, **Tiānjiàn Xiān** 天鑑暹.

**Work.** [[KR6q0498]] 《百愚禪師語錄》 20 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B359, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìcāo** [[智操]] + **Zhìhǎi** [[智海]] *děng biānyǔlù*. Sources: 《正源略集》 j. 13; 《百愚禪師語錄》 j. 20 *tǎzhìmíng*; DILA authority A016277.
