# 道燈

Dàodēng (sobriquet Yíng'ān 螢菴)

Mid-Míng Buddhist of the Zhìhuàsì 智化寺. Wrote the 1551 postface to the recompiled *Xiāoshì Jīngāng jīng kēyí huìyào zhùjiě* ([[KR6c0055]]) and was the editorial broker who travelled to Mt. Shǎoshì to commission [[覺連]] Juélián to undertake the *chóngjí* (recompiled compendium).
