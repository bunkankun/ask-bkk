# Běnshàn 本善 (Dúfēng Běnshàn 毒峰本善)

Mid-Míng Línjì-lineage Chán master of Sichuan. Also called Dúfēng Jìshàn 毒峰季善 (with *zì* Jìshàn and *hào* Dúfēng 毒峰 "Poison Peak"). Other *hào*: Bīnggǔ 冰谷 ("Ice Valley"). Lifedates 1419 – 1482 or 1483 (died at Tiānzhēnsì 天真寺 in the *rényín* year = 1482, aged 64, per the *Wǔ dēng quán shū* 五燈全書 juan 59 and the *Chán zōng shī chéng jì* 禪宗師承記 by Shuǐyuèzhāi zhǔrén 水月齋主人).

Dharma-heir of Wújì Míngwù 無際明悟 (*hào* Dōngpǔ Dàolín 東普道林 / Gǔtíng 古庭) in the Línjì line; grand-dharma-heir of Chǔshān Shàoqí 楚山紹琦. Active as abbot of **Tiānzhēnsì** 天真寺 in the Chéngdū area of Sichuan. Dharma-heir: [[悟深|Wùshēn]] 悟深.

Surviving work: the *Tiānzhēn Dúfēng Shàn chánshī yào yǔ* 天真毒峰善禪師要語 [[KR6q0194]], compiled by Wùshēn. Běnshàn's teaching-position distinguishes itself by its programmatic emphasis on pre-Bǎizhàng ascetic-discipline (*tóutuó* 頭陀, *dhūta*) practices, in contrast to the institutionalised-monastery Chán of his day.

Sources: *Bǔ xù gāo sēng zhuàn* 補續高僧傳: 毒峰善傳; *Nán Sòng Yuán-Míng chán lín sēng bǎo zhuàn* 南宋元明禪林僧寶傳; *Wǔ dēng huì yuán xù lüè* 五燈會元續略; *Wǔ dēng yán tǒng* 五燈嚴統; *Wǔ dēng quán shū* 五燈全書 juan 59; *Xù zhǐyuè lù* 續指月錄; *Xù dēng zhèng tǒng* 續燈正統; *Xù dēng cún gǎo* 續燈存稿; *Jǐnjiāng chán dēng* 錦江禪燈; *Àn hēi dòu jí* 揞黑豆集; *Jì dēng lù* 繼燈錄.
