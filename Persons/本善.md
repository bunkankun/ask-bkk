# Běnshàn 本善 (Dú-fēng Běn-shàn 毒峰本善)

Mid-Míng Línjì-lineage Chán master of Sichuan. Also called Dú-fēng Jì-shàn 毒峰季善 (with *zì* Jì-shàn and *hào* Dú-fēng 毒峰 "Poison Peak"). Other *hào*: Bīng-gǔ 冰谷 ("Ice Valley"). Lifedates 1419 – 1482 or 1483 (died at Tiān-zhēn-sì 天真寺 in the *rén-yín* year = 1482, aged 64, per the *Wǔ dēng quán shū* 五燈全書 juan 59 and the *Chán zōng shī chéng jì* 禪宗師承記 by Shuǐ-yuè-zhāi zhǔ-rén 水月齋主人).

Dharma-heir of Wú-jì Míng-wù 無際明悟 (*hào* Dōng-pǔ Dào-lín 東普道林 / Gǔ-tíng 古庭) in the Línjì line; grand-dharma-heir of Chǔ-shān Shào-qí 楚山紹琦. Active as abbot of **Tiān-zhēn-sì** 天真寺 in the Chéngdū area of Sichuan. Dharma-heir: [[悟深|Wùshēn]] 悟深.

Surviving work: the *Tiān-zhēn Dú-fēng Shàn chánshī yào yǔ* 天真毒峰善禪師要語 [[KR6q0194]], compiled by Wùshēn. Běn-shàn's teaching-position distinguishes itself by its programmatic emphasis on pre-Bǎizhàng ascetic-discipline (*tóu-tuó* 頭陀, *dhūta*) practices, in contrast to the institutionalised-monastery Chán of his day.

Sources: *Bǔ xù gāo sēng zhuàn* 補續高僧傳: 毒峰善傳; *Nán-Sòng Yuán-Míng chán lín sēng bǎo zhuàn* 南宋元明禪林僧寶傳; *Wǔ dēng huì yuán xù lüè* 五燈會元續略; *Wǔ dēng yán tǒng* 五燈嚴統; *Wǔ dēng quán shū* 五燈全書 juan 59; *Xù zhǐ-yuè lù* 續指月錄; *Xù dēng zhèng tǒng* 續燈正統; *Xù dēng cún gǎo* 續燈存稿; *Jǐn-jiāng chán dēng* 錦江禪燈; *Àn hēi dòu jí* 揞黑豆集; *Jì dēng lù* 繼燈錄.
