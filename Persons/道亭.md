Dàotíng 道亭 (active late 11th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk of the Zháxī Pǔjìngsì 霅溪普靜寺 (in Wúxīng / modern Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Author of the *[[KR6e0075|Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng yì yuàn shū 華嚴一乘教義分齊章義苑疏]]* in 10 fascicles (X995), the major Northern-Sòng commentary on Fǎzàng's *[[KR6e0074|Wǔ jiào zhāng]]*. Per the *Yì yuàn hòu xù* 義苑後序 (the postface, by *Yáng Jié* 楊傑) dated *Yuányòu* 5 (1090), the work was completed by that date. Dàotíng's birth and death dates are not preserved.
