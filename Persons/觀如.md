# Guānrú 觀如

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active at the **Yángzhōu Cángjīngyuàn** 揚州藏經院 (the Sūtra-Repository Cloister of Yángzhōu) during the *Guāngxù* 光緒 reign. Author of the **《蓮修必讀》** *Liánxiū bì dú* [[KR6p0132]] (X1214), a Pure Land devotional verse-and-*gāthā* anthology dated by his own preface to *Guāngxù bǐngxū* (= 12 August 1886). The work is a verse-and-*gāthā* counterpart to [[古崑]] Gǔkūn's prose-oriented *Liánzōng bì dú* [[KR6p0116]] (X1197), and represents the late-Qīng Yángzhōu regional Pure Land community parallel to Gǔkūn's Hangzhou tradition. Lifedates not preserved.
