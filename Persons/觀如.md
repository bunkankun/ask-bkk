# Guān-rú 觀如

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active at the **Yáng-zhōu Cáng-jīng-yuàn** 揚州藏經院 (the Sūtra-Repository Cloister of Yáng-zhōu) during the *Guāng-xù* 光緒 reign. Author of the **《蓮修必讀》** *Lián-xiū bì dú* [[KR6p0132]] (X1214), a Pure Land devotional verse-and-*gāthā* anthology dated by his own preface to *Guāng-xù bǐng-xū* (= 12 August 1886). The work is a verse-and-*gāthā* counterpart to [[古崑]] Gǔ-kūn's prose-oriented *Lián-zōng bì dú* [[KR6p0116]] (X1197), and represents the late-Qīng Yáng-zhōu regional Pure Land community parallel to Gǔ-kūn's Hangzhou tradition. Lifedates not preserved.
