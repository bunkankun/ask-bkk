# Táo Míngqián 陶明潛

Late-Míng lay Buddhist editor. Compiler of the *Xiānjué jí* 先覺集 ([[KR6q0049]]), a brief anthology of lay Chán exemplars. Exact lifedates and native place not transmitted.
