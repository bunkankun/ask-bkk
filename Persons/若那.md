**Ruònà** (Skt. *Jñāna*; 若那) was a Tang-period translator-monk recorded in the DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority Database (A000870) on the strength of a single attested translation: the *Fódǐng zūnshèng tuóluóní bié-fǎ* 佛頂尊勝陀羅尼別法 ([[KR6j0157]] T974F) — an alternate-practice manual for the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī*.

Beyond this single attribution no biographical details are preserved in the canonical bibliographies (no entry in the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* or in Zhìshēng's *Kāiyuán shìjiào lù*); his dates and biographical context cannot be reconstructed with confidence. The Tang-period assignation is conventional and rests on the place of his single translation within the Tang Esoteric *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya* corpus that supplements the canonical Buddhapāli, Dù Xíngyǐ, and Yìjìng / Amoghavajra translations.

Distinct from the contemporary *Jñānaśrī* 智嚴 ([[智嚴]]) and from the Sòng-period *Dharmadeva* 法天 ([[法天]]) of Magadha, whose names also begin with the *Jñāna*-element.
