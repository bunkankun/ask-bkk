Jiāng Kǎi 江闓 (*fl.* late 17th c.), *zì* Chén-liù 辰六; native of Guì-yáng 貴陽 in Guì-zhōu. Early-Qīng minor scholar; performed the final editorial *shān-dìng* (pruning-and-editing) of Wú Qǐ's *Lǐng-nán fēng-wù jì* 嶺南風物記 ([[KR2k0129]]) — the deletions Jiāng made are no longer ascertainable. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 55669 has no birth or death year.
