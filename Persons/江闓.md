Jiāng Kǎi 江闓 (*fl.* late 17th c.), *zì* Chénliù 辰六; native of Guìyáng 貴陽 in Guìzhōu. Early-Qīng minor scholar; performed the final editorial *shāndìng* (pruning-and-editing) of Wú Qǐ's *Lǐngnán fēngwù jì* 嶺南風物記 ([[KR2k0129]]) — the deletions Jiāng made are no longer ascertainable. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 55669 has no birth or death year.
