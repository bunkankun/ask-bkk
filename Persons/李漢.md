# Lǐ Hàn 李漢 (d. 844)

*Zì* Nánjì 南紀. Native of *Lǒngxī* 隴西. Son-in-law and pupil of Hán Yù [[韓愈]]; *jìnshì* of *Yuánhé* 7 (812). Successively *zuǒ shíyí* 左拾遺, *jiàoshūláng* 校書郎, and *Hézhōu cìshǐ* 河州刺史 in the *Chángqìng* / *Bǎolì* periods (820s).

His one major scholarly contribution is the original 40-*juǎn* edition of his father-in-law Hán Yù's collected works (the *Hán Chánglí jí* 韓昌黎集), compiled at Hán Yù's death in 824 with Lǐ's substantial preface (a foundational document of Tang literary biography, preserved in every later Hán Yù edition). The Lǐ Hàn 40-*juǎn* arrangement is the original table-of-contents form of the corpus, preserved across the entire Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng transmission and in the principal annotated editions ([[KR4c0044]], [[KR4c0045]], [[KR4c0046]]).
