# Lǐ Hàn 李漢 (d. 844)

*Zì* Nán-jì 南紀. Native of *Lǒng-xī* 隴西. Son-in-law and pupil of Hán Yù [[韓愈]]; *jìn-shì* of *Yuán-hé* 7 (812). Successively *zuǒ shí-yí* 左拾遺, *jiào-shū-láng* 校書郎, and *Hé-zhōu cì-shǐ* 河州刺史 in the *Cháng-qìng* / *Bǎo-lì* periods (820s).

His one major scholarly contribution is the original 40-*juǎn* edition of his father-in-law Hán Yù's collected works (the *Hán Cháng-lí jí* 韓昌黎集), compiled at Hán Yù's death in 824 with Lǐ's substantial preface (a foundational document of Tang literary biography, preserved in every later Hán Yù edition). The Lǐ Hàn 40-*juǎn* arrangement is the original table-of-contents form of the corpus, preserved across the entire Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng transmission and in the principal annotated editions ([[KR4c0044]], [[KR4c0045]], [[KR4c0046]]).
