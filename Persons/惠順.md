# 惠順
Huìshùn (Japanese: Esun)

Edo-period Hieizan 比叡 (Mt. Hiei) Tendai monk responsible for the Japanese printing of [[智顗]] Zhīyǐ's *Wéimó jīng wén shū* ([[KR6i0099]]) — a text that had been lost in China and preserved only in Japanese monastic libraries. The Edo printing brought the work back into circulation and later into the Chinese canon via the Xuzangjing. Lifedates unrecorded.
