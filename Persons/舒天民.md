Yuán-period educator, *hào* Yì-fēng 蓺風, of Yín-xiàn 鄞縣 (Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng). The author of the *Liù-yì gāng-mù* 六藝綱目 [[KR1j0088]] (2 *juàn*), a versified handbook (in 4-character rhyming lines) of the Six Arts of the *Zhōu-lǐ* Bǎo-shì office (*lǐ* / *yuè* / *shè* / *yù* / *shū* / *shù* — rites, music, archery, charioteering, script, mathematics), based on Zhèng Xuán's [[鄭玄]] commentary. The book was annotated by the author's son [[舒恭]] (*zì* Zì-qiān 自謙, *hào* Shuō-zhāi 說齋) and further co-annotated by the Yín-xiàn fellow-townsman Zhào Yí-zhōng 趙宜中.
