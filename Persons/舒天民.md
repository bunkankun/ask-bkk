Yuán-period educator, *hào* Yìfēng 蓺風, of Yínxiàn 鄞縣 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). The author of the *Liùyì gāngmù* 六藝綱目 [[KR1j0088]] (2 *juàn*), a versified handbook (in 4-character rhyming lines) of the Six Arts of the *Zhōulǐ* Bǎoshì office (*lǐ* / *yuè* / *shè* / *yù* / *shū* / *shù* — rites, music, archery, charioteering, script, mathematics), based on Zhèng Xuán's [[鄭玄]] commentary. The book was annotated by the author's son [[舒恭]] (*zì* Zìqiān 自謙, *hào* Shuōzhāi 說齋) and further co-annotated by the Yínxiàn fellow-townsman Zhào Yízhōng 趙宜中.
