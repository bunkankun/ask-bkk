**Wáng Chǔyī** 王處一 (1142–1217), *hào* Yù yáng zǐ 玉陽子, was **one of the Seven Perfected of Quán zhēn**, founder of the **Yù xiān** 嵛山 branch. Renowned as an ascetic practitioner. Author of [[KR5e0054]] *Yún guāng jí* 雲光集.
