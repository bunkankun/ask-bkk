Early-Qīng official and editor, *zì* Èrqí 二齊, of *Cíxī* 慈谿 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Shùnzhì* 順治 era; held provincial *zhīxiàn* and *zhīfǔ* posts. Active in the *Kāngxī* era as a literary collector. Compiled the *Yuánxiàn yíwén* [[KR4d0016]] in 1 *juǎn* — a Qīng gleaning of Yàn Shū's [[晏殊]] surviving fragments from Sòng *bǐjì*, *shīhuà* and *cí* anthologies. Also compiled the *Cíxī liùjiā shī chāo* 慈谿六家詩鈔 and other minor *cóngshū*. No CBDB match; he is a Qīng-era *Hàn* literary figure beyond the BIOG_MAIN cutoff.
