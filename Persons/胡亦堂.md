Early-Qīng official and editor, *zì* Èr-qí 二齊, of *Cí-xī* 慈谿 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìn-shì* of *Shùn-zhì* 順治 era; held provincial *zhī-xiàn* and *zhī-fǔ* posts. Active in the *Kāng-xī* era as a literary collector. Compiled the *Yuán-xiàn yí-wén* [[KR4d0016]] in 1 *juǎn* — a Qīng gleaning of Yàn Shū's [[晏殊]] surviving fragments from Sòng *bǐ-jì*, *shī-huà* and *cí* anthologies. Also compiled the *Cí-xī liù-jiā shī chāo* 慈谿六家詩鈔 and other minor *cóng-shū*. No CBDB match; he is a Qīng-era *Hàn* literary figure beyond the BIOG_MAIN cutoff.
