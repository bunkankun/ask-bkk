Yànqí 彥琪 (active Shàoshèng 1094–1098), Northern-Sòng Chán monk of the Shūzhōu Fàntiān sì 舒州梵天寺 (modern Ānqìng, Ānhuī). No independent biographical material survives beyond his authorship of the *Zhèng dào gē zhù* 證道歌註 ([[KR6q0133]]) — the earliest surviving systematic commentary on [[玄覺|Yǒngjiā Xuánjué]]'s *Zhèng dào gē* 證道歌, and the foundation for the subsequent commentarial tradition on that text.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0133]] *Zhèng dào gē zhù* (1 juan, X63 n1241) only.
