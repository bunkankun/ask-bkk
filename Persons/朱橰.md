Zhū Gāo 朱橰, *zì* Féngnián 逢年. Younger brother of Zhū Sōng 朱松 [[朱松]] and uncle of Zhū Xī 朱熹. Native of Wùyuán 婺源 (modern Jiāngxī). Lifedates not preserved in CBDB; standard biographical sources do not give a year of birth or death. Active in the early Southern Sòng period, contemporary with his elder brother (Zhū Sōng, 1097–1143).

Author of the *Yùlán jí* 玉瀾集 in 1 *juǎn* — recorded as a separately-circulating work in Chén Zhènsūn 陳振孫's *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* [[KR3h0011]]. The collection was nearly lost; in *Hóngzhì bǐngchén* (1496) Kuàng Fán 鄺璠 of *Rènqiū* 任邱 obtained a copy from Chén Xìngzhī 陳性之 of Suīyáng 睢陽 and appended it to the *Wéizhāi jí* [[KR4d0171]] reprint of his elder brother. Yóu Máo 尤袤 wrote a colophon for the *Yùlán jí*, especially praising the *Chūn fēng* (Spring Wind) piece and the three *Jí shì* (On the Affair) pieces. The *Sìkù* editors are sceptical of Yóu Máo's praise — judging Zhū Gāo's verse not equal to his elder brother's, and that the appended placement is a courtesy rather than equal merit.
