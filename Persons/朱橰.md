Zhū Gāo 朱橰, *zì* Féng-nián 逢年. Younger brother of Zhū Sōng 朱松 [[朱松]] and uncle of Zhū Xī 朱熹. Native of Wù-yuán 婺源 (modern Jiāng-xī). Lifedates not preserved in CBDB; standard biographical sources do not give a year of birth or death. Active in the early Southern Sòng period, contemporary with his elder brother (Zhū Sōng, 1097–1143).

Author of the *Yù-lán jí* 玉瀾集 in 1 *juǎn* — recorded as a separately-circulating work in Chén Zhèn-sūn 陳振孫's *Zhí-zhāi shū-lù jiě-tí* [[KR3h0011]]. The collection was nearly lost; in *Hóng-zhì bǐng-chén* (1496) Kuàng Fán 鄺璠 of *Rèn-qiū* 任邱 obtained a copy from Chén Xìng-zhī 陳性之 of Suī-yáng 睢陽 and appended it to the *Wéi-zhāi jí* [[KR4d0171]] reprint of his elder brother. Yóu Máo 尤袤 wrote a colophon for the *Yù-lán jí*, especially praising the *Chūn fēng* (Spring Wind) piece and the three *Jí shì* (On the Affair) pieces. The *Sì-kù* editors are sceptical of Yóu Máo's praise — judging Zhū Gāo's verse not equal to his elder brother's, and that the appended placement is a courtesy rather than equal merit.
