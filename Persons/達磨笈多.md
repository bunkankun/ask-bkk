Dámójíduō 達磨笈多 (\*Dharmagupta; *d.* 619 CE) — the full transliterated name of [[笈多|Jíduō 笈多]]. The two forms refer to the same person: 達磨笈多 / 達摩岌多 / 法藏 are all variants of the same Suí-period Indian translator. Both forms appear in the canonical signatures.

This person-note exists to support wikilinks from work-notes that use the longer form 達磨笈多 in their translator-signature; for the principal biographical entry, see [[笈多]].

Works in the Kanripo corpus: see [[笈多]].
