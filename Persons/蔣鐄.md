Late-Míng *Tiānqǐ* 天啟-era literatus of *Yíxīng* 宜興 (Chángzhōu), the 20th-generation descendant of Jiǎng Táng [[蔣堂]] (980–1054). Compiled the *Chūnqīng yígǎo* [[KR4d0018]] (1 *juǎn*) by gathering surviving fragments of his ancestor's lost 20-*juǎn* literary corpus from family manuscripts and external citations.
