# Tóngjiāng Yīng 桐江瑛

Sòng-period Vinaya / *zhǐguān* master (lifedates not preserved), author of the *Guānxīn míng* 觀心銘 ("Mind-Contemplation Inscription") appended as a *fùlù* in [[性祇]] Xìng Qí's *Píní rìyòng lù* ([[KR6k0258]]), where the work is cited as a staple of the late-Míng Vinaya *xíngchí* 行持 curriculum. The 桐江 prefix is a place-style (lit. "Tóng-river", a Zhèjiāng locale); "Yīng" is the single-character given dharma-name. No independent biography preserved.
