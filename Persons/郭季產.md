**Guō Jì-chǎn** 郭季產 (Liú-Sòng, *floruit* mid-5th century), little-attested early-medieval southern writer credited in the *Suí shū jīng-jí zhì* 隋書經籍志 and both Táng bibliographic treatises with a *Jíyì jì* 集異記 in 1 *juàn* (cited in those catalogs as a Sòng-period *zhì-guài* compilation) and a *Xù Jìn jì* 續晉紀 (5 *juàn*) — a continuation of Liú-Sòng historical writing on the preceding Eastern-Jìn period. No biography of Guō Jì-chǎn is preserved in the *Sòng shū* or *Nán shǐ*; the man is known only from these book-list entries. Neither lifedates nor official career are known. He is conventionally placed in the Liú-Sòng dynasty (420–479), with his work transmitted into Táng catalogs but lost as unitary text by the Sòng. The principal modern reconstruction of the *Jíyì jì* is Lǔ Xùn's *Gǔ xiǎoshuō gōuchén* 古小說鉤沉.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the credited author of [[KR3l0153]] *Jíyì jì*.
