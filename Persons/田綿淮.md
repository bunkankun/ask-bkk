**Tián Miánhuái** 田綿淮 (*hào* **Hánjìnzǐ** 寒勁子, "The Cold-Tempered One", *fl.* mid-Tóng-zhì era, ca. 1860s–1880s), Qīng literatus-physician of the immediate post-Tài-píng restoration period. Compiler of the *Yīfāng shíjǐn* 醫方拾錦 ([[KR3ed132]]) in 4 *juǎn*, brought to print in 1873 with a literary preface by his colleague [[莫知之]] (Mò Zhīzhī, *hào* Yúnyán) and calligraphy by Wáng Fēngnián of Dàliáng. The work is in the *jīngyàn fāng* tradition with an explicit literary-curated framing. Sparse further biographical record.
