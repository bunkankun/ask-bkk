Jīn-gāng-xiān lùn-shī 金剛仙論師 (\*Vajra-ṛṣi, "Vajra-Sage Master"; lifedates unknown), Indic Buddhist commentator, named in [[KR6c0033|*Jīn-gāng-xiān lùn* 金剛仙論 (T1512)]] as the *釋者* (interpreter / sub-commentator) on Vasubandhu's *Vajracchedikā-vyākhyā*. The Indic identification of *Vajra-ṛṣi* is uncertain — possibly a *Mahāyāna-Yogācāra* sub-commentator of Vasubandhu's school, or a Chinese-period redactor's identification.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6c0033]] *Jīn-gāng-xiān lùn* (T1512).
