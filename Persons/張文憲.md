Zhāng Wénxiàn 張文憲 (lifedates unrecorded), Qing-dynasty lay-scholar editor. Brother of [[張文嘉]] Zhāng Wénjiā; with Wénjiā compiled the *Chánlín bǎoxùn hé zhù* 禪林寶訓合註 ([[KR6q0154]], X64 n1263). Wénxiàn served as *cān yuè* 參閱 (editorial reviewer) while Wénjiā supplied the *jiào dìng* 較定 collation-and-correction.

No independent biographical material; no DILA authority entry. Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0154]] only (editorial-review role).
