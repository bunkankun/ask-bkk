Jīnéng 機能, *zì* **Tiānzé** 天則 ("Heaven-Norm"), *biéhào* **Jìngshàn** 淨善; often referred to as **Yílín Tiānzé** 宜林天則 or **Yǔqí Tiānzé** 語崎天則 from his abbacy and his hermitage. LínjìYángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Shānduó Zhēnzài** [[真在]] (1621–1672). Born **明萬曆戊午 9/26 午時** = **1618-11-12** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 60 in **康熙丁巳** = **1677** at the time of the *yǔlù* preface. Native of **Huánggāng** 黃岡 (Húběi); lay surname **Kāng** 康.

**Formation.** Tonsured at **Ānguósì** 安國寺; studied Mahāyāna scriptures and concluded that the matter of Chán "is not in words and texts." Took full ordination at **Xìngyán** 杏嵒. First sought instruction at **Jīnlíng Tiānjièsì** 金陵天界寺, where the abbot **Shèng** 盛 (Juélàng Dàoshèng?) gave him the *kōan* "三不是" — partial breakthrough. Continued *cān* at **Tiāntóng** and under **Yúnmén chánshī** 雲門禪師; received deep guidance from **Shānwēng Mǐn** 山翁忞 at **Dàochǎngshān** 道場山, who appointed him *shǒuzhòng*. After unrest forced him to leave, returned to **Yíshuǐ** 義水 (Húběi) and built a hermitage at **Yǔqíshān** 語崎山. Final *fùfǎ* under **Shānduó Zhēnzài** in **康熙乙巳秋** = **1665** autumn.

**Abbacy.** **Yíshuǐ Yílínsì** 義水宜林寺.

**Disciples.** **Méichè Xùmí** 梅徹續禰 (variant 續彌, [[續彌]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B417, recorded by his attendants **Dàochóng** [[道崇]] and **Xùmí** [[續彌]] *tóng lù*; with prefaces by lay disciple **Luó Kāilín** [[羅開驎]] and dharma-elder-cousin **Shíyuán Jīyún** [[機雲]] (preface dated **康熙十七季歲次丁巳陽月** = **1677** lunar 10).

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 102, vol. 108; 《天則能禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行略〉.
