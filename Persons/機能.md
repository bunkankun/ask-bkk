Jī-néng 機能, *zì* **Tiān-zé** 天則 ("Heaven-Norm"), *bié-hào* **Jìng-shàn** 淨善; often referred to as **Yí-lín Tiān-zé** 宜林天則 or **Yǔ-qí Tiān-zé** 語崎天則 from his abbacy and his hermitage. Línjì-Yángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Shān-duó Zhēn-zài** [[真在]] (1621–1672). Born **明萬曆戊午 9/26 午時** = **1618-11-12** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 60 in **康熙丁巳** = **1677** at the time of the *yǔ-lù* preface. Native of **Huáng-gāng** 黃岡 (Hú-běi); lay surname **Kāng** 康.

**Formation.** Tonsured at **Ān-guó-sì** 安國寺; studied Mahāyāna scriptures and concluded that the matter of Chán "is not in words and texts." Took full ordination at **Xìng-yán** 杏嵒. First sought instruction at **Jīn-líng Tiān-jiè-sì** 金陵天界寺, where the abbot **Shèng** 盛 (Jué-làng Dào-shèng?) gave him the *kōan* "三不是" — partial breakthrough. Continued *cān* at **Tiān-tóng** and under **Yún-mén chán-shī** 雲門禪師; received deep guidance from **Shān-wēng Mǐn** 山翁忞 at **Dào-chǎng-shān** 道場山, who appointed him *shǒu-zhòng*. After unrest forced him to leave, returned to **Yí-shuǐ** 義水 (Hú-běi) and built a hermitage at **Yǔ-qí-shān** 語崎山. Final *fù-fǎ* under **Shān-duó Zhēn-zài** in **康熙乙巳秋** = **1665** autumn.

**Abbacy.** **Yí-shuǐ Yí-lín-sì** 義水宜林寺.

**Disciples.** **Méi-chè Xù-mí** 梅徹續禰 (variant 續彌, [[續彌]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B417, recorded by his attendants **Dào-chóng** [[道崇]] and **Xù-mí** [[續彌]] *tóng lù*; with prefaces by lay disciple **Luó Kāi-lín** [[羅開驎]] and dharma-elder-cousin **Shí-yuán Jī-yún** [[機雲]] (preface dated **康熙十七季歲次丁巳陽月** = **1677** lunar 10).

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 102, vol. 108; 《天則能禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行略〉.
