# Hóng Qiáo-zǔ 洪喬祖

Yuán-dynasty lay Chán *cān-xué* participant at the Xī-fēng assembly of [[原妙]] Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào (1238–1296) on Tiān-mù-shān; editor (*biān* 編) of the *Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào chánshī chán-yào* ([[KR6q0334]]), compiled from Chí-zhèng's transcripts at the request of Yǒng-zhōng shàng-rén 永中上人 of Gū-sū. His preface to the *Chán-yào* states the *gāng-mù / lǐng-lǚ* (net-rope / robe-collar) compilation principle by which the *shì-zhòng* talks were distilled into essentials. Lifedates unrecorded.
