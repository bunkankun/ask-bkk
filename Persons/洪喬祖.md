# Hóng Qiáozǔ 洪喬祖

Yuán-dynasty lay Chán *cānxué* participant at the Xīfēng assembly of [[原妙]] Gāofēng Yuánmiào (1238–1296) on Tiānmùshān; editor (*biān* 編) of the *Gāofēng Yuánmiào chánshī chányào* ([[KR6q0334]]), compiled from Chízhèng's transcripts at the request of Yǒngzhōng shàngrén 永中上人 of Gūsū. His preface to the *Chányào* states the *gāngmù / lǐnglǚ* (net-rope / robe-collar) compilation principle by which the *shìzhòng* talks were distilled into essentials. Lifedates unrecorded.
