Hé Liángjùn 何良俊 (1506–1573), *zì* Yuánlǎng 元朗, *hào* Bǎizhāi 柏齋 (also *Qīngsēn jūshì* 清森居士), a mid-Míng *Sōngjiāngfǔ* *Huátíng* 華亭 (modern Shànghǎi) literatus, bibliophile, and connoisseur. Brother of the *qǔ* dramatist Hé Liángfù 何良傅. As a youth he reportedly read widely in his family's library, the *Sìyǒuzhāi* 四友齋 ("Studio of Four Friends" — i.e., Hé himself and Zhuāngzǐ, Wéimójié, and Bái Jūyì, the three companions of his solitude), which became the *hào* of his collected *bǐjì*. He never passed the *jìnshì* examination; on the strength of his *gòngshēng* standing he was appointed *Hànlínyuàn kǒngmù* 翰林院孔目 (a junior copyist office in the Hanlin Academy) in *Jiājìng* 32 (1553). Disillusioned by his stagnant career and by the political climate of the late Jiājìng court, he retired to Sōngjiāng in *Jiājìng* 35 (1556) and devoted the rest of his life to scholarship, collecting, and connoisseurship of painting, calligraphy, and *kūnqǔ* music — he was one of the earliest patrons of the emerging *Kūnqǔ* style in the *Wú* region.

The catalog meta `data/catalogs/meta/KR3l.yaml` gives his birth year as 1526; this appears to be a typographical slip. CBDB (id 128402, drawing on the *Míngrén zhuànjì zīliào suǒyǐn* p. 270) and standard reference works (Wikipedia, *Zhōngguó wénxué jiā dàcídiǎn*) give 1506, followed here.

He was closely associated with Wén Zhēngmíng 文徵明 (who supplied the 1551 preface to the *Yǔlín*) and with the broader *Wújùn* circle of Wén, Zhù Yǔnmíng 祝允明, Lù Shēn 陸深, and Lù Cài 陸采.

Major surviving works in the Kanripo corpus:
- [[KR3l0089]] *Yǔlín* 語林 — a 30-*juàn* *Shìshuō*-style topical anecdote-compendium covering Hàn through Yuán, the principal *Vorlage* for Wáng Shìzhēn's *Shìshuō xīnyǔ bǔ*.
- [[KR3l0090]] *Sìyǒuzhāi cóngshuō* 四友齋叢說 — his *bǐjì* miscellany, an important source for mid-Míng cultural, art-historical, and theatrical history.

He also left the *Hé Hànlín jí* 何翰林集 (poetry and prose collection, 28 *juàn*) and a substantial body of *Kūnqǔ* connoisseurial criticism preserved within the *Sìyǒuzhāi cóngshuō*.
