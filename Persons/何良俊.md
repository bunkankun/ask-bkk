Hé Liángjùn 何良俊 (1506–1573), *zì* Yuán-lǎng 元朗, *hào* Bǎi-zhāi 柏齋 (also *Qīng-sēn jū-shì* 清森居士), a mid-Míng *Sōng-jiāng-fǔ* *Huá-tíng* 華亭 (modern Shàng-hǎi) literatus, bibliophile, and connoisseur. Brother of the *qǔ* dramatist Hé Liángfù 何良傅. As a youth he reportedly read widely in his family's library, the *Sì-yǒu-zhāi* 四友齋 ("Studio of Four Friends" — i.e., Hé himself and Zhuāng-zǐ, Wéi-mó-jié, and Bái Jū-yì, the three companions of his solitude), which became the *hào* of his collected *bǐ-jì*. He never passed the *jìn-shì* examination; on the strength of his *gòng-shēng* standing he was appointed *Hàn-lín-yuàn kǒng-mù* 翰林院孔目 (a junior copyist office in the Hanlin Academy) in *Jiā-jìng* 32 (1553). Disillusioned by his stagnant career and by the political climate of the late Jiā-jìng court, he retired to Sōng-jiāng in *Jiā-jìng* 35 (1556) and devoted the rest of his life to scholarship, collecting, and connoisseurship of painting, calligraphy, and *kūn-qǔ* music — he was one of the earliest patrons of the emerging *Kūn-qǔ* style in the *Wú* region.

The catalog meta `data/catalogs/meta/KR3l.yaml` gives his birth year as 1526; this appears to be a typographical slip. CBDB (id 128402, drawing on the *Míng-rén zhuàn-jì zī-liào suǒ-yǐn* p. 270) and standard reference works (Wikipedia, *Zhōng-guó wén-xué jiā dà-cí-diǎn*) give 1506, followed here.

He was closely associated with Wén Zhēngmíng 文徵明 (who supplied the 1551 preface to the *Yǔlín*) and with the broader *Wú-jùn* circle of Wén, Zhù Yǔn-míng 祝允明, Lù Shēn 陸深, and Lù Cài 陸采.

Major surviving works in the Kanripo corpus:
- [[KR3l0089]] *Yǔlín* 語林 — a 30-*juàn* *Shìshuō*-style topical anecdote-compendium covering Hàn through Yuán, the principal *Vorlage* for Wáng Shìzhēn's *Shìshuō xīnyǔ bǔ*.
- [[KR3l0090]] *Sì-yǒu-zhāi cóng-shuō* 四友齋叢說 — his *bǐ-jì* miscellany, an important source for mid-Míng cultural, art-historical, and theatrical history.

He also left the *Hé Hàn-lín jí* 何翰林集 (poetry and prose collection, 28 *juàn*) and a substantial body of *Kūn-qǔ* connoisseurial criticism preserved within the *Sì-yǒu-zhāi cóng-shuō*.
