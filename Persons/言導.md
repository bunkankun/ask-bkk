# Xuánjué Yándǎo 玄覺言導

Mid-tenth-century Chán monk; disciple of [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益, opened dharma at the Bàocí yuàn 報慈院 established by the Southern Táng ruler [[李煜]] Lǐ Yù.
