# Liáng-guī 良圭

Yuán Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]), where he is named alongside 思敬 in the *juan* 1 colophon. Lifedates unrecorded.
