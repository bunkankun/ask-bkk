# 曇摩跋檀

Tánmóbátán 曇摩跋檀 (Skt. *Dharmabhadanta*?; *fl.* 394 CE), Indian Abhidharma master active at Kucha 龜茲 under Lǚ Guāng's Hòu-Liáng 後涼 administration. According to the closing colophon of the *Chēngyáng zhū fó gōngdé jīng* 稱揚諸佛功德經 ([[KR6i0010]]), he first rendered the Sanskrit *vorlage* into Tokharian (the Kucha vernacular, 龜茲語) on the 20th day of the 6th month of Línjiā 麟嘉 6 (394 CE) at the Jīnhuácí 金華祠 shrine in Kucha; [[慧海]] Huìhǎi then translated his Tokharian draft into Chinese. The episode is one of the very few attested cases of a Tokharian intermediary stage in the Sanskrit-to-Chinese transmission of a sūtra.
