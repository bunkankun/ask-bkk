# Rú-chóng 如崇

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[璧|Shān-huī Bì]] 山暉璧 in the Pò-shān / Yuán-liáo Sichuan-Guì-zhōu sub-line. Head recorder (*děng lù* 等錄) of [[KR6q0437]] 《山暉禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B223).

The 如-generation character is non-standard for the main Mìyún lineage-poem — likely a Guì-zhōu regional variant in the Shān-huī sub-line or a personal *fǎ-huì*. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
