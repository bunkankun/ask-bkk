# Rúchóng 如崇

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[璧|Shānhuī Bì]] 山暉璧 in the Pòshān / Yuánliáo Sichuan-Guì-zhōu sub-line. Head recorder (*děng lù* 等錄) of [[KR6q0437]] 《山暉禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B223).

The 如-generation character is non-standard for the main Mìyún lineage-poem — likely a Guìzhōu regional variant in the Shānhuī sub-line or a personal *fǎhuì*. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
