**Lǐ Lì** 李力, modern Chinese-medicine editor; principal editor of *Chén Xiūyuán yīxué quánshū* 陳修園醫學全書 (Beijing: Rénmín wèishēng chūbǎnshè, 2002), the comprehensive modern edition of [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ's complete medical corpus.
