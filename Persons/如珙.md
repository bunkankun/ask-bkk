# Rú-gǒng 如珙

Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì master, *zì* Zǐ-pú 子璞, *hào* Héng-chuān 橫川. Dàjiàn-下第 21 世 (21st-generation descendant of Huì-néng), dharma-heir of Tiān-mù Wén-lǐ 天目文禮 (1167–1250) at Tàibǎi-shān (Tiān-tóng). Native of Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (Zhèjiāng), lay surname Lín 林; born in Jiādìng 15 (1222) of the Sòng Níngzōng era; died at Āyù-wáng on the 18th of the 3rd month of Zhìyuán 26 (16 April 1289), aged 68. Tonsured at fifteen by his uncle, the monk Zhèng-zé 正則 (DILA A022208), received the precepts at Guǎng-cí-yuàn 廣慈院, and trained early at Líng-yǐn under Shí-tián Fǎ-xūn 石田法薰 and Chī-jué Dào-chōng 癡絕道冲 before attaining awakening at Tàibǎi-shān under Wén-lǐ.

He held three major abbacies — Ruì-ān-fǔ Yàndàng-shān Líng-yán chán-sì 靈巖禪寺 (from Xiánchún 4 / 1268, at the request of a *píng-zhāng guó-gōng* 平章國公), Néng-rén chán-sì 能仁禪寺 (from Xiánchún 8 / 1272), and Míng-zhōu Āyù-wáng-shān Guǎng-lì chán-sì 阿育王山廣利禪寺 (from Zhìyuán 16 / 1279, by Yuán imperial edict) — whose records form the three main sections of his two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0344]]). He retired briefly to a Fàng-mù-liáo 放牧寮 on Yàndàng-shān between abbacies. In his 66th year (1287) he composed the *tǎ-míng* that would mark his own stupa at Gǔ-mào-shān 古鄮山.

The name under which he is best known in Chinese scholarship — including DILA, the *Mù-qián jí* 牧潛集, *Bǔ-xù gāo-sēng zhuàn* *juan* 12, *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 4, and *Wǔ-dēng yán-tǒng* *juan* 21 — is 如珙; the form 行珙 appears on the CBETA Xuzangjing edition of his *yǔlù* and in some later lamp-records, and DILA treats the two as alternate names of the same person (A007633).

Named dharma-heirs include [[妙道]] Zhú-yuán Miào-dào 竺原妙道, [[清茂]] Gǔ-lín Qīng-mào 古林清茂 (1262–1329; see [[KR6q0345]], [[KR6q0346]]), and [[覺恩]] Duàn-jiāng Jué-ēn 斷江覺恩. Through Qīng-mào's dense later transmission to Japanese 入元僧, Rú-gǒng's line — "Héng-chuān's stream" — became the dominant strand of late-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí orthodoxy carried into Japanese Zen.
