Yú Yòu 余祐 (1465–1528), of **Bōyáng** 鄱陽 (Ráozhōu, Jiāngxī); disciple and editor of the orthodox-Zhū *Lǐxué* master **Hú Jūrén** 胡居仁 ([[胡居仁]]). Editor of *Hú Wénjìng jí* in 3 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0144]]), gathered together from the scattered remains of his teacher's writings — Hú had explicitly refused to author much, holding that *xiānrú zhī yán zhì yǐ, shān qí fán kě yě* (the prior Confucians' words have reached the limit; one need only cut the redundancies). CBDB id 128359, 1465–1528.
