**Mǎ Qí** 馬齊 (Manchu *Maci* of the Magiyaca / 馬佳氏 clan; *zì* Èndū-fū 恩都府; 1652–1739), early-Qīng Manchu Grand Secretary and one of the most senior civil officials of the Kāngxī–Yōngzhèng–Qiánlóng transition. Bordered Yellow Banner; son of the Kāngxī-era Board of Revenue President **Mishan** 米思翰 (1632–1675). Rose through the Imperial Household and the Boards (兵部尚書 1689, 戶部尚書 1690s) to become Grand Secretary (武英殿大學士 1699) and Captain-General of the Imperial Bodyguard (領侍衛內大臣). One of the four Manchu plenipotentiaries who negotiated and signed the *Treaty of Kyakhta* (1727) demarcating the Sino-Russian frontier.

A central figure in the Kāngxī succession dispute: Mǎ Qí led the Manchu faction supporting **Aisin Gioro Yìnsì** 胤禩 ("the Eighth Prince") as Kāngxī's successor and was consequently demoted on Yōngzhèng's accession in 1722–23, before being partially rehabilitated under Qiánlóng. He is the compiler of *Yǎng-shēng mì-zhǐ* 養生秘旨 ([[KR3eo005]]) — an anthology of canonical Hàn *yǎng-shēng* texts drawn together during his retirement / disgrace period (probably 1715–1735). Also held overall editorial supervision of the *Lì-fǎ tǒng-zhì* 曆法統志 (1690s) and the early phases of the *Bā-qí tōng-zhì* 八旗通志.
