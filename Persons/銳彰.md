# Ruì-zhāng 銳彰

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[子益]] Jiàn-guān Zǐ-yì (d. 1267); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0320]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
