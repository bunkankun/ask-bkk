**Dharmasena** / **Dámóqīnà** 達磨栖那 / 達摩栖那 (Tang, lifedates unknown) — Tang-period Indian Buddhist translator-monk. His sole attested Chinese translation is:

- **Dà miào jīngāng dà gānlù jūn-ná-lì yàn-mán chì-shèng fódǐng jīng** 大妙金剛大甘露軍拏利焰鬘熾盛佛頂經 ([[KR6j0142]], T19n0965) — *Sūtra of the Great-Wonderful Vajra Great-Amṛta Kuṇḍalin Flame-Garland Blazing-Buddha-Crown*.

The text is an Esoteric *Buddhoṣṇīṣa* scripture in the *uṣṇīṣa-cakra-rāja* family (alongside the various Buddha-Crown / Sitātapatra / Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya scriptures of the Tang Esoteric corpus). Dharmasena's lifedates and place of origin are not preserved in surviving records.
