Péng Guīnián 彭龜年 (1142–1206), *zì* Zǐshòu 子壽, of *Qīngjiāng* 清江 (modern Jiāngxī), *jìnshì* of *Qiándào* 5 (1169). A pupil of Zhāng Shì [[張栻]] and Zhū Xī [[朱熹]], he served as *yòushǐ* 右史 (Right Recorder) and tutor to the future *Níngzōng* in the latter's princely residence. Famous for repeated face-to-face remonstrations against *Guāngzōng*'s refusal to attend court at *Chónghuágōng* during the 1190s filial-piety crisis. Aligned with the *Dàoxué* faction; was demoted alongside Zhū Xī when both petitioned against *Hán Tuōzhòu* 韓侂胄 in 1196. Restored to office; retired with *Bǎomógé dàizhì*; posthumously honored as *Zhōngsù* 忠肅. *Sòngshǐ* j. 393. CBDB id 14833. Author of [[KR4d0251]] *Zhǐtáng jí*.
