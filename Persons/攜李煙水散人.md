**Xīlǐ Yānshuǐ Sǎnrén** 攜李煙水散人 ("the wandering recluse of misty waters from Xīlǐ") is the pen name of the anonymous author of the Qīng romance novel *Hépǔ Zhū* 合浦珠 [[KR4k0128]], composed after 1644. The toponym Xīlǐ 攜李 is an ancient name for Jiāxīng 嘉興 in Zhèjiāng, suggesting a possible connection to that region. The true identity of the author has not been established. Biographical details — native place, examination record, official career — are unknown. CBDB contains no entry for this pen name.
