Xǔ Xiàochóng 許孝崇, *fl.* mid-7th century, Imperial Physician (尚藥奉御) under Táng Gāozōng, and one of the senior medical members of the editorial committee that produced the *Xīnxiū běncǎo* ([[KR3ec004]]) in 659. He held the further rank of Tàizhōng dàfū 太中大夫. Kǒng Zhìyuē's preface names him alongside Lǐ Jì and Zhǎngsūn Wújì as one of the principal supervising signatories. No independent biography survives; he is known only from the *Xīnxiū běncǎo*'s editorial list and a handful of incidental imperial-physician references.
