**Liú Dàozhēn** 劉道真, *hào* Chóngxuán zǐ 重玄子 ("Master of the Doubled Mystery" — alluding to Lǎozǐ chap. 1 *xuánzhīyòu xuán* 玄之又玄), was a Quánzhēn 全真 Daoist of Rǔnán 汝南 (Hénán) active in the late thirteenth century. He authored the colophon (跋) to [[KR5a0288|*Xī yí zhǐ mí lùn* 析疑指迷論]] (DZ 276), dated *Dàdé wùxū zhòngchūn shuò* 大德戊戌仲春朔 (the new moon of the second month of the *wùxū* year of the *Dàdé* reign-period, 1298). The colophon praises Niú Dàochún 牛道淳's "lofty discussion in two installments, called *Xī yí zhǐ mí*: pure conversation that withholds nothing of the breast, profound subtlety borne forth in brushwork; what he has nourished his whole life, here is seen." Beyond this single attested writing, no biographical record survives.
