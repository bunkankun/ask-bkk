# Wáng Xī 王晳

Northern-Sòng *Chūnqiū* and *Yìjīng* scholar; native of Tài-yuán 太原 (modern Shānxī). Active in the Tiān-xǐ 天禧 (1017–1021) and Zhì-hé 至和 (1054–1056) eras under Sòng Zhēnzōng and Sòng Rénzōng. The graphic 晳 in the catalog meta is a variant of 皙; both forms are attested. Lifedates not preserved.

The *Sìkù tíyào* (under [[KR1e0019]]) corrects Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí* attribution of him as a *Tài-cháng bóshì* 太常博士: Gōng Dǐng-chén's 龔鼎臣 *Dōng-yuán lù* 東原錄 records that in the Tiān-xǐ era he was already a Hànlín *xué-shì* 學士 conferring with Yàn Shū 晏殊 on Cáo Lì-yòng and Dīng Wèi's continued tenure — a more senior post.

CBDB id 38388 records 王皙 as fl. 1078 — likely a different person, since Wáng Xī of the *Chūnqiū huáng gāng lùn* was a senior Hànlín scholar in the Tiān-xǐ era of 1017–1021, not 1078. The CBDB record may have conflated two separate figures.

Three *Chūnqiū* works are recorded in Wáng Yīnglín's *Yùhǎi*:
- *Chūnqiū tōng yì* 春秋通義 in 12 juan (lost).
- *Chūnqiū yì yì* 春秋異義 in 12 juan (lost).
- *Chūnqiū huáng gāng lùn* 春秋皇綱論 in 5 juan ([[KR1e0019]], surviving).

Also wrote on the *Yìjīng* — a *Yì shuō* 易說 listed in the *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*. None of his other writings is firmly extant.

The *Huáng gāng lùn* is methodologically distinctive among Northern-Sòng *Chūnqiū* commentaries for its balance: against Sūn Fù [[孫復]]'s rigorist *yǒu biǎn wú bāo* 有貶無褒 doctrine, Wáng holds that the *Chūnqiū* must contain praise as well as blame; against the Dàn-Zhào-Lù tendency to discard the three commentaries, Wáng argues for selective use of all three. The *Sìkù tíyào* describes this as "not losing the ancient meaning" — among the highest praise the editors give to a Sòng *Chūnqiū* work.
