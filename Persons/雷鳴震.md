**Léi Mínzhèn** 雷鳴震 (*Léishì zǐ* 雷氏子) of Nándū 南都 (Nánjīng) — Míng-period publisher of the 1535 (Jiājìng 14) print of *Jìshēng chǎnbǎo* ([[KR3ei068]]). Acquired the Sòng-edition manuscript by chance and prepared the wood-blocks; preface by [[王子沖]] Wáng Zǐchōng. Not in CBDB.
