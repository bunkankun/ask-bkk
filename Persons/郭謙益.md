**Guō Qiānyì** 郭謙益 of Yǒngshàng 甬上 (= Níngbō 寧波, Zhèjiāng), late-Qīng literatus; author of the second preface (dated 10th month *yǐwèi* / late 1895) to Wáng Shàngdá's *Jìshēng jí* 濟生集 ([[KR3ei014]]), written from the *Xiàoliántáng dōngshè* 孝廉堂之東舍. No further biographical identification located.
