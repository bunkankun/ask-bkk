Lǐ Guāngpō 李光坡 (1651–1723). *Zì* Sìqīng 耜卿, *hào* Màofū 茂夫. Native of Ān-xī 安溪 in Quán-zhōu prefecture (modern Ān-xī, Fújiàn). Younger brother of the major early-Qīng court scholar-official Lǐ Guāngdì 李光地 (1642–1718, Grand Secretary). Per Háng Shìjùn's 杭世駿 *Róngchéng shīhuà* 榕城詩話, Lǐ Guāngpō remained at home and never took office, devoting himself to classical scholarship and producing a complete *Sānlǐ shùzhù* 三禮述註: [[KR1d0019]] *Zhōulǐ shùzhù* 周禮述註 in 24 juan, [[KR1d0040]] *Yílǐ shùzhù* 儀禮述註 in 17 juan, and [[KR1d0071]] *Lǐjì shùzhù* 禮記述註 in 28 juan. The *Sānlǐ shùzhù* set is one of the few comprehensive Qīng-period commentaries on all three Ritual Classics by a single author, and the family scholarly tradition continued in his nephew Lǐ Zhōnglún 李鍾倫 (cf. [[KR1d0020]]) and his elder brother's *Zhōuguān bǐjì* 周官筆記 in the *Róngcūn jí*.

The Sìkù *tíyào* of [[KR1d0019]] characterises Lǐ's method as a middle path between Hàn-Táng evidential scholarship (which Lǐ regards as too vast and complex for pedagogical use) and Sòng *Dàoxué* speculation (which Lǐ regards as too discursive and disconnected from the classical text). Lǐ trims the *zhùshū* and supplements with selected later material, with the explicit goal of pedagogical accessibility. CBDB id 77440.
