Wáng Dàokūn 王道焜 was a late-Míng Hángzhōu 杭州 scholar-bookseller; passed the *xiāng-shì* 鄉試 (provincial examination) in Tiānqǐ *xīn-yǒu* 天啓辛酉 (1621). Death year 1645 (Shùnzhì 2) recorded in CBDB id 124346, consistent with his apparent disappearance from the record at the Míng-Qīng transition; birth year not attested. Lù Yuán-fǔ 陸元輔 (cited in Zhū Yízūn's 朱彝尊 *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考) gives the basic biographical notice.

Together with his fellow-townsman Zhào Rúyuán 趙如源 ([[趙如源]]) he co-edited the composite *Zuǒ-zhuàn Dù-Lín hé zhù* 左傳杜林合注 ([[KR1e0092]]) in 50 *juǎn* — the late-Míng student edition of the *Zuǒzhuàn* combining Dù Yù's classical *jí jiě* with Lín Yáosǒu's 林堯叟 ([[林堯叟]]) Sòng paraphrase, with Lù Démíng's 陸德明 *Yīn-yì* 音義 incorporated. The work was printed in Hángzhōu and circulated widely, though commercial reprints in the late Míng and early Qīng dropped the editors' names. CBDB id 124346.
