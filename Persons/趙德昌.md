The personal name of the Northern-Sòng emperor **Zhào Héng** 趙恆 — born Zhào Déchāng 趙德昌 (968–1022; r. 997–1022) — better known by his temple-name **Sòng Zhēnzōng** 宋真宗. Third son of Sòng Tàizōng 太宗 (r. 976–997), succeeding to the throne on 8 May 997. Zhào Déchāng was the personal name he bore as a prince before his elevation to crown prince and renaming as Zhào Héng. He is celebrated in Daoist history for the spectacular *Tiānshū* 天書 ("Heavenly Writs") episode of 1008, when, claiming the descent of imperial revelation, he proclaimed the *Yùdì* 玉帝 (Jade Emperor) as a Sòng dynastic ancestor — the *Shèngzǔ* 聖祖 — and inaugurated a Daoist–imperial cult that produced the *fēng-shàn* 封禪 sacrifice on Tài-shān 泰山 (1008), the imperial Daoist *miào* 廟 at Bó-zhōu 亳州, and the great Daoist canon edition (*Dà Sòng tiāngōng bǎozàng* 大宋天宮寶藏, completed under Zhāng Jūnfáng 張君房). His Daoist-related compositions are preserved especially in the [[KR5a0327|*Sòng Zhēnzōng yù-zhì Yùjīng jí* 宋真宗御製玉京集]] (DZ 315), a six-juan collection of *yì* 意 ("Intentions") for memorials presented during Daoist rituals — *xiè* 謝 ("thanksgivings"), *jiāng* 將 ("statements"), *biǎo* 表 ("memorials"). His biography is in *Sòng-shǐ* 宋史 6–8.
