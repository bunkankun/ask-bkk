**Chéng Guó-líng** 程國齡 — variant catalog form of [[程國彭]] Chéng Guó-péng (*zì* Zhōng-líng 鍾齡, original *zì* Shān-líng 山齡, *hào* Xìng-hǎi yīn-jūn 杏海隱君, 1662–1735), the Hī-zhōu Xīn-ān physician of the *Kāng-xī* / early-*Yōng-zhèng* era. The Kanripo catalog meta records the same author under both names: [[KR3er048]] *Yī-xué xīn-wù* (under 程國彭) and [[KR3er113]] *Yī-xué xīn-wù zá-zhèng yào-yì* (under 程國齡). The discrepancy is a scribal alternation between *péng* 彭 and *líng* 齡 (the original name was Shān-líng, of which Zhōng-líng / Guó-líng are *zì*-variations, with Guó-péng a separate alternative form); the modern Chinese-medicine consensus treats these as a single author. For full biography see [[程國彭]].
