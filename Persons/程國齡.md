**Chéng Guólíng** 程國齡 — variant catalog form of [[程國彭]] Chéng Guópéng (*zì* Zhōnglíng 鍾齡, original *zì* Shānlíng 山齡, *hào* Xìnghǎi yīnjūn 杏海隱君, 1662–1735), the Hīzhōu Xīnān physician of the *Kāngxī* / early-*Yōngzhèng* era. The Kanripo catalog meta records the same author under both names: [[KR3er048]] *Yīxué xīnwù* (under 程國彭) and [[KR3er113]] *Yīxué xīnwù zázhèng yàoyì* (under 程國齡). The discrepancy is a scribal alternation between *péng* 彭 and *líng* 齡 (the original name was Shānlíng, of which Zhōnglíng / Guólíng are *zì*-variations, with Guópéng a separate alternative form); the modern Chinese-medicine consensus treats these as a single author. For full biography see [[程國彭]].
