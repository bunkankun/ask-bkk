Xǔ Róng 許容 (b. unknown, d. Qiánlóng 16 = 1751), native of Yú-chéng 虞城 (Guī-dé prefecture, modern Hénán). His Yōngzhèng career: Sù-zhōu 肅州 *zhōu* official; Gānsù governor (Yōngzhèng 7 = 1729 – Qiánlóng 1 = 1736), in which capacity he commissioned and substantively directed the foundational *Gānsù tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0051]]) from 1729 through to its completion in 1736. The catalog meta dates him "fl. 1640–1723" — this is incorrect: it conflates him with an earlier Míng homonym Xǔ Róng (CBDB 99290, 1127–1139 fl., or another). The relevant Yōngzhèng-era figure is CBDB 58655, with death year 1751 per the *Qīngdài rénwù shēng-zú niánbiǎo*; index_addr 8096 (Yú-chéng) confirmed; *floruit* runs from 1720s through 1750. Followed here. *Qīng shǐ liè-zhuàn* j. 78 has his biography.
