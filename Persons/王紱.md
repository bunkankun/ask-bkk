Wáng Fú 王紱 (1362–1416), *zì* **Mèng-duān** 孟端, *bié-hào* **Yǒu-shí shēng** 友石生 / **Jiǔ-lóng shān-rén** 九龍山人, native of **Wú-xī** 無錫 (Bǐ-líng / Cháng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). Summoned to the capital in Hóngwǔ; implicated in some affair and banished to garrison *Shuò-zhōu* 朔州 (Shānxī); recalled in early Yǒng-lè by recommendation for his calligraphy, put in attendance at the **Wén-yuān-gé** 文淵閣; eventually appointed *zhōng-shū shè-rén* 中書舍人; died in office. *Míng shǐ* j. 286 (*Wén-yuàn*). Principally important as one of the great early-Míng landscape and ink-bamboo painters, the principal early-Míng inheritor of the manner of his fellow-townsman **Ní Zàn** 倪瓚 (Yuán). Surviving works are [[KR4e0085]] *Wáng Shè-rén shī-jí* (= *Yǒu-shí shān-fáng gǎo*), edited by his son Wáng Mò 王默. CBDB id 34474 (1362–1416).
