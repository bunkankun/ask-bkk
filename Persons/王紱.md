Wáng Fú 王紱 (1362–1416), *zì* **Mèngduān** 孟端, *biéhào* **Yǒushí shēng** 友石生 / **Jiǔlóng shānrén** 九龍山人, native of **Wúxī** 無錫 (Bǐlíng / Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū). Summoned to the capital in Hóngwǔ; implicated in some affair and banished to garrison *Shuòzhōu* 朔州 (Shānxī); recalled in early Yǒnglè by recommendation for his calligraphy, put in attendance at the **Wényuāngé** 文淵閣; eventually appointed *zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人; died in office. *Míng shǐ* j. 286 (*Wényuàn*). Principally important as one of the great early-Míng landscape and ink-bamboo painters, the principal early-Míng inheritor of the manner of his fellow-townsman **Ní Zàn** 倪瓚 (Yuán). Surviving works are [[KR4e0085]] *Wáng Shèrén shījí* (= *Yǒushí shānfáng gǎo*), edited by his son Wáng Mò 王默. CBDB id 34474 (1362–1416).
