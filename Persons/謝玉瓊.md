**Xiè Yùqióng** 謝玉瓊, Qīng-period measles specialist, *fl.* mid-Qīng (*Qián-lóng* — *Jiā-qìng* era). Author of *Má-kē huó-rén quán-shū* 麻科活人全書 ([[KR3ej047]]), one of the principal Qīng-period specialty treatises on measles. The work systematically presents 72 measles symptom-patterns (the *qī-shí-èr zhèng* 七十二症 referred to by later Qīng commentators) with prescription. Together with the *Mài-fù qīng-bù* (Mài-fù qīng-bǔ 麻症集成 of Zhāng Lín 張霖) and the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* measles chapter, Xiè's *Má-kē huó-rén quán-shū* is one of the three principal measles references of the High Qīng. Lifedates and native place not securely recorded. No CBDB record.
