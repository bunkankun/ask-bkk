**Shionoya Tōin** 鹽谷世弘 (also Shionoya Sei-kō 鹽谷世弘; *hào* Tōin 宕陰; 1809–1867), late-Edo Japanese Confucian scholar, calligrapher, and minor official. Author of the autumn-1862 preface to [[尾臺榕堂]] Odai Yōdō's *Yī yú* 醫餘 ([[KR3eq051]]) recording Odai's biographical background — Echigo 北越 origins, original Kosugi 小杉 surname, study under [[尾臺淺岳]] Odai Sengaku and [[龜田鵬齋]] Kameda Bōsai.
