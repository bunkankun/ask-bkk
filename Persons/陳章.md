Chén Zhāng 陳章, late-Southern-Sòng disciple of [[易祓]] Yì Fú. His preface dated *Shàodìng wùzǐ* (1228) fourth month first day to Yì Fú's *Zhōuyì zǒng yì* 周易總義 ([[KR1a0048]]) records that Yì Fú spent "more than twenty years" on the work after his initial *jīngyán* 經筵 (imperial-classroom) lectures on the *Yì*, and preserves Yì Fú's signature cosmological-anthropological formulas about the *Yì* as controller of *yuánqì* 元氣 and the sage as its *quánhéng* 權衡. Also records the now-lost companion four-juan *Yì xué jǔyú* 易學舉隅.
