# 曹雲洲

Cáo Yúnzhōu 曹雲洲 (late Qīng), Wúmén (Sūzhōu) physician, established internist and *yángkē* 瘍科 (ulcer-surgery) specialist. Grandfather of [[曹元恒]] Cáo Yuánhéng (Cāngzhōu 滄洲, 1849–1931); together with his son [[曹承洲]] Cáo Chéngzhōu, founder of the third-generation Cáo medical lineage of late-Qīng Sūzhōu (see [[KR3ep101]] *Cáo Cāngzhōu yīàn*).
