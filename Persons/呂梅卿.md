**Lǚ Méiqīng** 呂梅卿 of Huìjī 會稽, late-Qīng book-collector whose retained manuscript of the [[KR3eq006]] *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* came into the possession of [[繆萼聊]] Miù Èliáo and made the 1894 (Guāngxù 20) recovery cut possible.
