Liú Zhífāng 劉直方, *hào* Zhúxī 竺溪, a Yuán-period scholar of Nánchāng 南昌; a fellow student of [[熊良輔]] Xióng Liángfǔ who, with other classmates, underwrote the printing costs of the *Zhōuyì běnyì jíchéng* ([[KR1a0079]]) in 1322. Lifedates not securely recorded.
