Mid-Northern-Sòng councillor and *Yánguān* statesman, *zì* Zhìguī 稚圭, posthumous *Zhōngxiàn* 忠獻, of *Xiāngzhōu Ānyáng* 相州安陽 (Héběi). *Jìnshì* of *Tiānshèng* 5 / 1027 (joint third). Held *Yánguān* offices, prefect of multiple regional capitals, *Shǎnxī jīnglüèshǐ* during the early SòngXīXià war (with Fàn Zhòngyān as his deputy at the *Hǎoshuǐchuān* defeat of 1041); *Cānzhī zhèngshì* under Rénzōng in *Qìnglì* 5 / 1045 and again 1060; *Tóng zhōngshū ménxià píngzhāngshì* (*Zǎixiàng*) under Yīngzōng (1063–1067) — the principal political figure of the *Púyì* 濮議 succession-ritual debate of 1065–1066. Under Shénzōng sent out as *Pàn Xiāngzhōu*; the principal *Yánguān* opponent of Wáng Ānshí's *Xīníng* reforms (his *Lùn Qīngmiáo* memorial of 1069 survives in his collection). Died *Xīníng* 8 / 1075 age 68. Together with Fù Bì 富弼 and Wén Yànbó 文彦博 the canonical "*Sān shàngxiàng*" 三上相 of the *Qìnglì / Jiāyòu* generation. Surviving collection: *Ānyáng jí* [[KR4d0024]] (50 *juǎn*, original size, with *Jiāzhuàn* / *Biélù* / *Yíshì* appendices). Composed *shéndào bēi* for Zhāng Yǒng [[張詠]]. CBDB id 630.
