Mid-Northern-Sòng councillor and *Yán-guān* statesman, *zì* Zhì-guī 稚圭, posthumous *Zhōng-xiàn* 忠獻, of *Xiāng-zhōu Ān-yáng* 相州安陽 (Hé-běi). *Jìn-shì* of *Tiān-shèng* 5 / 1027 (joint third). Held *Yán-guān* offices, prefect of multiple regional capitals, *Shǎn-xī jīng-lüè-shǐ* during the early Sòng-Xī-Xià war (with Fàn Zhòng-yān as his deputy at the *Hǎo-shuǐ-chuān* defeat of 1041); *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* under Rén-zōng in *Qìng-lì* 5 / 1045 and again 1060; *Tóng zhōng-shū mén-xià píng-zhāng-shì* (*Zǎi-xiàng*) under Yīng-zōng (1063–1067) — the principal political figure of the *Pú-yì* 濮議 succession-ritual debate of 1065–1066. Under Shén-zōng sent out as *Pàn Xiāng-zhōu*; the principal *Yán-guān* opponent of Wáng Ān-shí's *Xī-níng* reforms (his *Lùn Qīng-miáo* memorial of 1069 survives in his collection). Died *Xī-níng* 8 / 1075 age 68. Together with Fù Bì 富弼 and Wén Yàn-bó 文彦博 the canonical "*Sān shàng-xiàng*" 三上相 of the *Qìng-lì / Jiā-yòu* generation. Surviving collection: *Ān-yáng jí* [[KR4d0024]] (50 *juǎn*, original size, with *Jiā-zhuàn* / *Bié-lù* / *Yí-shì* appendices). Composed *shén-dào bēi* for Zhāng Yǒng [[張詠]]. CBDB id 630.
