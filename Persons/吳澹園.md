**Wú Dànyuán** 吳澹園 (*fl.* late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng physician and in-law of [[沈文彬]]. Compiled the *Yàonéng* 藥能 — a now-rare pharmacological manual that Shěn merged with [[高鼓峰]]'s *Yàolùn* notes to produce the late-Qīng *Yàolùn* of 1901 ([[KR3ec062]]). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
