# 曉柔

Xiǎoróu 曉柔, late-Míng / early-Qīng monk, author of the *Xīnkè Fǎhuá yǎnyì xù* 新刻法華演義序, the principal documentary source for the biographical obscurity of [[一松]] Yīsōng (dàshī). Notes that "the Master is not known of what country he was, nor is his era clearly understood." Referenced in connection with the *Fǎhuá jīng yǎnyì* ([[KR6d0091]]) and the related *Yǎnyì kē* ([[KR6d0090]]).
