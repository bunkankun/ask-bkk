Cāng Jié 蒼頡 (also 倉頡), legendary scribe of the Yellow Emperor 黃帝 and conventional inventor of Chinese characters. Falsely credited as the original compiler of [[KR1a0163]] *Qián Kūn záo dù* — an attribution the Qiánlóng emperor and *Sìkù* editors firmly reject as a Sòng-period fabrication.
