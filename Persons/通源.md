Modern Chinese Buddhist scholar/monk who edited the *Èr-yào jīn-gāng hé-bì* 二曜金剛合璧 [[KR6v0089]] — a Qīng-period popular Buddhist *jī-shū* 乩書 (spirit-writing) compendium combining the *Sun*- and *Moon*-cult sūtras with the *Vajracchedikā jīng-zuǎn* 金剛經纂 — for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 8.
