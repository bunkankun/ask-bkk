彌堅堂主人 (*Mí Jiān Táng Zhǔrén*, "Master of the Míjiān Hall") is the studio-style pen name used by the author of the Qīng romance novel [[KR4k0291]] *Zhōng Xū Mèng* 終須夢. No other biographical details are known. The pseudonym is formed on the common model of a hall or studio name followed by 主人 ("master/proprietor"), a convention widespread among Qīng popular fiction authors who wished to preserve anonymity. The internal evidence of the novel — the Fujian setting, detailed references to the Zhangzhou 漳州 region, and familiarity with local religious customs — suggests a possible Fujian connection, but this cannot be confirmed. No CBDB entry has been located for this pseudonym.
