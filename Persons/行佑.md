# Xíngyòu 行佑

Southern-Sòng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[普度]] Xūzhōu Pǔdù (1199–1280); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0340]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
