# Yīniàn 一念

Late-Míng Pure Land monk, *hào* **Xī-zhāi** 西齋 ("Western Studio"). Lifedates not preserved with precision; he was active in the **Wàn-lì** 萬曆 reign (1573–1620) and was associated with the late-Míng Pure Land revival centred on Yúnqī Zhūhóng [[袾宏]] in Hángzhōu. His major surviving work is the **《西方直指》** *Xī-fāng zhí-zhǐ* [[KR6p0066]] (X1163, 3 *juǎn*) — a comprehensive Pure Land doctrinal-practical handbook in the *zhí-zhǐ* 直指 ("direct pointing") genre, oriented toward lay-Buddhist readers of the late-Míng literati class. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
