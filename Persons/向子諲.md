Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional official and *cí*-master (1086–1152), *zì* Bógōng 伯恭, *hào* Xiānglín jūshì 薌林居士, of Línjiāng 臨江 (Jiāngxī). Maternal-great-nephew of Empress Qīnshèng Xiànsù 欽聖憲肅 (the wife of Sòng Shénzōng); entered office on imperial grace in *Yuánfú* (early 1100s). Held a succession of magistracies and prefectures during *Zhènghé* and *Xuānhé*; led the famous defence of Tánzhōu 潭州 (modern Chángshā) against the Jīn invasion of *Jiànyán* 4 / 1130. Restored under *Shàoxīng* to *Huīyóugé zhí xuéshì* and Prefect of Píngjiāngfǔ; refused to serve under Qín Guì 秦檜 and retired in *Shàoxīng* 12 / 1142 to his Qīngjiāng 清江 estate at Wǔliǔfāng 五柳坊, which he named *Xiānglín* — whence his *hào* *Xiānglín jūshì*. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* 377. His sole literary work is the *Jiǔbiān cí* [[KR4j0025]] in two *juǎn*, explicitly divided into the *Jiāngběi jiù cí* (pre-1127 lyrics) and *Jiāngnán xīn cí* (post-1127 lyrics) — one of the most articulate personal-collection responses to the Sòng-Jīn rupture. Hú Yín 胡寅's preface, written for Xiàng's collection at Wǔyí, is a foundational document of Sòng *cí*-criticism (formulating the *Huājiān* → Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] → Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] genealogy of the *háofàng* line). Modern critical edition: Wáng Zhàopéng 王兆鵬, *Xiàng Zǐyīn niánpǔ jiàozhèng* (Sìchuān University, 1990s). His *cí* corpus in the *Quán Sòng cí* runs to around 174 pieces.
