Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional official and *cí*-master (1086–1152), *zì* Bó-gōng 伯恭, *hào* Xiāng-lín jūshì 薌林居士, of Lín-jiāng 臨江 (Jiāng-xī). Maternal-great-nephew of Empress Qīn-shèng Xiàn-sù 欽聖憲肅 (the wife of Sòng Shén-zōng); entered office on imperial grace in *Yuán-fú* (early 1100s). Held a succession of magistracies and prefectures during *Zhèng-hé* and *Xuān-hé*; led the famous defence of Tán-zhōu 潭州 (modern Cháng-shā) against the Jīn invasion of *Jiàn-yán* 4 / 1130. Restored under *Shào-xīng* to *Huī-yóu-gé zhí xué-shì* and Prefect of Píng-jiāng-fǔ; refused to serve under Qín Guì 秦檜 and retired in *Shào-xīng* 12 / 1142 to his Qīng-jiāng 清江 estate at Wǔ-liǔ-fāng 五柳坊, which he named *Xiāng-lín* — whence his *hào* *Xiāng-lín jūshì*. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* 377. His sole literary work is the *Jiǔ-biān cí* [[KR4j0025]] in two *juǎn*, explicitly divided into the *Jiāng-běi jiù cí* (pre-1127 lyrics) and *Jiāng-nán xīn cí* (post-1127 lyrics) — one of the most articulate personal-collection responses to the Sòng-Jīn rupture. Hú Yín 胡寅's preface, written for Xiàng's collection at Wǔ-yí, is a foundational document of Sòng *cí*-criticism (formulating the *Huā-jiān* → Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] → Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] genealogy of the *háo-fàng* line). Modern critical edition: Wáng Zhào-péng 王兆鵬, *Xiàng Zǐ-yīn nián-pǔ jiào-zhèng* (Sì-chuān University, 1990s). His *cí* corpus in the *Quán Sòng cí* runs to around 174 pieces.
