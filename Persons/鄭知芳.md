Zhèng Zhīfāng 鄭知芳, Kāngxī-period resident of Zǎoqiáng 棗強 (Héběi) whose household hosted [[李塨]] Lǐ Gōng (1659–1733) during the winter 1712 revision of his *Zhōuyì zhuàn zhù* ([[KR1a0140]]). No further biographical details preserved.
