# 雪竇重顯

Northern Sòng Yúnmén-school Chán master, abbot of Xuědòu-shān 雪竇山 (Míngzhōu 明州, modern Zhèjiāng) for over thirty years. Native of Suìzhōu 遂州 (modern Sìchuān). Famous as the author of the verse-cycle *Sòng-gǔ bǎi-zé* 頌古百則 — the hundred-koan verse collection later incorporated into the *Bìyán-lù* 碧巖錄 (the *Blue Cliff Record*) by Yuánwù Kèqín 圜悟克勤. The Xuědòu lineage of Yúnmén descendants includes [[大隱]] Dàyǐn (compiler of the *Dà bōrě jīng guānfǎ*, [[KR6c0227]]) as a fourth-generation descendant via Běnjué Shǒuyī 本覺守一.
