Qiě-zhuō Jìngnè 且拙淨訥 (also called Yúnzōng Jìngnè 雲淙淨訥, 1610–1673), late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞宗 Chán master. *Hào* Qiě-zhuō 且拙 ("Still-Clumsy") or Yúnzōng 雲淙. Native of Héngzhōu Ānrén 衡州安仁 (modern Húnán), lay surname Wáng 王.

Orphaned young; inspired to enter monastic life after seeing a Buddha-image in a temple. Ordained at age 26 at Jīngzǐ 荊紫; subsequently studied under Ruìbái Míngxuě 瑞白明雪 (1584–1641, DILA A007133) at the Biànshān 弁山 Kǒngtóng, attaining dharma-transmission in the Cáodòng lineage. Returned to Húnán; first established the Yìshān 義山 (later Dàyì-shān 大義山) monastery at Yí-yáng 宜陽, then succeeded Míngxuě at Biànshān, later held the Xiǎnshèng 顯聖 monastery for winter retreats. Died on Kāngxī 12.4.23 (7 June 1673), aged 64, *sēnglà* 54. Principal dharma-heir Duānzhōu Yuè-shān Dòng-chū-dù chánshī 端州月山洞初度禪師.

Jìngnè's principal surviving written work is the *Bǎo jìng sānmèi yuán zōng biàn miù shuō* 寶鏡三昧原宗辨謬說 ([[KR6q0130]], X63 n1238), a Cáodòng-internal polemical-doctrinal defense of the traditional reading of the *Bǎo jìng sānmèi gē*. The text circulates alongside [[KR6q0129]] Xíngcè's *Bǎo jìng sānmèi běn yì* as the two principal late-imperial commentarial works on the same canonical Cáodòng verse.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0130]] *Bǎo jìng sānmèi yuán zōng biàn miù shuō* only.
