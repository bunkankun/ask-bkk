Lǐ Kuāngyì 李匡乂 (late Táng, *fl.* *c.* 860–896 per CBDB; *zì* Jì Wēng 濟翁), of the *Lǐ* 李 imperial clan. The variant transmissions of his name (*Kuāngyì* 匡乂 / *Kuāngwén* 匡文 / *Kuāngyì* 匡義 / *Zhèngwén* 正文 / 李乂) all derive from the Sòng *Tàizǔ* 太祖 taboo on the *Kuāng* 匡 character: Sòng prints either substituted his *zì* (*Jì Wēng*), or replaced 匡 with 正, or removed strokes from the personal name (匡乂 → 乂, hence "Lǐ Yì" 李乂). The standard Southern-Sòng *Dúshū zhì* 讀書志 reading is *Kuāngyì* 匡乂; the *Sìkù* editors follow it. The same person is recorded in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* under *Lǐ Kuāngwén* with the office *Zōngzhèng shàoqīng* 宗正少卿 (Vice Director of the Imperial Clan Court) under Zhāozōng 昭宗 (r. 888–904), and as the author of a *LiǎngHàn zhì Táng niánjì* 兩漢至唐年紀 in 1 juan. The CBDB id 92898 is registered under the *Kuāngwén* 匡文 form. (Two further CBDB hits for *Lǐ Kuāngyì* 李匡義 — id 33497 [Sòng-era person from Rénmíng dàcídiǎn] and id 163287 [a YuánPíng new-epitaph entry] — are unrelated homonyms.)

Self-statements within [[KR3j0026]] *Zīxiá jí* 資暇集 identify Lǐ as a grand-nephew (*zàicóngshūwēng* 再從叔翁) of Lǐ Miǎn 李勉 (717–788, the great late-Xuán-zōng-and-Dài-zōng-era statesman of the Lǐ-clan branch descended from *Zhèngwáng* Yuányì 鄭王元懿) and as a clansman of Lǐ Hàn 李瀚 (the *Méngqiú* 蒙求 author). His one statement of office — *shǒu Nánzhāng* 守南漳 — names a service post (perhaps *cìshǐ* of Nánzhāngxiàn in Xiāngyáng), not his terminal office. The *Zōngzhèng shàoqīng* under Zhāozōng noted in the *Yìwén zhì* must be later. CBDB id 92898.

Author of [[KR3j0026]] *Zīxiá jí* 資暇集 (*Sìkù* *Zájiā · Zákǎo zhī shǔ*), a 3-juan late-Táng *kǎozhèng* miscellany — one of the substantive pre-Sòng monuments of the *kǎozhèng* tradition, regularly cited and rebutted by Northern-Sòng *biji*-writers (Huáng Bósī, Huáng Cháoyīng, Hú Zǎi, Wáng Mào).
