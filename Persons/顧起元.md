Gù Qǐ-yuán 顧起元 (1565–1628), *zì* Tài-chū 太初, *hào* Dùn-yuán jū-shì 遯園居士, posthumous title *Wén-zhuāng* 文莊, native of Jiāng-níng 江寧 (Nán-jīng). Late-Míng *Wàn-lì wù-xū* (1598) *jìnshì*. He served at the *Hàn-lín yuàn* and rose through the *Lì-bù* to *Lì-bù yòu shì-láng* (Vice-Minister of Personnel). He withdrew from court in the late *Wàn-lì* / early *Tiān-qǐ* period, retiring to his *Dùn-yuán* (Garden of Withdrawal) at Nán-jīng. He was one of the most distinguished late-Míng *kǎo-jù* scholars and a leading figure of the Nán-jīng literary world. His principal works are: the *Kè-zuò zhuì-yǔ* 客座贅語 (a *bǐ-jì* on late-Míng Nán-jīng intellectual and social life, including the first Chinese discussion of European Jesuit science at Nán-jīng); the *Jīn-líng gǔ-jīn tú-kǎo* 金陵古今圖考 (historical-geographical study of Nán-jīng); the *Shuō-lüè* 說畧 ([[KR3k0048]]) in 30 *juan*; and the literary collection *Lǎn-zhēn cǎo-táng jí* 懶真草堂集.
