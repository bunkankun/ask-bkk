Gù Qǐyuán 顧起元 (1565–1628), *zì* Tàichū 太初, *hào* Dùnyuán jūshì 遯園居士, posthumous title *Wénzhuāng* 文莊, native of Jiāngníng 江寧 (Nánjīng). Late-Míng *Wànlì wùxū* (1598) *jìnshì*. He served at the *Hànlín yuàn* and rose through the *Lìbù* to *Lìbù yòu shìláng* (Vice-Minister of Personnel). He withdrew from court in the late *Wànlì* / early *Tiānqǐ* period, retiring to his *Dùnyuán* (Garden of Withdrawal) at Nánjīng. He was one of the most distinguished late-Míng *kǎojù* scholars and a leading figure of the Nánjīng literary world. His principal works are: the *Kèzuò zhuìyǔ* 客座贅語 (a *bǐjì* on late-Míng Nánjīng intellectual and social life, including the first Chinese discussion of European Jesuit science at Nánjīng); the *Jīnlíng gǔjīn túkǎo* 金陵古今圖考 (historical-geographical study of Nánjīng); the *Shuōlüè* 說畧 ([[KR3k0048]]) in 30 *juan*; and the literary collection *Lǎnzhēn cǎotáng jí* 懶真草堂集.
