Lì Tíngyí 勵廷儀 (1669–1732), *zì* Lìngshì 令式, *hào* Bànyán 半巖, posthumous title Wéngōng 文恭. Native of Jìng-hǎi 靜海 (Tiānjīn). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 39 (1700); rose through the *Hàn-lín* to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* 吏部尚書 under Yōngzhèng. His father Lì Dùyuè 勵杜訥 had been a Kāngxī confidential secretary and *Hàn-lín shì-dú xué-shì* 翰林院侍讀學士; the family is one of the small group of Hàn-Bànnermen-style Hàn-lín dynasties of the early Qīng. CBDB id 59384.

Listed in [[KR2j0002]] *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* as a *jiào kān guān* 校勘官 (collation officer) on the strength of his then position as *rì-jiǎng-guān qǐjū-zhù Hàn-lín-yuàn shì-jiǎng xué-shì* 日講官起居注翰林院侍講學士. He served on multiple late-Kāngxī imperial compilations including the *Pèi-wén yùn-fǔ* 佩文韻府 and the *Yuán jiàn lèi-hán* 淵鑑類函. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 289.
