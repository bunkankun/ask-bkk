Late-Míng *Lǐbù* official and *Sìkù*-listed scholar; native of Pútián 莆田 (Fújiàn). *Zì* Dàoxiàn 道憲; *hào* Pénglái 蓬萊. Active late Wànlì through Chóngzhēn. Compiled the *Zhōulǐ gǔběn dìngzhù* 周禮古本訂注 (in *Sìkù*, KR1) and the *Míng shì jì huìbiān* 明謚紀彙編 ([[KR2m0031]]), among others. Precise life-dates not transmitted; *fl.* c. 1600–1640.
