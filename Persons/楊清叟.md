**Yáng Qīngsǒu** 楊清叟 (*fl.* mid-Yuán, 元), Yuán-period physician of Héchuān 禾川 / Jí'ān 吉安 (modern Jiāngxī). The original author of the surgical treatise that descended through the Jìngmíng 淨明 Daoist lineage as [[KR3ek004]] *Xiānchuán wàikē jíyàn fāng* and [[KR3ek007]] *Mìchuán wàikē fāng*, compiled and edited by [[趙宜真]] Zhào Yízhēn (d. 1382). The manuscript passed through Wú Níngjí 吳寧極 → Wú Yǒuběn 吳有本 → Lǐ Shànguān 李善觀 → Zhào Yízhēn. Yáng's original work introduced the foundational *yīnyáng* differential of *yōng* 癰 versus *jū* 疽 together with the three Daoist-medical signature plasters *huíyáng yùlóng gāo* 回陽玉龍膏, *hóngbǎo dān* 洪寶丹, and *chōnghé xiān gāo* 沖和仙膏 that became the standard external preparations of Míng-Qīng surgery. No further biographical record. Not in CBDB.
