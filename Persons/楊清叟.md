**Yáng Qīngsǒu** 楊清叟 (*fl.* mid-Yuán, 元), Yuán-period physician of Hé-chuān 禾川 / Jí'ān 吉安 (modern Jiāngxī). The original author of the surgical treatise that descended through the Jìng-míng 淨明 Daoist lineage as [[KR3ek004]] *Xiān-chuán wài-kē jí-yàn fāng* and [[KR3ek007]] *Mì-chuán wài-kē fāng*, compiled and edited by [[趙宜真]] Zhào Yízhēn (d. 1382). The manuscript passed through Wú Níng-jí 吳寧極 → Wú Yǒu-běn 吳有本 → Lǐ Shàn-guān 李善觀 → Zhào Yízhēn. Yáng's original work introduced the foundational *yīn-yáng* differential of *yōng* 癰 versus *jū* 疽 together with the three Daoist-medical signature plasters *huí-yáng yù-lóng gāo* 回陽玉龍膏, *hóng-bǎo dān* 洪寶丹, and *chōng-hé xiān gāo* 沖和仙膏 that became the standard external preparations of Míng-Qīng surgery. No further biographical record. Not in CBDB.
