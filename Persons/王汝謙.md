**Wáng Rǔqiān** 王汝謙 (*hào* Jìngtáng 鏡堂, *fl.* mid – late 19th c., Jīngdé 旌德 / Ānhuī) — late-Qīng physician; supplementer-annotator and 1891 (*Guāngxù* 17) editor of the *Yīzōng jǐrèn biān* 醫宗己任編 ([[KR3er067]]). His preface narrates the loss of his early childhood copy in the Tàipíng 太平 (Taiping) conflict and the subsequent 30-year reliance on Gāo Gǔfēng's methods (recalled from memory) in his own clinical practice, before a recovered manuscript surfaced in a Jīngdé bookshop. He supplied the *méipī* 眉批 head-of-page annotations representing his disciple-circle's clinical discussions.

This Wáng Rǔqiān is distinct from the *Jiāqìng*-era Wáng Rǔqiān (1777–1855, CBDB 71621); not in CBDB under his own record.
