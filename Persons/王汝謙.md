**Wáng Rǔ-qiān** 王汝謙 (*hào* Jìng-táng 鏡堂, *fl.* mid – late 19th c., Jīng-dé 旌德 / Ān-huī) — late-Qīng physician; supplementer-annotator and 1891 (*Guāng-xù* 17) editor of the *Yī-zōng jǐ-rèn biān* 醫宗己任編 ([[KR3er067]]). His preface narrates the loss of his early childhood copy in the Tài-píng 太平 (Taiping) conflict and the subsequent 30-year reliance on Gāo Gǔ-fēng's methods (recalled from memory) in his own clinical practice, before a recovered manuscript surfaced in a Jīng-dé bookshop. He supplied the *méi-pī* 眉批 head-of-page annotations representing his disciple-circle's clinical discussions.

This Wáng Rǔ-qiān is distinct from the *Jiā-qìng*-era Wáng Rǔ-qiān (1777–1855, CBDB 71621); not in CBDB under his own record.
