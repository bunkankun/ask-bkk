# Wúniàn jūshì 無念居士

Mid-Míng lay devotee (jūshì), otherwise unidentified, who compiled the *Bōrě xīnjīng jiěyì jiéyào* ([[KR6c0154]]) in Jiājìng 12 (1533) at a residence "beneath Zǐwēihuā" 紫微華下. The sobriquet *Wúniàn* 無念 ("no-thought") echoes a central Chan term associated with the Sixth Patriarch [[慧能]] Huìnéng, suggesting Chan-affiliated leanings. No other writings attributed.
