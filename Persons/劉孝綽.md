Liú Xiàochuò 劉孝綽 (481–539 CE; original name Rǎn 冉) was a Liáng 梁 court poet from Pénchéng 彭城 and the eldest of a large clan of literary siblings. He held a precocious poetic reputation that his erratic official career undermined. His verse belongs to the Palace Style (*gōngtǐ* 宮體) tradition. Ranked upper grade in Zhōng Róng's 鍾嶸 *Shīpǐn*. Biography in *Liángshū* juǎn 33 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 40. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0059]].
