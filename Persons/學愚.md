Xué-yú 學愚. Personal attendant of **Tiān-yī Dà-yuè** [[大悅]] (1631–1697). Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0582]] 《天一悅禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B470.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the modern Republican-era / contemporary scholar-monk **Xué-yú** 學愚 (DILA A035263), graduate of Sri Lanka's University of Kelaniya, professor at Chinese University of Hong Kong, author of *Buddhism, War, and Nationalism*.
