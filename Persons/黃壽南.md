**Huáng Shòu-nán** 黃壽南 (*fl.* mid 19th c., late Qīng), Sū-zhōu physician-disciple of [[張大爔]] (Zhāng Dà-xī, *hào* Ài-lú). In early summer 1852 (*Rén-zǐ chū-xià*) he made the manuscript copy of his teacher's *Línzhèng jīngyàn fāng* 臨證經驗方 ([[KR3ed134]]) — signing himself *hòu-xué* 後學 (later-student) — that is the principal extant witness to the work. No further biographical record.
