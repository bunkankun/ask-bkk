**Lù Shījiàn** 陸師鑑 (style **Shèngcāng** 聖蒼, *fl.* 1772), Sūzhōu literatus and medical-book editor. Author of the 1772 (*Qiánlóng rénchén* 乾隆壬辰) preface to [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng's *(Dòuzhěn) Shēngmín qièyào* （痘疹）生民切要 ([[KR3ej064]]), explaining that the manuscript had circulated privately in Sūzhōu for over a century after Yù's death (1664), with each subsequent possessor treasuring it as a *zhěnzhōng mì* (pillow-secret). Lù purchased the manuscript for collation and re-publication, arguing that the hoarding behavior contradicted Yù's original *jìshì* (world-saving) intent. CBDB has no entry.
