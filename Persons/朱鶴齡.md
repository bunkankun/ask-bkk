Late-Míng / early-Qīng 明清 classical scholar and poet, native of Wújiāng 吳江 in Sūzhōu 蘇州 prefecture (modern Jiāngsū). Zì Chángrú 長孺; hào Yúān 愚菴 (= "the Hut of the Foolish"). Lifedates 1606–1683 are firm.

Late-Míng *zhū shēng* 諸生 (registered student); never passed the Míng or Qīng examinations and never held office. Lived as a private scholar through the Míng-Qīng transition; did not serve the new dynasty. Bio in *Qīngshǐ liè zhuàn* 清史列傳 *rúlín zhuàn* 儒林傳; collected works *Yúān xiǎo jí* 愚菴小集 (in the *Sìkù*).

A prolific commentator on canonical and post-canonical texts. His two most-cited works in modern scholarship are: (1) *Dù Gōngbù shī jí jí jiě* 杜工部詩集集解 ("Collected Annotations on Dù Fǔ's Poetry") in 20 *juǎn*, the standard pre-modern Dù Fǔ commentary that combined the principal Sòng-Yuán-Míng readings into a comprehensive synthesis; and (2) *Lǐ Yìshān shī jí jiān zhù* 李義山詩集箋註 ("Annotations on Lǐ Shāngyǐn's Poetry"), the parallel synthetic Lǐ Shāngyǐn commentary. He also wrote substantially on the *Shī*, the *Yì*, and the *Shū*: the *Shàngshū bì zhuàn* 尚書埤傳 ([[KR1b0051]]) in 17 *juǎn* (15 main + *Kǎo yì* 1 + *Yì piān wěi shū jí Shū shuō yú* 1) is his principal canonical work, distinguished by its synthetic-arbitrative method that — per the *Sìkù tíyào* — "weighs Hàn-learning and Sòng-learning against each other" (*zhēn zhuó yú Hàn xué Sòng xué zhī jiān* 斟酌于漢學宋學之間).

His firmest scholarly conviction was the authenticity of the *Gǔwén Shàngshū Kǒng zhuàn*, in tension with the rising anti-*Gǔwén* tide of his contemporaries (Yán Ruòqú, [[KR1b0048]]; Wáng Fūzhī, [[KR1b0047]]). The *Sìkù* compilers note this commitment as "*piān pì*" 偏僻 (biased and partial) — most pointedly, his classification of the *Tāng gào* preserved in *Shǐjì* (which Sīmǎ Qiān had received directly from Kǒng Ān'guó) as forged, in order to defend the very different *Tāng gào* of the *Méi Zé* recension.
