# Chāowù 超悟

Early-Qīng Chán practitioner; dharma-heir (or senior disciple) of [[徐昌治|Xú Chāngzhì]] 徐昌治, *hào* Wúyī dàorén 無依道人. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Compiler (*lù* 錄) of the *Wúyī dàorén lù* 無依道人錄 [[KR6q0192]] — Xu's late-life lay Chán *yǔlù* — gathering his master's teaching-responses into a two-juan volume published in 1667 with a preface by Sēng-jiàn 僧鑑 of Nán-cūn. Beyond this compilational role, no independent material is preserved.
