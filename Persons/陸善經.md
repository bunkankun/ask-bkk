Lù Shàn-jīng 陸善經 (*fl.* mid-8th c., d. ca. 739 by Tackett's reconstruction in CBDB id 191869, drawing on *Quán Táng wén* 678 and the *Bái Jū-yì jí* 41) was a Táng exegete attached to the *Jíxián diàn* 集賢殿 court compilation bureau under Xuán-zōng 玄宗 (*r.* 712–756). He is named in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* 新唐書·藝文志 as a contributor to the imperially-sponsored continuation of several earlier compilations and is the author or co-author of expansions to Liáng Yuán-dì's *Gǔjīn tóngxìngmíng lù* ([[KR3k0001]]) and of independent Classics commentaries cited in Lù Dé-míng's 陸德明 *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 colophons. His native place and lineage are not recorded; what survives of his work is preserved only as embedded fragments in *lèishū* and in the *Sìkù* recensions of works he revised.
