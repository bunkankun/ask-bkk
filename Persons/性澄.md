# Xìngchéng 性澄 / Zhàn-táng Xìngchéng 湛堂性澄 (1265–1342)

A leading Yuán-period Tiāntái master, *hào* **Zhàn-táng** 湛堂 ("Hall of Tranquillity") and **Yuèxī** 越溪 ("Yuè-stream") — the latter from the Yuè 越 (Shàoxīng) region in Zhèjiāng. He is one of the principal restorers of Tiāntái doctrine in the Yuán dynasty and the immediate teacher of *Wú-xìn Tiānrú Wéi-zé* 無心天如惟則, the Yuán Pure Land master whose *Jìngtǔ huò-wèn* 淨土或問 [[KR6p0053]] is one of the standard Yuán Pure Land texts. Native of Yuè 越 (Shàoxīng); ordained at Tiāntái-shān 天台山 (the school's home mountain) and held the abbacy of *Yán-qìng-yuàn* 延慶院 in Sì-míng 四明 (Níngbō) — the same temple where [[知禮]] Sìmíng Zhīlǐ had taught — and later of *Hù-shèng-sì* 護聖寺 in Hangzhou. Lifedates 1265–1342, age 78. Alternative writings of his style include 越溪澄 (Yuèxī Chéng) used in [[KR6p0016]] *Ēmítuó jīng jù-jiě* 阿彌陀經句解.

His major writings include the *Tiāntái sì-jiào yí jí-zhù* 天台四教儀集註 (a standard Yuán-period commentary on Dì-guān 諦觀's late-Tang Tiāntái compendium, widely used in late-imperial Tiāntái pedagogy and re-cut as a teaching text in early-Edo Japan), the *Ēmítuó jīng jù-jiě* 阿彌陀經句解 [[KR6p0016]] (his sentence-by-sentence commentary on the smaller Pure Land sūtra), and a number of records and sermons preserved in the *Bùjì sēng-shǐ luè* 補續僧史略 and *Shì shì jī-gǔ lüè* 釋氏稽古略.

His Pure Land doctrine combines the Tiāntái *yīxīn-sānguān* 一心三觀 framework of Sìmíng Zhīlǐ with the late-Sòng / Yuán emphasis on lay-Buddhist devotional practice; he is one of the principal figures in the transmission of Tiāntái Pure Land into the Yuán-Míng period.

Sources: *Bùjì sēng-shǐ luè* 補續僧史略 vol. 4; *Tiāntái shān fāng wài zhì* 天台山方外志; *Shì shì jī-gǔ lüè*. The DILA Authority id is A001282.
