# Juélái xiānshēng 覺來先生

Mid-Qīng paediatrician (sobriquet only); teacher of [[王錫鑫]] Wáng Xīxīn, whose paediatric diagnostic method (five-step observation: constitution → temperature → mouth → finger-vein → bowels) is recorded in Wáng's autobiographical self-preface to *Yòukē qièyào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]], 1847). Personal name and lifedates unrecorded.
