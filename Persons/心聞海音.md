# Xīnwén Hǎiyīn 心聞海音

Japanese (Edo-period) monk; supplied the second 1715 Japanese postface to [[KR6q0072]] *Tiāntóng Rújìng chánshī xù yǔlù*, signed at Takagamine Hōjurin 鷹峯寶樹林 north of Kyoto.
