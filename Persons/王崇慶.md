Wáng Chóng-qìng 王崇慶 (1484–1565; CBDB id 35038), *zì* Dé-zhēng 德徵, *hào* Duān-xī 端溪, was a mid-Míng *Lǐ-xué* official from Kāi-zhōu 開州 (modern Hé-nán). *Jìnshì* of *Zhèng-dé* 3 (1508); rose through provincial and central posts to *Lǐ-bù* and *Lì-bù shàng-shū* in Nán-jīng, retiring under *Jiā-jìng*. His scholarship is moderate-Chéng-Zhū, with strong interest in the Sòng *Lǐ-xué* sources. He produced commentaries on the *Shàng shū*, the *Zhōu yì*, and *Mèng zǐ*, as well as the *Yuán-chéng yǔ lù jiě* 元城語錄解 ([[KR3j0102]]) — his explanatory glosses on [[馬永卿]]'s record of [[劉安世]]'s sayings — and the *Hǎi qiáo jí* 海樵集 (collected works). Biography in *Míng shǐ* j. 287.
