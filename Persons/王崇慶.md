Wáng Chóngqìng 王崇慶 (1484–1565; CBDB id 35038), *zì* Dézhēng 德徵, *hào* Duānxī 端溪, was a mid-Míng *Lǐxué* official from Kāizhōu 開州 (modern Hénán). *Jìnshì* of *Zhèngdé* 3 (1508); rose through provincial and central posts to *Lǐbù* and *Lìbù shàngshū* in Nánjīng, retiring under *Jiājìng*. His scholarship is moderate-Chéng-Zhū, with strong interest in the Sòng *Lǐxué* sources. He produced commentaries on the *Shàng shū*, the *Zhōu yì*, and *Mèng zǐ*, as well as the *Yuánchéng yǔ lù jiě* 元城語錄解 ([[KR3j0102]]) — his explanatory glosses on [[馬永卿]]'s record of [[劉安世]]'s sayings — and the *Hǎi qiáo jí* 海樵集 (collected works). Biography in *Míng shǐ* j. 287.
