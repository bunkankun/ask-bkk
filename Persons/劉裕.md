**Liú Yù** 劉裕 (南朝·宋). *Sòng Wǔdì* Liú Yù 劉裕 (363–422), founder of the Liú-Sòng dynasty (r. 420–422); under his northern expedition the formulae attributed to [[劉涓子]] in [[KR3ek040]] *Liú Juānzǐ guǐyí fāng* were said to have been clinically tested on battle-wounds.
