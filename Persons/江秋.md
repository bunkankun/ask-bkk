**Jiāng Qiū** 江秋 (also recorded as Jiāng Hán-dūn 江涵暾, *zì* Yǔ-yún 雨耘, *hào* Bǐ-huā 筆花, c. 1770 – c. 1837), Qīng official and physician of Wǎn-jiāng / Wú-yuán 婺源 region (Ān-huī). *Jìn-shì* of 1799 in the *Jiā-qìng* reign; subsequent provincial-official career in Guǎng-dōng (Shàn-tóu and other prefectural posts). His principal medical work, the *Bǐ-huā yī-jìng* 筆花醫鏡 ([[KR3er009]], composed c. 1824 during his Guǎng-dōng official career, first prefaced for print by Zhōng Chéng-lù 鍾承露 in 1834), is a four-*juǎn* didactic handbook of internal medicine, gynaecology, and pediatrics organised under the *zàng-fǔ* viscera-and-bowels framework with characteristic *jūn-chén* drug-classification tables. The work became a standard text in late-Qīng / Republican-period folk and apprentice-physician training; it was repeatedly reprinted through the Tóng-zhì and Guāng-xù eras. CBDB 354753 records *c_index_year* 1778 (the most plausible candidate among CBDB homonyms). The *Bǐ-huā* literary cover-name became conventional in Chinese-medicine reference works for the author.
