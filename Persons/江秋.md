**Jiāng Qiū** 江秋 (also recorded as Jiāng Hándūn 江涵暾, *zì* Yǔyún 雨耘, *hào* Bǐhuā 筆花, c. 1770 – c. 1837), Qīng official and physician of Wǎnjiāng / Wúyuán 婺源 region (Ānhuī). *Jìnshì* of 1799 in the *Jiāqìng* reign; subsequent provincial-official career in Guǎngdōng (Shàntóu and other prefectural posts). His principal medical work, the *Bǐhuā yījìng* 筆花醫鏡 ([[KR3er009]], composed c. 1824 during his Guǎngdōng official career, first prefaced for print by Zhōng Chénglù 鍾承露 in 1834), is a four-*juǎn* didactic handbook of internal medicine, gynaecology, and pediatrics organised under the *zàngfǔ* viscera-and-bowels framework with characteristic *jūnchén* drug-classification tables. The work became a standard text in late-Qīng / Republican-period folk and apprentice-physician training; it was repeatedly reprinted through the Tóngzhì and Guāngxù eras. CBDB 354753 records *c_index_year* 1778 (the most plausible candidate among CBDB homonyms). The *Bǐhuā* literary cover-name became conventional in Chinese-medicine reference works for the author.
