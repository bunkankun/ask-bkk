Xú Jiē 徐階 (1503–1583), *zì* Zǐ-shēng 子升, *hào* Shǎo-hú 少湖 (also Cún-zhāi 存齋), native of Sōng-jiāng Huá-tíng 松江華亭 (modern Shàng-hǎi region). *Jìn-shì* of *Jiā-jìng* 2 (1523, *tàn-huā*). Reached *Shǒu-fǔ* 首輔 (Senior Grand Secretary) under *Jiā-jìng* and *Lóng-qìng*; principal architect of the post-Yán Sōng 嚴嵩 reform of the *Nèi-gé* (Grand Secretariat); known for his integrity in eventually compiling the case against Yán.

Editor of the present 1-*juǎn* recension of Yuè Fēi 岳飛 [[岳飛]]'s surviving writings, the *Yuè Wǔ-mù yí-wén* [[KR4d0191]] — collected from fragmentary sources including the *Yuè-miào jí* 岳廟集 (Yuè-Fēi shrine collection). The *Sì-kù* editors note that Xú's recension was originally placed as appendix to a 4-*juǎn* shrine-collection, with later-period poems-and-prose at the front; the *Sì-kù* editors disapprove of the placement as inverted and excise the Míng-period miscellaneous appendices, retaining only Yuè's surviving writings.
