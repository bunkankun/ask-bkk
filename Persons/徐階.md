Xú Jiē 徐階 (1503–1583), *zì* Zǐshēng 子升, *hào* Shǎohú 少湖 (also Cúnzhāi 存齋), native of Sōngjiāng Huátíng 松江華亭 (modern Shànghǎi region). *Jìnshì* of *Jiājìng* 2 (1523, *tànhuā*). Reached *Shǒufǔ* 首輔 (Senior Grand Secretary) under *Jiājìng* and *Lóngqìng*; principal architect of the post-Yán Sōng 嚴嵩 reform of the *Nèigé* (Grand Secretariat); known for his integrity in eventually compiling the case against Yán.

Editor of the present 1-*juǎn* recension of Yuè Fēi 岳飛 [[岳飛]]'s surviving writings, the *Yuè Wǔmù yíwén* [[KR4d0191]] — collected from fragmentary sources including the *Yuèmiào jí* 岳廟集 (YuèFēi shrine collection). The *Sìkù* editors note that Xú's recension was originally placed as appendix to a 4-*juǎn* shrine-collection, with later-period poems-and-prose at the front; the *Sìkù* editors disapprove of the placement as inverted and excise the Míng-period miscellaneous appendices, retaining only Yuè's surviving writings.
