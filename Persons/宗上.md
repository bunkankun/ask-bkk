# Zōngshàng 宗上

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[璧|Shānhuī Bì]] 山暉璧 in the Pòshān / Yuánliáo Guìzhōu sub-line. Head editor (*biān* 編) of [[KR6q0437]] 《山暉禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B223).

The 宗-generation character is non-standard for the main Mìyún lineage-poem — likely a Guìzhōu regional variant. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
