Wú Liàng 吳亮 (*fl.* early 14th c., Yuán *Dà-dé* / *Zhì-dà*). A Yuán-era literatus and moralist; native place and career not firmly attested. Author of the *Rěn jīng* 忍經 ([[KR3j0193]]) in 1 *juàn*, a Yuán-era moral compendium devoted entirely to the virtue of *rěn* 忍 (forbearance). The work was widely circulated in late-imperial East Asia, including Korean and Japanese Confucian education. No further biographical record survives in the standard Yuán-Míng compilations. He is sometimes confused with later figures of the same name; the Yuán *Rěn jīng* author is the one referenced here.
