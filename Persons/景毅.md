# Jǐng-yì 景毅

Late-Yuán Chán *xiǎo-shī bǐ-qiū* 小師比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[大圓]]) of *juan* 8 (*fǎ-yǔ* 法語) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
