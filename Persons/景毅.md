# Jǐngyì 景毅

Late-Yuán Chán *xiǎoshī bǐqiū* 小師比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[大圓]]) of *juan* 8 (*fǎyǔ* 法語) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
