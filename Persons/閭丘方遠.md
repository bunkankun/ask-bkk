Lǘqiū Fāngyuǎn 閭丘方遠 (*hào* Xuándòng xiānshēng 玄洞先生; d. 902) was a late-Táng Shàngqīng 上清 master of Sōushān 嵩山 and then of Tiānzhù guàn 天柱觀 on Mount Tiānzhù (Yúhángzhōu 餘杭州 / modern Zhèjiāng). A polymath catalogue-maker, commentator, and ritual authority, he produced among other works:

- The *Tiānzhù guàn jì* 天柱觀記 of 900, cited in DZ 782 *Dàdì dòngtiān jì* 3.3b.
- The *Tàishàng dòngxuán língbǎo dàgāng chāo* 太上洞玄靈寶大綱鈔 [[KR5b0077]] (DZ 393), an outline of the Língbǎo tradition organised by the five-kalpa / five-element correspondence.
- A commentary on the *Nánhuá zhēnjīng* 南華真經 (partly preserved) and other Shàngqīng exegetical materials.

His biography is preserved in DZ 296 *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn* 歷世真仙體道通鑑 (*Xù xiān zhuàn* 續仙傳 tradition) 3.4a–6a, which records his audiences at the court of Qián Liú 錢鏐 of Wúyuè 吳越. CBDB has an entry (id 450098) but gives no lifedates; the death date 902 is universally accepted in the modern scholarship following DZ 296. The name is frequently miscopied as 方達 — e.g. in the source file of DZ 393 itself — and this error should be watched for.
