Lǐ Sīyì 李斯義, Kāngxī-period Běijīng-resident host whose household sheltered [[李塨]] Lǐ Gōng (1659–1733) during spring 1704, when Lǐ Gōng was completing the hexagram sections of his *Zhōuyì zhuàn zhù* ([[KR1a0140]]). No further biographical details preserved.
