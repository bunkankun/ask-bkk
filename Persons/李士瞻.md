Lǐ Shì-zhān 李士瞻 (1313–1367), *zì* Yàn-wén 彥聞. Family origin Xīn-yě 新野 (Hé-nán); relocated to Jīng-mén 荊門 (Hú-běi). Yuán Zhì-zhèng-era *jìnshì* by the *Dà-dū-lù* roll. Career: *Zhōng-shū yòu-sī yuán* (recommendation), *Xíng-bù zhǔ-shì*, then up through *Hù-bù shàng-shū*, *Fú-jiàn hǎi-cáo dū-bù*; appointed *Fú-jiàn xíng-shěng zuǒ-chéng*; called back as *cān-zhī zhèng-shì*; *Shū-mì fù-shǐ*; *Hàn-lín xué-shì chéng-zhǐ*; enfeoffed *Chǔ-guó-gōng* 楚國公; died Zhì-zhèng 27 (1367). The *Yuán-shǐ* did not give him a separate biography; only the *Shùn-dì běn-jì* records his 22-item *shí-zhèng-shū* (twenty-item current-government memorial) of Zhì-zhèng 22 (1362). Edited posthumously by his great-grandson **Shēn** 伸 (= 李伸 [[李伸]]). The 70+ correspondence letters in the collection [[KR4d0526]] are the principal contribution to *Shùn-dì-shí běn-jì* documentation.
