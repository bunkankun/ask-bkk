Lǐ Shìzhān 李士瞻 (1313–1367), *zì* Yànwén 彥聞. Family origin Xīnyě 新野 (Hénán); relocated to Jīngmén 荊門 (Húběi). Yuán Zhì-zhèng-era *jìnshì* by the *Dàdūlù* roll. Career: *Zhōngshū yòusī yuán* (recommendation), *Xíngbù zhǔshì*, then up through *Hùbù shàngshū*, *Fújiàn hǎicáo dūbù*; appointed *Fújiàn xíngshěng zuǒchéng*; called back as *cānzhī zhèngshì*; *Shūmì fùshǐ*; *Hànlín xuéshì chéngzhǐ*; enfeoffed *Chǔguógōng* 楚國公; died Zhìzhèng 27 (1367). The *Yuánshǐ* did not give him a separate biography; only the *Shùndì běnjì* records his 22-item *shízhèngshū* (twenty-item current-government memorial) of Zhìzhèng 22 (1362). Edited posthumously by his great-grandson **Shēn** 伸 (= 李伸 [[李伸]]). The 70+ correspondence letters in the collection [[KR4d0526]] are the principal contribution to *Shùndìshí běnjì* documentation.
