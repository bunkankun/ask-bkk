**Fāng Xián** 方賢 (*fl.* late 15th c., *Tiānshùn* / *Chénghuà* era). Of Hèjiān 河間 (modern Hèjiān-shì, Héběi). Ming court physician. Held the office of *Tài-yī yuàn shǐ* 太醫院使 (Director of the Imperial Medical Academy) under the *Chénghuà* and / or *Hóngzhì* reigns. Successor to [[董宿]] (Dǒng Sù) at the Tài-yī yuàn; completed and edited Dǒng's posthumous manuscript of the *Qíxiào liángfāng* 奇效良方 ([[KR3ed038]]) for publication. The work's standard print probably dates to the *Chénghuà gēng-yín* (1470, with the surviving preface's date corrupted in transmission to *Shàodìng gēng-yín* 1230, a non-Ming reign-period). Fāng Xián has no separate biographical record in *Míng shǐ*; not in CBDB.
