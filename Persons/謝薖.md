**Xiè Kē** 謝薖 (d. *Zhènghé* 6 / 1116), *zì* Yòupán 幼槃, *hào* Zhúyǒu 竹友, of Línchuān 臨川 (Jiāngxī). Younger brother of Xiè Yì 謝逸 [[謝逸]]. Like his brother, lived as a recluse-poet without entering office. Listed by Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 in the *Jiāngxī shīshè zōngpài tú* — a canonical *Jiāngxī shīpài* figure. Author of *Zhúyǒu jí* 竹友集 [[KR4d0119]] in 10 *juǎn*. Together with his brother known as the *Èr Xiè* 二謝. The *Sìkù* tíyào on Ráo Jié [[KR4d0100]] notes that Xiè Kē's *Zhúyǒu jí*, the Hán Jū collection, and the Ráo Jié *Yǐsōng shījí* together preserve the *hángkuǎn* of the lost *Jiāngxī shīpài jí* in 137 *juǎn*. CBDB has *fl.* dates only; death 1116 confirmed by external sources.
