**Xiè Kē** 謝薖 (d. *Zhèng-hé* 6 / 1116), *zì* Yòu-pán 幼槃, *hào* Zhú-yǒu 竹友, of Lín-chuān 臨川 (Jiāng-xī). Younger brother of Xiè Yì 謝逸 [[謝逸]]. Like his brother, lived as a recluse-poet without entering office. Listed by Lǚ Běn-zhōng 呂本中 in the *Jiāng-xī shī-shè zōng-pài tú* — a canonical *Jiāng-xī shī-pài* figure. Author of *Zhú-yǒu jí* 竹友集 [[KR4d0119]] in 10 *juǎn*. Together with his brother known as the *Èr Xiè* 二謝. The *Sìkù* tíyào on Ráo Jié [[KR4d0100]] notes that Xiè Kē's *Zhú-yǒu jí*, the Hán Jū collection, and the Ráo Jié *Yǐ-sōng shī-jí* together preserve the *háng-kuǎn* of the lost *Jiāng-xī shī-pài jí* in 137 *juǎn*.
