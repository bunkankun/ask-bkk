# Shìcǎn 士慘

Yuán Cáodòng Chán *xiǎoshī bǐqiū* 小師比丘 of [[雲岫]] Yúnwài Yúnxiù (1242–1324); sole named editor of his master's one-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0361]]). Chén Shèng's 1300 preface at the head of that text names him explicitly as the disciple who "brought out the master's language in a single volume and asked me [Chén Shèng] for a preface." Lifedates unrecorded.
