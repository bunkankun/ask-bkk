**Xiāo Xiǎotíng** 肖曉亭 (also written 蕭曉亭, *fl.* late Qiánlóng – early Dàoguāng, c. 1796–1845, 清), Qīng-period specialist on leprosy. Of Lúlíng 廬陵 (Jíān 吉安, Jiāngxī); a *lǐn shàn shēng* 廩膳生 (stipendiary student of the local *xuégōng*). Lifedates unknown; flourished from the late Qiánlóng through the early Dàoguāng period. Author of [[KR3ek041]] ***Fēngmén quánshū*** 瘋門全書 (initial compilation 1796; formally printed under the supervision of Liú Quánshí 劉全石 of Fēnyí 分宜 and edited by Hé Shíqīng 何石卿 of Gāoyào 高要, with a Dàoguāng 25 / 1845 re-cut by Jìngyètáng 敬業堂). The work is one of the most fully developed Qīng *wàikē* monographs on *máfēng* / *dàfēng* / *lì*, systematising thirty-six varieties of *fēng* and explicitly rejecting both Chinese purgative methods and Western surgical excision in favour of patient internal regulation. The principal contemporary Chinese source on leprosy for Angela Leung's *Leprosy in China* (2009). Not in CBDB.
