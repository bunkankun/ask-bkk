**Xiāo Xiǎotíng** 肖曉亭 (also written 蕭曉亭, *fl.* late Qián-lóng – early Dào-guāng, c. 1796–1845, 清), Qīng-period specialist on leprosy. Of Lú-líng 廬陵 (Jí-ān 吉安, Jiāngxī); a *lǐn shàn shēng* 廩膳生 (stipendiary student of the local *xué-gōng*). Lifedates unknown; flourished from the late Qián-lóng through the early Dào-guāng period. Author of [[KR3ek041]] ***Fēng-mén quán-shū*** 瘋門全書 (initial compilation 1796; formally printed under the supervision of Liú Quán-shí 劉全石 of Fēn-yí 分宜 and edited by Hé Shí-qīng 何石卿 of Gāo-yào 高要, with a Dào-guāng 25 / 1845 re-cut by Jìng-yè-táng 敬業堂). The work is one of the most fully developed Qīng *wài-kē* monographs on *má-fēng* / *dà-fēng* / *lì*, systematising thirty-six varieties of *fēng* and explicitly rejecting both Chinese purgative methods and Western surgical excision in favour of patient internal regulation. The principal contemporary Chinese source on leprosy for Angela Leung's *Leprosy in China* (2009). Not in CBDB.
