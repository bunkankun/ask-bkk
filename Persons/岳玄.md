Yuèxuán 岳玄 (fl. early Qing) was the disciple of [[靈椉]] Língshèng who arranged the printing of his master's [[KR6h0017|*Dìzàng běnyuàn jīng kēwén* 地藏本願經科文]]. The colophon to the *Kēwén* signs the work as "秦谿青蓮大師定，門人岳玄排" — the Blue Lotus Master defining the structural divisions, the disciple Yuèxuán arranging the printed text. No further biographical data have been located.
