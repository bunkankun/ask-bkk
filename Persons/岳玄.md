Yuè-xuán 岳玄 (fl. early Qing) was the disciple of [[靈椉]] Líng-shèng who arranged the printing of his master's [[KR6h0017|*Dì-zàng běn-yuàn jīng kē-wén* 地藏本願經科文]]. The colophon to the *Kē-wén* signs the work as "秦谿青蓮大師定，門人岳玄排" — the Blue Lotus Master defining the structural divisions, the disciple Yuè-xuán arranging the printed text. No further biographical data have been located.
