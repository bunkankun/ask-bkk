# 勤操

Gonsō (Chinese: Qíncāo, 758–827), early-Heian Japanese Sanron-school 三論 master of Daian-ji 大安寺 in Nara. His Sanron commentarial heritage was synthesised by his disciple [[願曉]] Gangyō in the *Konkōmyō saishōōkyō genzū* ([[KR6i0318]]).
