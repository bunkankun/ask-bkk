Pān Wèi 潘霨 (*zì* Wěirú 偉如; 1826–1894) was a Wúmén 吳門 (Sūzhōu) gentry-physician and Qīng official, governor of Jiāngxī and later Guìzhōu. He compiled an 1879 expanded edition with notes for the *Shíyào shénshū* ([[KR3eh041]]).
