**Wáng Dào** 王道 was a Sòng Daoist alchemical exegete, author of [[KR5d0013]] *Gǔ wén Lóng hǔ jīng zhù shū* 古文龍虎經註疏 — a three-*juàn* commentary and sub-commentary on the *Gǔ wén Lóng hǔ jīng* 古文龍虎經. The *Lóng hǔ jīng* is an alchemical classic closely affiliated with the *Cān tóng qì* tradition. (Distinguish from the Míng-era Confucian Wáng Dào 王道, 1487–1547.)
