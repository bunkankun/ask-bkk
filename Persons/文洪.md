Mid-Míng literatus and educational official; **founder of the Wén-family literary lineage** at **Chángzhōu** 長洲 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Zì* **Gōngdà** 功大. **CBDB id 34670**. *Jǔrén* (provincial-degree, not full *jìnshì*) of **Chénghuà *yǐyǒu* (1465)**. Served as **Láishuǐ *jiàoyù*** (Láishuǐ district school-instructor).

He is the **grandfather of the famous painter [[文徵明]] Wén Zhēngmíng** (1470–1559); through Wén Zhēngmíng, ancestor of the late-Míng painter-official Wén Péng 文彭, Wén Jiā 文嘉, and Wén Zhàozhǐ 文肇祉. The four-generation Wén-family collection [[KR4h0112]] *Wénshì wǔ jiā jí* (14 *juǎn*) opens with Wén Hóng's *Kuònáng gǎo* 括囊稿 (1 *juǎn* verse + 1 *juǎn* prose). The *Jìngzhìjū shīhuà* praises his lines for their *tiándàn* (tranquil-and-calm) quality.
