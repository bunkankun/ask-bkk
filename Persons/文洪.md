Mid-Míng literatus and educational official; **founder of the Wén-family literary lineage** at **Cháng-zhōu** 長洲 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). *Zì* **Gōng-dà** 功大. **CBDB id 34670**. *Jǔ-rén* (provincial-degree, not full *jìnshì*) of **Chéng-huà *yǐ-yǒu* (1465)**. Served as **Lái-shuǐ *jiào-yù*** (Lái-shuǐ district school-instructor).

He is the **grandfather of the famous painter [[文徵明]] Wén Zhēng-míng** (1470–1559); through Wén Zhēng-míng, ancestor of the late-Míng painter-official Wén Péng 文彭, Wén Jiā 文嘉, and Wén Zhào-zhǐ 文肇祉. The four-generation Wén-family collection [[KR4h0112]] *Wén-shì wǔ jiā jí* (14 *juǎn*) opens with Wén Hóng's *Kuò-náng gǎo* 括囊稿 (1 *juǎn* verse + 1 *juǎn* prose). The *Jìng-zhì-jū shī-huà* praises his lines for their *tián-dàn* (tranquil-and-calm) quality.
