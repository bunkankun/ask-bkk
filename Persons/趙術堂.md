**Zhào Shù-táng** 趙術堂 (*hào* Shuāng-hú 雙湖, mid-Dào-guāng-era physician of Jiāng-Sū), a Gāo-yóu 高郵-born physician who settled in Xīng-huà 興化 and conducted his clinical practice across the Jiāng-Huái region. His reputation in his lifetime was specifically for the treatment of damp diseases (人多病濕), endemic in the Jiāng-Huái river-and-marsh environment. His principal work is the *Yī-xué zhǐ-guī* 醫學指歸 ([[KR3er050]], dated 1848), a two-*juǎn* didactic medical handbook focused on the *jīng-luò* 經絡 system. He also produced a commentary on Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng's *Shāng-hán lùn* and miscellaneous case-records. Honoured posthumously in the *Xīng-huà xiàn zhì* 興化縣志 *liú-yù* 流寓 emigré-biography section. No CBDB record. Conventional lifedates c. 1800 – c. 1850.
