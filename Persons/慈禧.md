**Cíxǐ** 慈禧 (清). Empress Dowager Xiàoqīnxiǎn 孝欽顯 (Yèhènàlā Xìngzhēn 葉赫那拉·杏貞, 1835–1908); de-facto ruler of Qīng China during the latter half of the 19th c. In 1880 (Guāngxù 6) she was treated by [[馬培之]] (cf. [[KR3ek008]], [[KR3ek017]]).
