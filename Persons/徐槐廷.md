Late-Qīng (Dào-guāng / Xián-fēng / Tóng-zhì-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Yún-hè** 雲鶴, sobriquets **Lè-jìng jū-shì** 樂淨居士, **Jìng-rú jū-shì** 淨如居士, **Wèi-sān** 位三. Also written **Xú Huái-tíng** 徐槐庭. Born **嘉慶 5 = 1800**; death year unrecorded. Author of *Jīn-gāng jīng jiě-yì* 金剛經解義 ([[KR6c0097]], X25 no. 509), a two-juan *Vajracchedikā* commentary, with paratext by **Huáng Lè-zhī** 黃樂之 dated **咸豐八年戊午秋八月 = autumn 1858**, fixing the work's effective publication.
