Late-Qīng (Dàoguāng / Xiánfēng / Tóng-zhì-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Yúnhè** 雲鶴, sobriquets **Lèjìng jūshì** 樂淨居士, **Jìngrú jūshì** 淨如居士, **Wèisān** 位三. Also written **Xú Huáitíng** 徐槐庭. Born **嘉慶 5 = 1800**; death year unrecorded. Author of *Jīngāng jīng jiěyì* 金剛經解義 ([[KR6c0097]], X25 no. 509), a two-juan *Vajracchedikā* commentary, with paratext by **Huáng Lèzhī** 黃樂之 dated **咸豐八年戊午秋八月 = autumn 1858**, fixing the work's effective publication.
