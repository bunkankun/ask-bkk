# Rúyuān 如淵

Mid-Míng Chán monk; disciple of [[道濟|Fǎzhōu Dàojì]] 法舟道濟 (1487–1560). Lifedates unrecorded. Lead compiler (*děng biān* 等編 "et al. edited") of his master's *Shèng yǔ* 剩語 [[KR6q0238]].
