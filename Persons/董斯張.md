Dǒng Sīzhāng 董斯張 (1586–1628), *zì* Xiázhōu 遐周, was a native of Wū-chéng 烏程 (Hú-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng — the Wúxīng 吳興 of the older toponymy). A late-Míng *jūshì* 居士 of Buddhist learning and a prolific compiler, he is best known for the 32-juan *Wúxīng bèizhì* 吳興備志 ([[KR2k0034]]) — the comprehensive thematic *bèi-zhì* of Hú-zhōu — and for the *Guǎng bówù zhì* 廣博物志 (50 juan), a major expansion of Zhāng Huá 張華's *Bówù zhì* drawing on more than three thousand earlier sources. He also wrote the 文集 *Jìng-xiào zhāi gǎo* 靜嘯齋稿 / 集 in poetry and prose. He died at age 43 (1628). His circle in Wū-chéng included his brothers Dǒng Sī-zǐ-jīng 子京 / 元京 and Dǒng Rén-shēng 人生 / 生甫, and the Mǐn 閔 brothers Mǐn Yuán-héng 元衢 (康侯) and Mǐn Yuán-jīng 元京 — collaborators in the compilation of the *Wúxīng bèizhì*.
