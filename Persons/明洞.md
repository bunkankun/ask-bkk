# Míngdòng 明洞

Late-17th century LínjìYángqí Chán monk; senior dharma-heir of [[如一|Jífēi Rúyī]] 即非如一 (1616–1671). Lifedates unrecorded. Lead compiler (*děng biān* 等編 "et al. edited") of his master's *Jífēi chánshī quán lù* 即非禪師全錄 [[KR6q0231]] — Jífēi's 25-juan comprehensive *yǔlù* documenting his bi-national Chinese-Japanese teaching-career as a co-founder of the Ōbakushū.
