Yuán-period *xiǎo-xué* scholar, *zì* Zǐ-zhōng 子中, of Zhāo-wǔ 昭武 (Shào-wǔ, Fú-jiàn). *fl.* late 13th century. The principal compiler-redactor of the *Gǔ-jīn yùn-huì jǔ-yào* 古今韻會舉要 [[KR1j0065]] (30 *juàn*) — the abridgement and practical edition of his teacher Huáng Gōng-shào's [[黃公紹]] *Gǔ-jīn yùn-huì* (now lost in its full form). Liú Chén-wēng 劉辰翁 wrote the preface in 1297 — providing a *terminus ad quem* for the work. The *Jǔ-yào* takes the rhyme-divisions of Liú Yuān 劉淵 (1252) and the *zì-mǔ* arrangement of Hán Dào-zhāo's [[韓道昭]] *Wǔ-yīn jí-yùn* and combines them — radically transforming the inherited rhyme-book genre. The Sìkù tíyào notes the work's dual achievement: an extensive citation-base (every reading is anchored to a source) but also a serious confusion of Old and Modern phonology (e.g., placing 忩 under *dōng* 東 and 西 under *xiān* 先).
