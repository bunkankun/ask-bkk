Yuán-period *xiǎoxué* scholar, *zì* Zǐzhōng 子中, of Zhāowǔ 昭武 (Shàowǔ, Fújiàn). *fl.* late 13th century. The principal compiler-redactor of the *Gǔjīn yùnhuì jǔyào* 古今韻會舉要 [[KR1j0065]] (30 *juàn*) — the abridgement and practical edition of his teacher Huáng Gōngshào's [[黃公紹]] *Gǔjīn yùnhuì* (now lost in its full form). Liú Chénwēng 劉辰翁 wrote the preface in 1297 — providing a *terminus ad quem* for the work. The *Jǔyào* takes the rhyme-divisions of Liú Yuān 劉淵 (1252) and the *zìmǔ* arrangement of Hán Dàozhāo's [[韓道昭]] *Wǔyīn jíyùn* and combines them — radically transforming the inherited rhyme-book genre. The Sìkù tíyào notes the work's dual achievement: an extensive citation-base (every reading is anchored to a source) but also a serious confusion of Old and Modern phonology (e.g., placing 忩 under *dōng* 東 and 西 under *xiān* 先).
