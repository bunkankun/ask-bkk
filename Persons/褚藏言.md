# Chǔ Cángyán 褚藏言 (fl. mid-9th c.)

A late-Táng scholar from the West-river (Xī-jiāng 西江) region (style "*Xī-jiāng yì-mín*" 西江逸民, "Recluse of the Western River"). He is the compiler of the *Dòu shì lián-zhū jí* 竇氏聯珠集 [[KR4h0018]] — a joint anthology of the *Wǔ Dòu* (five Dòu brothers) of Fú-fēng — for which he composed the principal preface and the individual *zhuàn* attached to each brother. The preface is dated to the years immediately after Dòu Cháng's death (825); Cángyán was therefore active in the 820s and 830s. No further biographical detail is recoverable.
