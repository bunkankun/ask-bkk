# 世親

Vasubandhu (Shìqīn 世親; also Tiānqīn 天親; transcribed 婆藪槃豆 Pósǒupándòu) — c. 4th–5th century Indian Buddhist master, brother of Asaṅga 無著 and one of the founding figures of the Yogācāra school. Author of the *Abhidharmakośa* 俱舍論, the *Triṃśikāvijñaptimātratā* 唯識三十頌, and numerous commentaries and treatises on Mahāyāna sūtras; in the East-Asian tradition many *upadeśa*-style commentaries are attributed to him. The historical biography is complex: traditional sources speak of a single Vasubandhu but modern scholarship (Frauwallner 1951) distinguishes an "earlier" Sarvāstivāda-Yogācāra master from a "younger" Mahāyāna author.
