Jīng Zhōngyǔn 荊中允 (*fl.* late 17th c., 清) is an otherwise obscure figure named as the alternative author of the *Běncǎo yìdú* 本草易讀 ([[KR3ec028]]), a work more commonly attributed to [[汪昂]] (Wāng Áng, 1615–1694). The dual attribution — "汪昂（一說荊中允）" — has been transmitted in catalog meta and may reflect a Húīzhōu publishing-network where Wāng Áng's name was used on a text actually authored by a less-known associate. No independent biographical information is preserved.
