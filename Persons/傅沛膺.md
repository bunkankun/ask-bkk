# Fù Pèiyīng 傅沛膺

Contemporary historian of Chinese medicine; editor of *Wàn Mìzhāi yīxué quánshū* 萬密齋醫學全書 (Beijing: Zhōngguó zhōngyīyào chūbǎnshè, 1999), the standard modern collected edition of the works of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán.
