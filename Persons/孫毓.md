Sūn Yù 孫毓 (*fl.* late 3rd century) was a Western Jìn 西晉 classicist and ritualist who held the office of Grand Protector of Cháng-shā 長沙太守 under Jìn Wǔ-dì 武帝 (r. 265–290). He is best known for his ritual scholarship, particularly the *Máo shī yì wèn* 毛詩異問 (a collation of variant readings in the *Máo* recension of the *Shī jīng*) and a *Lǐ jì zhù* 禮記注, both listed in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 and now lost. He is regularly cited in *Tōng diǎn* 通典 and in the *Lǐjì* sub-commentaries on questions of mourning grade and ancestral ritual.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0126]] *Qù fá lùn* 去伐論. CBDB has no entry; precise birth and death years are not preserved.
