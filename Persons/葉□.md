Late Southern Sòng *bǐjì* author, surname Yè 葉, personal name lost. Known only from Táo Zōngyí's 陶宗儀 *Shuō fú* 說郛 j. 17, which attributes 22 entries of the *Ài-rì-zhāi cóng-chāo* 愛日齋叢抄 ([[KR3j0057]]) to "*Sòng Yè* 葉 *mǒu*" — Sòng Yè ___, without naming him. Lifedates undeterminable. The work is internally dated to the *Xián-chún* reign (1265–1274) of Dù-zōng 度宗, so the author flourished in the last decades of the Sòng. Various modern identifications (with Yè Dàqìng 葉大慶 of the *Kǎo gǔ zhì yí*, with Yè Tíngguī 葉廷珪) have been proposed but none has won acceptance. The Kanripo catalog meta gives the placeholder name 葉□ ("Yè ___"); used here as the wikilink anchor.
