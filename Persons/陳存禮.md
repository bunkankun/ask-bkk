Yuán disciple of **Yáng Wéizhēn** 楊維楨, editor of Yáng's *fù* anthology *Lìzé yíyīn* 麗則遺音 ([[KR4d0589]]) which was cut at Qiántáng (Hángzhōu) at the close of the Zhìyuán era. Yáng wrote a self-preface to the resulting print in *Zhìzhèng* 2 *rénwǔ* (1342). Chén's biography is otherwise undocumented; CBDB has 陳存禮 id 303454 with no dates given.
