# Chāo-jùn 超晙 (Xiá-qǐ Chāo-jùn 霞起超晙)

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master, dharma-heir of [[明哲|Xiào-táng Míng-zhé]] 笑堂明哲 in the Lín-yě Tōng-qí sub-line of the Mìyún Yuán-wù tradition. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Chāo-jùn** 超晙 (超-generation character, dharma-grandson level of Lín-yě), *hào* **Xiá-qǐ** 霞起 ("Cloud-Rising"). Principal dharma-heir of Xiào-táng at the time of the *yǔlù* compilation. Resident at **Yán-guān Bāo-shān Líng-quán-ān** 鹽官包山靈泉菴 (a hermitage on **Bāo-shān** 包山, either an island in Tài-hú or a peak near Sū-zhōu).

Head editor (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0425]] 《笑堂和尚語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B206). Chén Zhī-shēn's 1665 preface describes personally meeting Xiá-qǐ Chāo-jùn at Bāo-shān Líng-quán-ān and examining the ongoing cutting of the master's *yǔlù*: "*guò Bāo-shān jìng-shì xǐ jiē Xiá-qǐ Jùn-gōng* 過包山靜室喜接霞起晙公 ... *jí guān qí suǒ juē Wò-lóng yī luán* 及觀其所劂臥龍一臠" ("passing through Bāo-shān's quiet-chamber, gladly received Xiá-qǐ Jùn-gōng ... examined what he was carving, the single morsel of Wò-lóng").

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
