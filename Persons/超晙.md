# Chāojùn 超晙 (Xiáqǐ Chāojùn 霞起超晙)

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master, dharma-heir of [[明哲|Xiàotáng Míngzhé]] 笑堂明哲 in the Línyě Tōngqí sub-line of the Mìyún Yuánwù tradition. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Chāojùn** 超晙 (超-generation character, dharma-grandson level of Línyě), *hào* **Xiáqǐ** 霞起 ("Cloud-Rising"). Principal dharma-heir of Xiàotáng at the time of the *yǔlù* compilation. Resident at **Yánguān Bāoshān Língquánān** 鹽官包山靈泉菴 (a hermitage on **Bāoshān** 包山, either an island in Tàihú or a peak near Sūzhōu).

Head editor (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0425]] 《笑堂和尚語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B206). Chén Zhīshēn's 1665 preface describes personally meeting Xiáqǐ Chāojùn at Bāoshān Língquánān and examining the ongoing cutting of the master's *yǔlù*: "*guò Bāoshān jìngshì xǐ jiē Xiáqǐ Jùngōng* 過包山靜室喜接霞起晙公 ... *jí guān qí suǒ juē Wòlóng yī luán* 及觀其所劂臥龍一臠" ("passing through Bāoshān's quiet-chamber, gladly received Xiáqǐ Jùngōng ... examined what he was carving, the single morsel of Wòlóng").

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
