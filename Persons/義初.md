# Yì-chū 義初

Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[清了]] Zhēn-xiē Qīng-liǎo (1088–1151); also served as his master's *shì-zhě* 侍者, co-editing (with [[德初]]) the latter's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0359]]; *Jié-wài lù* 劫外錄). Lifedates unrecorded.
