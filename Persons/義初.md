# Yìchū 義初

Southern-Sòng Cáodòng Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[清了]] Zhēnxiē Qīngliǎo (1088–1151); also served as his master's *shìzhě* 侍者, co-editing (with [[德初]]) the latter's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0359]]; *Jiéwài lù* 劫外錄). Lifedates unrecorded.
