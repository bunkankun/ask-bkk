**Páng Yùn** 龐蘊 (c. 740–808), *zì* **Dàoxuán** 道玄, widely known as **Páng Jūshì** 龐居士 ("Layman Páng"). Táng-era Chán Buddhist layman, disciple of Shítóu Xīqiān and Mǎzǔ Dàoyī, native of Xiāngzhōu 襄州 in Húběi. His residence near Luótián 羅田 (Húběi) is mentioned in [[李鵬飛]] Lǐ Péngfēi's preface to *Sānyuán cānzàn yánshòu shū* ([[KR3eo012]]) as a landmark on his pilgrimage; Lǐ records meeting an aged Daoist near the former site of Páng Jūshì.
