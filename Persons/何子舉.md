Hé Zǐjǔ 何子舉, an early-Yuán scholar with the rank *Guózhèng* 國正 (Director of the Imperial Academy's correction office). Cited as a teacher of [[胡震]] Hú Zhèn (auto-preface to *Zhōuyì yǎn yì* [[KR1a0075]], dated 1305). Lifedates and biographical details are not securely attested in CBDB or standard reference works.
