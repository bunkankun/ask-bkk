Dàoxíng 道行 (1089 – 5 March 1151), Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng Línjì 楊岐 Chán master; *hào* **Xuětáng** 雪堂 ("Snow-Hall"), abbacy-name **Wūjù** 烏巨. Native of **Chǔzhōu** 處州 (Kuòcāng 括蒼, modern Lìshuǐ 麗水, Zhèjiāng), lay surname **Yè** 葉. Dharma-heir of **Fóyǎn Qīngyuǎn** 佛眼清遠 of Lóngménsì 龍門寺 in Shūzhōu (the major *Yángqí* 楊岐 sub-lineage).

He took ordination at age 19 under **Pǔzhào Juéyìn Yīng** 普照覺印英. After unsuccessful inquiry under **Zhǐyuán Rùn** 指源潤 he travelled to Shūzhōu and studied under **Fóyǎn Qīngyuǎn** as personal attendant (*shìzhě*); he attained awakening on the *Xuánshā zhúzháo jiǎozhǐtóu* 玄沙築著脚指頭 *huàtóu* and was named Fóyǎn's dharma-heir. From **Jiànyán** 建炎 2 (1128) onwards he held abbacies at Shòuníng 壽寧, then at Fǎhǎi 法海, Tiānníng 天寧, Nánmíng 南明, **Wūjù** 烏巨, and finally Jiànfúsì 薦福寺 in Ráozhōu, where he died in **Shàoxīng** 紹興 21 / 2 / 9 (= 5 March 1151) at age 63 (45 monastic years). The senior Línjì master **Dàhuì Zōnggǎo** 大慧宗杲 wrote the preface to his *yǔlù*.

Among Xuětáng's principal works are the **《雪堂行和尚語要》** *Xuětáng Xíng héshàng yǔyào* (1 juan) and the **《雪堂行拾遺錄》** *Xuětáng Xíng shíyí lù* ([[KR6r0091]], X83 no. 1576, 1 juan), the latter compiled and edited by his disciples after his death. Dharma-heirs include Tuìān Xiū 退庵休, Huìān Huìguāng 晦庵慧光, and Qiěān Shǒurén 且庵守仁. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001504.
