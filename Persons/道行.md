Dàoxíng 道行 (1089 – 5 March 1151), Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng Línjì 楊岐 Chán master; *hào* **Xuě-táng** 雪堂 ("Snow-Hall"), abbacy-name **Wū-jù** 烏巨. Native of **Chǔ-zhōu** 處州 (Kuò-cāng 括蒼, modern Lì-shuǐ 麗水, Zhèjiāng), lay surname **Yè** 葉. Dharma-heir of **Fó-yǎn Qīng-yuǎn** 佛眼清遠 of Lóng-mén-sì 龍門寺 in Shū-zhōu (the major *Yáng-qí* 楊岐 sub-lineage).

He took ordination at age 19 under **Pǔ-zhào Jué-yìn Yīng** 普照覺印英. After unsuccessful inquiry under **Zhǐ-yuán Rùn** 指源潤 he travelled to Shū-zhōu and studied under **Fó-yǎn Qīng-yuǎn** as personal attendant (*shì-zhě*); he attained awakening on the *Xuán-shā zhú-zháo jiǎo-zhǐ-tóu* 玄沙築著脚指頭 *huà-tóu* and was named Fó-yǎn's dharma-heir. From **Jiàn-yán** 建炎 2 (1128) onwards he held abbacies at Shòu-níng 壽寧, then at Fǎ-hǎi 法海, Tiān-níng 天寧, Nán-míng 南明, **Wū-jù** 烏巨, and finally Jiàn-fú-sì 薦福寺 in Ráo-zhōu, where he died in **Shào-xīng** 紹興 21 / 2 / 9 (= 5 March 1151) at age 63 (45 monastic years). The senior Línjì master **Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo** 大慧宗杲 wrote the preface to his *yǔ-lù*.

Among Xuě-táng's principal works are the **《雪堂行和尚語要》** *Xuě-táng Xíng héshàng yǔ-yào* (1 juan) and the **《雪堂行拾遺錄》** *Xuě-táng Xíng shí-yí lù* ([[KR6r0091]], X83 no. 1576, 1 juan), the latter compiled and edited by his disciples after his death. Dharma-heirs include Tuì-ān Xiū 退庵休, Huì-ān Huì-guāng 晦庵慧光, and Qiě-ān Shǒu-rén 且庵守仁. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001504.
