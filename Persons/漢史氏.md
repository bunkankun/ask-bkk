Hàn Shǐshì 漢史氏 ("A Han Historian") is a pen name used by the author of *Mǎnqīng Xīngwáng Shǐ* 滿清興亡史 ([[KR4k0304]]), a Republican-era popular history of the Qīng dynasty. The pen name signals a Han-nationalist perspective on the Manchu Qīng. The real identity is unknown. Active after 1912.
