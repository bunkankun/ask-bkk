**Wáng Gǔn** 王袞 (1037–1078, 宋), Northern-Sòng physician of Tài-yuán 太原, compiler of the ***Bó-jì fāng*** 博濟方 ([[KR3ed008]]) — a personal-experience clinical formulary of approximately five hundred prescriptions selected (from over seven thousand collected over a twenty-year span) during his time as Wine Inspector (酒官) at Qián-táng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu). His career details are not well recorded; the original preface by Láng Jiǎn 郎簡 attests only the Qián-táng wine-office. The biographical detail in the SKQS *tíyào* — that Wáng Gǔn's father (not Gǔn himself) served at Huá-tái 滑臺, and that Cháo Gōngwǔ's *Dúshū zhì* misattributes the Huá-tái posting to Gǔn — is a useful philological correction. CBDB id 38043 (1037–1078) is consistent with his composition of the *Bó-jì fāng* during the Qìng-lì 慶曆 period (1041–1048) at Huá-tái under his father's posting and his subsequent Qián-táng tenure.
