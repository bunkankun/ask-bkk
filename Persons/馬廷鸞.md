**Mǎ Tíngluán** 馬廷鸞 (1222–1289), *zì* Xiángzhòng 翔仲, *hào* Wán-fāng-bìng-sǒu 玩芳病叟, *zhāi-míng* 齋名 Bì-wú 碧梧, was one of the last Right Grand Councilors of the Southern Sòng. Native of Lèpíng 樂平 in Ráozhōu 饒州 (Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Chún-yòu* 7 (1247). He held a long sequence of mid-level court posts under *Lǐzōng* 理宗: starting as a Hànlín *biān-xiū* 編脩, then on the Yú-shǐ-tái 御史台 (Censorate), then on the *Qǐ-jū shě-rén* 起居舍人 (Diarist) bench. In 1265 he was appointed Vice-Commissioner of the Bureau of Military Affairs (*qiān-shū Shū-mì-yuàn shì* 簽書樞密院事), promoted to *Tóng-zhī Shū-mì-yuàn shì* 同知樞密院事 in 1267, concurrently *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* 參知政事 in 1268, and rose to Right Grand Councilor (*yòu chéng-xiàng* 右丞相) concurrently *Shū-mì shǐ* 樞密使 in *Xián-chún* 5 (1269). He withdrew from the chancellorship in *Xián-chún* 8 (1272), refusing to remain under [[賈似道]] Jiǎ Sìdào's regime, and spent the last seventeen years of his life in retirement at Lèpíng, surviving the conquest. He did not serve under the Yuán (CBDB *Notes*: "He did not take office under Yuán. CHECK wife's, born Zhāng, funerary inscription"). The *Sòng-shǐ* 414 carries his biography.

The catalog meta lists Mǎ as 1223–1289; CBDB (15501) and the standard biographies give 1222–1289, with the 1222 birth date preferred here. The composition window of his surviving collection (1248–1289) covers his full official-and-retirement career.

The *Sòng-shǐ* records a substantial list of Mǎ's writings, most now lost: *Liù-jīng jí-zhuàn* 六經集傳 (Collected Commentaries on the Six Classics), *Yǔ-Mèng huì-biān* 語孟會編 (Compilation on the *Lúnyǔ* and *Mèngzǐ*), *Chǔ-cí bǔ-jì* 楚辭補記, *Zhū-Sì yì-biān* 洙泗裔編 (a Zhū Xī-school history), *Dú Zhuāng bǐ-jì* 讀莊筆記, and the historical *Dú-shǐ xún-biān* 讀史旬編 (a Lǚ Zǔqiān-style *Dà-shì jì*-format chronological compendium from Yáo through the end of the Five Dynasties). All but the *Dú-shǐ xún-biān* fragments — preserved in the *Yǒnglè dà-diǎn* and recovered by the Sì-kù editors as the final *juàn* of the *Bìwú wánfāng jí* — are lost. His son [[馬端臨]] Mǎ Duānlín (c. 1254–1323) inherited the family's historiographic project and produced the *Wénxiàn tōng-kǎo* 文獻通考 [[KR2m0014]], one of the most important historical-and-institutional encyclopaedias of the Sòng-Yuán transition.

Compiled works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR4d0381]] *Bìwú wánfāng jí* 碧梧玩芳集 — 24 *juàn*, edited by his son Mǎ Duānlín and preserved only in the Sì-kù *Yǒnglè dà-diǎn* reconstruction.
