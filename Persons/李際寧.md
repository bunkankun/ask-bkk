Contemporary Chinese specialist of Buddhist manuscripts and printed editions, longtime senior researcher in the rare-book department of the National Library of China (中國國家圖書館). Principal scholar of the Buddhist Dūnhuáng holdings of the National Library and editor of *Fójiào dàzàngjīng yánjiū lùnjí* 佛教大藏經研究論集 (Beijing: Yànshān, 2007). He served as one of the principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editors of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series, contributing the present *Tiān qǐngwèn jīng shū* edition ([[KR6v0006]]) along with major studies of the *Jīn cáng* 金藏 and other tenth- to twelfth-century printed canons.
