Zūn 尊, *hào* **Gǔ-sù** 古宿 ("Ancient Lodging"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiāng-yán Dàng-shān Yuǎn-wēng** 香巖宕山遠翁 — likely a *yuǎn*-generation Mì-yún line successor in Hé-nán / Húběi region. Lifedates not located.

Native of **Chéng-dū** 成都 (Sì-chuān); tonsured at **Táng-ān** 唐安 (Sì-chuān); pilgrimage through Sì-chuān, then via Wǔ-dīng 五丁 corridor to Chǔ (Hú-běi/Hú-nán). Final transmission from Yuǎn-wēng at **Xiāng-yán-shān Dàng-shān** 香巖山宕山. Abbacies: **Dèng-zhōu Dān-xiá** 鄧州丹霞, **Xiāng-zhōu Dòng-shān** 襄州洞山, and several major monasteries in **Shān-yòu** 山右 (Shǎn-xī).

**Work.** [[KR6q0512]] 《古宿尊禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B387, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Bīng-lún Yuán** 冰輪圓 = **Míng-yuán** [[明圓]] *děng biān*. Front-preface mentions correspondence with the late-Míng / early-Qīng officials **Hú Jú-tán** 胡菊潭, **Zhū Cāng-qǐ** 朱滄起, **Sòng Èr-yǒu** 宋二酉.
