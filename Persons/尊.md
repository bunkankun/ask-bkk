Zūn 尊, *hào* **Gǔsù** 古宿 ("Ancient Lodging"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiāngyán Dàngshān Yuǎnwēng** 香巖宕山遠翁 — likely a *yuǎn*-generation Mìyún line successor in Hénán / Húběi region. Lifedates not located.

Native of **Chéngdū** 成都 (Sìchuān); tonsured at **Tángān** 唐安 (Sìchuān); pilgrimage through Sìchuān, then via Wǔdīng 五丁 corridor to Chǔ (Húběi/Húnán). Final transmission from Yuǎnwēng at **Xiāngyánshān Dàngshān** 香巖山宕山. Abbacies: **Dèngzhōu Dānxiá** 鄧州丹霞, **Xiāngzhōu Dòngshān** 襄州洞山, and several major monasteries in **Shānyòu** 山右 (Shǎnxī).

**Work.** [[KR6q0512]] 《古宿尊禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B387, compiled by *fǎsì* **Bīnglún Yuán** 冰輪圓 = **Míngyuán** [[明圓]] *děng biān*. Front-preface mentions correspondence with the late-Míng / early-Qīng officials **Hú Jútán** 胡菊潭, **Zhū Cāngqǐ** 朱滄起, **Sòng Èryǒu** 宋二酉.
