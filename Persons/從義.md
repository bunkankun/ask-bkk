Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master of the *shān-jiā* 山家 lineage; *zì* **Shū-duān** 叔端, sobriquet **Shén-zhì** 神智. DILA Authority A000571. Native of **Píng-yáng** 平陽 (modern Zhèjiāng). Born 1042 (Qìngí 慶曆 2); died Yuán-yòu 元祐 6, 1st month, 21st day (= 18 February 1091), aged 50.

Active in the second-generation post-Zhīlǐ Tiāntái revival, building on the doctrinal apparatus established by Sì-míng Zhīlǐ (960–1028) and his immediate successors. Cóngyì was particularly prolific as a commentator on the Tiāntái scholastic tradition.

Major work: the **Fǎhuá jīng sān-dà-bù bǔ-zhù** 法華經三大部補注 ([[KR6d0063]], X28n0586, 14 *juan*) — supplementary annotations on the Tiāntái triple-treatise corpus, complementing the comprehensive study guide of Fǎ-zhào's *Dú-jiào jì* ([[KR6d0062]]). His other works include the *Jīn-guāng-míng wén-jù xīn-jì* 金光明文句新記 (subcommentary on Zhìyǐ's *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* commentary) and various other Tiāntái commentaries.

Sources: *Shìmén zhèngtǒng* 釋門正統 *juan* 5; *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀 (T2035); DILA A000571.
