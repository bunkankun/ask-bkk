Xiè Yuè 解悅 (Kāngxī era, fl. early 18th cent.), tenth-generation descendant of the early-Míng *Hànlín* scholar **Xiè Jìn** 解縉 ([[解縉]]) of **Jíshuǐ** 吉水. Compiled the supplementary 16-*juǎn* recension of his ancestor's writings — [[KR4e0083]] *Wényì jí* — in Kāngxī *wùxū* (1718), recovering material lost since the Jiā-jìng-era 10-*juǎn* compilation by **Luó Hóngxiān** 羅洪先 and **Xiè Xiàng** 解相. This Kāngxī recension is the base text of the WYG transmission. No further biographical detail is preserved in standard sources.
