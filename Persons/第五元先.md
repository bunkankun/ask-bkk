# Dìwǔ Yuánxiān 第五元先

Late Eastern Hàn 東漢 *Yì* master, surname Dìwǔ 第五 (a rare Hàn surname), personal name Yuánxiān 元先. According to the *Hòu Hànshū* biography of [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán, he was Zhèng Xuán's first teacher in the [[京房]] Jīng Fáng line of *Yì* learning, instructing him in his early years before Zhèng moved on to study the Fèi Zhí line with [[馬融]] Mǎ Róng. No independent work survives.
