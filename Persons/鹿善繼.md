Lù Shànjì 鹿善繼 (1575–1636), *zì* **Bóshùn** 伯順, *hào* **Qiányuè** 乾岳, posthumous title **Zhōngjié** 忠節; native of **Dìngxīng** 定興 (Bǎodìng prefecture, Héběi). *Jìnshì* of Wànlì 41 / *guǐchǒu* (1613); rose through the Ministry of Revenue to *Bīngbù lángzhōng* and *Tàichángsì shàoqīng*. A *Wáng Yángmíngxué* exponent in the Northern Héběi tradition (the *Běifāng wángxué* of Sūn Qífēng 孫奇逢, with whom Lù was a close associate) and an outspoken member of the *Dōnglín* circle in its post-1626 phase. During the *Tiānqǐ* persecution of 1625, when the *Dōnglín liù jūnzǐ* including [[魏大中]] were arrested, [[魏學洢]] hid at Lù's Dìngxīng residence under disguise while attempting to ransom his father — an episode preserved both in the *Máoyán jí* ([[KR4e0243]]) and in Lù's own *Wúzhī táng yíshū* 無知堂遺書. He retired in *Tiānqǐ* 5; recalled in *Chóngzhēn* 9 / 1636 in the emergency of the Manchu invasion, he died defending Dìngxīng against the Qīng cavalry on the eleventh month, twenty-eighth day of that year.
