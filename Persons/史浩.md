Shǐ Hào 史浩 (1106–1194), zì Zhíwēng 直翁, hào Zhēnyǐn 真隱, posthumous title Wénhuì 文惠, was a Southern-Sòng official, Grand Councilor (zhèng-shì-táng cān-zhèng 正事堂參政), and *Shàngshū*-classicist from Yín-xiàn 鄞縣 (Níngbō 寧波, modern Zhèjiāng 浙江). He passed the *jìnshì* in *Shàoxīng* 紹興 14 = 1144 and rose to Right-Vice-Grand-Councilor (Yòu chéng-xiàng 右丞相). His main classical work is the *Shàngshū jiǎng yì* 尚書講義 ([[KR1b0010]]) in twenty juàn — the Southern-Sòng *jiǎng yì* (lecture-meaning) genre exposition of the *Shàngshū*, drawn from his lecture-transmission to his pupils.
