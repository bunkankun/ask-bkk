**Chén Chéng** 陳承 (*fl.* late 11th c., Northern Sòng), of Lín'ān 臨安 (modern Hángzhōu). Senior medical officer of the early *Tài-yī jú* 太醫局 (Imperial Medical Office) and one of the three principal compilers — with [[裴宗元]] and [[陳師文]] — of the foundational 1078–1085 redaction of the *Tàipíng huìmín héjì jú-fāng* 太平惠民和劑局方 ([[KR3ed011]]). He also produced an important pharmacological supplement, the *Chóng-guǎng bǔ-zhù Shén-nóng běn-cǎo bìng tú-jīng* 重廣補注神農本草並圖經, by collating Sū Sòng 蘇頌's *Tú-jīng běn-cǎo* 圖經本草 with the official *Jiā-yòu bǔ-zhù běncǎo* 嘉祐補注本草, an early example of the Sòng *bencao* collation tradition. The biographical record is sparse; he is not in CBDB. The principal evidence for his career is his prefatory presence on the 1078 redaction of the *Héjì júfāng*.
