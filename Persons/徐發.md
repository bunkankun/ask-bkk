Early-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) layman of **Jiāxīng** 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng), sobriquet **Nánhú Pǔrén** 南湖圃人. Lifedates unrecorded. Author of three works: *Jīngāng jīng yǐngshuō* 金剛經郢說 ([[KR6c0076]], X25 no. 488), prefaced **Kāngxī 12 = 1673** (康熈十有二年癸丑); a *Zhùshū biànzhèng* 注疏辯正 (commentarial polemic, lost or extra-canonical); and a calendrical-mathematical work *Tiānyuán lìlǐ quánshū* 天元歷理全書 (a treatise on the *tiānyuán* algebra of calendrical computation, marking him as one of the early-Qīng *Vajracchedikā*-commenting laymen who simultaneously pursued Western-influenced mathematics). Co-compiled the **Jiāxīngxiàn zhì** 嘉興縣志 in 9 juan during the Kāngxī era. The combination of Buddhist sūtra exegesis with mathematical-astronomical scholarship is characteristic of the *kǎozhèng*-flavoured early-Qīng Jiāxīng intellectual milieu.
