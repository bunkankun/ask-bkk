Early-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) layman of **Jiā-xīng** 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng), sobriquet **Nán-hú Pǔ-rén** 南湖圃人. Lifedates unrecorded. Author of three works: *Jīn-gāng jīng yǐng-shuō* 金剛經郢說 ([[KR6c0076]], X25 no. 488), prefaced **Kāng-xī 12 = 1673** (康熈十有二年癸丑); a *Zhù-shū biàn-zhèng* 注疏辯正 (commentarial polemic, lost or extra-canonical); and a calendrical-mathematical work *Tiān-yuán lì-lǐ quán-shū* 天元歷理全書 (a treatise on the *tiān-yuán* algebra of calendrical computation, marking him as one of the early-Qīng *Vajracchedikā*-commenting laymen who simultaneously pursued Western-influenced mathematics). Co-compiled the **Jiā-xīng-xiàn zhì** 嘉興縣志 in 9 juan during the Kāng-xī era. The combination of Buddhist sūtra exegesis with mathematical-astronomical scholarship is characteristic of the *kǎo-zhèng*-flavoured early-Qīng Jiā-xīng intellectual milieu.
