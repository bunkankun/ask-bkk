# Liú Biǎo 劉表

*Zì* Jǐngshēng 景升. Late Eastern Hàn 東漢 warlord and classicist, Inspector (*cìshǐ* 刺史) and Governor (*mù* 牧) of Jīngzhōu 荊州 (modern Húběi-Húnán region), one of the major regional powers of the late Hàn collapse before his death in 208 — shortly after which Jīngzhōu fell to Cáo Cāo 曹操. Maintained an active scholarly court at Xiāngyáng 襄陽; his lost *Zhōuyì zhāng jù* 周易章句 is preserved in fragments via [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Hòu Hànshū* (juan 74下) gives his biography.
