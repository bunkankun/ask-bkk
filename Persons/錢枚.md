Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng-era poet and literatus of Rén-hé 仁和 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhè-jiāng). A documented member of the late-Qián-lóng Háng-zhōu literary circle around Yuán Méi 袁枚 and Wáng Wén-zhì 王文治 (he should be carefully distinguished from his namesake Yuán Méi 袁枚 — the great Suí-yuán shī-rén — to whom he is sometimes mistakenly conflated). He contributed the postface to his neighbour-physician [[毛世洪]] Máo Shì-hóng's [[KR3er095]] *Yī-xué sān-xìn biān*, identifying Máo as *Fēng-shān Máo xiān-shēng*. His death in 1804 provides a *terminus ante quem* for the *Sān-xìn biān*.
