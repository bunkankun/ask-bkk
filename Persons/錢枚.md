Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng-era poet and literatus of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). A documented member of the late-Qián-lóng Hángzhōu literary circle around Yuán Méi 袁枚 and Wáng Wénzhì 王文治 (he should be carefully distinguished from his namesake Yuán Méi 袁枚 — the great Suíyuán shīrén — to whom he is sometimes mistakenly conflated). He contributed the postface to his neighbour-physician [[毛世洪]] Máo Shìhóng's [[KR3er095]] *Yīxué sānxìn biān*, identifying Máo as *Fēngshān Máo xiānshēng*. His death in 1804 provides a *terminus ante quem* for the *Sānxìn biān*.
