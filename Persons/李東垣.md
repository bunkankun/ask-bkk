**Lǐ Dōngyuán** 李東垣 (real name 李杲 Lǐ Gǎo, *zì* Míngzhī 明之, *hào* Dōngyuán lǎorén 東垣老人, 1180–1251), Jīn-Yuán physician of Zhēndìng 真定 (Héběi), disciple of Zhāng Yuánsù 張元素, and one of the *Jīn-Yuán sì dàjiā* 金元四大家. Founder of the *Bǔtǔ pài* 補土派 (Supplementing-the-Earth / Spleen-Stomach School). Principal works: *Pí wèi lùn* 脾胃論, *Nèi-wài shāng biàn huò lùn* 內外傷辨惑論, *Lánshì mìcáng* 蘭室秘藏. His signature formula *Bǔzhōng yìqì tāng* 補中益氣湯 became foundational to late-imperial spleen-stomach therapeutics.
