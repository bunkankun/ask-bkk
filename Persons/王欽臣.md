Wáng Qīnchén 王欽臣 (*fl.* 1067–1093, lifedates uncertain), *zì* Zhòngzhì 仲至. Son of Wáng Zhū 王洙 (998–1057, *Shìdú xuéshì* and *Shìjiǎng xuéshì*). Granted *jìnshì* status by imperial appointment (rather than by examination). Career: *Dàizhì*, *Zhī Chéngdé jūn* 知成德軍. Compiler of the *Wángshì tán lù* 王氏談錄 ([[KR3j0089]]), a 1-juan filial recording of his father Wáng Zhū's words on calligraphy, classical exegesis, painting, music, family-instruction, antiquities, and contemporary court affairs.
