Wáng Qīnchén 王欽臣 (*fl.* 1067–1093, lifedates uncertain), *zì* Zhòng-zhì 仲至. Son of Wáng Zhū 王洙 (998–1057, *Shì-dú xué-shì* and *Shì-jiǎng xué-shì*). Granted *jìnshì* status by imperial appointment (rather than by examination). Career: *Dài-zhì*, *Zhī Chéng-dé jūn* 知成德軍. Compiler of the *Wáng-shì tán lù* 王氏談錄 ([[KR3j0089]]), a 1-juan filial recording of his father Wáng Zhū's words on calligraphy, classical exegesis, painting, music, family-instruction, antiquities, and contemporary court affairs.
