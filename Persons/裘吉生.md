Qiú Jíshēng 裘吉生 (1873–1947), *míng* Qìngyuán 慶元, *zì* Jíshēng 吉生. Native of **Shàoxīng** 紹興 (Yuèzhōu 越州, Zhèjiāng). Republican-period physician, publisher, and rare-medical-book collector. Founder of the *Shàoxīng yīyào xuébào* 紹興醫藥學報 (Shàoxīng Medical Bulletin, 1909) and the *Sānsān yībào* 三三醫報. The most important Republican-period editor-publisher of the *guóyī* (national-medicine) movement, responsible for the systematic recovery, printing, and circulation of rare late-Imperial Chinese-medical manuscripts that would otherwise have been lost — including the 1915 print of [[KR3eq090]] *Yīxué kèér cè* prepared from Zhōu Zhèn's manuscript. His massive *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 and *Zhēnběn yīshū jíchéng* 珍本醫書集成 corpora are standard primary sources for late-Imperial and Republican Chinese-medical bibliography. Not in CBDB.
