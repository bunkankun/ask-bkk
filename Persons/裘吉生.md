Qiú Jí-shēng 裘吉生 (1873–1947), *míng* Qìng-yuán 慶元, *zì* Jí-shēng 吉生. Native of **Shào-xīng** 紹興 (Yuè-zhōu 越州, Zhèjiāng). Republican-period physician, publisher, and rare-medical-book collector. Founder of the *Shào-xīng yī-yào xué-bào* 紹興醫藥學報 (Shàoxīng Medical Bulletin, 1909) and the *Sān-sān yī-bào* 三三醫報. The most important Republican-period editor-publisher of the *guó-yī* (national-medicine) movement, responsible for the systematic recovery, printing, and circulation of rare late-Imperial Chinese-medical manuscripts that would otherwise have been lost — including the 1915 print of [[KR3eq090]] *Yī-xué kè-ér cè* prepared from Zhōu Zhèn's manuscript. His massive *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 and *Zhēn-běn yī-shū jí-chéng* 珍本醫書集成 corpora are standard primary sources for late-Imperial and Republican Chinese-medical bibliography. Not in CBDB.
