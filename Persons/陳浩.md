# Chén Hào 陳浩

Mid-Qīng court scholar and Sìkù-period editor, attached to the editorial committee of the *Shísān jīng zhùshū* imperial reissue (1747, Qiánlóng 12) under the Qiánlóng court. Co-author with [[朱良裘]] (Zhū Liángqiú) and [[李清植]] (Lǐ Qīngzhí) of the *kǎozhèng* 考證 (textual-critical) apparatus appended to the *Zhōuyì zhèngyì* in the WYG / Sìkù recension (= part of [[KR1a0007]]).

CBDB has multiple homonymous "Chén Hào" entries; the Qīng Sìkù-period figure is most likely either CBDB 68827 (b. 1692) or CBDB 68828 (b. 1693), both Qīng-dynasty *jìnshì* of the Yōngzhèng or Qiánlóng era. Disambiguation is not attempted here.

Note: a Yuán-dynasty *Chén Hào* 陳浩 (1244–1294, CBDB 107959) is the author of the standard *Lǐjì jí shuō* 禮記集說 (1308 preface posthumously by his son), an unrelated figure who happens to share the same name.
