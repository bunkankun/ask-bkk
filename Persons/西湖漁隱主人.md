**Xīhú Yúyǐn Zhǔrén** 西湖漁隱主人 ("The Recluse-Fisherman of West Lake") is a late-Míng pen name, identity unknown. Associated with the Hángzhōu 杭州 region (West Lake = Xīhú 西湖). The author of [[KR4k0050]] *Huānxǐ Yuānjiā* 歡喜冤家, a twelve-chapter short-story collection. No CBDB record has been located.
