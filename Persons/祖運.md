# Zǔ-yùn 祖運

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[志道]]) of *juan* 3 (Líng-yán abbacy, from 1345) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
