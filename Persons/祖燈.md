# Zǔdēng 祖燈

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán *sìfǎ* 嗣法 dharma-heir of [[文寶]] Fāngshān Wénbǎo; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0328]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the earlier Ānguó Zǔdēng 安國祖燈 (tonsure-master of [[了慧]] Xīyán Liǎohuì).
