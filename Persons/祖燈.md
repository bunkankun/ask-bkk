# Zǔ-dēng 祖燈

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán *sì-fǎ* 嗣法 dharma-heir of [[文寶]] Fāng-shān Wén-bǎo; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0328]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the earlier Ān-guó Zǔ-dēng 安國祖燈 (tonsure-master of [[了慧]] Xī-yán Liǎo-huì).
