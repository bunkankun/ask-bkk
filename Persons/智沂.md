# Zhìyí 智沂

Southern-Sòng Chán dharma-heir disciple (*sì fǎ mén rén* 嗣法門人) of Chījué Dàochōng 痴絕道冲 (1169–1250). Lead-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0310]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
