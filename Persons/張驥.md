**Zhāng Jì** 張驥 (1859–1924), *zì* Yǐn-líng 隱陵, *hào* Zhì-jì 治紀, was a late-Qīng / early-Republican Chéngdū 成都 (Sìchuān) physician and bibliophile, a major figure in the early-Republican movement to recover and reprint Hàn–Sòng medical texts. He compiled the *Sān sān yī jí* 三三醫籍 series, one of the principal Republican-era medical *cóngshū*, which printed (inter alia) Hú Shù's [[胡澍]] *Sùwèn jiào yì*, several pre-Sòng pharmacopoeia fragments, and numerous Tamba-family Edo prints. His own original works are oriented to systematic readings of the *Nèijīng* — *Nèijīng yào yuè* 內經藥瀹 ([[KR3ea042]]), *Nèijīng fāng* 內經方, *Nèijīng yùn-qì zhì yào* 內經運氣治要, *Léi-gōng zhāng* 雷公章. He died in 1924, aged 65, the year of his *Nèijīng yào yuè*'s first print.
