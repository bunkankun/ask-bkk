Liú Xiàowēi 劉孝威 (d. 548 CE) was a Liáng 梁 court poet from Pénchéng 彭城, a younger brother of Liú Xiàochuò 劉孝綽 and Liú Xiàoyí 劉孝儀 in the Péngchéng Liú literary family. He participated in the Palace Style (*gōngtǐ* 宮體) literary circles associated with Emperor Jianwen (Xiao Gang) and was included in Xú Líng's 徐陵 *Yùtái xīnyǒng* 玉臺新詠 anthology. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0062]].
