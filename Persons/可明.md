# 可明

Kěmíng

Tiāntái master at the Nánhú 南湖 monastic community of Níngbō in the late-thirteenth century. Wrote the covering letter to [[徐行善]] Xú Xíngshàn for the 1294 reformatted edition of [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ's *Jīngāng bānruò shū* ([[KR6c0054]]). Beyond this paratext his life is not preserved in standard biographies.
