Late-Yuán / early-Míng provincial official, native of **Lóng-xī** 龍溪 (Zhāngzhōu 漳州, Fújiàn). *Zì* Yuán-kǎi 元凱; *hào* Méi-xuān 梅軒. Yuán *jìnshì* of **Zhì-zhèng 8** (1348); served as instructor at the Yìwū 義烏 county school under the late Yuán. In **Hóngwǔ 2** (1369) selected as junior member of the embassy of **Zhāng Yǐ-níng** [[張以寧]] to **Annan** 安南 (Đại Việt under the Trần 陳 dynasty) to recognize King Trần Dụ-tông's successor; his *Shǐ Jiāo-zhǐ shī* 使交阯詩 sequence in [[KR4e0015]] is one of the principal records of the mission. Subsequently appointed *Dēng-zhōu zhī-zhōu* 登州知州 (Prefect of Dēng-zhōu, Shāndōng), in which post he died, c. 1382. Standard biography: *Fújiàn tōng-zhì* j. 178; brief notice in *Míng shǐ yì-wén zhì*. Collected works *Lín Dēng-zhōu jí* 林登州集 at [[KR4e0015]]. No confident CBDB match.
