Late-Yuán / early-Míng provincial official, native of **Lóngxī** 龍溪 (Zhāngzhōu 漳州, Fújiàn). *Zì* Yuánkǎi 元凱; *hào* Méixuān 梅軒. Yuán *jìnshì* of **Zhìzhèng 8** (1348); served as instructor at the Yìwū 義烏 county school under the late Yuán. In **Hóngwǔ 2** (1369) selected as junior member of the embassy of **Zhāng Yǐníng** [[張以寧]] to **Annan** 安南 (Đại Việt under the Trần 陳 dynasty) to recognize King Trần Dụ-tông's successor; his *Shǐ Jiāozhǐ shī* 使交阯詩 sequence in [[KR4e0015]] is one of the principal records of the mission. Subsequently appointed *Dēngzhōu zhīzhōu* 登州知州 (Prefect of Dēngzhōu, Shāndōng), in which post he died, c. 1382. Standard biography: *Fújiàn tōngzhì* j. 178; brief notice in *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*. Collected works *Lín Dēngzhōu jí* 林登州集 at [[KR4e0015]]. No confident CBDB match.
