Lí Yuánkuān 黎元寬, *zì* **Bóān** 博菴, alternate **Zuǒyán** 左嚴. Late-Míng / early-Qīng scholar-official from **Nánchāng** 南昌 (Jiāngxī). **Chóngzhēn 戊辰** = **1628** *jìnshì*; held office as **Zhèjiāng xuéshǐ** 浙江學使 (provincial education commissioner of Zhèjiāng) in the late Míng. After the dynastic transition retired to private writing.

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the lead preface to [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 — describes himself as *fǎdì* 法弟 ("dharma younger brother") of **Dōngyán Zhēncàn** [[真璨]], suggesting either lay-Buddhist transmission from the same teacher (**Jièān Wùjìn**) or simply the conventional self-deprecation of literati prefacing a *yǔlù*. The preface explicitly traces the lineage as "斷橋 [-下] 十五世金明介和尚之嗣子" — Línjì-Yángqí Duànqiáo 斷橋妙倫 → ... → Jièān Wùjìn → Dōngyán Zhēncàn.
