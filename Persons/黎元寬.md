Lí Yuán-kuān 黎元寬, *zì* **Bó-ān** 博菴, alternate **Zuǒ-yán** 左嚴. Late-Míng / early-Qīng scholar-official from **Nán-chāng** 南昌 (Jiāng-xī). **Chóngzhēn 戊辰** = **1628** *jìn-shì*; held office as **Zhè-jiāng xué-shǐ** 浙江學使 (provincial education commissioner of Zhèjiāng) in the late Míng. After the dynastic transition retired to private writing.

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the lead preface to [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 — describes himself as *fǎ-dì* 法弟 ("dharma younger brother") of **Dōng-yán Zhēn-càn** [[真璨]], suggesting either lay-Buddhist transmission from the same teacher (**Jiè-ān Wù-jìn**) or simply the conventional self-deprecation of literati prefacing a *yǔ-lù*. The preface explicitly traces the lineage as "斷橋 [-下] 十五世金明介和尚之嗣子" — Línjì-Yángqí Duàn-qiáo 斷橋妙倫 → ... → Jiè-ān Wù-jìn → Dōng-yán Zhēn-càn.
