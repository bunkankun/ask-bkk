# 服部東庵 Fúbù Dōng'ān / Hattori Tōan

**Hattori Tōan** 服部東庵, early-to-mid-Edo Japanese kanpō physician and acupuncturist; associated with the kogaku 古学 ("ancient-learning") current in Edo medicine, alongside [[杉山和一]] Sugiyama Wa'ichi (KR3ee052). Cited as a representative of the Edo acupuncture-revivalist tradition in [[管周桂]] Sugahara Shūkei's *Zhēnjiǔ xué gāngyào* 針灸學綱要 ([[KR3ee040]]) preface.
