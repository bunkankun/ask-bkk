Lǐ Xuéqín 李學勤 (1933–2019) was one of the most prominent 20th-century historians and palaeographers of early China, long associated with the Zhōngguó Shèhuì Kēxuéyuàn 中國社會科學院 and later Qīnghuá University, where he led the Qīnghuá-jiǎn 清華簡 manuscript editing project. He was a champion of the "走出疑古時代" reorientation of early-China studies. With [[張政烺]] he concurred (1975) with [[馬繼興]]'s judgement on the medieval status of the reconstructed *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]).
