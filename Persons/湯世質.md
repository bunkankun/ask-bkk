**Tāng Shìzhì** 湯世質 (*hào* **Yúncháo lǎorén** 雲巢老人), Qīng physician of **Nánshuǐ** 難水 (a place name in Jiāngxī or the lower Yangtze region), active in the Jiāqìng era. Republished [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's **[[KR3eq046]]** *Yīxué qióngyuán jí* 醫學窮源集 in Jiāqìng 13 (1808) at the *Yùmíng cǎotáng* 玉茗草堂 after a decade of editorial revision; his preface narrates his earlier travel through the Yangtze region in search of "*jīshì* 畸士" (extraordinary persons) who could resolve the doctrinal contradictions among the JīnYuán Four Masters, his eventual meeting with **Yīn Hézōng** 殷合宗 (descendant of Yīn Zháixīn) at a Nánchāng inn, and his purchase of the manuscript.
