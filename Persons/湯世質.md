**Tāng Shì-zhì** 湯世質 (*hào* **Yún-cháo lǎo-rén** 雲巢老人), Qīng physician of **Nán-shuǐ** 難水 (a place name in Jiāngxī or the lower Yangtze region), active in the Jiā-qìng era. Republished [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěn-táng's **[[KR3eq046]]** *Yī-xué qióng-yuán jí* 醫學窮源集 in Jiā-qìng 13 (1808) at the *Yù-míng cǎo-táng* 玉茗草堂 after a decade of editorial revision; his preface narrates his earlier travel through the Yangtze region in search of "*jī-shì* 畸士" (extraordinary persons) who could resolve the doctrinal contradictions among the Jīn-Yuán Four Masters, his eventual meeting with **Yīn Hé-zōng** 殷合宗 (descendant of Yīn Zhái-xīn) at a Nán-chāng inn, and his purchase of the manuscript.
