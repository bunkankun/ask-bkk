Jìn-period (3rd–4th c.) *Yì jīng* exegete. Author of the *Zhōu Yì guà xù lùn* 周易卦序論 ("Discussion of the Hexagram Sequence of the Zhōu Yì"), 1 *juàn*, now lost except for fragments preserved in Táng *lèishū* 類書 (notably *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽 38, *Chū xué jì* 初學記 5) and reconstructed by [[馬國翰]] Mǎ Guóhàn under that title in *Yùhán shānfáng jíyì shū* 玉函山房輯佚書 ([[KR1a0173]]). The biographical record is minimal: the *Suí shū·Jīng jí zhì* 隋書經籍志 registers the work alongside other Jìn *Yì* compositions; nothing further is securely known of his life.
