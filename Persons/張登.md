**Zhāng Dēng** 張登 (*zì* Dànxiān 誕先, *fl.* early Qīng), Qīng-period physician of Cháng-zhōu 長洲 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāngsū). Son of [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù (the renowned early-Qīng physician, author of the *Zhāng-shì yī-tōng* 張氏醫通). Author of the ***Shānghán shé jiàn*** 傷寒舌鑑 ([[KR3e0093]], 1 juan) — the principal Qīng tongue-diagnostic specialist treatise. Brother of [[張倬]] Zhāng Zhuō.
