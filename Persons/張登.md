**Zhāng Dēng** 張登 (*zì* Dànxiān 誕先, *fl.* mid–late 17th century), Qīng-period physician of Chángzhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Son of [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù (the renowned early-Qīng physician, author of the *Zhāngshì yītōng* 張氏醫通); brother of [[張倬]] Zhāng Zhuō. Author of the ***Shānghán shé jiàn*** 傷寒舌鑑 ([[KR3e0093]] WYG / [[KR3eb054]] hxwd, 1 juan, self-prefaced *Kāngxī wùshēn* 康熙戊申 = **1668** at the Juànyǒngtáng 雋永堂) — the principal Qīng tongue-diagnostic specialist treatise. The work draws on the 137-figure *Guān shé xīn fǎ* 觀舌心法 of Shēn Dǒuyuán 申斗垣, slimming it down to 120 figures and integrating his father Zhāng Lù's clinical experience and his own case observations.
