Senior Yuán-period 元 Censorate official, "Central-Plains elegant-tune" *Yuányòu*-style poet. *Zì* **Shìkāi** 士開; posthumous *shì* **Wénzhēn** 文貞. Native of **Dàngshān** 碭山 in the Jiāngběi (north-of-the-Yangtze) region (modern Ānhuī).

**Career.** Zhì-yuán-era recommended as *Jìzhōu jiàoshòu* 冀州教授. Under Tiān-lì-era rose to *Shǎnxī zhūdào xíngtái yùshǐ zhōngchéng* 陜西諸道行臺御史中丞 (Vice-Censor-in-Chief at the Shǎnxī Mobile Censorate).

**Poetic distinction.** A *Jiāngběi*-region poet, identifiable in literary-historical terms as outside the Southern-Sòng *Jiānghú* and Yùzhāng (Jiāngxī) schools dominant in late-Sòng-and-early-Yuán southern verse. The Sìkù editors place him in the *Yuányòu gé* (Yuányòu era 1086–1094 style — of Sū Shì, Huáng Tíngjiān) tradition — *chōngróng xiányǎ* (harmonious-vast, leisurely-and-refined). The "Central-Plains elegant-tune" tradition.

**Collection.** *Cáo Wénzhēn gōng shījí* [[KR4d0467]] in 10 *juàn* — alternative title *Hànquán màngǎo* 漢泉漫藁 — compiled by his son **Cáo Fùhēng** 曹復亨 (*Jiāngnán zhūdào xíngtái guǎngōu*) with the assistance of the *Guózǐjiān* student **Hú Yì** 胡益. Prefaced originally by **Zhāng Mèngchén** 張夢臣, **Ōuyáng Xuán** [[歐陽玄]], **Sū Tiānjué** [[蘇天爵]], and **Lǚ Zhòngshí** 呂仲實. The Quánzhēn Daoist master **Wú Quánjié** 吳全節 supplied the Zhìyuán *wùyín* (1338) *bá*. The *Hòulù* (1 *juàn*) is Cáo Jiàn 曹鑑's imperially-commissioned biographical-memorial supplement.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0467]] *Cáo Wénzhēn gōng shījí* 曹文貞公詩集 (撰).

**Reference.** *Yuánshǐ* j. 176.
