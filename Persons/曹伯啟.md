Senior Yuán-period 元 Censorate official, "Central-Plains elegant-tune" *Yuán-yòu*-style poet. *Zì* **Shì-kāi** 士開; posthumous *shì* **Wén-zhēn** 文貞. Native of **Dàng-shān** 碭山 in the Jiāng-běi (north-of-the-Yangtze) region (modern Ān-huī).

**Career.** Zhì-yuán-era recommended as *Jì-zhōu jiào-shòu* 冀州教授. Under Tiān-lì-era rose to *Shǎn-xī zhū-dào xíng-tái yù-shǐ zhōng-chéng* 陜西諸道行臺御史中丞 (Vice-Censor-in-Chief at the Shǎn-xī Mobile Censorate).

**Poetic distinction.** A *Jiāng-běi*-region poet, identifiable in literary-historical terms as outside the Southern-Sòng *Jiāng-hú* and Yù-zhāng (Jiāng-xī) schools dominant in late-Sòng-and-early-Yuán southern verse. The Sìkù editors place him in the *Yuán-yòu gé* (Yuán-yòu era 1086–1094 style — of Sū Shì, Huáng Tíng-jiān) tradition — *chōng-róng xián-yǎ* (harmonious-vast, leisurely-and-refined). The "Central-Plains elegant-tune" tradition.

**Collection.** *Cáo Wén-zhēn gōng shī-jí* [[KR4d0467]] in 10 *juàn* — alternative title *Hàn-quán màn-gǎo* 漢泉漫藁 — compiled by his son **Cáo Fù-hēng** 曹復亨 (*Jiāng-nán zhū-dào xíng-tái guǎn-gōu*) with the assistance of the *Guó-zǐ-jiān* student **Hú Yì** 胡益. Prefaced originally by **Zhāng Mèng-chén** 張夢臣, **Ōu-yáng Xuán** [[歐陽玄]], **Sū Tiān-jué** [[蘇天爵]], and **Lǚ Zhòng-shí** 呂仲實. The Quán-zhēn Daoist master **Wú Quán-jié** 吳全節 supplied the Zhì-yuán *wù-yín* (1338) *bá*. The *Hòu-lù* (1 *juàn*) is Cáo Jiàn 曹鑑's imperially-commissioned biographical-memorial supplement.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0467]] *Cáo Wén-zhēn gōng shī-jí* 曹文貞公詩集 (撰).

**Reference.** *Yuán-shǐ* j. 176.
