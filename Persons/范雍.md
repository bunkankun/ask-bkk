Northern-Sòng official, *zì* Bóchún 伯純, of *Hénán* 河南 (Luòyáng). *Jìnshì* of Xiánpíng 4 / 1001 (or thereabouts). Held a long career of provincial *zhīzhōu* and central court posts including *Yánzhōu zhīzhōu* (in which capacity he managed the early phase of Sòng-Xī-Xià hostilities), *Lóngtúgé zhíxuéshì*, and *Zīzhèng diàn xuéshì*. Compiled the *Zhōngmǐn jí* [[KR4d0006]] of Kòu Zhǔn 寇準 [[寇準]] in 3 *juǎn* (with substantial additions to the smaller Sòng *Láigōng jí*). CBDB id 593, lifedates 979–1046.
