Northern-Sòng official, *zì* Bó-chún 伯純, of *Hé-nán* 河南 (Luò-yáng). *Jìn-shì* of Xián-píng 4 / 1001 (or thereabouts). Held a long career of provincial *zhī-zhōu* and central court posts including *Yán-zhōu zhī-zhōu* (in which capacity he managed the early phase of Sòng-Xī-Xià hostilities), *Lóng-tú-gé zhí-xué-shì*, and *Zī-zhèng diàn xué-shì*. Compiled the *Zhōng-mǐn jí* [[KR4d0006]] of Kòu Zhǔn 寇準 [[寇準]] in 3 *juǎn* (with substantial additions to the smaller Sòng *Lái-gōng jí*). CBDB id 593, lifedates 979–1046.
