# Dàojīng 道京

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué; one of the section-compilers of juan 8 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
