Chén Xiànzhāng 陳獻章 (1428–1500), *zì* **Gōngfǔ** 公甫, *hào* **Shízhāi** 石齋 (later **Báishā** 白沙 — from his native village Báishālǐ 白沙里). Native of **Xīnhuì** 新會 (Guǎngzhōu region, Guǎngdōng). Pupil of **Wú Yǔbì** 吳與弼 ([[KR4e0122]]); went beyond Wú's orthodox *Zhūzǐxué* into a contemplative, *jìngzuò* (still-sitting meditation) and *zìdé* (self-realization) frame that explicitly anticipates **Wáng Yángmíng**'s 王陽明 *xīnxué* (Heart-Learning). Founder of the **Báishā xuépài** 白沙學派. Entered the *Tàixué* (Imperial Academy); sat for the *Hànlín* examination on **Yáng Pǔ**'s recommendation; achieved no rank beyond *Hànlín jiǎntǎo*; the *xuézhě* (gentleman-scholar) status decisively outweighs the official position. Principal disciple: **Zhàn Ruòshuǐ** 湛若水 ([[湛若水]]), founder of the **Gānquán xuépài**. *Míng shǐ* j. 283 (*Rúlín zhuàn* 1). Surviving works are [[KR4e0108]] *Chén Báishā jí* / *Báishāzǐ* (in Zhàn Ruòshuǐ's vetted recension). CBDB id 29570 (1428–1500).
