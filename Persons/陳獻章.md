Chén Xiàn-zhāng 陳獻章 (1428–1500), *zì* **Gōng-fǔ** 公甫, *hào* **Shí-zhāi** 石齋 (later **Bái-shā** 白沙 — from his native village Bái-shā-lǐ 白沙里). Native of **Xīn-huì** 新會 (Guǎng-zhōu region, Guǎng-dōng). Pupil of **Wú Yǔ-bì** 吳與弼 ([[KR4e0122]]); went beyond Wú's orthodox *Zhū-zǐ-xué* into a contemplative, *jìng-zuò* (still-sitting meditation) and *zì-dé* (self-realization) frame that explicitly anticipates **Wáng Yáng-míng**'s 王陽明 *xīn-xué* (Heart-Learning). Founder of the **Bái-shā xué-pài** 白沙學派. Entered the *Tài-xué* (Imperial Academy); sat for the *Hàn-lín* examination on **Yáng Pǔ**'s recommendation; achieved no rank beyond *Hàn-lín jiǎn-tǎo*; the *xué-zhě* (gentleman-scholar) status decisively outweighs the official position. Principal disciple: **Zhàn Ruò-shuǐ** 湛若水 ([[湛若水]]), founder of the **Gān-quán xué-pài**. *Míng shǐ* j. 283 (*Rú-lín zhuàn* 1). Surviving works are [[KR4e0108]] *Chén Bái-shā jí* / *Bái-shā-zǐ* (in Zhàn Ruò-shuǐ's vetted recension). CBDB id 29570 (1428–1500).
