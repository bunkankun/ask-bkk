Myŏng-hyo / Míng-xiǎo 明皛 (active mid-Silla period, 503–935 broad bracket per DILA A000670), Korean Hwaeom-school scholar-monk; author of *[[KR6e0113|Hǎi yìn sānmèi lùn 海印三昧論]]* (T1889), the *Treatise on the Ocean-Seal Samādhi* — a meditation-doctrinal treatise on the *Avataṃsaka*'s key contemplative state, the *sāgaramudrā-samādhi* / "ocean-seal *samādhi*" (海印三昧). His birth and death dates are not preserved.
