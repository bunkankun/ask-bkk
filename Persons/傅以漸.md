Fù Yǐjiàn 傅以漸 (1609–1665), *zì* Yúqìng 於磬, *hào* Xīngyán 星巖, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was the first *zhuàngyuán* of the Qīng dynasty (*Shùnzhì* 3 / 1646). Native of Niánchéng 聊城 (Shāndōng). Rose to *Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì* and *Bīngbù shàngshū*. Compiled (under Shùnzhì's direction) the *Yù dìng nèizé yǎnyì* 御定內則衍義 ([[KR3a0106]]). Co-composed (with Jiǎng Hèdé) the *hòu xù* of the *Yù dìng zīzhèng yào lǎn* ([[KR3a0101]]). CBDB id 59084.
