Fù Yǐ-jiàn 傅以漸 (1609–1665), *zì* Yúqìng 於磬, *hào* Xīngyán 星巖, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was the first *zhuàng-yuán* of the Qīng dynasty (*Shùnzhì* 3 / 1646). Native of Nián-chéng 聊城 (Shān-dōng). Rose to *Wǔ-yīng-diàn dà-xué-shì* and *Bīng-bù shàng-shū*. Compiled (under Shùnzhì's direction) the *Yù dìng nèi-zé yǎn-yì* 御定內則衍義 (KR3a0106). Co-composed (with Jiǎng Hè-dé) the *hòu xù* of the *Yù dìng zī-zhèng yào lǎn* (KR3a0101). CBDB id 59084.
