# Dàofù 道覆

Early Chinese Vinaya master, listed as third patriarch in [[懷顯]] Huáixiǎn's five-patriarch *Sìfēn lǜ* scheme (*Lǜzōng xīnxué míngjù* [[KR6k0255]]): *Fǎzhèng* 法正 → [[法聰]] Fǎcōng → *Dàofù* 道覆 → [[智首]] Zhìshǒu → [[道宣]] Nánshān (Dàoxuān). Conventionally placed in the *Sìfēn lǜ* lineage stemming from the early Northern translators. No independent biography preserved.
