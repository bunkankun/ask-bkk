# 唐養愚

Táng Yǎngyú (sobriquet Láiyúnzǐ 來雲子)

Late-Qīng layman who supplied a sponsoring preface to [[謝承謨]] Xiè Chéngmó's 1889 *Jīngāng jīng yìjiě* ([[KR6c0098]]).
