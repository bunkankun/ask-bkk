Guō Yuán-fǔ 郭元釪 (d. 1722, *zì* Zǐ-shàng 子尚), of Jiāng-dū 江都 (Yáng-zhōu prefecture, Jiāng-sū). Early-Kāngxī scholar, principal compiler of the *Quán Jīn shī* 全金詩 — the imperial supplement to Yuán Hǎo-wèn's *Zhōng-zhōu jí* 中州集. The Kāngxī preface to [[KR4h0144]] *Yù-dìng Quán Jīn shī zēng-bǔ Zhōng-zhōu jí* records Guō's own *zòu-zhé* (memorial): in his youth at home he was fond of poetry, read Yuán Hǎo-wèn's *Zhōng-zhōu jí*, and noted its incomplete coverage. From his middle years he gathered Jīn poems missing from the *Zhōng-zhōu jí*, drawing on *jùn-xiàn zhì* (prefectural-county gazetteers) — finally producing a *quán Jīn shī* draft on his own. He submitted this to the Kāngxī court, which ordered it formally edited under imperial supervision; the resulting *Yù-dìng Quán Jīn shī* (1711) is the standard imperial Jīn-poetry compilation, vastly larger than Yuán Hǎo-wèn's original (6 times the *juǎn*-count, 3 times the poet-count, 2 times the poem-count). The Kāngxī edict explicitly **retained Guō Yuán-fǔ's name** at the head of the table-of-contents — the *Sìkù tíyào* notes this as a mark of imperial-magnanimity. Guō also participated in the *Yù-xuǎn Sòng-Jīn-Yuán-Míng sì-cháo shī* [[KR4h0143]] editorial team — though there as one *lù-xuǎn guān* among many.
