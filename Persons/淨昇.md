Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A001094. Productive in the early-to-mid Kāngxī 康熙 era. Compiler (集 *jí*) of the **Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng yīnyì** 法華經大成音義 ([[KR6d0086]], X32n0620, 1 *juan*) — a phonetic-and-meaning glossary to the *Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng* tradition. The preface by **Zhìchōng** 智沖 dates the work to Kāngxī *jiǎshēn* 康熙甲申 (= 1704 CE).

Source: *Dàchéng yīnyì xù* 大成音義序 (preface, 1704); DILA A001094.
