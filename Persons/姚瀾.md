**Yáo Lán** 姚瀾 (fl. early 19th c.), *zì* Wéizhōu 維舟, *hào* Róngpǔ 蓉浦. Mid-Qīng physician of Cíxī 慈谿 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Author of the *Běncǎo fēn jīng* 本草分經 ([[KR3ec060]]) — a working pharmacopoeia organised entirely by *channel-entry* (歸經): substances are classified into chapters for each of the twelve channels and the *qí jīng* eight extraordinary vessels, so that a physician treating a specific-channel pathology can read off the appropriate substances in sequence. The work was printed in 道光二十年 (1840). Yáo also compiled the *Yīxué chá jiǎn* 醫學查鑑.
