Lù Guǎngwēi 陸廣微 (fl. late 9th c.) is the traditional attributed author of the *Wú dì jì* ([[KR2k0091]]), a 1-juan early-Sòng gazetteer of the Sūzhōu region. The Sìkù tíyào identifies the surviving text as not by Guǎngwēi (it presupposes a post-978 Sòng viewpoint) but as a later reconstruction incorporating fragments of his lost original; he himself is otherwise unattested. The *Sòngshǐ Yìwén-zhì* records the *Wú dì jì* in 1 *juan*. CBDB has no entry under this name.
