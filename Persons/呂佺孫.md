# 呂佺孫 Lǚ Quánsūn

Mid-Qīng official, governor of Fújiàn during the Xiánfēng era; wrote the 1856 (咸豐六年) preface to and funded the printing of [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ's *Shānghán yī jué chuàn jiě* ([[KR3ef066]]).
