**Okada Masaharu** 岡田昌春 (*zì* Jūkoku 柔克), early-Meiji Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician. Staff physician of the imperially-chartered Hakusaidō 博濟堂 beriberi hospital in Tokyo from its founding by official charter in the 8th month of Meiji 11 (1878). Co-author with Kiyokawa Gendō [[清川玄道]] of [[KR3eh054]] *Hakusaidō Kakke gairon* 博濟堂腳氣概論 (1879), the institutional clinical manual that summarizes the *kanpō* response to the early-Meiji beriberi epidemic. He signs the joint preface of *Meiji* 12 / 5 (May 1879). No detailed biographical material located in standard *kanpō* prosopography; not in CBDB.
