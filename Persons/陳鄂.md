Chén È 陳鄂 (fl. late 10th c.; CBDB id 533430), held the office *Tàizǐ zhōngshè* 太子中舎 at the time of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* commission of 977 and was one of the fourteen named compilers. No independent biography survives.
