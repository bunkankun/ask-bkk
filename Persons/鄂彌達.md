Émídá 鄂彌達 (d. 1761), early-Qiánlóng-period official who served as Governor-General of the Two Guǎng. Cut the blocks for the posthumous edition of his former superior [[朱軾]] Zhū Shì's *Zhōuyì zhuàn yì hé dìng* ([[KR1a0142]]); the resulting edition received an imperial preface from the Qiánlóng emperor in 1737.
