Huáng Dàozhōu 黃道周 (1585–1646), *zì* Yòupíng 幼平 / Yòuxuán 幼玄, *hào* Shízhāi 石齋, posthumously *Zhōngduān* 忠端, was a major Late-Míng *Lǐxué* polymath, *Yìjīng* specialist, and Míng-loyalist martyr. Native of Zhāng-pǔ 漳浦 (Fú-jiàn). *Jìnshì* of *Tiān-qǐ rén-xū* (1622); rose to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* under the Lóng-wǔ régime in 1646. After the fall of Fú-jiàn to the Qīng, he was captured by Qīng forces and executed at Nán-jīng — refusing to surrender. His celebrated *Yì xiàng zhèng* 易象正 in the SKQS is the major Late-Míng *Yìjīng*-numerology work. The present *Róng tán wèn yè* 榕壇問業 (KR3a0099, 18 juan) is his lecturing-record from the *Róng tán* lecture-hall at Pǔ-běi-shān during his exile (1632–1635) and immediately afterward (1636). Major works also: the *Sān-yì dòng-jí* 三易洞璣, *Hóng-fàn míng-yì* 洪範明義, *Tài-hán jīng* 太函經, *Bó-wù diǎn-huì* 博物典彙, plus the two great *Yì*-numerology works. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 255.
