Chéng 誠, *hào* **Qǐ-zhēn** 啟真. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hù-guó Biàn-zhī** 護國遍知 (= **Bié-fēng Xiù** 別峰秀, the *Wǔ-zǔ* fifth-patriarch lineage). Native of **Jīng-mén-zhōu Lǐ-yán** 荊門州里閈; lay surname **Xǔ** 許. Lifedates not preserved.

**Abbacy.** **Jīng-nán Nèi Hù-guó-sì** 荊南內護國寺.

**Work.** [[KR6q0520]] 《荊南內護國寺啟真誠禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B395, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhèn-wén** [[振聞]] *biān*; juan 3 *Ēn-mǔ chuán* 恩母傳.
