SòngYuán transition 元 Wúzhōng (Sūzhōu) gentry-recluse-poet. *Zì* **Tōngfǔ** 通甫; *hào* **Jìngchūn** 靜春 ("Quiet-Spring"). Native of **Chángzhōu** 長洲 (Sūzhōu, modern Jiāngsū). CBDB 35238 records 1262–1306.

**Retreat-pattern.** Refused official advancement (*bù qiú shìjìn*). Declined the *Bù shǐzhě* (Ministry Commissioner) recommendation to the imperial court. Briefly held the *Huīzhōufǔ Shídòng shūyuàn shānzhǎng* (Huīzhōu Stone-Cave Academy Headship); soon resigned and returned home. Built the *Jìngchūntáng* between the Wúsōng River and Jùqū (Tàihú); maintained a 10,000-*juàn* personally-collated home library; occasionally travelled in a small skiff carrying writing-board, tea-stove, and ancient instruments along the rivers and lakes.

**Wúzhōng sān jūnzǐ.** Honored by **Zhào Mèngfǔ** [[趙孟頫]] together with **Gōng Sù** [[龔璛]] [[KR4d0457]] and **Guō Línsūn** 郭麟孫 as the **Wúzhōng sān jūnzǐ** 吳中三君子 (Three Gentlemen of Wúzhōng) — Zhào painted Yuán Yì's *Wòxuě tú* (Lying-in-Snow Picture) commemorating this.

**Poetic style.** Sìkù editors place him in the **Chén Yǔyì** 陳與義 (Chén Qùfēi 陳去非, 1090–1138) lineage — *tǔyán tiānbá* (spit-out-words, heaven-elevated). Distinct from both Huáng Tíngjiān's *róngzhù chánxuē* and Chén Shīdào's *shēnkè shòuyìng* styles (against which Lì È's later *bá* incorrectly compared him).

**Reception.** Gōng Sù 龔璛 composed the principal preface in Yányòu 4 (1317), 11 years after Yuán's death. Lì È 厲鶚 (Qīng-era) supplied a later *bá*. Wú Nè 吳訥 (MíngZhèngtǒng era, 1436–1449) added an admiring postscript.
