Sòng-Yuán transition 元 Wú-zhōng (Sū-zhōu) gentry-recluse-poet. *Zì* **Tōng-fǔ** 通甫; *hào* **Jìng-chūn** 靜春 ("Quiet-Spring"). Native of **Cháng-zhōu** 長洲 (Sū-zhōu, modern Jiāng-sū). CBDB 35238 records 1262–1306.

**Retreat-pattern.** Refused official advancement (*bù qiú shì-jìn*). Declined the *Bù shǐ-zhě* (Ministry Commissioner) recommendation to the imperial court. Briefly held the *Huī-zhōu-fǔ Shí-dòng shū-yuàn shān-zhǎng* (Huī-zhōu Stone-Cave Academy Headship); soon resigned and returned home. Built the *Jìng-chūn-táng* between the Wú-sōng River and Jù-qū (Tài-hú); maintained a 10,000-*juàn* personally-collated home library; occasionally travelled in a small skiff carrying writing-board, tea-stove, and ancient instruments along the rivers and lakes.

**Wú-zhōng sān jūn-zǐ.** Honored by **Zhào Mèng-fǔ** [[趙孟頫]] together with **Gōng Sù** [[龔璛]] [[KR4d0457]] and **Guō Lín-sūn** 郭麟孫 as the **Wú-zhōng sān jūn-zǐ** 吳中三君子 (Three Gentlemen of Wú-zhōng) — Zhào painted Yuán Yì's *Wò-xuě tú* (Lying-in-Snow Picture) commemorating this.

**Poetic style.** Sìkù editors place him in the **Chén Yǔ-yì** 陳與義 (Chén Qù-fēi 陳去非, 1090–1138) lineage — *tǔ-yán tiān-bá* (spit-out-words, heaven-elevated). Distinct from both Huáng Tíng-jiān's *róng-zhù chán-xuē* and Chén Shī-dào's *shēn-kè shòu-yìng* styles (against which Lì È's later *bá* incorrectly compared him).

**Reception.** Gōng Sù 龔璛 composed the principal preface in Yán-yòu 4 (1317), 11 years after Yuán's death. Lì È 厲鶚 (Qīng-era) supplied a later *bá*. Wú Nè 吳訥 (Míng-Zhèng-tǒng era, 1436–1449) added an admiring postscript.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0488]] *Jìng-chūn-táng shī-jí* 靜春堂詩集 (撰).
