# 馬鳴

Mǎmíng 馬鳴 (\*Aśvaghoṣa, c. 80–150 CE), Indian Buddhist poet, dramatist, and *bodhisattva*-class master. Traditionally listed as the first patriarch in the Huáyán seven-patriarch genealogy. See [[馬鳴菩薩]] for the fuller entry.
