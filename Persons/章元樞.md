Southern-Sòng 南宋 Daoist scholar-hagiographer, *fl.* 1180. Author of the original *[[KR5c0174|Huá gài shān Fú qiū Wáng Guō sān zhēn jūn shì shí]]* (DZ 779) — a regional hagiographic chronicle of three Daoist immortals associated with Mt Huá gài 華蓋山 (Jiāng xī 江西).

**Activity.** 1180 (attested). No precise dates. No CBDB record identified.

**Work.** [[KR5c0174|DZ 779]] — subsequently revised and extended by Huáng Míjiān, Liú Xiáng, Wáng Kèmíng, and ultimately in 1407 (Míng Yǒng lè era).
