# Chén Jìnmó 陳藎謨

Style name *Xiànkě* 獻可, sobriquet *Zàigōng* 在公. Native of Xiùshuǐ 秀水 (Jiāxìng 嘉興 prefecture, Zhèjiāng). *Fl.* late Wànlì through Chóngzhēn (c. 1610–c. 1650). Birth and death years not securely recorded; CBDB id 131036 confirms the name and dynasty but supplies no dates.

A late-Míng mathematical scholar specializing in surveying-and-instrumentation and root-extraction methodology. His mathematical career was in the immediate aftermath of the early Jesuit-translation period (after Ricci, Schreck, Schall) but largely outside the Beijing-based Jesuit collaborative circle of [[徐光啟]] and [[李之藻]]. His mathematical work belongs instead to the lower-Yangzi independent tradition that absorbed and re-presented the imported European mathematical content for a wider Chinese audience.

His principal surviving works:

(1) **The *Dùcè* 度測** ([[KR3fc030]]) in 6 *juàn* — the *survey-and-measurement* treatise.

(2) **The *Dùsuàn jiě* 度算解** ([[KR3fc031]]) — supplementary commentary on the *Dùcè*'s computational procedures, treating the proportional-divider instrument (the *bǐlìguī* 比例規 / Galilean *compasso proporzionale*).

(3) **The *Kāipíngfāng shuō* 開平方說** ([[KR3fc032]]) — exposition of the square-root extraction procedure.

(4) **The *Kāilìfāng shuō* 開立方說** ([[KR3fc033]]) — exposition of the cube-root extraction procedure.

Through these works, Chén Jìnmó systematizes a particular aspect of late-Míng / Chóng-zhēn-era mathematical pedagogy: the integration of imported European instrument-based surveying methods (the proportional-divider, the *jiǎnpíng yí* 簡平儀 simple-flat astrolabe, etc.) with the indigenous *kāifāng* (root-extraction) computational tradition. His expositions are presented as practical pedagogy for the working mathematician, not as theoretical innovation, but they document the mid-Chóng-zhēn level of integrated Sino-European mathematical practice. The series was repeatedly cited in Qīng mathematical works, including those of [[梅文鼎]] Méi Wéndǐng, who treated Chén Jìnmó as an important late-Míng predecessor.
