**Liú Wēnshū** 劉溫舒 was a Northern-Sòng medical scholar, author of [[KR5d0044]] *Sù wèn rù shì yùn qì lùn ào* 素問入式運氣論奧 (1099) — a key early-medieval treatise on the **Five Movements and Six Pneumas** (*wǔ yùn liù qì* 五運六氣) cosmological-medical system from Wáng Bīng's supplementary chapters of the *Sù wèn*.
