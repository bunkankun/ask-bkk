# Chén Guìxīn 陳桂馨

Late-Qīng physician of Gānquán 甘泉 (Yángzhōu, Jiāngsū); preface-writer to [[KR3ee054]] *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù* (1888) in the late-Qīng Jiāngnán pediatric-medicine network around [[張筱衫]] Zhāng Xiǎoshān.
