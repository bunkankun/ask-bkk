**Zhū Zuǒ** 朱佐, *zì* **Jūnfǔ** 君輔 (*fl.* mid-late 13th c.). Southern-Sòng physician of Méishān 眉山 (Sìchuān), kinsman of the kinsman-prefacer Zhū Jǐngxíng 朱景行. Author of the *Lèibiān Zhūshì jíyàn yīfāng* 類編朱氏集驗醫方 ([[KR3ed030]]), 15 *juǎn*, compiled *xiánchún* era (1265–1266). The Zhū family of Méishān is a documented late-Southern-Sòng literati-medical lineage, the same prefecture as Sū Shì and Sū Zhé. Zhū Zuǒ is not in CBDB; no biographical record outside his own work.
