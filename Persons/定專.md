Jōsen 定專, fifth abbot of the **Senju-ji** 専修寺 (head temple of the **Takada** 高田 branch of Jōdo Shinshū, in Mie). Active in the **early to mid 14th c.**; precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works. He succeeded the fourth abbot **Junshō** 順性 and was succeeded by the sixth abbot. His chief surviving work is the *Ji-yō-shū* [[KR6t0383]], a doctrinal commonplace-book whose autograph colophon (preserved in [[眞慧]] **Shin'e**'s annotation, *Enbun 3 / 1358*) is the *terminus ante quem* for his life. The DILA authority id is `A001243`. He is one of the principal figures in the **Senju-ji scholastic line** which preserved and developed the Shinran tradition outside the rival Hongan-ji branch.
