Mid-to-late Yuán 元 Daoist scholar, *hào* **Qián yáng zǐ** 潛陽子 ("Master of the Hidden *Yáng*"). Active c. 1334 as the **corrector / collator** (*jiào zhèng* 校正) of [[徐道齡]] Xú Dàolíng's *Tài shàng xuán líng Běi dǒu běn mìng yán shēng zhēn jīng zhù* ([[KR5c0146|DZ 750]], printed 1334).

**Relationship to Xú Dàolíng.** The shared Daoist *dào* 道 generation-name of the two men (*Dàolíng* 道齡, *Dàoxuán* 道玄) strongly suggests same-lineage affiliation — probably **fellow disciples** of the same Daoist master, or **lineage-brothers**. Together they produced the DZ 750 commentary as a collaborative project, with Xú Dàolíng as primary author and Xú Dàoxuán as corrector.

**Activity.** Attested 1334 (the publication date of DZ 750). No lifedates or other biographical details. No CBDB record identified.

**Disambiguation.** The *hào* **Qián yáng zǐ** 潛陽子 distinguishes this Xú Dàoxuán from other Xú Dào- names in Yuán-era Daoist sources.
