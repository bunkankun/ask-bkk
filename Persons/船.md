# Chuán 船 (Zǐzhōu Chāochuán 梓舟超船)

DILA-standardized **Chāochuán** 超船 (catalog meta uses the bare single-character 船), *hào* **Zǐzhōu** 梓舟. Born **己未 3/16 辰時 = 1619-04-29 Gregorian**; native of **Tóngchuānzhōu** 童川州 (= 潼川州) in **Chéngdūfǔ** 成都府 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Hé** 何. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Èryǐn Bǎoān Mì** 二隱寶安謐 at **Jīngxī Bǎoānsì** 荊溪保安寺.

**Formation.** **庚辰 = 1640**: tonsured under **Yìngchūn héshàng** 映春和尚 at **Báiliánān** 白蓮庵. **壬午 = 1642**: full precepts at **Rónghuáshān** 榮華山, where he prostrated through the *Fǎhuá* and *Huáyán* sūtras for three years (*xié bù zhì xí* 脅不至席). Late-Míng warfare-disruption and famine; began *Guānyīn* invocation; traveled to **Nánhǎi** and **Bǎoníngfǔ** 保寧府. **順治二 = 1645**: pilgrimage circuit to **Cuìyán** 翠巖 (Jiāngxī), **Yíxīng** 宜興 to meet **Wànrú Tōngwēi** [[通微]], **Húzhōu Bàoēnsì** to meet **Yùlín Tōngxiù** 玉林通琇, **Jìngshān** to meet **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容, **Tiāntóng** to meet **Mùyún Tōngmén** 牧雲通門, **Língyǐn** to meet **Jùdé Hónglǐ** 具德弘禮, **Tiāntóng** to meet **Mùchén Dàomín** [[道忞]], **Sāntǎ** to meet **Zìxián** 自閒 — total nine years (*bìng zhū dàlǎo gòng jiǔ zǎi* 并諸大老共九載). The pilgrimage embraces **virtually every major Mì-yún-line successor** of the period.

**Two-yǐn transmission and abbacies.** Final teacher **Èryǐn Mì** 二隱謐 at **Huátíng Chuánzǐ Fǎrěnsì** 華亭船子法忍寺 — four years with frequent *tòngbàng* 痛棒 beatings. Three-year *yǐnguān* 隱關 closed retreat at **Gǔxīnān** 古心庵 with *tányuè* lay-patron **Wánggōng** 王公. **庚子 = 1660**: summoned by Èryǐn at **Jīngxī Bǎoānsì** 荊溪保安寺; on the *chūguān* 出關 question Chuán's reply *pò* 破 ("smashed") prompted Èryǐn to formally transmit *fú* whisk and verse, appointing him *dūjiān* 都監. Retreat to **Lúshān** 廬山 after the master's death; subsequent abbacies at **Xiāngzhōu Lùménsì** 襄州鹿門寺 (Húběi, 2 years) and **Tánxī gǔsì** 檀溪古寺 (which he restored, hence the title-form 壇溪梓舟船 / 檀溪梓舟船).

**Work.** [[KR6q0464]] 《壇溪梓舟船禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B281, compiled by *ménrén* **Míngfǎ** [[明法]] *děng*, with front-preface by **Zhāng Lián / Tōngshēng** 武張聯法名通陞 (lay-Chán figure who met Chuán in **甲辰 = 1664** at **Lùméntān** 鹿門灘 and again in **丙午 = 1666** at **Wǔ-Hàn**), framing Chuán as *Nánzōng Běizōng hé yī yě* 南宗北宗合一也, *yī yè wǔ huā huì yuán yě* 一葉五花匯源也 — ie. uniting the Línjì and Cáodòng currents.

Death-date not located. Sources: [[KR6q0464]] juan 3 *xíngshí*; DILA authority A019256 (cross-listed from 如璽 entry).
