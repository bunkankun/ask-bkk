# Chuán 船 (Zǐ-zhōu Chāo-chuán 梓舟超船)

DILA-standardized **Chāo-chuán** 超船 (catalog meta uses the bare single-character 船), *hào* **Zǐ-zhōu** 梓舟. Born **己未 3/16 辰時 = 1619-04-29 Gregorian**; native of **Tóng-chuān-zhōu** 童川州 (= 潼川州) in **Chéng-dū-fǔ** 成都府 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Hé** 何. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Èr-yǐn Bǎo-ān Mì** 二隱寶安謐 at **Jīng-xī Bǎo-ān-sì** 荊溪保安寺.

**Formation.** **庚辰 = 1640**: tonsured under **Yìng-chūn héshàng** 映春和尚 at **Bái-lián-ān** 白蓮庵. **壬午 = 1642**: full precepts at **Róng-huá-shān** 榮華山, where he prostrated through the *Fǎ-huá* and *Huá-yán* sūtras for three years (*xié bù zhì xí* 脅不至席). Late-Míng warfare-disruption and famine; began *Guān-yīn* invocation; traveled to **Nán-hǎi** and **Bǎo-níng-fǔ** 保寧府. **順治二 = 1645**: pilgrimage circuit to **Cuì-yán** 翠巖 (Jiāng-xī), **Yí-xīng** 宜興 to meet **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** [[通微]], **Hú-zhōu Bào-ēn-sì** to meet **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** 玉林通琇, **Jìng-shān** to meet **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容, **Tiān-tóng** to meet **Mù-yún Tōng-mén** 牧雲通門, **Líng-yǐn** to meet **Jù-dé Hóng-lǐ** 具德弘禮, **Tiān-tóng** to meet **Mù-chén Dào-mín** [[道忞]], **Sān-tǎ** to meet **Zì-xián** 自閒 — total nine years (*bìng zhū dà-lǎo gòng jiǔ zǎi* 并諸大老共九載). The pilgrimage embraces **virtually every major Mì-yún-line successor** of the period.

**Two-yǐn transmission and abbacies.** Final teacher **Èr-yǐn Mì** 二隱謐 at **Huá-tíng Chuán-zǐ Fǎ-rěn-sì** 華亭船子法忍寺 — four years with frequent *tòng-bàng* 痛棒 beatings. Three-year *yǐn-guān* 隱關 closed retreat at **Gǔ-xīn-ān** 古心庵 with *tán-yuè* lay-patron **Wáng-gōng** 王公. **庚子 = 1660**: summoned by Èr-yǐn at **Jīng-xī Bǎo-ān-sì** 荊溪保安寺; on the *chū-guān* 出關 question Chuán's reply *pò* 破 ("smashed") prompted Èr-yǐn to formally transmit *fú* whisk and verse, appointing him *dū-jiān* 都監. Retreat to **Lú-shān** 廬山 after the master's death; subsequent abbacies at **Xiāng-zhōu Lù-mén-sì** 襄州鹿門寺 (Hú-běi, 2 years) and **Tán-xī gǔ-sì** 檀溪古寺 (which he restored, hence the title-form 壇溪梓舟船 / 檀溪梓舟船).

**Work.** [[KR6q0464]] 《壇溪梓舟船禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B281, compiled by *mén-rén* **Míng-fǎ** [[明法]] *děng*, with front-preface by **Zhāng Lián / Tōng-shēng** 武張聯法名通陞 (lay-Chán figure who met Chuán in **甲辰 = 1664** at **Lù-mén-tān** 鹿門灘 and again in **丙午 = 1666** at **Wǔ-Hàn**), framing Chuán as *Nán-zōng Běi-zōng hé yī yě* 南宗北宗合一也, *yī yè wǔ huā huì yuán yě* 一葉五花匯源也 — ie. uniting the Línjì and Cáo-dòng currents.

Death-date not located. Sources: [[KR6q0464]] juan 3 *xíng-shí*; DILA authority A019256 (cross-listed from 如璽 entry).
