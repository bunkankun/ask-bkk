# Guǎngguì 廣貴

Late-Míng Pure Land monk active in the late Wànlì or early Tiānqǐ period; identified in the colophons of his work as **Yùfēng Guǎngguì** 玉峰廣貴, suggesting an affiliation with **Yù-fēng** 玉峰 (Jade Peak) — likely Yù-fēng-shān in modern Kūnshān 崑山 (Jiāngsū) or possibly Yùfēng-shān in Zhèjiāng. Lifedates not preserved. His major surviving work is the **《蓮邦詩選》** *Lián-bāng shī-xuǎn* [[KR6p0072]] (X1207, 1 *juǎn*), an anthology of Pure Land devotional verse — *zàn* 讚, *jié* 偈, and *shī* 詩 — drawn from across the Chinese Pure Land tradition. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
