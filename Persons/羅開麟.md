Luó Kāilín 羅開麟 (also written 羅開驎), early-Qīng Jiā-xīng-region lay-disciple. Listed in the *zhòng wénxué qǐngqǐ* 眾文學請啟 invitation-memorial of [[KR6q0456]] (j. 1) as one of about thirty *fǎmò* 法末 invitation-petitioners requesting **Tǎnān Jījùn** [[機峻]] 坦菴機峻 to take up **Pǔguāngsì** 普光寺 in **1670/5**. The catalog meta of [[KR6q0456]] credits Luó Kāilín with *xù* 序 (preface) authorship; the actually-signed front-preface in the surviving Jiāxīng Canon imprint is, however, signed *Kōngzhū Luó Zhēnxìng* 空諸羅真性 (康熙庚戌孟秋丙辰 = 1670/8/15) — possibly the same Luó family-circle. No further biographical information located.
