**Hè Lóngxiāng** 賀龍驤 (*fl.* 1880s–1900s, late Qīng / early Republican), Sìchuān-based Daoist scholar-publisher. Issued a substantial series of Daoist anthologies in the late *Guāng-xù* and early-Republican periods, including the *Nǚdān hé-biān xuǎn-zhù* 女丹合編選註 ([[KR3ei036]]) — a four-*juǎn* anthology of *nǚ-dān* (female internal-alchemy) texts. Hè's publication programme was part of a broader late-Qīng / early-Republican revival of *nǚ-dān* internal-alchemical practice in Sìchuān Daoist networks. No further biographical detail located in standard reference works.
