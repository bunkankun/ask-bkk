# Zhēnqǐ 真啟

Late-Míng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[圓悟|Mìyún Yuánwù]] 密雲圓悟 at Tiāntóng-sì 天童寺. Lifedates unrecorded. Editor of Yuánwù's 1638 polemical treatise *Pì wàng jiù lüè shuō* 闢妄救略說 [[KR6q0168]]; identified in the text's juan-headers as *shìzhě Zhēnqǐ biān* 侍者真啟編 ("edited by the attendant Zhēnqǐ"). No independent biographical material survives beyond his editorial role in this text.

Distinct from the later Qīng Chán monk 天岩真啟 (DILA A041631), *hào* Hǎiyuè 海月 — a separate person.
