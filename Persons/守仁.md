# Shǒurén 守仁

Northern Sòng Vinaya scholar of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). One of the four major Sòng Vinaya scholars whose patriarchate-lineage tabulations are preserved at the close of *juàn xià* of [[懷顯]] Huáixiǎn's *Lǜzōng xīnxué míngjù* 律宗新學名句 ([[KR6k0255]], X1107, 1094). Shǒurén's enumeration gives a seven-patriarch scheme ending with the *Zēnghuī jìzhǔ* 增輝記主. His own writings are not extant; he is known through the patriarchate-list and through quotation in later Sòng Vinaya scholasticism.
