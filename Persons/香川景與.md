**Kagawa Shukugen** 香川景與 (Kagawa Shūan 香川修庵 / 修菴, 1745–1803), Edo-period Japanese *koihō* 古醫方 physician and grandson of the school-founder Kagawa Shūtoku 香川修徳. Authority cited by [[岡田昌春]] Okada Masaharu in [[KR3eh054]] for dating the resurgence of Japanese *kakke* to the Hōreki era (1751–1764).
