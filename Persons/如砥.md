# Rúdǐ 如砥

Yuán-dynasty Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Píngshí 平石 ("Level-Stone"), *zì* Shípíng 石平. Per DILA A009007: death Zhìzhèng 17 (1357). Eighteenth-generation descendant of Línjì via Wújùn Shīfàn → Xīyán [[了慧]] Liǎohuì → Dōngyán Jìngrì 東巖淨日 (1221–1308); dharma-heir of Dōngyán. Abbacies: Qìngyuán Bǎoshèng 保聖 (1299), Dìngshuǐsì 定水禪寺 (1313), Tiāntóng 天童 (1329). Dharma-heirs include Zhēnrú Wénqī 真如文栖 and Wúzuò Wénshù 無作文述. Recorded sayings: *Píngshí Rúdǐ chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]), compiled and printed by his disciples c. 1366.
