# Rú-dǐ 如砥

Yuán-dynasty Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Píng-shí 平石 ("Level-Stone"), *zì* Shí-píng 石平. Per DILA A009007: death Zhì-zhèng 17 (1357). Eighteenth-generation descendant of Línjì via Wújùn Shīfàn → Xī-yán [[了慧]] Liǎo-huì → Dōng-yán Jìng-rì 東巖淨日 (1221–1308); dharma-heir of Dōng-yán. Abbacies: Qìngyuán Bǎo-shèng 保聖 (1299), Dìng-shuǐ-sì 定水禪寺 (1313), Tiān-tóng 天童 (1329). Dharma-heirs include Zhēn-rú Wén-qī 真如文栖 and Wú-zuò Wén-shù 無作文述. Recorded sayings: *Píng-shí Rú-dǐ chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]), compiled and printed by his disciples c. 1366.
