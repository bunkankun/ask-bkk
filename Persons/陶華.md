**Táo Huá** 陶華 (*zì* Shàngwén 尚文, *hào* Jiéān 節庵, conventional lifedates ca. 1369 – ca. 1463, 明), early-Míng Hángzhōu physician. His magnum opus is the *Shānghán liù shū* 傷寒六書 ([[KR3ef038]], 6 juan, 1445) — a compendium of six independent *Shānghán* treatises that became the standard Míng-period clinical reference on cold-damage before the Fāng Yǒuzhí / Yú Chāng revisionist movement of the late 16th century. Táo also wrote a *Yī liào yān zhōng dì jiè* 醫療巖中堤頦 (rare) and clinical case-records (lost). Conventional lifedates 1369–1463 are widely cited but not securely documented. No CBDB record.
