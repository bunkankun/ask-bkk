Hé Xīngpǔ 何星浦 (fl. mid-19th c.) was the grand-nephew of [[何彤園]] Hé Tóngyuán who, together with [[何靜安]], published the *Dàfāngmài* ([[KR3eh020]]) posthumously in 1855.
