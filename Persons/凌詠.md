# 凌詠

Líng Yǒng 凌詠 (號 Yǒngyán Yīsǒu 永言醫叟), of Wúxīng. Late-Qīng / early-Republican river-engineer turned medical-text custodian who surrendered the manuscript of *Qiānlǐ yīàn* ([[KR3ep093]]) to [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jíshēng for publication in 1920.
