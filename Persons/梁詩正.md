Mid-Qīng official, *zì* Yǎngzhòng 養仲, *hào* Xiānglín 薌林, of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hangzhou). *jìnshì* Yōngzhèng 8 (1730), 探花 (third place); rose through the Hànlín to *Dōnggé Dàxuéshì* and *Bīngbù shàngshū* under Qiánlóng. Posthumous title 文莊. CBDB cbdbId 58587, lifedates 1697–1763. Distinguished calligrapher (his small-*kǎi* style was a Qián-lóng-court favourite). The principal-named compiler of the imperial *Qīndìng xiéyùn huìjí* [[KR1j0076]] (1750), Qiánlóng's compendium of cross-rhyme attestation. Also served as supervisor on multiple other Qián-lóng-era imperial scholarly projects.
