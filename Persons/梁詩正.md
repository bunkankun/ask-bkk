Mid-Qīng official, *zì* Yǎng-zhòng 養仲, *hào* Xiāng-lín 薌林, of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Hangzhou). *jìn-shì* Yōng-zhèng 8 (1730), 探花 (third place); rose through the Hàn-lín to *Dōng-gé Dà-xué-shì* and *Bīng-bù shàng-shū* under Qián-lóng. Posthumous title 文莊. CBDB cbdbId 58587, lifedates 1697–1763. Distinguished calligrapher (his small-*kǎi* style was a Qián-lóng-court favourite). The principal-named compiler of the imperial *Qīn-dìng xié-yùn huì-jí* [[KR1j0076]] (1750), Qián-lóng's compendium of cross-rhyme attestation. Also served as supervisor on multiple other Qián-lóng-era imperial scholarly projects.
