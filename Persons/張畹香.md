Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng 張畹香 (CBDB 569619 — name only, no dates) — late-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician, conventionally identified in some modern reference works as Zhāng Hùn-zhāi 張混齋 of Shàoxīng 紹興, active in the mid-to-late nineteenth century, possibly within the Shàoxīng warm-disease tradition (compare [[邵蘭蓀]] and the Shàoxīng *wēn-bìng bā-jiā* 紹興溫病八家 grouping). The exact identification is not secure and biographical data are thin. His [[KR3ep026]] *Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng yī-àn* 張畹香醫案 survives in two *juǎn* through late-Qīng manuscript copies.
