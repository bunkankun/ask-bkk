# 祖淳

Southern-Sòng Chán disciple (*cān xué* 參學) of Xiātáng Huìyuǎn 瞎堂慧遠 (1103–1176). Co-editor of his master's *guǎng lù* [[KR6q0294]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
