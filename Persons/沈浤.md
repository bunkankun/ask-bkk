# Shěn Hóng 沈浤

Early-Kāngxī Yangtze-delta lay devotee (*jūshì*) from Xiùshuǐ 秀水 (Jiāxīng region); courtesy name *Sīfǔ* 思甫. Collator-and-printer (校梓) of [[仲之屏]] Zhòng Zhīpíng's *Bōrě xīnjīng zhùshū* ([[KR6c0183]]), Kāngxī 14 (1675). Associated with the Jiāxīng-canon publication network at Lèngyán-sì.
