Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* scholar, *zì* Jìmíng 季明. A senior student of [[張載]] Zhāng Zài at the Héngqú 橫渠 academy and one of the principal early transmitters of Guānxué 關學 doctrine. With his brother [[蘇昱]] Sū Yùn and the [[呂大臨]] Lǚ Dàlín / [[呂大防]] Lǚ Dàfáng brothers, he formed the inner circle of Zhāng Zài's school.
