Dá-qiān 達謙, *zì* **Xuán-cí** 玄慈 ("Mysterious Compassion"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shān-cí Tōng-jì** [[通際]] (1608–1645). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān-cì*) with co-disciple [[達尊]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0608]] 《山茨際禪師語錄》 4 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1649.
