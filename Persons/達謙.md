Dáqiān 達謙, *zì* **Xuáncí** 玄慈 ("Mysterious Compassion"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shāncí Tōngjì** [[通際]] (1608–1645). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biāncì*) with co-disciple [[達尊]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0608]] 《山茨際禪師語錄》 4 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1649.
