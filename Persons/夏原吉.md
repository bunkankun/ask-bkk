Xià Yuánjí 夏原吉 (1366–1430), *zì* **Wéizhé** 維喆, posthumous title **Zhōngjìng** 忠靖. Native of **Xiāngyīn** 湘陰 (Yuèzhōu, Húnán). By *xiāngjiàn* recommended to the *Tàixué* (Imperial Academy); selected and appointed *Hùbù zhǔshì* 戶部主事; rose to *Hùbù shàngshū* 戶部尚書. Principal architect of the early-Míng (Yǒnglè / Hóngxī / Xuāndé) fiscal-and-water-conservancy system; reformed the TàiHú 太湖 watershed and the Sōngjiāng drainage works in Yǒnglè 1–3 (1403–05). Tutor to the future **Xuānzōng** 宣宗 as Imperial Grandson; jointly *jiānxiū* on the *shílù* of three reigns. *Míng shǐ* j. 149. Surviving works are [[KR4e0093]] *Zhōngjìng jí*.
