Xià Yuán-jí 夏原吉 (1366–1430), *zì* **Wéi-zhé** 維喆, posthumous title **Zhōng-jìng** 忠靖. Native of **Xiāng-yīn** 湘陰 (Yuè-zhōu, Hú-nán). By *xiāng-jiàn* recommended to the *Tài-xué* (Imperial Academy); selected and appointed *Hù-bù zhǔ-shì* 戶部主事; rose to *Hù-bù shàng-shū* 戶部尚書. Principal architect of the early-Míng (Yǒng-lè / Hóng-xī / Xuān-dé) fiscal-and-water-conservancy system; reformed the Tài-Hú 太湖 watershed and the Sōng-jiāng drainage works in Yǒng-lè 1–3 (1403–05). Tutor to the future **Xuān-zōng** 宣宗 as Imperial Grandson; jointly *jiān-xiū* on the *shí-lù* of three reigns. *Míng shǐ* j. 149. Surviving works are [[KR4e0093]] *Zhōng-jìng jí*.
