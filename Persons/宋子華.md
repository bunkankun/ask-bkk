# 宋子華 Sòng Zǐhuá

**Sòng Zǐhuá** 宋子華, Sòng-period mountain-recluse (*shānrén*) named in [[竇傑]] Dòu Jié's *Zhēn jīng zhǐnán* 針經指南 ([[KR3ee017]]) as the immediate transmitter to Dòu of the *Liúzhù bāxué* 流注八穴 ("eight confluent acupoints") doctrine, said to originate with an anonymous "recluse of Shǎoshì" 少室隱者. Otherwise unidentified; likely a Daoist persona.
