**Chén Zhūquán** 陳珠泉 of Jiāngpǔ 江浦 (Jiāngsū) — Republican-era physician who held for some thirty years the manuscript of *Tāichǎn zhèngzhì* ([[KR3ei064]]) attributed to [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng (via [[岳泗庵]] Yuè Sìān's redaction). Acquired the manuscript at Jiāngyòu 江右 from the Huáng family. Brought the work to print in 1929 via the Zhōngyī shūjú 中醫書局 in Shànghǎi with prefaces by himself and [[秦之萬]] Qín Zhīwàn; textual editing supervised by [[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi. Not in CBDB.
