**Qīng xiá zǐ** 青霞子 ("Master of Azure Mist") — traditionally identified with **Sū Yuánlǎng** 蘇元朗, a semi-legendary SuíTáng alchemical author. Credited with [[KR5d0106]] *Lóng hǔ yuán zhǐ* 龍虎元旨 and further alchemical writings. The actual dating of the received text(s) is probably 9th–10th century.
