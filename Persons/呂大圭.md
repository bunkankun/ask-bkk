Lǚ Dàguī 呂大圭 (1227–1275), *zì* Guīshū 圭叔, *hào* Pǔqīng 樸卿, native of Nánān 南安 (in modern Quánzhōu 泉州, Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of Chúnyòu 7 (1247). His official career took him to *Cháosàn dàfū xíng Shàngshū lìbù yuánwàiláng jiān Guózǐ biānxiū shílù jiǎntǎoguān* 朝散大夫行尚書吏部員外郎兼國子編修實録檢討官 and to *Chóngzhèng diàn shuōshū* 崇政殿說書, then out as prefect of Xīnghuà jūn 興化軍. In Déyòu 1 (1275), as Sòng was collapsing, he was reassigned from Xīnghuà to Zhāngzhōu 漳州; he had not yet taken up the post when Mongol forces arrived and Pú Shòugēng 蒲壽庚, the *Yánhǎi dū zhìzhìshǐ* 沿海都制置使, surrendered Quánzhōu to the Yuán. Lǚ refused to submit and was put to death — his political martyrdom is what the SKQS *tíyào* singles out for special praise. He composed both the *Chūnqiū jízhuàn* 春秋集傳 (now lost) and the *Chūnqiū huòwèn* 春秋或問 [[KR1e0053]] (extant), the latter a *huòwèn*-style supplement to the former. The five short essays *Chūnqiū wǔ lùn* 春秋五論 are appended to the *Huòwèn* in the SKQS edition. CBDB person 39292.
