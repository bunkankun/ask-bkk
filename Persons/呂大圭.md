Lǚ Dà-guī 呂大圭 (1227–1275), *zì* Guī-shū 圭叔, *hào* Pǔ-qīng 樸卿, native of Nán-ān 南安 (in modern Quán-zhōu 泉州, Fú-jiàn). *Jìnshì* of Chún-yòu 7 (1247). His official career took him to *Cháo-sàn dà-fū xíng Shàng-shū lì-bù yuán-wài-láng jiān Guó-zǐ biān-xiū shí-lù jiǎn-tǎo-guān* 朝散大夫行尚書吏部員外郎兼國子編修實録檢討官 and to *Chóng-zhèng diàn shuō-shū* 崇政殿說書, then out as prefect of Xīng-huà jūn 興化軍. In Dé-yòu 1 (1275), as Sòng was collapsing, he was reassigned from Xīng-huà to Zhāng-zhōu 漳州; he had not yet taken up the post when Mongol forces arrived and Pú Shòu-gēng 蒲壽庚, the *Yán-hǎi dū zhì-zhì-shǐ* 沿海都制置使, surrendered Quán-zhōu to the Yuán. Lǚ refused to submit and was put to death — his political martyrdom is what the SKQS *tíyào* singles out for special praise. He composed both the *Chūnqiū jí-zhuàn* 春秋集傳 (now lost) and the *Chūnqiū huò-wèn* 春秋或問 [[KR1e0053]] (extant), the latter a *huò-wèn*-style supplement to the former. The five short essays *Chūnqiū wǔ lùn* 春秋五論 are appended to the *Huò-wèn* in the SKQS edition. CBDB person 39292.
