Péng Yùnzhāng 彭蘊章 (1792–1862), *zì* Yǒngé 詠莪, *hào* Zhuótáng 琢堂. Native of Chángzhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū); *jìnshì* of 道光 15 (1835); a major mid-Qīng statesman who served as *Jūnjī dàchén* 軍機大臣 (Grand Council member) under Xiánfēng. Named in the front-matter of [[KR3eq085]] *Shènjí chúyán* 慎疾芻言 as the printer's-preface author who, late in life, encountered Xú Dàchūn's lay-medical treatise and sent it to be printed at the *Róngchéng* (Fúzhōu) block-print shop "to give it wider circulation". His own collected works are titled *Sōngfēng yuèjūnlóu shī wén jí* 松風閣詩文集.
