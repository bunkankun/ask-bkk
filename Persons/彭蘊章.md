Péng Yùn-zhāng 彭蘊章 (1792–1862), *zì* Yǒng-é 詠莪, *hào* Zhuó-táng 琢堂. Native of Cháng-zhōu 長洲 (Sū-zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū); *jìnshì* of 道光 15 (1835); a major mid-Qīng statesman who served as *Jūn-jī dà-chén* 軍機大臣 (Grand Council member) under Xián-fēng. Named in the front-matter of [[KR3eq085]] *Shèn-jí chú-yán* 慎疾芻言 as the printer's-preface author who, late in life, encountered Xú Dà-chūn's lay-medical treatise and sent it to be printed at the *Róng-chéng* (Fú-zhōu) block-print shop "to give it wider circulation". His own collected works are titled *Sōng-fēng yuè-jūn-lóu shī wén jí* 松風閣詩文集.
