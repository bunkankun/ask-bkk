Liú Lián 劉璉 (1348–1379), *zì* **Mèng-zǎo** 孟藻, native of **Qīng-tián** 青田 (Zhèjiāng). **Eldest son of [[劉基]] Liú Jī** (Chéng-yì-bó 誠意伯, 1311–1375). In Hóngwǔ 10 (1377) appointed *Kǎo-gōng jiān chéng* 考功監丞 with concurrent *shì jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 試監察御史; sent out as *Jiāng-xī bù-zhèng-sī yòu cān-zhèng* 江西布政司右參政; died in office at age 32. Proposed the establishment of the *Tán-yáng xún-jiǎn* 談洋巡檢 to control banditry at the Zhèjiāng-Jiāng-xī border. His son **Liú Zhì** 劉廌 (the second-generation *Chéng-yì-bó*) compiled the surviving verse collection [[KR4e0066]] *Zì-yí jí* 自怡集 in one *juǎn* (94 poems), with preface by **Huáng Bó-shēng** 黃伯生 (*Qín-fǔ jǐ-shàn*) and *āi-cí* by **Wú Cóng-shàn** 吳從善 (Hóngwǔ 13 / 1380 *Guó-shǐ-yuàn biān-xiū*). The *Sìkù* editors regard him as continuing his father's verse tradition; the **Lí-méi-gōng** 犁眉公 (Liú Jī's *hào*) literary lineage is documented across two generations.
