Liú Lián 劉璉 (1348–1379), *zì* **Mèngzǎo** 孟藻, native of **Qīngtián** 青田 (Zhèjiāng). **Eldest son of [[劉基]] Liú Jī** (Chéngyìbó 誠意伯, 1311–1375). In Hóngwǔ 10 (1377) appointed *Kǎogōng jiān chéng* 考功監丞 with concurrent *shì jiānchá yùshǐ* 試監察御史; sent out as *Jiāngxī bùzhèngsī yòu cānzhèng* 江西布政司右參政; died in office at age 32. Proposed the establishment of the *Tányáng xúnjiǎn* 談洋巡檢 to control banditry at the ZhèjiāngJiāngxī border. His son **Liú Zhì** 劉廌 (the second-generation *Chéngyìbó*) compiled the surviving verse collection [[KR4e0066]] *Zìyí jí* 自怡集 in one *juǎn* (94 poems), with preface by **Huáng Bóshēng** 黃伯生 (*Qínfǔ jǐshàn*) and *āicí* by **Wú Cóngshàn** 吳從善 (Hóngwǔ 13 / 1380 *Guóshǐyuàn biānxiū*). The *Sìkù* editors regard him as continuing his father's verse tradition; the **Líméigōng** 犁眉公 (Liú Jī's *hào*) literary lineage is documented across two generations.
