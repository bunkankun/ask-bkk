**Wǔ Zhòngcháng** 伍仲常 (*fl.* early to mid-19th c., 清), disciple of [[楊時泰]]. He raised the funds for and oversaw the posthumous printing of his teacher's *Běncǎo shù gōuyuán* 本草述鉤元 ([[KR3ec052]]) in 道光壬寅 (1842). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
