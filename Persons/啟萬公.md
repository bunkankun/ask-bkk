# 啟萬公

**Qǐwàngōng** ("Honourable Sir Qǐwàn", *fl.* late Qiánlóng / early Jiāqìng), Shàngyì 上邑 (Nányì 南邑) literatus and physician; great-uncle of [[胡青崑]] Hú Qīngkūn and original compiler — across "several decades of investigation" — of the manuscript that became [[KR3el016]] *Diēdǎ sǔnshāng huíshēng jí* in its 1856 published form. Personal name not recoverable from the surviving preface, which refers to him only by this respect-title.
