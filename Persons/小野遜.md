**Ono Son** 小野遜 (*fl.* late eighteenth / early nineteenth century), Edo-period Japanese physician of the third generation of a hereditary medical family. After family training he travelled to Kyoto to study under Nakagami Kinkei 中神琴溪 at the *Shēngshēngtáng* 生生堂. Compiled his master's clinical case-records over multiple years into the *Shēngshēngtáng zhìyàn* 生生堂治驗 ([[KR3eq055]]). Cataloged under 江戶.
