**Zhào Sìzhēn** 趙嗣真, Yuán-period *shānghán* commentator whose annotations on the *Shānghán lùn* are cited extensively in [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's [[KR3eh052]] *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng · Shānghán* among the post-Hàn supplementary methods (alongside [[成無己]] Chéng Wújǐ, [[張兼善]] Zhāng Jiānshàn, and others). Few independent biographical details survive.
