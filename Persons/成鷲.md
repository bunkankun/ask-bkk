Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáo-dòng-school Chán monk and prolific Lǐngnán literatus; lay name **Fāng Línzhǐ** 方麟趾 (also 方顓愷), monastic name **Chéngjiù** 成鷲 (with the many *zì* / *hào* — **Jīshān** 迹刪 / 跡刪, **Guāngjiù** 光鷲, **Jíshān** 即山, **Dōngqiáo** 東樵 / **Dōngqiáo shānrén** 東樵山人). **15 April 1637 – 29 November 1722**, age **86**. Native of **Pānyú** 番禺 (Guǎngdōng). Active as abbot and teacher in Guǎngdōng monasteries through the late seventeenth century into the early Kāngxī decades, and a prolific literary author (the *Xiánzhìtáng jí* 咸陟堂集 collects his poetic and prose corpus). His Buddhist commentary on the *Vajracchedikā*, *Jīngāng jīng zhíshuō* ([[KR6c0084]], X25 no. 496), survives in the *Xùzàngjīng*. Modern scholarship: Yáng Quán 楊權, "成鹫及其《咸陟堂集》" — the standard study.
