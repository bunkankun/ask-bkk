Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáo-dòng-school Chán monk and prolific Lǐng-nán literatus; lay name **Fāng Lín-zhǐ** 方麟趾 (also 方顓愷), monastic name **Chéng-jiù** 成鷲 (with the many *zì* / *hào* — **Jī-shān** 迹刪 / 跡刪, **Guāng-jiù** 光鷲, **Jí-shān** 即山, **Dōng-qiáo** 東樵 / **Dōng-qiáo shān-rén** 東樵山人). **15 April 1637 – 29 November 1722**, age **86**. Native of **Pān-yú** 番禺 (Guǎngdōng). Active as abbot and teacher in Guǎngdōng monasteries through the late seventeenth century into the early Kāng-xī decades, and a prolific literary author (the *Xián-zhì-táng jí* 咸陟堂集 collects his poetic and prose corpus). His Buddhist commentary on the *Vajracchedikā*, *Jīn-gāng jīng zhí-shuō* ([[KR6c0084]], X25 no. 496), survives in the *Xù-zàng-jīng*. Modern scholarship: Yáng Quán 楊權, "成鹫及其《咸陟堂集》" — the standard study.
