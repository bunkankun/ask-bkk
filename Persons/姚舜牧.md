# Yáo Shùnmù 姚舜牧

*Zì* Yúzuǒ 虞佐, *hào* Chéng'ān 承庵. Native of Wūchéng 烏程 (modern Húzhōu 湖州, Zhèjiāng). 1543–1627. Late-Míng *jǔrén*-rank scholar known for systematic *yíwèn* (questioning) commentaries on multiple canons:

- *Yìjīng yíwèn* 易經疑問 ([[KR1a0107]])
- *Shūjīng yíwèn* 書經疑問
- *Chóngdìng Shī jīng yíwèn* 重訂詩經疑問 in 12 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0040]]), original preface dated *Wànlì xīnhài* (1611) — the revised version is later
- *Lǐjì yíwèn* 禮記疑問
- *Chūnqiū yíwèn* 春秋疑問
- *Sì shū yíwèn* 四書疑問

His self-preface to the *Chóngdìng Shī jīng yíwèn* records that he had been working on his *Shī*-canon doubts "for several decades" before producing the revised version, willing to override the *zhìyì* (examination-format) reading wherever his philological investigation pointed. The Sìkù editors regard the *Shī*-volume as his best work among the *yíwèn* series.
