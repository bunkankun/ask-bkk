# Shàn-qīng 善清

Early-Southern-Sòng Yún-mén-zōng 雲門宗 Chán *shì-zhě* 侍者 of [[懷深]] Cí-shòu Huái-shēn (1077–1132); named first among the four attendant-editors of his master's four-juan *guǎng-lù* ([[KR6q0381]]; X73 n1451). Lifedates unrecorded.
