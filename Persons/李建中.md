Lǐ Jiànzhōng 李建中, elder son of the great pharmacologist Lǐ Shízhēn [[李時珍]], *fl.* late 16th century. He assisted his father in the editorial work on the *Běncǎo gāngmù* 本草綱目 ([[KR3ec025]]) and is named in the editorial colophon as a co-collator. After his father's death (1593) he and his younger brother Lǐ Jiànyuán [[李建元]] presented the work to the imperial court (1596). No CBDB entry; biographical details are sparse, available only through references in the *Gāngmù* paratext and Qí-zhōu local gazetteers.
