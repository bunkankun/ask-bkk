Shěn Zìnán 沈自南 (*fl.* mid-seventeenth century, lifedates unrecoverable), *zì* Liúhóu 留侯. Native of Wújiāng 吳江 in Sūzhōu (Jiāngsū). Shùnzhì *rénchén* 9 (1652) *jìnshì*. Served as magistrate of Pénglái 蓬萊 county in Shāndōng — his only known office. Compiler of the *Yì lín huì kǎo* 藝林彙考 ([[KR3j0071]]), an ambitious 24-section categorical-encyclopedic *kǎozhèng* compendium of which only 5 sections (40 juan total) survive — the rest never printed and presumably lost. Not in CBDB.
