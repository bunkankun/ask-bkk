Shěn Zìnán 沈自南 (*fl.* mid-seventeenth century, lifedates unrecoverable), *zì* Liú-hóu 留侯. Native of Wú-jiāng 吳江 in Sū-zhōu (Jiāng-sū). Shùn-zhì *rén-chén* 9 (1652) *jìnshì*. Served as magistrate of Péng-lái 蓬萊 county in Shān-dōng — his only known office. Compiler of the *Yì lín huì kǎo* 藝林彙考 ([[KR3j0071]]), an ambitious 24-section categorical-encyclopedic *kǎo-zhèng* compendium of which only 5 sections (40 juan total) survive — the rest never printed and presumably lost. Not in CBDB.
