Zhēnshí 真實 (also **Chèshí** 徹實), *zì* **Nòuyún** 耨雲 ("Cloud-Hoer"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696). Born **天啟丙寅 6/15** = **1626-07-08 Gregorian**; alive in **康熙丙寅 = 1686** at age 61; death-date not preserved. Native of **Làngzhōng** 閬中 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Childhood vow to renounce; parents refused. At 16 (1641) both parents died; tonsured. Joined **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** circuit through Jiāngnán to Tiāntóng. Final *fǎfù* from Zhàngxuě at the **Cǎotáng** 草堂 succession-seat (Sìchuān).

**Heirs.** Ten *fǎsì* including **Huīzhǐ Déchéng** 揮指德成, **Fómǐn Jìnè** [[寂訥]] 佛敏寂訥, **Zàikōng Zhàomǎn** 載空照滿; plus two lay-disciples.

**Work.** [[KR6q0507]] 《草堂耨雲實禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B370, compiled by *fǎsì* **Jìnè** [[寂訥]] *děng biān*; juan 2 *xíngyóu* 行繇 + *fēndēng* 分燈. Sources: 《草堂耨雲實禪師語錄》 j. 1 *xù* + j. 2.
