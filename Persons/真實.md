Zhēn-shí 真實 (also **Chè-shí** 徹實), *zì* **Nòu-yún** 耨雲 ("Cloud-Hoer"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696). Born **天啟丙寅 6/15** = **1626-07-08 Gregorian**; alive in **康熙丙寅 = 1686** at age 61; death-date not preserved. Native of **Làng-zhōng** 閬中 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Childhood vow to renounce; parents refused. At 16 (1641) both parents died; tonsured. Joined **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** circuit through Jiāng-nán to Tiān-tóng. Final *fǎ-fù* from Zhàng-xuě at the **Cǎo-táng** 草堂 succession-seat (Sì-chuān).

**Heirs.** Ten *fǎ-sì* including **Huī-zhǐ Dé-chéng** 揮指德成, **Fó-mǐn Jì-nè** [[寂訥]] 佛敏寂訥, **Zài-kōng Zhào-mǎn** 載空照滿; plus two lay-disciples.

**Work.** [[KR6q0507]] 《草堂耨雲實禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B370, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Jì-nè** [[寂訥]] *děng biān*; juan 2 *xíng-yóu* 行繇 + *fēn-dēng* 分燈. Sources: 《草堂耨雲實禪師語錄》 j. 1 *xù* + j. 2.
