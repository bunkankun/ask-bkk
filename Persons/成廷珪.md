Chéng Tíngguī 成廷珪 (Yuán, *fl.* 1360, d. after age 70 c. 1370), *zì* Yuáncháng 原常 (also Yuánzhāng 原章), *yīzì* Lǐzhí 禮執. Native of Yángzhōu 揚州. *Hào* Jūzhúxuān 居竹軒 ("Studio-of-Living-in-Bamboo," whence the collection title [[KR4d0543]]) — from the bamboo planted in his courtyard at Yángzhōu while caring for his mother. *Bù qiú shìjìn* (did not pursue office); supported his mother in the marketplace. Late life when *xiāngluàn* (late-Yuán war) — *bìdì Wúzhōng* — *zōngjì duō zài Sōngjiāng* — collection has "*yù bǔjū hǎishàng*" poem; died at Yúnjiān (Sōngjiāng) at age over 70. Long-friend Zhāng Zhù [[張翥]] (forty-year friendship, *wàngnián jiāo*) — Chéng's verse-method and *gézhì* from Zhāng. Exchange-correspondents include Yáng Wéizhēn 楊維禎, Wēi Sù 危素, Yáng Jī 楊基, Lǐ Fǔ 李黼, Yú Què [[余闕]], Zhāng Yǔ [[張雨]], Ní Zàn 倪瓚 — *yīdài shèngliú* (the era's elite). Collection edited posthumously by his friends Gào Sù 郜肅 and Liú Qīn 劉欽.
