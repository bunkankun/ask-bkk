Chéng Tíng-guī 成廷珪 (Yuán, *fl.* 1360, d. after age 70 c. 1370), *zì* Yuán-cháng 原常 (also Yuán-zhāng 原章), *yī-zì* Lǐ-zhí 禮執. Native of Yáng-zhōu 揚州. *Hào* Jū-zhú-xuān 居竹軒 ("Studio-of-Living-in-Bamboo," whence the collection title [[KR4d0543]]) — from the bamboo planted in his courtyard at Yáng-zhōu while caring for his mother. *Bù qiú shì-jìn* (did not pursue office); supported his mother in the marketplace. Late life when *xiāng-luàn* (late-Yuán war) — *bì-dì Wú-zhōng* — *zōng-jì duō zài Sōng-jiāng* — collection has "*yù bǔ-jū hǎi-shàng*" poem; died at Yún-jiān (Sōng-jiāng) at age over 70. Long-friend Zhāng Zhù [[張翥]] (forty-year friendship, *wàng-nián jiāo*) — Chéng's verse-method and *gé-zhì* from Zhāng. Exchange-correspondents include Yáng Wéi-zhēn 楊維禎, Wēi Sù 危素, Yáng Jī 楊基, Lǐ Fǔ 李黼, Yú Què [[余闕]], Zhāng Yǔ [[張雨]], Ní Zàn 倪瓚 — *yī-dài shèng-liú* (the era's elite). Collection edited posthumously by his friends Gào Sù 郜肅 and Liú Qīn 劉欽.
