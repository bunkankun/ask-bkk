È'ěrtài 鄂爾泰 (Manchu: Ortai, 1677–1745), *zì* Yì'ān 毅菴, posthumously *Wénduān* 文端, was a leading Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng-court Manchu official. Of the Xīlínjuéluó 西林覺羅 lineage. Rose under Yōngzhèng to *Xībǎoyīnfú dàxuéshì* 武英殿大學士 and Chancellor; central to Yōngzhèng's *gǎi tǔ guī liú* 改土歸流 frontier-administrative reforms in Yún-Guì in the 1720s. Co-supervised the imperial *Qīn dìng shòu shí tōng kǎo* 欽定授時通考 ([[KR3d0010]]) with Zhāng Tíngyù under Qiánlóng. Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 288.
