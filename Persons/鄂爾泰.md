È'ěr-tài 鄂爾泰 (Manchu: Ortai, 1677–1745), *zì* Yì'ān 毅菴, posthumously *Wénduān* 文端, was a leading Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng-court Manchu official. Of the Xī-lín-jué-luó 西林覺羅 lineage. Rose under Yōngzhèng to *Xī-bǎo-yīn-fú dà-xué-shì* 武英殿大學士 and Chancellor; central to Yōngzhèng's *gǎi tǔ guī liú* 改土歸流 frontier-administrative reforms in Yún-Guì in the 1720s. Co-supervised the imperial *Qīn dìng shòu shí tōng kǎo* 欽定授時通考 (KR3d0010) with Zhāng Tíng-yù under Qiánlóng. Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 288.
