Huáng Gēng 黃庚 (lifedates uncertain; *fl.* through Yuán Tàidìng era — his self-preface to the present collection is dated 1327), *zì* Xīngfǔ 星甫, *hào* Yuèwū 月屋 ("Moon-Chamber"), native of Tiāntái 天台 (Tāizhōu, Zhèjiāng). Born at the end of the Sòng; did not take office under the Yuán but apparently lived well into the Tàidìng era. Was a literary client of Wáng Yīngsūn 王英孫 at Shānyīn (Shàoxīng); took first place in the Yuèzhōng poetry society's *Zhěnyì* (Pillow / *Yìjīng*) theme, with the *Lǐbù shìláng* of the time as examiner. His sole surviving work is the *Yuèwū màngǎo* [[KR4d0430]] in one *juàn*. The *Sìkù* editors reclassified him from the earlier Sòng *yímín* attribution to Yuán dynasty on the strength of the 1327 self-preface date. CBDB person 11114 (lifedates blank).
