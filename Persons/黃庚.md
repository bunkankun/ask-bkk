Huáng Gēng 黃庚 (lifedates uncertain; *fl.* through Yuán Tài-dìng era — his self-preface to the present collection is dated 1327), *zì* Xīng-fǔ 星甫, *hào* Yuè-wū 月屋 ("Moon-Chamber"), native of Tiān-tái 天台 (Tāi-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng). Born at the end of the Sòng; did not take office under the Yuán but apparently lived well into the Tài-dìng era. Was a literary client of Wáng Yīng-sūn 王英孫 at Shān-yīn (Shào-xīng); took first place in the Yuè-zhōng poetry society's *Zhěn-yì* (Pillow / *Yì-jīng*) theme, with the *Lǐ-bù shì-láng* of the time as examiner. His sole surviving work is the *Yuè-wū màn-gǎo* [[KR4d0430]] in one *juàn*. The *Sìkù* editors reclassified him from the earlier Sòng *yí-mín* attribution to Yuán dynasty on the strength of the 1327 self-preface date. CBDB person 11114 (lifedates blank).
