# Ruì Tǐngzhāng 芮挺章 (fl. ca. 744)

A mid-eighth-century Táng *jìnshì* about whom almost nothing else is securely known. He is the compiler of the *Guóxiù jí* 國秀集 [[KR4h0010]], a one-*juǎn* anthology of *Kāiyuán* and *Tiānbǎo* poetry put together — at the urging of the *Mìshūjiàn* Chén Xīliè 陳希烈 and the *Guózǐ sīyè* Sū Yuánmíng 蘇源明 — about *Tiānbǎo* 3 (744), shortly before the senior of the two patrons (Chén) died. The preface to that work is the only first-hand document. No CBDB entry; no firm lifedates.
