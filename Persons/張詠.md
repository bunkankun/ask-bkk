Early-Northern-Sòng prefect-administrator and poet, *zì* Fù-zhī 復之, *hào* Guāi-yá 乖崖, posthumous *Zhōng-dìng* 忠定, of *Pú-zhōu* 濮州. *Jìn-shì* of Tài-píng xīng-guó 5 / 980. Best known as the "iron prefect" of *Yì-zhōu* 益州 (Chéng-dū): twice civil governor of Sìchuān (998–1000 and 1006–1009), where he stabilized the region after the *Wáng Xiǎo-bō / Lǐ Shùn* rising and presided over the early steps in the development of *jiāo-zǐ* 交子 paper money. Earlier prefect of Chóng-yáng (whence Wáng Yǔ-chēng's *Sòng zǎi Chóng-yáng xù*) and held *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* among his late offices. Died 1015, age 70. *Shén-dào bēi* by Hán Qí [[韓琦]]. *Yǔ-lù* preserved by Lǐ Tián 李畋. Listed at no. 11 in the *Xī-Kūn chóu-chàng* group, though the *Sìkù tíyào* observes that his actual prose style was plain rather than decorative. Surviving collection: *Guāi-yá jí* [[KR4d0005]] (12 *juǎn*, Guō Sēn-qīng's *Jiā-dìng* 1208 recension). CBDB id 332.
