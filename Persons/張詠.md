Early-Northern-Sòng prefect-administrator and poet, *zì* Fùzhī 復之, *hào* Guāiyá 乖崖, posthumous *Zhōngdìng* 忠定, of *Púzhōu* 濮州. *Jìnshì* of Tàipíng xīngguó 5 / 980. Best known as the "iron prefect" of *Yìzhōu* 益州 (Chéngdū): twice civil governor of Sìchuān (998–1000 and 1006–1009), where he stabilized the region after the *Wáng Xiǎobō / Lǐ Shùn* rising and presided over the early steps in the development of *jiāozǐ* 交子 paper money. Earlier prefect of Chóngyáng (whence Wáng Yǔchēng's *Sòng zǎi Chóngyáng xù*) and held *Lǐbù shàngshū* among his late offices. Died 1015, age 70. *Shéndào bēi* by Hán Qí [[韓琦]]. *Yǔlù* preserved by Lǐ Tián 李畋. Listed at no. 11 in the *XīKūn chóuchàng* group, though the *Sìkù tíyào* observes that his actual prose style was plain rather than decorative. Surviving collection: *Guāiyá jí* [[KR4d0005]] (12 *juǎn*, Guō Sēnqīng's *Jiādìng* 1208 recension). CBDB id 332.
