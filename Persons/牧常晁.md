**Mù Chángzhào** 牧常晁 was a Yuán Daoist author, of [[KR5d0089]] *Xuán zōng zhí zhǐ wàn fǎ tóng guī* 玄宗直指萬法同歸 — a syncretic treatise on the "ten-thousand dharmas returning to one" in the post-1294 Yuán Daoist synthesis.
