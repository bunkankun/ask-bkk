Late-Yuán palace functionary and *gōng-cí* (palace-verse) poet of Jí-shuǐ 吉水 (Jiāngxī). Style-name **Héjí** 和吉. His career is otherwise unrecorded except through his own collection. From the self-glosses on his *Luán-jīng zá-yǒng* 灤京雜詠 ([[KR4d0570]]) — in particular the note on the *tāng-yáng* (mutton-broth) imperial meal allocation — it is clear that under the Yuán Shùn-dì he served as a *shàng-shí* (palace-kitchen) supply official rather than as a wandering literatus, despite **Luó Dà-yǐ** 羅大已's *bá* describing him as a *bùyī* (commoner) traveler. He saw the dynastic fall and survived: several poems in the collection postdate 1368 and explicitly mourn the lost Yuán. The collection is the principal extant record of the Yuán Shàng-dū / Luán-jīng (the summer capital on the Luán river — i.e. the Khan's annual northern court) court life and is one of the most-cited late-Yuán *gōng-cí* corpora.
