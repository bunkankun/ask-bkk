# Miù Zhījìn 繆之晉

Qīng-period calendrical compiler. No CBDB record located (search on both 繆之晉 and the simplified 繆之晋 returned empty); no biographical detail recoverable from the standard prosopographies. The catalog meta records him as the *jí* 輯 (compiler) of [[KR3fb022]] *DàQīng Shíxiànshū jiānshì* 大清時憲書箋釋 in 3 *juàn* — an annotated commentary on the official Qīng *Shíxiàn lì* civil calendar.

The work itself is the only securely recoverable document of his activity. The Qīng *Shíxiàn lì* having been promulgated from 1645, the work is necessarily later, and the absence of post-Qián-lóng features in the framing places it conventionally in the Kāngxī to mid-Qián-lóng period (notBefore 1645 / notAfter 1795), within which the dating cannot be further refined on internal evidence.
