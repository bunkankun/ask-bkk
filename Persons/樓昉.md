Lóu Fǎng 樓昉, zì *Yángshū* 暘叔 (also spelled 𤾉叔 in some witnesses), hào *Yūzhāi* 迂齋, was a Southern Sòng prose-stylist and compiler from Yìnxiàn 鄞縣 (modern Níngbō). A student of Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙 (1137–1181), he served as *Zōngzhèngsì zhǔbù* 宗正寺主簿. He is best known for two complementary anthological works in the *gǔwén* tradition:

1. **Dōng Hàn zhàolìng** 東漢詔令 in 12 *juàn* — extending Lín Fú 林虙's *Xī Hàn zhàolìng* to the Eastern Hàn dynasty, arranged by reign in the same manner; his own preface is dated *Jiādìng* 嘉定 15 (1222). The work was joined with Lín Fú's earlier compilation into the 23-*juàn* *Liǎng Hàn zhàolìng* 兩漢詔令 ([[KR2f0011]]).

2. **Chóngbiān gǔwén guānjiàn** 崇編古文關鍵 (also called *Lóu shìzhāi gǔwén guānjiàn* 樓氏迂齋古文關鍵) — an anthology of pre-Tang–early Sòng prose with line-by-line stylistic commentary, fundamental to the Sòng canon-of-prose tradition that Lǚ Zǔqiān had pioneered with the original *Gǔwén guānjiàn*.

CBDB has no lifedates for him; his floruit is anchored by his 1222 preface to the *Dōng Hàn zhàolìng* and by his recorded discipleship under Lǚ Zǔqiān (d. 1181). He thus must have been active from at least the 1170s into the 1220s, with a lifespan plausibly c. 1155 – c. 1230.
