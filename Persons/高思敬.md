**Gāo Sī-jìng** 高思敬 (*zì* Qì-yún 憩雲, *hào* Zhēn-ān 貞庵, *fl.* Guāng-xù – early Republic, 清), late-Qīng surgical physician of Jiāngyīn 江陰 (Chéng-jiāng 澄江, Jiāngsū). Trained first in internal medicine under Zhào Yún-quán 趙雲泉 and then in external medicine under Lǐ Yù-liáng 李遇良; claimed forty-odd years of practice. His mother's protracted *tún-jū* 臀疽 (buttock abscess) in 1858 drove his decision to abandon the examination track for medicine. The most prolific late-Qīng exponent of the Wáng Hóng-xù *quán-shēng pài* 全生派 (cf. [[王洪緒]], [[KR3ek017]]); his ten surgical works are collected as ***Gāo Qì-yún wài-kē quán-shū shí-zhǒng*** 高憩雲外科全書十種 (1909), with [[KR3ek010]] *Wài-kē yī-jìng* as the principal title. His *Yùn-qì zhǐ-zhǎng* 運氣指掌 was published in 1916, placing his career across both the late Qīng and the early Republic. CBDB has two homonyms; 575570 (dyn 20 = 清) is the correct match.
