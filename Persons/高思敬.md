**Gāo Sījìng** 高思敬 (*zì* Qìyún 憩雲, *hào* Zhēnān 貞庵, *fl.* Guāngxù – early Republic, 清), late-Qīng surgical physician of Jiāngyīn 江陰 (Chéngjiāng 澄江, Jiāngsū). Trained first in internal medicine under Zhào Yúnquán 趙雲泉 and then in external medicine under Lǐ Yùliáng 李遇良; claimed forty-odd years of practice. His mother's protracted *túnjū* 臀疽 (buttock abscess) in 1858 drove his decision to abandon the examination track for medicine. The most prolific late-Qīng exponent of the Wáng Hóngxù *quánshēng pài* 全生派 (cf. [[王洪緒]], [[KR3ek017]]); his ten surgical works are collected as ***Gāo Qìyún wàikē quánshū shízhǒng*** 高憩雲外科全書十種 (1909), with [[KR3ek010]] *Wàikē yījìng* as the principal title. His *Yùnqì zhǐzhǎng* 運氣指掌 was published in 1916, placing his career across both the late Qīng and the early Republic. CBDB has two homonyms; 575570 (dyn 20 = 清) is the correct match.
