Mid-to-late Qīng (Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Shùfǔ** 述甫, sobriquets **Jiézhāi jūshì** 潔齋居士 ("Pure-Vegetarian Layman"), **Níngwǔ** 寧武. Born **乾隆 7 = 1742**; death-date unrecorded. Compiled the two-juan *Jīngāng jīng huìzuǎn* 金剛經彙纂 ([[KR6c0092]], X25 no. 504), with two prefaces — first composed **Qiánlóng 58 *guǐchǒu* 3rd-month full-moon = 1793-04**, revised in **Jiāqìng 1 *bǐngchén* 12th-month full-moon = 1797-01**. The four-year revision interval indicates a serious editorial labor.
