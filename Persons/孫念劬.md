Mid-to-late Qīng (Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Shù-fǔ** 述甫, sobriquets **Jié-zhāi jū-shì** 潔齋居士 ("Pure-Vegetarian Layman"), **Níng-wǔ** 寧武. Born **乾隆 7 = 1742**; death-date unrecorded. Compiled the two-juan *Jīn-gāng jīng huì-zuǎn* 金剛經彙纂 ([[KR6c0092]], X25 no. 504), with two prefaces — first composed **Qián-lóng 58 *guǐ-chǒu* 3rd-month full-moon = 1793-04**, revised in **Jiā-qìng 1 *bǐng-chén* 12th-month full-moon = 1797-01**. The four-year revision interval indicates a serious editorial labor.
