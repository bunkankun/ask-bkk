# Zenzan 禪山

Japanese (Edo-period) Sōtō monk and disciple of [[卍山道白]] Manzan Dōhaku; in summer 1680 brought two independent manuscript recensions of [[KR6q0071]] *Rújìng héshàng yǔlù* to Manzan, enabling the collation that produced the received text.
