# Táng Jī 唐積

Sòng official, *fl.* mid-Northern Sòng. Held the offices of *tài-zǐ zhōng-shè* 太子中舍 (junior tutor of the heir apparent) and concurrently *zhī Wù-yuán xiàn* 知婺源縣 (magistrate of Wùyuán, in Shèzhōu 歙州 — the modern Wùyuán County, Jiāngxī). His one surviving work is the *Shè-zhōu yàn-pǔ* 歙州硯譜 ([[KR3i0004]]), an authoritative monograph on the Shè-stone industry composed in Zhìpíng 3 (1066), which Chén Zhènsūn's 陳振孫 *Shū-lù jiě-tí* lists under the (slightly differing) title *Shè yàn tú-pǔ* 歙硯圖譜 with Táng explicitly named as author. The original presumably included engraved illustrations of typical Shè-stone forms; these did not survive into the Sòng-end Bǎichuān xuéhǎi 百川學海 recension printed by Zuǒ Guī 左圭. Lifedates beyond his Zhìpíng *fl.* are not securely recoverable.
