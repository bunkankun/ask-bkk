# Sūn Shào-yuǎn 孫紹遠 (fl. 1187)

*Zì* Jī-zhòng 稽仲, *hào* Gǔ-qiáo 谷橋 (the location of "Gǔ-qiáo" is unknown). A Southern-Sòng official whose career took him to **Guǎng-zhōu** in *Chún-xī* 13 or 14 (1186 or 1187), where he completed the *Shēng-huà jí* 聲畫集 [[KR4h0039]] in 8 *juǎn* — a topical anthology of Táng-Sòng *tí-huà* (painting-inscription) poetry organised by subject. His self-preface is dated *Chún-xī dīng-wèi* (1187, 10th month). The catalog meta gives "d. 1128," obviously erroneous; the *Chún-xī* date in the preface fixes the *floruit* at 1187. The compiler is otherwise unknown. No CBDB entry.
