**Fāng Yànzhāo** 方燕昭 (1854–1908, *zì* Bóróng 伯融), late-Qīng official of Dìngyuǎn 定遠, Ānhuī. CBDB id 54628. Holding the post of *guāncháshǐ* 觀察使 ("Observation Commissioner", i.e. *dàotái* 道台) in the Hánjiāng 邗江 (Yángzhōu) region, he came into close friendship with the physician [[沈青芝]] Shěn Qīngzhī over a span of ten years in the early 1880s.

He wrote the friend-preface (*xiǎoxù* 小序) to Shěn's [[KR3em028]] *Hóukē jíyè* 喉科集腋, dated *Guāngxù* 7 *xīnsì* 辛巳, first month of autumn (1881), at the *Wēncuì xuān* 溫翠軒 of his Yángzhōu residence. The self-preface to the published work records that Fāng "offered Shěn silver to buy the work for printing", an offer Shěn declined, electing instead to publish the manual freely. Fāng's preface explicitly attests to having compiled with Shěn a comparative survey of "thirty-six laryngology authorities" with their diagnostic typologies, prescriptions, emetic methods, and needling protocols — a piece of late-Qīng *hóukē* bibliographic synthesis preserved only through this preface.
