**Fāng Yànzhāo** 方燕昭 (1854–1908, *zì* Bó-róng 伯融), late-Qīng official of Dìng-yuǎn 定遠, Ān-huī. CBDB id 54628. Holding the post of *guān-chá-shǐ* 觀察使 ("Observation Commissioner", i.e. *dào-tái* 道台) in the Hán-jiāng 邗江 (Yáng-zhōu) region, he came into close friendship with the physician [[沈青芝]] Shěn Qīngzhī over a span of ten years in the early 1880s.

He wrote the friend-preface (*xiǎo-xù* 小序) to Shěn's [[KR3em028]] *Hóu-kē jí-yè* 喉科集腋, dated *Guāng-xù* 7 *xīn-sì* 辛巳, first month of autumn (1881), at the *Wēn-cuì xuān* 溫翠軒 of his Yáng-zhōu residence. The self-preface to the published work records that Fāng "offered Shěn silver to buy the work for printing", an offer Shěn declined, electing instead to publish the manual freely. Fāng's preface explicitly attests to having compiled with Shěn a comparative survey of "thirty-six laryngology authorities" with their diagnostic typologies, prescriptions, emetic methods, and needling protocols — a piece of late-Qīng *hóu-kē* bibliographic synthesis preserved only through this preface.
