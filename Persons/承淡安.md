Chéng Dàn'ān 承淡安 (1899–1957) was the principal 20th-century revivalist of acupuncture-moxibustion in modern China, founder of the Wújìn 無錫 Zhōngguó Zhēnjiǔ Xuéshè 中國針灸學社 (1932). He co-led the 1956 reconstruction of [[姜禮]] Jiāng Lǐ's *Fēngláogǔgé sìdà zhèngzhì* ([[KR3eh038]]) from surviving journal-serialised fragments.
