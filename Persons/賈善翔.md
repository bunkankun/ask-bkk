Northern-Sòng 北宋 Daoist scholar, *zì* **Hóng jǔ** 鴻舉. Active 1078–1094 (the Yuán fēng 元豐 and Yuán yòu 元祐 eras). Author of several Daozang works including:

1. **[[KR5c0132|*Nán huá zhēn jīng zhí yīn*]]** 南華真經直音 (DZ 739, 1086) — a phonetic-gloss apparatus to the *Zhuāngzǐ*.

2. ***Yóu lóng zhuàn*** 猶龍傳 (DZ 774) — a Lǎozǐ hagiography in six *juàn*, one of the most elaborate medieval Chinese treatments of the Lǎozǐ legend. Still extant.

3. ***Gāo dào zhuàn*** 高道傳 — a hagiographical collection of famous Daoists; preserved only in fragments (in DZ 783 *Zhèng tǒng dào zàng mù lù*).

**Affiliation.** Jiǎ Shànxiáng was apparently a Daoist cleric or lay Daoist scholar, active at the Northern-Sòng court in the late Shénzōng 神宗 and early Zhézōng 哲宗 eras. No precise biographical details are recorded.

**Dating.** Active 1078–1094 (attested). No lifedates. No CBDB record identified.
