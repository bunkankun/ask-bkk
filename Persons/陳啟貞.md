**Chén Qǐzhēn** 陳啟貞 (*fl.* late 17th c., 清), self-styled "白沙友人" ("friend from Báishā"). Wrote the 暮春 (late-spring) preface 康熙己未 (1679) to [[蔣介繁]]'s *Běncǎo zéyào gāngmù* 本草擇要綱目 ([[KR3ec057]]). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
