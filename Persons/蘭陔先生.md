Lán-gāi xiānshēng 蘭陔仁長先生, anonymous late-Qīng physician, *fl.* 1862–1879+. Author of [[KR3ep067]] *Shèn-wǔ-táng zhì-yàn lù*, a fifteen-*juǎn* casebook transcribed by his sons (the eldest, [[雅樂]] Yǎ-lè, compiled the editorial preface). The studio name *Shèn-wǔ-táng* 慎五堂 ("Hall of the Five Cautions") refers to the classical *wǔ-shèn* injunction. A 1884 dedicatory verse confirms his *bié-hào* as Lán-gāi 蘭陔. Surname not securely identified in the transmitted text; tentatively associated with the Qián 錢 family medical lineage of Wú-jiāng. Not in CBDB.
