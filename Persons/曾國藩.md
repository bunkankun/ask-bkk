Zēng Guófān 曾國藩 (1811–1872), *zì* Bó-hán 伯涵 (also Dí-shēng 滌生), posthumous title *Wén-zhèng* 文正 (the highest civil posthumous title, given to only a few Qīng officials). Native of Xiāng-xiāng 湘鄉, Húnán. The single most influential individual figure of the Tóngzhì 同治 restoration; founder of the Xiāng-jūn 湘軍 (Hunan Army), suppressor of the Tài-píng Rebellion (capital broken 1864), senior arbiter of the early *Yáng-wù* (Self-Strengthening) movement, governor-general of Liǎng-jiāng (1860–1868) and Zhí-lì (1868–1870), and posthumously enrolled in the Confucian temple. *Jìnshì* of Dàoguāng 18 (1838); rose through the Hànlín and the central bureaucracy. Major works: the *Zēng Wén-zhèng-gōng quán-shū* 曾文正公全書 (145 juan), comprising the *shī-jí* [[KR4f0068]], 3-juan *wén-jí*, 4-juan *zá-zhù*, the famous *jiā-shū* 家書 family letters, the *rì-jì* 日記 diary, and memorials and military correspondence; the *Shèng-zhé huà-xiàng jì* 聖哲畫像記 (his programmatic statement on the canonical pantheon of historical sages); the *Jīng-shǐ bǎi-jiā zá-chāo* 經史百家雜鈔 anthology (the foundational document of the Xiāng-xiāng 湘鄉 school of late-Qīng prose, extending Tóngchéng's [[姚鼐]] [[KR4f0052]] program). *ECCP* 751–756 (Hummel); CBDB id 34344.
