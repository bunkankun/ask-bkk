Zēng Guófān 曾國藩 (1811–1872), *zì* Bóhán 伯涵 (also Díshēng 滌生), posthumous title *Wénzhèng* 文正 (the highest civil posthumous title, given to only a few Qīng officials). Native of Xiāngxiāng 湘鄉, Húnán. The single most influential individual figure of the Tóngzhì 同治 restoration; founder of the Xiāngjūn 湘軍 (Hunan Army), suppressor of the Tàipíng Rebellion (capital broken 1864), senior arbiter of the early *Yángwù* (Self-Strengthening) movement, governor-general of Liǎngjiāng (1860–1868) and Zhílì (1868–1870), and posthumously enrolled in the Confucian temple. *Jìnshì* of Dàoguāng 18 (1838); rose through the Hànlín and the central bureaucracy. Major works: the *Zēng Wénzhènggōng quánshū* 曾文正公全書 (145 juan), comprising the *shījí* [[KR4f0068]], 3-juan *wénjí*, 4-juan *zázhù*, the famous *jiāshū* 家書 family letters, the *rìjì* 日記 diary, and memorials and military correspondence; the *Shèngzhé huàxiàng jì* 聖哲畫像記 (his programmatic statement on the canonical pantheon of historical sages); the *Jīngshǐ bǎijiā záchāo* 經史百家雜鈔 anthology (the foundational document of the Xiāngxiāng 湘鄉 school of late-Qīng prose, extending Tóngchéng's [[姚鼐]] [[KR4f0052]] program). *ECCP* 751–756 (Hummel); CBDB id 34344.
