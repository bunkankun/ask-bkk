Early-Qīng physician and literatus of Sōngjiāng 松江 / Yúnjiān 雲間 (上海); *zì* Qíshū 期叔 and Chénshān 辰山, *hào* Yúcūn 漁村. *Cóng-sūn* 從孫 (grand-nephew) and principal medical successor of the late-Ming master Lǐ Zhōngzǐ 李中梓 (字士材, see [[KR3eb020]]). Lifedates 1628–1697 are conventional and not securely fixed; he was active from the 1640s into the 1680s. After the Ming collapse he refused civil-service career and devoted himself to medicine, travelling through Húnán (Xiāng-jiāng), Hénán (Tiānzhōng), and Shāndōng (Jì-shàng) for thirty years before returning south in 1666. Author of [[KR3eb018]] *Mài jué huì biàn* (1664–66), which reconstructs and expands his great-uncle's *Zhěn jiā zhèng yǎn*; also edited and corrected several other Lǐ Zhōngzǐ titles. Friend of Yú Jiāyán 喻嘉言, Zhāng Qīngzǐ 張卿子, and Lú Zǐyóu 盧子繇.

**Name variants:** 罡 (gāng) — the conventionally cited form — and the graphic variants 昰 / 是 (shì, "right / is") are interchangeably attested in the catalog literature. The hxwd meta uses 李延是; the standard modern bibliographic form is 李延罡 (or 李延昰). No CBDB record.
