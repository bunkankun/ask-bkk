Xíngdǎo 行導, *hào* **Nuònuò** 諾諾 ("Yes-Yes"). Sole Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Cháozōng Tōngrěn** [[通忍]] 朝宗通忍 (1604–1648). Abbot at **Gànzhōu Bǎohuásì** 贛州寶華寺 (Jiāngxī) — the same seat where his master Tōngrěn died and was interred. Lead compiler of the 10-juan 《朝宗禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0474]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Xíngdǎo biān* 行導編. No independent *yǔlù* located.
