Xíng-dǎo 行導, *hào* **Nuò-nuò** 諾諾 ("Yes-Yes"). Sole Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Cháo-zōng Tōng-rěn** [[通忍]] 朝宗通忍 (1604–1648). Abbot at **Gàn-zhōu Bǎo-huá-sì** 贛州寶華寺 (Jiāng-xī) — the same seat where his master Tōng-rěn died and was interred. Lead compiler of the 10-juan 《朝宗禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0474]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Xíng-dǎo biān* 行導編. No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
