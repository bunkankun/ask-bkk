Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* student of Dàmíng 大名. One of two student-confidants (with [[張子望]] Zhāng Zǐwàng of Yíngyáng) of [[邵雍]] Shào Yōng, said by [[邵伯溫]] Shào Bówēn's *Yìxué biànhuò* ([[KR1a0017]]) to have received his master's *xiāntiān* 先天 chart-and-numerology corpus in confidential transmission. Wáng died young; on his deathbed his manuscript copy was stolen by [[鄭夬]] Zhèng Guài — the foundational episode of Shào Bówēn's polemic.
