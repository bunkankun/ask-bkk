Wáng Zhì-qìng 王志慶 (fl. late Míng), native of Kūn-shān 崑山 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū), youngest of three literary brothers. His elder brother Wáng Zhì-jiān 王志堅 held the post of *qiān-shì* (Surveillance Vice Commissioner) and was a recognized *gǔ-wén* (ancient-prose) scholar (his *Sì shǐ lùn yú* 四史論餘 is preserved in the *Sìkù*); the middle brother Wáng Zhì-cháng 王志長 was a *jǔ-rén*. Wáng Zhì-qìng's principal surviving work is the *Gǔ lì fǔ* 古儷府 ([[KR3k0053]]), a 12-*juǎn* late-Míng parallel-prose *lèishū* anthologizing Six-Dynasties through Sòng *pián-wén*. The *Sìkù* editors praise his method of going directly to *bié-jí* and *zǒng-jí* sources rather than reproducing earlier *lèishū* second-hand. CBDB id 235162.
