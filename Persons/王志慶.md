Wáng Zhìqìng 王志慶 (fl. late Míng), native of Kūnshān 崑山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū), youngest of three literary brothers. His elder brother Wáng Zhìjiān 王志堅 held the post of *qiānshì* (Surveillance Vice Commissioner) and was a recognized *gǔwén* (ancient-prose) scholar (his *Sì shǐ lùn yú* 四史論餘 is preserved in the *Sìkù*); the middle brother Wáng Zhìcháng 王志長 was a *jǔrén*. Wáng Zhìqìng's principal surviving work is the *Gǔ lì fǔ* 古儷府 ([[KR3k0053]]), a 12-*juǎn* late-Míng parallel-prose *lèishū* anthologizing Six-Dynasties through Sòng *piánwén*. The *Sìkù* editors praise his method of going directly to *biéjí* and *zǒngjí* sources rather than reproducing earlier *lèishū* second-hand. CBDB id 235162.
