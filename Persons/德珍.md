# Dé-zhēn 德珍

Late Southern-Sòng / Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Yù-shān 玉山; imperial title Fó-guāng Hǎi-yìn 佛光海印. Dharma-heir of [[普度]] Xū-zhōu Pǔ-dù (1199–1280), under whom he appears among the editors of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0340]]); succeeded as abbot of Hángzhōu-lù Jǐng-dé Líng-yǐn chán-sì 杭州路景德靈隱禪寺. His own principal dharma-heir was [[守忠]] Tán-fāng Shǒu-zhōng (1275–1348, [[KR6q0343]]), who attained awakening under him on the "cypress tree in the courtyard" 庭前柏樹子 *huàtóu*. Listed in *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 1 as "Líng-yǐn Yù-shān Dé-zhēn" among Pǔ-dù's dharma-heirs; further noticed in *Wǔ-lín Líng-yǐn sì zhì* 武林靈隱寺志 *juan* 3. Lifedates unrecorded.
