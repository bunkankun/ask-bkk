Sòng Zhēnqīng 宋真卿, late-Southern-Sòng *Yì* teacher of the Wùzhōu Dōnglái-school orbit. Per the preface of [[徐子東]] Xú Zǐdōng to *Yì chuán dēng* ([[KR1a0044]]), the unidentified father "Xú Zǒnggàn" studied successively under [[呂祖謙]] Lǚ Zǔqiān, [[唐仲友]] Táng Zhòngyǒu, and Sòng Zhēnqīng — at whose "book-hall" the father saw a Fú-Xī *Xiāntiān bā guà* diagram on the wall and from that moment dedicated himself to *Yì*-study.
