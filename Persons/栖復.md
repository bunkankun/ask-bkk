Mid- to late-Táng Cí'ēn 慈恩 / Yogācāra master and exegete. DILA Authority A001009. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded; productive period probably mid- to late-eighth century, or possibly into the early ninth. Compiler (集) of the **Fǎhuá jīng xuánzàn yàojí** 法華經玄贊要集 ([[KR6d0030]], X34n0638, in 31 *juan*) — the most extensive surviving subcommentary on Kuījī's *Miàofǎ liánhuá jīng xuánzàn* ([[KR6d0026]], T1723), longer than Huìzhāo's *Yìjué* ([[KR6d0027]]), Zhìzhōu's *Shèshì* ([[KR6d0028]]), and Chóngjùn's *Juézé jì* ([[KR6d0029]]) combined.
