Dàozhèng 道正 (1614–1691), *zì* **Liányuè** 蓮月, was a Línjì *chánshī* of the early-Qīng Sìchuān "Shǔzōng" 蜀宗 lineage and a dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). The DILA authority standardizes the dharma-name as **印正**, with **道正** / **蓮月正** / **蓮月印正** all attested as variants — the Jiāxīng Canon colophons and the 嘉興藏 catalog meta for both [[KR6q0445]] and [[KR6q0446]] use **道正**, which is followed here. Lay surname **Jiāng** 姜; mother Yáng 楊; native of **Yuèchíxiàn** 岳池縣 in **Guǒzhōu** 果州 (modern Sìchuān, Nánchōng prefecture). Tonsured after his mother's death when he overheard a sūtra-recitation containing the *sānjiè huǒzhái* 三界火宅 simile; cut his own hair against his father's refusal; trained first under **Jìngyǔ** 淨宇 (on the *Shǒuléngyán*) and **Gāoyuán** 高原 (on *Wéishí*); turned to Chán after meeting **Xiàngyái Xìngtǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 in Guìzhōu; ultimately crossed the Fúlíng 涪陵 to meet **Pòshān** at **Zhōnglù Dàshùtáng** 忠路大樹堂 and received transmission after an exchange over a stumble on the road to **Nánpǔ** 南浦.

First abbacy: **Nánpǔ Tàibáiyán** 南浦太白巖 (at Pòshān's appointment). Subsequent posts: **Liángshān Jīnchéngsì** 梁山金城寺 (where he was appointed *xītáng* 西堂 under Pòshān); **Yāshān Qīfèngsì** 鴉山栖鳳寺; six years of seated practice at **Yèlángshān Huáibáitáng** 夜郎山懷白堂; and from **Shùnzhì 戊戌 = 1658** the **Jiǔqīngshān Dōngyìn Chánsì** 九青山東印禪寺 at **Zūnyìfǔ** 遵義府 (Guìzhōu). Later successively **Jiàolín** 覺林, **Bǎolún** 寶輪, **Dùdá** 度達, **Bǎoshòu** 保壽 (all Sìchuān); then **Lóngxīng** 龍興, **Jǐngdé** 景德, **Fúchāng** 福昌, **Yùquán** 玉泉, **Tiānhuáng** 天皇 (all Húběi). Resident at **Yùquánsì** 玉泉寺 in **Dāngyáng** 當陽 (Húběi) for at least seven years from c. 1671, whence the 2-juan 《玉泉蓮月正禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0446]], prefaced 康熙十七 / 1678 by 俞廷瑞). Chief *xíngshí* 行實 narrated to disciples in 康熙癸卯 / 1663; chief *yǔlù* prefaced 康熙庚戌 / 1670 by 施博. Died 康熙三十年 1691 7/10, *shìshòu* 78. Chief heirs **Yìtiān Hǎi** 溢天海 and **Chūnyán Róng** 春巖容; the compiler of the six-juan *yǔlù* **Xìngróng** [[性容]] is of that latter circle, with the 雲幢 *fā*-generation heir **Fāhuì** [[發慧]] compiling the Yùquán recension. Biographies: 《錦江禪燈》 j. 10; 《新續高僧傳四集》 j. 56; 《五燈全書》 j. 88; *Fóguāng dàcídiǎn* 2205.
