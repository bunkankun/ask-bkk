Dào-zhèng 道正 (1614–1691), *zì* **Lián-yuè** 蓮月, was a Línjì *chán-shī* of the early-Qīng Sìchuān "Shǔ-zōng" 蜀宗 lineage and a dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). The DILA authority standardizes the dharma-name as **印正**, with **道正** / **蓮月正** / **蓮月印正** all attested as variants — the Jiā-xīng Canon colophons and the 嘉興藏 catalog meta for both [[KR6q0445]] and [[KR6q0446]] use **道正**, which is followed here. Lay surname **Jiāng** 姜; mother Yáng 楊; native of **Yuè-chí-xiàn** 岳池縣 in **Guǒ-zhōu** 果州 (modern Sì-chuān, Nán-chōng prefecture). Tonsured after his mother's death when he overheard a sūtra-recitation containing the *sān-jiè huǒ-zhái* 三界火宅 simile; cut his own hair against his father's refusal; trained first under **Jìng-yǔ** 淨宇 (on the *Shǒu-léng-yán*) and **Gāo-yuán** 高原 (on *Wéi-shí*); turned to Chán after meeting **Xiàng-yái Xìng-tǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 in Guì-zhōu; ultimately crossed the Fú-líng 涪陵 to meet **Pòshān** at **Zhōng-lù Dà-shù-táng** 忠路大樹堂 and received transmission after an exchange over a stumble on the road to **Nán-pǔ** 南浦.

First abbacy: **Nán-pǔ Tài-bái-yán** 南浦太白巖 (at Pòshān's appointment). Subsequent posts: **Liáng-shān Jīn-chéng-sì** 梁山金城寺 (where he was appointed *xī-táng* 西堂 under Pòshān); **Yā-shān Qī-fèng-sì** 鴉山栖鳳寺; six years of seated practice at **Yè-láng-shān Huái-bái-táng** 夜郎山懷白堂; and from **Shùn-zhì 戊戌 = 1658** the **Jiǔ-qīng-shān Dōng-yìn Chán-sì** 九青山東印禪寺 at **Zūn-yì-fǔ** 遵義府 (Guì-zhōu). Later successively **Jiào-lín** 覺林, **Bǎo-lún** 寶輪, **Dù-dá** 度達, **Bǎo-shòu** 保壽 (all Sì-chuān); then **Lóng-xīng** 龍興, **Jǐng-dé** 景德, **Fú-chāng** 福昌, **Yù-quán** 玉泉, **Tiān-huáng** 天皇 (all Hú-běi). Resident at **Yù-quán-sì** 玉泉寺 in **Dāng-yáng** 當陽 (Hú-běi) for at least seven years from c. 1671, whence the 2-juan 《玉泉蓮月正禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0446]], prefaced 康熙十七 / 1678 by 俞廷瑞). Chief *xíng-shí* 行實 narrated to disciples in 康熙癸卯 / 1663; chief *yǔ-lù* prefaced 康熙庚戌 / 1670 by 施博. Died 康熙三十年 1691 7/10, *shì-shòu* 78. Chief heirs **Yì-tiān Hǎi** 溢天海 and **Chūn-yán Róng** 春巖容; the compiler of the six-juan *yǔ-lù* **Xìng-róng** [[性容]] is of that latter circle, with the 雲幢 *fā*-generation heir **Fā-huì** [[發慧]] compiling the Yù-quán recension. Biographies: 《錦江禪燈》 j. 10; 《新續高僧傳四集》 j. 56; 《五燈全書》 j. 88; *Fó-guāng dà-cí-diǎn* 2205.
