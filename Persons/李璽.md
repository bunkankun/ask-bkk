# Lǐ Xǐ 李璽 (Míng, fl. 1522–1566)

A Jiājìng-era *Chóngqìng tuīguān* 重慶推官 (Magistrate-Inspector of Chóngqìng prefecture) responsible for recovering the 4-*juǎn* surviving recension of Féng Shíxíng's [[馮時行]] *Jìnyún wénjí* [[KR4d0205]] from a damaged old transcript and putting it into print. Beyond this editorial act, Lǐ has no substantial biographical record. Bibliographic identifier only.
