Jiānyì púsà 堅意菩薩 (reconstructed Sanskrit *Sāramati*) was an Indian Buddhist *śāstra*-author of uncertain date, conventionally placed between Asaṅga and Sthiramati (so c. 4th–5th century), to whom the *Rù dà-shèng lùn* 入大乘論 [[KR6o0038]] (T1634, translated by [[道泰]] under the Northern Liáng 北涼 in c. 426) is ascribed. The same author may also be the author of the *Ratnagotravibhāga / Mahāyānottaratantraśāstra* (the *Bǎo-xìng lùn* 寶性論 / *Jiūjìng yīchéng bǎo-xìng lùn* 究竟一乘寶性論) — a classical attribution debated in modern scholarship (Takasaki Jikidō 高崎直道 reviews the evidence in his 1966 study). His name is sometimes Sanskritised as 娑囉摩底 *Sāramati* in later sources. No biographical material survives.
