Huànlún 幻輪 was a late-Míng 明 Buddhist monk, active in the late sixteenth century. The preface to his sole surviving work, the *Shìjiàn jīgǔ lüè xùjí* ([[KR6r0015]]), is dated 1576 (Wànlì 萬曆 4). He identifies himself as belonging to the Tiāntái 天台 lineage and his work continues that of [[覺岸]] ([[KR6r0014]]).

No further biographical data is preserved. The compilation of his continuation supplies most of what is known about him: a monk well-read in both Buddhist and Confucian historiography, with access to the imperial records and earlier Buddhist universal histories.
