Lǐ Xiū-zhī 李修之, *fǔ* (style 父), early-Qīng physician of Yún-jiān 雲間 (Sōng-jiāng, now Shàng-hǎi). A Sōng-jiāng cultivated-gentry-circle physician active in the late seventeenth and early eighteenth century, with clinical reputation in the Hǎi-yī 海邑 district. The studio-name *Jiù-dé-táng* 舊德堂 ("Hall of Inherited Virtue") was conferred by his patron **Tián Yuán-kǎi** 田元愷 after Lǐ successfully treated Tián's father's chronic diarrhea-disease. Lǐ's clinical signature is bold *bǔ-yáng* (warming-tonifying) prescribing in the Sōng-jiāng [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōng-zǐ school manner. His casebook is preserved as [[KR3ep098]] *Jiù-dé-táng yī-àn* with prefaces dated *xīn-chǒu* (probably 1721). Not in CBDB.
