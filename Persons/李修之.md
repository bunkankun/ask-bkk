Lǐ Xiūzhī 李修之, *fǔ* (style 父), early-Qīng physician of Yúnjiān 雲間 (Sōngjiāng, now Shànghǎi). A Sōngjiāng cultivated-gentry-circle physician active in the late seventeenth and early eighteenth century, with clinical reputation in the Hǎiyī 海邑 district. The studio-name *Jiùdétáng* 舊德堂 ("Hall of Inherited Virtue") was conferred by his patron **Tián Yuánkǎi** 田元愷 after Lǐ successfully treated Tián's father's chronic diarrhea-disease. Lǐ's clinical signature is bold *bǔyáng* (warming-tonifying) prescribing in the Sōngjiāng [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ school manner. His casebook is preserved as [[KR3ep098]] *Jiùdétáng yīàn* with prefaces dated *xīnchǒu* (probably 1721). Not in CBDB.
