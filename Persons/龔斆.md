Gōng Xiào 龔斆 (active 1370s–c. 1390s), native of **Yánshān** 鉛山 (Jiāngxī), home of the famous **Éhú** 鵞湖 (Goose Lake) academy. Has no *Míng shǐ* biography; the *Tàizǔ běnjì* records his appointment in Hóngwǔ 13 / *bāyuè bǐngwǔ* (1380) as one of five *Sìfǔ guān chūnxiàguān* 四輔官春夏官 (the short-lived Four Supporters of the Throne offices, established to replace the abolished *Zhōngshūshěng* and disestablished in Hóngwǔ 15 / 1382). The *Yánshān xiàn zhì* preserves the rest of his career: *míngjīng* teaching at **Guǎngxìn**; compiled *Zhūzǐ shuō* 朱子說 and *Bǔ Liùjīng tú* 補六經圖; recommended by *yùshǐ* **Yè Mèngfāng** 葉孟芳; *Sìfǔ guān* (1380–1382); retired on age; recalled as *Guózǐ jìjiǔ* 國子祭酒; died in office. Authored *Jīngyě lèichāo* 經野類鈔 in 28 *juǎn* (lost) and the surviving *Éhú jí* 鵞湖集 [[KR4e0069]] in six *juǎn*, recovered by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. The collection's two prefaces for the 1385 Korea embassy and the 1369–1370 Brunei embassy are documentary discoveries supplementing the *Míng shǐ*.
