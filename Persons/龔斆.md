Gōng Xiào 龔斆 (active 1370s–c. 1390s), native of **Yán-shān** 鉛山 (Jiāng-xī), home of the famous **É-hú** 鵞湖 (Goose Lake) academy. Has no *Míng shǐ* biography; the *Tàizǔ běn-jì* records his appointment in Hóngwǔ 13 / *bā-yuè bǐng-wǔ* (1380) as one of five *Sì-fǔ guān chūn-xià-guān* 四輔官春夏官 (the short-lived Four Supporters of the Throne offices, established to replace the abolished *Zhōng-shū-shěng* and disestablished in Hóngwǔ 15 / 1382). The *Yán-shān xiàn zhì* preserves the rest of his career: *míng-jīng* teaching at **Guǎng-xìn**; compiled *Zhū-zǐ shuō* 朱子說 and *Bǔ Liù-jīng tú* 補六經圖; recommended by *yù-shǐ* **Yè Mèng-fāng** 葉孟芳; *Sì-fǔ guān* (1380–1382); retired on age; recalled as *Guó-zǐ jì-jiǔ* 國子祭酒; died in office. Authored *Jīng-yě lèi-chāo* 經野類鈔 in 28 *juǎn* (lost) and the surviving *É-hú jí* 鵞湖集 [[KR4e0069]] in six *juǎn*, recovered by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*. The collection's two prefaces for the 1385 Korea embassy and the 1369–1370 Brunei embassy are documentary discoveries supplementing the *Míng shǐ*.
