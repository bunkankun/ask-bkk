# Jìng-tǐng 淨挺 (Liáng-tíng Jìng-tǐng 俍亭淨挺)

Early-Qīng Chán master and prolific Buddhist author. Lay name **Xú Jì-ēn** 徐繼恩 (alt. Xú Shì-chén 徐世臣, Xú Yì-tíng 徐逸亭). *Hào* Liáng-tíng 俍亭; also Cí-yún Liáng-tíng Jìng-tǐng 慈雲俍亭淨挺 (for the Cí-yún-sì 慈雲寺 abbacy), Liáng-tíng dàorén 俍亭道人. Native of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). Lifedates 1615/11/24 – 1684/11/1 (Wànlì 43/10/4 – Kāngxī 23/9/24, age 70, per the tomb-inscription in *Xī hé wén jí* 西河文集 juan 9).

Before Buddhist ordination, Jìng-tǐng was a Confucian scholar and Míng-era aspirant to the civil service. Following the 1644 Míng-Qīng collapse he took refuge in Buddhist practice and became one of the most productive Chán-master authors of the early-Qīng Hángzhōu monastic-publishing community.

Major works surviving in the Kanripo corpus:
- [[KR6q0471]] 《雲溪俍亭挺禪師語錄》 18 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B294 — his collected sermons, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-zōng** [[智淙]] 智琮 *děng biān*. Bears the **Cáo-dòng / 三宜明盂** lineage-stamp.
- *Xué fó kǎo xùn* 學佛考訓 [[KR6q0218]] (1670) — ten-juan Chinese Buddhist encyclopedic reference-compilation.
- *Yīn fó wèn* 因佛問 / *Yù lù* 遇錄 / similar — his individual doctrinal essays (subsequent catalog entries, [[KR6q0219]] and beyond).
- Front-preface to [[KR6q0459]] 《明州天童景德禪寺宏智覺禪師語錄》 (the Qīng-period *zhòng-biān* of Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué) — signed *Yì-tíng jū-shì Xú Jì-ēn* 逸亭居士徐繼恩.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665). Hóng-guāng-court 1645 *míng-jīng* graduate; trained as *jū-shì* 居士 under Sān-yí with verification at Yún-mén-sì; tonsured at age 46 (Chóng-zhēn 15 = 1642) after his parents' deaths. Three abbacies: **Cí-yún** 慈雲 (Háng-zhōu), **Fàn-shòu** 梵受, **Xiǎn-shèng** 顯聖. Thirty named heirs including **Zhì-zōng** [[智淙]] (compiler of his *yǔ-lù*).

Bibliophilic orientation — per Yán Hàng's preface to the *Xué fó kǎo xùn*: *yú xué wú bù kuī, zhù shū mǎn jiā* 于學無不窺，著書滿家 ("his learning omits no field, his books fill the house").

Sources: *Xī hé wén jí* 西河文集 juan 9 (Liáng-tíng Tǐng chánshī tomb-inscription).
