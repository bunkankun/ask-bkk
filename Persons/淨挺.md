# Jìngtǐng 淨挺 (Liángtíng Jìngtǐng 俍亭淨挺)

Early-Qīng Chán master and prolific Buddhist author. Lay name **Xú Jìēn** 徐繼恩 (alt. Xú Shìchén 徐世臣, Xú Yìtíng 徐逸亭). *Hào* Liángtíng 俍亭; also Cíyún Liángtíng Jìngtǐng 慈雲俍亭淨挺 (for the Cíyúnsì 慈雲寺 abbacy), Liángtíng dàorén 俍亭道人. Native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). Lifedates 1615/11/24 – 1684/11/1 (Wànlì 43/10/4 – Kāngxī 23/9/24, age 70, per the tomb-inscription in *Xī hé wén jí* 西河文集 juan 9).

Before Buddhist ordination, Jìngtǐng was a Confucian scholar and Míng-era aspirant to the civil service. Following the 1644 MíngQīng collapse he took refuge in Buddhist practice and became one of the most productive Chán-master authors of the early-Qīng Hángzhōu monastic-publishing community.

Major works surviving in the Kanripo corpus:
- [[KR6q0471]] 《雲溪俍亭挺禪師語錄》 18 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B294 — his collected sermons, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìzōng** [[智淙]] 智琮 *děng biān*. Bears the **Cáodòng / 三宜明盂** lineage-stamp.
- *Xué fó kǎo xùn* 學佛考訓 [[KR6q0218]] (1670) — ten-juan Chinese Buddhist encyclopedic reference-compilation.
- *Yīn fó wèn* 因佛問 / *Yù lù* 遇錄 / similar — his individual doctrinal essays (subsequent catalog entries, [[KR6q0219]] and beyond).
- Front-preface to [[KR6q0459]] 《明州天童景德禪寺宏智覺禪師語錄》 (the Qīng-period *zhòngbiān* of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué) — signed *Yìtíng jūshì Xú Jìēn* 逸亭居士徐繼恩.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665). Hóng-guāng-court 1645 *míngjīng* graduate; trained as *jūshì* 居士 under Sānyí with verification at Yúnménsì; tonsured at age 46 (Chóngzhēn 15 = 1642) after his parents' deaths. Three abbacies: **Cíyún** 慈雲 (Hángzhōu), **Fànshòu** 梵受, **Xiǎnshèng** 顯聖. Thirty named heirs including **Zhìzōng** [[智淙]] (compiler of his *yǔlù*).

Bibliophilic orientation — per Yán Hàng's preface to the *Xué fó kǎo xùn*: *yú xué wú bù kuī, zhù shū mǎn jiā* 于學無不窺，著書滿家 ("his learning omits no field, his books fill the house").

Sources: *Xī hé wén jí* 西河文集 juan 9 (Liángtíng Tǐng chánshī tomb-inscription).
