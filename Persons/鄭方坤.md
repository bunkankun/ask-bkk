# Zhèng Fāngkūn 鄭方坤

Sobriquet *Lìxiāng* 荔鄉. Native of Jiàn'ān 建安 in Fújiàn. *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng *guǐmǎo* (1723; the catalog meta carries this date as the work's date — it is in fact Zhèng's *jìnshì* date). He served in a series of provincial posts, rising to *Yǎnzhōu fǔ zhīfǔ* 兗州府知府 (Prefect of Yǎnzhōu in Shāndōng).

His scholarship is in the broad eighteenth-century *kǎozhèng* mainstream — a careful, citation-rich, philologically-conscientious style without major methodological innovation. Beyond the *Jīng bài* 經稗 ([[KR1g0025]]) on canonical scholarship, he produced the *Quán Mín shī huà* 全閩詩話 (a comprehensive collection of poetry-commentary on Fújiàn poets), the *Bǔ Wǔdài shī huà* 補五代詩話 (supplements to *Wǔdài shī huà*), and the *Lǐnghǎi shī huà* 嶺海詩話. He is in scholarly posterity as much a *shī huà* (poetry-criticism) writer as a classical scholar; the *Jīng bài* is his single substantial contribution to *jīngxué*.
