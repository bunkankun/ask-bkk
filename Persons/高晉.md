Mid-Qiánlóng Manchu official, *Hàn jūn xiāng huáng qí* (Han-Banner Bordered Yellow); native of Liáoyáng, of the Gāo Jiǎshì 高佳氏 lineage. *Zì* Zhāodé 昭德; posthumous name Wénduān 文端. Son of Gāo Bīn 高斌, the prominent Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng water-conservancy official. Took office through hereditary privilege; served Qiánlóng as Liǎng-Jiāng Governor-General (1765–1779), Senior Grand Secretary, and director of multiple imperial compilations including the *Qīndìng Nánxún Shèngdiǎn* 欽定南巡盛典 ([[KR2m0039]]) of 1770. He died in office in 1779. Wikipedia and conventional Manchu prosopography give 1707–1779.
