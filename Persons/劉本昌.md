Late-Qing / early-Republican Húnán physician; *zì* Dǔshēng 篤生. Active in Xiāngtán 湘潭 county circles. Trained under Liú Shíruò 劉時若, Hè Zǐrú 賀子儒, and Bīn Jǐntáng 賓錦堂. Author of [[KR3eb030]] *Mài jué xīn biān* (manuscript 1939, printed 1941), a Republican-era pulse compendium defending Wáng Shūhé's pulse-position assignments against the Lǐ Zhōngzǐ / Shěn Wēiyuán Qing critique. Otherwise undocumented; lifedates unknown. No CBDB record.
