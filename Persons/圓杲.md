Late-Míng Buddhist monk; lifedates and biographical detail unrecorded (DILA "生平不詳"). His sole surviving work is the *Jīngāng jīng yīnshì zhíjiě* 金剛經音釋直解 ([[KR6c0071]], X25 no. 483) — a one-juan vernacular *Vajracchedikā* commentary with phonetic glosses (*yīnshì*) intended for non-specialist readers. The work is undated; dynasty assignment 明 follows DILA.
