Yuán-period 元 Shǎnxī Confucian scholar-official; founding-master of the **Lǔzhāi shūyuàn** 魯齋書院 (the regional Yuán Confucian academy named for [[許衡]]); architect of the Yuán Crown-Prince's education under Rénzōng. *Zì* **Kuānfǔ** 寬甫; *hào* (collection-name) **Jǔān** 榘菴; posthumous *shì* **Wénzhēn** 文貞; posthumously enfeoffed **Jīngzhào jùnhóu** 京兆郡侯; posthumously appointed *Hànlín zhíxuéshì*. CBDB 34968 records 1254–1331.

**Origins.** Originally a **Tàiyuán** 太原 (modern Shǎnxī) family that relocated to **Fèngyuán** 奉元 (modern Xīān). At age 13 placed first in the local examination on the *Shūjīng*.

**Career.**
- Zhìyuán era: appointed *Guózǐ sīyè* 國子司業 — declined
- *Shǎnxī xíngtái shì yùshǐ* **Zhào Shìyán** 趙世延 requested establishment of the **Lǔzhāi shūyuàn**; Tóng presided over the teaching
- Yányòu 6 (1319): on the establishment of the Yuán Crown Prince (*huáng tàizǐ*), summoned as *Fèngyì dàfū zuǒ zànshàn* 奉議大夫左贊善
- Next year (1320): on Yīngzōng's succession, returned home on illness
- Zhìhé 1 (1328): appointed *Jíxián shìdú xuéshì* — again declined
- Died 1331

**Imperial-archive function.** Per *Fùzhūlǐ chōng*'s *shéndào bēi* (preserved in the *Jǔān jí*): in Zhìyuán 31 (1294), participated as *biānlù* (compiler-recorder) in the gathering of materials for the **Khubilai (Shìzǔ) *Shílù* and *Yuánshǐ Shìzǔ běnjì***. The *Shǎnxī xíngshěng píngzhāng zhèngshì* **Xiánníngwáng** 咸寧王 (who received Khubilai's posthumous-canonization stele) recruited Tóng for this archive-compilation work.

**Poetic-and-prose taste.** Per Jiǎ Rén's *xíngzhuàng*: loved **Lù Yóu** 陸放翁; admired **Zhōu Bìdà** 周益公 in prose.

**Sìkù editorial intervention.** The Sìkù editors deleted the *qírǎng qīngcí* (Daoist sacrificial blue-words) from the collection on imperial-instruction-authorized grounds that Tóng's self-position as *míngdào xīngjiào* (clarifying-the-way and reviving-the-teaching) Confucian made such pieces inappropriate.
