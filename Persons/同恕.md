Yuán-period 元 Shǎn-xī Confucian scholar-official; founding-master of the **Lǔ-zhāi shū-yuàn** 魯齋書院 (the regional Yuán Confucian academy named for [[許衡]]); architect of the Yuán Crown-Prince's education under Rén-zōng. *Zì* **Kuān-fǔ** 寬甫; *hào* (collection-name) **Jǔ-ān** 榘菴; posthumous *shì* **Wén-zhēn** 文貞; posthumously enfeoffed **Jīng-zhào jùn-hóu** 京兆郡侯; posthumously appointed *Hàn-lín zhí-xué-shì*. CBDB 34968 records 1254–1331.

**Origins.** Originally a **Tài-yuán** 太原 (modern Shǎn-xī) family that relocated to **Fèng-yuán** 奉元 (modern Xī-ān). At age 13 placed first in the local examination on the *Shū-jīng*.

**Career.**
- Zhì-yuán era: appointed *Guó-zǐ sī-yè* 國子司業 — declined
- *Shǎn-xī xíng-tái shì yù-shǐ* **Zhào Shì-yán** 趙世延 requested establishment of the **Lǔ-zhāi shū-yuàn**; Tóng presided over the teaching
- Yán-yòu 6 (1319): on the establishment of the Yuán Crown Prince (*huáng tài-zǐ*), summoned as *Fèng-yì dà-fū zuǒ zàn-shàn* 奉議大夫左贊善
- Next year (1320): on Yīng-zōng's succession, returned home on illness
- Zhì-hé 1 (1328): appointed *Jí-xián shì-dú xué-shì* — again declined
- Died 1331

**Imperial-archive function.** Per *Fù-zhū-lǐ chōng*'s *shén-dào bēi* (preserved in the *Jǔ-ān jí*): in Zhì-yuán 31 (1294), participated as *biān-lù* (compiler-recorder) in the gathering of materials for the **Khubilai (Shì-zǔ) *Shí-lù* and *Yuán-shǐ Shì-zǔ běn-jì***. The *Shǎn-xī xíng-shěng píng-zhāng zhèng-shì* **Xián-níng-wáng** 咸寧王 (who received Khubilai's posthumous-canonization stele) recruited Tóng for this archive-compilation work.

**Poetic-and-prose taste.** Per Jiǎ Rén's *xíng-zhuàng*: loved **Lù Yóu** 陸放翁; admired **Zhōu Bì-dà** 周益公 in prose.

**Sìkù editorial intervention.** The Sìkù editors deleted the *qí-rǎng qīng-cí* (Daoist sacrificial blue-words) from the collection on imperial-instruction-authorized grounds that Tóng's self-position as *míng-dào xīng-jiào* (clarifying-the-way and reviving-the-teaching) Confucian made such pieces inappropriate.
