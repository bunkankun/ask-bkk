Late-Míng *shān-rén* 山人 literatus, antiquarian, and prolific publisher of Huá-tíng 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng prefecture, mod. Shàng-hǎi). After abandoning the examination track in his late twenties, he settled on Mt. Kūn 崑山 and Mt. Shé 佘山 as a celebrated recluse-author, becoming, together with his friend Dǒng Qí-chāng 董其昌, one of the two pre-eminent cultural figures of the Sōng-jiāng region in the late Wàn-lì and Tiān-qǐ eras. His vast output of *bǐ-jì* 筆記, anthologies, and miscellanies — much of it under the rubric of the *Bǎo-yán táng mì-jí* 寶顏堂秘笈 series he edited — made him one of the most-published authors of the late Míng. He provided the 1637 preface to [[KR3er083]] *Yī-zōng bì-dú* (1637) for [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōng-zǐ, whose father he had known.
