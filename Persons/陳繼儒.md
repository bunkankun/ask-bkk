Late-Míng *shānrén* 山人 literatus, antiquarian, and prolific publisher of Huátíng 華亭 (Sōngjiāng prefecture, mod. Shànghǎi). After abandoning the examination track in his late twenties, he settled on Mt. Kūn 崑山 and Mt. Shé 佘山 as a celebrated recluse-author, becoming, together with his friend Dǒng Qíchāng 董其昌, one of the two pre-eminent cultural figures of the Sōngjiāng region in the late Wànlì and Tiānqǐ eras. His vast output of *bǐjì* 筆記, anthologies, and miscellanies — much of it under the rubric of the *Bǎoyán táng mìjí* 寶顏堂秘笈 series he edited — made him one of the most-published authors of the late Míng. He provided the 1637 preface to [[KR3er083]] *Yīzōng bìdú* (1637) for [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, whose father he had known.
