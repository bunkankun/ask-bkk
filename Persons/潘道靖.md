# Pān Dàojìng 潘道靖

Early-Qīng lay disciple (*yōupósāi* 優婆塞) of [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi (1615–1702) during the master's Báiyúnshān 白雲山 retreat-period; co-recorder of the *Wèilín chánshī yúnshān fǎhuì lù* ([[KR6q0371]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
