**Méi Biāo** 梅彪 was a mid-Táng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0299]] *Shí yào ěr yǎ* 石藥爾雅 — a systematic **alchemical lexicon** of mineral drugs (*shí yào* 石藥), dated by its preface to 806 (Yuán hé 1). The work catalogues alternate names (*yì míng* 異名) for minerals used in elixir-making, serving as a *síngua non* for decoding the secretive nomenclature of the alchemical corpus.
