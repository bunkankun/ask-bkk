# Zhìdào 志道

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[祖運]]) of *juan* 3 (Língyán abbacy, from 1345) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
