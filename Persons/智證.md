Late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist monk; lifedates not preserved (DILA A001300). Religious style **Zhòuzhōng** 晝中. Disciple of *Gāofēng Bàopú Dàlián chánshī* 高峰抱璞大蓮禪師 (?–1629); his floruit is therefore datable to the second quarter of the 17th century. Resident at the *Qīngluóān* 青螺菴 in Gǔlóu 古婁 (Tàicāng 太倉, Jiāngsū). Author of the *Cíbēi dàochǎng shuǐchànfǎ suíwén lù* 慈悲道場水懺法隨聞錄 ([[KR6k0202]], X1495), a three-fascicle running commentary on the Táng *Shuǐ chàn*. The DILA entry cites prefaces by **Ruìbīn** 瑞斌, **Yànjìng** 彥淨 and **Dàzhēn** 大貞 to his work; the *Bùshuǐtái jí* 布水臺集 contains the preface of his teacher's *yǔlù*.
