Late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist monk; lifedates not preserved (DILA A001300). Religious style **Zhòu-zhōng** 晝中. Disciple of *Gāo-fēng Bào-pú Dà-lián chán-shī* 高峰抱璞大蓮禪師 (?–1629); his floruit is therefore datable to the second quarter of the 17th century. Resident at the *Qīng-luó-ān* 青螺菴 in Gǔ-lóu 古婁 (Tài-cāng 太倉, Jiāng-sū). Author of the *Cí-bēi dào-chǎng shuǐ-chàn-fǎ suí-wén lù* 慈悲道場水懺法隨聞錄 ([[KR6k0202]], X1495), a three-fascicle running commentary on the Táng *Shuǐ chàn*. The DILA entry cites prefaces by **Ruì-bīn** 瑞斌, **Yàn-jìng** 彥淨 and **Dà-zhēn** 大貞 to his work; the *Bù-shuǐ-tái jí* 布水臺集 contains the preface of his teacher's *yǔ-lù*.
