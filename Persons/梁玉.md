**Liáng Yù** 梁玉, Qīng-period scholar-official of Lóng-quán 龍泉 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìn-shì* of the Ministry of Rites (*Lǐ-bù jìn-shì* 禮部進士), bearer of the *wén-lín-láng* 文林郎 honorific (a 7th-grade civil rank), and *hòu-xuǎn xiàn-yǐn* 候選縣尹 (Magistrate Designate, awaiting appointment). He prefaced Chén Fù-zhèng 陳復正's *Yòuyòu jíchéng* 幼幼集成 ([[KR3ej013]]) c. 1750. His preface, in its informal biographical sketch of Chén's Daoist-physician life and his contrast of medical *yì* (intuitive grasp) with *fāng-shū* (prescription-text learning), is one of the more biographically informative documents on Chén Fù-zhèng.
