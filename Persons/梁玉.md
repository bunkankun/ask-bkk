**Liáng Yù** 梁玉, Qīng-period scholar-official of Lóngquán 龍泉 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of the Ministry of Rites (*Lǐbù jìnshì* 禮部進士), bearer of the *wénlínláng* 文林郎 honorific (a 7th-grade civil rank), and *hòuxuǎn xiànyǐn* 候選縣尹 (Magistrate Designate, awaiting appointment). He prefaced Chén Fùzhèng 陳復正's *Yòuyòu jíchéng* 幼幼集成 ([[KR3ej013]]) c. 1750. His preface, in its informal biographical sketch of Chén's Daoist-physician life and his contrast of medical *yì* (intuitive grasp) with *fāngshū* (prescription-text learning), is one of the more biographically informative documents on Chén Fùzhèng.
