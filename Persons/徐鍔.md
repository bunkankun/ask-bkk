Xú È 徐鍔 (active early 8th c.), Tang court official; per the colophon to [[KR6f0001|the *Mahā-ratnakūṭa* T0310]], he was *cháo yì láng* 朝議郎 ("Court-Discussing Gentleman") and *Hé-nán fǔ Gào-chéng xiàn zhǔ-bù* 河南府告成縣主簿 ("Recorder of Gào-chéng County in Hé-nán Prefecture"). Author of the [[KR6f0104|*Dà bǎo jī jīng shù* 大寶積經述]] (X264) — the *Account of the Mahā-ratnakūṭa* — and the parallel preface-postface to [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci's *Bǎo jī* compilation. Birth and death dates not preserved.
