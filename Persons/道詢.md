Southern-Sòng Buddhist monk and Vinaya editor; lifedates not preserved (DILA A001543). Disciple-successor (in the lineage rather than necessarily a personal disciple, since Yuánzhào died in 1116) of *Língzhī Yuánzhào* 靈芝元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116) at *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 in Hángzhōu. His one surviving work is the editorial compilation *Zhīyuán yíbiān* 芝園遺編 ([[KR6k0216]], X1104), the three-fascicle gathering of Yuánzhào's miscellaneous writings (*jiètǐ* 戒體 treatises, lecture pieces for major patrons including *Ŭich'ŏn 義天* of Goryeo, the founding-inscription for the *Kāiyuánsì* ordination-platform at Míngzhōu, etc.). Distinct from the homonymous *Dìngguāng Dàoxún* 定光道詢 (DILA A014705).
