Sòng Xián 宋咸 (fl. 1037–1058), *zì* Guànzhī 貫之, was a Northern Sòng official and classicist of the Jǐnfēngfǔ 建州 / Jiànyáng 建陽 region (Fújiàn). He took the *jìnshì* in Tiānshèng 2 (1024) and held a sequence of provincial posts, rising to Auxiliary in the Bureau of Sacrifices (*Sīfēng yuánwài láng* 司封員外郎) and Magistrate of Yóuxī 尤溪 in Fúzhōu, with a parallel office as Inspector of Criminal Affairs in Guǎngnánxīlù 廣南西路 諸州軍刑獄. His scholarly work in the 1030s–1050s is concentrated in Confucian classicism and *Zǐ*-tradition recovery: his *Chóngguǎng zhù Yángzǐ Fǎ yán* 重廣注揚子法言 was completed in Jǐngyòu 3 (1036) and presented in Jǐngyòu 4 (1037); his *Kǒng cóngzǐ* commentary in Jiāyòu 3 (1058). Both are absorbed into the SKQS texts ([[KR3a0009]] and [[KR3a0003]] respectively). His *Fǎ yán* commentary added more than five hundred new annotations to fill perceived gaps in Lǐ Guǐ and Liǔ Zōngyuán; together with Wú Mì's parallel work, it forms the layer of Northern Sòng commentary that Sīmǎ Guāng integrated into the standard *Fǎ yán jí zhù*.
