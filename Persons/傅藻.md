Fù Zǎo 傅藻 (fl. late Southern Sòng), compiler of the *Dōngpō Sū xiānshēng jìnián lù* 東坡蘇先生紀年錄 — a chronological record of Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]]'s poetic and prose composition, appended to the SBCK recension of *Zēngkān jiàozhèng Wáng zhuàngyuán jízhù fēnlèi Dōngpō xiānshēng shī* 增刊校正王狀元集註分類東坡先生詩 [[KR4d0078]] (in 25 *juǎn*). Lifedates and offices not preserved in extant Sòng sources; CBDB id 542813 has no further data. Fù Zǎo's *Jìnián lù* is one of the earliest detailed SūShì *biānnián* (chronological) compilations and is a major source for later Wáng Wéngào 王文誥 (Qing) and modern *biānnián jiàozhù* scholarship.
