Fù Zǎo 傅藻 (fl. late Southern Sòng), compiler of the *Dōng-pō Sū xiān-shēng jì-nián lù* 東坡蘇先生紀年錄 — a chronological record of Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]]'s poetic and prose composition, appended to the SBCK recension of *Zēng-kān jiào-zhèng Wáng zhuàng-yuán jí-zhù fēn-lèi Dōng-pō xiān-shēng shī* 增刊校正王狀元集註分類東坡先生詩 [[KR4d0078]] (in 25 *juǎn*). Lifedates and offices not preserved in extant Sòng sources; CBDB id 542813 has no further data. Fù Zǎo's *Jì-nián lù* is one of the earliest detailed Sū-Shì *biān-nián* (chronological) compilations and is a major source for later Wáng Wén-gào 王文誥 (Qing) and modern *biān-nián jiào-zhù* scholarship.
