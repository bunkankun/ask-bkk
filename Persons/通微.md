Wànrú Tōngwēi 萬如通微 (Wànlì 22.5.15 *yín*-hour = 2 July 1594 – Shùnzhì 14.10.29 *sì*-hour = 4 November 1657), dharma-heir of **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] 密雲圓悟 (1566/7–1642) in the Línjì-Yángqí line and the abbatial successor to the Mìyún *zǔtíng* 祖庭 at **Lóngchí Yǔménsì** 荊溪龍池山禹門禪寺 (Yíxīng, Sūzhōu prefecture). Lay surname **Zhāng** 張; native of **Xiùshuǐ** 秀水 (Jiāxīng, Zhèjiāng). Tonsured aged 19 at Xīngshànsì 興善寺 in Jiāxīng under **Hánchū** 涵初; consulted **Zhànrán Yuánchéng** [[圓澄]] 湛然圓澄 on the *wúmèng wúxiǎng* kōan; broke through on the *Shǒuléngyán* passage *zhū kě huán zhě zì rán fēi rǔ* 諸可還者自然非汝. First met Mìyún at Yánguān Jīnsù; sealed at Jīnsù aged 39 *sùi* (1632). Held abbacies at **Dōngtǎ** 東塔 (Jiāxīng, early–mid Chóngzhēn, pre-1640), **Rúrú chányuàn** 如如禪院 (Jiāxīng, Chóngzhēn 13 = 1640 winter onward), **Cáoshān Shàngshēngsì** 曹山上生禪寺 (Pútián, Fújiàn), and finally **Lóngchí Yǔménsì** from 1645 until his death, succeeding the Mìyún-home-seat of his uncle-generation. *Shìshòu* 64 / *fǎlà* 45.

Ten dharma-heirs are enumerated (several dozens of attested audience members); the four principal heirs in the 《五燈全書》 cluster are **Yǒngtài Mó** 永泰模, **Tiěguān Néng** 鐵關能, **Gǔfān Jí** 古帆楫, and **Fókě Shì** 佛可式. Other significant heirs are **Jièwéi Xíngzhōu** 介為行舟 (1611–1670), the principal editor of Wànrú's *yǔlù* and the Yíxīng / Tiānjīn / Běijīng Mìyún-line proselytizer; **Dàbó Xíngqián** 大博行乾 (1602–1673), the Línjì proselytizer to Běizhílì and founder of a Pánshān 盤山 *chányuàn* network; and **Yìnxū Xíngyóu** [[行猷]] 印虛行猷 (fl. mid-Qīng Shùnzhì), the nominal *děngbiān* of the present *yǔlù*. The cumulative effect is that Wànrú is the most institutionally consequential Mìyún heir on the Jiāngnán home front (alongside Fèiyǐn Tōngróng 費隱通容 at Huángbò and Pòshān Hǎimíng at Sichuan Shuāngguì) — he held the *zǔtíng* itself.

Notable Kanripo work: [[KR6q0404]] 《萬如禪師語錄》 10 juan (J26 B182), cut on-site at Lóngchí Yǔménsì in Shùnzhì 14 (1657) winter through Shùnzhì 15 (1658) early summer, framed by prefaces dating across the Chóngzhēn–Shùnzhì transition by **Táng Shìjì** 唐世濟, **Huáng Duānbó** 黃端伯 (1585–1645, the Ming-loyalist martyr), **Gāo Shìtài** 高世泰 (Wúxī Dōnglín), and **Tán Zhēnmò** 譚貞默 (1641 and 1659 double-preface). The text is the primary source for his life and is uniquely self-curated (Wànrú in spring 1657, months before his death, reduced his full working *yǔlù* to one-fifth and edited it to its present ten-juan extent). Secondary biographies in 《五燈全書》 76, 《五燈會元續略》 1, 《正源略集》 10.
