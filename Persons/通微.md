Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi 萬如通微 (Wàn-lì 22.5.15 *yín*-hour = 2 July 1594 – Shùn-zhì 14.10.29 *sì*-hour = 4 November 1657), dharma-heir of **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] 密雲圓悟 (1566/7–1642) in the Línjì-Yángqí line and the abbatial successor to the Mìyún *zǔ-tíng* 祖庭 at **Lóng-chí Yǔ-mén-sì** 荊溪龍池山禹門禪寺 (Yí-xīng, Sūzhōu prefecture). Lay surname **Zhāng** 張; native of **Xiù-shuǐ** 秀水 (Jiā-xīng, Zhè-jiāng). Tonsured aged 19 at Xīng-shàn-sì 興善寺 in Jiā-xīng under **Hán-chū** 涵初; consulted **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** [[圓澄]] 湛然圓澄 on the *wú-mèng wú-xiǎng* kōan; broke through on the *Shǒu-léng-yán* passage *zhū kě huán zhě zì rán fēi rǔ* 諸可還者自然非汝. First met Mìyún at Yán-guān Jīn-sù; sealed at Jīn-sù aged 39 *sùi* (1632). Held abbacies at **Dōng-tǎ** 東塔 (Jiā-xīng, early–mid Chóng-zhēn, pre-1640), **Rú-rú chán-yuàn** 如如禪院 (Jiā-xīng, Chóng-zhēn 13 = 1640 winter onward), **Cáo-shān Shàng-shēng-sì** 曹山上生禪寺 (Pú-tián, Fú-jiàn), and finally **Lóng-chí Yǔ-mén-sì** from 1645 until his death, succeeding the Mìyún-home-seat of his uncle-generation. *Shì-shòu* 64 / *fǎ-là* 45.

Ten dharma-heirs are enumerated (several dozens of attested audience members); the four principal heirs in the 《五燈全書》 cluster are **Yǒng-tài Mó** 永泰模, **Tiě-guān Néng** 鐵關能, **Gǔ-fān Jí** 古帆楫, and **Fó-kě Shì** 佛可式. Other significant heirs are **Jiè-wéi Xíng-zhōu** 介為行舟 (1611–1670), the principal editor of Wàn-rú's *yǔlù* and the Yí-xīng / Tiān-jīn / Běi-jīng Mìyún-line proselytizer; **Dà-bó Xíng-qián** 大博行乾 (1602–1673), the Línjì proselytizer to Běi-zhí-lì and founder of a Pán-shān 盤山 *chán-yuàn* network; and **Yìn-xū Xíng-yóu** [[行猷]] 印虛行猷 (fl. mid-Qīng Shùn-zhì), the nominal *děng-biān* of the present *yǔlù*. The cumulative effect is that Wàn-rú is the most institutionally consequential Mìyún heir on the Jiāng-nán home front (alongside Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng 費隱通容 at Huáng-bò and Pòshān Hǎi-míng at Sichuan Shuāng-guì) — he held the *zǔ-tíng* itself.

Notable Kanripo work: [[KR6q0404]] 《萬如禪師語錄》 10 juan (J26 B182), cut on-site at Lóng-chí Yǔ-mén-sì in Shùn-zhì 14 (1657) winter through Shùn-zhì 15 (1658) early summer, framed by prefaces dating across the Chóng-zhēn–Shùn-zhì transition by **Táng Shì-jì** 唐世濟, **Huáng Duān-bó** 黃端伯 (1585–1645, the Ming-loyalist martyr), **Gāo Shì-tài** 高世泰 (Wú-xī Dōng-lín), and **Tán Zhēn-mò** 譚貞默 (1641 and 1659 double-preface). The text is the primary source for his life and is uniquely self-curated (Wàn-rú in spring 1657, months before his death, reduced his full working *yǔlù* to one-fifth and edited it to its present ten-juan extent). Secondary biographies in 《五燈全書》 76, 《五燈會元續略》 1, 《正源略集》 10.
