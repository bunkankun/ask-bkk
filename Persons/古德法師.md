# Gǔdé fǎshī 古德法師 ("the Master of Antique-Virtue", late Míng)

A late-Míng monk active at Yún-qī-sì 雲棲寺 — the temple of [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng — known almost exclusively for his *Ē-mí-tuó jīng shū-chāo yǎn-yì* 阿彌陀經疏鈔演義 [[KR6p0022]] in four *juǎn*, an explanatory amplification (*yǎn-yì* 演義) of Zhūhóng's *Shū-chāo* [[KR6p0019]]. The *Yǎn-yì* signs him simply as 雲棲古德法師, with the parallel attribution 門人慈帆智願定本 (*finalised by his disciple Cíháng Zhì-yuàn*).

His personal name (*míng* 名) is not preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* recension or in the standard biographical literature; "Gǔdé" 古德 ("antique virtue") functions as a *hào* 號 rather than as a personal name, and his identity is now lost behind the title. The implication of *gǔ-dé* in the immediate Yúnqī-circle context is that he was a senior disciple — perhaps an honorific designation given by Zhūhóng or by a Yún-qī-sì abbot. The *Yǎn-yì* was completed and finalised by his pupil [[慈航智願]] Cíháng Zhì-yuàn shortly after his death; the date of composition is not preserved but plausibly falls in the early seventeenth century, after Zhūhóng's own death (1615).
