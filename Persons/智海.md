Huìshān Zhìhǎi 慧山智海 (fl. mid-17th c.). Early-Qīng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) Chán master, dharma-heir of **Yǎng-zhuō Zhèng-míng** [[正明]] 養拙正明 (? – 1649) at **Dà-wéi Mì-yìn-sì** 大溈密印寺 (Hú-nán), and thus dharma-grandson (*fǎ-sūn* 法孫) of **Wǔ-fēng Rúxué** [[如學]] 五峰如學 (1585–1633). Native of **Shàng-háng** 上杭 (Fú-jiàn), lay surname **Lín** 林; precise lifedates not transmitted. As a boy of fifteen he was travelling with his merchant father in Hé-nán; stranded without resources one night, he took shelter at a small monastery, where a monk exhorted him to leave the household. At twenty-one he reached **Hàn-yáng** 漢陽 and tonsured at the **Dì-zàng-ān** 地藏菴, then sought Zhèng-míng at Tóng-qìng-sì on Dà-wéi-shān and served him for fifteen years. He then withdrew to **Nán-yuè** 南嶽 (Héng-shān) and built a straw hut at the **Pí-lú-dòng** 毘盧洞. When Zhèng-míng was approaching death in 1649 he sent a hand-written summons and his *seng-jiā-lí* robe, commanding the community to retrieve Zhì-hǎi to succeed him at **Mì-yìn-sì**. Zhì-hǎi duly took the abbacy of Mì-yìn and "re-cut" (*chóng-kè* 重刻) the *yǔlù* of his grandfather Rúxué — the one-juan *Dà-wéi Wǔ-fēng Xué chán-shī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0400]], J25 B175) — and compiled (*biān* 編) his own teacher Zhèng-míng's *yǔlù* in one juan ([[KR6q0401]]; *Dà-wéi Mì-yìn-sì Yǎng-zhuō Míng chán-shī yǔlù* 大溈密印寺養拙明禪師語錄, J25 B176). His seven principal dharma-heirs include **Yǔ-fēng Zhì** 與峯智, **Gǔ-méi Liè** 古梅冽, and **Yì-ān Yìng** 易菴應. Sources: *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 82; *Zhèng-yuán lüè-jí* 正源略集 juan 9 and 11.

# Zhì-hǎi 智海 (Cáo-dòng / Bǎi-yú line)

A **different** Zhì-hǎi: Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Bǎi-yú Jìng-sī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (1610–1665) in the Ruì-bái Míng-xuě → Bǎi-yú line. Co-compiler with **Zhì-cāo** [[智操]] of the 20-juan 《百愚禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0498]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
