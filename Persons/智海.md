Huìshān Zhìhǎi 慧山智海 (fl. mid-17th c.). Early-Qīng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) Chán master, dharma-heir of **Yǎngzhuō Zhèngmíng** [[正明]] 養拙正明 (? – 1649) at **Dàwéi Mìyìnsì** 大溈密印寺 (Húnán), and thus dharma-grandson (*fǎsūn* 法孫) of **Wǔfēng Rúxué** [[如學]] 五峰如學 (1585–1633). Native of **Shàngháng** 上杭 (Fújiàn), lay surname **Lín** 林; precise lifedates not transmitted. As a boy of fifteen he was travelling with his merchant father in Hénán; stranded without resources one night, he took shelter at a small monastery, where a monk exhorted him to leave the household. At twenty-one he reached **Hànyáng** 漢陽 and tonsured at the **Dìzàngān** 地藏菴, then sought Zhèngmíng at Tóngqìngsì on Dàwéishān and served him for fifteen years. He then withdrew to **Nányuè** 南嶽 (Héngshān) and built a straw hut at the **Pílúdòng** 毘盧洞. When Zhèngmíng was approaching death in 1649 he sent a hand-written summons and his *seng-jiā-lí* robe, commanding the community to retrieve Zhìhǎi to succeed him at **Mìyìnsì**. Zhìhǎi duly took the abbacy of Mìyìn and "re-cut" (*chóngkè* 重刻) the *yǔlù* of his grandfather Rúxué — the one-juan *Dàwéi Wǔfēng Xué chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0400]], J25 B175) — and compiled (*biān* 編) his own teacher Zhèngmíng's *yǔlù* in one juan ([[KR6q0401]]; *Dàwéi Mìyìnsì Yǎngzhuō Míng chánshī yǔlù* 大溈密印寺養拙明禪師語錄, J25 B176). His seven principal dharma-heirs include **Yǔfēng Zhì** 與峯智, **Gǔméi Liè** 古梅冽, and **Yìān Yìng** 易菴應. Sources: *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 82; *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* 正源略集 juan 9 and 11.

# Zhìhǎi 智海 (Cáodòng / Bǎiyú line)

A **different** Zhìhǎi: Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Bǎiyú Jìngsī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (1610–1665) in the Ruìbái Míngxuě → Bǎiyú line. Co-compiler with **Zhìcāo** [[智操]] of the 20-juan 《百愚禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0498]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
