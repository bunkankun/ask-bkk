**Rén Yuèān** 任越庵 (*fl.* mid-Qiánlóng, ca. 1760s–1790s, 清), Qīng physician and disciple of the [[柯琴]] Kē Qín *Shānghán*-school tradition. He is known principally as the transcriber and publisher of Kē Qín's posthumous *Shānghán fǎ zǔ* 傷寒法祖 ([[KR3ef042]], 2 juan) — manuscripts preserved in the Kē family and brought to print in the mid-Qiánlóng period. His exact lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
