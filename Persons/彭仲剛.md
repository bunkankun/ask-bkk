Péng Zhòng-gāng 彭仲剛 (Southern Sòng; CBDB id 33929) was a county magistrate (*lìng-jūn*) of an unspecified locality. He composed the *Yù sú xù* 諭俗續 in 1 *juàn* — a continuation of [[鄭玉道]] (Zhèng Yù-dào)'s *Yù sú biān*. The *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén zhì* records it as 1 *juàn*. The two books were later combined and supplemented by [[應俊]] (Yīng Jùn) as the *Qín táng yù sú biān* ([[KR3j0126]]).
