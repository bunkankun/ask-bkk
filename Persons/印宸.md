Yìn-chén 印宸, *hào* **Yún-huàn** 雲幻 ("Cloud-Illusion"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **1623**; died **康熙己酉 4/16** = **1669-05-15 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **47**. Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān).

**Abbacy.** **Bā-zhōu Lóng-tán Zhōng-fēng Chán-sì** 巴州龍潭中峰禪寺 — entry-sermon dated **順治戊戌 = 1658**.

**Heir.** **Héng-xiù Lín** [[發林]] 恒秀發林 (compiler of [[KR6q0493]] and own *yǔ-lù* at [[KR6q0494]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0493]] 《雲幻宸禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B353, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fā-lín** [[發林]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Wáng Mín-hào** [[王民皞]] of **Gǔ-lín** 古臨, dated **康熙癸丑春 = 1673 spring**, written at **Diān-nán Ā-mí** 滇南阿迷 *zhì-suǒ* (= Wáng's local-administration post in Yún-nán). *Tǎ-jì* by **Lǐ Dào-tài** [[李道泰]] in juan 1.

Sources: [[KR6q0493]] juan 1 *xù* + *tǎ-jì*; DILA authority A016490.
