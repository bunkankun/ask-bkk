# Cháng-jǐn 常謹

A Northern-Sòng Buddhist monk-historiographer; identified in the colophon of his sole transmitted work as **傳教沙門常謹** ("Cháng-jǐn, Dharma-transmitting śramaṇa"). Lifedates not preserved; the work — the **《地藏菩薩像靈驗記》** [[KR6r0182]] *Dì-zàng pú-sà xiàng líng-yàn jì* (X1638, 1 *juǎn*) — is dated by the author's own colophon to **大宋端拱己丑歲 = Duān-gǒng 2 = 989 CE**, fixing his *floruit* in the early Sòng (Sòng Tài-zōng's reign). He is therefore an early-Sòng monk active in the immediate post-Wǔ-dài transitional period.

The compilation methodology described in his preface — "**Cháng-jǐn, the Dharma-transmitting śramaṇa, has *zhí-bǐ ér jí* 直筆而集 [recorded with a straight brush and compiled]**" — places him in the Tang-Sòng *gǎn-tōng-lù* tradition descending from Dào-xuān, with the same documentary-veracitative posture. He is otherwise undocumented in standard Buddhological reference works.
