# Kǒng Qiū 孔丘 (551–479 BCE)

*Zì* Zhòngní 仲尼; later styled Kǒngzǐ 孔子, Confucius. Native of Zōuyì 陬邑 in the state of Lǔ 魯 (modern Qūfù 曲阜, Shāndōng), of an aristocratic but impoverished Sòng-descended Lǔ family. The pre-eminent figure of the Confucian tradition; teacher to a community of disciples whose dialogues with the Master, recorded and transmitted, became the *Lúnyǔ* 論語.

The principal early biography is the *Kǒngzǐ shìjiā* 孔子世家 in *Shǐjì* 史記 47, supplemented by the *Zhòngní dìzǐ lièzhuàn* 仲尼弟子列傳 (juan 67) and notices throughout the *Zuǒzhuàn* 左傳 and *Lúnyǔ* itself. Conventional dates are 551–479 BCE; he was an itinerant counsellor in Lǔ, Wèi, Sòng, Chén, Cài, and Chǔ before returning to Lǔ to teach.

Confucius is **not the author** of any work in the *Sì shū* (the *Lúnyǔ* is a posthumous compilation by his disciples and grand-disciples; the *Dàxué* 大學 is a *Lǐjì* chapter conventionally attributed to his disciple Zēngzǐ 曾參; the *Zhōngyōng* 中庸 to his grandson Zǐsī 子思 / Kǒng Jí 孔伋), but the entire *Sì shū* tradition turns on his teaching. Late Hàn and Song traditions also credit him with editing the *Five Classics*: the *Yì* 易, *Shū* 書, *Shī* 詩, *Lǐ* 禮, and *Chūnqiū* 春秋 — though only the *Chūnqiū* is securely his own redaction. His attributed compilation of the *Xiào jīng* 孝經 is similarly a Hàn-period attribution.

Surviving works in Kanripo to which he is the **subject** rather than the author: the *Lúnyǔ* canonical text [[KR1h0004]]; HànWèi *jíjiě* edition by Hé Yàn [[KR1h0005]], [[KR1h0006]]; Sòng *zhèngyì* [[KR1h0007]]; Hán Yù / Lǐ Áo *Lúnyǔ bǐjiě* [[KR1h0008]]; and the entire *Sì shū* tradition through Zhū Xī's *Sì shū jí zhù* and its later commentaries.
