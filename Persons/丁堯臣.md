**Dīng Yáochén** 丁堯臣 (*fl.* mid-19th c., late Qīng), compiler of the *Qíxiào jiǎnbiàn liángfāng* 奇效簡便良方 ([[KR3ed114]]), a popular emergency-and-clinical formulary in 4 *juǎn* organised by body-region. The work circulated in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries; the principal extant recension is the 1924 *Guāngyùtáng* 光裕堂 (Jílín) Buddhist-charitable reprint, which marks itself as an edited version with animal-sacrifice recipes systematically removed. Sparse biographical information beyond the title-line.
