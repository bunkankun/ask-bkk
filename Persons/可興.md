# Kě-xīng 可興

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[文康]]) of *juan* 2 (Jiā-xīng Běn-jué abbacy, from 1333) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
