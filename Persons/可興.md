# Kěxīng 可興

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[文康]]) of *juan* 2 (Jiāxīng Běnjué abbacy, from 1333) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
