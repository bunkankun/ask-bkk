# Yáo Fàn 姚範

*Zì* Nánjīng 南菁; *hào* Jiāngwù 薑塢. Native of Tǒngchéng 桐城 (Anhuī). *Jìnshì* of *Qiánlóng* 7 (1742). Lifedates 1702–1771.

Member of the great Tǒngchéng-school 桐城派 of Qing prose, paternal uncle (or older relative) of [[姚鼐]] (Yáo Nài, 1731–1815), the dean of the school. Held *Hànlín shìjiǎng* 翰林侍講 and various editorial posts in the Wǔyīngdiàn. As Wǔyīngdiàn editor he contributed *kǎozhèng* notes to several of the *Sìkù* *zhèngshǐ* — most prominently the *Běi Qí shū* ([[KR2a0021]]).

Principal works: the *Yuán Chún táng bǐ jì* 援鶉堂筆記 (50 *juǎn*), a *kǎozhèng*-style miscellany covering classics, history, and *yīnyùn* — the principal vehicle of his scholarly opinions; the *Yuán Chún táng wén jí* 援鶉堂文集. Yáo Fàn's writings are particularly important as a transitional document between the Qing kǎozhèng tradition (in which he was deeply trained) and the Tǒngchéng *gǔwén* aesthetic (which he passed to his nephew Yáo Nài).
