Xú Zǐdōng 徐子東, son of [[徐總幹]] "Xú Zǒnggàn" (the Comptroller-Xú), the unidentified author of *Yì chuán dēng* 易傳燈 ([[KR1a0044]]). Wrote the preface dated *Bǎoyòu dīngsì* (1257) mid-spring sixteenth day that is the principal source for the father's biography (Wùzhōu Dōnglái-school discipleship under [[呂祖謙]] Lǚ Zǔqiān, [[唐仲友]] Táng Zhòngyǒu, and [[宋真卿]] Sòng Zhēnqīng). Cut the woodblocks of the *Yì chuán dēng* posthumously.
