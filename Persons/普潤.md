Pǔrùn 普潤. Personal attendant (*shìzhě*) of **Dǒunán Wěi** [[暐]]. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0596]] 《斗南暐禪師語錄》 3 juan, J40 B488.

- - - - -

A separate Wàn-lì-era monk **Pǔrùn** 普潤 (DILA A012513) of **Jīzúshān** 雞足山 (Yúnnán) was active in the late Míng / early Qīng founding of **Fèngzhēnān** 奉真庵 there. Not the same person.
