Pǔ-rùn 普潤. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě*) of **Dǒu-nán Wěi** [[暐]]. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0596]] 《斗南暐禪師語錄》 3 juan, J40 B488.

- - - - -

A separate Wàn-lì-era monk **Pǔ-rùn** 普潤 (DILA A012513) of **Jī-zú-shān** 雞足山 (Yún-nán) was active in the late Míng / early Qīng founding of **Fèng-zhēn-ān** 奉真庵 there. Not the same person.
