# Wàn Lán 萬蘭

Early-Míng paediatrician of Yùzhāng 豫章 (modern Nánchāng 南昌, Jiāngxī), *hào* Xìngchéng 杏城翁. First-generation founder of the Luótián 羅田 Wàn-family medical lineage; died young. Father of [[萬筱齋]] (萬筱齋) and grandfather of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (1499–1582). Lifedates unrecorded.
