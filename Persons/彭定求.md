**Péng Dìng-qiú** 彭定求 (1645–1719), *zì* **Qín-zhǐ** 勤止, *hào* **Yǒng-zhēn shān-rén** 詠眞山人 / **Nán-yún** 南畇 — Kāng-xī era *zhuàng-yuán* (top-of-class jìn-shì, 1670), Hanlin academician, principal editorial hand on the *Quán Táng shī* 全唐詩 (the imperial Tang-poetry compilation, 1707) and the *Pèi-wén yùn-fǔ* 佩文韻府. Native of Cháng-zhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu, Jiāng-sū). Like his contemporary Chén Tíng-jìng (cf. [[陳廷敬]]), Péng combined orthodox-Confucian high office with private interest in Daoist *yǎng-shēng*: he was the steward of the family's manuscript copy of the **Zhēn quán** 真詮 inner-alchemical anthology that his father had received in his youth, and re-engraved it in **Kāng-xī gēng-yín = mid-winter 1710** ([[KR5i0074]]) under his *hào* Yǒng-zhēn shān-rén.
