**Péng Dìngqiú** 彭定求 (1645–1719), *zì* **Qínzhǐ** 勤止, *hào* **Yǒngzhēn shānrén** 詠眞山人 / **Nányún** 南畇 — Kāngxī era *zhuàngyuán* (top-of-class jìnshì, 1670), Hanlin academician, principal editorial hand on the *Quán Táng shī* 全唐詩 (the imperial Tang-poetry compilation, 1707) and the *Pèiwén yùnfǔ* 佩文韻府. Native of Chángzhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Like his contemporary Chén Tíngjìng (cf. [[陳廷敬]]), Péng combined orthodox-Confucian high office with private interest in Daoist *yǎngshēng*: he was the steward of the family's manuscript copy of the **Zhēn quán** 真詮 inner-alchemical anthology that his father had received in his youth, and re-engraved it in **Kāngxī gēngyín = mid-winter 1710** ([[KR5i0074]]) under his *hào* Yǒngzhēn shānrén.
