Wáng Zhìdào 王志道 (1574–1646), *zì* **Érhóng** 而宏, *hào* **Dōnglǐ** 東里; native of **Zhāngpǔ** 漳浦 (Zhāngzhōu prefecture, Fújiàn). *Jǔrén* of Wànlì 34 / *bǐngwǔ* (1606), *jìnshì* of Wànlì 41 / *guǐchǒu* (1613); appointed *Dānyáng zhīxiàn*. Rose to *Lǐkē jǐshìzhōng* (1621) and *Bīngkē yòu jǐshìzhōng* (1622), then retired to care for aged parents after his discussion of the *sānàn* (three cases) was rebutted by Gāo Pānlóng 高攀龍. Recalled in *Chóngzhēn* 1 / 1628 as *Zuǒ tōngzhèng*; rose to *Dàlǐ yòu shàoqīng* and *Zuǒ fù dū yùshǐ*. Stripped of office after his memorial in defence of Chén Yútài 陳于泰 against the Xuānfǔ supervising eunuch Wáng Kūn 王坤 angered the throne. Under the Southern-Míng Hóngguāng régime, *Hùbù yòu shìláng*; under the Lóngwǔ (Tángwáng) régime, *Lìbù zuǒ shìláng*. Died at home in the fourth month of *bǐngxū* (= 1646) aged 73. His preface to **[[施邦曜]]**'s *Yángmíng xiānshēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) is dated *Chóngzhēn yǐhài* 7th month, *yǐmǎo* day = 1635, signing himself *Mǐn-Zhāng hòuxué Wáng Zhìdào*. CBDB 101221.
