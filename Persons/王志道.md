Wáng Zhì-dào 王志道 (1574–1646), *zì* **Ér-hóng** 而宏, *hào* **Dōng-lǐ** 東里; native of **Zhāng-pǔ** 漳浦 (Zhāng-zhōu prefecture, Fú-jiàn). *Jǔ-rén* of Wàn-lì 34 / *bǐng-wǔ* (1606), *jìnshì* of Wàn-lì 41 / *guǐ-chǒu* (1613); appointed *Dān-yáng zhī-xiàn*. Rose to *Lǐ-kē jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1621) and *Bīng-kē yòu jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1622), then retired to care for aged parents after his discussion of the *sān-àn* (three cases) was rebutted by Gāo Pān-lóng 高攀龍. Recalled in *Chóng-zhēn* 1 / 1628 as *Zuǒ tōng-zhèng*; rose to *Dà-lǐ yòu shào-qīng* and *Zuǒ fù dū yù-shǐ*. Stripped of office after his memorial in defence of Chén Yú-tài 陳于泰 against the Xuān-fǔ supervising eunuch Wáng Kūn 王坤 angered the throne. Under the Southern-Míng Hóng-guāng régime, *Hù-bù yòu shì-láng*; under the Lóng-wǔ (Táng-wáng) régime, *Lì-bù zuǒ shì-láng*. Died at home in the fourth month of *bǐng-xū* (= 1646) aged 73. His preface to **[[施邦曜]]**'s *Yáng-míng xiān-shēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) is dated *Chóng-zhēn yǐ-hài* 7th month, *yǐ-mǎo* day = 1635, signing himself *Mǐn-Zhāng hòu-xué Wáng Zhì-dào*. CBDB 101221.
