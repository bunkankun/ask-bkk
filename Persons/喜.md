# Xǐ 喜 (Yúné Xǐ 雲峨喜)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán master of **Sichuan origin**, **32nd-generation Línjì** in the Mìyún line. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Xǐ** 喜 (single-character, as with other 行-generation irregulars), *hào* **Yúné** 雲峨 ("Cloud-Tall"). A **Sichuan-born master dispatched on the northern-expansion Chán mission of the 1650s**, holding seven successive abbacies across Ānhuī, Hénán, Húběi, and Shǎnxī in the period 1650–c. 1663. **Double-transmission**: first trained under **Pòshān Hǎimíng** 破山海明 (1597–1666, Sichuan-base, cf. [[KR6q0402]]) and then formally sealed by **Línyě Tōngqí** [[通奇]] 林野通奇 (1595/6–1652, cf. [[KR6q0408]]) at **Tiāntóng** during Línyě's 1650–52 abbacy. Yúné appears as one of the **18 named Tiān-tóng-period dharma-heirs** of Línyě in the Mùchén Dàomǐn *tǎmíng* at [[KR6q0408]] juan 8.

**Seven abbacies (1650–c. 1663).** (1) **Huòqiū Dàbiéshān Pǔjì Chánsì** 霍丘大別山普濟禪寺 — first abbacy, entered Shùnzhì 7 庚寅 10.8 = **2 November 1650**. (2) **Rǔníng Guāngshānxiàn Pǔfú Chányuàn** 汝寧光山縣普福禪院. (3) **Luóshān Guóxiángshān Lóngchí Chánsì** 羅山國祥山龍池禪寺 (a symbolic-lineage name after **Lóngchí** of Huànyǒu Zhèngchuán 幻有正傳, Mi-yun's teacher). (4) **Húguǎng Suízhōu Luòhuáng Fǎxìngsì** 湖廣隨州落黃法興寺. (5) **Hénánfǔ Rǔzhōu Fēngxué Chánsì** 河南府汝州風穴禪寺 — **the ancestral seat of Fēngxué Yánzhào** 風穴延沼 (896–973), classical Línjì-revival site. (6) **Shǎnxī Chángān Dàxīngshànsì** 陝西長安大興善寺 — **the Táng imperial sūtra-translation center**, among the most prestigious ancient Buddhist institutional sites. (7) **Re-zhù Fēngxué** 再住風穴 — second tenure at Fēngxué.

**Historical significance.** Yúné's northern-mission, parallel to **Běnshēng Tiānàn** 天岸本昇 (at [[KR6q0409]], Shāndōng Qīngzhōu under Mùchén patronage), represents the **Pòshān / Línyě dual-patronage wing** of the Mìyún-line post-1644 northern-expansion program. The sequential holding of three prestige-seats (Lóngchí, Fēngxué, Dàxīngshàn) constructs a deliberate lineage-claim to ancient Línjì and imperial-Buddhist institutional heritage.

Work: [[KR6q0422]] 《雲峨喜禪師語錄》 (2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J28 B203, compiled by [[智恒]] Zhìhéng). Cut with two 1663 prefaces: one by Pòshān Hǎimíng himself (lunar 11), and one by Yán dàcān *tóngzōng fǎdì* (lunar 9, age 74).

Native place: **Sichuan**. Lay surname, specific birthdate, and death-date unrecovered from accessible sources.

Sources: [[KR6q0422]] juan 1 Pòshān Hǎimíng preface (1663-11) and Yán dàcān preface (1663-9); [[KR6q0408]] juan 8 Mùchén Dàomǐn *tǎmíng* for Línyě (lists Yúné as Línyě's Tiān-tóng-period dharma-heir); *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 65.
