# Xǐ 喜 (Yún-é Xǐ 雲峨喜)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán master of **Sichuan origin**, **32nd-generation Línjì** in the Mìyún line. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Xǐ** 喜 (single-character, as with other 行-generation irregulars), *hào* **Yún-é** 雲峨 ("Cloud-Tall"). A **Sichuan-born master dispatched on the northern-expansion Chán mission of the 1650s**, holding seven successive abbacies across Ān-huī, Hé-nán, Hú-běi, and Shǎn-xī in the period 1650–c. 1663. **Double-transmission**: first trained under **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** 破山海明 (1597–1666, Sichuan-base, cf. [[KR6q0402]]) and then formally sealed by **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** [[通奇]] 林野通奇 (1595/6–1652, cf. [[KR6q0408]]) at **Tiān-tóng** during Lín-yě's 1650–52 abbacy. Yún-é appears as one of the **18 named Tiān-tóng-period dharma-heirs** of Lín-yě in the Mù-chén Dào-mǐn *tǎ-míng* at [[KR6q0408]] juan 8.

**Seven abbacies (1650–c. 1663).** (1) **Huò-qiū Dà-bié-shān Pǔ-jì Chán-sì** 霍丘大別山普濟禪寺 — first abbacy, entered Shùn-zhì 7 庚寅 10.8 = **2 November 1650**. (2) **Rǔ-níng Guāng-shān-xiàn Pǔ-fú Chán-yuàn** 汝寧光山縣普福禪院. (3) **Luó-shān Guó-xiáng-shān Lóng-chí Chán-sì** 羅山國祥山龍池禪寺 (a symbolic-lineage name after **Lóng-chí** of Huàn-yǒu Zhèng-chuán 幻有正傳, Mi-yun's teacher). (4) **Hú-guǎng Suí-zhōu Luò-huáng Fǎ-xìng-sì** 湖廣隨州落黃法興寺. (5) **Hé-nán-fǔ Rǔ-zhōu Fēng-xué Chán-sì** 河南府汝州風穴禪寺 — **the ancestral seat of Fēng-xué Yán-zhào** 風穴延沼 (896–973), classical Línjì-revival site. (6) **Shǎn-xī Cháng-ān Dà-xīng-shàn-sì** 陝西長安大興善寺 — **the Táng imperial sūtra-translation center**, among the most prestigious ancient Buddhist institutional sites. (7) **Re-zhù Fēng-xué** 再住風穴 — second tenure at Fēng-xué.

**Historical significance.** Yún-é's northern-mission, parallel to **Běn-shēng Tiān-àn** 天岸本昇 (at [[KR6q0409]], Shāndōng Qīng-zhōu under Mù-chén patronage), represents the **Pò-shān / Lín-yě dual-patronage wing** of the Mìyún-line post-1644 northern-expansion program. The sequential holding of three prestige-seats (Lóng-chí, Fēng-xué, Dà-xīng-shàn) constructs a deliberate lineage-claim to ancient Línjì and imperial-Buddhist institutional heritage.

Work: [[KR6q0422]] 《雲峨喜禪師語錄》 (2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B203, compiled by [[智恒]] Zhì-héng). Cut with two 1663 prefaces: one by Pò-shān Hǎi-míng himself (lunar 11), and one by Yán dà-cān *tóng-zōng fǎ-dì* (lunar 9, age 74).

Native place: **Sichuan**. Lay surname, specific birthdate, and death-date unrecovered from accessible sources.

Sources: [[KR6q0422]] juan 1 Pò-shān Hǎi-míng preface (1663-11) and Yán dà-cān preface (1663-9); [[KR6q0408]] juan 8 Mù-chén Dào-mǐn *tǎ-míng* for Lín-yě (lists Yún-é as Lín-yě's Tiān-tóng-period dharma-heir); *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 65.
