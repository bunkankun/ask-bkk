Táng Zhíyù 唐執玉 (1669–1733), *zì* Yìgōng 益功, *hào* Jiè'ān 介菴, native of Wǔjìn 武進 (Cháng-Zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 42 (1703); served successively as *zhīxiàn* of Déhuà 德化, *yùshǐ* in the Censorate, prefect of Lǐ-zhōu 澧州, *fǔ-yùshǐ* of Guǎngdōng, *xún'fǔ* of Zhèjiāng, and Governor-General of Zhílì (Yōngzhèng 7/9, 1729 – 1731 and 1732–1733). His Zhílì governor-generalship coincided with the launch of the Yōngzhèng provincial-gazetteer revision programme; he initiated the *Jīfǔ tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0041]]) in 1729, retaining Tián Yì 田易 as principal compiler. Posthumous title 文端 Wénduān. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 290 has his biography. CBDB lifedates verified against *Qīngdài rénwù shēng-zú niánbiǎo* (#20085).
