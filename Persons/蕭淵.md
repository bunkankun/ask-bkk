**Xiāo Yuān** 蕭淵 of Gǔyáng 古揚, Later-Táng figure (Qīngtài era, 935 CE), to whom is attributed the foundational transmission preface of [[KR3eq029]] *Chǔshì yíshū*, narrating the recovery of the stone-engraved tablets by his ancestor in the post-[[黃巢]] Huáng Cháo tomb-excavations.
