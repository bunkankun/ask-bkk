# Jīnmíng Jiè 金明介

Late-17th-century Chán master; teacher of the lay-Chán practitioner [[陸瑛|Lù Yīng]] 陸瑛 (*hào* Tiáoshí jūshì 調實居士), to whom he transmitted formal dharma-recognition (*shòu jì bié* 受記莂) and whom he received as a close-room disciple (*rù shì gāo zú* 入室高足). Lifedates and further biographical details unrecorded.

Sources: opening of [[KR6q0230]] *Tiáoshí jūshì zhèng yuán lù*.
