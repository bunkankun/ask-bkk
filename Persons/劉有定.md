Mid-Yuán calligrapher and scholar from Pútián 莆田 in Fújiàn; *zì* Néngjìng 能靜, *hào* Yuánfàn 原範. Contemporary of [[鄭枃]] (Zhèng Bǐng); his name appears in Lín Chénglín's *Púyáng shībiān* 莆陽詩編 and in Táo Zōngyí's [[KR3h0043]] *Shūshǐ huìyào*. Author of the canonical exegetical commentary on Zhèng Bǐng's [[KR3h0038]] *Yǎnjí* 衍極, transmitted with the text in all extant editions. The Sìkù editors describe him as a "refined man of letters" (文雅之士).
