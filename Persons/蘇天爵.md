Sū Tiānjué 蘇天爵 (1294–1352), *zì* Bóxiū 伯修, *hào* Zīxī xiānsheng 滋溪先生, was a major late-Yuán official, historian and *Lǐxué*-aligned classicist. Native of Zhēndìng 真定 (Héběi). *Jǔrén* of *Tàidìng* 1 (1324). Held a sequence of senior posts under late Yuán: *Hànlín shìjiǎng xuéshì*, *Cānzhī zhèngshì* of Jiāng-Zhè, *Cānzhèng* of Jiāng-Zhè (twice — *Zhìzhèng* 7 / 1347 and *Zhìzhèng* 12 / 1352). Major works: the *Yuán cháo míngchén shì lüè* 元朝名臣事略 (15 juan, the principal Yuán-period biographical compendium); the *Wénlèi* 文類 (70 juan, anthology of Yuán-period prose); the *Sōngtīng zhāngshū* 松廳章疏 (5 juan, his memorials); the *Chūnfēngtíng bǐjì* 春風亭筆記 (2 juan); and the *Zhìshì guī jiàn* 治世龜鑑 ([[KR3a0074]], the present work). The *Yuán shǐ* biography mentions all but the *Zhìshì guī jiàn*. Biography: *Yuán shǐ* j. 183. CBDB id 10792, dates 1294–1352.
