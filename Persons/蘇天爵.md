Sū Tiānjué 蘇天爵 (1294–1352), *zì* Bóxiū 伯修, *hào* Zīxī xiānsheng 滋溪先生, was a major late-Yuán official, historian and *Lǐxué*-aligned classicist. Native of Zhēn-dìng 真定 (Hé-běi). *Jǔ-rén* of *Tài-dìng* 1 (1324). Held a sequence of senior posts under late Yuán: *Hàn-lín shì-jiǎng xué-shì*, *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* of Jiāng-Zhè, *Cān-zhèng* of Jiāng-Zhè (twice — *Zhì-zhèng* 7 / 1347 and *Zhì-zhèng* 12 / 1352). Major works: the *Yuán cháo míng-chén shì lüè* 元朝名臣事略 (15 juan, the principal Yuán-period biographical compendium); the *Wén-lèi* 文類 (70 juan, anthology of Yuán-period prose); the *Sōng-tīng zhāng-shū* 松廳章疏 (5 juan, his memorials); the *Chūn-fēng-tíng bǐ-jì* 春風亭筆記 (2 juan); and the *Zhì-shì guī jiàn* 治世龜鑑 (KR3a0074, the present work). The *Yuán shǐ* biography mentions all but the *Zhì-shì guī jiàn*. Biography: *Yuán shǐ* j. 183. CBDB id 10792, dates 1294–1352.
