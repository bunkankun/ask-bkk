Northern-Sòng scholar-official, a registrar (*zhǔbù* 主簿 or similar minor post) of the Chángshā 長沙 district (Húnán), *fl.* 11th century. Author of [[KR5a0128|Huángdì yīnfú jīng jíjiě 黃帝陰符經集解]] (DZ 128), a three-juan *Yīnfú jīng* commentary whose substance also appears in [[KR5a0111|DZ 111 *Huángdì yīnfú jīng shū*]] falsely ascribed to Lǐ Quán — Yuán Shūzhēn is the actual author. She is not listed in the Sòng catalogues under the present title, but is connected with a separate *zhù* 註 in one juan and *shū* 疏 in three juan to the *Yīnfú jīng* (VDL 141). No CBDB record was found. A summary of her explanations is given in Christopher Rand, "Li Ch'üan and Chinese Military Thought," *HJAS* 39 (1979), at 120–133.
