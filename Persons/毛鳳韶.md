# Máo Fèngsháo 毛鳳韶 (Míng, fl. 1526)

A late-Jiājìng-era *Má-chéng* 麻城 (modern Hú-běi) compiler. In *Jiā-jìng bǐng-xū* (1526) he gathered the surviving prose of Ní Pǔ [[倪朴]] into 1 *juǎn* under the title *Ní Shí-líng shū* [[KR4d0239]] — naming the text *shū* (letters) rather than *jí* (collection) to emphasize the centrality of the *Nǐ shàng Gāo-zōng shū* (Memorial Drafted for Submission to Gāo-zōng).
