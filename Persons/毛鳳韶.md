# Máo Fèngsháo 毛鳳韶 (Míng, fl. 1526)

A late-Jiājìng-era *Máchéng* 麻城 (modern Húběi) compiler. In *Jiājìng bǐngxū* (1526) he gathered the surviving prose of Ní Pǔ [[倪朴]] into 1 *juǎn* under the title *Ní Shílíng shū* [[KR4d0239]] — naming the text *shū* (letters) rather than *jí* (collection) to emphasize the centrality of the *Nǐ shàng Gāozōng shū* (Memorial Drafted for Submission to Gāozōng).
