**Yì Lìzhāo** 易麗昭, Jiāqìng-era Húnán patron of medical publication; raised the subscription that funded the 1817 printing of [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī's *Shānghán wēnyì jué yào* 傷寒瘟疫抉要, the first half of what later became [[KR3eh051]] *Nèishāng jí yào*. Known only from Cài's preface.
