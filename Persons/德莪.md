Déé 德莪, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Yīngníng Zhìjìng** [[智靜]] 攖寧智靜. Co-recorder of the 6-juan 《攖寧靜禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0468]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Déé děng lù* 德莪等錄. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
