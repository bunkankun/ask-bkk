# Nánfēng Lǐshì 南豐李氏

Edo-period Japanese paediatrician (a sinicised "Mr Lǐ of Nánfēng" sobriquet of uncertain identification); contributor of clinical commentary to [[下津壽泉]] Shimotsu Jusen's *Yòukē zhèngzhì dàquán* 幼科證治大全 ([[KR3ej041]], 1709). Personal name and dates unrecorded.
