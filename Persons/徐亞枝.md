Xú Yà-zhī 徐亞枝, late-Qīng disciple of [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèng-yīng, *fl.* 1850s–1870s. Compiled [[KR3ep085]] *Wáng-shì yī-àn sān-biān* (four *juǎn*), the third installment of the *Wáng-shì yī-àn* trilogy. He also preserved the manuscript of Wáng's *Gǔ-jīn yī-àn àn xuǎn* ([[KR3ep072]]) through the Jiāng-xī Tài-píng disturbances of the 1850s, as recorded in Wáng's 1857 preface. No further transmitted biographical record. Not in CBDB.
