Jìjī 濟璣, *hào* **Xièyún** 燮雲. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 in the **Hànyuè Sānfēng** sub-line. Native of **Tōngzhōu** 通州 (Jiāngsū). Died **順治戊子 = 1648/1/25–1649/2/10 Gregorian** (per 《五燈全書》 j. 84).

**Formation.** Initially under **Sānfēng Fǎzàng** [[法藏]] for three years; after Fǎzàng's 1635 death continued under heir **Língyán Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] at **Xiángfúsì** 祥符寺. Decisive *chèzhèng* 徹證 on the **Mìaoxǐ qǐngyì Fóguǒ yǒujù wújù** 妙喜請益佛果有句無句 *gōngàn*.

**Abbacy.** **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** 天台國清寺 — the historic Tiāntái-school flagship monastery, rare for a Línjì-line abbot. Stūpa at Guóqīng.

**Work.** [[KR6q0476]] 《燮雲璣禪師國清無畏堂語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B303, recorded by *fǎsì* **Xuángǎo** [[玄杲]] *lù*; preface by **Zhāng Yǒuyù** [[張有譽]]. Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 84.

**Note on namesake.** Distinct from another **Jìjī** [[濟機]] of the same generation (heir of the same teacher Hóngchǔ; compiler of [[KR6q0475]]). Disambiguation: 濟璣 = 燮雲璣 with 玉-radical 璣; 濟機 has 木-radical 機.
