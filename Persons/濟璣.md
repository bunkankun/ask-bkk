Jì-jī 濟璣, *hào* **Xiè-yún** 燮雲. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 in the **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng** sub-line. Native of **Tōng-zhōu** 通州 (Jiāng-sū). Died **順治戊子 = 1648/1/25–1649/2/10 Gregorian** (per 《五燈全書》 j. 84).

**Formation.** Initially under **Sān-fēng Fǎ-zàng** [[法藏]] for three years; after Fǎ-zàng's 1635 death continued under heir **Líng-yán Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] at **Xiáng-fú-sì** 祥符寺. Decisive *chè-zhèng* 徹證 on the **Mìao-xǐ qǐng-yì Fó-guǒ yǒu-jù wú-jù** 妙喜請益佛果有句無句 *gōng-àn*.

**Abbacy.** **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** 天台國清寺 — the historic Tiāntái-school flagship monastery, rare for a Línjì-line abbot. Stūpa at Guó-qīng.

**Work.** [[KR6q0476]] 《燮雲璣禪師國清無畏堂語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B303, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Xuán-gǎo** [[玄杲]] *lù*; preface by **Zhāng Yǒu-yù** [[張有譽]]. Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 84.

**Note on namesake.** Distinct from another **Jì-jī** [[濟機]] of the same generation (heir of the same teacher Hóng-chǔ; compiler of [[KR6q0475]]). Disambiguation: 濟璣 = 燮雲璣 with 玉-radical 璣; 濟機 has 木-radical 機.
