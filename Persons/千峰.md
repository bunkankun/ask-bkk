# Qiānfēng 千峰

Early-Kāngxī Buddhist monk; hào **Wúyǐn** 無隱; associated with Táocūn 桃村. Lifedates not preserved; floruit Kāngxī-era (1670s).

Author of the preface (*Zuǎnyào xù* 纂要序) to [[寂暹]]'s *Yúqié yànkǒu zhùjí zuǎnyào yíguǐ* 瑜伽燄口註集纂要儀軌 ([[KR6j0757]], X59 no. 1084). The preface is dated **乙卯 *yǐmǎo***, five days before *Chóngyáng* (the Double-Ninth Festival) — most plausibly Kāngxī 14 = **1675** — and signed *Táocūn Wúyǐn Qiānfēng* 桃村無隱千峰. He frames [[寂暹]] Jùchè's compilation as a corrective continuation of [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng's earlier ritual-reform project for the *yànkǒu* food-bestowal liturgy.
