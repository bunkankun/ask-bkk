Dàshū 大樞, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (1593–1659). Co-recorder with **Dàyīng** [[大英]] of the 1-juan 《天界覺浪盛禪師嘉禾語錄》 ([[KR6q0482]]). Likely also among the larger *quánlù* compilation team for [[KR6q0221]]. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
