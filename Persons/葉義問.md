**Yè Yìwèn** 葉義問 (1098–1170), *zì* Shěn yán 審言, was a Southern-Sòng official, author of [[KR5g0095]] ***Lú shān Tài píng xīng guó gōng cǎi fǎng zhēn jūn shì shí*** 盧山太平興國宮採訪真君事實 — a local Daoist record.
