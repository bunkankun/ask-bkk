Putative Táng author of [[KR3h0011]] *Mò sǒu* 墨藪, a two-*juàn* compendium of calligraphic literature. The attribution is found only on late editions; the *Tángshū·Yìwénzhì* does not record the work, and the Sòng catalogues of Cháo Gōngwǔ and Chén Zhènsūn give it as anonymous. The Sìkù editors flag the attribution as uncertain, noting that the book itself records material as late as the *Kāichéng* 開成 (836) affair of Liǔ Gōngquán 柳公權, so its compiler must be a post-*Kāichéng* hand. Nothing else of him is known.
