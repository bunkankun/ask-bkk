# Liú Sīdào 劉斯道

Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán paediatrician of Héngyáng 衡陽 (Húnán); teacher of [[曾世榮]] Zēng Shìróng (1253–1332). His paediatric instruction is the immediate transmission lineage Zēng acknowledges in the preface to *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
