# 體仁子

**Tǐrénzǐ** (*hào*, *fl.* Xuāndé era / 1427), otherwise unknown Míng-period writer of the preface dated *Xuāndé* 2 (1427) to [[KR3el001]] *Mìchuán Liú Bówēn jiācáng jiēgǔ jīnchuāng jìnfāng*. The preface identifies him as a friend "of several decades" of a self-described descendant of [[劉基]] Liú Jī (Liú Bówēn), the Sōngshí Wēng [[松石翁]], from whom he received the manuscript. Real identity not recoverable from the surviving paratext; the *hào* "Embodier of Humaneness" is a stock literatus-style pseudonym, and the figure is almost certainly part of the late-Míng pseudepigraphic framing of the work rather than an early-Míng historical individual.
