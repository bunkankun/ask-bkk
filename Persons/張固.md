**Zhāng Gù** 張固 (fl. *Xián-tōng* — *Guāng-huà*, c. 860–883). Late-Táng author of the [[KR3l0012]] *Yōu-xián gǔ-chuī* 幽閒鼓吹 (1 *juàn*, in 25 or 26 entries). Native place and official career are not securely transmitted; the *Sìkù* compilers note that "his beginnings and ends are not known in detail." From internal evidence of the *Yōu-xián gǔ-chuī* he was writing in the late-*Dà-zhōng* — *Xián-tōng* — *Guāng-huà* period, with entries covering events from *Yuán-hé* through *Dà-zhōng*. CBDB id 93447 records *c_fl_earliest_year* 860; id 190999 (*c_index_year* 883) may be the same person or a relative of the same name. No other surviving work is attested under his name.
