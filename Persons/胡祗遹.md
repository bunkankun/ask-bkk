Hú Zhīyù 胡祗遹 (1227–1295), *zì* Shàokāi 紹開 (per *Yuánshǐ*; the *Sìkù* editors suspect this is a transcription error for Shàowén 紹聞, since Hú's family-letter quotes the *Zhōushū Kānggào* "*shàowén yīdé yán*"), *hào* Zǐshān 紫山, posthumous *shì* Wénjìng 文靖, native of Wǔān 武安 in Cízhōu 磁州 (Héběi). In Zhōngtǒng beginning (1260) recruited by Zhāng Wénqiān 張文謙 (Vice-Director-of-the-Left at Dàmíng) as *yuánwàiláng*; entered the central *Zhōngshūshěng* as *xiángdìngguān*; in Zhìyuán 1 (1264) by imperial edict joined the newly-founded Yuán Hànlínyuàn as *Yìngfèng Hànlín wénzì* concurrent *Tàichángbóshì* — playing a major role in restoring the Yuán ritual-and-music system. Successively *Hùbù yuánwàiláng*, *Zuǒyòusī*; ran afoul of the senior Mongol financial-administrator Ahmad Fanākatī (Āhāmǎ 阿哈瑪) and was nominally lenient-promoted out of the central government to Vice-Director of Tàiyuán with concurrent *tíjǔ tiěyě* (iron-foundry inspection); replaced as *Hédōng tíxíng fùshǐ*; rose to *JīngHúběidào xuānwèi fùshǐ*, *Jìnínglù zǒngguǎn*, ultimately *Jiāngnán Zhèxīdào tíxíng àncháshǐ*. Posthumously promoted *Lǐbù shàngshū* in Yányòu 5 (1318), *shì* Wénjìng. His original 67-*juàn* collection was lost; the present 26-*juàn* *Zǐshān dàquánjí* [[KR4d0443]] was reassembled by his son Hú Chí 胡持 and his student Liú Gēng 劉賡. *Yuánshǐ* j. 170.
