Hóngchǔ 弘儲 (also 宏儲 — DILA standardizes both), *zì* **Tuìwēng** 退翁 ("Retired Old Man"), also **Jìqǐ** 繼起 ("Successor to Rise"). Born **明萬曆乙巳 2/8 日出時** = **1605-03-26 Gregorian**; died **清康熙壬子 9/27** = **1672-11-16 Gregorian**, age **67** (《五燈全書》卷 69). Lay name **Lǐ Jīn** 李金, *zì* **Zhònglián jūshì** 仲連居士.

**Lineage and significance.** Principal Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hànyuè Fǎzàng** [[法藏]] 漢月法藏 (1573–1635) — the chief continuator of the **Sānfēng** 三峰 sub-line which became the central target of imperial-era doctrinal suppression. Carried the Fǎzàng line forward through **Sūzhōu Língyánsì** 蘇州靈巖寺 (whence the *biéhào* 靈巖儲) and **Yáofēng** 堯峯 abbacies. Recorder of his master Fǎzàng's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0473]] 《三峰藏和尚語錄》.

**Imperial suppression.** The Yōngzhèng emperor's 1733 《御製揀魔辨異錄》 ([[KR6q0168]]) declared the Fǎzàng → Hóngchǔ Sānfēng line heretical, banned their writings, and ordered the dismissal of their dharma-heirs. Despite this, Hóngchǔ's [[KR6q0475]] 《南嶽繼起和尚語錄》 was preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon's J34 cluster.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0473]] 《三峰藏和尚語錄》 16 juan — recording his master Fǎzàng's collected sermons.
- [[KR6q0475]] 《南嶽繼起和尚語錄》 10 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B301 — his own collected sermons, compiled by *fǎsì* **Jìjī** [[濟機]] *děng biān*. The title *Nányuè Jìqǐ* 南嶽繼起 references his Línjì-school identification with the **Nányuè Huáiràng** 南嶽懷讓 lineage and his own *hào* **Jìqǐ** 繼起.
- [[KR6r0106]] 《南嶽單傳記》 1 juan — Línjì-line lineage-historical essay defending Línjì's priority over the other four Chán "houses." Postscript by his disciple **Nánqián** 南潛.
