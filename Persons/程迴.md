Chéng Huí 程迴 (*zì* Kějiǔ 可久, of Shāsuí 沙隨), Southern-Sòng *Yì* scholar of the *gǔyì* (ancient-*Yì*) reconstruction movement. Author of *Gǔ Zhōuyì kǎo* 古周易考 in 1 juan, one of the seven principal Sòng-period *gǔyì* recensions enumerated in the *tiyao* of [[呂祖謙]] Lǚ Zǔqiān's *Gǔ Zhōuyì* ([[KR1a0043]]). Counted by [[馮椅]] Féng Yǐ in his auto-preface to *Hòuzhāi Yì xué* ([[KR1a0046]]) among the Sòng line ([[邵雍]] → [[呂大防]] / [[晁說之]] → [[吳仁傑]] → Chéng Huí + [[朱熹]]) that progressively restored the canonical 12-*piān* structure.
