Zhōu Rǔnéng 周汝能, Southern-Sòng Wùzhōu 婺州 *jiàoshòu* 教授 (Prefectural Instructor) under Xiàozōng, co-author with [[樓鍔]] Lóu È of the original colophon to the Qiándào 6 (1170) Wùzhōu printing of [[李衡]] Lǐ Héng's *Zhōuyì yì hǎi cuō yào* ([[KR1a0034]]). Otherwise undocumented.
