Jiǎng Shènxiū 蔣慎修 was a Táng-dynasty court Daoist. His sole known work is the ten-*juàn* *Huángtíng nèiwài yùjǐng jīng jiě* 黃庭內外玉景經解 [[KR5b0087]] (DZ 403), now surviving only as a fragment. The rubric of the text identifies him by his court titles — *cháo sàn dàfū cì fēi yú dài* 朝散大夫賜緋魚袋 (grand master for closing court, with the purple-fish-bag privilege) — indicating a mid- to high-ranking official or court Daoist of the Táng. He is otherwise unattested in the historical record. The full ten-*juàn* form of his commentary is listed in *Tōngzhì* 通志 "Yìwén lüè" 藝文略 5.5b. No CBDB record.
