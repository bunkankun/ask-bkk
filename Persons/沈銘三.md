**Shěn Míngsān** 沈銘三 (*fl.* mid-Qīng), original compiler of the manuscript that became the *Língyàn liángfāng huìbiān* 靈驗良方彙編 ([[KR3ed150]]) after expansion-and-recompilation by [[田間來]] (Tián Jiānlái). Sparse biographical record; Shěn is not securely identified in CBDB or the standard Qīng biographical dictionaries.
