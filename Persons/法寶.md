# 法寶 (唐, Abhidharma scholar)

Fǎbǎo 法寶 (DILA A000746; fl. late 7th–early 8th century CE) was a Tang dynasty student of Xuanzang ([[玄奘]]) who composed the *Jùshě lùn shū* 俱舍論疏 ([[KR6l0035]], T41n1822, 30 juan), one of the three major Tang commentaries on the *Abhidharmakośa* (倶舍論三疏). His biographical details are otherwise unrecorded.

# 法寶 (宋, Chán attendant)

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Yuèlín Shīguān 月林師觀 (1143–1217); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0288]]. Lifedates unrecorded. (Note: this is a different 法寶 from the Tang Abhidharma scholar above.)
