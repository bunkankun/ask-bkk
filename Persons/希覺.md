# Xījué 希覺

Vinaya master at the Yùwáng sì 育王寺 of Míngzhōu 明州 in the early tenth century; supervised the *Vinaya* studies of the young [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益 and remarked of him as "the Yóu-and-Xià of my gate".
