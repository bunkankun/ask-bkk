Bào Róng 鮑溶 (*zì* Dé-yuán 德源, lifedates not securely fixed; *jìn-shì* of *Yuán-hé* 4 = 809). Career details unrecorded in the *Xīn Táng-shū*. Bào was reckoned a major figure in his own day — **Zhāng Wèi**'s 張為 *Zhǔ-kè tú* 主客圖 (a late-Táng or *Wǔ-dài* school-of-poetry typology) makes Bào the *zhǔ* (host / leader) of the *bó-jiě hóng-bá* (broad-explanatory, generously-extracting) school, with **Lǐ Qún-yù** [[李羣玉]] as his *shàng rù-shì* (top disciple) and Sī-mǎ Tuì-zhī as another *rù-shì*-rank disciple. His later reception was modest, partly because his collection's transmission was damaged: the Sòng *Shǐ-guǎn* old recension was 5 *juǎn*, mistakenly attributed to Bào Fáng 鮑防; **Zēng Gǒng** 曾鞏 corrected the attribution against the *Táng wén-cuì* and added 33 pieces from Ōuyáng Xiū's holding.

Principal work in the corpus: *Bào Róng shī jí* [[KR4c0071]] in 6 *juǎn* + *wài-jí* 1 *juǎn*, the post-Zēng-Gǒng recovery line. CBDB id 94860 has no dates.
