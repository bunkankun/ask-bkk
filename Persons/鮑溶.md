Bào Róng 鮑溶 (*zì* Déyuán 德源, lifedates not securely fixed; *jìnshì* of *Yuánhé* 4 = 809). Career details unrecorded in the *Xīn Tángshū*. Bào was reckoned a major figure in his own day — **Zhāng Wèi**'s 張為 *Zhǔkè tú* 主客圖 (a late-Táng or *Wǔdài* school-of-poetry typology) makes Bào the *zhǔ* (host / leader) of the *bójiě hóngbá* (broad-explanatory, generously-extracting) school, with **Lǐ Qúnyù** [[李羣玉]] as his *shàng rùshì* (top disciple) and Sīmǎ Tuìzhī as another *rùshì*-rank disciple. His later reception was modest, partly because his collection's transmission was damaged: the Sòng *Shǐguǎn* old recension was 5 *juǎn*, mistakenly attributed to Bào Fáng 鮑防; **Zēng Gǒng** 曾鞏 corrected the attribution against the *Táng wéncuì* and added 33 pieces from Ōuyáng Xiū's holding.

Principal work in the corpus: *Bào Róng shī jí* [[KR4c0071]] in 6 *juǎn* + *wàijí* 1 *juǎn*, the post-Zēng-Gǒng recovery line. CBDB id 94860 has no dates.
