# Chén Dì 陳棣 (Sòng, fl. 1144)

*Zì* È-fù 鄂父. Son of Chén Rǔ-xī 陳汝錫 (*zì* Shī-yǔ 師予; *jìnshì* of *Shàoxīng-zhī-qián Shàoshèng* 4 / 1097; ended as *Zhè-dōng ān-fǔ-shǐ*). Birth and death years not recorded; CBDB id 3064 (no dates). The catalog meta gives "fl. 1144" — based on the single internally-dated piece in his collection (*Jiǎ-zǐ chú-xī*, 1144 New Year's Eve).

Career: by *fù-rèn* (paternal-merit) entered office, initially as *Tóng-chuān yuán* (functionary at *Tóng-chuān*, in modern Sì-chuān), later ending as *Tōng-pàn Tán-zhōu* (per Líng Dí-zhī's *Wàn-xìng tǒng-pǔ*). No *Sòng-shǐ* biography.

His father Chén Rǔ-xī's poetry — surviving line "*Xián chóu mò làng qiǎn / Liú wéi tòng-yǐn zī*" preserved in the *Kuò-cāng huì-jì* — was appreciated by Huáng Tíng-jiān 黃庭堅, placing the family-poetic genealogy in the Yuán-yòu / Jiāng-xī school. The *Sì-kù* editors detect in Chén Dì himself an early prefigurative mood of the late-Sòng *Jiāng-hú* school of regional-circle poetry.
