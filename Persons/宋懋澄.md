Sòng Mào-chéng 宋懋澄 (1570–1622), *zì* Yòu-qīng 幼清, *hào* Zhì-yuán 稚源; native of Huá-tíng 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng prefecture). Grandson of [[宋詡]] (Sòng Xǔ) and son of [[宋公望]] (Sòng Gōng-wàng). *Jǔrén* of *Wàn-lì* 28 (1600); never *jìnshì*. Late-Míng literatus and short-story writer; principal published works include the *Jiǔ yuè jí* 九籥集 (collected works) and the *Jiǔ yuè bié jí* 九籥別集. He compiled his grandfather Sòng Xǔ's and father Sòng Gōng-wàng's writings into the family corpus *Zhú yǔ shān fáng zá bù* 竹嶼山房雜部 ([[KR3j0171]]). Mào-chéng's *Liú-cuì-yī jì* 劉翠翠記 is one of the precursor stories to Féng Mèng-lóng's *Yù-shì míng-yán*, making him an important transitional figure in the late-Míng vernacular short-story tradition.
