# Guāng-mù 光睦

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, variously styled Ruìyán 瑞巖, Shàoshì 少室, and Xiāngshān 香山 Guāng-mù. Dharma-heir of [[崇岳]] Sōngyuán Chóngyuè (1132–1202) per DILA A021432; one of the two heirs (with [[善開]] Yúnjū Shàn-kāi) to whom Sōngyuán addressed a deathbed letter of dharma-entrustment, as recorded in Sōngyuán's *tǎ-míng* by Lù Yóu. Four years after Sōngyuán's pagoda was erected, Guāng-mù — by then abbot at Xiāngshān Zhìdù-chán-yuàn 香山智度禪院 in Míngzhōu — sent his attendant Dào-fū 道敷 to the ageing Lù Yóu (then living by Jìng-hú 鏡湖) to request the inscription that now closes the second juan of Sōngyuán's *yǔlù*. Named among the 參學 recorders of that *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0311]]). He subsequently held the abbacy at Ruìyán-sì 瑞巖寺 in Táizhōu. Lifedates unrecorded.
