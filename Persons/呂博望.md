# 呂博望 Lǚ Bówàng

**Lǚ Bówàng** 呂博望, Three-Kingdoms-era physician traditionally identified as an early commentator on the *Nànjīng* 難經. Cited in [[楊繼洲]] Yáng Jìzhōu's *Zhēndào yuánliú* essay in the *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng* ([[KR3ee027]]). Likely a variant for [[呂廣]] Lǚ Guǎng (alt. *zì* Bówàng).
