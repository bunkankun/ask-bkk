**Xiàng Jùn** 項峻 (life-dates unknown, fl. mid–3rd century), an official of Sān-guó Wú 三國吳 under Sūn Quán 孫權 and Sūn Liàng 孫亮. He served as *láng-zhōng* 郎中 and was, together with the Grand Astrologer (*tài-shǐ-lìng*) Dīng Fú 丁孚, commissioned in the later Wú reign to compile an official *Wú-shū* 吳書. The draft proved historiographically deficient and was rejected by reviewers Xuē Yíng 薛瑩 and Huá Hé 華覈, who reassigned the project to Wéi Zhāo 韋曜 and others. Xiàng's surviving work is the *Shǐxué piān* 始學篇 [[KR3j0196]] — one of the earliest documented Chinese *lèi-shū* (encyclopedic primers) — long lost and known only through citations in *Chū-xué jì* 初學記, *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* 太平御覽, *Běi-táng shū-chāo* 北堂書鈔, and the *Nán-Qí shū yú-fú zhì* 南齊書·輿服志; the work was reconstructed in the Qīng by Mǎ Guóhàn 馬國翰 in his *Yùhán shānfáng jí-yì shū* 玉函山房輯佚書. He is not recorded in CBDB.
