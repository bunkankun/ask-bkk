Yuán Gōngfǔ 遠公輔 (also written 袁公輔; fl. 1202) was a Southern-Sòng compiler of *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇 commentaries. He compiled in 1202 the collected Sòng commentaries on Zhāng Bóduān's [[張伯端]] *Wùzhēn piān*, preserved as juan 26–30 of the *Xiūzhēn shíshū* [[KR5a0268]] (DZ 263e); the collection was originally entitled *Wùzhēn piān jízhù* 悟真篇集注 in five juan (cf. *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* 直齋書錄解題 12.349). The Yuán Daoist Dài Qǐzōng 戴起宗 (ca. 1337) records having seen an edition of Yuán Gōngfǔ's compilation that included his own preface dated 1202 alongside the earlier preface by Yè Wénshū 葉文叔 (Yè Shìbiǎo, dated 1161); see *[[KR5d0143|Sānchéng bìyào]]* 19b–20a. No further biographical information survives. The graphical alternation 遠/袁 is common in Sòng-era manuscripts; Schipper-Verellen and most Western scholarship use the form **Yuán Gōngfǔ 袁公輔**.
