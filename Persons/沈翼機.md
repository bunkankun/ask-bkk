# Shěn Yìjī 沈翼機

*Zì* Dànchū 澹初, *hào* Xīyuán 西園. Native of Hǎiníng 海寧 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 45 (1706), the same cohort as [[嵇曾筠]]. Birth year 1664 per CBDB (person id 67543); death year not transmitted.

Offices: Hanlin *biānxiū* 編修 → *shìdú xuéshì* 侍讀學士. Served as an examiner in Sìchuān; *xuézhèng* 學政 of Guìzhōu and then of Jiāngxī. Attested alive at least through 1736 (the presentation of the *Zhèjiāng tōngzhì*).

Principal *biānzuǎn* 編纂 of the *Zhèjiāng tōngzhì* 浙江通志 ([[KR2k0044]], 280 juan, submitted 1736), working with the *biānxiū* Fù Wánglù 傅王露 and the *jiǎntǎo* Lù Kuíxūn 陸奎勳 under Jī Zēngyún's 嵇曾筠 supervision. His own poetry collection *Dànchū shīgǎo* 澹初詩稿 in 8 juan also survives.
