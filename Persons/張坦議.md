**Zhāng Tǎn-yì** 張坦議 (*hào* Kè-zhāi 恪齋), Míng-period scholar of Hàn-yáng 漢陽 (Wǔ-hàn, Húběi). Owner of the *Wǔ-zhī zhuāng* 五知莊 study. Prefaced Wàn Quán 萬全's paediatric treatise *Wàn-shì mì-chuán Piàn-yù xīn-shū* 萬氏秘傳片玉心書 ([[KR3ej044]]) c. 1550–1580. The preface frames the work in the conventional *yǎ-kē* (silent-department) paediatric idiom and praises Wàn Quán's three-part paediatric textual organisation. No CBDB record; lifedates not securely recorded.
