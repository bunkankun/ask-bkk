**Zhāng Tǎnyì** 張坦議 (*hào* Kèzhāi 恪齋), Míng-period scholar of Hànyáng 漢陽 (Wǔhàn, Húběi). Owner of the *Wǔzhī zhuāng* 五知莊 study. Prefaced Wàn Quán 萬全's paediatric treatise *Wànshì mìchuán Piànyù xīnshū* 萬氏秘傳片玉心書 ([[KR3ej044]]) c. 1550–1580. The preface frames the work in the conventional *yǎkē* (silent-department) paediatric idiom and praises Wàn Quán's three-part paediatric textual organisation. No CBDB record; lifedates not securely recorded.
