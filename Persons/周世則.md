Zhōu Shì-zé 周世則 (*fl.* late 12th – early 13th century), Southern-Sòng local scholar, native of Shèng-xiàn 嵊縣 in Shào-xīng. The earliest annotator of Wáng Shí-péng's *Kuài-jī fēng-sú fù* — the first of the three rhapsodies that make up the *Kuài-jī sān-fù* 會稽三賦 ([[KR2k0113]]). His annotations were judged not detailed enough by the early-13th-century Kuài-jī scholar Shǐ Zhù 史鑄, who supplemented them in *Jiā-dìng dīng-chǒu* (1217). CBDB id 26970; otherwise undocumented.
