Zhōu Shìzé 周世則 (*fl.* late 12th – early 13th century), Southern-Sòng local scholar, native of Shèngxiàn 嵊縣 in Shàoxīng. The earliest annotator of Wáng Shípéng's *Kuàijī fēngsú fù* — the first of the three rhapsodies that make up the *Kuàijī sānfù* 會稽三賦 ([[KR2k0113]]). His annotations were judged not detailed enough by the early-13th-century Kuàijī scholar Shǐ Zhù 史鑄, who supplemented them in *Jiādìng dīngchǒu* (1217). CBDB id 26970; otherwise undocumented.
