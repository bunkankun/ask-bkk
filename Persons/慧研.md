# 慧研

Huìyán; late-Míng *śramaṇa* and Buddhist editor, author of the editorial preface that appears at the head of [[KR6e0066|*Huáyán jīng hé lùn* 華嚴經合論]] (X223) and again at the head of [[KR6e0068|*Huáyán jīng hé lùn zuǎn yào* 華嚴經合論纂要]] (X226). Lifedates not preserved.
