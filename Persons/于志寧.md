# Yú Zhìníng 于志寧

*Zì* Zhòngmì 仲謐. Native of Yōngzhōu Gāolíng 雍州高陵 (modern Shǎnxī). Lifedates 588–665.

Tang minister and senior historiographer. Held *Zhōngshū lìng* 中書令 from 643, *Tàizǐ tàifǔ* 太子太傅, and *Sīkōng* 司空; involved in the demotion of Lǐ Chéngqián 李承乾 as Crown Prince (he had been Chéngqián's mentor and his repeated remonstrations with Chéngqián's misbehaviour led to an assassination attempt by Chéngqián). On Wǔ Zétiān's faction's victory in 659, demoted to provincial post, dying *in officio*.

Scholarly contributions: chief compiler (alongside Lǐ Chúnfēng) of the *Wǔdài shǐ zhì* 五代史志 attached to the *Suí shū* ([[KR2a0023]]); contributor to the *Suí shū jìzhuàn*; chief editor of the *Tang Liù diǎn* 唐六典 in the early *Zhēnguān* period; co-compiler with [[長孫無忌]] of the *Yǒnghuī lǜ shū* 永徽律疏 (the *Tang lǜ shūyì*, 653).

His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 78 and *Xīn Tángshū* 104 ([[KR2a0026]], [[KR2a0027]]).
