# Yú Zhìníng 于志寧

*Zì* Zhòngmì 仲謐. Native of Yōng-zhōu Gāo-líng 雍州高陵 (modern Shǎnxī). Lifedates 588–665.

Tang minister and senior historiographer. Held *Zhōng-shū lìng* 中書令 from 643, *Tài-zǐ tài-fǔ* 太子太傅, and *Sī-kōng* 司空; involved in the demotion of Lǐ Chéng-qián 李承乾 as Crown Prince (he had been Chéng-qián's mentor and his repeated remonstrations with Chéng-qián's misbehaviour led to an assassination attempt by Chéng-qián). On Wǔ Zétiān's faction's victory in 659, demoted to provincial post, dying *in officio*.

Scholarly contributions: chief compiler (alongside Lǐ Chúnfēng) of the *Wǔ-dài shǐ zhì* 五代史志 attached to the *Suí shū* (KR2a0023); contributor to the *Suí shū jì-zhuàn*; chief editor of the *Tang Liù diǎn* 唐六典 in the early *Zhēnguān* period; co-compiler with [[長孫無忌]] of the *Yǒng-huī lǜ shū* 永徽律疏 (the *Tang lǜ shū-yì*, 653).

His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 78 and *Xīn Tángshū* 104 (KR2a0026, KR2a0027).
