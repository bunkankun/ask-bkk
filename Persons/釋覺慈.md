Shì Juécí 釋覺慈 (fl. *Jiànyán* / *Shàoxīng*, c. 1130s), *Chán*-Buddhist monk, *ménrén* (disciple) of Shì Huìhóng 釋惠洪 [[釋惠洪]] (1071–1128). After his teacher's death, Juécí assembled and edited Huìhóng's poetic and prose corpus into the *Shímén wénzìchán* 石門文字禪 [[KR4d0095]] in 30 *juǎn* — the principal Northern-Sòng monastic *biéjí* — collected into the Buddhist canon (*dàzàng*) and surviving through the *Zhīnà zhùshù* and *Sìkù* recensions.
