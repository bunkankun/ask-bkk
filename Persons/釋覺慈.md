Shì Jué-cí 釋覺慈 (fl. *Jiàn-yán* / *Shào-xīng*, c. 1130s), *Chán*-Buddhist monk, *mén-rén* (disciple) of Shì Huì-hóng 釋惠洪 [[釋惠洪]] (1071–1128). After his teacher's death, Jué-cí assembled and edited Huì-hóng's poetic and prose corpus into the *Shí-mén wén-zì-chán* 石門文字禪 [[KR4d0095]] in 30 *juǎn* — the principal Northern-Sòng monastic *bié-jí* — collected into the Buddhist canon (*dà-zàng*) and surviving through the *Zhī-nà zhù-shù* and *Sìkù* recensions.
