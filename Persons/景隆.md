# Jǐnglóng 景隆

Míng Línjì-school Chán master Kōnggǔ Jǐnglóng 空谷景隆 (1393–1470), based at the Qīngpíngshān Kōngxué Méngtáng 清平山空穴蒙堂; teacher of [[如巹]] Mìān Rújǐn and author of the Chénghuà 6 (1470) preface to the *Zī mén jǐng xùn* ([[KR6q0100]]).
