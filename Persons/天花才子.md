**Tiānhuā Cáizǐ** 天花才子 ("The Talented Child of Heavenly Flowers") is a Qīng-dynasty pen name used by the author of [[KR4k0075]] *Hòu Xīyóu Jì* 後西游記. The name was used by multiple Qīng authors across different publications, making attribution to a single historical individual uncertain. No confident identification with a named historical person has been established in the secondary literature, and no CBDB record exists for this pseudonym. The author of the *Hòu Xīyóu Jì* is presumed to have been a Jiāngnán literatus of the seventeenth century with Buddhist learning, based on the content and frame of the novel.
