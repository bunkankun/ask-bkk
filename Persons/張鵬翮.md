**Zhāng Pénghé** 張鵬翮 (1649–1725), of Chéngdū 成都, major early-Qīng official, *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* 文淵閣大學士 (Grand Secretary of the Wényuāngé); wrote the 1724 preface to [[葉其蓁]] Yè Qízhēn's *Nǚkē zhǐzhǎng* 女科指掌 ([[KR3ei027]]).
