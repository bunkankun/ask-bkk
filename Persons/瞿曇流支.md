# Qútán Liúzhī 瞿曇流支

Skt. Gautama-ruci. Indian Buddhist translator active at Yè 鄴, the Eastern Wèi 東魏 capital, in the mid-sixth century. Disciple of [[毘目智仙]] Pímùzhìxiān (Vimokṣaprajñā-ṛṣi); collaborator on Vasubandhu *upadeśa* translations including the *Zhuǎn fǎlún jīng yōubōtíshè* ([[KR6i0592]], 541 CE) and other Yogācāra works.
