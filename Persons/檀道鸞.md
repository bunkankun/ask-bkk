Tán Dàoluán 檀道鸞 (*fl.* Liu Song dynasty, 5th century CE) was a historian, author of the *Xù Jìn yángqiū* 續晉陽秋 ([[KR2k0201]]), a continuation of the Eastern Jin annalistic history in the tradition of Sūn Shèng's 孫盛 *Jìn yángqiū* 晉陽秋. His work, now fragmentary, is a major source for Eastern Jin social history and biography, cited extensively in the commentaries to Liú Yìqìng's 劉義慶 *Shìshuō xīnyǔ* 世說新語. The CBDB records him without dates (CBDB 410514). No further biographical details are attested in the standard histories.
