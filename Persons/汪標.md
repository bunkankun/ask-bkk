Wāng Biāo 汪標, Yuán-period scholar of Póyáng 鄱陽 (Jiāngxī). Per [[胡一桂]] Hú Yīguì's *Yì xué qǐméng yì zhuàn* 易學啟蒙翼傳, compiled a great *Jīngzhuàn tōngjiě* 經傳通解 master-collection of *Yì*-commentaries, using [[馮椅]] Féng Yǐ's *Hòuzhāi Yì xué* ([[KR1a0046]]) as the base-text and seeking out further ancient and contemporary glosses. Wāng Biāo's compilation is itself no longer transmitted, but its existence is documentary evidence of Féng Yǐ's high reputation at the Sòng-Yuán transition.
