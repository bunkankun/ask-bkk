Early-Qīng poet of the Shāndōng *Yúyáng* circle, *zì* Bīnjiāo 賓郊, *hào* Xiāotíng 蕭亭; native of Zōupíng 鄒平 (Shāndōng). Brother of Wáng Shìzhēn [[王士禎]]'s wife (Wáng's wife was Zhāng Shíjū's elder sister), and one of Wáng's closest poetic interlocutors. Approximate lifedates 1634–1713. A Míng *lóyī*-cum-Qīng retired figure (his father served in the late Míng and the family withdrew from public office after the dynastic transition); he never sat for the Qīng examinations and remained at his Zōupíng estate. His critical positions, recorded in juǎn 1 of Láng Tínghuái [[郎廷槐]]'s *Shīyǒu shīzhuàn lù* 師友詩傳錄 ([[KR4i0059]]) alongside Wáng Shìzhēn and Zhāng Dǔqìng [[張篤慶]], emphasize the technical *shēngdiào* foundations of regulated verse and anchor much of what would become Wáng's *shényùn* doctrine. Wáng Shìzhēn collected his poetry as the *Xiāotíng shī xuǎn* 蕭亭詩選, which he edited and prefaced. Not in CBDB; lifedates per Wáng Shìzhēn's preface to the *Xiāotíng shī xuǎn*.
