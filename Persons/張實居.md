Early-Qīng poet of the Shāndōng *Yú-yáng* circle, *zì* Bīn-jiāo 賓郊, *hào* Xiāo-tíng 蕭亭; native of Zōu-píng 鄒平 (Shāndōng). Brother of Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]]'s wife (Wáng's wife was Zhāng Shí-jū's elder sister), and one of Wáng's closest poetic interlocutors. Approximate lifedates 1634–1713. A Míng *lóyī*-cum-Qīng retired figure (his father served in the late Míng and the family withdrew from public office after the dynastic transition); he never sat for the Qīng examinations and remained at his Zōu-píng estate. His critical positions, recorded in juǎn 1 of Láng Tíng-huái [[郎廷槐]]'s *Shī-yǒu shī-zhuàn lù* 師友詩傳錄 ([[KR4i0059]]) alongside Wáng Shì-zhēn and Zhāng Dǔ-qìng [[張篤慶]], emphasize the technical *shēng-diào* foundations of regulated verse and anchor much of what would become Wáng's *shén-yùn* doctrine. Wáng Shì-zhēn collected his poetry as the *Xiāo-tíng shī xuǎn* 蕭亭詩選, which he edited and prefaced. Not in CBDB; lifedates per Wáng Shì-zhēn's preface to the *Xiāo-tíng shī xuǎn*.
