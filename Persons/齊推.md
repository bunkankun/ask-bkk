**Qí Tuī** 齊推 was a mid-Táng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0343]] *Líng fēi sǎn chuán xìn lù* 靈飛散傳信錄 (title's last character as 言 / 信 varies in editions) — a transmission-record of the "numinous-flying powder" (*líng fēi sǎn* 靈飛散) elixir. Dated 812 (Yuán hé 7, under Táng Xiàn zōng).
