**Huáng Yìngzǐ** 黃應紫 (*fl.* late Yuán to early Ming, c. 1300–1450), compositional layer in the composite *Ān-lǎo huái-yòu shū* 安老懷幼書 ([[KR3eo032]]) — most plausibly responsible for the **child-rearing** (*huái-yòu*) supplementation of the existing Chén Zhí / Zōu Xuàn elder-care core.
