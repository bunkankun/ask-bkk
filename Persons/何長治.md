# Hé Chángzhì 何長治

Mid-nineteenth-century Ménghé 孟河 (Jiāngnán) physician, *zì* Shūtián 書田 (under which he is often cited as **Hé Shūtián** 何書田), *hào* Hóngfáng 鴻舫. One of the principal prolific physicians of the Ménghé Hé 何 family medical lineage, conventionally identified as a younger brother of [[何游]] Hé Yóu (= Hé Dànān 何澹安, see [[KR3ep005]]). The Hé family is one of the four great Ménghé medical lineages alongside the Fèi 費, Mǎ 馬, and Cháo 巢 families systematically reconstructed in Volker Scheid, *Currents of Tradition in Chinese Medicine 1626–2006* (Eastland Press, 2007).
