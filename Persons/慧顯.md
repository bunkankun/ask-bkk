Huì-xiǎn 慧顯 was a Southern Sòng Vinaya monk; lifedates and biographical details unknown. His sole transmitted work is the **Xíng-shì chāo zhū-jiā jì biāo-mù** 行事鈔諸家記標目 ([[KR6k0170]], X44n0741) — a one-juan checklist of *Xíng-shì chāo* sub-commentaries by the *liù-shí jiā* 六十家 ("sixty houses") of Tang–Sòng commentators. The work survives in the Manji canon only in the form of a Japanese editorial revision (*gǎi-lù* 改錄) by **Kaigetsu** 戒月 ([[戒月]]). Sources: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001745.
