# Huáng Rǔlín 黃汝霖

Southern-Sòng layman styled Jìng'ān 敬菴, descendant of a room-student of Dàhuì Zōnggǎo; recorded in Zhāng Lún's 1183 preface to the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī niánpǔ* ([[KR6q0063]]) as the patron whose request prompted Zǔyǒng's compilation of the *niánpǔ*.
