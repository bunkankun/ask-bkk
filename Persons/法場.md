Fǎchǎng 法場 (flourished late fifth century) was a monk active at Luòyáng 洛陽 during the Northern Wèi 後魏 (Tuòbá Wèi 拓跋魏) dynasty, primarily during the *Tàihé* 太和 reign of Xiàowén 孝文 (471–499 CE). He is recorded as the translator of [[KR6i0175]] *Biànyì zhǎngzhě zǐ jīng* 辯意長者子經 (T544) and a small number of other short sūtras. His monastic biography is poorly documented; he is one of several minor Northern Wèi translators whose work was preserved in the canon despite the lack of personal records.
