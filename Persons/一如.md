Early-Míng **Tiāntāi** 天臺 exegetical master and senior monastic administrator. Native of **Shàngyú** 上虞 in Zhèjiāng. Born **1352**; died at **Hǎiyìnsì** 海印寺 in Beijing in **Hóngxī** 1 (3rd lunar month of 1425 = 29 March – 26 April 1425), age 74 (sources: *Bǔ xù gāosēng zhuàn* 補續高僧傳, j. 4).

He served as abbot of two of the most prestigious Tiāntái-lineage seats of the south — **Yǎnfúsì** 演福寺 and **ShàngTiānzhúsì** 上天竺寺 in Hángzhōu — and was finally summoned to the early-Míng northern capital as rectifier-of-doctrine at **Hǎiyìnsì** 海印寺 in Beijing under the Yǒnglè and Hóngxī courts. He held the styles **Yīān** 一菴, **Tuìwēng** 退翁, and (in later Tiāntái-lineage citation) **Tiānzhúrú** 天竺如.

His principal scholarly legacy is the **Dà Míng sānzàng fǎshù** 大明三藏法數 ([[KR6s0007]], P1615), a fifty-juan imperial-commission Buddhist numerical-list lexicon completed in **Yǒnglè** 17 (1419). The work — produced under the byline **上天竺前住持沙門一如等奉勑集註** ("former abbot of ShàngTiānzhú, Yīrú and others, by imperial command compiled and annotated") — became the standard pre-modern Chinese-Buddhist reference for *fǎshù* (numerical-category Dharma sets) and continues to underlie the corresponding entries in nearly every Chinese, Japanese, and Western Buddhist dictionary.
