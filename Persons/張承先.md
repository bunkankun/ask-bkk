Zhāng Chéngxiān 張承先 was a Táng-dynasty Daoist master and liturgical editor. He is attested in DZ 508 *Wúshàng huánglù dàzhāi lìchéng yí* 無上黃籙大齋立成儀 20.9b–11b, and is the editor of DZ 411 *Tàishàng dòngxuán língbǎo wǔdì jiàojì zhāozhēn yùjué* [[KR5b0095]] — a stand-alone Five-Emperors offering-rite extracted by him from DZ 352 *Chìshū yùjué miàojīng* 2.20a–28b. His editorial note at the close of DZ 411 states his purpose in excerpting the ritual — "that it might serve for an independent ceremony." No other biographical details are recorded; no CBDB record.
