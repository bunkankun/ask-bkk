Zhào Péng-fēi 趙鵬飛, *zì* Qǐ-míng 企明, *hào* Mù-nè 木訥, native of Mián-zhōu 綿州 (modern Mián-yáng 綿陽, Sìchuān). Active in the late Southern Sòng. Lifedates not recorded; the prefatorial poem to his *Chūnqiū jīng-quán* 春秋經筌 is dated by Qīng-yáng Mèng-yán 青陽夢炎 (a fellow Mián-zhōu native) in Xián-chún 8 *rén-shēn* 壬申 (1272), referring to Zhào in the past tense as "Mù-nè *xiān-shēng*" and noting his lost *Shī gù* 詩故 was destroyed in the wars; Zhào's own activity is therefore datable broadly to the early-to-mid thirteenth century. Mèng-yán links his philosophical lineage to the Sìchuān Yī-chuān 伊川 transmission via Xiè Chí-zhèng 謝持正. Beyond the *Chūnqiū jīng-quán* in sixteen juan (the only extant work), the lost *Shī gù* is mentioned. Two CBDB records (51066, 530639) carry no biographical detail; the Sìkù *tíyào* notes him only as a Mián-zhōu literatus committed to "untraditional" *Chūnqiū* exegesis.
