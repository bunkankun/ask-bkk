Zhào Péngfēi 趙鵬飛, *zì* Qǐmíng 企明, *hào* Mùnè 木訥, native of Miánzhōu 綿州 (modern Miányáng 綿陽, Sìchuān). Active in the late Southern Sòng. Lifedates not recorded; the prefatorial poem to his *Chūnqiū jīngquán* 春秋經筌 is dated by Qīngyáng Mèngyán 青陽夢炎 (a fellow Miánzhōu native) in Xiánchún 8 *rénshēn* 壬申 (1272), referring to Zhào in the past tense as "Mùnè *xiānshēng*" and noting his lost *Shī gù* 詩故 was destroyed in the wars; Zhào's own activity is therefore datable broadly to the early-to-mid thirteenth century. Mèngyán links his philosophical lineage to the Sìchuān Yīchuān 伊川 transmission via Xiè Chízhèng 謝持正. Beyond the *Chūnqiū jīngquán* in sixteen juan (the only extant work), the lost *Shī gù* is mentioned. Two CBDB records (51066, 530639) carry no biographical detail; the Sìkù *tíyào* notes him only as a Miánzhōu literatus committed to "untraditional" *Chūnqiū* exegesis.
