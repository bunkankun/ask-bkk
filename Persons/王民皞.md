Wáng Mínhào 王民皞, early-Qīng lay scholar-official of **Gǔlín** 古臨 (location uncertain — likely Gǔlínzhōu in Yúnnán). Posted as local administrator at **Diānnán Āmí** 滇南阿迷 (Yúnnán). Author of the front-preface to [[KR6q0493]] 《雲幻宸禪師語錄》 (the Pòshān-line **Yúnhuàn Yìnchén** [[印宸]]), dated **康熙癸丑春 = 1673 spring** at his Āmí Lǎngjìngxuān 朗鏡軒 office. The preface mentions Wáng's three-year acquaintance with [[發林]] **Héngxiù Fālín** (Yìnchén's heir and Wáng's contemporary in Yúnnán) as the bridge to the publication.
