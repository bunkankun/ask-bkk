Wáng Mín-hào 王民皞, early-Qīng lay scholar-official of **Gǔ-lín** 古臨 (location uncertain — likely Gǔ-lín-zhōu in Yún-nán). Posted as local administrator at **Diān-nán Ā-mí** 滇南阿迷 (Yún-nán). Author of the front-preface to [[KR6q0493]] 《雲幻宸禪師語錄》 (the Pòshān-line **Yún-huàn Yìn-chén** [[印宸]]), dated **康熙癸丑春 = 1673 spring** at his Ā-mí Lǎng-jìng-xuān 朗鏡軒 office. The preface mentions Wáng's three-year acquaintance with [[發林]] **Héng-xiù Fā-lín** (Yìn-chén's heir and Wáng's contemporary in Yún-nán) as the bridge to the publication.
