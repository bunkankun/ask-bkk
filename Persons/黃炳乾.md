Late-Qīng physician of Xiāngxī 湘西 (western Húnán), courtesy name 陶普 *Táopǔ*. From his own preface (sixth month of *Guāngxù* 5 / 己卯 / 1879, signed at the 酒泉公廨 Jiǔquán yamen), began *mùyóu* 幕遊 itinerant medical-advisory service in the *Qīnghǎi* corridor in *Guāngxù chūyuán* (1875), encountered a *báihóu* epidemic in Huángzhōng 湟中 in 1876, and subsequently practised in 甘泉 and 酒泉. Augmented [[張紹修]]'s diphtheria tracts with named-master secret prescriptions, an acupuncture-needle protocol (including a recipe for forging and tempering a needle from horse-bit iron), and prognostic rules; his additions form the second half of [[KR3em031]] *Báihóu jiéyào hébiān*. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
