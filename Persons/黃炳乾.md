Late-Qīng physician of Xiāng-xī 湘西 (western Húnán), courtesy name 陶普 *Táo-pǔ*. From his own preface (sixth month of *Guāng-xù* 5 / 己卯 / 1879, signed at the 酒泉公廨 Jiǔ-quán yamen), began *mù-yóu* 幕遊 itinerant medical-advisory service in the *Qīng-hǎi* corridor in *Guāng-xù chū-yuán* (1875), encountered a *bái-hóu* epidemic in Huáng-zhōng 湟中 in 1876, and subsequently practised in 甘泉 and 酒泉. Augmented [[張紹修]]'s diphtheria tracts with named-master secret prescriptions, an acupuncture-needle protocol (including a recipe for forging and tempering a needle from horse-bit iron), and prognostic rules; his additions form the second half of [[KR3em031]] *Bái-hóu jié-yào hé-biān*. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
