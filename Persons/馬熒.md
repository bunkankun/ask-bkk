Late-Míng (Wàn-lì-era) literatus of **Fú-zhōu** 福州 (Fú-jiàn). *Zì* **Yòng-zhāo** 用昭. Co-editor with [[袁表]] Yuán Biǎo of the **[[KR4h0098]]** *Mǐn-zhōng shí-zǐ shī* 閩中十子詩 (30 *juǎn*, compiled **Wàn-lì 4 = 1576**). CBDB id 526702 (no dates). No other works under his name are transmitted.
