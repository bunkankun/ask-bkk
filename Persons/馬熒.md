Late-Míng (Wàn-lì-era) literatus of **Fúzhōu** 福州 (Fújiàn). *Zì* **Yòngzhāo** 用昭. Co-editor with [[袁表]] Yuán Biǎo of the **[[KR4h0098]]** *Mǐnzhōng shízǐ shī* 閩中十子詩 (30 *juǎn*, compiled **Wànlì 4 = 1576**). CBDB id 526702 (no dates). No other works under his name are transmitted.
