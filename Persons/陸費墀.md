Lùfèi Chí 陸費墀 (1731–1790; surname Lùfèi 陸費 is a compound), zì Dānshū 丹叔, hào Yízhāi 頤齋, of Tóngxiāng 桐鄉 (Zhèjiāng), took the *jìnshì* in 1766 and served as Chief Collation Officer (*zǒng jiào guān* 總校官) of the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 under Jǐ Yún and Lù Xīxióng. He was personally responsible for the Wényuān gé 文淵閣 and Wénsù gé 文溯閣 fair copies. After errors were found in the Wényuān copies in 1787, he was demoted and ordered to bear part of the cost of the corrections from his own estate, which depleted his family fortune by the time of his death. His name closes the *zǒngjiàoguān* line on most WYG *tíyào*. CBDB dates from *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú nián biǎo*.
