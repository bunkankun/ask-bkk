Mid- to late-Qing physician of Yìhuáng 宜黃 (Jiāngxī); *zì* Jǐnfāng 錦芳. Conventional lifedates 1731–1818. Best known for two companion *Qiú zhēn* 求真 ("Searching for the Truth") medical works of 1769: the *Běncǎo qiú zhēn* 本草求真 (a pharmacopoeia) and [[KR3eb024]] *Mài lǐ qiú zhēn* (a pulse manual). Doctrinally aligned with the High-Qing *fǎn-gǔ* 返古 tendency that returned to the *Nèijīng* against late-Ming speculative pulse doctrine. No CBDB record.
