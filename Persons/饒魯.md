Ráo Lǔ 饒魯 (1194–1264), *zì* Bóyú 伯輿, *hào* Shuāngfēng 雙峰 (whence *Shuāngfēng xiānshēng* 雙峰先生), a Southern-Sòng Zhū-Xī-school *Dàoxué* scholar of Yúgān 餘干 (modern Jiāngxī). A second-generation disciple of Zhū Xī via Huáng Gàn 黃榦. Best known for his lectures at the Shíshī shūyuàn 石室書院 and the Yánxī jīngshè 硯溪精舍; transmission of his teaching into the Yuán-period Jiāngxī Zhū-school lineage was important for the late-Sòng / early-Yuán continuation of the *Dàoxué* curriculum. Recognized as one of the principal Sòng-Yuán-period transmitters of the Zhū-school orthodox-canonical exegesis.
