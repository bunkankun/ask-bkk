Lǐ Guāngdì 李光地 (1642–1718), *zì* Jìnqīng 晉卿, *hào* Hòu'ān 厚菴 and Róngcūn 榕村, was the leading Kāngxī-era Confucian classicist and political adviser. Native of Ānxī 安溪 (Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 9 (1670); rose through the Hànlín to Grand Secretary (*Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* 文淵閣大學士) under Kāngxī, with extensive responsibility for the imperial classical-compilation projects of the late Kāngxī court — most prominently the *Yù zuǎn Zhūzǐ quánshū* 御纂朱子全書 ([[KR3a0108]]) and the *Yù zuǎn Xìnglǐ jīngyì* 御纂性理精義 ([[KR3a0107]]), both produced under his direction.

He has biographies in *Qīng shǐ gǎo* 清史稿 j. 262 and *Bēizhuàn jí* 碑傳集. Both CBDB and the Kanripo catalog meta give 1642–1718. (The CLAUDE.md notes a possible 1720 alternative, but CBDB id 59056 reads 1642–1718, and the *Qīng shǐ gǎo* matches; the 1718 reading is followed here.)

His own writings, distinguished from the imperially-commissioned ones, include the *Zhù jiě Zhèng méng* 注解正蒙 ([[KR3a0028]]) — the standard early-Qīng commentary on Zhāng Zǎi's *Zhèng méng* — the *Sìshū jiě yì* 四書解義, and the substantial *yǔlù* preserved in the posthumous *Róngcūn yǔlù* 榕村語錄 ([[KR3a0114]]) edited by his pupils Xú Yòngxī 徐用錫 and his grandson Lǐ Qīngzhí 李清植. Within the late-Kāngxī Hànlín *Lǐxué* milieu his standing is paramount; on Zhū-Lù-style intra-*Lǐxué* polemic he is moderate and integrating, often defending Sòng-canonical *Zhūzǐxué* against Wáng Yángmíng critiques. CBDB id 59056, dates 1642–1718.
