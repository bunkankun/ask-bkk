# Zhìzhào 治兆

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active during the mid-to-late nineteenth century. According to the *Qīngzhū jí xù* of **Zhào Chéngxià** 趙成夏 (*hào* Shǎohé jūshì 少荷居士) — dated *gēngwǔ zhòngqiū* 庚午仲秋, most plausibly *Tóngzhì* 9 (= 1870) — Zhìzhào had by that point observed the Buddhist summer retreat (*jiéxià* 結夏) for thirty years, indicating sustained Pure Land society leadership from the late Dàoguāng era onwards. He is the compiler of the **《清珠集》** *Qīngzhū jí* [[KR6p0111]] (X1192), a Pure Land devotional anthology defending orthodox *niànfó* practice against contemporary Chán literalism, drawing explicitly on the Confucian-Buddhist conciliation tradition descending from **Liǔ Zōngyuán** 柳宗元. Lifedates not preserved.
