# Zhì-zhào 治兆

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active during the mid-to-late nineteenth century. According to the *Qīng-zhū jí xù* of **Zhào Chéng-xià** 趙成夏 (*hào* Shǎo-hé jū-shì 少荷居士) — dated *gēng-wǔ zhòng-qiū* 庚午仲秋, most plausibly *Tóng-zhì* 9 (= 1870) — Zhì-zhào had by that point observed the Buddhist summer retreat (*jié-xià* 結夏) for thirty years, indicating sustained Pure Land society leadership from the late Dào-guāng era onwards. He is the compiler of the **《清珠集》** *Qīng-zhū jí* [[KR6p0111]] (X1192), a Pure Land devotional anthology defending orthodox *niàn-fó* practice against contemporary Chán literalism, drawing explicitly on the Confucian-Buddhist conciliation tradition descending from **Liǔ Zōng-yuán** 柳宗元. Lifedates not preserved.
