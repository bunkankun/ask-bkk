Zhōu Fāngwén 周方文 (also known by the honorific *Zhōu zhēn-gōng* 周真公, "Perfected Lord Zhōu") was a Southern-Sòng to Yuán *Jìng-míng* 淨明 master active in the Xī-shān 西山 lineage based at Nánchāng 南昌, Jiāng-xī. He is identified in the *Zhèngtǒng dàozàng* as the *jiào-shī* 教師 (teaching-master) of the *Língbǎo Jìng-míng yuàn* (Pure-Brilliance Cloister), and is the named compiler of [[KR5b0258]] (*Língbǎo jìng-míng yuàn jiào-shī Zhōu Zhēn-gōng qǐ-qǐng huà-yī*) and [[KR5b0260]] (*Língbǎo jìng-míng huáng-sù shū shì-yì mì-jué*). The texts present him as a Jìng-míng disciple posing detailed doctrinal questions to his master and receiving precise answers; the master in the *Huáng-sù shū shì-yì mì-jué* is the same teacher as in the *Huáng-sù shū* (commentary by [[傅飛卿]] in [[KR5b0259]]). No precise lifedates are recoverable from CBDB or from the standard Jìng-míng biographical compendia; Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1095) place him in the late Southern-Sòng to Yuán generation.
