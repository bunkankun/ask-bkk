Zhōu Fāngwén 周方文 (also known by the honorific *Zhōu zhēngōng* 周真公, "Perfected Lord Zhōu") was a Southern-Sòng to Yuán *Jìngmíng* 淨明 master active in the Xīshān 西山 lineage based at Nánchāng 南昌, Jiāngxī. He is identified in the *Zhèngtǒng dàozàng* as the *jiàoshī* 教師 (teaching-master) of the *Língbǎo Jìngmíng yuàn* (Pure-Brilliance Cloister), and is the named compiler of [[KR5b0258]] (*Língbǎo jìngmíng yuàn jiàoshī Zhōu Zhēngōng qǐqǐng huàyī*) and [[KR5b0260]] (*Língbǎo jìngmíng huángsù shū shìyì mìjué*). The texts present him as a Jìngmíng disciple posing detailed doctrinal questions to his master and receiving precise answers; the master in the *Huángsù shū shìyì mìjué* is the same teacher as in the *Huángsù shū* (commentary by [[傅飛卿]] in [[KR5b0259]]). No precise lifedates are recoverable from CBDB or from the standard Jìngmíng biographical compendia; Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1095) place him in the late Southern-Sòng to Yuán generation.
