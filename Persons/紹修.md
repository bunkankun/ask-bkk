# Shàoxiū 紹修

Early-tenth-century Chán monk; travelling companion of [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益 and [[法進]] Fǎjìn at the snowstorm-halt encounter with [[桂琛|Luóhàn Guìchēn]] 羅漢桂琛 at the Dìzàng yuàn 地藏院.
