Tōnglái 通來, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Zhīkōng Xuéyùn** [[學蘊]] 知空學蘊 (1613–1689) in the Yúnnán Kāifēng line. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《知空蘊禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0523]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
