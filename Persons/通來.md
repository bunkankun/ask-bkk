Tōng-lái 通來, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Zhī-kōng Xué-yùn** [[學蘊]] 知空學蘊 (1613–1689) in the Yún-nán Kāi-fēng line. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《知空蘊禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0523]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
