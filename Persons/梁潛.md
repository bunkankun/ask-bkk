Liáng Qián 梁潛 (1356–1418), *zì* **Yòngzhī** 用之, *hào* **Bóān** 泊菴, native of **Tàihé** 泰和 (Jíān, Jiāngxī). Hóngwǔ 29 *bǐngzǐ* (1396) *jǔrén*; appointed *Cāngxī xùndǎo* 蒼溪訓導 → magistrate successively at *Sìhuì* 四會, *Yángjiāng* 陽江, *Yángchūn* 陽春. Summoned in early Yǒnglè to compile the *Tàizǔ shílù*; rose by stages to *yòu Chūnfáng yòu zànshàn* 右春坊右贊善; replaced *Lǐbù shàngshū* **Zhèng Cì** 鄭賜 as chief editor of the **Yǒnglè dàdiǎn**; promoted *shìdú* 侍讀. In Yǒnglè 15 (1417) the northern campaign — **Rénzōng** as regent — by reason of releasing the *Chén qiānhù*, Liáng was implicated and put to death (1418). His confiscated literary remains were redeemed at double price from the government book-market by his fellow Tàihé native **Chén Xún** 陳循 (the documentary trace is preserved in Xiāo Zī's epitaph). *Míng shǐ* appended to the **Zōu Jì** 鄒濟 biography. Surviving works are [[KR4e0086]] *Bóān jí* (prose only; the poetic collection is lost). CBDB id 34478 (1356–1418).
