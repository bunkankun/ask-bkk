Liáng Qián 梁潛 (1356–1418), *zì* **Yòng-zhī** 用之, *hào* **Bó-ān** 泊菴, native of **Tài-hé** 泰和 (Jí-ān, Jiāng-xī). Hóngwǔ 29 *bǐng-zǐ* (1396) *jǔ-rén*; appointed *Cāng-xī xùn-dǎo* 蒼溪訓導 → magistrate successively at *Sì-huì* 四會, *Yáng-jiāng* 陽江, *Yáng-chūn* 陽春. Summoned in early Yǒng-lè to compile the *Tài-zǔ shí-lù*; rose by stages to *yòu Chūn-fáng yòu zàn-shàn* 右春坊右贊善; replaced *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* **Zhèng Cì** 鄭賜 as chief editor of the **Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn**; promoted *shì-dú* 侍讀. In Yǒng-lè 15 (1417) the northern campaign — **Rén-zōng** as regent — by reason of releasing the *Chén qiān-hù*, Liáng was implicated and put to death (1418). His confiscated literary remains were redeemed at double price from the government book-market by his fellow Tài-hé native **Chén Xún** 陳循 (the documentary trace is preserved in Xiāo Zī's epitaph). *Míng shǐ* appended to the **Zōu Jì** 鄒濟 biography. Surviving works are [[KR4e0086]] *Bó-ān jí* (prose only; the poetic collection is lost). CBDB id 34478 (1356–1418).
