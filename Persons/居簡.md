# Jūjiǎn 居簡

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; named first among the twenty-one editors of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]) and identified in the text as abbot of Píngjiānglù Tàipíng chánsì 平江路太平禪寺. Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Southern-Sòng poet-monk Běijiàn Jūjiǎn 北磵居簡 (1164–1246), a distinct figure.
