# Jū-jiǎn 居簡

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; named first among the twenty-one editors of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]) and identified in the text as abbot of Píng-jiāng-lù Tài-píng chán-sì 平江路太平禪寺. Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Southern-Sòng poet-monk Běi-jiàn Jū-jiǎn 北磵居簡 (1164–1246), a distinct figure.
