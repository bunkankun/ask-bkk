# Jīn Dàbēi 金大悲

Korean monk (Kim Tae-bi) named in the appended hagiographic materials of the Zōngbǎo recension of the *Tánjīng* as instigator, through his agent [[張淨滿]] Zhāng Jìngmǎn, of the 722 attempt to steal Huìnéng's mummified head from Cáoxī.
