Guǎng Mò 廣莫 (fl. late Míng) was a late-Míng Vinaya specialist who compiled the **Sìfēn jiè-běn yuán-qǐ shì-yì** 四分戒本緣起事義 ([[KR6k0148]]) — a one-fascicle compilation on the origin-narratives and explanatory notes for the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa* rules. The work belongs to the late-Míng Vinaya revival.
