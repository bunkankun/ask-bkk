**Wáng Dàohéng** 王道恒 was a Quánzhēn 全真 Daoist active in the closing years of the thirteenth century. He authored the second of three paratexts framing *[[KR5a0288|Xī yí zhǐ mí lùn 析疑指迷論]]* (DZ 276): a preface dated 1299 in which (according to Floriàn C. Reiter's notice in Schipper & Verellen, *The Taoist Canon* 2:1178) he explains that the author Niú Dàochún 牛道淳 continues the Quánzhēn tradition of the four Quánzhēn patriarchs after Wáng Chóngyáng — Mǎ Dānyáng 馬丹陽, Tán Chùduān 譚處端, Liú Chùxuán 劉處玄, and Qiū Chùjī 丘處機. Beyond this preface, no biographical record survives.
