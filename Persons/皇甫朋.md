Huángfǔ Péng 皇甫朋 was a Táng-period Daoist of whom little independent biographical data survives. He is named in the Daoist canon as the author of the *xù* 序 (preface) to [[KR5b0285]] (*Xuánpǔshān língmí mìlù* 玄圃山靈[匚@金]祕錄). The preface narrates a long transmission-history of the title from *Xiàpī xiānshēng* 下邳先生 (Master from Xiàpī) through Zhāng Liáng 張良 (i.e. Zǐfáng 子房), Lǐ Guǎnglì 李廣利, Fèi Chángfáng 費長房, and onward to Daoist masters at various sacred mountains — making it a paradigmatic *xiányìn* 仙隱 ("immortal hidden") text. No precise lifedates are recoverable; the Schipper & Verellen catalogue (*Taoist Canon* 2: 358, Catherine Despeux) places him in the Táng.
