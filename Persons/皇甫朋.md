Huáng-fǔ Péng 皇甫朋 was a Táng-period Daoist of whom little independent biographical data survives. He is named in the Daoist canon as the author of the *xù* 序 (preface) to [[KR5b0285]] (*Xuán-pǔ-shān líng-mí mì-lù* 玄圃山靈[匚@金]祕錄). The preface narrates a long transmission-history of the title from *Xià-pī xiān-shēng* 下邳先生 (Master from Xià-pī) through Zhāng Liáng 張良 (i.e. Zǐ-fáng 子房), Lǐ Guǎng-lì 李廣利, Fèi Cháng-fáng 費長房, and onward to Daoist masters at various sacred mountains — making it a paradigmatic *xián-yìn* 仙隱 ("immortal hidden") text. No precise lifedates are recoverable; the Schipper & Verellen catalogue (*Taoist Canon* 2: 358, Catherine Despeux) places him in the Táng.
