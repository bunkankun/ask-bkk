Chén Shǎohǎi 陳少海 (pen name Hónglóu Fùmèng Rén 紅樓複夢人; fl. c. 1799) was a Qīng-dynasty author, apparently from Guǎngdōng. He composed the 100-chapter *Hónglóu Fùmèng* 紅樓複夢, a sequel to *Hónglóu Mèng* 紅樓夢, which he dated in the preface to the ninth month of Jiājìng 己未 (1799), written at his "Rónggzhú Shānfáng" 蓉竹山房 retreat in Chūnzhōu. His brother contributed a further preface to the work. No biographical details beyond those extractable from the text's paratexts are known; no CBDB entry has been located.
