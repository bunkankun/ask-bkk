**Shǐ Yáo** 史瑤 (fl. mid-8th c.) — Tang disciple of [[不空]] Amoghavajra. He prepared the first translation draft of the *Xiùyào jīng* 宿曜經 ([[KR6j0530]], T21n1299) under Amoghavajra's direction in Qián-yuán 2 (759 CE); his draft was judged stiff and was subsequently revised and annotated by [[楊景風]] Yáng Jǐngfēng in 764.
