# Zhōu Yùkǎi 周裕鍇

Modern Chinese scholar of Sòng-dynasty literature and Chán Buddhism, author of *Wénzì chán yǔ Sòngdài shīxué* 文字禪與宋代詩學 (1998), a foundational study of *wénzì chán* 文字禪 ("literary Chán") and its relation to Sòng poetics.
