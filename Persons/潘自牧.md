Pān Zì-mù 潘自牧 (fl. 1195–1210), native of Jīn-huá 金華 in Wù-zhōu 婺州 (Zhèjiāng). Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* of *Qìng-yuán* 1 (1195) per the *Zhè-jiāng tōng-zhì*. He served as Magistrate of Lóng-yóu 龍游 and then as *jiào-shòu* (Education Officer); the only major work attributed to him is the *Jì-zuǎn yuān-hǎi* 記纂淵海 ([[KR3k0025]]) in 100 *juan*. No biography in the *Sòng shǐ*; his career is reconstructed only from the catalog *tíyào* and the *Zhè-jiāng tōng-zhì*.
