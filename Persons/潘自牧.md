Pān Zìmù 潘自牧 (fl. 1195–1210), native of Jīnhuá 金華 in Wùzhōu 婺州 (Zhèjiāng). Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* of *Qìngyuán* 1 (1195) per the *Zhèjiāng tōngzhì*. He served as Magistrate of Lóngyóu 龍游 and then as *jiàoshòu* (Education Officer); the only major work attributed to him is the *Jìzuǎn yuānhǎi* 記纂淵海 ([[KR3k0025]]) in 100 *juan*. No biography in the *Sòng shǐ*; his career is reconstructed only from the catalog *tíyào* and the *Zhèjiāng tōngzhì*.
