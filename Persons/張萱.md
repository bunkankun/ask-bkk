Zhāng Xuān 張萱 (1558–1641), *zì* Mèng-qí 孟奇, *hào* Jiǔ-shāng 九嶂. Native of Bó-luó 博羅 county, Huì-zhōu 惠州, Guǎng-dōng. Wàn-lì *jìnshì* (1582); served as *zhōng-shū shě-rén* 中書舍人 with charge of compiling the imperial *Wén-yuān-gé shū-mù* 文淵閣書目 — a key Míng-court bibliographic register — and rose to *Hù-bù láng-zhōng* 戶部郎中 with assignment to Sū-zhōu. A major late-Míng bibliophile whose private library *Huì-yán-tāng* 彙雁堂 was among the great Wàn-lì collections. Best known as the actual author of the *Yí yào* 疑耀 ([[KR3j0063]]), originally published in 1608 under the name of Lǐ Zhì 李贄 (a commercial fraud the *Sìkù* editors corrected with overwhelming internal evidence). CBDB has no clear matching record (the entries under 張萱 in CBDB are predominantly a different earlier person of 1459–1527). Lifedates 1558–1641 follow late-Míng biographical and gazetteer sources.
