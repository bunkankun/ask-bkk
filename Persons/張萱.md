Zhāng Xuān 張萱 (1558–1641), *zì* Mèngqí 孟奇, *hào* Jiǔshāng 九嶂. Native of Bóluó 博羅 county, Huìzhōu 惠州, Guǎngdōng. Wànlì *jìnshì* (1582); served as *zhōngshū shěrén* 中書舍人 with charge of compiling the imperial *Wényuāngé shūmù* 文淵閣書目 — a key Míng-court bibliographic register — and rose to *Hùbù lángzhōng* 戶部郎中 with assignment to Sūzhōu. A major late-Míng bibliophile whose private library *Huìyántāng* 彙雁堂 was among the great Wànlì collections. Best known as the actual author of the *Yí yào* 疑耀 ([[KR3j0063]]), originally published in 1608 under the name of Lǐ Zhì 李贄 (a commercial fraud the *Sìkù* editors corrected with overwhelming internal evidence).
