# Cí'ēn fǎshī 慈恩法師 (= [[窺基]])

The honorific *Cí'ēn fǎshī* 慈恩法師 ("Master of [Dà] cí'ēn[-sì]") refers to [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 632–682), the disciple of [[玄奘]] and founder of the Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng 慈恩 / 法相 school of Chinese Yogācāra Buddhism. The honorific is used in catalog meta entries and in textual colophons in place of the personal name; thus the catalog entry for [[KR6n0100]] *Dàshèng bǎifǎ míngmén lùn zhù* names *Cí'ēn fǎshī* 慈恩法師 as the commentator, identifying Kuījī by his school-name.

For the full biography see [[窺基]] (per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001131).
