Fàn Jǐngwén 范景文 (1587–1644), zì Mèngzhāng 夢章 / Sīrén 思仁, hào Zhìgōng 質公, posthumous title Wénzhōng 文忠 (Qing-bestowed), was the last *Gōngbù shàngshū* 工部尚書 (Minister of Works) and *Dōnggé dàxuéshì* 東閣大學士 (Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion) of the Míng. Native of Wúqiáo 吳橋 (Bǎodìng prefecture, modern Héběi). *Jìnshì* in *Wànlì xīnhài* 萬曆辛亥 = 1611; rose through *Lìbù lángzhōng*, *Tàichángsì qīng*, etc., to senior court office under *Tiānqǐ* and *Chóngzhēn*. He committed suicide by drowning in a well (*Gǔjǐng* 古井 at his Beijing residence) on 4th month, 19th day of *Chóngzhēn* 17 = 25 April 1644, the day after Lǐ Zìchéng entered the capital and the **Chóngzhēn** emperor hanged himself at Méishān. Posthumously honored *Wénzhōng* under the Qing *Shùnzhì* régime as one of the Míng *xùnjié* (martyrs-for-principle). His main biography is in *Míng shǐ* j. 265. CBDB 34752. The *Wénzhōng jí* 文忠集 ([[KR4e0234]]) is his collected works, edited posthumously by his nephew **Fàn Yùxiù** 范毓秀 and **Wáng Sūnxī** 王孫錫.

(There is a homonymous **Fàn Jǐngwén** in CBDB row 105088 / 690610 with dates 1285–1343 — a Yuán-period figure; this is unrelated to the present subject.)
