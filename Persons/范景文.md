Fàn Jǐngwén 范景文 (1587–1644), zì Mèngzhāng 夢章 / Sīrén 思仁, hào Zhìgōng 質公, posthumous title Wénzhōng 文忠 (Qing-bestowed), was the last *Gōng-bù shàng-shū* 工部尚書 (Minister of Works) and *Dōng-gé dà-xué-shì* 東閣大學士 (Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion) of the Míng. Native of Wú-qiáo 吳橋 (Bǎo-dìng prefecture, modern Hé-běi). *Jìnshì* in *Wàn-lì xīn-hài* 萬曆辛亥 = 1611; rose through *Lì-bù láng-zhōng*, *Tài-cháng-sì qīng*, etc., to senior court office under *Tiān-qǐ* and *Chóng-zhēn*. He committed suicide by drowning in a well (*Gǔ-jǐng* 古井 at his Beijing residence) on 4th month, 19th day of *Chóng-zhēn* 17 = 25 April 1644, the day after Lǐ Zì-chéng entered the capital and the **Chóng-zhēn** emperor hanged himself at Méi-shān. Posthumously honored *Wén-zhōng* under the Qing *Shùn-zhì* régime as one of the Míng *xùn-jié* (martyrs-for-principle). His main biography is in *Míng shǐ* j. 265. CBDB 34752. The *Wén-zhōng jí* 文忠集 ([[KR4e0234]]) is his collected works, edited posthumously by his nephew **Fàn Yù-xiù** 范毓秀 and **Wáng Sūn-xī** 王孫錫.

(There is a homonymous **Fàn Jǐng-wén** in CBDB row 105088 / 690610 with dates 1285–1343 — a Yuán-period figure; this is unrelated to the present subject.)
