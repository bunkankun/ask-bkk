**Zhào Kāiměi** 趙開美 (1563–1624, 明), Cháng-shú 常熟 bibliophile and woodblock-print publisher, best known for the *Zhòngjǐng quánshū* 仲景全書 of Wànlì 27 (1599), a facsimile reprint of a Northern-Sòng print of the *Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef001]]) — the unique surviving witness to the Sòng校正醫書局 recension. His *Mài-wàng-guǎn* 脈望館 library was one of the major Wànlì-period private collections.
