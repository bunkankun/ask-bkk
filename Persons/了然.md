Northern-Sòng through Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master and *Vinaya* expert. *Zì* **Zhì-yǒng** 志涌 (also written 智涌, 智湧, **Zhì-yǒng Liǎo-rán** 智涌了然), honorifics **Hǔ-xī zūn-zhě** 虎溪尊者 ("Venerable of Tiger-Stream") and **Liǎo-rán lǜ-shī** 了然律師 ("Vinaya-Master Liǎo-rán"). DILA Authority A000035. Born 1077; died Shàoxīng 紹興 11, 5th month (= 13 June – 12 July 1141), aged 65.

Major work: the **Dà-shèng zhǐ-guān fǎ-mén zōng-yuán jì** 大乘止觀法門宗圓記 ([[KR6d0153]], X55n0904, 5 *juan*) — Sòng-period subcommentary on Huì-sī's *Dà-shèng zhǐ-guān fǎ-mén* ([[KR6d0152]]).

Sources: *Shìmén zhèngtǒng* 釋門正統 *juan* 7; DILA A000035.
