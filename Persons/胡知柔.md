Late-Sòng descendant of Hú Mèngyù 胡夢昱 of Jíshuǐ 吉水 (the Sòng *Dàlǐ píngshì* 大理評事 who in 1225 was demoted and died in exile in Xiàngzhōu 象州 for memorializing on the *Jìwáng* 濟王 succession affair). Hú Zhīróu's floruit is fixed by the date of his compilation, *Bǎoyòu* 4 (1256). He compiled in his father's defence the *Xiàngtái shǒumò* 象臺首末 ([[KR2g0011]]), gathering the surviving memorials, posthumous rehabilitation papers, exchange-poetry, and commemorative writings — a Sòng family-archive *zhuànjì* of the *Jìwáng* affair that the family had begun to recover under Sòng Lǐzōng. No further biographical detail is securely attested.
