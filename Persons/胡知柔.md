Late-Sòng descendant of Hú Mèng-yù 胡夢昱 of Jí-shuǐ 吉水 (the Sòng *Dà-lǐ píng-shì* 大理評事 who in 1225 was demoted and died in exile in Xiàng-zhōu 象州 for memorializing on the *Jì-wáng* 濟王 succession affair). Hú Zhī-róu's floruit is fixed by the date of his compilation, *Bǎo-yòu* 4 (1256). He compiled in his father's defence the *Xiàng-tái shǒu-mò* 象臺首末 ([[KR2g0011]]), gathering the surviving memorials, posthumous rehabilitation papers, exchange-poetry, and commemorative writings — a Sòng family-archive *zhuàn-jì* of the *Jì-wáng* affair that the family had begun to recover under Sòng Lǐ-zōng. No further biographical detail is securely attested.
