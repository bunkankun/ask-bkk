**Xiè Yì** 謝逸 (c. 1068–1113), *zì* Wúyì 無逸, *hào* Xītáng 溪堂, of Línchuān 臨川 (Jiāngxī). Brother of Xiè Kē 謝薖 [[謝薖]]. Did not enter office; lived as a recluse-poet. Listed by Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 in the *Jiāngxī shīshè zōngpài tú* 江西詩社宗派圖 — a canonical Jiāngxī school figure. Renowned in the literary world for his *Bǎi yǒnghú dié shī* 百詠蝴蝶詩 ("Hundred Butterfly Chants") — earning him the nickname *Xiè Húdié* 謝蝴蝶 ("Xiè Butterfly"). Author of *Xītáng jí* 溪堂集 [[KR4d0118]] in 10 *juǎn*. Brother and brother are recorded together as the *Èr Xiè* 二謝. Lifedates 1068–1113 from CBDB and standard reference works.
