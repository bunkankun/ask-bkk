# Yuánxián 元賢

Sobriquet Yǒngjué 永覺 ("Eternal Awakening"). Late-Míng / early-Qīng Chán master of the Cáodòng 曹洞 line, abbot of the Gǔshān Yǒngqiān-sì 鼓山湧泉寺 in Fújiàn. Dharma heir of Wúmíng Huìjīng 無明慧經 (1548–1618). Lifedates 1578–1657.

One of the two most important seventeenth-century Cáodòng masters (alongside Wúyì Yuánlái 無異元來, 1575–1630). Compiler of the *Jìdēng lù* 繼燈錄 ([[KR6q0038]]), a six-juan Cáodòng-side lamp-record continuation; the *Chánlín shū yǔ kǎo zhèng* 禪林疏語考證 ([[KR6q0143]], 4 juan) — his accumulated ritual-petition compositions; and of a substantial independent *yǔlù* corpus preserved in the Jiāxīng canon (*Yǒngjué Yuánxián chánshī guǎnglù* 永覺元賢禪師廣錄, 30 juan). He was also the principal editor (*chóng-biān* 重編) of his master [[慧經]]'s *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0362]]; X72 n1432) — the received recension bears his mid-17th-century editorial stamp. Also compiled monastic gazetteers: the *Wēnlíng Kāiyuán sì zhì* 溫陵開元寺志 and *Gǔshān zhì* 鼓山志. Central figure in Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (OUP, 2008), and in Chinese-language scholarship on seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán.

Entered monastic life late (at 40, in 1617); lay surname Cài 蔡 with given-names Àn-xiū 闇修 (early) and Mào-qīng 懋清 (later), a fourteenth-generation descendant of the Sòng Neo-Confucian scholar Cài Yuándìng 蔡元定. Native of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Fújiàn). Received precepts at Bóshān 博山 in 1618; full awakening at Jiànyáng in Tiānqǐ 3 (1623). Held successive abbacies at the Ōu-níng Jīn-xiān ān 甌寧金仙庵, Bǎo-shàn ān 寶善庵, Fúzhōu Gǔshān Yǒngquán chánsì 鼓山湧泉寺 (his principal seat — hence Gǔshān Yuánxián 鼓山元賢), Jìngcí ān 淨慈庵, and Quánzhōu Kāiyuán sì 泉州開元寺. Died on Shùnzhì 14.10.7 (12 November 1657), aged 80. Per DILA A000135.
