**Wú Kèqián** 吳克潛 (1898–1991) was a Republican- and PRC-period Chinese physician and medical educator of Hǎiníng 海寧 (Zhèjiāng), active in Shànghǎi. He taught paediatrics successively at the Shànghǎi zhōngyī zhuānmén xuéxiào 上海中醫專門學校, the Shànghǎi guóyī xuéyuàn 上海國醫學院, and the Shànghǎi zhōngguó yīxuéyuàn 上海中國醫學院 during the late 1920s and 1930s. His major work *Érkē yàolüè* 兒科要略 ([[KR3ej002]]) was compiled from teaching notes over five years of teaching and published in 1934 (*Mínguó* 23). He was a prolific author of practical medical textbooks (pulse diagnosis, gynaecology, paediatrics) for the rising Republican-era TCM curriculum.
