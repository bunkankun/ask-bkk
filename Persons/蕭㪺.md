Yuán-period 元 Neo-Confucian patriarch and Imperial Academy *Jìjiǔ*; successor to **Xǔ Héng** [[許衡]] as the principal northern Yuán *Lǐxué* institutional figure. *Zì* **Wéidǒu** 維斗; *hào* (collection-name) **Qínzhāi** 勤齋; posthumous *shì* **Zhēnmǐn** 貞敏. Native of **Fèngyuán** 奉元 (modern Xīān 西安).

CBDB 690674 records 1241–1318; catalog meta alternative dates 1230–1307. Standard sources (*Yuánshǐ Rúlín zhuàn*) support the CBDB dates.

**Career.**
- *Jíxián xuéshì* 集賢學士
- *Guózǐ jìjiǔ* 國子祭酒 (Imperial Academy Chancellor) — succeeding Xǔ Héng

**Foundational retreat.** Originally **dug a chamber in the earth at the base of Mt. Zhōngnán** 終南山; arranged the Six Classics and the Hundred-Schools commentaries on left and right; pursued *sīsuǒ qí yì zhì yú wàngmèi* (sought-and-pondered their meaning to the point of forgetting sleep) for **thirty years** — emerging with comprehensive scholarly mastery.

**Scholarly distinction.** Expert across the Six Classics and Hundred Schools; especially profound in:
- **Sānlǐ** 三禮 (the three Confucian ritual classics: *Yílǐ*, *Lǐjì*, *Zhōulǐ*)
- **Yì** 易 (Classic of Changes)
- **Liùshū** 六書 (six script-categories — the canonical Chinese paleographical-classification scheme)

**Teaching method.** Strictly required the **Xiǎoxué** 小學 as the entry-point for all students.

**Doctrinal lineage.** *Yuánshǐ* characterization: "Uniformly takes *ZhūSì* (Confucius and Mencius) as origin, with *LiánLuò Kǎotíng* (the Zhōu Dūnyí / Chéng Brothers / Zhū Xī lineage) as basis."

**Collection.** *Qínzhāi jí* [[KR4d0490]] — original 15 *juàn* compiled by **Sū Tiānjué** [[蘇天爵]] in Zhìzhèng 4 (1344), cut at Huáidōng. The Sìkù *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* reconstruction preserves 8 *juàn*.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0490]] *Qínzhāi jí* 勤齋集 (撰).

**Reference.** *Yuánshǐ Rúlín zhuàn* (j. 189); Sū Tiānjué's *Zīxī jí* preserves the *Mùzhì míng* for Xiāo.
