Yuán-period 元 Neo-Confucian patriarch and Imperial Academy *Jì-jiǔ*; successor to **Xǔ Héng** [[許衡]] as the principal northern Yuán *Lǐ-xué* institutional figure. *Zì* **Wéi-dǒu** 維斗; *hào* (collection-name) **Qín-zhāi** 勤齋; posthumous *shì* **Zhēn-mǐn** 貞敏. Native of **Fèng-yuán** 奉元 (modern Xī-ān 西安).

CBDB 690674 records 1241–1318; catalog meta alternative dates 1230–1307. Standard sources (*Yuán-shǐ Rú-lín zhuàn*) support the CBDB dates.

**Career.**
- *Jí-xián xué-shì* 集賢學士
- *Guó-zǐ jì-jiǔ* 國子祭酒 (Imperial Academy Chancellor) — succeeding Xǔ Héng

**Foundational retreat.** Originally **dug a chamber in the earth at the base of Mt. Zhōng-nán** 終南山; arranged the Six Classics and the Hundred-Schools commentaries on left and right; pursued *sī-suǒ qí yì zhì yú wàng-mèi* (sought-and-pondered their meaning to the point of forgetting sleep) for **thirty years** — emerging with comprehensive scholarly mastery.

**Scholarly distinction.** Expert across the Six Classics and Hundred Schools; especially profound in:
- **Sān-lǐ** 三禮 (the three Confucian ritual classics: *Yí-lǐ*, *Lǐ-jì*, *Zhōu-lǐ*)
- **Yì** 易 (Classic of Changes)
- **Liù-shū** 六書 (six script-categories — the canonical Chinese paleographical-classification scheme)

**Teaching method.** Strictly required the **Xiǎo-xué** 小學 as the entry-point for all students.

**Doctrinal lineage.** *Yuán-shǐ* characterization: "Uniformly takes *Zhū-Sì* (Confucius and Mencius) as origin, with *Lián-Luò Kǎo-tíng* (the Zhōu Dūn-yí / Chéng Brothers / Zhū Xī lineage) as basis."

**Collection.** *Qín-zhāi jí* [[KR4d0490]] — original 15 *juàn* compiled by **Sū Tiān-jué** [[蘇天爵]] in Zhì-zhèng 4 (1344), cut at Huái-dōng. The Sìkù *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* reconstruction preserves 8 *juàn*.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0490]] *Qín-zhāi jí* 勤齋集 (撰).

**Reference.** *Yuán-shǐ Rú-lín zhuàn* (j. 189); Sū Tiān-jué's *Zī-xī jí* preserves the *Mù-zhì míng* for Xiāo.
