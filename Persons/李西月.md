**Lǐ Xīyuè** 李西月 (1806–1856), founder of the inner-alchemical **Xīpài** 西派 lineage; author of *Sānchē mìzhǐ* 三車秘旨 and ultimate doctrinal source for Wāng Dōngtíng's *Xìngmìng yàozhǐ*.
