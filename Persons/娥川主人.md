**Échuan Zhǔrén** 娥川主人 (Master of Goose Creek) is the pseudonym of the otherwise unidentified author of *Shì Wú Pǐ* 世無匹 [[KR4k0224]], a Qīng-dynasty social romance novel in 16 chapters. No biographical information has been located and no CBDB entry exists for this pen name.
