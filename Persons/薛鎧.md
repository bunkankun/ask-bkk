**Xuē Kǎi** 薛鎧 (*zì* Liángwǔ 良武), Míng-period imperial physician (*Tàiyīyuàn yīshì* 太醫院醫士) of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Active in the *Hóngzhì*–*Zhèngdé* period. Specialist in paediatrics; bearer of the Xuē-family medical practice continued by his son **Xuē Jǐ** 薛己 ([[薛己]], 1487–1559). The paediatric work **Bǎoyīng cuōyào** 保嬰撮要 ([[KR3ej015]], 20 *juǎn*, 1556) is conventionally attributed in the bibliographic literature primarily to Xuē Kǎi as initial compiler, with Xuē Jǐ as the principal editor-completer who brought it into final form and publication. The relation of attribution is similar to that of the *Yǎngshēng sìyào* 養生四要 of the Wàn family between Wàn Quán's grandfather and Wàn Quán himself. Lifedates are not securely recorded.
