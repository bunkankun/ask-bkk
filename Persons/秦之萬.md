**Qín Zhīwàn** 秦之萬 (alias **Qín Yòuān** 秦又安) of Shànghǎi — Republican-era physician of the Qín family medical circle. Prefacer of the 1930 print of *Tāichǎn zhèngzhì* ([[KR3ei064]]), in collaboration with the Qín family editorial work supervised by his relative [[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi. Not in CBDB.
