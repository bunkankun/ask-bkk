Ryōson 了尊 (1230–1296), Shingon-line Siddham scholar of the **Mt. Kōya** lineage in the Kamakura period. His magnum opus is the *Shittan ryaku-zu shō / Shittan rin ryaku-zu shō* [[KR6t0420]], a ten-fascicle Siddham encyclopedic compendium that is the **most comprehensive Japanese Siddham survey** of the Kamakura period and the **last major creative work** of the classical Japanese Siddham tradition before its slow decline. The work supersedes the earlier compendia (Annen, Junnyū, Myōkaku, Shinkaku) by gathering and synthesizing their material together with the late-Heian and Kamakura philological developments.
