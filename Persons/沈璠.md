Shěn Fán 沈璠 (*zì* Lǔ-zhēn 魯珍; CBDB 243314 / 270217 / 336233 records the name without dates) — mid-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician with a great clinical reputation in his region for about forty years (according to the preface to his casebook). The internal evidence places his floruit conservatively in the early-to-mid eighteenth century (Yōng-zhèng / early Qián-lóng era). His clinical style is the Dān-xī-school *qīng-huǒ huò-tán* 清火豁痰 (phlegm-clearing fire-cooling) synthesis in its mid-Qīng Jiāng-nán continuation, citing the *Nèi-jīng*, [[朱震亨]] Dān-xī, and [[李杲]] Dōng-yuán as principal authorities. His [[KR3ep033]] *Shěn-shì yī-àn* 沈氏醫案 survives in one *juǎn*.
