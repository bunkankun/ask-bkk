Shěn Fán 沈璠 (*zì* Lǔzhēn 魯珍; CBDB 243314 / 270217 / 336233 records the name without dates) — mid-Qīng Jiāngnán physician with a great clinical reputation in his region for about forty years (according to the preface to his casebook). The internal evidence places his floruit conservatively in the early-to-mid eighteenth century (Yōngzhèng / early Qiánlóng era). His clinical style is the Dān-xī-school *qīnghuǒ huòtán* 清火豁痰 (phlegm-clearing fire-cooling) synthesis in its mid-Qīng Jiāngnán continuation, citing the *Nèijīng*, [[朱震亨]] Dānxī, and [[李杲]] Dōngyuán as principal authorities. His [[KR3ep033]] *Shěnshì yīàn* 沈氏醫案 survives in one *juǎn*.
