Zhèngzhǐ 正止 (active late Míng), Míng Buddhist scholar; the final editorial collaborator on the late-Míng recension of [[KR6e0150|*Huáyán jīng hǎi yìn dào chǎng chàn yí*]] (X1470). His function was *zhì dìng* 治定 ("definitive editorial establishment"). Birth and death dates not preserved.
