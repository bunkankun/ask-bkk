Fù Liàng 傅亮 (374–426 CE; *zì* Jìyǒu 季友) was a Liú Sòng 劉宋 founding-era senior official from Línhǎi 臨海. He served as Shàngshū pú shè 尚書僕射 and was one of the principal aides who drafted key imperial rescripts during Liú Yù's 劉裕 establishment of the Song dynasty (420 CE). He was executed in 426 CE during Emperor Wén's 文帝 purge of the regency council. His biography is in *Sòngshū* 宋書 juǎn 43 and *Nán shǐ* 南史 juǎn 15. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0040]].
