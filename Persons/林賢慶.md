**Lín Xiánqìng** 林賢慶 (*fl.* late Guāngxù era, 1890s), late-Qīng Fujian-coast literatus from **Hǎitán** 海壇 (modern Píngtán 平潭 island, off the Fujian coast). In 1898 he was serving in the *mù* (secretariat) of the *Tóngān cānzhèn* 同安參鎮 Chén Zǐmíng 陳子明 (*dūdū* 都督, Brigade-Commander); a colleague in the same prefectural administration as [[周子薌]] (Zhōu Zǐxiāng). Wrote the preface to Zhōu's *Jīngyàn qífāng* 經驗奇方 ([[KR3ed117]]), dated *Guāngxù* 24 *wùxū* mid-winter = winter 1898. No further biographical record is securely identifiable.
