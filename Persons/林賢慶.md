**Lín Xián-qìng** 林賢慶 (*fl.* late Guāng-xù era, 1890s), late-Qīng Fujian-coast literatus from **Hǎitán** 海壇 (modern Píng-tán 平潭 island, off the Fujian coast). In 1898 he was serving in the *mù* (secretariat) of the *Tóng-ān cān-zhèn* 同安參鎮 Chén Zǐ-míng 陳子明 (*dū-dū* 都督, Brigade-Commander); a colleague in the same prefectural administration as [[周子薌]] (Zhōu Zǐ-xiāng). Wrote the preface to Zhōu's *Jīngyàn qífāng* 經驗奇方 ([[KR3ed117]]), dated *Guāng-xù* 24 *wù-xū* mid-winter = winter 1898. No further biographical record is securely identifiable.
