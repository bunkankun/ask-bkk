Northern-Sòng to early-Southern-Sòng official, son of Sū Sòng [[蘇頌]] (1020–1101) and editor of his father's collected works *Sū Wèigōng wén jí* [[KR4d0035]] (72 *juǎn*). The original 72-*juǎn* arrangement preserved in the WYG is Sū Xié's editorial work. No CBDB match (the BIOG_MAIN does not record a confident entry); fl. ca. 1080–1140.
