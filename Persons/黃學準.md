Huáng Xuézhǔn 黃學準 (*fl.* mid-16th c.). Mid-Míng minor scholar; clan-nephew (*zúzǐ*) of [[黃衷]] of Nánhǎi. Supplied supplementary annotations (*zēngzhù* 增注) to his clansman's *Hǎi yǔ* 海語 ([[KR2k0144]]); his notes were included in the printed editions and preserved in the Sìkù recension. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 436340 has no birth or death year.
