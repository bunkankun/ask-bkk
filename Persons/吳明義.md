Wú Míngyì 吳明義 (*zì* Zhòngyí 仲誼), Yuán-period Daoist of Póyáng 番(鄱)陽 (Jiāngxī), *shānzhǎng* 山長 ("mountain-chief", senior abbot) of the *Dúfēng* 獨峰 abbey on Mt. Xiāndū / Jìnyún 仙都/縉雲 in Chùzhōu (modern Zhèjiāng). He served as collator (校正) of [[陳性定]] (Chén Xìngdìng)'s *Xiāndū zhì* 仙都志 [[KR5b0307]] (DZ 602), prefaced 1348. The table of compilers names him *Dúfēng shānzhǎng Póyáng Wú Míngyì Zhòngyí* 獨峯山長番陽吳明義仲誼. CBDB has no entry.
