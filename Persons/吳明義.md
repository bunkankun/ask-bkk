Wú Míng-yì 吳明義 (*zì* Zhòng-yí 仲誼), Yuán-period Daoist of Pó-yáng 番(鄱)陽 (Jiāng-xī), *shān-zhǎng* 山長 ("mountain-chief", senior abbot) of the *Dú-fēng* 獨峰 abbey on Mt. Xiān-dū / Jìn-yún 仙都/縉雲 in Chù-zhōu (modern Zhèjiāng). He served as collator (校正) of [[陳性定]] (Chén Xìng-dìng)'s *Xiān-dū zhì* 仙都志 [[KR5b0307]] (DZ 602), prefaced 1348. The table of compilers names him *Dú-fēng shān-zhǎng Pó-yáng Wú Míng-yì Zhòng-yí* 獨峯山長番陽吳明義仲誼. CBDB has no entry.
