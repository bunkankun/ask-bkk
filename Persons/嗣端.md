# Sì-duān 嗣端

Southern-Sòng Chán disciple (*cān xué* 參學) of Hǔ-qiū Shào-lóng 虎丘紹隆 (1077–1136). Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0292]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
