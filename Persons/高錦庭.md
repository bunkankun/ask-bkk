# Gāo Jǐntíng 高錦庭

Mid-Qīng Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu) physician (1755–1827), *míng* Bǐngjūn 秉鈞, *zì* Jǐntíng 錦庭. Specialist in external-medicine and warm-disease, author of the *Yángkē xīndé jí* 瘍科心得集 (1805) — one of the major Qīng *yángkē* (external-medicine / surgery) texts. Pupil of [[薛雪]] Xuē Shēngbái. Anthologised by Liǔ Bǎoyí 柳寶詒 in *Liǔxuǎn sìjiā yīàn* ([[KR3ep021]]).
