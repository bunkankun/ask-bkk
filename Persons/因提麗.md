Yīntílì 因提麗 (\*Indrali / \*Indra-…; lifedates unknown), foreign *śramaṇa* mentioned in [[道安|Dàoān]]'s preface to [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (T1505) as the monk who had earlier brought [[婆素跋陀|Vasubhadra]]'s *Tridharmaka* abstract to the Central-Asian kingdom of Qiánbù 前部國 (Turfan region), keeping the text concealed on his person. The abstract was disseminated only after King Mídì 彌第 of Qiánbù learned to recite it; no further biographical record survives.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (named as transmitter of the Indic exemplar).
