# Cúnjiǎng 存獎

Late-Táng Chán master, styled Xīnghuà Cúnjiǎng 興化存獎 after the Xīnghuà yuàn 興化院 in Wèifǔ 魏府. Senior dharma-heir of Línjì [[義玄]] Yìxuán; the principal conduit through which the Línjì line passed to subsequent generations (via Nányuàn Huìyóng 南院慧顒, Fēngxué Yánzhǎo 風穴延沼, Shǒushān Shěngniàn 首山省念, and on to Fényáng [[善昭]] Shànzhāo and Shíshuāng [[楚圓]] Chǔyuán).
