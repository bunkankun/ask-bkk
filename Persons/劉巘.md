# Liú Yǎn 劉巘

Southern Qí 南齊 classicist. Author of *Yì zhù* 易註 and *Lúnyǔ yì shū* 論語義疏, both lost in transmission. *Yì* fragments are preserved in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Nán Qí shū* 南齊書 (juan 39) and *Nán shǐ* 南史 give his biography.
