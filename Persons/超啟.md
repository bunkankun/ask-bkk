Chāoqǐ 超啟, *zì* **Zǔliàng** 祖亮 ("Patriarchal Lustre"); often referred to as **Zǔliàng Qǐ** 祖亮啟. LínjìYángqí monk in the **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 line — successor at his teacher **Zīfú Língjī Xíngguān** [[行觀]] (1616–1681)'s principal abbacy at **Húzhōu Xiángfèng Zīfúsì** 翔鳳資福寺. Lifedates unknown — active **1690–1701**. The DILA database does not register a separate entry; the lineage placement is inferred from the abbacy succession at Zīfúsì and from the Mì-yún-line generation-character *chāo* 超.

**Abbacies (three).** **Húzhōu Xiángfèngshān Zīfú chánsì** 湖州翔鳳資福禪寺 (entered **康熙庚午二月十五日** = **1690-03-25** — nine years after his teacher's 1681 death); **Hángzhōu Wūshān Jìngmíng chánsì** 杭州㞾山淨明禪寺 (entered **康熙三十二年三月初八日** = **1693-04-12**); **Jiāxīng Shíménxiàn Fúyán chánsì** 嘉興石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙三十三年九月二十一日** = **1694-11-08** — the same Fúyánsì his teacher had taken over in 1671).

**Work.** [[KR6q0563]] 《祖亮啟禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B449, recorded by *fǎsì* **Guǎnghé** [[廣和]] *jìlù*; preface dated **康熙四十年孟夏蓂生莢之四日普明法弟** = **1701** lunar 4 (Kāngxī 40, by 普明 法弟 — likely a co-disciple).
