Chāo-qǐ 超啟, *zì* **Zǔ-liàng** 祖亮 ("Patriarchal Lustre"); often referred to as **Zǔ-liàng Qǐ** 祖亮啟. Línjì-Yángqí monk in the **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 line — successor at his teacher **Zī-fú Líng-jī Xíng-guān** [[行觀]] (1616–1681)'s principal abbacy at **Hú-zhōu Xiáng-fèng Zī-fú-sì** 翔鳳資福寺. Lifedates unknown — active **1690–1701**. The DILA database does not register a separate entry; the lineage placement is inferred from the abbacy succession at Zī-fú-sì and from the Mì-yún-line generation-character *chāo* 超.

**Abbacies (three).** **Hú-zhōu Xiáng-fèng-shān Zī-fú chán-sì** 湖州翔鳳資福禪寺 (entered **康熙庚午二月十五日** = **1690-03-25** — nine years after his teacher's 1681 death); **Háng-zhōu Wū-shān Jìng-míng chán-sì** 杭州㞾山淨明禪寺 (entered **康熙三十二年三月初八日** = **1693-04-12**); **Jiā-xīng Shí-mén-xiàn Fú-yán chán-sì** 嘉興石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙三十三年九月二十一日** = **1694-11-08** — the same Fú-yán-sì his teacher had taken over in 1671).

**Work.** [[KR6q0563]] 《祖亮啟禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B449, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Guǎng-hé** [[廣和]] *jì-lù*; preface dated **康熙四十年孟夏蓂生莢之四日普明法弟** = **1701** lunar 4 (Kāngxī 40, by 普明 法弟 — likely a co-disciple).
