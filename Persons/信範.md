Shinhan 信範, Shingon-line Siddham scholar of the **late Heian / early Kamakura period**. Precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works; he is conventionally placed in the period **c. 1200–1265** on the basis of doctrinal vocabulary in his *Shittan hi-den-ki* [[KR6t0419]]. The work is one of a class of late-Heian / early-Kamakura **Shingon esoteric transmission texts** characterized by their framing of doctrinal material as *secret oral teaching*. The DILA authority id is `A001255`.
