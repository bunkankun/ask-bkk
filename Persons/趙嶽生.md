# 趙嶽生

Zhào Yuèshēng (*zì* Shìgōngfǔ 視公甫)

Early-Qīng layman who wrote the 1681 preface to [[翁春]] Wēng Chūn and [[王錫琯]] Wáng Xīguǎn's [[KR6c0078]] *Jīngāng jīng qiǎnjiě*.
