Fāng Shí-sūn 方實孫 (lifedates not securely recorded; fl. mid-thirteenth century, late Lǐ-zōng / early Dù-zōng), *zì* Duān-zhòng 端仲. Native place plausibly Fú-jiàn per *Fú-jiàn tōng-zhì* 149.31b reference. CBDB id 20436. Father-in-law: Liú Guāng-shū 劉光叔 (CBDB id 20437).

The Sìkù *tiyao* on his *Yì*-work explicitly registers "*one does not know whence he comes from*" — biographical record obscure beyond Liú Kè-zhuāng 劉克莊's references in the latter's collected works.

Career: submitted his *Yì shuō* (= [[KR1a0060]] *Cóng-shān dú Zhōuyì*) to the Lǐ-zōng court c. 1258; admitted in commoner-status (*bù-yī* 布衣) to the *Shǐ-jú* 史局 (Historiographical Bureau). The contemporary chief minister, on account of his repeated submission of memorials, wished to have him exempted from the spring-office requirement; Fāng Shí-sūn responded in the negative; he was then dismissed by the *fēng-wén* (rumor-and-hearsay) procedural mechanism and "*returned hào-rán [in flowing-vast spirit]*." His ultimate fate is unrecorded.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0060]] *Cóng-shān dú Zhōuyì* 淙山讀周易 (also titled *Cóng-shān dú Zhōuyì jì* 淙山讀周易記; 21 juan in the Sìkù base, with *Sòng-zhì* recording 8 juan, *Dàn-shēng-táng* 10 juan, *Jù-lè-táng* 16 juan; one of the multiple-juan-reckoning works of the Sòng *Yì* corpus). Composed by 1258 (*Bǎo-yòu* 6, third-month-first-day auto-preface).

Methodologically a late-Lǐ-zōng-period Zhū-Cài-school *xiàng-shù-and-textual-criticism* synthesizer. His *Yì guà-biàn hé tú* 易卦變合圖 is a methodologically clean integration of Zhū Xī's *guà-biàn* (hexagram-transformation) materials into a single visualizable structure, supplementing the *Yì xué qǐ-méng*'s incomplete coverage.
