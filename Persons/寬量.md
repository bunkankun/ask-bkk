# Kuānliàng 寬量

A late-Qīng monk active in the *Sìmíng* 四明 (= Níngbō) region of eastern Zhèjiāng during the *Guāngxù* 光緒 reign. Native of *Yíndōng* 鄞東 (eastern *Yín* 鄞 county = the Níngbō region). *Zì* **Yuánhǎi** 源海; *hào* **Dànyún** 澹雲 ("Light Cloud") and **Xīduò** 西舵 ("Western Rudder"). His self-designation is *bùxiào yúxià fánfū sēng* 不肖愚下凡夫僧 ("unworthy stupid-and-low ordinary-fellow monk") — a characteristic late-Qīng self-effacing monastic register.

Author of the **《淨土救生船詩》** *Jìngtǔ jiùshēng chuán shī* [[KR6p0134]] (X1216) — a sequence of forty-eight (matching Amitābha's forty-eight vows) seven-character four-line *juéjù* poems on Pure Land themes, each accompanied by an extended self-commentary of several hundred to several thousand characters drawing out the *sān guān yuán xiū* 三觀圓修 doctrine. The work was published in 1896 with prefatory and postface contributions from a *Jīngāng jīng* commentator and from the late-Qīng / early-Republican Confucian-Buddhist *zhèngxué* figure **Xià Zhènwǔ** 夏震武 (1854–1930).
