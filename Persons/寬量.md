# Kuān-liàng 寬量

A late-Qīng monk active in the *Sì-míng* 四明 (= Níng-bō) region of eastern Zhèjiāng during the *Guāng-xù* 光緒 reign. Native of *Yín-dōng* 鄞東 (eastern *Yín* 鄞 county = the Níng-bō region). *Zì* **Yuán-hǎi** 源海; *hào* **Dàn-yún** 澹雲 ("Light Cloud") and **Xī-duò** 西舵 ("Western Rudder"). His self-designation is *bù-xiào yú-xià fán-fū sēng* 不肖愚下凡夫僧 ("unworthy stupid-and-low ordinary-fellow monk") — a characteristic late-Qīng self-effacing monastic register.

Author of the **《淨土救生船詩》** *Jìng-tǔ jiù-shēng chuán shī* [[KR6p0134]] (X1216) — a sequence of forty-eight (matching Amitābha's forty-eight vows) seven-character four-line *jué-jù* poems on Pure Land themes, each accompanied by an extended self-commentary of several hundred to several thousand characters drawing out the *sān guān yuán xiū* 三觀圓修 doctrine. The work was published in 1896 with prefatory and postface contributions from a *Jīn-gāng jīng* commentator and from the late-Qīng / early-Republican Confucian-Buddhist *zhèng-xué* figure **Xià Zhèn-wǔ** 夏震武 (1854–1930).
