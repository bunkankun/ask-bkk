# 楊圭

Yáng Guī

Sòng layman of Pǔchéng 浦城, retired *Zhōngdàifū* 中大夫, holder of the rank *Pǔchéngxiàn kāiguónán* 浦城縣開國男 and the 紫金魚袋. Compiler of the seventeen-house collected *Vajracchedikā* commentary that became the Sòng substrate of [[KR6c0056]], with co-editors Yáng Zōngyuán 楊宗元 and Pān Shùnlóng 潘舜龍.
