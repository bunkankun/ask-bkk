Xiè Gān-shù 謝甘澍 (*hào* Xìng-yuán 杏園, "Apricot Garden" — taking over his father's studio name) — mid-nineteenth-century Jiāngxī Fēng-chéng 豐城 physician, third son of [[謝星煥]] Xiè Xīng-huàn (1788–1855) and the editor who prepared his father's [[KR3ep040]] *Dé-xīn-jí yī-àn* 得心集醫案 for posthumous publication in the 1860s. Beyond his editorial role no further biographical data are available; CBDB has no entry.
