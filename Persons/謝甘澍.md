Xiè Gānshù 謝甘澍 (*hào* Xìngyuán 杏園, "Apricot Garden" — taking over his father's studio name) — mid-nineteenth-century Jiāngxī Fēngchéng 豐城 physician, third son of [[謝星煥]] Xiè Xīnghuàn (1788–1855) and the editor who prepared his father's [[KR3ep040]] *Déxīnjí yīàn* 得心集醫案 for posthumous publication in the 1860s.
