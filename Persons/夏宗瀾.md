Xià Zōnglán 夏宗瀾, Yōngzhèng-period pupil of [[楊名時]] Yáng Míngshí (1660–1736). Followed Yáng during his tenure as Yúnnán Governor and recorded master-pupil dialogues on the *Yì* as the *Yì shuō* 易說 — a derivative that the *Sìkù* editors regard as "quite shallow" by comparison with Yáng's own *Zhōuyì zhájì* ([[KR1a0141]]).
