Chuán Ào 傳奧 (fl. mid-Tang) was a Tang-dynasty Buddhist scholar whose extant works include the **Fànwǎng-jīng jì** 梵網經記 ([[KR6k0089]]) — a two-fascicle commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* in Huayan-influenced exegetical style. His biographical details are sparse; he is associated with the Cháng-ān monastic-scholarly milieu of the late 7th to early 8th century.
