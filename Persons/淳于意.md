# 淳于意 Chúnyú Yì

**Chúnyú Yì** 淳于意 (also known as **Cānggōng** 倉公 "Granary Master", c. 205 BCE – c. 150 BCE), Western Hàn physician of Qí 齊; one of the earliest named figures in Chinese medical history. Subject of an extensive biographical record in *Shǐjì* 105 (*Biǎn Què Cānggōng lièzhuàn*), which preserves twenty-five of his clinical case records — the earliest extant Chinese medical case histories. Studied with [[公乘陽慶]] Gōngshèng Yángqìng.
