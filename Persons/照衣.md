Zhào-yī 照衣, *hào* **Huì-jué** 慧覺. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). Born **明萬曆乙巳 2/21 子時** = **1605-04-08 Gregorian**; died **康熙12 9/18** = **1673-10-27 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **69**, *fǎ-là* **38**. Native of **Qí-jiāng-xiàn** 綦江縣 (Yú-zhōu / Chóng-qìng region, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Xióng** 熊.

**Formation.** Lay-life until **age 30**, then awakening on *shēn-shì kǔ-kōng* 身世苦空; pilgrimage to **Huá-yín-shān** 華銀山 to revere **Pǔ-xián**; tonsured under **Cháng-bái** 常白. After Cháng-bái's death (six months later) Zhào-yī served as abbot of the home-monastery for 4 years before retiring. Pilgrimage to **Jiāng-nán**; **1644 (順治元 jiǎ-shēn 之變)** retreat to **Yè-láng** 夜郎 (Sì-chuān-Guì-zhōu border) for 8 years. **順治癸巳 9 = 1653/9**: traveled to **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** at **Liáng-shān Jīn-chéng-sì** 梁山金城寺, then to **Shuāng-guì-sì**, where transmission was bestowed.

**Eight abbacies.** **Chóng-qìng Chóng-yīn-sì** 重慶崇因寺; **Hú-shān Zhì-píng-sì** 湖山治平寺; **Tài-gōng-shān Shí-bǎo-sì** 太公山石寶寺; **Jiāng-jīn Yuán-tōng-sì** 江津圓通寺; **Zhū-lín Hóng-fú-sì** 珠林洪福寺; **Bā-xiàn Bǎo-lián-yuàn** 巴縣寶蓮院; etc. Stūpa at one of these.

**Heirs.** **Pò-fēng Zhòng** [[普重]] 破峰重 (cf. [[KR6q0492]]); **Fó-yǔ Yù** [[徹御]] 佛語御 (cf. [[KR6q0487]] and compiler of [[KR6q0486]]); **Cuì-é Dēng** 翠峩登; **Xiù-é Mài** 琇峩脈; **Zhèng-é Hé** 正峩合; **Qīng-yuán Yì** 清源懿.

**Work.** [[KR6q0486]] 《慧覺衣禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B340, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chè-yù** [[徹御]] *děng biān*. Sources: [[KR6q0486]] j. 3 *xíng-lù*; 《錦江禪燈》 j. 10; *Fó-guāng dà-cí-diǎn* 5519.
