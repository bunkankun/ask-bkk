Zhàoyī 照衣, *hào* **Huìjué** 慧覺. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). Born **明萬曆乙巳 2/21 子時** = **1605-04-08 Gregorian**; died **康熙12 9/18** = **1673-10-27 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **69**, *fǎlà* **38**. Native of **Qíjiāngxiàn** 綦江縣 (Yúzhōu / Chóngqìng region, Sìchuān); lay surname **Xióng** 熊.

**Formation.** Lay-life until **age 30**, then awakening on *shēnshì kǔkōng* 身世苦空; pilgrimage to **Huáyínshān** 華銀山 to revere **Pǔxián**; tonsured under **Chángbái** 常白. After Chángbái's death (six months later) Zhàoyī served as abbot of the home-monastery for 4 years before retiring. Pilgrimage to **Jiāngnán**; **1644 (順治元 jiǎshēn 之變)** retreat to **Yèláng** 夜郎 (SìchuānGuìzhōu border) for 8 years. **順治癸巳 9 = 1653/9**: traveled to **Pòshān Hǎimíng** at **Liángshān Jīnchéngsì** 梁山金城寺, then to **Shuāngguìsì**, where transmission was bestowed.

**Eight abbacies.** **Chóngqìng Chóngyīnsì** 重慶崇因寺; **Húshān Zhìpíngsì** 湖山治平寺; **Tàigōngshān Shíbǎosì** 太公山石寶寺; **Jiāngjīn Yuántōngsì** 江津圓通寺; **Zhūlín Hóngfúsì** 珠林洪福寺; **Bāxiàn Bǎoliányuàn** 巴縣寶蓮院; etc. Stūpa at one of these.

**Heirs.** **Pòfēng Zhòng** [[普重]] 破峰重 (cf. [[KR6q0492]]); **Fóyǔ Yù** [[徹御]] 佛語御 (cf. [[KR6q0487]] and compiler of [[KR6q0486]]); **Cuìé Dēng** 翠峩登; **Xiùé Mài** 琇峩脈; **Zhèngé Hé** 正峩合; **Qīngyuán Yì** 清源懿.

**Work.** [[KR6q0486]] 《慧覺衣禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J35 B340, compiled by *fǎsì* **Chèyù** [[徹御]] *děng biān*. Sources: [[KR6q0486]] j. 3 *xínglù*; 《錦江禪燈》 j. 10; *Fóguāng dàcídiǎn* 5519.
