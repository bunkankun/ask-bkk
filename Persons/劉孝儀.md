Liú Xiàoyí 劉孝儀 (fl. ca. 510–549 CE; also written 劉孝義) was a Liáng 梁 official and court poet, a younger brother of Liú Xiàochuò 劉孝綽 and Liú Xiàowēi 劉孝威 in the Péngchéng Liú 彭城劉 literary family. He served as Yùshǐ zhōngchéng 御史中丞. His poetic output belongs to the Palace Style (*gōngtǐ* 宮體) tradition. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0061]].
