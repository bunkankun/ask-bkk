Yī Yǐn 伊尹 was the legendary chief minister of Tāng 湯, founder of the Shāng dynasty (trad. 17th c. BCE). The medical tradition attributes to him the lost *Tāngyè jīngfǎ* 湯液經法 — the decoction-pharmacology that, in the revisionist reading of [[馮世綸]] and others, is supposed to be preserved in fragmentary form in the *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]). The attribution is essentially mythological.
