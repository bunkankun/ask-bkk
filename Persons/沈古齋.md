**Shěn Gǔ-zhāi** 沈古齋 (*zì* Huà-sān 化三, *fl.* mid – late 19th c., Pí-tǒng 郫筒 / Pí-xiàn 郫縣, Sì-chuān) — late-Qīng Sì-chuān physician within the [[鄭欽安]] Zhèng Qīn-ān circle. Author of one of the 1874 prefaces to Zhèng's *Yī-fǎ yuán-tōng* ([[KR3er080]]), dated *Tóng-zhì* 13, *pú-jié yuè* 蒲節月 (Dragon-Boat month). Not in CBDB.
