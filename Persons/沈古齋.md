**Shěn Gǔzhāi** 沈古齋 (*zì* Huàsān 化三, *fl.* mid – late 19th c., Pítǒng 郫筒 / Píxiàn 郫縣, Sìchuān) — late-Qīng Sìchuān physician within the [[鄭欽安]] Zhèng Qīnān circle. Author of one of the 1874 prefaces to Zhèng's *Yīfǎ yuántōng* ([[KR3er080]]), dated *Tóngzhì* 13, *pújié yuè* 蒲節月 (Dragon-Boat month). Not in CBDB.
