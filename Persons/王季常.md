# 王季常

Wáng Jìcháng, late-Míng lay Buddhist (*jūshì* 居士) and official of the Gōngbù 工部 (Board of Works). Sponsor and editor of the late-Míng combined-edition (*huìběn*) project on the Tiāntái *Pǔmén* corpus undertaken at Jīnlíng 金陵 in Chóngzhēn 崇禎 1–2 (1628–1629); his patronage produced the four combined-edition and auxiliary navigational works ([[KR6d0050]], [[KR6d0051]], [[KR6d0052]], [[KR6d0053]], [[KR6d0054]], [[KR6d0055]]) connected with Zhīlǐ's subcommentaries on the *Guānyīn jīng*.
