Zhēn-huì 真慧, Línjì-line dharma-disciple of **Wén-mù Niàn** [[念]] 文穆念 (1633–1678) in the Qí-shān Lǎn-fàng line. Lead compiler of the 5-juan 《文穆念禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0500]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
