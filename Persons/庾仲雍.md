Yú Zhōngyōng 庾仲雍 (*fl.* Southern Qi–Liang, late 5th–early 6th century CE) was a geographic writer of the Southern Dynasties period. His family name Yú 庾 is prominent in Eastern Jin–Southern Dynasties court culture (the Yú family being one of the great noble clans). He is the author of both a *Xiāngzhōng jì* 湘中記 ([[KR2k0178]]) and a *Xiāngzhōu jì* 湘州記 ([[KR2k0180]]), covering the geography and legends of the Hunan region. No further biographical details are attested; his works are known only through quotations in medieval encyclopedias.
