Dàobiāo 道摽 (also Shì Dàobiāo 釋道摽) was a late-fourth- / early-fifth-century Chinese monk active under the Yáo Qín 姚秦, known principally as the author of the preface to the *Shèlìfú Āpítán Lùn* 舍利弗阿毘曇論 ([[KR6l0013]], T1548), the Dharmaguptaka Abhidharma translated by Tánmóyēshè 曇摩耶舍 ([[曇摩耶舍]]) and Tánmójuéduō 曇摩崛多 ([[曇摩崛多]]). His preface explicates the title 阿毘曇 as 無比法 ("incomparable dharma"), names Śāriputra as the source, and warns against sectarian fragmentation. No independent biography survives.
