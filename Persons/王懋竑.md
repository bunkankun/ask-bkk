Qīng scholar from Bǎoyīng 寶應 (modern Jiāngsū), courtesy name Yǔzhōng 予中, sobriquet *Báitián* 白田 (after his Báitián cǎotáng 白田草堂 retreat). CBDB id 35054 attests his lifedates as 1668–1741. He took the *jìnshì* in *Kāngxī wùxū* (1718) and was appointed *Ānqìngfǔ jiàoshòu* 安慶府教授 (Education Officer of Ānqìng prefecture). In *Yōngzhèng guǐmǎo* (1723) he was specially summoned to serve in the inner court and reassigned as Compiler of the *Hànlín* Academy 翰林院編修. He is principally remembered as a Qīng *Zhūxué* loyalist and the author of the standard Qīng *Zhūzǐ niánpǔ* 朱子年譜 ([[KR2g0016]]; *Niánpǔ* 4 *juàn* + *Kǎoyì* 4 *juàn* + *Lùnxué yàoyǔ* 2 *juàn*), prepared in deliberate refutation of the Wáng Yángmíng *wǎnnián dìnglùn* 晚年定論 thesis. His *Báitián cǎotáng cúngǎo* 白田草堂存稿 (literary collection) survives separately. The *Sìkù* editors place his *Zhūzǐ niánpǔ* at the head of the Qīng-period *zhuànjì·míngrén* class.
