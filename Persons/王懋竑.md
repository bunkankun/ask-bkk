Qīng scholar from Bǎo-yīng 寶應 (modern Jiāng-sū), courtesy name Yǔ-zhōng 予中, sobriquet *Bái-tián* 白田 (after his Bái-tián cǎo-táng 白田草堂 retreat). CBDB id 35054 attests his lifedates as 1668–1741. He took the *jìnshì* in *Kāng-xī wù-xū* (1718) and was appointed *Ān-qìng-fǔ jiào-shòu* 安慶府教授 (Education Officer of Ān-qìng prefecture). In *Yōng-zhèng guǐ-mǎo* (1723) he was specially summoned to serve in the inner court and reassigned as Compiler of the *Hàn-lín* Academy 翰林院編修. He is principally remembered as a Qīng *Zhū-xué* loyalist and the author of the standard Qīng *Zhū-zǐ niánpǔ* 朱子年譜 ([[KR2g0016]]; *Niánpǔ* 4 *juàn* + *Kǎo-yì* 4 *juàn* + *Lùn-xué yào-yǔ* 2 *juàn*), prepared in deliberate refutation of the Wáng Yáng-míng *wǎn-nián dìng-lùn* 晚年定論 thesis. His *Bái-tián cǎo-táng cún-gǎo* 白田草堂存稿 (literary collection) survives separately. The *Sì-kù* editors place his *Zhū-zǐ niánpǔ* at the head of the Qīng-period *zhuàn-jì·míng-rén* class.
