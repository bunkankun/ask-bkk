**Shěn Zǐ** 沈梓 (明). Mid-Míng figure, son of [[沈啟原]]; associated with the Wújùn 吳郡 circle around [[薛己]] and the posthumous publication of [[KR3ek038]] *Lìyáng jīyào*.
