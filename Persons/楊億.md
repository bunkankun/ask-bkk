Northern-Sòng *Hànlín xuéshì* and central figure of the *XīKūn tǐ* 西崑體, *zì* Dànián 大年, of *Pǔchéng* 浦城 (Fújiàn). A child prodigy: summoned to court at age 11 by Sòng Tàizōng and granted *jìnshì* status; entered *Hànlín* in *Jǐngdé* 3 / 1006. Co-compiler (with Wáng Qīnruò 王欽若 and others) of the encyclopedic *Cèfǔ yuánguī* 冊府元龜 of 1005–1013; his work on this project produced the famous *XīKūn chóuchàng jí* 西崑酬唱集 of mutually-imitative *Lǐ Shāngyǐn*-style verse with Liú Yún 劉筠 and Qián Wéiyǎn 錢惟演 — the school that defined early-Sòng court ornamental poetry. *Sòngshǐ* records seven of his collections (*Kuòcāng*, *Wǔyí*, *Yǐngyīn*, *Hánchéng*, *Tuìjū*, *Rǔyáng*, *Péngshān*, *Guànáo*) plus *Nèiwàizhì* and *Dāobǐ*; only the *Wǔyí xīn jí* [[KR4d0012]] survives substantially. Author also of *Lìdài quánzhèng yào lüè* 歷代銓政要略. The *Sìkù* tíyào defends him against Shí Jiè's [[石介]] *Guài jì* polemic and against Wú Zhīzhèn's later *Sòng shī chāo* exclusion.
