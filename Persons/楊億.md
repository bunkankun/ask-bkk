Northern-Sòng *Hàn-lín xué-shì* and central figure of the *Xī-Kūn tǐ* 西崑體, *zì* Dà-nián 大年, of *Pǔ-chéng* 浦城 (Fú-jiàn). A child prodigy: summoned to court at age 11 by Sòng Tài-zōng and granted *jìn-shì* status; entered *Hàn-lín* in *Jǐng-dé* 3 / 1006. Co-compiler (with Wáng Qīn-ruò 王欽若 and others) of the encyclopedic *Cè-fǔ yuán-guī* 冊府元龜 of 1005–1013; his work on this project produced the famous *Xī-Kūn chóu-chàng jí* 西崑酬唱集 of mutually-imitative *Lǐ Shāng-yǐn*-style verse with Liú Yún 劉筠 and Qián Wéi-yǎn 錢惟演 — the school that defined early-Sòng court ornamental poetry. *Sòng-shǐ* records seven of his collections (*Kuò-cāng*, *Wǔ-yí*, *Yǐng-yīn*, *Hán-chéng*, *Tuì-jū*, *Rǔ-yáng*, *Péng-shān*, *Guàn-áo*) plus *Nèi-wài-zhì* and *Dāo-bǐ*; only the *Wǔ-yí xīn jí* [[KR4d0012]] survives substantially. Author also of *Lì-dài quán-zhèng yào lüè* 歷代銓政要略. The *Sìkù* tíyào defends him against Shí Jiè's [[石介]] *Guài jì* polemic and against Wú Zhī-zhèn's later *Sòng shī chāo* exclusion.
