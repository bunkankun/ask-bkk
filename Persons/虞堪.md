Yuán-Míng transition Cháng-zhōu (Sū-zhōu) recluse-scholar; grand-nephew of **Yú Jí** [[虞集]] (1272–1348). *Zì* **Kè-yòng** 克用; alternate *zì* **Shèng-bó** 勝伯. CBDB 34423 records *fl. lat.* 1383.

**Career.** Retired-in-recluse at Cháng-zhōu (Sū-zhōu). In Míng Hóng-wǔ-mid once held office as ***Yún-nán-fǔ xué jiào-shòu*** 雲南府學教授.

**Scholarly distinction.** Loved learning; possessed literary skill. Composed the *Gǔ-yè gǎo* 鼓枻稿 (separately catalogued in the Sìkù).

**Principal contribution.** Compiler-editor of his grand-uncle Yú Jí's *Dào-yuán yí-gǎo* 道園遺稿 [[KR4d0494]] — the supplementary collection to the *Dào-yuán xué-gǔ lù* [[KR4d0493]]. Gathered 741 *gǔ-lǜ shī* (plus appended *yuè-fǔ*) not preserved elsewhere; cut at Zhì-zhèng 14 (1354).

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0494]] *Dào-yuán yí-gǎo* 道園遺稿 (編).
