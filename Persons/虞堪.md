Yuán-Míng transition Chángzhōu (Sūzhōu) recluse-scholar; grand-nephew of **Yú Jí** [[虞集]] (1272–1348). *Zì* **Kèyòng** 克用; alternate *zì* **Shèngbó** 勝伯. CBDB 34423 records *fl. lat.* 1383.

**Career.** Retired-in-recluse at Chángzhōu (Sūzhōu). In Míng Hóng-wǔ-mid once held office as ***Yúnnánfǔ xué jiàoshòu*** 雲南府學教授.

**Scholarly distinction.** Loved learning; possessed literary skill. Composed the *Gǔyè gǎo* 鼓枻稿 (separately catalogued in the Sìkù).

**Principal contribution.** Compiler-editor of his grand-uncle Yú Jí's *Dàoyuán yígǎo* 道園遺稿 [[KR4d0494]] — the supplementary collection to the *Dàoyuán xuégǔ lù* [[KR4d0493]]. Gathered 741 *gǔlǜ shī* (plus appended *yuèfǔ*) not preserved elsewhere; cut at Zhìzhèng 14 (1354).

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0494]] *Dàoyuán yígǎo* 道園遺稿 (編).
