# Xíngduān 行端

Late-Southern-Sòng / Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Yuánsǒu 元叟, posthumous *shìhào* Jìzhào 寂照 (also self-style Hánshānzǐ 寒山子). 48th abbot of Jìngshān Xīngshèng Wànshòu chánsì 徑山興聖萬壽禪寺 (from Zhìzhì 2 / 1322 until his death in 1341, thirty years). 大鑑下第 20 世 dharma-heir of Cángsǒu Shànzhēn 藏叟善珍 (Xuěfēng Zhēn 雪峰珍, 1194–1277).

Born on the 16th of the 2nd month of Bǎo-yòu 3 (1 April 1255, "one day after the Buddha's *nirvāṇa*"); died on the 4th of the 8th month of Zhì-zhèng 1 (23 September 1341), *shì-shòu* 88, *sēng-là* 76. Native of Línghǎi 臨海 (Tái-zhōu), lay surname Hé 何. Tonsured at twelve at Yú-háng Huà-chéng-yuàn 餘杭化城院 by his paternal uncle Mào Shàng-rén 茂上人 (also eventual guardian of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù); ordained at eighteen. First awakening under Cáng-sǒu at Jìng-shān; further study at Jìng-cí under Shí-lín Gǒng 石林鞏 (who became his close associate), then at Chéng-tiān under Jué-ān Zhēn 覺菴真, and at Yǎng-shān under Xuě-yán Zǔ-qīn 雪巖祖欽. Served as *dì-yī zuò* 第一座 under [[淨伏]] Hǔ-yán Jìng-fú at Jìng-shān before his first abbacy.

Abbacies in sequence: Húzhōu Xiángfèngshān Zīfú 翔鳳山資福 (from Dàdé 4 / 1300), Hángzhōu Zhōngtiānzhú Wànshòu, Hángzhōu Língyǐn Jǐngdé (from Huángqìng 1 / 1312), and Jìngshān (from Zhìzhì 2 / 1322). Received three imperial titles: **Huìwén Zhèngbiàn chánshī** 慧文正辯禪師 in Dàdé 7 (1303), **Fórì Pǔzhào** 佛日普照 in 1312 (after presiding over the Jīnshān *shuǐlù* 水陸 assembly by imperial order), and a third robe-gift; "in all he was bestowed the *jīnlán jiāshā* 金襴袈裟 three times" (*tǎmíng*).

Major dharma-heirs — at least thirteen named — include [[法林]] Zhúquán Fǎlín (1284–1355), [[曇噩]] Mèngtáng Tánè (1285–1373, author of *Xīnxiū kēfēn liùxué sēngzhuàn*), [[祖銘]] Gǔdǐng Zǔmíng (1280–1358, his successor as 49th Jìngshān abbot), [[梵琦]] Chǔshí Fànqí (1296–1370, [[KR6q0353]]), Língyǐn Huìmíng 靈隱慧明, Jìngshān Zhìjí 徑山智及, Xíngzhōng Zhìrén 行中至仁, Fúbào 福報, Yuánjìng 原瀞, Qīngliáng Zǐpián 清涼子楩, Jīnshān Xìngyuán Huìmíng 金山性原慧明, and Tiānníng Zǔchǎn 天寧祖闡 (who jointly commissioned the 1374 re-cutting of the *yǔlù*).

Principal works: *Yuánsǒu Xíngduān chánshī yǔlù* 元叟行端禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0352]]; X71 n1419). Biographical sources: *tǎmíng* by Huáng Jìn 黃溍 (Huáng Wénxiàn 黃文獻), *xùxù* by Sòng Lián 宋濂 (1374); *Bǔxù gāosēng zhuàn* *juan* 12; *Zēngjí Xùchuándēnglù* *juan* 5; *Wǔdēng huìyuán xùlüè* *juan* 1; *Jìngshān zhì*.
