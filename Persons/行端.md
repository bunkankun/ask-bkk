# Xíng-duān 行端

Late-Southern-Sòng / Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Yuán-sǒu 元叟, posthumous *shì-hào* Jì-zhào 寂照 (also self-style Hán-shān-zǐ 寒山子). 48th abbot of Jìng-shān Xīng-shèng Wàn-shòu chán-sì 徑山興聖萬壽禪寺 (from Zhì-zhì 2 / 1322 until his death in 1341, thirty years). 大鑑下第 20 世 dharma-heir of Cáng-sǒu Shàn-zhēn 藏叟善珍 (Xuě-fēng Zhēn 雪峰珍, 1194–1277).

Born on the 16th of the 2nd month of Bǎo-yòu 3 (1 April 1255, "one day after the Buddha's *nirvāṇa*"); died on the 4th of the 8th month of Zhì-zhèng 1 (23 September 1341), *shì-shòu* 88, *sēng-là* 76. Native of Línghǎi 臨海 (Tái-zhōu), lay surname Hé 何. Tonsured at twelve at Yú-háng Huà-chéng-yuàn 餘杭化城院 by his paternal uncle Mào Shàng-rén 茂上人 (also eventual guardian of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù); ordained at eighteen. First awakening under Cáng-sǒu at Jìng-shān; further study at Jìng-cí under Shí-lín Gǒng 石林鞏 (who became his close associate), then at Chéng-tiān under Jué-ān Zhēn 覺菴真, and at Yǎng-shān under Xuě-yán Zǔ-qīn 雪巖祖欽. Served as *dì-yī zuò* 第一座 under [[淨伏]] Hǔ-yán Jìng-fú at Jìng-shān before his first abbacy.

Abbacies in sequence: Hú-zhōu Xiáng-fèng-shān Zī-fú 翔鳳山資福 (from Dà-dé 4 / 1300), Hángzhōu Zhōng-tiān-zhú Wàn-shòu, Hángzhōu Líng-yǐn Jǐng-dé (from Huáng-qìng 1 / 1312), and Jìng-shān (from Zhì-zhì 2 / 1322). Received three imperial titles: **Huì-wén Zhèng-biàn chánshī** 慧文正辯禪師 in Dà-dé 7 (1303), **Fó-rì Pǔ-zhào** 佛日普照 in 1312 (after presiding over the Jīn-shān *shuǐ-lù* 水陸 assembly by imperial order), and a third robe-gift; "in all he was bestowed the *jīn-lán jiā-shā* 金襴袈裟 three times" (*tǎ-míng*).

Major dharma-heirs — at least thirteen named — include [[法林]] Zhú-quán Fǎ-lín (1284–1355), [[曇噩]] Mèng-táng Tán-è (1285–1373, author of *Xīn-xiū kē-fēn liù-xué sēng-zhuàn*), [[祖銘]] Gǔ-dǐng Zǔ-míng (1280–1358, his successor as 49th Jìng-shān abbot), [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí (1296–1370, [[KR6q0353]]), Líng-yǐn Huì-míng 靈隱慧明, Jìng-shān Zhì-jí 徑山智及, Xíng-zhōng Zhì-rén 行中至仁, Fú-bào 福報, Yuán-jìng 原瀞, Qīng-liáng Zǐ-pián 清涼子楩, Jīn-shān Xìng-yuán Huì-míng 金山性原慧明, and Tiān-níng Zǔ-chǎn 天寧祖闡 (who jointly commissioned the 1374 re-cutting of the *yǔlù*).

Principal works: *Yuán-sǒu Xíng-duān chánshī yǔlù* 元叟行端禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0352]]; X71 n1419). Biographical sources: *tǎ-míng* by Huáng Jìn 黃溍 (Huáng Wén-xiàn 黃文獻), *xù-xù* by Sòng Lián 宋濂 (1374); *Bǔ-xù gāo-sēng zhuàn* *juan* 12; *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 5; *Wǔ-dēng huì-yuán xù-lüè* *juan* 1; *Jìng-shān zhì*.
