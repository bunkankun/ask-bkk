Japanese Tendai 天台 monk; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Provided the *fēn-huì* 分會 ("division-and-combination" / structural-analysis-and-synthesis) editorial work on the canonical recension of **Shén-zhào Běn-rú**'s *Pǔ-xián guān jīng yì-shū* ([[KR6d0123]], X35n0650), with **Chǔ-xián**'s *xù-jiě* supplementary commentary integrated into the text. His Japanese affiliation indicates that the work survived through Japanese Tendai library transmission, from which it was eventually reprinted in the Manji-zoku canonical apparatus.

Source: *Pǔ-xián guān jīng yì-shū* (X35n0650).
