# 李梴

Lǐ Chān, late-Míng physician of Jiāngxī; author of the comprehensive medical primer *Yīxué rùmén* 醫學入門 (1575), cited in [[KR3em033]] *Hóukē dàchéng* 喉科大成 alongside the other standard Sòng–Yuán–Míng medical sources.
