Mid-Qiánlóng *Lǐbù* official; co-director of compilation for the *Qīndìng Dà Qīng Tōnglǐ* 欽定大清通禮 ([[KR2m0035]]) commissioned in 1736 and completed in 1756. CBDB lists the entry without dates. Precise biographical information is not transmitted in surviving sources beyond his role as co-editor with Lái-bǎo on the imperial ritual code. Active mid-eighteenth century.
