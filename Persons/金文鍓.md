**Jīn Wénzhōng** 金文鍓 (*zì* Yìshēng 繹聲), late-Qīng / early-Republican Yuánhé 元和 (Sūzhōu) physician who wrote the 1910 (Xuāntǒng 2) preface to the Shànghǎi lithographic edition of [[KR3eq006]] *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* prepared at the Shànghǎi Medical Research Institute.
