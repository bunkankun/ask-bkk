Northern-Sòng official, *zì* Dūnlǐ 敦禮, native of Shàowǔ 邵武 (Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of 1070; rose to *Cháosàn dàifu* 朝散大夫 and *Mìshū jiānjiào* 祕書監校 offices in the Yuányòu / Shàoshèng eras. He composed *Chén Xiāng xíngzhuàng* 陳襄行狀 (memorial-deeds biography of [[陳襄]] Chén Xiāng), which preserves the only documentary reference to [[倪天隱]] Ní Tiānyǐn's marriage to one of Chén Xiāng's sisters.
