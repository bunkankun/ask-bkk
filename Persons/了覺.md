# Liǎojué 了覺

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*ménrén* 門人 / *shìzhě* 侍者) of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249) and, after Wújùn's death, of [[法薰]] Shítián Fǎxūn (1171–1245); co-editor of Wújùn's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0315]]) and — together with [[師坦]] — principal named editor (*děng biān* 等編) of the *Shítián Fǎxūn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0319]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000041).
