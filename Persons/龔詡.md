Gōng Xǔ 龔詡 (1382–1469), *zì* **Dà-zhāng** 大章, *hào* **Yě-gǔ** 野古 (his self-deprecating studio name combining *yě* "rustic" and *gǔ* "antique-and-upright"). Native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). His father **Gōng Chá** 詧, *jǐ-shì-zhōng* under Hóngwǔ, was banished by *yán-shì* censure to garrison *Wǔ-kāi-wèi* 五開衛, and Gōng Xǔ was therefore enrolled in the military registry and stationed as a guard at the **Jīn-chuān-mén** 金川門 of Nán-jīng — the very gate at which the Jiàn-wén regime fell when the Yān army arrived (1402). On the Yǒng-lè usurpation, Gōng Xǔ changed his name and surname, fled, and lived out 67 years selling drugs and teaching pupils in the Sū-zhōu / Sōng-jiāng area. Twice in the late 1430s recommended by **Zhōu Chén** 周忱 (the great Jiāng-nán *xún-fǔ*) for *xué-guān* posts at *Sōng-jiāng* and *Tài-cāng*; refused both, telling Censor-in-Chief **Wú Nè** 吳訥: *"Office in itself does no harm to righteousness — but I fear betraying that one weeping at the city-gate."* Died Chéng-huà *jǐ-chǒu* (1469) at age 88. *Míng shǐ* j. 143 appended to the **Niú Jǐng-xiān** 牛景先 biography. Surviving works are [[KR4e0080]] *Yě-gǔ jí*. CBDB id 34464 (1382–1469); the catalog meta's death-year 1467 is a slip — the tíyào's *jǐ-chǒu* (Chéng-huà 5 = 1469) and the *aged 88* figure agree on 1469.
