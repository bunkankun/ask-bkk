Gōng Xǔ 龔詡 (1382–1469), *zì* **Dàzhāng** 大章, *hào* **Yěgǔ** 野古 (his self-deprecating studio name combining *yě* "rustic" and *gǔ* "antique-and-upright"). Native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). His father **Gōng Chá** 詧, *jǐshìzhōng* under Hóngwǔ, was banished by *yánshì* censure to garrison *Wǔkāiwèi* 五開衛, and Gōng Xǔ was therefore enrolled in the military registry and stationed as a guard at the **Jīnchuānmén** 金川門 of Nánjīng — the very gate at which the Jiànwén regime fell when the Yān army arrived (1402). On the Yǒnglè usurpation, Gōng Xǔ changed his name and surname, fled, and lived out 67 years selling drugs and teaching pupils in the Sūzhōu / Sōngjiāng area. Twice in the late 1430s recommended by **Zhōu Chén** 周忱 (the great Jiāngnán *xúnfǔ*) for *xuéguān* posts at *Sōngjiāng* and *Tàicāng*; refused both, telling Censor-in-Chief **Wú Nè** 吳訥: *"Office in itself does no harm to righteousness — but I fear betraying that one weeping at the city-gate."* Died Chénghuà *jǐchǒu* (1469) at age 88. *Míng shǐ* j. 143 appended to the **Niú Jǐngxiān** 牛景先 biography. Surviving works are [[KR4e0080]] *Yěgǔ jí*. CBDB id 34464 (1382–1469); the catalog meta's death-year 1467 is a slip — the tíyào's *jǐchǒu* (Chénghuà 5 = 1469) and the *aged 88* figure agree on 1469.
