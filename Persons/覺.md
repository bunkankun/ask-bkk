# Jué 覺 (Zì-xián Xíng-jué 自閒行覺)

DILA-standardized **Xíng-jué** 行覺; *zì* **Zì-xián** 自閒 ("Self-Leisured"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) at **Tōng-xuán-sì** 通玄寺. Born **萬曆庚戌 8/7** = **1610-09-23 Gregorian**; died **辛丑 12/27 午時** = **1662-02-14 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **53**, *sēng-là* **34**. Native of **Hé-zhōu** 合州 (Chóng-qìng, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Yú** 余 (also attested as 徐).

**Formation.** Tonsured at age 8 at **Mǎ-dāng-shān** 馬當山 under **Tài-kōng héshàng** 太空和尚. **乙亥 = 1635**: left Sì-chuān, first met **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] at Tiān-tóng. Decisive *xǐng* on a *lù-zhù* 露柱 collision incident. Final transmission from **Tōng-xuán Lín-lǎo** 通玄林老 = **Lín-yě Tōng-qí**.

**Six abbacies (1648–1656).** **戊子 1648**: Sū-zhōu Huáng-xī Luó-hàn-sì → **己丑 1649**: Chóng-chuān Cí-jì → **庚寅 1650**: Fèng-chuān Fǎ-hǎi → **辛卯 1651**: Fèng-chuān Yuè-lín → **甲午 1654**: Jiā-xīng Lóng-yuān Jǐng-dé → **丙申 1656**: Jīng-xī Fú-róng-sì 芙蓉禪寺 (death-seat). Thirty-three named *fǎ-sì* heirs including **Cí-lǎng Jiù** 慈朗鷲, **Hàn-guān Yù** 漢關喻, **Tiān-mù Zhì** 天目智, **Hān-yú Xiān** [[洪暹]] 憨予暹 (the principal compiler of [[KR6q0469]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0469]] 《自閒覺禪師語錄》 8 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B287, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hān-yú Xiān** [[洪暹]] *děng*. Sources: [[KR6q0469]] juan 5 *xíng-shí* + juan 8 *xíng-zhuàng*; 《五燈全書》 j. 98.
