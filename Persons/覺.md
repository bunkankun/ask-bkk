# Jué 覺 (Zìxián Xíngjué 自閒行覺)

DILA-standardized **Xíngjué** 行覺; *zì* **Zìxián** 自閒 ("Self-Leisured"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) at **Tōngxuánsì** 通玄寺. Born **萬曆庚戌 8/7** = **1610-09-23 Gregorian**; died **辛丑 12/27 午時** = **1662-02-14 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **53**, *sēnglà* **34**. Native of **Hézhōu** 合州 (Chóngqìng, Sìchuān); lay surname **Yú** 余 (also attested as 徐).

**Formation.** Tonsured at age 8 at **Mǎdāngshān** 馬當山 under **Tàikōng héshàng** 太空和尚. **乙亥 = 1635**: left Sìchuān, first met **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] at Tiāntóng. Decisive *xǐng* on a *lùzhù* 露柱 collision incident. Final transmission from **Tōngxuán Línlǎo** 通玄林老 = **Línyě Tōngqí**.

**Six abbacies (1648–1656).** **戊子 1648**: Sūzhōu Huángxī Luóhànsì → **己丑 1649**: Chóngchuān Cíjì → **庚寅 1650**: Fèngchuān Fǎhǎi → **辛卯 1651**: Fèngchuān Yuèlín → **甲午 1654**: Jiāxīng Lóngyuān Jǐngdé → **丙申 1656**: Jīngxī Fúróngsì 芙蓉禪寺 (death-seat). Thirty-three named *fǎsì* heirs including **Cílǎng Jiù** 慈朗鷲, **Hànguān Yù** 漢關喻, **Tiānmù Zhì** 天目智, **Hānyú Xiān** [[洪暹]] 憨予暹 (the principal compiler of [[KR6q0469]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0469]] 《自閒覺禪師語錄》 8 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B287, compiled by *fǎsì* **Hānyú Xiān** [[洪暹]] *děng*. Sources: [[KR6q0469]] juan 5 *xíngshí* + juan 8 *xíngzhuàng*; 《五燈全書》 j. 98.
