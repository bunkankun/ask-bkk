Wáng Jūnhuì 王君慧, modern PRC editor / collator (*diǎnjiào* 點校). Named on the *hxwd* (*Hǎiwài huíliú zhōngyī gǔjí cóngshū*) edition of [[KR3eq081]] *Shìè yīhuà* 士諤醫話 as the punctuator-collator of Lù Shìè's Republican-period medical talks. No standard biographical entry located.
