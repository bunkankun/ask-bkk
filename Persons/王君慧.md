Wáng Jūn-huì 王君慧, modern PRC editor / collator (*diǎn-jiào* 點校). Named on the *hxwd* (*Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī gǔ-jí cóng-shū*) edition of [[KR3eq081]] *Shì-è yī-huà* 士諤醫話 as the punctuator-collator of Lù Shì-è's Republican-period medical talks. No standard biographical entry located.
