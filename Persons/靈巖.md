Early-Heian Japanese Shingon monk (Jp. *Reigan-oshō*) known principally as the teacher of [[南忠]] Nánzhōng (Nanchū). Travelled to Tang China in the first half of the ninth century to receive instruction in the **大佛頂** dhāraṇī apparatus and brought back to Japan its ritual codification 儀軌. The transmission underlies Nanchū's 847 commentary *Zhù Dàfódǐng zhēnyán* ([[KR6j0182]]).
