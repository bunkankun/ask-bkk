**Zhāng Fèngkuí** 張鳳逵 (= **Zhāng Hèténg** 張鶴騰, *zì* Yuánhàn 元漢, *hào* Fèngkuí 鳳逵; ?–1635, 明). Late-Míng official and physician of Yǐngzhōu 潁州 in Fèngyáng prefecture (modern Fùnán 阜南 county, Ānhuī). Younger brother of **Zhāng Hèmíng** 張鶴鳴 (Tiān-qǐ-era 兵部尚書).

A *jǔrén* of Wànlì 19 (1591, ranked 6th in the Hénán provincial exam) and a *jìnshì* of Wànlì 23 (1595). Served as observer at the Court of Judicial Review, then magistrate of Lùchéng 潞城 (Shānxī, 1596) and Yúcì 榆次 (Tàiyuán prefecture, 1597); rose through the Ministries of Justice and Revenue; was 廣西 provincial chief examiner in 1606; later 雲南 Vice Surveillance Commissioner. Resigned for eye disease and retired to Yǐngzhōu. In Chóngzhēn 8 (1635) Lǐ Zìchéng's army captured Yǐngzhōu; Zhāng was seized and **died cursing his captors** — biography appended to that of Zhāng Hèmíng in the *Míng shǐ* 明史.

Skilled in medicine; author of **《傷暑全書》** *Shāngshǔ quánshū* (2 *juǎn*, completed Tiānqǐ 3 = 1623) — see [[KR3eg005]] for the later expanded recension with Yè Tiānshì's annotations. Also wrote 《傷寒全書》 *Shānghán quánshū* (2 *juǎn*).

The biographical reconstruction here follows the standard modern accounts (*Míng shǐ* 明史; *Zhōng yī rén wù cí diǎn*); birth year is unknown but his 1595 *jìnshì* implies a birth date in the 1560s–1570s.
