**Zhāng Fèng-kuí** 張鳳逵 (= **Zhāng Hè-téng** 張鶴騰, *zì* Yuán-hàn 元漢, *hào* Fèng-kuí 鳳逵; ?–1635, 明). Late-Míng official and physician of Yǐng-zhōu 潁州 in Fèng-yáng prefecture (modern Fù-nán 阜南 county, Ān-huī). Younger brother of **Zhāng Hè-míng** 張鶴鳴 (Tiān-qǐ-era 兵部尚書).

A *jǔ-rén* of Wàn-lì 19 (1591, ranked 6th in the Hé-nán provincial exam) and a *jìn-shì* of Wàn-lì 23 (1595). Served as observer at the Court of Judicial Review, then magistrate of Lù-chéng 潞城 (Shānxī, 1596) and Yú-cì 榆次 (Tài-yuán prefecture, 1597); rose through the Ministries of Justice and Revenue; was 廣西 provincial chief examiner in 1606; later 雲南 Vice Surveillance Commissioner. Resigned for eye disease and retired to Yǐng-zhōu. In Chóng-zhēn 8 (1635) Lǐ Zì-chéng's army captured Yǐng-zhōu; Zhāng was seized and **died cursing his captors** — biography appended to that of Zhāng Hè-míng in the *Míng shǐ* 明史.

Skilled in medicine; author of **《傷暑全書》** *Shāng-shǔ quán-shū* (2 *juǎn*, completed Tiān-qǐ 3 = 1623) — see [[KR3eg005]] for the later expanded recension with Yè Tiānshì's annotations. Also wrote 《傷寒全書》 *Shāng-hán quán-shū* (2 *juǎn*).

The biographical reconstruction here follows the standard modern accounts (*Míng shǐ* 明史; *Zhōng yī rén wù cí diǎn*); birth year is unknown but his 1595 *jìn-shì* implies a birth date in the 1560s–1570s.
