Yáng Yàn-líng 楊彥齡 (*fl. c.* 1085–1100, lifedates and place of origin unknown). A late-Northern-Sòng scholar-official whose career-positions are reconstructible only from internal references in his *Yáng-gōng bǐ lù* 楊公筆錄 ([[KR3j0098]]): magistrate of Shān-yīn 山陰 in *Yuán-fēng* (1078–1085); *sī-hù* (registrar) of Xí-zhōu 隰州; magistrate of Fǔ-yáng 滏陽 in *Yuán-fēng* 8 (1085); vice-prefect of Guó-zhōu 虢州; magistrate of Wǔ-jìn 武進 in Cháng-zhōu 常州. Retired with *Cháo-fèng láng* 朝奉郎 rank. Personally connected with Chéng Yí 程頤 (visited at Luò-yáng), the Wáng Ān-shí circle, and Lù Diàn 陸佃 — placing him at the intersection of multiple late-Northern-Sòng intellectual circles.
