Yáng Yànlíng 楊彥齡 (*fl. c.* 1085–1100, lifedates and place of origin unknown). A late-Northern-Sòng scholar-official whose career-positions are reconstructible only from internal references in his *Yánggōng bǐ lù* 楊公筆錄 ([[KR3j0098]]): magistrate of Shānyīn 山陰 in *Yuánfēng* (1078–1085); *sīhù* (registrar) of Xízhōu 隰州; magistrate of Fǔyáng 滏陽 in *Yuánfēng* 8 (1085); vice-prefect of Guózhōu 虢州; magistrate of Wǔjìn 武進 in Chángzhōu 常州. Retired with *Cháofèng láng* 朝奉郎 rank. Personally connected with Chéng Yí 程頤 (visited at Luòyáng), the Wáng Ānshí circle, and Lù Diàn 陸佃 — placing him at the intersection of multiple late-Northern-Sòng intellectual circles.
