# Cài Jǐngjūn 蔡景君

Pre-Táng *Yì* scholar of obscure dates; preserved only through citations in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). Identified by [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考 as one of the three masters supplementing Zhū Mùwēng's count, alongside [[姚規]] Yáo Guī and [[朱仰之]] Zhū Yǎngzhī.
