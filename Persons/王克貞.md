Wáng Kèzhēn 王克貞 (930–989) was a Northern-Sòng official of the Tàizǔ and Tàizōng reigns. A *jìnshì* graduate of the early Sòng, he served in the Hànlín drafting establishment and on the editorial commission for [[KR3l0118]] *Tàipíng guǎngjì* (978) and the *Tàipíng yù lǎn* ([[KR4d0080]], 983) under Lǐ Fǎng 李昉's directorship.
