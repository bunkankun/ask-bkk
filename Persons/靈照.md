# Língzhào 靈照

Daughter of the celebrated Táng lay Chán practitioner [[龐蘊|Páng Yùn]] 龐蘊 (Páng jūshì 龐居士). Lived with her family the bamboo-basket weaving and serious Chán-practice life; the dialogue surrounding her death has become one of the most beloved stories in Chán literature.
