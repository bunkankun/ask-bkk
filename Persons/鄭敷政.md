**Zhèng Fūzhèng** 鄭敷政 (*hào* Héyáng 和陽, *fl.* 1569 明), mid-Míng physician of *Píngqiáo Kūnshān* 崑山平橋 (Jiāngsū), marriage-heir to the Xuē 薛 family medical lineage of Kūnshān. Editor and 1569 prefacer of the [[KR3ei033]] *Xuēshì jìyīn wànjīn shū* 薛氏濟陰萬金書, the gynecological compilation of his wife's family. Zhèng's preface (dated *Lóngqìng* 3 = 1569) explicitly identified certain prescriptions as secret family-transmitted formulae and enjoined his descendants not to share them — an injunction overturned by an anonymous 1701 (*Kāngxī* 40) editor who arranged the work for general printing.
