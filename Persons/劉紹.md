Late-Yuán Jiāng-xī literatus of **Lí-chuān** 黎川 (Jiāng-xī); *zì* **Zǐ-xiàn** 子憲. *Yīn-jiā* (in-law) of [[胡布]]; once served in the **Rǔ-nán-wáng** 汝南王 *mù* (staff). Hú Bù records that the two of them both served as advisers in the **Fú-jiàn shuài-mù** at the end of the Yuán without realising their ambitions. His verse is co-anthologised with that of [[張達]] in the final 2 *juǎn* of **[[KR4h0089]]** *Yuán-yīn yí-xiǎng*. The SKQS editors group him as a Yuán *yí-lǎo*. No CBDB entry confidently matched. Note: distinct from the much later Yuán-Míng man **Liú Shào 劉昭** with similar name.
