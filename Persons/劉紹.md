Late-Yuán Jiāngxī literatus of **Líchuān** 黎川 (Jiāngxī); *zì* **Zǐxiàn** 子憲. *Yīnjiā* (in-law) of [[胡布]]; once served in the **Rǔnánwáng** 汝南王 *mù* (staff). Hú Bù records that the two of them both served as advisers in the **Fújiàn shuàimù** at the end of the Yuán without realising their ambitions. His verse is co-anthologised with that of [[張達]] in the final 2 *juǎn* of **[[KR4h0089]]** *Yuányīn yíxiǎng*. The SKQS editors group him as a Yuán *yílǎo*. No CBDB entry confidently matched. Note: distinct from the much later Yuán-Míng man **Liú Shào 劉昭** with similar name.
