**Mǎ Shī** 馬蒔 (*fl. c.* 1568–1620, 明), *zì* Xuántái 玄臺 (also Zhònghuà 仲化), was a late-Wànlì physician of Kuàijī 會稽 (modern Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng) and the principal late-Míng exegete of the foundational medical canon. His three major commentaries —

- *Huángdì nèijīng Sùwèn zhù zhèng fā wēi* 黃帝內經素問注證發微, 9 juan (the *Sùwèn* commentary, c. 1580).
- *Huángdì nèijīng Língshū zhù zhèng fā wēi* 黃帝內經靈樞注證發微, 9 juan, c. 1586 ([[KR3ea035]]).
- *Nán jīng zhèng yì* 難經正義 (a *Nán jīng* commentary, completed shortly after the *Língshū*).

— were composed over a single decade of intense work and established Mǎ as the dominant *Nèijīng* commentator of the late Míng. His *Língshū zhù zhèng fā wēi* is the **first comprehensive systematic commentary on the *Língshū*** (Wáng Bīng having commented only the *Sùwèn*), and remained the dominant *Língshū* commentary until displaced by Zhāng Zhìcōng's *Jí zhù* ([[KR3ea025]]) a century later. His clinical orientation is broadly Dānxī-school. His lifedates are not precisely known; the *Sìkù tíyào* on the *Sùwèn fā wēi* gives him as *Wànlì zhōng rén* (a person of the mid-Wànlì period).
