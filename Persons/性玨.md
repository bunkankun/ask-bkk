Xìng-jué 性玨, *zì* **Zhuó-fēng** 卓峰 ("Standing Peak", post-*fù-fǎ* name), early style **Líng-yī** 靈一 ("Sole Numinous"); often referred to as **Zhuó-fēng Jué** 卓峰玨. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] (1611–1680) in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line — see [[KR6q0554]]. Born **明天啟乙丑七月十九日丑時** = **1625-08-21** Gregorian; died **乙丑秋** = **1685** autumn (DILA narrow range: 1685-02-03 to 1686-01-23), age 61. Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Chéng** 成.

**Formation.** At 15 took tonsure under **Xiào-sōng** 笑松, then studied scripture and Vinaya under **Sōng-yùn** 松蘊教典師. Hearing of **Héng-shān** 衡老人's reputation, traveled to *guī-lǐ* (formally take refuge); the master "valued him as a vessel" and gave him the early name *Líng-yī*. Trained twenty-some years and received final *fù-fǎ*, on which occasion the name *Zhuó-fēng* was bestowed.

**Abbacy.** **Zhōng-zhōu Qìng-yún chán-sì** 忠州慶雲禪寺 (the same seat his teacher Dēng-bǐng had held).

**Disciple.** **Zōng-wèi** [[宗位]] (sole *fǎ-sì*; lead compiler of multiple J39 *yǔ-lù* — both [[KR6q0554]] and [[KR6q0559]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0559]] 《卓峰玨禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B444, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zōng-wèi** [[宗位]] *biān*. Two prefaces: by **Chāo-yuán Dàn-yá** [[超源]] (Mǐn-pǔ Lóng-chí line) dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; by **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] (1649–1720) dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1.

**Sources.** 《卓峰玨禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈塔銘〉; 《五燈全書》.
