Xìngjué 性玨, *zì* **Zhuófēng** 卓峰 ("Standing Peak", post-*fùfǎ* name), early style **Língyī** 靈一 ("Sole Numinous"); often referred to as **Zhuófēng Jué** 卓峰玨. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Héngshān Dēngbǐng** [[燈炳]] (1611–1680) in the Sìchuān **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line — see [[KR6q0554]]. Born **明天啟乙丑七月十九日丑時** = **1625-08-21** Gregorian; died **乙丑秋** = **1685** autumn (DILA narrow range: 1685-02-03 to 1686-01-23), age 61. Native of **Zhōngzhōu** 忠州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Chéng** 成.

**Formation.** At 15 took tonsure under **Xiàosōng** 笑松, then studied scripture and Vinaya under **Sōngyùn** 松蘊教典師. Hearing of **Héngshān** 衡老人's reputation, traveled to *guīlǐ* (formally take refuge); the master "valued him as a vessel" and gave him the early name *Língyī*. Trained twenty-some years and received final *fùfǎ*, on which occasion the name *Zhuófēng* was bestowed.

**Abbacy.** **Zhōngzhōu Qìngyún chánsì** 忠州慶雲禪寺 (the same seat his teacher Dēngbǐng had held).

**Disciple.** **Zōngwèi** [[宗位]] (sole *fǎsì*; lead compiler of multiple J39 *yǔlù* — both [[KR6q0554]] and [[KR6q0559]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0559]] 《卓峰玨禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B444, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zōngwèi** [[宗位]] *biān*. Two prefaces: by **Chāoyuán Dànyá** [[超源]] (Mǐnpǔ Lóngchí line) dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; by **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] (1649–1720) dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1.

**Sources.** 《卓峰玨禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈塔銘〉; 《五燈全書》.
