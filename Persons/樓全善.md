# 樓全善 Lóu Quánshàn

Late-Yuán / early-Míng physician (Lóu Yīng 樓英, *zì* Quánshàn 全善, ca. 1320–1389), author of the *Yīxué gāngmù* 醫學綱目. Cited in [[李彣]] Lǐ Wén's *Jīnguì yàoluè guǎngzhù* ([[KR3ef087]]).
