# Gù Kuā 顧夸

*Zì* Yuèzhī 悅之, by which name he is also cited (顧悅之 Gù Yuèzhī) — the *Sìkù* *tiyao* of [[KR1a0007]] gives both forms with a note that the second is his *zì*. Eastern Jìn 東晉 official, prefect (*nèishǐ* 內史) of Yangzhou (according to the *Suíshū jīngjí zhì*). Author of a *Zhōuyì nàn Wáng Fǔsì yì* 周易難王輔嗣義 ("Refutation of Wáng Fǔsì's *Yì* meanings") in one *juàn* — recorded by *Suíshū jīngjí zhì*, and reported in the *Cèfǔ yuánguī* 冊府元龜 as comprising "over forty articles" of objections to [[王弼]] Wáng Bì's *Yì*. The work is lost; the rebuttal in turn drew a counter-refutation from [[閔康之]] Mǐn Kāngzhī.
