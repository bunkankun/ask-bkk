# Wén-zhì 文智

Northern-Sòng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[承古]] Jiàn-fú Chéng-gǔ (d. 1045); styled *Yù-zhāng cān-xué mén-rén* 豫章參學門人 in the byline. Editor (*biān* 編) of his master's single-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0377]]; X73 n1447). Lifedates unrecorded.
