# Xiān-dǔ 先覩

Yuán-dynasty Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Wú-jiàn 無見 ("Without-Seeing"); also Zhēn-jué 真覺, Wú-jiàn-dǐng 無見頂, and the self-deprecating *Xiā-lǘ* 瞎驢 ("Blind Donkey"). Per DILA: born Xiánchún 1.5.6 (29 May 1265), died Yuán-tǒng 2.5.15 (24 June 1334), *shì* 70. Native of Tiāntái. Dharma-heir of [[文寶]] Fāng-shān Wén-bǎo; dharma-nephew of Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào 高峰原妙. Co-editor of his master Wén-bǎo's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0328]]). Lived forty years in solitary practice on Huá-dǐng-fēng 華頂峯 of Tiāntái without descending, cultivating the *jù-chǎo* hermit pattern shared with Gāo-fēng's "death-barrier" at Tiān-mù-shān. Recorded sayings: *Wú-jiàn Xiān-dǔ chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0329]]), first printed in Yuán Zhì-zhèng 17 (1357) and republished in the Shùnzhì era (1657).
