Hé Qùfēi 何去非 (fl. 1082–1090), *zì* Zhèngtōng 正通, was a Northern Sòng *bīng-jiā* writer of Pǔ-chéng 浦城 (Fú-jiàn). Held the title *bóshì* in the *Wǔ-xué* (military-school examinations system established under Wáng Ān-shí) and rose to *Tài-cháng bóshì*. Patron-supported by Sū Shì 蘇軾, who pressed for his promotion. Author of the *Hé bóshì bèi lùn* 何博士備論 (KR3b0014, his historical-strategic essay-collection on military case-studies). His writings were collected by Sū Shì and presented to court.
