**Xiāo Lìzhī** 蕭立之 (b. 1203, alive into the early Yuán; conventional *fl.* 1259), *zì* Sīlì 斯立, *hào* Bīngyá 冰厓 (also written 氷厓), was a late-Sòng poet of Xiāo-tián 蕭田, Níngdū 寧都 county, Jiāngxī. The catalog occasionally writes his name as the truncated 蕭立 — apparently a transcription error in the SBCK title page reading — but CBDB (35544) and the standard reference works (*Sòng-rén zhuàn-jì zī-liào suǒ-yǐn* 5: 4040; *Wǔ-dài-yǐ-lái Yǎn-jiū* 3: 1974) settle on Xiāo Lìzhī. He earned the *jìnshì* in *Bǎoyòu* 7 (1259), per the *Jiāngxī tōngzhì* 江西通志 *xuǎn-jǔ zhì* 選舉志, and rose to assistant prefect (*tōngshǒu* 通守), though he never took up that final assignment because of the political and military turbulence of the late Sòng. He was a poet of the Jiāngxī-school 江西派 inheritance, with close ties to Zhào Fán 趙蕃 (Jiàn-quán 澗泉) and Hán Bū 韓淲 (Zhāng-quán 章泉), and to the *yímín* circles of Xiè Fāngdé 謝枋得 (Dié-shān 疊山) and Wú Chéng 吳澄 (Cǎo-lú 草廬). Luó Lún's *Chénghuà* 8 (1472) preface to the *Bīngyá shī jí shíyí* describes him as one of the foremost southeastern poets of the post-*Nándù* age. His seven-character regulated piece *Dú Wén-shān shī* 讀文山詩 (reading [[文天祥]] Wén Tiānxiáng's poetry) is widely anthologised as a *yímín*-loyalist statement.

His son **Xiāo Shìyún** 蕭士贇 (fl. early Yuán) prepared the *Lǐ Tàibái shī fēnlèi bǔ-zhù* 李太白詩集分類補注 (an extension of Yáng Qíxián 楊齊賢's commentary on Lǐ Bái), preserved in the *Sì-kù quán-shū* (KR4h division); the descendant Xiāo Yífèng 蕭儀鳳 reprinted the family's poetry in 1472, the edition photo-reproduced in the *Sì-bù cóng-kān xù-biān*. Compiled work by Xiāo in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR4d0373]] *Xiāo Bīngyá shī jí shíyí*.
