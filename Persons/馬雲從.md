**Mǎ Yún-cóng** 馬雲從 (*zì* Yún-cóng 雲從; *fl.* late Kāng-xī, c. 1660–1700), Qīng-period physician of Wéi-xī 濰西 (modern Wéi-fāng 濰坊, Shāndōng). According to the prefaces preserved in [[KR3em004]] *Yǎn-kē mì-jué* 眼科秘訣, Mǎ was originally a candidate in the Qīng examination system who had to abandon his examination career because of severe eye disease. He received the manuscript that became *Yǎn-kē mì-jué* (claiming Sūn Sī-miǎo / [[孫思邈]] descent) from the itinerant Jiāng-nán physician Wáng Fù-wàn 王覆萬 — whose source was named as the otherwise unattested *Dà-piáo qī-méi xiān-shēng* 大瓢七眉先生 — and cured himself with it before practising professionally. He was unable to fund the work's printing, which was eventually arranged by his patron [[隋平]] Suí Píng 隋平 (*hào* Kūn-tiě 昆鐵) of Dōng-wǔ, with a prefatory commendation by his contemporary [[李煥章]] Lǐ Huàn-zhāng 李煥章 (1614–). Mǎ Yún-cóng is also the redactor of the companion volume [[KR3em008]] *Yǎn-kē chǎn-wēi* 眼科闡微. CBDB c_personid 472106 (dynasty code 20 = 清, no recorded lifedates).
