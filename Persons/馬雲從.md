**Mǎ Yúncóng** 馬雲從 (*zì* Yúncóng 雲從; *fl.* late Kāngxī, c. 1660–1700), Qīng-period physician of Wéixī 濰西 (modern Wéifāng 濰坊, Shāndōng). According to the prefaces preserved in [[KR3em004]] *Yǎnkē mìjué* 眼科秘訣, Mǎ was originally a candidate in the Qīng examination system who had to abandon his examination career because of severe eye disease. He received the manuscript that became *Yǎnkē mìjué* (claiming Sūn Sīmiǎo / [[孫思邈]] descent) from the itinerant Jiāngnán physician Wáng Fùwàn 王覆萬 — whose source was named as the otherwise unattested *Dàpiáo qīméi xiānshēng* 大瓢七眉先生 — and cured himself with it before practising professionally. He was unable to fund the work's printing, which was eventually arranged by his patron [[隋平]] Suí Píng 隋平 (*hào* Kūntiě 昆鐵) of Dōngwǔ, with a prefatory commendation by his contemporary [[李煥章]] Lǐ Huànzhāng 李煥章 (1614–). Mǎ Yúncóng is also the redactor of the companion volume [[KR3em008]] *Yǎnkē chǎnwēi* 眼科闡微. CBDB c_personid 472106 (dynasty code 20 = 清, no recorded lifedates).
