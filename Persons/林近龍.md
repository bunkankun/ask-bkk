Lín Jìnlóng 林近龍 (dates unrecorded; fl. early 16th c.), Pútián native; disciple of **Zhōu Yīng** ([[周瑛]]). Editor of his teacher's selected works, the *Cuìqú zhāigǎo* 翠渠摘稿 in 7 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0130]]). His selection from the larger *Cuìqú lèigǎo* is the basis for the WYG and subsequent transmissions of Zhōu's writings. CBDB id 128604.
