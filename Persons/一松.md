Late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist exegete; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. DILA Authority A000019. Honorific **Yīsōng dàshī** 一松大師 ("Great Master One-Pine"); also referenced as **Sōngshī** 松師 ("Master Pine"). Per **Xiǎoróu** 曉柔's preface to the *Xīnkè Fǎhuá yǎnyì xù* 新刻法華演義序: "The *Fǎhuá yǎnyì* is the lecture-record of Yīsōng dàshī. The Master is not known of what country he was, nor is his era clearly understood."

Major works:
- **Fǎhuá jīng yǎnyì** 法華經演義 ([[KR6d0091]], X33n0625, 7 *juan*) — Lotus Sūtra commentary, his lecture-record edited by **Guǎnghé** 廣和 ([[廣和]]).
- **Lèngyán jīng mì-lù** 楞嚴經秘錄 (X13n0283, 10 *juan*) — Śūraṃgama commentary.

Source: *Xīnkè Fǎhuá yǎnyì xù* by Xiǎoróu (X33n0624 preface); DILA A000019.
