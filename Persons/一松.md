Late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist exegete; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. DILA Authority A000019. Honorific **Yī-sōng dà-shī** 一松大師 ("Great Master One-Pine"); also referenced as **Sōng-shī** 松師 ("Master Pine"). Per **Xiǎo-róu** 曉柔's preface to the *Xīn-kè Fǎhuá yǎn-yì xù* 新刻法華演義序: "The *Fǎhuá yǎn-yì* is the lecture-record of Yī-sōng dà-shī. The Master is not known of what country he was, nor is his era clearly understood."

Major works:
- **Fǎhuá jīng yǎn-yì** 法華經演義 ([[KR6d0091]], X33n0625, 7 *juan*) — Lotus Sūtra commentary, his lecture-record edited by **Guǎng-hé** 廣和 ([[廣和]]).
- **Lèngyán jīng mì-lù** 楞嚴經秘錄 (X13n0283, 10 *juan*) — Śūraṃgama commentary.

Source: *Xīn-kè Fǎhuá yǎn-yì xù* by Xiǎo-róu (X33n0624 preface); DILA A000019.
