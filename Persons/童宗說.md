# Tóng Zōngshuō 童宗說

Sòng-period scholar; floruit late 12th century. His one substantial scholarly contribution is the *zhù-shì* 註釋 (running prose commentary) layer of the *Liǔ Hé-dōng jí zhù* 柳河東集注 [[KR4c0049]] — the second principal Sòng annotated edition of Liǔ Zōng-yuán [[柳宗元]], in which he collaborated with Zhāng Dūn-yí [[張敦頤]] (*yīn-biàn* phonetic analysis) and Pān Wěi [[潘緯]] (*yīn-yì* phonetic-and-meaning glosses). CBDB cbdbId 697378 has no fixed dates; no other works are securely attributed to him.
