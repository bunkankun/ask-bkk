# 吉原杏隱

**Yoshihara Kyōin** 吉原杏隱 (*fl.* late 18th c.), Nagasaki *bushi*-turned-bone-setter; former martial artist (*yuánwǔfū* 元武夫) who developed the *jujutsu* / *kuatsu* 死活拳法 ("dead-or-alive boxing-method") into a clinical system of orthopaedic manipulation. Teacher of [[二宮獻]] Ninomiya Ken, who travelled west to Nagasaki to study under him and subsequently synthesised the Yoshihara manual-manipulation tradition with the imperial Qīng *Zhènggǔ xīnfǎ yàozhǐ* ([[KR3el015]]) and Dutch *rangaku* anatomy in [[KR3el019]] *Seikotsuhan* / *Zhōngguó jiēgǔ túshuō* (1808). No further biographical record located; Yoshihara himself wrote nothing that survives.
