# Huìrán 慧然

Táng-dynasty Chán monk of the Línjì school, usually called Sānshèng Huìrán 三聖慧然 after his residence at the Sānshèng Yuàn 三聖院 in Zhènzhōu 鎮州 (present-day Héběi). Native place and exact lifedates are not transmitted. A direct heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of Línjì Yìxuán 臨濟義玄 ([[義玄]]), he received his master's *fùzhǔ* 付囑 to compile the master's recorded sayings — the received *Zhènzhōu Línjì Huìzhào chánshī yǔlù* 鎮州臨濟慧照禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0053]]) is traditionally attributed to him, though its transmitted form is a Northern-Sòng (1120) editorial recension by Yuánjué Zōngyǎn 圓覺宗演. After Línjì's death Huìrán is recorded as travelling widely, calling on Yǎngshān Huìjí 仰山慧寂, Déshān Xuānjiàn 德山宣鑒, and Xuěfēng Yìcún 雪峰義存.
