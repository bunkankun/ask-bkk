**Zhōu Yún** 周雲 (Míng; dates and native place unrecorded) is the attributed author of the *Shéndào dàbiān xiàngzōng huátiān wǔxīng* 神道大編象宗華天五星 ([[KR3fa011]]), an eleven-*juǎn* compendium of stellar astrology and *fēnyě* 分野 doctrine. Nothing further is reliably recorded; the *Sìkù tíyào* notes that the attribution is conventional and that the work has the character of an enlarged late-Míng astrological encyclopaedia built up around an earlier nucleus. CBDB has six homonyms; id 42831 is the conventional match without confident disambiguation.
