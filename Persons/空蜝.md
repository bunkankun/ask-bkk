**Kōng-lè** 空蜝 (or possibly Kōng-luó, fl. *c.* 750–900) — Tang-period Esoteric *ācārya* named in the colophon of [[KR6j0448]] (T1221) as 「鮫龍洞阿闍梨位空蜝述」 — "[the] *ācārya* of [the] Shark-Dragon Grotto, in Position Kōng-lè, expounds [the text]".

The graph **蜝** (Unicode U+87DD) is rare; it appears with a phonetic value *lè* / *qí* in standard reference works, but is sometimes given as a variant of 蠡 *luó* in the Buddhist transmission. The transcription "Kōng-lè" (空蜝) follows the more common reading; some witnesses read **空羅** (*Kōng-luó*).

The colophon places him at **Jiāo-lóng Dòng** 鮫龍洞 ("Shark-Dragon Grotto"), an unidentified meditation-site that has not been securely located in the geographic record. The use of 「位」 (*wèi* = "position, station") rather than the more common 「沙門」 (*śramaṇa*) or 「三藏」 (*Tripitaka-master*) is unusual and suggests a particular Esoteric initiation-hierarchy in which 「位」 names a specific *abhiṣeka*-rank.

He is one of many obscure local Esoteric *ācāryas* of late-Tang and Five-Dynasties China whose names survive only in the colophon-records of small ritual handbooks — collectively pointing to a wide diffusion of Esoteric ritual practice beyond the major Cháng-ān centres, even as the imperial Esoteric school there suffered the disruptions of the An-lushan rebellion and the Huì-chāng persecution.
