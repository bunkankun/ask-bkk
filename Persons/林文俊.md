Lín Wén-jùn 林文俊 (1487–1536), *zì* **Rǔ-yīng** 汝英, *hào* **Fāng-zhāi** 方齋, posthumous *shì* **Wén-xiū** 文修, of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Xīng-huà, Fú-jiàn). Zhèng-dé 6 / *xīn-wèi* (1511) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Nán-jīng Lì-bù yòu shì-láng*. *Míng-shǐ* main biography. **Zhàn Ruò-shuǐ** ([[湛若水]])'s composed *shén-dào-bēi* (spirit-way-tablet) records his writings: *Fāng-zhāi cún-gǎo* (no world-cut version); the WYG *běn* is the family-stored old transcript — *zòu*, *biǎo*, prefaces, prose, miscellaneous 9 *juǎn* + poetry 1 *juǎn* = 10 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0174]]). Famous as the **Nán-jiān Guó-zǐ-jiàn** chief who oversaw the Wàn-lì-era cutting of the **Nán-jiān 21 dynastic histories** boards — though as **Chén Kuí**'s *Guǎn-gé xù-lù* notes *jīng-jìn bù-jīng-xiū* (presented [boards] but not gone-through-revision), thus the editorial errors are not properly his.
