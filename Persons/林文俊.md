Lín Wénjùn 林文俊 (1487–1536), *zì* **Rǔyīng** 汝英, *hào* **Fāngzhāi** 方齋, posthumous *shì* **Wénxiū** 文修, of **Pútián** 莆田 (Xīnghuà, Fújiàn). Zhèngdé 6 / *xīnwèi* (1511) *jìnshì*; rose to *Nánjīng Lìbù yòu shìláng*. *Míngshǐ* main biography. **Zhàn Ruòshuǐ** ([[湛若水]])'s composed *shéndàobēi* (spirit-way-tablet) records his writings: *Fāngzhāi cúngǎo* (no world-cut version); the WYG *běn* is the family-stored old transcript — *zòu*, *biǎo*, prefaces, prose, miscellaneous 9 *juǎn* + poetry 1 *juǎn* = 10 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0174]]). Famous as the **Nánjiān Guózǐjiàn** chief who oversaw the Wàn-lì-era cutting of the **Nánjiān 21 dynastic histories** boards — though as **Chén Kuí**'s *Guǎngé xùlù* notes *jīngjìn bùjīngxiū* (presented [boards] but not gone-through-revision), thus the editorial errors are not properly his. CBDB id 128571, 1487–1536.
