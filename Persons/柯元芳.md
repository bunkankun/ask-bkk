**Kē Yuánfāng** 柯元芳 (*zì* Chǔhéng 楚蘅), late-Míng magistrate at Jiànān 建安 (Fújiàn); wrote the 1641 (Chóngzhēn 14 *xīnsì*) preface to [[KR3eq043]] *Yī jìng*, the [[蔣儀]] Jiǎng Yí selection from [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's late-Míng clinical-theoretical corpus.
