# Guǎngréng 廣仍

Early-Qīng Chán monk; disciple of [[僧遠|Língshù Yuǎn]] 靈樹遠 in the Sānfēngpài 三峰派 Línjì lineage. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*jì* 記 "recorded") of his master's *yǔlù* the *Língshù Yuǎn chánshī Yúnyán jí* 靈樹遠禪師雲嵒集 [[KR6q0220]]. No independent biographical material preserved.
