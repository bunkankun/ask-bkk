Zhāng Chūnfān 張春帆 was a late-Qīng Cantonese author, active in the reform-era press of the early twentieth century, best known as the author of the satirical "condemnation novel" (*qiǎnzé xiǎoshuō* 譴責小說) *Huànhǎi* 宦海 ([[KR4k0146]]). His birth and death dates are not recorded in standard reference works, and CBDB contains no entry for him. The novel's internal references to the constitutional reform movement and late-Qīng Guǎngdōng politics suggest he was writing c. 1905–1912.
