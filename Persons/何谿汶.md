Hé Xī-wèn 何谿汶 (also recorded as Hé Wèn 何汶 in CBDB, where 谿 is treated as part of the *míng*; this is the form preferred by the *Sòng-shǐ Yìwénzhì* and adopted by Sìkù), *hào* Zhú-zhuāng jū-shì 竹莊居士, a Southern Sòng *shī*-話 critic and anthologist whose dates are not fixed. Qián Zēng's 錢曾 *Dú-shū mǐn-qiú jì* 讀書敏求記 already noted "Zhú-zhuāng jū-shì, of unknown date"; the *Sìkù* editors infer Sòng affiliation from his quotation patterns and from the inclusion of his *Zhú-zhuāng shī huà* 竹莊詩話 ([[KR4i0041]]) in the *Sòng-shǐ Yìwénzhì* 宋史·藝文志 (which records the work in 27 *juàn*, vs. the received 24). No biographical data beyond this *hào* and the compiled work survives. CBDB id 17558 records him without dates.
