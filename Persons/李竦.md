Lǐ Sǒng 李竦 (zì Quánměi 全美, self-styled *Sānxiān mén dìzǐ tiānxià dū xiānkè* 三仙門弟子天下都仙客 — "Disciple of the Three-Immortal Gate, All-Realms Chief Immortal-Guest") was the editor and compiler of the Zhōng–Lǚ 鍾呂 *nèidān* compendium [[KR5a0247|*Xīshān qúnxiān huìzhēn jì* 西山群仙會真記 ([[KR5a0247]] / DZ 246)]], compiled under the name of his teacher Shī Jiānwú 施肩吾. His discipleship is attested in [[KR5c1248|DZ 1248 *Sāndòng qúnxiān lù*]] 14.22a. Whether the master under whom he studied is the Táng poet Shī Jiānwú or a distinct tenth-century Daoist of the same name is unsettled (see *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* 直齋書錄解題 12.348, and the Abstract to [[KR5a0247]]). Internal evidence dates his editorial activity to the late tenth or early eleventh century. CBDB has several homonyms for 李竦 but none of them a confident match; no CBDB id assigned.
