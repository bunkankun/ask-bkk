# 丹波 Dānbō

**Dānbō** 丹波 (Japanese **Tanba**), 15th-century Japanese physician named in [[韓繼禧]] Hán Jìxǐ's 1474 preface to the Korean *Shényìng jīng* 神應經 ([[KR3ee029]]) as a transmitter of the Japanese *Yōngjū bāxué fǎ* 癰疽八穴法 needling method. The Tanba family (丹波氏) was one of the principal Japanese imperial-medical hereditary lineages of the medieval period.
