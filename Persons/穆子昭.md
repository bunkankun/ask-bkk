Mù Zǐzhāo 穆子昭 (fl. early 13th c.) was a member of the [[麻九疇]] Má Jiǔchóu circle who helped transmit the manuscript of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù's *Sānxiāo lùn* ([[KR3eh047]]) from Liú's descendants to the *Jǐnxī yělǎo* 錦溪野老 recension of 1232.
