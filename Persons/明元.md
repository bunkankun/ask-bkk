Míngyuán 明元. Disciple-attendant of **Yìgōng Chāokē** [[超珂]] (1615–1661) at **Fúshī chányuàn** 伏獅禪院 (Méixī, Jiāxīng). Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*jìlù*) of [[KR6q0552]] 《伏獅義公禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B435; in the postface dated **康熙拾柒年七月之望** = **1678-08-02** Míngyuán signs as "不肖徒明元稽首百拜" — i.e. she explicitly identifies herself as a (female) disciple of the *yǔlù*'s subject, presenting the volume to her elder dharma-aunt [[超琛]] for the *bá*.
