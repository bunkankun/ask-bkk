**Lǐ Zhèntíng** 李振霆 (*hào* Běitíng 北亭), late-Qīng scholar of Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Prefaced Yè Lín 葉霖's specialty paediatric treatise *Shāzhěn jíyào* 痧疹輯要 ([[KR3ej049]]) in 1890 (*Guāngxù gēngyín* 庚寅 *mùchūn zhī chū* — early third lunar month). His preface is one of the more substantive contemporary witnesses for Yè Lín's biographical context and intellectual reception. Lifedates and other biographical detail not securely recorded.
