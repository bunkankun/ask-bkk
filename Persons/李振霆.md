**Lǐ Zhèn-tíng** 李振霆 (*hào* Běi-tíng 北亭), late-Qīng scholar of Sì-míng 四明 (Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng). Prefaced Yè Lín 葉霖's specialty paediatric treatise *Shā-zhěn jí-yào* 痧疹輯要 ([[KR3ej049]]) in 1890 (*Guāng-xù gēng-yín* 庚寅 *mù-chūn zhī chū* — early third lunar month). His preface is one of the more substantive contemporary witnesses for Yè Lín's biographical context and intellectual reception. Lifedates and other biographical detail not securely recorded.
