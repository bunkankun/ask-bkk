Zhōu Fán 周燔, Sòng-period *Yì*-commentator. Cited in [[KR1a0162]]'s structural-variant catalogue for his recension's distinctive arrangement, in which the *Dà xiàng* 大象 is placed before each hexagram-statement and the *Tuàn zhuàn* 彖傳 follows below.
