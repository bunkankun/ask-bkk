# Yuánmì 緣密

One of the eighty-eight recorded dharma-heirs of [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃; later held the Déshān 德山 abbacy and is known as Déshān Yuánmíng dàshī 德山圓明大師. Author of the verse appendix *Sòng Yúnmén sān jù yǔ* 頌雲門三句語 included in [[KR6q0073]].
