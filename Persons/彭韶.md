Péng Sháo 彭韶 (1430–1495), *zì* **Fèngyí** 鳳儀, *hào* **Cóngwú** 從吾, posthumous title **Huìān** 惠安. Native of **Pǔtián** 莆田 (Quánzhōu, Fújiàn). Reached *Xíngbù shàngshū* 刑部尚書 under Hóngzhì. Famous for the 8 *zàohù tú* 灶户圖 (Salt-Worker Illustrations) drawn during his **Zhèjiāng** *xúnshì* (touring-inspector) tenure with concurrent administration of salt — each illustration accompanied by a poem, in the **Yuán Jié** *Chōnglíng xíng* / **Zhèng Xiá** *Liúmín tú* visual-remonstrance tradition. *Míng shǐ* j. 183. Surviving works are [[KR4e0112]] *Péng Huìān jí* (originally *Cóngwú zhìgǎo* 從吾滯稿; title changed in mid-Jiā-jìng on a recutting; the recovered text is impaired, with the famous *Línjiāng cí* attacking **Yáng Shìqí** preserved only in **Zhū Yízūn**'s *Míngshī zōng* outside the present recension); also *Zhèngxùn* 政訓, separately catalogued.
