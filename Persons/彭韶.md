Péng Sháo 彭韶 (1430–1495), *zì* **Fèng-yí** 鳳儀, *hào* **Cóng-wú** 從吾, posthumous title **Huì-ān** 惠安. Native of **Pǔ-tián** 莆田 (Quán-zhōu, Fú-jiàn). Reached *Xíng-bù shàng-shū* 刑部尚書 under Hóng-zhì. Famous for the 8 *zào-hù tú* 灶户圖 (Salt-Worker Illustrations) drawn during his **Zhè-jiāng** *xún-shì* (touring-inspector) tenure with concurrent administration of salt — each illustration accompanied by a poem, in the **Yuán Jié** *Chōng-líng xíng* / **Zhèng Xiá** *Liú-mín tú* visual-remonstrance tradition. *Míng shǐ* j. 183. Surviving works are [[KR4e0112]] *Péng Huì-ān jí* (originally *Cóng-wú zhì-gǎo* 從吾滯稿; title changed in mid-Jiā-jìng on a recutting; the recovered text is impaired, with the famous *Lín-jiāng cí* attacking **Yáng Shì-qí** preserved only in **Zhū Yí-zūn**'s *Míng-shī zōng* outside the present recension); also *Zhèng-xùn* 政訓, separately catalogued.
