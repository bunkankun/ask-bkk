# Zōng-yǒng 宗永

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán *sì-fǎ mén-rén* 嗣法門人 of [[可湘]] Jué-àn Kě-xiāng (1206–1290); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0317]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the earlier Northern-Sòng Huì-yán Zōng-yǒng 慧嚴宗永 (A000569).
