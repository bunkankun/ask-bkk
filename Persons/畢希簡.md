Bì Xījiǎn 畢希簡, *zì* Shūwén 叔文, late-twelfth-century Southern-Sòng scholar of Wǔlíng 武陵 (modern Chángdé in Húnán). Friend of [[秦焴]] Qín Yù; sent Qín Yù the copy of [[趙善譽]] Zhào Shànyù's *Zhàoshì Yì shuō* ([[KR1a0039]]) on which Qín Yù based his *Chúnxī bǐngwǔ* (1186) Jiànkāng reprint.
