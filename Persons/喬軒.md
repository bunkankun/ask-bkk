Qiáo Xuān 喬軒 was a Republican-era popular fiction writer, author of *Qīnggōng Yuàn* 清宮怨 ([[KR4k0309]]), a romantic-historical novel set in the early Qīng court centered on the figure of Empress Dowager Zhuāng 孝莊文皇后. No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1912–1940. The name may be a pen name.
