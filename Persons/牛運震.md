Niú Yùnzhèn 牛運震 (1706–1758), zì Zhēngǔ 真谷 (or 真菴), hào Kōngshān 空山, of Zīyáng 滋陽 (modern Yǎnzhōu, Shāndōng), was an early-Qing classicist and *jīnshí* commentator. *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng 11 (1733), he served briefly as magistrate before retirement to scholarly study at his Kōngshāntáng 空山堂 studio. His major works include the *Kōngshāntáng Yìjiě* 空山堂易解 (Yì commentary), *Shījīng kǎo* 詩經考, *Cǎolú zhái* 草堂記, and the *bǔshuō* (supplementary commentary) to Chǔ Jùn's *Jīnshí jīngyǎn lù* [[KR2n0046]] — providing for each of Chǔ's 47 stele facsimiles the height, breadth, location, and abbreviated philological gloss.
