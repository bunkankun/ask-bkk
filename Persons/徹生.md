Chè-shēng 徹生, *zì* **Zhú-làng** 竹浪 ("Bamboo-Wave"); often referred to as **Qīng-chéng Zhú-làng** 青城竹浪 from his abbacy at the famous Daoist-Buddhist mountain. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696) — the major Sì-chuān post-Pò-shān regional patriarch. Born **明崇禎甲戌正月二十八日巳時** = **1634-02-25** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 60 at **康熙癸酉** = **1693**. Native of **Chuān-dōng Dìng-yuǎn** 川東定遠 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** Tonsured at 22 (**乙未** = **1655**) at **Róng-chāng-xiàn Luó-hàn-sì** 榮昌縣羅漢寺 under **Yǒu-yú** 有餘師. At 24 (**丁酉冬** = **1657** winter) took *shā-mí* precepts at **Lú-zhōu Fāng-shān Yún-fēng-sì** 瀘州方山雲峰寺 under **Tǐ-zōng** 體宗和尚. **戊戌** = **1658** at the *dān-chí* (red-painted altar steps) forgot the time of an incense errand; the moon emerged from a mountain hollow, and "in one glance everything was clear, his whole body relaxed as if a heavy load were set down" — sudden insight. Took full ordination at Yún-fēng-sì later that winter. Sequential *cān*-fǎng under **Zǐ-zhī** 紫芝 and **Dàn-zhú** 淡竹 etc., each producing *jī-yuán*. Final *fù-fǎ* at **Zhāo-jué-sì** 昭覺寺 under **Zhàng-xuě**.

**Abbacies.** **Qīng-chéng-shān Fèng-lín-sì** 青城山鳳林寺 (Sì-chuān, his principal seat where he read the *Tripiṭaka* in three full passes); **昭覺寺 Zhāo-jué-sì** (Chéng-dū — succeeded Zhàng-xuě here in **辛酉** = **1681**).

**Disciples.** Eleven *fǎ-sì* listed in the *yǔ-lù*, including **Yì-yún Rú-péng** 翼雲如鵬 [[如鵬]] (compiler of his *yǔ-lù*), **Zhèng-guǒ Liǎo-yīn** 正果了因, **Shào-méi Zhēn-ruì** 紹梅真瑞.

**Editorial work.** **康熙癸酉冬** = **1693** winter, at his teacher's command, traveled east to **Jiā-hé** 嘉禾 (Jiā-xīng) to engrave 《錦江禪燈》 *Jǐn-jiāng chán-dēng* (X85 n1590) — the major Sì-chuān-region Chán biographical anthology compiled by Zhàng-xuě's earlier disciples and consolidated under his direction — and to deposit the blocks at **Léng-yán-sì** 楞嚴寺 流通 alongside the official **Pǔ-jì-tánɡ Léng-yán-bǎn** Tripiṭaka catalog.

**Work.** [[KR6q0544]] 《青城竹浪生禪師語錄》 7 juan, J38 B427, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Yì-yún Rú-péng** [[如鵬]] *děng biān*; multiple prefaces dated 1683–1694, including one by his cousin-master **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] (signed "華嵒拙叔") in **康熙癸酉冬** = **1693**.

**Sources.** 《青城竹浪生禪師語錄》 vol. 4 〈行繇〉, 〈分燈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 83.
