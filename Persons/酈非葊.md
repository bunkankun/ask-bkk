# Lì Fēi'ān 酈非葊

Mid-Qīng *Dàoguāng*-era editor; collated the *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* 麻疹闡註 ([[KR3ej046]]) of [[張霞谿]] Zhāng Xiáxī for publication but did not complete the print run before his death. The 1848 publication was completed by the Zhāng family. Lifedates unrecorded.
