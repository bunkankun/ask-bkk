# Wúwài Wéidà 無外惟大

Yuán-dynasty Chán monk of the Kūnshān Jiànyán sì 崑山薦嚴寺, responsible for the Dàdé 7 (1303) Yuán-period reprint and preface of Zōngmì's *Chányuán zhūquánjí dūxù* ([[KR6q0091]]).
