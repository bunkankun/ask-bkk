Late-Qīng / early-Republican Húnán figure; *zì* Júshēng 菊生. Elder cousin of [[劉本昌]] Liú Běnchāng and author of one of the prefaces (*Xù èr* 敘二) to the *Mài jué xīn biān* 脈訣新編 ([[KR3eb030]]). Not in CBDB; no further biographical material located.
