Gōng Zìzhēn 龔自珍 (1792–1841; CBDB gives 1793–1842 by traditional reckoning, but the conventional dates 1792–1841 follow modern Western-calendar conversion), *zì* Sè-rén 璱人 (also Ěr-yù 爾玉), *hào* Dìng-ān 定盦 (also written 定庵), additional *hào* Yǔ-líng-shān-mín 羽琌山民. Native of Rén-hé 仁和, Hángzhōu, Zhè-jiāng. The single most influential intellectual transitional figure between Qián-Jiā *kǎo-zhèng* and the late-Qīng reform movements; prophet of dynastic decline and principal late-Cháng-zhōu transmitter of the *Gōng-yáng* New Text program. Son of the Hànlín scholar [[龔麗正]] Gōng Lì-zhèng; maternal grandson of [[段玉裁]] Duàn Yù-cái 段玉裁 (Duàn's daughter was Gōng's mother). Studied under Liú Féng-lù 劉逢祿 (1776–1829), the central second-generation Cháng-zhōu *Gōng-yáng* master. *Jìnshì* of Dàoguāng 9 (1829); held minor posts in the Boards of Rites and Personnel; resigned in 1839 disillusioned with deepening dynastic crisis (the Opium War broke out the next year) and returned south. Died at Dān-yáng in 1841 under unexplained circumstances (rumors of poisoning persisted in late-Qīng memoir). Major works: *Dìng-ān wén-jí* [[KR4f0064]] (3 juan + 4-juan *xù-jí*) and *Dìng-ān wén-jí bǔ-biān* [[KR4f0065]] (4 juan); the *Jǐ-hài zá-shī* 己亥雜詩 series of 315 quatrains (composed 1839); the *Yǐ-bǐng zhī jì zhù yì* 乙丙之際著議 essays; the *Zūn yǐn* 尊隱; the *Píng-jūn piān* 平均篇. His *Gōng-yáng* New Text program descends to [[康有為]] Kāng Yǒu-wéi and [[梁啟超]] Liáng Qǐ-chāo's late-Qīng historiographical formulations. *ECCP* 431–434 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 66057.
