# Yú Zhí-xī 虞執西

A late-Míng / early-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar of the **Yúnqī Zhūhóng** 雲棲袾宏 [[袾宏]] circle, identified in the colophons of his work as a *jūshì* 居士 ("layman") and devotee of Yúnqī. His exact lifedates and biographical details are not preserved in detail. He collaborated with [[嚴培西]] **Yán Péi-xī** 嚴培西 on the compilation of the **《雲棲淨土彙語》** *Yúnqī jìng-tǔ huì-yǔ* [[KR6p0089]] — an anthology of Yúnqī Zhūhóng's Pure Land sayings collated from across his prose corpus. The compilation is one of the principal early-Qīng anthologies of Yúnqī's Pure Land teaching and is part of the broader effort by the Yúnqī lay-Buddhist circle to consolidate and transmit the master's intellectual legacy after his death in 1615. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
