# Zōng-wén 宗文

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě mén-rén* 侍者門人) of Huái-hǎi Yuán-zhào 淮海原肇 (1189–1275). Co-lead-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0304]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
