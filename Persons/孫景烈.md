Sūn Jǐng-liè 孫景烈 (1706–1782), editor of the Qián-lóng *xīn-sì* (1761) 10-*juǎn* recension of **Kāng Hǎi** ([[康海]])'s *Duì-shān jí* ([[KR4e0159]]), based on **Zhāng Tài-wēi** 張太微's earlier selection with additional cutting. Of the Wǔ-gōng (Shǎn-xī) literary circle. By his official career, *biān-xiū* (compiler-editor) of the Hàn-lín during Qián-lóng. CBDB id 76189, 1706–1782.
