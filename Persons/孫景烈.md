Sūn Jǐngliè 孫景烈 (1706–1782), editor of the Qiánlóng *xīnsì* (1761) 10-*juǎn* recension of **Kāng Hǎi** ([[康海]])'s *Duìshān jí* ([[KR4e0159]]), based on **Zhāng Tàiwēi** 張太微's earlier selection with additional cutting. Of the Wǔgōng (Shǎnxī) literary circle. By his official career, *biānxiū* (compiler-editor) of the Hànlín during Qiánlóng. CBDB id 76189, 1706–1782.
