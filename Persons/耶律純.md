# Yēlǜ Chún 耶律純

Allegedly a Liáo-dynasty (Khitan) Hànlín scholar; the work [[KR3g0039]] *Xīngmìng zǒngkuò* 星命總括 is attributed to him with a self-preface dated Tǒnghé 2 (984) of the Liáo Shèngzōng era. However, the Sìkù 提要 of [[KR3g0039]] identifies multiple historical inconsistencies in this attribution:

(a) The Liáo *Běnjì* records no Korean diplomatic mission for Tǒnghé 2 (the year claimed by the preface for Yēlǜ Chún's mission to Goryeo Korea, where he allegedly received the *xīngmìng* technique from a Korean *guóshī*).

(b) The Liáo *Wàijì* records a Tǒnghé 3 (985) eastern-expedition against Goryeo, but explicitly states that the campaign was abandoned due to the Liáo's flooded territory — "*also no record of an envoy-mission to discuss border-territory*".

(c) Throughout the Liáo dynasty, "*nobility-and-office did not extend beyond the Yēlǜshì and Xiāoshì two clans*", making any nominal Yēlǜ-clan member possible — but checking all *Lièzhuàn* (biographies), there is no Yēlǜ Chún recorded.

The 提要 concludes: "*Probably this also issues from reliance-and-attribution*" — i.e., the *Yēlǜ Chún* attribution is pseudepigraphic. The actual author is unknown. The work's substantive content is genuine, but the attribution to the Liáo period is a literary device.
