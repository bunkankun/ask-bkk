**Liào Rùnhóng** 廖潤鴻 (*hào* Sōng-pǔ 松圃, fl. 1860s–1890s, 清), Húnán-born late-Qīng official and acupuncturist. Active in Beijing in the Tóngzhì and Guāngxù reigns, where he served in the Imperial Astronomical Office (Qīn-tiān-jiàn 欽天監). Compiled the *Zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* 針灸集成 ([[KR3ee007]], 1874), the *Miǎn-xué-táng zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* 勉學堂針灸集成 ([[KR3ee037]]), and the stand-alone *Kǎozhèng zhōushēn xué-fǎ gē* 考正周身穴法歌 ([[KR3ee032]]) — collectively the most widely-used late-Qīng portable acupuncture-handbook series. Liào's distinctive contribution is the systematic re-versification of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn cì-jiǔ xīn-fǎ yào-jué* ([[KR3ee015]]) into five-character mnemonic verse coupled with reduced-scale bronze-figure acupoint diagrams. Birth and death dates are not securely recorded; his own 1874 preface places him at age 40 in that year, suggesting birth ca. 1834, but this is not independently confirmed.
