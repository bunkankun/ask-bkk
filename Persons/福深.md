# Fúshēn 福深

Northern-Sòng Huánglóng-lineage Chán monk; dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) of Yúnān Zhēnjìng Kèwén 雲庵真淨克文 (1025–1102). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of the paratexts-and-appendices for Kèwén's *yǔlù*, preserved as [[KR6q0277]].
