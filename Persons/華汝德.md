Huá Rǔ-dé 華汝德 (late 15th / early 16th cent.), friend of the great Wú-school painter **Shěn Zhōu** 沈周 ([[沈周]]). *Guāng-lù-sì shǔ-chéng* 光祿寺署丞. Compiled and printed the topical 10-*juǎn* selection [[KR4e0118]] *Shí-tián shī-xuǎn* from Shěn Zhōu's poetry, arranged in 31 categories on the model of the Sòng-era category-arrangement of **Dù Fǔ**'s poetry. The **Cí-xī Zhāng Fū** 慈谿張鈇 *bá* (postface) attests Huá's editorial role. No further biographical detail preserved in standard sources.
