# Chén Zǎo 陳藻 (Sòng, fl. late 12th c. – early 13th c.)

*Zì* Yuán-jié 元潔. *Hào* Lè-xuān 樂軒 ("Joyful-Pavilion"). Native of *Fú-qīng* 福清 (Fú-jiàn). Birth and death years unknown; CBDB id 22966 (no dates).

The third-generation transmitter of the *Ài-xuān* school of pre-Zhū-Xī Fú-qīng *Lǐ-xué*: Lín Guāng-cháo [[林光朝]] (*Ài-xuān*, [[KR4d0225]]) → Lín Yì-zhī [[林亦之]] (*Wǎng-shān*, [[KR4d0231]]) → Chén Zǎo (this generation). His own disciple was Lín Xī-yì [[林希逸]] (1193–1271), the late-Sòng senior official and Daoist-Buddhist commentator.

After Lín Guāng-cháo's death (1178), Liú Kè-zhuāng's preface to Chén Zǎo's collection [[KR4d0240]] notes, the school's disciples mostly dispersed and some changed teachers; "only *Wǎng-shān* and *Lè-xuān* faithfully kept the old learning, to the end of their lives without regretting." The school's distinctive aesthetic — concision over expansiveness, density over sparseness, refinement over coarseness — gave it an independent place in Southern-Sòng *Lǐ-xué* alongside the Cheng-Zhū mainstream.

Surviving in Kanripo:
- [[KR4d0240]] *Lè-xuān jí* (8 *juǎn*, WYG; edited by his disciple Lín Xī-yì with Liú Kè-zhuāng preface).
