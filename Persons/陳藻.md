# Chén Zǎo 陳藻 (Sòng, fl. late 12th c. – early 13th c.)

*Zì* Yuánjié 元潔. *Hào* Lèxuān 樂軒 ("Joyful-Pavilion"). Native of *Fúqīng* 福清 (Fújiàn). Birth and death years unknown; CBDB id 22966 (no dates).

The third-generation transmitter of the *Àixuān* school of pre-Zhū-Xī Fúqīng *Lǐxué*: Lín Guāngcháo [[林光朝]] (*Àixuān*, [[KR4d0225]]) → Lín Yìzhī [[林亦之]] (*Wǎngshān*, [[KR4d0231]]) → Chén Zǎo (this generation). His own disciple was Lín Xīyì [[林希逸]] (1193–1271), the late-Sòng senior official and Daoist-Buddhist commentator.

After Lín Guāngcháo's death (1178), Liú Kèzhuāng's preface to Chén Zǎo's collection [[KR4d0240]] notes, the school's disciples mostly dispersed and some changed teachers; "only *Wǎngshān* and *Lèxuān* faithfully kept the old learning, to the end of their lives without regretting." The school's distinctive aesthetic — concision over expansiveness, density over sparseness, refinement over coarseness — gave it an independent place in Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* alongside the Cheng-Zhū mainstream.

Surviving in Kanripo:
- [[KR4d0240]] *Lèxuān jí* (8 *juǎn*, WYG; edited by his disciple Lín Xīyì with Liú Kèzhuāng preface).
