# Tāng Hàn 湯漢 (1202–1272)

*Zì* Bójì 伯紀, *hào* Dōngjiàn 東澗 ("East-Stream"). Late-Southern-Sòng scholar-official of the *Lǐzōng* and *Dùzōng* reigns. Patronised in his youth by **Chái Zhōngxíng** 柴中行 (*Mìjiàn*), **Zhēn Déxiù** 真德秀 (西山先生), and **Zhào Rǔténg** 趙汝騰 (*Zǐxiá lǎorén*, the figure who wrote a second preface to his *Miàojué gǔjīn*). Reached high office; his *Dōngjiàn yí jí* 東澗遺集 [[KR4c0252]] survives in 13 *juǎn*. The *Miàojué gǔjīn* 妙絕古今 [[KR4h0050]] was compiled in *Chúnyòu rényín* (1242) when he was still *wèishì* (not yet in office); the SKQS *tíyào* establishes his authorship by cross-reference with Zhào Fāng's *Dōngshān cúngǎo*. No firm catalog meta dates (the meta gives *fl. 1252–1257* which is consistent with the Zhào Rǔténg *Bǎoyòu* 5 (1257) preface).
