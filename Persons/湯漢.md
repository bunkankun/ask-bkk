# Tāng Hàn 湯漢 (1202–1272)

*Zì* Bó-jì 伯紀, *hào* Dōng-jiàn 東澗 ("East-Stream"). Late-Southern-Sòng scholar-official of the *Lǐ-zōng* and *Dù-zōng* reigns. Patronised in his youth by **Chái Zhōng-xíng** 柴中行 (*Mì-jiàn*), **Zhēn Dé-xiù** 真德秀 (西山先生), and **Zhào Rǔ-téng** 趙汝騰 (*Zǐ-xiá lǎo-rén*, the figure who wrote a second preface to his *Miào-jué gǔ-jīn*). Reached high office; his *Dōng-jiàn yí jí* 東澗遺集 [[KR4c0252]] survives in 13 *juǎn*. The *Miào-jué gǔ-jīn* 妙絕古今 [[KR4h0050]] was compiled in *Chún-yòu rén-yín* (1242) when he was still *wèi-shì* (not yet in office); the SKQS *tíyào* establishes his authorship by cross-reference with Zhào Fāng's *Dōng-shān cún-gǎo*. No firm catalog meta dates (the meta gives *fl. 1252–1257* which is consistent with the Zhào Rǔ-téng *Bǎo-yòu* 5 (1257) preface).
