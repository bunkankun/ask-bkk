**Lǐ Gōng** 李龏 (fl. early-to-mid 13th c.), *zì* **Hé-fǔ** 和父. A native of **Hé-zé** 菏澤 in **Cáo-zhōu** 曹州 (modern Shāndōng 山東). A late-Sòng poet-editor and the lifelong friend, co-cyclical-year coeval, and posthumous anthologist of **Zhōu Bì** 周弼 ([[周弼]]).

**Lifedates.** From his own preface to [[KR4d0369]] (dated **Bǎoyòu dīng-sì 1257**, winter solstice), Lǐ Gōng records that he and Zhōu Bì were "**born in the same *gēng-zǐ* cyclical year**" (同庚生), were "**neighbours**" (同寓里), and had "**discussed poetry for more than thirty years**" (相與往來論詩三十餘年). The shared *gēng-zǐ* year is conventionally taken to be **Qìngyuán 6 (1200)**, though some scholars argue for **Jiā-xī 4 (1204)**. In 1257 Zhōu was already deceased; Lǐ Gōng was still living and editing his works. Lǐ's own death-year is unknown but presumably falls in the late 1250s or 1260s. CBDB person 41398.

**Identity and editorial role.** Lǐ Gōng's principal documented activity is his **two-stage editorial work on Zhōu Bì's poetry**: (1) the earlier 12-*juàn* *Duānpíng jí* 端平集, which Lǐ had cut for circulation in Zhōu's lifetime (the parent collection, now lost); and (2) the 4-*juàn* posthumous **Duānpíng shī-jùn** 端平詩雋 ([[KR4d0369]]), assembled in 1257 by selecting from the parent collection and adding "some two hundred further pieces obtained outside the collection". The editorial principles — favouring works accessible to "late-learners" (*wǎn-xué*) over the more demanding pieces — are explicitly stated in his preface and represent one of the few surviving statements of editorial intent in Sòng-internal poetry anthologies.

Lǐ Gōng's printing arrangements for both collections were with the **Wàn-rén Hǎi Yún-chén shū-shú** 萬人海芸陳書塾 (or, in the Sì-kù tíyào's reading, the **Lín'ān-fǔ Péng-běi-jiē Chén Jiěyuán shū-jí-pù** 臨安府棚北街陳解元書籍舖) — the late-Sòng commercial book-shop of the publisher **Chén Qǐ** 陳起, central figure of the *Jiānghú* publishing milieu.

**Other writings.** Lǐ Gōng is also recorded as the author of poetry in the late-Sòng Jiānghú style; a small portion of his own verse survives in the *Quán Sòng shī* 全宋詩.

**Sources.** Lǐ Gōng's own preface to [[KR4d0369]] (the principal direct source); CBDB 41398.
