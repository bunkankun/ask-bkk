**Iwami Kyōkei** 岩恭敬 (*fl.* mid-eighteenth century), Edo-period Japanese physician, pupil of Yoshimasu Tōdō 吉益東洞 at Yoshimasu's Kyoto practice. Principal compiler of the *Jiànshū lù* 建殊錄 ([[KR3eq056]], 1763) — Yoshimasu Tōdō's case-record collection, the canonical clinical companion to the doctrinal *Idan* 醫斷. Cataloged under 江戶.
