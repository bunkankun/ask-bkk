**Luó Yuè-fēng** 羅越峰 (*fl.* late Guāng-xù era, ca. 1880–1900), late-Qīng gentleman-physician of Shào-xīng prefecture, Zhèjiāng. Compiler of the *Yínán jízhèng jiǎnfāng* 疑難急症簡方 ([[KR3ed122]]), printed 1895 from his own funds. The work specialises in difficult-to-diagnose chronic cases and clinical emergencies. Luó is the younger brother of *Luó Yàn-xiāng-gōng* 羅硯香公, who served as *zhōu-mù* secretary at Zhuō-zhōu 涿州 (Hé-běi); their grandfather *Jìng-xuān-gōng* 羅敬軒公 had begun the family's substantial medical library. Luó himself did not hold office and devoted his life to clinical practice and to compiling the family's medical learning. The preface to his work was provided by his nephew-in-law [[胡震]] (Hú Zhèn) of Yáo-běi 姚北 (Yú-yáo, Zhèjiāng).
