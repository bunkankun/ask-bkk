Huìzhèn 惠振 (lifedates unknown), Tang-period Buddhist monk and *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* commentator, named among the predecessors whose work was synthesised by the Sòng Huáyán-Chán scholar [[子璿]] Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán (965–1038) in his canonical commentary on the *Śūraṅgama*. Cited by [[來舟]] Lái Zhōu in the preface to [[KR6b0009]] *Dàshèng běnshēng xīndìguān jīng qiǎnzhù xuánshì* as one of the Tang masters whose strengths Zǐxuán drew upon. No biographical material survives.
