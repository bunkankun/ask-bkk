# Dàoshèng 道盛 (Juélàng Dàoshèng 覺浪道盛)

Major MíngQīng transitional Cáodòng-lineage Chán master and Míng-loyalist literary-religious figure. *Hào* Juélàng 覺浪 ("Awakening-Wave"), Làngzhàngrén 浪杖人 ("Staff-Man Làng"). Native of Pǔchéng 浦城 (Fújiàn). Lifedates 1593/1/18 – 1659/10/22 (Wànlì 20/12/16 – Shùnzhì 16/9/7, age 67, per the tomb-inscription in *Tiānjiè Juélàng Shèng chánshī yǔlù* juan 12).

**Thirty-third-generation Cáodòng-orthodox patriarch** (*Dòngshàng zhèngzōng sānshísān shì* 洞上正宗三十三世), dharma-heir of [[元鏡]] Huìtái Yuánjìng (1577–1630) and thus dharma-grandson of [[慧經]] Wúmíng Huìjīng in the Shòuchāng 壽昌 Cáodòng revival. One of the principal Cáodòng masters of the MíngQīng transition. Abbot at **Tiānjièsì** 天界寺 (in Nánjīng), **Qīxiásì** 棲霞寺 at Shèshān 攝山, Lónghú 龍湖, Shòuchāng 壽昌, Línggǔ 靈谷, Fènglín 鳳林, Jìngshān 徑山 — some fifty seats over his career. His institutional position near the former Míng capital Nánjīng placed him at the geographic and political centre of the Míng-loyalist Buddhist resistance.

Compiled (*jí* 集) his teacher [[元鏡]]'s *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0363]]; X72 n1433), supplying the principal biographical material for Yuánjìng's posthumous *tǎmíng* in the same volume. Before becoming Yuánjìng's heir, Dàoshèng had trained under Ruìyán Shígōng 瑞巖識公 (initial tonsure), taken the full precepts from Bóshān Wúyì Yuánlái 無異元來, and consulted Huìjīng directly — the Shòuchāng group was tight-knit, and Dàoshèng's dharma-lineage received its formal seal from Huìjīng himself after Yuánjìng's initial recognition.

Substantial Míng-loyalist intellectual-literary engagement: widely associated with the mid-17th-century Buddhist lay community including [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] 蕅益智旭 (who preserves ten Q&A exchanges with Dàoshèng in [[KR6q0183]]) and various *yí mín* 遺民 scholars.

Major works:
- [[KR6q0221]] *Tiānjiè Juélàng Shèng chánshī quán lù* 天界覺浪盛禪師全錄 — his comprehensive 33-juan *yǔlù* compiled posthumously by dharma-heirs [[大成|Dàchéng]] and [[大然|Dàrán]].
- [[KR6q0482]] 《天界覺浪盛禪師嘉禾語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B312 — a single-juan Jiā-xīng-region (= 嘉禾) selection from his preaching, recorded by **Dàshū** [[大樞]] + **Dàyīng** [[大英]] *lù*, with front-preface by **Tán Zhēnmò** [[譚貞默]] (the same Tán who wrote the *tǎmíng* for Miàoyòng [[妙用]]'s [[KR6q0416]] in 1654).

Sources: *Tiānjiè Juélàng Shèng chánshī yǔlù* juan 12 tomb-inscription; *Wǔ dēng quán shū*; Chén Yuán 陳垣. 1962. 《明季滇黔佛教考》 and 《清初僧諍記》.
