# Dào-shèng 道盛 (Jué-làng Dào-shèng 覺浪道盛)

Major Míng-Qīng transitional Cáodòng-lineage Chán master and Míng-loyalist literary-religious figure. *Hào* Jué-làng 覺浪 ("Awakening-Wave"), Làng-zhàng-rén 浪杖人 ("Staff-Man Làng"). Native of Pǔ-chéng 浦城 (Fú-jiàn). Lifedates 1593/1/18 – 1659/10/22 (Wànlì 20/12/16 – Shùnzhì 16/9/7, age 67, per the tomb-inscription in *Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Shèng chánshī yǔlù* juan 12).

**Thirty-third-generation Cáodòng-orthodox patriarch** (*Dòng-shàng zhèng-zōng sānshí-sān shì* 洞上正宗三十三世), dharma-heir of [[元鏡]] Huì-tái Yuán-jìng (1577–1630) and thus dharma-grandson of [[慧經]] Wú-míng Huì-jīng in the Shòu-chāng 壽昌 Cáo-dòng revival. One of the principal Cáodòng masters of the Míng-Qīng transition. Abbot at **Tiān-jiè-sì** 天界寺 (in Nán-jīng), **Qī-xiá-sì** 棲霞寺 at Shè-shān 攝山, Lóng-hú 龍湖, Shòu-chāng 壽昌, Líng-gǔ 靈谷, Fèng-lín 鳳林, Jìng-shān 徑山 — some fifty seats over his career. His institutional position near the former Míng capital Nán-jīng placed him at the geographic and political centre of the Míng-loyalist Buddhist resistance.

Compiled (*jí* 集) his teacher [[元鏡]]'s *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0363]]; X72 n1433), supplying the principal biographical material for Yuán-jìng's posthumous *tǎ-míng* in the same volume. Before becoming Yuán-jìng's heir, Dào-shèng had trained under Ruì-yán Shí-gōng 瑞巖識公 (initial tonsure), taken the full precepts from Bó-shān Wú-yì Yuán-lái 無異元來, and consulted Huì-jīng directly — the Shòu-chāng group was tight-knit, and Dào-shèng's dharma-lineage received its formal seal from Huì-jīng himself after Yuán-jìng's initial recognition.

Substantial Míng-loyalist intellectual-literary engagement: widely associated with the mid-17th-century Buddhist lay community including [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] 蕅益智旭 (who preserves ten Q&A exchanges with Dào-shèng in [[KR6q0183]]) and various *yí mín* 遺民 scholars.

Major works:
- [[KR6q0221]] *Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Shèng chánshī quán lù* 天界覺浪盛禪師全錄 — his comprehensive 33-juan *yǔ-lù* compiled posthumously by dharma-heirs [[大成|Dà-chéng]] and [[大然|Dà-rán]].
- [[KR6q0482]] 《天界覺浪盛禪師嘉禾語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B312 — a single-juan Jiā-xīng-region (= 嘉禾) selection from his preaching, recorded by **Dà-shū** [[大樞]] + **Dà-yīng** [[大英]] *lù*, with front-preface by **Tán Zhēn-mò** [[譚貞默]] (the same Tán who wrote the *tǎ-míng* for Miào-yòng [[妙用]]'s [[KR6q0416]] in 1654).

Sources: *Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Shèng chánshī yǔlù* juan 12 tomb-inscription; *Wǔ dēng quán shū*; Chén Yuán 陳垣. 1962. 《明季滇黔佛教考》 and 《清初僧諍記》.
