# 屠道和

Tú Dàohé, mid-Qīng physician of Huánggāng 黃岡 (Húběi); compiler of *Pǔjì liángfāng* 普濟良方, the source-collection from which the [[屠燮臣]] anti-opium-addiction formula was excerpted into [[KR3em041]] *Hóushé bèiyào mìzhǐ* 喉舌備要秘旨.
