Late-Yuán paleographer, calligrapher, and senior official, *zì* Bówēn 伯温, *hào* Yùxuěpō zhēnyì 玉雪坡真逸, of Ráozhōu 饒州 (modern Poyang, Jiangxi). Held senior offices including *Bīngbù shìláng*, *Tàicháng lǐyíyuàn shì*, *Hànlín shìjiǎng xuéshì*, *Zhèngshū shèngjīn*. Two paleographic monuments survive in the Sìkù: the *Shuōwén zìyuán* [[KR1j0040]] in 1 *juàn* — a re-organization of the 540 *Shuōwén* radicals; and the *Liùshū zhèngé* [[KR1j0041]] in 5 *juàn* — a *Lǐbù yùnlüè*-arranged orthographic dictionary. The Sìkù compilers position him between Zhāng Yǒu's [[張有]] more precise *Fùgǔ biān* and Yáng Huán's [[楊桓]] more idiosyncratic *Liùshū tǒng*. The Míng *Qīxiū lèigǎo* claims he was executed by Míng Tàizǔ for Zhāng Shì-chéng-faction sympathies; the official *Yuánshǐ* (compiled under Tàizǔ) gives him a peaceful death of illness in Póyáng — the Sìkù compilers accept the latter. CBDB lifedates 1298–1369, in agreement with the catalog meta and the *Yuánshǐ*.
