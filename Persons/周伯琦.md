Late-Yuán paleographer, calligrapher, and senior official, *zì* Bó-wēn 伯温, *hào* Yù-xuě-pō zhēn-yì 玉雪坡真逸, of Ráo-zhōu 饒州 (modern Poyang, Jiangxi). Held senior offices including *Bīng-bù shìláng*, *Tài-cháng lǐ-yí-yuàn shì*, *Hànlín shì-jiǎng xuéshì*, *Zhèng-shū shèng-jīn*. Two paleographic monuments survive in the Sìkù: the *Shuōwén zì-yuán* [[KR1j0040]] in 1 *juàn* — a re-organization of the 540 *Shuōwén* radicals; and the *Liù-shū zhèng-é* [[KR1j0041]] in 5 *juàn* — a *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè*-arranged orthographic dictionary. The Sìkù compilers position him between Zhāng Yǒu's [[張有]] more precise *Fù-gǔ biān* and Yáng Huán's [[楊桓]] more idiosyncratic *Liù-shū tǒng*. The Míng *Qī-xiū lèi-gǎo* claims he was executed by Míng Tài-zǔ for Zhāng Shì-chéng-faction sympathies; the official *Yuán-shǐ* (compiled under Tài-zǔ) gives him a peaceful death of illness in Pó-yáng — the Sìkù compilers accept the latter. CBDB lifedates 1298–1369, in agreement with the catalog meta and the *Yuán-shǐ*.
