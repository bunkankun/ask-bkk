**Shǐ Mínfàn** 史民範 (*zì* Zhìmíng 秩銘, *fl.* early Republican period) — early-Republican-era physician of *Gǔlèjiāo* 古樂郊 (the ancient name for an area near Lètíng 樂亭, Héběi); described himself as a *hòuxué* 後學 (later-generation disciple) of the late-Qīng tradition. Author of the *Mínguó* 2 / 1913 reprint preface to [[慶恕]] Qìng Shù's *Yīxué zhāicuì* 醫學摘粹 ([[KR3er069]]). Not in CBDB.
