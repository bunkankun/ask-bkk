**Shǐ Mín-fàn** 史民範 (*zì* Zhì-míng 秩銘, *fl.* early Republican period) — early-Republican-era physician of *Gǔ-lè-jiāo* 古樂郊 (the ancient name for an area near Lè-tíng 樂亭, Hé-běi); described himself as a *hòu-xué* 後學 (later-generation disciple) of the late-Qīng tradition. Author of the *Mín-guó* 2 / 1913 reprint preface to [[慶恕]] Qìng Shù's *Yī-xué zhāi-cuì* 醫學摘粹 ([[KR3er069]]). Not in CBDB.
