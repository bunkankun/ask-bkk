Zhī Shī-lún 支施崙 (active 4th c.), Yuèzhī (or Yuèzhī-descended) translator at the Former-Liáng 前涼 (314–376) court of Liángzhōu 涼州 in the Hé-xī corridor. Per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145), translator of [[KR6f0021|*Fó shuō xū lài jīng* 佛說須賴經]] (T0329) — the second Chinese version of the *Surata-paripṛcchā*, parallel to [[KR6f0020|Bái-yán's earlier T0328]]. Birth and death dates not preserved.
