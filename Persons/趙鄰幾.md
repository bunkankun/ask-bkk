Zhào Línjī 趙鄰幾 (also written 趙隣幾, 921–979) was a Northern-Sòng Hànlín scholar and member of the editorial commission for [[KR3l0118]] *Tàipíng guǎngjì* (978) under Lǐ Fǎng 李昉's directorship. He served as *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士 under Sòng Tàizōng. The *Sìkù* tíyào of the *Guǎngjì* writes the name as 趙隣 (likely a copyist's abbreviation); the full form is 趙鄰幾. CBDB 51052.
