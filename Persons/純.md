Chún 純 (catalog form; full reference **Biémù Chún** 別牧純), *zì* **Biémù** 別牧 ("Detached Shepherd"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jìngtíng Dézōng** [[德宗]] (1621–1684, cf. [[KR6q0587]]). Born **明崇禎戊寅五月二十日** = **1638-07-01** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 60 in **康熙丁丑** = **1697**. Native of **Sānwúzhàn** 三吾站 (Húnán); lay name **Lǐ Shànbǎo** 李善寶.

**Formation.** Vegetarian from infancy; as a child accompanied his mother to nunneries and would clasp hands and kneel to listen, sit in *jiā-fū*. At 15 a dream resolved his determination; secretly fled at night to **Fú-yuán-ān** 福緣菴 to take tonsure under **Hóng-jué** 弘覺禪師. Took *shā-mí* precepts at **Yí-shān** 嶷山 — given the *běn-lái miàn-mù* *gōng-àn*. One day on hearing a cat cry, had insight. Listened to **Zēng-shān** lecturing the *Śūraṅgama*. Met **Dé-zōng** 逕和尚. **順治己亥** = **1659** took full ordination; the next year accompanied Dé-zōng to take the abbacy of Yí-shān. **康熙壬寅** = **1662** they jointly founded **Nán-yuán** 南源. Eventually *fù-fǎ* in **康熙丁巳** = **1677**.

**Abbacies.** **Nányuán Hóngshèngsì** 南源洪聖寺 (succeeded his teacher); **Cáoshān Jíshèngsì** 曹山集聖寺.

**Disciples.** **Fúyuè** [[福月]] (compiler).

**Work.** [[KR6q0588]] 《別牧純禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B476, compiled by *fǎsì* **Fúyuè** [[福月]] *děng biān*; preface by **Huìlù Dìhuī** [[慧輅]] (1627–1725).

**Sources.** 《別牧純禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉.
