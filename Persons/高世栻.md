Gāo Shìshì 高世栻 (*zì* Shìyù 士御, CBDB 438036, no confident dates), *fl.* late 17th century, native of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). The leading disciple of the Lú-shān 侶山 medical academy founder Zhāng Zhìcōng [[張志聰]], and the principal continuator of the Hángzhōu Lú-shān school after Zhāng's death.

After Zhāng's death (*c.* 1695) Gāo took up multiple unfinished manuscripts and brought them to completion. His principal completions and works:

1. *Běncǎo chóngyuán* 本草崇原 ([[KR3ec050]]) — completed posthumously from Zhāng's manuscript.
2. *Sùwèn jí zhù* 素問集注 — collaborative commentary on the *Sùwèn*.
3. *Língshū jí zhù* 靈樞集注 — on the *Língshū*.
4. *Yīxué zhēn zhuàn* 醫學真傳 (1699) — Gāo's own clinical synthesis.

No confident lifedates; his *floruit* is approximately 1660–1700.
