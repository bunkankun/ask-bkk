Yóu Shìfǔ 尤世輔 (*hào* Yuèyán lǎorén 岳岩老人; fl. mid-18th c.) was the uncle of [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí; he sponsored the first printing of the *Jīnguì yì* ([[KR3eh003]]) at the Sīyǒngtáng 思永堂 in Hǔqiū Shāntáng 虎丘山塘 in 1768.
