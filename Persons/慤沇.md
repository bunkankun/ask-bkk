Quèyǎn 慤沇 (lifedates unknown), Tang-period Buddhist monk and *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* commentator, named alongside [[惠振]] Huìzhèn as one of the Tang masters whose commentarial work on the *Śūraṅgama* was synthesised by [[子璿]] Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán in the Sòng. The reference is in [[來舟]] Lái Zhōu's preface to [[KR6b0009]] *Dàshèng běnshēng xīndìguān jīng qiǎnzhù xuánshì*. No biographical material survives.
