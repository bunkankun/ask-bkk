# 冶子裘

Yězǐ Qiú, elder brother (同懷兄) of the mid-Qīng ophthalmologist [[黃庭鏡]] Huáng Tíngjìng; supplied a biographical preface to [[KR3em006]] *Mùjīng dàchéng* 目經大成 recording details of Huáng's family and travels.
