# 余瀳 Yú Jí

**Yú Jí** 余瀳, late-Qīng literatus, friend of [[陳衡山]] Chén Héngshān; wrote a preface to Chén's Chinese reprint of the Sòng *Bèijí jiǔfǎ* 備急灸法 ([[KR3ee049]]).
