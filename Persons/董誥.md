Dǒng Gào 董誥 (1740–1818), *zì* Yǎlún 雅倫, *hào* Zhèlín 蔗林, posthumously *Wéngōng* 文恭, was a leading late-Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng-court official. Son of Dǒng Bāngdá 董邦達, the major Qiánlóng-court painter and Hànlín. Rose under Qiánlóng and Jiāqìng to *Wénhuádiàn dàxuéshì* and *Tàizǐ tàibǎo*. Co-supervisor (with Dài Qúhēng 戴衢亨) of the imperial *Qīn dìng quán Táng wén* 欽定全唐文 — the comprehensive Tang-prose anthology. Co-editor of the supplemented edition of the *Qīn dìng shòu shí tōng kǎo* ([[KR3d0010]]). Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 340.
