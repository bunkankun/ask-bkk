Dǒng Gào 董誥 (1740–1818), *zì* Yǎlún 雅倫, *hào* Zhèlín 蔗林, posthumously *Wéngōng* 文恭, was a leading late-Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng-court official. Son of Dǒng Bāng-dá 董邦達, the major Qiánlóng-court painter and Hàn-lín. Rose under Qiánlóng and Jiā-qìng to *Wén-huá-diàn dà-xué-shì* and *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo*. Co-supervisor (with Dài Qú-hēng 戴衢亨) of the imperial *Qīn dìng quán Táng wén* 欽定全唐文 — the comprehensive Tang-prose anthology. Co-editor of the supplemented edition of the *Qīn dìng shòu shí tōng kǎo* (KR3d0010). Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 340.
