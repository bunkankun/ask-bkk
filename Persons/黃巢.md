**Huáng Cháo** 黃巢 (d. 884), late-Táng rebel-leader, principal architect of the 878–884 rebellion that broke the Táng dynasty's effective control. The *Chǔshì yíshū* tomb-excavation transmission narrative ([[KR3eq029]]) is set in the wake of his depredations.
