Zhāng Qiānlǐ 張千里, *zì* Qiānlǐ 千里 (original given-name not securely identified), late-Qiánlóng to Dào-guāng-period physician of Tóngxiāng 桐鄉 (Zhèjiāng). Held the *xuébó* (district education-supervisor) honorific, indicating *gòngshēng* status. Renowned in his time as the leading Zhèjiāng physician; widely held up as an exemplar for younger physicians of the lower-Yangtze school. His surviving casebook [[KR3ep093]] *Qiānlǐ yīàn* was recovered from a Sūzhōu used-bookstall in the Dàoguāng period and published in 1920 by Líng Yǒng 凌詠 as part of an organised Yuèzhōng salvage of provincial physicians' lost manuscripts. Not in CBDB.
