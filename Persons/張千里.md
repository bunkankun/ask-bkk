Zhāng Qiān-lǐ 張千里, *zì* Qiān-lǐ 千里 (original given-name not securely identified), late-Qiánlóng to Dào-guāng-period physician of Tóng-xiāng 桐鄉 (Zhèjiāng). Held the *xué-bó* (district education-supervisor) honorific, indicating *gòng-shēng* status. Renowned in his time as the leading Zhèjiāng physician; widely held up as an exemplar for younger physicians of the lower-Yangtze school. His surviving casebook [[KR3ep093]] *Qiān-lǐ yī-àn* was recovered from a Sūzhōu used-bookstall in the Dào-guāng period and published in 1920 by Líng Yǒng 凌詠 as part of an organised Yuè-zhōng salvage of provincial physicians' lost manuscripts. Not in CBDB.
