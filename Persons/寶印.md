# Bǎoyìn 寶印 (1109–1190)

Southern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, *shì* Fózhào chánshī 佛照禪師, abbot of the Jìngshān Wànshòu sì 徑山萬壽寺 (foremost of the Five-Mountain Wǔshān 五山 Chán institutions). Principal monastic interlocutor of Sòng Xiàozōng ([[趙昚]], 1127–1194); recipient of the emperor's *Yùzhù Yuánjué jīng* ([[KR6i0562]]) in 1183, which he had engraved at Jìngshān.
