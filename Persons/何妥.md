# Hé Tuǒ 何妥

*Zì* Qīfèng 棲鳳. Sui-period 隋 classicist of Western Hú 胡 origin, native of Pídù 郫都 in Sìchuān; held high posts as *Guózǐ jìjiǔ* 國子祭酒 ("Director of the Imperial Academy"). Active under emperors Wén 文帝 and Yáng 煬帝. Author of *Zhōuyì jiǎngshū* 周易講疏 in thirteen *juàn*; the work is lost but is extensively cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Suíshū* (juan 75) gives his biography.
