Zhōu Qí 周祈 (*fl.* Wànlì period, late sixteenth century), native of Qízhōu 蘄州 in Húguǎng (modern Húběi). Held a *mínbù lángzhōng* 民部郎中 (= *hùbù lángzhōng* 戶部郎中) office; the *jìnshì* date and the exact provincial commission are unrecoverable. Compiler of the *Míng yì kǎo* 名義考 ([[KR3j0064]]), a twelve-juan lexical-etymological compendium organized in four domains (heaven, earth, the human, things), printed in 1583/1584. Not in CBDB. The *Sìkù* editors' note that "his beginning and end cannot be traced" remains the best summary of available biographical evidence.
