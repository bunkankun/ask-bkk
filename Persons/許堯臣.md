# Xǔ Yáochén 許堯臣

Southern-Sòng medical scholar of Sānshān 三山. Known from Yè Dàlián's 1186 postface to the *Yèshì lùyàn fāng* ([[KR3ed130]]) as one of the two collators (alongside Liú Liángshū of Shòuchūn) of the family-transmitted formulary, prepared for the Lóngshū jùnzhāi woodblock printing.
