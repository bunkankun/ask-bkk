Gāo Dǐng-fén 高鼎汾, *hào* Shàng-chí 上池 (b. ca. 1794, died after 1860). Mid-19th-century **Wú-xī** 無錫 physician. Son of the much more famous [[高秉鈞]] Gāo Bǐng-jūn (Jǐn-tíng 錦亭, 1755–1827, author of the *Yáng-kē xīn-dé jí* 瘍科心得集). Gāo Dǐng-fén pursued medical study from about age 30 and was clinically active in Wú-xī through the 1840s and 1850s, in close professional association with [[王泰林]] Wáng Xù-gāo (1798–1862), who had also been a disciple of Gāo Jǐn-tíng. His sole transmitted work is [[KR3eq090]] *Yī-xué kè-ér cè* 醫學課兒策 (1843), the *cè-wèn* (examination-question) text composed to instruct his son Gāo Dòu-jī 鬥機, surviving in a manuscript marginally annotated by Wáng Xù-gāo and rediscovered by Zhōu Zhèn in his great-grandson Yán-wǔ's possession in 1915. Lifedates derived from the self-preface, which reports him aged 50 in 1843. Not in CBDB.
