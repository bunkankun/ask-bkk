Gāo Dǐngfén 高鼎汾, *hào* Shàngchí 上池 (b. ca. 1794, died after 1860). Mid-19th-century **Wúxī** 無錫 physician. Son of the much more famous [[高秉鈞]] Gāo Bǐngjūn (Jǐntíng 錦亭, 1755–1827, author of the *Yángkē xīndé jí* 瘍科心得集). Gāo Dǐngfén pursued medical study from about age 30 and was clinically active in Wúxī through the 1840s and 1850s, in close professional association with [[王泰林]] Wáng Xùgāo (1798–1862), who had also been a disciple of Gāo Jǐntíng. His sole transmitted work is [[KR3eq090]] *Yīxué kèér cè* 醫學課兒策 (1843), the *cèwèn* (examination-question) text composed to instruct his son Gāo Dòujī 鬥機, surviving in a manuscript marginally annotated by Wáng Xùgāo and rediscovered by Zhōu Zhèn in his great-grandson Yánwǔ's possession in 1915. Lifedates derived from the self-preface, which reports him aged 50 in 1843. Not in CBDB.
