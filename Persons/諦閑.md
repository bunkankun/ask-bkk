# Dìxián 諦閑

Late-Qīng / early-Republican-period Buddhist monk; the principal **Tiāntái-school revivalist** of the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. *Zì* **Gǔ-xū** 古虛 (commonly cited as **Dì-xián Gǔ-xū** 諦閑古虛); also self-styled **Zhuó-sān** 卓三. Lay surname **Zhū** 朱. Native of **Huáng-yán** 黃巖 (Tāizhōu, Zhèjiāng), the historical seat of the Tiāntái school. Lifedates **1858 – 3 July 1932** (Mínguó 21.5.30, age 75). Per DILA A000048.

Forty-third-generation lineal heir of the Tiāntái school (台宗裔孫); Dharma name through the **Guǎn-zōng-sì** 觀宗寺 lineage at Níngbō, where he served as abbot and revived Tiāntái doctrinal study after a long period of decline. Founder of the **Guǎn-zōng yán-jiū-shè** 觀宗研究社 (Guǎn-zōng Buddhist Studies Society), the principal modern Tiāntái-school institutional foundation, whose graduates included **Tán-xū** 倓虛, **Bǎo-jìng** 寶靜, and **Tàn-xū** 倓虛 (the founder of modern Tiāntái Buddhism in Northern China and Manchuria).

Major works include the *Lèngyán-jīng xù zhǐ-wèi shū* 楞嚴經序指味疏 ([[KR6j0721]], X16 no. 313, dated 1893), a commentary on the prefatory section of the *Śūraṃgama* arranged under a Tiāntái-school *kē* 科 outline; numerous *Lèngyán*, *Diamond*, and *Pure Land* commentaries collected in his published *yǔlù*; and the *Yuán-juéjīng jiǎng-yì* 圓覺經講義. Modern Republican-period editions of his collected works (*Dì-xián dà-shī yí-jí* 諦閑大師遺集) are widely circulated.
