# Déhóng 德弘

Yuán-dynasty Chán monk; disciple and attendant of [[永盛|Zhú-yuán Yǒng-shèng]] 竺源永盛 (1275–1347). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler of the *Zhèng dào gē zhù* 證道歌註 [[KR6q0179]] from his master's extempore commentarial responses to visiting students at the Nánchāo Zhú-yuán-ān 南巢竺源菴; identified in the colophon as *cān-xué mén-rén Déhóng biān* 參學門人德弘編 ("disciple-of-Chán-study Déhóng, editor"). One of three named dharma-heirs at Yǒng-shèng's Nánchāo memorial (alongside Huì-yuè 慧月 and Huì-guān 慧觀). No independent biographical material is preserved.
