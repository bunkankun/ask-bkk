# Déhóng 德弘

Yuán-dynasty Chán monk; disciple and attendant of [[永盛|Zhúyuán Yǒngshèng]] 竺源永盛 (1275–1347). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler of the *Zhèng dào gē zhù* 證道歌註 [[KR6q0179]] from his master's extempore commentarial responses to visiting students at the Nánchāo Zhúyuánān 南巢竺源菴; identified in the colophon as *cānxué ménrén Déhóng biān* 參學門人德弘編 ("disciple-of-Chán-study Déhóng, editor"). One of three named dharma-heirs at Yǒngshèng's Nánchāo memorial (alongside Huìyuè 慧月 and Huìguān 慧觀). No independent biographical material is preserved.
