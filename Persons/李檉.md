# 李檉 Lǐ Chēng

Northern-Sòng physician, author of a *Shānghán* commentary in the early Sòng tradition; named alongside [[王叔和]] Wáng Shūhé and [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ in the bibliographic preface to the Yuán-edition *Shānghán bǎi zhèng gē* / *Fā wēi lùn* ([[KR3ef061]] / [[KR3ef063]]).
