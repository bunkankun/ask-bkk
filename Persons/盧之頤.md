**Lú Zhīyí** 盧之頤 (*zì* Zǐyóu 子繇, *fl.* late Míng / early Qīng), late-Míng physician of Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng); the catalog meta has a transcriptional slip 廬 for 盧 in the surname (preserved per CLAUDE.md). Author of the ***Jiēnüè lùn shū*** 痎瘧論疏 ([[KR3e0088]], 1 juan) on malaria-class diseases, and the ***Běncǎo chéngyǎ bàn jì*** 本草乘雅半偈 ([[KR3e0089]], 10 juan). Háng Shìjùn's *Dàogǔtáng jí* contains a *Lú Zhīyí xiǎo zhuàn* 小傳, which records that Lú's earlier *Jīnguì yàoluè móxiàng* 金匱要略模象 was burned by his father (regretting Lú's medical practice over Confucian scholarship); subsequent works include the *Bàn jì* (transmitted), *Mōsuǒ Jīnguì* 摩索金匱 (9 juan, lost), *Shānghán jīnbì chāo* 傷寒金鎞鈔 (lost), and *Yī nán xī yí* 醫難析疑 (lost). Háng's biography apparently does not mention this *Jiēnüè lùn shū*, though the work survives in the SKQS recension.
