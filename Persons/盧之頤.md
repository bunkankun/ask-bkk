**Lú Zhīyí** 盧之頤 (*zì* Zǐyóu 子繇, *fl.* late Míng / early Qīng), late-Míng physician of Qián-táng 錢塘 (modern Háng-zhōu, Zhèjiāng); the catalog meta has a transcriptional slip 廬 for 盧 in the surname (preserved per CLAUDE.md). Author of the ***Jiē-nüè lùn shū*** 痎瘧論疏 ([[KR3e0088]], 1 juan) on malaria-class diseases, and the ***Běn-cǎo chéng-yǎ bàn jì*** 本草乘雅半偈 ([[KR3e0089]], 10 juan). Háng Shì-jùn's *Dào-gǔ-táng jí* contains a *Lú Zhīyí xiǎo zhuàn* 小傳, which records that Lú's earlier *Jīn-guì yàoluè mó-xiàng* 金匱要略模象 was burned by his father (regretting Lú's medical practice over Confucian scholarship); subsequent works include the *Bàn jì* (transmitted), *Mō-suǒ Jīn-guì* 摩索金匱 (9 juan, lost), *Shānghán jīn-bì chāo* 傷寒金鎞鈔 (lost), and *Yī nán xī yí* 醫難析疑 (lost). Háng's biography apparently does not mention this *Jiē-nüè lùn shū*, though the work survives in the SKQS recension.
