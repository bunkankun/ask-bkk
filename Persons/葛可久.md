# 葛可久 Gě Kějiǔ

Yuán-dynasty Sūzhōu physician (ca. 1305–1353), specialist in tuberculosis-spectrum pulmonary disorders. Author of *Shí yào shén shū* 十藥神書, the principal late-Yuán handbook on the treatment of *láozhài* 癆瘵; the work was later annotated by [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ as *Shí yào shén shū zhù jiě* 十藥神書註解.
