Féng Fú 馮黼, paternal great-uncle (*bózǔfù* 伯祖父) of [[馮椅]] Féng Yǐ. Per Féng Yǐ's auto-preface to *Hòuzhāi Yì xué* ([[KR1a0046]]), his name "*was famous in the three-academies for this canon [the *Yì*]*" — the immediate family-source of Féng Yǐ's *Yì*-scholarship.
