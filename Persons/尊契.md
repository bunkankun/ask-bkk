Zūnqì 尊契 (Jp. Sonkei) was a Tendai monk of Miidera 三井寺 who in 1507 recopied [[源信]] Genshin's *Jùshě Lùn Sòngshū Zhèngwén* ([[KR6l0043]], T2253) from the earlier transcript by [[清澊]] Seijun (1468). His manuscript is the immediate ancestor of the 1651 Keian woodblock edition that underlies the Taishō text.
