# Féng Fùjīng 馮復京

*Zì* Sìzōng 嗣宗. Native of Chángshú 常熟 (Hǎiyú 海虞, Sūzhōu, modern Jiāngsū). 1573–1622 (CBDB). Nephew of the better-known late-Míng official-scholar Féng Yīngjīng 馮應京 (1555–1606), with whom he is sometimes confused in the bibliographic tradition.

Author of the *Liù jiā Shī míngwù shū* 六家詩名物疏 in 55 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0039]]), an exhaustive catalog of the *Shī jīng*'s natural-history, ritual, and material vocabulary, building on Cài Biàn 蔡卞's earlier *Shī míngwù shū*. The work circulated under Jiāo Hóng 焦竑's preface dated *Wànlì yǐsì* (1605), which directly addresses Féng Fùjīng — settling the question of attribution against the rival *Sìkù tíyào* line that names Féng Yīngjīng instead. Modern scholarship overwhelmingly accepts the Féng Fùjīng attribution; the Sìkù WYG *tíyào* itself names Féng Fùjīng. The Kyoto Zinbun digital *tíyào*'s "明馮應京撰" is an editorial intrusion against the WYG copy.
