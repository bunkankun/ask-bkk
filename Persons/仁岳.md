Northern-Sòng Tiāntái master and the principal *shān-wài* 山外 (off-mountain) tradition figure of the Sòng *shān-jiā* / *shān-wài* polemic; *zì* **Jìng-jué** 淨覺 (also referenced as **Jìng-jué Rén-yuè** 淨覺仁岳, **Gū-shān Jìng-jué** 孤山淨覺). DILA Authority A000165. Born 992. Death-date uncertain; productive into the late Northern Sòng.

Originally a disciple of **Sì-míng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]]) at the Yán-qìng-sì 延慶寺, but subsequently broke with Zhīlǐ over doctrinal disputes and became one of the principal *shān-wài* spokespersons against the *shān-jiā* tradition. The *shān-wài* tradition rejected Zhīlǐ's *xìng-jù-è* doctrine and the strict identification of meditative practice with doctrinal classification, advocating instead for a more spiritualised reading of the Tiāntái apparatus that aligned with the Huāyán-school's *yī-zhēn fǎ-jiè* framework.

Major works (some preserved in the Manji-zoku canon): the **Shí bù-èr mén wén-xīn jiě** 十不二門文心解 ([[KR6d0162]], X56n0928, 1 *juan*) — Rényuè's *shān-wài* commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén*, providing the principal *shān-wài* alternative to Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐ-yào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]]); various other Tiāntái commentaries.

Note: distinct from the Northern-Sòng Línjì Chán master **Xīng-huà Rén-yuè** 興化仁岳 (DILA A012216, d. 1077–1078).
