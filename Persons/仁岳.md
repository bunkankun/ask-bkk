Northern-Sòng Tiāntái master and the principal *shānwài* 山外 (off-mountain) tradition figure of the Sòng *shānjiā* / *shānwài* polemic; *zì* **Jìngjué** 淨覺 (also referenced as **Jìngjué Rényuè** 淨覺仁岳, **Gūshān Jìngjué** 孤山淨覺). DILA Authority A000165. Born 992. Death-date uncertain; productive into the late Northern Sòng.

Originally a disciple of **Sìmíng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]]) at the Yánqìngsì 延慶寺, but subsequently broke with Zhīlǐ over doctrinal disputes and became one of the principal *shānwài* spokespersons against the *shānjiā* tradition. The *shānwài* tradition rejected Zhīlǐ's *xìngjùè* doctrine and the strict identification of meditative practice with doctrinal classification, advocating instead for a more spiritualised reading of the Tiāntái apparatus that aligned with the Huāyán-school's *yīzhēn fǎjiè* framework.

Major works (some preserved in the Manji-zoku canon): the **Shí bùèr mén wénxīn jiě** 十不二門文心解 ([[KR6d0162]], X56n0928, 1 *juan*) — Rényuè's *shānwài* commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén*, providing the principal *shānwài* alternative to Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐyào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]]); various other Tiāntái commentaries.

Note: distinct from the Northern-Sòng Línjì Chán master **Xīnghuà Rényuè** 興化仁岳 (DILA A012216, d. 1077–1078).
