**Zhōu Lǐ** 周禮 (Míng, fl. late 15th c.). Author of the *Fāmíng* 發明 (elucidations) appended to Shāng Lù's [[KR2o0022]] *Xù Zīzhì tōngjiàn gāngmù* 續資治通鑑綱目. Native place and biographical details not preserved; he was associated with the *Chénghuà*-Hóngzhì Wù-Zhē *Gāngmù*-school circle around Shāng Lù. CBDB has no datable record (the homonym CBDB ids 103929, 122686, 148823 etc. are all undated separate persons of the same name; none can be confidently identified with this commentator). The *Sìkù* tiyao on the *Xù biān* is highly critical of the *Fāmíng*'s "absurdities are too many to list," and the work has not been independently scholarly received.

The name 周禮 is also the title of the classic [[KR1d0001]] *Zhōulǐ* (Rites of Zhōu); references to "周禮" as a person should be carefully distinguished from the canonical work.
