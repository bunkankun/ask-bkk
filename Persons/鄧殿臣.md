Modern Chinese Theravāda specialist, professor at the Buddhist Academy of China (中國佛學院). Studied Sinhalese and Pali in Sri Lanka in the 1980s. Foremost late-20th-century Chinese translator working from Sinhalese-printed Pali sources; produced Chinese versions of *Khuddakapāṭha* [[KR6v0057]], *Udāna* [[KR6v0058]], *Mahā-Ummagga Jātaka* [[KR6v0059]], *Mahāsatipaṭṭhāna-sutta* [[KR6v0060]] (with [[趙桐]]), and several other canonical Pali texts, all included in *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 5. Also author of the *Fódà de qián-shēng* 佛陀的前生 (Beijing: Fǎ-yīn wén-kù, 1993), a Jātaka anthology in modern Chinese.
