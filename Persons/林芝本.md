**Lín Zhī-běn** 林芝本 (*fl.* mid-19th c.) — son of the Dān-yáng physician [[林佩琴]] Lín Pèi-qín. Carried his late father's manuscript of the *Lèi-zhèng zhì-cái* 類證治裁 ([[KR3er061]]) to the Sū-zhōu prefect [[桂超萬]] Guì Chāo-wàn in the winter of 1847, securing its first printing some eight years after his father's death.
