**Lín Zhīběn** 林芝本 (*fl.* mid-19th c.) — son of the Dānyáng physician [[林佩琴]] Lín Pèiqín. Carried his late father's manuscript of the *Lèizhèng zhìcái* 類證治裁 ([[KR3er061]]) to the Sūzhōu prefect [[桂超萬]] Guì Chāowàn in the winter of 1847, securing its first printing some eight years after his father's death.
