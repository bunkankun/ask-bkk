**Zhào Shànmíng** 趙善鳴, early-Qīng figure of the Shùnzhì era; wrote the 1657 (Shùnzhì 14, *dīngyǒu* 丁酉) preface to [[裴一中]] Péi Yīzhōng's original *Yányī* 言醫, the work later re-edited by [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng as [[KR3eq040]] *Yányī xuǎnpíng* (1851).
