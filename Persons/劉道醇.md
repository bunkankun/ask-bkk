Northern Sòng art historian; a man of Dàliáng 大梁 (Kāifēng). Active in the *Jiāyòu* 嘉祐 to *Yuányòu* 元祐 decades (c. 1050–c. 1090). Author of two complementary single-dynasty painting catalogues: [[KR3h0014]] *Wǔdài mínghuà bǔyí* 五代名畫補遺 (preface 1059), supplying twenty-four Five-Dynasties painters omitted from Hú Qiáo 胡嶠's lost *Liángcháo mínghuà lù*; and [[KR3h0015]] *Sòngcháo mínghuà píng* 宋朝名畫評 (compiled under Zhézōng, c. 1086–1100), the first comprehensive catalogue of Northern Sòng painters. Cháo Gōngwǔ's *Dúshū zhì* miscalls him Liú Dàochéng 劉道成; the correct form is preserved in Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí* and in the Sòng print Máo Jìn reproduced. Beyond these two works, his career is otherwise unrecorded.
