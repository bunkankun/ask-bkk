# Hán Lín 韓林

Eastern Hàn lay disciple of [[安世高]] Ān Shìgāo, named (with [[皮業]] Pí Yè and [[陳慧]] Chén Huì) in Kāng Sēnghuì's 康僧會 preface to the *Dà ānbān shǒuyì jīng* 大安般守意經 ([[KR6i0239]]) as one of three transmitters of the *ānāpānasmṛti* meditation lineage from Ān Shìgāo into the next generation. Lifedates unrecorded.
