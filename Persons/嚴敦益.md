Yán Dūn-yì 嚴敦益, *zì* Shòu-fǔ 受甫, *hào* Yú-yǐn 漁隱 ("the Fisher-Hermit"), Republican-period scholar-physician of Zhū-fāng 朱方 (Dān-tú / Zhèn-jiāng, Jiāngsū). His 1923 preface to [[KR3ep076]] *Wáng Jiǔ-fēng yī-àn (yī)* was written from Běijīng (都門 — the capital), where he had been residing with his fellow Dān-tú colleague **Wáng Shào-táng** 王紹棠. He compiled and edited the recovered Wáng Jiǔ-fēng casebooks ([[KR3ep076]], [[KR3ep077]]) from multiple manuscript sources. No further transmitted biographical record.
