Yán Dūnyì 嚴敦益, *zì* Shòufǔ 受甫, *hào* Yúyǐn 漁隱 ("the Fisher-Hermit"), Republican-period scholar-physician of Zhūfāng 朱方 (Dāntú / Zhènjiāng, Jiāngsū). His 1923 preface to [[KR3ep076]] *Wáng Jiǔfēng yīàn (yī)* was written from Běijīng (都門 — the capital), where he had been residing with his fellow Dāntú colleague **Wáng Shàotáng** 王紹棠. He compiled and edited the recovered Wáng Jiǔfēng casebooks ([[KR3ep076]], [[KR3ep077]]) from multiple manuscript sources. No further transmitted biographical record.
