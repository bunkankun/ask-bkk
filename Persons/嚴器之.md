**Yán Qìzhī** 嚴器之 (*fl.* mid-12th c., 金), friend and disciple of [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ, author of the principal supporting preface to Chéng's *Zhù jiě Shānghán lùn* 註解傷寒論 ([[KR3ef005]]), dated Shàoxīng 紹興 16 (1146). His preface is the chief biographical source on Chéng.
