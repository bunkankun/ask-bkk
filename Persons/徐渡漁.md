Xú Dù-yú 徐渡漁, late-Qīng physician of obscure biographical record. Author of [[KR3ep096]] *Xú Dù-yú xiānshēng yī-àn*, a single-*juǎn* casebook with clinical signature placing him within the late-Qīng Wáng Mèng-yīng-school *wēn-bìng* tradition. The use of the *xiān-shēng* (Master) form in the title indicates posthumous compilation by disciples or family. Not in CBDB.
