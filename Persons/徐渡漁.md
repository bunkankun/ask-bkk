Xú Dùyú 徐渡漁, late-Qīng physician of obscure biographical record. Author of [[KR3ep096]] *Xú Dùyú xiānshēng yīàn*, a single-*juǎn* casebook with clinical signature placing him within the late-Qīng Wáng Mèng-yīng-school *wēnbìng* tradition. The use of the *xiānshēng* (Master) form in the title indicates posthumous compilation by disciples or family. Not in CBDB.
