Zhèng Jì 鄭紀 (1433–1508), *zì* **Tíng-gāng** 廷綱, *bié-hào* **Dōng-yuán xiān-yóu rén** 東園仙逰人 ('Eastern-Garden Immortal-Wanderer'). Native of **Xiān-yóu** 仙游 (Pǔ-tián / Quán-zhōu vicinity, Fú-jiàn). Tiān-shùn *gēng-chén* (1460) *jìn-shì*; office reaching *Nán-jīng Hù-bù shàng-shū* 南京戶部尚書. After his Hàn-lín career, retired to *Píng-shān* 屏山 in his native place to read books for **20+ years**. Composed without forced thought, without revising drafts. Notable post-retirement works include the *Zēng-xiū xiāng-yuē* 增修鄉約 (an augmented community-compact, anticipating the early-16th-century *xiāng-yuē* revival of **Wáng Yáng-míng**). *Míng shǐ* j. 184. Surviving works: [[KR4e0119]] *Dōng-yuán wén-jí* (13 *juǎn*; recut by his ninth-generation descendant **Zhèng Yīng-liáng** 鄭英梁); also *Dōng-yuán yín-gǎo* 東園吟稿, *Guī-tián lù* 歸田錄, *Yì-jù jiā-fàn* 義聚家範, *Zēng-xiū xiāng-yuē* — separately catalogued. Pupil: **Wú Yǎn** 吳儼. CBDB id 34525 (1433–1508).
