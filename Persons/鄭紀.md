Zhèng Jì 鄭紀 (1433–1508), *zì* **Tínggāng** 廷綱, *biéhào* **Dōngyuán xiānyóu rén** 東園仙逰人 ('Eastern-Garden Immortal-Wanderer'). Native of **Xiānyóu** 仙游 (Pǔtián / Quánzhōu vicinity, Fújiàn). Tiānshùn *gēngchén* (1460) *jìnshì*; office reaching *Nánjīng Hùbù shàngshū* 南京戶部尚書. After his Hànlín career, retired to *Píngshān* 屏山 in his native place to read books for **20+ years**. Composed without forced thought, without revising drafts. Notable post-retirement works include the *Zēngxiū xiāngyuē* 增修鄉約 (an augmented community-compact, anticipating the early-16th-century *xiāngyuē* revival of **Wáng Yángmíng**). *Míng shǐ* j. 184. Surviving works: [[KR4e0119]] *Dōngyuán wénjí* (13 *juǎn*; recut by his ninth-generation descendant **Zhèng Yīngliáng** 鄭英梁); also *Dōngyuán yíngǎo* 東園吟稿, *Guītián lù* 歸田錄, *Yìjù jiāfàn* 義聚家範, *Zēngxiū xiāngyuē* — separately catalogued. Pupil: **Wú Yǎn** 吳儼. CBDB id 34525 (1433–1508).
