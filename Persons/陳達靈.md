Southern-Sòng *nèidān* 內丹 transmitter, *hào* Zǐyáng wēng 紫陽翁 ("the Old Man of Zǐyáng") — a contemporary of Wēng Bǎoguāng 翁葆光. According to the standard Wēng-Chén-Bóyúnzǐ lineage account preserved in *[[KR5a0142|DZ 141 Zǐyáng zhēnrén Wùzhēn piān zhùshū]]* and the appendix *[[KR5a0144|DZ 143 Sānchéng bìyào]]*, Chén Dálíng received the Wēng commentary on Zhāng Bóduān 張伯端's *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇 directly from Wēng and transmitted it to his disciple Bóyúnzǐ 白雲子, who in turn produced the preface dated 1204 (*Jiātài jiǎzǐ* 嘉泰甲子). Chén's own preface to the Wēng-commentary (preserved in [[KR5a0142|DZ 141]]) maintains, against the testimony of others, that Wēng had received his edition from Liú Yǒngnián 劉永年; this assertion appears to attach Wēng's transmission to a line distinct from the original Lù-family transmission of Zhāng Bóduān.
