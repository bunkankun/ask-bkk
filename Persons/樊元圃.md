**Fán Yuánpǔ** 樊元圃 (*fl.* late 18th c. 清), late-*Qiánlóng* literatus and in-law of [[吳煜]] Wú Yù; transmitter of the manuscript that became the *Nǚkē mìyào* 女科秘要 ([[KR3ei020]]) — Fán brought the manuscript to Wú Yù, asking him to collate it on the grounds that Wú was knowledgeable in pharmacopoeia.
