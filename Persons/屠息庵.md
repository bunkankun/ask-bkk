# 屠息庵

Tú Xī'ān

Early-Qīng lay friend of [[王起隆]] Wáng Qǐlóng. Circulated a *Vajracchedikā* commentary draft that prompted Wáng's 1655 reply-letter [[KR6c0072]] *Jīngāng jīng dàyì*. Possibly identical with [[屠垠]] Tú Yín of [[KR6c0058]], but identification uncertain.
