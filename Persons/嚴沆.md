# Yán Hàng 嚴沆 (1617–1678)

Early-Qīng Hangzhou literatus and disciple of the Chán master [[淨挺]] Liángtíng Jìngtǐng. *Jìnshì* of Shùnzhì 6 (1649). Author of biographical and literary writings preserving information about Jìngtǐng's *Yuèjīng shí'èrzhǒng* ([[KR6i0587]]).
