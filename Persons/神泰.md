Shéntài 神泰 (DILA A000987; fl. mid-7th century CE) was a Tang dynasty Buddhist monk and one of the inner-circle disciples of Xuanzang ([[玄奘]]). He served as *zhèng yì* 證義 (doctrinal proof-reader) on Xuanzang's translation projects, a senior scholarly role certifying the accuracy of technical Abhidharma terminology. He composed a commentary on Xuanzang's translation of the *Abhidharmakośa* that survives partially as 俱舍論疏 ([[KR6l0037]], X53n836, 7 surviving juan). His biographical details are otherwise unrecorded. Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6l0037]] 俱舍論疏.
