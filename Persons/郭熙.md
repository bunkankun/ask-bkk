The dominant court landscape painter of the mid- to late-Northern Sòng; *zì* Chúnfū 淳夫, a man of Wēnxiàn 溫縣 in Hénán. Lifedates traditionally given as c. 1020 – c. 1090. Began as a *dàoshì* of the *Jīngmíng* lineage; entered the *Túhuàyuàn* 圖畫院 under Rénzōng 仁宗 in the 1060s and rose to *Hànlín dàizhào zhícháng* 翰林待詔直長, the highest painting rank in the imperial academy. Painter of choice to Emperor Shénzōng 神宗 (r. 1067–1085); credited with the wall paintings of the *Yǎnhéguàn* 延和觀, the *Zhèngyángmén* 正陽門, the *Jǐngwéiguāng* 景靈宮 and other major Northern Sòng imperial sites — almost none of which survive. His principal extant works include *Zǎochūn tú* 早春圖 (*Zǎochūn*, 1072, Národní palace, Taipei) and *Línquán tú* 林泉圖. His landscape doctrine — preserved orally and edited by his son [[郭思]] (Guō Sī) as the [[KR3h0018]] *Línquán gāozhì jí* — is the founding theoretical document of the *LǐGuō* 李郭 landscape tradition; its *Sānyuǎn* 三遠 compositional formula (high / deep / level distance) is among the most influential single doctrines in Chinese art theory. Posthumously bestowed *Zhèngyì dàifū* 正議大夫.
