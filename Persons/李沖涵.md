# 李沖涵

Lǐ Chōnghán, mid-Míng official, colleague of [[齊一經]] Qí Yījīng in the Hénán censorial bureau; presented Qí with a manuscript of [[KR3em011]] *Yínhǎi jīngwēi* 銀海精微 — the proximate source of the earliest known printed edition.
