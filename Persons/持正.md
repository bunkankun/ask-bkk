# Chí-zhèng 持正

Yuán-dynasty Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[原妙]] Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào (1238–1296) during his seclusion at Xī-fēng Shī-guān on Tiān-mù-shān; recorder (*lù* 錄) of the transcripts that the lay disciple [[洪喬祖]] Hóng Qiáo-zǔ subsequently edited into the *Chán-yào* ([[KR6q0334]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
