# Chízhèng 持正

Yuán-dynasty Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[原妙]] Gāofēng Yuánmiào (1238–1296) during his seclusion at Xīfēng Shīguān on Tiānmùshān; recorder (*lù* 錄) of the transcripts that the lay disciple [[洪喬祖]] Hóng Qiáozǔ subsequently edited into the *Chányào* ([[KR6q0334]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
