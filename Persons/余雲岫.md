**Yú Yúnxiù** 余雲岫 (real name 余巖 Yú Yán, 1879–1954), Republican-era Western-trained physician and prominent anti-*Zhōngyī* polemicist. Author of the *Língsù shāngduì* 靈素商兌 (1917) and proponent of the 1929 motion before the Nationalist Ministry of Health to abolish the practice of traditional Chinese medicine — a proposal that catalysed the Republican-era institutional defence of *Zhōngyī* (cf. [[張贊臣]] Zhāng Zànchén, [[嚴蒼山]] Yán Cāngshān, and others).
