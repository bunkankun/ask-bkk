Cài Mèngbì 蔡夢弼, *zì* Fùqīng 傅卿 (so given in the *Dū-gōngbù cǎo-táng shī-jiān* 杜工部草堂詩箋 colophon), was a native of Jiàn'ān 建安 (modern Jiàn'ōu 建甌, Fújiàn). His lifedates and official posts are not preserved; the *Sìkù tíyào* writes "his beginning and end are unclear" (始末未詳). The conventional floruit of 1204 (catalog meta) derives from the preface and colophons in his great Dù Fǔ commentary, the *Cǎo-táng shī-jiān* 草堂詩箋, completed during the *Jiātài* 嘉泰 reign (1201–1204). CBDB has no entry. He is the author of two interconnected works: the *Cǎo-táng shī-jiān* (a fifty-*juǎn* variorum commentary on Dù Fǔ — long the standard Sòng Dù commentary; partial Sòng impressions survive in Japanese collections and were the basis of the modern reprint), and a derivative *shīhuà*, the *Cǎo-táng shīhuà* 草堂詩話 [[KR4i0040]] in two *juǎn*, also devoted entirely to Dù Fǔ. The latter draws heavily on Gě Lìfāng's 葛立方 *Yùnyǔ yáng-qiū* 韻語陽秋 ([[KR4i0028]]) and other Sòng critical writings. The *Cǎo-táng shī-jiān* was praised in the Yuán by Yú Jí 虞集 and in the Qīng by Wáng Shìzhēn 王士禎 (whose colophon survives) as one of the best Sòng Dù-commentaries.
