**Jìngguānglún Yīng chánshī** 靜光輪應禪師 (*fl.* late-Qīng), Chán Buddhist monk and physician. Credited as the *kǎodìng* (verified-and-fixed) editor of the *Tāichǎn xīnshū* 胎產新書 ([[KR3ei078]]), the work being *zuǎnjí* (compiled) by **Xuěyán chánshī** 雪巖禪師 ([[雪巖禪師]]). Both figures are part of the late-Qīng Chán-Buddhist popular-charity (*císhàn*) medical tradition — temple-based physician-monks who prepared obstetrical and other clinical manuals for free distribution as merit-accumulation works. Not in CBDB; not in DILA Buddhist person authority.
