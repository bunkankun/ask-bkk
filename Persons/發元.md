Fā-yuán 發元, *zì* **Bàn-shuǐ** 半水 ("Half-Water"); often referred to as **Sān-fēng Bàn-shuǐ** 三峰半水 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Dà-hǒu Yìn-chuán** 大吼印傳 — thus a *fǎ-sūn* of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) and dharma-nephew of **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] (1628–1701). Birth year unknown; died **康熙三十年辛未前七月初二日未時** = **1691-07-26**. Native of **Bā-xiàn** 巴縣 (Sì-chuān, Chóng-qìng region); lay surname **Yú** 余.

**Formation.** Took precepts (*shī-luó* 尸羅) under **Shí-gǔ** 石谷和尚 (a *fǎ-zhí* of Dé-yù — described in Dé-yù's preface as "吾姪"); received final *yìn-jì* under **Dà-hǒu Yìn-chuán** (described as "吾兄").

**Abbacies.** **Sān-fēng-sì** 三峰 (in Bā-xiàn region, Sì-chuān) and **Xìng-yún chán-yuàn** 興雲禪院 — recorded in the *yǔ-lù* opening at **康熙丙午冬** = **1666** winter receiving the lay-patron invitation from 雲海羅公 + 相廷朱公 to open 爐 at the same monastery.

**Disciples.** Eleven *fǎ-sì* listed in the *yìn-jì* of his *yǔ-lù*: **Shé-duān Guāng-cháng** 舌端光長, **Yuè-lǎng Guāng-jìng** 月朗光靜, **Tiān-mù Guāng-chǎng** 天目光昶, **Shuāng-fēng Guāng-ái** 霜峰光皚, **Wàn-zhú Guāng-yíng** 萬竹光盈, **Zhì-guān Guāng-dà** 至觀光大 (Jīn-líng-rén), **Xīng-rán Guāng-shēn** 惺然光深 [[光深]] (recorder of his *yǔ-lù*), **Bō-shēng Guāng-róng** 波生光榮 (Wàn-xiàn-rén), **Cāng-wú Guāng-huá** 蒼梧光華, **Yī-wěi Guāng-zhēn** 一葦光真, **Yún-kōng Guāng-liù** 雲空光六.

**Work.** [[KR6q0539]] 《三峰半水元禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B420, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xīng-rán Guāng-shēn** [[光深]] *děng biān*; preface (康熙辛未 = 1691 spring) by his uncle [[德玉]] (signed "華岩玉老僧"); engraved by his cousin [[道樂]] (法弟不厭代刻).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 13; 《三峰半水元禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈印偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 88.
