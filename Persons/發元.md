Fāyuán 發元, *zì* **Bànshuǐ** 半水 ("Half-Water"); often referred to as **Sānfēng Bànshuǐ** 三峰半水 from his abbacy. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Dàhǒu Yìnchuán** 大吼印傳 — thus a *fǎsūn* of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) and dharma-nephew of **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] (1628–1701). Birth year unknown; died **康熙三十年辛未前七月初二日未時** = **1691-07-26**. Native of **Bāxiàn** 巴縣 (Sìchuān, Chóngqìng region); lay surname **Yú** 余.

**Formation.** Took precepts (*shīluó* 尸羅) under **Shígǔ** 石谷和尚 (a *fǎzhí* of Déyù — described in Déyù's preface as "吾姪"); received final *yìnjì* under **Dàhǒu Yìnchuán** (described as "吾兄").

**Abbacies.** **Sānfēngsì** 三峰 (in Bāxiàn region, Sìchuān) and **Xìngyún chányuàn** 興雲禪院 — recorded in the *yǔlù* opening at **康熙丙午冬** = **1666** winter receiving the lay-patron invitation from 雲海羅公 + 相廷朱公 to open 爐 at the same monastery.

**Disciples.** Eleven *fǎsì* listed in the *yìnjì* of his *yǔlù*: **Shéduān Guāngcháng** 舌端光長, **Yuèlǎng Guāngjìng** 月朗光靜, **Tiānmù Guāngchǎng** 天目光昶, **Shuāngfēng Guāngái** 霜峰光皚, **Wànzhú Guāngyíng** 萬竹光盈, **Zhìguān Guāngdà** 至觀光大 (Jīnlíngrén), **Xīngrán Guāngshēn** 惺然光深 [[光深]] (recorder of his *yǔlù*), **Bōshēng Guāngróng** 波生光榮 (Wànxiànrén), **Cāngwú Guānghuá** 蒼梧光華, **Yīwěi Guāngzhēn** 一葦光真, **Yúnkōng Guāngliù** 雲空光六.

**Work.** [[KR6q0539]] 《三峰半水元禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B420, compiled by *fǎsì* **Xīngrán Guāngshēn** [[光深]] *děng biān*; preface (康熙辛未 = 1691 spring) by his uncle [[德玉]] (signed "華岩玉老僧"); engraved by his cousin [[道樂]] (法弟不厭代刻).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 13; 《三峰半水元禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈印偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 88.
