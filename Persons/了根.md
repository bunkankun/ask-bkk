# Liǎogēn 了根 (Qīng, fl. mid–late 18th c.)

A Qīng Pure Land monk of *Jué-hǎi-sì* 覺海寺 in Xiù-shuǐ 秀水 (Jiā-xīng 嘉興 prefecture, Zhèjiāng), known principally for his *Ē-mí-tuó jīng zhí-jiě zhèng-xíng* 阿彌陀經直解正行 [[KR6p0029]] (X22n0434), a brief commentary-with-practice-manual on the *Smaller Sukhāvatī-vyūma*. The colophon identifies him as 後學秀水覺海寺沙門了根纂註, with co-collators *Dá-zhì* 達智 and *Dá-chún* 達純* (his pupils, *fǎ-sì* 法嗣) and the Confucian-trained lay-Buddhists *Dá-běn Líng jūshì* 達本凌居士 and *Dá-qīng Chén jūshì* 達清陳居士. Lifedates not preserved.

His *zhí-jiě* genre indicates a popularising rather than scholastic register: the work serves the late-Qīng devotional movement of *cún-zhèng-xíng* 存正行 ("preserving the correct practice"). Otherwise undocumented in standard biographical literature; he should not be confused with the Yuán Pure Land master Liǎogēn 了根 of *Sì-míng* 四明.
