# Liǎogēn 了根 (Qīng, fl. mid–late 18th c.)

A Qīng Pure Land monk of *Juéhǎisì* 覺海寺 in Xiùshuǐ 秀水 (Jiāxīng 嘉興 prefecture, Zhèjiāng), known principally for his *Ēmítuó jīng zhíjiě zhèngxíng* 阿彌陀經直解正行 [[KR6p0029]] (X22n0434), a brief commentary-with-practice-manual on the *Smaller Sukhāvatīvyūma*. The colophon identifies him as 後學秀水覺海寺沙門了根纂註, with co-collators *Dázhì* 達智 and *Dáchún* 達純* (his pupils, *fǎsì* 法嗣) and the Confucian-trained lay-Buddhists *Dáběn Líng jūshì* 達本凌居士 and *Dáqīng Chén jūshì* 達清陳居士. Lifedates not preserved.

His *zhíjiě* genre indicates a popularising rather than scholastic register: the work serves the late-Qīng devotional movement of *cúnzhèngxíng* 存正行 ("preserving the correct practice"). Otherwise undocumented in standard biographical literature; he should not be confused with the Yuán Pure Land master Liǎogēn 了根 of *Sìmíng* 四明.
