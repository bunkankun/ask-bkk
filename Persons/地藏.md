# 地藏
Dìzàng (Sanskrit: Kṣitigarbha)

Mahāyāna bodhisattva of "Earth Treasury", patron of beings in the *mòfǎ* 末法 (latter dharma) age and savior of those bound for the lower destinies including hell. The principal Chinese scriptural witnesses are the *Daśacakra-kṣitigarbha-sūtra* ([[KR6h0014]] / [[KR6h0015]]) and the *Dìzàng běnyuàn jīng* ([[KR6h0016]]).
