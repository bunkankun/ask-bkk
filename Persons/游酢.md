**Yóu Zuò** 游酢 (1053–1123), *zì* Dìngfū 定夫, *hào* Zhìshān xiānshēng 廌山先生, of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of *Yuánfēng* 5 / 1082. One of the *Chéngmén sì xiānshēng* 程門四先生 (Four Masters of the Chéng School: Yáng Shí 楊時, Yóu Zuò, Lǚ Dàlín 呂大臨, Xiè Liángzuǒ 謝良佐). Famous for the *Lì xuě* 立雪 ("standing in snow") episode with Yáng Shí — they waited outside Chéng Yí's door in the snow until he awoke from meditation. Held office to *Jiānchá yùshǐ* in *Jiànzhōng jìngguó* 1 / 1101, eventually *Zhī Háozhōu* in *Xuānhé*. Author of *Yóu Zhìshān jí* 游廌山集 [[KR4d0114]] in 4 *juǎn* (incomplete recension). *Sòng shǐ* j. 428 (Dàoxué) has his biography. The catalog gives 1045–1115 but CBDB and standard references give 1053–1123 — followed here.
