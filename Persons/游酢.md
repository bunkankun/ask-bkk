**Yóu Zuò** 游酢 (1053–1123), *zì* Dìng-fū 定夫, *hào* Zhì-shān xiānshēng 廌山先生, of Jiàn-yáng 建陽 (Fú-jiàn). *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-fēng* 5 / 1082. One of the *Chéng-mén sì xiān-shēng* 程門四先生 (Four Masters of the Chéng School: Yáng Shí 楊時, Yóu Zuò, Lǚ Dà-lín 呂大臨, Xiè Liáng-zuǒ 謝良佐). Famous for the *Lì xuě* 立雪 ("standing in snow") episode with Yáng Shí — they waited outside Chéng Yí's door in the snow until he awoke from meditation. Held office to *Jiān-chá yù-shǐ* in *Jiàn-zhōng jìng-guó* 1 / 1101, eventually *Zhī Háo-zhōu* in *Xuān-hé*. Author of *Yóu Zhì-shān jí* 游廌山集 [[KR4d0114]] in 4 *juǎn* (incomplete recension). *Sòng shǐ* j. 428 (Dào-xué) has his biography. The catalog gives 1045–1115 but CBDB and standard references give 1053–1123 — followed here.
