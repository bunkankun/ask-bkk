# Zìqiān 自謙

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Chán-Vinaya monk; abbot of the Jiāshān 嘉山 monastery in Jīngjiāng 京江 (Zhènjiāng region, Jiāngsū). Ordaining teacher of [[書玉|Yíjié Shūyù]] 宜潔書玉 (1645–1722), the major Qīng Vinaya master and abbot of Zhāoqìngsì 昭慶寺. Lifedates unrecorded.

Sources: biographical preface of [[KR6q0210]] *Yíshān lǐ fó fā yuàn wén lüè shì*.
