Northern–Southern Sòng transition official and antiquarian; native of Shǔ (the Chéngdūfǔ region), active *fl.* 1111–1149. *Zì* Hǎodé 好德 (from the *Hóngfàn* 洪範). Compiled the *Xīshān tújīng* 西山圖經 and *Jiǔyù zhì* 九域志 in the early *Zhènghé* era and held the rank of *Chéngyì láng*; was promoted three offices on completing his Lúzhōu commission. After the *Jìngkāng* sack of 1126–27, he applied himself to recovering Northern-Sòng institutional documents and produced the *Sòngcháo Shìshí* 宋朝事實 ([[KR2m0004]])—originally 60 *juǎn*, of which 20 *juǎn* survive, reconstructed from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. He famously submitted a memorial to Qín Huì 秦檜 admonishing him to "in favour, think of peril" (*jū chǒng sī wēi* 居寵思危); Qín suppressed the second half of the *Sòngcháo Shìshí* in retaliation. He also wrote *Tōngjīn jí* 通今集 in 20 *juǎn*, listed in the *Sòngshǐ yìwénzhì* under *gùshì* and now lost.
