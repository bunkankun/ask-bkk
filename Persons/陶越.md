# Táo Yuè 陶越

Early-Qīng scholar and anthologist; co-editor (with [[曹溶]] Cáo Róng) of the *Xuéhǎi lèibiān* 學海類編, a major late-Míng / early-Qīng anthology of scholarly extracts including a recension of Qián Yǐ's *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhíjué* 小兒藥證直訣 ([[KR3ej020]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
