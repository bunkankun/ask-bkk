# Tú Wényī 屠文漪

Style name *Chúnzhōu* 蒓洲. Native of Sōngjiāng 松江 (in modern Shànghǎi). Birth and death years not securely recorded; flourished in the early-to-mid Kāngxī period (active c. 1680–1720).

A Kāngxī-period mathematical-pedagogical compiler, contemporary with [[杜知耕]] Dù Zhīgēng. His sole Sìkù-preserved work is the *Jiǔzhāng lùyào* 九章錄要 ([[KR3f0056]]) in 12 *juàn* — a re-organization of the *Jiǔzhāng* tradition with imported Western methods, parallel-and-comparable to Dù Zhīgēng's [[KR3f0050]] *Shùxué yào* but with different emphasis (Tú Wényī more thorough on *Gōugǔ*, less so on *Fāngtián*; more thorough on *Shǎoguǎng* edge-and-corner relations, less so on solid-geometry).

The Sìkù 提要 of [[KR3f0056]] notes that Tú Wényī's work introduces a method called *jièzhēng* 借徵 (borrowing-evidence), equivalent to the *jiècuī diéjiè* (borrowing-decreasing iterated-borrowing) method — a method not addressed in [[杜知耕]] Dù Zhīgēng's parallel work. The 提要: "*although [Tú Wényī's exposition of *jièzhēng*] is not extremely refined-and-dense, on the cleverness of borrowing-numbers [he] has fundamentally obtained the great essentials*".
