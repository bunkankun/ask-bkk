Jīn-dynasty Daoist, *hào* Tàixuánzǐ 太玄子 ("Master of Supreme Mystery"), resident on Mount Gūshè 姑射山 (Shānxī). Author of [[KR5a0124|Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註]] (DZ 124) — a nine-folio *Yīnfú jīng* commentary on the long-version text (c. 400 words) without paragraph subdivision. Hóu also wrote *[[KR5a1061|DZ 1061 Shàngqīng tàixuán jí]]* and other Daozang works; he is a figure of the Jīn-era Shānxī Daoist tradition. No CBDB record was found.
