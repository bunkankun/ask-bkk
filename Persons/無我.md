# Wú-wǒ 無我

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[惟一]] Liǎo-táng Wéi-yī; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0350]]). Lifedates unrecorded. (The name — *anātman*, "no-self" — is a common later-medieval Chán monastic style; this is the Yuán-period figure among Wéi-yī's editorial cohort.)
