# Wúwǒ 無我

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[惟一]] Liǎotáng Wéiyī; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0350]]). Lifedates unrecorded. (The name — *anātman*, "no-self" — is a common later-medieval Chán monastic style; this is the Yuán-period figure among Wéiyī's editorial cohort.)
