**Shěn Tāo** 沈翿, Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū) lyric poet of the late Qiánlóng — Jiāqìng era, twenty-year friend of [[計楠]] Jì Nán; wrote the 1804 postface to [[KR3eq010]] *Kèchén yīhuà*.
