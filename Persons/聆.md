# Líng 聆 (Lǎn-shí Líng 懶石聆)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, **34th-generation Línjì** in the **Mìyún → Pò-shān Hǎi-míng → Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì → Lǎn-shí** Sichuan Chán line. Principal dharma-heir of [[通醉|Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695, cf. [[KR6q0415]]); successor to Zhàng-xuě at **Hàn-zhōng Jìng-míng-sì** 漢中靜明寺 during his master's Tiān-tóng visit. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Líng** 聆, *hào* **Lǎn-shí** 懶石 ("Lazy-Stone"). Lay surname **Zhāng** 張, native of **Shǔ-Chóng-qìng Zhōng-zhōu** 蜀重慶忠州 (modern **Zhōng-xiàn**, Sichuan).

**Training.** Mother-dream omen: swallowing the moon at conception. **Age 12** dream-summoned to tonsure; **age 20** full ordination. First Chán-training under **Xiàng-yá héshàng** 象崖和尚 on the *wàn-fǎ guī-yī* *huà-tóu*. First awakening on sudden world-vacating experience. Sealed by Zhàng-xuě on the *nián-huā wēi-xiào* exchange after five years of service. Visited grand-teacher **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** and received his confirmation. Final formal transmission from Zhàng-xuě at Hàn-zhōng Jìng-míng-sì on the *Hán-yù cān Dà-diān* 韓愈參大顛 *gōng-àn* exchange.

**Three abbacies.** (1) **Hàn-zhōng-fǔ Jìng-míng-sì** 漢中府靜明寺 (primary abbacy, succeeded Zhàng-xuě c. 1655 during Tiān-tóng visit). (2) **Hàn-zhōng-fǔ Bǎo-lín-sì** 漢中府寶林寺. (3) **Yún-nán-fǔ Shāng-shān-sì** 雲南府商山寺 — a rare mid-century Chán Yún-nán frontier abbacy.

Birthdate and death-date not preserved in the *yǔlù*; active through 1659 (cutting-date of [[KR6q0432]]); probably died c. 1670s or later.

Work: [[KR6q0432]] 《懶石聆禪師語錄》 (4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B218, compiled by dharma-heirs [[海瑞]] Hǎi-ruì and [[真蘊]] Zhēn-yùn; two 1659 prefaces by **Liú Dào-kāi** 劉道開 and **Shī-bó** 施博).

**Historical significance.** Lǎn-shí's Shāng-shān-sì abbacy extends the Pò-shān / Zhàng-xuě sub-line's northern-and-northwest mission program (cf. [[KR6q0422]] Hé-nán / Shǎn-xī / Cháng-ān) southward into Yún-nán — the southwestern-frontier counterpart to the contemporary mission-sweeps.

Sources: [[KR6q0432]] juan 3 *xíng-yáo* 行繇 (self-biographical); juan 1 prefaces by Liú Dào-kāi (1659 summer) and Shī-bó (1659 Buddha's Birthday); *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 65.
