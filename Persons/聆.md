# Líng 聆 (Lǎnshí Líng 懶石聆)

Mid-seventeenth-century LínjìYángqí Chán master, **34th-generation Línjì** in the **Mìyún → Pòshān Hǎimíng → Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì → Lǎnshí** Sichuan Chán line. Principal dharma-heir of [[通醉|Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695, cf. [[KR6q0415]]); successor to Zhàngxuě at **Hànzhōng Jìngmíngsì** 漢中靜明寺 during his master's Tiāntóng visit. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Líng** 聆, *hào* **Lǎnshí** 懶石 ("Lazy-Stone"). Lay surname **Zhāng** 張, native of **ShǔChóngqìng Zhōngzhōu** 蜀重慶忠州 (modern **Zhōngxiàn**, Sichuan).

**Training.** Mother-dream omen: swallowing the moon at conception. **Age 12** dream-summoned to tonsure; **age 20** full ordination. First Chán-training under **Xiàngyá héshàng** 象崖和尚 on the *wànfǎ guīyī* *huàtóu*. First awakening on sudden world-vacating experience. Sealed by Zhàngxuě on the *niánhuā wēixiào* exchange after five years of service. Visited grand-teacher **Pòshān Hǎimíng** and received his confirmation. Final formal transmission from Zhàngxuě at Hànzhōng Jìngmíngsì on the *Hányù cān Dàdiān* 韓愈參大顛 *gōngàn* exchange.

**Three abbacies.** (1) **Hànzhōngfǔ Jìngmíngsì** 漢中府靜明寺 (primary abbacy, succeeded Zhàngxuě c. 1655 during Tiāntóng visit). (2) **Hànzhōngfǔ Bǎolínsì** 漢中府寶林寺. (3) **Yúnnánfǔ Shāngshānsì** 雲南府商山寺 — a rare mid-century Chán Yúnnán frontier abbacy.

Birthdate and death-date not preserved in the *yǔlù*; active through 1659 (cutting-date of [[KR6q0432]]); probably died c. 1670s or later.

Work: [[KR6q0432]] 《懶石聆禪師語錄》 (4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J28 B218, compiled by dharma-heirs [[海瑞]] Hǎiruì and [[真蘊]] Zhēnyùn; two 1659 prefaces by **Liú Dàokāi** 劉道開 and **Shībó** 施博).

**Historical significance.** Lǎnshí's Shāngshānsì abbacy extends the Pòshān / Zhàngxuě sub-line's northern-and-northwest mission program (cf. [[KR6q0422]] Hénán / Shǎnxī / Chángān) southward into Yúnnán — the southwestern-frontier counterpart to the contemporary mission-sweeps.

Sources: [[KR6q0432]] juan 3 *xíngyáo* 行繇 (self-biographical); juan 1 prefaces by Liú Dàokāi (1659 summer) and Shībó (1659 Buddha's Birthday); *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 65.
