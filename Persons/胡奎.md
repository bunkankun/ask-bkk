Hú Kuí 胡奎 (1331–1405), *zì* **Xū-bái** 虛白, *hào* **Dǒu-nán lǎo-rén** 斗南老人 ("Old Man of the South-of-the-Dipper" — a title with a legendary origin preserved in **Zhū Quán** 朱權's preface to his collection, involving a Daoist's *jué-jù* riddle that the *Sìkù* editors regard as a *hào-shì zhě* fabrication), native of **Hǎi-níng** 海寧 (Zhèjiāng). Born in Yuán Zhì-shùn era. As a youth a pupil of **Gòng Shī-tài** 貢師泰. In the early Míng summoned as a *rú-shì* and appointed *Níng-wáng-fǔ jiào-shòu* 寧王府教授 — household tutor at the Princely Establishment of Níng under [[朱權]] **Zhū Quán** 朱權 (1378–1448), Hóngwǔ's seventeenth son, one of the principal early-Míng literary patrons. His verse collection [[KR4e0067]] *Dǒu-nán lǎo-rén jí* 斗南老人集 in six *juǎn* (over 1,900 poems — one of the largest early-Míng single-author *bié-jí*) was cut by the **Níng-wáng-fǔ Wén-yīng-guǎn** 寧王府文英館 in the Hóng-wǔ era and survives via the **Kūn-shān Xú-shì Chuán-shì-lóu** *yǐng-chāo*; a separate four-*juǎn* manuscript by **Yáo Shòu** 姚綮 (Yún-dōng yì-shǐ 雲東逸史) passed through **Xiàng Yuán-biàn**'s 項元汴 *Tiān-lài-gé* and afterward through **Gāo Jī-gǔ-táng** 高氏稽古堂 and **Mǎ Sī-zàn** 馬思贊's libraries.
