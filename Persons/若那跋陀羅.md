Jñānabhadra (Ruò-nà-bá-tuó-luó 若那跋陀羅, "Wisdom-Worthy") was a Tang-period Indian translator. Per the *Kāiyuán shìjiào lù* (T2154), he produced the *[[KR6g0023|Dà bān niè pán jīng hòu fēn 大般涅槃經後分]]* (T0377, 2 fasc.) — the "Latter Section of the Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra," providing additional material continuing the Buddha's last discourses beyond what was available in *[[KR6g0001|Dharmakṣema's Northern Recension]]*. Birth and death dates not preserved.
