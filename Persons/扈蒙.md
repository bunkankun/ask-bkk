**Hù Méng** 扈蒙 (915–986) was a Northern-Sòng court official and scholar, a participant in the compilation of the **Tài píng guǎng jì** 太平廣記 and the **Tài píng yù lǎn** 太平御覽. Author of [[KR5c0365]] *Sòng Dōng Tài yī gōng bēi míng* 宋東太乙宮碑銘 (984) — a stele-inscription for the Eastern Tài yī Palace, one of the Sòng imperial Daoist temples dedicated to the Great One (Tài yī 太乙 / Tài yǐ 太一).
