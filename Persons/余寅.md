Yú Yín 余寅 (1519–1595; CBDB id 128361), *zì* Sēng-gǎo 僧杲, native of Yín 鄞 in Níng-bō 寧波 (Zhèjiāng). Mid-Míng *gǔ-xué* (ancient-learning) Confucian and scholar-official, *Wàn-lì gēng-chén* (1580) *jìnshì*. He served at the *Hàn-lín yuàn* and rose to *Tài-cháng sì qīng*. His scholarship was in the *gǔ-wén* (ancient-prose) tradition associated with Wáng Shì-zhēn 王世貞 and Lǐ Pān-lóng 李攀龍, who together are sometimes named the two leading mid-Míng *gǔ-xué* polymaths. His chief surviving work is the *Tóng-xìng-míng lù* 同姓名錄 ([[KR3k0047]]), the most comprehensive late-Míng compendium of historical homonyms — a successor to Liáng Yuán-dì's foundational *Gǔ-jīn tóng-xìng-míng lù*. The 12-*juan* form was completed shortly before his death; his fellow-townsman Zhōu Yīng-bīn 周應賓 added a supplementary *juan*.
