# Chéng Qǐ-yòng 程啟用

Late-Míng layman of Xīn-ān 新安 (modern Huīzhōu 徽州, southern Ānhuī). *Zì* Yòng-jiǔ-fǔ 用九甫; *hào* Mèng-shì 夢士 ("Dream-Gentleman"). Also referred to in the source text as Chéng Zhì-yòng 程智用 (with 智 / 啟 being alternate characters for the *zì*-initial syllable). Lifedates unrecorded.

Associate of [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] 蕅益智旭. Contributed the postface (*bá* 跋) to Zhìxù's *Pì xié jí* 闢邪集 [[KR6q0190]] and served as evaluator (*píng* 評) of the *Tiān xué chū zhēng* section. No further biographical material is preserved.
