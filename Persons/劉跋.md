# Liú Bá 劉跋

Late-Northern-Sòng official or layman of Héjiān 河間; author of one of the two Shàoshèng 2 (1095, twelfth lunar month) prefaces to the *Fǎyǎn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0058]]), prepared during the lifetime of Wǔzǔ [[法演]] Fǎyǎn.
