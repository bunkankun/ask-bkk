Jōkai 貞海 (Zhēnhǎi), Japanese Sanron 三論 monk of indeterminate date (late-medieval to early-modern; conventionally placed 14th–17th century). The DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000875 records his name only on the basis of T2301, with no securely recorded dates.

Author of [[KR6m0029]] *Sānlùn xuányì chāo* 三論玄義鈔 (T70n2301, 3 fasc.), a *chāo* 鈔 classroom-style sub-commentary on [[吉藏]] Jízàng's *Sānlùn xuányì* (T1852 = [[KR6m0026]]). He signed the work as "*Dúshī Zhēnhǎi héshàng* 讀師貞海和尚" — "Lecturer-Master Jōkaioshō" — confirming that he held the Tōdaiji 東大寺 Sanron academic rank of *dúshī* 讀師 ("lecturer"), the working-classroom rank below *yi-kō* 已講. Not the same as the homophonous Insei-period Tōdaiji Sanron master [[珍海]] Chinkai (1092–1152).
