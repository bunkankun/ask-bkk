# Jīn Wéndùn 金文淳

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīng-diàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as the *kǎozhèng* compiler for the *Zhōu shū* (KR2a0022) in the *Sìkù quánshū*. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
