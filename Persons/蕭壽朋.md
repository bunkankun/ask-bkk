Xiāo Shòupéng 蕭壽朋, mid-Southern-Sòng scholar of Lúlíng 廬陵 (modern Jí'ān 吉安, Jiāngxī); father of Xiāo Cháng 蕭常 (see [[蕭常]]). Disturbed by Chén Shòu 陳壽's recognition of Wèi 魏 as the legitimate inheritor of the Hàn 漢 in his *Sānguó zhì*, Xiāo Shòupéng began a project to revise the work on Xí Zuòchǐ 習鑿齒 lines (recognising ShǔHàn 蜀漢 instead) but died before completion. The project was taken up and brought to completion by his son as the *Xù HòuHànshū* 續後漢書 ([[KR2d0012]]). Not in CBDB with confident match; no other particulars known.
