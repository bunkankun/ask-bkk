# 尹慎徽

Yǐn Shènhuī, mid-Qīng Húnán physician, maternal grandfather and teacher of [[李紀方]] Lǐ Jìfāng; transmitted the internal-medicine, external-medicine, laryngology, and oncology training that underlies [[KR3em027]] *Báihóu quánshēng jí* 白喉全生集.
