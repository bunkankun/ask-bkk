The **third patriarch of the Chinese Yogācāra (Fǎxiàng / Cí'ēn) school** 法相宗 / 慈恩宗, in succession to **Xuánzàng** 玄奘, **Kuījī** 窺基 ([[窺基]]), and **Huìzhāo** 慧沼 ([[慧沼]]). DILA Authority A001260. Honorific **Púyáng dàshī** 濮陽大師 ("Great Master of Púyáng"). Native of **Sìzhōu** 泗州 (modern Jiāngsū). Born Yífèng 儀鳳 3 (= 678 CE); died Kāiyuán 21, 6th month, 21st day (= 9 August 733 CE), aged 56.

Took monastic vows in his youth at the Púyángsì 濮陽寺. Studied under **Huìzhāo** at the Dàyúnsì 大雲寺 in Zīzhōu and inherited the Cí'ēn Yogācāra doctrinal apparatus. After Huìzhāo's productive period at Cháng'ān, Zhìzhōu became the principal southern transmitter of Cí'ēn doctrine, teaching at Púyáng and Sìzhōu and consolidating the Cí'ēn scholastic curriculum for transmission to the Japanese Hossō 法相 school.

Major surviving works include subcommentaries on the principal Yogācāra texts: the **Chéngwéishí lùn yǎnmì** 成唯識論演秘 (T1833, alongside Huìzhāo's *Liǎoyì dēng*), the **Yīnmíng rù zhènglǐ lùn shū qiánjì** 因明入正理論疏前記 and **hòujì** 後記 (T1841, T1842), the **Dàshèng rù dào cìdì zhāng** 大乘入道次第章 (T1864), and the present **Fǎhuá jīng xuánzàn shèshì** 法華經玄贊攝釋 ([[KR6d0028]], X34n0636) — Zhìzhōu's textual condensation of Kuījī's *Xuánzàn*.

His Japanese disciples — most importantly **Genbō** 玄昉 (?–746) and **Dōshō** 道昭 (629–700, in fact already in China before Zhìzhōu's productive period) — carried the Cí'ēn doctrinal apparatus to Japan, where it became the basis of the Hossō school of Nara Buddhism.

Note: distinct from the SuíTáng Chán-school **Zhìzhōu** 智周 (DILA A006125, 556–622) of **Xiàpī** 下邳, and from the Southern-Sòng **Zhìzhōu** (DILA A009603), a dharma-heir of **Biéfēng Bǎoyìn** 別峯寶印 (1110–1191).
