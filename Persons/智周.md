The **third patriarch of the Chinese Yogācāra (Fǎxiàng / Cí'ēn) school** 法相宗 / 慈恩宗, in succession to **Xuánzàng** 玄奘, **Kuījī** 窺基 ([[窺基]]), and **Huìzhāo** 慧沼 ([[慧沼]]). DILA Authority A001260. Honorific **Pú-yáng dà-shī** 濮陽大師 ("Great Master of Pú-yáng"). Native of **Sì-zhōu** 泗州 (modern Jiāngsū). Born Yífèng 儀鳳 3 (= 678 CE); died Kāiyuán 21, 6th month, 21st day (= 9 August 733 CE), aged 56.

Took monastic vows in his youth at the Pú-yáng-sì 濮陽寺. Studied under **Huìzhāo** at the Dà-yún-sì 大雲寺 in Zī-zhōu and inherited the Cí'ēn Yogācāra doctrinal apparatus. After Huìzhāo's productive period at Cháng'ān, Zhì-zhōu became the principal southern transmitter of Cí'ēn doctrine, teaching at Pú-yáng and Sì-zhōu and consolidating the Cí'ēn scholastic curriculum for transmission to the Japanese Hossō 法相 school.

Major surviving works include subcommentaries on the principal Yogācāra texts: the **Chéng-wéishí lùn yǎn-mì** 成唯識論演秘 (T1833, alongside Huìzhāo's *Liǎo-yì dēng*), the **Yīn-míng rù zhèng-lǐ lùn shū qián-jì** 因明入正理論疏前記 and **hòu-jì** 後記 (T1841, T1842), the **Dà-shèng rù dào cì-dì zhāng** 大乘入道次第章 (T1864), and the present **Fǎhuá jīng xuán-zàn shè-shì** 法華經玄贊攝釋 ([[KR6d0028]], X34n0636) — Zhì-zhōu's textual condensation of Kuījī's *Xuán-zàn*.

His Japanese disciples — most importantly **Genbō** 玄昉 (?–746) and **Dōshō** 道昭 (629–700, in fact already in China before Zhì-zhōu's productive period) — carried the Cí'ēn doctrinal apparatus to Japan, where it became the basis of the Hossō school of Nara Buddhism.

Note: distinct from the Suí-Táng Chán-school **Zhì-zhōu** 智周 (DILA A006125, 556–622) of **Xià-pī** 下邳, and from the Southern-Sòng **Zhì-zhōu** (DILA A009603), a dharma-heir of **Bié-fēng Bǎo-yìn** 別峯寶印 (1110–1191).
