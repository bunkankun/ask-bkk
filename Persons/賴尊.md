**Raison** 賴尊 / 頼尊 (Heian or Kamakura period; lifedates unknown) — Japanese Esoteric (Shingon-tradition) scholar known principally as the author of the **Jīngāngdǐng jīng jì-shì** 金剛頂經偈釋 ([[KR6j0028]], T61n2224) — a verse-commentary (*jì-shì* = explanation of the *gāthās*) on the *Vajraśekhara-sūtra* ([[KR6j0024]], T18n0865). The work is a doctrinal exegesis of the verse-passages of the *Vajraśekhara* — one of the principal Esoteric scriptures — with characteristic Shingon-school doctrinal interpretation.

His other works are not preserved in the Buddhist canon. The DILA biographical information records only his Japanese provenance and his authorship of the *Jìshì*; no further dates or biographical data survive.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001827; Taishō No. 2224 colophon.
