# Sǐxīn Wùxīn 死心悟新

Northern-Sòng Línjì Huánglóng-line master (1043–1114). *Hào* Sǐxīn 死心 ("Dead Mind"). Dharma-heir of [[祖心|Huánglóng Zǔxīn]] 黃龍祖心. Third master of the Huánglóng sub-lineage as enumerated in the *Huánglóng sìjiā lù* 黃龍四家錄.
