# Xīlái Hào 西來灝

Late-Míng Chán monk at Lóngshānsì 龍山寺 (Fújiàn). Tonsuring teacher of [[如一|Jífēi Rúyī]] 即非如一 (1616–1671), who received first ordination under him at age 17. Lifedates and further biographical details unrecorded.
