# 中柱
Zhōngzhù

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist monk, disciple of [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù (1599–1655). Mentioned in the Edo-period preface to [[KR6i0099]] *Wéimó jīng wén shū* as having lamented with his master that [[智顗]] Zhīyǐ's 28-fascicle *Vimalakīrti* commentary was lost in China and preserved only in Japan. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded.
