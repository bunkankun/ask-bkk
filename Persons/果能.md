# Guǒnéng 果能

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk (also styled *dàorén* 道人, suggesting either monastic or lay-recluse status) active at *Xīxī* 西溪 in *Gǔ-Háng* 古杭 (Hangzhou), specifically at the locality of *Yùchí* 玉池 ("Jade Pond"). His full self-designation is **Gǔ-Háng Xīxī Yùchí dàorén** 古杭西溪玉池道人. He is the author of the **《時時好念佛》** *Shíshí hǎo niànfó* [[KR6p0119]] (X1200), a short Pure Land devotional tract dated by his own preface to the 7th month of *Guāngxù* 3 (= August 1877). The tract consists of accessible verse-pieces (*shíèr ge yuè niànfó jì* 十二個月念佛偈, *shíèr shí zhōng niànfó jì* 十二時中念佛偈, etc.) and pictorial *bǎotǎ tú* 寶塔圖 stūpa-diagrams designed for elementary lay-Buddhist readers. Lifedates not preserved.
