Jīn-lú 今盧 (DILA notes the variant 今廬). Cáodòng disciple of **Qiān-shān Shèng-rén Hán-kě** [[函可]] (1612–1660). Lifedates unknown. Together with **Jīn-yòu** [[今又]], the *zhòng-zǐ* (re-cutter / re-publisher) of [[KR6q0527]] 《千山剩人禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B407 — the colophon at juan 1 reads "門徒比丘 今盧 今又 重梓". The reissue was prepared at **Suì-chéng Huáng-huá-sì** 穗城黃華寺 (Guǎngzhōu) and printed in **康熙庚午** = **1690**, with a preface by their *fǎ-zhí* 法姪 [[今辯]]. Compare also DILA A008311 *Jīn-lú* 今廬 = a *dé-dù dìzǐ* of **Tiān-rán Hán-shì** 天然函昰 ([[函昰]]) recorded in the J38 B406 register, indicating possible identity or homonymity within the closely-knit Lǐng-nán Cáodòng cohort.
