Zhào Xióngzhào 趙熊詔 (1663–1721), *zì* Hóuchì 侯赤, *hào* Qiú'è 裘萼. Native of Wǔjìn 武進 (Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Zhuàngyuán* (top-ranked *jìnshì*) of Kāngxī 48 (1709). Hànlín *xiūzhuàn* 修撰 then *shìdú* 侍讀; his father was the senior official Zhào Shēnqiáo 趙申喬, *Hùbù shàngshū* 戶部尚書. The early Yōngzhèng-period investigation of the 1709 examination irregularities — in which it was alleged that Dài Mínhéng 戴名衡 (the actual high-scoring graduate) had been displaced in favour of Zhào by the action of Zhào's father — temporarily compromised his standing, but he was not formally degraded. CBDB id 84376.

Listed in [[KR2j0002]] *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* as a *jiào kān guān* 校勘官 on the strength of his then position as *rìjiǎngguān qǐjūzhù Hànlínyuàn shìdú* 日講官起居注翰林院侍讀. He worked on numerous late-Kāngxī imperial compilations.
