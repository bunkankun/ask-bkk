**Shǐ Sōng** 史崧 was a Southern-Sòng physician, responsible for the **standard received recension of the *Líng shū*** 靈樞 — second half of the foundational medical classic *Huáng dì nèi jīng*. His 1155 edition, with commentary, supplanted all earlier forms of the *Líng shū* and has been the standard ever since. Author of [[KR5d0042]] *Huáng dì Sù wèn Líng shū jí zhù* 黃帝素問靈樞集註.
