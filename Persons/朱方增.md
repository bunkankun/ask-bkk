# 朱方增

Zhū Fāngzēng of Hǎiyán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng), early-nineteenth-century literatus; supplied an 1809 (*Jiāqìng jǐsì*) preface to [[KR3em012]] *Yínhǎi zhǐnán* 銀海指南 recording how Gù Xī had cured his elder brother.
