# 許天錫 Xǔ Tiānxī

**Xǔ Tiānxī** 許天錫, Qīng Dàoguāng-era literatus, fellow-townsman of [[李守先]] Lǐ Shǒuxiān; wrote the 1847 preface to the illustrated reprint *Huìtú zhēnjiǔ yìxué* 繪圖針灸易學 ([[KR3ee012]]).
