**Yóu Zhòngrén** 尤仲仁 (late Míng – early Qīng), founder of the Liáng-xī 梁溪 (Wúxī 無錫) Yóu-clan school of laryngology (*hóu-kē* 喉科). No CBDB record. He is the conventionally named originator of the family-transmitted *Yóu-shì hóu-kē* tradition preserved as [[KR3em021]] *Yóu-shì hóu-kē* 尤氏喉科; the lineage was continued in print by his descendant or clan-relative [[尤乘]] Yóu Chéng in [[KR3em022]] *Yóu-shì hóu-kē mì-shū* 尤氏喉科秘書. Outside this hereditary medical lineage his biographical record is exiguous.
