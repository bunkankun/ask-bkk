Kǒng Zhìyuē 孔志約, *fl.* mid-7th century, was a Táng court physician-scholar and a member of the editorial committee for the *Xīnxiū běncǎo* ([[KR3ec004]]) of 659. He is best known as the author of its principal preface, a polished piece of court rhetoric that catalogues Táo Hóngjǐng's errors and credits Sū Jìng [[蘇敬]] with the original petition. He may have been a collateral descendant of the Kǒng clan of Qū​fù; the family connection is not securely documented, but his preface displays the polished classical register expected of Kǒng-family scholars.
