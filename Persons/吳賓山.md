# 吳賓山

Wú Bīnshān (recluse)

Mid-Míng recluse who transcribed the manuscript of [[覺連]] Juélián's recompiled *Xiāoshì Jīngāng jīng kēyí huìyào zhùjiě* ([[KR6c0055]]) prior to its 1551 printing.
