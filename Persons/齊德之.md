**Qí Dézhī** 齊德之 (*fl.* 元), Yuán-period imperial-medical-bureau official-physician with the title ***Yī xué bóshì chōng Yùyào yuàn wàikē Tàiyī*** 醫學博士充御藥院外科太醫 (Medical Erudite, Filling the Imperial Pharmacy's External-Medicine Imperial-Physician Position). Author of the ***Wàikē jīngyì*** 外科精義 ([[KR3e0064]], 2 juan; the SKQS *tíyào* opens "*1 juan*" — a slip in the SKQS text; catalog and present recension are 2 juan) — the principal Yuán-period external-medicine treatise. The work was wrongly appended to the *Dōngyuán shíshū* 東垣十書 (Ten Works of Dōngyuán) by Míng commercial-publishers — leading to the SKQS *tíyào*'s correction noting that Lǐ Gǎo had no external-medicine practice and that the *Wàikē jīngyì* is therefore a misappropriated work in the *Shíshū* corpus. Sūn Yīkuí's *Chìshuǐ yuánzhū* 赤水元珠 perpetuates the misattribution as "*Dōngyuán's* Wàikē jīngyì" — a serious error. Qí has no further transmitted biographical record.
