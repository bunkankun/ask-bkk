# Zàoxuán 造玄

Late-Tang Esoteric Buddhist monk and lineage-transmitter; resident at the **Cí-ēn-sì** 慈恩寺 of the Cháng-ān **Zuǒ-jiē** 左街 (left-ward), datable per his preface to **Xián-tōng 6.8.17 (8 September 865 CE)**. Honorific *ā-shé-lí* 阿闍梨 (*ācārya*).

His attested work is the **Tāi-jīn liǎng-jiè xuè-mài** 胎金兩界血脈 ([[KR6j0749]], X59 no. 1074) — a one-fascicle Esoteric *xuè-mài* 血脈 ("blood-line") lineage-record of the Garbhadhātu and Vajradhātu transmissions, composed (or *fù-shǔ* 付屬, "entrusted") on **Xián-tōng 6.8.17** (8 September 865 CE) at the Cí-ēn-sì. The opening colophon: *Dà Táng Xián-tōng liù nián bā yuè shí qī rì Cháng-ān-chéng zuǒ-jiē Cí-ēn-sì Zàoxuán ā-shé-lí fù-shǔ shī-zī xuè-mài* 大唐咸通六年八月十七日長安城左街慈恩寺造玄阿闍梨付屬師資血脈.

The work parallels [[海雲]]'s *Jīn-tāi liǎng-jiè shī-zī xiāng-chéng* ([[KR6j0748]]) as one of the principal late-Tang dual-maṇḍala lineage records and is an important source for the institutional history of the Tang Esoteric school. Per DILA A001813.
