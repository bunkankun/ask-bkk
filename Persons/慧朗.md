# Huìlǎng 慧朗

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[汝現]] and [[元寶]]) of *juan* 6–7 (*jìsòng* 偈頌) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
