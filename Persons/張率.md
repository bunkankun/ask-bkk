Zhāng Lǜ 張率 (475–527 CE; *zì* Shìjiǎn 士简) was a Liáng 梁 court official and literary figure from Wújùn 吳郡, a close associate of the Zhāomíng Tàizǐ 昭明太子 (Xiao Tong) literary circle. A lay Buddhist and literary drafter, he held senior literary court positions. Biography in *Liángshū* juǎn 33 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 40. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0064]].
