**Wú Táng** 吳瑭 (1758–1836), *zì* Pèihéng 配珩, *hào* Jūtōng 鞠通, was a Huái'ān 淮安 (Jiāngsū) physician and one of the foundational figures of the late-imperial *wēnbìng* 溫病 (warm-disease) school. His *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn* 溫病條辨 (6 juan, 1798) systematized the diagnostic framework of "*sānjiāo biànzhèng*" 三焦辨證 (syndrome-differentiation by upper, middle, and lower *jiāo*) and remains the locus classicus of the *wēnbìng* curriculum in modern TCM. He stands with [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì, [[薛雪]] Xuē Xuě, and Wáng Shìxióng 王士雄 as one of the "Four Greats of Warm-Disease Medicine" (溫病四大家).
