Xiè Wéi-xīn 謝維新 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* Qù-jiù 去咎, native of Jiàn-ān 建安 (modern Fú-jiàn). Late-Southern-Sòng *Jiāo-xiáng jìn-shì* 膠庠進士 — i.e. a graduate of the Imperial University (*Tài-xué*) who had not advanced through the regular *jìn-shì* examination. His career is otherwise unrecorded. His one surviving work is the *Gǔ-jīn hé-bì shì-lèi bèi-yào* 古今合璧事類備要 ([[KR3k0030]]), one of the largest late-Southern-Sòng *fāng-běn* compendia, commissioned by his friend Liú Dé-hēng 劉德亨 and published in five *jí* (366 *juan* total) culminating in the *Bǎo-yòu dīng-sì* (1257) printing. He was clearly a working professional compiler within the Jiàn-yáng / Jiàn-ān commercial publishing tradition.
