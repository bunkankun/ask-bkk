Xiè Wéixīn 謝維新 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* Qùjiù 去咎, native of Jiànān 建安 (modern Fújiàn). Late-Southern-Sòng *Jiāoxiáng jìnshì* 膠庠進士 — i.e. a graduate of the Imperial University (*Tàixué*) who had not advanced through the regular *jìnshì* examination. His career is otherwise unrecorded. His one surviving work is the *Gǔjīn hébì shìlèi bèiyào* 古今合璧事類備要 ([[KR3k0030]]), one of the largest late-Southern-Sòng *fāngběn* compendia, commissioned by his friend Liú Déhēng 劉德亨 and published in five *jí* (366 *juan* total) culminating in the *Bǎoyòu dīngsì* (1257) printing. He was clearly a working professional compiler within the Jiànyáng / Jiànān commercial publishing tradition.
