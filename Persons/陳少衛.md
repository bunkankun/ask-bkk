**Chén Shàowèi** 陳少衛 was an early-to-mid Táng Daoist alchemist, author of two paired alchemical instruction-texts — [[KR5c0288]] *Dà dòng liàn zhēn bǎo jīng xiū fú líng shā miào jué* and [[KR5c0289]] *Dà dòng liàn zhēn bǎo jīng jiǔ huán jīn dān miào jué*, both composed in the Kāi yuán 開元 reign (712–734) under Táng Xuán zōng. His works occupy an important place in the mid-Táng *wài dān* tradition, particularly on the preparation and "subduing" (*xiū fú* 修伏) of **numinous cinnabar** (*líng shā* 靈砂) and nine-cycle gold elixirs.
