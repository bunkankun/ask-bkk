**Fù Nánān** 傅南安, Qīng-period figure, younger brother of [[傅廉該]] Fù Liángāi (Provincial Censor of Qīnghé 清河); credited with bringing a manuscript copy of [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ's *Nǚkē yàozhǐ* 女科要旨 ([[KR3ei008]]) to circulation.
