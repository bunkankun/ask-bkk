# Wáng Péngshòu 王朋壽

Wáng Péngshòu 王朋壽 (fl. 1189), self-styled 魯老 (*Lǔ lǎo*, "old man of Lǔ"), native of Píngyáng 平陽 (modern Línfén 臨汾, Shǎnxī), Jīn dynasty literatus. He edited and greatly expanded the Táng-dynasty *Lèilín* 類林 by [[于立政]], producing the *Zēngguǎng fēnmén lèilín záshuo* 增廣分門類林雜說 ([[KR3k0080]]) in 15 *juan*, doubled from the original 50 categories to 100 and augmented with miscellaneous notes (*záshuo*). The preface he wrote is dated "大定己酉歲夏晦" (the intercalary-summer month of *Dàdìng* 29 = 1189 CE). He is not in CBDB. Píngyáng was the major woodblock-printing center of Jīn-dynasty northern China, and Wáng's work is typical of the region's editorial and publishing activity under the Jīn.
