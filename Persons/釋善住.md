Shì Shànzhù 釋善住 (lifedates uncertain; *fl. c.* 1290–1325), *zì* Wúzhù 無住, alternate *hào* Yúnwū 雲屋 ("Cloud-Chamber"), a Yuán-period Buddhist monk-poet of the Sūzhōu region. Once resident at the Bàoēn Temple 報恩寺 of Wújùn 吳郡 (Sūzhōu); much traveled on the Wúsōngjiāng. Internal poetic evidence (the *Guǐhàisuì yùjū Qiántáng Qiānqǐngsì shùhuái* of 1323) attests his book-study from 1294 (Zhìyuán 31). One of the principal Yuán *shīsēng*; close to Qiú Yuǎn [[仇遠]], Bái Tǐng [[白珽]], Yú Jí 虞集, and Sòng Wú 宋无. His sole surviving collection is the *Gǔxiǎng jí* [[KR4d0441]] in three *juàn*.
