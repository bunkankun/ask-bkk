Shì Shàn-zhù 釋善住 (lifedates uncertain; *fl. c.* 1290–1325), *zì* Wú-zhù 無住, alternate *hào* Yún-wū 雲屋 ("Cloud-Chamber"), a Yuán-period Buddhist monk-poet of the Sū-zhōu region. Once resident at the Bào-ēn Temple 報恩寺 of Wú-jùn 吳郡 (Sū-zhōu); much traveled on the Wú-sōng-jiāng. Internal poetic evidence (the *Guǐ-hài-suì yù-jū Qián-táng Qiān-qǐng-sì shù-huái* of 1323) attests his book-study from 1294 (Zhì-yuán 31). One of the principal Yuán *shī-sēng*; close to Qiú Yuǎn [[仇遠]], Bái Tǐng [[白珽]], Yú Jí 虞集, and Sòng Wú 宋无. His sole surviving collection is the *Gǔ-xiǎng jí* [[KR4d0441]] in three *juàn*.
