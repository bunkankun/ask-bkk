# Wèi Duānxiàn Wáng 魏端獻王

Northern Sòng imperial-clan figure (titled Prince of Wèi, posthumous title Duānxiàn 端獻); responsible for the Yuánfēng-era (1078–1085) imperial-patronage re-cutting of Yánshòu's *Zōngjìng lù* ([[KR6q0092]]).
