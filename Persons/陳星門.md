Chén Xīngmén 陳星門 (fl. mid-18th c.) was a Sūzhōu pupil of [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí who, with [[沈平舟]], preserved Yóu's manuscript of the *Jīnguì yì* ([[KR3eh003]]) until publication in 1768.
