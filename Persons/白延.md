Bái Yán 白延 (active mid-3rd c.), Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 period Buddhist translator at the Wèi court of Luòyáng. Per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 (T2145), he was one of the principal Three-Kingdoms-period Northern Chinese translators, contemporary with [[支謙]] Zhī Qiān in the Wú state. Author of [[KR6f0020|*Fó shuō xū lài jīng* 佛說須賴經]] (T0328) — the earliest Chinese version of the *Surata-paripṛcchā* — and several other shorter Mahāyāna translations. Birth and death dates not preserved.
