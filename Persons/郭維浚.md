Guō Wéi-jùn 郭維浚 — early-nineteenth-century Sūzhōu editor and physician active in the early-Dào-guāng era (c. 1820s–1830s), in the same Wú-zhōng medical-publishing milieu as Wú Jīn-shòu 吳金壽 [[吳金壽]]. He compiled [[KR3ep023]] *Méi-shòu-táng fāng-àn xuǎn-cún* 眉壽堂方案選存, a selection of Yè Tiān-shì 葉天士 ([[葉桂]]) cases drawn from manuscript sources outside the standard *Lín-zhèng zhǐ-nán* [[KR3ep010]] tradition.
