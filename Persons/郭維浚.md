Guō Wéijùn 郭維浚 — early-nineteenth-century Sūzhōu editor and physician active in the early-Dào-guāng era (c. 1820s–1830s), in the same Wúzhōng medical-publishing milieu as Wú Jīnshòu 吳金壽 [[吳金壽]]. He compiled [[KR3ep023]] *Méishòutáng fāngàn xuǎncún* 眉壽堂方案選存, a selection of Yè Tiānshì 葉天士 ([[葉桂]]) cases drawn from manuscript sources outside the standard *Línzhèng zhǐnán* [[KR3ep010]] tradition.
