Shī Chóu 施讐, Western Hàn *Yì*-master, pupil of [[田王孫]] Tián Wángsūn, founder of the Shī school of *Yì*-learning — one of the three principal Hàn *Yì*-lineages with [[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ and [[梁丘賀]] Liáng Qiūhè.
