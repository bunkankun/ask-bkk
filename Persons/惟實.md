# 惟實

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě ménrén* 侍者門人) of Yǎnxī Guǎngwén 偃溪廣聞 (1189–1263). Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0302]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
