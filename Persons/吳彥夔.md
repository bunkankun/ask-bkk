**Wú Yánkuí** 吳彥夔 (*hào* Zhuō'ān 拙菴, *fl.* Chúnxī geng-zǐ, 1180, 南宋), Southern-Sòng physician. Author of the ***Chuánxìn shìyòng fāng*** 傳信適用方 ([[KR3ed028]]) — a clinical formulary in 2 juan, dated to Chúnxī gēngzǐ (1180) per Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*. The work was originally titled *Chuánxìn shìyòng fāng* but transmitted in some sources as *Chuándào shìyòng fāng* 傳道適用方 (Mǎ Duānlín *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo*) — a transmissional error with 道 for 信. Each prescription is accompanied by a notation of its source-transmitter (傳自某人), making the work an unusual witness for Sòng-period prescription-circulation networks. The work appends 38 prescriptions for unusual conditions by Xià Zǐyì 夏子益. Wú Yánkuí has no other transmitted biographical record.
