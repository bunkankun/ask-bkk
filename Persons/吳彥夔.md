**Wú Yánkuí** 吳彥夔 (*hào* Zhuō'ān 拙菴, *fl.* Chún-xī geng-zǐ, 1180, 南宋), Southern-Sòng physician. Author of the ***Chuán-xìn shì-yòng fāng*** 傳信適用方 ([[KR3e0034]]) — a clinical formulary in 2 juan, dated to Chún-xī gēng-zǐ (1180) per Chén Zhèn-sūn's *Shū-lù jiětí*. The work was originally titled *Chuán-xìn shì-yòng fāng* but transmitted in some sources as *Chuán-dào shì-yòng fāng* 傳道適用方 (Mǎ Duānlín *Wénxiàn tōng-kǎo*) — a transmissional error with 道 for 信. Each prescription is accompanied by a notation of its source-transmitter (傳自某人), making the work an unusual witness for Sòng-period prescription-circulation networks. The work appends 38 prescriptions for unusual conditions by Xià Zǐ-yì 夏子益. Wú Yánkuí has no other transmitted biographical record.
