# Shěn Tàisù 沈太素

Late-Míng *Wànlì*-era official; *dà zhōngchéng* 大中丞 (Vice Censor-in-chief) of Dàliáng 大梁 (Kāifēng). Contributed *fèngjīn* 俸金 (one hundred *liǎng* of official-salary funds) to assist the printing of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng yòukē* 證治準繩·幼科 ([[KR3ej037]], 1607), as recorded in Wáng's self-preface. Full given name and lifedates unrecorded.
