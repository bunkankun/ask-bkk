Liáng Lán 梁蘭 (1343–1410), *zì* **Tíng-xiù** 庭秀 (alternate *zì* **Bù-yí** 不移), *hào* **Qí-lè** 畦樂, native of **Tài-hé** 泰和 (Jiāng-xī). Father of **Liáng Qián** 梁潛 (1366–1418), the Yǒng-lè-era *yòu zàn-shàn* 右贊善. Lived in retirement throughout his life and never held office; self-styled **Qí-lè** (Garden-Plot-Pleasure). **Yīn-jiā** 姻家 (in-law family) of [[楊士奇]] **Yáng Shì-qí** 楊士奇 (1366–1444) — Yáng studied verse with him directly. The teacher-pupil relationship Liáng → Yáng is one of the most consequential late-Yuán / early-Míng verse transmissions, since Yáng went on to dominate Yǒng-lè–Xuān-dé court literary culture. His verse collection [[KR4e0060]] *Qí-lè shī-jí* 畦樂詩集 was compiled and prefaced by Yáng Shì-qí in Hóngwǔ 31 (1398), six years before Liáng's death. The *Sìkù* editors place Liáng's verse explicitly in the **Táo Yuān-míng** / **Wéi Yīng-wù** pastoral-classical lineage. CBDB id 34477: 1343–1410, confirmed.
