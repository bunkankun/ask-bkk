Liáng Lán 梁蘭 (1343–1410), *zì* **Tíngxiù** 庭秀 (alternate *zì* **Bùyí** 不移), *hào* **Qílè** 畦樂, native of **Tàihé** 泰和 (Jiāngxī). Father of **Liáng Qián** 梁潛 (1366–1418), the Yǒng-lè-era *yòu zànshàn* 右贊善. Lived in retirement throughout his life and never held office; self-styled **Qílè** (Garden-Plot-Pleasure). **Yīnjiā** 姻家 (in-law family) of [[楊士奇]] **Yáng Shìqí** 楊士奇 (1366–1444) — Yáng studied verse with him directly. The teacher-pupil relationship Liáng → Yáng is one of the most consequential late-Yuán / early-Míng verse transmissions, since Yáng went on to dominate Yǒnglè–Xuāndé court literary culture. His verse collection [[KR4e0060]] *Qílè shījí* 畦樂詩集 was compiled and prefaced by Yáng Shìqí in Hóngwǔ 31 (1398), six years before Liáng's death. The *Sìkù* editors place Liáng's verse explicitly in the **Táo Yuānmíng** / **Wéi Yīngwù** pastoral-classical lineage. CBDB id 34477: 1343–1410, confirmed.
