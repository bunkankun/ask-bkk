Late-Southern-Sòng 南宋 scholar, kinsman or disciple of [[林希逸]] Lín Xīyì (c. 1193–1271). Author of the postface (dated **1260**) to Lín Xīyì's *Nán huá zhēn jīng kǒu yì* ([[KR5c0128]], DZ 745). The postface records the printing of the commentary and its dissemination.

**Relationship to Lín Xīyì.** The surname match (Lín 林) and the timing (postface dated 1260, within Lín Xīyì's active period) strongly suggest a family or lineage relationship — probably a **son or nephew** of Lín Xīyì, given the typical pattern of family-authored postfaces in Southern-Sòng scholarly publishing. However, no firm biographical information on Lín Jīngdé himself is preserved.

**Work.** The 1260 postface to DZ 745 is his only attested work.

**Dating.** Active 1260 (the postface date). No other chronological information.
