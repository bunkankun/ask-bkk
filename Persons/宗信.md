# Zōngxìn 宗信

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué during the Chángfú chányuàn 崇福禪院 abbacy at Chánglú 長蘆 (Zhēnzhōu); recorded as a section-compiler of juan 1 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
