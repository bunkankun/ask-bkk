# 李千戶 Lǐ Qiānhù

**Lǐ Qiānhù** 李千戶 (title-name "Lǐ the Chiliarch"), mid-Míng Liùhé 六合 acupuncturist; mentioned in [[汪機]] Wāng Jī's *Zhēnjiǔ wènduì* 針灸問對 ([[KR3ee016]], 1530) preface for the meticulous practice of measuring and ink-marking each point before needling.
