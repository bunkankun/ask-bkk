# Chén Zǐshān 陳紫山

Qīng pediatric physician; author of the pediatric *tuīná* monograph *Tuīná guǎngyì* 推拿廣意 ([[KR3ee043]]), one of the three principal Qīng pediatric-massage handbooks named by [[塗蔚生]] Tú Wèishēng in the preface to [[KR3ee053]].
