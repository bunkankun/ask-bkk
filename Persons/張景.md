Northern-Sòng official and disciple of Liǔ Kāi [[柳開]], *zì* Huìzhī 晦之, of *Jiānglíng* 江陵. *Jìnshì* of Xiánpíng 3 / 1000 — the same year his teacher Liǔ Kāi died. Held the *Tàicháng bóshì* and *Lǐbù lángzhōng* posts. Edited Liǔ Kāi's *Hédōng jí* [[KR4d0002]] in 15 *juǎn*, supplying the *xíngzhuàng* 行狀 (carried as the 1-*juǎn* appendix) and overseeing the first printed edition. CBDB 18342, lifedates 970–1018. (CBDB also lists a Táng-period 張景, id 13792, and a number of homonyms — the early-Sòng disciple of Liǔ Kāi is unambiguously CBDB 18342.)
