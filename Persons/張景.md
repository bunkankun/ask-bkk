Northern-Sòng official and disciple of Liǔ Kāi [[柳開]], *zì* Huì-zhī 晦之, of *Jiāng-líng* 江陵. *Jìn-shì* of Xián-píng 3 / 1000 — the same year his teacher Liǔ Kāi died. Held the *Tài-cháng bó-shì* and *Lǐ-bù láng-zhōng* posts. Edited Liǔ Kāi's *Hé-dōng jí* [[KR4d0002]] in 15 *juǎn*, supplying the *xíng-zhuàng* 行狀 (carried as the 1-*juǎn* appendix) and overseeing the first printed edition. CBDB 18342, lifedates 970–1018. (CBDB also lists a Táng-period 張景, id 13792, and a number of homonyms — the early-Sòng disciple of Liǔ Kāi is unambiguously CBDB 18342.)
