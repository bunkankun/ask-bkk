Dài Qúhēng 戴衢亨 (1755–1811), *zì* Liánshì 蓮士 / Liántīng 蓮汀, posthumously *Wénduān* 文端, was a late-Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng court official. *Zhuàng-yuán* (top *jìnshì*) of *Qiánlóng* 43 (1778). Rose to *Bǎo-hé-diàn dà-xué-shì*. Co-editor of the supplemented edition of the *Qīn dìng shòu shí tōng kǎo* (KR3d0010). Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo*.
