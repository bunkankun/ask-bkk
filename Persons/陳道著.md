**Chén Dàozhù** 陳道著 (*hào* Lú Biān-fēng 盧扁風, *fl.* mid-nineteenth century, 清), nephew (*yóu zǐ* 猶子) and disciple of [[陳念祖]] (Chén Niànzǔ / Chén Xiūyuán) of Cháng-lè 長樂 (Fú-jiàn). After his uncle's death in 1823 Chén Dàozhù continued the family's medical practice and pedagogical work at Fúzhōu, where he was known as one of the leading Fú-jiàn physicians of the Dàoguāng-Xiánfēng era. He is the principal continuator of Chén Xiūyuán's *Shānghán yī jué chuàn jiě* 傷寒醫訣串解 ([[KR3ef066]]) — completing the sixth (and final) chapter, which Chén Xiūyuán had left unwritten at his death — and preserved Xiūyuán's manuscript writings for transmission to the next generation. Lifedates not preserved.
