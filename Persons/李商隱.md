Lǐ Shāngyǐn 李商隱 (813–858, *zì* Yìshān 義山, *hào* Yùxī shēng 玉谿生 and Fánnán shēng 樊南生), of *Huáizhōu Hénèi* 懷州河內 (modern Hénán *Qìnyáng*), was *jìnshì* of *Kāichéng* 2 (837). His career was structurally entangled with the *NiúLǐ* factional struggle: he received early literary patronage from the *Niú* faction's **Lìnghú Chǔ** 令狐楚 (his *gǔwén* and *sìliù* parallel-prose teacher), then in *Kāichéng* 2 married the daughter of **Wáng Màoyuán** 王茂元 (a *Lǐ Déyù* faction adherent), permanently alienating **Lìnghú Tāo** 令狐綯 (Chǔ's son, leading *Niú* faction politician). His subsequent career was a sequence of mid-rank staff positions in regional military and gubernatorial offices; he ended as *Dōngchuān jiédù pànguān* and *jiǎnjiào Gōngbù lángzhōng*, dying at 46.

Lǐ is, with **Dù Mù** [[杜牧]], the supreme *WǎnTáng* poet — the *LǐDù* (or *WēnLǐ*, with **Wēn Tíngyún** [[溫庭筠]]) late-Táng pairing. His verse — including the *Jǐn sè* 錦瑟, the *Wútí* 無題 series, *Mǎwéi èr shǒu*, *Lè yóu yuán*, *Jiǎshēng*, and *Bì chéng sān shǒu* — is the canonical exemplar of the dense, allusive, polysemous late-style. The Sòng-period *Xīkūn tǐ* 西崑體 took Lǐ as its model. Lǐ also wrote the *Lǐ Chángjí xiǎozhuàn* (the small biography of [[李賀]] appended to the *Chānggǔ jí* = [[KR4c0060]]), and major *sìliù* parallel-prose works of which only fragments survive (the bulk of his prose corpus, originally 80+ *juǎn*, is now lost).

Principal works in the corpus: *Lǐ Yìshān shī jí* [[KR4c0074]] (3 *juǎn*, the canonical verse collection); *Lǐ Yìshān shī jí zhù* [[KR4c0075]] (Zhū Hèlíng's [[朱鶴齡]] foundational Qīng commentary on the verse); *Lǐ Yìshān wénjí jiānzhù* [[KR4c0076]] (the Xú Shùgǔ + Xú Jiǒng Qīng prose-recovery edition). Also the appended biography of Lǐ Hè in [[KR4c0060]]. CBDB id 24412.
