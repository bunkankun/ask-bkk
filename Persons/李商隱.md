Lǐ Shāng-yǐn 李商隱 (813–858, *zì* Yì-shān 義山, *hào* Yù-xī shēng 玉谿生 and Fán-nán shēng 樊南生), of *Huái-zhōu Hé-nèi* 懷州河內 (modern Hé-nán *Qìn-yáng*), was *jìn-shì* of *Kāi-chéng* 2 (837). His career was structurally entangled with the *Niú-Lǐ* factional struggle: he received early literary patronage from the *Niú* faction's **Lìng-hú Chǔ** 令狐楚 (his *gǔ-wén* and *sì-liù* parallel-prose teacher), then in *Kāi-chéng* 2 married the daughter of **Wáng Mào-yuán** 王茂元 (a *Lǐ Dé-yù* faction adherent), permanently alienating **Lìng-hú Tāo** 令狐綯 (Chǔ's son, leading *Niú* faction politician). His subsequent career was a sequence of mid-rank staff positions in regional military and gubernatorial offices; he ended as *Dōng-chuān jié-dù pàn-guān* and *jiǎn-jiào Gōng-bù láng-zhōng*, dying at 46.

Lǐ is, with **Dù Mù** [[杜牧]], the supreme *Wǎn-Táng* poet — the *Lǐ-Dù* (or *Wēn-Lǐ*, with **Wēn Tíng-yún** [[溫庭筠]]) late-Táng pairing. His verse — including the *Jǐn sè* 錦瑟, the *Wú-tí* 無題 series, *Mǎ-wéi èr shǒu*, *Lè yóu yuán*, *Jiǎ-shēng*, and *Bì chéng sān shǒu* — is the canonical exemplar of the dense, allusive, polysemous late-style. The Sòng-period *Xī-kūn tǐ* 西崑體 took Lǐ as its model. Lǐ also wrote the *Lǐ Cháng-jí xiǎo-zhuàn* (the small biography of [[李賀]] appended to the *Chāng-gǔ jí* = [[KR4c0060]]), and major *sì-liù* parallel-prose works of which only fragments survive (the bulk of his prose corpus, originally 80+ *juǎn*, is now lost).

Principal works in the corpus: *Lǐ Yì-shān shī jí* [[KR4c0074]] (3 *juǎn*, the canonical verse collection); *Lǐ Yì-shān shī jí zhù* [[KR4c0075]] (Zhū Hè-líng's [[朱鶴齡]] foundational Qīng commentary on the verse); *Lǐ Yì-shān wén-jí jiān-zhù* [[KR4c0076]] (the Xú Shù-gǔ + Xú Jiǒng Qīng prose-recovery edition). Also the appended biography of Lǐ Hè in [[KR4c0060]]. CBDB id 24412.
