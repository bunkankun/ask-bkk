Yè Fēng 葉封 (1623–1687), zì Jǐngshū 井叔, of Huángzhōu 黃州 (modern Huánggāng, Húběi), was an early-Qing official and *jīnshí* scholar. *Jìnshì* of Shùnzhì *jǐhài* (1659), he served as magistrate of Dēngfēng 登封 (Hénán, in the southern Sōngshān 嵩山 region) during the Kāngxī *guǐchǒu* (1673), where he compiled his *Sōngyáng shíkè jíjì* 嵩陽石刻集記 [[KR2n0041]] (the work's title takes "*Sōngyáng*" because Dēngfēng lies south of Sōngshān). He rose to *gōngbù Yúhéng-sī zhǔshì* 工部虞衡司主事 (Manager in the *Yúhéng* Bureau of Public Works). Wáng Shìzhēn's 王士禎 *Cánwěi jí* 蠶尾集 commemorates him as "skilled in *Ěryǎ* and *Shuōwén* glosses, expert in *zhuàn-lì*" and contemporaries compared him to Liú Yuánfǔ 劉原父 and Xuē Shànggōng 薛尚功. He also compiled the 21-juan *Sōngshān zhì* 嵩山志 (gazetteer of Mt. Sōng). CBDB 73061 confirms 1623–1687.
