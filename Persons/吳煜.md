**Wú Yù** 吳煜 of *Cháchá* 茶垞 (*fl.* 1793), late-*Qiánlóng* literatus and pharmacopoeia-knowledgeable layman; transcriber-collator and author of the second preface (dated *Qiánlóng* 58, 17th day of the 10th lunar month = late 1793) of the *Nǚkē mìyào* 女科秘要 ([[KR3ei020]]) — one of the privately-transmitted late-*Qiánlóng* editions of the Xiāoshān Zhúlínsì gynecological corpus. The preface was written at the *Qīnghuī shūwū* 清暉書屋 (Hall of Pure Brightness Studio).
