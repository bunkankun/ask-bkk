# Běnzhēn 本真

Yuán Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]), where he is named in *juan* 1. DILA records the teacher-student relation explicitly (A009687 → A000254). Lifedates unrecorded.

A separate LínjìYángqí Chán master named **Běnzhēn** 本真, *zì* **Xiànglín** 象林; often referred to as **Xiànglín Běnzhēn** 象林本真 (DILA A016469). Born **1620** (estimated); died **康熙辛未夏** = **1691** summer, age 72. Native of **Suízhōu** 隨州 (Húběi).

**Abbacies (four).** The principal seat was **Qízhōu Zǐyúnshān Báiyún chánsì** 蘄州紫雲山白雲禪寺 (entered **順治甲午仲冬初** = **1654-12**).

**Disciple.** **Xǐxīn Zhàoshuǐ** 洗心照水 [[照水]] (b. ~1654), sole *fǎsì* — received *fùfǎ* in **癸亥春** = **1683** at age 30.

**Work.** [[KR6q0571]] 《白雲象林本真禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B457, *zhòngzǐ* (re-engraved) by *fǎsì* [[照水]]; preface by lay disciple **Miàoxīn** [[妙心]] dated **順治巳亥歲佛歡喜日** = **1659-08-30** (Yúlánpén day).

Sources: 《白雲象林本真禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉.
