# Běn-zhēn 本真

Yuán Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]), where he is named in *juan* 1. DILA records the teacher-student relation explicitly (A009687 → A000254). Lifedates unrecorded.

A separate Línjì-Yángqí Chán master named **Běn-zhēn** 本真, *zì* **Xiàng-lín** 象林; often referred to as **Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** 象林本真 (DILA A016469). Born **1620** (estimated); died **康熙辛未夏** = **1691** summer, age 72. Native of **Suí-zhōu** 隨州 (Hú-běi).

**Abbacies (four).** The principal seat was **Qí-zhōu Zǐ-yún-shān Bái-yún chán-sì** 蘄州紫雲山白雲禪寺 (entered **順治甲午仲冬初** = **1654-12**).

**Disciple.** **Xǐ-xīn Zhào-shuǐ** 洗心照水 [[照水]] (b. ~1654), sole *fǎ-sì* — received *fù-fǎ* in **癸亥春** = **1683** at age 30.

**Work.** [[KR6q0571]] 《白雲象林本真禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B457, *zhòng-zǐ* (re-engraved) by *fǎ-sì* [[照水]]; preface by lay disciple **Miào-xīn** [[妙心]] dated **順治巳亥歲佛歡喜日** = **1659-08-30** (Yú-lán-pén day).

Sources: 《白雲象林本真禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉.
