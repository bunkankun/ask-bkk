**Wāng Rǔmào** 汪汝懋 (*zì* Yǐjìng 以敬, 1308–1369), Yuán-Míng transition civil official from Tóngjiāng 桐江 / Tónglú 桐廬 prefecture in Zhèjiāng. Served as district magistrate in his home region during the *Zhìzhèng* era; reputation for benevolent administration with particular attention to the *yǎnglǎo cíyòu* (elder-care and child-rearing) infrastructure of the prefectural community. His sole transmitted work is the *Shānjū sìyào* 山居四要 ([[KR3eo013]]) — an expansion of an earlier compendium by Yáng Yuánchéng 楊元誠 (former Director of the Astronomical Service, 太史令, who had earlier served as prefect of Wāng's home district). Completed in *Zhìzhèng* 20 = 1360, with preface by Liú Rénběn 劉仁本 of Tiāntái. CBDB 103663.
