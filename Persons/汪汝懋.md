**Wāng Rǔmào** 汪汝懋 (*zì* Yǐ-jìng 以敬, 1308–1369), Yuán-Míng transition civil official from Tóng-jiāng 桐江 / Tóng-lú 桐廬 prefecture in Zhèjiāng. Served as district magistrate in his home region during the *Zhì-zhèng* era; reputation for benevolent administration with particular attention to the *yǎng-lǎo cí-yòu* (elder-care and child-rearing) infrastructure of the prefectural community. His sole transmitted work is the *Shān-jū sì-yào* 山居四要 ([[KR3eo013]]) — an expansion of an earlier compendium by Yáng Yuán-chéng 楊元誠 (former Director of the Astronomical Service, 太史令, who had earlier served as prefect of Wāng's home district). Completed in *Zhì-zhèng* 20 = 1360, with preface by Liú Rén-běn 劉仁本 of Tiān-tái. CBDB 103663.
