# Qí Jùn-jiā 祁駿佳

Late-Míng Shào-xīng lay literatus and Chán-circle disciple of [[圓澄]] Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng (1561–1627); co-editor (*biān* 編), with [[丁元公]] Dīng Yuán-gōng, of his master's eight-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0374]]). A relative of the Shào-xīng Qí family of officials and literati — possibly a son of the Míng loyalist martyr Qí Biāo-jiā 祁彪佳 (1602–1645) — but the direct genealogical identification is uncertain; the KR6q catalogue's "祁駿隹" is a graphic variant for 佳. Lifedates unrecorded.
