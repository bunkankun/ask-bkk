Dài Fùgǔ 戴復古 (c. 1167–c. 1248, *zì* Shìzhī 式之, *hào* Shípíng 石屏), of *Huángyán* 黃巖 in *Tāizhōu* 台州 (modern Zhèjiāng). Senior figure of the late-Southern-Sòng *Jiānghúpài* 江湖派 poetic movement. Pupil of Lù Yóu [[陸游]]; never held office; itinerant poet who roamed *Ōu-Mǐn* (Fújiàn), *Wú-Yuè* (Jiāng-Zhè), and northward to the *Huái-Sì*. Deliberately modeled on Dù Fǔ rather than the *Jiāngxīpài* exemplar Huáng Tíngjiān.
