**Jīn Zhúpō** 金竹坡 was a Sòng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0323]] *Dà dān qiān gǒng lùn* 大丹鉛汞論 ("Discourse on the Lead-and-Mercury of the Great Elixir"). Biographical details are unrecorded; the work is 11th century or later.
