Contemporary Chinese specialist of Dūnhuáng popular-Buddhist manuscripts, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of the five-recension *Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng* 佛說相好經 cluster ([[KR6v0038]]–[[KR6v0042]]) — the *guān-fó sānmèi* visualisation-recitation manual carved out of the *Fó shuō Guān-fó sānmèi-hǎi jīng* (T15n0643).
