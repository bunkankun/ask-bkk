**Luó Xū** 羅頊, Southern-Sòng official (*cháofèng dàfū quán fāqiǎn Yǐngzhōu* 朝奉大夫權發遣郢州), wrote the *jǐyǒu* preface (likely 1249) to [[張杲]] Zhāng Gǎo's [[KR3eq024]] *Yī shuō*; the preface develops the famous "medicine is like war" metaphor and is the principal early external witness for the Zhāng family's three-generation Xīnān medical lineage.
