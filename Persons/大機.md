# Dàjī 大機

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]), where he is identified as abbot of Húzhōulù Xiǎncí chánsì 湖州路顯慈禪寺 and heads the editorial line for the third juan. Lifedates unrecorded.
