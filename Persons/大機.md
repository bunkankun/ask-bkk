# Dà-jī 大機

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]), where he is identified as abbot of Hú-zhōu-lù Xiǎn-cí chán-sì 湖州路顯慈禪寺 and heads the editorial line for the third juan. Lifedates unrecorded.
