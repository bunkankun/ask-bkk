**Zhào Yǐdé** 趙以德 (*zì* Liángrén 良仁, *hào* Yúnjū 雲居, *fl.* late Yuán / early Míng), YuánMíng transitional physician of Pújiāng 浦江 (Zhèjiāng), best known as the principal early *Jīnguì yào lüè* commentator. A student of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī, Zhào is the author of the *Jīnguì yùhán jīng yǎn yì* 金匱玉函經衍義 (Amplification of the *Jīnguì yùhán jīng*) — the first sustained commentary on the *Jīnguì yào lüè* and one of the principal vehicles for the transmission of Zhū Dānxī's clinical doctrine in *Jīnguì* exegesis. The work circulated independently until the late seventeenth century, when [[周揚俊]] (Zhōu Yángjùn) supplemented Zhào's commentary with additional annotations and produced the **二註** 二註 ("Two Annotators") edition ([[KR3ef083]], 1687) — the form in which Zhào's commentary is now principally transmitted. Detailed lifedates are not preserved.
