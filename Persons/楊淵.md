Yáng Yuān 楊淵, *zì* Zǐ-ān 子安 (b. 1813, died after 1881). Late-Qīng physician active across the entire mid-to-late 19th century. Author of [[KR3eq095]] *Shòu-shān bǐ-jì* 壽山筆記, whose self-preface he signed in 1881 at the age of 69. He explicitly positions himself in the *bǐ-jì*-medical lineage of Miù Xī-yōng (the *Sōng-xīn bǐ-jì*) and Yóu Yí (the *Yī-xué dú-shū jì*). Distinctive for integrating the forensic *Xǐ-yuān lù* 洗冤錄 morphology of blood clots into the gynaecological differential of *tāi-lòu* / *tāi-xià*. Not in CBDB.
