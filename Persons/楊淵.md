Yáng Yuān 楊淵, *zì* Zǐān 子安 (b. 1813, died after 1881). Late-Qīng physician active across the entire mid-to-late 19th century. Author of [[KR3eq095]] *Shòushān bǐjì* 壽山筆記, whose self-preface he signed in 1881 at the age of 69. He explicitly positions himself in the *bǐjì*-medical lineage of Miù Xīyōng (the *Sōngxīn bǐjì*) and Yóu Yí (the *Yīxué dúshū jì*). Distinctive for integrating the forensic *Xǐyuān lù* 洗冤錄 morphology of blood clots into the gynaecological differential of *tāilòu* / *tāixià*. Not in CBDB.
