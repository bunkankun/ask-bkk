**Lín Mào-jǔ** 林懋舉, Míng-period official of Fújiàn (*Mǐn* 閩). *Jìn-shì* of an unidentified cohort. Served as *Gōng-kē jǐ-shì-zhōng* 工科給事中 (Supervising Secretary of the Office of Scrutiny for Works) and *zhī Sū-zhōu fǔ shì* 知蘇州府事 (Prefect of Sū-zhōu), bearing the *zhōng-xiàn dà-fū* 中憲大夫 honorific (a 4th-grade civil rank). In 1556 (*Jiā-jìng* 35), as Sū-zhōu prefect, he prefaced and funded the printing of Xuē Kǎi / Xuē Jǐ's paediatric compendium *Bǎo-yīng cuō-yào* 保嬰撮要 ([[KR3ej015]]). His preface is one of the principal contemporary documentary witnesses to Xuē Jǐ's working practice as a scholar-physician.
