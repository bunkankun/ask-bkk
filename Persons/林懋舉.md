**Lín Màojǔ** 林懋舉, Míng-period official of Fújiàn (*Mǐn* 閩). *Jìnshì* of an unidentified cohort. Served as *Gōngkē jǐshìzhōng* 工科給事中 (Supervising Secretary of the Office of Scrutiny for Works) and *zhī Sūzhōu fǔ shì* 知蘇州府事 (Prefect of Sūzhōu), bearing the *zhōngxiàn dàfū* 中憲大夫 honorific (a 4th-grade civil rank). In 1556 (*Jiājìng* 35), as Sūzhōu prefect, he prefaced and funded the printing of Xuē Kǎi / Xuē Jǐ's paediatric compendium *Bǎoyīng cuōyào* 保嬰撮要 ([[KR3ej015]]). His preface is one of the principal contemporary documentary witnesses to Xuē Jǐ's working practice as a scholar-physician.
