Shutoku 守篤 (active Japan, premodern period), Japanese Buddhist scholar; the *fēn huì* 分會 (sectional-combiner) of [[KR6g0013|*Niè pán jīng xuán yì wén jù* 涅槃經玄義文句]] (X656) — the Japanese editorial collaborator who combined Guàn-dǐng's *Xuán yì* and Dào-xiān's *Wén jù* into a unified work. Birth and death dates not preserved.
