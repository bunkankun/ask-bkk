# Shū Jùn-jí 舒峻極

Early-Qīng layman; native of Yǒng-níng 永寧. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Self-identified as *Yǒng-níng dì* 永寧弟 ("younger-brother [from] Yǒng-níng") and close friend of [[蘊上|Lóng-guāng Dá-fū Yùn-shàng]] 龍光達夫蘊上.

Contributor of the principal preface to Dá-fū's *Jí wénzì chán* 集文字禪 [[KR6q0208]], dated *Kāngxī dīng-mǎo mèng qiū yuè shuò rì* 康熙丁卯孟秋月朔日 (first day of the seventh month of Kāngxī 26 = 1687). The preface is a key biographical source for Dá-fū and his brother [[蘊宏|Kuān-fū]], revealing their Míng imperial-descent and their post-1644 flight-and-return narrative.
