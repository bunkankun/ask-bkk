Southern-Sòng Chán monk, training disciple (*cānxué* 參學) and co-editor of the *Yúngǔ héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0384]]) of the Línjì master Yúngǔ 雲谷. The second element of the name is the rare character 㞧 (U+37A7), glossed as *yù* in Sòng-era Chán prosopography but probably simply an *yìtǐzì* 異體字 of a more common graph; no further biographical information survives.
