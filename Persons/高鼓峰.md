**Gāo Gǔfēng** 高鼓峰 (= **Gāo Dǒukuí** 高斗魁, 1623–1670), early-Qīng physician of Yīnxiàn 鄞縣 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Best known for his clinical compendium *Sì míng xīn fǎ* 四明心法. Informal pharmacological notes attributed to him circulated posthumously as the *Yàolùn* 藥論 — apparently compiled by students from his teaching memoranda rather than written by Gāo himself as a finished work; these notes formed the core of [[沈文彬]]'s late-Qīng merger printed as the *Yàolùn* in 1901 ([[KR3ec062]]).
