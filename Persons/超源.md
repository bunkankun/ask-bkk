# Chāo-yuán 超源 (Chāo-yuán Huì-fù 超源慧復)

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shì-zhě* 侍者 to [[明哲|Xiào-táng Míng-zhé]] 笑堂明哲 at **Yú-háng Wò-lóng-shān Guǎng-huà Chán-sì** and head recorder (*děng jì-lù* 等記錄) of [[KR6q0425]] 《笑堂和尚語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B206). Full form **Chāo-yuán Huì-fù** 超源慧復.

The 超-generation character places him in the same Mìyún-line **超-generation** cohort as **Chāo-jùn** 超晙 ([[超晙]]) who co-edited the same *yǔlù* — both are dharma-sons of Xiào-táng Míng-zhé, and thus dharma-grandsons of Lín-yě Tōng-qí. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0425]] juan-head signature line "*shì-zhě (Chāo-yuán Huì-fù) jì-lù* 侍者(超源慧復)記錄".

A separate Línjì monk also named **Chāo-yuán** 超源 (variant 超原), *zì* **Dàn-yá** 澹崖 ("Bland Cliff"). Native of **Mǐn-pǔ** 閩莆 (= 福建莆田) — i.e. of the **Lóng-chí 龍池** line of Línjì-Yángqí (rather than the Lín-yě / Yún-é line above). Lifedates unknown. Author of the lead preface to [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》, signed "龍池後裔閩莆超原澹崖氏拜撰" dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring. Distinct from the Xiào-táng-line *Chāo-yuán Huì-fù* recorder of [[KR6q0425]] above.

Other contemporary monks bearing the name **Chāo-yuán** 超源 are registered in DILA: **Lián-fēng Chāo-yuán** 蓮峰超源 (A018937, 1691–1745, registered in 五燈全書) — too young to have written the 1695 preface; **Chāo-yuán** of 笑堂 (A037276), attendant — likely identical with the Lín-yě-line *Chāo-yuán Huì-fù* in the first entry above; **Ruì-wēng Chāo-yuán** 瑞翁超源 (A039742).
