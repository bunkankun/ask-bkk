# Chāoyuán 超源 (Chāoyuán Huìfù 超源慧復)

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shìzhě* 侍者 to [[明哲|Xiàotáng Míngzhé]] 笑堂明哲 at **Yúháng Wòlóngshān Guǎnghuà Chánsì** and head recorder (*děng jìlù* 等記錄) of [[KR6q0425]] 《笑堂和尚語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B206). Full form **Chāoyuán Huìfù** 超源慧復.

The 超-generation character places him in the same Mìyún-line **超-generation** cohort as **Chāojùn** 超晙 ([[超晙]]) who co-edited the same *yǔlù* — both are dharma-sons of Xiàotáng Míngzhé, and thus dharma-grandsons of Línyě Tōngqí. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0425]] juan-head signature line "*shìzhě (Chāoyuán Huìfù) jìlù* 侍者(超源慧復)記錄".

A separate Línjì monk also named **Chāoyuán** 超源 (variant 超原), *zì* **Dànyá** 澹崖 ("Bland Cliff"). Native of **Mǐnpǔ** 閩莆 (= 福建莆田) — i.e. of the **Lóngchí 龍池** line of Línjì-Yángqí (rather than the Línyě / Yúné line above). Lifedates unknown. Author of the lead preface to [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》, signed "龍池後裔閩莆超原澹崖氏拜撰" dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring. Distinct from the Xiào-táng-line *Chāoyuán Huìfù* recorder of [[KR6q0425]] above.

- - - - -

Other contemporary monks bearing the name **Chāoyuán** 超源 are registered in DILA: **Liánfēng Chāoyuán** 蓮峰超源 (A018937, 1691–1745, registered in 五燈全書) — too young to have written the 1695 preface; **Chāoyuán** of 笑堂 (A037276), attendant — likely identical with the Lín-yě-line *Chāoyuán Huìfù* in the first entry above; **Ruìwēng Chāoyuán** 瑞翁超源 (A039742).
