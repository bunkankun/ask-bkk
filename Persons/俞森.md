Mid-Kāngxī administrative-reform scholar; native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu). *Hào* Cúnzhāi 存齋. From *gōngshēng* (tributary student) rose to *Hú-Guǎng Bù-zhèng-sī cān-yì* (Vice-Director of the Húguǎng Provincial Treasury). Earlier served as Vice-Censor of Hé-nán; later as Vice-Director of *Jīngnán Dào*. Compiler of the *Huāngzhèng cóngshū* 荒政叢書 ([[KR2m0048]]) (1690), the principal Qīng compendium of famine-relief literature, and author of the appended *Yún-Xiāng zhèn-jì shìyí* and *Bǔ huáng jíyào*. Active *fl.* 1690.
