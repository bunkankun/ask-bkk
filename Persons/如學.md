Wǔfēng Rúxué 五峰如學 (1585–1633), *zì* **Wúwéi** 無為. Late-Míng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) Chán master, 35th-generation dharma-heir of **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642) of **Tiāntóng** 天童, and principal founder-reviver of the **Dàwéi Tóngqìngsì** 大溈同慶寺 on **Dàwéishān** 大溈山 in Húnán. Native of **Líntóng** 臨潼 (modern Xīān), lay surname **Rèn** 任, son of a local official. Tonsured *fǎmíng* **Rúxué** at twenty (Wànlì 32 *jiǎchén* = 1604) at **Dàlǐngsì** 大嶺寺 on **Wǔtáishān** 五臺山 under the elder **Tiānqí** 天齊, having been prevented from earlier ordination by his surviving mother. Received full precepts at Wǔtái from the *lǜzhǔ* **Chéngfāng** 澄方律主. After years of pilgrimage — through **Xióngěrshān** 熊耳山 (where the elder **Wúyán** 無言 set him on *Qīngyuán*'s 廬陵米作麼價 huàtóu), **Hǔpáo** 虎跑, **Fúniú** 伏牛 (the cloister-*dǎqī* in which a numinous out-of-body episode occurred at day six), **Zhōngnán Wǔfēngyán** 終南五峰巖 (three-year solitude, whence his epithet *Wǔfēng*), **Kuānglú** 匡廬 (Hánshān Déqīng's monastery), **Yúnmén** 雲門 under **Zhànrán Yuánchéng** 湛然圓澄, and **Jīngshān** — he received final dharma-transmission from **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Lóngchí, perfected it at **Tiān-tai Tōngxuán** 通玄 and **Jīnsù** 金粟, and was formally sealed in Tiānqǐ 7 *dīngmǎo* (1627). He then closed himself in *bìguān* at **Nánjīng Hóngjìsì** 弘濟寺 for several years (1627–1631), then in Chóngzhēn 4 *xīnwèi* (1631) accepted the invitation to revive **Dàwéi Tóngqìngsì**, cutting stone and gathering branches under harsh conditions; the community grew rapidly. In Chóngzhēn 6 *guǐyǒu* (1633) he set out for Wǔtái, stopped at Jīnlíng at the invitation of **Yú Jíshēng** 余集生 the *zhōngchéng*, and on Chóngzhēn 6.7.22 *zhèngwǔ* (26 August 1633) died seated at the Jítuólín 祇陀林 in Nánjīng, aged 49 (29 dharma-years). Before his death he dispatched the **Láiyuán fúzǐ** 來源拂子 to his sole dharma-heir **Yǎngzhuō Zhèngmíng** [[正明]] 養拙正明 (? – 1649) at Dàwéi, and charged him with continuing the abbacy. His body was cremated by his disciples *Yǐngsù* 郢素 and *Fùzhì* 復智 et al. in Chóngzhēn 15 *rénwǔ* (1642); the stūpa at Dàwéi was completed in Chóngzhēn 16 *guǐwèi* (1643), with *tǎmíng* by **Táo Rǔnài** 陶汝鼐 (1601–1683). Surviving works: the one-juan *Dàwéi Wǔfēng Xué chánshī yǔlù* 大溈五峰學禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0400]], re-cut by his dharma-grandson **Zhìhǎi** [[智海]] in the Qīng) and the *Wǔzōng pàixù* 五宗派敘.
