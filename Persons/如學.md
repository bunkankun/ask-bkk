Wǔ-fēng Rúxué 五峰如學 (1585–1633), *zì* **Wúwéi** 無為. Late-Míng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) Chán master, 35th-generation dharma-heir of **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642) of **Tiān-tóng** 天童, and principal founder-reviver of the **Dà-wéi Tóng-qìng-sì** 大溈同慶寺 on **Dà-wéi-shān** 大溈山 in Hú-nán. Native of **Lín-tóng** 臨潼 (modern Xī-ān), lay surname **Rèn** 任, son of a local official. Tonsured *fǎ-míng* **Rúxué** at twenty (Wàn-lì 32 *jiǎ-chén* = 1604) at **Dà-lǐng-sì** 大嶺寺 on **Wǔ-tái-shān** 五臺山 under the elder **Tiān-qí** 天齊, having been prevented from earlier ordination by his surviving mother. Received full precepts at Wǔ-tái from the *lǜ-zhǔ* **Chéng-fāng** 澄方律主. After years of pilgrimage — through **Xióng-ěr-shān** 熊耳山 (where the elder **Wú-yán** 無言 set him on *Qīng-yuán*'s 廬陵米作麼價 huàtóu), **Hǔ-páo** 虎跑, **Fú-niú** 伏牛 (the cloister-*dǎ-qī* in which a numinous out-of-body episode occurred at day six), **Zhōng-nán Wǔ-fēng-yán** 終南五峰巖 (three-year solitude, whence his epithet *Wǔ-fēng*), **Kuāng-lú** 匡廬 (Hán-shān Dé-qīng's monastery), **Yún-mén** 雲門 under **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄, and **Jīng-shān** — he received final dharma-transmission from **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Lóng-chí, perfected it at **Tiān-tai Tōng-xuán** 通玄 and **Jīn-sù** 金粟, and was formally sealed in Tiān-qǐ 7 *dīng-mǎo* (1627). He then closed himself in *bì-guān* at **Nánjīng Hóng-jì-sì** 弘濟寺 for several years (1627–1631), then in Chóngzhēn 4 *xīn-wèi* (1631) accepted the invitation to revive **Dà-wéi Tóng-qìng-sì**, cutting stone and gathering branches under harsh conditions; the community grew rapidly. In Chóngzhēn 6 *guǐ-yǒu* (1633) he set out for Wǔ-tái, stopped at Jīn-líng at the invitation of **Yú Jí-shēng** 余集生 the *zhōng-chéng*, and on Chóngzhēn 6.7.22 *zhèng-wǔ* (26 August 1633) died seated at the Jí-tuó-lín 祇陀林 in Nánjīng, aged 49 (29 dharma-years). Before his death he dispatched the **Lái-yuán fú-zǐ** 來源拂子 to his sole dharma-heir **Yǎng-zhuō Zhèng-míng** [[正明]] 養拙正明 (? – 1649) at Dà-wéi, and charged him with continuing the abbacy. His body was cremated by his disciples *Yǐng-sù* 郢素 and *Fù-zhì* 復智 et al. in Chóngzhēn 15 *rén-wǔ* (1642); the stūpa at Dà-wéi was completed in Chóngzhēn 16 *guǐ-wèi* (1643), with *tǎ-míng* by **Táo Rǔ-nài** 陶汝鼐 (1601–1683). Surviving works: the one-juan *Dà-wéi Wǔ-fēng Xué chán-shī yǔlù* 大溈五峰學禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0400]], re-cut by his dharma-grandson **Zhì-hǎi** [[智海]] in the Qīng) and the *Wǔ-zōng pài-xù* 五宗派敘.
