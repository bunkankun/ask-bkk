Huò 豁 (DILA-standardized **Yìnhuò** 印豁), *hào* **Jìguāng** 寂光 / **Shèngshān** 聖山. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **己未 11/24 子時** = **1619-12-29 Gregorian**; alive in 1660 at age 42; death-date not preserved. Native of **Nánchōng** 南充 (Guǒchéng, Sìchuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Childhood vow to renounce the world; parents refused, so he took up *Jīngāng jīng* recitation. **戊子 = 1648** (age 30): both parents died; tonsured under **Fǎyǔ chánshī** 法雨禪師. Pilgrimage south with **Báizhāi chánshī** 白齋. Met **Wúzhuó chánshī** 無著 on the *wànfǎ* *gōngàn*. Then **Yǔncè chánshī** 允冊. Final transmission from **Pòshān Hǎimíng** in **己亥 = 1659**.

**Three abbacies.** **Lóngyìn Fózǐsì** 龍印佛子寺; **Suìníng Guǎngdésì** 遂寧廣德寺; **Xīchōng Zīfúsì** 西充資福寺. Title-form *Shèngshān* 聖山 references a Hénán seat.

**Twelve heirs** including **Yuèhuī Chángmíng** 月輝常明; **Xuěxuān Fāyī** 雪軒發一; **Xiàocāng Fāyù** [[發育]] 笑蒼發育.

**Work.** [[KR6q0504]] 《寂光豁禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B367, compiled by *fǎsì* **Fāyù** [[發育]] *děng biān*; juan 4 *xíngshí*. Sources: 《正源略集》 j. 5; DILA A016439.
