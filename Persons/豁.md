Huò 豁 (DILA-standardized **Yìn-huò** 印豁), *hào* **Jì-guāng** 寂光 / **Shèng-shān** 聖山. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **己未 11/24 子時** = **1619-12-29 Gregorian**; alive in 1660 at age 42; death-date not preserved. Native of **Nán-chōng** 南充 (Guǒ-chéng, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Childhood vow to renounce the world; parents refused, so he took up *Jīn-gāng jīng* recitation. **戊子 = 1648** (age 30): both parents died; tonsured under **Fǎ-yǔ chán-shī** 法雨禪師. Pilgrimage south with **Bái-zhāi chán-shī** 白齋. Met **Wú-zhuó chán-shī** 無著 on the *wàn-fǎ* *gōng-àn*. Then **Yǔn-cè chán-shī** 允冊. Final transmission from **Pòshān Hǎi-míng** in **己亥 = 1659**.

**Three abbacies.** **Lóng-yìn Fó-zǐ-sì** 龍印佛子寺; **Suì-níng Guǎng-dé-sì** 遂寧廣德寺; **Xī-chōng Zī-fú-sì** 西充資福寺. Title-form *Shèng-shān* 聖山 references a Hé-nán seat.

**Twelve heirs** including **Yuè-huī Cháng-míng** 月輝常明; **Xuě-xuān Fā-yī** 雪軒發一; **Xiào-cāng Fā-yù** [[發育]] 笑蒼發育.

**Work.** [[KR6q0504]] 《寂光豁禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B367, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fā-yù** [[發育]] *děng biān*; juan 4 *xíng-shí*. Sources: 《正源略集》 j. 5; DILA A016439.
