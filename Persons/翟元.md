# Dí Yuán 翟元

Originally 翟玄 Dí Xuán; the form 翟元 reflects Táng-period taboo on the character 玄. Pre-Táng *Yì* scholar of obscure dates, conventionally placed in the Eastern Jìn 東晉. His commentary on the *Zhōuyì* is lost as an independent work but is cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]).
