Lǐ Yóng 李顒 (Chéng-huà era), grandson of **Lǐ Shí-miǎn** 李時勉 ([[李時勉]]). *Cháng-lè zhī-xiàn* 長樂知縣. Printed the Chéng-huà edition of his grandfather's collected works ([[KR4e0098]] *Gǔ-lián wén-jí*), edited by Lǐ Shí-miǎn's pupil **Dài Nán** 戴難 ([[戴難]]), and added a *fù-lù* of epitaph, biography, and *zàn* documents. Not to be confused with the Qīng-era philosopher Lǐ Yóng 李顒 (1627–1705), the great *Guān-zhōng* school teacher.
