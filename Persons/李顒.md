Lǐ Yóng 李顒 (Chénghuà era), grandson of **Lǐ Shímiǎn** 李時勉 ([[李時勉]]). *Chánglè zhīxiàn* 長樂知縣. Printed the Chénghuà edition of his grandfather's collected works ([[KR4e0098]] *Gǔlián wénjí*), edited by Lǐ Shímiǎn's pupil **Dài Nán** 戴難 ([[戴難]]), and added a *fùlù* of epitaph, biography, and *zàn* documents. Not to be confused with the Qīng-era philosopher Lǐ Yóng 李顒 (1627–1705), the great *Guānzhōng* school teacher.
