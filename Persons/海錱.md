Hǎizhèn 海錱 (the catalog form; the *yǔlù*'s *liánfāngjì* gives the name as **Hǎijīn** 海金, *zì* **Yúcān** 愚參). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Línwǒ Jiàn** [[鑑|Línwǒ Jiàn]] (1612–1679); held the office of *xītáng* 西堂 at the master's monastery. Native of **Shífāng** 什邡 (Sìchuān). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biānlù*) with co-disciple [[海茲]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0541]] 《林我禪師語錄》 4 juan, J38 B422, printed 1692.
