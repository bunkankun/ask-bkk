Hǎi-zhèn 海錱 (the catalog form; the *yǔ-lù*'s *lián-fāng-jì* gives the name as **Hǎi-jīn** 海金, *zì* **Yú-cān** 愚參). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-wǒ Jiàn** [[鑑|Lín-wǒ Jiàn]] (1612–1679); held the office of *xī-táng* 西堂 at the master's monastery. Native of **Shí-fāng** 什邡 (Sì-chuān). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān-lù*) with co-disciple [[海茲]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0541]] 《林我禪師語錄》 4 juan, J38 B422, printed 1692.
