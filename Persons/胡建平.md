Hú Jiànpíng 胡建平 is a contemporary historian of Xīn'ān medicine, editor of the *Xīn'ān yījí cóngshū* 新安醫籍叢書 series (Hefei: Anhui kēxué jìshù chūbǎnshè, 1990s).
