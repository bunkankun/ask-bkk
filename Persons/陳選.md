Chén Xuǎn 陳選 (1429–1486), *zì* Shìxián 士賢, posthumously *Gōngquè* 恭慤 (Reverent and Sincere — Wǔ-zōng-period grant), was a major early-Míng *Lǐxué* official. Native of Línhǎi 臨海 (Táizhōu 台州, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of Tiānshùn *gēngchén* (1460); held office to *Guǎngdōng bùzhèngshǐ* 廣東布政使 (Provincial Administration Commissioner of Guǎngdōng), promoted posthumously to *Guānglùsì qīng* 光祿寺卿. As *Yùshǐ* he supported Luó Lún 羅倫 and impeached Ní Qiān 倪謙, Qián Pǔ 錢溥, Mǎ Áng 馬昂, and the eunuch Wāng Zhí 汪直 — earning a reputation for upright remonstrance. As Guǎngdōng *bùzhèngshǐ*, he opposed the eunuch faction's interference in the *shìbó* 市舶 (foreign trade) administration; was arrested and died in prison. His major scholarly work is the *Xiǎoxué jízhù* 小學集註 — a commentary on Zhū Xī's *Xiǎo xué* — adopted under Yōngzhèng 5 (1727) as the imperial standard *Xiǎo xué* commentary, and reproduced in the SKQS as [[KR3a0046]] under the title *Yùdìng Xiǎoxué jízhù* 御定小學集註. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 161. CBDB id 67109, dates 1429–1486.
