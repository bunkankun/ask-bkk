Chén Xuǎn 陳選 (1429–1486), *zì* Shìxián 士賢, posthumously *Gōng-què* 恭慤 (Reverent and Sincere — Wǔ-zōng-period grant), was a major early-Míng *Lǐxué* official. Native of Lín-hǎi 臨海 (Tái-zhōu 台州, Zhè-jiāng). *Jìnshì* of Tiān-shùn *gēng-chén* (1460); held office to *Guǎng-dōng bù-zhèng-shǐ* 廣東布政使 (Provincial Administration Commissioner of Guǎng-dōng), promoted posthumously to *Guāng-lù-sì qīng* 光祿寺卿. As *Yù-shǐ* he supported Luó Lún 羅倫 and impeached Ní Qiān 倪謙, Qián Pǔ 錢溥, Mǎ Áng 馬昂, and the eunuch Wāng Zhí 汪直 — earning a reputation for upright remonstrance. As Guǎng-dōng *bù-zhèng-shǐ*, he opposed the eunuch faction's interference in the *shì-bó* 市舶 (foreign trade) administration; was arrested and died in prison. His major scholarly work is the *Xiǎo-xué jí-zhù* 小學集註 — a commentary on Zhū Xī's *Xiǎo xué* — adopted under Yōng-zhèng 5 (1727) as the imperial standard *Xiǎo xué* commentary, and reproduced in the SKQS as KR3a0046 under the title *Yù-dìng Xiǎo-xué jí-zhù* 御定小學集註. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 161. CBDB id 67109, dates 1429–1486.
