Gāo Zhù 高翥 (1170–1241, *zì* Jiǔwàn 九萬, *hào* Jújiàn 菊磵 / Xìntiāncháo 信天巢), of *Yúyáo* 餘姚 in *Yuèzhōu* 越州 (modern Zhèjiāng). Itinerant Sòng poet who never held office; lectured privately at his *Xìntiāncháo* dwelling; wandered the *Qiántáng*–*Jīnlíng*–*Dòngtíng*–*Pénglǐ* circuit. Late-Sòng *Jiānghúpài* poet whose *Chéngzhāitǐ* affinity was noted by Liú Kèzhuāng [[劉辰翁]]; also noticed favorably by Chén Zhènsūn. Father Gāo Xuǎn 高選 and uncle Gāo Mài 高邁 were *Shàoxīng*-era *jìnshì*. Died at the *Gélǐng tánjiāshān* lodging at age 72.
