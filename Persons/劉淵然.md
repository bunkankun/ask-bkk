**Liú Yuánrán** 劉淵然 (明). Early-Míng Daoist patriarch (1351–1432) of the Jìngmíng 淨明 / Qìngwēi 清微 school. Associated with the Daoist lineage that transmitted [[KR3ek004]] *Xiānchuán wàikē jíyàn fāng*.
