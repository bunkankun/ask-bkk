Shītuópánnī 尸陀槃尼 (Skt. *Dhṛtakavarman* or *Śīlabhattanikā*; DILA Authority A000088) was a Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma master described as an arhat (*āluóhàn* 阿羅漢) and credited with composing the *Pípóshā lùn* 鞞婆沙論 ([[KR6l0012]], T1547), an early Vibhāṣā commentary on the *Jñāna-prasthāna* Abhidharma root text. His dates are entirely unknown; he belongs to the Kaśmīra Sarvāstivāda commentarial tradition, probably in the first to third centuries CE. Nothing further is known of his biography.
