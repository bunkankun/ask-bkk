Xiáng-gōng 翔公 (lifedates unknown; *fl.* mid-fifth century), Liú-Sòng Chinese Buddhist *śramaṇa*, *fl.* in the Nán-hǎi 南海 commandery (modern Guǎngdōng / Hǎinán region). The canonical signature for [[KR6c0022|*Rǔ-shǒu púsà wú-shàng qīngjìng fēn-wèi jīng* 濡首菩薩無上清淨分衛經 (T234)]] reads 「宋沙門翔公於南海郡譯」 ("translated by the *śramaṇa* Xiáng-gōng of the Sòng in the Nán-hǎi commandery"). The reference to the Nán-hǎi commandery is unusual — most Liú-Sòng translations were produced at Jiànkāng — and suggests Xiáng-gōng was working at one of the southern Buddhist centres along the Pearl River trade-corridor through which Funán and Indian merchants regularly travelled. No further biographical material survives. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001305.
