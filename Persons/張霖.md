# Zhāng Lín 張霖

Late-Qīng measles specialist; author of *Mázhèng jíchéng* 麻症集成 (Comprehensive Compilation on Measles), one of the three principal Qīng-period measles references alongside the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn yòukē xīnfǎ yàojué* measles chapter ([[KR3ej029]]) and [[謝玉瓊]] Xiè Yùqióng's *Mákē huórén quánshū* ([[KR3ej047]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
