Xìng-cōng 性聰, *zì* **Hān-pú** 憨璞 ("Foolish Plain"); often referred to as **Hān-pú Xìng-cōng** 憨璞性聰. Imperially-titled **Míng-jué chán-shī** 明覺禪師 by the **Shùnzhì emperor** in 1657. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎi-chī Xíng-yuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662) — the same teacher of [[素]] **Lián-fēng Sù** ([[KR6q0530]]) and [[德風]] **Dé-fēng** ([[KR6q0531]]). Born **1611**; died **康熙丙午臘月十三日** = **1667-01-07**, age 57, *sēng-là* 39. Native of **Yán-píng-fǔ Shào-wǔ** 邵武 (Fú-jiàn); lay surname **Lián** 連.

**Formation.** At 15 took tonsure at **Tiān-wáng-sì** 天王寺. At 25 took full ordination at **Nán-hǎi Pǔ-tuó** 南海普陀. Studied with **Yǒng-jué Xián** 永覺賢 (Cáodòng) at **Wǔ-lín Píng-yáo** 武林瓶窑, given the *wàn-fǎ guī yī* *gōng-àn*. Continued with **Dōng-shān Ěr-mì Fú** 東山爾密澓 — sudden insight. Then **Tiān-tāi Tōng-xuán Qí** 通玄奇 and finally **Bǎi-chī Yuán** 百癡元 for *fù-fǎ*.

**Imperial preceptorship and abbacies.** **順治十四年丁酉秋** = **1657** autumn invited by **Shùnzhì emperor** to **Wàn-shàn-diàn** 萬善殿 (within the Forbidden City) for the summer-retreat — the major imperial-Buddhist event of the era. **己亥冬** = **1659** winter ordered to do retreat at **Mǐn-zhōng-sì** 愍忠禪寺 (Beijing's principal precept-platform). **庚子秋** = **1660** autumn requested to return south; granted by imperial decree on lunar 8月. Held abbacies at **Háng-zhōu Guān-yīn-sì** 杭州觀音寺, **Yú-háng Fǎ-xǐ-sì** 餘杭法喜寺, **Qián-táng Guǎng-fú-yuàn** 錢塘廣福院, **Shùn-tiān Hǎi-huì-sì** 順天海會寺, **Shùn-tiān Yán-shòu-sì** 順天延壽寺, **Shào-wǔ Ān-guó-sì** 邵武安國寺, **Shùn-tiān Mǐn-zhōng-sì** 順天愍忠禪寺.

**Disciples.** **Shào-wǔ Ān-guó Kāi-lián Shòu** 邵武安國開蓮受 and **Pán-shān Gān-quán Yǔ-shí Dé** 盤山甘泉語石德.

**Work.** [[KR6q0609]] 《明覺聰禪師語錄》 20 juan (DILA: 16 juan), Qiánlóng-canon No. 1652, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Jì-kōng** [[寂空]] and **Pǔ-yuè Fāng-xǐng** [[方醒]] *děng biān*. Includes imperial *chì-shū* 敕書, *xiè-ēn biǎo* 謝恩表, and *jìn Wǔ-dēng biǎo* 進五燈表 — i.e. the *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* presentation memorial.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 89, vol. 106; 《佛光大辭典》 p. 3242.
