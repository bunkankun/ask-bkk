Xìngcōng 性聰, *zì* **Hānpú** 憨璞 ("Foolish Plain"); often referred to as **Hānpú Xìngcōng** 憨璞性聰. Imperially-titled **Míngjué chánshī** 明覺禪師 by the **Shùnzhì emperor** in 1657. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎichī Xíngyuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662) — the same teacher of [[素]] **Liánfēng Sù** ([[KR6q0530]]) and [[德風]] **Défēng** ([[KR6q0531]]). Born **1611**; died **康熙丙午臘月十三日** = **1667-01-07**, age 57, *sēnglà* 39. Native of **Yánpíngfǔ Shàowǔ** 邵武 (Fújiàn); lay surname **Lián** 連.

**Formation.** At 15 took tonsure at **Tiānwángsì** 天王寺. At 25 took full ordination at **Nánhǎi Pǔtuó** 南海普陀. Studied with **Yǒngjué Xián** 永覺賢 (Cáodòng) at **Wǔlín Píngyáo** 武林瓶窑, given the *wànfǎ guī yī* *gōngàn*. Continued with **Dōngshān Ěrmì Fú** 東山爾密澓 — sudden insight. Then **Tiāntāi Tōngxuán Qí** 通玄奇 and finally **Bǎichī Yuán** 百癡元 for *fùfǎ*.

**Imperial preceptorship and abbacies.** **順治十四年丁酉秋** = **1657** autumn invited by **Shùnzhì emperor** to **Wànshàndiàn** 萬善殿 (within the Forbidden City) for the summer-retreat — the major imperial-Buddhist event of the era. **己亥冬** = **1659** winter ordered to do retreat at **Mǐnzhōngsì** 愍忠禪寺 (Beijing's principal precept-platform). **庚子秋** = **1660** autumn requested to return south; granted by imperial decree on lunar 8月. Held abbacies at **Hángzhōu Guānyīnsì** 杭州觀音寺, **Yúháng Fǎxǐsì** 餘杭法喜寺, **Qiántáng Guǎngfúyuàn** 錢塘廣福院, **Shùntiān Hǎihuìsì** 順天海會寺, **Shùntiān Yánshòusì** 順天延壽寺, **Shàowǔ Ānguósì** 邵武安國寺, **Shùntiān Mǐnzhōngsì** 順天愍忠禪寺.

**Disciples.** **Shàowǔ Ānguó Kāilián Shòu** 邵武安國開蓮受 and **Pánshān Gānquán Yǔshí Dé** 盤山甘泉語石德.

**Work.** [[KR6q0609]] 《明覺聰禪師語錄》 20 juan (DILA: 16 juan), Qiánlóng-canon No. 1652, compiled by *fǎsì* **Jìkōng** [[寂空]] and **Pǔyuè Fāngxǐng** [[方醒]] *děng biān*. Includes imperial *chìshū* 敕書, *xièēn biǎo* 謝恩表, and *jìn Wǔdēng biǎo* 進五燈表 — i.e. the *Wǔdēng quánshū* presentation memorial.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 89, vol. 106; 《佛光大辭典》 p. 3242.
