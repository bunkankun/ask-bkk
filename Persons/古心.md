# Gǔ-xīn 古心

Late-Míng Buddhist monk associated with [[廣真|Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn]] 吹萬廣真's Jù-yún-pài 聚雲派 teaching-community in Sichuan. Lifedates unrecorded. Principal commentator (*píng bìng yǐn fán zé* 評並引凡則) on Guǎng-zhēn's *Gǔ yīn wáng zhuàn* 古音王傳 [[KR6q0236]], providing editorial principles and running commentary.
