Yīn Jī 殷基 was a Sūn-Wú 孫吳 official active in the mid-3rd century, son of Yīn Lǐ 殷禮 (a Lú-jiāng 廬江 commandery elder and trusted minister of Sūn Quán 孫權; the elder Yīn served as Inspector of Shàngshū 尚書 and later as governor of Líng-líng 零陵). Yīn Jī's own biography is not preserved separately; he is known only through Péi Sōngzhī's 裴松之 commentary to the *Sānguó zhì* 三國志, where his *Tōngyǔ* 通語 is repeatedly cited as a source for the politics of Sūn Liàng's 孫亮 reign and the *Èr-gōng zhī zhēng* 二宮之爭 succession dispute. He held the post of Grand Master of Palace Leisure (郎中) under Sūn Liàng. CBDB has no entry for him.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0122]] *Tōngyǔ* 通語.
