# Wéi Hú 韋縠 (fl. 10th c., Hòu Shǔ)

A *Jiānchá yùshǐ* (Investigating Censor) of the HòuShǔ 後蜀 kingdom (934–965) in Chéngdū. Compiler of the *Cáidiào jí* 才調集 [[KR4h0019]] in ten *juǎn* (one thousand poems, one hundred per *juǎn*), the largest of the Five-Dynasties Táng-poetry anthologies. His own preface gives biographical hints but no dates; the SKQS *tíyào* places him at the literary end of the Five Dynasties period, when "literary decay" was already in evidence. Nothing else of his career or family survives. No CBDB entry.
