Jìnè 寂訥 (catalog [[KR6q0507]] uses 寂訥; catalog [[KR6q0509]] uses 寂納; same person), *hào* **Fómǐn** 佛敏 / **Yìnxīn** 印心. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Nòuyún Zhēnshí** [[真實]] 耨雲真實 (b. 1626) at the Cǎotáng (Zhàngxuě line) succession-seat.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0507]] 《草堂耨雲實禪師語錄》 — compiled his master Zhēnshí's *yǔlù*.
- [[KR6q0509]] 《印心佛敏訥禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B372 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by *fǎsì* **Fǎdòng** [[法棟]] + **Fǎtōng** [[法通]] *děng lù/biān*.
