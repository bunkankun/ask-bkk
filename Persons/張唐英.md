Zhāng Táng-yīng 張唐英 (1029–1071), *zì* Cì-gōng 次功, *hào* Huáng-sōng-zǐ 黃松子, of Xīn-jīn 新津 in Shǔ-zhōu 蜀州 (modern Sìchuān). Northern-Sòng *Xī-níng*-era 熙寧 senior official, rising to *Diàn-zhōng shì-yù-shǐ* 殿中侍御史 (Imperial Censor in the Hall). Elder brother of the more famous [[張商英]] Zhāng Shāng-yīng (1043–1121), Sòng *Wǔ-zōng*-era chancellor; his career is preserved in the appended notice to Shāng-yīng's biography in the *Sòng-shǐ* juàn 351. Two historical works are associated with his name: the *Jiā-yòu míng-chén-zhuàn* 嘉祐名臣傳 (now lost), and the *Shǔ Táo-wù* 蜀檮杌 [[KR2i0016]] in 2 juàn — a chronological history of the Former and Latter Shǔ regimes (907–965), based on his own earlier *Qián-Shǔ kāi-guó-jì* 前蜀開國記 and *Hòu-Shǔ shí-lù* 後蜀實錄. CBDB id 10221 gives 1029–1071, in agreement with the *Sòng-shǐ* and the catalog meta. (CBDB id 47197 is a homonymous figure with dates 1031–1101 — almost certainly a CBDB-internal confusion of two records that should be merged.)
