Zhāng Tángyīng 張唐英 (1029–1071), *zì* Cìgōng 次功, *hào* Huángsōngzǐ 黃松子, of Xīnjīn 新津 in Shǔzhōu 蜀州 (modern Sìchuān). Northern-Sòng *Xīníng*-era 熙寧 senior official, rising to *Diànzhōng shìyùshǐ* 殿中侍御史 (Imperial Censor in the Hall). Elder brother of the more famous [[張商英]] Zhāng Shāngyīng (1043–1121), Sòng *Wǔzōng*-era chancellor; his career is preserved in the appended notice to Shāngyīng's biography in the *Sòngshǐ* juàn 351. Two historical works are associated with his name: the *Jiāyòu míngchénzhuàn* 嘉祐名臣傳 (now lost), and the *Shǔ Táowù* 蜀檮杌 [[KR2i0016]] in 2 juàn — a chronological history of the Former and Latter Shǔ regimes (907–965), based on his own earlier *Qián-Shǔ kāiguójì* 前蜀開國記 and *Hòu-Shǔ shílù* 後蜀實錄. CBDB id 10221 gives 1029–1071, in agreement with the *Sòngshǐ* and the catalog meta. (CBDB id 47197 is a homonymous figure with dates 1031–1101 — almost certainly a CBDB-internal confusion of two records that should be merged.)
