Zhào Wánbì 趙完璧, *zì* Quánqīng 全卿, *hào* Yúnhè 雲壑 (later Hǎihè 海壑), of Jiāozhōu 膠州 (Shāndōng). Entered office via *suìgòngshēng* (provincial graduate by seniority); rose to *Gǒngchāngfǔ tōngpàn*. As a junior *jǐnyīwèi* (Imperial Guard) officer at *Sīchéng* in Jiājìng 33 (1554), he resisted **Lù Bǐng** 陸炳 (the **Yán Sōng**-ally Imperial Guard commander) and was imprisoned in the *Běisī yù* — barely saved by senior censors. His prison was contemporaneous with **Yáng Jìshèng**'s; the *Hǎihè yíngǎo* [[KR4e0205]] preserves their prison exchange-poems and Zhào's 1555 *Yáng lièfù cí* commemorating Yáng's wife's suicide. CBDB 207168 has a *fl.* 1559 marker; full lifedates are unresolved but Wáng Sānxī's preface dates Zhào still living past 80 *sui*.
