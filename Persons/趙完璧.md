Zhào Wánbì 趙完璧, *zì* Quánqīng 全卿, *hào* Yúnhè 雲壑 (later Hǎihè 海壑), of Jiāozhōu 膠州 (Shān-dōng). Entered office via *suìgòng-shēng* (provincial graduate by seniority); rose to *Gǒngchāng-fǔ tōngpàn*. As a junior *jǐn-yī-wèi* (Imperial Guard) officer at *Sī-chéng* in Jiā-jìng 33 (1554), he resisted **Lù Bǐng** 陸炳 (the **Yán Sōng**-ally Imperial Guard commander) and was imprisoned in the *Běi-sī yù* — barely saved by senior censors. His prison was contemporaneous with **Yáng Jì-shèng**'s; the *Hǎi-hè yín-gǎo* [[KR4e0205]] preserves their prison exchange-poems and Zhào's 1555 *Yáng liè-fù cí* commemorating Yáng's wife's suicide. CBDB 207168 has a *fl.* 1559 marker; full lifedates are unresolved but Wáng Sān-xī's preface dates Zhào still living past 80 *sui*.
