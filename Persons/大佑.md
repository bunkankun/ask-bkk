# Dàyòu 大佑 / Qú'ān Dàyòu 蘧菴大佑 (1334–1407)

An early-Míng Tiāntái-school 天台 master, *hào* **Qú'ān** 蘧菴 ("Hut of Qú [Bóyù]") — frequently cited as **Qú'ān Dàshī** 蘧菴大師 ("the Master of the Qú-Hut"). Native of Wú-jùn 吳郡 (Sūzhōu), so he is also identified as 吳郡沙門大佑 in colophons. Lifedates 1334 (Yuán Yuántǒng 2) – 1407 (Míng Yǒnglè 5), age 74. He is one of the principal early-Míng restorers of Tiāntái Pure Land doctrine and the immediate transmission link from the Yuán [[性澄]] Xìngchéng line to the late-Míng [[傳燈]] Chuándēng / [[袾宏]] Zhūhóng synthesis.

His major Pure Land works are: the *Ē-mí-tuó jīng lüè-jiě* 阿彌陀經略解 [[KR6p0017]] — the standard early-Míng abbreviated commentary on the *Smaller Sukhāvatī-vyūha*, in one *juǎn*; and the same *Lüè-jiě* with the supplementary *Yuánzhōng-chāo* 圓中鈔 of [[傳燈]] Yōu-xī Chuándēng [[KR6p0018]], the latter a substantially expanded sub-commentary added a century after Dàyòu's death. He also produced the *Jìngtǔ zhǐ-guī jí* 淨土指歸集 (a Pure Land collectanea), the *Tiāntái sì-jiào yí jí-jiě* 天台四教儀集解, and the *Sēng shǐ luè* 僧史略 (a Buddhist historical compendium). Beyond Pure Land, he was a substantial Tiāntái doctrinal scholar in his own right, with works on the *Lotus*, *Vimalakīrti*, and *Diamond Sūtras*.

He served as abbot of *Bào'ēn-sì* 報恩寺 in Sūzhōu and was a major figure in the Wú region of Pure Land circles in the late Yuán / early Míng. His doctrinal position is the classic Sòng-Yuán Tiāntái synthesis: Pure Land devotion subordinated to but harmonised with the *yīxīn-sānguān* doctrine of Sìmíng [[知禮]] Zhīlǐ.
