**Wáng Dà-lún** 王大綸 (*zì* / *hào* Yí-gāng 怡岡, *fl.* late Wàn-lì — *Tiān-qǐ*, early 17th c.), late-Míng paediatrician of a multi-generation medical lineage. His postface (*Tiān-qǐ* 2 *jú-yuè* 1622 chrysanthemum month, ninth lunar month) to his own *Yīng-tóng lèi-cuì* 嬰童類萃 ([[KR3ej026]]) records that his family had practised medicine for *jiǔ-yè* 九葉 (nine generations), with paediatrics (*xiǎo-fāng-mài* 小方脈) as their principal specialism. His collected family medical writings — *Dòu-shā xīn-fǎ* 痘痧心法, *Yīng-tóng lèi-cuì* 嬰童類萃, and an external-medicine appendix — were issued together as *Wáng-shì jiā-chāo* 王氏家抄. No CBDB record.
