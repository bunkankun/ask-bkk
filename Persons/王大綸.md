**Wáng Dàlún** 王大綸 (*zì* / *hào* Yígāng 怡岡, *fl.* late Wànlì — *Tiānqǐ*, early 17th c.), late-Míng paediatrician of a multi-generation medical lineage. His postface (*Tiānqǐ* 2 *júyuè* 1622 chrysanthemum month, ninth lunar month) to his own *Yīngtóng lèicuì* 嬰童類萃 ([[KR3ej026]]) records that his family had practised medicine for *jiǔyè* 九葉 (nine generations), with paediatrics (*xiǎofāngmài* 小方脈) as their principal specialism. His collected family medical writings — *Dòushā xīnfǎ* 痘痧心法, *Yīngtóng lèicuì* 嬰童類萃, and an external-medicine appendix — were issued together as *Wángshì jiāchāo* 王氏家抄. No CBDB record.
