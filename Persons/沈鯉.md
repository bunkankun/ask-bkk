Shěn Lǐ 沈鯉 (1531–1615), *zì* Zhònghuà 仲化, *hào* Lóngjiāng 龍江, posthumous *shì* Wénduān 文端, of Guīdé 歸德 (Hénán). Took the *jìnshì* in Jiājìng 44 (1565); *Hànlínyuàn biānxiū*; rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū*, then *Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì* (Cabinet Grand Secretary). He is the author of the *Wényǎ shèyuē* 文雅社約 (separately *Sìkù*-recorded). In late life he clashed with the Cabinet rival **Shěn Yīguàn**, who attempted to implicate him in the 1602 **Yāoshū** (Witchcraft-Book) affair; Shěn Lǐ survived. His most famous extant document is the *Jiànzhǐ kuàngshuì shū* — the canonical Wàn-lì-era anti-mine-tax memorial. His collection is the *Yìyùtáng gǎo* [[KR4e0213]], whose Wànlì cutting was destroyed at the end of the Míng; the surviving WYG-base text is the 1690 reconstruction by **Liú Zhēn** 劉榛. CBDB 34728.
