**Shì Yìkān** 釋義堪, Buddhist monk of Wèiguó 衛國; wrote the 1128 (Sòng Jiànyán 2) preface to [[KR3eq029]] *Chǔshì yíshū*, recording the Xiāo-family preservation of the stone-tablet transmission across thirteen generations from 935.
