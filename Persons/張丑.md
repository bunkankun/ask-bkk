Major late-Míng connoisseur and bibliophile; originally named Qiāndé 謙德 (*zì* Shūyì 叔益), later changed his name to Chǒu (*zì* Qīngfù 青父); *hào* Mǐ-ān 米庵, of Kūnshān 崑山. Lifedates 1577–1635 per catalog meta. Fourth-generation of a major Sòng-and-Yuán piece-collecting family. The studio name *Mǐ-ān* and the cognate *Bǎo-mǐ-xuān* 寶米軒 are taken from his acquisition of Mǐ Fú's *Bǎozhāng dàifǎng lù* [[KR3h0023]] autograph in *Wànlì yǐ-mǎo* (1615). Author of an extraordinary cluster of connoisseurial works: [[KR3h0055]] *Qīnghé shūhuà fǎng* 清河書畫舫 (1616, the principal late-Míng connoisseur's compendium); [[KR3h0056]] *Zhēnjī rìlù* 真蹟日錄 (his daily authentic-piece register); [[KR3h0057]] *Qīnghé shūhuà biǎo* 清河書畫表 (a tabular summary); [[KR3h0058]] *Shū-huà jiànwén biǎo* 書畫見聞表; [[KR3h0059]] *Nányáng shūhuà jí* 南陽書畫集. Zhāng's establishment of Mǐ Fú's correct life-dates as 1051–1107 (57 years) — against the *Sòngshǐ* biography's 48-year error — is one of the most precise pieces of late-Míng connoisseurial *kǎo-zhèng*.
