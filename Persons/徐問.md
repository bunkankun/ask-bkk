Xú Wèn 徐問 (d. 1550), *zì* Yòngzhōng 用中, *hào* Yǎngzhāi 養齋, posthumously *Zhuāngyù* 莊裕, was a Mid-Míng *Lǐxué*-loyalist official of Wǔjìn 武進 (Chángzhōu region, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Hóngzhì rénxū* (1502); rose to *Nánjīng Hùbù shàngshū* 南京戶部尚書. His major work is the *Dú shū zhā jì* 讀書劄記 ([[KR3a0085]]) — composed during his tenure as Provincial Governor of Guìzhōu (catalog meta date 1602 is anomalous; should be 1500s) — a polemical anti-Wáng-Yáng-míng *yǔlù*. CBDB id 30618 records his death year as 1550 only.
