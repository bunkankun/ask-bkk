**Wú Jūtōng** 吳鞠通 (清). Wú Tāng 吳瑭 (1758–1836), *zì* Pèizhī 配之, *hào* Jūtōng 鞠通; major late-Qīng Wēnbìng 溫病學 theorist of Huái'ān 淮安, author of *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn* 溫病條辨 (1798). The Wēnbìng theoretical framework systematised by Wú was decisive for [[高秉鈞]]'s [[KR3ek042]] *Yángkē xīndé jí*.
