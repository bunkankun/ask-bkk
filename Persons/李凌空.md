# Lǐ Língkōng 李凌空

Contemporary Chinese-medicine philologist; editor of the modern critical edition *Yòukē zhézhōng jiàozhù* 幼科折衷校注 ([[KR3ej018]]) in the *Zhōngguó gǔ-yījí zhěnglǐ cóngshū* 中國古醫籍整理叢書 (Beijing: Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè, 2016).
