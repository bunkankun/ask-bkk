# Chén Fùliáng 陳傅良

Style name *Jūnjǔ* 君舉 (taking the meaning "Fù Yuè 傅說 was raised from the rammed earth" — *jǔ* "raised" matching *fù* "submitted-earth-walling," fixing the standard form 傅 of his given name; the variant 傳 sometimes appears but the SKQS *tíyào* on [[KR1e0038]] standardises to 傅). Sobriquet *Zhǐ-zhāi* 止齋. Posthumous title *Wén-jié* 文節. Native of Ruì-ān 瑞安 in Wēn-zhōu 溫州 (modern Zhèjiāng). Lifedates 1137–1203, given by the *Sòng shǐ* (juan 434) and the SKQS *tíyào*. CBDB id 10585.

*Jìnshì* of Qián-dào 8 (1172). Held the senior court posts of *Zhōng-shū shè-rén* 中書舍人 (Drafting Edict-Drafter) and *Bǎo-mó-gé dài-zhì* 寶謨閣待制. Was the principal *Chūnqiū* scholar of the *Yǒng-jiā* school 永嘉學派 — the Southern-Sòng "utilitarian" intellectual current opposed to Zhū Xī's *Dàoxué* — and a senior contemporary of Yè Shì 葉適 (1150–1223), who succeeded him as the school's leading figure. The *Yǒng-jiā* school was politically aligned with the resistance party against Hán Tuō-zhòu 韓侂胄's eventual New Policies revival.

His principal *Chūnqiū* works:
- *Chūnqiū hòu zhuàn* 春秋後傳 in 12 juan ([[KR1e0038]]) — surviving running commentary, the principal Yǒng-jiā-school *Chūnqiū* statement.
- *Zuǒshì zhāng zhǐ* 左氏章旨 in 30 juan — a Zuǒ-zhuàn paraphrase work; lost as a complete text, partial fragments in *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*.

Also wrote on the *Shàngshū*, *Sānlǐ* (especially the *Zhōulǐ* — his *Zhōulǐ shuō* 周禮說 in 12 juan), military strategy (*Lì dài bīng zhì* 歷代兵制 in 8 juan, surviving), and substantial historical works including *Liǎng Hàn shǐ-jì* 兩漢史記 and *Qián Hàn liè-zhuàn* 前漢列傳. His collected works survive as *Zhǐ-zhāi xiān-shēng wén jí* 止齋先生文集 in 51 juan.

His pupils Cài Yòuxué 蔡幼學, Hú Zōng 胡宗, and Zhōu Miǎn 周勉 were charged with reading the three commentaries respectively; one of them always accompanied Chén in office, providing immediate reference for *Chūnqiū* questions. Chén's *Chūnqiū* method — the conjunction of *Gōngyáng* / *Gǔliáng* "not-recorded" hermeneutic with the *Zuǒzhuàn*'s factual record — was admired by Zhào Fǎng 趙汸 in the Yuán as the most accomplished Sòng *Chūnqiū* scholarship.

Buried at Ruì-ān; epitaph by his contemporary Lóu Yuè 樓鑰.
