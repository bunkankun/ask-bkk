# Lǐ Huá 李華 (715–766)

*Zì* Xiáshū 遐叔. Native of *Zhàozhōu* 趙州 *Zànhuáng* 賛皇 (modern *Zànhuáng xiàn* in Héběi), of the *Zhàojùn Lǐ* 趙郡李 clan (the same *jùnwàng* lineage as the imperial Lǐ family). One of the four canonical *Tiānbǎo* / *Dàlì*-period *fùgǔ* prose stylists, alongside Xiāo Yǐngshì [[蕭穎士]], Dúgū Jí [[獨孤及]], and Yuán Jié [[元結]].

*Jìnshì* and *hóngcí* 宏辭 (vast-erudition) examination, both passed. *Jiānchá yùshǐ* 監察御史 in *Tiānbǎo*; demoted by powerful figures to *yòu bǔquē* 右補闕. Captured by the An Lùshān rebels in 756 and forced to take the rebel office of *Fènggé shèrén* 鳳閣舍人 — the disgrace he could not escape. After the rebellion he was demoted to *Hángzhōu sīhù cānjūn*; later Lǐ Xiàn 李峴 memorialized to place him in his staff and promoted him to *lìbù yuánwài láng* 吏部員外郎. He resigned for *fēngbì* 風痺 (paralysis-illness) and died in 766, aged 51.

His extant collection is the *Lǐ Xiáshū wénjí* [[KR4c0036]] in 4 *juǎn* — a Míng-Qīng editorial reconstruction from the original 30-*juǎn* corpus described in Dúgū Jí's preface (10 *juǎn* of *qiánjí*, 20 *juǎn* of *zhōngjí*). His most-anthologized work is the *Diào gǔ zhàncháng wén* 弔古戰場文 ("Lament for the Ancient Battle-Field"), a meditation on desert warfare that decisively shaped the late-Tang and Sòng *biānsài* and *huáigǔ* genres. His late writings (the *Quán Gāo bēimíng*, the *Yuán Déxiù bēimíng*, the *Sì-Hào míng*) are saturated with subtle moral self-vindication for the rebel-court episode.
