# Lǐ Huá 李華 (715–766)

*Zì* Xiá-shū 遐叔. Native of *Zhào-zhōu* 趙州 *Zàn-huáng* 賛皇 (modern *Zàn-huáng xiàn* in Hé-běi), of the *Zhào-jùn Lǐ* 趙郡李 clan (the same *jùn-wàng* lineage as the imperial Lǐ family). One of the four canonical *Tiān-bǎo* / *Dà-lì*-period *fù-gǔ* prose stylists, alongside Xiāo Yǐng-shì [[蕭穎士]], Dúgū Jí [[獨孤及]], and Yuán Jié [[元結]].

*Jìn-shì* and *hóng-cí* 宏辭 (vast-erudition) examination, both passed. *Jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 監察御史 in *Tiān-bǎo*; demoted by powerful figures to *yòu bǔ-quē* 右補闕. Captured by the An Lùshān rebels in 756 and forced to take the rebel office of *Fèng-gé shè-rén* 鳳閣舍人 — the disgrace he could not escape. After the rebellion he was demoted to *Háng-zhōu sī-hù cān-jūn*; later Lǐ Xiàn 李峴 memorialized to place him in his staff and promoted him to *lì-bù yuán-wài láng* 吏部員外郎. He resigned for *fēng-bì* 風痺 (paralysis-illness) and died in 766, aged 51.

His extant collection is the *Lǐ Xiá-shū wén-jí* [[KR4c0036]] in 4 *juǎn* — a Míng-Qīng editorial reconstruction from the original 30-*juǎn* corpus described in Dúgū Jí's preface (10 *juǎn* of *qián-jí*, 20 *juǎn* of *zhōng-jí*). His most-anthologized work is the *Diào gǔ zhàn-cháng wén* 弔古戰場文 ("Lament for the Ancient Battle-Field"), a meditation on desert warfare that decisively shaped the late-Tang and Sòng *biān-sài* and *huái-gǔ* genres. His late writings (the *Quán Gāo bēi-míng*, the *Yuán Dé-xiù bēi-míng*, the *Sì-Hào míng*) are saturated with subtle moral self-vindication for the rebel-court episode.
