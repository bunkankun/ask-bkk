Gù Qīng 顧清 (1460–1528), *zì* **Shì-lián** 士廉, *hào* **Dōng-jiāng** 東江, posthumously *shì* **Wén-xī** 文僖, of **Huá-tíng** 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng-fǔ, Jiāng-sū). Hóng-zhì 6 / *guǐ-chǒu* (1493) *jìn-shì*; reached *Nán-jīng Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. *Míng shǐ* j. 184. Editor of the *Sōng-jiāng-fǔ zhì* 松江府志 (independently catalogued under KR2k). Disciple of the **Chá-líng** (Lǐ Dōng-yáng) school of literature — in the Zhèng-dé era resisted the rising *Qián-Qī-zǐ* (former-seven-masters) tide; submitted *over ten* memorials of remonstrance; in early Jiā-jìng pressed for the cessation of sending out the imperial guards. His writings — chronologically arranged in *Shān-zhōng gǎo* (pre-office), *Běi-yóu gǎo* (post-office), *Guī-lái gǎo* (post-retirement), with later 留都稿 and 存稿 supplements no longer extant — are gathered in the *Dōng-jiāng jiā-cáng jí* in 42 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0149]]).
