Gù Qīng 顧清 (1460–1528), *zì* **Shìlián** 士廉, *hào* **Dōngjiāng** 東江, posthumously *shì* **Wénxī** 文僖, of **Huátíng** 華亭 (Sōngjiāngfǔ, Jiāngsū). Hóngzhì 6 / *guǐchǒu* (1493) *jìnshì*; reached *Nánjīng Lǐbù shàngshū*. *Míng shǐ* j. 184. Editor of the *Sōngjiāngfǔ zhì* 松江府志 (independently catalogued under KR2k). Disciple of the **Chálíng** (Lǐ Dōngyáng) school of literature — in the Zhèngdé era resisted the rising *QiánQīzǐ* (former-seven-masters) tide; submitted *over ten* memorials of remonstrance; in early Jiājìng pressed for the cessation of sending out the imperial guards. His writings — chronologically arranged in *Shānzhōng gǎo* (pre-office), *Běiyóu gǎo* (post-office), *Guīlái gǎo* (post-retirement), with later 留都稿 and 存稿 supplements no longer extant — are gathered in the *Dōngjiāng jiācáng jí* in 42 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0149]]).
