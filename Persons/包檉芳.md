# Bāo Chéngfāng 包檉芳

Southern-Sòng layman of Jiāxīng who in 1205 donated ten *liǎng* of silver to fund the block-cutting of the revised *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī niánpǔ* ([[KR6q0063]]); recorded in the final colophon of the Jiāxīng-canon witness.
