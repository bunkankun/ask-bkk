**Chén Cǎizhōng** 陳彩盅 of Tiāntái 天台 (Zhèjiāng) — Qīng editor of the 1856 wood-block edition of [[單南山]] Shàn Nánshān's *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* ([[KR3ei053]]), printed at the Ōu family Zhōngxiàn dì mansion under [[歐孔章]] Ōu Kǒngzhāng's patronage. Not in CBDB.
