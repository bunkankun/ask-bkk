Jīnglǐ xiānshēng 京里先生 ("Master of the Capital Neighbourhood") is the pseudonymous or legendary author to whom two Six-Dynasties alchemical compilations are ascribed:

- *Shénxiān fú'ěr dānshí xíngyào fǎ* 神仙服餌丹石行藥法 [[KR5b0104]] (DZ 420), a compendium of mineral-ingestion recipes.
- *Shénxiān shíqì jīnguì miàolù* 神仙食氣金匱妙錄 (DZ 836), a companion *qì*-cultivation / dietary text.

Nothing is known of the historical person, if any, behind the pseudonym. The attribution appears in *Bìshū shěng xù biàndào sìkù quēshū mù* 2.36b, where the title is slightly variant (*Shénxiān fúshí ěrshí bìng xíngyào fǎ*). The two works reflect the typical Six-Dynasties genre of the mineral-elixir *fāngshù* compilation, drawing on the tradition of the *Lièxiān zhuàn* and *Shénxiān zhuàn* hagiographies. No CBDB record.
