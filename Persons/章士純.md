**Zhāng Shìchún** 章士純 (Míng; dates and native place unrecorded) is the attributed author of *Sānyuán qīzhèng èrshíbāxiù zhōutiān jīngjiàn* 三垣七政二十八宿周天精鑒 ([[KR3fa013]]), a one-*juǎn* concise star-and-planet manual. He is otherwise undocumented in CBDB or in standard biographical references.
