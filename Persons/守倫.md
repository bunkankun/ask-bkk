Sòng-period Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A000359. Resident at the **Kē-shān Jīn-qī Qī-yún-sì** 柯山金谿棲雲寺. Devoted to filial piety and to retreat among forests and springs; composed the **Fǎhuá jīng kē-zhù** 法華經科註 ([[KR6d0071]], X30n0605, 10 *juan*) — a Lotus Sūtra subcommentary combining sectional analysis (*kē* 科) with phrase-glosses (*zhù* 註).

The work was subsequently collated and corrected (參訂 *cān-dìng*) by the Míng monk **Fǎ-jì** 法濟 ([[法濟]]) of Yù-xī Pútí-ān 玉溪菩提菴, with a preface by **Shèng-xíng** 聖行 dated Chóngzhēn 1 (1628) — a Míng productive-redactional layering whose primary substance remains Shǒulún's Sòng original.

Note: distinct from a Míng-period **Shǒulún** of Ān-huī Jiǔhuá-shān 安徽九華山 (DILA A008103), an unrelated figure.

Sources: *Fǎhuá jīng kē-zhù juàn-shǒu* 法華經科註卷首 (preface); DILA A000359.
