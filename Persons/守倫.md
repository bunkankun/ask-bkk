Sòng-period Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A000359. Resident at the **Kēshān Jīnqī Qīyúnsì** 柯山金谿棲雲寺. Devoted to filial piety and to retreat among forests and springs; composed the **Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù** 法華經科註 ([[KR6d0071]], X30n0605, 10 *juan*) — a Lotus Sūtra subcommentary combining sectional analysis (*kē* 科) with phrase-glosses (*zhù* 註).

The work was subsequently collated and corrected (參訂 *cāndìng*) by the Míng monk **Fǎjì** 法濟 ([[法濟]]) of Yùxī Pútíān 玉溪菩提菴, with a preface by **Shèngxíng** 聖行 dated Chóngzhēn 1 (1628) — a Míng productive-redactional layering whose primary substance remains Shǒulún's Sòng original.

Note: distinct from a Míng-period **Shǒulún** of Ānhuī Jiǔhuáshān 安徽九華山 (DILA A008103), an unrelated figure.

Sources: *Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù juànshǒu* 法華經科註卷首 (preface); DILA A000359.
