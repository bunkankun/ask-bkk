Mid- to late-Táng monk; affiliation and lineage unrecorded. DILA Authority A001038. Lifedates not preserved. Author of the **Fǎhuá jīng xuán-zàn jué-zé jì** 法華經玄贊決擇記 ([[KR6d0029]], X34n0637, 2 *juan*) — a Cí'ēn-affiliated Yogācāra commentary on Kuījī's *Miào-fǎ liánhuá jīng xuán-zàn* ([[KR6d0026]], T1723), preserved in the canonical record together with collated subcommentary by **Fǎ-qīng** 法清 ([[法清]], 集疏 jí-shū). The *Jué-zé* (*viniścaya*, "decisive determination") in the title points to the Yogācāra exegetical procedure of resolving doctrinal ambiguities through systematic application of the Cí'ēn scholastic apparatus.

Source: DILA A001038; X34n0637.
