Tōng-liàng 通量, *hào* **Xī-lín** 西臨. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hái-chū Guāng-fó** [[光佛]] 還初光佛 (b. 1631) at **Huá-yán Chán-yuàn** in Chóng-qìng. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《華巖還初佛禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0518]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
