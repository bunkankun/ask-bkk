Tōngliàng 通量, *hào* **Xīlín** 西臨. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Háichū Guāngfó** [[光佛]] 還初光佛 (b. 1631) at **Huáyán Chányuàn** in Chóngqìng. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《華巖還初佛禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0518]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
