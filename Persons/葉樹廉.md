**Yè Shùlián** 葉樹廉 (fl. early Qīng, late Míng / Shùnzhì–Kāngxī era), is known principally as the editor (*jí jiào bǔ* 輯校補) of the ten-*juǎn* SBCK [[KR4b0016]] *Liáng Jiāng Wéntōng wén jí* 梁江文通文集. His edition restores the older ten-*juǎn* division of Jiāng Yān's collection, distinct from the four-*juǎn* line consolidated by the *Sìkù*-WYG. CBDB registers a Yè Shùlián at id 438830 (dynasty marker only) but no firm dates; no separate biographical record survives. His editorial work is conventionally dated to the late Míng / early Qīng on the basis of internal evidence and the SBCK transmission.
