Xíng-qiān 行謙, *zì* **Wú-zhèng** 無諍 ("Without Contention"); often referred to as **Wú-zhèng Xíng-qiān** 無諍行謙. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jìng-tíng Dé-zōng** [[德宗]] (1621–1684). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0587]] 《逕庭宗禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B475.
