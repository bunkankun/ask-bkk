Xíngqiān 行謙, *zì* **Wúzhèng** 無諍 ("Without Contention"); often referred to as **Wúzhèng Xíngqiān** 無諍行謙. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Jìngtíng Dézōng** [[德宗]] (1621–1684). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0587]] 《逕庭宗禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B475.
