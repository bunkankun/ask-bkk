# 孫應科

**Sūn Yìngkē** (*zì* Yànzhī 彥之, *fl.* early 19th c., b. ca. 1777 — sixty *suì* in *Dàoguāng* 16 = 1836), Gāoyóu 高郵 (Jiāngsū) literatus and convalescent-editor of [[KR3el010]] *Diēsǔn miàofāng*. In the summer of *Dàoguāng* 13 / *guǐsì* 癸巳 (1833) he broke his left upper arm and travelled south in search of treatment; cured by the methods of the Yìyuǎn Zhēnrén [[異遠真人]] manuscript, he assembled the *editio princeps* of 1836, with Hú Quán [[胡泉]] underwriting the printing costs and Liú Bǎonán [[劉寶楠]] contributing a sixtieth-birthday poem. He was thus the key figure in the public diffusion of the Buddhist-monastic *shāngkē* tradition out of three centuries of manuscript circulation.
