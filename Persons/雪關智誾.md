# Xuěguān Zhìyín 雪關智誾

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng-school Chan master, dharma-teacher of Luófēng [[弘麗]] Hónglì (author of [[KR6c0169]]) at Rìqīnshān 日勤山 in Sháozhōu 韶州 (Guǎngdōng). Active in the Lǐngnán Cáodòng monastic networks of the mid-seventeenth century.
