Shěn Chén-lín 沈陳麟, *zì* Chūn-sūn 春孫, *bié-shǔ* Náng-yǐn dào-rén 囊隱道人 (the "Hidden-Bag Daoist") of the Zhěn-shí-zhāi 枕石齋 studio; native of Wú-jiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū), active late nineteenth century. A friend and colleague of [[凌淦]] Líng Gàn, he transcribed Líng's surviving casebook as [[KR3ep053]] *Tuì-ān yī-àn* (1896) and an earlier ten-volume transcript of cases by Lǐ Líng-shòu 李齡壽 (Líng's teacher). No further biographical record beyond his own colophons.
