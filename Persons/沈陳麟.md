Shěn Chénlín 沈陳麟, *zì* Chūnsūn 春孫, *biéshǔ* Nángyǐn dàorén 囊隱道人 (the "Hidden-Bag Daoist") of the Zhěnshízhāi 枕石齋 studio; native of Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū), active late nineteenth century. A friend and colleague of [[凌淦]] Líng Gàn, he transcribed Líng's surviving casebook as [[KR3ep053]] *Tuìān yīàn* (1896) and an earlier ten-volume transcript of cases by Lǐ Língshòu 李齡壽 (Líng's teacher). No further biographical record beyond his own colophons.
