# Shinchi Kakushin 心地覺心

Japanese (Kamakura-period) Rinzai monk (1207–1298), also known as Hottō Enmyō Kokushi 法燈圓明國師; studied under [[慧開|Wúmén Huìkāi]] 無門慧開 at the Hùguó Rénwáng sì in 1249 and carried the [[KR6q0080]] *Wúmén guān* back to Japan, transmitting it through the Myōshinji 妙心寺 / Daitokuji 大德寺 Rinzai lineage.
