# 陳耕道

Chén Gēngdào, late-Qīng physician; author of *Yìshā cǎo* 疫痧草, a standard reference for the *hóushā* 喉痧 (scarlet-fever-type throat-rash) tradition that informs the later Republican-era [[KR3em038]] *Hóukē jiāxùn* 喉科家訓.
