**Lǐ Zhī-yí** 李之儀 (c. 1048–c. 1117), *zì* Duān-shū 端叔, *hào* Gū-xī jūshì 姑溪居士, of Cāng-zhōu 滄州 (or Lè-shòu 樂壽 / Jǐng-chéng 景城; the *Sìkù* tíyào diagnoses the *Sòng shǐ* error). *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-fēng*. *Yuán-yòu* 1 (1086) appointed *Shū-mì-yuàn biān-xiū-guān*, then *Tōng-pàn Yuán-zhōu*. In *Yuán-fú* (1098–1100) made *Jiān nèi xiāng-yào kù* (Inspector of the Inner Aromatic-Drug Storehouse). After serving in Sū Shì 蘇軾's [[蘇軾]] *mù-fǔ*, was impeached by censor Shí Yù 石豫 and dismissed. *Chóng-níng* 1 (1102) made *Tí-jǔ Hé-dōng cháng-píng*; for drafting Fàn Chún-rén 范純仁's posthumous memorial in too-direct terms, offending Cài Jīng, was *biān-guǎn* (registered-and-confined) at *Tài-píng-zhōu* (Dāng-tú 當塗, in *Gū-xī* 姑溪) — whence his *hào*. Author of the *Gū-xī jū-shì qián-jí* 姑溪居士前集 [[KR4d0110]] in 50 *juǎn* and *hòu-jí* in 20 *juǎn*; one of the *Sū-mén* secondary literary network. The *Yáng Wàn-lǐ Chéng-zhāi shī-huà* and Wáng Míng-qīng *Huī-zhǔ hòu-lù* both record Lǐ's *cháng-duǎn jù* (lyrics) — particularly his "*Wǒ zhù Cháng-jiāng tóu, jūn zhù Cháng-jiāng wěi*" — as among the most-loved of the period. CBDB confirms *fl.* 1085–1102; lifedates 1048–1117 are best-supported. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444.
