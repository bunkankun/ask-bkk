**Lǐ Zhīyí** 李之儀 (c. 1048–c. 1117), *zì* Duānshū 端叔, *hào* Gūxī jūshì 姑溪居士, of Cāngzhōu 滄州 (or Lèshòu 樂壽 / Jǐngchéng 景城; the *Sìkù* tíyào diagnoses the *Sòng shǐ* error). *Jìnshì* of *Yuánfēng*. *Yuányòu* 1 (1086) appointed *Shūmìyuàn biānxiūguān*, then *Tōngpàn Yuánzhōu*. In *Yuánfú* (1098–1100) made *Jiān nèi xiāngyào kù* (Inspector of the Inner Aromatic-Drug Storehouse). After serving in Sū Shì 蘇軾's [[蘇軾]] *mùfǔ*, was impeached by censor Shí Yù 石豫 and dismissed. *Chóngníng* 1 (1102) made *Tíjǔ Hédōng chángpíng*; for drafting Fàn Chúnrén 范純仁's posthumous memorial in too-direct terms, offending Cài Jīng, was *biānguǎn* (registered-and-confined) at *Tàipíngzhōu* (Dāngtú 當塗, in *Gūxī* 姑溪) — whence his *hào*. Author of the *Gūxī jūshì qiánjí* 姑溪居士前集 [[KR4d0110]] in 50 *juǎn* and *hòují* in 20 *juǎn*; one of the *Sūmén* secondary literary network. The *Yáng Wànlǐ Chéngzhāi shīhuà* and Wáng Míngqīng *Huīzhǔ hòulù* both record Lǐ's *chángduǎn jù* (lyrics) — particularly his "*Wǒ zhù Chángjiāng tóu, jūn zhù Chángjiāng wěi*" — as among the most-loved of the period. CBDB confirms *fl.* 1085–1102; lifedates 1048–1117 are best-supported. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444.
