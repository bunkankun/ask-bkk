# 支敏度

Zhī Mǐndù (*fl.* early 4th century), Eastern Jìn-period Buddhist monk and bibliographer. Best known for his "composite-sūtra" (合本) editorial method: he combined the multiple Chinese recensions of the *Śūraṅgama* and the *Vimalakīrti* into single working texts (五本合為一部八卷《合首楞嚴經》; 三本合為一部五卷《合維摩詰經》). His editorial precedent is explicitly cited by [[寶貴]] in the preface to [[KR6i0302]] as a model for the Sui composite *Suvarṇaprabhāsa*. Compiled a scriptural catalog 經論都錄 (now lost) — among the earliest Chinese Buddhist bibliographies.
