Xìng-jiǒng 性炅, *hào* **Cháng-míng** 長明 ("Eternal Light"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yǐng-xiù Zhēn-wù** 穎秀真悟. Native of **Shǔ-dōng Fú-líng** 蜀東涪陵 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Shí** 石. Birth date unknown; died **Kāngxī wù-shēn** = **1668** (DILA: between 1668-02-12 and 1669-01-31, since the lunar Wùshēn year straddles two Gregorian years).

**Formation.** Tonsured young under elder **Jí-chū** 極初. Studied Mahāyāna scripture-treatise under **Bó-shuǐ** 渤水法師. Trained under **Xiàng-yá** 象崖和尚 and **Yǐng-xiù** 穎秀和尚, receiving final dharma-transmission from Yǐng-xiù Zhēn-wù.

**Abbacies.** **Chén-zhōu Lóng-shān** 辰州龍山, **Chén-zhōu Pǔ-fēng** 辰州浦峰, **Lǐ-zhōu Pú-tí** 灃州菩提, **Yuán-zhōu Tài-hé** 沅州太和.

**Disciples.** Two *fǎ-sì*: **Fǎ-zhù Hǎi-dòng** 法柱海棟 [[海棟]] (compiler of his *yǔ-lù*, in turn author of [[KR6q0525]]) and **Zhǐ-àn Hǎi-cí** 止岸海慈.

**Work.** [[KR6q0524]] 《浦峰長明炅禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B401, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hǎi-dòng** [[海棟]] *biān*.
