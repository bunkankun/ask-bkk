Xìngjiǒng 性炅, *hào* **Chángmíng** 長明 ("Eternal Light"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yǐngxiù Zhēnwù** 穎秀真悟. Native of **Shǔdōng Fúlíng** 蜀東涪陵 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Shí** 石. Birth date unknown; died **Kāngxī wùshēn** = **1668** (DILA: between 1668-02-12 and 1669-01-31, since the lunar Wùshēn year straddles two Gregorian years).

**Formation.** Tonsured young under elder **Jíchū** 極初. Studied Mahāyāna scripture-treatise under **Bóshuǐ** 渤水法師. Trained under **Xiàngyá** 象崖和尚 and **Yǐngxiù** 穎秀和尚, receiving final dharma-transmission from Yǐngxiù Zhēnwù.

**Abbacies.** **Chénzhōu Lóngshān** 辰州龍山, **Chénzhōu Pǔfēng** 辰州浦峰, **Lǐzhōu Pútí** 灃州菩提, **Yuánzhōu Tàihé** 沅州太和.

**Disciples.** Two *fǎsì*: **Fǎzhù Hǎidòng** 法柱海棟 [[海棟]] (compiler of his *yǔlù*, in turn author of [[KR6q0525]]) and **Zhǐàn Hǎicí** 止岸海慈.

**Work.** [[KR6q0524]] 《浦峰長明炅禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B401, compiled by *fǎsì* **Hǎidòng** [[海棟]] *biān*.
