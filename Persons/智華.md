# Zhì-huá 智華

Northern-Sòng Chán disciple (*cān-xué* 參學) of Bái-yún Shǒu-duān 白雲守端 (1025–1072). Co-editor of his master's *guǎng lù* [[KR6q0286]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
