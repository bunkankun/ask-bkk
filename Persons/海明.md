Pòshān Hǎimíng 破山海明 (Wàn-lì 25 *dīng-yǒu* 1/21 wǔ-hour = 8 March 1597 – Kāng-xī 5 *bǐng-wǔ* 3/16 hài-hour = 19 April 1666) was the thirty-fifth-generation Línjì (Yángqí-branch) patriarch and the sole Sichuan-based dharma-heir of **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] 密雲圓悟 (1566/7–1642). Lay surname **Jiǎn** 蹇 (branch of the Yǒng-lè chief grand secretary Jiǎn Yì 蹇義 1364–1435, the 忠定公), lay name **Jiǎn Dòng-yǔ** 蹇棟宇, self-styled **Lǎn-yú** 懶愚. Native of **Dà-zhú-xiàn** 大竹縣 in **Shùn-qìng-fǔ** 順慶府 (modern Nán-chōng area, Sì-chuān). Tonsured at 19 *sùi* (1615) at Fó-ēn-sì 佛恩寺 in his home district under the elder **Dà-chí** 大持; the Shǒu-léng-yán crisis on *cháng-zhù zhēn-xīn* drove him out of Sichuan; trained three years at **Pò-tóu-shān** 破頭山 at Huáng-méi (whence the *bié-hào* **Pò-shān**), where a cliff-side self-oath and literal fall from the precipice resolved his doubt. Full ordination under **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄 in *guǐ-hài* (1623) at age 27 *sùi*. Joined **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Jīn-sù 金粟 in *jiǎ-zǐ* (1624); received the *Cáo-qī zhèng-mài lái-yuán* 曹谿正脈來源 transmission-slip in *dīng-mǎo* (1627).

Fourteen abbacies across a 37-year preaching career, beginning with **Jiā-hé Dōng-tǎ** 嘉禾東塔 in *jǐ-sì* autumn (1629) and **Liáng-shān Wàn-fēng** 梁山萬峰 from *guǐ-yǒu* (1633) when he returned to Sichuan. After 1644 sheltered as village-level *hù-fǎ* chaplain in Sichuan-interior monasteries through the Zhāng Xiàn-zhōng devastations, and in Shùn-zhì *rén-chén/guǐ-sì* (1652–53) founded **Shuāng-guì-sì** 雙桂寺 at Jīn-chéng-zhài (Liáng-píng, modern Chóng-qìng) on the remains of a Míng-elite villa with a pair of surviving ancient *guì* 桂 trees. Died in residence at Shuāng-guì in spring 1666, *shì-shòu* 70 / *sēng-là* 43, eighty-seven dharma-heirs enumerated including **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (Zhāo-jué line), **Lián-yuè Yìn-zhèng** [[印正]] 蓮月印正 (Yù-quán line), **Yàn-jū Hé-shēn** 燕居合申, **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** 聖可德玉 (Huá-yán line), and **Kōng-gǔ Dào-chéng** 空谷道澄. Full-body stūpa at **Gèn-lóng-shān** 艮龍山; shadow-hall at Chéng-dū Zhāo-jué-sì.

Notable Kanripo works by Pòshān: [[KR6q0402]] 《破山禪師語錄》 20 juan (J26 B177, the complete Yìn-zhèng recension of 1680). Other non-Kanripo works include the twelve-juan Zhāo-jué edition of 1657 (compiled by Zhàng-xuě), 《山居詩》, 《雙桂草》, and scattered inscriptions in 《錦江禪燈》 (also by Zhàng-xuě). The sole Mìyún-line patriarch whose recorded sayings anchor a full Sichuanese "Shǔ-zōng" 蜀宗 lineage, and the subject of Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (Oxford 2008) chs. 4 and 7; also central to the early-Qīng military-chaplaincy material (the "*jiǔ-ròu sēng*" apologetic, the 1644+ Lǐ Yào-zi / Lǐ Yī-yáng episode).
