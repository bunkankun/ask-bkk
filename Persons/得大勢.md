# 得大勢
Dédàshì (Sanskrit: Mahāsthāmaprāpta)

Mahāyāna bodhisattva, "attainer of great power"; one of the principal interlocutors in the Pure Land sūtras and frequently paired with Avalokiteśvara (觀世音) as attendant of Amitābha.
