**Jiāng Yìngsù** 江應宿 (*fl.* late Míng), son of [[江瓘]] Jiāng Guàn. Continuator and supplementer of his father's ***Míngyī lèiàn*** 名醫類案 ([[KR3e0075]]) — adding case-records of late-Míng physicians to the original 1549 compilation. Has no separate transmitted biographical record beyond his role on his father's work.
