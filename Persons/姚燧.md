The singular Yuán-period 元 *gǔ-wén* master, characterized by the imperial-Yuán editors of his collection as the Yuán-dynastic equivalent of Sī-mǎ Qiān, Bān Gù, Yáng Xióng (Hàn), Hán Yù, Liǔ Zōng-yuán (Táng), Ōu-yáng Xiū, Sū Shì (Sòng). *Zì* **Duān-fǔ** 端甫; *hào* **Mù-ān** 牧庵 ("Herding-Hut"). Native of **Yíng-zhōu Liǔ-chéng** 營州柳城 (modern Liáo-níng).

**Career.** Rose through the Yuán imperial bureaucracy to *Hàn-lín xué-shì chéng-zhǐ* 翰林學士承旨 — the senior Hàn-lín literary office. His prose became the canonical model for Yuán-period imperial state-papers, *bēi-míng* (steles), and *mù-zhì* (epitaphs) for the senior Mongol-aristocratic and Hàn-Confucian elite.

**Principal corpus.** The 50-*juàn* / 689-piece *Mù-ān jí* [[KR4d0465]], canonized by Wú Shàn's 1332 collation at the Jiāng-zhè Rú-xué tí-jǔ-sī under the supervision of Yáo's disciple **Liú Zhì** 劉致 [[劉致]]. The composition comprises 3 *gǔ-fù*, 222 *shī*, 38 *xù*, 53 *jì*, **140 *bēi-míng* and *mù-zhì*** (the largest single category — Yáo was the senior Yuán composer of imperial-grade memorial inscriptions for founding-Mongol-aristocratic families), 58 *zhì-gào* (imperial-decree drafts), 2 *zhuàn*, 15 *zàn*, 11 *shuō*, 10 *zhù-cè*, 13 *zá-zhù*, and 124 *yuè-fǔ* (*cí*-form).

**Notable compositions.** Yáo's *mù-jié míng* for **Téng Ān-shàng** 滕安上 [[滕安上]] (preserved in this collection) is the principal contemporary biographical document for that figure.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0465]] *Mù-ān jí* 牧庵集 (撰).
