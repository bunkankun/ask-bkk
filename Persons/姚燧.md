The singular Yuán-period 元 *gǔwén* master, characterized by the imperial-Yuán editors of his collection as the Yuán-dynastic equivalent of Sīmǎ Qiān, Bān Gù, Yáng Xióng (Hàn), Hán Yù, Liǔ Zōngyuán (Táng), Ōuyáng Xiū, Sū Shì (Sòng). *Zì* **Duānfǔ** 端甫; *hào* **Mùān** 牧庵 ("Herding-Hut"). Native of **Yíngzhōu Liǔchéng** 營州柳城 (modern Liáoníng).

**Career.** Rose through the Yuán imperial bureaucracy to *Hànlín xuéshì chéngzhǐ* 翰林學士承旨 — the senior Hànlín literary office. His prose became the canonical model for Yuán-period imperial state-papers, *bēimíng* (steles), and *mùzhì* (epitaphs) for the senior Mongol-aristocratic and Hàn-Confucian elite.

**Principal corpus.** The 50-*juàn* / 689-piece *Mùān jí* [[KR4d0465]], canonized by Wú Shàn's 1332 collation at the Jiāngzhè Rúxué tíjǔsī under the supervision of Yáo's disciple **Liú Zhì** 劉致 [[劉致]]. The composition comprises 3 *gǔfù*, 222 *shī*, 38 *xù*, 53 *jì*, **140 *bēimíng* and *mùzhì*** (the largest single category — Yáo was the senior Yuán composer of imperial-grade memorial inscriptions for founding-Mongol-aristocratic families), 58 *zhìgào* (imperial-decree drafts), 2 *zhuàn*, 15 *zàn*, 11 *shuō*, 10 *zhùcè*, 13 *zázhù*, and 124 *yuèfǔ* (*cí*-form).

**Notable compositions.** Yáo's *mùjié míng* for **Téng Ānshàng** 滕安上 [[滕安上]] (preserved in this collection) is the principal contemporary biographical document for that figure.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0465]] *Mùān jí* 牧庵集 (撰).
