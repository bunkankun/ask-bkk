Xiè Huì 謝晦 (390–426 CE; *zì* Xuǎnmíng 宣明) was a Liú Sòng 劉宋 general and court official from Chéngjùn 陳郡 Yángxià 陽夏. He was a key military supporter of Liú Yù 劉裕 in the founding of the Liu Song dynasty, reaching high military commands over the Jīng and Yù regions. He rebelled against Emperor Wén 文帝 in 426 CE and was captured and executed. Known for his 〈鼓吹鐃歌十五首〉 preserved in *Sòngshū Yuèzhì*. Biography in *Sòngshū* 宋書 juǎn 44 and *Nán shǐ* 南史 juǎn 19. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0041]].
