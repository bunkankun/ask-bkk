**Shūxiāng jūshì** 蔬香居士 ("the Layman of Vegetable-Fragrance Studio") is the *hào* of an unidentified Republican-period (*Mínguó*) scholar who prefaced the 1925 reprinting of Zhōu Zhèn 周震's Qīng paediatric handbook *Yòukē zhǐnán* 幼科指南 ([[KR3ej016]]) on behalf of his contemporary, **Hénglú zhǔrén** 衡廬主人 ("the Master of the Hengshan-cottage"). The preface is dated 民國十四年歲次乙丑孟夏月 = 1925 fourth lunar month. The personal names of both Shūxiāng jūshì and Hénglú zhǔrén are not known.
