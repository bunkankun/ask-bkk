Chāojiàn 超見, *zì* **Liǎozōng** 了宗 ("Penetrating the School"); often referred to as **Pánshān Liǎozōng** 盤山了宗. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Dàbó Xíngqián** [[乾]] (1602–1673, cf. [[KR6q0584]]). Born **明萬曆甲寅九月十五日** = **1614-10-17** Gregorian; died **康熙二十七年** = **1688**, age 75. Native of **Shùntiānfǔ Bǎodǐxiàn** 順天府寶坻縣 (Héběi); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Vegetarian from childhood. After an illness in youth, his parents permitted ordination. Tonsured at **Lóng-quán-ān** 龍泉菴, took full ordination at **Mǐn-zhōng-sì** 愍忠寺 (the major Beijing precept-platform), studied scriptures. One day during a *Śūraṅgama* lecture at the *qī chù zhēng xīn, bā huán biàn jiàn* 七處徵心八還辯見 passage, suddenly had insight. Went south to *cān*-fǎng — **Tiān-tóng**, **Xuě-dòu**, **Tiān-tāi**. Returned to study under **Dà-bó Xíng-qián** [[乾]] and received final *fù-fǎ*. Served three years closely.

**Abbacies.** Began with retreat at **Rénshòusì** 仁壽寺, then moved to **Pánshān shíshì** 盤山石室. Then held a sequence of major Héběi abbacies: **Nántāi** 南臺, **Lóngyún** 隆阭, **Wànshànsì** 萬善寺, **Zhèngfǎsì** 正法寺 (= **Pánshān Gǔzhōngpán Zhèngfǎsì**, the principal seat), **Fàngshēngyuàn** 放生院, **Pǔdùsì** 普渡寺, **Guānyīnsì** 觀音寺, **Rénshèngsì** 仁聖寺.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* include **Huīzhōu Hào** [[昊]] (cf. [[KR6q0573]]), **Níngyī Xiān** 寧一暹, **Lǎngkōng Shùn** [[順]] (cf. [[KR6q0585]]), **Chèxuān Xīng** 徹軒惺, plus others.

**Work.** [[KR6q0586]] 《盤山了宗禪師語錄》 4 juan, J40 B474, recorded by *fǎsì* **Chèfán** [[徹凡]] and **Xīnyuè** [[心月]] *děng lù*.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 93, vol. 106; 《盤山了宗禪師語錄》 vol. 4 〈行實〉.
