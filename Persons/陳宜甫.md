Yuán-period 元 imperial-attendant and minor poet. *Hào* **Qiūyán** 秋巖 ("Autumn-Cliff"). Native of **Mǐn** 閩 (Fújiàn). The catalog meta gives lifedates 1255–1299; no firm CBDB match located (the Sìkù editors reconstructed his biography from internal evidence of his poetry collection in the absence of preserved external biographical record).

**Career.**
- Imperial-attendant (*shìcóng* 侍從) under **Khubilai** 元世祖. Accompanied the imperial procession north in *Gēngchén* (Zhìyuán 17 = 1280) — recorded in his *Gēngchén zài suí jià běi xíng* poems.
- Documented residence at **Hélín** 和林 (Karakorum) in Mongolia — established by the letter-poem to Liú Jièchén.
- Subordinate (*liáoshǔ* 僚屬) of **Jìnwáng Gāmǎlā** 晉王噶瑪喇 (the Yuán imperial prince Kammala, eldest son of Yùzōng) under **Chéngzōng**. Recorded in his *Bǐngshēn shíyuè hùcóng Jìnwáng lǐng xiángbīng rù jīng cháojìn shī* — the *Yuánzhēn 2* (1296) tenth-month entry of surrendered troops from rebel-prince **Yǒnghéěr** 永和爾 into the imperial capital.

**Network.** Exchange-poetry with **Lú Zhì** 盧摰 (1242–1314), **Yáo Suì** [[姚燧]] (1238–1313), **Zhào Mèngfǔ** [[趙孟頫]] (1254–1322), **Chéng Jùfū** [[程鉅夫]] (1249–1318), and **Liú Mèngyán** 留夢炎 — but these figures' own collections rarely reciprocate Chén, accounting for his historiographical obscurity.

**Poetic style.** Yuán-Zhěn-Bái-Jūyì origin; *píngzhèng tōngdá* (level-correct, conveyed-clearly). The Sìkù editors evaluate it as "an elegant-sound" though without deep originality.
