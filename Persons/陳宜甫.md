Yuán-period 元 imperial-attendant and minor poet. *Hào* **Qiū-yán** 秋巖 ("Autumn-Cliff"). Native of **Mǐn** 閩 (Fú-jiàn). The catalog meta gives lifedates 1255–1299; no firm CBDB match located (the Sìkù editors reconstructed his biography from internal evidence of his poetry collection in the absence of preserved external biographical record).

**Career.**
- Imperial-attendant (*shì-cóng* 侍從) under **Khubilai** 元世祖. Accompanied the imperial procession north in *Gēng-chén* (Zhì-yuán 17 = 1280) — recorded in his *Gēng-chén zài suí jià běi xíng* poems.
- Documented residence at **Hé-lín** 和林 (Karakorum) in Mongolia — established by the letter-poem to Liú Jiè-chén.
- Subordinate (*liáo-shǔ* 僚屬) of **Jìn-wáng Gā-mǎ-lā** 晉王噶瑪喇 (the Yuán imperial prince Kammala, eldest son of Yù-zōng) under **Chéng-zōng**. Recorded in his *Bǐng-shēn shí-yuè hù-cóng Jìn-wáng lǐng xiáng-bīng rù jīng cháo-jìn shī* — the *Yuán-zhēn 2* (1296) tenth-month entry of surrendered troops from rebel-prince **Yǒng-hé-ěr** 永和爾 into the imperial capital.

**Network.** Exchange-poetry with **Lú Zhì** 盧摰 (1242–1314), **Yáo Suì** [[姚燧]] (1238–1313), **Zhào Mèng-fǔ** [[趙孟頫]] (1254–1322), **Chéng Jù-fū** [[程鉅夫]] (1249–1318), and **Liú Mèng-yán** 留夢炎 — but these figures' own collections rarely reciprocate Chén, accounting for his historiographical obscurity.

**Poetic style.** Yuán-Zhěn-Bái-Jū-yì origin; *píng-zhèng tōng-dá* (level-correct, conveyed-clearly). The Sìkù editors evaluate it as "an elegant-sound" though without deep originality.
