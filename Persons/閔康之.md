# Mǐn Kāngzhī 閔康之

Eastern Jìn 東晉 scholar of Jīngkǒu 京口 (modern Zhènjiāng 鎮江, Jiāngsū). According to the Sìkù *tiyao* of [[KR1a0007]] (citing the *Cèfǔ yuánguī* 冊府元龜), he wrote a defence of [[王弼]] Wáng Bì's *Yì* against the rebuttal by [[顧夸]] Gù Kuā (Gù Yuèzhī). The work is lost; he is known only through this notice.
