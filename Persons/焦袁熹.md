Jiāo Yuánxī 焦袁熹 (1661–1736), zì Guǎngqī 廣期, native of Jīnshān 金山 (Sōngjiāng 松江 prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Kāngxī bǐngzǐ* (1696) *jǔrén*. He held no major office and lived as a retired *jīngxué* scholar; he is one of the *Sìkù* editors' favourite early-Qing *Chūnqiū* commentators and is bracketed with Xú Tíngyuán 徐庭垣 ([[KR1e0106]]) for restoring sober interpretation against the harsh moralism of the Sūn Fù / Hú Ānguó tradition. The catalog gives 1660–1725; CBDB and Wikidata give 1661–1736, followed here. His *Chūnqiū quē rú biān* 春秋闕如編 ([[KR1e0108]]) was unfinished at his death, breaking off at Chéng-gōng 8; its grandson-edited manuscript reached the *Sìkù* in autograph form.
