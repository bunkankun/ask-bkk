Dào-zhì 道智, *hào* **Yún-fù** 雲腹 (also **Xī-fēng** 西峰). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiàng-yái Xìng-tǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (1598–1651) in the Pòshān → Xìng-tǐng line. Born **1613** (壬子 5月午時 — mother dreamed of a monk with begging-bowl); died **癸丑 5/29 = 1673-07-13 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **61**, *fǎ-là* **42**. Native of **Qú-xiàn** 渠縣 (Shùn-qìng, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Lǐ** 李, mother **Hé-shì** 何氏.

**Heirs.** **Huì-yě Yì-xǐng** 會也益惺; **Huì-yuè Yì-yǎn** 慧月益眼.

**Work.** [[KR6q0480]] 《雲腹智禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B309, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Yuè-xián** [[嶽賢]] *jì-lù* + **Lián-shēng** [[聯昇]] *jì-lù*. Sources: 《雲腹智禪師語錄》 j. 2; DILA authority A011951.
