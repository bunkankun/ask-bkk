Dàozhì 道智, *hào* **Yúnfù** 雲腹 (also **Xīfēng** 西峰). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Xiàngyái Xìngtǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (1598–1651) in the Pòshān → Xìngtǐng line. Born **1613** (壬子 5月午時 — mother dreamed of a monk with begging-bowl); died **癸丑 5/29 = 1673-07-13 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **61**, *fǎlà* **42**. Native of **Qúxiàn** 渠縣 (Shùnqìng, Sìchuān); lay surname **Lǐ** 李, mother **Héshì** 何氏.

**Heirs.** **Huìyě Yìxǐng** 會也益惺; **Huìyuè Yìyǎn** 慧月益眼.

**Work.** [[KR6q0480]] 《雲腹智禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B309, recorded by *fǎsì* **Yuèxián** [[嶽賢]] *jìlù* + **Liánshēng** [[聯昇]] *jìlù*. Sources: 《雲腹智禪師語錄》 j. 2; DILA authority A011951.
