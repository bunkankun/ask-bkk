Níng 寧 (full dharma-name **Dào-níng** 道寧), *zì* **Tǐ-zōng** 體宗 ("Bodily-Tradition"); often referred to as **Tǐ-zōng Dào-níng** 體宗道寧 or **Yún-fēng Tǐ-zōng** 雲峰體宗 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **明萬曆戊戌六月初十日午時** = **1598-07-12** Gregorian; died **康熙八年** = **1669**, age 72, *sēng-là* 37. Native of **Lú-zhōu** 瀘州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** At 15 left home (told only his mother). At 25 traveled to attend lectures. At 35 met **Shuāng-guì** 雙桂 (= [[海明]] **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng**); served at his side for ten years and received final *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Lú-zhōu Fāng-shān Yún-fēng-sì** 瀘州方山雲峰寺 (the principal seat — entered **順治九年三月十三日** = **1652-04-21**, on the invitation of *Chuān-nán-dào* official **Wú Dēng** 吳登 of Lú-zhōu); **Jiāng-ān Pán-lóng-sì** 江安蟠龍寺; **Nán-xī Kāi-yuán-sì** 南溪開元寺. The Yún-fēng-sì abbacy is famous for having ordained the young **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] in **丁酉冬** = **1657** winter (cf. [[KR6q0544]]).

**Disciples (six).** **Cáng-zhōu Pǔ-zé** 藏舟普澤, **Qīng-sù Dé-nè** 清素德訥, **Xiào-xū Xìng-kōng** 嘯虛性空, **Dàn-xuán Fāng-yáo** 澹玄方瑤, **Wéi-mì Dào-shēn** 微密道深, **Zhàn-yī Xù-qīng** 湛一續清 [[續清]] (compiler of the *yǔ-lù*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0548]] 《雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B431, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhàn-yī Xù-qīng** [[續清]] *děng biān*. Two prefaces: by **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] (signed 華岩法弟德玉) dated **康熙乙丑孟夏浴佛日** = **1685-05-10**, and by **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] (signed 青城法姪徹生) dated **康熙乙亥正陽佛誕之吉** = **1695-05-19**.

**Sources.** 《雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉, 〈分燈〉; 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 9; 《五燈全書》 vol. 88.
