Níng 寧 (full dharma-name **Dàoníng** 道寧), *zì* **Tǐzōng** 體宗 ("Bodily-Tradition"); often referred to as **Tǐzōng Dàoníng** 體宗道寧 or **Yúnfēng Tǐzōng** 雲峰體宗 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **明萬曆戊戌六月初十日午時** = **1598-07-12** Gregorian; died **康熙八年** = **1669**, age 72, *sēnglà* 37. Native of **Lúzhōu** 瀘州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** At 15 left home (told only his mother). At 25 traveled to attend lectures. At 35 met **Shuāngguì** 雙桂 (= [[海明]] **Pòshān Hǎimíng**); served at his side for ten years and received final *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Lúzhōu Fāngshān Yúnfēngsì** 瀘州方山雲峰寺 (the principal seat — entered **順治九年三月十三日** = **1652-04-21**, on the invitation of *Chuānnándào* official **Wú Dēng** 吳登 of Lúzhōu); **Jiāngān Pánlóngsì** 江安蟠龍寺; **Nánxī Kāiyuánsì** 南溪開元寺. The Yúnfēngsì abbacy is famous for having ordained the young **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] in **丁酉冬** = **1657** winter (cf. [[KR6q0544]]).

**Disciples (six).** **Cángzhōu Pǔzé** 藏舟普澤, **Qīngsù Dénè** 清素德訥, **Xiàoxū Xìngkōng** 嘯虛性空, **Dànxuán Fāngyáo** 澹玄方瑤, **Wéimì Dàoshēn** 微密道深, **Zhànyī Xùqīng** 湛一續清 [[續清]] (compiler of the *yǔlù*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0548]] 《雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B431, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhànyī Xùqīng** [[續清]] *děng biān*. Two prefaces: by **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] (signed 華岩法弟德玉) dated **康熙乙丑孟夏浴佛日** = **1685-05-10**, and by **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] (signed 青城法姪徹生) dated **康熙乙亥正陽佛誕之吉** = **1695-05-19**.

**Sources.** 《雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉, 〈分燈〉; 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 9; 《五燈全書》 vol. 88.
