Yuán-period 元 *Shàngshū* sub-commentator. Native of Pénglí 彭蠡 — i.e., the Pénglí Lake region (modern Lake Poyang 鄱陽湖) in Jiāngxī. Signs himself "*Dōng huì zé*" 東滙澤 ("Eastern Confluence Marsh") in self-attribution. Lifedates and detailed career are unrecorded; CBDB id 27572 has only an index year of 1291. The Sìkù *tíyào* states explicitly that "the beginning and end of his career cannot be ascertained." His one surviving work, the *Shū Càizhuàn pángtōng* 書蔡傳旁通 ([[KR1b0031]]) in 6 *juǎn*, is precisely dated by his autograph preface to Zhìzhì 1 / 1321 (至治辛酉). Geographic and intellectual proximity to [[董鼎]] of Póyáng ([[KR1b0029]]) — both Lake-Pénglí region scholars — is presumably not coincidental: Chén Shīkǎi's preface explicitly positions the *Pángtōng* as a beginner-oriented complement to Dǒng Dǐng's more advanced *Shū zhuàn jí lù zuǎn zhù*.
