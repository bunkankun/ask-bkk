# Zōng-jiān 宗堅 (仝 "together")

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[福慧|Sōng-shān Yě-zhú Fú-huì]] 嵩山野竹福慧 in the Pò-shān / Yuán-liáo / Shān-huī / Fú-huì Yún-nán mission sub-line. Co-editor (with [[宗上]] Zōng-shàng) of [[KR6q0438]] 《嵩山野竹禪師錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B224).

The **仝** character in "宗堅仝" in the catalog-meta is a Chinese "together / same" marker (= 同) indicating "Zōng-jiān together [with]" — i.e., the co-editor tag rather than part of the fǎ-huì. The name proper is **Zōng-jiān** 宗堅.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0438]] catalog-meta "*biān 宗上 宗堅仝*" (= "edited by Zōng-shàng and Zōng-jiān together").
