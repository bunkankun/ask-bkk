# Zōngjiān 宗堅 (仝 "together")

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[福慧|Sōngshān Yězhú Fúhuì]] 嵩山野竹福慧 in the Pòshān / Yuánliáo / Shānhuī / Fúhuì Yúnnán mission sub-line. Co-editor (with [[宗上]] Zōngshàng) of [[KR6q0438]] 《嵩山野竹禪師錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B224).

The **仝** character in "宗堅仝" in the catalog-meta is a Chinese "together / same" marker (= 同) indicating "Zōngjiān together [with]" — i.e., the co-editor tag rather than part of the fǎhuì. The name proper is **Zōngjiān** 宗堅.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0438]] catalog-meta "*biān 宗上 宗堅仝*" (= "edited by Zōngshàng and Zōngjiān together").
