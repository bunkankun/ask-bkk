# 王民鶴 Wáng Mínhú

**Wáng Mínhú** 王民鶴, modern Chinese medical lexicographer; editor of the *Zhōngguó yīxué dàcídiǎn* 中國醫學大辭典 (Shanghai, 1921; many later reprints), an early-Republican comprehensive dictionary of Chinese medicine.
