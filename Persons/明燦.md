Míngcàn 明燦, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Tiānmù / Qírán Chāozhì** [[超智]]. Co-compiler with **Xiùyě Mínglín** [[明林]] of the 2-juan 《奇然智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0495]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
