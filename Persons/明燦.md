Míng-càn 明燦, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Tiān-mù / Qí-rán Chāo-zhì** [[超智]]. Co-compiler with **Xiù-yě Míng-lín** [[明林]] of the 2-juan 《奇然智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0495]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
