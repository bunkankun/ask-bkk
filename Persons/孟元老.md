Mèng Yuán-lǎo 孟元老 (*fl. Chóng-níng* 1103 – *Shào-xīng* 1147), *hào* Yōu-lán jū-shì 幽蘭居士. The name is almost certainly a *hào* rather than the author's actual name; modern scholarship (after Dèng Zhī-chéng 鄧之誠 1959) has tentatively identified him with one Mèng Yuè 孟鉞 of the Mèng family of Bó-zhōu 亳州, a low-level Northern-Sòng *huì-jì sī* official, but no identification is universally accepted (see Stephen H. West, "The Interpretation of a Dream," *T'oung Pao* 71 [1985]: 63–108). According to his autograph preface (dated the last day of *Shào-xīng* 17 = early 1148), he had followed his father in official travel to the south and the north; in *Chóng-níng* 2 *guǐ-wèi* (1103) he reached the metropolis Biàn-jīng and settled at the western *Jīn-liáng qiáo*, where he lived through the Northern Sòng's last decades of peace; after the catastrophe of *Jìng-kāng bǐng-wǔ* (1126) he fled south to the Jiāng-zuǒ region. In *Shào-xīng* 17 (1147) at Hángzhōu he completed the *Dōngjīng mèng-huá lù* 東京夢華錄 ([[KR2k0111]]), the principal documentary source for the urban culture of the Northern-Sòng capital. No further biographical information is preserved.
