Mèng Yuánlǎo 孟元老 (*fl. Chóngníng* 1103 – *Shàoxīng* 1147), *hào* Yōulán jūshì 幽蘭居士. The name is almost certainly a *hào* rather than the author's actual name; modern scholarship (after Dèng Zhīchéng 鄧之誠 1959) has tentatively identified him with one Mèng Yuè 孟鉞 of the Mèng family of Bózhōu 亳州, a low-level Northern-Sòng *huìjì sī* official, but no identification is universally accepted (see Stephen H. West, "The Interpretation of a Dream," *T'oung Pao* 71 [1985]: 63–108). According to his autograph preface (dated the last day of *Shàoxīng* 17 = early 1148), he had followed his father in official travel to the south and the north; in *Chóngníng* 2 *guǐwèi* (1103) he reached the metropolis Biànjīng and settled at the western *Jīnliáng qiáo*, where he lived through the Northern Sòng's last decades of peace; after the catastrophe of *Jìngkāng bǐngwǔ* (1126) he fled south to the Jiāngzuǒ region. In *Shàoxīng* 17 (1147) at Hángzhōu he completed the *Dōngjīng mènghuá lù* 東京夢華錄 ([[KR2k0111]]), the principal documentary source for the urban culture of the Northern-Sòng capital. No further biographical information is preserved.
