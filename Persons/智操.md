Zhì-cāo / Zhì-cào 智操, *hào* **Hán-sōng** 寒松 ("Cold Pine"), also **Yǐn-wēng** 隱翁. Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Bǎi-yú Jìng-sī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (1610–1665) in the Ruì-bái Míng-xuě → Bǎi-yú line. Born **天啟丙寅 8/18** = **1626-10-07 Gregorian**; died **康熙戊辰 = 1688-02-02/1689-01-20** Gregorian, *shì-shòu* **63**. Native of **Tóng-chéng** 桐城 (Ān-huī); lay surname **Yán** 嚴.

Active at **Sōng-jiāng Lóng-fú** 松江隆福.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0498]] 《百愚禪師語錄》 — co-compiled with **Zhì-hǎi** [[智海]] his master Bǎi-yú's 20-juan *yǔ-lù*.
- [[KR6q0517]] 《寒松操禪師語錄》 20 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B392 — his own *yǔ-lù*, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-hào** [[德昊]] *biān*; juan 17 *zì-xù*.
