Zhìcāo / Zhìcào 智操, *hào* **Hánsōng** 寒松 ("Cold Pine"), also **Yǐnwēng** 隱翁. Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Bǎiyú Jìngsī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (1610–1665) in the Ruìbái Míngxuě → Bǎiyú line. Born **天啟丙寅 8/18** = **1626-10-07 Gregorian**; died **康熙戊辰 = 1688-02-02/1689-01-20** Gregorian, *shìshòu* **63**. Native of **Tóngchéng** 桐城 (Ānhuī); lay surname **Yán** 嚴.

Active at **Sōngjiāng Lóngfú** 松江隆福.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0498]] 《百愚禪師語錄》 — co-compiled with **Zhìhǎi** [[智海]] his master Bǎiyú's 20-juan *yǔlù*.
- [[KR6q0517]] 《寒松操禪師語錄》 20 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B392 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by *fǎsì* **Déhào** [[德昊]] *biān*; juan 17 *zìxù*.

Sources: 《寒松操禪師語錄》 j. 17 *zìxù*; 《宗統編年》 j. 32; DILA A016512.
