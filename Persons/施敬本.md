Shī Jìngběn 施敬本 (fl. early 8th c.; CBDB id 32099), Táng *Jíxián diàn* 集賢殿 academician. Named in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* among the seven compilers of Xú Jiān's *Chūxué jì* 初學記 ([[KR3k0006]]) under Táng Xuánzōng's commission of Kāiyuán 13 (725). The *Cèfǔ yuánguī* 冊府元龜 records him serving as *Tàicháng bóshì* 太常博士 in the Kāiyuán reign and contributing to ritual reform debates. No independent biography survives in the Two Táng Histories.
