Shī Jìng-běn 施敬本 (fl. early 8th c.; CBDB id 32099), Táng *Jí-xián diàn* 集賢殿 academician. Named in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* among the seven compilers of Xú Jiān's *Chū-xué jì* 初學記 ([[KR3k0006]]) under Táng Xuán-zōng's commission of Kāi-yuán 13 (725). The *Cè-fǔ yuán-guī* 冊府元龜 records him serving as *Tài-cháng bóshì* 太常博士 in the Kāi-yuán reign and contributing to ritual reform debates. No independent biography survives in the Two Táng Histories.
