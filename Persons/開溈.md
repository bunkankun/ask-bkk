# Kāi-wéi 開溈

Late-17th-century Qīng Chán monk; disciple of [[弘贊|Zàishēn Hóngzàn]] 在犙弘贊 (1612–1686) at Dǐnghú-shān Qìngyún-sì 鼎湖山慶雲寺. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of his master's *Mù rén shèng gǎo* 木人剩稿 [[KR6q0223]] — Hóngzàn's five-juan supplementary literary collection.
