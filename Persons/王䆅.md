Wáng Bǎo 王䆅 (mid-15th cent.), elder son of the *Lìbù shàngshū* **Wáng Zhí** 王直 ([[王直]]) of **Tàihé** 泰和. *Jiǎntǎo* 檢討 in the *Hànlínyuàn*. Edited the original 13-*juǎn* recension of his father's collected works, [[KR4e0095]] *Yìān wénjí*. No further biographical details preserved in standard sources.
