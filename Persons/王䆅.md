Wáng Bǎo 王䆅 (mid-15th cent.), elder son of the *Lì-bù shàng-shū* **Wáng Zhí** 王直 ([[王直]]) of **Tài-hé** 泰和. *Jiǎn-tǎo* 檢討 in the *Hàn-lín-yuàn*. Edited the original 13-*juǎn* recension of his father's collected works, [[KR4e0095]] *Yì-ān wén-jí*. No further biographical details preserved in standard sources.
