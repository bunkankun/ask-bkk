Jīn Hóng 金鉷 (1678–1740), *zì* Yǎngfēng 養峰, was a Hànjūn Bordered-Yellow Banner official and a key Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng frontier administrator. He served as Governor of Guǎngxī from 1727 to 1739, the longest tenure in that office during the Qīng, and superintended the principal phase of the *gǎitǔ guīliú* 改土歸流 reforms in the southwestern frontier. Under his governorship the Yōngzhèng-era *Guǎngxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0054]]) of 1733 in 128 *juan* was compiled.
