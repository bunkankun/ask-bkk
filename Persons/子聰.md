# Zǐcōng 子聰

Early-Yuán monk styled Tiāntái Zǐcōng 天台子聰; commissioner, together with [[德海]] Déhǎi of Jiànyán sì, of the Zhìdà 3–4 (1310–1311) reprint of the *Fényáng Wúdé chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0054]]) — the Yuán recension from which the Taishō witness descends.
