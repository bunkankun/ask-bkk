Chūn-yún Zhuó 春雲倬, *zì* Qīng-fǔ 清甫. Late-Qīng literatus. Author of the first preface to [[KR3eq092]] *Yī-yī xiǎo cǎo* by [[寶輝]] Bǎo Huī, dated *Guǎngxù* 27 (1901) late summer. He signs as Bǎo Huī's *yàn-yú dì* 硯愚弟 ("inkstone-fool younger brother") — a self-deprecating epistolary formula for an old fellow-student. The compound name *Chūn-yún* with the surname-position third character 倬 is consistent with a Manchu Bannerman name, supporting the inference (also from the surname 寶) that Bǎo Huī's social milieu was the Manchu Bannerman literate-medical community of the late-Qīng capital region. No standard biographical record located.
