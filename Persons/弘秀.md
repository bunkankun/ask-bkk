# Hóngxiù 弘秀

Late-17th / early-18th century Qīng Chán monk; dharma-heir of [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統 (1661–1717). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler (*biān* 編) of Xìngtǒng's *Biéān chánshī tóng mén lù* 別菴禪師同門錄 [[KR6q0233]] — a three-juan fellow-lineage genealogical record documenting the Línjì Chán brotherhood of the Sānshān Dēnglái line.
