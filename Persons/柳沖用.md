**Liǔ Chōngyòng** 柳沖用 was a Daoist alchemical poet, author of [[KR5c0331]] *Jù shèng gē* 巨勝歌 — a short "song" (*gē* 歌) on **giant-victory-sesame** (*jù shèng* 巨勝 = *hú má* 胡麻, black sesame), an important longevity and elixir ingredient. Biographical details are unrecorded.
