Lín Qìngqí 林慶祺 (fl. late 19th c.) prepared the *editio princeps* of [[熊笏]] Xióng Hù's *Zhōngfēng lùn* ([[KR3eh035]]) from manuscript copy in *Guāngxù jiǎshēn* (1884).
