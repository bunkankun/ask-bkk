**Shí Shòutáng** 石壽棠 (*hào* **Fúnán** 芾南; *zhāi* "studio name" 石壽堂), late-Qīng *xiàngshēng* / *jǔrén* of **Huátíng** 華亭 (modern Sōngjiāng district of Shànghǎi), active in the Xiánfēng — Guāngxù era (1850s–1890s). Scholar-physician of the **late-Qīng *yījīng tōngrán* 醫易通然 cosmological-medical synthesis** tradition, distinct from the better-known *kǎozhèng yīxué* line of [[徐大椿]] and [[莫枚士]]. Author of **[[KR3eq015]]** *Yīyuán* 醫原 (twenty doctrinal essays in two *juǎn*, manuscript circulating from the 1850s and first cut in 1861 with Zhōu Bǐngkuí's preface; the 1891 Zhāng Shēngchí edition is the standard cut). The catalog meta records the author as 石壽堂 (the studio-name), and the work itself uses 石芾南 / 石孝廉 in the prefaces; the personal name 石壽棠 is followed here in line with modern reference works. Not in CBDB.
