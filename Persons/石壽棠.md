**Shí Shòu-táng** 石壽棠 (*hào* **Fú-nán** 芾南; *zhāi* "studio name" 石壽堂), late-Qīng *xiàng-shēng* / *jǔ-rén* of **Huá-tíng** 華亭 (modern Sōng-jiāng district of Shànghǎi), active in the Xián-fēng — Guāng-xù era (1850s–1890s). Scholar-physician of the **late-Qīng *yī-jīng tōng-rán* 醫易通然 cosmological-medical synthesis** tradition, distinct from the better-known *kǎo-zhèng yī-xué* line of [[徐大椿]] and [[莫枚士]]. Author of **[[KR3eq015]]** *Yī-yuán* 醫原 (twenty doctrinal essays in two *juǎn*, manuscript circulating from the 1850s and first cut in 1861 with Zhōu Bǐng-kuí's preface; the 1891 Zhāng Shēng-chí edition is the standard cut). The catalog meta records the author as 石壽堂 (the studio-name), and the work itself uses 石芾南 / 石孝廉 in the prefaces; the personal name 石壽棠 is followed here in line with modern reference works. Not in CBDB.
