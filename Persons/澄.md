# Shèng'ān Dènggōng 聖安澄公

Jīn-dynasty Chán monk at the Shèng'ān monastery of Yànjīng 燕京; the first Chán teacher of [[耶律楚材]] Yēlü Chǔcái, as recorded in Yēlü's preface to [[KR6q0079]] *Cóngróng lù*.
