**Bǎi xuán zǐ** 百玄子 ("Master Hundred-Mysteries") was a pseudonymous late-Northern-Sòng Daoist author, of [[KR5d0103]] ***Jīn dān zhēn yī lùn*** 金丹真一論 — a discourse on the gold-elixir and the One-of-Truth.
