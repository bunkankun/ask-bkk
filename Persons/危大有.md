Early-Míng 明 Daoist scholar and compiler, author of the ten-*juàn* [[KR5c0100|*Dàodé zhēn jīng jí yì* 道德真經集義]] (DZ 712) — a collected-commentary compilation of the *Dàodé jīng* with preface dated 1387 and a secondary preface by the 43rd Celestial Master [[張宇初]] Zhāng Yǔchū dated 1393.

**Activity period.** Active during the **Hóng wǔ** 洪武 era (1368–1398) under Míng Tàizǔ 明太祖 ([[朱元璋]]). The 1387 preface-date is the only firm chronological anchor. No lifedates or biographical details are recorded beyond the compilation.

**Work.** His sole known work is the *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí yì* (DZ 712) — a collection of approximately sixteen commentaries on the *Lǎozǐ*. The compilation's interpretive orientation endorses the ***zhì shēn zhì guó*** 治身治國 ("governing the body and governing the state") reading of the *Dàodé jīng* — a dual framework that aligns Wēi Dàyǒu with:

1. **The Tāng-Sòng imperial commentary tradition** (Xuánzōng, Huīzōng, Míng Tàizǔ himself) that treats the *Lǎozǐ* as a political-philosophical text.
2. **The Southern-Sòng / Yuán *nèi dān* tradition** (as represented by Hé Xīn shān 何心山 and Lǐ Dàochún) that treats the scripture as a text of self-cultivation.

The compilation's *zhì shēn zhì guó* synthesis was evidently congenial to the early-Míng Zhèng yī Daoist establishment, hence Zhāng Yǔchū's endorsement-preface of 1393.

**Dating.** Active 1387 (preface date). No lifedates or further biographical details are recorded. No CBDB record identified.
