Lè Déhóng 勒德洪, Manchu official of the early Kāngxī 康熙 reign. Grand Secretary (dàxué shì 大學士) and one of the principal compilation directors at the Manchu-Han interface in the years immediately after the Three Feudatories war. He is named at the head of the *zǒngcái* 總裁 list of the imperially commissioned *Píngdìng sānnì fānglüè* 平定三逆方略 ([[KR2c0009]], commissioned Kāngxī 21 / 1682), with as fellow chief compilers Mínghū 明珠, Lǐ Wèi 李霨, Wáng Xī 王熙, Huáng Jī 黃機, and Wú Zhèngzhì 吳正治. He is conventionally cited as the lead compiler of that work. CBDB has him at personid 62398 without recorded lifedates; no firm birth or death year has been established. Beyond his role on the *Sānnì* commission and routine appearances in the Kāngxī-era veritable records, his career is sparsely documented.
