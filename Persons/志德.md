# 志德

Zhìdé (*fl.* late 6th century), a North Indian (Gandhāran) tripiṭaka master who arrived at the Suí court in 597 carrying a fresh Sanskrit manuscript of the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa-sūtra*. His new exemplar yielded the previously missing *Zhǔlèi pǐn* 囑累品 (entrustment chapter) and an additional *Yínzhǔ tuóluóní* 銀主陀羅尼 chapter, which [[寶貴]] incorporated into the composite [[KR6i0302]] *Hébù Jīnguāngmíng jīng*.
