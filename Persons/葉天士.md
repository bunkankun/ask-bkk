**Yè Tiānshì** 葉天士 (real name 葉桂 Yè Guì, *zì* Tiānshì 天士, *hào* Xiāngyán 香巖 / Shàngjīn lǎorén 上津老人, 1666–1745), pre-eminent early-Qīng physician of Sūzhōu and the founding figure of the *Wēnbìng* 溫病 (warm-disease) school. Originator of the *wèi qì yíng xuè* 衛氣營血 four-stage syndrome-differentiation framework systematised in *Wēnrè lùn* 溫熱論 (transmitted from his oral teaching by [[顧景文]] Gù Jǐngwén). The disciple-compiled *Línzhèng zhǐnán yī'àn* 臨證指南醫案 is the foundational case-record of the school; Yè's *yīàn* are cited extensively as authoritative throughout the Qīng *záběng* and dysentery literature (cf. [[KR3eh053]]).
