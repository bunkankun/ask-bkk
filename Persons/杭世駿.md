Mid-Qing scholar-official and philologist, *zì* Dà-zōng 大宗, *hào* Jǐn-pǔ 菫浦, of Rén-hé 仁和 (modern Hangzhou, Zhejiang). Granted *jǔ-rén* status 1724; one of fifteen successful candidates of Qián-lóng 1's *Bó-xué hóng-cí* examination (1736), appointed *Hànlín Yuàn biān-xiū*. Removed from office in 1741 after his memorial advocating equal Han-Manchu treatment in official appointments incurred imperial displeasure; thereafter pursued a private scholarly career. His philological works include the *Xù Fāngyán* [[KR1j0015]] (in Sìkù), the *Shǐjì kǎo-yì* 史記考異, the *Jǐn-pǔ shī-shuō* 菫浦詩説, and *Shí-qī-shǐ shāng-quē* 十七史商榷 (with Wáng Míng-shèng 王鳴盛). Author also of the major Qing reference work *Zhū-shǐ rán-yí* 諸史然疑. CBDB lifedates 1696–1773 in agreement with the catalog meta.
