Mid-Qing scholar-official and philologist, *zì* Dàzōng 大宗, *hào* Jǐnpǔ 菫浦, of Rénhé 仁和 (modern Hangzhou, Zhejiang). Granted *jǔrén* status 1724; one of fifteen successful candidates of Qiánlóng 1's *Bóxué hóngcí* examination (1736), appointed *Hànlín Yuàn biānxiū*. Removed from office in 1741 after his memorial advocating equal Han-Manchu treatment in official appointments incurred imperial displeasure; thereafter pursued a private scholarly career. His philological works include the *Xù Fāngyán* [[KR1j0015]] (in Sìkù), the *Shǐjì kǎoyì* 史記考異, the *Jǐnpǔ shīshuō* 菫浦詩説, and *Shíqīshǐ shāngquē* 十七史商榷 (with Wáng Míngshèng 王鳴盛). Author also of the major Qing reference work *Zhūshǐ rányí* 諸史然疑. CBDB lifedates 1696–1773 in agreement with the catalog meta.
