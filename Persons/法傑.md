Fǎ-jié 法傑, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Fó-yǔ Chè-yù** [[徹御]] 佛語徹御 (d. 1674) at **Yú-zhōu Xiāng-guó-sì** 渝州香國寺 (Chóng-qìng). Co-compiler with **Yì-táng Fǎ-xiù** [[法秀]] of the 1-juan 《佛語御禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0487]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
