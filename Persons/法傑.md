Fǎjié 法傑, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Fóyǔ Chèyù** [[徹御]] 佛語徹御 (d. 1674) at **Yúzhōu Xiāngguósì** 渝州香國寺 (Chóngqìng). Co-compiler with **Yìtáng Fǎxiù** [[法秀]] of the 1-juan 《佛語御禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0487]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
