**Cáo Bǐnggāng** 曹秉綱 (late Qīng, fl. 1886), of *Rénhé Lǐtáng* 仁和禮堂 (Hángzhōu), physician and collator of the *Nǚkē mìzhǐ* 女科秘旨 ([[KR3ei022]]); medical pupil of his father-in-law [[陳貞甫]] Chén Zhēnfǔ, and through that connection a follower of the [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ (Xiūyuán) pedagogical lineage.
