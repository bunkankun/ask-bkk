# Yìng-huái 應槐

Yuán Chán dharma-heir *xiǎo-shī* 嗣法小師 of [[清茂]] Gǔ-lín Qīng-mào (1262–1329); abbot of Ráo-zhōu-lù Chóng-bào chán-sì 饒州路崇報禪寺. Editor of *juan* 2 (Yǒng-fú abbacy, from Yánhòu 2 / 1315) of Qīng-mào's five-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0345]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
