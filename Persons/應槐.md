# Yìnghuái 應槐

Yuán Chán dharma-heir *xiǎoshī* 嗣法小師 of [[清茂]] Gǔlín Qīngmào (1262–1329); abbot of Ráozhōulù Chóngbào chánsì 饒州路崇報禪寺. Editor of *juan* 2 (Yǒngfú abbacy, from Yánhòu 2 / 1315) of Qīngmào's five-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0345]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
