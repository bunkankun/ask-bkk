Early-Northern-Sòng *gǔ-wén* polemicist, *zì* Zhòng-tú 仲塗 (originally *Shào-xuán* 紹元), of *Dà-míng-fǔ* 大名 (Hé-běi). Took the names *Kāi* 開 ("Opener") and *Zhòng-tú* in self-conscious emulation of Hán Yù 韓愈 — claiming to "open" the Way and to walk the middle road. *Jìn-shì* of Kāi-bǎo 6 / 973; held a long series of provincial *zhī-zhōu* posts (Cāng-zhōu, Dài-zhōu, Yīng-zhōu, Bīn-zhōu) and the *Diàn-shū-yùan zhī-zhì*; died in office at Bīn-zhōu in Xián-píng 3 / 1000, age 54. Author of the *Hé-dōng jí* [[KR4d0002]], whose programmatic *xù* and *shū* announced the Sòng *gǔ-wén* revival a generation before Ōuyáng Xiū. The transmitted text was edited by his disciple Zhāng Jǐng [[張景]]. The catalog meta gives Liǔ as "d. 995" but CBDB and *Sòng-shǐ* j. 440 give the standard date 1000, followed here. CBDB id 18341.
