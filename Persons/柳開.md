Early-Northern-Sòng *gǔwén* polemicist, *zì* Zhòngtú 仲塗 (originally *Shàoxuán* 紹元), of *Dàmíngfǔ* 大名 (Héběi). Took the names *Kāi* 開 ("Opener") and *Zhòngtú* in self-conscious emulation of Hán Yù 韓愈 — claiming to "open" the Way and to walk the middle road. *Jìnshì* of Kāibǎo 6 / 973; held a long series of provincial *zhīzhōu* posts (Cāngzhōu, Dàizhōu, Yīngzhōu, Bīnzhōu) and the *Diànshūyùan zhīzhì*; died in office at Bīnzhōu in Xiánpíng 3 / 1000, age 54. Author of the *Hédōng jí* [[KR4d0002]], whose programmatic *xù* and *shū* announced the Sòng *gǔwén* revival a generation before Ōuyáng Xiū. The transmitted text was edited by his disciple Zhāng Jǐng [[張景]]. The catalog meta gives Liǔ as "d. 995" but CBDB and *Sòngshǐ* j. 440 give the standard date 1000, followed here. CBDB id 18341.
