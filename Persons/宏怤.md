Hóngfú 宏怤, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Yúnwài Xíngzé** [[行澤]] 雲外行澤 (1607–1654). Likely identical with the **Xīngmèi chánshī** 惺寐禪師 named in the Wáng Guāngchéng preface to [[KR6q0463]] (also styled **Xīngjì** 惺寂 in some sources) — Hóngfú's *tóngcān xiōngdì* 同參兄弟 dharma-brother who personally collected and edited the *yǔlù* after Xíngzé's 1654 death (*Xīnggōng lì yě* 惺公力也, per the Wáng preface). Lead compiler of the 15-juan 《神鼎雲外澤禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0463]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Hóngfú děng biān* 宏怤等編 and in the j. 1 colophon as *cānxué ménrén* 參學門人.
