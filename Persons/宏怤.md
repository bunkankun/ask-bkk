Hóng-fú 宏怤, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Yún-wài Xíng-zé** [[行澤]] 雲外行澤 (1607–1654). Likely identical with the **Xīng-mèi chán-shī** 惺寐禪師 named in the Wáng Guāng-chéng preface to [[KR6q0463]] (also styled **Xīng-jì** 惺寂 in some sources) — Hóng-fú's *tóng-cān xiōng-dì* 同參兄弟 dharma-brother who personally collected and edited the *yǔ-lù* after Xíng-zé's 1654 death (*Xīng-gōng lì yě* 惺公力也, per the Wáng preface). Lead compiler of the 15-juan 《神鼎雲外澤禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0463]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Hóng-fú děng biān* 宏怤等編 and in the j. 1 colophon as *cān-xué mén-rén* 參學門人.
