Pǔ-dìng 普定, *shū-jì* 書記 dharma-disciple of **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685) in the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派. Lead compiler of the 16-juan 《三山來禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0454]]), signing the juan 1–2 (and *passim*) colophons as *shū-jì Pǔ-dìng biān* 書記普定編. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
