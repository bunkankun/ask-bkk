# Jù-dé 具德

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Known only from this editorial credit. Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Ming–Qing Línjì master Jù-dé Hóng-lǐ 具德弘禮 (1600–1667).
