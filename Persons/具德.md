# Jùdé 具德

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Known only from this editorial credit. Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Ming–Qing Línjì master Jùdé Hónglǐ 具德弘禮 (1600–1667).
