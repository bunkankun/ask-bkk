# Zhāng Jiāfèng 張嘉鳳

Contemporary Taiwanese historian of Chinese medicine; author of *Aspects of Smallpox and Its Significance in Chinese History* (PhD diss., SOAS, 1996) and a series of articles on the history of variolation, *dòuzhěn* 痘疹 doctrine, and the social history of smallpox in late-imperial China. National Taiwan University.
