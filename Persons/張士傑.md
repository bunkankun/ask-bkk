# Zhāng Shìjié 張士傑

Republican-era friend and preface-writer for [[塗蔚生]] Tú Wèishēng; author of the friendship-preface to [[KR3ee053]] *Tuīná juéwēi* dated *Mínguó* 17 (1928), sixth month. No further biographical detail survives in the work's paratext.
