Péng Dàyì 彭大翼 (fl. *Wànlì* 萬曆, *floruit* 1595 per CBDB), *zì* Yúnjǔ 雲舉, alternate name Yīhè 一鶴, native of Yángzhōu 揚州. He spent twenty-odd years as the top-ranked *zhūshēng* (district student) without ever passing the *jǔrén* examination; later held an unspecified provincial appointment in the *LiǎngGuǎng* region before retiring to the coast. His sole substantial work, the *Shāntáng sìkǎo* 山堂肆考 ([[KR3k0052]]) — a 228-*juǎn* late-Míng general *lèishū* — occupied him for more than thirty years and was completed in 1595; it was subsequently augmented with a 12-*juǎn* *Bǔyí* by his grandson-in-law [[張幼學]] in 1619.
