Péng Dà-yì 彭大翼 (fl. *Wàn-lì* 萬曆, *floruit* 1595 per CBDB), *zì* Yún-jǔ 雲舉, alternate name Yī-hè 一鶴, native of Yáng-zhōu 揚州. He spent twenty-odd years as the top-ranked *zhū-shēng* (district student) without ever passing the *jǔ-rén* examination; later held an unspecified provincial appointment in the *Liǎng-Guǎng* region before retiring to the coast. His sole substantial work, the *Shān-táng sì-kǎo* 山堂肆考 ([[KR3k0052]]) — a 228-*juǎn* late-Míng general *lèishū* — occupied him for more than thirty years and was completed in 1595; it was subsequently augmented with a 12-*juǎn* *Bǔ-yí* by his grandson-in-law [[張幼學]] in 1619.
