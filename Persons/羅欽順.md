Luó Qīnshùn 羅欽順 (1465–1547), *zì* Yǔnshēng 允升, *hào* Zhěngān 整菴, posthumously *Wénzhuāng* 文莊, was the principal Mid-Míng *Zhūzǐxué* loyalist defender against Wáng Yángmíng's *xīnxué*. Native of Tàihé 泰和 (Jíān, Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* — top three (*tànhuā* 探花) — of *Hóngzhì guǐchǒu* (1493); rose to *Nánjīng Lìbù shàngshū* with posthumous *Tàizǐ tàibǎo*. His main work is the *Kùnzhī jì* 困知記 ([[KR3a0084]]), a *Lǐxué*-doctrinal *yǔlù* with two main records (*qián jì* of 156 *zhāng* completed *Jiājìng wùzǐ* / 1528, *xù jì* of 113 *zhāng* completed *Jiājìng xīnmǎo* / 1531) and a *fùlù* of six discussion-letters. Within Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* he is the principal Zhū Xī defender against Wáng Yángmíng — the *fùlù* preserves his exchange with Wáng on the metaphysical-doctrinal questions of *xīn* / *xìng* / *lǐ* / *qì*. His own positions on the *qì* monism — that *lǐ* is not separable from *qì* — make him an interesting transitional figure between Zhū Xī orthodoxy and the late-Míng / early-Qīng *qì*-philosophy of Wáng Fūzhī and others. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 33645, dates 1465–1547. (Note: the Kanripo catalog meta gives the author's name as 羅欽訓 — typographical slip 訓 for 順. The work title in catalog also has 因 for 困. The correct forms 羅欽順 / 困知記 are followed here.)
