Luó Qīnshùn 羅欽順 (1465–1547), *zì* Yǔnshēng 允升, *hào* Zhěngān 整菴, posthumously *Wénzhuāng* 文莊, was the principal Mid-Míng *Zhū-zǐ-xué* loyalist defender against Wáng Yáng-míng's *xīn-xué*. Native of Tài-hé 泰和 (Jí-ān, Jiāng-xī). *Jìnshì* — top three (*tàn-huā* 探花) — of *Hóng-zhì guǐ-chǒu* (1493); rose to *Nán-jīng Lì-bù shàng-shū* with posthumous *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo*. His main work is the *Kùn-zhī jì* 困知記 (KR3a0084), a *Lǐxué*-doctrinal *yǔ-lù* with two main records (*qián jì* of 156 *zhāng* completed *Jiā-jìng wù-zǐ* / 1528, *xù jì* of 113 *zhāng* completed *Jiā-jìng xīn-mǎo* / 1531) and a *fù-lù* of six discussion-letters. Within Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* he is the principal Zhū-Xī defender against Wáng Yáng-míng — the *fù-lù* preserves his exchange with Wáng on the metaphysical-doctrinal questions of *xīn* / *xìng* / *lǐ* / *qì*. His own positions on the *qì* monism — that *lǐ* is not separable from *qì* — make him an interesting transitional figure between Zhū-Xī orthodoxy and the late-Míng / early-Qīng *qì*-philosophy of Wáng Fū-zhī and others. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rú-lín zhuàn). CBDB id 33645, dates 1465–1547. (Note: the Kanripo catalog meta gives the author's name as 羅欽訓 — typographical slip 訓 for 順. The work title in catalog also has 因 for 困. The correct forms 羅欽順 / 困知記 are followed here.)
