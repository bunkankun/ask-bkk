# Yù Chū 喻樗

*Zì* Zǐcái 子才. Of Yánlíng 嚴陵 (modern Tónglú 桐廬 in Zhèjiāng). 1097–1167. A substantial early-Southern-Sòng *Dàoxué* figure with his own surviving corpus of Confucian-classics commentaries. Together with [[聞人茂德]] Wénrén Màodé, one of the principal teachers of [[程迥]] Chéng Jiǒng — placing Chéng Jiǒng in a particular early-Southern-Sòng *Dàoxué* branch with distinctive interest in the divinatory-cosmological dimension of the *Yì* tradition (see [[KR1a0030]]).
