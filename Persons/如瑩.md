Late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk, disciple of **Mìyún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] and lead compiler of Yuán-wù's 12-juan *Mìyún chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0390]], *Jiāxīng zàng* A158). Distinct from the Yuán-era scholar-monk Rú-yíng of Pǔ-níng-sì who compiled the Bái-yún-zōng Pǔ-níng-sì Tripiṭaka catalogue (1299), and from the Yuán-Míng monk Rú-yíng of Yán-qìng-sì (disciple of Yǔn-ruò). No further biographical data on the Yuán-wù disciple is located.

Yuán-dynasty Bái-yún-zōng 白雲宗 monk of Pǔ-níng-sì 普寧寺 at Yú-háng 餘杭 (Hángzhōu). In Dà-dé 3 (1299) he re-organised the canon held at Pǔ-níng-sì and compiled the *Hángzhōu-lù Yú-háng-xiàn Bái-yún-zōng Nán-shān Dà Pǔ-níng-sì dà-zàng-jīng mù-lù* 杭州路餘杭縣白雲宗南山大普寧寺大藏經目錄 (*Fó-jiào dà-zàng-jīng* T10 no. 2067), the principal catalogue of the Pǔ-níng edition of the Tripiṭaka.

Southern-Sòng Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk of Chéngtiānsì 承天寺; *hào* Gūchán 孤蟾. Disciple of **Rújìng** 如淨 (1162–1228) of Tiāntóngsì (the master whose seat Dōgen later occupied through his dharma-heir Téngzhè). Attested in the *Xù chuándēng lù* 續傳燈錄 juan 17.
