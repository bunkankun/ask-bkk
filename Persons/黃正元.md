# 黃正元

Huáng Zhèngyuán (styled Tàiyī 泰一)

Mid-Qīng Fújiàn military commander (*Mǐn zǒngróng* 閩總戎) who, as a disciple of the *fújī* community attributing texts to [[孚佑帝君]] Fúyòu dìjūn, sponsored the planchette-mediated *Vajracchedikā* commentary [[KR6c0091]] preserved at X25 no. 503.
