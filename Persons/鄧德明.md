Dèng Démíng 鄧德明 (*fl.* Liu Song dynasty, Yuánjia period, c. 424–453 CE) was a geographic writer, author of a *Nánkāng jì* 南康記 ([[KR2k0173]]), a record of the Nankang region (modern southern Jiangxi). No further biographical details are attested. Note: The CBDB records a homonymous Dèng Démíng 鄧德明 (CBDB 111810, 1265–1358), a Yuan-dynasty figure, who is a different person entirely.
