**Hóng Xīngzǔ** 洪興祖 (1090–1155), *zì* Qìng-shàn 慶善, *hào* Liàn-táng 練塘, of Dānyáng 丹陽 in Zhèn-jiāng-fǔ 鎮江府 (modern Dānyáng, Jiāngsū). Southern-Sòng *Yì*-, *Chǔ-cí*-, and *Hán-shī*-scholar.

Passed the *jìn-shì* 進士 examination of 1118 (Zhèng-hé 8 / Chóng-níng special exam, per the CBDB *fl.* notation 1118). Held a series of provincial appointments under Gāo-zōng, including secretarial posts at Sūzhōu and Hú-zhōu. Politically aligned with the anti-Qín-Huì 秦檜 faction; impeached for praising Zhāng Jiǔ-chéng 張九成 and dismissed to a punitive transfer to Zhāo-zhōu 昭州 (modern Píng-lè 平樂, Guǎngxī), where he died.

His *Chǔ cí bǔ zhù* 楚辭補注 ([[KR4a0003]]) is the standard supplement to Wáng Yì's *zhāng jù*, fusing Wáng's commentary with new philological work, citation of intervening scholarship (especially Hàn–Táng glossaries and the lost *Chǔ cí* commentary of Xíng Bǐng 邢昺), and a substantial corrective program at the lexical and prosodic level. Together with Wáng Yì's [[KR4a0002]] and Zhū Xī's [[KR4a0004]] it forms the third pillar of the orthodox *Chǔ cí* commentary tradition.

CBDB id 14172. Birth–death dates 1090–1155 are the standard *Sòng shǐ* derivation followed in *Sì-kù tí-yào* and modern reference works.
