Chén Bāngyàn 陳邦彥 (1678–1752), *zì* Shìnán 世南 (other readings: Lǐbó 匡九), *hào* Chūnhuī 春暉, also Páolú 匏廬, posthumous title Wénjiǎn 文簡. Native of Hǎiníng 海寧 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 42 (1703). Long Hànlín career through the late Kāngxī, Yōngzhèng, and early Qiánlóng courts, ultimately *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書. CBDB id 61337; *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 304. (CBDB also lists a Míng person of the same name [c_personid 206051, b. 1537] — distinct.)

He was one of the principal calligraphers of the late-Kāngxī court, especially celebrated for his small-running-script (*xiǎo xíngshū* 小行書) and for his *yùzhì shī wén* 御製詩文 transcriptions. He compiled the imperial *Lìdài tíhuà shī* 歷代題畫詩 (120 juan) and edited the *Yùxuǎn yǔlù* 御選語錄 alongside the imperial princes. Listed in [[KR2j0002]] *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* as a *jiào kān guān* 校勘官 on the strength of his then position as *rìjiǎngguān qǐjūzhù Hànlínyuàn shìjiǎng xuéshì*.
