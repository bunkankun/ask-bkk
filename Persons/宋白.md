Sòng Bái 宋白 (936–1012; CBDB id 15396), *zì* Tài-sù 太素, native of Dà-míng 大名. Northern-Sòng *jìnshì* (957) and *Hàn-lín xuéshì*. At the time of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* commission he held *Zuǒ shí-yí* 左拾遺 and was one of the fourteen named compilers. He was also co-chief compiler (with Lǐ Fǎng) of the *Wén-yuàn yīng-huá* 文苑英華 (KR4i0006), the great literary anthology of the early Sòng. His later career included successive senior offices including *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* 禮部尚書. His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 439.
