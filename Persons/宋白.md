Sòng Bái 宋白 (936–1012; CBDB id 15396), *zì* Tàisù 太素, native of Dàmíng 大名. Northern-Sòng *jìnshì* (957) and *Hànlín xuéshì*. At the time of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* commission he held *Zuǒ shíyí* 左拾遺 and was one of the fourteen named compilers. He was also co-chief compiler (with Lǐ Fǎng) of the *Wényuàn yīnghuá* 文苑英華 ([[KR4i0006]]), the great literary anthology of the early Sòng. His later career included successive senior offices including *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書. His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 439.
