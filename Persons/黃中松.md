Huáng Zhōngsōng 黃中松, *zì* Zhòngyán 仲嚴, was an early-mid-Qīng *Shī* scholar from Shànghǎi 上海 (then under Sōngjiāng 松江 prefecture, modern Shànghǎi). Lifedates not preserved in the standard sources; CBDB carries no entry for him under this name. Active in the Kāngxī-Qiánlóng transition; the Sìkù-recension of his *Shī yí biàn zhèng* 詩疑辨證 ([[KR1c0061]]) was respectfully collated in *Qiánlóng* 43 (1778), so completion was before that date.

His *Shī yí biàn zhèng* is his sole transmitted work — a 6-*juǎn* *Shī*-natural-history-and-textual-criticism monograph in the *biàn zhèng* (distinguishing-and-corroborating) genre. The Kanripo catalog meta carries the title as *詩欵辨證* (a typographical slip — 欵 for 疑 — common in Qīng-period catalogs); the source-edition (WYG / Sìkù) consistently has 詩疑辨證, which is the canonical title.

Otherwise nothing about Huáng's life is securely documented. The Sìkù *tíyào* identifies him as a Shànghǎi scholar focused on *Shī* identification-and-collation — characteristic of the lesser-known *Shī*-class authors collected in the late-Qiánlóng *Sìkù* expansion.
