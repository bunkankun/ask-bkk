Xióng Cìlǚ 熊賜履 (1635–1709), *zì* Qīngyuè 青嶽, *hào* Sùjiǔ 素九, posthumously *Wénduān* 文端, was a leading early-Qīng Kāngxī-court *Lǐxué* official and Hànlín scholar. Native of Xiàogǎn 孝感 (Húběi). *Jìnshì* of *Shùnzhì shíèr* (1655); rose to *Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì*. With Lǐ Guāngdì he co-supervised the *Yù zuǎn Zhūzǐ quán shū* 御纂朱子全書 ([[KR3a0108]]) under Kāngxī's direction. Author of *Xué tǒng* 學統 (a comprehensive *Lǐxué*-historical treatise) and *Jīng yì zhāi xué shù* 經義齋學述. CBDB id 57338.
