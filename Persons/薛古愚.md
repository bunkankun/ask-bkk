**Xuē Gǔyú** 薛古愚 ("Old-and-Foolish" Xuē), mid-Míng physician of the Xuē 薛 family medical lineage of Kūnshān 崑山 (Jiāngsū). Author of the *Jìyīn wànjīn shū* 濟陰萬金書 (in three *juǎn*, on women's medicine), preserved through family transmission and edited into its received form in 1569 (*Lóngqìng* 3) by his marriage-heir Zhèng Fūzhèng 鄭敷政 of *Píngqiáo Kūnshān*. The received text is [[KR3ei033]] *Xuēshì jìyīn wànjīn shū*. Whether Xuē Gǔyú is identical with or related to the famous Xuē Jǐ 薛己 (1487–1559, [[薛己]]) — also of the Sūzhōu / Kūnshān prefecture — is not established in the prefatorial evidence.
