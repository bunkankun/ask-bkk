**Luó Měi** 羅美 (*fl.* 1675, 清), *zì* Dàn-shēng 澹生, *hào* Dōng-yì 東逸, was an early-Kāngxī physician of Xīnzhōu 新洲 (Ānhuī, or some sources Húběi). His one transmitted work is the *Nèijīng bó yì* 內經博議 ([[KR3ea046]]) in four juan, dated *Kāngxī* 14 = 1675 — a series of about thirty discursive essays on selected doctrinal topics of the *Sùwèn* and *Língshū*. Admitted to the *Sìkù quánshū*. The *Sìkù* tíyào records no further biographical data; his clinical orientation appears broadly Liú-Hé-jiān (cooling) tempered by Sōngjiāng *Mìng-mén* warming.
