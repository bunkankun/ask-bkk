# Shěn Línshì 沈驎士

*Zì* Yúnzhēn 雲禎. Southern Qí 南齊 / Liáng 梁 *recluse* scholar (*chùshì* 處士), native of Wúxīng Wǔkāng 吳興武康 (modern Zhèjiāng); declined imperial summons throughout his life. Wrote on the *Yì*, *Lǐ*, *Chūnqiū*, and *Lúnyǔ*; the *Yì* commentary is lost but cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Nán Qí shū* 南齊書 (juan 54, *Gāo yì zhuàn*) gives his biography.
