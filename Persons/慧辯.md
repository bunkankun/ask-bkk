# Huìbiàn 慧辯

Northern-Sòng Yúnménzōng 雲門宗 Chán master, imperial title Wújì dàshī 無際大師. Principal dharma-heir of [[宗本]] Huìlín Yuánzhào Zōngběn (1020–1100); abbot of Sūzhōu Língyánshān Xiùfēngsì 靈巖山秀峰寺. Also written 惠辯 in variant sources. Recorded (*lù* 錄) the supplementary *Huìlín Zōngběn chánshī biélù* 慧林宗本禪師別錄 ([[KR6q0380]]; X73 n1450), gathering his master's previously-unpublished *shàngtáng* and *jìsòng* material including the imperial 1085 Fúníngdiàn session. Lifedates unrecorded.
