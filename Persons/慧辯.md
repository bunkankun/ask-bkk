# Huì-biàn 慧辯

Northern-Sòng Yún-mén-zōng 雲門宗 Chán master, imperial title Wú-jì dà-shī 無際大師. Principal dharma-heir of [[宗本]] Huì-lín Yuán-zhào Zōng-běn (1020–1100); abbot of Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-shān Xiù-fēng-sì 靈巖山秀峰寺. Also written 惠辯 in variant sources. Recorded (*lù* 錄) the supplementary *Huì-lín Zōng-běn chánshī bié-lù* 慧林宗本禪師別錄 ([[KR6q0380]]; X73 n1450), gathering his master's previously-unpublished *shàng-táng* and *jì-sòng* material including the imperial 1085 Fú-níng-diàn session. Lifedates unrecorded.
