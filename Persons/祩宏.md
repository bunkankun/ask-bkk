# 祩宏

Yúnqī Zhūhóng 雲棲祩宏 (1535–1615), late-Míng Pure-Land master also known as Liánchí dàshī 蓮池大師. One of the *Míngmò sìdà gāosēng* 明末四大高僧 ("four great monks of the late Míng") together with [[達觀]] Zǐbǎi Dáguān, [[德清]] Hānshān Déqīng, and [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù. Founder of the Yúnqīsì 雲棲寺 monastic community at Hángzhōu.
