# Lù Zhì 陸贄 (754–805)

*Zì* Jìngyú 敬輿; posthumous *shì* Xuān 宣 (whence Xuāngōng 宣公). Native of *Wújùn* 吳郡 *Sūzhōu* 蘇州 *Lìyáng* 溧陽 (modern *Lìyáng* in Jiāngsū). One of the great political-literary figures of the Dézōng court; conventionally regarded as the most accomplished Tang prose stylist of the official-document genre.

*Jìnshì* of *Dàlì* 8 (773) at age 18 (an unusually young examination success); *bóxué hóngcí* 博學宏辭 in the same year. After early service as *Zhèngxiàn wèi* 鄭縣尉 and *Wèinán bù* 渭南簿, he was identified by Dézōng (then Crown Prince) as a major talent and promoted to *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士. During the *Zhū Cī* 朱泚 rebellion of 783, when Dézōng was in exile at *Fèngtiān* 奉天, Lù was the principal court drafter — Quán Déyú [[權德輿]]'s preface to the collection reports that his memoranda were composed extemporaneously without redrafting. After the recovery of the capital he served as *zǎixiàng* (792–794) at the height of his influence; subsequently demoted to *Zhōngzhōu biéjià* 忠州別駕 (in modern *Chóngqìng*) over a factional dispute with Péi Yánlíng 裴延齡, where he died in 805 after eleven years of internal exile.

His extant collection is the *Hànyuàn jí* [[KR4c0039]] in 22 *juǎn* — almost entirely official-document prose, the foundational model of Sòng-period *zhìgào* drafting and of the Sòng *kējǔ* curriculum's *zhìgào* training. Sū Shì 蘇軾 considered it "the model of *zhìgào* prose for all time."
