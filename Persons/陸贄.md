# Lù Zhì 陸贄 (754–805)

*Zì* Jìng-yú 敬輿; posthumous *shì* Xuān 宣 (whence Xuān-gōng 宣公). Native of *Wú-jùn* 吳郡 *Sū-zhōu* 蘇州 *Lì-yáng* 溧陽 (modern *Lì-yáng* in Jiāng-sū). One of the great political-literary figures of the Dé-zōng court; conventionally regarded as the most accomplished Tang prose stylist of the official-document genre.

*Jìn-shì* of *Dà-lì* 8 (773) at age 18 (an unusually young examination success); *bó-xué hóng-cí* 博學宏辭 in the same year. After early service as *Zhèng-xiàn wèi* 鄭縣尉 and *Wèi-nán bù* 渭南簿, he was identified by Dé-zōng (then Crown Prince) as a major talent and promoted to *Hàn-lín xué-shì* 翰林學士. During the *Zhū Cī* 朱泚 rebellion of 783, when Dé-zōng was in exile at *Fèng-tiān* 奉天, Lù was the principal court drafter — Quán Dé-yú [[權德輿]]'s preface to the collection reports that his memoranda were composed extemporaneously without redrafting. After the recovery of the capital he served as *zǎi-xiàng* (792–794) at the height of his influence; subsequently demoted to *Zhōng-zhōu bié-jià* 忠州別駕 (in modern *Chóng-qìng*) over a factional dispute with Péi Yán-líng 裴延齡, where he died in 805 after eleven years of internal exile.

His extant collection is the *Hàn-yuàn jí* [[KR4c0039]] in 22 *juǎn* — almost entirely official-document prose, the foundational model of Sòng-period *zhì-gào* drafting and of the Sòng *kē-jǔ* curriculum's *zhì-gào* training. Sū Shì 蘇軾 considered it "the model of *zhì-gào* prose for all time."
