Northern-Sòng scholar of Luòyáng 洛陽. He is recorded in [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī's *Zhū Zǐ yǔlèi* 朱子語類 as the source from whom Zhū Xī obtained an incomplete copy of [[司馬光]] Sīmǎ Guāng's *Wēngōng Yì shuō* 溫公易說 ([[KR1a0013]]) — running only as far as the *Suí* 隨 6-2 line and breaking off.
