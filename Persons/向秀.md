# Xiàng Xiù 向秀

*Zì* Zǐqī 子期. Wèi-Jìn 魏晉 philosopher, native of Huáinèi Huáixiàn 懷縣 (modern Hénán). One of the "Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove" (*Zhúlín qī xián* 竹林七賢), close associate of Jī Kāng 嵇康 and Ruǎn Jí 阮籍. Author of a *Zhuāngzǐ* commentary that was later (according to the *Shìshuō xīnyǔ* tradition) appropriated and extended by Guō Xiàng 郭象 — the basis for the long-running "plagiarism question" attached to Guō Xiàng's *Zhuāngzǐ* *zhù*. Also wrote a *Zhōuyì zhù* 周易註, lost as an independent work but cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Jìnshū* (juan 49) gives his biography.
