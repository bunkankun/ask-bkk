Early-Qīng 清 grand secretary (*dàxuéshì* 大學士) and senior imperial-commission director, native of Huá-tíng 華亭 in Sōngjiāng 松江 (modern Shànghǎi). Zì Zǐqí 子琪; hào Mào-hú 瑁湖; posthumously canonized Wéngōng 文恭. Lifedates 1642–1725 are firm (CBDB id 57204).

*Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 15 / 1676. Held a sequence of high *Hànlín* and grand-secretarial appointments under both Kāngxī and Yōngzhèng. By the late Kāngxī reign he was *Wǔyīng-diàn dàxuéshì* 武英殿大學士, and was repeatedly appointed *zǒng cái* 總裁 ("Director-General") of major imperial-commission compilation projects, including the *Quán Táng shī* 全唐詩 (under Cáo Yín 曹寅 as compiler), the *Pèi-wén yùn fǔ* 佩文韻府, and most notably the *Qīn-dìng Shū jīng zhuàn shuō huì zuǎn* 欽定書經傳說彙纂 ([[KR1b0046]]) — the late-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng-completed imperial standard *Shàngshū* commentary that superseded the Yǒnglè-era *Shū jīng dàquán* (KR1b0037) and remained the *yùdìng* imperial standard for the rest of the Qīng. The *Huì zuǎn* was begun in late Kāngxī and completed in Yōngzhèng 8 / 1730, after Wáng Xū-líng's death; he is named on the recension as the principal compiler. Biography: *Qīngshǐ gǎo* 清史稿 j. 268; collected works *Shìjīng-táng wénjí* 世經堂文集.
