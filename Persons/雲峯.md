Yuán-period Cí'ēn-school 慈恩 / Yogācāra scholar-monk, *zì* **Tōngjì** 通際 (alt. **通濟**), with the imperially-bestowed honorific **Zōngfǎ yuánmíng tōngjì dàshī** 宗法圓明通濟大師 ("Great Master of Sect-Doctrine, Perfect Illumination, and Comprehensive Salvation"). Lifedates unrecorded. Held the abbacy (*xuānshòu* 宣授, "imperially appointed") of **Huáiyìlù Yìtáisì** 懷益路義臺寺 in the Huáiyì circuit (modern eastern Húběi / north-western Anhuī region under Yuán administration). Author of the influential two-fascicle introductory Yogācāra catechism [[KR6n0142]] *Wéishí kāiméng wèndá* 唯識開蒙問答 (X55n0888) — one of the very few Yuán-period Yogācāra primers to survive, and a key textual link in the transmission of Cí'ēn doctrine across the Sòng / Yuán boundary into the late-Míng Fǎxiàng revival. Reissued in Chóngzhēn *gēngwǔ* 崇禎庚午 (1630) by the **Gǔ Yánshòu Yuàn** 古延壽院 monk Dàzhēn 大真, who supplied the *Chóngkān Wéishí kāiméng báyǔ* 重刻唯識開蒙跋語. Not to be confused with the Línjì-school **Yúnfēng Miàogāo** 雲峯妙高 (1219–1293, DILA A000472), a contemporary Chán master of a different line.
