Yú Shènxíng 于慎行 (1545–1607), *zì* Kěyuǎn 可遠 / Wúgòu 無垢, *hào* Gǔchéng shānrén 穀城山人, posthumous *shì* Wéndìng 文定, of Dōng'ē 東阿 (Shān-dōng). Took the *jìnshì* in Lóng-qìng 2 (1568); rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū* and *Wǔyīng-diàn dàxuéshì* (Cabinet Grand Secretary). He is the author of the *Dú-shǐ màn-lù* 讀史漫錄 (separately *Sìkù*-recorded) and the literary collection *Gǔ-chéng shān-guǎn shī-jí* [[KR4e0219]]. Though geographically a compatriot of **Lǐ Pānlóng** of *Lì-chéng* (Shān-dōng), he explicitly rejected the **Hòu Qī Zǐ** archaist program — his statements on *gǔ yuè-fǔ* and *wǔ-yán gǔ-shī* in the collection are among the most articulate late-Wàn-lì *anti-archaist* critical positions. He also rejected the *Jìng-líng* and *Gōng-ān* counter-movements, preferring the *diǎn-yǎ hé-píng* (canonical-elegant, harmonious-peaceful) Mid-Táng register. CBDB 126359.
