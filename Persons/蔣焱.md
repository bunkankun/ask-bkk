Jiǎng Yàn 蔣焱 (Daoist name Chōngyī 冲一), younger son of Jiǎng Shūyú [[蔣叔輿]] and joint signatory, with his elder brother Jiǎng Xī [[蔣焈]] (Daoist name Chōngsù 冲素), of the colophon to the *Wúshàng huánglù dàzhāi lìchéng yí* [[KR5b0211]] (DZ 508), dated 嘉定癸未 (= 1223). The brothers describe themselves as *bùxiào gū* 不孝孤 ("unfilial orphans"), indicating that their father had recently died; both bear Daoist names following the Zhèngyī lineage of their father and grandmaster (Liú Yòngguāng [[留用光]]). No independent records of Jiǎng Yàn have been located; not in CBDB.
