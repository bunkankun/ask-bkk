Liáng Qiūhè 梁丘賀, Western Hàn *Yì*-master, pupil of [[田王孫]] Tián Wángsūn, founder of the Liáng Qiū school of *Yì*-learning — one of the three principal Hàn *Yì*-lineages with [[施讐]] Shī Chóu and [[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ.
