# 王用汲

Wáng Yòngjí (*zì* Chángrú 長孺) of Huátíng 華亭, early-Qīng literatus; supplied the 1701 (*Kāngxī xīnsì* 康熙辛巳) preface to [[KR3em008]] *Yǎnkē chǎnwēi* 眼科闡微, recording the author's youth and transmission history.
