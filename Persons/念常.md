Niànchang 念常 (1282–after 1341), with sobriquet Méiwū 梅屋 ("Plum Cottage"), was a Línjì 臨濟 Chán monk of the late Yuán 元 active principally in Sūzhōu 蘇州 and the lower Yangzi region. The autobiographical preface to his *Fózǔ lìdài tōngzài* ([[KR6r0013]]) gives his native place as Huátíng 華亭 (modern Sōngjiāng 松江, near Shànghǎi). He took ordination in his youth and became a Dharma-heir in the Línjì line.

His sole surviving work is the *Fózǔ lìdài tōngzài* 佛祖歷代通載, the major Yuán-period Buddhist universal history. Compilation occupied him over the years roughly 1325–1341, and the work was presented to the throne in 1341 (Zhìzhèng 1). [[念常]]'s historiographical project was synoptic — he used Chán lamp records, Tiāntái histories, doctrinal-school compendia, and the standard Confucian dynastic histories as sources, and his work was sufficiently broad-based that the *Sìkù quánshū* editors later admitted it to the imperial collection.

His death-date is not recorded; he was alive in 1341 and presumably died not long thereafter.
