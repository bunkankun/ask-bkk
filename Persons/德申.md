Dé-shēn 德申, *zì* **Yàn-jū** 燕居 ("Quiet Dwelling"); often referred to as **Yàn-jū Dé-shēn** 燕居德申. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **1610** (estimated; some sources give age 72 = b. 1607); died **康熙十七年戊午八月十五日酉/戌時** = **1678-09-30**, age 69, *sēng-là* 50. Native of **Xī-Shǔ Zhōng-nán** 西蜀忠南 (Sì-chuān, Chóng-qìng region); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** At 19 took tonsure under **Dà-xiū Xiān** 大休仙法師. Studied *Wéi-shí*, *Lèng-jiā*, *Xiàng-zōng* under **Gāo-yuán Yù** 高原昱論師 (Yogācāra texts). Illness drove him to take up Chán; recovered and entered seclusion at **Zhōng-nán Diàn-yì Diào-yán-shān** 忠南墊邑吊巖山. Then went to **Liáng-shān-xiàn Tài-píng chán-sì** 梁山縣太平禪寺 to *cān* under **Pò-shān**. Returned to **Wàn-fēng** 萬峰. One day at **Bái-yún-ān** 白雲菴 helping with construction, watched a dirt-pile collapse — like the bottom falling out of a barrel; sudden insight. **崇禎癸未** = **1643** received final *fù-fǎ* from Pò-shān.

**Abbacies (seven).** **Liú-zhōng** 流中, **Wàn-xiàng** 萬項, **Dà-xīng** 大興, **Tài-píng** 太平, **Guān-yīn** 觀音, **Shòu-jiè** 受戒, **Yún-tiān** 雲天 (the principal seat — **Guì-zhōu Wēi-qīng Máo-pō-shān Yún-tiān chán-sì** 貴州威青茅坡山雲天禪寺, where he died). Stupa at Yún-tiān-shān.

**Disciples.** Forty-three *fǎ-sì* listed in the *lián-fāng-jì* of his *yǔ-lù*, including **Xuě-lín Méi** 雪林梅, **Líng-bì Zhé** 靈璧喆, **Yǐn-yǐn Xuǎn** 隱隱選, **Ruò-yú Mǐn** 若愚敏 (cf. **Zhú-fēng Mǐn** [[幻敏]] / [[KR6q0592]] potentially), **Shù-zhōng Hé-shùn** 述中合舜, **Chì-bō Chuáng** 赤旛幢, **Tiě-méi Zhēn** 鐵梅珍 — plus eight lay disciples (**Zhāng Chén-shū** 張辰樞, **Chén Yù-míng** 陳昱明, etc.).

**Work.** [[KR6q0590]] 《雲山燕居申禪師語錄》 8 juan, J40 B478, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hé-zhé** [[合哲]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《雲山燕居申禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉, 〈聯芳偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 88.
