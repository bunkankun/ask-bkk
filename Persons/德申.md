Déshēn 德申, *zì* **Yànjū** 燕居 ("Quiet Dwelling"); often referred to as **Yànjū Déshēn** 燕居德申. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Born **1610** (estimated; some sources give age 72 = b. 1607); died **康熙十七年戊午八月十五日酉/戌時** = **1678-09-30**, age 69, *sēnglà* 50. Native of **Xī Shǔ Zhōngnán** 西蜀忠南 (Sìchuān, Chóngqìng region); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** At 19 took tonsure under **Dàxiū Xiān** 大休仙法師. Studied *Wéishí*, *Lèngjiā*, *Xiàngzōng* under **Gāoyuán Yù** 高原昱論師 (Yogācāra texts). Illness drove him to take up Chán; recovered and entered seclusion at **Zhōngnán Diànyì Diàoyánshān** 忠南墊邑吊巖山. Then went to **Liángshānxiàn Tàipíng chánsì** 梁山縣太平禪寺 to *cān* under **Pòshān**. Returned to **Wànfēng** 萬峰. One day at **Báiyúnān** 白雲菴 helping with construction, watched a dirt-pile collapse — like the bottom falling out of a barrel; sudden insight. **崇禎癸未** = **1643** received final *fùfǎ* from Pòshān.

**Abbacies (seven).** **Liúzhōng** 流中, **Wànxiàng** 萬項, **Dàxīng** 大興, **Tàipíng** 太平, **Guānyīn** 觀音, **Shòujiè** 受戒, **Yúntiān** 雲天 (the principal seat — **Guìzhōu Wēiqīng Máopōshān Yúntiān chánsì** 貴州威青茅坡山雲天禪寺, where he died). Stupa at Yúntiānshān.

**Disciples.** Forty-three *fǎsì* listed in the *liánfāngjì* of his *yǔlù*, including **Xuělín Méi** 雪林梅, **Língbì Zhé** 靈璧喆, **Yǐnyǐn Xuǎn** 隱隱選, **Ruòyú Mǐn** 若愚敏 (cf. **Zhúfēng Mǐn** [[幻敏]] / [[KR6q0592]] potentially), **Shùzhōng Héshùn** 述中合舜, **Chìbō Chuáng** 赤旛幢, **Tiěméi Zhēn** 鐵梅珍 — plus eight lay disciples (**Zhāng Chénshū** 張辰樞, **Chén Yùmíng** 陳昱明, etc.).

**Work.** [[KR6q0590]] 《雲山燕居申禪師語錄》 8 juan, J40 B478, compiled by *fǎsì* **Hézhé** [[合哲]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《雲山燕居申禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉, 〈聯芳偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 88.
