Yīn Fù-chūn 陰復春 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* Zhōng-fū 中夫, late-Southern-Sòng *Bǎo-yòu jiǔ-jīng kē* passer of Fèng-xīn 奉新 in Jiāng-xī, elder brother of Yīn Jìng-xián 陰勁弦. His sole attributed work is the annotation of his brother's *Yùn-fǔ qún-yù* 韻府群玉 ([[KR3k0039]]). The Yīn family lived together as a single *jiā* for several generations in the late-Southern-Sòng eremitic loyalist pattern; neither brother served under the Yuán.
