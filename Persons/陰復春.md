Yīn Fùchūn 陰復春 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* Zhōngfū 中夫, late-Southern-Sòng *Bǎoyòu jiǔjīng kē* passer of Fèngxīn 奉新 in Jiāngxī, elder brother of Yīn Jìngxián 陰勁弦. His sole attributed work is the annotation of his brother's *Yùnfǔ qúnyù* 韻府群玉 ([[KR3k0039]]). The Yīn family lived together as a single *jiā* for several generations in the late-Southern-Sòng eremitic loyalist pattern; neither brother served under the Yuán.
