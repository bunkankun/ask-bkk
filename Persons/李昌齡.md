**Lǐ Chānglíng** 李昌齡 (Sòng) — compiler of [[KR5f0001]] ***Tài shàng gǎn yìng piān*** 太上感應篇 (preface attributed), the most famous Chinese popular morality tract. Multiple CBDB homonyms; the identification here is of the Sòng compiler of the *Gǎn yìng piān*, distinct from the earlier 937–1008 Sòng official. The *Gǎn yìng piān* preface attribution to Lǐ is traditional but insecure.
