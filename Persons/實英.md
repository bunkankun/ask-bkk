**Jitsuei** 實英 — Medieval Japanese **Kegon** 華嚴 (Avataṃsaka) monk, identifiable only as the author of *Huā-yán wǔ-jiào zhāng bù-shěn* 華嚴五教章不審 ([[KR6t0039]], T73n2343), a twenty-fascicle disputational sub-commentary on Fa-zang's *Wǔ jiào zhāng* focused on unresolved doubts (*bù-shěn*). The work's "*Not yet revised*" notation indicates it survives as a working manuscript rather than as a finalised redaction.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001609. No further biographical detail is preserved.
