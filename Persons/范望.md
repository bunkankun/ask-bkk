# Fàn Wàng 范望

Western Jìn 西晉 commentator on Yáng Xióng's [[KR3g0001]] *Tàixuán jīng*. Birth and death years not securely recorded; flourished in the late 3rd / early 4th century. The Sìkù 提要 of [[KR3g0001]] notes that Fàn Wàng's commentary postdates Sòng Zhōng 宋衷 (Eastern Hàn / early Wèi) and Lù Jì 陸績 (Three Kingdoms Wú, 187–219), whose earlier *Tàixuán* commentaries Fàn Wàng synthesized. The Tang *Yìwén zhì* attributes to Fàn Wàng the consolidating-and-restructuring of the *Tàixuán*'s component sections — particularly the integration of the *Xuáncè* 玄測 section that earlier recensions had positioned differently.

Fàn Wàng's commentary became the standard transmitted recension of the *Tàixuán jīng*; subsequent commentators (Wáng Yá 王涯's *Shuōxuán* 說玄, Sīmǎ Guāng 司馬光's *Tàixuán jízhù* 太玄集注, Yè Zǐqí 葉子奇's [[KR3g0002]] *Tàixuán běnzhǐ* 太玄本旨, and others) all build on Fàn Wàng's text. Through this commentarial tradition, Yáng Xióng's *Tàixuán* — modeled on the *Yìjīng* but using a 9-fold rather than 8-fold cosmological structure — entered the standard Chinese philosophical-cosmological canon.
