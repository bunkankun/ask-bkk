# Fàn Wàng 范望

Western Jìn 西晉 commentator on Yáng Xióng's [[KR3g0001]] *Tài-xuán jīng*. Birth and death years not securely recorded; flourished in the late 3rd / early 4th century. The Sìkù 提要 of [[KR3g0001]] notes that Fàn Wàng's commentary postdates Sòng Zhōng 宋衷 (Eastern Hàn / early Wèi) and Lù Jì 陸績 (Three Kingdoms Wú, 187–219), whose earlier *Tài-xuán* commentaries Fàn Wàng synthesized. The Tang *Yì-wén zhì* attributes to Fàn Wàng the consolidating-and-restructuring of the *Tài-xuán*'s component sections — particularly the integration of the *Xuán-cè* 玄測 section that earlier recensions had positioned differently.

Fàn Wàng's commentary became the standard transmitted recension of the *Tài-xuán jīng*; subsequent commentators (Wáng Yá 王涯's *Shuō-xuán* 說玄, Sī-mǎ Guāng 司馬光's *Tài-xuán jí-zhù* 太玄集注, Yè Zǐqí 葉子奇's [[KR3g0002]] *Tài-xuán běn-zhǐ* 太玄本旨, and others) all build on Fàn Wàng's text. Through this commentarial tradition, Yáng Xióng's *Tài-xuán* — modeled on the *Yìjīng* but using a 9-fold rather than 8-fold cosmological structure — entered the standard Chinese philosophical-cosmological canon.
