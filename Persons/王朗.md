Wáng Lǎng 王朗 (d. 228 CE; *zì* Jǐngxīng 景興) was a Cáo Wèi 曹魏 grand official, classicist, and literary author. Native of Dōnghǎi 東海 Tán 郯 (modern southern Jiangsu / northern Jiangsu). He served under Cáo Cāo and then under the early Wèi emperors, reaching the position of Sītú 司徒 (Grand Councillor). A leading classicist of the early Wèi, he wrote the *Zhōuyì chuán* 周易傳 and *Shūzhuàn* 書傳, and was the great-grandfather of Wáng Sù 王肅. His biography is in *Sānguózhì* 三國志 Wèishū juǎn 13.
