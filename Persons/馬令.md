Mǎ Lìng 馬令 (*fl.* 1105), of Yí-xīng 宜興 (modern Jiāngsū), late-Northern-Sòng historian. He was the grandson of [[馬元康]] Mǎ Yuán-kāng (*Tài-bó* 太傅), a man of Jīn-líng 金陵 (Nánjīng) for generations who had collected the *gù-shì* 故事 of the Southern Táng but had not lived to compile them. Mǎ Lìng undertook the project in fulfillment of the family *xiān-zhì* and produced the *Mǎ-shì Nán-Táng Shū* 馬氏南唐書 [[KR2i0017]] in 30 juàn, dated by the author's own preface to *Chóng-níng* 崇寧 4 / *Yǐ-yǒu* 乙酉 (= 1105). His official career — if any — is not preserved; he is known solely as a historian. CBDB id 449664 has no dates. Beyond the firm *floruit* of 1105, no biographical detail survives. (The Yuán-period preface to [[陸游]]'s recension by Zhào Shì-yán 趙世延 confused Mǎ Lìng's grandfather Mǎ Yuán-kāng with the actual author, an error long since corrected.)
