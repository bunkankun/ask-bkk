Luó Bì 羅璧 (1244–1309), *zì* Zǐcāng 子蒼, *hào* Mògēng 嘿耕 ("Silent Tiller"), of Xīn'ān 新安 (modern Huīzhōu, Anhui). A Sòng *yímín* loyalist who lived through the conquest of 1276 into the Yuán without holding office under the new dynasty. The *Sòng shǐ* carries no biography; his identification rests on internal evidence in the *Zhì yí* 識遺 ([[KR3j0056]]) and on later genealogical and gazetteer records. A private Chéng-Zhū-school philologist whose actual scholarship was overwhelmingly empirical-evidential rather than metaphysical. CBDB id 112837 (lifedates 1244–1309, flourished 1270–1299). Several homonyms (a 羅璧 1531– of Míng, several without dates) exist in CBDB and are distinct.
