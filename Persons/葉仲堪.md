Yè Zhōngkān 葉仲堪, a Sòng-period scholar identified by Marc Kalinowski (in Schipper & Verellen, *The Taoist Canon*, 2004) as belonging to the circle from which the *Yì* chart-corpus surviving as DZ 158 ([[KR5a0159]]) and circulating in the WYG *Sìkù* as *Dàyì xiàngshù gōushēn tú* ([[KR1a0080]]) actually derives — against the conventional attribution to [[張理]] Zhāng Lǐ. Lifedates not securely recorded.
