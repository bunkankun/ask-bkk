**Luó Guógāng** 羅國綱 (*zì* Zhènzhān 振占, 1739–after 1789, Shàngxiāng 上湘 / Xiāngxiāng 湘鄉, Húnán) — late-Qiánlóng scholar-physician with the *chìfēng* 敕封 (honorific imperial-grant) titulature of *Hànlín yuàn jiǎntǎo* 翰林院檢討 and *chéngdé láng* 承德郎 rank. Author of the twenty-*juǎn* *Luóshì huìyuē yī jìng* 羅氏會約醫鏡 ([[KR3er075]]), self-published *Qiánlóng* 54 / 1789. Not in CBDB.
