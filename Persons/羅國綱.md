**Luó Guó-gāng** 羅國綱 (*zì* Zhèn-zhān 振占, 1739–after 1789, Shàng-xiāng 上湘 / Xiāng-xiāng 湘鄉, Hú-nán) — late-Qiánlóng scholar-physician with the *chì-fēng* 敕封 (honorific imperial-grant) titulature of *Hàn-lín yuàn jiǎn-tǎo* 翰林院檢討 and *chéng-dé láng* 承德郎 rank. Author of the twenty-*juǎn* *Luó-shì huì-yuē yī jìng* 羅氏會約醫鏡 ([[KR3er075]]), self-published *Qián-lóng* 54 / 1789. Not in CBDB.
