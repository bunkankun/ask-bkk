**Wú Jìngsuǒ** 吳敬所 (style name Yǎngchún 養純) was a late-Míng compiler active in the Wànlì period (1572–1620). He is known primarily as the compiler of the vernacular fiction anthology *Guósè Tiānxiāng* 國色天香 [[KR4k0121]], preface-dated 1587 (Wànlì 15). Beyond this anthology, biographical details remain unknown; CBDB contains no entry for him. The preface to *Guósè Tiānxiāng* describes him as dissatisfied with the disorder of existing story collections and as a discerning literary anthologist. His native place and examination history are unrecorded.
