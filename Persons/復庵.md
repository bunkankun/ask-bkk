# Fùān 復庵

Sòng-dynasty monk of Western Sìchuān whose *jiǎngyì* 講義 lecture-notes on Guīfēng's *Lüèshū* underlie [[行霆]] Xíngtíng's *Yuánjué jīng lèijiě* ([[KR6i0563]]). Identity uncertain; sometimes tentatively identified with the Northern-Sòng Yúnmén-lineage Fùān Zōngyǎn 復庵宗演, but the identification is inconclusive. No independent works survive.
