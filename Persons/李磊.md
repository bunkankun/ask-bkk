# Lǐ Lěi 李磊

Contemporary Chinese-medicine scholar; author of "*Tuīná dàiyào fù* píngxī" 《推拿代藥賦》評析 (*Hong Kong Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine* 18.1, 2023: 94–98), a study of the *Tuīná dàiyào fù* of [[夏鼎]] Xià Dǐng's *Yòukē tiějìng* 幼科鐵鏡 ([[KR3ej011]]).
