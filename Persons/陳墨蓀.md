Chén Mòsūn 陳墨蓀 (fl. late 19th c.) reconstructed the received recension of [[何其偉]] Hé Qíwěi's *Yīxué miàodì* ([[KR3eh017]]) from surviving manuscript copies after the Tàipíng war, printed at the YúnGuì jiéshǔ 雲貴節署 in 1892.
