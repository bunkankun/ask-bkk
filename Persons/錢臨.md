Qián Lín 錢臨, conventional editorial attribution for the anonymous Qīng commentator on [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's medical cases ([[KR3ep082]] *Xuē-àn biàn-shū*). The attribution is not securely supported by the preserved text — the preface itself calls the author "anonymous" 無名氏. The commentator's defense of Xuē Jǐ against Chén Xiū-yuán's polemic places the composition in the second half of the nineteenth century, and the work remained as a secret manuscript until the late-nineteenth- or early-twentieth-century publication by **Xú Lián-táng** 徐蓮塘. No further transmitted biographical record. Not in CBDB.
