# 徐之才 Xú Zhīcái

Northern-Qí physician (505–572), author of the *Léigōng yàoduì* 雷公藥對 fragment tradition and other pharmacological materials. One of the principal medical authorities for the *Sòng-Yuán-Míng* medical canon and cited as a foundational source in [[李彣]] Lǐ Wén's *Jīnguì yàoluè guǎngzhù* ([[KR3ef087]]).
