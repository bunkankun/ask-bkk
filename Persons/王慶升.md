**Wáng Qìngshēng** 王慶升, *hào* Yuán qīng zǐ 爰清子, was a Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5d0112]] *Yuán qīng zǐ Zhì mìng piān* 爰清子至命篇 (1249).
