**Xǔ Zhònglín** 許仲琳 (fl. ca. 1567–1619), *hào* Zhōngshān Yìsǒu 鍾山逸叟. A Nanjing author conventionally named as the compiler or author of [[KR4k0049]] *Fēngshén Yǎnyì* 封神演義. The evidence for his authorship rests primarily on prefaces to early editions; some modern scholars argue that the text was substantially composed or edited by Lú Xīxīng 陸西星 (1520–1606), a Yangzhou Daoist master and prolific author. No CBDB record has been confirmed. The attribution remains a matter of scholarly debate; the text is currently assigned to Xǔ Zhònglín following the standard modern convention.
