Zhī Zhōu 知周 (668–723) was a mid-Tang Yogācāra and Vinaya scholar at Chángān, a third-generation disciple in the **Cí'ēn** 慈恩 (Yogācāra) lineage of **Xuánzàng** 玄奘 ([[玄奘]]) and **Kuījī** 窺基. His extant works include comprehensive Yogācāra commentaries (the *Chéngwéishí lùn yǎnmì* 成唯識論演祕 etc.) and the **Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn shū** 梵網經菩薩戒本疏 ([[KR6k0092]]) — a two-fascicle Yogācāra commentary on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts. He is a major figure in the Cí'ēn Yogācāra scholastic tradition.
