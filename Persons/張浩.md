**Zhāng Hào** 張浩 (*fl.* late 16th c.). Of Héng-jùn 恆郡 (modern Zhèngdìng 正定 prefecture, central Héběi). Late-Ming physician with documented Daoist physiological-cultivation interests. Author of the *Rénshù biànlǎn* 仁術便覽 ([[KR3ed040]]), 4 *juǎn*, completed c. 1585. His Confucian-Daoist medical synthesis places him in the late-Ming Daoist-medical milieu that also produced *Yīxué rù-mén* (Lǐ Chān 李梃, 1575) and *Lèi-jīng* (Zhāng Jièbīn, 1624). Not in CBDB; no other biographical record outside his own work and the preface by his kinsman / colleague Zhōu Jiǔgāo 周九皋. Multiple Ming physicians named Zhāng Hào exist in popular medical anthologies; the specific Héng-jùn Zhāng Hào is distinguished by the *Rénshù biànlǎn* attribution and the 1585 date.
