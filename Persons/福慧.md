# Fúhuì 福慧 (Yězhú Fúhuì 野竹福慧 / Sōngshān Yězhú 嵩山野竹)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master and **principal Qīng-era Chán mission-founder in Yúnnán** (southwestern frontier). Dharma-heir of [[璧|Shānhuī Bì]] 山暉璧 (at [[KR6q0437]]) in the **Pòshān Hǎimíng** 破山海明 → [[圓䂐|Zìshuǐ Yuánliáo]] → **Shānhuī** → **Fúhuì** Sichuan-Guì-Yún sub-line of the Mìyún Yuánwù tradition. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Fúhuì** 福慧, *hào* **Yězhú** 野竹 ("Wild-Bamboo") / **Sōngshān Yězhú** 嵩山野竹 (from his **Yúnnánfǔ Sōngshān Chányuàn** 嵩山禪院 seat at Kūnmíng). Native of **Yúzhōu** 渝州 (modern Chóngqìng, Sichuan) per his own self-identification in the [[KR6q0437]] preface ("*Yúzhōu ménrén Fúhuì* 渝州門人福慧"). Active c. 1650s–1670s.

**Nine-abbacy Yúnnán mission-network (c. 1650–65).** Documented in [[KR6q0438]]: seats spanning the entire Yúnnán plateau from Kūnmíng west to **Ménghuà / Shùnníng** (Dàlǐ region) and south to **Chéngjiāng / Mílè**:

1. **Yúnnánfǔ Cíyún Chányuàn** 慈雲禪院 (Kūnmíng);
2. **Ménghuàfǔ Zhúlín Chányuàn** 竹林禪院 (Wèishān);
3. **Shùnníngfǔ Yúnzhōu Wǔfú Chányuàn** 五福禪院 (Féngqìng, SW Yúnnán);
4. **Shùnníngfǔ Shùzōngshān Shànfǎ Chányuàn** 善法禪院;
5. 再住五福禪院;
6. 再住慈雲禪院;
7. **Yúnnánfǔ Sōngshān Chányuàn** 嵩山禪院 (Kūnmíng, the seat-*hào*);
8. **Yúnnánfǔ Fùmínxiàn Wòlóngshān Fǎhuá Chányuàn** 法華禪院;
9. **Chéngjiāngfǔ Lùnánzhōu Mílè Chányuàn** 彌勒禪院;
10. 再住嵩山禪院 (final abbacy).

**Historical significance.** Together with [[璧|Shānhuī Bì]]'s Guìzhōu network (at [[KR6q0437]]), Fúhuì's Yúnnán mission-program constitutes **the Qīng-era Sichuan-Pò-shān / Yuánliáo sub-line's southwestern-frontier establishment**. The two dharma-brothers divided the frontier: Shānhuī → Guìzhōu, Fúhuì → Yúnnán. Both operated under the semi-autonomous **Wú Sānguì** 吳三桂 *Pínxī Wáng* 平西王 administration (1660–73). Characterized by his layman-preface-writer (see [[KR6q0216]]) as *bó xué néng wén, dòng xī chán lǐ* 博學能文洞晰禪理.

**Works in Kanripo:**
- [[KR6q0438]] 《嵩山野竹禪師錄》 — his principal 14-juan *yǔlù* (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B224, cut 1665) — the *qiánlù* 前錄.
- [[KR6q0466]] 《益州嵩山野竹禪師後錄》 — 8-juan supplement *hòulù* 後錄 (Jiāxīng Canon J33 B284), compiled by *fǎsì* **Hóngxī** [[洪希]] 洪希 with front-preface by **Gōng Dǐngzī** 龔鼎孳 (1615–1673) dated **康熙十一年壬子孟冬 = 1672/11**, covering Fúhuì's 1671 entry into the **Yúnnán Dàlǐ Bīnchuānzhōu Jīzúshān Shízhōngsì** 雞足山石鐘寺 abbacy. Brokered by Gōng Dǐngzī through his Tiāntóng acquaintance **Shānxiǎo Běnxī** 山曉本皙 (Tiāntóng abbot, Sì-chuān-native fellow of Fúhuì).
- [[KR6q0216]] 《天台三聖詩集和韻》 — a second-stratum re-harmonising verse contribution (*chóng hé* 重和) on the Hánshān-Shídé-Fēnggān poetic corpus, extending the Yuán-era first-stratum harmonisations of [[梵琦|Chǔshí Fànqí]].
- Preface to his master's [[KR6q0437]] 山暉禪師語錄 (signed "*Yúzhōu ménrén Fúhuì* 渝州門人福慧" at **Sōngshān zuìzǔtáng** 嵩山醉祖堂).

**Regional Yúnnán context.** Part of the broader Yúnnán Chán regional tradition documented in the Kanripo corpus through the compilations of [[周理|Chèyōng Zhōulǐ]] 徹庸周理 and his successors (see [[KR6q0195]], [[KR6q0196]], [[KR6q0197]], [[KR6q0198]]).
