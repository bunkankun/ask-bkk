# Fú-huì 福慧 (Yě-zhú Fú-huì 野竹福慧 / Sōng-shān Yě-zhú 嵩山野竹)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master and **principal Qīng-era Chán mission-founder in Yún-nán** (southwestern frontier). Dharma-heir of [[璧|Shān-huī Bì]] 山暉璧 (at [[KR6q0437]]) in the **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** 破山海明 → [[圓䂐|Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo]] → **Shān-huī** → **Fú-huì** Sichuan-Guì-Yún sub-line of the Mìyún Yuán-wù tradition. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Fú-huì** 福慧, *hào* **Yě-zhú** 野竹 ("Wild-Bamboo") / **Sōng-shān Yě-zhú** 嵩山野竹 (from his **Yún-nán-fǔ Sōng-shān Chán-yuàn** 嵩山禪院 seat at Kūn-míng). Native of **Yú-zhōu** 渝州 (modern Chóng-qìng, Sichuan) per his own self-identification in the [[KR6q0437]] preface ("*Yú-zhōu mén-rén Fú-huì* 渝州門人福慧"). Active c. 1650s–1670s.

**Nine-abbacy Yún-nán mission-network (c. 1650–65).** Documented in [[KR6q0438]]: seats spanning the entire Yún-nán plateau from Kūn-míng west to **Méng-huà / Shùn-níng** (Dà-lǐ region) and south to **Chéng-jiāng / Mí-lè**:

1. **Yún-nán-fǔ Cí-yún Chán-yuàn** 慈雲禪院 (Kūn-míng);
2. **Méng-huà-fǔ Zhú-lín Chán-yuàn** 竹林禪院 (Wèi-shān);
3. **Shùn-níng-fǔ Yún-zhōu Wǔ-fú Chán-yuàn** 五福禪院 (Féng-qìng, SW Yún-nán);
4. **Shùn-níng-fǔ Shù-zōng-shān Shàn-fǎ Chán-yuàn** 善法禪院;
5. 再住五福禪院;
6. 再住慈雲禪院;
7. **Yún-nán-fǔ Sōng-shān Chán-yuàn** 嵩山禪院 (Kūn-míng, the seat-*hào*);
8. **Yún-nán-fǔ Fù-mín-xiàn Wò-lóng-shān Fǎ-huá Chán-yuàn** 法華禪院;
9. **Chéng-jiāng-fǔ Lù-nán-zhōu Mí-lè Chán-yuàn** 彌勒禪院;
10. 再住嵩山禪院 (final abbacy).

**Historical significance.** Together with [[璧|Shān-huī Bì]]'s Guì-zhōu network (at [[KR6q0437]]), Fú-huì's Yún-nán mission-program constitutes **the Qīng-era Sichuan-Pò-shān / Yuán-liáo sub-line's southwestern-frontier establishment**. The two dharma-brothers divided the frontier: Shān-huī → Guì-zhōu, Fú-huì → Yún-nán. Both operated under the semi-autonomous **Wú Sān-guì** 吳三桂 *Pín-xī Wáng* 平西王 administration (1660–73). Characterized by his layman-preface-writer (see [[KR6q0216]]) as *bó xué néng wén, dòng xī chán lǐ* 博學能文洞晰禪理.

**Works in Kanripo:**
- [[KR6q0438]] 《嵩山野竹禪師錄》 — his principal 14-juan *yǔlù* (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B224, cut 1665) — the *qián-lù* 前錄.
- [[KR6q0466]] 《益州嵩山野竹禪師後錄》 — 8-juan supplement *hòu-lù* 後錄 (Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B284), compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hóng-xī** [[洪希]] 洪希 with front-preface by **Gōng Dǐng-zī** 龔鼎孳 (1615–1673) dated **康熙十一年壬子孟冬 = 1672/11**, covering Fú-huì's 1671 entry into the **Yún-nán Dà-lǐ Bīn-chuān-zhōu Jī-zú-shān Shí-zhōng-sì** 雞足山石鐘寺 abbacy. Brokered by Gōng Dǐng-zī through his Tiān-tóng acquaintance **Shān-xiǎo Běn-xī** 山曉本皙 (Tiān-tóng abbot, Sì-chuān-native fellow of Fú-huì).
- [[KR6q0216]] 《天台三聖詩集和韻》 — a second-stratum re-harmonising verse contribution (*chóng hé* 重和) on the Hánshān-Shídé-Fēng-gān poetic corpus, extending the Yuán-era first-stratum harmonisations of [[梵琦|Chǔ-shí Fànqí]].
- Preface to his master's [[KR6q0437]] 山暉禪師語錄 (signed "*Yú-zhōu mén-rén Fú-huì* 渝州門人福慧" at **Sōng-shān zuì-zǔ-táng** 嵩山醉祖堂).

**Regional Yún-nán context.** Part of the broader Yúnnán Chán regional tradition documented in the Kanripo corpus through the compilations of [[周理|Chè-yōng Zhōu-lǐ]] 徹庸周理 and his successors (see [[KR6q0195]], [[KR6q0196]], [[KR6q0197]], [[KR6q0198]]).
