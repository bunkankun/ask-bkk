# Hónghàn 弘瀚

Early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Sùrú 粟如, 別號 Sháoān 勺菴; 16 March 1630 – 10 April 1706 (lunar 崇禎 3.2.3 – 康熙 45.2.27), *shìshòu* 77. 6th abbot of Xìnzhōu Bóshān Néngrén chánsì 博山能仁禪寺 in the Bóshān Cáodòng tradition. Qīngyuán 33rd-generation *fǎsūn* 法孫 of [[元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái (1576–1630; Hónghàn was literally born in the year of his dharma-grandfather's death).

Principal compiler (*huìbiān* 彙編) of [[元來]]'s extensive *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0365]]; X72 n1435, *Wúyì Yuánlái chánshī guǎnglù*), jointly with [[弘裕]] Yuánzuò Hóngyù.

**Direct teacher**: **Xuějiàn Dàofèng** 雪澗道奉 (1592–1670). At 21 took tonsure at Bóshān, given the *Chuánzǐ cángshēn* 船子藏身 *gōngàn*. Later at **Yíngshān** 瀛山, hearing a ghost wail, broke through the **Pòzàoduò** *gōngàn*. Took full ordination at Língyǐn under **Lǐ chánshī** 禮禪師. Final *fùfǎ* under Xuějiàn in **戊申** = **1668**.

**Abbacies (three)**: **Xìnzhōu Bóshān Néngrénsì** 信州博山能仁寺 (twice as 6th-generation abbot); **Xìnzhōu Gāoquán** 信州高泉; **Xìnzhōu Yíngshān** 信州瀛山.

**Disciples**: **Língjiù Rén** 靈鷲仁, **Bīngyá Xiǎng** 冰崖響, **Mèngbǐ Guān** 夢筆觀, **Bóshān Péng** 博山鵬, **Shíān Zhǐ** 石菴祉, **Rúyú Ēn** 如愚恩, **Qǐfàn Zhì** 起範智, plus lay editor **Fù Péng** [[傅鵬]].

**Own *yǔlù***: [[KR6q0597]] 《博山粟如瀚禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B490, compiled by **Fù Péng** [[傅鵬]] *biān*. Principal source for his life: 《博山粟如瀚禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈塔銘〉.
