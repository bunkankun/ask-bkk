# Hóng-hàn 弘瀚

Early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Sù-rú 粟如, 別號 Sháo-ān 勺菴; 16 March 1630 – 10 April 1706 (lunar 崇禎 3.2.3 – 康熙 45.2.27), *shì-shòu* 77. 6th abbot of Xìn-zhōu Bó-shān Néng-rén chán-sì 博山能仁禪寺 in the Bó-shān Cáo-dòng tradition. Qīng-yuán 33rd-generation *fǎ-sūn* 法孫 of [[元來]] Wú-yì Yuán-lái (1576–1630; Hóng-hàn was literally born in the year of his dharma-grandfather's death).

Principal compiler (*huì-biān* 彙編) of [[元來]]'s extensive *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0365]]; X72 n1435, *Wú-yì Yuán-lái chánshī guǎng-lù*), jointly with [[弘裕]] Yuán-zuò Hóng-yù.

**Direct teacher**: **Xuě-jiàn Dào-fèng** 雪澗道奉 (1592–1670). At 21 took tonsure at Bó-shān, given the *Chuán-zǐ cáng-shēn* 船子藏身 *gōng-àn*. Later at **Yíng-shān** 瀛山, hearing a ghost wail, broke through the **Pò-zào-duò** *gōng-àn*. Took full ordination at Líng-yǐn under **Lǐ chán-shī** 禮禪師. Final *fù-fǎ* under Xuě-jiàn in **戊申** = **1668**.

**Abbacies (three)**: **Xìn-zhōu Bó-shān Néng-rén-sì** 信州博山能仁寺 (twice as 6th-generation abbot); **Xìn-zhōu Gāo-quán** 信州高泉; **Xìn-zhōu Yíng-shān** 信州瀛山.

**Disciples**: **Líng-jiù Rén** 靈鷲仁, **Bīng-yá Xiǎng** 冰崖響, **Mèng-bǐ Guān** 夢筆觀, **Bó-shān Péng** 博山鵬, **Shí-ān Zhǐ** 石菴祉, **Rú-yú Ēn** 如愚恩, **Qǐ-fàn Zhì** 起範智, plus lay editor **Fù Péng** [[傅鵬]].

**Own *yǔlù***: [[KR6q0597]] 《博山粟如瀚禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B490, compiled by **Fù Péng** [[傅鵬]] *biān*. Principal source for his life: 《博山粟如瀚禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈塔銘〉.
