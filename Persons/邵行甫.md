**Shào Xíng-fǔ** 邵行甫 (*fl.* early 17th c., late Wàn-lì 萬曆 / early Tiān-qǐ 天啟 era) — son of *Niàn-shān-wēng* 念山翁, a Suzhou-district *Fēng-xī* 葑溪 physician who turned to medicine after failing the *jìnshì* examination. Shào is otherwise unrecorded; he is known principally as the reviser and supplementer of [[皇甫中]]'s *Míng-yī zhǐ-zhǎng* ([[KR3er053]]), the manuscript of which he transmitted to [[許士柔]] Xǔ Shì-róu in Běijīng around 1621–1622 for publication. Not in CBDB.
