**Shào Xíngfǔ** 邵行甫 (*fl.* early 17th c., late Wànlì 萬曆 / early Tiānqǐ 天啟 era) — son of *Niànshānwēng* 念山翁, a Suzhou-district *Fēngxī* 葑溪 physician who turned to medicine after failing the *jìnshì* examination. Shào is otherwise unrecorded; he is known principally as the reviser and supplementer of [[皇甫中]]'s *Míngyī zhǐzhǎng* ([[KR3er053]]), the manuscript of which he transmitted to [[許士柔]] Xǔ Shìróu in Běijīng around 1621–1622 for publication. Not in CBDB.
