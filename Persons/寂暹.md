# Jìxiān 寂暹

Mid-Qīng Buddhist monk; honorific *Jù-chè shàng-zuò* 巨澈上座 ("Senior Monk Jù-chè"). Lifedates not preserved; floruit early-to-mid Kāngxī period (c. 1670–1700).

His attested work is the **Yú-qié yàn-kǒu zhù-jí zuǎn-yào yí-guǐ** 瑜伽燄口註集纂要儀軌 ([[KR6j0757]], X59 no. 1084) — a two-fascicle annotated and compiled ritual manual for the *yú-qié yàn-kǒu* (Yoga food-bestowal-to-flaming-mouths) liturgy. The preface to the work, dated *yǐ-mǎo* 乙卯 (most plausibly **Kāngxī 14 / 1675**), praises Jù-chè's compendium as recovering the original integrity of the ritual that **Lián-chí dà-shī** 蓮大師 (Yún-qī Zhū-hóng, 1535–1615) had earlier corrected against late-Míng performance corruptions. Per DILA A003253.

The *Zuǎn-yào* belongs to the cluster of Qing-period *yàn-kǒu* ritual manuals that responded to the late-Míng / early-Qīng polemical disputes between the Yún-qī Pure Land-aligned tradition (Zhū-hóng's [[KR6j0753]] / [[KR6j0754]]) and the Sān-fēng Línjì-Chán-aligned tradition ([[法藏]] Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng's [[KR6j0755]] / [[KR6j0756]]).
