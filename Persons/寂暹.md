# Jìxiān 寂暹

Mid-Qīng Buddhist monk; honorific *Jùchè shàngzuò* 巨澈上座 ("Senior Monk Jùchè"). Lifedates not preserved; floruit early-to-mid Kāngxī period (c. 1670–1700).

His attested work is the **Yúqié yànkǒu zhùjí zuǎnyào yíguǐ** 瑜伽燄口註集纂要儀軌 ([[KR6j0757]], X59 no. 1084) — a two-fascicle annotated and compiled ritual manual for the *yúqié yànkǒu* (Yoga food-bestowal-to-flaming-mouths) liturgy. The preface to the work, dated *yǐmǎo* 乙卯 (most plausibly **Kāngxī 14 / 1675**), praises Jùchè's compendium as recovering the original integrity of the ritual that **Liánchí dàshī** 蓮大師 (Yúnqī Zhūhóng, 1535–1615) had earlier corrected against late-Míng performance corruptions. Per DILA A003253.

The *Zuǎnyào* belongs to the cluster of Qing-period *yànkǒu* ritual manuals that responded to the late-Míng / early-Qīng polemical disputes between the Yúnqī Pure Land-aligned tradition (Zhūhóng's [[KR6j0753]] / [[KR6j0754]]) and the Sānfēng Línjì-Chán-aligned tradition ([[法藏]] Hànyuè Fǎzàng's [[KR6j0755]] / [[KR6j0756]]).
