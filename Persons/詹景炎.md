# 詹景炎 Zhān Jǐngyán

**Zhān Jǐngyán** 詹景炎 (*hào* Gǔ Lè 古扐), Qīng-dynasty Dàoguāng-era figure who at age 82 wrote the 1836 preface to the *Zhēnjiǔ shénshū* 針灸神書 ([[KR3ee004]]) attributed to [[瓊瑤真人]] Qióngyáo zhēnrén. Otherwise unidentified.
