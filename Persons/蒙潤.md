Yuán-dynasty Tiāntái 天台 master and editor; sobriquet **Yùgāng** 玉岡 (also referenced as **Gù Yùgāng** 顧玉岡, **Rùn Yùgāng** 潤玉岡). DILA Authority A000110. Born 1276; died Zhìzhèng 至正 2, 12th month, 26th day (= 30 January 1343), aged 68.

Major work: the **Sìjiào yí jízhù kē** 四教儀集註科 ([[KR6d0171]], X57n0978, 1 *juan*) — sectional analysis of the *Sìjiào yí jízhù* (the Yuán-period combined edition of Dìguān's *Sìjiào yí* with its principal commentaries).

Source: *Xù Fózǔ tǒngjì* 續佛祖統紀 *juan* 1; DILA A000110.
