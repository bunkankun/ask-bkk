Yuán-dynasty Tiāntái 天台 master and editor; sobriquet **Yù-gāng** 玉岡 (also referenced as **Gù Yù-gāng** 顧玉岡, **Rùn Yù-gāng** 潤玉岡). DILA Authority A000110. Born 1276; died Zhì-zhèng 至正 2, 12th month, 26th day (= 30 January 1343), aged 68.

Major work: the **Sì-jiào yí jí-zhù kē** 四教儀集註科 ([[KR6d0171]], X57n0978, 1 *juan*) — sectional analysis of the *Sì-jiào yí jí-zhù* (the Yuán-period combined edition of Dìguān's *Sì-jiào yí* with its principal commentaries).

Source: *Xù Fózǔ tǒngjì* 續佛祖統紀 *juan* 1; DILA A000110.
