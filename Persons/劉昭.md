# Liú Zhāo 劉昭

*Zì* Xuānqīng 宣卿. Native of Píngyuán 平原 (modern Shāndōng). Active in the early Liáng dynasty (early 6th century); held the post of *Yǎn xiàn lìng* 剡縣令 (magistrate of Yǎn County in Kuàijī 會稽).

His sole transmitted work is the *Hòu Hàn shū bǔzhù* 後漢書補注 in 180 *juǎn* (the figure given by the *Suí zhì*; the work in its complete form has not survived). Of this corpus, only the commentary on the eight *zhì* of Sīmǎ Biāo 司馬彪's *Xù Hàn shū* 續漢書 — 30 *juǎn* — was extant by Sòng times, and was then attached to Fàn Yè's *Hòu Hànshū* ([[KR2a0009]]) by Sūn Shì 孫奭's imperial commission of *Qiánxīng* 1 (1022) to fill the *zhì* lacuna in Fàn's text. Liú Zhāo's general preface (*zǒngxù* 總序) to the *zhìzhù* survives in many editions and is one of the principal early documents of Eastern-Hàn historical bibliography.

His biographical particulars are scant; the *Liáng shū* / *Nán shǐ* do not give him a separate biography. The Sìkù *tíyào* and modern catalog references treat him as a Liáng-era specialist in Eastern-Hàn historiography. CBDB has multiple homonyms; no confident match.
