# Zǐshēng 子昇

Southern-Sòng Chán monk. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Co-compiler (with [[如祐|Rúyòu]] 如祐) of the *Chán mén zhū zǔ shī jì sòng* 禪門諸祖師偈頌 [[KR6q0247]] — a two-juan Chán transmission-verse anthology.
