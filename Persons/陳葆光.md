**Chén Bǎoguāng** 陳葆光 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist, compiler of [[KR5g0057]] ***Sān dòng qún xiān lù*** 三洞群仙錄 — a comprehensive **20-*juàn* hagiographical encyclopedia** of Daoist immortals drawn from the Three Caverns.
