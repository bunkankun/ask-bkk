Zǔ-yuán 祖圓. Female disciple-attendant of **Zǐ-yōng Chéng-rú** [[成如]] (b. 1648). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*jì-lù*) of her master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0578]] 《子雍如禪師語錄》 4 juan, J39 B465.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the famous Japanese Rinzai master **Kian Soen** 規庵祖圓 (DILA A001144, *Nán-yuàn Guó-shī* 南院国師, ~1261–1313/14), of medieval Japan.
