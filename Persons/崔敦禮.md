# Cuī Dūnlǐ 崔敦禮 (Sòng, d. 1181)

A senior Southern-Sòng official whose tenure as *Tàizǐ gōng jiàoshòu* (Crown-Prince's Palace Tutor) gives the title to his collection [[KR4d0238]]. Younger brother of Cuī Dūnshī 崔敦詩 (*zì* Dàyǎ 大雅), who held the *Zhíyuàn* (Cabinet-Drafter) office and led the post-1181 emendation of Lǚ Zǔqiān's [[呂祖謙]] *Sòng wénjiàn* anthology. CBDB id 10210, death year 1181. Birth year and *zì* not preserved.

Author of the *Chúyán* 芻言 (a collection of administrative observations, separately listed in *Zǐbù*) and of the lost 20-*juǎn* original *Cuī Dūnlǐ jí* (per Jiāo Hóng's *Jīngjízhì*); the surviving *Gōngjiào jí* in 12 *juǎn* [[KR4d0238]] is the *Sìkù* reconstruction from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*.

The *Sìkù* editors detect a possibly-misattributed memorial in his collection at *juǎn* 5 — the *Jìn chóngshàndìng Lǚ Zǔqiān suǒ biān Wénjiàn zházǐ* — which by Lǐ Xīnchuán's *Cháoyě zájì* and Zhū Xī's *Yǔlèi* should belong to the elder brother Dūnshī, not to Dūnlǐ; alternatively, Dūnlǐ may have drafted the memorial on his elder brother's behalf.

Surviving in Kanripo:
- [[KR4d0238]] *Gōngjiào jí* (12 *juǎn*, WYG; reconstructed from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*).
- *Chúyán* (separately in *Zǐbù*).
