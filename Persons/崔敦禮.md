# Cuī Dūnlǐ 崔敦禮 (Sòng, d. 1181)

A senior Southern-Sòng official whose tenure as *Tài-zǐ gōng jiào-shòu* (Crown-Prince's Palace Tutor) gives the title to his collection [[KR4d0238]]. Younger brother of Cuī Dūn-shī 崔敦詩 (*zì* Dà-yǎ 大雅), who held the *Zhí-yuàn* (Cabinet-Drafter) office and led the post-1181 emendation of Lǚ Zǔ-qiān's [[呂祖謙]] *Sòng wén-jiàn* anthology. CBDB id 10210, death year 1181. Birth year and *zì* not preserved.

Author of the *Chú-yán* 芻言 (a collection of administrative observations, separately listed in *Zǐ-bù*) and of the lost 20-*juǎn* original *Cuī Dūn-lǐ jí* (per Jiāo Hóng's *Jīng-jí-zhì*); the surviving *Gōng-jiào jí* in 12 *juǎn* [[KR4d0238]] is the *Sì-kù* reconstruction from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*.

The *Sì-kù* editors detect a possibly-misattributed memorial in his collection at *juǎn* 5 — the *Jìn chóng-shàn-dìng Lǚ Zǔ-qiān suǒ biān Wén-jiàn zhá-zǐ* — which by Lǐ Xīn-chuán's *Cháo-yě zá-jì* and Zhū Xī's *Yǔ-lèi* should belong to the elder brother Dūn-shī, not to Dūn-lǐ; alternatively, Dūn-lǐ may have drafted the memorial on his elder brother's behalf.

Surviving in Kanripo:
- [[KR4d0238]] *Gōng-jiào jí* (12 *juǎn*, WYG; reconstructed from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*).
- *Chú-yán* (separately in *Zǐ-bù*).
