**Wú Fú** 吳芾 (1104–1183), *zì* Míngkě 明可, *hào* Húshān jūshì 湖山居士, of Táizhōu 台州 Xiānjū 仙居 (Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* 1135. Held office to *Lìbù shìláng* (Vice-Director of Personnel), *Cháofèng dàifū*, *Zhī Lóngtúgé*, and *Zhī Dāngtú jūnshì* (Prefect of Tàipíngzhōu, *Dāngtú*). In *Qiándào* 3 / 1167 (*dīnghài*), as *Zhī Dāngtú jūnshì*, compiled and printed *Gūxī jūshì qiánjí* 姑溪居士前集 [[KR4d0110]] of Lǐ Zhīyí 李之儀 [[李之儀]] in 50 *juǎn*, providing the principal channel of transmission. Author of his own *Húshān jí* 湖山集. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 387. CBDB id should be checked.
