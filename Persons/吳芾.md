**Wú Fú** 吳芾 (1104–1183), *zì* Míng-kě 明可, *hào* Hú-shān jūshì 湖山居士, of Tái-zhōu 台州 Xiān-jū 仙居 (Zhè-jiāng). *Jìnshì* 1135. Held office to *Lì-bù shì-láng* (Vice-Director of Personnel), *Cháo-fèng dài-fū*, *Zhī Lóng-tú-gé*, and *Zhī Dāng-tú jūn-shì* (Prefect of Tài-píng-zhōu, *Dāng-tú*). In *Qián-dào* 3 / 1167 (*dīng-hài*), as *Zhī Dāng-tú jūn-shì*, compiled and printed *Gū-xī jū-shì qián-jí* 姑溪居士前集 [[KR4d0110]] of Lǐ Zhī-yí 李之儀 [[李之儀]] in 50 *juǎn*, providing the principal channel of transmission. Author of his own *Hú-shān jí* 湖山集. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 387. CBDB id should be checked.
