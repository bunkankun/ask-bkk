**Jiǎng Yí** 蔣儀 (*zì* Yíyòng 儀用) of Jiànān 建安 (Fújiàn), late-Míng / Chóngzhēn-era pupil-collator of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng; edited the 1641 cut of [[KR3eq043]] *Yī jìng* under the patronage of [[柯元芳]] Kē Yuánfāng.
