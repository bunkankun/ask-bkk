# Xūrán 虗然

Early-Southern-Sòng Chán disciple of Yuánwù [[克勤]] Kèqín; named alongside Hǔqiū [[紹隆]] Shàolóng as one of the senior editorial hands on the *Yuánwù Fóguǒ chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0059]]).
