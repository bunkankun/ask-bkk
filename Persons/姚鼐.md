Yáo Nài 姚鼐 (1732–1815), *zì* Jī-chuán 姬傳, *hào* Xī-bào 惜抱 (whence the studio name *Xī-bào-xuān* 惜抱軒), native of Tóngchéng 桐城, Ānhuī. The third-generation master of the Tóngchéng 桐城 school of *gǔwén* prose, after [[方苞]] Fāng Bāo and [[劉大櫆]] Liú Dà-kuí (his immediate teacher). *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 28 (1763), Hànlín *biānxiū*. He served briefly (1773–1774) as a *zǒng-cuǎn* 總纂 / sub-editor on the *Sìkù quán shū* editorial board but withdrew in disagreement with the dominant evidential-research approach of [[戴震]] Dài Zhèn and the central editorial circle. From 1774 until his death he led the Yáng-zhōu Méi-huā 揚州梅花, Ān-qìng Jǐng-shān 安慶敬山, Shè-xiàn Zǐ-yáng 歙縣紫陽, and (from 1801) Nánjīng Zhōngshān 鍾山 academies in succession — over forty years of academic leadership. His principal theoretical contribution is the integration of *yì-lǐ* 義理 (Sòng moral metaphysics), *kǎo-jù* 考據 (Hàn-Qīng philology), and *cí-zhāng* 詞章 (literary craft) as a single learning, and the *yáng-gāng / yīn-róu* 陽剛陰柔 typology of literary style. Major works: *Xī-bào-xuān wénjí* [[KR4f0052]], *Xī-bào-xuān shījí*, *Gǔwén-cí lèi-zuǎn* 古文辭類纂 (the most widely used Qīng prose anthology), *Lǎo-zǐ zhāng-yì* 老子章義, *Jiǔ-jīng shuō* 九經說, *Zuǒ zhuàn bǔ zhù* 左傳補註. *ECCP* 900–901 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 35102.
