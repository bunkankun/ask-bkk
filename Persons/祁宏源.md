**Qí Hóngyuán** 祁宏源 (*fl.* early Qiánlóng, 清), grandson of the early-Qīng Imperial Physician [[祁坤]] (author of [[KR3ek011]] *Wàikē dà chéng*, 1665). Qí Hóngyuán served on the editorial commission of the imperially commissioned *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* 醫宗金鑑 (completed Qiánlóng 7 = 1742) under [[吳謙]], heading the surgical compilation team and effectively re-mediating his grandfather's *Wàikē dà chéng* as the surgical division of the imperial curriculum (cf. [[KR3ek009]]). No firm lifedates are documented. Not in CBDB.
