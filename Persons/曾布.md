Northern-Sòng statesman, *zì* Zǐxuān 子宣, younger brother of the historian Zēng Gǒng 曾鞏. *Jìnshì* of 1057. A major figure of the *Xīnfǎ* 新法 (New Policies) reformist faction, rising to *Shàngshū yòupúyè* 尚書右僕射 (Vice Director of the Department of State Affairs) under Huīzōng. Reproved in *Jiànzhōng jìngguó* 1 (1101) by [[陳瓘]] Chén Guàn in a memorial that begins the latter's lifelong campaign against [[蔡京]] Cài Jīng's consolidation of the reformist programme.
