# Shòuyuǎn 授遠 (Pǔwén Shòuyuǎn 普聞授遠)

Mid-seventeenth-century female Línjì Chán master (nun 尼), principal dharma-heir of [[行剛|Qíyuán Xínggāng]] 祇園行剛 (1597–1654) in the Mìyún-line's **Fúshī Chányuàn** 伏獅禪院 female-lineage. *Hào* **Pǔwén** 普聞. Listed first among Xínggāng's **seven named dharma-heirs** at the *xíngzhuàng* close.

Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0428]] 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B210), working with six dharma-sisters (all in the **超-generation character** except Shòuyuǎn herself, whose 授 character is an alternative generation-marker).

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under her name.
