# Shòu-yuǎn 授遠 (Pǔ-wén Shòu-yuǎn 普聞授遠)

Mid-seventeenth-century female Línjì Chán master (nun 尼), principal dharma-heir of [[行剛|Qí-yuán Xíng-gāng]] 祇園行剛 (1597–1654) in the Mìyún-line's **Fú-shī Chán-yuàn** 伏獅禪院 female-lineage. *Hào* **Pǔ-wén** 普聞. Listed first among Xíng-gāng's **seven named dharma-heirs** at the *xíng-zhuàng* close.

Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0428]] 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B210), working with six dharma-sisters (all in the **超-generation character** except Shòu-yuǎn herself, whose 授 character is an alternative generation-marker).

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under her name.
