The DILA Buddhist Person Authority records **Chāo-hǎi** 超海 (*zì* **Sì-háng** 四航; native of **Hǎi-yán** 海鹽 in Zhèjiāng, lay surname **Lù** 陸) as a Qīng monk born **3 October 1662** (Kāngxī 1, 8th month, 22nd day) and died **9 May 1728** (Yōngzhèng 6, 4th month, 1st day) at age 67, abbot of **Chóng-fú-sì** 崇福寺. He left a one-juan *yǔ-lù* (recorded sayings).

— — — — —

The **Chāo-hǎi 超海** named as principal editor of the **Chóng-dìng jiào-shèng fǎ-shù** ([[KR6s0009]], L1666) under the **Yōng-zhèng** 雍正 emperor's commission, with the project running through **Yōng-zhèng** 12–13 (1734–1735), is **most probably a different person** — the imperial preface (dated 7th month full-moon, Yōng-zhèng 13 = August 1735) describes the editing as still actively in progress in 1734, by which date the Hǎi-yán Chóng-fú-sì abbot above had already been dead seven years. The 1730s imperial-court editor is plausibly a Beijing-based monastic scholar of the same name otherwise unrecorded in DILA. The catalog meta and the Authority XML have apparently confused the two figures. The matter remains to be clarified by direct consultation of *Lóng-zàng* (Qiánlóng canon) editorial archives.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A022509 (Hǎi-yán Chóng-fú-sì); *Chóng-fú-sì zhì* 崇福寺志, j. 4. For the Yōng-zhèng-era namesake: *Lóng-zàng* L1666 imperial preface.
