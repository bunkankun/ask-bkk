The DILA Buddhist Person Authority records **Chāohǎi** 超海 (*zì* **Sìháng** 四航; native of **Hǎiyán** 海鹽 in Zhèjiāng, lay surname **Lù** 陸) as a Qīng monk born **3 October 1662** (Kāngxī 1, 8th month, 22nd day) and died **9 May 1728** (Yōngzhèng 6, 4th month, 1st day) at age 67, abbot of **Chóngfúsì** 崇福寺. He left a one-juan *yǔlù* (recorded sayings).

— — — — —

The **Chāohǎi 超海** named as principal editor of the **Chóngdìng jiàoshèng fǎshù** ([[KR6s0009]], L1666) under the **Yōngzhèng** 雍正 emperor's commission, with the project running through **Yōngzhèng** 12–13 (1734–1735), is **most probably a different person** — the imperial preface (dated 7th month full-moon, Yōngzhèng 13 = August 1735) describes the editing as still actively in progress in 1734, by which date the Hǎiyán Chóngfúsì abbot above had already been dead seven years. The 1730s imperial-court editor is plausibly a Beijing-based monastic scholar of the same name otherwise unrecorded in DILA. The catalog meta and the Authority XML have apparently confused the two figures. The matter remains to be clarified by direct consultation of *Lóngzàng* (Qiánlóng canon) editorial archives.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A022509 (Hǎiyán Chóngfúsì); *Chóngfúsì zhì* 崇福寺志, j. 4. For the Yōng-zhèng-era namesake: *Lóngzàng* L1666 imperial preface.
