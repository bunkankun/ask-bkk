# Hǎi-yán 海鹽

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[行舟|Jiè-wéi Xíng-zhōu]] 介為行舟 (1611–1670) in the Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi sub-line of the Mìyún Yuán-wù lineage. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0424]] 《介為舟禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B205). The *tǎ-míng* identifies him as the principal *sì-fǎ* 嗣法 ("*shǒu Chāo-xiū fǎ-sì Hǎi-yán děng jù zhuàng lái qǐ míng* 首超修法嗣海鹽等具狀來乞銘" — "head among Chāo-xiū's 超修 dharma-heirs, Hǎi-yán and others brought the *zhuàng* requesting the *tǎ-míng*") — suggesting Hǎi-yán is a grand-dharma-son of Xíng-zhōu via **Chāo-xiū** 超修 (the 超-generation heir who executed the pagoda-burial in 1671).

The 海-generation character aligns with the standard 33→34-generation Línjì naming scheme in this sub-line (行 → 海 / 本 parallel conventions at the 34th-generation level). Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under his name.

Sources: [[KR6q0424]] juan-head signature line "*mén-rén Hǎi-yán Yì-zhèng děng biān* 門人海鹽益證等編" + juan 10 *tǎ-míng* mention.
