Hán Tǎn 韓菼 (1637–1704), *zì* Yuánshào 元少, *hào* Mùlú 慕廬, posthumously *Wényì* 文懿, was an early-Qīng Hàn-lín scholar of Cháng-zhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu region). *Zhuàng-yuán* (top *jìnshì*) of *Kāngxī shí'èr* (1673); rose to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Compiler-in-chief of the *Yù dìng xiào jīng yǎn yì* 御定孝經衍義 (KR3a0105). Author of a substantial *yǐn lǐ kǎo* 飲禮考 and other Confucian classical works. CBDB id 34139.
