Fǎ Xiǎn 法銑 (fl. mid-Tang) was a mid-Tang scholar-monk known for his commentary work on the *Fànwǎng jīng*. His extant work is the **Fànwǎng-jīng púsà-jiè shū** 梵網經菩薩戒疏 ([[KR6k0093]]) — a one-fascicle commentary on the bodhisattva-precepts.
