Kyōshun 教舜 (fl. late Kamakura / early Nanbokuchō) was a Japanese Shingon monk of the **Daigo-ji Sanbō-in** 醍醐寺三寶院 transmission line. His sole surviving work [[KR6t0205]] *Chuán-fǎ guàn-dǐng sī-jì* records the *denbō kanjō* procedure as performed at Sanbō-in. He signs himself "*Vajra Buddha-disciple Kyōshun*" (金剛佛子教舜) in the work's header. His precise lifedates are not securely recoverable; the DILA Buddhist person authority records him under A001129.

Surviving work in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0205]] *Denbō kanjō shiki* (3 fasc.).
