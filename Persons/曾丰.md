Zēng Fēng 曾丰 (b. 1142, *zì* Yòudù 幼度), of *Lè'ān* 樂安 in *Fǔzhōu* 撫州 (modern Jiāngxī), *jìnshì* of *Qiándào* 5 (1169). Held office to *Déqìng tàishǒu* 德慶太守 (Guǎngdōng) and *cháosàn dàfū* 朝散大夫. Teacher in his youth of Zhēn Déxiù [[真德秀]]; built a studio in old age and self-named it *Zūnzhāi* 樽齋, hence the alternate title of his collection. CBDB id 15571. Sometimes mistakenly written 曾耒 (the catalog meta of [[KR4d0252]] follows this slip; the *Sìkù* tíyào and Yú Jí's *Yuántǒng*-era preface are unanimous on 曾丰). Author of [[KR4d0252]] *Yuándū jí*.
