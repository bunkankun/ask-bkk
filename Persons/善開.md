# Shàn-kāi 善開

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, styled Yǎn'ān 掩庵 (also 掩室) Shàn-kāi. Native of Chéngdū fǔ 成都府 per DILA A001343. Dharma-heir of [[崇岳]] Sōngyuán Chóngyuè (1132–1202), from whom he received a deathbed letter entrusting the teaching (together with [[光睦]] Xiāngshān Guāng-mù), as recorded in Sōngyuán's *tǎ-míng* by Lù Yóu. He is named as a co-recorder of Sōngyuán's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0311]]) under the 參學 byline. He subsequently occupied abbacies at Yúnjū 雲居 (hence the style 雲居善開) and ended at Jīnshān 金山 (Jīnshān Shàn-kāi 金山善開). Lifedates unrecorded.
