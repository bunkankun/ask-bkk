Méi Táo 梅陶 (*fl.* early to mid 4th century), *zì* Shūzhēn 叔真, was a minor Eastern Jìn 東晉 official from Rǔ-nán 汝南. He served at the Jìn court in middle-ranking metropolitan posts; the precise titles are not securely recorded. He is most often encountered in modern reading through the *Shìshuō xīnyǔ* 世說新語 anecdote in which Wáng Dūn 王敦 addresses him in a letter laden with double-edged praise. Méi Táo also appears in the *Jìn shū* 晉書 in passing notices, and a short literary collection (*Méi Táo jí* 梅陶集) is recorded in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 but is now lost.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0127]] *Méizǐ xīnlùn* 梅子新論. CBDB has no entry; precise birth and death years are not preserved.
