Southern-Sòng scholar-official and natural-history lexicographer, *zì* Duānliáng 端良, *hào* Cúnzhāi 存齋, of Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Huīzhōu, modern Anhui). Son of the prominent statesman Luó Rǔjí 羅汝楫. Granted *jìnshì* 1166. Held offices including *Bìshū láng* and *Èzhōu zhīzhōu* under Xiàozōng. He compiled the *Ěryǎ yì* [[KR1j0012]] — the most ambitious of the medieval *yǎ*-style natural-history dictionaries — completed by Chúnxī 1 (1174); it was carved at the prefectural seat through Wáng Yìnglín's [[王應麟]] efforts and provides for ~400 plant, bird, beast, insect, and fish names extended commentaries drawing on the entire pre-Sòng corpus of citations. Luó also produced the *Xīnān zhì* 新安志 (1175), the foundational gazetteer of his native Huīzhōu prefecture, and a collected works *Luó Cúnzhāi jí* 羅鄂州小集. CBDB lifedates 1136–1184, in agreement with the *Sòngshǐ* biography and the catalog meta.
