Southern-Sòng scholar-official and natural-history lexicographer, *zì* Duān-liáng 端良, *hào* Cún-zhāi 存齋, of Shè-xiàn 歙縣 (Huīzhōu, modern Anhui). Son of the prominent statesman Luó Rǔ-jí 羅汝楫. Granted *jìn-shì* 1166. Held offices including *Bì-shū láng* and *È-zhōu zhī-zhōu* under Xiào-zōng. He compiled the *Ěryǎ yì* [[KR1j0012]] — the most ambitious of the medieval *yǎ*-style natural-history dictionaries — completed by Chún-xī 1 (1174); it was carved at the prefectural seat through Wáng Yìnglín's [[王應麟]] efforts and provides for ~400 plant, bird, beast, insect, and fish names extended commentaries drawing on the entire pre-Sòng corpus of citations. Luó also produced the *Xīn-ān zhì* 新安志 (1175), the foundational gazetteer of his native Huīzhōu prefecture, and a collected works *Luó Cún-zhāi jí* 羅鄂州小集. CBDB lifedates 1136–1184, in agreement with the *Sòng-shǐ* biography and the catalog meta.
