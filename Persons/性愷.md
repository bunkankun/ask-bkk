Xìngkǎi 性愷, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Tiěméi Sānbāzhǎng Huìlì** [[慧麗]] (via heir [[嵩]] Ěrān Sōng) in the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派. *Shūjì* 書記 (recorder) compiler of the 1-juan 《耳庵嵩禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0453]]), signing the colophon as *shūjì Xìngkǎi biān* 書記性愷編. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
