Xìng-kǎi 性愷, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Tiě-méi Sān-bā-zhǎng Huì-lì** [[慧麗]] (via heir [[嵩]] Ěr-ān Sōng) in the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派. *Shū-jì* 書記 (recorder) compiler of the 1-juan 《耳庵嵩禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0453]]), signing the colophon as *shū-jì Xìng-kǎi biān* 書記性愷編. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
