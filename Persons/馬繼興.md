Mǎ Jìxìng 馬繼興 (1925–2019) was the principal 20th-century philologist of the early Chinese medical corpus and a long-serving researcher at the Zhōngguó Zhōngyī yánjiū yuàn 中國中醫研究院. His magnum opus *Dūnhuáng gǔ yījí kǎoshì* 敦煌古醫籍考釋 (Nánchāng: Jiāngxī kēxué jìshù chūbǎnshè, 1988) is the foundational critical edition of the Dunhuang medical manuscripts; he is also the principal vetter and *editio princeps* editor of the reconstructed *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]).
