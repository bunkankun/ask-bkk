Late-Míng Confucian-physician of Xīnān 新安 (Huīzhōu, Ānhuī). Active in the *Chóngzhēn* era as a physician and editor; the principal contemporary description (in Jīn Shēng's preface to the *Wàitái mìyào* recut) characterises him as *chún rú* 醇儒 ("a pure Confucian") who is also penetrating in medicine, with painstakingly thorough clinical examinations of even the poorest patients. His principal editorial achievement is the 1640 *Xīnān* recutting of Wáng Tāo's [[KR3er091]] *Wàitái mìyào* 外臺秘要 in 40 *juǎn*, undertaken over ten years and published with the patronage of the regional military governor Zhāng Tiānlù. He followed this with a recutting of the *Shèngjì zǒnglù* 聖濟總錄. He is best known by his *zì* Jìngtōng 敬通.
