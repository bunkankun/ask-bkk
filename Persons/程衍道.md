Late-Míng Confucian-physician of Xīn-ān 新安 (Huī-zhōu, Ān-huī). Active in the *Chóng-zhēn* era as a physician and editor; the principal contemporary description (in Jīn Shēng's preface to the *Wài-tái mì-yào* recut) characterises him as *chún rú* 醇儒 ("a pure Confucian") who is also penetrating in medicine, with painstakingly thorough clinical examinations of even the poorest patients. His principal editorial achievement is the 1640 *Xīn-ān* recutting of Wáng Tāo's [[KR3er091]] *Wài-tái mì-yào* 外臺秘要 in 40 *juǎn*, undertaken over ten years and published with the patronage of the regional military governor Zhāng Tiān-lù. He followed this with a recutting of the *Shèng-jì zǒng-lù* 聖濟總錄. He is best known by his *zì* Jìng-tōng 敬通.
