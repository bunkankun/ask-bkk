# Zhāng Xiànzhī 張獻之

Great-grandson of [[張浚]] Zhāng Jùn (1097–1164). Active in the early-thirteenth century: his postface to his great-grandfather's *Zǐyán Yìzhuàn* 紫巖易傳 ([[KR1a0021]]) is dated *Jiādìng gēngchén* 嘉定庚辰 = 1220. He transcribed the autograph manuscript of the work for the family library, appending the *Dú Yì zá shuō* 讀易雜說 as a tenth juan. The Sìkù editors note that he was a copyist rather than a compiler — Zhū Xī's earlier *xíngzhuàng* of Zhāng Jùn already records the work as ten juan, so Xiànzhī's transcription only preserved a pre-existing textus.

Otherwise undocumented in CBDB or in the *Sòngshǐ*.
