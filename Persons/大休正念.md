# Dàxiū Zhèngniàn 大休正念

Southern Sòng Línjì-school Chan master who emigrated to Japan in 1269 at the invitation of Hōjō Tokimune 北條時宗. Served as abbot at Zenkō-ji 禪光寺, Jufuku-ji 壽福寺, Kenchō-ji 建長寺, and Engaku-ji 圓覺寺 in Kamakura. Founder of the Bukkō-ha-related Daikyū sub-lineage of Japanese Rinzai Zen. Died at Engaku-ji in 1290.
