Contemporary Chinese specialist of Buddhist textual history at the Institute of World Religions, Chinese Academy of Social Sciences. Long-time collaborator with Fāng Guǎngchāng on the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of *Qīzǔ fǎbǎo jì xià juǎn* ([[KR6v0023]]) and of the Nāgārjuna-attributed *Yīnyuán xīn lùn sòng* / *shì* / *kāijué jì* triad ([[KR6v0033]], [[KR6v0034]], [[KR6v0035]]) and the *Jīngāng bōrě jīng shū* ([[KR6v0036]]).
