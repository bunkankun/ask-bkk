# Yáo Xuàn 姚鉉 (968–1020)

*Zì* Bǎo-chén 寶臣. Native of Wú-xī 無錫 (the Sòng *Cháng-zhōu*, modern Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Tài-píng xīng-guó* 8 (983) at sixteen; appointed *Tāng-yīn-wèi* (Tāng-yīn military deputy), then *Dà-lǐ-pínɡ-shì*, *Zuǒ shí-yí*, *Zhí Shǐ-guǎn* (Hanlin attendant in the History Office). He moved through a succession of provincial posts — prefect of Hé-yáng, Jiànzhōu — and rose to *Liǎng-Zhè zhuǎn-yùn-shǐ*. He was impeached and dismissed under Zhēn-zōng (mid-1010s) and spent his late years out of office, dying in *Tiān-xǐ* 4 (1020) at 52. His magnum opus is the *Táng wén-cuì* 唐文粹 [[KR4h0024]] in 100 *juǎn* — a distillation of Táng prose and poetry from the *Wényuàn yīnghuá* [[KR4h0022]], completed *ca.* 1011, on which his scholarly reputation rests. The catalog meta gives 968–1020; CBDB confirms.
