# Yáo Xuàn 姚鉉 (968–1020)

*Zì* Bǎochén 寶臣. Native of Wúxī 無錫 (the Sòng *Chángzhōu*, modern Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Tàipíng xīngguó* 8 (983) at sixteen; appointed *Tāngyīnwèi* (Tāngyīn military deputy), then *Dàlǐ-pínɡ-shì*, *Zuǒ shíyí*, *Zhí Shǐguǎn* (Hanlin attendant in the History Office). He moved through a succession of provincial posts — prefect of Héyáng, Jiànzhōu — and rose to *LiǎngZhè zhuǎnyùnshǐ*. He was impeached and dismissed under Zhēnzōng (mid-1010s) and spent his late years out of office, dying in *Tiānxǐ* 4 (1020) at 52. His magnum opus is the *Táng wéncuì* 唐文粹 [[KR4h0024]] in 100 *juǎn* — a distillation of Táng prose and poetry from the *Wényuàn yīnghuá* [[KR4h0022]], completed *ca.* 1011, on which his scholarly reputation rests. The catalog meta gives 968–1020; CBDB confirms.
