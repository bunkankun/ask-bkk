# Zhèng Wéiān 鄭韋庵

A mid-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar of *Jiāngdū* 江都 (= Yángzhōu 揚州, Jiāngsū). *Wéiān* 韋庵 is most likely a *hào* (sobriquet); his given name (*míng*) is not preserved. Author of the **《持名四十八法》** *Chímíng sìshíbā fǎ* [[KR6p0125]] (X1206) — a forty-eight-method handbook of Pure Land *chímíng* recitation practice that has remained in continuous popular circulation in Chinese Pure Land circles from its late-Qīng composition to the present. He also composed the *Jièshā fàngshēng cí* 戒殺放生詞 — seven *cí* 詞 on the prohibition-of-killing and animal-release theme, set to the tune *Xījiāng yuè* 西江月 — appended to the *Sìshíbā fǎ*. Lifedates not preserved; the editorial layer of the work dates to the early 1860s, with the original composition somewhat earlier.
