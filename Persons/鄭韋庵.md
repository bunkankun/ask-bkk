# Zhèng Wéi-ān 鄭韋庵

A mid-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar of *Jiāng-dū* 江都 (= Yáng-zhōu 揚州, Jiāngsū). *Wéi-ān* 韋庵 is most likely a *hào* (sobriquet); his given name (*míng*) is not preserved. Author of the **《持名四十八法》** *Chí-míng sì-shí-bā fǎ* [[KR6p0125]] (X1206) — a forty-eight-method handbook of Pure Land *chí-míng* recitation practice that has remained in continuous popular circulation in Chinese Pure Land circles from its late-Qīng composition to the present. He also composed the *Jiè-shā fàng-shēng cí* 戒殺放生詞 — seven *cí* 詞 on the prohibition-of-killing and animal-release theme, set to the tune *Xī-jiāng yuè* 西江月 — appended to the *Sì-shí-bā fǎ*. Lifedates not preserved; the editorial layer of the work dates to the early 1860s, with the original composition somewhat earlier.
