Late-Míng Buddhist monk and Lotus Sūtra exegete; *zì* **Yī-yǔ** 一雨 (also referenced as **Yī-yǔ Tōngrùn** 一雨通潤), self-style **Èr-lèng zhǔ-rén** 二楞主人 ("Master of the Two-Lèngyán [Sūtras]"). DILA Authority A001425. Native of **Dòng-tíng Xī-shān** 洞庭西山 (Lake Tài, Sūzhōu region). Born 1565; died Tiānqǐ 天啟 4, 9th month, 18th day (= 29 October 1624), aged 60.

Major work: the **Fǎhuá jīng dà-kuǎn** 法華經大窾 ([[KR6d0080]], X31n0614, 7 *juan*) — a Lotus Sūtra commentary in the *jiān* 箋 ("annotated") tradition. The work belongs to the late-Míng critical-evaluative tradition of Lotus Sūtra commentary, alongside Jiāo Hóng's *Jīng-jiě píng-lín* ([[KR6d0078]]) and Hān-shān Dé-qīng's productions ([[KR6d0076]], [[KR6d0077]]).

Sources: *Wú-dū fǎ-shèng* 吳都法乘 *juan* 6 (the *Yī-yǔ fǎ-shī tǎ-míng* 一雨法師塔銘); DILA A001425.
