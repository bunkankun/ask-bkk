# Cíyuán Gāochún 慈元高淳

Edo-period Japanese Vinaya editor (*Jigen Kōjun* in Japanese reading), based at *Jīnfēng* 金峰. Recorded as the collator of a *Kyōhō* 享保 2 (1717) Japanese edition of the Sòng Vinaya subcommentary *Shìmén guījìng yí hùfǎ jì* 釋門歸敬儀護法記 by [[彥起]] Yànqǐ ([[KR6k0252]], X1094) — itself a copy made at the *Xīnwángyuàn dōngliáo* 心王院東寮 in *Kanbun* 寬文 9 (1669) by [[宗覺]] Zōngjué. No further biographical information preserved.
