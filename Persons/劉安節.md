**Liú Ānjié** 劉安節 (1068–1116), *zì* Yuánchéng 元承, of Wēnzhōu Yǒngjiā 永嘉. *Jìnshì* of *Yuánfú* 3 / 1100. Held office to *Qǐjūláng* (Diary Recorder); promoted *Tàicháng shǎoqīng*; out as *Zhī Ráozhōu*; transferred *Zhī Xuānzhōu* — died in office. Cousin / near-relative of Liú Ānshàng 劉安上 [[劉安上]]; with him and Zhōu Xíngjǐ 周行己 [[周行己]] known as the *Sān xiānshēng* 三先生 of Yǒngjiā — foundational triad of the *Yǒngjiā xuépài*. All three studied with Chéng Yí 程頤 [[程頤]]. Author of *Liú zuǒshǐ jí* 劉左史集 [[KR4d0128]] in 4 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 347. Catalog confirms 1068–1116.
