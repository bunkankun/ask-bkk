**Liú Ān-jié** 劉安節 (1068–1116), *zì* Yuán-chéng 元承, of Wēn-zhōu Yǒng-jiā 永嘉. *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-fú* 3 / 1100. Held office to *Qǐ-jū-láng* (Diary Recorder); promoted *Tài-cháng shǎo-qīng*; out as *Zhī Ráo-zhōu*; transferred *Zhī Xuān-zhōu* — died in office. Cousin / near-relative of Liú Ān-shàng 劉安上 [[劉安上]]; with him and Zhōu Xíng-jǐ 周行己 [[周行己]] known as the *Sān xiān-shēng* 三先生 of Yǒng-jiā — foundational triad of the *Yǒng-jiā xué-pài*. All three studied with Chéng Yí 程頤 [[程頤]]. Author of *Liú zuǒ-shǐ jí* 劉左史集 [[KR4d0128]] in 4 *juǎn*. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 347. Catalog confirms 1068–1116.
