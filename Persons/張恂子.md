Zhāng Xúnzǐ 張恂子 was a Republican-era popular fiction writer, author of *Suídài Gōngwéi Shǐ* 隋代宮闈史 ([[KR4k0321]]), a historical novel on the Suí dynasty. No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1910s–1940s.
