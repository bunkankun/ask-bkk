# Huáng Xī 黃希

*Zì* Mèng-dé 夢得. Native of *Yí-huáng xiàn* 宜黃 in *Fú-zhōu* 撫州 (modern *Yí-huáng* in central Jiāng-xī). Sòng-period *jìn-shì*, who rose to *Yí-huáng xiàn lìng* 永新令 (county magistrate of *Yǒng-xīn* in *Jí-zhōu* 吉州). Floruit late 12th century. Built the *Chūn-fēng-táng* 春風堂 ("Hall of the Spring Wind") at his magistracy; Yáng Wàn-lǐ 楊萬里 (1127–1206) wrote a *jì* for it, preserved in *Chéng-zhāi jí* 誠齋集.

His scholarly significance is as the original editor of the *Bǔ qiān-jiā jí-zhù Dù gōng-bù shī shǐ* — the running supplementary annotation to Dù Fǔ that he undertook around 1180, drawing on the prior Sòng *jí-zhù* tradition (*Wáng Zhū*, *Zhào Cì-gōng*, *Shī Yǐn*, *Bào Biāo*, *Dù Xiū-kě*, *Lǔ Yìn*). He died with the work unfinished; his son Huáng Hè [[黃鶴]] completed it in *Jiā-dìng* 9 (1216) as the *Bǔ-zhù Dù shī* [[KR4c0017]]. CBDB has no entry.
