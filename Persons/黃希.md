# Huáng Xī 黃希

*Zì* Mèngdé 夢得. Native of *Yíhuáng xiàn* 宜黃 in *Fúzhōu* 撫州 (modern *Yíhuáng* in central Jiāngxī). Sòng-period *jìnshì*, who rose to *Yíhuáng xiàn lìng* 永新令 (county magistrate of *Yǒngxīn* in *Jízhōu* 吉州). Floruit late 12th century. Built the *Chūnfēngtáng* 春風堂 ("Hall of the Spring Wind") at his magistracy; Yáng Wànlǐ 楊萬里 (1127–1206) wrote a *jì* for it, preserved in *Chéngzhāi jí* 誠齋集.

His scholarly significance is as the original editor of the *Bǔ qiānjiā jízhù Dù gōngbù shī shǐ* — the running supplementary annotation to Dù Fǔ that he undertook around 1180, drawing on the prior Sòng *jízhù* tradition (*Wáng Zhū*, *Zhào Cìgōng*, *Shī Yǐn*, *Bào Biāo*, *Dù Xiūkě*, *Lǔ Yìn*). He died with the work unfinished; his son Huáng Hè [[黃鶴]] completed it in *Jiādìng* 9 (1216) as the *Bǔzhù Dù shī* [[KR4c0017]]. CBDB has no entry.
