Eastern-Hàn classicist of the *guǔwén* school, *fl.* under Guāngwǔ 25–57 and into the early *Yǒngpíng* reign. Native of Dōnghǎi 東海. *Zì* Jìngzhòng 敬仲. Studied the *Máo Shī* with Xiè Mànqīng 謝曼卿; in the *Jiànwǔ* era was summoned to court as Erudite (*Bóshì*). His *Máo Shī xù* 毛詩序 (the *Greater* and *Lesser* Prefaces of the *Máo Shī*, in his received version) is the foundational interpretive frame for the *Máo* tradition; the attribution is standard in the *Hòu Hàn shū* biography but contested in modern scholarship. He also wrote a *Máo Shī Zá yì* 毛詩雜義. His name is associated with several Hàn institutional manuals—*Hàn yí zhù* 漢儀注, *Hàn lǐ yí* 漢禮儀, and the *Hàn guān jiù yí* 漢官舊儀 ([[KR2m0020]])—of which only the last survives in fragmentary reconstructed form. The conventional *fl.* date is mid-1st century CE; precise life-dates are not transmitted.
