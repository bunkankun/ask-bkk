**Tián Kuàng** 田況 (1005–1063), *zì* Yuánjūn 元均. Family ancestral home Jīngzhào 京兆, moved to Xīndū 新都 (Sìchuān). *Jìnshì* and *xiánliáng fāngzhèng* examination success early under *Rénzōng*; rose through *Tàicháng chéng* → *Shǎnxī jīnglüè pànguān* (assigned to the XīXià frontier under Xià Sòng 夏竦) → *Yòu zhèngyán* → military commissions in QínShǔ → finally *Shūmì shǐ* 樞密使 (Military Affairs Commissioner; chief minister-equivalent for military affairs) → retired as *Guānwén diàn dàxuéshì* 觀文殿大學士 with the *Jǐnglíng gōng* honorific. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* 292. CBDB id 1617 records the lifedates 1005–1063.

His career identifies him squarely with the senior *Rénzōng* civil-military elite of the *Qìnglì* — *Jiāyòu* generation. As a young officer he had been Xià Sòng's *mùliáo* at the XīXià frontier; the Hǎoshuǐchuān defeat (1041) is the formative event of his early career, and his vigorous memorial-criticism after it shaped his reputation as a constructive critic of policy. His one surviving prose work is [[KR3l0029]] *Rúlín gōngyì* 儒林公議 in 1 *juàn*, the principal contemporary witness to the rise of *péngdǎng* (faction) rhetoric in the *Qìnglì xīnzhèng* period. His 30-*juàn* memorial collection is lost; some pieces survive in *Sòng huìyào jígǎo* and other collections.
