**Tián Kuàng** 田況 (1005–1063), *zì* Yuán-jūn 元均. Family ancestral home Jīng-zhào 京兆, moved to Xīn-dū 新都 (Sìchuān). *Jìnshì* and *xián-liáng fāng-zhèng* examination success early under *Rén-zōng*; rose through *Tài-cháng chéng* → *Shǎn-xī jīng-lüè pàn-guān* (assigned to the Xī-Xià frontier under Xià Sòng 夏竦) → *Yòu zhèng-yán* → military commissions in Qín-Shǔ → finally *Shū-mì shǐ* 樞密使 (Military Affairs Commissioner; chief minister-equivalent for military affairs) → retired as *Guān-wén diàn dà-xué-shì* 觀文殿大學士 with the *Jǐng-líng gōng* honorific. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* 292. CBDB id 1617 records the lifedates 1005–1063.

His career identifies him squarely with the senior *Rén-zōng* civil-military elite of the *Qìng-lì* — *Jiā-yòu* generation. As a young officer he had been Xià Sòng's *mù-liáo* at the Xī-Xià frontier; the Hǎo-shuǐ-chuān defeat (1041) is the formative event of his early career, and his vigorous memorial-criticism after it shaped his reputation as a constructive critic of policy. His one surviving prose work is [[KR3l0029]] *Rú-lín gōng-yì* 儒林公議 in 1 *juàn*, the principal contemporary witness to the rise of *péng-dǎng* (faction) rhetoric in the *Qìng-lì xīn-zhèng* period. His 30-*juàn* memorial collection is lost; some pieces survive in *Sòng huì-yào jí-gǎo* and other collections.
