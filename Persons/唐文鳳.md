Táng Wén-fèng 唐文鳳 (mid-Yǒng-lè to mid-Tiān-shùn era; d. aged 86), *zì* **Zǐ-yí** 子儀, *hào* **Mèng-hè** 夢鶴 / **Wú-gāng** 梧岡. Second son of **Táng Guì-fāng** 唐桂芳 and grandson of **Táng Yuán** 唐元 (the three jointly known as **Xiǎo Sān Sū** 小三蘇 in deliberate analogy with the great Sòng Sū lineage); native of *Wù-yuán* 婺源 / *Xīn-ān* 新安 (Huī-zhōu). By recommendation appointed *Jiāng-xī Gàn-zhōu-fǔ Xīng-guó-xiàn zhī-xiàn* 江西贛州府興國縣知縣; reassigned *Zhào-fǔ jì-shàn* 趙府紀善. As Xīng-guó magistrate praised for administrative achievements; **Liú Hóng** 劉鴻 of Tài-hé composed his *Xián-lìng-cí jì* 賢令祠記. The Chéng-huà *Hàn-lín* scholar **Chéng Mǐn-zhèng** 程敏政 ([[程敏政]]) compiled the family lineage's collective edition *Táng-shì sān xiān-shēng jí* 唐氏三先生集. Wén-fèng's surviving works are [[KR4e0097]] *Wú-gāng jí* (10 *juǎn*; less than 30–40% of the original six-context corpus per his fifth-generation descendant Táng Zé 唐澤's epitaph).
