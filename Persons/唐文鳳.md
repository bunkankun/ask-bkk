Táng Wénfèng 唐文鳳 (mid-Yǒng-lè to mid-Tiān-shùn era; d. aged 86), *zì* **Zǐyí** 子儀, *hào* **Mènghè** 夢鶴 / **Wúgāng** 梧岡. Second son of **Táng Guìfāng** 唐桂芳 and grandson of **Táng Yuán** 唐元 (the three jointly known as **Xiǎo Sān Sū** 小三蘇 in deliberate analogy with the great Sòng Sū lineage); native of *Wùyuán* 婺源 / *Xīnān* 新安 (Huīzhōu). By recommendation appointed *Jiāngxī Gànzhōufǔ Xīngguóxiàn zhīxiàn* 江西贛州府興國縣知縣; reassigned *Zhàofǔ jìshàn* 趙府紀善. As Xīngguó magistrate praised for administrative achievements; **Liú Hóng** 劉鴻 of Tàihé composed his *Xiánlìngcí jì* 賢令祠記. The Chénghuà *Hànlín* scholar **Chéng Mǐnzhèng** 程敏政 ([[程敏政]]) compiled the family lineage's collective edition *Tángshì sān xiānshēng jí* 唐氏三先生集. Wénfèng's surviving works are [[KR4e0097]] *Wúgāng jí* (10 *juǎn*; less than 30–40% of the original six-context corpus per his fifth-generation descendant Táng Zé 唐澤's epitaph).
