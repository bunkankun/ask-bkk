Dé-néng 德能, *zì* **Yù-rì** 浴日 ("Bathing Sun"); often referred to as **Yù-rì Dé-néng** 浴日德能. **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng** [[智祥]] (1637–1709) — eighth-generation Cáodòng descendant in the **Qīng-yuán** 青原 → **Zōng-jìng** 宗鏡 line. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Guǎng-zhōu** 廣州 (Guǎng-dōng); lay surname **Wèi** 魏.

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0568]] 《頻吉祥禪師語錄》 15 juan, J39 B454.
- Own *yǔ-lù* 《弁山龍華浴日能禪師語錄》 10 juan (separate canonical entry, J39).

**Sources.** 《正源略集》 vol. 13.
