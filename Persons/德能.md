Dénéng 德能, *zì* **Yùrì** 浴日 ("Bathing Sun"); often referred to as **Yùrì Dénéng** 浴日德能. **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Pínjí Zhìxiáng** [[智祥]] (1637–1709) — eighth-generation Cáodòng descendant in the **Qīngyuán** 青原 → **Zōngjìng** 宗鏡 line. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Guǎngzhōu** 廣州 (Guǎngdōng); lay surname **Wèi** 魏.

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0568]] 《頻吉祥禪師語錄》 15 juan, J39 B454.
- Own *yǔlù* 《弁山龍華浴日能禪師語錄》 10 juan (separate canonical entry, J39).

**Sources.** 《正源略集》 vol. 13.
