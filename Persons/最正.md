Zuì-zhèng 最正, *hào* **Zhì-tán** 智覃. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiù-yě Míng-lín** [[明林]] in the Lín-yě → Zì-xián → Tiān-mù → Xiù-yě → Zuì-zhèng sub-line.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0496]] 《秀野林禪師語錄》 — compiled his master Míng-lín's *yǔ-lù*.
- [[KR6q0497]] 《智覃正禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B358 — his own *yǔ-lù*, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Shàng-néng** [[尚能]] + **Shàng-yíng** [[尚瑩]] *děng biān*.
