Zuìzhèng 最正, *hào* **Zhìtán** 智覃. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiùyě Mínglín** [[明林]] in the Línyě → Zìxián → Tiānmù → Xiùyě → Zuìzhèng sub-line.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0496]] 《秀野林禪師語錄》 — compiled his master Mínglín's *yǔlù*.
- [[KR6q0497]] 《智覃正禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B358 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by *fǎsì* **Shàngnéng** [[尚能]] + **Shàngyíng** [[尚瑩]] *děng biān*.
