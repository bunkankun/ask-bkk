Dépǔ 德普, *zì* **Cíjì** 慈濟 ("Compassionate Aid"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bùyàn Dàolè** [[道樂]] (b. 1640) at **Chóngqìng Huáyán chányuàn** 重慶府華嚴禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*jìlù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0538]] 《華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B419, prefaced 1693.

- - - - -

A separate **Dépǔ** 德普 (DILA A014486, **Héshān Dépǔ** 禾山德普, ?1025–?1091/2) was a Northern-Sòng Línjì-Huánglóng monk, dharma-heir of **Yúnfēng Wényuè** in the Jízhōu Héshān line — not the same person.
