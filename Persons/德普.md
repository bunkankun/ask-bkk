Dé-pǔ 德普, *zì* **Cí-jì** 慈濟 ("Compassionate Aid"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bù-yàn Dào-lè** [[道樂]] (b. 1640) at **Chóng-qìng Huá-yán chán-yuàn** 重慶府華嚴禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*jì-lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0538]] 《華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B419, prefaced 1693.

- - - - -

A separate **Dé-pǔ** 德普 (DILA A014486, **Hé-shān Dé-pǔ** 禾山德普, ?1025–?1091/2) was a Northern-Sòng Línjì-Huánglóng monk, dharma-heir of **Yún-fēng Wén-yuè** in the Jí-zhōu Hé-shān line — not the same person.
