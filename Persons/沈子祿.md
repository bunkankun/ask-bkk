**Shěn Zǐlù** 沈子祿 (*zì* Chéngzhī 承之, fl. mid-16th c., 明), Wú-jiāng 吳江 / Sūzhōu region literatus-physician of the Jiā-jìng period; close friend of Xú Shīcēng 徐師曾. Author of the original *Jīng-mài fēn-yě* 經脈分野, a monograph on the channel-collateral system that he gave to Xú Shīcēng with a request to edit and preface it. Shěn died in Beijing in the Jiā-jìng final years without seeing the project to publication; Xú revised, expanded, and printed the work in 1576 (Wàn-lì 4) as the *Jīngluò quánshū* 經絡全書 ([[KR3ee011]]). No further biographic record survives.
