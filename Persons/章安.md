Northern Sòng 北宋 court official and Daoist-exegetical scholar, author of the ten-*juàn* [[KR5c0064|*Sòng Huī zōng Dàodé zhēn jīng jiě yì*]] 宋徽宗道德真經解義 (DZ 681) — a subcommentary on the imperial *Dàodé jīng* commentary of Sòng Huīzōng ([[徽宗]]; [[KR5c0063]], DZ 680). Composed before the Jurchen fall of the Northern Sòng in 1125–26.

**Office.** Zhāng Ān is identified in his own preface to DZ 681 by the official title **承議郎** (*chéng yì láng*, "Gentleman of the Consultation") — an eighth-grade imperial court rank attested in the *Sòng huì yào jí gǎo* 宋會要輯稿 for the Zhènghé 政和 era (1111–1118) and later. No other biographical details are available; he does not appear in the *Sòng shǐ* 宋史 biographies.

**Work.** His one transmitted work, DZ 681, is a major document of the Northern-Sòng imperial-Daoist exegetical tradition. The subcommentary responds to Huīzōng's *yù jiě* of c. 1111–1118 and was probably addressed personally to the emperor shortly after. Its philosophical character — echoes of [[郭象]]'s *Zhuāngzǐ* commentary, sustained articulation of the *Chóngxuán* 重玄 doctrine of *fēi yǒu fēi wú* 非有非無, and a *wú wéi* 無為 reading of the *Lǎozǐ* as a doctrine of *róu ruò* 柔弱 (weakness) and *bù zhēng* 不爭 (non-contention) — places it within the late Northern-Sòng synthesis of *Chóngxuán*, Mādhyamika-Buddhist, and emergent Neo-Confucian philosophical vocabularies.

**Preface highlights.** Zhāng Ān's preface is an elegant specimen of Sòng imperial-memorial prose. He frames Huīzōng as a sage-ruler of divinely endowed learning (*tiān zòng shén shèng* 天縱神聖), whose imperial commentary has made the *Dàodé jīng* "match Lǎozǐ across a thousand years as if by tally"; and positions his own subcommentary as a humble gloss — "a well-frog before Heaven, a firefly before the sun and moon" — written at the emperor's charge. The rhetorical self-positioning is a classical instance of court-Daoist commentary-writing convention.

**Dating.** Activity attested for the late Zhènghé era (c. 1115–1125). No lifedates are known. No CBDB record.

**Disambiguation.** Not to be confused with **章安大師 Zhāng'ān dàshī** (Guàn dǐng 灌頂, 561–632), the Suí-early-Táng Tiāntái 天台 Buddhist patriarch and compiler of Zhìyǐ's 智顗 lectures — a different figure from a different era.
