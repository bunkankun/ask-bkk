Jiǎng Pǔ 蔣溥 (1708–1761), *zì* Zhìfǔ 質甫, posthumously *Wénkè* 文恪, was a Mid-Qīng Qiánlóng-court official. Son of Jiǎng Tíngxī 蔣廷錫. Native of Chángshú 常熟 (Sūzhōu region). *Jìnshì* of *Yōngzhèng shíèr* (1734); rose to *Tàizǐ tàibǎo* and *Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì*. Compiler-in-chief (with Liú Tǒngxùn) of the *Yù lǎn jīngshǐ jiǎng yì* 御覽經史講義 ([[KR3a0110]]). CBDB id 57226.
