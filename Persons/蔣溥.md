Jiǎng Pǔ 蔣溥 (1708–1761), *zì* Zhìfǔ 質甫, posthumously *Wénkè* 文恪, was a Mid-Qīng Qiánlóng-court official. Son of Jiǎng Tíng-xī 蔣廷錫. Native of Cháng-shú 常熟 (Sūzhōu region). *Jìnshì* of *Yōngzhèng shí-èr* (1734); rose to *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo* and *Wǔ-yīng-diàn dà-xué-shì*. Compiler-in-chief (with Liú Tǒng-xùn) of the *Yù lǎn jīng-shǐ jiǎng yì* 御覽經史講義 (KR3a0110). CBDB id 57226.
