**Lù Mào-xiū** 陸懋修 (1818–1886), *zì* Jiǔ-zhī 九芝, *hào* Jiāng-zuǒ xià-gōng 江左下工 (sometimes 林屋山人), was the leading Sūzhōu 蘇州 (Yuán-hé 元和) physician of the late Tóngzhì / Guāngxù generation and the principal continuator of Yè Tiānshì's *wēnbìng* tradition in late Qīng. A *xiùcái* of the Dào-guāng era, he turned fully to medicine after the Tàipíng wars (during which his family was displaced from Sūzhōu) and built a major Shànghǎi practice in the 1860s–1880s. He compiled the *Shì-bǔ-zhāi yī shū* 世補齋醫書 (1884), one of the major late-Qing medical *cóngshū* — 35 *zhǒng*, including several of Lù's own original works:

- *Nèijīng yùnqì bìng shì* 內經運氣病釋, 9 juan ([[KR3ea047]]).
- *Nèijīng yùnqì biǎo* 內經運氣表 ([[KR3ea048]]).
- *Nèijīng nán zì yīn yì* 內經難字音義 ([[KR3ea049]]).
- *Bù lǐ yú jí* 不謝齋集 (his collected medical essays).
- *Shì jīng yù dú* 釋經餘讀.

His clinical orientation is *wēnbìng*-school but with a strong commitment to grounding *wēnbìng* in *yùnqì* doctrine. He died in 1886 aged 68.
