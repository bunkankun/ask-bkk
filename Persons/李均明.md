**Lǐ Jūnmíng** 李均明, modern Chinese historian and epigrapher specializing in Qin and Han dynasty bamboo and wooden slip (*jiǎndú* 簡牘) documents. Active from the 1980s through the 2010s at minimum. Researcher affiliated with major Chinese academic institutions for the study of excavated documents.

Co-editor (with [[謝桂華]] Xiè Guìhuá, 1938–2006) of the ***Jūyán Hànjian shìwén héjiào*** 居延漢簡釋文合校 (Wenwu, 1987, 2 vols.) — the standard collated-transcription edition of the old Juyan bamboo slips ([[KR5a0332]]). Also co-edited (with Rào Zōngyí 饒宗頤 as chief ed.) the ***Jūyán Hànjian biānnián*** 居延漢簡編年 (Xinwenfeng, 2004), a chronological arrangement of the Juyan documents. His monograph ***Qín Hàn jiǎndú wénshū fēnlèi jíjiě*** 秦汉简牍文书分类集解 (Wenwu, 2009) sorts documents from both Juyan finds by documentary category with detailed annotations, and has been described as "the perfect introduction to this type of historical source" (Wilkinson, §59.7.2.1).

Precise birth and death dates are not recorded in the sources consulted.
