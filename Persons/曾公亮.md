Zēng Gōngliàng 曾公亮 (998–1078), *zì* Míngzhòng 明仲, posthumously *Wényì* 文翊, was a leading Northern-Sòng minister and Chancellor under Rén-zōng and Yīng-zōng. Rose to *Tóng zhōng-shū-mén-xià píng-zhāng-shì* (Chancellor) under Rénzōng. Co-supervised (with Dīng Dù 丁度) the imperial compilation of the *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* 武經總要 (KR3b0012, *Bǎo-yuán* 1 / 1038–1044), the major comprehensive Northern-Sòng military encyclopedia. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 312.
