# 黃易 Huáng Yì

**Huáng Yì** 黃易, mid-Míng literatus of Yìyáng 弋陽 (Jiāngxī); wrote the 1537 preface (at Jiǔtán jīngshè 九潭精舍) to [[高武]] Gāo Wǔ's *Zhēnjiǔ Sù Nàn yàozhǐ* 針灸素難要旨 ([[KR3ee021]]). Not the well-known Qīng seal-engraver of the same name.
