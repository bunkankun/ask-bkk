Huì-lù 慧輅, *zì* **Dì-huī** 諦暉 (variant 諦輝); often referred to as **Dì-huī Huì-lù** 諦暉慧輅. Born **明天啟丁卯十月初八** = **1627-11-15** Gregorian; died **雍正乙巳三月廿日** = **1725-05-02**, age 99. Long-lived Buddhist scholar-monk. Author of the principal preface to [[KR6q0588]] 《別牧純禪師語錄》 J40 B476.

**Sources.** 《雲棲山志》 vol. 22.

- - - - -

This **Huì-lù** 慧輅 is also the same person who wrote the third preface (1695) to the *Hè-fēng chán-shī yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0540]], identifying himself as a 靈隱同門弟 (Líng-yǐn co-disciple). The long lifespan (99 years) accounts for prefaces written across more than three decades.
