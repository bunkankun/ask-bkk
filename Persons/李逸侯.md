Lǐ Yìhóu 李逸侯 was a Republican-era popular fiction writer, author of *Sòngdài Shíbā Cháo Gōngtíng Yànshǐ* 宋代十八朝宮廷豔史 ([[KR4k0319]], [[KR4k0320]]), a historical novel covering the Sòng dynasty. The catalog entry erroneously records the author as "宮廷豔史" (a genre label); the source file clearly identifies the author as Lǐ Yìhóu. No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1910s–1940s.
