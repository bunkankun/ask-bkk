# Lín Guāngcháo 林光朝 (1114–1178)

*Zì* Qiān-zhī 謙之. *Hào* Ài-xuān 艾軒 ("Mugwort Pavilion"). Posthumous *shì* Wén-jié 文節 (one tradition); also styled *Nán fū-zǐ* 南夫子 ("Master of the South") — the last by his contemporaries' acclaim. Native of Pú-tián 莆田 (Fú-jiàn). Lifedates 1114–1178, CBDB id 10612.

The principal *Lǐ-xué* figure of pre-Zhū-Xī Southern-Sòng Pú-tián. Son-in-law of Zhèng Xiá 鄭俠; disciple of Lù Zǐ-zhèng 陸子正 and (through Lù) of Yáng Shí 楊時 / Yǐn Tūn 尹焞 — the principal channel of Northern-Sòng *Lǐ-xué* into Pú-tián before Zhū Xī's mature synthesis. Sixteen years senior to Zhū Xī [[朱熹]]; Zhū is recorded as having addressed Lín as elder brother (per Liú Kè-zhuāng's lost-but-cited preface to the 20-*juǎn* recension of Lín's collection).

*Jìn-shì* of *Lóng-xīng* 1 (1163) at age 50. Court career: *Guó-zǐ-jiān jì-jiǔ* concurrent *Tài-zǐ Zuǒ yù-dé* (Imperial Academy Director and Crown Prince's Tutor); *Zhōng-shū shě-rén* concurrent *shì-jiǎng*. As *Shě-rén* he returned the drafted edict (*cí-tóu jiǎo-huán*) for Xiè Kuò-rán's 謝廓然 appointment — an unusual formal administrative protest, particularly remembered in the Sòng tradition. Reassigned as *Gōng-bù shì-láng* concurrent *Jí-yīng-diàn xiū-zhuàn zhī Wù-zhōu* (Prefect of Wù-zhōu); died in office.

In life he disliked composing books. The *Lǐ-xué* tradition holds his works as authoritative *yǔ-lù* (lecture-transmissions) recorded by disciples rather than written compositions. Compositions surviving were assembled posthumously: Lín's clansman Tóng-shū 同叔 made a 10-*juǎn* recension (with Chén Mì 陳宓 preface); his nephew Fāng Zhī-tài expanded to 20 *juǎn* (with Liú Kè-zhuāng 劉克莊 preface, carved at Pó-yáng); both lost by the Míng. Zhèng Yuè 鄭岳 in 1521 made a *xuǎn-běn* from a surviving manuscript, which is the present 9-*juǎn* + 1-*juǎn* recension.
