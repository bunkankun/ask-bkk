# Lín Guāngcháo 林光朝 (1114–1178)

*Zì* Qiānzhī 謙之. *Hào* Àixuān 艾軒 ("Mugwort Pavilion"). Posthumous *shì* Wénjié 文節 (one tradition); also styled *Nán fūzǐ* 南夫子 ("Master of the South") — the last by his contemporaries' acclaim. Native of Pútián 莆田 (Fújiàn). Lifedates 1114–1178, CBDB id 10612.

The principal *Lǐxué* figure of pre-Zhū-Xī Southern-Sòng Pútián. Son-in-law of Zhèng Xiá 鄭俠; disciple of Lù Zǐzhèng 陸子正 and (through Lù) of Yáng Shí 楊時 / Yǐn Tūn 尹焞 — the principal channel of Northern-Sòng *Lǐxué* into Pútián before Zhū Xī's mature synthesis. Sixteen years senior to Zhū Xī [[朱熹]]; Zhū is recorded as having addressed Lín as elder brother (per Liú Kèzhuāng's lost-but-cited preface to the 20-*juǎn* recension of Lín's collection).

*Jìnshì* of *Lóngxīng* 1 (1163) at age 50. Court career: *Guózǐjiān jìjiǔ* concurrent *Tàizǐ Zuǒ yùdé* (Imperial Academy Director and Crown Prince's Tutor); *Zhōngshū shěrén* concurrent *shìjiǎng*. As *Shěrén* he returned the drafted edict (*cítóu jiǎohuán*) for Xiè Kuòrán's 謝廓然 appointment — an unusual formal administrative protest, particularly remembered in the Sòng tradition. Reassigned as *Gōngbù shìláng* concurrent *Jíyīngdiàn xiūzhuàn zhī Wùzhōu* (Prefect of Wùzhōu); died in office.

In life he disliked composing books. The *Lǐxué* tradition holds his works as authoritative *yǔlù* (lecture-transmissions) recorded by disciples rather than written compositions. Compositions surviving were assembled posthumously: Lín's clansman Tóngshū 同叔 made a 10-*juǎn* recension (with Chén Mì 陳宓 preface); his nephew Fāng Zhītài expanded to 20 *juǎn* (with Liú Kèzhuāng 劉克莊 preface, carved at Póyáng); both lost by the Míng. Zhèng Yuè 鄭岳 in 1521 made a *xuǎnběn* from a surviving manuscript, which is the present 9-*juǎn* + 1-*juǎn* recension.
