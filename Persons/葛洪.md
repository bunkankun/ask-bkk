**Gě Hóng** 葛洪 (283–343), *zì* Zhì chuān 稚川, self-styled Bào pǔ zǐ 抱朴子 ("Master Embracing the Unhewn"), from Jù róng 句容 (Jiāngsū). Eastern-Jìn alchemist, physician, and author of the foundational Daoist alchemical encyclopedia the ***Bào pǔ zǐ***, divided into Inner Chapters (*Nèi piān* 內篇, on immortality, alchemy, and techniques) and Outer Chapters (*Wài piān* 外篇, on socio-political thought). Also author of the *Shén xiān zhuàn* 神仙傳 (Biographies of Divine Immortals) and the medical *Zhǒu hòu bèi jí fāng* 肘後備急方. The third *juàn* of [[KR5c0278]] *Tài qīng jīn yè shén dān jīng* is attributed to him. Gě's dates (283–343) come from his *Jìn shū* 晉書 biography (*juàn* 72).
