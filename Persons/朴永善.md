Pak Yŏngsŏn 朴永善 (Sino-Korean transcription Bú Yǒngshàn), late-Joseon Korean lay Buddhist scholar; lifedates not transmitted; floruit late 19th c. Native of **Hanyang** 漢陽 (Seoul). His sole Kanripo work is the **《朝鮮禪教考》** *Cháoxiǎn chánjiào kǎo* ([[KR6r0114]], X1622, 1 juan) — a chronological-annal compendium of the introduction and development of Buddhism in Korea from the 4th c. CE through the 10th-c. Sòng-Koryŏ transmission, anchored to **Kim Pusik's** 金富軾 *Sānguó shǐ* 三國史. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000384.
