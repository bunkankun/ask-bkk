**Liú Zhōnghéng** 劉鍾衡 (fl. late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng physician and military-affairs official, active in the Tóngzhì–Guāng-xù period. His autobiography in the *Zhōng-xī huì-cān tóng-rén tú-shuō* 中西匯參銅人圖說 ([[KR3ee034]], 1894) preface records his initial training in the standard Míng-Qīng medical canon (Xuē Lì-zhāi, Lǐ Shìcái, Zhāng Jǐng-yuè, Zhào Xiàn-kě, Zhāng Shí-wán), his observation of anatomical structures during anti-rebellion campaigns, his acquisition of Wáng Qīngrén's *Yī-lín gǎi-cuò* in Chénzhōu 辰州, and his subsequent engagement with Benjamin Hobson's *Quán-tǐ xīn-lùn* and other 19th-century Western medical translations after arriving in Shàng-hǎi in 1884.
