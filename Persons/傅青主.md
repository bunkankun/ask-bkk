*This file is an alias-only stub.* Fù Qīngzhǔ 傅青主 is the *zì* (courtesy name) by which Fù Shān 傅山 (1607–1684) is most commonly cited in medical contexts. The primary person note is at [[傅山]]. Several works of disputed attribution circulate under the name *Fù Qīngzhǔ* and are likely **pseudepigraphic** — including the *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē* 傅青主女科 (gynaecology, with strong evidence of Chén Shìduó 陳士鐸 authorship) and the *Dàxiǎo zhūzhèng fānglùn* 大小諸證方論 ([[KR3ed080]]; the surviving text borrows substantially from Chén Shìduó's *Shíshì mìlù* 石室秘錄).
