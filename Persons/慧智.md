Huìzhì 慧智 (active c. 670s), Indian (梵僧) monk resident at the Chán-lín sì 禪林寺 in Xī-jīng 西京 (Chāng'ān), who served as oral translator / interpreter (*chuán yì* 傳譯) for the direct Chán teaching-exchange between the Kashmīrī monk Buddhapālita ([[覺愛]]) and the Chinese monk [[明恂]] Míngxún at the same monastery in Yí-fèng 2 (677), preserved as the *Xiū chán yào jué* 修禪要訣 ([[KR6q0116]]). No independent biographical material survives; no DILA authority entry. Distinct from other monks of the name 慧智 in Táng tradition.
