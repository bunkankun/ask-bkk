Mid-Qīng scholar from Wúxīng 吳興 (Guī'ān 歸安), Zhèjiāng, younger brother of [[姚衡]] Yáo Héng and uncle of [[姚覲元]] Yáo Jìn-yuán. CBDB id 514458 (index year 1788). Wrote one of the two introductory prefaces (the 姚序 dated *Dàoguāng* 21, leap third month / 1841) to [[KR3em029]] *Yān-hóu mài-zhèng tōng-lùn*; his signature reads 吳興姚晏芳林北堂書, identifying his *zì* / studio-name as 芳林 *Fāng-lín* and his *táng-míng* as 北堂. His preface uses the strategic-geography metaphor (the throat as Sword-Pass 劍閣, or as the iron-chain river-barrier broken by Wáng Jùn 王浚) to frame Qīng laryngology and singles out [[許槤]]'s editorial recovery of the *Tōng-lùn* as a major contribution to public health.
