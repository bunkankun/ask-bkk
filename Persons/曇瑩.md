# Tányíng 曇瑩

Sobriquet *Luóyuè* 蘿月 (Vine-Moon). Native of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). Active in the early Southern Sòng (Jiànyán era, 1127–1130). Birth and death years not securely recorded.

A Sòng Buddhist monk (*shì*) celebrated for his expertise in *Yìjīng*-studies — Hóng Mài's 洪邁 *Róngzhāi suíbǐ* records him as the *Yìsēng* (Yì-monk) of the period. He extended his *Yì*-studies into *xīngmìng* (astrological-fate) divination through the *Luòlùzǐ fù* 珞琭子賦 commentary tradition — composing the *Luòlùzǐ fù zhù* 珞琭子賦注 ([[KR3g0036]]) in the Jiànyán *dīngwèi* year (1127) that became one of the principal four-house commentaries on the foundational *Luòlùzǐ* text. The Sìkù-recension's specific commentary on the *Luòlùzǐ* is by Tányíng (with embedded references to Wáng Tíngguāng 王廷光 and Lǐ Tóng 李同's earlier commentaries).
