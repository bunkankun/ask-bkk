# Tán-yíng 曇瑩

Sobriquet *Luó-yuè* 蘿月 (Vine-Moon). Native of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). Active in the early Southern Sòng (Jiàn-yán era, 1127–1130). Birth and death years not securely recorded.

A Sòng Buddhist monk (*shì*) celebrated for his expertise in *Yìjīng*-studies — Hóng Mài's 洪邁 *Róng-zhāi suí-bǐ* records him as the *Yì-sēng* (Yì-monk) of the period. He extended his *Yì*-studies into *xīng-mìng* (astrological-fate) divination through the *Luò-lù-zǐ fù* 珞琭子賦 commentary tradition — composing the *Luò-lù-zǐ fù zhù* 珞琭子賦注 ([[KR3g0036]]) in the Jiàn-yán *dīng-wèi* year (1127) that became one of the principal four-house commentaries on the foundational *Luò-lù-zǐ* text. The Sìkù-recension's specific commentary on the *Luò-lù-zǐ* is by Tán-yíng (with embedded references to Wáng Tíng-guāng 王廷光 and Lǐ Tóng 李同's earlier commentaries).
