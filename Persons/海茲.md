Hǎi-cí 海茲, *zì* **Xuě-lín** 雪林 ("Snow-Forest"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-wǒ Jiàn** [[鑑|Lín-wǒ Jiàn]] (1612–1679); held the office of *zhī-cáng* 知藏 (canon-keeper) at the master's monastery. Native of **Shuò-fāng** 朔方 (= Níng-xià region). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān-lù*) with co-disciple [[海錱]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0541]] 《林我禪師語錄》 4 juan, J38 B422, printed 1692.
