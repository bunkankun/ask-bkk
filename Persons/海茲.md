Hǎicí 海茲, *zì* **Xuělín** 雪林 ("Snow-Forest"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Línwǒ Jiàn** [[鑑|Línwǒ Jiàn]] (1612–1679); held the office of *zhīcáng* 知藏 (canon-keeper) at the master's monastery. Native of **Shuòfāng** 朔方 (= Níngxià region). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biānlù*) with co-disciple [[海錱]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0541]] 《林我禪師語錄》 4 juan, J38 B422, printed 1692.
