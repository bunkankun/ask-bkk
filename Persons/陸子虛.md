Lù Zǐyú 陸子虛 (also written 陸子虡 in the WYG; the SBCK *Wèinán wénjí* attributes its 1220 postface to Lù Zǐyù 陸子遹), son of Lù Yóu [[陸游]]. Held office to *Cháoqǐng dàfū* and *Zhī Jiāngzhōu jūnshì*; was *Zhī Liàyáng-xiàn* in *Jiāndìng* 13 (1220) when he edited and printed his father's collected works. Edited [[KR4d0267]] *Jiànnán shīgǎo* (1220 Jiǔjiāng cut) and [[KR4d0268]] *Wèinán wénjí* (1220 Liàyáng cut). The Sìkù editors note — though sources differ on whether 子虛, 子虡, and 子遹 are the same person or different sons of Lù Yóu — they were all involved in compiling their father's posthumous works.
