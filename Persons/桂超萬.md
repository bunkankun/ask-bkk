**Guì Chāowàn** 桂超萬 (1784–1863) — mid-Qīng official; *jǔrén* of *Jiāqìng* 13 / 1808 (in the same class as [[林佩琴]] Lín Pèiqín). Successive *zhīfǔ* of various Jiāng-Zhè prefectures; serving as *zhī Sūzhōu fǔ shì* 知蘇州府事 (prefect of Sūzhōu) in 1847, he supplied the publication preface to Lín Pèiqín's posthumous *Lèizhèng zhìcái* 類證治裁 ([[KR3er061]]) at the request of Lín's son Lín Zhīběn.
