**Guì Chāo-wàn** 桂超萬 (1784–1863) — mid-Qīng official; *jǔ-rén* of *Jiā-qìng* 13 / 1808 (in the same class as [[林佩琴]] Lín Pèi-qín). Successive *zhī-fǔ* of various Jiāng-Zhè prefectures; serving as *zhī Sū-zhōu fǔ shì* 知蘇州府事 (prefect of Sū-zhōu) in 1847, he supplied the publication preface to Lín Pèi-qín's posthumous *Lèi-zhèng zhì-cái* 類證治裁 ([[KR3er061]]) at the request of Lín's son Lín Zhī-běn.
