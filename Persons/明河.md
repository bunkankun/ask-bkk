Mínghé 明河 (1588–1640), late-Míng monk-historian; *zì* Tài-rú 汰如, *hào* Gāo-sōng dàozhě 高松道者. Native of **Tōng-zhōu** 通州 (modern Nán-tōng, Jiāngsū), lay surname Chén 陳. Dharma-heir of **Yī-yǔ Tōng-rùn** 一雨通潤 (1565–1624), himself a transmission-disciple of **Xuě-làng Hóng-ēn** 雪浪洪恩 (the major late-Míng *jiǎng-jīng* exegete) — i.e., Mínghé belongs to the Línjì 楊岐 sub-lineage of the **Xuě-làng** 雪浪 reformed *jiǎng-jīng* school.

He was first abbot of **Huá-shān-sì** 華山寺 in Wú-yì 吳邑 (Sūzhōu) and subsequently of the imperial **Dà-bào'ēn-sì** 大報恩寺 in Jiāng-níng (Nán-jīng) — one of the two greatest Míng establishments. His lectures regularly drew over ten thousand listeners. Over the course of thirty years he assembled biographies of c. 400 monks of the Sòng through the Míng — across all schools, "without distinguishing sects" 不分宗派 — into the **《補續高僧傳》** *Bǔ-xù gāosēng zhuàn* in 26 juan ([[KR6r0063]], X77 no. 1524), the principal supplement to [[KR6r0054]] and [[KR6r0055]] for the post-Sòng period. He also produced commentaries on the *Lotus*, *Śūraṃgama*, and *Yuán-jué jīng*.

Mínghé died at Dà-bào'ēn-sì in **Chóng-zhēn** 崇禎 13 (1640) at the age of 53. The *Bǔ-xù gāosēng zhuàn* was posthumously printed by his disciples, with the colophon-postface (跋) added by his disciple **Zì-jiōng** 自扃 / Dào-kāi 道開. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000658.
