**Xú Shīcēng** 徐師曾 (*zì* Bólǔ 伯魯, *hào* Lǔshān 魯山, 1517–1580, 明), Wújiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu) literatus-philologist; *jìnshì* of Jiājìng 32 (1553); served as Drafter (*zhōngshū*) in the Hànlínyuàn and as a censor. Best known for his *Wéntǐ míngbiàn* 文體明辨 (1573), a comprehensive late-Míng treatise on literary genres that remains the canonical reference on Chinese literary classification, and for his *Lǐjì jízhù* 禮記集註. Edited and printed the late-Míng *Jīngluò quánshū* 經絡全書 ([[KR3ee011]], 1576), expanding the manuscript left by his deceased friend Shěn Zǐlù 沈子祿.
