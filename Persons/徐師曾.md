**Xú Shīcēng** 徐師曾 (*zì* Bólǔ 伯魯, *hào* Lǔshān 魯山, 1517–1580, 明), Wú-jiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu) literatus-philologist; *jìnshì* of Jiā-jìng 32 (1553); served as Drafter (*zhōng-shū*) in the Hàn-lín-yuàn and as a censor. Best known for his *Wén-tǐ míng-biàn* 文體明辨 (1573), a comprehensive late-Míng treatise on literary genres that remains the canonical reference on Chinese literary classification, and for his *Lǐjì jí-zhù* 禮記集註. Edited and printed the late-Míng *Jīngluò quánshū* 經絡全書 ([[KR3ee011]], 1576), expanding the manuscript left by his deceased friend Shěn Zǐlù 沈子祿.
