# Jué-cǐ 覺此

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán *cān-xué* 參學 disciple of [[惟一]] Huán-xī Wéi-yī (1202–1281); sole named editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0321]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A001943).
