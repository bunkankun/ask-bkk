Hé Táng 何瑭 (1474–1543), *zì* **Cuìfū** 粹夫, *hào* **Bǎizhāi** 柏齋 (or 栢齋), *shì* **Wéndìng** 文定, of **Wǔzhì** 武陟 (Huáiqìngfǔ, Hénán). Hóngzhì 15 / *rénxū* (1502) *jìnshì*; rose to *Nánjīng yòu dūyùshǐ*. *Míngshǐ* main biography. **Dǔxíng lìzhì** (substantial-practice, encouraging-resolve); his learning takes *gézhì* (investigate-things, extend-knowing) as primary — explicitly against **Zhàn Ruòshuǐ** ([[湛若水]])'s *cúnxīn* (preserving-heart) emphasis and against **Wáng Yángmíng**'s *liángzhī*. Author of *Bǎizhāi sānshū* (Bǎizhāi Three Books): *Yīnyáng* (Yin-Yang Discussion), *Yuèlǜ* (Pitches-and-Measures), *Rúxué guǎnjiàn* (Confucian-Learning Tube-View) — *Yáojiāng* anti-Yáng-míng systematic critique, printed separately by **Zhào Wáng** 趙王. His literary collection *Bǎizhāi jí* in 11 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0160]]) was edited and cut in Jiājìng *jǐyǒu* (1549) by **Zhèng Wáng** 鄭王. CBDB id 34646, 1474–1543.
