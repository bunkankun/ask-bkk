Hé Táng 何瑭 (1474–1543), *zì* **Cuì-fū** 粹夫, *hào* **Bǎi-zhāi** 柏齋 (or 栢齋), *shì* **Wén-dìng** 文定, of **Wǔ-zhì** 武陟 (Huái-qìng-fǔ, Hé-nán). Hóng-zhì 15 / *rén-xū* (1502) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Nán-jīng yòu dū-yù-shǐ*. *Míng-shǐ* main biography. **Dǔ-xíng lì-zhì** (substantial-practice, encouraging-resolve); his learning takes *gé-zhì* (investigate-things, extend-knowing) as primary — explicitly against **Zhàn Ruò-shuǐ** ([[湛若水]])'s *cún-xīn* (preserving-heart) emphasis and against **Wáng Yáng-míng**'s *liáng-zhī*. Author of *Bǎi-zhāi sān-shū* (Bǎi-zhāi Three Books): *Yīn-yáng* (Yin-Yang Discussion), *Yuè-lǜ* (Pitches-and-Measures), *Rú-xué guǎn-jiàn* (Confucian-Learning Tube-View) — *Yáo-jiāng* anti-Yáng-míng systematic critique, printed separately by **Zhào Wáng** 趙王. His literary collection *Bǎi-zhāi jí* in 11 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0160]]) was edited and cut in Jiā-jìng *jǐ-yǒu* (1549) by **Zhèng Wáng** 鄭王. CBDB id 34646, 1474–1543.
