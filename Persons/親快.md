Shinkai 親快 (also read *Chinkai*; 1205–1271) was a Japanese Shingon master of the mid-Kamakura period, senior disciple of **Kenjin** 憲深 (1192–1263) in the **Ninniku-yama** 忍辱山 transmission line of the Ono-ryū. His principal scholarly contribution is the five-fascicle [[KR6t0204]] *Xìng-xīn chāo* (*Kōshinshō*), recorded from Kenjin's oral teachings, the central document of the Ninniku-yama Ono-ryū. He rose to senior monastic office in the Ono-ryū hierarchy and was instrumental in the diffusion of the Ninniku-yama transmission into the Sanbō-in branch of mid-Kamakura Shingon.

Surviving work in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0204]] *Kōshinshō* (5 fasc., recorded from Kenjin).
