# Shī Pèi 施沛

Late-Míng lay Chán compiler; principal *huìjí* 彙集 editor of the *Xùdēng cúngǎo* 續燈存稿 ([[KR6q0030]]) under Tōngwèn 通問's final editorial revision. A literatus with substantial Chán engagement; exact lifedates and native place not transmitted in Chán sources.
