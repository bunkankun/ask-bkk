# Guīfēng Yuánzhōu 圭峰元周

Late-Míng Buddhist monk, associated with Xuānchéng 宣城 in Nán-Zhílì (modern Ānhuī). Compiler of the **Kǔhǎi fúnáng** 苦海浮囊 ("Floating-bag-on-the-sea-of-suffering"), a *daily-use* Vinaya verse anthology cited as a forerunner of [[性祇]] Xìng Qí's *Píní rìyòng lù* ([[KR6k0258]], X1114). Apparently distinct from the much earlier Tang Huáyán-Chán master *Guīfēng Zōngmì* 圭峰宗密 (780–841), who shares the *Guīfēng* place-style. No independent biographical information preserved.
