Yuán-period 元 scholar and official; grandson of the great Neo-Confucian classicist Wú Chéng [[吳澄]] (1249–1333). *Zì* **Bó-shàng** 伯尚. Native of Lè-ān 樂安 in Fǔ-zhōu 撫州 (modern Jiāng-xī).

**Career.** Inherited his grandfather's classical learning. Recommended (*jiàn*) to the Yuán court and appointed *Guó-zǐ zhù-jiào* 國子助教, later *Wén-lín láng* 文林郎, *Hàn-lín dài-zhì* 翰林待制, and eventually *Lǐ-bù láng-zhōng* 禮部郎中. During the Zhèng-tǒng / Zhì-zhèng period of Yuán imperial decline (late 1340s–1350s), Wú Dāng was dispatched as *Jiāng-xī xíng-shěng cān-zhèng* 江西行省參政 to suppress the Red Turban risings in Jiāng-xī; he resisted Chén Yǒu-liàng 陳友諒 and was eventually captured, refused to surrender, and died in confinement around 1361. *Yuán-shǐ* j. 187 gives the standard biography (appended to his grandfather's biography in *juàn* 171 and supplemented here).

**Editorial role.** Editor of his grandfather Wú Chéng's *Wú Wén-zhèng jí* [[KR4d0446]] in 100 *juàn*; the surviving Yǒng-lè 1406 re-cut by Wú Guàn 吳爟 (Wú Chéng's fifth-generation descendant) preserves Wú Dāng's editorial arrangement.
