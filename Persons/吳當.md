Yuán-period 元 scholar and official; grandson of the great Neo-Confucian classicist Wú Chéng [[吳澄]] (1249–1333). *Zì* **Bóshàng** 伯尚. Native of Lèān 樂安 in Fǔzhōu 撫州 (modern Jiāngxī).

**Career.** Inherited his grandfather's classical learning. Recommended (*jiàn*) to the Yuán court and appointed *Guózǐ zhùjiào* 國子助教, later *Wénlín láng* 文林郎, *Hànlín dàizhì* 翰林待制, and eventually *Lǐbù lángzhōng* 禮部郎中. During the Zhèngtǒng / Zhìzhèng period of Yuán imperial decline (late 1340s–1350s), Wú Dāng was dispatched as *Jiāngxī xíngshěng cānzhèng* 江西行省參政 to suppress the Red Turban risings in Jiāngxī; he resisted Chén Yǒuliàng 陳友諒 and was eventually captured, refused to surrender, and died in confinement around 1361. *Yuánshǐ* j. 187 gives the standard biography (appended to his grandfather's biography in *juàn* 171 and supplemented here).

**Editorial role.** Editor of his grandfather Wú Chéng's *Wú Wénzhèng jí* [[KR4d0446]] in 100 *juàn*; the surviving Yǒnglè 1406 re-cut by Wú Guàn 吳爟 (Wú Chéng's fifth-generation descendant) preserves Wú Dāng's editorial arrangement.
