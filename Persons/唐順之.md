Táng Shùnzhī 唐順之 (1507–1560), *zì* Yīngdé 應德 / Yìxiū 義修, *hào* Jīngchuān 荊川, posthumously *Wénxiāng* 文襄, was a major Mid-Míng official, classicist, and military strategist. *Huìshì* first place in *Jiājìng wùzǐ* (1528). Famous for organising defence against the *wō kòu* 倭寇 (Japanese pirates) in the Jiājìng era. Polymath; wrote across military, classical, literary, and astronomical fields. Major works: the *Wǔ biān* 武編 ([[KR3b0016]], his comprehensive military encyclopedia in 10 juan); *Jīngchuān bài biān* 荊川稗編 (a major *biji* compendium); the *Liù biān* 六編 (history-classical compendium). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 205.
