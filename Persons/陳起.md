**Chén Qǐ** 陳起 (*fl.* Qīng dynasty), gynecological physician. Author of the *Fùkē mìshū* 婦科秘書 ([[KR3ei012]]), a one-*juǎn* gynecology manual transmitted in manuscript and preserved in the *Hǎiwài huíguī Zhōngyī shànběn gǔjí cóngshū* 海外回歸中醫善本古籍叢書 (hxwd). No further biographical information located in standard reference works; CBDB has multiple homonyms but none confidently identifiable with this physician.
