**Hé Róng** 何榮 (*hào* **Xìngyuán** 杏園) of Gǔyú 古虞 (Shàngyú, Zhèjiāng) — Qīng physician and Húnán private secretary; prefacer (1809, Jiāqìng *jǐsì*) of [[陳笏庵]] Chén Hùān's *Tāichǎn mìshū* ([[KR3ei054]]). Received the manuscript from his teacher [[陳勿庵]] Chén Wùān. First cut wood-blocks at Yuèyángjùn 岳陽郡 in 1795 (Qiánlóng *yǐmǎo*) while serving in Húnán; in 1809 he re-cut blocks at Ānjiāng 皖江 for wider charitable circulation. His preface frames the work as superseding the *Dáshēng biān* ([[KR3ei055]]) and pairs the Shānyīn [[錢氏]] Qián family with the Bamboo-Grove Monastery (Xiāoshān) monks as the two great Zhèjiāng obstetrical lineages. Not in CBDB.
