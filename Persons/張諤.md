Republican-era physician of Hǎiyú 海虞 (Chángshú 常熟, Jiāngsū), studio name 壽石居 *Shòushí jū*, courtesy name 汝偉 *Rǔwěi*. Pupil of the late-Qīng physician Táng Jūnliáng 唐均良. In *Mínguó* 4 / 1915 (*yǐmǎo* 乙卯) re-edited the 1903 Wáng Jǐnghuá redaction of *Chóngdìng nángmì hóushū* ([[KR3em030]]), adding throughout the text his own annotations marked 【諤按】 — drawing on the *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*, [[王孟英]]'s *Qīnglóng báihǔ tāng*, the *Língshū* / *Sùwèn*, and contemporary Western-medicine practice (early-twentieth-century diphtheria antitoxin) — together with an appendix of 白喉 prescriptions and case-comments by his teacher Táng Jūnliáng. No CBDB entry; lifedates not recoverable from the work alone.
