Republican-era physician of Hǎi-yú 海虞 (Cháng-shú 常熟, Jiāngsū), studio name 壽石居 *Shòu-shí jū*, courtesy name 汝偉 *Rǔ-wěi*. Pupil of the late-Qīng physician Táng Jūn-liáng 唐均良. In *Mín-guó* 4 / 1915 (*yǐ-mǎo* 乙卯) re-edited the 1903 Wáng Jǐng-huá redaction of *Chóng-dìng náng-mì hóu-shū* ([[KR3em030]]), adding throughout the text his own annotations marked 【諤按】 — drawing on the *Wēn-bìng tiáo-biàn*, [[王孟英]]'s *Qīng-lóng bái-hǔ tāng*, the *Líng-shū* / *Sù-wèn*, and contemporary Western-medicine practice (early-twentieth-century diphtheria antitoxin) — together with an appendix of 白喉 prescriptions and case-comments by his teacher Táng Jūn-liáng. No CBDB entry; lifedates not recoverable from the work alone.
