# Huìzhēn 慧真

Western Xià (西夏 / *Dà-xià*) Buddhist monk; co-compiler — with **Zhì-guǎng** 智廣 [[智廣]] (the Western Xià namesake of the Tang *Xī-tán zì-jì* author) — of the **Mì-zhòu yuán-yīn wǎng-shēng jí** 密呪圓因往生集 ([[KR6j0742]], T46 no. 1956), a Western Xià dhāraṇī-compilation prefaced and dated by the Xià Counselor **Hè Zōng-shòu** 賀宗壽 to **Tiān-qìng 7 = 1200**. The work integrates Tibetan-tradition Vajrayāna ritual elements with Chinese Esoteric and Pure Land soteriology, characteristic of Western Xià Buddhist culture's distinctive Tibetan-Chinese synthesis. Lifedates and broader biographical particulars unrecorded; the compilation is his only attested work. Per DILA A001841.
