Cáo Xiùjūn 曹繡君 was a Republican-era compiler responsible for the anthology *Gǔjīn Qínghǎi* 古今情海 ([[KR4k0302]]), a thematic collection of love and romance anecdotes drawn from Chinese history. No biographical details have been located; the name may be a pen name. Active ca. 1910s–1930s.
