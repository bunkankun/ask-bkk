**Zhū Yòuyuán** 朱祐杬 (明). Prince of Xīngxiàn 興獻王 (1476–1519), father of the Jiājìng Emperor; his princely household employed [[周文采]] (author of [[KR3ek006]] *Wàikē jíyàn fāng*) as Vice-Medical-Officer (*liángyī fù* 良醫副).
