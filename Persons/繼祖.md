# Jì-zǔ 繼祖

Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*sì-fǎ* 嗣法) of [[守忠]] Tán-fāng Shǒu-zhōng (1275–1348); abbot of Chángzhōu-lù Shàn-quán chán-sì 常州路善權禪寺, under which capacity he headed the compilation of his master's two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0343]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
