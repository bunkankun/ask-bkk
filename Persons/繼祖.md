# Jìzǔ 繼祖

Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*sìfǎ* 嗣法) of [[守忠]] Tánfāng Shǒuzhōng (1275–1348); abbot of Chángzhōulù Shànquán chánsì 常州路善權禪寺, under which capacity he headed the compilation of his master's two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0343]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
