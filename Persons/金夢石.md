**Jīn Mèngshí** 金夢石, late-Míng physician serving as house physician to the Dìngxī Marquis 定西侯; teacher of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jǐngyuè, as recorded in [[黃宗羲]] Huáng Zōngxī's biographical essay on Zhāng appended to [[KR3eq016]] *Zhìyí lù*.
