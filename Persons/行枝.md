Southern-Sòng *Nán-shān* Vinaya monk; lifedates not preserved (DILA A000410). Disciple (*mén-rén* 門人) of *Tiě-wēng Shǒu-yī* 鐵翁守一 ([[守一]]) at *Tāi-zhōu Rì-shān* 台州日山. Editor of his master's *Zhōng-nán jiā-yè* 終南家業 ([[KR6k0219]], X1109).
