Mid-Qīng (Yōng-zhèng / early Qián-lóng-era) Buddhist layman, sobriquet **Shèng-xián jū-shì** 剩閒居士 ("Layman of Leftover Leisure"). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiled the two-juan *Jīn-gāng jīng zhèng-jiě* 金剛經正解 ([[KR6c0086]], X25 no. 498), with editorial collation by the layman **Hù Zhèng-zhì** 扈正智 (sobriquet **Lián-fǎng jū-shì** 蓮舫居士). The work is a verse-by-verse annotated commentary in the lay-Buddhist devotional register, organized by the **Liáng Zhāo-míng** 32-section division.
