Yuè Jùn 岳濬 (1704–1753), *zì* Xuězhōu 雪洲, native of Chéngdū 成都 (Sìchuān), great-great-grandson of the Sòng patriot Yuè Fēi 岳飛 by direct line of descent. *Yīn* (hereditary) recommendation rather than *jìnshì*. His career: Shāndōng governor (Yōngzhèng 6 = 1728 – Yōngzhèng 13 = 1735); briefly governor of Shǎnxī. As Shāndōng governor he supervised the *Shāndōng tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0048]]) from its launch in 1729 through to its near-completion in 1735 — the *jìnbiǎo* was actually submitted by his successor Fǎ Mǐn 法敏. His own preface to the work is dated Qiánlóng 1/3 (1736/4).
