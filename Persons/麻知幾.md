**Má Zhī-jǐ** 麻知幾 (*fl.* early 13th c., Jīn / early Yuán) — principal disciple and editorial compiler of [[張從正]] Zhāng Cóng-zhèng (Dài-rén 戴人, 1156–1228). Má collected Zhāng's writings, case-records, and oral teachings into the fifteen-*juǎn* *Rú-mén shì-qīn* 儒門事親 ([[KR3er064]]), conventionally dated to *Zhèng-dà* 5 / 1228. The work would not have come down to us without Má's editorial labour; Má himself is otherwise unattested. Not in CBDB.
