# Zōng-qīng 宗清

Southern-Sòng Chán *mén-rén shì-zhě* 門人侍者 of [[了慧]] Xī-yán Liǎo-huì (1198–1262); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0324]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
