# Zōngqīng 宗清

Southern-Sòng Chán *ménrén shìzhě* 門人侍者 of [[了慧]] Xīyán Liǎohuì (1198–1262); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0324]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
