Qī Jì-guāng 戚繼光 (1528–1587), *zì* Yuánjìng 元敬, *hào* Nántáng 南塘, posthumously *Wǔyì* 武毅, was the most celebrated Late-Míng military commander, famed for his suppression of the *wō kòu* on the Zhè-Mǐn coast (1559–1567) and his subsequent reform of the northern frontier defences (1568–1582). Native of Pēng-lái 蓬萊 (Shān-dōng). His military reforms — based on training disciplined civilian-recruited *qì jiā jūn* 戚家軍 ("Qī family troops") with new tactical formations and Western-influenced firearms — established the model for the late-Míng Chinese army. Major works in the SKQS: *Jì xiào xīn shū* 紀效新書 (KR3b0019, the foundational training manual based on Zhè-Mǐn coastal-defence experience, in 18 juan); *Liàn bīng shí jì* 練兵實紀 (KR3b0020, the parallel northern-frontier training manual). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 212.
