Jiǎng Wéi-jūn 蔣維鈞 (early eighteenth century, lifedates not securely known). The editor who assembled the 58-juan *Yì mén dú shū jì* 義門讀書記 ([[KR3j0076]]) from Hé Zhuō's 何焯 reading marginalia and textual-collation notes — building on the earlier 6-juan compilation by Hé's nephew Hé Táng 何堂. The biographical record is thin; he is known almost entirely through his role as Hé Zhuō's literary executor. Not in CBDB.
