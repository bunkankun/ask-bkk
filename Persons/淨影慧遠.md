# 淨影慧遠

Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn 淨影慧遠 (523–592), the great *Dìlùn* 地論-school master and one of the most important Buddhist scholars of the Suí imperial period. Resident at the imperial *Jìngyǐngsì* 淨影寺 ("Pure Reflection Monastery") in Cháng'ān, whence his epithet. Distinct from the earlier [[慧遠]] Lúshān Huìyuǎn (334–416). Composed extensive doctrinal works including the *Dàchéng yìzhāng* 大乘義章 (T1851).
