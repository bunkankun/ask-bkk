# 胡宣明 Hú Xuānmíng

Republican-period Chinese public-health physician, US-trained (returned to China in 1917). Co-founder with [[聶雲臺]] Niè Yúntái of the *Zhōngguó wèishēng huì* 中國衛生會 in 1923; author of the preface to Niè's *Shānghán jiě dú liáo fǎ* ([[KR3ef068]]).
