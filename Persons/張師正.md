Northern-Sòng official and *zhì-guài* compiler, *zì* Bù-yí 不疑, of *Xiāng-guó* 襄國 (modern Xíng-zhōu 邢州 / Xíng-tái 邢台, Héběi) — whence he is conventionally referred to in colophons as *Xiāng-guó Zhāng-mǒu*. Active under *Rén-zōng* and *Shén-zōng*; held a series of *láng-zhōng* / *yuán-wài-láng* postings in the northern provinces (Héběi and Shǎn-xī especially), never advancing into the senior ranks. Cháo Gōng-wǔ's *Jùn-zhāi dú-shū zhì* says he had served as *Cì-shǐ* of several prefectures and "having repeatedly been an official without advancement, he turned his frustration into *xiǎo-shuō*" (累任不遷以發其鬱悒). Author of [[KR3l0117]] *Kuò-yì zhì* 括異志 in 10 *juǎn* (completed in *Xī-níng* / *Yuán-fēng*, c. 1078–1085); also conventionally credited with the *Juàn-yóu zá-lù* 倦遊雜錄 (or *Juàn-yóu lù*) — a similar *bǐjì* collection mostly preserved in *Tài-píng guǎng-jì* and *Lèi-shuō* quotations — and with the now-lost *Yì-shuō* 譯說 (or *Bǔ Zhōng-tán* 補倩談). His lifedates are not known; CBDB id 47206 is used as the conventional anchor (there is a homonym at 47207). The pseudonym *Xīn Sǒu* 心叟, sometimes attached to *Juàn-yóu zá-lù*, has been disputed in modern scholarship; the *Juàn-yóu* attribution is increasingly accepted as Zhāng's.
