Northern-Sòng official and *zhìguài* compiler, *zì* Bùyí 不疑, of *Xiāngguó* 襄國 (modern Xíngzhōu 邢州 / Xíngtái 邢台, Héběi) — whence he is conventionally referred to in colophons as *Xiāngguó Zhāngmǒu*. Active under *Rénzōng* and *Shénzōng*; held a series of *lángzhōng* / *yuánwàiláng* postings in the northern provinces (Héběi and Shǎnxī especially), never advancing into the senior ranks. Cháo Gōngwǔ's *Jùnzhāi dúshū zhì* says he had served as *Cìshǐ* of several prefectures and "having repeatedly been an official without advancement, he turned his frustration into *xiǎoshuō*" (累任不遷以發其鬱悒). Author of [[KR3l0117]] *Kuòyì zhì* 括異志 in 10 *juǎn* (completed in *Xīníng* / *Yuánfēng*, c. 1078–1085); also conventionally credited with the *Juànyóu zálù* 倦遊雜錄 (or *Juànyóu lù*) — a similar *bǐjì* collection mostly preserved in *Tàipíng guǎngjì* and *Lèishuō* quotations — and with the now-lost *Yìshuō* 譯說 (or *Bǔ Zhōngtán* 補倩談). His lifedates are not known; CBDB id 47206 is used as the conventional anchor (there is a homonym at 47207). The pseudonym *Xīn Sǒu* 心叟, sometimes attached to *Juànyóu zálù*, has been disputed in modern scholarship; the *Juànyóu* attribution is increasingly accepted as Zhāng's.
