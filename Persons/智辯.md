# Zhìbiàn 智辯

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán dharma-heir (*sìfǎ* 嗣法) of [[文康]] Mùān Wénkāng; co-editor of the one-juan *Mùān Wénkāng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0348]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with earlier Chán figures of the same name (Táng-era Zhìbiàn, DILA A012859; another Zhìbiàn at A004808).
