# Zhì-biàn 智辯

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán dharma-heir (*sì-fǎ* 嗣法) of [[文康]] Mù-ān Wén-kāng; co-editor of the one-juan *Mù-ān Wén-kāng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0348]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with earlier Chán figures of the same name (Táng-era Zhì-biàn, DILA A012859; another Zhì-biàn at A004808).
