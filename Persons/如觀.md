Late-Míng (Chóng-zhēn-era) Buddhist monk, sobriquet **Yùn-xū** 蘊虗 ("Aggregate-Empty"); abbot of the **Chóng-fú Ān** 崇福菴 at **Wǔ-yuán** 武原 (modern Hǎi-yán 海鹽 in Jiā-xīng, Zhèjiāng). Lifedates unrecorded; only certain literary act is the *Jīn-gāng jīng bǐ-jì* 金剛經筆記 ([[KR6c0066]], X25 no. 478), self-prefaced **Chóng-zhēn 10 = 1637**. The preface to that work, by an admiring lay correspondent, characterizes Rú-guān as 履踐真實，貌癯趣澹，道風肅然 ("treading the truth, gaunt of countenance and bland of taste, of austere dharmic bearing") and notes a circle of *fā-cóng* 白法琮公 in mutual admiration. Beyond this, no biographical record survives.
