Qián Lè-tiān 錢樂天, otherwise undocumented Qīng physician named in the meta of [[KR3eq079]] *Yī-xué chuán-xīn lù* 醫學傳心錄 as the transmitter who **obtained the secret manuscript** (得秘本) of Liú Yī-rén's *chuán-xīn* (heart-transmitted) didactic compendium. Not in CBDB. No standard biographical record located.
