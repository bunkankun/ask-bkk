# Jiāoguāng Zhēnjiàn 交光真鑑

A late-Míng (Wànlì-period) Buddhist commentator on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*; author of the influential *Lèngyán jīng zhèngmài shū* 楞嚴經正脈疏 ([[KR6j0703]]), which became the principal target of subsequent Tiāntái-school and Huá-yán-school refutations.
