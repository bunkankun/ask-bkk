Chén Niànzǔ 陳念祖 (*zì* Xiūyuán 修園, also *zì* Liángyǒu 良有, 1753–1823, CBDB 81917), native of Chánglè 長樂 (modern Fújiàn). He passed the *jǔrén* exam (Qiánlóng 57 = 1792) and served briefly as Hé-bǎo zhīxiàn 河北 magistrate before retiring to teach medicine at Fúzhōu.

Chén is the most prolific medical popularizer of the Qiánlóng-Jiāqìng era. His pedagogical output is enormous and his works served as the standard *zhōngyī* textbooks for two centuries. The principal works are:

1. *Yīxué shí zài yì* 醫學實在易 (1808) — accessible introduction to medicine.
2. *Yīxué cóng zhòng lù* 醫學從眾錄 — clinical reference.
3. *Shānghán lùn qiǎn zhù* 傷寒論淺注 — accessible *Shānghán lùn* commentary.
4. *Jīnguì yàoluè qiǎn zhù* 金匱要略淺注 — companion to (3).
5. *Yī yī ǒu cuì* 醫醫偶萃 — collected medical essays.
6. *Niǔ wàn lùn* / *Nǚ kē yào zhǐ* 女科要旨 — gynaecology.
7. *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng dú* 神農本草經讀 ([[KR3ec046]]) — pharmacological reading.

The collected *Xiūyuán quánshū* 修園全書 served as the standard physician textbook for two centuries.

CBDB has two Chén Niànzǔ records: 81917 with lifedates 1753–1823 (the relevant entry) and 81918 with 1852–1890 (a different, later figure).

Principal modern edition: Lǐ Lǐ 李力, *Chén Xiūyuán yīxué quánshū* 陳修園醫學全書 (Renmin weisheng, 2002).
