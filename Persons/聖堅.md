Shèngjiān 聖堅 (\*Āryasthira, "Noble-Firm"; lifedates unknown), Buddhist translator active in the Western Qín 西秦 (385–431, in modern Gānsù) and adjacent Hè-xī 河西 polities, *fl.* late-fourth / early-fifth century. According to the *Lìdài sānbǎo jì* 歷代三寶紀 and the *Kāi-yuán shìjiào lù*, Shèngjiān worked at the Western-Qín capital Yǒngshì-chéng 苑川 / 罕井 by imperial commission of the Qǐ-fú 乞伏 ruling house, producing some 14 to 17 translations including *[[KR6b0023|Tàizǐ Sūdánà jīng 太子須大拏經 (T171)]]*, *[[KR6b0027|Shǎn-zǐ jīng 睒子經 (T175a)]]*, and the *Luó-mō qié jīng* 羅摩伽經 — a partial early Chinese rendering of the *Gaṇḍavyūha* / "Searching for Maitreya" section of the *Avataṃsaka*. Different catalogs variously assign him to Western Qín, Western Jìn, or "Qín" generically; modern scholarship (Tang Yongtong; Mizuno) accepts the Western-Qín register-attribution. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A002174.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6b0023]] *Tàizǐ Sūdánà jīng*, [[KR6b0027]] *Shǎnzǐ jīng* (T175a), [[KR6b0028]] *Fóshuō Shǎnzǐ jīng* (T175b), [[KR6b0029]] *Fóshuō Shǎnzǐ jīng* (T175c), and others.
