**Huáiyuǎn** 懷遠 — Northern-Sòng Buddhist monk, disciple of the Sòng **Huá-yán-Chán** master **Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán** 長水子璿 ([[子璿]], 965 – 1038). Lifedates unrecorded.

His sole transmitted work is the **Lèngyán-jīng yì-shū shìyào chāo** 楞嚴經義疏釋要鈔 in 6 fascicles ([[KR6j0675]], X11 no. 267), a sub-commentary that distills and elucidates the "essential meanings" (*shìyào* 釋要) of his teacher's *Yì-shū* ([[KR6j0673]], the Sòng-Huá-yán Lèngyán commentary). The preface explains the work's genesis: Zǐ-xuán's *Yì-shū* had circulated for some decades but no sub-commentary (*chāo* 鈔) had yet been composed; Huáiyuǎn's fellow-disciples pressed him to provide one for pedagogical use ("from the master, [the meaning] is illuminated; from us, it is yet obscure" 於師雖明於某猶昧). The preface is dated **Jiā-yòu *xīn-chǒu* (the 6th cyclical year of the Jiā-yòu era) = the 15th day of the 10th month, 1061 CE** (時鉅宋四葉嘉祐辛丑十月望日序). The work is one of the foundational documents of the Northern-Sòng Huá-yán-school reading of the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001902; *Lèngyán-jīng yì-shū shìyào chāo* fasc. 1 self-preface (X11n0267).
