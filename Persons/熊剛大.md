Xióng Gāngdà 熊剛大 (Sòng), native of Jiàn-yáng 建陽 (Fú-jiàn). Studied under Cài Yuān 蔡淵 and Huáng Gàn 黃榦 (both senior Zhū-Xī disciples); calls himself *Juéxuān-mén-rén* 覺軒門人 (in reference to Cài Yuān's *hào* Juéxuān). *Jìnshì* of Jiā-dìng era; held charge of the *Jiàn-ān shū-yuàn* 建安書院 academy where Zhū Xī and other late-Sòng Confucian sages were ritually venerated (*cóng sì*). His commentary references Lǐzōng's recent imperial gift of the *Bái-lù-dòng guī* 白鹿洞規 to Nán-kāng 南康, dating it to the Lǐzōng reign (1224–1264). His main extant work is the annotation of Xióng Jié's *Xìng-lǐ qún-shū jù-jiě* 性理群書句解 (KR3a0067). CBDB id 11823, no dates.
