Xióng Gāngdà 熊剛大 (Sòng), native of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Fújiàn). Studied under Cài Yuān 蔡淵 and Huáng Gàn 黃榦 (both senior ZhūXī disciples); calls himself *Juéxuānménrén* 覺軒門人 (in reference to Cài Yuān's *hào* Juéxuān). *Jìnshì* of Jiādìng era; held charge of the *Jiànān shūyuàn* 建安書院 academy where Zhū Xī and other late-Sòng Confucian sages were ritually venerated (*cóng sì*). His commentary references Lǐzōng's recent imperial gift of the *Báilùdòng guī* 白鹿洞規 to Nánkāng 南康, dating it to the Lǐzōng reign (1224–1264). His main extant work is the annotation of Xióng Jié's *Xìnglǐ qúnshū jùjiě* 性理群書句解 ([[KR3a0067]]). CBDB id 11823, no dates.
