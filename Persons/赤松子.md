**Chì sōng zǐ** 赤松子 ("Master Red-Pine") is one of the earliest legendary *xiān* 仙 (immortals) of the Chinese tradition, traditionally said to be the rain-master (*yǔ shī* 雨師) of the Shén nóng / Yellow-Thearch era, who ingested *shuǐ yù* 水玉 (water-jade) and ascended in a fire. Cited in the *Liè xiān zhuàn* 列仙傳 (attributed to Liú Xiàng 劉向) and countless Daoist scriptures thereafter. Many Six-Dynasties and Táng Daoist texts are pseudepigraphically attributed to him, including [[KR5c0306]] *Shàng qīng jiǔ zhēn zhōng jīng nèi jué*.
