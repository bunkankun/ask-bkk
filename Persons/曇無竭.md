Tán-wú-jié 曇無竭 (Skt. *Dharmodgata*, "Dharma-Lifted-Up"; Chinese rendering 法勇 *Fǎ-yǒng*) — Liú-Sòng-period Buddhist translator and Indian / Central-Asian-region pilgrim. Per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145), he led a group of Chinese monks on a pilgrimage to India in the early Liú-Sòng period and brought back Buddhist texts. Author of *[[KR6f0097|Guān shì yīn pútísà shòu jì jīng 觀世音菩薩授記經]]* (T0371), the Mahāyāna sūtra on Avalokiteśvara's prophesied future Buddhahood. Birth and death dates not preserved.
