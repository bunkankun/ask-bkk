# Déchū 德初

Southern-Sòng Cáodòng Chán *shìzhě* 侍者 of [[清了]] Zhēnxiē Qīngliǎo (1088–1151); named alongside [[義初]] Yìchū in the editorial byline of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0359]]; *Jiéwài lù* 劫外錄). Lifedates unrecorded.
