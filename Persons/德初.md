# Dé-chū 德初

Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng Chán *shì-zhě* 侍者 of [[清了]] Zhēn-xiē Qīng-liǎo (1088–1151); named alongside [[義初]] Yì-chū in the editorial byline of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0359]]; *Jié-wài lù* 劫外錄). Lifedates unrecorded.
