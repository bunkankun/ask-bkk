Luó Jùn 羅濬 (fl. mid-12th century CE) was a Southern Song Buddhist layman from Chángxī County 長溪縣, Fúzhōu prefecture (modern Fújiàn Province), who compiled the *Púsà míng jīng* 菩薩名經 ([[KR6i0029]], C071n1665), a ten-fascicle systematic catalogue of bodhisattva names drawn from the entire Chinese Tripiṭaka. He assumed the Buddhist sobriquet *Cháng Bùqīng jūshì* 常不輕居士 ("Lay Buddhist of Constant Non-Contempt"), alluding to the self-designation of the bodhisattva Sadāparibhūta in the *Lotus Sūtra*, signaling Tiantai/Lotus devotional affiliation. His donation inscription for the *Púsà míng jīng* is dated Shàoxīng 紹興 29 (1159 CE), providing a secure terminus ante quem for the compilation.

CBDB records a 羅濬 with index year 1144 and *juren* degree in 1171 (Jiāngxī local gazetteer), plausibly the same person given the overlapping date range, though the gazetteer entry identifies him as from Jiāngxī while the *Púsà míng jīng* preface identifies Fúzhōu — possibly different individuals, or a migration/inconsistency in the sources.

Luó Jùn 羅濬 (fl. 1226–1227), of Lúlíng 廬陵 (Jiāngxī), Southern Sòng official whose recorded position was *Gànzhōu lùshì cānjūn* 贛州錄事參軍. He is the principal compiler of the *Bǎoqìng Sìmíng zhì* 寶慶四明志 [[KR2k0018]] in 21 juan, brought to completion in 150 days during the second year of the Bǎoqìng reign (1226–1227) at the invitation of Hú Jǔ 胡榘, the prefect of Qìngyuánfǔ (Níngbō) — Hú being a fellow native of Lúlíng. The work continues a project begun in the Qiándào by Zhāng Jīn 張津, partially advanced by the school officer Fāng Wànlǐ 方萬里, and completed by Luó. The *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* 文獻通考 erroneously gives the compiler's name as Luó Xuán 羅璿 — a transcription error. The CBDB candidate (id 20478, index year 1144, *juren* 1171, Jiāngxī Tōngzhì 22.9b) is chronologically plausible (a 1144 index year would make him 82 in 1226 — possible but old; alternatively id 685882, with index year 1193, would have him 33 in 1226 — also plausible). The identification of CBDB id 20478 as the gazetteer compiler is widely repeated but the dating is awkward; we therefore record CBDB id 20478 here as the leading candidate without claiming certainty. The gazetteer compiler Luó Jùn is securely distinct from the earlier Buddhist Luó Jùn entered above (Fúzhōu / Tiāntái-Lotus, fl. 1159).
