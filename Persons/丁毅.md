**Dīng Yì** 丁毅 (catalog attribution) / **Dīng Fèng** 丁鳳 (prefatory-signature form), *hào* Zhú-xī-zǐ 竹溪子, of Jiāng-pǔ 江浦 (Jiāng-sū), mid-Míng physician. The catalog meta of the *Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī gǔ-jí cóng-shū* records the author of the *Yī-fāng jí-yí* 醫方集宜 ([[KR3er021]]) as 丁毅; the prefatory signature in the hxwd transmission, however, reads 丁鳳 of Jiāng-pǔ, dated *Jiā-jìng jiǎ-yín* (1554). Both names are *míng* (formal personal name) forms; the difference is not a *zì* / *hào* relationship, and the discrepancy is most plausibly a scribal misreading or alternative-name tradition. CBDB has a Míng-period 丁毅 at id 344030 with *c_index_year* 1385, but this is two centuries before the 1554 preface and unlikely to be the same person. Dīng's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered.
