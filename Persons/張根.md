# Zhāng Gēn 張根

Style name *Zhīcháng* 知常; *hào* (sobriquet) *Wúyuán* 吳園 ("Wú-Garden"). Native of Déxīng 德興 in Ráo-zhōu 饒州 (modern eastern Jiāngxī). Lifedates 1062–1121 (CBDB; the Kanripo catalog gives 1061–1120, a one-year-off variant). The *Sòngshǐ* (juan 356) gives him a brief biography.

*Jìnshì* of 1081 (Yuán-fēng 4) at age twenty. Held a series of central and provincial offices under Zhézōng and Huīzōng, rising in the *Dàguān* 大觀 era (1107–1110) to *Huáinán zhuǎn-yùn shǐ* 淮南轉運使 ("Fiscal Commissioner of the Huáinán Circuit"); died at home with the rank of *Cháo-sàn dàfū* 朝散大夫. Father of Zhāng Tāo 張燾, who served the Southern Sòng court under Xiào-zōng as Vice Privy Councillor (*Cān-zhī zhèngshì* 參知政事) and obtained a posthumous elevation of his father's rank to "Grand Tutor" (*Tài-shī* 太師), as recorded by his grandson Zhāng Gāi 張垓 in the postface to the present text.

His scholarly output was substantial — Zhāng Gāi's postface lists several hundred juan of *Sòng-cháo biān-nián* 宋朝編年 ("Annal-Style History of the Sòng") and commentaries on the Five Classics and the various philosophers; Cháo Gōngwǔ's *Dúshū zhì* lists a further *Chūnqiū zhǐnán* 春秋指南 in ten juan. Of all this, only the *Yì*-commentary survives:

- **[[KR1a0019|*Wúyuán Zhōuyì jiě*]]** 吳園周易解 in nine juan — the only of his works to come down complete, with a *Tài* 泰 hexagram essay appended that is read as a *zhī wēi* 識微 ("perceiving the subtle") prediction of the late-Huīzōng political collapse.

A modern punctuated edition is in the Sìkù WYG reprints; no Zhōnghuá shūjú critical edition has been issued.
