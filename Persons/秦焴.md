Qín Yù 秦焴, late-twelfth-century Southern-Sòng provincial administrator. Temporarily governed Ānlù 安陸 (modern Húběi) in the mid-Chúnxī era. Author of the *Chúnxī bǐngwǔ* (1186) Jiànkāng 建康 reprint preface to [[趙善譽]] Zhào Shànyù's *Zhàoshì Yì shuō* ([[KR1a0039]]), in which he records his thirty-year struggle with the *Yì* and his eventual breakthrough via the *time-and-position* hermeneutic principle (*one hexagram = one time, one line = one position*) imparted by an *Yì*-scholar friend. The reprint, in a smaller format aimed at student use, was based on a copy sent to him by his Wǔlíng 武陵 friend [[畢希簡]] Bì Xījiǎn.
