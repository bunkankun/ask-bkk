**Liáng Zhèng** 梁正 (Táng-period, dates not preserved), Táng-period ritualist known principally as the author of an illustrated *Sānlǐ* compendium *Sānlǐ tú* 三禮圖 ([[KR1d0135]]) — one of the two principal Táng-period precursor sources for [[聶崇義]] Niè Chóngyì's authoritative Sòng-period *Sānlǐ tú* ([[KR1d0017]]). His work is preserved only through Niè Chóngyì's citations; no other independent transmission survives.

He has no standard biography in *Jiù Táng shū* or *Xīn Táng shū*, and his lifespan, native place, and offices are not preserved in standard sources. He is conventionally paired with the other Táng-period *Sānlǐ tú* author [[張鎰]] Zhāng Yì in Niè Chóngyì's citation chain. No CBDB id assigned. No DILA authority record.
