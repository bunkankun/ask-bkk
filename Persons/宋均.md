Sòng Jūn 宋均 (d. 76), Eastern Hàn classical scholar. The *Suí zhì* and *Táng zhì* record his commentary on the *Yì wěi* — a key Hàn-period *Yì wěi* commentary alongside [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán's, cited in the *Sìkù* notice on [[KR1a0164]].
