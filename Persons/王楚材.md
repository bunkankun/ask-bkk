**Wáng Chǔcái** 王楚材 (*fl.* mid-19th c., 清), native of Yīnxiàn 鄞縣 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Wrote the 道光三十年庚戌夏五 (the fifth lunar month of 1850) preface to [[葉志洗]]'s *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng zàn* 神農本草經贊 ([[KR3ec069]]) at the Guǎngzhōu prefectural office, providing the literary-historical justification for the *zàn* verse-paraphrase method by reference to Guō Pú [[郭璞]]'s *Shānhǎi jīng tú zàn*. No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
