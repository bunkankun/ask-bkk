Late-Míng *vinaya*-school monk of the Lùtōng Lǜsì 陸通律寺 in Wǔlín 武林 (= Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng); author of [[KR6i0571]] *Yuánjué jīng yàojiě* 圓覺經要解 (X10 no. 260, 2 fascicles). His autograph preface to that work is dated *Wànlì 45 / 12 / 8* = the Buddha's-enlightenment day of 1617 (= early 1618 CE), signed *Wǔlín shì* Jìzhèng 武林釋寂正. He is identified at the head of the main text as *Lùtōnglǜsì qínxī* 陸通律寺勤息. Lifedates and detailed biographical information not preserved in standard biographical sources; he was part of the late-Wàn-lì Hángzhōu *vinaya*-revival circle but his specific place within that movement cannot be reconstructed.
