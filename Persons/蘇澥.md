# Sū Xiè 蘇澥

Northern Sòng official (1039–1094), at the time *quán fāqiǎn liǎngZhè zhuǎnyùn fùshǐ* 權發遣兩浙轉運副使; collated and newly printed [[KR6q0073]] *Yúnmén Kuāngzhēn chánshī guǎnglù* in 1076, supplying the principal extant preface.
