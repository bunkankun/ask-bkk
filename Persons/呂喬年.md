Lǚ Qiáonián 呂喬年 (Sòng) was a Southern Sòng official, son of Lǚ Zǔjiǎn 呂祖儉 and nephew of Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙. He completed the editorial work on his uncle's *yǔlù* materials gathered by his father, producing the standard recension of *Lìzé lùn shuō jí lù* 麗澤論說集錄 ([[KR3a0052]]) in 10 juan. His own preface (cited in the SKQS *tíyào*) acknowledges that he supplemented his father's compilation and rearranged the order. CBDB id 10321, no dates.
