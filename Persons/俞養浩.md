# 俞養浩

Yú Yǎnghào (*zì* Zhōngluán 鍾鑾) of Hǎiyú 海虞 / Chángshú 常熟, late-Qīng Sūzhōu physician. Acquired the anonymous manuscript *Nángmì hóushū* 囊秘喉書 and during the *Guāngxù* 27 / *xīnchǒu* (1901) Sūzhōu throat-disease epidemic commissioned his pupil [[王景華]] Wáng Jǐnghuá to recast it; the revised text is [[KR3em030]] *Chóngdìng nángmì hóushū* 重訂囊秘喉書.
