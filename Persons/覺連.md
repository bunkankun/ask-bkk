Míng-dynasty Buddhist monk, **abbot at Mt. Shǎoshì** (少室山, the western peak of Mt. Sòng — i.e., **Shàolínsì** 少林寺), in the lineage that styled itself the **Cáodòng zhèngzōng** 曹洞正宗 (the orthodox Cáodòng line transmitted through the Shàolín seat). Alternate appellation **Dàfāng Lián** 大方連. Lifedates unrecorded; only known literary act is the **1551 (Jiājìng *xīnhài* 嘉靖辛亥) compilation** of the annotated *Xiāoshì Jīngāng jīng kēyí huìyào zhùjiě* 銷釋金剛經科儀會要註解 ([[KR6c0055]], X24 no. 467), undertaken at the request of the layman **Xǔ Jìngyú** 許敬愚 of Sūzhōu and conveyed by the Chán master **Yíngān Dàodēng** 螢菴道燈 (沙門 of the imperially-patronized 智化寺) on commission of Juélián's master **Dàníng** 大寧. Juélián describes his own role as *huì qǔ zhū jiā jiéyào zhī zhù, ān yú kēyí běnwén zhī xià* 會取諸家捷要之註，安於科儀本文之下 — gathering the most pointed annotations of earlier hands and arranging them under the *kēyí* base text — explicitly disclaiming originality (*非敢胸臆強為，祇是述成而巳*).
