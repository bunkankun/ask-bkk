Míng-dynasty Buddhist monk, **abbot at Mt. Shǎoshì** (少室山, the western peak of Mt. Sòng — i.e., **Shàolín-sì** 少林寺), in the lineage that styled itself the **Cáo-dòng zhèng-zōng** 曹洞正宗 (the orthodox Cáo-dòng line transmitted through the Shàolín seat). Alternate appellation **Dà-fāng Lián** 大方連. Lifedates unrecorded; only known literary act is the **1551 (Jiājìng *xīn-hài* 嘉靖辛亥) compilation** of the annotated *Xiāo-shì Jīn-gāng jīng kē-yí huì-yào zhù-jiě* 銷釋金剛經科儀會要註解 ([[KR6c0055]], X24 no. 467), undertaken at the request of the layman **Xǔ Jìng-yú** 許敬愚 of Sū-zhōu and conveyed by the Chán master **Yíng-ān Dào-dēng** 螢菴道燈 (沙門 of the imperially-patronized 智化寺) on commission of Jué-lián's master **Dà-níng** 大寧. Jué-lián describes his own role as *huì qǔ zhū jiā jié-yào zhī zhù, ān yú kē-yí běn-wén zhī xià* 會取諸家捷要之註，安於科儀本文之下 — gathering the most pointed annotations of earlier hands and arranging them under the *kē-yí* base text — explicitly disclaiming originality (*非敢胸臆強為，祇是述成而巳*).
