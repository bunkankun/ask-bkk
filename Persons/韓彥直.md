# Hán Yànzhí 韓彥直

Mid-Southern-Sòng official, *zì* Zǐ-wēn 子溫. Native of Yán-ān 延安 (Shǎnxī). Eldest son of the famous Southern-Sòng general Hán Shìzhōng 韓世忠 (1090–1151), the *Qí Zhōng-wǔ Wáng* 蘄忠武王 of *Jiàn-yán*-restoration fame. CBDB id 667 records the birth-year 1131.

*Jìnshì* of Shào-xīng 18 (1148), aged 17. Career: rose through central offices to *Lóng-tú-gé xué-shì* (Dragon-Diagram Pavilion Academician); held *tí-jǔ Wàn-shòu guān* (Director of the Temple of Ten Thousand Longevities — a Sòng sinecure for senior retired-or-honoured officials). Late in his career was prefect (*zhī-zhōu*) of Wēn-zhōu in Yǒng-jiā 永嘉 (modern Wēn-zhōu, Zhèjiāng), during which time (Chún-xī 5 / 1178) he composed the *Júlù* 橘錄 ([[KR3i0039]]) — the foundational Chinese citrus-monograph. Retired with the title *Guāng-lù dà-fū* and was enfeoffed *Qí-chūn jùn-gōng* (Duke of Qí-chūn Commandery — inheriting his father's principal title).

Also compiled the *Shuǐ-xīn jìng* 水心鏡 — a 160+ *juàn* compilation of Sòng dynastic-historical anecdotes, praised by Yóu Mào 尤袤 but now lost. His career is appended to his father's biography in the *Sòngshǐ*.
