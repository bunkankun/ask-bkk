# Hán Yànzhí 韓彥直

Mid-Southern-Sòng official, *zì* Zǐwēn 子溫. Native of Yánān 延安 (Shǎnxī). Eldest son of the famous Southern-Sòng general Hán Shìzhōng 韓世忠 (1090–1151), the *Qí Zhōngwǔ Wáng* 蘄忠武王 of *Jiànyán*-restoration fame. CBDB id 667 records the birth-year 1131.

*Jìnshì* of Shàoxīng 18 (1148), aged 17. Career: rose through central offices to *Lóngtúgé xuéshì* (Dragon-Diagram Pavilion Academician); held *tíjǔ Wànshòu guān* (Director of the Temple of Ten Thousand Longevities — a Sòng sinecure for senior retired-or-honoured officials). Late in his career was prefect (*zhīzhōu*) of Wēnzhōu in Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (modern Wēnzhōu, Zhèjiāng), during which time (Chúnxī 5 / 1178) he composed the *Júlù* 橘錄 ([[KR3i0039]]) — the foundational Chinese citrus-monograph. Retired with the title *Guānglù dàfū* and was enfeoffed *Qíchūn jùngōng* (Duke of Qíchūn Commandery — inheriting his father's principal title).

Also compiled the *Shuǐxīn jìng* 水心鏡 — a 160+ *juàn* compilation of Sòng dynastic-historical anecdotes, praised by Yóu Mào 尤袤 but now lost. His career is appended to his father's biography in the *Sòngshǐ*.
