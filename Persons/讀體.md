Late-Míng / early-Qīng *Vinaya* master, the principal figure of the **Bǎohuáshān Lǜzōng** 寶華山律宗 revival; sobriquet **Jiànyuè** 見月 ("Seeing-the-Moon"; with full name **Jiànyuè Dútǐ** 見月讀體, **Jiànyuè lǜshī** 見月律師), *zì* **Shàorú** 紹如. DILA Authority A001972. Born Wànlì 29, 3rd month, 3rd day (= 5 April 1601); died Kāngxī *jǐwèi* (1679), 1st month, 22nd day (= 4 March 1679), aged 79.

Dharma-disciple of **Sānmèi Jìguāng** 三昧寂光 (1580–1645), the founding figure of the Bǎohuáshān 寶華山 *Vinaya* revival in early-Qīng China. After his teacher's death in 1645, Dútǐ succeeded to the Bǎohuáshān seat and presided over the institution for some thirty-four years (1645–1679), making it the principal *Vinaya*-school institution of early-Qīng China and the institutional centre from which the *Vinaya* tradition was systematically restored after late-Míng decline.

Major works: substantial *Vinaya* corpus including the **Pí-ní rì-yòng qiè-yào** 毘尼日用切要 (T1115), the **Sān-tán fà-jiè zhèng-fàn** 三壇傳戒正範 (the foundational early-Qīng ordination manual), and the *Bǎo-huá-shān zhì* 寶華山志 (the institutional gazetteer of his own monastery). For the present catalog, Dú-tǐ provided the *chóng-zuǎn* 重纂 ("re-compiled") editorial work on Sì-míng Zhīlǐ's *Qiān-shǒu qiān-yǎn dà-bēi-xīn-zhòu xíng-fǎ* ([[KR6d0202]], X74n1480), preserving Zhīlǐ's foundational Avalokiteśvara liturgical material in early-Qīng recension.

Sources: *Bǎohuáshān zhì* 寶華山志 *juan* 7 (the *Jiànyuè dàlǜshī tǎmíng* 見月大律師塔銘); DILA A001972.
