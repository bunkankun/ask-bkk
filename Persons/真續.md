Zhēnxù 真續, *zì* **Zhúfēng** 竹峰 ("Bamboo Peak"); often referred to as **Zhāojué Zhúfēng** 昭覺竹峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fóyuān Chègāng** 佛冤徹綱 (1626–?). Born **順治十七年庚子四月初八日辰時** = **1660-05-16** Gregorian (Buddha's Birthday); death year unknown. Native of **Sōngzhōu** 松州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Shěn** 沈.

**Formation.** Sent at age 1 to **Sōngzhōu Miàojuésì** 松州妙覺寺 to be raised by **Rúshān** 如山師. Took full ordination under **Shēnghuì** 生慧和尚. One day reading the *Lotus Sūtra* at the line "*fù shǎo ér zǐ lǎo*" 父少兒子老 (the father young, the son old), doubt arose. Left home for **Zhāojuésì** 昭覺寺 to *cān* under **Zhàngxuě** 丈和尚 (= Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì), stayed a year. Three years later returned to Zhāojué where **Fóyuān** 佛冤和尚 had become abbot. One night at the fifth-watch, on hearing the morning bell, suddenly felt great ease; presented his understanding for testing and received final *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies (five Sìchuān seats).** **Wēifèng Zhāojué** 威鳳昭覺 (the historic Sìchuān Línjì-Yángqí mother-temple); **Miàojué Chóngshàn** 妙覺崇善; **Xīlóng Dàbēi** 西龍大悲; **Xīchóng Wúliàng** 西崇無量; **Fènghuáng Fǎzàng** 鳳凰法藏.

**Disciples.** Eighty-three (!) *fǎsì* listed (one of the largest in J40), including **Déyún Shíwù** 德雲實悟 [[實悟]] (compiler), **Yǒutáng Déjì** 有堂德紀, **Jìnghuái Zhèngyì** 敬懷正義, plus six lay disciples (**Qiáo Yīnggāo** 喬應高, **Fāng Liándēng** 方聯登, **Shěn Guóxiāng** 沈國鄉, etc.).

**Work.** [[KR6q0591]] 《昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B479, compiled by *fǎsì* **Déyún Shíwù** [[實悟]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄》 vol. 5 〈行實〉, 〈分燈〉.
