Zhēn-xù 真續, *zì* **Zhú-fēng** 竹峰 ("Bamboo Peak"); often referred to as **Zhāo-jué Zhú-fēng** 昭覺竹峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fó-yuān Chè-gāng** 佛冤徹綱 (1626–?). Born **順治十七年庚子四月初八日辰時** = **1660-05-16** Gregorian (Buddha's Birthday); death year unknown. Native of **Sōng-zhōu** 松州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Shěn** 沈.

**Formation.** Sent at age 1 to **Sōng-zhōu Miào-jué-sì** 松州妙覺寺 to be raised by **Rú-shān** 如山師. Took full ordination under **Shēng-huì** 生慧和尚. One day reading the *Lotus Sūtra* at the line "*fù shǎo ér zǐ lǎo*" 父少兒子老 (the father young, the son old), doubt arose. Left home for **Zhāo-jué-sì** 昭覺寺 to *cān* under **Zhàng-xuě** 丈和尚 (= Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì), stayed a year. Three years later returned to Zhāo-jué where **Fó-yuān** 佛冤和尚 had become abbot. One night at the fifth-watch, on hearing the morning bell, suddenly felt great ease; presented his understanding for testing and received final *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies (five Sì-chuān seats).** **Wēi-fèng Zhāo-jué** 威鳳昭覺 (the historic Sì-chuān Línjì-Yángqí mother-temple); **Miào-jué Chóng-shàn** 妙覺崇善; **Xī-lóng Dà-bēi** 西龍大悲; **Xī-chóng Wú-liàng** 西崇無量; **Fèng-huáng Fǎ-zàng** 鳳凰法藏.

**Disciples.** Eighty-three (!) *fǎ-sì* listed (one of the largest in J40), including **Dé-yún Shí-wù** 德雲實悟 [[實悟]] (compiler), **Yǒu-táng Dé-jì** 有堂德紀, **Jìng-huái Zhèng-yì** 敬懷正義, plus six lay disciples (**Qiáo Yīng-gāo** 喬應高, **Fāng Lián-dēng** 方聯登, **Shěn Guó-xiāng** 沈國鄉, etc.).

**Work.** [[KR6q0591]] 《昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B479, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-yún Shí-wù** [[實悟]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄》 vol. 5 〈行實〉, 〈分燈〉.
