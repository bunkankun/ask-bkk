**Iyo Heijū** 伊豫平住 (Chinese: Yīyù Píngzhù, fl. 18th c.), Edo-period Japanese physician of the *Iyo* (伊豫) province of Shikoku. Compiler of the *Chūgoku yakubutsugaku taikō* 中國藥物學大綱 ([[KR3ec080]]) — a substantial Japanese-language survey of the Chinese pharmacopoeia in the *Honzō gangmoku* (本草綱目) tradition, with detailed *jiànbié* (鑑別 = identification) sections for each substance discussing Chinese versus Japanese drug supply and the *honzōgaku* (本草学) school controversies over substance identification. Standard reference works place the compilation in the mid-18th century. Japanese physician; not in CBDB.
