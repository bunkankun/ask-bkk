# Xiè Fū 謝敷

Eastern Jìn lay-Buddhist literatus, *zì* Qìngxù 慶緒. Native of Kuàijī 會稽. Author of the surviving second preface to the *Dà ānbān shǒuyì jīng* 大安般守意經 ([[KR6i0239]]) (preserved in the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集). Active in the 4th c.; lifedates not precisely recorded. *Jìn shū* 晉書 juan 94.
