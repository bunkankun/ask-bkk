# Hé Píngzǐ 何平子

A late-Yuán / early-Míng mathematician active in the southeastern coastal region; *fl.* c. 1373, co-author with [[安止齋]] Ān Zhǐzhāi of the *Xiángmíng suànfǎ* 詳明算法 ([[KR3fc014]]). Birth and death years not securely recorded; no other works survive. Some MíngQīng sources conflate Hé Píngzǐ with Ān Zhǐzhāi as a single author under a sobriquet, but the joint-authorship presentation in the *Xiángmíng*'s own preface is generally taken as correct.
