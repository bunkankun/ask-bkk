# Zōngxiān 宗先

Early-Southern-Sòng Yúnménzōng 雲門宗 Chán *shìzhě* 侍者 of [[懷深]] Císhòu Huáishēn (1077–1132); co-editor of his master's four-juan *guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0381]]; X73 n1451). Lifedates unrecorded.
