Liú Kěshū 劉可書, mid-Kāngxī Qīng official from Qīng-yuàn 清苑 (Bǎodìng prefecture, Zhílì 直隸, modern Héběi). Editor of the 10-juan *Fàn Zhōngzhēn jí* 范忠貞集 ([[KR4f0017]]) — the collected poetry-and-prose of the martyred Fú-jiàn governor-general [[范承謨]] (1635–1676), assembled in the years immediately following Fàn's posthumous canonization and including the imperially-composed sacrificial text, stele inscription, and shrine-tablet inscription. Liú's editorial work is preserved as the *fù-lù* materials in juan 9–10. No independent CBDB record under this name; Liú is otherwise a minor figure attested only through the Fàn-corpus editorial colophons.
