Rúxiāng 如相, *zì* **Mǐnshù** 敏樹 ("Quick Tree"); often referred to as **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** 敏樹如相. LínjìYángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) — one of the most senior of Pòshān's heirs, founder of the major **Mǐnshù** 敏樹 sub-line of southwestern LínjìYángqí Buddhism. Born **1603** (estimated); died **康熙壬子十一月初三日亥時** = **1672-12-21**, age 70, *sēnglà* 45 — at **Cíyúntáng** 慈雲堂 in Sìchuān, with stupa erected to the right of **Cíyúnān** 慈雲菴. Native of **Tóngchuānzhōu Línshuǐ** 潼川州鄰水 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** At 25 took tonsure under **Jiàn-kōng** 鑑空 in his hometown — set himself the *wǔ-yùn jiē-kōng* 五蘊皆空 *gōng-àn*. Then read the *Śūraṅgama jīng* and felt the *chén-láo* (defilements) temporarily quiet. Sought out **Pò-shān** 破山, who gave him the *gǒu-zǐ wú fó-xìng* 狗子無佛性 *gōng-àn*. One day on hearing the bell-and-board sound, "his whole body felt like a barrel-hoop suddenly snapping" — Pò-shān sealed him.

**Abbacies (seven, across SìchuānHúběiGuìzhōu).** **Sìchuān Diànjiāngxiàn Bǎizhàng chányuàn** 四川墊江縣百丈禪院 (the principal seat); **Húguǎng Shīzhōuwèi Wànshòu chánsì** 湖廣施州衛萬壽禪寺; **Sìchuān Péngshuǐxiàn Tàipíng chánsì** 四川彭水縣太平禪寺; **Guìzhōu Shíqiānfǔ Sānmèi chányuàn** 貴州石阡府三昧禪院; **Sìchuān Zūnyìfǔ Hǎilóngshān Gǔlóng chányuàn** 四川遵義府海龍山古龍禪院; **Wànxiàn Cíyún chányuàn** 萬縣慈雲禪院 (returned twice); **Guìzhōu Dàxīng chánsì** 貴州大興禪寺.

**Disciples.** Twenty-four *fǎsì* listed in his *yǔlù*, including **Ěrdú Tài** 耳毒泰 (teacher of [[明|Huànzhù Míng]] cf. [[KR6q0549]]; teacher of [[廣德]] cf. [[KR6q0550]]); **Tiānyǐn Dàochóng** 天隱道崇 (compiler of this *yǔlù*); **Chìsōng Dàolǐng** 赤松道領 [[道領]] (recorder; subject of [[KR6q0565]]); **Yǐngxiù Zhēnwù** 穎秀真悟 (teacher of [[性炅]] cf. [[KR6q0524]]); **Bùyàn Dàolè** 不厭道樂 [[道樂]] (cf. [[KR6q0538]] — though more directly transmitted through 聖可德玉); and many others spanning the SìchuānGuìzhōuYúnnán southwestern frontier.

**Work.** [[KR6q0564]] 《敏樹禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B450, compiled by *fǎsì* **Tiānyǐn Dàochóng** [[道崇]] *biān* + recorded by **Chìsōng Dàolǐng** [[道領]] *lù*. Two prefaces dated **康熙戊辰蒲月** = **1688** lunar 5 by 貴陽黔靈山弘福寺 (the principal monastery of Dàolǐng).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 9, vol. 11; 《黔南會燈錄》 vol. 1; 《五燈全書》 vol. 70, 87, 88; 《正源略集目錄》 vol. 1.
