Rú-xiāng 如相, *zì* **Mǐn-shù** 敏樹 ("Quick Tree"); often referred to as **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** 敏樹如相. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) — one of the most senior of Pò-shān's heirs, founder of the major **Mǐn-shù** 敏樹 sub-line of southwestern Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism. Born **1603** (estimated); died **康熙壬子十一月初三日亥時** = **1672-12-21**, age 70, *sēng-là* 45 — at **Cí-yún-táng** 慈雲堂 in Sì-chuān, with stupa erected to the right of **Cí-yún-ān** 慈雲菴. Native of **Tóng-chuān-zhōu Lín-shuǐ** 潼川州鄰水 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** At 25 took tonsure under **Jiàn-kōng** 鑑空 in his hometown — set himself the *wǔ-yùn jiē-kōng* 五蘊皆空 *gōng-àn*. Then read the *Śūraṅgama jīng* and felt the *chén-láo* (defilements) temporarily quiet. Sought out **Pò-shān** 破山, who gave him the *gǒu-zǐ wú fó-xìng* 狗子無佛性 *gōng-àn*. One day on hearing the bell-and-board sound, "his whole body felt like a barrel-hoop suddenly snapping" — Pò-shān sealed him.

**Abbacies (seven, across Sì-chuān-Húběi-Guì-zhōu).** **Sì-chuān Diàn-jiāng-xiàn Bǎi-zhàng chán-yuàn** 四川墊江縣百丈禪院 (the principal seat); **Hú-guǎng Shī-zhōu-wèi Wàn-shòu chán-sì** 湖廣施州衛萬壽禪寺; **Sì-chuān Péng-shuǐ-xiàn Tài-píng chán-sì** 四川彭水縣太平禪寺; **Guì-zhōu Shí-qiān-fǔ Sān-mèi chán-yuàn** 貴州石阡府三昧禪院; **Sì-chuān Zūn-yì-fǔ Hǎi-lóng-shān Gǔ-lóng chán-yuàn** 四川遵義府海龍山古龍禪院; **Wàn-xiàn Cí-yún chán-yuàn** 萬縣慈雲禪院 (returned twice); **Guì-zhōu Dà-xīng chán-sì** 貴州大興禪寺.

**Disciples.** Twenty-four *fǎ-sì* listed in his *yǔ-lù*, including **Ěr-dú Tài** 耳毒泰 (teacher of [[明|Huàn-zhù Míng]] cf. [[KR6q0549]]; teacher of [[廣德]] cf. [[KR6q0550]]); **Tiān-yǐn Dào-chóng** 天隱道崇 (compiler of this *yǔ-lù*); **Chì-sōng Dào-lǐng** 赤松道領 [[道領]] (recorder; subject of [[KR6q0565]]); **Yǐng-xiù Zhēn-wù** 穎秀真悟 (teacher of [[性炅]] cf. [[KR6q0524]]); **Bù-yàn Dào-lè** 不厭道樂 [[道樂]] (cf. [[KR6q0538]] — though more directly transmitted through 聖可德玉); and many others spanning the Sì-chuān-Guì-zhōu-Yún-nán southwestern frontier.

**Work.** [[KR6q0564]] 《敏樹禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B450, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Tiān-yǐn Dào-chóng** [[道崇]] *biān* + recorded by **Chì-sōng Dào-lǐng** [[道領]] *lù*. Two prefaces dated **康熙戊辰蒲月** = **1688** lunar 5 by 貴陽黔靈山弘福寺 (the principal monastery of Dào-lǐng).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 9, vol. 11; 《黔南會燈錄》 vol. 1; 《五燈全書》 vol. 70, 87, 88; 《正源略集目錄》 vol. 1.
