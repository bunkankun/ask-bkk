**Pān Chéng** 潘誠 (*zì* *Bǎozhēn* 葆真; Qīng), late-Qīng *hóukē* (laryngology) specialist active in the *Dàoguāng* era (1820s–1850s), evidently of the Húnán region (his case-records refer to the 1846 [*Dàoguāng bǐngwǔ* 道光丙午] throat-disease epidemic in his district, and the work was reprinted from his manuscript in 1878 [*Guāngxù wùyín*] by the *Wǎnyuè jūshì* 浣月居士 of Héngyáng 衡陽 at the *Shùdé táng* 樹德堂 in Yángzhōu). Author of the *Hóukē xīnfǎ* 喉科心法 ([[KR3em036]]), a programmatic anti-*kǔhán* (anti-bitter-cold) Qīng throat-medicine treatise that recovers [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's *Bǔzhōng yìqì* method and the cold-pattern *Sìnì* / *Lǐzhōng* / *Fùzǐ* tradition for treating *yīn*-pattern throat-occlusion. The author's colophon is signed *Pān Chéng Bǎozhēn shì* 潘誠葆真氏 and dated 1847 (*Dàoguāng dīngwèi* 道光二十七年歲次丁未). CBDB has no entry; no further biographical detail located.
