**Pān Chéng** 潘誠 (*zì* *Bǎo-zhēn* 葆真; Qīng), late-Qīng *hóu-kē* (laryngology) specialist active in the *Dào-guāng* era (1820s–1850s), evidently of the Húnán region (his case-records refer to the 1846 [*Dào-guāng bǐng-wǔ* 道光丙午] throat-disease epidemic in his district, and the work was reprinted from his manuscript in 1878 [*Guāng-xù wù-yín*] by the *Wǎn-yuè jū-shì* 浣月居士 of Héng-yáng 衡陽 at the *Shù-dé táng* 樹德堂 in Yáng-zhōu). Author of the *Hóu-kē xīn-fǎ* 喉科心法 ([[KR3em036]]), a programmatic anti-*kǔ-hán* (anti-bitter-cold) Qīng throat-medicine treatise that recovers [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's *Bǔ-zhōng yì-qì* method and the cold-pattern *Sì-nì* / *Lǐ-zhōng* / *Fù-zǐ* tradition for treating *yīn*-pattern throat-occlusion. The author's colophon is signed *Pān Chéng Bǎo-zhēn shì* 潘誠葆真氏 and dated 1847 (*Dào-guāng dīng-wèi* 道光二十七年歲次丁未). CBDB has no entry; no further biographical detail located.
