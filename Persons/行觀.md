Xíngguān 行觀, *zì* **Língjī** 靈機 ("Numinous Trigger"); often referred to as **Zīfú Língjī** 資福靈機 from his abbacy at the famous **Húzhōu Wǔkāng Xiángfèngshān Zīfúsì** 湖州武康翔鳳山顯忠崇孝資福禪寺. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661), thus a co-disciple of **Mùchén Dàomǐn** 木陳道忞, **Yǐnyuán Lóngqí** 隱元隆琦, **Shíguān Xínglíng** [[行凌]] (cf. [[KR6q0542]]) etc. Born **明萬曆丙辰正月廿三日寅刻** = **1616-03-10** Gregorian; died **康熙辛酉九月廿九日子時** = **1681-11-08**, age 66, *sēnglà* 49. Native of **Lóngxī** 龍溪 (Fújiàn); lay surname **Zhōu** 周.

**Formation.** Vegetarian from childhood. At 20 was struck by an experience of *wúcháng* (impermanence), grew despondent and went to **Jiǔyún Huìtán** 九雲慧曇 to take tonsure. First *cān*-fǎng under **Tiāntóng Yuánwù** 天童圓悟 (= **Mìyún**); then **Jīnsù Fèiyǐn**. One night during a sudden thunderstorm "the *wú* 無 *gōngàn* dropped away — mountains and rivers congealed into a single piece — limitless joy." Appointed *shìxiāng* (incense-attendant). One day Fèiyǐn used the proverb "*zuǒbiān bànjīn yòubiān bāliǎng*" 左邊半觔右邊八兩 (half a *jīn* on the left, eight ounces on the right) — at this Xíngguān *huòrán* 豁然. Submitted a verse, was rebuked, sweated profusely, and at length grasped the *quánjī dàyòng* (whole-trigger great-function); presented a second verse on the *wú* *gōngàn* and received final *yìnkě*.

**Abbacies (two).** **Húzhōu Wǔkāng Xiángfèngshān Xiǎnzhōng Chóngxiào Zīfú chánsì** 湖州武康翔鳳山顯忠崇孝資福禪寺 (built initial hut **崇禎甲申春** = **1644** spring; took formal abbacy **順治甲午冬** = **1654** winter — 30 years total residency); **Jiāxīngfǔ Shíménxiàn Fúyán chánsì** 嘉興府石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙辛亥** = **1671** on the invitation of regional gentry).

**Disciples (twenty-one).** **Zhìqióng Jìfāng** 至卭寂方 [[寂方]] (compiler of his *yǔlù*, native of Mǐn 福清, lay 許), **Rúbò Pèi** 茹檗霈, **Sōngān Zhèng** 嵩庵正, lay disciple **Wèi Wēi** 魏巍, etc.

**Work.** [[KR6q0562]] 《靈機禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B448, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìqióng Jìfāng** [[寂方]] *děng biān*. Three prefaces are preserved in juan 1, including one labeled *zhòngkè* (重刻) and one *kèzhèng* (刻正), indicating a corrected reissue.

Buried at **Zīfúsì Lóngshān** 資福寺龍山 in Húzhōu.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 71, vol. 91; 《靈機禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈行狀〉, 〈翔鳳山顯忠崇孝資福禪寺靈機和尚塔銘〉.

**Gyōkan** 行觀 (dates uncertain; *fl.* probably late-medieval to early-modern Japan), Jōdoshū monk of the **Seizan-school catechetical tradition**, author of [[KR6t0345]] **Jōdo hōmon dai-zu meimoku** 淨土法門大圖名目 — a gloss-edition / explanatory companion to [[覺融]] Kakuyū's earlier *Jōdoshū hōmon dai-zu* [[KR6t0344]]. The *meimoku* (topical-headings) format is characteristic of the Seizan-school *gakumon-bukkyō* catechetical genre.

Gyōkan is not separately attested in the standard Pure-Land historiographical sources; the work was first printed in **Bunka 13 / 8 / upper-third = 1816** by **Chōzan** 超山 of Saikō-ji 西向寺 in west Kyoto, which gives only a *terminus ante quem*. Gyōkan's actual composition-date is probably late-medieval to early-Edo, given the typical lag between composition and first printing in the Seizan-school textual corpus. This person is to be distinguished from the homonymous Chinese LínjìYángqí Chán master Xíngguān 行觀 (1616–1681; *zì* Língjī 靈機) treated above.
