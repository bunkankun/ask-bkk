**Wú Yìdǐng** 吳亦鼎 (*zì* Yúnzhāi 雲齋, fl. Xiánfēng period, mid-19th c., 清), late-Qīng moxibustion specialist. Author of the *Shénjiǔ jīnglún* 神灸經綸 ([[KR3ee050]], 1851), a four-juan moxibustion-specific monograph polemically arguing for the restoration of moxibustion as the canonical-Língshū equal of needling and herbal therapy. No further biographical record survives; not in CBDB.
