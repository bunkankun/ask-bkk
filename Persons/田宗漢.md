Mid-19th-century physician, *zì* Yúnchá 雲槎. Active around the 1850s. Author of the original *Shí xíng fú yīn lùn* 時行伏陰論 (1854), re-edited in 1922 as [[KR3eb042]] *Chóng dìng shí xíng fú yīn chú yán*. The work argues for a clinical distinction between cholera and the *fú yīn* 伏陰 latent-yīn disorder, with substantial implications for prescriptive practice during the Qing cholera pandemics.
