Modern Chinese Buddhist studies scholar associated with the Yúnnán Buddhist Association (雲南省佛教協會). Specialist in the regional *Āzhālì* 阿吒力 ritual tradition of Yúnnán. Co-edited (with [[侯沖]]) several Yúnnán *Āzhālì* liturgical manuals for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vols 6–8: [[KR6v0063]] *Léngyán jiěyuān shìjié dàochǎng yí*, [[KR6v0064]] *Dìzàng cíbēi jiùkǔ jiànfú lìshēng dàochǎng yí*, [[KR6v0065]] *Xiāoshì Jīngāngjīng kēyí*, [[KR6v0088]] *Rúlái guǎngxiào shízhǒng bàoēn dàochǎng yí*. Personal possessor of several of the Qīng-period base manuscripts used by the *Zàngwài* editors.
