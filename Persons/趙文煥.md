Modern Chinese Buddhist studies scholar associated with the Yúnnán Buddhist Association (雲南省佛教協會). Specialist in the regional *Āzhālì* 阿吒力 ritual tradition of Yúnnán. Co-edited (with [[侯沖]]) several Yúnnán *Āzhālì* liturgical manuals for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vols 6–8: [[KR6v0063]] *Léng-yán jiě-yuān shì-jié dào-chǎng yí*, [[KR6v0064]] *Dìzàng cíbēi jiùkǔ jiànfú lìshēng dào-chǎng yí*, [[KR6v0065]] *Xiāo-shì Jīngāng-jīng kē-yí*, [[KR6v0088]] *Rúlái guǎngxiào shí-zhǒng bào-ēn dào-chǎng yí*. Personal possessor of several of the Qīng-period base manuscripts used by the *Zàngwài* editors.
