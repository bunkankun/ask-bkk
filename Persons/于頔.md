# Yú Dí 于頔

Táng-era scholar-official and lay Buddhist; patron and interlocutor of the great Chán layman **Páng Yùn** 龐蘊 (c. 740–808). Compiler (*biān jí* 編集) of the *Páng jūshì yǔlù* 龐居士語錄 [[KR6q0273]] — the classical three-juan *yǔlù* of Páng Yùn. Lifedates approximately contemporary with Páng (mid-to-late 8th to early 9th century). Full biographical details preserved in Táng historical records.
