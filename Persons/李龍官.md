# Lǐ Lóngguān 李龍官

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A member of the Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 editorial committee for the *Sìkù quánshū*; principally documented as the *kǎozhèng* compiler for the *Sānguó zhì* ([[KR2a0012]]). Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
