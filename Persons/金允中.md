**Jīn Yǔnzhōng** 金允中 was a Southern-Sòng **Shàng qīng Líng bǎo** 上清靈寶 ritual compiler, author of [[KR5g0032]] *Shàng qīng Líng bǎo dà fǎ* (Recension II) — parallel to Wáng Qìzhēn's compilation.
