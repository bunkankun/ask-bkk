Dàhè 大壑 (字 **Yuánjīn** 元津 / **Xuánjīn** 玄津), late-Míng Buddhist monk; born 1576, died 1627 (Tiānqǐ 7) at age 52. Native of **Qiántáng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu), secular surname **Xuē** 薛.

He took ordination at **Wànfēngyuàn** 萬峰院 under the monk **Zhūxūn** 袾薰, then deepened his Huáyán study under **Xuělàng Hóngēn** 雪浪洪恩 (1545–1607) at the **Báimén** 白門 (= Nánjīng) Buddhist establishment. He served as senior abbot of the **Jìngcísì** 淨慈寺 in Hángzhōu — the great Sòng-period **Yǒngmíng Yánshòu** 永明延壽 monastery — and was associated with **Hāngshān Déqīng** 憨山德清 (1546–1623). At the **Zōngjìngtáng** 宗鏡堂 within Jìngcísì he served as resident-master, giving extended lectures on the *Zōngjìnglù* of Yánshòu.

His principal Kanripo work is the **《永明道蹟》** *Yǒngmíng dàojì* ([[KR6r0109]], X1599, 1 juan, 1606), a votive site-specific compilation of the Yánshòu tradition at Jìngcísì. He is also documented in the **《徑山志》** *Jìngshānzhì* (under both 玄津 and 大壑) and the *Jìngcísìzhì* stele-inscriptions. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000069 (with cross-references to *Yínián lù* 疑年錄 386).
