Dà-hè 大壑 (字 **Yuán-jīn** 元津 / **Xuán-jīn** 玄津), late-Míng Buddhist monk; born 1576, died 1627 (Tiān-qǐ 7) at age 52. Native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu), secular surname **Xuē** 薛.

He took ordination at **Wàn-fēng-yuàn** 萬峰院 under the monk **Zhū-xūn** 袾薰, then deepened his Huá-yán study under **Xuě-làng Hóng-ēn** 雪浪洪恩 (1545–1607) at the **Bái-mén** 白門 (= Nánjīng) Buddhist establishment. He served as senior abbot of the **Jìng-cí-sì** 淨慈寺 in Hángzhōu — the great Sòng-period **Yǒng-míng Yán-shòu** 永明延壽 monastery — and was associated with **Hāng-shān Dé-qīng** 憨山德清 (1546–1623). At the **Zōng-jìng-táng** 宗鏡堂 within Jìng-cí-sì he served as resident-master, giving extended lectures on the *Zōng-jìng-lù* of Yán-shòu.

His principal Kanripo work is the **《永明道蹟》** *Yǒng-míng dào-jì* ([[KR6r0109]], X1599, 1 juan, 1606), a votive site-specific compilation of the Yán-shòu tradition at Jìng-cí-sì. He is also documented in the **《徑山志》** *Jìng-shān-zhì* (under both 玄津 and 大壑) and the *Jìng-cí-sì-zhì* stele-inscriptions.
