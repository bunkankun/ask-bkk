Wèi Yìjiè 魏裔介 (1616–1686), *zì* Shíshēng 石生, *hào* Zhēn'ān 貞庵, posthumously *Wényì* 文毅, of Bǎi-xiāng 栢鄉 / 柏鄉 (Zhèngdìng prefecture, modern Héběi). CBDB id 34350; lifedates firm.

*Jìnshì* of Shùnzhì 3 (1646, *bǐngxū*); rose through the censorial and Hànlín hierarchies to *Dà-xué-shì* 大學士 (Grand Secretary) under the Kāngxī emperor. A leading early-Qīng *Lǐ-xué* official, advocating a robust Chéng-Zhū orthodoxy and known for forthright censorial speech ("standing in court with marked breeze and integrity," *lì cháo pǒ zhù fēng-jié*, says the *Sìkù tíyào*). His extensive bibliography includes the *Shèng xué zhī tǒng hé lù* 聖學知統合錄, *Sì shū dà-quán zuǎn-yào* 四書大全纂要, *Xiào jīng zhù yì* 孝經註義, *Shān bǔ Gāo-shì zhuàn* 刪補髙士傳, *Lǐ-xué zhū rú yǔ-lù hé chāo* 理學諸儒語錄合鈔, and the present *Jiān-jì-táng wénjí* 兼濟堂文集 ([[KR4f0015]]) in 20-juan recension by his student Zhān Míng-zhāng 詹明章. Earlier editorial recensions of his works include the *Jiān-jì-táng wén-xuǎn* 兼濟堂文選 (14 juan, *Jiāng-nán* imprint, 1666) by [[彭士] Péng Shì-bào] and Wú Bó-qí 吳伯其, the *Kūn-lín xiǎo-pǐn* 崑林小品 (Bei jīng imprint, 1670) by Hú Tóng-shēng 胡同升, and the *Lín-xià jí* 林下集 self-imprint (Kāngxī 13 / 1674) of 81 pieces with a same-year supplement of 119 pieces.
