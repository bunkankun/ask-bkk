Wèi Yìjiè 魏裔介 (1616–1686), *zì* Shíshēng 石生, *hào* Zhēn'ān 貞庵, posthumously *Wényì* 文毅, of Bǎixiāng 栢鄉 / 柏鄉 (Zhèngdìng prefecture, modern Héběi). CBDB id 34350; lifedates firm.

*Jìnshì* of Shùnzhì 3 (1646, *bǐngxū*); rose through the censorial and Hànlín hierarchies to *Dàxuéshì* 大學士 (Grand Secretary) under the Kāngxī emperor. A leading early-Qīng *Lǐxué* official, advocating a robust ChéngZhū orthodoxy and known for forthright censorial speech ("standing in court with marked breeze and integrity," *lì cháo pǒ zhù fēngjié*, says the *Sìkù tíyào*). His extensive bibliography includes the *Shèng xué zhī tǒng hé lù* 聖學知統合錄, *Sì shū dàquán zuǎnyào* 四書大全纂要, *Xiào jīng zhù yì* 孝經註義, *Shān bǔ Gāoshì zhuàn* 刪補髙士傳, *Lǐxué zhū rú yǔlù hé chāo* 理學諸儒語錄合鈔, and the present *Jiānjìtáng wénjí* 兼濟堂文集 ([[KR4f0015]]) in 20-juan recension by his student Zhān Míngzhāng 詹明章. Earlier editorial recensions of his works include the *Jiānjìtáng wénxuǎn* 兼濟堂文選 (14 juan, *Jiāngnán* imprint, 1666) by [[彭士] Péng Shìbào] and Wú Bóqí 吳伯其, the *Kūnlín xiǎopǐn* 崑林小品 (Bei jīng imprint, 1670) by Hú Tóngshēng 胡同升, and the *Línxià jí* 林下集 self-imprint (Kāngxī 13 / 1674) of 81 pieces with a same-year supplement of 119 pieces.
