Shàng Bǐnghé 尚秉和 (1870–1950), *zì* Jiézhī 節之, late-Qīng / Republican-period *Yì*-scholar of Xíngtái 邢臺. Author of a substantial corpus of *xiàngshù* studies (most notably *Zhōuyì shàngshì xué* 周易尚氏學) that attempted to reconstruct Hàn-period image-and-number traditions on philological foundations after the *kǎozhèng* settlement of [[胡渭]] Hú Wèi ([[KR1a0138]]).
