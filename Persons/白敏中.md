**Bái Mǐnzhōng** 白敏中 (792–861), mid-Táng official; younger cousin of the poet Bái Jūyì 白居易. Served as governor of Chéngdū 成都 and later as Chancellor (宰相); transmitted Zàn Yīn's obstetrical prescriptions to the imperial court, the textual basis of the *Jīngxiào chǎnbǎo*.
