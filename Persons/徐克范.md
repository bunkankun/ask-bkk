# Xú Kèfàn 徐克范

*Zì* Yáomín 堯民. Native of Nánlíng 南陵 (in modern Anhuī, then under Tàipíngfǔ 太平府). Floruit ca. 1700–1720.

Fellow-villager and friend of [[汪越]] (Wāng Yuè). Known principally as the supplementer (*bǔ* 補) of Wāng's *Dú Shǐjì shí biǎo* 讀史記十表 ([[KR2a0005]]), to which he contributed annotations during Wāng's lifetime — an unusual collaboration noted by the Sìkù compilers as evidence of the work's careful collation. No other works are transmitted under his name.
