# Fú Mànróng 伏曼容

*Zì* Gōngyí 公儀. Southern Qí 南齊 / Liáng 梁 classicist of Pínglíng 平陵 (originally Píngchāng 平昌, modern Shāndōng). Wrote a *Zhōuyì jí jiě* 周易集解 and a *Máoshī jiǎngshū* 毛詩講疏, both lost; *Yì* fragments are preserved in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Liáng shū* 梁書 (juan 48) gives his biography.
