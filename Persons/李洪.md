Lǐ Hóng 李洪 (b. 1129), son of the Northern-and-Southern-Sòng senior official Lǐ Zhèngmín 李正民 (the *Dàyǐn xiānshēng*, *shìláng*-rank). Originally of *Yángzhōu* 揚州; family relocated to *Hǎiyán* 海鹽 (modern Zhèjiāng) during the *nándù* of 1127–1129; Lǐ Hóng later moved to *Húzhōu* 湖州. Career undocumented in standard biographical sources except as reconstructed by the *Sìkù* editors from his collection and Chén Guìqiān 陳貴謙's preface: court attendance at *Déshòugōng* and *Jǐnglínggōng* (capital-office service), prefect of *Wēnzhōu*, ending as prefect of *Téngzhōu* 藤州 (modern Guǎngxī). Death year unrecorded; flourished c. 1150–1190. CBDB id 19568. Author of [[KR4d0263]] *Yún'ān lèigǎo*.
