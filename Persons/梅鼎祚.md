Méi Dǐng-zuò 梅鼎祚 (1549–1618), *zì* Yǔ-jīn 禹金, also recorded with the *zì* Yàn-hé 彥和, native of Xuān-chéng 宣城 (modern Anhuī). Late-Míng bibliophile, dramatist, and editor; son of the official Méi Shǒu-dé 梅守德 and elder relation of the great essayist Méi Yáo-chén's 梅堯臣 line of the same prefecture. He famously declined an examination career to devote himself to literature and to his library *Tiān-yì gé* 天逸閣. He produced the *qū* drama *Yù-hé jì* 玉合記, the historical compendium *Cái-guǐ jí* 才鬼集, and a major series of pre-Táng anthologies — *Hàn Wèi shī chéng* 漢魏詩乘, *Gǔ yuè-yuàn* 古樂苑, *Sì-cháo shī jí* 四朝詩集, and the substantial editorial achievement *Wén-jì lù* 文紀錄 (collecting prose by period from antiquity through the Liù-cháo). In this Sòng-collection context he is the editor responsible for assembling Wú Qián's 吳潛 *Lǚ-zhāi yí-gǎo* 履齋遺稿 [[KR4d0330]], which the *Sìkù tíyào* judges to be a careful — if at points imperfect — Míng reconstruction of a now-lost Sòng original.
