Gù Dònggāo 顧棟高 (1679–1759), *zì* Zhèncāng 震滄, also styled Fùchū 復初, was a Kāngxī-Qiánlóng-period scholar and *Chūnqiū* / *Shī* specialist, native of Wúxī 無錫 (Cháng-zhōu 常州 prefecture, modern Jiāngsū 江蘇).

He passed the *jǔrén* in *Kāngxī* 59 *gēng-zǐ* (1720) and was appointed *Nèigé zhōng-shū-shè-rén* 內閣中書舍人 (Drafter of the Grand Secretariat). In *Qiánlóng* 16 *xīn-wèi* (1751) he was nominated by special imperial recommendation under the *jīng-xué* (classical learning) summons and appointed *Guó-zǐ-jiān sī-yè* 國子監司業 (Vice-Director of the Imperial Academy); subsequently honored with the title of *Guó-zǐ-jiān jì-jiǔ* 國子監祭酒 (Director of the Imperial Academy). He died at home.

His most famous work is the *Chūnqiū dà-shì biǎo* 春秋大事表 (Tabulation of Major Events of the Chūnqiū period; printed in 1748) in 50 *juǎn* — the standard pre-modern systematic tabulation of the political, geographical, genealogical, and institutional state of the Spring-and-Autumn-period feudal world; he also wrote a *Bǔ Shàngshū* 補尚書 (Supplements to the *Shàngshū*) and a substantial body of poetry. His *Shī*-class works in the Kanripo corpus are the *Máo Shī lèi shì* 毛詩類釋 ([[KR1c0060]]) in 21 *juǎn* with a 3-*juǎn* continuation — completed in *Qiánlóng* 17 *rén-shēn* (1752) and the continuation in *Qiánlóng* 18 *guǐ-yǒu* (1753).

CBDB id 66061 confirms 1679–1759. The standard reference for his *Chūnqiū* work is Wilkinson, *Chinese History: A New Manual*, where the *Chūnqiū dà-shì biǎo* is identified as compiled by "the classical scholar Gu Donggao 顧棟高 (1679–1759) and printed in 1748".
