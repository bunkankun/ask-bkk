**Jiǎ Suǒ-xué** 賈所學 (*zì* Jiǔ-sī 九思, *fl.* late Míng – early Qīng, Yáng-zhōu) — late-Míng / early-Qīng physician and editor; recipient and custodian of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jǐng-yuè's manuscript of the *Jǐng-yuè quán-shū* 景岳全書 ([[KR3er073]]). Jiǎ saw the work into print posthumously; the *editio princeps* (1700, *Kāng-xī* 39) was finalised by Jiǎ's grandson **Lǔ Chāo** 魯超. Author of the principal publication-preface to the work. Not in CBDB.
