**Xǔ Shèn** 許慎 (c. 58 – c. 147), *zì* Shū zhòng 叔重, was the great Eastern-Hàn lexicographer, author of the ***Shuō wén jiě zì*** 說文解字 — **the foundational Chinese etymological dictionary** (completed 100 CE, presented to the throne in 121). Also traditionally credited with a commentary on the ***Huái nán zǐ*** 淮南子 ([[KR5f0018]]), though this attribution is insecure and the surviving "Xǔ commentary" may represent a composite Hàn commentarial tradition.
