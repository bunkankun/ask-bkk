# Yóu Tóng 尤侗 (1618–1704)

*Zì* Zhǎn-chéng 展成. *Hào* Xī-táng lǎo-rén 西堂老人; also Huǐ-ān 悔庵. Originally of *Liáng-xī* (Wú-xī) but the family had moved to *Wú-jùn* (Sūzhōu); held himself a descendant of Yóu Mào [[尤袤]] of the *Liáng-xī* Yóu line. Selected as a *Bó-xué hóng-cí* (Broadly-Learned and Vastly-Erudite) of *Kāng-xī* 18 (1679); appointed *Hàn-lín-yuàn jiǎn-tǎo* and *shì-jiǎng* — a participant in the *Kāng-xī*-era *Míng shǐ* compilation. A leading figure of early-Qīng Sūzhōu literary culture; author of the *Xī-táng zá-zǔ* and the dramas *Diào Pí-pa* 弔琵琶, *Hēi bái wèi* 黑白衛, and others.

His relevance to KR4d is editorial: in the early Kāng-xī era he gathered the surviving fragments of his ancestor Yóu Mào's literary collection from miscellaneous sources, edited them into 2 *juǎn*, and put them into print as *Liáng-xī yí-gǎo* [[KR4d0229]] — preserving the only surviving substantial fragment of the literary corpus of one of the four great Southern-Sòng poets.

(The principal subject of this person note is the editor of [[KR4d0229]]. His own substantial literary corpus is later than the KR4d horizon and not catalogued in the Kanripo KR4d division.)
