# Yóu Tóng 尤侗 (1618–1704)

*Zì* Zhǎnchéng 展成. *Hào* Xītáng lǎorén 西堂老人; also Huǐān 悔庵. Originally of *Liángxī* (Wúxī) but the family had moved to *Wújùn* (Sūzhōu); held himself a descendant of Yóu Mào [[尤袤]] of the *Liángxī* Yóu line. Selected as a *Bóxué hóngcí* (Broadly-Learned and Vastly-Erudite) of *Kāngxī* 18 (1679); appointed *Hànlínyuàn jiǎntǎo* and *shìjiǎng* — a participant in the *Kāngxī*-era *Míng shǐ* compilation. A leading figure of early-Qīng Sūzhōu literary culture; author of the *Xītáng zázǔ* and the dramas *Diào Pí-pa* 弔琵琶, *Hēi bái wèi* 黑白衛, and others.

His relevance to KR4d is editorial: in the early Kāngxī era he gathered the surviving fragments of his ancestor Yóu Mào's literary collection from miscellaneous sources, edited them into 2 *juǎn*, and put them into print as *Liángxī yígǎo* [[KR4d0229]] — preserving the only surviving substantial fragment of the literary corpus of one of the four great Southern-Sòng poets.

(The principal subject of this person note is the editor of [[KR4d0229]]. His own substantial literary corpus is later than the KR4d horizon and not catalogued in the Kanripo KR4d division.)
