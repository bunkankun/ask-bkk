Yuán-dynasty Quánzhēn 全真 master and Daozang commentator, *zì* Dàoyuán 道淵, *hào* Hùnránzǐ 混然子. Author of [[KR5a0101|Tàishàng shēngxuán shuō xiāozāi hùmìng miàojīng zhù 太上昇玄說消災護命妙經註]] (DZ 101), a Quánzhēn-school inner-alchemy commentary on the Táng [[KR5a0019|Shēngxuán xiāozāi scripture]]. Wáng's reading deploys the *dàoyòng* 道用 ("the use of the Dào") vs. *shìfǎ* 世法 ("the method of the age") distinction to contrast internal-alchemical interpretation with literal religious recitation — a characteristic Quánzhēn hermeneutic procedure also used by [[陳致虛|Chén Zhìxū]]. His commentary represents the influence of Neo-Confucian *tǐ/yòng* 體/用 dialectics on Yuán Daoist exegesis. No CBDB record was found. Wáng's alchemical vocabulary aligns with the Quánzhēn school's inner-alchemical idiom; he is quoted by Isabelle Robinet and others in Yuán *nèidān* studies.
