Chán-xiū 禪修 (active late Míng), Míng Buddhist scholar-monk; author of [[KR6e0158|*Yī lèng yán jiū jìng shì chàn* 依楞嚴究竟事懺]] (X1478, 2 fasc.) — a Míng-period repentance ritual based on the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra*. Birth and death dates not preserved.
