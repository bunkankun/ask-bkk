# Lǐ Délín 李德林

*Zì* Gōngfǔ 公輔. Native of Bóling 博陵 in Ān-píng 安平 (modern Héběi). Father of [[李百藥]] (Lǐ Bǎiyào, 565–648). Lifedates 532–592.

Career across three dynasties — Northern Qí, Northern Zhōu, Suí. Under Northern Qí he held *Zhōng-shū lìng* 中書令 and was the principal court historian and *zhì-zhào* 制詔 drafter. After the Northern Qí's fall in 577 he served Northern Zhōu and helped Yáng Jiān (the future Suí Wéndì) draft the documents of the dynastic transition; under Suí he was *Nèi-shǐ lìng* 內史令 (the Suí equivalent of *Zhōng-shū lìng*) and one of the chief architects of the Suí law-code revision and bureaucratic reform.

Began the *Běi Qí shū* (KR2a0021) under the Northern Qí itself, continued it under the Suí, but died in 592 with the work unfinished. The unfinished manuscripts passed to his son Lǐ Bǎiyào, who completed and presented them under Tang Tàizōng in *Zhēnguān* 10 (636).

His biographies are in *Suí shū* 42 (KR2a0023) and *Běi shǐ* 72 (KR2a0025).
