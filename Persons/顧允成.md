Gù Yǔnchéng 顧允成 (1554–1607), *zì* Jìshí 季時, of Wúxī 無錫 (Jiāngsū), younger brother of **Gù Xiànchéng** 顧憲成 (cf. [[KR4e0223]]). Passed the metropolitan examination in 1583 but only completed the palace examination in 1586, when his *duìcè* (response-essay) attacking the imperial-favorites placed him in the lowest grade. Officed *Lǐbù zhǔshì*; demoted to *Guāngzhōu zhōupàn* over the *Sānwáng bìngfēng* (Three Princes Joint-Investiture) memorial. After returning to Wúxī, he was a co-founder (with his elder brother and **Gāo Pānlóng**) of the **Dōnglín shūyuàn** in 1604, where he lectured until his death. His collection is the *Xiǎobiànzhāi ǒucún* [[KR4e0224]]. CBDB 34743.
