Yǒng-guāng 永光 (active late Qīng), Buddhist scholar-monk; resident of *Suìníng Guǎngdé-sì* 遂寧廣德寺 (in modern Sìchuān). Per DILA A000281, in *Guāng-xù* 光緒 *yǐ-sì* (1905) he composed the [[KR6e0126|*Dà fāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng sānshí jiǔ pǐn dà yì* 大方廣佛華嚴經三十九品大意]] (X242), and also composed the [[KR6e0125|*Dà fāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng gāng mù guàn shè* 大方廣佛華嚴經綱目貫攝]] (X241). Both are late-Qīng compilations summarising the doctrinal-narrative content of the [[KR6e0010|80-fascicle *Avataṃsaka*]]. Birth and death dates not preserved.
