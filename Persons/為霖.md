# Wèilín 為霖

Wèilín Dàopèi 為霖道霈 (1615–1702), early-Qīng Cáodòng-line Chán master and prolific author, a leading Cáodòng-side respondent to Fèiyǐn [[通容]] Tōngróng's *Wǔdēng yántǒng* lineage reattributions.
