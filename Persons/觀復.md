Guānfù 觀復 (active Sòng), Sòng Buddhist scholar styled *Xiàoān* 笑庵 ("Smile-Hermitage"); author of *[[KR6g0050|Yí jiào jīng lùn jì 遺教經論記]]* (X846, 3 fascicles). Birth and death dates not preserved.
