# Jī Zēngyún 嵇曾筠

*Zì* Sōngyǒu 松友, *hào* Lǐzhāi 禮齋; posthumous title Wénmǐn 文敏. Native of Wúxī 無錫 (modern Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 45 (1706).

Career arc: Hanlin *shùjíshì* 庶吉士 → *biānxiū* 編修 → *xuézhèng* 學政 of Shānxī → Left Associate Censor-in-chief of the Censorate (*Dūcháyuàn zuǒ qiān dū yùshǐ* 都察院左僉都御史, 1723) → Director-general of Yellow River conservancy for Hénán and Shāndōng (*Hénán Shāndōng hédào zǒngdū* 河南山東河道總督, 1729) → *Wénhuádiàn dàxuéshì* 文華殿大學士 兼 *Lìbù shàngshū* 吏部尚書 (1733). In Qiánlóng 1 (1736) appointed Superintendent of the Zhèjiāng Sea-walls (*Zǒnglǐ Zhèjiāng hǎitáng gōngchéng* 總理浙江海塘工程) and concurrent governor-general of the two Zhè 總督. Granted the honorific *Tàizǐ tàifù* 太子太傅; posthumously *Shàobǎo* 少保.

The principal hydraulic engineer of the Yōngzhèng era. The "Jī dam" method (*Jī bà* 嵇壩) on the Yellow River bears his name; he oversaw more than 7,400 *zhàng* of fish-scale stone sea-wall (魚鱗石塘) at the Hǎiníng Jiānshān 海寧尖山 breach on the Qiántáng 錢塘 coast — a defining achievement of YōngzhèngQiánlóng hydraulic governance in Zhèjiāng.

Editorial supervision: *Hénán tōngzhì* 河南通志 in 80 juan; *Zhèjiāng tōngzhì* 浙江通志 ([[KR2k0044]], 280 juan, submitted 1736, supervised *jiānxiū* 監修), the latter undertaken in parallel with his sea-wall duties. Lifedates: 1671–1739 by Western reckoning; Chinese sources sometimes give 1670–1738 by lunar-calendar count.
