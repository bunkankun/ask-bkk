**Itō Daigyō** 伊東大業, Genroku-era Japanese physician; prepared the 1692 (Genroku 5, winter solstice) Edo-era selective edition [[KR3eq044]] *Yī biàn* from [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* [[KR3e0078]] and *Yīlùn biàn*.
