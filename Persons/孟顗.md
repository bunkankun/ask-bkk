# Mèng Yǐ 孟顗

Eastern Jìn official, Inner Historian (內史) of Wú Commandery (吳郡), who together with the Right Guard General [[褚叔度]] Chǔ Shūdù served as principal lay donor (檀越, *dānapati*) for [[佛馱跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra's 418–420 translation of the 60-fascicle *[[KR6e0001|Dà fāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng 大方廣佛華嚴經]]* at the Dàochǎngsì 道場寺 in Jiànkāng. His patronage is recorded in the closing colophon of the work. Lifedates unrecorded.
