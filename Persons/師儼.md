# Shīyǎn 師儼

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué; recorded as the section-compiler of juan 9 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
