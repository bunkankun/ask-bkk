Hé Yóu 何游 (*zì* Dàn-ān 澹安, CBDB 263567) — Jiāng-nán physician of the early-to-mid nineteenth century in the Sūzhōu / Wú-zhōng post-Yè Tiān-shì 葉天士 ([[葉桂]]) clinical tradition. He is conventionally identified with the Hé family of Méng-hé 孟河 in Jiāngsū — one of the four principal Méng-hé medical lineages (Fèi 費, Mǎ 馬, Cháo 巢, Hé 何) systematically reconstructed in Volker Scheid's *Currents of Tradition in Chinese Medicine 1626–2006* (Eastland Press, 2007). Some sources identify him as the elder of the Hé brothers, but the genealogical data are not secure and his exact lifedates are unknown. His casebook is [[KR3ep005]] *Hé Dàn-ān yī-àn* 何澹安醫案. CBDB records the name but no dates.
