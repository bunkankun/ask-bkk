Zhōu Chén-kē 周沈珂 (fl. *Kāng-xī*, late 17th century), Qing-period descendant (*yì-sūn* 裔孫) of Zhōu Dūn-yí 周敦頤 [[周敦頤]] (1017–1073). In *Kāng-xī* he re-cut the *Zhōu Yuán-gōng jí* 周元公集 [[KR4d0065]] — re-issuing the Míng *Jiā-jìng* edition of Wáng Huì-céng 王會曾 of *Zhāng-pǔ*, with the appended *Yí-fāng jí* 遺芳集 in 5 *juǎn* (a *yì-sūn*-compiled collection of his Sòng-and-later descendants' works and biographies, separately re-categorized into the *zǒng-jí* category by the *Sìkù* editors).
