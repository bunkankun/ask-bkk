Zhōu Chénkē 周沈珂 (fl. *Kāngxī*, late 17th century), Qing-period descendant (*yìsūn* 裔孫) of Zhōu Dūnyí 周敦頤 [[周敦頤]] (1017–1073). In *Kāngxī* he re-cut the *Zhōu Yuángōng jí* 周元公集 [[KR4d0065]] — re-issuing the Míng *Jiājìng* edition of Wáng Huìcéng 王會曾 of *Zhāngpǔ*, with the appended *Yífāng jí* 遺芳集 in 5 *juǎn* (a *yìsūn*-compiled collection of his Sòng-and-later descendants' works and biographies, separately re-categorized into the *zǒngjí* category by the *Sìkù* editors).
