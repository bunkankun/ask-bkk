Late-Yuán *jìnshì* and early-Míng *Guó-zǐ jì-jiǔ* 國子祭酒 (Master of the Imperial College), native of **Huá-xiàn** 滑縣 (Hénán). *Zì* Zhòng-mǐn 仲敏; *hào* Xī-yǐn 西隱 ("Western Retreat") from his villa *Bái-yún máo-wū* 白雲茅屋. *Jìnshì* under the Zhì-zhèng 至正 reign of the Yuán; briefly served as *Yán-shān yǐn* 鹽山尹 before resigning and returning home during the late-Yuán disorders. In the early Hóngwǔ era summoned to the capital as *Guó-zǐ zhù-jiào* 國子助教; promoted to *Wén-yuān-gé dà-xué-shì* 文淵閣大學士; eventually appointed *Guó-zǐ jì-jiǔ* (the head of the imperial university) and died in office in Hóngwǔ 23 (1390) at age 80. Famous for the strictness of his pedagogical discipline; his *Tài-xué bēi* 太學碑 [National-University Stele], composed by imperial command, was greatly admired by Hóngwǔ. Posthumous title **Wén-kè** 文恪 granted in the Zhèngdé period (1506–1521). His son **Sòng Lín** 宋麟 succeeded him as *Guó-zǐ jì-jiǔ*. Standard biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 137; Goodrich & Fang, *Dictionary of Ming Biography* 2:1218–1219. Collected works *Xī-yǐn jí* 西隱集 at [[KR4e0009]]. Notable also for the nineteen poems on the old Yuán palace at Dà-dū, in *yí-mín* register (*Shǔ-lí*-*Mài-xiù* sentiment), preserved in the *Xī-yǐn jí*.
