Late-Yuán *jìnshì* and early-Míng *Guózǐ jìjiǔ* 國子祭酒 (Master of the Imperial College), native of **Huáxiàn** 滑縣 (Hénán). *Zì* Zhòngmǐn 仲敏; *hào* Xīyǐn 西隱 ("Western Retreat") from his villa *Báiyún máowū* 白雲茅屋. *Jìnshì* under the Zhìzhèng 至正 reign of the Yuán; briefly served as *Yánshān yǐn* 鹽山尹 before resigning and returning home during the late-Yuán disorders. In the early Hóngwǔ era summoned to the capital as *Guózǐ zhùjiào* 國子助教; promoted to *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* 文淵閣大學士; eventually appointed *Guózǐ jìjiǔ* (the head of the imperial university) and died in office in Hóngwǔ 23 (1390) at age 80. Famous for the strictness of his pedagogical discipline; his *Tàixué bēi* 太學碑 [National-University Stele], composed by imperial command, was greatly admired by Hóngwǔ. Posthumous title **Wénkè** 文恪 granted in the Zhèngdé period (1506–1521). His son **Sòng Lín** 宋麟 succeeded him as *Guózǐ jìjiǔ*. Standard biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 137; Goodrich & Fang, *Dictionary of Ming Biography* 2:1218–1219. Collected works *Xīyǐn jí* 西隱集 at [[KR4e0009]]. Notable also for the nineteen poems on the old Yuán palace at Dàdū, in *yímín* register (*Shǔlí*-*Màixiù* sentiment), preserved in the *Xīyǐn jí*.
