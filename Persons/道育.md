# Dàoyù 道育

Dàoyù 道育, early-6th-century Chinese monk, named alongside [[慧可|Huìkě]] in Tánlín 曇琳's preface to the *Èrrù sìxíng lùn* [[KR6q0112]] as one of the two principal disciples of [[菩提達磨|Bodhidharma]]. Practically nothing else is known of him; in later tradition Dàoyù is overshadowed by Huìkě as the recognised second patriarch.
