**Xuē Yúsī** 薛漁思 (fl. mid-to-late Táng dynasty, probably 9th century CE) is the compiler of the *chuánqí* and *zhìguài* collection *Hédōng Jì* 河東記 [[KR4k0129]]. CBDB id 95069; CBDB records no birth or death years, and no floruit dates. Nothing is known of his biography beyond his authorship of this collection; he does not appear in the standard Táng histories. The toponym Hédōng 河東 in the collection's title suggests a possible connection to the Shānxī region, though this is uncertain. The stories in *Hédōng Jì* reference Táng reign periods of the Bǎoyìng 寶應 (762–763) and Zhēnyuán 貞元 (785–805) eras, suggesting the compiler was active in the ninth century.
