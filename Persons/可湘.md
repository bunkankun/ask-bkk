# Kě-xiāng 可湘

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì (Pò-ān *pài*) Chán master, *hào* Jué-àn 絕岸 ("Cut-Bank"), also Xuě-fēng Xiāng 雪峯湘. Surname Gě 葛; native of Níng-hǎi 寧海 in Táizhōu. Per DILA A003948: 1206 – Zhìyuán 27 (1290), *shì* 85.

Principal dharma-heir of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249). Held eight successive abbacies — Jiāxīng Liú-hóng Xīng-shèng (entered 1253), Wēnzhōu Yàn-shān Néng-rén, Yuèzhōu Jiǔ-yán Huì-yún, Tiāntái Hùguó Guǎng-ēn, Lín'ān Chóng-ēn Yǎn-fú, Wēnzhōu Jiāng-xīn Lóngxiáng Xīngqìng, and twice at Fúzhōu Xuě-fēng Chóng-shèng (from 1272, ten years). Retired to Bǎo-shòu-sì 寶壽寺 in Hángzhōu. Dharma-heirs include [[妙恩]] Duàn-yá Miào-ēn (abbot of Quánzhōu Kāi-yuán-sì and principal editor of this master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0317]]), Yún-sǒu Cóng 雲叟從, and Duàn-yá Ēn 斷崖恩.
