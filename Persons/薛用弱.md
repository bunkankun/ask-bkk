Xuē Yòng-ruò 薛用弱 (*zì* Zhòng-shèng 仲勝, *fl.* early 9th c.), mid-Táng scholar-official and author of the *Jí-yì jì* 集異記 [[KR3l0104]]. The opening of his book gives his *jùn-wàng* (ancestral commanderie) as Hé-dōng 河東 (modern southern Shānxī); his place of birth is unknown, and the *Sìkù* tiyao explicitly notes that Táng *shì-zú* customarily used *jùn-wàng* designations rather than birth-places, so the Hé-dōng tag indicates only family origin. The *Xīn Táng shū Yì-wén zhì* records that he served as Prefect of Guāng-zhōu 光州 (in modern southern Hé-nán) during the *Cháng-qìng* 長慶 reign-period (821–824) of Táng Mù-zōng 穆宗; no other office or biographical detail is recorded. The dates of his birth and death are unknown; his floruit is fixed only by the Guāng-zhōu prefecture appointment. CBDB id 95051 (his name appears in BIOG_MAIN without dates). His single surviving work, the *Jí-yì jì*, is one of the principal sources for Táng-Xuán-zōng anecdotal lore: it preserves the canonical versions of Wáng Wéi 王維's *Yù-lún páo* 鬱輪袍, the Wáng Zhī-huàn 王之渙 / Wáng Chāng-líng 王昌齡 / Gāo Shì 高適 *Qí-tíng huà-bì* 旗亭畫壁 wineshop-wager, Dí Rén-jié 狄仁傑's refusal of the *Jí-cuì* 集翠 kingfisher-feather robe, and the weiqi-master Wáng Jī-xīn 王積薪's overhearing the *fù-gū* 婦姑 (mother- and daughter-in-law) blind-weiqi game in the Mín-shān 岷山 cottage.
