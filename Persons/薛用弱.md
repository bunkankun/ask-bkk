Xuē Yòngruò 薛用弱 (*zì* Zhòngshèng 仲勝, *fl.* early 9th c.), mid-Táng scholar-official and author of the *Jíyì jì* 集異記 [[KR3l0104]]. The opening of his book gives his *jùnwàng* (ancestral commanderie) as Hédōng 河東 (modern southern Shānxī); his place of birth is unknown, and the *Sìkù* tiyao explicitly notes that Táng *shìzú* customarily used *jùnwàng* designations rather than birth-places, so the Hédōng tag indicates only family origin. The *Xīn Táng shū Yìwén zhì* records that he served as Prefect of Guāngzhōu 光州 (in modern southern Hénán) during the *Chángqìng* 長慶 reign-period (821–824) of Táng Mùzōng 穆宗; no other office or biographical detail is recorded. The dates of his birth and death are unknown; his floruit is fixed only by the Guāngzhōu prefecture appointment. CBDB id 95051 (his name appears in BIOG_MAIN without dates). His single surviving work, the *Jíyì jì*, is one of the principal sources for TángXuánzōng anecdotal lore: it preserves the canonical versions of Wáng Wéi 王維's *Yùlún páo* 鬱輪袍, the Wáng Zhīhuàn 王之渙 / Wáng Chānglíng 王昌齡 / Gāo Shì 高適 *Qítíng huàbì* 旗亭畫壁 wineshop-wager, Dí Rénjié 狄仁傑's refusal of the *Jícuì* 集翠 kingfisher-feather robe, and the weiqi-master Wáng Jīxīn 王積薪's overhearing the *fùgū* 婦姑 (mother- and daughter-in-law) blind-weiqi game in the Mínshān 岷山 cottage.
