Ài Tóng 愛同 (also Shì Ài-tóng 釋愛同, fl. early Tang) was a Vinaya specialist who is recorded in **Zànníng's** 贊寧 *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* 宋高僧傳 (T50n2061, j. 14) as a transmitter of the Mahīśāsaka tradition. His principal extant work is the **Míshāsāi jié-mó-běn** 彌沙塞羯磨本 ([[KR6k0005]]), the *karmavācanā* extract from the *Wǔfēn lǜ* ([[KR6k0001]]) reformatted as a one-fascicle handbook for the conduct of monastic legal acts. The compilation is dated to the 7th century on the basis of his entry in the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn*.
