Dé-fēng 德風 (also styled **Qīng-lóng Dé-fēng** 青龍德風 from his abbacy at **Hù-guó chán-yuàn** 護國禪院 / **Qīng-lóng-ān** 青龍庵). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎi-chī Xíng-yuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662) — same teacher as **Lián-fēng Sù** [[素]]. Born **明天啟二年壬戌 9/4 丑時** = **1622-10-08** Gregorian. Death year unknown — still active and extending the *yǔ-lù* into Kāngxī era. Native of **Shàng-yáng** 上洋 (= Sōngjiāng / Shànghǎi area, Chuān-shā 川沙); lay surname **Gù** 顧.

**Formation.** **順治乙酉** = **1645** sought tonsure under **Dìng-shēng** 定生 of **Fú-tián-sì** 福田寺, but the teacher returned to lay life within five days; Dé-fēng moved to **Xīn-róng** 心融. Within three months war broke out; took refuge at **Jīn-zé chán-táng** 金澤禪堂. While listening to a *Śūraṅgama* lecture, the phrase 空如來藏 ignited his *yí-qíng* 疑情. Sequential *cān*: **Tāo-míng** 韜明 at Lóng-huá; **Jù-dé** 具德 at Lù-zhí 甪直; **Héng** 恒 at Nán-xiáng Bái-hè-sì 南翔白鶴寺; **Chún-pò** 純朴 at Qī-zhēn 棲真. Final *fù-fǎ* under **Bǎi-chī** at **Yún-jiān Chāo-guǒ-sì** 雲間超果寺.

**Abbacies.** **Wǔ-hú Bō-rě** 鵡湖般若 (formally entered **戊戌 12/12** = **1659-01-04**, but accepted invitation in **丁酉正月初三** = **1657-02-15**); **Hù-guó chán-yuàn** 護國禪院; smaller *jīng-shè* at **Lú-xī** 蘆溪 (an early three-year retreat) and **Wèi-táng** 魏塘.

**Work.** [[KR6q0531]] 《德風禪師般若語錄》 6 juan, J38 B411, compiled by his attendant *shì-zhě* **Rú-xīng** [[如惺]] *děng biān*. Prefaced **康熙二十八年八月朔** = **1689-09-14** by lay disciple **Shī-fēng jū-shì Shěn Zōng-jìng** 獅峰居士沈宗敬. Juan 6 contains an autobiographical *xíng-yóu* 行繇 — the principal source for his life.
