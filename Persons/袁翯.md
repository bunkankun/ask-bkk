# 袁翯

Yuán Hè (taboo-name 諱翯; *Hègōng* 翯公), early-Qiánlóng-era ancestor of [[袁仁賢]] Yuán Rénxián; received a *Qiánlóngcháo* imperial 神醫 plaque during the Jiāng-Huái plague. The family's transmitted medical manuscript *Yīxué yīguàn* 醫學一貫 underlies [[KR3em034]] *Hóukē jīnyuè quánshū* 喉科金鑰全書.
