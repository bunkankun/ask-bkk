# Jiǎng Shènzhāi 蔣慎齋

Early-Qīng official; recommended the Húběi paediatrician and essayist [[程雲鵬]] Chéng Yúnpéng to [[張希良]] Zhāng Xīliáng during the latter's tenure as Provincial Education Officer (*sīduó* 司鐸) of Jiāngxià 江夏, an introduction reflected in Zhāng's preface to Chéng's *Cíyòu xīnshū* 慈幼新書 ([[KR3ej022]]). Full given name and lifedates unrecorded.
