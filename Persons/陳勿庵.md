**Chén Wùān** 陳勿庵 (*fl.* mid–late 18th c.) — Qīng physician, teacher of [[何榮]] Hé Róng (Xìngyuán), who transmitted to him the manuscript of *Tāichǎn mìshū* ([[KR3ei054]]). May or may not be identical with [[陳笏庵]] Chén Hùān, the named author of the printed work — the two studio-names differ by a single *hào* character and may represent confusion in the late-18th-century manuscript transmission. Not in CBDB.
