**Shēn Shíxíng** 申時行 (明). Late-Míng Senior Grand Secretary (1535–1614), Wànlì-era chief councillor of state; not directly the surgical author Shēn Gǒngchén, but kinsman cited in [[KR3ek021]] *Wàikē qǐxuán*.
