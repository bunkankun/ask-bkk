**Fù Chá** 傅察 (1089–1126), *zì* Gōng-huì 公晦, of Mèng-zhōu 孟州 (Hé-nán). *Jìnshì* 1109. Held office to *Bīng-bù yuán-wài láng*. In *Xuān-hé* 7 / 1125, dispatched as *jiē-bàn-shǐ* (envoy to receive Jin embassy) on the eve of the *Jìng-kāng* invasion. Encountering the Jin invasion army on the road, Fù was captured; he refused to bow to the Jin commander Zhān-hàn 粘罕 (Wányán Zōng-hàn 完顏宗翰), repeatedly cursed him, and was killed. Posthumously canonised *Zhōng-sù* 忠肅 (Loyal-and-Solemn). Author of *Zhōng-sù jí* 忠肅集 [[KR4d0134]] in 3 *juǎn*, edited by his son Fù Bó-shòu 傅伯壽. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 446 (Zhōng-yì 1). Catalog confirms 1089–1126.
