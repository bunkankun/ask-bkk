**Fù Chá** 傅察 (1089–1126), *zì* Gōnghuì 公晦, of Mèngzhōu 孟州 (Hénán). *Jìnshì* 1109. Held office to *Bīngbù yuánwài láng*. In *Xuānhé* 7 / 1125, dispatched as *jiēbànshǐ* (envoy to receive Jin embassy) on the eve of the *Jìngkāng* invasion. Encountering the Jin invasion army on the road, Fù was captured; he refused to bow to the Jin commander Zhānhàn 粘罕 (Wányán Zōnghàn 完顏宗翰), repeatedly cursed him, and was killed. Posthumously canonised *Zhōngsù* 忠肅 (Loyal-and-Solemn). Author of *Zhōngsù jí* 忠肅集 [[KR4d0134]] in 3 *juǎn*, edited by his son Fù Bóshòu 傅伯壽. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 446 (Zhōngyì 1). Catalog confirms 1089–1126.
