Xiāngōng 先公 — literally "the Former Gentleman" or "Elder Sir" — is a translator known only by this designation, attested in the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145) and subsequent canonical bibliographies as the translator of two short single-fascicle excerpts from the *Samādhirāja-sūtra*: [[KR6i0277]] *Fó-shuō yuèdēng sānmèi jīng* (T640) and [[KR6i0278]] *Fó-shuō yuèdēng sānmèi jīng* (T641). The catalogs class him in the Liú-Sòng era (420–479), but no other biographical information has been recovered. Some later bibliographical attempts at identification (e.g. with Xiān-gōng-zhú 先公竺) are speculative and not accepted in modern scholarship. Both of his surviving translations are partial Chinese versions of the same Indic *Samādhirāja* underlying the much fuller [[KR6i0276|T639]] (Narendrayaśas, Northern Qí 557–559).
