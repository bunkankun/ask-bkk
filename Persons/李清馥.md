Lǐ Qīngfù 李清馥 (born 1703; death year not in CBDB), zì Gēnhóu 根侯, of *Ānxī* 安溪 (Quánzhōu 泉州 prefecture, Fújiàn). Grandson of the great KāngxīYōngzhèng *Cheng-Zhū* synthesizer and Grand Secretary [[李光地]] (1642–1718) and son of Lǐ Zhōnglún 李鍾倫 (who died young). Following the posthumous reduction of his grandfather's rank he was awarded the position of Vice-Director in the Ministry of War (*Bīngbùyuánwàiláng* 兵部員外郎) by hereditary privilege, and rose eventually to be Magistrate of *Guǎngpíngfǔ* 廣平府 (in modern Héběi). His scholarly position is strictly *Cheng-Zhū*: trained at his grandfather's side, he absorbed the *Ānxī* school's distinctive synthesis (which had quietly absorbed elements from the *Yáojiāng* tradition) but emphasized lineage-and-classics fidelity. Beginning in 1742 (after a period of illness) he began collecting materials, which by 1749 had become his preface-launched *Mǐnzhōng shīyǒu yuānyuán kǎo* 閩中師友淵源考 (later renamed *Mǐnzhōng lǐxué yuānyuán kǎo* 閩中理學淵源考, [[KR2g0052]]) — a 92-*juàn* prosopographical lineage-tracing of the Cheng-Zhū tradition through its Fújiàn (Mǐnzhōng) descent from Yáng Shí 楊時 to the late Míng. He also compiled *Fùzhāi yǔlù* 復齋語錄 / *Yǒuzhì xué lù* 有志學錄.
