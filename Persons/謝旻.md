Xiè Mín 謝旻 (b. unknown, d. 1751), native of Wǔjìn 武進 (Cháng-Zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). Career in the Yōngzhèng provincial bureaucracy: *bùzhèngshǐ* of Húnán, then Jiāngxī governor (Yōngzhèng 6 = 1728 to Yōngzhèng 10 = 1732), in which capacity he supervised the *Jiāngxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0043]]) from 1730 to its presentation in 1732. Subsequently *zǒngdū* of Yún-Guì briefly. CBDB id 59402; Wǔjìn (index_addr 7530) confirmed; death year per *Qīngdài rénwù shēng-zú niánbiǎo*. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* gives his biography in passing in the Jiāngxī officials' table; no separate *liè-zhuàn*.
