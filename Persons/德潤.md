# Dérùn 德潤

A Qīng-era *hòuxué* 後學 ("later-student") disciple of the Chán master [[普能嵩]] **Pǔnéng Sōng**. He is named in the colophon of the **《普能嵩禪師淨土詩》** *Pǔnéng Sōng chánshī jìngtǔ shī* [[KR6p0133]] (X1215) as the respectful recorder (*jìng lù* 敬錄) of his master's Pure Land verses. Lifedates and other biographical details not preserved.
