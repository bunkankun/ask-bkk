**Táng Qiān-qǐng** 唐千頃 (*fl.* 18th c. 清), Qīng-era physician. Compiled the *Dà-shēng yào-zhǐ* 大生要旨 (Essential Points for Vigorous Life), a popular fertility-and-childbirth manual that became one of the most-reprinted Qīng obstetrical-fertility works. Subsequent editors continued and supplemented his text; the *Xù-zēng dà-shēng yào-zhǐ* 續增大生要旨 ([[KR3ei075]]) — *Continued and Augmented* — represents the late-Qīng supplement. The Hinrichs & Barnes (2013) reference index entry "(*Dasheng yaozhi*)" places the *Dà-shēng yào-zhǐ* genre alongside the *Dáshēng biān* as one of the principal Qīng popular obstetrical handbooks.
