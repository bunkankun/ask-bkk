# Guāntōng 觀通

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yúān Zhìjí (1311–1378); abbot of Ruìguāngsì 瑞光寺, *hào* Xīyuǎn 希遠. Heads the editorial byline of his master's ten-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]); also the intermediary through whom the manuscript reached Sòng Lián (via Qīngliáng Zǐpián) for the Hóngwǔ 8 (1375) preface. Lifedates unrecorded.
