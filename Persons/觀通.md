# Guān-tōng 觀通

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yú-ān Zhì-jí (1311–1378); abbot of Ruì-guāng-sì 瑞光寺, *hào* Xī-yuǎn 希遠. Heads the editorial byline of his master's ten-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]); also the intermediary through whom the manuscript reached Sòng Lián (via Qīng-liáng Zǐ-pián) for the Hóng-wǔ 8 (1375) preface. Lifedates unrecorded.
