# Lǐ Míngfù 李明復

Also called Lǐ Yú 李俞. Style name *Bóyǒng* 伯勇. Native of Hé-yáng 合陽 (modern Shǎn-xī). Active in the Jiā-dìng 嘉定 era (1208–1224) of Sòng Níng-zōng. Career details otherwise unrecoverable except for his enrolment as a *Tài-xué* student (*shàng-shè* 上舍 grade) and a brief biographical mention in Wèi Liǎowēng [[魏了翁]]'s preface to his *Chūnqiū jí yì*.

His one surviving work is the *Chūnqiū jí yì* 春秋集義 ([[KR1e0047]]) — the most ambitious Southern-Sòng *Dàoxué*-school *Chūnqiū* compilation, in 50 juan with a separate *Gāng lǐng* 綱領 in 3 juan. The work assembles the *Chūnqiū* views of the *Dàoxué* lineage from Zhōu Dūnyí through the Chéng brothers and Zhāng Zǎi, with eclectic supplements from Yáng Shí, Xiè Shī, Hú Ānguó, Zhū Xī, and Lǚ Zǔqiān. Wèi Liǎowēng's preface explicitly endorses the work, saying Lǐ "anticipated my heart" — Wèi having himself begun a *Dàoxué Chūnqiū* compilation that he abandoned in favour of Lǐ's.

Older bibliographic records (*Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*) erroneously list a parallel "Wáng Mèngyīng 王夢應 *Chūnqiū jí yì* in 50 juan" alongside Lǐ's. The SKQS *tíyào* on [[KR1e0047]] corrects this: Wáng Mèngyīng was Lǐ's publisher (a junior *Bā-chuān* scholar mentioned in the title-page), not a separate author.

CBDB id 41092 records the name without lifedates.
