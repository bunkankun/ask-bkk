# Dào-zhāng 道彰

Early-Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[昭如]] Hǎi-yìn Zhāo-rú; co-compiler (*jí* 集) of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0331]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
