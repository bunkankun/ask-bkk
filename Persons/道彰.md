# Dàozhāng 道彰

Early-Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[昭如]] Hǎiyìn Zhāorú; co-compiler (*jí* 集) of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0331]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
