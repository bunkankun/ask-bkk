Shěn Dàchéng 沈大成 (1700–1771), Qiánlóng-period Sōngjiāng scholar. Wrote one of the two posthumous postscripts to [[潘思榘]] Pān Sījǔ's *Zhōuyì qiǎn shì* ([[KR1a0152]]).
