The singular Yuán-period 元 imperial Hàn-Confucian *Yùtáng* (Hànlín) official, bridge-figure of Khubilai-and-successor empire-cultural policy, founder of Yuán-imperial-Hàn-Confucian cultural-recovery of southern literati after 1276. Original given-name **Wénhǎi** 文海; taboo-avoided for the Yuán Wǔzōng's *miàohuì* "Hǎishān" 海山 — Chéng adopted his *zì* **Méngqīng** 孟卿 as substitute name in formal usage. *Hào* **Xuělóu** 雪樓 ("Snow-Tower"). Native of **Yǐngzhōu Jīngshān** 郢州京山 (modern Húběi).

**Foundational mission.** Imperial commissioner of the **Zhìyuán 23 (1286) southern-recruitment expedition**: Chéng travelled south by imperial commission to recruit Sòng-remnant scholars into Yuán court service. The mission recovered into Yuán imperial service:
- **Zhào Mèngfǔ** 趙孟頫 [[趙孟頫]] (the great calligrapher-painter-scholar)
- **Yú Jí** 虞集 [[虞集]] (the foundational Yuán-Hànlín scholar)
- And dozens of other major southern literati.

This is the single most-consequential Yuán-Confucian recruitment act.

**Career.** Successive *Hànlín* offices over three decades through the Khubilai → Chéngzōng → Wǔzōng → Rénzōng reigns. Composer of substantial portions of Yuán imperial *zhìzhào* (edicts), *zhìcí* (decree-words), *cèwén* (investiture-texts), *jìwén* (sacrificial-texts), *zhāiyì* (vegetarian-feast intentions), *qīngcí* (blue-words for Daoist rituals), *biǎo* (memorials), and *zhìyǔ* (offerings-words). The body of his *Chìcì bēi* (imperially-commissioned steles) for senior Mongol-Khangli aristocrats and Hàn-Confucian elders forms the largest single Yuán-imperial epigraphic-biography corpus preserved through manuscript transmission.
