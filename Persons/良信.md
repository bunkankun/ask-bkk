Japanese (Edo-period) Buddhist monk; lifedates not preserved (DILA A000536). Active in the early 18th century at *Pútíān* 菩提菴 in Nánjīng Xīshān 南京西山 (i.e. one of the Edo *Tōzai-Yamabe* sub-temples conventionally identified as a *Yamato* foundation under that classical-Chinese style). Produced the *huìběn* 會本 (interleaved-source-and-commentary edition) of Sòng Yuánzhào's *Shìmén zhāngfú yí yìngfǎ jì* ([[KR6k0214]], X1097), with a 1713 (Shōtoku 3) preface by his colleague *Dànzhēn* 憺真 of the same monastery. The collation belongs to the broader Edo Vinaya-revival movement (Tōdai-ji *Kanjō* circle, *Jiun Sonja* 慈雲尊者) that recovered and reprinted Sòng *Língzhīsì* texts.
