Liú Yìngshí 劉應時 (*zì* Liángzuǒ 良佐, *hào* Yí'ān 頤菴), of *Sìmíng* 四明 (modern Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Southern-Sòng minor poet of modest official standing whose work was endorsed in turn by Fàn Chéngdà [[范成大]], Lù Yóu [[陸游]] (preface dated *Qìngyuán* 6 = 1200), and Yáng Wànlǐ [[楊萬里]]. Lifedates unknown. CBDB id 33596 (no further data). Author of [[KR4d0272]] *Yí'ān jūshì jí*.
