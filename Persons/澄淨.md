Chéngjìng 澄淨 was a Tang-period Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng commentator, co-author with [[清素]] Qīngsù of the 32-fascicle *Yúqié shī dì lùn yìyǎn* 瑜伽師地論義演 ([[KR6n0015]]) on the first 32 *juǎn* of [[KR6n0001]] *Yogācārabhūmi*. Like Qīngsù, no biographical material survives independently of the work; he is to be dated to the late-eighth or early-ninth century on the basis of the same internal evidence. The work is preserved through the Jin-dynasty Guǎngshèng-sì 廣勝寺 manuscript tradition recovered in the twentieth century.
