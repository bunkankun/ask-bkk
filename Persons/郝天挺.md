**Hǎo Tiān-tǐng** 郝天挺 (1247–1313), *zì* **Jì-xiān** 繼先. Native of **Líng-chuān** 陵川 in **Zé-zhōu** 澤州 (Shān-xī). Disciple (*mén-rén*) of **Yuán Hǎo-wèn** ([[元好問]]). Pupils' student of the older Hǎo Tiān-tǐng (*zì* Jìn-qīng 晉卿, 1161–1217), Yuán Hǎo-wèn's own teacher and grandfather of the famous Hǎo Jīng 郝經.

**Career.** Held office under the early Yuán as **Píng-zhāng Zhèng-shì** of the *Hé-nán Xíng-shěng* 河南行省 (Hé-nán Branch Secretariat) and was posthumously enfeoffed **Jì-guó-gōng** 冀國公, with the canonical title **Wén-dìng** 文定. He compiled the **Yún-nán shí-lù** 雲南實錄 in 5 *juǎn*.

**Tang-shi guchui.** Hǎo Tiān-tǐng's celebrated achievement is the **annotation** of his teacher Yuán Hǎo-wèn's *Táng-shī gǔ-chuī* 唐詩鼓吹 [[KR4h0070]] — an anthology of 596 Táng heptasyllabic regulated-verse poems by 96 poets. The commentary, **simple but accurate** (per the Sìkù editors), confines itself to identifying allusions without speculation. The work was first cut at the *Jiāng-Zhè Rú-sī* in *Zhì-dà* 1 (*wù-shēn*, 1308) under Hǎo's lifetime supervision, with prefaces by **Yáo Suì** 姚燧 and **Wǔ Yī-chāng** 武一昌**.

**Homonym confusion.** Most catalogues confuse Hǎo Tiān-tǐng (b. 1247) with the older **Hǎo Tiān-tǐng** *zì* Jìn-qīng (1161–1217, CBDB 29519 / 690353), Yuán Hǎo-wèn's *teacher* (not pupil) and grandfather of Hǎo Jīng. The *Jīn-shǐ Yǐn-yì zhuàn* records the older Jìn-qīng. The SKQS editors carefully distinguish the two — citing Wáng Shì-zhēn's *Chí-běi ǒu-tán* and the *Yuán-shǐ Hǎo Jīng zhuàn* — and identify the annotator of the *Táng-shī gǔ-chuī* as the **younger** Hǎo Tiān-tǐng (*zì* Jì-xiān).

**Hǎo Tiān-tǐng** 郝天挺 (1161–1217), *zì* **Jìn-qīng** 晉卿. Native of **Líng-chuān** 陵川 (Zé-zhōu, Shān-xī). Grandfather of the major Yuán scholar **Hǎo Jīng** 郝經 (1223–1275). Teacher of **Yuán Hǎo-wèn** ([[元好問]], who entered his school as a boy). Early in life weakened by illness, he disliked the examination system and refused all *jǔ-yè* (examination-prep) after the first attempt. *Jīn-shǐ Yǐn-yì zhuàn* (j. 127) provides his biography. He had no extant collection. CBDB person 29519 / 690353.
