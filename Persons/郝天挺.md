**Hǎo Tiāntǐng** 郝天挺 (1247–1313), *zì* **Jìxiān** 繼先. Native of **Língchuān** 陵川 in **Zézhōu** 澤州 (Shānxī). Disciple (*ménrén*) of **Yuán Hǎowèn** ([[元好問]]). Pupils' student of the older Hǎo Tiāntǐng (*zì* Jìnqīng 晉卿, 1161–1217), Yuán Hǎowèn's own teacher and grandfather of the famous Hǎo Jīng 郝經.

**Career.** Held office under the early Yuán as **Píngzhāng Zhèngshì** of the *Hénán Xíngshěng* 河南行省 (Hénán Branch Secretariat) and was posthumously enfeoffed **Jìguógōng** 冀國公, with the canonical title **Wéndìng** 文定. He compiled the **Yúnnán shílù** 雲南實錄 in 5 *juǎn*.

**Tang-shi guchui.** Hǎo Tiāntǐng's celebrated achievement is the **annotation** of his teacher Yuán Hǎowèn's *Tángshī gǔchuī* 唐詩鼓吹 [[KR4h0070]] — an anthology of 596 Táng heptasyllabic regulated-verse poems by 96 poets. The commentary, **simple but accurate** (per the Sìkù editors), confines itself to identifying allusions without speculation. The work was first cut at the *Jiāng-Zhè Rúsī* in *Zhìdà* 1 (*wùshēn*, 1308) under Hǎo's lifetime supervision, with prefaces by **Yáo Suì** 姚燧 and **Wǔ Yīchāng** 武一昌**.

**Homonym confusion.** Most catalogues confuse Hǎo Tiāntǐng (b. 1247) with the older **Hǎo Tiāntǐng** *zì* Jìnqīng (1161–1217, CBDB 29519 / 690353), Yuán Hǎowèn's *teacher* (not pupil) and grandfather of Hǎo Jīng. The *Jīnshǐ Yǐnyì zhuàn* records the older Jìnqīng. The SKQS editors carefully distinguish the two — citing Wáng Shìzhēn's *Chíběi ǒután* and the *Yuánshǐ Hǎo Jīng zhuàn* — and identify the annotator of the *Tángshī gǔchuī* as the **younger** Hǎo Tiāntǐng (*zì* Jìxiān).

**Hǎo Tiāntǐng** 郝天挺 (1161–1217), *zì* **Jìnqīng** 晉卿. Native of **Língchuān** 陵川 (Zézhōu, Shānxī). Grandfather of the major Yuán scholar **Hǎo Jīng** 郝經 (1223–1275). Teacher of **Yuán Hǎowèn** ([[元好問]], who entered his school as a boy). Early in life weakened by illness, he disliked the examination system and refused all *jǔyè* (examination-prep) after the first attempt. *Jīnshǐ Yǐnyì zhuàn* (j. 127) provides his biography. He had no extant collection. CBDB person 29519 / 690353.
