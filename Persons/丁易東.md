Late-Sòng / early-Yuán 宋-元 scholar of the *Yì* and Daoist studies, *zì* **Hàn-chén** 漢臣, *hào* **Shí-tán** 石潭 ("Stone Pool"), of Wǔ-líng 武陵 (modern Cháng-dé 常德 in Húnán). CBDB id 37940. *Jìnshì* of *Xián-chún* 4 (1268). Career under late Sòng: *Cháo-fèng dàfū* 朝奉大夫, *Tài-fǔ-sì bù* 太府寺簿 (Court of the Imperial Treasury record-keeper) concurrent with *Shū-mì-yuàn biān-xiū-guān* 樞密院編修官 (Bureau of Military Affairs Compiler). Refused service under the Yuán; built the *Shí-tán jīng-shè* 石壇精舍 (Stone-Altar Refined Lodge) at Wǔ-líng and taught students to his death. Documentary references: *Yuán-rén zhuàn-jì zī-liào suǒ-yǐn* 元人傳記資料索引 entry 34.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of:

- [[KR1a0066]] ***Yì xiàng yì*** 易象義 — a 16-juan systematic *xiàng-shù-and-yìlǐ* synthesizer of the *Zhōuyì* (composed 1290–1294 at the *Shí-tán jīng-shè*), giving the most ambitious late-Sòng / early-Yuán articulation of *Yì*-imagery-derivation theory. Methodologically distinctive for two systematic taxonomies: a 12-class image-taking framework and a 12-school *Yì*-tradition taxonomy.
- Co-compiler (with [[劉惟永]] Liú Wéiyǒng and others) of the major Yuán *Dàodé jīng* commentary-anthology [[KR5c0113|DZ 724 *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí yì*]] 道德真經集義 and its introductory [[KR5c0112|DZ 723 *Dà zhǐ*]] 大旨 (1299–1300). His own commentary on the *Lǎozǐ* is cited within DZ 724 under the *hào* "*Shí-tán*."

**Disciple.** **Yú Qīng-zhōng** 喻清中 is identified as a disciple of Dīng Yì-dōng; Yú's postface to DZ 724 mentions his own collaborative commentary composed with his master.

His scholarly career thus combines Confucian *Yì*-canonical scholarship (the *Xiàng yì*, completed 1294) with Daoist textual-tradition compilation (the *Dàodé jīng* anthology, 1299–1300). The combination is one of the cleaner late-Sòng / early-Yuán *sān-jiào hé-yī* (three-teachings-one) intellectual integrations.
