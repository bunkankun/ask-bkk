Mid-Qīng Cantonese official and amateur physician of Fèng-chéng 鳳城 (= Shùn-dé 順德, Guǎng-zhōu prefecture). *Jìn-shì* of *Dào-guāng* 30 / 1850; *Hàn-lín shù-jí-shì* 庶吉士 thereafter; later *xué-shì* in Jiāng-xī. As a young man during a 1843 convalescence in Háng-zhōu from haemorrhoid trouble, he assembled a collection of 700-plus simple medical formulae from the medical literature; in collaboration with the Rén-hé hereditary physician [[龔自璋]] Gōng Zì-zhāng (Yuè-chuān 月川) and his own Cantonese family connections, he produced and published the [[KR3er114]] *Jiā-yòng liáng-fāng* 家用良方 (1851) — one of the most successful 19th-c. Chinese popular household-formulary. Best known by his *zì* Bó-chuí 伯垂.
