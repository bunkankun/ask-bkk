Mid-Qīng Cantonese official and amateur physician of Fèngchéng 鳳城 (= Shùndé 順德, Guǎngzhōu prefecture). *Jìnshì* of *Dàoguāng* 30 / 1850; *Hànlín shùjíshì* 庶吉士 thereafter; later *xuéshì* in Jiāngxī. As a young man during a 1843 convalescence in Hángzhōu from haemorrhoid trouble, he assembled a collection of 700-plus simple medical formulae from the medical literature; in collaboration with the Rénhé hereditary physician [[龔自璋]] Gōng Zìzhāng (Yuèchuān 月川) and his own Cantonese family connections, he produced and published the [[KR3er114]] *Jiāyòng liángfāng* 家用良方 (1851) — one of the most successful 19th-c. Chinese popular household-formulary. Best known by his *zì* Bóchuí 伯垂.
