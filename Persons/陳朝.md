**Chén Cháo** 陳朝 (fl. early-Míng *Hóngwǔ* era), an early-Míng scholar of Yín 鄞 county (modern Níngbō 寧波), Zhèjiāng. Possibly the same person as Chén Cháofǔ 陳朝輔 found in some Míng-era sources — the catalog meta of [[KR4d0382]] *Sì-míng wén-xiàn jí* writes only 陳朝, which the Sì-kù *tíyào* expands to 陳朝輔 in its prose. He was the co-compiler, with [[鄭眞]] Zhèng Zhēn, of the Yín-county literary anthology *Sì-míng wén-xiàn* 四明文獻 — an early-Míng project of local recovery of Sòng literary materials from Yín, of which the surviving piece in the *Sì-kù* is the 5-*juàn* recovery of [[王應麟]] Wáng Yīnglín's prose, catalogued as [[KR4d0382]]. The CBDB contains four candidates of this name (457265, 493706, 506362, 507254), all without confident lifedates or location; no confident identification of "Chén Cháo of Yín" is possible from the present CBDB data alone, so no CBDB id is recorded here. The reference is therefore flagged as uncertain; the identification with 陳朝輔 is reported in the Sì-kù tíyào but is not independently established. Compiled work in the Kanripo corpus as co-compiler: [[KR4d0382]] *Sì-míng wén-xiàn jí*.
