Sòng official and member of the imperial clan, courtesy name Mèng-shòu 夢授, sixth-generation descendant of Sòng Tàizǔ 太祖. He took the *jìnshì* in *Yuán-yòu* 6 (1091) and rose under the Southern Sòng to *Bǎowén-gé zhí-xuéshì* 寶文閣直學士 in the *Shàoxīng* reign. CBDB id 43643 attests his death in 1137. He is principally known as the compiler of an idiosyncratic single-*juàn* *Dù gōng-bù niánpǔ* 杜工部年譜 ([[KR2g0007]]) of the Táng poet Dù Fǔ — one of the early surviving Sòng *niánpǔ* of Dù Fǔ, alongside [[魯訔]]'s and the lost *pǔ* of Lǚ Dà-fáng 呂大防, Cài Xìng-zōng 蔡興宗, and Huáng Hè 黃鶴.
