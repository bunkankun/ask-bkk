Shī Yúqín 施虞琴, early-Republican Hénán literatus. Named in the 1914 postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔnzǐ yī* as one of the friends-of-the-editor who collated and proof-corrected (參訂校正) Lóng Huìtáng's manuscript before lithographic printing. No standard biographical record located.
