Shī Yú-qín 施虞琴, early-Republican Hé-nán literatus. Named in the 1914 postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔn-zǐ yī* as one of the friends-of-the-editor who collated and proof-corrected (參訂校正) Lóng Huì-táng's manuscript before lithographic printing. No standard biographical record located.
