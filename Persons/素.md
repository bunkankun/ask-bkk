Sù 素, *zì* **Liánfēng** 蓮峰 ("Lotus Peak"), also styled **Xīmíng Sù** 西明素 from his last abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎichī Xíngyuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662, of the Tiāntóng Mìyún line). Born in **萬曆四十年** = **1612–1613** (per "辛丑秋…時年五十", *Liánfēng yǔlù* j. 7); death year unknown — was still living in **Kāngxī 30** = **1691** at age 80. Native of **Pútián** 莆田 (Fújiàn); lay surname **Kē** 柯.

**Formation.** Drawn to mountain seclusion in childhood. At 18 took the five lay precepts under **Qiáoyún** 樵雲大師 and devoted himself to *niànfó*. At 27 entered **Guóhuānsì** 國懽寺 and tonsured under **Yìsǒu** 逸叟和尚. While reading the *Xuěfēng yǔlù* and the line on **Tóuzǐ** 投子 pointing to a stone — "all the buddhas of the three worlds are inside this" — he doubted half a year. Went to **Cáoshān** 曹山 and was struck by **Bǎohuá Rěn** 寶華忍 with the staff: a first opening. Subsequently received final *jìbié* (sealing) under **Jīnsù Yuán** 金粟元 (= **Bǎichī Xíngyuán**) at **Jiànzhōu Bǎishān** 建州百山 — "needle and mustard-seed met."

**Six successive abbacies (all in Xīnghuàfǔ 興化府, Fújiàn).** **Gūshānsì** 孤山寺 (entered **順治十年癸巳 2/28** = **1653-03-27**), **Jiǔzuòshān Xīngguósì** 九座山興國寺, **Bàoēnsì** 報恩寺, **Lónghuá Wànshòusì** 龍華萬壽寺, **Lúshān Tōngtiānsì** 蘆山通天寺, **Shīshān Xīmíngsì** 獅山西明寺. Retired to **Yúnfēng** 雲峰 by ca. 1666.

**Work.** [[KR6q0530]] 《蓮峰禪師語錄》 10 juan, J38 B410, compiled by *fǎsì* **Ruòhǎi Xìngshēn** [[性深]] *děng biān*.
