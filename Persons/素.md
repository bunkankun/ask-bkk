Sù 素, *zì* **Lián-fēng** 蓮峰 ("Lotus Peak"), also styled **Xī-míng Sù** 西明素 from his last abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎi-chī Xíng-yuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662, of the Tiān-tóng Mì-yún line). Born in **萬曆四十年** = **1612–1613** (per "辛丑秋…時年五十", *Lián-fēng yǔ-lù* j. 7); death year unknown — was still living in **Kāngxī 30** = **1691** at age 80. Native of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Fú-jiàn); lay surname **Kē** 柯.

**Formation.** Drawn to mountain seclusion in childhood. At 18 took the five lay precepts under **Qiáo-yún** 樵雲大師 and devoted himself to *niàn-fó*. At 27 entered **Guó-huān-sì** 國懽寺 and tonsured under **Yì-sǒu** 逸叟和尚. While reading the *Xuě-fēng yǔ-lù* and the line on **Tóu-zǐ** 投子 pointing to a stone — "all the buddhas of the three worlds are inside this" — he doubted half a year. Went to **Cáo-shān** 曹山 and was struck by **Bǎo-huá Rěn** 寶華忍 with the staff: a first opening. Subsequently received final *jì-bié* (sealing) under **Jīn-sù Yuán** 金粟元 (= **Bǎi-chī Xíng-yuán**) at **Jiàn-zhōu Bǎi-shān** 建州百山 — "needle and mustard-seed met."

**Six successive abbacies (all in Xīng-huà-fǔ 興化府, Fú-jiàn).** **Gū-shān-sì** 孤山寺 (entered **順治十年癸巳 2/28** = **1653-03-27**), **Jiǔ-zuò-shān Xīng-guó-sì** 九座山興國寺, **Bào-ēn-sì** 報恩寺, **Lóng-huá Wàn-shòu-sì** 龍華萬壽寺, **Lú-shān Tōng-tiān-sì** 蘆山通天寺, **Shī-shān Xī-míng-sì** 獅山西明寺. Retired to **Yún-fēng** 雲峰 by ca. 1666.

**Work.** [[KR6q0530]] 《蓮峰禪師語錄》 10 juan, J38 B410, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Ruò-hǎi Xìng-shēn** [[性深]] *děng biān*.
