**Huáng Zǐfā** 黃子發 (*fl.* Táng, dates and native place unrecorded) is named in the bibliographical record as the author of the *Xiàng yǔ shū* 相雨書 ("Book of Reading Rain") [[KR3fa004]], a one-*juǎn* manual of *zhàn-yǔ* 占雨 (rain-prognostication) by atmospheric, cloud and astronomical signs. Nothing else is reliably recorded of him; some catalog notices treat his name as a pseudonymous attribution rather than a person. The *Sòngshǐ yìwénzhì* 宋史藝文志 already lists the work under his name.
