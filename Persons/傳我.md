# Chuánwǒ 傳我

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[真哲|Gǔxuě Zhēnzhé]] 古雪真哲 (1614–c. 1675) in the Mìyún Yuánwù line. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0426]] 《古雪哲禪師語錄》 (20 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J28 B208). Resident at **Wànān Lóngdéyuàn** 萬安龍德院 in Jiāngxī (the seat from which the juan-head signature line reads).

The 傳-generation character is an uncommon variant in the Mìyún lineage-poem; may reflect a Jiāngxī sub-branch local convention. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under his name.

Sources: [[KR6q0426]] juan-head signature line "*Wànān Lóngdéyuàn sìfǎ ménrén Chuánwǒ děng biān* 萬安龍德院嗣法門人傳我等編".
