# Chuán-wǒ 傳我

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[真哲|Gǔ-xuě Zhēn-zhé]] 古雪真哲 (1614–c. 1675) in the Mìyún Yuán-wù line. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0426]] 《古雪哲禪師語錄》 (20 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B208). Resident at **Wàn-ān Lóng-dé-yuàn** 萬安龍德院 in Jiāng-xī (the seat from which the juan-head signature line reads).

The 傳-generation character is an uncommon variant in the Mìyún lineage-poem; may reflect a Jiāng-xī sub-branch local convention. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under his name.

Sources: [[KR6q0426]] juan-head signature line "*Wàn-ān Lóng-dé-yuàn sì-fǎ mén-rén Chuán-wǒ děng biān* 萬安龍德院嗣法門人傳我等編".
