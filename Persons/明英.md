Míng-yīng 明英, Línjì-Yángqí (Sān-fēng line) dharma-disciple of the nun **Bǎo-chí Xuán-zǒng** [[玄總]] 寶持玄總. Lead recorder of the 2-juan 《寶持總禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0485]]). Almost certainly herself a nun (*bhikṣuṇī*), continuing the rare female-line transmission within the Sān-fēng tradition. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
