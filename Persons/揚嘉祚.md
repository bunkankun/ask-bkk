Yáng Jiā-zuò 揚嘉祚 (active late Míng / early Qīng), Buddhist editor; the *shān hé* 刪合 ("editorial-merger") of [[圓覺]] Yuán-jué's [[KR6e0108|*Huáyán yuán rén lùn hé jiě*]] (X1033). His function — *shān hé* "to abridge and combine" — indicates that he edited and merged Yuán-jué's earlier separate commentaries into a single combined text. Birth and death dates not preserved.
