Yáng Jiāzuò 揚嘉祚 (active late Míng / early Qīng), Buddhist editor; the *shān hé* 刪合 ("editorial-merger") of [[圓覺]] Yuánjué's *[[KR6e0108|Huáyán yuán rén lùn hé jiě]]* (X1033). His function — *shān hé* "to abridge and combine" — indicates that he edited and merged Yuánjué's earlier separate commentaries into a single combined text. Birth and death dates not preserved.
