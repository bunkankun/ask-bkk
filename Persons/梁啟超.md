# 梁啟超 Liáng Qǐchāo

**Liáng Qǐchāo** 梁啟超 (1873–1929, of Xīnhuì 新會, Guǎngdōng), leading late-Qīng / Republican-era reformer, journalist, and scholar; with [[康有為]] Kāng Yǒuwéi the principal voice of the 1898 reform movement. Mentioned in [[熊希齡]] Xióng Xīlíng's 1927 colophon to [[譚志光]] Tán Zhìguāng's *Zhēnjiǔ wèndá* 針灸問答 ([[KR3ee035]]) as part of the 1890s Húnán Nánxuéhuì 南學會 reformist circle.
