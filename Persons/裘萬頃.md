Qiú Wànqǐng 裘萬頃 (*zì* Yuánliàng 元量, *hào* Zhúzhāi 竹齋), of *Xīnjiàn* 新建 (modern Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī* 14 (1187). Held office to *Dàlǐsī zhí*; resigned to be reassigned as *Jiāngxī fǔgàn*. Eulogized by Yáng Jiǎn [[楊簡]] (who composed his tomb-inscription) for *mòshí* (silent recognition). Identified as one of the *Sìjié* 四傑 (Four Eminent Ones) along with Hú Tóngyuán 胡桐原, Wàn Dàn'ān 萬澹菴, and Xú Zhútáng 徐竹堂. Liú Kèzhuāng's *Hòucūn jí* has a postface to his *sīzhí shī*. Lifedates unknown. CBDB id 27715. Author of [[KR4d0289]] *Zhúzhāi shījí*.
