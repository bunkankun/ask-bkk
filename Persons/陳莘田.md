Chén Xīn-tián 陳莘田 — mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgeon, conventionally dated to the early-to-mid nineteenth century (Dào-guāng / Xián-fēng era). He is treated in modern Chinese surveys as one of the principal Sūzhōu *wài-kē* 外科 (external-medicine / surgical) practitioners of his generation. His [[KR3ep042]] *Chén Xīn-tián wài-kē fāng-àn* 陳莘田外科方案 (six *juǎn*) opens with a long mnemonic-verse compendium of surgical prescriptions covering the standard Qīng *wài-kē* repertoire.
