Chén Xīntián 陳莘田 — mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgeon, conventionally dated to the early-to-mid nineteenth century (Dàoguāng / Xiánfēng era). He is treated in modern Chinese surveys as one of the principal Sūzhōu *wàikē* 外科 (external-medicine / surgical) practitioners of his generation. His [[KR3ep042]] *Chén Xīntián wàikē fāngàn* 陳莘田外科方案 (six *juǎn*) opens with a long mnemonic-verse compendium of surgical prescriptions covering the standard Qīng *wàikē* repertoire.
