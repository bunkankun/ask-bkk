# Wèi Zhòngjǔ 魏仲擧

Sòng-period commercial publisher and editor, active at *Jiàn-ān* 建安 (modern *Jiàn-ōu* 建甌, Fú-jiàn) — one of the principal Sòng commercial-print centers. Floruit *Shào-dìng* / *Duān-píng* 紹定/端平 period (*ca.* 1228–1233).

His two principal scholarly products are the parallel *Wǔ-bǎi-jiā zhù* 五百家注 anthologies of the foundational Tang *gǔ-wén* writers — the *Wǔ-bǎi-jiā zhù Chāng-lí wén-jí* [[KR4c0045]] for Hán Yù [[韓愈]] and the *Wǔ-bǎi-jiā zhù Liǔ xiān-shēng jí* [[KR4c0050]] for Liǔ Zōng-yuán [[柳宗元]]. Both consolidate over a century of Sòng *jí-zhù* into 40-*juǎn* / 21-*juǎn* anthologies, drawing on the foundational *jiào-kān* of Fāng Sōng-qīng [[方崧卿]] and the *kǎo-yì* of Zhū Xī [[朱熹]] together with the running commentary of dozens of named Sòng readers. Wèi's *Jiàn-ān* prints became the standard Yuán-Míng *kē-jǔ* preparation editions of Hán Yù and Liǔ Zōng-yuán. CBDB has no entry.
