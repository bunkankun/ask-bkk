# Wèi Zhòngjǔ 魏仲擧

Sòng-period commercial publisher and editor, active at *Jiànān* 建安 (modern *Jiànōu* 建甌, Fújiàn) — one of the principal Sòng commercial-print centers. Floruit *Shàodìng* / *Duānpíng* 紹定/端平 period (*ca.* 1228–1233).

His two principal scholarly products are the parallel *Wǔbǎijiā zhù* 五百家注 anthologies of the foundational Tang *gǔwén* writers — the *Wǔbǎijiā zhù Chānglí wénjí* [[KR4c0045]] for Hán Yù [[韓愈]] and the *Wǔbǎijiā zhù Liǔ xiānshēng jí* [[KR4c0050]] for Liǔ Zōngyuán [[柳宗元]]. Both consolidate over a century of Sòng *jízhù* into 40-*juǎn* / 21-*juǎn* anthologies, drawing on the foundational *jiàokān* of Fāng Sōngqīng [[方崧卿]] and the *kǎoyì* of Zhū Xī [[朱熹]] together with the running commentary of dozens of named Sòng readers. Wèi's *Jiànān* prints became the standard Yuán-Míng *kējǔ* preparation editions of Hán Yù and Liǔ Zōngyuán. CBDB has no entry.
