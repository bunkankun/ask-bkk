Zhā Wéirén 查爲仁 (1693–1749), *zì* Xīngǔ 心榖, *hào* Liánpō 蓮坡, of Wǎnpíng 宛平 (modern Bĕijīng region; the family was originally from Hǎiníng). *Jǔrén* of *Kāngxī xīnmǎo* / 1711. Major figure of the *Tiānjīn* literary circle of the early *Qiánlóng* era. Best known as joint annotator of Zhōu Mì's [[周密]] *Juémiào hǎocí*, producing — with Lì È [[厲鶚]] — the *Juémiào hǎocí jiān* 絕妙好詞箋 [[KR4j0071]]. Compiled the *Liánpō shīhuà* 蓮坡詩話 and *Cíyú jìshì* 詩餘紀事 (the latter mentioned by Lì È in the preface to the *Jiān* but lost). The Liánpō *cíyú jìshì* is the second-tier *cíhuà* of the early Qīng.
