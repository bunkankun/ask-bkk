Zhā Wéi-rén 查爲仁 (1693–1749), *zì* Xīn-gǔ 心榖, *hào* Lián-pō 蓮坡, of Wǎn-píng 宛平 (modern Bĕijīng region; the family was originally from Hǎi-níng). *Jǔ-rén* of *Kāng-xī xīn-mǎo* / 1711. Major figure of the *Tiān-jīn* literary circle of the early *Qián-lóng* era. Best known as joint annotator of Zhōu Mì's [[周密]] *Jué-miào hǎo-cí*, producing — with Lì È [[厲鶚]] — the *Jué-miào hǎo-cí jiān* 絕妙好詞箋 [[KR4j0071]]. Compiled the *Lián-pō shī-huà* 蓮坡詩話 and *Cí-yú jì-shì* 詩餘紀事 (the latter mentioned by Lì È in the preface to the *Jiān* but lost). The Lián-pō *cí-yú jì-shì* is the second-tier *cí-huà* of the early Qīng. CBDB 55472 gives 1693–1749.
