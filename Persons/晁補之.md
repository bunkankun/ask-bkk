**Cháo Bǔzhī** 晁補之 (1053–1110), *zì* Wújiù 无咎, *hào* Guīláizǐ 歸來子, of Jùyě 鉅野 (Shāndōng). *Jìnshì* 1079. One of the *Sūmén sì xuéshì* 蘇門四學士 (Four Scholars of the Sū School — Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅, Qín Guān 秦觀, Zhāng Lěi 張耒, and Cháo). Cousin of Cháo Yuèzhī 晁說之 [[晁說之]]. Held appointments under Yuányòu including *Lǐbù lángzhōng*; demoted in *Shàoshèng* / *Yuánfú* under *Xīnfǎ*–*Yuányòu* dispute; recalled at *Chóngníng* but again exiled. Author of the *Jīlèi jí* 雞肋集 [[KR4d0105]] in 70 *juǎn* — one of the principal Northern-Sòng *biéjí* — and a major *cí*-poet in the SūShì manner. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444.
