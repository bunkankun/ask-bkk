**Cháo Bǔ-zhī** 晁補之 (1053–1110), *zì* Wú-jiù 无咎, *hào* Guī-lái-zǐ 歸來子, of Jù-yě 鉅野 (Shān-dōng). *Jìnshì* 1079. One of the *Sū-mén sì xué-shì* 蘇門四學士 (Four Scholars of the Sū School — Huáng Tíng-jiān 黃庭堅, Qín Guān 秦觀, Zhāng Lěi 張耒, and Cháo). Cousin of Cháo Yuè-zhī 晁說之 [[晁說之]]. Held appointments under Yuán-yòu including *Lǐ-bù láng-zhōng*; demoted in *Shào-shèng* / *Yuán-fú* under *Xīn-fǎ*–*Yuán-yòu* dispute; recalled at *Chóng-níng* but again exiled. Author of the *Jī-lèi jí* 雞肋集 [[KR4d0105]] in 70 *juǎn* — one of the principal Northern-Sòng *bié-jí* — and a major *cí*-poet in the Sū-Shì manner. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444.
