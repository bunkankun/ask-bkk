# 何光約

Hé Guāngyuē (style Míng 明) of Dàpǔ 大埔 (Guǎngdōng), early-Republican Hakka physician practising at the *Xìnghétáng* 杏和堂 in Dàshānjiǎo 大山腳 on the island of Penang (Bīnlángyǔ 檳榔嶼, British Malaya). Recovered the manuscript of [[KR3em019]] *Hóukē mìjué* 喉科秘訣 and sent it to [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jíshēng for publication in 1922.
