Guī Chúzǐ 歸鋤子 ("Returning Hoe Master"; fl. 1819) is the pseudonym of the author of *Hónglóumèng Bǔ* 紅樓夢補, a 48-chapter sequel to *Hónglóu Mèng* 紅樓夢. The preface of the work is dated three days before the Double Ninth Festival of Jiājìng *jǐmǎo* (1819). The pseudonym evokes the *yǐnshì* 隱士 (recluse) tradition of Táo Qián 陶潛. The author's real name, native place, and official career are unknown; no CBDB entry has been located.
