**Sòng Zhòngfǔ** 宋仲甫 (also recorded as **Qí Zhòngfǔ** 齊仲甫, *fl.* c. 1220 南宋), Southern-Sòng gynecologist. Author of the *Nǚkē bǎiwèn* 女科百問 ([[KR3ei031]]), a two-*juǎn* catechetical gynecology in one hundred numbered questions covering the canonical *fùkē* topics. The work is one of the major Southern-Sòng gynecologies alongside Chén Zìmíng 陳自明's *Fùrén dàquán liángfāng* (1237). The Sòng / Qí variant of the surname probably reflects medieval scribal variation; the *Sòngshǐ yìwén zhì* and several early bibliographies use the form Qí. No further biographical detail located in standard reference works.
