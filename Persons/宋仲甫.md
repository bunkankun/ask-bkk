**Sòng Zhòngfǔ** 宋仲甫 (also recorded as **Qí Zhòngfǔ** 齊仲甫, *fl.* c. 1220 南宋), Southern-Sòng gynecologist. Author of the *Nǚkē bǎi-wèn* 女科百問 ([[KR3ei031]]), a two-*juǎn* catechetical gynecology in one hundred numbered questions covering the canonical *fù-kē* topics. The work is one of the major Southern-Sòng gynecologies alongside Chén Zì-míng 陳自明's *Fù-rén dà-quán liáng-fāng* (1237). The Sòng / Qí variant of the surname probably reflects medieval scribal variation; the *Sòng-shǐ yì-wén zhì* and several early bibliographies use the form Qí. No further biographical detail located in standard reference works.
