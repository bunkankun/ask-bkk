Liáng Liánfū 梁廉夫 (*fl.* mid-late 19th c., late Qīng) was a Guǎngxī 廣西 physician of the Tóngzhì–Guāngxù years, native of Pínglè 平樂. His extant work is the *Bùzhī yī bìyào* 不知醫必要 ([[KR3ed059]]), a four-*juǎn* lay-orientated clinical handbook completed and printed in 1881 (the conventionally cited Guāngxù 7 *xīnsì* preface-date). The work was widely reprinted in late-Qīng and Republican popular-medicine collections and became a staple of household reference in southern China. CBDB does not list him.
