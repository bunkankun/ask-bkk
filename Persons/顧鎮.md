# Gù Zhèn 顧鎮

*Zì* Bèijiǔ 備九, *hào* Gǔqiū 古湫; also Yúdōng 虞東, from the ancient name Hǎiyú 海虞 of his native Chángshú 常熟 (modern Jiāngsū) and his residence east of the city wall. *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 19 (1754, 甲戌科 二甲 11). Rose to *zhǔshì* 主事 in the Imperial Clan Court (宗人府主事), and directed the Báilùdòng 白鹿洞 and Zhōngshān 鍾山 academies. Biography: *Qīng shǐ lièzhuàn* 清史列傳 j. 68.

The catalog meta file gives his birth year as 1718 but CBDB and Wikidata (Q45515381) both give 1720, followed here.

Surviving works: *Yúdōng xuéshī* 虞東學詩 ([[KR1c0065]], 12 juan, WYG — his most substantial surviving scholarly work); *Sānlǐ zhájì* 三禮札記 (attested in *Qīng shǐ lièzhuàn*); literary collection *Yúdōng xiānshēng wénlù* 虞東先生文錄. The sometimes-attributed *Sānjiā shī bǔzhù* 三家詩補注 is not reliably his and should not be cited to him without fresh verification.

Gù is best known for his mediation between Hàn-learning and Sòng-learning positions on the *Shījīng* — neither wholly defending the *Xiǎo xù* against Zhū Xī's *Shī jí zhuàn*, nor wholly siding with Zhū against the *Xù* — a position set out most fully in the ten *shī shuō* 詩說 essays prefixed to *Yúdōng xuéshī*.
