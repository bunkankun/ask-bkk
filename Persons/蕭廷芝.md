Late-Southern-Sòng *nèidān* 內丹 author of the Southern Lineage (*Nán-zōng* 南宗), *zì* Yuánruì 元瑞 (also Tiānruì 天瑞), *hào* Zǐxū liǎozhēnzǐ 紫虛了眞子 ("Master of the Purple Void who has Realised the Truth"). A native of Fúzhōu 福州 (Fújiàn). Disciple of Péng Sì 彭耜 (one of [[白玉蟾|Bái Yùchán]]'s most prominent followers); the discipleship is attested in Xiāo's preface (dated 1260) to [[KR5c0687|DZ 687 *Dàodé zhēn-jīng sānjiě*]] (preface, 8a). For the second *zì* Tiānlái 天賴, see *Quán Sòng cí* 全宋詞 4.2777. Author of [[KR5a0265|*Jīndān dàchéng jí* 金丹大成集]] (DZ 263.9–13 = the present work in the Kanripo split), a five-juan compendium that became a standard later anthology of Nán-zōng *nèidān* terminology and method. Xiāo is repeatedly quoted in [[KR5c1005|DZ 1005 *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì fāhuī*]] (e.g., 3.3b, 5.18b, 6.2b), and his commentary on the *Cuīgōng rǔyào jīng* 崔公入藥鏡 is preserved in [[KR5c0135|DZ 135 *Cuīgōng rǔyào jīng zhùjiě*]]. He also wrote a commentary on Lǚ Dòngbīn's 呂洞賓 *cí*-lyric *Qìnyuán-chūn* 沁園春 (cf. [[KR5c0136|DZ 136]]). Some of his *cí* are reproduced without attribution in [[KR5c1100|DZ 1100 *Mínghè yúyīn* 鳴鶴餘音]] (1347). The *Dàozàng jíyào* 道藏輯要 edition of his *Jīndān dàchéng* is incomplete.
