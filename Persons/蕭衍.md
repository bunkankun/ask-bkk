# 蕭衍

*Xiāo Yǎn*, posthumously **Liáng Wǔdì** 梁武帝 (r. 502–549), founder and longest-reigning emperor of the **Liáng** dynasty of the Southern Dynasties.

A devout Buddhist patron whose long reign at Jiànkāng (modern Nánjīng) established Buddhism as the de facto state religion of the southern court. He sponsored sūtra translations, monastery construction, vegetarian dietary reforms, and a series of high-profile public Buddhist events (the famous *wúzhē dàhuì* 無遮大會 universal-equal assemblies). The hagiographic tradition records his interactions with the master **Bǎozhì** 寶誌 and the lay-bodhisattva **Fù Dàshì** 傅大士; the latter's celebrated forty-nine *gāthā* on the *Vajracchedikā* (T2732) is set at his Liáng court. He famously corresponded with **Bodhidharma** 達摩 in the Chán transmission narratives.
