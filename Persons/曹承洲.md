# 曹承洲

Cáo Chéngzhōu 曹承洲 (late Qīng), Wúmén (Sūzhōu) internist and *yángkē* 瘍科 specialist; son of [[曹雲洲]] Cáo Yúnzhōu and father of [[曹元恒]] Cáo Yuánhéng (Cāngzhōu, 1849–1931). The middle generation of the three-generation Cáo medical lineage of late-Qīng Sūzhōu (see [[KR3ep101]] *Cáo Cāngzhōu yīàn*).
