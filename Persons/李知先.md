**Lǐ Zhīxiān** 李知先 (*fl.* twelfth century, 宋), Sòng-period physician. Author of the *Huó rén shū kuò* 活人書括 — a versified digest of [[朱肱]] Zhū Hóng's *Lèi zhèng huó rén shū* 類證活人書 (preface 1107), the dominant Northern-Sòng *Shānghán* clinical reference. The *Huó rén shū kuò* renders Zhū Hóng's clinical material as mnemonic verse for student memorization, in the same *gē-jué* 歌訣 tradition as [[許叔微]]'s *Shānghán bǎi zhèng gē* ([[KR3ef061]]). The work was later re-illustrated and republished in the Míng by the Fú-jiàn pedagogue-publisher [[熊宗立]] (Xióng Zōnglì, 1409–1482) under the title *Lèi biān Shānghán huó rén shū kuò zhǐ-zhǎng tú lùn* 類編傷寒活人書括指掌圖論 ([[KR3ef077]]). Detailed biographical information about Lǐ is not preserved.
