**Mò Wényuán** 莫文遠 (*fl.* mid-19th c., 清), Qīng scholar known for various commentaries on the reconstructed *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* 神農本草經 ([[KR3ec001]]). His work belongs to the late-Qīng antiquarian-literary reception of the rediscovered *Běnjīng* that also produced [[葉志洗]]'s *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng zàn* ([[KR3ec069]]). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
