**Gāo Dǒukuí** 高斗魁 (*zì* Dànzhōng 旦中, *hào* Gǔfēng 鼓峰, 1623–1670) — early-Qīng physician of Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). One of the most distinctive clinicians of the early-Qīng Zhèdōng 浙東 medical scene, classically trained in the Confucian curriculum (*由儒而精於醫*) before turning to private medical practice. Gāo did not publish in his lifetime; his clinical case-records and doctrinal essays were collected after his death by [[楊乘六]] Yáng Chéngliù into the *Yīzōng jǐrèn biān* 醫宗己任編 ([[KR3er067]]), which through Yáng's editorial work and Wáng Rǔqiān's 1891 supplemented edition has remained the principal witness to Gāo's medical thought.
