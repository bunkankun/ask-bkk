**Gāo Dǒu-kuí** 高斗魁 (*zì* Dàn-zhōng 旦中, *hào* Gǔ-fēng 鼓峰, 1623–1670) — early-Qīng physician of Sì-míng 四明 (Níng-bō, Zhè-jiāng). One of the most distinctive clinicians of the early-Qīng Zhè-dōng 浙東 medical scene, classically trained in the Confucian curriculum (*由儒而精於醫*) before turning to private medical practice. Gāo did not publish in his lifetime; his clinical case-records and doctrinal essays were collected after his death by [[楊乘六]] Yáng Chéng-liù into the *Yī-zōng jǐ-rèn biān* 醫宗己任編 ([[KR3er067]]), which through Yáng's editorial work and Wáng Rǔ-qiān's 1891 supplemented edition has remained the principal witness to Gāo's medical thought.
