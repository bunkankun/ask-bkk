# Wànlián 卍蓮

A late-Qīng *Tiāntái* 天台 monk active in the Wēnzhōu / *Lèchéng* 樂城 (modern Lèqīng 樂清) region of southeastern Zhèjiāng — the *Dōngōu* 東甌 region. Religious name **Gǔhé** 古和; sobriquets **Wànlián** 卍蓮 and **Xiǎoróu** 曉柔. He took the tonsure (*tìrǎn* 薙染) in *Dàoguāng* 26 autumn (= 1846) and received full ordination (*shòujù* 受具) in *Dàoguāng* 27 spring (= 1847); he records additional training through *Xiánfēng* 4 winter (= 1854) and thereafter some twenty years of practice culminating in the composition of his major work in 1875. He held the lineage of the *Tiāntái* school (*chuán Tiāntái Gǔhé* 傳天台古和) but explicitly took the Pure Land as ultimate refuge — a characteristic late-Qīng *tāijìng shuāngxiū* 台淨雙修 (Tiāntái–Pure Land integration) position.

His major work is the **《淨土證心集》** *Jìngtǔ zhèngxīn jí* [[KR6p0115]] (X1196) in three *juǎn*, an integrative Pure Land devotional anthology containing 29 miscellaneous expository pieces, 48 image-encomia, the 108-verse *Wéixīn sòng* 唯心頌, the 20-stanza *Yàoyuē lǜ* 要約律, *xīnyàn míng* 欣厭銘 (acceptance-rejection inscriptions), and *jǐngcè jì* 警䇿偈 (admonitory gāthās). His studio was the **Yuēxī jīngshè** 約西精舍.
