# Wàn-lián 卍蓮

A late-Qīng *Tiāntái* 天台 monk active in the Wēn-zhōu / *Lè-chéng* 樂城 (modern Lè-qīng 樂清) region of southeastern Zhèjiāng — the *Dōng-ōu* 東甌 region. Religious name **Gǔ-hé** 古和; sobriquets **Wàn-lián** 卍蓮 and **Xiǎo-róu** 曉柔. He took the tonsure (*tì-rǎn* 薙染) in *Dào-guāng* 26 autumn (= 1846) and received full ordination (*shòu-jù* 受具) in *Dào-guāng* 27 spring (= 1847); he records additional training through *Xián-fēng* 4 winter (= 1854) and thereafter some twenty years of practice culminating in the composition of his major work in 1875. He held the lineage of the *Tiāntái* school (*chuán Tiāntái Gǔ-hé* 傳天台古和) but explicitly took the Pure Land as ultimate refuge — a characteristic late-Qīng *tāi-jìng shuāng-xiū* 台淨雙修 (Tiāntái–Pure Land integration) position.

His major work is the **《淨土證心集》** *Jìng-tǔ zhèng-xīn jí* [[KR6p0115]] (X1196) in three *juǎn*, an integrative Pure Land devotional anthology containing 29 miscellaneous expository pieces, 48 image-encomia, the 108-verse *Wéi-xīn sòng* 唯心頌, the 20-stanza *Yào-yuē lǜ* 要約律, *xīn-yàn míng* 欣厭銘 (acceptance-rejection inscriptions), and *jǐng-cè jì* 警䇿偈 (admonitory gāthās). His studio was the **Yuē-xī jīng-shè** 約西精舍.
