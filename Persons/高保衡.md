**Gāo Bǎohéng** 高保衡 (*fl.* 1057–1067, 宋), Northern Sòng official-physician and senior member of the imperial **Bureau for Editing Medical Books** (*Jiàozhèng yīshū jú* 校正醫書局) under [[林億]]. He served as Cháofèng láng Guózǐ bóshì 朝奉郎守國子博士 with the bureau title 同校正醫書 ("Joint Collator of Medical Books"); the Sòng presentation memorial at the head of the Lín Yì recension of the *Sùwèn* names him alongside Lín Yì, Sūn Qí 孫奇, and Sūn Zhào 孫兆. He participated in the bureau's collation of the *Sùwèn* ([[KR3e0001]]), *Língshū jīng* ([[KR3e0002]]), *Zhēnjiǔ jiǎyǐ jīng* ([[KR3e0005]]), *Mài jīng*, *Shānghán lùn*, *Jīnguì yàoluè*, and other foundational medical classics presented to the throne in 1069. He has no transmitted biographical record beyond his bureau service.
