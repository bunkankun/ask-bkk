**Dù Qiú** 杜荍 (Sòng), compiler of the *Chǎnyù bǎoqìng jí* 產育寶慶集 ([[KR3e0040]]); listed by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng among the principal Sòng obstetrical authorities in his preface to the *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* ([[KR3ei030]]).
