Dàobiāo 道標 was a Sòng Vinaya monk; lifedates and biographical details unknown. Sobriquet **Fóhuì** 佛慧 ("Buddha-Wisdom"); studio name **Móyúntáng** 摩雲堂. His sole surviving work is the **Zīchí jì lìtí shíyì** 資持記立題拾義 ([[KR6k0171]], X44n0742) — a brief one-juan exposition of the title of **Yuánzhào**'s ([[元照]]) *Zīchí jì*. The work belongs to the Sòng-period *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 *Nánshānlǜ* school. Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001548.
