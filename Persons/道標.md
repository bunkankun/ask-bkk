Dào-biāo 道標 was a Sòng Vinaya monk; lifedates and biographical details unknown. Sobriquet **Fó-huì** 佛慧 ("Buddha-Wisdom"); studio name **Mó-yún-táng** 摩雲堂. His sole surviving work is the **Zī-chí jì lì-tí shí-yì** 資持記立題拾義 ([[KR6k0171]], X44n0742) — a brief one-juan exposition of the title of **Yuán-zhào**'s ([[元照]]) *Zī-chí jì*. The work belongs to the Sòng-period *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 *Nán-shān-lǜ* school. Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001548.
