Sòng-period Tiāntái 天台 monk; sobriquets **Pǔwēng** 朴翁 (with full name **Pǔwēng Yìxiān** 朴翁義銛), **Wúhuái** 無懷, **Shàngfāng** 上方, also referenced under the lay name **Gě Tiānmín** 葛天民. DILA Authority A000242. Lifedates unrecorded.

Author of the **Bùkě chànà wú cǐ jūn** 不可剎那無此君 ([[KR6d0238]], X57n0962, 1 *juan*) — a brief Sòng treatise (the title literally "[One] cannot for a moment be without this lord"; the metaphor draws on the bamboo metaphor [此君 = "this lord" = bamboo] in Wáng Huīzhī's 王徽之 famous *quotation*).
