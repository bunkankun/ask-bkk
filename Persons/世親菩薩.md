Shìqīn púsà 世親菩薩 (\*Vasubandhu, "Kinsman-of-the-Vasus"; conventionally c. 320–400 CE) — the more usual modern Chinese form of the name [[天親菩薩|Tiānqīn 天親]]. Both 天親 ("Heaven-Kinsman") and 世親 ("World-Kinsman") are calque-translations of the Sanskrit *Vasu-bandhu* and refer to the same person.

This person-note exists to support wikilinks from work-notes that use the form 世親 in their author-attribution; for the principal biographical entry, see [[天親菩薩]].

Works in the Kanripo corpus (attributed under 世親 form): [[KR6c0033]] *Jīngāngxiān lùn* (T1512); [[KR6c0034]] *Néngduàn jīngāng bōrě bōluómìduō jīng lùn shì* (T1513); and others.
