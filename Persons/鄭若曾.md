Zhèng Ruòzēng 鄭若曾 (1505–1580), *zì* Bólǔ 伯魯, *hào* Kāiyáng 開陽, was a Late-Míng coastal-defence and frontier-strategy specialist. Native of Kūnshān 崑山 (Sūzhōu region). Personally close to Hú Zōngxiàn 胡宗憲 (Mid-Míng wōkòu suppression chief) and Qī Jìguāng. Major works in the SKQS: *Jiāngnán jīng lüè* 江南經畧 ([[KR3b0018]], his comprehensive treatment of Lower-Yangzi defence in 8 juan); *Chóu hǎi tú biān* 籌海圖編 (separately preserved, the major coastal-defence atlas).
