Sòng Qí 宋祁 (998–1061), *zì* Zǐ-jīng 子京, posthumous title Jǐng-wén 景文; native of Yōng-qiū 雍邱 (Hé-nán). One of the great mid-Northern-Sòng officials, historians, and poets. *Tiānshèng* 2 *jìn-shì* (1024), in the same examination as his elder brother Sòng Xiáng 宋庠; the brothers were known as the "Two Sòngs" (*Èr Sòng*) — Sòng Qí being "Lesser Sòng" (*Xiǎo Sòng*). Held senior offices including *Hàn-lín xué-shì chéng-zhǐ*, *Lì-bù shì-láng*, *Duān-míng diàn xué-shì*, and concurrent Prefect of Yì-zhōu 益州 (1056–1058). Co-compiler with Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修 of the *Xīn Táng-shū* 新唐書 [[KR2a0009]]; Sòng Qí drafted the *Liè-zhuàn* (biographies); the work was completed in 1060 just before his death. His other principal works include the *Yìbù fāngwù lüèjì* 益部方物略記 ([[KR2k0109]]), the *Sòng Jǐng-wén jí* 宋景文集 ([[KR4d0049]]), and the famous *Yùlóu chūn* 玉樓春 ci-poem with the line *hóng-xìng zhī tóu chūn-yì nào* 紅杏枝頭春意鬧, which earned him the further sobriquet *Hóng-xìng shàng-shū* 紅杏尚書. Biography in *Sòng-shǐ* 284. CBDB id 1558.
