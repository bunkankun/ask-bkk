Sòng Qí 宋祁 (998–1061), *zì* Zǐjīng 子京, posthumous title Jǐngwén 景文; native of Yōngqiū 雍邱 (Hénán). One of the great mid-Northern-Sòng officials, historians, and poets. *Tiānshèng* 2 *jìnshì* (1024), in the same examination as his elder brother Sòng Xiáng 宋庠; the brothers were known as the "Two Sòngs" (*Èr Sòng*) — Sòng Qí being "Lesser Sòng" (*Xiǎo Sòng*). Held senior offices including *Hànlín xuéshì chéngzhǐ*, *Lìbù shìláng*, *Duānmíng diàn xuéshì*, and concurrent Prefect of Yìzhōu 益州 (1056–1058). Co-compiler with Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修 of the *Xīn Tángshū* 新唐書 [[KR2a0009]]; Sòng Qí drafted the *Lièzhuàn* (biographies); the work was completed in 1060 just before his death. His other principal works include the *Yìbù fāngwù lüèjì* 益部方物略記 ([[KR2k0109]]), the *Jǐngwén jí* 景文集 ([[KR4d0021]]), and the famous *Yùlóu chūn* 玉樓春 ci-poem with the line *hóngxìng zhī tóu chūnyì nào* 紅杏枝頭春意鬧, which earned him the further sobriquet *Hóngxìng shàngshū* 紅杏尚書. Biography in *Sòngshǐ* 284. CBDB id 1558.
