# 黄龍祥 Huáng Lóngxiáng

**Huáng Lóngxiáng** 黄龍祥 (b. 1959), contemporary Chinese acupuncture historian and lexicographer at the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences (中國中醫科學院); author of *Zhōngguó zhēnjiǔ xué shùyǔ cídiǎn* 中國針灸學術語辭典 (1999) and the standard *Zhōngguó zhēnjiǔ shǐ tújiàn* 中國針灸史圖鑑.
