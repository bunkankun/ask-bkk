# Zìxiāng 自相

Mid-ninth-century Silla 新羅 (Korean) Vinaya monk of *Dōngquánsì* 東泉寺. Named in [[允堪]] Yǔn Kān's preface to [[KR6k0177]] *Sìfēnlǜ shí píní yìchāo fǔyào jì* as one of the two Korean monks (with [[玄靈]] Xuánlíng of *Huìmíngsì*) who collated [[道宣]] Dàoxuān's *Yìchāo* in the Silla royal capital and dispatched the copy to Táng China in 850. The episode is a key documentary witness to Silla-Táng Vinaya text-exchange.
