**Xú Bīn** 徐彬 (*zì* Zhōngkě 忠可, native of Jiāxīng 嘉興), early-Qīng physician and a disciple of the Jiāngxī clinician Yú Chāng 喻昌 (1585–1664). His major surviving works are the ***Jīnguì yàoluè lùn-zhù*** 金匱要略論註 ([[KR3e0006]]) of Kāngxī 康熙 辛亥 (1671) — the SKQS-edited recension of the *Jīnguì yàoluè* with commentary, in twenty-four juan — and the *Shānghán yī zhù* 傷寒一注 (companion commentary on the *Shānghán lùn*, not in the SKQS). The *Sìkù tíyào* on the *Jīnguì lùn-zhù* notes that as Yú Chāng's *dìzǐ* his learning has a clear teacher-line, and selects his commentary precisely for this lineage. Beyond his medical studies he has no extended biographical record.
