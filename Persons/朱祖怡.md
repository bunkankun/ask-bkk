**Zhū Zǔ-yí** 朱祖怡 (mid-twentieth-century Chinese-medicine scholar and editor, fl. 1950s–1960s), the principal modern editor of [[費伯雄]] Fèi Bó-xióng's *Yī-chún shèng-yì* 醫醇賸義 ([[KR3er049]]). Zhū's editorial *bá* (postface) characterises Fèi as having had little leisure-time, having completed the *Shèng-yì* only in old age, and disclaiming any motivation of profit or reputation. Zhū's editorial framing reflects the new pedagogy of the early-PRC era: the reader is urged to "from the clinical text discover the principles of pattern-distinction; from the formula-methods discover the principles of drug use; from the relative comparison and the contrary comparison discover the correct viewpoint of Chinese medical theory". The Zhū Zǔ-yí annotated edition is the standard modern recension of the *Yī-chún shèng-yì*. Biographical detail otherwise unrecovered.
