Wáng Jiǔ-fēng 王九峰 (Wáng Zhī-zhèng 王之政), c. 1753–1831, native of Dān-tú 丹徒 (now Zhèn-jiāng, Jiāngsū). The leading Zhèn-jiāng physician of the late-Qiánlóng to Dào-guāng period and the most famous medical disciple of [[徐大椿]] Xú Dà-chūn (Xú Líng-tāi, 1693–1771). After Xú's death he carried on the Wú-jiāng-school *Shāng-hán* classicism in the Zhèn-jiāng / Dān-tú area, and his clinical practice drew patients from across the lower Yangtze. His senior disciple Jiǎng Bǎo-sù 蔣寶素 preserved many of his cases in *Jiǎng Bǎo-sù yī-lüè* 蔣寶素醫略, the principal pre-Tài-píng-war source for his clinical activity. Two surviving Republican-period editions of his cases — [[KR3ep076]] and [[KR3ep077]] — were compiled by Yán Dūn-yì 嚴敦益 in 1923 from multiple manuscript recoveries.
