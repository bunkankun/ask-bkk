**Sonben** 尊辨 (also written 尊辧) — Medieval Japanese **Hossō** 法相 (Yogācāra / Faxiang) scholar-monk, known from the colophon of his only surviving canonical work [[KR6o0080]] *Qǐ-xìn-lùn chāo-chū* 起信論抄出 (T69n2283), a two-fascicle question-and-answer exposition of [[KR6o0078]] *Dà-chéng qǐ-xìn-lùn* 大乘起信論 (T1666) using Hossō scholastic categories. Precise lifedates are not preserved in standard biographical sources. On internal grounds — the work cites Fǎzàng 法藏 (Hossō reception of his *Yìjì*) and the standard Heian-era Hossō *inmyō* terminology, but no Kamakura material — composition is conventionally placed in the late Heian period.
