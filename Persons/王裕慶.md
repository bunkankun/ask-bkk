Late-Qīng physician of Húnán; courtesy name 祉庭 *Zhǐ-tíng*. Self-described in his preface as a thirty-year student of *qí-huáng* 岐黃 medicine (i.e. classical Chinese medicine) who came late to laryngology. Author of [[KR3em032]] *Bái-hóu biàn-zhèng* (composition window ca. 1880, Jiāngxī reprint *Guāng-xù* 壬寅 / 1902), a polemical *Shāng-hán*-school critique of the cooling-only diphtheria therapy advocated by his Húnán neighbour [[張紹修]] and propagated in [[KR3em031]] *Bái-hóu jié-yào hé-biān*. Wáng argues that *bái-hóu* is not in itself a *yì* 疫 (epidemic disease-category) but a manifestation site for ordinary cold-or-heat imbalance, and that mis-applied cooling is the principal cause of *bái-hóu* mortality.
