Late-Qīng physician of Húnán; courtesy name 祉庭 *Zhǐtíng*. Self-described in his preface as a thirty-year student of *qíhuáng* 岐黃 medicine (i.e. classical Chinese medicine) who came late to laryngology. Author of [[KR3em032]] *Báihóu biànzhèng* (composition window ca. 1880, Jiāngxī reprint *Guāngxù* 壬寅 / 1902), a polemical *Shānghán*-school critique of the cooling-only diphtheria therapy advocated by his Húnán neighbour [[張紹修]] and propagated in [[KR3em031]] *Báihóu jiéyào hébiān*. Wáng argues that *báihóu* is not in itself a *yì* 疫 (epidemic disease-category) but a manifestation site for ordinary cold-or-heat imbalance, and that mis-applied cooling is the principal cause of *báihóu* mortality.
