**Líng Mèngchū** 淩濛初 (1580–1644; CBDB id 691000), *zì* Xuánfáng 玄房, *hào* Jíkōng Guān Zhǔrén 即空觀主人. A native of Wūchéng 烏程 (modern Húzhōu 湖州, Zhèjiāng). He repeatedly failed the imperial examinations, despite coming from a literati family, and turned to commercial publishing and literary composition in Sūzhōu 蘇州 and Hángzhōu 杭州. Late in life he obtained a minor official post in Xúzhōu 徐州 and died there in 1644, during the chaos of the Ming–Qing transition.

He is best known as the compiler of the *Erpai* 二拍 collections: [[KR4k0043]] *Chūkè Pāi'àn Jīngqí* 初刻拍案驚奇 (1628) and [[KR4k0048]] *Èrkè Pāi'àn Jīngqí* 二刻拍案驚奇 (1632), together comprising 78 vernacular short stories (*huàběn* 話本) and forming the second half of the *Sānyán Erpāi* 三言二拍 canonical corpus (alongside Féng Mènglóng's 馮夢龍 *Sānyán* 三言). He also compiled *Nánxī* southern drama texts and wrote dramatic and poetic works.
