The transliteration **Āmójiā** 阿謨伽 (= Sanskrit *Amogha*, "Unfailing") is one of the recorded alternate Chinese names of the Tang-period Tantric master **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]], 705–774; full transliteration **Āmùqūbázhéluó** 阿目佉跋折羅, abbreviated **A-mó-jiā** = *Amogha-* without the *-vajra* element). Some Xùzàngjīng-only Esoteric ritual texts (including the present *Mìmì yàoshù fǎ* 祕密要術法 [[KR6j0226]]) are catalogued under this name rather than the more common 不空 — likely reflecting a manuscript-line transmission distinct from the Taishō recension.

The DILA authority does not assign a separate id to **阿謨伽** as a person, treating it as an alternate name of A000095 (不空). For corpus-level cataloguing in the present knowledgebase, however, since the Kanripo catalog meta records the translator name as **阿謨伽**, this entry preserves the catalog form. For the full biographical treatment see [[不空]].
