Hú Míngyù 胡鳴玉 (*fl.* 1736; lifedates not securely known), *zì* Tíngpèi 廷佩, *hào* Yínōu 吟鷗. Native of Qīngpǔ 青浦 in Sòngjiāng 松江 prefecture (in modern Shànghǎi). *Suì gòng shēng* (annual-quota provincial student). Recommended for the *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 special examination of Qiánlóng 1 (1736); did not pass. Compiler of the *Dìng é zá lù* 訂譌雜錄 ([[KR3j0078]]), a 10-juan high-Qiánlóng-period evidential miscellany on phonological and lexicographical errors. Otherwise unrecorded; not in CBDB.
