Major works:
- **Yuán-dùn zōng-yǎn** 圓頓宗眼 ([[KR6d0234]], X57n0958, 1 *juan*) — Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* essay on the perfect-sudden doctrine.
- **Yì zhōng-xīng jiào-guān** 議中興教觀 ([[KR6d0235]], X57n0959, 1 *juan*) — discussion of the mid-revival of Tiāntái doctrine-and-contemplation.
