**Tián Jiānlái** 田間來 (*fl.* mid-to-late Qīng), Qīng compiler-expander of [[沈銘三]] (Shěn Míngsān)'s original manuscript, producing the present recension of the *Língyàn liángfāng huìbiān* 靈驗良方彙編 ([[KR3ed150]]). Sparse biographical record.
