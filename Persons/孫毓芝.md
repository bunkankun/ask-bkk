Sūn Yùzhī 孫毓芝 (fl. 19th c.) was a Shānxī figure said to have transmitted the manuscript of the *Fùshì nánkē* ([[KR3eh034]]) from the Luó family before it reached [[王道平]] Wáng Dàopíng's 1863 first printing.
