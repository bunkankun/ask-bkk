Sòng Jiǒng 宋褧 (1294–1346), *zì* Xiǎnfū 顯夫, native of Dàdū 大都 (Yānjīng / Běijīng region). Tàidìng 1 (1324) *jìnshì*. Rose to *Hànlín zhíxuéshì* concurrently *jīngyán jiǎngguān*; posthumous name 文清 *Wénqīng*. With his elder brother **Sòng Běn** 宋本 (the senior of two distinguished brothers from the same family, both in the *guǎngé*), contemporaries called them the "Greater and Lesser Sòng" 大宋 / 小宋 (after the famous Sòng-dynasty Sòng Yǎzhī and Sòng Qí brothers). His collection *Yānshí jí* [[KR4d0512]] was edited by his nephew **Kuò** 彍 (*Tàicháng fènglǐ láng*). Recommended for the Zhèjiāng *xíngzhōngshūshěng* re-cut in Zhìzhèng 8 (1348). Prefaces by Ōuyáng Xuán [[歐陽玄]], Sū Tiānjué [[蘇天爵]], Xǔ Yǒurén [[許有壬]], Lǚ Sīchéng 呂思誠, Wēi Sù 危素 — a roster of late-Yuán *guǎngé* eminences. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes Sòng's defect of *cái duō, jù huò bùjiǎn* (talent too abundant, occasionally letting *jù* slip past inspection).
