Sòng Jiǒng 宋褧 (1294–1346), *zì* Xiǎn-fū 顯夫, native of Dà-dū 大都 (Yān-jīng / Běi-jīng region). Tài-dìng 1 (1324) *jìnshì*. Rose to *Hàn-lín zhí-xué-shì* concurrently *jīng-yán jiǎng-guān*; posthumous name 文清 *Wén-qīng*. With his elder brother **Sòng Běn** 宋本 (the senior of two distinguished brothers from the same family, both in the *guǎn-gé*), contemporaries called them the "Greater and Lesser Sòng" 大宋 / 小宋 (after the famous Sòng-dynasty Sòng Yǎ-zhī and Sòng Qí brothers). His collection *Yān-shí jí* [[KR4d0512]] was edited by his nephew **Kuò** 彍 (*Tài-cháng fèng-lǐ láng*). Recommended for the Zhè-jiāng *xíng-zhōng-shū-shěng* re-cut in Zhì-zhèng 8 (1348). Prefaces by Ōuyáng Xuán [[歐陽玄]], Sū Tiān-jué [[蘇天爵]], Xǔ Yǒu-rén [[許有壬]], Lǚ Sī-chéng 呂思誠, Wēi Sù 危素 — a roster of late-Yuán *guǎn-gé* eminences. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes Sòng's defect of *cái duō, jù huò bù-jiǎn* (talent too abundant, occasionally letting *jù* slip past inspection).
