# Huìwēi 慧威

Early-Táng Tiāntái monk based at Zuǒxī 左溪, grand-disciple of [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ in the Tiāntái lineage; teacher of [[玄覺]] Yǒngjiā Xuánjué in the *zhǐguān* tradition before Xuánjué's encounter with [[惠能]] Huìnéng.
