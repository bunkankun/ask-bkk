Gondō Seikyō 權藤成卿 (1868–1937) was a Japanese political and social thinker, known for his agrarian-cooperatist *jichi-shugi* 自治主義 and for his sympathy with early-Shōwa medical revivalism. He wrote a 1933 preface to [[大塚敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu's *Zhōngguó nèikē yījiàn* ([[KR3eh027]]).
