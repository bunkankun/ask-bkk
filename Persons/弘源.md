Hóng-yuán 弘源, *zì* **Hān-táng** 憨堂 ("Honest Hall"). Late-17th-century monk of the **Yí-líng** 彝陵 region (Húběi). Lifedates unknown. Author of the principal preface to [[KR6q0551]] 《法幢遠禪師語錄》 — the *yǔ-lù* of **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** [[智遠]] — signed at **Yí-líng** on **康熙三十四年四月佛誕日** = **1695-05-21** (Buddha's Birthday).

- - - - -

Not to be confused with **Yǒng-chéng Hóng-yuán** 永澄弘源 (DILA A046709), a separate Míng/Qīng-transition monk.
