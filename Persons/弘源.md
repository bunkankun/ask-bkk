Hóngyuán 弘源, *zì* **Hāntáng** 憨堂 ("Honest Hall"). Late-17th-century monk of the **Yílíng** 彝陵 region (Húběi). Lifedates unknown. Author of the principal preface to [[KR6q0551]] 《法幢遠禪師語錄》 — the *yǔlù* of **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** [[智遠]] — signed at **Yílíng** on **康熙三十四年四月佛誕日** = **1695-05-21** (Buddha's Birthday).

- - - - -

Not to be confused with **Yǒngchéng Hóngyuán** 永澄弘源 (DILA A046709), a separate Míng/Qīng-transition monk.
