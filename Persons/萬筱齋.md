# Wàn Xiǎozhāi 萬筱齋

Mid-Míng paediatrician, *hào* Júxuān 菊軒翁. Second-generation bearer of the Wàn-family medical lineage; son of [[萬蘭]] and father of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán. In the *Chénghuà gēngzǐ* year (1480) he relocated from Yùzhāng 豫章 to Luótián 羅田 (modern Húběi), where the family practice was thereafter centred. Lifedates unrecorded.
