Late-Qīng official, mathematician, phonologist, and educational reformer; native of Tóng-xiāng 桐鄉, Zhèjiāng. CBDB id 61773 (1843–1921). *Jìnshì* 1871; served in the Hàn-lín Academy and held a series of provincial education postings, culminating in *Jiāngsū tí-xué-shǐ* 提學使 (Provincial Education Commissioner) in the *Xuān-tǒng* period. Best known as the author of the *Jiǎn-zì pǔ* 簡字譜 (1907) and other phonetic-script reform proposals, the *Gǔ-chóu suàn-xué* 古籌算學, and as one of the post-1911 Qing-loyalist scholars who continued to support the restoration cause. Wrote the second preface to [[KR3em034]] *Hóu-kē jīn-yuè quán-shū* in mid-summer 1911, extending the throat-as-strategic-pass metaphor across cosmology, geography, and government. CBDB also has a second entry id 589469 with no dates, likely a homonym variant.
