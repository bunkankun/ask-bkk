Late-Qīng official, mathematician, phonologist, and educational reformer; native of Tóngxiāng 桐鄉, Zhèjiāng. CBDB id 61773 (1843–1921). *Jìnshì* 1871; served in the Hànlín Academy and held a series of provincial education postings, culminating in *Jiāngsū tíxuéshǐ* 提學使 (Provincial Education Commissioner) in the *Xuāntǒng* period. Best known as the author of the *Jiǎnzì pǔ* 簡字譜 (1907) and other phonetic-script reform proposals, the *Gǔchóu suànxué* 古籌算學, and as one of the post-1911 Qing-loyalist scholars who continued to support the restoration cause. Wrote the second preface to [[KR3em034]] *Hóukē jīnyuè quánshū* in mid-summer 1911, extending the throat-as-strategic-pass metaphor across cosmology, geography, and government.
