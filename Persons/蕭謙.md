**Xiāo Qiān** 蕭謙 (*zì* Zǐyù 子豫), Míng-period scholar of Cháng-ān 長安. *Tóng-nián* 同年 (same-year examination cohort) of Guō Dìng-jìng 郭定靜, magistrate of Pī-zhōu 邳州 who first recovered the manuscript of the paediatric compendium *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]]) from his county archives. Xiāo wrote the second preface (*xù-èr*) to the 1506 *Zhèng-dé* printing, the most philologically careful of the three: he expressed open doubt about the conventional attribution to Lǔ Bósì 魯伯嗣, citing the absence of independent evidence (*nài wú zuǒ yàn* 奈無左驗) and the overlap with material plausibly traceable to Qián Yǐ 錢乙's lost *Yīng-rú lùn bǎi-piān*. Lifedates and exam record uncertain in the standard biographical sources.
