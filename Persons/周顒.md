Liú Sòng / Southern Qí lay Buddhist scholar and exegete; *zì* Yànlún 彥倫. Native of Rǔnán 汝南. One of the most important early proponents of the *Sānlùn* 三論 (Mādhyamika) Buddhist philosophy in southern China and a celebrated literary stylist. Held minor offices under both dynasties. Close associate of [[明僧紹]] Míng Sēngshào and of the early Shè Shān 攝山 Buddhist community at Qī xiá Sì 棲霞寺 outside Jiànkāng. His best-known surviving work is the *Sān zōng èr dì lùn* 三宗二諦論 ("On the Two Truths of the Three Schools"), a foundational early-Chinese *Mādhyamika* treatise. Source: *Nán Qí shū* 南齊書 41; *Nán shǐ* 南史 34.
