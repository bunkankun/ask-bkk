Gōng Dǐngchén 龔鼎臣 (1010–1086), *zì* Fǔ-zhī 輔之, *hào* Dōng-yuán xiānshēng 東原先生 (whence the title of his *bǐjì* the [[KR3j0088|Dōng yuán lù]] 東原錄). Native of Yùn-zhōu Xū-chéng 鄆州須城 (modern Dōng-píng 東平, Shān-dōng). *Jǐng-yòu* 1 (1034) *jìnshì*; rose through *Jiàn-yì dà-fū* (Right Remonstrating Grandee), *Jīng-dōng dōng-lù ān-fǔ-shǐ* (Eastern Capital Pacification Commissioner), *Zhī Qīng-zhōu* (Prefect of Qīng-zhōu); transferred to *Tài-zhōng dà-fū, Tí-jǔ Bó-zhōu Tài-qīng-gōng* (a *fèng-cí* sinecure); retired with *Zhèng-yì dà-fū* rank. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 320. Active in opposition to Wáng Ān-shí's *Xī-níng* reforms.
