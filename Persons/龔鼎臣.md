Gōng Dǐngchén 龔鼎臣 (1010–1086), *zì* Fǔzhī 輔之, *hào* Dōngyuán xiānshēng 東原先生 (whence the title of his *bǐjì* the [[KR3j0088|Dōng yuán lù]] 東原錄). Native of Yùnzhōu Xūchéng 鄆州須城 (modern Dōngpíng 東平, Shāndōng). *Jǐngyòu* 1 (1034) *jìnshì*; rose through *Jiànyì dàfū* (Right Remonstrating Grandee), *Jīngdōng dōnglù ānfǔshǐ* (Eastern Capital Pacification Commissioner), *Zhī Qīngzhōu* (Prefect of Qīngzhōu); transferred to *Tàizhōng dàfū, Tíjǔ Bózhōu Tàiqīnggōng* (a *fèngcí* sinecure); retired with *Zhèngyì dàfū* rank. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 320. Active in opposition to Wáng Ānshí's *Xīníng* reforms.
