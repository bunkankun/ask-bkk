# 戴東石 Dài Dōngshí

**Dài Dōngshí** 戴東石 (titled *Shàocān* 少參), mid-Míng official; supervised the editorial preparation of the 1537 edition of [[高武]] Gāo Wǔ's *Zhēnjiǔ Sù Nàn yàozhǐ* 針灸素難要旨 ([[KR3ee021]]).
