**Lǐ Jí** 李籍 (*fl.* Táng), Táng-period mathematician-and-philologist. Author of phonetic-and-meaning glosses (*yīn-yì* 音義) on the foundational Chinese mathematical classics, including the *Zhōubì suàn-jīng yīn-yì* 周髀算經音義 ([[KR3f0001]]'s appended 1 juan), the *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-shù yīn-yì* 九章算術音義, and others. Lǐ has no further transmitted biographical record.
