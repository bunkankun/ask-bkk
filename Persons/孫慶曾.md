# Sūn Qìngzēng 孫慶曾

Early-Qīng physician of Wúxī 無錫 (Jiāngsū); fellow-student of [[葉天士]] Yè Tiānshì (Yè Guì) in paediatric *dòuzhěn* 痘疹 practice. Master and clinical instructor of [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn'áo (1717–1776); his paediatric expertise — especially in smallpox — is acknowledged by Shěn in the preface to *Yòukē shìmí* 幼科釋謎 ([[KR3ej004]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
