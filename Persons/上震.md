Shàngzhèn 上震. Sole Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hèfēng Jìwù** [[濟悟]] (1626–1687). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0540]] 《鶴峰禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B421, prefaced 1692 and 1695.
