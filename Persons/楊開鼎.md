# Yáng Kāidǐng 楊開鼎

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīng-diàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as one of the *kǎozhèng* compilers for the *Sòng shǐ* (KR2a0032) in the *Sìkù quánshū*, working alongside [[林蒲封]] and [[齊召南]]. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
