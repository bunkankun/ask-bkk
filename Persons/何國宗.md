# Hé Guózōng 何國宗

Native of Dàxīng 大興 (Beijing area; some sources give Héběi). Birth year not securely recorded; died Qiánlóng 31 (1766).

*Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 51 (1712), *jíshì* (probationary appointment) at the Hànlín. Senior position in Kāngxī's mathematical-and-astronomical academy (the *Méngyǎng zhāi* 蒙養齋, established 1713 as the imperial workshop for the *Lǜlì yuānyuán* compilation). Successive offices: *Hànlínyuàn shìjiǎngxuéshì* 翰林院侍讲学士 (Hànlín Reader-Lecturer); *Lǐbù shìláng* 禮部侍郎 (Vice Minister of Rites); *Jiǎnzhèng* 檢正 (Inspector). 

Lead Chinese-mathematical-staff editor of all three principal volumes of the *Lǜlì yuānyuán* synthesis: the [[KR3f0018]] *Lìxiàng kǎochéng* (Investigation of the Calendrical Phenomena), the *Lǜlǚ zhèngyì* (Pitch-Pipes), and the *Shùlǐ jīngyùn* (Mathematics). His name appears as the senior *huìbiān* 彙編 (gathering-and-compiling editor) on the *Lìxiàng kǎochéng* personnel list of Yōngzhèng 2 (1724). Hé continued as principal editor on the Qián-lóng-period [[KR3f0019]] *Lìxiàng kǎochéng hòubiān* (1742) and the [[KR3f0020]] *Qīndìng yíxiàng kǎochéng* (1744) — his sustained service across three reigns making him probably the single most consequential Chinese-mathematical bureaucrat of the high-Qīng synthesis-period. 

His brothers Hé Guódòng 何國棟 (Magistrate of Tīngzhōu in Fújiàn) and Hé Guózhù 何國柱 (Director-of-the-*Qīntiānjiān*-Left-Section) also held offices in the *Méngyǎng zhāi* / *Qīntiānjiān* circuit; the Hé family of Dàxīng was one of the principal hereditary mathematical-astronomical lines of the high Qīng.
