# Hé Guózōng 何國宗

Native of Dà-xīng 大興 (Beijing area; some sources give Hé-běi). Birth year not securely recorded; died Qián-lóng 31 (1766).

*Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 51 (1712), *jí-shì* (probationary appointment) at the Hànlín. Senior position in Kāngxī's mathematical-and-astronomical academy (the *Méng-yǎng zhāi* 蒙養齋, established 1713 as the imperial workshop for the *Lǜ-lì yuān-yuán* compilation). Successive offices: *Hànlín-yuàn shì-jiǎng-xué-shì* 翰林院侍讲学士 (Hànlín Reader-Lecturer); *Lǐ-bù shì-láng* 禮部侍郎 (Vice Minister of Rites); *Jiǎn-zhèng* 檢正 (Inspector). 

Lead Chinese-mathematical-staff editor of all three principal volumes of the *Lǜ-lì yuān-yuán* synthesis: the [[KR3f0018]] *Lì-xiàng kǎo-chéng* (Investigation of the Calendrical Phenomena), the *Lǜ-lǚ zhèng-yì* (Pitch-Pipes), and the *Shù-lǐ jīng-yùn* (Mathematics). His name appears as the senior *huì-biān* 彙編 (gathering-and-compiling editor) on the *Lì-xiàng kǎo-chéng* personnel list of Yōngzhèng 2 (1724). Hé continued as principal editor on the Qián-lóng-period [[KR3f0019]] *Lì-xiàng kǎo-chéng hòu-biān* (1742) and the [[KR3f0020]] *Qīn-dìng yí-xiàng kǎo-chéng* (1744) — his sustained service across three reigns making him probably the single most consequential Chinese-mathematical bureaucrat of the high-Qīng synthesis-period. 

His brothers Hé Guódòng 何國棟 (Magistrate of Tīng-zhōu in Fú-jiàn) and Hé Guózhù 何國柱 (Director-of-the-*Qīntiān-jiān*-Left-Section) also held offices in the *Méng-yǎng zhāi* / *Qīntiān-jiān* circuit; the Hé family of Dà-xīng was one of the principal hereditary mathematical-astronomical lines of the high Qīng.
