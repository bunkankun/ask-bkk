**Míngzhōng Jiàorén** 名中教人 is the pseudonymous author of the Qīng romance novel *Hǎo Qiú Zhuàn* 好逑傳 [[KR4k0126]]. The pen name is a cryptic phrase, variously interpreted as a riddling self-description. The author's true identity has not been established.
