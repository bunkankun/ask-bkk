# 朱吉川

Zhū Jíchuān

Early-Qīng layman of Yúnqī 雲捿 who recovered an old Yúnqī printing of the Huáyán *Vajracchedikā* commentary apparatus and sponsored the reissue that survives as [[KR6c0079]] *Jīngāng jīng shū jì kē huì*.
