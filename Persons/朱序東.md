Late-Qīng physician of Lánzhōu 蘭州 (Gānsù), the founder of the Lánzhōu Zhūshì hereditary medical lineage. Documented through a Gānsù *ànchá* 案察 Dèng Chéngwěi 鄧承偉's plaque inscription *Nián gāo déshào* 年高德邵 ("Aged in years, lofty in virtue"), inscribed after Zhū cured Dèng's son of smallpox following the deaths of his previous children. Composed eyebrow-notes on Chéng Guópéng's *Yīxué xīnwù* (1732) — preserved as [[KR3er113]] *Yīxué xīnwù zázhèng yàoyì* 醫學心悟雜症要義. The notes were supplemented by his son [[朱儀亭]] Zhū Yítíng and finally edited for publication by his great-grandson Zhū Tàiyán 朱太岩 in 1991. Not in CBDB.
