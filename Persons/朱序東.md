Late-Qīng physician of Lán-zhōu 蘭州 (Gān-sù), the founder of the Lán-zhōu Zhū-shì hereditary medical lineage. Documented through a Gān-sù *àn-chá* 案察 Dèng Chéng-wěi 鄧承偉's plaque inscription *Nián gāo dé-shào* 年高德邵 ("Aged in years, lofty in virtue"), inscribed after Zhū cured Dèng's son of smallpox following the deaths of his previous children. Composed eyebrow-notes on Chéng Guó-péng's *Yī-xué xīn-wù* (1732) — preserved as [[KR3er113]] *Yī-xué xīn-wù zá-zhèng yào-yì* 醫學心悟雜症要義. The notes were supplemented by his son [[朱儀亭]] Zhū Yí-tíng and finally edited for publication by his great-grandson Zhū Tài-yán 朱太岩 in 1991. Not in CBDB.
