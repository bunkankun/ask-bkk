# Zìwù 自悟

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xīsǒu Shàotán (d. by 1279); principal named editor of the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
