# Zì-wù 自悟

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xī-sǒu Shào-tán (d. by 1279); principal named editor of the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
