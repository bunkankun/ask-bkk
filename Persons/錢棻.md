Qián Fēn 錢棻 of Jiā-shàn / Jiā-xīng 嘉善 / 嘉興 (Zhè-jiāng), son of [[錢士升]] Qián Shì-shēng (1575–1652, *Chóng-zhēn*-era *Lǐ-bù shì-láng* and Grand Secretary). Documented in two editorial capacities in the Kanripo corpus:

1. As disciple of the *Dōng-lín* martyr [[魏大中]] (1575–1625), the first editor and publisher of the *Máo-yán jí* 茅檐集 ([[KR4e0243]]) of [[魏學洢]] (1596–1625) shortly after the editor's father and Wèi Xué-yī both died in 1625; the WYG text descends not from his first impression but from the later re-engraving by [[魏學濂]].

2. As fellow-townsman of Wú Zhèn 吳鎭 in Jiā-xīng (the Yuán painter), editor (*jūn-shí*) of the *Méi-huā dào-rén yí-mò* 梅花道人遺墨 ([[KR4d0539]]), assembling *tí-huà zhī zuò* (inscription-on-painting pieces) into a 2-*juǎn* edition. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes Qián's editorial failures — including the inclusion of Xiān-yú Shū's 鮮于樞 *tí-zhú shī* mistakenly under Wú's name (which Wú had merely copied) — and the inclusion of *zàn-shǒu Qū-bàn* (suspect counterfeit) pieces like the *Qìn-yuán-chūn* on a skeleton and the *Jiā-hé bā-jǐng* *Jiǔ-quán-zǐ* lyrics.

No precise lifedates located; floruit 1620s–1650s. CBDB 689514.
