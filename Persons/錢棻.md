Qián Fēn 錢棻 (Míng), fellow-townsman of Wú Zhèn [[吳鎭]] in Jiā-xīng. Editor (*jūn-shí*) of the *Méi-huā dào-rén yí-mò* 梅花道人遺墨 [[KR4d0539]], assembling *tí-huà zhī zuò* (inscription-on-painting pieces) into a 2-*juǎn* edition. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes Qián's editorial failures — including the inclusion of Xiān-yú Shū's 鮮于樞 *tí-zhú shī* mistakenly under Wú's name (which Wú had merely copied) — and the inclusion of *zàn-shǒu Qū-bàn* (suspect counterfeit) pieces like the *Qìn-yuán-chūn* on a skeleton and the *Jiā-hé bā-jǐng* *Jiǔ-quán-zǐ* lyrics.
