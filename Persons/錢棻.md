Qián Fēn 錢棻 of Jiāshàn / Jiāxīng 嘉善 / 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng), son of [[錢士升]] Qián Shìshēng (1575–1652, *Chóngzhēn*-era *Lǐbù shìláng* and Grand Secretary). Documented in two editorial capacities in the Kanripo corpus:

1. As disciple of the *Dōnglín* martyr [[魏大中]] (1575–1625), the first editor and publisher of the *Máoyán jí* 茅檐集 ([[KR4e0243]]) of [[魏學洢]] (1596–1625) shortly after the editor's father and Wèi Xuéyī both died in 1625; the WYG text descends not from his first impression but from the later re-engraving by [[魏學濂]].

2. As fellow-townsman of Wú Zhèn 吳鎭 in Jiāxīng (the Yuán painter), editor (*jūnshí*) of the *Méihuā dàorén yímò* 梅花道人遺墨 ([[KR4d0539]]), assembling *tíhuà zhī zuò* (inscription-on-painting pieces) into a 2-*juǎn* edition. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes Qián's editorial failures — including the inclusion of Xiānyú Shū's 鮮于樞 *tízhú shī* mistakenly under Wú's name (which Wú had merely copied) — and the inclusion of *zànshǒu Qūbàn* (suspect counterfeit) pieces like the *Qìnyuánchūn* on a skeleton and the *Jiāhé bājǐng* *Jiǔquánzǐ* lyrics.

No precise lifedates located; floruit 1620s–1650s. CBDB 689514.
