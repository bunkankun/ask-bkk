Chén Xìng-dìng 陳性定 (*hào* Shǎo-wēi 少微), Yuán-period Daoist of the *Yù-xū-gōng* 玉虛宫 on Mt. Xiān-dū / Jìn-yún 仙都/縉雲 in Chù-zhōu 處州 (modern Lì-shuǐ 麗水, Zhèjiāng). His sole transmitted work is the *Xiān-dū zhì* 仙都志 [[KR5b0307]] (DZ 602), compiled in two juǎn and prefaced in the fifth month of Zhì-zhèng 8 (1348) under Yuán Shùn-dì. Chén styles himself *Yù-xū zhù-shān Shǎo-wēi* 玉虛住山少微 ("Shǎo-wēi, resident of Yù-xū[gong]") in the work's table of compilers. The cult-site he served was the canonical twenty-ninth of the thirty-six Daoist grotto-heavens (the *Xuán-dū qí-xiān dòng-tiān* 玄都祈仙洞天) and the legendary place of Huáng-dì's 黃帝 ascent on the *huǒ-lóng* 火龍.
