Chén Xìngdìng 陳性定 (*hào* Shǎowēi 少微), Yuán-period Daoist of the *Yùxūgōng* 玉虛宫 on Mt. Xiāndū / Jìnyún 仙都/縉雲 in Chùzhōu 處州 (modern Lìshuǐ 麗水, Zhèjiāng). His sole transmitted work is the *Xiāndū zhì* 仙都志 [[KR5b0307]] (DZ 602), compiled in two juǎn and prefaced in the fifth month of Zhìzhèng 8 (1348) under Yuán Shùndì. Chén styles himself *Yùxū zhùshān Shǎowēi* 玉虛住山少微 ("Shǎowēi, resident of Yùxū[gong]") in the work's table of compilers. The cult-site he served was the canonical twenty-ninth of the thirty-six Daoist grotto-heavens (the *Xuándū qíxiān dòngtiān* 玄都祈仙洞天) and the legendary place of Huángdì's 黃帝 ascent on the *huǒlóng* 火龍.
