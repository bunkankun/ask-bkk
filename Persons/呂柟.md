Lǚ Nán 呂柟 (1479–1542), *zì* Zhòngmù 仲木, *hào* Jīngyě 涇野, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was a leading Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* official of Gāolíng 高陵 (Shǎnxī, Wèinán region). *Zhuàngyuán* (top *jìnshì*) of *Zhèngdé wùchén* (1508); appointed *Hànlín xiūzhuàn*; rose to *Nánjīng Lǐbù yòu shìláng*. His learning derives from his master Xuē Jìngzhī 薛敬之 (a direct disciple of Xuē Xuān, [[KR3a0079]] author) — making Lǚ a third-generation *Hé-Dōng xuépài* figure. At Nánjīng he held lecture-sessions jointly with Zhàn Ruòshuǐ 湛若水 ([[KR3a0094]] author, the *gānquán* school founder) and Zōu Shǒuyì 鄒守益 (a major Wáng Yángmíng disciple) — making him one of the key Mid-Míng intersection-figures between the *Hé-Dōng* / *Gānquán* / *Yáojiāng* lines. He was a sharp critic of Wáng Yángmíng's *liáng zhī* doctrine. Major works: the *Jīngyězǐ nèi piān* 涇野子內篇 ([[KR3a0087]], 27 juan, his comprehensive *yǔlù*); plus the four "*chāo shì*" series — *Zhōuzǐ chāo shì* 周子抄釋 ([[KR3a0089]]), *Zhāngzǐ chāo shì* 張子抄釋 ([[KR3a0090]]), *Èr-Chéngzǐ chāo shì* 二程子抄釋 ([[KR3a0091]]), *Zhūzǐ chāo shì* 朱子抄釋 ([[KR3a0092]]) — abridged-and-glossed selections from the four foundational Northern-Sòng *daoxué* sources. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 33860, dates 1479–1542.
