Lǚ Nán 呂柟 (1479–1542), *zì* Zhòngmù 仲木, *hào* Jīngyě 涇野, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was a leading Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* official of Gāo-líng 高陵 (Shǎn-xī, Wèi-nán region). *Zhuàng-yuán* (top *jìnshì*) of *Zhèng-dé wù-chén* (1508); appointed *Hàn-lín xiū-zhuàn*; rose to *Nán-jīng Lǐ-bù yòu shì-láng*. His learning derives from his master Xuē Jìng-zhī 薛敬之 (a direct disciple of Xuē Xuān, KR3a0079 author) — making Lǚ a third-generation *Hé-Dōng xué-pài* figure. At Nán-jīng he held lecture-sessions jointly with Zhàn Ruòshuǐ 湛若水 (KR3a0094 author, the *gān-quán* school founder) and Zōu Shǒuyì 鄒守益 (a major Wáng Yáng-míng disciple) — making him one of the key Mid-Míng intersection-figures between the *Hé-Dōng* / *Gān-quán* / *Yáo-jiāng* lines. He was a sharp critic of Wáng Yáng-míng's *liáng zhī* doctrine. Major works: the *Jīng-yě-zǐ nèi piān* 涇野子內篇 (KR3a0087, 27 juan, his comprehensive *yǔ-lù*); plus the four "*chāo shì*" series — *Zhōu-zǐ chāo shì* 周子抄釋 (KR3a0089), *Zhāng-zǐ chāo shì* 張子抄釋 (KR3a0090), *Èr-Chéng-zǐ chāo shì* 二程子抄釋 (KR3a0091), *Zhū-zǐ chāo shì* 朱子抄釋 (KR3a0092) — abridged-and-glossed selections from the four foundational Northern-Sòng *daoxué* sources. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rú-lín zhuàn). CBDB id 33860, dates 1479–1542.
