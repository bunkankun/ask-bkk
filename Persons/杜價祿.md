**Dù Jiàlù** 杜價祿 (*zì* Bīnwǔ 賓五), Qīng-period stipended licentiate (*lǐnshēng* 廩生) of Jiāngyīn 江陰 (Jiāngsū), *fl. Dàoguāng* era. Prefaced Wáng Xīxīn 王錫鑫's paediatric handbook *Yòukē qièyào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]]) in 1847 (*Dàoguāng* 27 *dīngwèi*) at Chóngqìng during a visit to the author. The preface is dated the same day as Wáng's self-preface — both signed *Dàoguāng* 27 *guìyuè* 桂月 (eighth lunar month) first day, suggesting a small Chóngqìng publication ceremony attended by both. No other biographical detail recorded.
