**Dù Jià-lù** 杜價祿 (*zì* Bīn-wǔ 賓五), Qīng-period stipended licentiate (*lǐn-shēng* 廩生) of Jiāng-yīn 江陰 (Jiāng-sū), *fl. Dào-guāng* era. Prefaced Wáng Xī-xīn 王錫鑫's paediatric handbook *Yòu-kē qiè-yào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]]) in 1847 (*Dào-guāng* 27 *dīng-wèi*) at Chóng-qìng during a visit to the author. The preface is dated the same day as Wáng's self-preface — both signed *Dào-guāng* 27 *guì-yuè* 桂月 (eighth lunar month) first day, suggesting a small Chóng-qìng publication ceremony attended by both. No other biographical detail recorded.
