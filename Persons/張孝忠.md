**Zhāng Xiàozhōng** 張孝忠 (*fl.* early 13th c., 南宋), Southern-Sòng author of a 拔 (colophon) to [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ's *Zhù jiě Shānghán lùn* 註解傷寒論 ([[KR3ef005]]) dated Kāixǐ 開禧 1 (1205); the second early biographical witness for Chéng.
