# Dézhēn 德真

A late-Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng-era *Línjì* 臨濟 Chán master, abbot of **Fǎzhuàngshān** 法幢山. *Hào* **Xūzhōu** 虛舟 ("Empty-Boat"), conventionally cited as **Fǎzhuàngshān Xūzhōu zhǎnglǎo** 法幢山虛舟長老. According to his own *zìxù* of the **《淨土紺珠》** *Jìngtǔ gànzhū* [[KR6p0121]] (X1202), composed at *Fǎzhuàng* in *jǐmǎo* (most plausibly 1819 = *Jiāqìng* 24), he had by that point been teaching the *Línjì* doctrine for some forty-plus years (*sìshí yú nián fǎyán xióngtú* 四十餘年法筵雄徒); his Pure Land turn is presented as a pastoral response to the *mòfǎ* 末法 condition of declining capacities. The *Jìngtǔ gànzhū* — a single-*juǎn* numerically-organised Pure Land doctrinal anthology — is his principal extant work. Lifedates and other biographical details not preserved.
