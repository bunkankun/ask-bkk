# Dé-zhēn 德真

A late-Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng-era *Lín-jì* 臨濟 Chán master, abbot of **Fǎ-zhuàng-shān** 法幢山. *Hào* **Xū-zhōu** 虛舟 ("Empty-Boat"), conventionally cited as **Fǎ-zhuàng-shān Xū-zhōu zhǎng-lǎo** 法幢山虛舟長老. According to his own *zì-xù* of the **《淨土紺珠》** *Jìng-tǔ gàn-zhū* [[KR6p0121]] (X1202), composed at *Fǎ-zhuàng* in *jǐ-mǎo* (most plausibly 1819 = *Jiā-qìng* 24), he had by that point been teaching the *Lín-jì* doctrine for some forty-plus years (*sì-shí yú nián fǎ-yán xióng-tú* 四十餘年法筵雄徒); his Pure Land turn is presented as a pastoral response to the *mò-fǎ* 末法 condition of declining capacities. The *Jìng-tǔ gàn-zhū* — a single-*juǎn* numerically-organised Pure Land doctrinal anthology — is his principal extant work. Lifedates and other biographical details not preserved.
