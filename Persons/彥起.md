Yànqǐ 彥起 (lifedates not preserved), Southern Sòng vinaya scholar of the *Yǒngjiā Chángníngsì* 永嘉常寧寺 in Wēnzhōu 溫州 (modern Zhèjiāng). The sole datum about his life comes from the colophon to his own work *Shìmén guījìng yí hùfǎ jì* 釋門歸敬儀護法記 ([[KR6k0252]]), a three-fascicle subcommentary on *Nánshān Dàoxuān* 南山道宣's *Shìmén guījìng yí* 釋門歸敬儀 ([[KR6k0182]]), the preface of which is dated to the *Shàoxīng* 紹興 20 *gēngwǔ* year (1150) at the close of the summer-retreat. Per DILA A000826: monk; *shēngpíng bùxiáng* (life not in detail). Yànqǐ's evident concern was the erosion of *guījìng* 歸敬 ritual decorum among Sòng-period Buddhists, and he wrote his subcommentary as a corrective to perceived doctrinal looseness in the prior reading-tradition of Dàoxuān's text — most notably the schematics of the *Xuānhé* 宣和 era *Fànqí lǜshī* 梵琦律師, which he found incomplete.
