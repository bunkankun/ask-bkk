Yànqǐ 彥起 (lifedates not preserved), Southern Sòng vinaya scholar of the *Yǒngjiā Cháng-níng-sì* 永嘉常寧寺 in Wēnzhōu 溫州 (modern Zhèjiāng). The sole datum about his life comes from the colophon to his own work *Shìmén guī-jìng yí hù-fǎ jì* 釋門歸敬儀護法記 ([[KR6k0252]]), a three-fascicle subcommentary on *Nán-shān Dào-xuān* 南山道宣's *Shìmén guī-jìng yí* 釋門歸敬儀 ([[KR6k0182]]), the preface of which is dated to the *Shàoxīng* 紹興 20 *gēng-wǔ* year (1150) at the close of the summer-retreat. Per DILA A000826: monk; *shēng-píng bù-xiáng* (life not in detail). Yànqǐ's evident concern was the erosion of *guī-jìng* 歸敬 ritual decorum among Sòng-period Buddhists, and he wrote his subcommentary as a corrective to perceived doctrinal looseness in the prior reading-tradition of Dào-xuān's text — most notably the schematics of the *Xuān-hé* 宣和 era *Fàn-qí lǜ-shī* 梵琦律師, which he found incomplete.
