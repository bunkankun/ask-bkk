**Yè Xīnpéi** 葉心培 (early Qīng, fl. 1724), son of [[葉其蓁]] Yè Qízhēn; brought his father's medical manuscripts to Beijing during the 1724 examination season, where they were prefaced and printed as the *Nǚkē zhǐzhǎng* 女科指掌 ([[KR3ei027]]) and other works.
