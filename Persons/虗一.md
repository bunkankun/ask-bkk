Fāngjué Xūyī 方覺虛一 (lifedates unrecorded; active early 17th century), *hào* Xūyī 虛一 ("Empty-Unity"); late-Ming Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk.

Known only through his single surviving work, the *Zōngmén xuán jiàn tú* 宗門玄鑑圖 ([[KR6q0147]], X63 n1256), a short diagrammatic treatise on the Five Houses' doctrinal-symbolic apparatus, with self-preface dated Wànlì 35 (1607) mid-winter. No independent biographical material; no DILA authority entry beyond the bare A001362 record.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0147]] only.
