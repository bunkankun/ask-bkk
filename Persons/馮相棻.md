# 馮相棻

Féng Xiāngfēn (*hào* Shínóng 石農) of Tiānjīn, mid-nineteenth-century physician and patron; acquired a manuscript of [[KR3em017]] *Chónglóu yùyuè* 重樓玉鑰 in *Jiāqìng yǐhài* (1815), used it through two decades of practice and the 1838 Tiānjīn throat-disease epidemic, and arranged for its *editio princeps* in Sūzhōu under [[孫學詩]] Sūn Xuéshī's editorial supervision (1839).
