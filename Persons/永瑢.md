# Yǒng Róng 永瑢

The Qiánlóng emperor's sixth son, by birth a member of the Aisin-Gioro 愛新覺羅 imperial clan. Posthumously enfeoffed as *Zhì qīnwáng* 質親王. Lifedates 1744–1790.

A senior *Sìkù quánshū* editorial figure. Served as *Sìkù quánshū guǎn* 四庫全書館 chief supervisor (*zǒngcái* 總裁) alongside [[紀昀]] (Jǐ Yún), and after the *Sìkù* was completed in 1782 became one of the principal authorities responsible for the imperial library's organisation. The presentation memorial of the *Jiù Wǔdài shǐ* ([[KR2a0029]]) recovery was issued under his name. Skilled in calligraphy and in landscape painting; one of the principal Qiánlóng-era imperial-clan literary patrons.

His biography is in *Qīng shǐ gǎo* 221 ([[KR2a0039]]) under the imperial-clan section.
