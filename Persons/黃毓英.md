# Huáng Yùyīng 黃毓英

Late-Míng lay Buddhist associated with [[廣真|Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn]] 吹萬廣真's Jùyúnpài teaching-community in Sichuan. Lifedates unrecorded. Commentator (*píng* 評) on Guǎngzhēn's *Gǔ yīn wáng zhuàn* 古音王傳 [[KR6q0236]].
