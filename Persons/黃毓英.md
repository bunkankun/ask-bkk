# Huáng Yù-yīng 黃毓英

Late-Míng lay Buddhist associated with [[廣真|Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn]] 吹萬廣真's Jù-yún-pài teaching-community in Sichuan. Lifedates unrecorded. Commentator (*píng* 評) on Guǎng-zhēn's *Gǔ yīn wáng zhuàn* 古音王傳 [[KR6q0236]].
