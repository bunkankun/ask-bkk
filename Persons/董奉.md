# Dǒng Fèng 董奉

Three-Kingdoms-period physician of Hóuguān 侯官 (modern Fúzhōu 福州, Fújiàn), *zì* Jūnyì 君異; together with [[華佗]] Huà Tuó and [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng one of the *Jiàn'ān sān shényī* 建安三神醫 (Three Divine Physicians of Jiàn'ān). According to the *Shénxiānzhuàn* 神仙傳 he treated patients in Lúshān 廬山 free of charge, asking only that those cured plant apricot trees in his orchard — origin of the proverbial expression *xìnglín* 杏林 (apricot grove) for the medical profession.
