# 康有為 Kāng Yǒuwéi

**Kāng Yǒuwéi** 康有為 (1858–1927, of Nánhǎi 南海, Guǎngdōng — hence the styling *Nánhǎi*), leading late-Qīng reformer and Confucian theorist; chief architect of the 1898 Hundred Days' Reform. Mentioned in [[熊希齡]] Xióng Xīlíng's 1927 colophon to [[譚志光]] Tán Zhìguāng's *Zhēnjiǔ wèndá* 針灸問答 ([[KR3ee035]]) as part of the 1890s Húnán Nánxuéhuì 南學會 reformist circle.
