法敏 Fǎ-mǐn ("Dharma-acuity") was a Táng-period or Five-Dynasties Chinese Buddhist monk associated with the commentarial tradition on the [[KR6o0084|*Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* 釋摩訶衍論]]. He is known only as the author/compiler of the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn shū* 釋摩訶衍論疏 ([[KR6o0086]]), a two-*juǎn* prose commentary on the parent treatise. He should be distinguished from at least two earlier Chinese monks of the same name: a Tang Tiāntái master (active c. 580–650) and a Sui-Tang vinaya master (c. 615–660). The Fǎ-mǐn of [[KR6o0086]] is otherwise unattested in standard biographical sources, and is conventionally placed loosely in the late Tang or Five Dynasties period on the basis of internal vocabulary and the assumption that his work post-dates the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* (itself a late seventh- or eighth-century apocryphon). His work survived only through Japanese manuscript transmission preserved in the *Manji-zokuzōkyō* 卍續藏經.
