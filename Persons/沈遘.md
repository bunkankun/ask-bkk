Mid-Northern-Sòng *Hànlín xuéshì*, *zì* Wéntōng 文通, of *Qiántáng* 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). Entered office at age 16 by *yīn* (hereditary privilege) as *Jiāoshè zhāiláng*; took the *jìnshì* in *Huángyòu* 1 / 1049 in first place at age 25 (adjusted to second on the technicality of his already holding office); held *Zhīzhìgào*, *zhī Hángzhōu*, *zhī Kāifēngfǔ*; final office *Hànlín xuéshì*. Younger brother of the poet Shěn Liáo 沈遼; first cousin of the polymath Shěn Kuò 沈括. Author of the lost *Běnzhì lùn* 本治論 in 10 *piān* (a major political-philosophical treatise praised by Rénzōng but not preserved in his collection). Died young at *Zhìpíng* 4 / 1067 age 43. Surviving collection: *Xīxī jí* [[KR4d0049]] (10 *juǎn*; combined with his brother's and cousin's works as the *Sān xiānshēng wén jí* [[KR4d0036]] in early-Southern-Sòng *Kuòcāng* publication).
