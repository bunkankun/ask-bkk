Mid-Northern-Sòng *Hàn-lín xué-shì*, *zì* Wén-tōng 文通, of *Qián-táng* 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). Entered office at age 16 by *yīn* (hereditary privilege) as *Jiāo-shè zhāi-láng*; took the *jìn-shì* in *Huáng-yòu* 1 / 1049 in first place at age 25 (adjusted to second on the technicality of his already holding office); held *Zhī-zhì-gào*, *zhī Hángzhōu*, *zhī Kāi-fēng-fǔ*; final office *Hàn-lín xué-shì*. Younger brother of the poet Shěn Liáo 沈遼; first cousin of the polymath Shěn Kuò 沈括. Author of the lost *Běn-zhì lùn* 本治論 in 10 *piān* (a major political-philosophical treatise praised by Rén-zōng but not preserved in his collection). Died young at *Zhì-píng* 4 / 1067 age 43. Surviving collection: *Xī-xī jí* [[KR4d0049]] (10 *juǎn*; combined with his brother's and cousin's works as the *Sān xiān-shēng wén jí* [[KR4d0036]] in early-Southern-Sòng *Kuò-cāng* publication).
