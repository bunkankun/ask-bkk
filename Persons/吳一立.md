Wú Yīyī 吳一立 (Yi-Li Wu) is a historian of Chinese medicine specialising in late-imperial gender and bodily knowledge. Her *Reproducing Women: Medicine, Metaphor, and Childbirth in Late Imperial China* (Berkeley, 2010) is the principal English-language monograph on Qīng medical reproduction; her earlier "A Medical Line of Many Masters" (*Chinese Science* 11, 1994: 36–65) traces the Jīn-Yuán Liú Héjiān–Dànxī prosopography.
