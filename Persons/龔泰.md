# 龔泰

Gōng Tài (sobriquet Yīzhǐ jūshì 一止居士 of Kēshān 柯山)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng layman (75 *suì* in 1647) who funded the 1648 printing of [[屠垠]] Tú Yín's *Jīngāng jīng zhùjiě tiězǎnxián* ([[KR6c0058]]). Lost his father in childhood and pursued a career of trade in Tiānjīn before encountering a *míngshī* 明師 who taught him a discipline of fasting and precepts as a "repaying-mother" practice.
