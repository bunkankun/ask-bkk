# Yìn 印 (Fǎ-xǐ-yìn 法璽印 / Zhèng-yìn 正印)

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master, **31st-generation Línjì** in the Xiào-yán Dé-bǎo → Hàn-shān Dé-qīng → [[觀衡|Zhuān-yú Guān-héng]] 顓愚觀衡 → Fǎ-xǐ-yìn sub-line. Principal dharma-heir and textual-editorial executor of Guān-héng (1579–1646). Full-form **Fǎ-xǐ-yìn** 法璽印 or **Zhèng-yìn** 正印; bare-form **Yìn** 印 per the Jiā-xīng catalog. Sichuan-origin ("蜀人"). See also cross-reference to the alternative name-form at [[正印]] (second entry in the dotted-line pattern).

**1642 transmission.** Received the *fǎ-yī* + *rú-yì* + Tripiṭaka + *cí-zhǐ wén-quàn* lineage-transmission from Guān-héng at **Yún-jū-shān Míng-yuè-táng** 明月堂 in Kāng-xī 1 winter (**1642**) along with dharma-brother **Fāng-róng-xǐ** 方融璽.

**Four abbacies.** (1) **Jiāng-xī Nán-kāng-fǔ Fèng-qī-shān Tóng-ān Chán-sì** 同安禪寺 — first abbacy, entered **Kāng-xī 1 壬寅 11.4** = **14 December 1662**; restoration of the Cáo-dòng ancestral seat. (2) **Yuè-shān Lóng-mén** 越山龍門. (3) **Dū-chéng Qīng-huà-sì** 都城清化寺 (Běi-jīng). (4) **Yù-zhāng běi-dì** 豫章北地 (Jiāng-xī Nán-chāng area).

**Editorial life's work.** Spent three decades (1646–1675) compiling his master Guān-héng's 20-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0433]]), surviving the 1662 Yún-jū tomb-fire manuscript-loss and eventually completing the cutting at **Jiā-xīng Léng-yán-sì** in **Kāng-xī 14 / 1675 lunar 11**. His own 12-juan *yǔlù* [[KR6q0434]] was cut at the same house six months later, in **Kāng-xī 15 / 1676 lunar 4**.

Work: [[KR6q0434]] 《法璽印禪師語錄》 (12 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B220, compiled by dharma-heirs [[性圓]] Xìng-yuán, **Xuán-jī** 旋璣, **Xuán-zhé** 旋哲, **Xuán-xián** 旋賢; front-preface by **Yú Sān-yíng** 余三瀛 dated **Kāng-xī 15 / 1676 Buddha's Birthday**).

**Editorial counterpart work.** [[KR6q0433]] 《紫竹林顓愚衡和尚語錄》 — edited by Fǎ-xǐ-yìn across 1646–75.

Native place Sichuan; precise lay surname and lifedates unrecovered; born c. 1610s–20s, active through 1676 at least.

Sources: [[KR6q0434]] juan-head signature + [[KR6q0433]] juan 20 *tǎ-míng* (mentions his role); Yú Sān-yíng 1676 preface.
