Southern-Sòng *Nánshān* Vinaya master and the principal Southern-Sòng restorer of *Nánshānlǜ* learning at *Tāizhōu Rìshān* 台州日山. Religious style **Tiěwēng** 鐵翁 ("Iron Elder"); lifedates not preserved (DILA A011485). Dharma-heir of *Rúān Liǎohóng* 如庵了宏. Settled at Rìshān 日山 in **1239** (Jiāxī 3), where he produced his two principal works:

- *Zhōngnán jiāyè* 終南家業 ([[KR6k0219]], X1109), three fascicles, his collected doctrinal essays and replies on *jiàoguān* 教觀 and *jiètǐ* 戒體 — including the historically important *Dá Rìběn Réng fǎshī jiàoguān zhū wèn* 答日本芿法師教觀諸問, his reply to the Japanese pilgrim-monk **Shunjō Réng** 俊芿 (1166–1227);
- *Lǜzōng huìyuán* 律宗會元 ([[KR6k0220]], X1110), three fascicles, a topical anthology of Vinaya passages organised under ten gates (*shímén* 十門).

He is distinct from the Qīng homonym *Shǒuyī Kōngchéng* 守一空成 (DILA A000356), described below.

# Shǒuyī Kōngchéng 守一空成 (Qīng)

A Qīng-period Línjì Chán monk; *míng* **Shǒu-yī** 守一, *zì* / *hào* **Kōng-chéng** 空成. Affiliated with the **Sū-zhōu Nán-chán-sì** 蘇州南禪寺 (the *Wú-zhōng* / Sū-zhōu region), where he is identified in the colophon of his principal work as "**吳中南禪沙門守一空成**" ("the Wú-zhōng Nán-chán śramaṇa Shǒu-yī Kōng-chéng"). Lifedates not transmitted; the work **《宗教律諸宗演派》** [[KR6r0173]] *Zōng-jiào-lǜ zhū-zōng yǎn-pài* (X1667, 1 *juǎn*) — his **re-edited compilation of the *yǎn-pài* generational-character mnemonic verses for the Chán, doctrinal-school, and *vinaya*-school sub-lineages of late-imperial Chinese Buddhism** — is dateable to the late 19th century by its inclusion of recent Qīng-period sub-lineage materials. He is **distinct from** the Southern-Sòng *Nán-shān* Vinaya master *Tiě-wēng Shǒu-yī* described above.
