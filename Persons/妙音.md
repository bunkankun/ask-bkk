Southern Sòng Vinaya scholar **Miào-yīn** 妙音 (lifedates not preserved), resident at the *Zhī-yán Jìng-huái Jìng-fàn* 芝嵓淨懷淨梵 monastery — i.e. an institution affiliated with the *Líng-zhī* 靈芝 line that descended from *Yuán-zhào* 元照 (1048–1116). Miào-yīn was the third of the three Sòng Vinaya respondents to the thirty Vinaya questions submitted in *Jiā-dìng* 嘉定 2 (1209) by the Japanese pilgrim [[俊芿]] *Shunjō* and preserved in *Lǜ-zōng wèn-dá* ([[KR6k0256]], X1108). His answers in the *Wèn-dá* show a *Líng-zhī* school perspective — distinct from the *Tiāntái-Vinaya* integrative reading that [[了然]] *Liǎo-rán* and [[智瑞]] *Zhì-ruì* both represent — providing the reader with a useful three-way comparison of late-Sòng Vinaya thought. Per DILA A000469: Sòng dynasty; monk; sole recorded work the *Lǜ-zōng wèn-dá*.

The DILA Buddhist Person Authority distinguishes this *Miào-yīn* from the East-Jìn nun *Zhī Miào-yīn* 支妙音 of the *Jiǎn-jìng-sì* 簡靜寺 (DILA A014989), who is unrelated.
