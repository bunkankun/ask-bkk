Southern Sòng Vinaya scholar **Miàoyīn** 妙音 (lifedates not preserved), resident at the *Zhīyán Jìnghuái Jìngfàn* 芝嵓淨懷淨梵 monastery — i.e. an institution affiliated with the *Língzhī* 靈芝 line that descended from *Yuánzhào* 元照 (1048–1116). Miàoyīn was the third of the three Sòng Vinaya respondents to the thirty Vinaya questions submitted in *Jiādìng* 嘉定 2 (1209) by the Japanese pilgrim [[俊芿]] *Shunjō* and preserved in *Lǜzōng wèndá* ([[KR6k0256]], X1108). His answers in the *Wèndá* show a *Língzhī* school perspective — distinct from the *Tiāntái-Vinaya* integrative reading that [[了然]] *Liǎorán* and [[智瑞]] *Zhìruì* both represent — providing the reader with a useful three-way comparison of late-Sòng Vinaya thought. Per DILA A000469: Sòng dynasty; monk; sole recorded work the *Lǜzōng wèndá*.

The DILA Buddhist Person Authority distinguishes this *Miàoyīn* from the East-Jìn nun *Zhī Miàoyīn* 支妙音 of the *Jiǎnjìngsì* 簡靜寺 (DILA A014989), who is unrelated.
