# Fóhǎi Xìngzhèng 佛海性證

Yuán-dynasty Buddhist master, author of a *zhù* 注 (commentary) on the *Heart Sūtra* that does not survive independently but is partially preserved in citations within Wúniàn jūshì's 1533 *Bōrě xīnjīng jiěyì jiéyào* ([[KR6c0154]]). Biographical details are otherwise unknown.
