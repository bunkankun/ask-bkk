# Xuē Qǐfèng 薛起鳳

Mid-Qīng Sūzhōu literatus, contemporary of [[彭紹升]] Péng Shàoshēng (1740–1796). Composed the *Wénxīnggé jiàn fàngshēng huì shū* 文星閣建放生會疏 ("Proclamation on founding the Wénxīnggé Animal-Release Assembly"), a classical-prose document preserved in Péng's *Tǐrén yàoshù* 體仁要術 ([[KR6k0263]], X1139). Xuē's *shū* drew on *Yìjīng* cosmogony and the *Lùnyǔ* "heaven gives life and the sage completes its capacity" topos to ground the lay-Buddhist *fàngshēng* practice cosmologically.
