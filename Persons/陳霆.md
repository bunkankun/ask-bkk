Chén Tíng 陳霆 (fl. *Hóng-zhì* / *Zhèng-dé*; *jìn-shì* 1502), *zì* Shēng-bó 聲伯, *hào* Shuǐ-nán 水南, of Dé-qīng 德清 in Huí-jī 會稽 (modern Zhèjiāng). *Jìn-shì* of *Hóng-zhì rén-xū* 弘治壬戌 / 1502; rose to *Shān-xī tí-xué qiān-shì* (Education Intendant of Shān-xī). Author of two important Míng-period *huà* (informal critical compendia): the *Zhū-shān-táng shī-huà* 渚山堂詩話 and the *Zhū-shān-táng cí-huà* [[KR4j0083]]. The *Sìkù* tíyào prefers the *cí-huà* to the *shī-huà* — Chén's poetic instincts ran closer to *cí*. CBDB id 25854 has zero birth-and-death year recorded; floruit fixed by his 1502 *jìn-shì* and known service through the 1530s.
