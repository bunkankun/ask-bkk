Chén Tíng 陳霆 (fl. *Hóngzhì* / *Zhèngdé*; *jìnshì* 1502), *zì* Shēngbó 聲伯, *hào* Shuǐnán 水南, of Déqīng 德清 in Huíjī 會稽 (modern Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Hóngzhì rénxū* 弘治壬戌 / 1502; rose to *Shānxī tíxué qiānshì* (Education Intendant of Shānxī). Author of two important Míng-period *huà* (informal critical compendia): the *Zhūshāntáng shīhuà* 渚山堂詩話 and the *Zhūshāntáng cíhuà* [[KR4j0083]]. The *Sìkù* tíyào prefers the *cíhuà* to the *shīhuà* — Chén's poetic instincts ran closer to *cí*. CBDB id 25854 has zero birth-and-death year recorded; floruit fixed by his 1502 *jìnshì* and known service through the 1530s.
