Wáng Tài-yuè 王太岳 (1722–1785), *zì* Jī-píng 基平, *hào* Jiè-zǐ 芥子, of Bǎo-dǐ 寶坻 in Zhí-lì (modern Tiānjīn region). *Jìn-shì* of *Qián-lóng* 7 / 1742. Held senior posts in the *Wén-yuān gé* (the imperial Hàn-lín library) and the *Sì-kù guǎn* — the editorial bureau of the *Sì-kù quán-shū*. A senior editor under *Jì Yún* [[紀昀]]; principal compiler (with Wáng Yàn-xù 王燕緒) of the *Qīn-dìng sì-kù quán-shū kǎo-zhèng* [[KR4j0091]] in 100 *juǎn* (1786 completion, after Wáng's death). The *Kǎo-zhèng* is the methodological companion volume to the entire *Sì-kù quán-shū*: its 100-*juǎn* record of all textual collation decisions taken across the imperial library's compilation.
