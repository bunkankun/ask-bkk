Wáng Tàiyuè 王太岳 (1722–1785), *zì* Jīpíng 基平, *hào* Jièzǐ 芥子, of Bǎodǐ 寶坻 in Zhílì (modern Tiānjīn region). *Jìnshì* of *Qiánlóng* 7 / 1742. Held senior posts in the *Wényuān gé* (the imperial Hànlín library) and the *Sìkù guǎn* — the editorial bureau of the *Sìkù quánshū*. A senior editor under *Jì Yún* [[紀昀]]; principal compiler (with Wáng Yànxù 王燕緒) of the *Qīndìng sìkù quánshū kǎozhèng* [[KR4j0091]] in 100 *juǎn* (1786 completion, after Wáng's death). The *Kǎozhèng* is the methodological companion volume to the entire *Sìkù quánshū*: its 100-*juǎn* record of all textual collation decisions taken across the imperial library's compilation.
