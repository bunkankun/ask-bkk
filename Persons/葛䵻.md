Late-Míng / early-Qīng *jūshì* lay devotee. Sobriquets *Qī-kōng jūshì* 七空居士 ("Seven-Emptinesses Lay Devotee") and *Fù-ān jūshì* 復菴居士. Personal names also recorded as Gě Yìdiào 葛毅調, Gě Dǐng 葛鼎, Gě Dǐnghuì 葛鼎慧. Native of Kūnshān 昆山 (in Sūzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). Lifedates unknown; the *floruit* given here (1640–1700) reflects the late-Míng / early-Qīng window of his attested compositional activity.

His major attested work is the *Dà bōrě jīng gāngyào* 大般若經綱要 (X449 = KR6c0228), a substantial 10-fascicle synoptic compendium of Xuánzàng's 600-fascicle *Mahāprajñāpāramitā-sūtra* (T220), arranged for accessibility to lay students.

He represents the late-Míng / early-Qīng *jūshì* (lay devotee) Buddhist scholarship tradition, paralleling the broader pattern of Yangtze-delta lay scholars producing substantial Buddhist commentary and reference works in the period.
