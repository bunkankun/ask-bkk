# Zhèng Chūnsù 鄭春素

Contemporary Chinese-medicine philologist; editor of the modern critical edition *Yòukē yīxué zhǐnán* 幼科醫學指南 ([[KR3ej016]]) in the *Zhōngguó gǔ-yījí zhěnglǐ cóngshū* 中國古醫籍整理叢書 (Beijing: Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè, 2013).
