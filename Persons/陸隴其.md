Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其 (1630–1692), *zì* Jiàshū 稼書, *hào* Sānyú 三魚, posthumously *Qīngxiàn* 清獻, was a foundational early-Qīng *Zhū-zǐ-xué* loyalist official. Native of Píng-hú 平湖 (Jiā-xīng region, Zhè-jiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī jiǔ* (1670); held provincial magistracies and rose to *Sì-chuān dào jiān-chá yù-shǐ*. Within early-Qīng *Lǐxué* he is the principal Zhū-Xī-purist among the post-Sòng tradition, fierce critic of Wáng Yáng-míng's *xīn-xué*. Major works in the SKQS *Rújiā* class: *Sān-yú-táng shèng yán* 三魚堂賸言 (KR3a0116, with Chén Jì); *Sōng-yáng chāo cún* 松陽鈔存 (KR3a0117, with Yáng Kāi-jī); *Dú Zhū suí bǐ* 讀朱隨筆 (KR3a0115). Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 480 (Rú-lín zhuàn). CBDB id 33770, dates 1630–1692.
