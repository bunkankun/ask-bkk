Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其 (1630–1692), *zì* Jiàshū 稼書, *hào* Sānyú 三魚, posthumously *Qīngxiàn* 清獻, was a foundational early-Qīng *Zhūzǐxué* loyalist official. Native of Pínghú 平湖 (Jiāxīng region, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī jiǔ* (1670); held provincial magistracies and rose to *Sìchuān dào jiānchá yùshǐ*. Within early-Qīng *Lǐxué* he is the principal Zhū-Xī-purist among the post-Sòng tradition, fierce critic of Wáng Yángmíng's *xīnxué*. Major works in the SKQS *Rújiā* class: *Sānyútáng shèng yán* 三魚堂賸言 ([[KR3a0116]], with Chén Jì); *Sōngyáng chāo cún* 松陽鈔存 ([[KR3a0117]], with Yáng Kāijī); *Dú Zhū suí bǐ* 讀朱隨筆 ([[KR3a0115]]). Biography: *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 480 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 33770, dates 1630–1692.
