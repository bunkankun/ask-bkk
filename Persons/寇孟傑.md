**Kòu Mèngjié** 寇孟傑, late-Míng commentator on the *Nèijīng*, specifically on the *Sùwèn* "*biāo / běn / zhōngqì cónghuà*" 標本中氣從化 doctrine of the relations between surface, root, and intermediate *qì* of the six channels. Cited in [[陸錦燧]] Lù Jǐnsuì's *Cúncuì yīhuà* ([[KR3eq061]]).
