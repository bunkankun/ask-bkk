Gāo Sìsūn 高似孫 (Sòng), *zì* Xùgǔ 續古, *hào* Shūliáo 疎寮, was a Late-Southern-Sòng bibliographer and *biji* writer. *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī shíyī* (1184); held office to *Hànlín shìzhì*. Major works: *Zǐ lüè* 子略 (a Sòng-period bibliographic work on *zǐ*-class books); *Shū lüè* 書略; *Xiè lüè* 蟹略 (on crabs!); *Shī lüè* 詩略. Annotated and supplied the *bá* (postface) for the *Wòjī jīng* ([[KR3b0001]]). CBDB id 13584.
