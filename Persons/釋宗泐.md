Shī Zōng-lè 釋宗泐 (1318–1391), *zì* **Jì-tán** 季潭, *hào* **Quán-shì** 全室, native of **Lín-ān** 臨安 (Hángzhōu). Líng-jì school (臨濟宗) Buddhist monk; pupil of **Yuán-xī** 元熈 / **Guǎng-zhì Quán-wù chán-shī** **Dà-xīn** 大訢 (1284–1344), the *Lóng-xiáng-sì* 龍翔寺 abbot at Nán-jīng — making Zōng-lè a *Lín-jì* 21st-generation monk in the Wàn-fēng 萬峰 line. In early Hóngwǔ years recommended as a *gāo-xíng shā-mén* 高行沙門 and ordered to the **Tiān-jiè-sì** 天界寺 at Nán-jīng; ordered (1378) to the **Western Regions** (Central Asia / India) to seek surviving sūtras; returned 1382 and appointed *zuǒ shàn-shì* 左善世 (chief Buddhist officer). Hóngwǔ Tàizǔ wished to appoint him to secular office; he firmly declined; Tàizǔ wrote a *Miǎn-guān shuō* 免官說 for him. Implicated in the **Hú Wéi-yōng** 胡惟庸 case (1380) but specifically pardoned by Tàizǔ. Authored: (1) [[KR4e0072]] *Quán-shì wài-jí* 全室外集 (the surviving literary collection); (2) lost *Xī-yóu jí* 西游集 (record of the Western Regions mission, refuted by **Xú Zhēn-qīng** 徐禎卿 as fictitious but confirmed by the *Sìkù* editors as actually existing); (3) joint *zhù-jiě* 註解 of three major sūtras with **Rú-qǐ** 如玘 (T39 *Léng-jiā jīng zhù-jiě*; T33 *Bō-rě bō-luó-mì-duō xīn jīng zhù-jiě*; T33 *Jīn-gāng bō-rě jīng zhù-jiě*) — all preserved in the *Dà-zhèng-zàng* (Taishō Canon).
