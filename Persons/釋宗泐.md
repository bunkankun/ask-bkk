Shī Zōnglè 釋宗泐 (1318–1391), *zì* **Jìtán** 季潭, *hào* **Quánshì** 全室, native of **Línān** 臨安 (Hángzhōu). Língjì school (臨濟宗) Buddhist monk; pupil of **Yuánxī** 元熈 / **Guǎngzhì Quánwù chánshī** **Dàxīn** 大訢 (1284–1344), the *Lóngxiángsì* 龍翔寺 abbot at Nánjīng — making Zōnglè a *Línjì* 21st-generation monk in the Wànfēng 萬峰 line. In early Hóngwǔ years recommended as a *gāoxíng shāmén* 高行沙門 and ordered to the **Tiānjièsì** 天界寺 at Nánjīng; ordered (1378) to the **Western Regions** (Central Asia / India) to seek surviving sūtras; returned 1382 and appointed *zuǒ shànshì* 左善世 (chief Buddhist officer). Hóngwǔ Tàizǔ wished to appoint him to secular office; he firmly declined; Tàizǔ wrote a *Miǎnguān shuō* 免官說 for him. Implicated in the **Hú Wéiyōng** 胡惟庸 case (1380) but specifically pardoned by Tàizǔ. Authored: (1) [[KR4e0072]] *Quánshì wàijí* 全室外集 (the surviving literary collection); (2) lost *Xīyóu jí* 西游集 (record of the Western Regions mission, refuted by **Xú Zhēnqīng** 徐禎卿 as fictitious but confirmed by the *Sìkù* editors as actually existing); (3) joint *zhùjiě* 註解 of three major sūtras with **Rúqǐ** 如玘 (T39 *Léngjiā jīng zhùjiě*; T33 *Bōrě bōluómìduō xīn jīng zhùjiě*; T33 *Jīngāng bōrě jīng zhùjiě*) — all preserved in the *Dàzhèngzàng* (Taishō Canon).
