Chén Jiāmó 陳嘉謨 (*zì* Tíngcǎi 廷采, *hào* Yuèpéng 月朋, 1521–1603), native of Qímén 祁門 in Húīzhōu 徽州 (modern Ānhuī). Mid-Míng pharmacologist, the author of the *Běncǎo méngquán* 本草蒙筌 ([[KR3ec024]]) — one of the principal Míng-dynasty pharmacological textbooks. He studied medicine under Xú Chǔn 徐春 and devoted his life to clinical practice and teaching at Qímén, holding no official appointment. Other works (lost): *Yī xué chū rù mén* 醫學初入門, *Yào xìng kǒu jué* 藥性口訣.

He died at age 82 in 1603. No CBDB id located. Biographical information from Húīzhōu local gazetteers (《祁門縣志》). His pharmacological work is heavily cited by Lǐ Shízhēn [[李時珍]] in the *Běncǎo gāngmù* [[KR3ec025]].
