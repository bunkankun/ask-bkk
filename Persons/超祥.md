# Chāo-xiáng 超祥

Mid-seventeenth-century female Línjì Chán nun, dharma-disciple of [[徹|Jì-zǒng Chè (= Bǎo-chí Jì-zǒng)]] 季總徹 / 繼總 (1606–1678) in the Shān-cí / Wàn-rú-line female-Chán network. Head recorder (*jì-lù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0429]] 《季總徹禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B211).

The 超-generation character aligns with the same cohort as [[KR6q0428]] Qí-yuán Xíng-gāng's dharma-heirs (授遠 超宿 超朗 超珂 超見 超振 超琛) — the general **超-generation** female-Chán-nun generation of the Mìyún line's 34th-generation tier.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under her name.

Sources: [[KR6q0429]] juan-head signature line.
