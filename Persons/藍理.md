Lán Lǐ 藍理 (**1649 – 1720**), *zì* **Yìshān** 義山 (alt. **Yìfǔ** 義甫). Important Qīng-dynasty military commander of Zhāngzhōu 漳州 / Zhāngpǔ 漳浦 (Fújiàn) origin, of the **Shē** 畬 ethnic group. Distinguished himself in the Qīng campaign against Zhèng Zhènggōng's grandson Zhèng Kèshuǎng 鄭克塽 (Tāiwān) in **康熙二十二年** = **1683** under Admiral Shī Láng 施琅 — wounded in the abdomen at the **Pénghú** sea-battle. Subsequently rose through provincial commands. As of the **康熙丙子** = **1696** preface to [[KR6q0554]], he held the post of *zhènshǒu Zhèjiāng děngchù dìfāng Dìnghǎi zǒngbīngguān zuǒ dūdū* (military governor of Dìnghǎi naval district, Zhèjiāng). Later promoted to *fùjiāngjūn* 福將軍 of Fújiàn. Died in Beijing in **康熙五十九年** = **1720**, age 72.

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the second preface to [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》 (1696). Catalog dynasty: 清.
