Lán Lǐ 藍理 (**1649 – 1720**), *zì* **Yì-shān** 義山 (alt. **Yì-fǔ** 義甫). Important Qīng-dynasty military commander of Zhāng-zhōu 漳州 / Zhāng-pǔ 漳浦 (Fú-jiàn) origin, of the **Shē** 畬 ethnic group. Distinguished himself in the Qīng campaign against Zhèng Zhèng-gōng's grandson Zhèng Kè-shuǎng 鄭克塽 (Tāi-wān) in **康熙二十二年** = **1683** under Admiral Shī Láng 施琅 — wounded in the abdomen at the **Pénghú** sea-battle. Subsequently rose through provincial commands. As of the **康熙丙子** = **1696** preface to [[KR6q0554]], he held the post of *zhèn-shǒu Zhè-jiāng děng-chù dì-fāng Dìng-hǎi zǒng-bīng-guān zuǒ dū-dū* (military governor of Dìng-hǎi naval district, Zhè-jiāng). Later promoted to *fù-jiāng-jūn* 福將軍 of Fú-jiàn. Died in Beijing in **康熙五十九年** = **1720**, age 72.

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the second preface to [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》 (1696). Catalog dynasty: 清.
