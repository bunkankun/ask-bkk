Shěn Jiǒng 沈炯 (502–560 CE; *zì* Chū 初) was a literary official who served under both the Liáng 梁 and Chén 陳 dynasties. He served under the Liang general Wáng Sēngbiàn 王僧辯 and witnessed the transfer of power to the Chen dynasty under Chén Gāozǔ 陳高祖. His verse reflects the Liang-Chen transitional experience. Biography in *Chén shū* juǎn 19 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 62. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0067]].
