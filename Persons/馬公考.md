Mǎ Gōngkǎo 馬公考, Yuán-period book-collector who supplied the family-held copy of [[項安世]] Xiàng Ānshì's *Zhōuyì wán cí* ([[KR1a0038]]) used as base-text for the *Dàdé dīngwèi* (1307) Qíān 齊安 prefectural-school reprint. Otherwise undocumented; the name may be a *biéhào* or appellation rather than a personal name proper.
