Tang-period translator-monk; transliteration **Mí-tuó-shān** = Sanskrit **Mitraśānta** (also semantic rendering **Jì-yǒu** 寂友, "Friend-of-Quiescence"). Native of **Tukhāra** (覩貨羅國 / Tokharistan, present-day northern Afghanistan). Came to Tang China during the reign of **Wǔ Zé-tiān** and joined the translation establishment.

His two principal recorded translations are: collaboration with **Śikṣānanda** [[實叉難陀]] on the *Dà-chéng rù lèngqié jīng* 大乘入楞伽經 (T672, the seven-fascicle late-recension *Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra*); and during the **Tiān-shòu** era (690–692) the present *Wú-gòu jìng-guāng dà-tuóluóní jīng* [[KR6j0218]] (T1024, the *Raśmivimalaviśuddhaprabhā-dhāraṇī*) co-translated with **Fǎ-zàng** 法藏 (643–712) and others. He also produced the *Fó jí-xiáng-dé zàn* 佛吉祥德讚 (T1681). Lifedates not preserved. Returned to Tukhāra after the translation work was completed.
