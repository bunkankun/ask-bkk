Wú Shì-yù 吳士玉 (1669–1733; CBDB id 58867), *zì* Jīng-shān 荊山, *hào* Dōng-bì 東璧, posthumously *Wénkè* 文恪, was a middle-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng court scholar-official. Native of Wú-jiāng 吳江 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 45 (1706); rose through the Hàn-lín ranks to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. In the Yōngzhèng-period imperial-publication programme he served as principal *zǒng-cái* (general director) of both the *Pián zì lèi-biān* 駢字類編 ([[KR3k0056]]) — the great companion-piece to the *Pèi-wén yùn-fǔ* — and the *Zǐ-shǐ jīng-huá* 子史精華 ([[KR3k0058]]); the *Fēn lèi zì jǐn* 分類字錦 ([[KR3k0057]]) also lists him as a *fēn-zuǎn* compiler. He was one of the most consistent imperial-*lèishū* directors of the Yōngzhèng court.
