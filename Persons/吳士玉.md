Wú Shìyù 吳士玉 (1669–1733; CBDB id 58867), *zì* Jīngshān 荊山, *hào* Dōngbì 東璧, posthumously *Wénkè* 文恪, was a middle-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng court scholar-official. Native of Wújiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 45 (1706); rose through the Hànlín ranks to *Lǐbù shàngshū*. In the Yōngzhèng-period imperial-publication programme he served as principal *zǒngcái* (general director) of both the *Pián zì lèibiān* 駢字類編 ([[KR3k0056]]) — the great companion-piece to the *Pèiwén yùnfǔ* — and the *Zǐshǐ jīnghuá* 子史精華 ([[KR3k0058]]); the *Fēn lèi zì jǐn* 分類字錦 ([[KR3k0057]]) also lists him as a *fēnzuǎn* compiler. He was one of the most consistent imperial-*lèishū* directors of the Yōngzhèng court.
