Qīng-period Buddhist lay scholar; surname **Zhōu** 周, given name **Shàng-zhì** 上智. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Compiler-extractor (錄出 *lù-chū*) of the **Gāo-wáng Guānyīn jīng zhù-shì** 高王觀音經註釋 ([[KR6d0125]], X35n0648, 1 *juan*) — an annotated text of the *Gāo-wáng Guānyīn jīng* 高王觀音經, an apocryphal Avalokiteśvara devotional sūtra widely circulated in late-imperial Chinese folk Buddhism.

Source: *Gāowáng Guānyīn jīng zhùshì* (X35n0648).
