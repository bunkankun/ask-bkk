Hú Zhì 胡稺 (also written 胡穉; *zì* Zhòngrú 仲孺, of Zhúpō 竹坡), Southern-Sòng (active *Shàoxī* 紹熙 era, c. 1190–1194) literary scholar from Sìmíng 四明. Identified through his preface (dated *Shàoxī rénzǐ* 紹熙壬子 = 1192) and self-postface (dated *Shàoxī gǎiyuán làyuè* 紹熙改元臘月 = late 1190) to his *Zēngguǎng jiānzhù Jiǎnzhāi shījí* 增廣箋註簡齋詩集 [[KR4d0154]] — the standard annotated commentary on the poetry of Chén Yǔyì [[陳與義]].

Hú's commentary, composed over several years, is preserved in the SBCK transmission of Chén's poetry. The Sòng scholar Lóu Yuè 樓鑰 [[樓鑰]] of Sìmíng 四明 supplied a preface dated 1192 (*Shàoxī rénzǐ*) which praises the work as exceeding the comparable annotation of Du Fu / Su Shi by Zhào Yàncái 趙彥材 in coverage and detail (Chén's six hundred or so poems being a smaller corpus to handle than the much larger output of Du and Su). Hú's commentary is the principal Sòng-period scholarly engagement with the *Jiāngxī*-school's late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng heir.
