Xīng-lín 興林, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Fāng-róng Rú-xǐ** [[如璽]] 方融如璽 (1602–post-1662) in the **Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] sub-line. Lead compiler of the 3-juan 《方融璽禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0458]]), signing the j. 1 colophon as *mén-rén Xīng-lín děng biān* 門人興林等編. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
