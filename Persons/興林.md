Xīnglín 興林, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Fāngróng Rúxǐ** [[如璽]] 方融如璽 (1602–post-1662) in the **Tiānjiè Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] sub-line. Lead compiler of the 3-juan 《方融璽禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0458]]), signing the j. 1 colophon as *ménrén Xīnglín děng biān* 門人興林等編. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
