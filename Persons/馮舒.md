Late-Míng / early-Qīng textual critic of **Cháng-shú** 常熟 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). **CBDB id 30245, dates 1593–1649**. *Zì* **Jǐ-cāng** 已蒼; *hào* **Mò-ān** 默庵. Brother of **Féng Bān** 馮班 (1602–1671), also a noted textual critic. The brothers were active in the Wǔ-zhōng **Cháng-shú scholastic culture** — alongside **Mao Jìn** 毛晉 of the *Jí-gǔ-gé* — that pioneered systematic textual collation through appeal to Sòng imprints.

Féng Shū's principal independent work is the **[[KR4h0108]]** *Shī-jì kuāng-miù* 詩紀匡謬 (1 *juǎn*) — a 112-entry textual-critical correction of [[馮惟訥]] Féng Wéi-nè's [[KR4h0107]] *Gǔ-shī jì*. The two works are paired in the *Sìkù* as mutually-supplementary, **standard recension + textual corrections** — a unique pairing in the corpus. He also collaborated with Féng Bān on a much-cited critical edition of the *Yù-tái xīn-yǒng* 玉臺新詠 (the **Féng-brothers' edition**), and his marginalia on Táng poetry (the **Féng commentary** on the *Cái-diào jí* 才調集) was used by Wáng Shì-zhēn 王士禎 and other Qīng critics.
