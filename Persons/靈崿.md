# Língè 靈崿

Táng Vinaya exegete of the post-[[道宣]] Dàoxuān generation, cited by [[志鴻]] Zhìhóng in [[KR6k0162]] *Sìfēnlǜ sōuxuán lù* among the more than forty earlier *jì* and *chāo* writers digested in that work. Independent biographical data is not preserved; the writings survive only as citations.
