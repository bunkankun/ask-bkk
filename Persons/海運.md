# Hǎiyùn 海運

A name attested in the Chinese Esoteric Buddhist tradition, possibly identified as the author of the *Pútíxīn yì* ([[KR6j0739]]) on the basis of a parenthetical sub-title; the identification is not independently verified in the DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority.
