Zhìmíng 志明 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* late 12th – early 13th c.), Jīn-dynasty 金 Cáo-dòng-school Chán master; *zì* **Bó-hūn** 伯昏, *hào* **Cuò-ān** 錯庵 and **Lè-zhēn-zǐ** 樂真子. Native of **Ān-zhōu** 安州, lay surname **Hǎo** 郝.

He was tonsured under **Jié shǒu-zuò** 潔首座 and received full ordination at **Xiāng-lín-sì** 香林寺 under **Jìng** 淨. He pursued advanced Chán practice under **Shèng-jìng** 勝靜 and **Pǔ** 普 and attained final realisation under **Dōng-lín** 東林. He served as abbot of **Sōng-shān Shào-lín-sì** 嵩山少林寺 — the celebrated Cáo-dòng establishment that since the late Northern Sòng had been one of the principal Chán monasteries of the north and the symbolic seat of the Bodhidharma-lineage.

He compiled the **《禪苑蒙求瑤林》** *Chán-yuàn méng-qiú yáo-lín* ([[KR6r0097]], X87 no. 1614, 3 juan), a Chán-school analogue of the secular *méng-qiú* 蒙求 ("introductory inquiry") learning-handbook tradition: a short-couplet-format compendium of paired references to canonical figures and events, designed for memorisation by Chán novices. The work was subsequently annotated by the Yuán Cáo-dòng master [[德諫]] Dé-jiàn (Xuě-táng Dé-jiàn 雪堂德諫). Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000496.
