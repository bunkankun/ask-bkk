**Sōtei** 宗禎 (also **Teizan** 禎山, by-name **Onoyama** 尾野山) — Mid-Edo period Japanese **Abhidharma** scholar-monk, abbot of **Onoyama-ji** 尾野山寺. He is documented only through the dated colophons of his sole canonical work, *Yǒuzōng qīshíwǔ fǎ jì* 有宗七十五法記 ([[KR6t0021]], T71n2325) — a three-fascicle Abhidharma primer composed in **Kyōhō 13 = 1728** as an improvement upon the previously-circulating anonymous primer [[KR6t0020]] *Qīshíwǔ fǎ míngmù*, and published with his disciples' annotations in **Kyōhō 15 = 1730**.

He should **not** be confused with the homonymous **Sōtei** 宗禎 (also written 瑞秀宗禎) of the Sōtō Zen lineage (DILA A040085) — disciple of Kikuen Sōki 菊湲宗喜.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000599. No biographical detail beyond the work's colophons is preserved.
