# Zǐ-wén 子文

Northern-Sòng Chán monk; dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) of Yuán-wù [[克勤|Kè-qín]] 圓悟克勤 (1063–1135). Lifedates unrecorded. Editor (*biān* 編) of the *Fó-guǒ Kè-qín chánshī xīn yào* 佛果克勤禪師心要 [[KR6q0291]] — his master's four-juan essentials-collection.
