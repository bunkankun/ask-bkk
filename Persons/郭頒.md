郭頒 Guō Bān was a Jin-dynasty scholar whose biographical details are largely unknown. He compiled the *Wèi-Jìn Shìyǔ* 魏晉世語 ([[KR4k0002]]), a collection of anecdotes about prominent Wei and Jin figures. The work was cited extensively by Péi Sōngzhī 裴松之 in his 429 CE commentary on the *Sānguózhì* 三國志, where it is quoted alongside other lost Wei-Jin historical miscellanies. Beyond this, nothing further is known of Guo Ban's life or other writings. CBDB id 428072 records the name without dates.
