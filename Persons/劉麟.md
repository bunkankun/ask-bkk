Liú Lín 劉麟 (1475–1561; CBDB 34637 gives 1475 — preferred over the catalog meta's 1474), *zì* **Yuánruì** 元瑞, alt. *zì* **Zǐzhèn** 子振, late-life *hào* **Tǎnwēng** 坦翁, posthumous *shì* **Qīnghuì** 清惠, native of **Ānrén** 安仁 (Jiāngxī); resettled in **Chángxīng** 長興 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng), where his descendants enrolled in the local register. Hóngzhì 9 / *bǐngchén* (1496) *jìnshì*; rose to *Gōngbù shàngshū*. *Míngshǐ* main biography. As *Gōngbù* probationer protested with **Lù Kūn** 陸崑 against the imprisonment of *jiànguān*; as *Shàoxīngfǔ zhīfǔ* offended **Liú Jǐn** 劉瑾 and was removed; as *shàngshū* opposed Sū Sōng *zhīzào* (Sū Sōng silk-tribute) and was forced out. Retired to Chángxīng to live with his marriage-relative **Wú Chōng** 吳珫; consorted with **Sūn Yīyuán** ([[孫一元]]) and **Wén Zhēngmíng** 文徵明; the famous **Shénlóu tú** 神樓圖 by Wén Zhēngmíng was painted for him. His writings are gathered in the *Qīnghuì jí* in 12 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0155]]). CBDB id 34637, 1475–1561.
