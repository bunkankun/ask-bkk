Liú Lín 劉麟 (1475–1561; CBDB 34637 gives 1475 — preferred over the catalog meta's 1474), *zì* **Yuán-ruì** 元瑞, alt. *zì* **Zǐ-zhèn** 子振, late-life *hào* **Tǎn-wēng** 坦翁, posthumous *shì* **Qīng-huì** 清惠, native of **Ān-rén** 安仁 (Jiāng-xī); resettled in **Cháng-xīng** 長興 (Hú-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng), where his descendants enrolled in the local register. Hóng-zhì 9 / *bǐng-chén* (1496) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Gōng-bù shàng-shū*. *Míng-shǐ* main biography. As *Gōng-bù* probationer protested with **Lù Kūn** 陸崑 against the imprisonment of *jiàn-guān*; as *Shào-xīng-fǔ zhī-fǔ* offended **Liú Jǐn** 劉瑾 and was removed; as *shàng-shū* opposed Sū-Sōng *zhī-zào* (Sū-Sōng silk-tribute) and was forced out. Retired to Cháng-xīng to live with his marriage-relative **Wú Chōng** 吳珫; consorted with **Sūn Yī-yuán** ([[孫一元]]) and **Wén Zhēng-míng** 文徵明; the famous **Shén-lóu tú** 神樓圖 by Wén Zhēng-míng was painted for him. His writings are gathered in the *Qīng-huì jí* in 12 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0155]]). CBDB id 34637, 1475–1561.
