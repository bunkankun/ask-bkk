Wáng Shūmín 王淑民 was a researcher at the Zhōngguó Zhōngyī yánjiū yuàn 中國中醫研究院 who carried out field-work and philological collation under [[馬繼興]] on the reconstruction of the *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]) in the 1970s.
