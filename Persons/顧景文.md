**Gù Jǐngwén** 顧景文 (mid-18th c., 清), Sūzhōu physician and disciple of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiān-shì. He is known almost exclusively as the scribe of Yè's *Wēn-rè lùn* 溫熱論 ([[KR3eg001]]), which Yè is said to have dictated to him extempore during a boat journey on Lake Tài 太湖. Lifedates are not preserved in the standard biographies — the CBDB Gù Jǐngwén entry (id 86348, 1631–1675) is a different person, well before Yè's circle. No reliable independent biographical material survives; he is sometimes conjectured to be a Sūzhōu *xiùcái* who studied with Yè in the 1730s–1740s.
