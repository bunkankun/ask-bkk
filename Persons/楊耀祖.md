**Yáng Yàozǔ** 楊耀祖 (*fl.* late 17th c., 清), native of Tàiyuán 太原. Served as *zhī Hújiāng wèi* (commander at Hújiāng) and wrote one of the 康熙己未 (1679) prefaces to [[蔣介繁]]'s *Běncǎo zéyào gāngmù* 本草擇要綱目 ([[KR3ec057]]), praising the work as "concise but comprehensive, clear but apt, careful and exact yet simply systematic" — the very book he had himself wished to compile. No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
