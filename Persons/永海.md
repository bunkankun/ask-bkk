Yǒng Hǎi 永海 (fl. late Míng) was a Vinaya specialist of the late Míng. His extant work is the **Fó shuō Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn jièlǜzhōng qīngzhòngshì jīngshì** 佛說目連五百問戒律中輕重事經釋 ([[KR6k0075]]) — a two-fascicle commentary on the *Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn jīng* (T1483b), explaining each of Maudgalyāyana's casuistic Vinaya questions. Together with Xìng Qí's parallel commentary ([[KR6k0074]]), the work belongs to the late-Míng revival of Vinaya casuistic scholarship.
