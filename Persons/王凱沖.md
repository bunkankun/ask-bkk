# Wáng Kǎichōng 王凱沖

Pre-Táng *Yì* scholar of obscure dates; his commentary is lost as an independent work but cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]) — listed among the thirty-five masters identified in the Sìkù *tiyao*. No biographical record survives.
