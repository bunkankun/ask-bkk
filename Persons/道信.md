# Dào-xìn 道信

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xī-sǒu Shào-tán (d. by 1279); co-editor of the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Fourth Chán Patriarch Dào-xìn (580–651).
