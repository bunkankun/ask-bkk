# Dàoxìn 道信

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xīsǒu Shàotán (d. by 1279); co-editor of the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Fourth Chán Patriarch Dàoxìn (580–651).
