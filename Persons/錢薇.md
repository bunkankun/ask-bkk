**Qián Wēi** 錢薇 (*zì* Yīfū 一夫, *hào* Hǎishí 海石, 1504–1567 conventional dating), mid-Míng official and scholar of Hǎiyán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng); *jìnshì* of *Jiājìng* 17 (1538), served as 禮科右給事中 in the censorial system. Qián's medical-literary contribution is the 1549 commentary preface (*zhùxù* 注序) to [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's annotated edition of [[王綸]] Wáng Lún's *Míngyī zázhù* ([[KR3er040]]), which presents the principal mid-Míng medical-historical lineage account of the Sūzhōu medical school running from Gě Yìngléi 葛應雷 through Wáng Āndào and Zhào Liángrén to Xuē Jǐ.
