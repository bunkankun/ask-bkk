**Qián Wēi** 錢薇 (*zì* Yī-fū 一夫, *hào* Hǎi-shí 海石, 1504–1567 conventional dating), mid-Míng official and scholar of Hǎi-yán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng); *jìn-shì* of *Jiā-jìng* 17 (1538), served as 禮科右給事中 in the censorial system. Qián's medical-literary contribution is the 1549 commentary preface (*zhù-xù* 注序) to [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's annotated edition of [[王綸]] Wáng Lún's *Míng-yī zá-zhù* ([[KR3er040]]), which presents the principal mid-Míng medical-historical lineage account of the Sūzhōu medical school running from Gě Yìng-léi 葛應雷 through Wáng Ān-dào and Zhào Liáng-rén to Xuē Jǐ.
