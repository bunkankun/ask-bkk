# 鄒握虎 Zōu Wòhǔ

**Zōu Wòhǔ** 鄒握虎, Southern-Sòng acupuncturist of Sānqú 三衢 (Qúzhōu, Zhèjiāng); mentioned in [[徐正卿]] Xú Zhèngqīng's 1220 preface to [[王執中]] Wáng Zhízhōng's *Zhēnjiǔ zīshēng jīng* 針灸資生經 ([[KR3ee020]]) as having codified an acupuncture treatment method in poetic mnemonic.
