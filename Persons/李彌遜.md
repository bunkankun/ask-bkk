Lǐ Míxùn 李彌遜 (1089–1153), *zì* Sìzhī 似之, *hào* Yún-xī jū-shì 筠溪居士, native of Lián-jiāng 連江 (Fú-jiàn), settled in Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu). With his elder brother Lǐ Mídà 李彌大 and younger brother Lǐ Mízhèng 李彌正 he was one of three brothers of considerable repute. *Shàng-shè dì-yī* graduate of *Dà-guān* 3 (1109). Under Gāozōng he served as *Zhōng-shū shè-rén* 中書舍人 and twice as *Hù-bù shì-láng* 戶部侍郎; for opposing the *Shào-xīng hé-yì* (peace negotiations) he ran afoul of Qín Guì 秦檜 and was forced into retirement. He withdrew to his Xī-shān 西山 villa for sixteen years, devoting himself to poetry. Both Lóu Yuè 樓鑰 (preface) and Zhū Xī (colophon to a Yún-xī poem) praised him highly. His record is in *Sòng shǐ* (biography). CBDB id 1062 confirms 1089–1153.

Lǐ's literary collection survives as *Yún-xī jí* 筠溪集 [[KR4d0158]] in 24 *juǎn* with one *juǎn* of *yuè-fǔ* 樂府 (*cí*) appended. Catalog meta gives only "d. 1153"; CBDB and the *Sòng shǐ* biography both supply the birthdate 1089, followed here.
