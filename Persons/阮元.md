Ruǎn Yuán 阮元 (1764–1849), *zì* Bóyuán 伯元, *hào* Yúntái 芸臺, also Léitángānzhǔ 雷塘庵主, native of Yízhēng 儀徵, Yángzhōu, Jiāngsū. The central patron-organizer of late-Qiánlóng / Dàoguāng *kǎozhèng* scholarship. *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 54 (1789); Hànlín *biānxiū*; rose through a sequence of senior provincial appointments: governor of Zhèjiāng (1799–1809), governor of Hénán (1810), governor-general of Hú Guǎng (1816), governor-general of Liǎng Guǎng (1817–1826), governor-general of Yún-Guì (1826–1835), grand secretary (*tǐréngé dàxuéshì* 體仁閣大學士, 1838–1849). Posthumously honored *Wéndá* 文達. His three landmark editorial projects, all conducted under official sponsorship: (1) the *Jīngjí zhuàngǔ* 經籍籑詁 (1798, the great *jīng* glossatory concordance); (2) the *Shísān jīng zhù shū jiàokān jì* 十三經注疏校勘記 (1815, the definitive collated edition of the Thirteen Classics with commentary); (3) the *Huáng Qīng jīngjiě* 皇清經解 (1825–1829, 1400-juan anthology of canonical Qīng *kǎozhèng* commentaries, published at Xuéhǎitáng 學海堂 in Guǎngzhōu). He founded the Gūshān 詁山 (also written 詁經精舍) academy in Hángzhōu (1801) and the Xuéhǎitáng academy in Guǎngzhōu (1820), the two principal nineteenth-century *kǎozhèng* training centers. His collected writings are the *Yánjīngshì jí* 揅經室集 (40 juan + *xùjí*), of which the *yī jí* [[KR4f0062]] (Classics, 14 juan) is principal. Major classical essays: *Lùnyǔ lùnrén lùn* 論語論仁論; *Xìngmìng gǔxùn* 性命古訓; *Wényán shuō* 文言說 (the influential late-Qīng essay arguing that genuine *gǔwén* must be parallel-prose, against Tóngchéng). *ECCP* 399–402 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 29653.
