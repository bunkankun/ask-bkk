Ruǎn Yuán 阮元 (1764–1849), *zì* Bó-yuán 伯元, *hào* Yún-tái 芸臺, also Léi-táng-ān-zhǔ 雷塘庵主, native of Yí-zhēng 儀徵, Yáng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū. The central patron-organizer of late-Qiánlóng / Dàoguāng *kǎo-zhèng* scholarship. *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 54 (1789); Hànlín *biānxiū*; rose through a sequence of senior provincial appointments: governor of Zhè-jiāng (1799–1809), governor of Hé-nán (1810), governor-general of Hú-Guǎng (1816), governor-general of Liǎng-Guǎng (1817–1826), governor-general of Yún-Guì (1826–1835), grand secretary (*tǐ-rén-gé dà-xué-shì* 體仁閣大學士, 1838–1849). Posthumously honored *Wén-dá* 文達. His three landmark editorial projects, all conducted under official sponsorship: (1) the *Jīng-jí zhuàn-gǔ* 經籍籑詁 (1798, the great *jīng* glossatory concordance); (2) the *Shí-sān jīng zhù shū jiào-kān jì* 十三經注疏校勘記 (1815, the definitive collated edition of the Thirteen Classics with commentary); (3) the *Huáng-Qīng jīng-jiě* 皇清經解 (1825–1829, 1400-juan anthology of canonical Qīng *kǎo-zhèng* commentaries, published at Xué-hǎi-táng 學海堂 in Guǎng-zhōu). He founded the Gū-shān 詁山 (also written 詁經精舍) academy in Hángzhōu (1801) and the Xué-hǎi-táng academy in Guǎng-zhōu (1820), the two principal nineteenth-century *kǎo-zhèng* training centers. His collected writings are the *Yán-jīng-shì jí* 揅經室集 (40 juan + *xù-jí*), of which the *yī jí* [[KR4f0062]] (Classics, 14 juan) is principal. Major classical essays: *Lùn-yǔ lùn-rén lùn* 論語論仁論; *Xìng-mìng gǔ-xùn* 性命古訓; *Wén-yán shuō* 文言說 (the influential late-Qīng essay arguing that genuine *gǔwén* must be parallel-prose, against Tóngchéng). *ECCP* 399–402 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 29653.
