**Wèi Lǔ** 魏魯 was a Sòng commentator on the ***Huáng shí gōng Sù shū*** 黃石公素書 ([[KR5f0012]]) — a military-strategic classic pseudepigraphically attributed to the Hàn immortal Huáng shí gōng 黃石公 (traditional transmitter of the *Tài gōng Liù tāo* to Zhāng Liáng 張良).
