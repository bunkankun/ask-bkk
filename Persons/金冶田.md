# 金冶田 Jīn Yětián

**Jīn Yětián** 金冶田, late-Qīng physician of the Léi-family circle in Qúzhōu; maternal relative of [[雷豐]] Léi Fēng who obtained the original Sìchuān-monk manuscript that became the *Jiǔfǎ mìchuán* 灸法秘傳 ([[KR3ee047]]).
