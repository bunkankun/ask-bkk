**Chén Jué** 陳桷 (*fl.* mid 16th c., 明), Míng-period physician of Qí-mén 祁門 (Ān-huī); disciple of [[汪機]] Wāng Jī. Compiler of the ***Shíshān yī àn*** 石山醫案 ([[KR3e0074]], 3 juan) — gathering Wāng Jī's clinical case-records as recorded by his various disciples. Chén also collated and printed [[戴原禮]] Dài Yuánlǐ's *Tuī-qiú shī yì* ([[KR3e0071]]) at Wāng Jī's suggestion. Beyond his role as Wāng Jī's principal disciple-editor, he has no further transmitted biographical record.
