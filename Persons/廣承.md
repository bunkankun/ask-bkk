Guǎng Chéng 廣承 (fl. late Míng) was a late-Míng Vinaya specialist who compiled the **Pínì zhēn-jìng lù** 毗尼珍敬錄 ([[KR6k0143]]) — a two-fascicle Vinaya selections-anthology, supplemented and finalized by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕩益智旭 ([[智旭]]) (會補 *huì-bǔ* "compiled and supplemented"). The work is part of the late-Míng Vinaya revival.
