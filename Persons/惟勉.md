Wéimiǎn 惟勉 (lifedates unrecorded; active late Southern-Sòng through early-Yuán, 1270s–1280s), *hào* Píngyán 屏岩. Monastic-regulatory compiler-editor of the Jīnhuá Hòuhú 金華后湖 community (modern Zhèjiāng). Best known through his editorial compilation of the *Cónglín jiào dìng qīng guī zǒng yào* 叢林校定清規總要 ([[KR6q0140]], X63 n1249), a critical collation of the accumulated Sòng-dynasty monastic regulatory tradition; preface dated Xiánchún 10 (1274) at the Jì-wán xuān 寄玩軒, postface dated Zhìyuán 21 (1284), indicating Wéimiǎn's survival through the Sòng-Yuán transition and continued editorial activity into the early Yuán.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0140]] only.

Per DILA A001065. The text Wéimiǎn compiled was subsequently drawn on by Déhuī in his 1336 *Chì xiū Bǎizhàng qīng-guī* ([[KR6q0102]]), establishing Wéimiǎn's editorial work as a standard reference for the later Yuán imperial regulatory project.
