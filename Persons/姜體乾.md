**Jiāng Tǐqián** 姜體乾, mid-Qīng physician of Huáshù 華墅 (near Jiāngyīn 江陰); older contemporary and direct associate of [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng (cf. [[KR3eh053]]) and the addressee of a "miraculous *yītāng*" note in [[王旭高]] Wáng Xùgāo's *Yī shū*. The locus classicus of the Huáshù-school dysentery tradition that Wú Shìyīng claims to inherit.
