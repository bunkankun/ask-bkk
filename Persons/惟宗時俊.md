**Korēmune no Tokitoshi** 惟宗時俊 (catalog variant 唯宗時俊; *fl.* late 13th c., Kamakura period), Japanese court physician of the Korēmune 惟宗 clan — a hereditary medical lineage of immigrant Chinese (Hata 秦 clan) descent attached to the *Ten-yaku-ryō* 典藥寮 (the imperial Medicines Bureau) of the Heian and Kamakura imperial court. He held the bureau title *Ten-yaku-no-suke* 典藥助 (vice-director). His one transmitted work is the *Ika senjimon* 醫家千字文 ([[KR3ea033]]), the earliest substantial original Japanese medical work in classical Chinese, conventionally dated between *Kōan* 10 (1287) and *Kagen* 1 (1304). The Korēmune clan also produced the Heian-era *Ishinpō* compiler Tanba no Yasuyori 丹波康賴 (though Yasuyori in fact belongs to the rival Tanba lineage; the two Heian medical families served in parallel under the imperial bureau).
