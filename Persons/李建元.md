Lǐ Jiànyuán 李建元, second son of Lǐ Shízhēn [[李時珍]], *fl.* late 16th century. After his father's death (1593) and the printing of the *Běncǎo gāngmù* [[KR3ec025]] in Nánjīng, Lǐ Jiànyuán presented the work to the imperial court in 1596 (Wànlì 24) with a *jìn shū* 進書 memorial that reproduces his father's posthumous *yí biǎo* 遺表 — the principal first-person autobiographical statement by Lǐ Shízhēn. At the time of presentation Lǐ Jiànyuán held the rank of *Húguǎng Húáng-zhōu fǔ rúxué zēng-guǎng shēng-yuán* 湖廣黃州府儒學增廣生員.

No CBDB record. Biographical details are sparse, available only through the presentation paratext and Qí-zhōu local gazetteers.
