# Zì-jué 自覺

Northern-Sòng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *hào* Lù-mén 鹿門 (also styled Huì-dìng 慧定 and Jìng-yīn Jué 淨因覺 after his abbacies); posthumous title Dìng-huì chánshī 定慧禪師. Dharma-heir of **Fú-róng Dào-kǎi** 芙蓉道楷 (1043–1118), thus grand-disciple (孫) of [[義青]] Tóu-zǐ Yì-qīng (1032–1083). Native of Qīng-zhōu 青州, lay surname Wáng 王; died on the 16th of the 2nd month of Zhèng-hé 7 (27 March 1117).

Entered orders under Dào-kǎi during the Shào-shèng era (1094–1098) and received his dharma-transmission. Abbacy sequence: Nán-yáng-fǔ Dà-chéng-shān Pǔ-yán-sì 普嚴寺 (from Chóng-níng 3 / 1104), Shàng-dū Zuǒ-jiē Shí-fāng Jìng-yīn chán-yuàn 上都左街十方淨因禪院 (from Dà-guān 1 / 1107, in the Northern-Sòng capital Biàn-jīng), and Lù-mén-shān 鹿門山 (from Zhèng-hé 1 / 1111). Buried in a stupa at Lù-mén-shān.

His principal editorial achievement was the *chóng-biān* 重編 of [[義青]] Yì-qīng's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0356]]), carried out during his Jìng-yīn abbacy — the closing *bá* of which identifies "原本全稿者其孫淨因覺公之所編集也." Through him, the main-line Cáo-dòng recension of Yì-qīng's teaching was fixed at the imperial capital for subsequent transmission. Named dharma-heirs include Huì-lán 慧蘭 and Pǔ-zhào Yī-biàn 普照一辦 (Xī-biàn 希辨); a supplementary note in *Wǔ-dēng huì-yuán xù-lüè* *juan* 1 re-identifies him as a student of Tiān-tóng Rú-jìng, which appears to conflate two different figures of the same name.
