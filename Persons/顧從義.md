Gù Cóngyì 顧從義 (1523–1588), zì Rǔhé 汝和, of Shànghǎi (Sōngjiāng prefecture, Jiāngsū), was a Jiājìng-era senior calligrapher selected by imperial summons during the Jiājìng era to enter direct imperial service for his calligraphic skill. He was awarded the post of *zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人 and served on the *Wénhuádiàn zhí* 文華殿直 staff. After contributing to the *Guóshǐ* 國史 compilation in the early Lóngqìng era (1567–), he was promoted to *Dàlǐsì píngshì* 大理寺評事. His one notable surviving work is the *Fǎtiè shìwén kǎoyì* 法帖釋文考異 [[KR2n0031]] in 10 juan — a Míng critical re-collation of the *Chúnhuà gétiè shìwén* tradition. CBDB 133659 confirms 1523–1588.
