Wáng Xiàngzhī 王象之 (fl. ca. 1196–1227), zì Yífù 儀父, of Jīnhuá 金華 (Wúzhōu, Zhèjiāng), was a Southern Sòng historical-geographer and county magistrate. He served as *zhī Jiāngníng xiàn* 知江寧縣 (magistrate of Jiāngníng, Nánjīng's county). His monumental geographical work the *Yúdì jìshèng* 輿地紀勝 in 200 juan (the most important Southern Sòng comprehensive gazetteer) covered the territories of Sòng under the post-1141 Shàoxīng peace; the *Yúdì bēijì mù* 輿地碑記目 [[KR2n0025]] in 4 juan is the *jīnshí* portion extracted from it. The *Yúdì jìshèng* itself is partially preserved (about 100 juan); the *Bēijì mù* survived intact through the Sìkù transmission. CBDB 10689 records *fl. 1195* and confirms the figure but supplies no precise dates.
