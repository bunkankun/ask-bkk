**Táo Hóngyǎn** 陶鴻響 (*hào* Yányá Liánpǔ 研牙蓮浦, *fl.* early 19th c., 清), mid-Qīng literatus, friend of the physician [[周詒觀]] Zhōu Yíguān. Author of the 1830 (*Dàoguāng* 10) preface to Zhōu's *Mìzhēn jìyīn* 秘珍濟陰 ([[KR3ei035]]).
