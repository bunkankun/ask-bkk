**Chái Déhuá** 柴得華 (also recorded as Chái Huáshēng 柴華生, *fl.* mid-to-late Qīng), gynecological physician. Author of the *Fùkē bīng-jiàn* 婦科冰鑑 ([[KR3ei044]]), an eight-*juǎn* systematic gynecology. According to his *zì-xù*, Chái had been gravely misdiagnosed in his youth, an experience that drove him to abandon the literati examination track for medicine. The work is in the mature Qīng systematic-gynecology tradition. No further biographical detail located; not in CBDB.
