Contemporary Chinese specialist of Dūnhuáng Buddhist manuscripts and Vinaya literature, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of *Xíngshì chāo zhōng fēnmén túlù* ([[KR6v0007]]) — Dàoxuān's authorial outline of his *Xíngshì chāo* — and of *Ēpídámó jùshèlùn shíyì shū* fascicle three ([[KR6v0008]]) — the complete Sthiramati *Tattvārtha*-commentary preserved at Beijing Library.
