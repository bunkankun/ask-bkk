# Jiǎ Chānglíng 賈昌齡

Northern Sòng Chief Councillor (*shìzhōng* 侍中); requested the posthumous title "Míngjué chánshī" 明覺禪師 for [[重顯|Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn]] 雪竇重顯 after Chóngxiǎn's death in 1052.
