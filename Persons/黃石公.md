Huáng-shí-gōng 黃石公 ("the Yellow-Stone Lord") is the legendary Qín-period figure of the *Shǐjì*'s Zhāng Liáng 張良 narrative (j. 55) — an old man who appeared to the young Zhāng Liáng on the Xià-pī 下邳 bridge, gave him a military classic with the prophecy that "in thirteen years you will see me as the yellow stone at Gǔ-chéng-shān", and disappeared. Zhāng Liáng's later success aiding Liú Bāng was attributed to study of this text. Two SKQS works in KR3b are attributed to him: *Huáng-shí-gōng sān-lüè* 黃石公三畧 (KR3b0007) — the canonical military classic in 3 juan, included in the *Wǔ jīng qī shū*; and *Huáng-shí-gōng sù-shū* 黃石公素書 (KR3b0009) — also a military-strategic *biji*, with Sòng commentary by Zhāng Shāngyīng 張商英. Both works are post-Qín pseudepigrapha drawing on the legendary Yellow-Stone-Lord identity.
