**Yuè Xīzài** 岳熙載 (Yuán; also written 岳煕載 with the variant form of 熙; dates and native place unrecorded) is the attributed author of the *Tiānwén jīngyì fù* 天文精義賦 ([[KR3fa009]]), a Yuán-period verse treatise on astronomy in five *juǎn*. He is otherwise unrecorded; some catalog notices treat the attribution as conventional. The work is plausibly a late-Yuán Mongol-court astrologer's compendium of the kind that circulated in *fǔ* (rhapsody) form for ease of memorisation, comparable in genre to the [[王希明]] *Bùtiān gē*.
