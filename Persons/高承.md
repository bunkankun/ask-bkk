Gāo Chéng 高承 (fl. *Yuán-fēng* 元豐 1078–1085), Northern-Sòng resident of Kāi-fēng. No biographical material survives beyond his attestation as compiler of the *Shì-wù jì-yuán* 事物紀原 ([[KR3k0014]]) — the principal Northern-Sòng *qǐ-yuán* compendium. Zhào Xī-biàn (in *Dúshū fù-zhì*) and Chén Zhèn-sūn (in *Zhízhāi shū-lù jiětí*) both record him as a *Yuán-fēng*-era Kāi-fēng man; no other writings are attributed to him, no biography appears in the *Sòng shǐ*. Xiàng Bīn 項彬 of the Shuāng-xī 雙溪 wrote the original preface, now lost.
