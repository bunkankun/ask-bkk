Gāo Chéng 高承 (fl. *Yuánfēng* 元豐 1078–1085), Northern-Sòng resident of Kāifēng. No biographical material survives beyond his attestation as compiler of the *Shìwù jìyuán* 事物紀原 ([[KR3k0014]]) — the principal Northern-Sòng *qǐyuán* compendium. Zhào Xībiàn (in *Dúshū fùzhì*) and Chén Zhènsūn (in *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí*) both record him as a *Yuánfēng*-era Kāifēng man; no other writings are attributed to him, no biography appears in the *Sòng shǐ*. Xiàng Bīn 項彬 of the Shuāngxī 雙溪 wrote the original preface, now lost.
