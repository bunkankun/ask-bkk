Zhāng Míngfèng 張鳴鳳 (fl. late Wànlì era; *fl.* 1613) was a Wàn-lì-era *yúdì* (topographical) writer. The principal CBDB candidates 130592 (1465–1522) and 123174 are date-incompatible with the firmly-dated Wànlì 41 (1613) prefaces of the *Guìshèng* and *Guìgù*, his principal works ([[KR2k0087]]); a confident CBDB match has not been established. The Sìkù tíyào also catalogues his *Xīqiān zhù* 西遷註 (a commentary on the *Hànshū* "*Xīqiān*" passage) elsewhere. The Sìkù tíyào's own typographical inversion of his name as 張鳳鳴 (in the [[KR2k0054]] tíyào) is preserved in the Wényuāngé text but does not affect the consensus correct form. He is cited by the Sìkù compilers as the principal late-Míng model for the documentary-anthology *míngshèng* genre — Zhū Yízūn's *Rìxià jiùwén* and Dǒng Sīzhāng's *Wúxìng bèizhì* both follow his editorial pattern.
