**Chén dà shī** 陳大師 ("Master Chén") was a Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0296]] *Bì yù zhū shā hán lín yù shù guì* 碧玉朱砂寒林玉樹匱 — an alchemical text on jade, cinnabar, and their elixir products. His personal name is not recorded.
