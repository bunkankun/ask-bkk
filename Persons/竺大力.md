Zhú Dàlì 竺大力 (\*Mahābala; lifedates unknown), Eastern-Hàn Indian Buddhist *śramaṇa* of the Western-region (西域) origin (the surname-element 竺 traditionally indicates Indian descent), *fl.* late second century CE. According to the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 and the *Lìdài sānbǎo jì* 歷代三寶紀, he collaborated with the Hàn-Chinese *upāsaka* [[康孟詳|Kāng Mèngxiáng]] in producing [[KR6b0040|*Xiūxíng běnqǐ jīng* 修行本起經 (T184)]], one of the earliest Chinese Buddhist *Buddha-biography* texts, in 197 CE — a date sometimes cited as the *terminus post quem* for stand-alone Chinese-language Buddha-biographies. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001230.
