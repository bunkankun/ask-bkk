Zhāng Lǐ 張禮 (*fl.* 1086), *zì* Mào-zhōng 茂中, native of Zhèjiāng. Northern-Sòng minor scholar otherwise undocumented. In *Yuán-yòu* 1 (1086) he travelled with the Chǔ man Chén Wéi-míng 陳微明 in the southern environs of Cháng-ān (modern Xī'ān) examining the surviving traces of Táng-period imperial wards and monasteries, and produced the *Yóu Chéng-nán jì* 遊城南記 ([[KR2k0133]]) — the earliest surviving Chinese-language *yóu-jì* on Cháng-ān recording post-Táng topography from on-the-ground inspection. The work has self-annotation by Zhāng, with later continuation-notes added by an unidentified late-Jīn / early-Yuán hand. The CBDB has multiple persons named Zhāng Lǐ, none securely identifiable as this Northern-Sòng *Yuán-yòu*-era figure; not assigned a CBDB id.
