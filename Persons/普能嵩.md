# Pǔ-néng Sōng 普能嵩

A Qīng-era Chán master, dharma-name **Pǔ-néng** 普能, compound name with **Sōng** 嵩 (likely a reference to *Sōng-shān* 嵩山 monastery). Author of the **《普能嵩禪師淨土詩》** *Pǔ-néng Sōng chán-shī jìng-tǔ shī* [[KR6p0133]] (X1215), a seven-character-regulated verse-cycle on Pure Land themes recorded by his disciple [[德潤]] **Dé-rùn**. The verses indicate a Chán-Pure Land integrationist (*chán-jìng shuāng-xiū* 禪淨雙修) practitioner active in a *Lián-shè* 蓮社 / *fǎ-huì* 法會 devotional-assembly setting. Lifedates and native place not preserved.
