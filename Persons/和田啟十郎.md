# 和田啟十郎 Wada Keijūrō

Meiji–Taishō Japanese physician (1872–1916), pioneer of the early-twentieth-century *kanpō* 漢方 revival. His *Igaku no tettsui* 醫界鐵椎 (1910) was a major polemical defence of traditional East Asian medicine against the Meiji-era policy of exclusive Western-medical licensing, and the principal influence on [[湯本求真]] Yumoto Kyūshin's *Kōkan igaku* ([[KR3ef059]]).
