# 永覺元賢

Yǒngjué Yuánxián 永覺元賢 (1578–1657), late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master of the Gǔshān 鼓山 lineage in Fújiàn. Master of [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi. Principal figure in the early-Qīng Cáodòng revival.
