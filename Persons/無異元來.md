# Wúyì Yuánlái 無異元來

Late-Míng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master (1575–1630), one of the two most important seventeenth-century Cáodòng-line masters alongside Yǒngjué Yuánxián 永覺元賢 (1578–1657).
