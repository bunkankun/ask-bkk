# Wén-zǎo 文藻

Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Also styled Nán-zhōu 南洲 / 南州, under which name he appears in the *Xù-dēng zhèng-tǒng mù-lù* 續燈正統目錄 as "Yù-wáng Yìn chánshī fǎ-sì, Nán-zhōu Zǎo chánshī" 育王印禪師法嗣南洲藻禪師 (X84 no. 1582). Lifedates unrecorded.
