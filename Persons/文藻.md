# Wénzǎo 文藻

Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Also styled Nánzhōu 南洲 / 南州, under which name he appears in the *Xùdēng zhèngtǒng mùlù* 續燈正統目錄 as "Yùwáng Yìn chánshī fǎsì, Nánzhōu Zǎo chánshī" 育王印禪師法嗣南洲藻禪師 (X84 no. 1582). Lifedates unrecorded.
