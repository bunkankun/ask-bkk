# Zhōulǐ 周理 (Chèyōng Zhōulǐ 徹庸周理)

Late-Míng Línjì-lineage Chán master of the Yúnnán regional Chán community. *Zì* Yīchè 一徹 ("Single-Thorough"); earlier *hào* Chèróng 徹融 ("Thoroughly-Melting"), later revised to Chèyōng 徹庸 ("Thoroughly-Ordinary"); *shì* Mèngān 夢庵 ("Dream Hermitage"). Lay name Dù Huìjiǔ 杜慧九. Native of Yúnnánxiàn 雲南縣 (modern Xiángyún 祥雲, Yúnnán). Lifedates 1591–1647 (died Yǒnglì 1 = 1647 of the Southern Míng, age 57, per the *Yí nián lù* 疑年錄 p. 395; alternative death-date 1636 per *Jìng shí dī rǔ jí* 徑石滴乳集 juan 4).

Tonsured at 11 at Jīzúshān Dàjuésì 鷄足山大覺寺 (Yúnnán) under Biànzhōu héshàng 徧周和尚. Initial awakening through Guānyīn-name chanting. Further Chán guidance from Mìzàngkāi 密藏開 ([[道開|Mìzàng Dàokāi]]) of Jiāxīng-Canon fame. After study at various monasteries outside Yúnnán, received dharma-transmission from **Lǎngmù Běnzhì 朗目本智** (1556–1606; DILA variously gives Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟 as an additional dharma-giver). Returned to Jīzúshān as abbot of **Miàofēngsì** 妙峰寺, where he taught for the remainder of his life.

Editorial projects:
- *Cáoxī yī dī* 曹溪一滴 [[KR6q0195]] (1636) — the Yúnnán Chán regional anthology preserving works of Gǔtíng Shànzūn 古庭善堅 and others.
- *Gǔtíng chánshī zhú shì jí* 古庭禪師竹室集 — his edition of Gǔtíng's writings.
- *Fúshān fǎ jù* 浮山法句.

Authored works:
- *Chèyōng héshàng gǔ xiǎng jí* 徹庸和尚谷響集 (The Valley-Echo Collection of Chèyōng héshàng) — his collected sermons and writings.
- *Yúnshān mèng yǔ* 雲山夢語 (Dream-Talk of Cloud-Mountain) — his miscellaneous writings.

Sources: *Jīzúshān zhì* 鷄足山志 juan 6; *Jìng shí dī rǔ jí* 徑石滴乳集 juan 4.
