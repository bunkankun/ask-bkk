# Zhōu-lǐ 周理 (Chè-yōng Zhōu-lǐ 徹庸周理)

Late-Míng Línjì-lineage Chán master of the Yúnnán regional Chán community. *Zì* Yī-chè 一徹 ("Single-Thorough"); earlier *hào* Chè-róng 徹融 ("Thoroughly-Melting"), later revised to Chè-yōng 徹庸 ("Thoroughly-Ordinary"); *shì* Mèng-ān 夢庵 ("Dream Hermitage"). Lay name Dù Huì-jiǔ 杜慧九. Native of Yúnnán-xiàn 雲南縣 (modern Xiáng-yún 祥雲, Yúnnán). Lifedates 1591–1647 (died Yǒng-lì 1 = 1647 of the Southern Míng, age 57, per the *Yí nián lù* 疑年錄 p. 395; alternative death-date 1636 per *Jìng shí dī rǔ jí* 徑石滴乳集 juan 4).

Tonsured at 11 at Jī-zú-shān Dà-jué-sì 鷄足山大覺寺 (Yúnnán) under Biàn-zhōu héshàng 徧周和尚. Initial awakening through Guānyīn-name chanting. Further Chán guidance from Mì-zàng-kāi 密藏開 ([[道開|Mìzàng Dàokāi]]) of Jiāxīng-Canon fame. After study at various monasteries outside Yúnnán, received dharma-transmission from **Lǎng-mù Běn-zhì 朗目本智** (1556–1606; DILA variously gives Mì-yún Yuán-wù 密雲圓悟 as an additional dharma-giver). Returned to Jī-zú-shān as abbot of **Miào-fēng-sì** 妙峰寺, where he taught for the remainder of his life.

Editorial projects:
- *Cáo-xī yī dī* 曹溪一滴 [[KR6q0195]] (1636) — the Yúnnán Chán regional anthology preserving works of Gǔ-tíng Shàn-zūn 古庭善堅 and others.
- *Gǔ-tíng chánshī zhú shì jí* 古庭禪師竹室集 — his edition of Gǔ-tíng's writings.
- *Fú-shān fǎ jù* 浮山法句.

Authored works:
- *Chè-yōng héshàng gǔ xiǎng jí* 徹庸和尚谷響集 (The Valley-Echo Collection of Chè-yōng héshàng) — his collected sermons and writings.
- *Yún-shān mèng yǔ* 雲山夢語 (Dream-Talk of Cloud-Mountain) — his miscellaneous writings.

Sources: *Jī-zú-shān zhì* 鷄足山志 juan 6; *Jìng shí dī rǔ jí* 徑石滴乳集 juan 4.
