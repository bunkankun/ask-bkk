**Lǐ Jìnghán** 李鏡涵 (*fl.* late 19th c., 清), native of Shānxī Língshí 山右靈石. Wrote the 光緒十九年中秋後五日 (1893) preface to [[陳其瑞]]'s *Běncǎo cuōyào* 本草撮要 ([[KR3ec055]]). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
