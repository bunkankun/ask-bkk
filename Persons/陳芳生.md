Early-Qīng scholar; native of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu, modern Zhèjiāng). *Zì* Shùliù 漱六. Author of the *Bǔ Huáng kǎo* 捕蝗考 ([[KR2m0047]])—the principal early-Qīng locust-control treatise, valuable especially for its complete preservation of Xú Guāngqǐ's *Chú huáng shū*. Precise life-dates not transmitted; *fl.* mid-Kāngxī era.
