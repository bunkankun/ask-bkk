# 齊玉

Qíyù

Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng Tiāntái master at the Shàngtiānzhúsì 上天竺寺 in Hángzhōu. Teacher of [[曇應]] Tányìng, who became the abbot of Huìjiěsì 慧解寺 and composed the *Jīngāng jīng cǎiwēi* trilogy ([[KR6c0051]], [[KR6c0052]], [[KR6c0053]]). DILA authority A011364.
