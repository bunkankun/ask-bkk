**Zhū Chóngzhèng** 朱崇正 (*zì* Zōngrú 宗儒, *fl.* Jiājìng gēngxū = 1550, 明), Míng-period physician of Huīzhōu 徽州. Editor and printer of the Jiājìng 1550 reprint of [[楊士瀛]] Yáng Shìyíng's *Rénzhāi zhízhǐ* ([[KR3e0045]]), to which he added supplementary entries (附遺 *fùyí*) under each chapter — the SKQS-base recension. His name is attached as *fùyí* author to both the *Rénzhāi zhízhǐ* and the *Shānghán lèishū* in the Jiājìng print, although the SKQS *tíyào* notes that his actual editorial intervention is more visible in the *Zhízhǐ* than in the *Lèishū*. Beyond this editorial-printer role he has no transmitted biographical record.
