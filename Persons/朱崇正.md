**Zhū Chóngzhèng** 朱崇正 (*zì* Zōngrú 宗儒, *fl.* Jiājìng gēng-xū = 1550, 明), Míng-period physician of Huī-zhōu 徽州. Editor and printer of the Jiājìng 1550 reprint of [[楊士瀛]] Yáng Shìyíng's *Rénzhāi zhí-zhǐ* ([[KR3e0045]]), to which he added supplementary entries (附遺 *fù-yí*) under each chapter — the SKQS-base recension. His name is attached as *fù-yí* author to both the *Rénzhāi zhí-zhǐ* and the *Shānghán lèi-shū* in the Jiājìng print, although the SKQS *tíyào* notes that his actual editorial intervention is more visible in the *Zhí-zhǐ* than in the *Lèi-shū*. Beyond this editorial-printer role he has no transmitted biographical record.
