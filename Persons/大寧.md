# 大寧

Dàníng

Mid-Míng Sūzhōu monk, teacher of the layman [[許敬愚]] Xǔ Jìngyú. Worked on annotated materials for the *Xiāoshì Jīngāng kēyí* that were inherited by Xǔ and ultimately recompiled in [[KR6c0055]] (1551).
