# 張思聰 Zhāng Sīcōng

Early-Qīng *Shānghán* commentator (Zhāng Zhìcōng 張志聰, *zì* Yǐn'ān 隱菴, 1610–1674); editor of the *Lǚshān-táng* 侶山堂 collective *Shānghán lùn jí zhù*. Named among the late-Míng / early-Qīng *Shānghán* commentators criticized in [[後藤省]] Gotō Sei's *Shāng fēng yuē yán* ([[KR3ef074]]).
