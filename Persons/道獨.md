# Dàodú 道獨

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Zōngbǎo 宗寶, 別號 Kōngyǐn 空隱. Dharma-heir of [[元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái (1576–1630) in the Shòuchāng 壽昌 Cáodòng revival; the principal Guǎngdōng figure of the line. Native of Nánhǎi 南海 (Guǎngzhōufǔ), lay surname Lù 陸. 1600 – 16 August 1661 (lunar 順治 18.7.22), *shìshòu* 62, *xià* 33.

At fourteen left his mother and entered a temple; practised *jìngdìng* 靜定 under a tree with a spontaneous opening that startled his elders. At sixteen tonsured himself, set up a thatched hermitage at Lóngshān 龍山 and lived in solitary practice for more than ten years. At twenty-nine, after his mother's death, went with his brother Língmì 靈泌 to Bóshān, where Yuánlái renamed him Dàodú and conferred the full precepts; stayed nine months and departed. Held a shut-retreat at Jīnlún 金輪, then moved to Huángyán 黃巖.

Abbacies in sequence: **Luófú** 羅浮 (his Guǎngdōng seat, from which the Bóshān line extended into the south); **Hǎizhuàng** 海幢 in Guǎngzhōu; **Léifēng** 雷峰; **Yǒngníng** 永寧; and — in Fújiàn — **Yànhú** 雁湖 and finally **Xīchán Chángqìngsì** 西禪長慶寺 (the seat that gives him his most common style Chángqìng Zōngbǎo Dú). Died seated at Chángqìng in the 7th month of 1661.

Principal dharma-heirs: **[[圅昰]] Tiānrán Hánshì** 天然圅昰 (1608–1685, DILA A010057), the major Guǎngdōng continuer of the line; **Zǔxīn Hánkě** 祖心圅可 (1612–1660, DILA A012087). His 6-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0373]]; X72 n1443) was compiled by the Ming-loyalist-official-turned-monk [[今釋]] Jīnshì 今釋 / Dànguī 澹歸 (1615–1680) — a dharma-grandson through Tiānrán. Dàodú's Guǎngdōng line is particularly well-documented for its role in the Ming-loyalist Buddhist resistance: the Tiānrán Hánshì circle absorbed many *yímín* 遺民 literati into monastic ordination in the years after 1644.
