# Dào-dú 道獨

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Zōng-bǎo 宗寶, 別號 Kōng-yǐn 空隱. Dharma-heir of [[元來]] Wú-yì Yuán-lái (1576–1630) in the Shòu-chāng 壽昌 Cáo-dòng revival; the principal Guǎng-dōng figure of the line. Native of Nán-hǎi 南海 (Guǎng-zhōu-fǔ), lay surname Lù 陸. 1600 – 16 August 1661 (lunar 順治 18.7.22), *shì-shòu* 62, *xià* 33.

At fourteen left his mother and entered a temple; practised *jìng-dìng* 靜定 under a tree with a spontaneous opening that startled his elders. At sixteen tonsured himself, set up a thatched hermitage at Lóng-shān 龍山 and lived in solitary practice for more than ten years. At twenty-nine, after his mother's death, went with his brother Líng-mì 靈泌 to Bó-shān, where Yuán-lái renamed him Dào-dú and conferred the full precepts; stayed nine months and departed. Held a shut-retreat at Jīn-lún 金輪, then moved to Huáng-yán 黃巖.

Abbacies in sequence: **Luó-fú** 羅浮 (his Guǎng-dōng seat, from which the Bó-shān line extended into the south); **Hǎi-zhuàng** 海幢 in Guǎng-zhōu; **Léi-fēng** 雷峰; **Yǒng-níng** 永寧; and — in Fú-jiàn — **Yàn-hú** 雁湖 and finally **Xī-chán Cháng-qìng-sì** 西禪長慶寺 (the seat that gives him his most common style Cháng-qìng Zōng-bǎo Dú). Died seated at Cháng-qìng in the 7th month of 1661.

Principal dharma-heirs: **[[圅昰]] Tiān-rán Hán-shì** 天然圅昰 (1608–1685, DILA A010057), the major Guǎng-dōng continuer of the line; **Zǔ-xīn Hán-kě** 祖心圅可 (1612–1660, DILA A012087). His 6-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0373]]; X72 n1443) was compiled by the Ming-loyalist-official-turned-monk [[今釋]] Jīn-shì 今釋 / Dàn-guī 澹歸 (1615–1680) — a dharma-grandson through Tiān-rán. Dào-dú's Guǎng-dōng line is particularly well-documented for its role in the Ming-loyalist Buddhist resistance: the Tiān-rán Hán-shì circle absorbed many *yí-mín* 遺民 literati into monastic ordination in the years after 1644.
