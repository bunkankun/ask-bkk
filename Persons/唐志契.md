Late-Míng landscape painter and theorist; *zì* Fūwǔ 敷五, a *zhū-shēng* 諸生 of Jiāngdū 江都 (modern Yángzhōu). Born 1579 per CBDB; active in the late *Wànlì* through *Chóngzhēn* decades (c. 1610–1640). With his brother Táng Zhìyǐn 唐志尹 (also a painter) constituted a brother-painter pair celebrated locally. Especially noted for landscape. Author of [[KR3h0049]] *Huì-shì wēi-yán* 繪事微言 (2 *juàn*), the principal late-Míng landscape-painting compendium and a major source for the Kāng-xī [[KR3h0061]] *Pèiwén-zhāi shū-huà pǔ*. His doctrinal alignment is with the Dǒng Qíchāng 董其昌 *literati* school: "the foundation of painting is *qì-yùn*; the priority is *reading books*."
