Huáng Yàn-píng 黃彥平 (d. 1139), *zì* Jì-cén 季岑, *hào* Cì-shān 次山 (also written Cì-cén 次岑 — these variants are responsible for the bibliographic confusion documented in the *Sì-kù* tíyào of [[KR4d0165]]). Native of Liú-jiāng 流江 (Fēng-chéng 豐城-xiàn, modern Jiāng-xī). *Jìn-shì* of Xuān-hé 4 or *Zhèng-hé* later years (date variously reported); *Yuán-yòu*-faction descendant.

His father was Huáng Dé-lǐ 黃得禮 (CBDB id 15448), *zì* Zhí-zhōng 執中, *Yuán-yòu*-period (1086–94) *jìn-shì* who served as *Liǔ-zhōu jūn-shì cān-jūn*. Yàn-píng's elder brother was Huáng Yàn-fǔ 黃彥輔 (CBDB id 15451), *zì* Bó-qiáng 伯強, *Zhèng-hé jìn-shì*. Yàn-píng was the second son.

In *Jìng-kāng* beginning, Yàn-píng was demoted for being close-friends with Lǐ Gāng 李綱. After the southward crossing, repeatedly memorialised on policy. Reached *Lì-bù láng-zhōng* 吏部郎中, served as *Tí-diǎn Hú-nán xíng-yù* 提點湖南刑獄. The *Sì-kù* editors note that his evaluation memorial is *píng-yǔn* (balanced-and-fair), and that his prediction concerning Liú Guāng-shì 劉光世 and Lǚ Zhī 呂祉 was borne out *rú cāo quàn rán* (as if holding a tally).

Zhāng Duān-yì's 張端義 *Guì-ěr jí* preserves a self-correction by Yàn-píng of a *qǐ-cí* (felicitation-piece) — Yàn-píng changed *fāng sì-jiǔ zhī nián* (just at the age of 4 × 9 = 36) on the model of "*Mǎi-chén zì-zhī jiāng-guì*" — showing his *xū-xīn cóng-shàn* (humble-mind to follow good).

CBDB id 15449 has *deathyear* 1139 only.

His collection survives as *Sān-yú jí* 三餘集 [[KR4d0165]] in 4 *juǎn* (Sì-kù *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* reconstruction). The *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén-zhì* mistakenly titles it *Yù-yú jí* 玉餘集 (玉 ↔ 三 graphic confusion).
