Huáng Yànpíng 黃彥平 (d. 1139), *zì* Jìcén 季岑, *hào* Cìshān 次山 (also written Cìcén 次岑 — these variants are responsible for the bibliographic confusion documented in the *Sìkù* tíyào of [[KR4d0165]]). Native of Liújiāng 流江 (Fēngchéng 豐城-xiàn, modern Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of Xuānhé 4 or *Zhènghé* later years (date variously reported); *Yuányòu*-faction descendant.

His father was Huáng Délǐ 黃得禮 (CBDB id 15448), *zì* Zhízhōng 執中, *Yuányòu*-period (1086–94) *jìnshì* who served as *Liǔzhōu jūnshì cānjūn*. Yànpíng's elder brother was Huáng Yànfǔ 黃彥輔 (CBDB id 15451), *zì* Bóqiáng 伯強, *Zhènghé jìnshì*. Yànpíng was the second son.

In *Jìngkāng* beginning, Yànpíng was demoted for being close-friends with Lǐ Gāng 李綱. After the southward crossing, repeatedly memorialised on policy. Reached *Lìbù lángzhōng* 吏部郎中, served as *Tídiǎn Húnán xíngyù* 提點湖南刑獄. The *Sìkù* editors note that his evaluation memorial is *píngyǔn* (balanced-and-fair), and that his prediction concerning Liú Guāngshì 劉光世 and Lǚ Zhī 呂祉 was borne out *rú cāo quàn rán* (as if holding a tally).

Zhāng Duānyì's 張端義 *Guìěr jí* preserves a self-correction by Yànpíng of a *qǐcí* (felicitation-piece) — Yànpíng changed *fāng sìjiǔ zhī nián* (just at the age of 4 × 9 = 36) on the model of "*Mǎichén zìzhī jiāngguì*" — showing his *xūxīn cóngshàn* (humble-mind to follow good).

CBDB id 15449 has *deathyear* 1139 only.

His collection survives as *Sānyú jí* 三餘集 [[KR4d0165]] in 4 *juǎn* (Sìkù *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* reconstruction). The *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì* mistakenly titles it *Yùyú jí* 玉餘集 (玉 ↔ 三 graphic confusion).
