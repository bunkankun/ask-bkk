**Lǚ Yuánsù** 呂元素 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist ritualist, author of [[KR5g0033]] ***Dào mén dìng zhì*** 道門定制 (preface 1188) — an authoritative ritual-regulations manual for Southern-Sòng Daoist monasteries.
