# Shī-zhù 師住

Mid-Qīng Línjì Chán monk, *shū-jì* 書記 (archivist) in the Liáo-dōng assembly of [[融|Yǒng-jì Róng]] 永濟融 at **Guǎng-níng Pǔ-cí-sì** 廣寧普慈寺 and related seats. Co-recorder (*děng lù* 等錄) of [[KR6q0427]] 《永濟融禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B209) together with [[妙印]] Miào-yìn.

The *fǎ-huì* 師住 ("Teacher-Dwelling") is unusual and does not align with standard Mìyún-line generation-character conventions — possibly reflects a Liáo-dōng regional naming practice in the Manchu-frontier Chán community, or a personal *fǎ-huì* rather than generational. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0427]] juan-head signature line "*shū-jì Shī-zhù Miào-yìn lù* 書記師住妙印錄".
