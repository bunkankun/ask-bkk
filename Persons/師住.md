# Shīzhù 師住

Mid-Qīng Línjì Chán monk, *shūjì* 書記 (archivist) in the Liáodōng assembly of [[融|Yǒngjì Róng]] 永濟融 at **Guǎngníng Pǔcísì** 廣寧普慈寺 and related seats. Co-recorder (*děng lù* 等錄) of [[KR6q0427]] 《永濟融禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B209) together with [[妙印]] Miàoyìn.

The *fǎhuì* 師住 ("Teacher-Dwelling") is unusual and does not align with standard Mìyún-line generation-character conventions — possibly reflects a Liáodōng regional naming practice in the Manchu-frontier Chán community, or a personal *fǎhuì* rather than generational. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0427]] juan-head signature line "*shūjì Shīzhù Miàoyìn lù* 書記師住妙印錄".
