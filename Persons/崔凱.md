**Cuī Kǎi** 崔凱 (third century, Cáo-Wèi 曹魏), court ritualist, dates not preserved. He has no standard biography in *Sānguó zhì* and is known principally through citation in Dù Yòu 杜佑's *Tōngdiǎn*, where his *Sāngfú nánwèn* 喪服難問 ([[KR1d0123]]) is preserved as a single-juàn fragment.

His principal preserved contribution is the *Sì-gū lùn* 四孤論 (Four Orphans Discussion), an early *biàn-lǐ* treatment of mass-displacement-and-orphan mourning protocols set against the Three-Kingdoms famine context. He is most likely a member of the prominent Bó-líng 博陵 / Qīng-hé 清河 Cuī clan (which produced numerous Cáo-Wèi court officials), but the specific lineage-link is not preserved.
