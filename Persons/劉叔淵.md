**Liú Shū-yuān** 劉叔淵 (late Yuán / early Míng), a senior disciple (*gāo-dì* 高弟) of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhèn-hēng (1281–1358) and the father of the early-Míng physician [[劉純]] Liú Chún (*c.* 1340 – 1412). Known almost exclusively through Liú Chún's filial preface to the *Yī-jīng xiǎo-xué* ([[KR3er051]]), in which Shū-yuān is credited with the personal transmission of the *Dān-xī* 丹溪 doctrines to his son. No independently transmitted writings survive. Not in CBDB.
