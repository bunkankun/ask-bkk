**Liú Shūyuān** 劉叔淵 (late Yuán / early Míng), a senior disciple (*gāodì* 高弟) of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng (1281–1358) and the father of the early-Míng physician [[劉純]] Liú Chún (*c.* 1340 – 1412). Known almost exclusively through Liú Chún's filial preface to the *Yījīng xiǎoxué* ([[KR3er051]]), in which Shūyuān is credited with the personal transmission of the *Dānxī* 丹溪 doctrines to his son. No independently transmitted writings survive. Not in CBDB.
