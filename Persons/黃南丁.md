"Huáng Nándīng 黃南丁" is the name (very likely a pen name) of the author of [[KR4k0268]] *Yáng Nǎiwǔ yǔ Xiǎo Báicài* 楊乃武與小白菜. No historical individual with this name has been identified in Chinese biographical or bibliographic sources. The name may be a pseudonym constructed from the Heavenly Stem *dīng* 丁 (the fourth stem) combined with a directional element.
