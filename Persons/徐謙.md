**Xú Qiān** 徐謙 (*zì* Zhòngguāng 仲光, *fl.* mid-late Míng), Míng-period physician of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng); specialist in pediatric smallpox medicine. Author of the ***Rén-duān lù*** 仁端錄 ([[KR3e0069]], 16 juan) — a comprehensive treatise on smallpox and rashes, with the final juan covering measles. The work was edited and trimmed by Xú's disciple [[陳葵]] Chén Kuí. Xú has no further transmitted biographical record beyond the work.
