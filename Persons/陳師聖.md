**Chén Shīshèng** 陳師聖 (correctly read as 李師聖 Lǐ Shīshèng per Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*; *fl.* late Northern / early Southern Sòng), Sòng-period scholar of Púyáng 濮陽. Discovered 21 *piān* of an old anonymous *Chǎn lùn* (Discussion on Childbirth) — discussions only, with no prescriptions — to which the contemporary Imperial Medical Academy Professor (醫學教授) [[郭稽中]] Guō Jīzhōng appended prescriptions, completing the work as the ***Chǎnyù bǎoqìng jí*** 產育寶慶集 ([[KR3e0040]], 2 juan). The catalog meta lists Chén Shīshèng but Chén Zhènsūn gives the surname as Lǐ — likely a transcriptional error. Lǐ Shīshèng has no further transmitted biographical record.
