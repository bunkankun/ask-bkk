Wáng Tǎn 王坦, *zì* Jítú 吉途. Native of Nán-tōng-zhōu 南通州 (modern Nán-tōng, Jiāngsū). Mid-Qīng *qín* music-theorist. *Fl.* ca. 1730–1755 — his sole surviving work, the *Qín zhǐ* 琴旨 ([[KR1i0022]]) in 2 *juàn*, is dependent on the imperial *Lǜ-lǚ zhèng-yì* (presented 1714) and was current by the time of the *Sìkù* compilation.

CBDB returns multiple homonymous *Wáng Tǎn*s but none of them confidently match — none have Nán-tōng-zhōu provenance and none give the *zì* Jítú. CBDB id is therefore left blank, with the conventional flourishing-period assignment based on the work's textual dependence.
