**Wú Dàoyuán** 吳道源 (*fl.* mid-Qīng), gynecological physician. Sole transmitted work: *Nǚkē qièyào* 女科切要 ([[KR3ei017]]), an eight-*juǎn* systematic gynecology treatise. On internal citation evidence (extensive reference to Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* and Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jì-yīn gāng-mù*), composition is bracketed in the mid-to-late 18th century.
