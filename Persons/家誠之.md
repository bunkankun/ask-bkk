Late-Northern-Sòng official, nephew (sister's son) of Wén Tóng [[文同]] (1018–1079); compiler of his uncle's collected works *Dān-yuān jí* [[KR4d0048]] in 40 *juǎn* shortly after Wén's death in 1079. The original 40-*juǎn* arrangement preserved through the Sòng-Yuán-Míng witnesses (and in the late-Míng Chén Jì-rú 陳繼儒 SBCK recension of 1633) is Jiā Chéng-zhī's editorial work. No CBDB match (BIOG_MAIN does not have a confident entry); fl. ca. 1080–1100.
