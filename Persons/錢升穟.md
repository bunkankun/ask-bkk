**Qián Shēngsuì** 錢升穟 of Yuèjùn 越郡 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng) — Qīng obstetrician documented in Zhōu Dānchén's 1856 preface to [[KR3ei053]] *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* as the physician who first received the manuscript of [[單南山]] Shàn Nánshān's work and gained province-wide fame in obstetrics through its application. Active before 1773, when his manuscript was further transmitted to [[伊學曾]] Yī Xuézēng of Níngbō. Not in CBDB.
