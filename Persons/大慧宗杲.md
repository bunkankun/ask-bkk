# Dàhuì Zōnggǎo 大慧宗杲

Southern-Sòng Chán master (1089–1163), Dharma heir of Yuánwù Kèqín 圜悟克勤; principal advocate of *kànhuà* 看話 ("contemplating the phrase") practice and the most influential Línjì figure of his generation.
