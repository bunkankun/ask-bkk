Fán Gōng 樊恭 (fl. early-to-mid third century), a Three Kingdoms 三國魏 lexicographer known almost exclusively as the compiler of *Guǎng cāng* 廣蒼 ([[KR1j0100]]), a *Cāngjié*-tradition character primer in three *juàn* recorded in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志. The work was lost in the Sòng but is repeatedly cited in *Yánshì jiāxùn* 顏氏家訓 (by [[顏之推]]), the *Yùpiān* 玉篇 (by [[顧野王]]), and the *Guǎngyùn* 廣韻; surviving fragments are reconstructed in the CHANT entry. No biography in any standard history; he is sometimes conflated in late catalogs with other Hàn–Wèi Fán-surname philologists but no firm identification is established. CBDB has no matching entry.
