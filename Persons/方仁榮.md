Fāng Rénróng 方仁榮 (fl. 1260–1263) — a late-Southern-Sòng prefectural school officer who served as *xuélù* 學錄 (school-recorder) of Yánzhōu 嚴州 during the early Jǐngdìng era. With his colleague Zhèng Bǎo 鄭珤 (the prefectural *jiàoshòu*) he co-compiled the *Jǐngdìng Yánzhōu xùzhì* 景定嚴州續志 [[KR2k0020]] in 10 juan, completed in Jǐngdìng *rénxū* (1262), under the patronage of the prefect Qián Kězé 錢可則. The *Sìkù* tíyào explicitly states "*shǐmò jūn wèixiáng*" — his career is otherwise undocumented. A search of CBDB returns no matching entries (no `cbdbId` recorded). He is potentially related to the prominent Yánzhōu Fāng lineage that produced Fāng Féngchén 方逢辰 (1221–1291, *zhuàngyuán* 1247, who wrote the gazetteer's preface), but no direct kinship is established.
