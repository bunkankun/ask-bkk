Shin'yo 眞譽 (also written 真譽; fl. mid-to-late 12th c.), known by his *bō*-name **Jimyōbō** 持明房, was a Japanese Shingon master of the **Ono-ryū** 小野流 lineage associated with **Daigo-ji** 醍醐寺. His precise lifedates are not recoverable from extant records, but the contents of his sole surviving work [[KR6t0183]] *Kakibukuro* place him in the mid-to-late Heian generation of Ono-ryū ritual systematisers (ca. 1130–1190). The DILA Buddhist person authority records him under code A001113.

Surviving work in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0183]] *Shì dài* (1 fasc.).
