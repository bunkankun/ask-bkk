SòngYuán transitional historian; native of Lèpíng 樂平 in Jiāngxī, son of the late-Sòng grand councilor Mǎ Tíngluán 馬廷鸞. *Zì* Guìyǔ 貴與. Placed first in the Sòng-period *cáoshì* examinations during the *Xiánchún* era but stayed at home with his father after the latter's break with Jiǎ Sìdào 賈似道, and never travelled to take the metropolitan *jìnshì*. Under the Yuán he served as Director of the Kěshān 柯山 Academy and finally as Education Officer of Táizhōu 台州 *rúxué*. He devoted some forty years of private scholarship to the *Wénxiàn Tōngkǎo* 文獻通考 ([[KR2m0009]]), presented to the throne in 1307 and printed by imperial authorization in 1319—the third and largest of the *Sāntōng* and the most detailed surviving institutional history of the Sòng. The catalog meta entry gives Mǎ's lifedates as "*fl.* 1254–1322"; CBDB and Wikidata give 1254–1323, followed here.
