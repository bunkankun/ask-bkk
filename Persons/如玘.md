Late-Yuán / early-Míng Tiāntái-school exegete and imperial Buddhist editor, sobriquets Tàipú 太璞 (also written 大璞) and Jù'ān 具菴. Native of Yúyáo 餘姚 (in modern Zhèjiāng). Lifedates 1320–1385: died Hóngwǔ 18 (1385), eleventh month, seventeenth day, age 66, per the *Xù fózǔ tǒngjì* 續佛祖統紀 j. 2.

Trained in the Tiāntái scholastic tradition, he became an imperial textual collaborator of [[宗泐]] (1318–1390) under the Hóngwǔ emperor's programme to produce authoritative state-sponsored Buddhist commentaries. The pair co-edited the three so-called "imperially-decreed commentaries" of Hóngwǔ 11 (1378): the *Bōrě bōluómìduō xīn jīng zhùjiě* 般若波羅蜜多心經註解 (T1714 = KR6c0141), the *Jīngāng bōrě bōluómì jīng zhùjiě* 金剛般若波羅蜜經註解, and the *Léngqié ābáduóluó bǎojīng zhùjiě* 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經註解 — each prefaced by the Hóngwǔ emperor in person. The pairing of a Línjì Chan abbot (Zōnglè) with a Tiāntái doctrinal scholar (Rúqǐ) reflects the Hóngwǔ programme of cross-school imperial canonisation.

Beyond the imperial commentaries his works include various Tiāntái-style sub-commentaries in the Wàn xù-zàng 卍續藏. He represents the late-Yuán tradition of Tiāntái scholasticism transmitted from Sòng masters via the Wú region, here repurposed into the new Míng state-Buddhist programme.
