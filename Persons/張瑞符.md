# 張瑞符

Zhāng Ruìfú, Qīng-period figure named in [[KR3em032]] *Báihóu biànzhèng* 白喉辨證 as the original recipient of the famous *Cuòlèi sǎn* 錫類散 formula — said to have "obtained a son" through its transmission, whence the formula's name (*cuòlèi* "extending to one's kind").
