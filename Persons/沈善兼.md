**Shěn Shàn-jiān** 沈善兼 (Qīng), name given in the *Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī shàn-běn gǔ-jí cóng-shū* (hxwd) catalog as the author of the *Hóu-kē xīn-fǎ* 喉科心法 ([[KR3em036]]). The text's internal colophon, however, is signed by *Pān Chéng* 潘誠, *zì* *Bǎo-zhēn* 葆真 ([[潘誠]]), dated *Dào-guāng dīng-wèi* (1847); no internal evidence supports the *Shěn* attribution. The catalog metadata is followed here for cross-referencing convenience but the substantive author of the *Hóu-kē xīn-fǎ* is *Pān Chéng*. CBDB has no entry for *Shěn Shàn-jiān*; no further biographical detail located.
