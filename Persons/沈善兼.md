**Shěn Shànjiān** 沈善兼 (Qīng), name given in the *Hǎiwài huíliú zhōngyī shànběn gǔjí cóngshū* (hxwd) catalog as the author of the *Hóukē xīnfǎ* 喉科心法 ([[KR3em036]]). The text's internal colophon, however, is signed by *Pān Chéng* 潘誠, *zì* *Bǎozhēn* 葆真 ([[潘誠]]), dated *Dàoguāng dīngwèi* (1847); no internal evidence supports the *Shěn* attribution. The catalog metadata is followed here for cross-referencing convenience but the substantive author of the *Hóukē xīnfǎ* is *Pān Chéng*. CBDB has no entry for *Shěn Shànjiān*; no further biographical detail located.
