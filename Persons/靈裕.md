Língyù 靈裕 (518–605, conventional dates per DILA A005283 and the *Xù gāosēng zhuàn* 續高僧傳), styled *Yù fǎshī* 裕法師 ("Master Yù") and posthumously *Yù púsà* 裕菩薩 ("Bodhisattva Yù"), the senior Buddhist scholar-monk of the late Northern-Dynasties / Suí transition. A *Dìlùn*-school master in the lineage of [[慧光]] Huìguāng, he was active across the Northern Qí, Northern Zhōu, and early Suí periods, surviving the Northern Zhōu persecution of Buddhism (574 – 578) to emerge as a major Buddhist authority under Suí Wéndì 文帝 (r. 581 – 604), who invited him to lecture in the imperial palace and conferred upon him the title *Yù púsà*. Author of the *[[KR6e0116|Huáyán jīng wén yì jì 華嚴經文義記]]* in 100 fascicles per the *Xù gāosēng zhuàn*, of which only fascicle 6 survives (preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* X211). Per the *Xù gāosēng zhuàn*, his works also include the *Nièpán jīng yì jì* 涅槃經義記, the *Dà chéng yì zhāng* 大乘義章, and a great many other doctrinal-cosmological treatises, of which most are lost.
