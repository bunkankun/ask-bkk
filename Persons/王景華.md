Late-Qīng physician of Xīn-yáng 新陽 (modern Kūn-shān 崑山, Jiāngsū), pupil of the Suzhou physician [[俞養浩]] Yú Yǎng-hào. In the winter of *Guāng-xù* 27 / *xīn-chǒu* 辛丑 (1901) Yú asked him to revise the anonymous manuscript *Náng-mì hóu-shū* 囊秘喉書 during a Suzhou throat-disease epidemic, and Wáng spent the next year sectioning, regularising the prescription names, and editing the text, with collaboration from Xiāo Dōng-yǒu 蕭冬友. The result, his postface for which is dated the fourth month of *Guāng-xù* 28 / 1902, is [[KR3em030]] *Chóng-dìng náng-mì hóu-shū* 重訂囊秘喉書. No CBDB entry; lifedates not recoverable.
