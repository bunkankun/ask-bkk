Late-Qīng physician of Xīnyáng 新陽 (modern Kūnshān 崑山, Jiāngsū), pupil of the Suzhou physician [[俞養浩]] Yú Yǎnghào. In the winter of *Guāngxù* 27 / *xīnchǒu* 辛丑 (1901) Yú asked him to revise the anonymous manuscript *Nángmì hóushū* 囊秘喉書 during a Suzhou throat-disease epidemic, and Wáng spent the next year sectioning, regularising the prescription names, and editing the text, with collaboration from Xiāo Dōngyǒu 蕭冬友. The result, his postface for which is dated the fourth month of *Guāngxù* 28 / 1902, is [[KR3em030]] *Chóngdìng nángmì hóushū* 重訂囊秘喉書. No CBDB entry; lifedates not recoverable.
