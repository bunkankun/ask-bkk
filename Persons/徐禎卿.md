Xú Zhēnqīng 徐禎卿 (1479–1511), *zì* **Chānggǔ** 昌穀 (also *zì* **Chāngguó** 昌國), *hào* **Dígōng** 迪功 (after his post), of **Wúxiàn** 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū) — one of the **Wúzhōng sìcái** 吳中四傑 (Four Talents of Wúzhōng) with **Táng Yín** 唐寅, **Wén Zhēngmíng** 文徵明, **Zhù Yǔnmíng** ([[祝允明]]). Hóngzhì 18 / *yǐchǒu* (1505) *jìnshì*; appointed *Dàlǐsì zuǒ sìfù*; *zuò shīqiú* (because of escape-of-prisoner) demoted to *Guózǐjiàn bóshì*; died at 33. *Míngshǐ Wényuàn zhuàn*. Earliest *Sūzhōu* member of the *Qián Qīzǐ* (Former Seven Masters); his exchange-of-letters with **Lǐ Mèngyáng** ([[李夢陽]]) and his treatise *Tányì lù* 談藝錄 are foundational documents of *Qián Qīzǐ* poetics. Famous for *lǜdàn ér sī shēn* (thought-calm and reflection-deep), the opposite pole of Lǐ Mèngyáng's aggressive *fùgǔ*. **Wáng Shìzhēn**'s parallel of Xú's 300-poem *Dígōng jí* self-edition with **Huáng Tíngjiān**'s 300-poem self-edition of his *Jīnghuá lù* is a documented critical pairing. His writings are the *Dígōng jí* in 6 *juǎn* + appended *Tányì lù* in 1 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0166]]). CBDB id 33842, 1479–1511.
