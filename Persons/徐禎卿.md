Xú Zhēn-qīng 徐禎卿 (1479–1511), *zì* **Chāng-gǔ** 昌穀 (also *zì* **Chāng-guó** 昌國), *hào* **Dí-gōng** 迪功 (after his post), of **Wú-xiàn** 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū) — one of the **Wú-zhōng sì-cái** 吳中四傑 (Four Talents of Wú-zhōng) with **Táng Yín** 唐寅, **Wén Zhēng-míng** 文徵明, **Zhù Yǔn-míng** ([[祝允明]]). Hóng-zhì 18 / *yǐ-chǒu* (1505) *jìn-shì*; appointed *Dà-lǐ-sì zuǒ sì-fù*; *zuò shī-qiú* (because of escape-of-prisoner) demoted to *Guó-zǐ-jiàn bó-shì*; died at 33. *Míng-shǐ Wén-yuàn zhuàn*. Earliest *Sū-zhōu* member of the *Qián-Qī-zǐ* (Former Seven Masters); his exchange-of-letters with **Lǐ Mèng-yáng** ([[李夢陽]]) and his treatise *Tán-yì lù* 談藝錄 are foundational documents of *Qián-Qī-zǐ* poetics. Famous for *lǜ-dàn ér sī shēn* (thought-calm and reflection-deep), the opposite pole of Lǐ Mèng-yáng's aggressive *fù-gǔ*. **Wáng Shì-zhēn**'s parallel of Xú's 300-poem *Dí-gōng jí* self-edition with **Huáng Tíng-jiān**'s 300-poem self-edition of his *Jīng-huá lù* is a documented critical pairing. His writings are the *Dí-gōng jí* in 6 *juǎn* + appended *Tán-yì lù* in 1 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0166]]). CBDB id 33842, 1479–1511.
