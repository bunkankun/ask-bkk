**Diāo Bùzhōng** 刁步忠 (Qīng), late-Qīng *hóukē* (throat-medicine) specialist of *Chóngmíng* 崇明 (the Yangtze-estuary island, now a district of Shànghǎi). Father of [[刁質明]] Diāo Zhìmíng (*zì* *Shǒuyú* 守愚). His unpublished manuscript notes on family-tradition throat-medicine were redacted into the four-*juǎn* *Hóukē jiāxùn* 喉科家訓 ([[KR3em038]]) by his son in 1918. He is known only through the prefaces of that work: *Yáng Hànchūn* 楊漢春's preface dated 1924 reports that "Lord Bùzhōng" had died seventeen years earlier (i.e. *ca.* 1907) and that Yáng had befriended both father and son during his own student years. Death date *ca.* 1907 follows Yáng's chronology. CBDB has no entry.
