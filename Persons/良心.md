# 良心 Liángxīn

**Liángxīn** 良心 (Japanese **Ryōshin** / Korean **Ryangsim**), 15th-century Japanese monk and acupuncturist; named in [[韓繼禧]] Hán Jìxǐ's 1474 preface to the Korean *Shényìng jīng* 神應經 ([[KR3ee029]]) as the bearer of the Chinese work and of the Japanese *Yōngjū bāxué fǎ* 癰疽八穴法 to the Chosŏn court of King Sŏngjong.
