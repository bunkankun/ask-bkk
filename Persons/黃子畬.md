**Huáng Zǐyú** 黃子畬 (*fl.* early 20th c.) — Qīng publisher of the 1906 (Guāngxù *bǐngwǔ*) stone-lithograph edition of [[倪枝維]] Ní Zhīwéi's *Chǎnbǎo* ([[KR3ei057]]) at his Quánshíshānfáng 拳石山房 studio. Likely a descendant or relative of [[黃祖經]] Huáng Zǔjīng. Not in CBDB.
