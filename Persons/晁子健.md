**Cháo Zǐjiàn** 晁子健 (fl. mid-12th c.), grandson of Cháo Yuèzhī 晁說之 [[晁說之]], of Jùyě 鉅野 (Shāndōng). Compiled and printed his grandfather's literary collection *Jǐngyūshēng jí* 景迂生集 / *Sōngshān wénjí* 嵩山文集 [[KR4d0104]] in 20 *juǎn* in the early Southern-Sòng period, providing the principal channel of transmission for Cháo Yuèzhī's prose. His own dates and career are not securely established beyond his role as family editor.
