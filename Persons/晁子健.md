**Cháo Zǐjiàn** 晁子健 (fl. mid-12th c.), grandson of Cháo Yuè-zhī 晁說之 [[晁說之]], of Jù-yě 鉅野 (Shān-dōng). Compiled and printed his grandfather's literary collection *Jǐng-yū-shēng jí* 景迂生集 / *Sōng-shān wén-jí* 嵩山文集 [[KR4d0104]] in 20 *juǎn* in the early Southern-Sòng period, providing the principal channel of transmission for Cháo Yuè-zhī's prose. His own dates and career are not securely established beyond his role as family editor.
