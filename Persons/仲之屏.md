Early-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) Buddhist layman of **Cháng-shuǐ** 長水 (Jiā-xīng region, Zhèjiāng), *zì* **Shì-shí** 士石. Lifedates unrecorded. Trained in Confucian classics in youth, turned to Buddhist study in old age. Compiled *Jīn-gāng jīng zhù zhèng-é* 金剛經註正訛 ([[KR6c0077]], X25 no. 489), a one-juan critical-edition commentary that *huì-zuǎn zhū zhù, shān fán jiǎn yào, jiě yì zhèng-é* 彚纂諸註，刪繁簡要，解義正譌 ("compendizes the commentaries, prunes the diffuse to keep the essential, explains the meanings and rectifies the errors"). Also composed a *Bān-ruò xīn-jīng zhù-shū* 般若心經註疏 in the Kāng-xī era. Both works belong to the early-Qīng lay-Buddhist *kǎo-zhèng*-tinged commentary genre.
