**Jiǎng Jì** 蔣驥 (b. 1714, *fl.* 1707–1727 per the Kanripo catalog meta — see disambiguation note below), *zì* Sùchéng 涑塍, studio name Shāndàigé 山帶閣 ("Mountain-Girdle Pavilion"), of Jīntán 金壇 in Chángzhōufǔ 常州府 (modern Jīntán, Jiāngsū).

Qīng *Chǔcí* scholar of the early-eighteenth century. Author of **《山帶閣註楚辭》** ([[KR4a0007]]), a six-juan annotated edition completed in the Yōngzhèng era; the work is a major Qīng-period reading of the anthology, distinguished by its systematic geographical reconstruction of Qū Yuán's exile route and by its methodologically clear distinction between text-criticism, gloss, and paraphrase. It is one of the standard Qīng *Chǔcí* commentaries cited in modern variorum editions alongside Wáng Fūzhī, Wáng Yuán 王邦采, and Dài Zhènyǐng 戴震影.

CBDB id 89573 (江蘇·金壇 native, b. 1714). The catalog meta's *fl.* 1707–1727 may reflect the working window of his *Chǔcí* studies rather than firm biographical dates; CBDB records the birth year only. There is also a Míng-period 蔣驥 (CBDB 132576, 1378–1430), with whom this Qīng scholar is sometimes confused — the *Shāndàigé* attribution unambiguously identifies the Qīng figure.
