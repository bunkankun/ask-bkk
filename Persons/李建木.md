**Lǐ Jiànmù** 李建木 (*fl.* late 16th c., 明), son of [[李時珍]] Lǐ Shízhēn. Together with his brother [[李建元]] Lǐ Jiànyuán, he oversaw the posthumous 1596 first printing of his father's *Běncǎo gāngmù* 本草綱目 ([[KR3ec025]]) and likely had a hand in compiling derivative works such as the *Bīnhú pàozhì fǎ* 瀕湖炮炙法 ([[KR3ec087]]). No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
