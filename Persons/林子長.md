# Lín Zǐ-cháng 林子長 (fl. mid-13th c.)

*Hào* Bǐ-fēng 筆峯. Native of Mǐn (Fújiàn). A *jīng-xué jiào-yù* 京學教諭 (instructor at the capital school) of the late Southern Sòng who annotated and supplied gloss for the *Lùn-xué shéng-chǐ* 論學繩尺 [[KR4h0056]] compiled by **Wèi Tiān-yīng** 魏天應. Lín's annotations distil the *Yuán-yòu* through *Xián-chún* examination-essay tradition into a model-essay handbook with formal categories (*pò-tí*, *jiē-tí*, *xiǎo-jiǎng*, *dà-jiǎng*, *rù-tí*, *yuán-tí*) — the proto-form of the later Míng-Qīng *bā-gǔ wén* eight-legged essay structure.
