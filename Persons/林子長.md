# Lín Zǐcháng 林子長 (fl. mid-13th c.)

*Hào* Bǐfēng 筆峯. Native of Mǐn (Fújiàn). A *jīngxué jiàoyù* 京學教諭 (instructor at the capital school) of the late Southern Sòng who annotated and supplied gloss for the *Lùnxué shéngchǐ* 論學繩尺 [[KR4h0056]] compiled by **Wèi Tiānyīng** 魏天應. Lín's annotations distil the *Yuányòu* through *Xiánchún* examination-essay tradition into a model-essay handbook with formal categories (*pòtí*, *jiētí*, *xiǎojiǎng*, *dàjiǎng*, *rùtí*, *yuántí*) — the proto-form of the later MíngQīng *bāgǔ wén* eight-legged essay structure.
