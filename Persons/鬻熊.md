**Yù Xióng** 鬻熊 was a legendary counselor to the Zhōu founding king Wén wáng 文王; traditional attributed author of the ***Yù zǐ*** 鬻子 ([[KR5f0005]]). The received *Yù zǐ* text is a later Hàn or Six-Dynasties compilation circulating under his name.
