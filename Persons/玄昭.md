Genshō 玄昭, Shingon-line Siddham scholar of the **mid-Heian period**. Precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works; the *Shittan ryakki* [[KR6t0415]] is the principal surviving work and is dated to the late 10th or early 11th c. on the basis of doctrinal vocabulary. He stands in the **Kūkai → Annen → Genshō** line of Japanese Siddham scholarship and his work, though derivative of his predecessors, was the **principal Shingon noviciate Siddham primer** of the late Heian and Kamakura periods. The DILA authority id is `A001253`.
