# Língyīn 靈因

Tang Buddhist monk; member of [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng's translation committee at Hóngfúsì 弘福寺 in Cháng'ān, named in [[智光]] Chikō's Nara-period *Bōrě xīnjīng shùyì* ([[KR6c0196]]) translation-history section as one of the committee that produced Xuánzàng's *Heart Sūtra* translation (Zhēnguān 19 = 645).
