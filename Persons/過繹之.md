**Guò Yìzhī** 過繹之, Kāngxī-era Sūzhōu physician, compiler of *Wúzhōng yīàn* 吳中醫案; cited in [[唐大烈]] Táng Dàliè's preface to [[KR3eq017]] *Wúyī huìjiǎng* (1792) as the institutional precedent for the Sūzhōu-region focus.
