Shùn 順 (the catalog form; full reference **Lǎngkōng Shùn** 朗空順), *zì* **Lǎngkōng** 朗空 ("Bright Emptiness"); often referred to as **Pánshān Lǎngkōng** 盤山朗空. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pánshān** 盤山 — thus a co-disciple of **Huīzhōu Hào** [[昊]] (cf. [[KR6q0573]]). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Yǒngpíngfǔ Chānglíxiàn** 永平府昌黎縣 (Héběi); lay surname **Zhào** 趙.

**Formation.** Vegetarian from childhood. Drawn to the dharma; introduced by lay-Buddhist **Zhāng Mínghuī** 張明輝 to **Pánshān Gǔzhōngpán Zhèngfǎsì** 盤山古中盤正法寺 to take refuge under **Liǎozōng** 了宗和尚, receiving three refuges and five precepts plus a *huàtóu* (meditation phrase). One day during *zhèngzuò* "got it" suddenly. After both parents' deaths formally took tonsure under Liǎozōng; took full ordination at **Wànshànsì** 萬善寺 (the same monastery his co-disciple [[昊]] later abbacied). Served Liǎozōng over a decade; received final *fùzhǔ* on his teacher's deathbed (1688).

**Abbacies.** **Lóngquánsì** 龍泉寺; **Yǒngshèngān** 永盛菴 (= the *Lèyì Yǒngshèngān* 樂邑永盛菴 named in juan 1).

**Disciples.** **Shíxìng** [[實性]] (recorder; nun); **Shíxiáng** 實祥 (jointly engraved the *yǔlù*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0585]] 《盤山朗空順禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B473, recorded by attendant **Shíxìng** [[實性]] *jìlù*.

**Sources.** 《盤山朗空順禪師語錄》 vol. 1; 《五燈全書》 vol. 106.
