Shùn 順 (the catalog form; full reference **Lǎng-kōng Shùn** 朗空順), *zì* **Lǎng-kōng** 朗空 ("Bright Emptiness"); often referred to as **Pán-shān Lǎng-kōng** 盤山朗空. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pán-shān** 盤山 — thus a co-disciple of **Huī-zhōu Hào** [[昊]] (cf. [[KR6q0573]]). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Yǒng-píng-fǔ Chāng-lí-xiàn** 永平府昌黎縣 (Hé-běi); lay surname **Zhào** 趙.

**Formation.** Vegetarian from childhood. Drawn to the dharma; introduced by lay-Buddhist **Zhāng Míng-huī** 張明輝 to **Pán-shān Gǔ-zhōng-pán Zhèng-fǎ-sì** 盤山古中盤正法寺 to take refuge under **Liǎo-zōng** 了宗和尚, receiving three refuges and five precepts plus a *huà-tóu* (meditation phrase). One day during *zhèng-zuò* "got it" suddenly. After both parents' deaths formally took tonsure under Liǎo-zōng; took full ordination at **Wàn-shàn-sì** 萬善寺 (the same monastery his co-disciple [[昊]] later abbacied). Served Liǎo-zōng over a decade; received final *fù-zhǔ* on his teacher's deathbed (1688).

**Abbacies.** **Lóng-quán-sì** 龍泉寺; **Yǒng-shèng-ān** 永盛菴 (= the *Lè-yì Yǒng-shèng-ān* 樂邑永盛菴 named in juan 1).

**Disciples.** **Shí-xìng** [[實性]] (recorder; nun); **Shí-xiáng** 實祥 (jointly engraved the *yǔ-lù*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0585]] 《盤山朗空順禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B473, recorded by attendant **Shí-xìng** [[實性]] *jì-lù*.

**Sources.** 《盤山朗空順禪師語錄》 vol. 1; 《五燈全書》 vol. 106.
