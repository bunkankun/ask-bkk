Yè Yǒu 葉酉 (mid-eighteenth century, fl. *Qiánlóng* era), zì Shūshān 書山, hào Huānán 花南, native of Tóngchéng 桐城 (Ān-qìng 安慶 prefecture, Ān-huī). *Qiánlóng jǐwèi* (1739) *jìnshì*; rose to Left Adjutant Tutor of the Heir-Apparent's Household (左春坊左庶子). He was a direct disciple of Fāng Bāo 方苞 (1668–1749) — the founding figure of the Tóngchéng school — and his sole *Sìkù*-admitted work, *Chūnqiū jiū yí* 春秋究遺 ([[KR1e0120]]) in 16 *juǎn*, was composed as a continuation and elaboration of Fāng Bāo's *Chūnqiū tōng lùn* ([[KR1e0110]]). Birth and death dates not on record.
