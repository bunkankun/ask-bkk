# Huì-jìng 慧鏡

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[智及]] Yú-ān Zhì-jí (1311–1378); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
