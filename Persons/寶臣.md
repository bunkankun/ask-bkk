Bǎochén 寶臣 (DILA Authority A001919; Sòng dynasty, lifedates not securely recorded) was a Sòng-period monk active in the eastern capital (東都僧 — i.e. Kāifēng under the Northern Sòng, before the move to Lín'ān in 1127). He is known almost exclusively for his single surviving work, the *Zhù dàshèng rù léngqié jīng* 注大乘入楞伽經 ([[KR6i0334]], T1791) in ten fascicles — a substantial commentary on [[實叉難陀]]'s *[[KR6i0329|Dàshèng rù léngqié jīng 大乘入楞伽經]]* (T672), the seven-fascicle Tang version of the *Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra*. The biographical record of [[寶臣]] is otherwise essentially empty, with the exception of the colophonic notice at the head of T1791 that identifies him with the eastern-capital saṅgha. Should not be confused with the Sòng commoner Fēng-yǒu-fú 豐有孚 (*zì* Bǎochén; DILA A001920), who was a layman and an official, not a monk.
