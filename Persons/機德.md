Jī-dé 機德, *zì* **Tiān-wéi** 天惟. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Miào-yún Zhēn-xióng** [[真雄]] (b. 1634). Lifedates unknown, late 17th / early 18th century. Native of **Dān-yáng** 丹陽 (Jiāng-sū). Recorder *jì-lù* in his master's *fǎ-yǔ* — the third entry of juan 4 is the *shì* "示天惟德記錄". Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of [[KR6q0535]] 《大悲妙雲禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B415, printed in 1713.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 102 (X82 n1571 p. 611c16).
