Xǔ Dòng 許洞 (Sòng), *zì* Dòngfū 洞夫, was a Northern Sòng official and military-strategy writer of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu region). *Jìnshì* of *Xiánpíng* 3 (1000). Author of the *Hǔ qián jīng* 虎鈐經 ([[KR3b0013]], the comprehensive 20-juan military treatise of his Sòng generation, completed 1000), the principal post-Táng predecessor to the *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* ([[KR3b0012]]).
