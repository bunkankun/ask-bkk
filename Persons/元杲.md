Gangō 元杲 (914–995) was a mid-Heian Japanese Shingon master, senior disciple of **Junnyū** 淳祐 of Ishiyama-dera. He rose successively to the abbacy of **Hannya-ji** 般若寺, to **Tōji chōja** 東寺長者 (head of Tōji), and to *Dainagon Sōzu* 大納言僧都. He resided principally at **Daigo-ji** 醍醐寺 — the *Gankai* sub-temple — and was active at the Tōji Hōbodai-in 寶菩提院, the Hirosawa-ryū-aligned doctrinal centre.

His three surviving works in the Kanripo corpus collectively span the principal scholarly domains of mid-Heian Shingon: ritual procedure ([[KR6t0176]] *Jù-zhī guàn-dǐng yí-shì*, on the fully-equipped *abhiṣeka*), the Vajra-realm contemplation ([[KR6t0177]] *Jīn-gāng-jiè jiǔ-huì mì-jì*, on the Nine Assemblies of the Vajra Mandala), and the Garbha-realm exegesis ([[KR6t0178]] *Tāi-zàng-jiè sān-bù mì-shì*, on the Three Families of the Garbha Mandala). In Shingon lineage genealogy he stands as a bridge between the early-Heian Junnyū systematization and the late-Heian Ningai 仁海 / Saisen 濟暹 / Kakuban 覺鑁 doctrinal florescence.

Surviving works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0176]] *Gushi kanjō gishiki* (1 fasc.); [[KR6t0177]] *Kongōkai kue mikki* (1 fasc.); [[KR6t0178]] *Taizō-kai sanbu hishaku* (1 fasc.).
