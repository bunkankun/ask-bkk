**Lù Xīn-yuán** 陸心源 (*zì* **Gāng-fǔ** 剛甫, *hào* **Cún-zhāi** 存齋, library-name **Bì-Sòng-lóu** 㠦宋樓 "Pavilion-of-Hoarding-Sòng-Editions", 1834–1894), the great late-Qīng book-collector of Guī-ān 歸安 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* 1862, served in various provincial posts before retiring to his collection. His **Bì-Sòng-lóu** was the largest private library of late-Qīng China, holding more than 150,000 *juǎn* of rare Sòng and Yuán editions; the entire collection was sold to the Iwasaki family of Japan in 1907 (his son Lù Chún 陸樹聲's reluctant sale during the post-1900 financial difficulties) and forms the founding core of the **Seikadō Bunko** 静嘉堂文庫 in Tōkyō. Lù was also a major *kǎo-zhèng* textual scholar, with substantial works including the *Sòng shǐ yì* 宋史翼 and the *Yí-shū lù* 儀顧錄. He was an early supporter of [[莫枚士]] Mò Méi-shì's medical-philological project, contributing the 1871 preface to **[[KR3eq014]]** *Yán-jīng yán*. Not in CBDB.
