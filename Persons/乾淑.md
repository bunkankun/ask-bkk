Mid- to late-Táng Tiāntái 天台 disciple; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. DILA Authority A001011. Compiler (集 *jí*) of the **Zhǐguān jìzhōng yìyì** 止觀記中異義 ([[KR6d0136]], X55n0918) — a one-*juan* record of the variant readings (*yìyì* 異義) in Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān fǔxíng zhuànhóngjué*, expounded by **Dàosuì** 道邃 ([[道邃]]) and compiled by Qiánshū. The work belongs to the post-Zhànrán Tiāntái scholastic transmission at Tiāntáishān.

Source: DILA A001011; X55n0918.
