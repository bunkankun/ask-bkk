Zhōngfēng Míngběn 中峰明本 (1263–1323), one of the most influential Yuán-dynasty Chán masters and the key figure for the early-14th-century Línjì 臨濟 Yángqí-branch 楊岐派 revival. *Hào* Zhōngfēng 中峰 ("Central Peak"), also Huànzhù dàorén 幻住道人 ("Illusory-Dwelling Way-Man") or Huànzhù lǎorén 幻住老人. Posthumous titles: *shì* Pǔyìng guóshī 普應國師 ("National Master of Universal Response"), Fó-cí Yuánzhào Guǎnghuì chánshī 佛慈圓照廣慧禪師, and Zhìjué chánshī 智覺禪師; honorific epithet Jiāng-nán gǔ fó 江南古佛 ("Old Buddha of Jiāng-nán"). Native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu), lay surname Sūn 孫.

Born Zhìyuán 1.1.8 (8 January 1263). Entered monastic life in youth at Tiānmù shān 天目山; received dharma-transmission from Gāofēng Yuánmiào 高峰原妙 (1238–1295) as his principal dharma-heir, subsequently becoming the principal bearer of the Gāofēng-Yuánmiào line of the Línjì-Yángqí lineage into the early Yuán. Held multiple hermitage-residencies around Tiānmù shān, famously refusing the major institutional abbacies when they were offered. The Huànzhù-ān 幻住庵 ("Illusory-Dwelling Hermitage") on Tiānmù shān became his principal residence from the late 1290s onward.

Authored an extensive corpus of Chán writings, including:

- *Huànzhù-ān qīng guī* 幻住庵清規 ([[KR6q0139]], X63 n1248, 1 juan, Yánhòu 4 = 1317) — hermitage-level monastic code;
- Extensive *yǔlù* material preserved in the *Tiānmù Zhōngfēng héshàng guǎnglù* 天目中峰和尚廣錄 (30 juan);
- Various pedagogical-verse and letter compositions.

Died on Zhìyuán 3.8.14 (22 September 1323), aged 61.

Míngběn's principal dharma-heirs carried the Línjì-Yángqí lineage into the later Yuán and early Ming periods; the lineage-line reaching from Gāofēng → Míngběn → Míngběn's disciples → successors forward is the principal Chinese Línjì-Yángqí genealogy through the Yuán-Ming transition.

Míngběn's influence in Japan was extensive: several Japanese monks studied under him at Tiānmù shān in the 1310s and 1320s, and the *Huànzhù-ān qīng guī* and Míngběn's *yǔlù* material circulated widely in Muromachi-period Rinzai monastic culture.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0139]] *Huànzhù-ān qīng guī*, and [[KR6q0335]] *Tiān-mù Míng-běn chánshī zá-lù* (3 juan, miscellaneous teaching compilation parallel to the 30-juan *guǎng-lù*).
