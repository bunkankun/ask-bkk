Chén Zétōng 陳則通 (lifedates not preserved), known by his style *Tiěshān xiānshēng* 鐵山先生, native place not preserved. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that Zhū Yízūn's 朱彝尊 *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考 lists him between Liú Zhuāngsūn 劉莊孫 and Wáng Shēnzǐ 王申子, securely placing him in the early Yuán dynasty. His only surviving work is the *Chūnqiū tígāng* 春秋提綱 [[KR1e0058]], for which Hú Guāngshì 胡光世 of Yújiāng 旴江 (Jiāngxī) wrote a 1306 preface (the *dàbǐ zhī suì xià sìyuè bǐngxū* date in Hú's preface points to *Tàidìng* 3 (1326), the year of a *dàbǐ* national examination). CBDB person 108228 (Yuán) carries no further detail.
