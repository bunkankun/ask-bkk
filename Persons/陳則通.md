Chén Zé-tōng 陳則通 (lifedates not preserved), known by his style *Tiě-shān xiānshēng* 鐵山先生, native place not preserved. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that Zhū Yí-zūn's 朱彝尊 *Jīng-yì kǎo* 經義考 lists him between Liú Zhuāng-sūn 劉莊孫 and Wáng Shēn-zǐ 王申子, securely placing him in the early Yuán dynasty. His only surviving work is the *Chūnqiū tí-gāng* 春秋提綱 [[KR1e0058]], for which Hú Guāng-shì 胡光世 of Yú-jiāng 旴江 (Jiāngxī) wrote a 1306 preface (the *dà-bǐ zhī suì xià sì-yuè bǐng-xū* date in Hú's preface points to *Tài-dìng* 3 (1326), the year of a *dà-bǐ* national examination).
