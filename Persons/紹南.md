# 紹南

Shàonán 紹南 (*fl.* mid-12th century), Southern Sòng Chán monk, formerly resident at the Dàtóng Chán-yuàn 大同禪院 in Jiànzhōu 建州 (modern Jiànōu, Fújiàn). He composed the preface (*xù* 序) to the *Púsà míng jīng* 菩薩名經 ([[KR6i0029]]) compiled by the lay devotee [[羅濬]] Luó Jùn, attesting that Luó had read the entire Tripiṭaka five times in order to extract every bodhisattva name preserved therein.
