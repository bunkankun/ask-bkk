Zhèng Zōngyán 鄭宗顏 (Northern Sòng, dates unknown), a Wáng Ānshí 王安石 follower in the Reform school. According to Cháo Gōngwǔ's 晁公武 *Jùnzhāi dúshū zhì* 郡齋讀書志 (cited in the Sìkù *tíyào* of [[KR1d0004]] *Zhōuguān xīnyì*), Wáng Ānshí had not himself completed an annotation of the *Kǎogōngjì* 考工記 portion of the *Zhōulǐ*; Zhèng Zōngyán compiled the surviving two-juan *Kǎogōngjì jiě* 考工記解 by extracting Wáng's *Zì shuō* 字說 (graphological lexicon) glosses on the technical vocabulary and arranging them as commentary, in order to fill the lacuna. The work circulates as the appendix to *Zhōuguān xīnyì* in the Sìkù presentation.
