**Zhū Chén** 朱臣 (*zì* Shí-zuǒ 時佐), Míng-period official of Jǐ-nán 濟南 (Shān-dōng), *xiāng-jìn-shì* 鄉進士 (provincial graduate). Served as *Níng-guó tōng-shǒu* 寧國通守 (deputy prefect of Níng-guó, Ān-huī) in the late *Hóng-zhì* era. Rediscovered and brought into print the anonymous Sòng paediatric encyclopaedia *Xiǎo-ér wèi-shēng zǒng-wēi lùn-fāng* 小兒衛生總微論方 ([[KR3ej017]]) in 1489 with a preface by his Jǐ-nán fellow-townsman Lǐ Yánshòu 李延壽. Through misinterpretation of his role as publisher, later Míng bibliography (e.g. the *Fàn-shì shū-mù*) wrongly attributed the original Sòng work to Zhū Chén as author.
