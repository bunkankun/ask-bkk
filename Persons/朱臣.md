**Zhū Chén** 朱臣 (*zì* Shízuǒ 時佐), Míng-period official of Jǐnán 濟南 (Shāndōng), *xiāngjìnshì* 鄉進士 (provincial graduate). Served as *Níngguó tōngshǒu* 寧國通守 (deputy prefect of Níngguó, Ānhuī) in the late *Hóngzhì* era. Rediscovered and brought into print the anonymous Sòng paediatric encyclopaedia *Xiǎoér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* 小兒衛生總微論方 ([[KR3ej017]]) in 1489 with a preface by his Jǐnán fellow-townsman Lǐ Yánshòu 李延壽. Through misinterpretation of his role as publisher, later Míng bibliography (e.g. the *Fànshì shūmù*) wrongly attributed the original Sòng work to Zhū Chén as author.
