Wèi Liǎo-wēng 魏了翁 (1178–1237), *zì* Huá-fū 華夫 (also Huá-fù 華父), *hào* Hè-shān 鶴山, posthumous title *Wén-jìng* 文靖. Of Pú-jiāng 蒲江 in Lín-qióng 臨卭 (modern Pújiāng county, Sìchuān, Chéngdū metropolitan region). CBDB id 4001. *Sòngshǐ* biography in juan 437.

*Jìnshì* of *Qìng-yuán* 5 (1199). Career under Níng-zōng / Lǐ-zōng included high central-government posts: *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* 禮部尚書 (Minister of Rites), *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* 參知政事 (Vice Chief Councillor), *Qiān-shū Shū-mì-yuàn shì* 僉書樞密院事 (Joint Senior Officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs); final post *Zī-zhèng-diàn dà-xué-shì* 資政殿大學士.

Studied with Lǐ Fán 李燔 (1163–1232, *zì* Jìng-zǐ 敬子), one of Zhū Xī's principal late disciples. The Qiánlóng Imperial Topic-Poem prefixed to the Sìkù base of his *Zhōuyì yào yì* makes this pedagogical lineage prominent: "*Huá-fū studied under Master Jìng-zǐ; his learning transmits Zǐ-yáng [= Zhū Xī].*"

Demoted to Jìng-zhōu 靖州 (modern Jìng county, Hunan) in *Bǎo-qìng* 1 (1225) following the political crisis triggered by the death of Níng-zōng and the Shǐ Mí-yuǎn 史彌遠 chief-ministership's irregular elevation of Lǐ-zōng (the *hēi yī cān-zhèng* 黑衣參政 / "black-coat-attendance" affair, where Shǐ Mí-yuǎn allegedly arranged for the new emperor's attendance in mourning-attire to legitimize the deposition of Níng-zōng's adopted heir Zhào Hóng 趙竑). The Jìng-zhōu exile (1225 onward) was when the *Jiǔ-jīng yào yì* 九經要義 ("Essentials of the Nine Classics") project was substantively compiled. Eventually recalled to high office under Lǐ-zōng's later years.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the compiler of [[KR1a0054]] *Zhōuyì yào yì* 周易要義 (10 juan; the *Yì* portion of the *Jiǔ-jīng yào yì*). He also separately compiled the *Zhōuyì jí yì* 周易集義 (64 juan, Lián-Luò-and-after great-*ru*'s *Yì*-expositions, recovered by the Sìkù editors from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* — Zhū Yízūn conflated this with the *Yào yì*; the Sìkù editors corrected the conflation).

Methodologically Wèi Liǎo-wēng is one of the most balanced *Hàn-and-Sòng* synthesizers of the late Southern Sòng. The *Yào yì* + *Jí yì* compilation pair gives the late-Sòng student of the *Yì* both the substantive Hàn-Táng *zhù-shū* base (Kǒng Yǐngdá's *Zhèngyì* + Lù Démíng's *Shì wén* + Mǎ Róng / Zhèng Xuán / Wáng Sù glosses, pruned of apocryphal citations) and the substantive Sòng *yìlǐ* superstructure (the Lián-Luò-and-after great-*ru* line).

Substantial collected works: *Hè-shān xiānshēng dà-quán wén-jí* 鶴山先生大全文集 (110 juan, in the Sìkù).
