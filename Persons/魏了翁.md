Wèi Liǎowēng 魏了翁 (1178–1237), *zì* Huáfū 華夫 (also Huáfù 華父), *hào* Hèshān 鶴山, posthumous title *Wénjìng* 文靖. Of Pújiāng 蒲江 in Línqióng 臨卭 (modern Pújiāng county, Sìchuān, Chéngdū metropolitan region). CBDB id 4001. *Sòngshǐ* biography in juan 437.

*Jìnshì* of *Qìngyuán* 5 (1199). Career under Níngzōng / Lǐzōng included high central-government posts: *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書 (Minister of Rites), *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 (Vice Chief Councillor), *Qiānshū Shūmìyuàn shì* 僉書樞密院事 (Joint Senior Officer of the Bureau of Military Affairs); final post *Zīzhèngdiàn dàxuéshì* 資政殿大學士.

Studied with Lǐ Fán 李燔 (1163–1232, *zì* Jìngzǐ 敬子), one of Zhū Xī's principal late disciples. The Qiánlóng Imperial Topic-Poem prefixed to the Sìkù base of his *Zhōuyì yào yì* makes this pedagogical lineage prominent: "*Huáfū studied under Master Jìngzǐ; his learning transmits Zǐyáng [= Zhū Xī].*"

Demoted to Jìngzhōu 靖州 (modern Jìng county, Hunan) in *Bǎoqìng* 1 (1225) following the political crisis triggered by the death of Níngzōng and the Shǐ Míyuǎn 史彌遠 chief-ministership's irregular elevation of Lǐzōng (the *hēi yī cānzhèng* 黑衣參政 / "black-coat-attendance" affair, where Shǐ Míyuǎn allegedly arranged for the new emperor's attendance in mourning-attire to legitimize the deposition of Níngzōng's adopted heir Zhào Hóng 趙竑). The Jìngzhōu exile (1225 onward) was when the *Jiǔjīng yào yì* 九經要義 ("Essentials of the Nine Classics") project was substantively compiled. Eventually recalled to high office under Lǐzōng's later years.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the compiler of [[KR1a0054]] *Zhōuyì yào yì* 周易要義 (10 juan; the *Yì* portion of the *Jiǔjīng yào yì*). He also separately compiled the *Zhōuyì jí yì* 周易集義 (64 juan, Lián-Luò-and-after great-*ru*'s *Yì*-expositions, recovered by the Sìkù editors from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* — Zhū Yízūn conflated this with the *Yào yì*; the Sìkù editors corrected the conflation).

Methodologically Wèi Liǎowēng is one of the most balanced *Hàn-and-Sòng* synthesizers of the late Southern Sòng. The *Yào yì* + *Jí yì* compilation pair gives the late-Sòng student of the *Yì* both the substantive Hàn-Táng *zhùshū* base (Kǒng Yǐngdá's *Zhèngyì* + Lù Démíng's *Shì wén* + Mǎ Róng / Zhèng Xuán / Wáng Sù glosses, pruned of apocryphal citations) and the substantive Sòng *yìlǐ* superstructure (the Lián-Luò-and-after great-*ru* line).

Substantial collected works: *Hèshān xiānshēng dàquán wénjí* 鶴山先生大全文集 (110 juan, in the Sìkù).
