Wáng Zhì 王銍 (fl. 1122–1144), *zì* Xìngzhī 性之, *hào* Xuěxī 雪溪, native of Rǔyīn 汝陰 (modern Ānhuī, Fùyáng). Son of Wáng Píngzhī 王萍之 (a Northern-Sòng *yǐngzǐ* (yīn) descendant). Active in the late Northern Sòng / early Southern Sòng. Author of the *Mòjì* 默記, *Bù shìér xiǎomíng lù bǔyí* 補侍兒小名錄補遺, and *Xuěxī jí* 雪溪集 [[KR4d0193]].

The *Sìkù* editors of [[KR4d0193]] note that Wáng's poetic style is close to Wēn Tíngyún 溫庭筠 and Lǐ Shāngyǐn (晚唐 Late-Tang). Wáng Shìzhēn's 王士禎 *Jūyì lù* criticised Wáng Zhì's poetry as not very skillful but admired the appended *Lúshān sēng Kěhé héshī* one piece — the *Sìkù* editors disagree, considering this not a definitive judgment.

The collection in 5 *juǎn* (Sìkù version) is a fraction of the 8-*juǎn* recension recorded by Chén Zhènsūn [[KR3h0011]] and *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì*. Three identifiable losses: a *Wáng Wénrú Qúān shī*; a *Shāncūn shī*; and a *Yúnménsì shī* — all preserved in other Sòng *bǐjì* but absent from the 5-*juǎn* recension.

The *Sìkù* editors flag two of Wáng's philological errors: (a) his *Báitóu yín xù* incorrectly drew on Wú Jīng's 吳兢 *Yuèfǔ jiětí* rather than the original *Xījīng zájì* (which though false-attributed predates Wú Jīng and is the source of Wú's record); (b) Wáng claimed *Sòngzhì* records Wénjūn's poem, when in fact the *Sòngshū yuèzhì* records *Báitóu yín* as *gǔcí* (anonymous), not as Wénjūn's. These are taken as cautionary instances of philological *kǎozhèng zhī nán* (the difficulty of textual research).
