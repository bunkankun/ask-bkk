Wáng Zhì 王銍 (fl. 1122–1144), *zì* Xìng-zhī 性之, *hào* Xuě-xī 雪溪, native of Rǔ-yīn 汝陰 (modern Ān-huī, Fù-yáng). Son of Wáng Píng-zhī 王萍之 (a Northern-Sòng *yǐng-zǐ* (yīn) descendant). Active in the late Northern Sòng / early Southern Sòng. Author of the *Mò-jì* 默記, *Bù shì-ér xiǎo-míng lù bǔ-yí* 補侍兒小名錄補遺, and *Xuě-xī jí* 雪溪集 [[KR4d0193]].

The *Sì-kù* editors of [[KR4d0193]] note that Wáng's poetic style is close to Wēn Tíng-yún 溫庭筠 and Lǐ Shāng-yǐn (晚唐 Late-Tang). Wáng Shì-zhēn's 王士禎 *Jū-yì lù* criticised Wáng Zhì's poetry as not very skillful but admired the appended *Lú-shān sēng Kě-hé hé-shī* one piece — the *Sì-kù* editors disagree, considering this not a definitive judgment.

The collection in 5 *juǎn* (Sì-kù version) is a fraction of the 8-*juǎn* recension recorded by Chén Zhèn-sūn [[KR3h0011]] and *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén-zhì*. Three identifiable losses: a *Wáng Wén-rú Qú-ān shī*; a *Shān-cūn shī*; and a *Yún-mén-sì shī* — all preserved in other Sòng *bǐ-jì* but absent from the 5-*juǎn* recension.

The *Sì-kù* editors flag two of Wáng's philological errors: (a) his *Bái-tóu yín xù* incorrectly drew on Wú Jīng's 吳兢 *Yuè-fǔ jiě-tí* rather than the original *Xī-jīng zá-jì* (which though false-attributed predates Wú Jīng and is the source of Wú's record); (b) Wáng claimed *Sòng-zhì* records Wén-jūn's poem, when in fact the *Sòng-shū yuè-zhì* records *Bái-tóu yín* as *gǔ-cí* (anonymous), not as Wén-jūn's. These are taken as cautionary instances of philological *kǎo-zhèng zhī nán* (the difficulty of textual research).
