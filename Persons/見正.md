# 見正

Jiànzhèng ("Right View") — a bhikṣu interlocutor of the Buddha in the *Fó shuō jiànzhèng jīng* 佛說見正經 ([[KR6i0501]], T17n0796). The text takes its title from him: a newly ordained monk who silently doubts the doctrine of rebirth, prompting the Buddha's exposition on the transformation of consciousness across lives. He has no independent textual life outside this *sūtra* and is presumably an Indian figure preserved in the Chinese rendering of [[竺曇無蘭]] Zhú Tánwúlán.
