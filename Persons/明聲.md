Late-Míng Yún-mén-branch (*Yún-mén dì-zǐ* 雲門弟子) Chán monk; *hào* Cóng-xīn 叢心. Re-printed (*zhòng-kè* 重刻) the Míng edition of the *Zhào-zhōu héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0391]]), collaborating with the lay-editor **Dà-cān** [[大參]]. Preface of the reprint notes that Míng-shēng self-identified as a descendant of **Yún-mén Wén-yǎn** 雲門文偃 ([[文偃]]) — the great Táng-Sòng master whose own *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0392]]) stood as a model for this Zhào-zhōu reprint. No further biographical data located.
