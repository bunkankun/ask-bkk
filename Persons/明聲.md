Late-Míng Yún-mén-branch (*Yúnmén dìzǐ* 雲門弟子) Chán monk; *hào* Cóngxīn 叢心. Re-printed (*zhòngkè* 重刻) the Míng edition of the *Zhàozhōu héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0391]]), collaborating with the lay-editor **Dàcān** [[大參]]. Preface of the reprint notes that Míngshēng self-identified as a descendant of **Yúnmén Wényǎn** 雲門文偃 ([[文偃]]) — the great TángSòng master whose own *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0392]]) stood as a model for this Zhàozhōu reprint. No further biographical data located.
