# Huì-hào 慧浩

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *sì-fǎ cān-xué* 嗣法參學 of [[無慍]] Shù-zhōng Wú-yùn (1309–1386); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0349]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
