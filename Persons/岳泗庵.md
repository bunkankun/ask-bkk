**Yuè Sìān** 岳泗庵 (also written **Yuè Chāngyuán** 岳昌源) — early-Qīng editor credited with the redaction of *Tāichǎn zhèngzhì* ([[KR3ei064]]) attributed to [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng (1549–1613). Likely a Qīng-era abridger and reorganiser of Wáng's gynecological materials whose work was preserved in manuscript and brought to print only in 1929–1930 via [[陳珠泉]] Chén Zhūquán. Not in CBDB.
