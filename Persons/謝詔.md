**Xiè Zhào** 謝詔 (1512–1567; probable CBDB id 133188) is a mid-Míng author attributed with the historical novel [[KR4k0045]] *Dōnghàn Mìshǐ* 東漢秘史. Little biographical detail is available. CBDB records several persons of this name; id 133188 (1512–1567) and id 206056 (fl. 1543) are the most likely candidates for the novelist, given the mid-16th-century dating of the *Dōnghàn Mìshǐ*. Confirmation of the attribution is not currently possible.
