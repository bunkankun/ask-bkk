Hé Mèng 和㠓 (956–1000), son of Hé Níng 和凝 (898–955). Co-author with his father of the *Yí yù jí* 疑獄集 (KR3c0006, the early-Sòng / Five-Dynasties judicial-decision casebook). CBDB id 17669.
