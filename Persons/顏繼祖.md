Yán Jìzǔ 顏繼祖 (d. 1639), *zì* **Shéngqí** 繩其, *hào* **Tónglán** 同蘭, studio-style **Yòuhóngtáng** 又紅堂; native of **Lóngxī** 龍溪 (Zhāngzhōu prefecture, Fújiàn; modern Lónghǎi). *Tóngjìnshì* of Wànlì 47 / *jǐwèi* (1619). Served as *xíngrén*, *Gōngkē jǐshìzhōng* (1628), and successively *Yòu jǐshìzhōng* (1629), *Hùkē yòu jǐshìzhōng* (1630), *Zuǒ jǐshìzhōng* (1631), *Lìkē dūjǐshìzhōng* (1631), and *Tàichángsì shàoqīng* (1632). In *Chóngzhēn* 9 / 1636 appointed *Yòu fù dū yùshǐ* and *Shāndōng xúnfǔ* (Grand Coordinator of Shāndōng). His preface to **[[施邦曜]]**'s *Yángmíng xiānshēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) is preserved among the SBCK prefaces. In the winter of *Chóngzhēn* 11 / 1638 the Qīng army broke through Jūyōngguān and pressed south through Shāndōng; under orders from Yáng Sìchāng 楊嗣昌 to hold Dézhōu, Yán left Jǐnán undefended, the Qīng army stormed it, the Prince of Dé (Zhū Yóushū) was captured, and several provincial officials died on duty. Yán impeached Yáng Sìchāng and requested his own dismissal, but the *Chóngzhēn* emperor instead imprisoned him and executed him in the marketplace in *Chóngzhēn* 12 / 1639. CBDB 63703.
