Yán Jì-zǔ 顏繼祖 (d. 1639), *zì* **Shéng-qí** 繩其, *hào* **Tóng-lán** 同蘭, studio-style **Yòu-hóng-táng** 又紅堂; native of **Lóng-xī** 龍溪 (Zhāng-zhōu prefecture, Fú-jiàn; modern Lóng-hǎi). *Tóng-jìnshì* of Wàn-lì 47 / *jǐ-wèi* (1619). Served as *xíng-rén*, *Gōng-kē jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1628), and successively *Yòu jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1629), *Hù-kē yòu jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1630), *Zuǒ jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1631), *Lì-kē dū-jǐ-shì-zhōng* (1631), and *Tài-cháng-sì shào-qīng* (1632). In *Chóng-zhēn* 9 / 1636 appointed *Yòu fù dū yù-shǐ* and *Shān-dōng xún-fǔ* (Grand Coordinator of Shān-dōng). His preface to **[[施邦曜]]**'s *Yáng-míng xiān-shēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) is preserved among the SBCK prefaces. In the winter of *Chóng-zhēn* 11 / 1638 the Qīng army broke through Jū-yōng-guān and pressed south through Shān-dōng; under orders from Yáng Sì-chāng 楊嗣昌 to hold Dé-zhōu, Yán left Jǐ-nán undefended, the Qīng army stormed it, the Prince of Dé (Zhū Yóu-shū) was captured, and several provincial officials died on duty. Yán impeached Yáng Sì-chāng and requested his own dismissal, but the *Chóng-zhēn* emperor instead imprisoned him and executed him in the marketplace in *Chóng-zhēn* 12 / 1639. CBDB 63703.
