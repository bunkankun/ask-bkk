Jìyuán 寂源, *zì* **Qiányù** 乾御 ("Heavenly Drive"); referred to as **Qiányù Jìyuán** 乾御寂源 (variant 乾御宏源). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Chìsōng Dàolǐng** [[道領]] (b. 1634) at **Qiánlíngshān Hóngfúsì** 黔靈山弘福寺 (Guìyáng). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Sìchuānshěng** 四川省. Lead recorder (*lù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0565]] 《赤松領禪師語錄》 5 juan, J39 B451, prefaced 1691.
