**Zhū Duānzhāng** 朱端章 (also written **朱瑞章** Zhū Ruìzhāng in the SKQS / hxwd recension orthography), Southern-Sòng official-physician of Chánglè 長樂 (Fújiàn). In the *Chúnxī* era 淳熙 (1174–1189) served as *zhī Jiāngxī Nánkāngjūn* 知江西南康軍 (administrator of Nánkāng commandery in Jiāngxī), a position attested in the *Sòng shǐ — yìwén zhì* and the *Qiánlóng Jiāngxī tōngzhì*. Renowned for his *kuān* (lenient) administration and *lián* (incorrupt) personal conduct; established public *guāntián* (official-fields) at Báilù dòng 白鹿洞 to subsidise students. A *liáng lì* "good local-official with the heart for relieving the people" rather than a professional physician proper.

His medical writings, all of compilatory character, are recorded in the *Sòng shǐ — yìwén zhì*:
- *Wèishēng jiābǎo fāng* 衛生家寶方 (6 *juǎn*)
- *Wèishēng jiābǎo xiǎoér fāng* 衛生家寶小兒方 (1 *juǎn*)
- *Wèishēng jiābǎo tāng fāng* 衛生家寶湯方 (2 *juǎn*)
- ***Wèishēng jiābǎo chǎnkē bèiyào*** 衛生家寶產科備要 ([[KR3ei056]], 8 *juǎn*)

The *chǎnkē bèiyào* is the only one of his medical works to survive in the Sòng-print transmission, rediscovered in Sòng-edition by the Qīng bibliophile Lù Xīnyuán 陸心源 of Guīān 歸安 (the *Wànlíguǎn* 穰梨館 collector); Lù re-cut it in 1887 (Guāngxù 13). It is uniquely valuable as the principal surviving witness to the lost Sòng obstetrical compilations *Chǎnyù bǎoqìng jí* 產育寶慶集 (Lǐ Shīshèng) and *Bèichǎn jìyòng fāng* 備產濟用方 (Yú Liú).
