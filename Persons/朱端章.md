**Zhū Duān-zhāng** 朱端章 (also written **朱瑞章** Zhū Ruì-zhāng in the SKQS / hxwd recension orthography), Southern-Sòng official-physician of Cháng-lè 長樂 (Fú-jiàn). In the *Chún-xī* era 淳熙 (1174–1189) served as *zhī Jiāng-xī Nán-kāng-jūn* 知江西南康軍 (administrator of Nán-kāng commandery in Jiāng-xī), a position attested in the *Sòng shǐ — yì-wén zhì* and the *Qián-lóng Jiāng-xī tōng-zhì*. Renowned for his *kuān* (lenient) administration and *lián* (incorrupt) personal conduct; established public *guān-tián* (official-fields) at Bái-lù dòng 白鹿洞 to subsidise students. A *liáng lì* "good local-official with the heart for relieving the people" rather than a professional physician proper.

His medical writings, all of compilatory character, are recorded in the *Sòng shǐ — yì-wén zhì*:
- *Wèi-shēng jiā-bǎo fāng* 衛生家寶方 (6 *juǎn*)
- *Wèi-shēng jiā-bǎo xiǎo-ér fāng* 衛生家寶小兒方 (1 *juǎn*)
- *Wèi-shēng jiā-bǎo tāng fāng* 衛生家寶湯方 (2 *juǎn*)
- ***Wèi-shēng jiā-bǎo chǎn-kē bèi-yào*** 衛生家寶產科備要 ([[KR3ei056]], 8 *juǎn*)

The *chǎn-kē bèi-yào* is the only one of his medical works to survive in the Sòng-print transmission, rediscovered in Sòng-edition by the Qīng bibliophile Lù Xīn-yuán 陸心源 of Guī-ān 歸安 (the *Wàn-lí-guǎn* 穰梨館 collector); Lù re-cut it in 1887 (Guāng-xù 13). It is uniquely valuable as the principal surviving witness to the lost Sòng obstetrical compilations *Chǎn-yù bǎo-qìng jí* 產育寶慶集 (Lǐ Shī-shèng) and *Bèi-chǎn jì-yòng fāng* 備產濟用方 (Yú Liú).
