# 虛空藏
Xūkōngzàng (Sanskrit: Ākāśagarbha)

Mahāyāna bodhisattva of the "Sky-Treasury" (空 ākāśa + 藏 garbha), patron of *prajñā* (wisdom), vow, and inexhaustible boons. The principal scriptural witnesses in the Chinese canon are gathered in the KR6h Mahāsaṃnipāta cluster ([[KR6h0008]]–[[KR6h0013]]).
