Sòng-Yuán transitional scholar, *zì* Zhí-wēng 直翁, of Shào-wǔ 邵武 (Fú-jiàn). Sòng *jìn-shì* (Xián-chún era, c. 1265); a Sòng *yí-mín* who lived on into the early Yuán. Compiler of the *Gǔ-jīn yùn-huì* 古今韻會, an Yuán-period rhyme-book completing and re-organising the work of Liú Yuān 劉淵 (whose 1252 *Rén-zǐ xīn-kān Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* had reduced the 206 rhymes to 107) and Hán Dào-zhāo's [[韓道昭]] *Wǔ-yīn jí-yùn*. The *Gǔ-jīn yùn-huì* itself was not printed until subsequently consolidated into the *Gǔ-jīn yùn-huì jǔ-yào* 古今韻會舉要 [[KR1j0065]] by his student-collaborator Xióng Zhōng [[熊忠]] of Zhāo-wǔ. Liú Chén-wēng's preface (composed 1297) introduces the abridged *jǔ-yào* edition. Huáng's Lǐ Pèi *yú-lùn* polemic against Jiāng-zuǒ Wú dialect — preserved in Xióng Zhōng's *zì-mǔ tōng-kǎo* — is the seed of the early-Míng *Hóng-wǔ zhèng-yùn*'s [[KR1j0068]] anti-Wú dialect program.
