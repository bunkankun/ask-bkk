SòngYuán transitional scholar, *zì* Zhíwēng 直翁, of Shàowǔ 邵武 (Fújiàn). Sòng *jìnshì* (Xiánchún era, c. 1265); a Sòng *yímín* who lived on into the early Yuán. Compiler of the *Gǔjīn yùnhuì* 古今韻會, an Yuán-period rhyme-book completing and re-organising the work of Liú Yuān 劉淵 (whose 1252 *Rénzǐ xīnkān Lǐbù yùnlüè* had reduced the 206 rhymes to 107) and Hán Dàozhāo's [[韓道昭]] *Wǔyīn jíyùn*. The *Gǔjīn yùnhuì* itself was not printed until subsequently consolidated into the *Gǔjīn yùnhuì jǔyào* 古今韻會舉要 [[KR1j0065]] by his student-collaborator Xióng Zhōng [[熊忠]] of Zhāowǔ. Liú Chénwēng's preface (composed 1297) introduces the abridged *jǔyào* edition. Huáng's Lǐ Pèi *yúlùn* polemic against Jiāngzuǒ Wú dialect — preserved in Xióng Zhōng's *zìmǔ tōngkǎo* — is the seed of the early-Míng *Hóngwǔ zhèngyùn*'s [[KR1j0068]] anti-Wú dialect program.
