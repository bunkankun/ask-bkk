**Ōu yáng Wén** 歐陽雯 was a Sòng Daoist, transmitter of [[KR5g0221]] *Tài shàng Yuán shǐ tiān zūn shuō Běi dì fú mó shén zhòu miào jīng* — a Northern-Thearch demon-subduing scripture.
