# 菅原是善

Sugawara no Koreyoshi (812–880), Heian-period Japanese scholar-bureaucrat and Bunshō hakase 文章博士 (Professor of Letters). Father of the more celebrated Sugawara no Michizane. Wrote the preface to [[願曉]] Gangyō's *Konkōmyō saishōōkyō genzū* [[KR6i0318]], the principal Japanese Sanron commentary on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa-sūtra*.
