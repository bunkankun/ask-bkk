**Yīn Chángshēng** 陰長生 was a legendary Eastern-Hàn alchemical *xiān* 仙 (immortal), traditionally the disciple of **Mǎ Míngshēng** 馬鳴生 from whom he received the Tài qīng 太清 alchemical scriptures. His hagiography appears in Gě Hóng's *Shén xiān zhuàn* 神仙傳 (biographies of the immortals), and he is the nominal author of the second *juàn* of [[KR5c0278]] *Tài qīng jīn yè shén dān jīng*. The attribution is pseudepigraphic — a standard Six-Dynasties legitimizing device.
