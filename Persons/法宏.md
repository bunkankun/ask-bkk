# 法宏

Southern-Sòng Chán dharma-heir disciple (*cān xué* 參學) of Dàhuì [[宗杲|Zōnggǎo]] 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163). Co-editor of his master's compact *yǔlù* [[KR6q0296]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
