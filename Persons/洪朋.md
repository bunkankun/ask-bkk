**Hóng Péng** 洪朋 (fl. *Yuányòu*–*Chóngníng*, c. 1090s; died at age 38), *zì* Guīfù 龜父, of Nánchāng 南昌 (Jiāngxī). Eldest of the *Sì Hóng* 四洪 brothers (Hóng Péng, Hóng Chú 洪芻 [[KR4d0142]], Hóng Yán 洪炎 [[KR4d0141]], Hóng Yǔ 洪羽). All four were nephews of Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]] (Huáng's sister married into the Hóng family). Twice tried the *jìnshì* exam without success; died at 38, never having held office. Listed in Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中's *Jiāngxī shīshè zōngpài tú* 江西詩社宗派圖 — one of the four Hóng brothers in the school. Huáng Tíngjiān praised him: "Guīfù's *bǐlì kāngdǐng* (brush-power lifts-the-tripod); other days, no worry of lacking *wénzhāng chuíshì* (writing to-pass-down-to-the-age)." Lǚ Běnzhōng ranked the *Jiāngxī shīpài* with Chén Shīdào first, Pān Dàlín second, Xiè Yì third, Hóng Péng fourth — among Huáng's nephews, ranked highest. Author of *Hóng Guīfù jí* 洪龜父集 [[KR4d0131]] — originally 100 *piān* compiled by Huáng Jūn 黃君著 of the same county after Hóng Péng's death; *Sìkù* reconstruction is 2 *juǎn* with 178 poems.
