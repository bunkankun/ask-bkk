Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Shénzhào** 神照 (also referenced as **Shénzhào Běnrú** 神照本如, **Shénchào** 神炤, **Rúhǔ** 如虎). DILA Authority A000338. Born 982; died 4 July 1051, aged 70.

Principal disciple of **Sìmíng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]], 960–1028) at the Yánqìngsì 延慶寺 in Sìmíng. Subsequently teacher of **Yǒuyán** 有嚴 ([[有嚴]], 1021–1101). Through this lineage Shénzhào Běnrú is one of the principal second-generation transmitters of the Sòng *shānjiā* 山家 Tiāntái tradition founded by Zhīlǐ.

Major work: the **Pǔxián guān jīng yìshū** 普賢觀經義疏 ([[KR6d0123]], X35n0650, 2 *juan*) — a substantial Sòng-period subcommentary on the *Pǔxián guān jīng* 普賢觀經 (T277, the standard Tiāntái epilogue text to the Lotus Sūtra). The work was subsequently extended (*xùjiě* 續解) by **Chǔxián** 處咸 and divided-and-collated (*fēnhuì* 分會) by the Japanese Tendai monk **Liàngrùn** 亮潤.

Note: distinct from the Tang-period **Běnrú** 本如 of Silla (DILA A009579), a southern-Chán disciple of Nányuè Huáiràng 南嶽懷讓.
