Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Shén-zhào** 神照 (also referenced as **Shén-zhào Běn-rú** 神照本如, **Shén-chào** 神炤, **Rú-hǔ** 如虎). DILA Authority A000338. Born 982; died 4 July 1051, aged 70.

Principal disciple of **Sì-míng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]], 960–1028) at the Yán-qìng-sì 延慶寺 in Sì-míng. Subsequently teacher of **Yǒu-yán** 有嚴 ([[有嚴]], 1021–1101). Through this lineage Shén-zhào Běnrú is one of the principal second-generation transmitters of the Sòng *shān-jiā* 山家 Tiāntái tradition founded by Zhīlǐ.

Major work: the **Pǔ-xián guān jīng yì-shū** 普賢觀經義疏 ([[KR6d0123]], X35n0650, 2 *juan*) — a substantial Sòng-period subcommentary on the *Pǔ-xián guān jīng* 普賢觀經 (T277, the standard Tiāntái epilogue text to the Lotus Sūtra). The work was subsequently extended (*xù-jiě* 續解) by **Chǔ-xián** 處咸 and divided-and-collated (*fēn-huì* 分會) by the Japanese Tendai monk **Liàng-rùn** 亮潤.

Note: distinct from the Tang-period **Běnrú** 本如 of Silla (DILA A009579), a southern-Chán disciple of Nán-yuè Huái-ràng 南嶽懷讓.
