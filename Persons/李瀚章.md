# 李瀚章 Lǐ Hànzhāng

Late-Qīng official (1821–1899), elder brother of [[李鴻章]] Lǐ Hóngzhāng; governor-general of Húguǎng 湖廣 during the post-Tàipíng reconstruction. Wrote the 1870 preface to [[包誠]] Bāo Chéng's *Shānghán shěn zhèng biǎo* ([[KR3ef065]]) and funded its block-printing as a measure of post-war medical relief.
