Xíng-wù 行悟. **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Bǎi-shān Dé-kǎi** [[德楷]] (1629–1702) at **Lóng-shān Huá-yán chán-yuàn** 龍山華嚴禪院 (Shān-xī). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān-cì*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0579]] 《山西柏山楷禪師語錄》 5 juan, J39 B466.
