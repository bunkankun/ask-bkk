Xíngwù 行悟. **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Bǎishān Dékǎi** [[德楷]] (1629–1702) at **Lóngshān Huáyán chányuàn** 龍山華嚴禪院 (Shānxī). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biāncì*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0579]] 《山西柏山楷禪師語錄》 5 juan, J39 B466.
