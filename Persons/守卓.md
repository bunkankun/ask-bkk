# Chánglíng Shǒuzhuó 長靈守卓

Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional Línjì-Yángqí Chán master (1065–1123). Dharma-heir of [[克文|Bǎofēng Kèwén]] 寶峰克文 (= Yúnān Zhēnjìng Kèwén 雲庵真淨克文, 1025–1102). Active at Chánglíng 長靈.
