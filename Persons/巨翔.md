Jù-xiáng 巨翔, *zì* **Tiān-yì** 天翼 ("Heavenly Wing"); often referred to as **Tiān-yì Jù-xiáng** 天翼巨翔. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wéi-shān Gǔ-méi Dìng-liè** [[定冽]] (cf. [[KR6q0576]]). Born **康熙壬寅十二月初五辰時** = **1663-01-13** Gregorian; died **康熙乙亥五月初九** = **1695-06-20**, age 34, *sēng-là* 14 (an exceptionally short life). Native of **Shǎn-xī Líng-líng** 陵霄 (interior Shǎn-xī); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Formation.** Sent by parents to **Líng-shān-sì** 靈山寺 under **Jìng-yuè** 淨月 for tonsure. At 9 began studying the *Śūraṅgama jīng*; at the line "*qīng-jìng běn-rán, yún hé hū shēng shān-hé dà-dì*" 清淨本然云何忽生山河大地 had insight. At 19 visited **Xī-shān Téng-huàn** 西山藤煥 for full ordination. At 24 sought out **Fú-róng-shān Gǔ-méi Dìng-liè** 芙蓉山古梅定冽. **康熙二十八年** = **1689** Dìng-liè ordered him into *bì-guān* at **Zǐ-zhú-lín** 紫竹林 — there reached great awakening. Received final *fù-zhǔ* on Dìng-liè's deathbed.

**Abbacies.** **康熙三十年** = **1691** entered **Tán-zhōu Dà-Wéi-shān Mì-yìn-sì** 潭州大溈山密印寺 (the historic Wéi-yǎng home-temple) and **Fú-róng-shān Guǎng-huà-sì** 芙蓉山廣化寺 — the same two seats his teacher had held.

**Disciples.** **Cáng-ān Fēng** 藏庵鋒 = **Jì-fēng** [[際鋒]] (lead compiler).

**Work.** [[KR6q0589]] 《天翼翔禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B477, recorded by attendant **Jì-qián** [[寂乾]] *lù*; compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Jì-fēng** [[際鋒]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《天翼翔禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈行實〉; 《正源略集》 vol. 16.
