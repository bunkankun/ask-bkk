**Wáng Zhízhōng** 王執中 (*zì* Shūquán 叔權, *fl.* Jiā-dìng gēng-chén = 1220, 南宋), Southern-Sòng physician of Dōng-jiā 東嘉 (modern Wēn-zhōu, Zhèjiāng); served as Erudite (博士) at the Lǐ-yáng Commandery (澧陽郡) academy. Author of the ***Zhēnjiǔ zī-shēng jīng*** 鍼灸資生經 ([[KR3e0037]]) — a Southern-Sòng acupuncture-and-moxibustion treatise in 7 juan, with juan 1 a comprehensive point-and-channel reference and juan 2–7 organized by symptom-pattern. The work was first printed by Xú Zhèngqīng 徐正卿 in Jiā-dìng gēng-chén (1220) and reprinted by Zhào Lún 趙綸 in Shào-dìng 4 (1231); the SKQS recension is from the Yè-shì Guǎng-qín-táng 葉氏廣勤堂 print (a Má-shā 麻沙 commercial print). Wáng has no further transmitted biographical record.
