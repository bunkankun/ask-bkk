Lù Xīxióng 陸錫熊 (1734–1792), zì Jiànnán 健男, hào Ěrshān 耳山, was a Shànghǎi 上海 scholar-official who served alongside Jǐ Yún 紀昀 as a chief editor (*zǒngzuǎn guān* 總纂官) of the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書. He took the *jìnshì* in 1761 and rose to vice-minister of the Board of Punishments (Xíngbù shìláng 刑部侍郎). He died on his way north to oversee the recopying of the four imperial sets after the 1790 fire damaged some volumes. He co-signed the great majority of WYG *tíyào* with Jǐ Yún and Sūn Shìyì 孫士毅.
