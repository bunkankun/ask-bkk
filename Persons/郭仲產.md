Guō Zhòngchǎn 郭仲產 (*fl.* Liu Song dynasty, 5th century CE) was a geographic writer, author of both a *Qínzhōu jì* 秦州記 ([[KR2k0169]]) and a *Xiāngzhōu jì* 湘州記 ([[KR2k0199]]). He appears to have served or traveled in both the upper Wei River region (Qinzhou, modern Gansu) and the Hunan region (Xiangzhou). His works are cited in the major Tang-Song encyclopedias. No further biographical details are attested.
