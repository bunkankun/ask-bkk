# Ànwēng 按翁

Late-Míng Chán master, master of [[蘊上|Lóngguāng Dáfū Yùnshàng]] 龍光達夫蘊上 (active late Míng to early Qīng). Born in the West-Chǔ 西楚 region. Lost his mother in childhood; vowed chastity and entered monastic life in the Chóngzhēn era (1628–1644). Obtained awakening at Xīfēng 西峰 in the "Lion's Roar Chamber" (*Shī hǒu zhī shì* 師吼之室). His three-session dharma-language was printed after his cremation by disciples. Lifedates unrecorded.

Sources: tomb-inscription and elegy in *Èzhōu Lóngguāng Dáfū chánshī jī lèi jí* [[KR6q0206]].
