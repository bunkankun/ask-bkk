# 秦承祖 Qín Chéngzǔ

**Qín Chéngzǔ** 秦承祖, Liú-Sòng physician and acupuncturist; reputed author of a *Míngtáng* 明堂 acupoint manual (now lost), cited in later acupuncture sources. Served at the early Liú-Sòng imperial medical establishment.
