Southern-Sòng (Xiánchún-era) Chán monk, training disciple (*cān-xué* 參學) of the Línjì master Yún-gǔ 雲谷 [[KR6q0384]]. Editor of juan 1 (the Shèng-shòu chán-sì abbacy record, dated Bǎoyòu 4 = 1256) of Yún-gǔ's *yǔlù*. No further biographical information survives.
