Southern-Sòng (Xiánchún-era) Chán monk, training disciple (*cānxué* 參學) of the Línjì master Yúngǔ 雲谷 [[KR6q0384]]. Editor of juan 1 (the Shèngshòu chánsì abbacy record, dated Bǎoyòu 4 = 1256) of Yúngǔ's *yǔlù*. No further biographical information survives.
