# Aì Xūtáng 艾頊堂

Mid-Qīng Chóngqìng-resident scholar; one of three friends (with [[傅怡如]] Fù Yírú and [[邱東山]] Qiū Dōngshān) who patronised the 1847 printing of [[王錫鑫]] Wáng Xīxīn's *Yòukē qièyào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
