**Wāng Hù** 汪琥 (*zì* Yìnxí 苓友, *hào* Yìnxí Lǎorén 苓友老人, *fl.* late Kāngxī, ca. 1680s, 清), Sūzhōu-region physician. He produced two complementary *Shānghán* commentaries:

- *Shānghán lùn biànzhèng guǎng zhù* 傷寒論辯證廣註 ([[KR3ef045]], 14 juan, 1680).
- *Zhōnghán lùn biànzhèng guǎng zhù* 中寒論辯證廣註 ([[KR3ef046]]) — on the parallel "central cold-damage" tradition.

Wāng works in the moderate-revisionist *Shānghán* line, accepting Fāng Yǒuzhí / Yú Chāng's *Chángshā fùgǔ* program but retaining selected Chéng Wúyǐ glosses. His exact lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
