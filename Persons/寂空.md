Jìkōng 寂空. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Hānpú Xìngcōng** [[性聰]] (1611–1667). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with co-disciple **Pǔyuè Fāngxǐng** [[方醒]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0609]] 《明覺聰禪師語錄》 20 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1652. Named in juan 20 of the *yǔlù* as *fùfǎ dìzǐ* 付法弟子.
