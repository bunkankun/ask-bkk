Jì-kōng 寂空. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Hān-pú Xìng-cōng** [[性聰]] (1611–1667). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with co-disciple **Pǔ-yuè Fāng-xǐng** [[方醒]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0609]] 《明覺聰禪師語錄》 20 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1652. Named in juan 20 of the *yǔ-lù* as *fù-fǎ dì-zǐ* 付法弟子.
