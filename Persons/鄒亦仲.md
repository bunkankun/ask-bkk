Zōu Yìzhòng 鄒亦仲 — late-Qīng to early-Republican physician, clearly active into the Republican era. The preface to his [[KR3ep041]] *Zōu Yìzhòng yīàn xīnbiān* 鄒亦仲醫案新編 invokes Sun Yat-sen's *Mínshēng zhǔyì* 民生主義 doctrine, anchoring the editorial project to after 1924. Biographical data are otherwise thin; CBDB has no entry.
