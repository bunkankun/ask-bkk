Shāng Qǐwēng, *zì* Jìbó 繼伯, was a native of Cáozhōu 曹州 (modern Hézé 菏澤, Shāndōng) under the late Yuán. He served as Editorial Director (Zhùzuò zuǒláng 著作佐郎) of the Imperial Library Directorate at Dàdū. Together with [[王士點]] he compiled the *Mìshū jiàn zhì* 祕書監志 ([[KR2l0008]]), the principal Yuán-era administrative monograph on the *Mìshū jiàn*, in or around the late Zhìzhèng era (1341–1352). His CBDB record (id 106350) gives no firm lifedates. The work survives via the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* and the Sìkù transmission line.
