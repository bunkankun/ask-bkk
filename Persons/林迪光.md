Lín Díguāng 林迪光, Qiánlóng-period pupil of [[沈大成]] Shěn Dàchéng (1700–1771) of Fútáng. Wrote one of the two posthumous postscripts to [[潘思榘]] Pān Sījǔ's *Zhōuyì qiǎn shì* ([[KR1a0152]]); his postscript preserves Pān's "*shí lái*" methodological pronouncement.
