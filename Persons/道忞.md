# Dàomín 道忞 (Mùchén Dàomín 木陳道忞)

Pivotal early-Qīng Línjì-lineage Chán master and spiritual advisor to the Shùnzhì emperor. *Hào* Shānwēng 山翁 ("Mountain Old-Man"), Mùchén 木陳 ("Wood-Chén"), Mèngyǐn dàorén 夢隱道人 ("Dream-Hidden Daoist"); *shì* Hóngjué 弘覺 / 宏覺 ("Vast Awakening"). Posthumous imperial title *Hóngjué chánshī* 弘覺禪師. Lay surname Lín 林. Native of Cháoyáng 潮陽 (Guǎngdōng). Lifedates 1596–1674.

Dharma-heir of [[圓悟|Mìyún Yuánwù]] 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642); successor to Yuánwù as abbot of **Tiāntóngsì** 天童寺 in Míngzhōu 明州 (Nìngbō, Zhèjiāng). One of the two or three most important Yuán-wù-lineage Chán masters of his generation.

**Imperial connection.** Invited by the Shùnzhì 順治 emperor in Shùnzhì 16 (1659) to travel to Beijing and teach at the Wànshàndiàn 萬善殿 within the Forbidden City; his sermons and conversations with Shùnzhì are preserved in the *Tiāntóng Hóngjué Mín chánshī běi yóu jí* 天童弘覺忞禪師北遊集 [[KR6q0199]]. The imperial relationship made Dàomín one of the most politically-prominent Buddhist monks of the early Qīng, alongside the imperially-recognised Línjì master Yùlín Tōngxiù 玉林通琇.

Dharma-heirs included [[真樸|Zhēnpǔ]] 真樸 and a substantial lineage of early-to-mid-Qīng Línjì masters. Collected sermons preserved as the *Mùchén héshàng yǔlù* 木陳和尚語錄 (J26 B182). Also compiled the **《禪燈世譜》** *Chándēng shìpǔ* ([[KR6r0110]], X1601, 9 juan), a tabular LínjìCáodòng lineage-genealogy with sub-lines, prefaced 1632 by his lay-disciple **Huáng Duānbó** 黃端伯.

Sources: *Tiāntóng Hóngjué Mín chánshī běi yóu jí* juan 1; *Mùchén héshàng yǔlù*; Jiang Wu (2008) ch. 7.
