# Língyǐn Gāoyuán Zǔquán 靈隱高原祖泉

Southern Sòng Chán monk based at Língyǐn 靈隱; supplied the winter 1228 postface to [[KR6q0071]] *Rújìng héshàng yǔlù* and collated the 1229 Guǎngzōng printing.
