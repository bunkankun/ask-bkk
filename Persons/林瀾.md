# 林瀾 Lín Lán

Early-Qīng physician (*zì* Guānzǐ 觀子, *fl.* late 17th c.), author of the 20-juan *Shānghán zhé zhōng* 傷寒折衷 (printed 1680), one of the principal sources reorganized in [[強健]] Qiáng Jiàn's *Shānghán zhí zhǐ* ([[KR3ef075]]).
