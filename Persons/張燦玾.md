# 張燦玾 Zhāng Cànjiǎ

**Zhāng Cànjiǎ** 張燦玾 (1928–2017), modern Chinese physician and classical medical-text scholar at Shāndōng University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (山東中醫藥大學); one of the first thirty PRC "Masters of Chinese Medicine" (國醫大師). Editor of *Zhēnjiǔ jiǎyǐ jīng jiàoshì* 針灸甲乙經校釋 (1996), the standard modern critical edition of the *Zhēnjiǔ jiǎyǐ jīng* [[KR3ee005]].
