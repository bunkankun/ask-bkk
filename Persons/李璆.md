**Lǐ Qiú** 李璆, *zì* **Yìngxián** 應賢 (*fl.* mid-late 13th c.). Late-Southern-Sòng official posted to Lǐngnán 嶺南. Co-author with [[張致遠]] of the *Lǐngnán wèishēng fāng* 嶺南衛生方 ([[KR3ed031]]), the earliest extant Chinese tropical-medicine specialty treatise. His historical record outside this work is sparse; not in CBDB. The work's preservation in Japan and re-edition by Edo-period Japanese physicians is the principal reason that even Lǐ Qiú's *zì* "Yìng-xián" is documented. Sòng prefectural records identify a Lǐ Qiú as senior official at Guǎng-zhōu prefecture in the *Xián-chún* era (1265–1274), consistent with the *Lǐngnán wèishēng fāng* attribution.
