Wáng Jùn 王雋 (Sòng) was a Sòng disciple of Chén Chún 陳淳, native of Qīng-yuán 清源 (Quán-zhōu region, Fú-jiàn). He recorded the *Běixī zì yì* 北溪字義 (KR3a0065), Chén Chún's terminological lexicon, into its 26-*mén* form. CBDB id 37813, no dates. He has no separate biographical record.
