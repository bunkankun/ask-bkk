Wáng Jùn 王雋 (Sòng) was a Sòng disciple of Chén Chún 陳淳, native of Qīngyuán 清源 (Quánzhōu region, Fújiàn). He recorded the *Běixī zì yì* 北溪字義 ([[KR3a0065]]), Chén Chún's terminological lexicon, into its 26-*mén* form. CBDB id 37813, no dates. He has no separate biographical record.
