**Zhāng Tíngzuǒ** 張廷佐 (*zì* Mǐnchén 敏臣) of Yānshān 燕山, late-Qīng editor; in 1897 (*Guāngxù dīngyǒu*) reprinted and revised the *Nǚkē xiānfāng* 女科仙方, correcting the *rénshēn* / *dǎngshēn* substitution convention in the original *fánlì*.
