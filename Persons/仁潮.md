Late-Míng Buddhist monk; sobriquet **Bèilín** 貝琳 / 貝林 (with full name **Bèilín Réncháo** 貝琳仁潮; also referenced as **Yānshān Bèilín** 燕山貝琳). DILA Authority A000168. Lifedates unrecorded.

Compiler (錄 *lù*) of the **Fǎjiè ānlì tú** 法界安立圖 ([[KR6d0248]], X57n0972, 3 *juan*) — a Míng-period systematic exposition of the Buddhist cosmological structure (*fǎjiè* 法界, *dharmadhātu*) with topographical-cosmological diagrams. The work belongs to the late-Míng Buddhist cosmographic tradition.

Source: DILA A000168.
