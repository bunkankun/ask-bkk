Late-Míng Buddhist monk; sobriquet **Bèi-lín** 貝琳 / 貝林 (with full name **Bèi-lín Rén-cháo** 貝琳仁潮; also referenced as **Yān-shān Bèi-lín** 燕山貝琳). DILA Authority A000168. Lifedates unrecorded.

Compiler (錄 *lù*) of the **Fǎ-jiè ān-lì tú** 法界安立圖 ([[KR6d0248]], X57n0972, 3 *juan*) — a Míng-period systematic exposition of the Buddhist cosmological structure (*fǎ-jiè* 法界, *dharmadhātu*) with topographical-cosmological diagrams. The work belongs to the late-Míng Buddhist cosmographic tradition.

Source: DILA A000168.
