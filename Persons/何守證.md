Hé Shǒu-zhèng 何守證 was a Southern-Sòng *Jìng-míng* 淨明 Daoist active in the Xī-shān 西山 lineage at Yù-lóng wàn-shòu gōng 玉隆萬壽宫 in Jiāng-xī (the principal cult-site of Xǔ Sūn). The Daoist canon assigns him the office *Yì-zhēn-tán fù yǎn-jiào shī* 翼真壇副演教師 (Vice-Propagator-of-Teaching of the Wing-the-Perfected Altar). He compiled [[KR5b0267]] (*Língbǎo jìng-míng xīn-xiū jiǔ-lǎo shén-yìn fú-mó mì-fǎ*), whose preface is dated *Shàoxīng xīn-hài* 紹興辛亥 重陽日 — the *Double-Ninth* of *Shàoxīng* 21 = autumn of 1131. The preface narrates that, two years after the *Liù-zhēn* (Six Perfected) revealed the Jìng-míng *mì-fǎ* at the *zuò-è* year (Shàoxīng 9 = 1139, per the *jiǎ-zǐ* cycle "*suì zài zuò-è*" — but Hé places the revelation chronologically before his own preface; the dating remains debated), the *Gāo-míng dà-shǐ* descended to the *Yóu-wéi gù-dì* in Jiāng-xī (i.e. Yù-lóng wàn-shòu gōng) and instituted the *Yì-zhēn tán*. Hé was attached as a junior disciple and was commissioned to compile and emend the *Jiǔ-lǎo shén-yìn* texts. No CBDB record located; the figure is known only from this preface and the Jìng-míng canon traditions.
