Hé Shǒuzhèng 何守證 was a Southern-Sòng *Jìngmíng* 淨明 Daoist active in the Xīshān 西山 lineage at Yùlóng wànshòu gōng 玉隆萬壽宫 in Jiāngxī (the principal cult-site of Xǔ Sūn). The Daoist canon assigns him the office *Yìzhēntán fù yǎnjiào shī* 翼真壇副演教師 (Vice-Propagator-of-Teaching of the Wing-the-Perfected Altar). He compiled [[KR5b0267]] (*Língbǎo jìngmíng xīnxiū jiǔlǎo shényìn fúmó mìfǎ*), whose preface is dated *Shàoxīng xīnhài* 紹興辛亥 重陽日 — the *Double-Ninth* of *Shàoxīng* 21 = autumn of 1131. The preface narrates that, two years after the *Liùzhēn* (Six Perfected) revealed the Jìngmíng *mìfǎ* at the *zuòè* year (Shàoxīng 9 = 1139, per the *jiǎzǐ* cycle "*suì zài zuòè*" — but Hé places the revelation chronologically before his own preface; the dating remains debated), the *Gāomíng dàshǐ* descended to the *Yóuwéi gùdì* in Jiāngxī (i.e. Yùlóng wànshòu gōng) and instituted the *Yìzhēn tán*. Hé was attached as a junior disciple and was commissioned to compile and emend the *Jiǔlǎo shényìn* texts. No CBDB record located; the figure is known only from this preface and the Jìngmíng canon traditions.
