**Chén Xuézhōu** 陳學周, mid-Qiānlóng-era physician of Yōuxiàn 攸縣 (Húnán); the family doctor who repeatedly treated the sickly child [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī and encouraged him to take up medical study after he qualified as *dìzǐyuán* 弟子員 — known only from Cài's three prefaces to [[KR3eh051]]. No further biographical detail recoverable.
