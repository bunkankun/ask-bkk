Jīn Yòu-zī 金幼孜 (1368–1431), *míng* **Shàn** 善, *zì* **Yòu-zī** 幼孜 (he went by his *zì*), posthumous title **Wén-jìng** 文靖. Native of **Xīn-gàn** 新淦 (Lín-jiāng, Jiāng-xī). Hóngwǔ 33 (= Jiàn-wén 2 = 1400) *jìn-shì*; entered the cabinet in early Yǒng-lè (1402); served the four reigns Tài-zōng (Yǒng-lè), Rén-zōng (Hóng-xī), Xuān-zōng (Xuān-dé), early Yīng-zōng; rose to *Wén-yuān-gé dà-xué-shì* 文淵閣大學士 jointly *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Accompanied **Chéng-zǔ** on the northern campaigns; author of *Běi-zhēng lù* 北征錄 (catalogued in KR2c). One of the five participants in the **pact-of-mutual-death** at the Yān-army's entry into Nán-jīng (1402; cf. [[KR4e0077]] tíyào). *Míng shǐ* j. 147. Surviving works: [[KR4e0094]] *Jīn Wén-jìng jí* (10 *juǎn*), edited by his son Jīn Zhāo-bó 金昭伯; the *Wài-jí* (1 *juǎn*) and *Běi-zhēng jí* (1 *juǎn*) recorded by *Qiān-qǐng-táng shū-mù* are no longer extant.
