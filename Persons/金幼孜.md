Jīn Yòuzī 金幼孜 (1368–1431), *míng* **Shàn** 善, *zì* **Yòuzī** 幼孜 (he went by his *zì*), posthumous title **Wénjìng** 文靖. Native of **Xīngàn** 新淦 (Línjiāng, Jiāngxī). Hóngwǔ 33 (= Jiànwén 2 = 1400) *jìnshì*; entered the cabinet in early Yǒnglè (1402); served the four reigns Tàizōng (Yǒnglè), Rénzōng (Hóngxī), Xuānzōng (Xuāndé), early Yīngzōng; rose to *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* 文淵閣大學士 jointly *Lǐbù shàngshū*. Accompanied **Chéngzǔ** on the northern campaigns; author of *Běizhēng lù* 北征錄 (catalogued in KR2c). One of the five participants in the **pact-of-mutual-death** at the Yān-army's entry into Nánjīng (1402; cf. [[KR4e0077]] tíyào). *Míng shǐ* j. 147. Surviving works: [[KR4e0094]] *Jīn Wénjìng jí* (10 *juǎn*), edited by his son Jīn Zhāobó 金昭伯; the *Wàijí* (1 *juǎn*) and *Běizhēng jí* (1 *juǎn*) recorded by *Qiānqǐngtáng shūmù* are no longer extant.
