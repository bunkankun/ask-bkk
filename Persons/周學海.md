**Zhōu Xué-hǎi** 周學海 (1856–1906), *zì* Chéng-zhī 澄之 (also 澂之), *hào* Jiàn-xiá 健霞, was a late-Qīng scholar-official and physician of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Ānhuī). *Jìnshì* of 1888 (Guāngxù 14), he served briefly in the Hé-zhōu 河州 prefecture in Gānsù and then retired in his thirties to Wúhú 蕪湖, where he devoted himself to medical scholarship. He is the most prolific Late-Qīng compiler of medical reference works, having assembled the *Zhōu shì yī xué cóng shū* 周氏醫學叢書 in thirty-two *zhǒng* — the most comprehensive medical *cóngshū* of the late Qīng and an indispensable witness for the printing history of the medical canon. His original works include: *Nèijīng píng wén* 內經評文 ([[KR3ea032]]), *Mài yì jiǎn mó* 脈義簡摩 (3 juan), *Mài jiǎn bǔ* 脈簡補 (2 juan), *Dú yī suí bǐ* 讀醫隨筆 (3 juan, on clinical case-records). His "literary-rhetorical" reading of the *Nèijīng* — assessing it as a piece of Hàn-period rhetoric rather than purely as a medical-doctrinal text — was admired by Zhāng Tài-yán 章太炎 and other Republican-era scholar-physicians.
