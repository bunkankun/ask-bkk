**Huáng Zǔjīng** 黃祖經 of Shíyáng 石陽 — Qīng publisher who in 1871 (Tóngzhì *xīnwèi*) prepared a re-cut wood-block edition of [[倪枝維]] Ní Zhīwéi's *Chǎnbǎo* ([[KR3ei057]]) at his family school, completing a project his late father (a *guānglù* official) had intended. Not in CBDB.
