**Wáng Luán** 王鑾 (*zì* Wénróng 文融, *hào* Rónghú 容湖), Míng-period paediatrician active in the *Hóngzhì* to *Jiājìng* eras (c. 1502–1534), of Wūchéng 烏程 (modern Wúxīng 吳興, Zhèjiāng). Bearer of a multi-generation family medical practice with paediatric specialism. Compiler of *Yòukē lèicuì* 幼科類萃 ([[KR3ej014]]) in 28 *juǎn*, organising paediatric disorders into 26 categories from neonatal disorders through smallpox. The work was published with a preface by Lǐ Lián 李濂 of Sōngzhǔ 嵩渚 dated 嘉靖十三年甲午冬十一月朔 = 1534.11.06. The work's intellectual foundation lies in the *Nèijīng*, *Nánjīng*, and *Shānghán lùn* canon, integrated with the *Qián-Yǐ* paediatric tradition and the four great masters of the Jīn-Yuán synthesis.
