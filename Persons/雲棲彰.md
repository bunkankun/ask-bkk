# Yúnqī Zhāng 雲棲彰

A late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist monk associated with the Yúnqī tradition, who recorded the *Zūnshèng* dhāraṇī from a dhāraṇī-pillar at the Xiángfú [-sì] and began raising funds to print it, according to the preface of Xùfǎ's *Fódǐng zūnshèng tuóluóní jīng shì* ([[KR6j0733]]).
