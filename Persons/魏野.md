Northern-Sòng recluse-poet, *zì* Zhòngxiān 仲先, *hào* Cǎotáng jūshì 草堂居士, of *Shǎnzhōu* 陜州 (his ancestors were Shǔ men resettled in Hénán). Refused all summons to office under Sòng Zhēnzōng. Died *Tiānxǐ* 3 / 1019 age 60; posthumous *Mìshūshěng zhùzuòláng*. Together with Lín Bū [[林逋]] the canonical pair of early-Northern-Sòng recluse-poets — but with greater contemporary court fame than Lín (the famous Liáo envoy anecdote of *Dàzhōngxiángfú* records the Khitan court asking the Sòng for the second half of his *Cǎotáng jí*). Original *Cǎotáng jí* 草堂集 in 10 *juǎn*, expanded posthumously by his son Wèi Xián 魏閑 into the *Dōngguān jí* [[KR4d0019]] (the title taken from his posthumous *Mìshūshěng* office, the *Dōngguān*) with preface by Xuē Tián 薛田 in 1023.
