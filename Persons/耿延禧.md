# Gěng Yánxǐ 耿延禧

Early-Southern-Sòng official with the appointment *lóngtú gé zhíxuéshì* 龍圖閣直學士; lay disciple first of Tàipíng [[慧懃]] Huìqín and subsequently of Yuánwù [[克勤]] Kèqín. Author of the Shàoxīng 3 (1133) preface to the *Yuánwù Fóguǒ chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0059]]).
