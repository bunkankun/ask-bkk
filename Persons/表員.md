Pyowŏn 表員 (active late Silla period), Korean Hwaeom-school scholar-monk; compiler of the [[KR6e0121|*Huáyán jīng wén yì yào jué wèn dá* 華嚴經文義要決問答]] (X237) in 4 fascicles. Birth and death dates not preserved. The work is a Hwaeom catechetical compilation of the principal doctrinal cruxes of the *Avataṃsaka*, drawing on the Korean and Chinese commentarial traditions.
