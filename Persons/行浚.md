# Xíngjùn 行浚

Mid-seventeenth-century LínjìYángqí Chán monk of the 行-generation (= dharma-grandson of Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟; dharma-son of [[通賢|Fúshí Tōngxián]] 浮石通賢, 1594–1667). Head compiler (*sìfǎ ménrén ... děng biān* 嗣法門人...等編) of [[KR6q0407]] 《浮石禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J26 B185), cut Kāngxī 1 (1662) at the request to **Shǐ Dàchéng**, **Tán Zhēnmò**, and **Qián Qiānyì** for front-prefaces. Named as one of Fúshí's dharma-heirs in the *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 67 Línjì-line roster.

Identifiable with **Quánshí Wògōng** 拳石沃公 mentioned by Shǐ Dàchéng in his preface to [[KR6q0407]] as the *fǎsì* 法嗣 who directly transmitted the request for the preface ("*shī fǎsì Quánshí Wògōng zhī qǐng* 師法嗣拳石沃公之請") — *Quánshí* 拳石 and *Wògōng* 沃公 being his *zì* and honorific-name respectively. Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates unrecovered from the sources consulted. No independent *yǔlù* or literary collection survives under his name; he is known only through the Fúshí Tōngxián materials he curated.
