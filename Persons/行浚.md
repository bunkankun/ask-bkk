# Xíng-jùn 行浚

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk of the 行-generation (= dharma-grandson of Mìyún Yuán-wù 密雲圓悟; dharma-son of [[通賢|Fú-shí Tōng-xián]] 浮石通賢, 1594–1667). Head compiler (*sì-fǎ mén-rén ... děng biān* 嗣法門人...等編) of [[KR6q0407]] 《浮石禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J26 B185), cut Kāng-xī 1 (1662) at the request to **Shǐ Dà-chéng**, **Tán Zhēn-mò**, and **Qián Qiān-yì** for front-prefaces. Named as one of Fú-shí's dharma-heirs in the *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 67 Línjì-line roster.

Identifiable with **Quán-shí Wò-gōng** 拳石沃公 mentioned by Shǐ Dà-chéng in his preface to [[KR6q0407]] as the *fǎ-sì* 法嗣 who directly transmitted the request for the preface ("*shī fǎ-sì Quán-shí Wò-gōng zhī qǐng* 師法嗣拳石沃公之請") — *Quán-shí* 拳石 and *Wò-gōng* 沃公 being his *zì* and honorific-name respectively. Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates unrecovered from the sources consulted. No independent *yǔlù* or literary collection survives under his name; he is known only through the Fú-shí Tōng-xián materials he curated.
