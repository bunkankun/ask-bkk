Zhāng Bóxíng 張伯行 (1651–1725), *zì* Xiàoxiān 孝先, *hào* Shùzhāi 恕齋, posthumously *Qīngkè* 清恪, was a major early-Qīng *Lǐxué*-loyalist official and editor. Native of Yí-fēng 儀封 (Hé-nán). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī èr-shí-sān* (1684); rose to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Founded the *Zhèng yì táng* 正誼堂 imperial publishing-house at Sū-zhōu, which produced the standard editions of dozens of Sòng-Yuán-Míng *Lǐxué* texts. Edited and printed Lù Shìyí's *Sī biàn lù jí yào* 思辨錄輯要 (KR3a0111). His own *Zhèng yì táng wén jí* 正誼堂文集 and his commentaries on Sòng *Lǐxué* texts are extensively in circulation. CBDB id 30621, dates 1651–1725. (Note: Wáng Zhí's *Zhèng méng chū yì*, KR3a0029, includes a passage suggesting that the *Zhèng méng* commentary attributed to Zhāng Bóxíng is in fact a forgery by another under his name — on Wáng's reported oral testimony from Zhāng himself.)
