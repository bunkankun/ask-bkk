**Gāo Shū-zōng** 高叔宗 (mid-Míng physician-editor, fl. *Chéng-huà* / *Hóng-zhì* era), the editor and compiler of the *Dān-xī zhì-fǎ xīn-yào* 丹溪治法心要 ([[KR3er027]]) — an eight-*juǎn* clinical case-record handbook drawn from Zhū Dān-xī's medical practice. Gāo's editorial intervention is conventionally placed in the mid-Míng *Chéng-huà* / *Hóng-zhì* era, contemporary with Chéng Chōng's 1481 *Dān-xī xīn-fǎ* recension ([[KR3er018]]). The catalog meta records the work's dynasty as 元 (reflecting the underlying Dān-xī material) and the compiler as 高叔宗 (the Míng editor). Gāo's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered.
