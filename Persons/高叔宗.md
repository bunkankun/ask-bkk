**Gāo Shūzōng** 高叔宗 (mid-Míng physician-editor, fl. *Chénghuà* / *Hóngzhì* era), the editor and compiler of the *Dānxī zhìfǎ xīnyào* 丹溪治法心要 ([[KR3er027]]) — an eight-*juǎn* clinical case-record handbook drawn from Zhū Dānxī's medical practice. Gāo's editorial intervention is conventionally placed in the mid-Míng *Chénghuà* / *Hóngzhì* era, contemporary with Chéng Chōng's 1481 *Dānxī xīnfǎ* recension ([[KR3er018]]). The catalog meta records the work's dynasty as 元 (reflecting the underlying Dānxī material) and the compiler as 高叔宗 (the Míng editor). Gāo's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered.
