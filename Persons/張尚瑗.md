Zhāng Shàngyuàn 張尚瑗 (1656–1731), zì Hóngqú 宏蘧 and also Sǔnshí 損時, native of Wújiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu 蘇州 prefecture). *Kāngxī wùchén* (1688) *jìnshì*; admitted as *shùjíshì* 庶吉士 to the Hànlín Academy and after the *sǎnguǎn* posting was rotated out as magistrate of Xìngguó 興國 county. He studied *Chūnqiū* under Zhū Hèlíng 朱鶴齡 (zì Yúan-cǎo 愚菴; 1606–1683), one of the most authoritative seventeenth-century *Zuǒ-shì* exegetes, whose *Dú zuǒ rì chāo* 讀左日鈔 prompted Zhāng's parallel manuscript *Dú sān zhuàn suí bǐ* 讀三傳隨筆, which Zhāng eventually re-cast as *Sān zhuàn zhé zhū* 三傳折諸 ([[KR1e0107]]) — completed in *Yōngzhèng* 1 (1723) per his own preface.
