**Lǐ Fǎng** 李昉 (925–996), *zì* Míng yuǎn 明遠, was a senior Northern-Sòng scholar-official, Grand Minister under Sòng Tài zōng. Chief compiler of the **Four Great Books of Sòng** (*Sòng sì dà shū* 宋四大書): the *Tài píng guǎng jì* 太平廣記, *Tài píng yù lǎn* 太平御覽, and *Wén yuàn yīng huá* 文苑英華 (with Sòng Bái 宋白) — together the foundational imperially-commissioned compendia of the Northern Sòng. [[KR5g0039]] is a Daozang inclusion of selected materials from his *Tài píng yù lǎn* (983).
