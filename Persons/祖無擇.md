Zǔ Wúzé 祖無擇 (1011–1085, *zì* Zézhī 擇之, customarily called Lóngxué 龍學 from his eventual *Lóngtúgé xuéshì* office), of Shàngcài 上蔡 (Hénán). *Bǎoyuán* 1 / 1038 *jìnshì* in third place. Studied *jīngshù* under Sūn Fù 孫復 [[孫復]] and *wénzhāng* under Mù Xiū 穆修 [[穆修]] — placing him in the early-Northern-Sòng *gǔwén* / *Chūnqiūxué* lineage that runs from Shí Jiè 石介 to Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修 [[歐陽修]]. Held *zhī Nánkāngjūn*, *zhī Shǎnfǔ*, *Zhīzhìgào*, and finally *Lóngtúgé xuéshì zhī Tōngjìn yíntáisī*; impeached on charges and demoted to *Zhōngzhèngjūn jiédù fùshǐ*, transferred to *zhī Xìnyángjūn*; died there. Catalog meta gives 1006–1085 but CBDB and *Sòngshǐ* materials give 1011–1085 (followed here). *Sòngshǐ* has his biography. The *Sòngshǐ Yìwénzhì* lists his collection in 50 *juǎn*; what remains is the *Lóngxué wénjí* 龍學文集 [[KR4d0055]] in 16 *juǎn*, reconstituted by his great-grandson Zǔ Xíng 祖行 in *Shàoxī* 3 / 1192. Zǔ's sister-husbands (per CBDB) included Méi Yáochén 梅堯臣 [[梅堯臣]] and Hán Yì 韓繹 — locating him at the very center of the *Qìnglì–Jiāyòu* literary network. *Sòngshǐ* 331.
