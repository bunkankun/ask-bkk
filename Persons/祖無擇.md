Zǔ Wú-zé 祖無擇 (1011–1085, *zì* Zé-zhī 擇之, customarily called Lóng-xué 龍學 from his eventual *Lóng-tú-gé xué-shì* office), of Shàng-cài 上蔡 (Hénán). *Bǎo-yuán* 1 / 1038 *jìn-shì* in third place. Studied *jīng-shù* under Sūn Fù 孫復 [[孫復]] and *wén-zhāng* under Mù Xiū 穆修 [[穆修]] — placing him in the early-Northern-Sòng *gǔ-wén* / *Chūn-qiū-xué* lineage that runs from Shí Jiè 石介 to Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修 [[歐陽修]]. Held *zhī Nán-kāng-jūn*, *zhī Shǎn-fǔ*, *Zhī-zhì-gào*, and finally *Lóng-tú-gé xué-shì zhī Tōng-jìn yín-tái-sī*; impeached on charges and demoted to *Zhōng-zhèng-jūn jié-dù fù-shǐ*, transferred to *zhī Xìn-yáng-jūn*; died there. Catalog meta gives 1006–1085 but CBDB and *Sòng-shǐ* materials give 1011–1085 (followed here). *Sòng-shǐ* has his biography. The *Sòng-shǐ Yì-wén-zhì* lists his collection in 50 *juǎn*; what remains is the *Lóng-xué wén-jí* 龍學文集 [[KR4d0055]] in 16 *juǎn*, reconstituted by his great-grandson Zǔ Xíng 祖行 in *Shào-xī* 3 / 1192. Zǔ's sister-husbands (per CBDB) included Méi Yáo-chén 梅堯臣 [[梅堯臣]] and Hán Yì 韓繹 — locating him at the very center of the *Qìng-lì–Jiā-yòu* literary network. *Sòng-shǐ* 331.
