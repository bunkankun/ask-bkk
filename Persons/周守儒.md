**Zhōu Shǒurú** 周守儒 (d. 1916, late Qīng / early Republic). Practitioner-compiler of the *Zēngyǎn yìjīn xǐsuǐ nèigōng túshuō* 增演易筋洗髓內功圖說, having received the technique from Jìngyī Kōngwù dàshī; teacher of Zhāng Yáo.
