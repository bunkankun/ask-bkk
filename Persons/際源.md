# Jìyuán 際源

Late-Qīng Chán monk; co-compiler (輯) of the *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* 正源略集 ([[KR6q0032]]) together with Liǎozhēn 了貞, edited by Dázhēn 達珍. Active in the early nineteenth century; exact lifedates not transmitted.
