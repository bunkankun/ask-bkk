Xìng-chún 性純, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Fó-yuān Chè-gāng** [[徹綱]] 佛冤徹綱 in the Pò-shān → Zhàng-xuě → Chè-gāng line. Lead compiler of the 12-juan 《佛冤禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0508]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
