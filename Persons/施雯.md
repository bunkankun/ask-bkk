**Shī Wén** 施雯 (fl. mid-18th c.), *zì* Dànníng 澹寧. Mid-Qīng physician of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). Co-compiler with Yán Jié [[嚴潔]] and Hóng Wěiquán [[洪煒全]] of the *Dé pèi běncǎo* 得配本草 ([[KR3ec058]], 1761). His role in the collaboration was the substance-by-substance compilation of the *pèiwǔ* (drug-pairing) tables. Not in CBDB.
