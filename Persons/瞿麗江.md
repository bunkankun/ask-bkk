**Qū Lìjiāng** 瞿麗江 (*fl.* early 19th c., 清), friend of [[楊時泰]]. In 道光六年丙戌 (1826) he gifted Yáng a copy of [[劉若金]]'s *Běncǎo shù* 本草述 in Běijīng — the manuscript that became the basis for Yáng's seven-year condensation, the *Běncǎo shù gōuyuán* 本草述鉤元 ([[KR3ec052]]). No further biographical detail; no CBDB record.
