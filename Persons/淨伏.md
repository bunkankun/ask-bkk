# Jìng-fú 淨伏

Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Hǔ-yán 虎巖 ("Tiger-Cliff"). Principal dharma-heir of [[普度]] Xū-zhōu Pǔ-dù (1199–1280); named first among the editors of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0340]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
