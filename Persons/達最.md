Dá-zuì 達最, *hào* **Zǔ-bí** 祖鼻, was a *dá*-generation Línjì (Pòshān → Zhàng-xuě → Yuè-chuáng) dharma-heir of **Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo** [[了]] 月幢徹了 (1614–1667), one of five named *fǎ-sì* of that master per 《黔南會燈錄》 j. 1. Lead compiler of the 4-juan 《月幢了禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0447]]), signing the juan 1 colophon as *sì-fǎ mén-rén Dá-zuì děng biān* 嗣法門人達最等編 — principal witness to the Yún-nán **Shí-bǎo Chán-yuàn** 石寶禪院 ministry from 1660. No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
