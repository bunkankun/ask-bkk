Xuángǎo 玄杲, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Xièyún Jìjī** [[濟璣]] 燮雲濟璣 (d. 1648) at **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** 天台國清寺. Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《燮雲璣禪師國清無畏堂語錄》 ([[KR6q0476]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
