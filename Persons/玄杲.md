Xuán-gǎo 玄杲, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Xiè-yún Jì-jī** [[濟璣]] 燮雲濟璣 (d. 1648) at **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** 天台國清寺. Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《燮雲璣禪師國清無畏堂語錄》 ([[KR6q0476]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
