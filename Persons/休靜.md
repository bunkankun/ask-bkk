# Xiūjìng 休靜

Korean (Chosŏn) Sŏn master Hyujŏng 休靜 (1520–1604), known as Sŏsan Taesa 西山大師, central figure in late-Chosŏn Sŏn lineage reconstruction; associated in modern Korean polemics with the *dùnwù dùnxiū* 頓悟頓修 ("sudden awakening, sudden cultivation") position contrasted with [[知訥]] Chinul's *dùnwù jiànxiū* in the *Susim gyŏl* ([[KR6q0097]]) controversy.
