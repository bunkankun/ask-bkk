Rúxǐ 如璽, *zì* **Fāngróng** 方融, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Tiānjiè Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] 天界覺浪道盛 (1593–1659). Born **Wànlì 30 壬寅 7/7 子時** = **1602-08-23 Gregorian**; native of **Jīngyángxiàn** 涇陽縣 in **Xīānfǔ** 西安府 (Shǎnxī); lay surname **Rèn** 任, lay-name **Fúshēng** 福生 (bestowed by his nine-decade-old *zúzǔ fēngjūn* 族祖封君 ancestor at birth on auspicious dream-grounds). Death-date not located.

**Formation.** Childhood vegetarianism after refusing chicken on the urging of his mother (a pious *chángsù* practitioner who taught him *xīnjīng* recitation in his fifth year). At age 16 (**萬曆四十五 丁巳 7/7 = 1617**) tonsured at **Bǎolínsì** 寶林寺 on **Wǔtáishān** 五臺山 under **Dàcái héshàng** 大材和尚, receiving the *shāmí shí jiè* and the dharma-name 如璽. Subsequent pilgrimage circuit: **Pǔxián** at Éméi (the *huó Pǔxián* 活普賢 awakening on the master's *zhènshēng yī hè* 震聲一喝); **Wǔdāng**, **Bóshān** (the *shēngcónghélái* awakening on a broken-pole rice-carrying incident); **Yīān**, **Huìdēng**, **Fǎchuáng**, **Zhànrán**, **Xuějiāo Yuánxìn** at Jìngshān (the *liǎngshǒu chéngzhǐ* 兩手呈紙 exchange); **Mìyún Yuánwù** at **Jīnsùshān** (the *yī bàng qí dǎshā* exchange); **Wúniàn** at **Huángbò** (where Wúniàn drove him out as *yóuzuǐ huā-zi* 油嘴花子). At 35 *suì* (**1636 丙子**) received the **三壇大戒 sāntán dàjiè** triple-platform precepts under **Zhuānyú héshàng** 顓愚和尚 at **Sūxī Bǎiguì shūyuán** 蘇溪百桂書園.

**Dao-shèng connection and abbacies.** **1637 丁丑 spring**: Zhuānyú swept **Hānshān**'s tomb at Kuānglú; that autumn entered **Yúnjū** 雲居 under invitation of *jǐjiàn* **Xióng Qīngyǔ** 給諫熊青嶼 with **Wèibáishī** 味白師 to revive the monastery — appointed *jiānyuàn* 監院. **順治丙戌 = 1646**: independent abbacy at **Zhēnrú Chánsì** 真如禪寺. **己亥 = 1659**: received transmission-mandate from **Tiānjiè Juélàng Dàoshèng** just before Dàoshèng's death. **庚子 = 1660**: assumed abbacy of **Tiānjièsì** 天界寺 (Nánjīng) — the seat that gives the *Tiānjiè Fāngróng Xǐ* 天界方融璽 catalog-form. **康熙壬寅 = 1662**: also assumed **Hóngjì Chánsì** 弘濟禪寺. The 《方融璽禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0458]]) covers all three abbacies (Tiānjiè, Hóngjì, Yúnjū). The closing 行實 was self-narrated when *nián mǎn liùshí* 年滿六十 = at age 60 = **1661**.

**Heirs and writings.** Sole named dharma-heir: **Yíwén Dé jiānsì** 遺聞德 (lit. "*yíwén Dé* the supervisor", per the j. 3 *fùfǎ*). Work: [[KR6q0458]] 《方融璽禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B249, compiled by *ménrén* **Xīnglín** [[興林]] *děng* 興林等編.
