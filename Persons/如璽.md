Rú-xǐ 如璽, *zì* **Fāng-róng** 方融, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] 天界覺浪道盛 (1593–1659). Born **Wàn-lì 30 壬寅 7/7 子時** = **1602-08-23 Gregorian**; native of **Jīng-yáng-xiàn** 涇陽縣 in **Xī-ān-fǔ** 西安府 (Shǎn-xī); lay surname **Rèn** 任, lay-name **Fú-shēng** 福生 (bestowed by his nine-decade-old *zú-zǔ fēng-jūn* 族祖封君 ancestor at birth on auspicious dream-grounds). Death-date not located.

**Formation.** Childhood vegetarianism after refusing chicken on the urging of his mother (a pious *cháng-sù* practitioner who taught him *xīn-jīng* recitation in his fifth year). At age 16 (**萬曆四十五 丁巳 7/7 = 1617**) tonsured at **Bǎo-lín-sì** 寶林寺 on **Wǔ-tái-shān** 五臺山 under **Dà-cái héshàng** 大材和尚, receiving the *shā-mí shí jiè* and the dharma-name 如璽. Subsequent pilgrimage circuit: **Pǔ-xián** at Éméi (the *huó Pǔ-xián* 活普賢 awakening on the master's *zhèn-shēng yī hè* 震聲一喝); **Wǔ-dāng**, **Bó-shān** (the *shēng-cóng-hé-lái* awakening on a broken-pole rice-carrying incident); **Yī-ān**, **Huì-dēng**, **Fǎ-chuáng**, **Zhàn-rán**, **Xuě-jiāo Yuán-xìn** at Jìng-shān (the *liǎng-shǒu chéng-zhǐ* 兩手呈紙 exchange); **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** at **Jīn-sù-shān** (the *yī bàng qí dǎ-shā* exchange); **Wú-niàn** at **Huáng-bò** (where Wú-niàn drove him out as *yóu-zuǐ huā-zi* 油嘴花子). At 35 *suì* (**1636 丙子**) received the **三壇大戒 sān-tán dà-jiè** triple-platform precepts under **Zhuān-yú héshàng** 顓愚和尚 at **Sū-xī Bǎi-guì shū-yuán** 蘇溪百桂書園.

**Dao-shèng connection and abbacies.** **1637 丁丑 spring**: Zhuān-yú swept **Hān-shān**'s tomb at Kuāng-lú; that autumn entered **Yún-jū** 雲居 under invitation of *jǐ-jiàn* **Xióng Qīng-yǔ** 給諫熊青嶼 with **Wèi-bái-shī** 味白師 to revive the monastery — appointed *jiān-yuàn* 監院. **順治丙戌 = 1646**: independent abbacy at **Zhēn-rú Chán-sì** 真如禪寺. **己亥 = 1659**: received transmission-mandate from **Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Dào-shèng** just before Dào-shèng's death. **庚子 = 1660**: assumed abbacy of **Tiān-jiè-sì** 天界寺 (Nán-jīng) — the seat that gives the *Tiān-jiè Fāng-róng Xǐ* 天界方融璽 catalog-form. **康熙壬寅 = 1662**: also assumed **Hóng-jì Chán-sì** 弘濟禪寺. The 《方融璽禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0458]]) covers all three abbacies (Tiān-jiè, Hóng-jì, Yún-jū). The closing 行實 was self-narrated when *nián mǎn liù-shí* 年滿六十 = at age 60 = **1661**.

**Heirs and writings.** Sole named dharma-heir: **Yí-wén Dé jiān-sì** 遺聞德 (lit. "*yí-wén Dé* the supervisor", per the j. 3 *fù-fǎ*). Work: [[KR6q0458]] 《方融璽禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B249, compiled by *mén-rén* **Xīng-lín** [[興林]] *děng* 興林等編.
