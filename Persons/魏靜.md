# Wèi Jìng 魏靜

Early-Táng official, *cìshǐ* of Qìngzhōu 慶州; lay disciple and friend of [[玄覺]] Yǒngjiā Xuánjué (665–713) during a minor post in Yǒngjiā; compiler and preface-writer of Xuánjué's collected essays as [[KR6q0089]] *Chánzōng Yǒngjiā jí*. Not otherwise attested in Táng historical records.
