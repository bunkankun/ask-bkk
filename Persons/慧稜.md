# Chángqìng Huìléng 長慶慧稜

Late-Táng / Five-Dynasties Chán master at Chángqìng in Fúzhōu 福州; first teacher of [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益 before Wényì transferred to [[桂琛|Luóhàn Guìchēn]] 羅漢桂琛.
