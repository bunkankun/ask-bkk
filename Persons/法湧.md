# Fǎyǒng 法湧

Northern Sòng Chán master (chánshī 禪師) who participated in the Hángzhōu editorial recension of Yánshòu's *Zōngjìng lù* ([[KR6q0092]]) during the Yuánfēng / Yuányòu period (late 11th century).
