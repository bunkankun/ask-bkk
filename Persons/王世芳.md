**Wáng Shìfāng** 王世芳 (1659–1772?), Kāngxī-era *jìnshì* and Hànlín *sīyè* emeritus, famed for unusual longevity; wrote the 1771 preface to Xú Wénbì's *Shòushì chuánzhēn* at the reported age of 113 *suì*.
