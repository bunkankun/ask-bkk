Early-Qīng Han-Bannerman (漢軍) literatus and disciple of Wáng Shìzhēn [[王士禎]]. CBDB (personid 83941) gives an index year of 1667; precise birth and death dates are not recorded in the standard biographical sources. The Hànjūn registration is given in the *Sìkù* edition's title-page rubric ("漢軍郎廷槐編"). He is known almost exclusively for his compilation of the *Shīyǒu shīzhuàn lù* 師友詩傳錄, in which he presented questions on poetics to three masters of the Shāndōng *Yúyáng* circle (Wáng Shìzhēn, Zhāng Dǔqìng [[張篤慶]] (*zì* Lìyǒu 歷友), and Zhāng Shíjū [[張實居]] (*hào* Xiāotíng 蕭亭) — Wáng Shìzhēn's nephew and brother-in-law respectively) and recorded their answers. He held no recorded office of substance; the work itself is his enduring contribution to Qīng poetic theory.
