Early-Qīng Han-Bannerman (漢軍) literatus and disciple of Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]]. CBDB (personid 83941) gives an index year of 1667; precise birth and death dates are not recorded in the standard biographical sources. The Hàn-jūn registration is given in the *Sìkù* edition's title-page rubric ("漢軍郎廷槐編"). He is known almost exclusively for his compilation of the *Shī-yǒu shī-zhuàn lù* 師友詩傳錄, in which he presented questions on poetics to three masters of the Shāndōng *Yú-yáng* circle (Wáng Shì-zhēn, Zhāng Dǔ-qìng [[張篤慶]] (*zì* Lì-yǒu 歷友), and Zhāng Shí-jū [[張實居]] (*hào* Xiāo-tíng 蕭亭) — Wáng Shì-zhēn's nephew and brother-in-law respectively) and recorded their answers. He held no recorded office of substance; the work itself is his enduring contribution to Qīng poetic theory.
