**Xǔ Yù-hé** 許豫和 (*hào* Xiàng-cūn 橡村 / Yí-táng 怡堂, 1724–1805 by standard biographical reference), Qīng-period **Xīn-ān** 新安 (Huī-zhōu / Anhui) physician of the late Qián-lóng — early Jiā-qìng period. Major figure of the late-eighteenth-century Xīn-ān-school regional-medical tradition. Principal medical specialty was paediatrics (especially smallpox) but moved beyond the specialty into broader clinical-theoretical synthesis in his later years. Major works: *Xiàng-cūn dòu-jué* 橡村痘訣 (smallpox), *Yí-táng sàn-jì* 怡堂散記 ([[KR3eq063]]) with *Doutiao Yuyi* 痘訣余議 supplement. Not in CBDB.
