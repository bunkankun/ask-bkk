**Xǔ Yùhé** 許豫和 (*hào* Xiàngcūn 橡村 / Yítáng 怡堂, 1724–1805 by standard biographical reference), Qīng-period **Xīnān** 新安 (Huīzhōu / Anhui) physician of the late Qiánlóng — early Jiāqìng period. Major figure of the late-eighteenth-century Xīn-ān-school regional-medical tradition. Principal medical specialty was paediatrics (especially smallpox) but moved beyond the specialty into broader clinical-theoretical synthesis in his later years. Major works: *Xiàngcūn dòujué* 橡村痘訣 (smallpox), *Yítáng sànjì* 怡堂散記 ([[KR3eq063]]) with *Doutiao Yuyi* 痘訣余議 supplement. Not in CBDB.
