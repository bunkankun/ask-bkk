# 松石翁

**Sōngshí Wēng** ("Old Pine-and-Stone", *hào*, *fl.* early-15th c.), otherwise unidentified figure named in the preface to [[KR3el001]] *Mìchuán Liú Bówēn jiācáng jiēgǔ jīnchuāng jìnfāng* as a descendant of [[劉基]] Liú Jī (Liú Bówēn) and the source of the manuscript transmitted to the preface-writer [[體仁子]] Tǐrénzǐ. The Liú-family genealogy preserved in the *Chéngyìbó wénjí* lineage does not record a *hào* "Sōngshí" for any documented descendant within the Xuāndé window; the figure is best read as part of the late-Míng pseudepigraphic framing of the work rather than as a historically verifiable Liú clansman.
