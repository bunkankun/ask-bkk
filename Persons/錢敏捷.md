Qián Mǐnjié 錢敏捷 (*zì* Qínmín 勤民, *fl.* mid-late 19th c., 清) was a mid-late-Qing literatus-physician whose principal work is the *Yīfāng jiédù* 醫方絜度 ([[KR3ed062]]), a 3-*juǎn* formulary of c. 260 formulas drawn from post-Zhòngjǐng physicians with the author's own annotations and tested additions. The preface preserved in the KR transmission is by his younger brother Qián Huǎnzhī 錢緩之, who relates that the elder Qián's intellectual horizon extended beyond medicine to astronomy, geography, and divinatory arts (*qí-dùn* 奇遁), and that the formulary was completed during his lifetime; the brother undertook the printing.
