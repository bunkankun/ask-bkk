Early-Qiánlóng-period physician of Shíyáng 石陽 (Jiāngxī); essentially unattested outside his own surviving works. Author of [[KR3eb013]] *Mài què* (pre-1746) and a *Shānghán lùn yì* 傷寒論翼 (a commentary on the Zhāng Zhòngjǐng *Shānghán* tradition). His *Mài què* was endorsed and printed by Chéng Yín 程崟 of Shèzhōu 歙州 in 1746 with backing from the Shānyáng and Zhēnzhōu physician circles. No CBDB record. Stylistically and doctrinally allied with the early-Qing *Nèijīng*-first reformist movement.
