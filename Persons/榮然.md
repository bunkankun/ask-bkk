Yōnen 榮然 (fl. late 12th to mid-13th c.) was a Japanese Shingon master of the **Chū'in-ryū** 中院流 transmission line on **Kōyasan**, in the generation following **Kōnen** 興然 (1121–1203). His precise lifedates are not securely recoverable. His sole surviving work [[KR6t0207]] *Shī kǒu* (*Shiku*) records the *master's-mouth* oral transmissions of the Kōyasan Chū'in-ryū lineage in four fascicles.

Surviving work in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0207]] *Shiku* (4 fasc.).
