# Qīngyì 清逸

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*sìfǎ ménrén* 嗣法門人) of [[文康]] Mùān Wénkāng; abbot of Tāizhōufǔ Huángyán Guǎngxiào chánsì 台州府黃巖廣孝禪寺. Principal editor of the one-juan *Mùān Wénkāng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0348]]), where his name opens the editorial byline on the first section (Tiāntái Míngyán, 1357). Lifedates unrecorded.
