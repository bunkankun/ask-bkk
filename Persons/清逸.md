# Qīng-yì 清逸

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*sì-fǎ mén-rén* 嗣法門人) of [[文康]] Mù-ān Wén-kāng; abbot of Tāi-zhōu-fǔ Huáng-yán Guǎng-xiào chán-sì 台州府黃巖廣孝禪寺. Principal editor of the one-juan *Mù-ān Wén-kāng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0348]]), where his name opens the editorial byline on the first section (Tiān-tái Míng-yán, 1357). Lifedates unrecorded.
