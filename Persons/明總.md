# Míngzǒng 明總

Southern-Sòng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *hào* Shíshuāng 石霜. Dharma-sibling of [[慧暉]] Jìngcí Huìhuī (1097–1183) within the Hóngzhì Zhèngjué / Cáodòng circle. Supplied the *xiàyǔ jìyán* 下語寄言 commentary (Cáo-dòng-style doctrinal exegesis) for his dharma-brother's six-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0360]]) — giving, for each of Huìhuī's *shàngtáng* passages, an *en face* unpacking of its coded Cáo-dòng-register vocabulary. Lifedates unrecorded.
