# Míng-zǒng 明總

Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *hào* Shí-shuāng 石霜. Dharma-sibling of [[慧暉]] Jìng-cí Huì-huī (1097–1183) within the Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué / Cáo-dòng circle. Supplied the *xià-yǔ jì-yán* 下語寄言 commentary (Cáo-dòng-style doctrinal exegesis) for his dharma-brother's six-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0360]]) — giving, for each of Huì-huī's *shàng-táng* passages, an *en face* unpacking of its coded Cáo-dòng-register vocabulary. Lifedates unrecorded.
