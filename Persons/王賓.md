**Wáng Bīn** 王賓 (*fl.* late Yuán to early Hóngwǔ era, 14th c.), *zì* Zhòngguāng 仲光, *hào* Guāng'ān 光菴, was a Sūzhōu physician of the early-Míng Wú-medical lineage and a pupil of [[戴原禮]] Dài Yuánlǐ (himself a student of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng / Dānxī). He passed on the Dānxī tradition to [[盛啟東]] Shèng Qǐdōng and [[韓叔陽]] Hán Shūyáng, whose lineage reached the early-Míng imperial 御藥院 through the Yǒnglè court; the Jiājìng-era [[顧從德]] Gù Cóngdé invoked this transmission in the postface to the 1550 facsimile of the Sòng校正 *Sùwèn* ([[KR3ea001]]).
