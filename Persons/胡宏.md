Hú Hóng 胡宏 (1106–1162), *zì* Rénzhòng 仁仲, *hào* Wǔfēng xiānsheng 五峯先生, was the founder of the *HúXiāng xuépài* 湖湘學派 ("Hu-nan school") of mid-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué*. Native of Chóngān 崇安 (Fújiàn). Son of Hú Ānguó 胡安國 ([[KR3a0039]] co-recorder); brother of Hú Yìn 胡寅. Took refuge from the Jīn invasion at Húnán's Héshān 衡山 (Mt. Héng) and settled at Wǔfēngshān, where he taught — including Zhāng Shì 張栻, the principal channel of his teaching to the larger Sòng *Lǐxué* mainstream. The Hu-Xiāng school stands in tension with Zhū Xī's *Mǐnxué* — Zhū wrote a *Zhī yán yí yì* 知言疑義 against Hú's *Zhī yán* ([[KR3a0049]]), and the Hu-Xiāng / *Mǐn* divergence on *xìng* 性 doctrine was one of the major mid-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* doctrinal disputes. Major works: the *Huáng wáng dà jì* 皇王大紀 (history); the *Zhī yán* 知言 ([[KR3a0049]]); the *Wǔfēng jí* 五峯集. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 435 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 7252, dates 1106–1162.
