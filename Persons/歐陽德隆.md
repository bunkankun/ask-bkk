Southern-Sòng *xiǎo-xué* scholar, *jìn-shì* of Lú-líng 廬陵 (Jí-shuǐ, Jiāng-xī), Shào-dìng *gēng-yín* (1230). The compiler of the *Yā-yùn shì-yí* 押韻釋疑 (5 *juàn*), a working examination-rhyme guide developed jointly with his friend Yì Yǒu-kāi 易有開 in response to the *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè*'s confusing entries where graphs share form-but-not-meaning or meaning-but-not-form. Yú Wén-yù 余文焴 of Chén-líng wrote the preface. After Ōu-yáng's book had been re-printed many times by commercial book-shops with extensive corruption, the work was *zēng-xiū* (re-augmented) by Guō Shǒu-zhèng [[郭守正]] in Jǐng-dìng *jiǎ-zǐ* (1264), the version preserved as [[KR1j0062]].
