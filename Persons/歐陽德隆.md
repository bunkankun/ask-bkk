Southern-Sòng *xiǎoxué* scholar, *jìnshì* of Lúlíng 廬陵 (Jíshuǐ, Jiāngxī), Shàodìng *gēngyín* (1230). The compiler of the *Yāyùn shìyí* 押韻釋疑 (5 *juàn*), a working examination-rhyme guide developed jointly with his friend Yì Yǒukāi 易有開 in response to the *Lǐbù yùnlüè*'s confusing entries where graphs share form-but-not-meaning or meaning-but-not-form. Yú Wényù 余文焴 of Chénlíng wrote the preface. After Ōuyáng's book had been re-printed many times by commercial book-shops with extensive corruption, the work was *zēngxiū* (re-augmented) by Guō Shǒuzhèng [[郭守正]] in Jǐngdìng *jiǎzǐ* (1264), the version preserved as [[KR1j0062]].
