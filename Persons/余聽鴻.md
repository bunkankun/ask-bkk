**Yú Tīnghóng** 余聽鴻 (*zì* Jǐnghé 景和, 1847–1907), late-Qīng physician of Chángshú 常熟 (Jiāngsū), of the Mènghé–Wú-region medical tradition. Author of *Yú Tīnghóng yīàn* 余聽鴻醫案 and *Zhèngbiàn lù* 證辨錄. Engaged with [[吉益為則]] Yoshimasu Tōdō's *kohōha* positions in the late-Qīng Chinese reception of Japanese Edo medicine ([[KR3eq037]]).
