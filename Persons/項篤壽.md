Mid-late-Míng scholar from Xiù-shuǐ 秀水 (modern Jiā-xìng 嘉興 in Zhèjiāng), courtesy name Zǐ-cháng 子長. CBDB id 30327 attests his name and dynasty without firm lifedates; he flourished in the *Jiā-jìng* and *Lóng-qìng* periods (c. 1530s–1580s). He was the elder brother of the renowned late-Míng calligrapher, painter, and book/painting collector Xiàng Yuán-biàn 項元汴 (Xiàng Mò-lín 項墨林, 1525–1590); the Xiàng family's library at Xiù-shuǐ was one of the major Jiāng-nán late-Míng book repositories. He is principally known for the *Jīn-xiàn bèi-yí* 今獻備遺 ([[KR2g0039]]), a 42-*juàn* / 204-man collective biography of Míng officials from *Hóng-wǔ* through *Jiā-jìng*, refined from Yuán Biǎo's 袁袠 earlier *Xiàn-zhāng wài-shǐ* 獻徵外史 — among the more carefully edited of the mid-Míng *míng-chén* anthologies.
