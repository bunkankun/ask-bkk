# Mì 謐 (Èryǐn Xíngmì 二隱行謐)

Single-character catalog-form of **二隱行謐** (**Èryǐn Xíngmì**), the principal dharma-heir and *yǔlù*-editor of [[通奇|Línyě Tōngqí]] 林野通奇 (1595/6–1652, at [[KR6q0408]]). For the full biography see the parallel person-note at [[行謐]].

**Line.** 33rd-generation Línjì (行-generation Mìyún sub-line), dharma-heir of Línyě Tōngqí after earlier direct training under Mìyún Yuánwù. Lay surname **Jīn** 金, of **Xī Shǔ Gǔ-Yú Chāngzhōu** 西蜀古渝昌州 (modern Dàzú, Chóngqìng, Sichuan). Lifedates **c. 1606 – 康熙 4 乙巳 6.28 午時** = **c. 1606 – 9 August 1665**, age **60 *suì*** / *sēnglà* **32**.

**Works in Kanripo:**

- [[KR6q0408]] 《林野奇禪師語錄》 — Xíngmì's **editorial compilation** of his master's *yǔlù*, cut with his signed *hòubá* dated **順治戊戌佛歡日** = 1658-08-13.
- [[KR6q0430]] 《二隱謐禪師語錄》 — Xíngmì's **own** *yǔlù* in 10 juan, compiled by his dharma-heirs [[超巨]] Chāojù and [[超秀]] Chāoxiù, cut 1678 with a preface by the Manchu prince **Chání** 察尼 at Jīngnán during the Three Feudatories Revolt.

**Six-abbacy sequence:** **Fǎxiáng** (1648) → **Qīzhēn** (1650) → **Dàmíng** (1651) → **Chuánzǐ Fǎrěnsì** (1653, site of his 1658 Línyě-*yǔlù* compilation) → **Lóngyuānsì** (1656, from which the 1658 *hòubá*-signature "龍淵行謐") → **Qǐshān Bǎoānsì** (1659–65, his final and *hào*-giving seat). Additional 3-year **bìguān** 1645–48 at Tāizhōu Huángyá Fǎlúnsì between Línyě's 1644 sealing and his first abbacy.
