# Mì 謐 (Èr-yǐn Xíng-mì 二隱行謐)

Single-character catalog-form of **二隱行謐** (**Èr-yǐn Xíng-mì**), the principal dharma-heir and *yǔlù*-editor of [[通奇|Lín-yě Tōng-qí]] 林野通奇 (1595/6–1652, at [[KR6q0408]]). For the full biography see the parallel person-note at [[行謐]].

**Line.** 33rd-generation Línjì (行-generation Mìyún sub-line), dharma-heir of Lín-yě Tōng-qí after earlier direct training under Mìyún Yuán-wù. Lay surname **Jīn** 金, of **Xī-Shǔ Gǔ-Yú Chāng-zhōu** 西蜀古渝昌州 (modern Dà-zú, Chóng-qìng, Sichuan). Lifedates **c. 1606 – 康熙 4 乙巳 6.28 午時** = **c. 1606 – 9 August 1665**, age **60 *suì*** / *sēng-là* **32**.

**Works in Kanripo:**

- [[KR6q0408]] 《林野奇禪師語錄》 — Xíng-mì's **editorial compilation** of his master's *yǔlù*, cut with his signed *hòu-bá* dated **順治戊戌佛歡日** = 1658-08-13.
- [[KR6q0430]] 《二隱謐禪師語錄》 — Xíng-mì's **own** *yǔlù* in 10 juan, compiled by his dharma-heirs [[超巨]] Chāo-jù and [[超秀]] Chāo-xiù, cut 1678 with a preface by the Manchu prince **Chá-ní** 察尼 at Jīng-nán during the Three Feudatories Revolt.

**Six-abbacy sequence:** **Fǎ-xiáng** (1648) → **Qī-zhēn** (1650) → **Dà-míng** (1651) → **Chuán-zǐ Fǎ-rěn-sì** (1653, site of his 1658 Lín-yě-*yǔlù* compilation) → **Lóng-yuān-sì** (1656, from which the 1658 *hòu-bá*-signature "龍淵行謐") → **Qǐ-shān Bǎo-ān-sì** (1659–65, his final and *hào*-giving seat). Additional 3-year **bì-guān** 1645–48 at Tāi-zhōu Huáng-yá Fǎ-lún-sì between Lín-yě's 1644 sealing and his first abbacy.
