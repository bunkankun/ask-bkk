**Mǎ Zìrán** 馬自然 was a (probably) Northern-Sòng Daoist alchemist, attributed author of [[KR5e0059]] *Mǎ Zìrán jīn dān kǒu jué* 馬自然金丹口訣. Distinct from the Quán zhēn figure Mǎ Yù 馬鈺.
