# Tán-dùn 曇頓

Early-Míng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[普莊]] Dāi-ān Pǔ-zhuāng (1347–1403); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0351]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
