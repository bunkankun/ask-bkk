# 俞桂庭

Yú Guìtíng 俞桂庭 (Qīng), source of the *dictum* "medical principle is deep and subtle; of one hundred who study it, ten will succeed; of those, one will master it" cited in the *Chóngkān* preface of [[KR3ep085]] *Wángshì yīàn sānbiān*.
