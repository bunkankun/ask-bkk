Lǚ Wàng 呂望 — better known as Jiāng Shàng 姜尚, Tàigōng Wàng 太公望, or Jiāng Zǐyá 姜子牙 — was the legendary chief military strategist of King Wén 文 and King Wǔ 武 of Zhōu, central to the Zhōu founding (ca. 1046 BCE). His traditional dates are conventionally 1100–1015 BCE, but historicity is contested. Founder of the state of Qí 齊. Traditional author of the *Liù tāo* 六韜 ([[KR3b0002]]) — the canonical military classic — and *Tàigōng* 太公 in the *Hàn shū yìwén zhì*. The received *Liù tāo* is universally regarded as a much later compilation (Warring States or Hàn) attributed to Tàigōng under the standard pre-Qín pseudepigraphic convention.
