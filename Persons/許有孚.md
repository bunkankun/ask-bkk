Xǔ Yǒu-fú 許有孚 (Yuán, fl. 1341; survived into the Hóng-wǔ era — preface dated *tú-wéi zuò-è* = Hóng-wǔ 2 [1369] *èr-yuè*), younger brother and editor of the works of Xǔ Yǒu-rén [[許有壬]]. Compiled *Guī-táng xiǎo-gǎo* [[KR4d0509]] from his own carried-over copies (the master collection *Zhì-zhèng jí* having been lost when their nephew Xǔ Zhēn 許楨, *Tài-cháng bó-shì*, was driven south under emergency conditions and abandoned his books); the recension preserved the *zhì-zhèng-géng-zǐ* (1360) brother-edition that Xǔ Yǒu-rén himself had self-edited but not yet printed, plus 85 prose pieces sent in correspondence, plus 93 further pieces from various sources. He also wrote a *bā* in 1369 documenting the collection's survival. CBDB gives no firm dates; the 1369 *bá* date is the latest absolute marker.
