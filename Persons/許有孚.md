Xǔ Yǒufú 許有孚 (Yuán, fl. 1341; survived into the Hóngwǔ era — preface dated *túwéi zuòè* = Hóngwǔ 2 [1369] *èryuè*), younger brother and editor of the works of Xǔ Yǒurén [[許有壬]]. Compiled *Guītáng xiǎogǎo* [[KR4d0509]] from his own carried-over copies (the master collection *Zhìzhèng jí* having been lost when their nephew Xǔ Zhēn 許楨, *Tàicháng bóshì*, was driven south under emergency conditions and abandoned his books); the recension preserved the *zhìzhènggéngzǐ* (1360) brother-edition that Xǔ Yǒurén himself had self-edited but not yet printed, plus 85 prose pieces sent in correspondence, plus 93 further pieces from various sources. He also wrote a *bā* in 1369 documenting the collection's survival. CBDB gives no firm dates; the 1369 *bá* date is the latest absolute marker.
