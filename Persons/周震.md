**Zhōu Zhèn** 周震 (*hào* Shèn-zhāi 慎齋, *fl.* late Míng / early Qīng, mid-17th century), paediatrician active in the *Shùn-zhì* — *Kāng-xī* transitional era. His principal work *Yòu-kē zhǐ-nán* 幼科指南 ([[KR3ej016]], also known as *Yòu-kē yī-xué zhǐ-nán* 幼科醫學指南) is conventionally dated to *Shùn-zhì* 18 (1661) or *Kāng-xī* 18 (1679); it was first printed in 1789 (*Qián-lóng* 54) by the Yí-xīng Dào-shēng-táng 宜興道生堂 publishing house. The work consists of four *juǎn* covering paediatric verse mnemonics, miscellaneous disorders, and the five *zàng-meridian* paediatric disorders. Zhōu's *Hǔ-kǒu mài-wén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 (Verses on the Tiger's-mouth Pulse-Pattern) is a notable supplement to the conventional *sān-guān zhǐ-wén* 三關指紋 finger-vein diagnostic doctrine. His clinical writing is conventionally praised for balance and *píng-zhèng* 平正 (moderation) in prescription. Lifedates and native place are not securely recorded.
