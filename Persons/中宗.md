Lǐ Xiǎn 李顯 (656–710), posthumously titled Emperor Zhōngzōng 中宗 of Táng. Third son of Emperor Gāozōng 高宗 and Wǔ Zétiān 武則天; reigned briefly in 684 before being deposed by his mother, and again from 705 to 710 after the *Shénlóng* 神龍 coup that restored the Táng. During his second reign he patronized Buddhist translation projects, including [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci's *Mahāratnakūṭa* assemblage; his preface to that work, together with Ruìzōng's, marks the Bodhiruci team's status at the *Chóngfúsì* 崇福寺.
