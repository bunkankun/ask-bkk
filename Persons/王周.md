Wáng Zhōu 王周 (active mid-14th c.), son of [[王冕]] **Wáng Miǎn** 王冕 (c. 1287–1359). Native of **Zhū-jì** 諸暨 (Zhèjiāng). Compiler of his father's verse collection [[KR4e0061]] *Zhú-zhāi jí* 竹齋集 in three *juǎn*; **Liú Jī** 劉基 contributed the preface. **Lǚ Shēng** 呂升 wrote a *Wáng Zhōu xíng-zhuàng* 王周行狀 of Wáng Zhōu himself, preserved in the *xù-jí* of *Zhú-zhāi jí* (compiled by Wáng Miǎn's great-granddaughter's-son **Luò Jū-jìng** 駱居敬). CBDB returns several Yuán-Míng homonyms (id 19905 etc.); the present figure is documented only as Wáng Miǎn's son and his collection's compiler — no other career details are transmitted.
