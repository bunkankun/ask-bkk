Dà-yuè 大悅, *zì* **Tiān-yī** 天一 ("Heaven-One"); often referred to as **Tiān-yī Dà-yuè** 天一大悅 or **Sōng-kuī Tiān-yī** 松巋天一. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shàn-quán Dá-wèi** [[達位]] (1619–1684). Born **1631** (estimated); died **康熙丁丑孟冬** = **1697-11-14 to 1697-12-12** (lunar 10), age 67. "*Wú-miè hòu bù-láo pán-xīn fèi-shì, shēng-zàng kě yě*" — final words: "After my passing, do not waste effort on fuel and ceremony — burial alive (i.e. without cremation) is enough."

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0581]] 《善權位禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B469.
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0582]] 《天一悅禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B470, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Xué-yú** [[學愚]].

**Sources.** 《天一悅禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉.
