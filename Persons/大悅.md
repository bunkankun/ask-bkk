Dàyuè 大悅, *zì* **Tiānyī** 天一 ("Heaven-One"); often referred to as **Tiānyī Dàyuè** 天一大悅 or **Sōngkuī Tiānyī** 松巋天一. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Shànquán Dáwèi** [[達位]] (1619–1684). Born **1631** (estimated); died **康熙丁丑孟冬** = **1697-11-14 to 1697-12-12** (lunar 10), age 67. "*Wúmiè hòu bùláo pánxīn fèishì, shēngzàng kě yě*" — final words: "After my passing, do not waste effort on fuel and ceremony — burial alive (i.e. without cremation) is enough."

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0581]] 《善權位禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B469.
- Own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0582]] 《天一悅禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B470, recorded by *fǎsì* **Xuéyú** [[學愚]].

**Sources.** 《天一悅禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉.
