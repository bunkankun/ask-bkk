# Xú Lín 徐琳

Late-Míng official, Huátíng 華亭 native and prefect of Chǔxióngfǔ; supplied the 1607 *Chóngkè Sìjiā píngchàng xù* preface to the [[KR6q0079]] *Cóngróng lù* reprint at the request of [[覺虛]] Juéxū.
