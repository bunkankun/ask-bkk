# Shíxián 實賢 / Xǐng'ān 省庵 (1685–1734)

A leading early-Qīng Pure Land master, conventionally counted as the **Eleventh Patriarch** of the orthodox Chinese Pure Land lineage. *Hào* **Xǐng'ān** 省庵 / 省菴 ("Reflection-Hut"); *zì* **Sīqí** 思齊. Native of **Cháng-shú** 常熟 (Jiāngsū); lay surname Shí 時. Born in 康熙 24 (1685); died in 雍正 12 (1734) at age 50, *séng-là* 25. He was active principally at **Fèng-shān** 鳳山 in Hángzhōu and at **Méi-fēng-sì** 梅峰寺 in Cháng-shú.

His major Pure Land works, all preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng*, are the **《淨土生無生論註》** *Jìng-tǔ shēng wú-shēng lùn zhù* — his sub-commentary on [[傳燈]] Yōuxī Chuándēng's *Lùn* [[KR6p0056]]; the **《西方發願文註》** *Xī-fāng fā-yuàn wén zhù* [[KR6p0087]], his commentary on [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng's *Xī-fāng yuàn-wén* [[KR6p0064]]; the **《東海若解》** *Dōng-hǎi ruò jiě* [[KR6p0097]], his exegetical work on the *Dōng-hǎi ruò* of [[柳子厚]] Liǔ Zǐ-hòu (Liǔ Zōngyuán); and his recorded sayings — the **《省菴法師語錄》** *Xǐng'ān fǎshī yǔ-lù* [[KR6p0098]] — edited by [[彭際清]] **Péng Jì-qīng** and his lay-Buddhist circle.

His most famous individual composition is the **《勸發菩提心文》** *Quàn fā pú-tí xīn wén* ("Essay Exhorting [the Generation of] Bodhicitta"), a brief but highly influential pastoral essay on the cultivation of *bodhicitta* in the Pure Land tradition that has remained in continuous active use as a standard pastoral text in Chinese Pure Land monasticism through the late-imperial and Republican periods to the present.
