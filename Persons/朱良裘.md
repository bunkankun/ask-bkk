# Zhū Liángqiú 朱良裘

Mid-Qīng *kǎozhèng* scholar and Sìkù-period editor, attached to the editorial committee of the *Shísān jīng zhùshū* imperial reissue (1747, Qiánlóng 12) commissioned in conjunction with Qiánlóng's renewed sponsorship of the canonical sub-commentaries. Co-author with [[陳浩]] (Chén Hào) and [[李清植]] (Lǐ Qīngzhí) of the *kǎozhèng* 考證 (textual-critical) apparatus appended to the *Zhōuyì zhèngyì* in the WYG / Sìkù recension (= part of [[KR1a0007]]).

Biographical particulars are not in the standard biographical references; the name appears only in editorial-attribution capacity in Sìkù-period imperial-publication signatures.
