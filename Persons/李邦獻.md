Lǐ Bāngxiàn 李邦獻 (Sòng) was a Northern–Southern Sòng transition official, younger brother of *Tài-zǎi* Lǐ Bāngyàn 李邦彥 (the chief minister of the late Huīzōng / early Qīnzōng). Native of Huái-zhōu 懷州 (Hé-nán). Held office to *Zhí Fū-wén-gé* 直敷文閣. Composer of the *Shěng-xīn zá-yán* 省心雜言 (KR3a0038) — a *biji* of moral aphorisms — which was, on the SKQS *tíyào*'s correction of the prevailing misattributions, in fact his work; misattributed in pre-SKQS tradition variously to Lín Bū 林逋 (Northern Sòng poet, *hào* Hé-jìng 和靖) and Yǐn Tūn 尹焞 (whose *hào* also resembles Lín Bū's), or to Shěn Dào-yuán 沈道原 (per Wáng Bì's compilation of Zhū Xī's *yǔ-lù* supplements). The *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*-preserved Sòng full text contains five prefaces (Qí Kuān 祁寬, Zhèng Wàng-zhī 鄭望之, Shěn Jùn 沈濬, Wāng Yīng-chén 汪應辰, Wáng Dà-shí 王大實) and three colophons (Mǎ Zǎo 馬藻, Xiàng Ān-shì 項安世, Lè Zhāng 樂章), plus three further colophons by Lǐ Bāngxiàn's grandson Lǐ Qí-gāng 耆岡 and his fourth-generation descendant Lǐ Jǐng-chū 景初 — all attributing the work to Lǐ Bāngxiàn and refuting the Lín Bū attribution; Lǐ Qí-gāng explicitly says he had seen the manuscript draft.
