Chǔ Dàwén 儲大文 (1665–1743), *zì* Liùyǎ 六雅, *hào* Huàshān 畫山, native of Yíxīng 宜興 (ChángZhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 60 (1721), at the relatively advanced age of 57; entered the Hanlin and rose to *shùjíshì* 庶吉士. He is one of the most accomplished historical-geographers of the early eighteenth century, recognised as such by the *Sìkù tíyào* of [[KR2k0049]] (where the editors note his particular versatility in *dìlǐ zhī xué* 地理之學). His own *Cún yánlóu jí* 存研樓集 (24 *juan*) collects his geographical and historical scholarship and was itself given a *Sìkù* entry. As principal *biānzuǎn* of the Yōngzhèng-era *Shānxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0049]]), he wrote the work's *fánlì* (general guidelines) and is identified by the *Sìkù* editors as the hand whose geographical training produced the work's distinctive *shānchuān xíngshì* sections.
