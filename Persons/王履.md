**Wáng Lǚ** 王履 (*zì* Āndào 安道, born 1332, *fl.* late Yuán to early Míng), Yuán-period physician of Kūn-shān 崑山 (modern Jiāngsū). Studied medicine with [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng of Jīn-huá, fully inheriting his teacher's transmission. Lived into the early Míng (Hóngwǔ period); the *Míng shǐ Fāng-jì zhuàn* records him among Míng practitioners although his work was completed in the Yuán. Author of the ***Yī jīng sù-huí jí*** 醫經溯洄集 ([[KR3e0066]], 2 juan, 21 essays) — a critical philological-and-doctrinal work on the *Shānghán* tradition, with particular focus on the textual integrity of the 397-method (三百九十七法) classification of the *Shānghán lùn* and the discrimination of internal-vs-external damage. Also a notable Yuán painter and poet; *Jiǔ líng shānfáng jí* contains references to his artistic-and-literary work alongside his medical career.
