Hú Jìtáng 胡季堂 (1729–1800), Qiánlóng-period official; son of [[胡煦]] Hú Xù (1655–1736). After the loss of his father's 99-juàn original *Zhōuyì hán shū* manuscript through pupil [[李學裕]] Lǐ Xuéyù's premature death, Hú Jìtáng reconstructed the surviving material into the three component works that became the *Sìkù*-preserved Hú Xù *Yì*-corpus, including [[KR1a0145]] *Zhōuyì hán shū yuē cún*.
