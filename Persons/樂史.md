Yuè Shǐ (930–1007; *zì* Zhèngzǐ 正子, also given as Zǐzhèng 子正), a native of Yízhōu 宜州 (modern Yízhōu, Jiāngxī, sometimes Línchuān 臨川), was a transitional Wǔdài–Sòng official and writer. He served the Southern Táng before submitting to the Sòng in 975; in the early Tàipíng Xīngguó era he passed the *jìnshì* and entered Sòng service, rising to *Tàicháng bóshì* (erudite of the Court of Imperial Sacrifices) and *Zhí shǐguǎn* (erudite of the Office of Historiography). He is best known as the compiler of the *Tàipíng huányǔ jì* [[KR2k0004]], the first comprehensive Sòng empire-wide gazetteer; he was also a prolific compiler of biographical and anecdotal works, including the *Guǎng zhuóyì jì* 廣卓異記, the *Yáng tàizhēn wàizhuàn* 楊太眞外傳, and many others. His biography is in *Sòngshǐ Wényuàn zhuàn* 宋史文苑傳 306. CBDB id 4141 (1023–1101 row removed; this Yuè Shǐ is the 930–1007 entry).
