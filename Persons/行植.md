Xíngzhí 行植, *zì* **Tiānshù** 天樹 ("Heavenly Tree"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Mùyún Tōngmén** 牧雲通門 (1599–1671). Born **崇禎壬申 12/14** = **1633-01-23 Gregorian**; died **康熙19 10** = **1680-11-21/12-20 Gregorian**, age **49**. Native of **Yúshān** 虞山 (Chángshú, Sūzhōu prefecture); lay surname **Liú** 劉.

**Formation.** At 15 chose to leave home; at 19 (1651) tonsured under **Pòshān Màiāndé** 破山邁菴德. **辛卯秋 = 1651/秋** met **Mùyún** 牧雲. Full precepts **丙申冬 = 1656** under **Yáofēng Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 堯峰費隱通容. Subsequent training under **Shíqí** 石奇 at Nánguǎng (awakening on a flour-grinding incident); **Fúyán Róng**; **Píngyáng Mín**. **辛亥冬 = 1671** Mùyún's death; received transmission and succession at **Hèlín** 鶴林 (Jīngkǒu, the title-form 鶴林天樹).

**Heir.** **Tiānfú Zhànyòu** [[湛祐]] 天孚湛祐.

**Work.** [[KR6q0522]] 《鶴林天樹植禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B399, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhànyòu** [[湛祐]] *biān*; juan 1 *xíngshí*.
