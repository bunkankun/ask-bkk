Chāozhì 超智 (also 超志), *zì* **Tiānmù** 天目 (also catalog-form **Qírán** 奇然). Born **明天啟六 = 1626/01/28–1627/02/15 Gregorian**; alive in **1685** (康熙24, age 60). Native of **Sōngjiāng Qīngpǔ** 松江青浦; lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zìxián Xíngjué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (1611–1662) at [[KR6q0469]] line — one of the 33 Xíngjué heirs.

**Formation.** At 12 mother died; raised by paternal grandmother. Visited **Jīnsù Tōngróng** 金粟容. At 22 father died; tonsured at **Jiāshàn Shāndūnān** 嘉善山墩菴. Studied under **Tōngxuán [Línyě Tōng-]qí** 通玄奇 at **Qīzhēnsì** 棲真寺 (= [[覺]]'s teacher Línyě Tōngqí); then with **Fúróng [Zìxián] Jué** 芙蓉覺 at **Luóhànsì** 羅漢寺 with formal *jìbié*.

**Three abbacies.** **Yǒngjiā Tiānwángsì** 永嘉天王寺; **Ruìān Xiānyánsì** 瑞安仙巖寺; **Kūnyáng Bǎoxīngsì** 昆陽寶興諸寺.

**Heirs.** **Zhōngān Zhì** 中安質; **Fànyuǎn Yàn** 範遠彥; **Zhuórán Lì** 卓然立 (and others).

**Work.** [[KR6q0495]] 《奇然智禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B356, compiled by *fǎsì* **Mínglín** [[明林]] + **Míngcàn** [[明燦]] *děng biān*. (DILA-standardized title would be 《天目智禪師語錄》; the Jiāxīng catalog uses the alternate *hào* 奇然.) Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 98; 《仙巖仙志》 j. 2.
