Chāo-zhì 超智 (also 超志), *zì* **Tiān-mù** 天目 (also catalog-form **Qí-rán** 奇然). Born **明天啟六 = 1626/01/28–1627/02/15 Gregorian**; alive in **1685** (康熙24, age 60). Native of **Sōng-jiāng Qīng-pǔ** 松江青浦; lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zì-xián Xíng-jué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (1611–1662) at [[KR6q0469]] line — one of the 33 Xíng-jué heirs.

**Formation.** At 12 mother died; raised by paternal grandmother. Visited **Jīn-sù Tōng-róng** 金粟容. At 22 father died; tonsured at **Jiā-shàn Shān-dūn-ān** 嘉善山墩菴. Studied under **Tōng-xuán [Lín-yě Tōng-]qí** 通玄奇 at **Qī-zhēn-sì** 棲真寺 (= [[覺]]'s teacher Lín-yě Tōng-qí); then with **Fú-róng [Zì-xián] Jué** 芙蓉覺 at **Luó-hàn-sì** 羅漢寺 with formal *jì-bié*.

**Three abbacies.** **Yǒng-jiā Tiān-wáng-sì** 永嘉天王寺; **Ruì-ān Xiān-yán-sì** 瑞安仙巖寺; **Kūn-yáng Bǎo-xīng-sì** 昆陽寶興諸寺.

**Heirs.** **Zhōng-ān Zhì** 中安質; **Fàn-yuǎn Yàn** 範遠彥; **Zhuó-rán Lì** 卓然立 (and others).

**Work.** [[KR6q0495]] 《奇然智禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B356, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Míng-lín** [[明林]] + **Míng-càn** [[明燦]] *děng biān*. (DILA-standardized title would be 《天目智禪師語錄》; the Jiā-xīng catalog uses the alternate *hào* 奇然.) Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 98; 《仙巖仙志》 j. 2.
