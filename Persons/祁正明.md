# 祁正明

Qí Zhèngmíng 祁正明, early-Qīng Sūzhōu physician of the Yè-school clinical circle, contemporary of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì. Some of his cases are preserved in the appendix to [[KR3ep079]] *Yèshì yīàn cúnzhēn*.
