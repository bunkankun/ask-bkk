Cáo Cúnxīn 曹存心 (*zì* Rénbó 仁伯, *hào* Yuècūn 樂存; 1767–1834, CBDB 88559) — Chángshú 常熟 (Jiāngsū) physician of the early nineteenth century, conventionally treated as one of the principal "Sūzhōu masters" of the post-Yè-Tiān-shì tradition. His teaching at his *Liúyīngtáng* 留影堂 studio at Chángshú attracted leading pupils from across Jiāngnán — including Wáng Xùgāo 王旭高 ([[王旭高]]) — and his clinical synthesis, heavy on the *jīngluò* 經絡 / *jīnyè* 津液 framework and on the use of light moistening prescriptions, was foundational for the late-Qīng *wēnbìng* tradition. His principal works are [[KR3ep025]] *Cáo Rénbó yīàn lùn* 曹仁伯醫案論 (discussion-style casebook), *Liúyīng yīàn* 留影醫案 (version anthologised in [[KR3ep021]] by [[柳寶詒]] Liǔ Bǎoyí), [[KR3ep069]] *Cáo Rénbó yīàn*, and [[KR3ep095]] *Yánlíng dìzǐ jìyào* 延陵弟子紀要 (record of his teaching to his disciples). For modern treatment see Scheid 2007.
