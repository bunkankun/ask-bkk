Cáo Cún-xīn 曹存心 (*zì* Rén-bó 仁伯, *hào* Yuè-cūn 樂存; 1767–1834, CBDB 88559) — Cháng-shú 常熟 (Jiāngsū) physician of the early nineteenth century, conventionally treated as one of the principal "Sūzhōu masters" of the post-Yè-Tiān-shì tradition. His teaching at his *Liú-yīng-táng* 留影堂 studio at Cháng-shú attracted leading pupils from across Jiāng-nán — including Wáng Xù-gāo 王旭高 ([[王旭高]]) — and his clinical synthesis, heavy on the *jīng-luò* 經絡 / *jīn-yè* 津液 framework and on the use of light moistening prescriptions, was foundational for the late-Qīng *wēn-bìng* tradition. His principal works are [[KR3ep025]] *Cáo Rén-bó yī-àn lùn* 曹仁伯醫案論 (discussion-style casebook), *Liú-yīng yī-àn* 留影醫案 (version anthologised in [[KR3ep021]] by [[柳寶詒]] Liǔ Bǎo-yí), [[KR3ep069]] *Cáo Rén-bó yī-àn*, and [[KR3ep095]] *Yán-líng dì-zǐ jì-yào* 延陵弟子紀要 (record of his teaching to his disciples). For modern treatment see Scheid 2007.
