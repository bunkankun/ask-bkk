Early-Southern-Sòng official, grandson of [[陳瓘]] Chén Guàn. He printed his grandfather's *Liǎozhāi Yì shuō* 了齋易說 ([[KR1a0018]]) in the *Shàoxīng* period (1131–1162) — the recension witnessed by [[胡一桂]] Hú Yīguì and the basis of all later transmission of the work.
