Líng Gàn 凌淦, *zì* Zhòngqīng 仲清, *hào* Lìshēng 礪生 (late *hào* Tuìān 退庵), 1833–1895 (CBDB 87718), native of Xīntǎzhèn 莘塔鎮 in Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū). *Xiánfēng jǐwèi* (1859) *jǔrén*, candidate for *nèibù zhèngláng* 內部正郎 (Ministry of the Interior). A gentry-scholar / physician working in the Sūzhōu Yè Tiānshì lineage; studied under **Lǐ Língshòu** 李齡壽 (字 Xīntuó 辛坨) and his clan elder **Lǐ Jiāliù** 李嘉六. His fragmentary single-*juǎn* casebook survives as [[KR3ep053]] *Tuìān yīàn*, transcribed in 1896 by his Wújiāng colleague Shěn Chénlín 沈陳麟 (Nángyǐn dàorén 囊隱道人).
