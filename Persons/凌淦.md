Líng Gàn 凌淦, *zì* Zhòng-qīng 仲清, *hào* Lì-shēng 礪生 (late *hào* Tuì-ān 退庵), 1833–1895 (CBDB 87718), native of Xīn-tǎ-zhèn 莘塔鎮 in Wú-jiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū). *Xián-fēng jǐ-wèi* (1859) *jǔ-rén*, candidate for *nèi-bù zhèng-láng* 內部正郎 (Ministry of the Interior). A gentry-scholar / physician working in the Sūzhōu Yè Tiān-shì lineage; studied under **Lǐ Líng-shòu** 李齡壽 (字 Xīn-tuó 辛坨) and his clan elder **Lǐ Jiā-liù** 李嘉六. His fragmentary single-*juǎn* casebook survives as [[KR3ep053]] *Tuì-ān yī-àn*, transcribed in 1896 by his Wú-jiāng colleague Shěn Chén-lín 沈陳麟 (Náng-yǐn dào-rén 囊隱道人).
