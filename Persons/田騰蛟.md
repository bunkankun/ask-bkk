Tián Téngjiao 田騰蛟 was a Republican-era popular fiction writer, author of *Yuándài Yěshǐ* 元代野史 ([[KR4k0330]]), a 100-chapter historical novel on the Yuán dynasty, focusing on Han Chinese resistance to Mongol rule. No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1910s–1940s.
