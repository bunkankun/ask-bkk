Hánshì 函昰 (also written 函是 or 圅昰), *hào* **Tiānrán** 天然 ("Spontaneously So"), with sobriquets **Lìzhōng** 麗中 and **Léifēng Lǎorén** 雷峯老人 ("Old Man of Léifēng"). Cáodòng 34th-generation dharma-heir of **Chángqìng Kōngyǐn Dàodú** 長慶空隱道獨, dharma-grandson of **Bóshān Wúyì Yuánlái** 博山無異元來. Born **明萬曆戊申 10/14 亥時** = **1608-11-21 Gregorian**; died **清康熙乙丑 8/27 酉時** = **1685-09-25 Gregorian**, age 78. Native of **Pānyú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu); lay surname **Zēng** 曾.

**Formation.** Lay scholar background. At 7 sat in seclusion and was overcome by an existential awakening of the question "Whence have I come?" — the experience recurred at 9. At 13, while reading the *Yìjīng* on *tàijí* generating the two principles, he could not locate where *tàijí* itself rested, and his earlier doubts revived. Successful in the provincial examination (*xiāngjiàn*); fell ill on the boat at Jízhōu and vowed to renounce the world if he recovered. From then on no meat or wine, no inner-quarters, intensive *cānjiū*. Heard the *Diamond Sūtra* line "*yīng wú suǒ zhù ér shēng qí xīn*" 應無所住而生其心 and broke through. Tonsured under **Kōngyǐn Dàodú** 空隱道獨 at **Lúshān Guīzōng** 廬山歸宗 in **崇禎己卯** = **1639**. Two years later accompanied his teacher to **Huáshǒu** 華首 and was raised to *lìsēng*. **崇禎壬午** = **1642** opened the dharma at **Guǎngzhōu Hēlín** 廣州訶林.

**Abbacies.** **Léifēng Hǎizhuàng** 雷峰海幢 (Guǎngzhōu), **Huáshǒu Jièān** 華首芥菴, **Lúshān Qīxián** 廬山棲賢, **Lúshān Guīzōng** 廬山歸宗, **Sháozhōu Dānxiá** 韶州丹霞, **Hēlín** 訶林.

**Disciples.** Major dharma-heirs include **Jīnshì Dànguī** 今釋澹歸 (the poet-monk and historian), **Jīnbiàn Lèshuō** 今辯樂說 [[今辯]] (compiler of his *yǔlù*), **Jīnzhuǎn** 今轉, **Jīngèn Lù Shìkǎi** 今亙陸世楷 (lay), and many others — the cohort with the generation-character "Jīn" 今 forms the second generation of Tiānrán's Hǎiyún 海雲 lineage that dominated Lǐngnán late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhism.

**Works.** [[KR6q0526]] 《廬山天然禪師語錄》 12 juan, J38 B406, *zhòngbiān* by **Jīnbiàn** [[今辯]], printed 1670. Several earlier sub-corpus *yǔlù* from individual abbacies are mentioned in the prefaces (Léifēng, Qīxián, Huáshǒu, Hēlín).
