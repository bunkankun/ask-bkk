Hán-shì 函昰 (also written 函是 or 圅昰), *hào* **Tiān-rán** 天然 ("Spontaneously So"), with sobriquets **Lì-zhōng** 麗中 and **Léi-fēng Lǎo-rén** 雷峯老人 ("Old Man of Léi-fēng"). Cáodòng 34th-generation dharma-heir of **Cháng-qìng Kōng-yǐn Dào-dú** 長慶空隱道獨, dharma-grandson of **Bó-shān Wú-yì Yuán-lái** 博山無異元來. Born **明萬曆戊申 10/14 亥時** = **1608-11-21 Gregorian**; died **清康熙乙丑 8/27 酉時** = **1685-09-25 Gregorian**, age 78. Native of **Pān-yú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu); lay surname **Zēng** 曾.

**Formation.** Lay scholar background. At 7 sat in seclusion and was overcome by an existential awakening of the question "Whence have I come?" — the experience recurred at 9. At 13, while reading the *Yìjīng* on *tài-jí* generating the two principles, he could not locate where *tài-jí* itself rested, and his earlier doubts revived. Successful in the provincial examination (*xiāng-jiàn*); fell ill on the boat at Jí-zhōu and vowed to renounce the world if he recovered. From then on no meat or wine, no inner-quarters, intensive *cān-jiū*. Heard the *Diamond Sūtra* line "*yīng wú suǒ zhù ér shēng qí xīn*" 應無所住而生其心 and broke through. Tonsured under **Kōng-yǐn Dào-dú** 空隱道獨 at **Lú-shān Guī-zōng** 廬山歸宗 in **崇禎己卯** = **1639**. Two years later accompanied his teacher to **Huá-shǒu** 華首 and was raised to *lì-sēng*. **崇禎壬午** = **1642** opened the dharma at **Guǎng-zhōu Hē-lín** 廣州訶林.

**Abbacies.** **Léi-fēng Hǎi-zhuàng** 雷峰海幢 (Guǎngzhōu), **Huá-shǒu Jiè-ān** 華首芥菴, **Lú-shān Qī-xián** 廬山棲賢, **Lú-shān Guī-zōng** 廬山歸宗, **Sháo-zhōu Dān-xiá** 韶州丹霞, **Hē-lín** 訶林.

**Disciples.** Major dharma-heirs include **Jīn-shì Dàn-guī** 今釋澹歸 (the poet-monk and historian), **Jīn-biàn Lè-shuō** 今辯樂說 [[今辯]] (compiler of his *yǔ-lù*), **Jīn-zhuǎn** 今轉, **Jīn-gèn Lù Shì-kǎi** 今亙陸世楷 (lay), and many others — the cohort with the generation-character "Jīn" 今 forms the second generation of Tiān-rán's Hǎi-yún 海雲 lineage that dominated Lǐng-nán late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhism.

**Works.** [[KR6q0526]] 《廬山天然禪師語錄》 12 juan, J38 B406, *zhòng-biān* by **Jīn-biàn** [[今辯]], printed 1670. Several earlier sub-corpus *yǔ-lù* from individual abbacies are mentioned in the prefaces (Léi-fēng, Qī-xián, Huá-shǒu, Hē-lín).

**Secondary literature.** 時志明 (2006) 〈論詩僧函昰的山水詩〉, *Sháo-guān Xué-yuàn xué-bào* 27.2; 馮煥珍 (2003) 〈天然函昰禪師的悟道因緣及其禪教並重的宗趣觀〉, *Xiàn-dài zhé-xué* 2; Araki Kengo / Liào Zhào-hēng tr. (2006) 《明末清初的思想與佛教》, Táiběi: Liánjīng, p. 124.
