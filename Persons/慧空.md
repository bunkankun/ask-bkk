# Xuěfēng Huìkōng 雪峰慧空

Northern-Sòng-to-Southern-Sòng transitional Línjì-Yángqí Chán master (1096–1158); abbot of Xuěfēngsì 雪峰寺 in Fúzhōu 福州. Dharma-heir of [[文準|Zhàntáng Wénzhǔn]] 湛堂文準 in the Línjì line.
