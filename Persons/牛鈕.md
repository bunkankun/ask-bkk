Niú Niǔ 牛鈕 (d. 1737), was a Kāngxī-period 康熙 court official and compiler. He was active in the imperial diary-lecture (*rì jiǎng* 日講) office from the late 1670s, and in *Kāngxī* 19 (1680) was commanded — together with Sūn Zàifēng 孫在豐 ([[孫在豐]]) — to compile the *Rì jiǎng Yìjīng jiěyì* 日講易經解義 ([[KR1a0116]]) in eighteen juàn, the principal early-Kāngxī imperial *Yìjīng* commentary. The work was completed and presented in *Kāngxī* 22 (1684). His other roles included compilation work on the *Mǎn-zhōu shílù* 滿洲實錄 and other imperial historiographic projects.

CBDB id 72293 has death year 1737; birth year is undocumented. He held the imperial degree status of *jìnshì* and rose to senior Hànlín positions in the late Kāngxī period.
