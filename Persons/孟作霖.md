**Mèng Zuòlín** 孟作霖 (*zì* Yǔsūn 雨蓀, studio-name *Jìshìxuān* 濟世軒), late-Qīng paediatrician. His preface to the recovered manuscript copy of *Yòukē zhézhōng mìchuán zhēnběn* 幼科折衷秘傳真本 ([[KR3ej034]]) is dated *Guāngxù* 18 *rénchén* 壬辰 = 1892 *zhuàngyuè* 壯月 (eighth lunar month). The preface explains that the original *Yòukē zhézhōng* of Qín Chāngyù 秦昌遇 was effectively destroyed in print after the *Yuèfěi* 粵匪 (Tàipíng) disturbances, and that Mèng's family-transmission of paediatric medicine, lasting many years, led him to recopy a borrowed manuscript-witness for preservation. The preface includes a touching cautionary note to descendants — *yí zūn zhī, zhòng zhī, jiǔ ér cáng zhī, qiè wù jiè yú wàirén* 宜尊之、重之、久而藏之，切勿借於外人 (revere it, treasure it, keep it long, never lend it to outsiders).
