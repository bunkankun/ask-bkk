**Mèng Zuò-lín** 孟作霖 (*zì* Yǔ-sūn 雨蓀, studio-name *Jì-shì-xuān* 濟世軒), late-Qīng paediatrician. His preface to the recovered manuscript copy of *Yòu-kē zhé-zhōng mì-chuán zhēn-běn* 幼科折衷秘傳真本 ([[KR3ej034]]) is dated *Guāng-xù* 18 *rén-chén* 壬辰 = 1892 *zhuàng-yuè* 壯月 (eighth lunar month). The preface explains that the original *Yòu-kē zhé-zhōng* of Qín Chāng-yù 秦昌遇 was effectively destroyed in print after the *Yuè-fěi* 粵匪 (Tài-píng) disturbances, and that Mèng's family-transmission of paediatric medicine, lasting many years, led him to recopy a borrowed manuscript-witness for preservation. The preface includes a touching cautionary note to descendants — *yí zūn zhī, zhòng zhī, jiǔ ér cáng zhī, qiè wù jiè yú wài-rén* 宜尊之、重之、久而藏之，切勿借於外人 (revere it, treasure it, keep it long, never lend it to outsiders).
