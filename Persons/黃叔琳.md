Huáng Shūlín 黃叔琳 (1672–1756), *zì* Kūnpǔ 崑圃, *hào* Běiyànzhāi 北硯齋, of Dàxīng 大興 (Beijing). CBDB id 57278; lifedates firm.

*Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 30 (1691, *xīnwèi*), entering the Hànlín. Long career through the late-Kāngxī, Yōngzhèng, and into the early-Qiánlóng courts. Rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū* and *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* (Grand Secretary). One of the most prolific Qing literary editors and bibliographic-philologists: famous for his *Wénxīn diāolóng jí zhù* 文心雕龍輯註 (a major recension of Liú Xié's *Wén xīn diāo lóng* 文心雕龍 critical treatise, with synoptic annotation) and for his work on the various early-Qīng *Sìkù*-precursor projects.

In the present context, Huáng is the editor of the final 8-juan recension of [[姜宸英]] Jiāng Chényīng's *Zhànyuán jí* ([[KR4f0037]]), drawing on Jiāng's own preliminary *Zhànyuán wèidìng gǎo* and supplemented from manuscript materials gathered after Jiāng's death in prison. Huáng's editorial work is generally considered authoritative for early-Qīng *gǔwén* canon-formation.
