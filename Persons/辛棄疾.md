Southern-Sòng *cí*-master and patriot-statesman (1140–1207); *zì* Yòu-ān 幼安, *hào* Jià-xuān jūshì 稼軒居士 ("Recluse of the Plowman's Studio"), of Lì-chéng 歷城 (Jǐ-nán, Shān-dōng) — born in territory under Jīn occupation. The supreme master of the *háo-fàng* 豪放 ("heroic-and-roaming") line of the *cí* and, with Sū Shì [[蘇軾]], one of the two greatest *cí*-writers of the Sòng. Joined the Jīn-resisting insurgent army of Gěng Jīng 耿京 at age 22; in *Shào-xīng* 32 / 1162, with fifty horsemen, raided the Jīn camp to capture the defector Zhāng Ān-guó 張安國 and bring him alive to the Sòng court for execution — one of the iconic individual exploits of Sòng history. Joined the Sòng officialdom thereafter; served as Vice-Magistrate, Prefect, and *Ān-fǔ shǐ* across Hú-běi, Hú-nán, Jiāng-xī, and Zhè-jiāng through *Lóng-xīng*–*Chún-xī*; recurringly dismissed for proposing northern recovery; long forced retirement at his Shàng-ráo 上饒 *Jià-xuān* estate during *Chún-xī*–*Shào-xī* (the years of his greatest *cí* production). Briefly recalled under Hán Tuō-zhòu 韓侂胄's *Kāi-xǐ Běi-fá* preparations; died at Qiān-shān (Jiāng-xī) in *Kāi-xǐ* 3 / 1207. His *cí* corpus, in the *Jià-xuān cí* [[KR4j0040]] (4 *juǎn* Sìkù form, descending from the Sòng "Xìn-zhōu cut" of 12 *juǎn*), runs to around 626 pieces — the largest single-author Sòng *cí* corpus. Famous *cí* include *Yǒng-yù lè · Qiān-gǔ jiāng-shān* (Jīng-kǒu 1205, with closing *Píng shuí wèn, Lián Pō lǎo yǐ, shàng néng fàn fǒu*), *Mó yú ér · Gēng néng xiāo* (Hú-nán c. 1180), *Qīng-yù àn · Dōng-fēng yè fàng huā qiān shù* (the *Yuán-xī* piece), and the *Hè xīn láng* pieces exchanged with Chén Liàng [[陳亮]] at Goose-Lake 1188. Standard modern edition: Dèng Guǎng-míng 鄧廣銘, *Jià-xuān cí biān-nián jiān-zhù* 稼軒詞編年箋註 (Shàng-hǎi gǔ-jí, rev. 1993) and *Xīn Jià-xuān nián-pǔ* 辛稼軒年譜 (Shàng-hǎi rén-mín, rev. 1979).
