Southern-Sòng *cí*-master and patriot-statesman (1140–1207); *zì* Yòuān 幼安, *hào* Jiàxuān jūshì 稼軒居士 ("Recluse of the Plowman's Studio"), of Lìchéng 歷城 (Jǐnán, Shāndōng) — born in territory under Jīn occupation. The supreme master of the *háofàng* 豪放 ("heroic-and-roaming") line of the *cí* and, with Sū Shì [[蘇軾]], one of the two greatest *cí*-writers of the Sòng. Joined the Jīn-resisting insurgent army of Gěng Jīng 耿京 at age 22; in *Shàoxīng* 32 / 1162, with fifty horsemen, raided the Jīn camp to capture the defector Zhāng Ānguó 張安國 and bring him alive to the Sòng court for execution — one of the iconic individual exploits of Sòng history. Joined the Sòng officialdom thereafter; served as Vice-Magistrate, Prefect, and *Ānfǔ shǐ* across Húběi, Húnán, Jiāngxī, and Zhèjiāng through *Lóngxīng*–*Chúnxī*; recurringly dismissed for proposing northern recovery; long forced retirement at his Shàngráo 上饒 *Jiàxuān* estate during *Chúnxī*–*Shàoxī* (the years of his greatest *cí* production). Briefly recalled under Hán Tuōzhòu 韓侂胄's *Kāixǐ Běifá* preparations; died at Qiānshān (Jiāngxī) in *Kāixǐ* 3 / 1207. His *cí* corpus, in the *Jiàxuān cí* [[KR4j0040]] (4 *juǎn* Sìkù form, descending from the Sòng "Xìnzhōu cut" of 12 *juǎn*), runs to around 626 pieces — the largest single-author Sòng *cí* corpus. Famous *cí* include *Yǒngyù lè · Qiāngǔ jiāngshān* (Jīngkǒu 1205, with closing *Píng shuí wèn, Lián Pō lǎo yǐ, shàng néng fàn fǒu*), *Mó yú ér · Gēng néng xiāo* (Húnán c. 1180), *Qīngyù àn · Dōngfēng yè fàng huā qiān shù* (the *Yuánxī* piece), and the *Hè xīn láng* pieces exchanged with Chén Liàng [[陳亮]] at Goose-Lake 1188. Standard modern edition: Dèng Guǎngmíng 鄧廣銘, *Jiàxuān cí biānnián jiānzhù* 稼軒詞編年箋註 (Shànghǎi gǔjí, rev. 1993) and *Xīn Jiàxuān niánpǔ* 辛稼軒年譜 (Shànghǎi rénmín, rev. 1979).
