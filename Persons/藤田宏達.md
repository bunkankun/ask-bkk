# Fujita Kōtatsu 藤田宏達

Modern Japanese scholar of Indian and Pure Land Buddhism; author of *Genshi Jōdo shisō no kenkyū* 原始浄土思想の研究 (Iwanami shoten, 1970), the foundational study of early Pure Land thought.
