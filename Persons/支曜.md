Zhī Yào 支曜 (DILA Authority A000165) was a "Western Regions" (西域) monk active at Luòyáng under the late Later Hàn. According to the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 (T2145) and the *Gāosēng zhuàn* 高僧傳 (T2059), he arrived at Luòyáng in Zhōngpíng 中平 2 (185 CE), under Hàn Língdì 靈帝, and won renown in the capital for his scholarship. He is credited with some 10 translations in 11 fascicles, including the *Chéngjù guāngmíng dìngyì jīng* 成具光明定意經 (T630, an early *prajñāpāramitā* text), the *[[KR6a0046|Ānàlǜ bā-niàn jīng 阿那律八念經]]* (T46), the *Fó shuō mǎ yǒu bā-tài pìrén jīng* 佛說馬有八態譬人經 (T570), the *Xiǎo dàodì jīng* 小道地經 (T608), and the *Wén-chéng shí'èr yīnyuán jīng* 聞城十二因緣經. His exact dates of birth and death are unrecorded.
