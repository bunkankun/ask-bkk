# Wáng Yì 王廙

*Zì* Shìjiāng 世將. Eastern Jìn 東晉 court official, painter, and *Yì* scholar, native of Lángyé Línyí 琅邪臨沂 (modern Shāndōng); uncle of the calligrapher Wáng Xīzhī 王羲之. Author of a *Zhōuyì zhù* 周易註 in ten *juàn* (recorded in the *Suíshū jīngjí zhì*); the work is lost but is cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Jìnshū* (juan 76) gives his biography.
