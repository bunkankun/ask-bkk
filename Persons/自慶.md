Yuán-dynasty Tiāntái 天台 monk; sobriquet **Yún-wài** 雲外 (with full name **Yún-wài Zì-qìng** 雲外自慶, also referenced as **Yuán-jué Yún-wài** 圓覺雲外). DILA Authority A001985. Lifedates unrecorded.

Compiler of the **Zēng-xiū Jiào-yuàn qīng-guī** 增修教苑清規 ([[KR6d0244]], X57n0968, 2 *juan*) — a Yuán-period revision of the *qīng-guī* 清規 (pure-rules monastic regulations) for Tiāntái-school monasteries.
