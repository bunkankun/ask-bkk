**Bào Yúnlóng** 鮑雲龍 (1226–1296) was a Sòng-Yuán cosmological-diagrammatic scholar, author of [[KR5f0016]] ***Tiān yuán fā wēi*** 天原發微 — a treatise on cosmological origins drawing on *Yì jīng*, Shào Yōng, and Zhū Xī's cosmology.
