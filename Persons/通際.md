Tōng-jì 通際, *zì* **Shān-cí** 山茨 ("Mountain Thatch"), *bié-hào* **Dùn-sǒu** 鈍叟 ("Slow Old Man"); often referred to as **Shān-cí Tōng-jì** 山茨通際. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635) — one of the four founders of the **Qìng-shān** 磬山 sub-branch (alongside [[通琇]] **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù**, **Líng-gāo Tōng-yù**, [[通問]] **Ruò-ān Tōng-wèn**). Born **明萬曆戊申七月十一日** = **1608-08-20** Gregorian; died **乙酉夾鍾之八日** = **順治二年二月八日** = **1645-03-05**, age 38 (only 24 years as monk) — the youngest death among the four Qìng-shān founders. Native of **Tōng-zhōu** 通州 (Jiāng-sū); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Started as a *shā-mí* under **Jiàn-chuān Dūn** 鑑川敦. While listening to a lecture, was struck by the question "*yī kǒu qì bù lái, xiàng shén-me chù ān-shēn lì-mìng*" 一口氣不來向甚麼處安身立命. One night during *jīng-xíng* tripped — sudden insight. First *cān*-ed **Tiān-tóng Wù** 天童悟 (= Mì-yún) at **Jīn-sù**; later met **Qìng-shān Xiū** 磬山修 (= Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū) and was sealed at first sight.

**Abbacies.** **Háng-zhōu Dōng-míng chán-sì** 杭州府東明禪寺; **Nán-yuè Lǜ-luó-ān** 南嶽綠蘿菴; **Cháng-shā-fǔ Liú-yáng Nán-yuán chán-sì** 長沙府瀏陽南源禪寺.

**Major historiographical work.** Compiler of 《南嶽禪燈錄》 *Nán-yuè chán-dēng-lù* — drawing together 133 monks from the new and old gazetteers, an important Húnán Chán biographical anthology.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎ-sì* include **Ěr-zhān Zūn** 爾瞻尊 (= **Dá-zūn** [[達尊]], lead compiler of his *yǔ-lù*), **Xuán-cí Qiān** 玄慈謙 (= **Dá-qiān** [[達謙]]), **Mì-yán Gāng** 密巖剛, **Qiě-ān Chú** 且菴芻, **Zōng-xuán Zhǐ** 宗玄旨.

**Work.** [[KR6q0608]] 《山茨際禪師語錄》 4 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1649, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dá-zūn** [[達尊]] and **Dá-qiān** [[達謙]] *děng biān-cì*.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 68; 《正源略集》 vol. 2, vol. 4.
