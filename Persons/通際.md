Tōngjì 通際, *zì* **Shāncí** 山茨 ("Mountain Thatch"), *biéhào* **Dùnsǒu** 鈍叟 ("Slow Old Man"); often referred to as **Shāncí Tōngjì** 山茨通際. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635) — one of the four founders of the **Qìngshān** 磬山 sub-branch (alongside [[通琇]] **Yùlín Tōngxiù**, **Línggāo Tōngyù**, [[通問]] **Ruòān Tōngwèn**). Born **明萬曆戊申七月十一日** = **1608-08-20** Gregorian; died **乙酉夾鍾之八日** = **順治二年二月八日** = **1645-03-05**, age 38 (only 24 years as monk) — the youngest death among the four Qìngshān founders. Native of **Tōngzhōu** 通州 (Jiāngsū); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Started as a *shāmí* under **Jiànchuān Dūn** 鑑川敦. While listening to a lecture, was struck by the question "*yī kǒu qì bù lái, xiàng shén-me chù ānshēn lìmìng*" 一口氣不來向甚麼處安身立命. One night during *jīngxíng* tripped — sudden insight. First *cān*-ed **Tiāntóng Wù** 天童悟 (= Mìyún) at **Jīnsù**; later met **Qìngshān Xiū** 磬山修 (= Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū) and was sealed at first sight.

**Abbacies.** **Hángzhōu Dōngmíng chánsì** 杭州府東明禪寺; **Nányuè Lǜluóān** 南嶽綠蘿菴; **Chángshāfǔ Liúyáng Nányuán chánsì** 長沙府瀏陽南源禪寺.

**Major historiographical work.** Compiler of 《南嶽禪燈錄》 *Nányuè chándēnglù* — drawing together 133 monks from the new and old gazetteers, an important Húnán Chán biographical anthology.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* include **Ěrzhān Zūn** 爾瞻尊 (= **Dázūn** [[達尊]], lead compiler of his *yǔlù*), **Xuáncí Qiān** 玄慈謙 (= **Dáqiān** [[達謙]]), **Mìyán Gāng** 密巖剛, **Qiěān Chú** 且菴芻, **Zōngxuán Zhǐ** 宗玄旨.

**Work.** [[KR6q0608]] 《山茨際禪師語錄》 4 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1649, compiled by *fǎsì* **Dázūn** [[達尊]] and **Dáqiān** [[達謙]] *děng biāncì*.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 68; 《正源略集》 vol. 2, vol. 4.
