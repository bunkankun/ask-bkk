# Lǐ Jǐngràng 李景讓

Mid-Táng official, *liánshuài* (military commissioner); requested the imperial bestowal of the monastery name Tóngqìng sì 同慶寺 for [[靈祐|Wéishān Língyòu]] 溈山靈祐's Dàguīshān foundation.
