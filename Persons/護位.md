# Hùwèi 護位

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[智及]] Yúān Zhìjí (1311–1378); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
