**Chén Yí** 陳嶷 (*fl.* mid-15th c., *Zhèngtǒng* 正統 era) — early-Míng official, *jiānchá yùshǐ* 監察御史 (Censor). In *Zhèngtǒng* 6 / 1441 (*xīnyǒu* 辛酉) he was circuit-inspector of Zhèjiāng; in the course of his circuit, passing through Cíxī 慈溪 (Níngbō region), he visited the Yǒnglè monastery 永樂寺 — where his late father had served as deputy magistrate, and where the abbot Xīxù 西緒 was custodian of the manuscript of Dài Yuánlǐ's medical compendium. Chén obtained the manuscript and saw it through the press in 1443 under [[胡濚]] Hú Yíng's patronage. CBDB id 430950.
