**Chén Yí** 陳嶷 (*fl.* mid-15th c., *Zhèng-tǒng* 正統 era) — early-Míng official, *jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 監察御史 (Censor). In *Zhèng-tǒng* 6 / 1441 (*xīn-yǒu* 辛酉) he was circuit-inspector of Zhè-jiāng; in the course of his circuit, passing through Cí-xī 慈溪 (Níng-bō region), he visited the Yǒng-lè monastery 永樂寺 — where his late father had served as deputy magistrate, and where the abbot Xī-xù 西緒 was custodian of the manuscript of Dài Yuán-lǐ's medical compendium. Chén obtained the manuscript and saw it through the press in 1443 under [[胡濚]] Hú Yíng's patronage. CBDB id 430950.
