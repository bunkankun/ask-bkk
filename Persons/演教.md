# Yǎnjiào 演教

Yúnwò Yǎnjiào 雲臥演教, twelfth-century Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant of Dàhuì Zōnggǎo during the Héngyáng–Méizhōu exile years; his manuscript of the *Dàhuì niánpǔ* supplied corrections that Huázàng Zōngyǎn used to revise the received recension ([[KR6q0063]]) in 1205.
