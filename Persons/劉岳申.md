Sòng-Yuán transition 元 Lú-líng (Jí-zhōu) literary figure and minor official. *Zì* **Gāo-zhòng** 高仲; students called him **Shēn-zhāi xiān-shēng** 申齋先生. Native of **Jí-shuǐ** 吉水 (Lú-líng / Jí-zhōu, Jiāng-xī). CBDB 28015 records *b.* 1260; death-date uncertain.

**Literary placement.** One of the three principal **Lú-líng** Sòng-Yuán-transition literary figures (together with **Liú Shēn** 劉詵 [[KR4d0436]] and **Lóng Rén-fū** 龍仁夫). The triad continues the Liú Chén-wēng [[KR4d0397]] / Liú Jiāng-sūn [[劉將孫]] [[KR4d0456]] Jiāng-xī Sòng-Yuán transition literary lineage.

**Career.** Esteemed by **Liú Chén-wēng** 劉辰翁 and **Wú Chéng** [[吳澄]]. On Wú Chéng's recommendation summoned as *Liáo-yáng rú-xué fù-tí-jǔ* 遼陽儒學副提舉 but declined the appointment. Later appointed *Tài-hé-zhōu pàn* 泰和州判 (Tài-hé Department Vice-Magistrate) and retired.

**Collection.** *Shēn-zhāi jí* [[KR4d0476]] — originally 15 *juàn* per the Sìkù *zǒng-mù*; the present *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* reconstruction is 10 *juàn*. Edited by his disciple **Xiāo Xún** 蕭洵; prefaced by **Lǐ Qí** 李祁. The Yuán print was destroyed during the Yuán-Míng transition; only manuscript copies survived.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0476]] *Shēn-zhāi jí* 申齋集 (撰).
