SòngYuán transition 元 Lúlíng (Jízhōu) literary figure and minor official. *Zì* **Gāozhòng** 高仲; students called him **Shēnzhāi xiānshēng** 申齋先生. Native of **Jíshuǐ** 吉水 (Lúlíng / Jízhōu, Jiāngxī). CBDB 28015 records *b.* 1260; death-date uncertain.

**Literary placement.** One of the three principal **Lúlíng** Sòng-Yuán-transition literary figures (together with **Liú Shēn** 劉詵 [[KR4d0436]] and **Lóng Rénfū** 龍仁夫). The triad continues the Liú Chénwēng [[KR4d0397]] / Liú Jiāngsūn [[劉將孫]] [[KR4d0456]] Jiāngxī SòngYuán transition literary lineage.

**Career.** Esteemed by **Liú Chénwēng** 劉辰翁 and **Wú Chéng** [[吳澄]]. On Wú Chéng's recommendation summoned as *Liáoyáng rúxué fùtíjǔ* 遼陽儒學副提舉 but declined the appointment. Later appointed *Tàihézhōu pàn* 泰和州判 (Tàihé Department Vice-Magistrate) and retired.

**Collection.** *Shēnzhāi jí* [[KR4d0476]] — originally 15 *juàn* per the Sìkù *zǒngmù*; the present *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* reconstruction is 10 *juàn*. Edited by his disciple **Xiāo Xún** 蕭洵; prefaced by **Lǐ Qí** 李祁. The Yuán print was destroyed during the YuánMíng transition; only manuscript copies survived.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0476]] *Shēnzhāi jí* 申齋集 (撰).
