Late-Míng Wàn-lì-era literatus and parallel-prose theorist. **CBDB id 126537, dates 1576–1633**. *Zì* **Shū-dí** 叔狄 (or **Tíng-xiào** 廷孝). Native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū).

His principal work is the **[[KR4h0118]]** *Sì-liù fǎ-hǎi* 四六法海 (12 *juǎn*), the canonical late-Míng anthology of parallel prose (*sì-liù wén*) — establishing the theoretical position that **parallel prose and ancient prose share a common origin** (*tóng-yuán*) and develop continuously, against the *gǔ-wén pài*'s dismissal of parallel prose. His other major work is the *Dú-shǐ shāng-yǔ* 讀史商語 (Discussions on Reading History), separately catalogued.
