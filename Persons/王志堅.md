Late-Míng Wàn-lì-era literatus and parallel-prose theorist. **CBDB id 126537, dates 1576–1633**. *Zì* **Shūdí** 叔狄 (or **Tíngxiào** 廷孝). Native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū).

His principal work is the **[[KR4h0118]]** *Sìliù fǎhǎi* 四六法海 (12 *juǎn*), the canonical late-Míng anthology of parallel prose (*sìliù wén*) — establishing the theoretical position that **parallel prose and ancient prose share a common origin** (*tóngyuán*) and develop continuously, against the *gǔwén pài*'s dismissal of parallel prose. His other major work is the *Dúshǐ shāngyǔ* 讀史商語 (Discussions on Reading History), separately catalogued.
