Zhǒng Fàng 種放 (955–1015), *zì* Míngyì 明逸, a Northern-Sòng recluse-scholar of Mt. Zhōngnán 終南山 (near Cháng'ān). One of the principal disciples of [[陳摶]] Chén Tuán; received the *Xiāntiān tú* transmission from Chén Tuán and is reported to have transmitted it onward to [[穆修]] Mù Xiū (and thence to [[李之才]] Lǐ Zhīcái and [[邵雍]] Shào Yōng), and the *Hé túLuò shū* charts to Lǐ Gài, Xǔ Jiān, Fàn Èchāng, and ultimately [[劉牧]] Liú Mù. Honored with the rank *Gōngbù shìláng* 工部侍郎.
