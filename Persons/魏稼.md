# 魏稼 Wèi Jià

**Wèi Jià** 魏稼 (b. 1933), contemporary Chinese acupuncture scholar at the Jiāngxī University of Chinese Medicine. Editor of *Qióngyáo shénshū jiào-zhù* 瓊瑤神書校註 (2007, the standard modern critical edition of the *Zhēnjiǔ shénshū* [[KR3ee004]]) and of several major collections on Chinese acupuncture history.
