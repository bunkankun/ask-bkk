**Zhāng Zǐhé** 張子和 (1151–1231, Jīn). Also known as [[張從正]] Zhāng Cóngzhèng; one of the *JīnYuán sìdà jiā* "Four Great Masters", noted for his *gōngxié* (attacking-pathogens) doctrine.
