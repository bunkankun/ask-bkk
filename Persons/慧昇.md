Huìshēng 慧昇, *hào* **Zōngqìng** 宗慶. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎzhù Hǎidòng** [[海棟]] (35th generation Línjì). Lifedates unknown, active late 17th century. One of twelve dharma-heirs of Hǎidòng formally invested with verse-bestowal (*liánfāngjì* 聯芳偈) — Huìshēng held the office of *wéinuó* 維那 ("精進首領") at Pǔfēngsì at the time. Co-compiler of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 — *jí* (collation), printed in Jiāxīng Canon J37 B402 in 1691.

- - - - -

There is a separate Qīng-dynasty monk **Huìshēng 慧昇** (*hào* 心耀; DILA A045939), who held the abbacy of an unspecified monastery in **Qiánlóng 59 = 1794**. Not the same person.
