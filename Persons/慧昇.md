Huì-shēng 慧昇, *hào* **Zōng-qìng** 宗慶. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎ-zhù Hǎi-dòng** [[海棟]] (35th generation Línjì). Lifedates unknown, active late 17th century. One of twelve dharma-heirs of Hǎi-dòng formally invested with verse-bestowal (*lián-fāng-jì* 聯芳偈) — Huì-shēng held the office of *wéi-nuó* 維那 ("精進首領") at Pǔ-fēng-sì at the time. Co-compiler of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 — *jí* (collation), printed in Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B402 in 1691.

- - - - -

There is a separate Qīng-dynasty monk **Huì-shēng 慧昇** (*hào* 心耀; DILA A045939), who held the abbacy of an unspecified monastery in **Qiánlóng 59 = 1794**. Not the same person.
