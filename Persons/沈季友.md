Shěn Jìyǒu 沈季友 (1652–1698, *zì* Kèzǐ 客子), of Pínghú 平湖 (Jiāxīng prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Early-Kāngxī scholar; major compiler of the *Zuìlǐ shī xì* 檇李詩繫 [[KR4h0163]] — the comprehensive Jiāxīng prefectural poetry anthology in 42 *juǎn*, covering poets from the Hàn-Jìn period down to his own day. The compilation continues the Míng-period precedents of **Zhū Hàn** 朱翰 (*Jǐngtài*-era *Zuìlǐ yīnghuá*) and **Jiǎng Zhīqiáo** 蔣之翹 (*Chóngzhēn*-late *Zuìlǐ shī chéng* 檇李詩乘) — both of which were either unsatisfactory in selection or lost in transmission. Shěn also produced his own poetic collection, the *Xuégǔtáng shī jí* 學古堂詩集. CBDB id 79671. Lifedates 1652–1698 from CBDB.
