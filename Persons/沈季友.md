Shěn Jì-yǒu 沈季友 (1652–1698, *zì* Kè-zǐ 客子), of Píng-hú 平湖 (Jiā-xīng prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Early-Kāngxī scholar; major compiler of the *Zuì-lǐ shī xì* 檇李詩繫 [[KR4h0163]] — the comprehensive Jiā-xīng prefectural poetry anthology in 42 *juǎn*, covering poets from the Hàn-Jìn period down to his own day. The compilation continues the Míng-period precedents of **Zhū Hàn** 朱翰 (*Jǐng-tài*-era *Zuì-lǐ yīng-huá*) and **Jiǎng Zhī-qiáo** 蔣之翹 (*Chóng-zhēn*-late *Zuì-lǐ shī chéng* 檇李詩乘) — both of which were either unsatisfactory in selection or lost in transmission. Shěn also produced his own poetic collection, the *Xué-gǔ-táng shī jí* 學古堂詩集. CBDB id 79671. Lifedates 1652–1698 from CBDB.
