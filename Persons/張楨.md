Zhāng Zhēn 張楨 (fl. early 16th c.), youngest son (*jì-zǐ*) of **Zhāng Yǔ** 張羽 (1467–?, *zì* Fèng-jǔ, of Fèng-xīn) ([[張羽]]) — editor of his father's *Dōng-tián yí-gǎo* in 2 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0154]]). The preface to the recension was contributed by his father's disciple **Chǔ Xún** 儲洵.
