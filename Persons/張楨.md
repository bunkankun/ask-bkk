Zhāng Zhēn 張楨 (fl. early 16th c.), youngest son (*jìzǐ*) of **Zhāng Yǔ** 張羽 (1467–?, *zì* Fèngjǔ, of Fèngxīn) ([[張羽]]) — editor of his father's *Dōngtián yígǎo* in 2 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0154]]). The preface to the recension was contributed by his father's disciple **Chǔ Xún** 儲洵.
