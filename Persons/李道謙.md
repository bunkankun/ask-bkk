**Lǐ Dàoqiān** 李道謙 (1219–1296), *hào* Hé zhēn zǐ 和真子, was a prominent **Quán zhēn** 全眞 Daoist historian of the Yuán era, based at **Zhōng nán shān** 終南山 (Shǎnxī) — the Quán zhēn ancestral site. He compiled two major hagiographical works on the Zhōng nán lineage: [[KR5c0355]] *Zhōng nán shān zǔ tíng xiān zhēn nèi zhuàn* 終南山祖庭仙眞內傳 (preface 1284) and the *Gān shuǐ xiān yuán lù* 甘水仙源錄, both crucial primary sources for the early history of the Quán zhēn school (founders Wáng Zhé 王嚞 = Wáng Chóngyáng 王重陽 and the Seven Perfected Qī zhēn 七眞).
