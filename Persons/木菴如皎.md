# Mùān Rújiǎo 木菴如皎

Southern Sòng Vinaya master, abbot of the *Zhāoqìng jiètán yuàn* 昭慶戒壇院 in *Shàoxìngfǔ* 紹興府. Recorded as having overseen the *Kāixī* 開禧 3 (1207) re-carving of [[了然]] Zhìyǒng Liǎorán's *Shìmén guījìng yí tōngzhēn jì* 釋門歸敬儀通真記 ([[KR6k0253]], X1095) — the textual base for the later Edo-period Japanese interlineated edition of 1689. No independent biography preserved.
