# Wénmíng 文明

Late-Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Yuèjiàn 月磵 ("Moon-Ravine"). Lifedates unrecorded per DILA A013871. Dharma-heir of [[了慧]] Xīyán Liǎohuì (1198–1262). Received his precepts at Huánglóngsì; first abbacy Xìnzhōu Éhú Rénshòu 鵝湖仁壽 in Jǐngdìng 5 (1264), then Ráozhōu Tiānníng, Qīxián Miàoguǒ, and two tenures at Ráozhōu Jiànfú 薦福 (whence Jiànfú Yuèjiàn 薦福月磵). Recorded sayings: *Yuèjiàn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0325]]). Dharma-heir: Dōngshān Chóng 東山崇.
