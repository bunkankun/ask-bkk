# Wén-míng 文明

Late-Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Yuè-jiàn 月磵 ("Moon-Ravine"). Lifedates unrecorded per DILA A013871. Dharma-heir of [[了慧]] Xī-yán Liǎo-huì (1198–1262). Received his precepts at Huáng-lóng-sì; first abbacy Xìn-zhōu É-hú Rén-shòu 鵝湖仁壽 in Jǐngdìng 5 (1264), then Ráo-zhōu Tiān-níng, Qī-xián Miào-guǒ, and two tenures at Ráo-zhōu Jiàn-fú 薦福 (whence Jiàn-fú Yuè-jiàn 薦福月磵). Recorded sayings: *Yuè-jiàn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0325]]). Dharma-heir: Dōng-shān Chóng 東山崇.
