# 心石

Xīnshí

Late-Míng monk who requested [[元賢]] Yǒngjué Yuánxián to review his *Jīngāng dúméng* 金剛凟蒙 ("Diamond Sūtra Beginner's Primer") during Yuánxián's 1641 visit to Bǎoshànsì 寶善寺 in Jiànníng — a request that prompted Yuánxián to compose his own [[KR6c0070]] *Jīngāng jīng lüèshū*.
