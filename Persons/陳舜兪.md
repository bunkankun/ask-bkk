Chén Shùnyú 陳舜兪 (d. 1074), *zì* Lìngjǔ 令舉, *hào* Báiniú jūshì 白牛居士 (from his residence at Báiniúcūn), of Wūchéng 烏程 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng), was a Northern Sòng official and topographical writer. *Jìnshì* of Qìnglì 6 (1046); first-place winner of the Special Examination of Jiāyòu 4 (1059); rose to *Dūguān yuánwàiláng*. As Magistrate of Shānyīn County in the Xīníng era, he refused to implement Wáng Ānshí's *Qīngmiáo* (Green Sprouts) law and was demoted to Tax Inspector at Nánkāng. During this exile he undertook a 60-day systematic field-tour of Mt. Lú with the retired official Liú Huàn 劉渙 (1000–1080), composing the *Lúshān jì* ([[KR2k0084]]) — the principal Northern Sòng monograph on Mt. Lú. He died in 1074 still in demotion. His career is recorded in the *Sòngshǐ*.
