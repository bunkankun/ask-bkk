Chén Shùnyú 陳舜兪 (d. 1074), *zì* Lìng-jǔ 令舉, *hào* Bái-niú jūshì 白牛居士 (from his residence at Bái-niú-cūn), of Wū-chéng 烏程 (Hú-zhōu, Zhèjiāng), was a Northern Sòng official and topographical writer. *Jìnshì* of Qìng-lì 6 (1046); first-place winner of the Special Examination of Jiā-yòu 4 (1059); rose to *Dū-guān yuán-wài-láng*. As Magistrate of Shān-yīn County in the Xī-níng era, he refused to implement Wáng Ān-shí's *Qīng-miáo* (Green Sprouts) law and was demoted to Tax Inspector at Nán-kāng. During this exile he undertook a 60-day systematic field-tour of Mt. Lú with the retired official Liú Huàn 劉渙 (1000–1080), composing the *Lú-shān jì* ([[KR2k0084]]) — the principal Northern Sòng monograph on Mt. Lú. He died in 1074 still in demotion. His career is recorded in the *Sòngshǐ*.
