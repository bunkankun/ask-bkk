**Lǐ Gōnghuàn** 李公煥 (fl. late Southern Sòng to early Yuán, ca. mid-13th c.), is known almost solely as the *jiānzhù* (interpolated-annotation) editor of [[KR4b0009]] *Jiānzhù Táo Yuānmíng jí* 箋註陶淵明集 — the standard Sòng-Yuán annotated edition of Táo Qián's collection. His annotations gather the Sòng commentarial tradition (Sū Shì 蘇軾, Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅, Sū Zhé 蘇轍, Sī Mǎ Wēngōng 司馬溫公, Jiāng Jūn 江君, Wú Yīn 吳因 and others) on each Táo poem and *fù*. The Yuán *Cuìyánjīngshè* 翠巖精舍 cutting is the SBCK base. No personal biographical record survives; his life dates are unknown and CBDB does not register him. The dating of his edition is conventionally placed in the late 13th century on internal evidence (he cites no Yuán-period sources but his text is preserved in a Yuán print). The edition itself is one of the three canonical Sòng-Yuán annotation traditions of Táo (alongside Tāng Hàn 湯漢 1241 and the lost Hé Tàibǎo 何泰保).
