Mid-Northern-Sòng *Qìnglì* 慶曆 reform-era *Yánguān* and military-diplomatic statesman, *zì* Āndào 安道, posthumous *Xiāng* 襄, of *Sháozhōu Qǔjiāng* 韶州曲江 (Guǎngdōng). *Jìnshì* of *Tiānshèng* 2 / 1024. Held *Yòuzhèngyán*, *Zhīzhìgào*; banished out of court in *Jǐngyòu* 3 / 1036 for defending Fàn Zhòngyān, becoming one of the famous "Four Worthies" (*Sì xián* 四賢) defended in Cài Xiāng's *Sì xián yī bù xiào shī*. Three diplomatic missions to the Liáo (1042–1045); produced the *Qìdān guānyí* 契丹官儀 — a key Sòng eyewitness account of Khitan administrative practice. *Jīnglüè* of *Guǎngxī Nánlù ānfǔshǐ* (1052–1053), in which capacity he co-suppressed Nóng Zhìgāo's 儂智高 rising and composed the *Móyá* commemorative inscription at *Kūnlún Guān*. Promoted to *Gōngbù shàngshū* under Yīngzōng. Died *Zhìpíng* 1 / 1064, age 65. Surviving collection *Wǔxī jí* [[KR4d0023]] (20 *juǎn*, edited by his son Yú Zhòngxún [[余仲荀]] with preface by Zhōu Yuán). CBDB id 12319, lifedates 1000–1064.
