Mid-Northern-Sòng *Qìng-lì* 慶曆 reform-era *Yán-guān* and military-diplomatic statesman, *zì* Ān-dào 安道, posthumous *Xiāng* 襄, of *Sháo-zhōu Qǔ-jiāng* 韶州曲江 (Guǎng-dōng). *Jìn-shì* of *Tiān-shèng* 2 / 1024. Held *Yòu-zhèng-yán*, *Zhī-zhì-gào*; banished out of court in *Jǐng-yòu* 3 / 1036 for defending Fàn Zhòng-yān, becoming one of the famous "Four Worthies" (*Sì xián* 四賢) defended in Cài Xiāng's *Sì xián yī bù xiào shī*. Three diplomatic missions to the Liáo (1042–1045); produced the *Qì-dān guān-yí* 契丹官儀 — a key Sòng eyewitness account of Khitan administrative practice. *Jīng-lüè* of *Guǎng-xī Nán-lù ān-fǔ-shǐ* (1052–1053), in which capacity he co-suppressed Nóng Zhì-gāo's 儂智高 rising and composed the *Mó-yá* commemorative inscription at *Kūn-lún Guān*. Promoted to *Gōng-bù shàng-shū* under Yīng-zōng. Died *Zhì-píng* 1 / 1064, age 65. Surviving collection *Wǔ-xī jí* [[KR4d0023]] (20 *juǎn*, edited by his son Yú Zhòng-xún [[余仲荀]] with preface by Zhōu Yuán). CBDB id 12319, lifedates 1000–1064.
