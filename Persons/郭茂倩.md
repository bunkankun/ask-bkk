# Guō Màoqiàn 郭茂倩 (1041–1099)

A Northern-Sòng official from Yúncheng 鄆城 (Shāndōng — though some sources give Tàiyuán 太原), eldest son of the courtier **Guō Yuán** 郭源 of the *Qízhōu* lineage. *Jìnshì* of approximately *Xīníng* 4 (1071). He served as *Hénán fǔ fǎcáo cānjūn* and later as a senior official in the *Tàicháng* (Court of Sacrifices) before retiring to compile the *Yuèfǔ shījí* 樂府詩集 [[KR4h0034]] — the comprehensive 100-*juǎn* anthology of *yuèfǔ* (musical/song) poetry from antiquity to the late Táng. The catalog meta gives *fl. 1264–1269*, an evident corruption of the Northern Sòng dates; the actual lifespan is 1041–1099, with the *Yuèfǔ shījí* completed *ca.* 1090–1099. No CBDB entry. The book is the principal pre-modern Chinese anthology of song-poetry and remains the foundational source for *yuèfǔ* studies.
