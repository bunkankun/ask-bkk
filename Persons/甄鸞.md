**Zhēn Luán** 甄鸞 (*zì* Shūzūn 叔遵, *fl.* Northern Zhōu, 6th c.), Northern-Zhōu mathematician and calendar-scholar. Major work: re-elaboration (重述 *zhòngshù*) of [[趙君卿]] Zhào Jūnqīng's *Zhōubì suànjīng* commentary ([[KR3f0001]]); also ***Wǔ jīng suànshù*** 五經算術, ***Sūnzǐ suànjīng*** 孫子算經 commentary, ***Wǔcáo suànjīng*** 五曹算經 commentary, ***Hǎidǎo suànjīng*** 海島算經 commentary, ***Tiānhé lì*** 天和曆 (Tiānhé calendar, 566). Author of the polemical anti-Daoist work ***Xiàodào lùn*** 笑道論 (570). One of the major Liù Cháo period mathematician-astronomers in the transmission line that culminated in the Táng *Shí bù suàn jīng* 十部算經 imperial-mathematical canon.
