# Jìnghuàn 淨煥

Early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán *dìzǐ* 弟子 of [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi (1615–1702); co-recorder of his master's four-juan *Wèilín chánshī lǚbóān gǎo* ([[KR6q0372]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
