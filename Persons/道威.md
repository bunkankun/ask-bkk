Sòng-period Tiāntái 天台 master of Sìmíng 四明 (modern Níngbō); also referenced as **Sìmíng Wēi** 四明威. DILA Authority A001512. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Author of the **Fǎhuá jīng rùshū** 法華經入疏 ([[KR6d0065]], X30n0600, in 12 *juan*) — a Sòng-period subcommentary on the Lotus Sūtra. Affiliation with the Sìmíng *shānjiā* 山家 lineage of Zhīlǐ is plausible on the basis of his geographical association but is not securely established.

Source: DILA A001512; X30n0600.
