# 法和

Fǎhé (法和) was a Buddhist monk active in late-fourth-century Cháng'ān 長安, in the circle of [[道安]] Dàoān (312–385). He is named in Daoan's preface to the *Ekottara-āgama* [[KR6a0126]] (T125) as having jointly revised the translation produced by [[曇摩難提]] Dharmanandi in 384–385. Dates are not recorded.
