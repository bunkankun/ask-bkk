**Shàn Nánshān** 單南山 (*fl.* late 18th / early 19th c. 清), late-Qīng obstetrical specialist. Documented in Chinese-medicine bibliographic literature as author of two coordinated works on pregnancy and childbirth:

1. *Pánzhū jí tāichǎn zhèngzhì* 盤珠集胎產症治 ([[KR3ei049]]) — three-*juǎn* systematic obstetrical treatise.
2. *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* 胎產指南 ([[KR3ei053]]) — companion obstetrical manual.

The two works are sometimes printed together as a paired set. No further biographical detail located in standard reference works; not in CBDB.
