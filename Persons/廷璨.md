# Tíng-càn 廷璨

Early-Míng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[文琇]] Nán-shí Wén-xiù (1345–1418); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0355]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
