Southern-Sòng *chuánjiào* 傳教 (doctrinal-preacher) monk styled Jìngān 鏡庵; abbacy at the **Wūxū tǎyuàn Jìngān** 烏戍墖院鏡庵 (in 紹興 reign, 1131–1162) and afterwards in residence at **Fútián Xīhuáyán Lánruò** 福田西華嚴蘭若. Author of [[KR6i0563]] *Yuánjué jīng lèijiě* 圓覺經類解 (X10 no. 252, 4 fascicles), compiled at the commission of the Wúxīng 吳興 layman Shěn Yīngchén 沈應辰 from a Sichuan master Fùān's 復庵 lecture-notes on the *Yuánjué jīng* ([[KR6i0551]]); and the **《重編諸天傳》** *Chóngbiān zhūtiān zhuàn* [[KR6r0171]] (X88 no. 1658, 2 *juǎn*), a re-editing and expansion of an earlier (Northern-Sòng or pre-1162) Tiāntái *zhūtiān* compilation, completed and prefaced in **Qiándào** 乾道 9.8 = autumn **1173**. His specific lifedates are not preserved; on the basis of his datable activity (1131–1173 attested) his *floruit* may be set at the **mid-12th century**, with active years ca. 1130–1180. He is otherwise undocumented in standard Buddhological reference works; biographical data limited to what his own works' prefaces supply.
