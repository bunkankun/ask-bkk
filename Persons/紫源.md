Zǐyuán 紫源, Southern-Sòng court physician of Gāozōng's Hànlín Medical Bureau, *fl.* mid-12th century. One of the four named editors of the *Shàoxīng běncǎo* 紹興本草 ([[KR3ec014]]) of 1159. The unusual single-character given name "Zǐyuán" suggests this may be a Daoist *hào* (religious sobriquet) rather than a personal name; if so, the underlying identity is not now recoverable. No independent biography; no CBDB record.
