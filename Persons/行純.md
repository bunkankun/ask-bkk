# Xíngchún 行純

Early-Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[昭如]] Hǎiyìn Zhāorú; principal compiler (*jí* 集) of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0331]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
