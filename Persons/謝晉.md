Xiè Jìn 謝晉 (also written 縉; fl. early 15th cent.; latest dated piece in his collection from 1417), *zì* **Kǒngzhāo** 孔昭, *hào* **Lántíng** 蘭庭. Native of **Wúxiàn** 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Early-Yǒng-lè-era poet-and-landscape-painter; once summoned to court as a *bùyī* (commoner) — recorded in the *Chéngtiānmén xièēn* poem in his collection. Self-jokingly styled himself **Xiè Diéshān** 謝叠山 (a deliberate evocation of the Sòng martyr **Xiè Fángdé** 謝枋得). Principal early-Yǒng-lè continuator of the Sūzhōu poetic tradition of **Yáng Jī** 楊基 and **Gāo Qǐ** 高啟. Surviving works are [[KR4e0103]] *Lántíng jí*. Not to be confused with the famous **Hànlín** scholar **Xiè Jìn** 解縉 ([[解縉]]) of the same period — different surname (謝 vs. 解). CBDB id 34507 (no lifedates).
