Tán Zhēnmò 譚貞默, *zì* **Liángshēng** 梁生, *hào* **Sǎoān** 掃菴. Late-Míng / early-Qīng Jiāxīng (Zuìlǐ 檇李) lay-Chán scholar-official, prominent across multiple Cáodòng and Línjì publications. **Chóngzhēn 元年戊辰** = **1628** *jìnshì*; began as 工部虞衡司主事, rose through 大理寺副 and 太僕寺少卿 to **Guózǐjiān sīyè jiān jìjiǔ** 國子監司業兼祭酒. After the MíngQīng transition was recommended for the same post under the Qīng but refused, retiring to compose privately. Younger brother: **Tán Zhēnliáng** 譚貞良. Son of **Tán Chāngyán** 譚昌言.

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the *tǎmíng* 塔銘 for **Yuānhú Miàoyòng** [[妙用]] (1587–1642) at [[KR6q0416]], dated **1654-12-25**. Preface-author of [[KR6q0482]] 《天界覺浪盛禪師嘉禾語錄》 — the Jiā-xīng-area selection of **Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]]'s sermons. Lead signer of the *qǐngqǐ* (invitation petition) at the head of [[KR6q0532]] 《三塔主峰禪師語錄》 (signed jointly with **Cáo Róng** 曹溶, **Zhāng Jìnzhēng** 張晉徵, and other Jiāxīngfǔ gentry). One of the senior Jiāngnán Buddhist lay-patron-scholars of his generation, alongside [[嚴大參]] Yán Diàoyù.
