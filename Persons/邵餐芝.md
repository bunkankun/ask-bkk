**Shào Cān-zhī** 邵餐芝 (*fl.* 1920s–1930s), Republican-era Zhèjiāng literatus-physician based in the small town of **Lán-xī Nǚ-bù** 蘭溪女埠 (in Zhèjiāng). Self-taught in medicine through Confucian classical-reading marginal-annotation (*zhá-jì* 札記). Came to broader public attention only through the recommendation of Zhāng Shān-léi 張山雷 (1873–1934), founder of the *Lán-xī Zhōng-yī zhuān-mén xué-xiào* 蘭溪中醫專門學校. His manuscript came to publication through the Shanghai *Shén-zhōu yī-bào* 神州醫報 monthly publication of extracts. Author of the *Sù-xuān yī-yǔ* 素軒醫語 ([[KR3eq071]]). Not in CBDB.
