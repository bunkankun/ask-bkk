**Shào Cānzhī** 邵餐芝 (*fl.* 1920s–1930s), Republican-era Zhèjiāng literatus-physician based in the small town of **Lánxī Nǚbù** 蘭溪女埠 (in Zhèjiāng). Self-taught in medicine through Confucian classical-reading marginal-annotation (*zhájì* 札記). Came to broader public attention only through the recommendation of Zhāng Shānléi 張山雷 (1873–1934), founder of the *Lánxī Zhōngyī zhuānmén xuéxiào* 蘭溪中醫專門學校. His manuscript came to publication through the Shanghai *Shénzhōu yībào* 神州醫報 monthly publication of extracts. Author of the *Sùxuān yīyǔ* 素軒醫語 ([[KR3eq071]]). Not in CBDB.
