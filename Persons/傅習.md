Yuán literatus and anthologist of Qīngjiāng 清江 (Jiāngxī). *Zì* **Shuōqīng** 説卿. Active in the early-to-mid Yuán (late 13th – early 14th century); life-dates not precisely known. He travelled widely in the southern and central circuits gathering contemporary verse, amassing a very large body of material, which the Lúlíng man [[孫存吾]] subsequently edited into the 24-*juǎn* anthology **[[KR4h0082]]** *Huáng-Yuán fēngyǎ* 皇元風雅 (later *Yuán fēngyǎ* 元風雅). Beyond his role as collector for that anthology, no separate works by him are known to survive.
