# Xīn-tài 心泰

A Yuán-Míng-transition Buddhist monk-historiographer; *zì* / hào **Dài-zōng** 岱宗 (alternately styled **Dài-zōng-tài-gōng** 岱宗泰公 or **Dōng-shān Dài-zōng** 東山岱宗 — the latter from his abbacy of **Yuè-zhī Dōng-shān** 越之東山 = the Dōng-shān monastery of Kuài-jī 會稽 in modern Shàoxīng). Native of **Kuài-jī** 會稽 (Zhèjiāng); identified in the colophon of his **《佛法金湯編》** [[KR6r0161]] simply as **會稽沙門心泰** ("the Kuài-jī śramaṇa Xīn-tài"). Biographical sources are scant; the standard reference works (e.g. 《新續高僧傳四集》by 喻謙) place his lifedates as **1327–1415**, identifying him as a Tiāntái-school monk of the early Míng who witnessed the Yuán-Míng dynastic transition.

His sole surviving work is the **《佛法金湯編》** *Fó-fǎ jīn-tāng biān* (X1628, 16 *juǎn* in the catalog meta — but the prefatorial sources call it 10 *juǎn*; the discrepancy reflects the late-Míng repagination of the 1600 reprint, which split the original ten into sixteen sub-units), an apologetic compilation of the **lay supporters and imperial patrons of Buddhism** from antiquity through the Yuán dynasty. The work was completed in **Hóng-wǔ** 24 = **1391** (so per the preface of the *zuǒ-jiǎng-jīng-shā-mén* **Qīng-jùn** 清濬, dated **洪武二十四年歲在辛未秋七月初吉**) and prefaced again in **Hóng-wǔ** 26 = **1393** by **Sū Bó-héng** 蘇伯衡 of Méi-shān; the work was reprinted in **Wàn-lì** 萬曆 28 = **1600** by **Tiān-tái Xǐng-shàng-rén** 天台惺上人, with a re-engraving preface by the layman **Yú Rǔ-wéi** 俞汝為 of Huá-tíng.
