**Mèng Jīn-shì** 孟今氏 (signs as **Mèng Jīn** 孟今; in [[徐紹楨]]'s preface to **[[KR3eq019]]** *Yī yī yī* 醫醫醫 referred to as "Mèng Jīn of Fēng-bā" 孟今於風八), late-Qīng / Xuān-tǒng-era *jǔ-rén* and physician-administrator of the **Guǎngxī** (Guì-lín region) cultural circuit. Author of the 1909-dated medical-reform polemic **[[KR3eq019]]** *Yī yī yī* 醫醫醫 — one of the earliest substantial Chinese-language reform programmes for medicine, anticipating the Republican-era *zhōng-yī cún-fèi* 中醫存廢 debates. Xú Shào-zhēn's preface lists his prior scholarly works as *Sì-shū zhì-yí* 四書質疑, *Xiào-jīng zhì-yí* 孝經質疑, *Sān-guó zhì zhì-yí* 三國志質疑, *Suàn-xué rù-mén* 算學入門, *Gōu-gǔ jīng-yì* 勾股精義, *Jìng-míng-guǎn shī gǔ-wén-cí jí* 靖冥館詩古文詞集 — a corpus that locates him in the late-Qīng *kǎo-jù*-and-mathematics scholarly milieu. The personal-name identification (catalog-meta 孟今氏 vs. the alternate 孟今 / 於風八 forms) is uncertain; the catalog tag-form is preserved. Not in CBDB.
