**Mèng Jīnshì** 孟今氏 (signs as **Mèng Jīn** 孟今; in [[徐紹楨]]'s preface to **[[KR3eq019]]** *Yī yī yī* 醫醫醫 referred to as "Mèng Jīn of Fēngbā" 孟今於風八), late-Qīng / Xuān-tǒng-era *jǔrén* and physician-administrator of the **Guǎngxī** (Guìlín region) cultural circuit. Author of the 1909-dated medical-reform polemic **[[KR3eq019]]** *Yī yī yī* 醫醫醫 — one of the earliest substantial Chinese-language reform programmes for medicine, anticipating the Republican-era *zhōngyī cúnfèi* 中醫存廢 debates. Xú Shàozhēn's preface lists his prior scholarly works as *Sìshū zhìyí* 四書質疑, *Xiàojīng zhìyí* 孝經質疑, *Sānguó zhì zhìyí* 三國志質疑, *Suànxué rùmén* 算學入門, *Gōugǔ jīngyì* 勾股精義, *Jìngmíngguǎn shī gǔwéncí jí* 靖冥館詩古文詞集 — a corpus that locates him in the late-Qīng *kǎojù*-and-mathematics scholarly milieu. The personal-name identification (catalog-meta 孟今氏 vs. the alternate 孟今 / 於風八 forms) is uncertain; the catalog tag-form is preserved. Not in CBDB.
