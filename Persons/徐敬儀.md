**Xú Jìngyí** 徐敬儀 (Míng; dates and native place unrecorded) is named in the catalog as the compiler (*jí* 輯) of the *Tiān-xiàng yí quán-tú* 天象儀全圖 ([[KR3fa014]]), a one-*juǎn* compendium of complete-sky diagrams. He is otherwise undocumented; the name is sufficiently common that no confident CBDB match is available.
