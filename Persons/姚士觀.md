Late-Wànlì Míng official, active in the 1580s. Held the post of *Xúnàn zhílì dūxué yùshǐ* 巡按直隸督學御史 (Censor in Patrol of the Northern Metropolitan Region and Superintendent of Education) at the time of his joint cutting of the *Míng Tàizǔ wénjí* 明太祖文集 [[KR4e0001]] at the secondary capital Zhōngdū 中都 (Fèngyáng 鳳陽) in Wànlì 14 (1586). The *Sìkù tíyào* notes that he and **Shěn Fū** [[沈鈇]] signed the colophon claiming only to reproduce an older base text, without further documentation. Otherwise obscure in the standard biographies.
