Northern-Sòng court Daoist, *hào* Bǎoníng dàshī 保寧大師. Author of [[KR5a0114|Huángdì yīnfú jīng jiě 黃帝陰符經解]] (DZ 114), a twenty-one-folio commentary on the *Yīnfú jīng* in which he distinguishes the c. 300-word "scripture" from what he takes to be the c. 100 extra words of Huángdì's own commentary, producing a unique four-paragraph division of the text.
