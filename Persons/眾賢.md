Zhòngxián 眾賢 (Skt. *Saṃghabhadra* "Good in the Community"; also transliterated 桑伽跋陀羅; DILA A001123; c. 4th–5th century CE) was the leading orthodox Vaibhāṣika Abhidharma master and the principal intellectual opponent of Vasubandhu 世親 ([[世親菩薩]]). He was a Kaśmīri scholar trained in the Sarvāstivāda/Vaibhāṣika tradition and devoted his career to defending the *Mahāvibhāṣā* interpretation against Vasubandhu's *Kośa*.

According to the account in Xuanzang's *Dà Tāng xīyù jì* 大唐西域記, Saṃghabhadra spent twelve years composing the *Nyāyānusāra* 順正理論 as a detailed refutation of the *Kośa*. He originally called the work *Tattvasiddhi*, but after Vasubandhu acknowledged its quality and declined to debate, Saṃghabhadra renamed it *Nyāyānusāra* ("in conformity with correct method"). He also composed the briefer *Xiǎnzōng lùn* 顯宗論 as a positive systematic statement of orthodox Vaibhāṣika teaching.

Works in the Kanripo corpus:

- [[KR6l0032]] 阿毘達磨藏顯宗論 (T29n1563), 40 juan

Both texts were translated by Xuanzang ([[玄奘]]) in the Tang dynasty.
