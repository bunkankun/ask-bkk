Lù Zhīzhù 陸之柷, *fl.* Míng (dates unknown), minor figure attested only as the author of the *Zhèngzhì běncǎo* 證治本草 ([[KR3ec022]]). The text survives only in fragmentary or empty witness in current repositories, and Lù has no independent biographical record in extant Míng sources. No CBDB id located.
