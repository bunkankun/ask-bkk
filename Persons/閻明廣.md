**Yán Míngguǎng** 閻明廣 (fl. mid-12th c., 金), Jīn-dynasty acupuncturist of Chángshān 常山 (modern Héběi). Author of the foundational *Zǐwǔ liúzhù zhēn jīng* 子午流注針經 ([[KR3ee045]], 1153, *zhēnyuán guǐyǒu*), the systematic Jīn-period treatise that establishes the *zǐwǔ liúzhù* chronoacupuncture doctrine — the assignment of specific acupoints to specific celestial-stem / earthly-branch hour-and-day combinations. Yán's work synthesized the earlier 10th-century *Zhǐwēi lùn* 指微論 and *Zhǐwēi zhēn fù* 指微針賦 of Hé Ruòyú 何若愚 (南唐何公). The doctrine was further developed in the JīnYuán by his successor Dòu Hànqīng ([[KR3ee017]]) and systematized into the standard form by the Míng Xú Fèng ([[KR3ee002]]).
