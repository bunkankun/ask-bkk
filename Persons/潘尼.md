Pān Ní 潘尼 (ca. 250–311 CE; *zì* Zhèngshū 正叔) was a Western Jìn 西晉 poet and court official from Yíngchuān 潁川 Zhōngmǔ 中牟, the nephew of the celebrated poet Pān Yuè 潘岳. He held court positions including Tàicháng qīng 太常卿 and perished in the Yǒngjia Disaster 永嘉之亂 of 311 CE when the Xiōngnú sacked Luòyáng. He is evaluated in Zhōng Róng's 鍾嶸 *Shīpǐn* 詩品 in the upper middle grade. His biography appears alongside his uncle's in *Jìnshū* 晉書 juǎn 55. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0035]].
