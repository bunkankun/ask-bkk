Lù Chízhī 陸持之 (1171–1225, *zì* Bówēi 伯微), of *Jīnxī* 金谿. Eldest son and primary editor of his father Lù Jiǔyuān [[陸九淵]]. Compiled the original 32-*juǎn* recension of his father's *Xiàngshān jí* and authored the *niánpǔ* (chronological biography) appended to the collection — the principal source for Lù Jiǔyuān's life. Held minor office. CBDB id 20637. Editor of [[KR4d0253]] *Xiàngshān jí*.
