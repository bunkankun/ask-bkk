Yìwén 益聞, LínjìYángqí (Pòshān → 性珽 line) dharma-grandson of **Xiàngyái Xìngtǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (1598–1651). *Chóngkān* 重刊 (re-cutting) editor of the 4-juan 《象崖珽禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0479]]) — i.e., he prepared the surviving Jiāxīng Canon imprint J34 B308 from an earlier no-longer-circulating recension. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
