Yì-wén 益聞, Línjì-Yángqí (Pòshān → 性珽 line) dharma-grandson of **Xiàng-yái Xìng-tǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (1598–1651). *Chóng-kān* 重刊 (re-cutting) editor of the 4-juan 《象崖珽禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0479]]) — i.e., he prepared the surviving Jiā-xīng Canon imprint J34 B308 from an earlier no-longer-circulating recension. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
