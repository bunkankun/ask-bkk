# 黃超凡

Huáng Chāofán 黃超凡 (referred to as 超凡黃公), late-eighteenth-century Húběi physician active at Wǔchāng. A disciple of [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng's school via [[舒馳遠]] Shū Chíyuǎn; teacher of [[齊秉慧]] Qí Bǐnghuì, who apprenticed under him from 1797 ([[KR3ep074]]).
