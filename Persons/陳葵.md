**Chén Kuí** 陳葵 (*zì* Jìnfū 藎夫, *fl.* mid-late Míng), Míng-period physician of Jiāshàn 嘉善 (Zhèjiāng); disciple of [[徐謙]] Xú Qiān. Editor and trimmer (刪定) of his teacher's ***Rénduān lù*** 仁端錄 ([[KR3e0069]]) for publication. Beyond this editorial role he has no transmitted biographical record.
