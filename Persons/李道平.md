# Lǐ Dàopíng 李道平

*Zì* Yuǎnshān 遠山, *hào* Púmián jūshì 蒲眠居士. Mid-Qīng 清 *kǎozhèng* 考證 scholar, native of Ānlù 安陸 (modern Húběi). His major work is *Zhōuyì jíjiě zuǎn shū* 周易集解纂疏 (preface 1842) — the most ambitious Qīng sub-commentary on [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]), reorganising and re-attributing the *Jíjiě* citations under each master and supplementing them with further evidential material. The work is the standard scholar's gateway into Hàn-Wèi *Yì* citations preserved through the *Jíjiě*.
