Gautama Dharmajñāna 瞿曇法智 (DILA Authority A001879; cf. the disambiguation note pointing to the unrelated 法智 at A000725) was an early Suí-period 隋 monk and translator, son of the Sarvāstivāda master Gautama Prajñāruci 瞿曇般若流支 (who had translated under the Eastern Wèi 東魏 in the mid-sixth century). The biographical information is sparse: the *Lìdài sānbǎo jì* 歷代三寶紀 (T2034) records that he held office as Prefect of Yángchuān 洋川郡守 (in modern Shǎnxī) and translated the [[KR6a0080|*Fó wèi Shǒujiā zhǎngzhě shuō yè-bào chābié jīng* 佛為首迦長者說業報差別經]] (T80) under the Suí. His exact dates are unrecorded.
