**Gotō Sei** 後藤省 (sinicized **Hòuténg Shěng**, *fl.* eighteenth century, 江戶), Japanese physician of the *kohō-ha* 古方派 (ancient-formula school) tradition. The Kanripo catalog assigns him to 清 dynasty (i.e. roughly contemporaneous with mid-Qīng China). Author of the *Shāng fēng yuē yán* 傷風約言 ([[KR3ef074]]) — a short *kohō*-school clinical handbook critical of the Edo *yùn-qì* / six-conformation pedagogical tradition. Distinct from Gotō Konzan 後藤艮山 (1659–1733), the founder of the *kohō* movement (whose name in Kanji form is 後藤艮山 — different from 後藤省). Detailed lifedates are not preserved in standard biographical references; Gotō Sei is one of the many *fl.* eighteenth-century minor *kohō* physicians.
