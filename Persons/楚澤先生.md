**Chǔ zé xiān sheng** 楚澤先生 ("Master of the Chǔ Marshes") was the pseudonymous Táng Daoist compiler of [[KR5c0279]] *Tài qīng shí bì jì* 太清石壁記 — an alchemical compendium compiled 758 or 759. The pen-name suggests a recluse of the lower Yángzǐ / former Chǔ 楚 region; his real name is unrecorded.
