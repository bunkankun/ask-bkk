# Dào-pú 道璞

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[元謐]] Jiàn-rú Yuán-mì (1579–1649); compiler (*jí* 集) of his master's single-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0364]]; X72 n1434). The editorial work was in fact contrary to Yuán-mì's own stated wish — Yuán-mì cultivated a deliberately silent, Chén-Zūn-sù–like reclusive posture, and the surviving *yǔlù* was quietly assembled from fragments by the *rù-shì dì-zǐ* "against the master's intention" (per Huáng Duān-bó's 1637 preface). Lifedates unrecorded.
