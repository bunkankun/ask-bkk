# Dàopú 道璞

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[元謐]] Jiànrú Yuánmì (1579–1649); compiler (*jí* 集) of his master's single-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0364]]; X72 n1434). The editorial work was in fact contrary to Yuánmì's own stated wish — Yuánmì cultivated a deliberately silent, Chén-Zūnsù–like reclusive posture, and the surviving *yǔlù* was quietly assembled from fragments by the *rùshì dìzǐ* "against the master's intention" (per Huáng Duānbó's 1637 preface). Lifedates unrecorded.
