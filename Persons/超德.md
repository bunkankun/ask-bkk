Chāo-dé 超德. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Máo-xī Xíng-sēn** [[行森]] (1614–1677). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0606]] 《明道正覺森禪師語錄》 3 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1642.
