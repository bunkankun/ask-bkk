# Yáng Wúwéi 楊無為

Northern-Sòng lay Chán practitioner and literatus (also known as Yáng Jié 楊傑), author of the *Wǔ zōng sòng* 五宗頌, gathic praise-verses on the Five Houses of Chán, transmitted within the *Rén tiān yǎnmù* compilation of [[智昭]] Zhìzhāo.
