# Máo Bānghàn 毛邦翰

Native place not preserved. Active under Sòng Xiàozōng's Qiándào 乾道 era (1165–1173). Held the post of Professor (*jiàoshòu* 教授) at the Fǔzhōu 撫州 prefectural school. While Yáng Jiǎ's 楊甲 ([[楊甲]]) original *Liù jīng tú* 六經圖 ([[KR1g0006]]) had been engraved on stone in Chāngzhōu under the Shàoxīng era, Máo was tasked by the Fǔzhōu prefect Chén Sēn 陳森 with re-editing, augmenting, and printing the work as a teaching tool in the Fǔzhōu school. The augmented recension — totalling 309 charts according to the *Guǎngé shūmù* 館閣書目 (*Yì* 70, *Shū* 55, *Shī* 47, *Zhōulǐ* 65, *Lǐjì* 43, *Chūnqiū* 29) — is the form in which the work has descended to the present and is the basis of the WYG 10-*juàn* edition. The preface by the Fǔzhōu Erudite Miáo Chāngyán 苗昌言 dates the augmented edition to the first year of Qiándào (1165). Beyond this Máo is known only by the function annotation 補 attached to his name in the WYG transmission line.
