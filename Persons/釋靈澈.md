# Shì Línghé 釋靈澈 (746–816)

Lay surname Tāng 湯, *zì* Yuán-chéng 源澄, native of Yuèzhōu 越州 (Shàoxīng). Major mid-Táng poet-monk of the Jiāngnán Tiāntái 天台-school current, contemporary and close associate of **Jiǎo-rán** 皎然 (in the *Tāng-yuán-tóng zōng-pài*), with whom he travelled extensively. He was patronized in Cháng'ān by the *shì-láng* Bāo Jí 包佶 and Lǐ Shū 李紓 — connections that gave his work circulation beyond the south. A *xù* by **Liú Yǔxī** 劉禹錫 to his ten-*juǎn* collection survives; the *Wén-xiàn tōng-kǎo* cites it. By the SKQS editors' time only one *juǎn* survived, included in the *Táng sì sēng shī* [[KR4h0016]]. CBDB has no entry.
