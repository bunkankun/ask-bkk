Mayanagi Makoto 真柳誠 (b. 1950) is the principal contemporary Japanese historian-philologist of premodern East Asian medical bibliography, long associated with Ibaraki University. His bibliographic-historical work on the Taki / Tamba philological circle and on Edo *kōshōgaku* underwrites all modern study of [[丹波元堅]] Tamba Motokata's *Zábìng guǎngyào* ([[KR3eh008]]).
