Shèngzǔ 聖祖 of the Qīng — Xuányè 玄燁 (1654–1722), reigning under the *Kāngxī* 康熙 era-name (1661–1722), posthumously *Rén-huángdì* 仁皇帝 — second emperor of the Qīng dynasty at Beijing and one of the longest-reigning emperors in Chinese history (61 years). Patron of major *Lǐxué*-canonical compilations: the *Yù zuǎn xìng-lǐ jīng yì* 御纂性理精義 (KR3a0107) under Lǐ Guāngdì's supervision, and the *Yù zuǎn Zhū-zǐ quán shū* 御纂朱子全書 (KR3a0108) co-supervised by Lǐ Guāngdì and Xióng Cì-lǚ 熊賜履. Author of *Shèng yù shí-liù tiáo* 聖諭十六條 (the Sixteen Maxims) which were extended by Yōngzhèng into the *Shèng yù guǎng xùn* 聖諭廣訓 (KR3a0102). Posthumously the *yǔ-lù* of his court conversations were compiled by his successor as *Shèngzǔ Rén-huángdì tíng-xùn gé yán* 聖祖仁皇帝庭訓格言 (KR3a0103).
