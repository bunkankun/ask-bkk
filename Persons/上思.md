Shàng-sī 上思, *zì* **Xuě-wù** 雪悟 ("Snow Awakening"), late-life *hào* **Yǔ-shān** 雨山 ("Rain Mountain"); often referred to as **Yǔ-shān Shàng-sī** 雨山上思. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jù-bó Jì-héng** 巨渤濟恒 (1605–1666). Born **1630** (estimated); died **康熙戊辰二月廿五日** = **1688-03-26**, age 59, *sēng-là* 39. Native of **Tài-zhōu** 泰州 (Jiāng-sū); lay surname **Yú** 於.

**Formation.** Tonsured at **Jìng-yè-ān** 淨業菴 under **Xīn-kāi shàng-rén** 新開上人. Took full ordination from **Jì-héng** 恒和尚. Then *cān*-ed under **Líng-yǐn Jù** 靈隱具 (= **Líng-yǐn Jù-dé Hóng-lǐ**) — served closely. Returned to **Tiān-níng** 天寧 and received final *fù-fǎ* from Jì-héng.

**Abbacies (eight).** **Lú-shān Jìng-hú chán-yuàn** 廬山鏡湖禪院; **Yán-lìng Qìng-yún chán-sì** 延令慶雲禪寺; **Dōng-gǔ Fǎ-lún** 東鼓法輪; **Lóng-shū Bái-yún chán-yuàn** 龍舒白雲禪院; **Wéi-yáng Tiān-níng chán-sì** 維揚天寧禪寺 (= **Yáng-zhōu Tiān-níng-sì** — held *six times* across the *yǔ-lù*, with juans 5–14 documenting recurrent residencies); **Rú-gāo Dà-jué chán-yuàn** 如皋大覺禪院; **Zhāo-yì Jí-lè chán-yuàn** 昭易極樂禪院; **Sān-fēng Qīng-liáng chán-yuàn** 三峰清涼禪院.

**Disciples.** Twelve *fǎ-sì* including **Guǎng-yuán** 廣元.

**Work.** [[KR6q0600]] 《雨山和尚語錄》 20 juan, J40 B494 — among the largest single-author Línjì *yǔ-lù* in the entire Jiā-xīng Canon, comparable to [[KR6q0583]] (Kōng-gǔ Dào-chéng, 20 juan).

**Sources.** 《雨山和尚語錄》 vol. 20 〈揚州天寧雨山思和尚塔銘〉.
