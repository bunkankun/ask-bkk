Shàngsī 上思, *zì* **Xuěwù** 雪悟 ("Snow Awakening"), late-life *hào* **Yǔshān** 雨山 ("Rain Mountain"); often referred to as **Yǔshān Shàngsī** 雨山上思. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jùbó Jìhéng** 巨渤濟恒 (1605–1666). Born **1630** (estimated); died **康熙戊辰二月廿五日** = **1688-03-26**, age 59, *sēnglà* 39. Native of **Tàizhōu** 泰州 (Jiāngsū); lay surname **Yú** 於.

**Formation.** Tonsured at **Jìngyèān** 淨業菴 under **Xīnkāi shàngrén** 新開上人. Took full ordination from **Jìhéng** 恒和尚. Then *cān*-ed under **Língyǐn Jù** 靈隱具 (= **Língyǐn Jùdé Hónglǐ**) — served closely. Returned to **Tiānníng** 天寧 and received final *fùfǎ* from Jìhéng.

**Abbacies (eight).** **Lúshān Jìnghú chányuàn** 廬山鏡湖禪院; **Yánlìng Qìngyún chánsì** 延令慶雲禪寺; **Dōnggǔ Fǎlún** 東鼓法輪; **Lóngshū Báiyún chányuàn** 龍舒白雲禪院; **Wéiyáng Tiānníng chánsì** 維揚天寧禪寺 (= **Yángzhōu Tiānníngsì** — held *six times* across the *yǔlù*, with juans 5–14 documenting recurrent residencies); **Rúgāo Dàjué chányuàn** 如皋大覺禪院; **Zhāoyì Jílè chányuàn** 昭易極樂禪院; **Sānfēng Qīngliáng chányuàn** 三峰清涼禪院.

**Disciples.** Twelve *fǎsì* including **Guǎngyuán** 廣元.

**Work.** [[KR6q0600]] 《雨山和尚語錄》 20 juan, J40 B494 — among the largest single-author Línjì *yǔlù* in the entire Jiāxīng Canon, comparable to [[KR6q0583]] (Kōnggǔ Dàochéng, 20 juan).

**Sources.** 《雨山和尚語錄》 vol. 20 〈揚州天寧雨山思和尚塔銘〉.
