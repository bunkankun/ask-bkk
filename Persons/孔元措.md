51st-generation descendant of Confucius and hereditary holder of the *Yǎnshèng-gōng* 衍聖公 title under the Jīn 金 (and subsequently early Mongol) regimes. He held office under the Jīn as *Zī-zhèng dàfū* 資政大夫, Director of the Academy of Worthies (*Jí-xián yuàn* 集賢院), and concurrent *Tài-cháng chéng* 太常丞 — the senior ritual office of the late-Jīn court. He represents the Northern Kǒng line that remained at Qūfù 曲阜 under Jīn rule (in contrast to the Southern *Yǎnshèng-gōng* line at Qúzhōu 衢州, descended from his uncle Kǒng Duān-yǒu 孔端友). After the Mongol assault on Kāifēng in 1233, he was relocated to the Mongol court and under Ögödei played a key role in re-establishing the *Yǎnshèng-gōng* enfeoffment under early Yuán authority. He is principally known as the compiler of the *Kǒng-shì zǔ-tíng guǎng-jì* 孔氏祖庭廣記 ([[KR2g0006]]), completed in *Zhèng-dà* 4 (1227) — a twelve-*juàn* gazetteer-cum-genealogy of the Kǒng family at Qūfù, supplementing two earlier private Kǒng compilations (now lost) and the southern *Dōngjiā zájì* 東家雜記 ([[KR2g0002]]) of his kinsman Kǒng Chuán.
