# Zhào Dé 趙悳

A Sòng royal-house member (*Sòng zōngshì* 宋宗室). Passed the *jìnshì* in the Sòng. After the Yuán conquest withdrew into reclusion at Dōnghú 東湖 in Yùzhāng 豫章 (modern Nánchāng, Jiāngxī). Lifedates not recoverable.

Author of *Shī biàn shuō* 詩辨說 in 1 *juǎn*, transmitted as an appendix to Zhū Zhuō's *Shī yíwèn* ([[KR1c0031]]) on the editorial logic — preserved in the Sìkù *tíyào* — that "Zhū Zhuō was a *zhōng-liè* and Zhào Dé a *gāo-yǐn*; their personal characters complement each other, so later editors put the two works together." The work is otherwise rare; Zhū Mùjué's *Shòujīng tú* and Jiāo Hóng's *Jīngjí zhì* both record the combined edition (in 6 *juǎn*, against the 7 *juǎn* of the Sìkù base).

(Note: this Zhào Dé is distinct from the Northern-Sòng Zhào Dé, the *Píng-hú jí* compiler. The two share neither lifedates nor scholarly interests.)
