Rúlǐ 如理 was a late-Tang Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng commentator known principally for two surviving works: [[KR6n0038]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shū yìyǎn* 成唯識論疏義演 (X49n0815, 12 fascicles) and [[KR6n0039]] *Chéng wéishí lùn yǎn-mì shì* 成唯識論演秘釋 (X50n0816, 1 fascicle). The two together represent a careful sub-commentarial elaboration of the Cí'ēn corpus on the *Chéng wéishí lùn*. No biographical material survives independently. On internal grounds (his use of [[智周]]'s *yǎn-mì* and [[慧沼]]'s *liǎo-yì dēng*) Rúlǐ is dated to the eighth or early ninth century. He is to be distinguished from earlier figures of the same name (e.g. the Sanlun-school 如理 of the early Tang).
