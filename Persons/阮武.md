Ruǎn Wǔ 阮武 (*fl.* mid 3rd century), *zì* Wényè 文業, was a Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 official and *zǐ*-house writer of the famous Chén-liú 陳留 Wèi-shì 尉氏 Ruǎn lineage. He was an uncle of Ruǎn Jí 阮籍 (210–263) and is mentioned in *Sānguó zhì* 三國志 j. 21 (王粲傳附阮瑀傳) and in *Jìn shū* 晉書 j. 49 (Ruǎn Jí's biography) chiefly as the political teacher of his younger nephew. He held the office of *Qīng-zhōu cì-shǐ* 清河刺史 (Inspector of Qīng-zhōu commandery). His birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally placed in the mid-3rd century. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0141]] *Ruǎnzǐ zhènglùn* 阮子政論. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records this work in 5 *juàn*; only fragments survive.
