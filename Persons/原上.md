# Yuán-shàng 原上

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[慧海|Shuǐ-jiàn Huì-hǎi]] 水鑑慧海 (1626–1687, cf. [[KR6q0440]]) and dharma-brother of [[原澂|Fǎ-lán Yuán-chéng]]. Co-editor (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0442]] 《天王水鑑海和尚住金粟語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B232) — the Jīn-sù-abbacy standalone volume of Huì-hǎi's corpus. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
