# Morten Schlütter

Morten Schlütter, Danish-American scholar of Chinese Buddhism, professor at the University of Iowa. Author of *How Zen Became Zen: The Dispute over Enlightenment and the Formation of Chan Buddhism in Song-Dynasty China* (UH Press, 2008).
