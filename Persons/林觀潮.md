# Lín Guāncháo 林觀潮

Modern Chinese scholar of Sino-Japanese Buddhist exchange; author of the 2010 Chinese-language monographic study 《即非如一》 on the Ōbaku co-founder [[如一|Jífēi Rúyī]].
