# Méi Wénmǐng 梅文鼏

Style names *Ěrbiǎo* 爾表 (per the Sìkù 提要) and *Ěrsù* 爾素 (per his elder brother Méi Wéndǐng's preface to [[KR3f0029]] *Zhōng-Xī jīng-xīng tóng-yì kǎo*). Native of Xuān-chéng 宣城 (Níng-guó prefecture, Ān-huī). Birth and death years not securely recorded; flourished in the Kāngxī period (c. 1641–1710s, roughly contemporary with his elder brother).

The third of the Méi family of Xuān-chéng — younger brother of [[梅文鼎]] Méi Wéndǐng and brother of Méi Wénnài 梅文鼐 (the middle brother). All three Méi brothers worked together on calendrical-and-mathematical study, with Wéndǐng the senior figure but with substantive collaboration with the younger brothers especially in the early phases of the Méi family's mathematical project (Méi Wéndǐng's *Wù'ān lì-suàn shū-jì* records that the *Lì-xué pián-zhī* of 1661 was undertaken jointly by all three brothers under the tutelage of Ní Zhúguān 倪竹冠).

His sole surviving independent work is the *Zhōng-Xī jīng-xīng tóng-yì kǎo* 中西經星同異考 ([[KR3f0029]]), in 2 *juàn*, completed Kāngxī *jǐ-sì* (1689) — a comparative-cataloging study of Chinese-and-Western stellar nomenclature, juxtaposing the Chinese *Bù-tiān gē* 步天歌 of Dān-yuán-zǐ 丹元子 (the canonical Chinese stellar reference of the Suí period) with the Western star catalogs in Ricci's *Jīng-tiān gāi* 經天該, Schall's *Chóngzhēn lì-shū* tables, and Verbiest's *Líng-tái yí-xiàng zhì* of 1674. The work systematically identifies the stars known in both traditions, the stars known in one but not the other, and the differences in nomenclature-and-position-attribution. The 提要 of the work calls it "*the essential thread for the mutual penetration of the Chinese-and-Western two methods*" — a strong statement of its value as a foundational reference for Sino-Western astronomical concordance.

Méi Wénmǐng's broader contribution within the Méi family circle was apparently as the comparative-bibliographic specialist (his *Zhōng-Xī jīng-xīng tóng-yì kǎo* is the most thoroughly comparative of any Méi-family work). He may have served as a research-and-collation assistant on his elder brother's larger projects.
