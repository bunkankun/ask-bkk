Zhàn Ruòshuǐ 湛若水 (1466–1560), *zì* Yuánmíng 元明, *hào* Gānquán 甘泉, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was the founder of the *Gān-quán xué-pài* 甘泉學派 — the third major school of Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* alongside the *Yáo-jiāng* (Wáng Yáng-míng) and the *Hé-Dōng* (Lǚ Nán). Native of Zēng-chéng 增城 (Guǎng-zhōu region, Guǎng-dōng). *Jìnshì* of *Hóng-zhì yǐ-chǒu* (1505); rose under Jiā-jìng to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*, *Bīng-bù shàng-shū*, and *Nán-jīng Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Lived to 95. Major doctrinal innovation: *suí chù tǐ rèn tiān-lǐ* 隨處體認天理 ("everywhere embody-and-recognise *tiān-lǐ*") — a methodological position bridging Wáng Yáng-míng's *liáng zhī* and the more orthodox *Zhū-Lǐxué* *qióng lǐ*. Major works: the *Gé wù tōng* 格物通 (KR3a0094, 100 juan, presented to Jiā-jìng in 1528); the *Chūn qiū zhèng zhuàn* 春秋正傳; the *Gān-quán wén jí* 甘泉文集. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 283 (Rú-lín zhuàn).
