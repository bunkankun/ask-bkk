Zhàn Ruòshuǐ 湛若水 (1466–1560), *zì* Yuánmíng 元明, *hào* Gānquán 甘泉, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was the founder of the *Gānquán xuépài* 甘泉學派 — the third major school of Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* alongside the *Yáojiāng* (Wáng Yángmíng) and the *Hé-Dōng* (Lǚ Nán). Native of Zēngchéng 增城 (Guǎngzhōu region, Guǎngdōng). *Jìnshì* of *Hóngzhì yǐchǒu* (1505); rose under Jiājìng to *Lǐbù shàngshū*, *Bīngbù shàngshū*, and *Nánjīng Lǐbù shàngshū*. Lived to 95. Major doctrinal innovation: *suí chù tǐ rèn tiānlǐ* 隨處體認天理 ("everywhere embody-and-recognise *tiānlǐ*") — a methodological position bridging Wáng Yángmíng's *liáng zhī* and the more orthodox *Zhū-Lǐxué* *qióng lǐ*. Major works: the *Gé wù tōng* 格物通 ([[KR3a0094]], 100 juan, presented to Jiājìng in 1528); the *Chūn qiū zhèng zhuàn* 春秋正傳; the *Gānquán wén jí* 甘泉文集. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 283 (Rúlín zhuàn).
