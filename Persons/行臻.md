Xíng-zhēn 行臻, *zì* **Ruò-wēng** 若翁 (also **Xìng-kōng** 性空 ["Empty Nature"]); often referred to as **Xìng-kōng Xíng-zhēn** 性空行臻. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir. Born **1608** (estimated); died **康熙十七年冬** = **1678** winter (DILA: between 1678-01-23 and 1679-02-10), age 71. Native of **Dài-zhōu** 代州 (Shānxī). Buried in stupa at **Cháng-shú-xiàn Wéi-mó-sì** 常熟縣維摩寺.

**Abbacies (two).** **Sū-zhōu-fǔ Kūn-shān-xiàn Xuǎn-fó-chǎng** 蘇州府崑山縣選佛場 (entered **順治辛丑** = **1661** under the patronage of Manchu *Zhèng-huáng-qí Jiāng-jūn* 正黃旗將軍 **Lǐ-gōng** 李公 alongside *quán-bù* 銓部 **Lǐ Yáo-pǔ** 李瑤圃) — the principal early seat; **Sū-zhōu Cháng-shú-xiàn Wéi-mó-sì** 蘇州常熟縣維摩寺 (the burial site).

**Disciple.** **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** 何一超自 [[超自]], compiler of his *yǔ-lù* and subject of [[KR6q0575]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0574]] 《性空臻禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B461, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** [[超自]] *děng biān*. Two prefaces: dated **康熙三十二年癸酉桂月十五日** = **1693** lunar 8/15 by 中州 (Hé-nán) elder; and **康熙丁丑夏** = **1697** summer by **Zhāng Fā-chén** 中州張發辰.

**Sources.** 《性空臻禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈塔銘〉.
