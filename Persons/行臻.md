Xíngzhēn 行臻, *zì* **Ruòwēng** 若翁 (also **Xìngkōng** 性空 ["Empty Nature"]); often referred to as **Xìngkōng Xíngzhēn** 性空行臻. LínjìYángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir. Born **1608** (estimated); died **康熙十七年冬** = **1678** winter (DILA: between 1678-01-23 and 1679-02-10), age 71. Native of **Dàizhōu** 代州 (Shānxī). Buried in stupa at **Chángshúxiàn Wéimósì** 常熟縣維摩寺.

**Abbacies (two).** **Sūzhōufǔ Kūnshānxiàn Xuǎnfóchǎng** 蘇州府崑山縣選佛場 (entered **順治辛丑** = **1661** under the patronage of Manchu *Zhènghuángqí Jiāngjūn* 正黃旗將軍 **Lǐgōng** 李公 alongside *quánbù* 銓部 **Lǐ Yáopǔ** 李瑤圃) — the principal early seat; **Sūzhōu Chángshúxiàn Wéimósì** 蘇州常熟縣維摩寺 (the burial site).

**Disciple.** **Héyī Chāozì** 何一超自 [[超自]], compiler of his *yǔlù* and subject of [[KR6q0575]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0574]] 《性空臻禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B461, compiled by *fǎsì* **Héyī Chāozì** [[超自]] *děng biān*. Two prefaces: dated **康熙三十二年癸酉桂月十五日** = **1693** lunar 8/15 by 中州 (Hénán) elder; and **康熙丁丑夏** = **1697** summer by **Zhāng Fāchén** 中州張發辰.

**Sources.** 《性空臻禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈塔銘〉.
