**Fāng Yǒuzhí** 方有執 (*zì* Zhōngxíng 中行, born 1523, *fl.* late Wànlì period 萬曆, 明), Míng-period scholar-physician of Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Ānhuī). Author of the influential ***Shānghán lùn tiáobiàn*** 傷寒論條辨 ([[KR3e0082]], 8 juan, with appended *Běncǎo chāo* 本草鈔 1 juan, *Huò wèn* 或問 1 juan, *Jìng shū* 痙書 1 juan; preface 1589, postface 1591, *yǐn* 引 1593, printed Wànlì rénchén 1592). Composed over 20+ years. The work is the foundational late-Míng "**Restore-the-Old-Cháng-shā**" (*Chángshā fùgǔ pài* 長沙復古派) revolutionary critique of the Wáng Shūhé editorial sequence and Chéng Wúyǐ commentary of the *Shānghán lùn* — arguing that both Wáng Shūhé's editorial reorganization and Chéng Wúyǐ's annotation had corrupted Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's original meaning. Fāng's reconstruction of the *Shānghán lùn* attempts to restore the original *Chángshā* (i.e. Hàn-period) textual sequence. The work was the immediate ancestor of [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng's *Shànglùn piān* 尚論篇, and through Yú the foundational document of the late-Míng / early-Qīng *Shānghán* "ancient-text-restoration" school.
