Wú Tínghuá 吳廷華 (1682–1755). *Zì* Zhōnglín 中林; original given name Lánfāng 蘭芳. Native of Rénhé 仁和 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jǔrén* of Kāngxī *jiǎwǔ* (Kāngxī 53 = 1714). Career: rose from *Zhōngshū shělén* 中書舍人 (Drafter of the Central Secretariat) through to *Fújiàn hǎifáng tōngzhī* 福建海防同知 (Joint Magistrate for Coastal Defence of Fújiàn). In the early Qiánlóng era he was recommended for the *Sānlǐ* compilation commission [[KR1d0018]] [[KR1d0037]] [[KR1d0068]].

Author of two Sìkù-admitted *Sānlǐ* commentaries: [[KR1d0042]] *Yílǐ zhāngjù* 儀禮章句 in 17 juan (a chapter-and-section parsed edition of the *Yílǐ* with annotation and section-divisions) and *Zhōulǐ yíyì* 周禮疑義 (separately catalogued in the *Sìkù*). Per Háng Shìjùn's 杭世駿 *Róngchéng shīhuà*: "Tínghuá after retiring stayed at a temple, deeply boring through Jiǎ-and-Kǒng's [[賈公彥]] [[孔穎達]] commentaries, composing more than ten juan of *Èr-lǐ yíyì*" (Doubt-clarifying on the Two Rituals — the *Yílǐ* and *Lǐjì* presumably). The catalog meta separately registers this as *Yílǐ yíyì* but it is unclear if it is a separate work from the *Yílǐ zhāngjù* or a renaming.
