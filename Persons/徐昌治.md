# Xú Chāngzhì 徐昌治

*Zì* Jìnzhōu 覲周, *hào* Wúyī dàorén 無依道人 ("Daoist of No Dependence"). Late-Míng / early-Qīng lay Buddhist writer. Native of Hǎiyán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng). Born 1582; death year not firmly transmitted — active well into the 1660s / early 1670s.

Lay disciple and prolific editor of Chán and Pure Land materials; worked under the patronage of major late-Míng monks including Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟. Best known for the *Pò xié jí* 闢邪集 (1639), an anti-Christian polemical compendium gathering Buddhist and Confucian responses to the Jesuit mission, which became a major primary source for seventeenth-century Buddhist-Christian controversy.

Other works include the *Zǔtíng zhǐnán* 祖庭指南 ([[KR6q0048]]) and the *Gāosēng zhāiyào* 高僧摘要 (selected monastic biographies). Substantial lay output within the late-Míng Buddhist publishing ecosystem.
