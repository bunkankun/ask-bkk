Late-Ming physician of Gāoyóu 高郵 (Jiāngsū, "珠湖之浜"); *zì* Tǐ-ān 體庵. Served as *mù-bīn* 幕賓 — military-staff adviser and physician — to the Ming-loyalist general Shǐ Kěfǎ 史可法 in the Yángzhōu defence administration. The preface to his medical handbook [[KR3eb010]] *Zhèng zhì xīn chuán* (1643) is by Shǐ Kěfǎ himself, dated *Chóngzhēn guǐ-wèi* 崇禎癸未 mid-autumn = autumn 1643, less than two years before Shǐ's death at the fall of Yángzhōu (1645). No CBDB record. His clinical idiom blends the Jīn-Yuán doctrinal heritage (Liú Wánsù, Zhū Zhènhēng) with early-17th-century Jiāng-Huái practice.
