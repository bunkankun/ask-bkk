**Cuī Róng** 崔融 (653–706), *zì* Ān chéng 安成, was a Táng scholar-official under Wǔ Zé tiān 武則天 and Táng Zhōng zōng — one of the "Four Friends of Cuī" (崔氏四友) literary circle, noted for ceremonial prose. Author of [[KR5c0371]] *Táng Sōng gāo shān Qǐ mǔ miào bēi míng* 唐嵩高山啟母廟碑銘 — a stele-inscription for the Qǐ mǔ 啟母 ("Mother of Qǐ" = mother of Xià Qǐ 夏啟, i.e., the wife of Great Yǔ 禹) Temple at Sōng shān 嵩山.
