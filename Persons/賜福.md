# Cìfú 賜福

Mid-Qīng literary figure (perhaps a sobriquet rather than a personal name); composed the *Jīnlǚqū* 金縷曲 *cí* inscription verse (*tící* 題辭) prefacing the paediatric polemical treatise *Érkē xǐng* 兒科醒 ([[KR3ej008]]) by [[芝嶼樵客]] Zhīyǔ qiáokè. Lifedates unrecorded.
