Juéluó Shílín 覺羅石麟 (b. unknown, d. Qiánlóng 12 = 1747), member of the *Juéluó* (collateral imperial line — collateral descendants of the *Tàizǔ* line, holding the secondary imperial-clan rank below *Zōngshì* 宗室). His career: Shānxī governor (Yōngzhèng 5 = 1727 – Qiánlóng 9 = 1744) — an exceptionally long Shānxī governorship for the Yōngzhèng era — in which capacity he supervised the *Shānxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0049]]) from 1729 through to its completion in Yōngzhèng 12/3 (1734/4); his preface dated to that month is preserved at the head of the gazetteer. CBDB id 57375; death year per *Qīngdài rénwù shēng-zú niánbiǎo*. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 297 has his biography. Not to be confused with the early-Yuán official Shílín 石麟 (CBDB 101757), with whom there is no connection beyond name.
