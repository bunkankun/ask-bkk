Xiè Méng 解蒙, zì Qiúwǒ 求我, was a Yuán-dynasty *Yìjīng* scholar from Jíshuǐ 吉水 (Jí'ān 吉安, Jiāngxī 江西). The *Jiāngxī tōngzhì* 江西通志 spells his *zì* as Láiwǒ 來我, but the *Sìkù* editors regard this as a graphic confusion with Qiúwǒ 求我 (the characters 求 and 來 being similar in form). He sat the Jiāngxī provincial examination in *tiānlì yǐsì* 天厯乙巳 (1329) together with his elder brother's son Xiè Zǐshàng 解子尚 (zì Guānwǒ 觀我); the two were jointly known at the time as adept *Yìjīng* commentators. Xiè Zǐshàng's own *Zhōuyì yìyí tōngshì* 周易義疑通釋 was already lost by the early Qīng. Xiè Méng's *Yì jīngyùn dàyì* 易精蘊大義 ([[KR1a0084]]) was likewise listed as lost in Zhū Yízūn's 朱彝尊 *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考, but was substantially recovered by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* 永樂大典 (only seven hexagrams — *Yù* 豫, *Suí* 隨, *Wúwàng* 无妄, *Dàzhuàng* 大壯, *Kuí* 睽, *Jiǎn* 蹇, *Zhōngfú* 中孚 — and the last four lines of *Jìn* 晉 are missing).

The work's title is variably reported. Xiè Jìn's 解縉 *Chūnyǔ táng jí* 春雨堂集 calls it *Yìjīng jīngyì* 易經精義; Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* calls it *Zhōuyì jīngyùn* 周易精蘊; the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* itself ascribes it to Xiè Méng under the title *Zhōuyì jīngyùn dàyì* 周易精蘊大義. The *Sìkù* editors take the *Dàdiǎn* form as authoritative.
