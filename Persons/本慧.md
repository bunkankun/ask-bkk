# Běnhuì 本慧

Southern Sòng Vinaya monk, disciple of [[妙生]] Miàoshēng of *Huìjī* 會稽. Recorded as having reprinted his master's *Sānyī xiǎnzhèng tú* 三衣顯正圖 ([[KR6k0254]], X1103) in *Shàoxīng* 紹興 16 (1146). Possibly identifiable with the *Běnhuì* recorded in *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀 *juàn* 27. No further biographical material preserved.
