**Máo Péng** 毛鵬 (*fl.* 1829), Chángshā 長沙 (Húnán) publisher and medical compiler. Author of the 1829 (*Dàoguāng jǐchǒu* 道光九年) reprint preface to [[文起]] Wén Qǐ's *Dòukē jíyào* 痘科輯要 ([[KR3ej060]]), which he re-issued from a family copy donated by Jīn Zhújūn 金竹均 (son of the original sponsor [[金菊圃]] Jīn Júpǔ); he also incorporated a parallel *Dòukē mìchuán* manuscript from Guǎngdōng as a supplement.
