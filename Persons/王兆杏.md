Wáng Zhàoxìng 王兆杏 (*hào* Dìhuā 第花, *fl.* 1830s, 清) was a Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu) physician-editor whose principal recorded act is the recovery, editing, and 1838 (Dàoguāng 18, *wùxū*) printing of the *Yùxuǎn gǔfāng shìyàn* 喻選古方試驗 [[KR3ed057]] — a *Bencao gāngmù*-derived formulary marked with selections by the early-Qīng master physician Yù Jiāyán 喻嘉言 [[喻昌]], which Wáng received via the bureau official Wāng Zhúyǐn 汪竹隱 and supplemented with his own clinically tested formulas. The work's preface is the principal source for Wáng's life; he was evidently a literatus-physician working in Hángzhōu in the late Dàoguāng decades. No CBDB entry.
