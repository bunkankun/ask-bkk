**Lǚ Nángōng** 呂南公 (fl. *Xīníng*–early *Yuányòu*, c. 1072–1086), *zì* Cìrú 次儒, *hào* Guànyuán xiānshēng 灌園先生, of Nánchéng 南城 (Jiāngxī Fǔzhōu). *Sòng shǐ* j. 444 (Wényuán) has his biography. Read widely; refused to bend his prose to the *Xīnfǎ* / Wáng Ānshí curricular orthodoxy; failed his single try at the *jìnshì* examination; built a hermitage and "watered the garden" (*guànyuán*) without further career attempts. *Yuányòu* 1 (1086): Zēng Zhào 曾肇 *Zhōngshū shèrén* recommended him for the *shíkē* (ten-categories recommendation) as exemplary of *bù shì sú xué, ān pín shǒu dào* — but Lǚ died before the appointment could be made. Wished to compose a *Sānguó zhì*-revision under the studio-name *Gǔnfǔ* 衮斧 ("Brocade-and-Axe", from Confucian *Chūnqiū* tradition); died before completion. Author of *Guànyuán jí* 灌園集 [[KR4d0121]] originally in 30 *juǎn* compiled by his son Lǚ Yù 呂郁; *Sìkù* reconstruction is 20 *juǎn*. Lifedates not securely established.
