**Lǚ Nán-gōng** 呂南公 (fl. *Xī-níng*–early *Yuán-yòu*, c. 1072–1086), *zì* Cì-rú 次儒, *hào* Guàn-yuán xiānshēng 灌園先生, of Nán-chéng 南城 (Jiāng-xī Fǔ-zhōu). *Sòng shǐ* j. 444 (Wén-yuán) has his biography. Read widely; refused to bend his prose to the *Xīn-fǎ* / Wáng Ān-shí curricular orthodoxy; failed his single try at the *jìnshì* examination; built a hermitage and "watered the garden" (*guàn-yuán*) without further career attempts. *Yuán-yòu* 1 (1086): Zēng Zhào 曾肇 *Zhōng-shū shè-rén* recommended him for the *shí-kē* (ten-categories recommendation) as exemplary of *bù shì sú xué, ān pín shǒu dào* — but Lǚ died before the appointment could be made. Wished to compose a *Sān-guó zhì*-revision under the studio-name *Gǔn-fǔ* 衮斧 ("Brocade-and-Axe", from Confucian *Chūn-qiū* tradition); died before completion. Author of *Guàn-yuán jí* 灌園集 [[KR4d0121]] originally in 30 *juǎn* compiled by his son Lǚ Yù 呂郁; *Sìkù* reconstruction is 20 *juǎn*. Lifedates not securely established.
