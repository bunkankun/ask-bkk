Xú Yìng-qiū 徐應秋 (*fl.* 1616–1640), *zì* Jūn-yì 君義; native of Xī-ān 西安 (Qú-zhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Wàn-lì bǐng-chén* (1616). Held office through provincial administration to *Fú-jiàn zuǒ bù-zhèng-shǐ* (Provincial Administration Commissioner of Fú-jiàn). His studio name *Yù-zhī táng* 玉芝堂 (Jade-Fungus Hall) gives the title to his principal work, the *Yù-zhī táng tán huì* 玉芝堂談薈 ([[KR3j0186]]) — a 36-*juàn* late-Míng miscellany ranked by the Sìkù editors with Lǐ Fǎng's *Tài-píng guǎng jì* and Táo Zōng-yí's *Shuō fú*. No firm lifedates are recorded in the standard biographical compilations.
