Xú Yìngqiū 徐應秋 (*fl.* 1616–1640), *zì* Jūnyì 君義; native of Xīān 西安 (Qúzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Wànlì bǐngchén* (1616). Held office through provincial administration to *Fújiàn zuǒ bùzhèngshǐ* (Provincial Administration Commissioner of Fújiàn). His studio name *Yùzhī táng* 玉芝堂 (Jade-Fungus Hall) gives the title to his principal work, the *Yùzhī táng tán huì* 玉芝堂談薈 ([[KR3j0186]]) — a 36-*juàn* late-Míng miscellany ranked by the Sìkù editors with Lǐ Fǎng's *Tàipíng guǎng jì* and Táo Zōngyí's *Shuō fú*. No firm lifedates are recorded in the standard biographical compilations.
