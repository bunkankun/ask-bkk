**Zhāng Xiáxī** 張霞谿 (*míng* Lián 廉, *zì* Tōngyuán 通源, *fl. Dàoguāng* era, mid-19th c.), Qīng physician and Confucian scholar of Zhūjì 諸暨 (Zhèjiāng). A *gòngshì* 貢士 (tribute student / metropolitan examination presentee) of his native county, *bótōng jīngshǐ* 博通經史 (broadly conversant in canonical and historical literature), and an *jǔyè* (eight-legged-essay) instructor; many of his pupils succeeded in the imperial examinations. *Lǚ bùdé zhì yú míngchǎng* 屢不得志於名場 (repeatedly frustrated in the examination arena), he turned in his middle years to medical writing as a *yǒu guān yú shìdào rénxīn* 有關於世道人心 (means of acting on the moral order and public well-being) project. His *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* 麻疹闡註 ([[KR3ej046]]) — an elucidatory commentary on the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* measles chapter — is the principal medical work credited to him. The work was edited and published posthumously by his clan-nephew Jíyuán 輯園, his nephew Huìpō 蕙坡, his brother Zǐtíng 紫庭, and his clan-cousin Chónggāo 重皋, under the supervision of Lìzǐ Fēiān 酈子非葊.
