Liào Zì-shēn 廖自伸 (fl. late 16th cent.), *zì* Bó-cháng 伯常, native of Huáng-gāng 黄岡 (Hú-běi). He served as *zhī Hǎi-mén xiàn-shì* (Magistrate of Hǎi-mén) in late *Wàn-lì*. His sole prominent surviving trace is his *Wàn-lì yǐ-wèi* (1595) preface to [[彭大翼]]'s *Shān-táng sì-kǎo* 山堂肆考 ([[KR3k0052]]), in which he frames the work via the Wáng Tōng / Mǎ Féng-zhì trope of frustrated examination ambition channelled into encyclopaedic compilation.
