Liào Zìshēn 廖自伸 (fl. late 16th cent.), *zì* Bócháng 伯常, native of Huánggāng 黄岡 (Húběi). He served as *zhī Hǎimén xiànshì* (Magistrate of Hǎimén) in late *Wànlì*. His sole prominent surviving trace is his *Wànlì yǐwèi* (1595) preface to [[彭大翼]]'s *Shāntáng sìkǎo* 山堂肆考 ([[KR3k0052]]), in which he frames the work via the Wáng Tōng / Mǎ Féngzhì trope of frustrated examination ambition channelled into encyclopaedic compilation.
