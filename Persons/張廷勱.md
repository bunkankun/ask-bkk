Zhāng Tíng-mài 張廷勱. Late-17th-century lay disciple and preface-author. Lifedates unknown. Author of the principal preface to [[KR6q0558]] 《玉眉亮禪師語錄》 dated **康熙庚午秋九月上浣** = **1690** (Kāngxī 29, lunar 9, early decade). Catalog dynasty: 清.
