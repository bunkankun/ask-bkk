Wáng Guàn 王觀, Míng-period *Hóng-zhì* (1488–1505) period editor; tenth-generation descendant of the Sòng *Dào-xué* figure Wáng Píng 王蘋 [[王蘋]] (1082–1153). Compiler of the present 8-*juǎn* recension of his ancestor's *Wáng zhù-zuò jí* 王著作集 [[KR4d0185]] from family-preserved papers, image-encomia, *Sòng* recommendation memorials, colophons, pupil notes, etc. The Sòng-period 4-*juǎn* recension cut by Wáng Píng's great-grandson Wáng Sī-wén in *Bǎo-yòu* (with preface by Lú Yuè) had been substantially lost.

CBDB has no clearly-matching entry; multiple Wáng Guàn homonyms exist in CBDB without distinguishing dates.

# Wáng Guàn 王觀 (Northern Sòng; *zì* Dá-sǒu)

Northern-Sòng official and *pǔ-lù* author, *zì* Dá-sǒu 達叟. Native of Rú-gāo 如皋 in Yángzhōu (modern Jiāngsū). The *Sìkù* editors correct Wāng Shìxián's 汪士賢 *Shān-jū zá-zhì* recension, which calls him "*Jiāng-dū rén*" — but the *Jiājìng Wéi-yáng zhì* makes him a Rú-gāo native. (Note: do not confuse with the Yuán-Fēng-era Sòng *cí*-poet Wáng Guàn 王觀, also of Rú-gāo, *zì* Tōng-sǒu 通叟 — the *Bǔ-suàn-zǐ* author; the two are sometimes conflated but appear to be distinct persons, or possibly the same with two *zì*.)

During Xī-níng (1068–1077) Wáng held the post of *jiāng-shì láng zhī Yángzhōu Jiāng-dū xiàn* — a *Tài-cháng cì-shǐ* (Grand Constancy Court junior) appointment in the Yángzhōu *Jiāng-dū* magistracy. While in office he composed the *Yángzhōu fù* 揚州賦 and submitted it to the emperor; he received high imperial praise and was awarded a *fēi-yī yín-zhāng* (red-robe-and-silver-fish ceremonial honor). His career-record is in the *Jiā-jìng Wéi-yáng zhì*.

His one surviving *pǔ-lù* work, the *Yáng-zhōu sháo-yào pǔ* 揚州芍藥譜 ([[KR3i0030]]) — the first systematic treatise on the *sháo-yào* (herbaceous peony, *Paeonia lactiflora*) — is the most authoritative Sòng monograph on the herbaceous peony tradition centered at Yángzhōu, complementing Ōuyáng Xiū's [[KR3i0029]] on the tree-peony of Luòyáng.
