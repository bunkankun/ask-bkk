Wáng Guàn 王觀, Míng-period *Hóngzhì* (1488–1505) period editor; tenth-generation descendant of the Sòng *Dàoxué* figure Wáng Píng 王蘋 [[王蘋]] (1082–1153). Compiler of the present 8-*juǎn* recension of his ancestor's *Wáng zhùzuò jí* 王著作集 [[KR4d0185]] from family-preserved papers, image-encomia, *Sòng* recommendation memorials, colophons, pupil notes, etc. The Sòng-period 4-*juǎn* recension cut by Wáng Píng's great-grandson Wáng Sīwén in *Bǎoyòu* (with preface by Lú Yuè) had been substantially lost.

# Wáng Guàn 王觀 (Northern Sòng; *zì* Dásǒu)

Northern-Sòng official and *pǔlù* author, *zì* Dásǒu 達叟. Native of Rúgāo 如皋 in Yángzhōu (modern Jiāngsū). The *Sìkù* editors correct Wāng Shìxián's 汪士賢 *Shānjū zázhì* recension, which calls him "*Jiāngdū rén*" — but the *Jiājìng Wéiyáng zhì* makes him a Rúgāo native. (Note: do not confuse with the Yuán-Fēng-era Sòng *cí*-poet Wáng Guàn 王觀, also of Rúgāo, *zì* Tōngsǒu 通叟 — the *Bǔsuànzǐ* author; the two are sometimes conflated but appear to be distinct persons, or possibly the same with two *zì*.)

During Xīníng (1068–1077) Wáng held the post of *jiāngshì láng zhī Yángzhōu Jiāngdū xiàn* — a *Tàicháng cìshǐ* (Grand Constancy Court junior) appointment in the Yángzhōu *Jiāngdū* magistracy. While in office he composed the *Yángzhōu fù* 揚州賦 and submitted it to the emperor; he received high imperial praise and was awarded a *fēiyī yínzhāng* (red-robe-and-silver-fish ceremonial honor). His career-record is in the *Jiājìng Wéiyáng zhì*.

His one surviving *pǔlù* work, the *Yángzhōu sháoyào pǔ* 揚州芍藥譜 ([[KR3i0030]]) — the first systematic treatise on the *sháoyào* (herbaceous peony, *Paeonia lactiflora*) — is the most authoritative Sòng monograph on the herbaceous peony tradition centered at Yángzhōu, complementing Ōuyáng Xiū's [[KR3i0029]] on the tree-peony of Luòyáng.
