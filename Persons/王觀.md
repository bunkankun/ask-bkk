Wáng Guàn 王觀, Míng-period *Hóng-zhì* (1488–1505) period editor; tenth-generation descendant of the Sòng *Dào-xué* figure Wáng Píng 王蘋 [[王蘋]] (1082–1153). Compiler of the present 8-*juǎn* recension of his ancestor's *Wáng zhù-zuò jí* 王著作集 [[KR4d0185]] from family-preserved papers, image-encomia, *Sòng* recommendation memorials, colophons, pupil notes, etc. The Sòng-period 4-*juǎn* recension cut by Wáng Píng's great-grandson Wáng Sī-wén in *Bǎo-yòu* (with preface by Lú Yuè) had been substantially lost.
