Wú Zhuō 吳焯 (1676–1733, *zì* Chì-fú 尺鳬, *hào* Xiù-gǔ 繡谷), of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Early-Kāngxī / Yōng-zhèng Háng-zhōu *shī-rén*; major bibliophile (his *Yùn-tuán bié-shù* 韻團別墅 library was a noted Qián-táng private collection). Member of the Háng-zhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È, contributing 100 poems to the collaborative *Nán-Sòng zá-shì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]] (c. 1729). Also produced *Bǎo-jiè táng cí jí* 抱經堂詞集 (his collected *cí*) and various bibliographic notes. CBDB id 338342, lifedates 1676–1733.
