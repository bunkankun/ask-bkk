Wú Zhuō 吳焯 (1676–1733, *zì* Chìfú 尺鳬, *hào* Xiùgǔ 繡谷), of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Early-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng Hángzhōu *shīrén*; major bibliophile (his *Yùntuán biéshù* 韻團別墅 library was a noted Qiántáng private collection). Member of the Hángzhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È, contributing 100 poems to the collaborative *NánSòng záshì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]] (c. 1729). Also produced *Bǎojiè táng cí jí* 抱經堂詞集 (his collected *cí*) and various bibliographic notes. CBDB id 338342, lifedates 1676–1733.
