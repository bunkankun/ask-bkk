Xuéyùn 學蘊, *hào* **Zhīkōng** 知空 ("Knower of Emptiness"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Kāifēng Mìxíng Jìrěn** 開峰密行寂忍. Born **萬曆癸丑 9/1** = **1613-10-14 Gregorian**; died **康熙己巳 5/28 午時** = **1689-07-14 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **77**, *sēnglà* **65**. Native of **Ěrhǎi** 洱海 (Yúnnán); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** At 14 (1626) entered **Jīzúshān Jìguāngsì** 雞足寂光寺 under **Shànghé** 上和和尚. At 36 (1648) at **Yùlín Xuān** 玉林軒 reciting *Wànfómíng jīng* — at the second character 南無 of juan 3, breakthrough. Studied under **Shuǐmù Wúzhù** 水目無住; final *fǎsì* under **Kāifēng Mìxíng Jìrěn**.

**Five abbacies (Yúnnán region).** **Kāifēng Nányún** 開峰南雲, **Chǔxióng Fúchéng** 楚雄福城, **Jiǔtái Fāngguǎng** 九臺方廣, **Guǎngtōng Xiānyáng** 廣通仙羊, **Jìnglè** 淨樂.

**Work.** [[KR6q0523]] 《知空蘊禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B400, compiled by *fǎsì* **Tōnglái** [[通來]] *děng biān*; juan 1 *xínglù*. Also 《草堂集》 separately.
