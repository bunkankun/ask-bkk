Xué-yùn 學蘊, *hào* **Zhī-kōng** 知空 ("Knower of Emptiness"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Kāi-fēng Mì-xíng Jì-rěn** 開峰密行寂忍. Born **萬曆癸丑 9/1** = **1613-10-14 Gregorian**; died **康熙己巳 5/28 午時** = **1689-07-14 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **77**, *sēng-là* **65**. Native of **Ěr-hǎi** 洱海 (Yún-nán); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** At 14 (1626) entered **Jī-zú-shān Jì-guāng-sì** 雞足寂光寺 under **Shàng-hé** 上和和尚. At 36 (1648) at **Yù-lín Xuān** 玉林軒 reciting *Wàn-fó-míng jīng* — at the second character 南無 of juan 3, breakthrough. Studied under **Shuǐ-mù Wú-zhù** 水目無住; final *fǎ-sì* under **Kāi-fēng Mì-xíng Jì-rěn**.

**Five abbacies (Yún-nán region).** **Kāi-fēng Nán-yún** 開峰南雲, **Chǔ-xióng Fú-chéng** 楚雄福城, **Jiǔ-tái Fāng-guǎng** 九臺方廣, **Guǎng-tōng Xiān-yáng** 廣通仙羊, **Jìng-lè** 淨樂.

**Work.** [[KR6q0523]] 《知空蘊禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B400, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Tōng-lái** [[通來]] *děng biān*; juan 1 *xíng-lù*. Also 《草堂集》 separately.
