Shēn Hánguāng 申涵光 (1619–1677), *zì* **Fúmèng** 孚孟 (also written 鳧盟), *hào* **Cōngshān** 聰山; native of **Yǒngnián** 永年 (Héběi). Eldest son of the late-Míng martyr [[申佳胤]] (1603–1644). After his father's suicide at the *Chóngzhēn* end-times of 1644 he refused the new dynasty's calls to office and lived out his life as a *yímín* in Yǒngnián. Best known as the founder of the early-Qīng **Jīfǔ shīpài** 畿輔詩派 ('Northern Metropolitan poetry school' — i.e. the Hé-běi-based Northern alternative to the dominant WúYuè and Jiāngnán schools); with his younger brothers Hánpàn 涵盼 and Hánfēn 涵盻 (the *Yǒngnián Shēn shì sān xiōngdì* 永年申氏三兄弟) and his disciples Yǐn Yuè 殷岳, Liú Yīn 劉因 and others, he constituted the dominant Northern poetic circle of the *Shùnzhì*–*Kāngxī* transition. His own collected works are the *Cōngshānjí xuǎn* 聰山集選. He edited his father's surviving verse into the *Shēn Zhōngmǐn shījí* 申忠愍詩集 in six *juǎn* ([[KR4e0242]]) and supplied a *jiāzhuàn* (family biography) of his father, which the *Sìkù* tiyao quotes. The standard biography is in *Qīngshǐ lièzhuàn*. CBDB 65917.
