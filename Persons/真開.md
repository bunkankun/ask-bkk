# Zhēnkāi 真開

Late-seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[元|Yīchū Wùyuán]] 一初悟元 (second entry) in the Wúqù Rúkōng / Nánmíng / Miàoyòng / Yīchū sub-line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0444]] 《一初元禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B234), working with [[真智]] Zhēnzhì. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0444]] juan-head signature.
