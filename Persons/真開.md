# Zhēn-kāi 真開

Late-seventeenth-century Cáo-dòng Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[元|Yī-chū Wù-yuán]] 一初悟元 (second entry) in the Wú-qù Rú-kōng / Nán-míng / Miào-yòng / Yī-chū sub-line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0444]] 《一初元禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B234), working with [[真智]] Zhēn-zhì. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0444]] juan-head signature.
