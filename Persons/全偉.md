# Quánwěi 全偉

Late-Táng monk at the Xīndìng Zhìtōng yuàn 新定智通院; tonsuring master of [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益 (885–958). DILA registers the name variant as Tóngwěi 同偉.
