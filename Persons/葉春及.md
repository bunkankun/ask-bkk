Yè Chūnjí 葉春及 (1532–1595), *zì* Huàpǔ 化浦, of Guīshàn 歸善 (Huì-zhōu, Guǎng-dōng). Took the *jǔrén* in Jiā-jìng 31 (1552); officed to *Hùbù lángzhōng*. As *Fú-qīng jiàoyù* he submitted a *sān-wàn yán* (30,000-word) memorial on contemporary politics. As *Huì-ān zhīxiàn* he was famous for his administrative achievements — *dāng-shí dì-yī* in the *Sìkù* tíyào's report — producing the *Huì-ān zhèng-shū* in 12 *piān*. He was raised to replace **Ài Mù** as *xúnfǔ Sì-Chuān*'s deputy; later served as *Bīn-zhōu zhī-zhōu*. His intellectual affiliation was with **Chén Xiànzhāng** (Bái-shā). His collection is the *Shí-dòng jí* [[KR4e0208]]. CBDB 34724.
