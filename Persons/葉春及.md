Yè Chūnjí 葉春及 (1532–1595), *zì* Huàpǔ 化浦, of Guīshàn 歸善 (Huìzhōu, Guǎngdōng). Took the *jǔrén* in Jiājìng 31 (1552); officed to *Hùbù lángzhōng*. As *Fúqīng jiàoyù* he submitted a *sānwàn yán* (30,000-word) memorial on contemporary politics. As *Huìān zhīxiàn* he was famous for his administrative achievements — *dāngshí dìyī* in the *Sìkù* tíyào's report — producing the *Huìān zhèngshū* in 12 *piān*. He was raised to replace **Ài Mù** as *xúnfǔ Sìchuān*'s deputy; later served as *Bīnzhōu zhīzhōu*. His intellectual affiliation was with **Chén Xiànzhāng** (Báishā). His collection is the *Shídòng jí* [[KR4e0208]]. CBDB 34724.
