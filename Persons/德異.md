# Déyì 德異

Late-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán monk of the Xiūxiū chán'ān 休休禪庵 at Wúzhōng 吳中, styled Gǔjūn bǐqiū 古筠比丘; supplied the textual base for the 1291 [[宗寶]] Zōngbǎo recension of the *Tánjīng* (Platform Sūtra) by issuing his own recovered long-recension printing the previous year (1290).
