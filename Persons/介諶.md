# Jièchén 介諶

Southern-Sòng Chán monk; dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) of Chánglíng Shǒuzhuó 長靈守卓 (1065–1123). Lifedates unrecorded. Editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0282]].
