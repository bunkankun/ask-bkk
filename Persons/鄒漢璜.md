Late-Qīng physician of the Zōu medical family, otherwise undocumented. Author of *Zōu shì hán-yì lùn* 鄒氏寒疫論 ([[KR3eg036]]), a focused monograph on **cold-pestilence** (*hán-yì* 寒疫) — the category of epidemic febrile disease driven by cold rather than heat aetiology, an analytical contrast to the dominant Wú Yǒu-xìng / wēn-yì line. The Zōu family produced multiple physicians in the late Qīng (cf. Zōu Hàn-xūn 鄒漢勳); Zōu Hàn-huáng's specific lifedates are not preserved in readily accessible reference works. No CBDB record.
