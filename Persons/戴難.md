Dài Nán 戴難 (mid-15th cent.), pupil of **Lǐ Shí-miǎn** 李時勉 ([[李時勉]]), the great Zhèng-tǒng-era *Guó-zǐ-jiàn jì-jiǔ*. Compiled the surviving manuscripts of his teacher's writings into [[KR4e0098]] *Gǔ-lián wén-jí* in the Chéng-huà era. The compilation became necessary because Lǐ's draft manuscripts had been carried off by his contemporaries on release (*tuō-gǎo duō wéi rén chí-qù*). No further biographical detail preserved.
