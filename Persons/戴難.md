Dài Nán 戴難 (mid-15th cent.), pupil of **Lǐ Shímiǎn** 李時勉 ([[李時勉]]), the great Zhèng-tǒng-era *Guózǐjiàn jìjiǔ*. Compiled the surviving manuscripts of his teacher's writings into [[KR4e0098]] *Gǔlián wénjí* in the Chénghuà era. The compilation became necessary because Lǐ's draft manuscripts had been carried off by his contemporaries on release (*tuōgǎo duō wéi rén chíqù*). No further biographical detail preserved.
