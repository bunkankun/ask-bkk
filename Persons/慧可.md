# Huìkě 慧可

Second Patriarch of Chinese Chán in the canonical lineage; dharma-heir of [[菩提達磨]] Bodhidharma; teacher of [[僧璨]] Sēngcàn. Traditional dates 487–593. Subject of the famous "pacify-the-mind" exchange with Bodhidharma preserved in the *Ānxīn fǎmén* of [[KR6q0084]] *Shǎoshì liù mén*.
