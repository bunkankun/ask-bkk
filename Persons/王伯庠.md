# Wáng Bóxiáng 王伯庠

Southern-Sòng official, *zuǒ cháofèng dàfū shì yùshǐ* 左朝奉大夫侍御史; wrote the biographical *xíngshí* 行實 of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué (juan 9 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* [[KR6q0070]]), dated Qiándào 2 (1166).
