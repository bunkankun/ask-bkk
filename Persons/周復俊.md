Mid-Míng official and anthologist. **CBDB id 128888, dates 1496–1574**. *Zì* **Bócháng** 伯常. Native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of **Jiājìng *gēngxū* (1550)** per SKQS — though CBDB has him as earlier; this would make him much older than typical at *jìnshì*. (CBDB dates may be of different person; SKQS reliably places him as mid-Jiā-jìng.)

The *Jiāngnán tōngzhì* gives his name as "**周後淑**" *Zhōu Hòushū*, which the SKQS editors identify as a transcription error.

Served as **Sìchuān *ànchásī fùshǐ*** (Vice Commissioner of Surveillance for Sìchuān) under Jiājìng. During this tenure he compiled the **[[KR4h0109]]** *Quán-Shǔ yìwén zhì* 全蜀藝文志 (64 *juǎn*), the canonical Míng regional documentary anthology of Sìchuān — prefaced **Jiājìng *rényín* (1542), 4th month**. The work is methodologically significant for establishing the regional *yìwén zhì* genre as a parallel to the dynastic-history *yìwén zhì*.
