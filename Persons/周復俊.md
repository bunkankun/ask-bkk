Mid-Míng official and anthologist. **CBDB id 128888, dates 1496–1574**. *Zì* **Bó-cháng** 伯常. Native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of **Jiā-jìng *gēng-xū* (1550)** per SKQS — though CBDB has him as earlier; this would make him much older than typical at *jìnshì*. (CBDB dates may be of different person; SKQS reliably places him as mid-Jiā-jìng.)

The *Jiāng-nán tōng-zhì* gives his name as "**周後淑**" *Zhōu Hòu-shū*, which the SKQS editors identify as a transcription error.

Served as **Sì-chuān *àn-chá-sī fù-shǐ*** (Vice Commissioner of Surveillance for Sì-chuān) under Jiā-jìng. During this tenure he compiled the **[[KR4h0109]]** *Quán-Shǔ yì-wén zhì* 全蜀藝文志 (64 *juǎn*), the canonical Míng regional documentary anthology of Sì-chuān — prefaced **Jiā-jìng *rén-yín* (1542), 4th month**. The work is methodologically significant for establishing the regional *yì-wén zhì* genre as a parallel to the dynastic-history *yì-wén zhì*.
