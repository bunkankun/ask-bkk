# Wújìnzàng Wénshū 無盡藏文殊

A Tang-period commentator on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*, surveyed by [[通理]] Dátiān Tōnglǐ in his *Lèngyán jīng zhǐzhǎng shū xuánshì* ([[KR6j0715]]) as one of the major figures in the Tang-Sòng *Lèngyán* commentarial tradition.
