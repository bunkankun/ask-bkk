Cuī Xiàn 崔銑 (1478–1541), *zì* Zǐzhōng 子鍾 / Zhòngfú 仲鳬, *hào* Huányě 洹野, posthumously *Wénmǐn* 文敏 (some sources: *Wénjìng* 文敬), was a Mid-Míng *Lǐxué* official and biographer of Anyáng 安陽 (Hénán). *Jìnshì* of *Hóngzhì yǐchǒu* (1505); rose to *Nánjīng Lǐbù yòu shìláng*. His major works include the *Dú Yì yú yán* 讀易餘言 ([[KR1a0093]], 5 juàn, completed *Jiājìng gēngzǐ* 嘉靖庚子 = 1540, an *yìlǐ*-leaning *Yìjīng* commentary that fuses Chéng Yí, Wáng Bì, and Wú Chéng 吳澄 against the Chén Tuán chart-tradition); the *Shìyì* 士翼 (KR3a0086, 3 juàn, his *yǔlù*-style notebook); the *Míng chén shí jié* 明臣十節 (a comparative biographical assessment of mid-Míng pure-Confucian officials, cited in KR3a0082's *tíyào*); and the *Huányě jí* 洹野集 (collected works). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 34649, dates 1478–1541.
