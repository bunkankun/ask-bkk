Quán-lín 全琳, *zì* **Yùn-shí** 韞石. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě*) of **Huì-yuè Zhì-xù** 晦嶽智旭 [[智旭|Huì-yuè]] (cf. [[KR6q0537]]). Native of **Hǎi-yán** 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng); lay surname **Lù** 陸. Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0537]] 《晦嶽旭禪師語錄》 8 juan, J38 B418, prefaced 1703.
