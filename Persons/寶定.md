# Bǎodìng 寶定

Southern Sòng monk, abbot of the Jiāozhōng bàoguó chánsì 教忠報國禪寺 monastery at Dàcí míngshān 大慈名山 in Míngzhōu 明州 prefecture, where new blocks for the *Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* 禪林僧寶傳 ([[KR6q0040]]) were cut.
