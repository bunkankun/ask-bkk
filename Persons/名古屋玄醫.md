# Nagoya Gen'i 名古屋玄醫

Japanese physician of the early Edo period (1628–1696). Together with [[北山友松子]] Kitayama Yūshōshi and [[後藤艮山]] Gotō Konzan, he is conventionally credited as a founding figure of the Japanese *kohō* 古方 ("ancient-formula") school, which rejected the *Lǐ-Zhū* 李朱 Jīn-Yuán synthesis and returned to the *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 of [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng as the authoritative basis of clinical practice.
