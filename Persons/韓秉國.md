Northern-Sòng official and correspondent of [[司馬光]] Sīmǎ Guāng. The principal surviving document of his correspondence is Sīmǎ Guāng's *Dá Hán Bǐngguó shū* 答韓秉國書 (Letter in Reply to Hán Bǐngguó), preserved in the *Chuán jiā jí* 傳家集 — the locus classicus of Sīmǎ Guāng's programmatic rejection of [[王弼]] Wáng Bì's Lǎo-Zhuāng metaphysical reading of the *Yì*.
