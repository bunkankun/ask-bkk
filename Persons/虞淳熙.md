# Yú Chúnxī 虞淳熙

Late-Wànlì Hángzhōu literatus, *zì* **Déyuán** 德園, holder of the *jiǎshì* of the *Sīxūnyuán* 司勳員 (Bureau of Honors) of the Ministry of Personnel; lay-Buddhist patron of [[傳燈]] Yōuxī Chuándēng's late-Wànlì Tiāntái revival. Sponsored the **Fǎhuá sānmèi** 法華三昧 retreat at the Shèngguǒsì 勝果寺 (Hángzhōu) at which Chuándēng's *Lèngyán jīng yuántōng shū qiánmáo* ([[KR6j0705]]) was begun in 1601.
