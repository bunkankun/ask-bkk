Late-Míng Buddhist monk; sobriquet **Tàixū** 太虛 / **Dà-xū-xiàng** 大虗相. DILA Authority A001624. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the Wàn-lì 萬曆 era (1573–1620). Resident at the **Guǎng-líng Bǎo-chéng-sì** 廣陵寶城寺 (Guǎng-líng = literary name for Yáng-zhōu 揚州 in Jiāngsū). Author of the **Fǎhuá jīng dà-yì** 法華經大意 ([[KR6d0075]], X31n0609, 3 *juan*) — a Lotus Sūtra synoptic exposition.

Note: distinct from the Táng monk **Wúxiàng** of Fú-zhōu Xiāng-sī-sì 涪州相思寺 (DILA A006942), who appears in the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* with the *gǎn-tōng* (resonance-and-penetration) hagiographical attribution.

Sources: *Fǎhuá jīng dà-yì juàn-shǒu* 法華經大意卷首; DILA A001624.
