Zhāng Nèiyùn 張內蘊 (fl. 1580) was a Wàn-lì-era *shēng-yuán* (Tributary Student) of Wú-jiāng County in Sūzhōu Prefecture. With the Huá-tíng *jiān-shēng* Zhōu Dàsháo, he was assigned by Censor Lín Yìng-xùn 林應訓 to edit the *Sān-Wú shuǐ-kǎo* ([[KR2k0070]]) — a 16-juan documentary monograph on Lín's six-year (1576–1582) Wàn-lì-era hydraulic administration of the Sū-Sōng-Cháng-Zhèn region. CBDB has no entry under this name; he did not subsequently rise to high office.
