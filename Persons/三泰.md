Sān Tài 三泰 (d. 1759) was an early-Qiánlóng Manchu official, by 1740 *Xíngbù shàngshū* 刑部尚書 (Minister of Punishments), who served as one of the two principal compilers of the [[KR2m0055]] *Dà Qīng lǜ-lì* 大清律例 in its mature form, promulgated in *Qiánlóng* 5 (1740). With Xú Běn 徐本 (1683–1747), he conducted the article-by-article revision requested by Fù Nǎi 傅鼐, drawing more than a thousand established substatutes (*tiáo-lì* 條例) into the body of the code. He held a series of further senior central-government appointments through the 1740s and 1750s. CBDB id 57028 records death in 1759 and an index year of 1696. The CBDB also contains a homonymous Míng-period 三泰 (135208), unrelated to this person.
