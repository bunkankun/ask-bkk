**Chén Shào** 陳邵 (*fl.* mid-third century), Cáo-Wèi 曹魏-period classicist, dates not preserved. He is principally known through Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 *xù-lù* preface, which preserves a single substantial fragment of his *Zhōuguān lǐ yìtóng píng* 周官禮異同評 ([[KR1d0108]]) — the locus classicus for the standard Hàn-Wèi *Lǐjì* transmission genealogy from Dài Dé and Dài Shèng through Mǎ Róng and Lú Zhí to Zhèng Xuán's canonical annotation.

His standard biography is not in the *Sānguó zhì*; he is recorded only as a *zhì-zhōng* 治中 and *xiào-shū láng* 校書郎 (court editorial-secretary) in the late Cáo-Wèi court, with no other extant writings. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists his *Zhōu lǐ lùn* 周禮論 in 2 *juàn*, suggesting the *Zhōuguān lǐ yìtóng píng* may have been the *xù* 序 (preface) to that larger work; the *Suí shū* listing is the only attestation.
