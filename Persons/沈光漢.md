# 沈光漢

Shěn Guānghàn, early-Republican Chóngmíng 崇明 physician, disciple of [[刁質明]] Diāo Zhìmíng. Wrote the 1917 (*Dīngsì* 丁巳) preface to [[KR3em038]] *Hóukē jiāxùn* 喉科家訓, recording the master's reorganisation of the family manuscript.
