**Jì Yìngzhōng** 紀映鍾 (*hào* Juǎnlóu Jūshì 卷婁居士, *fl.* 17th c., 明-清), wrote one of the 康熙己未 (1679) prefaces to [[蔣介繁]]'s *Běncǎo zéyào gāngmù* 本草擇要綱目 ([[KR3ec057]]), framing the work in the language of Mòzǐ's "great use through no-use" and Sū Shì's *dà yī wáng* discussion. No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
