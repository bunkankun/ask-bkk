Shǒujiān 守堅, styled Míngshí dàshī 明識大師 (Great Master of Clear Discernment) and granted the purple robe (*cìzǐ* 賜紫), was a disciple (*ménrén* 門人) of [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃 (864–949) and the principal compiler of his master's recorded sayings. DILA A000360 classifies him as a Northern Sòng figure on the basis that the Shǒujiān compilation circulated into the Northern Sòng and was collated and reprinted by Sū Xiè 蘇澥 in Xīníng 9 = 1076 ([[KR6q0073]], T47 n1988). No independent biography, birth/death dates, or native place are recorded. The same DILA entry attributes to him both the *Yúnmén Kuāngzhēn chánshī guǎnglù* 雲門匡真禪師廣錄 (T1988) and the shorter *Yúnmén Kuāngzhēn chánshī yǔlù* 雲門匡真禪師語錄 (JB138), suggesting that Shǒujiān assembled multiple distinct versions of Yúnmén's sayings in the post-949 period.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0073]] *Yúnmén Kuāngzhēn chánshī guǎnglù* 雲門匡真禪師廣錄 — the *duìjī* 對機 / *chuídài* 垂代 / *kānbiàn* 勘辨 compilation of Yúnmén's dialogues across three juan, with the Léi Yuè *xínglù* and Yuánmì *sān jù yǔ* appendices bundled in by Shǒujiān or the later Sū Xiè editorial hand; [[KR6q0392]] *Yúnmén Kuāngzhēn chánshī yǔlù* 雲門匡真禪師語錄 (JB138) — the shorter three-juan parallel recension preserved in the *Jiāxīng zàng*, arranged as *jīyuán* 機緣 / *shìzhōng yǔyào* 室中語要 / *chuíshì dàiyǔ* 垂示代語, without the Léi Yuè or Yuánmì apparatus.
