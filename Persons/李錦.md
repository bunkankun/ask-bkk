**Lǐ Jǐn** 李錦 (1639–1708) — early-Qīng official-scholar of Dàliáng 大梁 (Kāifēng 開封, Hénán); served in successive Zhèjiāng postings (Bǎowù / Jīnhuá, Qiántáng / Hangzhou) through the *Shùnzhì* and early *Kāngxī* eras. Patron of the early-Qīng Xīnān physician-editor [[程雲來]] Chéng Yúnlái, whose *Jīnguì yàolüè zhíjiě* (1653) and *Cāngshēng sīmìng* ([[KR3er078]], 1677) Lǐ funded into print. His 1677 publication-preface to the *Cāngshēng sīmìng* is a valuable autobiographical window on the early-Qīng Lǐ / Chéng patronage circle.
