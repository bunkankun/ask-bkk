**Lǐ Jǐn** 李錦 (1639–1708) — early-Qīng official-scholar of Dà-liáng 大梁 (Kāi-fēng 開封, Hé-nán); served in successive Zhè-jiāng postings (Bǎo-wù / Jīn-huá, Qián-táng / Hangzhou) through the *Shùn-zhì* and early *Kāng-xī* eras. Patron of the early-Qīng Xīn-ān physician-editor [[程雲來]] Chéng Yún-lái, whose *Jīn-guì yào-lüè zhí-jiě* (1653) and *Cāng-shēng sī-mìng* ([[KR3er078]], 1677) Lǐ funded into print. His 1677 publication-preface to the *Cāng-shēng sī-mìng* is a valuable autobiographical window on the early-Qīng Lǐ / Chéng patronage circle.
