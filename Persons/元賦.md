Yuán-fù 元賦. Lay disciple (*sú-jiā dìzǐ* — DILA notes 否 in the monk-flag) active in the Liáo-yáng-Qiān-shān circle of **Qiān-shān Shèng-rén Hán-kě** [[函可]] (1612–1660). One of the principal compilers of [[KR6q0527]] 《千山剩人禪師語錄》 — *děng biān* (lead editor with others) of the J38 B407 corpus. Mentioned as *jiān-yuàn* 監院 of **Guǎng-cí-sì** 廣慈寺 in the 順治九年 = **1652** *qǐng-qǐ* (invitation document) preserved at the head of the *yǔ-lù* — written there as **玄賦** 玄賦, where 玄 was likely changed to 元 in the print edition to avoid the Kāngxī taboo (Kāngxī's personal name = 玄燁). Lifedates unknown.
