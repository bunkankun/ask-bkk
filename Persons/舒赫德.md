Shū Hèdé 舒赫德 (1710–1777), Manchu official of the Šumuru 舒穆魯 clan, of the Plain White Banner. Long career running from Yōngzhèng through high Qiánlóng. Held the offices of Minister of Personnel, Grand Councillor (junjī dàchén 軍機大臣), Wǔyīng diàn Grand Secretary, and was raised to a first-rank duke (Yīděng wényāng gōng 一等文襄公) for his services on the Dzungar campaign. Lieutenant of Iyi 伊犁 in the immediate post-conquest years; later involved in the management of the Hui Muslim revolts in Gānsù and Shāndōng. Senior compiler-of-record of the *Qīndìng jiǎopǔ Línqīng nìfěi jìlüè* 欽定剿捕臨清逆匪紀略 ([[KR2c0014]]) on the Wáng Lún 王倫 revolt of 1774, presented to the throne in 1777, the year of his own death — Ā-guì and Yú Mǐnzhōng appearing alongside him in the *zǒngcái* list. CBDB carries him at personid 57261 (under the Manchu form 舒穆魯舒赫德) with dates 1710–1777, followed here. Biography in *Qīngshǐ gǎo* 318. Not to be confused with the late-Qing Šumulu officer of the same Hàn name (CBDB 515120).
