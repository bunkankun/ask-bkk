Zhāng Jìnshēng 張晉生 (fl. early 18th c.) was the principal *biānzuǎn* 編纂 of the Yōngzhèng-era *Sìchuān tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0052]]) of 1733, working under the supervision of the Sìchuān Governor-General [[黃廷桂]]. He served as a Sìchuān local-administration *zhīfǔ* / *zhīxiàn* official; biographical details beyond his role in the gazetteer compilation are not preserved.
