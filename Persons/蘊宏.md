# Yùn-hóng 蘊宏 (薀宏)

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán practitioner and **Míng imperial descendant** (*Míng Gāo-huáng-dì yì* 明高皇帝裔 per Shū Jùn-jí's 1687 preface in [[KR6q0208]]). *Zì* Kuān-fū 寬夫 ("Broad Gentleman"); *hào* Cān-tóng 參同 ("Co-Study"). **Biological elder brother** of [[蘊上|Yùn-shàng Dá-fū]] 蘊上達夫, with whom he retired to Chán practice after the 1644 dynastic collapse.

Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Contributor of the running interlinear-commentary annotations (*zhuó yǔ* 著語) to his brother Dá-fū's *Sòng gǔ gōu jù* 頌古鉤鉅 [[KR6q0207]], and co-resident with Dá-fū at their East-suburbs hermitage where they jointly produced their literary-Chán output.

The shared dharma-name initial *Yùn* 蘊 / 薀 between the two brothers represents their biological-brother relationship rather than shared monastic ordination-line.
