**Sūn Yè** 孫燁 (*zì* Yuánsù 元素), late-Míng Xīnān 新安 (Huīzhōu) figure, clan-nephew of [[孫一奎]] Sūn Yīkuí; wrote the principal biographical preface to [[KR3eq028]] *Yīzhǐ xùyú*.
