**Bì Zhòngyóu** 畢仲游 (1047–1121), *zì* Gōng-shū 公叔, of Zhèng-zhōu 鄭州 Yuán-wǔ 原武. Great-grandson of Bì Shì-ān 畢士安 (chief councillor under Zhēn-zōng) and brother of Bì Zhòng-yǎn 畢仲衍. With his elder brother Zhòng-yǎn, both passed *jìnshì*; held office through provincial *zhōu-xiàn* posts. *Yuán-yòu* 1 (1086) summoned to test at the *Xué-shì-yuàn*, made *Jí-xián jiào-lǐ*; promoted to *Lì-bù láng-zhōng*. Listed in the *Yuán-yòu dǎng-jí* (1102), ending career at *Xī-jīng liú-sī yù-shǐ-tái tí-jǔ Hóng-qìng-gōng*. Author of *Xī-tái jí* 西臺集 [[KR4d0115]] originally in 50 *juǎn* (per *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén-zhì*) but more reliably 20 *juǎn* (per Cháo Gōng-wǔ); Sū Shì recommended him as his self-replacement, praising his learning. His *Shàng Sū xué-shì shū* warned Sū Shì against the dangers of writing too directly — preserved in this *bié-jí*, a key Sū-mén document. Biography: in *Sòng shǐ* (附 to Bì Shì-ān biography). The catalog gives "d. 1121" — followed here.
