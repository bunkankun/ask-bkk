**Bì Zhòngyóu** 畢仲游 (1047–1121), *zì* Gōngshū 公叔, of Zhèngzhōu 鄭州 Yuánwǔ 原武. Great-grandson of Bì Shìān 畢士安 (chief councillor under Zhēnzōng) and brother of Bì Zhòngyǎn 畢仲衍. With his elder brother Zhòngyǎn, both passed *jìnshì*; held office through provincial *zhōuxiàn* posts. *Yuányòu* 1 (1086) summoned to test at the *Xuéshìyuàn*, made *Jíxián jiàolǐ*; promoted to *Lìbù lángzhōng*. Listed in the *Yuányòu dǎngjí* (1102), ending career at *Xījīng liúsī yùshǐtái tíjǔ Hóngqìnggōng*. Author of *Xītái jí* 西臺集 [[KR4d0115]] originally in 50 *juǎn* (per *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì*) but more reliably 20 *juǎn* (per Cháo Gōngwǔ); Sū Shì recommended him as his self-replacement, praising his learning. His *Shàng Sū xuéshì shū* warned Sū Shì against the dangers of writing too directly — preserved in this *biéjí*, a key Sūmén document. Biography: in *Sòng shǐ* (附 to Bì Shìān biography). The catalog gives "d. 1121" — followed here.
