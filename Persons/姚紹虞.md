**Yáo Shàoyú** 姚紹虞 (*fl.* 1671, 清), *zì* Zhǐ'ān 止庵 (and consequently often cited as 姚止庵), was an early-Kāngxī physician of Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). His one transmitted work is the *Sùwèn jīng zhù jié jiě* 素問經注節解 in nine juan ([[KR3ea005]]), an abridged re-exposition of the *Huángdì nèijīng Sùwèn* with Wáng Bīng's commentary, dated by its prefaces to *Kāngxī xīnhài* 康熙辛亥 (1671). His arrangement of the eighty-one *pian* into 內篇 (diagnostic / therapeutic) and 外篇 (cosmological) reflects an early-Qīng tendency to read the *Sùwèn* primarily as a clinical text. Biographical details beyond his Qiántáng residence and the date of the work are not preserved.
