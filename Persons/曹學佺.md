Cáo Xuéquán 曹學佺 (1573–1646), *zì* Néngshǐ 能始, *hào* Yànshí 雁石, also Xīfēng jūshì 西峰居士 and Shícāng 石倉; native of Hóuguān 侯官 (modern Fúzhōu) in Fújiàn. Late-Míng polymath, official, scholar of the *Yìjīng* and *Shàngshū*, anthologist, and Míng-loyalist martyr. *Wànlì* 23 (1595) *jìnshì*. Held office as *Hànlín jiǎntǎo*; sent out as *Sìchuān yòu cānzhèng* and promoted *Sìchuān àncháshǐ* ca. 1605–1611, during which he composed the *Shǔzhōng guǎngjì* 蜀中廣記 ([[KR2k0126]]) and the *Quán-Shǔ yìwénzhì* 全蜀藝文志. After dismissal in 1612 he returned to Fúzhōu and devoted himself to scholarship and the patronage of the Mǐnzhōng poetic circle. One of the leading lights of the late-Míng *Dōnglín / Fùshè* movement. Recalled to office under the Hóngguāng court (1644) as *Lǐbù shìláng*; under the Lóngwǔ court at Fúzhōu as *Lǐbù shàngshū*; on the fall of Fúzhōu to Qīng forces in *Lóngwǔ* 2 (1646) he hanged himself rather than serve. Major works include the *Yìjīng tōnglùn* ([[KR1a0103]]?), the *Shǔzhōng guǎngjì* ([[KR2k0126]]), the *Mǐnzhōng zhì* 閩中志, and the monumental 506-juan *Shícāng lìdài shī xuǎn* 石倉歷代詩選 ([[KR4h0008]]?), one of the largest of all Míng poetry anthologies. Biography in *Míngshǐ* 288. CBDB id 124553.
