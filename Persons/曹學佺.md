Cáo Xué-quán 曹學佺 (1573–1646), *zì* Néng-shǐ 能始, *hào* Yàn-shí 雁石, also Xī-fēng jū-shì 西峰居士 and Shí-cāng 石倉; native of Hóu-guān 侯官 (modern Fú-zhōu) in Fú-jiàn. Late-Míng polymath, official, scholar of the *Yìjīng* and *Shàngshū*, anthologist, and Míng-loyalist martyr. *Wàn-lì* 23 (1595) *jìnshì*. Held office as *Hàn-lín jiǎn-tǎo*; sent out as *Sì-chuān yòu cān-zhèng* and promoted *Sì-chuān àn-chá-shǐ* ca. 1605–1611, during which he composed the *Shǔ-zhōng guǎng-jì* 蜀中廣記 ([[KR2k0126]]) and the *Quán-Shǔ yì-wén-zhì* 全蜀藝文志. After dismissal in 1612 he returned to Fú-zhōu and devoted himself to scholarship and the patronage of the Mǐn-zhōng poetic circle. One of the leading lights of the late-Míng *Dōng-lín / Fù-shè* movement. Recalled to office under the Hóng-guāng court (1644) as *Lǐ-bù shì-láng*; under the Lóng-wǔ court at Fú-zhōu as *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*; on the fall of Fú-zhōu to Qīng forces in *Lóng-wǔ* 2 (1646) he hanged himself rather than serve. Major works include the *Yì-jīng tōng-lùn* ([[KR1a0103]]?), the *Shǔ-zhōng guǎng-jì* ([[KR2k0126]]), the *Mǐn-zhōng zhì* 閩中志, and the monumental 506-juan *Shí-cāng lì-dài shī xuǎn* 石倉歷代詩選 ([[KR4h0008]]?), one of the largest of all Míng poetry anthologies. Biography in *Míng-shǐ* 288. CBDB id 124553.
