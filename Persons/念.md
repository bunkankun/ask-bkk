# Niàn 念 (Wénmù Niàn 文穆念)

*Hào* **Wénmù** 文穆 ("Cultured Solemn"). Born **明崇禎癸酉 6/23 巳時** = **1633-07-28 Gregorian**; died **康熙丁巳 12/13 卯時** = **1678-01-05 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **45**. Native of **Qíyáng** 祁陽 (Nán-Chǔ); lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣.

**Lineage.** Línjì-line dharma-heir of **Lǎnfàng Zhìdà** 嬾放智大 (Lǎnfàng's master was the Míng-loyalist *jìnshì* who refused to surrender at Qíshān 歧山 Rénruìsì 仁瑞寺 — preserved as a remarkable Cáodòng cross-loyalist line).

**Formation.** **甲申 = 1644** (the dynastic-transition year, age 11): tonsured at **Zǐyúnān** 紫雲菴 under **Fùzhī shī** 復之師. **順治甲午 = 1654** entered the Qíshān assembly; full precepts that winter. **戊戌 = 1658**: *fǎfù* from **Lǎnfàng**. **庚子春 = 1660** left Lǎnfàng to pilgrimage Jiāng-Zhè.

**Three abbacies.** **Fǎchuáng Shuānglín** 法幢雙林 (Xìnyángzhōu); **Dānxiá Xiānxiá** 丹霞僊霞; **Fúniú Yúnyán** 伏牛雲巖.

**Work.** [[KR6q0500]] 《文穆念禪師語錄》 5 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B363, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhēnhuì** [[真慧]] *děng biān*; juan 5 *xíngshí* 行實 dated post-1678/1.
