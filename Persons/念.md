# Niàn 念 (Wén-mù Niàn 文穆念)

*Hào* **Wén-mù** 文穆 ("Cultured Solemn"). Born **明崇禎癸酉 6/23 巳時** = **1633-07-28 Gregorian**; died **康熙丁巳 12/13 卯時** = **1678-01-05 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **45**. Native of **Qí-yáng** 祁陽 (Nán-Chǔ); lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣.

**Lineage.** Línjì-line dharma-heir of **Lǎn-fàng Zhì-dà** 嬾放智大 (Lǎn-fàng's master was the Míng-loyalist *jìn-shì* who refused to surrender at Qí-shān 歧山 Rén-ruì-sì 仁瑞寺 — preserved as a remarkable Cáo-dòng cross-loyalist line).

**Formation.** **甲申 = 1644** (the dynastic-transition year, age 11): tonsured at **Zǐ-yún-ān** 紫雲菴 under **Fù-zhī shī** 復之師. **順治甲午 = 1654** entered the Qí-shān assembly; full precepts that winter. **戊戌 = 1658**: *fǎ-fù* from **Lǎn-fàng**. **庚子春 = 1660** left Lǎn-fàng to pilgrimage Jiāng-Zhè.

**Three abbacies.** **Fǎ-chuáng Shuāng-lín** 法幢雙林 (Xìn-yáng-zhōu); **Dān-xiá Xiān-xiá** 丹霞僊霞; **Fú-niú Yún-yán** 伏牛雲巖.

**Work.** [[KR6q0500]] 《文穆念禪師語錄》 5 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B363, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhēn-huì** [[真慧]] *děng biān*; juan 5 *xíng-shí* 行實 dated post-1678/1.
