Zhuō 拙 (catalog form; full reference **Pǔ-fū Shí-zhuō** 樸夫實拙 — note that DILA uses 實拙, the catalog elides the first character), *zì* **Pǔ-fū** 樸夫 ("Plain Man"), *bié-hào* **Mù-tíng** 牧亭 ("Shepherd Pavilion"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔ-yún Pèi** 古雲沛 (= **Lú-mèng Pèi** 廬夢沛). Born **1682** (estimated, age 56 in **乾隆丁巳** = **1737**); death year unknown. Lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Tonsured under **Dòng-xū Guāng** 洞虛光禪師 at **Hǎi-fú-ān** 海福菴. Final *fù-fǎ* under **Gǔ-yún Pèi**.

**Abbacies (three principal seats).** **Pó-yáng Jiàn-xī Yǒng-zhèn chán-sì** 鄱陽劍溪永鎮禪寺; **Bà-líng Bái-lù-dù Xī-tīng-sì** 霸陵白鷺渡西汀寺; **Lín-chuān Qīng-yún cǎo-ān** 臨川青雲艸菴. Also delivered precept-instructions at **Fēng-chéng Qìng-yún** 豐城慶雲, **Fú-liáng Yuán-tōng-ān** 浮梁圓通菴 (in **乾隆丙辰冬** = **1736** winter), and **Chóng-rén Tiān-zhú-sì** 崇仁天竺寺 (1736 lunar 12).

**Disciples.** **Shí-tán Guāng-mó** 石壇光模 [[光模]] (lead compiler), **Shí-zhàng Yuè-lù** 石丈嶽麓.

**Work.** [[KR6q0599]] 《磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B493, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Guāng-mó** [[光模]] *děng biān*. The catalog dynasty 清 — but the abbacy materials extend into **Qiánlóng** = **1736–1737+**, making this one of the latest imprints in the Jiā-xīng-canon J40 sequence.

**Sources.** 《正源略集》 vol. 14; 《磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈傳〉.
