Zhuō 拙 (catalog form; full reference **Pǔfū Shízhuō** 樸夫實拙 — note that DILA uses 實拙, the catalog elides the first character), *zì* **Pǔfū** 樸夫 ("Plain Man"), *biéhào* **Mùtíng** 牧亭 ("Shepherd Pavilion"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔyún Pèi** 古雲沛 (= **Lúmèng Pèi** 廬夢沛). Born **1682** (estimated, age 56 in **乾隆丁巳** = **1737**); death year unknown. Lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Tonsured under **Dòngxū Guāng** 洞虛光禪師 at **Hǎifúān** 海福菴. Final *fùfǎ* under **Gǔyún Pèi**.

**Abbacies (three principal seats).** **Póyáng Jiànxī Yǒngzhèn chánsì** 鄱陽劍溪永鎮禪寺; **Bàlíng Báilùdù Xītīngsì** 霸陵白鷺渡西汀寺; **Línchuān Qīngyún cǎoān** 臨川青雲艸菴. Also delivered precept-instructions at **Fēngchéng Qìngyún** 豐城慶雲, **Fúliáng Yuántōngān** 浮梁圓通菴 (in **乾隆丙辰冬** = **1736** winter), and **Chóngrén Tiānzhúsì** 崇仁天竺寺 (1736 lunar 12).

**Disciples.** **Shítán Guāngmó** 石壇光模 [[光模]] (lead compiler), **Shízhàng Yuèlù** 石丈嶽麓.

**Work.** [[KR6q0599]] 《磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B493, compiled by *fǎsì* **Guāngmó** [[光模]] *děng biān*. The catalog dynasty 清 — but the abbacy materials extend into **Qiánlóng** = **1736–1737+**, making this one of the latest imprints in the Jiā-xīng-canon J40 sequence.

**Sources.** 《正源略集》 vol. 14; 《磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈傳〉.
