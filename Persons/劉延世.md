**Liú Yán-shì** 劉延世 (fl. late *Shào-shèng* — *Yuán-fú*, c. 1094–1100). Native of Lín-jiāng 臨江 (modern Jiāng-xī). Son of the magistrate (*zhī-xiàn*) of *Cháng-tīng* 長汀 in *Tīng-zhōu* 汀州 (modern Fú-jiàn) under the *Shào-shèng* reign-era. When the demoted *Yuán-yòu*-faction official *Sūn Shēng* 孫升 (1038–1099) was exiled to *Tīng-zhōu* in 1094, Liú Yán-shì — through his father's local position — gained access to Sūn's company and recorded his table-talk over the years 1094–1099, producing the [[KR3l0039]] *Sūn-gōng tán-pǔ* 孫公談圃 in 3 *juàn*. CBDB id 20440 records the name without lifedates or career detail; no other work is attested under his name. He is therefore known solely as a compiler.
