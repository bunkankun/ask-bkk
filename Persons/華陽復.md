Huáyáng Fù 華陽復 was an obscure Yuán-dynasty Daoist (1279–1368). Nothing is known about him except from his sole surviving work, the three-*juàn* *Dòngxuán língbǎo zìrán jiǔtiān shēngshén zhāngjīng zhù* [[KR5b0082]] (DZ 398), a commentary on the *Jiǔtiān shēngshén zhāngjīng* [[KR5b0002]] that draws extensively on Wáng Xīcháo's 王希巢 DZ 397 and cites the otherwise-lost commentaries of Xiāo Zhēnyòu 蕭真祐 and Zhēn Shuài 真率. Zhāng Shǒuqīng's 張守清 preface of 1332 to this work reports only that Huáyáng fù's commentary was brought to his attention by a visitor from Wú 吳 (Jiāngnán). The internal evidence of his commentary (1.4a–5b) alone places him in the Yuán. No CBDB record.
