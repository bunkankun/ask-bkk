# Zì-rán 自然

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. (DILA classifies as Míng, reflecting a later publication horizon; the editorial work is Yuán.)
