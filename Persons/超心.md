Chāo-xīn 超心, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Dú-lǎng Xíng-rì** [[行曰]] 獨朗行日 at **Tiān-tái Tōng-xuán-sì** 天台通玄寺. Co-compiler with **Chāo-xiāng** [[超香]] of the 2-juan 《天台通玄寺獨朗禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0505]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
