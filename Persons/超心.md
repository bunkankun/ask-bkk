Chāoxīn 超心, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Dúlǎng Xíngrì** [[行曰]] 獨朗行日 at **Tiāntái Tōngxuánsì** 天台通玄寺. Co-compiler with **Chāoxiāng** [[超香]] of the 2-juan 《天台通玄寺獨朗禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0505]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
