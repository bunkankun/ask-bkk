# Wáng Wénshī 王聞詩 (Sòng, fl. 1170s)

Son of Wáng Shí-péng [[王十朋]] (1112–1171). With his brother Wáng Wén-lǐ [[王聞禮]], edited their father's literary collection [[KR4d0235]] *Méi-xī xiān-shēng wén-jí* in 54 *juǎn* after Wáng Shí-péng's death (1171). Bibliographic identifier only; no separate biographical record.
