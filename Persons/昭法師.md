Zhāo fǎshī 昭法師 (active Tang or earlier), unidentified Buddhist scholar; the *Master Zhāo* of the Dūnhuáng manuscript [[KR6f0055|*Shèng mán jīng shū* 勝鬘經疏]] (T2762). The honorific *fǎshī* 法師 ("Dharma-Master") suggests a senior monk; the identity behind the *Zhāo* personal name is not preserved.
