# Hōnen 法然

Founder of the Japanese Jōdoshū (Pure Land school); author of the *Senchaku hongan nenbutsushū* 選擇本願念佛集. His teaching of exclusive *nenbutsu* practice was a direct development of [[善導]] Shàndǎo's *[[KR6f0076|Guānjīng shū]]*.
