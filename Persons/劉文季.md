# Liú Wénjì 劉文季

Qīng layman in Yúnnán, associate of [[福慧|Yězhú Fúhuì]] 野竹福慧. Circulated the drafts of Fúhuì's harmonising-verses in [[KR6q0216]] *Tiāntái sān shèng shī jí hé yùn* among local literati. Lifedates and further biographical details unrecorded.
