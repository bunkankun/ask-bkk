"Mr. Cài" (Cài shì 蔡氏) is the catalog-meta authorial attribution of [[KR5a0118|Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註]] (DZ 118); the name refers to Zhū Xī 朱熹's (1130–1200) student Cài Yuándǐng 蔡元定 (1135–1198), but the commentary itself is actually Zhū Xī's own exegesis of the *Yīnfú jīng*, transmitted via his student-circle and published under the student's name (cf. Reiter in Schipper & Verellen 2004, 2:696). The text is identical with the first portion of [[KR5a0124|DZ 124 *Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhùjiě*]] 1a–8a. The Zhū Xī / Cài Yuándǐng authorship combination is an important witness to Neo-Confucian engagement with the *Yīnfú jīng* within the Daoist-canon tradition. No independent biographical note is warranted for "Cài shì" since the attribution-name does not denote a distinct historical person beyond the Zhū–Cài Neo-Confucian master-disciple pair.
