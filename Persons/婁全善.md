**Lóu Quánshàn** 婁全善 (real name 婁英 Lóu Yīng, *zì* Quánshàn, c. 1320–1400), early-Míng physician of Xiāoshān 蕭山 (Zhèjiāng) in the [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī tradition; author of *Yī xué gāng mù* 醫學綱目 (40 *juǎn*, completed 1396, printed 1565) — the foundational late-Yuán / early-Míng nosological synthesis whose 六經 → *záběng* → seasonal → women's-and-children's architecture was adopted by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng for the *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* corpus.
