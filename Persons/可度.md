Southern-Sòng through Yuán Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquets **Wú-jí** 無極 (also referenced as **Wú-jí Kě-dù** 無極可度, **Wú-jí dà-shī** 無極大師, **Wú-jí dōng-táng Kě-dù** 無極東堂可度), **Shǒu-lèng dà-shī** 首楞大師 ("Great Master Śūraṃgama"), **Wǔ-lín-dù** 武林度 (after his Háng-zhōu residence). DILA Authority A000099. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the late Sòng / early Yuán transition.

Major work: the **Shí bù-èr mén zhǐ-yào chāo xiáng-jiě** 十不二門指要鈔詳解 ([[KR6d0165]], X56n0931, 2 *juan*) — a detailed commentary (*xiáng-jiě* 詳解 = "detailed-interpretation") on Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐ-yào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]]). The work was subsequently divided-and-collated (*fēn-huì* 分會) by the Míng monk **Zhèng-mì** 正謐 ([[正謐]]).
