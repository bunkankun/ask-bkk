**Gāo Huì-sǒu** 高晦叟 (fl. *Chóng-níng* — *Xuān-hé*, c. 1100–1130). The *Sìkù* compilers report explicitly that "*his official record is not recoverable*"; native place, official rank, and lifedates are all unknown. CBDB has no firm entry under this name. The author is therefore reconstructible solely from his sole surviving work, the [[KR3l0050]] *Zhēnxí fàngtán* 珍席放談 in 2 *juàn* (recovered from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* extracts by the *Sìkù* compilers). The work's chronological range (events down to *Zhé-zōng* reign, with *Chóng-níng*-and-after vocabulary) places the author active c. 1100–1130. His defensive treatment of Wáng Ān-shí — at the time when the *Xī-níng*-reform-faction historiographic position was politically dominant under the *Cài Jīng* regime — suggests sympathy with the reform tradition. No other work is attested under his name.
