**Yáng Jǐngfēng** 楊景風 (fl. mid-8th c.) — Tang lay disciple of [[不空]] Amoghavajra. In Guǎng-dé 2 (764 CE) he edited and supplied extensive interlinear commentary on Amoghavajra's translation of the *Xiùyào jīng* 宿曜經 ([[KR6j0530]], T21n1299), reorganising and clarifying the difficult first draft prepared by an earlier disciple [[史瑤]] Shǐ Yáo. His Iranian / Sogdian transliterations of the seven-day planetary week names are the principal Chinese textual evidence for the eastward transmission of the Hellenistic-Iranian planetary week.
