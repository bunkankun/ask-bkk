**Cáo Yuánhéng** 曹元恆, late-Qīng Sūzhōu Wúxiàn 吳縣 physician, active in the Guāngxù era. Wrote the 1890 preface to [[KR3eq006]] *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* recovered in the [[繆萼聊]] Miù Èliáo 1894 printing, lamenting the Tàipíng-era destruction of medical printing-blocks at Sūzhōu (the *Géngshēn zhī biàn* 庚申之變, 1860).
