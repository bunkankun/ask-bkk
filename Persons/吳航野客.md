吳航野客 (*Wú Háng Yě Kè*, "Rustic Guest of Wúháng") is the pen name of the author of [[KR4k0292]] *Zhù Chūn Yuán Xiǎo Shǐ* 駐春園小史, a 24-chapter Qīng romance novel. The name alludes to Wúháng 吳航, a classical toponym for the Changle 長樂 area near Fuzhou 福州 in Fujian province, suggesting the author had connections to that region. Beyond this pen name, no biographical information is recoverable. The novel's preface, dated to the Qiánlóng *rényín* year (1782), was written not by the author but by a commentator, Shuǐruò Sǎnrén 水箬散人. No CBDB entry has been located for this pseudonym.
