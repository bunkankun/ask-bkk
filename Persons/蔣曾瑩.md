Jiǎng Céngyíng 蔣曾瑩 of Sūzhōu 蘇州. The collector whose family-held copy of the *Dà Yì cuì yán* 大易粹言 ([[KR1a0041]]) — the *Jiādìng guǐyǒu* (1213) [[張嗣古]] Zhāng Sìgǔ supplement-cutting — served as the Sìkù base-text for that work.
