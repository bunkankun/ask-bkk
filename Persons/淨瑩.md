Jìng-yíng 淨瑩, *zì* **Yuán-jié** 元潔 ("Original Pure"), *bié-hào* **Mù-táng** 睦堂 (after **Chén Pú-xié** 陳蒲鞋, the figure he admired). **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Ruì-bái Míng-xuě** 瑞白明雪 — one of the major mid-17th-century Cáodòng masters. Born **明萬曆壬子三月十七子時** = **1612-04-17** Gregorian; died **康熙壬子閏七月廿四丑時** = **1672-08-16**, age 61, *sēng-là* 40+. Native of **Wéi-yáng Jiāng-dū** 維揚江都; lay surname **Zhuāng** 莊 (father **Jì-fāng** 繼芳, mother née **Shī** 施).

**Formation.** At 20 secretly went to **Jīn-líng Zhōng-shān** 金陵鐘山 to take tonsure under elder **Tuō-bái** 脫白, who set him to *niàn-fó*, *zhòu-chí* (mantra), and *shù-xī* (counting breaths) — practiced both day and night. The next year sought out **Biàn-shān Ruì** 弁山瑞和尚 (= **Ruì-bái Míng-xuě**) at **Háng-zhōu Bào-ēn-yuàn** 杭州報恩院. At 23 followed Ruì-bái to **Tiān-tāi Hù-guó** 天台護國 for full ordination.

**Eight abbacies (across the central-eastern arc).** Opened *fǎ* at **Pú-qí Yán-shòu-sì** 蒲圻延壽寺 in **順治十一年五月** = **1654** lunar 5. Then **Yù-yá-shān Shàng-fāng chán-yuàn** 玉崖山上方禪院; **Hóng-dū Níng-zhōu Gǔ-dòng-shān Ē-nòu chán-sì** 洪都寧州古洞山阿耨禪寺; **Hú-zhōu Biàn-shān Lóng-huá chán-sì** 浙江湖州弁山龍華禪寺; **Guǎng-líng Píng-shān Qī-líng chán-sì** 廣陵平山棲靈禪寺; **Jiāng-xī Níng-zhōu Yún-yán Wú-zhù chán-sì** 江西寧州雲巖無住禪寺; **Lǐ-zhōu Yào-shān Cí-yún chán-sì** 澧州藥山慈雲禪寺; **Yuè-zhōu Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng chán-sì** 越州雲門顯聖禪寺; **Tāi-zhōu Tiān-tāi Hù-guó chán-sì** 台州天台護國禪寺.

**Death.** "Dropped his auspicious-form" (*jí-xiáng ér shì*) on the 24th of leap-7th-month, 1672. Disciples carried his coffin west to **Yún-yán** 雲巖 in spring 1673, building a stupa at **Fèng-shān** 鳳山 within the temple grounds. Two years later relocated to **Gǔ-dòng-shān Ē-nòu** 古洞山阿耨; a stupa was erected on **1674-04-18** *jí-shí* and his relics enshrined there.

**Disciples.** Forty *fǎ-sì* listed (per [[KR6q0567]] juan 10), including **Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng** 頻吉智祥 [[智祥]] (1637–1709, of Héng-yáng Yún-fēng Fó-guó-sì, lead compiler of this *yǔ-lù* and subject of [[KR6q0568]]); **Gǔ-shān Jǐng** 谷山景; **Dòng-shān Bì** 洞山弼; and others.

**Work.** [[KR6q0567]] 《元潔瑩禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B453, compiled by his principal *fǎ-sì* **Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng** [[智祥]] *biān*. Total *yǔ-lù* output (per the *tǎ-míng*) was over 30 juan; the canonical edition is condensed.

**Sources.** 《元潔瑩禪師語錄》 vol. 10 〈塔銘〉; 《正源略集》 vol. 13; 《南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄》 vol. 3, 6; 〈道影〉 vol. 3.
