Jìngyíng 淨瑩, *zì* **Yuánjié** 元潔 ("Original Pure"), *biéhào* **Mùtáng** 睦堂 (after **Chén Púxié** 陳蒲鞋, the figure he admired). **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Ruìbái Míngxuě** 瑞白明雪 — one of the major mid-17th-century Cáodòng masters. Born **明萬曆壬子三月十七子時** = **1612-04-17** Gregorian; died **康熙壬子閏七月廿四丑時** = **1672-08-16**, age 61, *sēnglà* 40+. Native of **Wéiyáng Jiāngdū** 維揚江都; lay surname **Zhuāng** 莊 (father **Jìfāng** 繼芳, mother née **Shī** 施).

**Formation.** At 20 secretly went to **Jīnlíng Zhōngshān** 金陵鐘山 to take tonsure under elder **Tuōbái** 脫白, who set him to *niànfó*, *zhòuchí* (mantra), and *shùxī* (counting breaths) — practiced both day and night. The next year sought out **Biànshān Ruì** 弁山瑞和尚 (= **Ruìbái Míngxuě**) at **Hángzhōu Bàoēnyuàn** 杭州報恩院. At 23 followed Ruìbái to **Tiāntāi Hùguó** 天台護國 for full ordination.

**Eight abbacies (across the central-eastern arc).** Opened *fǎ* at **Púqí Yánshòusì** 蒲圻延壽寺 in **順治十一年五月** = **1654** lunar 5. Then **Yùyáshān Shàngfāng chányuàn** 玉崖山上方禪院; **Hóngdū Níngzhōu Gǔdòngshān Ēnòu chánsì** 洪都寧州古洞山阿耨禪寺; **Húzhōu Biànshān Lónghuá chánsì** 浙江湖州弁山龍華禪寺; **Guǎnglíng Píngshān Qīlíng chánsì** 廣陵平山棲靈禪寺; **Jiāngxī Níngzhōu Yúnyán Wúzhù chánsì** 江西寧州雲巖無住禪寺; **Lǐzhōu Yàoshān Cíyún chánsì** 澧州藥山慈雲禪寺; **Yuèzhōu Yúnmén Xiǎnshèng chánsì** 越州雲門顯聖禪寺; **Tāizhōu Tiāntāi Hùguó chánsì** 台州天台護國禪寺.

**Death.** "Dropped his auspicious-form" (*jíxiáng ér shì*) on the 24th of leap-7th-month, 1672. Disciples carried his coffin west to **Yúnyán** 雲巖 in spring 1673, building a stupa at **Fèngshān** 鳳山 within the temple grounds. Two years later relocated to **Gǔdòngshān Ēnòu** 古洞山阿耨; a stupa was erected on **1674-04-18** *jíshí* and his relics enshrined there.

**Disciples.** Forty *fǎsì* listed (per [[KR6q0567]] juan 10), including **Pínjí Zhìxiáng** 頻吉智祥 [[智祥]] (1637–1709, of Héngyáng Yúnfēng Fóguósì, lead compiler of this *yǔlù* and subject of [[KR6q0568]]); **Gǔshān Jǐng** 谷山景; **Dòngshān Bì** 洞山弼; and others.

**Work.** [[KR6q0567]] 《元潔瑩禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B453, compiled by his principal *fǎsì* **Pínjí Zhìxiáng** [[智祥]] *biān*. Total *yǔlù* output (per the *tǎmíng*) was over 30 juan; the canonical edition is condensed.

**Sources.** 《元潔瑩禪師語錄》 vol. 10 〈塔銘〉; 《正源略集》 vol. 13; 《南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄》 vol. 3, 6; 〈道影〉 vol. 3.
