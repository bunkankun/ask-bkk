Fàn Èchāng 范諤昌, a Northern-Sòng *Yì*-scholar of Jiànchāng 建昌, appearing in the standard transmission-line of the *Hé túLuò shū* charts: [[陳摶]] Chén Tuán → [[種放]] Zhǒng Fàng → [[李溉]] Lǐ Gài → [[許堅]] Xǔ Jiān → Fàn Èchāng → [[劉牧]] Liú Mù. Reportedly identified the *5-positions-9-palaces* chart with Fúxī's making — a position that became influential through Liú Mù. Lifedates not securely recorded.
