Miào-shèng 妙聖, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Bù-lín Zōng-jiān** [[宗堅]] 不磷宗堅 in the Pòshān → Yě-zhú Fú-huì → Bù-lín Sì-chuān-Yún-nán sub-line. Lead recorder of the 3-juan 《兜率不磷堅禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0467]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Miào-shèng děng jì* 妙聖等記. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
