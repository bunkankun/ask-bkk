Lù Yǒurén 陸友仁 / Lù Yǒu 陸友 (*fl. Yuántǒng / Zhìyuán* 1333–1338, died young), *zì* Fǔzhī 輔之, *hào* Yánběi shēng 研北生; native of Sūzhōu (Píngjiāng). Yuán-period bibliophile, art-historian, *jīnshí*-and-ink scholar, and *bǐjì* author. A central figure of the late-Yuán Sūzhōu literati circle around Kē Jiǔsī 柯九思 and Wú Quánjié 吳權節. Author of the *Mò shǐ* 墨史 ([[KR3j0034]]) — the principal Yuán-period treatise on Chinese ink and ink-making; the *Yánběi zázhì* 研北雜志 — a major bibliographic-and-art-historical *bǐjì*; the *Wúzhōng jiùshì* 吳中舊事 ([[KR2k0122]]) — a topographical *bǐjì* on Sūzhōu; and various other works.
