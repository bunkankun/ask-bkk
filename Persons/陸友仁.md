Lù Yǒu-rén 陸友仁 / Lù Yǒu 陸友 (*fl. Yuán-tǒng / Zhì-yuán* 1333–1338, died young), *zì* Fǔ-zhī 輔之, *hào* Yán-běi shēng 研北生; native of Sū-zhōu (Píng-jiāng). Yuán-period bibliophile, art-historian, *jīn-shí*-and-ink scholar, and *bǐ-jì* author. A central figure of the late-Yuán Sū-zhōu literati circle around Kē Jiǔ-sī 柯九思 and Wú Quán-jié 吳權節. Author of the *Mò shǐ* 墨史 ([[KR3j0034]]) — the principal Yuán-period treatise on Chinese ink and ink-making; the *Yán-běi zá-zhì* 研北雜志 — a major bibliographic-and-art-historical *bǐ-jì*; the *Wú-zhōng jiùshì* 吳中舊事 ([[KR2k0122]]) — a topographical *bǐ-jì* on Sū-zhōu; and various other works.
