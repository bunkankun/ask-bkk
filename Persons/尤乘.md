**Yóu Chéng** 尤乘 (*zì* Shēngzhōu 生洲, *fl.* Kāngxī era, late 17th c.), Qing physician and disciple of [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ. No CBDB record. Active publishing 1660s–1690s.

His principal contribution is the supplementing-and-redaction (*zēng-jí* 增輯) of his teacher's manuscript *Bìng-jī shā-zhuàn* 病機沙篆 ([[KR3eh023]]), which he printed in *Kāngxī bǐng-wǔ* = 1666 with his own added prescriptions and case-notes. The combined work is part of the *Shìcái sān shū* 士材三書 (the "Three Books of Shìcái," Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's *zì*) collection — alongside *Yī-zōng bì-dú* 醫宗必讀 and *Zhěn-jiā zhèng-yǎn* 診家正眼 — which was Yóu Chéng's editorial project to consolidate his master's medical legacy.

Yóu also produced his own *Shòu-shì qīng-biān* 壽世青編 (a compendium on health-cultivation and longevity), continuing the family-school transmission from Lǐ Zhōngzǐ.
