Lǐ Zōng-è 李宗諤 (*zì* Chāng-wǔ 昌武, 964–1012), Northern Sòng scholar-official, son of the great early-Sòng chancellor and bibliophile Lǐ Fǎng 李昉 (925–996, principal compiler of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* and *Tài-píng guǎng-jì*). Native of Shēn-zhōu Ráo-yáng 深州饒陽 (modern Héběi). *Jìn-shì* of Yōng-xī 雍熙 2 (985). He held a long string of cultural offices culminating in *Hàn-lín xué-shì* 翰林學士 and *Cí-zhèng-diàn xué-shì* 資政殿學士. He was one of the most prolific Sòng-period editors of court literary and ritual projects, and his hand is visible in numerous early Sòng compendia. The Daozang gazetteer *Lóng-ruì-guàn Yǔ-xué Yáng-míng dòng-tiān tú-jīng* [[KR5b0309]] preserves a heading attributing the work's *xiū-dìng* 修定 (revision and finalisation) to him in his Hàn-lín xué-shì capacity, which would place the original recension before his 1012 death; the work was later revised again by [[葉樞]] in 1114. Biography in *Sòng-shǐ* j. 265. CBDB id 3541 gives 965–1013, slightly off from the standard 964–1012 in *Sòng-shǐ* — the latter is followed here.
