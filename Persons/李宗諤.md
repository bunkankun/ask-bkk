Lǐ Zōngè 李宗諤 (*zì* Chāngwǔ 昌武, 964–1012), Northern Sòng scholar-official, son of the great early-Sòng chancellor and bibliophile Lǐ Fǎng 李昉 (925–996, principal compiler of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* and *Tàipíng guǎngjì*). Native of Shēnzhōu Ráoyáng 深州饒陽 (modern Héběi). *Jìnshì* of Yōngxī 雍熙 2 (985). He held a long string of cultural offices culminating in *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士 and *Cízhèngdiàn xuéshì* 資政殿學士. He was one of the most prolific Sòng-period editors of court literary and ritual projects, and his hand is visible in numerous early Sòng compendia. The Daozang gazetteer *Lóngruìguàn Yǔxué Yángmíng dòngtiān tújīng* [[KR5b0309]] preserves a heading attributing the work's *xiūdìng* 修定 (revision and finalisation) to him in his Hànlín xuéshì capacity, which would place the original recension before his 1012 death; the work was later revised again by [[葉樞]] in 1114. Biography in *Sòngshǐ* j. 265. CBDB id 3541 gives 965–1013, slightly off from the standard 964–1012 in *Sòngshǐ* — the latter is followed here.
