Late-Yuán official and anthologist. *Zì* **Shūzhì** 叔志; native of **Wùyuán** 婺源 (Huīzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Yányòu *wùwǔ* (1318). Served successively as joint-administrator of Yuèzhōu Circuit; *tuīguān* of the Nánān and Xìnzhōu *zǒngguǎnfǔ*; *tuīguān* of Píngjiāng Circuit; prefect of Yǎnzhōu in Jǐníng Circuit; *guózǐ sīyè* (Vice-Director of the National University) from Zhìzhèng 3 (1343), in which capacity he participated in the compilation of the *Three Histories* (Sòngshǐ 宋史, Liáoshǐ 遼史, Jīnshǐ 金史) under **Tuōtuō** 脫脫. On completion of the histories, promoted to *jíxián zhíxuéshì* (Hanlin-allied Academician); retired with the rank of *lǐbù shàngshū* and settled in **Xuānchéng** 宣城, where he self-styled **Kānlǎo zhēnyì** 堪老真逸 ("Old-Endurer of the True Recluse"). In Zhìzhèng 15 (1355) the rebel *Chángqiāng* (Long-Spear) Hóngjīn forces sacked Níngguó; Wāng was captured, refused submission, cursed his captors, and was killed at age 83. Posthumously enfeoffed **Duke of Qiáo Commandery** 譙國郡公, canonized **Wénjié** 文節. Co-compiled in retirement the **[[KR4h0080]]** *Wǎnlíng qúnyīng jí* 宛陵羣英集 (1341), the principal Xuānchéng-region poetry anthology of the Sòng-Yuán period.
