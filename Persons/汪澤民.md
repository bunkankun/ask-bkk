Late-Yuán official and anthologist. *Zì* **Shū-zhì** 叔志; native of **Wùyuán** 婺源 (Huīzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Yán-yòu *wùwǔ* (1318). Served successively as joint-administrator of Yuè-zhōu Circuit; *tuī-guān* of the Nán-ān and Xìn-zhōu *zǒng-guǎn-fǔ*; *tuī-guān* of Píng-jiāng Circuit; prefect of Yǎn-zhōu in Jǐ-níng Circuit; *guó-zǐ sī-yè* (Vice-Director of the National University) from Zhì-zhèng 3 (1343), in which capacity he participated in the compilation of the *Three Histories* (Sòng-shǐ 宋史, Liáo-shǐ 遼史, Jīn-shǐ 金史) under **Tuō-tuō** 脫脫. On completion of the histories, promoted to *jí-xián zhí-xué-shì* (Hanlin-allied Academician); retired with the rank of *lǐ-bù shàng-shū* and settled in **Xuānchéng** 宣城, where he self-styled **Kān-lǎo zhēn-yì** 堪老真逸 ("Old-Endurer of the True Recluse"). In Zhì-zhèng 15 (1355) the rebel *Cháng-qiāng* (Long-Spear) Hóng-jīn forces sacked Níng-guó; Wāng was captured, refused submission, cursed his captors, and was killed at age 83. Posthumously enfeoffed **Duke of Qiáo Commandery** 譙國郡公, canonized **Wén-jié** 文節. Co-compiled in retirement the **[[KR4h0080]]** *Wǎnlíng qúnyīng jí* 宛陵羣英集 (1341), the principal Xuānchéng-region poetry anthology of the Sòng-Yuán period.
