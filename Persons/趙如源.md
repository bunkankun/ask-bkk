Zhào Rúyuán 趙如源, *zì* Jùnzhī 濬之, late-Míng Hángzhōu 杭州 scholar; fellow-townsman of Wáng Dàokūn 王道焜 ([[王道焜]]). Lifedates 1573–1644 (Lónggqìng / Wànlì transition to the fall of the Míng) per CBDB id 54290.

He is known principally for his joint editorship with Wáng Dàokūn of the late-Míng composite *Zuǒzhuàn* edition *Zuǒzhuàn Dù-Lín hé zhù* 左傳杜林合注 ([[KR1e0092]]) in 50 *juǎn*, which combined Dù Yù's 杜預 ([[杜預]]) classical *jí jiě* with the Sòng paraphrase of Lín Yáosǒu 林堯叟 ([[林堯叟]]). The work was printed in Hángzhōu in the Chóngzhēn 崇禎 era and circulated as a standard student text into the Qīng. CBDB id 54290.
