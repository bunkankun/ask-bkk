# 張善吾

*Zhāng Shànwú* is the *zì* (courtesy name) of [[張紹修]] Zhāng Shàoxiū, late-Qīng Huáichuān 淮川 (Liúyáng 瀏陽, Húnán) physician and educational official. He is the author of *Báihóu jiéyào* / *Shíyì báihóu zhènglùn* 時疫白喉證論 (1867), the lead tract of [[KR3em031]] *Báihóu jiéyào hébiān* 白喉捷要合編 and a doctrinal target of [[KR3em020]] *Báihóu tiáobiàn* 白喉條辨.

See the primary entry at [[張紹修]].
