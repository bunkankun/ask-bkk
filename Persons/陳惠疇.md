**Chén Huìchóu** 陳惠疇 (fl. Tóngzhì–Guāngxù, 1860s–1880s, 清), late-Qīng acupuncturist and illustrator. Author of the *Jīngmài túkǎo* 經脈圖考 ([[KR3ee036]], 1878), a four-juan illustrated reference on the channel-and-acupoint system. No further biographical record survives; not in CBDB.
