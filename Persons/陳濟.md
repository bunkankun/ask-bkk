Chén Jì 陳濟 (1665–1718), early-Qīng pupil and editor of Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其. Edited the *Sānyútáng shèng yán* 三魚堂賸言 ([[KR3a0116]]) — Lù Lǒngqí's *yǔlù* materials gathered after his death. CBDB id 81393.
