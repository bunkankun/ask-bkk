# Yùn-shàng 蘊上 (Lóng-guāng Dá-fū Yùn-shàng 龍光達夫蘊上)

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán master of Hú-běi, and **a scion of the Míng imperial house** (*Míng Gāo-huáng-dì yì* 明高皇帝裔, descendant of the Míng founding emperor Zhū Yuán-zhāng), per Shū Jùn-jí's 1687 preface to the *Jí wénzì chán* [[KR6q0208]]. *Zì* Dá-fū 達夫 ("Well-Penetrating Husband"); abbacy-associated name Lóng-guāng 龍光 ("Dragon-Light"), from his abbacy of Lóng-guāng-sì 龍光寺 in È-zhōu 鄂州 (modern Wǔ-hàn 武漢 area). Biological younger-brother of [[蘊宏|Yùn-hóng Kuān-fū]] 蘊宏寬夫. Lifedates unrecorded; active late Míng through early Qīng.

After the 1644 Míng-Qīng collapse, Dá-fū fled successively through Lóng-hú 龍湖, Yún-guì 滇黔 (Yúnnán-Guìzhōu), Wù-yuè 吳越 (Jiāng-sū-Zhè-jiāng), and "southern lands of barbarian-smoke and malarial-rain" before settling at an East-suburbs hermitage (probably in his home region of Hú-běi near È-zhōu), where he composed literary-Chán poetry and played musical instruments with his elder brother Kuān-fū.

Dharma-heir of An-wēng héshàng 按翁和尚 (An-wēng's own lifedates inferred from the *Jī lèi jí* elegy: began monastic career in the Chóngzhēn 崇禎 era, 1628–1644; attained awakening at Xī-fēng 西峰; died after his disciples had compiled his three-session teachings for printing). Lineage-affiliation likely Línjì, though not firmly identified in the surviving records.

Surviving works:
- *È-zhōu Lóng-guāng Dá-fū chánshī jī lèi jí* 鄂州龍光達夫禪師雞肋集 [[KR6q0206]] — his one-juan teaching-collection.
- *Sòng gǔ gōu jù* 頌古鉤鉅 [[KR6q0207]] — his *sòng gǔ* collection with commentary by his brother Kuān-fū.
- *Jí wénzì chán* 集文字禪 [[KR6q0208]] — his Míng-loyalist literary-Chán poetry anthology, modelled on the Sòng master Juéfàn Huìhóng's *Shímén wénzì chán* [[KR6q0193]] and comparable to Wén Tiān-xiáng's *jí Dù* 集杜 poetic practice.

Principal attendant-editors: [[道汜|Dào-sì]] 道汜 and [[道沖|Dào-chōng]] 道沖.

The shared dharma-name initial *Yùn* 蘊 between Dá-fū (*Yùn-shàng*) and Kuān-fū (*Yùn-hóng*) represents their biological-brother relationship rather than shared monastic ordination-line.
