# Yùnshàng 蘊上 (Lóngguāng Dáfū Yùnshàng 龍光達夫蘊上)

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán master of Húběi, and **a scion of the Míng imperial house** (*Míng Gāohuángdì yì* 明高皇帝裔, descendant of the Míng founding emperor Zhū Yuánzhāng), per Shū Jùnjí's 1687 preface to the *Jí wénzì chán* [[KR6q0208]]. *Zì* Dáfū 達夫 ("Well-Penetrating Husband"); abbacy-associated name Lóngguāng 龍光 ("Dragon-Light"), from his abbacy of Lóngguāngsì 龍光寺 in Èzhōu 鄂州 (modern Wǔhàn 武漢 area). Biological younger-brother of [[蘊宏|Yùnhóng Kuānfū]] 蘊宏寬夫. Lifedates unrecorded; active late Míng through early Qīng.

After the 1644 Míng-Qīng collapse, Dáfū fled successively through Lónghú 龍湖, Yúnguì 滇黔 (Yúnnán-Guìzhōu), Wùyuè 吳越 (Jiāngsū-Zhèjiāng), and "southern lands of barbarian-smoke and malarial-rain" before settling at an East-suburbs hermitage (probably in his home region of Húběi near Èzhōu), where he composed literary-Chán poetry and played musical instruments with his elder brother Kuānfū.

Dharma-heir of An-wēng héshàng 按翁和尚 (An-wēng's own lifedates inferred from the *Jī lèi jí* elegy: began monastic career in the Chóngzhēn 崇禎 era, 1628–1644; attained awakening at Xīfēng 西峰; died after his disciples had compiled his three-session teachings for printing). Lineage-affiliation likely Línjì, though not firmly identified in the surviving records.

Surviving works:
- *Èzhōu Lóngguāng Dáfū chánshī jī lèi jí* 鄂州龍光達夫禪師雞肋集 [[KR6q0206]] — his one-juan teaching-collection.
- *Sòng gǔ gōu jù* 頌古鉤鉅 [[KR6q0207]] — his *sòng gǔ* collection with commentary by his brother Kuānfū.
- *Jí wénzì chán* 集文字禪 [[KR6q0208]] — his Míng-loyalist literary-Chán poetry anthology, modelled on the Sòng master Juéfàn Huìhóng's *Shímén wénzì chán* [[KR6q0193]] and comparable to Wén Tiānxiáng's *jí Dù* 集杜 poetic practice.

Principal attendant-editors: [[道汜|Dàosì]] 道汜 and [[道沖|Dàochōng]] 道沖.

The shared dharma-name initial *Yùn* 蘊 between Dáfū (*Yùnshàng*) and Kuānfū (*Yùnhóng*) represents their biological-brother relationship rather than shared monastic ordination-line.
