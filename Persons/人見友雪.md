# Rénjiàn Yǒuxuě 人見友雪

Edo-period Japanese physician and book-collector, descendant of the Hitomi (人見) physician lineage (the *Zhúdòng hòurén* 竹洞後人 of the postface). His library held a complete manuscript copy of [[曾世榮]] Zēng Shìróng's *Huóyòu kǒuyì* 活幼口議 ([[KR3ej024]]) which was acquired by the Tanba family and used as base-text for the 1820 critical edition by [[丹波元胤]] Tanba Genin.
