Miù Yí-tíng 繆宜亭, mid-Qīng Sūzhōu *jìn-shì* and scholar-physician, *fl.* mid-eighteenth century, of obscure biographical record. He appears in the Sūzhōu Yè-school *sān-jiā* (three-master) compilations alongside [[葉桂]] Yè Tiān-shì and [[薛雪]] Xuē Shēng-bái — see [[KR3ep020]] *Sān-jiā yī-àn hé-kè* and [[KR3ep080]] *Yè Tiān-shì yī-àn* — though his individual casebook does not survive separately. Not in CBDB.
