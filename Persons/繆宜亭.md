Miù Yítíng 繆宜亭, mid-Qīng Sūzhōu *jìnshì* and scholar-physician, *fl.* mid-eighteenth century, of obscure biographical record. He appears in the Sūzhōu Yè-school *sānjiā* (three-master) compilations alongside [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì and [[薛雪]] Xuē Shēngbái — see [[KR3ep020]] *Sānjiā yīàn hékè* and [[KR3ep080]] *Yè Tiānshì yīàn* — though his individual casebook does not survive separately. Not in CBDB.
