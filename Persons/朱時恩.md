# Zhū Shí'ēn 朱時恩

Late-Míng lay Chán practitioner and compiler. Active in the early Chóngzhēn era; exact lifedates and native place not transmitted.

Compiler of the *Jūshì fēndēng lù* 居士分燈錄 ([[KR6q0039]]), preface 1631 — the principal late-Míng compilation devoted exclusively to lay (*jūshì*) Chán practitioners.
