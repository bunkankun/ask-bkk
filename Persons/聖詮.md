**Shōzen** 聖詮 — Medieval Japanese **Kegon** 華嚴 (Avataṃsaka) monk, identifiable only as the author of *Huā-yán wǔ-jiào zhāng shēn-yì chāo* 華嚴五教章深意鈔 ([[KR6t0037]], T73n2341), a ten-fascicle sub-commentary on Fa-zang's *Wǔ jiào zhāng* focused on the philosophically substantive ("deep-meaning") doctrinal positions. Doctrinal style and citational patterns place him in the post-Gyōnen Tōdaiji-Kegon tradition of the 14th–15th centuries.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001485. No further biographical detail is preserved.
