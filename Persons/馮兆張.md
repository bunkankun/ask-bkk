**Féng Zhàozhāng** 馮兆張 (*zì* Chǔzhān 楚瞻, *fl.* 1670–1710, 清), Hǎi-yán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng) physician of the early *Kāng-xī* era. Author of the substantial early-Qīng systematic medical encyclopedia ***Féng-shì jǐn-náng mì-lù*** 馮氏錦囊秘錄 (50 *juǎn*, completed 1702 = *Kāng-xī* 41), one of the principal bridges between the late-Míng *gāng-mù* synthesis (Wáng Kěntáng, Wǔ Zhīwàng) and the imperially-sponsored *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* of 1742. The *Jǐn-náng mì-lù* is organised by sub-discipline (internal medicine, paediatrics, gynecology, *shāng-hán*, *wài-kē*, pulse-diagnosis, *materia medica*) and was widely reprinted throughout the Qīng. The gynecology section was extracted and separately circulated as the *Nǚkē jīng-yào* 女科精要 ([[KR3ei018]]). Féng's clinical approach combines the Lǐ-Zhū *bǔ-zhōng* / *zī-yīn* synthesis with extensive Sòng-Yuán canonical engagement. CBDB has no entry; no further biographical detail located.
