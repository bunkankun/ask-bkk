**Lín Qǐlóng** 林起龍 (*fl.* Kāngxī, 清), Qīng physician and bibliophile who in 1674 produced an annotated reprint of [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí's *Shānghán lùn tiáo biàn* 傷寒論條辨 ([[KR3ef015]]). His preface to that reprint accused [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng of plagiarizing Fāng's *Tiáo biàn* in his *Shànglùn piān* ([[KR3ef026]]) — a polemic that became a central controversy of early-Qīng *Shānghán* scholarship.
