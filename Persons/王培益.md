Wáng Péi-yì 王培益, early-Qīng (康熙) compiler. Otherwise unrecorded; named in the *Sìkù* tiyao to the *Sòng bùyī jí* 宋布衣集 ([[KR4e0238]]) as the publisher who in *Kāng-xī yǐ-chǒu* 康熙乙丑 (= Kāng-xī 24 = 1685) combined the previously separately-printed *É-chí jí* 鵝池集 (poetry) and *Yān-shí jí* 燕石集 (prose) of the late-Míng *bùyī* poet [[宋登春]] into the present three-*juǎn* unified collection.
