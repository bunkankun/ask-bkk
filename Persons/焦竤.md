**Jiāo Hóng** 焦竤 / 焦竑 (1540–1620), *zì* Ruò hóu 弱侯, *hào* Dàn yuán 澹園. Late-Míng scholar-official of Jīn líng 金陵 (Nánjīng), first-placed *zhuàng yuán* of 1589, Hàn lín bachelor. A leading historian, bibliographer (*Guó shǐ jīng jí zhì* 國史經籍志), and Confucian-Daoist-Buddhist syncretist. Author of [[KR5h0056]] *Lǎo zǐ yì* 老子翼 and [[KR5h0057]] *Zhuāng zǐ yì* 莊子翼 — **the standard collected-commentary editions** of the *Lǎo zǐ* and *Zhuāng zǐ* from the late Míng, compiling the exegetical traditions.
