Yú Qīn 余欽 (fl. early 8th c.), Táng *Jíxián diàn* 集賢殿 academician. Named in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* among the seven compilers of Xú Jiān's *Chūxué jì* 初學記 ([[KR3k0006]]) under Táng Xuánzōng's commission of Kāiyuán 13 (725). No independent biography survives; he is identified only as a member of the *Jíxián* compiling staff during Xú Jiān's tenure as senior academician. The CBDB record (Qīng 余欽, id 205675, fl. 1540) is a homonymous Míng figure, unrelated.
