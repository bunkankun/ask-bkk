Xú Qīn 徐嶔, mid-19th-century descendant — **great-grandson** (曾孫) — of [[徐大椿]] Xú Dà-chūn (1693–1771). Named in [[KR3eq085]] *Shèn-jí chú-yán* as the family member who carried out the *Róng-chéng* (Fú-zhōu) block-print reprint of the text at the urging of Péng Yùn-zhāng. Otherwise undocumented in standard biographical sources.
