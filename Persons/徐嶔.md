Xú Qīn 徐嶔, mid-19th-century descendant — **great-grandson** (曾孫) — of [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (1693–1771). Named in [[KR3eq085]] *Shènjí chúyán* as the family member who carried out the *Róngchéng* (Fúzhōu) block-print reprint of the text at the urging of Péng Yùnzhāng. Otherwise undocumented in standard biographical sources.
