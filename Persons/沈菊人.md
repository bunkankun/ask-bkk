Shěn Jú-rén 沈菊人, mid-Qīng Jiāng-nán scholar-physician, *fl.* mid-19th c. According to the preface to [[KR3ep061]] *Shěn Jú-rén yī-àn* (written by his son-in-law), he was a Confucian polymath, accomplished in poetry, painting, and medicine, who studied *jīng-luò*, pulse-theory, *yào-xìng*, and *bìng-qíng* in depth. His principal disciples Lǐ Xiào-yún 李莜云 and Lǚ Bǎi-chún 呂伯純 compiled his cases into the two-*juǎn* surviving casebook. No further transmitted biographical record. Not in CBDB.
