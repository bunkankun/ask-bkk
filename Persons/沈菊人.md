Shěn Júrén 沈菊人, mid-Qīng Jiāngnán scholar-physician, *fl.* mid-19th c. According to the preface to [[KR3ep061]] *Shěn Júrén yīàn* (written by his son-in-law), he was a Confucian polymath, accomplished in poetry, painting, and medicine, who studied *jīngluò*, pulse-theory, *yàoxìng*, and *bìngqíng* in depth. His principal disciples Lǐ Xiàoyún 李莜云 and Lǚ Bǎichún 呂伯純 compiled his cases into the two-*juǎn* surviving casebook. No further transmitted biographical record. Not in CBDB.
