Dìngliè 定冽, *zì* **Gǔméi** 古梅 ("Old Plum"); often referred to as **Wéishān Gǔméi** 溈山古梅 from his abbacy at the famous **Wéishān** 溈山 (Wéi-yǎng-school's home mountain). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huìshān Zhìhǎi** 慧山智海. Lifedates unknown — active **1669+**. Native of **Shàolíng** 邵陵 (Húnán); lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** As a youth attended the *yìxiáng* 邑庠 (local Confucian academy), but was struck by the urgency of *shēngsǐ* (life-and-death). Took tonsure and full ordination. Trained for fifteen years under **Huìshān Zhìhǎi**.

**Abbacies (two).** **Tánzhōu Fúróngshān Guǎnghuà chánsì** 潭州芙蓉山廣化禪寺 (Húnán); **Tánzhōu Dà-Wéishān Mìyìn chánsì** 潭州大溈山密印禪寺 (entered **康熙己酉九月廿一日** = **1669-10-15** as the historic Wéi-yǎng-school home-mountain seat).

**Disciple.** **Tiānyì Jùxiáng** 天翼巨翔.

**Work.** [[KR6q0576]] 《溈山古梅冽禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B463, recorded by his attendant **Zhēnqiān** [[真謙]] *jìlù*.

**Sources.** 《溈山古梅冽禪師語錄》 vol. 1; 《五燈全書》 vol. 103.
