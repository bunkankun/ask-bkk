Liú Jǐngyín 劉景寅 (Míng), the editor named in the catalog meta of [[KR4d0269]] *Jiàngǔ jīngxuǎn Lù Fàngwēng shījí qiánjí* as 選別集 ("selected the *biéjí*"). The *Sìkù* tíyào of [[KR4d0270]] notes only that the *biéjí* in 1 *juǎn* is an anonymous appendix drawing on Fāng Huí's *Yíngkuí lǜsuǐ*; the SBCK catalog metadata attribution to Liú Jǐngyín reflects a Míng-era reattribution preserved in the printed source, consistent with the *Hóngzhì* re-cut by Rǎn Xiàolóng of *Yúháng*. CBDB id 276955 (lifedates not recorded). Distinct from the Míng-era Liú Jǐngyín 劉景寅 (b. 1466 or b. 1484, CBDB ids 200885 / 201885), separate persons.
