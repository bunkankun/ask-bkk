Rèn Yuān 任淵 (d. 1144, *zì* Zǐyuān 子淵, *hào* Tiānshè 天社 — after the *Tiānshèshān* mountain in *Xīnjīn* 新津), of *Xīnjīn* (Sìchuān). *Shàoxīng* 1 / 1131 *yǐchǒu wényì lèishì yú yǒusī dìyī* — i.e. ranked first in the *literary-arts* special category in the *Sìchuān* regional examination of 1131. Held office through *Tóngchuān* 潼川 *xiàn* (judicial commissioner). The principal Southern-Sòng *zhùjiā* (annotator) of the *Jiāngxī*-school poets: composed both the *Shāngǔ Nèijí shīzhù* 山谷內集詩注 [[KR4d0085]] (the principal annotated edition of Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]]'s post-*Yuánfēng* poetry, in 20 *juǎn* with embedded *niánpǔ*) and the *Hòushān shīzhù* 後山詩注 [[KR4d0088]] (the annotated edition of Chén Shīdào 陳師道 [[陳師道]]'s poetry, in 12 *juǎn*) — together the foundational Sòng *Jiāngxī*-school annotation pair. Also composed the *Shāngǔ jīnghuá lù* 山谷精華錄 in 6 *juǎn shīfùmíngzàn* + 2 *juǎn záwén* (a selective annotated edition of Huáng Tíngjiān; original lost; what circulates under that title is a Míng-period forgery). The *Yuánxù* by Xǔ Yǐn 許尹 of *Póyáng* (the *HuángChén shīzhù xù*) — preserved at the head of the *Sìkù* recension of [[KR4d0085]] — is the principal external attestation of Rèn Yuān's annotation method.
