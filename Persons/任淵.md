Rèn Yuān 任淵 (d. 1144, *zì* Zǐ-yuān 子淵, *hào* Tiān-shè 天社 — after the *Tiān-shè-shān* mountain in *Xīn-jīn* 新津), of *Xīn-jīn* (Sì-chuān). *Shào-xīng* 1 / 1131 *yǐ-chǒu wén-yì lèi-shì yú yǒu-sī dì-yī* — i.e. ranked first in the *literary-arts* special category in the *Sì-chuān* regional examination of 1131. Held office through *Tóng-chuān* 潼川 *xiàn* (judicial commissioner). The principal Southern-Sòng *zhù-jiā* (annotator) of the *Jiāng-xī*-school poets: composed both the *Shān-gǔ Nèi-jí shī-zhù* 山谷內集詩注 [[KR4d0085]] (the principal annotated edition of Huáng Tíng-jiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]]'s post-*Yuán-fēng* poetry, in 20 *juǎn* with embedded *nián-pǔ*) and the *Hòu-shān shī-zhù* 後山詩注 [[KR4d0088]] (the annotated edition of Chén Shī-dào 陳師道 [[陳師道]]'s poetry, in 12 *juǎn*) — together the foundational Sòng *Jiāng-xī*-school annotation pair. Also composed the *Shān-gǔ jīng-huá lù* 山谷精華錄 in 6 *juǎn shī-fù-míng-zàn* + 2 *juǎn zá-wén* (a selective annotated edition of Huáng Tíng-jiān; original lost; what circulates under that title is a Míng-period forgery). The *Yuán-xù* by Xǔ Yǐn 許尹 of *Pó-yáng* (the *Huáng-Chén shī-zhù xù*) — preserved at the head of the *Sìkù* recension of [[KR4d0085]] — is the principal external attestation of Rèn Yuān's annotation method.
