**Liú Yǎn** 劉弇 (1048–1102), *zì* Wěimíng 偉明, *hào* Yúnlóng 雲龍, of Jízhōu 吉州 Ānfú 安福 (Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* 1079; passed the *hóngcí kē* (broad erudition exam) in the *Yuányòu* period and was selected for the *Hànlínyuàn*. A talented stylist of the late *Yuányòu* / early *Yuánfú* period, allied with the Yuányòu (SūHuáng) faction in literary positioning. Held office as *Mìshūshěng zhèngzì*, *Jiàoshūláng*, and *Zhī Chángshā xiàn*. Author of *Lóngyún jí* 龍雲集 [[KR4d0107]] in 32 *juǎn* — preserved through the editorial labour of his student Luō Liángbì 羅良弼. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444. The catalog gives 1048–1102; CBDB confirms.
