**Liú Yǎn** 劉弇 (1048–1102), *zì* Wěimíng 偉明, *hào* Yún-lóng 雲龍, of Jí-zhōu 吉州 Ān-fú 安福 (Jiāng-xī). *Jìnshì* 1079; passed the *hóng-cí kē* (broad erudition exam) in the *Yuán-yòu* period and was selected for the *Hàn-lín-yuàn*. A talented stylist of the late *Yuán-yòu* / early *Yuán-fú* period, allied with the Yuán-yòu (Sū-Huáng) faction in literary positioning. Held office as *Mì-shū-shěng zhèng-zì*, *Jiào-shū-láng*, and *Zhī Cháng-shā xiàn*. Author of *Lóng-yún jí* 龍雲集 [[KR4d0107]] in 32 *juǎn* — preserved through the editorial labour of his student Luō Liáng-bì 羅良弼. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444. The catalog gives 1048–1102; CBDB confirms.
