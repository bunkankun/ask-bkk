Zhōu Dé-qīng 周德清 (1277–1365), *zì* Tǐng-zhāi 挺齋 (or Tíng-zhāi 庭齋), of Gāo-ān 高安 in Lóng-xīng 龍興 / Ruì-zhōu 瑞州 (modern Jiāng-xī). The principal Yuán-period *yīn-yùn* (phonology) scholar for *qǔ*-writing. His magnum opus, the *Zhōng-yuán yīn-yùn* [[KR4j0090]] (completed in *Tài-dìng jiǎ-zǐ* / 1324), is the founding work of modern *Mandarin* phonology — the first rhyme-book to classify *píng-shēng* into *yīn-yáng* sub-tones and to drop the *rù-shēng* category, instead distributing *rù-shēng* characters into *píng-shǎng-qù*. The work was prepared for *běi-qǔ* practice (the Yuán *zá-jù* tradition). CBDB 104040 with no recorded dates; standard reference works (Chinese encyclopedia entries) supply 1277–1365.
