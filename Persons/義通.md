# 義通

Yìtōng (寶雲義通 Bǎoyún Yìtōng, 927–988) was the abbot of **Bǎoyúnsì** 寶雲寺 (Níngbō / Sìmíng) and a major figure of the early Northern-Sòng Tiāntái revival. He was the teacher under whom [[知禮]] Sìmíng Zhīlǐ received his Tiāntái formation; the Bǎoyúnsì lineage that he headed is documented in [[宗曉]] Zōngxiǎo's *Bǎoyún zhènzǔ jí* ([[KR6d0222]]).
