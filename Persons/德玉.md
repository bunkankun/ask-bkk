# Déyù 德玉 (Shèng-kě Déyù 聖可德玉)

Early-Qīng Chán master of Sichuan. *Hào* Shèng-kě 聖可 ("Saintly-Permissible"); also Yìn-yù 印玉 ("Seal-Jade"), Huá-yán lǎo-rén 華嚴老人 ("Old-Man of Huá-yán"), Shèng-kě dàshī 聖可大師. Native of Yíng-shān 營山 (Sichuan). Lifedates 1628/8/12 – 1701/02 (Chóngzhēn 1/7/13 per *Huá-yán-sì xù zhì* 華嚴寺續志 juan 2; death in Kāngxī 40 = 1701/1702 per *Yí nián lù* 疑年錄 p. 461, age 73).

Abbot of **Huá-yán-sì** 華嚴寺 in Sichuan, one of the major Qīng-era regional Buddhist monastic centres.

Produced multiple *shùn zhū* 順硃 ("following red-marking") commentaries — a distinctive Qīng-Buddhist commentary-format using red-ink/black-ink typesetting conventions:

- *Chánlín bǎoxùn shùn zhū* 禪林寶訓順硃 [[KR6q0156]] (X64 n1265) — four-juan commentary on the *Chánlín bǎoxùn* [[KR6q0099]].
- *Dào dé jīng shùn zhū* 道德經順硃 [[KR6q0227]] (J36 B351) — two-juan commentary on *Lǎo-zǐ*.

Dé-yù represents a distinct Qīng-era pattern of Chán-master-as-text-commentator across both Buddhist canonical texts (the *Chánlín bǎoxùn*) and classical Chinese philosophical classics (the *Dào dé jīng*), paralleling the integrative-doctrinal project of [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] and [[淨挺|Liáng-tíng Jìng-tǐng]].

**Lineage and other works.**
- Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666) — same line as [[照衣]], [[徹御]], [[法通]], [[道智]] etc.
- [[KR6q0488]] 《華嚴聖可禪師語錄》 5 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B342, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Guāng-fó** [[光佛]] *děng biān*.
- Wrote front-preface to [[KR6q0487]] 《佛語御禪師語錄》 (his dharma-nephew Chè-yù's *yǔ-lù*).
- Listed among Pòshān's principal heirs in the [[海明]] note ("Shèng-kě Dé-yù 聖可德玉, Huá-yán line").
- Teacher of **Bù-yàn Dào-lè** [[道樂]] (1640–?), whose *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0538]] (J38 B419) was prefaced 1693.
- Author of the preface to his nephew **Bàn-shuǐ Fā-yuán** [[發元]]'s *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0539]] (J38 B420), signed "華岩玉老僧" and dated **康熙辛未花朝月補天日** = **1691** — the same year his nephew died (1691-07-26) and just before [[道樂]] engraved the volume on his behalf ("法弟不厭代刻").
