Liáng Chǔ 梁儲 (1451–1527), *zì* **Shū-hòu** 叔厚, *hào* **Hòu-zhāi** 厚齋, in old age **Yù-zhōu** 鬱洲, *shì* **Wén-kāng** 文康, native of **Shùn-dé** 順德 (Guǎng-zhōu, Guǎng-dōng). Chéng-huà 14 / *wù-xū* (1478) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Huá-gài diàn dà-xué-shì*; through three reigns (Xiào-zōng / Wǔ-zōng / Shì-zōng) served in the Inner Cabinet. With **Yáng Tíng-hé** 楊廷和 in the late Zhèng-dé era he held the cabinet against the dissolution of the **Wǔ-zōng** court — repeatedly memorialising against Wǔ-zōng's self-enfeoffment as *Zhèn-guó-gōng* 鎮國公, against the gift of Shǎn-zhōng pastureland to Prince Qín, and pleading at least 8 or 9 times for the emperor's return from his northern pleasure-tours. *Míng-shǐ* main biography. **Huáng Zuǒ** 黃佐 wrote the preface for the *Yù-zhōu jí* recension; the expanded *Yù-zhōu yí-gǎo* in 10 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0134]]) was prepared by Liáng's grandson **Liáng Zī** 梁孜 (*zhōng-shū shè-rén*), who from the Inner Cabinet records transcribed Liáng's memorials and incorporated them.
