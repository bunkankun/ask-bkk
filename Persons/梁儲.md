Liáng Chǔ 梁儲 (1451–1527), *zì* **Shūhòu** 叔厚, *hào* **Hòuzhāi** 厚齋, in old age **Yùzhōu** 鬱洲, *shì* **Wénkāng** 文康, native of **Shùndé** 順德 (Guǎngzhōu, Guǎngdōng). Chénghuà 14 / *wùxū* (1478) *jìnshì*; rose to *Huágài diàn dàxuéshì*; through three reigns (Xiàozōng / Wǔzōng / Shìzōng) served in the Inner Cabinet. With **Yáng Tínghé** 楊廷和 in the late Zhèngdé era he held the cabinet against the dissolution of the **Wǔzōng** court — repeatedly memorialising against Wǔzōng's self-enfeoffment as *Zhènguógōng* 鎮國公, against the gift of Shǎnzhōng pastureland to Prince Qín, and pleading at least 8 or 9 times for the emperor's return from his northern pleasure-tours. *Míngshǐ* main biography. **Huáng Zuǒ** 黃佐 wrote the preface for the *Yùzhōu jí* recension; the expanded *Yùzhōu yígǎo* in 10 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0134]]) was prepared by Liáng's grandson **Liáng Zī** 梁孜 (*zhōngshū shèrén*), who from the Inner Cabinet records transcribed Liáng's memorials and incorporated them.
