Shèngkě 聖可 = *hào* of [[德玉]] **Shèngkě Déyù** 聖可德玉 (1628–1701). The catalog meta of [[KR6q0487]] credits *Shèngkě* as the *xù* preface-writer; this resolves to the [[德玉]] person-note (Línjì Pòshān-line, abbot of Huáyánsì in Sìchuān, dharma-uncle of [[徹御]]). See [[德玉]] for full biographical material and the broader work-list.
