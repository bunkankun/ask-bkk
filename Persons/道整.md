Dàozhěng 道整, an Eastern-Jìn monk, was one of the original companions of [[法顯]] Faxian on his overland pilgrimage to India, setting out from Cháng'ān in Lóng'ān 隆安 3 (399 CE). According to Faxian's own *Fóguó jì* 佛國記 ([[KR2k0136]]), Dàozhěng decided to remain in India rather than return to China, finding there the monastic life he had long sought. No independent biography survives.
