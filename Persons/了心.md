# Liǎo-xīn 了心

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249); named among the fifteen co-editors of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0315]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Whether the same person as the Liǎo-xīn attendant of Wúmén Huìkāi above cannot be confirmed — both are active mid-thirteenth century in the same Línjì orbit.
