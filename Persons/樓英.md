**Lóu Yīng** 樓英 (*zì* Quánshàn 全善, *hào* Gǔquán 谷泉, 1332–1401) — late-Yuán / early-Míng physician of Xiāoshān 蕭山 (Zhèjiāng); third-generation disciple of Zhū Zhènhēng (Dānxī) through his teacher [[王履]] Wáng Lǚ. Compiled the forty-*juǎn* *Yīxué gāngmù* 醫學綱目 ([[KR3er074]]) at *Hóngwǔ* 29 / 1396 — the earliest major Míng *gāngmù*-style medical compendium and an explicit structural model for Lǐ Shízhēn's *Běncǎo gāngmù* (1593).
