**Lóu Yīng** 樓英 (*zì* Quán-shàn 全善, *hào* Gǔ-quán 谷泉, 1332–1401) — late-Yuán / early-Míng physician of Xiāo-shān 蕭山 (Zhè-jiāng); third-generation disciple of Zhū Zhèn-hēng (Dān-xī) through his teacher [[王履]] Wáng Lǚ. Compiled the forty-*juǎn* *Yī-xué gāng-mù* 醫學綱目 ([[KR3er074]]) at *Hóng-wǔ* 29 / 1396 — the earliest major Míng *gāng-mù*-style medical compendium and an explicit structural model for Lǐ Shí-zhēn's *Běn-cǎo gāng-mù* (1593).
