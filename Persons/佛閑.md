Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; sobriquet **Xù-yī** 勗伊 (also referenced as **Fó-xián Xù-yī dà-shī** 佛閑勗伊大師, **Xù dà-shī** 勖大師). DILA Authority A000817. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the early-to-mid Kāngxī 康熙 era. Resident at the **Pǔ-dé-sì** 普德寺 in **Nán-jīng** 南京.

Major works (preserved as a coordinated three-text set on the Lotus Sūtra):
- **Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí xuán-tán juàn-shǒu** 法華經科拾懸談卷首 ([[KR6d0092]], X33n0626, 1 *juan*) — front-matter
- **Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí kē** 法華經科拾科 ([[KR6d0093]], X33n0627, 1 *juan*) — sectional analysis
- **Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí** 法華經科拾 ([[KR6d0094]], X33n0628, 7 *juan*) — main commentary

The three works were finalised and provided with editorial *shí-yí* 拾遺 ("gathering of remnants") by his disciple **Zhì-yī** 智一 ([[智一]]); preface by Zhì-yī dated Kāngxī *yǐ-yǒu* 康熙乙酉 (= 1705 CE).

Sources: *Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí xuán-tán juàn-shǒu* preface (1705); DILA A000817.
