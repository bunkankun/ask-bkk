Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; sobriquet **Xùyī** 勗伊 (also referenced as **Fóxián Xùyī dàshī** 佛閑勗伊大師, **Xù dàshī** 勖大師). DILA Authority A000817. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the early-to-mid Kāngxī 康熙 era. Resident at the **Pǔdésì** 普德寺 in **Nánjīng** 南京.

Major works (preserved as a coordinated three-text set on the Lotus Sūtra):
- **Fǎhuá jīng kēshí xuántán juànshǒu** 法華經科拾懸談卷首 ([[KR6d0092]], X33n0626, 1 *juan*) — front-matter
- **Fǎhuá jīng kēshí kē** 法華經科拾科 ([[KR6d0093]], X33n0627, 1 *juan*) — sectional analysis
- **Fǎhuá jīng kēshí** 法華經科拾 ([[KR6d0094]], X33n0628, 7 *juan*) — main commentary

The three works were finalised and provided with editorial *shíyí* 拾遺 ("gathering of remnants") by his disciple **Zhìyī** 智一 ([[智一]]); preface by Zhìyī dated Kāngxī *yǐyǒu* 康熙乙酉 (= 1705 CE).

Sources: *Fǎhuá jīng kēshí xuántán juànshǒu* preface (1705); DILA A000817.
