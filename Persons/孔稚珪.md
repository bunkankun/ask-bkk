Kǒng Zhìguī 孔稚珪 (ca. 447–501 CE; *zì* Dézhàng 德璋) was a Southern Qí 南齊 official and literary figure from Kuàijī 會稽 Shānyīn 山陰. He served as Yùshǐ zhōngchéng 御史中丞 under the Southern Qí. His most celebrated work is the satirical prose piece 〈北山移文〉 (Proclamation from Northern Mountain, *Wénxuǎn* juǎn 38), mocking false recluses. Biography in *Nán Qí shū* juǎn 48 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 49. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0049]].
