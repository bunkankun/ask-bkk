**Wáng Bīng** 王冰 (*c.* 710 – *c.* 805), *hào* Qǐ xuán zǐ 啟玄子, was a mid-Táng physician and medical scholar, compiler of the **standard received recension** of the ***Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn*** 黃帝內經素問 — the foundational classic of Chinese medicine. His edition (762) rearranged the chapters of the *Sù wèn*, supplied the "seven great treatises" (七篇大論) on cosmological correspondences, and included extensive commentary. Wáng's recension has been the received text ever since. The Dào zàng copy is catalogued here as [[KR5d0040]] *Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn bǔ zhù shì wén* 黃帝內經素問補註釋文.
