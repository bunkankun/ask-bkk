Guō Qiūquán 郭秋泉 (fl. early 19th c.) was a pupil of [[陳念祖]] Chén Xiūyuán's circle who in 1818 clandestinely copied [[熊笏]] Xióng Hù's *Zhōngfēng lùn* ([[KR3eh035]]) manuscript; his copy survived to become the basis of the 1884 *editio princeps*.
