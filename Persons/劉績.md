Liú Jì 劉績 (b. 1468, Mid-Míng), *zì* Zǐxiàng 子相, *hào* Lúquán 蘆泉, was a Mid-Míng classicist and *Zhū-zǐ-xué* commentator. *Jìnshì* of *Hóng-zhì sān* (1490). Author of the *Guǎn-zǐ bǔ zhù* 管子補注 (KR3c0002, supplementary commentary on the *Guǎn-zǐ*, dated 1490) and other classical commentaries. CBDB id 200709 with birth year 1468.
