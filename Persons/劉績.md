Liú Jì 劉績 (b. 1468, Mid-Míng), *zì* Zǐxiàng 子相, *hào* Lúquán 蘆泉, was a Mid-Míng classicist and *Zhūzǐxué* commentator. *Jìnshì* of *Hóngzhì sān* (1490). Author of the *Guǎnzǐ bǔ zhù* 管子補注 ([[KR3c0002]], supplementary commentary on the *Guǎnzǐ*, dated 1490) and other classical commentaries. CBDB id 200709 with birth year 1468.
