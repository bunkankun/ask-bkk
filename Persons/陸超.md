**Lù Chāo** 陸超 of Yángzhōu 揚州, Qīng-dynasty editor and transcriber, credited with bringing the *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē* 傅青主女科 into print through Yáng Zhōuhē's 楊州何 Yīwèizhāi 衣味齋 in 1827 (*Dàoguāng* 7), claiming to have transcribed it from a Fù Shān family manuscript.
