Zhèng Qián 鄭潛 (c. 1310s–1379), *zì* **Yàn-zhāo** 彥昭, *hào* **Chū-ān** 樗菴, native of **Shè-xiàn** 歙縣 (Huī-zhōu, modern Ān-huī). Yuán-Míng transition official: under the Yuán rose from *Nèi-tái yuàn* 內臺掾 through *Guǎng-dōng shuài-fǔ cóng-shì* 廣東帥府從事, *Jiān-xiū guó-shǐ yuàn* 監修國史掾, *Zhèng-zì* 正字, *Jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 監察御史, *Fú-jiàn xíng-shěng yuán-wài-láng* 福建行省員外郎, *Hǎi-běi-dào lián-fǎng fù-shǐ* 海北道亷訪副使, and *Quán-zhōu lù zǒng-guǎn* 泉州路總管. Under the Míng served as *Bǎo-yìng xiàn zhǔ-bù* 寳應縣主簿 and *Lù-zhōu tóng-zhī* 潞州同知; retired in Hóngwǔ 10 (1377). His verse collection is [[KR4e0054]] *Chū-ān lèi-gǎo* 樗菴類藁, reconstructed by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* (196 poems plus three Yuán-period prefaces by **Chéng Yǐ-wén** 程以文, **Gòng Shī-tài** 貢師泰, and **Jiē Hàng** 揭汯, son of **Jiē Xī-sī** 揭傒斯). His verse is in the post-*Yuán-Sì-jiā* (Yú, Yáng, Fàn, Jiē) classical Huī-zhōu register. CBDB id 34394: d. 1379, *fl.* 1378.
