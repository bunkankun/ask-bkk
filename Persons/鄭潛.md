Zhèng Qián 鄭潛 (c. 1310s–1379), *zì* **Yànzhāo** 彥昭, *hào* **Chūān** 樗菴, native of **Shèxiàn** 歙縣 (Huīzhōu, modern Ānhuī). Yuán-Míng transition official: under the Yuán rose from *Nèitái yuàn* 內臺掾 through *Guǎngdōng shuàifǔ cóngshì* 廣東帥府從事, *Jiānxiū guóshǐ yuàn* 監修國史掾, *Zhèngzì* 正字, *Jiānchá yùshǐ* 監察御史, *Fújiàn xíngshěng yuánwàiláng* 福建行省員外郎, *Hǎiběidào liánfǎng fùshǐ* 海北道亷訪副使, and *Quánzhōu lù zǒngguǎn* 泉州路總管. Under the Míng served as *Bǎoyìng xiàn zhǔbù* 寳應縣主簿 and *Lùzhōu tóngzhī* 潞州同知; retired in Hóngwǔ 10 (1377). His verse collection is [[KR4e0054]] *Chūān lèigǎo* 樗菴類藁, reconstructed by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* (196 poems plus three Yuán-period prefaces by **Chéng Yǐwén** 程以文, **Gòng Shītài** 貢師泰, and **Jiē Hàng** 揭汯, son of **Jiē Xīsī** 揭傒斯). His verse is in the post-*Yuán-Sìjiā* (Yú, Yáng, Fàn, Jiē) classical Huīzhōu register. CBDB id 34394: d. 1379, *fl.* 1378.
