Dǒng Chún 董淳 (*fl.* late 10th c.) was a Northern-Sòng court official and member of the editorial commission for [[KR3l0118]] *Tàipíng guǎngjì* (978) and the *Tàipíng yù lǎn* under Lǐ Fǎng's directorship. Dates not preserved in the standard biographical sources; CBDB 49689 has no year-data. Several homonyms (notably a Qīng-era 董淳, CBDB 438987, born 1759) must be disambiguated by context.
