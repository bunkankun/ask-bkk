# Lǐ Yuánzuǒ 李元佐

Mid-ninth-century Táng imperial inspector. Named in [[允堪]] Yǔn Kān's preface to [[KR6k0177]] *Sìfēnlǜ shí píní yìchāo fǔyào jì* as the official who, together with the Korean envoy Jīn shèrén 金舍人, arranged for the repatriation of [[道宣]] Dàoxuān's *Yìchāo* from the Silla royal capital to Tang China in *Huìchāng* 5 = 845, with the repatriated text reaching China on the eighth day of the second month of *Dàzhōng* 4 = 850.
