**Yán Yòumíng** 顏幼明 was an Eastern-Jìn figure, traditional author of [[KR5d0064]] *Líng qí běn zhāng zhèng jīng* 靈棋本章正經 — a divinatory text using **spirit-chess** (*líng qí* 靈棋, a set of 12 inscribed pieces).
