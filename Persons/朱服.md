Zhū Fú 朱服 (*zì* Xíngzhōng 行中; 1048–c. 1102), Northern-Sòng official and *Lóngtúgé* 龍圖閣 academician. Native of Wūchéng 烏程 (modern Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Xīníng* 6 (1073); aligned with the *Xīnfǎ* (New Policies) faction through patronage of Shū Dǎn 舒亶 and Lǚ Huìqīng 呂惠卿, though he was on personal terms with Sū Shì and was for a time dismissed in the late-*Yuánfēng* purge of Sū's associates. Held in succession the prefectures of Láizhōu 萊州 and Rùnzhōu 潤州 during *Yuánfēng*; in *Shàoshèng* (1094–98) was sent as envoy to the *Liáo*; in *Yuánfú* 2 (1099) was appointed Prefect of *Guǎngzhōu* 知廣州 with concurrent *Guǎngnándōnglù* commissions, the post on which his son [[朱彧]] draws for the maritime-trade material of [[KR3l0059]] *Píngzhōu kětán*. He was banished to *Bīnzhōu* 賓州 in the late-*Chóngníng* purge of c. 1102 despite his *Xīnfǎ* alignment, and died shortly thereafter. *Sòng shǐ* 347 carries his *lièzhuàn*. (The *Sìkù tiyao* of *Píngzhōu kětán* glosses his *Guǎngzhōu* office as 通判 vice-prefect, but the *Sòng shǐ* and the catalog meta both record him as *shuài* / *zhī Guǎngzhōu*, the senior prefectural office; both terms circulate.)
