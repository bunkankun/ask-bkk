Zhū Fú 朱服 (*zì* Xíng-zhōng 行中; 1048–c. 1102), Northern-Sòng official and *Lóng-tú-gé* 龍圖閣 academician. Native of Wū-chéng 烏程 (modern Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìn-shì* of *Xī-níng* 6 (1073); aligned with the *Xīn-fǎ* (New Policies) faction through patronage of Shū Dǎn 舒亶 and Lǚ Huì-qīng 呂惠卿, though he was on personal terms with Sū Shì and was for a time dismissed in the late-*Yuán-fēng* purge of Sū's associates. Held in succession the prefectures of Lái-zhōu 萊州 and Rùn-zhōu 潤州 during *Yuán-fēng*; in *Shào-shèng* (1094–98) was sent as envoy to the *Liáo*; in *Yuán-fú* 2 (1099) was appointed Prefect of *Guǎng-zhōu* 知廣州 with concurrent *Guǎng-nán-dōng-lù* commissions, the post on which his son [[朱彧]] draws for the maritime-trade material of [[KR3l0059]] *Píngzhōu kětán*. He was banished to *Bīn-zhōu* 賓州 in the late-*Chóng-níng* purge of c. 1102 despite his *Xīn-fǎ* alignment, and died shortly thereafter. *Sòng shǐ* 347 carries his *liè-zhuàn*. (The *Sìkù tiyao* of *Píngzhōu kětán* glosses his *Guǎng-zhōu* office as 通判 vice-prefect, but the *Sòng shǐ* and the catalog meta both record him as *shuài* / *zhī Guǎng-zhōu*, the senior prefectural office; both terms circulate.)
