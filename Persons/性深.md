Xìngshēn 性深, *zì* **Ruòhǎi** 若海 ("Sea of Suchness"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Liánfēng Sù** [[素]] (b. 1612). Lifedates unknown, active mid-17th century. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0530]] 《蓮峰禪師語錄》 10 juan, J38 B410. Addressed in juan 6 of his teacher's *yǔlù* in a *fǎyǔ* "示若海深西堂" — i.e. served as *xītáng* 西堂 (assistant abbot) under his teacher.

- - - - -

DILA registers two further homonyms: **Círén Xìngshēn** 慈仁性深 (A027398, Cáodòng, dharma-heir of **Wénzǎi** 文載 / Sōngshān, Míng-dynasty) and others — neither is the editor of this *yǔlù*.
