Xìng-shēn 性深, *zì* **Ruò-hǎi** 若海 ("Sea of Suchness"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lián-fēng Sù** [[素]] (b. 1612). Lifedates unknown, active mid-17th century. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0530]] 《蓮峰禪師語錄》 10 juan, J38 B410. Addressed in juan 6 of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* in a *fǎ-yǔ* "示若海深西堂" — i.e. served as *xī-táng* 西堂 (assistant abbot) under his teacher.

- - - - -

DILA registers two further homonyms: **Cí-rén Xìng-shēn** 慈仁性深 (A027398, Cáodòng, dharma-heir of **Wén-zǎi** 文載 / Sōng-shān, Míng-dynasty) and others — neither is the editor of this *yǔ-lù*.
