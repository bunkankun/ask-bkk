Yuán-dǐng 圓頂, *zì* **Róng-chè** 融徹 ("Penetrating Fusion"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qí-bái Dé-fù** [[德富]] (1628–1690). Lifedates unknown — served his master at Yù-quán-sì for over forty years. Native of **Shǔ-nán** 蜀南.

**Formation.** Tonsured young by **Qí-bái** at Yù-quán-sì. Took full ordination at age 20. Served his master directly under his *chuí-fú* 椎拂 (staff and whisk) for "over forty winters and summers." One day on hearing the *Fēng-fān* 風幡 (wind-flag) story, broke open the lacquer-bucket — "as if pushing aside clouds to see the sun, the great earth flat-sunken — and at once knew the matter from countless eons before is no different from now."

**Abbacy.** **Pán-lóng-shān Yù-quán chán-sì** 蟠龍山玉泉禪寺 (succeeded his teacher).

**Disciples (six).** **Yù-ruì Míng-shèng** 玥睿明盛, **Lù-ruì Míng-cōng** 璐睿明聰, **Míng-xiū Jì-jìng** 明修寂鏡, **Gǔ-lín Lóng-yù** 古林隆玉, **Yìng-yuán Pǔ-wén** 應緣普聞, **Bīn-ruì Míng-qìng** 璸睿明慶.

**Works.**
- Compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0547]] 《玉泉其白富禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B430.
- Own *yǔ-lù* 《蟠龍山玉泉融徹頂禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 (separate canonical entry).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 15; 《蟠龍山玉泉融徹頂禪師語錄》 〈序〉, 〈聯芳〉.
