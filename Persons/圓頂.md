Yuándǐng 圓頂, *zì* **Róngchè** 融徹 ("Penetrating Fusion"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qíbái Défù** [[德富]] (1628–1690). Lifedates unknown — served his master at Yùquánsì for over forty years. Native of **Shǔnán** 蜀南.

**Formation.** Tonsured young by **Qíbái** at Yùquánsì. Took full ordination at age 20. Served his master directly under his *chuífú* 椎拂 (staff and whisk) for "over forty winters and summers." One day on hearing the *Fēngfān* 風幡 (wind-flag) story, broke open the lacquer-bucket — "as if pushing aside clouds to see the sun, the great earth flat-sunken — and at once knew the matter from countless eons before is no different from now."

**Abbacy.** **Pánlóngshān Yùquán chánsì** 蟠龍山玉泉禪寺 (succeeded his teacher).

**Disciples (six).** **Yùruì Míngshèng** 玥睿明盛, **Lùruì Míngcōng** 璐睿明聰, **Míngxiū Jìjìng** 明修寂鏡, **Gǔlín Lóngyù** 古林隆玉, **Yìngyuán Pǔwén** 應緣普聞, **Bīnruì Míngqìng** 璸睿明慶.

**Works.**
- Compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0547]] 《玉泉其白富禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B430.
- Own *yǔlù* 《蟠龍山玉泉融徹頂禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 (separate canonical entry).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 15; 《蟠龍山玉泉融徹頂禪師語錄》 〈序〉, 〈聯芳〉.
