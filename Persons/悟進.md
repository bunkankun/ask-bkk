# Wùjìn 悟進 (Jièān Wùjìn 介菴悟進)

Mid-seventeenth-century **Cáodòng** 曹洞 Chán master of the 悟-generation (the generation-character following 妙 in the **Wúqù Rúkōng** → **Wúhuàn** → **Nánmíng** → **Yuānhú Miàoyòng** sub-line). *Hào* **Jièān** 介菴. Dharma-heir of [[妙用|Yuānhú Miàoyòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (1587–1642); one of three named dharma-heirs (with **Yúnfēng Jūn** 雲峰鈞 and [[悟元|Yīchū Wùyuán]] 一初悟元).

After Miàoyòng's death 1642, Wùjìn served as the principal **custodian of the master's textual legacy**: he authored the 行狀 *xíngzhuàng* (preserved in [[KR6q0416]] juan 2 and signed "*Jīnmíng xiǎoshī Wùjìn bài zhuàng* 金明小師悟進拜狀") and co-compiled the *yǔlù* proper with Wùyuán. Later held the abbacy of **Jiāxīng Jīnmíngsì** 嘉興金明寺 ("*Zuìlǐ Jīnmíngsì tángtóu Jièān Jìn chánshī* 檇李金明寺堂頭介菴進禪師" per Tán Zhēnmò's 1654 *tǎmíng*). Jointly with [[悟元|Yīchū Wùyuán]], bore the master's relics across many thousand *lǐ* of war-torn Jiānán to return them to the Jia-xing Xīngshànsì for burial after the Míng-Qīng transition.

Born **Wànlì** 萬曆 40 / 10 / 9 = **1 November 1612**, died **Kāngxī** 康熙 12 / 9 / 23 = **1 November 1673**, age 62 (僧臘 37). Native of **Jiāxìng** 嘉興, secular surname **Zhāng** 張; *zì* **Juéxiān** 覺先, *hào* **Jièān** 介菴.

He compiled the **《佛祖宗派世譜》** *Fó-zǔ zōng-pài shì-pǔ* ([[KR6r0111]], X1602, 1 juan), a comprehensive Chán transmission-genealogy from Mahākāśyapa down to his own day. (Note: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A010710 records him as **Línjì 楊岐派**; the existing note's classification of him as Cáo-dòng appears to derive from **Yuān-hú Miào-yòng's** 鴛湖妙用 own school-affiliation, which itself is contested.)

Sources: DILA A010710; [[KR6q0416]] juan 2 *xíngzhuàng* (self-authored) and *tǎmíng* (Tán Zhēnmò 1654-12-25 references "*jièshī jì tóng Yīchū Yuán chánshī, cóng cāngsāng bīngxiǎn zhōng wǎnghái shùqiān lǐ, jīfù línggǔ guī Hé* 介師既同一初元禪師從滄桑兵燹中往還數千里齎負靈骨歸禾"); juan-head signature lines of both juan.
