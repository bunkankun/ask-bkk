# Wù-jìn 悟進 (Jiè-ān Wù-jìn 介菴悟進)

Mid-seventeenth-century **Cáo-dòng** 曹洞 Chán master of the 悟-generation (the generation-character following 妙 in the **Wú-qù Rú-kōng** → **Wú-huàn** → **Nán-míng** → **Yuān-hú Miào-yòng** sub-line). *Hào* **Jiè-ān** 介菴. Dharma-heir of [[妙用|Yuān-hú Miào-yòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (1587–1642); one of three named dharma-heirs (with **Yún-fēng Jūn** 雲峰鈞 and [[悟元|Yī-chū Wù-yuán]] 一初悟元).

After Miào-yòng's death 1642, Wù-jìn served as the principal **custodian of the master's textual legacy**: he authored the 行狀 *xíng-zhuàng* (preserved in [[KR6q0416]] juan 2 and signed "*Jīn-míng xiǎo-shī Wù-jìn bài zhuàng* 金明小師悟進拜狀") and co-compiled the *yǔlù* proper with Wù-yuán. Later held the abbacy of **Jiā-xīng Jīn-míng-sì** 嘉興金明寺 ("*Zuì-lǐ Jīn-míng-sì táng-tóu Jiè-ān Jìn chán-shī* 檇李金明寺堂頭介菴進禪師" per Tán Zhēn-mò's 1654 *tǎ-míng*). Jointly with [[悟元|Yī-chū Wù-yuán]], bore the master's relics across many thousand *lǐ* of war-torn Jiā-nán to return them to the Jia-xing Xīng-shàn-sì for burial after the Míng-Qīng transition.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0416]] juan 2 *xíng-zhuàng* (self-authored) and *tǎ-míng* (Tán Zhēn-mò 1654-12-25 references "*jiè-shī jì tóng Yī-chū Yuán chán-shī, cóng cāng-sāng bīng-xiǎn zhōng wǎng-hái shù-qiān lǐ, jī-fù líng-gǔ guī Hé* 介師既同一初元禪師從滄桑兵燹中往還數千里齎負靈骨歸禾"); juan-head signature lines of both juan.
