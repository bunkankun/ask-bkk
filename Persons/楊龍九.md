Mid- to late-Qīng physician of throat medicine, conventionally listed in modern catalogues as the original author of *Nángmì hóushū* 囊秘喉書 ("Pouch-Secret Throat Manual"), the underlying text revised in 1903 by [[王景華]] Wáng Jǐnghuá as [[KR3em030]] *Chóngdìng nángmì hóushū* 重訂囊秘喉書. Almost nothing is known of him independently of the text. No CBDB record, no standard biographical entry; his name does not appear in either preface to the received version, and is asserted only in later bibliographic tradition. His *floruit* is conventionally placed in the late-*Jiāqìng* to *Dàoguāng* period on the strength of the rare-book chains by which the manuscript reached Yú Yǎnghào in Suzhou in 1901, but this is inferential rather than documented.
