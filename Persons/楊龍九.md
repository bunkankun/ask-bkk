Mid- to late-Qīng physician of throat medicine, conventionally listed in modern catalogues as the original author of *Náng-mì hóu-shū* 囊秘喉書 ("Pouch-Secret Throat Manual"), the underlying text revised in 1903 by [[王景華]] Wáng Jǐng-huá as [[KR3em030]] *Chóng-dìng náng-mì hóu-shū* 重訂囊秘喉書. Almost nothing is known of him independently of the text. No CBDB record, no standard biographical entry; his name does not appear in either preface to the received version, and is asserted only in later bibliographic tradition. His *floruit* is conventionally placed in the late-*Jiā-qìng* to *Dào-guāng* period on the strength of the rare-book chains by which the manuscript reached Yú Yǎng-hào in Suzhou in 1901, but this is inferential rather than documented.
