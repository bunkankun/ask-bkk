Zhēn Liè 甄烈 (*fl.* Southern Dynasties, 5th–6th century CE) was a geographic writer of the Southern Dynasties period, author of a *Xiāngzhōu jì* 湘州記 ([[KR2k0179]]) covering the geography of the Hunan region. No biographical details are attested; his work is known only from three passages preserved in *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽.
