# 楊漢春

Yáng Hànchūn, early-Republican principal of the Chóngmíng 崇明 Sixth Lower Primary School; fellow-student of [[刁質明]] Diāo Zhìmíng under "Gāo fūzǐ" 高夫子. Wrote the 1924 preface to [[KR3em038]] *Hóukē jiāxùn* 喉科家訓.
