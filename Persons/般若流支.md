Prajñāruci (Bōrě-liú-zhī 般若流支, "Wisdom-Flow") was a Northern-Indian Buddhist translator active in mid-sixth-century Northern-Wèi / Eastern-Wèi 北魏-東魏 China. Distinct from his slightly earlier homophone Bodhiruci (菩提流支). Worked in Yè 鄴 (the Eastern-Wèi capital), often in collaboration with [[毘目智仙]] Pí-mù-zhì-xiān; together they produced [[KR6f0033|T0341]] and other Mahāyāna translations. Birth and death dates not preserved.
