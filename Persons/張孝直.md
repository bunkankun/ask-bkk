Zhāng Xiàozhí 張孝直, Southern-Sòng court physician of Gāozōng's Hànlín Medical Bureau, *fl.* mid-12th century. One of the four named editors of the *Shàoxīng běncǎo* 紹興本草 ([[KR3ec014]]) of 1159 under the chief editor Wáng Jìxiān [[王繼先]]. No independent biography; no CBDB record.
