Tánguǒ 曇果 (\*Dharmaphala, "Dharma-Fruit"; lifedates unknown), Eastern-Hàn Western-region Buddhist *śramaṇa*, *fl.* late second century CE at Luòyáng. According to the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 (T2145, juan 2 and 13), Tánguǒ collaborated with the Hàn-Chinese *upāsaka* [[康孟詳|Kāng Mèngxiáng]] in producing [[KR6b0053|*Zhōng běnqǐ jīng* 中本起經 (T196)]] — the post-awakening Buddha-biography continuing [[KR6b0040|*Xiūxíng běnqǐ jīng* (T184)]] — c. 207 CE. The two-text Buddha-biography cycle is one of the foundational documents of Hàn-period Chinese Buddhist hagiography. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A002151.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6b0053]] *Zhōng běnqǐ jīng* (T196).
