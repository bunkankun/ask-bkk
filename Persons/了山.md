# Liáoshān 了山

Japanese (Edo-period) monk of Déyún; co-underwrote the 1715 combined printing of [[KR6q0072]] *Tiāntóng Rújìng chánshī xù yǔlù* edited by [[卍山道白]] Manzan Dōhaku.
