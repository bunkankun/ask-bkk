Hán Shànzhēng 韓善徵 (fl. late 19th c.) was a Qīng author of a *Nüèzhèng lùn* 瘧症論 monograph cited by [[劉裁吾]] Liú Cáiwú in the *Zhìnüè jīyào* ([[KR3eh049]]).
