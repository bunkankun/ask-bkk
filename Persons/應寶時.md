**Yīng Bǎoshí** 應寶時 (1825–1890, *zì* Mǐn-zhāi 敏齋), late-Qīng official from Yǒng-kāng 永康, Zhèjiāng. *Dàoguāng* 24 *jǔrén* 舉人 (1844). He held a series of significant administrative posts in the Jiāng-nán region during the Tóng-zhì and Guāng-xù eras, including Sūzhōu *zhīfǔ* 知府, Shànghǎi *dào-tái* 道台 (during the post-Taiping reconstruction), and Jiāng-sū *àn-chá-shǐ* 按察使. In Shànghǎi he was active in foreign-affairs negotiation in the wake of the Western-power treaty arrangements and in the rebuilding of the local civilian administration.

His 1868 preface to [[KR3em023]] *Jiāo-shì hóu-kē zhěn-mì* 焦氏喉科枕秘 records the textual transmission of that work via Jīn Bǎo-sān 金保三 of Wú-zhōng and Sūn Yún-zhāi 孫雲齋, and stands as one of the better-attested points of contact between high-ranking late-Qīng Jiāng-nán officialdom and the publication economy of late-imperial medical books.
