**Yīng Bǎoshí** 應寶時 (1825–1890, *zì* Mǐnzhāi 敏齋), late-Qīng official from Yǒngkāng 永康, Zhèjiāng. *Dàoguāng* 24 *jǔrén* 舉人 (1844). He held a series of significant administrative posts in the Jiāngnán region during the Tóngzhì and Guāngxù eras, including Sūzhōu *zhīfǔ* 知府, Shànghǎi *dàotái* 道台 (during the post-Taiping reconstruction), and Jiāngsū *àncháshǐ* 按察使. In Shànghǎi he was active in foreign-affairs negotiation in the wake of the Western-power treaty arrangements and in the rebuilding of the local civilian administration.

His 1868 preface to [[KR3em023]] *Jiāoshì hóukē zhěnmì* 焦氏喉科枕秘 records the textual transmission of that work via Jīn Bǎosān 金保三 of Wúzhōng and Sūn Yúnzhāi 孫雲齋, and stands as one of the better-attested points of contact between high-ranking late-Qīng Jiāngnán officialdom and the publication economy of late-imperial medical books.
