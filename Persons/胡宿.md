Mid-Northern-Sòng *Hànlín xuéshì* and court drafter, *zì* Wǔpíng 武平, posthumous *Wéngōng* 文恭, of *Chángzhōu Jìnlíng* 常州晉陵. *Jìnshì* of *Tiānshèng* 2 / 1024. Held *LiǎngZhè zhuǎnyùnshǐ*, *Qǐjūzhù*, *Zhīzhìgào*, *Hànlín xuéshì*, *Shūmì fùshǐ*; retired with *Tàizǐ shàobǎo*. Famous in the mid Sòng for the dignified *sìliù piānǒu* 四六駢偶 register of his court drafts — the principal exemplar of mid-Sòng *piānlì* between Yáng Yì 楊億 and Sū Shì 蘇軾. Surviving collection: *Wéngōng jí* [[KR4d0022]] (50 *juǎn* + 1 *bǔyí*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* against the original 70-*juǎn* recension). Composed *shéndào bēi* for Jiǎng Táng [[蔣堂]] preserved in the same collection. CBDB id 8064, lifedates 996–1067.
