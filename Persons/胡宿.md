Mid-Northern-Sòng *Hàn-lín xué-shì* and court drafter, *zì* Wǔ-píng 武平, posthumous *Wén-gōng* 文恭, of *Cháng-zhōu Jìn-líng* 常州晉陵. *Jìn-shì* of *Tiān-shèng* 2 / 1024. Held *Liǎng-Zhè zhuǎn-yùn-shǐ*, *Qǐ-jū-zhù*, *Zhī-zhì-gào*, *Hàn-lín xué-shì*, *Shū-mì fù-shǐ*; retired with *Tài-zǐ shào-bǎo*. Famous in the mid Sòng for the dignified *sì-liù piān-ǒu* 四六駢偶 register of his court drafts — the principal exemplar of mid-Sòng *piān-lì* between Yáng Yì 楊億 and Sū Shì 蘇軾. Surviving collection: *Wén-gōng jí* [[KR4d0022]] (50 *juǎn* + 1 *bǔ-yí*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* against the original 70-*juǎn* recension). Composed *shén-dào bēi* for Jiǎng Táng [[蔣堂]] preserved in the same collection. CBDB id 8064, lifedates 996–1067.
