Qīng *Yìxué* scholar of Sānyuán 三原 (Shǎnxī), author of *Zhōuyì xiángshuō* 周易詳説 — a substantial close commentary on the *Yì* that is influential as a source for the *Sìkù* *tíyào* treatment of the Sòng *yìlǐ* tradition. He is repeatedly cited by the *Sìkù* editors for his observation that [[程頤]] Chéng Yí's *Yìzhuàn* substantively derives from [[胡瑗]] Hú Yuán's classroom teaching rather than from the canonical [[周敦頤]] Zhōu Dūnyí → Chéng-brother lineage that [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī later codified.
