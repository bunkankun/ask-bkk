**Zhāng Yáo** 張瑤 (*hào* Yìyún 藝耘, late Qīng / early Republic). Disciple of Zhōu Shǒurú and transmitter of the *Yìjīn jīng* / *Xǐsuǐ jīng* lineage; arranged for the 1930 lithograph reprint of the surviving Zhōu Shǒurú manuscript and wrote one of its prefaces.
