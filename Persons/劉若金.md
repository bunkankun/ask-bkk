**Liú Ruòjīn** 劉若金 (*hào* Yúnmì 雲密, 1584–1665), late-Míng / early-Qīng physician. Author of the *Běncǎo shù* 本草述 (30 *juǎn*, completed in 1664), an ambitious pharmacopoeia attempting to integrate the *qìwèishēngjiàng* 氣味升降 doctrine of the JīnYuán four masters with the *Běnjīng* substance corpus. The work's prose was famously verbose ("文辭蔓衍，讀者幾莫測其所歸") and it circulated only patchily; it was rescued from obscurity by [[楊時泰]]'s critical condensation, the *Běncǎo shù gōuyuán* 本草述鉤元 ([[KR3ec052]], printed posthumously 1842).
