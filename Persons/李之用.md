**Lǐ Zhīyòng** 李之用 — Míng *jìnshì* from Huánggāng 黃岡 (the native region of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán). Prefacer of Wàn Quán's *Guǎngsì jìyào* ([[KR3ei074]]); his preface establishes the work's theoretical-ethical framework, situating the *guǎngsì* (heir-producing) topic within Confucian *xiàozǐ* (filial-son) doctrine alongside the *bǎomìng* (life-preservation) topic. Not in CBDB.
