Mid-Northern-Sòng official and *mù-fǔ* secretary, *zì* Jǐ-shèng 幾聖, of *Qián-táng* 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). *Jìn-shì* of *Qìng-lì* 6 / 1046. Held *Sì-yuàn pàn-guān* (his first office), *zhī-xiàn* of *Pǔ-jiāng*, *Dōng-yáng*, *Yuán-chéng*; *Jīng-cáo*; finally *Sān-sī Hù-bù pàn-guān* and *Shàng-shū Cí-bù láng-zhōng* (whence the title of his collection). Long service in Hán Qí's [[韓琦]] *mù-fǔ* (Shǎn-xī, then *Běi-jīng* / *Dà-míng-fǔ*); the principal drafter of Hán's late memorials, including the celebrated 1069 *Lùn Qīng-miáo* memorial against Wáng Ān-shí's "green-sprouts" loan policy — recognized by Shén-zōng on internal reading as Qiáng's hand. Compiler of the *Hán Zhōng-xiàn yí-shì* 韓忠獻遺事, the principal anecdotal source for Hán Qí's career. Surviving collection: *Cí-bù jí* [[KR4d0030]] (35 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* against the original 40-*juǎn* recension). CBDB id 7138, lifedates 1022–1076. (Catalog meta gives "*date: 1046*" — i.e., *jìn-shì* year, not lifedate; CBDB and standard Sòng-shǐ-derived chronology of 1022–1076 are followed here.)
