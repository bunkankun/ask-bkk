Mid-Northern-Sòng official and *mùfǔ* secretary, *zì* Jǐshèng 幾聖, of *Qiántáng* 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). *Jìnshì* of *Qìnglì* 6 / 1046. Held *Sìyuàn pànguān* (his first office), *zhīxiàn* of *Pǔjiāng*, *Dōngyáng*, *Yuánchéng*; *Jīngcáo*; finally *Sānsī Hùbù pànguān* and *Shàngshū Cíbù lángzhōng* (whence the title of his collection). Long service in Hán Qí's [[韓琦]] *mùfǔ* (Shǎnxī, then *Běijīng* / *Dàmíngfǔ*); the principal drafter of Hán's late memorials, including the celebrated 1069 *Lùn Qīngmiáo* memorial against Wáng Ānshí's "green-sprouts" loan policy — recognized by Shénzōng on internal reading as Qiáng's hand. Compiler of the *Hán Zhōngxiàn yíshì* 韓忠獻遺事, the principal anecdotal source for Hán Qí's career. Surviving collection: *Cíbù jí* [[KR4d0030]] (35 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* against the original 40-*juǎn* recension). CBDB id 7138, lifedates 1022–1076. (Catalog meta gives "*date: 1046*" — i.e., *jìnshì* year, not lifedate; CBDB and standard Sòng-shǐ-derived chronology of 1022–1076 are followed here.)
