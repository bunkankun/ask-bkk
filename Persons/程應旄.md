# 程應旄 Chéng Yīngmáo

Early-Qīng *Shānghán* commentator (*fl.* 17th c.), author of *Shānghán lùn hòutiáobiàn* 傷寒論後條辨; cited alongside [[喻昌]] Yú Jiāyán and [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí as a representative of the late-Míng / early-Qīng *cuòjiǎn* (textual-rearrangement) school of *Shānghán* exegesis criticized in [[後藤省]] Gotō Sei's *Shāng fēng yuē yán* ([[KR3ef074]]).
