# 昭
Zhāo (Zhāo Shàngrén 昭上人)

An early-Northern-Sòng *shānwài* 山外 Tiāntái lecture-master active at Zhèjiāngyáng 浙陽 in Jǐngdé 景德 4 (1007). Recipient of Sìmíng Zhīlǐ's 四明知禮 ([[知禮]]) two-hundred *guānxīn* questions, the principal documentary record of which is preserved in the *Fǎzhì yíbiān guānxīn èrbǎi wèn* ([[KR6d0184]]). Likely an alternate name or honorific designation for one of the principal *shānwài* spokespersons of the period.
