Liào Píng 廖平 (1852–1932), *zì* Jìpíng 季平, late-Qīng / Republican-period Sìchuān scholar. Among the most influential late-Qīng theorists of *jīnwén* 今文 (New Text) classical learning, working broadly downstream of [[胡渭]] Hú Wèi's *kǎozhèng* settlement on the Sòng chart-tradition; his late-period *Yìxué* writings attempt to reconstruct a *xiàngshù* programme on revised philological foundations.
