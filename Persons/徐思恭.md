# Xú Sīgōng 徐思恭

Northern Sòng lay Buddhist patron, native of Wú 吳, whose initiative led to the Hángzhōu editorial redaction of Yánshòu's *Zōngjìng lù* ([[KR6q0092]]) in the late 11th century.
