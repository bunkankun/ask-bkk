**Luó Yǐn** 羅隱 (833–910), *zì* Zhāo jiàn 昭諫, was a late-Táng poet, political essayist, and satirist of Xīn chéng 新城 (Zhè jiāng). Famed for acerbic prose critiques of late-Táng politics and for his *Chán shū* 讒書. Author of [[KR5e0037]] ***Tài píng liǎng tóng shū*** 太平兩同書 — a dual-aspect Daoist-Confucian treatise on *Tài píng* (Great Peace).
