Dǒng Shòuzhī 董受芝, Republican-period physician of Jiāxīng 嘉禾 (Zhèjiāng), *fl.* early twentieth century. Acquired a manuscript copy of Miù Zūnyì's casebook from a Sūzhōu book-dealer and prepared it for publication as [[KR3ep075]] *Sōngxīn yīàn bǐjì* (1913), with preface by **Zhāng Héfēn** 張龢芬 of Cíxī 慈溪. The preface describes him as a "fellow scholar" with whom Zhāng had a long discussion of medical lore. No further transmitted biographical record.
