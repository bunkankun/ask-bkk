Dǒng Shòu-zhī 董受芝, Republican-period physician of Jiā-xīng 嘉禾 (Zhèjiāng), *fl.* early twentieth century. Acquired a manuscript copy of Miù Zūn-yì's casebook from a Sūzhōu book-dealer and prepared it for publication as [[KR3ep075]] *Sōng-xīn yī-àn bǐ-jì* (1913), with preface by **Zhāng Hé-fēn** 張龢芬 of Cí-xī 慈溪. The preface describes him as a "fellow scholar" with whom Zhāng had a long discussion of medical lore. No further transmitted biographical record.
