# Niè Xiān 聶先

Early-Qīng lay Chán practitioner and editor. Active in the Kāngxī era (mid-to-late seventeenth century); exact lifedates and native place not transmitted.

Compiler of the *Xù zhǐyuè lù* 續指月錄 ([[KR6q0024]]), 20 juan, with preface dated 1679 — the early-Qīng continuation of Qú Rǔjī's *Zhǐyuè lù*.
