Zhì-jìng 智靜, *zì* **Yīng-níng** 攖寧 ("Cradled Quiet"). Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Mián-shí Jìng-yùn** 眠石淨蘊 at **Tiān-tái Hù-guó-sì** 天台護國寺. Native of **Shān-yīn** 山陰 (Shào-xīng). Also extensively trained under [[圓悟]] **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** at Tiān-tóng and **Dōng-shān Ěr-mì** 東山爾密 (1590–1642). Decisive *xǐng* on a candle-extinguishment exchange with Ěr-mì: when an attendant accidentally extinguished a glass-lamp, Ěr-mì shouted *huǒ niè* 火聻 ("where is the fire?"), and the attendant's quick reply *yǒu zài zhè-lǐ* 有在這裡 ("right here") triggered Zhì-jìng's awakening to the *zhǐ* (DILA A019266).

**Abbacies.** **Jí-shān Jiè-zhū-sì** 蕺山戒珠寺 (Shào-xīng), **Wú-jiāng Yù-zhǐ-ān** 吳江喻指菴, **Lí-chuān Luó-hàn-sì** 黎川羅漢寺 (Jiāng-xī).

**Work.** [[KR6q0468]] 《攖寧靜禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B286, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-liàng** [[德亮]] *děng biān* + recorded by **Dé-é** [[德莪]] *děng lù*. Lifedates not located.
