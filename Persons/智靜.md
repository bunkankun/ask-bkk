Zhìjìng 智靜, *zì* **Yīngníng** 攖寧 ("Cradled Quiet"). Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Miánshí Jìngyùn** 眠石淨蘊 at **Tiāntái Hùguósì** 天台護國寺. Native of **Shānyīn** 山陰 (Shàoxīng). Also extensively trained under [[圓悟]] **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Tiāntóng and **Dōngshān Ěrmì** 東山爾密 (1590–1642). Decisive *xǐng* on a candle-extinguishment exchange with Ěrmì: when an attendant accidentally extinguished a glass-lamp, Ěrmì shouted *huǒ niè* 火聻 ("where is the fire?"), and the attendant's quick reply *yǒu zài zhèlǐ* 有在這裡 ("right here") triggered Zhìjìng's awakening to the *zhǐ* (DILA A019266).

**Abbacies.** **Jíshān Jièzhūsì** 蕺山戒珠寺 (Shàoxīng), **Wújiāng Yùzhǐān** 吳江喻指菴, **Líchuān Luóhànsì** 黎川羅漢寺 (Jiāngxī).

**Work.** [[KR6q0468]] 《攖寧靜禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B286, compiled by *fǎsì* **Déliàng** [[德亮]] *děng biān* + recorded by **Déé** [[德莪]] *děng lù*. Lifedates not located.
