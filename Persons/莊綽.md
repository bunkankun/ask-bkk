**Zhuāng Chuò** 莊綽 (*zì* Jìyù 季裕, fl. early-Southern-Sòng, ca. 1090–1150, 宋), Southern-Sòng literatus-physician of Qīng-yuán 清源 (Quánzhōu); also a noted prose-stylist. Author of the *Jiǔ gāo-huāng shù-xué fǎ* 灸膏肓腧穴法 ([[KR3ee046]]) — the foundational Southern-Sòng monograph on *gāo-huāng* acupoint moxibustion — and of the famous miscellany *Jī-lè biān* 雞肋編 ("Chicken-Rib Compilation", a major Southern-Sòng *bǐ-jì* 筆記 collection preserving anecdotes, customs, and historical observations of the Jīn-conquest period).
