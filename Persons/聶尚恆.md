Late-Míng (*Wàn-lì*-era) physician and official of Qīng-jiāng 清江 (Jiāng-xī), of the Xū-jiāng 旴江 medical lineage. *Wàn-lì xiāng jìn-shì* (provincial-examination success) and magistrate of Níng-huà 寧化 (Tīng-zhōu prefecture, Fú-jiàn). His clinical specialty was paediatrics and smallpox (*dòu-zhěn* 痘疹). Best known by his *zì* Jiǔ-wú 久吾. His principal works are *Huó-yòu xīn-fǎ* 活幼心法 (= *Dòu-zhěn huó-yòu xīn-fǎ* 痘疹活幼心法, earliest extant edition *Wàn-lì* 44 / 1616, the principal late-Míng paediatric-smallpox handbook), *Yī-xué huì-hán* 醫學彙函, and [[KR3er127]] *Qí-xiào yī-shù* 奇效醫述 (c. 1620), a post-magistracy clinical case-record collection whose appended *zhì wēn-yì fāng-fǎ* 治瘟疫方法 explicitly distinguishes pestilence treatment from *shāng-hán* methods — antedating Wú Yòu-kě's *Wēn-yì lùn* by two decades.
