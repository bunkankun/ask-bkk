Late-Míng (*Wànlì*-era) physician and official of Qīngjiāng 清江 (Jiāngxī), of the Xūjiāng 旴江 medical lineage. *Wànlì xiāng jìnshì* (provincial-examination success) and magistrate of Nínghuà 寧化 (Tīngzhōu prefecture, Fújiàn). His clinical specialty was paediatrics and smallpox (*dòuzhěn* 痘疹). Best known by his *zì* Jiǔwú 久吾. His principal works are *Huóyòu xīnfǎ* 活幼心法 (= *Dòuzhěn huóyòu xīnfǎ* 痘疹活幼心法, earliest extant edition *Wànlì* 44 / 1616, the principal late-Míng paediatric-smallpox handbook), *Yīxué huìhán* 醫學彙函, and [[KR3er127]] *Qíxiào yīshù* 奇效醫述 (c. 1620), a post-magistracy clinical case-record collection whose appended *zhì wēnyì fāngfǎ* 治瘟疫方法 explicitly distinguishes pestilence treatment from *shānghán* methods — antedating Wú Yòukě's *Wēnyì lùn* by two decades.
