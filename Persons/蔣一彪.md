**Jiǎng Yībiāo** 蔣一彪 was a Míng Daoist alchemical commentator, compiler of [[KR5d0026]] *Gǔ wén Cān tóng qì jí jiě* 古文參同契集解 — a collected-commentary edition of the "ancient-script" (*gǔ wén* 古文) recension of the *Cān tóng qì*.
