# Xuánzé 玄賾

Xuánzé 玄賾, Northern-School Chán master, one of [[神秀|Shénxiù]]'s four major dharma-heirs. Author of the lost *Lèngjiā rénfǎ zhì* 楞伽人法志 (a precursor lineage-record). Principal teacher of [[淨覺|Jìngjué]] in his post-Shénxiù period, and a key informant for the *Lèngjiā shī zī jì* [[KR6q0109]].
