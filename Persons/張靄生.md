Zhāng Ǎi-shēng 張靄生 (清, dates unrecorded) was a Kāng-xī-era literatus and friend of [[陳潢]] (Chén Huáng), who redacted Chén's hydraulic-engineering doctrine into the 12-chapter *Hé-fáng shù-yán* 河防述言 (Related-Words on River Defense), appended to [[靳輔]]'s *Zhì-hé zòu-jì shū* ([[KR2k0076]]). He also drew the *Huáng-hé quán-tú* (Comprehensive Map of the Yellow River) prefacing that work — described by the Sìkù tíyào as more accurate than any pre-Qiánlóng representation.
