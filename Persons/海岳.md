# Hǎiyuè 海岳

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shìzhě* 侍者 to [[鑑|Bīnyǎ Jiàn]] 斌雅鑑 in Bīnyǎ's Gānsù / Níngxià / Hénán abbacies of 1657 onwards. Head recorder (*jìlù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0423]] 《斌雅禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B204).

The 海-generation character aligns with a **Lóngchí sub-lineage** naming convention (海 as one of several possible generation-poem alternatives at this stratum, alongside 本, 行). As Bīnyǎ is a 33rd-generation Línjì master in the Mìyún → Línyě → Yúné → Bīnyǎ line, Hǎiyuè would be 34th-generation — active in the 1660s–80s. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0423]] juan-head signature line "*shìzhě Hǎiyuè jìlù* 侍者海岳記錄".
