# Hǎi-yuè 海岳

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shì-zhě* 侍者 to [[鑑|Bīn-yǎ Jiàn]] 斌雅鑑 in Bīn-yǎ's Gān-sù / Níng-xià / Hé-nán abbacies of 1657 onwards. Head recorder (*jì-lù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0423]] 《斌雅禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B204).

The 海-generation character aligns with a **Lóng-chí sub-lineage** naming convention (海 as one of several possible generation-poem alternatives at this stratum, alongside 本, 行). As Bīn-yǎ is a 33rd-generation Línjì master in the Mìyún → Lín-yě → Yún-é → Bīn-yǎ line, Hǎi-yuè would be 34th-generation — active in the 1660s–80s. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0423]] juan-head signature line "*shì-zhě Hǎi-yuè jì-lù* 侍者海岳記錄".
