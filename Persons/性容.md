Xìng-róng 性容, *hào* **Chūn-yán** 春巖, was a *xìng*-generation Línjì (Pò-shān line) dharma-heir of **Lián-yuè Dào-zhèng** [[道正]] 蓮月道正 (1614–1691). Listed among Liányuè's chief *fǎ-sì* alongside **Yì-tiān Hǎi** 溢天海 (*Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* j. 88). Lead compiler of the six-juan 《蓮月禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0445]]), signing the juan 1 colophon as *mén-rén Xìng-róng lù* 門人性容錄. Juan 5 of the same collection was recorded separately by the fellow-disciple **Fā-wù** 發悟; the parallel Yù-quán two-juan recension ([[KR6q0446]]) was compiled by **Fā-huì** [[發慧]]. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
