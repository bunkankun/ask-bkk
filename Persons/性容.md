Xìngróng 性容, *hào* **Chūnyán** 春巖, was a *xìng*-generation Línjì (Pòshān line) dharma-heir of **Liányuè Dàozhèng** [[道正]] 蓮月道正 (1614–1691). Listed among Liányuè's chief *fǎsì* alongside **Yìtiān Hǎi** 溢天海 (*Wǔdēng quánshū* j. 88). Lead compiler of the six-juan 《蓮月禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0445]]), signing the juan 1 colophon as *ménrén Xìngróng lù* 門人性容錄. Juan 5 of the same collection was recorded separately by the fellow-disciple **Fāwù** 發悟; the parallel Yùquán two-juan recension ([[KR6q0446]]) was compiled by **Fāhuì** [[發慧]]. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
