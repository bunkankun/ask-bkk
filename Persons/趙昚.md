# Zhào Shèn 趙昚 (1127–1194)

Eleventh emperor of the Sòng 宋 dynasty and second of the Southern Sòng, posthumously Xiàozōng 孝宗 (r. 1162–1189). A devout patron of Buddhism with strong Chán affinities. Author of the *Sānjiào lùn* 三教論 (preserved in the *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀, T49 no. 2035, juan 47) and of the imperially annotated *Yùzhù Yuánjué jīng* ([[KR6i0562]], 1183), bestowed on the Jìngshān 徑山 master [[寶印]] Bǎoyìn (1109–1190).
