**Wáng Mèngyīng** 王孟英 (real name 王士雄 Wáng Shìxióng, *zì* Mèngyīng 孟英, *hào* Mèngyǐn 夢隱 / Bànchī shānrén 半癡山人, 1808–1868), influential late-Qīng physician of Hángzhōu and one of the four great masters of the *Wēnbìng* 溫病 (warm-disease) school. Author of the *Wēnrè jīngwěi* 溫熱經緯, *Suíxī jū yīnshí pǔ* 隨息居飲食譜, and many other works. His advocacy of *qīnghuǒ* (clearing-heat) and rejection of routine warming-tonification in epidemic medicine elicited a late-Qīng *wēnbǔ* 溫補 reaction in paediatric and smallpox practice (cf. *Jìshēng jí* [[KR3ei014]]).
