# Wáng Zhū 王洙

*Zì* Yuánshū 原叔. Native of Sòngchéng 宋城 (Yīngtiānfǔ 應天府, modern Shāngqiū, Henán). Polymath learning: classics, calligraphy, mathematics, *yīnyáng* cosmology. Hanlin academician (*Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士); *Shǐguǎn jiǎntǎo* 史館檢討 at the Imperial History Office.

Rediscovered in the imperial library Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's 張仲景 long-lost *Jīnguì yùhán yàolüè fāng* 金匱玉函要略方 in 3 juan — the editorial foundation of the transmitted *Jīnguì yàolüè* 金匱要略 canonical in later Chinese medicine. Served as one of the three principal compilers, with [[王堯臣]] and [[歐陽修]], of the *Chóngwén zǒngmù* 崇文總目 ([[KR2n0001]], submitted 1041).

Aligned with Fàn Zhòngyān's 范仲淹 reform faction; demoted to Háozhōu 濠州 in the aftermath of the collapse of the Qìnglì 慶曆 reforms (1044). Sayings preserved in *Wángshì tánlù* 王氏談錄. Lifedates: 997–1057 (standard figures).
