Huìxiá 慧霞 (lifedates unrecorded), Sòng-dynasty Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk and editor (*biān* 編) of the *(Chóngbiān) Cáodòng wǔ wèi xiǎn jué* 重編曹洞五位顯訣 ([[KR6q0128]], X63 n1236), the standard Sòng-period Cáodòng *wǔ wèi* doctrinal compendium. No independent biographical material survives; no DILA authority entry. The companion commentator is [[廣輝]] Guǎnghuī; both are known principally through their editorial roles on this text.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0128]] *(Chóngbiān) Cáodòng wǔ wèi xiǎn jué* only.
