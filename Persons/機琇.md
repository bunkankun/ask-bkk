Jīxiù 機琇. Personal attendant and recorder (*jìlù*) of **Huánglián Dōngyán Zhēncàn** [[真璨]] (b. 1623–11–16). Sole compiler of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B413. Lifedates unknown.
