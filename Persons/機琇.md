Jī-xiù 機琇. Personal attendant and recorder (*jì-lù*) of **Huáng-lián Dōng-yán Zhēn-càn** [[真璨]] (b. 1623–11–16). Sole compiler of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B413. Lifedates unknown.
