Yáo Wénhào 姚文灝 (1455–1504), of Guì-xī 貴溪 (Jiāngxī), was a Míng-era Chéng-huà 20 (1484) *jìnshì* who rose to *Gōng-bù zhǔshì* 工部主事 (Director of the Bureau of Works). In Hóng-zhì 9 (1496) he was appointed Inspector of the Sōng-jiāng and Other Hydraulic Works (*Tí-dū Sōng-jiāng děng-chù shuǐlì gōng-bù zhǔshì*); his six-point memorial on water-management of that year was approved by the emperor and is preserved in the *Míng Xiào-zōng shí-lù*. He compiled the *Zhè-xī shuǐlì shū* ([[KR2k0065]]) in connection with this office, assembling a documentary anthology on the hydraulic system of western Zhèjiāng / southern Jiāngnán.
