Yáo Wénhào 姚文灝 (1455–1504), of Guìxī 貴溪 (Jiāngxī), was a Míng-era Chénghuà 20 (1484) *jìnshì* who rose to *Gōngbù zhǔshì* 工部主事 (Director of the Bureau of Works). In Hóngzhì 9 (1496) he was appointed Inspector of the Sōngjiāng and Other Hydraulic Works (*Tídū Sōngjiāng děngchù shuǐlì gōngbù zhǔshì*); his six-point memorial on water-management of that year was approved by the emperor and is preserved in the *Míng Xiàozōng shílù*. He compiled the *Zhèxī shuǐlì shū* ([[KR2k0065]]) in connection with this office, assembling a documentary anthology on the hydraulic system of western Zhèjiāng / southern Jiāngnán.
