Quān Chēng 圈稱 (*fl.* Eastern Han, late 2nd century CE) was a geographic writer, author of the *Chénliú fēngsú zhuàn* 陳留風俗傳 ([[KR2k0164]]), a record of the customs and notable persons of Chénliú Commandery 陳留郡 (modern eastern Henan). His first name 稱 is sometimes read as a different character in variant editions. No further biographical details are attested; his work survives only in fragments preserved in medieval encyclopedias.
