# Wáng Yìzhī 王益之

Mid-Southern-Sòng historian and Hàn-history specialist. *Zì* Xíngfǔ 行甫. Native of Jīnhuá 金華 (modern Zhèjiāng). Held office as *Dàlǐsī sīzhí* 大理寺司直 (Justice of the Court of Judicial Review). CBDB id 22239 records *fl.* 1187. The catalog meta gives no dates.

The *Wénxiàn tōng kǎo* records that he was thoroughly versed in Hàn-period institutional history; his works comprise the *Hànguān zǒng lù* 漢官總錄 (a comprehensive register of Hàn officialdom) and the *Zhí yuán* 職原 (origins of administrative posts) — both lost; and the *Xī Hàn nián jì* 西漢年紀 ([[KR2b0028]]) in 30 *juǎn*, the only surviving major work, with an originally separate *Kǎo yì* in 10 *juǎn* (now distributed in-line, after the Hú Sānxǐng manner) and a separately-published *Jiàn lùn* (lost). The *Xī Hàn nián jì* is the principal Sòng-period *biānnián* chronicle of the Western Hàn — composed not in lieu of but in supplement to Sīmǎ Guāng's *Tōng jiàn*, drawing on the *ChǔHàn chūnqiū*, the *Shuō yuàn*, and other sources Sīmǎ Guāng had not exploited.
