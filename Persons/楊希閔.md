**Yáng Xī-mín** 楊希閔 (*zì* Tiě-bèi 鐵備, mid-Dào-guāng / Tóng-zhì-era scholar of Jiāng-yòu 江右 / Jiāng-xī), the 1838 publisher of the eight-work *Huáng Yuán-yù yī-shū bā-zhǒng* 黃元御醫書八種 collected edition ([[KR3er047]] and related works). Yáng's *hòu-bá* 後跋 (postface), composed for the 1838 collected printing, narrates the manuscript-recovery story: only four of Huáng's eight works had been printed in the previous century (in the *Wǎn-lín cóng-shū* 宛鄰叢書 of Zhāng Cí-yǐn 張慈尹 of Cháng-zhōu), with the other four works circulating in manuscript. In *Dào-guāng wù-xū* (1838), Yáng obtained the manuscript via the calligrapher Bāo Shèn-bó 包慎伯 (= Bāo Shì-chén 包世臣) and arranged for the eight-work corpus to be jointly printed under the patronage of the *Mǐn-fǔ* educational commissioner Xú Hóu-lǐng 徐侍郎. Yáng was the principal late-Qīng editorial transmitter of Huáng-shì doctrine. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
