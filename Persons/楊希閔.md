**Yáng Xīmín** 楊希閔 (*zì* Tiěbèi 鐵備, mid-Dào-guāng / Tóng-zhì-era scholar of Jiāngyòu 江右 / Jiāngxī), the 1838 publisher of the eight-work *Huáng Yuányù yīshū bāzhǒng* 黃元御醫書八種 collected edition ([[KR3er047]] and related works). Yáng's *hòubá* 後跋 (postface), composed for the 1838 collected printing, narrates the manuscript-recovery story: only four of Huáng's eight works had been printed in the previous century (in the *Wǎnlín cóngshū* 宛鄰叢書 of Zhāng Cíyǐn 張慈尹 of Chángzhōu), with the other four works circulating in manuscript. In *Dàoguāng wùxū* (1838), Yáng obtained the manuscript via the calligrapher Bāo Shènbó 包慎伯 (= Bāo Shìchén 包世臣) and arranged for the eight-work corpus to be jointly printed under the patronage of the *Mǐnfǔ* educational commissioner Xú Hóulǐng 徐侍郎. Yáng was the principal late-Qīng editorial transmitter of Huángshì doctrine. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
