# Xián-huī 咸輝

Southern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; native of Mǐn 閩 (Fújiàn). Floruit Qián-dào period (mid-twelfth century); compiler of the **Shǒu-lèngyán-jīng yì-hǎi** 首楞嚴經義海 ([[KR6j0727]]) — a thirty-fascicle compilation that arranges the running commentary of the Táng monk **Huái-dí** 懷迪 (the Tang-period co-translator-collator of the *Lèngyán*) together with later Tang-Sòng exegetical materials, organizing each phrase of the sūtra under its accumulated commentarial layers (*pái-jīng rù-zhù* 排經入注). The work was prefaced by the *cān-zhī-zhèng-shì* 參知政事 of the Sòng court on **Qián-dào 8.11.15 (15 December 1172)**. The compilation was undertaken at the Gū-sū Dìng-huì-sì 姑蘇定慧寺 in Sūzhōu, where Xián-huī solicited patronage to cut blocks for printing.

The preface describes Xián-huī as having "kept in mind the entrustment of the Buddhas and Patriarchs, lamenting the decay of the Dharma-way, devoting his energies — beyond his Chán practice — to gathering many books and bringing them to a complete major work." Lifedates and biographical particulars are otherwise not recorded.
