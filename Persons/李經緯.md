# 李經緯 Lǐ Jīngwěi

**Lǐ Jīngwěi** 李經緯 (b. 1929), contemporary Chinese historian of medicine at the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences (中國中醫科學院); former editor-in-chief of *Zhōnghuá yīshǐ zázhì* 中華醫史雜誌 and chief editor of numerous reference works including *Zhōngyī gǔjí zhēnběn jíchéng* 中醫古籍珍本集成.
