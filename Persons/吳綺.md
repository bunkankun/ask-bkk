Wú Qǐ 吳綺 (1619–1694), *zì* Yuán-cì 園次, *hào* Fēng-nán 豐南, also Lín-huì 林蕙 and Hóng-dòu cí-rén 紅豆詞人; native of Jiāng-dū 江都 (modern Yáng-zhōu). Early-Qīng *cí*-poet, official, and *bǐ-jì* author, one of the leading figures of the *Yáng-zhōu cí pài* 揚州詞派. *Shùn-zhì* 11 (1654) *jǔrén*; held office as *Bīng-bù lì-jià sī yuán-wài-láng* and Prefect of Hú-zhōu 湖州. After dismissal, lived in retirement at Yáng-zhōu, devoted to literature. His *Lín-huì jí* 林蕙集 and *Tíng-cài jí* 停采集 collected works are catalogued separately. Author of the *Lǐng-nán fēng-wù jì* 嶺南風物記 ([[KR2k0129]]), composed during his Lǐng-nán travels and supplemented by Sòng Jùn 宋俊 and edited by Jiāng Kǎi 江闓. CBDB id 55666.
