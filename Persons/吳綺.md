Wú Qǐ 吳綺 (1619–1694), *zì* Yuáncì 園次, *hào* Fēngnán 豐南, also Línhuì 林蕙 and Hóngdòu círén 紅豆詞人; native of Jiāngdū 江都 (modern Yángzhōu). Early-Qīng *cí*-poet, official, and *bǐjì* author, one of the leading figures of the *Yángzhōu cí pài* 揚州詞派. *Shùnzhì* 11 (1654) *jǔrén*; held office as *Bīngbù lìjià sī yuánwàiláng* and Prefect of Húzhōu 湖州. After dismissal, lived in retirement at Yángzhōu, devoted to literature. His *Línhuì jí* 林蕙集 and *Tíngcài jí* 停采集 collected works are catalogued separately. Author of the *Lǐngnán fēngwù jì* 嶺南風物記 ([[KR2k0129]]), composed during his Lǐngnán travels and supplemented by Sòng Jùn 宋俊 and edited by Jiāng Kǎi 江闓. CBDB id 55666.
