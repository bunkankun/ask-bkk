**Lǚ Yánjì** 呂延濟 (fl. 718) was a Tang scholar who, while serving as *xiànwèi* 縣尉 (county sub-prefect) of Chángshān 常山 district in Qúzhōu 衢州, was recruited by Lǚ Yánzuò 呂延祚 (Vice-Minister of Works) to co-author what became known as the *Wǔchén jízhù Wén xuǎn* 五臣集註文選, the Five Officials' interpretive commentary on Xiāo Tǒng's anthology. The work was presented to Tang Xuánzōng on the tenth day of the ninth month of Kāiyuán 6 (718). Lǚ Yánjì's commentary appears in [[KR4h0003]]. He is otherwise unattested in the standard histories; CBDB has no entry. Nothing of his biography survives beyond the Kāiyuán memorial.
