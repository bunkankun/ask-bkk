Tánmójuéduō 曇摩崛多 (Skt. *Dharmagupta* "Protected by the Dharma"; also written 曇摩掘多; DILA Authority A001797) was a Buddhist monk-translator of the Yao Qin (姚秦, 384–417 CE) period. He collaborated with Dharmayaśas 曇摩耶舍 ([[曇摩耶舍]]) on the translation of the *Śāriputrābhidharma* 舍利弗阿毘曇論 ([[KR6l0013]], T1548), the canonical Abhidharma text of the Dharmaguptaka school. His precise dates and further biographical details are not recorded.

Note: This monk should not be confused with the earlier translator *Dharmagupta* (*Tánmójùduō* 曇摩笈多), a distinct figure active in the Northern Qi.
