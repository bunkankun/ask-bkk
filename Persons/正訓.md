# Zhèngxùn 正訓

Late-Joseon Buddhist monk (Kor. Jeong-hun); preserved and transmitted the lost Míng paediatric handbook *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* 保幼新編 ([[KR3ej005]]) and brought it to [[盧光履]] No Kwang-ri (Lú Guānglǚ, 1730–1784) for prefatory inscription before its publication in 1785. Lifedates unrecorded.
