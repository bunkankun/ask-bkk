# Máo Cháng 毛萇

The "Lesser Máo" (*Xiǎo Máo gōng* 小毛公) of the Hàn *Shī* 詩 tradition. Pupil (in some accounts, nephew) of [[毛亨]] the "Greater Máo." Native of Zhào 趙. According to the *Hàn shū · Rúlín zhuàn* 漢書·儒林傳 he served as Erudite (*bóshì* 博士) under Hé Jiān Xiànwáng 河間獻王 (Liú Dé 劉德, d. 130 BCE), to whom he transmitted the *Máoshī* recension; thereafter the *Máoshī* circulated as the official *gǔwén* 古文 *Shī* of the Hé Jiān principality and gradually displaced the three *jīnwén* 今文 traditions of Lǔ 魯, Qí 齊, and Hán 韓.

His role in the formation of the received *Máoshī gǔxùn zhuàn* 毛詩故訓傳 (the *Máo zhuàn* layer of [[KR1c0001]] / [[KR1c0002]] / [[KR1c0004]]) is disputed. Some modern scholars regard him as the actual editor-redactor whose work was retrospectively attributed to his teacher; others treat both Máos as semi-legendary projections of the Western-Hàn *gǔwén* school's self-presentation. Lifedates are unrecoverable; he was active in the second century BCE (mid-Hàn, under Jǐngdì 景帝 and Wǔdì 武帝). No CBDB record was located.
