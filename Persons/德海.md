# Déhǎi 德海

Early-Yuán monk, resident of Jiànyán sì 薦嚴寺; commissioner, with Tiāntái [[子聰]] Zǐcōng, of the Zhìdà 3–4 (1310–1311) reprint of the *Fényáng Wúdé chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0054]]) — the Yuán recension underlying the Taishō witness.
