Mò Méishì 莫枚士 (*míng* Wénquán 文泉, *zì* Méishì, 1837–1907) was a late-Qīng *jīng-fāng* scholar and *kǎozhèng-yīxué* (philological medical) physician of Guī-ān 歸安 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). He devoted his career to the careful philological-and-clinical analysis of Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's classical-formula corpus and produced two major scholarly contributions: the *Yán-jīng yán* 研經言 (an essay-collection on the *Shāng-hán lùn* and *Jīn-guì yào-lüè*, c. 1880) and the *Jīng-fāng lì-shì* 經方例釋 [[KR3ed081]] (a systematic formulary-commentary analysing the classical formulas by category-and-variation, c. 1885). His method — *jīng-fāng* doctrine plus *Qīng kǎozhèng* philology — is one of the cleanest late-Qīng integrations of textual scholarship and clinical practice and was praised by the most rigorous physicians of the next generation (Yú Yúng-chū 余聽鴻, Zhāng Xī-chún 張錫純).
