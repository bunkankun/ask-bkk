Mò Méishì 莫枚士 (*míng* Wénquán 文泉, *zì* Méishì, 1837–1907) was a late-Qīng *jīngfāng* scholar and *kǎozhèngyīxué* (philological medical) physician of Guīān 歸安 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). He devoted his career to the careful philological-and-clinical analysis of Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's classical-formula corpus and produced two major scholarly contributions: the *Yánjīng yán* 研經言 (an essay-collection on the *Shānghán lùn* and *Jīnguì yàolüè*, c. 1880) and the *Jīngfāng lìshì* 經方例釋 [[KR3ed081]] (a systematic formulary-commentary analysing the classical formulas by category-and-variation, c. 1885). His method — *jīngfāng* doctrine plus *Qīng kǎozhèng* philology — is one of the cleanest late-Qīng integrations of textual scholarship and clinical practice and was praised by the most rigorous physicians of the next generation (Yú Yúngchū 余聽鴻, Zhāng Xīchún 張錫純).
