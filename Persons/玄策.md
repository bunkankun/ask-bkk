# Xuáncè 玄策

Early-Táng Chán monk of Dōngyáng 東陽, fellow-student and friend of [[玄覺]] Yǒngjiā Xuánjué; instigator of Xuánjué's pilgrimage to [[惠能]] Huìnéng at Cáoxī, which led to the famous "one-night awakening" (*yīsù jué* 一宿覺) encounter.
