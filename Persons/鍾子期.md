# 鍾子期

Spring-and-Autumn-period figure; the proverbial *zhīyīn* 知音 ("one who knows the sound") of the qín-player [[伯牙]] Bóyá. Source of the *zhīyīn* metaphor that titles [[如愚]] Rúyú's late-Míng Lotus Sūtra commentary *Fǎhuá jīng zhīyīn* 法華經知音 ([[KR6d0074]]).
