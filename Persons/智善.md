# Zhìshàn 智善

Yúngài Zhìshàn 雲蓋智善, Northern-Sòng Línjì-line Chán master remembered for his stern pedagogy of his student Dòushuài Cóngyuè; featured in an extended anecdote at the close of the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī zōngmén wǔkù* ([[KR6q0061]]).
