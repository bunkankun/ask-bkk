Wéigàizhú 惟蓋竺 (also 惟蓋), a Sòng-dynasty disciple (*cānxué xiǎoshī* 參學小師) of [[重顯|Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn]] 雪竇重顯 (980–1052), and the principal compiler of his master's *Míngjué chánshī yǔlù* 明覺禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0074]], T47 n1996). Signed each of juan 1, 2, and 3 — containing the *Dòngtíng yǔlù*, the *Xuědòu kāitáng lù*, the *niāngǔ* 拈古 collections (which preserve Chóngxiǎn's *Sòng gǔ bǎi zé* 頌古百則), and the *Xuědòu hòulù* — as compiler. The compilation post-dates Chóngxiǎn's 1052 death and is bounded above by the 1065 Lǚ Xiàqīng 呂夏卿 tomb-inscription appended to juan 6.

No biographical data survive beyond the compiler signatures. DILA A001075 registers him under the short form 惟蓋 with 惟蓋竺 as an alternate; the "竺" element in the expanded form reflects either a second name-component or an unusual personal designation. Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0074]] only.
