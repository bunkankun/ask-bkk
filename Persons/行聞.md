Xíng-wén 行聞, *hào* **Fó-gǔ** 佛古 ("Buddha-Antique"). Born **萬曆庚戌 6/22 午時** = **1610-08-10 Gregorian**; died **丙午 11/23 子時** = **1666-12-18 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **57**, *fǎ-là* **35**. Native of **Tóng-chuān-zhōu** 潼川州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) — same teacher as [[覺]] **Zì-xián Xíng-jué** at [[KR6q0469]].

**Formation.** At 5 entered **Qīng-lián-sì** 青蓮寺 under **Yìn-zōng Xī chán-shī** 印宗錫. **崇禎庚午 = 1630** (age 21): visited **Jīn-líng Bào-ēn-sì** 金陵報恩寺 *suí-xǐ*, where seeing the *tǎ-guāng* 塔光 (pagoda-light) triggered breakthrough. Full precepts at **Jīn-líng Gǔ-lín** 金陵古林; **癸未 = 1643** met **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** at **Tōng-xuán** 通玄; **甲申 季春 = 1644** spring received transmission.

**Four abbacies.** **Tài-cāng Xiāng-lín-sì** 太倉香林寺 (Sū-zhōu, the title-form 香林佛古); **Píng-hú Cáo-xī-sì** 平湖曹溪寺; **Yān-dū Qiān-fó-sì** 燕都千佛寺 (Beijing); **Yān-jīng Yuán-níng-sì** 燕京圓寧寺. Stūpa at Xiāng-lín-sì.

**Sixteen heirs** including **Shí-pú Chāo-zhì** [[超質]] 石璞超質 (cf. [[KR6q0502]]); **Zhì-zhōng Chāo-yuán** 智中超沅; etc.

**Work.** [[KR6q0501]] 《佛古聞禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B364, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chāo-zhì** [[超質]] *děng biān*; juan 4 *tǎ-míng* 塔銘.
