Xíngwén 行聞, *hào* **Fógǔ** 佛古 ("Buddha-Antique"). Born **萬曆庚戌 6/22 午時** = **1610-08-10 Gregorian**; died **丙午 11/23 子時** = **1666-12-18 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **57**, *fǎlà* **35**. Native of **Tóngchuānzhōu** 潼川州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) — same teacher as [[覺]] **Zìxián Xíngjué** at [[KR6q0469]].

**Formation.** At 5 entered **Qīngliánsì** 青蓮寺 under **Yìnzōng Xī chánshī** 印宗錫. **崇禎庚午 = 1630** (age 21): visited **Jīnlíng Bàoēnsì** 金陵報恩寺 *suíxǐ*, where seeing the *tǎguāng* 塔光 (pagoda-light) triggered breakthrough. Full precepts at **Jīnlíng Gǔlín** 金陵古林; **癸未 = 1643** met **Línyě Tōngqí** at **Tōngxuán** 通玄; **甲申 季春 = 1644** spring received transmission.

**Four abbacies.** **Tàicāng Xiānglínsì** 太倉香林寺 (Sūzhōu, the title-form 香林佛古); **Pínghú Cáoxīsì** 平湖曹溪寺; **Yāndū Qiānfósì** 燕都千佛寺 (Beijing); **Yānjīng Yuánníngsì** 燕京圓寧寺. Stūpa at Xiānglínsì.

**Sixteen heirs** including **Shípú Chāozhì** [[超質]] 石璞超質 (cf. [[KR6q0502]]); **Zhìzhōng Chāoyuán** 智中超沅; etc.

**Work.** [[KR6q0501]] 《佛古聞禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B364, compiled by *fǎsì* **Chāozhì** [[超質]] *děng biān*; juan 4 *tǎmíng* 塔銘.

Sources: [[KR6q0501]] juan 1 + juan 4 *tǎmíng*; DILA authority A016279.
