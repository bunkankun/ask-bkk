**Dài Gǔ-sūn** 戴谷蓀 (d. *c.* 1933), Republican-era literatus-physician originally from **Xiū-níng** 休寧 (Huī-zhōu / Anhui), relocated to **Tài-xīng Jì-jiā-shì** 泰興季家市 (Jiāng-Sū) and finally to **Tōng-zhōu** 通州 (Nán-tōng). Confucian *zhū-shēng* 諸生 (licentiate); accomplished poet (compared to Zhào Yì 趙翼) and painter in the *Sì-Wáng* 四王 Qīng-orthodox tradition. Medical talent discovered only late in life, after a striking clinical-acumen episode at a Tōng-zhōu lodging-house. Arrived in Tōng-zhōu in *Gēng-wǔ* 庚午 = 1930. Author of *Gǔ-sūn yī-huà* 谷蓀醫話 ([[KR3eq074]]) — preserved through the 1937–1939 Sino-Japanese war by his friend "*Chù-ān*" 觸庵 (evacuated to Shanghai). Son: 蓉生 Róng-shēng. Not in CBDB.
