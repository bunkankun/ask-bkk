**Dài Gǔsūn** 戴谷蓀 (d. *c.* 1933), Republican-era literatus-physician originally from **Xiūníng** 休寧 (Huīzhōu / Anhui), relocated to **Tàixīng Jìjiāshì** 泰興季家市 (JiāngSū) and finally to **Tōngzhōu** 通州 (Nántōng). Confucian *zhūshēng* 諸生 (licentiate); accomplished poet (compared to Zhào Yì 趙翼) and painter in the *SìWáng* 四王 Qīng-orthodox tradition. Medical talent discovered only late in life, after a striking clinical-acumen episode at a Tōngzhōu lodging-house. Arrived in Tōngzhōu in *Gēngwǔ* 庚午 = 1930. Author of *Gǔsūn yīhuà* 谷蓀醫話 ([[KR3eq074]]) — preserved through the 1937–1939 Sino-Japanese war by his friend "*Chùān*" 觸庵 (evacuated to Shanghai). Son: 蓉生 Róngshēng. Not in CBDB.
