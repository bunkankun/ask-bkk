Mid-Míng physician of Hǎi-yán 海鹽, Zhè-jiāng. A documented member of the Hǎi-yán Hè family hereditary medical lineage, working in the post-Zhū Dān-xī Zhè-jiāng tradition. *Zì* Rǔ-zhān 汝瞻. His principal extant work is [[KR3er122]] *Yī-jīng dà-zhǐ* 醫經大旨 in four *juǎn* (*editio princeps* *Jiā-jìng* 35 / 1556), a Dān-xī-school nosology in *Nèi-jīng* exegetical guise. His other works (*Míng-yī huì-yào* 明醫會要, *Yào-xìng zhǔn-shéng* 藥性準繩, *Zhěn-mài jiā-bǎo* 診脈家寶) are mostly lost. Not in CBDB.
