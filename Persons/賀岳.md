Mid-Míng physician of Hǎiyán 海鹽, Zhèjiāng. A documented member of the Hǎiyán Hè family hereditary medical lineage, working in the post-Zhū Dānxī Zhèjiāng tradition. *Zì* Rǔzhān 汝瞻. His principal extant work is [[KR3er122]] *Yījīng dàzhǐ* 醫經大旨 in four *juǎn* (*editio princeps* *Jiājìng* 35 / 1556), a Dān-xī-school nosology in *Nèijīng* exegetical guise. His other works (*Míngyī huìyào* 明醫會要, *Yàoxìng zhǔnshéng* 藥性準繩, *Zhěnmài jiābǎo* 診脈家寶) are mostly lost. Not in CBDB.
