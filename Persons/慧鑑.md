# Huìjiàn 慧鑑 (*Huìjiàn lǜshī* 慧鑑律師)

Northern-Sòng Vinaya master, *Dōngcáng lǜshī* 東藏律師, who served as teacher of *Dàzhì lǜshī* 大智律師 [[元照]] Yuánzhào (1048–1116). The genealogical line is preserved in the closing prosopographical note of [[妙生]] Miàoshēng's *Sānyī xiǎnzhèng tú* 三衣顯正圖 ([[KR6k0254]], X1103): "*the Vinaya-master Dōngcáng 東藏律師 was the teacher of the Vinaya-master Dàzhì 大智律師 — namely Huìjiàn lǜshī 慧鑑律師*". No further biographical material preserved.
