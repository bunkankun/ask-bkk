Matsumoto Bunshiki 松本文識 (Sōngběn Wénshí), Japanese Meiji-era Buddhist editor and bibliophile, recorded as the compiler of the brief antiquarian note *Yuánjué jīng yìwén* 圓覺經佚文 ([[KR6i0554]]) prefixed to *Wànzì xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏經 vol. 1 (X01n0001) in 明治辛亥 (December 1911). His broader biography is not preserved in standard Japanese Buddhological reference works; he is best identified as one of the Kyōto-area canon-editing collaborators who supplied the *Xùzàng* with rare and supplementary materials. Lifedates unknown.
