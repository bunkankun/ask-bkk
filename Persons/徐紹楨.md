**Xú Shàozhēn** 徐紹楨 (*zì* **Gùqīng** 固卿, 1861–1936), late-Qīng *jǔrén* of **Pānyú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu, Guǎngdōng) and prominent military-administrative reformer of the late-Qīng *xīnzhèng* 新政 era. Served as Jiāngběi 江北 *tídū* (Provincial Military Commander), Jiāngnán *quánshěng liànbīng dìjiǔzhèn tǒngzhì* (Commander of the New Army Ninth Division and director of military training for the entire Jiāngnán province), and Sū-Sōngzhèn 蘇松鎮 *zǒngbīng* in the final years of the Qīng dynasty. Led the Nánjīng-region Republican forces during the 1911 Xīnhài revolution; subsequently served in various senior Republican-era posts including Guǎngdōng provincial governor (1925). Author of the 1909 preface to **[[KR3eq019]]** *Yī yī yī* 醫醫醫 — a key witness to the late-Qīng reform circle's engagement with medical-institutional questions in the months immediately preceding the dynasty's collapse. Not in CBDB.
