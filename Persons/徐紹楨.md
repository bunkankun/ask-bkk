**Xú Shào-zhēn** 徐紹楨 (*zì* **Gù-qīng** 固卿, 1861–1936), late-Qīng *jǔ-rén* of **Pān-yú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu, Guǎngdōng) and prominent military-administrative reformer of the late-Qīng *xīn-zhèng* 新政 era. Served as Jiāng-běi 江北 *tí-dū* (Provincial Military Commander), Jiāng-nán *quán-shěng liàn-bīng dì-jiǔ-zhèn tǒng-zhì* (Commander of the New Army Ninth Division and director of military training for the entire Jiāng-nán province), and Sū-Sōng-zhèn 蘇松鎮 *zǒng-bīng* in the final years of the Qīng dynasty. Led the Nánjīng-region Republican forces during the 1911 Xīn-hài revolution; subsequently served in various senior Republican-era posts including Guǎngdōng provincial governor (1925). Author of the 1909 preface to **[[KR3eq019]]** *Yī yī yī* 醫醫醫 — a key witness to the late-Qīng reform circle's engagement with medical-institutional questions in the months immediately preceding the dynasty's collapse. Not in CBDB.
