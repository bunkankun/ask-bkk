Sòng-period Tiāntái monk; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Provided the *xù-jiě* 續解 ("continuing-interpretation" / supplementary commentary) to **Shén-zhào Běn-rú**'s *Pǔ-xián guān jīng yì-shū* ([[KR6d0123]], X35n0650). His editorial role suggests he was a member of the Sì-míng Tiāntái lineage in the second-or-third generation post-Zhīlǐ, but no further biographical evidence is preserved.

Source: *Pǔ-xián guān jīng yì-shū* (X35n0650).
