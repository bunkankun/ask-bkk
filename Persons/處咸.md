Sòng-period Tiāntái monk; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Provided the *xùjiě* 續解 ("continuing-interpretation" / supplementary commentary) to **Shénzhào Běnrú**'s *Pǔxián guān jīng yìshū* ([[KR6d0123]], X35n0650). His editorial role suggests he was a member of the Sìmíng Tiāntái lineage in the second-or-third generation post-Zhīlǐ, but no further biographical evidence is preserved.

Source: *Pǔxián guān jīng yìshū* (X35n0650).
