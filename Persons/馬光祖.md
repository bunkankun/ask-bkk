Mǎ Guāngzǔ 馬光祖 (fl. 1196 *suǒ-jǔ* / floruit through ca. 1270), *zì* Huáfǔ 華父, *hào* Yùzhāi 裕齋, native of Jīnhuá 金華 (modern Zhèjiāng); enfeoffed *Jīnhuá-jùn kāiguó-gōng* 金華郡開國公 with food allowances of 3,000 households. *Jìnshì* of Bǎoqìng 2 (1226). His career, as recovered from CBDB, includes: 1258 in office as Húguǎng *zǒnglǐng* 湖廣總領; 1261 appointed *Tóngzhī Shūmìyuàn shì* 同知樞密院事 (Vice State Councillor for Military Affairs); 1263 from that office moved to a temple sinecure; 1267 appointed *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 (Vice Grand Councillor); 1269 concurrently *Zhī Shūmìyuàn shì* 知樞密院事, and in the same year retired to a temple sinecure. Across his career he served four times as Jiāngdōng *ānfǔshǐ* and prefect of Jiànkāng (Bǎoyòu 5 / 1257; revisits in Bǎoyòu 6 = 1258 and Jǐngdìng 1 = 1260, then again as *Yán-jiāng zhìzhì dàshǐ* in 1261); the second tenure produced both his vigorous defense of the prefecture against Mongol pressure and his commission of Zhōu Yìnghé's *Jǐngdìng Jiànkāng zhì* ([[KR2k0021]]). The work's *yuán-xù* and *jìn-biǎo* are by his hand. CBDB: 20699.
