**Wú Shòu** 吳綬, mid-Míng physician; author of the *Shānghán Cuìshū* 傷寒粹書 (printed 1505 by imperial-tutor 韓國公 sponsorship), a focused *Shānghán* compendium. Cited by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng in [[KR3eh052]] on the Kāiyuán-coin standard for *dùliànghéng* 度量衡 conversion of Hàn-Táng dose-equivalents.
