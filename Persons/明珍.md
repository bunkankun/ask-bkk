Míngzhēn 明珍. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsūn* (dharma-grandson) of **Mìxíng Jìrěn** [[寂忍]] (?–?). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with co-disciple [[明廣]] of his dharma-grandfather's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0545]] 《密行忍禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B428, prefaced 1694.
