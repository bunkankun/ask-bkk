Zhào Shēng 趙升 (*fl.* early-mid thirteenth century), *zì* Xiàngchén 向辰, self-styled "*Wénchāng* Zhào Shēng" 文昌趙升. A private Southern Sòng compiler whose only known work is the *Cháoyě lèi yào* 朝野類要 ([[KR3j0054]]), a five-juan dictionary of contemporary court and administrative terminology completed at the *Shuāngguì shūyuàn* 雙桂書院 during the Chóngyáng festival of *Duānpíng* 3 (1236) under Lǐzōng. The *Sìkù* editors note that his official career, native place, and lifedates are all undeterminable from the available evidence; "*Wénchāng*" is variously read as an antiquarian *hào* or as referring to *Tōngzhōu* 通州 (whose old name was Wénchāng).
