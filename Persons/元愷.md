# Yuán-kǎi 元愷

Southern-Sòng Chán disciple (*mén rén* 門人) of Dà-chuān Pǔ-jì 大川普濟 (1179–1253). Compiler of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0303]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
