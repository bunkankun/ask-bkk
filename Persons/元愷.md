# Yuánkǎi 元愷

Southern-Sòng Chán disciple (*mén rén* 門人) of Dàchuān Pǔjì 大川普濟 (1179–1253). Compiler of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0303]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
