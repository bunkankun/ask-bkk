# Chéng Jǔ 成矩

Mid-Míng *Yì* editor, otherwise undocumented. CBDB has only the bare entry (id 403120, dynasty Míng = 19) without lifedates, native place, or office record.

His one editorial work attested in the bibliographic record:

- **[[KR1a0032|*Bié běn Zhōuyì běnyì*]]** 別本周易本義 in four juan — the Míng-period editorial recension of Zhū Xī's *Zhōuyì běnyì*, fixing the post-Yǒnglè *dà quán*-mediated condensed form (4 juan) for circulation. The corresponding *Yuán běn Zhōuyì běnyì* in twelve juan is registered in parallel as [[KR1a0031]]; both Sìkù registrations of Zhū Xī's *Běnyì* belong together, with Chéng Jǔ responsible for the redactional layer of the post-Yǒnglè recension.

The *Sìkù tiyao* registers his work without further biographical comment. The post-Yǒnglè condensed *Bié běn* form was the recension actually used in late-Míng / Qīng *Yì*-pedagogy and the imperial-examination tradition; Chéng Jǔ's editorial fixing is therefore historically consequential despite his obscurity.
