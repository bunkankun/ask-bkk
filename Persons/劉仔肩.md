Early-Hóng-wǔ literatus of **Póyáng** 鄱陽 (Jiāngxī). *Zì* **Rǔbì** 汝弼. Summoned to the early-Míng capital (Nánjīng) by recommendation in early Hóngwǔ (likely Hóngwǔ 2–3 / 1369–1370); compiled the **[[KR4h0094]]** *Yǎsòng zhèngyīn* 雅頌正音 (5 *juǎn*), an anthology of contemporary poetry by 50-plus authors with his own compositions appended, prefaced by **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 in **Hóngwǔ 3 (1370)**. No separate biography in standard sources; not in the Míngshǐ *Wényuàn zhuàn*. No other works under his name are transmitted.
