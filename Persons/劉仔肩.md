Early-Hóng-wǔ literatus of **Pó-yáng** 鄱陽 (Jiāng-xī). *Zì* **Rǔ-bì** 汝弼. Summoned to the early-Míng capital (Nán-jīng) by recommendation in early Hóng-wǔ (likely Hóng-wǔ 2–3 / 1369–1370); compiled the **[[KR4h0094]]** *Yǎ-sòng zhèng-yīn* 雅頌正音 (5 *juǎn*), an anthology of contemporary poetry by 50-plus authors with his own compositions appended, prefaced by **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 in **Hóng-wǔ 3 (1370)**. No separate biography in standard sources; not in the Míng-shǐ *Wén-yuàn zhuàn*. No other works under his name are transmitted.
