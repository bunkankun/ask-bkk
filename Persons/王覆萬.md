# 王覆萬

Wáng Fùwàn, otherwise unattested itinerant Jiāngnán physician of the late-Míng / early-Qīng said in the preface narrative of [[KR3em004]] *Yǎnkē mìjué* 眼科秘訣 to have transmitted a manuscript of the work to [[馬雲從]] Mǎ Yúncóng. Probably a late-imperial-Daoist literary frame-figure rather than a verifiable historical person.
