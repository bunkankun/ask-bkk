**Zhāng Shòuyí** 張壽頤 (1873–1934), *zì* Shān-léi 山雷 (commonly used as the form of citation; sometimes also given as *zì* Shòufǔ 壽甫). Native of Jiādìng 嘉定 (modern Shànghǎi). Late-Qīng / early-Republican *jīngfāng* 經方-leaning physician and pharmacologist. He was one of the founding teachers of the modern Chinese-medicine school system: after the 1911 revolution he taught at the *Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānménxuéxiào* 蘭谿中醫專門學校 (Zhèjiāng), one of the first formal TCM colleges, where he trained a generation of Mínguó physicians. His major works include the *Běncǎo zhèngyì* 本草正義 ([[KR3ec063]], printed 1932), the *Zhōngfēng kǎo* 中風斠詮, and *Shānléi yīxué cóngshū* 山雷醫學叢書. The *Běncǎo zhèngyì* is the principal Republican-era critical pharmacopoeia and the immediate predecessor of the modern PRC TCM materia medica curriculum.
