Hè Shìzī 賀士諮 (dates unrecorded; fl. early 16th c.), son of **Hè Qīn** ([[賀欽]]) of Yìzhōuwèi (Liáodōng). Editor of his father's collected words and writings, the *Yīlǘ jí* in 9 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0129]]) — the first three *juǎn* being his record of his father's words-and-deeds (言行錄), the rest being his father's surviving prose, memorials, and poetry.
