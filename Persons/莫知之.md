**Mò Zhī-zhī** 莫知之 (*hào* **Yún-yán** 雲岩, *fl.* mid-Tóng-zhì era, ca. 1870s), Qīng literatus, preface-writer for the *Yīfāng shíjǐn* 醫方拾錦 ([[KR3ed132]]) of [[田綿淮]] (Tián Mián-huái), dated *Tóng-zhì guǐ-yǒu* winter-solstice = 1873. The preface displays unusual literary sophistication in unpacking the title's *shí-jǐn* (picked-brocades) trope and in positioning the work alongside Chén Jiǎ-mó's *Běncǎo méng-quán shí-yí* and Féng Zhào-zhāng's *Féng-shì jǐn-náng mì-lù*. The *hào* Yún-yán identifies him with the *Yún-yán* studio-circle but no further biographical record is securely identifiable.
