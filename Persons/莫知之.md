**Mò Zhīzhī** 莫知之 (*hào* **Yúnyán** 雲岩, *fl.* mid-Tóng-zhì era, ca. 1870s), Qīng literatus, preface-writer for the *Yīfāng shíjǐn* 醫方拾錦 ([[KR3ed132]]) of [[田綿淮]] (Tián Miánhuái), dated *Tóngzhì guǐyǒu* winter-solstice = 1873. The preface displays unusual literary sophistication in unpacking the title's *shíjǐn* (picked-brocades) trope and in positioning the work alongside Chén Jiǎmó's *Běncǎo méngquán shíyí* and Féng Zhàozhāng's *Féngshì jǐnnáng mìlù*. The *hào* Yúnyán identifies him with the *Yúnyán* studio-circle but no further biographical record is securely identifiable.
