**Zhāng Shòurén** 張壽仁, Southern-Sòng physician of Xīnān 新安, father of [[張杲]] Zhāng Gǎo (author of [[KR3eq024]] *Yī shuō*) and second of the three documented generations of the Xīnān Zhāng medical lineage.
