# 譚嗣同 Tán Sìtóng

**Tán Sìtóng** 譚嗣同 (1865–1898, of Liúyáng 瀏陽, Húnán), late-Qīng radical reformer; executed as one of the "Six Gentlemen" (戊戌六君子) of the failed 1898 Hundred Days' Reform. Mentioned in [[熊希齡]] Xióng Xīlíng's 1927 colophon to [[譚志光]] Tán Zhìguāng's *Zhēnjiǔ wèndá* 針灸問答 ([[KR3ee035]]) as part of the 1890s Húnán Nánxuéhuì 南學會 reformist circle.
