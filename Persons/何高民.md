Hé Gāomín 何高民 is a contemporary editor of the standard critical edition of the *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē jiàoshì* 傅青主女科校釋 (Tàiyuán: Shānxī rénmín, 1979), with detailed treatment of the *Fùshì nánkē* ([[KR3eh034]]) attribution problem.
