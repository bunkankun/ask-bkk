**Xuě Qiáo Zhǔrén** 雪樵主人 (Master of the Snowy Woodcutter) is the pseudonym of the otherwise unidentified author of two Qīng-dynasty historical romances centered on the Wang Zhaojun legend: *Shuāng Fèng Qí Yuán* 雙鳳奇緣 [[KR4k0226]] (80 chapters) and *Shuāng Fēng Qí Yuán* 雙風奇緣 [[KR4k0227]] (fragment, *juàn* 1 only). No biographical information has been located. CBDB contains no entry for this pen name.
