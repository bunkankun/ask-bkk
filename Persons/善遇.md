# Shàn-yù 善遇

Yuán-dynasty Chán *xiǎo-shī* 小師 of [[惟則]] Tiān-rú Wéi-zé (1286–1354); sole named compiler of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0336]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
