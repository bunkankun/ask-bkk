# Shànyù 善遇

Yuán-dynasty Chán *xiǎoshī* 小師 of [[惟則]] Tiānrú Wéizé (1286–1354); sole named compiler of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0336]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
