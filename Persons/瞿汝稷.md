# Qú Rǔjī 瞿汝稷

*Zì* Yuánlì 元立, *hào* Dòngguān 洞觀. Late-Míng official and lay Chán practitioner. Native of Chángshú 常熟 (Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of Wànlì 5 (1577); served as Vice Director of the Ministry of Works (工部主事) and in other senior offices. Lifedates 1548–1610.

Compiler of the *Zhǐyuè lù* 指月錄 ([[KR6q0023]]), 32 juan, completed by 1602 — a major late-Míng Chán teaching anthology widely transmitted through the late-imperial literati world. Also a noted scholar of the *Yìjīng* and a poet; literary collection *Yǒngzhèng jí* 永正集.
