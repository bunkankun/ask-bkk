**Tiěfēng jūshì** 鐵峰居士 ("Lay Practitioner of Iron-Peak"), pseudonymous *yǎngshēng* compiler of the early Míng, of Gǔ Nánshā 古南沙 (probably Shāzhōu 沙洲 = modern Chángshú in Jiāngsū). Active in the *Hóngzhì* to *Zhèngdé* transition: in autumn of *Hóngzhì yǐchǒu* = 1505 he encountered the *Shèngxián bǎoxiū tōngjiàn* 聖賢保修通鑑 (a work since lost), of which he secretly transcribed an outline; after personal experimentation he expanded and corrected the material, producing the *Bǎoshēng xīnjiàn* 保生心鑑 ([[KR3eo041]]) with 32 illustrated *dǎoyǐn* figures. His preface is dated *Zhèngdé bǐngyín* (1506). True identity unrecovered. No CBDB record.
