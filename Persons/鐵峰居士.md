**Tiěfēng jūshì** 鐵峰居士 ("Lay Practitioner of Iron-Peak"), pseudonymous *yǎng-shēng* compiler of the early Míng, of Gǔ Nán-shā 古南沙 (probably Shā-zhōu 沙洲 = modern Cháng-shú in Jiāng-sū). Active in the *Hóng-zhì* to *Zhèng-dé* transition: in autumn of *Hóng-zhì yǐ-chǒu* = 1505 he encountered the *Shèng-xián bǎo-xiū tōng-jiàn* 聖賢保修通鑑 (a work since lost), of which he secretly transcribed an outline; after personal experimentation he expanded and corrected the material, producing the *Bǎo-shēng xīn-jiàn* 保生心鑑 ([[KR3eo041]]) with 32 illustrated *dǎo-yǐn* figures. His preface is dated *Zhèng-dé bǐng-yín* (1506). True identity unrecovered. No CBDB record.
