**Liǔ Shǒuyuán** 柳守元 (fl. early-to-mid 18th century, Qīng), Daoist spirit-writing master and prolific compiler within the **Quánzhēn / Lóngmén** 全眞龍門 lineage in the Yōngzhèng / Qiánlóng era. He is the primary editorial voice behind a substantial number of *fújī* 扶乩-revealed scriptures and exegetical works gathered into the *Dào zàng jí yào* (1809) — many bearing his self-description "宏教弟子" ("disciple of broadened teaching") or his planchette-style title 玉樞右宰宏教眞君 ("Pacific-Pivot Right-Minister, Perfected of Broadened Teaching"). Works in the Kanripo corpus carrying his preface, postface, or compilation include [[KR5i0005]] *Tài shàng xuán yuán dào dé jīng jiě* (commentary by the "Eight-Cavern Patriarchs" edited by Liǔ); the various *jiè* texts in [[KR5i0095]]–0099 (the *Liǔshì sān tán dà jiè* recension); and several other DZJY revelation scriptures. Liǔ's oeuvre is the chief vehicle through which the early-Qīng Lóngmén planchette milieu transmitted scriptural production into the print canon. No CBDB record exists; Liǔ was a Daoist priest, not an examination-degree holder, and his lifedates are inferred from internal datings on his prefaces (e.g. those signed 雍正 / Yōngzhèng = 1723–1735).
