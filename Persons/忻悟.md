# Xīnwù 忻悟

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yúān Zhìjí (1311–1378); *hào* Kōngsǒu 空叟, abbot of Hángzhōu Língyǐn Jǐngdé chánsì 靈隱景德禪寺. Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
