# Mǎzǔ Dàoyī 馬祖道一

Mǎzǔ Dàoyī 馬祖道一 (709–788), Táng Chán master, central founder of the Hóngzhōu school 洪州宗 and dharma-grandfather of the line leading through [[懷海]] Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi and [[希運]] Huángbò Xīyùn to [[義玄]] Línjì Yìxuán; lineally the dharma-heir of Nányuè Huáiràng 南嶽懷讓 and through him of [[惠能]] Huìnéng.
