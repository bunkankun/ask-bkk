# 陳藏器 Chén Cángqì

Táng-dynasty pharmacologist (ca. 687–757), author of *Běncǎo shíyí* 本草拾遺 (739), one of the principal Táng supplements to the canonical *Běncǎo*. Cited as a foundational pharmacological source in [[李彣]] Lǐ Wén's *Jīnguì yàoluè guǎngzhù* ([[KR3ef087]]).
