Chén Chún 陳淳 (1159–1223), *zì* Ānqīng 安卿, *hào* Běixī xiānsheng 北溪先生, was one of the most important first-generation post-Zhū-Xī disciples and the author of the canonical Sòng *Lǐxué* terminological lexicon. Native of Lóng-xī 龍溪 (Zhāng-zhōu 漳州, Fú-jiàn). Studied directly with Zhū Xī in his late years (1190 and 1199 visits to Zhū's residence). Awarded *Dí-gōng láng* 廸功郎 in Jiā-dìng 10 (1217) with assignment as *Quán-zhōu Ān-xī Zhǔ-bù* 泉州安溪主簿; died before taking up the post. Major works: the *Běixī zì yì* 北溪字義 (KR3a0065 — the standard *Lǐxué* terminological lexicon, recorded by his disciple Wáng Jùn 王雋); the *Běixī Dà-quán jí* 北溪大全集 (the collected works); the *Yán-líng jiǎng-yì* 嚴陵講義 (his Yán-líng / Jiàn-dé lectures of 1216, appended to the *Zì yì*). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 430 (Dào-xué zhuàn). CBDB id 10882, dates 1159–1223.
