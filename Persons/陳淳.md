Chén Chún 陳淳 (1159–1223), *zì* Ānqīng 安卿, *hào* Běixī xiānsheng 北溪先生, was one of the most important first-generation post-Zhū-Xī disciples and the author of the canonical Sòng *Lǐxué* terminological lexicon. Native of Lóngxī 龍溪 (Zhāngzhōu 漳州, Fújiàn). Studied directly with Zhū Xī in his late years (1190 and 1199 visits to Zhū's residence). Awarded *Dígōng láng* 廸功郎 in Jiādìng 10 (1217) with assignment as *Quánzhōu Ānxī Zhǔbù* 泉州安溪主簿; died before taking up the post. Major works: the *Běixī zì yì* 北溪字義 ([[KR3a0065]] — the standard *Lǐxué* terminological lexicon, recorded by his disciple Wáng Jùn 王雋); the *Běixī Dàquán jí* 北溪大全集 (the collected works); the *Yánlíng jiǎngyì* 嚴陵講義 (his Yánlíng / Jiàndé lectures of 1216, appended to the *Zì yì*). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 430 (Dàoxué zhuàn). CBDB id 10882, dates 1159–1223.
