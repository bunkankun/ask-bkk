Zuòhuā Sǎnrén 坐花散人 ("the Scattered Person Sitting among Flowers") is the pen name of the unidentified author of *Fēngliú Wù* 風流悟 ([[KR4k0113]]), a short Qīng vernacular fiction in 8 chapters. No biographical information is recoverable from the text. The pseudonym is not otherwise attested in standard literary or bibliographic sources.
