**Yán Chún-xǐ** 閻純璽 (*zì* Wén-hóu 文侯, *hào* Chéng-zhāi 誠齋), early-Qīng physician-official of Shàng-gǔ 上谷 (modern Hé-běi). Held the post of **Zuǒ-jiāng guān-chá-shǐ** 左江觀察使 (Circuit Intendant of the Left River) of Guǎng-xī, where he composed and printed the *Tāi-chǎn xīn-fǎ* 胎產心法 ([[KR3ei058]]) in 1730 (Yōng-zhèng *gēng-xū*, prefaced *jì-jú yuè zhī zhōng-huàn*) at his official residence. The author's prefaces — the 1725 preface by Gōng Jiàn-yáng 龔健揚 (1725: *Yōng-zhèng 3*) and his own 1730 self-preface — record that Yán spent over thirty years gathering and digesting the prior obstetrical literature. Distinct from his namesake **閻純璽** (Buddhist) and from the Sòng-Yuán *Chéng-zhāi yǎ-tán* family of scholar-officials. Not in CBDB.
