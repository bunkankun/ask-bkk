**Yán Chúnxǐ** 閻純璽 (*zì* Wénhóu 文侯, *hào* Chéngzhāi 誠齋), early-Qīng physician-official of Shànggǔ 上谷 (modern Héběi). Held the post of **Zuǒjiāng guāncháshǐ** 左江觀察使 (Circuit Intendant of the Left River) of Guǎngxī, where he composed and printed the *Tāichǎn xīnfǎ* 胎產心法 ([[KR3ei058]]) in 1730 (Yōngzhèng *gēngxū*, prefaced *jìjú yuè zhī zhōnghuàn*) at his official residence. The author's prefaces — the 1725 preface by Gōng Jiànyáng 龔健揚 (1725: *Yōngzhèng 3*) and his own 1730 self-preface — record that Yán spent over thirty years gathering and digesting the prior obstetrical literature. Distinct from his namesake **閻純璽** (Buddhist) and from the SòngYuán *Chéngzhāi yǎtán* family of scholar-officials. Not in CBDB.
