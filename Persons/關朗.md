Guān Lǎng 關朗, *zì* Zǐmíng 子明, a Northern-Wèi-period *Yì*-scholar to whom an *Yì zhuàn* 易傳 has traditionally been attributed. The Sòng tradition treated the *Guān Lǎng Yì zhuàn* as a witness for the *Hé túLuò shū* 9/10 question in support of [[蔡元定]] Cài Yuándìng's position, but Zhū Xī condemned the work as a forgery and [[項安世]] Xiàng Ānshì identified [[阮逸]] Ruǎn Yì (active in the *Huángyòu* era, 1049–1054) as its actual author. Modern scholarship generally accepts the forgery verdict.
