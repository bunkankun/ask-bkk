# Tokugawa Tsunayoshi 德川綱吉 (1646–1709)

The fifth Tokugawa shōgun, ruling 1680–1709. Patron of [[杉山和一]] Sugiyama Wa'ichi: appointed Sugiyama *kengyō* 検校 (the highest official rank for blind people in Edo Japan) in 1682, and in 1693 endowed him with a residence at Honjo Ichinohashi 本所一橋 in Edo and chartered the *Sugiyama-ryū hari-jutsu kōshūjo* 杉山流鍼術講習所, the school of acupuncture for the blind. The patronage underlies the existence and circulation of [[KR3ee052]] *Xuǎnzhēn sānyào jí* 選針三要集.
