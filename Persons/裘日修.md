Qiú Rìxiū 裘日修 (1712–1773), zì Shūdù 叔度, hào Mànshì 漫士, posthumous name Wéndá 文達, of Xīn-jiàn 新建 (Jiāngxī), was a Qiánlóng-era senior official. *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 4 (1739), he rose through Hànlín posts to *gōngbù shàngshū* 工部尚書 (Minister of Works) and senior cabinet membership, with periods on the *Wǔyīngdiàn* 武英殿 imperial editorial supervisorship. He served on the *Qīndìng chóngkè Chúnhuàgé tiè shìwén* 欽定重刻淳化閣帖釋文 [[KR2n0035]] (1769) editorial team alongside Yú Mǐnzhōng and Wáng Jìhuá, all by imperial command. He was prominent in Qing river engineering: his memorials on Yellow-River and Huái works are preserved in his collected *Qiú Wéndá-gōng wénjí* 裘文達公文集. *Qīngshǐ gǎo* j. 327 has his biography.
