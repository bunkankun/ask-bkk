Late-Míng imperial clansman and prolific compiler; descendant of Zhū Quán 朱權 (the Yǒnglè-era *Níngwáng*), of the Níngfán 寧藩 branch based at Nánchāng 南昌. Active in the *Wànlì* through *Chóngzhēn* decades. Author of numerous *lèishū*-style reference works and historiographical studies, including [[KR3h0053]] *Huàshǐ huìyào* 畫史會要 in 5 *juàn* (completed *Chóngzhēn xīnwèi* = 1631), the principal Míng biographical reference for the history of painting; *Shīgǔxué* 詩故學; *Língtán* 駢雅; and several other works in classical philology and historiography.
