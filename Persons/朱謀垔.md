Late-Míng imperial clansman and prolific compiler; descendant of Zhū Quán 朱權 (the Yǒnglè-era *Níng-wáng*), of the Níngfán 寧藩 branch based at Nánchāng 南昌. Active in the *Wànlì* through *Chóngzhēn* decades. Author of numerous *lèi-shū*-style reference works and historiographical studies, including [[KR3h0053]] *Huà-shǐ huì-yào* 畫史會要 in 5 *juàn* (completed *Chóngzhēn xīn-wèi* = 1631), the principal Míng biographical reference for the history of painting; *Shī-gǔ-xué* 詩故學; *Líng-tán* 駢雅; and several other works in classical philology and historiography.
