# 張居正

Zhāng Jūzhèng (1525–1582)

Late-Míng grand secretary, the most powerful chief minister of the Wànlì era, and author of the *Sìshū zhíjiě* 四書直解, the dominant Míng-Qīng vernacular-leaning *Sìshū* commentary. His four-step pedagogical format (*zhù* annotation, *lùn* discussion, *jiǎng* exposition, *zhèng* verification) is the explicit model for [[石成金]] Shí Chéngjīn's [[KR6c0085]] *Jīngāng jīng Shí zhù*.
