**Wáng Hèlǚ** 王鶴侶 ("Master of the Crane Companion"), Jiāqìng-era Shāndōng friend of [[臧達德]] Zāng Dádé; urged Zāng to commit the family medical methods to print, leading to the [[KR3eq030]] *Lǚshuāng jí* (1814).
