Shěn Dēng-jiē 沈登階 (*hào* Qīng-xiá 青霞) — mid-to-late Qīng physician of the Jiāng-nán region, associated with the Yáng-zhōu / Hàn-shàng 邗上 area. The internal-evidence dating of his [[KR3ep036]] *Qīng-xiá yī-àn* 青霞醫案 (a major *qiū-zào* 秋燥 autumn-dryness case dated 1857 / *dīng-chǒu* in the ninth month, treating the son of *guān-chá* Fāng Zǐ-yán at Fèng-yáng) anchors his floruit to the late-Xián-fēng / Tóng-zhì era (c. 1850s–1880s). His clinical work continues the post-[[喻昌]]-[[吳塘]] *qiū-zào* tradition. CBDB has no entry; biographical data are limited.
