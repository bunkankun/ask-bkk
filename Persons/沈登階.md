Shěn Dēngjiē 沈登階 (*hào* Qīngxiá 青霞) — mid-to-late Qīng physician of the Jiāngnán region, associated with the Yángzhōu / Hànshàng 邗上 area. The internal-evidence dating of his [[KR3ep036]] *Qīngxiá yīàn* 青霞醫案 (a major *qiūzào* 秋燥 autumn-dryness case dated 1857 / *dīngchǒu* in the ninth month, treating the son of *guānchá* Fāng Zǐyán at Fèngyáng) anchors his floruit to the late-Xián-fēng / Tóngzhì era (c. 1850s–1880s). His clinical work continues the post-[[喻昌]]-[[吳塘]] *qiūzào* tradition. CBDB has no entry; biographical data are limited.
