Wáng Shēnsǒu 王莘叟, disciple of [[呂祖謙]] Lǚ Zǔqiān. Compiled the 2-juan *Yīn xùn* 音訓 ("Phonetic Glosses") that accompanied Lǚ Zǔqiān's *Gǔ Zhōuyì* recension ([[KR1a0043]]) — taken down from the master's oral instruction rather than composed by Lǚ Zǔqiān himself. Recorded in [[陳振孫]] Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*; the supplement was lost in the Sìkù base-text and recovered only in modern reissues.
