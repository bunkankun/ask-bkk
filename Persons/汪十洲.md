Wāng Shízhōu 汪十洲 (fl. late 17th – early 18th c.) was the father of [[汪文綺]] Wāng Wénqǐ and a Wùyuán 婺源 (Xīn'ān) physician; his clinical legacy informed his son's *Zázhèng huìxīn lù* ([[KR3eh007]]).
