Huìjī 慧機 (1603-11-22 – 1668-10-26), *hào* **Tiěbì** 鐵壁 ("Iron-Wall"), was the chief Sìchuān Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jùyún Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639) and the principal intermediary of the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派 to its third generation. Native of **Yíngshān** 營山 (Shǔběi, Sìchuān); lay surname **Luó** 羅; family of scholar-gentry with multiple *jǔrén* / *jìnshì* ancestors. Born **Wànlì 31 癸卯 10/20 寅時** = **1603-11-22 Gregorian**, died **Kāngxī 7 戊申 9/21** = **1668-10-26**, *shìshòu* **66**, *sēnglà* **50**. Source: 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》 by Wànfēng Zhìshàn 萬峰至善, preserved in [[KR6q0451]] juan 20 (preface by Lǐ Dàojì 李道濟 dated **Kāngxī 甲寅 孟春 = 1674/1**).

Practiced an eight-year household vegetarian fast after his father's death at 8 *suì*; at age 19 *bǐguān* 閉關 three years at **Dàzhú** 大竹 in Sìchuān. Tonsured at **25** under a Western-Indian monk (**西竺僧**); full precepts at the precipice of **Zhōngnánshān** 終南山 where he met Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn. Decisive awakening at age **33** (1635) under Guǎngzhēn at **Jùyúnsì**. Continuous residence as Guǎngzhēn's chief *shǒuzhòng* 首眾 from then until Guǎngzhēn's death in **Chóngzhēn 12 = 1639/8/28**, when Huìjī succeeded to the Jùyún abbacy. Ten subsequent abbacies over a 32-year preaching-career: **Jùyún** 聚雲, **Fēngdū Dìzàngyuàn** 酆都地藏院, **Fúzhōu Yínwēng** 涪州吟翁, **Shízhù Sānjiào** 石砫三教, **Chángxī Yúnjí** 長溪雲集, **Wànxiàn Yúnlái** 萬縣雲來, **Liángshān Qìngzhōngsì** 梁山慶忠寺 (where the *hào* "*Qìngzhōng*" attaches), **Zhōngzhōu Yúnyányuàn** 忠州雲巖院, **Nánbīn Bǎoshèngsì** 南濵寶聖寺, and finally **Zhōngzhōu Lóngchāng / Zhìpíng Chányuàn** 龍昌 / 治平禪院 — the last being the Táng-era willow-planting site of Bái Jūyì 白居易, from which the closing Tiěbì *hào* **Zhìpíng** derives. Died at Zhìpíng, with half his relic-remains interred at [[燈來]] 三山燈來's **Gāofēng** 高峰 and half at Zhìpíng's **Báilù Cave** 白鹿洞 vicinity.

**Twenty-three named dharma-heirs** per the *Xùdēng zhèngtǒng* roster: **Sānshān Dēnglái** [[燈來]] (compiler of [[KR6q0448]], [[KR6q0449]], [[KR6q0450]], [[KR6q0451]]; author of [[KR6q0169]] and [[KR6q0454]]), **Chéngdū Shílóu Dēngyù** 成都石樓燈昱, **Dōngmíng Méishān Dēngfǔ** 東明眉山燈甫, **Qìngyún Héngshān Dēngbǐng** 慶雲衡山燈炳, **Tàipíng Sānkōng Dēnggǎo** 太平三空燈杲, **Gāofēng Qiáosōng Dēngyì** 高峰橋松燈億, **Tánzhōu Wànfēng Zhìshàn** 潭州萬峰至善, **Zhìpíng Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] 治平竺峰幻敏 (compiler of [[KR6q0451]]), and others — twenty-three named monks plus eighteen lay-disciples per the 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 juan 5 *liánfāngjì*. Principal works: [[KR6q0450]] 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 3 juan (J29 B240), [[KR6q0451]] 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 20 juan (J29 B241); and the non-Canon 《藥病隨宜》 2 juan, 《慶忠集》 2 juan.

Sources: 《續燈正統》 j. 16; 《續燈正統目錄》 j. 1; 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 j. 20 *xíngshí* and year-chronology; 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 j. 5–6. *Fóguāng dàcídiǎn* 6056.
