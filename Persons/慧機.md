Huì-jī 慧機 (1603-11-22 – 1668-10-26), *hào* **Tiě-bì** 鐵壁 ("Iron-Wall"), was the chief Sì-chuān Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639) and the principal intermediary of the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派 to its third generation. Native of **Yíng-shān** 營山 (Shǔ-běi, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Luó** 羅; family of scholar-gentry with multiple *jǔ-rén* / *jìn-shì* ancestors. Born **Wàn-lì 31 癸卯 10/20 寅時** = **1603-11-22 Gregorian**, died **Kāng-xī 7 戊申 9/21** = **1668-10-26**, *shì-shòu* **66**, *sēng-là* **50**. Source: 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》 by Wàn-fēng Zhì-shàn 萬峰至善, preserved in [[KR6q0451]] juan 20 (preface by Lǐ Dào-jì 李道濟 dated **Kāng-xī 甲寅 孟春 = 1674/1**).

Practiced an eight-year household vegetarian fast after his father's death at 8 *suì*; at age 19 *bǐ-guān* 閉關 three years at **Dà-zhú** 大竹 in Sì-chuān. Tonsured at **25** under a Western-Indian monk (**西竺僧**); full precepts at the precipice of **Zhōng-nán-shān** 終南山 where he met Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn. Decisive awakening at age **33** (1635) under Guǎng-zhēn at **Jù-yún-sì**. Continuous residence as Guǎng-zhēn's chief *shǒu-zhòng* 首眾 from then until Guǎng-zhēn's death in **Chóng-zhēn 12 = 1639/8/28**, when Huì-jī succeeded to the Jù-yún abbacy. Ten subsequent abbacies over a 32-year preaching-career: **Jù-yún** 聚雲, **Fēng-dū Dì-zàng-yuàn** 酆都地藏院, **Fú-zhōu Yín-wēng** 涪州吟翁, **Shí-zhù Sān-jiào** 石砫三教, **Cháng-xī Yún-jí** 長溪雲集, **Wàn-xiàn Yún-lái** 萬縣雲來, **Liáng-shān Qìng-zhōng-sì** 梁山慶忠寺 (where the *hào* "*Qìng-zhōng*" attaches), **Zhōng-zhōu Yún-yán-yuàn** 忠州雲巖院, **Nán-bīn Bǎo-shèng-sì** 南濵寶聖寺, and finally **Zhōng-zhōu Lóng-chāng / Zhì-píng Chán-yuàn** 龍昌 / 治平禪院 — the last being the Táng-era willow-planting site of Bái Jū-yì 白居易, from which the closing Tiě-bì *hào* **Zhì-píng** derives. Died at Zhì-píng, with half his relic-remains interred at [[燈來]] 三山燈來's **Gāo-fēng** 高峰 and half at Zhì-píng's **Bái-lù Cave** 白鹿洞 vicinity.

**Twenty-three named dharma-heirs** per the *Xù-dēng zhèng-tǒng* roster: **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]] (compiler of [[KR6q0448]], [[KR6q0449]], [[KR6q0450]], [[KR6q0451]]; author of [[KR6q0169]] and [[KR6q0454]]), **Chéng-dū Shí-lóu Dēng-yù** 成都石樓燈昱, **Dōng-míng Méi-shān Dēng-fǔ** 東明眉山燈甫, **Qìng-yún Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** 慶雲衡山燈炳, **Tài-píng Sān-kōng Dēng-gǎo** 太平三空燈杲, **Gāo-fēng Qiáo-sōng Dēng-yì** 高峰橋松燈億, **Tán-zhōu Wàn-fēng Zhì-shàn** 潭州萬峰至善, **Zhì-píng Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] 治平竺峰幻敏 (compiler of [[KR6q0451]]), and others — twenty-three named monks plus eighteen lay-disciples per the 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 juan 5 *lián-fāng-jì*. Principal works: [[KR6q0450]] 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 3 juan (J29 B240), [[KR6q0451]] 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 20 juan (J29 B241); and the non-Canon 《藥病隨宜》 2 juan, 《慶忠集》 2 juan.

Sources: 《續燈正統》 j. 16; 《續燈正統目錄》 j. 1; 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 j. 20 *xíng-shí* and year-chronology; 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 j. 5–6. *Fó-guāng dà-cí-diǎn* 6056.
