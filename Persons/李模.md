# Lǐ Mó 李模

Late-17th-century Sūzhōu layman (*hào* Mìān jūshì 密菴居士 "Secret-Hermitage Layman") of Wújùn 吳郡. Preface-writer for [[KR6q0224]] *Língruì ní Zǔkuí Fú chánshī Miàozhàn lù*, the *yǔlù* of the Chán nun [[符|Língruì Fúānzhǔ]]. Lifedates and further biographical details unrecorded.
