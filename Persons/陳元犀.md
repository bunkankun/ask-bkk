Chén Yuánxī 陳元犀 (*zì* Língshí 靈石, *fl.* early 19th c., 清) was the son and continuator of the eminent Chén Niànzǔ [[陳念祖]] (Xiūyuán 修園, 1753–1823), the Chánglè 長樂 physician who systematised the Qīng *shífāng / jīngfāng* pedagogical corpus. Chén Yuánxī continued his father's project of pedagogical commentary on the classical pharmaco-therapeutic corpus, producing the *Jīnguì yàolüè qiǎnzhù* 金匱要略淺注 (a parallel to his father's *Shānghán lùn qiǎnzhù*) and its companion *Jīnguì fāng gēkuò* 金匱方歌括 [[KR3ed086]] (rhymed mnemonic formulary for the *Jīnguì yàolüè* formulas). The latter work is attributed to Chén Niànzǔ in the catalog meta but a postface by Chén Yuánxī's disciple Yè Xiǎnghuì 葉享會 makes clear that the son completed it after his father's death.
