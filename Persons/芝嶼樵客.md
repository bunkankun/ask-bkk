**Zhīyǔ qiáokè** 芝嶼樵客 ("the Woodcutter of Zhīyǔ Islet") is the studio-name (*hào*) of the Qīng-period author of the paediatric treatise *Érkē xǐng* 兒科醒 ([[KR3ej008]]). The preface to the work, by his teacher **Huáyáng shānrén** 華陽山人, identifies the author only as the teacher's disciple (*yǔ dìzǐ Zhīyǔ* 予弟子芝嶼). The full personal name has not been securely identified in modern scholarship. Mid-to-late-Qīng dating is established by the work's vocabulary, its references to the *Dòuzhěn zhèngzōng* 痘疹正宗, and (in the editorial principles) to organised mass *zhòng dòu* 種痘 variolation programmes such as the Bóài táng 博愛堂.
