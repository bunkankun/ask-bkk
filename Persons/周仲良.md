# 周仲良 Zhōu Zhòngliáng

**Zhōu Zhòngliáng** 周仲良, Yuán-dynasty disciple of [[王國瑞]] Wáng Guóruì; wrote the 1329 postface to the *Biǎnquè shényìng zhēnjiǔ Yùlóng jīng* 扁鵲神應針灸玉龍經 ([[KR3ee008]]), recording forty years of clinical acupuncture practice between the Mǐn (Fújiàn) and Zhè (Zhèjiāng) regions.
