# Zhào Gōngyù 趙公豫 (1135–1212)

*Zì* Zhòngqiān 仲謙 (so the *Sìkù* tíyào; the *běnzhuàn* preserved at the front of his collection [[KR4d0221]] gives Zhòngmóu 仲謀 — both forms current). Native of Chángshú 常熟 (modern Jiāngsū), but originally a member of the Sòng imperial-clan (*zōngshì*) who, after the 1127 southern crossing, moved south to Chángshú; the precise lineage is no longer determinable, since the *Sòngshǐ* imperial-clan genealogy does not record the *Gōng* generation-character among the standard lines. Lifedates 1135–1212 per CBDB id 14746 and the catalog meta. (The *běnzhuàn* preserved with the collection states he died at age 64, which would imply 1135–1198 and an inner inconsistency in the bibliographic record; the catalog's 1212 figure is retained.)

*Jìnshì* in the *Shàoxīng* era. Career: prefect of *Zhēnzhōu* 真州 (modern Yízhēng, Jiāngsū); rose by stages to *Bǎomógé dàizhì* and retired. His administrative reputation rested on his *liánglì* (good administrator) maxim, and the popular farewell of *juéjiāng pānyuán* (carrying wine-flagons and clinging to the carriage-shafts) on his leaving office. Mǎ Hézhī's epitaph for him is "Qīngxiǎn" (Pure-and-Manifest, 清顯).

His youthful associations included Pútián's Chén Yuánjǔ, Jīnhuá's Chén Tiānyǔ, Huátíng's Xǔ Shàngdá, Lánqī's Fàn Jùn [[范浚]], and Xīnhuì's Mǎ Gěngchén — a substantial Southern-Sòng literary network of mid-rank scholars.

Surviving in Kanripo:
- [[KR4d0221]] *Yàntáng shīgǎo* (1 *juǎn*, WYG; the surviving fragment of an originally 16-*juǎn* *Yàntáng lèigǎo*, the prose having been lost when the prefect Jiǎng Yōng reduced the original collection to a poetry-only abridgement).
