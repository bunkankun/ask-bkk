# Zhào Gōngyù 趙公豫 (1135–1212)

*Zì* Zhòng-qiān 仲謙 (so the *Sì-kù* tíyào; the *běn-zhuàn* preserved at the front of his collection [[KR4d0221]] gives Zhòng-móu 仲謀 — both forms current). Native of Cháng-shú 常熟 (modern Jiāng-sū), but originally a member of the Sòng imperial-clan (*zōng-shì*) who, after the 1127 southern crossing, moved south to Cháng-shú; the precise lineage is no longer determinable, since the *Sòng-shǐ* imperial-clan genealogy does not record the *Gōng* generation-character among the standard lines. Lifedates 1135–1212 per CBDB id 14746 and the catalog meta. (The *běn-zhuàn* preserved with the collection states he died at age 64, which would imply 1135–1198 and an inner inconsistency in the bibliographic record; the catalog's 1212 figure is retained.)

*Jìn-shì* in the *Shào-xīng* era. Career: prefect of *Zhēn-zhōu* 真州 (modern Yí-zhēng, Jiāng-sū); rose by stages to *Bǎo-mó-gé dài-zhì* and retired. His administrative reputation rested on his *liáng-lì* (good administrator) maxim, and the popular farewell of *jué-jiāng pān-yuán* (carrying wine-flagons and clinging to the carriage-shafts) on his leaving office. Mǎ Hé-zhī's epitaph for him is "Qīng-xiǎn" (Pure-and-Manifest, 清顯).

His youthful associations included Pú-tián's Chén Yuán-jǔ, Jīn-huá's Chén Tiān-yǔ, Huá-tíng's Xǔ Shàng-dá, Lán-qī's Fàn Jùn [[范浚]], and Xīn-huì's Mǎ Gěng-chén — a substantial Southern-Sòng literary network of mid-rank scholars.

Surviving in Kanripo:
- [[KR4d0221]] *Yàn-táng shī-gǎo* (1 *juǎn*, WYG; the surviving fragment of an originally 16-*juǎn* *Yàn-táng lèi-gǎo*, the prose having been lost when the prefect Jiǎng Yōng reduced the original collection to a poetry-only abridgement).
