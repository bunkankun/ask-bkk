**Zhāng Zhìcōng** 張志聰 (1610–1674), *zì* Yǐn'ān 隱菴 (hence often 張隱菴), was a Qiántáng 錢塘 physician of the late-Míng / early-Qīng transition and the founder of the **Lǚshān jiǎngtáng** 侶山講堂, the West-Lake medical academy in which a circle of some forty pupils collectively re-glossed the *Huángdì nèijīng* and the Zhāng Zhòngjǐng corpus during the 1660s. The "錢塘醫派" or 侶山 school that emerged from Zhāng's teaching dominated late-Qīng *Nèijīng* exegesis. His major surviving works are:

- *Huángdì nèijīng Sùwèn jí zhù* 黃帝內經素問集註, 9 juan, 1670 ([[KR3ea008]]).
- *Huángdì nèijīng Língshū jí zhù* 黃帝內經靈樞集註, 9 juan, 1672 ([[KR3ea025]]).
- *Shānghán lùn zōng yìn* 傷寒論宗印 (1663) and *Shānghán lùn jí zhù* 傷寒論集註, completed by his pupil **Gāo Shìshì** [[高士宗]] after Zhāng's death.
- *Jīnguì yàoluè jí zhù* 金匱要略集註.
- *Běn cǎo chóng yuán* 本草崇原 (3 juan, on materia medica, completed posthumously by Gāo Shìshì).

Zhāng's distinctive doctrinal contribution is a systematic-cosmological reading of the *Nèijīng* under the rubric of *qì-huà* 氣化, integrating the *wǔyùn liùqì* 五運六氣 doctrine across the whole eighty-one *pian* of the *Sùwèn*. The original Lǚshān jiǎngtáng print-blocks were destroyed in the Tàipíng wars (1860s) and the text survives via late-Qīng restorations from a handful of Hángzhōu private libraries. The 1610 birth-date follows Pān Qiūchén's 2009 reconstruction; older biographies give c. 1644.
