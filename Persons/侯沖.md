Modern Chinese scholar of regional Buddhism, professor at Shanghai Normal University (上海師範大學), specialist in the Yúnnán *Āzhālì* 阿吒力 ritual lineage and in Chinese Buddhist liturgy more broadly. Author of *Zhōngguó fójiào yí-shì yán-jiū* 中國佛教儀式研究 (Shanghai: Shànghǎi gǔjí, 2011) and *Yúnnán Āzhālì jiào-pài jí qí jīngdiǎn yán-jiū* 雲南阿吒力教派及其經典研究. Co-edited (with [[趙文煥]]) the Yúnnán Āzhālì ritual manuals in *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vols 6–8: [[KR6v0063]], [[KR6v0064]], [[KR6v0065]], [[KR6v0088]]. Sole editor of [[KR6v0066]] *Guǎng-shī wú-zhē dào-chǎng yí*, [[KR6v0067]] *Dà-hēi-tiān shén dào-chǎng yí*, [[KR6v0069]] *Yúnnán Āzhālì jiào jīngdiǎn jí qí zài Zhōngguó fójiào yán-jiū zhōng de jiàzhí*, [[KR6v0074]] *Hùguó sī-nán chāo*, [[KR6v0075]] *Fó-shuō xiāo-zāi yán-shòu Yàoshī guàn-dǐng zhāngjù yí*.
