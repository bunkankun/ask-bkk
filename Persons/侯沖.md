Modern Chinese scholar of regional Buddhism, professor at Shanghai Normal University (上海師範大學), specialist in the Yúnnán *Āzhālì* 阿吒力 ritual lineage and in Chinese Buddhist liturgy more broadly. Author of *Zhōngguó fójiào yíshì yánjiū* 中國佛教儀式研究 (Shanghai: Shànghǎi gǔjí, 2011) and *Yúnnán Āzhālì jiàopài jí qí jīngdiǎn yánjiū* 雲南阿吒力教派及其經典研究. Co-edited (with [[趙文煥]]) the Yúnnán Āzhālì ritual manuals in *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vols 6–8: [[KR6v0063]], [[KR6v0064]], [[KR6v0065]], [[KR6v0088]]. Sole editor of [[KR6v0066]] *Guǎngshī wúzhē dàochǎng yí*, [[KR6v0067]] *Dàhēitiān shén dàochǎng yí*, [[KR6v0069]] *Yúnnán Āzhālì jiào jīngdiǎn jí qí zài Zhōngguó fójiào yánjiū zhōng de jiàzhí*, [[KR6v0074]] *Hùguó sīnán chāo*, [[KR6v0075]] *Fóshuō xiāozāi yánshòu Yàoshī guàndǐng zhāngjù yí*.
