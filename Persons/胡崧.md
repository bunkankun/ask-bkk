# 胡崧

Hú Sōng (*zì* Zhīqiáo 芝樵), late-Qīng physician and editor; reprinted [[KR3em009]] *Yīcǎotíng mùkē quánshū* 一草亭目科全書 together with [[KR3em013]] *Yìshòu yǎnkē* 異授眼科 in 1882 under the joint title *Qǐméng zhēndì* 啟蒙真諦, after curing his own son's *yúnyì* with prescriptions from the two texts.
