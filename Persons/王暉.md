**Wáng Huī** 王暉 was a Táng Daoist author, compiler of the ***Xuán zhū lù*** 玄珠錄 ([[KR5d0071]]) — a Táng Chóng xuán 重玄-school philosophical-aphoristic collection. Often associated with **Wáng Xuán lǎn** 王玄覽 (626–697), the great Chóngxuán thinker whose *Xuán zhū lù* (compiled by disciples from his lectures) this likely is. The Dào zàng attribution "Wáng Huī" 王暉 may be a corruption / variant.
