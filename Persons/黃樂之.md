# 黃樂之

Huáng Lèzhī

Mid-Qīng layman who in autumn 1858 (Xiánfēng 8) supplied a verse benediction paratext to [[徐槐廷]] Xú Huáitíng's [[KR6c0097]] *Jīngāng jīng jiěyì*, framing the *Vajracchedikā* as a devotional-protective text.
