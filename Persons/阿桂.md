# Āguì 阿桂

The pre-eminent Manchu statesman and military commander of the late Qiánlóng era. Manchu Bordered-Yellow Banner; *zì* **Guǎngtíng** 廣廷; *hào* **Yúnyán** 雲巖; posthumous title **Wénchéng** 文成. Lifedates 1717–1797 (CBDB id 510929 confirms Qing dynasty).

Father: Ākèdūn 阿克敦 (1685–1756), grand secretary under Yōngzhèng. Began career as a *bǐtièshì* 筆帖式 (Manchu translator-scribe). Rose through frontier military command during the second Jīnchuān 金川 campaign (1771–1776) to become commander-in-chief; received the highest honors — *Yīděng chéngmóu Yīngyǒng gōng* 一等誠謀英勇公 (First-Class Loyal-and-Strategist Heroic Duke), entered the *Zǐguānggé* 紫光閣 (Hall of Purple Light) of imperial heroes. Subsequently *Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì* 武英殿大學士, supervisor of the Qiánlóng emperor's military councils for two decades, and one of the principal regents in the emperor's late life.

Co-compiled (as *fèng chì* — "by imperial commission") with Liáng Guózhì the *Huáng Qīng kāiguó fānglüè* 皇清開國方略 ([[KR2b0039]]) in 32 *juǎn*, the Qiánlóng-imperial chronicle of the Qing founding (1583–1644). Composed in *Qiánlóng* 38 / 1773 under imperial commission; completed *Qiánlóng* 51 / 1786 (the *Sìkù* tíyào dates the commission to *Qiánlóng* 38 / 1773; the catalog meta gives *Qiánlóng* 51 / 1786 as the completion date). Āguì's role on the project was the institutional-supervisory one of *zǒng cái* (chief director); the substantive compositional work was done by the *Mǎnzhōu yuánliú kǎo* historiographical bureau under his supervision.

Other significant historical-editorial roles: *zǒng cái* of the *Sìkù quánshū* compilation (1773–1782); supervisor of the *Mǎnzhōu yuánliú kǎo* 滿洲源流考 (1789); supervisor of the *Bā qí tōng zhì* 八旗通志. He was also the chief compiler-of-record of three *fānglüè* in the present division: *Píngdìng liǎng Jīnchuān fānglüè* ([[KR2c0013]], 1781–86) on the Second Jīnchuān war (which he himself commanded) — co-compiled with Fù Kāng'ān 福康安 and Liáng Guózhì 梁國治; *Qīndìng Lánzhōu jìlüè* ([[KR2c0015]], 1781) on the Sūsì sìshísān 蘇四十三 revolt; and the *Qīndìng jiǎopǔ Línqīng nìfěi jìlüè* ([[KR2c0014]], 1777) on the Wáng Lún 王倫 revolt.

Biography in *Qīng shǐ gǎo* 318. CBDB id 510929.
