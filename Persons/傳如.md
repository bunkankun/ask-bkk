**Chuánrú** 傳如 (1562 – 1624), *zì* **Jièshān** 戒山 (also written 介山), original Buddhist name **Xīngrú** 興如. Native of **Hǎiyán** 海鹽 (in modern Zhèjiāng, then administered as part of Wǔyuán 武原 / north-west of Sūzhōu); lay surname **Gù** 顧. Late-Ming **Tiāntái** 天台 monk.

Took the tonsure in youth at the **Zhāoqìngsì** 昭慶寺 in Hangzhou under **Jìnghú** 鏡湖. He was particularly esteemed by **Bǎisōng Zhēnjué** 真寂百松 ([[一松]], 1537–1589), who urged him to "shoulder the great Dharma" (荷擔大法). Studied Tiāntái doctrine and meditation under **Miàofēng** 妙峰; became known for unimpeded preaching and disputation (講辨無礙). Together with **Zǐbǎi Zhēnkě** 紫柏真可 he was caught up in the great late-Wàn-lì *fǎnàn* 法難 ("Dharma persecution") of the Buddhist *sangha* under court politics, but was eventually released without conviction. In later life he resided at the **Dàshànsì** 大善寺 in **Tángqī** 塘棲 (in Hangzhou prefecture).

He is reported to have died in **Tiānqǐ 4 = 1624** at the age of 63 (per the *Shìshì yínián lù* 釋氏疑年錄 fasc. 11), implying a birth year of 1562 (calculated *sui*).

His extant works include the **Lèngyánjīng jiéliú** 楞嚴經截流 in 2 fascicles ([[KR6j0704]], X14 no. 296), the **Fǎhuá shūhǎi** 法華抒海, and the **Lèngyánjīng xiē** 楞嚴歇 (the latter two preserved only in fragments). In **Wànlì 25 = 1597 (春)**, at the **Jiǔshā chànshì** 九沙懺室 in **Xīxī** 西溪, he composed the principal preface to **Yōuxī Chuándēng**'s *Lèngyánjīng xuányì* ([[KR6j0690]]).
