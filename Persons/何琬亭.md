# 何琬亭

Hé Wǎntíng 何琬亭 (mid-Qīng), Sūzhōu physician of the Hé-family medical lineage; one of the "Hé-family three" alongside [[何元長]] Hé Yuáncháng and [[何書山]] Hé Shūshān, ancestors of the Ménghé Hé-family clinicians.
