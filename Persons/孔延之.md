# Kǒng Yánzhī 孔延之 (1014–1074)

*Zì* Cháng-yuán 長源. Native of *Xīn-gàn* 新淦 in Línjiāng-fǔ (modern Jiāngxī). A descendant of Kǒng Anguó's branch of the Confucius family. *Jìnshì* of *Bǎo-yuán* 1 (1038). His career included *Shàng-shū sī-fēng láng-zhōng*, *Zhī Yuè-zhōu jūn-zhōu shì*, and *Zhè-dōng bīng-mǎ qián-xiá* (regional commander) in *Xī-níng* 4–5 (1071–72). His major scholarly output is the *Huì-jī duō-yīng zǒng-jí* 會稽掇英總集 [[KR4h0028]], a twenty-*juǎn* topographic anthology of poetry and prose on Kuài-jī (Shàoxīng), compiled while in office at Yuè-zhōu and prefaced *Xī-níng rén-zǐ* 5/1 (1072). He died in *Xī-níng* 7 (1074). Father of **Kǒng Wén-zhòng** 孔文仲 (1038–1088), **Kǒng Wǔ-zhòng** 孔武仲, and **Kǒng Píng-zhòng** 孔平仲 — the *Qīng-jiāng sān Kǒng* whose joint collection is [[KR4h0029]]. The catalog meta gives 1021–1086; CBDB and Wikidata give 1014–1074 (followed here).
