# 浣月居士

Wǎnyuè jūshì (style; no confirmed personal name) of Héngyáng 衡陽, late-Qīng editor; organised the 1878 (光緒四年戊寅) *Shùdé táng* 樹德堂 reissue at Yángzhōu 揚州 of [[潘誠]] Pān Chéng's [[KR3em036]] *Hóukē xīnfǎ* 喉科心法.
