# Yán Wénxuǎn 顏文選

Míng-period scholar-official; held the post of *jǐ-shì-zhōng* 給事中 (Secretariat Drafter / Censor). His principal scholarly output is the running commentary (*zhù* 註) to the *Luò chéng jí* [[KR4c0006]], the surviving 4-*juǎn* recension of Luò Bīn-wáng [[駱賓王]]. The Sìkù compilers — receiving Yán's edition as the only available annotation of Luò — printed it for completeness while criticizing his attestations as "loose and faulty, almost without value, but in the absence of any other commentary tradition retained for reference" (lit. *yuán-yǐn shū-chuǎn dài wú-kě qǔ* 援引疎舛殆無可取). His exact dates and biography are not recorded in the standard sources; CBDB has no entry. The position *jǐ-shì-zhōng* in the late Míng was a senior *Mén-xià-shěng* drafting post normally held by experienced examination graduates.
