Early-Qīng scholar and bibliophile of Xiùshuǐ 秀水 (modern Jiāxīng, Zhèjiāng), *zì* Wǔzēng 武曾, *hào* Qiūjǐn 秋錦. He composed the 1692 (Kāngxī rénshēn) postface to [[張根]] Zhāng Gēn's *Wúyuán Zhōuyì jiě* ([[KR1a0019]]) on the Xú-family *Zhuàn shì lóu* 傳是樓 manuscript — a small monument of late-Míng / early-Qīng *cáng shū* 藏書 book-culture, marking the work as already rare in his day.
