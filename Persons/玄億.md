Xuán-yì 玄億 (the catalog form; original dharma-name **Xuán-yì** 玄憶, post-1660s also written **Dēng-yì** 燈億 — the latter is DILA's primary form, possibly reflecting Kāngxī-era avoidance), *zì* **Qiáo-sōng** 喬松 ("Tall Pine"); often referred to as **Qiáo-sōng Yì** 喬松億 or **Gāo-fēng Qiáo-sōng** 高峰喬松 from his abbacy. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1608–1663), thus a co-disciple of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] (cf. [[KR6q0554]]) in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line. Born **明萬曆己未十一月初六日** = **1619-12-11** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 51 in **康熙八年** = **1669**. Native of **Lín-shuǐ** 鄰水 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Féng** 馮.

**Formation.** Almost 17 went to **Huì-kōng-ān** 慧空菴 to take tonsure; soon after met **Fù-chū** 復初和尚 and joined his community at **Yán-fú-sì** 延福寺 in his hometown — given dharma-name **Xuán-yì** 玄憶 by Fù-chū. Doubt about his own matter unresolved, traveled to **Huá-yáng-shān** 華陽山 to study under **Nán-zōng** 南宗禪師, then went to **Jù-yún** 聚雲 to study under **Qìng-zhōng** 慶忠和尚. Followed Qìng-zhōng on his refuge-flight to **Nán-bīn Shí-fēng-sì** 南濱石峰寺; then served the *Cáng-hǎi* (Tripiṭaka-storage hall) for six years. Received final *fù-fǎ* during a *jié-zhì* retreat at **Qīng-shān** 青山.

**Abbacies.** **Zhōng-zhōu Bǎi-yán-sì** 忠州柏巖寺, **Liáng-shān Gāo-fēng chán-yuàn** 梁山高峰禪院 (the seat giving him his sobriquet **Gāo-fēng**), **È-zhōu Dà-bié-shān Wò-yún chán-yuàn** 鄂州大別山臥雲禪院 (Hú-běi, where he was abbot in 1669 at age 51).

**Work.** [[KR6q0555]] 《高峰喬松億禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B440, recorded by *shū-jì* **Dēng-hóng** [[燈洪]] *lù*.

**Sources.** 《高峰喬松億禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行由〉; 《續燈正統》 (X84 n1583 p. 506c22).
