**Bāo Sānshù** 包三述 (Qīng, dates and place of activity not recorded), the named compiler of [[KR3em025]] *Bāo-shì hóu-zhèng jiā-bǎo* 包氏喉證家寶, a single-*juǎn* family-tradition laryngology manual in the *qī-shí-èr zhǒng yān-hóu* 七十二種咽喉 (seventy-two throat-disorders) genre. No CBDB record; he is not recorded in *Zhōng-yī dà-cí-diǎn* 中醫大辭典 or other standard biographical references with any detail beyond his authorship of this work. The text's closing colophon, which collates his recension with the parallel Jiāo-clan ([[KR3em023]]) and Zhāng-clan ([[KR3em024]]) witnesses, suggests he was a clinically and bibliographically literate Qīng *hóu-kē* practitioner active in the period when the seventy-two-condition catalogue genre had matured — most plausibly 18th to early-19th century.
