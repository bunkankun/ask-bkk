**Shào Jīngbāng** 邵經邦 (1491–1565) was a mid-Míng scholar-official and Daoist bibliographer, author of [[KR5h0033]] ***Hóng dào lù*** 弘道錄 — an extensive 25-*juàn* compendium of Daoist biographies and teachings.
