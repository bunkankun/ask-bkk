# 聖行

Late-Míng Buddhist monk; teacher of [[法濟]] (Fǎjì) of the Yùxī Pútíān 玉溪菩提菴. He wrote the *Preface to the Engraving of the Annotated Lotus Sūtra* (《刻註法華經序》) dated Chóngzhēn 1 (1628) for the re-cut edition of [[守倫]] Shǒulún's *Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù* 法華經科註 ([[KR6d0071]]).
