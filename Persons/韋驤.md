Wéi Xiāng 韋驤 (1033–1105), *zì* Zǐjùn 子駿, of *Qián-táng* 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu). *Huáng-yòu* 5 / 1053 *jìn-shì*. Held *zhī Yuán-zhōu Píng-xiāng-xiàn* 知袁州萍鄉縣, *Fú-jiàn zhuǎn-yùn pàn-guān* 福建轉運判官, and as *Kuí-lù tí-xíng* 夔路提刑; under *Jiàn-zhōng Jìng-guó* (1101) was *zhī Míng-zhōu* 知明州 and ended his career as *Zuǒ Cháo-yì-dài-fū Tí-jǔ Hángzhōu Dòng-xiāo-gōng* 左朝議大夫提舉杭州洞霄宫. The *Sòng-shǐ* gives him no biography; his offices are reconstructed from the *zhuàng* and *jì-wén* preserved in his collection. In youth he was known for *cí-fù*; Wáng Ān-shí 王安石 [[王安石]] particularly praised his *Jiè-zhù fù* 借箸賦. The *Sòng-shǐ Yì-wén-zhì* lists a separate *Wéi Zǐ-jùn fù* 韋子駿賦 in 20 *juǎn*, now lost. His extant collection is the *Qián-táng jí* 錢塘集 [[KR4d0051]].
