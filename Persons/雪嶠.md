# Xuějiào 雪嶠

Late-Míng Línjì-school Chán master **Xuějiào Yuánxìn** 雪嶠圓信 (1571–1647). Dharma-heir of Lóngchí Huànyǒu Zhèngchuán 龍池幻有正傳, brother-disciple of Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟; an influential Buddhist master of the late-Wànlì and Chóngzhēn eras.

Named in [[譚貞默]] Tán Zhēnmò's 1657 preface to [[KR6j0760]] as one of Tán's personal initiators in *Cuṇḍā* devotion, alongside [[憨山德清]] Hānshān Déqīng.
