Xīngqìng 興磬, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Yàodì Wúkě Dàzhì** [[智]] (= **Fāng Yǐzhì** 方以智, 1611–1671) at **Qīngyuánshān Jìngjūsì** 青原山淨居寺 (Jízhōu, Jiāngxī). Co-compiler with **Xīngfǔ** [[興斧]] of the 4-juan 《青原愚者智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0483]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
