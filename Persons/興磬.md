Xīng-qìng 興磬, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Yào-dì Wú-kě Dà-zhì** [[智]] (= **Fāng Yǐ-zhì** 方以智, 1611–1671) at **Qīng-yuán-shān Jìng-jū-sì** 青原山淨居寺 (Jí-zhōu, Jiāng-xī). Co-compiler with **Xīng-fǔ** [[興斧]] of the 4-juan 《青原愚者智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0483]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
