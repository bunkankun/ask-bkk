Míngdé 明德. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsūn* (dharma-grandson) of **Tōngtiān Yìsǒu Xínggāo** [[行高]] (1616–1669) and *fǎsì* of **Tōngtiān Dànyá Chāoyuán** [[超原]] (1630–1698). Lifedates unknown. Two roles in J39:
- *Zhòngzǐ* (re-engraver / re-publisher) of his dharma-grandfather's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0569]] 《通天逸叟高禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B455.
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0570]] 《通天澹崖原禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B456.

The DILA database does not register a separate entry; lineage placement is inferred from the *zhòngzǐ* and *biān* roles in the two paired *yǔlù*.
