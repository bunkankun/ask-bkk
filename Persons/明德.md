Míng-dé 明德. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sūn* (dharma-grandson) of **Tōng-tiān Yì-sǒu Xíng-gāo** [[行高]] (1616–1669) and *fǎ-sì* of **Tōng-tiān Dàn-yá Chāo-yuán** [[超原]] (1630–1698). Lifedates unknown. Two roles in J39:
- *Zhòng-zǐ* (re-engraver / re-publisher) of his dharma-grandfather's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0569]] 《通天逸叟高禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B455.
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0570]] 《通天澹崖原禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B456.

The DILA database does not register a separate entry; lineage placement is inferred from the *zhòng-zǐ* and *biān* roles in the two paired *yǔ-lù*.
