Táng Yúnzhōu 唐芸洲 was a late Qīng dynasty popular fiction writer, the author of the 180-chapter *wǔxiá* novel *Qī Jiàn Shísān Xiá* 七劍十三俠 ([[KR4k0195]]). Beyond the novel itself, no biographical information has been located. He was probably active in the last quarter of the nineteenth century, writing for the burgeoning commercial print market in Beijing or Shanghai. No CBDB record has been found.
