Late-Míng (Lóngqìng / Wàn-lì era) classicist, prose stylist, and provincial official, native of Huá-tíng 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng prefecture, modern Shànghǎi). *Zì* Mǐnqīng 敏卿; *hào* Yuánchéng 元成. *Jìnshì* of *Lóngqìng* 5 / *xīn-wèi* 辛未 (1571). His career rose through provincial administration to the senior post of *Húguǎng bùzhèng-sī cān-zhèng* 湖廣布政司參政 (Provincial Administration Commissioner of Húguǎng). Biography appears as an appendix to the biography of his father Féng Ēn 馮恩 in the *Míng shǐ*. CBDB (id 30243) records the *jìnshì* year 1571 but no firm birth or death dates; floruit is securely the 1570s–1610s. *Míng-rén zhuànjì zīliào suǒyǐn* p. 209.5522 is the standard modern reference.

Féng was a prolific late-Míng polymath, with substantial writings in *jīng*-, *shǐ*-, and *zǐ*-traditions; the SKQS preserves his *Zuǒ-shì shì* 左氏釋 ([[KR1e0086]]) — originally one component of his composite *Yuán-Mǐn Tiān-chí jí* 元敏天池集, recovered as an independent classical work by the *Sìkù* editors. He also produced *Zuǒ-shì tǎo* 左氏討 and *Zuǒ-shì lùn* 左氏論, both of which the SKQS editors note in their *cún mù* 存目 catalogue. The *Sìkù* editors evaluate Féng's *Zuǒ-shì shì* as "second only to Zhào Fǎng's *Bǔ zhù*" (Zhào Fǎng [[KR1e0070]], the late-Yuán *Zuǒ-zhuàn* commentator) — a comparatively warm Míng-period verdict.

In medicine, Féng is the author of **[[KR3eq020]]** *Shàng-chí zá-shuō* 上池雜說 (catalogued under his *hào* 馮元成), a one-*juǎn* late-Míng *zá-shuō* of doctrinal essays and clinical case-records that gives one of the earliest sustained late-Míng critiques of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī's "陽常有餘陰常不足" doctrine — anticipating by approximately a generation the warming-tonifying school of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jiè-bīn. The work includes a substantial appended proven-recipe section (*Tài-jí guāng-tǒng* 太極光統, *Gàn-xuě dān* 紺雪丹, *Qīng-lián sǎn* 青蓮散 etc.) and preserves notable early-modern case-records of aconite-tonification misuse and brain-abscess death.
