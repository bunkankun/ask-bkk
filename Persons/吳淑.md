Late-Southern-Táng / early-Sòng scholar-official and compiler, *zì* Zhèngyí 正儀, of *Rùnzhōu Dānyáng* 潤州丹陽. Son-in-law of Xú Xuàn [[徐鉉]] (he married Xú's daughter and was groomed in his house). Served the Southern Táng as *Nèishǐ shěrén*; after the Sòng conquest passed the *jìnshì* examination (979) and entered the *Tàizǐ zhōngyǔn* and *Diànzhōng chéng* posts. Worked alongside his father-in-law on Sòng Tàizōng's three great encyclopedic projects (*Tàipíng yùlǎn*, *Tàipíng guǎngjì*, *Wényuàn yīnghuá*) and edited Xú Xuàn's posthumous *Qíshěng jí* [[KR4d0001]] in 30 *juǎn*. Author of the *Shìlèi fù* 事類賦 (*Fù* on Categories of Things, with self-commentary, 30 *juǎn*) — an unusual *lèishū* in *fù* form, transmitted in WYG and a long-running scholarly favorite. CBDB 12238, lifedates 947–1002 in agreement with *Sòngshǐ* j. 441 (附傳).
