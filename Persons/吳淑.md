Late-Southern-Táng / early-Sòng scholar-official and compiler, *zì* Zhèng-yí 正儀, of *Rùn-zhōu Dān-yáng* 潤州丹陽. Son-in-law of Xú Xuàn [[徐鉉]] (he married Xú's daughter and was groomed in his house). Served the Southern Táng as *Nèi-shǐ shě-rén*; after the Sòng conquest passed the *jìn-shì* examination (979) and entered the *Tài-zǐ zhōng-yǔn* and *Diàn-zhōng chéng* posts. Worked alongside his father-in-law on Sòng Tài-zōng's three great encyclopedic projects (*Tài-píng yù-lǎn*, *Tài-píng guǎng-jì*, *Wén-yuàn yīng-huá*) and edited Xú Xuàn's posthumous *Qí-shěng jí* [[KR4d0001]] in 30 *juǎn*. Author of the *Shì-lèi fù* 事類賦 (*Fù* on Categories of Things, with self-commentary, 30 *juǎn*) — an unusual *lèi-shū* in *fù* form, transmitted in WYG and a long-running scholarly favorite. CBDB 12238, lifedates 947–1002 in agreement with *Sòng-shǐ* j. 441 (附傳).
