Gé Rú-lóng 葛如龍, son of the Wàn-lì-era *Shàng-bǎo-sī qīng* **Gé Xīn** 葛昕 (*hào* Lóng-chí 龍池) of Dé-píng 德平 (Shān-dōng). He served, with brothers and assistants whom the *Sìkù* tiyao does not name, as posthumous editor of his father's collected writings, the *Jí-yù-shān-fáng gǎo* 集玉山房稿 ([[KR4e0237]]) in ten *juǎn*, arranging the material by genre: one *juǎn* memorials, one *juǎn* *zàn-yǔ*, one *juǎn* verse, one *juǎn* prefaces, one *juǎn* tomb-records and biographies, one *juǎn* records / inscriptions / colophons, one *juǎn* opening-petitions, two *juǎn* letters, one *juǎn* sacrificial liturgies.
