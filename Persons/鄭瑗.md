Zhèng Yuàn 鄭瑗 (*fl.* late 15th c.), *zì* Zhòng-bì 仲璧; native of Pútián 莆田, Fújiàn. *Jìnshì* of *Chénghuà xīn-chǒu* (1481). Held office as *Nán-jīng Lǐ-bù láng-zhōng* (Director, Bureau of Rites, Southern Capital). His critical *bǐjì* *Jǐng guān suǒ yán* 井觀瑣言 ([[KR3j0154]]) in 3 *juàn* — a substantial *kǎo-zhèng* work — was for centuries miscatalogued under a Sòng "Mǐn-nán Zhèng Yuàn" until the *Sìkù* editors corrected the attribution. Zhū Yízūn's 朱彝尊 *Míng shī zōng* preserves his poetic remnants and records his literary collection *Shěng-zhāi jí* 省齋集 (no longer extant). Biographical evidence chiefly from the *Hóng-zhì Bā-mǐn tōng-zhì* and *Míng shī zōng*.
