Zhèng Yuàn 鄭瑗 (*fl.* late 15th c.), *zì* Zhòngbì 仲璧; native of Pútián 莆田, Fújiàn. *Jìnshì* of *Chénghuà xīnchǒu* (1481). Held office as *Nánjīng Lǐbù lángzhōng* (Director, Bureau of Rites, Southern Capital). His critical *bǐjì* *Jǐng guān suǒ yán* 井觀瑣言 ([[KR3j0154]]) in 3 *juàn* — a substantial *kǎozhèng* work — was for centuries miscatalogued under a Sòng "Mǐnnán Zhèng Yuàn" until the *Sìkù* editors corrected the attribution. Zhū Yízūn's 朱彝尊 *Míng shī zōng* preserves his poetic remnants and records his literary collection *Shěngzhāi jí* 省齋集 (no longer extant). Biographical evidence chiefly from the *Hóngzhì Bāmǐn tōngzhì* and *Míng shī zōng*.
