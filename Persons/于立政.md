# Yú Lìzhèng 于立政

Yú Lìzhèng 于立政 (fl. 656–666; CBDB id 31486), early to mid-Táng official and compiler. Active during the reign of Táng Gāozōng 唐高宗 (r. 650–683). He is the attributed compiler of the *Lèilín* 類林 (Forest of Categories), a topical anecdote collection originally comprising 50 categories (*mén* 門) of moral exempla drawn from canonical texts and histories, which served as a source for examination study and ethical instruction. The *Lèilín* is recorded in the *Jiù Táng shū · Jīngjí zhì* and *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* under the *zájiā* 雜家 classification.

The *Lèilín* was greatly expanded in the Jīn dynasty by [[王朋壽]] of Píngyáng in 1189, resulting in the *Zēngguǎng fēnmén lèilín záshuo* 增廣分門類林雜說 ([[KR3k0080]]).
