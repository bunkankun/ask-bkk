# Zhèngshòu 正受

Sobriquet Léi'ān 雷庵. Southern Sòng Chán monk, Dharma heir of Zhuó'ān Déguāng 拙庵德光 (1121–1203) in the Línjì 臨濟 — Yángqí 楊岐 — Dàhuì 大慧 line. Active in the late twelfth and very early thirteenth century; exact lifedates not transmitted.

Compiler of the *Jiātài pǔdēng lù* 嘉泰普燈錄 ([[KR6q0010]]) and its separately-transmitted table of contents ([[KR6q0009]]), completed in 1204 — the fifth of the canonical Sòng Chán lamp records and notable for its more expansive inclusion of lay figures.
