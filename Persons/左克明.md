Late-Yuán literatus of **Yùzhāng** 豫章 (Nánchāng, Jiāngxī). Life-dates not preserved; active in the 1340s. He compiled the **[[KR4h0084]]** *Gǔ yuèfǔ* 古樂府 (10 *juǎn*), prefaced and presumably completed in **Zhìzhèng *bǐngxū*** (= 1346, 10th month). The work is an eight-category anthology of pre-Táng *yuèfǔ* deliberately positioned against the *xīnshēng yuèfǔ* of Yáng Wéizhēn 楊維楨. No other works under his name are transmitted; he describes himself in his preface as "a humble man of the mountains and forests" (*kèmíng qièfú shānlín*).
