Late-Yuán literatus of **Yù-zhāng** 豫章 (Nán-chāng, Jiāng-xī). Life-dates not preserved; active in the 1340s. He compiled the **[[KR4h0084]]** *Gǔ yuè-fǔ* 古樂府 (10 *juǎn*), prefaced and presumably completed in **Zhì-zhèng *bǐng-xū*** (= 1346, 10th month). The work is an eight-category anthology of pre-Táng *yuè-fǔ* deliberately positioned against the *xīn-shēng yuè-fǔ* of Yáng Wéi-zhēn 楊維楨. No other works under his name are transmitted; he describes himself in his preface as "a humble man of the mountains and forests" (*kè-míng qiè-fú shān-lín*).
