**Yúnyáng Chīchī Dàorén** 雲陽嗤嗤道人 ("The Snickering Daoist of Yúnyáng") is the pseudonymous author of *Jǐngwù Zhōng* 警寤鍾 ([[KR4k0158]]). The sobriquet *Chīchī* 嗤嗤 ("snickering," "mocking") is characteristic of Qīng popular fiction pen names that signal satirical or moralist intent. The toponym Yúnyáng 雲陽 may refer to a location in Jiāngsū or Jiāngxī, but the identification is uncertain. No independent biographical record has been found in standard Qīng sources or CBDB. No confident CBDB match exists.
