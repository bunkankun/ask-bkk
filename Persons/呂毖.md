Late-Míng / early-Qīng eunuch and editor; surnamed 呂, *zì* Chìyǐn 赤隱; the *Lúchéng* in the *Sìkù*-bestowed inscription identifies a place-name (Lúchéng 蘆城) attached to him. Active in the late-Míng inner palace; survived the dynastic transition. The *Sìkù* editors record only that "Bì's biography is unknown" and that he was "a late-Míng eunuch." His principal extant work is the *Míng gōng shǐ* 明宮史 ([[KR2m0032]]), a late-Míng inner-palace memoir thought by some to be a redaction of Liú Ruòyú's much larger *Zhuō zhōng zhì*. CBDB confirms 1611–1664.
