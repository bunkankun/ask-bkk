**Táo Chéng-xī** 陶承熹 (*fl.* mid-Qīng, ca. 1750–1820), co-compiler with [[王承勳]] (Wáng Chéng-xūn) of the *Huìzhí-táng jīng-yàn fāng* 惠直堂經驗方 ([[KR3ed115]]), a 4-*juǎn* clinical formulary of the late-eighteenth-century Jiāng-Nán popular-pharmacology tradition. The *Huìzhí-táng* 惠直堂 ("Studio of the Direct Benefactor") was apparently a clinical practice or publishing house in which Táo and Wáng worked. No further biographical record is available in CBDB or the standard Qīng biographical sources.
