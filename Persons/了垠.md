# Liǎo-yín 了垠

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249); with [[了南]], the principal named editor of Wújùn's court-audience record *Wújùn hé-shàng zòu-duì yǔlù* ([[KR6q0316]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000025).
