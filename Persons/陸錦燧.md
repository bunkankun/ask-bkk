**Lù Jǐnsuì** 陸錦燧 (*zì* Jìnshēng 晉笙, *fl.* 1900s–1920s, early Republican period), Sūzhōu (Wújùn) literatus-physician of the early Republican-era popular-pharmacology circle. Active at his **Pūxī** 䱐溪 ("Pū Stream") studio in Sūzhōu. Author of the *Pūxī mìchuán jiǎnyàn fāng* 䱐溪秘傳簡驗方 ([[KR3ed149]]), an early-1918 combined edition of *dānfāng* and *wàizhì fāng* prepared through a subscription-voucher scheme funded by Zhuāng Xīnrú 莊心如 and Liú Jiàntáng 劉建堂. The work's preface articulates one of the clearest late-Imperial / early-Republican statements of **formulary-curation criterion** — explicit exclusion of low-efficacy, hard-to-procure, expensive, side-effect-prone, and extreme-natured formulas. Sparse further biographical record.
