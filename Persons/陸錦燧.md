**Lù Jǐn-suì** 陸錦燧 (*zì* Jìn-shēng 晉笙, *fl.* 1900s–1920s, early Republican period), Sū-zhōu (Wú-jùn) literatus-physician of the early Republican-era popular-pharmacology circle. Active at his **Pū-xī** 䱐溪 ("Pū Stream") studio in Sū-zhōu. Author of the *Pūxī mìchuán jiǎnyàn fāng* 䱐溪秘傳簡驗方 ([[KR3ed149]]), an early-1918 combined edition of *dān-fāng* and *wài-zhì fāng* prepared through a subscription-voucher scheme funded by Zhuāng Xīn-rú 莊心如 and Liú Jiàn-táng 劉建堂. The work's preface articulates one of the clearest late-Imperial / early-Republican statements of **formulary-curation criterion** — explicit exclusion of low-efficacy, hard-to-procure, expensive, side-effect-prone, and extreme-natured formulas. Sparse further biographical record.
