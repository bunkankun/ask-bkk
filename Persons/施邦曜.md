Shī Bāng-yào 施邦曜 (1585–1644), *zì* **Ěr-tāo** 爾韜, *hào* **Sì-míng** 四明 (whence prefaces address him as *Sì-míng Shī gōng* 四明施公); posthumous title from the Southern-Míng régime **Zhōng-jiè** 忠介, re-canonised by the Qīng *Qián-lóng* emperor as **Zhōng-mǐn** 忠愍 in the *Qīn-dìng Shèng-cháo xùn-jié zhū chén lù* 欽定勝朝殉節諸臣錄 (1776). Native of **Yú-yáo** 餘姚 (Shào-xīng, Zhè-jiāng) — Wáng Yáng-míng's home county, whence the *tóng-yì hòu-xué* ('a later student of the same place') self-designation in his preface to the *Yáng-míng xiān-shēng jí yào*. *Jìnshì* of Wàn-lì 41 / *guǐ-chǒu* (1613) — note that Wikipedia gives Wàn-lì 47 / *jǐ-wèi* (1619), but the standard *Míng-shǐ* and CBDB place him in 1613. Refused alliance with Wèi Zhōng-xián during *Tiān-qǐ* and was demoted to **Zhāng-zhōu zhī-fǔ** 漳州知府, in which capacity he subdued the maritime rebels Liú Xiāng 劉香 and his band; served in Fú-jiàn for some eight years across promotions as *fù-shǐ* and *zuǒ cān-zhèng*, then transferred to Sì-chuān *àn-chá-shǐ*, *Fú-jiàn zuǒ bù-zhèng-shǐ*, **Nán-jīng / Běi-jīng Guāng-lù-sì qīng**, and at length *Tōng-zhèng-shǐ* and *Zuǒ fù dū yù-shǐ* (1643). In the **Huáng Dào-zhōu impeachment case** of *Chóng-zhēn* 15 / 1642 his refusal to forward the appeal of Tú Zhòng-jí 涂仲吉 (with the celebrated note '*Shū bù bì shàng, lùn bù kě bù cún*' 疏不必上，論不可不存) ended in his being stripped of office. When Lǐ Zì-chéng entered Běi-jīng in *Chóng-zhēn jiǎ-shēn* (1644) and the *Chóng-zhēn* emperor hanged himself, Shī first attempted suicide by the cord (rescued by his servant), then took arsenic mixed with wine in the street and died — '*the blood spurted and he died*'. Throughout his career in Fú-jiàn he had laboured at compiling and editing the writings of Wáng Yáng-míng, his fellow-countryman; the principal product is the *Yáng-míng xiān-shēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) in three structural divisions — *Lǐxué-biān* 理學編, *Jīngjì-biān* 經濟編, and *Wénzhāng-biān* 文章編 — first printed at Zhāng-zhōu in *Chóng-zhēn yǐ-hài* / 1635, with his own preface and four further prefaces by Lín Hán 林釬, [[王志道]], [[顏繼祖]], and others.
