Shī Bāngyào 施邦曜 (1585–1644), *zì* **Ěrtāo** 爾韜, *hào* **Sìmíng** 四明 (whence prefaces address him as *Sìmíng Shī gōng* 四明施公); posthumous title from the Southern-Míng régime **Zhōngjiè** 忠介, re-canonised by the Qīng *Qiánlóng* emperor as **Zhōngmǐn** 忠愍 in the *Qīndìng Shèngcháo xùnjié zhū chén lù* 欽定勝朝殉節諸臣錄 (1776). Native of **Yúyáo** 餘姚 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng) — Wáng Yángmíng's home county, whence the *tóngyì hòuxué* ('a later student of the same place') self-designation in his preface to the *Yángmíng xiānshēng jí yào*. *Jìnshì* of Wànlì 41 / *guǐchǒu* (1613) — note that Wikipedia gives Wànlì 47 / *jǐwèi* (1619), but the standard *Míngshǐ* and CBDB place him in 1613. Refused alliance with Wèi Zhōngxián during *Tiānqǐ* and was demoted to **Zhāngzhōu zhīfǔ** 漳州知府, in which capacity he subdued the maritime rebels Liú Xiāng 劉香 and his band; served in Fújiàn for some eight years across promotions as *fùshǐ* and *zuǒ cānzhèng*, then transferred to Sìchuān *àncháshǐ*, *Fújiàn zuǒ bùzhèngshǐ*, **Nánjīng / Běijīng Guānglùsì qīng**, and at length *Tōngzhèngshǐ* and *Zuǒ fù dū yùshǐ* (1643). In the **Huáng Dàozhōu impeachment case** of *Chóngzhēn* 15 / 1642 his refusal to forward the appeal of Tú Zhòngjí 涂仲吉 (with the celebrated note '*Shū bù bì shàng, lùn bù kě bù cún*' 疏不必上，論不可不存) ended in his being stripped of office. When Lǐ Zìchéng entered Běijīng in *Chóngzhēn jiǎshēn* (1644) and the *Chóngzhēn* emperor hanged himself, Shī first attempted suicide by the cord (rescued by his servant), then took arsenic mixed with wine in the street and died — '*the blood spurted and he died*'. Throughout his career in Fújiàn he had laboured at compiling and editing the writings of Wáng Yángmíng, his fellow-countryman; the principal product is the *Yángmíng xiānshēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) in three structural divisions — *Lǐxuébiān* 理學編, *Jīngjìbiān* 經濟編, and *Wénzhāngbiān* 文章編 — first printed at Zhāngzhōu in *Chóngzhēn yǐhài* / 1635, with his own preface and four further prefaces by Lín Hán 林釬, [[王志道]], [[顏繼祖]], and others.
