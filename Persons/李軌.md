Lǐ Guǐ 李軌 (fl. 4th c.), *zì* Hóngfàn 弘範, was an Eastern Jìn 東晉 official and classicist. Sòng Xián's preface to the *Fǎyán* describes him as having served as Director of the Court of Sacrifices (*Cí-bù lángzhōng* 祠部郎中). His commentary on Yáng Xióng's *Fǎ yán* — referenced by Sòng Xián as the only pre-Táng survival of the early *Fǎyán* commentarial tradition (others by Hóu Bā 侯芭, Sòng Zhōng 宋衷 and Xīn Déyuán 辛德源 had been lost) — was later partially supplemented by Liǔ Zōngyuán 柳宗元 in the Táng and absorbed wholesale into Sīmǎ Guāng's *Yáng-zǐ Fǎyán jí zhù* 揚子法言集注 (the SKQS-base of KR3a0009). The CBDB carries three homonyms named 李軌 (ids 220350, 318167, 378333), none with confirmed dates corresponding to the Eastern Jìn commentator.
