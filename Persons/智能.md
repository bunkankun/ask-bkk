# Zhì-néng 智能

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[普巖]] Yùn'ān Pǔ-yán (1156–1226); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0313]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A001272). Not to be confused with the Suí-dynasty monk of the same name (A007309).
