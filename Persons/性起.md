Mid- to late-Qīng (Qián-lóng–Jiā-qìng-era) Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk; native of **Wǔ-lín** 武林 (Hángzhōu). Original dharma-name **Miào-dé** 妙德 (changed to **Yuán-fēng** 元峯 to avoid the *bì-huì* "name-tabu" of his uncle **Xiāng-lín** 香林). *Zì* often given as **Yuán-fēng** 元峯, occasionally **Yuán-fēng lǎo-rén** 元峯老人. Trained first under **Huái-shī** 懷師 of Beijing; later received the *xīn-yìn* (mind-seal) of the **Qìng-shān** 磬山 line via **Tiān-tāo Yún** 天濤雲 of Jīn-shān. Resided at the **imperially-built Xī-tiān-sì** 勅建西天寺 in Wǔ-lín. Died **嘉慶 *wù-wǔ* 1/14 = 1798-03-01** per *Zhèng-yuán lüè-jí* j. 16. Two important *Vajracchedikā* commentaries: the eight-juan *Jīn-gāng jīng fǎ-yǎn xuán-pàn shū-chāo* 金剛經法眼懸判疏鈔 ([[KR6c0087]], X25 no. 499) and the two-juan condensed *Jīn-gāng jīng fǎ-yǎn zhù-shū* 金剛經法眼註疏 ([[KR6c0088]], X25 no. 500); the latter prefaced **Qián-lóng 40 = 1775**.
