Mid- to late-Qīng (Qiánlóng–Jiā-qìng-era) LínjìYángqí Chán monk; native of **Wǔlín** 武林 (Hángzhōu). Original dharma-name **Miàodé** 妙德 (changed to **Yuánfēng** 元峯 to avoid the *bìhuì* "name-tabu" of his uncle **Xiānglín** 香林). *Zì* often given as **Yuánfēng** 元峯, occasionally **Yuánfēng lǎorén** 元峯老人. Trained first under **Huáishī** 懷師 of Beijing; later received the *xīnyìn* (mind-seal) of the **Qìngshān** 磬山 line via **Tiāntāo Yún** 天濤雲 of Jīnshān. Resided at the **imperially-built Xītiānsì** 勅建西天寺 in Wǔlín. Died **嘉慶 *wùwǔ* 1/14 = 1798-03-01** per *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* j. 16. Two important *Vajracchedikā* commentaries: the eight-juan *Jīngāng jīng fǎyǎn xuánpàn shūchāo* 金剛經法眼懸判疏鈔 ([[KR6c0087]], X25 no. 499) and the two-juan condensed *Jīngāng jīng fǎyǎn zhùshū* 金剛經法眼註疏 ([[KR6c0088]], X25 no. 500); the latter prefaced **Qiánlóng 40 = 1775**.
