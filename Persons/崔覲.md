# Cuī Jǐn 崔覲

Pre-Táng *Yì* scholar of obscure dates; his commentary is lost as an independent work but cited in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). Conventionally distinguished from [[崔憬]] Cuī Jǐng (Tàn xuán 探玄 author) despite the graphic similarity of the personal names.
