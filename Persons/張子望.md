Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* student of Yíngyáng 滎陽. With [[王天悅]] Wáng Tiānyuè of Dàmíng, one of two student-confidants of [[邵雍]] Shào Yōng in the *xiāntiān* 先天 *Yì*-numerology transmission. Both died young, according to [[邵伯溫]] Shào Bówēn's *Yìxué biànhuò* ([[KR1a0017]]).
