# Liǎo-huì 了慧

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Xī-yán 西巖 ("Western Cliff"). Surname Luó 羅; native of Péng-chí 蓬池 in Péng-zhōu 蓬州 (modern Sìchuān). Per DILA A009257 and the *xíng-zhuàng*: 1198 – Jǐngdìng 3.3.22 (19 April 1262), *shì* 65, *xià* 47.

Tonsured at 19 under Zǔ-dēng 祖燈 of Yù-zhǎng-shān Ān-guó-sì. Studied with Huài-ān Zhào 壞菴照 at Zhāo-jué (Chéngdū), Zhè-wēng Rú-yǎn 浙翁如琰, Gāo-yuán Zǔ-quán 高原祖泉, Shí-tián Fǎ-xūn [[法薰]], and Miào-fēng Zhī-shàn 妙峰之善; dharma-transmission from [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Five abbacies: Píngjiāng Dìng-huì, Wēnzhōu Yànshān Néng-rén, Jiāng-zhōu Dōng-lín, Qìngyuán Tài-bái Tiān-tóng, and Ruì-yán Kāi-shàn. Retired to the Huàn-zhì-ān. His *yǔlù* is [[KR6q0324]]. Principal dharma-heirs: Dōng-yán Jìng-rì 東巖淨日 and [[文明]] Yuè-jiàn Wén-míng 月磵文明 (cf. [[KR6q0325]]).
