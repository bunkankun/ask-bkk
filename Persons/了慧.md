# Liǎohuì 了慧

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Xīyán 西巖 ("Western Cliff"). Surname Luó 羅; native of Péngchí 蓬池 in Péngzhōu 蓬州 (modern Sìchuān). Per DILA A009257 and the *xíngzhuàng*: 1198 – Jǐngdìng 3.3.22 (19 April 1262), *shì* 65, *xià* 47.

Tonsured at 19 under Zǔdēng 祖燈 of Yùzhǎngshān Ānguósì. Studied with Huàiān Zhào 壞菴照 at Zhāojué (Chéngdū), Zhèwēng Rúyǎn 浙翁如琰, Gāoyuán Zǔquán 高原祖泉, Shítián Fǎxūn [[法薰]], and Miàofēng Zhīshàn 妙峰之善; dharma-transmission from [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Five abbacies: Píngjiāng Dìnghuì, Wēnzhōu Yànshān Néngrén, Jiāngzhōu Dōnglín, Qìngyuán Tàibái Tiāntóng, and Ruìyán Kāishàn. Retired to the Huànzhìān. His *yǔlù* is [[KR6q0324]]. Principal dharma-heirs: Dōngyán Jìngrì 東巖淨日 and [[文明]] Yuèjiàn Wénmíng 月磵文明 (cf. [[KR6q0325]]).
