Cáo Hé 曹禾, *hào* Jīān 畸庵 ("the Odd-Numbered Hut"; the *jī* is the Zhuāng-zǐ-tradition "imbalanced / fractional" trope). Mid-19th-century physician-bibliographer of **Yánghú** 陽湖 (Chángzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). The catalog meta gives him as 清; the closing *bá* of [[KR3eq097]] *Yīxué dúshū zhì* by his disciple Liú Rǔháng is dated 1851. Cáo's intellectual trajectory, per Liú's postface: he first studied the Jīn-Yuán *Liú-Zhāng-Lǐ-Zhū* synthesis and the Míng *Xuē Jǐ* / *Wáng Lún* / *Lǐ Shízhēn* corpus, eventually rejected them all as inadequate, and returned to the *yījīng* / *jīngfāng* / *Shānghán* / *Běncǎo* foundational layer — a representative voice of the **late-Qīng *fùgǔ* (return-to-antiquity) medical movement**. Other works: *Yángyī yǎyán* 瘍醫雅言, *Dòuzhěn suǒyǐn* 豆疹索隱. Not in CBDB.
