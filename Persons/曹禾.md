Cáo Hé 曹禾, *hào* Jī-ān 畸庵 ("the Odd-Numbered Hut"; the *jī* is the Zhuāng-zǐ-tradition "imbalanced / fractional" trope). Mid-19th-century physician-bibliographer of **Yáng-hú** 陽湖 (Cháng-zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). The catalog meta gives him as 清; the closing *bá* of [[KR3eq097]] *Yī-xué dú-shū zhì* by his disciple Liú Rǔ-háng is dated 1851. Cáo's intellectual trajectory, per Liú's postface: he first studied the Jīn-Yuán *Liú-Zhāng-Lǐ-Zhū* synthesis and the Míng *Xuē Jǐ* / *Wáng Lún* / *Lǐ Shí-zhēn* corpus, eventually rejected them all as inadequate, and returned to the *yī-jīng* / *jīng-fāng* / *Shāng-hán* / *Běn-cǎo* foundational layer — a representative voice of the **late-Qīng *fù-gǔ* (return-to-antiquity) medical movement**. Other works: *Yáng-yī yǎ-yán* 瘍醫雅言, *Dòu-zhěn suǒ-yǐn* 豆疹索隱. Not in CBDB.
