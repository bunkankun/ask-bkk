Zhēnchuán 真傳, *hào* **Mìyìn** 密印 ("Secret Seal"). Born **天啟乙丑 6/28** = **1625-07-31 Gregorian**; alive in **康熙戊午 = 1678** at age 54. Native of **Xùzhōufǔ** 敘州府 (Sìchuān-Nán Shǔnán); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Língyǐn Yìnwén** 靈隱印文 (in turn dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]]) — i.e., dharma-grandson of Pòshān. Listed alongside **Cāngbì Míngruì** 蒼璧明睿, **Dàtóng Hérén** 大同合仁, **Tiānrú Qīngjué** 天如清覺, **Méixī Fúdù** 梅溪福度 etc. as one of fourteen named *fǎsì* of Yìnwén.

**Formation.** Childhood vegetarian; observed life as illusory; on parents' deaths went to **Guānyīndòng** 觀音洞 to take the cloth; awakening on hearing a bell during night-walking moonlit *jīngxíng* 經行. Full precepts under **Yǔsōng héshàng** 語嵩和尚; *jìbié* 記莂 from **Língyǐn Yìnwén** 靈隱文.

**Three abbacies.** (i) **Húguǎng Huìtóng Huílóng Chányuàn** 湖廣會同迴龍禪院; (ii) **Qíshān Yǒngxīng Chánsì** 旗山永興禪寺; (iii) **Qiányáng Shèngjuésì** 黔陽勝覺寺 — the long-tenure 30+ year seat at **Guǎngfúshān** 廣福山 (Húnán / Guìzhōu border, Qiányáng) whence the title-form *Mìyìn* derives. Per the [[KR6q0489]] preface: *fēixī Chǔnán, zī yù Guǎngfúshān zhě sānshí yú nián yǐ* 飛錫楚南茲寓廣福山者三十餘年矣.

**Heirs.** **Xuějiàn Rúkǎi** 雪澗如暟; **Xiàofēng Rúwěi** [[如暐]] 嘯峰如暐 (compiler of [[KR6q0489]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0489]] 《廣福山勝覺寺密印禪師語錄》 12 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J35 B343, compiled by *fǎsì* **Rúwěi** [[如暐]] *děng biān*; *tǎmíng bēijì* in juan 12. Sources: 《錦江禪燈》 j. 12.
