Zhēn-chuán 真傳, *hào* **Mì-yìn** 密印 ("Secret Seal"). Born **天啟乙丑 6/28** = **1625-07-31 Gregorian**; alive in **康熙戊午 = 1678** at age 54. Native of **Xù-zhōu-fǔ** 敘州府 (Sì-chuān-Nán Shǔ-nán); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Lineage.** Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén** 靈隱印文 (in turn dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]]) — i.e., dharma-grandson of Pòshān. Listed alongside **Cāng-bì Míng-ruì** 蒼璧明睿, **Dà-tóng Hé-rén** 大同合仁, **Tiān-rú Qīng-jué** 天如清覺, **Méi-xī Fú-dù** 梅溪福度 etc. as one of fourteen named *fǎ-sì* of Yìn-wén.

**Formation.** Childhood vegetarian; observed life as illusory; on parents' deaths went to **Guān-yīn-dòng** 觀音洞 to take the cloth; awakening on hearing a bell during night-walking moonlit *jīng-xíng* 經行. Full precepts under **Yǔ-sōng héshàng** 語嵩和尚; *jì-bié* 記莂 from **Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén** 靈隱文.

**Three abbacies.** (i) **Hú-guǎng Huì-tóng Huí-lóng Chán-yuàn** 湖廣會同迴龍禪院; (ii) **Qí-shān Yǒng-xīng Chán-sì** 旗山永興禪寺; (iii) **Qián-yáng Shèng-jué-sì** 黔陽勝覺寺 — the long-tenure 30+ year seat at **Guǎng-fú-shān** 廣福山 (Hú-nán / Guì-zhōu border, Qián-yáng) whence the title-form *Mì-yìn* derives. Per the [[KR6q0489]] preface: *fēi-xī Chǔ-nán, zī yù Guǎng-fú-shān zhě sān-shí yú nián yǐ* 飛錫楚南茲寓廣福山者三十餘年矣.

**Heirs.** **Xuě-jiàn Rú-kǎi** 雪澗如暟; **Xiào-fēng Rú-wěi** [[如暐]] 嘯峰如暐 (compiler of [[KR6q0489]]).

**Work.** [[KR6q0489]] 《廣福山勝覺寺密印禪師語錄》 12 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B343, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Rú-wěi** [[如暐]] *děng biān*; *tǎ-míng bēi-jì* in juan 12. Sources: 《錦江禪燈》 j. 12.
