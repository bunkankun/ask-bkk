Qín Zhì'ān 秦志安 (Daoist appellations Tōngzhēnzǐ 通真子, Chángchūn hútiān línjiān yùkè 長春壺天林間羽客, and Shùlí dàorén 樗櫟道人) was the principal early historian of the Quánzhēn 全真 order in the years immediately after the Mongol conquest of Jīn. He joined Sòng Défāng 宋德方 (1183–1247) in Hénán to take part in the editorial board compiling the *Xuándū bǎozàng* 玄都寶藏 (the Yuán-era canon, later destroyed), and inserted into it his own lineage-history of Quánzhēn under the title *Jīnlián zhèngzōng jì* 金蓮正宗記 ("Record of the True Line of Transmission of the Golden Lotus School" — see [[KR5a0174]] / DZ 173), the preface of which he signed from Píngshuǐ 平水 in the *xīnchǒu* year (1241). His activity is also reported in Yuán Hǎowèn's 元好問 *Yíshān xiānshēng wénjí* 遺山先生文集 31 ("Tōngzhēn zǐ mùbēi míng" 通真子墓碑銘). No CBDB record located.
