**Chén Nán** 陳楠 (*d.* 1213), styled Cuì xū zhēn rén 翠虛真人 and Ní wán wēng 泥丸翁, was the **fourth patriarch of the Southern Lineage** (*Nán zōng* 南宗) of Daoist inner alchemy, disciple of Xuē Dàoguāng and teacher of [[白玉蟾]] Bái Yùchán. Noted both as a *nèi dān* transmitter and as a ritual-thunder specialist. Author of [[KR5d0113]] *Cuì xū piān* 翠虛篇.
