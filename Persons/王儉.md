# Wáng Jiǎn 王儉

*Zì* Zhòngbǎo 仲寶. Southern Qí 南齊 statesman and bibliographer, native of Lángyé Línyí 琅邪臨沂 (modern Shāndōng). Author of the *Qīzhì* 七志 ("Seven Records"), a seven-part bibliographic catalogue (compiled ca. 472) that supplemented Liú Xīn's 劉歆 *Qīlüè* 七略 with Buddhist and Daoist divisions; an important precursor to [[阮孝緒]] Ruǎn Xiàoxù's *Qīlù* 七錄 of the Liáng. The *Qīzhì* is no longer extant but is quoted extensively in [[陸德明]] Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 and in the *Suíshū jīngjí zhì* derivation. *Nán Qí shū* 南齊書 (juan 23) gives his biography.
