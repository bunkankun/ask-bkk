Jiǎ Sōng 賈嵩 (signing himself **Bìluó rúzǐ** 薜蘿孺子, "Child of the Briar Vines") is the compiler of the *Huáyáng Táo yǐnjū nèizhuàn* 華陽陶隱居內傳 ([[KR5a0312]] / DZ 300), a three-juan biography of Táo Hóngjǐng 陶弘景 (456–536). The text was for long dated by Qīng-period scholars (e.g., Yè Déhuī's 葉德輝 1903 edition in *Guāngǔ táng huìkè shū* 觀古堂彙刻書) to the Sòng dynasty; Ursula-Angelika Cedzich, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:428–429, argues with some plausibility that he is identical with the late-Táng prose writer of the same name whose *Xián kěwèi fù* 閒可畏賦 is preserved in *Wényuàn yīnghuá* 文苑英華 5.9a–10a. On that identification, he flourished in the late ninth or early tenth century. He is also the author of the prefatorial signature appended to the *Huáyáng Táo yǐnjū nèizhuàn*. Beyond these sparse data and the present biography, no further information is preserved. The CBDB returns several persons of this name; none can be confidently matched.
