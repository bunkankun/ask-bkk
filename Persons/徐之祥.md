Xú Zhīxiáng 徐之祥, Yuán-period scholar, author of one of the three Yuán-period prefaces (alongside [[虞集]] Yú Jí and [[馬端臨]] Mǎ Duānlín) to the *Dàdé dīngwèi* (1307) Qíān 齊安 prefectural-school reprint of [[項安世]] Xiàng Ānshì's *Zhōuyì wán cí* ([[KR1a0038]]). The three prefaces are preserved at the head of the *Sìkù* base-text. Otherwise undocumented.
