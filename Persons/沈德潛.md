# Shěn Déqián 沈德潛

*Zì* Quèshì 確士; *hào* Guīyú 歸愚; posthumous title Wénquè 文慤. Native of Chángzhōu 長洲 (modern Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Qiánlóng* 4 (1739) at the unusually advanced age of 67 — having failed the metropolitan examination 16 times before. Lifedates 1673–1769 (96 years).

One of the most influential Qing literary critics and anthologists of the eighteenth century. Held *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士, *Lǐbù shìláng* 禮部侍郎, *Tàizǐ shǎoshī* 太子少師. Founder of the *Géliào pài* 格律派 of Qing poetic theory ("strict observation of regulated form"). His three great anthologies — the *Gǔ shī yuán* 古詩源 (1719, *Yùxuǎn gǔ shī yuán*), the *Tang shī biéshí jí* 唐詩別裁集 (1717, expanded 1763), and the *Míng shī biéshí jí* 明詩別裁集 (1739) — were the canonical poetic anthologies of the late Qing and early Republican period and shaped the standard Tang and Ming poetic canons through the early 20th century.

As a Wǔyīngdiàn editor, Shěn Déqián was the principal *kǎozhèng* compiler for the *Jiù Tángshū* ([[KR2a0026]]) — 88 *juǎn* of collation notes — and contributed to the *kǎozhèng* of the *Xīn Tángshū* ([[KR2a0027]]) and the *Jiù Wǔdài shǐ* ([[KR2a0029]]). His *kǎozhèng* work was substantial.

Posthumously implicated in the *Xú Shùkuí* 徐述夔 case under the *wénzì yù* 文字獄 of 1778; his ranks were posthumously revoked, his name removed from the *Xiánliáng cí*, and his collected works were proscribed — though restored later under the Jiāqìng emperor.

His biography is in *Qīng shǐ gǎo* 305 ([[KR2a0039]]).
