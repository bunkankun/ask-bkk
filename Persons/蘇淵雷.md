# Sū Yuānléi 蘇淵雷

Sū Yuānléi (1908–1995), modern Chinese philosopher and historian of Buddhism. Editor of the authoritative critical edition of the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* 五燈會元 (3 vols., Zhōnghuá Shūjú, 1984).
