# 彌伽釋迦

Míjiā Shìjiā (Skt. perhaps *Meghaśikha*). Indian monk recorded as the oral interpreter (譯語) collaborating with **Pāramiti** 般剌蜜帝 and **Fáng Róng** [[房融]] in the 705 Guǎngzhōu translation of the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]], T19n0945). Nothing else of his biography is preserved.
