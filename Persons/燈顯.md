Dēng-xiǎn 燈顯, *zì* **Pǔ-mén** 普門 ("Universal Gate"); often referred to as **Pǔ-mén Dēng-xiǎn** 普門燈顯. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668), thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]], [[玄億]], [[至善]], [[幻敏]] in the **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Fú-líng** 涪陵 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Xià** 夏.

**Formation.** Began as a young disciple at a *yìng-yuàn* 應院 (a service-monastery). First *cān*-ed under **Wàn-sōng** 萬松禪師 for years; when Wàn-sōng died, transferred to **Tiě-bì** 鐵壁禪師 and received final *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies (two).** **Fú-líng Huí-lóng-sì** 涪陵迴龍寺; **Tóng-bǎi Tán-huá-sì** 桐柏曇華寺.

**Disciples.** **Xìng-yìn** [[性胤]] (recorder), **Xìng-zài** 性在 (*fǎ-sì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0593]] 《普門顯禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B485, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-yìn** [[性胤]] *lù*.

**Sources.** 《普門顯禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈普門顯禪師塔銘〉; 《續燈正統》 vol. 18.
