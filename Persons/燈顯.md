Dēngxiǎn 燈顯, *zì* **Pǔmén** 普門 ("Universal Gate"); often referred to as **Pǔmén Dēngxiǎn** 普門燈顯. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668), thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]], [[玄億]], [[至善]], [[幻敏]] in the **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Fúlíng** 涪陵 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Xià** 夏.

**Formation.** Began as a young disciple at a *yìngyuàn* 應院 (a service-monastery). First *cān*-ed under **Wànsōng** 萬松禪師 for years; when Wànsōng died, transferred to **Tiěbì** 鐵壁禪師 and received final *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies (two).** **Fúlíng Huílóngsì** 涪陵迴龍寺; **Tóngbǎi Tánhuásì** 桐柏曇華寺.

**Disciples.** **Xìngyìn** [[性胤]] (recorder), **Xìngzài** 性在 (*fǎsì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0593]] 《普門顯禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B485, recorded by *fǎsì* **Xìngyìn** [[性胤]] *lù*.

**Sources.** 《普門顯禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈普門顯禪師塔銘〉; 《續燈正統》 vol. 18.
