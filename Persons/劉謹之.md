Liú Jǐn-zhī 劉謹之 (1739–1787), Qīng official of the Qiánlóng era. He is recorded as *Lǐ-kē zhǎng-yìn jǐ-shì-zhōng* 禮科掌印給事中 ("Supervising Secretary in charge of the Seal of the Office of Scrutiny for Rites") at the time of the compilation of the *Qīndìng Shèngjīng tōngzhì* 欽定盛京通志 ([[KR2k0040]]), in which he served as one of the two Hàn *zǒng-zuǎn* 總纂 alongside Chéng Wéi-yuè 程維岳, *Lì-bù kǎo-gōng-sī yuán-wài-láng* 吏部考功司員外郎. CBDB c_personid 75336. Birth and death dates per the *Qīng-dài rén-wù shēng-zú nián-biǎo*. His standard dynastic-history biography is brief; his major editorial role is the *Shèngjīng tōngzhì* compilation. Native place not securely transmitted in the references consulted.
