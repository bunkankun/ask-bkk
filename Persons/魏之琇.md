**Wèi Zhīxiù** 魏之琇 (*zì* Yùhuáng 玉璜, 1722–1772, 清), Qīng-period scholar-physician. Annotator (考證) of the [[江瓘]] Jiāng Guàn — [[江應宿]] Jiāng Yìngsù ***Míngyī lèiàn*** 名醫類案 ([[KR3e0075]]) for the SKQS recension; further author of the ***Xù míngyī lèiàn*** 續名醫類案 (Continuation of *Míngyī lèiàn*, 60 juan, completed 1770) — the principal Qīng-period continuation of the *Míngyī lèiàn* tradition, gathering Qīng-period case-records.
