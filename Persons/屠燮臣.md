# 屠燮臣

Tú Xièchén of Huánggāng 黃岡 (Húběi), mid-Qīng physician credited (in a *Tóngzhì* 2 = 1863 colophon preserved in [[KR3em041]] *Hóushé bèiyào mìzhǐ* 喉舌備要秘旨) with an anti-opium-addiction formula (*Zhì yāpiàn yānyǐn qíxiào fāng*). Likely a relative of [[屠道和]] Tú Dàohé.
