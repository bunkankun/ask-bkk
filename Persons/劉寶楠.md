# 劉寶楠

**Liú Bǎonán** (*zì* Chǔzhēn 楚楨, *hào* Niànlóu 念樓, 1791–1855), Bǎoyìng 寶應 (Jiāngsū) Confucian scholar of the Yángzhōu School (*Yángzhōu xuépài* 揚州學派); *jìnshì* of *Dàoguāng* 20 (1840) and prefect of several Zhílì counties. Best known as author of the *Lúnyǔ zhèngyì* 論語正義, the standard Qīng-evidential-philology commentary on the *Analects*, completed by his son [[劉恭冕]] Liú Gōngmiǎn after his death and printed in 1866. *Qīng shǐ liè zhuàn* 67. He contributed a sixtieth-birthday poem to the 1836 *editio princeps* of [[KR3el010]] *Diēsǔn miàofāng* in honour of his fellow-townsman [[孫應科]] Sūn Yìngkē, whose recovery from a broken arm had occasioned the publication.
