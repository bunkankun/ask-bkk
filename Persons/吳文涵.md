**Wú Wénhán** 吳文涵, Republican-era Jiāngyīn 江陰 *hòuxué* who transcribed [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng's [[KR3eh053]] *Lìjí míngbiàn* in 1922 (Mínguó 11) and supplied one of the three prefaces preserved in the 1940 first imprint. His preface transmits anecdotal biography of Wú Shìyīng.
