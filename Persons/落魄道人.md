Luòpò Dàorén 落魄道人 ("the Down-and-Out Daoist") is the pen name of the unidentified author of *Fùwēng Xǐng Shì Lù* 富翁醒世錄 ([[KR4k0117]]), a 16-chapter Qīng didactic novel on the moral dangers of avarice. No biographical information is recoverable from the text, and no external source has identified the historical person behind this pseudonym.
