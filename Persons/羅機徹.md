# Luó Jīchè 羅機徹

Late-17th-century Qīng lay Buddhist; compiler (*biān* 編) of [[陸瑛|Lù Yīng]]'s *Tiáoshí jūshì zhèng yuán lù* 調實居士證源錄 [[KR6q0230]]. Likely a lineage-brother (*tóng mén fǎ dì* 同門法弟) to Lù Yīng in the Jīnmíng Jiè teaching-community. Lifedates unrecorded.
