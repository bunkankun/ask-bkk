# Shàndào 善道 (Tang, fl. late 7th c.?)

A Tang Pure Land monk, co-compiler with [[道鏡]] Dàojìng of the *Niànfó jìng* 念佛鏡 [[KR6p0045]] (T47N1966). Lifedates not preserved. The Taishō colophon assigns him as 共集 ("jointly compiled"). DILA Authority id A001298.

**He is not the same person as [[善導]] Shàndǎo (613–681)**, the great Tang Pure Land patriarch — the names use different characters (道 vs. 道, but the first character differs: 善導 with 導 vs. 善道 with 道). The two are routinely confused in pre-modern catalogue traditions, including some Sòng-era references; the modern DILA Authority distinguishes them.

He is otherwise undocumented in the standard biographical literature.
