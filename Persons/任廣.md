Rèn Guǎng 任廣 (fl. *Chóng-níng* 崇寧 1102–1106), *zì* Dé-jiǎn 德儉, native of Jùn-yí 浚儀 (modern Kāi-fēng). The *Wén-xiàn tōng-kǎo*'s record of "Rèn Jùn 任浚" is an error noted in the *Sìkù tíyào*. No biographical material survives in the *Sòng shǐ*. He was a Northern-Sòng scholar of the late Huī-zōng court who did not cross over into the Southern Sòng. His one surviving work is the *Shū-xù zhǐ-nán* 書敘指南 ([[KR3k0016]]), a 20-*juan* phraseological handbook for *chǐ-dú* composition. The work was first printed at Jìng-kāng (1126–7) but the blocks were burnt almost immediately in the Jīn sack; a single Wú copy preserved the text into the Southern-Sòng catalogue tradition.
