Rèn Guǎng 任廣 (fl. *Chóngníng* 崇寧 1102–1106), *zì* Déjiǎn 德儉, native of Jùnyí 浚儀 (modern Kāifēng). The *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo*'s record of "Rèn Jùn 任浚" is an error noted in the *Sìkù tíyào*. No biographical material survives in the *Sòng shǐ*. He was a Northern-Sòng scholar of the late Huīzōng court who did not cross over into the Southern Sòng. His one surviving work is the *Shūxù zhǐnán* 書敘指南 ([[KR3k0016]]), a 20-*juan* phraseological handbook for *chǐdú* composition. The work was first printed at Jìngkāng (1126–7) but the blocks were burnt almost immediately in the Jīn sack; a single Wú copy preserved the text into the Southern-Sòng catalogue tradition.
