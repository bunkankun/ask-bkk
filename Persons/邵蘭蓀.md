Shào Lánsūn 邵蘭蓀 (1855–1919) — leading Shàoxīng 紹興 (Zhèjiāng) clinician of the late Qīng and early Republican period, one of the eight Shàoxīng physicians of the late Qīng often grouped as the *Shàoxīng wēnbìng bājiā* 紹興溫病八家. He worked in private practice in Shàoxīng across the 1880s–1910s. His characteristic clinical signature is a Shàoxīng-region development of the Yè Tiānshì 葉天士 ([[葉桂]]) warm-disease (*wēnbìng* 溫病) tradition emphasising damp-heat (*shīrè* 濕熱) patterns appropriate to the humid Zhèjiāng climate and the use of light, aromatic, dispersing prescriptions in preference to heavier dose-forms.

His clinical records were edited posthumously by his disciples into [[KR3ep004]] *Shào Lánsūn yīàn* 邵蘭蓀醫案. His cases are also prominently anthologised in He Liánchén's 何廉臣 [[KR3ep099]] *Quánguó míngyī yànàn lèibiān* 全國名醫驗案類編 (1921). CBDB and DILA have no entry.
