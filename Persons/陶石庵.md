# Táo Shíān 陶石庵

Late-Qīng / early-Republican local pediatric *tuīná* practitioner of Xìnyáng 信陽 (Hénán); the teacher of [[塗蔚生]] Tú Wèishēng, named in the self-preface to [[KR3ee053]] *Tuīná juéwēi* (1928) as the *tuīná* master under whom Tú apprenticed after his Confucian-to-medical conversion. No further biographical detail survives.
