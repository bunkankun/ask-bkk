Fàn Zhòng-yān 范仲淹 (989–1052), zì *Xī-wén* 希文, posthumous *Wén-zhèng* 文正, was the leading Northern Sòng statesman, military commander, and reformer of the early-mid eleventh century. His ancestral home was Bīn-zhōu 邠州 in the northwest, but his family migrated south after his father's death and he was raised in Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu). He passed the *jìn-shì* in *Dà-zhōng xiáng-fú* 8 (1015) and rose through *jiàn* (remonstrance) and frontier-commander posts to *Zī-zhèng diàn xué-shì* 資政殿學士, *Hù-bù shì-láng* 戶部侍郎, and *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* 參知政事. After death he was promoted *Bīng-bù shàng-shū* 兵部尚書 with the posthumous *Wén-zhèng* 文正 — a title bestowed on him as the first scholar-official since Wáng Dàn 王旦 (957–1017) to receive it, and one that thereafter became the highest standard of Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng official reputation.

His tenure as *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* under Rén-zōng (*Qìng-lì* 3–5 = 1043–1045) saw the famous *Qìng-lì xīn zhèng* 慶曆新政 (Qìng-lì Reforms) — ten administrative measures developed in the *Tiān-zhāng gé* 天章閣 imperial-question session and condensed into Fàn's *Dá zhào tiáo-chén shí shì* 答詔條陳十事 memorial. The reforms tightened evaluation of officials, restricted the *rèn-zǐ* 任子 (yīn) hereditary appointment privilege, and reorganized the examination system; they were sharply opposed by entrenched interests and Fàn was driven from office in 1045. He spent his last years on the northwest frontier as *Shǎn-xī sì-lù xuān-fǔ shǐ* 陜西四路宣撫使, dying in office in *Huáng-yòu* 4 (1052).

Fàn's writings remain canonical in the Chinese tradition: the *Yuè-yáng-lóu jì* 岳陽樓記 with its line "*xiān tiānxià zhī yōu ér yōu, hòu tiānxià zhī lè ér lè*" 先天下之憂而憂後天下之樂而樂 ("be the first to worry the realm's worries, the last to enjoy its joys") — the moral motto of subsequent Confucian administrative culture. His reformist memorials are gathered in the [[KR2f0012]] *Fàn Wén-zhèng zòu-yì* 范文正奏議 in 2 *juàn* (with prefaces by Hán Qí 韓琦 in 1053). His full collected works are the *Fàn Wén-zhèng gōng jí* 范文正公集 ([[KR4d0079]] in 24 *juàn*, with multiple supplements). *Sòng shǐ* j. 314 has his biography. CBDB id 8043. He is the maternal great-grandfather of his successor-generation reformer Sī-mǎ Guāng 司馬光.
