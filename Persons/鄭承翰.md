# 鄭承翰

Zhèng Chénghàn (*hào* Shūfú 樞扶), younger brother of the Xīn'ān 新安 laryngologist [[鄭宏綱]] Zhèng Hónggāng (Méijiàn 梅澗). Edited the manuscript of [[KR3em017]] *Chónglóu yùyuè* 重樓玉鑰 after his elder brother's death (1787) and contributed the embedded *Shūfúshì yuē* 樞扶氏曰 commentary.
