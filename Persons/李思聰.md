**Lǐ Sīcōng** 李思聰 was a Northern-Sòng Daoist author, compiler of [[KR5d0086]] *Dòng yuān jí* 洞淵集 (c. 1050) — a Daoist poetic-philosophical collection. His *Jìn dòng tiān hǎi yuè biǎo* 進洞天海嶽表 ([[KR5d0085]], 1050) is the accompanying submission memorial.
