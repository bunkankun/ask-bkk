# 海順 Kaijun

Japanese Tendai 天台 monk who travelled to Sòng China together with [[延慶]] Enkei to study with [[法照]] Huìyán Fǎzhào at Hángzhōu Shàngtiānzhúsì. The two had Fǎzhào's portrait painted and brought it back to Japan, where the *Fǎhuá jīng sāndàbù dújiào jì* ([[KR6d0062]]) was subsequently studied in the medieval Tendai tradition.
