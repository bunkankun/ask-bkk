Yínméi Shānrén 吟梅山人 ("Mountain-Man Who Sings of the Plum Blossom") is the pen name of the author of the late-Qīng vernacular novels *Lánhuā Mèng* 蘭花夢 ([[KR4k0168]]) and *Lánhuā Mèng Qízhuàn* 蘭花夢奇傳 ([[KR4k0169]]). The real identity behind this pen name is not established in standard reference works. The novels' preface was written by "Yānbō Sǎnrén" 煙波散人 in 1905 in Shànghǎi, suggesting that Yínméi Shānrén was active in the Shanghai commercial fiction milieu in the first decade of the twentieth century.
