Chóng 崇 (full dharma-name **Xìng-chóng** 性崇), *zì* **Dà-xiào** 大笑 ("Great Laugh"); often referred to as **Dà-xiào Chóng** 大笑崇. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gāo-fēng Sān-shān Dēng-lái** 高峰三山燈來 [[燈來]] (1614–1685). Born **明崇禎癸酉腊八日** = **1634-01-07** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 56 in **康熙戊辰** = **1688**. Native of **Shùn-qìng-fǔ Yuè-chí-xiàn** 順慶府岳池縣 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Lǔ** 魯.

**Formation.** At 5, while accompanying his father studying at **Yā-shān-sì** 鴨山寺, heard a monk reading the *Huá-yán jīng* and the daily liturgy — wept "as if rain." Always preferred to play with the *shā-mí* and dreaded going home. **甲申** = **1644** during the Sì-chuān civil war was abducted to **Chuān-dōng** 川東. **壬辰** = **1652** the regional commander **Yuán Bǎo-shàn** 袁寶善居士 brought him to **Gāo-fēng lǎo-rén** 高峰老人 (= 三山燈來) for tonsure. Given the *gōng-àn* "*niàn-fó shì shuí*" 念佛是誰 ("Who is reciting the Buddha's name?"); after long practice, had insight. **丁未** = **1667** received final *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Kuí-zhōu-fǔ Wàn-xiàn Guān-yīn-shān Bǎo-jī-sì** 夔州府萬縣觀音山寶積寺 and **Zhōng-zhōu Gāo-fēng chán-yuàn** 忠州高峰禪院.

**Work.** [[KR6q0557]] 《大笑崇禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B442, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Kōng-qíng** [[空情]] *lù*; with prefaces dated **康熙庚午** = **1690** lunar 9 and **康熙壬申** = **1692-02-04** (the latter signed 莆陽超原澹崖氏 — i.e. [[超源]] of Mǐn, the same Lóng-chí-line preface-writer of [[KR6q0554]]).

**Sources.** 《大笑崇禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉; 《別菴禪師同門錄》 vol. 2 (in J39 B445).
