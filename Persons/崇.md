Chóng 崇 (full dharma-name **Xìngchóng** 性崇), *zì* **Dàxiào** 大笑 ("Great Laugh"); often referred to as **Dàxiào Chóng** 大笑崇. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gāofēng Sānshān Dēnglái** 高峰三山燈來 [[燈來]] (1614–1685). Born **明崇禎癸酉腊八日** = **1634-01-07** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 56 in **康熙戊辰** = **1688**. Native of **Shùnqìngfǔ Yuèchíxiàn** 順慶府岳池縣 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Lǔ** 魯.

**Formation.** At 5, while accompanying his father studying at **Yāshānsì** 鴨山寺, heard a monk reading the *Huáyán jīng* and the daily liturgy — wept "as if rain." Always preferred to play with the *shāmí* and dreaded going home. **甲申** = **1644** during the Sìchuān civil war was abducted to **Chuāndōng** 川東. **壬辰** = **1652** the regional commander **Yuán Bǎoshàn** 袁寶善居士 brought him to **Gāofēng lǎorén** 高峰老人 (= 三山燈來) for tonsure. Given the *gōngàn* "*niànfó shì shuí*" 念佛是誰 ("Who is reciting the Buddha's name?"); after long practice, had insight. **丁未** = **1667** received final *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Kuízhōufǔ Wànxiàn Guānyīnshān Bǎojīsì** 夔州府萬縣觀音山寶積寺 and **Zhōngzhōu Gāofēng chányuàn** 忠州高峰禪院.

**Work.** [[KR6q0557]] 《大笑崇禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B442, recorded by *fǎsì* **Kōngqíng** [[空情]] *lù*; with prefaces dated **康熙庚午** = **1690** lunar 9 and **康熙壬申** = **1692-02-04** (the latter signed 莆陽超原澹崖氏 — i.e. [[超源]] of Mǐn, the same Lóng-chí-line preface-writer of [[KR6q0554]]).

**Sources.** 《大笑崇禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉; 《別菴禪師同門錄》 vol. 2 (in J39 B445).
