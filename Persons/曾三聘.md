Zēng Sānpìn 曾三聘 (1144–1210), *zì* Wúyì 無逸. Native of Jíshuǐ 吉水, Lúlíng 廬陵 prefecture (mod. Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Qiándào* 5 (1169). Eldest son of [[曾敏行]] Zēng Mǐnxíng (1118–1175). Held posts as magistrate of *Yǒngxīn* 永新 and *Tóngshān* 桐山, vice-prefect of *Quánzhōu* 泉州, and later *Tàifǔsì shǎoqīng* 太府寺少卿. On his father's death in 1175 he gathered the manuscripts left behind and arranged them into the ten-*juàn* *Dúxǐng zázhì* [[KR3l0073]], the work of his father's life; he obtained prefaces and postfaces from the leading Jiāngxī literati of the day — Yáng Wànlǐ wrote the preface in 1185, with postfaces by Xiè È, Zhào Rǔyú, Zhōu Bìdà, and Lóu Yuè following. His own writings have not survived independently; he is principally remembered as the filial transmitter of his father's collection.
