# Zǔrì 祖日

Southern Sòng Cáodòng monk, attendant of [[如淨|Tiāntóng Rújìng]] 天童如淨 (1163–1228) at the Tiāntóng Jǐngdé sì abbacy; recorded as compiler of the opening Tiāntóng section in juan 2 of [[KR6q0071]] *Rújìng héshàng yǔlù*.
