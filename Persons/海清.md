Hǎi-qīng 海清. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě*) of **Xǐ-xīn Zhào-shuǐ** [[照水]] at **Xiāng-yán-sì** 香巖寺. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler with **Shí-yún** [[實雲]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0572]] 《香巖洗心水禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B459.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the early-Qīng imperial commissioner **Hǎi-qīng** 海清 (DILA A009748), who in **Kāngxī 41** = **1702** accompanied **Prince Yìn-qí** 胤祺 (1680–1732) to É-méi-shān to bestow imperial books, plaques, and poems on the local monasteries.
