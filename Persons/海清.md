Hǎiqīng 海清. Personal attendant (*shìzhě*) of **Xǐxīn Zhàoshuǐ** [[照水]] at **Xiāngyánsì** 香巖寺. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler with **Shíyún** [[實雲]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0572]] 《香巖洗心水禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B459.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the early-Qīng imperial commissioner **Hǎiqīng** 海清 (DILA A009748), who in **Kāngxī 41** = **1702** accompanied **Prince Yìnqí** 胤祺 (1680–1732) to Éméishān to bestow imperial books, plaques, and poems on the local monasteries.
