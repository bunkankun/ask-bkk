# Xīqiān 希遷 (Shítóu Xīqiān 石頭希遷)

Mid-Tang Chan master, dharma-heir of Qīngyuán Xíngsī 青原行思 and one of the two principal lineage-founders (with Mǎzǔ Dàoyī 馬祖道一) of the post-Huìnéng Southern Chan tradition. Author of the *Cān tóng qì* 參同契. His lineage gave rise to the Cáodòng, Yúnmén, and Fǎyǎn houses of Sòng Chan. Posthumous title Wújì Dàshī 無際大師. Native of Duānzhōu 端州 in Guǎngdōng; resided on Héngshān 衡山, where the stone-platform from which he took the *hào* "Shítóu" 石頭 ("Stone-head") is preserved.
