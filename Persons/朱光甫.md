Zhū Guāng-fǔ 朱光甫, Yuán-period *shù-shì* 術士 (technical specialist) in the *nà-yīn* 納音 (sexagenary-cycle tonal assignments). Named by Wú Chéng 吳澄 (Wú Cǎo-lú 吳草廬, 1249–1333) as a competent expositor of the *nà-yīn* theory. His treatise on *nà-yīn shì-yì* 納音釋義 — the assignment of the five-phase identifications to the sixty stem-branch combinations (e.g. *Jiǎ-zǐ Yǐ-chǒu = Hǎi-zhōng jīn* "Gold-in-the-Sea") — survives only because **[[程林]]** Chéng Lín appended it to [[KR3eq088]] *Yī-xiá zhī-yán* (early Qīng) as the most reliable account he had found. Not in CBDB.
