Sū Yàn 蘇彥 (*fl.* 4th century) was an Eastern Jìn 東晉 literatus, best known in the broader bibliographic record as the putative author of the early *Bù qián tiān* 步天歌 (the astronomical-mnemonic verse later associated with the Suí–Táng astrologer Wáng Xīmíng 王希明). The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 attributes a *Sūzǐ* 蘇子 to him. His exact birth and death years and his office record are not preserved in the standard histories; the dynastic placement under Jìn follows the *Suí shū* and the *Yìwén lèijù*. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0138]] *Sūzǐ* 蘇子.
