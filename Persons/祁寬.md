Qí Kuān 祁寬 (*zì* Jūzhī 居之), Southern-Sòng disciple of [[尹焞]] Yǐn Tūn (Héjìng 和靖, 1071–1142) and a transmitter of the Chéng-school *Yì*-pedagogical tradition. The [[尹焞]] Yǐn → Qí Kuān transmission account is preserved in *Dà Yì cuì yán* 大易粹言 ([[KR1a0041]]).
