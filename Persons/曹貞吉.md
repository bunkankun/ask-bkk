Cáo Zhēn-jí 曹貞吉 (1634–1698), *zì* Shēng-liù 升六 (or Yǔ-bǐng 禹捧), *hào* Shí-ān 實庵 and (after his most famous collection) Kē-xuě 珂雪, of Ān-qiū 安丘 in Shān-dōng. *Jìn-shì* of *Kāng-xī* 3 (1664); rose to *Lì-bù-láng-zhōng* 吏部郎中. Member of the great early-*Kāng-xī* *cí* revival circle around Chén Wéi-sōng 陳維崧 (the *Yáng-xiàn-pài* 陽羨派) and Zhū Yí-zūn [[朱彝尊]] (the *Zhè-xī-pài* 浙西派); his *Kē-xuě cí* [[KR4j0061]] in two *juǎn* is the textbook early-Qīng *cí* collection, and the *Sìkù* editors single him out as the only practising *cí*-poet of the Qīng included in the *Quán-shū* in his own right (and on the strength of his *cí*, not his *shī*).
