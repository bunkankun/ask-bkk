Cáo Zhēnjí 曹貞吉 (1634–1698), *zì* Shēngliù 升六 (or Yǔbǐng 禹捧), *hào* Shíān 實庵 and (after his most famous collection) Kēxuě 珂雪, of Ānqiū 安丘 in Shāndōng. *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 3 (1664); rose to *Lìbùlángzhōng* 吏部郎中. Member of the great early-*Kāngxī* *cí* revival circle around Chén Wéisōng 陳維崧 (the *Yángxiànpài* 陽羨派) and Zhū Yízūn [[朱彝尊]] (the *Zhèxīpài* 浙西派); his *Kēxuě cí* [[KR4j0061]] in two *juǎn* is the textbook early-Qīng *cí* collection, and the *Sìkù* editors single him out as the only practising *cí*-poet of the Qīng included in the *Quánshū* in his own right (and on the strength of his *cí*, not his *shī*).
