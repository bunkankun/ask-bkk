**Benchō** 辨阿 (also written 弁阿; lay name **Mōri Senchiyo-maru** 毛利千代丸; dharma-name **Benchō** 弁長; common designation **Shōkōbō Benchō** 聖光房辨阿 — frequently **Shōkō** 聖光 for short), **second patriarch** (二祖) of the **Chinzei** 鎮西 line of Jōdoshū 浄土宗 and the principal direct successor of [[源空]] **Hōnen**. Posthumously titled **Daishō Shōshū Kokushi** 大紹正宗國師 by Emperor Meiji (1885). Born in Kaō 2 (Ōhō 2) / 1162 in **Kaga-no-shō Yamada** 加賀庄山田 in Chikuzen 筑前 province (modern Ukiha, Fukuoka). Lived to age 77, dying in Ryakunin 1 / 2 / 29 = 1238-04-15.

Initially trained on Hieizan in Tendai-shū under **Kanchō** 觀朝 and **Shōshin** 證眞 (1173–c. 1212). Returned to Chikuzen on his mother's death, and from 1190 was active as a Tendai-school monk in Kyūshū, building temples including Hōonji 報恩寺 in his native Yamada. In **Kennin 1 / 1197** at age 36 — and after his younger brother's accidental death prompted a religious crisis — he went to Kyoto and entered the assembly of **Hōnen** at Yoshimizu 吉水, where he studied for seven years and received Hōnen's dharma-transmission, including the *Senchakushū* 選擇集 (in 1201 according to traditional dates).

Returned to Kyūshū c. 1204 and spent the rest of his life there, establishing the doctrinal centre of the Chinzei line at **Zendō-ji** 善導寺 in Chikugo 筑後 (founded 1208) and **Kōmyō-ji** 光明寺 in Chikuzen, attracting disciples including [[良忠]] **Ryōchū** (third patriarch), Renjaku 蓮寂, and Bencha 弁長. The Chinzei line — by way of Ryōchū and the Kantō expansion of the thirteenth century — became the **mainstream of Jōdoshū** as institutionally constituted in the Edo period and through to the present, with Zōjō-ji 増上寺 and Chion'in 知恩院 as principal head temples.

Principal works: **Tetsu senchaku hongan nenbutsu shū** 徹選擇本願念佛集 ([[KR6t0315]], completed 1237 at age 76); **Matsudai nenbutsu jushuin** 末代念佛授手印 ([[KR6t0319]]); **Jōdo shūyō shū** 浄土宗要集 (*Tōshūyō* 東宗要; T 2607); **Kanmuryōjukyō myōgenshō** 觀無量壽經妙玄章. Wikidata Q11328856.
