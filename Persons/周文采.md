**Zhōu Wéncǎi** 周文采 (*fl.* Hóngzhì period, late 15th c., 明), Míng-period hereditary physician (世業醫) in the household of the Prince of Xīngxiàn 興獻王 (Zhū Yòuyuán 朱祐杬, father of the Jiājìng Emperor); held the office of Vice-Medical-Officer (*liángyī fù* 良醫副) in the princely *liángyī suǒ* 良醫所. Two works survive, both compiled under princely command: the ten-*juǎn* *Yīfāng xuǎn yào* 醫方選要 (1496) on internal medicine, and the two-*juǎn* [[KR3ek006]] *Wàikē jíyàn fāng* 外科集驗方 (1498) on external medicine. Both works are representative late-Hóng-zhì *wángfǔ* 王府 (princely-court) medical compilations. Not in CBDB.
