# 程瘦樵

Chéng Shòuqiáo, mid-Qīng throat-discipline practitioner; compiler of the *dìngběn* of the *Hóukē jīngyàn mìchuán* 喉科經驗秘傳, the standard pre-1839 manuscript laryngology manual displaced by the publication of [[KR3em017]] *Chónglóu yùyuè* 重樓玉鑰.
