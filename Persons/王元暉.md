Early-Yuán 元 Daoist scholar, active c. 1312. Author of the commentary on the *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* 常清靜經 preserved as [[KR5c0153|DZ 757]], published under a pseudo-Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾 ([[葛長庚]]) attribution.

**Activity.** 1312 (attested). No lifedates or other biographical details recorded. No CBDB record identified.

**Lineage.** Evidently associated with the Southern Lineage (*Nán zōng* 南宗) *nèi dān* 內丹 tradition — the [[KR5c0153|DZ 757]] commentary bears the distinctive stamp of Zhāng Bóduān-lineage inner-alchemical vocabulary, and its pseudo-attribution to Bái Yùchán (the fifth Southern-Lineage patriarch) places Wáng Yuánhuī firmly within that tradition.

**Work.** His sole attested work is [[KR5c0153|DZ 757]] — a commentary on the *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* dated 1312.
