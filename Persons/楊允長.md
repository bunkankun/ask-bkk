Yáng Yǔncháng 楊允長, a Qīng-period pupil of Zhāng Liè 張烈 (1622–1685). After Zhāng's death he composed a *Sī shì yì* 私謚議 ("Argument for a Private Posthumous Title") defending the pupils' privately conferring on Zhāng the title *Zhìdào xiānsheng* 志道先生; the *Sìkù* editors excised this defense when admitting [[KR1a0131]] *Dú Yì rì chāo*. No further biographical details are recorded in the *Sìkù* notice.
