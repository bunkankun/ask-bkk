**Zhāng Liǔyín** 張柳吟 of Wūdì 無棣, mid-Qīng physician; collaborator with [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng in the 1850 re-edition of [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn's *Shènjí chúyán* under the new title [[KR3eq039]] *Yī biān*.
