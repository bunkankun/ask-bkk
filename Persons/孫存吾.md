Yuán literatus of Lú-líng 廬陵 (Jiāng-xī). Active in the early-to-mid Yuán. Co-editor with [[傅習]] of the dynastic poetry anthology **[[KR4h0082]]** *Huáng-Yuán fēngyǎ* 皇元風雅 (24 *juǎn*, c. 1330–1350): Fù Xí collected the material from contemporary poets, Sūn Cún-wú classified and arranged it editorially. Likely identical with the **Sūn Cún-wú** named among the contributing poets in the same anthology (*juǎn* 5, alongside Fàn Dé-jī, Zhāng Xī-táng, etc.). No other works by him are independently transmitted.
