Yuán literatus of Lúlíng 廬陵 (Jiāngxī). Active in the early-to-mid Yuán. Co-editor with [[傅習]] of the dynastic poetry anthology **[[KR4h0082]]** *Huáng-Yuán fēngyǎ* 皇元風雅 (24 *juǎn*, c. 1330–1350): Fù Xí collected the material from contemporary poets, Sūn Cúnwú classified and arranged it editorially. Likely identical with the **Sūn Cúnwú** named among the contributing poets in the same anthology (*juǎn* 5, alongside Fàn Déjī, Zhāng Xītáng, etc.). No other works by him are independently transmitted.
