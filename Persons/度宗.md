# 度宗 Dùzōng

Sixth emperor of the Southern Sòng (r. 1264–1274), personal name **Zhào Qí** 趙禥. Successor of [[理宗]] Lǐzōng; his short reign covered the final decade of the Southern-Sòng Tiāntái and Chán scholastic renaissance at Hángzhōu. In 1274 he conferred the posthumous honorific **Pǔtōng fǎshī** 普通法師 on the Tiāntái master [[法照]] Huìyán Fǎzhào.
