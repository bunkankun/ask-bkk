Pú Jī 蒲機 (Yuán, fl. c. 1332), son of Pú Dào-yuán [[蒲道源]] and editor of the *Xián-jū cóng-gǎo* 閑居叢稿 [[KR4d0507]]. CBDB records *fl.* 1332. No separate biographical material located beyond his editorial role.
