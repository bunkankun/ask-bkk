Wáng Jìhuá 王際華 (1717–1776), zì Qiūruì 秋瑞, hào Bǎizhī 白齋, posthumous name Wénzhuāng 文莊, was a Qiánlóng-era senior Hànlín official from Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Eldest son of Wáng Yìnggǔ 王應榖, *jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 10 (1745) with the second-class third-rank distinction (*tànhuā* 探花), he served successively as *xíngbù* 刑部, *bǐngbù* 兵部, *gōngbù* 工部, and *hùbù shàngshū* 戶部尚書. He was an active member of the Sìkù editorial board, working alongside Yú Mǐnzhōng on the *Tiānlù línláng shūmù* [[KR2n0009]] (1775) and the *Qīndìng chóngkè Chúnhuàgé tiè shìwén* [[KR2n0035]] (1769), and as one of the *Wǔyīngdiàn jǔshì* general supervisors. CBDB 57212 confirms 1717–1776; *Qīngshǐ gǎo* j. 322 has his biography.
