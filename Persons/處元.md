Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master of the *shānjiā* 山家 lineage; sobriquet **Cǎotáng** 草堂 ("Thatched-Hall"; also referenced as **Cǎotáng Chǔyuán** 草堂處元), official title **Sēngzhèng** 僧正 ("Sangha-Inspector"). DILA Authority A001138. Born 1030; died c. 1119–1120, aged 90.

Major work: the **Móhē zhǐguān yìlì suíshì** 摩訶止觀義例隨釋 ([[KR6d0141]], X56n0923, 6 *juan*) — a Northern-Sòng running subcommentary on Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān yìlì* ([[KR6d0132]], T1913), parallel to Cóngyì's *Zuǎnyào* ([[KR6d0139]]).

Chǔyuán's productive period (c. 1060–1119) overlaps substantially with Cóngyì's (1042–1091) and Yǒuyán's (1021–1101), placing him in the second-and-third generation of the post-Zhīlǐ *shānjiā* tradition.

Sources: *Shìshì yínián lù* 釋氏疑年錄ʔ; DILA A001138.
