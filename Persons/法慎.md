# Fǎshèn 法慎

Suí-period Buddhist monk and disciple of [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ. Named in the editorial framing to the *Xiǎo zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0143]]) as the original recorder (in thirty *juan*) of Zhìyǐ's *Jiàncì zhǐguān* 漸次止觀 lectures delivered at Wǎguānsì 瓦官寺, which were subsequently redacted into ten *juan* by [[灌頂]] Zhāngān Guàndǐng as the *Chánbōluómì cìdì fǎmén* ([[KR6d0144]], T1916). Lifedates not recorded.
