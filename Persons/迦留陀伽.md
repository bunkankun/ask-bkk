Jiā-liú-tuó-jiā 迦留陀伽 (\*Kālodaka, "Black-Water" / "Time-Water"; lifedates unknown), Eastern-Jìn Indian / Western-region Buddhist *śramaṇa*, *fl.* late fourth / early fifth century. The catalog signature for [[KR6b0052|*Shí'èr-yóu jīng* 十二遊經 (T195)]] reads 「東晉西域沙門迦留陀伽譯」, providing the principal evidence of his activity. The *Lìdài sānbǎo jì* assigns him to the Eastern-Jìn period under emperor An-dì 安帝 (397–419). No further biographical material survives. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001066.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6b0052]] *Shí'èryóu jīng* (T195).
