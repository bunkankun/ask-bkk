Huáng Mǐn 黃閔 (*fl.* Southern Qi dynasty, 479–502 CE) was a geographic writer who served as a local official in the Wuling region (modern western Hunan). He is the author of a *Wǔlíng jì* 武陵記 ([[KR2k0181]]), a substantial geographic record of the Wuling commandery, preserved in fragments cited in the *Hòu Hàn shū* commentary and major Tang-Song encyclopedias. No further biographical details are attested.
