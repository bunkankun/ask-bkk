Sūn Hào 孫灝 (1700–1766), *zì* Xūchuán 虛船, *hào* Yìtíng 嶧亭, native of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng 1 (1723); entered the Hanlin and rose to *biānxiū*. From 1731 to 1735 he served as principal *biānzuǎn* of the *Hénán tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0047]]), under Tián Wénjìng and (after Tián's death) Wáng Shìjùn. Subsequently appointed Hénán education commissioner (*xuézhèng*) and rose to *Tài-cháng-sì qīng* 太常寺卿.
