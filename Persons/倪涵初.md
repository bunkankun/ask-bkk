**Ní Hánchū** 倪涵初, Qīng physician; author of the widely circulated *Ní shì sān fāng* 倪氏三方 ("Ní's three formulas") on dysentery, which became the standard template of late-Qīng popular dysentery practice. Criticised by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]] for having locked the field into what Wú considered a wrong therapeutic template.
