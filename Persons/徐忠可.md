**Xú Zhōngkě** 徐忠可 (also known as 徐彬 Xú Bīn, *fl.* late 17th c., 清), disciple of [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng, transmitter of Yú's clinical-ethics teachings as the *Yī mén fǎlǜ* 醫門法律 program, and author of the *Jīnguì yàoluè lùn zhù* 金匱要略論註.
