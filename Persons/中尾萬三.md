**Nakao Manzō** 中尾萬三 (Chinese: Zhōngwěi Wànsān, 1879–1947), important early-Shōwa Japanese pharmacist, pharmacologist, and historian of East Asian pharmacology. Professor at Mitsui Memorial Hospital (三井記念病院) and later affiliated with the *Manshū igaku* (滿洲醫學) research institutes. Combined laboratory pharmacology with serious philological-historical scholarship on the Chinese pharmacopoeia tradition; his *Chūgokuyaku hyakushu no kagaku jikken* 中國藥一百種之化學實驗 ([[KR3ec083]]) is the principal Japanese chemical-analytical survey of major Chinese drugs of the 1920s–30s. Nakao also published important historical-pharmacological studies, including monographs on the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* tradition, the medieval Japanese *honzō wamyō*, and the materia medica of the Shōsōin imperial repository. His combined scientific-philological approach makes him one of the most distinguished 20th-century Japanese scholars of East Asian pharmacology. Japanese scholar; not in CBDB.
