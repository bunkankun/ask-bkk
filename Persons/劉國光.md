**Liú Guóguāng** 劉國光 (*zì* Bīnchén 賓臣, fl. Guāng-xù period, late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng official of Chǔ-běi 楚北 (Húběi); served as Prefect (*zhī-fǔ*) of Qú-zhōu 衢州 (Zhèjiāng) in the Guāng-xù 9 (1883) period. Acquainted with the Qú-zhōu physician Léi Fēng 雷豐 (Léi Shàoyì 雷少逸); prefaced and printed Léi's editions of the *Shí-bìng lùn* 時病論 and the *Jiǔ-fǎ mì-chuán* 灸法秘傳 ([[KR3ee047]], 1883). The catalog meta lists him as author of [[KR3ee047]]; properly speaking he is the preface-writer and patron-printer rather than the editorial compiler (that is Léi Fēng).
