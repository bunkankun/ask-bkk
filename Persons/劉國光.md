**Liú Guóguāng** 劉國光 (*zì* Bīnchén 賓臣, fl. Guāngxù period, late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng official of Chǔběi 楚北 (Húběi); served as Prefect (*zhīfǔ*) of Qúzhōu 衢州 (Zhèjiāng) in the Guāngxù 9 (1883) period. Acquainted with the Qúzhōu physician Léi Fēng 雷豐 (Léi Shàoyì 雷少逸); prefaced and printed Léi's editions of the *Shíbìng lùn* 時病論 and the *Jiǔfǎ mìchuán* 灸法秘傳 ([[KR3ee047]], 1883). The catalog meta lists him as author of [[KR3ee047]]; properly speaking he is the preface-writer and patron-printer rather than the editorial compiler (that is Léi Fēng).
