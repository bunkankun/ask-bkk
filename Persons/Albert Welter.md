# Albert Welter

Albert Welter, American scholar of Chinese Buddhism, professor at the University of Arizona. Author of *Monks, Rulers, and Literati: The Political Ascendancy of Chan Buddhism* (OUP, 2006) and related studies of the lamp-record tradition.
