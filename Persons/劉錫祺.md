**Liú Xíqí** 劉錫祺, early-Dàoguāng-era Húnán patron of medical publication; co-sponsored the 1823 printing of [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī's [[KR3eh051]] *Nèishāng jí yào* alongside [[楊廣先]] Yáng Guǎngxiān, [[鍾昌言]] Zhōng Chāngyán, and [[蔡磻溪]] Cài Pánxī. Known only from Cài's preface.
