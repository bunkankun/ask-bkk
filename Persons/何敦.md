Hé Dūn 何敦 (*hào* Dūn'ān 敦庵; fl. mid-19th c.) was the grandnephew of [[何彤園]] Hé Tóngyuán; he wrote the *Xiánfēng* 5 (1855) preface to the family-printed posthumous edition of the *Dàfāngmài* ([[KR3eh020]]).
