# Dépǔ 德溥

Southern-Sòng Chán monk; dharma-heir disciple (*mén rén* 門人) of Wùchū [[大觀|Dàguān]] 物初大觀 (1201–1268). Lead-editor (*děng biān jiào* 等編校) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0300]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
