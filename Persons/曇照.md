Tánzhào 曇照 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* mid-Northern Sòng), Sòng-dynasty Tiāntāi 天台 monk of the **Sìmíng** 四明 (Níngbō) establishment, also designated 四明曇照 ("Tánzhào of Sìmíng") and 方廣曇照 ("Tánzhào of Fāngguǎng[-sì]"). He stands within the lineage of [[四明知禮|Sìmíng Zhīlǐ]] 四明知禮 (960–1028) — the dominant *Shānjiā* 山家 patriarch of the Northern-Sòng Tiāntāi revival.

He is the author of the **《天台智者大師別傳註》** *Tiāntāi zhìzhě dàshī biézhuàn zhù* ([[KR6r0069]], X77 no. 1535), a 2-juan annotated commentary on Guàndǐng 灌頂's biography of Zhìyǐ — the *Suí Tiāntāi Zhìzhě dàshī biézhuàn* (T2050). Tánzhào's commentary is the principal Sòng exegesis of the Tiāntāi school's foundation-narrative and the standard Sòng-Tiāntāi reading of Zhìyǐ's life. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001790.

He is to be distinguished from the Táng monk **Báiyún Tánzhào** 白雲曇照 (DILA A019938) and from the Yuán-Míng homonym Tánzhào.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6r0069]] *Tiāntāi zhìzhě dàshī biézhuàn zhù*.
