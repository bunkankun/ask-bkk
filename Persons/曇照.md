Tánzhào 曇照 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* mid-Northern Sòng), Sòng-dynasty Tiāntāi 天台 monk of the **Sì-míng** 四明 (Níng-bō) establishment, also designated 四明曇照 ("Tánzhào of Sì-míng") and 方廣曇照 ("Tánzhào of Fāng-guǎng[-sì]"). He stands within the lineage of [[四明知禮|Sì-míng Zhī-lǐ]] 四明知禮 (960–1028) — the dominant *Shān-jiā* 山家 patriarch of the Northern-Sòng Tiāntāi revival.

He is the author of the **《天台智者大師別傳註》** *Tiāntāi zhì-zhě dà-shī bié-zhuàn zhù* ([[KR6r0069]], X77 no. 1535), a 2-juan annotated commentary on Guàn-dǐng 灌頂's biography of Zhì-yǐ — the *Suí Tiāntāi Zhì-zhě dà-shī bié-zhuàn* (T2050). Tánzhào's commentary is the principal Sòng exegesis of the Tiāntāi school's foundation-narrative and the standard Sòng-Tiāntāi reading of Zhì-yǐ's life. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001790.

He is to be distinguished from the Táng monk **Bái-yún Tánzhào** 白雲曇照 (DILA A019938) and from the Yuán-Míng homonym Tánzhào.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6r0069]] *Tiāntāi zhì-zhě dà-shī bié-zhuàn zhù*.
