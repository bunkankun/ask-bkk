Liú Sòng-period (5th c.) *Yì jīng* exegete. Author of a *Zhōu Yì Xún shì shì* 周易荀氏釋, of which only fragments survive — preserved in [[陸德明]] Lù Démíng's *Jīng diǎn shì wén* 經典釋文 and reconstructed by [[馬國翰]] Mǎ Guóhàn in *Yùhán shānfáng jíyì shū* 玉函山房輯佚書 (*Zhōu Yì xìcí Xún shì zhù* 周易繫辭荀氏注). To be distinguished from the Hàn-period [[荀爽]] Xún Shuǎng. Source: *Nán shǐ* 南史; minor mentions only.
