**Mǎ Jūnliáng** 馬俊良 (*fl.* late Kāngxī, 清), Sūzhōu physician and teacher of [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí, transmitting the early-Qīng Sūzhōu *Shānghán* line that descended from [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù and [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng.
