Zhāng Chún 張淳 (1121–1181). *Zì* Zhōngfǔ 忠甫. Native of Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (modern Wēnzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Career under Sòng Xiàozōng: in the eighth year of Qiándào (1172), held office as *Liǎng-zhè zhuǎnyùn pànguān* 兩浙轉運判官 and concurrently *Zhí Mìgé* 直秘閣 of the Imperial Library. The Wēnzhōu prefect Zēng Dài 曾逮 commissioned a new printing of the *Yílǐ Zhèng-shì zhù* in 17 juan with Lù Démíng's *Shìwén* in 1 juan; Zhāng Chún was charged with the textual collation. The collation results, organised separately as a list of corrections, became his [[KR1d0029]] *Yílǐ shíwù* 儀禮識誤 in 3 juan (1172).

Zhāng's collation work drew on multiple Sòng-period exemplars: the imperial Stone Classic engraved 953 and re-engraved 959; the Bīanjīng (Kāifēng) *jīnxiāng* (small-format wallet) edition; the Hángzhōu small-character edition; the Yánzhōu *jīnxiāng* re-cut edition; and Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén*. The work was praised by Zhū Xī as "thorough and careful, exceeding all other editions." It is the principal Southern Sòng critical-edition study of the *Yílǐ* and the foundation of the late-Qīng *dān-shū* recovery work (cf. [[KR1d0027]]).
