Sēng Qú 僧璩 (fl. mid-5th c.) was a Liú-Sòng dynasty Vinaya specialist of the *Shísòng* (Sarvāstivāda) tradition. He is recorded in the *Gāosēng zhuàn* 高僧傳 (T50n2059) as a disciple of **Sēngyè** 僧業 in the second-generation transmission of the Sarvāstivāda Vinaya in southern China. His extant work is the **Shísòng jiémó bǐqiū yàoyòng** 十誦羯磨比丘要用 ([[KR6k0020]], T23n1439), a one-fascicle practical compendium of essential *karmavācanā* formulae extracted from the Sarvāstivāda Vinaya for daily monastic use.
