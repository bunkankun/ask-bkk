Yuányú 元瑜 (CBDB ID 545190; fl. late 7th–early 8th century CE) was a Tang dynasty Buddhist monk in the Xuanzang transmission lineage who composed the *Shùnzhènglǐ Lùn Shùwén Jì* 順正理論述文記 ([[KR6l0033]], X53n843), a commentary on Saṃghabhadra's *Nyāyānusāra* 順正理論 ([[KR6l0031]]). The text identifies him as 沙門元瑜 ("the monk Yuányú"). His dates and biographical details are otherwise unrecorded. Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6l0033]] 順正理論述文記.
