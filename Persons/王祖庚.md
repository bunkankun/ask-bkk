# Wáng Zǔgēng 王祖庚

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as the *kǎozhèng* compiler for the *Nán Qí shū* ([[KR2a0017]]) and the *Yuán shǐ* ([[KR2a0036]]) in the *Sìkù quánshū*. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
