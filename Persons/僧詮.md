Sēngquán 僧詮 was a Six-Dynasties Sānlùn / Mādhyamika master, a member of the transmission lineage from [[僧肇]] Sēngzhào and [[僧叡]] Sēngruì that culminated in [[吉藏]] Jízàng's mature systematic Sānlùn tradition. Few details of his biography are preserved.
