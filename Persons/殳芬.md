# 殳芬

Shū Fēn (*zì* Yùfāng 鬱芳) of Pínghú 平湖 (Zhèjiāng), early-nineteenth-century literatus, disciple of [[顧錫]] Gù Xī. Sponsored and prefaced (*Jiāqìng* 12 = 1807) the publication of [[KR3em012]] *Yínhǎi zhǐnán* 銀海指南.
