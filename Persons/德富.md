Dé-fù 德富, *zì* **Qí-bái** 其白 ("Solely White"); often referred to as **Qí-bái Dé-fù** 其白德富 or **Yù-quán Fù** 玉泉富 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696), thus a co-disciple of **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] (cf. [[KR6q0544]]). Born **明天啟七年十二月二十九日午時** = **1628-02-04** Gregorian; died **康熙庚午仲夏廿七日** = **1690-07-03**, age 64. Native of **Shǔ-nán Méi-zhōu** 蜀南眉州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Gōng** 龔.

**Formation.** **甲戌** = **1634** at age 7 took tonsure under **Gǔ-yú Cí-háng** 古渝慈航 in **Yú-zhōu** 渝州 (Chóng-qìng region), receiving the dharma-name 德富. **丁酉** = **1657** at **Yuán-jué-shān** 圓覺山 took full ordination under **Dà-rú** 大如和尚. Wandered widely *cān*-fǎng. Final *fù-fǎ* under **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** at **Zhāo-jué-sì** 昭覺寺.

**Abbacy.** **Méi-zhōu Pán-lóng-shān Yù-quán-sì** 眉州蟠龍山玉泉禪寺 (sole, lifelong abbacy giving him his sobriquet **Yù-quán Fù**).

**Disciples (five).** **Róng-chè Yuán-dǐng** 融徹圓頂 [[圓頂]] (compiler of the *yǔ-lù* and author of his own *yǔ-lù* preserved separately), **Huì-kōng Zhì-hǎi** 慧空智海, **Róng-mù Yuán-zōng** 融穆圓宗, **Mín-ruì Míng-kūn** 玟睿明崑, **Lín-ruì Míng-xuān** 琳睿明宣.

**Work.** [[KR6q0547]] 《玉泉其白富禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B430, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Yuán-dǐng** [[圓頂]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《玉泉其白富禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉, 〈塔銘〉, 〈分燈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 83.
