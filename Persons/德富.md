Défù 德富, *zì* **Qíbái** 其白 ("Solely White"); often referred to as **Qíbái Défù** 其白德富 or **Yùquán Fù** 玉泉富 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696), thus a co-disciple of **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] (cf. [[KR6q0544]]). Born **明天啟七年十二月二十九日午時** = **1628-02-04** Gregorian; died **康熙庚午仲夏廿七日** = **1690-07-03**, age 64. Native of **Shǔnán Méizhōu** 蜀南眉州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Gōng** 龔.

**Formation.** **甲戌** = **1634** at age 7 took tonsure under **Gǔyú Cíháng** 古渝慈航 in **Yúzhōu** 渝州 (Chóngqìng region), receiving the dharma-name 德富. **丁酉** = **1657** at **Yuánjuéshān** 圓覺山 took full ordination under **Dàrú** 大如和尚. Wandered widely *cān*-fǎng. Final *fùfǎ* under **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** at **Zhāojuésì** 昭覺寺.

**Abbacy.** **Méizhōu Pánlóngshān Yùquánsì** 眉州蟠龍山玉泉禪寺 (sole, lifelong abbacy giving him his sobriquet **Yùquán Fù**).

**Disciples (five).** **Róngchè Yuándǐng** 融徹圓頂 [[圓頂]] (compiler of the *yǔlù* and author of his own *yǔlù* preserved separately), **Huìkōng Zhìhǎi** 慧空智海, **Róngmù Yuánzōng** 融穆圓宗, **Mínruì Míngkūn** 玟睿明崑, **Línruì Míngxuān** 琳睿明宣.

**Work.** [[KR6q0547]] 《玉泉其白富禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B430, compiled by *fǎsì* **Yuándǐng** [[圓頂]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《玉泉其白富禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉, 〈塔銘〉, 〈分燈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 83.
