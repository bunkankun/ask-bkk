# 趙文業

Zhào Wényè (趙文業) served as Wǔwēi 武威 Prefect under Fú Jiān 苻堅's Former Qín 前秦 (Fú-Qín) regime in Cháng'ān. He is named in [[道安]] Dàoān's preface to the *Ekottara-āgama* [[KR6a0126]] (T125) as the official who commissioned the translation begun in 384 by [[曇摩難提]] Dharmanandi. Dates are not recorded.
