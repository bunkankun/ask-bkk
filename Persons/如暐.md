Rúwěi 如暐, *hào* **Xiàofēng** 嘯峰. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Mìyìn Zhēnchuán** [[真傳]] 密印真傳 (b. 1625) at **Qiányáng Shèngjuésì** 黔陽勝覺寺. One of two named heirs alongside **Xuějiàn Rúkǎi** 雪澗如暟. Lead compiler of the 12-juan 《廣福山勝覺寺密印禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0489]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
