Rú-wěi 如暐, *hào* **Xiào-fēng** 嘯峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mì-yìn Zhēn-chuán** [[真傳]] 密印真傳 (b. 1625) at **Qián-yáng Shèng-jué-sì** 黔陽勝覺寺. One of two named heirs alongside **Xuě-jiàn Rú-kǎi** 雪澗如暟. Lead compiler of the 12-juan 《廣福山勝覺寺密印禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0489]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
