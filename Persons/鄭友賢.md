**Zhèng Yǒuxián** 鄭友賢 (late Northern / early Southern Sòng) was a Sòng military-classic commentator, author of [[KR5f0015]] ***Sūn zǐ yí shuō*** 孫子遺說 — supplementary comments on the *Sūn zǐ bīng fǎ* 孫子兵法.
