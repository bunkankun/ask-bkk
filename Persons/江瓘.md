**Jiāng Guàn** 江瓘 (*zì* Mínyíng 民瑩, *hào* Huángnán 篁南, 1503–1565, 明), Míng-period scholar-physician of Shè-xiàn 歙縣 (Ān-huī). Compiler of the great ***Míng-yī lèi-àn*** 名醫類案 ([[KR3e0075]], 12 juan, 180+ categorical gates) — the most comprehensive Chinese pre-modern collection of medical case-records. Jiāng compiled ancient and contemporary case-records by name, with appended discussion. The work was completed in Jiā-jìng yǐ-yǒu (1549, the *mò-qiū jì-wàng* 莫秋既望 of his preface). Posthumously expanded by his son [[江應宿]] Jiāng Yìngsù (Míng) and further annotated by [[魏之琇]] Wèi Zhīxiù (early Qīng).
