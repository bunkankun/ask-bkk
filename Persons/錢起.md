# Qián Qǐ 錢起 (710–782)

*Zì* Zhòngwén 仲文. Native of *Wúxìng* 吳興 (modern *Húzhōu*, Zhèjiāng). The leading figure of the *Dàlì shí cáizǐ* 大曆十才子 ("Ten Talented Men of the Dàlì period"), the standard mid-Tang literary circle whose remaining names — Lú Lún 盧綸, Jí Zhōngfǔ 吉中孚, Hán Hóng 韓翃, Sīkōng Shǔ 司空曙, Miáo Fā 苗發, Cuī Dòng 崔峒, Gěng Wěi 耿湋, Xiàhóu Shěn 夏侯審, Lǐ Duān 李端 — vary by source.

*Jìnshì* of *Tiānbǎo* 10 (751); his examination-piece *Xiānglíng gǔ sè* 湘靈鼓瑟 (with its iconic closing line *qū zhōng rén bù jiàn, jiāng shàng shù fēng qīng* 曲終人不見江上數峰青) is one of the most-anthologized Tang quatrains. Successively *Mìshūshěng* drafter, *jiàoshūláng* 校書郎, and (in the late *Dàlì*) *Kǎogōng lángzhōng* 考功郎中 — whence the *Qián Kǎogōng* 錢考工 honorific. Died *ca.* 782.

His extant collection is the *Qián Zhòngwén jí* [[KR4c0037]] in 10 *juǎn*. CBDB confirms 710–782 (cbdbId 94271). The catalog meta's "fl. 755–766" is conservative but consistent with this floruit.
