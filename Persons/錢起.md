# Qián Qǐ 錢起 (710–782)

*Zì* Zhòng-wén 仲文. Native of *Wú-xìng* 吳興 (modern *Hú-zhōu*, Zhèjiāng). The leading figure of the *Dà-lì shí cái-zǐ* 大曆十才子 ("Ten Talented Men of the Dà-lì period"), the standard mid-Tang literary circle whose remaining names — Lú Lún 盧綸, Jí Zhōng-fǔ 吉中孚, Hán Hóng 韓翃, Sī-kōng Shǔ 司空曙, Miáo Fā 苗發, Cuī Dòng 崔峒, Gěng Wěi 耿湋, Xià-hóu Shěn 夏侯審, Lǐ Duān 李端 — vary by source.

*Jìn-shì* of *Tiān-bǎo* 10 (751); his examination-piece *Xiāng-líng gǔ sè* 湘靈鼓瑟 (with its iconic closing line *qū zhōng rén bù jiàn, jiāng shàng shù fēng qīng* 曲終人不見江上數峰青) is one of the most-anthologized Tang quatrains. Successively *Mì-shū-shěng* drafter, *jiào-shū-láng* 校書郎, and (in the late *Dà-lì*) *Kǎo-gōng láng-zhōng* 考功郎中 — whence the *Qián Kǎo-gōng* 錢考工 honorific. Died *ca.* 782.

His extant collection is the *Qián Zhòng-wén jí* [[KR4c0037]] in 10 *juǎn*. CBDB confirms 710–782 (cbdbId 94271). The catalog meta's "fl. 755–766" is conservative but consistent with this floruit.
