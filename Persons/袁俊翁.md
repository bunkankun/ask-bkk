# Yuán Jùnwēng 袁俊翁 (fl. mid-14th century)

*Zì* Mǐn-zhāi 敏齋. Native of Yuán-zhōu 袁州 (modern Yí-chūn 宜春, Jiāng-xī). The graphic variant 雋翁 in Lí Lì-wǔ's preface to [[KR1h0036]] is a transmitted-character difference for the same name. Lifedates not securely recorded; he was active during the late Yuan, ca. 1330s–1350s.

The Sìkù tíyào at [[KR1h0036]] reports: prefaces to his work are by Lí Lì-wǔ ([[黎立武]], who was older and locally based), Lǐ Yìng-xīng 李應星, and Péng Yuán-lóng 彭元龍. Lí Lì-wǔ's preface speaks of him as having "*yǐ chóng-wú băng* 以重吾榜" (graced our examination roster); Lǐ Yìng-xīng's preface speaks of his "*yì-yì kuí-wén* 奕奕魁文" (eminent first-rank composition) — confirming that Yuán Jùnwēng had taken first place in the local *xiāng-shì* 鄉試 (provincial examination) preliminary. His subsequent career is otherwise undocumented.

His work *Sì-shū yí-jié* 四書疑節 ([[KR1h0036]]) survives as one section (the *Sì-shū jīng-yí* 四書經疑 portion) of a larger lost work, the *Dài-wèn jí* 待問集 ("Collection of Pending-Question essays") — the "*jīng-shǐ yí-yì*" 經史疑義 section being already lost. The discrepancy between section-titles and external prefaces in the surviving manuscript reflects this partial-transmission history.
