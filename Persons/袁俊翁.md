# Yuán Jùnwēng 袁俊翁 (fl. mid-14th century)

*Zì* Mǐnzhāi 敏齋. Native of Yuánzhōu 袁州 (modern Yíchūn 宜春, Jiāngxī). The graphic variant 雋翁 in Lí Lìwǔ's preface to [[KR1h0036]] is a transmitted-character difference for the same name. Lifedates not securely recorded; he was active during the late Yuan, ca. 1330s–1350s.

The Sìkù tíyào at [[KR1h0036]] reports: prefaces to his work are by Lí Lìwǔ ([[黎立武]], who was older and locally based), Lǐ Yìngxīng 李應星, and Péng Yuánlóng 彭元龍. Lí Lìwǔ's preface speaks of him as having "*yǐ chóngwú băng* 以重吾榜" (graced our examination roster); Lǐ Yìngxīng's preface speaks of his "*yìyì kuíwén* 奕奕魁文" (eminent first-rank composition) — confirming that Yuán Jùnwēng had taken first place in the local *xiāngshì* 鄉試 (provincial examination) preliminary. His subsequent career is otherwise undocumented.

His work *Sìshū yíjié* 四書疑節 ([[KR1h0036]]) survives as one section (the *Sìshū jīngyí* 四書經疑 portion) of a larger lost work, the *Dàiwèn jí* 待問集 ("Collection of Pending-Question essays") — the "*jīngshǐ yíyì*" 經史疑義 section being already lost. The discrepancy between section-titles and external prefaces in the surviving manuscript reflects this partial-transmission history.
