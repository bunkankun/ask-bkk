# 劉紹南

Liú Shàonán (sobriquet Yuánjué jūshì 圓覺居士)

Late-Qīng layman of Zhēnzhōu 真州 (Jiāngsū) who wrote a sponsoring preface to [[謝承謨]] Xiè Chéngmó's 1889 *Jīngāng jīng yìjiě* ([[KR6c0098]]).
