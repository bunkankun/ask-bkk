Fǎshèng 法勝 (Skt. *Dharmaśreṣṭhin* "Supreme in Dharma"; also transliterated 達磨尸梨帝; DILA Authority A000722) was a Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma master credited with composing the *Abhidharma-hṛdaya-śāstra* 阿毘曇心論 ([[KR6l0015]], T1550), the foundational text of the *Hṛdaya* summary tradition within Sarvāstivāda scholasticism. He is placed by modern scholars (Frauwallner 1995) in approximately the 2nd–3rd century CE within the Kaśmīra-Gandhāra region. Nothing further is known of his biography.

Note: 法勝 (*Dharmaśreṣṭhin*) should not be confused with 法救 (*Dharmatrāta*, [[法救]]), another Sarvāstivāda master of the same era, attributed with the *Zá āpítán xīn lùn* ([[KR6l0017]]). Both names refer to distinct figures in the *Hṛdaya* commentary tradition.
