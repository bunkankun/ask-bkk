*Mǎ-hòu* 馬后 (the catalog meta form) appears as the nominal author of the *Wò-jī jīng* 握奇經 (KR3b0001) — the legendary military treatise traditionally attributed to *Fēng-hòu* 風后, an archaic minister of the Yellow Emperor (Huáng-dì). The form 馬后 (instead of 風后) in the catalog meta may reflect a transcription error. The SKQS *tíyào* establishes the work as a Sòng-period production attributed to the legendary Fēng-hòu — comparable in genre to the various pre-Qín-attributed military works also of demonstrable post-Qín composition (e.g. *Sūn-zǐ bīng-fǎ*'s long edited tradition). Per the SKQS, the work was composed by Sòng-period editors elaborating from Dú-gū Jí 獨孤及 (Mid-Táng)'s *Bā-zhèn tú jì* 八陣圖記 and from Zhū-gě Liàng's 諸葛亮 *bā-zhèn fǎ* tradition.
