Máo Zài 毛在 (b. 1544; death year uncertain) was a late-Míng *shì-rén* of Tàicāng 太倉 (then in Sūzhōu prefecture, Nán-zhílì; modern Jiāngsū). *Zì* Jūn-míng 君明. CBDB (id 206137) records the birth year 1544 but no death year and no firm office record, consistent with the *Sìkù* tíyào's observation that he "signed himself as a native of Tàicāng, but his start and end are not detailed" (自署太倉人其始末則未詳也).

He is known principally as the compiler of supplementary entries to [[耿定向]] Gěng Dìngxiàng's *Xiānjìn yífēng* 先進遺風 [[KR3l0087]]. The supplemented work was printed in Chén Jì-rú 陳繼儒's *Mì-jí* 秘笈 imprint; the *Sìkù* compilers found that the merged text no longer cleanly distinguishes Gěng's original entries from Máo's *zēng-bǔ* (增補) continuations, and only a typographic hypothesis (indented vs. flush-left opening characters) is available to attempt a separation.
