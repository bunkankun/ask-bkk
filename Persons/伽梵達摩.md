# 伽梵達摩

Jiāfàndámó 伽梵達摩 (Bhagavaddharma), seventh-century Indian Buddhist translator active in Táng China. Best known for his Chinese rendering of the *Qiānshǒu qiānyǎn Guānshìyīn púsà guǎngdà yuánmǎn wúài dàbēi xīn tuóluóní jīng* ([[KR6j0260]], T20n1060), the principal sūtra of the Sahasrabhuja-Avalokiteśvara cult.
