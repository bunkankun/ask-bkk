Xuē Xuān 薛瑄 (1389–1464), *zì* Déwēn 德温, *hào* Jìngxuān 敬軒, posthumously *Wénqīng* 文清, was the foundational early-Míng *Zhūzǐxué* loyalist and the founder of the *Hé-Dōng xuépài* 河東學派. Native of Héjīn 河津 (Shānxī). *Jìnshì* of *Yǒnglè xīnchǒu* (1421); rose to *Lǐbù yòu shìláng* and entered the cabinet (*rù gé yù jī wù*); died with posthumous title *Lǐbù shàngshū*. Within the early-Míng triad of upright Confucians (Cáo Duān 曹端, Hú Jūrén 胡居仁, Xuē Xuān), Xuē is the latest in time and represents the most institutionally established line. The Qiánlóng emperor's *yùzhì* opening this work places Xuē above Cáo and Hú as the principal early-Míng *daoxué* transmitter. Major work: the *Dú shū lù* 讀書錄 ([[KR3a0079]], 11 juan + 12 juan continuation) — his lifelong *yǔlù*-style notebook of *gōngxíng xīndé* (personal practice and heart-attainment). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 30622, dates 1389–1464.
