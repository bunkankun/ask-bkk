Xuē Xuān 薛瑄 (1389–1464), *zì* Déwēn 德温, *hào* Jìngxuān 敬軒, posthumously *Wénqīng* 文清, was the foundational early-Míng *Zhū-zǐ-xué* loyalist and the founder of the *Hé-Dōng xué-pài* 河東學派. Native of Hé-jīn 河津 (Shānxī). *Jìnshì* of *Yǒng-lè xīn-chǒu* (1421); rose to *Lǐ-bù yòu shì-láng* and entered the cabinet (*rù gé yù jī wù*); died with posthumous title *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Within the early-Míng triad of upright Confucians (Cáo Duān 曹端, Hú Jūrén 胡居仁, Xuē Xuān), Xuē is the latest in time and represents the most institutionally established line. The Qiánlóng emperor's *yù-zhì* opening this work places Xuē above Cáo and Hú as the principal early-Míng *daoxué* transmitter. Major work: the *Dú shū lù* 讀書錄 (KR3a0079, 11 juan + 12 juan continuation) — his lifelong *yǔ-lù*-style notebook of *gōng-xíng xīn-dé* (personal practice and heart-attainment). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rú-lín zhuàn). CBDB id 30622, dates 1389–1464.
