Dàochéng 道澄, *zì* **Kōnggǔ** 空谷 ("Empty Valley"); often referred to as **Kōnggǔ Dàochéng** 空谷道澄. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** [[如相]] (1603–1672, cf. [[KR6q0564]]). Born **1616**; death year unknown — was 79 in **1694** (per the *yǔlù* line "*qīshí jiǔ lái cháo Pǔtuó*" 七十九來朝普陀). Native of **Zhōngzhōu** 忠州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Early breakthrough on the *wànfǎ guī yī* *gōngàn*; visited **Wànfēng** 萬峰 and received first sealing from **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** 敏樹相. After traveling many places, returned to the **Sìchuān gorge** region (峽) to formally inherit dharma from **Pòshānlǎorén** 破山老人 (= Pòshān Hǎimíng → 樹 lineage; the lineage notation is partly garbled in the *xíngshí*).

**Abbacies (the major Línjì-court appointment).** **Sānshèngsì** 三聖寺 (early, in Sìchuān); then **Chénjīng** 宸京 (Beijing) where he received an imperial-class invitation from **Shùnchéngwáng** 順承王 (a Manchu Prince of the Eight Banners) to take the **Báitǎ Zhēnrú chányuàn** 白塔真如禪院 abbacy. **癸亥十月** = **1683-10** returned to Sìchuān to perform the stupa-cleaning ritual at his teacher **Mǐnshù** [[如相]]'s grave at **Wànxiàn** 萬縣.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* include **Chuándēng Xù** 傳燈續, **Chèmíng Xiàn** 徹明現, **Xǐzhǐ Qín** 璽旨勤, **Hánrùn Cí** 含潤慈, **Xiānglín Rén** 香林仁, **Xuějū Yáng** 雪居暘, **Fóshān** 佛山禪師, **Jìngfán Rèn** 淨凡任, **Zhànqīng Dérú** 湛清德儒 [[德儒]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0583]] 《空谷道澄禪師語錄》 — a massive **20-juan** *yǔlù*, J39 B471, compiled by *fǎsì* **Dérú** [[德儒]] *biān*. Among the largest single-author Línjì *yǔlù* in the Jiāxīng Canon.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 88; 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 11; 《空谷道澄禪師語錄》 vol. 1, 15, 20.
