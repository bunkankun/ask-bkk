Dào-chéng 道澄, *zì* **Kōng-gǔ** 空谷 ("Empty Valley"); often referred to as **Kōng-gǔ Dào-chéng** 空谷道澄. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** [[如相]] (1603–1672, cf. [[KR6q0564]]). Born **1616**; death year unknown — was 79 in **1694** (per the *yǔ-lù* line "*qī-shí jiǔ lái cháo Pǔ-tuó*" 七十九來朝普陀). Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Early breakthrough on the *wàn-fǎ guī yī* *gōng-àn*; visited **Wàn-fēng** 萬峰 and received first sealing from **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** 敏樹相. After traveling many places, returned to the **Sì-chuān gorge** region (峽) to formally inherit dharma from **Pò-shān-lǎo-rén** 破山老人 (= Pò-shān Hǎi-míng → 樹 lineage; the lineage notation is partly garbled in the *xíng-shí*).

**Abbacies (the major Línjì-court appointment).** **Sān-shèng-sì** 三聖寺 (early, in Sì-chuān); then **Chén-jīng** 宸京 (Beijing) where he received an imperial-class invitation from **Shùn-chéng-wáng** 順承王 (a Manchu Prince of the Eight Banners) to take the **Bái-tǎ Zhēn-rú chán-yuàn** 白塔真如禪院 abbacy. **癸亥十月** = **1683-10** returned to Sì-chuān to perform the stupa-cleaning ritual at his teacher **Mǐn-shù** [[如相]]'s grave at **Wàn-xiàn** 萬縣.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎ-sì* include **Chuán-dēng Xù** 傳燈續, **Chè-míng Xiàn** 徹明現, **Xǐ-zhǐ Qín** 璽旨勤, **Hán-rùn Cí** 含潤慈, **Xiāng-lín Rén** 香林仁, **Xuě-jū Yáng** 雪居暘, **Fó-shān** 佛山禪師, **Jìng-fán Rèn** 淨凡任, **Zhàn-qīng Dé-rú** 湛清德儒 [[德儒]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0583]] 《空谷道澄禪師語錄》 — a massive **20-juan** *yǔ-lù*, J39 B471, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-rú** [[德儒]] *biān*. Among the largest single-author Línjì *yǔ-lù* in the Jiā-xīng Canon.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 88; 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 11; 《空谷道澄禪師語錄》 vol. 1, 15, 20.
