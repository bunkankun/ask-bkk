Xióng Sānbá 熊三拔 — Italian Jesuit Sabatino de Ursis (1575–1620) — was an Italian Jesuit missionary in late-Míng China, junior collaborator of Matteo Ricci. The catalog form *Xīyáng Xióng Sānbá* 西洋熊三拔 ("the Westerner Xióng Sānbá") preserves the Late-Míng convention for naming European missionaries with their Sinicised names plus the *Xīyáng* ("Western Sea = Europe") modifier. Major collaborative works: with Xú Guāngqǐ, the *Tàixī shuǐ fǎ* 泰西水法 ([[KR3d0008]], 1612 — translation/adaptation of European hydraulic-engineering and irrigation knowledge); with the Calendar Bureau astronomers, the *Biǎo dù shuō* 表度說 (1614, treatise on gnomon-based solar measurement). Worked extensively on Chinese calendrical reform from 1610 onward. CBDB id 132256.
