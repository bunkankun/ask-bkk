# Wú Hào 吳浩

Style name *Yǎngzhāi* 養齋. Native of Huá-tíng 華亭 (modern Sōngjiāng 松江, Shànghǎi). His preface to the *Shí sān jīng yì yí* 十三經義疑 ([[KR1g0023]]) — the only document of his life preserved in the *Sìkù* — gives no biographical detail beyond his native place and the fact that he had spent more than thirty years in classical study at his retreat (*qiū-yuán* 丘園) in Huá-tíng. He was active in the Yōngzhèng / early-Qiánlóng era (c. 1730–1745).

His sole surviving work is the *Shí sān jīng yì yí* in 12 *juàn*, which the *Sìkù* compilers rate as one of the more conscientious early-Qing compendia of doubts on the *Thirteen Classics*: not perfectly comprehensive, but methodologically careful in citation, with the strength of working systematically through the *zhùshū* tradition. The work is part of the broader trend of Yōngzhèng-era classical compendia produced by scholars unaffiliated with the *Bóxué hóngcí* circle and not employed in the imperial editorial projects.
