# Huáiràng 懷讓 (Nányuè Huáiràng 南嶽懷讓)

Táng-era Chán master (677–744), dharma-heir of the Sixth Patriarch [[慧能|Huìnéng]] 慧能 and teacher of [[道一|Mǎzǔ Dàoyī]] 馬祖道一. Active on Mount Héng 衡山 / Nányuè 南嶽; founder of the line that became the Hóngzhōu 洪州 school and, through Mǎzǔ's heirs, the principal post-Huìnéng Chán lineage from which Línjì and Wěiyǎng schools both descend.
