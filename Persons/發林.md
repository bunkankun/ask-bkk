# Fālín 發林 (Héngxiù Fālín 恒秀發林)

*Hào* **Héngxiù** 恒秀 ("Constantly Beautiful"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yúnhuàn Yìnchén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623–1669) at **Bāzhōu Lóngtán Zhōngfēng Chánsì** 巴州龍潭中峰禪寺 in the PòshānSìchuān sub-line. Subsequently active at the **Lǜluó Héngxiù** 綠蘿恒秀 abbacy. Lifedates not preserved.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0493]] 《雲幻宸禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B353 — compiled his master Yìnchén's *yǔlù*.
- [[KR6q0494]] 《綠蘿恒秀林禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B355 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by *fǎsì* **Guāngyōu** [[光悠]] *děng biān*.
- Likely also contributor of the *xíngzhuàng* of [[悟卓]] **Pòshí Wùzhuó** 破石悟卓 (1609–1654), preserved in 《錦屏破石卓禪師雜著》 [[KR6q0228]] — though identification with the Pòshān-line Fālín above is plausible but not certain.
