# Fā-lín 發林 (Héng-xiù Fā-lín 恒秀發林)

*Hào* **Héng-xiù** 恒秀 ("Constantly Beautiful"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yún-huàn Yìn-chén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623–1669) at **Bā-zhōu Lóng-tán Zhōng-fēng Chán-sì** 巴州龍潭中峰禪寺 in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Subsequently active at the **Lǜ-luó Héng-xiù** 綠蘿恒秀 abbacy. Lifedates not preserved.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0493]] 《雲幻宸禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B353 — compiled his master Yìn-chén's *yǔ-lù*.
- [[KR6q0494]] 《綠蘿恒秀林禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B355 — his own *yǔ-lù*, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Guāng-yōu** [[光悠]] *děng biān*.
- Likely also contributor of the *xíng-zhuàng* of [[悟卓]] **Pò-shí Wù-zhuó** 破石悟卓 (1609–1654), preserved in 《錦屏破石卓禪師雜著》 [[KR6q0228]] — though identification with the Pòshān-line Fā-lín above is plausible but not certain.
