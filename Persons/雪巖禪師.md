**Xuěyán chánshī** 雪巖禪師 (*fl.* late-Qīng), Chán Buddhist monk and physician credited as compiler (*zuǎnjí* 纂輯) of the *Tāichǎn xīnshū* 胎產新書 ([[KR3ei078]]). The work was *kǎodìng* (verified-and-fixed) by **Jìngguānglún Yīng chánshī** 靜光輪應禪師. Both figures are part of the late-Qīng Chán-Buddhist popular-charity (*císhàn*) medical tradition — temple-based physician-monks who compiled obstetrical and other clinical manuals for free distribution as merit-accumulation works. The works are typically anonymous-attributive (carrying only a *chánshī* signature without given names), and the figures are not recorded in standard biographical sources. The *Tāichǎn xīnshū* is preserved in the Hǎiwài huíguī recension. Not in CBDB; not in DILA Buddhist person authority.
