**Xuě-yán chán-shī** 雪巖禪師 (*fl.* late-Qīng), Chán Buddhist monk and physician credited as compiler (*zuǎn-jí* 纂輯) of the *Tāi-chǎn xīn-shū* 胎產新書 ([[KR3ei078]]). The work was *kǎo-dìng* (verified-and-fixed) by **Jìng-guāng-lún Yīng chán-shī** 靜光輪應禪師. Both figures are part of the late-Qīng Chán-Buddhist popular-charity (*cí-shàn*) medical tradition — temple-based physician-monks who compiled obstetrical and other clinical manuals for free distribution as merit-accumulation works. The works are typically anonymous-attributive (carrying only a *chán-shī* signature without given names), and the figures are not recorded in standard biographical sources. The *Tāi-chǎn xīn-shū* is preserved in the Hǎi-wài huí-guī recension. Not in CBDB; not in DILA Buddhist person authority.
