Late-Míng (Wàn-lì-era) classicist and minor official, native of Quán-jiāo 全椒 (Chú-zhōu prefecture, modern Ān-huī). *Zì* Dàoxíng 道行. *Jìnshì* of *Wàn-lì* 8 / *gēng-chén* 庚辰 (1580). CBDB records the name (id 131727, index year 1550, suggesting a birth around mid-century) but no firm lifedates. Two further homonymous entries (293590, 488328) appear in CBDB; the present id is the most plausible match given the *jìnshì* year and the late-Wàn-lì *Chūnqiū* activity. *Míng-rén zhuànjì zīliào suǒyǐn* p. 695 is the standard modern reference.

His one canonical work is the *Chūnqiū zhì yí* 春秋質疑 ([[KR1e0087]]) — a focused, item-by-item critique of Hú Ānguó's [[KR1e0036]] *Chūnqiū zhuàn*, finished around the very end of the sixteenth century: Yáng's own preface is dated *Wàn-lì jǐ-hài* 萬曆己亥 (1599), and the second preface by Qiū Yīnghé 邱應和 is dated *Wàn-lì gēng-zǐ* 萬曆庚子 (1600). The work is broadly aligned with the contemporaneous anti-Hú current of Yuán Rén [[袁仁]], Xú Xuémó [[徐學謨]], and Jiāng Bǎo [[姜寶]], but Yáng's tone is distinctly less polemical and more probabilistic — its title-character *zhì* 質 ("to put a question, to seek confirmation") signals scholarly inquiry rather than wholesale rejection.
