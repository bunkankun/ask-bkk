Sòng-era Chán monk of the *chuán-fǎ shā-mén* 傳法沙門 rank at the Zhāng-jiāng Chán Yuàn 章江禪院 in Lóngshā 龍沙 (= Nánchāng 南昌, Jiāngxī, the regional name "Lóngshā" being a literary epithet for the city); author of two short commentaries on Péi Xiū's prefaces to the *Yuánjué jīng* commentary apparatus: [[KR6i0566]] *Yuánjué jīng xù-zhù* (X10 no. 255) on Péi's preface to the sūtra, and [[KR6i0567]] *Yuánjué jīng lüèshū xù-zhù* (X10 no. 256) on Péi's preface to Zōngmì's *Lüèshū*. His lifedates and biographical details are not preserved; the works themselves carry no date and his active period is bracketed at 1100–1279 on the basis of internal evidence and dependence on a fully developed Zōngmì exegetical apparatus.
