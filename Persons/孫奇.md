**Sūn Qí** 孫奇 (*fl.* 1057–1067, 宋), Northern Sòng official-physician and senior collator in the imperial **Bureau for Editing Medical Books** (*Jiàozhèng yīshū jú* 校正醫書局) under [[林億]]. The presentation memorial of the 1069 *Chóng guǎng bǔ zhù Huángdì nèijīng Sùwèn* ([[KR3ea003]] / [[KR3e0001]]) lists him with the title 朝奉郎守國子博士同校正醫書. He collaborated on the Sòng-period codification of the *Sùwèn*, *Língshū* ([[KR3ea023]] / [[KR3e0002]]), *Nán jīng*, *Shānghán lùn*, *Jīnguì yàoluè*, and other foundational classics. He is conventionally paired with his brother (or kinsman) Sūn Zhào 孫兆, also a bureau collator. He has no transmitted free-standing biography.
