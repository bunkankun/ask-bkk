Chāo-zì 超自, *zì* **Hé-yī** 何一 ("How One"); often referred to as **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** 何一超自. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xìng-kōng Xíng-zhēn** [[行臻]] (1608–1678). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Hé-nán Qǐ-xiàn** 河南杞縣; lay surname **Wáng** 王.

**Formation.** At 19 met an unusual monk who urged him to leave the world; tonsured at **Dà-liáng Mí-tuó-sì** 大梁彌陀寺 under **Jìng-wú** 靜梧法師. Day-and-night devotions to Avalokiteśvara; dreams of a **gā-lán** (monastery-protector spirit). Went south, joined **Qīng-quán-sì** 清泉寺 as *zhī-kè* 知客, then promoted to *chán-táng*. Drowsy at first, took up *cān*-fǎng under **Cuì-lín chán-shī** 翠林禪師 — finally found grip in *cān-chán*. Travelled north to **Yān-shān** 燕山 to study under **Bù-yè-tiān-shī** 不夜天師 (= **Tiān-tóng Bù-yè-tiān**) and took precepts under **Yù-lín** 玉林和尚. Held abbacy at **Chóng-níng chán-yuàn** 崇寧禪院. Later visited by **Xìng-kōng** [[行臻]] and received *fù-fǎ*.

**Disciples.** Twelve *fǎ-sì* including **Yuán-fēng Míng-xiū** 元峰明修 [[明修]], **Kǒng-yì Míng-huì** 孔意明惠 [[明惠]], **Lǚ-ān Míng-zhì** 旅菴明智 [[明智]] (all named in [[KR6q0575]] as compilers).

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0574]] 《性空臻禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B461.
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0575]] 《何一自禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B462.

**Sources.** 《何一自禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈行實〉; 《五燈全書》.
