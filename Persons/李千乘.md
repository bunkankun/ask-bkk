**Lǐ Qiānshèng** 李千乘 was a late-Táng Daoist commentator, author of [[KR5g0210]] *Tài shàng Huáng tíng zhōng jǐng jīng* 太上黃庭中景經 — a Huáng tíng 黃庭 meditation text.
