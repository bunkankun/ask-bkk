**Zhāng Zhuō** 張倬 (*zì* Fēichóu 飛疇, *fl.* early Qīng), Qīng-period physician of Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū). Son of [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù (author of *Zhāngshì yītōng*); brother of [[張登]] Zhāng Dēng. Author of the ***Shānghán jiānzhèng xīyì*** 傷寒兼證析義 ([[KR3e0094]], 1 juan) — a specialist treatise on cold-damage with comorbid miscellaneous-disease conditions.
