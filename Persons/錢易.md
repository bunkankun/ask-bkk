**Qián Yì** 錢易 (c. 970–1024), *zì* Xī-bái 希白. Native of Lín-ān 臨安 (Wú-yuè / modern Hángzhōu). Son of the Wú-yuè king Qián Cóng 錢俶 (929–988), the last Wú-yuè ruler who surrendered to the Sòng in 978. *Jìnshì* of *Chún-huà* 3 (992). Held a succession of central and metropolitan posts under *Zhēn-zōng* — *Hàn-lín xué-shì*, *Kāi-fēng zhī-xiàn* (during the *Dà-zhōng xiáng-fú* era, 1008–1016, the period of [[KR3l0026]] *Nánbù xīnshū* composition), *Zhī zhì-gào*. Author also of the now-lost *Jiā-zhī táng jí* 嘉枝堂集 (literary collection) and several other titles listed in *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén zhì*. CBDB id 146 records the name without firm lifedates; the standard scholarly dates (c. 970–1024) are based on the *Sòng shǐ* 317 biography and the *Yù-hǎi* notice. He was a recognised literary figure of the *Dà-zhōng xiáng-fú* — *Tiān-xǐ* era.
