# Fǎyè 法業

Eastern Jìn *śramaṇa* (沙門 / 釋) who served as scribe (筆受) at the Dàochǎngsì 道場寺 in Jiànkāng for [[佛馱跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra's 418–420 translation of the 60-fascicle [[KR6e0001|*Dà fāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng* 大方廣佛華嚴經]]. His role as scribe (literally "personally received [the rendering] with the brush") is recorded in the closing colophon of the work. Lifedates unrecorded.
