Modern Chinese scholar of Buddhism, formerly of the Shanghai Academy of Social Sciences (上海社會科學院). Specialist in *Sānjiē-jiào* 三階教 and Dūnhuáng Buddhist manuscripts. Edited the *Fóxìng wèndá* [[KR6v0052]] for the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* 藏外佛教文獻 series.
