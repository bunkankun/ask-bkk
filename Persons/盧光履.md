**Lú Guānglǚ** 盧光履 (Korean **No Kwang-ri** 노광리, 1730–1784, *hào* Wù-zhāi 勿齋), Joseon-Korean *yangban* Confucian scholar of the late-eighteenth century. He wrote the preface and oversaw the publication of the Korean paediatric handbook *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* 保幼新編 ([[KR3ej005]]), a work which the preface attributes to a Míng-period Chinese gentleman of unknown name styled Wújì-xiānshēng 無忌先生 whose manuscript had been preserved by the Buddhist monk Zhèngxùn 正訓. Lú's preface, dated *yǐ-sì* 乙巳 (1785, *Qī-xī* 七夕), is signed *Wù-zhāi bìng-fū Lú Guānglǚ* 勿齋病夫盧光履 ("the sickly fellow of the Wùzhāi studio"). Although the catalog records his dynasty as 清 (the Sino-centric framing of the Joseon tributary period), Lú was a Korean scholar and the work belongs to the Joseon-Korean medical tradition.
