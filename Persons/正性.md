# Zhèngxìng 正性

Late-Míng Buddhist monk, self-styled *Tóngxī tuìyuàn tóutuó* 桐溪退院頭陀 ("retired-abbot ascetic of Tóngxī"); author of the *Bōrě jìjué tící* preface (No. 552-B) to [[大慧]] Dàhuì's *Bōrě xīnjīng jìjué* ([[KR6c0171]]), dated Chóngzhēn 14 (1641), composed "at the East Tiānlóng Side-Chamber of the Pure-Brahma Altar". Associated with the Sūzhōu Wú-mén Sānfēng-circle Línjì revival.
