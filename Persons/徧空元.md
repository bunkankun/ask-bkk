# Biànkōng Yuán 徧空元

A mid-Qīng monk from Xiùyún 岫雲 who came in 1752 to ask [[通理]] Dátiān Tōnglǐ to lecture on Jiāoguāng Zhēnjiàn's *Zhèngmài shū*, occasioning the composition of Tōnglǐ's own *Zhǐzhǎng shū* ([[KR6j0716]]).
