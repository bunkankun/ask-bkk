Sēngxiáng 僧詳 (also written 僧祥; lifedates unknown, *fl.* late 7th – early 8th c.), Táng-dynasty Buddhist monk, compiler of the **《法華傳記》** *Fǎhuá zhuànjì* ([[KR6r0067]], T2068), a 10-juan biographical-thaumaturgical compendium of the *Lotus Sūtra*'s exemplars on the model of [[KR6r0066]] *Hóngzàn Fǎhuá zhuàn* of [[惠詳]], and a parallel partner-text to it.

The compiler's lifedates and biographical particulars are not securely transmitted; he is registered in the catalogs simply as a Táng monk known for this single biographical-historical work on the *Lotus*-cult. The orthographic confusion of his name (僧詳 / 僧祥) is paralleled by that of his contemporary [[惠詳]] (惠詳 / 慧詳), and the two are not infrequently conflated in the secondary literature. They are however distinct persons, with distinct works, attested by separate authorial colophons. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001597.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6r0067]] *Fǎhuá zhuànjì*.
