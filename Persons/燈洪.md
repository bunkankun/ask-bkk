Dēnghóng 燈洪. Línjì-school *shūjì* (*shūjì* 書記, scribe) of **Qiáosōng Xuányì** [[玄億]] (b. 1619) at **Wòyún chányuàn** 臥雲禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0555]] 《高峰喬松億禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B440. The juan-1 inner-title gives his role as "高峰喬松億禪師語錄卷一 書記燈洪錄".
