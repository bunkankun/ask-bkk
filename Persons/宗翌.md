Northern-Sòng Buddhist monk; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. DILA Authority A000478. Author of the **Zhù Fǎhuá běnjì shí bùèr mén** 註法華本迹十不二門 ([[KR6d0161]], X56n0927, 1 *juan*) — a Northern-Sòng annotated commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* ([[KR6d0157]]). His own preface to the work is dated Xiánpíng 咸平 1 (= 998 CE).

Source: *Zhù Fǎhuá běnjì shí bùèr mén* preface (998); DILA A000478.
