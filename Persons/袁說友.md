# Yuán Shuōyǒu 袁說友 (1140–1204)

*Zì* Qǐ-yán 起巖. *Hào* Dōng-táng 東塘. Native of *Jiàn-ān* 建安 (modern Jiàn-ōu, Fú-jiàn); family resettled at *Hú-zhōu* 湖州 (Zhè-jiāng). Lifedates 1140–1204, CBDB id 14881.

*Jìnshì* of *Lóng-xīng* 1 (1163). Career: extensive; *yáng-lì zhōng-wài* (extended-experience in court and provinces) for thirty years. As *Sì-chuān ān-fǔ-shǐ* (Pacification Commissioner of Sì-chuān) in the 1180s, commanded his subordinates Chéng Yù-sūn 程遇孫 et al. to compile *Shǔ*-region poetry-and-prose from Western Hàn through *Chún-xī* into the *Chéng-dū wén-lèi* in 50 *juǎn* — one of the principal Southern-Sòng regional literary anthologies. Under Níng-zōng in the *Jiā-tài* era (1201–1204), held office to *Tóng-zhī Shū-mì-yuàn* and *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì*. No *Sòng-shǐ* biography (the *Sì-kù* editors find this incomprehensible).

His policy memorials are particularly noted: the *Lùn shǒu-Huái yí yòng wǔ-chén* (Defending the Huái should use military officials) and the *Shǔ-jiàng dāng lǜ qí biàn* (On the Sì-chuān Generals — anticipate their treason) — the latter precisely anticipated the 1206 Wú Xī rebellion in Sì-chuān, which broke out two years after Yuán's death.

His prose-genealogy is in the *Yuán-yòu* surviving-tradition — he composed colophons on Sī-mǎ Guāng, Hán Qí, Ōuyáng Xiū, Sū Shùn-qīn, Sū Shì, Huáng Tíng-jiān, Cài Xiāng, and Mǐ Fú. His ancient-style poetry is placed by the *Sì-kù* editors at the level of Fàn Chéng-dà 范成大 and Lù Yóu 陸游 — among the *Sì-dà-jiā* tier — making this an unusually high senior-poet evaluation.

- *Chéng-dū wén-lèi* 成都文類 (50 *juǎn*; commissioned anthology, separately listed in *Jí-bù zǒng-jí*).
