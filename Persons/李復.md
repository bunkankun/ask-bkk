**Lǐ Fù** 李復 (fl. *Yuánfēng* 1079– *Chóngníng*), *zì* Lǚzhōng 履中, ancestrally of Kāifēng Xiángfú 開封祥符 but resettled in Chángān 長安 (Shǎnxī) following his father's appointment in *Guānyòu*. *Jìnshì* of *Yuánfēng* 2 / 1079; reached *Xī-Hé zhuǎnyùnshǐ* (Fiscal Intendant of the *Xī-Hé* circuit) and ultimately *Zhōngdàfū jíxiándiàn xiūzhuàn*. As a *xībiān shǐzhě* (envoy on the western frontier) in *Shàoshèng* he was a noted military-administrative specialist; long resident on the frontier, his memorial-prose handles military and frontier affairs with unusual fluency. Author of *Juéshuǐ jí* 潏水集 [[KR4d0111]] originally in 40 *juǎn*. Met Zhāng Zǎi 張載 [[張載]] in his youth — preserved in the *Juéshuǐ jí*. *Sòng shǐ* has no biography of him; sources are Hóng Mài 洪邁's *Róngzhāi suíbǐ* and Zhū Xī's *yǔlù*. The *Sìkù* tíyào notes Zhū Xī's slip of attributing him to Mǐn 閩 (Fújiàn) — Lǐ Fù was not from Fújiàn.
