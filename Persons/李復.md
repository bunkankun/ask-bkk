**Lǐ Fù** 李復 (fl. *Yuán-fēng* 1079– *Chóng-níng*), *zì* Lǚ-zhōng 履中, ancestrally of Kāi-fēng Xiáng-fú 開封祥符 but resettled in Cháng-ān 長安 (Shǎn-xī) following his father's appointment in *Guān-yòu*. *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-fēng* 2 / 1079; reached *Xī-Hé zhuǎn-yùn-shǐ* (Fiscal Intendant of the *Xī-Hé* circuit) and ultimately *Zhōng-dà-fū jí-xián-diàn xiū-zhuàn*. As a *xī-biān shǐ-zhě* (envoy on the western frontier) in *Shào-shèng* he was a noted military-administrative specialist; long resident on the frontier, his memorial-prose handles military and frontier affairs with unusual fluency. Author of *Jué-shuǐ jí* 潏水集 [[KR4d0111]] originally in 40 *juǎn*. Met Zhāng Zǎi 張載 [[張載]] in his youth — preserved in the *Jué-shuǐ jí*. *Sòng shǐ* has no biography of him; sources are Hóng Mài 洪邁's *Róng-zhāi suí-bǐ* and Zhū Xī's *yǔ-lù*. The *Sìkù* tíyào notes Zhū Xī's slip of attributing him to Mǐn 閩 (Fú-jiàn) — Lǐ Fù was not from Fú-jiàn.
