**Lǔ Bósì** 魯伯嗣 (sometimes written **Lǔ Bósìxué** 魯伯嗣學, on the supposition that *Bósìxué* is a fuller form of the personal name; equally likely the *xué* is the verb-suffix *xué* "studied", indicating *Bósì who studied [medicine]"). Early Míng paediatrician of unknown native place and lifedates, traditionally credited with compilation of the paediatric *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]]). The 1506 *Zhèngdé* edition prefaces by Wáng Yúnfèng 王云鳳 and Xiāo Qiānzǐyù 蕭謙子豫 question the secure attribution: both note that no independent evidence (*zuǒ yàn* 左驗) for Lǔ's authorship survives, and that material in the work overlaps with the *Yīngrú lùn bǎipiān* 嬰孺論百篇 traditionally ascribed to Qián Yǐ 錢乙 of the Northern Sòng. Lǔ is plausibly an early-Míng (14th–early 15th c.) compiler-editor working with received Sòng paediatric materials. No CBDB record; no securely datable biographical evidence beyond the *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* attribution.
