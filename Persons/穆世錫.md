**Mù Shìxī** 穆世錫 (*fl.* late Wàn-lì era, late 16th to early 17th c.), late-Míng physician and dietary-medical compiler. The sole transmitted attribution is to the *Shí-wù jí-yào* 食物輯要 ([[KR3eo046]]). No biographical data recoverable. No CBDB record.
