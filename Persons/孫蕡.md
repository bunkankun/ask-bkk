Sūn Fén 孫蕡 (1338–1393, per CBDB id 129790; the catalog meta has 1334–1389 which is inconsistent with the documented Lán Yù execution date and is corrected here), *zì* **Zhòng-yǎn** 仲衍, *hào* **Xī-ān** 西菴, native of **Shùn-dé** 順德 (Guǎng-dōng). Head of the **Yuè Wǔ xiānshēng** 粵五先生 (Five Masters of Yuè) — with **Wáng Zuǒ** 王佐, **Zhào Jiè** 趙介, **Lǐ Dé** 李德, and **Huáng Zhé** 黃哲. Hóngwǔ 3 (1370) *jǔ-rén*, soon *jìn-shì*; *Gōng-bù Zhī-rǎn jú shǐ* 工部織染局使; *Hóng-xiàn zhǔ-bù* 虹縣主簿; *Hàn-lín-yuàn diǎn-jí* 翰林院典籍; *Píng-yuán zhǔ-bù* 平原主簿; *Sū-zhōu jīng-lì* 蘇州經歷; banished to Liáo-dōng; ultimately executed in 1393 in the **Lán Yù** 藍玉 case for having inscribed a painting for Lán. **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 wrote the preface for Sūn's now-lost *Xiào-jīng jí-shàn* 孝經集善. Authored: [[KR4e0048]] *Xī-ān jí* 西菴集 (surviving); *Tōng-jiàn qián-biān gāng-mù* 通鑑前編綱目, *Xiào-jīng jí-shàn*, *Lǐ-xué xùn-méng* 理學訓蒙, *Hé Táo shī* 和陶詩, *Jí gǔ lǜ-shī* 集古律詩 (all lost or fragmentary). Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §28.4, places Sūn at the head of the early-Míng Guǎng-dōng literary tradition.
