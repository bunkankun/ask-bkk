Sūn Fén 孫蕡 (1338–1393, per CBDB id 129790; the catalog meta has 1334–1389 which is inconsistent with the documented Lán Yù execution date and is corrected here), *zì* **Zhòngyǎn** 仲衍, *hào* **Xīān** 西菴, native of **Shùndé** 順德 (Guǎngdōng). Head of the **Yuè Wǔ xiānshēng** 粵五先生 (Five Masters of Yuè) — with **Wáng Zuǒ** 王佐, **Zhào Jiè** 趙介, **Lǐ Dé** 李德, and **Huáng Zhé** 黃哲. Hóngwǔ 3 (1370) *jǔrén*, soon *jìnshì*; *Gōngbù Zhīrǎn jú shǐ* 工部織染局使; *Hóngxiàn zhǔbù* 虹縣主簿; *Hànlínyuàn diǎnjí* 翰林院典籍; *Píngyuán zhǔbù* 平原主簿; *Sūzhōu jīnglì* 蘇州經歷; banished to Liáodōng; ultimately executed in 1393 in the **Lán Yù** 藍玉 case for having inscribed a painting for Lán. **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 wrote the preface for Sūn's now-lost *Xiàojīng jíshàn* 孝經集善. Authored: [[KR4e0048]] *Xīān jí* 西菴集 (surviving); *Tōngjiàn qiánbiān gāngmù* 通鑑前編綱目, *Xiàojīng jíshàn*, *Lǐxué xùnméng* 理學訓蒙, *Hé Táo shī* 和陶詩, *Jí gǔ lǜshī* 集古律詩 (all lost or fragmentary). Wilkinson, *Chinese History*, §28.4, places Sūn at the head of the early-Míng Guǎngdōng literary tradition.
