**Yìcāo** 義操 (fl. early 9th c., active under emperors Shùn-zōng 順宗 / Xiàn-zōng 憲宗 / Mù-zōng 穆宗 = ca. 805–824) — Tang Esoteric master of the **Qīng-lóng-sì** 青龍寺 East-Pagoda Cloister (青龍寺東塔院) at Cháng-ān; direct disciple of **Huì-guǒ** 惠果 (746–805) and a major node in the Tang Esoteric lineage that transmitted both the Garbhadhātu and Vajradhātu *abhiṣeka* into the second Tang Esoteric generation. Lifedates and native place unknown. He was conferred the title of **State Preceptor** (*guóshī* 國師) under three reigns.

He received the dual-mandala **Garbhadhātu** (胎藏界) and **Vajradhātu** (金剛界) transmissions from Huì-guǒ at the same imperial Esoteric establishment where Kūkai had received them slightly earlier. Among his transmitted disciples were **Hǎiyún** 海雲, **Fǎrùn** 法潤, **Yìzhēn** 義真, **Shēndá** 深達, **Dàyù** 大遇, **Wénfàn** 文范, and most consequentially **Fǎquán** 法全 ([[法全]]), the master who taught **Ennin** ([[圓仁]]), **Enchin** ([[圓珍]]), and **Shūei** 宗叡.

His extant works include the **Liǎngbù jīngāng míngháo** 兩部金剛名號 (a 2-fascicle directory of the names of the dual-mandala Vajra deities) and the **Tāizàng jīngāng jiàofǎ míngháo** 胎藏金剛教法名號 ([[KR6j0023]], T18n0864B).
