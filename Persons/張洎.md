Zhāng Jì 張洎 (933–996; CBDB id 8012), *zì* Shī-àn 師黯, native of Chú-zhōu 滁州 Quán-jiāo 全椒. Originally a senior Southern-Táng official (held *Tài-zǐ zhōng-yǔn* 太子中允 and other Southern-Táng court offices); entered Northern-Sòng service after the 975 conquest. He was at the time of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* commission the *Tài-zǐ zhōng-yǔn*. He rose under Tài-zōng to *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* 參知政事 (Grand Councillor). He was one of the fourteen named compilers of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* ([[KR3k0012]]). His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 267.
