Zhāng Jì 張洎 (933–996; CBDB id 8012), *zì* Shīàn 師黯, native of Chúzhōu 滁州 Quánjiāo 全椒. Originally a senior Southern-Táng official (held *Tàizǐ zhōngyǔn* 太子中允 and other Southern-Táng court offices); entered Northern-Sòng service after the 975 conquest. He was at the time of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* commission the *Tàizǐ zhōngyǔn*. He rose under Tàizōng to *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 (Grand Councillor). He was one of the fourteen named compilers of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* ([[KR3k0012]]). His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 267.
