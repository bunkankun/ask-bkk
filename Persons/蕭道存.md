Northern-Sòng Daoist, *hào* Hùnyīzǐ 混一子 ("Master Mixed-into-One"), of Zhānggòng 章貢 (modern Gànzhōu 贛州, Jiāngxī). The author of [[KR5a0150|DZ 149 *Xiūzhēn tàijí hùnyuán tú* 修真太極混元圖]], an illustrated *nèidān* 內丹 treatise of the ZhōngLǚ 鍾呂 transmission. According to his own preface, dated *guǐmǎo*, Xiāo received the secret of the *jīnhuǒ huánfǎn* 金火返還 (return-and-revolution of metal and fire) from a "former teacher" who took pity on his austerities; he travelled to Nanjing where he encountered (in vision or reality) the Táng patriarch Shī Jiānwú 施肩吾 (Huáyáng zhēnrén 華陽真人) and based his diagram on Shī's lost *Xiūliàn tàijí hùnyuán tú* 修煉太極混元圖. The work is closely linked to the practice expounded in the *Xiūzhēn zhǐxuán piān* 修真指玄篇 of [[KR5c1017|DZ 1017 *Dàoshū*]] 19. No CBDB record was found.
