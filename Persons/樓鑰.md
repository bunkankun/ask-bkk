Lóu Yuè 樓鑰 (1137–1213), zì Dàfáng 大防, hào Gōngkuì zhǔrén 攻媿主人, posthumous title Xuānxiàn 宣獻, was a Southern Sòng official, statesman, and bibliographer of the Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (Sìmíng 四明 = Níngbō) school. He took the *jìnshì* in 1163, rose to Hànlín xuéshì 翰林學士, Lǐbù shàngshū 禮部尚書 and Cānzhī zhèngshì 參知政事 under Níngzōng. His collected works *Gōngkuì jí* 攻媿集 (or *Gōngkuì xiānsheng wénjí*) survives in 112 *juàn* and is a fundamental source for late-12th- / early-13th-century Sòng history; he also undertook a notable 1169 mission to the Jīn court (recorded in *Běi xíng rì lù* 北行日錄). His preface (嘉定元年, 1208) to Shěn Shū's 沈樞 *Tōngjiàn zǒnglèi* 通鑑總類 [[KR2h0002]] is the best surviving notice of that work's origin. CBDB dates from standard biographies; *Sòngshǐ* 宋史 j.395.
