Yáng Chéngqìng 楊承慶 (fl. early-to-mid sixth century), a Northern Wèi 北魏 lexicographer. *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records his *Zì tǒng* 字統 ([[KR1j0109]]) in twenty *juàn* — one of the larger pre-Suí character lexicons, in the *Shuōwén* / *Yùpiān* lineage. The work was lost between the Táng and Sòng but is among the most frequently cited Northern-Wèi *xiǎoxué* sources in the [[KR1j0022]] *Yùpiān* of [[顧野王]] Gù Yěwáng, in the *Guǎngyùn* 廣韻, and in Buddhist *yīnyì* literature; reconstructed in CHANT.
