Hóngxiē 弘歇. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Xuějiāo Yuánxìn** [[圓信]] (1571–1647). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (jointly with **Hóngzhū** 弘珠) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0602]] 《雪嶠信禪師語錄》 10 juan, J40 / Qiánlóng-canon No. 1638.
