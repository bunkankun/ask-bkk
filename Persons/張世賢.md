Early-to-mid Ming physician; *zì* Tiānchéng 天成 (in some accounts). Author of [[KR3eb048]] *Tú zhù Wáng Shū hé Mài jué*, an early-Ming illustrated commentary on the pseudo-Wáng-Shūhé *Mài jué*, widely reprinted in the late Ming. Otherwise lightly documented; no CBDB record.
