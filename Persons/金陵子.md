**Jīn líng zǐ** 金陵子 ("Master of Jīn líng [= Nánjīng]") was an early Northern-Sòng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0307]] *Lóng hǔ huán dān jué* 龍虎還丹訣 ("Instructions on the Dragon-Tiger Return-Elixir"), traditionally dated 1042 (Qìng lì 2). The "dragon-tiger" (*lóng hǔ* 龍虎) pair denotes lead and mercury (or *yáng* and *yīn*) in the alchemical lexicon.
