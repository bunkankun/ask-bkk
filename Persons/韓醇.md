# Hán Chún 韓醇

*Zì* Zhòngsháo 仲韶. Native of *Línqióng* 臨邛 (modern *Qiónglái* 邛崍 in Sìchuān). Sòng-period scholar; floruit *ca.* 1170–1180 by internal evidence. Per the Sìkù tíyào of [[KR4c0048]], "his beginning and end are unknown" — no biographical data has come down. Not to be confused with Hán Yù 韓郁 (a *Dàyú chéng* 大庾丞, related to Hán Yù 韓愈 by the same surname; Hán Yù 韓郁 produced an annotated edition of Liǔ Zōngyuán [[柳宗元]] mentioned in the Sòng catalogs).

His principal scholarly contribution is the *gǔxùn* 詁訓 (gloss-and-explanation) annotation of the Liǔ Zōngyuán [[柳宗元]] collection — the *Gǔxùn Liǔ xiānshēng wénjí* / *Liǔ Hédōng jí* [[KR4c0048]] in 45 *juǎn*, the foundational Sòng annotated edition of the corpus. The Sòng catalogs also record a parallel *Hán Chánglí jí jiàozhèng* 韓昌黎集校正 by him (an annotated edition of Hán Yù 韓愈), suggesting a pairing of HánLiǔ commentary work in the broader Sòng *fùgǔ* commemorative scholarship of the *Lóngxìng* / *Qiándào* generation.
