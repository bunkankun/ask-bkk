# Hán Chún 韓醇

*Zì* Zhòng-sháo 仲韶. Native of *Lín-qióng* 臨邛 (modern *Qióng-lái* 邛崍 in Sì-chuān). Sòng-period scholar; floruit *ca.* 1170–1180 by internal evidence. Per the Sìkù tíyào of [[KR4c0048]], "his beginning and end are unknown" — no biographical data has come down. Not to be confused with Hán Yù 韓郁 (a *Dà-yú chéng* 大庾丞, related to Hán Yù 韓愈 by the same surname; Hán Yù 韓郁 produced an annotated edition of Liǔ Zōng-yuán [[柳宗元]] mentioned in the Sòng catalogs).

His principal scholarly contribution is the *gǔ-xùn* 詁訓 (gloss-and-explanation) annotation of the Liǔ Zōng-yuán [[柳宗元]] collection — the *Gǔ-xùn Liǔ xiān-shēng wén-jí* / *Liǔ Hé-dōng jí* [[KR4c0048]] in 45 *juǎn*, the foundational Sòng annotated edition of the corpus. The Sòng catalogs also record a parallel *Hán Cháng-lí jí jiào-zhèng* 韓昌黎集校正 by him (an annotated edition of Hán Yù 韓愈), suggesting a pairing of Hán-Liǔ commentary work in the broader Sòng *fù-gǔ* commemorative scholarship of the *Lóng-xìng* / *Qián-dào* generation.
