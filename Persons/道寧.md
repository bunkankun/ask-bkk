# Kāifú Dàoníng 開福道寧

Northern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master (1053–1113), abbot of Kāifúsì 開福寺. Dharma-heir of [[法演|Wǔzǔ Fǎyǎn]] 五祖法演 in the Yángqí sub-lineage.
