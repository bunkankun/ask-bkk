Late-Yuán 元 Daoist scholar. **Died 1367** — one year before the Míng conquest (1368). Author of the **30-*juàn* *Nán huá zhēn jīng xún běn* 南華真經循本** ([[KR5c0135|DZ 742]]), a *Zhuāngzǐ* commentary.

**Activity period.** Active in the early-to-middle 14th century, contemporary with the decline of Mongol-Yuán rule and the rise of the Red Turban rebellions (1351 onwards). His death in 1367 places him in the very last generation of Mongol-Yuán Daoist scholars.

**Works.** His sole substantial surviving work is the ***Nán huá zhēn jīng xún běn*** (DZ 742) — a commentary on the *Zhuāngzǐ* that integrates Neo-Confucian, Chán-Buddhist, and Daoist interpretive categories. See [[KR5c0135]] for the full entry.

**Dating.** Died 1367. No precise birth date or other biographical details are recorded. No CBDB record identified.
