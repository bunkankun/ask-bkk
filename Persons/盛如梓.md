Shèng Rúzǐ 盛如梓 (d. *c.* 1302; CBDB id 10763), self-styled Shùzhāi 庶齋, was a Yuán *bǐjì* writer of Qúzhōu 衢州 (Zhèjiāng). He served as *Chóngmíngxiàn pànguān* 崇明縣判官. His one surviving work is the *Shùzhāi lǎo xué cóng tán* 庶齋老學叢談 ([[KR3j0142]]) in 3 *juàn* (the second *juàn* further split into a *sub-juàn*, effectively 4), a *bǐjì* of classical-historical debate, poetic criticism, and court-and-society *yì shì* (omitted-affairs). He met with elder Yuán-period literati (the *Sìkù* editors note that he "*reached the early-Yuán elders' acquaintance — hence his record carries much elders' lingering discussion*"), giving the book primary-source value for the Yuán-period transmission of late-Sòng / Jīn literary memory.
