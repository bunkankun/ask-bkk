**Gāo Xuěqīng** 高雪清 — Qīng editor and supplier of supplementary prescriptions to [[袁于江]] Yuán Yújiāng's *Shēngshēng bǎolù* ([[KR3ei061]]); contributor to the *Shēngshēng wàilù* 生生外錄 appendix on neonatal care and external-medicine prescriptions. Not in CBDB.
