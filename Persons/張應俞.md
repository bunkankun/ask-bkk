**Zhāng Yìngyú** 張應俞 (fl. ca. 1617) is a late-Míng writer known only as the author of [[KR4k0047]] *Dù Piàn Xīnshū* 杜騙新書 (*New Book for Foiling Swindlers*). No biographical detail is available; no CBDB record has been located. The catalog gives the name in simplified-character form (张应俞); the traditional form is 張應俞.
