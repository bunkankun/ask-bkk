Guǎngdé 廣德, *zì* **Chúnbèi** 純備 ("Pure-and-Complete"); often referred to as **Chúnbèi Guǎngdé** 純備廣德. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Ěrdú Dàotài** 耳毒道泰 (in the Pòshān Hǎimíng line) — thus a co-disciple of **Huànzhù Míng** [[明|Huànzhù Míng]] (cf. [[KR6q0549]]). Lifedates unknown — active **1666–1672**+. Native of **Shǔdōng Fēngdū** 蜀東鄷都 (= 酆都, Sìchuān); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Took tonsure in middle age under **Huànzhù** 幻住和尚 (his elder dharma-cousin). Took full ordination at the seat of **Ěrdú** 耳毒和尚 and received final *fùfǎ* there.

**Abbacies.** **Yílíngzhōu Huánglíngshān Yuèxiá chányuàn** 彝陵州黃陵月峽禪院 (entered **康熙丙午** = **1666** spring on the invitation of *cānfǔ* 參府 **Fùgōng** 傅公 and *yóufǔ* 遊府 **Wèigōng** 魏公); **Sìguī Fóxīng Dàbēi chányuàn** 秭歸佛興大悲禪院 (founded by him in **康熙壬子** = **1672**).

**Disciples (three).** **Yuèchuáng Zhìshēn** 月幢智深, **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** 法幢智遠 [[智遠]] (lead compiler of his *yǔlù* and author of his own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0551]]), **Juéchuáng Zhìhóng** 覺幢智洪.

**Work.** [[KR6q0550]] 《純備德禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B433, compiled by *fǎsì* **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** [[智遠]] *děng biān*. Preface dated **康熙庚戌三月上巳** = **1670-04-21** by **Gǔtíng Dàolóng** 古庭道隆 + **Bódá** 博達 at **Qīnglínzhī Méi-...** 青林之梅...

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 14; 《純備德禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈聯芳〉; 《五燈全書》.
