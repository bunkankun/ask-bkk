Guǎng-dé 廣德, *zì* **Chún-bèi** 純備 ("Pure-and-Complete"); often referred to as **Chún-bèi Guǎng-dé** 純備廣德. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěr-dú Dào-tài** 耳毒道泰 (in the Pò-shān Hǎi-míng line) — thus a co-disciple of **Huàn-zhù Míng** [[明|Huàn-zhù Míng]] (cf. [[KR6q0549]]). Lifedates unknown — active **1666–1672**+. Native of **Shǔ-dōng Fēng-dū** 蜀東鄷都 (= 酆都, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** Took tonsure in middle age under **Huàn-zhù** 幻住和尚 (his elder dharma-cousin). Took full ordination at the seat of **Ěr-dú** 耳毒和尚 and received final *fù-fǎ* there.

**Abbacies.** **Yí-líng-zhōu Huáng-líng-shān Yuè-xiá chán-yuàn** 彝陵州黃陵月峽禪院 (entered **康熙丙午** = **1666** spring on the invitation of *cān-fǔ* 參府 **Fù-gōng** 傅公 and *yóu-fǔ* 遊府 **Wèi-gōng** 魏公); **Sì-guī Fó-xīng Dà-bēi chán-yuàn** 秭歸佛興大悲禪院 (founded by him in **康熙壬子** = **1672**).

**Disciples (three).** **Yuè-chuáng Zhì-shēn** 月幢智深, **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** 法幢智遠 [[智遠]] (lead compiler of his *yǔ-lù* and author of his own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0551]]), **Jué-chuáng Zhì-hóng** 覺幢智洪.

**Work.** [[KR6q0550]] 《純備德禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B433, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** [[智遠]] *děng biān*. Preface dated **康熙庚戌三月上巳** = **1670-04-21** by **Gǔ-tíng Dào-lóng** 古庭道隆 + **Bó-dá** 博達 at **Qīng-lín-zhī Méi-...** 青林之梅...

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 14; 《純備德禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈聯芳〉; 《五燈全書》.
