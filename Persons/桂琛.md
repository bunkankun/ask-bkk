# Luóhàn Guìchēn 羅漢桂琛

Five-Dynasties Chán master (867–928), abbot of the Dìzàng yuàn 地藏院; heir of [[師備|Xuánshā Shībèi]] 玄沙師備, dharma-transmission teacher of [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益, and the *Dìzàng* middle link in the Xuěfēng–Xuánshā–Dìzàng–Fǎyǎn lineage.
