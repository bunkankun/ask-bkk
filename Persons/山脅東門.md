**Yamawaki Tōmon** 山脅東門 (Sino-Japanese reading: **Shānxié Dōngmén**, *fl.* mid-late 18th c.) — Edo-period Japanese physician and disciple of [[賀川玄悅]] Kagawa Gen'etsu, founder of the Kagawa school of obstetrics. Compiler of the 48-case clinical appendix *Zǐxuánzǐ zhìyàn sìshíbā zé* 子玄子治驗四十八則 attached to Kagawa's *Sǎn lùn* ([[KR3ei062]], 1765), preserving detailed clinical-history records of Kagawa-school obstetrical practice (malpresentation, manual extraction, post-partum haemorrhage, pseudocyesis).
