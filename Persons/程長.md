Chéng Cháng 程長 (Jiā-jìng era, 16th cent.), *cóng-sūn* (collateral grand-nephew) of the Jiàn-wén martyr Chéng Tōng 程通 ([[程通]]), of **Jì-xī** 績溪 (Huī-zhōu). Compiled the recovered remnants of his great-uncle's writings into the 6 *juǎn* + 4 *juǎn* recension that became [[KR4e0075]] *Zhēn-bái yí-gǎo*. The compilation became possible only after the Jiā-jìng-era relaxation of the Yǒng-lè proscription on the Jiàn-wén loyalist families. No CBDB id confidently identified.
