Chéng Cháng 程長 (Jiājìng era, 16th cent.), *cóngsūn* (collateral grand-nephew) of the Jiànwén martyr Chéng Tōng 程通 ([[程通]]), of **Jìxī** 績溪 (Huīzhōu). Compiled the recovered remnants of his great-uncle's writings into the 6 *juǎn* + 4 *juǎn* recension that became [[KR4e0075]] *Zhēnbái yígǎo*. The compilation became possible only after the Jiā-jìng-era relaxation of the Yǒnglè proscription on the Jiànwén loyalist families. No CBDB id confidently identified.
