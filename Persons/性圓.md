# Xìngyuán 性圓

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[印|Fǎxǐyìn]] 法璽印 in the Xiàoyán / Hànshān Déqīng / Guānhéng sub-line. Head compiler (*děng biāncì* 等編次) of [[KR6q0434]] 《法璽印禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B220), working with three dharma-brothers **Xuánjī** 旋璣, **Xuánzhé** 旋哲, and **Xuánxián** 旋賢.

The 性-generation character is standard for the Xiào-yán-line 32nd-generation Línjì tier; the 旋-character co-editors represent a parallel generational branch. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0434]] juan-head signature line "*sìfǎ ménrén Xìngyuán Xuánjī Xuánzhé Xuánxián děng biāncì* 嗣法門人性圓旋璣旋哲旋賢等編次".
