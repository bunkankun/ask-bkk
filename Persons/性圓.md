# Xìng-yuán 性圓

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[印|Fǎ-xǐ-yìn]] 法璽印 in the Xiào-yán / Hàn-shān Dé-qīng / Guān-héng sub-line. Head compiler (*děng biān-cì* 等編次) of [[KR6q0434]] 《法璽印禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B220), working with three dharma-brothers **Xuán-jī** 旋璣, **Xuán-zhé** 旋哲, and **Xuán-xián** 旋賢.

The 性-generation character is standard for the Xiào-yán-line 32nd-generation Línjì tier; the 旋-character co-editors represent a parallel generational branch. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0434]] juan-head signature line "*sì-fǎ mén-rén Xìng-yuán Xuán-jī Xuán-zhé Xuán-xián děng biān-cì* 嗣法門人性圓旋璣旋哲旋賢等編次".
