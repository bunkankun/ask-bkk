# Jiǎ Rǔzhōu 賈汝舟

Yuán-dynasty official with the title *guóshǐ* 國史 (state-historian), known as the author of one of the Yuán-period reprint prefaces to Zōngmì's *Chányuán zhūquánjí dūxù* ([[KR6q0091]]).
