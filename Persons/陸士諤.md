Lù Shì-è 陸士諤, *míng* Shǒu-xiān 守先, *zì* Yún-xiáng 雲翔, *hào* Shì-è 士諤 (1878–1944), native of Qīng-pǔ 青浦 (now in Shàng-hǎi, then in Sōng-jiāng 松江 prefecture). A Republican-period scholar-physician and novelist of the Shàng-hǎi medical-literary milieu. He maintained a clinical practice in Sōng-jiāng (his preface to [[KR3ep078]] *Wáng Mèng-yīng yī-àn* is dated 1921 at "松江醫室"), in parallel with a substantial career as a prolific popular fiction-writer — his 1910 utopian novel *Xīn Zhōng-guó* 新中國 is one of the early classics of Chinese science-fiction. Medical works include the topical rearrangement of Wáng Mèng-yīng casebooks, *Yī-xué nán-zhēn* 醫學南針, and others. Not in CBDB.
