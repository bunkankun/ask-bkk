# Zēng Wéndiàn 曾文辿 (also written 曾文遄 Zēng Wénchuán)

Late-Tang / Five-Dynasties geomantic specialist; the principal disciple of Yáng Yúnsōng ([[楊筠松]]). Per the *Sìkù* 提要 of [[KR3g0022]] *Qīng-náng xù*: native of Gàn-shuǐ 贛水 (Jiāng-xī); his father Qiú-yǐ 求己 had earlier fled to Jiāng-nán under the Five Dynasties, where the *Jié-zhì* (Provincial Governor) Lǐ *sī-kōng* recruited him to administer the Nán-kāng military matters; Zēng Wéndiàn accordingly obtained Yáng Yúnsōng's geomantic technique. Later (per the 提要) the technique was transmitted to Chén Tuán 陳摶 — providing the institutional bridge between the *Yáng-shì* geomantic school and the Northern-Sòng *xiān-tiān* cosmological tradition.

His sole *Sìkù*-preserved work is the *Qīng-náng xù* 青囊序 ([[KR3g0022]]) — the geomantic-disciple's preface-and-exposition of Yáng Yúnsōng's [[KR3g0023]] *Qīng-náng ào-yǔ*.
