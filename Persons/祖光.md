# Zǔ-guāng 祖光

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *xiǎo-shī bǐ-qiū* 小師比丘 of [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí (1296–1370); heads the editorial byline of *juan* 1 (Fǔ-zhēn, 1324) of his master's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]). DILA carries a further lamp-record notice in *Xù-dēng zhèng-tǒng mù-lù* 續燈正統目錄 *juan* 1 ("Tiān-níng Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí chánshī fǎ-sì Zǔ-guāng shàng-zuò" 天寧楚石梵琦禪師法嗣祖光上座, X84 n1582), placing him as one of Fàn-qí's *sì-fǎ* 嗣法. Lifedates unrecorded.
