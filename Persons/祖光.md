# Zǔguāng 祖光

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *xiǎoshī bǐqiū* 小師比丘 of [[梵琦]] Chǔshí Fànqí (1296–1370); heads the editorial byline of *juan* 1 (Fǔzhēn, 1324) of his master's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]). DILA carries a further lamp-record notice in *Xùdēng zhèngtǒng mùlù* 續燈正統目錄 *juan* 1 ("Tiānníng Chǔshí Fànqí chánshī fǎsì Zǔguāng shàngzuò" 天寧楚石梵琦禪師法嗣祖光上座, X84 n1582), placing him as one of Fànqí's *sìfǎ* 嗣法. Lifedates unrecorded.
