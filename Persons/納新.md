Nàxīn 納新 (*fl.* 1340s, *Zhìzhèng* era), *zì* Yìzhī 易之. *Sèmùrén* (Yuán-period bureaucratic classification for non-Han peoples of the Western Regions), of the *Bóloluò* 博囉洛 / *Bóluòluò* clan — identified by the *Qīndìng Xīyù túzhì* with the Tarbaghatai region (modern north-western Xīnjiāng / eastern Kazakhstan). His ancestors migrated to China and settled first at Nányáng 南陽 in Hénán; the family later moved to Yínxiàn 鄞縣 (modern Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Held office as *shānzhǎng* (rector) of the Zhèdōng *Dōnghú shūyuàn*; by recommendation appointed *Hànlín guóshǐyuàn biānxiūguān*. In *Zhìzhèng* 5 (1345) he undertook a long antiquarian journey from Zhèjiāng across the Huái and the Yellow River through the historical heartland (Qí, Lǔ, Chén, Cài, Jìn, Wèi, Yān, Zhào), producing the *Héshuò fǎnggǔ jì* 河朔訪古記 ([[KR2k0134]]) — originally 16 juan with appended verses, now extant only in fragmentary 3-juan reconstruction. His other surviving work is the poetry collection *Jīntái jí* 金臺集 ([[KR4k0014]]?). One of the principal Sèmùrén Chinese-language literati of the Yuán. Not in CBDB.
