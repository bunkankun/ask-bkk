Nà-xīn 納新 (*fl.* 1340s, *Zhì-zhèng* era), *zì* Yì-zhī 易之. *Sè-mù-rén* (Yuán-period bureaucratic classification for non-Han peoples of the Western Regions), of the *Bóloluò* 博囉洛 / *Bóluòluò* clan — identified by the *Qīn-dìng Xī-yù tú-zhì* with the Tarbaghatai region (modern north-western Xīnjiāng / eastern Kazakhstan). His ancestors migrated to China and settled first at Nán-yáng 南陽 in Hé-nán; the family later moved to Yín-xiàn 鄞縣 (modern Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng). Held office as *shān-zhǎng* (rector) of the Zhè-dōng *Dōng-hú shū-yuàn*; by recommendation appointed *Hàn-lín guó-shǐ-yuàn biān-xiū-guān*. In *Zhì-zhèng* 5 (1345) he undertook a long antiquarian journey from Zhèjiāng across the Huái and the Yellow River through the historical heartland (Qí, Lǔ, Chén, Cài, Jìn, Wèi, Yān, Zhào), producing the *Hé-shuò fǎng-gǔ jì* 河朔訪古記 ([[KR2k0134]]) — originally 16 juan with appended verses, now extant only in fragmentary 3-juan reconstruction. His other surviving work is the poetry collection *Jīn-tái jí* 金臺集 ([[KR4k0014]]?). One of the principal Sè-mù-rén Chinese-language literati of the Yuán. Not in CBDB.
