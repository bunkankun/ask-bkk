**Xiāo Zhèn** 蕭震 (*fl.* mid-17th c., Mǐnzhōng 閩中 / Fújiàn) — son of the late-Míng / early-Qīng physician [[蕭京]] Xiāo Jīng. Carried his father's *Xuān-Qí jiùzhèng lùn* 軒岐救正論 ([[KR3er054]]) into print after his father's death, supplying a mourning postface (*bá* 跋) that opens with the conventional mourning vigil-in-the-straw-hut formula (倚廬伏苫). Not in CBDB.
