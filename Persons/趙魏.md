**Zhào Wèi** 趙魏 (1746–1825, *zì* 晉齋 Jìnzhāi, *hào* 洛生 Luòshēng), Qīng bibliophile and bronze-and-stone (*jīnshí*) scholar of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu). Friend of [[許槤]] Xǔ Lián; transmitted to him the manuscript of [[倪枝維]] Ní Zhīwéi's *Chǎnbǎo* ([[KR3ei057]]) which Xǔ Lián later printed in 1842. Compiler of several epigraphical-bronze catalogues; a minor but documented figure in Qīng late-Qiánlóng-to-Dàoguāng bibliophile circles. Not in CBDB.
