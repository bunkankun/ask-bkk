**Xú Jiànkuí** 徐漸逵 (*hào* Xùzhāi 煦齋), mid-Qīng scholar, *fl. Dàoguāng* era. Prefaced Zhāng Xiáxī 張霞谿's posthumous *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* 麻疹闡註 ([[KR3ej046]]) at the Lǐchéng *shìyù* 蠡城試寓 (examination lodge) in *Dàoguāng* 28 (1848). Lifedates and other biographical detail not securely recorded.
