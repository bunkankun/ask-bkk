**Xú Jiàn-kuí** 徐漸逵 (*hào* Xù-zhāi 煦齋), mid-Qīng scholar, *fl. Dào-guāng* era. Prefaced Zhāng Xiá-xī 張霞谿's posthumous *Má-zhěn chǎn-zhù* 麻疹闡註 ([[KR3ej046]]) at the Lǐ-chéng *shì-yù* 蠡城試寓 (examination lodge) in *Dào-guāng* 28 (1848). Lifedates and other biographical detail not securely recorded.
