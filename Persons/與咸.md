Sòng-period Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Xūzhōng** 虛中 (also 虗中), sobriquet **Zéshān** 澤山 (with full name **Zéshān Yǔxián** 澤山與咸; sometimes also **Zéshānsǒu** 澤山叟 "Old Man of Zéshān"). DILA Authority A000953. Lifedates unrecorded.

Author of the **Fùzōng jí** 復宗集 ([[KR6d0231]], X57n0955, 1 *juan*) — Sòng Tiāntái-school polemical-doctrinal treatise.
