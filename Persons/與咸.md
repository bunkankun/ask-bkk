Sòng-period Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Xū-zhōng** 虛中 (also 虗中), sobriquet **Zé-shān** 澤山 (with full name **Zé-shān Yǔ-xián** 澤山與咸; sometimes also **Zé-shān-sǒu** 澤山叟 "Old Man of Zé-shān"). DILA Authority A000953. Lifedates unrecorded.

Author of the **Fù-zōng jí** 復宗集 ([[KR6d0231]], X57n0955, 1 *juan*) — Sòng Tiāntái-school polemical-doctrinal treatise.
