**Hé Bǐngyuán** 何炳元 (1861–1929), better known by his *zì* **Hé Liánchén** 何廉臣, was the leading Shàoxīng 紹興 physician of the late Qīng and early Republic and a major reformer of Chinese-medicine pedagogy. Native of Shàoxīng (Yuè 越), where he practiced and taught. He edited and printed numerous earlier *wēn-bìng* and *shānghán* works under the imprint of the Shàoxīng Yī-xué Bào 紹興醫學報 and the Shàoxīng Yī-yào Xué Bào 紹興醫藥學報, including the *Gǎn zhèng bǎo fá* 感症寶筏 ([[KR3ef055]], 1911, his re-edition and retitling of Wú Kūn'ān's *Shānghán zhǐ-zhǎng*) and many other clinical compilations. His own works include *Chóng dìng Guǎng wēn rè lùn* 重訂廣溫熱論 (a major epidemic-medicine reference) and *Quán guó míng-yī yàn àn lèi biān* 全國名醫驗案類編 (a nationwide case-records compilation that remains an indispensable reference). He died in 1929. He is one of the principal figures of the late-Qīng Shàoxīng medical school.
