**Hóng Yán** 洪炎 (1067–1133), *zì* Yùfù 玉父, of Nánchāng 南昌 (Jiāngxī). Third of the *Sì Hóng* 四洪 brothers (Hóng Péng [[洪朋]], Hóng Chú [[洪芻]], Hóng Yán, Hóng Yǔ); all four were Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]]'s nephews. *Jìnshì* of late-*Yuányòu*. After the southern crossing held office as *Mìshū shǎojiān* (Vice-Director of the Imperial Library). Of the *Sì Hóng*, Hóng Yán's collection alone survived through to the Qīng in a complete form: the *Xīdù jí* 西渡集 [[KR4d0141]] in 2 *juǎn*, preserved in the Bào family's *Zhībùzúzhāi* in Zhèjiāng. The other three brothers' collections (Hóng Péng's *Hóng Guīfù jí* [[KR4d0131]], Hóng Chú's *Lǎopǔ jí* [[KR4d0142]], Hóng Yǔ's collection — lost) are *Sìkù* reconstructions from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. Listed in Lǚ Běnzhōng's *Jiāngxī shīshè zōngpài tú*. Catalog gives no dates; lifedates 1067–1133 from CBDB.
