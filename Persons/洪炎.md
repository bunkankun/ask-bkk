**Hóng Yán** 洪炎 (1067–1133), *zì* Yù-fù 玉父, of Nán-chāng 南昌 (Jiāng-xī). Third of the *Sì Hóng* 四洪 brothers (Hóng Péng [[洪朋]], Hóng Chú [[洪芻]], Hóng Yán, Hóng Yǔ); all four were Huáng Tíng-jiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]]'s nephews. *Jìnshì* of late-*Yuán-yòu*. After the southern crossing held office as *Mì-shū shǎo-jiān* (Vice-Director of the Imperial Library). Of the *Sì Hóng*, Hóng Yán's collection alone survived through to the Qīng in a complete form: the *Xī-dù jí* 西渡集 [[KR4d0141]] in 2 *juǎn*, preserved in the Bào family's *Zhī-bù-zú-zhāi* in Zhè-jiāng. The other three brothers' collections (Hóng Péng's *Hóng Guī-fù jí* [[KR4d0131]], Hóng Chú's *Lǎo-pǔ jí* [[KR4d0142]], Hóng Yǔ's collection — lost) are *Sìkù* reconstructions from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*. Listed in Lǚ Běn-zhōng's *Jiāng-xī shī-shè zōng-pài tú*. Catalog gives no dates; lifedates 1067–1133 from CBDB.
