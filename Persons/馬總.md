**Mǎ Zǒng** 馬總 (*d.* 823) was a mid-Táng scholar-official, compiler of the ***Yì lín*** 意林 ([[KR5g0071]]) — an anthology of extracts (*leishu*-style) from non-canonical philosophical texts. The *Yì lín* is a significant source for fragments of otherwise-lost Warring-States and Hàn works.
