**Wáng Qiú** 王璆, *zì* **Shíkě** 師可, *hào* **Shìzhāi** 是齋 (*fl.* late 12th c., precise lifedates uncertain). Southern-Sòng official with serious medical interest. Native place uncertain (the 1196 preface to his *Shìzhāi bǎiyī xuǎnfāng* does not record it). Compiler of the *Shìzhāi bǎiyī xuǎnfāng* 是齋百一選方 ([[KR3ed027]]) in 20 *juǎn*, completed *qìngyuán* 2 (1196). The work was lost in China by the mid-Ming but preserved in Tokugawa-era Japan through the Tanba 丹波 medical-family library; the 1799 reprint by Sēndà Gōngshī 千田恭子 returned the work to China. Not in CBDB.
