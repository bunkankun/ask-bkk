Lǐ Guāngtíng 李光廷 (fl. mid-19th c.) was a Cantonese literatus who contributed an 1865 preface to [[潘名熊]] Pān Míngxióng's *Píngqín shūwū yīlüè* ([[KR3eh033]]).
