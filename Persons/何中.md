Yuán-period 元 Jiāngxī polymath: commentator on the *Yì*, *Shū*, *Yùnpǔ*, *Liùshū*; *Tōngjiàn*-classical exegete; prose-stylist. *Zì* **Tàixū** 太虛. Native of **Lèān** 樂安 in **Fǔzhōu** 撫州 (Jiāngxī) — the same county as **Wú Chéng** [[吳澄]] [[KR4d0446]] (his *biǎodì* / cousin-relation). CBDB 35415 records 1265–1332.

**Reception.** **Wú Chéng**'s preface to the *Zhīfēitáng gǎo* (Zhìshùn 2 = 1331, one year before Hé's death) places Hé Zhōng in the *Hàn-Sòng* eight-masters prose-tradition — evaluating him as having "reached" the Hàn-master level. A remarkable late-life endorsement from the major Yuán Confucian patriarch.
