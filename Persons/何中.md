Yuán-period 元 Jiāng-xī polymath: commentator on the *Yì*, *Shū*, *Yùn-pǔ*, *Liù-shū*; *Tōng-jiàn*-classical exegete; prose-stylist. *Zì* **Tài-xū** 太虛. Native of **Lè-ān** 樂安 in **Fǔ-zhōu** 撫州 (Jiāng-xī) — the same county as **Wú Chéng** [[吳澄]] [[KR4d0446]] (his *biǎo-dì* / cousin-relation). CBDB 35415 records 1265–1332.

**Reception.** **Wú Chéng**'s preface to the *Zhī-fēi-táng gǎo* (Zhì-shùn 2 = 1331, one year before Hé's death) places Hé Zhōng in the *Hàn-Sòng* eight-masters prose-tradition — evaluating him as having "reached" the Hàn-master level. A remarkable late-life endorsement from the major Yuán Confucian patriarch.
