# Zhìshàn 至善

Late-17th to early-18th century Qīng Línjì-lineage Chán monk; dharma-heir of [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685). Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Preface-writer for the *Gāofēng Sānshān Lái chánshī nián pǔ* 高峰三山來禪師年譜 [[KR6q0209]] — the year-by-year biographical chronology of Dēnglái compiled by [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統. The preface represents Zhìshàn's institutional endorsement of Xìngtǒng's biographical project as a legitimate preservation of their shared master's memory.

A separate Línjì-school Chán master also named **Zhìshàn** 至善, *zì* **Tóngzhēn** 童真 ("Childlike Truth"), *biéhào* **Wènwēng** 汶翁 / **Wànfēng** 萬峰. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1608–1663), thus a co-disciple of **Héngshān Dēngbǐng** [[燈炳]] (cf. [[KR6q0554]]) and **Qiáosōng Xuányì** [[玄億]] (cf. [[KR6q0555]]). Lifedates unknown — active **1654–1678+**. Native of **Yúzhōu Jiāngjīn** 渝州江津 (Sìchuān, Chóngqìng region); lay surname **Jiāng** 江; descendant of the Sòng-era 少師 **Wéndìnggōng Yuān** 文定公淵.

**Formation.** Began as *shāmí* under Qìngzhōng. At 15, reading the **Dàhuì zǔlù** at the *zhúbìzǐ* 竹篦子 *gōngàn*, raised doubt. **甲午** = **1654** met **Sānshān** 三山和尚 (= 三山燈來!) at **Chóngshèng** 崇聖. After two more years and a reading of the **Jùyún lù** 聚雲錄 at the *Chángshā Huìān* *gōngàn*, broke through and received final *yìnkě* from Qìngzhōng.

**Abbacies (Húběi).** **Húguǎng Déānfǔ Xiàogǎnxiàn Jiǎntáng chányuàn** 湖廣德安府孝感縣簡堂禪院; **Déānfǔ Xiàogǎnxiàn Gāofēng chányuàn** 德安府孝感縣高峰禪院 (entered **康熙十七年戊午閏三月十二日** = **1678**). In **康熙七年** = **1668** at **Èzhōu Dàbiéshān Wòyúnān** 鄂州大別山臥雲庵 (WǔHàn region) overlapping with [[玄億]]'s tenure.

**Work.** [[KR6q0556]] 《萬峰童真禪師語錄》 6 juan (DILA: 5 juan), J39 B441, recorded by *shìzhě* **Délín** [[德林]] *jì*; preface by **Lǐ Dàojì** 李道濟 (Píngshān Lóngsūn) of Jízhōu dated **康熙丁未孟夏** = **1667** lunar 4.

Sources: 《萬峰童真禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行由〉; 《續燈正統》 vol. 18 (X84 n1583).
