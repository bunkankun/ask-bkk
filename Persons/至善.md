# Zhì-shàn 至善

Late-17th to early-18th century Qīng Línjì-lineage Chán monk; dharma-heir of [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685). Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Preface-writer for the *Gāofēng Sānshān Lái chánshī nián pǔ* 高峰三山來禪師年譜 [[KR6q0209]] — the year-by-year biographical chronology of Dēnglái compiled by [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統. The preface represents Zhì-shàn's institutional endorsement of Xìngtǒng's biographical project as a legitimate preservation of their shared master's memory.

A separate Línjì-school Chán master also named **Zhì-shàn** 至善, *zì* **Tóng-zhēn** 童真 ("Childlike Truth"), *bié-hào* **Wèn-wēng** 汶翁 / **Wàn-fēng** 萬峰. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1608–1663), thus a co-disciple of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] (cf. [[KR6q0554]]) and **Qiáo-sōng Xuán-yì** [[玄億]] (cf. [[KR6q0555]]). Lifedates unknown — active **1654–1678+**. Native of **Yú-zhōu Jiāng-jīn** 渝州江津 (Sì-chuān, Chóng-qìng region); lay surname **Jiāng** 江; descendant of the Sòng-era 少師 **Wén-dìng-gōng Yuān** 文定公淵.

**Formation.** Began as *shā-mí* under Qìng-zhōng. At 15, reading the **Dà-huì zǔ-lù** at the *zhú-bì-zǐ* 竹篦子 *gōng-àn*, raised doubt. **甲午** = **1654** met **Sān-shān** 三山和尚 (= 三山燈來!) at **Chóng-shèng** 崇聖. After two more years and a reading of the **Jù-yún lù** 聚雲錄 at the *Cháng-shā Huì-ān* *gōng-àn*, broke through and received final *yìn-kě* from Qìng-zhōng.

**Abbacies (Húběi).** **Hú-guǎng Dé-ān-fǔ Xiào-gǎn-xiàn Jiǎn-táng chán-yuàn** 湖廣德安府孝感縣簡堂禪院; **Dé-ān-fǔ Xiào-gǎn-xiàn Gāo-fēng chán-yuàn** 德安府孝感縣高峰禪院 (entered **康熙十七年戊午閏三月十二日** = **1678**). In **康熙七年** = **1668** at **È-zhōu Dà-bié-shān Wò-yún-ān** 鄂州大別山臥雲庵 (Wǔ-Hàn region) overlapping with [[玄億]]'s tenure.

**Work.** [[KR6q0556]] 《萬峰童真禪師語錄》 6 juan (DILA: 5 juan), J39 B441, recorded by *shì-zhě* **Dé-lín** [[德林]] *jì*; preface by **Lǐ Dào-jì** 李道濟 (Píng-shān Lóng-sūn) of Jí-zhōu dated **康熙丁未孟夏** = **1667** lunar 4.

Sources: 《萬峰童真禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行由〉; 《續燈正統》 vol. 18 (X84 n1583).
