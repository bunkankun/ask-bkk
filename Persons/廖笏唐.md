# 廖笏唐 Liào Bǐtáng

**Liào Bǐtáng** 廖笏唐, early-Republican-period Húnán literatus; member of the 1916 Chuánshān xuéshè 船山學社 reunion mentioned in [[熊希齡]] Xióng Xīlíng's 1927 colophon to [[譚志光]] Tán Zhìguāng's *Zhēnjiǔ wèndá* 針灸問答 ([[KR3ee035]]).
