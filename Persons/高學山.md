**Gāo Xuéshān** 高學山 (*zì* Hànzhì 漢峙, *fl.* mid-eighteenth century, 清), early-mid Qīng physician, native of Yúyáo 餘姚 (Zhèjiāng). Author of two principal extant works on the Zhāng Zhòngjǐng canon: the *Gāo zhù Shānghán lùn* 高註傷寒論 ([[KR3ef043]]?) and the *Gāo zhù Jīnguì yào lüè* 高註金匱要略 ([[KR3ef081]]). Gāo's commentary method is characterized by close clause-by-clause exegesis with attention to pulse-and-syndrome correlation, set against an explicit doctrinal background drawn from the *Nèi jīng* "*五常*" 五常 cosmology. Detailed biographical information is sparse; his works were transmitted independently and not collected into a *cóngshū*. The conventional dating places Gāo's productive period in the early to mid eighteenth century.
