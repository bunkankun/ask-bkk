Late-Míng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquets **Bǎi-sōng** 百松 (with full names **Bǎi-sōng Zhēn-jué** 百松真覺, **Bǎi-sōng Miào-fēng** 百松妙峰), **Miào-fēng shī** 妙峰師, **Fó-lǒng Zhēn-jué** 佛隴真覺. DILA Authority A001394. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the late Wàn-lì 萬曆 era.

Author of the **Sān-qiān yǒu-mén sòng lüè-jiě** 三千有門頌略解 ([[KR6d0230]], X57n0954, 1 *juan*) — brief commentary on the Tiāntái *sān-qiān yǒu-mén sòng* doctrine, with Ǒu-yì Zhìxù [[智旭]] providing the *jiào* 較 ("collation") editorial work.

Note: distinct from the Míng monk **Zhēn-jué** of Pǔ-tuó-shān 普陀山 (DILA A008041), founder of the Bàn-yún-ān 伴雲庵.
