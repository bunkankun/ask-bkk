Late-Míng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquets **Bǎisōng** 百松 (with full names **Bǎisōng Zhēnjué** 百松真覺, **Bǎisōng Miàofēng** 百松妙峰), **Miàofēng shī** 妙峰師, **Fólǒng Zhēnjué** 佛隴真覺. DILA Authority A001394. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the late Wànlì 萬曆 era.

Author of the **Sānqiān yǒumén sòng lüèjiě** 三千有門頌略解 ([[KR6d0230]], X57n0954, 1 *juan*) — brief commentary on the Tiāntái *sānqiān yǒumén sòng* doctrine, with Ǒuyì Zhìxù [[智旭]] providing the *jiào* 較 ("collation") editorial work.

Note: distinct from the Míng monk **Zhēnjué** of Pǔtuóshān 普陀山 (DILA A008041), founder of the Bànyúnān 伴雲庵.
