# Chéng Chuān 程川

Style name *Fūqú* 鄜渠, sobriquet *Chūn-tán* 春曇. Native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu 杭州). Nominated to the Qiánlóng 1 (1736) special *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 examination but the result is not preserved. The sole surviving documentation of his life is the *Sìkù tíyào* on his *Zhū zǐ Wǔ jīng yǔ lèi* 朱子五經語類 ([[KR1g0027]]), which records that he completed the work in *Yōngzhèng yǐ-sì* 雍正乙巳 (Yōngzhèng 3 = 1725) while studying at the Fū-wén shū-yuàn 敷文書院 in Hángzhōu. He must therefore have been an active scholar by the late Yōng-zhèng era, born no later than c. 1700.

His work the *Zhū zǐ Wǔ jīng yǔ lèi* is one of the more useful Yōngzhèng-era extracts of Zhū Xī's recorded sayings on the *Wǔ jīng*: it organizes the relevant passages from Lí Jìng-dé 黎靖德's *Zhū zǐ yǔ lèi* by *jīng* and topic, with the disciple-recorder's name and the year of recording noted under each entry — a useful prosopographic apparatus for tracking the development of Zhū's classical thought.
