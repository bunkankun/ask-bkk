**Dài Zuò** 戴祚 (late 4th century), sparsely attested late-Eastern-Jìn writer. No standard biography in the *Jìn shū*; he is known only from the *Suí shū jīng-jí zhì* 隋書經籍志 attribution of the *Zhēnyì zhuàn* 甄異傳 / *Zhēnyì jì* 甄異記 in 3 *juàn* to him, with corroborating entries in both Táng bibliographic treatises. Some Sòng *léi-shū* citations identify him as *Tài-zǐ shē-rén* 太子舍人 ("Companion to the Heir Apparent"), but the attribution cannot be securely cross-checked. He is conventionally placed in the late Eastern Jìn (c. 380–420), contemporary with Zǔ Tái-zhī. He has no CBDB id in the current dump.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the credited author of [[KR3l0164]] *Zhēnyì jì*.
