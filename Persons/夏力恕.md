Xià Lìshù 夏力恕 (1690–1754), *zì* Guānchuān 觀川, native of Xiàogǎn 孝感 (Hànyáng prefecture, modern Húběi). *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng 1 (1723); entered the Hanlin and rose to *biānxiū* and *jīngyán rìjiǎngguān* 經筵日講官. Principal *biānzuǎn* of the *Húguǎng tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0046]]) under the Húguǎng Governor-General Màizhù 邁柱 from 1730 to 1733; a substantial Húběi literatus with a reputation as a classical scholar. His own writings include the *Yáofēng tíng jí* 堯峰亭集 and a *Sì shū jí jiě* 四書集解. CBDB id 86068; lifedates per *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú niánbiǎo* (#18276); index_addr 8213 (Xiàogǎn) confirmed.
