Northern-Sòng *Yún-mén Chán* master, original surname Lǐ 李, *zì* Yǐn-zhī 隱之, conventionally known as Xuě-dòu Zhòng-xiǎn 雪竇重顯 from his late-life seat at Xuě-dòu sì 雪竇寺 on Mount Xuě-dòu in *Míng-zhōu* 明州 (modern *Fèng-huà* 奉化, Zhèjiāng); posthumous *Míng-jué dà-shī* 明覺大師. Of *Suì-zhōu* 遂州 origin (Sìchuān). Tonsured young under monk Rén-ráo 仁鐃 of *Pǔ-ān yuàn*; trained at *Líng-yǐn sì* in Hángzhōu and at *Cuì-fēng* under the *Yún-mén* line. Settled at Xuě-dòu sì in *Tiān-shèng* 8 / 1030 and led the monastery for 22 years. Died *Huáng-yòu* 4 / 1052 age 73. The principal Sòng *Chán* literary master: composed the *Sòng gǔ bǎi zé* 頌古百則 (Hundred *Gāthā*-Eulogies on Old Cases), the foundational verse-armature of Yuán-wù Kè-qín's *Bì-yán lù* [[KR6q0007]] and the founding work of the *kōan-shū* genre. Surviving collection: *Zǔ-yīng jí* [[KR4d0032]] (2 *juǎn*, 220 poems, edited by his pupil Wén-zhèng in 1032), and the SBCK *Xuě-dòu sì jí* [[KR4d0033]].
