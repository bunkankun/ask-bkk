Northern-Sòng *Yúnmén Chán* master, original surname Lǐ 李, *zì* Yǐnzhī 隱之, conventionally known as Xuědòu Zhòngxiǎn 雪竇重顯 from his late-life seat at Xuědòu sì 雪竇寺 on Mount Xuědòu in *Míngzhōu* 明州 (modern *Fènghuà* 奉化, Zhèjiāng); posthumous *Míngjué dàshī* 明覺大師. Of *Suìzhōu* 遂州 origin (Sìchuān). Tonsured young under monk Rénráo 仁鐃 of *Pǔān yuàn*; trained at *Língyǐn sì* in Hángzhōu and at *Cuìfēng* under the *Yúnmén* line. Settled at Xuědòu sì in *Tiānshèng* 8 / 1030 and led the monastery for 22 years. Died *Huángyòu* 4 / 1052 age 73. The principal Sòng *Chán* literary master: composed the *Sòng gǔ bǎi zé* 頌古百則 (Hundred *Gāthā*-Eulogies on Old Cases), the foundational verse-armature of Yuánwù Kèqín's *Bìyán lù* [[KR6q0007]] and the founding work of the *kōanshū* genre. Surviving collection: *Zǔyīng jí* [[KR4d0032]] (2 *juǎn*, 220 poems, edited by his pupil Wénzhèng in 1032), and the SBCK *Xuědòu sì jí* [[KR4d0033]].
