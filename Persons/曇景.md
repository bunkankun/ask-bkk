Tánjǐng 曇景 (active XiāoQí 蕭齊 / Southern Qí period, 479–502), Buddhist translator at the southern Qí court. Author of [[KR6g0029|*Mó hē mó yē jīng* 摩訶摩耶經]] (T0383), the *Mahāmāyāsūtra* on the Buddha's mother. Birth and death dates not preserved.
