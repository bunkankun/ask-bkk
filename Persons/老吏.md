Lǎo Lì 老吏 ("Old Official/Clerk") is a pen name used by the author of *Núcái Xiǎoshǐ* 奴才小史 ([[KR4k0306]]), a Republican-era critique of political servility in the Qīng dynasty framed as historical biography. The real identity is unknown. Active ca. 1910s–1930s.
