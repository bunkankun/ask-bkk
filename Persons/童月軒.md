Tóng Yuèxuān 童月軒 (*fl.* late 19th–early 20th c.) was a literatus-collector of Húnán Xiāngxī 湘西, scion of a hereditary household, who assembled over decades a manuscript collection of more than 200 "tested" family formulas from kin and acquaintances. This manuscript collection was published in 1923 by his friend Cài Lùqiū 蔡鹿秋 as the *Lìyàn zàishòu biān* 歷驗再壽編 ([[KR3ed065]]), distributed through provincial and county-level charitable halls and benevolent societies. Tóng's collecting activity falls late-Qīng but the work as printed is Republican-era; the catalog meta assigns a Qīng dynasty marker on the basis of the underlying material. No CBDB entry.
