Xióng Mèngbǐng 熊孟秉 (lifedates unknown, fl. early-to-mid Yuán), *zúsūn* (clan-grandson) of Xióng Hé 熊禾 [[熊禾]], native of Jiànyáng 建陽. Known only as the posthumous editor of Xióng Hé's collected works *Wùxuān jí* [[KR4d0396]], which he compiled from the surviving manuscripts in eight *juàn* in the early Yuán. No CBDB record. The *Sìkù* editors note that his recension was the basis for the Míng Tiān-shùn-era (1457–1464) print by Xióng Bīn 熊斌, Xióng Hé's sixth-generation descendant.
