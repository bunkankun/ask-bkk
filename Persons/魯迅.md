Lǔ Xùn 魯迅 (pen name of Zhōu Shùrén 周樹人, 1881–1936), the foundational modern Chinese literary author. In the Kanripo context he appears as the editor of an early-modern Chinese edition of the *Bǎiyú jīng* 百喻經 ([[KR6b0066]]) published in Beijing in 1914 — one of the foundational documents of the early-modern Chinese Buddhist text-edition tradition.
