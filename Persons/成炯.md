Chéngjiǒng 成炯 (also written 烱), *hào* **Yùtáng** 昱堂. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yīkuí Yuánkuí** [[元揆]] 一揆元揆 (b. 1634) in the Mùchén Dàomín → Yuǎnān → Yīkuí line. Lead compiler of the 12-juan 《神鼎一揆禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0513]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
