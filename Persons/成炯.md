Chéng-jiǒng 成炯 (also written 烱), *hào* **Yù-táng** 昱堂. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yī-kuí Yuán-kuí** [[元揆]] 一揆元揆 (b. 1634) in the Mù-chén Dào-mín → Yuǎn-ān → Yī-kuí line. Lead compiler of the 12-juan 《神鼎一揆禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0513]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
