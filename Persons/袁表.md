Late-Míng (Wàn-lì-era) literatus of **Fúzhōu** 福州 (Fújiàn). *Zì* **Jǐngcóng** 景從. Co-editor with [[馬熒]] Mǎ Yíng of the **[[KR4h0098]]** *Mǐnzhōng shízǐ shī* 閩中十子詩 (30 *juǎn*, compiled **Wànlì 4 = 1576**), the canonical Míng-era anthology of the **Jìnān** (Fúzhōu) regional poetry school's founding generation — the **Ten Masters of Mǐnzhōng** including [[高棅]] Gāo Bǐng. The CBDB has several "袁表" but none with confident dating match for this Wàn-lì-era Fúzhōu man; no entry retained. No other works under his name are transmitted.
