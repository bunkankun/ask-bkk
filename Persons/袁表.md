Late-Míng (Wàn-lì-era) literatus of **Fú-zhōu** 福州 (Fú-jiàn). *Zì* **Jǐng-cóng** 景從. Co-editor with [[馬熒]] Mǎ Yíng of the **[[KR4h0098]]** *Mǐn-zhōng shí-zǐ shī* 閩中十子詩 (30 *juǎn*, compiled **Wàn-lì 4 = 1576**), the canonical Míng-era anthology of the **Jìn-ān** (Fú-zhōu) regional poetry school's founding generation — the **Ten Masters of Mǐn-zhōng** including [[高棅]] Gāo Bǐng. The CBDB has several "袁表" but none with confident dating match for this Wàn-lì-era Fú-zhōu man; no entry retained. No other works under his name are transmitted.
