Yú Jìdēng 余繼登 (1544–1600), *zì* Shìyòng 世用, *hào* Yúnqú 雲衢, posthumous *shì* Wénquè 文恪, of Jiāohé 交河 (Zhílì). Took the *jìnshì* in Wànlì 5 (1577); cumulatively officed *Lǐbù shàngshū*. He is the principal *anti-kuàng-shuì* (anti-mine-tax) memorialist of the late-Wàn-lì period, successfully winning the cancellation of the **SìChuān kuàngshuì** to fund the 1600 **Bōzhōu** campaign — the only mine-tax suspension successfully won under Wànlì. He also requested the emperor to install the heir-apparent and withdraw the **eunuch envoys**. He died in office in 1600. His friend **Féng Qí** 馮琦 compiled and cut his *Dànránxuān jí* [[KR4e0222]] posthumously. CBDB 34741 has zero markers; *Míngshǐ* j. 216 confirms 1544–1600.
