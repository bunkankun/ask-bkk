# Jiāng Diànyáng 姜殿揚

Republican-era (民國) editor associated with the Sìbù cóngkān 四部叢刊 (SBCK) project. He is responsible for the recovery and editing of the complete 50-*juǎn* recension of Quán Déyú's [[權德輿]] *Quán Zàizhī wénjí* [[KR4c0041]] — a manuscript that the *Sìkù* compilers had been unable to locate (they printed only the truncated 10-*juǎn* Yáng Shèn 楊慎 1541 recovery as [[KR4c0040]]). Jiāng's collation and supplementation work (撰校勘記) preserved the only complete witness of one of the principal mid-Tang prose corpora. CBDB (which only covers pre-modern figures) does not register him.
