Zhāng Xiǎn 張顯 (*fl.* Jìn) is an Eastern Jìn 東晉 figure known almost exclusively from the title-attribution on his lost *Xīyán lùn* 析言論 (preserved in *Tài-píng yù-lǎn*, *Yìwén lèijù*, *Ěr-yǎ shì-wén*, and *Lù shǐ*). No biography survives in the standard histories. His exact birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally assigned to the Eastern Jìn period on the basis of the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* placement. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0155]] *Xīyán lùn* 析言論.
