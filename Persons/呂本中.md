Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 (1084–1145), *zì* Jūrén 居仁, *hào* Zǐwēi 紫微, posthumously called *Dōnglái xiānsheng* 東萊先生 (a name later more commonly used for his great-grand-cousin Lǚ Zǔqiān), was a major Northern–Southern Sòng transition classicist, poet, and *Lǐxué* figure. Native of Shòuzhōu 壽州 (An-huī). Member of the great Lǚ lineage of Lǚ Yīhào 呂夷浩 and Lǚ Gōngzhuó 呂公著 — the same line that produced Lǚ Zǔqiān (1137–1181). Held a sequence of central posts and rose under Gāozōng to *Zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人, *Quán Hànlín xuéshì* 權翰林學士. Demoted in 1141 over his anti-Qín Huì position; exiled and died there. His major works: the *Chūnqiū jíjiě* 春秋集解; the *Tóngméng xùn* 童蒙訓 ([[KR3a0037]], the principal Sòng family-school instructional manual); *Dōnglái shīhuà* 東萊詩話 (poetic-criticism notebook); *Zǐwēi shīhuà* 紫微詩話; the *Dōnglái xiān-sheng shī jí* 東萊先生詩集 (collected poems). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 376. CBDB id 10703, dates 1084–1145.
