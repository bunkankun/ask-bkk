Chén Yì 陳鎰 (Yuán, *fl.* mid-14th c.), *zì* Bó-zhū 伯銖, *hào* Wǔ-xī 午溪 (from the *Wǔ-xī* stream beside which he built his retirement house, whence the *Wǔ-xī jí* [[KR4d0536]] title). Native of Lì-shuǐ 麗水 (Chǔ-zhōu, Zhèjiāng). Once *Sōng-yáng jiào-shòu*. Studied verse from his maternal-uncle Zhōu Héng 周衡; later from Zhāng Zhù [[張翥]] (see Sūn Yán 孫炎's preface). Edited by Qǔ-fù Kǒng Yáng 孔暘 (*qián-jìnshì*); collated by Liú Jī 劉基 of Qīng-tián (also *qián-jìnshì*). The Liú-Jī letter at the head of the collection — recommending that Chén drop "*jìn-rì yìng-chóu zhī zuò*" — is one of the few extant Liú-Jī editorial letters. The catalog meta gives no lifedates; the *fl.* mid-14th-c. is inferred from Zhāng Zhù *zì-jiào* and Liú Jī *jiào-zhèng* dates.
