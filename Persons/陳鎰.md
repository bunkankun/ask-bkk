Chén Yì 陳鎰 (Yuán, *fl.* mid-14th c.), *zì* Bózhū 伯銖, *hào* Wǔxī 午溪 (from the *Wǔxī* stream beside which he built his retirement house, whence the *Wǔxī jí* [[KR4d0536]] title). Native of Lìshuǐ 麗水 (Chǔzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Once *Sōngyáng jiàoshòu*. Studied verse from his maternal-uncle Zhōu Héng 周衡; later from Zhāng Zhù [[張翥]] (see Sūn Yán 孫炎's preface). Edited by Qǔfù Kǒng Yáng 孔暘 (*qiánjìnshì*); collated by Liú Jī 劉基 of Qīngtián (also *qiánjìnshì*). The LiúJī letter at the head of the collection — recommending that Chén drop "*jìnrì yìngchóu zhī zuò*" — is one of the few extant LiúJī editorial letters. The catalog meta gives no lifedates; the *fl.* mid-14th-c. is inferred from Zhāng Zhù *zìjiào* and Liú Jī *jiàozhèng* dates.
