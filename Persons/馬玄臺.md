**Mǎ Xuántái** 馬玄臺 (given name 馬蒔 Mǎ Shí, *zì* Zhònghuà 仲化, *hào* Xuántái 玄臺), Míng-dynasty physician of Wàn-lì-era Sūzhōu / Huì-jī, principal Míng commentator on the *Nèijīng* — author of the *Sùwèn zhùzhèng fāwēi* 素問註證發微 and *Língshū zhùzhèng fāwēi* 靈樞註證發微 (9 *juǎn* each), the first full Míng commentaries on both the *Sùwèn* and *Língshū*. His readings are cited extensively in the late-Edo *kǎojù* medical tradition (e.g. by [[多紀元簡]] Taki Mototane).
