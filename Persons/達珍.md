# Dázhēn 達珍

Late-Qīng Chán monk; editor (編) of the *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* 正源略集 ([[KR6q0032]]) compiled by Jìyuán 際源 and Liǎozhēn 了貞. Active in the early nineteenth century; exact lifedates not transmitted.
