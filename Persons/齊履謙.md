Qí Lǚqiān 齊履謙 (1263–1329), *zì* Bóhéng 伯恒, native of Dàmíng 大名 (sometimes given as Shālù 沙鹿; modern Héběi). He was one of the leading Yuán mathematicians and calendrical scientists, eventually rising to *Tàishǐyuàn shǐ* 太史院使 (Director of the Astronomical Bureau). He served as *Guózǐ sīyè* 國子司業 in *Yányòu* 4 (1317), in which year he composed his self-preface to the *Chūnqiū zhūguó tǒngjì* 春秋諸國統紀 [[KR1e0059]]. Wú Chéng 吳澄 wrote the preface for him, treating him as a serious classical scholar in addition to his calendrical work. His biography is in *Yuán shǐ* 元史 j. 172. CBDB person 110780. He stands somewhat apart from the standard Yuán *Chūnqiū* school in that he came at the *Chūnqiū* with the analytical apparatus of an astronomer-calendrician.
