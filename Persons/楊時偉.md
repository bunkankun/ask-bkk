Late-Míng scholar from Sūzhōu 蘇州, flourished c. 1600–1625 (Wànlì–Tiānqǐ). His exact lifedates are not securely attested. He is principally known as the compiler of the *Zhūgě zhōngwǔ shū* 諸葛忠武書 ([[KR2g0014]]), a ten-*juàn* topical compilation on Zhūgě Liàng 諸葛亮 (Zhōngwǔhóu 忠武侯, 181–234), Shǔ Hàn 蜀漢 chancellor — building on Wáng Jiǒngbó's 王冏伯 sixteen-*juàn* *Wǔhóu quánshū* 武侯全書 of 1568 and supplementing it with the table of contents of Chén Shòu's 陳壽 lost *Zhūgě shì jí* 諸葛氏集. He is part of the constellation of Jiāngnán late-Míng scholars whose Sānguó 三國 historical-philological work runs in parallel to the popular *Sānguó yǎnyì* novelistic tradition.
