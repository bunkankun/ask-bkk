Late-Míng scholar from Sūzhōu 蘇州, flourished c. 1600–1625 (Wàn-lì–Tiān-qǐ). His exact lifedates are not securely attested. He is principally known as the compiler of the *Zhū-gě zhōng-wǔ shū* 諸葛忠武書 ([[KR2g0014]]), a ten-*juàn* topical compilation on Zhū-gě Liàng 諸葛亮 (Zhōng-wǔ-hóu 忠武侯, 181–234), Shǔ Hàn 蜀漢 chancellor — building on Wáng Jiǒng-bó's 王冏伯 sixteen-*juàn* *Wǔ-hóu quán-shū* 武侯全書 of 1568 and supplementing it with the table of contents of Chén Shòu's 陳壽 lost *Zhū-gě shì jí* 諸葛氏集. He is part of the constellation of Jiāng-nán late-Míng scholars whose Sān-guó 三國 historical-philological work runs in parallel to the popular *Sān-guó yǎn-yì* novelistic tradition.
