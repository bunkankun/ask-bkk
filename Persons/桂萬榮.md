Guì Wànróng 桂萬榮 (Sòng), *zì* Mèngxié 夢協, was a late-Southern-Sòng official. *Jìnshì* of *Shàoxī* 4 (1193). Author of the *Táng yīn bǐ shì* 棠陰比事 ([[KR3c0008]], dated 1196) — the classical *yí yù*-genre work in 4-character / 4-character paired-couplet form. CBDB id 33287.
