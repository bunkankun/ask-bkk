Hé Yǔdù 何宇度 (*fl.* late *Wànlì*, late 16th – early 17th c.). Late-Míng official; held the post of *Tōngpàn* of Kuízhōu 夔州 in eastern Sìchuān. Author of the *Yìbù tánzī* 益部談資 ([[KR2k0127]]) — a 3-juan late-Míng *bǐjì* on Sìchuān mountains, rivers, products, and historical anecdotes; the original imprint carried a colophon by Lǐ Wéizhēn 李維楨 (1547–1626) recommending the work, placing it in the late *Wànlì* period. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 128397 has no birth or death year.
