Hé Yǔ-dù 何宇度 (*fl.* late *Wàn-lì*, late 16th – early 17th c.). Late-Míng official; held the post of *Tōng-pàn* of Kuí-zhōu 夔州 in eastern Sìchuān. Author of the *Yì-bù tán-zī* 益部談資 ([[KR2k0127]]) — a 3-juan late-Míng *bǐ-jì* on Sìchuān mountains, rivers, products, and historical anecdotes; the original imprint carried a colophon by Lǐ Wéi-zhēn 李維楨 (1547–1626) recommending the work, placing it in the late *Wàn-lì* period. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 128397 has no birth or death year.
