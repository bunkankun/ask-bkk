# Dàojì 道漈

Eighth-century Chán monk recorded in the closing transmission-notice of the Dūnhuáng recension of the *Tánjīng* as fellow-student of [[法海]] Fǎhǎi and intermediary in the four-generation textual transmission Huìnéng → Fǎhǎi → Dàojì → Wùzhēn.
