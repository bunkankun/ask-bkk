Southern-Sòng official and book-printer, descendant of Liú Mù [[劉牧]]. As superintendent of the Zhèyòu Cáo Sī 浙右漕司 (Zhèjiāng Right-Circuit Transport Commission), he produced an edition of his ancestor's *Yìshù gōuyǐn tú* 易數鉤隱圖 ([[KR1a0011]]) carrying a preface attributed to Ōuyáng Xiū [[歐陽修]] — a preface later judged spurious by Wú Chéng [[吳澄]] and Yú Yǎn [[俞琰]] on the grounds that Ōuyáng's surviving writings show explicit disbelief in the *Hétú*.
