# Yuán-bǎo 元寶

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[汝現]] and [[慧朗]]) of *juan* 6–7 (*jì-sòng* 偈頌) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
