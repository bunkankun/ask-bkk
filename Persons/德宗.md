Dézōng 德宗, *zì* **Jìngtíng** 逕庭 ("Path-Garden") and **Yìwěi** 義偉 (the latter bestowed by **Qiězhuō** 且拙和尚); often referred to as **Jìngtíng Dézōng** 逕庭德宗. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yíshān Wòzhōng Zhìfú** 嶷山握中智符 (1625–1688). Born **明天啟辛酉八月四日** = **1621-09-19** Gregorian; died **康熙甲子九月初七日** = **1684-10-15**, age 64, *fǎlà* 35. Native of **Línglíng** 零陵 (Húnán); lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣.

**Formation.** At 13 felt the urge to leave the world but was restrained by his father and brothers; only after illness did they permit ordination at **Dōnglètáng** 東樂堂 under **Jiànqú** 見渠和尚. Wandered widely (Tiāndé, Xīlián, Luóhàn). Visited **Qiězhuō** who gave him the name *Yìwěi*. **順治辛卯** = **1651** returned home, took residence at **Shuǐyuèān** 水月菴. **癸巳** = **1653** read the **嶷山老人語錄** *Yíshānlǎorén yǔlù*, was struck, and invited Yíshān to take residence at his hermitage. **丁酉佛成道日** = **1657-12-30** received Yíshān's *fùzhǔ*.

**Abbacies (a remarkable twelve+).** **Qīngxiāng Nányuán Hóngshèngsì** 清湘南源洪聖寺 (the principal seat); **Shòufóān** 壽佛菴; **Lǐshān Shèngmíngsì** 理山聖明寺; **Xīnglóngān** 興隆菴; **Yíshān Zhùshèngsì** 嶷山祝聖寺; **Cáoyuán Bǎojìngsì** 曹源寶鏡寺; **Wòyúnshān Zuìshèngsì** 臥雲山最勝寺; **Biéchénān** 別塵菴; **Fúshòutáng** 福壽堂; **Qiānfólín** 千佛林; **Bèiyèlín** 貝葉林; **Bìluóān** 碧羅菴; **Pílúān** 毘盧菴; **Liánchíshān** 蓮池山. Buried at **Nányuán Běnshānsì** 南源本山寺.

**Disciples.** Eight *fǎsì* listed: **Wúzhèng Qiān** 無諍謙 [[行謙]], **Wéisù Dàn** 維素淡, **Xuánbǎi Zhū** 懸柏袾, **Biémù Chún** 別牧純 [[純]] (cf. [[KR6q0588]]), **Jièyuè Huái** 戒月懷, **Yúntāo Tán** 雲濤潭, **Biélàng Hǎi** 別浪海, **Zìtán Xī** 自曇希; plus lay disciple **Táng Xiāngshēng** 唐香生.

**Work.** [[KR6q0587]] 《逕庭宗禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B475, compiled by *fǎsì* **Wúzhèng Xíngqiān** [[行謙]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《逕庭宗禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈塔銘〉.
