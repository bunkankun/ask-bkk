Dé-zōng 德宗, *zì* **Jìng-tíng** 逕庭 ("Path-Garden") and **Yì-wěi** 義偉 (the latter bestowed by **Qiě-zhuō** 且拙和尚); often referred to as **Jìng-tíng Dé-zōng** 逕庭德宗. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yí-shān Wò-zhōng Zhì-fú** 嶷山握中智符 (1625–1688). Born **明天啟辛酉八月四日** = **1621-09-19** Gregorian; died **康熙甲子九月初七日** = **1684-10-15**, age 64, *fǎ-là* 35. Native of **Líng-líng** 零陵 (Húnán); lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣.

**Formation.** At 13 felt the urge to leave the world but was restrained by his father and brothers; only after illness did they permit ordination at **Dōng-lè-táng** 東樂堂 under **Jiàn-qú** 見渠和尚. Wandered widely (Tiān-dé, Xī-lián, Luó-hàn). Visited **Qiě-zhuō** who gave him the name *Yì-wěi*. **順治辛卯** = **1651** returned home, took residence at **Shuǐ-yuè-ān** 水月菴. **癸巳** = **1653** read the **嶷山老人語錄** *Yí-shān-lǎo-rén yǔ-lù*, was struck, and invited Yí-shān to take residence at his hermitage. **丁酉佛成道日** = **1657-12-30** received Yí-shān's *fù-zhǔ*.

**Abbacies (a remarkable twelve+).** **Qīng-xiāng Nán-yuán Hóng-shèng-sì** 清湘南源洪聖寺 (the principal seat); **Shòu-fó-ān** 壽佛菴; **Lǐ-shān Shèng-míng-sì** 理山聖明寺; **Xīng-lóng-ān** 興隆菴; **Yí-shān Zhù-shèng-sì** 嶷山祝聖寺; **Cáo-yuán Bǎo-jìng-sì** 曹源寶鏡寺; **Wò-yún-shān Zuì-shèng-sì** 臥雲山最勝寺; **Bié-chén-ān** 別塵菴; **Fú-shòu-táng** 福壽堂; **Qiān-fó-lín** 千佛林; **Bèi-yè-lín** 貝葉林; **Bì-luó-ān** 碧羅菴; **Pí-lú-ān** 毘盧菴; **Lián-chí-shān** 蓮池山. Buried at **Nán-yuán Běn-shān-sì** 南源本山寺.

**Disciples.** Eight *fǎ-sì* listed: **Wú-zhèng Qiān** 無諍謙 [[行謙]], **Wéi-sù Dàn** 維素淡, **Xuán-bǎi Zhū** 懸柏袾, **Bié-mù Chún** 別牧純 [[純]] (cf. [[KR6q0588]]), **Jiè-yuè Huái** 戒月懷, **Yún-tāo Tán** 雲濤潭, **Bié-làng Hǎi** 別浪海, **Zì-tán Xī** 自曇希; plus lay disciple **Táng Xiāng-shēng** 唐香生.

**Work.** [[KR6q0587]] 《逕庭宗禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B475, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Wú-zhèng Xíng-qiān** [[行謙]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《逕庭宗禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈塔銘〉.
