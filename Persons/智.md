# Zhì 智 (Tuì-ān Duàn-yú Zhì 退菴斷愚智)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí *chán-shī* of the **Pǔ-míng Yòng** 普明用 → **Yǒng-zhèng Yī-lǎo** 永正一老 sub-line of the **Hǔ-qiū → Jìng-shān → Duàn-qiáo Lún** 虎丘 → 徑山 → 斷橋倫 transmission. *Zì* **Duàn-yú** 斷愚 ("Severing Folly"); *hào* **Tuì-ān** 退菴 ("Retreat Hermit", bestowed by his teacher Yǒng-zhèng Yī-lǎo in **乙未春 = 1655 spring** for his Cháng-shuǐ 長水 retreat). Born **Tiān-qǐ 壬戌 4/27** = **1622-06-05 Gregorian**; native of **Xiù-shuǐ** 秀水 (Jiā-xīng), lay surname **Jīn** 金 (father styled **Chéng-dān** 成丹, mother **Táng** 唐). Death-date not located; *yǔ-lù* compiled c. 1670.

**Formation.** Both parents died young; raised by paternal grandmother (老祖). At 16 *suì* (**辛巳 = 1641**) tonsured at **Dōng-tǎ Chán-sì** 東塔禪寺 under **Jué-rú lǎo-shī** 覺如. **癸未 = 1643**: served as *xíng-zhě* under **Xuě-jiāo dà-shī** 雪嶠 (= **Xuě-jiāo Yuán-xìn** 圓信, 1571–1647) at Gāo-tíng 皋亭, who set him on the *wú shēng-sǐ* 無生死 *gōng-àn*. **Bǐng-xū = 1646** (age 25): awakening while wood-cutting at Gāo-tíng on the rabbit-leaping incident, *jì*: *chù pò niáng-shēng miàn, quán-shēn mò-chù cáng* 觸破孃生面全身沒處藏. After Xuě-jiāo's death, consulted **Gǔ-nán Mù lǎo-héshàng** 古南牧 at Méi-xī, **Fú lǎo-héshàng** 浮 (full-precept), **Yù lǎo-héshàng** 玉 at Bào-ēn, and finally **Yǒng-zhèng Yī-lǎo héshàng** 永正一老 at Xìng-shàn 興善 (Jiā-xīng), under whom decisive transmission came in **戊子 = 1648** (age 27). Subsequent service as *jiān-yuàn* at **Zhú-yǐn Chán-yuàn** 竺隱禪院 (Dāng-hú, 1651–53) and **Yǒng-zhèng Chán-yuàn** 永正禪院 (1653–55).

**Tuì-ān phase.** **乙未春 = 1655**: at the master's request established the **Tuì-ān** 退菴 retreat at **Cháng-shuǐ** 長水 with the lay-patron **Shěn Jìng-quán** 沈敬泉 (the *hào* "Tuì-ān" was bestowed by Yǒng-zhèng Yī-lǎo at this time). Co-supervised the rebuilding of **Pǔ-míng Yòng-zǔ tǎ** 普明用祖塔 at Xìng-shàn with **Jīn-míng lǎo-héshàng** 金明 (the dharma-brother). **己亥冬 = 1659**: closed entry into *yǎn-guān* 掩關 retreat at Yǒng-zhèng. The 行實 was narrated **巳酉 12/8** = **1669/12/30 Gregorian** at the request of the lay-patron **Páng Quán-lì** 龐全力 of **Zhèn-zé** 震澤.

**Lineage position.** *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* line: 斷橋妙倫 (Sòng) — 14 generations — 普明真用 — 永正一 (法叔 of the preface-writer Zhēn-běn) — 斷愚智. Hence **Sān-shān Lái** [[燈來]] of the Jù-yún line and Duàn-yú Zhì of this Yǒng-zhèng / Pǔ-míng-Yòng line are roughly contemporary but in **distinct Línjì sub-lineages** — Hǔ-qiū-school for Duàn-yú, Yáng-qí-school for Sān-shān.

**Work.** [[KR6q0455]] 《嘉興退菴斷愚智禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B246, compiled by **Jī-lún** [[機輪]] *děng* 機輪等編. Front-preface by **Zhēn-běn Xiàng Qiān** [[真本]] 真本項謙 (*fǎ-dì* dharma-brother of Píng-shān 瓶山) dated **Kāng-xī 庚戌 仲秋 巳亥望旦** = **1670/8/15 Gregorian**.

Sources: [[KR6q0455]] juan 1 *xíng-shí* (narrated 1669/12/30 to lay-patron Páng Quán-lì); juan 1 preface by Zhēn-běn Xiàng Qiān 1670/8.

# Zhì 智 (Yào-dì Wú-kě Dà-zhì 藥地無可大智 / Fāng Yǐ-zhì 方以智)

A **different** master sharing the catalog-form *Zhì* 智: the polymath, late-Míng grand secretary, scholar-philosopher, and Cáo-dòng Chán master **Fāng Yǐ-zhì** 方以智 (**1611 – 1671**), one of the most distinguished intellectual figures of the Míng-Qīng transition. Lay-name **Fāng Yǐ-zhì**, *zì* **Mì-zhī** 密之 (with multiple lay *hào* including **Màn-gōng** 曼公, **Mì-shān-zǐ** 宓山子). Tonsured monastic-name **Wú-kě** 無可 (also written **Hóng-zhì** 弘智); *hào* **Yào-dì** 藥地, **Lóng-mián yú-zhě** 龍眠愚者, **Fú-shān yú-zhě** 浮山愚者, **Qīng-yuán yú-zhě** 青原愚者; later monastic-name **Dà-zhì** 大智 (the catalog-form 智 derives from this).

**Lay career.** Tóng-chéng 桐城 native (Ān-huī); from a distinguished scholar-gentry family. Late-Míng 進士 1640. Active in the Hóng-guāng court resistance to the 1644 Qīng conquest. Refugee in Guǎng-xī during the Yǒng-lì period.

**Cáo-dòng monastic ordination.** Tonsured under **Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (1593–1659) — making him a dharma-brother of [[如璽]] **Fāng-róng Rú-xǐ** (cf. [[KR6q0458]]). Multiple subsequent abbacies, most importantly **Qīng-yuán-shān Jìng-jū-sì** 青原山淨居寺 in **Jí-zhōu** 吉州 (Jiāng-xī) — the historic seat of **Qīng-yuán Xíng-sī** 青原行思 — whence his abbacy-form 青原愚者智. Died c. 1671 — likely Wàn-ān-jiāng 萬安江 incident with disputed circumstances (suicide / loyalist-arrest / drowning).

**Work.** [[KR6q0483]] 《青原愚者智禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B313, compiled by *mén-rén* **Xīng-qìng** [[興磬]] + **Xīng-fǔ** [[興斧]]. Apart from his Chán *yǔ-lù*, Fāng's monumental scholarly output includes the *Tōng-yǎ* 通雅 (encyclopedic philology), *Wù-lǐ xiǎo-shí* 物理小識 (natural philosophy), *Yào-dì pǎo-zhuāng* 藥地炮莊 (Cáo-dòng-school *Zhuāng-zǐ* commentary) — a unique combination of late-Míng polymath learning and Cáo-dòng Chán authority.

Sources: [[KR6q0483]] juan 1; DILA authority A007940; voluminous secondary literature on Fāng Yǐ-zhì.

A third master sharing the catalog-form *Zhì* 智: **Gǔ-lín Zhì** 古林智, a Línjì-Yángqí Manchurian (Liáo-yáng) Chán master. Born **1623** (per derived calculation); died **康熙乙亥 11/16 夜間** = **1695-12-21**, age 73, *sēng-là* 67. Native of **Hú-guǎng Cháng-shā** 長沙; lay surname **Zhū** 朱 (variant 李). Línjì-Yángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Yǔ-sōng Chuán-yì** 語嵩傳裔 (1611–1666) in the Pò-shān Hǎi-míng line.

Tonsured at age 6 at **É-méi-shān** 峨嵋 — exceptional childhood ordination giving him a 67-year monastic life. Served **Shuāng-guì lǎo-rén** 雙桂老人 (= [[海明]] **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng**) for over ten years; finally awakened when a wooden post struck his calf-muscle. Took full ordination at 20. Final *fù-fǎ* from **Dé-shān Yǔ-sōng Chuán-yì** in Húnán.

**Manchurian abbacies (in succession).** **Shèng-jīng Fèng-tiān Bō-rě chán-sì** 盛京奉天般若禪寺 (Mukden / Shenyang — entered **康熙壬子** = **1672** winter, his principal seat); **Liáo-yáng Rú-lái chán-yuàn** 遼陽如來禪院; **Liáo-yáng Jiàn-níng chán-sì** 遼陽建寧禪寺; **Fèng-tiān-fǔ Dōng Qián-shān-tái Dà-bēi chán-sì** 奉天府東前山臺大悲禪寺. Together with [[函可]]'s seven Liáo-yáng abbacies a generation earlier, these form the institutional core of post-1644 Línjì Buddhism in Manchuria.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎ-sì* include **Zhèng-dēng** 正燈 [[正燈]] (compiler of the *yǔ-lù*), **Zhèng-jì** 正繼 [[正繼]] (recorder), **Zhèng-wù** 正悟, **Zhèng-xún** 正巡.

**Work.** [[KR6q0546]] 《盛京奉天般若古林禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B429, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Zhèng-jì** *děng lù* + compiled by **Zhèng-dēng** *děng biān*; preface dated **康熙乙卯菊月** = **1675** lunar 9 by the regional Liáodōng official 奉天府通判.

Sources: 《盛京奉天般若古林禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈行狀〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 105; 《東北文獻零拾》 vol. 2.
