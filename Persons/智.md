# Zhì 智 (Tuìān Duànyú Zhì 退菴斷愚智)

Early-Qīng LínjìYángqí *chánshī* of the **Pǔmíng Yòng** 普明用 → **Yǒngzhèng Yīlǎo** 永正一老 sub-line of the **Hǔqiū → Jìngshān → Duànqiáo Lún** 虎丘 → 徑山 → 斷橋倫 transmission. *Zì* **Duànyú** 斷愚 ("Severing Folly"); *hào* **Tuìān** 退菴 ("Retreat Hermit", bestowed by his teacher Yǒngzhèng Yīlǎo in **乙未春 = 1655 spring** for his Chángshuǐ 長水 retreat). Born **Tiānqǐ 壬戌 4/27** = **1622-06-05 Gregorian**; native of **Xiùshuǐ** 秀水 (Jiāxīng), lay surname **Jīn** 金 (father styled **Chéngdān** 成丹, mother **Táng** 唐). Death-date not located; *yǔlù* compiled c. 1670.

**Formation.** Both parents died young; raised by paternal grandmother (老祖). At 16 *suì* (**辛巳 = 1641**) tonsured at **Dōngtǎ Chánsì** 東塔禪寺 under **Juérú lǎoshī** 覺如. **癸未 = 1643**: served as *xíngzhě* under **Xuějiāo dàshī** 雪嶠 (= **Xuějiāo Yuánxìn** 圓信, 1571–1647) at Gāotíng 皋亭, who set him on the *wú shēngsǐ* 無生死 *gōngàn*. **Bǐngxū = 1646** (age 25): awakening while wood-cutting at Gāotíng on the rabbit-leaping incident, *jì*: *chù pò niángshēng miàn, quánshēn mòchù cáng* 觸破孃生面全身沒處藏. After Xuějiāo's death, consulted **Gǔnán Mù lǎohéshàng** 古南牧 at Méixī, **Fú lǎohéshàng** 浮 (full-precept), **Yù lǎohéshàng** 玉 at Bàoēn, and finally **Yǒngzhèng Yīlǎo héshàng** 永正一老 at Xìngshàn 興善 (Jiāxīng), under whom decisive transmission came in **戊子 = 1648** (age 27). Subsequent service as *jiānyuàn* at **Zhúyǐn Chányuàn** 竺隱禪院 (Dānghú, 1651–53) and **Yǒngzhèng Chányuàn** 永正禪院 (1653–55).

**Tuìān phase.** **乙未春 = 1655**: at the master's request established the **Tuìān** 退菴 retreat at **Chángshuǐ** 長水 with the lay-patron **Shěn Jìngquán** 沈敬泉 (the *hào* "Tuìān" was bestowed by Yǒngzhèng Yīlǎo at this time). Co-supervised the rebuilding of **Pǔmíng Yòngzǔ tǎ** 普明用祖塔 at Xìngshàn with **Jīnmíng lǎohéshàng** 金明 (the dharma-brother). **己亥冬 = 1659**: closed entry into *yǎnguān* 掩關 retreat at Yǒngzhèng. The 行實 was narrated **巳酉 12/8** = **1669/12/30 Gregorian** at the request of the lay-patron **Páng Quánlì** 龐全力 of **Zhènzé** 震澤.

**Lineage position.** *Wǔdēng quánshū* line: 斷橋妙倫 (Sòng) — 14 generations — 普明真用 — 永正一 (法叔 of the preface-writer Zhēnběn) — 斷愚智. Hence **Sānshān Lái** [[燈來]] of the Jùyún line and Duànyú Zhì of this Yǒngzhèng / PǔmíngYòng line are roughly contemporary but in **distinct Línjì sub-lineages** — Hǔ-qiū-school for Duànyú, Yáng-qí-school for Sānshān.

**Work.** [[KR6q0455]] 《嘉興退菴斷愚智禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B246, compiled by **Jīlún** [[機輪]] *děng* 機輪等編. Front-preface by **Zhēnběn Xiàng Qiān** [[真本]] 真本項謙 (*fǎdì* dharma-brother of Píngshān 瓶山) dated **Kāngxī 庚戌 仲秋 巳亥望旦** = **1670/8/15 Gregorian**.

Sources: [[KR6q0455]] juan 1 *xíngshí* (narrated 1669/12/30 to lay-patron Páng Quánlì); juan 1 preface by Zhēnběn Xiàng Qiān 1670/8.

# Zhì 智 (Yàodì Wúkě Dàzhì 藥地無可大智 / Fāng Yǐzhì 方以智)

A **different** master sharing the catalog-form *Zhì* 智: the polymath, late-Míng grand secretary, scholar-philosopher, and Cáodòng Chán master **Fāng Yǐzhì** 方以智 (**1611 – 1671**), one of the most distinguished intellectual figures of the MíngQīng transition. Lay-name **Fāng Yǐzhì**, *zì* **Mìzhī** 密之 (with multiple lay *hào* including **Màngōng** 曼公, **Mìshānzǐ** 宓山子). Tonsured monastic-name **Wúkě** 無可 (also written **Hóngzhì** 弘智); *hào* **Yàodì** 藥地, **Lóngmián yúzhě** 龍眠愚者, **Fúshān yúzhě** 浮山愚者, **Qīngyuán yúzhě** 青原愚者; later monastic-name **Dàzhì** 大智 (the catalog-form 智 derives from this).

**Lay career.** Tóngchéng 桐城 native (Ānhuī); from a distinguished scholar-gentry family. Late-Míng 進士 1640. Active in the Hóngguāng court resistance to the 1644 Qīng conquest. Refugee in Guǎngxī during the Yǒnglì period.

**Cáodòng monastic ordination.** Tonsured under **Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (1593–1659) — making him a dharma-brother of [[如璽]] **Fāngróng Rúxǐ** (cf. [[KR6q0458]]). Multiple subsequent abbacies, most importantly **Qīngyuánshān Jìngjūsì** 青原山淨居寺 in **Jízhōu** 吉州 (Jiāngxī) — the historic seat of **Qīngyuán Xíngsī** 青原行思 — whence his abbacy-form 青原愚者智. Died c. 1671 — likely Wànānjiāng 萬安江 incident with disputed circumstances (suicide / loyalist-arrest / drowning).

**Work.** [[KR6q0483]] 《青原愚者智禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B313, compiled by *ménrén* **Xīngqìng** [[興磬]] + **Xīngfǔ** [[興斧]]. Apart from his Chán *yǔlù*, Fāng's monumental scholarly output includes the *Tōngyǎ* 通雅 (encyclopedic philology), *Wùlǐ xiǎoshí* 物理小識 (natural philosophy), *Yàodì pǎozhuāng* 藥地炮莊 (Cáo-dòng-school *Zhuāngzǐ* commentary) — a unique combination of late-Míng polymath learning and Cáodòng Chán authority.

Sources: [[KR6q0483]] juan 1; DILA authority A007940; voluminous secondary literature on Fāng Yǐzhì.

A third master sharing the catalog-form *Zhì* 智: **Gǔlín Zhì** 古林智, a LínjìYángqí Manchurian (Liáoyáng) Chán master. Born **1623** (per derived calculation); died **康熙乙亥 11/16 夜間** = **1695-12-21**, age 73, *sēnglà* 67. Native of **Húguǎng Chángshā** 長沙; lay surname **Zhū** 朱 (variant 李). LínjìYángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Yǔsōng Chuányì** 語嵩傳裔 (1611–1666) in the Pòshān Hǎimíng line.

Tonsured at age 6 at **Éméishān** 峨嵋 — exceptional childhood ordination giving him a 67-year monastic life. Served **Shuāngguì lǎorén** 雙桂老人 (= [[海明]] **Pòshān Hǎimíng**) for over ten years; finally awakened when a wooden post struck his calf-muscle. Took full ordination at 20. Final *fùfǎ* from **Déshān Yǔsōng Chuányì** in Húnán.

**Manchurian abbacies (in succession).** **Shèngjīng Fèngtiān Bōrě chánsì** 盛京奉天般若禪寺 (Mukden / Shenyang — entered **康熙壬子** = **1672** winter, his principal seat); **Liáoyáng Rúlái chányuàn** 遼陽如來禪院; **Liáoyáng Jiànníng chánsì** 遼陽建寧禪寺; **Fèngtiānfǔ Dōng Qiánshāntái Dàbēi chánsì** 奉天府東前山臺大悲禪寺. Together with [[函可]]'s seven Liáoyáng abbacies a generation earlier, these form the institutional core of post-1644 Línjì Buddhism in Manchuria.

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* include **Zhèngdēng** 正燈 [[正燈]] (compiler of the *yǔlù*), **Zhèngjì** 正繼 [[正繼]] (recorder), **Zhèngwù** 正悟, **Zhèngxún** 正巡.

**Work.** [[KR6q0546]] 《盛京奉天般若古林禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B429, recorded by *fǎsì* **Zhèngjì** *děng lù* + compiled by **Zhèngdēng** *děng biān*; preface dated **康熙乙卯菊月** = **1675** lunar 9 by the regional Liáodōng official 奉天府通判.

Sources: 《盛京奉天般若古林禪師語錄》 vol. 6 〈行狀〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 105; 《東北文獻零拾》 vol. 2.
