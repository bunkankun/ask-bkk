Jingen 深賢 (also read *Jinken*; fl. early-to-mid 13th c.) was a Japanese Shingon master of the **Daigo-ji / Tōji** transmission line in the early Kamakura period. His precise lifedates and primary affiliation are not securely recoverable, but his sole surviving work [[KR6t0203]] *Shíguī chāo* (*Jikkishō*) draws on the post-Jōken Daigo Kakudō-in tradition and is preserved with CANWWW code A001126.

Surviving work in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6t0203]] *Jikkishō* (1 fasc.).
