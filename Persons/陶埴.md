**Táo Zhí** 陶埴 was a Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0322]] *Huán jīn shù* 還金述 — a short alchemical tract on the "return-gold" elixir. Biographical details are unrecorded; the work is late Táng / Five Dynasties / Sòng.
