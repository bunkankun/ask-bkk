Yě-shì Shān-rén 也是山人 ("the 'Also-So' Mountain Recluse") — pseudonym of an anonymous mid-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician writing in the post-Yè-Tiān-shì 葉天士 ([[葉桂]]) Sūzhōu *wēn-bìng* tradition of the mid-Qīng to mid-nineteenth century. His [[KR3ep028]] *Yě-shì Shān-rén yī-àn* 也是山人醫案 survives in a single *juǎn* preserving classical Yè-school clinical cases. The author's identity has not been securely established; some modern reference works identify him as a disciple or near-contemporary of Yè Tiān-shì, others leave the question open. The pseudonym is one of several Daoist-style *shān-rén* names favoured by Qīng Jiāng-nán physicians (compare Yè Tiān-shì's own *Shàngjīn lǎorén* 上津老人).
