Yěshì Shānrén 也是山人 ("the 'Also-So' Mountain Recluse") — pseudonym of an anonymous mid-Qīng Jiāngnán physician writing in the post-Yè-Tiān-shì 葉天士 ([[葉桂]]) Sūzhōu *wēnbìng* tradition of the mid-Qīng to mid-nineteenth century. His [[KR3ep028]] *Yěshì Shānrén yīàn* 也是山人醫案 survives in a single *juǎn* preserving classical Yè-school clinical cases. The author's identity has not been securely established; some modern reference works identify him as a disciple or near-contemporary of Yè Tiānshì, others leave the question open. The pseudonym is one of several Daoist-style *shānrén* names favoured by Qīng Jiāngnán physicians (compare Yè Tiānshì's own *Shàngjīn lǎorén* 上津老人).
