Sìzhào 嗣詔, Yuán-dynasty LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Qiānyán Yuáncháng** [[元長]] 千巖元長 (1284–1357) at **Fúlóngshān** 伏龍山. Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《千巖和尚語錄》 ([[KR6q0460]]). The original recording was made *zài shī shí* 在師時 (during the master's lifetime), per Sòng Lián's preface; the original Yuán-era cutting was destroyed in the YuánMíng transition warfare and reconstituted from an old manuscript by **Yīān Lín shàngrén** 一庵鄰上人 with a 1376 re-cut.
