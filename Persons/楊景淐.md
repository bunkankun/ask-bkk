Yáng Jǐngchuō 楊景淐, pen name Dànyóu 澹遊, was a Qīng literatus from Dōng Mǎo 東泖 in Sōngjiāng 松江 prefecture (modern Qīngpǔ 青浦 district, Shanghai). He is the author of *Guǐgǔ Sì Yǒu Zhì* 鬼谷四友志 ([[KR4k0120]]), a historical novel about the four disciples of Guǐgǔzi 鬼谷子 (Sūn Bìn, Páng Juān, Sū Qín, and Zhāng Yí), composed at his studio "Lèzhì Xuān 樂志軒" in the early ninth month of Qiánlóng 60th year (1795).
