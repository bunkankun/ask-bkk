Late-Sòng / early-Yuán 元 Fú-jiàn-regional educator and poet. *Zì* **Dé-zhāng** 德章; *hào* **Wú-pǔ** 吾圃 ("My-Garden"). Native of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Fú-jiàn). 1239–1306 (CBDB 43440 records *fl. 1280–1306*; the catalog meta gives full dates 1239–1306, confirmed by Wikipedia).

**Career.** Late-Sòng *jiào-yú* 教諭 (school instructor).

**Family.** Father of **Hóng Xī-wén** [[洪希文]] (1282–1366).

**Original collection.** *Xuān-qú jí* 軒渠集 (the "Hilarity Collection"). Lost in the Yuán-Míng transition warfare. Only fragments survive as the postscript to his son's *Xù Xuān-qú jí* [[KR4d0480]].

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0480]] *Xù Xuān-qú jí* 續軒渠集 (撰附).
