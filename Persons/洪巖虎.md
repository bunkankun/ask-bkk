Late-Sòng / early-Yuán 元 Fú-jiàn-regional educator and poet. *Zì* **Dézhāng** 德章; *hào* **Wúpǔ** 吾圃 ("My-Garden"). Native of **Pútián** 莆田 (Fújiàn). 1239–1306 (CBDB 43440 records *fl. 1280–1306*; the catalog meta gives full dates 1239–1306, confirmed by Wikipedia).

**Career.** Late-Sòng *jiàoyú* 教諭 (school instructor).

**Family.** Father of **Hóng Xīwén** [[洪希文]] (1282–1366).

**Original collection.** *Xuānqú jí* 軒渠集 (the "Hilarity Collection"). Lost in the YuánMíng transition warfare. Only fragments survive as the postscript to his son's *Xù Xuānqú jí* [[KR4d0480]].

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0480]] *Xù Xuānqú jí* 續軒渠集 (撰附).
