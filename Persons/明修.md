Míng-xiū 明修, *zì* **Yuán-fēng** 元峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** [[超自]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Míng-huì** [[明惠]] and **Míng-zhì** [[明智]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0575]] 《何一自禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B462.
