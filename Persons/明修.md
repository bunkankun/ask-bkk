Míngxiū 明修, *zì* **Yuánfēng** 元峰. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Héyī Chāozì** [[超自]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Mínghuì** [[明惠]] and **Míngzhì** [[明智]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0575]] 《何一自禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B462.
