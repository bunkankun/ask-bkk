**Tamba no Motoin** 丹波元胤 (also 多紀元胤 *Taki no Motoin*, sinicized **Dānbō Yuányìn**, 1789–1827), *zì* Yì-xǐ 奕禧, *hào* Shào-wēng 紹翁, was the elder son of [[丹波元簡]] (Tamba no Mototane) and the principal continuator of the Tamba philological program at the **Igaku-kan** 醫學館 during the brief period (1810–1827) between his father's death and his own early death at thirty-eight. His magnum opus is the *Yī jí kǎo* 醫籍考 (Chinese: *Zhōng-guó yī jí kǎo* 中國醫籍考) in 80 juan, an exhaustive bibliographic survey of Chinese medical literature — Mototane began the work and Motoin completed it; printed posthumously in 1832 — which remains an indispensable reference for the philology of Chinese medicine. He died in *Bunsei* 10 = 1827. After his death, the leadership of the Tamba circle passed to his younger brother [[丹波元堅]] (Tamba no Motokata).
