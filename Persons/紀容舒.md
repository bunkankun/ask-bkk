Mid-Qīng official, *zì* Chísǒu 遲叟, *hào* Zhúyá 竹厓, of Xiànxiàn 獻縣 (Héběi). *Jǔrén* Kāngxī *guǐsì* (1713); rose to *Yáoānfǔ zhīfǔ* (Prefect of Yáo'ān, Yúnnán). Father of the famous Sìkù *zǒngcuàn* Jì Yún 紀昀 (1724–1805). CBDB lifedates 1686–1764. The author of the *Sūnshì Tángyùn kǎo* 孫氏唐韻考 [[KR1j0086]] (5 *juàn*), a careful reconstruction of the lost Táng-period *Tángyùn* by Sūn Miǎn [[孫愐]] (Tiānbǎo 10 / 751) on the evidence of the *fǎnqiè* spellings preserved in Xú Xuàn 徐鉉's *Yōngxī 3 / Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú-pre-1011* recension of the *Shuōwén jiězì*. Methodologically: since *fǎnqiè* upper-character preserves initial-class and lower-character preserves rhyme-class, the lower-characters in Xú Xuàn's *Shuōwén* readings can be back-extracted into Sūn Miǎn's original Táng-period rhyme-classification — a clean philological reconstruction. The book documents both Táng-period entries that have dropped out of the *Guǎngyùn* and *Guǎngyùn* mis-edits where post-1011 editors changed the rhyme-class but forgot to change the *fǎnqiè*.
