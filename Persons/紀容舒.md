Mid-Qīng official, *zì* Chí-sǒu 遲叟, *hào* Zhú-yá 竹厓, of Xiàn-xiàn 獻縣 (Hé-běi). *Jǔ-rén* Kāng-xī *guǐ-sì* (1713); rose to *Yáo-ān-fǔ zhī-fǔ* (Prefect of Yáo'ān, Yúnnán). Father of the famous Sìkù *zǒng-cuàn* Jì Yún 紀昀 (1724–1805). CBDB lifedates 1686–1764. The author of the *Sūn-shì Táng-yùn kǎo* 孫氏唐韻考 [[KR1j0086]] (5 *juàn*), a careful reconstruction of the lost Táng-period *Táng-yùn* by Sūn Miǎn [[孫愐]] (Tiān-bǎo 10 / 751) on the evidence of the *fǎn-qiè* spellings preserved in Xú Xuàn 徐鉉's *Yōng-xī 3 / Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú-pre-1011* recension of the *Shuōwén jiě-zì*. Methodologically: since *fǎn-qiè* upper-character preserves initial-class and lower-character preserves rhyme-class, the lower-characters in Xú Xuàn's *Shuōwén* readings can be back-extracted into Sūn Miǎn's original Táng-period rhyme-classification — a clean philological reconstruction. The book documents both Táng-period entries that have dropped out of the *Guǎng-yùn* and *Guǎng-yùn* mis-edits where post-1011 editors changed the rhyme-class but forgot to change the *fǎn-qiè*.
