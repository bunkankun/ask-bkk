Guǎngdù 廣度 (Guǎngdù Fǎshī 廣度法師, lifedates unknown; *fl.* early-Qing, late 17th / early 18th century), Buddhist monk and Huáyán-school lecturer, dharma-heir of [[賢首]] Xiánshǒu's Huáyán lineage. Master of [[來舟]] Lái Zhōu (the author of the *[[KR6b0008|Dàshèng běnshēng xīndìguān jīng (T159)]]* commentarial set, [[KR6b0009]] / [[KR6b0010]] / [[KR6b0011]]), under whom Lái Zhōu studied for over thirty years. According to Lái Zhōu's preface to the *Xuánshì* (X365), Guǎngdù "occupied the lecture-seat" of his lineage's Huáyán teaching throughout the late Kāngxī period. No independent biographical material survives.
