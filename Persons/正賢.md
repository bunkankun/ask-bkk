# Zhèng-xián 正賢

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*cān-xué shìzhě* 參學侍者) of Jiè-shí Zhì-péng 介石智朋. Lead-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0305]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
