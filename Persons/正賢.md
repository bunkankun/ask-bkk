# Zhèngxián 正賢

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*cānxué shìzhě* 參學侍者) of Jièshí Zhìpéng 介石智朋. Lead-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0305]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
