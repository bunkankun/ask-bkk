Guō Zhōngxiào 郭忠孝 (d. 1127, *Jìngkāng* 2), *zì* Lìzhī 立之, *hào* Jiānshān 兼山先生, of Hénán 河南 / Luòyáng 洛陽. A direct pupil of [[程頤]] Chéng Yí for over two decades and a Chéng-school *Yì*-master in the *xiàngshù*-leaning branch — sometimes counted as a fifth alongside the canonical *Chéngmén sì xiānshēng* 程門四先生 ([[楊時]] Yáng Shí, [[游酢]] Yóu Zuò, [[謝良佐]] Xiè Liángzuǒ, [[呂大臨]] Lǚ Dàlín).

Held office to *Yǒngxīngjūn lù tíxíng* 永興軍路提刑 (provincial judicial commissioner of Yǒngxīngjūn) under Huīzōng and Qīnzōng. At the *Jìngkāng* catastrophe (1126–1127) refused to surrender Yǒngxīngjūn to the Jin and died in the defense of the city.

His principal scholarly work, the *Jiānshān Yì jiě* 兼山易解, was substantively complete before his death but was scattered in the chaos of the Jin invasion. His son [[郭雍]] Guō Yōng (1091–1187) reconstructed and extended the family-*Yì* doctrine as the *Guō shì chuánjiā Yì shuō* 郭氏傳家易說 ([[KR1a0033]]) over the next sixty years in mountain retreat at Xiázhōu's Chángyáng valley.

The *Sòngshǐ* (juan 448) places him in the *Zhōng yì zhuàn* 忠義傳 ("Loyalty-and-Righteousness Biographies").
