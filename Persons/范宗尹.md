# Fàn Zōngyǐn 范宗尹

Fàn Zōngyǐn 范宗尹 (1100–1136), Southern-Sòng chief councillor (*zǎixiàng*) under Gāozōng; *hào* Tuìhuì jūshì 退晦居士. Met Hóngzhì Zhèngjué in southern exile after political disgrace; wrote the 1132 primary preface to the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
