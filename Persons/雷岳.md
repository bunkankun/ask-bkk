# Léi Yuè 雷岳

Southern Hàn court official of the Jíxiándiàn 集賢殿; composed the *xínglù* 行錄 biography of [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃 in 949, preserved in juan 3 of [[KR6q0073]] *Yúnmén Kuāngzhēn chánshī guǎnglù*.
