Late-Qīng Buddhist monk, alternate name **Xīn-sēng-tán** 心僧談. Lifedates unrecorded. Compiled (錄, "transcribed") the *Jīn-gāng jīng kē-yí* 金剛經科儀 ([[KR6c0116]], X74 no. 1494) — a one-juan *bǎo-juàn*-style liturgical edition of the *Vajracchedikā* — composed in **道光乙未年孟夏 = early-summer 1835** per the colophon. The work belongs to the *kē-yí* 科儀 / *bǎo-juàn* 寶卷 popular-liturgical genre that descends through Sòng Zōng-jìng's [[KR6c0055]] and incorporates the famous **Yìjìng pilgrimage poem** *Jìn Sòng Qí Liáng Táng-dài jiān …* 晉宋齊梁唐代間 as opening invocation.
