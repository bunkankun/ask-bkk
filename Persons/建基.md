Late-Qīng Buddhist monk, alternate name **Xīnsēngtán** 心僧談. Lifedates unrecorded. Compiled (錄, "transcribed") the *Jīngāng jīng kēyí* 金剛經科儀 ([[KR6c0116]], X74 no. 1494) — a one-juan *bǎojuàn*-style liturgical edition of the *Vajracchedikā* — composed in **道光乙未年孟夏 = early-summer 1835** per the colophon. The work belongs to the *kēyí* 科儀 / *bǎojuàn* 寶卷 popular-liturgical genre that descends through Sòng Zōngjìng's [[KR6c0055]] and incorporates the famous **Yìjìng pilgrimage poem** *Jìn Sòng Qí Liáng Tángdài jiān …* 晉宋齊梁唐代間 as opening invocation.
