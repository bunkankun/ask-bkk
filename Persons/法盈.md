Fǎyíng 法盈 (DILA A000708; fl. late 7th–early 8th century CE) was a Tang dynasty Abhidharma monk associated with the tradition of 圓暉 ([[圓暉]]). He compiled introductory preface-notes (序記) for 圓暉's *Jùshě lùn sòng shū* ([[KR6l0036]]). His biographical details are otherwise unrecorded. Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6l0039]] 俱舍論頌疏序記 (X53n838).
