Chén Yìngfāng 陳應芳 (b. 1545), *zì* Yuánzhèn 元振, of Tàizhōu Garrison 泰州衛, was a *jìnshì* of Wànlì 23 (1595) who rose to *Bùzhèngsī cānzhèng* 布政司參政 (Provincial Administration Vice-Commissioner) of Fújiàn. In retirement at his native Tàizhōu, he compiled the *Jìngzhǐ jí* ([[KR2k0069]]), a 4-juan documentary anthology on the hydraulic system of the *Xiàhé* watershed of northern Jiāngsū — the principal late-Míng monograph on the region. CBDB id 206180 (b. 1545) corresponds to the Wàn-lì-era *jìnshì*; homonymous entries 321214, 370931, 475187 lack matching dates.
