**Shěn Míngzōng** 沈明宗 (*zì* Mùcún 目存, *hào* Qiūchí 秋池, *fl.* late seventeenth / early eighteenth century, Kāngxī period), early-Qīng physician of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). One of the principal theorist-physicians of the early-Qīng period, integrating the Míng LǐZhū 李朱 (Lǐ Dōngyuán / Zhū Dānxī) clinical tradition with the *yùnqì* 五運六氣 cosmological framework and the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* classical-formulary tradition. Major works: the *Shānghán Liùshū chánjiě* 傷寒六書纏解 (commentary on the six standard *Shānghán* commentaries), the *Jīnguì yàolüè biānzhù* 金匱要略編注 (1692, a major early-Qīng *Jīnguì* commentary), the *Wēnrè bìng piān* 溫熱病篇, and the *Kèchuāng ǒután* 客窗偶談 ([[KR3eq062]]) clinical-theoretical miscellany. His integrative doctrinal-cosmological position prefigures the Dàoguāng — Xián-fēng-era Mèng-hé-school integrative method. Not in CBDB.
