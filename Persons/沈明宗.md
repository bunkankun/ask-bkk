**Shěn Míng-zōng** 沈明宗 (*zì* Mù-cún 目存, *hào* Qiū-chí 秋池, *fl.* late seventeenth / early eighteenth century, Kāng-xī period), early-Qīng physician of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). One of the principal theorist-physicians of the early-Qīng period, integrating the Míng Lǐ-Zhū 李朱 (Lǐ Dōng-yuán / Zhū Dān-xī) clinical tradition with the *yùn-qì* 五運六氣 cosmological framework and the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* classical-formulary tradition. Major works: the *Shānghán Liù-shū chán-jiě* 傷寒六書纏解 (commentary on the six standard *Shānghán* commentaries), the *Jīnguì yào-lüè biān-zhù* 金匱要略編注 (1692, a major early-Qīng *Jīnguì* commentary), the *Wēn-rè bìng piān* 溫熱病篇, and the *Kè-chuāng ǒu-tán* 客窗偶談 ([[KR3eq062]]) clinical-theoretical miscellany. His integrative doctrinal-cosmological position prefigures the Dào-guāng — Xián-fēng-era Mèng-hé-school integrative method. Not in CBDB.
