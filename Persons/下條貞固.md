**Shimojō Sadakata** 下條貞固 (*imina* Michiharu 通春), early-Meiji Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician, staff member of the Hakusaidō 博濟堂 beriberi hospital from its founding in 1878. Named among the institutional co-compilers of [[KR3eh054]] *Kakke gairon* 腳氣概論 (1879). No further biographical material located.
