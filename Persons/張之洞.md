Zhāng Zhīdòng 張之洞 (*zì* Xiàodá 孝達; *hào* Xiāngtāo 香濤; 1837–1909) was a senior late-Qīng statesman and Self-Strengthening reformer, governor-general of Húguǎng 湖廣 and Liǎngjiāng 兩江, and author of the bibliographic primer *Shūmù dáwèn* 書目答問.
