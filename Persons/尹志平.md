**Yǐn Zhìpíng** 尹志平 (1169–1251), *zì* Dà cháng 大常, *hào* Qīng hé zǐ 清和子, was a second-generation Quán zhēn patriarch, disciple of Qiū Chǔjī 丘處機 and his immediate successor as head of the Quán zhēn order (1227–1238). Author of [[KR5e0048]] *Bǎo guāng jí* 葆光集 (preface 1299, posthumous).
