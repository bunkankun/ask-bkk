Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* scholar, *zì* Sīshū 思叔. A principal disciple of [[程頤]] Chéng Yí, whom he served closely in Chéng's last years. According to [[楊時]] Yáng Shí's postface to the *Yīchuān Yìzhuàn* ([[KR1a0016]]), Chéng Yí on his deathbed entrusted the unfinished *Yìzhuàn* manuscript to Zhāng Yì; Zhāng died shortly after, and the manuscript scattered. The work was eventually recovered in the capital by [[謝良佐]] Xiè Liángzuǒ and edited into final form by Yáng Shí.
