# Xīlíng 希陵

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Xūgǔ 虛谷 ("Empty-Valley"); further honorific names Xībái 西白, Dàyuán 大圓, Huìzhào 慧照, Dàbiàn 大辨. Dharma-heir of [[祖欽]] Xuěyán Zǔqīn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0330]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
