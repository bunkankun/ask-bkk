# Xī-líng 希陵

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Xū-gǔ 虛谷 ("Empty-Valley"); further honorific names Xī-bái 西白, Dà-yuán 大圓, Huì-zhào 慧照, Dà-biàn 大辨. Dharma-heir of [[祖欽]] Xuě-yán Zǔ-qīn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0330]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
