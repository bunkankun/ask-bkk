# Tàijìng 太靖

Early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán *shūjì* 書記 under [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi (1615–1702) at Fúzhōu Gǔshān 鼓山 in the immediate aftermath of Dàopèi's succession to [[元賢]]'s seat (Shùnzhì 14 / 1657); recorder (*lù* 錄) of the two-juan *Wèilín Dàopèi chánshī bǐngfú yǔlù* ([[KR6q0368]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
