# Tài-jìng 太靖

Early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán *shū-jì* 書記 under [[道霈]] Wèi-lín Dào-pèi (1615–1702) at Fú-zhōu Gǔ-shān 鼓山 in the immediate aftermath of Dào-pèi's succession to [[元賢]]'s seat (Shùn-zhì 14 / 1657); recorder (*lù* 錄) of the two-juan *Wèi-lín Dào-pèi chánshī bǐng-fú yǔlù* ([[KR6q0368]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
