# Xíng-wěi 行瑋 (Yún-xíng Xíng-wěi 雲行瑋)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk of the 行-generation (= dharma-grandson of Mìyún Yuán-wù 密雲圓悟). Dharma-heir of **Mù-yún Tōng-mén** [[通門]] 牧雲通門 (1599–1671) and acting abbot of **Gū-sū Xī-huá-shān Xiù-fēng-sì** 姑蘇西華山秀峯寺 (in Sū-zhōu) during the compilation of Mù-yún's supplementary *yǔlù*. His *zì* Yún-xíng 雲行 follows the standard Mìyún-line *bié-zì* 別字 convention of inserting a prefix marking the master's generation (Mìyún → Yún-xíng).

Named in the *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 67 as one of the ten principal dharma-heirs of Mù-yún, alongside Chóng-yǐ Jùn 崇已峻 and Nán-yīn Yán 南音言. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0406]] *Mù-yún héshàng Qī-huì yú-lù* (Jiā-xīng Canon J26 B184), cut at Xiù-fēng-sì some time between Mù-yún's death in 1671 and Xíng-wěi's own tenure-end (both unknown). No independent *yǔlù* or literary collection survives under his name; he is known only through the Mù-yún materials he curated.
