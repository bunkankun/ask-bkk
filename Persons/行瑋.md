# Xíngwěi 行瑋 (Yúnxíng Xíngwěi 雲行瑋)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk of the 行-generation (= dharma-grandson of Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟). Dharma-heir of **Mùyún Tōngmén** [[通門]] 牧雲通門 (1599–1671) and acting abbot of **Gūsū Xīhuáshān Xiùfēngsì** 姑蘇西華山秀峯寺 (in Sūzhōu) during the compilation of Mùyún's supplementary *yǔlù*. His *zì* Yúnxíng 雲行 follows the standard Mìyún-line *biézì* 別字 convention of inserting a prefix marking the master's generation (Mìyún → Yúnxíng).

Named in the *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 67 as one of the ten principal dharma-heirs of Mùyún, alongside Chóngyǐ Jùn 崇已峻 and Nányīn Yán 南音言. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0406]] *Mùyún héshàng Qīhuì yúlù* (Jiāxīng Canon J26 B184), cut at Xiùfēngsì some time between Mùyún's death in 1671 and Xíngwěi's own tenure-end (both unknown). No independent *yǔlù* or literary collection survives under his name; he is known only through the Mùyún materials he curated.
