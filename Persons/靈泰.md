Língtài 靈泰 was a Tang-period Cí'ēn / Fǎxiàng commentator known principally for the 16-fascicle [[KR6n0042]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shū chāo* 成唯識論疏抄 (X50n0819) — a sub-commentary on Kuījī's [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì*. He was a direct disciple of [[窺基]] (632–682) and later of [[慧沼]] (648–714), and his work consequently transmits the orthodox Cí'ēn-sì tradition more or less directly. The *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* 宋高僧傳 ([[KR2m0019]] T2061, j. 4) gives a brief notice but no precise dates; he is conventionally placed in the late seventh and early eighth century, contemporaneous with Huìzhāo and the elder generation of [[智周]]. His other works are mostly lost.
