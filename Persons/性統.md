# Xìngtǒng 性統 (Biéān Xìngtǒng 別庵性統)

Early-Qīng LínjìYángqí Chán master. *Hào* Biéān 別庵 ("Different Hermitage"), also Biéāntǒng 別庵統 or Biégōng 別公. Lifedates 1661 – 1717/11/3 (Kāngxī 56 / 10 朔旦, age 57, per the *Shèngyīn jiēdài sì* 聖因接待寺 temple gazetteer juan 3 "Biography of the 34th-generation master Biéān Xìngtǒng").

Dharma-heir of [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685); abbot at Shèngyīn jiēdàisì 聖因接待寺 as the 34th-generation descendant master. Compiler-editor of two major late-seventeenth-century Chán texts:

- *Wǔ jiā zōng zhǐ zuǎn yào* 五家宗旨纂要 [[KR6q0169]] — his master Dēnglái's systematic exposition of the distinctive teaching-methods of the Five Houses, with Xìngtǒng's own commentary annotations.
- *Xù dēng zhèng tǒng* 續燈正統 [[KR6q0028]] (with its table of contents [[KR6q0027]]) — a major continuation lamp-record of the LínjìYángqí lineage, prefaced 1691, the principal "orthodox" lamp-record of the late Kāngxī lineage-dispute era.

Note: an earlier note identified Xìngtǒng as Cáodòng; the DILA record and his lineage through Dēnglái and Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī place him firmly in the LínjìYángqí line, correcting this earlier attribution.
