# Xíngzhōu 行舟 (Jièwéi Xíngzhōu 介為行舟)

Early-Qīng LínjìYángqí Chán master, **33rd-generation Línjì** (行-generation Mìyún sub-line). Principal dharma-heir and textual-editorial executor of [[微|Wànrú Tōngwēi]] 萬如通微 (1594–1657, cf. [[KR6q0404]]). *Fǎhuì* **Xíngzhōu** 行舟, *zì* **Jièwéi** 介為, *biéhào* **Kòujì** 扣寂. Lay surname **Zhào** 趙, of **Chǔ Miǎnyángzhōu** 楚沔陽州 (Húběi Qiánjiāng region). Father **Zhào Shìjié** 趙世節 (*hào* **Jìmíng** 季明), mother **Dùshì** 杜氏. Lifedates **c. 1611 – 康熙 9 庚戍 4.9** = **c. 1611 – 29 May 1670**, age **60 *suì*** / *sēnglà* **44** per the *xíngzhuàng* at [[KR6q0424]] juan 10.

**Training.** Age 11: mother's death → abandoned Confucian studies; tonsured at **Huángānxiàn Dōnglínsì Xīchántáng** 黃安縣東林寺西禪堂 in Húběi under **Chúnxīnshī** 純心師. Age 18: traveled 5 years through WúYuè with *shībó* 師伯 **Tóngshān** 童山. At **Tiāntóngsì** under **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 for棒打 testing. Decisive awakening at **Biànshān Lónghuásì** under [[明雪|Rùjiù Míngxuě]] 入就明雪 on the *rìwǔ dǎ sāngēng* 日午打三更 phrase. Returned to Tiāntóng for 10+ years. Under **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 at **Jīnsù** served as *zhīkè* 知客. Co-managed the 1647 funeral of **Xuějiào Yuánxìn** 雪嶠圓信 at Yúnménsì (built the **辨才古塔** 辨才古塔 for the master). Finally arrived at **Lóngchíshān** 龍池山 under **Wànrú Tōngwēi** — received formal sealing as principal dharma-heir.

**Six abbacies (1649–1670).** (1) **Jiāxīng Nánhú Rúrúān** 嘉興南湖如如菴 — **Shùnzhì 6.2.13** (= 25 March 1649) entry, escorted personally by Wànrú; 7-year tenure. (2) **Dūmén Pǔjì Chányuàn** 都門普濟禪院 (Běijīng) — 1656. (3) **Lóngchíshān** as post-Wàn-rú administrator 1657–60 (supervised Wànrú's [[KR6q0404]] *yǔlù* cutting). (4) **Wújiāng Hǎiyúnsì** 吳江海雲寺 — 1661–64. (5) **Sūjùn Bàoguósì** 蘇郡報國寺 — 1664–68. (6) **Wújiāng Chéngtiān Wànshòusì** 吳江承天萬壽寺 — 1669–70 (final abbacy, restoring the Sòng-era Bàoběn Yuán 報本元 chánshī seat with Míng Zǐbó Zhēnkě and Chán-enriched literati-patronage background).

**Editorial significance.** Xíngzhōu supervised the post-mortem cutting of **Wànrú Tōngwēi's *yǔlù*** immediately after Wànrú's 1657 death (= the present [[KR6q0404]] = Jiāxīng J26 B182). He is therefore the direct editorial link connecting Wànrú's textual corpus to the J26–J28 Jiāxīng Canon tradition.

Work: [[KR6q0424]] 《介為舟禪師語錄》 (10 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J28 B205, compiled by dharma-heirs [[海鹽]] Hǎiyán and [[益證]] Yìzhèng; front preface by [[嚴大參]] Yán dàcān 1661-04-01; *xíngzhuàng* by **Xíngyuān** 行淵 at Yǒngníngsì; *tǎmíng* by unnamed dharma-brother — integrated cutting c. **1672**).

Sources: [[KR6q0424]] juan 10 *xíngzhuàng* (by Xíngyuān) + *tǎmíng* (unnamed); *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 69.
