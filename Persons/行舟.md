# Xíng-zhōu 行舟 (Jiè-wéi Xíng-zhōu 介為行舟)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, **33rd-generation Línjì** (行-generation Mìyún sub-line). Principal dharma-heir and textual-editorial executor of [[微|Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi]] 萬如通微 (1594–1657, cf. [[KR6q0404]]). *Fǎ-huì* **Xíng-zhōu** 行舟, *zì* **Jiè-wéi** 介為, *bié-hào* **Kòu-jì** 扣寂. Lay surname **Zhào** 趙, of **Chǔ Miǎn-yáng-zhōu** 楚沔陽州 (Hú-běi Qián-jiāng region). Father **Zhào Shì-jié** 趙世節 (*hào* **Jì-míng** 季明), mother **Dù-shì** 杜氏. Lifedates **c. 1611 – 康熙 9 庚戍 4.9** = **c. 1611 – 29 May 1670**, age **60 *suì*** / *sēng-là* **44** per the *xíng-zhuàng* at [[KR6q0424]] juan 10.

**Training.** Age 11: mother's death → abandoned Confucian studies; tonsured at **Huáng-ān-xiàn Dōng-lín-sì Xī-chán-táng** 黃安縣東林寺西禪堂 in Hú-běi under **Chún-xīn-shī** 純心師. Age 18: traveled 5 years through Wú-Yuè with *shī-bó* 師伯 **Tóng-shān** 童山. At **Tiān-tóng-sì** under **Mìyún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 for棒打 testing. Decisive awakening at **Biàn-shān Lóng-huá-sì** under [[明雪|Rù-jiù Míng-xuě]] 入就明雪 on the *rì-wǔ dǎ sān-gēng* 日午打三更 phrase. Returned to Tiān-tóng for 10+ years. Under **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 at **Jīn-sù** served as *zhī-kè* 知客. Co-managed the 1647 funeral of **Xuě-jiào Yuán-xìn** 雪嶠圓信 at Yún-mén-sì (built the **辨才古塔** 辨才古塔 for the master). Finally arrived at **Lóng-chí-shān** 龍池山 under **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** — received formal sealing as principal dharma-heir.

**Six abbacies (1649–1670).** (1) **Jiā-xīng Nán-hú Rú-rú-ān** 嘉興南湖如如菴 — **Shùn-zhì 6.2.13** (= 25 March 1649) entry, escorted personally by Wàn-rú; 7-year tenure. (2) **Dū-mén Pǔ-jì Chán-yuàn** 都門普濟禪院 (Běi-jīng) — 1656. (3) **Lóng-chí-shān** as post-Wàn-rú administrator 1657–60 (supervised Wàn-rú's [[KR6q0404]] *yǔlù* cutting). (4) **Wú-jiāng Hǎi-yún-sì** 吳江海雲寺 — 1661–64. (5) **Sū-jùn Bào-guó-sì** 蘇郡報國寺 — 1664–68. (6) **Wú-jiāng Chéng-tiān Wàn-shòu-sì** 吳江承天萬壽寺 — 1669–70 (final abbacy, restoring the Sòng-era Bào-běn Yuán 報本元 chán-shī seat with Míng Zǐ-bó Zhēn-kě and Chán-enriched literati-patronage background).

**Editorial significance.** Xíng-zhōu supervised the post-mortem cutting of **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi's *yǔlù*** immediately after Wàn-rú's 1657 death (= the present [[KR6q0404]] = Jiā-xīng J26 B182). He is therefore the direct editorial link connecting Wàn-rú's textual corpus to the J26–J28 Jiā-xīng Canon tradition.

Work: [[KR6q0424]] 《介為舟禪師語錄》 (10 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B205, compiled by dharma-heirs [[海鹽]] Hǎi-yán and [[益證]] Yì-zhèng; front preface by [[嚴大參]] Yán dà-cān 1661-04-01; *xíng-zhuàng* by **Xíng-yuān** 行淵 at Yǒng-níng-sì; *tǎ-míng* by unnamed dharma-brother — integrated cutting c. **1672**).

Sources: [[KR6q0424]] juan 10 *xíng-zhuàng* (by Xíng-yuān) + *tǎ-míng* (unnamed); *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 69.
