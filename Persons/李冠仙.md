Lǐ Guànxiān 李冠仙, also written Lǐ Wénróng 李文榮, *zì* Yúshū 餘墅, *hào* Guànxiān 冠仙 (c. 1772 – after 1860), native of Dāntú 丹徒 (now Zhènjiāng, Jiāngsū). A contemporary of [[王九峰]] Wáng Jiǔfēng in the same locality; his clinical maturity is documented by [[KR3ep060]] *Lǐ Guànxiān yīàn*, whose opening case is dated *Jiāqìng* 14 (1809). The casebook is distinctive for its long-form first-person narrative style and its open critique of contemporaries who misused dispersing-and-cooling drugs in *dàiyáng* (false-heat from yang-floating) presentations. Not in CBDB.
