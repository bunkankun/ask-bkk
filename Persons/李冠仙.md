Lǐ Guàn-xiān 李冠仙, also written Lǐ Wén-róng 李文榮, *zì* Yú-shū 餘墅, *hào* Guàn-xiān 冠仙 (c. 1772 – after 1860), native of Dān-tú 丹徒 (now Zhèn-jiāng, Jiāngsū). A contemporary of [[王九峰]] Wáng Jiǔ-fēng in the same locality; his clinical maturity is documented by [[KR3ep060]] *Lǐ Guàn-xiān yī-àn*, whose opening case is dated *Jiā-qìng* 14 (1809). The casebook is distinctive for its long-form first-person narrative style and its open critique of contemporaries who misused dispersing-and-cooling drugs in *dài-yáng* (false-heat from yang-floating) presentations. Not in CBDB.
