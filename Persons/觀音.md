**Guān-yīn** 觀音 / **Guān-shì-yīn** 觀世音 — the Chinese name for the Mahāyāna bodhisattva **Avalokiteśvara**, "Lord Who Perceives the World's Sounds." The cardinal Buddhist Mahā-bodhisattva of compassion, expounded principally in the *Saddharma-puṇḍarīka-sūtra* ch. 25 (*Pǔ mén pǐn*, the Universal-Gate chapter on the Bodhisattva's Boundless Compassion), with cult-recipient since at least the third century CE in China. From the Sòng onward, Guān-yīn was progressively absorbed into the Daoist pantheon under Daoist epithets such as **Cí háng dào rén** 慈航道人 ("Master Compassion-Ferry") and **Yuán tōng zì zài** 圓通自在 ("Penetrating-Awakening Sovereign"), and by the late Míng / Qīng was a fully participant figure in Daoist *fú-jī* 扶乩 spirit-writing communities. The figure delivers the *Lián chuán jīng* in [[KR5i0025]] under the planchette persona *Guān-yīn dà shì*, in a programmatic Buddhist-Daoist syncretist register. Standard reference: Yü Chün-fang, *Kuan-yin: The Chinese Transformation of Avalokiteśvara* (Columbia 2001).
