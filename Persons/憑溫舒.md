# Píng Wēnshū 憑溫舒

Southern-Sòng layman and student of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué; signed the preface to the *xiǎocān* material in juan 5 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]) at year-end Shàoxīng *dīngsì* (1137).
