**Wáng Qìzhēn** 王契真 was a Southern-Sòng **Shàng qīng Líng bǎo** 上清靈寶 ritual compiler, author of [[KR5g0030]] *Shàng qīng Líng bǎo dà fǎ* (Recension I) — a major Southern-Sòng ritual manual.
