**Pān Shīzhèng** 潘師正 (586–684) was the **eleventh Shàng qīng patriarch**, disciple of the tenth patriarch Wáng Yuǎnzhī 王遠知, and teacher of the twelfth, [[司馬承禎]] Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn. Resided at Sōng shān 嵩山. Received at the Táng court by Gāo zōng and Wǔ Zé tiān. Author of [[KR5e0030]] *Dào mén jīng fǎ xiāng chéng cì xù* 道門經法相承次序 (c. 680) — a systematic ordering of the Daoist scriptural transmission.
