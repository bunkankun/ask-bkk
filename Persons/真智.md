# Zhēnzhì 真智

Late-seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[元|Yīchū Wùyuán]] 一初悟元 (second entry) in the Wúqù Rúkōng / Nánmíng / Miàoyòng / Yīchū sub-line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0444]] 《一初元禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B234), working with [[真開]] Zhēnkāi.

Possibly also identifiable with the **Zhēnzhì** 真智 dharma-grandson of **Yuānhú Miàoyòng** who re-cut [[KR6q0416]] 《鴛湖用禪師語錄》 at **Chángshuǐ Tuìān** 長水退菴 — "*Chángshuǐ Tuìān fǎsūn Zhēnzhì chóng zǐ* 長水退菴法孫真智重梓". If identical, this Zhēnzhì served as multi-generational textual-custodian of the Miàoyòng → Yīchū line.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0444]] juan-head signature; [[KR6q0416]] juan 1 re-cutting line.
