# Zhēn-zhì 真智

Late-seventeenth-century Cáo-dòng Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[元|Yī-chū Wù-yuán]] 一初悟元 (second entry) in the Wú-qù Rú-kōng / Nán-míng / Miào-yòng / Yī-chū sub-line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0444]] 《一初元禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B234), working with [[真開]] Zhēn-kāi.

Possibly also identifiable with the **Zhēn-zhì** 真智 dharma-grandson of **Yuān-hú Miào-yòng** who re-cut [[KR6q0416]] 《鴛湖用禪師語錄》 at **Cháng-shuǐ Tuì-ān** 長水退菴 — "*Cháng-shuǐ Tuì-ān fǎ-sūn Zhēn-zhì chóng zǐ* 長水退菴法孫真智重梓". If identical, this Zhēn-zhì served as multi-generational textual-custodian of the Miào-yòng → Yī-chū line.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0444]] juan-head signature; [[KR6q0416]] juan 1 re-cutting line.
