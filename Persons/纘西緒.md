**Zàn Xī-xù** 纘西緒 (*fl.* late 14th – mid 15th c.) — early-Míng Buddhist monk, abbot of the Yǒng-lè monastery 永樂寺 (in the Líng-yǐn-sì 靈隱寺 establishment at Hangzhou), and *fāng-wài qì-jiāo* 方外契交 (Buddhist friend) of the imperial physician [[戴思恭]] Dài Yuán-lǐ. The sole monastic custodian of Dài's unpublished medical manuscript, the *Mì-chuán zhèng-zhì yào-jué jí lèi-fāng* ([[KR3er068]]), which he showed to the imperial inspector [[陳嶷]] in 1441; the manuscript reached print under Chén's patronage in 1443.
