Dé-kǎi 德楷, *zì* **Bǎi-shān** 柏山 ("Cypress Mountain"); often referred to as **Bǎi-shān Dé-kǎi** 柏山德楷. **Cáodòng-school** 31st-generation dharma-heir of **Zhú-làng** 竹浪和尚 (i.e. **Zhú-làng Tōng-ér** 竹浪通爾, Cáodòng) at **Jué-huá** 覺華. Born **1629** (estimated); died **康熙壬午三月廿二日** = **1702-04-19**, age 74, *sēng-là* 42. Native of **Wéi-yáng Tài-xìng** 維揚泰興 (Yáng-zhōu region); lay surname **Zhuāng** 莊.

**Formation.** From infancy showed Buddhist disposition: clasped his hands at Buddha-images, retched at smell of meat or wine, loved sitting in *fú-zuò*. At 11 his father died, and he resolved on monasticism. Visited **Jué-huá** 覺華 to study *zài-shì* (lay-style) *cān-chán* under **Zhú-làng** for years. To "leave the burning house" (出火宅), at 32 took tonsure under Zhú-làng. Took full ordination at **Hán-jiāng Tiān-níng-sì** 邗江天寧寺. Sequential *cān*-fǎng under **Bīn-yǐn** 彬隱, **Líng-yǐn Jù-zǔ** 靈隱具祖, **Chén-xiān** 塵仙 — at the *guò-táng* meal-hall, seeing a rice-ladle drop and bounce twice, suddenly grasped the *xuán-yào* (mysterious-essential) *zhǐ*. Returned to Jué-huá and reported to **Jué-làng** 覺浪禪師 (the lineage-name "Jué-làng" of his teacher's continuation), receiving final *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies (three Shānxī sites).** **Shān-xī Fēn-zhōu-fǔ Fēn-yáng-xiàn Pǔ-tōng chán-sì** 山西汾州府汾陽縣普通禪寺; **Bái-yún chán-sì** 白雲禪寺; **Fēn-zhōu-fǔ Xiào-yì-xiàn Lóng-shān Huá-yán chán-yuàn** 汾州府孝義縣龍山華嚴禪院 (the title-seat — Cáodòng 31-generation throne).

**Disciple.** **Xíng-wù** [[行悟]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0579]] 《山西柏山楷禪師語錄》 5 juan, J39 B466, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xíng-wù** [[行悟]] *děng biān-cì*. Three prefaces; one labeled *zhòng-biān* 重編 (re-edited). Closing *xíng-shí* preserves dating evidence.

**Sources.** 《山西柏山楷禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈汾陽龍山華嚴堂上傳曹洞正宗第三十一世柏山楷禪師行實〉.
