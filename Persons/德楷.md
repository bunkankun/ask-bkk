Dékǎi 德楷, *zì* **Bǎishān** 柏山 ("Cypress Mountain"); often referred to as **Bǎishān Dékǎi** 柏山德楷. **Cáodòng-school** 31st-generation dharma-heir of **Zhúlàng** 竹浪和尚 (i.e. **Zhúlàng Tōngér** 竹浪通爾, Cáodòng) at **Juéhuá** 覺華. Born **1629** (estimated); died **康熙壬午三月廿二日** = **1702-04-19**, age 74, *sēnglà* 42. Native of **Wéiyáng Tàixìng** 維揚泰興 (Yángzhōu region); lay surname **Zhuāng** 莊.

**Formation.** From infancy showed Buddhist disposition: clasped his hands at Buddha-images, retched at smell of meat or wine, loved sitting in *fúzuò*. At 11 his father died, and he resolved on monasticism. Visited **Juéhuá** 覺華 to study *zàishì* (lay-style) *cānchán* under **Zhúlàng** for years. To "leave the burning house" (出火宅), at 32 took tonsure under Zhúlàng. Took full ordination at **Hánjiāng Tiānníngsì** 邗江天寧寺. Sequential *cān*-fǎng under **Bīnyǐn** 彬隱, **Língyǐn Jùzǔ** 靈隱具祖, **Chénxiān** 塵仙 — at the *guòtáng* meal-hall, seeing a rice-ladle drop and bounce twice, suddenly grasped the *xuányào* (mysterious-essential) *zhǐ*. Returned to Juéhuá and reported to **Juélàng** 覺浪禪師 (the lineage-name "Juélàng" of his teacher's continuation), receiving final *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies (three Shānxī sites).** **Shānxī Fēnzhōufǔ Fēnyángxiàn Pǔtōng chánsì** 山西汾州府汾陽縣普通禪寺; **Báiyún chánsì** 白雲禪寺; **Fēnzhōufǔ Xiàoyìxiàn Lóngshān Huáyán chányuàn** 汾州府孝義縣龍山華嚴禪院 (the title-seat — Cáodòng 31-generation throne).

**Disciple.** **Xíngwù** [[行悟]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0579]] 《山西柏山楷禪師語錄》 5 juan, J39 B466, compiled by *fǎsì* **Xíngwù** [[行悟]] *děng biāncì*. Three prefaces; one labeled *zhòngbiān* 重編 (re-edited). Closing *xíngshí* preserves dating evidence.

**Sources.** 《山西柏山楷禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈汾陽龍山華嚴堂上傳曹洞正宗第三十一世柏山楷禪師行實〉.
