**Zhúquánshēng** 竹泉生 ("Master Bamboo-Spring"), *hào* of an early-Republican Chinese-medicine physician of the Péng 彭 family. Author of the *Zhúquánshēng nǚkē jíyào* 竹泉生女科集要 ([[KR3ei019]]), a four-*juǎn* Republican-era systematic gynecology compendium published in Shanghai in 1931 (*Mínguó* 20) with prefaces by Zhāng Zànchén 張贊臣 and Yán Cāngshān 嚴蒼山, two major Republican Chinese-medicine reformers. The author's personal name is not preserved in the KR `hxwd` recension; the ZhāngZànchén preface refers to him as "*Péngzǐ*" 彭子 ("Master Péng"). The work's *qìhuà cìxù shuō* opening exemplifies Republican-era Chinese-medicine reform discourse, reformulating classical gynecology in modernised idiom.
