**Zhúquánshēng** 竹泉生 ("Master Bamboo-Spring"), *hào* of an early-Republican Chinese-medicine physician of the Péng 彭 family. Author of the *Zhúquánshēng nǚkē jíyào* 竹泉生女科集要 ([[KR3ei019]]), a four-*juǎn* Republican-era systematic gynecology compendium published in Shanghai in 1931 (*Mín-guó* 20) with prefaces by Zhāng Zàn-chén 張贊臣 and Yán Cāng-shān 嚴蒼山, two major Republican Chinese-medicine reformers. The author's personal name is not preserved in the KR `hxwd` recension; the Zhāng-Zàn-chén preface refers to him as "*Péng-zǐ*" 彭子 ("Master Péng"). The work's *qì-huà cì-xù shuō* opening exemplifies Republican-era Chinese-medicine reform discourse, reformulating classical gynecology in modernised idiom.
