Huìqú 慧渠, *hào* **Bìyán** 碧巖 ("Emerald Cliff"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎzhù Hǎidòng** [[海棟]] (35th generation Línjì). Lifedates unknown, active late 17th century. One of twelve dharma-heirs of Hǎidòng formally invested with verse-bestowal (*liánfāngjì* 聯芳偈) — Huìqú is described as *tóutuó* 頭陀, a wandering ascetic disciple. Co-compiler of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 — *biān* (editing), printed in Jiāxīng Canon J37 B402 in 1691.
