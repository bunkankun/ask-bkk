**Zhāng Zōngyào** 張宗耀, Republican-era physician of Xīchéng 錫城 in the Huáshù 華墅 medical lineage; the holder from whom [[陳韶九]] Chén Sháojiǔ obtained the manuscript of [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng's [[KR3eh053]] *Lìjí míngbiàn* in spring 1940.
