Liàng 亮 (full dharma-name **Dēng-liàng** 燈亮), *zì* **Yù-méi** 玉眉 ("Jade Eyebrow"); often referred to as **Yù-méi Liàng** 玉眉亮 or **Yù-méi Dēng-liàng** 玉眉燈亮. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir primarily of **Ěr-ān Dēng-sōng** 耳菴燈嵩 (DILA A019189), with secondary transmission also from **Gāo-fēng Sān-shān Dēng-lái** 三山燈來 [[燈來]] (1614–1685) — see the 《高峰三山來禪師年譜》 [[KR6q0209]] account of Dēng-lái's deathbed entrustment. Lifedates unknown — active **1669–1690+**. Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān).

**Abbacies (two).** **Zhōng-zhōu Wǔ-fēng-shān Tiān-níng-sì** 忠州五峰山天寧寺 (entered **康熙八年九月十八日** = **1669-10-23**); **Wàn-sōng Tiān-tái** 萬松天台.

**Disciple.** **Bīng-xián Fǎ** 冰絃法 (sole *fǎ-sì*, addressed in juan 1 *zhǔ-bīng-xián shǒu-zuò* 囑冰絃首座).

**Work.** [[KR6q0558]] 《玉眉亮禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B443, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Kōng-mì** [[空謐]] *biān*; preface by lay disciple **Zhāng Tíng-mài** [[張廷勱]] dated **康熙庚午秋九月上浣** = **1690** lunar 9.

**Sources.** 《玉眉亮禪師語錄》 vol. 1; 《高峰三山來禪師年譜》 vol. 1 [[KR6q0209]]; 《別菴禪師同門錄》 (J39 B445).
