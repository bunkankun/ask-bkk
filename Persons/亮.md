Liàng 亮 (full dharma-name **Dēngliàng** 燈亮), *zì* **Yùméi** 玉眉 ("Jade Eyebrow"); often referred to as **Yùméi Liàng** 玉眉亮 or **Yùméi Dēngliàng** 玉眉燈亮. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir primarily of **Ěrān Dēngsōng** 耳菴燈嵩 (DILA A019189), with secondary transmission also from **Gāofēng Sānshān Dēnglái** 三山燈來 [[燈來]] (1614–1685) — see the 《高峰三山來禪師年譜》 [[KR6q0209]] account of Dēnglái's deathbed entrustment. Lifedates unknown — active **1669–1690+**. Native of **Zhōngzhōu** 忠州 (Sìchuān).

**Abbacies (two).** **Zhōngzhōu Wǔfēngshān Tiānníngsì** 忠州五峰山天寧寺 (entered **康熙八年九月十八日** = **1669-10-23**); **Wànsōng Tiāntái** 萬松天台.

**Disciple.** **Bīngxián Fǎ** 冰絃法 (sole *fǎsì*, addressed in juan 1 *zhǔbīngxián shǒuzuò* 囑冰絃首座).

**Work.** [[KR6q0558]] 《玉眉亮禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B443, compiled by *fǎsì* **Kōngmì** [[空謐]] *biān*; preface by lay disciple **Zhāng Tíngmài** [[張廷勱]] dated **康熙庚午秋九月上浣** = **1690** lunar 9.

**Sources.** 《玉眉亮禪師語錄》 vol. 1; 《高峰三山來禪師年譜》 vol. 1 [[KR6q0209]]; 《別菴禪師同門錄》 (J39 B445).
