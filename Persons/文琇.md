# Wén-xiù 文琇

Early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Nán-shí 南石. 55th *liè-zǔ* of Jìng-shān. Native of Kūn-shān 崑山 (Sū-zhōu), lay surname Lǐ 李. Born 1345; died on the 24th of the 9th month of Yǒng-lè 16 (1 November 1418), *shì-shòu* 74, *sēng-là* 67. Dharma-heir of Xíng-zhōng Zhì-rén 行中至仁 (1309–1382) — one of [[行端]] Yuán-sǒu Xíng-duān's heirs — and thus in the same fourth-generation-from-Xíng-duān cohort as Yáo Guǎng-xiào (i.e. Dú-ān Dào-yǎn, [[智及]]-heir).

Ordained at Shào-lóng-ān 紹隆菴 under Zhì-xīng 智興; in Hóng-wǔ 4 (1371) went to Hǔ-qiū-shān 虎丘山 to hear Xíng-zhōng Zhì-rén expound, was recognised by Xíng-zhōng, and became *shì-sī* 侍司 and then *jì-shì* 記室 under him. Received dharma-transmission from Xíng-zhōng before the latter's death in 1382.

Abbacy sequence: Sū-zhōu Pǔ-mén 普門禪寺, Líng-yán Bào-guó Yǒng-zuò 靈巖報國永祚禪寺, Sū-zhōu Wàn-shòu Bào-ēn Guāng-xiào 萬壽報恩光孝禪寺. In the early Yǒng-lè era (from c. 1403) was summoned by imperial edict to Běi-jīng to participate in the editorial team for the **Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn** 永樂大典, where he remained for three years. On completion of that work — and after participating in the great *dà-bào-ēn* 大報恩 court liturgy — was installed by the *Sēng-lù sī* 僧錄司 as Jìng-shān abbot; later retired near the stupa of Xíng-duān (Jì-zhào) to the south. Died at Jìng-shān in 1418.

His four-juan *yǔlù* survives as **Nán-shí Wén-xiù chánshī yǔlù** 南石文琇禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0355]]; X71 n1422), with preface by Yáo Guǎng-xiào 姚廣孝 dated Yǒng-lè 11 (1413). He also authored a six-juan *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* 增集續傳燈錄 ([[KR6q0017]], completed 1417) — distinct from the better-known 30-juan work of the same title by [[居頂]]. Principal dharma-heir: [[宗謐]] Cuì-fēng Zōng-mì 翠峰宗謐.
