Cóngzhàn 琮湛 (active late 11th – early 12th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; the prose annotator of [[本嵩]] Běnsōng's verse-hymn on the *Fǎ jiè guānmén* in the [[KR6e0102|*Zhù huáyán jīng tí fǎ jiè guānmén sòng*]] (T1885). Born and death dates not preserved.
