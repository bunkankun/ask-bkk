Cóng-zhàn 琮湛 (active late 11th – early 12th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; the prose annotator of [[本嵩]] Běn-sōng's verse-hymn on the *Fǎ jiè guān-mén* in the [[KR6e0102|*Zhù huáyán jīng tí fǎ jiè guān-mén sòng*]] (T1885). Born and death dates not preserved.
