Qīng-period Buddhist monk-commentator. The DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority (A001435) records only that he was a monk and that his lifedates are unknown. The *floruit* given here (1700–1800) reflects a conservative Qīng-period window without firmer evidence.

His sole attested work is the *Bōrě xīnjīng zhùjiě* 般若心經註解 (X571 = KR6c0190) — a one-fascicle Heart Sūtra commentary that draws explicitly on the framework of the Hóngwǔ-era state-Buddhist commentary by [[宗泐]] Zōnglè and [[如玘]] Rúqǐ (T1714 = KR6c0141). Beyond this, no biographical details are recoverable.
