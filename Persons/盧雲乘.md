Mid-19th-c. Hángzhōu disciple of [[王香岩]] Wáng Xiāngyán (1787–1857). His role is as the principal disciple-amanuensis who recorded Wáng's oral teachings as the [[KR3er099]] *Yīxué tǐyòng* 醫學體用; his name is attached to the work in the catalog as nominal author. The work was first printed in 1924 as part of [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jíshēng's *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 series, with a foreword by Wáng's later disciple [[沈仲圭]] Shěn Zhòngguī. No further biographical record located.
