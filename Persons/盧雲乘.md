Mid-19th-c. Háng-zhōu disciple of [[王香岩]] Wáng Xiāng-yán (1787–1857). His role is as the principal disciple-amanuensis who recorded Wáng's oral teachings as the [[KR3er099]] *Yī-xué tǐ-yòng* 醫學體用; his name is attached to the work in the catalog as nominal author. The work was first printed in 1924 as part of [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jí-shēng's *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 series, with a foreword by Wáng's later disciple [[沈仲圭]] Shěn Zhòng-guī. No further biographical record located.
