**Dǒng Huáng** 董熿 (fl. early-mid Qīng, 17th–18th c., 清), Qīng-period compiler of the four-juan *Zī-shēng jí* 資生集 ([[KR3ee033]]), a Qīng gynecology-with-acupuncture compendium. No further biographical record survives; not in CBDB.
