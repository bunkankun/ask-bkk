**Jiǎng Guó-zuò** 蔣國祚 (fl. early-to-mid 18th century, Qīng), *zì* **Méi-zhōng** 梅中 — early-Qīng Daoist exegete who, with his elder brother (the *Zì xù* names a *zhòng-shì* 仲氏 collaborator), produced the inner-alchemical *Huáng tíng nèi jǐng jīng zhù* and *Huáng tíng wài jǐng jīng zhù* preserved in the *Dào zàng jí yào* as [[KR5i0011]] and [[KR5i0012]]. His commentary takes the **Nèi jǐng / Wài jǐng** as twin scriptures of the **Southern lineage** (南宗祖述) of Daoist *nèi dān*, and reads them through the lens of *xìng-mìng* dual cultivation. CBDB ID 422156 (and possibly the duplicate 698910) preserves him with no dates; lifedates are inferred from the dating of the prefaces (Xǔ Shàng-zhì's preface signed at the end of his Jiāng-yīn life, c. early Qián-lóng, places Jiǎng's commentary in the same window). The *Zì xù* describes his work as a long-summer sustained re-collation undertaken with his brother.
