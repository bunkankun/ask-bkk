Shìzōng 世宗 of the Qīng — Yìnzhēn 胤禛 (1678–1735), reigning under the *Yōngzhèng* 雍正 era-name (1722–1735), posthumously *Xiànhuángdì* 憲皇帝 — third emperor of the Qīng at Beijing, son of Kāngxī. His reign emphasised administrative tightening and *Lǐxué* reaffirmation. Author / compiler of: the *Shèng yù guǎng xùn* 聖諭廣訓 ([[KR3a0102]]) — extending Kāngxī's Sixteen Maxims with vernacular elaboration; the *Shèngzǔ Rénhuángdì tíngxùn gé yán* 聖祖仁皇帝庭訓格言 ([[KR3a0103]]) — compiled posthumously from his father Kāngxī's court-instructions; the *Qīndìng Zhí zhōng chéng xiàn* 欽定執中成憲 ([[KR3a0109]], *Yōngzhèng* 6 / 1728). His Manchu name 胤禛 is preserved in the catalog form 世宗胤禛.
