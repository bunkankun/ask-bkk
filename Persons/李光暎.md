Lǐ Guāngyīng 李光暎 (fl. 1729), zì Zǐzhōng 子中, of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng), was a Yōngzhèng-era *jīnshí* collector and scholar. He inherited the *jīnshí* holdings of Zhū Yízūn 朱彝尊 (Zhū's *Jíjīn zhēnshí zhì* 吉金貞石志 was never finished as a book; Lǐ obtained the materials and continued the work). His major work the *Guānmiàozhāi jīnshí wén kǎolüè* 觀妙齋金石文考略 (catalogued by the Sìkù as 金石文考略 [[KR2n0042]]) in 16 juan is the principal Jiāxīng *jīnshí* compendium of the 18th century. The work has Jīn Jièfù 金介復's preface dated Yōngzhèng 7 (1729). Jiāxīng's connoisseurial tradition runs Cáo Róng 曹溶 (1613–1685, *Gǔlín jīnshí biǎo* 古林金石表), then Zhū Yízūn (1629–1709), then Lǐ — three generations of consolidators.
