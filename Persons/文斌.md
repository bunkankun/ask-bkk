# Wénbīn 文斌

Yuán / early-Míng Chán *sìfǎ ménrén* 嗣法門人 of [[如砥]] Píngshí Rúdǐ (d. 1357); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]). Also — per DILA A000181's record — named among the fifteen editors of [[梵琦]] Chǔshí Fànqí's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]), consistent with both Rúdǐ and Fànqí being active in the same Jiāngnán Línjì circle. Lifedates unrecorded.
