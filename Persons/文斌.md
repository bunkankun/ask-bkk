# Wén-bīn 文斌

Yuán / early-Míng Chán *sì-fǎ mén-rén* 嗣法門人 of [[如砥]] Píng-shí Rú-dǐ (d. 1357); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]). Also — per DILA A000181's record — named among the fifteen editors of [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]), consistent with both Rú-dǐ and Fàn-qí being active in the same Jiāng-nán Línjì circle. Lifedates unrecorded.
