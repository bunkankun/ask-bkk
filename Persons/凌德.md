**Líng Dé** 凌德 (*fl.* late *Guāngxù* era, late Qīng), Hángzhōu 杭州 (Zhèjiāng) physician. Author of the *Nǚkē zhézhōng zuǎnyào* 女科折衷纂要 ([[KR3ei024]]), a late-Qīng systematic gynecology programmatically attempting to reconcile classical Chinese gynecological doctrine with contemporaneous Western biomedical anatomy and physiology. The work is one of the earliest serious gynecological *zhézhōng* (East-West synthesis) compositions in the late-Qīng Chinese-medicine reform tradition, anticipating the more radical Republican-era reformulations.
