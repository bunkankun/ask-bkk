Zhāng Xiè 張燮 (1574–1640), *zì* Shàohé 紹和, *hào* Hǎibīn yìshǐ 海濱逸史, also Xīyuán 西園; native of Lóngxī 龍溪 in Zhāngzhōu, Fújiàn. Late-Míng polymath and private scholar. *Wànlì* 22 (1594) *jǔrén*; declined to take further examinations and lived as a Lóngxī private literatus. Commissioned by the Lóngxī prefectural-level officials in *Wànlì* 45 (1617) to compile the *Dōngxī yáng kǎo* 東西洋考 ([[KR2k0145]]) — the most important late-Míng monograph on the South China Sea and the Yuègǎng (Hǎichéng) maritime trade, with its *Zhōushī kǎo* the foundational pre-modern Chinese sailing-route manual. Other works: edited the *Hàn Wèi liùcháo bǎisānjiā jí* 漢魏六朝百三家集 — a 118-juan anthology of pre-Táng literary collections (one of the largest pre-modern Chinese literary anthologies, edition: [[KR4h0007]]?); the *Qīshíèr jiā jí* 七十二家集; the *QuánHàn wén* 全漢文 (preliminary version of the great early-Qīng project); the *Lóngxī xiànzhì*; and his collected works *Qúnyù lóu jí* 群玉樓集.
