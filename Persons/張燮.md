Zhāng Xiè 張燮 (1574–1640), *zì* Shào-hé 紹和, *hào* Hǎi-bīn yì-shǐ 海濱逸史, also Xī-yuán 西園; native of Lóng-xī 龍溪 in Zhāng-zhōu, Fú-jiàn. Late-Míng polymath and private scholar. *Wàn-lì* 22 (1594) *jǔrén*; declined to take further examinations and lived as a Lóng-xī private literatus. Commissioned by the Lóng-xī prefectural-level officials in *Wàn-lì* 45 (1617) to compile the *Dōng-xī yáng kǎo* 東西洋考 ([[KR2k0145]]) — the most important late-Míng monograph on the South China Sea and the Yuè-gǎng (Hǎi-chéng) maritime trade, with its *Zhōu-shī kǎo* the foundational pre-modern Chinese sailing-route manual. Other works: edited the *Hàn Wèi liù-cháo bǎi-sān-jiā jí* 漢魏六朝百三家集 — a 118-juan anthology of pre-Táng literary collections (one of the largest pre-modern Chinese literary anthologies, edition: [[KR4h0007]]?); the *Qī-shí-èr jiā jí* 七十二家集; the *Quán-Hàn wén* 全漢文 (preliminary version of the great early-Qīng project); the *Lóng-xī xiàn-zhì*; and his collected works *Qún-yù lóu jí* 群玉樓集.
