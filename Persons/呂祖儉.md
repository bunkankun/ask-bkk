Lǚ Zǔjiǎn 呂祖儉 (d. 1198), *zì* Zǐyuē 子約, *hào* Dàyú-sǒu 大愚叟, was the younger brother of Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙 and a major figure of the Wù-zhōu *Lì-zé Shū-yuàn* 麗澤書院 lineage. *Jìnshì*; rose to *Tài-cháng bóshì* 太常博士. In Qìng-yuán 1 (1195), at the start of the *Qìng-yuán dǎng-jìn* 慶元黨禁 (the proscription of the *Dào-xué* faction), he memorialised in defence of Zhū Xī and was banished to Sháo-zhōu 韶州 (Guǎng-dōng), where he died. He is the editor of the materials that became the *Lì-zé lùn shuō jí lù* 麗澤論說集錄 (KR3a0052), gathered after his elder brother's death (1181) and intended for publication.
