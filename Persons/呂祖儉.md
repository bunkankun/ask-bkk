Lǚ Zǔjiǎn 呂祖儉 (d. 1198), *zì* Zǐyuē 子約, *hào* Dàyúsǒu 大愚叟, was the younger brother of Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙 and a major figure of the Wùzhōu *Lìzé Shūyuàn* 麗澤書院 lineage. *Jìnshì*; rose to *Tàicháng bóshì* 太常博士. In Qìngyuán 1 (1195), at the start of the *Qìngyuán dǎngjìn* 慶元黨禁 (the proscription of the *Dàoxué* faction), he memorialised in defence of Zhū Xī and was banished to Sháozhōu 韶州 (Guǎngdōng), where he died. He is the editor of the materials that became the *Lìzé lùn shuō jí lù* 麗澤論說集錄 ([[KR3a0052]]), gathered after his elder brother's death (1181) and intended for publication.
