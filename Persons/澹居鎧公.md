# Dànjū Kǎigōng 澹居鎧公

Late-Míng Chán abbot of **Jìngshān** 徑山 in Zhèjiāng, named in [[德清]] Hānshān Déqīng's preface to *Xìngxiàng tōngshuō* ([[KR6n0102]]) as the figure whose request prompted Hānshān to compose that text at Jìngshān. The form "鎧公" — "Master Kǎi" — is a respectful sobriquet; the personal name 澹居 may be his studio-name. No further biographical details located.
