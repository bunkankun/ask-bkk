Quánhóng 全弘, *shìzhě* 侍者 attendant-disciple of **Tǎnān Jījùn** [[機峻]] 坦菴機峻 of **Pǔguāngsì** 普光寺 (Yǒngfēng, Jiāxīng). Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《普光坦菴禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0456]]), signing the colophon as *shìzhě Quánhóng jìlù* 侍者全弘記錄. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
