Quán-hóng 全弘, *shì-zhě* 侍者 attendant-disciple of **Tǎn-ān Jī-jùn** [[機峻]] 坦菴機峻 of **Pǔ-guāng-sì** 普光寺 (Yǒng-fēng, Jiā-xīng). Lead recorder of the 1-juan 《普光坦菴禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0456]]), signing the colophon as *shì-zhě Quán-hóng jì-lù* 侍者全弘記錄. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
