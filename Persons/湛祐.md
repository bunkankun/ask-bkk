Zhàn-yòu 湛祐, *hào* **Tiān-fú** 天孚. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-shù Xíng-zhí** [[行植]] 天樹行植 (1633–1680) at **Hè-lín-sì** 鶴林寺 in Jīng-kǒu. Lead compiler of the 1-juan 《鶴林天樹植禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0522]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
