Zhànyòu 湛祐, *hào* **Tiānfú** 天孚. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānshù Xíngzhí** [[行植]] 天樹行植 (1633–1680) at **Hèlínsì** 鶴林寺 in Jīngkǒu. Lead compiler of the 1-juan 《鶴林天樹植禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0522]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
