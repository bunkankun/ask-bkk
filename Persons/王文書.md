# 王文書

Wáng Wénshū 王文書 (fl. mid-19th c.), late-Qīng collector and editor active around the Dàoguāng era. In Dàoguāng 30 (1850), writing from the Shěn family's Tàinán villa, he assembled scattered case-records of [[俞震]] Yú Zhèn and [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng obtained through book-traders into a four-volume manuscript titled *[[昔賢醫案]]* 昔賢醫案. His preface is preserved in [[KR3ep051]] *Shěn-Yú yīàn héchāo*.
