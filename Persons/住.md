# Zhù 住 (Yúnsǒu Běnzhù 雲叟本住)

DILA-standardized **Běnzhù** 本住; *hào* **Yúnsǒu** 雲叟 ("Cloud Old-Man"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mùchén Shānwēng Dàomín** [[道忞]] 木陳山翁道忞 (1596–1674). Born **1620**; died **康熙丙辰 8/10 = 1676-09-17 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **57**. Native of **Ānlù** 安陸 (Húběi).

**Formation.** Full precepts at **Dàwéishān** 大溈山 (the Húnán Wéi-Yǎng-school heartland); studied at **Hèlín** 鶴林 with initial *xǐng*; final transmission under **Shānwēng Dàomín** at **Qīngzhōu** 青州 (Shāndōng).

**Abbacy.** **Tánzhōu Lóngyásì** 潭州龍牙寺 (Húnán) — the historic seat of Tang-era **Lóngyá Jūdùn** 龍牙居遁. Stūpa not located.

**Work.** [[KR6q0481]] 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B310, recorded by *fǎsì* **Yuányī** [[元一]]. The juan-1 序 is signed by [[睿]] (Ruì) of **Wòlóng** 臥龍 — Běnzhù's dharma-brother under Dàomín — at the request of the publication-organizer **Yuǎnān héshàng** 遠菴和尚 of **Míngzhōu Ruìyán** 明州瑞巖 (the same Wǔlěishān region as [[KR6q0457]]).

Sources: [[KR6q0481]] juan 1 *xù*; 《五燈全書》 j. 73.
