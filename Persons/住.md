# Zhù 住 (Yún-sǒu Běn-zhù 雲叟本住)

DILA-standardized **Běn-zhù** 本住; *hào* **Yún-sǒu** 雲叟 ("Cloud Old-Man"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mù-chén Shān-wēng Dào-mín** [[道忞]] 木陳山翁道忞 (1596–1674). Born **1620**; died **康熙丙辰 8/10 = 1676-09-17 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **57**. Native of **Ān-lù** 安陸 (Hú-běi).

**Formation.** Full precepts at **Dà-wéi-shān** 大溈山 (the Hú-nán Wéi-Yǎng-school heartland); studied at **Hè-lín** 鶴林 with initial *xǐng*; final transmission under **Shān-wēng Dào-mín** at **Qīng-zhōu** 青州 (Shān-dōng).

**Abbacy.** **Tán-zhōu Lóng-yá-sì** 潭州龍牙寺 (Hú-nán) — the historic seat of Tang-era **Lóng-yá Jū-dùn** 龍牙居遁. Stūpa not located.

**Work.** [[KR6q0481]] 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B310, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Yuán-yī** [[元一]]. The juan-1 序 is signed by [[睿]] (Ruì) of **Wò-lóng** 臥龍 — Běn-zhù's dharma-brother under Dào-mín — at the request of the publication-organizer **Yuǎn-ān héshàng** 遠菴和尚 of **Míng-zhōu Ruì-yán** 明州瑞巖 (the same Wǔ-lěi-shān region as [[KR6q0457]]).

Sources: [[KR6q0481]] juan 1 *xù*; 《五燈全書》 j. 73.
