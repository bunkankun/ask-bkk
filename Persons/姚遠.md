**Yáo Yuǎn** 姚遠 (*zì* Shèng-gōng 聖功, *fl.* early 18th c., Qián-táng 錢塘 / Hangzhou) — author of the second-edition preface to [[高世栻]] Gāo Shì-zōng's *Yī-xué zhēn-chuán* 醫學真傳 ([[KR3er060]]), dated *Kāng-xī gēng-yín* 康熙庚寅 = 1710. Yáo's preface contains the autobiographical episode of his having lost a son to incompetent paediatric care and his consequent decision to reprint Gāo's work as a public service. Not in CBDB.
