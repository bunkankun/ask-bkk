**Yáo Yuǎn** 姚遠 (*zì* Shènggōng 聖功, *fl.* early 18th c., Qiántáng 錢塘 / Hangzhou) — author of the second-edition preface to [[高世栻]] Gāo Shìzōng's *Yīxué zhēnchuán* 醫學真傳 ([[KR3er060]]), dated *Kāngxī gēngyín* 康熙庚寅 = 1710. Yáo's preface contains the autobiographical episode of his having lost a son to incompetent paediatric care and his consequent decision to reprint Gāo's work as a public service. Not in CBDB.
