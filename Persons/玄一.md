# Xuányī 玄一 (Korean *Hyŏn'il*, fl. late 7th–early 8th c.)

A Korean Silla 新羅 monk active in the late seventh and early eighth century, traditionally counted among the *yúqié* 瑜伽 / Faxiang 法相-school commentators of the Tang period and closely associated with the broader Silla exegetical tradition that included Wŏnhyo 元曉 [[元曉]], Wŏnch'ŭk 圓測, and Kyŏnghŭng 憬興. Lifedates unrecorded. He is known almost exclusively from a small number of doctrinal commentaries that survive only as Dunhuang manuscript fragments or in incomplete *Xù-zàng-jīng* recensions: most importantly the *Wúliángshòu jīng jì* 無量壽經記 [[KR6p0002]] (X22n0397), of which only the upper *juǎn* survives, and citations from a parallel *Amituó jīng* 阿彌陀經 commentary preserved in Japanese Pure Land scholastic literature. His writings are routinely cited by later Sino-Korean Pure Land authorities (Yuán-xiào, Hwadōng, the Hossō transmissions in Japan) and his attribution as *Shì Xuányī* 釋玄一 (without surname) is preserved in the Dunhuang colophons.

He is sometimes confused with the Jingtu 淨土 thinker **Xuán-yī of Heng-shan** (恒山玄一, fl. mid-8th c.) cited in Japanese ōjō 往生 literature; the two should not be conflated. The DILA Authority id A001339 covers the Silla commentator.

Sources: *Niàn-fó tàn* (Jp. *Senchaku-shū*) citations; Mochizuki, *Bukkyō daijiten* s.v. 玄一; Pak Chong-hong, *Hanguk Pulgyo sasangsa* (Korean Buddhist intellectual history), ch. on Silla doctrinal exegetes; cf. Sungtaek Cho's discussion in *Korean Studies* 30 (2006).
