Mid-Táng 唐 Daoist master, *hào* **Mò xī zǐ** 默希子 ("Master of Silent Hopes"). Active in the early 9th century under the Yuán hé 元和 era (806–820). Author of the foundational Táng commentary on the *Wénzǐ* (*[[KR5c0140|Tōng xuán zhēn jīng DZ 746]]*, c. 809–815) — the principal surviving ancient commentary on that text.

**Religious lineage.** Xú Língfǔ was a Daoist master in the **Tiān tái shān** 天台山 (Zhè jiāng) tradition — the foundational Shàng qīng 上清 mountain. He was associated with (or a successor to) the lineage of **Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn** 司馬承禎 (646–735, Twelfth Shàng qīng Patriarch), though the precise lineage-link is not fully documented.

**Activity period.** Attested 809–815 (the Yuán hé era). No precise lifedates. No CBDB record identified.

**Works.**

1. ***[[KR5c0140|Tōng xuán zhēn jīng zhù]]*** 通玄真經註 (DZ 746) — his major surviving work, a commentary on the *Wénzǐ* (12 juan). The primary Táng commentary on the text.

2. **Xī shēng jīng** 西昇經 commentary material — Xú Língfǔ's commentary on the *Xī shēng jīng* is preserved in fragmentary form in [[KR5c0115]] DZ 726 *Xī shēng jīng jí zhù* (among the five commentators there integrated). Xú's commentary was evidently substantial; its inclusion in Chén Jǐngyuán's ([[陳景元]]) compilation testifies to its authority.

3. **《天台山記》** *Tiāntáishān jì* ([[KR6r0131]], T2096, 1 juan, **Yuánhé** 元和 era ca. 815) — a brief Daoist gazetteer of Mt **Tiāntái** 天台, composed by Xú in his capacity as a Tiāntáishān Daoist master. The work is preserved in the Buddhist Taishō because it was incorporated by **Cháo Yīng** 趙瑛 into the **Sòng** Mt Tiāntái historical-geographical compendium and circulated through Buddhist channels. (Per the Sòng-period **Jiāxī Bǐngshēn** 嘉熙丙申 = 1236 colophon: "Tang Daoist Xú Língfǔ composed [it]. He was a Yuánhé era person.")

4. **Various other short works and poems** — some attested in Daoist hagiography.

**Philosophical orientation.** Xú Língfǔ represents the **mature mid-Táng *Chóngxuán* 重玄 synthesis** — integrating Daoist cosmology with *Xuánxué* 玄學 philosophical apparatus and Buddhist-Mādhyamika dialectical categories. His commentary on the *Wénzǐ* applies this framework to a text previously lacking substantial scholarly treatment.

**Disambiguation.** Not to be confused with other mid-Táng figures named Xú (notably Xú Dòng 徐洞 and Xú Dào miào 徐道邈).
