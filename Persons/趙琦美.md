Late-Míng bibliophile, bibliographer, and book collector; *zì* Yuándù 元度, *hào* Qīngcháng dàorén 清常道人, of Chángshú 常熟. Lifedates 1563–1624 (CBDB). Son of Zhào Yòngxián 趙用賢 (1535–1596) and inheritor of the great Zhào-family library at *Màiwàngguǎn* 脈望館; the family library was one of the most important late-Míng bibliographic collections in Jiāngnán. Held office through *Tàibǔsì cèng* and *Cìbù lángzhōng*. Author and compiler of bibliographic, dramatic and connoisseurial works, including the *Màiwàngguǎn shūmù* 脈望館書目, the *Màiwàngguǎn chāojiào běn gǔjīn zázhōu* (a major collection of Yuán dramas), and — as the Sìkù editors demonstrated — the [[KR3h0045]] *Zhàoshì tiěwǎng shānhú* 趙氏鐵網珊瑚 (a 16-*juàn* compilation of inscriptions and colophons on calligraphies and paintings, traditionally mis-attributed to [[朱存理]] but in fact compiled by Zhào from an anonymous Wú-school manuscript expanded with material from Jiāo Hóng 焦竑 and from his own viewing).
