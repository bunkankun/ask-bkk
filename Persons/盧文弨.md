Lú Wénzhāo 盧文弨 (1716–1795; some traditional sources give 1717–1796), *zì* Shàogōng 紹弓 (also Cháyú 磋餘 / Cuōyú, Yígōng 矣公), *hào* Bàojīng 抱經. Native of Yúyáo 餘姚, Zhèjiāng (resident in Hángzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 17 (1752), reaching Hànlín *shìjiǎngxuéshì* (lecturer to the heir apparent). Dismissed from office in 1771 on grounds of supervisory negligence in a metropolitan-examination case. From 1771 until his death in 1795 he led successively the Zhōngshān (Nánjīng), Lóngchéng (Chángzhōu), Yángzhōu Āndìng, and Hángzhōu Chóngwén academies. The greatest *jiàokān* 校勘 (textual-critical) scholar of his generation; his personal *cóngshū* the *Bàojīngtáng cóngshū* 抱經堂叢書 contains his collations of the *Yì lǐ zhù shū*, *Zhōu lǐ zhù shū*, *Lǚ shì chūnqiū*, *Báihǔ tōng*, *Xīn xù*, *Shuō yuàn*, *Hán Fēi zǐ*, *Yánzǐ chūnqiū*, and many other pre-Tang texts. The *Bàojīngtáng wénjí* [[KR4f0058]] is the companion *biéjí*. Kinsman of [[盧見曾]] Lú Jiàncéng (his father's sworn brother). *ECCP* 549–551 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 33851.
