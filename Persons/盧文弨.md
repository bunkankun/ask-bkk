Lú Wénzhāo 盧文弨 (1716–1795; some traditional sources give 1717–1796), *zì* Shào-gōng 紹弓 (also Chá-yú 磋餘 / Cuō-yú, Yí-gōng 矣公), *hào* Bào-jīng 抱經. Native of Yú-yáo 餘姚, Zhè-jiāng (resident in Hángzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 17 (1752), reaching Hànlín *shì-jiǎng-xué-shì* (lecturer to the heir apparent). Dismissed from office in 1771 on grounds of supervisory negligence in a metropolitan-examination case. From 1771 until his death in 1795 he led successively the Zhōng-shān (Nán-jīng), Lóng-chéng (Cháng-zhōu), Yáng-zhōu Ān-dìng, and Hángzhōu Chóng-wén academies. The greatest *jiào-kān* 校勘 (textual-critical) scholar of his generation; his personal *cóng-shū* the *Bào-jīng-táng cóng-shū* 抱經堂叢書 contains his collations of the *Yì lǐ zhù shū*, *Zhōu lǐ zhù shū*, *Lǚ shì chūn-qiū*, *Bái-hǔ tōng*, *Xīn xù*, *Shuō yuàn*, *Hán Fēi zǐ*, *Yán-zǐ chūn-qiū*, and many other pre-Tang texts. The *Bào-jīng-táng wénjí* [[KR4f0058]] is the companion *bié-jí*. Kinsman of [[盧見曾]] Lú Jiàn-céng (his father's sworn brother). *ECCP* 549–551 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 33851.
