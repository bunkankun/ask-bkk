Dàjué 大覺 was a Tang Vinaya monk based at the **Huáyánsì** 華嚴寺 in **Hángzhōu** 杭州 (per the source signature: 江東杭州華嚴寺沙門 大覺). He was a dharma-disciple of **Xuánlǎn** 玄覽; the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* records: 「僧明了、大覺、普賢、神滿、懷遜皆參預法流」 (T2061, 875b16). Lifedates not preserved. His sole transmitted work is the substantial **Sìfēnlǜ xíngshì chāo pī** 四分律行事鈔批 ([[KR6k0166]], X42n0736) in fourteen *juan*, a Tang sub-commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēnlǜ shānfán bǔquē xíngshì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]).
