# Tōshun 東晙

Japanese Rinzai Zen monk of the Edo period (late seventeenth / early eighteenth century), styled Kōhō Tōshun 高峰東晙; resident of Ryōsoku-in 兩足院 at Kennin-ji 建仁寺 in Kyōto. Compiler of the *xùbǔ* 續補 ("supplementary addendum") appended to the Taishō recension of the *Huánglóng Huìnán chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0055]]), gathering some fifty additional Huìnán items and twelve verses from the *Xùdēng lù* ([[KR6q0007]]), *Jiātài pǔdēng lù* ([[KR6q0010]]), *Wǔdēng huìyuán* ([[KR6q0012]]), *Línjiān lù* ([[KR6r0157]]), *Yúnwò jìtán* ([[KR6r0093]]), and the *Wúshì Jièchén chánshī yǔlù*.
