**Chén Gùlú** 陳顧廬, early-Jiāqìng-era patron of medical publication in Húnán; sponsored the 1812 printing of [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī's *Yī xué zhǐ yào* 醫學指要 ([[KR3eb006]]). Known only from Cài's preface.
