Early-Southern-Sòng 南宋 Daoist commentator, *hào* **本來子** Běn lái zǐ ("Master of the Originally-Thus"), author of the [[KR5c0071|*Dàodé zhēn jīng zhí jiě* 道德真經直解]] (DZ 688, preface 1159).

**Native place.** **錢塘** Qián táng — modern Hángzhōu 杭州, Zhèjiāng — which had become the Southern Sòng capital (under the name Lín ān 臨安) in 1138, two decades before the commentary's composition. Shào Ruòyú was thus a native of the Southern Sòng capital region. His name appears in the bibliography of Sòng commentators *Sòng jié jīng xìng shì* 宋解經姓氏 appended to DZ 707 *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí zhù* (Péng Sì 彭耜, 1229), confirming his circulation among the Southern Sòng collected-commentary tradition.

**Known work.** His sole known surviving work is the [[KR5c0071|*Dàodé zhēn jīng zhí jiě* 道德真經直解]] (DZ 688) — a four-*juàn* commentary on the *Dàodé jīng* with preface dated 1159 (Shào) and epilogue dated 1160 (Chén Yuán qīng 陳元慶). The commentary is distinguished by its editorial radicalism (rejecting the 81-chapter scheme and the upper/lower *Dào*/*Dé* division of the received text) and its ambitious Three Teachings synthesis integrating Mādhyamika Buddhism, *Chóngxuán* Daoism, and *Zhōng yōng* Confucianism. See the main work note at [[KR5c0071]].

**Philosophical orientation.** Shào is a **quietist** (rejecting all external religious practices — *fáng zhōng*, *huáng-bái* alchemy, *xiū zhēn* regimens — in favour of interior quiescence), a *Chóngxuán* 重玄 reader of the *Dàodé jīng* (working through the dialectics of *yǒu* 有 and *wú* 無 to the ultimate *fēi yǒu fēi wú* 非有非無), and a proponent of a distinctive four-step schema mapping the *Lièzǐ*'s Four Primal Stages (*Tài yī*, *Tài chū*, *Tài shǐ*, *Tài sù*) onto the *Lǎozǐ*'s *dào shēng yī* (ch. 42) sequence.

**Associations.** His commentary is revised and textually annotated by **[[張知新]]** Zhāng Zhīxīn (*hào* Qīng hé jū shì 清和居士), a fellow Southern-Sòng Daoist figure whose role seems to have been text-critical and philological. The epilogue is signed by a third figure, Chén Yuán qīng 陳元慶.

**Dating.** Active 1159–1160 (the preface and epilogue dates of DZ 688). No lifedates or other biographical details are recorded. No CBDB record identified.
