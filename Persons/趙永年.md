Zhào Yǒng-nián 趙永年 of **Yáng-zhōu** (the *Yáng-zǐ* 揚子 of his signature is the conventional Yáng-zhōu locative). Mid-19th-century literatus. Named as the author of the printer's preface to [[KR3eq086]] *Shì-yǐn-lú yī-xué zá-zhù*, dated *Guǐ-chǒu* (1853) early summer. He explains that he urged Wáng Dé-sēn to publish the essays for the public benefit. No standard biographical record located.
