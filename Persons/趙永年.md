Zhào Yǒngnián 趙永年 of **Yángzhōu** (the *Yángzǐ* 揚子 of his signature is the conventional Yángzhōu locative). Mid-19th-century literatus. Named as the author of the printer's preface to [[KR3eq086]] *Shìyǐnlú yīxué zázhù*, dated *Guǐchǒu* (1853) early summer. He explains that he urged Wáng Désēn to publish the essays for the public benefit. No standard biographical record located.
