Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A001643. Resident at the **Jìnníng Guǎngjìsì** 晉寧廣濟寺 (modern Yúnnán). Productive in the Kāngxī 康熙 era. Compiler (排錄 *páilù*, "sequential arrangement") of the **Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng kē** 法華經大成科 ([[KR6d0084]], X32n0618, 1 *juan*) — the structural-outline navigational apparatus to **Dàyì** 大義's *Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng* ([[KR6d0085]], X32n0619). Preface by **Wéixuán** 惟鉉 dated Kāngxī 丙戌 (= 1706 CE).

Source: *Dàchéng kē xù* 大成科序 (preface, 1706); DILA A001643.
