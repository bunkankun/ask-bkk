Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A001643. Resident at the **Jìn-níng Guǎng-jì-sì** 晉寧廣濟寺 (modern Yúnnán). Productive in the Kāngxī 康熙 era. Compiler (排錄 *pái-lù*, "sequential arrangement") of the **Fǎhuá jīng dà-chéng kē** 法華經大成科 ([[KR6d0084]], X32n0618, 1 *juan*) — the structural-outline navigational apparatus to **Dà-yì** 大義's *Fǎhuá jīng dà-chéng* ([[KR6d0085]], X32n0619). Preface by **Wéi-xuán** 惟鉉 dated Kāngxī 丙戌 (= 1706 CE).

Source: *Dà-chéng kē xù* 大成科序 (preface, 1706); DILA A001643.
