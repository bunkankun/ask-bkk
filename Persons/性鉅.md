Xìngjù 性鉅. LínjìYángqí *fǎsì* of **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] (1639–1707). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Xìngzhàn** [[性湛]], **Xìnghéng** [[性珩]], **Xìngjì** [[性濟]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0592]] 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B483.
