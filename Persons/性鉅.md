Xìng-jù 性鉅. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] (1639–1707). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Xìng-zhàn** [[性湛]], **Xìng-héng** [[性珩]], **Xìng-jì** [[性濟]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0592]] 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B483.
