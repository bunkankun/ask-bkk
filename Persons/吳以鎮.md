Wú Yǐzhèn 吳以鎮 (fl. mid-18th c.) was a Wùyuán nephew of [[汪文綺]] Wāng Wénqǐ who wrote the *Qiánlóng yǐhài* (1755) preface to the *Zázhèng huìxīn lù* ([[KR3eh007]]).
