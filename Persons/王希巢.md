Wáng Xīcháo 王希巢 (*zì* Yǐnxián 隱賢) was a late-Southern-Sòng Daoist master of the Chóngxū guàn 崇虛觀 in Miǎnzhōu 綿州 (modern Miányáng, Sìchuān). His single surviving work is a three-*juàn* commentary on the *Jiǔtiān shēngshén zhāngjīng* [[KR5b0002]], the *Dòngxuán língbǎo zìrán jiǔtiān shēngshén yùzhāng jīng jiě* [[KR5b0081]] (DZ 397), dated 1205 — composed at the suggestion of a certain Rèn Xiánliáng 任仙亮 who found the earlier explanations of a Yùchán zǐ 玉蟾子 (surnamed Zhào 趙) inadequate. Zhào Xībiàn's 趙希弁 supplement to the *Jùnzhāi dúshū zhì* notes that Wáng's book once circulated with a preface by Chéng Gōngxū 程公許 (fl. 1211) and a colophon by Zhào Rìxiū 趙日休, both now lost. Wáng's commentary is cited by Dǒng Sījìng 董思靖 in DZ 396 as the *Shǔ zhù* 蜀註 ("Sichuan commentary"), and is the principal source drawn upon by Huáyáng fù 華陽復 in DZ 398. No CBDB record.
