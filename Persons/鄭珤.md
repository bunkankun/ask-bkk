Zhèng Bǎo 鄭珤 (fl. 1260–1263) — a late-Southern-Sòng prefectural school officer who served as *jiàoshòu* 教授 of Yánzhōu 嚴州 (modern western Zhèjiāng, the Xīn'ānjiāng valley) during the early Jǐngdìng era. With his colleague Fāng Rénróng 方仁榮 (the prefectural *xuélù* 學錄) he co-compiled the *Jǐngdìng Yánzhōu xùzhì* 景定嚴州續志 [[KR2k0020]] in 10 juan, completed in Jǐngdìng *rénxū* (1262) under the patronage of the prefect Qián Kězé 錢可則 (*tàifǔ chéng* 太府丞), with a preface by the local *zhuàngyuán* of 1247 Fāng Féngchén 方逢辰. The catalog meta gives his floruit as 1260–1263. The *Sìkù* tíyào explicitly states "*shǐmò jūn wèixiáng* 始末均未詳" — his beginnings and ends are not known. CBDB id 51243 confirms a unique person identification but supplies no further data; the homonymous CBDB id 141729 (a Táng person, 791–856) is unrelated.
