Zhèng Bǎo 鄭珤 (fl. 1260–1263) — a late-Southern-Sòng prefectural school officer who served as *jiàoshòu* 教授 of Yán-zhōu 嚴州 (modern western Zhèjiāng, the Xīn'ān-jiāng valley) during the early Jǐngdìng era. With his colleague Fāng Rén-róng 方仁榮 (the prefectural *xué-lù* 學錄) he co-compiled the *Jǐngdìng Yán-zhōu xù-zhì* 景定嚴州續志 [[KR2k0020]] in 10 juan, completed in Jǐngdìng *rén-xū* (1262) under the patronage of the prefect Qián Kězé 錢可則 (*tài-fǔ chéng* 太府丞), with a preface by the local *zhuàngyuán* of 1247 Fāng Féngchén 方逢辰. The catalog meta gives his floruit as 1260–1263. The *Sìkù* tíyào explicitly states "*shǐmò jūn wèi-xiáng* 始末均未詳" — his beginnings and ends are not known. CBDB id 51243 confirms a unique person identification but supplies no further data; the homonymous CBDB id 141729 (a Táng person, 791–856) is unrelated.
