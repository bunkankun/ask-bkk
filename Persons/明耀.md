Míng-yào 明耀. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiāng-yán Fāng-guì** [[芳桂]] (1631–1708) at **Pǔ-míng-sì** 普明禪寺 (Jiā-xīng). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān-lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0543]] 《香嚴禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B424.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Míng-dynasty **Míng-yào** 明耀 (DILA A011041), Wàn-lì-era 阿育王寺 monk and brother of abbot **Míng-jiàn** 明見, who organized the Wàn-lì-era reconstruction of the temple after a prophetic dream — recorded in **Dài Xún** 戴洵's "Ā-yù-sì gǎn-mèng jì" 阿育寺感夢記.
