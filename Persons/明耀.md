Míngyào 明耀. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Xiāngyán Fāngguì** [[芳桂]] (1631–1708) at **Pǔmíngsì** 普明禪寺 (Jiāxīng). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biānlù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0543]] 《香嚴禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B424.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Míng-dynasty **Míngyào** 明耀 (DILA A011041), Wàn-lì-era 阿育王寺 monk and brother of abbot **Míngjiàn** 明見, who organized the Wàn-lì-era reconstruction of the temple after a prophetic dream — recorded in **Dài Xún** 戴洵's "Āyùsì gǎnmèng jì" 阿育寺感夢記.
