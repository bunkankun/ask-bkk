Bāo Chéng 包誠 (*zì* Xīngyán 興言, *fl.* mid-late 19th c.) was a late-Qing physician active in the Jiāngnán literati-medical circle, a follower of the Sūzhōu *wēn-bù* tradition descending from Yè Guì [[葉桂]] through the Wú Tángsōng — Wáng Tàilín [[王旭高]] line. His principal extant work is the *Shí jì biǎo* 十劑表 ([[KR3ed054]]), a concise pharmaco-therapeutic chart that re-tabulates the classical "ten formula-categories" (*xuān, tōng, bǔ, xiè, qīng, dí, gǔn, sè, zào, shī*) into a one-volume teaching aid. He also edited or annotated several smaller therapeutic compendia preserved in 19th-century *yī-shū* collectanea. No CBDB entry; biographical details are sparse.
