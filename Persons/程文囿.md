Chéng Wén-yòu 程文囿 (*zì* Guān-quán 觀泉, *hào* Xìng-xuān 杏軒, c. 1761–1833) — Xīn-ān 新安 (Ān-huī) physician of the early nineteenth century, native of Shè 歙. He is one of the principal regional practitioners of the Xīn-ān medical tradition (*Xīn-ān yī-pài* 新安醫派) at its early-Jiā-qìng / Dào-guāng moment of consolidation. His principal works are [[KR3ep019]] *Chéng Xìng-xuān yī-àn* 程杏軒醫案 (published in three successive series across his working life from 1796 onward) and the *Yī-shù* 醫述 (1833), in 16 *juǎn*, one of the most ambitious Qīng-period medical encyclopaedias drawing on hundreds of earlier sources.

CBDB lists multiple Chéng Wén-yòu records (e.g. 89952 with dates 1671–1733); none is the right physician, who is properly disambiguated by his *hào* Xìng-xuān 杏軒 and his early-nineteenth-century floruit (the Liú Quán-zhī 劉權之 preface to the *Yī-àn* is dated 1805, Jiā-qìng 10). The 1761–1833 dates are conventional in modern Chinese reference works.
