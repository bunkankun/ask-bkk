Chéng Wényòu 程文囿 (*zì* Guānquán 觀泉, *hào* Xìngxuān 杏軒, c. 1761–1833) — Xīnān 新安 (Ānhuī) physician of the early nineteenth century, native of Shè 歙. He is one of the principal regional practitioners of the Xīnān medical tradition (*Xīnān yīpài* 新安醫派) at its early-Jiā-qìng / Dàoguāng moment of consolidation. His principal works are [[KR3ep019]] *Chéng Xìngxuān yīàn* 程杏軒醫案 (published in three successive series across his working life from 1796 onward) and the *Yīshù* 醫述 (1833), in 16 *juǎn*, one of the most ambitious Qīng-period medical encyclopaedias drawing on hundreds of earlier sources.

CBDB lists multiple Chéng Wényòu records (e.g. 89952 with dates 1671–1733); none is the right physician, who is properly disambiguated by his *hào* Xìngxuān 杏軒 and his early-nineteenth-century floruit (the Liú Quánzhī 劉權之 preface to the *Yīàn* is dated 1805, Jiāqìng 10). The 1761–1833 dates are conventional in modern Chinese reference works.
