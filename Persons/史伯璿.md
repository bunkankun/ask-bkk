Shǐ Bóxuán 史伯璿 (Yuán-Míng transition), *zì* Wénjī 文璣, was a late-Yuán / early-Míng *Lǐxué* commentator of Píng-yáng 平陽 (Wēn-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng). His *Guǎn-kuī wài-piān* 管窺外篇 (KR3a0075) was completed in *Zhì-zhèng dīng-wèi* (1367) — the year before the Yuán dynasty fell, on Yáng Shìqí's reasoning placing him just over the Yuán-Míng border. He also wrote a *Sì shū guǎn kuī* 四書管窺 in 5 juan (now extant in fragments through the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* and Huáng Yújì's *Qiānqǐng-táng shū-mù*), of which the *Wài-piān* is the supplementary essay-volume.
