Shǐ Bóxuán 史伯璿 (Yuán-Míng transition), *zì* Wénjī 文璣, was a late-Yuán / early-Míng *Lǐxué* commentator of Píngyáng 平陽 (Wēnzhōu, Zhèjiāng). His *Guǎnkuī wàipiān* 管窺外篇 ([[KR3a0075]]) was completed in *Zhìzhèng dīngwèi* (1367) — the year before the Yuán dynasty fell, on Yáng Shìqí's reasoning placing him just over the Yuán-Míng border. He also wrote a *Sì shū guǎn kuī* 四書管窺 in 5 juan (now extant in fragments through the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* and Huáng Yújì's *Qiānqǐngtáng shūmù*), of which the *Wàipiān* is the supplementary essay-volume.
