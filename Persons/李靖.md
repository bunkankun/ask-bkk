Lǐ Jìng 李靖 (571–649), *zì* Yàoshī 藥師, was the leading early-Táng general and the principal architect of Tang Tài-zōng's frontier conquests. Native of Sān-yuán 三原 (Shǎn-xī). Defeated the Eastern Tūjué 東突厥 (630), the Tǔ-gǔ-hún 吐谷渾 (635), and other frontier campaigns. Enfeoffed *Wèi-guó-gōng* 衛國公. Author / co-subject of the *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì* 李衛公問對 (KR3b0010, the question-and-answer dialogue with Tang Tài-zōng on military strategy) — itself one of the seven *Wǔ jīng qī shū*. Biography: *Jiù Táng shū* j. 67, *Xīn Táng shū* j. 93. CBDB id 31312, dates 571–649.
