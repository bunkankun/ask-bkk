# Jiè 戒

Northern-Sòng Yúnmén-school Chán master conventionally styled Wǔzǔ Jiè 五祖戒 after his abbacy at Wǔzǔ shān 五祖山 in Qízhōu — the same seat later occupied by Wǔzǔ [[法演]] Fǎyǎn of the Línjì line. Cited as one of the conventional commentarial voices judging old cases in the *Dìwáng wèndào lù* selection of *Xiānjué zōngshèng* ([[KR6q0052]]).
