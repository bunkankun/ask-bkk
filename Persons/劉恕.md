# Liú Shù 劉恕

Northern Sòng historian and one of Sīmǎ Guāng's three principal collaborators on the *Zī zhì tōng jiàn*. *Zì* Dàoyuán 道原. Ancestrally of Wàn-nián 萬年 in Jīng-zhào 京兆 (Cháng'ān region); his grandfather Liú Shòu 劉受 was magistrate of Lín-chuān 臨川 and was buried at Gāo-ān 高安 (modern Jiāngxī), where the family settled. Lifedates 1032–1078 (CBDB id 20419 confirmed).

*Jìnshì* of Huáng-yòu 1 / 1049 (passed in the *gāo-děng* "high" pass-list) at age 18 — a *néng-tóng* 神童 ("divine child") class of pass. First appointed *Jù-lù zhǔ-bù* 鉅鹿主簿; rose to magistracies at Hé-zhōu 和州 and Wēng-yuán 翁源. When Sīmǎ Guāng received the imperial commission to compile the *Tōng jiàn* (1066), he memorialized to have Liú Shù seconded as a co-compiler; Liú Shù was promoted to *Zhù-zuò láng* 著作郎.

In *Xī-níng* 4 / 1071, having opposed Wáng Ān-shí's *xīn fǎ* in close concert with Sīmǎ Guāng, Liú Shù requested a *zhōng-yǎng* 終養 leave (final care for an aged parent) and was reassigned to *Mì-shū chéng* 祕書丞 with permission to continue the *Tōng jiàn* drafts at home. He drafted the *cháng biān* for the Three Kingdoms through the Northern and Southern Dynasties — the most fragmentary and difficult portion of the source-base — and it is largely on his foundation that this section of the *Tōng jiàn* rests.

Late in life he suffered a paralytic stroke and lost the use of his right side. Knowing he could not now obtain the Sòng-dynastic state-archives needed for a planned *Hòu jì* of the Sòng founders, he abandoned that project and dictated to his son Liú Yì-zhòng 劉羲仲 the *Zī zhì tōng jiàn wài jì* 資治通鑑外紀 ([[KR2b0015]]) — the *outer chronicle* covering the period before the *Tōng jiàn*'s opening date of 403 BCE, going back to legendary antiquity (Bāo-xī / Fú-xī). He died shortly after its completion in *Yuán-fēng* 1 / 1078, aged 47. Sīmǎ Guāng wrote the preface to the *Wài jì* mourning him.

Also author of the *Lì nián biǎo* 歷年表 (a yearly synoptic table of universal history, transmitted via the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*) and the *Shí-guó jì nián* 十國紀年 (history of the Five-Dynasties Ten States, lost). His son Liú Yì-zhòng was a notable late-Northern-Sòng historian in his own right. Biographies in *Sòng shǐ* 444. CBDB id 20419.
