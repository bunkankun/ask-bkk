Guǒxìng 果性, early-Qīng Línjì-school monk, dharma-disciple of **Yúné Xíngxǐ** 雲峨行喜 (1613–1672). Lifedates not transmitted; active in the **Kāngxī** 康熙 era (the colophon to his *Fózǔ zhèngzhuàn gǔjīn jiélù* dates the work to Kāngxī 5 = **1666**). His scholarly work consists of (i) the **《佛祖正傳古今捷錄》** *Fózǔ zhèngzhuàn gǔjīn jiélù* ([[KR6r0104]], X1595, 1 juan) — a compressed-format Chán transmission record from the Indian patriarchs to his own teacher's generation — and (ii) editorial work on his master's discourse-record, the **《雲峨喜禪師語錄》** *Yúné Xǐ chánshī yǔlù* (J28, 2 juan). Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000679.
