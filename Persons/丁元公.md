# Dīng Yuángōng 丁元公

Late-Míng lay disciple and literary editor of [[圓澄]] Zhànrán Yuánchéng (1561–1627); co-editor (*biān* 編), with [[祁駿佳]] Qí Jùnjiā, of his master's eight-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0374]]). A Shào-xīng-region painter and literary figure of the late-Wàn-lì / Tiānqǐ period. Lifedates unrecorded.
