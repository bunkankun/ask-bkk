**Chén Bǎoshàn** 陳葆善 (*zì* Lìān 慄盦 / 慄庵; also *Liáofǔ* 漻甫, late 19th c. – early 20th c.), late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of Wēnzhōu (Ōujùn 甌郡), Zhèjiāng. CBDB id 672132 (no dates in the CBDB row; active 1880s–1910s on internal preface evidence). The first graduating physician of the *Lǐjīng yīyuàn* 利濟醫院 (a foundational late-Qīng modern Chinese-medicine school established in 1885 in Wēnzhōu by his teacher [[陳虯]] Chén Qiú, 1851–1903) and a leading figure in the late-Qīng / early-Republican reform of throat-medicine.

Chén Bǎoshàn is the author of *Báihóu tiáobiàn* 白喉條辨 ([[KR3em020]]), the foundational late-Qīng monograph on diphtheria in fifteen "discriminations" modelled on Wú Jūtōng's *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*. The work relocates the diagnosis of *báihóu* 白喉 from the *Shǎoyīn* kidney channel (where [[鄭宏綱]] Zhèng Méijiàn had placed it in [[KR3em017]] *Chónglóu yùyuè*) to the *Hand-Tài-yīn* lung channel under the *zàohuǒ* 燥火 dryness-fire rubric, integrating diphtheria into the *wēnbìng* heat-and-dryness epidemic-disease framework. The book was composed 1893–1897 in the wake of the author's own family tragedy (he and his three children all contracted diphtheria in spring 1893; the youngest son died under the then-orthodox nourish-*yīn* protocol), serially published in 1898 in the *Lǐjīng xuétáng yībào* 利濟學堂醫報 with editorial reorganisation by his fellow-student Hú Xīn 胡鑫 (son-in-law of Chén Qiú), and supplemented by his own 1909 *Bǔyì* 補義 addressing the avoid-resolving-the-exterior controversy. His signature emergency formula is the *Sānqì jiànglóng dān* 三炁降龍丹 for late-stage diphtheria with phlegm-and-wheeze terminal symptoms in which the lung's *yīn* has been exhausted to the point of *lóngléi* surging.
