**Xuē Gōng-wàng** 薛公望 (mid-Qīng, 清). Sūzhōu physician of the Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng era; maternal grandfather of [[宋兆淇]] Sòng Zhào-qí. Author of *Shāng-hán gǔ-fēng* 傷寒古風 (also called *Shāng-hán biàn-zhèng gē* 傷寒辨證歌), a versified *Shāng-hán* differential-syndrome treatise that Sòng later edited into *Nán-bìng bié-jiàn* ([[KR3eg029]]). Despite the *Shāng-hán* title, the work is doctrinally aligned with the Yè-Tiān-shì / Xuē-Shēng-bái *wēn-bìng* synthesis — Sòng's preface to *Nán-bìng bié-jiàn* explicitly notes "名雖襲北治，實偏南" (the title borrows from the northern *Shāng-hán* tradition, but the substance leans south). No CBDB record; lifedates not preserved.
