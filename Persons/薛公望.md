**Xuē Gōngwàng** 薛公望 (mid-Qīng, 清). Sūzhōu physician of the Qiánlóng / Jiāqìng era; maternal grandfather of [[宋兆淇]] Sòng Zhàoqí. Author of *Shānghán gǔfēng* 傷寒古風 (also called *Shānghán biànzhèng gē* 傷寒辨證歌), a versified *Shānghán* differential-syndrome treatise that Sòng later edited into *Nánbìng biéjiàn* ([[KR3eg029]]). Despite the *Shānghán* title, the work is doctrinally aligned with the Yè-Tiānshì / Xuē-Shēngbái *wēnbìng* synthesis — Sòng's preface to *Nánbìng biéjiàn* explicitly notes "名雖襲北治，實偏南" (the title borrows from the northern *Shānghán* tradition, but the substance leans south). No CBDB record; lifedates not preserved.
