# Wénzhèng 文政

Northern Sòng disciple of [[重顯|Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn]] 雪竇重顯; assembled and prefaced the *Zǔyīng jí* 祖英集 stratum (1032) of [[KR6q0074]] *Míngjué chánshī yǔlù* during Chóngxiǎn's lifetime.
