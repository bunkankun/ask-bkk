**Liú Tōngwēi** 劉通微 (*c.* 1167–1196), *hào* Mò rán zǐ 默然子, was a Jīn-Yuán **Quán zhēn** 全眞 Daoist disciple of Wáng Chóngyáng 王重陽 (Wáng Zhé 王嚞), the founder of Quán zhēn. Author of [[KR5c0374]] *Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng jīng sòng zhù* 太上老君說常清靜經頌註 — a verse-commentary (*sòng zhù* 頌註) on the *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* 常清靜經.
