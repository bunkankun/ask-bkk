Southern-Sòng 南宋 scholar, active 1208. Reviser (*gǎi dìng* 改訂) of [[寇宗奭]] Kòu Zōngshì's *Běn cǎo yǎn yì* preface and general principles — *[[KR5c0164|DZ 768 Tú jīng jí zhù yǎn yì běn cǎo xù lì]]*.

**Activity.** 1208 (attested). No precise dates. No CBDB record identified.

**Role.** Editorial-revisional work on Kòu Zōngshì's medical-pharmacological treatise, making it available to the Southern-Sòng audience nearly a century after its original 1116 composition.
