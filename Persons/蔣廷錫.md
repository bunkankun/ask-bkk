Early-Qīng 清 grand secretary, court painter, and classical scholar; native of Chángshú 常熟 in Sūzhōu fǔ 蘇州府 (modern Jiāngsū). Zì Yángsūn 揚孫; hào Xīgǔ 西谷; posthumously canonized Wénsù 文肅. Lifedates 1669–1732 are firm (CBDB id 65840).

*Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 42 / 1703 (癸未). Held a series of senior Hànlín and central-administrative posts under Kāngxī and Yōngzhèng, ending as *Wényuángé dàxuéshì* 文淵閣大學士 (Grand Secretary). Bio in *Qīngshǐ gǎo* 清史稿 j. 289.

He is celebrated chiefly as a master of court painting (*huā niǎo* 花鳥, flowers-and-birds; lent his hand to imperial commissions including illustrations for the imperial gardens) and as the editor of major Yōngzhèng-era imperial scholarly compilations. His participation in classical scholarship is more limited but notable: as the *Sìkù* compilers note in their *tíyào* on his *Shàngshū dìlǐ jīn shì* 尚書地理今釋 ([[KR1b0057]]), the work was composed during his service as *Nèigé xuéshì* 內閣學士 (Inner Cabinet Reader) and was prefaced under "*shèng xùn*" 聖訓 (imperial instructions) — i.e. it was undertaken in response to imperial direction received during his palace duties.

The *Shàngshū dìlǐ jīn shì* is a focused 1-*juǎn* monograph correcting a comprehensive catalog of geographical-identification errors in earlier *Shàngshū* commentators, particularly Cài Shěn's *Shū jízhuàn* ([[KR1b0017]]). Methodologically grounded in mainstream Qing-period *kǎojù*, it draws on the *Hàn shū dìlǐ zhì*, the *Hòu Hàn shū*, the *Shuǐ jīng zhù*, the *Yuánhé jùnxiàn zhì*, the *Yuánfēng jiǔyù zhì*, and the parallel philological work of [[傅寅]] ([[KR1b0012]]), [[陳師凱]] ([[KR1b0031]]), [[時瀾]] ([[KR1b0013]] *Shū shuō*), and [[顧炎武]]'s *Zhàoyù zhì* 肇域志 — drawing systematically on the early-Qīng historical-geography apparatus.
