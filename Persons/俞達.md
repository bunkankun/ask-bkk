**Yú Dá** 俞達 (fl. late nineteenth century) is the attributed author of the romantic novel *Qīnglóu Mèng* 青樓夢 (Dream of the Green Bower) in 64 chapters ([[KR4k0206]]). Very little is known of his biography. The CBDB records him with id 85025 and a *floruit* year of 1884, with no birth or death years given. He is not to be confused with the far more prominent scholar [[俞樾]] Yú Yuè (1821–1907), whose surname is homophonous.

*Qīnglóu Mèng* is Yú Dá's only known work. The novel romanticizes the world of cultured courtesans in the Tóngzhì-Guāngxù era and belongs to the *qīnglóu* 青樓 subgenre of late Qīng fiction. An anonymous copy of the same text is also preserved in the Kanripo corpus as [[KR4k0204]].
