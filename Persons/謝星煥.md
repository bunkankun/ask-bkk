Xiè Xīnghuàn 謝星煥 (*zì* Yìnglú 映廬, *hào* Xìngyuán 杏園; 1788–1855) — Jiāngxī Fēngchéng 豐城 physician of the early-to-mid nineteenth century, founder of the Fēngchéng Xiè family medical lineage. He came from a literati background and combined a regional medical practice with classical-literary cultivation; his teaching attracted pupils across the Jiāngxī region. His [[KR3ep040]] *Déxīnjí yīàn* 得心集醫案 (six *juǎn*) was prepared posthumously by his son [[謝甘澍]] Xiè Gānshù with the assistance of his pupil Liú Shàojī 劉紹基, and printed in the 1860s during the Tàipíng disturbances. He is conventionally treated as the principal Jiāngxī Fēngchéng family-medicine founder. CBDB has no entry.
