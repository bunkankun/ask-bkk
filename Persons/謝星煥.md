Xiè Xīng-huàn 謝星煥 (*zì* Yìng-lú 映廬, *hào* Xìng-yuán 杏園; 1788–1855) — Jiāngxī Fēng-chéng 豐城 physician of the early-to-mid nineteenth century, founder of the Fēng-chéng Xiè family medical lineage. He came from a literati background and combined a regional medical practice with classical-literary cultivation; his teaching attracted pupils across the Jiāngxī region. His [[KR3ep040]] *Dé-xīn-jí yī-àn* 得心集醫案 (six *juǎn*) was prepared posthumously by his son [[謝甘澍]] Xiè Gān-shù with the assistance of his pupil Liú Shào-jī 劉紹基, and printed in the 1860s during the Tài-píng disturbances. He is conventionally treated as the principal Jiāngxī Fēng-chéng family-medicine founder. CBDB has no entry.
