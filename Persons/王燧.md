Wáng Suì 王燧 (also written 王璲; d. c. 1425), *zì* **Rǔyù** 汝玉, *hào* **Qīngchéng [shānrén]** 青城[山人], posthumous title (Hóngxī 1) **Wénjìng** 文靖. Native of **Chángzhōu** 長洲 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). In Hóngwǔ raised in the Zhèjiāng *xiāngshì*; by recommendation acting *fǔxué jiàoshòu*; reassigned *Yìngtiān xùndǎo* 應天訓導; promoted *Hànlín wǔjīng bóshì* 翰林五經博士; rose to *yòu Chūnfáng yòu zànshàn* 右春坊右贊善 jointly *Hànlín jiǎntǎo*. At Hóngxī's accession (1425) given posthumous *Tàizǐ bīnkè* 太子賓客 and the title **Wénjìng**. Surviving works are [[KR4e0089]] *Qīngchéng shānrén jí*, posthumously compiled by his grandson **Wáng Táng** 王鏜 (Zhèngtǒng 12 / 1447) and edited into 8 *juǎn* by his in-law **Huá Jìng** 華靖. Wrote the head preface to [[KR4e0084]] *Wáng Chēng*'s 王偁 *Xūzhōu jí* — also as Wáng Rǔyù — placing him firmly in the early-Yǒng-lè *Hànlín* literary network. The poetic mode is the early-Yǒng-lè Sūzhōu / Chángzhōu Táng-revival school, parallel to **Lín Hóng** / **Gāo Bǐng**'s **Mǐn** school. CBDB has multiple homonyms; none confidently identified.
