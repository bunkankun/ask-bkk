Wáng Suì 王燧 (also written 王璲; d. c. 1425), *zì* **Rǔ-yù** 汝玉, *hào* **Qīng-chéng [shān-rén]** 青城[山人], posthumous title (Hóng-xī 1) **Wén-jìng** 文靖. Native of **Cháng-zhōu** 長洲 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). In Hóngwǔ raised in the Zhèjiāng *xiāng-shì*; by recommendation acting *fǔ-xué jiào-shòu*; reassigned *Yìng-tiān xùn-dǎo* 應天訓導; promoted *Hàn-lín wǔ-jīng bó-shì* 翰林五經博士; rose to *yòu Chūn-fáng yòu zàn-shàn* 右春坊右贊善 jointly *Hàn-lín jiǎn-tǎo*. At Hóng-xī's accession (1425) given posthumous *Tài-zǐ bīn-kè* 太子賓客 and the title **Wén-jìng**. Surviving works are [[KR4e0089]] *Qīng-chéng shān-rén jí*, posthumously compiled by his grandson **Wáng Táng** 王鏜 (Zhèng-tǒng 12 / 1447) and edited into 8 *juǎn* by his in-law **Huá Jìng** 華靖. Wrote the head preface to [[KR4e0084]] *Wáng Chēng*'s 王偁 *Xū-zhōu jí* — also as Wáng Rǔ-yù — placing him firmly in the early-Yǒng-lè *Hàn-lín* literary network. The poetic mode is the early-Yǒng-lè Sū-zhōu / Cháng-zhōu Táng-revival school, parallel to **Lín Hóng** / **Gāo Bǐng**'s **Mǐn** school. CBDB has multiple homonyms; none confidently identified.
