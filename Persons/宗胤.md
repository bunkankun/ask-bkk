# Zōngyìn 宗胤

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); named among the twenty collaborative editors of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
