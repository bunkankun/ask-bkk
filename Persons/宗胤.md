# Zōng-yìn 宗胤

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); named among the twenty collaborative editors of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
