Mid-Qīng official, *zì* Jiē-píng 階平, *hào* Yáo-fēng 瑤峰, of Huì-jī 會稽 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). *jìn-shì* Qián-lóng 13 (1748), 一甲第一名 (top-rank first-place — the *zhuàng-yuán*). Distinguished career through the Hàn-lín to *Dōng-gé Dà-xué-shì* and *Hù-bù shàng-shū*; supervised the *Sìkù quán-shū* compilation as a senior *Sìkù-guǎn zǒng-cái*. Posthumous title 文定. CBDB cbdbId 55817, lifedates 1723–1786. The principal-named compiler of the imperial *Qīn-dìng yīn-yùn shù-wēi* [[KR1j0077]] (Qián-lóng 38 / 1773 commission, presented Qián-lóng 51 / 1786), the late-Qián-lóng follow-up to the *Yīn-yùn chǎn-wēi* [[KR1j0074]]. Liáng Guó-zhì died the year of presentation; the imperial preface dating it to "Qián-lóng 30" in the catalog meta is a probable mis-dating — the work was actually commissioned 1773 and completed 1786 (per the Sìkù *tíyào* citation of "Qián-lóng 38 commission").
