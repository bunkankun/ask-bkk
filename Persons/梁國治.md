Mid-Qīng official, *zì* Jiēpíng 階平, *hào* Yáofēng 瑤峰, of Huìjī 會稽 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). *jìnshì* Qiánlóng 13 (1748), 一甲第一名 (top-rank first-place — the *zhuàngyuán*). Distinguished career through the Hànlín to *Dōnggé Dàxuéshì* and *Hùbù shàngshū*; supervised the *Sìkù quánshū* compilation as a senior *Sìkùguǎn zǒngcái*. Posthumous title 文定. CBDB cbdbId 55817, lifedates 1723–1786. The principal-named compiler of the imperial *Qīndìng yīnyùn shùwēi* [[KR1j0077]] (Qiánlóng 38 / 1773 commission, presented Qiánlóng 51 / 1786), the late-Qián-lóng follow-up to the *Yīnyùn chǎnwēi* [[KR1j0074]]. Liáng Guózhì died the year of presentation; the imperial preface dating it to "Qiánlóng 30" in the catalog meta is a probable mis-dating — the work was actually commissioned 1773 and completed 1786 (per the Sìkù *tíyào* citation of "Qiánlóng 38 commission").
