Yìnxū Xíngyóu 印虛行猷 (fl. mid-Shùn-zhì, dates not recorded). A dharma-heir of **Wànrú Tōngwēi** [[通微]] 萬如通微 (1594–1657) in the Línjì-Yángqí branch of the Mìyún Yuánwù line. No separate *yǔlù* or biographical extract survives; his biographical footprint reduces to his credit as nominal chief editor (*děng biān* 等編) of his master's [[KR6q0404]] 《萬如禪師語錄》 10 juan (Jiāxīng Canon J26 B182). The principal working editor of that volume was in fact his dharma-brother **Jièwéi Xíngzhōu** 介為行舟 (1611–1670), whose re-cutting preface dated Shùnzhì 16 (1659) spring forms the closing prefatory piece. DILA assigns him A037269 but leaves the birth/death fields empty — the authority record is anchored only by his editor-credit at Jiāxīng J26nB182_p0442b02.
