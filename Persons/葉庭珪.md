Yè Tíngguī 葉庭珪 (fl. 1115–1149), *zì* Sìzhōng 嗣忠, native of Chóngān 崇安 (mod. Fújiàn). Northern-Sòng *jìnshì* of *Zhènghé* 5 (1115); held *Zhī Déxìngxiàn* 知德興縣 as his first office, then *Tàicháng sì chéng* 太常寺丞 under Shàoxīng. Fell foul of Qín Guì 秦檜 and was demoted to *Zuǒ cháoqǐng dàfū zhī Quánzhōu jūnzhōu shì* 左朝請大夫知泉州軍州事. Wáng Zhīwàng's 王之望 *Hànbīn jí* preserves the official appointment-document for his Quánzhōu posting, praising his learning at length. His sole surviving work is the *Hǎilù suìshì* 海錄碎事 ([[KR3k0017]]), compiled at Quánzhōu during 1148–1149 — the surviving half of his original six-part *Hǎilù* 海錄 compilation project, the other five parts (*Hǎilù záshì*, *Hǎilù wèijiàn shì*, *Hǎilù shìshǐ*, *Hǎilù jǐngjù*, *Hǎilù běnshì shī*) being lost.
