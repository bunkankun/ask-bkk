Yè Tíng-guī 葉庭珪 (fl. 1115–1149), *zì* Sì-zhōng 嗣忠, native of Chóng-ān 崇安 (mod. Fú-jiàn). Northern-Sòng *jìnshì* of *Zhèng-hé* 5 (1115); held *Zhī Dé-xìng-xiàn* 知德興縣 as his first office, then *Tài-cháng sì chéng* 太常寺丞 under Shào-xīng. Fell foul of Qín Guì 秦檜 and was demoted to *Zuǒ cháo-qǐng dà-fū zhī Quán-zhōu jūn-zhōu shì* 左朝請大夫知泉州軍州事. Wáng Zhī-wàng's 王之望 *Hàn-bīn jí* preserves the official appointment-document for his Quán-zhōu posting, praising his learning at length. His sole surviving work is the *Hǎi-lù suì-shì* 海錄碎事 ([[KR3k0017]]), compiled at Quán-zhōu during 1148–1149 — the surviving half of his original six-part *Hǎi-lù* 海錄 compilation project, the other five parts (*Hǎi-lù zá-shì*, *Hǎi-lù wèi-jiàn shì*, *Hǎi-lù shì-shǐ*, *Hǎi-lù jǐng-jù*, *Hǎi-lù běn-shì shī*) being lost.
