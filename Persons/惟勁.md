Wéijìn 惟勁 (active early 10th c.), Later-Tang 後唐 Buddhist scholar; author of *[[KR6e0161|Shì huáyán xuán fù jì 釋華嚴漩澓偈]]* (F1082) — the *Explanation of the Huáyán Whirling-Eddy Verses* attributed to [[杜順]] Dùshùn ([[KR6e0160|F1083]]). Birth and death dates not preserved. The work is preserved in the Fángshān stone-canon and is one of the few extant Later-Tang (五代) Buddhist commentaries.
