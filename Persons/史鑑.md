Shǐ Jiàn 史鑑 (1434–1496), *zì* **Míng-gǔ** 明古, *hào* **Xī-cūn** 西村, of **Wú-jiāng** 吳江 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū) — note that another contemporary **Shǐ Jiàn** of **Cháng-zhōu** (Hóng-zhì *jǐ-wèi* [1499] *jìn-shì*) is recorded on the *Tài-xué tí-míng bēi* but is a different person. **Wáng Shù** 王恕 when *xún-fǔ* (governor) of Jiāng-nán heard of his name and met him, consulted him on timely-affairs; Shǐ pointed out *lì-bìng* (advantages-and-defects). The *Wú-zhōng shuǐ-lì* (Wú-region water-works) was his specialty. He chose not to take office. His writings — *Xī-cūn jí* in 8 *juǎn* + Míng-early *liè-zhuàn* in 1 *juǎn* + supplements — were compiled and cut in Jiā-jìng by his grandson **Zhōu** 周, with prefaces by **Zhōu Yòng** 周用 and **Lú Xiāng** 盧襄. The original recension was reportedly 28 *juǎn* (according to **Wáng Shì-zhēn**'s *Xiāng-zǔ bǐ-jì*, an edition from **Chén Jì-rú** 陳繼儒 — the **Xī-cūn jí** in 28 *juǎn*); the WYG preserves 8 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0143]]).
