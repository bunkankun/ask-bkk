Shǐ Jiàn 史鑑 (1434–1496), *zì* **Mínggǔ** 明古, *hào* **Xīcūn** 西村, of **Wújiāng** 吳江 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū) — note that another contemporary **Shǐ Jiàn** of **Chángzhōu** (Hóngzhì *jǐwèi* [1499] *jìnshì*) is recorded on the *Tàixué tímíng bēi* but is a different person. **Wáng Shù** 王恕 when *xúnfǔ* (governor) of Jiāngnán heard of his name and met him, consulted him on timely-affairs; Shǐ pointed out *lìbìng* (advantages-and-defects). The *Wúzhōng shuǐlì* (Wú-region water-works) was his specialty. He chose not to take office. His writings — *Xīcūn jí* in 8 *juǎn* + Míng-early *lièzhuàn* in 1 *juǎn* + supplements — were compiled and cut in Jiājìng by his grandson **Zhōu** 周, with prefaces by **Zhōu Yòng** 周用 and **Lú Xiāng** 盧襄. The original recension was reportedly 28 *juǎn* (according to **Wáng Shìzhēn**'s *Xiāngzǔ bǐjì*, an edition from **Chén Jìrú** 陳繼儒 — the **Xīcūn jí** in 28 *juǎn*); the WYG preserves 8 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0143]]).
