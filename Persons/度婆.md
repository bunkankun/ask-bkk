# 度婆

Dùpó. Indian *bhikṣu* recorded as a collaborator on the 679 Cháng'ān translation of the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra* by the lay translator **Dù Xíngyǐ** [[杜行顗]], with **Divākara** [[地婆訶羅]] as Sanskrit-verifier ([[KR6j0145]], T19n0968). No further biographical details are preserved.
