Yáng Jìshèng 楊繼盛 (1516–1555), *zì* Zhòngfāng 仲芳, *hào* Jiāoshān 椒山, posthumous *shì* Zhōngmǐn 忠愍, of Róngchéng 容城 (Bǎo-dìng, Zhí-lì). Took the *jìnshì* in Jiā-jìng 26 (1547); office reached *Bīngbù wǔxuǎn sī yuánwài láng*. The canonical *Míng zhōng-mǐn* (loyal-and-aggrieved) figure: his 1553 *Hé Yán Sōng shū* (Impeaching Yán Sōng) is the most famous single anti–Yán Sōng document of the Míng; he was framed by Yán's faction and *qì-shì* (executed in public) with **Zhāng Jīng** in 1555. Posthumously *Tàicháng sī qīng* and given the *shì* **Zhōngmǐn**. His *Yáng Zhōng-mǐn jí* [[KR4e0200]] preserves both his major memorials (the *Lùn mǎ-shì* against the **Ān-dā** Mongol horse-market and the *Hé Yán Sōng shū*) and a self-composed *nián-pǔ*, with appended *yí-zhǔ* composed the night before execution.
