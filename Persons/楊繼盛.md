Yáng Jìshèng 楊繼盛 (1516–1555), *zì* Zhòngfāng 仲芳, *hào* Jiāoshān 椒山, posthumous *shì* Zhōngmǐn 忠愍, of Róngchéng 容城 (Bǎodìng, Zhílì). Took the *jìnshì* in Jiājìng 26 (1547); office reached *Bīngbù wǔxuǎn sī yuánwài láng*. The canonical *Míng zhōngmǐn* (loyal-and-aggrieved) figure: his 1553 *Hé Yán Sōng shū* (Impeaching Yán Sōng) is the most famous single anti–Yán Sōng document of the Míng; he was framed by Yán's faction and *qìshì* (executed in public) with **Zhāng Jīng** in 1555. Posthumously *Tàicháng sī qīng* and given the *shì* **Zhōngmǐn**. His *Yáng Zhōngmǐn jí* [[KR4e0200]] preserves both his major memorials (the *Lùn mǎshì* against the **Āndā** Mongol horse-market and the *Hé Yán Sōng shū*) and a self-composed *niánpǔ*, with appended *yízhǔ* composed the night before execution.
