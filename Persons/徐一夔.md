Late-Yuán / early-Míng ritual scholar and historian; native of Tiāntái 天台 (Zhèjiāng). *Zì* Dàzhāng 大章; *hào* Shǐfēng 始豐. A reluctant servant of the new dynasty: declined repeated summons under Hóngwǔ, eventually serving as Education Officer of Hángzhōu prefecture and as principal compiler of the founding-Míng *Dà Míng jí lǐ* ([[KR2m0028]]) of 1370. He also composed a lost *Dà Míng yìshǐ* and the surviving local-history fragment *Yǒngjiā fēngsú jì* 永嘉風俗記. His prose collection is the *Shǐfēng gǎo* 始豐稿 (in *Sìkù*, KR4d). CBDB confirms 1318–1400 (catalog meta gives 1318 – ca. 1400, consistent).
