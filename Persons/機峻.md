# Jījùn 機峻 (Tǎnān Jījùn 坦菴機峻)

*Hào* **Tǎnān** 坦菴; also **Qiánfū** 潛夫. **32nd-generation Línjì** dharma-heir of **Píngshān Cìān Zhēnběn** [[真本]] 瓶山次菴真本 (= 項謙, b. 1618, the Gǔpíngshān 古瓶山 *Mùdàozhě* 牧道者) — formally received *jìbié* 記莂 transmission at Píngshān per the [[KR6q0456]] juan-1 colophon *Línjì zhèngchuán sānshíèr shì Píngshāntáng shàng Cìān běnshī lǎorén* 臨濟正傳三十二世瓶山堂上次菴本師老人. Stands in the **Hǔqiū → Wúzhǔn → Duànqiáo Lún → Jìngshān Chōng → Xìngshàn Guǎng → Pǔmíng Yòng → Jīnmíng Jièlǎo → Píngshān Cìān** sub-lineage. Native of **Jīnlíng** 金陵 (Nánjīng) — *Jīnlíng yùxiù, Zhōngfù chéngxiáng* 金陵毓秀鍾阜呈祥. Preached extensively in **Zhāngnán** 漳南 (Fújiàn) before invitation to Jiāxīng in 1670.

**Abbacy.** Took up **Pǔguāngsì** 普光寺 in **Yǒngfēng** 永豐 (Jiāxīng prefecture) in **康熙庚戌 5/19 = 1670/6/27 Gregorian** at the joint invitation of three lay-disciple groups (the *zhūxiāngshēn*, *zhòng wénxué*, and *Pǔguāng* circles, with about sixty named lay patrons including [[施博]] 施博 as a *fǎmò* lay-disciple); formal entry-sermon **6/6 = 1670/7/13**.

- [[KR6q0202]] 《古瓶山牧道者究心錄》 — co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of Zhēnběn's own collected sermons, in the *jī*-generation editorial circle.
