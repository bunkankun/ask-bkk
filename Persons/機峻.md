# Jī-jùn 機峻 (Tǎn-ān Jī-jùn 坦菴機峻)

*Hào* **Tǎn-ān** 坦菴; also **Qián-fū** 潛夫. **32nd-generation Línjì** dharma-heir of **Píng-shān Cì-ān Zhēn-běn** [[真本]] 瓶山次菴真本 (= 項謙, b. 1618, the Gǔ-píng-shān 古瓶山 *Mù-dào-zhě* 牧道者) — formally received *jì-bié* 記莂 transmission at Píng-shān per the [[KR6q0456]] juan-1 colophon *Lín-jì zhèng-chuán sān-shí-èr shì Píng-shān-táng shàng Cì-ān běn-shī lǎo-rén* 臨濟正傳三十二世瓶山堂上次菴本師老人. Stands in the **Hǔ-qiū → Wú-zhǔn → Duàn-qiáo Lún → Jìng-shān Chōng → Xìng-shàn Guǎng → Pǔ-míng Yòng → Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo → Píng-shān Cì-ān** sub-lineage. Native of **Jīn-líng** 金陵 (Nán-jīng) — *Jīn-líng yù-xiù, Zhōng-fù chéng-xiáng* 金陵毓秀鍾阜呈祥. Preached extensively in **Zhāng-nán** 漳南 (Fú-jiàn) before invitation to Jiā-xīng in 1670.

**Abbacy.** Took up **Pǔ-guāng-sì** 普光寺 in **Yǒng-fēng** 永豐 (Jiā-xīng prefecture) in **康熙庚戌 5/19 = 1670/6/27 Gregorian** at the joint invitation of three lay-disciple groups (the *zhū-xiāng-shēn*, *zhòng wén-xué*, and *Pǔ-guāng* circles, with about sixty named lay patrons including [[施博]] 施博 as a *fǎ-mò* lay-disciple); formal entry-sermon **6/6 = 1670/7/13**.

**Works.**

- [[KR6q0202]] 《古瓶山牧道者究心錄》 — co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of Zhēn-běn's own collected sermons, in the *jī*-generation editorial circle.
