# 宮本春仙 Gōngběn Chūnxiān / Miyamoto Shunsen

**Miyamoto Shunsen** 宮本春仙, mid-Edo Japanese kanpō physician of the Mito 水戶 domain; teacher of [[中島元春]] Nakajima Genshun and progenitor of the acupoint-transmission lineage that culminated in [[小阪營升]] Kosaka Genyū's *Jīngxué zuǎnyào* 經穴纂要 ([[KR3ee043]]).
