Zé-ān 則安 was a Northern Sòng Vinaya monk, *gāo-dì* 高弟 (senior disciple) of **Dà-zhì lǜ-shī** 大智律師 = Yuán-zhào 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116). His exact lifedates are not recorded; he was active in the late eleventh and early twelfth centuries. Sobriquet **Yán-xiāng** 巖香. His three extant short Vinaya works are preserved in the Manji canon: the *Dà-zhì lǜ-shī lǐ-zàn wén* 大智律師禮讚文 (X74n1507), the *Jié-mó jīng xù-jiě* 羯磨經序解 ([[KR6k0159]], X41n0729), and the *Zī-chí jì xù-jiě bìng wǔ-lì jiǎng-yì* 資持記序解并五例講義 ([[KR6k0169]], X44n0740). All three are short pedagogical works closely keyed to Yuán-zhào's principal *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 corpus, and together they preserve the immediate post-Yuán-zhào *Nán-shān-lǜ* teaching tradition.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000815; *Zī-chí jì xù-jiě* j. 1.
