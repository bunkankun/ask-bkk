Zéān 則安 was a Northern Sòng Vinaya monk, *gāodì* 高弟 (senior disciple) of **Dàzhì lǜshī** 大智律師 = Yuánzhào 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116). His exact lifedates are not recorded; he was active in the late eleventh and early twelfth centuries. Sobriquet **Yánxiāng** 巖香. His three extant short Vinaya works are preserved in the Manji canon: the *Dàzhì lǜshī lǐzàn wén* 大智律師禮讚文 (X74n1507), the *Jiémó jīng xùjiě* 羯磨經序解 ([[KR6k0159]], X41n0729), and the *Zīchí jì xùjiě bìng wǔlì jiǎngyì* 資持記序解并五例講義 ([[KR6k0169]], X44n0740). All three are short pedagogical works closely keyed to Yuánzhào's principal *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 corpus, and together they preserve the immediate post-Yuán-zhào *Nánshānlǜ* teaching tradition.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000815; *Zīchí jì xùjiě* j. 1.
