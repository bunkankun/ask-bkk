**Wǔ Shǒu-xū** 伍守虛 (fl. 1620–1640), *hào* **Zhēn-yáng-zǐ** 真陽子 — elder brother of [[伍守陽]] (Wǔ Shǒu-yáng). Co-collator of the *Xiān fó hé zōng yǔ lù* ([[KR5i0066]]) and minor expositional witness to the early *Wǔ-Liǔ pài* tradition. His annotations preserve the editorial form in which the *yǔ-lù* circulated within the brothers' circle. No CBDB record located; lifedates inferred from his brother's.
