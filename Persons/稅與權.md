Shuì Yúquán 稅與權 (lifedates not securely recorded; fl. 1248), *zì* Xùn-fǔ 巽甫, of Lín-qióng 臨卭 in Bā-jùn 巴郡 (modern Pújiāng county, Sìchuān, Chéngdū metropolitan region — the same district as his teacher Wèi Liǎo-wēng [[魏了翁]]). CBDB id 39042.

A second-generation disciple in the Zhū-Xī → Wèi-Liǎo-wēng → Shuì-Yúquán transmission line. Specifically a disciple of Wèi Liǎo-wēng (Hè-shān 鶴山, *Wén-jìng* 文靖); learned the Shào-Yōng-school *xiàng-shù* materials within Wèi's Zhū-school orthodox framework.

CBDB note: "*authored* Jiào-zhèng Zhōuyì gǔ-jīng *12 juan;* Yì xué qǐ-méng xiǎo zhuàn *1 juan*."

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0057]] *Yì xué qǐ-méng xiǎo zhuàn* 易學啟蒙小傳 (1 juan, dated 1248). He is also the author of a separate *Jiào-zhèng Zhōuyì gǔ-jīng* 校正周易古經 (12 juan), a critical-edition recension on the Lǚ Dàfáng / Lǚ Zǔqiān *gǔ-yì* tradition — cited by Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* and used by the Sìkù editors in their *tiyao* on [[KR1a0043]] (Lǚ Zǔqiān's *Gǔ Zhōuyì*). Also recorded by Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū-lù jiě-tí*: a *Zhōu-lǐ zhé-zhōng* 周禮折衷.

His specific contribution to the *Yì*-tradition: the *bù yì zhī bā guà* 不易之八卦 / *hù yì zhī wǔshí-liù guà* 互易之五十六卦 (eight non-varying / 56 mutually-varying) structural reading of the upper-and-lower *jīng* hexagram-sequence — explicitly addressing a methodological gap left by Zhū Xī's *Yì xué qǐ-méng*.
