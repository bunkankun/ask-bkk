Shuì Yúquán 稅與權 (lifedates not securely recorded; fl. 1248), *zì* Xùnfǔ 巽甫, of Línqióng 臨卭 in Bājùn 巴郡 (modern Pújiāng county, Sìchuān, Chéngdū metropolitan region — the same district as his teacher Wèi Liǎowēng [[魏了翁]]). CBDB id 39042.

A second-generation disciple in the ZhūXī → WèiLiǎowēng → ShuìYúquán transmission line. Specifically a disciple of Wèi Liǎowēng (Hèshān 鶴山, *Wénjìng* 文靖); learned the Shào-Yōng-school *xiàngshù* materials within Wèi's Zhū-school orthodox framework.

CBDB note: "*authored* Jiàozhèng Zhōuyì gǔjīng *12 juan;* Yì xué qǐméng xiǎo zhuàn *1 juan*."

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0057]] *Yì xué qǐméng xiǎo zhuàn* 易學啟蒙小傳 (1 juan, dated 1248). He is also the author of a separate *Jiàozhèng Zhōuyì gǔjīng* 校正周易古經 (12 juan), a critical-edition recension on the Lǚ Dàfáng / Lǚ Zǔqiān *gǔyì* tradition — cited by Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* and used by the Sìkù editors in their *tiyao* on [[KR1a0043]] (Lǚ Zǔqiān's *Gǔ Zhōuyì*). Also recorded by Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*: a *Zhōulǐ zhézhōng* 周禮折衷.

His specific contribution to the *Yì*-tradition: the *bù yì zhī bā guà* 不易之八卦 / *hù yì zhī wǔshíliù guà* 互易之五十六卦 (eight non-varying / 56 mutually-varying) structural reading of the upper-and-lower *jīng* hexagram-sequence — explicitly addressing a methodological gap left by Zhū Xī's *Yì xué qǐméng*.
