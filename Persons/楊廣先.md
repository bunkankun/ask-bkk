**Yáng Guǎngxiān** 楊廣先, early-Dàoguāng-era Húnán patron of medical publication; co-sponsored the 1823 printing of [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī's [[KR3eh051]] *Nèishāng jí yào*. Known only from Cài's preface.
