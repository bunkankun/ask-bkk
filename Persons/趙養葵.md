# 趙養葵 Zhào Yǎngkuí

Late-Míng physician (Zhào Xiànkě 趙獻可, *hào* Yǎngkuí 養葵, ca. 1573–1664), author of the *Yīguàn* 醫貫 and a major proponent of the *mìngmén* 命門 doctrinal school. Cited in [[李彣]] Lǐ Wén's *Jīnguì yàoluè guǎngzhù* ([[KR3ef087]]).
