Major late-Míng connoisseur and bibliophile of Jiāxīng 嘉興; *zì* Yùshuǐ 玉水, *hào* Lèqīng 樂卿. Lifedates 1587–1645 per catalog meta. Son of Wāng Àijīng 汪愛荊, an associate of Xiàng Yuánbiàn 項元汴, whose collection — housed in the *Níngxiá gé* 凝霞閣 — was one of the great Wàn-lì-era private collections of calligraphy and painting. Best known for compiling [[KR3h0060]] *Shānhú wǎng* 珊瑚網 (1643), the third of the three principal late-Míng connoisseurial compendia (alongside Zhāng Chǒu's [[KR3h0055]] *Qīnghé shūhuà fǎng* and [[KR3h0056]] *Zhēnjī rìlù*). Also author of the *Gǔjīn cuólüè* 古今鹺畧 on the salt monopoly (extant in Sìkù, KR2k). His painting and calligraphy records were the foundation for later compilations including [[KR3h0068]] Biàn Yǒngyù's *Shìgǔtáng shūhuà huìkǎo* and [[KR3h0069]] Lì È's *NánSòng yuànhuà lù*.
