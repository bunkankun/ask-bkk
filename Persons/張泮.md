**Zhāng Pàn** 張泮 — nominal author of [[KR5h0006]] *Tài shàng Lǎo jūn kāi tiān jīng* 太上老君開天經, a Six-Dynasties Daoist cosmogonic scripture on Lǎo jūn's opening of the heavens.
