Lín Hóng 林鴻 (1341–1383), *zì* **Zǐyǔ** 子羽, native of **Fúqīng** 福清 (Fújiàn). Leading figure of the **Mǐnzhōng Shízǐ** 閩中十才子 (Mǐnzhōng Ten Talents) — the second-generation **Mǐnzhōng shīpài** 閩中詩派 alongside [[陳亮]] Chén Liàng, **Gāo Bǐng** 高棅 (a.k.a. Gāo Tínglǐ 高廷禮), [[王恭]] Wáng Gōng, **Táng Tài** 唐泰, **Zhèng Dìng** 鄭定, **Wáng Bāo** 王褒, **Zhōu Yuán** 周元, **Wáng Chēng** 王偁, and **Huáng Yuán** 黃元. Entered Hóngwǔ service on recommendation as *Jiānglè xiàn xùndǎo* 將樂縣訓導; rose to *Lǐbù Jīngshàn sī yuánwàiláng* 禮部精膳司員外郎; resigned before forty and returned home. Author of the **Tángyīn** 唐音 manifesto — verse to be modelled on the High Táng — that is the foundational doctrine of the **Jìnān shīpài** 晉安詩派 (Jìnān = Fúzhōu); through his lineal heir **Gāo Bǐng** (whose *Tángshī pǐnhuì* 唐詩品彙 codifies the doctrine) it became one of the theoretical ancestors of the High-Míng *Qiánhòu Qīzǐ* *fùgǔ* movement. His verse collection is [[KR4e0044]] *Míngshèng jí* 鳴盛集, edited and printed posthumously in the early Chénghuà era by **Shào Tóng** 邵銅. CBDB id 34388: 1341–1383, confirmed.
