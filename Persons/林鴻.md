Lín Hóng 林鴻 (1341–1383), *zì* **Zǐ-yǔ** 子羽, native of **Fú-qīng** 福清 (Fú-jiàn). Leading figure of the **Mǐn-zhōng Shí-zǐ** 閩中十才子 (Mǐn-zhōng Ten Talents) — the second-generation **Mǐn-zhōng shī-pài** 閩中詩派 alongside [[陳亮]] Chén Liàng, **Gāo Bǐng** 高棅 (a.k.a. Gāo Tíng-lǐ 高廷禮), [[王恭]] Wáng Gōng, **Táng Tài** 唐泰, **Zhèng Dìng** 鄭定, **Wáng Bāo** 王褒, **Zhōu Yuán** 周元, **Wáng Chēng** 王偁, and **Huáng Yuán** 黃元. Entered Hóngwǔ service on recommendation as *Jiāng-lè xiàn xùn-dǎo* 將樂縣訓導; rose to *Lǐ-bù Jīng-shàn sī yuán-wài-láng* 禮部精膳司員外郎; resigned before forty and returned home. Author of the **Táng-yīn** 唐音 manifesto — verse to be modelled on the High Táng — that is the foundational doctrine of the **Jìn-ān shī-pài** 晉安詩派 (Jìn-ān = Fú-zhōu); through his lineal heir **Gāo Bǐng** (whose *Táng-shī pǐn-huì* 唐詩品彙 codifies the doctrine) it became one of the theoretical ancestors of the High-Míng *Qián-hòu Qī-zǐ* *fù-gǔ* movement. His verse collection is [[KR4e0044]] *Míng-shèng jí* 鳴盛集, edited and printed posthumously in the early Chéng-huà era by **Shào Tóng** 邵銅. CBDB id 34388: 1341–1383, confirmed.
