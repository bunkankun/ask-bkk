# Zhēnlǐ 真理

Mid-late seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[悟進|Jièān Wùjìn]] 介菴悟進 (1612–after 1680s) in the Wúqù Rúkōng → Nánmíng Zōngguǎng → Miàoyòng → Wùjìn sub-line. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0443]] 《介菴進禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B233). Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0443]] juan-head signature.
