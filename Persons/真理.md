# Zhēn-lǐ 真理

Mid-late seventeenth-century Cáo-dòng Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[悟進|Jiè-ān Wù-jìn]] 介菴悟進 (1612–after 1680s) in the Wú-qù Rú-kōng → Nán-míng Zōng-guǎng → Miào-yòng → Wù-jìn sub-line. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0443]] 《介菴進禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B233). Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0443]] juan-head signature.
