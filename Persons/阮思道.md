Ruǎn Sī-dào 阮思道 (fl. late 10th c.), Northern-Sòng official named in the *Sòng huì-yào* as one of the fourteen compilers of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* ([[KR3k0012]]) under the imperial commission of 977. The *Sòng shǐ* preserves no independent biography.
