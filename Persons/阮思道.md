Ruǎn Sīdào 阮思道 (fl. late 10th c.), Northern-Sòng official named in the *Sòng huìyào* as one of the fourteen compilers of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* ([[KR3k0012]]) under the imperial commission of 977. The *Sòng shǐ* preserves no independent biography.
