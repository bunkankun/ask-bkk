Zēng Jí 曾極 (d. 1221, *zì* Jǐngjiàn 景建), of *Línchuān* 臨川 in *Fǔzhōu* 撫州 (modern Jiāngxī), a *bùyī* 布衣 commoner who never held office. Late-Southern-Sòng anti-appeasement poet associated with the Liú Guò 劉過 / Xīn Qìjí [[辛棄疾]] circle. Inscribed an anti-Shǐ Míyuǎn 史彌遠 poem on a palace dragon-screen at *Jīnlíng*, offending the chief-councillor; subsequently implicated in the *Jiānghú jí* 江湖集 literary-political prosecution and banished to *Dàozhōu* 道州, where he died in 1221. His other work *Chōnglíng xiǎoyǎ* 舂陵小雅 is lost. CBDB id 48488. Author of [[KR4d0271]] *Jīnlíng bǎiyǒng*.
