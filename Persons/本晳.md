High-Qīng Línjì-zōng 臨濟宗 Yángqí 楊岐 branch Chán master, *hào* Shān-xiǎo 山曉 / Xiào-táng 嘯堂. Lifedates 1620 – middle-winter of Kāngxī *bǐng-yín* = 24 January 1686 / 11 February 1687 (*shì-shòu* 67, *sēng-là* 50). Native of Chángshòu 長壽 xiàn (in Chóngqìng, Sìchuān), lay surname Wèi 魏. Ordained under San-mèi chánshī 三昧和尚 [[寂光]] at age 21; principal dharma-heir of **Hóng-jué Dào-mǐn** [[道忞]] (Shān-wēng lǎorén 山翁老人, 1596–1674), whom he accompanied on the 1659–60 imperial summons to the capital. Assigned by the Kāng-xī court to the abbacy of the Lóng-ān-sì 隆安寺 in Shèng-jīng (Shenyang), where he received the purple robe. Subsequently held five abbot's seats: Lóng-ān, twice at Hángzhōu Fó-rì-sì 佛日寺, Sūzhōu Bāo-shān 包山, and the principal seat of Sì-míng Tiāntóng 四明天童. Buried near the stūpa of Mì-ān Xián-jié 密菴咸傑 at Zhōng-fēng.

Principal editorial achievement: the **93-juan *Zōng-mén bǎo-jī lù* 宗門寶積錄** (preface and table of contents only surviving as [[KR6q0388]] in Kanripo; full work elsewhere), completed in 1679 per his teacher Dào-mǐn's deathbed charge. Also wrote an *Zòu-duì lù* 奏對錄 (one juan, recording his imperial audiences) and a 10+-juan *quán-lù* 全錄. Epitaph by the Grand Minister of Revenue Lǐ Xiān-gēn 李仙根. Dharma-heirs include Wèi-hóng Yuán-shèng 慰弘元盛 (杼山), Shì-ān Lǐ 式菴理, Jìng 靜 (Bǎi-táng of Tiāntóng), and others.
