Early-Northern-Sòng remonstrance-official, *zì* Biǎoshèng 表聖. *Jīngzhào* family resettled in Shǔ (Sìchuān). *Jìnshì* of Tàipíng xīngguó 3 / 978, second place under Sū Yìjiǎn. Held *Yòushíyí*, *Yòubǔquē*, *Héběi zhuǎnyùnshǐ*, *Zhīzhìgào*, *Bīngbù yuánwàiláng*, and *Yòujiànyìdàfū shǐguǎn xiūzhuàn* (the post he held when he died). Demoted twice for the frankness of his remonstrance — once to *Hǎizhōu tuánliànfùshǐ* and once to *Yánlíng* (Wǎnqiū) — and twice rapidly restored. Died at his post in Xiánpíng 6 / 1003, age 64. Author of the *Xiánpíng jí* [[KR4d0003]]; epitaph by Fàn Zhòngyān, *shéndào bēi* by Sīmǎ Guāng, *xù* to his collected memorials by Sū Shì (who compared him to Jiǎ Yì 賈誼). Posthumously held up by the Northern-Sòng *yánshì* tradition as the moral exemplar of the *yùshǐ* function. CBDB id 1615, lifedates 940–1003.
