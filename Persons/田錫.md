Early-Northern-Sòng remonstrance-official, *zì* Biǎo-shèng 表聖. *Jīng-zhào* family resettled in Shǔ (Sìchuān). *Jìn-shì* of Tài-píng xīng-guó 3 / 978, second place under Sū Yì-jiǎn. Held *Yòu-shí-yí*, *Yòu-bǔ-quē*, *Hé-běi zhuǎn-yùn-shǐ*, *Zhī-zhì-gào*, *Bīng-bù yuán-wài-láng*, and *Yòu-jiàn-yì-dà-fū shǐ-guǎn xiū-zhuàn* (the post he held when he died). Demoted twice for the frankness of his remonstrance — once to *Hǎi-zhōu tuán-liàn-fù-shǐ* and once to *Yán-líng* (Wǎn-qiū) — and twice rapidly restored. Died at his post in Xián-píng 6 / 1003, age 64. Author of the *Xián-píng jí* [[KR4d0003]]; epitaph by Fàn Zhòng-yān, *shén-dào bēi* by Sī-mǎ Guāng, *xù* to his collected memorials by Sū Shì (who compared him to Jiǎ Yì 賈誼). Posthumously held up by the Northern-Sòng *yán-shì* tradition as the moral exemplar of the *yù-shǐ* function. CBDB id 1615, lifedates 940–1003.
