# Shàn-guǒ 善果

Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional Chán monk; dharma-heir junior-disciple (*sì fǎ xiǎo shī* 嗣法小師) of Kāi-fú Dào-níng 開福道寧 (1053–1113). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler (*jí* 集) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0287]].
