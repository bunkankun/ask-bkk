**Wáng Tíngzēng** 王庭曾 (early Qīng), *Zhī Yìwūxiàn* (Magistrate of Yìwū) — Zōng Zé's native county. Re-edited Zōng Zé's *Zōng Zhōngjiǎn jí* 宗忠簡集 [[KR4d0135]] in 8 *juǎn*, supplementing the earlier Sòng-period Lóu Fǎng [[樓昉]] compilation with the *Jiàn zhǐ gēdì* memorial that had not been included. The catalog gives *dynasty: 清 (Qīng)* — early-Qīng official functioning as the magistrate-editor of a famous-loyalist's collection. Precise dates and broader career undocumented.
