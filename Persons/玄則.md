Xuánzé 玄則 was a Tang-period Buddhist monk based at Xīmíngsì 西明寺 in Cháng'ān. He is best known as the author of the *Initial Assembly Preface* (《大般若經》初會序) to [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng's [[KR6c0001|*Mahāprajñāpāramitā-sūtra* (T220)]].
