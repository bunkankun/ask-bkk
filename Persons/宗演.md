# Zōngyǎn 宗演

Southern-Sòng Chán monk of the Línjì school, styled Huázàng Zōngyǎn 華藏宗演 after his residence at the Huázàng sì 華藏寺 (Línān / Hángzhōu). Active at the turn of the thirteenth century. Wrote the 1205 (Kāixǐ 1 = 開禧乙丑) collating-afterword to the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī niánpǔ* 大慧普覺禪師年譜 ([[KR6q0063]]) originally compiled by [[祖詠]] Zǔyǒng, into which he introduced sixty-plus emendations based on a manuscript of Yúngái Yúnwò 雲蓋雲臥 (sometimes also transmitted as 江西瑩雲臥), Dàhuì's attendant during the Héng-Méi 衡梅 exile years. Not to be confused with the earlier Yuánjué Zōngyǎn 圓覺宗演 (Northern Sòng, fl. 1120) who edited the *Línjì lù* ([[KR6q0053]]).
