# Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi 百丈懷海

Táng-dynasty Chán master (720–814), leading Hóngzhōu-school heir of Mǎzǔ Dàoyī 馬祖道一; teacher of [[靈祐|Wéishān Língyòu]] 溈山靈祐 and the figure traditionally credited with the *Bǎizhàng qīngguī* 百丈清規 monastic-regulations tradition.
