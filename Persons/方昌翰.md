**Fāng Chānghán** 方昌翰 (*fl.* late *Dào-guāng* through *Guāng-xù*, 19th c. 清), Ānhuī editor and physician of the Jīng-dé 旌德 district. Editor/collator of the *Zhúlín nǚkē zhèngzhì* 竹林女科證治 ([[KR3ei026]]), a Bamboo-Grove-Monastery (*Zhú-lín-sì* 竹林寺) gynecology in 4 *juǎn*, originally printed in *Dào-guāng* with woodblocks destroyed in the *Tàipíng* wars (c. 1861), then reconstructed from a surviving manuscript copy and reprinted in 1881 (*Guāng-xù xīn-sì*). Fāng's *bá* (colophon) is a key documentary record of the lineage transmission. No further biographical detail located.
