**Fāng Chānghán** 方昌翰 (*fl.* late *Dàoguāng* through *Guāngxù*, 19th c. 清), Ānhuī editor and physician of the Jīngdé 旌德 district. Editor/collator of the *Zhúlín nǚkē zhèngzhì* 竹林女科證治 ([[KR3ei026]]), a Bamboo-Grove-Monastery (*Zhúlínsì* 竹林寺) gynecology in 4 *juǎn*, originally printed in *Dàoguāng* with woodblocks destroyed in the *Tàipíng* wars (c. 1861), then reconstructed from a surviving manuscript copy and reprinted in 1881 (*Guāngxù xīnsì*). Fāng's *bá* (colophon) is a key documentary record of the lineage transmission. No further biographical detail located.
