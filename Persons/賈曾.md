Jiǎ Céng 賈曾 (d. 727) was a Tang-period official and *jìnshì* who served in high posts at court under Xuánzōng 玄宗. He is known to Buddhist studies as the author of the preface to [[圓暉]] Yuánhuī's *Jùshě lùn sòng shū* 俱舍論頌疏 ([[KR6l0036]], T1823) — a rare non-monastic witness to the Tang Kōsha commentary tradition that explicitly documents the transmission line from [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng through [[普光]] Pǔguāng to Yuánhuī.
