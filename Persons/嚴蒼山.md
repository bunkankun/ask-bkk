# 嚴蒼山 Yán Cāngshān

Republican-era and PRC-era Shanghai-school physician (1898–1968); student of [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐngfǔ; later one of the principal physicians of the Shanghai Chinese-medicine establishment.
