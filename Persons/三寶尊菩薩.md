Sān-bǎo-zūn púsà 三寶尊菩薩 (\*Triratnadāsa "Servant-of-the-Three-Jewels"; lifedates unknown), Indic Buddhist *bodhisattva*-class commentator, named in [[KR6c0017|*Fó-mǔ bōrě bōluómìduō yuán-jí yào-yì shì-lùn* 佛母般若波羅蜜多圓集要義釋論 (T1517)]] as the author of the commentary on [[大域龍菩薩|Dignāga]]'s *Prajñāpāramitā-piṇḍārtha-saṃgraha* (= [[KR6c0018|T1518]]). The Indic *Triratnadāsa* is also identified in some Tibetan sources as a *Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra* commentator. The relationship between this Triratnadāsa and the various other *bodhisattva*-named commentators in the Sòng-period Indic-translation corpus is debated.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6c0017]] *Fó-mǔ bōrě bōluómìduō yuán-jí yào-yì shì-lùn* (T1517).
