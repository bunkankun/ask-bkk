# Yuánxìn 圓信

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Chán monk of Jìngshān 徑山, styled Yǔfēng Yuánxìn 語風圓信 and Jìngshān Yǔfēng lǎorén 徑山語風老人. With the layman [[郭凝之]] Guō Níngzhī (the self-styled 無地地主人) he formed the most productive Chán editorial pair of the Míng-Qīng transition. Joint editorial work includes (in approximate order): the late-Míng *Ruìzhōu Dòngshān Liángjiè chánshī yǔlù* 瑞州洞山良价禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0067]], ca. 1630s–40s), the *Xiānjué zōngshèng* 先覺宗乘 ([[KR6q0050]], 1640–1670), and the *Yōupóyí zhì* 優婆夷志 ([[KR6q0051]], 1640–1670). Yuánxìn's role in the joint projects was variously *jiàodìng* 較定 (editorial correction) and *biānjí* 編集 (compilation / editing). Exact lifedates and lineage affiliation not transmitted. Not to be confused with other Chán figures of the same Dharma name.
