**Xiè Yīngcái** 謝應材 (*hào* Suìqiáo 邃喬, *fl.* late Qiánlóng – Jiāqìng, 清), Qīng-period scholar-physician trained in both internal and external medicine. Founder of a three-generation family practice (sons Dōnghuán 東環 and Dōngkuí 東揆 continued his clinical work). His method, distinguishing *fābèi* 發背 and *duìkǒu* 對口 carbuncles by *zuǒyòu shàngxià* 左右上下 (left / right / upper / lower), *sè zhī chìbái* 色之赤白, and *mài zhī fúchén* 脈之浮沉, was edited and printed posthumously by his grandsons in Dàoguāng 20 (1840) as [[KR3ek043]] *Fābèi duìkǒu zhìjué lùn* 發背對口治訣論. The therapeutic core of the work is *shēngyáng sànhuǒ* 升陽散火 with *zhì gāncǎo* 制甘草 as sovereign drug. Known only through his grandson's preface; lifedates not documented. Not in CBDB.
