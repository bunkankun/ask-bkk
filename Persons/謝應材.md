**Xiè Yīngcái** 謝應材 (*hào* Suì-qiáo 邃喬, *fl.* late Qián-lóng – Jiā-qìng, 清), Qīng-period scholar-physician trained in both internal and external medicine. Founder of a three-generation family practice (sons Dōng-huán 東環 and Dōng-kuí 東揆 continued his clinical work). His method, distinguishing *fā-bèi* 發背 and *duì-kǒu* 對口 carbuncles by *zuǒ-yòu shàng-xià* 左右上下 (left / right / upper / lower), *sè zhī chì-bái* 色之赤白, and *mài zhī fú-chén* 脈之浮沉, was edited and printed posthumously by his grandsons in Dào-guāng 20 (1840) as [[KR3ek043]] *Fā-bèi duì-kǒu zhì-jué lùn* 發背對口治訣論. The therapeutic core of the work is *shēng-yáng sàn-huǒ* 升陽散火 with *zhì gān-cǎo* 制甘草 as sovereign drug. Known only through his grandson's preface; lifedates not documented. Not in CBDB.
