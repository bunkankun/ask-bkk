A Five-Dynasties / early-Sòng Daoist master, whose biography is preserved at *[[KR5a0298|DZ 296 Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn 歷世真仙體道通鑑]]* 47.15a. Author of the *Tàishàng shēngxuán xiāozāi hùmìng miàojīng sòng* 太上昇玄消災護命妙經頌 ([[KR5a0324]] / DZ 312), a rhymed commentary on the Táng-period *[[KR5a0019|Tàishàng shēngxuán xiāozāi hùmìng miàojīng 太上昇玄消災護命妙經]]* (DZ 19). The *Daozàng* edition incorrectly attributes the work to *Zhēnyī xiānshēng* [[司馬承禎|Sīmǎ Zǐwēi]] 司馬子微 (i.e., Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn 司馬承禎, 647–735) of the Táng — but as Hans-Hermann Schmidt notes (Schipper & Verellen 2004, 1:317), the *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn* biography of Zhāng Bó states that he wrote a rhymed commentary on the work in question, and quotes the first and last lines of that commentary, which correspond exactly to the present text. The misattribution to Sīmǎ probably arose because the *Tàngrén* ascription of the parent scripture (in the Táng) was conflated with the authorship of the later commentary. CBDB returns several persons of this name; none can be confidently matched. The catalog meta dates Zhāng to *fl. 10th cent. 後半*, the second half of the tenth century.
