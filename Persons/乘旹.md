**Chéngshí** 乘旹 (also written 乘時 — both forms appear in the canonical record), original name **Liǎofán** 了凡, *hào* **Cháizǐ** 柴紫. Late-Ming **Línjì** 臨濟 Chán monk. Native of **Dāngyáng** 當陽 (in Húběi); lay surname **Jìn** 靳.

Took the tonsure in youth at the **Bào-ēn-sì** 報恩寺 under **Guǎng-tōng** 廣通; studied the sūtras and śāstras at length. Later met **Wú-jì Zhèng-huì** 無跡正誨 (1547–1627) at the **Dù-mén-sì** 度門寺, received Wú-jì's Dharma transmission, and changed his name to **Chéng-shí** 乘旹. Subsequently became abbot of the **Yù-quán Dù-mén-sì** 玉泉度門寺.

His extant work is the **Lèngyán-jīng jiǎng-lù** 楞嚴經講錄 ([[KR6j0707]], X15 no. 299), a transcription of his lectures on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* delivered at **Yú-shān** 虞山. The self-preface (*zì-xù* 自敘) is dated to the day of the **Buddha's Enlightenment, Tiān-qǐ 2, *rén-xū* = 1622 CE** (天啟壬戌（1622）佛成道日撰〈楞嚴講錄自敘〉).
