Fāngzé 方澤 (active late Míng), Buddhist scholar and editor known principally for compiling the [[KR6e0068|*Huáyán jīng hé lùn zuǎn yào* 華嚴經合論纂要]] (X226, 3 fascicles) — a further abridgement / editorial reorganisation of the Lǐ Tōngxuán *Hé lùn* tradition. His birth and death dates are not preserved; the work is conventionally dated to the late Míng period, broadly contemporary with [[李贄簡]] Lǐ Zhuówú's [[KR6e0067|abridgement]]. The principal modern study of Fāngzé is in Yoshizu Yoshihide's *Kegon zen no shisōshi-teki kenkyū* (1985), which treats him within the broader late-Míng Buddhist-publication context.
