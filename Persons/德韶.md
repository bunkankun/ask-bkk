# Déshào 德韶

Five Dynasties / early-Sòng Chán master (891–972), based on Mt Tiāntái 天台山 and a Fǎyǎn-school dharma-heir of [[文益]] Fǎyǎn Wényì; chief preceptor in *chándìng* 禪定 of [[延壽]] Yǒngmíng Yánshòu.
