Zhèng Kè 鄭克 (12th c., Sòng), *zì* Wǔzǐ 武子, was a Northern–Southern Sòng transition judicial official. *Jìnshì* of *Shào-xīng* 1 (1131). Held minor judicial posts. Author of the *Zhé yù guī jiàn* 折獄龜鑑 (KR3c0007), the major Sòng *yí yù*-genre work expanding from Hé Níng's foundational *Yí yù jí* (KR3c0006). The catalog meta gives "12th cent." as date.
