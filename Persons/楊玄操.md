# 楊玄操 Yáng Xuáncāo

**Yáng Xuáncāo** 楊玄操, Táng-era physician and commentator; author of an influential commentary on the *Nànjīng* 難經 (later cited as one of the principal Táng *Nànjīng* glosses), now surviving only in citations.
