Xiè Qiáo 謝嶠 (fl. early-to-mid sixth century), a Liáng-period 梁 *xiǎoxué* scholar known almost exclusively through Lù Démíng 陸德明's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 as the author of an *Ěryǎ yīn* 爾雅音 (now lost, reconstructed as [[KR1j0096]] *Ěryǎ Xiè-shì yīn*). Cited by Lù Démíng in the triplet *Gù, Shī, Xiè* 顧、施、謝 with [[顧野王]] Gù Yěwáng and [[施乾]] Shī Qián, representing the southern Liáng *xiǎoxué* trio of *Ěryǎ* phonological commentators. No biography in any standard history; he is sometimes identified speculatively with members of the prominent Chén-jùn Xiè 陳郡謝 lineage of the Liáng court but no evidence ties him to a specific person of that name. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* records his *Ěryǎ yīn* in two *juàn*; lost in the Sòng. CBDB has no matching entry.
