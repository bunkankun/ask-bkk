Xìngtǐng 性珽, *zì* **Xiàngyái** 象崖. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666); thirty-sixth-generation Línjì 大鑑下第三十六世. Born **1598**; died **大清順治8 辛卯 7月** = **1651-08-16/09-14**, *shìshòu* **54**, *fǎlà* **35**. Native of **Fúqīng** 福清 (Fúzhōu prefecture, Fújiàn); lay surname **Chén** 陳.

**Formation.** Reading the *Chánzōng zhèngmài* with his *jiùshì* uncle on a visit to **Wúshānsì** triggered the resolve to leave the world. At 19 *suì* tonsured at **Chánglè Chángxī Fúgōng** 長樂常熙福公 monastery. Studied *jiào* under **Kèróng fǎshī** 克融法師 and Chán under multiple teachers: **Huángbò Zhōng shīshū** 黃檗中師叔 (3 years on *wúzì*), **Gǔshān Wúyì héshàng** 鼓山無異和尚 (changed to *wànfǎ guīyī*), **Huángbò Mìzǔ** 黃檗密祖 (= [[圓悟]] Mìyún Yuánwù, full precepts), **Jīnsù Yuánwù** (long-term, but no transmission), and finally **Pòshān Hǎimíng** with *yìnkě*.

**Six abbacies.** **Lóngpán** 龍蟠; **Niānhuá** 拈華; **Bǎolín** 寶林; **Léngjiā** 楞伽; **Huángbò** 黃檗 (twice); finally at **Yǒuyáng** 酉陽 司主 **Rǎn Yùcén** 冉玉岑's invitation, **Huáyánsì** 華嚴寺. Sat-and-died (*zuòtuō* 坐脫) **順治8 = 1651**.

**Heirs.** **Shígǔ Huì** 石谷慧; **Yúnfù Zhì** [[道智]] 雲腹智 (cf. [[KR6q0480]]); **Yúshān Ruì** 余山瑞.

**Cross-reference.** Trained the young **Liányuè Dàozhèng** [[道正]] (cf. [[KR6q0445]] juan 5 *xíngshí*) c. early 1640s before Liányuè crossed to Pòshān himself.

**Work.** [[KR6q0479]] 《象崖珽禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B308, *chóngkān* 重刊 (re-cut) by *fǎsūn* **Yìwén** [[益聞]]. Sources: [[KR6q0479]] juan 4 *xíngzhuàng* + *tǎmíng*; 《黔南會燈錄》 j. 1; DILA authority A004939.
