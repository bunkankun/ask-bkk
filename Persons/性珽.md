Xìng-tǐng 性珽, *zì* **Xiàng-yái** 象崖. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666); thirty-sixth-generation Línjì 大鑑下第三十六世. Born **1598**; died **大清順治8 辛卯 7月** = **1651-08-16/09-14**, *shì-shòu* **54**, *fǎ-là* **35**. Native of **Fú-qīng** 福清 (Fú-zhōu prefecture, Fú-jiàn); lay surname **Chén** 陳.

**Formation.** Reading the *Chán-zōng zhèng-mài* with his *jiù-shì* uncle on a visit to **Wú-shān-sì** triggered the resolve to leave the world. At 19 *suì* tonsured at **Cháng-lè Cháng-xī Fú-gōng** 長樂常熙福公 monastery. Studied *jiào* under **Kè-róng fǎ-shī** 克融法師 and Chán under multiple teachers: **Huáng-bò Zhōng shī-shū** 黃檗中師叔 (3 years on *wú-zì*), **Gǔ-shān Wú-yì hé-shàng** 鼓山無異和尚 (changed to *wàn-fǎ guī-yī*), **Huáng-bò Mì-zǔ** 黃檗密祖 (= [[圓悟]] Mì-yún Yuán-wù, full precepts), **Jīn-sù Yuán-wù** (long-term, but no transmission), and finally **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** with *yìn-kě*.

**Six abbacies.** **Lóng-pán** 龍蟠; **Niān-huá** 拈華; **Bǎo-lín** 寶林; **Léng-jiā** 楞伽; **Huáng-bò** 黃檗 (twice); finally at **Yǒu-yáng** 酉陽 司主 **Rǎn Yù-cén** 冉玉岑's invitation, **Huá-yán-sì** 華嚴寺. Sat-and-died (*zuò-tuō* 坐脫) **順治8 = 1651**.

**Heirs.** **Shí-gǔ Huì** 石谷慧; **Yún-fù Zhì** [[道智]] 雲腹智 (cf. [[KR6q0480]]); **Yú-shān Ruì** 余山瑞.

**Cross-reference.** Trained the young **Lián-yuè Dào-zhèng** [[道正]] (cf. [[KR6q0445]] juan 5 *xíng-shí*) c. early 1640s before Lián-yuè crossed to Pòshān himself.

**Work.** [[KR6q0479]] 《象崖珽禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B308, *chóng-kān* 重刊 (re-cut) by *fǎ-sūn* **Yì-wén** [[益聞]]. Sources: [[KR6q0479]] juan 4 *xíng-zhuàng* + *tǎ-míng*; 《黔南會燈錄》 j. 1; DILA authority A004939.
