Xú Xuěxiāng 徐雪香 (fl. early 19th c.) was a Sūzhōu fellow-student of [[吳金壽]] Wú Jīnshòu through whom the manuscript of the *Yīxiào mìchuán* ([[KR3eh022]]) reached Wú for the 1831 first printing.
