Gù Yǔxián 顧予咸 (*zì* Xiǎoruǎn 小阮), of *Chángzhōu* 長洲 (Sūzhōu prefecture). *Shùnzhì dīnghài* (1647) *jìnshì*; rose to *Lìbù kǎogōngsī yuánwàiláng* (Vice-Director, Office of Merit Evaluation, Ministry of Personnel). Father of **Gù Sìlì** 顧嗣立 [[顧嗣立]], the celebrated late-*Kāngxī* / early-*Yōngzhèng* literary scholar and editor of the *Yuán shī xuǎn*.

Gù Yǔxián supplemented **Zēng Yì**'s [[曾益]] late-Míng commentary on **Wēn Tíngyún** [[溫庭筠]], adding the layer of notes signed *bǔ* 補 in the present *Wēn Fēiqīng shī jí jiānzhù* (= [[KR4c0078]]). His son Gù Sìlì subsequently re-edited the entire collection, restoring the 7-*juǎn* Sòng-print structure and correcting many of Zēng's errors. The collaborative production — Zēng (Míng base) + Gù Yǔxián (early Qīng supplement) + Gù Sìlì (Kāngxī re-edit) — is one of the more legible examples of how late-imperial *jíbù* commentary projects accumulated intergenerationally.
