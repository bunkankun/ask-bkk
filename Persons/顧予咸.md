Gù Yǔ-xián 顧予咸 (*zì* Xiǎo-ruǎn 小阮), of *Cháng-zhōu* 長洲 (Sū-zhōu prefecture). *Shùn-zhì dīng-hài* (1647) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Lì-bù kǎo-gōng-sī yuán-wài-láng* (Vice-Director, Office of Merit Evaluation, Ministry of Personnel). Father of **Gù Sì-lì** 顧嗣立 [[顧嗣立]], the celebrated late-*Kāng-xī* / early-*Yōng-zhèng* literary scholar and editor of the *Yuán shī xuǎn*.

Gù Yǔ-xián supplemented **Zēng Yì**'s [[曾益]] late-Míng commentary on **Wēn Tíng-yún** [[溫庭筠]], adding the layer of notes signed *bǔ* 補 in the present *Wēn Fēi-qīng shī jí jiān-zhù* (= [[KR4c0078]]). His son Gù Sì-lì subsequently re-edited the entire collection, restoring the 7-*juǎn* Sòng-print structure and correcting many of Zēng's errors. The collaborative production — Zēng (Míng base) + Gù Yǔ-xián (early Qīng supplement) + Gù Sì-lì (Kāng-xī re-edit) — is one of the more legible examples of how late-imperial *jí-bù* commentary projects accumulated intergenerationally.
