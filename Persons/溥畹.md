Mid-Qīng Buddhist scholar-monk, *zì* **Lángǔ** 蘭谷 ("Orchid-Valley"), commonly **Lángǔ Pǔwǎn** 蘭谷溥畹. Native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu prefecture). Lifedates unrecorded. Per his title-line *DàQīng qīncì Yúnnán Fǎjièsì jiǎngjīng Guǎnglíng shāmén Pǔwǎn shù* 大清欽賜雲南法界寺講經廣陵沙門溥畹述 ("Of the Great Qīng, by imperial bestowal Sūtra-Lecturer at Fǎjièsì in Yúnnán, monk of Guǎnglíng [Yángzhōu]"), Pǔwǎn was an **imperially-appointed sūtra-lecturer at the Fǎjièsì** 法界寺 in **Yúnnán**, originating from Yángzhōu — a relatively unusual long-distance imperial monastic posting. Author of *Jīngāng jīng xīnyìn shū* 金剛經心印疏 ([[KR6c0093]], X25 no. 505), a two-juan annotated commentary opened by a hierarchical *kē* outline.
