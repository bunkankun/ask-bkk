Mid-Qīng Buddhist scholar-monk, *zì* **Lán-gǔ** 蘭谷 ("Orchid-Valley"), commonly **Lán-gǔ Pǔ-wǎn** 蘭谷溥畹. Native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu prefecture). Lifedates unrecorded. Per his title-line *Dà-Qīng qīn-cì Yún-nán Fǎ-jiè-sì jiǎng-jīng Guǎng-líng shā-mén Pǔ-wǎn shù* 大清欽賜雲南法界寺講經廣陵沙門溥畹述 ("Of the Great Qīng, by imperial bestowal Sūtra-Lecturer at Fǎ-jiè-sì in Yún-nán, monk of Guǎng-líng [Yáng-zhōu]"), Pǔ-wǎn was an **imperially-appointed sūtra-lecturer at the Fǎ-jiè-sì** 法界寺 in **Yún-nán**, originating from Yáng-zhōu — a relatively unusual long-distance imperial monastic posting. Author of *Jīn-gāng jīng xīn-yìn shū* 金剛經心印疏 ([[KR6c0093]], X25 no. 505), a two-juan annotated commentary opened by a hierarchical *kē* outline.
