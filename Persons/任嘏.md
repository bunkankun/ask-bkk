Rén Gǔ 任嘏 (*fl.* mid 3rd century), *zì* Zhāoxiān 昭先, was a Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 scholar-official, a native of Lè-ān 樂安 (in modern Shān-dōng). He is mentioned in *Sānguó zhì* 三國志 commentary (Péi Sōngzhī's *zhù* to *Wèi shū*) as one of a circle of Wèi-period worthies and was reputedly precocious as a young man. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records his *Dàolùn* 道論 in 10 *juàn* (some manuscript variants 8). His exact birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally placed in the mid-3rd century.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0134]] *Rénzǐ dàolùn* 任子道論. CBDB has no entry.
