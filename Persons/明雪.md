# Míngxuě 明雪 (Rùjiù Míngxuě 入就明雪 / Ruìbái chánshī 瑞白禪師)

Late-Wàn-lì / Chóng-zhēn-era Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, **32nd-generation Cáodòng** descendant from **Dòngshān Liángjiè** 洞山良价 in the sub-line **Cízhōu Fāngniàn** 慈舟方念 → **Huànxiū Chángrùn** 幻休常潤 → **Zhànrán Yuánchéng** 湛然圓澄 (1561–1626) → Míngxuě. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Míngxuě** 明雪, *zì* **Ruìbái** 瑞白, *biéhào* 別號 **Rùjiù** 入就. Lay surname **Yáng** 楊, native of **Tóngchéng** 桐城 / Zōngyáng 樅陽 (Ānhuī). Father **Yáng Yījǔ** 楊一舉; orphaned of his father in childhood. Lifedates **Wànlì 12.11.26 戌時 – Chóngzhēn 14.3.19 午時** = **~5 January 1585 – 29 April 1641**, age **58 *suì*** / **38 *sēnglà*** per both *xíngzhuàng* in [[KR6q0410]] juan 18.

Tonsured at **Jiǔhuáshān Jùlóngān** 九華山聚龍庵 by **Huìgōng** 慧公. Early instruction from **Zǐbó Zhēnkě** 紫柏真可 disciples (the *Píshèfú Fó jì* 毘舍浮佛偈 practice). Full precepts (c. 1610) from **Yúnqī Zhūhóng** 雲棲袾宏 — placing Míngxuě in the late-Wàn-lì Yúnqī ordination-line shared with Fúshí Tōngxián [[通賢]] at [[KR6q0407]]. First Chán training at **Yúnmén Xiǎnshèngsì** 雲門顯聖寺 under **Zhànrán Yuánchéng** (from Gēngxū 庚戌 = 1610); sealed by Zhàn-ran on the quatrain "*méng shī ráoshé shí yú nián ... jīnwū yèbàn lì zhōngtiān* 蒙師饒舌十餘年 ... 金烏夜半麗中天" in Bǐngyín 丙寅 = **1626**, just before Zhàn-ran's death.

**Historically significant 1625 refusal of Mìyún Línjì-line sealing.** At Jīnsù 金粟 in *Yǐchǒu* 乙丑 winter 1625, Mìyún Yuánwù attempted to seal Míngxuě as a Línjì-line dharma-heir; Míngxuě refused on principled Cáo-dòng-boundary grounds ("*sì sù zé gū Yúnmén yě* 嗣粟則孤雲門也" — "to succeed Jīnsù would betray Yúnmén"). This is a rare documented cross-lineage transmission-refusal and is one of the key boundary-maintenance events in the late-Wàn-lì Cáodòng–Línjì negotiation. See [[KR6q0410]] *tǎmíng* and Jiang Wu 2008: 104–06.

**Nine abbacies** ("*jiǔ zuò dàochǎng* 九坐道場") between 1628 and 1641: Yuèzhōu Xiǎnshèng 越州顯聖 (first, 1628, succeeding Zhàn-ran); Yuèzhōu Yánqìng 延慶; Shàoxīng Jièzhū 戒珠 (1633); **Húzhōu Biànshān Lónghuásì** 湖州弁山龍華寺 (central abbacy, 1630 onwards, built up from hermitage to *dàchà*); Húzhōu Báiquè 白雀; **Tiāntāi Hùguósì** 天台護國寺 (1635, passed to [[寂蘊|Xiàoyún Jìyùn]] in 1635 autumn); Gànzhōu Kōngdòng 贛州崆峒 (1636–39); Kōngdòng Huìdēng 慧燈; **Nánchāng Bǎizhàngshān** 南昌百丈山 (final seat, 1640, petitioned by the **Prince of Jiànān** 建安王 *Qīn* 欽). Died at Bǎizhàng 1641; body transferred to Biànshān Lónghuá per pre-death instruction; hair-relic stupa (*fǎtǎ* 髮塔) built at Bǎizhàng.

Over **thirty dharma-heirs** in the Cáodòng 寂-generation (圓 → 明 → 寂). Principal heirs: **Jiǔmò Jìyīn** 久默寂音 (died shortly after the master), **Xiàoyún Jìyùn** [[寂蘊]] 嘯雲寂蘊 (head heir, compiler of the *yǔlù*), **Gūyá Jìcōng** 孤崖寂聰, **Líyán Jìyì** 離言寂義, **Lìrán Jìxiāng** 歷然寂相.

Works: [[KR6q0410]] 《入就瑞白禪師語錄》 (18 juan, Jiāxīng J26 B188, compiled by Xiàoyún Jìyùn 1649). Independent polemical works *Pìpàn* 闢判 and *Shī-zi hǒu* 獅子吼 are mentioned in the *xíngzhuàng* but were **omitted by Xiàoyún from the 1649 integrated cutting** on ecumenical-reconciliation grounds; the *Dámó zhèngzōng shuō* 達磨正宗說 reportedly survives separately in the Jiāxīng Canon J31 / J32.

Lay-patron network: **Chén Hánhuī** 陳函煇 (小寒山, the 1646 martyr); **Huáng Duānbó** 黃端伯 (1585?–1646, Jiāngyīn martyr); **Zhāng Shíhuà** 張時化; **Wú Yìngfāng** 吳應芳; **Yú Dàchéng** 余大成 (Ming-Qing 雙官 official, wrote the *tǎmíng*); **Táng Shìjì** 唐世濟 (1575–1646, wrote the *zhuàn*); the **Prince of Jiànān** 建安王 *Qīn* 欽 (imperial-grade Bǎizhàng patron).
