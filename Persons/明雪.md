# Míng-xuě 明雪 (Rù-jiù Míng-xuě 入就明雪 / Ruì-bái chán-shī 瑞白禪師)

Late-Wàn-lì / Chóng-zhēn-era Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, **32nd-generation Cáo-dòng** descendant from **Dòng-shān Liáng-jiè** 洞山良价 in the sub-line **Cí-zhōu Fāng-niàn** 慈舟方念 → **Huàn-xiū Cháng-rùn** 幻休常潤 → **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄 (1561–1626) → Míng-xuě. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Míng-xuě** 明雪, *zì* **Ruì-bái** 瑞白, *bié-hào* 別號 **Rù-jiù** 入就. Lay surname **Yáng** 楊, native of **Tóng-chéng** 桐城 / Zōng-yáng 樅陽 (Ān-huī). Father **Yáng Yī-jǔ** 楊一舉; orphaned of his father in childhood. Lifedates **Wàn-lì 12.11.26 戌時 – Chóng-zhēn 14.3.19 午時** = **~5 January 1585 – 29 April 1641**, age **58 *suì*** / **38 *sēng-là*** per both *xíng-zhuàng* in [[KR6q0410]] juan 18.

Tonsured at **Jiǔ-huá-shān Jù-lóng-ān** 九華山聚龍庵 by **Huì-gōng** 慧公. Early instruction from **Zǐ-bó Zhēn-kě** 紫柏真可 disciples (the *Pí-shè-fú Fó jì* 毘舍浮佛偈 practice). Full precepts (c. 1610) from **Yún-qī Zhū-hóng** 雲棲袾宏 — placing Míng-xuě in the late-Wàn-lì Yún-qī ordination-line shared with Fú-shí Tōng-xián [[通賢]] at [[KR6q0407]]. First Chán training at **Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng-sì** 雲門顯聖寺 under **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** (from Gēng-xū 庚戌 = 1610); sealed by Zhàn-ran on the quatrain "*méng shī ráo-shé shí yú nián ... jīn-wū yè-bàn lì zhōng-tiān* 蒙師饒舌十餘年 ... 金烏夜半麗中天" in Bǐng-yín 丙寅 = **1626**, just before Zhàn-ran's death.

**Historically significant 1625 refusal of Mìyún Línjì-line sealing.** At Jīn-sù 金粟 in *Yǐ-chǒu* 乙丑 winter 1625, Mìyún Yuán-wù attempted to seal Míng-xuě as a Línjì-line dharma-heir; Míng-xuě refused on principled Cáo-dòng-boundary grounds ("*sì sù zé gū Yún-mén yě* 嗣粟則孤雲門也" — "to succeed Jīn-sù would betray Yún-mén"). This is a rare documented cross-lineage transmission-refusal and is one of the key boundary-maintenance events in the late-Wàn-lì Cáo-dòng–Línjì negotiation. See [[KR6q0410]] *tǎ-míng* and Jiang Wu 2008: 104–06.

**Nine abbacies** ("*jiǔ zuò dào-chǎng* 九坐道場") between 1628 and 1641: Yuè-zhōu Xiǎn-shèng 越州顯聖 (first, 1628, succeeding Zhàn-ran); Yuè-zhōu Yán-qìng 延慶; Shào-xīng Jiè-zhū 戒珠 (1633); **Hú-zhōu Biàn-shān Lóng-huá-sì** 湖州弁山龍華寺 (central abbacy, 1630 onwards, built up from hermitage to *dà-chà*); Hú-zhōu Bái-què 白雀; **Tiān-tāi Hù-guó-sì** 天台護國寺 (1635, passed to [[寂蘊|Xiào-yún Jì-yùn]] in 1635 autumn); Gàn-zhōu Kōng-dòng 贛州崆峒 (1636–39); Kōng-dòng Huì-dēng 慧燈; **Nán-chāng Bǎi-zhàng-shān** 南昌百丈山 (final seat, 1640, petitioned by the **Prince of Jiàn-ān** 建安王 *Qīn* 欽). Died at Bǎi-zhàng 1641; body transferred to Biàn-shān Lóng-huá per pre-death instruction; hair-relic stupa (*fǎ-tǎ* 髮塔) built at Bǎi-zhàng.

Over **thirty dharma-heirs** in the Cáo-dòng 寂-generation (圓 → 明 → 寂). Principal heirs: **Jiǔ-mò Jì-yīn** 久默寂音 (died shortly after the master), **Xiào-yún Jì-yùn** [[寂蘊]] 嘯雲寂蘊 (head heir, compiler of the *yǔlù*), **Gū-yá Jì-cōng** 孤崖寂聰, **Lí-yán Jì-yì** 離言寂義, **Lì-rán Jì-xiāng** 歷然寂相.

Works: [[KR6q0410]] 《入就瑞白禪師語錄》 (18 juan, Jiā-xīng J26 B188, compiled by Xiào-yún Jì-yùn 1649). Independent polemical works *Pì-pàn* 闢判 and *Shī-zi hǒu* 獅子吼 are mentioned in the *xíng-zhuàng* but were **omitted by Xiào-yún from the 1649 integrated cutting** on ecumenical-reconciliation grounds; the *Dá-mó zhèng-zōng shuō* 達磨正宗說 reportedly survives separately in the Jiā-xīng Canon J31 / J32.

Lay-patron network: **Chén Hán-huī** 陳函煇 (小寒山, the 1646 martyr); **Huáng Duān-bó** 黃端伯 (1585?–1646, Jiāng-yīn martyr); **Zhāng Shí-huà** 張時化; **Wú Yìng-fāng** 吳應芳; **Yú Dà-chéng** 余大成 (Ming-Qing 雙官 official, wrote the *tǎ-míng*); **Táng Shì-jì** 唐世濟 (1575–1646, wrote the *zhuàn*); the **Prince of Jiàn-ān** 建安王 *Qīn* 欽 (imperial-grade Bǎi-zhàng patron).
