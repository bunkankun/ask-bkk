Lǚ Péi 呂培 (1776–1824), Cháng-zhōu disciple of [[洪亮吉]] Hóng Liàng-jí. The principal compiler (with several other Hóng-school disciples) of the *Hóng Běi-jiāng xiān-shēng nián-pǔ* 洪北江先生年譜 (chronological biography of Hóng Liàng-jí) prefixed to the SBCK *Hóng Běi-jiāng shī-wén-jí* [[KR4f0056]]. The *nián-pǔ* was completed in the early Dào-guāng years (after Hóng's death in 1809 and before Lǚ's own death in 1824) and is the principal documentary basis for modern reconstructions of Hóng's career, including the 1799 sealed-memorial incident and the Yī-lí banishment.
