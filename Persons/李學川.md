**Lǐ Xuéchuān** 李學川 (*zì* Sānyuán 三源, *hào* Línchéng 鄰澄, fl. early 19th c., 清), Qīng-period Sūzhōu (Wú-xiàn 吳縣) acupuncturist of the Jiā-qìng / Dào-guāng era. Author of the *Zhēnjiǔ féng-yuán* 針灸逢源 ([[KR3ee030]], 1815), one of the principal late-Qīng acupuncture compendia. Lǐ's editorial ambition was to restore the *Sùwèn-Língshū-Nànjīng* root-doctrine that he saw as having decayed in the Yīzōng jīnjiàn-dominated Qīng curriculum. No CBDB match.
