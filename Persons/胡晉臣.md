# Hú Jìnchén 胡晉臣

Southern-Sòng court official. Served as Censor (*Shìyùshǐ* 侍御史) under Xiàozōng. In 1188 (Chúnxī 15) sided with [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī in the celebrated court dispute against [[林栗]] Lín Lì (see *Zhōuyì jīngzhuàn jí jiě* 周易經傳集解, [[KR1a0028]]) and submitted an impeachment of Lín jointly with the *Tàicháng bóshì* [[葉適]] Yè Shì; Lín was demoted to prefect of Quánzhōu as a result. Otherwise sparsely documented in the *Sòngshǐ* record.
