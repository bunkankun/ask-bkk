**Lǐ Jiōng** 李駉 was a Southern-Sòng medical scholar, author of [[KR5d0047]] *Huáng dì bā shí yī Nán jīng zuǎn tú jù jiě* 黃帝八十一難經纂圖句解 (preface 1269) — a diagrammed exegesis of the ***Nán jīng*** 難經 ("Classic of Difficulties"), the companion-classic to the *Huáng dì nèi jīng*. (Multiple CBDB persons share this name; the relevant entry is from the medical literature.)
