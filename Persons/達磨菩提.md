Dharmabodhi (Dá-mó-pútí 達磨菩提, "Dharma-Awakening") was a Northern-Wèi-period Indian translator. Per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí*, translator of *[[KR6g0051|Niè pán lùn 涅槃論]]* (T1527, the *Nirvāṇa-śāstra* attributed to Vasubandhu). Birth and death dates not preserved.
