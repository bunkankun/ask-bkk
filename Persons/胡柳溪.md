# Hú Liǔxī 胡柳溪

Mid-Míng Confucian scholar of Luótián 羅田 (modern Húběi); teacher of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (1499–1582) in classical studies during the latter's early training. Lifedates unrecorded.
