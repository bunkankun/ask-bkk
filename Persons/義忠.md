# Yìzhōng 義忠

Mid-Tang Cí'ēn-school (法相 Hossō / Yogācāra) scholar-monk, *fl.* eighth century. Best known as the author of the [[KR6n0099]] *Dà-shèng bǎi-fǎ míng-mén lùn shū* 大乘百法明門論疏 (1368 in the *Southern Hongwu Edition of the Canon*) — a Cí'ēn commentary on Vasubandhu's hundred-dharma catalog [[KR6n0096]] (T31n1614), prefaced by [[窺基]]'s own preface. The work is preserved only in the Hongwu canon (the principal Ming southern canon, 洪武南藏); it does not appear in the Taishō. Lifedates not preserved in the catalogs.

Yìzhōng's *Bǎi-fǎ míng-mén lùn shū* circulated in parallel with [[大乘光]]'s [[KR6n0098]] commentary; the two together constitute the principal Tang Cí'ēn-school commentaries on Vasubandhu's hundred-dharma catalog. Beyond this work, biographical details are not preserved in the standard Buddhist biographical sources (the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* T2061 has no entry); the work itself is the principal evidence for his existence as a Cí'ēn-school scholar.

The dating of his floruit (c. 700–780) is inferred from the work's inclusion of [[窺基]]'s preface (which assumes Kuījī's authority is established) and from the standard mid-Tang Cí'ēn commentarial style of the work itself.
