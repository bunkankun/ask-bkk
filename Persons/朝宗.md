# Cháozōng 朝宗

Late-Míng Chán master, an early teacher of [[如一|Jífēi Rúyī]] 即非如一 (1616–1671) before Jífēi received final dharma-transmission from [[隱元隆琦|Yǐnyuán Lóngqí]]. Lifedates and further biographical details unrecorded.
