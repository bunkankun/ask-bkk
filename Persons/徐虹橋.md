**Xú Hóngqiáo** 徐虹橋, late-*Qiánlóng* / early-*Jiāqìng* physician. Son-in-law of [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng; inherited and added supplementary annotations to Shěn's manuscript *Nǚkē jíyào* ([[KR3ei010]]). Father-in-law of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng (Mèngyīng), through whom the work passed into the 1850 printed edition.
