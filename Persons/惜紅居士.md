**Xīhóng Jūshì** 惜紅居士 ("Gentleman Who Cherishes the Red") is the pseudonym of the unidentified author of the late-Qīng *gōng'àn* novel *Lǐ Gōng'àn Qíwén* 李公案奇聞 [[KR4k0173]]. No biographical details have been identified; the pen name is a literary sobriquet of the type common among late-Qīng popular fiction writers who preferred not to use their real names for works in the vernacular fiction genre. The name suggests a sentimental attachment to the feminine world (*hóng* 紅 conventionally evoking women, romance, or the world of the inner chambers), somewhat at odds with the detective-fiction content of the novel.
