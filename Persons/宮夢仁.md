Gōng Mèngrén 宮夢仁 (b. 1642 per CBDB id 65059; the *Sìkù* catalog meta records "b. 1632", which is preferred against — Wilkinson §62.3.8 gives his *jìnshì* as 1673, consistent with the CBDB 1642 birth-year), *zì* Dìngshān 定山, native of Tàizhōu 泰州 (Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 12 (1673), advanced through provincial appointments to *xúnfǔ Fújiàn* (Fújiàn governor) before retiring to Tàizhōu. His principal surviving work is the *Dúshū jì shù lüè* 讀書紀數略 ([[KR3k0062]]) — the 54-*juǎn* Qīng *lèishū* of numbered-set knowledge — which he presented in person to the Kāngxī emperor during the emperor's southern progress in 1707 and which received imperial printing-block patronage.
