Huáng Xùn 黃訓 (fl. mid-sixteenth c.), zì *Xué-gǔ* 學古, hào *Shòu-sōng* 瘦松, was a Míng official from Shè-xiàn 歙縣 (Huī-zhōu, modern Ān-huī). He passed the *jìn-shì* in *Jiā-jìng jǐ-chǒu* 嘉靖己丑 (1529) and rose to *Fù dū yù-shǐ* 副都御史 — the *Sì-kù tíyào* identifies him simply as "*Shè-xiàn rén, Jiā-jìng jǐ-chǒu jìn-shì, guān zhì Fù dū yù-shǐ*."

His one surviving major work is the [[KR2f0040]] *Míng-chén jīng-jì lù* 名臣經濟錄 in 53 *juàn*, an anthology of statecraft writings (memorials and related prose) from nine Míng reigns: Hóng-wǔ, Yǒng-lè, Hóng-xī, Xuān-dé, Zhèng-tǒng, Jǐng-tài, Tiān-shùn, Chéng-huà, Hóng-zhì, Zhèng-dé, Jiā-jìng (the *Jiàn-wén* reign deliberately omitted *yǐ gé chú huì zhī yě*, "to avoid mention of the deletion-of-reigns" — i.e. the *Yǒng-lè* successor's purging of *Jiàn-wén*-era references). The work is divided into ten administrative-bureau divisions: *Kāi-guó* 開國, *Bǎo-zhì* 保治, *Nèi-gé* 內閣, *Lì-bù*, *Hù-bù*, *Lǐ-bù*, *Bīng-bù*, *Xíng-bù*, *Gōng-bù*, and a joint *Dū-chá-yuàn* + *Tōng-zhèng-sī* + *Dà-lǐ-sì* division. Within each, sub-categories follow the actual sub-bureau structure of the Míng administrative apparatus — a remarkably clear administrative-historical organization.

CBDB has multiple 黃訓 entries (id 48596, 87949, 135081, 321832, 362453, 480049) but none with a confident match for the Shè-xiàn / *Jiā-jìng* 1529 *jìn-shì* — likely entry 48596 (Sòng dynasty, no dates) is wrong. The Shè-xiàn 黃訓 lifedates are not preserved in CBDB; he must be distinguished from a Qīng-period 黃訓 (CBDB id 87949, *d.* 1773).
