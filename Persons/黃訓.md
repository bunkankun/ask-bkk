Huáng Xùn 黃訓 (fl. mid-sixteenth c.), zì *Xuégǔ* 學古, hào *Shòusōng* 瘦松, was a Míng official from Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Huīzhōu, modern Ānhuī). He passed the *jìnshì* in *Jiājìng jǐchǒu* 嘉靖己丑 (1529) and rose to *Fù dū yùshǐ* 副都御史 — the *Sìkù tíyào* identifies him simply as "*Shèxiàn rén, Jiājìng jǐchǒu jìnshì, guān zhì Fù dū yùshǐ*."

His one surviving major work is the [[KR2f0040]] *Míngchén jīngjì lù* 名臣經濟錄 in 53 *juàn*, an anthology of statecraft writings (memorials and related prose) from nine Míng reigns: Hóngwǔ, Yǒnglè, Hóngxī, Xuāndé, Zhèngtǒng, Jǐngtài, Tiānshùn, Chénghuà, Hóngzhì, Zhèngdé, Jiājìng (the *Jiànwén* reign deliberately omitted *yǐ gé chú huì zhī yě*, "to avoid mention of the deletion-of-reigns" — i.e. the *Yǒnglè* successor's purging of *Jiànwén*-era references). The work is divided into ten administrative-bureau divisions: *Kāiguó* 開國, *Bǎozhì* 保治, *Nèigé* 內閣, *Lìbù*, *Hùbù*, *Lǐbù*, *Bīngbù*, *Xíngbù*, *Gōngbù*, and a joint *Dūcháyuàn* + *Tōngzhèngsī* + *Dàlǐsì* division. Within each, sub-categories follow the actual sub-bureau structure of the Míng administrative apparatus — a remarkably clear administrative-historical organization.

CBDB has multiple 黃訓 entries (id 48596, 87949, 135081, 321832, 362453, 480049) but none with a confident match for the Shèxiàn / *Jiājìng* 1529 *jìnshì* — likely entry 48596 (Sòng dynasty, no dates) is wrong. The Shèxiàn 黃訓 lifedates are not preserved in CBDB; he must be distinguished from a Qīng-period 黃訓 (CBDB id 87949, *d.* 1773).
