Modern Japanese Buddhist studies scholar (西本 照真), specialist in *Sānjiē-jiào* 三階教 (Three Stages Teaching). Author of the major monograph *Sangaikyō no kenkyū* 三階教の研究 (Tokyo: Shunjūsha, 1998) — the principal post-Yabuki study, revising and extending Yabuki Keiki 矢吹慶輝's 1927 foundational work. Edited the *Fóxìng guān xiūshàn fǎ* 佛性觀修善法 [[KR6v0092]] and the survey article *Sān-jiē-jiào wénxiàn zōng-shù* 三階教文獻綜述 [[KR6v0103]] for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 9, drawing on unpublished Dūnhuáng witnesses (*Běi-dūn* 07879 and 14802) that allow nearly complete reconstruction of this important Sānjiē text.
