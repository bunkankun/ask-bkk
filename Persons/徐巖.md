# 徐巖 Xú Yán

**Xú Yán** 徐巖 (late Yuán – early Míng), Jiāngxī 江西 acupuncturist; the teacher of [[徐鳳]] Xú Fèng (compiler of the *Zhēnjiǔ dàquán* 針灸大全, [[KR3ee002]], 1439). No independent biographical record survives; he is known only through Xú Fèng's preface.
