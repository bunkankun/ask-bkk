Contemporary Chinese specialist of modern Chinese Buddhist intellectual history, Yogācāra studies, and the early-twentieth-century *Wéishí* revival. Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of Hán Qīngjìng's posthumously discovered *Yújiā shī dì lùn pī xún jì xù* ([[KR6v0029]]) — the only autobiographical statement of the Northern-Chinese leader of the modern *Wéishí* movement.
