# Pǔyuàn 普願

Nánquán Pǔyuàn 南泉普願 (748–835), mid-Táng Chán master of the Hóngzhōu line, dharma-heir of Mǎzǔ Dàoyī 馬祖道一; one of the masters under whom Dòngshān Liángjiè studied during his pilgrimage years, as recorded in the *Dòngshān yǔlù* ([[KR6q0067]]).
