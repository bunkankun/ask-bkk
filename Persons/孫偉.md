**Sūn Wěi** 孫偉 (*fl.* late Kāng-xī era, ca. 1670–1720), Qīng clinical-pharmacology compiler. Compiler of the *Liángpéng huìjí jīngyàn shénfāng* 良朋彙集經驗神方 ([[KR3ed147]]) in 6 *juǎn*, with a distinctive opening section on **obstetric emergencies** (*Jí-jiù xiān-fāng* 急救仙方) prefaced by Xú Shǒu-zhēn 徐守真 of Jīn-chuān 金川. The work's editorial principle is the avoidance of *cǒng / pì / guì* (cumbersome / rare / expensive) ingredients in favour of formulas accessible to the rural poor — especially for the high-priority obstetric-emergency cases. Sparse further biographical record.
