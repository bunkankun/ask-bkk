Jīyún 機雲, *hào* **Shíyuán** 石源 ("Stone Source"); also **Shímén Jīyún** 石門機雲 / **Qíān Jīyún** 齊安機雲. Línjì-Yángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Shānduó Zhēnzài** [[真在]] (1621–1672). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Máchéng** 麻城 (Húběi); lay surname **Zhōu** 周. Self-identified as a 16th-generation descendant in the **Chǔ-Huáng Dàshíshān** 楚黃大石山 line.

**Works.**
- Co-recorder (*děng lù*) of his teacher's [[KR6q0534]] 《山鐸真在禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B414, jointly with co-disciple **Huìyuè Zhìxù** [[智旭]].
- *Xù* (continuator / co-compiler) of his teacher's 《徑石滴乳集》 5 juan, X67 n1308 [[KR6q0255]] — explicitly signed "清嗣祖沙門江州真在編 嗣法門人齊安機雲續".
- The Kāngxī 1692 editor (法孫) of [[KR6q0328]] 《方山文寶禪師語錄》 — the same person, his lineage placement matches.
