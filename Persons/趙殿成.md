# Zhào Diànchéng 趙殿成

*Zì* Sōng-gǔ 松谷. Native of *Rén-hé* 仁和 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Qīng *Yōng-zhèng* / *Qián-lóng*-period scholar; floruit *ca.* 1720–1745. His one major scholarly work is the *Wáng Yòu-chéng jí jiān-zhù* 王右丞集箋注 [[KR4c0022]] — the standard pre-modern critical and annotated edition of Wáng Wéi [[王維]] in 28 *juǎn*, begun in *Yōng-zhèng* 6 (1728) and completed in *Qián-lóng* 1 (1736). The work superseded the Míng-period Gù Qǐ-jīng 顧起經 *Wáng Yòu-chéng jí fēn-lèi zhù* and remains the foundational modern reference for Wáng Wéi.

A notable feature of the project is Zhào's collaboration with Wáng Qí 王琦 [[王琦]] (the same Qīng scholar who annotated Lǐ Bái) on the dense Buddhist allusions in Wáng Wéi's verse — Wáng Qí supplying *Tripiṭaka* references that Zhào himself was not equipped to handle. The Sìkù tíyào credits this collaboration as one of the substantive improvements over the older Gù annotation. CBDB has no entry.
