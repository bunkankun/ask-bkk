# Zhào Diànchéng 趙殿成

*Zì* Sōnggǔ 松谷. Native of *Rénhé* 仁和 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Qīng *Yōngzhèng* / *Qiánlóng*-period scholar; floruit *ca.* 1720–1745. His one major scholarly work is the *Wáng Yòuchéng jí jiānzhù* 王右丞集箋注 [[KR4c0022]] — the standard pre-modern critical and annotated edition of Wáng Wéi [[王維]] in 28 *juǎn*, begun in *Yōngzhèng* 6 (1728) and completed in *Qiánlóng* 1 (1736). The work superseded the Míng-period Gù Qǐjīng 顧起經 *Wáng Yòuchéng jí fēnlèi zhù* and remains the foundational modern reference for Wáng Wéi.

A notable feature of the project is Zhào's collaboration with Wáng Qí 王琦 [[王琦]] (the same Qīng scholar who annotated Lǐ Bái) on the dense Buddhist allusions in Wáng Wéi's verse — Wáng Qí supplying *Tripiṭaka* references that Zhào himself was not equipped to handle. The Sìkù tíyào credits this collaboration as one of the substantive improvements over the older Gù annotation. CBDB has no entry.
