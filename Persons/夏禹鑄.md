# Xià Yǔzhù 夏禹鑄

Early-Qīng pediatric physician; author of the pediatric *tuīná* monograph *Yòukē tiějìng* 幼科鐵鏡 ([[KR3ee042]]), one of the three principal Qīng pediatric-massage handbooks named by [[塗蔚生]] Tú Wèishēng in the preface to [[KR3ee053]] as authoritative-but-insufficient predecessors of his own work.
