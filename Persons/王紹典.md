**Wáng Shàodiǎn** 王紹典 (*fl.* 1791), Hángzhōu (*Wǔlín* 武林) literatus and friend of [[樓擁千]] Lóu Yōngqiān. Author of the 1791 (*Qiánlóng xīnhài* 乾隆辛亥) preface to Lóu's *Yòukē jīngchù mén* 幼科驚搐門 ([[KR3ej061]]). The preface frames the work biographically, describing how the two had been long-separated by social network until winter 1790 brought them together at Hangzhou and Lóu produced the manuscript for publication.
