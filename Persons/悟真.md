# Wùzhēn 悟真

Late-eighth / early-ninth-century Chán monk recorded in the closing transmission-notice of the Dūnhuáng recension of the *Tánjīng* as residing at the Fǎxìng sì 法興寺 on Cáoxīshān in Lǐngnán and transmitting the text; fourth in the line Huìnéng → Fǎhǎi → Dàojì → Wùzhēn.
