# Shī-zhào 師肇

Southern-Sòng Chán disciple (*cānxué* 參學) of [[崇岳]] Sōngyuán Chóngyuè (1132–1202); co-recorder of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0311]]). Known only from the 參學 byline; no independent biographical record survives (DILA A000917).
