**Lǐ Jùn** 李濬 (fl. late-*Xián-tōng* — *Guāng-huà*, c. 877–900). Late-Táng author of the [[KR3l0013]] *Sōng-chuāng záilù* 松窗雜錄. Native place and official career are not securely transmitted; from the preface he came from a senior official family and "from childhood mingled among ministers." The *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén zhì* attribution to Lǐ Jùn is followed here; the *Wén-xiàn tōng-kǎo*'s "Wéi Jùn" is generally taken as a transmission error of 李 → 韋. CBDB id 93129 records *c_fl_latest_year* 877. CBDB lists multiple unrelated persons named 李濬 (notably the Sòng figure id 41113, dated 965–1013, who is *not* this author).
