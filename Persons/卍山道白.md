# Manzan Dōhaku 卍山道白

Japanese (Edo-period) Sōtō Zen reformer (1636–1715) associated with the *menju shihō* 面授嗣法 dispute and central to the seventeenth- and early-eighteenth-century editorial recovery of the Tiāntóng Rújìng *yǔlù* corpus; collated and printed the main *Rújìng héshàng yǔlù* in 1680 and the supplementary *Tiāntóng Rújìng chánshī xù yǔlù* in 1715.
