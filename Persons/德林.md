Délín 德林. Personal attendant (*shìzhě*) of **Wànfēng Tóngzhēn Zhìshàn** [[至善]] (lifedates unknown, active 1654–1678+) at **Xiàogǎn Jiǎntáng** 孝感簡堂 and **Gāofēng** 高峰 *chányuàn* (Húběi). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*jì*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0556]] 《萬峰童真禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B441.
