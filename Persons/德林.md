Dé-lín 德林. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě*) of **Wàn-fēng Tóng-zhēn Zhì-shàn** [[至善]] (lifedates unknown, active 1654–1678+) at **Xiào-gǎn Jiǎn-táng** 孝感簡堂 and **Gāo-fēng** 高峰 *chán-yuàn* (Hú-běi). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*jì*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0556]] 《萬峰童真禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B441.
