Yěfù Dàochuān 冶父道川 (lay surname Dí 狄; *fl.* 1127) was a Línjì-Yángqí school Chán master active at Yěfùshān 冶父山 in the Liǎngzhè region. He is the author of the celebrated [[KR6c0049|*Jīngāng jīng zhù* (X461)]], a *zhuóyǔ* 著語 alternating verse-and-prose commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra* — one of the most popular Chán-school commentaries on the *Diamond Sūtra* in the late-imperial and modern liturgical traditions. The preface dates the work to Jiànyán 1 = 1127. He is sometimes also referred to as Shí'ān Dàochuān 實菴道川.
