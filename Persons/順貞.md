# 順貞

Shùnzhēn. Táng-period Chinese monk who collaborated with **Buddhapāli** [[佛陀波利]] on the 683 Cháng'ān translation of the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra* ([[KR6j0144]], T19n0967). No further biographical material is preserved.
