Xuán-zǒng 玄總 (尼 *bhikṣuṇī* nun), *zì* **Bǎo-chí** 寶持. **Female Línjì-Yángqí (Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng line) dharma-heir of [[弘儲]] Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** (1605–1672) — making her thirty-third-generation Línjì. Lay name **Jīn Shū-xiū** 金淑脩, lay surname **Jīn** 金. Originally *zhǒng-xí* 冢媳 (eldest daughter-in-law) of the **Suí-zhōu / Suí-zhōu Tài-pú** 隋州太僕 official family; after her husband's death, she left the household and pursued intensive Chán practice under Hóng-chǔ at Líng-yán-sì, with breakthrough and formal *fù-fǎ* — one of relatively few documented women in the Sān-fēng line to receive formal transmission.

**Abbacies.** **Jiā-hé Mìao-zhàn** 嘉禾妙湛 (Jiā-xīng); **Yán-guān Nán-xún** 鹽官南詢 / **Hǎi-yán Nán-xún** 海鹽南詢. Continued under the broader Líng-yán Sān-fēng affiliation.
