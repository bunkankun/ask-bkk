Mid-Yuán literatus and anthologist. *Zì* **Bó-qiān** 伯謙. Native of **Xiāngchéng** 襄城 (Hé-nán). Life-dates not securely established (active c. 1310s–1350s; active in literary networks around Yú Jí 虞集 and contemporaries). His sole transmitted compilation is the **[[KR4h0083]]** *Táng-yīn* 唐音 (14 *juǎn*, completed Zhì-zhèng 4 = 1344, prefaced by [[虞集]] Yú Jí), the principal Yuán-period anthology of Táng poetry. The work occupied ten years of compilatory labour (per the SKQS editors); its three-fold division *Shǐ-yīn / Zhèng-yīn / Yí-xiǎng* and its deliberate exclusion of Lǐ Bái, Dù Fǔ, and Hán Yù became the methodological template for Gāo Bǐng's **[[KR4h0095]]** *Táng-shī pǐn-huì* (1393). His other works are not transmitted.
