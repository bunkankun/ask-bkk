Early-Qīng Buddhist exegete; sobriquet **Rénshān** 仁山 (also referenced as **Rénshān 𧧌震**, **Rénshān dàshī** 仁山大師, **Jìzhèn** 寂震 — possibly an alternate transcription, **Huádǐng Zhèn** 華頂震, **Rénsǒu** 仁叟 "Old-Rén"). DILA Authority A000209. Lifedates unrecorded.

In Kāngxī 30 (= 1691 CE) became "ascending the hall" (*shàngtáng* 上堂) — i.e., began formally teaching as a master — at the **Dèngwèishān Shèngēnsì** 鄧尉山聖恩寺 in Sūzhōu. Composed the **Jīngāng sānmèi jīng tōngzōng jì** 金剛三昧經通宗記 ([[KR6d0115]], X35n0652, in 12 *juan*) — a substantial commentary on the *Vajrasamādhisūtra* (T273), with preface by the lay-disciple **Wèi Xuéqú** 魏學渠 (a *kānrěn xuérén* 堪忍學人, "patient-endurance student").

Note: The first character of the name 𧧌 is a rare Chinese character (CJK Extension B, U+27DCC); it should ideally be rendered as the *yán* 言 radical with the *cáo* 曺 phonetic. In monastic record contexts also written variously as 寂 (homophonous *jì*) or as the standard 𧧌.

Sources: *Dèngwèishān Shèngēnsì zhì* 鄧尉山聖恩寺志 *juan* 9; *Jīngāng sānmèi jīng tōngzōng jì xù* 金剛三昧經通宗記序; DILA A000209.
