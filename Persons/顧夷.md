Gù Yí 顧夷 (*fl.* mid 4th century), *zì* Jūnqí 君齊, was an Eastern Jìn 東晉 classicist of the prominent Wú-jùn 吳郡 Gù 顧 lineage — the same lineage that produced the Wú minister Gù Yōng 顧雍 (168–243). He is mentioned briefly in the *Jìn shū* 晉書 and in scattered notices in *Tōng diǎn* 通典 and *Shìshuō xīnyǔ* commentary. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records his *Yìxùn* 義訓 and a *Sūn-zǐ bīngfǎ zhù* 孫子兵法注. His precise birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally placed in the middle of the 4th century.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0128]] *Gùzǐ yìxùn* 顧子義訓. CBDB has no entry.
