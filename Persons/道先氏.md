**Dàoxiānshì** 道先氏 (*fl.* 1729 清), early-Qīng layman of 濛溪 (Méngxī, probably in Zhèjiāng or Jiāngsū), studio name 覺河圖 (Juéhétú, "Awakening Yellow-River Diagram"). Author of [[KR3ei051]] *Chǎnhòu shíbā lùn* 產後十八論 (1729). His preface to that work identifies him explicitly as a non-physician who came to medicine only through the obstetrical crisis of his own daughter-in-law (*dīngyǒu* year = 1717) and the gift of a "hereditary-medical-family" (*shìyī* 世醫) manuscript from his sister's husband Mr Shī 施. After a decade of using the formulas successfully on family, clan, and neighbours, he published the pamphlet in *Yōngzhèng* 7 (1729). The *shìyī* lineage that produced the original manuscript is unidentified. Not in CBDB.
