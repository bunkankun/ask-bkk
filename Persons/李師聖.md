**Lǐ Shīshèng** 李師聖 (Sòng), author of the obstetrical *Chǎnlùn* 產論; his work was paired with [[郭稽中]] Guō Jìzhōng's formulary in the genealogy of Sòng *fùkē* writings traced by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's preface to the *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* ([[KR3ei030]]).
