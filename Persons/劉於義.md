Liú Yúyì 劉於義 (1675–1748), *zì* Jièrén 介人, *hào* Wèigāng 蔚岡, native of Wǔjìn 武進 (Cháng-Zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 51 (1712); his Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng career is dominated by frontier-supply assignments. Among major posts: Shǎnxī *bùzhèngshǐ*; acting Zhílì Governor-General (1732–early 1733, with overlapping involvement in the *Jīfǔ tōngzhì* — [[KR2k0041]] — for which he wrote a preface dated Yōngzhèng 11/2 = 1733/3); transferred to Shǎnxī in 1733 as acting Governor-General with concurrent title Minister of Personnel and *bànlǐ* of military supply for the Dzungar campaigns, supervising the *Shǎnxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0050]]) and presenting it in 1735; later Grand Secretary of the Wén-yuān-gé and Minister of War. Posthumous title 文恪 Wénkè. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 308 has his biography. CBDB id 58150; lifedates per *Qīngdài rénwù shēng-zú niánbiǎo* (#5559); index_addr 7530 (Wǔjìn) confirmed.
