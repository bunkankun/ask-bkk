Yǒngchāo 永超 / Eichō (1014–1095) — Heian-period Japanese monk of the Kōfukuji 興福寺 lineage; cataloger of the *Tōiki dentō mokuroku* 東域傳燈目錄 and a key figure in the Japanese reception of Sanron 三論 exegesis.
