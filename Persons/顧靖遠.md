**Gù Jìng-yuǎn** 顧靖遠 (*hào* Sōng-yuán 松園, *fl.* late 17th – early 18th c.) — early-Qīng physician; *shēng-yuán* who turned to medicine after personal illness, with brief service at the imperial *Tài-yī yuàn* 太醫院 in Běijīng. Composed his sixteen-*juǎn* *Gù Sōng-yuán yī jìng* 顧松園醫鏡 ([[KR3er076]]) over thirty-plus years; the work was put through the press posthumously by his adopted son [[顧元宰]] Gù Yuán-zǎi at *Kāng-xī* 58 / 1719. Not in CBDB.
