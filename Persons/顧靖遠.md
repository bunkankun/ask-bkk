**Gù Jìngyuǎn** 顧靖遠 (*hào* Sōngyuán 松園, *fl.* late 17th – early 18th c.) — early-Qīng physician; *shēngyuán* who turned to medicine after personal illness, with brief service at the imperial *Tàiyī yuàn* 太醫院 in Běijīng. Composed his sixteen-*juǎn* *Gù Sōngyuán yī jìng* 顧松園醫鏡 ([[KR3er076]]) over thirty-plus years; the work was put through the press posthumously by his adopted son [[顧元宰]] Gù Yuánzǎi at *Kāngxī* 58 / 1719. Not in CBDB.
