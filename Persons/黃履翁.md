Huáng Lǚ-wēng 黃履翁 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* Jí-fǔ 吉父, a Fú-jiàn man of obscure native place. Late-Southern-Sòng scholar and colleague of Lín Jiōng 林駉. He wrote the preface to and the *bié-jí* (10 *juan*) of the *Gǔ-jīn yuán-liú zhì-lùn* 古今源流至論 ([[KR3k0031]]) — a late-Southern-Sòng *cè-lùn* examination crammer whose first three *jí* (30 *juan*) were by Lín Jiōng's hand.
