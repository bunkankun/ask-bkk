Huáng Lǚwēng 黃履翁 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* Jífǔ 吉父, a Fújiàn man of obscure native place. Late-Southern-Sòng scholar and colleague of Lín Jiōng 林駉. He wrote the preface to and the *biéjí* (10 *juan*) of the *Gǔjīn yuánliú zhìlùn* 古今源流至論 ([[KR3k0031]]) — a late-Southern-Sòng *cèlùn* examination crammer whose first three *jí* (30 *juan*) were by Lín Jiōng's hand.
