Yǔ-wén Shào-yì 宇文紹奕 (Southern Sòng; CBDB id 17173) was a literatus of Chéng-dū 成都. Few biographical details survive: the *Sì-kù* editors note that they could find nothing of his career, but observe that a Yǔ-wén Shào-jié 宇文紹節 served as *Shū-mì shǐ* under *Jiā-dìng* (1208–1224), and they suggest the two may have been brothers, placing Shào-yì in the late 12th – early 13th century. His one notable surviving contribution is the *Kǎo yì* 考異 (Critical Apparatus) to [[葉夢得]]'s *Shí-lín yàn yǔ* ([[KR3j0105]]) — a line-by-line *bǐjì* commentary correcting Yè's institutional and chronological lapses. The *Kǎo yì* was lost in transmission and was reconstructed from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* by the *Sì-kù* editors, who appended it under the corresponding entries of the SKQS recension. It is one of the more substantial Sòng-on-Sòng *bǐjì*-critical works.
