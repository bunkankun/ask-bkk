# Pǔhuì 普會

Yuán-dynasty Chán monk of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). Lifedates unrecorded; active early 14th century.

Continuer (*xù jí* 續集) of [[法應|Fǎyìng]]'s 法應 *Chán zōng sòng gǔ lián zhū tōng jí* 禪宗頌古聯珠通集 [[KR6q0243]], bringing the original Southern-Sòng compilation into the received 40-juan Yuán form with additional Sòng-Yuán master-verses added to each case. The completion was prefaced by the Yuán scholar-official Féng Zǐzhèn 馮子振 (1253–1348).
