# 年希堯

Nián Xīyáo (*zì* Ǒuzhāi 偶齋, d. 1738), high Qīng official, mathematician, and porcelain-supervisor at Jǐngdézhèn; elder brother of the *Yōngzhèng*-era general Nián Gēngyáo 年羹堯. Reprinted [[KR3em009]] *Yīcǎotíng mùkē quánshū* 一草亭目科全書 from his Jīnlíng office in *Kāngxī* 56 (1717), the earliest complete surviving edition.
