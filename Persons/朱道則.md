# Zhū Dàozé 朱道則

Qīng lay Buddhist and Pure-Land poet. *Hào* Tiānrán jūshì 天然居士 ("Natural-As-It-Is Layman"). Lifedates unrecorded.

Surviving work: the *Tiānrán jūshì huái jìng tǔ shī* 天然居士懷淨土詩 [[KR6q0239]] — a one-juan Chán–Pure-Land integrative poetic collection compiled by his dharma-nephew [[明康|Míngkāng]] 明康.
