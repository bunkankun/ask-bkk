# Dù Zhīgēng 杜知耕

Style name *Duānbó* 端伯. Native of Hénán; flourished in the early-to-mid Kāngxī period (active 1680s–1700s). Birth and death years not securely recorded.

A Kāngxī-period mathematical scholar; contemporary of [[梅文鼎]] Méi Wéndǐng. Two surviving works in the Sìkù — both 6-juan compendia for educational use:

(1) **Jǐhé lùnyuē** 幾何論約 ([[KR3f0049]]) — a 6-juan abridgment-and-commentary on the Ricci-Xú [[KR3f0047]] *Jǐhé yuánběn*. (The Sìkù-meta indicates this work is included in the Sìkù but the source files are missing in the present Kanripo recension.)

(2) **Shùxué yào** 數學鑰 ([[KR3f0050]]) — a 6-juan re-organization of the *Jiǔzhāng* tradition with diagrammatic-and-textual annotations. The Sìkù 提要 quotes [[梅文鼎]] Méi Wéndǐng's *Wù'ān lìsuàn shūjì* ([[KR3f0028]]) characterizing the work as "*Mr. Dù Duānbó's *Shùxué yào* — diagrammatically-annotated *Nine Chapters* — quite hits the essential vital-points; can serve as a model for the calculation school*". This is high praise from the leading mathematician of the period.
