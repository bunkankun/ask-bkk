**Mèng Zhèn** 孟震, Northern-Sòng official-scholar of the late Yuán-fēng to Yuán-yòu era (ca. 1080s–1090s), preface-writer for [[董汲]] (Dǒng Jí)'s *Lǚshě bèiyào fāng* 旅舍備要方 ([[KR3ed111]]). The preface places him as a colleague or senior acquaintance of Dǒng Jí, also literate in the medical classics and apparently in officialdom alongside Dǒng. No further biographical record is securely identifiable; the homonym 孟震 is reasonably common in Sòng-period records and disambiguation requires more contextual data than the preface gives.
