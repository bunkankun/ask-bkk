Mid-Míng official, native of **Póyáng** 鄱陽 (Jiāngxī). Served as *Yìwū xiànchéng* 義烏縣丞 in **Zhèngtǒng 6** (正統 6 = 1441), at which post he memorialized the throne regarding the loyalty and martyrdom of [[王禕]] **Wáng Yī** (1322–1374, native of Yìwū), and successfully obtained posthumous office and the posthumous title **Zhōngwén** 忠文 for Wáng. He simultaneously edited the *Wáng Zhōngwén jí* 王忠文集 [[KR4e0010]] jointly with **Liú Tóng** [[劉同]] of Lúlíng 廬陵 — Liú Jié editing *juǎn* 1–12 and Liú Tóng *juǎn* 13–24, with each collating the other's portion. Otherwise obscure in standard biographies. No confident CBDB match.
