**Fujiwara no Otsugu** 藤原緒嗣 (774–843), early-Heian-period Japanese statesman, *udaijin* 右大臣 and co-compiler of the *Nihon kōki* 日本後紀. Famously submitted a resignation memorial of Daidō 3 (808) attributing his ill health to *kakke* 脚気 (beriberi) — recorded in the *Nihon kōki* and cited in [[KR3eh054]] as the earliest dated reference to the disease in Japan.
