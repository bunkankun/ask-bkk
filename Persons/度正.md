Dù Zhèng 度正 (c. 1166–c. 1235, *zì* Zhōuqīng 周卿), of *Hézhōu* 合州 (modern Chóngqìng). Pupil of Zhū Xī [[朱熹]]; principal *Mǐnxué* exponent in the southwest. *Jìnshì* (early 1190s); held office to *Lǐbù shìláng*. Author of [[KR4d0294]] *Xìngshàntáng gǎo* and the canonical [[KR2k0017]] *Zhōuzǐ niánpǔ* 周子年譜 (chronological biography of Zhōu Dūnyí, separately cataloged in WYG). Printed the *Zhūzǐ Yǔlèi* at *Qīngyī* (the cut referenced in Wèi Liǎowēng's *Yǔlèi* preface); officed at *Tàicháng* during the *Tàimiào* fire and submitted two memorials on ritual. Lifedates approximate; CBDB id 13810 (no dates in CBDB). Author of [[KR4d0294]] *Xìngshàntáng gǎo*.
