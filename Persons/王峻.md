# Wáng Jùn 王峻

Early-Qīng scholar; floruit late-Kāng-xī / Yōng-zhèng / early-Qián-lóng (*ca.* 1700–1750). The principal contemporary biographer of Chén Jǐng-yún [[陳景雲]] (1670–1747); his *Wén-dào xiān-shēng mù-zhì-míng* 文道先生墓誌銘 — written for Chén — is preserved as appendix to Chén's *Hán jí diǎn-kān* [[KR4c0047]] and is the principal biographical source for Chén himself.
