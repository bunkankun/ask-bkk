Xìng Qí 性祇 (fl. late Míng) was a Vinaya specialist of the late Míng period. He is principally known for his **Fó shuō Mùlián wǔ-bǎi-wèn jīng lüè-jiě** 佛說目連五百問經略解 ([[KR6k0074]]) — a one-fascicle abridged commentary on the *Mùlián wǔ-bǎi wèn* literature, compiled in the late Míng. Along with **Yǒnghǎi** ([[永海]])'s parallel commentary ([[KR6k0075]]), Xìng Qí's work is part of the late-Míng revival of casuistic Vinaya scholarship.
