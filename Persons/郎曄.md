Láng Yè 郎曄 (fl. *Chún-xī* / *Shào-xī*, c. 1180s), of the *Dōng-zhōu* Láng family. Annotator of Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]]'s prose: the *Jīng-jìn Dōng-pō wén-jí shì-luè* 經進東坡文集事略 [[KR4d0079]] in 60 *juǎn*, presented to the throne under *Xiào-zōng* (presumably *Chún-xī*-period). The work is the principal Southern-Sòng prose-annotation of Sū Shì, complementing the contemporaneous Wáng Shí-péng *bǎi-jiā* poetry-annotation. Láng Yè's name *Yè* 曄 was changed to *Yù* 煜 under the Qing to avoid an imperial taboo, a practice noted by CBDB. CBDB id 33308; lineage identification on basis of *Dōng-zhōu* address.
