**Chén Sháojiǔ** 陳韶九, Republican-era physician active in the Jiāngyīn 江陰 / Xīchéng 錫城 area; obtained a clean manuscript of [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng's [[KR3eh053]] *Lìjí míngbiàn* from [[張宗耀]] Zhāng Zōngyào in spring 1940 and cut blocks for its first printing later that year, supplying a preface and running 按 annotations that polemicise against the Western anti-bacterial treatment of dysentery.
