# Yáo Guī 姚規

Pre-Táng *Yì* scholar of obscure dates; preserved only through a small number of citations in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). Identified by [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考 as one of the three masters supplementing Zhū Mùwēng's count, alongside [[朱仰之]] Zhū Yǎngzhī and [[蔡景君]] Cài Jǐngjūn.
