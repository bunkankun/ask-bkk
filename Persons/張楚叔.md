Zhāng Chǔ-shū 張楚叔 (fl. *Chóng-zhēn* era, 1628–1644), self-styled *Sāo-yǐn jū-shì* 騷隱居士 ("Recluse of *Sāo*"). A late-Míng *qǔ* anthologist of the Jiāng-nán region, otherwise undocumented. Elder brother of Zhāng Xù-chū [[張旭初]]. The two compiled the *Bái-xuě-zhāi xuǎn-dìng yuè-fǔ Wú-sāo hé-biān* 白雪齋選訂樂府吳騷合編 [[KR4j0069]], dated *Chóng-zhēn dīng-chǒu* (1637, by the editor's own colophon — though the proximate preface is dated *Chóng-zhēn bǐng-zǐ* / 1636 winter). The anthology was based on his elder-brother editing and his younger-brother's revision and proof-correction. Not in CBDB; the Zhāng brothers are known almost exclusively through this single anthology and the surrounding *Yuè-fǔ* compilations of the late Míng (the *Wú-sāo* series in particular).
