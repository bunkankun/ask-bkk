Zhāng Chǔshū 張楚叔 (fl. *Chóngzhēn* era, 1628–1644), self-styled *Sāoyǐn jūshì* 騷隱居士 ("Recluse of *Sāo*"). A late-Míng *qǔ* anthologist of the Jiāngnán region, otherwise undocumented. Elder brother of Zhāng Xùchū [[張旭初]]. The two compiled the *Báixuězhāi xuǎndìng yuèfǔ Wúsāo hébiān* 白雪齋選訂樂府吳騷合編 [[KR4j0069]], dated *Chóngzhēn dīngchǒu* (1637, by the editor's own colophon — though the proximate preface is dated *Chóngzhēn bǐngzǐ* / 1636 winter). The anthology was based on his elder-brother editing and his younger-brother's revision and proof-correction. Not in CBDB; the Zhāng brothers are known almost exclusively through this single anthology and the surrounding *Yuèfǔ* compilations of the late Míng (the *Wúsāo* series in particular).
