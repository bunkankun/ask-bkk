**Cài Pánxī** 蔡磻溪, early-Dàoguāng-era Húnán patron of medical publication; relative or clansman of [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī, co-sponsored the 1823 printing of [[KR3eh051]] *Nèishāng jí yào*. Known only from Cài Yíjī's preface.
