Qiū Chí 丘遲 (464–508 CE; *zì* Xīfàn 希範) was a Liáng 梁 literary official from Wúxīng 吳興 Wūchéng. Best known for the 〈與陳伯之書〉 (Letter to Chen Bozhi, *Wénxuǎn* juǎn 43), a masterpiece of persuasive parallel prose notable for its famous spring landscape passage. Biography in *Liángshū* juǎn 33 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 72. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0055]].
