**Wáng Zōngxiǎn** 王宗顯 (*fl.* late 16th c., precise lifedates and native place unrecorded). Mid-late-Ming physician. Author of the *Yīfāng jiéjìng zhǐnán quánshū* 醫方捷徑指南全書 ([[KR3ed050]]), an 8-*juǎn* practical clinical handbook compiled c. 1572. The work is one of the clearer mid-late-Ming examples of pedagogical-pharmacology — explaining drug actions through striking, memorable examples rather than dense theoretical apparatus. Not in CBDB; no biographical record outside the work attribution.
