Late-Qīng lay Buddhist; lifedates not preserved (DILA A001166). Lay-style **Zhēn-yì jū-shì** 真益居士 ("true-benefit layman"); also **Fù-zhāi** 復齋 (residence-name) and **Zhēn-yì-yuàn** 真益願. Active in the early-to-mid 19th century. Co-editor with **Yírùn** 儀潤 ([[儀潤]]) of the *Zài-jiā lǜ-yào guǎng-jí* 在家律要廣集 ([[KR6k0243]], X1123) — the expanded recension of Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù's late-Míng compilation on lay precepts (his expansion-preface dated *Dào-guāng* 4 / 1824). Also produced *Quàn xiū Jìng-tǔ qiè-yào* 勸修淨土切要 (one fascicle, X62), and a critical edition of *Huá-yán yuán-rén lùn hé-jiě* 華嚴原人論合解 (X58). His Pure-Land-and-Vinaya orientation is typical of the *jū-shì* 居士 ("layman") network around the *Jiāng-zhè* Buddhist revival of the early-19th-century Qīng.
