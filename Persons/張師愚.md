Mid-Yuán Xuānchéng literatus. *Zì* **Zhòng-yú** 仲愚; native of **Níngguó** 寧國 (Xuānchéng region, southern Anhuī). Twice recommended in the **Yán-yòu** (1314–1320) and **Tiān-lì** (1328–1330) *xiāng-jǔ* (provincial recommendations) but apparently did not pass the central examinations. Close friend of [[汪澤民]]; the two co-compiled the local poetry anthology **[[KR4h0080]]** *Wǎnlíng qúnyīng jí* 宛陵羣英集, completed and printed in 1341. He authored the second of the two prefaces of that work (also dated *zhèng-yuè bǐng-zǐ* of Zhì-zhèng *xīn-sì*). The *Jiāng-nán tōng-zhì* attributes a *Méi Yáo-chén nián-pǔ* to him, but the SKQS editors note this is in fact the work of his younger brother **Zhāng Shīzēng** 張師曾.
