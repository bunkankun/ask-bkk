# Miàoyǎn 妙儼

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[慧性]] Wúmíng Huìxìng (1162–1237); principal co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0312]]), where he is named as recorder of the opening Zīfúchányuàn 資福禪寺 abbacy record. Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000479).
