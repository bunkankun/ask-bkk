**Yamada Seichin** 山田正珍 (Sinicized Shānzhǔ Zhèngzhēn; *zì* Zōngjùn 宗俊, *hào* Túnán 圖南, 1749–1787, 江戶), late-Edo Japanese physician and philologist of the Chinese medical canon. A member of the bakufu *Igaku-kan* 醫學館 circle and a contemporary of [[丹波元簡]] Tamba no Mototane, Yamada produced the *Shānghán lùn jí chéng* 傷寒論集成 ([[KR3ef025]]) — a 10-juan systematic commentary harmonizing the major Chinese *Shānghán* commentary lines with Japanese *koihō*-school clinical practice. He died young at 38; his work was published posthumously in 1789 by his disciples. Other works include a *Jīnguì yàoluè jí chéng* 金匱要略集成 and clinical case-notes (lost). Reimported to Qīng China by [[楊守敬]] Yáng Shǒujìng in the 1880s, his commentary entered the Chinese medical-philological mainstream.
