# 洪壽椿

Hóng Shòuchūn

Late-Qīng layman of Táiyáng 臺陽 (southern Taiwan) who in 1867 (Tóngzhì 6) wrote the reprint preface for [[陳存吾]] Chén Cúnwú's [[KR6c0096]] *Jīngāng jīng chǎnshuō*, helping to circulate the work in nineteenth-century Taiwan.
