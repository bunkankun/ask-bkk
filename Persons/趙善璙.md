Zhào Shàn-liáo 趙善璙 (b. 1153, *fl. Duān-píng* 1234–1236). Sòng imperial-clansman, seventh-generation descendant of Tài-zōng (the second Sòng emperor, Tài-zǔ's younger brother and successor). Family settled at Nán-hǎi 南海 (in modern Guǎng-dōng). Served in administrative posts, reaching Prefect of Jiāng-zhōu 江州 during the *Duān-píng* reign (1234–1236) of Lǐ-zōng. During this prefectship he compiled the *Zì jǐng biān* 自警編 ([[KR3j0183]]) in 9 *juàn* — a topical compendium of Sòng worthy-figures' words and deeds modeled on Zhū Xī's *Bā cháo yán xíng lù*. No further details of his career are documented in the standard histories.
