Southern-Sòng *Nán-shān* Vinaya monk of the *Líng-zhī* 靈芝寺 (Hángzhōu) line; lifedates not preserved (DILA A000477). Sobriquet **Shàng-wēng** 上翁. According to his own *Péng-zhé zhēn*, he was abbot at *Hú-xīn-sì* 湖心寺 (one of West Lake's island monasteries) during the *Shào-dìng* 紹定 era (1228–1233). His two surviving polemical works are:

- *Péng-zhé zhí-biàn* 蓬折直辨 ([[KR6k0221]], X1111), one fascicle;
- *Péng-zhé zhēn* 蓬折箴 ([[KR6k0222]], X1112), one fascicle, with a colophon dated *Bǎo-yòu* 2 = **1254**.

Both are sustained refutations of *Tiě-wēng Shǒu-yī*'s ([[守一]]) *Zhōng-nán jiā-yè* ([[KR6k0219]]); the two together form one of the most polemically-charged exchanges within Southern-Sòng *Nán-shān* Vinaya history.
