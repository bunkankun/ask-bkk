Southern-Sòng *Nánshān* Vinaya monk of the *Língzhī* 靈芝寺 (Hángzhōu) line; lifedates not preserved (DILA A000477). Sobriquet **Shàngwēng** 上翁. According to his own *Péngzhé zhēn*, he was abbot at *Húxīnsì* 湖心寺 (one of West Lake's island monasteries) during the *Shàodìng* 紹定 era (1228–1233). His two surviving polemical works are:

- *Péngzhé zhíbiàn* 蓬折直辨 ([[KR6k0221]], X1111), one fascicle;
- *Péngzhé zhēn* 蓬折箴 ([[KR6k0222]], X1112), one fascicle, with a colophon dated *Bǎoyòu* 2 = **1254**.

Both are sustained refutations of *Tiěwēng Shǒuyī*'s ([[守一]]) *Zhōngnán jiāyè* ([[KR6k0219]]); the two together form one of the most polemically-charged exchanges within Southern-Sòng *Nánshān* Vinaya history.
