# 永眞

Eishin 永眞 (Yǒngzhēn), late-Heian Japanese Shingon monk. Recorded as the scribe who copied the *Qiānshǒu jīng èrshíbā bù zhòng shì* ([[KR6j0261]]) at the Tō-ji Provisional Hall on the 14th day of the 4th month of Kennyū 9 (= 1198 CE).
