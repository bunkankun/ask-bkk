**Ekō** 惠光 — Medieval Japanese **Vinaya** (Ritsu 律) monk of **Tōshōdai-ji** 唐招提寺, identifiable only through his sole canonical work, *Táng zhāo-tí-sì jiè-tán bié shòu-jiè-shì* 唐招提寺戒壇別受戒式 ([[KR6t0048]], T74n2351), a single-fascicle codification of the *traditional* (*bié-shòu* 別受) ordination procedure at the Tōshōdai-ji ordination platform — to be distinguished from the *universal-vow* ordination procedures established by **Kakujō** 覺盛 at the same monastery in 1236.

Not to be confused with the 5th-Dynasty Ekō (DILA A014213, d. 915) or the Edo Sōtō-Zen Ekō (DILA A038401). No DILA entry is matched to this Tōshōdai-ji Vinaya Ekō. The text survives only in a Genroku 11 (= 1698) transmission copy.
