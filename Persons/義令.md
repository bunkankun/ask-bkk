Yì-lìng 義令 (active Tang), Tang Buddhist scholar-monk; the disciple of [[窺基]] Kuī-jī (632–682, the great Yogācāra-school master) who recorded his master's lectures on the *[[KR6f0045|Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra]]* in the *[[KR6f0058|Shèng mán jīng shù jì 勝鬘經述記]]* (X352). Per DILA A001463: 唐 窺基說　義令記 ("Tang Kuī-jī recited, Yì-lìng recorded"). Birth and death dates not preserved.
