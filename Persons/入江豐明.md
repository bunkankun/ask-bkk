# Irie Toemon 入江豐明

Edo-period Japanese acupuncturist, founder or principal heir of the *Irie-ryū* 入江流 acupuncture lineage that descended from the Míng-influenced continental tradition imported into early-Edo Japan. Known almost entirely through [[杉山和一]] Sugiyama Wa'ichi's autobiographical preface to [[KR3ee052]] *Xuǎnzhēn sānyào jí* 選針三要集, where he is named as Sugiyama's teacher (*Iruka* 入江先生) under whom Sugiyama studied for three years. No independent biography survives.
