**Xiè Jiàng** 謝絳 (994–1039), *zì* Xīshēn 希深 (under which the *Sìkù* and most pre-modern catalogues cite him), was a Northern-Sòng scholar-official of Fùyáng 富陽 (Hángzhōu prefecture). He took the *jìnshì* in the eighth year of Dàzhōng-Xiángfú 大中祥符 (1015), held appointments as Yǒuzhèngyán 右正言 in the Imperial Censorate (御史臺), and served as prefect of Dèngzhōu 鄧州, where he died in office in Bǎoyuán 寶元 2 (1039) at age 46. Closely connected with the literary circle around Méi Yáochén 梅堯臣 — his sister was Méi's wife, and Méi's surviving correspondence preserves several memorial pieces for him. Authored a *Wényǐ jí* 文懿集 in collected works (now mostly lost). Best known to philological history as the commentator on the ***Gōngsūn Lóngzǐ*** 公孫龍子 ([[KR3j0007]]) — the only commentary on the work that survives, the earlier annotations by Chén Sìgǔ 陳嗣古 and Jiǎ Shìyǐn 賈士隱 (recorded in Zhèng Qiáo's 鄭樵 *Tōngzhì lüè* 通志畧) being now lost. The *Sìkù* editors note that his exegesis is "rather shallow and accessible" but retain it for want of a better. CBDB id 727.
