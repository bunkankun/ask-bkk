Anonymous late-Qīng physician signing himself as the *Rán-xī dào-rén* 燃犀道人 ("Rhino-horn-Burning Daoist," after the *Jìn-shū* legend of [[溫嶠]] Wēn Qiáo burning a rhino-horn to see into the river-demon depths). Sole documented work is [[KR3er107]] *Ōu-gǔ rán-xī lù* 歐蠱燃犀錄 (*Guāng-xù* 19 / 1893), a learned medical-exorcistic treatise on *gǔ* 蠱 (poison-magic / sorcery-illness) explicitly responding to the *Guāng-xù dīng-chǒu* 光緒丁丑 / 1877 "*yāo-rén*" 妖人 panic in southern China (the sorcery-panic in which people were reported having had their clothes cut with paper-soldier amulets or marked with five-coloured prints). The author treats nineteenth-century mass-magical-panic episodes as medical *gǔ*-events. Real-name identification not located.
