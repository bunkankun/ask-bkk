# Liú Sōngshí 劉鬆石

Mid-Míng *Jiājìng*-era senior official (*hào* Sōngshí 鬆石); commissioned the Kāifēng provincial-yamen printing of [[王鑾]] Wáng Luán's paediatric encyclopaedia *Yòukē lèicuì* 幼科類萃 ([[KR3ej014]]) and (according to the work's postface) also of the *Shānghán liùshū* 傷寒六書. Full given name and lifedates unrecorded.
