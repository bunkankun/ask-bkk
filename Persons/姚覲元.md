Late-Qīng bibliophile, woodblock-publisher and provincial official from Guī'ān 歸安 (Wúxīng 吳興), Zhèjiāng, son of [[姚衡]] Yáo Héng and nephew of [[姚晏]] Yáo Yàn. CBDB has two close records for the name 姚覲元: id 85603 (birth-year 1823, index year 1823) and id 438963 (death-year 1890, index year 1827); both are taken here as the same person, with composite dates 1823–1890 (the standard reference figure followed in Zhèjiāng bibliographic studies). Best known for the 咫進齋叢書 *Zhǐjìnzhāi cóngshū* and for the recutting (*chóngkān* 重刊) of inherited family imprints. Wrote the *Tóngzhì* 13 (1874) postface to [[KR3em029]] *Yānhóu màizhèng tōnglùn*, which records that the original *Dàoguāng* 21 (1841) Guǎngzhōu blocks his father [[姚衡]] had commissioned were destroyed in the Tàipíng war ("江浙寇亂"); having recovered an early-impression copy in Chuāndōng 川東 in the winter of *Tóngzhì* 13, Yáo recut the work, faithfully reproducing the original title-page and his father's hand-written signatures.
