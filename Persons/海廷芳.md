Hǎi Tíngfāng 海廷芳 (Kāngxī era, fl. late 17th c.), sixth-generation descendant of **Hǎi Ruì** 海瑞. He compiled and re-edited Hǎi Ruì's *Bèiwàng jí* [[KR4e0207]] in the Kāngxī period; his colophon claims 12 *juǎn* in 10 *cè*, though the *Sìkù*-received recension is only 10 *juǎn* (2 missing).
