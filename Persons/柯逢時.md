# Kē Féngshí 柯逢時

Late-Qīng official and bibliophile (1845–1912) of Wǔchāng 武昌 (modern Wǔhàn). *Jìnshì* of 1886; held various provincial posts. Sponsored the *Wǔchāng yī xué guǎn* 武昌醫學館 and the *Wǔchāng cóngshū* 武昌叢書 project, which reissued in critical recension a series of rare Sòng-Yuán medical imprints including the *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]]) in 1910. His editorial postfaces are valued for their careful collation notes.
