# 何書山

Hé Shūshān 何書山 (mid-Qīng), Sūzhōu physician of the Hé-family medical lineage; one of the "Hé-family three" alongside [[何琬亭]] Hé Wǎntíng and [[何元長]] Hé Yuáncháng, ancestors of the Ménghé Hé-family clinicians.
