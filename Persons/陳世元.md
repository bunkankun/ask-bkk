**Chén Shìyuán** 陳世元 (Qīng), Fújiàn-based author of the *Jīnshǔ pǔ* 金薯譜 (1768), an agricultural-medical treatise on the sweet potato (*gānshǔ* 甘薯), documenting its tonifying and longevity-promoting properties in coastal Fújiàn. Cited by [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ in the *yīntǐng* supplement to the *Nǚkē yàozhǐ* 女科要旨 ([[KR3ei008]]).
