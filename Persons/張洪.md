Zhāng Hóng 張洪 (Sòng), *zì* Bódà 伯大, was a late-Southern-Sòng / Sòng-Yuán transition *Lǐxué* compiler of Pó-yáng 鄱陽 (Jiāng-xī). In Xián-chún (1265–1274), held an instructor's post at Sì-míng 四明 (Níng-bō). With Qí Xī 齊𤇸, he compiled the *Zhū-zǐ dú-shū fǎ* 朱子讀書法 (KR3a0070) — a thematic anthology of Zhū Xī's positions on reading method, building on Fǔ Guǎng 輔廣's earlier work and Wáng-shì 王氏 of Pó-yáng's continuation. The work was printed at the Yín-pàn 鄞泮 (Yín-county academy) in the late 1260s or early 1270s. CBDB has multiple homonyms 張洪; no confident identification with any specific id.
