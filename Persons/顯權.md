Xiǎn-quán 顯權, *zì* **Dá-biàn** 達變, dharma-heir of **Mù-chén Shān-wēng Dào-mín** [[道忞]] 木陳山翁道忞 (1596–1674) at **Tiān-tóng-sì** 天童寺. Native of **Fēng-chéng** 豐城 (Jiāng-xī); lay surname **Wáng** 王. *Shì-shòu* **52** (lifedates not precisely recovered; predeceased his teacher Dào-mín, hence death before **1674**). First studied under **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] at Tiān-tóng before Yuán-wù's 1642 death (the preface-phrase *xiān Tiān-tóng zhuī-fú* 先天童椎拂); subsequently sealed by Dào-mín. Served as *fēn-zuò* 分座 (assistant teacher) at Tiān-tóng before assuming his own abbacy at **Wǔ-lěi-shān Líng-fēng Chán-sì** 五磊山靈峰禪寺 in **Cí-xī-xiàn** 慈谿縣 (Míng-zhōu, modern Níng-bō prefecture).

**Works.** (i) Co-compiler (lead, *sì-fǎ mén-rén děng biān*) of the 20-juan 《天童弘覺忞禪師語錄》 (= **Tiāntóng Hóng-jué Mín chán-shī yǔ-lù**) — the principal Mù-chén Dào-mín *yǔ-lù* preserved in the Zhōng-huá Tripitaka as No. 1746. (ii) [[KR6q0457]] 《達變權禪師語錄》 5 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B248, recorded by *shì-zhě* **Hǎi-chéng** [[海澂]], with front-preface by *tóng-mén fǎ-dì Lù-chéng Bīng-ān* **Zhāng Lì-lián** 鹿城冰菴張立廉. **Sole named heir**: **Wǔ-lěi Líng-shān Tiān-yīn Cháo chán-shī** 五磊靈山天音朝.

Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 72, j. 94; DILA authority A001978.
