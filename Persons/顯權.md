Xiǎnquán 顯權, *zì* **Dábiàn** 達變, dharma-heir of **Mùchén Shānwēng Dàomín** [[道忞]] 木陳山翁道忞 (1596–1674) at **Tiāntóngsì** 天童寺. Native of **Fēngchéng** 豐城 (Jiāngxī); lay surname **Wáng** 王. *Shìshòu* **52** (lifedates not precisely recovered; predeceased his teacher Dàomín, hence death before **1674**). First studied under **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] at Tiāntóng before Yuánwù's 1642 death (the preface-phrase *xiān Tiāntóng zhuīfú* 先天童椎拂); subsequently sealed by Dàomín. Served as *fēnzuò* 分座 (assistant teacher) at Tiāntóng before assuming his own abbacy at **Wǔlěishān Língfēng Chánsì** 五磊山靈峰禪寺 in **Cíxīxiàn** 慈谿縣 (Míngzhōu, modern Níngbō prefecture).

**Works.** (i) Co-compiler (lead, *sìfǎ ménrén děng biān*) of the 20-juan 《天童弘覺忞禪師語錄》 (= **Tiāntóng Hóngjué Mín chánshī yǔlù**) — the principal Mùchén Dàomín *yǔlù* preserved in the Zhōnghuá Tripitaka as No. 1746. (ii) [[KR6q0457]] 《達變權禪師語錄》 5 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B248, recorded by *shìzhě* **Hǎichéng** [[海澂]], with front-preface by *tóngmén fǎdì Lùchéng Bīngān* **Zhāng Lìlián** 鹿城冰菴張立廉. **Sole named heir**: **Wǔlěi Língshān Tiānyīn Cháo chánshī** 五磊靈山天音朝.

Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 72, j. 94; DILA authority A001978.
