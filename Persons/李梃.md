**Lǐ Tǐng** 李梃 (*zì* Jiànzhāi 健齋, *fl.* mid-Wàn-lì 萬曆, Nánfēng 南豐 / Jiāngxī) — mid-Míng physician. Compiled the eight-*juǎn* *Yīxué rùmén* 醫學入門 ([[KR3er072]]) at *Wànlì* 3 / 1575, a standard late-Míng curricular handbook that became the principal Chinese-medicine reference for the Chosŏn Korean *Dōngyī* tradition (cited more than 1,500 times in the *Dōngyī bǎojiàn* of 1613). Lǐ describes himself in the self-preface as having turned to medicine after a serious personal illness in young adulthood; otherwise poorly attested. CBDB id 277911.
