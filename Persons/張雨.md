Zhāng Yǔ 張雨 (1277–1348; catalog meta matches; CBDB gives 1283-1350 — minor discrepancy; the literary-historical preferred is 1277-1348), *zì* Bóyǔ 伯雨, *hào* Jùqū wàishǐ 句曲外史 (whence the collection title [[KR4d0544]]), also *Zhēnjūzǐ* 貞居子. Daoist priest at *Máoshān* / *Jùqū*. Also author of *Yuán pǐnlù* 元品錄 (in another section of the catalog). His verse and prose were hand-copied by himself; in his lifetime the *kèbǎn* (printing) did not happen, so *língjiān duànsù* (scattered fragments) were preserved by collectors. Míng Chénghuà *Yáo Shòu* 姚綬 first obtained the *gǎo*; Jiājìng *jiǎwǔ* (1534) Chén Yīngfú 陳應符 arranged as 3 *juǎn* and printed, with appended Liú Jī *mùzhì* and Yáo Shòu's *xiǎozhuàn*. Chóng-zhēn-era Chángshú Máo Jìn 毛晉 added *bǔyí* 3 *juǎn* (Wūchéng Mǐn Yuánhéng 閔元衡's *yìshī*-collection); Máo and his nephew Féng Wǔ 馮武 searched out further *jíwài shī*. Early met Zhào Mèngfǔ 趙孟頫; late saw Ní Zàn, Gù Āyīng 顧阿瑛, Yáng Wéizhēn. Middle period extensively exchanged with Yú Jí, Fàn Pèng [[范梈]], Yuán Jué 袁桷, Huáng Jìn [[黃溍]] — *fāngwài zhī jiāo* (outside-the-method, monastic friendship); placed in the literary canon despite his *huángguān* (Daoist priest) status.
