Zhāng Yǔ 張雨 (1277–1348; catalog meta matches; CBDB gives 1283-1350 — minor discrepancy; the literary-historical preferred is 1277-1348), *zì* Bó-yǔ 伯雨, *hào* Jù-qū wài-shǐ 句曲外史 (whence the collection title [[KR4d0544]]), also *Zhēn-jū-zǐ* 貞居子. Daoist priest at *Máo-shān* / *Jù-qū*. Also author of *Yuán pǐn-lù* 元品錄 (in another section of the catalog). His verse and prose were hand-copied by himself; in his lifetime the *kè-bǎn* (printing) did not happen, so *líng-jiān duàn-sù* (scattered fragments) were preserved by collectors. Míng Chéng-huà *Yáo Shòu* 姚綬 first obtained the *gǎo*; Jiā-jìng *jiǎ-wǔ* (1534) Chén Yīng-fú 陳應符 arranged as 3 *juǎn* and printed, with appended Liú Jī *mù-zhì* and Yáo Shòu's *xiǎo-zhuàn*. Chóng-zhēn-era Cháng-shú Máo Jìn 毛晉 added *bǔ-yí* 3 *juǎn* (Wū-chéng Mǐn Yuán-héng 閔元衡's *yì-shī*-collection); Máo and his nephew Féng Wǔ 馮武 searched out further *jí-wài shī*. Early met Zhào Mèng-fǔ 趙孟頫; late saw Ní Zàn, Gù Ā-yīng 顧阿瑛, Yáng Wéi-zhēn. Middle period extensively exchanged with Yú Jí, Fàn Pèng [[范梈]], Yuán Jué 袁桷, Huáng Jìn [[黃溍]] — *fāng-wài zhī jiāo* (outside-the-method, monastic friendship); placed in the literary canon despite his *huáng-guān* (Daoist priest) status.
