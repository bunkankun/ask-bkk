Silla 新羅 / Korean monk (also written 見登之 *Jiàndēngzhī*; Korean: Gyeondeungji), known by the colophon-honorific *Qīngqiū shāmén* 青丘沙門 ("Monk of the Blue Hills" — i.e., Korea). Lifedates unknown; active in the late Silla / Tang transitional period (broadly 8th–9th c.). The DILA authority record (A000544) treats him as distinct from another Tang-period 見登 (A000543), but the texts attributed to the two figures are sometimes confused in the canon.

Author of two extant works in the Buddhist canon:
- The *Huáyán yīshèng chéngfó miàoyì* 華嚴一乘成佛妙義 (T45n1890), a Huáyán doctrinal compendium on attainment of buddhahood through the One Vehicle.
- The *Qǐxìn lùn tóngyì jí* 起信論同異集 [[KR6o0113]] (X45n759), a topical compendium juxtaposing the *Awakening of Faith* and the *Cheng-wei-shi lun* on common doctrinal points to clarify their agreements and disagreements.

His method — comparative juxtaposition of *Tathāgatagarbha* and Yogācāra positions — places him in the broader Silla intellectual ecosystem that produced [[元曉]] and [[太賢]]; he is often grouped with the latter as a Yogācāra-affiliated Korean scholar engaging the *Qǐxìn lùn* tradition from a Cí'ēn-school standpoint.

Sources: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000544; *Huáyán yīshèng chéngfó miàoyì* T45n1890.
