Zhū Fǔ 朱輔 (*fl.* late Southern Sòng, ca. 1240–1270), *zì* Jìgōng 季公; native of Tóngxiāng 桐鄉 (modern Zhèjiāng). Late-Southern-Sòng minor official; appears in the *Hǔqiū zhì* on the basis of a poem on Hǔqiū (the famous Sūzhōu sight). Served officially in the Chénzhōu / Wǔxī region of western Húnán, where on the basis of personal observation he composed the *Xīmán cóngxiào* 溪蠻叢笑 ([[KR2k0140]]) — the principal Southern-Sòng ethnographic monograph on the *Wǔxī mán* (the indigenous peoples of the Wǔxī, ancestral to the modern Tǔjiā, Miáo, and Yáo). Otherwise undocumented; CBDB has multiple homonymous entries (10553, 127375, etc.) with no confident date-match.
