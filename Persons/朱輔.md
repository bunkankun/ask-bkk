Zhū Fǔ 朱輔 (*fl.* late Southern Sòng, ca. 1240–1270), *zì* Jì-gōng 季公; native of Tóng-xiāng 桐鄉 (modern Zhèjiāng). Late-Southern-Sòng minor official; appears in the *Hǔ-qiū zhì* on the basis of a poem on Hǔ-qiū (the famous Sū-zhōu sight). Served officially in the Chén-zhōu / Wǔ-xī region of western Húnán, where on the basis of personal observation he composed the *Xī-mán cóng-xiào* 溪蠻叢笑 ([[KR2k0140]]) — the principal Southern-Sòng ethnographic monograph on the *Wǔ-xī mán* (the indigenous peoples of the Wǔ-xī, ancestral to the modern Tǔ-jiā, Miáo, and Yáo). Otherwise undocumented; CBDB has multiple homonymous entries (10553, 127375, etc.) with no confident date-match.
