# 勝澤圭元璋

Shōzawa Kei Genshō, medical officer of Echizen 越前 in late-Edo Japan, disciple of [[本荘普一]] Honjō Fuichi. Wrote the *Tenpō* 6 (1835) Kyōto colophon to [[KR3em005]] *Yǎnkē jǐnnáng* 眼科錦囊 and supplied the surgical-instrument illustrations.
