# Liú Xiàosūn 劉孝孫

Liú Xiàosūn 劉孝孫 (d. 642; CBDB id 92625, fl. 617–641), early Táng literatus and calligrapher. He is recorded in the *Jiù Táng shū · Wénxué zhuàn* 舊唐書·文學傳 as active at the early Táng court, associated with the staff of Prince Qín (秦王府幕 — i.e., the entourage of Li Shìmín before his enthronement as Táng Tàizōng) alongside other court literati. He was known as a calligrapher and participated in literary discussions at the early court.

He is the attributed author of *Shì shǐ* 事始 ([[KR3k0074]]), a 3-*juan* classified reference tracing the first occurrences of cultural institutions, objects, and customs. The work was continued by [[馮鑑]] in the Five Dynasties period (*Xù shì shǐ* 續事始, [[KR3k0077]]).

Note: CBDB lists two other individuals named 劉孝孫 (ids 10653 with birth/death 0/0, and 449787 without dates); the identification of id 92625 (d. 642, fl. 617–641) as the attributed author of *Shì shǐ* follows the early-Táng date bracket consistent with the work's content and traditional attributions.
