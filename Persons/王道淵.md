**Wáng Dàoyuān** 王道淵 (fl. mid-14th century, late Yuán to early Míng), *hào* **Húnránzǐ** 混然子, native of **Xiūjiāng** 修江 in **Nánchāng** 南昌 (Jiāngxī). Yuán-Míng transition Quánzhēn / Lóngmén Daoist commentator. Author of the *Yīnfú jīng zhù* 陰符經注 (commonly bound with Zhāng Guǒ's Táng commentary; cf. [[KR5i0031]]) and the standard *Tài shàng dào dé bǎo zhāng* 太上道德寶章 commentary. His commentaries are the bridge between Sòng-Yuán Quánzhēn inner-alchemy and Míng-period reception, and underlie the late-Míng / Qīng common print recensions. CBDB ID 286304 has no dates, but the absence of a Yuán-Sòng dynasty marker and presence in late-Yuán *Dàozàng* materials place him in the Húnyuán transition era. Húnránzǐ is also the customary *hào* of the Sòng Quánzhēn lineage; the conflation has historically caused confusion.
