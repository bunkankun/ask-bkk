**Wáng Dào-yuān** 王道淵 (fl. mid-14th century, late Yuán to early Míng), *hào* **Hún-rán-zǐ** 混然子, native of **Xiū-jiāng** 修江 in **Nán-chāng** 南昌 (Jiāng-xī). Yuán-Míng transition Quán-zhēn / Lóng-mén Daoist commentator. Author of the *Yīn-fú jīng zhù* 陰符經注 (commonly bound with Zhāng Guǒ's Táng commentary; cf. [[KR5i0031]]) and the standard *Tài shàng dào dé bǎo zhāng* 太上道德寶章 commentary. His commentaries are the bridge between Sòng-Yuán Quán-zhēn inner-alchemy and Míng-period reception, and underlie the late-Míng / Qīng common print recensions. CBDB ID 286304 has no dates, but the absence of a Yuán-Sòng dynasty marker and presence in late-Yuán *Dào-zàng* materials place him in the Hún-yuán transition era. Hún-rán-zǐ is also the customary *hào* of the Sòng Quán-zhēn lineage; the conflation has historically caused confusion.
