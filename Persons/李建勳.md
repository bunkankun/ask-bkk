**Lǐ Jiànxūn** 李建勳 (873?–952), of *Lǒngxī* 隴西 (the *jùnwàng* / ancestral-clan designation; his actual residence was *Jīnlíng* / Nánjīng). Foundational poet of the early **Southern-Táng** 南唐 court and the dynasty's first-generation chancellor. CBDB id 92981 confirms 873?–952.

His career began in the *Wú* state (the late-Táng successor regime in the *JiāngHuái* region under Yáng Hàngmì 楊行密 and successors). With the Wú-Southern-Táng transition under Lǐ Biàn 李昪 (Lièzǔ, r. 937–943), Lǐ Jiànxūn rose to *Tóng zhōngshū ménxià píngzhāngshì* (chancellor) in the early *Shēngyuán* reign. He was retired with senior-honor titles in the early 940s and lived out his final years at *Jīnlíng* in poetic retirement, dying in 952 at the start of the *Bǎodà* reign of Lǐ Jǐng 李璟. The collection's title — *Lǐ chéngxiàng* (Chancellor Lǐ) — refers to this terminal office.

His poetry is preserved in the two-*juǎn* fragmentary [[KR4c0114]] *Lǐ chéngxiàng shījí* (SBCK reprint of the late-Sòng *Línān Chénzhái* cutting). Approximately 85 poems survive of an original 30-*juǎn* corpus (per *Sòngshǐ yìwénzhì*). His addressees document the early-Southern-Táng poetic community — Sūn Fāng 孫魴, Pī Bīn 沈彬, *zhèngshì xuéshì* and other Southern-Táng courtiers; his style — calmer and more meditative than Wéi Zhuāng [[韋莊]] and less imagistically dramatic than Guànxiū [[釋貫休]] or Qíjǐ [[釋齊己]] — defines a transitional voice between late-Táng and Sòng *shī*. A small body of *cí* is also attributed to him (preserved in the *Quán TángWǔdài cí*).
