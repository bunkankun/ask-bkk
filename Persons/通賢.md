# Tōngxián 通賢 (Fúshí Tōngxián 浮石通賢)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, 31st-generation Línjì descendant. *Zì* **Fúshí** 浮石 (from Fúshífó 浮石佛, the Buddhist allusion to a drifting stone-statue come ashore); self-deprecating *hào* **Dōngjiàn lǎorén** 東澗老人 ("Old Man of Eastern-Ravine"). Lay surname **Zhào** 趙, lay-name **Zhòngguāng** 重光 (given at age 6). Native of **Hǔlín** 虎林 (Hang-zhōu); the family moved to **Dānghú** 當湖 in Pínghú 平湖, Jiāxīngfǔ, when he was an infant. Lifedates **1594–1667** (Wànlì 22 – Kāngxī 6), age 74 per the *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 and cross-confirmed by the master's own 1648 *xíngshí* in [[KR6q0407]] juan 9 (internal datum: Mìyún sealed him in 1639 at age 47 *suì*, fixing birth to 1594).

Father **Zhào Wén** 趙文, mother **Lǐ-shì** 李氏. Adopted vegetarian discipline at 13; at 19 (1612) broke with parents, went to **Pǔ-tuó-shān** 普陀山, tonsured by **Shào-zōng chán-shī** 紹宗禪師; after three months returned home and grew his hair back to comfort his mother. Formal tonsure at **Wǔ-yuán Pǔ-jìng-ān** 武原普淨庵 by **Chéng-bái fǎ-shī** 乘白法師. Received *śrāmaṇera* precepts (c. 1613–14) from **Yún-qī Zhū-hóng** 雲棲袾宏 (1535–1615) — placing him among the very last ordinand-cohorts of Yún-qī. Scriptural training under **Míng-yé** 明耶. First Chán-training at **Yīng-wō-shān** 鶯窠山 under **Yī-chū chán-shī** 一初禪師 (three years attendant-duty). Full precepts at **Yán-guān Tiān-níng** 鹽官天寧 under **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄 (湛和尚).

First *xǐng* 省 at Yīngwōshān on the *Qī xiánnǚ guān shītuólín* 七賢女觀尸陀林 *gōngàn*. Final Chán-sealing and *fùfǎ* 付法 from **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 (1566/7–1642) at Mìyún's **Jīnsù** 金粟 seat on the *xūnfēng* 薰風 *gōngàn*, c. 1636–39. Three-year *bìguān* at Wǔyuán on Mìyún's injunction. Formal transmission received at **Huáng Lǚsù** 黃履素's **Sùyuán** 素園 garden in Jiāhé during Mìyún's 1639 illness-convalescence stay there, on the same day as his dharma-brother **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1595–1652; author of [[KR6q0408]]) — the famous "*wǔsè jīnlián shuāng kāi* 五色金蓮雙開" dream-of-lotuses episode, attested by three independent prefatorial witnesses ([[通賢]] *xíngshí*, [[錢謙益]] 1662 preface, and Línyě's postface).

Eight successive abbacies: (1) **Dānghú Qīngliánsì** 當湖青蓮寺 (from Chóngzhēn 12.12.17 = 29 January 1640); (2) **Wújiāng Bàoēn Chánsì** 吳江報恩禪寺 (Sōnglíng 松陵); (3) **Hǎimén Sānxiān Guǎnghuì Chánsì** 海門三仙廣慧禪寺 (Yángzhōufǔ, Guǎnglíng 廣陵); (4) **Jiāxīng Dōngtǎ Chánsì** 嘉興東塔禪寺 (Zuìlǐ 檇李); (5) **Níngbō Tiāntóng Jǐngdé Chánsì** 寧波天童景德禪寺 (main Tiāntóngsì abbacy, c. early 1650s, succeeding **Mùyún Tōngmén** [[通門]] per Qián Qiānyì's 1662 preface); (6) **Xiùzhōu Qīzhēnsì** 秀州棲真寺; (7) **Chángzhōu Yíxīng Shànquánsì** 常州宜興善權寺; (8) **Sūzhōu Chángshú Fúchéng Chánsì** 蘇州常熟福城禪寺 (the final abbacy, from which the abbreviated *Fúchéng yǔlù* title derives).

Works: [[KR6q0407]] 《浮石禪師語錄》 (10 juan, Jiāxīng J26 B185, compiled by dharma-heir [[行浚]] Xíngjùn and cut Kāngxī 1 / 1662 with prefaces by **Shǐ Dàchéng** 史大成, **Tán Zhēnmò** 譚貞默, and **Qián Qiānyì** 錢謙益).

Lay-disciple and patron network: **Huáng Lǚsù** 黃履素 (host of the 1639 Sùyuán transmission), **Huáng Ànwēng** 黃闇翁, **Shǐ Dàchéng** 史大成 (1621–1682, 1652 *zhuàngyuán*, formal *dìzǐ*), **Tán Zhēnmò** 譚貞默 (1590–1665, the Hànshān Déqīng lay-disciple and *Guózǐ sīyè*), and **Qián Qiānyì** 錢謙益 (1582–1664; three direct meetings in 1636, 1639, and 1657 per Qián's preface to [[KR6q0407]]).

Sources: *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 67; [[KR6q0407]] juan 9 行實 (the master's 1648 autobiographical life-record); Qián Qiānyì, *Yúshān Méngsǒu* preface to [[KR6q0407]] (1662); Tán Zhēnmò, *quánlù xù* to [[KR6q0407]] (1663-01-23).
