# Tōng-xián 通賢 (Fú-shí Tōng-xián 浮石通賢)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, 31st-generation Línjì descendant. *Zì* **Fú-shí** 浮石 (from Fú-shí-fó 浮石佛, the Buddhist allusion to a drifting stone-statue come ashore); self-deprecating *hào* **Dōng-jiàn lǎo-rén** 東澗老人 ("Old Man of Eastern-Ravine"). Lay surname **Zhào** 趙, lay-name **Zhòng-guāng** 重光 (given at age 6). Native of **Hǔ-lín** 虎林 (Hang-zhōu); the family moved to **Dāng-hú** 當湖 in Píng-hú 平湖, Jiā-xīng-fǔ, when he was an infant. Lifedates **1594–1667** (Wàn-lì 22 – Kāng-xī 6), age 74 per the *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 and cross-confirmed by the master's own 1648 *xíng-shí* in [[KR6q0407]] juan 9 (internal datum: Mìyún sealed him in 1639 at age 47 *suì*, fixing birth to 1594).

Father **Zhào Wén** 趙文, mother **Lǐ-shì** 李氏. Adopted vegetarian discipline at 13; at 19 (1612) broke with parents, went to **Pǔ-tuó-shān** 普陀山, tonsured by **Shào-zōng chán-shī** 紹宗禪師; after three months returned home and grew his hair back to comfort his mother. Formal tonsure at **Wǔ-yuán Pǔ-jìng-ān** 武原普淨庵 by **Chéng-bái fǎ-shī** 乘白法師. Received *śrāmaṇera* precepts (c. 1613–14) from **Yún-qī Zhū-hóng** 雲棲袾宏 (1535–1615) — placing him among the very last ordinand-cohorts of Yún-qī. Scriptural training under **Míng-yé** 明耶. First Chán-training at **Yīng-wō-shān** 鶯窠山 under **Yī-chū chán-shī** 一初禪師 (three years attendant-duty). Full precepts at **Yán-guān Tiān-níng** 鹽官天寧 under **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄 (湛和尚).

First *xǐng* 省 at Yīng-wō-shān on the *Qī xián-nǚ guān shī-tuó-lín* 七賢女觀尸陀林 *gōng-àn*. Final Chán-sealing and *fù-fǎ* 付法 from **Mìyún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 (1566/7–1642) at Mìyún's **Jīn-sù** 金粟 seat on the *xūn-fēng* 薰風 *gōng-àn*, c. 1636–39. Three-year *bì-guān* at Wǔ-yuán on Mìyún's injunction. Formal transmission received at **Huáng Lǚ-sù** 黃履素's **Sù-yuán** 素園 garden in Jiā-hé during Mìyún's 1639 illness-convalescence stay there, on the same day as his dharma-brother **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇 (1595–1652; author of [[KR6q0408]]) — the famous "*wǔ-sè jīn-lián shuāng kāi* 五色金蓮雙開" dream-of-lotuses episode, attested by three independent prefatorial witnesses ([[通賢]] *xíng-shí*, [[錢謙益]] 1662 preface, and Lín-yě's postface).

Eight successive abbacies: (1) **Dāng-hú Qīng-lián-sì** 當湖青蓮寺 (from Chóng-zhēn 12.12.17 = 29 January 1640); (2) **Wú-jiāng Bào-ēn Chán-sì** 吳江報恩禪寺 (Sōng-líng 松陵); (3) **Hǎi-mén Sān-xiān Guǎng-huì Chán-sì** 海門三仙廣慧禪寺 (Yáng-zhōu-fǔ, Guǎng-líng 廣陵); (4) **Jiā-xīng Dōng-tǎ Chán-sì** 嘉興東塔禪寺 (Zuì-lǐ 檇李); (5) **Níng-bō Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé Chán-sì** 寧波天童景德禪寺 (main Tiān-tóng-sì abbacy, c. early 1650s, succeeding **Mù-yún Tōng-mén** [[通門]] per Qián Qiān-yì's 1662 preface); (6) **Xiù-zhōu Qī-zhēn-sì** 秀州棲真寺; (7) **Cháng-zhōu Yí-xīng Shàn-quán-sì** 常州宜興善權寺; (8) **Sū-zhōu Cháng-shú Fú-chéng Chán-sì** 蘇州常熟福城禪寺 (the final abbacy, from which the abbreviated *Fú-chéng yǔ-lù* title derives).

Works: [[KR6q0407]] 《浮石禪師語錄》 (10 juan, Jiā-xīng J26 B185, compiled by dharma-heir [[行浚]] Xíng-jùn and cut Kāng-xī 1 / 1662 with prefaces by **Shǐ Dà-chéng** 史大成, **Tán Zhēn-mò** 譚貞默, and **Qián Qiān-yì** 錢謙益).

Lay-disciple and patron network: **Huáng Lǚ-sù** 黃履素 (host of the 1639 Sù-yuán transmission), **Huáng Àn-wēng** 黃闇翁, **Shǐ Dà-chéng** 史大成 (1621–1682, 1652 *zhuàng-yuán*, formal *dì-zǐ*), **Tán Zhēn-mò** 譚貞默 (1590–1665, the Hàn-shān Dé-qīng lay-disciple and *Guó-zǐ sī-yè*), and **Qián Qiān-yì** 錢謙益 (1582–1664; three direct meetings in 1636, 1639, and 1657 per Qián's preface to [[KR6q0407]]).

Sources: *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 67; [[KR6q0407]] juan 9 行實 (the master's 1648 autobiographical life-record); Qián Qiān-yì, *Yú-shān Méng-sǒu* preface to [[KR6q0407]] (1662); Tán Zhēn-mò, *quán-lù xù* to [[KR6q0407]] (1663-01-23).
