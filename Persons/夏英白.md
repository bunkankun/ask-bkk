# Xià Yīngbái 夏英白

Late-Qīng pediatric physician; author of *Bǎochì tuīná* [[保赤推拿]], one of the three principal Qīng pediatric-massage handbooks named by [[塗蔚生]] Tú Wèishēng in the preface to [[KR3ee053]] *Tuīná juéwēi*. Tú adopts Xià's technical method as the core of his own work, criticizing it however for lacking the diagnostic and pharmacotherapeutic apparatus Tú supplies.
