Gāo Yǐ-yǒng 高以永, *zì* **Zǐ-xiū** 子脩. Late-17th-century lay Buddhist patron and minor official of **Zuì-lǐ** 檇李 (Jiā-xīng-fǔ). Lifedates unknown. Author of the principal preface to [[KR6q0552]] 《伏獅義公禪師語錄》, signed **康熙戊午桂月檇李高以永子脩甫撰** = **1678** (8th lunar month). Catalog dynasty: 清.
