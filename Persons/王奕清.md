Early- to mid-Qīng Hanlin scholar, music theorist, and lexicographer; native of Tài-cāng 太倉 (Jiāngsū). *Zì* Yòufēn 幼芬; *hào* Zhuózhāi 拙齋. Took the *jìnshì* in *Kāngxī* 30 (1691); rose to *Lǐbù shìláng*. Continued the editorial work on the *Wànshòu shèngdiǎn chūjí* 萬壽盛典初集 ([[KR2m0034]]) after Wáng Yuánqí's 1715 death. Author also of the imperial-commissioned *Cí pǔ* 詞譜 (1715), the foundational reference for *cí* prosody, and the *Lǜlǚ zhèngyì hòubiān* 律呂正義後編 (Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng music compilation). CBDB confirms 1665–1737.
