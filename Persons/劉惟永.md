Late-13th-century Yuán 元 Daoist scholar, co-compiler (with [[丁易東]] Dīng Yìdōng and others) of the major Yuán *Dàodé jīng* commentary-anthology [[KR5c0113|DZ 724 *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí yì*]] 道德真經集義 and its introductory [[KR5c0112|DZ 723 *Dà zhǐ*]] 大旨. Postface dated 1299.

**Activity period.** Active 1296–1300. His 1299 postface to DZ 723 (at 3.21b–27a) is the principal datable anchor. He is otherwise little-documented — his position in the Yuán Daoist establishment is reflected in the endorsement of the compilation by [[張嗣成]] Zhāng Yǔcái's colophon (the 41st Celestial Master, d. 1316).

**Collaborative project.** Liú Wéiyǒng's 1299 postface explains that his anthology and that of **Dīng Yìdōng** were **amalgamated** into the received DZ 723–724 compilation. Dīng's own commentary is cited within the anthology under Dīng's *hào* **Shí tán** 石潭. Yú Qīng zhōng's postface mentions a **"new edition"** of the anthology that included his own collaborative commentary with Dīng Yìdōng — testifying to the multi-stage collaborative compilation.

**Dating.** Active 1296–1300. No lifedates recorded. No CBDB record identified.

**Work.** As co-compiler of DZ 723 + DZ 724, Liú Wéiyǒng's principal contribution is editorial rather than independently authored.
