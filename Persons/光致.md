**Guāngzhì** 光致 is the *zì*/*hào* by which the author of the Republican-period paediatric primer *Yòukē gàilùn* 幼科概論 ([[KR3ej003]]) identifies himself in the preface ("光致所講幼科…"). The full personal name has not been securely identified; *Guāngzhì* may be a courtesy name (*zì*) or studio name (*hào*) of an early-twentieth-century physician with at least twenty years of paediatric clinical experience. The text's vocabulary, theoretical framework, and polemical engagement with the *jīngfēng* 驚風 diagnostic category place him in the Republican period (*Mínguó*, 1912–1949).
