# Cónglún 從倫 (Línquán Cónglún 林泉從倫)

Yuán-dynasty Cáodòng-lineage Chán master. *Hào* Línquán 林泉 ("Forest-Spring"), Línquán lǎorén 林泉老人 ("Old-Man of Forest-Spring"). Also called Bàoēn Cónglún 報恩從倫 (for his Bàoēnsì 報恩寺 abbacy). Born 1223; death year unrecorded (active late 13th century).

Principal surviving Kanripo-canonical contribution: the *píng chàng* 評唱 ("evaluation-singing" running-commentary) on [[義青|Tóuzǐ Yìqīng]]'s 投子義青 (1032–1083) *sòng gǔ*, producing the *Línquán lǎorén píng chàng Tóuzǐ Qīng héshàng sòng gǔ Kōng gǔ jí* 林泉老人評唱投子青和尚頌古空谷集 [[KR6q0251]]. This composition parallels Wànsōng Xíngxiù 萬松行秀's earlier *Cóng róng lù* [[KR6q0114]] structure (Wànsōng on Hóngzhì) within the broader Yuán-era Cáodòng *píng chàng* tradition.
