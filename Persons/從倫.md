# Cóng-lún 從倫 (Lín-quán Cóng-lún 林泉從倫)

Yuán-dynasty Cáodòng-lineage Chán master. *Hào* Lín-quán 林泉 ("Forest-Spring"), Lín-quán lǎo-rén 林泉老人 ("Old-Man of Forest-Spring"). Also called Bào-ēn Cóng-lún 報恩從倫 (for his Bào-ēn-sì 報恩寺 abbacy). Born 1223; death year unrecorded (active late 13th century).

Principal surviving Kanripo-canonical contribution: the *píng chàng* 評唱 ("evaluation-singing" running-commentary) on [[義青|Tóu-zǐ Yì-qīng]]'s 投子義青 (1032–1083) *sòng gǔ*, producing the *Lín-quán lǎo-rén píng chàng Tóu-zǐ Qīng héshàng sòng gǔ Kōng gǔ jí* 林泉老人評唱投子青和尚頌古空谷集 [[KR6q0251]]. This composition parallels Wàn-sōng Xíng-xiù 萬松行秀's earlier *Cóng róng lù* [[KR6q0114]] structure (Wàn-sōng on Hóng-zhì) within the broader Yuán-era Cáodòng *píng chàng* tradition.
