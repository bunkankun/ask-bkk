Southern-Sòng Buddhist monk, abbot (*zhùchí* 住持) and "long-resident lecturer" (*chángjiǎng* 長講) of the *Dàzhōngxiángfúsì* 大中祥符寺 in *Miánzhú* 綿竹, *Hànzhōu* 漢州 (modern *Miánzhúshì*, Sìchuān). Carried the additional title *Huáyán hǎiyìn dàshī* 華嚴海印大師 ("Master of the Huáyán Ocean-Seal"), suggesting affiliation with the Huáyán school. Compiler of the *Rúlái guǎngxiào shízhǒng bàoēn dàochǎng yí* 如來廣孝十種報恩道場儀 [[KR6v0088]] (originally titled *Xiàoshùn shègòng bákǔ bàoēn dàochǎng yí* 孝順設供拔苦報恩道場儀). The work cites Sòng *Lóngxìng* 隆興 *chéngxiāng* 丞相 *Yú Yǔnwén* 虞允文 (1110–1174) and the work of *Zōngzé Cíjué chánshī* 宗賾慈覺禪師, placing Sījué in the late 12th century. The work is dated no earlier than *Lóngxìng* 1 (1163, the first year of *Sòng Xiàozōng* 孝宗's reign).
