Southern-Sòng Buddhist monk, abbot (*zhù-chí* 住持) and "long-resident lecturer" (*cháng-jiǎng* 長講) of the *Dà-zhōng-xiángfú-sì* 大中祥符寺 in *Mián-zhú* 綿竹, *Hàn-zhōu* 漢州 (modern *Mián-zhú-shì*, Sìchuān). Carried the additional title *Huá-yán hǎi-yìn dàshī* 華嚴海印大師 ("Master of the Huá-yán Ocean-Seal"), suggesting affiliation with the Huá-yán school. Compiler of the *Rúlái guǎngxiào shí-zhǒng bào-ēn dào-chǎng yí* 如來廣孝十種報恩道場儀 [[KR6v0088]] (originally titled *Xiào-shùn shè-gòng bá-kǔ bào-ēn dào-chǎng yí* 孝順設供拔苦報恩道場儀). The work cites Sòng *Lóng-xìng* 隆興 *chéng-xiāng* 丞相 *Yú Yǔnwén* 虞允文 (1110–1174) and the work of *Zōng-zé Cí-jué chánshī* 宗賾慈覺禪師, placing Sī-jué in the late 12th century. The work is dated no earlier than *Lóng-xìng* 1 (1163, the first year of *Sòng Xiàozōng* 孝宗's reign).
