**Miù Zhòngchún** 繆仲醇 (real name 繆希雍 Miù Xīyōng, *zì* Zhòngchún 仲醇 / Mùtái 慕台, 1546–1627), late-Míng physician of Cháng-Shú 常熟 (Jiāngsū); a leading *bēncǎo* scholar and master of clinical *yīàn*, author of *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng shū* 神農本草經疏 (1625) — the most influential late-Míng *běncǎo* commentary — and *Xiānxǐng zhāi yī xué guǎngbǐ jì* 先醒齋醫學廣筆記 (1613, case histories). Cited as a case-source by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]].
