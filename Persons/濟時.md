# Jìshí 濟時

Mid-Kāngxī-period Buddhist Chán monk; abbot of the **Gǔ Xìng-fú Chán-sì** 古興福禪寺 at **Lóu-dōng** 婁東 (Tài-cāng, Jiāng-sū). Honorific *Huì-gōng* 會公 (commonly *Xìng-fú Huì-gōng* 興福會公). Lifedates unrecorded; floruit Kāngxī 37–38 (1698–1699), when both his major works are dated. Trained at the **Jiè-yú-tíng** 戒𦤎亭 (precepts ordination); studied at the **Líng-yǐn** 靈隱; held abbatial residence at the **Jìng-shān** 徑山 monastery near Hángzhōu before retiring to the Xìng-fú-sì at Tài-cāng. Author of the **Jīn-gāng jīng tí-yào** 金剛提要 (Vajracchedikā commentary, completed *wù-yín* 戊寅 spring = 1698) and the ten-fascicle **Lèngyán-jīng zhèng-jiàn** 楞嚴經正見 ([[KR6j0725]], X16 no. 317), the latter prefaced in *Kāngxī jǐ-mǎo* 己卯 (1699) by his Kūnshān co-disciple Shèng Fú-shēng 盛符升 (then aged 89). The two works represent his "concentrated single-mind" (*zhù-zhóu yī-xīn* 杼軸一心) commentarial method on the two principal Mahāyāna sūtras of his Línjì-school formation.
