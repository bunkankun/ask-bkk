# Jìshí 濟時

Mid-Kāngxī-period Buddhist Chán monk; abbot of the **Gǔ Xìngfú Chánsì** 古興福禪寺 at **Lóudōng** 婁東 (Tàicāng, Jiāngsū). Honorific *Huìgōng* 會公 (commonly *Xìngfú Huìgōng* 興福會公). Lifedates unrecorded; floruit Kāngxī 37–38 (1698–1699), when both his major works are dated. Trained at the **Jièyútíng** 戒𦤎亭 (precepts ordination); studied at the **Língyǐn** 靈隱; held abbatial residence at the **Jìngshān** 徑山 monastery near Hángzhōu before retiring to the Xìngfúsì at Tàicāng. Author of the **Jīngāng jīng tíyào** 金剛提要 (Vajracchedikā commentary, completed *wùyín* 戊寅 spring = 1698) and the ten-fascicle **Lèngyánjīng zhèngjiàn** 楞嚴經正見 ([[KR6j0725]], X16 no. 317), the latter prefaced in *Kāngxī jǐmǎo* 己卯 (1699) by his Kūnshān co-disciple Shèng Fúshēng 盛符升 (then aged 89). The two works represent his "concentrated single-mind" (*zhùzhóu yīxīn* 杼軸一心) commentarial method on the two principal Mahāyāna sūtras of his Línjì-school formation.
