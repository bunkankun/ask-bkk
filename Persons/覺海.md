Juéhǎi 覺海. Personal attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) and recorder (*lù*) of **Sāntǎ Zhǔfēng Yuánfǎ** [[圓法]] (?–1669) at **Jiāxīng Sāntǎ Jǐngdé chánsì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺. Sole compiler of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0532]] 《三塔主峰禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B412. Lifedates unknown.

- - - - -

Several other Buddhist monks also bear the name **Juéhǎi** 覺海**:**
- **Zànyuán Juéhǎi** 蔣山贊元 / 覺海贊元 (DILA A014470, ?–1086), a Northern-Sòng Línjì monk, dharma-heir of **Címíng Chǔyuán** 楚圓 and resident of **Jiǎngshān** 蔣山, friend of Wáng Ānshí.
- **Shì Juéhǎi** 釋覺海 (DILA A032511), Republican-era monk active at Jiǔhuáshān.
