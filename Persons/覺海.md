Jué-hǎi 覺海. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě* 侍者) and recorder (*lù*) of **Sān-tǎ Zhǔ-fēng Yuán-fǎ** [[圓法]] (?–1669) at **Jiā-xīng Sān-tǎ Jǐng-dé chán-sì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺. Sole compiler of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0532]] 《三塔主峰禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B412. Lifedates unknown.

- - - - -

Several other Buddhist monks also bear the name **Jué-hǎi** 覺海**:**
- **Zàn-yuán Jué-hǎi** 蔣山贊元 / 覺海贊元 (DILA A014470, ?–1086), a Northern-Sòng Línjì monk, dharma-heir of **Cí-míng Chǔ-yuán** 楚圓 and resident of **Jiǎng-shān** 蔣山, friend of Wáng Ān-shí.
- **Shì Jué-hǎi** 釋覺海 (DILA A032511), Republican-era monk active at Jiǔ-huá-shān.
