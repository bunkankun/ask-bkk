# Fǎzhèng 法正

Early figure of the Chinese Vinaya transmission, named as the first patriarch (*lìzǔ* 立祖) in the five-patriarch scheme of the *Sìfēn lǜ* tradition presented by [[懷顯]] Huáixiǎn at the close of *juàn xià* of his *Lǜzōng xīnxué míngjù* 律宗新學名句 ([[KR6k0255]], X1107): *Fǎzhèng* 法正 → [[法聰]] Fǎcōng → [[道覆]] Dàofù → [[智首]] Zhìshǒu → [[道宣]] Nánshān (Dàoxuān). Identification with any specific independently-attested figure is uncertain.
