Luó Shìyáo 羅世瑤 (*fl.* mid-late 19th c., 清) is the otherwise undocumented compiler of the *Xíng-jūn fāng-biàn biàn-fāng* 行軍方便便方 ([[KR3ed068]]), a two-*juǎn* military-medical handbook drawn from a wide range of earlier formularies (the *Wǔ-bèi zhì* 武備志, the *Jìng-yún-zhāi* recipe series, and others) and arranged for use on military campaign. The work covers field-rations and famine-substitutes (*pì-gǔ* 辟穀), march-medicine (heat-stroke, frostbite, wound treatment, snakebite), and emergency-treatment of common diseases under field conditions.
