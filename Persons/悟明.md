# Wùmíng 悟明

Sobriquet Huìwēng 晦翁 ("the Obscure Elder"). Southern Sòng Chán monk, Dharma heir in the Línjì 臨濟 — Yángqí 楊岐 — Dàhuì 大慧 line. Active in the late twelfth century; exact lifedates not transmitted. Associated with Huìrìsì 慧日寺 and various other Zhèjiāng monasteries.

Compiler of the *Liándēng huìyào* 聯燈會要 ([[KR6q0008]]), 30 juan, completed 1183 (*Chúnxī* 10) — the fourth of the five canonical Sòng Chán lamp records and the principal historiographical vehicle through which the Dàhuì Zōnggǎo line entered the consolidated Chán genealogical record.
