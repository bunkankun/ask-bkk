**Lù Diàn** 陸佃 (1042–1102), *zì* Nóng shī 農師, was a Northern-Sòng scholar-official, a disciple of Wáng Ān shí 王安石 and author of the important *Pí yǎ* 埤雅 philological work. Author of a commentary on the ***Hé guān zǐ*** 鶡冠子 ([[KR5f0009]]).
