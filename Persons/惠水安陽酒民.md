**Huíshuǐ Ānyáng Jiǔmín** 惠水安陽酒民 (literally, "The Wine-Drinker of Ānyáng in Huíshuǐ") is the pseudonym of the unidentified author of *Qíng Mèng Chè* 情夢柝 ([[KR4k0208]]), a romantic social novel in 20 chapters. The compound pseudonym joins two place names — Huíshuǐ 惠水 (a county in present-day Guìzhōu province) and Ānyáng 安陽 (a city in Hénán province) — with the self-deprecating sobriquet "jiǔmín" 酒民 (wine-drinker, wine-lover). Whether both place-names reflect actual connections of the author, or whether the compound is a purely literary conceit or encoded name, cannot be determined.

The real identity behind this pseudonym has not been established in the standard bibliographies of Qīng fiction. No other works are attributed to it.
