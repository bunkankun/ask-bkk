Xīdí 希迪 (active early 13th c.), Southern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk active at the Huìyīnsì 慧因寺 in Wǔlín 武林 (modern Hángzhōu — the same monastery that had been [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's centre). Per the DILA Authority entry (A000487), he composed the *Zhù huáyán tóng jiào yī chéng cè* 註華嚴同教一乘策 in 1 fascicle in *Jiātài* 嘉泰 2 (1202), and in *Jiādìng* 嘉定 11 (1218) — when he had become abbot of the Huìyīnsì — composed the [[KR6e0079|*Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng jí chéng jì* 華嚴一乘教義分齊章集成記]] (X999). He also compiled the [[KR6e0080|*Píng fù gǔ jì* 評復古記]] (X1000), a critical commentary on [[師會]] Shīhuì's [[KR6e0078|*Fù gǔ jì*]] (X998). His birth and death dates are not preserved.
