**Guō Jīzhōng** 郭稽中 (*fl.* late Northern / early Southern Sòng), Sòng-period Imperial Medical Academy Professor (醫學教授) and "esteemed physician of the time" (時良醫). Co-compiler of the ***Chǎn-yù bǎo-qìng jí*** 產育寶慶集 ([[KR3e0040]], 2 juan), in which Guō appended prescriptions to the 21 *piān* of childbirth-discussions discovered by [[陳師聖]] Chén Shīshèng (= Lǐ Shīshèng 李師聖 per Chén Zhèn-sūn). The *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì* attributes the work to Guō alone. Guō has no further transmitted biographical record.
