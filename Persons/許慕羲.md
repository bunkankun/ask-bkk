Xǔ Mùxī 許慕羲 was a prolific Republican-era popular fiction writer specializing in dynastic historical novels in the *gōngwéi shǐ* 宮闈史 and *gōngtíng yànshǐ* 宮廷豔史 genres. Works in the Kanripo corpus include *Sòngdài Gōngwéi Shǐ* 宋代宮闈史 ([[KR4k0318]]) and *Yuándài Gōngtíng Yànshǐ* 元代宮廷豔史 ([[KR4k0329]]). No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1910s–1940s.
