# Chāozōng Huìfāng 超宗慧方

Northern-Sòng Línjì Huánglóng-line master. Fourth master in the canonical Huánglóng sub-lineage collection (*Huánglóng sìjiā lù* 黃龍四家錄). Lifedates unrecorded.
