**Liú Dàomíng** 劉道明 was a late-13th century Daoist priest at **Wǔ dāng shān** 武當山 (Húběi), the centre of the Zhēn wǔ 真武 cult. Compiler of [[KR5c0362]] *Wǔ dāng fú dì zǒng zhēn jí* 武當福地總真集 (1291) — a major gazetteer-hagiography of the Wǔ dāng sacred site and its associated immortals.
