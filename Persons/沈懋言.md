Mid-Qīng physician, otherwise undocumented. Sole identified work is [[KR3er124]] *Yī-xué yào-zé* 醫學要則 (composed 1743, first printed *Qián-lóng* 59 / 1794), a four-*juǎn* didactic primer in 24 *zé* 則 covering *Nèi-jīng* doctrine, materia medica, pulse-and-channel theory, and miscellaneous-disorder treatment. The generational *Mào* 懋 character is shared with the slightly earlier Qián-lóng-era [[沈懋發]] Shěn Mào-fā ([[KR3er093]] *Kuài-cán piān*); the two may be related but the relationship is not securely established. Not in CBDB.
