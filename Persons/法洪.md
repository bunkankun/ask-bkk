# Fǎhóng 法洪

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[慧性]] Wúmíng Huìxìng (1162–1237); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0312]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000705). Not to be confused with the earlier Fǎhóng of the Jìn 晉, Nán Qí 南齊, or Yuán 元 dynasties carrying the same name.
