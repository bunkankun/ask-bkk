**Gù Yuán-zǎi** 顧元宰 (*fl.* early 18th c.) — biological son of *Xī-hóu gōng* 西侯公 (middle brother of [[顧靖遠]] Gù Sōng-yuán), adopted by Sōng-yuán at age ten when the latter was without natural heirs. Carried his adoptive father's manuscript of the *Gù Sōng-yuán yī jìng* 顧松園醫鏡 ([[KR3er076]]) into print at *Kāng-xī* 58 / 1719 with an autobiographical postface. Not in CBDB.
