**Gù Yuánzǎi** 顧元宰 (*fl.* early 18th c.) — biological son of *Xīhóu gōng* 西侯公 (middle brother of [[顧靖遠]] Gù Sōngyuán), adopted by Sōngyuán at age ten when the latter was without natural heirs. Carried his adoptive father's manuscript of the *Gù Sōngyuán yī jìng* 顧松園醫鏡 ([[KR3er076]]) into print at *Kāngxī* 58 / 1719 with an autobiographical postface. Not in CBDB.
