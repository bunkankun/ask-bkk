Yáng Tíng-hé 楊廷和 (1459–1529), zì *Jiè-fū* 介夫, hào *Shí-zhāi* 石齋, posthumous *Wén-zhōng* 文忠, was the foremost Míng official of the *Zhèng-dé* and early-*Jiā-jìng* transition (c. 1505–1524) and one of the most celebrated Senior Grand Secretaries of the dynasty. From Xīn-dū 新都 (modern Sì-chuān), he passed the *jìn-shì* in *Chéng-huà wù-xū* 成化戊戌 (1478, aged only 19) — the youngest *jìn-shì* of his generation. He served the *Zhèng-dé* court as *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* and *Wén-yuān-gé dà-xué-shì*, then as Senior Grand Secretary (*Huá-gài-diàn dà-xué-shì*) from 1513 to 1524. Father of the great *Jiā-jìng*-period prose-stylist Yáng Shèn 楊慎 (1488–1559).

Yáng's two great political acts were:

1. **The post-Wǔ-zōng succession (1521)**: on Wǔ-zōng's death without an heir, Yáng — as Senior Grand Secretary in charge during the interregnum — orchestrated the selection of the cousin Zhū Hòu-cōng 朱厚熜 (the Jiā-jìng emperor) and personally drafted the *yí-zhào* 遺詔 (Wǔ-zōng's posthumous edict) that announced thirty-two reforms in a single document, including the dissolution of Wǔ-zōng's Tibetan-Buddhist establishments and the dismissal of the imperial-marriage favourites Qián Níng 錢寧 and Jiāng Bīn 江彬.

2. **The Great Ritual Controversy (*Dà-lǐ yì*, 1521–24)**: Yáng led the senior officialdom in opposing the new Jiā-jìng emperor's wish to elevate his biological father Zhū Yòu-yuán 朱祐杬 (Xīng-xiàn-wáng) to the Imperial Temple. Yáng resigned in 1524 in protest, refusing the emperor's demand; his son Yáng Shèn was beaten and exiled to Yún-nán in the same year (the famous *Zuǒ-shùn-mén* incident). Yáng died in 1529 still officially in disgrace but was rehabilitated under the *Long-qìng* emperor with the *Wén-zhōng* posthumous title.

His memorials and political writings are preserved in the [[KR2f0022]] *Yáng Wén-zhōng sān-lù* 楊文忠三錄 in 8 *juàn* (covering *Zhèng-dé* and *Jiā-jìng* periods through 1523), with the *Cí-xiè lù* 辭謝錄 in 4 *juàn* appended (resignation and gratitude memorials). Additional materials are in his collected works *Yáng Wén-zhōng gōng quán-jí* 楊文忠公全集. *Míng shǐ* j. 190 has his biography. CBDB id 68092 (1459–1529).
