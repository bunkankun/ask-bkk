Jiāng Wénjùn 姜文駿 (fl. early-mid 20th c.) was a descendant of the Lóngshā Jiāngshī medical lineage who contributed a family preface to the 1956 reconstruction of [[姜禮]] Jiāng Lǐ's *Fēngláogǔgé sìdà zhèngzhì* ([[KR3eh038]]).
