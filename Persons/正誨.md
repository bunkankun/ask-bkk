Late-Míng scholar-monk, *zì* **Wújì** 無跡 (also **Yǒngdēng** 永燈, his initial monastic name); also recorded as **Wújì Zhènghuì** 無跡正誨 and **Dùmén Zhènghuì** 度門正誨. Native of **Dāngyáng** 當陽 (Húběi), lay surname **Liú** 劉. Tonsured at age ten at **Shíbǎoshān** 石寶山 under the name Yǒngdēng. At twenty, struck by a passage of the *Zīmén jǐngxùn* 緇門警訓 carried by a passing monk, he travelled south to **Jīngnán** 荊南 to seek out **Tiānzhù Héshàng** 天柱和尚 at **Pǔyǎngsì** 普仰寺, who took him as a disciple and renamed him Zhènghuì. Held the abbacies of **Dùménsì** 度門寺 and **Pǔyǎngsì** 普仰寺. Died in Chóngzhēn 1 (1628). Author of [[KR6n0133]] *Bā shí guījǔ lüèshuō* 八識規矩略說 (X55n0891) on the *Bā shí guījǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌, plus a *Zhuāngzǐ zhù* 莊子注 and miscellaneous *shīwén zhū gǎo* 詩文諸稿. His dharma-heir was **Liǎofán Chéngshí** 了凡乘峕. (Sources: 《玉泉寺志》 juan 2; 《新續高僧傳四集》 juan 21; B27n0151_p0183a03; 《疑年錄》 p. 376.)
