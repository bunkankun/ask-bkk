Late-Míng scholar-monk, *zì* **Wú-jì** 無跡 (also **Yǒng-dēng** 永燈, his initial monastic name); also recorded as **Wú-jì Zhèng-huì** 無跡正誨 and **Dù-mén Zhèng-huì** 度門正誨. Native of **Dāng-yáng** 當陽 (Húběi), lay surname **Liú** 劉. Tonsured at age ten at **Shí-bǎo-shān** 石寶山 under the name Yǒng-dēng. At twenty, struck by a passage of the *Zī-mén jǐng-xùn* 緇門警訓 carried by a passing monk, he travelled south to **Jīng-nán** 荊南 to seek out **Tiān-zhù Hé-shàng** 天柱和尚 at **Pǔ-yǎng-sì** 普仰寺, who took him as a disciple and renamed him Zhèng-huì. Held the abbacies of **Dù-mén-sì** 度門寺 and **Pǔ-yǎng-sì** 普仰寺. Died in Chóng-zhēn 1 (1628). Author of [[KR6n0133]] *Bā shí guī-jǔ lüè-shuō* 八識規矩略說 (X55n0891) on the *Bā shí guī-jǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌, plus a *Zhuāngzǐ zhù* 莊子注 and miscellaneous *shī-wén zhū gǎo* 詩文諸稿. His dharma-heir was **Liǎo-fán Chéng-shí** 了凡乘峕. (Sources: 《玉泉寺志》 juan 2; 《新續高僧傳四集》 juan 21; B27n0151_p0183a03; 《疑年錄》 p. 376.)
