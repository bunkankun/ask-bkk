# Liǎoguǎng 了廣

Southern-Sòng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *hào* Zǎnxī 簪谿 (or Zānxī). Abbot of Wànshòusì 萬壽寺; dharma-brother of [[慧暉]] Jìngcí Huìhuī (1097–1183) within the Hóngzhì Zhèngjué / Cáodòng circle. Principal editor of his dharma-brother's six-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0360]]), with *xiàyǔ* commentary supplied by [[明總]] Shíshuāng Míngzǒng. Lifedates unrecorded.
