# Liǎo-guǎng 了廣

Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *hào* Zǎn-xī 簪谿 (or Zān-xī). Abbot of Wàn-shòu-sì 萬壽寺; dharma-brother of [[慧暉]] Jìng-cí Huì-huī (1097–1183) within the Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué / Cáo-dòng circle. Principal editor of his dharma-brother's six-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0360]]), with *xià-yǔ* commentary supplied by [[明總]] Shí-shuāng Míng-zǒng. Lifedates unrecorded.
