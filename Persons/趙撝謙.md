Early-Míng paleographer, original name Zhào Gǔzé 趙古則 (later changed to Huīqiān 撝謙), of Yúyáo 餘姚 (modern Ningbo, Zhejiang). Descendant of the Sòng *Qín Dàohuìwáng* 秦悼惠王. In early Hóngwǔ summoned to participate in compiling the *Hóngwǔ zhèngyùn* 洪武正韻; doctrinally at odds with the consensus, he was sent out as *Zhōngdū Guózǐjiàn bù*; dismissed and went home; later recommended as *Qióngshānxiàn jiàoyù*. His career is in the *Míngshǐ Wényuàn zhuàn*. Two surviving works: the *Liùshū běnyì* [[KR1j0043]] in 12 *juàn* (a paleographic dictionary that reduces Xǔ Shèn's [[許愼]] 540 *Shuōwén* radicals to 360, broadly extending Zhèng Qiáo's [[鄭樵]] *Liùshū lüè* doctrine); and the now-lost *Xuéfàn liù piān*. CBDB lifedates 1351–1395, in agreement with the standard biographical sources.
