Early-Míng paleographer, original name Zhào Gǔ-zé 趙古則 (later changed to Huī-qiān 撝謙), of Yú-yáo 餘姚 (modern Ningbo, Zhejiang). Descendant of the Sòng *Qín Dào-huì-wáng* 秦悼惠王. In early Hóng-wǔ summoned to participate in compiling the *Hóng-wǔ zhèng-yùn* 洪武正韻; doctrinally at odds with the consensus, he was sent out as *Zhōng-dū Guó-zǐ-jiàn bù*; dismissed and went home; later recommended as *Qióng-shān-xiàn jiào-yù*. His career is in the *Míng-shǐ Wén-yuàn zhuàn*. Two surviving works: the *Liù-shū běn-yì* [[KR1j0043]] in 12 *juàn* (a paleographic dictionary that reduces Xǔ Shèn's [[許愼]] 540 *Shuōwén* radicals to 360, broadly extending Zhèng Qiáo's [[鄭樵]] *Liù-shū lüè* doctrine); and the now-lost *Xué-fàn liù piān*. CBDB lifedates 1351–1395, in agreement with the standard biographical sources.
