# 洪瞻祖

Hóng Zhānzǔ (sobriquet Guǎng'ǎn 廣揞)

Late-Míng layman of Húlín 虎林 (Hángzhōu). Wrote the 1618 preface to [[廣伸]] Guǎngshēn's *Jīngāng jīng bēi* ([[KR6c0063]]).
