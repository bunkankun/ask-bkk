Fāngxǐng 方醒, *zì* **Pǔyuè** 普悅 ("Universal Joy"). LínjìYángqí *fǎsì* of **Hānpú Xìngcōng** [[性聰]] (1611–1667). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with co-disciple [[寂空]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0609]] 《明覺聰禪師語錄》 20 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1652.
