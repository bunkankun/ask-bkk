**Zhāng Shíchè** 張時徹 (1500–1577), *zì* **Wéijìng** 維靜, *hào* **Dōngshā** 東沙 (also *Zhīyuán Zhǔrén* 芝園主人, "Master of the Iris Garden"), of Sìmíng 四明 (modern Yīnxiàn 鄞縣 / Ningbo, Zhèjiāng). Mid-Ming official-scholar and prolific writer. *Jìnshì* of *jiā-jìng* 嘉靖 8 (1521). Career: rose through major central-court posts; *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo* 太子太保 (Grand Tutor to the Crown Prince) and senior *Liù-bù* official. Author of the *Míng wén héng* 明文衡 anthology of Ming literary writing, an important Ming military-statecraft compendium, and the *Jíjiù liángfāng* 急救良方 ([[KR3ed034]]). His medical work is a Confucian-philanthropic contribution to popular-medical publication, motivated by the Ming-era *jūnzǐ-gentleman's act of humane governance* topos. CBDB id 28100 (1500–1577). *Míng shǐ* 明史 j. 200 (biography in his patron's context); also covered in *Sìmíng zhī* 四明志 prefectural records.
