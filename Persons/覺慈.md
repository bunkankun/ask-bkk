Juécí 覺慈 (active early 12th century), Northern Sòng Chán monk, disciple of [[慧洪|Juéfàn Huìhóng]] 覺範慧洪 (1071–1128), and the named editor (*biān* 編) of Huìhóng's *Zhì zhèng zhuàn* 智證傳 ([[KR6q0127]], X63 n1235).
