Late Southern-Sòng Tiāntái historian-monk and ritualist; lifedates not preserved (DILA A000502). Style **Dàshí** 大石 ("Great Stone"), with the further sobriquets *Yuèbō* 月波 ("Moon-on-Waves") and *Dōnghú* 東湖 (after his residence). Dharma-heir of *Wúzhù Zōngjìng fǎshī* 無住宗淨法師. Tonsured at *Sìmíng Fúquán* 四明福泉, he then settled at *Dōnghúsì* 東湖寺 in Níngbō (Sìmíng 四明), where he produced two major works: the *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀, the standard 54-fascicle Tiāntái-perspective universal Buddhist history (its self-preface dated *Xiánchún* 5 = 1269; T49n2035 / [[KR6r0012]]), and the present *Fǎjiè shèngfán shuǐlù shènghuì xiūzhāi yíguǐ* ([[KR6k0204]], X1497), the standard liturgical handbook for the *Shuǐlù* mass. A third work attributed to him, the *Zōngmén zūnzǔ yí* 宗門尊祖儀, does not survive. He is described in later gazetteers as widely-read and elegantly-written, with a polemical eye for distinguishing legitimate Tiāntái transmission from rival lineages.
