# Wáng Wénqīng 王文清

*Zì* Tíngjiàn 廷鑑. Native of Níngxiāng 寧鄉 (Húnán). *Jìnshì* of *Yōngzhèng* 2 (1724). Lifedates 1688–1779.

A long-serving Hànlín scholar of the Yōngzhèng–Qiánlóng era. Twice held the directorship of the Yuèlù 嶽麓 Academy in Húnán (1738–1746 and 1762–1773), making him one of the most influential Húnán-school educators of the Qiánlóng period. His *Yuèlù shūyuàn xuéguī* 嶽麓書院學規 became a standard model for academy regulation.

As a Wǔyīngdiàn editor, Wáng Wénqīng was one of the *kǎozhèng* compilers for the *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* ([[KR2a0030]]) in the *Sìkù quánshū*, working alongside [[孫人龍]]. His scholarly works include the *Zhōulǐ huì zhèng* 周禮會箋 and the *Yī Hé jīng yì* 易河經疑.
