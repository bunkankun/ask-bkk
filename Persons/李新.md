**Lǐ Xīn** 李新 (fl. *Yuán-yòu*–*Xuān-hé*, c. 1090–1124), *zì* Yuán-yīng 元應, *hào* Kuà-áo jūshì 跨鼇居士, of Xiān-jǐng 仙井 (Sì-chuān). Per Cháo Gōng-wǔ's *Dú-shū-zhì*: early *jìnshì* of *Yuán-yòu gēng-wǔ* (1090; in fact *gēng-wǔ* was 1090 = *Yuán-yòu* 5). Liú Jīng 劉涇 once recommended him to Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]] — Sū commanded him to compose a *mò-zhú* (ink-bamboo) verse, which he did instantly. End-of-*Yuán-fú* (1100): submitted memorial; demoted, sent to *Suì-zhōu* 遂州 (per Cháo); *Chóng-níng* 2 (1103): per the *Féng yǐn-shì bēi-yīn* the *Kuà-áo jūshì* was registered-and-confined at *Wǔ-xìn*. *Xuān-hé* 5 (1123): held office as *Èr-jùn* (deputy-prefect) of Mào-zhōu 茂州. Personally an ambitious-pliant figure: praised Wáng Ān-shí, praised Cài Jīng, criticised his own earlier outspoken memorials (the *Sìkù* tíyào is severe). Author of *Kuà-áo jí* 跨鼇集 [[KR4d0132]] originally in 50 *juǎn*; *Sìkù* reconstruction from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* is 30 *juǎn*. A late-period *Sì-chuān* literary figure.
