**Lǐ Xīn** 李新 (fl. *Yuányòu*–*Xuānhé*, c. 1090–1124), *zì* Yuányīng 元應, *hào* Kuàáo jūshì 跨鼇居士, of Xiānjǐng 仙井 (Sìchuān). Per Cháo Gōngwǔ's *Dúshūzhì*: early *jìnshì* of *Yuányòu gēngwǔ* (1090; in fact *gēngwǔ* was 1090 = *Yuányòu* 5). Liú Jīng 劉涇 once recommended him to Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]] — Sū commanded him to compose a *mòzhú* (ink-bamboo) verse, which he did instantly. End-of-*Yuánfú* (1100): submitted memorial; demoted, sent to *Suìzhōu* 遂州 (per Cháo); *Chóngníng* 2 (1103): per the *Féng yǐnshì bēiyīn* the *Kuàáo jūshì* was registered-and-confined at *Wǔxìn*. *Xuānhé* 5 (1123): held office as *Èrjùn* (deputy-prefect) of Màozhōu 茂州. Personally an ambitious-pliant figure: praised Wáng Ānshí, praised Cài Jīng, criticised his own earlier outspoken memorials (the *Sìkù* tíyào is severe). Author of *Kuàáo jí* 跨鼇集 [[KR4d0132]] originally in 50 *juǎn*; *Sìkù* reconstruction from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* is 30 *juǎn*. A late-period *Sìchuān* literary figure.
