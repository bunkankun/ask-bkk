**Wú Shìchāng** 吳世昌 (*fl.* early Qīng, late Kāngxī era, ca. 1700–1730), Qīng physician known principally for his *Qífāng lèibiān* 奇方類編 ([[KR3ed104]]) — a 27-category formulary of 800+ recipes for "extraordinary" or unusual clinical presentations. The work was current by the time of Wáng Yuǎn's 1718 preface (which is appended to it as the *Qíjí fāng* introduction). Biographical detail is scant; Wú does not appear in CBDB and is known only from the title-line of his work and its catalogue traces. The work was reprinted by the Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè (Beijing, 2004).
