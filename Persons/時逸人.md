**Shí Yìrén** 時逸人 (1896–1966), Republican-era physician and medical educator, native of Wújìn 武進 (Jiāngsū). Author of *Wēnbìng quánshū* 溫病全書, a Republican-era warm-disease handbook later reworked by [[沈嘯谷]] Shěn Xiàogǔ and used as the warm-disease textbook at the Húnán State Medical College in the mid-1930s. Mentioned in [[王德宣]] Wáng Déxuān's *Wēnbìng zhèngzōng* ([[KR3eg012]]).
