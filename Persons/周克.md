# Zhōu Kè 周克 / Zhōu Kè-fù 周克復

An early-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar, identified in the colophons of his work as **Zhōu Kè-fù** 周克復 (the *fù* 復 element of the personal name is sometimes dropped in catalog references). Lifedates not preserved with precision; he was active in the early to mid Qīng (probably late seventeenth / early eighteenth century). His major surviving work is the **《淨土晨鐘》** *Jìng-tǔ chén-zhōng* [[KR6p0091]] (X1172, 10 *juǎn*) — a substantial ten-*juǎn* Pure Land anthology described in the colophon as a *fù-zuǎn* 復纂 ("re-compilation"), suggesting the work draws on and revises an earlier compilation. The work is one of the principal early-Qīng lay-compiled Pure Land anthologies. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
