Northern-Sòng official, son of Yú Jìng [[余靖]] (1000–1064), of *Sháo-zhōu Qǔ-jiāng* 韶州曲江. Held the office of *Tún-tián yuán-wài-láng* 屯田員外郎. Edited his father's collection *Wǔ-xī jí* [[KR4d0023]] (20 *juǎn*) shortly after Yú Jìng's death in 1064, with preface by Zhōu Yuán. CBDB id 536867 (no lifedates recorded; index year 1027 — likely a flawed CBDB record, more probably born ca. 1030–1040 to be a working *yuán-wài-láng* in the 1060s).
