**Xiāo Jīng** 蕭京 (*zì* Wànyú 萬輿, *hào* Tōngyǐnzǐ 通隱子, *fl.* late Wànlì 萬曆 – Chóngzhēn 崇禎, Mǐnzhōng 閩中 / Fújiàn) — late-Míng physician of Jìnān 晉安 (Fuzhou region) and author of the polemical medical treatise *XuānQí jiùzhèng lùn* 軒岐救正論 ([[KR3er054]]), the self-preface to which is dated *Chóngzhēn jiǎshēn* 崇禎甲申 = 1644. Xiāo died not long after completing the work, which was carried into print by his son [[蕭震]] Xiāo Zhèn in the early Qīng. Not in CBDB.
