**Xiāo Jīng** 蕭京 (*zì* Wàn-yú 萬輿, *hào* Tōng-yǐn-zǐ 通隱子, *fl.* late Wàn-lì 萬曆 – Chóng-zhēn 崇禎, Mǐn-zhōng 閩中 / Fú-jiàn) — late-Míng physician of Jìn-ān 晉安 (Fuzhou region) and author of the polemical medical treatise *Xuān-Qí jiù-zhèng lùn* 軒岐救正論 ([[KR3er054]]), the self-preface to which is dated *Chóng-zhēn jiǎ-shēn* 崇禎甲申 = 1644. Xiāo died not long after completing the work, which was carried into print by his son [[蕭震]] Xiāo Zhèn in the early Qīng. Not in CBDB.
