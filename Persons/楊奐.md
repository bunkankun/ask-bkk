Jīn-Yuán transition scholar, historian, and senior Mongol-period administrator. Alternate name **Zhīzhāng** 知章; *zì* **Huànrán** 煥然; *hào* **Zǐyáng** 紫陽 / **Zǐyánggé** 紫陽閣. Native of **Fèngtiān** 奉天 in **Qiánzhōu** 乾州 (modern Shǎnxī). 1186 (Jīn Shìzōng Dàdìng 26) – 1255.

**Career.** Repeatedly successful in the Jīn-period autumn (*xiāng*) examination — four times selected — but unable to pass the palace (*chūn*) examination. After the Mongol conquest of Jīn (1234), recommended to the Mongol court by **Yēlǜ Chǔcái** 耶律楚材 (1190–1244, the great Mongol Khitan administrator); appointed *Hénánlù zhēngshōu suìkè suǒ zhǎngguān* (Hénán Circuit Tax-Collection Office Chief) concurrent *Liánfǎngshǐ* 廉訪使. Retired after ten years in office.

**Scholarship.** Notable as one of the principal northern Confucian scholars of the early Mongol administration. By his own *Bìtóng jì* (Record of the Arm-Boy) listed:
- *Huánshān qiánjí* 還山前集 (81 *juàn*)
- *Huánshān hòují* 還山後集 (20 *juàn*)
- *Jìnjiàn* 近鑑 (30 *juàn*)
- *Hánzǐ* 韓子 (10 *juàn* — on Hán Yù)
- *Gàiyán* 槩言 (25 *piān*)
- *Yànzuǎn* 硯纂 (8 *juàn*)
- *Běijiànjì* 北見記 (3 *juàn*)
- *Zhèngtǒngjì* 正統記 (60 *juàn* — historiographical work on dynastic-legitimacy)

The *Yuánshǐ* j. 153 cites only *Huánshān jí* 60 *juàn*; Yuán Hǎowèn's *Shéndào bēi* cites 120 *juàn*. Of this enormous corpus, only fragments survive in the 2-*juàn* *Huánshān yígǎo* compiled by Sòng Tǐngzuǒ in Míng Jiājìng *chū*.

**Notable contributions.**
- The *Biàn gùgōng jì* 汴故宫記 (Record of the Old Palace at Biàn) is a major topographical-historical source on the Northern Sòng Imperial Palace at Kāifēng.
- His *Letter to Yáo Gōngmào* 與姚公茂書 discussing Zhū Xī's *Jiālǐ* 家禮 spirit-tablet form is an early evidential treatise on Sòng-period ritual.
- His *Dōngyóu jì* 東遊記 (Record of the Eastern Journey) describes the Kǒnglíng (Confucius-Forest) at Qūfù.
- Notably, Yáng anticipated Zhū Xī's *Tōngjiàn gāngmù* succession-judgment by independently rejecting Sīmǎ Guāng's Hàn-Wèi *zhèngrùn* on Shǔ-Hàn-legitimist grounds — though he abandoned his own *Hànshū* revision project upon learning of Zhū Xī's prior *Gāngmù* (recorded by Táo Zōngyí's *Chuògēng lù*).
