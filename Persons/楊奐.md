Jīn-Yuán transition scholar, historian, and senior Mongol-period administrator. Alternate name **Zhī-zhāng** 知章; *zì* **Huàn-rán** 煥然; *hào* **Zǐ-yáng** 紫陽 / **Zǐ-yáng-gé** 紫陽閣. Native of **Fèng-tiān** 奉天 in **Qián-zhōu** 乾州 (modern Shǎn-xī). 1186 (Jīn Shì-zōng Dà-dìng 26) – 1255.

**Career.** Repeatedly successful in the Jīn-period autumn (*xiāng*) examination — four times selected — but unable to pass the palace (*chūn*) examination. After the Mongol conquest of Jīn (1234), recommended to the Mongol court by **Yē-lǜ Chǔ-cái** 耶律楚材 (1190–1244, the great Mongol Khitan administrator); appointed *Hé-nán-lù zhēng-shōu suì-kè suǒ zhǎng-guān* (Hé-nán Circuit Tax-Collection Office Chief) concurrent *Lián-fǎng-shǐ* 廉訪使. Retired after ten years in office.

**Scholarship.** Notable as one of the principal northern Confucian scholars of the early Mongol administration. By his own *Bì-tóng jì* (Record of the Arm-Boy) listed:
- *Huán-shān qián-jí* 還山前集 (81 *juàn*)
- *Huán-shān hòu-jí* 還山後集 (20 *juàn*)
- *Jìn-jiàn* 近鑑 (30 *juàn*)
- *Hán-zǐ* 韓子 (10 *juàn* — on Hán Yù)
- *Gài-yán* 槩言 (25 *piān*)
- *Yàn-zuǎn* 硯纂 (8 *juàn*)
- *Běi-jiàn-jì* 北見記 (3 *juàn*)
- *Zhèng-tǒng-jì* 正統記 (60 *juàn* — historiographical work on dynastic-legitimacy)

The *Yuán-shǐ* j. 153 cites only *Huán-shān jí* 60 *juàn*; Yuán Hǎo-wèn's *Shén-dào bēi* cites 120 *juàn*. Of this enormous corpus, only fragments survive in the 2-*juàn* *Huán-shān yí-gǎo* compiled by Sòng Tǐng-zuǒ in Míng Jiā-jìng *chū*.

**Notable contributions.**
- The *Biàn gù-gōng jì* 汴故宫記 (Record of the Old Palace at Biàn) is a major topographical-historical source on the Northern Sòng Imperial Palace at Kāi-fēng.
- His *Letter to Yáo Gōng-mào* 與姚公茂書 discussing Zhū Xī's *Jiā-lǐ* 家禮 spirit-tablet form is an early evidential treatise on Sòng-period ritual.
- His *Dōng-yóu jì* 東遊記 (Record of the Eastern Journey) describes the Kǒng-líng (Confucius-Forest) at Qū-fù.
- Notably, Yáng anticipated Zhū Xī's *Tōng-jiàn gāng-mù* succession-judgment by independently rejecting Sī-mǎ Guāng's Hàn-Wèi *zhèng-rùn* on Shǔ-Hàn-legitimist grounds — though he abandoned his own *Hàn-shū* revision project upon learning of Zhū Xī's prior *Gāng-mù* (recorded by Táo Zōng-yí's *Chuò-gēng lù*).
