# Dīng Kuān 丁寬

*Zì* Zǐxiāng 子襄. Early Western Hàn 西漢 *Yì* master, native of Liáng 梁 (modern Hénán). Studied under [[田何]] Tián Hé alongside the principal heads of the Hàn *Yì* schools. According to the *Hànshū Rúlín zhuàn* 漢書·儒林傳 he authored an *Yì shuō* 易說 in eight *piān* 篇 and contributed to the transmission of the *Yì* through his disciple Tián Wángsūn 田王孫, who in turn taught [[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ, Shī Chóu 施讎, and Liáng Qiū Hè 梁丘賀. His work is lost; some bibliographies (the *Qīlù* of [[阮孝緒]] Ruǎn Xiàoxù) confusingly attributed the *Zǐxià Yìzhuàn* ([[KR1a0002]]) to him.
