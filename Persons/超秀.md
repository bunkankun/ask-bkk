# Chāoxiù 超秀

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[謐|Èryǐn Xíngmì]] 二隱行謐 (c. 1606–1665) in the Mìyún → Línyě line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0430]] 《二隱謐禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B212) alongside [[超巨]] Chāojù. Based at **Húguǎng Jīngzhōu Jiānglíng Shuǐyuè Chányuàn** 湖廣荊州江陵水月禪院 — the Jīngnán residence of **Zhīyán chánshī** 芝巖禪師 who brought Xíngmì's manuscript to the Manchu prince **Chání** 察尼 for the 1678 cutting-patronage.

The 超-generation character is standard for the 34th-generation Línjì tier. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
