# Chāo-xiù 超秀

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[謐|Èr-yǐn Xíng-mì]] 二隱行謐 (c. 1606–1665) in the Mìyún → Lín-yě line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0430]] 《二隱謐禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B212) alongside [[超巨]] Chāo-jù. Based at **Hú-guǎng Jīng-zhōu Jiāng-líng Shuǐ-yuè Chán-yuàn** 湖廣荊州江陵水月禪院 — the Jīng-nán residence of **Zhī-yán chán-shī** 芝巖禪師 who brought Xíng-mì's manuscript to the Manchu prince **Chá-ní** 察尼 for the 1678 cutting-patronage.

The 超-generation character is standard for the 34th-generation Línjì tier. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
