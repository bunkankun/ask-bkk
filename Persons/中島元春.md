# 中島元春 Zhōngdǎo Yuánchūn / Nakajima Genshun

**Nakajima Genshun** 中島元春, mid-Edo Japanese kanpō physician; member of the acupoint-transmission lineage from [[宮本春仙]] Miyamoto Shunsen through [[藤井貞三]] Fujii Teizō and [[良益]] Ryōeki to [[小阪營升]] Kosaka Genyū's *Jīngxué zuǎnyào* 經穴纂要 ([[KR3ee043]]).
