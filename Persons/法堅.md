Fǎjiān 法堅 (also written 釋法堅 *Shì Fǎjiān*; flourished late fourth to early fifth century) was a monk active during the Western Qín 乞伏秦 (385–431 CE), the small dynastic state of the Qǐfú clan in the Gānsù-Qīnghǎi region. He is recorded as the translator of [[KR6i0125]] *Ānán fēnbié jīng* 阿難分別經 (T495) and a small number of other short sūtras. His monastic biography is otherwise poorly documented; the Liáng-period *Chū sānzàng jì jí* notes him only by name. He is one of several minor translators of the Sixteen Kingdoms 十六國 period whose work survived in the canonical corpus through the Sui-Tang catalog system.
