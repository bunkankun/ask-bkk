# 思孝

Sīxiào 思孝 (*fl.* mid-11th century), Liáo 遼-dynasty monk resident at Hǎiyún-sì 海雲寺 on Juéhuā-dǎo 覺花島 (in the Bóhǎi-wān 渤海灣 off the Liáodōng coast). He composed by imperial command the preface (奉詔撰) to the *Dàzàng jiào zhū fó púsà mínghào jí xù* 大藏教諸佛菩薩名号集序 prefixed to the 22-fascicle *Yīqiè fó púsà míng jí* 一切佛菩薩名集 ([[KR6i0028]]) compiled by his contemporary [[非濁]] Fēizhuó.
