Southern-Sòng Daoist-scholar commentator, *hào* Xízhāi xiānshēng 息齋先生; obtained the *jìnshì* 進士 degree in the Qiándào 乾道 2 year (1166) under Xiàozōng 孝宗. His CBDB notice (c_personid 22635) remarks that the degree is not listed in the *Sìchuān tōngzhì* 四川通志, though Zhāng Shànyuān's 張善淵 preface to Lǐ's own commentary (DZ 3) explicitly locates the commentary's first printing in Western Shǔ 西蜀 (Sichuan), so some Sichuan connection — whether by native place, appointment, or retirement — is secure. No office or teacher is recorded. He composed the paragraph-by-paragraph commentary [[KR5a0003|Yuánshǐ shuō xiāntiān dàodé jīng zhùjiě 元始說先天道德經註解]] (DZ 3), printed in Shǔ in the late twelfth century; the Shǔ blocks were lost in the thirteenth century and the commentary survived only via a single manuscript recovered by Xiè Túnán 謝圖南 in the Bǎoyòu 寳祐 era (1253–1258) and re-engraved, eventually, by Zhāng Shànyuān *fl.* 1280–1294. No other works by Lǐ Jiāmóu survive in the Kanripo corpus. A homonymous 李嘉謀 (CBDB id 618812) without dates exists but cannot be identified with the commentator of DZ 3.
