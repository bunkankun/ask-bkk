**Zāng Dádé** 臧達德 (*hào* **Gōngsān** 公三, b. 1750 by self-statement in his 1814 preface "at age sixty-five"), Qīng physician of **Zhūchéngxiàn** 諸城縣 (Shāndōng). Third-generation family-tradition practitioner, associated with the **Wǔliánshān** 五蓮山 medical practice of eastern Shāndōng; signed his autobiographical preface to **[[KR3eq030]]** *Lǚshuāng jí* 履霜集 at the *Wànghǎi shūlóu* 望海書樓 (Watching-the-Sea Library) on Wǔliánshān in 1814. His *Lǚshuāng jí* is a useful regional-medical document for eastern Shāndōng practice — a tradition underrepresented in the Lower-Yangtze-dominated Qīng medical literature. The local friend **Wáng Hèlǚ** 王鶴侶 ("Master of the Crane Companion") encouraged him to publish his family methods. Not in CBDB.
