**Zāng Dá-dé** 臧達德 (*hào* **Gōng-sān** 公三, b. 1750 by self-statement in his 1814 preface "at age sixty-five"), Qīng physician of **Zhū-chéng-xiàn** 諸城縣 (Shāndōng). Third-generation family-tradition practitioner, associated with the **Wǔ-lián-shān** 五蓮山 medical practice of eastern Shāndōng; signed his autobiographical preface to **[[KR3eq030]]** *Lǚ-shuāng jí* 履霜集 at the *Wàng-hǎi shū-lóu* 望海書樓 (Watching-the-Sea Library) on Wǔ-lián-shān in 1814. His *Lǚ-shuāng jí* is a useful regional-medical document for eastern Shāndōng practice — a tradition underrepresented in the Lower-Yangtze-dominated Qīng medical literature. The local friend **Wáng Hè-lǚ** 王鶴侶 ("Master of the Crane Companion") encouraged him to publish his family methods. Not in CBDB.
