**Jī Zhìzhēn** 姬志真 (1193–1268), *hào* Yún shān 雲山, was a Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist, author of [[KR5e0042]] *Yún shān jí* 雲山集 — preface by Wáng È 王鶚 (1190–1273), dated 1265.
