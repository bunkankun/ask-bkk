Shǐ Jǐn 史謹 (active c. 1360s–1400s), *zì* **Gōng-jǐn** 公謹, native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu prefecture). Early-Hóngwǔ official: demoted on an unstated charge and banished to **Yún-nán** 雲南 (likely in or after the Hóngwǔ 15 / 1382 incorporation of Yún-nán under **Mù Yīng** 沐英); recommended back as *Yīng-tiān-fǔ tuī-guān* 應天府推官; demoted to *Xiāng-yīn xiàn chéng* 湘陰縣丞; dismissed and retired to Jīn-líng (Nán-jīng) as a sojourner. Close friend of the early-Míng anthologist **Wāng Huán** 偶桓 (compiler of *Qián-kūn qīng-qì jí* 乾坤清氣集). His verse collection [[KR4e0062]] *Dú-zuì-tíng jí* 獨醉亭集 contains his Yún-nán-exile verse (notably the *Yè Qián-níng-wáng miào shī* 謁黔寧王廟詩 — visit to Mù Yīng's posthumously-titled temple after 1392), Yīng-tiān verse, and verse-exchanges with **Gù Ā-yīng** 顧阿瑛 of the late-Yuán Sū-zhōu *Yù-shān cǎo-táng* circle. CBDB id 34409 records the name and Míng dynasty but no dates.
