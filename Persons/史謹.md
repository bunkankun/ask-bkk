Shǐ Jǐn 史謹 (active c. 1360s–1400s), *zì* **Gōngjǐn** 公謹, native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu prefecture). Early-Hóngwǔ official: demoted on an unstated charge and banished to **Yúnnán** 雲南 (likely in or after the Hóngwǔ 15 / 1382 incorporation of Yúnnán under **Mù Yīng** 沐英); recommended back as *Yīngtiānfǔ tuīguān* 應天府推官; demoted to *Xiāngyīn xiàn chéng* 湘陰縣丞; dismissed and retired to Jīnlíng (Nánjīng) as a sojourner. Close friend of the early-Míng anthologist **Wāng Huán** 偶桓 (compiler of *Qiánkūn qīngqì jí* 乾坤清氣集). His verse collection [[KR4e0062]] *Dúzuìtíng jí* 獨醉亭集 contains his Yún-nán-exile verse (notably the *Yè Qiánníngwáng miào shī* 謁黔寧王廟詩 — visit to Mù Yīng's posthumously-titled temple after 1392), Yīngtiān verse, and verse-exchanges with **Gù Āyīng** 顧阿瑛 of the late-Yuán Sūzhōu *Yùshān cǎotáng* circle. CBDB id 34409 records the name and Míng dynasty but no dates.
