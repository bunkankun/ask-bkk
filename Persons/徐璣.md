Xú Jǐ 徐璣 (1162–1214, *zì* Wényuān 文淵, alternate *zì* Zhìzhōng 致中, *hào* Língyuān 靈淵 — also written Líng囦 in archaic form), of *Yǒngjiā* 永嘉 in *Wēnzhōu*. Second-senior member of the *Yǒngjiā Sìlíng* 永嘉四靈 (with [[徐照]], Wēng Juàn 翁卷, Zhào Shīxiù 趙師秀). Held office as *Jiàn'ān zhǔbù*, *Lóngxī chéng*, *Wǔdāng zhǎng*, *Tàishǐ lìng*; died at 59 in *Jiādìng* 7 (1214). Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí* erroneously says he never held office; the *Sìkù* editors correct this from internal evidence in his collection. Hóng Mài [[洪邁]] commended his line "*Clouds returning, the green peaks' rain just stopped; / Flowers lying on the green moss — spring already over*". CBDB id 44994. Author of [[KR4d0298]] *Èrwēitíng shījí* (also called *Quánshān jí*).
