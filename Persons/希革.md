# Xīgé 希革

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xīsǒu Shàotán (d. by 1279); co-editor of both the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]) and the extended *guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0323]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
