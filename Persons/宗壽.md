Wúliàng Zōngshòu 無量宗壽 (also Wúliàng Chóngshòu 無量崇壽, with 崇壽 and 宗壽 as equivalent readings; lifedates unrecorded), Southern-Sòng Línjì 臨濟宗 Yángqí-branch 楊岐派 Chán monk; *hào* Wúliàng 無量 ("Immeasurable") or Wúliàngshòu 無量壽 ("Immeasurable-Life"). Native of Fǔzhōu 撫州 (modern Jiāngxī). Held the Ruìyán 瑞岩 abbacy (hence the alternate name Ruìyán Shòu 瑞岩壽).

Known principally through his authorship of the *Rù zhòng rì yòng* 入眾日用 ([[KR6q0137]], X63 n1246, 1 juan) — a short Southern-Sòng monastic daily-practice manual for novice monks, circulating also under the title *Wúliàngshòu chánshī rì yòng xiǎo qīng guī* 無量壽禪師日用小清規. No other major works transmitted.

Per DILA A000596.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0137]] only.

# Zōngshòu 宗壽

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xīsǒu Shàotán (d. by 1279); co-editor of both recensions of his master's sayings — the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]) and the extended *guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0323]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Whether identical with the above Wúliàng Zōngshòu cannot be confirmed.
