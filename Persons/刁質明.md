**Diāo Zhìmíng** 刁質明 (*zì* *Shǒuyú* 守愚; late-Qīng / early Republican), throat-medicine (*hóukē* 喉科) physician of *Wàishā* 外沙 (outer-sandbank district), *Chóngmíng* 崇明 (Yangtze-estuary island, now a district of Shànghǎi). Son of [[刁步忠]] Diāo Bùzhōng, from whom he received the family throat-medicine tradition. Practised at the *Yánshòu yīshì* 延壽醫室 (Hall of Extended Life). Studied as a youth under "*Gāo fūzǐ*" 高夫子 together with *Yáng Hànchūn* 楊漢春 (later principal of the Chóngmíng Sixth Lower Primary School), and was the teacher of *Shěn Guānghàn* 沈光漢. Author of the *Hóukē jiāxùn* 喉科家訓 ([[KR3em038]]), the four-*juǎn* family-tradition throat-medicine handbook he redacted in 1918 (preface dated *Mínguó* 7, 1918) from his father's manuscript notes, and which was printed in 1924 (*Mínguó* 13) under the auspices of the *Sānsān yīshè* 三三醫社. Catalog meta lists his father *Diāo Bùzhōng* as author and assigns the work to the Qīng; the redacted received recension is, however, his early-Republican production. CBDB has no entry.
