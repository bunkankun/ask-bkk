**Diāo Zhì-míng** 刁質明 (*zì* *Shǒu-yú* 守愚; late-Qīng / early Republican), throat-medicine (*hóu-kē* 喉科) physician of *Wài-shā* 外沙 (outer-sandbank district), *Chóng-míng* 崇明 (Yangtze-estuary island, now a district of Shàng-hǎi). Son of [[刁步忠]] Diāo Bù-zhōng, from whom he received the family throat-medicine tradition. Practised at the *Yán-shòu yī-shì* 延壽醫室 (Hall of Extended Life). Studied as a youth under "*Gāo fū-zǐ*" 高夫子 together with *Yáng Hàn-chūn* 楊漢春 (later principal of the Chóng-míng Sixth Lower Primary School), and was the teacher of *Shěn Guāng-hàn* 沈光漢. Author of the *Hóu-kē jiā-xùn* 喉科家訓 ([[KR3em038]]), the four-*juǎn* family-tradition throat-medicine handbook he redacted in 1918 (preface dated *Mín-guó* 7, 1918) from his father's manuscript notes, and which was printed in 1924 (*Mín-guó* 13) under the auspices of the *Sān-sān yī-shè* 三三醫社. Catalog meta lists his father *Diāo Bù-zhōng* as author and assigns the work to the Qīng; the redacted received recension is, however, his early-Republican production. CBDB has no entry.
