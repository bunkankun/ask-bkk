**Shī Liáng** 施梁 (*fl.* late Wànlì 萬曆 – Chóngzhēn 崇禎, Wúxīng 吳興 / Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng) — late-Míng official whose self-supplied titulature on the postface to *Jiǎnmíng yī gòu* ([[KR3er056]]) lists his postings as *jìnshì*, *Jiāngxī dào jiānchá yùshǐ* 江西道監察御史, *xúnàn Zhílì dūlǐ Chánglú jiān kè hédào yìchuán* 巡按直隸督理長蘆監課河道驛傳, with jurisdictional oversight of Shāndōng, Hénán and the Kāi-Guī (Kāifēng) region, and prior service as imperial inspector of the Imperial City, Lúgōu Bridge, the city-wall works of the Guǎngqú Gate. He wrote the printing-postface of Sūn Zhìhóng's *Jiǎnmíng yī gòu* c. 1629. Not in CBDB.
