**Shī Liáng** 施梁 (*fl.* late Wàn-lì 萬曆 – Chóng-zhēn 崇禎, Wú-xīng 吳興 / Hú-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng) — late-Míng official whose self-supplied titulature on the postface to *Jiǎn-míng yī gòu* ([[KR3er056]]) lists his postings as *jìnshì*, *Jiāng-xī dào jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 江西道監察御史, *xún-àn Zhí-lì dū-lǐ Cháng-lú jiān kè hé-dào yì-chuán* 巡按直隸督理長蘆監課河道驛傳, with jurisdictional oversight of Shān-dōng, Hé-nán and the Kāi-Guī (Kāifēng) region, and prior service as imperial inspector of the Imperial City, Lú-gōu Bridge, the city-wall works of the Guǎng-qú Gate. He wrote the printing-postface of Sūn Zhì-hóng's *Jiǎn-míng yī gòu* c. 1629. Not in CBDB.
