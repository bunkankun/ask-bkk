**Wēng Shìrú** 翁式如 (late Qīng, fl. 1885), intermediary between [[吳幼筠]] Wú Yòuyún and [[曹秉綱]] Cáo Bǐnggāng in the transmission of the Bamboo-Grove gynecology manuscript that became the *Nǚkē mìzhǐ* 女科秘旨 ([[KR3ei022]]).
