# Pān Wěi 潘緯

Sòng-period scholar; floruit *ca.* 1181–1182 by CBDB record (cbdbId 97736). His one substantial scholarly contribution is the *yīn-yì* 音義 (phonetic-and-meaning glosses) layer of the *Liǔ Hé-dōng jí zhù* 柳河東集注 [[KR4c0049]] — commissioned by the *Qián-shān jùn-zhāi* 灊山郡齋 (Sòng prefectural-school printing operation) several months before the SBCK *xù* was written, in collaboration with Tóng Zōng-shuō [[童宗說]] (*zhù-shì*) and Zhāng Dūn-yí [[張敦頤]] (*yīn-biàn*). CBDB has no other biographical detail.
