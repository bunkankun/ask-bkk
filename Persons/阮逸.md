Ruǎn Yì 阮逸 (fl. 1028–1063), *zì* Tiānyǐn 天隱, was a Northern Sòng official of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Fújiàn) and a court music-classical specialist. *Jìnshì* of Tiānshèng 5 (1027); rose under Rénzōng to *Tài-cháng bóshì* 太常博士 and supervisor of the *Lǜ-lǚ* 律呂 reform under the imperial commission of Sòng Qí 宋祁 and Hú Yuàn 胡瑗 (his close collaborator). He is the standard premodern commentator on Wáng Tōng's *Zhōng shuō* 中說 (KR3a0014), with his commentary preserved in the SKQS-base. The *Zhōng shuō* commentary is reliably his; the closing imperially-presented *Wén-Zhōng-zǐ shì-jiā* and Yuán-fēng additions are not. He also compiled the music-treatise *Huáng-yòu xīn yuè-tú jì* 皇祐新樂圖記 with Hú Yuàn (1052), which is included in the SKQS music section. CBDB id 14215, no dates confirmed; conventional fl. ca. 1028–1063 from his Sòng dating.
