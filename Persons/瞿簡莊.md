Qú Jiǎnzhuāng 瞿簡莊 (fl. early-mid 20th c.) was a Republican Chinese-medicine figure who contributed a preface to the 1956 reconstruction of [[姜禮]] Jiāng Lǐ's *Fēngláogǔgé sìdà zhèngzhì* ([[KR3eh038]]).
