# 胡泉

**Hú Quán** (*fl.* early 19th c.), Gāoyóu literatus who funded the *Dàoguāng* 16 / *bǐngshēn* 丙申 (1836) *editio princeps* of [[KR3el010]] *Diēsǔn miàofāng* of [[異遠真人]] Yìyuǎn Zhēnrén, edited by his fellow-townsman [[孫應科]] Sūn Yìngkē. His undated *shūhòu* 書後 postscript dated the ninth month of that year is preserved in the printed edition. No further biographical record located.
