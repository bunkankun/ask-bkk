Yuán-dynasty Buddhist lay scholar (DILA: 否, "not a monk"), native of **Sì-míng** 四明 (Níng-bō). Lifedates not preserved. His own colophon to *Jīn-gāng shū kē-shì* 金剛疏科釋 ([[KR6c0054]]) is dated **Zhì-yuán *jiǎ-wǔ*** 至元甲午 = **1294** (the last year of Khubilai Khan's Zhì-yuán reign), and identifies him as a 大居士 ("great householder," lay donor) attached to the **Nán-hú** 南湖 Tiāntái community at Níng-bō under the master **Kě-míng** 可明. Two extant works: *Jīn-gāng shū kē-shì* ([[KR6c0054]]), an interleaved restatement of Tiāntái Zhì-yǐ's *Jīn-gāng bān-ruò shū*; and the *Fǎ-huá jīng kē-zhù* 法華經科註 in 8 juan (X31 no. 606). Both works embody the late-Sòng / Yuán Tiāntái lay-scholarly genre of *jiāng shū rù jīng* 將疏入經 — interleaving received commentaries with the sūtra root-text for ease of study.
