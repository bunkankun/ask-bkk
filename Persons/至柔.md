# Zhì-róu 至柔

Yuán-dynasty Chán *cān-xué mén-rén* 參學門人 of [[清珙]] Shí-wū Qīng-gǒng (1272–1352); principal named editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0332]]) in the Jiāxīng-zàng recension. Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000402).
