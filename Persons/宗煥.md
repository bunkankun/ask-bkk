# Zōnghuàn 宗煥

Late-Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[文明]] Yuèjiàn Wénmíng; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0325]]). Also named among the co-editors of [[心月]] Shíxī Xīnyuè's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]]); same person plausible but unconfirmed. Lifedates unrecorded.
