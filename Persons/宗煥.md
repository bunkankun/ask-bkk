# Zōng-huàn 宗煥

Late-Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[文明]] Yuè-jiàn Wén-míng; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0325]]). Also named among the co-editors of [[心月]] Shí-xī Xīn-yuè's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]]); same person plausible but unconfirmed. Lifedates unrecorded.
