# Yuán 元 (Bǎichī Yuán 百癡元)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, **32nd-generation Línjì** (行-generation character per Yán dàcān's preface reading), **principal Fèi-yǐn-line master after Yǐnyuán Lóngqí's 1654 Japan emigration**. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Yuán** 元 (an unusual single-character dharma-name in the Mìyún line; some sources may list his full form as **Xíngyuán** 行元, though the juan-head signature consistently reads just 元). *Hào* **Bǎichī** 百癡 ("Hundred-Fool", from his 建寧百山景星禪院 seat). Lay surname **Cài** 蔡, of **Zhāngzhōu Zhāngpǔxiàn** 漳州漳浦縣 (Fújiàn). Father **Cài Cóngchéng** (*zì* **Zǐhǎi** 紫海), mother **Zhèngshì** 鄭氏. Born c. **1614** (age 24 in 1637 per his own *xíngshí*); death-date not precisely recovered but presumably c. **1660** (the *yǔlù* was cut 1659–61 in circumstances suggesting Bǎichī's declining health).

**Formation (1633–38).** Early Confucian study; failed exams at 20. *Guǐyǒu 5月* 癸酉 (= 1633 lunar 5): fled home, met **Zhēnjièshī** 真戒師 and **Niànguǒwēng** 念果翁 during a thunderstorm; tonsured at **Chángtài Shíshīyán** 長泰石獅巖. Then to **Quánzhōu Bàoqīnsì** 泉州報親寺 under **Gènxìn guānzhǔ** 亙信關主 for initial Chán training on the "*wànfǎ guīyī* 萬法歸一" *gōngàn*. **甲戌春** (= 1634 spring): traveled to **Fúzhōu Huángbòshān** to meet his 本師 [[通容|Fèiyǐn Tōngróng]] 費隱通容 (1593–1661) at age 24. Sealed on Fèiyǐn's "*dòngxián biéqǔ* 動絃別曲" exchange with decisive breakthrough in 1634–35 winter *rùshì*. **戊寅 7月** (1638): appointed **西堂** 西堂 at **Hǎiyán Jīnsùshān Guǎnghuìsì** when Fèiyǐn took that abbacy.

**Thirteen abbacies (1641–c. 1660).** Preserved juan-structure of [[KR6q0421]] lists thirteen successive seats, among which the most significant are (a) **Jiànníng Bǎishān Jǐngxīng Chányuàn** (the *hào*-giving seat, from 1642); (b) **Hǎiyán Jīnsùshān Guǎnghuìsì** (**戊子閏 4月** = **1648 intercalary 4**, entered as abbot succeeding Fèiyǐn at the Mìyún ancestral seat — five-year tenure to 1653 is the central abbacy of his career, covered in juan 4–8 of the *yǔlù*); (c) **Sōngjiāng Míngfā Chányuàn** (**甲午5月** = 1654 onwards, with multiple returns), and (d) the final **Fújiàn Xìnghuà Liánshān Guóhuān Chóngfú Chánsì** 興化蓮山國懽崇福禪寺. See [[KR6q0421]] Abstract for the full abbacy-sequence with dates.

**Historical significance.** Bǎichī is the **acting institutional successor** to Fèiyǐn Tōngróng at the Mìyún ancestral **Jīnsù** seat — 1648 entry at Jīnsù restores the institutional continuity that had been disrupted by Fèiyǐn's departure to Huángbò and by the 1644 Míng collapse. Among Fèiyǐn's named dharma-heirs he ranks second after Yǐnyuán Lóngqí (who emigrated to Japan in 1654), making Bǎichī the de facto leader of the Fèiyǐn branch after the 1654 emigration. His own dharma-heirs include **Hānpú Xìngcōng** 憨璞性聰 (1611–1684), summoned to the Shùnzhì palace in 1657 and given the **明覺禪師** 明覺禪師 title — the first Shùn-zhì-era palace-Chán master (preceding Mùchén Dàomǐn's 1659 audience).

Work: [[KR6q0421]] 《百癡禪師語錄》 (30 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J28 B202, compiled by dharma-heir [[超宣]] Chāoxuàn with prefaces by Yán dàcān (**1659**) and Xú Chāngzhì (**1661**)).

Sources: [[KR6q0421]] juan 29 *xíngshí* (the master's own self-biography c. 1657); juan 1 prefaces by **Yán dàcān** 嚴大參 (**Shùnzhì 16 己亥 暮春朔** = **early April 1659**) and **Xú Chāngzhì** 徐昌治 (**Shùnzhì 18 辛丑 暮春穀雨** = **April 1661**); *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 juan 69.

A **different** mid-seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán master with *fǎhuì* **元**: **Yīchū Wùyuán** 一初悟元 (catalog-shortened to 元 at [[KR6q0444]]), dharma-heir of [[妙用|Yuānhú Miàoyòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (1587–1642, cf. [[KR6q0416]]) in the Cáodòng **Wúqù Rúkōng** → **Nánmíng Zōngguǎng** → Miàoyòng sub-line. Dharma-brother of [[悟進|Jièān Wùjìn]] (at [[KR6q0443]]). To be distinguished from **Bǎichī Yuán** 百癡元 (the Fèiyǐn Línjì heir at [[KR6q0421]]) above.

See full biographical context at [[悟元]] and [[KR6q0416]]. Held abbacy at **Púxī Yǒngzhèng Chánsì** 濮溪永正禪寺 from **戊子 1648**.
