# Yuán 元 (Bǎi-chī Yuán 百癡元)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, **32nd-generation Línjì** (行-generation character per Yán dà-cān's preface reading), **principal Fèi-yǐn-line master after Yǐn-yuán Lóng-qí's 1654 Japan emigration**. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Yuán** 元 (an unusual single-character dharma-name in the Mìyún line; some sources may list his full form as **Xíng-yuán** 行元, though the juan-head signature consistently reads just 元). *Hào* **Bǎi-chī** 百癡 ("Hundred-Fool", from his 建寧百山景星禪院 seat). Lay surname **Cài** 蔡, of **Zhāng-zhōu Zhāng-pǔ-xiàn** 漳州漳浦縣 (Fú-jiàn). Father **Cài Cóng-chéng** (*zì* **Zǐ-hǎi** 紫海), mother **Zhèng-shì** 鄭氏. Born c. **1614** (age 24 in 1637 per his own *xíng-shí*); death-date not precisely recovered but presumably c. **1660** (the *yǔlù* was cut 1659–61 in circumstances suggesting Bǎi-chī's declining health).

**Formation (1633–38).** Early Confucian study; failed exams at 20. *Guǐ-yǒu 5月* 癸酉 (= 1633 lunar 5): fled home, met **Zhēn-jiè-shī** 真戒師 and **Niàn-guǒ-wēng** 念果翁 during a thunderstorm; tonsured at **Cháng-tài Shí-shī-yán** 長泰石獅巖. Then to **Quán-zhōu Bào-qīn-sì** 泉州報親寺 under **Gèn-xìn guān-zhǔ** 亙信關主 for initial Chán training on the "*wàn-fǎ guī-yī* 萬法歸一" *gōng-àn*. **甲戌春** (= 1634 spring): traveled to **Fú-zhōu Huáng-bò-shān** to meet his 本師 [[通容|Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng]] 費隱通容 (1593–1661) at age 24. Sealed on Fèi-yǐn's "*dòng-xián bié-qǔ* 動絃別曲" exchange with decisive breakthrough in 1634–35 winter *rù-shì*. **戊寅 7月** (1638): appointed **西堂** 西堂 at **Hǎi-yán Jīn-sù-shān Guǎng-huì-sì** when Fèi-yǐn took that abbacy.

**Thirteen abbacies (1641–c. 1660).** Preserved juan-structure of [[KR6q0421]] lists thirteen successive seats, among which the most significant are (a) **Jiàn-níng Bǎi-shān Jǐng-xīng Chán-yuàn** (the *hào*-giving seat, from 1642); (b) **Hǎi-yán Jīn-sù-shān Guǎng-huì-sì** (**戊子閏 4月** = **1648 intercalary 4**, entered as abbot succeeding Fèi-yǐn at the Mìyún ancestral seat — five-year tenure to 1653 is the central abbacy of his career, covered in juan 4–8 of the *yǔlù*); (c) **Sōng-jiāng Míng-fā Chán-yuàn** (**甲午5月** = 1654 onwards, with multiple returns), and (d) the final **Fú-jiàn Xìng-huà Lián-shān Guó-huān Chóng-fú Chán-sì** 興化蓮山國懽崇福禪寺. See [[KR6q0421]] Abstract for the full abbacy-sequence with dates.

**Historical significance.** Bǎi-chī is the **acting institutional successor** to Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng at the Mìyún ancestral **Jīn-sù** seat — 1648 entry at Jīn-sù restores the institutional continuity that had been disrupted by Fèi-yǐn's departure to Huáng-bò and by the 1644 Míng collapse. Among Fèi-yǐn's named dharma-heirs he ranks second after Yǐn-yuán Lóng-qí (who emigrated to Japan in 1654), making Bǎi-chī the de facto leader of the Fèi-yǐn branch after the 1654 emigration. His own dharma-heirs include **Hān-pú Xìng-cōng** 憨璞性聰 (1611–1684), summoned to the Shùn-zhì palace in 1657 and given the **明覺禪師** 明覺禪師 title — the first Shùn-zhì-era palace-Chán master (preceding Mù-chén Dào-mǐn's 1659 audience).

Work: [[KR6q0421]] 《百癡禪師語錄》 (30 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B202, compiled by dharma-heir [[超宣]] Chāo-xuàn with prefaces by Yán dà-cān (**1659**) and Xú Chāng-zhì (**1661**)).

Sources: [[KR6q0421]] juan 29 *xíng-shí* (the master's own self-biography c. 1657); juan 1 prefaces by **Yán dà-cān** 嚴大參 (**Shùn-zhì 16 己亥 暮春朔** = **early April 1659**) and **Xú Chāng-zhì** 徐昌治 (**Shùn-zhì 18 辛丑 暮春穀雨** = **April 1661**); *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 69.

A **different** mid-seventeenth-century Cáo-dòng Chán master with *fǎ-huì* **元**: **Yī-chū Wù-yuán** 一初悟元 (catalog-shortened to 元 at [[KR6q0444]]), dharma-heir of [[妙用|Yuān-hú Miào-yòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (1587–1642, cf. [[KR6q0416]]) in the Cáo-dòng **Wú-qù Rú-kōng** → **Nán-míng Zōng-guǎng** → Miào-yòng sub-line. Dharma-brother of [[悟進|Jiè-ān Wù-jìn]] (at [[KR6q0443]]). To be distinguished from **Bǎi-chī Yuán** 百癡元 (the Fèi-yǐn Línjì heir at [[KR6q0421]]) above.

See full biographical context at [[悟元]] and [[KR6q0416]]. Held abbacy at **Pú-xī Yǒng-zhèng Chán-sì** 濮溪永正禪寺 from **戊子 1648**.
