In the Kanripo catalog, the bare temple-name **Shìzōng** 世宗 (with lifedates 1678–1735) refers to the fifth Qīng emperor, *Shìzōng Xiànhuángdì* — personal name Yìnzhēn 胤禛, reigning under the *Yōngzhèng* 雍正 era-name (1722–1735), son of Kāngxī and father of Qiánlóng. Substantive biographical and bibliographical detail (Buddhist patronage, *Jūnjī chù* 軍機處 reform, the Yōngzhèng *yùxuǎn yǔlù* and other compositions) is given on the longer companion notes for the same person, [[世宗胤禛]] and [[世宗皇帝]].

In KR2f the name [[世宗]] appears in the catalog meta as the editor (敕編) of [[KR2f0005]] *Shèngzǔ Rénhuángdì shèngxùn* 聖祖仁皇帝聖訓 (the Yōngzhèng-edited *shèngxùn* of his father Kāngxī, 1731), and as the author of [[KR2f0006]] *Shìzōng Xiànhuángdì shèngxùn* 世宗憲皇帝聖訓 (the Qiánlóng-edited *shèngxùn* of Yōngzhèng's own pronouncements, 1741).
