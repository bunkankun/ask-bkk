Early-19th-century Imperial Medical Academy (太醫院) physician of Xīn'ān 新安 (Anhui); *zì* Liáofù 聊復, *hào* Liáofù Lǎorén 聊復老人. Active under the Jiāqìng emperor. Author of a six-volume Jiāqìng-period collection *Liáo fú lǎo rén suì cài* 聊復老人碎采 (c. 1809), which includes [[KR3eb008]] *Yī jiē biàn zhèng* and the parallel *Yī jiē sì zhěn* 醫階四診. No CBDB record.
