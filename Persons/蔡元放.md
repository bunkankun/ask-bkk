Cài Yuánfāng 蔡元放 was a Qīng editor and literary critic, active in the Qiánlóng-Jiāqìng period (roughly late 18th to early 19th century). He is known primarily for his revision of Féng Mènglóng's 馮夢龍 *Xīn Lièguó Zhì* 新列國志 into the standard 108-chapter *Dōngzhōu Lièguó Zhì* 東周列國志 ([[KR4k0097]]), to which he also added editorial commentary (*píng* 評). His biographical details — native place, examination record, official career — have not been recovered from standard sources; he does not appear in CBDB. He is recorded in Wilkinson (*Chinese History: A New Manual*) as the reviser of the canonical *Dōngzhōu Lièguó Zhì*.
