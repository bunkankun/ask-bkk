**Fāng Guǎng** 方廣 (*zì* Yuē-bǐng 約竚, *hào* Gū-shí shān-rén 古石山人, fl. *Jiā-jìng* era), Míng physician of Xiū-níng 休寧 (Hī-zhōu, Ān-huī). His principal work, the *Dān-xī xīn-fǎ fù-yú* 丹溪心法附餘 ([[KR3er019]], dated *Jiā-jìng* 15 = 1536), is a twenty-four-*juǎn* expanded recension of [[KR3er018]] *Dān-xī xīn-fǎ* incorporating extensive supplementary clinical material from Dài Sī-gōng's *Zhèng-zhì yào-jué* 證治要訣, Wáng Lún's 王綸 *Míng-yī zá-zhù* 明醫雜著, and other Míng Dān-xī-lineage works. The work circulated continuously through the Míng and Qīng and was particularly influential in Korea, where it was a standard reference in the *Dōng-yī bǎo-jiàn* 東醫寶鑑 (Hŏ Chun, 1613). The *Sì-kù quán-shū zǒng-mù* accepts the Fāng-Jiā-jìng attribution. No CBDB record under this *zì*. Not to be confused with the homonymous modern Buddhist-text scholar 方廣錩 Fāng Guǎng-chāng (b. 1948), Dūn-huáng manuscript specialist, who has a separate person note.
