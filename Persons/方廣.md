**Fāng Guǎng** 方廣 (*zì* Yuēbǐng 約竚, *hào* Gūshí shānrén 古石山人, fl. *Jiājìng* era), Míng physician of Xiūníng 休寧 (Hīzhōu, Ānhuī). His principal work, the *Dānxī xīnfǎ fùyú* 丹溪心法附餘 ([[KR3er019]], dated *Jiājìng* 15 = 1536), is a twenty-four-*juǎn* expanded recension of [[KR3er018]] *Dānxī xīnfǎ* incorporating extensive supplementary clinical material from Dài Sīgōng's *Zhèngzhì yàojué* 證治要訣, Wáng Lún's 王綸 *Míngyī zázhù* 明醫雜著, and other Míng Dān-xī-lineage works. The work circulated continuously through the Míng and Qīng and was particularly influential in Korea, where it was a standard reference in the *Dōngyī bǎojiàn* 東醫寶鑑 (Hŏ Chun, 1613). The *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù* accepts the Fāng-Jiājìng attribution. No CBDB record under this *zì*. Not to be confused with the homonymous modern Buddhist-text scholar 方廣錩 Fāng Guǎngchāng (b. 1948), Dūnhuáng manuscript specialist, who has a separate person note.
