**Xiāo Xūn** 蕭塤 (*zì* Gēngliù 賡六, *fl.* *Kāngxī*, late 17th c. 清), early-Qīng physician of Wúzhōu 撫州 (Jiāngxī). Author of two coordinated medical compilations:

1. *Yīxué jīnglún* 醫學經綸 — a comprehensive general-medicine encyclopedia organising 160-plus *zázhèng* 雜症 (miscellaneous-disease) categories with citations from over 7,000 passages drawn from "*míngxián zhī lùn*" 名賢之論 (named authorities). The work is one of the principal early-Qīng inheritors and consolidators of late-Míng general internal medicine.
2. *Nǚkē jīnglún* 女科經綸 ([[KR3ei029]]) — the *companion* gynecology, in 8 *juǎn*, separately constituted because of size and topical importance.

Both works were completed during the *Kāngxī* reign and printed during the late 17th and early 18th centuries. The works are particularly valuable as **secondary documentary witnesses** to the late-Míng gynecological synthesis (Chén Zìmíng, Xuē Jǐ, Wǔ Zhīwàng, Wáng Kěntáng, Zhāng Jièbīn, Zhào Xiànkě) since Xiāo cites all the major figures with critical editorial commentary.
