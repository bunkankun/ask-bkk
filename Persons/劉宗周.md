Liú Zōngzhōu 劉宗周 (1578–1645), *zì* Qǐdōng 起東, *hào* Niàntái 念臺 / Jíshān 蕺山 (often *Jíshān xiānsheng* 蕺山先生), posthumously *Zhōngjiè* 忠介 (Qing) and *Zhōngduān* 忠端 (early-Qing local), was a major Late-Míng *Lǐxué* figure and the founder of the *Jíshān xuépài* 蕺山學派. Native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Shàoxīng region, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Wànlì xīnchǒu* (1601); held *Yùshǐ* and other posts under late Wànlì and Tiānqǐ; out of office for much of Chóngzhēn; rose at the start of the Hóngguāng régime to *Yòu dūyùshǐ*. After the fall of Nánjīng to the Qīng (1645), he refused to take Qīng office; starved himself to death over 23 days in *Hóngguāng* 1 / 6th month at age 68. Founded the *Zhèngrén shūyuàn* 證人書院 (later *Jíshān shūyuàn*) which produced the next generation of Late-Míng *Lǐxué*: his disciples include Huáng Zōngxī 黃宗羲, Chén Què 陳確, Zhāng Lǚxiáng 張履祥, Wú Pèngshān, and others. Liú is foundational in the *Lǐxué* / *xīnxué* synthesis tradition: his *shèn dú* (cautious in solitude) doctrine bridges the Yáojiāng *liáng zhī* and the Zhū-Sòng *qióng lǐ*; his student Huáng Zōngxī's *Míngrú xué àn* makes him the culminating figure of Mid-Míng *Lǐxué*. Major works: *Liúzǐ yíshū* 劉子遺書 ([[KR3a0097]], 4 juan, including *Shèngxué zōng yào* and *Xué yán*); *Rén pǔ* 人譜 ([[KR3a0098]], his foundational moral-pedagogical compendium); *Liúzǐ quánshū* 劉子全書 (40+ juan, the comprehensive collected works). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 255, *Míngrú xué àn* j. 62. CBDB id 34748, dates 1578–1645.
