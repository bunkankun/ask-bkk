Féng Qùfēi 馮去非 (b. 1192), *zì* Kěqiān 可遷, *hào* Shēnjū 深居, of Nánkāng Dūchāng 都昌. Son of [[馮椅]] Féng Yǐ (author of *Hòuzhāi Yì xué* 厚齋易學 [[KR1a0046]]). *Jìnshì* of *Chúnyòu* 1 (1241); rose to *Zōngzhèng sī bù* 宗正寺簿; impeached by Dīng Dàquán 丁大全 (Lǐzōng era) and dismissed. His *Sòngshǐ* biography in juan 425 is the principal documentary source for his father Féng Yǐ's vast 200-juan canonical scholarship — most of which was lost in independent transmission, with the *Hòuzhāi Yì xué* the principal surviving fragment.
