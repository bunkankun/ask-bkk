# 王振吾

Wáng Zhènwú of Bózhōu 亳州 (Ānhuī), Míng-period figure named in the in-text transmission frame of [[KR3em013]] *Yìshòu yǎnkē* 異授眼科 as the original recipient of the work from a wandering Daoist who had cured his father's blindness. Likely a literary frame-figure rather than an independently attested historical person.
