Guāngdiàn 光電, *zì* **Chángmù** 長目. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Héngxiù Fālín** [[發林]] 恒秀發林 (cf. [[KR6q0494]]) — and thus dharma-grandson of **Yúnhuàn Yìnchén** [[印宸]] (cf. [[KR6q0493]]) in the Pòshān-line. Born **崇禎丙子 11/3** = **1636-11-29 Gregorian**; died **康熙乙丑 7/28** = **1685-08-27 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* **50**, *sēnglà* **32**. Native of **Xīnhuàxiàn** 新化縣 (Bǎoqìngfǔ); lay surname **Dèng** 鄧.

Tonsured at 17 (1652). *fǎfù* **康熙庚戌 = 1670**. Three abbacies: **Mítuó** 彌陀, **Yuánmíng** 圓明, **Bǎojué** 寶覺. Stūpa at the **Lǜluó** Pòtítǎyuàn 菩提塔院 (left of his master Héngxiù's *shòutǎ*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0516]] 《長目電禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B391, compiled by *fǎsì* **Wùběn** [[悟本]] *biān*; juan 2 *xíngzhuàng*.
