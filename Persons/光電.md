Guāng-diàn 光電, *zì* **Cháng-mù** 長目. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Héng-xiù Fā-lín** [[發林]] 恒秀發林 (cf. [[KR6q0494]]) — and thus dharma-grandson of **Yún-huàn Yìn-chén** [[印宸]] (cf. [[KR6q0493]]) in the Pòshān-line. Born **崇禎丙子 11/3** = **1636-11-29 Gregorian**; died **康熙乙丑 7/28** = **1685-08-27 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* **50**, *sēng-là* **32**. Native of **Xīn-huà-xiàn** 新化縣 (Bǎo-qìng-fǔ); lay surname **Dèng** 鄧.

Tonsured at 17 (1652). *fǎ-fù* **康熙庚戌 = 1670**. Three abbacies: **Mí-tuó** 彌陀, **Yuán-míng** 圓明, **Bǎo-jué** 寶覺. Stūpa at the **Lǜ-luó** Pò-tí-tǎ-yuàn 菩提塔院 (left of his master Héng-xiù's *shòu-tǎ*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0516]] 《長目電禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B391, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Wù-běn** [[悟本]] *biān*; juan 2 *xíng-zhuàng*.
