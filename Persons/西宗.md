Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; style **Chéngjiǎn** 成簡, religious name **Xīzōng** 西宗 (DILA A000429). Birth-date not preserved; died Kāngxī 26th year (1687/88), 12th lunar month (the *Jīn'ésì zhì* 金峩寺志, juan 5, says he "fell gravely ill at the close of *Kāngxī dīngmǎo* and laid down his brush"). Tonsured at *Jīn'ésì* 金峩寺 in Níngbō under the precept-master *Lǚpíng Zhìxián* 履平智賢 (1608–1673), whose dharma-heir he became after a long period of canon-reading retreat. He successively held abbacies at *Lóngzhùsì* 龍住寺 in Xīxiāng 西鄉, *Pǔqiūsì* 普邱寺, *Dìngqiáosì* 定橋寺 and *Jiāngxīnsì* 江心寺. His one surviving work is the *Cíbēi dàochǎng shuǐchànfǎ kēzhù* 慈悲道場水懺法科註 ([[KR6k0203]], X1496), a sub-section commentary on the Táng *Shuǐ chàn*, completed at Lóngzhùsì in his last years and left fractionally incomplete at his death.
