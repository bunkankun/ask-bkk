Early-Qīng Buddhist monk; style **Chéng-jiǎn** 成簡, religious name **Xī-zōng** 西宗 (DILA A000429). Birth-date not preserved; died Kāng-xī 26th year (1687/88), 12th lunar month (the *Jīn'é-sì zhì* 金峩寺志, juan 5, says he "fell gravely ill at the close of *Kāng-xī dīng-mǎo* and laid down his brush"). Tonsured at *Jīn'é-sì* 金峩寺 in Níng-bō under the precept-master *Lǚ-píng Zhì-xián* 履平智賢 (1608–1673), whose dharma-heir he became after a long period of canon-reading retreat. He successively held abbacies at *Lóng-zhù-sì* 龍住寺 in Xī-xiāng 西鄉, *Pǔ-qiū-sì* 普邱寺, *Dìng-qiáo-sì* 定橋寺 and *Jiāng-xīn-sì* 江心寺. His one surviving work is the *Cí-bēi dào-chǎng shuǐ-chàn-fǎ kē-zhù* 慈悲道場水懺法科註 ([[KR6k0203]], X1496), a sub-section commentary on the Táng *Shuǐ chàn*, completed at Lóng-zhù-sì in his last years and left fractionally incomplete at his death.
