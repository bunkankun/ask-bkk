Qìngxù 慶緒, *zì* **Huìyǐng** 慧頴 (also referred to as **Jīnmíng Qìngxù** 金鳴慶緒). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Méixī Fúdù** [[福度]] (1637–1701). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Húnán Yǒngjùn** 永郡 (Yǒngzhōu); lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0561]] 《東山梅溪度禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B447, prefaced 1691 and 1698.

**Sources.** 《黔南會燈錄》 vol. 1 (X85 n1591 p. 229b02), vol. 3 (X85 n1591 p. 254b13).
