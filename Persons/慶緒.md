Qìng-xù 慶緒, *zì* **Huì-yǐng** 慧頴 (also referred to as **Jīn-míng Qìng-xù** 金鳴慶緒). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Méi-xī Fú-dù** [[福度]] (1637–1701). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Hú-nán Yǒng-jùn** 永郡 (Yǒng-zhōu); lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0561]] 《東山梅溪度禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B447, prefaced 1691 and 1698.

**Sources.** 《黔南會燈錄》 vol. 1 (X85 n1591 p. 229b02), vol. 3 (X85 n1591 p. 254b13).
