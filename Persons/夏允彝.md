Late-Míng literatus and loyalist of Huátíng 華亭 (Sōngjiāng); *jìnshì* of *Chóngzhēn* 10 / 1637. With his friends Chén Zǐlóng 陳子龍 and Lǐ Wén 李雯 he founded the *Jǐshè* 幾社, the principal Sōngjiāng literary-political society of the late Míng and a satellite of the *Fùshè* 復社. Magistrate of Chánglè 長樂, Fújiàn; on the fall of Náanjīng in 1645 he organised loyalist resistance in Sōngjiāng, and after its failure drowned himself in a pond at his residence. His son [[夏完淳]] Xià Wánchún (1631–1647) continued the resistance and was executed at the age of sixteen. His preface to [[KR3er083]] *Yīzōng bìdú* (1637), written in the year of his *jìnshì* examination, is the most substantial of the four prefaces to that work.
