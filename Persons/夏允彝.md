Late-Míng literatus and loyalist of Huá-tíng 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng); *jìn-shì* of *Chóng-zhēn* 10 / 1637. With his friends Chén Zǐ-lóng 陳子龍 and Lǐ Wén 李雯 he founded the *Jǐ-shè* 幾社, the principal Sōng-jiāng literary-political society of the late Míng and a satellite of the *Fù-shè* 復社. Magistrate of Cháng-lè 長樂, Fú-jiàn; on the fall of Náan-jīng in 1645 he organised loyalist resistance in Sōng-jiāng, and after its failure drowned himself in a pond at his residence. His son [[夏完淳]] Xià Wán-chún (1631–1647) continued the resistance and was executed at the age of sixteen. His preface to [[KR3er083]] *Yī-zōng bì-dú* (1637), written in the year of his *jìn-shì* examination, is the most substantial of the four prefaces to that work.
