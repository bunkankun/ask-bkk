# Fāng Wěi 方煒

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as one of the *kǎozhèng* compilers for the *Míng shǐ* ([[KR2a0038]]) in the *Sìkù quánshū*, working alongside [[嚴福]]. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
