Fáng Shěnquán 房審權 (fl. Xīníng 熙寧 era, 1068–1077), Northern-Sòng *Yì* compilator from Sìchuān. Compiled the hundred-juan *Zhōuyì yì hǎi* 周易義海 ("Sea of Meanings of the *Zhōuyì*"), a major Northern-Sòng collation of post-Hàn *Yì*-readings extracting some hundred masters from [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán down to [[王安石]] Wáng Ānshí, capped with [[孔穎達]] Kǒng Yǐngdá's *Zhèngyì* and with editorial judgments at points of disagreement. The work was substantively lost shortly after the Southern-Sòng *Yì*-compendium [[李衡]] Lǐ Héng condensed it into twelve juan as *Zhōuyì yì hǎi cuō yào* ([[KR1a0034]]); [[陳振孫]] Chén Zhènsūn already records only a four-juan fragment. No biographical details survive beyond his Sìchuān origin and Xīníng-era *floruit*.
