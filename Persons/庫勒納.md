Early Qīng 清 high official and *Hànlín* administrator of Manchu lineage. Kuldena (Manchu, sinicized 庫勒納). Lifedates not securely recorded; floruit Kāngxī 康熙 era. Held the office of *Sì shū jiǎng zhāng* 四書講章 director and was the senior named *zǒng cái* 總裁 ("Director-General") of the Kāngxī-era imperial daily-lecture (*rì jiǎng* 日講) commentary commissions on the *Sì shū* and the *Shū jīng*. Together with [[葉方藹]] (Yè Fāngǎi, 1629–1682) he supervised the compilation of the *Rì jiǎng Shū jīng jiě yì* 日講書經解義 ([[KR1b0045]]) — the imperial daily-lecture text on the *Shàngshū* — between Kāngxī 17 / 1678 (II.2, formal commission) and Kāngxī 19 / 1680 (IV.12, imperial preface). The work was printed and promulgated to officials throughout the empire as the official Kāngxī-court statement of *Shàngshū* doctrine.
