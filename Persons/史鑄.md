Shǐ Zhù 史鑄 (*fl.* early 13th century, *Jiādìng* era, ca. 1208–1224), Southern-Sòng amateur scholar and chrysanthemum specialist, native of Kuàijī 會稽 in Shàoxīng. Author of the *Bǎijú jí pǔ* 百菊集譜 ([[KR3j0058]]) — one of the principal Sòng monographs on chrysanthemums — and the supplementary annotation to Wáng Shípéng's *Kuàijī sānfù* 會稽三賦 ([[KR2k0113]]); his colophon to the latter is dated *Jiādìng dīngchǒu* (1217). Otherwise undocumented; not in CBDB.
