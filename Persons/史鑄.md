Shǐ Zhù 史鑄 (*fl.* early 13th century, *Jiā-dìng* era, ca. 1208–1224), Southern-Sòng amateur scholar and chrysanthemum specialist, native of Kuài-jī 會稽 in Shào-xīng. Author of the *Bǎi-jú jí pǔ* 百菊集譜 ([[KR3j0058]]) — one of the principal Sòng monographs on chrysanthemums — and the supplementary annotation to Wáng Shí-péng's *Kuài-jī sān-fù* 會稽三賦 ([[KR2k0113]]); his colophon to the latter is dated *Jiā-dìng dīng-chǒu* (1217). Otherwise undocumented; not in CBDB.
