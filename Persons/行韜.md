Xíngtāo 行韜, *zì* **Dàyǔ** 大庾 (also bare-character form 庾, his original lay-name). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hànyuè Sānfēng** sub-line. Died **順治九壬辰 7/25** = **1652-08-28 Gregorian**. Native of **Wújiāng** 吳江 (Sūzhōu prefecture); lay surname **Zhào** 趙. As a youth (*yòu nián* 幼年) studied under his **伯父 paternal uncle [[法藏]] Hànyuè Fǎzàng** — a notable case of family-line transmission within the Sānfēng line.

**Formation.** Studied under uncle Hànyuè Fǎzàng in childhood. **順治三 = 1646**: retreated to **Wǔyíshān** 武夷山. Subsequently received full precepts under **Fúshí Tōngxián** 浮石通賢 at **Wújiāng Bàoēnsì** 吳江報恩寺 with *yìnkě*. Final formal succession under **Língyán Hóngchǔ** at **Língyánsì**, accompanying the master's tours to **Tiānníngsì** 天寧寺 and **Ruìyánsì** 瑞巖寺.

**Abbacy.** **順治七 = 1650**: by Hóngchǔ's instruction installed at **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** 天台國清寺 — the same Tiāntái-flagship seat just vacated by his dharma-brother **Xièyún Jìjī** [[濟璣]] (d. 1648, [[KR6q0476]]). Two consecutive Sān-fēng-line Línjì abbots at Guóqīng (1640s–1652) constitutes a notable Sānfēng claim on the Tiāntái flagship monastery.

**Work.** [[KR6q0477]] 《國清大庾韜禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B305, recorded by *shìzhě* **Xiǎnyù** [[顯裕]] *děng lù*; preserved tomb-inscription *Tiāntái Guóqīng Tāo chánshī yībō tǎmíng* 天台國清韜禪師衣缽塔銘 in juan 1.
