Xíng-tāo 行韜, *zì* **Dà-yǔ** 大庾 (also bare-character form 庾, his original lay-name). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng** sub-line. Died **順治九壬辰 7/25** = **1652-08-28 Gregorian**. Native of **Wú-jiāng** 吳江 (Sū-zhōu prefecture); lay surname **Zhào** 趙. As a youth (*yòu nián* 幼年) studied under his **伯父 paternal uncle [[法藏]] Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** — a notable case of family-line transmission within the Sān-fēng line.

**Formation.** Studied under uncle Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng in childhood. **順治三 = 1646**: retreated to **Wǔ-yí-shān** 武夷山. Subsequently received full precepts under **Fú-shí Tōng-xián** 浮石通賢 at **Wú-jiāng Bào-ēn-sì** 吳江報恩寺 with *yìn-kě*. Final formal succession under **Líng-yán Hóng-chǔ** at **Líng-yán-sì**, accompanying the master's tours to **Tiān-níng-sì** 天寧寺 and **Ruì-yán-sì** 瑞巖寺.

**Abbacy.** **順治七 = 1650**: by Hóng-chǔ's instruction installed at **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** 天台國清寺 — the same Tiāntái-flagship seat just vacated by his dharma-brother **Xiè-yún Jì-jī** [[濟璣]] (d. 1648, [[KR6q0476]]). Two consecutive Sān-fēng-line Línjì abbots at Guó-qīng (1640s–1652) constitutes a notable Sān-fēng claim on the Tiāntái flagship monastery.

**Work.** [[KR6q0477]] 《國清大庾韜禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B305, recorded by *shì-zhě* **Xiǎn-yù** [[顯裕]] *děng lù*; preserved tomb-inscription *Tiān-tái Guó-qīng Tāo chán-shī yī-bō tǎ-míng* 天台國清韜禪師衣缽塔銘 in juan 1.
