# Héng Zōngjì 衡宗繼

Míng-period Chán master of the Zhēnrú sì 真如寺, the initial teacher of [[如巹]] Mìān Rújǐn before Rújǐn became dharma-heir of [[景隆|Kōnggǔ Jǐnglóng]].
