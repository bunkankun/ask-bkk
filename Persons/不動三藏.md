# Bùdòng sānzàng 不動三藏

Sòng-era translator (sānzàng 三藏 "Tripiṭaka master"); credited with the Sòng translation that appended a further 53 Buddha-names to the *Sānshíwǔ fó míng jīng* 三十五佛名經 (Amoghavajra's Táng translation), producing — together with the *Pǔxián shí dà yuàn* 普賢十大願偈 — the composite repentance-liturgy *Dà chàn huǐ wén* 大懺悔文. Lifedates not preserved in the materials examined.

Sources: preface of [[KR6q0211]] *Dà chàn huǐ wén lüè jiě* by [[書玉|Yíjié Shūyù]].
