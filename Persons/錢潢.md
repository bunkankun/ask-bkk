**Qián Huáng** 錢潢 (*zì* Tiānlái 天來, *fl.* Kāngxī mid-period, ca. 1660s–1720s, 清), Sūzhōu 蘇州 physician. He is best known for the *Shānghán sùyuán jí* 傷寒溯源集 ([[KR3ef012]], 10 juan, 1707), a major early-Kāng-xī commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* in the "ancient-text-restoration" line of [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí and [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng. His exact lifedates are not preserved; the active years are inferred from the 1707 publication of the *Sùyuán* and references in the works of his contemporaries [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù and [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí. No other independent works survive. No CBDB record.
