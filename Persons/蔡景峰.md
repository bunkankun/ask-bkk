# Cài Jǐngfēng 蔡景峰

Contemporary Chinese-medicine historian and editor (China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences); editor of the standard punctuated annotated edition *Yòuyòu jíchéng jiàozhù* 幼幼集成校注 ([[KR3ej013]]; Rénmín wèishēng chūbǎnshè) and contributor to multiple standard reference works on the history of Chinese medicine.
