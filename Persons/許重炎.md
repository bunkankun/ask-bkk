Xǔ Chóngyán 許重炎, late-Kāngxī-to-Yōngzhèng-era *Lǐxué* disciple of [[潘天成]] Pān Tiānchéng (1654–1727), editor and compiler of the *Tiělú jí* 鐵廬集 ([[KR4f0036]]) — Pān's collected works in 3 juan plus 2 *wàijí* juan. The *Sìkù tíyào* records that Xǔ supplied the opening short biography (*xiǎo zhuàn*) and chronological biography (*niánpǔ*), recorded Pān's *yǔlù* (recorded sayings) in juan 3 jointly with Jiǎng Shīhán 蔣師韓, and produced the structural arrangement. No CBDB record. Xǔ is otherwise a minor figure attested only through this editorial work and the *PānXǔ* discipleship documented in the *Tiělú jí* front-matter.
