Early-mid Míng 明 scholar; recorded as the compiler (*biān* 編) of several Yuán-period *biéjí* in the Sìkù catalog meta:

- [[KR4d0460]] *Báiyún jí* 白雲集 (Xǔ Qiān 許謙's collection — though the principal Míng print was by Chén Xiāng 陳相 in Chénghuà 2 / 1466; Lǐ Shēn's compilation likely underlies)
- [[KR4d0526]] *Jīngjì wénjí* 經濟文集 (Lǐ Shìzhān 李士瞻's collection)
- [[KR4d0559]] *Yīshān wénjí* 一山文集 (his grandfather Lǐ Jìběn 李繼本's collection; Lǐ Shēn was *jiàoyù* at Róngchéng — see preface by Lí Gōngyǐng 黎公穎 of 1453)

No firm CBDB record located. Active before / during the mid-15th century.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0460]] *Báiyún jí* 白雲集 (編); [[KR4d0526]] *Jīngjì wénjí* 經濟文集 (編); [[KR4d0559]] *Yīshān wénjí* 一山文集 (編).
