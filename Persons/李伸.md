Early-mid Míng 明 scholar; recorded as the compiler (*biān* 編) of several Yuán-period *bié-jí* in the Sìkù catalog meta:

- [[KR4d0460]] *Bái-yún jí* 白雲集 (Xǔ Qiān 許謙's collection — though the principal Míng print was by Chén Xiāng 陳相 in Chéng-huà 2 / 1466; Lǐ Shēn's compilation likely underlies)
- [[KR4d0526]] *Jīng-jì wén-jí* 經濟文集 (Lǐ Shì-zhān 李士瞻's collection)
- [[KR4d0559]] *Yī-shān wén-jí* 一山文集 (his grandfather Lǐ Jì-běn 李繼本's collection; Lǐ Shēn was *jiàoyù* at Róng-chéng — see preface by Lí Gōng-yǐng 黎公穎 of 1453)

No firm CBDB record located. Active before / during the mid-15th century.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0460]] *Bái-yún jí* 白雲集 (編); [[KR4d0526]] *Jīng-jì wén-jí* 經濟文集 (編); [[KR4d0559]] *Yī-shān wén-jí* 一山文集 (編).
