Xǔ Ēn-pǔ 許恩普 — late-Qīng Běijīng physician, active in the late Guāng-xù 光緒 era (c. 1880s–1900s). The internal evidence of his [[KR3ep014]] *Xǔ-shì yī-àn* 許氏醫案 dates the opening clinical case to *jǐ-chǒu* 己丑 = 1889, with patients drawn predominantly from the Qīng bureaucratic elite — officials of the *Gōng-bù* 工部 and the *Jīng-jī Dào* 京畿道 administration. His clinical method is conservative-classical, working primarily within the *Shāng-hán lùn* tradition and the elaborate tongue-atlas (*shé-jiàn* 舌鑑) tradition descending from the *Áo-shì shāng-hán jīn-jìng-lù*. CBDB and DILA have no entry; biographical data beyond the cases themselves are not available.
