Xǔ Ēnpǔ 許恩普 — late-Qīng Běijīng physician, active in the late Guāngxù 光緒 era (c. 1880s–1900s). The internal evidence of his [[KR3ep014]] *Xǔshì yīàn* 許氏醫案 dates the opening clinical case to *jǐchǒu* 己丑 = 1889, with patients drawn predominantly from the Qīng bureaucratic elite — officials of the *Gōngbù* 工部 and the *Jīngjī Dào* 京畿道 administration. His clinical method is conservative-classical, working primarily within the *Shānghán lùn* tradition and the elaborate tongue-atlas (*shéjiàn* 舌鑑) tradition descending from the *Áoshì shānghán jīnjìnglù*. CBDB and DILA have no entry; biographical data beyond the cases themselves are not available.
