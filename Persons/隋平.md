# 隋平

Suí Píng (*hào* Kūntiě 昆鐵), local literatus of Dōngwǔ 東武 / Qiānshèng 千乘 / Zhūféng 諸馮 (Shāndōng), active in the late Kāng-xī era (fl. c. 1700). Patron and second-prefacer of the early-Qīng ophthalmological compendium [[KR3em004]] *Yǎnkē mìjué* 眼科秘訣 redacted by [[馬雲從]] Mǎ Yúncóng; supplied the print-funds for the original publication.
