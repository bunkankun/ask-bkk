# Cén Wénběn 岑文本

*Zì* Jǐngrén 景仁. Native of Jiānglíng 江陵 (modern Húběi). Lifedates 595–645.

Early-Tang chief minister and historiographer. Held *Zhōngshū lìng* 中書令 from 643 — the de facto leading civil position of the empire under Tang Tàizōng. Died on the *Liáodōng* expedition of 645 against Goguryeo. One of the principal Tang court drafters of edicts and memorials; his prose style was widely imitated.

As historiographer, he was one of the five compilers of the *Zhōu shū* ([[KR2a0022]]) under Lìnghú Détén's chief editorship, and a contributor to the *Suí shū* ([[KR2a0023]]) under Wèi Zhēng's editorship. His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 70 and *Xīn Tángshū* 102 ([[KR2a0026]], [[KR2a0027]]).
