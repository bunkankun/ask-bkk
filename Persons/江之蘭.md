Early-Qīng (*Shùn-zhì* / *Kāng-xī*-era) physician of Shè-xiàn 歙縣, Huī-zhōu (Ān-huī), and a member of the Xīn-ān 新安 medical lineage. His principal extant work is [[KR3er103]] *Yī-jīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (c. *Kāng-xī* 1 / 1662), a one-*juǎn* treatise in fourteen essays on the *zhì-fǎ* 治法 (therapeutic-method) passages of the *Nèi-jīng*; a separately-attested *Nèi-jīng shì-yào* 內經釋要 may be the same work under another title. The *Yī-jīn yī fá* was later pirated and re-attributed as [[KR3er105]] *Yī-jīng mì-zhǐ* 醫經秘旨, with the original authorship not securely restored until Gāo Sī-qián's 1925 philological investigation for the *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 series. His *zì* is given as Hán-zhēng 含徵 in most sources but as Hán-wéi 含微 in the *Sì-kù quán-shū zǒng-mù tí-yào*. Not in CBDB.
