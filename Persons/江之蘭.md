Early-Qīng (*Shùnzhì* / *Kāngxī*-era) physician of Shèxiàn 歙縣, Huīzhōu (Ānhuī), and a member of the Xīnān 新安 medical lineage. His principal extant work is [[KR3er103]] *Yījīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (c. *Kāngxī* 1 / 1662), a one-*juǎn* treatise in fourteen essays on the *zhìfǎ* 治法 (therapeutic-method) passages of the *Nèijīng*; a separately-attested *Nèijīng shìyào* 內經釋要 may be the same work under another title. The *Yījīn yī fá* was later pirated and re-attributed as [[KR3er105]] *Yījīng mìzhǐ* 醫經秘旨, with the original authorship not securely restored until Gāo Sīqián's 1925 philological investigation for the *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 series. His *zì* is given as Hánzhēng 含徵 in most sources but as Hánwéi 含微 in the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào*. Not in CBDB.
