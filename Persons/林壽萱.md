# 林壽萱 Lín Shòuxuān

Late-Qīng Fúzhōu bookseller-collector; assembled and arranged the 1856 joint printing of [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ's *Shānghán yī jué chuàn jiě* ([[KR3ef066]]) and *Shí yào shén shū zhù jiě* under the patronage of Fújiàn governor [[呂佺孫]] Lǚ Quánsūn.
