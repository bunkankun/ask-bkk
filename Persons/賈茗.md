**Jiǎ Míng** 賈茗, styled Jìngfēn 靚芬, from Gǔ Wú 古吳 (the Sūzhōu region of Jiāngsū), was a Qīng dynasty female literata (*nǚshǐ* 女史) and the compiler of *Nǚ Liáozhāi Zhìyì* 女聊齋志異 ([[KR4k0189]]). She is described in the work's prefatory material as a great admirer of Pǔ Sōnglíng 蒲松齡 (author of *Liáozhāi Zhìyì* [[KR3l0130]]) and assembled her anthology of tales about remarkable women explicitly in homage to his model. The *nǚshǐ* 女史 honorific designates her as a woman of letters and learning.
