**Zhūgě Xī** 諸葛羲 (fl. late Wànlì / Chóngzhēn era) — late-Míng official, the **36th-generation descendant** of [[諸葛亮]] (Zhūgě Liàng, 181–234 CE) of the ShǔHàn. Native of Fújiàn (his lineage: starting from Zhūgě An-jié 安節公, *Sòng Shàoxī*-era *Fújiàn zhuǎn yùn shǐ*, his family settled in Fújiàn; four generations to the *shàobǎo bīngbù shàngshū* Zhūgě Tíngruì 廷瑞 — 22 generations from Liàng; another 12 generations to the *zǔ Chángshǐgōng* Zhūgě Yìngkē 應科; Zhūgě Xī is the next surviving generation, the 36th from Liàng). Compiler-and-prefacer of the 23-juàn **Hàn chéngxiàng Zhūgě Zhōngwǔhóu jí** ([[KR5i0078]]) — the late-Míng anthology of his ancestor's writings — completed in **崇禎壬申十一月 = November–December 1632** at his official residence in *Línyú* 臨庾. The compilation drew on multiple manuscript sources including a base text from his grandfather, his uncle's hand-copy, the *Màoyuàn Yáng Qùshē* recension, and the *Lóudōng Wáng* family's *jiā chāo* (family manuscript). The preface is signed *Chóngzhēn rénshēn (= 1632), 11th month, 36th-generation grandson Xī kē shǒu jǐn xù yú Línyú zhī gōngshǔ*.
