**Zhū-gě Xī** 諸葛羲 (fl. late Wàn-lì / Chóng-zhēn era) — late-Míng official, the **36th-generation descendant** of [[諸葛亮]] (Zhū-gě Liàng, 181–234 CE) of the Shǔ-Hàn. Native of Fú-jiàn (his lineage: starting from Zhū-gě An-jié 安節公, *Sòng Shào-xī*-era *Fú-jiàn zhuǎn yùn shǐ*, his family settled in Fú-jiàn; four generations to the *shào-bǎo bīng-bù shàng-shū* Zhū-gě Tíng-ruì 廷瑞 — 22 generations from Liàng; another 12 generations to the *zǔ Cháng-shǐ-gōng* Zhū-gě Yìng-kē 應科; Zhū-gě Xī is the next surviving generation, the 36th from Liàng). Compiler-and-prefacer of the 23-juàn **Hàn chéng-xiàng Zhū-gě Zhōng-wǔ-hóu jí** ([[KR5i0078]]) — the late-Míng anthology of his ancestor's writings — completed in **崇禎壬申十一月 = November–December 1632** at his official residence in *Lín-yú* 臨庾. The compilation drew on multiple manuscript sources including a base text from his grandfather, his uncle's hand-copy, the *Mào-yuàn Yáng Qù-shē* recension, and the *Lóu-dōng Wáng* family's *jiā chāo* (family manuscript). The preface is signed *Chóng-zhēn rén-shēn (= 1632), 11th month, 36th-generation grandson Xī kē shǒu jǐn xù yú Lín-yú zhī gōng-shǔ*.
