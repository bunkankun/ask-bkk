The 67th-generation hereditary Duke *Yǎnshèng* 衍聖公 of the Kǒng lineage, head of the descendants of Confucius at Qūfù; native of Qūfù 曲阜 (Shāndōng). *Zì* Zhōngzài 鍾在; *hào* Lántáng 蘭堂. The catalog and *Sìkù* tradition writes the surname 弘 (rather than 孔) to mark the imperial avoidance—standard Kāngxī-and-after Manchu-influenced practice for sacred names; modern scholarship reverts to the canonical 孔毓圻. Succeeded as duke in 1672 at age 15. Hosted Kāngxī's 1684 imperial visit to *Quēlǐ* and led the multi-decade compilation of the *Xìnglǔ Shèngdiǎn* 幸魯盛典 ([[KR2m0033]]) recording it. Also patron of the *Quēlǐ Kǒngshì zōngpǔ* and major Kǒng-temple architectural renovations of the early Qīng. CBDB / Wikidata both confirm 1657–1723.
