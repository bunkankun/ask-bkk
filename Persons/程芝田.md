**Chéng Zhītián** 程芝田 (early 19th c., 清), Qúzhōu 衢州 physician and teacher of [[雷逸仙]] Léi Yìxiān (the father of [[雷豐]] Léi Fēng). Mentioned in Léi Fēng's *Shíbìng lùn* ([[KR3eg002]]) preface as the first-generation source of the family's *wēnbìng* tradition.
