**Hata Kōzan** 畑黃山 (also 畑栗山 / Hata Risshan; 1721–1804), Edo-period Japanese physician of the *gosehōha* 後世方派 school (the post-Hàn-positive doctrinal opposition to the *kohōha* 古方派 of Yoshimasu Tōdō). Author of the **Sekiidan** 斥醫斷 ([[KR3eq054]] in combined volume), the principal contemporary rebuttal of Tsuruoki Genitsu's *Idan* 醫斷 (Yoshimasu Tōdō's disciple-record). Defended the post-Hàn medical canon (*Shénnóng běncǎo*, *Sùwèn*, *Língshū*, *Nánjīng*, Sòng formulary tradition) against the Yoshimasu programme's radical reduction of all medicine to the *Shānghán lùn* clinical method. Cataloged under 江戶.
