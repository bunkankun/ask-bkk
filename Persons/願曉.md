Early-Heian Japanese **Sanron** 三論 monk, resident at **Gangō-ji** 元興寺 of Nara. Conventionally dated **835–871** (with a variant terminus of 874 reported by *Hōbōgirin*). Principal compiler of [[KR6i0318]] 《金光明最勝王經玄樞》 *Konkōmyō saishōō-kyō genzū* (T2196), a ten-fascicle Sanron-school exegesis of the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa-sūtra* (Yìjìng recension, T665 = [[KR6i0303]]). The work's own preface (by Sugawara no Koreyoshi 菅原是善, 812–880) frames the *Genzū* as the continuation of a Sanron transmission descending from his predecessor at Daian-ji, **Gonsō** 勤操 (758–827); a colophon-lineage in the Taishō text traces the further descent of the *Genzū* tradition through ten generations down to the late-Heian-period Kanjuji master Kanshin 寛信.

His doctrinal method, as articulated in the *Genzū* preface, is to take **Paramārtha** 真諦 (Zhēndì, 499–569) as the root of the commentarial tradition (*根本*) and **Jñānagupta** 闍那 as the branches (*枝葉*), collecting and harmonising the views of multiple earlier masters into a single exegetical synthesis. No other works survive securely under his name. The variant graph 願暁 appears in some Japanese reference works in place of 願曉.
