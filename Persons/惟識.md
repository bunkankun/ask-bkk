# Wéishí 惟識

Yuán–Míng-period monk (*śramaṇa*) of **Shīzǐlín** 師子林 (the famed Sūzhōu garden-monastery), known for the *Quànchí xù* 勸持敘 commendatory preface to [[陸西星]] Lù Xīxīng's *Lèngyán jīng shùzhǐ* ([[KR6j0703]]).
