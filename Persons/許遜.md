**Xǔ Xùn** 許遜 (trad. 239–374), *zì* Jìng zhī 敬之, honoured as **Xǔ zhēn jūn** 許眞君 or **Xǔ tiān shī** 許天師, was the Eastern-Jìn Daoist alchemist and exorcist revered as the founding patriarch of the **Jìng míng zhōng xiào dào** 淨明忠孝道 ("Way of Pure Illumination, Loyalty, and Filiality") centered at Xī shān 西山 (Nánchāng, Jiāngxī). His cult exploded in the Sòng-Yuán era, with pseudepigraphic alchemical and ritual literature attributed to him — including [[KR5c0351]] *Xǔ zhēn jūn shí hán jì* 許眞君石函記 ("Record of Perfected Xǔ's Stone Casket"). Traditional lifedates 239–374 are hagiographic; the historical figure is obscured behind centuries of cult legend.
