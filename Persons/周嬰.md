Zhōu Yīng 周嬰 (*fl.* late Chóngzhēn; lifedates unknown), *zì* Fāngshū 方叔. Native of Pútián 莆田, Fújiàn. Served as magistrate of Shàngyóu 上猶 county, Jiāngxī. Compiler of the *Zhī lín* 巵林 ([[KR3j0067]]), a 10-juan evidential miscellany dated internally to *Chóngzhēn* 15 (1642). His other works do not survive substantially. CBDB id 373318 (no dates).
