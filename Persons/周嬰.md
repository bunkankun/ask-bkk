Zhōu Yīng 周嬰 (*fl.* late Chóng-zhēn; lifedates unknown), *zì* Fāng-shū 方叔. Native of Pú-tián 莆田, Fú-jiàn. Served as magistrate of Shàng-yóu 上猶 county, Jiāng-xī. Compiler of the *Zhī lín* 巵林 ([[KR3j0067]]), a 10-juan evidential miscellany dated internally to *Chóng-zhēn* 15 (1642). His other works do not survive substantially. CBDB id 373318 (no dates).
