# Miù Quánsūn 繆荃孫

Late-Qīng to early-Republican bibliographer (1844–1919), *hào* Yìfēng 藝風, *zì* Xiǎoshān 筱珊, of Jiāngyīn 江陰 (Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of 1876; founding director of the Jiāngnán Library (江南圖書館) and the Capital Library (京師圖書館). Author of the *Yìfēng cángshūjì* 藝風藏書記 and editor of the *Yìfēng tángshū* 藝風堂書 series. Preserved a transcript of the Yuán *Zhìyuán* original of *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]]) in his Yìfēng library, which served as base-text for [[柯逢時]] Kē Féngshí's 1910 critical edition.
