Shǐ Róng 史容 (fl. *Jiā-dìng*–*Jǐng-dìng*, c. 1208–1264, *zì* Gōng-yí 公儀, *hào* Xiāng-shì jū-shì 薌室居士), of *Qīng-yī* 青衣 (Sì-chuān). Held office through *Tài-zhōng dà-fū*. Annotator of the *Shān-gǔ Wài-jí shī-zhù* 山谷外集詩註 [[KR4d0086]] in 17 *juǎn* — the annotated edition of Huáng Tíng-jiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]]'s pre-*Yuán-fēng* poetry (i.e. the *Wài-jí* in the original *Sū Shì* / *Hóng Yán* editorial division — younger and more Hán-Yù-influenced than the post-*Yuán-fēng* *Nèi-jí* annotated by Rèn Yuān 任淵 [[任淵]]). The work was completed *Jiā-dìng* (1208–1224) and revised in the decade after; preceded by Qián Wén-zǐ 錢文子 of *Jìn-líng*'s *Jiā-píng* 1 / 1224 *xù*. His grandson Shǐ Jì-wēn 史季溫 (*zì* Zǐ-wēi 子威, *jìn-shì* of *Bǎo-yòu*, *Mì-shū shǎo-jiān*) further composed the *Shān-gǔ Bié-jí shī-zhù* in 2 *juǎn* (the appended *Bié-jí* — Huáng's lost-and-recovered pieces). The Shǐ family of *Qīng-yī* was a major Sòng *Jiāng-xī*-school *zhù-jiā* (annotation) house.
