Shǐ Róng 史容 (fl. *Jiādìng*–*Jǐngdìng*, c. 1208–1264, *zì* Gōngyí 公儀, *hào* Xiāngshì jūshì 薌室居士), of *Qīngyī* 青衣 (Sìchuān). Held office through *Tàizhōng dàfū*. Annotator of the *Shāngǔ Wàijí shīzhù* 山谷外集詩註 [[KR4d0086]] in 17 *juǎn* — the annotated edition of Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅 [[黃庭堅]]'s pre-*Yuánfēng* poetry (i.e. the *Wàijí* in the original *Sū Shì* / *Hóng Yán* editorial division — younger and more Hán-Yù-influenced than the post-*Yuánfēng* *Nèijí* annotated by Rèn Yuān 任淵 [[任淵]]). The work was completed *Jiādìng* (1208–1224) and revised in the decade after; preceded by Qián Wénzǐ 錢文子 of *Jìnlíng*'s *Jiāpíng* 1 / 1224 *xù*. His grandson Shǐ Jìwēn 史季溫 (*zì* Zǐwēi 子威, *jìnshì* of *Bǎoyòu*, *Mìshū shǎojiān*) further composed the *Shāngǔ Biéjí shīzhù* in 2 *juǎn* (the appended *Biéjí* — Huáng's lost-and-recovered pieces). The Shǐ family of *Qīngyī* was a major Sòng *Jiāngxī*-school *zhùjiā* (annotation) house.
