**Dīng Yuánjiàn** 丁元薦 (*zì* Chángrú 長孺), late-Míng clinical disciple of [[繆希雍]] Miù Xīyōng who edited and compiled [[KR3eq034]] *Xiānxǐngzhāi yīxué guǎng bǐjì*, ensuring the work's posthumous circulation. The "Dīng Chángrú editorial precedent" was the canonical model invoked by [[周鑅]] Zhōu Yīng in his 1843 preface to [[KR3eq009]] *Huíchūn lù*. The 丁長孺 in earlier notes is the same person.
