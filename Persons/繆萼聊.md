**Miù È-liáo** 繆萼聊 (*zì* **Shào-chū** 少初, late-Qīng, *fl.* 1880s–1890s), late-Qīng official-turned-medical-book-publisher of **Yú-shān** 虞山 / Cháng-shú 常熟 (Jiāngsū). Best known for the 1894 re-printing of the [[葉桂]]-attributed **[[KR3eq006]]** *Yè-xuǎn yī-héng* 葉選醫衡 at his **Yī-yuán** 伊園 estate in Cháng-shú after long service as a circuit magistrate in Hé-nán (*Zhōng-zhōu* 中州). His preface to *Yè-xuǎn yī-héng* explains the recovery of the work from his own private library after the destruction of the Sū-zhōu blocks in the 1860 Tài-píng-era fall, and credits the Huì-jī 會稽 physician Lǚ Méi-qīng 呂梅卿 (encountered by Miù in Běi-jīng) as the senior authority who had recommended the work to him.
