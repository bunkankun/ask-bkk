**Miù Èliáo** 繆萼聊 (*zì* **Shàochū** 少初, late-Qīng, *fl.* 1880s–1890s), late-Qīng official-turned-medical-book-publisher of **Yúshān** 虞山 / Chángshú 常熟 (Jiāngsū). Best known for the 1894 re-printing of the [[葉桂]]-attributed **[[KR3eq006]]** *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* 葉選醫衡 at his **Yīyuán** 伊園 estate in Chángshú after long service as a circuit magistrate in Hénán (*Zhōngzhōu* 中州). His preface to *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* explains the recovery of the work from his own private library after the destruction of the Sūzhōu blocks in the 1860 Tài-píng-era fall, and credits the Huìjī 會稽 physician Lǚ Méiqīng 呂梅卿 (encountered by Miù in Běijīng) as the senior authority who had recommended the work to him.
