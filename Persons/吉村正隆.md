**Yoshimura Masataka** 吉村正隆 (Chinese reading **Jícūn Zhènglóng**, *zì* Shìxìng 士興 / Shixō), late-Edo (*Kansei* era, 1789–1801) physician of Nagasaki 長崎. He prefaced the Nagasaki edition of the Qīng-period Chinese paediatric treatise *Yīngér lùn* 嬰兒論 ([[KR3ej009]]) by Zhōu Shìmí 周士禰, brought to Nagasaki by the Kyoto physician Hirokawa-shi 平安廣川子. The preface is dated *Kansei* 9 / *Qīng* *Jiāqìng* 2, *dīngsì* 寬政丁巳 = 1797. He is otherwise poorly documented in the standard biographical reference works on Edo-period Japanese physicians.
