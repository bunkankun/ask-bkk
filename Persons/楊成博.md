**Yáng Chéngbó** 楊成博 is the named "originator" (先師) of the underground Qīng-period martial-arts injury-medicine manual [[KR3ee051]] *Yáng Chéngbó xiānshēng yíliú xuédào mìshū* 楊成博先生遺留穴道秘書. He is otherwise unknown to the standard bibliographies (*Zhōngguó zhōngyī gǔjí zǒngmù*, CBDB, Wikipedia); no biographical sources survive, and his lifedates cannot be established. The honorific *xiānshēng yíliú* 先生遺留 ("bequeathed by Master ...") and the *shī yuē* 師曰 ("the master says ...") frame of the text are consistent with a teaching-lineage origin in which "Yáng Chéngbó" may have been an early *wǔshù*-medicine ancestor cited by an anonymous later transcriber.
