# 呂光

Lǚ Guāng 呂光 (*zì* Shìmíng 世明, 338–399), Dī 氐 general of the Former Qín 前秦 and founder of the Hòu-Liáng 後涼 (386–403). Dispatched westward by Fú Jiān 苻堅 in 383 to subdue the Western Regions, he captured the kingdom of Kucha 龜茲 in 384 and seized the young [[鳩摩羅什]] Jiūmóluóshí, holding him in Liángzhōu 涼州 for some sixteen years; he proclaimed himself King of Liáng in 386 after the collapse of the Former Qín. The closing colophon of the *Chēngyáng zhū fó gōngdé jīng* 稱揚諸佛功德經 ([[KR6i0010]]) places its Tokharian-Chinese translation at Kucha in Línjiā 麟嘉 6 (394) of his regnal era.
