Mid-Táng calligraphy critic and minor official; *zì* Línglǐng 靈長, a man of Fúfēng 扶風. He served as *Jiǎnxiào hùbù yuánwài-láng* 檢校戶部員外郎 and as *cānmóu* 參謀 of the Sòng-Biàn 宋汴 Military Commission, both noted in Xú Hào 徐浩's *Gǔjì jì* 古迹記. Active in the *Tiānbǎo* 天寶 and post-rebellion years (fl. ca. 750s); composed [[KR3h0007]] *Shùshū fù* 述書賦, a two-*juàn fù*-form survey of 198 calligraphers from antiquity through the High Táng. His elder brother [[竇蒙]] (Dòu Méng) supplied the interlinear commentary that accompanies the text — though Sìkù editors note an internal cross-reference suggesting that some of the annotations may be Jì's self-glosses. Zhāng Yànyuǎn's [[KR3h0008]] *Fǎshū yàolù* (847) preserves the work and praises it.
