# 楊奉林

Yáng Fènglín, intermediate transmitter named in the frame-narrative of [[KR3em013]] *Yìshòu yǎnkē* 異授眼科 as having received the manuscript from [[王振吾]] Wáng Zhènwú. Likely a literary frame-figure rather than an independently attested historical person.
