# Wénzhǔn 文準

Zhàntáng Wénzhǔn 湛堂文準 (1061–1115), Northern-Sòng Línjì Huánglóng-line Chán master, dharma-heir of Zhēnjìng Kèwén; teacher of Yuánwù Kèqín's contemporaries and recurrently cited in the biographical anecdote material of Dàhuì Zōnggǎo.
