# Lǚ Chì 呂熾 (?–1778)

Qing scholar-official; *jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng 11 (1733). Held *Hànlínyuàn* offices and was attached to the *Sìkùguǎn* 四庫館 staff during the Qiánlóng compilation. In Kanripo, his *kǎozhèng* 考證 appendix is preserved in the WYG copy of [[KR1h0007]] *Lúnyǔ zhùshū* 論語注疏 (jointly with [[陸宗楷]]). The *kǎozhèng* genre, prepared in-house at the *Sìkùguǎn*, contains note-form remarks on textual variants and citation errors against earlier *zhùshū* witnesses.
