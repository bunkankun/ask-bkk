Xiè Tíngjié 謝廷傑 (b. 1529; death year unrecorded). Editor and compiler of the definitive Wàn-lì-era *Wáng Wénchéng quánshū* in 38 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0157]]), the imperial-commissioned full-collected-works of **Wáng Shǒurén** ([[王守仁]]) assembled from **Qián Déhóng** ([[錢德洪]])'s chronological *Wénlù*. CBDB id 204892, b. 1529.
