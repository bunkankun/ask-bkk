Xiè Tíng-jié 謝廷傑 (b. 1529; death year unrecorded). Editor and compiler of the definitive Wàn-lì-era *Wáng Wén-chéng quán-shū* in 38 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0157]]), the imperial-commissioned full-collected-works of **Wáng Shǒu-rén** ([[王守仁]]) assembled from **Qián Dé-hóng** ([[錢德洪]])'s chronological *Wén-lù*. CBDB id 204892, b. 1529.
