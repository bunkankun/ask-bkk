# Lǐ Bǎiyào 李百藥

*Zì* Zhòngguī 重規. Native of Bóling 博陵 in Ānpíng 安平 (modern Héběi). Member of the great Bóling Lǐ 博陵李氏 lineage. Son of [[李德林]] (Lǐ Délín, 532–592), the Northern-Qí *Zhōngshū lìng* and unfinished historian of the Northern Qí. CBDB id 92899; lifedates 565–648.

Career across four dynasties — Northern Qí, Northern Zhōu, Suí, Tang. As a child he was a court attendant under Northern Qí Hòuzhǔ. Under Suí he held *Mìshū chéng* 秘書丞 and was implicated in the Yáng Liàng 楊諒 affair, briefly demoted. Under Tang Tàizōng he held *Zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人, *Sànjì chángshì* 散騎常侍, *Tàizǐ yòu shùzǐ* 太子右庶子 (Right Mentor of the Crown Prince Lǐ Chéngqián 李承乾), and finally *Zōngzhèng shǎoqīng* 宗正少卿. Enfeoffed as *Ānpíng nán* 安平男.

Inheriting his father Lǐ Délín's unfinished Northern-Qí history, he was formally commissioned by Tang Tàizōng in *Zhēnguān* 1 (627), with renewal in *Zhēnguān* 3 (629) under the unified *Wǔdài shǐ* programme; he presented the completed *Běi Qí shū* ([[KR2a0021]]) in *Zhēnguān* 10 (636).

In addition to the *Běi Qí shū*, he composed substantial poetry (preserved in *Quán Táng shī*) and the *Zàn dào fù* 贊道賦, an important early-Tang admonitory text composed for the Crown Prince. His political position was conventional Confucian: his celebrated *Fēngjiàn lùn* 封建論 (a memorial against any restoration of the *fēngjiàn* feudal system) was Tang Tàizōng's principal authority for keeping the centralised *jùnxiàn* 郡縣 system rather than reverting to enfeoffment.

His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 72 and *Xīn Tángshū* 102 ([[KR2a0026]], [[KR2a0027]]).
