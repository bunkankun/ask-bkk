# Zhìhuì 至慧

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249); named among the fifteen co-editors of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0315]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000404).
