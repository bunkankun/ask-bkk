**Zhèngbǎo** 徵保 (Jiāqìng era, 清), friend of [[吳塘]] Wú Jūtōng, mentioned in the preface to *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn* ([[KR3eg010]]) as one of those who saw Wú's draft in Jiāqìng 9 (1804). No further biographical information located.
