Lǐ Chōng-zhāo 李沖昭 (fl. 902) was a late-Táng Daoist priest (*yǔ-shì* 羽士) at Mt. Héng 衡山 (Nán-yuè). His self-preface to the *Nán-yuè xiǎo-lù* ([[KR2k0083]]) dates the work to Tiān-fù 2 (902), in the penultimate year of the Táng dynasty. The work — the earliest surviving Táng-era monograph on any of the Five Marchmounts — is his sole transmitted writing. The variant form 仲昭 (Zhòng-zhāo) appearing in some catalogues is a printing error per the Sìkù tíyào. CBDB has no entry under either form.
