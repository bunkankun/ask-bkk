# 張龢芬

Zhāng Héfēn 張龢芬, of Cíxī 慈溪 (Zhèjiāng). Late-Qīng / early-Republican literatus who wrote the 1913 preface to [[KR3ep075]] *Sōngxīn yīàn bǐjì*, the recovered manuscript of [[繆遵義]] Miù Zūnyì's medical cases.
