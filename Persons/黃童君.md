**Huáng tóng jūn** 黃童君 ("Yellow-Lad Lord") was the Táng Daoist alchemist-commentator who annotated [[KR5c0286]] *Wèi Bóyáng qī fǎn dān shā jué* 魏伯陽七返丹砂訣. The name appears to be a hagionym rather than a personal name.
