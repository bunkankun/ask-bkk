Liú Bǐng 劉炳 (also written 劉昺, conventionally known by his *zì* **Yànbǐng** 彥昺), native of **Póyáng** 鄱陽 (Jiāngxī). Late-Yuán resistance-band leader: with his younger brother **Liú Yù** 劉煜 he organised local self-defence against bandits and was a personal client of **Yú Què** 余闕 at Ānqìng (the *Sèmù* commander whose suicide-defence of Ānqìng in 1358 was the canonical Yuán-loyalist last stand in Jiāngzhōu). After Yú's defeat Liú left and took service under the rising Míng. Submitted policy memorial in early Hóngwǔ and was appointed *Zhōngshū diǎnqiān* 中書典籤; sent out as *Dàdūdū fǔ zhǎngjì* 大都督府掌記 (with **Lǐ Wénzhōng** 李文忠 and later **Mù Yīng** 沐英); then *zhīxiàn* 知縣 of **Dōngē** 東阿 in Shāndōng; retired on grounds of illness after two evaluations. His career-chronology is given in *Míng shǐ Wényuàn zhuàn* (appended to **Wáng Miǎn** 王冕's biography); the *Jiāngxī tōngzhì*'s rival record is rejected by the *Sìkù* editors. Pupil **Liú Zǐshēng** 劉子昇 edited his collection [[KR4e0035]] *Liú Yànbǐng jí* (originally *Chūnyǔxuān jí* 春雨軒集), with critical comments by **Yáng Wéizhēn** 楊維楨 and prefaces by Yáng, **Wēi Sù** 危素, and **Sòng Lián** 宋濂. CBDB returns many Yuán-Míng homonyms with no usable dates; lifedates not securely fixed but bracketed c. 1310s–after 1379 from internal evidence in the collection.
