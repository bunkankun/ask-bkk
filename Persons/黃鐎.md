**Huáng Jiāo** 黃鐎 (*hào* Cāngxī 蒼溪, *fl.* early Xiánfēng era, mid-19th c., 清), Qīng-period scholar of Cāngxī 蒼溪 (a place name; most likely the Cāngxī district in northern Sìchuān, but possibly a Zhèjiāng locality). He is known from his preface to [[KR3el014]] *Jiùshāng mìzhǐ* 救傷秘旨, dated *Xiánfēng* 1 / first month of summer (1851), in which he records his friendship with [[趙蘭亭]] Zhào Lántíng (who had come to his locality to disseminate the European smallpox-vaccination technique) and his admiration for Zhào's collection of *shāngkē* prescriptions. The preface adopts the *xíngzhě qí wú shāng yě* 行者其無傷也 (let men have no injuries at all) prayer as its peroration. His full identity and lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
