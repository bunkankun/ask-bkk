**Huáng Jiāo** 黃鐎 (*hào* Cāng-xī 蒼溪, *fl.* early Xián-fēng era, mid-19th c., 清), Qīng-period scholar of Cāng-xī 蒼溪 (a place name; most likely the Cāng-xī district in northern Sì-chuān, but possibly a Zhèjiāng locality). He is known from his preface to [[KR3el014]] *Jiù-shāng mì-zhǐ* 救傷秘旨, dated *Xián-fēng* 1 / first month of summer (1851), in which he records his friendship with [[趙蘭亭]] Zhào Lán-tíng (who had come to his locality to disseminate the European smallpox-vaccination technique) and his admiration for Zhào's collection of *shāng-kē* prescriptions. The preface adopts the *xíng-zhě qí wú shāng yě* 行者其無傷也 (let men have no injuries at all) prayer as its peroration. His full identity and lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
