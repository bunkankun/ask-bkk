**Gāo Zōngyuè** 高宗岳 — son of [[高淑濂]] Gāo Shūlián, the Tàiān 泰安 (Shāndōng) obstetrician. Printed his father's *Gāo Shūlián tāichǎn fāngàn* ([[KR3ei067]]) in December 1934 at the **Yī Dài shānfáng** 依岱山房 / **Dàlù shūshè** 大陸書社 in Tàiān; oversaw the 200-copy print run divided between charitable free-distribution (*zèngběn* 贈本) and commercial sale. Not in CBDB.
