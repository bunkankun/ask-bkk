Northern-Sòng *Yúnmén Chán* monk and literary polemicist, original surname Lǐ 李, *zì* Zhònglíng 仲靈, self-styled *Qiánzǐ* 潛子, *Míngjiào dàshī* 明教大師 (title bestowed by Sòng Rénzōng in 1061), of *Téngzhōu Xínjīn* 藤州鐔津 (Guǎngxī). Took ordination at age 7. In *Qìnglì* settled at *Língyǐn sì* in Hángzhōu; in *Huángyòu* / *Jiāyòu* travelled to the capital and presented the *Fǔjiào biān* 輔教編 (defense of Buddhism, with strong filial-piety argument) to Rénzōng, with patronage from Hán Qí [[韓琦]], Ōuyáng Xiū, and Sīmǎ Guāng [[司馬光]]; the work was admitted into the *Dàzàng*. Author of *Fēi Hán* 非韓 (30-*piān* systematic refutation of Hán Yù), *Xiào lùn* 孝論, and the *Chán* historiographical *Chuánfǎ zhèng zōng jì* 傳法正宗記 (1061) — the principal Sòng *Yúnmén* assertion of the orthodox patriarchal lineage. Died 1072 age 66. Surviving collection: *Xínjīn jí* [[KR4d0031]] (22 *juǎn*, 1499 Rú Jǐn cut). CBDB id 34789. DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority A002099.
