Northern-Sòng *Yún-mén Chán* monk and literary polemicist, original surname Lǐ 李, *zì* Zhòng-líng 仲靈, self-styled *Qián-zǐ* 潛子, *Míng-jiào dà-shī* 明教大師 (title bestowed by Sòng Rén-zōng in 1061), of *Téng-zhōu Xín-jīn* 藤州鐔津 (Guǎng-xī). Took ordination at age 7. In *Qìng-lì* settled at *Líng-yǐn sì* in Hángzhōu; in *Huáng-yòu* / *Jiā-yòu* travelled to the capital and presented the *Fǔ-jiào biān* 輔教編 (defense of Buddhism, with strong filial-piety argument) to Rén-zōng, with patronage from Hán Qí [[韓琦]], Ōuyáng Xiū, and Sī-mǎ Guāng [[司馬光]]; the work was admitted into the *Dà-zàng*. Author of *Fēi Hán* 非韓 (30-*piān* systematic refutation of Hán Yù), *Xiào lùn* 孝論, and the *Chán* historiographical *Chuán-fǎ zhèng zōng jì* 傳法正宗記 (1061) — the principal Sòng *Yún-mén* assertion of the orthodox patriarchal lineage. Died 1072 age 66. Surviving collection: *Xín-jīn jí* [[KR4d0031]] (22 *juǎn*, 1499 Rú Jǐn cut). CBDB id 34789. DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority A002099.
