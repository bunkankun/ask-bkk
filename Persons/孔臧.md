Kǒng Zāng 孔臧 (fl. mid-2nd c. BCE) was the son of Kǒng Jù 孔聚 (a 功臣 founding-merit follower of Hàn Gāozǔ 高祖), inherited the title Marquis of Liǎo 蓼侯, and rose under Wǔdì 武帝 to Grand Master of Ceremonies (*tàicháng* 太常). The *Hàn shū yìwén zhì* lists *Kǒng Zāng* in ten 篇 under the *Rújiā* and a separate *Kǒng Zāng fù* 孔臧賦 in twenty 篇 under the *Cífù* class. The two short pieces (the *fù* and the letter) appended at the end of the received *Kǒng cóngzǐ* 孔叢子 (KR3a0003) under the title *Liáncóngzǐ* 連叢子 are conventionally — but already on Cháo Gōngwǔ's reading uncertainly — connected with him; the SKQS *tíyào* notes that the *Liáncóngzǐ* contains a *fù*, suggesting the *Cífù*-class Kǒng Zāng rather than the *Rújiā*-class one. He has no entry in CBDB (Western Hàn predates its scope).
