**Gù Mèng-xióng** 顧夢熊 (*zì* **Wèi-chuān** 渭川), late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of the **Mèng-hé** 孟河 medical school (the elite Cháng-zhōu 常州 lineage culminating in Fèi Bó-xióng 費伯雄, Mǎ Péi-zhī 馬培之, and Dīng Gān-rén 丁甘仁). Studied under his clan-uncle **Gù Zhào-lín** 顧兆麟; involved in the founding of the **Shàng-hǎi Yī-xué yán-jiū-suǒ** 上海醫學研究所 in the 1900s as a Mèng-hé-trained internal-medicine practitioner with a parallel interest in classical-Chinese acupuncture-moxibustion. Best known as the collator-editor of the 1910 (Xuān-tǒng 2) Shàng-hǎi lithographic edition of the [[葉桂]]-attributed **[[KR3eq006]]** *Yè-xuǎn yī-héng* 葉選醫衡 prepared for use by the Institute's students. Not in CBDB; the Wèi-chuān-name biographical entry in the *Mèng-hé* lineage references is the principal source.
