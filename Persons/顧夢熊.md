**Gù Mèngxióng** 顧夢熊 (*zì* **Wèichuān** 渭川), late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of the **Mènghé** 孟河 medical school (the elite Chángzhōu 常州 lineage culminating in Fèi Bóxióng 費伯雄, Mǎ Péizhī 馬培之, and Dīng Gānrén 丁甘仁). Studied under his clan-uncle **Gù Zhàolín** 顧兆麟; involved in the founding of the **Shànghǎi Yīxué yánjiūsuǒ** 上海醫學研究所 in the 1900s as a Mèng-hé-trained internal-medicine practitioner with a parallel interest in classical-Chinese acupuncture-moxibustion. Best known as the collator-editor of the 1910 (Xuāntǒng 2) Shànghǎi lithographic edition of the [[葉桂]]-attributed **[[KR3eq006]]** *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* 葉選醫衡 prepared for use by the Institute's students. Not in CBDB; the Wèi-chuān-name biographical entry in the *Mènghé* lineage references is the principal source.
