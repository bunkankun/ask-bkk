Late-Yuán / early-Míng literatus of Wùyuán (Huīzhōu) region, author of the *xíngzhuàng* (career-record) for **Zhào Fǎng** 趙汸 (1319–1369) included as appendix to the *Dōngshān cúngǎo* 東山存稿 ([[KR4d0584]]). The *xíngzhuàng* preserves substantial information about Zhào's career and his teacher **Huáng Zé** 黃澤's *jīngshù chuánshòu* (classical transmission lineage). Zhān's own biography is not separately documented; the catalog meta records him as the appendix-piece author for [[KR4d0584]]. He appears in CBDB id 338606 with no dates given.
