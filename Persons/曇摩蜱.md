Tánmópí 曇摩蜱 (\*Dharmapriya, "Dharma-Beloved"; lifedates unknown), Indian Buddhist *śramaṇa*, *fl.* late fourth century, working at Cháng'ān under Former Qín 前秦. According to the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 (T2145, juan 13), Dharmapriya collaborated with [[竺佛念|Zhú Fóniàn / Buddhanandi]] on the translation of [[KR6c0012|*Móhē bōrě chāo jīng* 摩訶般若鈔經 (T226)]] — a partial Chinese rendering of the *Aṣṭasāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā* — at Cháng'ān in 382 CE under Dàoān 道安's editorial direction. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A002150.
