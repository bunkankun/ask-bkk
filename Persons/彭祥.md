Late-Yuán 元 Daoist textual scholar, collator (*jiào zhèng* 校正) of [[羅勉道]] Luó Miǎndào's (d. 1367) *Nán huá zhēn jīng xún běn* ([[KR5c0135|DZ 742]]) — a *Zhuāngzǐ* commentary.

**Role.** Péng Xiáng's role in DZ 742 is philological-editorial — providing textual-critical annotations identifying variant readings across the editions available to him in the late Yuán. The collation apparatus is a standard feature of late-imperial Chinese scholarly publishing and indicates that Péng Xiáng had access to multiple textual witnesses of the *Zhuāngzǐ*.

**Activity.** Active late Yuán (contemporary with Luó Miǎndào's pre-1367 activity). No precise dates. No CBDB record identified.

**Disambiguation.** Multiple figures named Péng Xiáng appear in Chinese biographical sources; this Péng Xiáng is known only through the DZ 742 collation.
