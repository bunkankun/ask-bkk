# Zhuó'ān Déguāng 拙庵德光

Southern-Sòng Chán master (1121–1203) in the Línjì 臨濟 — Yángqí 楊岐 — Dàhuì 大慧 branch; Dharma heir of Dàhuì Zōnggǎo 大慧宗杲 and teacher of Zhèngshòu 正受, compiler of the *Jiātài pǔdēng lù* 嘉泰普燈錄.
