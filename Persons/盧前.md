Lú Qián 盧前 (1905–1951), *zì* Jì-yě 冀野, *hào* Yǐn-hóng 飲虹, of Jiāng-níng 江寧 (Nán-jīng). Major Republican-era *qǔ*-scholar, dramatist, and poet. Pupil of Wú Méi 吳梅 at Dōng-nán dà-xué; subsequently professor at *Jīn-líng dà-xué* (Nán-jīng) and elsewhere. Founder-editor of the *Yǐn-hóng yí-jí* 飲虹簃集 series of *cí-qǔ* texts. Best known to the *cí-qǔ* scholarly community for his *xiào-jì* (collations) on Yuán-Míng *sǎn-qǔ* anthologies, including the SBCK *Lí-yuán àn-shì yuè-fǔ xīn-shēng* [[KR4j0082]] reprint. Author of *Zhōng-guó xì-jù gài-lùn* 中國戲劇概論, *Yǐn-hóng cí* 飲虹詞, and a series of historical-drama works (1930s–1940s). Not in CBDB. Wikidata Q15920489 (Lú Qián).
