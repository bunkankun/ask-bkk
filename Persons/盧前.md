Lú Qián 盧前 (1905–1951), *zì* Jìyě 冀野, *hào* Yǐnhóng 飲虹, of Jiāngníng 江寧 (Nánjīng). Major Republican-era *qǔ*-scholar, dramatist, and poet. Pupil of Wú Méi 吳梅 at Dōngnán dàxué; subsequently professor at *Jīnlíng dàxué* (Nánjīng) and elsewhere. Founder-editor of the *Yǐnhóng yíjí* 飲虹簃集 series of *cíqǔ* texts. Best known to the *cíqǔ* scholarly community for his *xiàojì* (collations) on YuánMíng *sǎnqǔ* anthologies, including the SBCK *Líyuán ànshì yuèfǔ xīnshēng* [[KR4j0082]] reprint. Author of *Zhōngguó xìjù gàilùn* 中國戲劇概論, *Yǐnhóng cí* 飲虹詞, and a series of historical-drama works (1930s–1940s). Not in CBDB. Wikidata Q15920489 (Lú Qián).
