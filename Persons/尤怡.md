**Yóu Yí** 尤怡 (*zì* Zàijīng 在涇, *hào* Zhuózhái 拙齋, also Sìhè shānrén 飼鶴山人; conventional lifedates ca. 1650 – ca. 1749, 清), Sūzhōu-Chángshú scholar-physician, late-Kāngxī / early-Qiánlóng. A disciple of Mǎ Jūnliáng 馬俊良, Yóu is one of the most influential commentators on the Zhāng Jī corpus from the early-to-mid Qīng. Principal works:

- *Shānghán guànzhū jí* 傷寒貫珠集 ([[KR3ef019]], 8 juan, 1729) — major *Shānghán* commentary in the "treatment-method" line.
- *Jīnguì yàoluè xīn diǎn* 金匱要略心典 ([[KR3ef089]]) — parallel *Jīnguì* commentary.
- *Yī xué dú shū jì* 醫學讀書記 — clinical-doctrinal notebook.
- *Jìng xiāng lóu yī àn* 靜香樓醫案 — case records.

His commentary line in both *Shānghán* and *Jīnguì* is concise, clinically grounded, and has had wide pedagogical use through the late Qīng and into the modern period. No CBDB record.
