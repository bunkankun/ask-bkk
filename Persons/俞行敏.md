# Yú Xíngmǐn 俞行敏

A mid-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar, identified in the colophons of his work as a *jūshì* 居士. Lifedates not preserved with precision; he was active in the early to mid Qīng. His major surviving work is the **《淨土全書》** *Jìngtǔ quánshū* [[KR6p0095]] (X1176, 2 *juǎn*), a Pure Land anthological compendium described in the colophon as a *chóngjí* 重輯 ("re-compiled / re-edited") work, suggesting the present version is a revision of an earlier compilation. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
