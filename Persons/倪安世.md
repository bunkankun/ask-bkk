Ní Ānshì 倪安世, *yuánsūn* 元孫 (great-great-grandson) of the Míng martyr [[倪元璐]] (1593–1644) of Shàngyú 上虞. In *Qiánlóng rénchén* / 1772 he re-edited and re-printed his ancestor's surviving writings, building on the proofs that the martyr's son [[倪會鼎]] had preserved through the dynastic transition; this 1772 redaction is the immediate ancestor of the WYG text of the *Ní Wénzhēn jí* ([[KR4e0240]]).
