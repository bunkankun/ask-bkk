# Zhìmén Guāngzuò 智門光祚

Northern Sòng Yúnmén-school master of Suízhōu 隨州; dharma-transmission teacher of [[重顯|Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn]] 雪竇重顯, positioning Chóngxiǎn as the tenth-generation Yúnménzōng descendant.
