# Dàozhān 道霑

A Qīng-dynasty Pure Land monk, lifedates not preserved with precision; he was active in the mid-Qīng period (probably eighteenth century). His name is also written 道沾 in some recensions. His major surviving work is the **《淨土旨訣》** *Jìngtǔ zhǐjué* [[KR6p0090]] (X1171, 1 *juǎn*), a short Pure Land doctrinal handbook in the *zhǐjué* 旨訣 ("essential instructions") genre. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
