# Wùshēn 悟深

Mid-Míng Chán monk, disciple of [[本善|Dú-fēng Běn-shàn]] 毒峰本善 (1419–1482) at Tiān-zhēn-sì 天真寺. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Compiler (*biān* 編) of the *Tiān-zhēn Dú-fēng Shàn chánshī yào yǔ* 天真毒峰善禪師要語 [[KR6q0194]], gathering Běn-shàn's teaching-responses and sermons into a one-juan *yǔlù*-style volume. No independent biographical material is preserved.
