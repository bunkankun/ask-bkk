# Wùshēn 悟深

Mid-Míng Chán monk, disciple of [[本善|Dúfēng Běnshàn]] 毒峰本善 (1419–1482) at Tiānzhēnsì 天真寺. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Compiler (*biān* 編) of the *Tiānzhēn Dúfēng Shàn chánshī yào yǔ* 天真毒峰善禪師要語 [[KR6q0194]], gathering Běnshàn's teaching-responses and sermons into a one-juan *yǔlù*-style volume. No independent biographical material is preserved.
