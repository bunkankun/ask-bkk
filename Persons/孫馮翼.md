Sūn Féngyì 孫馮翼 (*zì* Fèngqīng 鳳卿, *fl.* late 18th – early 19th century) was the nephew (從子) and collaborator of the great evidential-research scholar Sūn Xīngyǎn [[孫星衍]]. He is best known as the co-compiler of the 1799 Sūn-family reconstruction of the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* [[KR3ec001]], for which Zhāng Jiǒng's preface specifically credits him with the cross-collation of *Shuōwén*, *Ěryǎ*, *Guǎngyǎ*, *Huáinánzǐ*, and *Bàopǔzǐ* citations. He assisted Sūn Xīngyǎn on the *Píngjīnguǎn cóngshū* 平津館叢書 and is associated with the textual recovery of several lost Han works (e.g. 《桓子新論》).

No CBDB record under this name; lifedates not securely fixed, but active in the Jiāqìng 嘉慶 era.
