Hé Níng 和凝 (898–955), *zì* Chéngjī 成績, was a Five Dynasties official and *cí* (lyric) poet. *Jìnshì* of the Late Liáng. Held office across all four post-Liáng Five-Dynasties courts. Co-author (with his son Hé Mèng 和㠓) of the *Yí yù jí* 疑獄集 (KR3c0006, judicial-decision casebook). CBDB id 17667.
