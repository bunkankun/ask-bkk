**Zhōu Yúqī** 周於漆 (Qīng; dates and native place unrecorded) is the attributed author of the *Sāncái shíyì tiānjí* 三才實義天集 ([[KR3fa027]]), a substantial twenty-two-*juǎn* work on the "*tiān*" portion of the classical *sāncái* 三才 (Heaven, Earth, Man) triad — i.e., the astronomical sub-volume of a presumed larger trilogy. He is otherwise undocumented; not in CBDB.
