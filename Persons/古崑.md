# Gǔkūn 古崑

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active in the **Hángzhōu** 杭州 region during the **Tóngzhì** 同治 / **Guāngxù** 光緒 reigns (mid-to-late nineteenth century). Lifedates not preserved with precision; he was active c. 1860–1890. He is the most prolific late-Qīng compiler of Pure Land popular-devotional anthologies — three of his works are preserved in this catalog division: the **《西歸行儀》** *Xīguī xíngyí* [[KR6p0085]] (X1468), the **《淨土隨學》** *Jìngtǔ suíxué* [[KR6p0106]] (X1187), and the **《淨土必求》** *Jìngtǔ bìqiú* [[KR6p0107]] (X1188) — all short, accessible Pure Land devotional handbooks aimed at lay-Buddhist readers.

His sobriquet **Gǔkūn lǎorén** 古崑老人 ("Old Man Gǔkūn") suggests a long-lived senior monastic; the consistent style and intellectual orientation across his three works (devotional, lay-oriented, drawing heavily on the late-Qīng [[彭際清]] Péng Jìqīng tradition of literati Pure Land scholarship) place him firmly in the late-Qīng Pure Land revival that fed into the early-Republican Pure Land masters Yìnguāng 印光 (1861–1940) and others. Beyond the colophons of his works no substantial biographical record survives.
