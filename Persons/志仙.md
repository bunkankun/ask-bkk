Zhì-xiān 志仙 (active Liáo), Liáo-dynasty Buddhist monk; author of [[KR6e0159|*Fā pútí xīn jiè* 發菩提心戒]] (preserved in the Fáng-shān stone-canon as F1076), a brief precept-text on the generation of *bodhicitta*. Birth and death dates not preserved.
