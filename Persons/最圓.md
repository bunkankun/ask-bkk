Saien 最圓 (1048–1102, traditional dates) was a mid-Heian Tendai esoteric (*Taimitsu* 台密) master in the **Kawa-ryū** 川流 lineage on Mt. Hiei, in the line of descent from Kōkei 皇慶 ([[皇慶]]). He is recorded as one of the Tendai *ajari* who developed the **structural-analytical** (*shōki* 生起) genre of liturgical exposition, applying the framework refined by Kakuchō ([[覺超]]) to the Vajra-realm 金剛界 (Vajradhātu) liturgy.

His single surviving work in the Kanripo corpus is the [[KR6t0105]] *Jīngāngjiè cìdì shēngqǐ* 金剛界次第生起, a one-fascicle structural exposition of the Vajradhātu liturgical sequence.

He should be distinguished from the later Tendai monk of the same name and from the Shingon-school 最圓 of the Ono lineage.
