# Liǎo 了 (Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo 月幢徹了)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí *chán-shī*, principal **Pòshān / Zhàng-xuě sub-line** dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695), and — by imperial grace — dharma-name-bearer of the name **Zhēn-fó** 真佛 bestowed on him by the Southern-Ming **Yǒng-lì** 永曆 emperor during his Yún-nán / Guì-zhōu ministry. DILA-standardized dharma-name **Chè-liǎo** 徹了 (sometimes just **Liǎo** 了 as in the Jiā-xīng Canon colophon of [[KR6q0447]]), *hào* **Yuè-chuáng** 月幢 ("Moon-Banner"). Lay name **Máo Yìng-sēng** 毛應僧, of **Jiāng-jīn** 江津 (Chóng-qìng, Sì-chuān). Born **Wàn-lì 甲寅 7/20 未時** = **1614-08-24** (Gregorian); died **Kāng-xī 丙午 季冬 12/22** = **1667-01-16** (Gregorian); *shì-shòu* **53**, *sēng-là* **37**.

**Formation.** Mother Táo 陶 dreamed of a monk presenting a peach before conception; tonsured in youth after contemplating the *Jīn-gāng jīng* on life-death mastery. At 16 *suì* (壬申 = 1632) visited **Bǎo-dǐng** 寶頂 with his mother; a young monk prompted him to take up *niàn-fó*. Awakened initial *yí-qíng* on reading the *Léng-yán jīng*; physically ill with a cold-fever, he vowed to leave the world and was tonsured at **Jì-míng-shī** 寄名師's monastery. Early training at **Chóng-qìng Pò-xuě** 破雪和尚 on "*niàn-fó yě rù dì-yù, bù niàn-fó yě rù dì-yù*"; three years under **Guī-yuán dà-shī** 歸源大師 on "*wàn-fǎ guī-yī, yī guī hé-chù*", with decisive *xǐng* 省 at the fourth watch. Subsequent study with **Líng-fá hé-shàng** 靈筏和尚 (甲申 = 1644 at **Tóng-liáng Bái-shuǐ-sì** 銅梁白水寺); with **Xiàng-yái héshàng** 象崖和尚 from **丙戌 9** (= 1646/9) at **Wù-chuān** 婺川, and on the Péng-shuǐ tour of 丁亥 (1647); three-year novice-instructor (戊子 = 1648 onwards).

**Transmission and abbacies.** Met **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** at **Yǔ-mén** 禹門 in **己丑 臘** = **1650 early** (post-jiǎ-shēn catastrophe refugee-abbacy phase of Tōng-zuì in Shǎn-xī); served as *wéi-nuó* and concurrent *fāng-zhàng li*; **庚寅** (1650) appointed *xī-táng* 西堂; formal transmission **辛卯 夏** (1651/summer) with *yuán-liú fú-zǐ xìn-jīn* 源流拂子信金. Refused the first formal abbacy appointment (Suí-yáng 綏陽 letter of 1650/8); formally took up his own seat only after leaving Yǔ-mén in **甲午 春** (1654) at **Ān-lóng Hú-shān** 安龍湖山. Subsequent seats: **癸巳 冬** (**1653**) first opening at **Diān-nán Shí-bǎo Chán-yuàn** 滇南石寶禪院 (Yún-nán-fǔ, during the Yǒng-lì court's residence there); **庚子 3/14** (**1660**) formally re-invited by *hù-fǎ* Yǔ-hóng Wáng and Míng-yǔ Yáng to reside at **Yún-nán-fǔ Shí-bǎo Chán-yuàn**; subsequent migration to Guì-zhōu seats **Jìng-yǐn** 靜隱, **Nán-míng** 南明, **Lóng-quán** 龍泉, and **Yù-quán** 玉泉.

**Imperial patronage.** The Southern-Míng **Yǒng-lì** emperor (reigned in Yún-nán / Guì-zhōu 1647–61) *xiàng qí dào-fēng, qǐng kāi-shì qiú jì, fǎ-míng Zhēn-fó; shàng xī chěn-fú, ēn wò shèn hòu* — requested dharma-instruction and bestowed the name **真佛** and the *chěn-fú* 椹服 robe, with generous treasury donations for state *zhù-xī* 祝釐 and two submitted memorials. The imperial connection makes Yuè-chuáng one of the few Chán masters formally patronized by the exiled Southern Míng court.

**Heirs.** Principal dharma-heirs **Jiàn-duān Dá-zǔ** 劒端達祖, **Zǔ-bí Dá-zuì** [[達最]] 祖鼻達最 (compiler of [[KR6q0447]]), **Shàn-quán Dá-wèi** 善權達位, **Jí-shèng Dào-zhēn** [[道真]] 極乘道真, and **Xiǎn-jīn Dá-gǔ** [[達古]] 顯今達古. The *dá*-generation constitutes the early-Qīng **Qián-nán** 黔南 (Guì-zhōu) Chán establishment.

**Work.** [[KR6q0447]] 《月幢了禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B237, compiled by **Dá-zuì** [[達最]] with front-preface by **Xú Shì-wěi** 徐士煒 of Hán-shān 寒山 (Gū-sū).

Sources: [[KR6q0447]] juan 2 *xíng-shí* (narrated 1654 at Ān-lóng to disciple **Gū-dēng**); 《錦江禪燈》 j. 12; 《黔南會燈錄》 j. 1, 6; *Yí-nián lù* 441; Fó-guāng.
