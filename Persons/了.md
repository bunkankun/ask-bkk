# Liǎo 了 (Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo 月幢徹了)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí *chánshī*, principal **Pòshān / Zhàngxuě sub-line** dharma-heir of **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695), and — by imperial grace — dharma-name-bearer of the name **Zhēnfó** 真佛 bestowed on him by the Southern-Ming **Yǒnglì** 永曆 emperor during his Yúnnán / Guìzhōu ministry. DILA-standardized dharma-name **Chèliǎo** 徹了 (sometimes just **Liǎo** 了 as in the Jiāxīng Canon colophon of [[KR6q0447]]), *hào* **Yuèchuáng** 月幢 ("Moon-Banner"). Lay name **Máo Yìngsēng** 毛應僧, of **Jiāngjīn** 江津 (Chóngqìng, Sìchuān). Born **Wànlì 甲寅 7/20 未時** = **1614-08-24** (Gregorian); died **Kāngxī 丙午 季冬 12/22** = **1667-01-16** (Gregorian); *shìshòu* **53**, *sēnglà* **37**.

**Formation.** Mother Táo 陶 dreamed of a monk presenting a peach before conception; tonsured in youth after contemplating the *Jīngāng jīng* on life-death mastery. At 16 *suì* (壬申 = 1632) visited **Bǎodǐng** 寶頂 with his mother; a young monk prompted him to take up *niànfó*. Awakened initial *yíqíng* on reading the *Léngyán jīng*; physically ill with a cold-fever, he vowed to leave the world and was tonsured at **Jìmíngshī** 寄名師's monastery. Early training at **Chóngqìng Pòxuě** 破雪和尚 on "*niànfó yě rù dìyù, bù niànfó yě rù dìyù*"; three years under **Guīyuán dàshī** 歸源大師 on "*wànfǎ guīyī, yī guī héchù*", with decisive *xǐng* 省 at the fourth watch. Subsequent study with **Língfá héshàng** 靈筏和尚 (甲申 = 1644 at **Tóngliáng Báishuǐsì** 銅梁白水寺); with **Xiàngyái héshàng** 象崖和尚 from **丙戌 9** (= 1646/9) at **Wùchuān** 婺川, and on the Péngshuǐ tour of 丁亥 (1647); three-year novice-instructor (戊子 = 1648 onwards).

**Transmission and abbacies.** Met **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** at **Yǔmén** 禹門 in **己丑 臘** = **1650 early** (post-jiǎ-shēn catastrophe refugee-abbacy phase of Tōngzuì in Shǎnxī); served as *wéinuó* and concurrent *fāngzhàng li*; **庚寅** (1650) appointed *xītáng* 西堂; formal transmission **辛卯 夏** (1651/summer) with *yuánliú fúzǐ xìnjīn* 源流拂子信金. Refused the first formal abbacy appointment (Suíyáng 綏陽 letter of 1650/8); formally took up his own seat only after leaving Yǔmén in **甲午 春** (1654) at **Ānlóng Húshān** 安龍湖山. Subsequent seats: **癸巳 冬** (**1653**) first opening at **Diānnán Shíbǎo Chányuàn** 滇南石寶禪院 (Yúnnánfǔ, during the Yǒnglì court's residence there); **庚子 3/14** (**1660**) formally re-invited by *hùfǎ* Yǔhóng Wáng and Míngyǔ Yáng to reside at **Yúnnánfǔ Shíbǎo Chányuàn**; subsequent migration to Guìzhōu seats **Jìngyǐn** 靜隱, **Nánmíng** 南明, **Lóngquán** 龍泉, and **Yùquán** 玉泉.

**Imperial patronage.** The Southern-Míng **Yǒnglì** emperor (reigned in Yúnnán / Guìzhōu 1647–61) *xiàng qí dàofēng, qǐng kāishì qiú jì, fǎmíng Zhēnfó; shàng xī chěnfú, ēn wò shèn hòu* — requested dharma-instruction and bestowed the name **真佛** and the *chěnfú* 椹服 robe, with generous treasury donations for state *zhùxī* 祝釐 and two submitted memorials. The imperial connection makes Yuèchuáng one of the few Chán masters formally patronized by the exiled Southern Míng court.

**Heirs.** Principal dharma-heirs **Jiànduān Dázǔ** 劒端達祖, **Zǔbí Dázuì** [[達最]] 祖鼻達最 (compiler of [[KR6q0447]]), **Shànquán Dáwèi** 善權達位, **Jíshèng Dàozhēn** [[道真]] 極乘道真, and **Xiǎnjīn Dágǔ** [[達古]] 顯今達古. The *dá*-generation constitutes the early-Qīng **Qiánnán** 黔南 (Guìzhōu) Chán establishment.

**Work.** [[KR6q0447]] 《月幢了禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B237, compiled by **Dázuì** [[達最]] with front-preface by **Xú Shìwěi** 徐士煒 of Hánshān 寒山 (Gūsū).

Sources: [[KR6q0447]] juan 2 *xíngshí* (narrated 1654 at Ānlóng to disciple **Gūdēng**); 《錦江禪燈》 j. 12; 《黔南會燈錄》 j. 1, 6; *Yínián lù* 441; Fóguāng.
