Chāozōng 超宗. Personal attendant of **Dàbó Xíngqián** [[乾]] (1602–1673). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0584]] 《大博乾禪師語錄》 5 juan, J40 B472.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Sòng-dynasty laywoman **Chāozōng dàorén** 超宗道人 (DILA A018432, ?1087–?1167), niece-by-marriage of *Liú shìláng Jìgāo* 劉侍郎季高, who together with **Wújì dàorén** 無際道人 received *jìbié* under **Dàhuì Zōnggǎo** 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163).
