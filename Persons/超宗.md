Chāo-zōng 超宗. Personal attendant of **Dà-bó Xíng-qián** [[乾]] (1602–1673). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0584]] 《大博乾禪師語錄》 5 juan, J40 B472.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Sòng-dynasty laywoman **Chāo-zōng dào-rén** 超宗道人 (DILA A018432, ?1087–?1167), niece-by-marriage of *Liú shì-láng Jì-gāo* 劉侍郎季高, who together with **Wú-jì dào-rén** 無際道人 received *jì-bié* under **Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo** 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163).
