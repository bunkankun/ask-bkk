**Junkei** 順繼 — Late-Kamakura or Nanbokuchō-period Japanese **Shingon** scholar-monk, the author of the *Shaku makaen-ron dai-jū kō tanzaku* 釋摩訶衍論第十廣短册 ([[KR6t0243]], T79n2537), a short scholastic text on the tenth chapter of the apocryphal *Mohe-yan-lun* 釋摩訶衍論 (the *Treatise on the Mahāyāna Commentary*, a *pseudo-Nāgārjuna* work attributed to Vajrabodhi-Vajrasattva).

The work treats the *kanjō-retsu-kōshō-futai-mon* 勸劣向勝不退門 ("Gate of Encouraging the Inferior toward the Superior, Non-Retrogression") and the *four-faith principle-contemplation* (四信理觀), framed as a scholastic question-and-answer document. The text indicates a scholastic-tradition setting (referencing 學者 — "scholars" — and the standard medieval-Shingon-Tendai *Shaku makaen-ron* commentary tradition).

No lifedates are recorded in DILA; the work itself has no internal date but is conventionally placed in the late Kamakura / Nanbokuchō period.
