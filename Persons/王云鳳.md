**Wáng Yúnfèng** 王云鳳 (also 王雲鳳, 1465–1518), *zì* Yìngsháo 應韶, *hào* Hǔgǔ 虎谷, native of Hé-shùn 和順 in Shānxī. *Jìn-shì* of the *Chéng-huà* era (1487), he served successively as imperial censor (御史), *Shǎn-xī àn-chá-sī fù-shǐ* 陝西按察司副使, and held the imperial commission *fèng-chì tí-xué* 奉勅提學. He composed the *xù-yī* (序一, "First Preface") to the 1506 *Zhèng-dé* edition of the paediatric *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]]), signed at the Zhèng-xué shū-yuàn 正學書院 Jiè-jù-zhāi 戒懼齋 study. He was a noted neo-Confucian scholar with a substantial *wén-jí* literary collection and a close associate of the editor-publisher Guō family who brought the *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* into print.
