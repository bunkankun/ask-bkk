Late-Yuán literatus of Fùchūn 富春 (Yán-zhōu, modern Hángzhōu) and a major disciple of **Yáng Wéizhēn** 楊維楨. Style-name originally **Zǐzhōng** 子中, later changed to **Jiànxīn** 見心 (as preserved in Yáng Wéizhēn's *mù-míng* for him). Wú Fù was the principal editor of Yáng's *Tiěyá xiānsheng gǔ yuèfǔ* 鐵崖先生古樂府 ([[KR4d0586]]): his sorting preface (*Jí-lù xù*) dated *Zhì-zhèng* 6 *bǐngxū* (1346) records that Yáng had once composed 1000+ poems at Kuàijī, judged them unsatisfactory in old age and burnt them, and the present collection is what remains from Yáng's later years at Qián-táng, Tài-hú, and Dòng-tíng. Wú Fù also has a CBDB record (id 66257) giving life-dates 1331–1393.
