Wén Dé-yì 文德翼. Late-Míng / early-Qīng scholar-official, originally of **Jiǔ-jiāng** 九江 (Jiāng-xī) — *zì* **Yì-shū** 翼舒, *hào* **Yān-fú** 燕父. **Chóngzhēn 七年甲戌** = **1634** *jìn-shì*; served as 嘉興府推官. Author of the Wén-cháng family collection 《讀史粹言》 and miscellaneous belles-lettres.

**Buddhist patronage.** Author of the second preface to [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》, written upon meeting **Dōng-yán Zhēn-càn** [[真璨]] at a roadside lodge en route through Jiāng-xī ("師移錫西江偈憩百福"). Catalog dynasty: 清.
