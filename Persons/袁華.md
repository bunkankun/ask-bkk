Yuán Huá 袁華 (1316–after 1373), *zì* **Zǐ-yīng** 子英, native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu prefecture). Birth-date 1316 fixed by his own poem *Zèng Mǐn Zhōng-fú chū-dù shī* 贈閔中孚初度詩 citing his birth as *Yán-yòu bǐng-chén*. Pupil of [[楊維楨]] **Yáng Wéi-zhēn** 楊維楨 — one of the small group whose verse Yáng selected and approved in Zhì-zhèng *guǐ-mǎo* (1363) for the *Kě-chuán jí* ("collection that can be transmitted"). Lived through the Yuán-Míng transition: thirty years of Yuán *Shùn-dì*-era verse plus mid-Hóngwǔ Míng-era service as *Sū-zhōu fǔ xué xùn-dǎo* 蘇州府學訓導; arrested in a *zuò-lèi* (implicating-by-association) charge in the Hóng-wǔ-era literati purges and died in Nán-jīng. Two collections: [[KR4e0056]] *Gēng-xué-zhāi shī-jí* 耕學齋詩集 (twelve *juǎn*, full collection of over 1,000 poems) and [[KR4e0057]] *Kě-chuán jí* 可傳集 (Yáng Wéi-zhēn's strict selection).
