Yuán Huá 袁華 (1316–after 1373), *zì* **Zǐyīng** 子英, native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu prefecture). Birth-date 1316 fixed by his own poem *Zèng Mǐn Zhōngfú chūdù shī* 贈閔中孚初度詩 citing his birth as *Yányòu bǐngchén*. Pupil of [[楊維楨]] **Yáng Wéizhēn** 楊維楨 — one of the small group whose verse Yáng selected and approved in Zhìzhèng *guǐmǎo* (1363) for the *Kěchuán jí* ("collection that can be transmitted"). Lived through the Yuán-Míng transition: thirty years of Yuán *Shùndì*-era verse plus mid-Hóngwǔ Míng-era service as *Sūzhōu fǔ xué xùndǎo* 蘇州府學訓導; arrested in a *zuòlèi* (implicating-by-association) charge in the Hóng-wǔ-era literati purges and died in Nánjīng. Two collections: [[KR4e0056]] *Gēngxuézhāi shījí* 耕學齋詩集 (twelve *juǎn*, full collection of over 1,000 poems) and [[KR4e0057]] *Kěchuán jí* 可傳集 (Yáng Wéizhēn's strict selection).
