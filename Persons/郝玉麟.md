Hǎo Yùlín 郝玉麟 (b. unknown, d. Qiánlóng 10 = 1745), Manchu *qíxiù* of the Bordered Yellow Banner (Chinese-Bannerman 漢軍鑲白旗 per CBDB index_addr 20072). His Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng career: Yúnnán-Guìzhōu Governor-General; Liǎng Guǎng Governor-General (Yōngzhèng 4 = 1726 – Yōngzhèng 10 = 1732); Mǐn-Zhè (Fújiàn-Zhèjiāng) Governor-General (Yōngzhèng 10 = 1732 – Qiánlóng 5 = 1740). It is in the Mǐn-Zhè office that he supervised the *Fújiàn tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0045]]) from 1732 through to its presentation in Qiánlóng 2 (1737). Posthumous title 文恪 Wénkè. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 295 has his biography. CBDB id 57772; death year per *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú niánbiǎo* (#16315).
