**Liú Támíng** 劉達名, modern Chinese-medicine editor; principal editor of *Xuē Lìzhāi yīxué quánshū* 薛立齋醫學全書 (Beijing: Zhōngguó Zhōngyīyào chūbǎnshè, 1999), the comprehensive modern edition of [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's medical corpus.
