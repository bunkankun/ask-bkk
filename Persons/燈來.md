# Dēnglái 燈來 (Sānshān Dēnglái 三山燈來)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng LínjìYángqí Chán master. *Hào* Sānshān 三山 ("Three Peaks"), Wěidù 葦渡 ("Reed-Crossing"), Pánshān dàorén 磐山道人 ("Daoist of Anchored Mountain"). Lay name Zēng Shǒudìng 曾守定, *zì* Bùbō 不波. Lifedates 1614/11/29 – 1685/8/17 (Wànlì 42 / 10/28 – Kāngxī 24 / 7/18, age 72, per the *Gāofēng Sānshān Lái chánshī niánpǔ* 高峰三山來禪師年譜 juan 1). Native of Zhōngzhōu 忠州 (modern Zhōngxiàn 忠縣 in Chóngqìng, Sichuan).

Dharma-heir of Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī 慶忠鐵壁慧機 of Zhōngzhōu, a prominent Sichuan LínjìYángqí master. Abbacies at Gāofēng Kāixī 高峰開禧 (Zhōngzhōu) and at Wǔyún 五雲. The full form of his title — *Zhōngzhōu Gāofēng Kāixī Sānshān Dēnglái* 忠州高峰開禧三山燈來 — records his most significant abbacy.

Author of the *Wǔ jiā zōng zhǐ zuǎn yào* 五家宗旨纂要 [[KR6q0169]], completed by 1657 and later edited and expanded by his dharma-heir [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統. The work is a comprehensive systematic exposition of the distinctive teaching-methods of the Five Houses of classical Chán. Also author of the *Gāofēng Sānshān Lái chánshī yǔlù* 高峰三山來禪師語錄, his collected Chán sermons.
