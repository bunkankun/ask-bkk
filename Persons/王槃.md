Wáng Pán 王槃, *fl.* late 15th / early 16th c., Hóngzhì-era Míng imperial physician of the Tàiyīyuàn 太醫院, served as 副總裁 (deputy chief editor) of the *Běncǎo pǐnhuì jīngyào* 本草品彙精要 ([[KR3ec021]]) of 1505 under Liú Wéntài [[劉文泰]]. No further biographical record. No CBDB id.
