# 巢今

Cháo Jīn 巢今, late-Qīng Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū) *jǔrén* and colleague of [[凌淦]] Líng Gàn. Contributed an editorial note to [[KR3ep053]] *Tuìān yīàn* identifying Líng's medical teachers [[李齡壽]] Lǐ Língshòu and [[李嘉六]] Lǐ Jiāliù.
