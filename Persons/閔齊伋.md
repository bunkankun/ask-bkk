Late-Míng publisher and connoisseur of Wūchéng 烏程 (modern Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng), *zì* Jíwǔ 及武, *hào* Yùwǔ 寓五. He is the principal innovator of the *zhū-mò tàoyìn* 朱墨套印 ("red-and-black colour-printing") technique in the late Wànlì and Tiānqǐ eras: multicolour woodblock printing applied to philological-critical editions of classical texts. His red-and-black-ink reprint of [[蘇軾]] Sū Shì's *Dōngpō Yìzhuàn* ([[KR1a0015]]) is the principal late-Míng exemplum of his aesthetic-but-uncritical editorial method, judged by the *Sìkù* editors as "presentation rather elegant but no actual collation."
