**Wú Zélǐ** 吳則禮 (d. *Xuānhé* 3 / 1121), *zì* Zǐfù 子副, *hào* Běihú jūshì 北湖居士, of Fùchuān 富川 (Yùzhāng / Jiāngxī). Son of the noted *Rénzōng*-era censor Wú Zhōngfù 吳中復 (*Tiě yùshǐ* — "Iron Censor"). Through his father's *yīn* (hereditary privilege) entered office; reached *Zhí Mìgé*, *Zhī Guózhōu*. At some point in mid-career was demoted to *Jīngzhōu* (the *Cù bǎiyōu jí xíng* poem refers to this). Late in life retired to *Yùzhāng*, taking the *hào* Běihú jūshì. Died at *Guózhōu*; his son Wú Jiōng 吳坰 collected his works as *Běihú jí* 北湖集 [[KR4d0117]] in 30 *juǎn* with a preface by Hán Jū 韓駒 dated *Xuānhé rényín* (1122). Associates included Táng Gēng 唐庚 [[唐庚]], Hán Jū, Zēng Yū 曾紆, Chén Shīdào 陳師道 [[陳師道]] — solidly within the late-*Yuányòu* / Jiāngxī school's pre-formation network. *Sìkù* reconstruction is 5 *juǎn*. The catalog gives "d. 1121" — confirmed.
