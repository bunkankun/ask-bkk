**Wú Zé-lǐ** 吳則禮 (d. *Xuān-hé* 3 / 1121), *zì* Zǐ-fù 子副, *hào* Běi-hú jūshì 北湖居士, of Fù-chuān 富川 (Yù-zhāng / Jiāng-xī). Son of the noted *Rén-zōng*-era censor Wú Zhōng-fù 吳中復 (*Tiě yù-shǐ* — "Iron Censor"). Through his father's *yīn* (hereditary privilege) entered office; reached *Zhí Mì-gé*, *Zhī Guó-zhōu*. At some point in mid-career was demoted to *Jīng-zhōu* (the *Cù bǎi-yōu jí xíng* poem refers to this). Late in life retired to *Yù-zhāng*, taking the *hào* Běi-hú jūshì. Died at *Guó-zhōu*; his son Wú Jiōng 吳坰 collected his works as *Běi-hú jí* 北湖集 [[KR4d0117]] in 30 *juǎn* with a preface by Hán Jū 韓駒 dated *Xuān-hé rén-yín* (1122). Associates included Táng Gēng 唐庚 [[唐庚]], Hán Jū, Zēng Yū 曾紆, Chén Shī-dào 陳師道 [[陳師道]] — solidly within the late-*Yuán-yòu* / Jiāng-xī school's pre-formation network. *Sìkù* reconstruction is 5 *juǎn*. The catalog gives "d. 1121" — confirmed.
