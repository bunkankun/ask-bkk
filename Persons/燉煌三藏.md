*Dūn-huáng sān zàng* 燉煌三藏 ("Tripiṭaka of Dūnhuáng") — an unidentified Western-Jìn translator working at Dūnhuáng. The pseudonym (likely a Chinese-language honorific rather than a personal name) is associated with the translation of *[[KR6f0017|Fó shuō jué dìng pí ní jīng 佛說決定毘尼經]]* (T0325). Catalogues do not preserve the translator's actual name. Birth and death dates not preserved. The "Tripiṭaka of Dūnhuáng" honorific is similar to that of [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù (the *Dūn-huáng pútísà* 敦煌菩薩) and may indicate a Western-Jìn translator working in the Dūnhuáng region in Zhú Fǎhù's circle.
