# Shǒu-lǐ 守禮 (Táng Shǒu-lǐ 唐守禮)

Late-Míng lay Buddhist and literary associate of [[張守約|Méi-cūn jūshì Zhāng Shǒu-yuē]] 梅村居士張守約. Surname Táng 唐; *hào* Zhèn-shān jū-shì 鎮山居士 ("Mountain-Anchored Layman"). Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Preface-writer for Zhāng's *Nǐ Hán-shān shī* [[KR6q0217]]; preface-signature reads *shè mò Zhèn-shān jū-shì Táng Shǒu-lǐ shí* 社末鎮山居士唐守禮識, identifying him as a junior member of the lay-Buddhist community ("*shè mò* 社末" — junior member of the *shè* 社, community) to which Zhāng belonged.

**Note**: The Kanripo catalog labels 守禮's dynasty as 唐 (Táng), which is a transcription error — 唐 is his surname, not a dynastic designation. He is properly a Míng figure.
