# Yuánjī 圓璣

Late-eleventh-century Chán monk of Lúshān 廬山 Yuántōng Chóngshèng chányuàn 圓通崇勝禪院; collator of the Northern Sòng printing (1101) of the *Fényáng Wúdé chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0054]]) reissued by [[守中]] Shǒuzhōng under the Jiànzhōng Jìngguó reign-period.
