Hú Guāng-dà 胡光大 (lifedates not recorded; fl. early 14th century, c. 1315). Son of [[胡震]] (Hú Zhèn, fl. 1305, of Lú-shān shēn-xī).

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the *posthumous compiler* of [[KR1a0075]] *Zhōuyì yǎn yì* 周易衍義. His father Hú Zhèn died c. 1305 with the manuscript almost-finished; ten years later (c. 1315) Hú Guāng-dà undertook the final compilation and editorial-arrangement to bring the work to publication. His *shí yǔ* 識語 (recognition-note, preserved at the back of his father's auto-preface in the Sìkù base) records the death-bed scene where his father committed the unfinished manuscript to his care.

CBDB has no Yuán-period *Hú Guāng-dà* entry under that name (the only CBDB *Hú Guāng-dà* entry, id 647570, is Qīng-period).
