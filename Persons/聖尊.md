Shōson 聖尊, Tendai *shōmyō* teacher of the **Ōhara Raigō-in** liturgical-music lineage in the **Kamakura / Nanboku-chō period**. Precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works; the *Shōmyō kuden* [[KR6t0428]] is conventionally dated **c. 1200–1400**. The work is one of the principal documentary records of the **textualization of the oral tradition** that occurred in the late-medieval *shōmyō* lineages as senior teachers wrote down material previously transmitted only orally. The DILA authority id is `A001262`.
