Lǔ Zēngyù 魯曾煜 (d. 1753), *zì* Qǐměi 啟美 (also 秋塍 Qiūchéng), a Kuàijī 會稽 (Zhèjiāng) native, was a *jǔrén* of Kāngxī 35 (1696) and one of the most prolific Yōngzhèng–Qiánlóng-era provincial-gazetteer compilers. He served as the principal *biānzuǎn* of the *Guǎngdōng tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0053]]) of 1731 under Governor Hǎo Yùlín, and later directed Fújiàn provincial-gazetteer projects in the early Qiánlóng period. He is also represented in the literary corpus through the anthology *Qiūchéng wénchāo* 秋塍文鈔.
