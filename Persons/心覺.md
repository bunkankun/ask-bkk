Shinkaku 心覺 (1117–1180), Shingon-line Siddham scholar of the **Ninna-ji** 仁和寺 lineage in mid-Heian Kyoto, son of the Fujiwara aristocrat **Fujiwara no Akiyori** 藤原顯頼. He trained at the Ninna-ji under **Kanken** 寛遍 and became a senior Shingon teacher in the **Hirosawa-ryū** 広沢流 esoteric lineage. His chief surviving work is the *Tara-yō-ki / Shittan yō-ki* [[KR6t0418]], a three-fascicle Siddham scholarly treatise notable for its pedagogical use of the *iroha*-Siddham correspondence as a Japanese mnemonic for the fifty Siddham letters. The DILA authority id is `A001254`. He died at Ninna-ji in **Jishō 4 / 1180**, age 64.
