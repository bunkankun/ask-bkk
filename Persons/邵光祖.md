Yuán-Míng *yímín* scholar, *zì* Hóngdào 宏道, of Wú 吳 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū); claims Luòyì 洛邑 as his ancestral place. CBDB lifedates 1301–1375 (cbdbId 104372). The *Jiāngnán tōngzhì* "*Rúlín zhuàn*" records him as having taught nearly 30 years, expert in three Classics, with a *Shàngshū jíyì* 尚書集義 to his credit. He is principally remembered for his *Jiǎntú zhī lì* 檢圖之例 (1 *juàn*), the worked-examples appendix to Sīmǎ Guāng's [[司馬光]] phonological chart-book *Qièyùn zhǐzhǎngtú* [[KR1j0058]] — which Shào composed because the earlier *jiǎnlì* in circulation conflicted with the charts and could not (he thought) be Sīmǎ Guāng's. From Wáng Xíng's postface dated Hóngwǔ 23 (1390), Shào had died "several years earlier" — a Yuán *yímín* still living into the early Míng (Sìkù *tíyào*).
