# Wèi Tiānyīng 魏天應 (fl. mid-13th c.)

*Hào* Méishù 梅墅. Native of Mǐn (Fújiàn). Self-styled "*xiānggòng jìnshì*" (a successful Provincial Examination *jǔrén* below the *jìnshì* degree). He is the principal compiler of the *Lùnxué shéngchǐ* 論學繩尺 [[KR4h0056]] in 10 *juǎn* — the foundational anthology of *lùn* (essay) examination-prose models from the late-Southern-Sòng examination system. Working with the annotator **Lín Zǐcháng** 林子長 (*hào* Bǐfēng 筆峯, *jīngxué jiàoyù*), he assembled and classified 156 model essays. *Floruit* in the late Southern Sòng, probably the *Bǎoyòu* (1253–1258) to *Xiánchún* (1265–1274) period. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
