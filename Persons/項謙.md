# Xiàng Qiān 項謙

Late-Míng / early-Qīng lay-Buddhist of the Xiàng 項 family of Jiāhé 嘉禾 (modern Jiāxīng 嘉興), Buddhist name **Hóngcí** 弘慈. Descendant of the mid-Míng general [[項忠]] Xiàng Zhōng (1421–1502), son of [[項俊卿]] Xiàng Jùnqīng (*Lánzhāi gōng* 蘭齋公).

Preface-author of the re-printed *Zhǔntí fénxiū xīdì chànhuǐ xuánwén* 准提焚修悉地懺悔玄文 ([[KR6j0760]]), titled *Chóngzǐ fómǔ Zhǔntí fénxiū xīdì chànhuǐ xuánwén xù* 重梓佛母准提焚修悉地懺悔玄文序, dated 壬辰季夏 (mid-summer 1652). The preface narrates his family's transmission of *Cuṇḍā* devotion and credits the dhāraṇī with his survival of the 1644 fall of Beijing to [[李自成]] Lǐ Zìchéng, his subsequent southward flight, and naval mishaps at Dòngtíng 洞庭 lake and during postings to Zhāngzhōu 漳州. He encountered the *xuánwén* in Fújiàn through the monk [[隱空]] Yǐnkōng of Guǎngjìsì 廣濟寺, who showed him both the liturgy and protector-deity images transmitted from the Míng-dynasty visionary lay practitioner [[夏道人]] Xià Dàorén.
