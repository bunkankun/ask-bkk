**Zhèng Yǐn** 鄭隱 was a Five-Dynasties Daoist author, traditional attributed author of [[KR5c0324]] *Zhēn yuán miào dào yào lüè* 真元妙道要略 — a Daoist alchemical summary, often cited as containing the earliest mention of an early form of **gunpowder** (*huǒ yào* 火藥) in connection with saltpetre/sulfur alchemical mishaps. (Distinguish from the Eastern-Jìn Zhèng Yǐn 鄭隱, teacher of [[葛洪]] — different person.)
