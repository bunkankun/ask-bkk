Yáng Guànqīng 楊冠卿 (b. 1139, *zì* Mèngxī 夢錫, *hào* Kètíng 客亭), of *Jiānglíng* 江陵 (modern Húběi). Son of Yáng Jìhóng 楊季洪 (*lǐyuán* of *Nánxú* during early *Shàoxīng*). *Jìnshì* candidate (did not pass); served at *Jiǔjiāng róngsī* as staff-officer; reached the prefecture of *Guǎngzhōu* but was dismissed for unspecified offenses. Retired at *Lín'ān* 臨安 in old age, where Jiāng Kuí 姜夔 wrote a poem to him. Famous in his day for *sìliù* parallel prose and *jíDù* (Dù Fǔ centos — the latter not preserved). CBDB id 27710. Author of [[KR4d0277]] *Kètíng lèigǎo*.
