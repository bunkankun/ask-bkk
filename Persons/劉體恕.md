**Liú Tǐshù** 劉體恕 (fl. mid-Qián-lóng era, c. 1730–1770), *hào* **Yìlíng Wúwǒzǐ** 義陵無我子, native of **Yìlíng** 義陵 (Húběi, modern *Yìzhōng* district). Mid-Qīng Lǚzǔ planchette compiler, principal editorial hand behind the **Lǚzǔ quán shū** 呂祖全書 — the most extensive Qīng compilation of the Lǚ Dòngbīn corpus, in 64 juàn — first printed mid-eighteenth century. Liú is the prefaces-author of the *Lǚ zǔ běn zhuàn* 呂祖本傳 ([[KR5i0045]]) preserved in DZJY; the *běn zhuàn* is an item from his broader compilation. He is the principal Húběi-Yízhōng counterpart to the Sìchuān planchette network represented by [[柳守元]] and [[張清夜]]. No CBDB record located.
