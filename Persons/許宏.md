**Xǔ Hóng** 許宏 (*fl.* early Ming, *Yǒnglè* era 1403–1424, precise lifedates unrecorded). Of Jiànān 建安 (modern Jiànōu 建甌, Fújiàn). Early-Ming physician. Compiler of the *Jīnjìng nèitái fāngyì* 金鏡內臺方議 ([[KR3ed048]]), a 12-*juǎn* systematic question-and-answer commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* and *Jīnguì yàolüè* recipe corpus of Zhāng Zhòngjǐng (conventional date *yǒnglè* 17 = 1419). His work was poorly transmitted until late Ming, when it was discovered in manuscript and prefaced by Féng Shìrén 馮士仁 of XīShǔ (Sìchuān). Xǔ Hóng is not in CBDB; no biographical record outside the *Fāngyì* attribution. Multiple Ming-era physicians named Xǔ Hóng exist in popular medical anthologies; this Xǔ Hóng is distinguished by the *Fāngyì* attribution and the Jiànān origin.
