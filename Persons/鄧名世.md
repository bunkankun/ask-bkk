Dèng Míng-shì 鄧名世 (fl. 1131; CBDB id 24920), native of Lín-chuān 臨川 in Fǔ-zhōu 撫州 (Jiāng-xī), was a Southern-Sòng *Shào-xīng* 1 (1131) *jìnshì*. He served under Gāo-zōng as *Sī-fēng yuán-wài láng* 司封員外郎 and *Bù-shū láng* 部部郎 in the *Tài-cháng sì*. His chief scholarly work is the *Gǔ-jīn xìng-shì shū biàn-zhèng* 古今姓氏書辯證 ([[KR3k0018]]) in 40 *juan*, the principal Southern-Sòng critical *xìng-shū* — a comprehensive correction of Lín Bǎo's *Yuán-hé xìng-zuǎn* and Zhèng Qiáo's *Tōng-zhì · Shì-zú lüè* — completed at the end of his life and edited posthumously by his son Dèng Chūn-nián 鄧椿年. His biography is not preserved in the *Sòng shǐ*; what survives is largely his work and the editorial preface by his son.
