Dèng Míngshì 鄧名世 (fl. 1131; CBDB id 24920), native of Línchuān 臨川 in Fǔzhōu 撫州 (Jiāngxī), was a Southern-Sòng *Shàoxīng* 1 (1131) *jìnshì*. He served under Gāozōng as *Sīfēng yuánwài láng* 司封員外郎 and *Bùshū láng* 部部郎 in the *Tàicháng sì*. His chief scholarly work is the *Gǔjīn xìngshì shū biànzhèng* 古今姓氏書辯證 ([[KR3k0018]]) in 40 *juan*, the principal Southern-Sòng critical *xìngshū* — a comprehensive correction of Lín Bǎo's *Yuánhé xìngzuǎn* and Zhèng Qiáo's *Tōngzhì · Shìzú lüè* — completed at the end of his life and edited posthumously by his son Dèng Chūnnián 鄧椿年. His biography is not preserved in the *Sòng shǐ*; what survives is largely his work and the editorial preface by his son.
