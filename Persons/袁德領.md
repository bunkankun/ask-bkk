Contemporary Chinese specialist of Dūnhuáng Buddhist manuscripts at the National Library of China, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Co-editor (with [[上山大峻]]) of *Púsà zǒngchí fǎ* ([[KR6v0030]]). His principal contribution was the identification of the previously mis-catalogued *Běi-chéng* 98 manuscript (formerly listed as *Shìshì zá-wén gǎo* 釋氏雜文稿 in the *Dūnhuáng jié yú lù*) as a second witness of the *Púsà zǒngchí fǎ*, allowing the first proper critical edition of the text.
