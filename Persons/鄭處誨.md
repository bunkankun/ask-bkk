**Zhèng Chǔhuì** 鄭處誨 (fl. 834–864), *zì* Yánměi 延美, native of *Xíngyáng* 滎陽. Grandson of the *Dézōng-Xiànzōng*-era chief minister Zhèng Yúqìng 鄭餘慶 (746–820). *Jìnshì* of *Tàihé* 8 (834). After early service as a *xiàoshū láng* in the Imperial Library — the post in which he is said to have begun compiling [[KR3l0009]] *Mínghuáng záilù* 明皇雜錄 — he rose through the bureaucracy to *Jiǎnxiào Xíngbù Shàngshū* and *Xuānwǔ Jūn Jiédùshǐ* (Military Commissioner of Xuānwǔ Army, Biànzhōu/Kāifēng region). His biography is attached to that of his grandfather in *Jiù Táng shū*. CBDB id 33161 records *c_fl_earliest_year* 844 and *c_fl_latest_year* 864. The completed *Mínghuáng záilù* is dated to *Dàzhōng* 9 (855); the *Biélù* of 12 supplementary entries is also attributed to him.
