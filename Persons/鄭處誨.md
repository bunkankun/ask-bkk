**Zhèng Chǔ-huì** 鄭處誨 (fl. 834–864), *zì* Yán-měi 延美, native of *Xíng-yáng* 滎陽. Grandson of the *Dé-zōng-Xiàn-zōng*-era chief minister Zhèng Yú-qìng 鄭餘慶 (746–820). *Jìnshì* of *Tài-hé* 8 (834). After early service as a *xiào-shū láng* in the Imperial Library — the post in which he is said to have begun compiling [[KR3l0009]] *Mínghuáng záilù* 明皇雜錄 — he rose through the bureaucracy to *Jiǎn-xiào Xíng-bù Shàng-shū* and *Xuān-wǔ Jūn Jié-dù-shǐ* (Military Commissioner of Xuān-wǔ Army, Biàn-zhōu/Kāi-fēng region). His biography is attached to that of his grandfather in *Jiù Táng shū*. CBDB id 33161 records *c_fl_earliest_year* 844 and *c_fl_latest_year* 864. The completed *Mínghuáng záilù* is dated to *Dà-zhōng* 9 (855); the *Bié-lù* of 12 supplementary entries is also attributed to him.
