**Hú Yáochén** 胡堯臣 (Míng-period publisher / editor; precise dates unknown). Compiled and printed *Fúxī wéncuì* 浮溪文粹 [[KR4d0146]] in 15 *juǎn* — a Míng-period *zhāicí* (extracts) compilation of Wāng Zǎo's [[汪藻]] writings drawn from secondary sources after the original *Fúxī jí* was lost. The compiler-of-record is unknown; Hú Yáochén is the publisher who *shòuzǐ* (committed-to-blocks-and-printed) the work for circulation. The catalog gives Hú's dynasty as 明 (Míng).
