Liú Tǒngxūn 劉統勳 (1700–1773), *zì* Yánqīng 延清, *hào* Ěrdùn 爾鈍, posthumously *Wénzhèng* 文正, was a leading Mid-Qīng Qiánlóng-court official. Native of Zhūchéng 諸城 (Shāndōng). Father of Liú Yōng 劉墉 (the famous calligrapher and Qiánlóng minister). *Jìnshì* of *Yōngzhèng èr* (1724); rose to *Dōnggé dàxuéshì*. Compiler-in-chief (with Jiǎng Pǔ) of the *Yù lǎn jīngshǐ jiǎng yì* 御覽經史講義 ([[KR3a0110]]). One of the foundational official-scholars of the Qiánlóng court. CBDB id 56847.
