Mid-Qiánlóng grand secretary and waterworks specialist; native of Wújiāng 吳江, Sūzhōu prefecture (modern Jiāngsū). *Zì* Shàngzuǒ 尚佐; *hào* Zhuóxiū 拙修; posthumous name Wéngōng 文恭. Took the *jìnshì* in Yōngzhèng 8 (1730), entering the Hanlin Academy. Spent decades in waterworks administration—on the Yellow River, the Grand Canal, and the Huái—as commissioner for water-conservancy in successive provincial postings. Promoted to *Tàizǐ tàibǎo* and Senior Grand Secretary in his late seventies. He served as the principal director of compilation for three of the Qiánlóng-era *Shítōng* continuations—*Xù Wénxiàn Tōngkǎo* 續文獻通考 ([[KR2m0014]]), *Xù Tōngdiǎn* 續通典 ([[KR2m0016]]), and the parallel *Xù Tōngzhì* 續通志—working alongside Cáo Rénhǔ 曹仁虎. The corpus of his memorials and case-records on river management is preserved in the *Hénán Héfáng tōngkǎo* and similar agency reports. CBDB confirms 1711–1794.
