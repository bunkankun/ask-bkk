# Rú Yīng 如瑛

Yuán-dynasty monk, junior-disciple (*xiǎo shī* 小師) of the Yuán Buddhist ritual master [[德因|Déyīn]] 德因 of Lóngquányuàn 龍泉院 on Gāofēng 高峰. Identified in the compilation's front matter as *Liùzǔyuàn zhùchí* 六祖院住持 ("Abbot of the Sixth Patriarch Cloister"), an affiliated or nearby establishment. Lifedates unrecorded.

Rú Yīng's one recorded contribution to the canonical tradition is his editorial compilation of Déyīn's collected ritual speech as the *Gāofēng lóngquán yuàn Yīn shī jí xián yǔlù* [[KR6q0165]] in 15 juan, presented with a preface by Shěn Shìchāng 沈世昌 in Zhìyuán 24 (1287). No independent biographical material survives.
