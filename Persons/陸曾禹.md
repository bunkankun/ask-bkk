Mid-Qiánlóng *jiānshēng* (Imperial-University student) of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu); composed the *Jiùjī pǔ* 救饑譜, which was selectively reformulated by Ní Guólián 倪國璉 (q.v.) into the *Kāngjì lù* 康濟錄 ([[KR2m0049]]) and given imperial approval in *Qiánlóng* 4 (1739). Precise life-dates and biographical details not transmitted; *fl.* c. 1735.
