# Jìngjiè 淨戒

Ming-era Chán monk; re-collator (*chóng jiào* 重校) of the 21-juan recension of the *Chán zōng sòng gǔ lián zhū tōng jí* 禪宗頌古聯珠通集 [[KR6q0244]] — a Míng-abridged form of the original 40-juan SòngYuán compilation by [[法應|Fǎyìng]] 法應 and [[普會|Pǔhuì]] 普會. Lifedates and specific biographical details unrecorded.
