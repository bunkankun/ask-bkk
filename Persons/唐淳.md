Southern-Sòng Daoist commentator, *hào* Jīnlíng dàorén 金陵道人 ("Daoist of Jīnlíng [= Nánjīng 南京]"), *fl.* 1229. Author of [[KR5a0122|Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註]] (DZ 122), a two-juan *Yīnfú jīng* commentary with preface by Mèng Chuòrán 孟綽然 dated 1229. The commentary selects quotations from Hèshànggōng 河上公, Lǚ Dòngbīn 呂洞賓, and the Old Mother (*Lǎomǔ* 老母) on meditative self-cultivation. No CBDB record was found.
