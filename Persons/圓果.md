# Yuánguǒ 圓果

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Buddhist practitioner; disciple-editor of [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655). Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of Zhìxù's *Jué yú biān* 絕餘編 [[KR6q0205]] — a four-juan collection of his master's vow-texts, letters, and doctrinal essays through 1642. No independent biographical material preserved.
