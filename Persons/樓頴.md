# Lóu Yǐng 樓頴

Táng-era Buddhist compiler. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of the *Shàn-huì dà-shì yǔlù* 善慧大士語錄 [[KR6q0272]] — a four-juan Táng-compiled *yǔlù* of the Liáng-dynasty lay Buddhist visionary Fù-dà-shì 傅大士 (Shàn-huì 善慧, 497–569).
