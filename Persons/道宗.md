# Liáo Dàozōng 道宗

The eighth emperor of the Liáo dynasty (r. 1055–1101), a major patron of Buddhism who ordered the carving of the Liáo Buddhist canon and assigned [[覺苑]] Juéyuàn the task of preparing the *Dàrì jīng yìshì yǎnmì chāo* ([[KR6j0735]]).
