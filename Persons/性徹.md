# Xìngchè 性徹

Twentieth-century Korean Chogye-order Sŏn master Sŏngch'ŏl 性徹 (1912–1993), based at Haein-sa 海印寺; reopened the *dùnwù dùnxiū* 頓悟頓修 / *dùnwù jiànxiū* 頓悟漸修 debate in late-twentieth-century Korean Buddhism by sharply criticising [[知訥]] Chinul's *Susim gyŏl* ([[KR6q0097]]) position from a "pure-sudden" standpoint.
