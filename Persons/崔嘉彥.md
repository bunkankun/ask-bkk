Southern-Sòng physician-Daoist (1108–1190) of the Lú Shān 廬山 region; *zì* Zǐxū 紫虛, *hào* Xīyuán Lǎorén 西原老人. His four-character mnemonic on pulse theory, *Sì yán jǔ yào* 四言舉要 (alternately *Cuī shì mài jué* 崔氏脈訣 or *Zǐxū mài jué* 紫虛脈訣), is one of the most widely transmitted Sòng-era pulse texts; later Yuán-Míng-Qīng compilations cite or paraphrase it extensively. Survives via numerous derivative editions, including Pān Jí's expansion [[KR3eb001]] *Yī dēng xù yàn*, the bare-text [[KR3eb025]] *Mài jué*, and [[KR3eb033]] *Mài xué lèi biān*.
