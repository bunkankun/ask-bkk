# Wújì Zhènghuì 無跡正誨

Late-Míng Línjì-Chán master (1547 – 1627), abbot of the **Dùménsì** 度門寺, in the [[圓悟|Mìyún Yuánwù]] / Tiāntóng line of late-Míng Línjì-revival lineage. Dharma-teacher of [[乘旹]] Cháizǐ Chéngshí (originally named Liǎofán 了凡), who later compiled the *Lèngyán jīng jiǎnglù* ([[KR6j0707]]).
