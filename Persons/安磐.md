Mid-Míng official, poet, and literary critic; native of Jiā-dìng zhōu 嘉定州 (modern Lè-shān 樂山, Sìchuān). *Zì* Gōng-shí 公石; *hào* Yí-shān 頤山, in old age Yí-shān lǎo-nóng 頤山老農. *Jìnshì* of Hóng-zhì 18 (1505), rising to Supervising Secretary of the Office of Scrutiny for Military Personnel (兵科給事中 Bīng-kē jǐ-shì-zhōng). Active in the early Jiā-jìng court remonstrance crisis ("Dà-lǐ yì" 大禮議, 1521–24), in which he was a prominent voice opposing the emperor's father-promotion programme; he was demoted and eventually retired to his native Jiā-dìng, where he passed his last years as the self-styled "Yí-shān lǎo-nóng" and produced the *Yí-shān shī-huà* 頤山詩話 (preface dated 1528). CBDB gives an index year of 1475 (consistent with a jìnshì of 1505); precise birth and death dates are not recorded in the standard biographical sources. Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]] praised his poetic style as *wǎn-yuē* 婉約 (gracefully restrained) and reprinted several of his pieces in the *Chí-běi ǒu-tán* 池北偶談.
