Mid-Míng official and literatus. **CBDB id 200828, birth 1471, death unrecorded**. *Zì* **Jìngfū** 敬夫; *hào* **Láishān** 來山. Native of **Yìdū** 益都 (Qīngzhōu, Shāndōng). *Jìnshì* of **Hóngzhì *gēngxū* (1490)**. Rose to **prefect** (*zhīfǔ*).

In retirement at Yìdū during **Jiājìng 14–15 (1535–1536)**, he led the eight-man literary society at the **Bēiguō Chánlín** 北郭禪林 (Northern-Suburb Chan Monastery) that produced the **[[KR4h0104]]** *Hǎidài huìjí* 海岱會集 (12 *juǎn*, 479 poems). His role in the society is as senior member and lead author; the compilation was edited by the younger figure [[馮琦]] Féng Qí (son of the also-society-member Féng Yù) and printed under Wàn-lì-era family auspices.
