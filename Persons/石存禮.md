Mid-Míng official and literatus. **CBDB id 200828, birth 1471, death unrecorded**. *Zì* **Jìng-fū** 敬夫; *hào* **Lái-shān** 來山. Native of **Yì-dū** 益都 (Qīng-zhōu, Shāndōng). *Jìnshì* of **Hóng-zhì *gēng-xū* (1490)**. Rose to **prefect** (*zhī-fǔ*).

In retirement at Yì-dū during **Jiā-jìng 14–15 (1535–1536)**, he led the eight-man literary society at the **Bēi-guō Chán-lín** 北郭禪林 (Northern-Suburb Chan Monastery) that produced the **[[KR4h0104]]** *Hǎi-dài huì-jí* 海岱會集 (12 *juǎn*, 479 poems). His role in the society is as senior member and lead author; the compilation was edited by the younger figure [[馮琦]] Féng Qí (son of the also-society-member Féng Yù) and printed under Wàn-lì-era family auspices.
