# Xíngshěng 行省 (Xūzhōu Xíngshěng 虛舟行省)

Born **萬曆己亥 12/22 亥時** = **1600-02-06 Gregorian**; died **康熙戊申 5/6 未時** = **1668-06-14 Gregorian**, *shìshòu* 70, *sēnglà* 24 (per the [[KR6q0465]] juan 4 *xīhú Xūzhōushěng héshàng tǎmíng* 西湖虛舟省和尚塔銘). *Zì* **Xūzhōu** 虛舟 ("Empty Boat"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** [[通容]] 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Native of **Cíxī** 慈谿 (Sìmíng / Níngbō, Zhèjiāng); lay surname **Yáo** 姚.

**Formation.** Erudite as a youth (*yòu tōng rúyè* 幼通儒業). At **18** (= **1617**) a sickness triggered a sudden *chūshì* 出世 vow; began with the *Dàhuì yǔlù* and the *Xūmí shān* 須彌山 *jùzǐ* with initial *xǐng*. Pilgrimage as lay-disciple through Yúnmén, Píngtáo (= 缾匋), Yǔfēng, Lóngmén, Lǐān; final lay-stage at **Jīnsù** under **Mìwēng lǎohéshàng** 密翁老和尚 = [[圓悟]] **Mìyún Yuánwù**, who bestowed the lay-name **Tōngshěng** 通省. **順治甲申 = 1644** (age 46): family ruined and mother dead in the MíngQīng warfare; tonsured at **Xuědòu** 雪竇 with full precepts; transferred to **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng**, who renamed him **Xíngshěng** 行省 to mark formal Chán entry. Transmission **甲午 = 1654** at *Yúyún Chāoguǒ* 寓雲超果.

**Abbacies.** **Xiǎnjìng Chánsì** 顯淨禪寺 (**1655 乙未**); **Hùshēng Chányuàn** 護生禪院 (**1657 丁酉**); **Liúxī Chányuàn** 留錫禪院 (**1659 己亥**); **Hǒushān èryuàn** 吼山二院 (referenced in [[KR6q0465]] Yán Dàcān preface as the seat at 1658 dating).

**Heirs.** Six *fǎsì*: **Méiān Chāozhū** 梅庵超諸, **Wènshí Chāozhí** [[超直]] 問石超直 (compiler of [[KR6q0465]]), **Kēxuě Chāocàn** 珂雪超璨, **Xuědìng Chāoshù** 雪定超潪. Two ordained *yìnzhèng* nuns: **Mìqǐ Chāochū** 密啟超初, **Juéwù Zǔní** 覺悟祖尼. Two principal lay-heirs: **Hézhuó Xuě Chāoxìn** 何卓雪超信, **Shěn Shàngxiān Chāoshàng** 沈尚先超尚.

**Works.** [[KR6q0465]] 《虛舟省禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B282, compiled by *fǎsì* **Wènshí Chāozhí** with prefaces by [[嚴大參]] Yán Dàcān (1658) and **Zhāng Wénjiā** 張文嘉 (post-1668). Also [[KR6q0215]] 《虛舟禪師註八識規矩頌》 — his ChánYogācāra commentary on Xuánzàng's *Bā shí guī jǔ sòng*, distinguished by sustained scholastic engagement with Yogācāra doctrine unusual for an early-Qīng Chán master. Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 92.
