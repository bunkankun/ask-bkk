# Xíng-shěng 行省 (Xū-zhōu Xíng-shěng 虛舟行省)

Born **萬曆己亥 12/22 亥時** = **1600-02-06 Gregorian**; died **康熙戊申 5/6 未時** = **1668-06-14 Gregorian**, *shì-shòu* 70, *sēng-là* 24 (per the [[KR6q0465]] juan 4 *xī-hú Xū-zhōu-shěng héshàng tǎ-míng* 西湖虛舟省和尚塔銘). *Zì* **Xū-zhōu** 虛舟 ("Empty Boat"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** [[通容]] 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Native of **Cí-xī** 慈谿 (Sì-míng / Níng-bō, Zhè-jiāng); lay surname **Yáo** 姚.

**Formation.** Erudite as a youth (*yòu tōng rú-yè* 幼通儒業). At **18** (= **1617**) a sickness triggered a sudden *chū-shì* 出世 vow; began with the *Dà-huì yǔ-lù* and the *Xū-mí shān* 須彌山 *jù-zǐ* with initial *xǐng*. Pilgrimage as lay-disciple through Yún-mén, Píng-táo (= 缾匋), Yǔ-fēng, Lóng-mén, Lǐ-ān; final lay-stage at **Jīn-sù** under **Mì-wēng lǎo-héshàng** 密翁老和尚 = [[圓悟]] **Mì-yún Yuán-wù**, who bestowed the lay-name **Tōng-shěng** 通省. **順治甲申 = 1644** (age 46): family ruined and mother dead in the Míng-Qīng warfare; tonsured at **Xuě-dòu** 雪竇 with full precepts; transferred to **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng**, who renamed him **Xíng-shěng** 行省 to mark formal Chán entry. Transmission **甲午 = 1654** at *Yú-yún Chāo-guǒ* 寓雲超果.

**Abbacies.** **Xiǎn-jìng Chán-sì** 顯淨禪寺 (**1655 乙未**); **Hù-shēng Chán-yuàn** 護生禪院 (**1657 丁酉**); **Liú-xī Chán-yuàn** 留錫禪院 (**1659 己亥**); **Hǒu-shān èr-yuàn** 吼山二院 (referenced in [[KR6q0465]] Yán Dà-cān preface as the seat at 1658 dating).

**Heirs.** Six *fǎ-sì*: **Méi-ān Chāo-zhū** 梅庵超諸, **Wèn-shí Chāo-zhí** [[超直]] 問石超直 (compiler of [[KR6q0465]]), **Kē-xuě Chāo-càn** 珂雪超璨, **Xuě-dìng Chāo-shù** 雪定超潪. Two ordained *yìn-zhèng* nuns: **Mì-qǐ Chāo-chū** 密啟超初, **Jué-wù Zǔ-ní** 覺悟祖尼. Two principal lay-heirs: **Hé-zhuó Xuě Chāo-xìn** 何卓雪超信, **Shěn Shàng-xiān Chāo-shàng** 沈尚先超尚.

**Works.** [[KR6q0465]] 《虛舟省禪師語錄》 4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B282, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Wèn-shí Chāo-zhí** with prefaces by [[嚴大參]] Yán Dà-cān (1658) and **Zhāng Wén-jiā** 張文嘉 (post-1668). Also [[KR6q0215]] 《虛舟禪師註八識規矩頌》 — his Chán-Yogācāra commentary on Xuán-zàng's *Bā shí guī jǔ sòng*, distinguished by sustained scholastic engagement with Yogācāra doctrine unusual for an early-Qīng Chán master. Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 92.
