# Sēngyuǎn 僧遠 (Língshù Yuǎn 靈樹遠)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master in the **Sānfēngpài** 三峰派 lineage (i.e., descendants of [[法藏|Hànyuè Fǎzàng]] 漢月法藏 who were later officially suppressed by the Yōngzhèng emperor's 1733 *Jiǎn mó biàn yì lù*). *Zì* Yíngfāng 鎣方; also Língyán Yíngfāng Yuǎn 靈巖鎣方遠 (for his Língyánsì 靈巖寺 affiliation) and Língshù Yuǎn 靈樹遠 (for his later Língshùsì 靈樹寺 abbacy). Native of Chángzhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu region, Jiāngsū). Lifedates unrecorded; active mid-to-late 17th century.

Dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchú** 退翁弘儲 (1605–1672; grand-dharma-heir of Fǎzàng), placing him at the third generation of the Sānfēngpài lineage.

Known for attaining awakening despite chronic illness (*sù yǒu léi jí* 素有贏疾 "long suffered from frailty"): per the preface to his *yǔlù*, he pledged to Hóngchú that "hearing it in the morning and dying in the evening would be without regret" (*zhāo wén xī sǐ wú hàn* 朝聞夕死無憾), and subsequently attained awakening under his master's teaching.

Surviving work: the *Língshù Yuǎn chánshī Yúnyán jí* 靈樹遠禪師雲嵒集 [[KR6q0220]] — his collected Chán sermons, compiled by his disciple [[廣仍|Guǎngréng]] 廣仍. Part of the same Sānfēngpài teaching-community that produced the nuns' *Sòng gǔ hé xiǎng jí* [[KR6q0204]].
