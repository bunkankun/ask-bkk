# Sēng-yuǎn 僧遠 (Líng-shù Yuǎn 靈樹遠)

Early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master in the **Sān-fēng-pài** 三峰派 lineage (i.e., descendants of [[法藏|Hànyuè Fǎzàng]] 漢月法藏 who were later officially suppressed by the Yōngzhèng emperor's 1733 *Jiǎn mó biàn yì lù*). *Zì* Yíng-fāng 鎣方; also Líng-yán Yíng-fāng Yuǎn 靈巖鎣方遠 (for his Líng-yán-sì 靈巖寺 affiliation) and Líng-shù Yuǎn 靈樹遠 (for his later Líng-shù-sì 靈樹寺 abbacy). Native of Cháng-zhōu 長洲 (Sū-zhōu region, Jiāng-sū). Lifedates unrecorded; active mid-to-late 17th century.

Dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chú** 退翁弘儲 (1605–1672; grand-dharma-heir of Fǎ-zàng), placing him at the third generation of the Sān-fēng-pài lineage.

Known for attaining awakening despite chronic illness (*sù yǒu léi jí* 素有贏疾 "long suffered from frailty"): per the preface to his *yǔlù*, he pledged to Hóng-chú that "hearing it in the morning and dying in the evening would be without regret" (*zhāo wén xī sǐ wú hàn* 朝聞夕死無憾), and subsequently attained awakening under his master's teaching.

Surviving work: the *Líng-shù Yuǎn chánshī Yún-yán jí* 靈樹遠禪師雲嵒集 [[KR6q0220]] — his collected Chán sermons, compiled by his disciple [[廣仍|Guǎng-réng]] 廣仍. Part of the same Sān-fēng-pài teaching-community that produced the nuns' *Sòng gǔ hé xiǎng jí* [[KR6q0204]].
