Guǎnghuī 廣輝 (lifedates unrecorded), Sòng-dynasty Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk and commentator (*shì* 釋) on the *(Chóngbiān) Cáodòng wǔ wèi xiǎn jué* 重編曹洞五位顯訣 ([[KR6q0128]], X63 n1236) — the Sòng-period Cáodòng *wǔ wèi* doctrinal compendium re-edited by [[慧霞]] Huìxiá. No independent biographical material survives; no DILA authority entry. Known principally through the editorial role on this text.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0128]] only.
