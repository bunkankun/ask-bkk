Xīngfǔ 興斧, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Yàodì Wúkě Dàzhì** [[智]] (= **Fāng Yǐzhì** 方以智, 1611–1671) at **Qīngyuánshān Jìngjūsì** 青原山淨居寺 (Jízhōu, Jiāngxī). Lead compiler of the 4-juan 《青原愚者智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0483]]); co-compiler with **Xīngqìng** [[興磬]]. Per DILA A007130 with cross-reference to 《九華山志》, Xīngfǔ is also referenced in connection to the Jiǔhuáshān 九華山 monastic-network — perhaps a later abbacy. No independent *yǔlù* located.
