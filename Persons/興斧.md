Xīng-fǔ 興斧, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Yào-dì Wú-kě Dà-zhì** [[智]] (= **Fāng Yǐ-zhì** 方以智, 1611–1671) at **Qīng-yuán-shān Jìng-jū-sì** 青原山淨居寺 (Jí-zhōu, Jiāng-xī). Lead compiler of the 4-juan 《青原愚者智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0483]]); co-compiler with **Xīng-qìng** [[興磬]]. Per DILA A007130 with cross-reference to 《九華山志》, Xīng-fǔ is also referenced in connection to the Jiǔ-huá-shān 九華山 monastic-network — perhaps a later abbacy. No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
