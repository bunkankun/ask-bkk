# Dào-chōng 道沖

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán attendant-monk; disciple of [[蘊上|Lóng-guāng Dá-fū Yùn-shàng]] 龍光達夫蘊上 at Lóng-guāng-sì 龍光寺, È-zhōu. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Co-compiler (with [[道汜|Dào-sì]] 道汜) of Dá-fū's *Jī lèi jí* 雞肋集 [[KR6q0206]]. No independent material preserved.

Distinct from other DILA-recorded monks named 道沖 from earlier periods.
