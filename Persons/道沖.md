# Dàochōng 道沖

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán attendant-monk; disciple of [[蘊上|Lóngguāng Dáfū Yùnshàng]] 龍光達夫蘊上 at Lóngguāngsì 龍光寺, Èzhōu. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Co-compiler (with [[道汜|Dàosì]] 道汜) of Dáfū's *Jī lèi jí* 雞肋集 [[KR6q0206]]. No independent material preserved.

Distinct from other DILA-recorded monks named 道沖 from earlier periods.
