Zhōu Yīng-bīn 周應賓 (CBDB id 128931; fl. *Wàn-lì* 1583–1620), native of Yín 鄞 in Níng-bō 寧波 (Zhèjiāng). Late-Míng *Wàn-lì guǐ-wèi* (1583) *jìnshì*; rose to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Fellow-townsman and scholarly colleague of Yú Yín 余寅. His chief surviving contribution to the catalog is a supplementary 1-*juan* expansion of Yú Yín's *Tóng-xìng-míng lù* 同姓名錄 ([[KR3k0047]]). His other works include a *Lǐ-bù tiáo-jì* 禮部條記 and several memorial collections, all preserved fragmentarily.
