Zhōu Yīngbīn 周應賓 (CBDB id 128931; fl. *Wànlì* 1583–1620), native of Yín 鄞 in Níngbō 寧波 (Zhèjiāng). Late-Míng *Wànlì guǐwèi* (1583) *jìnshì*; rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū*. Fellow-townsman and scholarly colleague of Yú Yín 余寅. His chief surviving contribution to the catalog is a supplementary 1-*juan* expansion of Yú Yín's *Tóngxìngmíng lù* 同姓名錄 ([[KR3k0047]]). His other works include a *Lǐbù tiáojì* 禮部條記 and several memorial collections, all preserved fragmentarily.
