# Ōuyáng Xiū 歐陽修

*Zì* Yǒngshū 永叔, *hào* Zuìwēng 醉翁 ("the drunken elder"), in old age Liùyī jūshì 六一居士 ("the retired gentleman of the six ones"); posthumous title Wénzhōng 文忠. Native of Jí-zhōu 吉州 Yǒng-fēng 永豐 (modern Jiāngxī), through Lúlíng 廬陵 identification. *Jìnshì* of Tiānshèng 8 (1030).

One of the Tang-Sòng *gǔwén* movement's principal figures and one of the most important statesmen, historians, and writers of the Northern Sòng. Offices included *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士, *Shūmì fùshǐ* 樞密副使, and *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事. Close associate of Fàn Zhòngyān 范仲淹 in the Qìnglì reforms; demoted to Chúzhōu 滁州 in 1045 following the collapse of those reforms (where he wrote the famous *Zuìwēng tíng jì* 醉翁亭記). Mentor to a generation of younger scholars including [[曾鞏]], [[蘇軾]], and [[王安石]].

Works include: the *Xīn Tángshū* 新唐書 (co-compiled with Sòng Qí 宋祁), the *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* 新五代史 (sole authorship), a monumental literary collection *Ōuyáng Wénzhōng gōng jí* 歐陽文忠公集, the *Jígǔlù* 集古錄 (one of the foundational works of Chinese epigraphy), the *Shīběnyì* 詩本義 (his *Shījīng* commentary, catalytic in the Sòng reinterpretation of the *Xù*), and the *Liùyī shīhuà* 六一詩話 (one of the earliest *shīhuà* genre works).

As junior member of the three principal compilers, with [[王堯臣]] and [[王洙]], of the *Chóngwén zǒngmù* 崇文總目 ([[KR2n0001]], submitted 1041). The per-class prefaces he wrote for that catalogue survive in his collected works and served as the primary source for the Sìkù editors' reconstruction of its discursive apparatus.
