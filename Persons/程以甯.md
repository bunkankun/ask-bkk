**Chéng Yǐníng** 程以甯 (also written 程以寧), *hào* **Fùguīzǐ** 復圭子 ("Master Returning to Jade-Tablet"), an early-Qīng Daoist exegete active c. late Kāngxī era (fl. 1670–1720). His best-attested work is the *Tài shàng dào dé bǎo zhāng yì* 太上道德寶章翼 ([[KR5i0006]]) — a paragraph-by-paragraph supplementary commentary ("wings") to Bái Yùchán's Sòng *Dào dé bǎo zhāng* — which he constructed by cross-stitching glosses from Bái Yùchán, Lǐ Dàochún 李道純 ([[李道純]], 淸菴), the planchette-deity 玄同子, and other SòngYuán inner-alchemical commentators with his own paraphrases. He is also credited with a *Nán huá zhēn jīng zhù shū* 南華真經注疏 (cf. [[KR5i0036]]) elaborating the Guō Xiàng / Chéng Xuányīng tradition. Chéng's signature line *復圭子曰* identifies his interpolations in the *Bǎozhāng yì*; his work circulated only in *Dào zàng jí yào* and was not part of the Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng. Lifedates inferred from his appearance in DZJY editorial layers and absence from earlier Daoist canons; CBDB has no matching record.
