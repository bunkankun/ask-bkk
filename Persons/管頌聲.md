**Guǎn Sòngshēng** 管頌聲 (*zì* Gēngtáng 賡堂; sometimes signed Guǎn Sòngshēngfǔ 管頌聲甫, *fl.* early Xiánfēng era, mid-19th c., 清), Qīng-period medical-charitable publisher of **Huángyán** 黃岩 (Tāizhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). He is known from his preface to [[KR3el014]] *Jiùshāng mìzhǐ* 救傷秘旨, in which he records that he met his friend [[趙蘭亭]] Zhào Lántíng during Zhào's smallpox-vaccination mission to Huángyán and undertook to publish Zhào's collected *shāngkē* prescription manuscript. The preface is signed "Huángyán Guǎn Sòngshēng Gēngtángfǔ wrote at the *Mǐchuán lóu* 米船樓 (Rice-Boat Tower)" — his studio name — and dated *Xiánfēng* 2 / Mid-Autumn, third day before (i.e., 1852, late lunar month 8). He also bound a separate edition of [[KR3el010]] *Diēsǔn miàofāng* (the Sūn Yìngkē 1836 Gāoyóu edition) with the publication. His personal name and lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
