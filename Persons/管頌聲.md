**Guǎn Sòng-shēng** 管頌聲 (*zì* Gēng-táng 賡堂; sometimes signed Guǎn Sòng-shēng-fǔ 管頌聲甫, *fl.* early Xián-fēng era, mid-19th c., 清), Qīng-period medical-charitable publisher of **Huáng-yán** 黃岩 (Tāi-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). He is known from his preface to [[KR3el014]] *Jiù-shāng mì-zhǐ* 救傷秘旨, in which he records that he met his friend [[趙蘭亭]] Zhào Lán-tíng during Zhào's smallpox-vaccination mission to Huáng-yán and undertook to publish Zhào's collected *shāng-kē* prescription manuscript. The preface is signed "Huáng-yán Guǎn Sòng-shēng Gēng-táng-fǔ wrote at the *Mǐ-chuán lóu* 米船樓 (Rice-Boat Tower)" — his studio name — and dated *Xián-fēng* 2 / Mid-Autumn, third day before (i.e., 1852, late lunar month 8). He also bound a separate edition of [[KR3el010]] *Diē-sǔn miào-fāng* (the Sūn Yìng-kē 1836 Gāo-yóu edition) with the publication. His personal name and lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
