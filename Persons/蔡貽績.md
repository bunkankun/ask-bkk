Late-Qīng polymathic literatus-physician of Yōu-xiàn 攸縣 in Húnán (楚攸); *zì* Nǎi-ān 乃庵. Active across the Jiāqìng and Dàoguāng reigns; over seventy in 1820 (Chén Zuǒ's preface) and still publishing in 1823, so born in the late Qiánlóng period. Outside medicine he produced classical commentary (*Sì shū shì kǎo huì chāo* 四書釋考匯鈔), stone-classic philology (*Shí jīng kǎo zhèng* 石經考正), and arts-historical works (*Lìdài dì-wáng shī fù huì jì* 歷代帝王詩賦會紀, *Lìdài shū-huà zōng lǎn* 歷代書畫綜覽). Surviving medical works in the catalog: [[KR3eb005]] *Yī huì yuán yào* (1823) and [[KR3eb006]] *Yī xué zhǐ yào* (1812). Two further medical titles are mentioned in the prefaces but not preserved here: *Nèi shāng jí yào* 內傷集要 and *Hán wēn jué yào* 寒瘟抉要. No CBDB record.
