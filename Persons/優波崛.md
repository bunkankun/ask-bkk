Yōubōjué 優波崛 (\*Upagupta), Indian Sarvāstivāda master of Mathurā, traditionally counted the fourth patriarch in the Indian *Vinaya* succession after the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* (following Mahākāśyapa, Ānanda, and Śāṇavāsa). His name is invoked in [[僧叡]]'s preface to *[[KR6i0251|Zuòchán sānmèi jīng (T614)]]* as one of the Sarvāstivāda *yoga* masters whose meditation teachings underlie [[鳩摩羅什]]'s Cháng'ān compilation. He is the central figure of the *Aśokāvadāna* tradition (cf. *Āyù-wáng zhuàn* 阿育王傳, T2042; *Āyù-wáng jīng* 阿育王經, T2043).
