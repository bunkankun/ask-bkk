Zhēn-xióng 真雄, *zì* **Miào-yún** 妙雲 / **玅雲** ("Wondrous Cloud"), early monastic name **Shòu-fēng** 壽峰, often referred to as **Dà-bēi Miào-yún** 大悲妙雲 from his first abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673), thus a co-disciple of [[圓法]], [[真璨]], [[真在]]. Born **明崇禎甲戌 9/7 巳時** = **1634-10-28** Gregorian; death year unknown (last fixed datum: at age 56 in 1690 winter; printing of the *yǔ-lù* in 1713 is posthumous or contemporary). Native of **Jīn-huá Wǔ-yì** 金華武義 (Zhèjiāng); lay surname **Xú** 徐.

**Formation.** Mother died when he was 9; he immediately sought tonsure at **Huá-yán-sì** 華嚴寺 under **Zì-huà** 自化律師, taking the early name **Shòu-fēng**. Took full ordination at **Líng-yǐn** 靈隱 under **Jù-dé** 具德和尚. One day during a manual-labor *pǔ-qǐng* he became absorbed and walked off to the **Běi-gāo-fēng** 北高峰 viewpoint — at midnight, looking up to find the moon shining brilliantly, "the great earth and rivers became transparent and unobstructed." Sought out a series of teachers: **Pí-líng Lú-shè** 毘陵盧舍 / **Zǔ-yìn** 祖印, **Yáo-fēng Fèi-yǐn** 堯峰費隱, **Nán-jiàn Ruò-ān** 南澗箬菴, **Dùn-cūn Fú-shí** 遯村浮石, **Yù-lín** 玉林, **Gǔ-nán Mù-yún** 古南牧雲. **康熙庚戌** = **1670** spring went to **Jīn-míng** 金明 to meet **Jiè-ān**; received final *fù-fǎ* in **壬子** = **1672**.

**Abbacies.** **Shāndōng Lín-qīng-zhōu Dà-bēi chán-sì** 山東臨清州大悲禪寺 (entered after **1672**); **Zhè-jiāng Jiā-xīng Sān-tǎ Jǐng-dé chán-sì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺 (succeeding [[圓法]], who had died in 1669); also **Hǎi-huì chán-yuàn** 海會禪院 within the same complex. The third *qǐng-qǐ* in the *yǔ-lù* is signed by 景德法姪 — younger dharma-nephew within the **Jīn-míng** 介菴 lineage.

**Disciples.** Twenty *fǎ-sì* listed in the *lián-fāng-jì* of juan 5; major figures include **Tiān-rǔ Jī-bǔ** 天乳機哺, **Tiān-chī Jī-shàn** 天癡機善, **Tiān-gǒng Jī-hóng** 天鞏機黌, **Tiān-wéi Jī-dé** 天惟機德 [[機德]] (lead compiler of the *yǔ-lù*), and the lay disciple **Hé Dòng** 何棟居士.

**Work.** [[KR6q0535]] 《大悲妙雲禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B415, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Tiān-wéi Jī-dé** [[機德]] *děng biān*; printed **康熙五十二年癸巳九月** = **1713-10**.

**Sources.** 《大悲妙雲禪師語錄》 vol. 4 〈行略〉, vol. 5 〈聯芳偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 102.
