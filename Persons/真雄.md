Zhēnxióng 真雄, *zì* **Miàoyún** 妙雲 / **玅雲** ("Wondrous Cloud"), early monastic name **Shòufēng** 壽峰, often referred to as **Dàbēi Miàoyún** 大悲妙雲 from his first abbacy. LínjìYángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jièān Wùjìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673), thus a co-disciple of [[圓法]], [[真璨]], [[真在]]. Born **明崇禎甲戌 9/7 巳時** = **1634-10-28** Gregorian; death year unknown (last fixed datum: at age 56 in 1690 winter; printing of the *yǔlù* in 1713 is posthumous or contemporary). Native of **Jīnhuá Wǔyì** 金華武義 (Zhèjiāng); lay surname **Xú** 徐.

**Formation.** Mother died when he was 9; he immediately sought tonsure at **Huáyánsì** 華嚴寺 under **Zìhuà** 自化律師, taking the early name **Shòufēng**. Took full ordination at **Língyǐn** 靈隱 under **Jùdé** 具德和尚. One day during a manual-labor *pǔqǐng* he became absorbed and walked off to the **Běigāofēng** 北高峰 viewpoint — at midnight, looking up to find the moon shining brilliantly, "the great earth and rivers became transparent and unobstructed." Sought out a series of teachers: **Pílíng Lúshè** 毘陵盧舍 / **Zǔyìn** 祖印, **Yáofēng Fèiyǐn** 堯峰費隱, **Nánjiàn Ruòān** 南澗箬菴, **Dùncūn Fúshí** 遯村浮石, **Yùlín** 玉林, **Gǔnán Mùyún** 古南牧雲. **康熙庚戌** = **1670** spring went to **Jīnmíng** 金明 to meet **Jièān**; received final *fùfǎ* in **壬子** = **1672**.

**Abbacies.** **Shāndōng Línqīngzhōu Dàbēi chánsì** 山東臨清州大悲禪寺 (entered after **1672**); **Zhèjiāng Jiāxīng Sāntǎ Jǐngdé chánsì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺 (succeeding [[圓法]], who had died in 1669); also **Hǎihuì chányuàn** 海會禪院 within the same complex. The third *qǐngqǐ* in the *yǔlù* is signed by 景德法姪 — younger dharma-nephew within the **Jīnmíng** 介菴 lineage.

**Disciples.** Twenty *fǎsì* listed in the *liánfāngjì* of juan 5; major figures include **Tiānrǔ Jībǔ** 天乳機哺, **Tiānchī Jīshàn** 天癡機善, **Tiāngǒng Jīhóng** 天鞏機黌, **Tiānwéi Jīdé** 天惟機德 [[機德]] (lead compiler of the *yǔlù*), and the lay disciple **Hé Dòng** 何棟居士.

**Work.** [[KR6q0535]] 《大悲妙雲禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B415, compiled by *fǎsì* **Tiānwéi Jīdé** [[機德]] *děng biān*; printed **康熙五十二年癸巳九月** = **1713-10**.

**Sources.** 《大悲妙雲禪師語錄》 vol. 4 〈行略〉, vol. 5 〈聯芳偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 102.
