**Zhèng Zhēn** 鄭眞 (also written 鄭真; d. 1372; fl. early-Míng *Hóngwǔ* era), *zì* Qiānzhī 千之, alt. *zì* Yuánshàn 元善, *hào* Yíngyáng-wàishǐ 滎陽外史, was an early-Míng scholar and editor of Yín 鄞 county (modern Níngbō 寧波), Zhèjiāng. He was a *xiào-lián* 孝廉 of *Hóngwǔ* 4 (1371). His personal collection, the *Yíngyáng-wàishǐ jí* 滎陽外史集, is preserved separately in the *Sì-kù quán-shū* (KR4d/4e branch). The catalog (and the *Sì-kù tíyào* of [[KR4d0382]] *Sì-míng wén-xiàn jí*) records him as *Míng rén* 明人 — co-compiler, with [[陳朝]] Chén Cháo (sometimes written 陳朝輔 Chén Cháofǔ), of the Yín-county literary anthology *Sì-míng wén-xiàn* 四明文獻, a project of early-Míng local recovery of Sòng literary materials from Yín. The collection's principal surviving piece is the 5-*juàn* selection of [[王應麟]] Wáng Yīnglín's prose — preserved in *Sì-kù* as [[KR4d0382]]. The CBDB identifies him as person 100237 (with the *zì* Qiānzhī and *hào* Yíngyáng-wàishǐ confirmed in the *Míng-rén zhuàn-jì zī-liào suǒ-yǐn* 明人傳記資料索引, p. 787). The duplicate CBDB record 132386 (same *zì* and *hào*) is the same person; consolidation is incomplete in the CBDB. The Kanripo catalog meta of [[KR4d0382]] writes 鄭眞 (the older orthography); the standard Míng-era texts use 鄭真. Compiled work in the Kanripo corpus as co-compiler: [[KR4d0382]] *Sì-míng wén-xiàn jí*.
