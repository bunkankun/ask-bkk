**Zhèng Zhēn** 鄭眞 (also written 鄭真; c. 1310s–c. 1390s; CBDB 100237 records *fl.* 1372 only; **catalog correction**: earlier person-note "d. 1372" was a misreading of the CBDB *fl.* date — Zhèng was demonstrably alive and active as *Guǎngxìnfǔ jiàoshòu* after his Hóngwǔ 4 / 1371 *xiāngshì* first-place), *zì* Qiānzhī 千之, alt. *zì* Yuánshàn 元善, *hào* Yíngyángwàishǐ 滎陽外史 (from the Zhèng-clan ancestral seat at **Xíngyáng** 滎陽 in Hénán), native of Yín 鄞 county (modern Níngbō 寧波), Zhèjiāng. Pupil of [[吳澄]] **Wú Chéng** 吳澄 in his Yuán years; placed first in Hóngwǔ 4 (1371) *xiāngshì*; appointed *Línhuái xiàn jiàoyù* 臨淮縣教諭; promoted to *Guǎngxìnfǔ jiàoshòu* 廣信府教授. With elder brother **Zhèng Jū** 駒 and younger brother **Zhèng Fèng** 鳳 — the **Zhèng sānxiōngdì** 鄭三兄弟 — dominated the early-Hóngwǔ **Sìmíng** (Níngbō) literary establishment. Initially of equal literary repute with **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 (the two jointly composed the *Péizhōng Zhùcúntáng jì* 裴中著存堂記; Zhèng's piece was completed first, and Sòng Lián laid down his brush in deference); but Sòng's later official ascent and Zhèng's continued career as regional *xuéguān* shaped the asymmetric reception. The *Sìkù* editors' explicit rehabilitation in the *Tíyào* to [[KR4e0070]] *Xíngyáng wàishǐ jí* is the principal modern revaluation. Within the Kanripo corpus: [[KR4e0070]] *Xíngyáng wàishǐ jí* 滎陽外史集 (撰, original 100 *juǎn*, surviving 63 *juǎn* in WYG); co-compiler of [[KR4d0382]] *Sìmíng wénxiàn jí* 四明文獻集 (with [[陳朝]] Chén Cháo). CBDB 100237; duplicate 132386 is the same person. The Kanripo catalog meta variously writes 鄭眞 (older) and 鄭真 (standard).
