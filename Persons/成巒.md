# Chéngluán 成巒 (Chéngluán Chuánshàn 成巒傳善)

Mid-seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán monk of the 成-generation (dharma-grandson of Bóshān Wúyì Yuánlái 博山無異元來 through [[智誾|Xuěguān Zhìyín]] 雪關智誾). *Shìzhě* 侍者 (attendant) to Zhìyín. Full form **Chéngluán Chuánshàn** 成巒傳善; commonly signed as simply **Chuánshàn** 傳善. See the cross-reference at [[傳善]] for the detailed editorial biography including the 1656 post-fire reconstruction of the 6-juan *yǔlù* recension.

The 成-generation character places him as a contemporary of **Chéngxǔ** 成許 (named in Zhìyín's 塔銘 as one of the disciples who carried the master's body back to Bóshān) and **Chéngzhèng** 成正 (the recorder of [[KR6q0417]] Yuánlái's *yǔlù*).

Works:
- [[KR6q0418]] 《雪關禪師語錄》 (J27 B198, 13 juan) — as *shìzhě lù* 侍者錄, head recorder.
- [[KR6q0419]] 《雪關和尚語錄》 (J27 B199, 6 juan) — as *shìzhě jí* 侍者輯, sole compiler-editor; *hòubá* dated **Shùnzhì 13 / 1656 2nd lunar month middle decade**.

Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0418]] juan-head signature line "*shìzhě Chéngluán Chuánshàn lù* 侍者成巒傳善錄"; [[KR6q0419]] *hòubá* (1656, self-authored).
