**Lǐ Bórén** 李博仁, Republican-period public-health official, served as *Hànkǒu tèbiéshì wèishēngjú zhǎng* 漢口特別市衛生局長 (Director, Hànkǒu Special Municipality Public Health Bureau) in 1929. He prefaced Chén Shǒuzhēn 陳守真's paediatric textbook *Érkē cuìjīng* 兒科萃精 ([[KR3ej012]]) in December 1929. His preface positions the work as a corrective for what Lǐ characterises as the divided contemporary Chinese-medicine paediatric trade. Otherwise undocumented in standard biographical references.
