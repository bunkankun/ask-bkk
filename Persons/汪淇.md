**Wāng Qí** 汪淇 (*hào* **Dànyīzǐ** 憺漪子, 17th c.), Qīng-period editor and physician. Compiler of the *Bǎoshēng suìshì* 保生碎事 / *Jìyīn cíyòu wàibiān* 濟陰慈幼外編, a neonatal-care supplement appended to the Qīng recension of [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jìyīn gāngmù* 濟陰綱目 ([[KR3ei006]]).
