**Dǒng Hànjié** 董漢傑 (style **Chéngzhāi** 誠齋, *fl.* 1713–1716), early-Qīng Hànjūn (Han Chinese Banner) magistrate of Xīncài 新蔡. Author of the 1716 (*Kāngxī wǔshíwǔ nián wǔ yuè* 康熙五十五年五月) reprint preface to [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Zhěnkē lèibiān* 疹科類編 ([[KR3ej062]]). According to the preface, Dǒng's father had owned a copy of Wǔ's text and used it to treat hundreds of children during the 1617 and 1621 epidemic outbreaks; Dǒng himself in 1713 used the same recension at Beijing to treat over a thousand patients without a single failure. The 1716 reprint became the foundation of the late-Qīng circulation of the work.
