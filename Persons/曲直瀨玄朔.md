**Manase Gensaku** 曲直瀨玄朔 (Chinese: Qūzhíshài Xuánshuò, 1549–1632), Azuchi-Momoyama / early-Edo Japanese physician. Adopted heir and principal successor of [[曲直瀨道三]] Manase Dōsan; carried the Manase-school *Goseihō* tradition into the early Tokugawa period. Wrote prefaces and editorial conventions for several later Edo printings of his master's *Yàoxìng néngdú* 藥性能毒 ([[KR3ec076]]) and other Manase-school medical works. Japanese physician; not in CBDB.
