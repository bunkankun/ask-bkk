**Xuē Dàoguāng** 薛道光 (trad. 1078–1191), *zì* Dàoyuán 道源, styled Zǐ xián zhēn rén 紫賢真人, was the **third patriarch of the Southern Lineage** (*Nán zōng* 南宗) of Daoist inner alchemy — successor to Shí Tài 石泰 (who received the transmission from Zhāng Bóduān). Originally a Buddhist monk before Shí Tài converted him to the Daoist *nèi dān* path. Author of [[KR5d0111]] *Huán dān fù mìng piān* 還丹復命篇 (preface 1126).
