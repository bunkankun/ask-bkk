**Mèng Wénruì** 孟文瑞 (*fl.* Dàoguāng era, ca. 1810s–1850s), Qīng literatus-physician of the North China Plain. Compiler of the *Chūnjiǎo jí* 春腳集 ([[KR3ed125]]), an 1845 selection of his thirty-year accumulation of *jīngyàn* (tested) prescriptions, brought to print at the urging of the charitable-publisher [[謝玉堂]] (Xiè Yùtáng) of Lùhé 潞河 (modern Tōngzhōu, Beijing). According to his preface, Mèng entered the gentry-school tradition and developed a parallel interest in *Qí-Huáng* medicine from his student years; his prescription-corpus drew on family transmission, broad reading, and the cherished collections of named-house physicians. Sparse further biographical record.
