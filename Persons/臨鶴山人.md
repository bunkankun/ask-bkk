Línhè Shānrén 臨鶴山人 (fl. c. 1814) is the pseudonym of the author of *Hónglóu Yuánmèng* 紅樓圓夢, a 31-chapter sequel to *Hónglóu Mèng* 紅樓夢. The prefix "長白" (Chángbái) in the preface description "長白臨鶴山人" suggests a Manchurian or northeastern Chinese origin; "Chángbái" 長白 designates the Changbai Mountains, associated with Manchu identity. The real name, official career, and dates of this author are unknown; no CBDB entry has been located.
