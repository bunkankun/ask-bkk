# Dàoyán 道巖

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, styled Yúncháo 雲巢 Dàoyán (also Fórì Yúncháo 佛日雲巢). Per DILA A001567, dharma-heir of [[崇岳]] Língyǐn Sōngyuán Chóngyuè (1132–1202); named among the 參學 recorders of Sōngyuán's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0311]]). Held successive abbacies at Qiónglóng 穹窿 in Wújùn, at Ruìguāng 瑞光, and at Ruìyán 瑞巖 in Táizhōu (hence Ruìyán Yúncháoyán 瑞巖雲巢巖). Lifedates unrecorded.
