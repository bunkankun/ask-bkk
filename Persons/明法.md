Míngfǎ 明法, LínjìYángqí (Wàn-rú-line / Èr-yǐn-line) dharma-disciple of **Zǐzhōu Chāochuán** [[船]] 梓舟超船 (b. 1619). Lead compiler of the 3-juan 《壇溪梓舟船禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0464]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Míngfǎ děng biān* 明法等編. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
