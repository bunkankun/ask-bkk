# Huìqín 慧懃

Late-Northern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, conventionally styled Tàipíng Huìqín 太平慧懃 after his residence at Tàipíng 太平 and given the honorific Fójiàn 佛鑑 by imperial bestowal — one of the "three buddhas" (*Fǎyǎn sānfó* 法演三佛) in the dharma-house of Wǔzǔ [[法演]] Fǎyǎn, alongside Yuánwù [[克勤]] Kèqín and Fóyǎn [[清遠]] Qīngyuǎn. His teaching, addressed by Fǎyǎn in distinctive instructional verses, is documented in the *Fǎyǎn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0058]]).
