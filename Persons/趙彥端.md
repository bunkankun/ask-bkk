Southern-Sòng *cí*-writer (1121–1175), *zì* Dé-zhuāng 德莊, *hào* Jiè-ān 介菴, seventh-generation descendant of Wèi-wáng Zhào Tíng-měi 趙廷美 (Sòng Tài-zǔ's father's third son, an imperial-clan member). Served as *Zhí Bǎo-wén-gé* Prefect of Jiàn-níng-fǔ during *Qián-dào* and *Chún-xī*; ended his career as *Zuǒ sī láng-guān*. The *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì* records his *Jiè-ān jí* in 10 *juǎn*, *wài jí* in 3 *juǎn*, and *Jiè-ān cí* in 4 *juǎn* — most lost; the transmitted *Jiè-ān cí* [[KR4j0036]] preserves around 142 *cí* in one *juǎn* via Máo Jìn [[毛晉]]'s late-Míng cutting. Famously, Gāo-zōng on reading Zhào's *Yè jīn mén · Xī-hú* line *bō dǐ xié-yáng hóng shī* 波底斜陽紅濕 declared "*Wǒ jiā lǐ rén yě huì zuò cǐ děng yǔ*" 我家裡人也會作此等語 ("our own household-folk can also produce such language") — the *locus classicus* of imperial endorsement of the *cí* form within the Sòng clan. Modern editions: collated text in the *Quán Sòng cí* (Táng Guī-zhāng [[唐圭璋]] ed.).
