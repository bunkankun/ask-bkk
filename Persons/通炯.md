Late-Míng Chán monk of Guǎngzhōufǔ 廣州府, *ménrén* 門人 and *shìzhě* 侍者 of **Hānshān Déqīng** [[德清]]; *zì* **Ruòxǐng** 若惺, *hào* **Jìān** 寄庵. According to Déqīng's own *Zìxù niánpǔ* 自序年譜 (entry for Wànlì 41 / 1613): "in my 68th year — when I first came to Guǎng[dōng], dharma-disciples who followed me numbered several tens, but with time they gradually fell away; only Tōngjiōng and Chāoyì [超逸], through wind and waves and sickness, have stayed by my side without leaving." Served as *shǒuzuò* 首座 at Wǔrǔsì 五乳寺. Principal editor of the *Hānshān lǎorén mèngyóu jí* ([[KR6q0386]]), working up into a compiled collection the *shìzhě* daily-records kept by fellow disciple **Fúshàn** [[福善]].
