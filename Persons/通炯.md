Late-Míng Chán monk of Guǎngzhōu-fǔ 廣州府, *mén-rén* 門人 and *shì-zhě* 侍者 of **Hān-shān Dé-qīng** [[德清]]; *zì* **Ruò-xǐng** 若惺, *hào* **Jì-ān** 寄庵. According to Déqīng's own *Zì-xù nián-pǔ* 自序年譜 (entry for Wànlì 41 / 1613): "in my 68th year — when I first came to Guǎng[dōng], dharma-disciples who followed me numbered several tens, but with time they gradually fell away; only Tōng-jiōng and Chāo-yì [超逸], through wind and waves and sickness, have stayed by my side without leaving." Served as *shǒu-zuò* 首座 at Wǔ-rǔ-sì 五乳寺. Principal editor of the *Hānshān lǎorén mèng-yóu jí* ([[KR6q0386]]), working up into a compiled collection the *shì-zhě* daily-records kept by fellow disciple **Fú-shàn** [[福善]].
