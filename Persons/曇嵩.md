# 曇嵩

Tánsōng (曇嵩) was a Buddhist monk active in Cháng'ān 長安 in 384–385. He is named in [[道安]] Dàoān's preface to the *Ekottara-āgama* [[KR6a0126]] (T125) as the scribe who took dictation during the translation produced by [[曇摩難提]] Dharmanandi with [[竺佛念]] Zhú Fóniàn as oral translator. Dates are not recorded.
