# Chí Běnlǐ 池本理

Native of Gànzhōu 贛州 (Jiāngxī). Active in the Míng period; birth and death years not securely recorded.

The *Míngshǐ Yìwén zhì* records two of his works: *Qíndùn dàquán* 禽遁大全 in 4 *juàn* (lost) and *Qínxīng yìjiàn* 禽星易見 ([[KR3g0049]]) in 4 *juàn* (preserved as 1 *juàn* in the *Sìkù*). Specialized in the *qínxīng* 禽星 (animal-star) divinatory methodology — a sub-tradition of the *Dùnjiǎ* / *Liùrén* system using 36 animal-symbols correlated with the 28 lunar mansions and 12 zodiacal palaces.
