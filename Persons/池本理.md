# Chí Běnlǐ 池本理

Native of Gàn-zhōu 贛州 (Jiāng-xī). Active in the Míng period; birth and death years not securely recorded.

The *Míng-shǐ Yì-wén zhì* records two of his works: *Qín-dùn dà-quán* 禽遁大全 in 4 *juàn* (lost) and *Qín-xīng yì-jiàn* 禽星易見 ([[KR3g0049]]) in 4 *juàn* (preserved as 1 *juàn* in the *Sìkù*). Specialized in the *qín-xīng* 禽星 (animal-star) divinatory methodology — a sub-tradition of the *Dùn-jiǎ* / *Liù-rén* system using 36 animal-symbols correlated with the 28 lunar mansions and 12 zodiacal palaces.
