Wáng Dé-sēn 王德森, *hào* Suì-jū-shì 歲居士 / Suì-hán-jū-shì 歲寒居士 (mid-19th c.; *fl.* 1853). Native of **Yù-fēng** 玉峰 (Kūn-shān, in Sū-zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū), writing at the *Shì-yǐn-lú* 市隱廬 in Wú-mén (Sū-zhōu city). By his own account a member of a medical family — "*fù-xiōng shī-yǒu, mò bù zhī yī*" 父兄師友，莫不知醫 — who entered clinical practice through the standard literatus pathway: family observation in youth, mid-career private reading of the *Sù-wèn* and *Líng-shū*, and gradual return to medicine through family and friendship requests. Author of [[KR3eq086]] *Shì-yǐn-lú yī-xué zá-zhù* 市隱廬醫學雜著, dated by its triply-*Guǐ-chǒu* frontmatter to 1853 (Xián-fēng 3). Not in CBDB.
