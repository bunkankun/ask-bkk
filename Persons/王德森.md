Wáng Désēn 王德森, *hào* Suìjūshì 歲居士 / Suìhánjūshì 歲寒居士 (mid-19th c.; *fl.* 1853). Native of **Yùfēng** 玉峰 (Kūnshān, in Sūzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū), writing at the *Shìyǐnlú* 市隱廬 in Wúmén (Sūzhōu city). By his own account a member of a medical family — "*fùxiōng shīyǒu, mò bù zhī yī*" 父兄師友，莫不知醫 — who entered clinical practice through the standard literatus pathway: family observation in youth, mid-career private reading of the *Sùwèn* and *Língshū*, and gradual return to medicine through family and friendship requests. Author of [[KR3eq086]] *Shìyǐnlú yīxué zázhù* 市隱廬醫學雜著, dated by its triply-*Guǐchǒu* frontmatter to 1853 (Xiánfēng 3). Not in CBDB.
