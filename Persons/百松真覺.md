# Bǎisōng Zhēnjué 百松真覺

Bǎisōng Zhēnjué 百松真覺 (1537–1589), a late-Wànlì Tiāntái 天台 patriarch and a central figure in the late-Míng revival of the Tiāntái school. Master of [[傳燈]] Yōuxī Chuándēng and [[傳如]], through whom his Tiāntái lineage was perpetuated into the seventeenth century.
