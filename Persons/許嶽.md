# Xǔ Yuè 許嶽

Late-Míng former *jìnshì* (前進士) of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu), self-styled *Qiónghǎi Fúcháshēng* 瓊海浮槎生 ("Floating-Raft Student of the Jade Sea"). Author of the introductory preface (No. 541-A) to [[謝觀光]] Xiè Guānguāng's *Bōrě xīnjīng shìyí* 般若心經釋疑 ([[KR6c0160]]), dated Wànlì 15 / 1587 spring — itself a notable late-Míng literary biographical portrait of Xiè as a *sānjiào* polymath.
