**Lǐ Jǐngyuán** 李景元 was a Sòng Daoist author, compiler of [[KR5d0012]] *Yuān yuán dào miào dòng zhēn jì piān* 淵源道妙洞真繼篇 — a three-*juàn* Daoist philosophical-ritual treatise. Biographical details are unrecorded.
