Late-Míng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Yǐ-xuán** 以玄 (also referenced as **Yǐ-xuán Zhèng-mì** 以玄正謐). DILA Authority A000714. Native of **Lín-hǎi** 臨海 (modern Zhèjiāng). Lifedates unrecorded.

Provided the *fēn-huì* 分會 ("division-and-combination") editorial work on the canonical recension of **Wú-jí Kě-dù** 無極可度's *Shí bù-èr mén zhǐ-yào chāo xiáng-jiě* ([[KR6d0165]], X56n0931). His Lín-hǎi (Tāizhōu) location places him within the late-Míng Tiāntái-shān scholastic network.
