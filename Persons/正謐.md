Late-Míng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Yǐxuán** 以玄 (also referenced as **Yǐxuán Zhèngmì** 以玄正謐). DILA Authority A000714. Native of **Línhǎi** 臨海 (modern Zhèjiāng). Lifedates unrecorded.

Provided the *fēnhuì* 分會 ("division-and-combination") editorial work on the canonical recension of **Wújí Kědù** 無極可度's *Shí bùèr mén zhǐyào chāo xiángjiě* ([[KR6d0165]], X56n0931). His Línhǎi (Tāizhōu) location places him within the late-Míng Tiāntáishān scholastic network.
