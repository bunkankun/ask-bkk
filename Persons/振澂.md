# Zhènchéng 振澂

Late-17th-century Qīng Buddhist nun (*bǐqiūní* 比丘尼); disciple of [[符|Língruì Fúānzhǔ]] 靈瑞符菴主 at the Hóngchú teaching-community in Sūzhōu / Wújùn. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Lead compiler-editor (*děng jì* 等記 "et al. recorded") of Fú-nun's *Língruì chánshī Yánhuá jí* 靈瑞禪師嵒華集 [[KR6q0225]]. Part of the Hóngchú teaching-community's distinctive editorial programme of publishing women-masters' canonical teaching-corpora.

Discussed in Grant, Beata. 2008. *Eminent Nuns: Women Chan Masters of Seventeenth-Century China*.
