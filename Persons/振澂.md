# Zhèn-chéng 振澂

Late-17th-century Qīng Buddhist nun (*bǐ-qiū-ní* 比丘尼); disciple of [[符|Líng-ruì Fú-ān-zhǔ]] 靈瑞符菴主 at the Hóng-chú teaching-community in Sū-zhōu / Wú-jùn. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Lead compiler-editor (*děng jì* 等記 "et al. recorded") of Fú-nun's *Líng-ruì chánshī Yán-huá jí* 靈瑞禪師嵒華集 [[KR6q0225]]. Part of the Hóng-chú teaching-community's distinctive editorial programme of publishing women-masters' canonical teaching-corpora.

Discussed in Grant, Beata. 2008. *Eminent Nuns: Women Chan Masters of Seventeenth-Century China*.
