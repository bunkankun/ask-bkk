# Jì Běn 季本

*Zì* Míngdé 明德, *hào* Péngshān 彭山. Native of Kuàijī 會稽 (modern Zhèjiāng). Senior pupil of Wáng Yángmíng 王陽明 (Yáojiāng 姚江) and one of the principal figures of the early *Yáojiāng* school. *Jìnshì* of *Zhèngdé* 12 (1517).

Author of:

- *Yìxué sì tóng* 易學四同 ([[KR1a0046]] / [[KR1a0047]], two related compilations on the *Zhōuyì*)
- *Shī shuō jiě yí* 詩說解頤 in 40 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0036]])
- *Shī jīng chéngxǐng cǎo* 詩經程省草 (now lost in independent form)
- Various studies on the *Lǐjì* and *Chūnqiū*

The Sìkù editors are notably positive on Jì Běn: while they regard the late Wáng Yángmíng school as the principal source of "*kuángChán* (mad-Chán) gloss-the-Classic" excesses, they treat Jì Běn as evidence that the *first generation* of Yángmíng pupils were genuinely engaged with philological and evidentiary scholarship — "in the *Yáojiāng* school's first establishment of teaching, the senior pupils' investigation of the canonical texts and pursuit of *xùngǔ* was like this — when did they actually hold the ['the Six Classics annotate me' (*liù jīng zhù wǒ*)] doctrine and refuse to set up *yǔyán wénzì* (language and writing)?" This is one of the more carefully nuanced *tíyào* judgements on the Yángmíng school.
