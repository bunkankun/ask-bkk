Dào-lè 道樂, *zì* **Bù-yàn** 不厭 ("Without Surfeit"), often referred to as **Huá-yán Bù-yàn-Lè** 華嚴不厭樂 from his principal abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** 聖可德玉 (1628–1701) — the Guì-zhōu / Sì-chuān regional patriarch in the Mǐn-shù 敏樹 sub-line. Born **明崇禎庚辰 8/23 子時** = **1640-10-08** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 51 in **康熙庚午** = **1690**, last fixed datum is the 1693 preface to his *yǔ-lù*. Native of **Sī-nán Yìn-jiāng** 思南印江 (Guì-zhōu); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Married at 13 (*pìn jù* 聘聚 = engagement / marriage), widowed of his father at 27, undertook a 29-year fast, took tonsure at **31** under **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** 敏樹如相. Studied with **Huá-yán Shèng-kě** 華嚴聖可 — questioned about the *Dà-tōng Zhì-shèng Fó* 大通智勝佛 *gōng-àn*, was struck so heavily on the head that "blood ran and I began to know pain." **丙辰** = **1676** spring returned to Guì-zhōu province. Held an interim abbacy at **Xìng-lóng-wèi Lóng-shān-sì** 興隆衛龍山寺 (entered **丙辰九月** = **1676-10**) for three years before war forced him to retreat to **Gāo-tūn-shān** 高坉山. **乙丑春** = **1685** spring at **Lú-zhōu** 瀘州 (Sì-chuān) sought out Shèng-kě again and received *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Xìng-lóng-wèi Lóng-shān-sì** 興隆衛龍山寺 (1676), **Zūn-yì Yù-quán-sì** 遵義玉泉寺 (entered **甲子春** = **1684**), **Chóng-qìng Huá-yán chán-yuàn** 重慶府華嚴禪院 (post-1685, the principal abbacy giving him his sobriquet).

**Disciples.** Eleven *fǎ-sì* are listed in the *yǔ-lù*'s *yìn-jì* 印偈, including **Cí-jì Dé-pǔ** 慈濟德普 [[德普]] (compiler of the *yǔ-lù*), **Huì-yī Pǔ-dìng** 慧一普定, and **Sōng-méi Chāo-huì** 松梅超慧.

**Work.** [[KR6q0538]] 《華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B419, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-pǔ** [[德普]] *jì-lù*; preface dated **康熙癸酉上巳日** = **1693-04-09** by **Yuān-shuǐ Zhòng Bì-xiān Gě-mín** 鴛水仲碧仙葛民.

**Sources.** 《華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行由〉, vol. 3 〈印偈〉.
