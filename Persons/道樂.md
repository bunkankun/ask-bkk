Dàolè 道樂, *zì* **Bùyàn** 不厭 ("Without Surfeit"), often referred to as **Huáyán BùyànLè** 華嚴不厭樂 from his principal abbacy. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Shèngkě Déyù** 聖可德玉 (1628–1701) — the Guìzhōu / Sìchuān regional patriarch in the Mǐnshù 敏樹 sub-line. Born **明崇禎庚辰 8/23 子時** = **1640-10-08** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 51 in **康熙庚午** = **1690**, last fixed datum is the 1693 preface to his *yǔlù*. Native of **Sīnán Yìnjiāng** 思南印江 (Guìzhōu); lay surname **Yáng** 楊.

**Formation.** Married at 13 (*pìn jù* 聘聚 = engagement / marriage), widowed of his father at 27, undertook a 29-year fast, took tonsure at **31** under **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** 敏樹如相. Studied with **Huáyán Shèngkě** 華嚴聖可 — questioned about the *Dàtōng Zhìshèng Fó* 大通智勝佛 *gōngàn*, was struck so heavily on the head that "blood ran and I began to know pain." **丙辰** = **1676** spring returned to Guìzhōu province. Held an interim abbacy at **Xìnglóngwèi Lóngshānsì** 興隆衛龍山寺 (entered **丙辰九月** = **1676-10**) for three years before war forced him to retreat to **Gāotūnshān** 高坉山. **乙丑春** = **1685** spring at **Lúzhōu** 瀘州 (Sìchuān) sought out Shèngkě again and received *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Xìnglóngwèi Lóngshānsì** 興隆衛龍山寺 (1676), **Zūnyì Yùquánsì** 遵義玉泉寺 (entered **甲子春** = **1684**), **Chóngqìng Huáyán chányuàn** 重慶府華嚴禪院 (post-1685, the principal abbacy giving him his sobriquet).

**Disciples.** Eleven *fǎsì* are listed in the *yǔlù*'s *yìnjì* 印偈, including **Cíjì Dépǔ** 慈濟德普 [[德普]] (compiler of the *yǔlù*), **Huìyī Pǔdìng** 慧一普定, and **Sōngméi Chāohuì** 松梅超慧.

**Work.** [[KR6q0538]] 《華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B419, recorded by *fǎsì* **Dépǔ** [[德普]] *jìlù*; preface dated **康熙癸酉上巳日** = **1693-04-09** by **Yuānshuǐ Zhòng Bìxiān Gěmín** 鴛水仲碧仙葛民.

**Sources.** 《華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行由〉, vol. 3 〈印偈〉.
