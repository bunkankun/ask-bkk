# Zhèng Xuán 鄭玄

Style name *Kāngchéng* 康成. Native of Gāomì 高密 in Běihǎi commandery 北海郡 (modern Shāndōng). The pre-eminent Confucian classicist of the Eastern Hàn and the foundational figure of the *Hàn Yì* (image-and-number) and ritual-classics traditions. Studied first with Dì-wǔ Yuánxiān 第五元先 (Jīng Fáng 京房 / Mèng Xǐ 孟喜 line of *Yì* learning), then in his maturity with Mǎ Róng 馬融 at Fúfēng 扶風, where he absorbed the older Fèi Zhí 費直 *Yì*, the *Sānlǐ*, the *Shī*, the *Lúnyǔ*, the *Xiàojīng*, and the apocryphal-classics (*chènwěi* 讖緯) literature. After returning to the East, he refused all official summonses, lived in semi-retirement in Gāomì, and produced commentaries on virtually the entire classical corpus.

His *Zhōuyì zhù* 周易註 ([[KR1a0003]] / [[KR1a0004]]) was lost in the Sòng and survives only through Wáng Yīnglín's late-Southern-Sòng compilation and Huì Dòng's Qīng augmentation; his *Sānlǐ zhù* 三禮註 (on *Zhōulǐ*, *Yílǐ*, *Lǐjì*) survives in full and underlies the standard *zhèngyì* 正義 of the Three Rites; his *Máoshī zhèng jiān* 毛詩鄭箋 became the canonical layer of the *Shījīng* commentary tradition. Note the post-Táng convention of writing his given name 玄 as 元 to avoid the taboo on Lǐ Lóngjī's grandson, the Táng emperor Xuánzōng's name (also 玄); he is therefore frequently called "Zhèng Yuán" 鄭元 in Suí, Táng, and Sòng catalogues.

The *Hòu Hànshū* devotes a long *liè zhuàn* biography to him (juan 35). The Northern Sòng erected him into the temple register of the *Wén miào* 文廟; he is the canonical patron of the *Hàn xué* 漢學 revival of Qīng evidential scholarship.
