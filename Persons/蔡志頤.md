Late thirteenth-century Quánzhēn 全真 Daoist of Wéiyáng 維揚 (Yángzhōu 揚州), *zì* Sǔn'ān 損菴, *hào* Yíngchánzǐ 瑩蟾子 (a *hào* he shared with his teacher). Disciple of [[李道純|Lǐ Dàochún]] (d. 1306) at the Hermitage of Central Harmony (Zhōnghé jīngshè 中和精舍) in Nánjīng 南京. After Lǐ's teaching had grown dispersed in fragmentary manuscripts, Cài Zhìyí gathered them together and edited them into the [[KR5a0250|*Zhōnghé jí* 中和集]] (DZ 249), the principal collection of Lǐ Dàochún's *nèidān* 內丹 sermons, dialogues, *cí* 詞 lyrics, regular and irregular verse, and short prose treatises. The work was carried by Cài to Dù Dàojiān 杜道堅 (1237–1318) at Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu) in 1306 (the *bǐngwǔ* year of *Dàdé* 大德) and printed there with Dù's preface.
