# Zōngróng 宗榮

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué during the Yuántōng Chóngshèng chányuàn 圓通崇勝禪院 abbacy at Lúshān; recorded as a section-compiler of juan 1 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
