Northern-Sòng Daoist of Hángzhōu 杭州 (Zhèjiāng), *zì* Zhúpō 竹坡, author of [[KR5a0151|DZ 150 *Xiūzhēn tàijí hùnyuán zhǐxuán tú* 修真太極混元指玄圖]], a *nèidān* 內丹 chart-text of the Zhōng Lǚ 鍾呂 transmission. According to his own preface (which the Dàozàng editors inadvertently bound at the head of [[KR5a0150|DZ 149]]), Jīn met the Táng patriarch Shī Jiānwú 施肩吾 (Huáyáng zhēnrén 華陽真人) in Nánjīng and received the diagrammatic transmission of the inner-alchemical method. A near-identical preface, with minor variations, is attached in [[KR5a1248|DZ 1248 *Sāndòng qúnxiān lù*]] 14.22a (dated 1154) to Lǐ Sōng 李竦, a disciple of Shī Jiānwú; whereas Jīn merely says *Nánjīng*, Lǐ has *Nánjīng Yīngtiān fǔ* 南京應天府, a designation in use 1008–1016. Jīn must not be confused with the (Táng) Jīn Zhúpō 金竹坡 to whom [[KR5c0923|DZ 923 *Dàdān qiāngōng lùn*]] is attributed: that work is a Southern-Sòng forgery and the attribution spurious.
