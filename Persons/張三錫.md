**Zhāng Sānxī** 張三錫 (*zì* Shūchéng 叔承, *hào* Jiāshān 嘉山, fl. late Wànlì period, 1573–1620, 明), late-Míng physician of Nánjīng 南京. Author of the six-juan *Yīxué zhǔnshéng liùyào* 醫學準繩六要 (printed 1593), a comprehensive late-Míng medical synthesis combining the JīnYuán four-master pharmacotherapy tradition with the standard Míng channel-doctrine emphasis. The *Jīngluò kǎo* 經絡考 ([[KR3ee025]]) is the channel-doctrine pillar of that compilation and also circulated independently. No CBDB match. Standard biographical reference is in Yán Shìyún's *Zhōngyī rénwù cídiǎn*.
