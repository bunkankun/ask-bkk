Huìgōng Kāixiòng 慧弓開詗 (1634–1676 or 1677), Qīng-dynasty Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk, disciple of [[弘贊|Zàishēn Hóngzàn]] 在犙弘贊 (1612–1686) at the Dǐnghúshān 鼎湖山 Qìngyún sì 慶雲寺. *Hào* Huìgōng 慧弓; secondary *hào* Shíjiàn 石箭 ("Stone-Arrow"). Lay surname Zhāng 張, given-name Míngdá 明達. Died on Kāngxī 15 (1676 or early 1677), aged 43.

Kāixiòng's principal surviving work is his collaborative *jì* 記 (supplementary-record annotation) on Hóngzàn's commentary [[KR6q0132]] *Wéishān jǐngcè jù shì jì* 溈山警策句釋記 (X63 n1240) — including the *kē wén* 科文 (topical outline) structuring the commentary. The master-disciple collaboration (Hóngzàn supplying the primary *jù shì* exposition, Kāixiòng the *jì* records) is characteristic of the late-imperial scholastic-commentarial tradition.

Works in the Kanripo corpus:
- [[KR6q0132]] 《溈山警策句釋記》 — collaborative *jì* 記 with his teacher Hóngzàn.
- [[KR6q0484]] 《南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J35 B336 — his own *yǔlù* from the **Nánhǎi Bǎoxiànglín** 南海寶象林 (Guǎngdōng) abbacy, recorded by *fǎsì* **Chuányī** [[傳一]] *děng lù*.

A **different** mid-seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán monk of the same name **開詗**, in the **Shòuchāng Wúmíng Huìjīng** 壽昌無明慧經 sub-line — a *fǎsūn* 法孫 (dharma-grandson/dharma-descendant) of [[智誾|Xuěguān Zhìyín]] 雪關智誾 (1585–1637). Editor (*biān* 編) of [[KR6q0418]] 《雪關禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J27 B198), where the juan-head signature line reads "*fǎsūn Kāixuàn biān* 法孫開詗編". This Kāixuàn is to be distinguished from the Huìgōng Kāixuàn (1634–1676) above: the two are separate contemporaries working in different Cáodòng sub-lineages (Shòuchāng at Bóshān / Yíngshān versus the Dǐnghú 鼎湖 / Yúnmén line at Qìngyúnsì). The Kāixuàn at [[KR6q0418]] was presumably active in the 1640s editing his dharma-grandfather Zhìyín's recorded sayings before and/or after Huáng Duānbó's 1646 death (since the Huáng *tǎmíng* is included in the compiled volume). Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates unrecovered.
