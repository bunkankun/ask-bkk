Huìgōng Kāixiòng 慧弓開詗 (1634–1676 or 1677), Qīng-dynasty Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk, disciple of [[弘贊|Zàishēn Hóngzàn]] 在犙弘贊 (1612–1686) at the Dǐnghú-shān 鼎湖山 Qìngyún sì 慶雲寺. *Hào* Huìgōng 慧弓; secondary *hào* Shí-jiàn 石箭 ("Stone-Arrow"). Lay surname Zhāng 張, given-name Míngdá 明達. Died on Kāngxī 15 (1676 or early 1677), aged 43.

Kāixiòng's principal surviving work is his collaborative *jì* 記 (supplementary-record annotation) on Hóngzàn's commentary [[KR6q0132]] *Wéishān jǐngcè jù shì jì* 溈山警策句釋記 (X63 n1240) — including the *kē wén* 科文 (topical outline) structuring the commentary. The master-disciple collaboration (Hóngzàn supplying the primary *jù shì* exposition, Kāixiòng the *jì* records) is characteristic of the late-imperial scholastic-commentarial tradition.

Works in the Kanripo corpus:
- [[KR6q0132]] 《溈山警策句釋記》 — collaborative *jì* 記 with his teacher Hóng-zàn.
- [[KR6q0484]] 《南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B336 — his own *yǔ-lù* from the **Nán-hǎi Bǎo-xiàng-lín** 南海寶象林 (Guǎng-dōng) abbacy, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Chuán-yī** [[傳一]] *děng lù*.

A **different** mid-seventeenth-century Cáo-dòng Chán monk of the same name **開詗**, in the **Shòu-chāng Wú-míng Huì-jīng** 壽昌無明慧經 sub-line — a *fǎ-sūn* 法孫 (dharma-grandson/dharma-descendant) of [[智誾|Xuě-guān Zhì-yín]] 雪關智誾 (1585–1637). Editor (*biān* 編) of [[KR6q0418]] 《雪關禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J27 B198), where the juan-head signature line reads "*fǎ-sūn Kāi-xuàn biān* 法孫開詗編". This Kāi-xuàn is to be distinguished from the Huì-gōng Kāi-xuàn (1634–1676) above: the two are separate contemporaries working in different Cáo-dòng sub-lineages (Shòu-chāng at Bó-shān / Yíng-shān versus the Dǐng-hú 鼎湖 / Yún-mén line at Qìng-yún-sì). The Kāi-xuàn at [[KR6q0418]] was presumably active in the 1640s editing his dharma-grandfather Zhì-yín's recorded sayings before and/or after Huáng Duān-bó's 1646 death (since the Huáng *tǎ-míng* is included in the compiled volume). Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0418]] juan-head signature line.
