Liú Píng 劉玶 (1138–1185), youngest son of Liú Zǐ-huī 劉子翬 [[劉子翬]] of Chóng-ān 崇安 (Fú-jiàn). His father died in 1147, so Liú Píng was nine when his father died; the editing of his father's literary collection (the *Píng-shān jí* [[KR4d0177]]) was undertaken in adulthood, based on family papers. Zhū Xī 朱熹 — his father's pupil and Liú Píng's older friend — wrote the preface to the resulting collection.
