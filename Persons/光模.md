Guāngmó 光模, *zì* **Shítán** 石壇 ("Stone Altar"). Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Pǔfū Shízhuō** [[拙]] (b. ~1682). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0599]] 《磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B493.
