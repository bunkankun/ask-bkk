Guāng-mó 光模, *zì* **Shí-tán** 石壇 ("Stone Altar"). Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Pǔ-fū Shí-zhuō** [[拙]] (b. ~1682). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0599]] 《磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B493.
