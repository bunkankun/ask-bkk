Zhèng Wén-kāng 鄭文康 (1413–1465), *zì* **Shí-yì** 時乂, *hào* **Jiè-ān** 介菴 (also self-styled **Píng-qiáo** 平橋 and *Kāi-fēng* 開封 — *Píng-qiáo* the local name of his Kūn-shān residence; *Kāi-fēng* his clan-ancestral place). Native of **Kūn-shān** 崑山 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). Zhèng-tǒng *wù-chén* (1448) *jìn-shì*; on completing the *shì-hè* (initial-tassel ceremony) renounced further official advancement and returned home to teach. *Míng shǐ* j. 161 (appended). Surviving works are [[KR4e0111]] *Píng-qiáo gǎo* (18 *juǎn*; the author's self-edited recension, prefaced 1461 by **Yè Shèng** 葉盛, reprinted 1693 by descendant **Zhèng Qǐ-hóng** 鄭起泓). CBDB id 34514 (1413–1465).
