Zhèng Wénkāng 鄭文康 (1413–1465), *zì* **Shíyì** 時乂, *hào* **Jièān** 介菴 (also self-styled **Píngqiáo** 平橋 and *Kāifēng* 開封 — *Píngqiáo* the local name of his Kūnshān residence; *Kāifēng* his clan-ancestral place). Native of **Kūnshān** 崑山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Zhèngtǒng *wùchén* (1448) *jìnshì*; on completing the *shìhè* (initial-tassel ceremony) renounced further official advancement and returned home to teach. *Míng shǐ* j. 161 (appended). Surviving works are [[KR4e0111]] *Píngqiáo gǎo* (18 *juǎn*; the author's self-edited recension, prefaced 1461 by **Yè Shèng** 葉盛, reprinted 1693 by descendant **Zhèng Qǐhóng** 鄭起泓). CBDB id 34514 (1413–1465).
