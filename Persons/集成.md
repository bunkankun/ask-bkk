# Jíchéng 集成

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué during the Pǔzhào chánsì 普照禪寺 abbacy at Sìzhōu 泗州; recorded as one of the section-compilers of juan 1 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]).
