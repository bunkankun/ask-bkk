Early-Qīng bannerman official and connoisseur of calligraphy and painting; *zì* Lìng-zhī 令之, *hào* Xiān-kè 仙客; studio name *Shìgǔtáng* 式古堂. Member of the Hàn-jūn 漢軍 *Xiāng-hóng-qí* 鑲紅旗. Lifedates 1645–1712 per CBDB; the catalog meta gives the death year as 1706 — CBDB and modern reference works prefer 1712, followed here. Rose through the Censorate to *Fú-jiàn xún-fǔ* 福建巡撫 (Fújiàn Provincial Governor) and finally to *Xíng-bù zuǒ shì-láng* 刑部左侍郎 (Left Vice-Minister of Punishments). Wáng Shìzhēn's *Jū-yì lù* and Zhū Yízūn's poetry both single him out alongside Sòng Luò 宋犖 as one of the "two *zhōng-chéng* of the age" in connoisseurship. Compiled [[KR3h0068]] *Shìgǔtáng shū-huà huì-kǎo* 式古堂書畫彙考 in 60 *juàn* (1682), the largest single pre-Pèi-wén Chinese compendium of calligraphy and painting records. His editorial protocol — first general category then specific item, first the work's main text then colophons — became the model for the imperial Qiánlóng catalogs.
