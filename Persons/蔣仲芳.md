# 蔣仲芳

Jiǎng Zhòngfāng, late-Qīng physician whose *báihóu* case-records, demonstrating success with the warming *Jīnguì shènqì wán* 金匱腎氣丸 against the orthodox cooling-school prescription, are appended in the *Bǔlù míngàn* 補錄名案 of [[KR3em032]] *Báihóu biànzhèng* 白喉辨證.
