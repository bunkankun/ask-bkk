# Guǎngchuānzǐ 廣川子

Late-Edo Kyoto physician (Jap. Hirokawa-shi), specialising in *yǎkē* 啞科 (paediatrics). Resided two years in Nagasaki, where he acquired a manuscript copy of the Qīng paediatric clinical discourse *Yīng'ér lùn* 嬰兒論 by [[周士禰]] Zhōu Shìmí ([[KR3ej009]]) and clinically tested it. Requested [[吉村正隆]] Yoshimura Masataka to write the 1797 (*Kansei* 9) Nagasaki preface to the Japanese printing. Lifedates unrecorded.
