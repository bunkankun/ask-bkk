# Xuánlíng 玄靈

Mid-ninth-century Silla 新羅 (Korean) Vinaya monk of *Wángchéng Huìmíngsì* 王城慧明寺 (in the Silla royal capital). Named in [[允堪]] Yǔn Kān's preface to [[KR6k0177]] *Sìfēnlǜ shí píní yìchāo fǔyào jì* as one of the two Korean monks (with [[自相]] Zìxiāng of *Dōngquánsì*) who collated [[道宣]] Dàoxuān's *Yìchāo* and dispatched a copy to Táng China in 850.
