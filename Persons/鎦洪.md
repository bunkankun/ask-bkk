**Liú Hóng** 鎦洪 (variant character form of 劉; *fl.* late Jīn / early Yuán, ca. 1200s–1270s), Hé-jiān-school physician in the lineage of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù. Author of the *Hé-jiān Shānghán xīn yào* 河間傷寒心要 ([[KR3ef034]], 1 juan), a digest of Liú Wánsù's *Shānghán* doctrine for the Yuán-period medical curriculum. His biographical details beyond the school affiliation are not preserved. No CBDB record.
