# Xǔ Xuándù 許玄度

Eastern Jìn 東晉 lay Buddhist, also known as Xǔ Xún 許詢, *zì* Xuándù 玄度. A friend of Zhī Dùn 支遁 and prominent figure in fourth-century *xuánxué* and Buddhist circles. In Sòng-era Buddhist *zhuàn* literature, Péi Xiū 裴休 (787–860) is identified as a re-incarnation of Xǔ Xuándù in the *sānshēng* 三生 ("three lives") narrative — see annotations in [[KR6i0566]] and [[KR6i0567]].
