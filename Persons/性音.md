# Xìngyīn 性音

Early-Qīng Línjì Chán master. *Hào* Jiālíng 迦陵 ("Kalaviṅka") and Chuīyú 吹餘 ("Played-Out Remnants"). Imperial title *Yuántōng miàojué dàzhì chánshī* 圓通妙覺大智禪師 ("Chán Master of Perfect Penetration, Wondrous Awakening, and Great Wisdom"). Native of Fèngtiān-fǔ 奉天府 (modern Shěnyáng 瀋陽 in Liáoníng) — his Manchurian / north-eastern origin, relatively rare among eminent Chán masters of this period, is notable. Lifedates 1671–1726 (died 雍正 4 / seventh month / twenty-ninth day, age 56 per the *Lúshān zhì* 廬山志 juan 9).

A major figure in the early-Qīng Línjì publishing and patronage network, with close imperial connections under the Yōngzhèng 雍正 emperor (who personally awarded him the imperial title). His own corpus includes the *Jiālíng Yīn chánshī lù* 迦陵音禪師語錄 (his *yǔlù*) and multiple compilations. Contributed the continuation-compilation (*xù jí* 續輯) and postface to the *Mùniú tú sòng* [[KR6q0164]] compiled by [[超格|Méng'ān Chāogé]] 夢菴超格, which the young Xìngyīn would have taken up from Chāogé's unfinished editorial project when Chāogé died in 1708 (the same year Nà Xìn sponsored the publication).
