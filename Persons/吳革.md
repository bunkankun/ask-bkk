Wú Gé 吳革, of Jiǔjiāng 九江, late-Southern-Sòng book-cutter and patron of the 1265 (*Xiányán yǐchǒu*) Jiǔjiāng print of [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī's *Yuán běn Zhōuyì běnyì* ([[KR1a0031]]) — the textually authoritative twelve-juan recension that the *Sìkù* editors restored as base text. Wú Gé contributed the preface; the print was collated by [[劉賓]] Liú Bīn of Fùyuán 敷原.
