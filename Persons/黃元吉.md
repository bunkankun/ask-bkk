**Huáng Yuánjí** 黃元吉 (1270–1324), styled Zhōng huáng xiān sheng 中黃先生, was the leading Yuán **Jìng míng** 淨明 patriarch, a chief organizer of the movement at Xī shān 西山 (Jiāngxī) and compiler of the key Jìng míng canonical collection [[KR5e0012]] *Jìng míng zhōng xiào quán shū* 淨明忠孝全書 (introduction dated 1327). Huáng systematized the Jìng míng teachings around the cult of [[許遜]] Xǔ Xùn and the Confucian virtues of loyalty (*zhōng* 忠) and filiality (*xiào* 孝).
