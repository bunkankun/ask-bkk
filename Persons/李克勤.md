Lǐ Kè-qín 李克勤 (fl. late 10th c.) held the office of *Zuǒ bǔ-quē* 左補闕 at the time of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* commission of 977, when he was named among the fourteen compilers. No independent biography survives in the *Sòng shǐ*.
