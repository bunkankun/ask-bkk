Lǐ Kèqín 李克勤 (fl. late 10th c.) held the office of *Zuǒ bǔquē* 左補闕 at the time of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* commission of 977, when he was named among the fourteen compilers. No independent biography survives in the *Sòng shǐ*.
