# Péng Shàoqiān 彭紹謙

Mid-Qīng Sūzhōu literatus of the *Chángzhōu* 長洲 Péng lineage, elder brother of [[彭紹升]] Péng Shàoshēng (1740–1796) and son of *Péng Qǐfēng* 彭啟豐. Recorded in Péng Shàoshēng's *Péngshì fàngshēngchí bēi* 彭氏放生池碑 (preserved in [[KR6k0263]] *Tǐrén yàoshù* 體仁要術, X1139) as having been ordered by their father in *Qiánlóng* 17 (1752) to construct the *fàngshēngchí* 放生池 garden at the back of the family compound. No independent biography preserved.
