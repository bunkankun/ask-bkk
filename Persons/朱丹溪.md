**Zhū Dānxī** 朱丹溪 (real name 朱震亨 Zhū Zhènhēng, *zì* Yànxiū 彥修, *hào* Dānxī 丹溪, 1281–1358), Yuán-period physician of Yìwū 義烏 (Zhèjiāng) and one of the *Jīn-Yuán sì dàjiā* 金元四大家. Founder of the *Zīyīn pài* 滋陰派 (Nourishing-Yīn School). Principal works: *Géhì yúlùn* 格致餘論, *Júfāng fāhuī* 局方發揮, *Jīnguì gōuxuán* 金匱鈎玄. His doctrine "*yáng* often surplus, *yīn* often insufficient" became foundational for late-imperial gynecological and *zábìng* therapeutics.
