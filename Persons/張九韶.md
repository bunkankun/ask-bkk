Zhāng Jiǔsháo 張九韶 (1314–1396), *zì* Měihé 美和 (later used as *míng* — hence often called Zhāng Měihé 張美和), was a Yuán-Míng transition Confucian classicist of Qīng-jiāng 清江 (Jiāng-xī). Several times unsuccessful in late-Yuán examinations; under Hóng-wǔ 3 (1370), recommended to the new Míng court and appointed *Xiàn-xué jiào-yù* 縣學教諭, then *Guó-zǐ-jiàn zhù-jiào* 國子監助教, then *Hàn-lín biān-xiū*; retired but recalled to *jiào shū* (collation) duty, and on completion was returned. His career-summary appears in the *Míng shǐ Sòng Nè zhuàn* 明史宋訥傳 附 (he is treated alongside Sòng Nè, Wáng Jiā-huì, Gōng Xiào, Niè Hóng, Bèi Qióng — the early-Míng *Guó-zǐ-jiàn* generation). Major work: the *Lǐ-xué lèi biān* 理學類編 (KR3a0077), composed in *Zhì-zhèng bǐng-wǔ* (1366) under his earlier title *Gé wù biān* and renamed by Wú Dāng 吳當 of Lín-chuān. CBDB id 66401, dates 1314–1396.
