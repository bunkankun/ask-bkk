Zhāng Jiǔsháo 張九韶 (1314–1396), *zì* Měihé 美和 (later used as *míng* — hence often called Zhāng Měihé 張美和), was a YuánMíng transition Confucian classicist of Qīngjiāng 清江 (Jiāngxī). Several times unsuccessful in late-Yuán examinations; under Hóngwǔ 3 (1370), recommended to the new Míng court and appointed *Xiànxué jiàoyù* 縣學教諭, then *Guózǐjiàn zhùjiào* 國子監助教, then *Hànlín biānxiū*; retired but recalled to *jiào shū* (collation) duty, and on completion was returned. His career-summary appears in the *Míng shǐ Sòng Nè zhuàn* 明史宋訥傳 附 (he is treated alongside Sòng Nè, Wáng Jiāhuì, Gōng Xiào, Niè Hóng, Bèi Qióng — the early-Míng *Guózǐjiàn* generation). Major work: the *Lǐxué lèi biān* 理學類編 ([[KR3a0077]]), composed in *Zhìzhèng bǐngwǔ* (1366) under his earlier title *Gé wù biān* and renamed by Wú Dāng 吳當 of Línchuān. CBDB id 66401, dates 1314–1396.
