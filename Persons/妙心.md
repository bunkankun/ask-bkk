# Miào-xīn 妙心

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. (DILA classifies him as Míng-dynasty, likely reflecting a later publication horizon; the editorial work is Yuán.)

A separate Qīng lay disciple also named **Miào-xīn** 妙心: lay-disciple of **Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** [[本真]] (1620–1691) at **Bái-yún chán-sì** 白雲禪寺 (Qí-zhōu Zǐ-yún-shān). Author of the principal preface to his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0571]] 《白雲象林本真禪師語錄》, signed "弟子妙心沐浴" dated **順治巳亥歲佛歡喜日** = **1659-08-30** (Yú-lán-pén day). Lifedates unknown.
