A separate Qīng lay disciple also named **Miàoxīn** 妙心: lay-disciple of **Xiànglín Běnzhēn** [[本真]] (1620–1691) at **Báiyún chánsì** 白雲禪寺 (Qízhōu Zǐyúnshān). Author of the principal preface to his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0571]] 《白雲象林本真禪師語錄》, signed "弟子妙心沐浴" dated **順治巳亥歲佛歡喜日** = **1659-08-30** (Yúlánpén day). Lifedates unknown.
