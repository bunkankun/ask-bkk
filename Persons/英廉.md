Yīnglián 英廉 (1707–1783), *zì* Jìliù 計六, *hào* Mèngtáng 夢堂, posthumous name *Wénsù* 文肅, of the *Hànjūn* (Chinese-martial) Plain Yellow Banner Féng 馮 family. A *jǔrén* of Yōngzhèng 10 (1732), he held a long succession of progressively senior offices under Qiánlóng, including *Hùbù shàngshū* 戶部尚書, *Liǎngjiāng zǒngdū* 兩江總督 (Jiāngnán-Jiāngxī Governor-General), and ultimately *Dōnggé dàxuéshì jiān Xíngbù shìwù* 東閣大學士兼刑部事務. He was a senior figure in the late-Qiánlóng court for hydraulic and fiscal administration, and was named to the *zǒngzǎi* leadership of the *Sìkù quánshū* compilation; his name appears at the head of the *biǎo* of presentation of the *Qīndìng Rìxià jiùwén kǎo* 欽定日下舊聞考 ([[KR2k0036]]) in 1783, the year of his death. He is the maternal grandfather of the late-Qīng statesman Yǐlìbù 伊里布. CBDB c_personid 57402 (a second, undated entry under c_personid 456110 likely refers to a homonym).
