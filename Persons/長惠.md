Chōe 長惠, Tendai *shōmyō* teacher of the **Ōhara Raigō-in** liturgical-music lineage in the **Kamakura / Nanboku-chō period**. Precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works; he is conventionally dated **c. 1300–1400** on the basis of doctrinal vocabulary and references in his *Gyo-san shi-shō* [[KR6t0424]]. As a working *shōmyō* performer and teacher he preserved important **oral-tradition** performance material in his "personal anthology" — texts that would otherwise have been transmitted only orally. The DILA authority id is `A001259`.
