# Zhōu Xuànxiàn 周鉉現

An early-Qīng lay Buddhist (居士) of Jīngshān, mentioned in the preface to [[續法]] Xùfǎ's *Guānzìzài púsà rúyìxīn tuóluóní jīng lüèshū* ([[KR6j0737]]) as one of the donors who funded its publication.
