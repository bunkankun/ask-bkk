**Wáng Shàngdá** 王上達 (*hào* Chūntíng 春亭), late-Qīng physician active in the *Guāngxù* era. Sole transmitted work: *Jìshēng jí* 濟生集 ([[KR3ei014]]), a six-*juǎn* combined gynecological-paediatric household manual printed in 1896 (*Guāngxù* 22) for charitable free distribution. The work is in the *Dàshēng yàozhǐ* / *Dáshēng biān* tradition of late-imperial *jiātíngyī* (family-medical) literature.
