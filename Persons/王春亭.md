**Wáng Shàngdá** 王上達 (*hào* Chūntíng 春亭), late-Qīng physician active in the *Guāng-xù* era. Sole transmitted work: *Jì-shēng jí* 濟生集 ([[KR3ei014]]), a six-*juǎn* combined gynecological-paediatric household manual printed in 1896 (*Guāng-xù* 22) for charitable free distribution. The work is in the *Dà-shēng yào-zhǐ* / *Dá-shēng biān* tradition of late-imperial *jiā-tíng-yī* (family-medical) literature.
