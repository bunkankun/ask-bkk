Tǐqǐng 體請 (Tang dynasty, otherwise unknown) is the named author of the Dunhuang manuscript *Shì Zhào xù* 釋肇序 ([[KR6i0095]] = T2776), a sub-commentary on Sēngzhào 僧肇's preface to the *Zhù Wéimójié jīng* ([[KR6i0078]] = T1775). His name does not appear in any major Buddhist biographical compilation (*Gāosēng zhuàn*, *Sòng Gāosēng zhuàn*, etc.), and his dates and monastic affiliation cannot be reconstructed. The Tang dating is established on the basis of the work's preservation only in the Dunhuang manuscript corpus.
