**Tamura Naohiro** 多村直寬 (*hào* *Kōsō-setsusha* 拷窗拙者), late-Edo Japanese physician of the Yoshimasu Tōdō *kohōha* lineage. Wrote the autumn-1862 preface to [[尾臺榕堂]] Odai Yōdō's *Yī yú* 醫餘 ([[KR3eq051]]), situating Odai's project as the continuation of Yoshimasu Tōdō's *fùgǔ* 復古 return-to-antiquity programme.
