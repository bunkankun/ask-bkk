Late-Yuán literatus of **Tiān-tái** 天台 (Zhèjiāng). *Zì* **Shàn-qīng** 善卿. Life-dates not recorded. He is known principally as the compiler of the **[[KR4h0088]]** *Dà-yǎ jí* 大雅集 (1361–1362), for which he travelled Wú-Yuè soliciting verse from contemporary regional poets and amassed 2,000-plus poems; the celebrated late-Yuán poet **Yáng Wéi-zhēn** 楊維楨 (*Tiě-yǎ dào-rén*) edited the corpus down to 300 pieces in 8 *juǎn*. The funding came from the Sōng-jiāng literatus **Xiè Lǚ-zhāi** 謝履齋. No other works under Lài's name are transmitted.
