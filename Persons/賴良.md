Late-Yuán literatus of **Tiāntái** 天台 (Zhèjiāng). *Zì* **Shànqīng** 善卿. Life-dates not recorded. He is known principally as the compiler of the **[[KR4h0088]]** *Dàyǎ jí* 大雅集 (1361–1362), for which he travelled WúYuè soliciting verse from contemporary regional poets and amassed 2,000-plus poems; the celebrated late-Yuán poet **Yáng Wéizhēn** 楊維楨 (*Tiěyǎ dàorén*) edited the corpus down to 300 pieces in 8 *juǎn*. The funding came from the Sōngjiāng literatus **Xiè Lǚzhāi** 謝履齋. No other works under Lài's name are transmitted.
