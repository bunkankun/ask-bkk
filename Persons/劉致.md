Mid-Yuán 元 Hànlín official, disciple of **Yáo Suì** [[姚燧]] (1238–1313). *Zì* **Shízhōng** 時中. CBDB 35431 records *fl. lat.* 1322; lifedates uncertain.

**Career.** *Hànlín dàizhì* 翰林待制 — Hànlín Reader-in-Waiting.

**Principal contribution.** Editor and compiler-supervisor of his teacher Yáo Suì's *Mùān jí* [[KR4d0465]]. In Zhìshùn *rénshēn* (1332), Liú secured the *Zhōngshū* order transferring the compilation work to the Jiāngzhè provincial-school system, with funding from county *shànxué* (subsidized-school) surplus. The collation was carried out at the Hàngzhōu *Rúxué tíjǔsī* by **Yè Jǐngxiū** 葉景修 under the supervision of **Wú Shàn** 吳善 (the *Jiāngzhè děngchù rúxué tíjǔ*). The result was the canonical 50-*juàn* / 689-piece corpus that established Yáo Suì as the singular Yuán *gǔwén* master.

**Also.** Liú Zhì composed the *niánpǔ* (chronological biography) of Yáo Suì appended to the *Mùān jí* (1 *juàn*) — the principal contemporary biographical witness for Yáo Suì.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0465]] *Mùān jí* 牧庵集 (撰附年譜).
