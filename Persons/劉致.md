Mid-Yuán 元 Hàn-lín official, disciple of **Yáo Suì** [[姚燧]] (1238–1313). *Zì* **Shí-zhōng** 時中. CBDB 35431 records *fl. lat.* 1322; lifedates uncertain.

**Career.** *Hàn-lín dài-zhì* 翰林待制 — Hàn-lín Reader-in-Waiting.

**Principal contribution.** Editor and compiler-supervisor of his teacher Yáo Suì's *Mù-ān jí* [[KR4d0465]]. In Zhì-shùn *rén-shēn* (1332), Liú secured the *Zhōng-shū* order transferring the compilation work to the Jiāng-zhè provincial-school system, with funding from county *shàn-xué* (subsidized-school) surplus. The collation was carried out at the Hàng-zhōu *Rú-xué tí-jǔ-sī* by **Yè Jǐng-xiū** 葉景修 under the supervision of **Wú Shàn** 吳善 (the *Jiāng-zhè děng-chù rú-xué tí-jǔ*). The result was the canonical 50-*juàn* / 689-piece corpus that established Yáo Suì as the singular Yuán *gǔ-wén* master.

**Also.** Liú Zhì composed the *nián-pǔ* (chronological biography) of Yáo Suì appended to the *Mù-ān jí* (1 *juàn*) — the principal contemporary biographical witness for Yáo Suì.
