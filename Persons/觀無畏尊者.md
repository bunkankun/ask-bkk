Guān-wú-wèi zūn-zhě 觀無畏尊者 ("The Venerable Who Looks-Without-Fear"; Skt. uncertain, possibly *Avalokita-vrata* or *Avalokita-abhaya*) is the attributed compiler of the *Zhū-fǎ jí-yào jīng* 諸法集要經 ([[KR6i0422]] / T728), translated into Chinese by [[日稱]] (Rì-chēng) and others at the Northern Sòng *Yìjīng-yuàn* 譯經院 in the mid-eleventh century. The 10-fascicle text is one of the principal late Sòng-period Mahāyāna doctrinal anthologies. Lifedates, Indian biographical details, and the precise Sanskrit identification of the name 觀無畏 are not preserved in available sources. The honorific 尊者 (*zūn-zhě*, "Venerable") indicates *sthavira* / elder status in the Indian Buddhist tradition. The text's compendium-anthology character (*jí-yào* = "compendium of essentials") is consistent with the late Pāla-period scholastic production from which the Sòng bureau's source manuscripts came.
