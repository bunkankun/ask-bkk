Ní Qiān 倪謙 (1415–1479), *zì* **Kè-ràng** 克讓, *hào* **Jìng-cún** 靜存, posthumous title **Wén-xǐ** 文僖. Native of **Shàng-yuán** 上元 (Nán-jīng). Zhèng-tǒng *jǐ-wèi* (1439) *jìn-shì jí-dì* (a top-three placement); office reaching *Nán-jīng Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. Best known for the **Cháo-xiǎn** (Korea) embassy of the early Jǐng-tài era; the embassy poems *Liáo-hǎi biān* 遼海編 circulated separately and are now lost. *Míng shǐ* j. 171 (appended). Surviving works: [[KR4e0106]] *Ní Wén-xǐ jí* (32 *juǎn*) — Ní's own self-selection out of an attested lifetime corpus of 171 *juǎn* (per **Lǐ Dōng-yáng** ([[李東陽]])'s head preface): *Yù-táng gǎo* 100 *juǎn*, *Shàng-gǔ gǎo* 8 *juǎn*, *Guī-tián gǎo* 43 *juǎn*, *Nán-gōng gǎo* 20 *juǎn*.
