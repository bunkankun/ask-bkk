Ní Qiān 倪謙 (1415–1479), *zì* **Kèràng** 克讓, *hào* **Jìngcún** 靜存, posthumous title **Wénxǐ** 文僖. Native of **Shàngyuán** 上元 (Nánjīng). Zhèngtǒng *jǐwèi* (1439) *jìnshì jídì* (a top-three placement); office reaching *Nánjīng Lǐbù shàngshū*. Best known for the **Cháoxiǎn** (Korea) embassy of the early Jǐngtài era; the embassy poems *Liáohǎi biān* 遼海編 circulated separately and are now lost. *Míng shǐ* j. 171 (appended). Surviving works: [[KR4e0106]] *Ní Wénxǐ jí* (32 *juǎn*) — Ní's own self-selection out of an attested lifetime corpus of 171 *juǎn* (per **Lǐ Dōngyáng** ([[李東陽]])'s head preface): *Yùtáng gǎo* 100 *juǎn*, *Shànggǔ gǎo* 8 *juǎn*, *Guītián gǎo* 43 *juǎn*, *Nángōng gǎo* 20 *juǎn*.
