# Dálín 達林 (Qīng, mid-19th c.)

A Qīng Pure Land monk associated with the *Hóngluóshān* 紅螺山 lineage. He is known almost exclusively as the *cāndìng* 參訂 ("collaborative-collator") of [[達默]] Dámò's *Ēmítuó jīng yàojiě biànméngchāo* 阿彌陀經要解便蒙鈔 [[KR6p0025]]. The shared *dá*- 達 prefix in their names indicates a common ordination cohort within the Hóngluóshān monastic lineage. Lifedates not preserved; he is otherwise undocumented in the standard biographical literature.
