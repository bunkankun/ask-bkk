Xīng-shū 興舒, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Guān-tāo Dà-qí** [[大奇]] 觀濤大奇 (1625–1678) in the Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Dào-shèng sub-line. Lead compiler of the 6-juan 《觀濤奇禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0499]]). Per the closing 《壽藏銘并序》 listing, possibly also identical with the named *fǎ-sì* **Qí-yuán Xīng-quán** 淇園興泉. No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
