# Cáo Róng 曹溶

*Zì* Qiūyuè 秋岳, *hào* Juànpǔ 倦圃. Late-Míng / early-Qīng official and bibliophile, native of Xiùshuǐ 秀水 (modern Jiāxīng 嘉興, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of 1637 (Chóngzhēn 崇禎 10). Held office under both the late Míng and the Qīng — censor in the Míng, Grand Coordinator (*xúnfǔ* 巡撫) of Guǎngdōng under the Qīng — before withdrawing from public life. Best known as a great private collector and bibliographer; *Jìngǔtáng shū mù* 靜古堂書目 records part of his library. Friend and patron of many late-Míng / early-Qīng *yímín* 遺民 scholars including [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn.
