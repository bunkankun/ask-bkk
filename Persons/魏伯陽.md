**Wèi Bóyáng** 魏伯陽 was the traditional Eastern-Hàn author of the ***Zhōu yì cān tóng qì*** 周易參同契 ("Token for the Agreement of the Three in the *Zhōu yì*") — the foundational alchemical-correlative classic that became the *Urtext* of Chinese alchemy, both *wài dān* 外丹 and (especially from Sòng onward) *nèi dān* 內丹. His historicity is debated; Gě Hóng's *Shén xiān zhuàn* gives a brief hagiography. He is named as originator in many later alchemical texts, including [[KR5c0286]] *Wèi Bóyáng qī fǎn dān shā jué*.
