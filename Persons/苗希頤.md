**Miáo Xīyí** 苗希頤 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist, compiler of [[KR5g0060]] *Xū jìng chōng hé xiān sheng Xú shén wēng yǔ lù* 虛靜沖和先生徐神翁語錄 — recorded sayings of Xú Shénwēng 徐神翁 (1033–1108), a Northern-Sòng Daoist thaumaturge.
