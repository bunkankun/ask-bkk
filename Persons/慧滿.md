# Xuěyán Huìmǎn 雪巖慧滿

Jīn-period Cáodòng master at Dàmíng 大明 in Cízhōu 磁州 (also styled Xuěyán Mǎn 雪巖滿); dharma-transmission teacher of [[行秀|Wànsōng Xíngxiù]] 萬松行秀.
