Hángzhōu physician active in the late Míng and early Qīng. *Zì* Shuòfǔ 碩甫, *hào* Shèn'ān 慎庵. Author of the *Yī dēng xù yàn* 醫燈續焰 ([[KR3eb001]], 1652), a 21-juan expansion of Cuī Jiāyàn's 崔嘉彥 four-character pulse mnemonic *Sì yán jǔ yào* 四言舉要. Pān is also cited as "潘碩甫" by Lín Zhīhàn 林之瀚 in the *Sì zhěn jué wēi* ([[KR3eb003]]) on the relationship between blood (*xuè* 血) and vessel (*mài* 脈). No CBDB record; lifedates undetermined.
