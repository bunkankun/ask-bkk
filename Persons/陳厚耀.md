Chén Hòuyào 陳厚耀 (1648–1722), zì Sìyuán 泗源, hào Shǔfēng 曙峰, native of Tàizhōu 泰州 (Yángzhōu 揚州 prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Kāngxī xīnsì* (1701) *jìnshì*. He served in the Hànlín Academy as Examination Editor and was specially summoned to the Inner Court for his expertise in mathematical astronomy and calendrical computation; on imperial commission he collaborated on the *Lǜ lì yuān yuán* 律歷淵源 mathematical-astronomical compendium. As an early-Qing astronomer-historian he produced a series of pivotal *Chūnqiū* reference works: *Chūnqiū shìzú pǔ* 春秋世族譜 ([[KR1e0111]]) — a genealogical reconstruction of the Zhōu kingdom and feudal states; *Chūnqiū cháng lì* 春秋長歷 ([[KR1e0112]]) — a complete reconstruction of the lunisolar calendar across 春秋's 242 years using techniques from Yī Xíng 一行 and Guō Shǒujìng 郭守敬; and a now-lost *Zhànguó cè biān nián* 戰國策編年. He is one of the most consequential evidential scholars of the *Kāngxī* generation and a direct forerunner of Wáng Yīnglín-style historical philology in *Chūnqiū* studies.
