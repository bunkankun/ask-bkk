# Táng Jiǎn 唐儉

*Zì* Mào-yuē 茂約. Native of Bīng-zhōu 并州 in Tài-yuán 太原 (modern Shānxī). Lifedates 579–656.

One of the founding general-officials of the Tang. A principal advisor to Lǐ Yuān 李淵 (the future Tang Gāozǔ) in the Tài-yuán uprising of 617; subsequently held *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書, *Mín-bù shàngshū* 民部尚書, and was the principal Tang diplomatic envoy in the early consolidation of the empire. Enfeoffed as *Jǔ-guó gōng* 莒國公.

As an early-Tang historiographer, he was one of the five compilers of the *Zhōu shū* (KR2a0022) under Lìng-hú Détén's chief editorship.

His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 58 and *Xīn Tángshū* 89 (KR2a0026, KR2a0027).
