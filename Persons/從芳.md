Sòng-period (or possibly late-Táng) Cí'ēn-school 慈恩 / Fǎxiàng 法相 scholar-monk, otherwise unknown except as the author (述) of the substantial three-fascicle Yogācāra commentary [[KR6n0101]] *Bǎifǎ lùn xiǎnyōu chāo* 百法論顯幽鈔 (X48n0799) on Vasubandhu's *Bǎifǎ míngmén lùn* 百法明門論. The Kanripo catalog tags him 宋; the DILA Buddhist authority records him as 唐, on the basis of his appearance in the Japanese Tendai bibliographic catalog *Tōiki dentō mokuroku* 東域傳燈目錄 (T2183). His doctrinal vocabulary and his use of the standard Cí'ēn glossary are post-Kuījī / post-Huìzhǎo, so a late-Táng to early-Sòng dating is most defensible; here we follow the catalog meta but flag the uncertainty. No biographical material survives.
