Yuán Cǎi 袁采 (Sòng), *zì* Jūnzài 君載, was a Southern Sòng official of Xìn-ān 信安 (Qú-zhōu 衢州 region, Zhè-jiāng). *Jìnshì*; held three terms as Magistrate of large counties (*jù-yì* 劇邑), serving with renown for *lián-míng gāng-zhí* 廉明剛直 (purity, clarity, uprightness). Reached the metropolitan office of *Jiān Dēng-wén jiǎn-yuàn* 監登聞檢院 (Supervisor of the Bureau Reviewing Petitions). His best-known surviving work is the *Yuán-shì shì fàn* 袁氏世範 (KR3a0040, originally titled *Xùn sú* 訓俗 — "Instructions on Custom" — and renamed *Shì fàn* "Family Standard" by his colleague Liú Zhèn 劉鎮 of Lè-qīng), composed during his term as Magistrate of Lè-qīng 樂清 (a date conventionally given as Chún-xī 5, 1178; the catalog meta gives a date of 1163, less likely). He also wrote a *Lè-qīng xiàn-zhì* 樂清縣志 in 10 juan (now lost), and the *Zhèng-hé zá zhì* 政和雜志 and *Xiàn-lìng xiǎo-lù* 縣令小錄 (also lost) per Wáng Qǐ 王圻's *Xù wén xiàn tōng kǎo*.
