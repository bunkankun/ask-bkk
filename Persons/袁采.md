Yuán Cǎi 袁采 (Sòng), *zì* Jūnzài 君載, was a Southern Sòng official of Xìnān 信安 (Qúzhōu 衢州 region, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì*; held three terms as Magistrate of large counties (*jùyì* 劇邑), serving with renown for *liánmíng gāngzhí* 廉明剛直 (purity, clarity, uprightness). Reached the metropolitan office of *Jiān Dēngwén jiǎnyuàn* 監登聞檢院 (Supervisor of the Bureau Reviewing Petitions). His best-known surviving work is the *Yuánshì shì fàn* 袁氏世範 ([[KR3a0040]], originally titled *Xùn sú* 訓俗 — "Instructions on Custom" — and renamed *Shì fàn* "Family Standard" by his colleague Liú Zhèn 劉鎮 of Lèqīng), composed during his term as Magistrate of Lèqīng 樂清 (a date conventionally given as Chúnxī 5, 1178; the catalog meta gives a date of 1163, less likely). He also wrote a *Lèqīng xiànzhì* 樂清縣志 in 10 juan (now lost), and the *Zhènghé zá zhì* 政和雜志 and *Xiànlìng xiǎolù* 縣令小錄 (also lost) per Wáng Qǐ 王圻's *Xù wén xiàn tōng kǎo*. CBDB id 35263, no dates.
