Fā-huì 發慧, *hào* **Xuě-chuáng** 雪幢, was a *fā*-generation Línjì (Pòshān → Liányuè) dharma-heir of **Lián-yuè Dào-zhèng** [[道正]] 蓮月道正 (1614–1691), named as recipient of transmission in the closing *fù-fǎ* notice of the 6-juan 《蓮月禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0445]]): *fù Xuě-chuáng Fā-huì chán-rén* 付雪幢發慧禪人 (CBETA J29nB235 p.0423a27). Lead compiler of the 2-juan Hú-běi recension 《玉泉蓮月正禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0446]]), signing the juan 1 colophon as *mén-rén Fā-huì lù* 門人發慧錄 — covering Liányuè's **Yù-quán-sì** 玉泉寺 abbacy (Dāng-yáng 當陽, Hú-běi) from 1676. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
