Fāhuì 發慧, *hào* **Xuěchuáng** 雪幢, was a *fā*-generation Línjì (Pòshān → Liányuè) dharma-heir of **Liányuè Dàozhèng** [[道正]] 蓮月道正 (1614–1691), named as recipient of transmission in the closing *fùfǎ* notice of the 6-juan 《蓮月禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0445]]): *fù Xuěchuáng Fāhuì chánrén* 付雪幢發慧禪人 (CBETA J29nB235 p.0423a27). Lead compiler of the 2-juan Húběi recension 《玉泉蓮月正禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0446]]), signing the juan 1 colophon as *ménrén Fāhuì lù* 門人發慧錄 — covering Liányuè's **Yùquánsì** 玉泉寺 abbacy (Dāngyáng 當陽, Húběi) from 1676. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
