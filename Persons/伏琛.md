Fú Chén 伏琛, an Eastern Jin dynasty 東晉 (317–420 CE) author whose biography is entirely unknown. He is known only through his single attributed work, the *Sān Qí lüèjì* 三齊略記 ([[KR2k0162]]), a brief geographic record of the Shandong region. The work is cited by Lì Dàoyuán 酈道元 in the *Shuǐjīng zhù* 水經注 (*c.*527 CE), establishing an Eastern-Jin or early Southern-dynasty date. No further biographical information is available.
