# Huì-yīn 惠洇

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xī-sǒu Shào-tán (d. by 1279); co-editor of both the short *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0322]]) and the extended *guǎng-lù* ([[KR6q0323]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
