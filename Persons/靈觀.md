# Línguān 靈觀

Yǐnān Línguān 隱庵靈觀 (Korean *Yeonggwan*; 1485–1571), Korean Chosŏn Sŏn 禪 master in the Jogye 曹溪 tradition. Teacher of [[退隱|Cheongheo Hyujeong]] 清虛休靜 (Sŏsan Taesa; 1520–1604), through whom his lineage came to dominate post-16th-century Korean Buddhism.
