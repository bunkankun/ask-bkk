Jīn-yòu 今又. Cáodòng *dé-dù dì-zǐ* (tonsure-disciple) of **Qiān-shān Shèng-rén Hán-kě** [[函可]] (1612–1660); also enrolled as a 得度弟子 in the *Lú-shān Tiān-rán chán-shī yǔ-lù* roster (J38 B406). Lifedates unknown. With **Jīn-lú** [[今盧]] re-cut and reissued [[KR6q0527]] 《千山剩人禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B407, in **康熙庚午** = **1690** at **Suì-chéng Huáng-huá-sì** 穗城黃華寺 (Guǎngzhōu) — the colophon reads "門徒比丘 今盧 今又 重梓". One of the prefaces (1690) by their *fǎ-zhí* [[今辯]] specifies that the project was undertaken because the original *Pǔ-jì lù* 普濟錄, printed thirty-odd years earlier in the Liáo-dōng frontier, had become hard to obtain south of the Cháng-jiāng.
