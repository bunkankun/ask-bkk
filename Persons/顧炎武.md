Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武 (1613–1682), original name Jiàng 絳, *zì* Níngrén 寧人, *hào* Tínglín 亭林 (after Tínglín lake in his native Kūnshān 崑山, Sūzhōu prefecture, modern Jiāngsū), self-styled also Jiǎng-shān-yōng 蔣山傭, was the founding figure of the Qīng evidential-research school (*Hàn-xué pài* 漢學派) and one of the "Three Great Confucians of the Early Qīng" (alongside Huáng Zōngxī 黃宗羲 and Wáng Fūzhī 王夫之 ([[王夫之]])). The *Sìkù* compilers state plainly that "of those at the founding of our dynasty whose learning had a real grounding, none surpassed Yánwǔ" (*guó chū chēng xué yǒu gēn-dǐ zhě, yǐ Yánwǔ wèi zuì* 國初稱學有根柢者以炎武為最). Lifedates 1613–1682 firm; CBDB id 34252.

A late-Míng *zhū-shēng* 諸生; took a leading role in the Sūzhōu-area Míng resistance after 1644, refused to serve the Qīng, and after the deaths of his foster mother Wáng-shì 王氏 (who starved herself to death rather than accept Qīng rule) and his teacher Zhōu Yǒng-fú 周永輔 spent the rest of his life as a wandering scholar moving between northern and central China, accumulating notes and inscriptions at every site he visited. His final years were spent at Huá-yīn 華陰 (Shǎn-xī).

His principal work, the *Rì zhī lù* 日知錄 ([[KR3a0091]]) — a vast notebook of philological, historical, geographic, and political observations — is the foundational text of *kǎo zhèng* 考證 ("evidential research") learning, and through Qián Dà-xīn 錢大昕 and Zhāng Zhī-dòng 張之洞's reception (Wilkinson, *Chinese History* §16.3.3, §55582) supplied the Qīng *Hàn-xué* movement with both its method and its programmatic framing. Other monumental works include the geographic compendium *Tiān-xià jùn-guó lì-bìng shū* 天下郡國利病書 (a vast collection of Míng provincial gazetteer extracts arranged by jurisdiction); the *Zhào-yù zhì* 肇域志 (geographic gazetteer); the historical-phonological masterwork *Yīn xué wǔ shū* 音學五書 (with its core *Shī běn yīn* 詩本音 and *Yì yīn* 易音), establishing the foundation of Qīng historical phonology by reconstructing Old Chinese rhyme categories from the *Shī jīng* and bronze inscriptions; the epigraphic *Jīn-shí wén-zì jì* 金石文字記; and a series of focused commentaries including the *Zuǒ zhuàn Dù jiě bǔ zhèng* 左傳杜解補正 ([[KR1e0096]]) — a 3-juǎn supplement and corrective to Dù Yù's 杜預 ([[杜預]]) classical *Zuǒ* commentary, which the *Sìkù tíyào* praises as having "swept away factional positions and held the balance of right and wrong" (*sǎo chú mén-hù, néng chí shì-fēi zhī píng* 掃除門户，能持是非之平).

A short biography (*xiǎo zhuàn* 小傳) of him by Lǐ Guāngdì 李光地 ([[李光地]]) is preserved in the *Róng-cūn jí* 榕村集. CBDB id 34252.
