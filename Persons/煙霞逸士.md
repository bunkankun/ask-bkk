Yānxiá Yìshì 煙霞逸士 ("The Recluse of Mist and Rosy Clouds") is the pseudonym of the author of *Qiǎo Liánzhū* 巧聯珠 ([[KR4k0200]]), a 15-chapter Qīng dynasty romance novel. The real identity behind this pen name is unknown. This pseudonym is distinct from Yānxiá Sǎnrén 煙霞散人 [[煙霞散人]], the pen name of the *Fēngyuè mèng* 風月夢 novelist. No CBDB record has been found.
