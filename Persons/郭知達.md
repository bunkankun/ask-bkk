# Guō Zhīdá 郭知達

Sòng-period scholar; native of *Shǔ* 蜀 (Sì-chuān) per his self-preface to the *Jiǔ-jiā jí-zhù Dù shī*. Floruit late 12th century. The preface is dated *Chún-xī* 8 (1181). His one surviving work — and the basis of his importance to the Dù Fǔ commentary tradition — is the *Jiǔ-jiā jí-zhù Dù shī* [[KR4c0015]], an editorially conscientious *jí-zhù* of Dù Fǔ's poetry that consolidates the annotations of nine major Sòng commentators (王洙, 宋祁, 王安石, 黃庭堅, 薛夢符, 杜田, 鮑彪, 師尹, 趙次公) while explicitly stripping out the apocryphal *Lǎo-Dù shì-shí* 老杜事實 forgeries that had circulated in Sòng book-stalls. Chén Zhèn-sūn's 陳振孫 *Zhí-zhāi shū-lù jiě-tí* praises this editorial discrimination in unusually warm terms. The text was reprinted in *Bǎo-qìng* 1 (1225) by Zēng È 曾噩 in *Guǎng-zhōu*; this *Bǎo-qìng* print is the basis of the WYG. CBDB has no entry.
