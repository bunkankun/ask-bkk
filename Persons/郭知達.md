# Guō Zhīdá 郭知達

Sòng-period scholar; native of *Shǔ* 蜀 (Sìchuān) per his self-preface to the *Jiǔjiā jízhù Dù shī*. Floruit late 12th century. The preface is dated *Chúnxī* 8 (1181). His one surviving work — and the basis of his importance to the Dù Fǔ commentary tradition — is the *Jiǔjiā jízhù Dù shī* [[KR4c0015]], an editorially conscientious *jízhù* of Dù Fǔ's poetry that consolidates the annotations of nine major Sòng commentators (王洙, 宋祁, 王安石, 黃庭堅, 薛夢符, 杜田, 鮑彪, 師尹, 趙次公) while explicitly stripping out the apocryphal *Lǎo Dù shìshí* 老杜事實 forgeries that had circulated in Sòng book-stalls. Chén Zhènsūn's 陳振孫 *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* praises this editorial discrimination in unusually warm terms. The text was reprinted in *Bǎoqìng* 1 (1225) by Zēng È 曾噩 in *Guǎngzhōu*; this *Bǎoqìng* print is the basis of the WYG. CBDB has no entry.
