# Hánxūzǐ Gùxuán 涵虗子固玄

Qīng lay-Buddhist / *sānjiào héyī* 三教合一 syncretic teacher (lifedates unrecorded), named in the preface to [[周上智]] Zhōu Shàngzhì's annotated *Gāowáng Guānyīn jīng zhùshì* ([[KR6d0125]], X35n0648) as the spiritual master under whom Zhōu studied. No independent biographical record located.
