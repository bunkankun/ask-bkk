**Hé Jíān** 何集庵 (*fl.* late Míng / Qīng), compiler associated with the popular Qīng-period obstetrical compendium containing the *Huíshēng dān* 回生丹 (Resuscitation Pill) formula, an appendix to the *Wànshì nǚkē* 萬氏女科 ([[KR3ei040]]) recension. No further biographical detail located.
