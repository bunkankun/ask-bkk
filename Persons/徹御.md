Chèyù 徹御, *hào* **Fóyǔ** 佛語. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huìjué Zhàoyī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) in the Pòshān-Sìchuān sub-line. Native of **Jiāzhōu** 嘉州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Fàn** 范. Tonsured at **10** under Zhàoyī; *xǐng* on a *guān sǐshī* 觀死屍 corpse-meditation incident. Abbot at **Yúzhōu Xiāngguósì** 渝州香國寺 (Chóngqìng). Died **康熙癸丑 12/15** = **1674-01-21 Gregorian** (*kāngxī 12 / 12*), *shìshòu* 40.

**Heirs.** **Yìtáng Fǎxiù** [[法秀]] 寱堂法秀 (Chóngqìng Xiāngguósì); **Dàxǐ Zōng** 大璽宗; **Yúruò** 輿若.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0486]] 《慧覺衣禪師語錄》 3 juan — recording his master Zhàoyī's collected sayings.
- [[KR6q0487]] 《佛語御禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J35 B341 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by **Fǎjié** [[法傑]] + **Fǎxiù** [[法秀]] *děng biān*, with front-preface by **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] 聖可德玉.
