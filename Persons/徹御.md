Chè-yù 徹御, *hào* **Fó-yǔ** 佛語. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huì-jué Zhào-yī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Native of **Jiā-zhōu** 嘉州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Fàn** 范. Tonsured at **10** under Zhào-yī; *xǐng* on a *guān sǐ-shī* 觀死屍 corpse-meditation incident. Abbot at **Yú-zhōu Xiāng-guó-sì** 渝州香國寺 (Chóng-qìng). Died **康熙癸丑 12/15** = **1674-01-21 Gregorian** (*kāng-xī 12 / 12*), *shì-shòu* 40.

**Heirs.** **Yì-táng Fǎ-xiù** [[法秀]] 寱堂法秀 (Chóng-qìng Xiāng-guó-sì); **Dà-xǐ Zōng** 大璽宗; **Yú-ruò** 輿若.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0486]] 《慧覺衣禪師語錄》 3 juan — recording his master Zhào-yī's collected sayings.
- [[KR6q0487]] 《佛語御禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B341 — his own *yǔ-lù*, compiled by **Fǎ-jié** [[法傑]] + **Fǎ-xiù** [[法秀]] *děng biān*, with front-preface by **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] 聖可德玉.
