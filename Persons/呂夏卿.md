# Lǚ Xiàqīng 呂夏卿

*Zì* Jìnshū 縉叔. Native of Quán-zhōu 泉州 (Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of *Qìng-lì* 2 (1042). Active mid-Northern Sòng, *Qìng-lì* through *Xī-níng* eras (1040s–1080s).

Held *Hànlín shìjiǎng* 翰林侍講 and various Hànlín posts in the imperial-academy bureaucracy. As a member of the *Xīn Tángshū* compilation team led by [[歐陽修]] and [[宋祁]], he was the principal compiler of the *Zǎi-xiàng shìxì biǎo* 宰相世系表 (KR2a0027 *juǎn* 71–75) — the great Tang chief-ministerial genealogical table — the largest medieval Chinese genealogical compilation extant in any *zhèngshǐ*. His work on the *Bīng zhì* 兵志 (the *Xīn Tángshū*'s monograph on Tang military institutions) was also substantial, though by the rule of conventional attribution the work is signed under Ōuyáng Xiū's name.

His biography is in *Sòngshǐ* 331 (KR2a0032).
