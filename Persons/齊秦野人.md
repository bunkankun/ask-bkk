Qí Qín Yěrén 齊秦野人 ("Wild Man of Qí and Qín") is a pen name used by the author of *Wǔzōng Yìshǐ* 武宗逸史 ([[KR4k0324]]), a Republican-era historical novel about the Zhèngdé Emperor of the Míng dynasty. The real identity is unknown. Active ca. 1910s–1940s.
