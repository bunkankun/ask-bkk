# 淨岳
Jìngyuè

A Sòng-period *śramaṇa* of Yúnjiān 雲間 (Sōngjiāng 松江), known from the *Kē Jīngāngbēi xù* 科金剛錍序 preserved in [[KR6d0175]] (Zhànrán's *Jīngāngbēi*) and the sectional analysis *Jīngāngbēi kē* [[KR6d0177]]. Body attribution: *Sòng Yúnjiān shāmén Jìngyuè zhuàn* 宋雲間沙門淨岳撰. Some catalogers identify Jìngyuè 淨岳 as an alternate name of the *shānwài* spokesperson [[仁岳]] Rényuè (cf. KR6d0177); the identification is conventional but remains contested.
