**Yè Qǐdōng** 葉啟東, early-Qīng Sūzhōu physician, disciple of [[葉桂]] Yè Guì (Tiānshì); listed in [[唐大烈]] Táng Dàliè's preface to [[KR3eq017]] *Wúyī huìjiǎng* among the early-Qīng Sūzhōu medical lineage.
