Guō Kuí 郭奎 (d. 1364), *zì* **Zǐzhāng** 子章, native of **Cháoxiàn** 巢縣 (Hézhōu, modern Ānhuī). As a youth a personal student of the Yuán loyalist **Yú Què** 余闕 at Ānqìng — sharing this Ān-qìng-school background with [[劉炳]] Liú Bǐng. Served as *cānmóu* 參謀 in the army of **Zhū Wénzhèng** 朱文正 (Zhū Yuánzhāng's nephew and adopted son, *Dàdūdū* of Nánchāng) at the *Dàdūdū fǔ*; implicated and executed when Wénzhèng was sentenced for treason in Zhìzhèng 24 (1364) — four years before the formal Hóngwǔ founding. His record is appended in *Míng shǐ Wényuàn zhuàn* to **Wáng Miǎn** 王冕's biography. **Zhào Fǎng** 趙汸 and **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 both wrote prefaces for his collection [[KR4e0050]] *Wàngyún jí* 望雲集.
