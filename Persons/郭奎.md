Guō Kuí 郭奎 (d. 1364), *zì* **Zǐ-zhāng** 子章, native of **Cháo-xiàn** 巢縣 (Hé-zhōu, modern Ān-huī). As a youth a personal student of the Yuán loyalist **Yú Què** 余闕 at Ān-qìng — sharing this Ān-qìng-school background with [[劉炳]] Liú Bǐng. Served as *cān-móu* 參謀 in the army of **Zhū Wén-zhèng** 朱文正 (Zhū Yuán-zhāng's nephew and adopted son, *Dà-dū-dū* of Nán-chāng) at the *Dà-dū-dū fǔ*; implicated and executed when Wén-zhèng was sentenced for treason in Zhì-zhèng 24 (1364) — four years before the formal Hóngwǔ founding. His record is appended in *Míng shǐ Wén-yuàn zhuàn* to **Wáng Miǎn** 王冕's biography. **Zhào Fǎng** 趙汸 and **Sòng Lián** 宋濂 both wrote prefaces for his collection [[KR4e0050]] *Wàng-yún jí* 望雲集.
