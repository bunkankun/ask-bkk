Lián-yuè Yìn-zhèng 蓮月印正 (1614 – Kāng-xī 30.7.10 = 3 August 1691), *zì* **Lián-yuè** 蓮月, was a Sì-chuān-born early-Qīng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) monk and the principal **Hú-běi** diaspora heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Lay surname **Jiāng** 姜; native of **Yuè-chí** 岳池 (north-east Sì-chuān, Guǎng-ān 廣安 prefecture). After the death of his mother, hearing a monk recite the *sān-jiè huǒ-zhái* 三界火宅 passage, he self-tonsured and received full ordination under Pòshān at **Fú-líng** 涪陵; the transmission occurred informally on the road when the master stumbled on an errand and the resulting exchange of *jī-yǔ* 機語 served as the occasion for Pòshān's *fù-fǎ* 付法. He was one of the eighty-seven enumerated Pòshān heirs.

Abbacies in two geographical clusters. **Guì-zhōu / Sì-chuān phase:** **Jiǔ-qīng-shān Dōng-yìn-sì** 九青山東印寺 in Zūn-yì-fǔ 遵義府 from Shùn-zhì 15 (1658), thereafter **Jué-lín** 覺林, **Bǎo-lún** 寶輪, **Dù-dá** 度達, **Bǎo-shòu** 保壽. **Hú-běi phase:** **Lóng-xìng** 龍興, **Jǐng-dé** 景德, **Fú-chāng** 福昌, **Dāng-yáng Yù-quán-sì** 當陽玉泉寺 in Jīng-mén (the great Zhì-zhě Tiān-tāi seat, re-taken by the Mìyún Línjì network), and **Tiān-huáng-sì** 天皇寺. Died at the age of 78 *sùi*, two heirs: **Yì-tiān-hǎi** 溢天海 and **Chūn-yán-róng** 春巖容.

Author of 《蓮月禪師語錄》 6 juan and 《玉泉蓮月正禪師語錄》 2 juan (both in Jiāxīng/Xù-zàng supplements, not in Kanripo). His principal textual achievement, however, is as editor (*biān*) of **[[KR6q0402]]** 《破山禪師語錄》 20 juan (J26 B177, Kāng-xī 19 = 1680) — the full posthumous recension of his master's sayings — for which he also wrote the 行狀 appended as the penultimate piece of juan 20, dated **Kāng-xī 癸丑 (12 = 1673) 孟夏月 門人印正謹狀** from his Yù-quán seat. Listed in the *Sòng-yuán xù-dēng** and *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū** 88; biography in 《錦江禪燈》 10, 《新續高僧傳四集》 56. DILA assigns him to the 臨濟宗 楊岐派 sect-bloc, i.e. the Mìyún Yuánwù main line through Pòshān.
