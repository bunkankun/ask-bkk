Liányuè Yìnzhèng 蓮月印正 (1614 – Kāngxī 30.7.10 = 3 August 1691), *zì* **Liányuè** 蓮月, was a Sì-chuān-born early-Qīng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) monk and the principal **Húběi** diaspora heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Lay surname **Jiāng** 姜; native of **Yuèchí** 岳池 (north-east Sìchuān, Guǎngān 廣安 prefecture). After the death of his mother, hearing a monk recite the *sānjiè huǒzhái* 三界火宅 passage, he self-tonsured and received full ordination under Pòshān at **Fúlíng** 涪陵; the transmission occurred informally on the road when the master stumbled on an errand and the resulting exchange of *jīyǔ* 機語 served as the occasion for Pòshān's *fùfǎ* 付法. He was one of the eighty-seven enumerated Pòshān heirs.

Abbacies in two geographical clusters. **Guìzhōu / Sìchuān phase:** **Jiǔqīngshān Dōngyìnsì** 九青山東印寺 in Zūnyìfǔ 遵義府 from Shùnzhì 15 (1658), thereafter **Juélín** 覺林, **Bǎolún** 寶輪, **Dùdá** 度達, **Bǎoshòu** 保壽. **Húběi phase:** **Lóngxìng** 龍興, **Jǐngdé** 景德, **Fúchāng** 福昌, **Dāngyáng Yùquánsì** 當陽玉泉寺 in Jīngmén (the great Zhìzhě Tiāntāi seat, re-taken by the Mìyún Línjì network), and **Tiānhuángsì** 天皇寺. Died at the age of 78 *sùi*, two heirs: **Yìtiānhǎi** 溢天海 and **Chūnyánróng** 春巖容.

Author of 《蓮月禪師語錄》 6 juan and 《玉泉蓮月正禪師語錄》 2 juan (both in Jiāxīng/Xùzàng supplements, not in Kanripo). His principal textual achievement, however, is as editor (*biān*) of **[[KR6q0402]]** 《破山禪師語錄》 20 juan (J26 B177, Kāngxī 19 = 1680) — the full posthumous recension of his master's sayings — for which he also wrote the 行狀 appended as the penultimate piece of juan 20, dated **Kāngxī 癸丑 (12 = 1673) 孟夏月 門人印正謹狀** from his Yùquán seat. Listed in the *Sòngyuán xùdēng** and *Wǔdēng quánshū** 88; biography in 《錦江禪燈》 10, 《新續高僧傳四集》 56. DILA assigns him to the 臨濟宗 楊岐派 sect-bloc, i.e. the Mìyún Yuánwù main line through Pòshān.
