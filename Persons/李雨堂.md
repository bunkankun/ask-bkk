Lǐ Yǔtáng 李雨堂 is the name under which the popular novel *Wànhuā Lóu* 萬花樓 ([[KR4k0240]]) was published in the Qīng period. Modern scholarship regards "李雨堂" as a pseudonym (筆名 bǐmíng) or publisher's attribution rather than the name of a historical individual. No biographical record has been identified, and no CBDB entry exists for a person of this name that can be connected to the novel. The actual authorship of *Wànhuā Lóu* is unknown.

The novel *Wànhuā Lóu Yǎnyì* 萬花樓演義 (also titled *Dí Qīng Yǎnyì* 狄青演義) in its printed form attributes composition to "Li Yutang" without further identification. This is consistent with the anonymous or pseudonymous tradition of mid-Qīng popular chivalric fiction (*gōng'àn xiá yì* 公案俠義 xiaoshuo), in which publisher-supplied or arbitrary pseudonyms are common. The novel centers on the Song-dynasty general Dí Qīng 狄青 and the judge Bāo Zhěng 包拯.
