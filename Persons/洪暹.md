Hóngxiān 洪暹 (= **Hānyú Xiān** 憨予暹), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zìxián Xíngjué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (1610–1662) at **Jīngxī Fúróngsì** 荊溪芙蓉禪寺. Listed among Xíngjué's 33 named *fǎsì* per DILA.

**Abbacy.** **Fényáng** 汾陽 (Shǎnxī / Shānxī Fénzhōu region — the historic seat of **Fényáng Shànzhāo** 汾陽善昭, 947–1024). The Hú Shìān preface to [[KR6q0470]] (dated **順治己亥 = 1659**) confirms Hóngxiān's installation at *Fényáng* by mid-1659, with the *kāifǎ* lineage-claim positioning him as continuator of the famous Línjì Fényáng pedagogical tradition.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0469]] 《自閒覺禪師語錄》 8 juan — his master Xíngjué's collected sayings, credited in the catalog as *Hóngxiān děng biān* 洪暹等編.
- [[KR6q0470]] 《憨予暹禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B288 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by his disciple **Fǎyún** [[法雲]] *děng biān*, with front-preface by **Hú Shìān** 胡世安 *Tàizǐ tàibǎo bīngbù shàngshū Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì Xīshǔ* 太子太保兵部尚書武英殿大學士西蜀 dated **順治己亥仲秋望日 = 1659/9/15 lunar = 1659-10-26 Gregorian**.
