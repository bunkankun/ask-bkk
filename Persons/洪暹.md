Hóng-xiān 洪暹 (= **Hān-yú Xiān** 憨予暹), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zì-xián Xíng-jué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (1610–1662) at **Jīng-xī Fú-róng-sì** 荊溪芙蓉禪寺. Listed among Xíng-jué's 33 named *fǎ-sì* per DILA.

**Abbacy.** **Fén-yáng** 汾陽 (Shǎn-xī / Shān-xī Fén-zhōu region — the historic seat of **Fén-yáng Shàn-zhāo** 汾陽善昭, 947–1024). The Hú Shì-ān preface to [[KR6q0470]] (dated **順治己亥 = 1659**) confirms Hóng-xiān's installation at *Fén-yáng* by mid-1659, with the *kāi-fǎ* lineage-claim positioning him as continuator of the famous Línjì Fén-yáng pedagogical tradition.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0469]] 《自閒覺禪師語錄》 8 juan — his master Xíng-jué's collected sayings, credited in the catalog as *Hóng-xiān děng biān* 洪暹等編.
- [[KR6q0470]] 《憨予暹禪師語錄》 6 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B288 — his own *yǔ-lù*, compiled by his disciple **Fǎ-yún** [[法雲]] *děng biān*, with front-preface by **Hú Shì-ān** 胡世安 *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo bīng-bù shàng-shū Wǔ-yīng-diàn dà-xué-shì Xī-shǔ* 太子太保兵部尚書武英殿大學士西蜀 dated **順治己亥仲秋望日 = 1659/9/15 lunar = 1659-10-26 Gregorian**.
