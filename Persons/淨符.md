# Jìng-fú 淨符 (Wèi-zhōng Jìng-fú 位中淨符)

Qīng Línjì-lineage Chán master. *Hào* Wèi-zhōng 位中 ("Within-Position"), Bái-yán 白巖 ("White Cliff"), Ǒu dàorén 偶道人 ("Coincidental Daoist"). Native of Lú-líng 廬陵 (modern Jí-ān 吉安, Jiāng-xī). Lifedates unrecorded; active late 17th to early 18th century.

Principal Kanripo-canonical contribution: the *Zōng mén niān gǔ huì jí* 宗門拈古彙集 [[KR6q0245]] — a massive 45-juan Chán prose-commentary anthology gathering *niān gǔ* responses to classical cases from across the Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng Chán tradition. Jìng-fú produced several other Chán anthology-compilations (preserved in related Jiāxīng-Canon entries).
