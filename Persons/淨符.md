# Jìng-fú 淨符 (Wèi-zhōng Jìng-fú 位中淨符)

Qīng **Cáo-dòng** 曹洞-lineage Chán master. *Hào* **Wèi-zhōng** 位中 ("Within-Position"), **Bái-yán** 白巖 ("White Cliff"), **Ǒu dàorén** 偶道人 ("Coincidental Daoist"). Native of **Lú-líng** 廬陵 (modern Jí-ān 吉安, Jiāng-xī), secular surname **Liú** 劉. Lifedates unrecorded; active mid-late 17th century, with documented works dating from **Kāng-xī 6** (1667) through **Kāng-xī 11** (1672).

Dharma-disciple of **Shí-yǔ Míng-fāng** 石雨明方 (1593–1648). After failed visits to **Biàn-shān Ruì-bái Míng-xuě** 弁山瑞白明雪 he returned to his native place; an awakening was triggered by witnessing a bird drop a fallen pear, then confirmed by reading the *Chuán-dēng-lù* passage on **Jiǔ-fēng** 九峰 ("How is the student-self?" / "What more does one ask?"). He went to **Bǎo-shòu** 寶壽 to receive transmission from Shí-yǔ Míng-fāng. From **Chóng-zhēn** 14 (1641) he held the abbacy of **Bái-yán Zūn-shèng-sì** 白巖尊聖寺 in **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu) for thirty years.

His Kanripo-canonical works include:
- **《法門鋤宄》** *Fǎ-mén chú-guǐ* ([[KR6r0113]], X1604, 1 juan, 1667) — Cáo-dòng-side polemic against rival Chán lineage-claims and against the Línjì-side appropriation of Cáo-dòng transmission.
- **《宗門拈古彙集》** *Zōng-mén niān-gǔ huì-jí* ([[KR6q0245]], X1296, 45 juan) — a massive Chán prose-commentary anthology gathering *niān-gǔ* responses to classical cases from across the Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng Chán tradition.
- **《祖燈大統》** *Zǔ-dēng dà-tǒng* (98 juan + 2-juan index, completed Kāng-xī 11 = 1672) — comprehensive Chán transmission compilation.
- **《人天眼目考》** *Rén-tiān yǎn-mù kǎo*, **《頌古摘珠》** *Sòng-gǔ zhāi-zhū* (each 1 juan).
