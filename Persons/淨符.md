# Jìngfú 淨符 (Wèizhōng Jìngfú 位中淨符)

Qīng **Cáodòng** 曹洞-lineage Chán master. *Hào* **Wèizhōng** 位中 ("Within-Position"), **Báiyán** 白巖 ("White Cliff"), **Ǒu dàorén** 偶道人 ("Coincidental Daoist"). Native of **Lúlíng** 廬陵 (modern Jíān 吉安, Jiāngxī), secular surname **Liú** 劉. Lifedates unrecorded; active mid-late 17th century, with documented works dating from **Kāngxī 6** (1667) through **Kāngxī 11** (1672).

Dharma-disciple of **Shíyǔ Míngfāng** 石雨明方 (1593–1648). After failed visits to **Biànshān Ruìbái Míngxuě** 弁山瑞白明雪 he returned to his native place; an awakening was triggered by witnessing a bird drop a fallen pear, then confirmed by reading the *Chuándēnglù* passage on **Jiǔfēng** 九峰 ("How is the student-self?" / "What more does one ask?"). He went to **Bǎoshòu** 寶壽 to receive transmission from Shíyǔ Míngfāng. From **Chóngzhēn** 14 (1641) he held the abbacy of **Báiyán Zūnshèngsì** 白巖尊聖寺 in **Qiántáng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu) for thirty years.

His Kanripo-canonical works include:
- **《法門鋤宄》** *Fǎmén chúguǐ* ([[KR6r0113]], X1604, 1 juan, 1667) — Cáo-dòng-side polemic against rival Chán lineage-claims and against the Línjì-side appropriation of Cáodòng transmission.
- **《宗門拈古彙集》** *Zōngmén niāngǔ huìjí* ([[KR6q0245]], X1296, 45 juan) — a massive Chán prose-commentary anthology gathering *niāngǔ* responses to classical cases from across the SòngYuánMíngQīng Chán tradition.
- **《祖燈大統》** *Zǔdēng dàtǒng* (98 juan + 2-juan index, completed Kāngxī 11 = 1672) — comprehensive Chán transmission compilation.
- **《人天眼目考》** *Réntiān yǎnmù kǎo*, **《頌古摘珠》** *Sònggǔ zhāizhū* (each 1 juan).
