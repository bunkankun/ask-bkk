**Jiāng Kǎoqīng** 江考卿 (alt. name **Jiāng Xiáng** 江祥; *zì* Guóxīng 國興; *hào* Ruìpíng 瑞屏; b. ca. 1770, *fl.* mid-Dào-guāng, 清), Qīng-period traumatology specialist (*shāngkē* 傷科) of **Qīnghuá** 清華 township in northern **Wùyuán** 婺源 county (then Huīzhōu prefecture, now Jiāngxī). He spent his career as a village physician in upper Wùyuán treating fall-and-blow trauma; the inference that he was born ca. 1770 comes from the colophon to his manuscript [[KR3el007]] / [[KR3el017]] *Jiāngshì shāngkē xué* 江氏傷科學 (also titled 江氏傷科方書), which states that he was seventy *suì* when he completed the work in *Dàoguāng* 20 (1840). The manuscript circulated only in hand-copy during his lifetime and was first printed in 1924 by [[裘吉生]] Qiū Jìshēng (1873–1947) in the *Sānsān yībào* 三三醫報 journal. No exact death year is preserved.
