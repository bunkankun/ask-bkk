**Zhèng Yùtán** 鄭玉壇 (*hào* Tóngyuán zhǔrén 彤園主人, *fl.* late *Jiā-qìng* / early *Dào-guāng*, early 19th c. 清), mid-Qīng physician. Author of the *Tóng-yuán yī-shū* 彤園醫書, a comprehensive medical compendium issued under the *Tóng-yuán* (Vermillion-Garden) studio imprint; the gynecology section is [[KR3ei037]] *Tóng-yuán yī-shū·Fù-rén-kē*. The compilation is in the standard mid-Qīng *zhé-zhōng* 折衷 (compromise-synthesis) tradition, drawing on the major late-imperial gynecological authorities. No further biographical detail located.
