Lǚ Gōng 呂宮 (1603–1664), early-Qīng official of Wǔjìn 武進 (Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Zhuàngyuán* (top *jìnshì*) of *Shùnzhì xīnmǎo* (1651); rose to *Hóngwényuàn dàxuéshì* and *Lǐbù shàngshū*. He compiled the *Yù dìng zīzhèng yào lǎn* 御定資政要覽 ([[KR3a0101]]) under Shùnzhì's direction. CBDB id 58963.
