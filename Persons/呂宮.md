Lǚ Gōng 呂宮 (1603–1664), early-Qīng official of Wǔ-jìn 武進 (Cháng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). *Zhuàng-yuán* (top *jìnshì*) of *Shùnzhì xīn-mǎo* (1651); rose to *Hóng-wén-yuàn dà-xué-shì* and *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. He compiled the *Yù dìng zī-zhèng yào lǎn* 御定資政要覽 (KR3a0101) under Shùnzhì's direction. CBDB id 58963.
