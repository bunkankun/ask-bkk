**Zhōu Shēng-yì** 周聲溢 (*hào* Jìng-ān 靖庵, *fl.* late Qīng; deceased before *c.* 1910), late-Qīng **Cháng-shā** 長沙 (Húnán) literatus-physician. *Zhōng-shū* 中書 (secretariat) official; scholar of *Shuō-wén jiě-zì* and small-seal calligraphy; member of the Zhōu family hereditary-medical lineage (grandfather Shǎo-lǚ 少呂 was a famous Cháng-shā physician). Author of the *Jìng-ān shuō-yī* 靖庵說醫 ([[KR3eq077]]) and the companion *Yī-xué shí-yàn* 醫學實驗 (experimental-medical observations). Elder brother: Zhōu Yǐng-shēng 周郢生 (a county magistrate who brought Zhōu Shēng-yì's manuscripts to Beijing for posthumous printing). The works were prefaced by **Yáo Yǒng-pǔ / Yáo Yǒng-gài** 姚永樸 / 姚永概 of the Tóng-chéng school. Not in CBDB.
