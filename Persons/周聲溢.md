**Zhōu Shēngyì** 周聲溢 (*hào* Jìngān 靖庵, *fl.* late Qīng; deceased before *c.* 1910), late-Qīng **Chángshā** 長沙 (Húnán) literatus-physician. *Zhōngshū* 中書 (secretariat) official; scholar of *Shuōwén jiězì* and small-seal calligraphy; member of the Zhōu family hereditary-medical lineage (grandfather Shǎolǚ 少呂 was a famous Chángshā physician). Author of the *Jìngān shuōyī* 靖庵說醫 ([[KR3eq077]]) and the companion *Yīxué shíyàn* 醫學實驗 (experimental-medical observations). Elder brother: Zhōu Yǐngshēng 周郢生 (a county magistrate who brought Zhōu Shēngyì's manuscripts to Beijing for posthumous printing). The works were prefaced by **Yáo Yǒngpǔ / Yáo Yǒnggài** 姚永樸 / 姚永概 of the Tóngchéng school. Not in CBDB.
