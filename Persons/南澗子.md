# 南澗子 Nánjiànzǐ

**Nánjiànzǐ** 南澗子 (*hào*), mid-Míng Ānhuī literatus, friend of [[汪機]] Wāng Jī; contributed a postface to Wāng's *Zhēnjiǔ wènduì* 針灸問對 ([[KR3ee016]], 1530). Otherwise unidentified.
