Late-Míng Buddhist layman, *cānxué ménrén* 參學門人 ("training lay-disciple") of the Cáodòng master **Màilàng Mínghuái** [[明懷]] at Yúnmén Xiǎnshèngsì 雲門顯聖寺 (Shàoxīng). Recorder of the one-juan *Yúnmén Màilàng Huái chánshī zōngmén shènàn* ([[KR6q0387]]), the 1620 catechism of Màilàng's replies to doctrinal objections. No further biographical data located.
