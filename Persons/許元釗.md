Late-Míng Buddhist layman, *cān-xué mén-rén* 參學門人 ("training lay-disciple") of the Cáo-dòng master **Mài-làng Míng-huái** [[明懷]] at Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng-sì 雲門顯聖寺 (Shàoxīng). Recorder of the one-juan *Yún-mén Mài-làng Huái chánshī zōng-mén shè-nàn* ([[KR6q0387]]), the 1620 catechism of Mài-làng's replies to doctrinal objections. No further biographical data located.
