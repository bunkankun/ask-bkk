Late-Míng *Lǐbù* (Board of Rites) official; native of Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Huīzhōu, modern Ānhuī). *Zì* Shānfù 山父. Took the *jìnshì* in *Wànlì yǐwèi* (23, 1595); rose to *Lǐbù cí-jì-sī láng-zhōng* (Director of the Sacrificial Bureau in the Board of Rites). His principal work is the *Míngchén shì kǎo* 明臣謚考 ([[KR2m0030]]), the standard reference for late-Míng posthumous-name conferral. Precise life-dates not transmitted; *fl.* late Wànlì, c. 1595–1620.
