# Cuī Rénshī 崔仁師

Native of Bóling 博陵 (modern Héběi). Member of the great Bóling Cuī 博陵崔氏 lineage. Died 651.

Held *Zhōng-shū shè-rén* 中書舍人, *Mín-bù shìláng* 民部侍郎, and *Tài-fǔ shǎo-qīng* 太僕少卿 under Tang Tàizōng. As an early-Tang historiographer, he was one of the five compilers of the *Zhōu shū* (KR2a0022) under Lìng-hú Détén's chief editorship.

His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 74 and *Xīn Tángshū* 99 (KR2a0026, KR2a0027).
