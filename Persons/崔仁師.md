# Cuī Rénshī 崔仁師

Native of Bóling 博陵 (modern Héběi). Member of the great Bóling Cuī 博陵崔氏 lineage. Died 651.

Held *Zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人, *Mínbù shìláng* 民部侍郎, and *Tàifǔ shǎoqīng* 太僕少卿 under Tang Tàizōng. As an early-Tang historiographer, he was one of the five compilers of the *Zhōu shū* ([[KR2a0022]]) under Lìnghú Détén's chief editorship.

His biographies are in *Jiù Tángshū* 74 and *Xīn Tángshū* 99 ([[KR2a0026]], [[KR2a0027]]).
