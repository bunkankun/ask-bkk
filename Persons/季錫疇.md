Qing dynasty literatus from Taicang 太倉, Jiangsu. Active in the Daoguang period (1821–1850). Wrote the preface to the 道光甲午 (1834) reprint of [[劉賡]]'s 《稽瑞》 (KR3h0104), tracing the text's near-loss and its rescue by the Changshu collector [[陳子準]]. No major official career has been located in available sources. CBDB id 82961, dates 1791–1862.
