**Xǔ Shàng-zhì** 許尚質 (b. 1659; CBDB index year 1659), *zì* **Yòu-wén** 又文 / 幼文, *hào* **Xiǎo-nè** 小訥, native of Shān-yīn 山陰 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). Early Qīng literary man and Daoist sympathiser; he contributed the long parallel-prose preface (*pián-wén*) to [[蔣國祚]]'s commentary on the *Huáng tíng nèi jǐng jīng* in the *Dào zàng jí yào* ([[KR5i0011]]), surveying the Yellow-Court tradition from the Hàn through the Táng with characteristic Shào-xīng-style erudition; his preface invokes Lǐ Yè-hóu, Sūn Sī-miǎo, the Hán Gǔ encounter of Lǎo zǐ and Yǐn Xǐ, and the Jiǎng-shì brothers as "san jué" (three peerless ones) of "the Xǔ family treasure." The signature reads *Shān-yīn Xǔ Shàng-zhì Yòu-wén-shì zhuàn* 山陰許尚質又文氏譔. CBDB ID 75743.
