# Zhī Fǎlǐng 支法領

Eastern Jìn monk who travelled to Khotan (Yútián 于闐) and obtained the 36,000-verse Buddhist Hybrid Sanskrit Vorlage of the *Buddhāvataṃsaka* / *Huáyán jīng* that was then translated by [[佛馱跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra at the Dàochǎngsì 道場寺 in Jiànkāng as the 60-fascicle [[KR6e0001|*Dà fāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng* 大方廣佛華嚴經]] in 418–420. He is named as the source of the manuscript in the colophon to the translation. Lifedates unrecorded.
