Fāng Wényī 方聞一 (fl. *Chún-xī*-era 1170s), of Shū-zhōu 舒州 (modern Qián-shān, Ān-huī). *Jùn-bóshì* 郡博士 (prefectural professor) at Shū-zhōu in the *Chún-xī* era, c. 1175. Otherwise unrecorded in the historical and biographical record beyond his commission of the *Dà Yì cuì yán*.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the compiler of [[KR1a0041]] *Dà Yì cuì yán* 大易粹言 (73 juan), the seven-master Northern-Sòng *Yì*-doctrine compilation undertaken at the order of the Shū-zhōu governor Zēng Tóng 曾穜 of Wēn-líng (Quán-zhōu) in 1175. The work draws on Chéng Hào 程顥, Chéng Yí 程頤, Zhāng Zài 張載, Yáng Shí 楊時, Yóu Zuò 游酢, Guō Zhōngxiào 郭忠孝, and Guō Yōng 郭雍.

CBDB id 38942 records him without lifedates or further biographical detail. The Sòngshǐ Yìwén-zhì erroneously gave authorship of the compilation to Zēng Tóng (which Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* followed, until corrected by the Sìkù editors). The auto-preface, however, is unambiguous: Zēng Tóng *commissioned* (*mìng* 命) and Fāng Wényī *compiled-and-assembled* (*jí* 輯).
