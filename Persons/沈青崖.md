Shěn Qīngyá 沈青崖 (fl. 1730s; lifedates not transmitted), native of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng) — the same regional milieu as the *Hénán tōngzhì* compiler Sūn Hào ([[孫灝]]), to whom he was loosely linked through Hangzhou literary circles. *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī cohort (date uncertain); rose to *liángchǔ-dào* 糧儲道 (Grain-Storage Circuit officer) of Shǎnxī, in which capacity he was assigned, by the Shǎnxī Governor-General and Governor in 1731, the principal *biānzuǎn* role on the Yōngzhèng-era *Shǎnxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0050]]). The *Sìkù tíyào* of [[KR2k0050]] singles out his use of running editorial *àn-yǔ* 案語 (annotations comparing variant readings) as a distinctive philological contribution, anticipating later Qiánlóng-era *kǎozhèng* method. CBDB id 67542 (no dates given). Pinyin tone for 青 standard *qīng*; the source orthographic variant 沈靑崖 (with traditional 靑) is the form printed in the WYG.
