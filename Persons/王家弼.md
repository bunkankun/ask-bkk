**Wáng Jiābì** 王家弼 (Qīng; dates and native place unrecorded) is the attributed author of the *Tiānxué chǎnwēi* 天學闡微 ([[KR3fa036]]), a ten-*juǎn* synthesist work on Western and Chinese astronomy. CBDB has two homonym entries (637173, 637174) without dates; no further biographical data is securely recoverable.
