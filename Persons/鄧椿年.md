Dèng Chūn-nián 鄧椿年 (fl. mid-12th c.), also known simply as Dèng Chūn 鄧椿, son of Dèng Míng-shì 鄧名世. Native of Lín-chuān 臨川 in Fǔ-zhōu 撫州. He is known for two scholarly editorial-compilation projects: the *Huà-jì* 畫繼 in 10 *juan* (a Southern-Sòng continuation of Zhāng Yàn-yuǎn's 張彥遠 *Lì-dài míng-huà jì* 歷代名畫記, KR3i0030, completed *Qián-dào* 3 = 1167) and the editorial completion of his father Dèng Míng-shì's *Gǔ-jīn xìng-shì shū biàn-zhèng* 古今姓氏書辯證 ([[KR3k0018]]). His biography survives in the *Huà-jì* preface and in mentions in later Sòng *huà-pǔ* literature.
