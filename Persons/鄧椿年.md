Dèng Chūnnián 鄧椿年 (fl. mid-12th c.), also known simply as Dèng Chūn 鄧椿, son of Dèng Míngshì 鄧名世. Native of Línchuān 臨川 in Fǔzhōu 撫州. He is known for two scholarly editorial-compilation projects: the *Huàjì* 畫繼 in 10 *juan* (a Southern-Sòng continuation of Zhāng Yànyuǎn's 張彥遠 *Lìdài mínghuà jì* 歷代名畫記, [[KR3i0030]], completed *Qiándào* 3 = 1167) and the editorial completion of his father Dèng Míngshì's *Gǔjīn xìngshì shū biànzhèng* 古今姓氏書辯證 ([[KR3k0018]]). His biography survives in the *Huàjì* preface and in mentions in later Sòng *huàpǔ* literature.
