Southern-Sòng *cí*-poet and orthographer, *zì* Jiān-wú 肩吾, of Péngshān 彭山 (modern Sichuan). *fl.* Jiā-dìng era (early thirteenth century). His sole surviving philological work, the *Zì-tōng* [[KR1j0035]] in 2 *juàn*, was prefaced by Wèi Liǎo-wēng 魏了翁 in Jiā-dìng 13 (1220) — establishing his terminus *ante quem*; Yú Shēn 虞兟's postface of Bǎo-yòu *jiǎ-yín* (1254) describes the work's transmission. The *Zì-tōng* is a Southern-Sòng paleographic dictionary that uses *Shuōwén* analysis of clerical-script (*lì-shū*) elements to clarify Sòng-period vulgar-form corruptions. Lǐ Cóng-zhōu also figures as a *cí*-poet of the *Jiā-dìng* generation (a small body of his lyrics survives). No CBDB entry under this exact identification can be confirmed (CBDB has multiple "Lǐ Cóng-zhōu" entries, none clearly the *Zì-tōng* author).
