Southern-Sòng *cí*-poet and orthographer, *zì* Jiānwú 肩吾, of Péngshān 彭山 (modern Sichuan). *fl.* Jiādìng era (early thirteenth century). His sole surviving philological work, the *Zìtōng* [[KR1j0035]] in 2 *juàn*, was prefaced by Wèi Liǎowēng 魏了翁 in Jiādìng 13 (1220) — establishing his terminus *ante quem*; Yú Shēn 虞兟's postface of Bǎoyòu *jiǎyín* (1254) describes the work's transmission. The *Zìtōng* is a Southern-Sòng paleographic dictionary that uses *Shuōwén* analysis of clerical-script (*lìshū*) elements to clarify Sòng-period vulgar-form corruptions. Lǐ Cóngzhōu also figures as a *cí*-poet of the *Jiādìng* generation (a small body of his lyrics survives). No CBDB entry under this exact identification can be confirmed (CBDB has multiple "Lǐ Cóngzhōu" entries, none clearly the *Zìtōng* author).
