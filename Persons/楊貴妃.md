**Yáng Guìfēi** 楊貴妃 / **Yáng Yùhuán** 楊玉環 (719–756), Imperial Consort of Táng Xuánzōng 玄宗 and one of the canonical "Four Beauties" of Chinese tradition. Conventionally invoked together with [[西施]] Xīshī as a paradigm of female beauty, as in [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn's preface to *Xiānglián rùnsè* 香奩潤色 ([[KR3eo020]]).
