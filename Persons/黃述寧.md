Huáng Shù-níng 黃述寧 (*hào* Dàn-wēng 澹翁) — late-Qīng physician, otherwise obscure. The internal evidence and the 1920 rescue-and-printing trajectory by Qiú Jí-shēng 裘吉生 (Shàoxīng *Yī-yào xué-bào* publisher) place him in the mid-to-late nineteenth century. His [[KR3ep038]] *Huáng Dàn-wēng yī-àn* 黃澹翁醫案 (two *juǎn* of cases, two further *juǎn* of *Fù-fāng* 附方) was edited for first printing by Zhōu Zhèn 周鎮 (*xiǎo-nóng* 小農) of Wú-xī in 1920.
