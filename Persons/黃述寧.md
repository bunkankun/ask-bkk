Huáng Shùníng 黃述寧 (*hào* Dànwēng 澹翁) — late-Qīng physician, otherwise obscure. The internal evidence and the 1920 rescue-and-printing trajectory by Qiú Jíshēng 裘吉生 (Shàoxīng *Yīyào xuébào* publisher) place him in the mid-to-late nineteenth century. His [[KR3ep038]] *Huáng Dànwēng yīàn* 黃澹翁醫案 (two *juǎn* of cases, two further *juǎn* of *Fùfāng* 附方) was edited for first printing by Zhōu Zhèn 周鎮 (*xiǎonóng* 小農) of Wúxī in 1920. CBDB has no entry.
