Shào Lánshēng 邵蘭生 — mid-to-late Qīng physician of the Jiāngnán / Shàoxīng region, otherwise poorly documented. He is distinct from [[邵蘭蓀]] Shào Lánsūn (1855–1919) of [[KR3ep004]] (the *shēng* / *sūn* characters and life-dates differ), although the two may be related members of a Shàoxīng-region Shào family medical lineage. His [[KR3ep034]] *Shàoshì yīàn* 邵氏醫案 (one *juǎn*) preserves mid-to-late nineteenth-century clinical cases in the standard Jiāngnán *wēnbìng* idiom. CBDB has no entry.
