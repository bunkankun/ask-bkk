Shào Lán-shēng 邵蘭生 — mid-to-late Qīng physician of the Jiāng-nán / Shàoxīng region, otherwise poorly documented. He is distinct from [[邵蘭蓀]] Shào Lán-sūn (1855–1919) of [[KR3ep004]] (the *shēng* / *sūn* characters and life-dates differ), although the two may be related members of a Shàoxīng-region Shào family medical lineage. His [[KR3ep034]] *Shào-shì yī-àn* 邵氏醫案 (one *juǎn*) preserves mid-to-late nineteenth-century clinical cases in the standard Jiāng-nán *wēn-bìng* idiom. CBDB has no entry.
