**Líng Yīkuí** 凌一揆 (現代). Modern PRC TCM scholar (1925–1992); responsible for one of the standard punctuated editions of [[KR3ek013]] *Yángyī dàquán*. Faculty member at Chéngdū University of TCM (成都中醫學院).
