**Nakanishi Iyū** 中西惟忠 (Sinicized Zhōngxī Wéizhōng; *zì* Shēnsì 深泗, *hào* Tànzhāi 探齋, 1724–1803, 江戶), Kyoto-based *koihō* 古醫方 school physician of the late Edo period, a second-generation disciple of Yoshimasu Tōdō 吉益東洞. Principal works: *Shānghán zhī yán-jiū* 傷寒之研究 ([[KR3ef029]], 10 juan, 1789) and a *Yào zhēng zhì jí* 藥徵績集. Nakanishi exemplifies the *koihō* school's clinical pragmatism — rejecting Sòng-period and Jīn-Yuán doctrinal accretions and grounding clinical practice in the prescriptive logic of the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* canon directly.
