# Liáng Yì 梁益

*Zì* Yǒuzhí 友直, *hào* Yōngzhāi 庸齋. Native of Jiāngyīn 江陰 (modern Jiāngsū); his ancestors were from Fúzhōu 福州, hence his self-designation Sānshān 三山. Lifedates not recoverable; *Yuán-shǐ* attaches his record to the *Rúlín zhuàn* biography of Lù Wénguī 陸文圭.

He passed the Jiāng-Zhè provincial examination but did not enter office, instead teaching in his home district to the end of his life. Author of *Shī zhuàn pángtōng* 詩傳旁通 in 15 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0029]]) — modeled on Dù Wényīng 杜文瑛's *Yǔ-Mèng pángtōng* (already extant under a similar title) and following the procedure of Kǒng Yǐngdá's and Jiǎ Gōngyàn's *zhèngyì*-style sub-commentaries: documenting every textual parallel and historical reference cited in Zhū Xī's *Shī jí zhuàn* with full source-attribution. The work was prefaced in *Zhìzhèng* 4 (1344) by the Tàipíng-lù *zǒngguǎn fǔ tuīguān* Bīnzhōu Dí Sīzhōng 翟思忠; the late-Míng *Shòujīng tú* of Zhū Mùjué incorrectly attributed authorship to Dí Sīzhōng — an error already corrected by Líng Yuánfǔ 陵元輔 (preserved in Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo*) and noted by the Sìkù editors here.
