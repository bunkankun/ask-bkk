Táng-period Buddhist monk; lifedates not preserved (DILA A001753). Identified in the *Xùzàngjīng* only by a single attribution: *Ānguósì shāmén Chéngzhào* 安國寺沙門澄照 — i.e. monk of *Ānguósì* 安國寺 in Cháng'ān, the famous Táng dynastic monastery on the Cháng'ān south-western ward. His one surviving work is the *Lüè shòu sānguī wǔbājiè bìng púsàjiè* 略授三歸五八戒並菩薩戒 ([[KR6k0250]], X1087). **Note**: distinct from the Sòng-era posthumous title 澄照大師 ("Master of the Clear Light") conferred on *Nánshān Dàoxuān* 南山道宣 by emperor *Huīzōng* 徽宗 in 1101 — that *Chéngzhào* designates Dàoxuān, while the present entry's *Chéngzhào* is a Táng-period monk independently bearing the same religious style.
