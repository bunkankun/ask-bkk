Táng-period Buddhist monk; lifedates not preserved (DILA A001753). Identified in the *Xùzàng-jīng* only by a single attribution: *Ān-guó-sì shā-mén Chéng-zhào* 安國寺沙門澄照 — i.e. monk of *Ān-guó-sì* 安國寺 in Cháng'ān, the famous Táng dynastic monastery on the Cháng'ān south-western ward. His one surviving work is the *Lüè shòu sān-guī wǔ-bā-jiè bìng pú-sà-jiè* 略授三歸五八戒並菩薩戒 ([[KR6k0250]], X1087). **Note**: distinct from the Sòng-era posthumous title 澄照大師 ("Master of the Clear Light") conferred on *Nán-shān Dào-xuān* 南山道宣 by emperor *Huī-zōng* 徽宗 in 1101 — that *Chéng-zhào* designates Dào-xuān, while the present entry's *Chéng-zhào* is a Táng-period monk independently bearing the same religious style.
