**Yīn Zháixīn** 殷宅心 (*zì* **Hézōng** 合宗), late-Míng physician of **Lújiāng** 廬江 (Ānhuī), pupil of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng during the latter's Huáihǎi (Yángzhōu) practice period. First met Wáng in *xīnhài* 辛亥 = 1611 in a Hánjiāng 邗江 / Yángzhōu inn, where Wáng inscribed his clinical case-records to Yīn as a record of the master's teaching. Editor of **[[KR3eq046]]** *Yīxué qióngyuán jí* 醫學窮源集 (6 *juǎn*, compiled posthumously and signed *Chóngzhēn yuánnián* 崇禎元年 = 1628); his editorial extraction of Wáng's *Chǐmùlóu túshuō* 尺木樓圖說 and his clinical case-records preserves the principal late-Míng exposition of the *yùnqì* doctrine. Not in CBDB.
