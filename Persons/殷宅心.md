**Yīn Zhái-xīn** 殷宅心 (*zì* **Hé-zōng** 合宗), late-Míng physician of **Lú-jiāng** 廬江 (Ān-huī), pupil of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěn-táng during the latter's Huái-hǎi (Yáng-zhōu) practice period. First met Wáng in *xīn-hài* 辛亥 = 1611 in a Hán-jiāng 邗江 / Yáng-zhōu inn, where Wáng inscribed his clinical case-records to Yīn as a record of the master's teaching. Editor of **[[KR3eq046]]** *Yī-xué qióng-yuán jí* 醫學窮源集 (6 *juǎn*, compiled posthumously and signed *Chóng-zhēn yuán-nián* 崇禎元年 = 1628); his editorial extraction of Wáng's *Chǐ-mù-lóu tú-shuō* 尺木樓圖說 and his clinical case-records preserves the principal late-Míng exposition of the *yùn-qì* doctrine. Not in CBDB.
