**Quán Yuánqǐ** 全元起 (*fl.* late 5th – early 6th c.) was a Southern Liáng physician and the first known commentator on the *Huángdì nèijīng Sùwèn* 黃帝內經素問 ([[KR3ea001]]). His eight-juan recension — the "Quán Yuánqǐ 注本" — preserved the pre-Táng arrangement of the *Sùwèn* with the seventh juan already missing; it was the principal witness from which [[王冰]] Wáng Bīng worked in 762 and which [[林億]] Lín Yì's Northern-Sòng 校正醫書局 still cited at length in its 1057–1069 collation. Quán's commentary is now lost as a continuous text, but extensive quotations survive in the 新校正 notes of the Sòng *Chóngguǎng bǔzhù* ([[KR3ea003]]); these are the principal evidence for the pre-Wáng-Bīng state of the *Sùwèn* text.
