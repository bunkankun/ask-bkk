Late-Qīng physician of Lánzhōu 蘭州 (Gānsù), son of [[朱序東]] Zhū Xùdōng and the second-generation head of the Lánzhōu Zhūshì hereditary medical lineage. Locally famous as the *Zhū sānyé* 朱三爺 ("Mr. Zhū Three") because of his pre-eminence in the small Lánzhōu medical community of his time. Documented by his grandson Zhū Tàiyán 朱太岩 in the 1991 preface to [[KR3er113]] *Yīxué xīnwù zázhèng yàoyì*: famed for the painstaking pulse-diagnosis (taking the pulse repeatedly to ensure accuracy), prudent prescription, attention to dosage even by fraction-cents, and willingness to attend emergency calls at any hour. Supplemented his father Zhū Xùdōng's eyebrow-notes on Chéng Guópéng's *Yīxué xīnwù* (1732). A *Liángzhōu jiàoyú* Sūn Shòushān 孫壽山 plaque inscribed *Dānxī pǐnwàng* 丹溪品望 ("Dān-xī-level reputation") hung in the family's southern hall in his honour. Not in CBDB.
