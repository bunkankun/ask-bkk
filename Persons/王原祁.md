Early-Qīng court painter and Hanlin official; native of Tài-cāng 太倉 (modern Jiāngsū). *Zì* Màojīng 茂京; *hào* Lùtái 麓臺. Grandson of the painter Wáng Shímǐn 王時敏 and one of the *Sì Wáng* 四王 of orthodox Qīng painting (with Wáng Shímǐn, Wáng Jiàn 王鑑, and Wáng Hǔi 王翬). Took the *jìnshì* in *Kāngxī* 9 (1670). Served the Kāngxī court as Hanlin Compiler, *Hǎn-lín shǎo zhān-shì*, and director of the imperial *Pèiwénzhāi shū-huà-pǔ* 佩文齋書畫譜 (1708) and the *Wànshòu shèngdiǎn chūjí* 萬壽盛典初集 ([[KR2m0034]]). His own painting corpus is preserved across major collections worldwide; his theory of orthodox-school painting, articulated in his *Yǔ-chuāng màn-bǐ* 雨窗漫筆, codifies the Dǒng Qíchāng-line lineage. Died in 1715, two years before the *Wànshòu shèngdiǎn* was completed; Wáng Yìqīng saw the work through. CBDB confirms 1642–1715.
