**Xīshī** 西施 (5th c. BCE), legendary beauty of the late Spring-and-Autumn period, native of Zhūluóshān 苧蘿山 in the state of Yuè 越. One of the canonical "Four Beauties" (*sì dà měirén* 四大美人) of Chinese tradition; conventionally invoked alongside [[楊貴妃]] Yáng Guīfēi as a paradigm of female beauty, as in [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn's preface to *Xiānglián rùnsè* 香奩潤色 ([[KR3eo020]]).
