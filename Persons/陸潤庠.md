Late-Qīng *zhuàng-yuán* 狀元 (top *jìnshì* of the *Tóng-zhì* 13 / 1874 examination), courtesy name 鳳石 *Fèng-shí*, native of 元和 *Yuán-hé* (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). CBDB id 61534, 1841–1915. Served at the highest levels of late-Qīng bureaucracy — *Hàn-lín xiū-zhuàn* 翰林修撰, *Tí-xué-shǐ* of multiple provinces, *Wén-yuān-gé dà-xué-shì* 文淵閣大學士 — and after the 1911 revolution refused to serve the Republic, becoming the principal classical-letter teacher of the deposed Xuāntǒng Emperor in the Imperial Household. Active calligrapher and bibliophile. Wrote the senior preface to [[KR3em034]] *Hóu-kē jīn-yuè quán-shū* (mid-summer 1911), modestly disclaiming medical expertise but praising the author袁仁賢 for "spirit-journeying in the age of Shén-nóng and Huáng-dì."
