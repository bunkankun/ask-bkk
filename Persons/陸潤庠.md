Late-Qīng *zhuàngyuán* 狀元 (top *jìnshì* of the *Tóngzhì* 13 / 1874 examination), courtesy name 鳳石 *Fèngshí*, native of 元和 *Yuánhé* (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). CBDB id 61534, 1841–1915. Served at the highest levels of late-Qīng bureaucracy — *Hànlín xiūzhuàn* 翰林修撰, *Tíxuéshǐ* of multiple provinces, *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* 文淵閣大學士 — and after the 1911 revolution refused to serve the Republic, becoming the principal classical-letter teacher of the deposed Xuāntǒng Emperor in the Imperial Household. Active calligrapher and bibliophile. Wrote the senior preface to [[KR3em034]] *Hóukē jīnyuè quánshū* (mid-summer 1911), modestly disclaiming medical expertise but praising the author袁仁賢 for "spirit-journeying in the age of Shénnóng and Huángdì."
