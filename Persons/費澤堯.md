**Fèi Zéyáo** 費澤堯 (*fl.* 1930s, Republican-era), early-Republican physician and friend of [[沈仲圭]] (Shěn Zhòngguī). Wrote the 1939 (*Huángdì jìyuán* 4637) preface to the *Líng Lín língfāng* 凌臨靈方 ([[KR3ed143]]) — the publication of the late-Qīng Húzhōu master Líng Xiǎowǔ's clinical formulas, organised by Shěn Zhòngguī. The use of the *Huángdì jìyuán* dating (rather than the Republican-period 民國 reign-year) signals Fèi's affinity with the early-Republican nationalist *jìyuán* dating movement. Sparse further biographical record.
