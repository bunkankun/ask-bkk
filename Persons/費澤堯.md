**Fèi Zé-yáo** 費澤堯 (*fl.* 1930s, Republican-era), early-Republican physician and friend of [[沈仲圭]] (Shěn Zhòng-guī). Wrote the 1939 (*Huáng-dì jì-yuán* 4637) preface to the *Líng Lín língfāng* 凌臨靈方 ([[KR3ed143]]) — the publication of the late-Qīng Hú-zhōu master Líng Xiǎo-wǔ's clinical formulas, organised by Shěn Zhòng-guī. The use of the *Huáng-dì jì-yuán* dating (rather than the Republican-period 民國 reign-year) signals Fèi's affinity with the early-Republican nationalist *jì-yuán* dating movement. Sparse further biographical record.
