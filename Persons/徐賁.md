Xú Bēn 徐賁 (1335–1380), *zì* **Yòu-wén** 幼文, *hào* **Běi-guō shēng** 北郭生 (whence the title of his collection), native of **Wú-chéng** 吳城 (Sū-zhōu), residing outside the **Wàng-qí gate** 望齊門 of Sū-zhōu. One of the canonical **Wú-zhōng Sì-jié** 吳中四傑 with [[高啟]] Gāo Qǐ, [[楊基]] Yáng Jī, and [[張羽]] Zhāng Yǔ. Refused service under **Zhāng Shì-chéng** 張士誠 in the late Yuán and retreated with Zhāng Yǔ to **Wú-xīng** 吳興 — Zhāng to Qīng-shān 菁山, Xú to **Shǔ-shān** 蜀山. Summoned in Hóngwǔ 9 (1376), appointed *gěi-shì-zhōng* 給事中, then *jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 監察御史; sent out as *àn Shān-xī*, promoted *Guǎng-xī cān-zhèng* 廣西參政, then *Hé-nán zuǒ bù-zhèng-shǐ* 河南左布政使. Died in office c. 1380; per DMB, imprisoned and died (likely starved) on charges of failing to provision Hóngwǔ's northern campaigns. His collection is [[KR4e0043]] *Běi-guō jí* 北郭集, first printed by **Zhāng Qǐ-áo** 張企翺 in the late 15th century. CBDB id 34387 records the name and Míng dynasty but no dates; the catalog meta lifedates 1335–1380 are followed here.
