Xú Bēn 徐賁 (1335–1380), *zì* **Yòuwén** 幼文, *hào* **Běiguō shēng** 北郭生 (whence the title of his collection), native of **Wúchéng** 吳城 (Sūzhōu), residing outside the **Wàngqí gate** 望齊門 of Sūzhōu. One of the canonical **Wúzhōng Sìjié** 吳中四傑 with [[高啟]] Gāo Qǐ, [[楊基]] Yáng Jī, and [[張羽]] Zhāng Yǔ. Refused service under **Zhāng Shìchéng** 張士誠 in the late Yuán and retreated with Zhāng Yǔ to **Wúxīng** 吳興 — Zhāng to Qīngshān 菁山, Xú to **Shǔshān** 蜀山. Summoned in Hóngwǔ 9 (1376), appointed *gěishìzhōng* 給事中, then *jiānchá yùshǐ* 監察御史; sent out as *àn Shānxī*, promoted *Guǎngxī cānzhèng* 廣西參政, then *Hénán zuǒ bùzhèngshǐ* 河南左布政使. Died in office c. 1380; per DMB, imprisoned and died (likely starved) on charges of failing to provision Hóngwǔ's northern campaigns. His collection is [[KR4e0043]] *Běiguō jí* 北郭集, first printed by **Zhāng Qǐáo** 張企翺 in the late 15th century. CBDB id 34387 records the name and Míng dynasty but no dates; the catalog meta lifedates 1335–1380 are followed here.
