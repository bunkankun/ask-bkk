# 文帝
Wéndì (Yáng Jiān 楊堅, 541–604)

Founding emperor of the Suí dynasty (r. 581–604). Patron of the Suí Buddhist establishment at the *Dàxīngshàn* 大興善 monastery; under his reign Indian translators such as [[闍那崛多]] Jñānagupta led the imperial translation programme.
