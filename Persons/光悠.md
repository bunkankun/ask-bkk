Guāngyōu 光悠, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Héngxiù Fālín** [[發林]] 恒秀發林 at the **Lǜluó Héngxiù** 綠蘿恒秀 abbacy. Lead compiler of the 2-juan 《綠蘿恒秀林禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0494]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
