Má Gé 麻革 (*zì* Xìnzhī 信之; mid-13th c.) of Wángguān 王官 wrote the *guǐmǎo* = 1243 preface to [[王好古]] Wáng Hǎogǔ's *Yīnzhèng lüèlì* ([[KR3eh045]]); the dated preface fixes the work's date of composition.
