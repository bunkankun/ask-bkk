# Guō Dìngjìng 郭定靜

Mid-Míng *jìnshì* of Gāopíng 高平; served as magistrate of Pīzhōu 邳州, where he discovered the manuscript of the paediatric question-and-answer compendium *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]]) in the county archives. After his death the work was carved and published in 1506 by his son [[郭坤]] Guō Kūn. Lifedates unrecorded.
