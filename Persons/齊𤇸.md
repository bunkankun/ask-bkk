Qí Xī 齊𤇸 (Sòng), *zì* Chōngfǔ 充甫, was a late-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* compiler of Pó-yáng 鄱陽 (Jiāng-xī). Co-compiler with Zhāng Hóng 張洪 of the *Zhū-zǐ dú-shū fǎ* 朱子讀書法 (KR3a0070); at the time of compilation he was visiting Zhè-dōng. CBDB id 49963, no dates. The character 𤇸 (= 熙 with 火 instead of 灬) is rare; the conventional simplification is 熙. The pinyin reading is *Xī*.
