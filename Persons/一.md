Yī 一 (catalog form; full reference **Jiàntáng Yī** 鑑堂一), *zì* **Jiàntáng** 鑑堂 ("Mirror Hall"). Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] (1639–1707) — **Nányuè 31st-generation** descendant in the Línjì line per *Zhōnghuá Dàzàngjīng zǒngmùlù*. Lifedates unknown.

**Abbacy.** **Chánglè chánsì** 常樂禪寺.

**Disciples / compilers.** **Míngmǎn** [[明滿]], **Mínghuì** [[明慧]], **Míngfú** 明福.

**Work.** [[KR6q0594]] 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B486, recorded by *fǎsì* **Míngmǎn** [[明滿]] and **Mínghuì** [[明慧]] *děng lù*; preface by lay scholar **Dù Yǔnzhēn** [[杜允貞]].

**Sources.** 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 vol. 1.
