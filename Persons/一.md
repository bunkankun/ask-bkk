Yī 一 (catalog form; full reference **Jiàn-táng Yī** 鑑堂一), *zì* **Jiàn-táng** 鑑堂 ("Mirror Hall"). Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] (1639–1707) — **Nán-yuè 31st-generation** descendant in the Línjì line per *Zhōnghuá Dà-zàng-jīng zǒng-mù-lù*. Lifedates unknown.

**Abbacy.** **Cháng-lè chán-sì** 常樂禪寺.

**Disciples / compilers.** **Míng-mǎn** [[明滿]], **Míng-huì** [[明慧]], **Míng-fú** 明福.

**Work.** [[KR6q0594]] 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B486, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Míng-mǎn** [[明滿]] and **Míng-huì** [[明慧]] *děng lù*; preface by lay scholar **Dù Yǔn-zhēn** [[杜允貞]].

**Sources.** 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 vol. 1.
