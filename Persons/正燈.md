Zhèngdēng 正燈. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔlín Zhì** [[智|Gǔlín Zhì]] (1623–1695) at **Shèngjīng Fèngtiān Bōrě chánsì** 盛京奉天般若禪寺 (Mukden). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0546]] 《盛京奉天般若古林禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B429, prefaced 1675.
