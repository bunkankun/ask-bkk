Late-Táng / early-Sòng scholar-official and philologist, *zì* Dǐng-chén 鼎臣, of Guǎngling (modern Yangzhou). Brother of Xú Kǎi [[徐鍇]] (the two are conventionally referred to as the "Two Xús" 二徐 in Chinese paleography). Held high office under the Southern Táng (rising to *Yù-shǐ dàfū* and *Lì-bù shàngshū*); after the Sòng absorption of the Southern Táng in 975, served the Sòng court as *Sàn-jì cháng-shì*. His monumental contribution is the imperially-commissioned revision of Xǔ Shèn's [[許愼]] *Shuōwén jiězì* [[KR1j0018]] (Yōngxī 3 / 986), the recension known as *Dà-Xú-běn* 大徐本 — adding *fǎn-qiè* readings from Sūn Miǎn's *Táng-yùn*, supplying the *xīn-fù-zì* 新附字, splitting the original 14 *piān* into 30 *juàn*, and adding editorial *àn-yǔ*. He also produced the *Shuōwén jiězì zhuàn-yùn-pǔ* [[KR1j0021]] (with his brother), a phonetically-organized re-presentation of the *Shuōwén* lemmata, and was a notable seal-script calligrapher in his own right. CBDB lifedates 916–991, in agreement with the *Sòng-shǐ* biography (juàn 441).
