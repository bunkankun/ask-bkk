# Gotō Konzan 後藤艮山

Japanese physician of the early-to-mid Edo period (1659–1733), patriarch of the Edo *kohō* 古方 synthesis. Together with [[北山友松子]] Kitayama Yūshōshi and [[名古屋玄醫]] Nagoya Gen'i, he is conventionally credited as a founding figure of the Japanese *kohō* school, which rejected the JīnYuán *LǐZhū* 李朱 synthesis and returned to [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 as the authoritative basis of clinical practice. His clinical maxims and case fragments dominate the opening *juǎn* of [[KR3ep002]] *Xiānzhé yīhuà* 先哲醫話 anthologised by [[淺田宗伯]] Asada Sōhaku.
