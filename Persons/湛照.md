**Tōzan Tanshō** 東山湛照 (1231–1291) — Kamakura-period Japanese **Rinzai-Zen** master, dharma-heir of [[圓爾]] Enni Ben'en, posthumous title **Hōgaku Zenji** 寶覺禪師 ("Treasure-Awakened Zen Master"). Active in the Tōfuku-ji 東福寺 lineage. Recorded Sayings preserved as the *Hōgaku Zenji goroku* 寶覺禪師語錄 ([[KR6t0251]], T80n2545).

Lifedates 1231–1291 from the *Bukkō* (佛光) Buddhist Encyclopedia, p. 6767.

Source: *Bukkō* p. 6767; standard Japanese Rinzai-Zen biographical sources.
