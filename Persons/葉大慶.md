Yè Dàqìng 葉大慶 (fl. early 13th century), *zì* Róng-fǔ 榮甫. CBDB id 49818 (no dates registered). The *Sòng shǐ* gives no biography and the *Sòng shǐ Yìwén zhì* does not record his work; the *Sìkù* editors reconstructed both his life and his book from two prefaces preserved in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*: one by Yè Wǔzǐ 葉武子 of Qiáo-yáng 樵陽 dated *Bǎo-qìng bǐng-xū* (1226), and one by his son Yè Shìzhī 葉釋之 dated *Chún-yòu jiǎ-chén* (1244). From these we know that Yè Dàqìng made his reputation in the Southern Sòng exam-preparation circles for his command of the *cí-fù* 詞賦 form, and that he served as Education Officer (*zhōu-xué jiàoshòu* 州學教授) at Jiàn-zhōu 建州 (modern Jiàn'ōu 建甌, Fújiàn). His native place is not recorded in either preface.

The *Sìkù* editors estimate that he was born after Chéng Dàchāng 程大昌 (whose *Kǎogǔ biān* 攷古編 he silently emulates) and lived into the Ní-zōng / Lǐ-zōng transition. His son's 1244 preface notes that the father had drafted the manuscript three times and never felt confident enough in it to commit to print; the work was nevertheless engraved at Jiàn-zhōu by another *Yè* gentleman shortly before the father's death, and re-cut after the original woodblocks had become illegible.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR3j0047]] *Kǎogǔ zhìyí* 攷古質疑 (6 juan in the *Sìkù* recompilation; the original *juan* count is unrecoverable).
