Jīnwú 今無, *zì* **Āzì** 阿字 ("the Letter A"), known as **Hǎizhuàng ĀzìWú** 海幢阿字無 from his principal abbacy. Cáodòng 35th-generation dharma-heir of **Tiānrán Hánshì** 天然函昰 [[函昰]] (Bóshān 3rd-gen). Born **1633**, died **康熙辛酉 9/22 辰時** = **1681-11-01**, age 49, *sēnglà* 30. Native of **Pānyú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu); lay surname **Wàn** 萬.

**Formation.** At 16 (1648) went to **Léifēng** 雷峰 to take tonsure under Tiānrán. At 17 broke through on the *cān Míng shàngzuò* 參明上座 *gōngàn* upon hearing a cat. At 19 followed Tiānrán into the **Kuānglú** 匡廬 mountains; nearly died of cold-fever en route, was urged in a dream by a deity to "come into the world" (preach). At **22** (1654), at his teacher's command, traveled north of Shānhǎiguān to study with the exiled **Qiānshān Shèngrén Hánkě** [[函可]] for three years. At 28 (1660) returned to Guǎngzhōu. After encountering a regional rebellion in **Qídiàn** 奇甸, returned via the northern frontier and the southern islands of Hǎinán, again attaching himself to Léifēng, where Tiānrán *yìn* him and made him head monk.

**Abbacies.** **壬寅** = **1662** entered **Hǎi-zhuàng-sì** 海幢寺 (Guǎngzhōu) — held this seat for **twelve years**. **癸丑** = **1673** went north as bearer of a Tripiṭaka petition; intercepted by political crisis (the Three Feudatories rebellion), wintered at the residence of **Xiāo-fǔ** 蕭府 in Shāndōng. **甲寅** = **1674** at Jīn-líng. **乙卯** = **1675** returned to Hǎi-zhuàng. **丙辰** = **1676** lectured on the *Yuán-jué jīng*; sealed himself in for half a year due to renewed war. The following year saw Tiān-rán off for his final return to Lú-shān. **戊午** = **1678** initiated the canon-storage hall (*cáng-gé* 藏閣) construction, sending his disciples out across the south to raise funds. Preached at six dharma-assemblies (É-hú 鵝湖, Hé-yuán 河源, Guǎngzhōu and elsewhere); transmitted the precepts for thirteen years. Total disciples (clerical and lay) over 1700.

**Sole *fǎsì*.** **Yúnān Yún** 雲菴雲 (Gǔyún 古雲, DILA A020908).
