Zhèng Zhòng-guāng 鄭重光 (*zì* Zài-xīn 在辛, *hào* Sù-pǔ 素圃; 1638–1716; CBDB 493426 / 502242 records the name without dates) — Xīn-ān 新安 physician of the early Qīng, contemporary of Yú Jiā-yán 喻昌 ([[喻昌]]) and Zhāng Lù 張璐, native of Shè 歙 (Ān-huī). He came to medicine after the death of his father and his own subsequent illness drove him to autodidactic study. His clinical synthesis is *Shāng-hán*-doctrinal, strongly aligned with the early-Qīng *Shāng-hán* commentary tradition of Zhāng Lù, Yú Jiā-yán, and Kē Qín 柯琴. His principal works are the [[KR3ep032]] *Sù-pǔ yī-àn* 素圃醫案 and the *Wēn-yì lùn bǔ-zhù* 溫疫論補注 — an annotated supplement to Wú Yǒu-xìng's *Wēn-yì lùn* that is a key transitional text in *wēn-yì* / *wēn-bìng* studies.
