Zhèng Zhòngguāng 鄭重光 (*zì* Zàixīn 在辛, *hào* Sùpǔ 素圃; 1638–1716; CBDB 493426 / 502242 records the name without dates) — Xīnān 新安 physician of the early Qīng, contemporary of Yú Jiāyán 喻昌 ([[喻昌]]) and Zhāng Lù 張璐, native of Shè 歙 (Ānhuī). He came to medicine after the death of his father and his own subsequent illness drove him to autodidactic study. His clinical synthesis is *Shānghán*-doctrinal, strongly aligned with the early-Qīng *Shānghán* commentary tradition of Zhāng Lù, Yú Jiāyán, and Kē Qín 柯琴. His principal works are the [[KR3ep032]] *Sùpǔ yīàn* 素圃醫案 and the *Wēnyì lùn bǔzhù* 溫疫論補注 — an annotated supplement to Wú Yǒuxìng's *Wēnyì lùn* that is a key transitional text in *wēnyì* / *wēnbìng* studies.
