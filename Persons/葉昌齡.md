Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* scholar active under Rénzōng (r. 1023–1063). He authored *Túyì* 圖義 ("The Meaning of the Charts"), a now-lost polemical work refuting Liú Mù [[劉牧]]'s *Yìshù gōuyǐn tú* 易數鉤隱圖 ([[KR1a0011]]). He is grouped with Sòng Xián [[宋咸]] (author of *Wáng-Liú Yì biàn* 王劉易辨) and Lǐ Gòu [[李覯]] (author of *Shāndìng Yìtú lùn* 刪定易圖論) as one of three contemporaries who attacked Liú Mù's chart-corpus.
