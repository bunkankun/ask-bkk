In the Kanripo catalog the bare temple-name **Gāozōng** 高宗 (with lifedates 1711–1799) refers to the sixth Qīng emperor, *Gāozōng Chún-huángdì* — personal name Hónglì 弘曆, reigning under the *Qiánlóng* 乾隆 era-name (1735–1796). Substantive biographical detail (the *Sì-kù quánshū* commission of 1773–1782, imperial poetry and prefaces, the personal-name taboo on 弘 throughout the SKQS) is given on the longer companion note [[高宗弘曆]].

In KR2f the name [[高宗]] appears in the catalog meta as the editor (敕編) of [[KR2f0006]] *Shìzōng Xiàn-huángdì shèng-xùn* 世宗憲皇帝聖訓 (1741), the Qiánlóng-edited *shèng-xùn* of his father Yōngzhèng's pronouncements.
