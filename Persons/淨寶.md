# Jìngbǎo 淨寶 (Tiānyú Jìngbǎo 天愚淨寶)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng Chán monk. *Hào* Tiānyú 天愚. Dharma-heir of [[明方|Shíyǔ Míngfāng]] 石雨明方 (1593–1648), fellow-disciple of [[淨柱|Yuǎnmén Jìngzhù]] 遠門淨柱; a continuing figure in the late-Míng Cáodòng revival around the Yúnménsì 雲門寺 cluster in Zhèjiāng. Lifedates not recorded in the materials examined.

Sources: *Shíyǔ chánshī fǎ tán* [[KR6q0201]] juan 20 (行狀 by Jìngzhù) lists Jìngbǎo among the named dharma-heirs of Míngfāng.
