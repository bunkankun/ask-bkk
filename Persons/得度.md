# Dé-dù 得度

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[祖杲]]) of *juan* 4 (*jǔ-gǔ* 舉古) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded. (The graph 得度 functions here as a name, not as the technical term for formal ordination, although DILA notes the possible overlap.)
