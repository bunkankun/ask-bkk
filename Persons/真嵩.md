# Zhēnsōng 真嵩 (Qīng, fl. 18th c.)

A Qīng Pure Land monk of *Tiānníngsì* 天甯寺 in Pílíng 毗陵 (Chángzhōu 常州, Jiāngsū), *hào* **Pántán** 槃譚 ("Coiled Discourse"). He is known principally for the *Ēmítuó jīng zhāiyào yìjiě* 阿彌陀經摘要易解 [[KR6p0030]] (X22n0435), a brief commentary on the *Smaller Sukhāvatīvyūma*. The colophon signs him as 後學毗陵天甯沙門真嵩槃譚述. Lifedates not preserved.

The Chángzhōu *Tiānníngsì* was one of the major Qīng Buddhist centres in the lower Yangtze, with a substantial Pure Land tradition and well-developed monastic publishing programme. Zhēnsōng's *Yìjiě* belongs to this tradition. Otherwise undocumented in the standard biographical literature.
