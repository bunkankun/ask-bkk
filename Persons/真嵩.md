# Zhēnsōng 真嵩 (Qīng, fl. 18th c.)

A Qīng Pure Land monk of *Tiān-níng-sì* 天甯寺 in Pí-líng 毗陵 (Cháng-zhōu 常州, Jiāngsū), *hào* **Pántán** 槃譚 ("Coiled Discourse"). He is known principally for the *Ē-mí-tuó jīng zhāi-yào yì-jiě* 阿彌陀經摘要易解 [[KR6p0030]] (X22n0435), a brief commentary on the *Smaller Sukhāvatī-vyūma*. The colophon signs him as 後學毗陵天甯沙門真嵩槃譚述. Lifedates not preserved.

The Cháng-zhōu *Tiān-níng-sì* was one of the major Qīng Buddhist centres in the lower Yangtze, with a substantial Pure Land tradition and well-developed monastic publishing programme. Zhēnsōng's *Yì-jiě* belongs to this tradition. Otherwise undocumented in the standard biographical literature.
