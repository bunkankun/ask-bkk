Hǎichéng 海澂, *shìzhě* 侍者 attendant-disciple of **Dábiàn Xiǎnquán** [[顯權]] 達變顯權 at **Wǔlěishān Língfēng Chánsì** 五磊山靈峰禪寺 (Cíxī, Míngzhōu). Lead recorder of the 5-juan 《達變權禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0457]]), signing the j. 1 colophon as *shìzhě Hǎichéng jìlù* 侍者海澂記錄. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
