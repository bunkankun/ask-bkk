Hǎi-chéng 海澂, *shì-zhě* 侍者 attendant-disciple of **Dá-biàn Xiǎn-quán** [[顯權]] 達變顯權 at **Wǔ-lěi-shān Líng-fēng Chán-sì** 五磊山靈峰禪寺 (Cí-xī, Míng-zhōu). Lead recorder of the 5-juan 《達變權禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0457]]), signing the j. 1 colophon as *shì-zhě Hǎi-chéng jì-lù* 侍者海澂記錄. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
