Dèng Tiětāo 鄧鐵濤 (1916–2019) was the principal 20th-century Cantonese scholar of TCM at the Guǎngzhōu Zhōngyīyào Dàxué 廣州中醫藥大學, recognised in 2003 as a *guóyī dàshī* 國醫大師. He led editorial work on Lǐngnán medical classics including [[何夢瑤]] Hé Mèngyáo's *Yī biàn* ([[KR3eh005]]).
