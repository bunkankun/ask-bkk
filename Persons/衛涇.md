Wèi Jīng 衛涇 (1159–1226, *zì* Qīngshū 清叔, successively *hào* Zhuōzhāi 拙齋 → Xīyuán 西園 → Hòulè 後樂 — the last from Fàn Zhòngyān's *Yuèyánglóu jì*), of *Huátíng* 華亭 (modern Sōngjiāng); settled at *Kūnshān* 崑山. *Jìnshì zhuàngyuán* (1st place) of *Chúnxī* 11 (1184). Held office to *Cānzhī zhèngshì* and *Qiānshū Shūmìyuàn shì*; ennobled as *Qín guógōng* 秦國公; canonized *Wénjié* 文節. *Sòngshǐ* did not give him a biography (the *Sìkù* editors note this omission). Opposed Hán Tuōzhòu 韓侂胄's 1206 *Kāixǐ běifá* on strategic grounds; demoted in 1208; retired at *Shípǔ Xīyuán* for ten years during Hán's domination. Brother of the canonical *Lǐxué* exegete Wèi Shí 衛湜 (author of *Lǐjì jíshuō*). Note variant character: 衞涇 in catalog meta and source 提要; standard form 衛涇. CBDB id 12066. Author of [[KR4d0290]] *Hòulè jí*.
