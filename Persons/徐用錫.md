Xú Yòngxī 徐用錫 (1656–1737), *zì* Tánzhǎng 壇長, *hào* Zhòutáng 晝堂, was an early-Qīng *Lǐxué* official and pupil of Lǐ Guāngdì 李光地. Native of Sùyù 宿豫 (Jiāngsū). Edited (with Lǐ Guāngdì's grandson Lǐ Qīngzhí) the *Róngcūn yǔlù* 榕村語錄 ([[KR3a0114]], the *yǔlù* of Lǐ Guāngdì). CBDB id 86875.
