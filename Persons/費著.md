Fèi Zhù 費著 (*fl.* early-to-mid fourteenth century, Yuán). Native of Huá-yáng 華陽 in Chéngdū-fǔ. Yuán-period ethnically-Hàn local official of Sìchuān. *Jǔ jìnshì* (date unknown); given *Guó-zǐ jiān zhù-jiào* (assistant instructor at the Imperial Academy); rose to *Chóng-qìng-fǔ zǒng-guǎn* (Prefectural Administrator of Chóng-qìng). Author of the *Suì-huá jì-lì pǔ* 嵗華紀麗譜 ([[KR2k0121]]) — the principal Yuán-period monograph on the annual festival cycle of Chéngdū — together with the appended *Jiān-zhǐ pǔ* 牋紙譜 on Shǔ paper and *Shǔ-jǐn pǔ* 蜀錦譜 on Sìchuān brocades, the two foundational pre-Míng technical monographs on Sìchuān paper-making and silk-weaving. Otherwise undocumented; not in CBDB.
