**Wáng Qīnruò** 王欽若 (962–1025), *zì* Dìng guó 定國, was a senior Northern-Sòng Chief Minister, the chief architect of Sòng Zhēn zōng's **"heaven-descending-tokens"** (*tiān shū* 天書) imperial-Daoist spectacle of 1008 and associated Daoist patronage. Author of [[KR5g0094]] ***Yì shèng bǎo dé zhuàn*** 翊聖保德傳 — a Daoist hagiographical-political text.
