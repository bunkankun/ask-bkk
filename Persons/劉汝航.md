Liú Rǔháng 劉汝航, mid-19th-century Yánghú 陽湖 (Chángzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū) literatus and disciple (門人) of [[曹禾]] Cáo Hé. Author of the 1851 (Xiánfēng 1, *Shàngsì* festival) postface to [[KR3eq097]] *Yīxué dúshū zhì*. No standard biographical record located.
