Liú Rǔ-háng 劉汝航, mid-19th-century Yáng-hú 陽湖 (Cháng-zhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū) literatus and disciple (門人) of [[曹禾]] Cáo Hé. Author of the 1851 (Xián-fēng 1, *Shàng-sì* festival) postface to [[KR3eq097]] *Yī-xué dú-shū zhì*. No standard biographical record located.
