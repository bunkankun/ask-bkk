Dài Rèn 戴任 (*fl.* late Wàn-lì, c. 1600), late-Míng compiler. Co-author with [[馮應京]] of the *Yuèlìng guǎngyì* 月令廣義 (Wàn-lì 30 / 1602; 24 juan), the late-Míng *yuèlìng* compendium that supplied the proximate base for the Kāngxī-era [[KR2j0002]] *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào*. He is identified as the editor / supplementer who reorganized and expanded Féng's draft. Not in CBDB; biographical detail is scant. The Kāngxī imperial preface to *Yuèlìng jíyào* names him alongside Féng as the Míng-time originators of the genre's last comprehensive formulation.
