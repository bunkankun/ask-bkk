**Lǚ Tàigǔ** 呂太古 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist, author of [[KR5g0035]] ***Dào mén tōng jiào bì yòng jí*** 道門通教必用集 (preface 1201) — a ritual-liturgy compendium on universal Daoist teachings.
