# Bǎorì 寶日

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *cānxué* 參學 under [[文康]] Mùān Wénkāng; co-editor of the one-juan *Mùān Wénkāng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0348]]). Known only from this editorial credit. Lifedates unrecorded.
