# Bǎo-rì 寶日

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *cān-xué* 參學 under [[文康]] Mù-ān Wén-kāng; co-editor of the one-juan *Mù-ān Wén-kāng chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0348]]). Known only from this editorial credit. Lifedates unrecorded.
