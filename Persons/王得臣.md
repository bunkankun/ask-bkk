Wáng Dé-chén 王得臣 (1036–after 1115), *zì* Yàn-fǔ 彥輔, *hào* Fèng-tíng zǐ 鳳亭子 ("Phoenix-Pavilion Scion"). Native of Ān-lù 安陸 (in Dé-ān 德安 prefecture, modern Hú-běi). *Jiā-yòu* 4 (1059) *jìnshì* — student first of Zhèng Xiè 鄭獬 and then of Hú Yuán 胡瑗 (the great Northern Sòng educator). Long career in central and provincial administration; ended at *Sī-nóng shǎo-qīng* 司農少卿 (Vice-Minister of the Agricultural Court); retired at age 62 in *Shàoshèng* 4 (1097) on grounds of eye-illness. Compiler of the *Zhǔ shǐ* 麈史 ([[KR3j0090]]), a 3-juan Northern Sòng *bǐjì* completed at age 80 in 1115. A Luò-school (Chéng Hào's circle) philosopher who refused factional partisanship — his name accordingly did not appear on the *Yuán-yòu dǎng bēi* 元祐黨碑 of 1102.
