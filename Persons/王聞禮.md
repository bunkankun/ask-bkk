# Wáng Wénlǐ 王聞禮 (Sòng, fl. 1170s)

Son of Wáng Shípéng [[王十朋]] (1112–1171). With his brother Wáng Wénshī [[王聞詩]], edited their father's literary collection [[KR4d0235]] *Méixī xiānshēng wénjí* in 54 *juǎn* after Wáng Shípéng's death (1171). Bibliographic identifier only; no separate biographical record.
