# Liú Xuàn 劉炫

*Zì* Guāngbó 光伯. Late-Suí 隋 classicist of remarkable erudition, native of Hèjiān 河間 (modern Héběi). Together with Liú Zhuó 劉焯 — the "two Liús" (*èr Liú* 二劉) of Suí classical learning — he stands at the boundary between Northern Dynasties and early-Táng exegesis. His Wing-by-Wing *yì* essays on the canonical classics were a principal underlying source for the Táng *Wǔ jīng zhèngyì* 五經正義 commission of [[孔穎達]] Kǒng Yǐngdá; the *jiān yì* 兼義 ("supplementary meaning") marginal heading at the openings of the *Zhèngyì* *juàn*-divisions is taken by the Sìkù *tiyao* of [[KR1a0007]] to reflect this older-essay layer that the Táng commission absorbed. *Suíshū* (juan 75) and *Běi shǐ* 北史 (juan 82) give his biography.
