Lǐ Hàn 李瀚 (fl. mid-8th c.) is the Táng author of the *Méng-qiú* 蒙求 ([[KR3k0010]]), the foundational rhymed primer of historical *gùshí* presented to the throne in Tiān-bǎo 天寶 5 (746) according to Hú Sān-xǐng's note on the *Zī-zhì tōng-jiàn*. No biography survives in either of the Two Táng Histories, and the CBDB record (id 185588, d. 802) probably refers to a Táng homonym rather than to the *Méng-qiú* author, who was, according to the *Tài-píng guǎng-jì* 太平廣記 colophon, a *Yīn-cí Hé-zhōng* 隂祠河中 official of the Tiān-bǎo period. The work was for over a millennium the standard East Asian primer of historical knowledge, and was glossed in Sòng China by Xú Zǐ-guāng (the surviving *Méng-qiú jí-zhù*) and independently in Heian Japan by Minamoto no Mitsuyuki. Multiple CBDB ids for "Lǐ Hàn" exist; none with confidence matches this figure.
