Lǐ Hàn 李瀚 (fl. mid-8th c.) is the Táng author of the *Méngqiú* 蒙求 ([[KR3k0010]]), the foundational rhymed primer of historical *gùshí* presented to the throne in Tiānbǎo 天寶 5 (746) according to Hú Sānxǐng's note on the *Zīzhì tōngjiàn*. No biography survives in either of the Two Táng Histories, and the CBDB record (id 185588, d. 802) probably refers to a Táng homonym rather than to the *Méngqiú* author, who was, according to the *Tàipíng guǎngjì* 太平廣記 colophon, a *Yīncí Hézhōng* 隂祠河中 official of the Tiānbǎo period. The work was for over a millennium the standard East Asian primer of historical knowledge, and was glossed in Sòng China by Xú Zǐguāng (the surviving *Méngqiú jízhù*) and independently in Heian Japan by Minamoto no Mitsuyuki.
