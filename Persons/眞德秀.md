Zhēn Déxiù 眞德秀 (1178–1235), *zì* originally Jǐngyuán 景元, later Xīyuán 希元, *hào* Xīshān xiānsheng 西山先生, posthumously *Wénzhōng* 文忠, was the leading mid-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* statesman and the principal channel of the ZhūXī tradition's institutional consolidation in the post-*Qìngyuán dǎngjìn* generation. Native of Pǔchéng 浦城 (Fújiàn). *Jìnshì* of Qìngyuán 5 (1199); *bóxué hóngcí* in 1205. Held a sequence of senior court and provincial posts, rising under Lǐzōng 理宗 to *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 in 1235, the year of his death. Personal disciple of Zhān Tǐrén 詹體仁 (a direct Zhū Xī student); his learning is therefore second-generation post-Zhū. With Wèi Liǎowēng 魏了翁, he is one of the *ÈrWèi* of the post-*dǎngjìn* recovery. His major works include the *Dàxué yǎnyì* 大學衍義 ([[KR3a0058]] — the master imperial-pedagogical *Dàxué* commentary, in 43 juan), the *Xīshān dúshū jì* 西山讀書記 ([[KR3a0059]], his comprehensive *Lǐxué* notebook in 40 juan), the *Xīn jīng* 心經 ([[KR3a0060]], 1 juan, the foundational *xīnxìng* compilation), the *Zhèng jīng* 政經 ([[KR3a0061]], 1 juan, on government), the *Wénzhāng zhèng zōng* 文章正宗 (the canonical literary anthology), and the substantial *Xīshān wénjí* 西山文集. The *Dàxué yǎnyì* in particular became the standard imperial-instructional text for the YuánMíngQīng courts. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 437 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 10258, dates 1178–1235.

(Note: the simplified-character form 真 and the variant 眞 — both used for the personal name — are interchangeable; the catalog meta and SKQS prefer 眞.)
