Zhēn Déxiù 眞德秀 (1178–1235), *zì* originally Jǐngyuán 景元, later Xīyuán 希元, *hào* Xīshān xiānsheng 西山先生, posthumously *Wénzhōng* 文忠, was the leading mid-Southern-Sòng *Lǐxué* statesman and the principal channel of the Zhū-Xī tradition's institutional consolidation in the post-*Qìng-yuán dǎng-jìn* generation. Native of Pǔ-chéng 浦城 (Fú-jiàn). *Jìnshì* of Qìng-yuán 5 (1199); *bó-xué hóng-cí* in 1205. Held a sequence of senior court and provincial posts, rising under Lǐzōng 理宗 to *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* 參知政事 in 1235, the year of his death. Personal disciple of Zhān Tǐ-rén 詹體仁 (a direct Zhū Xī student); his learning is therefore second-generation post-Zhū. With Wèi Liǎo-wēng 魏了翁, he is one of the *Èr-Wèi* of the post-*dǎng-jìn* recovery. His major works include the *Dà-xué yǎn-yì* 大學衍義 (KR3a0058 — the master imperial-pedagogical *Dà-xué* commentary, in 43 juan), the *Xī-shān dú-shū jì* 西山讀書記 (KR3a0059, his comprehensive *Lǐxué* notebook in 40 juan), the *Xīn jīng* 心經 (KR3a0060, 1 juan, the foundational *xīn-xìng* compilation), the *Zhèng jīng* 政經 (KR3a0061, 1 juan, on government), the *Wén-zhāng zhèng zōng* 文章正宗 (the canonical literary anthology), and the substantial *Xī-shān wén-jí* 西山文集. The *Dà-xué yǎn-yì* in particular became the standard imperial-instructional text for the Yuán-Míng-Qīng courts. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 437 (Rú-lín zhuàn). CBDB id 10258, dates 1178–1235.

(Note: the simplified-character form 真 and the variant 眞 — both used for the personal name — are interchangeable; the catalog meta and SKQS prefer 眞.)
