# Jìng-xiàn 淨現 (Jí-niàn Jìng-xiàn 即念淨現)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáo-dòng Chán master of the 淨-generation (grand-dharma-heir of **Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng** 湛然圓澄 through one of the 明-generation heirs). *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Jìng-xiàn** 淨現, *hào* **Jí-niàn** 即念 ("At-Thought"). Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates not recoverable from the accessible sources; active at **Sì-míng Xiàng-tián** 四明象田 (in the Níng-bō mountain cluster) from at least **崇禎己卯** (Chóng-zhēn 12 / 1639) winter onwards, succeeding **Dà-běn lǎo-shī** 大本老師 who had held the seat for twenty-plus years previously. Held Xiàng-tián as a hermitage-seat (*ān-zhǔ* 菴主) rather than a formal public *zhǔ-chí* 住持, as evidenced by the absence of *shàng-táng* 上堂 sermons from his *yǔlù*.

Work: [[KR6q0412]] 《象田即念禪師語錄》 (4 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J27 B191, compiled by the *shū-jì* [[淨癡]] 淨癡 and edited by the *mén-rén* [[本致]] 本致 — to be created if not yet present). The *yǔlù* carries four prefatorial pieces preserved in calligraphy-plate form (`<img:>`) in the Kanripo transcription; the textual content of these prefaces is not machine-readable. No *xíng-zhuàng*, no *tǎ-míng*.

Dharma-lineage connection: the 淨-generation places Jí-niàn alongside [[淨範]] Jìng-fàn (head heir of [[明盂|三宜明盂]]) and **Qí Jìng-chāo** 祁淨超 ([[KR6q0411]] collation-editor). His specific 明-generation teacher is not recoverable from the transcribed portions of the *yǔlù*; possible teachers include one of the lesser-documented Zhàn-ran heirs (**Mì-fú** 密澓, **Kōng-huá** 空華, or a sub-branch master).

Sources: [[KR6q0412]] — juan 1 opening sermon (dated 崇禎己卯 = 1639 winter); juan 4 *Xiàng-tián yǒng* 象田詠 for the toponymic identification; juan 1 passage on the predecessor **Dà-běn**.
