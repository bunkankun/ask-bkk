# Jìngxiàn 淨現 (Jíniàn Jìngxiàn 即念淨現)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng Chán master of the 淨-generation (grand-dharma-heir of **Zhànrán Yuánchéng** 湛然圓澄 through one of the 明-generation heirs). *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Jìngxiàn** 淨現, *hào* **Jíniàn** 即念 ("At-Thought"). Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates not recoverable from the accessible sources; active at **Sìmíng Xiàngtián** 四明象田 (in the Níngbō mountain cluster) from at least **崇禎己卯** (Chóngzhēn 12 / 1639) winter onwards, succeeding **Dàběn lǎoshī** 大本老師 who had held the seat for twenty-plus years previously. Held Xiàngtián as a hermitage-seat (*ānzhǔ* 菴主) rather than a formal public *zhǔchí* 住持, as evidenced by the absence of *shàngtáng* 上堂 sermons from his *yǔlù*.

Work: [[KR6q0412]] 《象田即念禪師語錄》 (4 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J27 B191, compiled by the *shūjì* [[淨癡]] 淨癡 and edited by the *ménrén* [[本致]] 本致 — to be created if not yet present). The *yǔlù* carries four prefatorial pieces preserved in calligraphy-plate form (`<img:>`) in the Kanripo transcription; the textual content of these prefaces is not machine-readable. No *xíngzhuàng*, no *tǎmíng*.

Dharma-lineage connection: the 淨-generation places Jíniàn alongside [[淨範]] Jìngfàn (head heir of [[明盂|三宜明盂]]) and **Qí Jìngchāo** 祁淨超 ([[KR6q0411]] collation-editor). His specific 明-generation teacher is not recoverable from the transcribed portions of the *yǔlù*; possible teachers include one of the lesser-documented Zhàn-ran heirs (**Mìfú** 密澓, **Kōnghuá** 空華, or a sub-branch master).

Sources: [[KR6q0412]] — juan 1 opening sermon (dated 崇禎己卯 = 1639 winter); juan 4 *Xiàngtián yǒng* 象田詠 for the toponymic identification; juan 1 passage on the predecessor **Dàběn**.
