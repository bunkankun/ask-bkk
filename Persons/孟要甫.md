**Mèng Yàofǔ** 孟要甫 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist, compiler of [[KR5c0318]] *Zhū jiā shén pǐn dān fǎ* 諸家神品丹法 ("Divine-Grade Elixir Methods from Various Authorities") — a 6-*juàn* alchemical encyclopedia dated 1163 (Lóng xīng 1).
