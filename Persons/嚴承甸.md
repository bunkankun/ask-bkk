Late-Qīng official of Mǎpíng 馬平 (modern Liǔzhōu 柳州, Guǎngxī), posted at Wǔgāng 武岡 (Húnán) in *bǐngwǔ* 丙午 (1906–7). After both he and his wife survived *báihóu* by means of formulae from the [[張紹修]] / [[黃炳乾]] tradition (the *Shéngōng bìxié sǎn* 神功辟邪散 and *Shénxiān huómìng dān* 神仙活命丹), he compiled the two earlier tracts together with his own foreword, dated late-spring *Guāngxù* 33 (1907), as [[KR3em031]] *Báihóu jiéyào hébiān*, sponsoring its printing for free local circulation. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
