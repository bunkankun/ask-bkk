Late-Qīng official of Mǎ-píng 馬平 (modern Liǔ-zhōu 柳州, Guǎngxī), posted at Wǔ-gāng 武岡 (Húnán) in *bǐng-wǔ* 丙午 (1906–7). After both he and his wife survived *bái-hóu* by means of formulae from the [[張紹修]] / [[黃炳乾]] tradition (the *Shén-gōng bì-xié sǎn* 神功辟邪散 and *Shén-xiān huó-mìng dān* 神仙活命丹), he compiled the two earlier tracts together with his own foreword, dated late-spring *Guāng-xù* 33 (1907), as [[KR3em031]] *Bái-hóu jié-yào hé-biān*, sponsoring its printing for free local circulation. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
