Late-Míng Vinaya master **Rúxīn** 如馨 (1541–1616), *zì* **Gǔxīn** 古心 ("Ancient Mind"), posthumous title **Huìyún lǜshī** 慧雲律師 ("Vinaya-master Wisdom-Cloud", conferred by the Wànlì 萬曆 emperor). The single most important figure in the late-Míng *zhōngxìng lǜzōng* 中興律宗 ("revival of the Vinaya school") movement, traditionally honoured with the title *Zhōngxìng lǜzǔ* 中興律祖 ("Patriarch of the Vinaya Revival"). Born *Jiājìng xīnchǒu* 嘉靖辛丑 6th month 10th day (= 13 July 1541) at *Lìyáng* 溧陽 (modern Jiāngsū), lay surname Yáng 楊. Dharma-heir of *Dàofú Rúhuàn lǜshī* 道孚如幻律師.

Rú-xīn was tonsured during the *Jiā-jìng* reign by *Sù-ān Zhēn-jié* 素菴真節 of *Qī-xiá-shān* 棲霞山 (modern Nánjīng) and undertook the long pilgrimage to *Wǔ-tái-shān* 五台山 to study Vinaya at the *Mañjuśrī* sacred mountain. Returning to Jiāngnán, he built the *Mǎ-ān-shān Gǔ-lín-ān* 馬鞍山古林庵 — afterwards expanded as the *Gǔ-lín lǜ-sì* 古林律寺 in Jīnlíng (Nánjīng) — as the *弘律道場* ("the dharma-platform for the spread of Vinaya") of his school. From this base he reformed the practice of *Vinaya* in late-Míng Buddhism, holding successive abbacies at *Líng-gǔ-sì* 靈谷寺, *Qī-xiá-sì* 棲霞寺, *Gān-lù-sì* 甘露寺, *Líng-yǐn-sì* 靈隱寺, and *Tiān-níng-sì* 天寧寺.

Rúxīn died on *Wànlì yǐmǎo* 萬曆乙卯 11th month 14th day (= 2 January 1616), aged 75 (*sēnglà* 27). His direct dharma-heirs include eleven *lǜshī*, of whom the most consequential were:
- **Sānmèi Jìguāng** 三昧寂光 (DILA A001028) — through whom the *Bǎohuáshān* 寶華山 *Lóngchāngsì* 隆昌寺 Vinaya school descended, the principal late-Míng / Qīng *Vinaya* lineage and source of the modern *jiètán* 戒壇 ordination tradition;
- **Liánzōng Xìngxiàng** 蓮宗性相 (DILA A042318);
- **Dàhuì Yǒnghǎi** 大會永海 (cf. [[KR6k0075]]);
- **Màolín Xìngzhī** 茂林性祇 (the [[性祇]] of [[KR6k0258]] *Píní rìyòng lù*).

Rú-xīn's principal recorded composition is the **Jīng-lǜ jiè-xiàng bù-sà guǐ-yí** 經律戒相布薩軌儀 ([[KR6k0261]], X1136, 1 *juàn*) — the late-Míng standard short-form combined precept-conferral and *poṣadha* manual.
