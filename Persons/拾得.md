# Shídé 拾得

Táng-era Chán eccentric and poet, traditional companion of [[寒山|Hánshān]] 寒山 and [[豐干|Fēnggān]] 豐干 at Mount Tiāntái 天臺. The three are paired in Chinese Buddhist iconography and literary tradition as the "Three Sages of Tiāntái" (*Tiāntái sān shèng* 天台三聖). His verses survive embedded in the *Hánshānzǐ shī jí* 寒山子詩集 and parallel collections. Lifedates uncertain.
