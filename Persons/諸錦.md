# Zhū Jǐn 諸錦

*Zì* Xiāngqī 襄七, *hào* Cǎolú 草廬 (also 孚文 Fúwén); studio name Jiàngfū-gé 絳跗閣 ("Crimson-Calyx Pavilion"). Native of Xiùshuǐ 秀水 (Jiāxīng prefecture, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng 2 (1724); selected *shùjíshì* 庶吉士; after dismissal from the Hanlin Academy he served briefly as a county magistrate. Summoned to the *bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 special examination of Qiánlóng 1 (1736) and passed; made Hanlin *biānxiū* 編修 and subsequently *zuǎnxiū* 纂修 on the Sānlǐ-guǎn 三禮館 (Bureau of the Three Ritual Classics).

Served as chief examiner for the Fújiàn provincial examination (1741), deputy examiner in Shānxī (1747), and chief examiner in Guìzhōu (1750), retiring at the rank of *zuǒ zànshàn* 左贊善 of the Left Spring Office. Enrolled in the shrine of local worthies of Xiùshuǐ in Jiāqìng 23 (1818).

Surviving works: *Bǔ xiǎnglǐ* 補饗禮 ([[KR1d0043]], 1 juan, WYG; originally titled *Xiǎnglǐ bǔwáng* 饗禮補亡), *Máoshī shuō* 毛詩說, *Xià xiǎo zhèng gǔ* 夏小正詁 (also titled *Xià xiǎo zhèng zhù* 夏小正注), and the poetry collection *Jiàngfū-gé shīgǎo* 絳跗閣詩稿.

Caution: his studio name *Jiàngfū-gé* is sometimes confused with *Yíshān-táng* 飴山堂 (the studio of Zhào Zhíxìn 趙執信) in unreliable biographical compilations; the two are unrelated.
