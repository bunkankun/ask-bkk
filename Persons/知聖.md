# Zhīshèng chánshī 知聖禪師

Abbot of Língshù 靈樹 in the early tenth century; transmitted the Língshù abbacy to [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃 via the famous secret-letter episode preserved in the *xínglù* of [[KR6q0073]].
