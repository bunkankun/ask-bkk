Early Republican-era physician of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu). Carried out the textual proofreading-and-transcription (*jiàolù* 校錄) for the *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 edition (c. 1925) of [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhīlán's [[KR3er103]] *Yījīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (1662), working in collaboration with [[高思潛]] Gāo Sīqián of Héxiàn (whose *kǎo* established the proper authorship attribution). Best known by his *zì* Shūpéng 叔鵬.
