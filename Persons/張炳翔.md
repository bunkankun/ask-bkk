Early Republican-era physician of Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu). Carried out the textual proofreading-and-transcription (*jiào-lù* 校錄) for the *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 edition (c. 1925) of [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhī-lán's [[KR3er103]] *Yī-jīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (1662), working in collaboration with [[高思潛]] Gāo Sī-qián of Hé-xiàn (whose *kǎo* established the proper authorship attribution). Best known by his *zì* Shū-péng 叔鵬.
