**Mèng-shì** 孟氏 ("Mr. Mèng" — given name not preserved), conventionally placed in the Liáng 梁 dynasty (502–557), is the most obscure of the eleven traditional commentators on the *Sūn-zǐ bīng-fǎ* 孫子兵法. His annotations were transmitted as one of the strands gathered in the late Northern-Sòng 《十一家注孫子》 (*Shí-yī jiā zhù Sūn-zǐ*) edition compiled by **Jí Tiān-bǎo** 吉天保 in the late 11th c., alongside those of Cáo Cāo 曹操, Lǐ Quán 李筌, Jiǎ Lín 賈林, Dù Mù 杜牧, Méi Yáo-chén 梅堯臣, Wáng Xī 王晳, Zhāng Yù 張預, et al. No personal name, native place, or office is preserved in any reliable source; the Liáng-dynasty attribution rests solely on the conventional ordering used in the *Shí-yī jiā* compilation. CBDB has two entries for "孟氏" without dates (c_personid 93945 and 163674), neither confidently identifiable with the *Sūn-zǐ* commentator. He is the attributed author of the commentary preserved in [[KR3b0022]] *Shí-yī jiā zhù Sūn-zǐ (Mèng-shì zhù)*.
