**Zhāng Zhìyuǎn** 張致遠 (*fl.* mid-late 13th c.). Late-Southern-Sòng official posted to Guǎngxī. Co-author with [[李璆]] of the *Lǐngnán wèishēng fāng* 嶺南衛生方 ([[KR3ed031]]). His historical record outside this work is sparse; not in CBDB. Sòng prefectural records mention a Zhāng Zhìyuǎn as official at the *Guǎngxī xuānfǔshǐsī* 廣西宣撫使司 in the late Sòng, consistent with the *Lǐngnán wèishēng fāng* attribution.
