Xiāo Zhuó-rú 蕭琢如, *zì* Bó-zhāng 伯章, *hào* Dùn-yuán 遯園 ("the Retreat Garden"), 1860–1932, native of Xiāng-xiāng 湘鄉 (Húnán). A late-Qīng / Republican Húnán scholar-official-physician who held official posts in Húnán mining (his 1922 preface to [[KR3ep068]] *Dùn-yuán yī-àn* was written at the Shuǐ-kǒu-shān 水口山 Mining Bureau) and educational administration while maintaining a parallel clinical practice. Studied medicine under his cross-cousin Hòu-shēng Péng xiānshēng 厚生彭先生. He is a principal twentieth-century *Shāng-hán* classicist in the Húnán medical tradition, with a strongly *liù-jīng* (six-channel) doctrinal orientation and unusually robust use of *Fù-zǐ* and *Gān-jiāng* relative to contemporary Jiāng-Zhè-school physicians. His clinical rhetorical signature was the *dictum* "仲景而後無完醫" ("after Zhòng-jǐng there has been no complete physician"). Not in CBDB.
