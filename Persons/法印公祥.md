Fǎyìn Gōngxiáng 法印公祥 / Hōin Kōshō (b. c. 1717) — Edo-period Japanese monk and copyist who in Meiwa 6 (1769) produced the clean-copy redaction of Kaiken's *Zhōngguān lùn èrshíqī pǐn biéshì*.
