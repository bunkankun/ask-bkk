Màntuóluó-xiān 曼陀羅仙 (\*Mandrasena / *Mandāra*; lifedates unknown), Liáng-dynasty Buddhist translator from Funán 扶南 (modern Cambodia / lower-Mekong region). According to the *Xù gāosēng zhuàn* 續高僧傳 (T2060) and the *Lìdài sānbǎo jì*, Mandrasena arrived at Liáng Wǔ-dì 武帝's court at Jiànkāng in Tiān-jiān 天監 2 = 503 CE and produced several Mahāyāna translations together with [[僧伽婆羅|Saṃghapāla]], including [[KR6c0020|*Wén-shū-shī-lì suǒ-shuō Móhē bōrě bōluómì jīng* (T232)]] and others. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001050.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6c0020]] *Wénshūshīlì suǒshuō Móhē bōrě bōluómì jīng* (T232); and others.
