Jiāng Chéng-zhī 姜成芝 — mid-Qīng compiler-physician of the Jiāng-yīn 江陰 region (Jiāngsū), about whom little is known beyond his editorial role in the [[KR3ep037]] *Lóng-shā bā-jiā yī-àn* 龍砂八家醫案 — the principal documentary source for the Lóng-shā 龍砂 medical lineage of Jiāng-yīn. The Jiāng family is itself one of the constituent families of the Lóng-shā tradition (compare Jiāng Lì-zhāi 姜禮齋 in the canonical eight-master roster), suggesting that the compiler was a member of the same family.
