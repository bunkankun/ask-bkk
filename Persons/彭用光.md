**Péng Yòngguāng** 彭用光 (*fl.* mid-late Míng), paediatrician of unknown native place and lifedates. Author of *Yuányòu xīnfǎ* 原幼心法 ([[KR3ej031]]). The work cites *Quányīng* 全嬰 (Lǔ Bósì's *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問) and the conventional Sòng-Yuán paediatric corpus, suggesting a mid-to-late Míng date (16th–17th c.). The *yòngguāng àn* 用光按 (editorial-comment) form distinctive of the work indicates an annotative compilation style. No CBDB record; no other biographical evidence.
