# Wéi-dào 唯道

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[慧性]] Wúmíng Huìxìng (1162–1237); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0312]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A001016).
