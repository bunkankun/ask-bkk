# Zhì-héng 智恒

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[喜|Yún-é Xǐ]] 雲峨喜 in the **Pò-shān / Lín-yě double-transmission** wing of the Mìyún-line. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0422]] 《雲峨喜禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B203). The 智-generation character is non-standard for the Mìyún lineage-poem (which runs 通 → 行 → 性/本 → ...); Zhì-héng's naming likely reflects a Pò-shān sub-line regional-poem variant or a personal rather than generation-based *fǎ-huì*. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0422]] juan-head signature line "*sì-fǎ mén-rén Zhì-héng děng biān* 嗣法門人智恒等編".
