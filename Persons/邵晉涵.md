# Shào Jìnhán 邵晉涵

*Zì* Èryún 二雲; *hào* Nánjiāng 南江; posthumous title Wénxiàn 文獻 (a rare distinction for a non-chief-minister). Native of Yúyáo 餘姚 (Zhèjiāng), of the Yúyáo Shào lineage that also produced the great early-Qing scholar Shào Tíngcǎi 邵廷采. *Jìnshì* of *Qiánlóng* 36 (1771). CBDB id 65847; lifedates 1742–1796.

A leading mid-Qiánlóng *kǎozhèng* scholar and one of the principal Sìkù editors. Held *Hànlín shìjiǎng* 翰林侍講, *Hànlín shìdú* 翰林侍讀, and other senior Hànlín posts. As a *Sìkù* editorial-team member, his most consequential project was the reconstitution of the *Jiù Wǔdài shǐ* ([[KR2a0029]]) — extracted from citations in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* 永樂大典 and supplemented from Sòng *lèishū* and *bǐjì* — in *Qiánlóng* 38–39 (1773–1774). The recovery of about 80–90% of the original 150-*juǎn* text from rhyme-character extracts is one of the most celebrated and successful textual reconstitution projects in Chinese scholarship.

His other major scholarly works: the *Ěryǎ zhèngyì* 爾雅正義 (20 *juǎn*, 1788) — a major Qing critical commentary on the *Ěryǎ*, the foundational Confucian *xiǎoxué* lexicon; the *Nánjiāng cǎo táng wén jí* 南江草堂文集 (his collected prose); the *Nánjiāng zhái jí* 南江札記 (a *kǎozhèng*-style miscellany).

His biography is in *Qīng shǐ gǎo* 481 ([[KR2a0039]]) under the *Rúlín* category.
