Mid-Míng official, native of **Lúlíng** 廬陵 (Jiāngxī), active in the Zhèngtǒng 正統 reign (1436–1449). Co-editor with **Liú Jié** [[劉傑]] of the *Wáng Zhōngwén jí* 王忠文集 [[KR4e0010]] — Liú Tóng editing *juǎn* 13–24 of the collection while Liú Jié edited *juǎn* 1–12, with each collating the other's portion. Otherwise obscure in standard biographies. No confident CBDB match.
