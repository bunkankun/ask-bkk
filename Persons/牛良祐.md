# 牛良祐 Niú Liángyòu

**Niú Liángyòu** 牛良祐, Yuán-dynasty disciple of [[竇傑]] Dòu Jié (Dòu Hànqīng); prepared and printed his teacher's *Zhēn jīng zhǐnán* 針經指南 ([[KR3ee017]]) in Yuánzhēn 1 (1295).
