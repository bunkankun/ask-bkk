Xìngmíng 性明, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Zǐsù Chāoyuǎn** [[超遠]] 子肅超遠 (1631–1689) at **Zhōngnánshān Pánlóngsì** 終南山蟠龍寺. Lead compiler of the 1-juan 《終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0521]]) with co-recorder **Hǎikuò** [[海闊]]. No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
