Xìng-míng 性明, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Zǐ-sù Chāo-yuǎn** [[超遠]] 子肅超遠 (1631–1689) at **Zhōng-nán-shān Pán-lóng-sì** 終南山蟠龍寺. Lead compiler of the 1-juan 《終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0521]]) with co-recorder **Hǎi-kuò** [[海闊]]. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
