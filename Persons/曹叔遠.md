# Cáo Shūyuǎn 曹叔遠 (Sòng, fl. 1208)

A *Chéngzhíláng Tàixué bóshì* (Honorary Court-Adviser and *Tàixué* Professor) of the early *Jiādìng* era. Disciple of Chén Fùliáng [[陳傅良]]. In *Jiādìng wùchén* (1208) he edited his master's collection [[KR4d0233]] *Zhǐzhāi xiānshēng wénjí*, providing a substantial preface that lays out Chén's intellectual development in five chronologically-fixed stages and that is the principal contemporary biographical document for Chén. Otherwise no substantial biographical record.
