# Cáo Shūyuǎn 曹叔遠 (Sòng, fl. 1208)

A *Chéng-zhí-láng Tài-xué bó-shì* (Honorary Court-Adviser and *Tài-xué* Professor) of the early *Jiā-dìng* era. Disciple of Chén Fù-liáng [[陳傅良]]. In *Jiā-dìng wù-chén* (1208) he edited his master's collection [[KR4d0233]] *Zhǐ-zhāi xiān-shēng wén-jí*, providing a substantial preface that lays out Chén's intellectual development in five chronologically-fixed stages and that is the principal contemporary biographical document for Chén. Otherwise no substantial biographical record.
