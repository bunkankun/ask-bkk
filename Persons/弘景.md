Early-Táng Buddhist monk, expert in the Tiāntái 天台 and *Lǜ* 律 (Vinaya) traditions. Native of *Xiāng-yáng* 襄陽 (modern Húběi). Studied under *Zhāng-ān Guàn-dǐng* 章安灌頂 (561–632) — the disciple of *Zhìyǐ* 智顗 and the principal compiler of the *Tiāntái* corpus. As an authoritative figure of his generation, Hóngjǐng was selected by the Wǔ-Zhōu court as the chief drafter of the *Jìn xīnyì Dà-fāng-guǎng-fó huāyán-jīng biǎo* 進新譯大方廣佛花嚴經表 [[KR6v0068]] — the formal memorial submitted to *Wǔ Zétiān* 武則天 in *Shèng-lì* 2 (699 CE) on the occasion of *Śikṣānanda* 實叉難陀 completing the new (eighty-fascicle) translation of the *Avataṃsaka-sūtra* (T10n0279). Distinct from the famous Daoist *Táo Hóngjǐng* 陶弘景 (456–536) of the *Shàngqīng* 上清 lineage.
