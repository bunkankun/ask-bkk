Wú Xiāng 吳襄 (1661–1735; CBDB id 58942), *zì* Qī-yún 七雲, *hào* Píng-wēng 平翁, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was a Kāngxī-Yōngzhèng court official. Native of Qián-shān 潛山 (Ān-huī). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 52 (1713); served in the Hàn-lín-yuàn and rose to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*. He was a prominent imperial-compilation editor of the Yōngzhèng era: *zuǎn-xiū* of the *Pián zì lèi-biān* 駢字類編 ([[KR3k0056]]), the *Fēn lèi zì jǐn* 分類字錦 ([[KR3k0057]]), and the *Zǐ shǐ jīng-huá* 子史精華 ([[KR3k0058]]).

Note: there are multiple individuals named 吳襄 in CBDB. This entry covers the Qián-shān *Wénjiǎn* (1661–1735), distinguished from the late-Míng/early-Qīng military commander 吳襄 (father of Wú Sān-guì 吳三桂) and other homonyms by the lifespan and the Kāngxī-Yōngzhèng court compilation involvement.
