Wú Xiāng 吳襄 (1661–1735; CBDB id 58942), *zì* Qīyún 七雲, *hào* Píngwēng 平翁, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was a Kāngxī Yōngzhèng court official. Native of Qiánshān 潛山 (Ānhuī). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 52 (1713); served in the Hànlínyuàn and rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū*. He was a prominent imperial-compilation editor of the Yōngzhèng era: *zuǎnxiū* of the *Pián zì lèibiān* 駢字類編 ([[KR3k0056]]), the *Fēn lèi zì jǐn* 分類字錦 ([[KR3k0057]]), and the *Zǐ shǐ jīnghuá* 子史精華 ([[KR3k0058]]).

Note: there are multiple individuals named 吳襄 in CBDB. This entry covers the Qiánshān *Wénjiǎn* (1661–1735), distinguished from the late-Míng/early-Qīng military commander 吳襄 (father of Wú Sānguì 吳三桂) and other homonyms by the lifespan and the Kāngxī Yōngzhèng court compilation involvement.
