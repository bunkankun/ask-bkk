# 沈目南

Shěn Mùnán (*zì* Zìnán 自南), eighteenth-century Qīng *wēnbìng* physician credited with one of the early systematic formulations of the *qiūzào* 秋燥 (autumn-dryness) doctrine, invoked in [[KR3em020]] *Báihóu tiáobiàn* 白喉條辨 as a doctrinal precursor.
