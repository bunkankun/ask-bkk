**Wāng Qǐshèng** 汪啟聖 (*fl.* mid-to-late Qīng), one of the two co-authors of the *Dònggōng ànmó mìjué* 動功按摩秘訣 ([[KR3eo035]]). The matching style of his name with co-author Wāng Qǐxián 汪啟賢 (both with the *Qǐ* 啟 element) suggests the two were brothers from a single family medical lineage. No biographical data recoverable. No CBDB record.
