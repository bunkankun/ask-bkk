**Zōu Cún-gàn** 鄒存淦 (*hào* Lì-shēng 儷笙, *fl.* 1870s, late Qīng / Guāng-xù era), late-Qīng physician-scholar specialising in *wài-zhì* (external-application therapy). Compiler of the *Wàizhì shòushì fāng* 外治壽世方 ([[KR3ed118]]) in 4 *juǎn* (the *chū-biān* / first compilation), printed in 1877 with the assistance of his colleague [[胡增彬]] (Hú Zēng-bīn) of Xīn-ān, who served as collator and publisher. The work is the principal late-Qīng compendium devoted to non-oral therapies — poultices, plasters, fumigations, hot-compresses, steam-baths — and aimed to recover *wài-zhì* methodology from neglect in the prevailing oral-decoction clinical mode. Sparse further biographical record.
