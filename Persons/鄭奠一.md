**Zhèng Diànyī** 鄭奠一 (early-to-mid Qiánlóng era, 清), Shèxiàn 歙縣 figure under whose name an early-Qiánlóng pirated edition of [[戴天章]] Dài Tiānzhāng's *Guǎng wēnyì lùn* ([[KR3eg023]]) circulated under the false title *Wēnyì míngbiàn* 瘟疫明辨. The pirated edition was corrected by Dài's grandson [[戴祖啟]] Dài Zǔqǐ in Qiánlóng 43 (1778).
