Guī Zhuāng 歸莊 (1613–1673), *zì* Xuángōng 玄恭, *hào* Héngxuān 恆軒 (also Pǔmíng tóutuó 普明頭陀 in late-life Buddhist retreat), of Kūnshān 昆山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Grand-nephew of **Guī Yǒuguāng** 歸有光. A late-Míng / early-Qīng *yímín* (loyalist) figure: with **Gù Yánwǔ** 顧炎武 he organized resistance to the Manchu conquest at Kūnshān; after the cities fell he lived in *yímín* retreat in Sūzhōu, supporting himself by selling calligraphy and painting. He is the principal compiler of the modern recension of **Guī Yǒuguāng**'s *Zhènchuān jí* [[KR4e0215]]: he drafted the *fánlì* (editorial principles) and gathered surviving manuscript fragments (with help from **Qián Qiānyì**) after both family cuttings were lost in the conquest. The Kāngxī 1675 consolidated edition was completed by his nephew **Guī Xuángōng**. CBDB 65939.
