# 天親

*Vasubandhu* (Tiānqīn; also 世親 Shìqīn in later translations), c. 4th–5th century CE.

The younger half-brother of **Asaṅga** 無著 and one of the two founding figures of Indian **Yogācāra** scholasticism. Author of the *Abhidharmakośa* (in his pre-Mahāyāna phase) and, after his Yogācāra conversion under Asaṅga, of the *Triṃśikāvijñaptimātratāsiddhi* (*Sānshí sòng* 三十頌), the *Viṃśatikā*, the *Mahāyānasaṃgraha-bhāṣya*, and the prose **Vajracchedikā vyākhyā** translated into Chinese by Bodhiruci as T1511 (the principal Indian commentary on the Diamond Sūtra used by Chinese Yogācāra exegetes).
