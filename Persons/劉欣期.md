Liú Xīnqī 劉欣期 (*fl.* Eastern Jin dynasty, late 4th century CE) was a geographic writer, author of a *Jiāozhōu jì* 交州記 ([[KR2k0196]]), a record of the Jiaozhou region (modern northern Vietnam and southern Guangdong/Guangxi). The *Suí shū · Jīngjí zhì* lists his work in one *juàn*. His account of the natural products and peoples of the far south is one of the most vivid early medieval records of tropical South and Southeast Asia from a Chinese perspective. No further biographical details are attested.
