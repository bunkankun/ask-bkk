Xióng Liángfǔ 熊良輔, zì Rènzhòng 任重, hào Méibiān 梅邊, was a Yuán-dynasty *Yìjīng* scholar from Nánchāng 南昌. According to his own preface to the *Zhōuyì běnyì jíchéng* 周易本義集成 ([[KR1a0079]]), he studied with Xióng Kǎi 熊凱 (Yáoxī xiānsheng 遥溪先生) of his own district in his youth and later, in the *dàdé rényín* 大德壬寅 year (1302), refined his learning under Gōng Quánfēng 龔泉峰 in the foothills of Mount Quán 泉山. He passed the provincial examination (xiānggòng 鄉貢) in the *yánvyòu* 延祐 fourth year (1317, *dīngsì* 丁巳), under the second iteration of the Yuán's revived examination system; his subsequent official career is not securely documented. The catalog conflation by Huáng Yújì 黃虞稷 in the *Qiānqǐng táng shūmù* 千頃堂書目 — which attributed an *Yì zhuàn jí shū* 易傳集疏 to him — is corrected by the *Sìkù* editors, who identify that work as Xióng Kǎi's; the *Sìkù* notice is the principal source for what is known of him.

The *Jíchéng* preface is dated *zhìzhì rénxū* 至治壬戌 (1322), giving a fixed terminus a quo for that work, but it indicates the manuscript was substantially complete by *zhìdà xīnhài* 至大辛亥 (1311) and only printed later when his colleagues underwrote the cost.
