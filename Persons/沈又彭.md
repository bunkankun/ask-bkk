**Shěn Yòu-péng** 沈又彭 (*fl.* 1750–1780, 清), *zì* Yáo-fēng 堯封, was a mid-Qiánlóng physician of Pínghú 平湖 (Zhèjiāng) who issued three coordinated medical works in 1764: *Shānghán lùn dú běn* 傷寒論讀本 (a re-ordered *Shānghán lùn* base text with brief notes), *Yī jīng dú* 醫經讀 ([[KR3ea043]], a topically-organized abridgment of the *Sùwèn* and *Língshū*), and *Nǚ kē jí yào* 女科輯要 (gynaecological reference). His clinical-pragmatic orientation, with open scepticism of the *yùnqì* cosmology, aligns with the mid-Qiánlóng Sūzhōu / Pínghú milieu surrounding Xuē Xuě [[薛雪]] and Yè Tiānshì 葉天士. No transmitted biographical record beyond these prefaces.
