王隱, styled 處叔, was an Eastern Jin historian from 陳郡陳縣. He compiled the *Jìn shū* 晉書 in 93 juǎn, a history of the Western Jin dynasty, working under the patronage of the powerful regent 庾亮. His history was reportedly plagiarized by his contemporary 虞預.
