Lǐ Rúguī 李如圭 (Southern Sòng, fl. Shàoxī era ca. 1190s; CBDB id 10620 has *fl. earliest* 1193). *Zì* Bǎozhī 寳之. Native of Lúlíng 廬陵 (modern Jí'ān, Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of Shàoxī 4 (1193) — corrected here from Chén Zhènsūn's incorrect "Chúnxī *guǐchǒu*" (which corresponds to no actual year, since the Chúnxī era 1174–1189 contains no *guǐchǒu*; the Sìkù *tíyào* of [[KR1d0030]] reasons that "Shàoxī *guǐchǒu* = 1193" is the intended date and that Chén made a one-character transcription error). Career rose to *Fújiàn lù fǔgān* 福建路撫幹 (Pacification-Office Officer of Fújiàn Circuit).

A friend of Zhū Xī 朱熹 (1130–1200) — Zhū Xī collaborated with him on critical-edition work for the *Yílǐ*. Author of three *Yílǐ* studies in the Sìkù: [[KR1d0030]] *Yílǐ jíshì* 儀禮集釋 in 30 juan (the principal Sòng-period commentary-anthology on the *Yílǐ*); [[KR1d0031]] *Yílǐ shìgōng* 儀禮釋宫 in 1 juan (on the architectural-spatial layout of pre-Qín ritual spaces); and *Yílǐ gāngmù* 儀禮綱目 (on the chapter-and-section structure of the *Yílǐ*; lost). The *Sòng zhōngxīng yìwén zhì* records that "after the Yílǐ was abandoned, scholars no longer chanted it; in the Qiándào era Zhāng Chún ([[KR1d0029]]) first corrected its errors as the *Yílǐ shíwù*; in the Chúnxī era Lǐ Rúguī produced the *Jíshì* drawing on classics and biographies; further produced *Gāngmù* to distinguish chapter-and-section purport, and *Shìgōng* to discuss the system of palace-and-room. Zhū Xī once collaborated with him on critical-edition work — apparently he was learned in ritual."
