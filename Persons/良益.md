# 良益 Liángyì / Ryōeki

**Ryōeki** 良益, late-Edo Japanese kanpō physician; immediate teacher of [[小阪營升]] Kosaka Genyū and member of the acupoint-transmission lineage transmitted in Kosaka's *Jīngxué zuǎnyào* 經穴纂要 ([[KR3ee043]]).
