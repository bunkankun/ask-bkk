Late-Qīng physician and educational official (*xué-bó* 學博) of Huái-chuān 淮川 (i.e. Liú-yáng 瀏陽, eastern Húnán); courtesy name 善吾 *Shàn-wú*. Author of the lead diphtheria-treatise *Shí-yì bái-hóu zhèng-lùn* 時疫白喉證論 and its sequel *Bái-hóu xù-lùn* 白喉續論, dating his clinical experience to *Tóng-zhì dīng-mǎo* 同治丁卯 (1867) and following years; these tracts were compiled by [[嚴承甸]] into [[KR3em031]] *Bái-hóu jié-yào hé-biān* (preface 1907). His position is strongly anti-diaphoretic (*jì biǎo* 忌表) and anti-warming, with the heroic *Lóng-hǔ èr-xiān tāng* 龍虎二仙湯 as central prescription. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
