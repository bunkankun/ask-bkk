Late-Qīng physician and educational official (*xuébó* 學博) of Huáichuān 淮川 (i.e. Liúyáng 瀏陽, eastern Húnán); courtesy name 善吾 *Shànwú*. Author of the lead diphtheria-treatise *Shíyì báihóu zhènglùn* 時疫白喉證論 and its sequel *Báihóu xùlùn* 白喉續論, dating his clinical experience to *Tóngzhì dīngmǎo* 同治丁卯 (1867) and following years; these tracts were compiled by [[嚴承甸]] into [[KR3em031]] *Báihóu jiéyào hébiān* (preface 1907). His position is strongly anti-diaphoretic (*jì biǎo* 忌表) and anti-warming, with the heroic *Lónghǔ èrxiān tāng* 龍虎二仙湯 as central prescription. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
