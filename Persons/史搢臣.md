**Shǐ Jìnchén** 史搢臣 (*fl.* mid-19th c., late Qīng), Qīng clinical-pharmacological writer, author of the *Yuàntǐ yīhuà* 願體醫話 ("Medical-Talk with the Body's Will") — a series of practical clinical-and-pharmacological observations focused on emergency-rescue and rare-syndrome material. The *yuángǎo* (original manuscript) was preserved by Xǔ Lǐngsān 許領三 and donated to [[王士雄]] (Wáng Mèngyīng), who collated and published it in the early 1850s as [[KR3ed140]] *Jiàodìng yuàntǐ yīhuà liángfāng*. Shǐ's *Yuàntǐ yīhuà* is one of the principal mid-19th-century works in the practical-clinical *yīhuà* (medical-talk) sub-genre — alongside the works of Wáng Mèngyīng and Hé Liánchén. Sparse further biographical record.
