**Shǐ Jìn-chén** 史搢臣 (*fl.* mid-19th c., late Qīng), Qīng clinical-pharmacological writer, author of the *Yuàn-tǐ yī-huà* 願體醫話 ("Medical-Talk with the Body's Will") — a series of practical clinical-and-pharmacological observations focused on emergency-rescue and rare-syndrome material. The *yuán-gǎo* (original manuscript) was preserved by Xǔ Lǐng-sān 許領三 and donated to [[王士雄]] (Wáng Mèng-yīng), who collated and published it in the early 1850s as [[KR3ed140]] *Jiào-dìng yuàn-tǐ yī-huà liáng-fāng*. Shǐ's *Yuàn-tǐ yī-huà* is one of the principal mid-19th-century works in the practical-clinical *yī-huà* (medical-talk) sub-genre — alongside the works of Wáng Mèng-yīng and Hé Lián-chén. Sparse further biographical record.
