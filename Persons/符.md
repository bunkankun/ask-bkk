# Fú 符 (Líng-ruì Fú-ān-zhǔ 靈瑞符菴主)

Mid-17th-century Chinese Buddhist nun (*bǐ-qiū-ní* 比丘尼) and Chán-literary practitioner. Dharma-name [[符]] 符 (single character, per the Kanripo catalog); *hào* Líng-ruì 靈瑞 ("Spirit-Auspicious"), Fú-ān-zhǔ 符菴主 ("Fú-Hermitage-Master"). Lay name and lifedates unrecorded.

Dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chú** 退翁弘儲 (1605–1672) at Líng-yán-sì 靈巖寺 (Sū-zhōu), contemporary and co-practitioner of [[徹|Miào-zhàn Zǒng-dàorén]] 玅湛總道人. The two nuns together produced the *Sòng gǔ hé xiǎng jí* 頌古合響集 [[KR6q0204]] — a collection of harmonizing verse-commentaries on classical Chán cases — with each contributing her own dual-voice response to each case.

Little independent biographical material is preserved beyond her co-authorship with Miào-zhàn and her inclusion in Hóng-chú's teaching-community at Líng-yán. See Grant, Beata. 2008. *Eminent Nuns: Women Chan Masters of Seventeenth-Century China*. University of Hawai'i Press, for detailed treatment.
