**Sūn Jùqīng** 孫炬卿 (fl. late Southern-Sòng, mid-13th c., 宋), Southern-Sòng printer-physician. Printed the original block-print of the *Bèi-jí jiǔ-fǎ* 備急灸法 ([[KR3ee049]]) composed by the *Tuán-liàn-shǐ* Zhāng Huàn 張渙. The original print survived in Japanese transmission and was rediscovered in the late Qīng. The catalog meta lists Sūn as author; properly he is the original printer-patron of Zhāng Huàn's text.
