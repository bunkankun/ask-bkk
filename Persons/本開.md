# Běn-kāi 本開

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shì-zhě* 侍者 to [[達尊|Shí-shuāng Ěr-zhān Dá-zūn]] 石霜爾瞻達尊 (1608–1663). Head recorder (*jì-lù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0420]] 《石霜爾瞻尊禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J27 B200). The juan-head signature line reads "*shì-zhě Běn-kāi jì-lù* 侍者本開記錄".

The 本-generation character places Běn-kāi in the Mù-chén Dào-mǐn line's 本-generation cohort together with **Běn-shēng** 本昇 ([[昇]] at [[KR6q0409]]), **Běn-xiù** 本秀 (the *tǎ-míng* author for [[KR6q0420]]), and the larger Mù-chén-line 本-character generation. Although Dá-zūn's direct teacher was Dùn-sǒu Zhì-jì rather than Mù-chén, the Dùn-sǒu sub-line shared the same generation-character convention with Mù-chén's line at the next level (本), suggesting a common Lóng-chí Huàn-yǒu Zhèng-chuán lineage-poem.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0420]] juan-head signature line.
