# Běnkāi 本開

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, *shìzhě* 侍者 to [[達尊|Shíshuāng Ěrzhān Dázūn]] 石霜爾瞻達尊 (1608–1663). Head recorder (*jìlù* 記錄) of [[KR6q0420]] 《石霜爾瞻尊禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J27 B200). The juan-head signature line reads "*shìzhě Běnkāi jìlù* 侍者本開記錄".

The 本-generation character places Běnkāi in the Mùchén Dàomǐn line's 本-generation cohort together with **Běnshēng** 本昇 ([[昇]] at [[KR6q0409]]), **Běnxiù** 本秀 (the *tǎmíng* author for [[KR6q0420]]), and the larger Mù-chén-line 本-character generation. Although Dázūn's direct teacher was Dùnsǒu Zhìjì rather than Mùchén, the Dùnsǒu sub-line shared the same generation-character convention with Mùchén's line at the next level (本), suggesting a common Lóngchí Huànyǒu Zhèngchuán lineage-poem.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0420]] juan-head signature line.
