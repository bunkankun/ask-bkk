# Dào-wù 道務

Northern-Sòng Chán disciple (*cān xué* 參學) of Bǎo-níng Rén-yǒng 保寧仁勇. Co-recorder of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0284]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
