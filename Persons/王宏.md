Wáng Hóng 王宏 (Qīng) was a Mid-Qīng *Lǐxué* writer of unrecorded provenance. Author of the *Zhèng xué yú jiàn shù* 正學隅見述 ([[KR3a0112]]). The catalog meta gives no dates. CBDB has multiple homonyms 王宏, none with secure identification with the *Zhèng xué yú jiàn shù* author.
