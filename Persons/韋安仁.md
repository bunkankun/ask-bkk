# Wéi Ān-rén 韋安仁

Floruit early Tang, *Zhēnguān*–*Yǒng-huī* era (640s–650s). Member of the great Jīng-zhào Wéi 京兆韋氏 lineage, one of the dominant aristocratic families of the early Tang. Held mid-rank Hànlín posts at the imperial court.

Documented as one of the principal compilers of the *Wǔ-dài shǐ zhì* 五代史志 — the ten *zhì* attached to the *Suí shū* (KR2a0023) — under [[長孫無忌]]'s supervision and Tang Gāozōng's commission, presented in *Xiǎn-qìng* 1 (656). Detailed biographical particulars not preserved in the *Tang shū* biographies; not in CBDB.
