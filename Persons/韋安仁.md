# Wéi Ānrén 韋安仁

Floruit early Tang, *Zhēnguān*–*Yǒnghuī* era (640s–650s). Member of the great Jīngzhào Wéi 京兆韋氏 lineage, one of the dominant aristocratic families of the early Tang. Held mid-rank Hànlín posts at the imperial court.

Documented as one of the principal compilers of the *Wǔdài shǐ zhì* 五代史志 — the ten *zhì* attached to the *Suí shū* ([[KR2a0023]]) — under [[長孫無忌]]'s supervision and Tang Gāozōng's commission, presented in *Xiǎnqìng* 1 (656).
