*Hào* of the late-Qīng physician who, residing as a guest at the provincial capital of Jiāngxī, sponsored the *Guāngxù* 壬寅 (1902) Jiāngxī reprint of [[KR3em032]] *Báihóu biànzhèng* by [[王裕慶]] and wrote its principal preface, signed 南昌方內散人. His personal name is not given. The preface adopts Wáng's anti-cooling stance, while pushing back against Wáng's denial of the疫 (epidemic) status of *báihóu*, arguing that contagiousness is compatible with non-uniform pathology.
