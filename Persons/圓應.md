# Yuányīng 圓應

Northern Sòng *cānxué xiǎoshī* 參學小師 of [[重顯|Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn]] 雪竇重顯; assembled and prefaced the *Pùquán jí* 瀑泉集 stratum (1030) of [[KR6q0074]] *Míngjué chánshī yǔlù* during Chóngxiǎn's lifetime.
