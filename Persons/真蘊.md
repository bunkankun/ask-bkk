# Zhēn-yùn 真蘊

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[聆|Lǎn-shí Líng]] 懶石聆 in the Pò-shān / Zhàng-xuě Sichuan sub-line. Direct recorder (*lù* 錄) of [[KR6q0432]] 《懶石聆禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B218), with the juan-head signature line "*sì-fǎ mén-rén Zhēn-yùn lù* 嗣法門人真蘊錄" appearing consistently across the individual juan.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0432]] juan-head signature line.
