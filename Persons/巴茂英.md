# Bā Màoyīng 巴茂英

Contemporary Chinese-medicine philologist; contributor to the *Sòng-Jīn-Yuán mìngyǐng yīzhù 50 zhǒng* 宋金元名醫醫著50種 modern critical-edition series edited by [[嚴世芸]] Yán Shìyún. Co-editor of the modern recension of [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ's *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhíjué* 小兒藥證直訣 ([[KR3ej020]]) in that series.
