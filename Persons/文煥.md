# Wénhuàn 文煥

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[克明]] and [[宗成]]) of *juan* 5 (*sònggǔ* + *zànyǔ*) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with Língyán Tiānzhāng Wénhuàn 靈巖天彰文煥 (DILA A021384), a dharma-heir of Qīngyù in the next generation.
