Kyŏnghŭng 璟興 (active mid-late 7th c.), Korean Silla Buddhist scholar-monk; one of the major figures of the *generation after Wǒnhyo* in Silla Buddhism. Author of [[KR6f0068|*Wú liàng shòu jīng lián yì shù wén zàn* 無量壽經連義述文贊]] (T1748, 3 fasc.) — the *Connected-Meaning Recorded-Text Eulogy on the Sūtra of Amitāyus*. The work is one of the principal Korean Hwaeom / Pure Land commentaries on the *Larger Sukhāvatīvyūha*, post-dating Wǒnhyo and parallel to the work of [[義湘]] Ŭisang's lineage. Birth and death dates not preserved.
