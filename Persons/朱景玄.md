Mid-Táng official and painting critic from Wújùn 吳郡 (modern Sūzhōu). Active in the *Tàihé* 太和 and *Kāichéng* 開成 reigns of Wénzōng (827–840), serving as *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士. Author of [[KR3h0010]] *Tángcháo mínghuà lù* 唐朝名畫錄 (c. 840–846), the first systematic single-dynasty painting catalogue and the work that introduced the *yìpǐn* 逸品 (untrammelled grade) into the Chinese ranking-system. Zhāng Yànyuǎn quotes him as a contemporary in [[KR3h0009]] *Lìdài mínghuà jì*. The *Tángshū·Yìwénzhì* mistitled his book *Tánghuà duàn* 唐畫斷, an error corrected by the Sìkù editors. No collected works survive.
