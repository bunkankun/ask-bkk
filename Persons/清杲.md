# Qīng-gǎo 清杲

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[原澂|Fǎ-lán Yuán-chéng]] 法瀾原澂 in the Jīng-zhōu-area Chán tradition. Head compiler (*biān-cì* 編次) of [[KR6q0439]] 《法瀾澂禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B229). Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0439]] juan-head signature "*sì-fǎ mén-rén Qīng-gǎo biān-cì* 嗣法門人清杲編次".
