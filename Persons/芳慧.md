# Fāng-huì 芳慧

A mid-Qīng monk, disciple of **[[妙能]] Miào-néng fǎ-shī** 妙能法師, principally remembered as compiler (*biān* 編) of the **《淨土承恩集》** *Jìng-tǔ chéng-ēn jí* [[KR6p0108]] (X1189) — a short hagiographical-pastoral anthology gathering Miào-néng's Pure Land teachings together with deathbed *wǎng-shēng* 往生 testimonies of practitioners under his guidance, including the monk **Chāo-zōng** 超宗 of **Cháng-níng-sì** 常甯寺 in *Ruì-yì* 瑞邑 (modern Ruì'ān, southeastern Zhèjiāng). Active in the late-Qián-lóng / Jiā-qìng / early-Dào-guāng period (c. 1750–1830) in the Wēn-zhōu / Ruì-ān regional Pure Land community. Lifedates not preserved.
