# Fānghuì 芳慧

A mid-Qīng monk, disciple of **[[妙能]] Miàonéng fǎshī** 妙能法師, principally remembered as compiler (*biān* 編) of the **《淨土承恩集》** *Jìngtǔ chéngēn jí* [[KR6p0108]] (X1189) — a short hagiographical-pastoral anthology gathering Miàonéng's Pure Land teachings together with deathbed *wǎngshēng* 往生 testimonies of practitioners under his guidance, including the monk **Chāozōng** 超宗 of **Chángníngsì** 常甯寺 in *Ruìyì* 瑞邑 (modern Ruì'ān, southeastern Zhèjiāng). Active in the late-Qián-lóng / Jiāqìng / early-Dào-guāng period (c. 1750–1830) in the Wēnzhōu / Ruìān regional Pure Land community. Lifedates not preserved.
