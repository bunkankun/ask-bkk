Yōupóshànduo 優波扇多 (Skt. *Upaśānta* "Calmed by Training"; DILA Authority A001846) was a Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma master credited with composing the commentary on Dharmaśreṣṭhin's root *Abhidharma-hṛdaya* preserved in the *Āpítán xīn lùnjīng* 阿毘曇心論經 ([[KR6l0016]], T1551). He belongs to the second generation of the *Hṛdaya* commentary tradition and is placed in approximately the 3rd–5th century CE. Nothing further is known of his biography beyond this attribution.
