Gōng Quánfēng 龔泉峰, an early-Yuán *Yì*-scholar from the Jiāngxī region; cited as a teacher of [[熊良輔]] Xióng Liángfǔ who reviewed and approved the manuscript that became the *Zhōuyì běnyì jíchéng* ([[KR1a0079]], printed 1322). Lifedates and further biographical details not securely recorded.
