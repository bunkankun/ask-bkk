# 楊星泉

Yáng Xīngquán, early-nineteenth-century Hǎiníng 海寧 physician (*Yǔfù yě* 俞附也); collaborator of [[許槤]] Xǔ Lián in the collation of the manuscript witnesses underlying [[KR3em029]] *Yānhóu màizhèng tōnglùn* 咽喉脈證通論 prior to its *Dàoguāng* 5 (1825) printing.
