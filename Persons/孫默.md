Sūn Mò 孫默 (1623–1678), *zì* Wú-yán 無言, *hào* Huáng-yuè shān-rén 黃岳山人, a *bù-yī* (commoner) of Xiū-níng 休寧 in Huī-zhōu. Lived in Guǎng-líng 廣陵 (Yáng-zhōu); famously poor but a passionate connoisseur and patron of the early-*Kāng-xī cí* revival. Walked thousands of *lǐ* to meet each fellow-poet; would forgo a meal to buy a fine *cí*-collection to print. Edited the *Shí-wǔ jiā cí* [[KR4j0076]] (37 *juǎn*) over fourteen years (*Kāng-xī jiǎ-chén* / 1664 → *Kāng-xī dīng-sì* / 1677), starting as a 3-author collection (Zōu Zhì-mó 鄒祗謨, Péng Sūn-yù 彭孫遹, Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]]) and successively expanding to 6, then 10, then 15. The *Sìkù* tíyào endorses Wáng Shì-zhēn's portrait of him from the *Jū-yì lù* 居易錄. CBDB 75963 gives 1623–1678.
