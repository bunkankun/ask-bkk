Zhào Jūn 趙均 (1591–1640), zì Língjūn 靈均, of Wú-xiàn (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū), was a late-Míng *jīnshí* and palaeography enthusiast, son of the famous Hánshān 寒山 hermit-philologist Zhào Yíguāng 趙宧光 (1559–1625). Zhào Yíguāng's *Shuōwén chángjiàn* 說文長箋 — the major Míng-era *Shuōwén jiězì* commentary, sometimes criticised for over-creative readings — gave the family its philological reputation; Zhào Jūn inherited this *liù-shū* and *zhuàn-lì* heritage, and pursued *jīnshí* collecting. His one surviving work the *Jīnshílín shídì kǎo* 金石林時地考 [[KR2n0032]] in 2 juan organises stelae geographically (on Chén Sī's *Bǎokè cóngbiān* model). CBDB 132154 confirms 1591–1640.
