Guī-tú 龜圖 (*fl.* late ninth century), styled *Dēng-shì láng* and former *Cān-jūn*, the otherwise unidentified annotator of the *Běihù lù* 北戶錄 ([[KR2k0106]]) by [[段公路]]. The Sìkù tíyào suggests he was probably a kinsman of Duàn Gōng-lù — though the *Táng-shū chéng-xiàng shì-xì biǎo* does not record his name; surname unknown. His annotations form a substantial parallel layer to Duàn's main text and are a major secondary witness to the lost late-Táng *fēng-tǔ* literature of Lǐng-nán.
