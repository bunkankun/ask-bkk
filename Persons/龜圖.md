Guītú 龜圖 (*fl.* late ninth century), styled *Dēngshì láng* and former *Cānjūn*, the otherwise unidentified annotator of the *Běihù lù* 北戶錄 ([[KR2k0106]]) by [[段公路]]. The Sìkù tíyào suggests he was probably a kinsman of Duàn Gōnglù — though the *Tángshū chéngxiàng shìxì biǎo* does not record his name; surname unknown. His annotations form a substantial parallel layer to Duàn's main text and are a major secondary witness to the lost late-Táng *fēngtǔ* literature of Lǐngnán.
