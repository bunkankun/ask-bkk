**Shěn Xiàogǔ** 沈嘯谷 (Republican era), reviser of [[時逸人]] Shí Yìrén's *Wēnbìng quánshū* 溫病全書; the reworked version served as the standard warm-disease textbook at the Húnán State Medical College in the mid-1930s. Mentioned in [[王德宣]] Wáng Déxuān's *Wēnbìng zhèngzōng* ([[KR3eg012]]).
