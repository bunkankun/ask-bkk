**Wáng Chéngxūn** 王承勳 (*fl.* mid-Qīng, ca. 1750–1820), co-compiler with [[陶承熹]] (Táo Chéngxī) of the *Huìzhítáng jīngyàn fāng* 惠直堂經驗方 ([[KR3ed115]]), a 4-*juǎn* clinical formulary of the late-eighteenth-century Jiāng Nán popular-pharmacology tradition. The *Huìzhítáng* 惠直堂 ("Studio of the Direct Benefactor") was apparently a clinical practice or publishing house in which Wáng and Táo worked. No further biographical record is available in CBDB or the standard Qīng biographical sources.
