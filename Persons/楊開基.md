Yáng Kāijī 楊開基 (Qīng) was a Mid-Qīng pupil of Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其. Edited Lù's *Sōngyáng chāo cún* 松陽鈔存 ([[KR3a0117]]) — material from Lù's tenure as Magistrate of Sōngyáng 松陽 in Zhèjiāng. Provenance and lifedates unrecorded. CBDB has no confirmed entry.
