Yáng Kāijī 楊開基 (Qīng) was a Mid-Qīng pupil of Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其. Edited Lù's *Sōng-yáng chāo cún* 松陽鈔存 (KR3a0117) — material from Lù's tenure as Magistrate of Sōng-yáng 松陽 in Zhè-jiāng. Provenance and lifedates unrecorded. CBDB has no confirmed entry.
