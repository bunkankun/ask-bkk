# Tánlín 曇琳

Tánlín 曇琳 (early 6th c.), known also as Lín fǎ shī 琳法師, an early 6th-century Chinese monk and a junior associate of the [[菩提達磨|Bodhidharma]]–[[慧可|Huìkě]] milieu. Recorded the *Èrrù sìxíng lùn* 二入四行論 [[KR6q0112]] and supplied its introductory biographical preface, the earliest surviving Chinese biographical note on Bodhidharma.
