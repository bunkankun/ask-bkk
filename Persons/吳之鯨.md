Wú Zhījīng 吳之鯨 (fl. 1609), *zì* Bóyì 伯裔, of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng), was a *jǔrén* of Wànlì 37 (1609) who served as Magistrate of Fúliáng County 浮梁縣 (Jiāngxī). His principal work is the *Wǔlín fànzhì* ([[KR2k0098]]), a 12-juan late-Míng Buddhist-monastic gazetteer of Hángzhōu documenting 426 temples — the most comprehensive Míng-era Buddhist-monastic gazetteer of any single Chinese city.
