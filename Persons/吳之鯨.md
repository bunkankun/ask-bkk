Wú Zhī-jīng 吳之鯨 (fl. 1609), *zì* Bó-yì 伯裔, of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng), was a *jǔrén* of Wàn-lì 37 (1609) who served as Magistrate of Fú-liáng County 浮梁縣 (Jiāng-xī). His principal work is the *Wǔ-lín fàn-zhì* ([[KR2k0098]]), a 12-juan late-Míng Buddhist-monastic gazetteer of Hángzhōu documenting 426 temples — the most comprehensive Míng-era Buddhist-monastic gazetteer of any single Chinese city.
