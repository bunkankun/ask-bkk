**Liú Ānshàng** 劉安上 (1069–1128), *zì* Yuánlǐ 元禮, of Wēnzhōu Yǒngjiā 永嘉. *Jìnshì* of *Shàoshèng* 4 / 1097 (or *Yuánfú* 3 / 1100 per some sources). Held office to *Jǐshìzhōng* (Director of Edicts). Cousin (or near-relative) of Liú Ānjié 劉安節 [[劉安節]]; together with their cousin Zhōu Xíngjǐ 周行己 [[周行己]] known as the *Sān xiānshēng* 三先生 of Yǒngjiā — the foundational triad of the Yǒngjiā school. All three studied with Chéng Yí 程頤 [[程頤]]. Author of *Gěishì jí* 給事集 [[KR4d0127]] in 5 *juǎn* — the title from his *Jǐshìzhōng* office. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 347 (附 to Liú Ānjié biography). The catalog confirms 1069–1128.
