**Liú Ān-shàng** 劉安上 (1069–1128), *zì* Yuán-lǐ 元禮, of Wēn-zhōu Yǒng-jiā 永嘉. *Jìnshì* of *Shào-shèng* 4 / 1097 (or *Yuán-fú* 3 / 1100 per some sources). Held office to *Jǐ-shì-zhōng* (Director of Edicts). Cousin (or near-relative) of Liú Ān-jié 劉安節 [[劉安節]]; together with their cousin Zhōu Xíng-jǐ 周行己 [[周行己]] known as the *Sān xiān-shēng* 三先生 of Yǒng-jiā — the foundational triad of the Yǒng-jiā school. All three studied with Chéng Yí 程頤 [[程頤]]. Author of *Gěi-shì jí* 給事集 [[KR4d0127]] in 5 *juǎn* — the title from his *Jǐ-shì-zhōng* office. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 347 (附 to Liú Ān-jié biography). The catalog confirms 1069–1128.
