Zhào Hóng'ēn 趙宏恩 (fl. 1720s–1740s; lifedates not transmitted in the standard sources), *zì* Rénpǔ 仁圃, *qǐbāoyī* of the Bordered Red Banner (Hànjūn). His career advanced through Yōngzhèng-era provincial postings: Húběi *bùzhèngshǐ* (1727), Húnán governor (1729–1731), Liǎng-Jiāng governor-general (Yōngzhèng 11 = 1733 – Qiánlóng 6 = 1741), and concurrently Liǎng Huái salt commissioner. He presented the *Jiāngnán tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0042]]) in Qiánlóng 1 (1736) and is the named *jiānxiū* on the imperial copy. His own collected works, the *Yùxǐzhāi shīwén jí* 玉牒齋詩文集 (12 *juan*), survive in Qiánlóng-era prints. *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 305 has a brief biography. The catalog meta records his name as "趙田恩", a clear miscopy for "趙宏恩" — corrected here. CBDB id 425826 (no dates given).
