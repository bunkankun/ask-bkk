Tōng-huì 通慧, *zì* **Ān-shí** 安石 ("Settled Stone"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** [[智遠]] at **Yí-líng-zhōu Huáng-líng chán-yuàn** 彝陵州黃陵禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0551]] 《法幢遠禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B434, prefaced 1695.

- - - - -

Several other monks named **Tōng-huì** 通慧 are registered: a Northern-Sòng Cí-ēn-school *fǎ-shī* (DILA A018972); a **Tōng-huì Guī** 通慧珪 (DILA A021102), Yún-mén-school dharma-heir of **Fǎ-yún Xiù** 法雲秀; **Wén Tōng-huì** 文通慧 (DILA A021523), Southern-Sòng monk of Kāifēng Bái-yún-sì; a Daoguang-era **Tōng-huì** 通慧 of Pǔtuó-shān (A013093). None is the same as the present compiler.
