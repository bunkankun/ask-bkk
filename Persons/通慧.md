Tōnghuì 通慧, *zì* **Ānshí** 安石 ("Settled Stone"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** [[智遠]] at **Yílíngzhōu Huánglíng chányuàn** 彝陵州黃陵禪院. Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0551]] 《法幢遠禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B434, prefaced 1695.

- - - - -

Several other monks named **Tōnghuì** 通慧 are registered: a Northern-Sòng Cí-ēn-school *fǎshī* (DILA A018972); a **Tōnghuì Guī** 通慧珪 (DILA A021102), Yún-mén-school dharma-heir of **Fǎyún Xiù** 法雲秀; **Wén Tōnghuì** 文通慧 (DILA A021523), Southern-Sòng monk of Kāifēng Báiyúnsì; a Daoguang-era **Tōnghuì** 通慧 of Pǔtuóshān (A013093). None is the same as the present compiler.
