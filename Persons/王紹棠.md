# 王紹棠

Wáng Shàotáng 王紹棠, of Dāntú 丹徒 (Zhènjiāng, Jiāngsū). Republican-period Běijīng housemate of [[嚴敦益]] Yán Dūnyì who supplied a second manuscript of [[王九峰]] Wáng Jiǔfēng's cases (preserved within [[蔣寶素]] Jiǎng Bǎosù's [[蔣寶素醫略]] *Jiǎng Bǎosù yīlüè*) for Yán's 1923 recovery-edition ([[KR3ep076]]).
