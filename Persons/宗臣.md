Zōng Chén 宗臣 (1525–1560), *zì* Zǐxiàng 子相, of Xìnghuà 興化 (Yáng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). Took the *jìnshì* in Jiā-jìng 29 (1550); appointed *Xíngbù zhǔshì*; transferred to *Lìbù wénxuǎn sī*; advanced to *Jìxūn sī yuánwài láng*. For his contribution-of-funds for **Yáng Jìshèng**'s funeral, offended **Yán Sōng**; sent out as *Fú-jiàn cānyì*; transferred to *Tíxué fùshǐ*. Died in office at only 36 *sui*. He is the youngest and shortest-lived of the **Hòu Qī Zǐ** (Latter Seven Masters), with **Lǐ Pānlóng**, **Wáng Shìzhēn**, **Xú Zhōngháng**, **Wú Guólún**, **Liáng Yǒuyù**. His *Bào Liú Yī-zhàng shū* — a satirical letter on Wàn-lì officialdom bribery customs — is among the most-anthologized pieces in modern *gǔ-wén* readers. His collection is the *Zōng Zǐ-xiàng jí* [[KR4e0209]]. CBDB 34725.
