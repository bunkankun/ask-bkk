# Jiǎng Chūntóng 蔣春同

Late-Qīng Jiāngsū lay devotee (*jūshì*); courtesy name *Lányán* 蘭言. Collator and editor (校訂) of [[謝承謨]] Xiè Chéngmó's *Bōrě xīnjīng yìjiě* ([[KR6c0191]]), Guāngxù 15 (1889). Associated with the late-Qīng *jūshì* Buddhist publication network in the Yangtze delta.
