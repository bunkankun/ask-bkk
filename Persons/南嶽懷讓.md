# Nányuè Huáiràng 南嶽懷讓

Táng Chán master (677–744), Dharma heir of the Sixth Patriarch Huìnéng 慧能; founder of the lineage from which the Línjì 臨濟 and Wěiyǎng 溈仰 houses descend.
