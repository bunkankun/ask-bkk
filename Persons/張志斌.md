# 張志斌 Zhāng Zhìbīn

**Zhāng Zhìbīn** 張志斌, contemporary Chinese historian of medicine at the China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences (中國中醫科學院); co-editor with [[李經緯]] Lǐ Jīngwěi of *Zhōngyī gǔjí zhēnběn jíchéng* 中醫古籍珍本集成 and other major reference works.
