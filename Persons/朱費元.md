**Zhū Fèiyuán** 朱費元 (*fl.* mid-19th c., late Qīng), late-Qīng *wàikē* specialist physician. Author of the *Línzhèng yīdé fāng* 臨證一得方 ([[KR3ed128]]) in 4 *juǎn*, a clinical case-record formulary of approximately 100 cases organised by body-region and clinical-presentation type. The work belongs to the Xuē Jǐ – Lǐ Dōngyuán doctrinal tradition of *wàikē* practice, with strong anti-surgical polemical position and corresponding emphasis on careful internal-medication. Sparse biographical record; Zhū is not securely identified in CBDB or the standard Qīng biographical dictionaries.
