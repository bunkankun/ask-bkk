徐炫 Xú Xuàn was a Tang-dynasty court compiler (著作郎 *zhùzuò láng*) who served in the Xiánhēng era (670–674 CE). His sole surviving work is the *Wǔdài Xīnshuō* 五代新說 ([[KR4k0014]]), a two-*juàn* collection of anecdotes about the five pre-Tang dynasties (Liang, Chen, Northern Qi, Northern Zhou, Sui). His dates are unknown; CBDB records three possible entries (ids 240454, 456081, 496903) without dates.

**Name disambiguation:** This 徐炫 is not to be confused with the Song-dynasty lexicographer 徐鉉 Xú Xuān (917–992), who edited the *Shuōwén jiězì*. Though the names are phonetically identical in Mandarin, they are different characters and different persons separated by nearly three centuries.
