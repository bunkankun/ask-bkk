Yè Sēn 葉森 (1265–1322; CBDB id 109719) was a Yuán-period scholar and prosopographical compiler. The lifedates come from CBDB's Yuán biography file; the *Sìkù* editors identify him simply as the *Yuán-rén* 元人 responsible for supplementing Liáng Yuán-dì and Lù Shàn-jīng's *Gǔjīn tóngxìngmíng lù* ([[KR3k0001]]) with additional entries on Táng, Sòng and Jīn–Yuán figures. His native place and offices are not recorded in the surviving sources; what is preserved of his hand is only the strand of annotation embedded in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* recension of the *Tóngxìngmíng lù*, from which the *Sìkù* compilers recovered it.

Yè Sēn 葉森 (1667–1729; CBDB id 73069). A separate Qīng-period figure of the same name, dated by the *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú nián biǎo* 清代人物生卒年表 (entry 3125) to Kāngxī 6 – Yōngzhèng 7. Unrelated to the Yuán prosopographer above and unconnected with the Kanripo *lèishū* tradition; included here only to disambiguate.
