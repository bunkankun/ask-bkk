# Zhāo-rú 昭如

Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Hǎi-yìn 海印 ("Ocean-Seal"); posthumously styled Pǔ-zhào dà chánshī 普照大禪師. Dharma-heir of [[祖欽]] Xuě-yán Zǔ-qīn (1216–1287); principal named co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0330]]). His own recorded sayings survive as *Hǎi-yìn Zhāo-rú chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0331]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
