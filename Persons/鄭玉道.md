Zhèng Yùdào 鄭玉道 (Southern Sòng) was a county magistrate (*lìngjūn*) of an unspecified locality in the early Southern Sòng. He composed the *Yù sú biān* 諭俗編 in 1 *juàn* — a moral-instructional pamphlet for the common people of his rural district, aimed at *xiān jiàohuà hòu xíngfá* (instruction first, punishment second) — which formed the basis of the later compiled *Qín táng yù sú biān* ([[KR3j0126]]). The *Sòng shǐ Yìwén zhì* records it as 1 *juàn*, classed in *zǐbù zájiā* (and a duplicate appearance in *shǐbù xíngfǎ lèi* — which the *Sìkù* editors flag as a misclassification).
