Táng-dynasty Buddhist *vinaya* exegete and encyclopedist. Native of **Yīquē** 伊闕 (south of Luòyáng), lay surname **Hán** 韓, *zì* **Xuányùn** 玄惲 (also written 元惲 or 玄暉; in some Sòng witnesses **Xuánhuī** is also given). Ordained at twelve at **Qīnglóngsì** 青龍寺, where he undertook deep study of the *vinaya*. He took full ordination together with **Dàoxuān** 道宣 (596–667) under the **Nánshān-律** master **Zhìshǒu** 智首 (567–635) at **Hóngfúsì** 弘福寺 in **Suí Dàyè** 11 = 615 CE — placing his birth ca. 600 by inference.

In **Xiǎnqìng** 顯慶 (656–660), at the imperial summons of the Crown Prince (later Gāozōng), he was installed at the newly-completed **Xīmíngsì** 西明寺 in Cháng'ān, where he resided alongside Dàoxuān and where the great compilations of his maturity were produced. The two masters jointly defined the *Nánshān-律* lineage's apex generation.

His written output is enormous: the hundred-juan **Fǎyuàn zhūlín** ([[KR6s0002]], T2122, completed 668) — a monumental Buddhist *lèishū* and the work for which he is most remembered — together with the twenty-juan **Zhūjīng yàojí** 諸經要集 ([[KR6s0003]], T2123, an abridgment of the canonical-citation portions of the *Fǎyuàn*), the **Sìfēnlǜ tǎoyào** 四分律討要 (six juan), the **Sìfēnlǜ níchāo** 四分律尼鈔 (five juan), the **Dàxiǎoshèng chánmén guān** 大小乘禪門觀 (ten juan), the **Xìnfú lùn** 信福論, the **Jīngāngjīng jízhù** 金剛經集註, and others.

Death: **Hóngdào** 弘道 1 (683 CE / early 684 by Julian conversion); the death notice is preserved at *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* j. 4 (T50n2061_p0726c07) and at the *Bǎizhàng qīngguī zhèngyì jì* 百丈清規證義記 (X76n1516_p0091c08).
