Gǔtíng Shànjiān 古庭善堅 (1414–1493) was a mid-Míng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) Chán master active chiefly in **Kūnmíng** 昆明 (Yúnnán) and at **Fúshān** 浮山 (Ānhuī). Native of the southern suburb of Kūnmíng, lay surname **Dīng** 丁. At seven he asked to leave the household; at ten he entered **Wǔhuásì** 五華寺 under the monk **Zōng** 宗上人, who gave him the name *Shànxián* 善賢. At nineteen he took further vows with *Báiyán* héshàng 白巖和尚 and was renamed *Shànjiān* 善堅; Báiyán set him to unremitting sitting practice and the Guānyīn *niànfó*. In the Xuāndé 5 year *gēngxū* (1430) he met *Wúyǐn* héshàng 無隱和尚, who set him on the classic *wànfǎ guī yī* 萬法歸一 huàtóu; some years on, reading the *Yuánjué jīng* 圓覺經 passage on the phantom nature of body-and-mind, he had an opening. In Xuāndé 10 (1435) he reached **Yōngluóshān** 擁蘿山 in Guìzhōu, then went on into Shǔ 蜀 (Sìchuān), where for several years he did not allow his flank to touch the mat; there he attained great awakening. In Zhèngtǒng 10 (1445) he travelled to **Jīntái** 金臺 (Běijīng) to seek validation from **Wújì Míngwù** [[明悟]] 無際明悟 (1383–1446); Wújì sealed his realisation, conferred the *xìnyī* 信衣 and *fúzǐ* 拂子 of transmission, and bestowed on him the name *Gǔtíng* 古庭 ("Ancient Courtyard"). During the Tiānshùn era (1457–) he taught at **Fúshān** 浮山 and drew a large following; his principal disciple there, **Yīguàn** 一貫, edited the first redaction of his *yǔlù* in 1457, furnished with a preface by the Nánjīng 禮部尚書 **Zhāng Huì** 張惠. In his later years he returned to Yúnnán and founded the **Guīhuà Chánlín** 歸化禪林 at Kūnmíng, whence he is sometimes called *Guīhuà Shànjiān*. He died on Hóngzhì 6.7.20 *wèi*-hour (9 September 1493), aged 80. His principal dharma-heir was **Dàwéi Jìnglún** 大巍淨倫 (1426–1492). His writings include the *Shānyún shuǐshí jí* 山雲水石集 (the parent collection) and the *Gǔtíng zǔshī yǔlù jílüè* 古庭祖師語錄輯略 ([[KR6q0396]], a Chóngzhēn-era re-edition). Together with **Pánlóng Zǔyuán** 盤龍祖源 he is traditionally counted as one of the two revivers of Chán Buddhism in Míng-dynasty Yúnnán.
