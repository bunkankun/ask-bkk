Late-Míng / early-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Yùshū** 玉叔, alternate name **Yǒujiào** 友教. Lifedates not preserved. Co-compiler with **Wēng Chūn** [[翁春]] of the *Jīngāng jīng qiǎnjiě* 金剛經淺解 ([[KR6c0078]], X25 no. 490), prefaced in **Kāngxī 20 = 1681** by **Zhào Yuèshēng** 趙嶽生 (sobriquet 視公甫). The text identifies the two compilers as *fèngfó dìzǐ* 奉佛弟子 ("disciples upholding the Buddha"). DILA gives Wáng's dynasty as 明 / 清, indicating activity across the dynastic transition.
