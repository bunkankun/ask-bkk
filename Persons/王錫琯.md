Late-Míng / early-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Yù-shū** 玉叔, alternate name **Yǒu-jiào** 友教. Lifedates not preserved. Co-compiler with **Wēng Chūn** [[翁春]] of the *Jīn-gāng jīng qiǎn-jiě* 金剛經淺解 ([[KR6c0078]], X25 no. 490), prefaced in **Kāng-xī 20 = 1681** by **Zhào Yuè-shēng** 趙嶽生 (sobriquet 視公甫). The text identifies the two compilers as *fèng-fó dì-zǐ* 奉佛弟子 ("disciples upholding the Buddha"). DILA gives Wáng's dynasty as 明 / 清, indicating activity across the dynastic transition.
