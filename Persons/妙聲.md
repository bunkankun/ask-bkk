Yuán-Míng transition Chán monk, native of **Wú xiàn** 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Personal name **Miào-shēng** 妙聲; *zì* / sobriquet **Jiǔ-gāo** 九臯 (also **Jiǔ-gāo shēng** 九臯聲); his secular *Dōng-gāo* 東臯 ("East-Mound") title comes from his retreat. Dharma-disciple of **Gǔ-tíng Shàn-xué** 古庭善學 (DILA A015571), in the lineage of the late-Yuán Sūzhōu Chán community. Studied the *Zhǐ-guān* 止觀 and was broadly versed in the Buddhist canonical literature; also distinguished in poetry. In the late Yuán resided at the **Jǐng-dé sì** 景德寺 in Sūzhōu; later moved to the **Cháng-shú Huì-rì sì** 常熟慧日寺; in the early Hóngwǔ era presided as abbot of the **Píng-jiāng Běi-chán sì** 平江北禪寺. In **Hóngwǔ 3** (1370) summoned to court together with the monk **Wàn-jīn** 萬金 to take part in the Hóngwǔ regulation of Buddhist affairs. On friendly terms with the major Yuán-Míng transition literati [[袁桷]], [[張翥]], [[危素]], and others; his verse exchanges with them form a substantial part of his collected work *Dōng-gāo lù* 東皋錄 [[KR4e0017]]. His disciple **Dé-huán** 德瓛 cut the blocks for the collection in Hóngwǔ 17 (1384), suggesting Miào-shēng may have died around that time, in his mid-sixties.

Lifedates unrecorded; the Sìkù tíyào of [[KR4e0017]] states he was "over sixty" entering the Míng (1368–70), implying a birth date in the 1300s. DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority: A018657.
