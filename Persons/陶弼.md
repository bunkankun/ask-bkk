Mid-Northern-Sòng frontier military-administrative official and poet, *zì* Shāngwēng 商翁, of *Qíyáng* 祁陽 (Húnán). In *Qìnglì* followed Yáng Tián 楊畋 in suppressing the *Húnán Yáo* uprising and was rewarded with *Yángshuò xiàn zhǔbù*; held *zhī Yōngzhōu* (Guǎngxī, on the southern Sòng-Annam frontier); promoted four ranks to *Dōngshàng Géménshǐ* and *Kángzhōu Tuánliànshǐ*. The *Sòngshǐ* j. 334 biography details his pacification of the *Mán* tribes. Original 18-*juǎn* literary corpus lost; *mùzhìmíng* by Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅 (preserved in *Shāngǔ jí*). Surviving collection: *Yōngzhōu xiǎo jí* [[KR4d0046]] (1 *juǎn*, 73 poems on Húnán / Guǎngxī frontier themes). CBDB id 26942, lifedates 1015–1078.
