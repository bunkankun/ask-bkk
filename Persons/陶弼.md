Mid-Northern-Sòng frontier military-administrative official and poet, *zì* Shāng-wēng 商翁, of *Qí-yáng* 祁陽 (Hú-nán). In *Qìng-lì* followed Yáng Tián 楊畋 in suppressing the *Hú-nán Yáo* uprising and was rewarded with *Yáng-shuò xiàn zhǔ-bù*; held *zhī Yōng-zhōu* (Guǎng-xī, on the southern Sòng-Annam frontier); promoted four ranks to *Dōng-shàng Gé-mén-shǐ* and *Káng-zhōu Tuán-liàn-shǐ*. The *Sòng-shǐ* j. 334 biography details his pacification of the *Mán* tribes. Original 18-*juǎn* literary corpus lost; *mù-zhì-míng* by Huáng Tíng-jiān 黃庭堅 (preserved in *Shān-gǔ jí*). Surviving collection: *Yōng-zhōu xiǎo jí* [[KR4d0046]] (1 *juǎn*, 73 poems on Hú-nán / Guǎng-xī frontier themes). CBDB id 26942, lifedates 1015–1078.
