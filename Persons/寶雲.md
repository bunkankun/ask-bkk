Bǎoyún 寶雲 (\*Ratnamegha; 376–449), Liú-Sòng-period 劉宋 monastic translator. Native of Liángzhōu 涼州, he is reckoned together with Faxian 法顯, Zhì-yán 智嚴 ([[智嚴]]), and others among the small group of fifth-century Chinese monks who travelled to Central Asia and India in search of scripture; he and Zhì-yán reached Khotan, the Pamirs, and Northwest India before returning by sea. Back in China he settled at the Liù-héshān 六合山 retreat near Jiànkāng and at the Dào-chǎng 道場 and Zhǐ-yuán 枳園 monasteries in the capital. His translation activity is documented for 397–449 CE; the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145) credits him with collaborative translations including the *Wúliángshòu jīng* 無量壽經 (in association with Buddhabhadra), [[KR6i0223|*Sì-tiānwáng jīng* 四天王經 (T590)]] and others. His linguistic competence in Indic languages is praised by Sēngyòu and Huìjiǎo. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001833.
