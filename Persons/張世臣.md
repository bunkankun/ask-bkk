**Zhāng Shìchén** 張世臣 (*fl.* Northern Sòng) — Sòng obstetrical compiler whose *Lěiyòng jīngxiào fāng* 累用經效方 (also given as *Lěi yòng jīngxiào fāng*) is preserved within *juǎn* 4 of [[朱端章]] Zhū Duānzhāng's *Wèishēng jiābǎo chǎnkē bèiyào* ([[KR3ei056]]), together with [[李師聖]] Lǐ Shīshèng's *Chǎnyù bǎoqìng jí* ([[KR3ei076]]). The work survives only through this single textual witness. Not in CBDB.
