**Lín Pèi-qín** 林佩琴 (*zì* Yún-hé 雲和, *hào* Xī-tóng 羲桐, 1772–1839) — mid-Qīng physician of Dān-yáng 丹陽 (Jiāng-sū). *Jǔ-rén* of *Jiā-qìng* 13 / 1808 (the so-called *wù-chén-bǎng* 戊辰榜, along with Guì Chāo-wàn); failed at the metropolitan examination of *Jiā-qìng* 14 / 1809 and turned to private medical practice, in which he was active for three decades. Distinguished for his blending of Yè Tiān-shì *wēn-bìng* doctrine with the late-Míng warming-tonifying tradition; author of the *Lèi-zhèng zhì-cái* 類證治裁 ([[KR3er061]]), completed shortly before his death and published posthumously by his son Lín Zhī-běn 林芝本 in 1847 under Guì Chāo-wàn's patronage.
