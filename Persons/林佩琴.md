**Lín Pèiqín** 林佩琴 (*zì* Yúnhé 雲和, *hào* Xītóng 羲桐, 1772–1839) — mid-Qīng physician of Dānyáng 丹陽 (Jiāngsū). *Jǔrén* of *Jiāqìng* 13 / 1808 (the so-called *wùchénbǎng* 戊辰榜, along with Guì Chāowàn); failed at the metropolitan examination of *Jiāqìng* 14 / 1809 and turned to private medical practice, in which he was active for three decades. Distinguished for his blending of Yè Tiānshì *wēnbìng* doctrine with the late-Míng warming-tonifying tradition; author of the *Lèizhèng zhìcái* 類證治裁 ([[KR3er061]]), completed shortly before his death and published posthumously by his son Lín Zhīběn 林芝本 in 1847 under Guì Chāowàn's patronage.
