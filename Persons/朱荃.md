# Zhū Quán 朱荃

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as one of the *kǎozhèng* compilers for the *Běi Qí shū* ([[KR2a0021]]) in the *Sìkù quánshū*, working alongside [[姚範]]. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
