**Míngbǎo** 明保 (*fl.* late 19th c.) — late-Qīng Manchu official, *xiāngyúdì* 鄉愚弟 (same-clan-region "humble younger brother") of [[慶恕]] Qìng Shù; *guānchá* 觀察 (intendant) and subsequently *tàishǒu* 太守 (prefect) of a Wǔliáng 五涼 (modern Wǔwēi 武威, Gānsù) prefecture. Author of the 1897 publication preface to Qìng Shù's *Yīxué zhāicuì* 醫學摘粹 ([[KR3er069]]), narrating his own recovery from oral inflammation under Qìng Shù's treatment. Not in CBDB.
