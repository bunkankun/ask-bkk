**Míng-bǎo** 明保 (*fl.* late 19th c.) — late-Qīng Manchu official, *xiāng-yú-dì* 鄉愚弟 (same-clan-region "humble younger brother") of [[慶恕]] Qìng Shù; *guān-chá* 觀察 (intendant) and subsequently *tài-shǒu* 太守 (prefect) of a Wǔ-liáng 五涼 (modern Wǔ-wēi 武威, Gān-sù) prefecture. Author of the 1897 publication preface to Qìng Shù's *Yī-xué zhāi-cuì* 醫學摘粹 ([[KR3er069]]), narrating his own recovery from oral inflammation under Qìng Shù's treatment. Not in CBDB.
