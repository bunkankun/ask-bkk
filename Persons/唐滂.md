Táng Pāng 唐滂 (sometimes written 唐溥; *fl.* mid 3rd century) was a SūnWú 孫吳 official and author. He is otherwise barely attested in the standard histories; the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records his *Tángzǐ* 唐子 in 10 *juàn* and assigns it to the Wú period. His exact birth and death years are not preserved. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0135]] *Tángzǐ* 唐子.
