# Shèng Fúshēng 盛符升

Early-Qīng literatus from Kūnshān 崑山, a co-disciple of the Chán master Jìshí 濟時; in his eighty-ninth year (1699) he composed a substantial historiographical preface to Jìshí's Lèngyán zhèng-jiàn ([[KR6j0725]]) surveying the Sòng-Yuán Lèngyán commentarial tradition.
