Yáo Xìn 姚信 (*fl.* mid 3rd century), *zì* Yuánzhí 元直, was a Sūn-Wú 孫吳 classicist and astronomer, a native of Wúxīng 吳興 (the Kanripo title-list places him under Shǔ, but the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* and the early bibliographic tradition place him under Wú; the dynasty here follows the *Suí shū*). He served at the Wú court as Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary (太常卿) under Sūn Hào 孫皓. His birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally placed in the mid 3rd century. CBDB has no entry.

He is best known for two works: a *Zhōu Yì zhù* 周易注 — Wú-period *Yì* commentary that survives in fragments preserved in Lǐ Dǐngzuò's 李鼎祚 Táng-period *Zhōu Yì jí jiě* 周易集解, and the *Shìwěi* 士緯 — a *rénwù* (personnel evaluation) treatise. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists the *Shìwěi* in 10 *juàn*.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0148]] *Shìwěi* 士緯.
