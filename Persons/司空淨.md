# Sīkōng Jìng 司空淨

Táng-period Chán master, resident at Sīkōng shān 司空山 (Shūzhōu 舒州); recipient of an imperial audience with Táng Xuánzōng recorded in the *Dìwáng wèndào lù* selection of *Xiānjué zōngshèng* ([[KR6q0052]]).
