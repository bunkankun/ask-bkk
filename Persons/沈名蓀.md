Shěn Míngsūn 沈名蓀, zì Jiànfāng 澗芳, was an early-Qīng scholar from Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu). With Zhū Kūntián 朱昆田, son of Zhū Yízūn 朱彝尊, he co-compiled the *Nán Běi shǐ shíxiǎo lù* 南北史識小錄 [[KR2h0003]] (preface dated 1690), a phrase- and anecdote-extract from the *Nánshǐ* 南史 and *Běishǐ* 北史 modeled on Yáng Kǎn's *Liǎng Hàn bówén* 兩漢博聞 [[KR2h0001]]. He is otherwise sparsely attested in the standard biographical compendia and is not yet entered in CBDB; the surviving *Sìkù* preface and his name in the Zhèjiāng zhì *xiānzhèng* lists are the principal traces of his life.
