Modern Japanese Buddhist studies scholar (齊藤 隆信), specialist in Chinese Buddhist apocrypha. Co-edited (with [[大內文雄]] Ōuchi Fumio) the critical text of the *Jìngdù sānmèi jīng* 淨度三昧經 [[KR6v0076]] for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 7.
