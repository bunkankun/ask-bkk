Yānxiá Sǎnrén 煙霞散人 ("the Scattered Person of Mist and Glow") is a Qīng-dynasty literary pseudonym found in the Kanripo catalog as the attributed compiler of *Fènghuáng Chí* 鳳凰池 ([[KR4k0114]]). The same pen name appears as the attributed author of at least one other Qīng vernacular novel (*Xīngyuán* 星緣 etc.), though whether these works share a single historical author is uncertain. No biographical information is recoverable.
