Late-Míng / early-Qīng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Tiān-xī** 天溪, sobriquet **Jǐng-chún** 景淳 (also referenced as **Tiān-xī Shòu-dēng** 天溪受登, **Jǐng-chún Shòu-dēng** 景淳受登, **Chū-yī** 初依). DILA Authority A000555. Born Wàn-lì 35, 6th month, 5th day (= 28 June 1607); death-date unrecorded (productive into the late seventeenth century). Native province unrecorded.

Composer (說 *shuō*) of the **Móhē zhǐguān guàn-yì kē** 摩訶止觀貫義科 ([[KR6d0142]], X56n0924, 2 *juan*) — a sectional analysis of the *Móhē zhǐguān*, with editorial supplementation (補定 *bǔ-dìng*) by **Líng-yào** 靈耀 ([[靈耀]], the early-Qīng Tiāntái master also responsible for [[KR6d0012]], [[KR6d0057]]).

Source: *Suí-yuán jí* 隨緣集 *juan* 2; DILA A000555.
