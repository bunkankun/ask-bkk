Republican-era physician, otherwise undocumented. Author of *Zhì-lì nán-zhēn* 治痢南針 ([[KR3eg043]]), a clinical handbook on the treatment of dysentery (*lì-jí* 痢疾). The book belongs to the early-Republican project of producing concise, practical Chinese-medical clinical primers for working physicians, in the same genre as Hé Liánchén's *Shī-wēn shí-yì zhì-liáo fǎ* ([[KR3eg034]]).
