Republican-era physician, otherwise undocumented. Author of *Zhìlì nánzhēn* 治痢南針 ([[KR3eg043]]), a clinical handbook on the treatment of dysentery (*lìjí* 痢疾). The book belongs to the early-Republican project of producing concise, practical Chinese-medical clinical primers for working physicians, in the same genre as Hé Liánchén's *Shīwēn shíyì zhìliáo fǎ* ([[KR3eg034]]).
