**Jiāng Zhòngwén** 姜仲文, late-Míng *jìnshì* classmate of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng; in the autumn of Wànlì 32 (1604) sent a copyist to obtain Wáng's *Shānghán* treatise, occasioning its single-autumn redaction as [[KR3eh052]] *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng · Shānghán*. Known only from Wáng's self-preface.
