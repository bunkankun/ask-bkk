**Kāngxī** 康熙 (the *Kāngxī* emperor, real name 玄燁 Xuányè, temple name 聖祖 Shèngzǔ, 1654–1722, r. 1661–1722), second emperor of the Qīng. Convened the 1679 *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 special examination as a recruitment device for late-Míng loyalist scholars; among those summoned but refusing service was [[傅山]] Fù Shān (cf. *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē gēkuò* [[KR3ei021]] preface).
