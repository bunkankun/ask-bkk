# Sòng Rénzōng 宋仁宗 (1010–1063)

Personal name Zhào Zhēn 趙禎. Fourth emperor of the Northern Sòng dynasty, reigning 1022–1063; the patron emperor of the *Tiānshèng* 天聖-era imperial-medicine standardization. In 1027 (*Tiānshèng* 5) commissioned [[王惟一]] Wáng Wéiyī to compile [[KR3ee056]] *Tóngrén shūxué zhēnjiǔ tújīng* and to cast the two life-size hollow bronze acupuncture mannequins; in 1030 ordered the engraving of the standardized acupoint text on two stone steles at the *Dàxiàngguó* 大相國 Temple in Kāifēng. His patronage underlies the entire post-Táng standardization of Chinese acupuncture.
