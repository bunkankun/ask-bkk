Conventional placeholder for anonymous or unidentified authors in the Chinese bibliographic tradition. *Wúmíngshì* 無名氏 (lit. "person of no name") and the variant *yìmíng* 佚名 ("name lost") are used interchangeably in modern catalog practice when the authorship of a work cannot be determined from colophons, prefaces, or contemporary bibliographies. In the Kanripo corpus this designation is applied to a range of anonymous popular fiction, especially Qīng vernacular novels of the heroic-chivalric (英雄傳奇 yīngxióng chuánqí) and historical-military (演義 yǎnyì) genres.
