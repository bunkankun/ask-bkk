Late-Yuán / early-Míng poet of Kūnshān 崑山 (in the Lóudōng 婁東 area, modern Tàicāng region). Style-name **Jìngfū** 敬夫; called **Láihètíng zhǔrén** 來鶴亭主人 after the pavilion he built when a second crane joined the one already kept in his garden. He was a member of the late-Yuán Kūnshān poetry circle with **Guō Yì** 郭翼 ([[KR4d0548]]), **Lù Rén** 陸仁, and **Yuán Huá** 袁華. He was a student of **Zhèng Dōng** 鄭東 (Kūnyáng) and an early correspondent of **Yáng Wéizhēn** 楊維楨 — the latter wrote his original 1347 preface for *Láihètíng jí* 來鶴亭集 ([[KR4d0578]]). Lǚ was caught up in the disorder of the early Hóngwǔ: in *Hóngwǔ gēngxū* 庚戌 (1370) he was banished south to Guǎngdōng, returning in *xīnhài* (1371). The reason for his exile is unrecorded. He lived to the late Hóngwǔ era — the latest internal cyclic year in the collection is *guǐyǒu* (Hóngwǔ 26 = 1393), implying that he died very late in the Hóngwǔ reign at considerable age. He never accepted Míng office. According to the *Tiānshùn* 3 (1459) postscript by **Zhèng Wénkāng** 鄭文康, Lǚ was repeatedly invited as *xùndǎo* but always declined. His other works (*Jìbáixuān*, *Zhúzhōu*, *Guītián gǎo* etc.) are referred to but no longer extant.
