Kuàibiàn 快辨 (Jp. Kaiben) was an eighteenth-century Shingon-school monk at the *Kongōsanmai-in* 金剛三昧院 on Kōyasan 高野山, host of the young [[法幢]] Hōdō during the latter's residence at the *Kongōsanmai*'s student-monks' hall. He wrote the preface (dated *Hōreki* 13 / 1763) to Hōdō's *Āpídámó Jùshě Lùn Jīgǔ* ([[KR6l0027]], T2252).
