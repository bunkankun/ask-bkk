# 瞿海如

Qú Hǎirú, late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of the Sino-Western Medical Hospital (中西醫醫院); reviewed the manuscript of [[KR3em034]] *Hóukē jīnyuè quánshū* 喉科金鑰全書 for [[勞乃宣]] Láo Nǎixuān prior to the *Xuāntǒng* 3 (1911) printing.
