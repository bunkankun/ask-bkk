Huáng Gōng 黃恭, an author of unknown dynasty and dates, attributed in the Kanripo corpus with the *Shísì zhōu jì* 十四州記 ([[KR2k0154]]). No biographical information survives. The work's citations in the Tang-era encyclopedias *Yìwén lèijù* 藝文類聚 and *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽 set a terminus ante quem in the Táng for the text's existence, but the author's period remains uncertain; a Wei-Jin or Southern dynasty date is consistent with the style and content of the surviving fragments.
