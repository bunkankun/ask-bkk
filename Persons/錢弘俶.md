# 錢弘俶
Qián Hóngchù (Qián Chù 錢俶, 929–988)

The fifth and last ruler of Wú-Yuè 吳越 (r. 947–978), and one of the most important Buddhist royal patrons of the Five Dynasties / early Sòng transition. His mission to Goryeo in 960–962 recovered substantial portions of the Tiāntái scholastic corpus — including Dìguān's 諦觀 ([[諦觀]]) *Tiāntái sìjiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]]) — which had been substantially lost during the Táng collapse, and made possible the Sòng Tiāntái revival under Sìmíng Zhīlǐ ([[知禮]]) and others.
