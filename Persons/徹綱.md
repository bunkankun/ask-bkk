# Chègāng 徹綱

Mid-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[通醉|Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695) in the Pòshān Hǎimíng 破山海明 sub-line of the Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟 tradition. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0415]] 《昭覺丈雪醉禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J27 B194, cut c. mid-to-late 1660s with 1657 Zuìlǐ front-prefaces and post-1663 Zhāojué content).

The generation-character **徹** (instead of the expected 行 or 本 of the standard Mìyún-line 33rd-generation conventions) suggests a Pò-shān-line Sichuan regional naming-convention, possibly influenced by the **Wǔdēng quánshū**'s alternative generation-poems for the 32-generation sequence. Other named dharma-heirs of Tōngzuì (**Yuèchuáng Cèméi** 月幢策眉, for example) likewise use generation-characters outside the standard Línjì-Mìyún sequence, consistent with the Pòshān line's regional-autonomy in naming practice.

Native place, lay surname, and precise lifedates unrecovered.

**Independent work.** [[KR6q0508]] 《佛冤禪師語錄》 12 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B371 — Chègāng's own *yǔlù* under the *hào* **Fóyuān** 佛冤 ("Buddha-Wronged"), compiled by *fǎsì* **Xìngchún** [[性純]] *děng biān*.
