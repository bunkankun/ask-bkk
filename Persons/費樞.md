Sòng scholar from Chéngdū 成都, courtesy name Bóshū 伯樞. CBDB ids 13977 and 686438 attest his name and dynasty but no lifedates. The *Sìkù* editors note that the beginning and end of his official career cannot be verified. He is principally known as the compiler of the *Liánlì zhuàn* 廉吏傳 ([[KR2g0021]]) — 114 short biographies of incorruptible officials from the Spring-and-Autumn period through SuíTáng, each closing with the editor's *lùnduàn* — one of the foundational Sòng instances of the moral-categorical *zhuànjì·zǒnglù* sub-genre. The original ten-*juàn* form attested in the *Shūlù jiětí* survives in the WYG only as a compressed two-*juàn* recension with the same 114 entries.
