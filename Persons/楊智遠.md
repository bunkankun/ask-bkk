Yáng Zhìyuǎn 楊智遠, Daoist of the *Xiāntánguàn* 仙壇觀 (the local Méixiānguàn 梅仙觀 at Nánqílǐ, Xuānfēng township, Fēngchéng 豐城 county, Jiāngxī), compiler of the *Méixiānguàn jì* 梅仙觀記 [[KR5b0305]] in the late thirteenth century — Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 909) date the work to ca. 1296. He is the temple custodian responsible for assembling the *shìshí* 事實 (biographical narrative of Méi Fú 梅福), local cult lore, surviving Sòng imperial-investiture documents (including Yuánfēng 元豐 5/1082 and Shàoxīng 紹興 2/1132 decrees), and Tang-period commemorative steles (notably Luó Yǐn's 梅先生碑) into a single shrine-gazetteer. No further biographical details are recorded; he is identified only by his temple affiliation in his compilation.
