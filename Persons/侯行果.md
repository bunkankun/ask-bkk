Hóu Xíngguǒ 侯行果 (also 侯果 Hóu Guǒ), Táng-period *Yìjīng* commentator. His readings — preserved in fragments through later citation — include a gloss of *Qián*'s *Tuàn zhuàn* phrase *dà míng zhōng shǐ* 大明終始 as "*dà míng* is the sun," revived in [[KR1a0150]] by [[沈起元]] Shěn Qǐyuán against the Sòng *Chéng zhuàn* and *Běnyì* readings.
