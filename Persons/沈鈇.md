Wànlì-era Míng official, b. **Zhāngpǔ** 漳浦 (Fújiàn) — sometimes given as **Yúnxiāo** 雲霄. *Jìnshì* of Wànlì 5 (1577); held the post of *Nánjīng Hùbù dū-chǔ zhǔ-shì* 南京户部督儲主事 (Director in the Nánjīng Ministry of Revenue's Granary-Supervisory bureau) when he co-edited and cut the *Míng Tàizǔ wénjí* 明太祖文集 [[KR4e0001]] with **Yáo Shìguān** [[姚士觀]] at the secondary capital Zhōngdū 中都 in Wànlì 14 (1586). Eventually rose to *Yán-suí xún-fǔ* 延綏巡撫 and is best known in late-Míng coastal history for his warning-memorials on the Dutch threat to Fújiàn in the 1620s. The catalog meta of [[KR4e0001]] spells the second graph **鉄** (a low-frequency variant); the *Sìkù tíyào* itself writes **鈇**, followed here. No confident CBDB match.
