Zhèng-jì 正繼. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě*) of **Gǔ-lín Zhì** [[智|Gǔ-lín Zhì]] (1623–1695) at **Shèng-jīng Fèng-tiān Bō-rě chán-sì** 盛京奉天般若禪寺 (Mukden). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0546]] 《盛京奉天般若古林禪師語錄》 6 juan, J38 B429, prefaced 1675.
