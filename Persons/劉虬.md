# Liú Qiú 劉虬

Southern-Qí lay Buddhist scholar (433–495) of Jīngzhōu 荊州, sometimes styled the *Jīngzhōu yǐnshì* 荊州隱士 ("hermit of Jīngzhōu"). Author of the celebrated *Wúliàngyì jīng xù* 無量義經序 (preface to the *Wúliàngyì jīng*, [[KR6d0118]]) whose seven-period (七階) classification of the Buddha's teaching became a foundational document of southern Sinitic Lotus exegesis, anticipating the Liáng-period exegesis of [[法雲]] Fǎyún, [[僧旻]] Sēngmín, and [[智藏]] Zhìzàng, and the Suí Tiāntái systematisation under [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ.
