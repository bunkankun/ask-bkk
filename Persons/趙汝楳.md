Zhào Rǔméi 趙汝楳 (lifedates not securely recorded; fl. mid-thirteenth century, late Lǐ-zōng / early Dù-zōng), of Biàn-shuǐ 汴水 (Kāi-fēng region, the *Sòngshǐ*-recorded ancestral home; resident in late-Sòng Lín-ān).

Imperial-clan member, seventh-generation descendant of the Northern-Sòng prince Shāng-wáng 商王 Zhào Yuán-fèn 趙元份. Youngest (4th) son of [[趙善湘]] (Zhào Shàn-xiāng), the *Zī-zhèng-diàn dà-xué-shì*. Held *Hù-bù shì-láng* 戶部侍郎 (Vice-Minister of Revenue) under Lǐ-zōng or early Dù-zōng.

CBDB id 19410. Documentary references: *Sòngshǐ* (PRC ed.; the imperial genealogies do not include him); *Níng-bō fǔ-zhì* 17.1096; Yuán Jué 袁桷's *Wén-jí* 33.6b.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0058]] *Zhōuyì jí wén* 周易輯聞 (6 juan; with appendices *Yì yǎ* 易雅 [Yǎ-style Glossary on the *Yì*] in 1 juan and *Shì zōng* 筮宗 [The Yarrow-Stalk Source-Doctrine] in 1 juan). Composed in his late-life maturity (1250s–1260s), consolidating and continuing the substantial *Yì*-corpus of his father Zhào Shàn-xiāng.

Methodologically distinctive for his text-critical reductionism: rejecting the *Shuō guà*, *Xù guà*, and *Zá guà* as Hàn-*ru* intrusions; treating the *Xìcí* as disciple-record rather than Confucius's text. The *Yì yǎ* glossary contains a methodologically clean *yǎn shù* / *jī shù* distinction that demotes the *Hé-tú-Luò-shū* numbers from canonical *shī*-procedure status while preserving their general-cosmology role.
