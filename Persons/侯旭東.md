Modern Chinese historian, professor at Tsinghua University, specialist in Northern-dynasties social history and popular Buddhism. Author of *Wǔ-liù shìjì běifāng mínzhòng fójiào xìnyǎng* 五、六世紀北方民眾佛教信仰 (1998), the standard study of stele-Buddhism and lay devotional culture in 5th–6th-century North China. Edited the apocryphal *Fó zài jīn-guān shàng zhǔ-lěi jīng* [[KR6v0054]] and *Rúlái zài jīn-guān zhǔ-lěi qīngjìng zhuāngyán jìngfú jīng* [[KR6v0055]] for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 4.
