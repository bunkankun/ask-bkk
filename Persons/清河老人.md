Late-Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Daoist known only from his hymns (*sòng* 頌) and undated preface to [[KR5a0088|Yuánshǐ wúliàng dùrén shàngpǐn miàojīng zhù 元始無量度人上品妙經註]] (DZ 88), styled "Elder of Qīnghé" — *Qīnghé* 清河 being a district created around 1270 within Huáiān prefecture 淮安府 (Jiāngsū 江蘇), near *Dōnghǎi* 東海, the locality of his master [[東海青元真人|Dōnghǎi Qīngyuán zhēnrén]]. His preface announces the publication of Qīngyuán's commentary as a "breakthrough" in *Dùrénjīng* understanding. No CBDB record exists. His *hào* also appears in the text at 1.16a and 2.17a, where his phrasing is noted for the vernacular demonstrative *zhègè* 這箇 — a Sòng-era colloquial diagnostic that, together with the *Qīnghé* toponym, anchors the text to the late thirteenth century.
