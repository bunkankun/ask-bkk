# Dēng-yìng 燈映 (Yě-yún Dēng-yìng 野雲燈映)

Qīng Chán master. *Hào* Yě-yún 野雲 ("Wild Cloud"). Lifedates unrecorded; active mid-to-late 17th century. Surviving work: the *Yě-yún Yìng chánshī zōng tǒng sòng* 野雲映禪師宗統頌 [[KR6q0237]] — a one-juan lineage-genealogical verse-commentary on the classical Línjì patriarchs.
