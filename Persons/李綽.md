Lǐ Chuò 李綽 (*fl.* late ninth century, lifedates uncertain), *zì* Jiān-mèng 肩孟. Of the Zhào-jùn 趙郡 (Hé-běi) Lǐ-shì *Nán zǔ* (southern progenitor) line, great-grandson of Lǐ Shū 李紓 (*Lì-bù shì-láng*) per the *Xīn Táng shū* *Zǎi-xiàng shì-xì biǎo*. Took refuge at Pǔ-tián 圃田 in Hé-nán during the *Huáng Cháo* rebellion of 880–884. Compiler of the *Shàng-shū gù shí* 尚書故實 ([[KR3j0084]]), a 1-juan late-Táng connoisseur's miscellany recording the family-traditions and connoisseur's knowledge of a senior Zhāng-clan *shàng-shū* (identified by the SKQS editors as falling within the *Zhāng Yàn-yuǎn* / *Lì-dài míng-huà-jì* generation). The catalog meta gives a *c.* 805–862 date-range, corresponding to the earlier-Lǐ-Chuò identification; this is not adopted in the work-note frontmatter, which follows the SKQS editors' later identification.
