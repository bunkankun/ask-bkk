Lǐ Chuò 李綽 (*fl.* late ninth century, lifedates uncertain), *zì* Jiānmèng 肩孟. Of the Zhàojùn 趙郡 (Héběi) Lǐshì *Nán zǔ* (southern progenitor) line, great-grandson of Lǐ Shū 李紓 (*Lìbù shìláng*) per the *Xīn Táng shū* *Zǎixiàng shìxì biǎo*. Took refuge at Pǔtián 圃田 in Hénán during the *Huáng Cháo* rebellion of 880–884. Compiler of the *Shàngshū gù shí* 尚書故實 ([[KR3j0084]]), a 1-juan late-Táng connoisseur's miscellany recording the family-traditions and connoisseur's knowledge of a senior Zhāng-clan *shàngshū* (identified by the SKQS editors as falling within the *Zhāng Yànyuǎn* / *Lìdài mínghuàjì* generation). The catalog meta gives a *c.* 805–862 date-range, corresponding to the earlier-Lǐ-Chuò identification; this is not adopted in the work-note frontmatter, which follows the SKQS editors' later identification.
