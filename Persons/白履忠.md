Bái Lǚzhōng 白履忠 (*hào* Liángqiū zǐ 梁丘子; fl. 722–729) was a court Daoist of the reign of Emperor Xuánzōng 玄宗 (r. 712–756) and the canonical Táng commentator on both the *Huángtíng nèijǐng* and *Huángtíng wàijǐng*. His biography is preserved in *Xīn Táng shū* 新唐書 192.5124, which records his commentaries on the two *Huángtíng* scriptures.

His surviving commentaries are:

- *Huángtíng nèijǐng yùjīng zhù* 黃庭內景玉經註 [[KR5b0086]] (DZ 402), 3 *juàn* — the canonical commentary on the *Nèijǐng* [[KR5b0015]], with two prefaces (Bái's own and that of the earlier Wǔchéng zǐ 務成子 commentary).
- *Huángtíng wàijǐng yùjīng zhù* 黃庭外景玉經註, 2 *juàn* — commentary on the *Wàijǐng* [[KR5b0016]], preserved only in DZ 263 *Xiūzhēn shíshū* 58–60.

The *hào* Liángqiū zǐ is the form under which Bái's commentaries are typically cited in later literature, including the *Huángtíng* citation-apparatus of the Sòng–Yuán period. No CBDB record.
