Zhēn-càn 真璨, *zì* **Dōng-yán** 東巖 ("East Cliff"); often referred to as **Huáng-lián Dōng-yán** 黃蓮東巖 from his principal abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673) — thus a co-disciple of **Sān-tǎ Zhǔ-fēng Yuán-fǎ** [[圓法]] (cf. [[KR6q0532]]). Born **明天啟癸亥 10/24** = **1623-11-16** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 40 in **Kāngxī yuán-nián** = **1662**, last fixed dating in his *yǔ-lù* is **辛丑** = **1661**. Native of **Xīn-ān-wèi** 新安衛 (Huī-zhōu region); lay surname **Bào** 鮑.

**Formation.** At 19 (1641) left home and traveled to **Jīn-líng** 金陵; en route met a wandering monk who urged him to practice. Tonsured at **Yí-xīng** 宜興 under **Jiàn-míng** 見明和尚. At 20 took full ordination at **Jīng-dū Qīng-liáng-sì** 京都清涼寺 under **Zhuān-yú** 顓愚和尚. Sequential *cān-fǎng*: **Lín-gāo** 林皋, **Shí-yǔ** 石雨, **Fèi-yǐn** 費隱; one day saw a monk pushed off a step by his master and rise to bow again, only to be pushed again — sudden insight. Continued *cān* under **Mù-yún** 牧雲 and **Yù-lín** 玉林. Final *fù-fǎ* under **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn** in **順治辛卯** = **1651**.

**Abbacies.** **Jiā-hé Jīn-míng-sì** 嘉禾金明寺, **Huáng-shān Méi-xī** 黃山梅溪, **Xīng-yuán Huáng-lián** 星源黃連 (entered **順治乙未 10/1** = **1655-10-29**), **Qí-zhōu Yǎng-tiān** 蘄州仰天.

**Disciples.** **Qí-zhōu Sān-jiǎo Lián-dù Yuān** 蘄州三角連度淵 and **Qí-shuǐ Wú-shān Chǔ-shí Xiù** 蘄水吳山楚石琇. Attendants: **Zhào-wù** 照務, **Zōng-zhì** 宗智. Personal recorder: **Jī-xiù** [[機琇]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B413, recorded by **Jī-xiù** *jì-lù*; with prefaces by **Lí Yuán-kuān** [[黎元寬]] (Nán-chāng, 1628 *jìn-shì*, *zì* 博菴) and **Wén Dé-yì** [[文德翼]]. The closing *xíng-shí* (autobiographical narration) was delivered to his disciples on **順治丁酉解夏** = **1657-08-23** (lunar Avalambana eve, end of summer retreat).

**Sources.** 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 102; 《徑石滴乳集》 (X67 n1308).
