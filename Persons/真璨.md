Zhēncàn 真璨, *zì* **Dōngyán** 東巖 ("East Cliff"); often referred to as **Huánglián Dōngyán** 黃蓮東巖 from his principal abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jièān Wùjìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673) — thus a co-disciple of **Sāntǎ Zhǔfēng Yuánfǎ** [[圓法]] (cf. [[KR6q0532]]). Born **明天啟癸亥 10/24** = **1623-11-16** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 40 in **Kāngxī yuánnián** = **1662**, last fixed dating in his *yǔlù* is **辛丑** = **1661**. Native of **Xīnānwèi** 新安衛 (Huīzhōu region); lay surname **Bào** 鮑.

**Formation.** At 19 (1641) left home and traveled to **Jīnlíng** 金陵; en route met a wandering monk who urged him to practice. Tonsured at **Yíxīng** 宜興 under **Jiànmíng** 見明和尚. At 20 took full ordination at **Jīngdū Qīngliángsì** 京都清涼寺 under **Zhuānyú** 顓愚和尚. Sequential *cānfǎng*: **Língāo** 林皋, **Shíyǔ** 石雨, **Fèiyǐn** 費隱; one day saw a monk pushed off a step by his master and rise to bow again, only to be pushed again — sudden insight. Continued *cān* under **Mùyún** 牧雲 and **Yùlín** 玉林. Final *fùfǎ* under **Jièān Wùjìn** in **順治辛卯** = **1651**.

**Abbacies.** **Jiāhé Jīnmíngsì** 嘉禾金明寺, **Huángshān Méixī** 黃山梅溪, **Xīngyuán Huánglián** 星源黃連 (entered **順治乙未 10/1** = **1655-10-29**), **Qízhōu Yǎngtiān** 蘄州仰天.

**Disciples.** **Qízhōu Sānjiǎo Liándù Yuān** 蘄州三角連度淵 and **Qíshuǐ Wúshān Chǔshí Xiù** 蘄水吳山楚石琇. Attendants: **Zhàowù** 照務, **Zōngzhì** 宗智. Personal recorder: **Jīxiù** [[機琇]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0533]] 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B413, recorded by **Jīxiù** *jìlù*; with prefaces by **Lí Yuánkuān** [[黎元寬]] (Nánchāng, 1628 *jìnshì*, *zì* 博菴) and **Wén Déyì** [[文德翼]]. The closing *xíngshí* (autobiographical narration) was delivered to his disciples on **順治丁酉解夏** = **1657-08-23** (lunar Avalambana eve, end of summer retreat).

**Sources.** 《黃蓮東巖禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 102; 《徑石滴乳集》 (X67 n1308).
