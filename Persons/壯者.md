**Zhuàngzhě** 壯者 is a pseudonym used by the otherwise unidentified author of *Sǎo Mí Zhǒu* 掃迷帚 [[KR4k0221]], a late-Qīng anti-superstition novel serialized in 1905. The name means "a vigorous man" or "a man of fortitude." No biographical details have been located; the true identity of this author remains unknown. CBDB contains no entry for this pseudonym.
