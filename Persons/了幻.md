# Liǎohuàn 了幻

Dharma-name of [[胡孝]] Hú Xiào, late-Míng former *jìnshì* who took up *yǒufǎ sēng* 有髮僧 ("haired-monk", lay-monastic) life at Wǔlín 武林 (Hángzhōu). Collator of [[KR6c0159]] (1587).
