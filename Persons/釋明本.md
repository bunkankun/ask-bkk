**Shì Míngběn** 釋明本 (1263–1323), commonly known as **Zhōngfēng Míngběn** 中峰明本 ("Central-Peak Míngběn"); imperial title **Pǔyìng guóshī** 普應國師, posthumous *shìhào* **Zhìjué chánshī** 智覺禪師. Layman's surname **Sūn** 孫. Native of **Qiántáng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu); lived at **Yàndàngcūn** 鴈蕩村. Took the tonsure at **Wúshān Shèngshuǐ Sì** 吳山聖水寺. Principal Yuán Lín-jì-school *huàtóu* Chán master, **Dharma-heir of Gāofēng Yuánmiào** 高峰原妙 of Mt. Tiānmù 天目山. Repeatedly declined invitations to head major monasteries (*míngshān zhǔxí*), retreated into hidden self-cultivation (*píngjì zìfàng*). Friend and Dharma-mentor of **Zhào Mèngfǔ** 趙孟頫 and Zhào's wife **Guǎn Dàoshēng** 管道昇; through Zhào, friend also of **Féng Zǐzhèn** ([[馮子振]]).

**Méihuā bǎi yǒng exchange.** When Zhào Mèngfǔ brought Míngběn to visit Féng Zǐzhèn — who had initially shown disdain — Féng produced his hundred plum-blossom heptasyllabic-quatrains; Míngběn answered them **piece-for-piece on the spot**, then produced his famous **九字梅花歌** *Jiǔzì méihuā gē* ("Nine-Character Plum-Blossom Song" — a tour-de-force in **nine-character lines**) in return. The two formed a friendship from that day. The exchange is preserved in [[KR4h0075]] *Méihuā bǎi yǒng*.

**Works.** *Zhōngfēng Míngběn Guǎnglù* 中峰明本廣錄 (the canonical record of his teachings) in 30 *juǎn* (T48n2026 *Tiānmù Zhōngfēng héshàng guǎnglù*) and the *Huànzhù Zhōngfēng Míngběn chánshī yǔlù* 幻住中峰明本禪師語錄. DILA Authority A002150. CBDB person 113216.
