**Shì Míng-běn** 釋明本 (1263–1323), commonly known as **Zhōng-fēng Míng-běn** 中峰明本 ("Central-Peak Míng-běn"); imperial title **Pǔ-yìng guó-shī** 普應國師, posthumous *shì-hào* **Zhì-jué chán-shī** 智覺禪師. Layman's surname **Sūn** 孫. Native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu); lived at **Yàn-dàng-cūn** 鴈蕩村. Took the tonsure at **Wú-shān Shèng-shuǐ Sì** 吳山聖水寺. Principal Yuán Lín-jì-school *huà-tóu* Chán master, **Dharma-heir of Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào** 高峰原妙 of Mt. Tiān-mù 天目山. Repeatedly declined invitations to head major monasteries (*míng-shān zhǔ-xí*), retreated into hidden self-cultivation (*píng-jì zì-fàng*). Friend and Dharma-mentor of **Zhào Mèng-fǔ** 趙孟頫 and Zhào's wife **Guǎn Dào-shēng** 管道昇; through Zhào, friend also of **Féng Zǐ-zhèn** ([[馮子振]]).

**Méi-huā bǎi yǒng exchange.** When Zhào Mèng-fǔ brought Míng-běn to visit Féng Zǐ-zhèn — who had initially shown disdain — Féng produced his hundred plum-blossom heptasyllabic-quatrains; Míng-běn answered them **piece-for-piece on the spot**, then produced his famous **九字梅花歌** *Jiǔ-zì méi-huā gē* ("Nine-Character Plum-Blossom Song" — a tour-de-force in **nine-character lines**) in return. The two formed a friendship from that day. The exchange is preserved in [[KR4h0075]] *Méi-huā bǎi yǒng*.

**Works.** *Zhōng-fēng Míng-běn Guǎng-lù* 中峰明本廣錄 (the canonical record of his teachings) in 30 *juǎn* (T48n2026 *Tiān-mù Zhōng-fēng héshàng guǎng-lù*) and the *Huàn-zhù Zhōng-fēng Míng-běn chán-shī yǔ-lù* 幻住中峰明本禪師語錄. DILA Authority A002150. CBDB person 113216.
