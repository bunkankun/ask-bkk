**Yú Gēnchū** 俞根初 (*zì* Tóngtián 桐田, 1734–1799, 清), Shàoxīng-region Qiánlóng-Jiāqìng-period physician, founder of the Shàoxīng *Shānghán* tradition that would become central to Zhèjiāng medicine through the late Qīng and Republican periods. His *Tōngsú Shānghán lùn* 通俗傷寒論 — composed in the late 18th century, circulating in manuscript and local prints — was edited and re-published in the early Republican period by [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén as the *Zēngdìng tōngsú Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef031]], 1916) and *Chóngdìng tōngsú Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef036]], 1934). Lifedates 1734–1799 are conventional. No CBDB record.
