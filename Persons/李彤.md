Lǐ Tóng 李彤 (fl. third or fourth century), a Jìn 晉 paleographer-lexicographer known principally as the compiler of two character-form glossaries: *Zì zhǐ* 字指 ([[KR1j0103]]) and *Jí gǔ zì* 集古字 / *Jí zì* 集字 / *Qí zì* 奇字 (all routinely cited together by Guō Zhōngshù 郭忠恕 in [[KR1j0026]] *Hànjiǎn*). The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records the *Zì zhǐ* in two *juàn*; all his works are lost, surviving only in Hànjiǎn-tradition paleographic citations. No biography in any standard history; sometimes speculatively placed at the Eastern-Jìn court but no firm identification. CBDB has no matching entry.
