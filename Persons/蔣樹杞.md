Republican-era physician, otherwise undocumented. Author of *Fú-wēn zhèng-zhì shí-yàn tán* 伏瘟證治實驗談 ([[KR3eg027]], "Clinical-Experience Talks on the Syndrome-Differentiation and Treatment of Latent Plague"), printed 1920 in the *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 collectanea edited by **Qiū Qìng-yún** 裘慶元. The work covers the aetiology, symptomatology, diagnosis, and treatment of *fú-wēn* 伏瘟 (latent-plague) — a sub-category of *fú-qì* 伏氣 latent-pathogen warm disease — and contains both clinical case material and doctrinal polemic, including (the *Zhōng yī cí diǎn* notes) a "one-sidedly disparaging" attitude toward Western medicine. No biographical material located.
