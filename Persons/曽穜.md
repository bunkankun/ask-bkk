Zēng Tóng 曽穜, Southern Sòng compiler of the *Dà Yì cuì yán* 大易粹言 in seventy juàn — an anthology of seven Sòng commentators' readings of the *Xìcí*, *Shuōguà*, *Xùguà*, and *Záguà*. The work was combined with [[陳友文]] Chén Yǒuwén's *Dà Yì jí yì* 大易集義 by [[徐乾學]] Xú Qiánxué (named to [[納喇性德]] Nàlǎ Xìngdé) into the Kāngxī-period *Hé dìng shān bǔ Dà Yì jí yì cuì yán* ([[KR1a0139]]).
