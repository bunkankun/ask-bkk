Táo Mó 陶模 (*zì* Zǐfāng 子方; 1835–1902) was a senior Qīng official: governor of Xīnjiāng 新疆, governor-general of Shǎn-Gān 陝甘 and later Liǎngguǎng 兩廣. He was the father of [[陶保廉]] Táo Bǎolián, who accompanied him to Lánzhōu in the 1880s–1890s; the *Yīxué dáwèn* ([[KR3eh030]]) was first printed in Lánzhōu in 1897 during Táo Mó's Shǎn-Gān governorship.
