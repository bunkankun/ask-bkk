Féng Yuàn 馮瑗 (fl. early 17th c.), younger brother of Féng Qí 馮琦 (the late-Míng *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*), native of Lín-qú 臨朐 in Shān-dōng. He is principally known for his posthumous editorial completion (with the disciples Zhōu Jiā-dòng 周家棟 and Wú Guāng-yì 吳光義) of Féng Qí's working manuscript of the *Jīng-jì lèi-biān* 經濟類編 ([[KR3k0046]]) in 100 *juan*. No separately-attributed work by Féng Yuàn survives.
