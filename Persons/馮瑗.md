Féng Yuàn 馮瑗 (fl. early 17th c.), younger brother of Féng Qí 馮琦 (the late-Míng *Lǐbù shàngshū*), native of Línqú 臨朐 in Shāndōng. He is principally known for his posthumous editorial completion (with the disciples Zhōu Jiādòng 周家棟 and Wú Guāngyì 吳光義) of Féng Qí's working manuscript of the *Jīngjì lèibiān* 經濟類編 ([[KR3k0046]]) in 100 *juan*. No separately-attributed work by Féng Yuàn survives.
