# 黃幼蘭 Huáng Yòulán

**Huáng Yòulán** 黃幼蘭, contemporary Chinese acupuncture historian; co-editor with [[黄龍祥]] Huáng Lóngxiáng of the standard *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng jiàoshì* 針灸大成校釋 (Beijing: Rénmín wèishēng, 1984), the modern critical edition of [[楊繼洲]] Yáng Jìzhōu's *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng* ([[KR3ee027]]).
