**Liú Xìnfǔ** 劉信甫 (*fl.* late 12th – early 13th c.). Southern-Sòng physician of uncertain native place. Author of the *Huórén shìzhèng fāng* 活人事證方 and its continuation *Huórén shìzhèng fāng hòují* 活人事證方後集 ([[KR3ed029]]), a syndrome-organised formulary with case-history attestation. His chronological anchor is the *Shàoxī rénzǐ* (1192) clinical anecdote preserved in his work, placing his *floruit* in the late Guāngzōng / early Níngzōng reigns. The final compilation date for the *Hòují* is conventionally given as 嘉定丙子 (1216). Not in CBDB; no biographical record outside his own work.
