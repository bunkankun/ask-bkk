# Zhìdù 智度 (Táng / Tiāntái)

A **different** Zhìdù: Táng-dynasty Tiāntái master, *shénzú* 神足 (foot-disciple) of **Jīngxī Zhànrán** 荊溪湛然 ([[湛然]], 711–782). DILA Authority A001265. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period c. 770s–early 800s. Although noted in the *Shìmén zhèngtǒng* 釋門正統 and the *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀 as a major Tiāntái figure, no biographical narrative survives. His sole surviving work is the **Fǎhuá jīng shū yìzuǎn** 法華經疏義纘 ([[KR6d0017]], X29n0594, 6 *juan*) — a Táng-period subcommentary on Zhìyǐ's *Wénjù* in the immediate succession to Zhànrán.

Source: *Fómíng* 752; X29n0594; DILA A001265.
