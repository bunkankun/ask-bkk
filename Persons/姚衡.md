Mid-Qīng official and bibliophile from Wúxīng 吳興 (Guī'ān 歸安), Zhèjiāng, the elder brother of [[姚晏]] Yáo Yàn and father of [[姚覲元]] Yáo Jìn-yuán. CBDB id 514465 (index year 1788). His own preface to [[KR3em029]] *Yān-hóu mài-zhèng tōng-lùn*, written from the *Yǔ-chūn-guǎn* 雨春館 of the *fǔshǐzhě* 撫使者 (acting administration commissioner) yamen in Guǎngzhōu in the leap-month of *Dàoguāng* 21 (1841), indicates that he held an administrative post in Guǎngdōng at that date; he there records that his courtesy name is 聖常 (named by his younger brother as 家兄聖常). The 1841 Guǎngzhōu re-cutting of *Yān-hóu mài-zhèng tōng-lùn*, undertaken because the earlier *Dàoguāng* 5 blocks had not circulated widely, is his principal known editorial intervention.
