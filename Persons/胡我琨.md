Late-Míng numismatist; *zì* Zìyù 自玉. Biography not transmitted; the *Sìkù* editors note that "his rank and place are unknown." Dated by internal evidence to the late-Wànlì through Chóngzhēn era. Author of the *Qián tōng* 錢通 ([[KR2m0046]]), the most comprehensive surviving pre-Qīng Chinese monograph on coinage.
