Early-Kāngxī physician of Yùtián 玉田 (Hèběi) or possibly Húběi; *zì* Xuéyuān 學淵, *hào* Bǐlín 筆林. Active around 1700–1715. Author of [[KR3eb034]] *Mài guàn* (1711, 9 juan), a comprehensive integrating pulse synthesis. Otherwise lightly documented; no CBDB record.
