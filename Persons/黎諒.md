Lí Liàng 黎諒 (Míng), prefect of *Wēnzhōu* during the *Jiājìng* era. Edited and printed [[KR4d0273]] *Shuǐxīn jí* in the form that became the textual ancestor of the WYG recension of Yè Shì's [[葉適]] collected works, after the original Sòng cuts had become rare. CBDB no record.
