Jiāng Qīng 薑清 was a Míng-dynasty author active likely in the Jiājìng 嘉靖 era (r. 1522–1566). He is the compiler of the *Jiāng Shì Mìshǐ* 薑氏秘史 ([[KR4k0053]]), a historical novel on the Jiànwén 建文 emperor's reign and the Jǐngnán 靖難 usurpation. No further biographical details are known; he does not appear in CBDB or in standard Ming biographical sources. The surname in the title ("薑氏") identifies the work as his.
