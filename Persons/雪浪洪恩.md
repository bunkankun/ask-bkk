# 雪浪洪恩

Late-Míng poet-monk of the Jīnlíng (Nánjīng) Bìfēngsì 碧峰寺. Celebrated literary figure of the Wànlì-era monastic-literati circles. Teacher of [[如愚]] Rúyú, who joined his Jīnlíng Bìfēngsì community as a poetry-disciple.
