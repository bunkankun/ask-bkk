# 本覺守一

Northern Sòng Yúnmén-school Chán master in the Xuědòu 雪竇 lineage descended from [[雪竇重顯]] Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn (980–1052). Named in the opening verse of [[大隱]] Dàyǐn's *Dà bōrě jīng guānfǎ* ([[KR6c0227]]) as the third-generation Xuědòu descendant, whose monastic engagement with the *Mahāprajñāpāramitā* tradition fed forward into Dàyǐn's fourth-generation *guān-fǎ* compilation. No independent biographical record located.
