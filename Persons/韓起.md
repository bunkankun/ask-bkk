Hán Qǐ 韓起 (Hán Xuānzǐ 韓宣子), Chūnqiū-period statesman of Jìn 晉. The *Zuǒ zhuàn* records that on visiting Lǔ 魯 he saw the *Yì xiàng* 易象 and the *Chūnqiū* — cited in [[KR1a0158]]'s *Sìkù* notice as evidence that the *Yì*'s symbol-tradition was extant and authoritative in pre-canonical antiquity.
