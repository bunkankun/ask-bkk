**Qiú Qìngyuán** 裘慶元 (*zì* Jíshēng 吉生, 1873–1947, 民國), Shàoxīng-region Republican-era physician, editor, and medical publisher. His most important contribution is the editing and publication of the *Sānsān yī shū* 三三醫書 — a 99-title anthology of short and minor Qīng / Republican medical works, published in three series (1924, 1925, 1936) — which preserves a substantial body of medical writing that would otherwise have been lost. He also edited the *Zhēn běn yī shū jí chéng* 珍本醫書集成 (in 90 titles, 1936), another major preservation anthology, and was a contemporary collaborator of [[惲鐵樵]] Yùn Tiěqiáo in the early-Republican Chinese-medicine reform movement.
