Mid-Táng grand councilor and ritual director under Xuánzōng; descendant of the Lánlíng Xiāo lineage, native of Kuàijī (modern Shàoxīng). *Zì* Qiáofǔ 喬甫; posthumous name Wénxiàn 文獻. Took office through hereditary privilege; rose through the *Zhōngshūshěng* and *Ménxiàshěng* under Xuánzōng to become *Tóng Zhōngshū Ménxià sānpǐn* and *Zhōngshū lìng* in 729. Distinguished himself as commander of the Héxī campaign against Tibet (727–729) and as a moderating voice on court ritual policy. He was the principal director of compilation of the *Dà Táng Kāiyuán Lǐ* 大唐開元禮 ([[KR2m0021]]), completed in *Kāiyuán* 20 (732). Eventually retired to *Tàizǐ tàishī* and died in 749.
