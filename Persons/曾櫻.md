# Zēng Yīng 曾櫻

Late-Míng official, *hào* **Èryún** 二雲, *fēnxiàn* (prefect-circuit official) of southern Quánzhōu in the late-Wànlì / Chóngzhēn period. Patron of [[元賢]] Yǒngjué Yuánxián at the Quánzhōu Kāiyuánsì 泉州開元寺; commissioned Yuánxián's *Lèngyán jīng lüèshū* ([[KR6j0710]]) c. 1636.
