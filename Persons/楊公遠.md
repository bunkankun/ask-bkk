Yáng Gōng-yuǎn 楊公遠 (1228 — after 1286), *zì* Shū-míng 叔明, native of Shè-xiàn 歙縣 in Huī-zhōu 徽州 (Ān-huī). An amateur painter and minor literary figure of the late-Sòng / early-Yuán Huī-zhōu poetic network, attached to Fāng Huí [[方回]] and Wú Lóng-hàn [[吳龍翰]] (whose 1270 preface and 1285 postscript respectively are preserved in this collection). Did not formally take Yuán office after the Sòng's fall in 1276 but maintained extensive social-poetic contact with Yuán-period officials; the *Sìkù* editors distinguish him from the stricter *yí-mín* like Zhōu Mì on the basis of his apparent equanimity at the 1279 Yái-shān catastrophe and his presentation poems to Yuán administrators. His sole surviving work is the *Yě-qù yǒu-shēng huà* [[KR4d0429]] in two *juàn* (originally one *juàn*, the second posthumously supplemented). CBDB person 35322.
