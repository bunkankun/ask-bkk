Mid-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) Buddhist monk, sobriquet **Yúnfēng dàorén** 雲峰道人 ("Cloud-Peak Way-Person"). Lifedates unrecorded. Per the preface to *Jīngāng jīng zhùshì* 金剛經註釋 ([[KR6c0082]], X25 no. 494), the layman **Zǐzhēn** [[子真]] (= **Gǔkǒu Zǐzhēn** 谷口子真) invited Pǔrén on **Kāngxī *xīnchǒu* 二月十九 = 1721-03-17** to the *xīnyì* 新邑 to set up a teaching platform for the *Vajracchedikā*; over **40 days, ending Kāngxī 60 / 4 / 9 (1721-05-04)**, Pǔrén delivered the lecture series whose transcription Zǐzhēn's *jīshū* 乩書 (planchette / spirit-writing) fixed as the present text — making this an unusual joint Buddhist-spirit-writing composition. The catalog credit-line for [[KR6c0082]] attributes Pǔrén as the *jīshū* (spirit-writing-recorder) and Zǐzhēn as *jīdìng* (spirit-writing-collator).
