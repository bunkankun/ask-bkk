Mid-Qīng (Kāng-xī-era) Buddhist monk, sobriquet **Yún-fēng dào-rén** 雲峰道人 ("Cloud-Peak Way-Person"). Lifedates unrecorded. Per the preface to *Jīn-gāng jīng zhù-shì* 金剛經註釋 ([[KR6c0082]], X25 no. 494), the layman **Zǐ-zhēn** [[子真]] (= **Gǔ-kǒu Zǐ-zhēn** 谷口子真) invited Pǔ-rén on **Kāng-xī *xīn-chǒu* 二月十九 = 1721-03-17** to the *xīn-yì* 新邑 to set up a teaching platform for the *Vajracchedikā*; over **40 days, ending Kāng-xī 60 / 4 / 9 (1721-05-04)**, Pǔ-rén delivered the lecture series whose transcription Zǐ-zhēn's *jī-shū* 乩書 (planchette / spirit-writing) fixed as the present text — making this an unusual joint Buddhist-spirit-writing composition. The catalog credit-line for KR6c0082 attributes Pǔ-rén as the *jī-shū* (spirit-writing-recorder) and Zǐ-zhēn as *jī-dìng* (spirit-writing-collator).
