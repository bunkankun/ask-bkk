# Pǔzhuāng 普莊

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Jìngzhōng 敬中, *hào* Dāiān 呆菴 (also 敬菴). 60th列祖 of Jìngshān (in the Xīngshèng Wànshòu chánsì 徑山興聖萬壽禪寺 abbot-list). Native of Xiānjū 仙居, lay surname Yuán 袁; his mother is said to have dreamt of an Indian monk entering her chamber before his birth.

Born on the 12th of the 8th month of Zhì-zhèng 7 (25 September 1347; DILA conventionally dates the year 至正丙戌 given in the *tǎ-míng*, which is Zhì-zhèng 6 / 1346 by the cycle, to 1347 — one of the standard textual cruxes). Sought ordination at twelve; was taken to Tiān-tóng-sì through a paternal-uncle and entrusted to Zuǒ-ān Yuán-liáng 左菴原良 禪師, under whom he was formally tonsured in Zhì-zhèng 19 (1359) aged thirteen. Unable to settle the great matter there, he went to Tiān-níng chán-sì 天寧禪寺 to meet [[惟一]] Liǎo-táng Wéi-yī 了堂惟一 — and attained awakening when, trying to speak on the *tíng-qián bǎi-shù-zǐ* 庭前柏樹子 *huà-tóu*, Wéi-yī struck him directly across the mouth. He returned to Tiān-tóng to serve Zuǒ-ān as *diǎn-zàng-yào* 典藏鑰, then — in Hóng-wǔ 10 (1377), after the Míng court issued an edict calling all monks to teach the *Heart*, *Laṅkāvatāra*, and *Diamond* sūtras — was engaged by Xìng-yuán Huì-míng 性原慧明 at Jīn-shān Lóng-yóu-sì to expound these scriptures. His first abbacy came in Hóng-wǔ 12 (1379) at Fǔ-zhōu Běi-chán-sì 撫州北禪寺; from there he moved to Yún-jū Zhēn-rú 雲居真如. In Hóng-wǔ 26 (1393) an imperial edict installed him as 60th abbot of Jìng-shān Xīng-shèng Wàn-shòu. He died on the 23rd of the 10th month of Yǒng-lè 1 (16 November 1403) at the Bù-dòng-xuān 不動軒, *shì-shòu* 58, *sēng-là* 45, with the enigmatic parting words "Hard, hard — a sixteen-year-old beauty on a mountain too high for this old monk to carry her up" 難難二八嬌娘上高山老僧扶不得.

As a *ménrén* 門人 under Wéiyī, co-edited the four-juan *Liǎotáng Wéiyī chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0350]]). His own eight-juan *yǔlù* survives as *Dāiān Pǔzhuāng chánshī yǔlù* 呆菴普莊禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0351]], X71 n1418), compiled by his dharma-heir [[慧啟]] Huìqǐ and others. Principal biographical sources: the *tǎmíng* in *juan* 8 of his own *yǔlù*; *Wǔdēng quánshū* *juan* 58; *Zēngjí Xùchuándēnglù* *juan* 6.
