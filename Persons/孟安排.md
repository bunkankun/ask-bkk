**Mèng Ānpái** 孟安排 (*fl.* late 7th century) was a Táng Daoist scholar, compiler of [[KR5e0031]] ***Dào jiào yì shū*** 道教義樞 — an authoritative **doctrinal encyclopedia of Daoist concepts**, drawn up in 10 *juàn* for systematic study. One of the foundational reference works for Táng Daoist doctrinal categories.
