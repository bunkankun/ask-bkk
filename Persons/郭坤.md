# Guō Kūn 郭坤

Mid-Míng official; son of [[郭定靜]] Guō Dìngjìng. Magistrate of Lántián 蘭田; in 1506 (*Zhèngdé* 1, *bǐngyín*) had his father's manuscript copy of the paediatric compendium *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]]) cut to blocks and printed with prefaces by [[王云鳳]] Wáng Yúnfèng and [[蕭謙]] Xiāo Qiān. Lifedates unrecorded.
