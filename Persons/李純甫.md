# Lǐ Chúnfǔ 李純甫

Jīn-dynasty layman and Chán practitioner, style PíngShān XiánXián 屏山閑閑; intermediate teacher of [[耶律楚材]] Yēlü Chǔcái between Yēlü's Shèng'ān studies and his definitive turn to [[行秀|Wànsōng Xíngxiù]] 萬松行秀.
