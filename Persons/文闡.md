# Wénchǎn 文闡

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Known only from this editorial credit. Lifedates unrecorded.
