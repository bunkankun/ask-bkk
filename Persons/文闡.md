# Wén-chǎn 文闡

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Known only from this editorial credit. Lifedates unrecorded.
