Bái Yúnjì 白雲霽, *zì* Míngzhī 明之, *hào* Zàixūzǐ 在虛子, a late-Míng Daoist scholar from Shàngyuán 上元 (Jiāngsū). Compiler of the *Dàozàng mùlù xiángzhù* 道藏目錄詳註, a four-juan annotated catalogue of the *Dào zàng* completed in *Tiānqǐ* 6 (1626). Cited by the Sìkù *tiyao* to *Dàyì xiàngshù gōushēn tú* [[KR1a0080]] for the (mistaken) attribution of that work to [[劉牧]] Liú Mù. Lifedates not securely recorded.
