Jiāngdǎo Huìjiào 江島惠教 / Ejima Yasunori (1934–1999) — modern Japanese Buddhologist; specialist on Bhāviveka and Madhyamaka; author of *Chūgan-shisō no tenkai: Bhāvaviveka kenkyū* (1980).
