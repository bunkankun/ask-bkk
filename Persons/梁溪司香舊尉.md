**Liángxī Sīxiāng Jiùwèi** 梁溪司香舊尉 is the pen name of the anonymous author of *Hǎishàng Chén Tiān Yǐng* 海上塵天影 [[KR4k0123]], composed in 1894–1895. The pen name translates as "the former garrison officer who burns incense at Wúxī" — Liángxī 梁溪 being an ancient toponym for Wúxī 無錫 in Jiāngsū. The sobriquet Sīxiāng 司香 ("supervisor of incense") is likely an honorific or literary alias. The true identity of the author has not been established. The pen name appears as a character name within the novel itself, suggesting self-insertion by the author into the narrative.
