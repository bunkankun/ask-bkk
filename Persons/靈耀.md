Early- to mid-Qīng Buddhist monk, *zì* **Quán-zhāng** 全彰 (commonly cited as **Líng-yào Quán-zhāng** 靈耀全彰). Lifedates unrecorded. Composed *Jīn-gāng jīng bù-zhǐ* 金剛經部旨 ([[KR6c0081]], X25 no. 493), a two-juan structural-doctrinal exposition of the *Vajracchedikā* arranged under a hierarchical Tiāntái-style outline (一總題 / 二別文 / 序分 / 正宗 / 流通) — a representative late example of the long Tiāntái commentary tradition on the sūtra. Distinguish from at least one other Qīng monk also called **Líng-yào** (e.g. **Líng-yào Jìng-huì** 靈耀淨慧, A?, separate person) — DILA disambiguates.
