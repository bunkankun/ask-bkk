**Liú Fǎng** 劉昉 (*zì* Fāng-míng 方明, c. 1100–1150), Southern-Sòng *jìn-shì* and official, native of Jiē-yáng 揭陽 (Guǎng-dōng). *Jìn-shì* of the *Xuān-hé* 6 (1124) cohort. Served in several civil-military posts before being appointed *Tán-zhōu shuài* 潭州帥 (military commissioner of Tán-zhōu, modern Cháng-shā 長沙) in the *Shào-xīng* era. While in office he commissioned and personally directed the compilation of *Yòuyòu xīnshū* 幼幼新書 ([[KR3ej010]]), an encyclopaedic forty-*juǎn* digest of all paediatric materials in the Chinese medical canon up to his time. He died at Tán-zhōu in the autumn of *gēng-wǔ* 庚午 = 1150, with thirty-eight of the forty *juǎn* completed; the work was completed by his successor Lóu Shú 樓璹 in the eleventh month of the same year.
