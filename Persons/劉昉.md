**Liú Fǎng** 劉昉 (*zì* Fāngmíng 方明, c. 1100–1150), Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* and official, native of Jiēyáng 揭陽 (Guǎngdōng). *Jìnshì* of the *Xuānhé* 6 (1124) cohort. Served in several civil-military posts before being appointed *Tánzhōu shuài* 潭州帥 (military commissioner of Tánzhōu, modern Chángshā 長沙) in the *Shàoxīng* era. While in office he commissioned and personally directed the compilation of *Yòuyòu xīnshū* 幼幼新書 ([[KR3ej010]]), an encyclopaedic forty-*juǎn* digest of all paediatric materials in the Chinese medical canon up to his time. He died at Tánzhōu in the autumn of *gēngwǔ* 庚午 = 1150, with thirty-eight of the forty *juǎn* completed; the work was completed by his successor Lóu Shú 樓璹 in the eleventh month of the same year.
