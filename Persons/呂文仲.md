Lǚ Wén-zhòng 呂文仲 (*fl.* late 10th c.) of Huīzhōu 徽州, was a Sòng Hànlín *dài-zhào* 翰林待詔 and member of the editorial commission for [[KR3l0118]] *Tàipíng guǎngjì* (978) and *Tàipíng yù lǎn* under Lǐ Fǎng's directorship. His CBDB record (id 3659) cites the *Huīzhōu fǔ-zhì* 徽州府志 and *Sòng rén zhuàn-jì zī-liào suǒ-yǐn* (CBD) 2.1191–2 for biographical sources.
