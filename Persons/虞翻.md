# Yú Fān 虞翻

*Zì* Zhòngxiáng 仲翔. Native of Yúyáo 餘姚 in Kuàijī 會稽 (modern Zhèjiāng). Late-Hàn / Wú-state official and the single most influential exponent of the Hàn-period *xiàngshù* 象數 ("image-and-number") *Yì* hermeneutic — characterised by *huà tǐ* 化體 (line-mutation), *páng tōng* 旁通 (lateral-trigram permutation), *zhī bǐ guà* 之卦 (hexagram-transformation), and *nà jiǎ* 納甲 (sexagenary-cycle correlation). Served the Sūn 孫 court of Wú; banished to Jiāozhōu 交州 (modern northern Vietnam) after offending Sūn Quán 孫權, and is said to have continued teaching the *Yì* there until his death.

His *Yì* commentary is lost as an independent work but survives in extensive citation in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* 周易集解 ([[KR1a0008]]) — the largest single block of Hàn-style *xiàngshù* material in the received tradition. Reconstructed in the Qīng by [[張惠言]] Zhāng Huìyán in his *Yú shì Yì* 虞氏易 (1808 preface), which became the standard scholar's text for Wú-school Hàn *Yì*. *Sānguó zhì · Wúzhì* (juan 57) carries his biography.
