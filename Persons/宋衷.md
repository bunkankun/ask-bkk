# Sòng Zhōng 宋衷

*Zì* Zhòngzǐ 仲子 (also written 宋忠). Late Eastern Hàn 東漢 / early Wèi 魏 classicist, native of Nányáng 南陽 (modern Hénán); a leading member of the [[劉表]] Liú Biǎo court's scholarly circle in Jīngzhōu. Author of *Zhōuyì zhù* 周易註 and *Tài xuán zhù* 太玄註; both lost in transmission but preserved in fragments through [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). Known for *xiàngshù* 象數 readings of the *Yì*.
