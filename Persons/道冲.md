# Dào-chōng 道冲

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Cáo-yuán Dào-shēng 曹源道生. Compiler (*biān* 編) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0309]]. Lifedates unrecorded.

Distinct from the homonymous Míng-Qīng transitional [[道沖|Dào-chōng]] 道沖 (with 沖 not 冲) who was a disciple of Lóng-guāng Dá-fū Yùn-shàng 龍光達夫蘊上 and editor of [[KR6q0206]].
