# Dàochōng 道冲

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Cáoyuán Dàoshēng 曹源道生. Compiler (*biān* 編) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0309]]. Lifedates unrecorded.

Distinct from the homonymous Míng-Qīng transitional [[道沖|Dàochōng]] 道沖 (with 沖 not 冲) who was a disciple of Lóngguāng Dáfū Yùnshàng 龍光達夫蘊上 and editor of [[KR6q0206]].
