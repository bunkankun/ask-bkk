Fēng Yǎn 封演 (*fl.* 756–805; lifedates uncertain). Native place unclear, but of the Bó-hǎi Tiáo-xiàn 渤海蓚縣 Fēng-clan; the *Táng shū* *Zǎi-xiàng shì-xì biǎo* does not list him, suggesting he was of the more distant branches. Tài-xué student during the *Tiān-bǎo* (742–756) period; *jìnshì* in the early *Dà-lì* (766–779) reign; rose to *Cháo-sàn dà-fū, Jiǎn-jiào shàng-shū lì-bù láng-zhōng* concurrent with *Yù-shǐ zhōng-chéng*; ended his career as prefect (*cì-shǐ*) of Xíng-zhōu 邢州 under *De-zōng* (785–805). Compiler of the *Fēng-shì wén jiàn jì* 封氏聞見記 ([[KR3j0083]]), one of the most informationally substantial and methodologically rigorous of all Táng *bǐjì*.
