Fēng Yǎn 封演 (*fl.* 756–805; lifedates uncertain). Native place unclear, but of the Bóhǎi Tiáoxiàn 渤海蓚縣 Fēng-clan; the *Táng shū* *Zǎixiàng shìxì biǎo* does not list him, suggesting he was of the more distant branches. Tàixué student during the *Tiānbǎo* (742–756) period; *jìnshì* in the early *Dàlì* (766–779) reign; rose to *Cháosàn dàfū, Jiǎnjiào shàngshū lìbù lángzhōng* concurrent with *Yùshǐ zhōngchéng*; ended his career as prefect (*cìshǐ*) of Xíngzhōu 邢州 under *De-zōng* (785–805). Compiler of the *Fēngshì wén jiàn jì* 封氏聞見記 ([[KR3j0083]]), one of the most informationally substantial and methodologically rigorous of all Táng *bǐjì*.
