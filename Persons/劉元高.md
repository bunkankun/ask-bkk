# Liú Yuángāo 劉元高 (fl. 1265–1270)

Late-Southern-Sòng *Yùshǐ* 御史 (Censor) and descendant of the Yúnzhōu Liú lineage of Liú Huàn 劉渙, Liú Shù [[劉恕]], and Liú Xīzhòng. He compiled the joint *Sān Liú jiā jí* 三劉家集 [[KR4h0030]] (one *juǎn*) in the *Xiánchún* (1265–1274) period — the last decade of the Southern Sòng — preserving the surviving fragments of his ancestors' literary remains by then almost entirely scattered. No further biographical detail recoverable; no CBDB entry.
