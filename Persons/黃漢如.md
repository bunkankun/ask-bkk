Huáng Hànrú 黃漢如 (early-Republican, *fl.* 1920s–1930s, Shànghǎi). A practitioner of *yīzhǐchán tuīná* 一指禪推拿 (One-Finger Chán medical massage), a Cháng-jiāng-delta single-thumb-pressure tradition with strong *nèigōng* 內功 underpinnings. By his own account a minor Qīng official ("一官瓠系") who turned to medicine as his sole profession only *after Xīnhài* 辛亥 (1911). Author of *Yīzhǐchán tuīná shuōmíngshū* 一指禪推拿說明書 (manual on One-Finger Chán *tuīná*; ten editions by the late 1920s) and [[KR3eq078]] *Huángshì yīhuà* 黃氏醫話 (collected case-records-with-discussion). His son **Yīzhào** 一照 and wife **Hànyún** 漢芸 were associated with his Shànghǎi clinical practice. Not in CBDB.
