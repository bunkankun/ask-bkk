Huáng Hàn-rú 黃漢如 (early-Republican, *fl.* 1920s–1930s, Shàng-hǎi). A practitioner of *yī-zhǐ-chán tuī-ná* 一指禪推拿 (One-Finger Chán medical massage), a Cháng-jiāng-delta single-thumb-pressure tradition with strong *nèi-gōng* 內功 underpinnings. By his own account a minor Qīng official ("一官瓠系") who turned to medicine as his sole profession only *after Xīn-hài* 辛亥 (1911). Author of *Yī-zhǐ-chán tuī-ná shuō-míng-shū* 一指禪推拿說明書 (manual on One-Finger Chán *tuī-ná*; ten editions by the late 1920s) and [[KR3eq078]] *Huáng-shì yī-huà* 黃氏醫話 (collected case-records-with-discussion). His son **Yī-zhào** 一照 and wife **Hàn-yún** 漢芸 were associated with his Shàng-hǎi clinical practice. Not in CBDB.
