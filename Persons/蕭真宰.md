Southern-Sòng official in Sichuan, author of [[KR5a0119|Huángdì yīnfú jīng jiěyì 黃帝陰符經解義]] (DZ 119) — a commentary on the *Yīnfú jīng* originally in three juan (per *Tōngzhì* "Yìwén lüè" 5.4b), of which the commentary on the first paragraph is no longer extant. Xiāo Zhēnzǎi critically rejects the long 400-word version of the *Yīnfú jīng* in favour of the short 300-word version, citing extensively from *Zhuāngzǐ*, *Lièzǐ*, *Lúnyǔ*, and *Mèngzǐ*.
