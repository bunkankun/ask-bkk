# Míngyī 明一

Late-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[秀|Zhīyán Xiù]] 芝巖秀 in the Mìyún → Línyě → Xíngmì → Xiù sub-lineage. Head compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0431]] 《芝巖秀禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B213). The 明-generation character places him at the 35th-generation Línjì tier — one generation below Xiù's 超 generation.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under his name.

Sources: [[KR6q0431]] juan-head signature line "*sìfǎ ménrén Míngyī děng biān* 嗣法門人明一等編".

Nara-to-early-Heian-era Japanese **Hossō** 法相 monk. *Hōbōgirin* TP595 (after *Hokke-kō shoshō* IV) places him at **Tōdai-ji** 東大寺 of Nara — the imperial Hossō-Kegon centre — where he is associated with the late-Nara consolidation of the Hossō (East Asian Yogācāra) tradition. His dates are conventionally given as **728–798** in Japanese reference works, though direct primary documentation is sparse and the dates carry some uncertainty.

Two works survive securely under his name in the East Asian canon:

- [[KR6d0034]] *Fǎhuá lüèchāo* 法華略抄 (T2188) — a brief excerpting commentary on the *Lotus Sūtra*.
- [[KR6i0319]] *Jīnguāngmíng zuìshèngwáng jīng zhùshì* 金光明最勝王經註釋 (T2197) — a ten-fascicle annotated commentary on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa-sūtra* (Yìjìng recension, T665), compiled mainly from the *shū* (疏) of the Tang Hossō master Huìzhāo 惠沼 (651–714) with supplementary materials from Shèngzhuāng 勝莊 of Da-jianfu-si and Kyŏnghŭng 憬興 of Silla.
