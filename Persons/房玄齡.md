Fáng Xuánlíng 房玄齡 (578–648), *zì* Qiáo 喬, posthumously *Wénzhāo* 文昭, was the leading early-Táng minister and Chancellor under Tang Tài-zōng. Native of Línzī 臨菑 (Shān-dōng). One of the Lǐ Shìmín *Xuán-wǔ-mén* faction; rose to *Shàng-shū yòu pú-shè* (Right Vice-Director) and was central to the Zhēn-guān reign. Co-supervised the imperial *Suí shū* 隋書 compilation. The *Guǎn-zǐ* commentary attributed to him (KR3c0001) is the standard Táng-period commentary on the *Guǎn-zǐ*. Biography: *Jiù Táng shū* j. 66, *Xīn Táng shū* j. 96. CBDB id 17130.
