# Xú Jìngkě 徐敬可

Early-Qīng layman, co-sponsor (with [[王庭|Wáng Tíng]] 王庭 and [[施易修|Shī Yìxiū]] 施易修) of the 1672 publication of [[KR6q0215]] *Xūzhōu chánshī zhù Bā shí guī jǔ sòng*. Lifedates unrecorded.
