Late-Tang Esoteric Buddhist (Tángmì 唐密) master, fl. 880s–890s; ordained as **Sān-zàng shā-mén** 三藏沙門 ("Tripiṭaka monk") at **Dà-ān-guó-sì** 大安國寺 ("Great State-Pacification Monastery") in the Tang capital **Shàng-dū** 上都 (Cháng-ān 長安); honored with the imperial-bestowed monastic title **Chuán mì-jiào Chāo-wù dà-shī** 傳密教超悟大師 ("Esoteric Teaching Transmitting Master of Surpassing Awakening") and the *zǐ* 紫 (purple-robe, *cì zǐ* 賜紫) imperial honor.

Compiler (集 *jí*) of the **Shì-jiào zuì-shàng-chéng mì-mì-zàng tuó-luó-ní jí** 釋教最上乘秘密藏陀羅尼集 [[KR6j0762]] (F28n1071, H068n1619) — a 30-fascicle systematic anthology of dhāraṇīs organized by deity-class, completed in the cyclical year *wù-wǔ* 戊午 of *Qián-níng* 5 of the Great Tang = **898 CE**. The work survives only in the Fáng-shān 房山 stone-sutra inscriptions at Yún-jū-sì 雲居寺 (Hebei) and is the principal witness to the institutional survival of Esoteric Buddhism at Cháng-ān after the Huì-chāng persecution of 845.

DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority A000420; native place, lineage-master, life-dates, and disciples are not recorded in extant sources. Not the same person as the *Yìnyuè Xínglín* 印月行霖 (DILA A006917), a different historical figure.

Source: DILA A000420; *Shìjiào zuìshàngchéng mìmìzàng tuóluóní jí* preface (898 CE).
