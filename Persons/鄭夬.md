Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* writer of Jiāngnán, *zì* Yángtíng 揚庭. The principal target of [[邵伯溫]] Shào Bówēn's *Yìxué biànhuò* ([[KR1a0017]]), where he is depicted as having stolen a manuscript copy of [[邵雍]] Shào Yōng's *xiāntiān* 先天 numerological materials from the deathbed of [[王天悅]] Wáng Tiānyuè (one of Shào's two student-confidants). On this purloined basis he is said to have composed *Yì zhuàn* 易傳, *Yì cè* 易測, *Míng fàn* 明範, *Shíyòng shū* 時用書, and *Wǔjīng míng yòng shù shū* 五經明用數書 — works recorded in the *Sòngshǐ Yìwén zhì* and the *Shūlù jiětí* but all now lost. [[司馬光]] Sīmǎ Guāng's *Jí* preserves a memorial *Jìn Zhèng Guài Yì cè zhāzǐ* praising the *Yì cè* in markedly positive terms — evidence, on the *Sìkù* editors' analysis, that Zhèng's corpus comprised both a substantive *yìlǐ* commentary and a separate divinatory-numerological handbook.
