Hé Liángchén 何良臣 (Míng), *zì* Jìmíng 際明, was a Late-Míng *wō-kòu* defence officer of Yú-yáo 餘姚 (Zhè-jiāng). Held office to *Tì zǒng* in coastal-defence command. Author of the *Zhèn jì* 陣紀 (KR3b0017, his 4-juan classical military formations treatise).
