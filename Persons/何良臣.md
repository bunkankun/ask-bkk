Hé Liángchén 何良臣 (Míng), *zì* Jìmíng 際明, was a Late-Míng *wōkòu* defence officer of Yúyáo 餘姚 (Zhèjiāng). Held office to *Tì zǒng* in coastal-defence command. Author of the *Zhèn jì* 陣紀 ([[KR3b0017]], his 4-juan classical military formations treatise).
