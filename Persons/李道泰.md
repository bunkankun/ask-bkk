Lǐ Dàotài 李道泰, early-Qīng lay-Chán scholar. Author of the *tǎjì* 塔記 (stūpa-record) of **Yúnhuàn Yìnchén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623–1669), preserved in juan 1 of [[KR6q0493]] 《雲幻宸禪師語錄》. Provides the principal date-source for Yìnchén's death (康熙己酉 4/16 = 1669-05-15). No further biographical information located.
