Tán Yuánchūn 譚元春 (1586–1637; CBDB 133498, dates from CBDB and standard biographical sources — CBDB records no birth/death years but 1586–1637 is the standard date per Wikipedia and *Míngshǐ*), *zì* Yǒuxià 友夏, *hào* Húwān 鵠灣, of Jìnglíng 竟陵 (modern Tiānmén 天門, Húběi). Co-founder with [[鍾惺]] 鍾惺 of the late-Míng **Jìnglíng school** (*Jìnglíng pài* 竟陵派) of poetics, which advocated a spare, recondite, and introspective aesthetic (*yōushēn gūjiào* 幽深孤峭) in opposition to both the archaizing *Qiánhòu Qīzǐ* 前後七子 school and the more spontaneous *Gōngān pài* 公安派 of Yuán Hóngdào 袁宏道.

Tán failed the *jìnshì* examination repeatedly and died having never achieved a metropolitan degree. He co-edited with Zhōng Xīng the influential poetry anthologies *Shīguī* 詩歸, *Gǔshī guī* 古詩歸, and *Tángshī guī* 唐詩歸, which defined the Jìnglíng aesthetic for a generation. His own poetry collections (*Tán Yǒuxià jí* 譚友夏集) were admired within the school but less widely circulated than those of his collaborator.
