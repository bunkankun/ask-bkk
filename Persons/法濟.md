Late-Míng Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A000743. Resident at the **Yù-xī Pú-tí-ān** 玉溪菩提菴 in Jīn-líng. Disciple of **Shèng-xíng** 聖行. He participated in collating and correcting (參訂 *cān-dìng*) Shǒu-lún's 守倫 *Fǎhuá jīng kē-zhù* 法華經科註 ([[KR6d0071]], X30n0605, 10 *juan*); his teacher Shèng-xíng wrote the preface in Chóngzhēn 1 (= 1628 CE).

Catalog note: the `data/catalogs/meta/KR6d.yaml` entry for [[KR6d0071]] gives the editor's name as `法濟參` (Fǎjì-cān, with the function-marker 參 incorrectly merged into the name); the actual person is Fǎjì 法濟 with function 參訂 ("collated and corrected").

Source: *Fǎhuá jīng kē-zhù juàn-shǒu* 法華經科註卷首 (preface, dated 1628); DILA A000743.
