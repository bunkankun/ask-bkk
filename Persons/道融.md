Dàoróng 道融 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* late 12th c.), Southern-Sòng Buddhist monk; *hào* **Gǔyuè** 古月 ("Ancient Moon"). According to the prefatory and postface materials of his work, he retired in middle life to the foot of **Zhōngfēng** 中峰 in **Dānqiū** 丹丘 (modern Tàizhōu 台州 region of Zhèjiāng), there compiling the **《叢林盛事》** *Cónglín shèngshì* ([[KR6r0094]], X86 no. 1611, 2 juan), an anecdote-anthology of the "flourishing affairs of the great monasteries" (*cónglín* 叢林 = the official Chán-school sammgha-houses) of the Northern and Southern Sòng. The author's preface is dated **Qìngyuán** 慶元 3 (1197). Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001556.
