**Tōng xuán xiān sheng** 通玄先生 ("Master Penetrating the Mystery") is a pseudonym (or honorific) used by a 10th-century(?) Daoist author, credited with [[KR5d0058]] *Dào tǐ lùn* 道體論 — a Daoist philosophical treatise on the body (*tǐ*) of the Way. (Distinguish from "通玄先生" as the imperial canonization-title given to Zhāng Zhìhé 張志和.)
