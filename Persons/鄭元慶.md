Zhèng Yuánqìng 鄭元慶 (b. 1660), *zì* Zhǐ-qí 芷畦, native of Guī-ān 歸安 (today's Hú-zhōu 湖州 in Zhèjiāng). An early-Qīng *kǎo-jù* scholar and local historian active in the *Kāng-xī* era. He recompiled the *Hú-zhōu fǔ-zhì* 湖州府志 (completed 1701) and recovered a rare Sòng printed edition of the Táng-era *Shízhù jì* 石柱記, on which he produced the annotated *Shízhù jì jiānshì* 石柱記箋釋 ([[KR2k0102]]); the Déqīng 德清 and Wǔ-kāng 武康 sections were supplemented by Zhū Yízūn 朱彝尊. Death year not recorded in CBDB (id 83738) or in standard dated catalogues; the Sìkù submission of his book in 1781 is far later than his presumed lifetime — that date reflects the Sìkù editorial process, not his floruit.
