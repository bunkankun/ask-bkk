# Qīngliáng Chéngguān 清涼澄觀 (738–839)

Tang Buddhist exegete, fourth patriarch of the Huáyán 華嚴 school and imperial-state preceptor (*Qīngliáng guóshī* 清涼國師) under successive Tang emperors. Native of Yuèzhōu 越州, lay surname Xiàhóu 夏侯. The author of the encyclopaedic *Huáyán-jīng shū* 大方廣佛華嚴經疏 and its *yǎnyì chāo* 演義鈔, the canonical Tang Huáyán commentary on the *Avataṃsaka-sūtra*; together with substantial sub-commentaries and devotional / self-disciplinary verse. The *Shí shì zì lì* 十事自勵 ("Ten-point self-exhortation") attributed to Chéngguān forms a staple of the late-imperial Vinaya *xíngchí* curriculum and is appended as a *fùlù* in [[性祇]] Xìng Qí's *Píní rìyòng lù* ([[KR6k0258]]).
