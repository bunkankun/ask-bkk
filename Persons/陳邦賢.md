**Chén Bāngxián** 陳邦賢 (1889–1976), Republican / PRC-era Chinese medical historian. Author of *Zhōngguó yīxué shǐ* 中國醫學史 (1919; revised editions through the PRC period), conventionally regarded as the first systematic modern Chinese history of medicine and the principal Chinese counterpart to [[富士川遊]] Fujikawa Yū's earlier Japanese-language medical-history scholarship.
