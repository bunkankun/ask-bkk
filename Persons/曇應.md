Southern-Sòng Tiāntái master, native of **Qián-táng** 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). Dharma-heir of **Qí-yù** 齊玉 of the **Shàng-tiān-zhú-sì** 上天竺寺 (the great Tiāntái centre at Hángzhōu); resided at the **Huì-jiě-sì** 慧解寺, hence his alternate appellation **Huì-jiě Tán-yìng** 慧解曇應. Active mid-twelfth century: his principal extant work, *Jīn-gāng jīng cǎi-wēi* 金剛經采微 ([[KR6c0052]]), carries a preface dated **Shàoxīng *rén-zǐ* 紹興壬子** (1132) composed at the **Sī-xī Lán-ruò** 思溪蘭若 in Hú-zhōu (the seat of the Sī-xī Tripiṭaka workshop), and was revised at **Bái-yún Ān** 白雲菴 in **Lóngxīng *guǐ-wèi* 隆興癸未** (1163). His Tiāntái re-reading of the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra* survives as a closely-bound trilogy in the *Xù-zàng-jīng*: the *kē* outline ([[KR6c0051]]), the main commentary ([[KR6c0052]]), and the supplementary *yú-shì* ([[KR6c0053]]).
