Late-Míng man of letters, official, and literary critic; native of Tài-cāng 太倉 (modern Sūzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Zì* Jìng-měi 敬美; *hào* Lín-zhōu 麟洲, Sǔn-zhāi 損齋, Qiáng-dōng-shēng 墻東生. Younger brother of the *Hòu-qī-zǐ* leader Wáng Shì-zhēn 王世貞 (1526–1590); the two are commonly called "the two Wángs of Tài-cāng" 太倉二王. *Jìnshì* of Jiā-jìng 38 (1559); held a range of administrative posts including Vice Surveillance Commissioner of Fú-jiàn (whence his *Mǐn-bù shū* 閩部疏, [[KR2k0238]]), Right Administration Vice-Commissioner of Shǎn-xī, and Senior Bureau Secretary of the Court of the Imperial Stud (Tài-pú-sì shǎo-qīng 太僕寺少卿). Author of more than a dozen works including the *Wáng Lín-zhōu jí* 王麟州集 ([[KR4d0291]]), the *Yì-pǔ xié-yú* 秇圃擷餘, and notes on antiquities and fruit cultivation. Critically he is treated by the *Sìkù* editors as a more measured voice than his celebrated elder brother: while sharing the *Hòu-qī-zǐ* commitment to High-Táng models, he was the first inside the school to acknowledge that the Archaist programme was running into mimetic excess and to predict ("*shù-shí nián hòu bì yǒu yàn ér sǎo chú zhě*") the reaction that would come from the late-Míng Gōng-ān 公安 school. Lifedates 1536–1588 are confirmed by CBDB (personid 34719).
