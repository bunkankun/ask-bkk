Late-Míng man of letters, official, and literary critic; native of Tàicāng 太倉 (modern Sūzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Zì* Jìngměi 敬美; *hào* Línzhōu 麟洲, Sǔnzhāi 損齋, Qiángdōngshēng 墻東生. Younger brother of the *Hòuqīzǐ* leader Wáng Shìzhēn 王世貞 (1526–1590); the two are commonly called "the two Wángs of Tàicāng" 太倉二王. *Jìnshì* of Jiājìng 38 (1559); held a range of administrative posts including Vice Surveillance Commissioner of Fújiàn (whence his *Mǐnbù shū* 閩部疏, [[KR2k0238]]), Right Administration Vice-Commissioner of Shǎnxī, and Senior Bureau Secretary of the Court of the Imperial Stud (Tàipúsì shǎoqīng 太僕寺少卿). Author of more than a dozen works including the *Wáng Línzhōu jí* 王麟州集 ([[KR4d0291]]), the *Yìpǔ xiéyú* 秇圃擷餘, and notes on antiquities and fruit cultivation. Critically he is treated by the *Sìkù* editors as a more measured voice than his celebrated elder brother: while sharing the *Hòuqīzǐ* commitment to High-Táng models, he was the first inside the school to acknowledge that the Archaist programme was running into mimetic excess and to predict ("*shùshí nián hòu bì yǒu yàn ér sǎo chú zhě*") the reaction that would come from the late-Míng Gōngān 公安 school. Lifedates 1536–1588 are confirmed by CBDB (personid 34719).
