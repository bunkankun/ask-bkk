Yáng Lìshān 楊栗山 (proper name Yáng Xuán 楊璿; 1705–1791) was a Qīng physician of the Liú-Héjiān cooling-fire school whose *Shānghán wēnyì tiáobiàn* 傷寒溫疫條辨 (1784) — often referred to as the *Hánwēn tiáobiàn* — was the standard differential-diagnosis manual on cold-damage vs warm-epidemic disease. His *tiáobiàn* schema served as the explicit model for [[郭楚賢]] Guō Chǔxián's *Diānkuáng tiáobiàn* ([[KR3eh048]]).
