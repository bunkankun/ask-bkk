**Zhāng Zànchén** 張贊臣 (1904–1993), Wǔjìn 武進 (Jiāngsū)-born Republican-era Chinese-medicine reformer; founder of the *Yījiè chūnqiū shè* 醫界春秋社 in Shanghai and a pivotal figure in the institutional defence of *Zhōngyī* during the Republican period, particularly in the resistance to the 1929 Yú Yúnxiù 余雲岫 proposal to abolish Chinese medicine. Author of a preface (dated 1 September 1931) to the *Zhúquánshēng nǚkē jíyào* [[KR3ei019]].
