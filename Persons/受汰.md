Shòutài 受汰 (DILA Authority A000553; Míng dynasty, dates not securely known) was a *bhikṣu* of the Ān-guó-sì 安國寺 in Yán-guān 鹽官 (modern Hǎi-níng, Zhèjiāng), and a Tiāntái-tradition exegete trained under Tóng-xī Héshàng 桐谿和尚. The DILA authority record draws on the preface to his sole surviving work, the *Jīn-guāngmíng jīng kē-zhù* 金光明經科註 ([[KR6i0317]], X362, 4 fascicles) — a structural-outline-cum-commentary on *[[KR6i0301|Jīn-guāngmíng jīng 金光明經]]* (T663) that synthesises the Tiāntái commentarial line of [[智顗]], [[灌頂]], and [[知禮]] into a single exegetical scheme.
