**Zhāng Jūnfáng** 張君房 (*fl.* early 11th century), *zì* Yǔn fāng 允方, was a Northern-Sòng scholar-official assigned by imperial commission to supervise the compilation of the **Dà Sòng Tiān gōng bǎo zàng** 大宋天宮寶藏 (Sòng Daoist Canon, completed 1019). From this project he also compiled the single most important pre-modern Daoist encyclopedia, the ***Yún jí qī qiān*** 雲笈七籤 ([[KR5d0055]]) — an abridged "Seven Lots from the Cloud-Satchel" synthesizing the canonical materials, in 120 *juàn*, dedicated to Sòng Rén zōng c. 1028–1029.
