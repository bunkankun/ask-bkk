**Shū Jìnxián** 舒進賢, putative early-Qīng physician described by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]] as a nephew and direct transmitter of [[喻嘉言]] Yú Jiāyán's dysentery teaching. Unattested in other sources; possibly a textual fiction by Wú to lend authority to his *nìliú wǎnzhōu* lineage claim.
