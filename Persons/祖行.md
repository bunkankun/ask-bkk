Zǔ Xíng 祖行 (fl. *Shào-xī* / 1190–1194), great-grandson (*zēng-sūn*) of Zǔ Wú-zé 祖無擇 [[祖無擇]] (1011–1085). Held *Yuán-zhōu jūn-shì pàn-guān* 袁州軍事判官. In *Shào-xī* 3 / 1192 he assembled the surviving fragments of his great-grandfather's literary corpus into the 10-*juǎn* *Huàn-dòu jí* 煥斗集 (named after Ōuyáng Xiū's farewell-poem line *zhōng-yòu wén-zhāng huàn xīng-dǒu* 中右文章煥星斗) plus a 1-*juǎn* *Míng-chén xián-shì shī-wén* and a 4-*juǎn* family-collection (*jiā-jí*) of his uncles' and cousins' writings — the present *Lóng-xué wén-jí* 龍學文集 [[KR4d0055]] in 16 *juǎn*. His preface and *Lóng-xué shǐ-mò* in *juǎn* 16 are the principal source on Zǔ Wú-zé's transmission.
