Zǔ Xíng 祖行 (fl. *Shàoxī* / 1190–1194), great-grandson (*zēngsūn*) of Zǔ Wúzé 祖無擇 [[祖無擇]] (1011–1085). Held *Yuánzhōu jūnshì pànguān* 袁州軍事判官. In *Shàoxī* 3 / 1192 he assembled the surviving fragments of his great-grandfather's literary corpus into the 10-*juǎn* *Huàndòu jí* 煥斗集 (named after Ōuyáng Xiū's farewell-poem line *zhōngyòu wénzhāng huàn xīngdǒu* 中右文章煥星斗) plus a 1-*juǎn* *Míngchén xiánshì shīwén* and a 4-*juǎn* family-collection (*jiājí*) of his uncles' and cousins' writings — the present *Lóngxué wénjí* 龍學文集 [[KR4d0055]] in 16 *juǎn*. His preface and *Lóngxué shǐmò* in *juǎn* 16 are the principal source on Zǔ Wúzé's transmission.
