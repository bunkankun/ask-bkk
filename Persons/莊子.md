Zhuāngzǐ 莊子 (莊周 Zhuāng Zhōu, ca. 369–286 BCE), Warring States Daoist philosopher and reputed author of the eponymous *Zhuāngzǐ* 莊子. Conventionally credited with the *Nèi piān* 內篇; the *Wài piān* 外篇 and *Zá piān* 雜篇 are now generally taken as later compilation. Cited in the *Sìkù* notice on [[KR1a0144]] as the surprising target of [[惠士奇]] Huì Shìqí's claim that Zhuāng Zhōu was "refined in the *Yì*" — an attribution the editors reject as unscriptural.
