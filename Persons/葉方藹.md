Yè Fāngǎi 葉方藹 (1629–1682), *zì* Zǐjí 子吉, *hào* Rěn'ān 訒菴, posthumously *Wénmǐn* 文敏, was an early-Qīng Hànlín scholar and imperial editorial supervisor. Native of Kūnshān 崑山 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Tànhuā* (third place *jìnshì*) of *Shùnzhì xīnchǒu* (1661); rose to *Lǐbù shìláng* and *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì*. Co-supervised the *Yù dìng xiàojīng yǎn yì* 御定孝經衍義 ([[KR3a0105]]) project. CBDB id 65970, dates 1629–1682.
