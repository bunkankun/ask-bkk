# Gān Shǐ 甘始

A *fāngshì* 方士 of Tàiyuán 太原 active in the late Hàn / early Wèi, named in the *Hòu Hàn shū* (j. 82 *Fāngshù zhuàn*) and 《三國志·魏志》 j. 29 alongside Zuǒ Cí 左慈 and Xī Jiǎn 郄儉 as one of the *fāngshì* gathered at the court of Cáo Cāo 曹操. Cited in the *Yàolù* 藥錄 ([[KR3e0108]]) and in Gě Hóng's 葛洪 [[葛洪]] *Bàopǔzǐ nèipiān* ([[KR5f0019]]) as having lived "more than three hundred years among men" through his command of breath-cultivation and the use of immortality drugs, including *tiānméndōng* 天門冬. He stands in the *bĕncǎo* and Daoist hagiographic record as a paradigmatic late-Hàn *xiānrén* 仙人 figure of the *fāngshì* / *yǎngshēng* tradition.
