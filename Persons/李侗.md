Southern-Sòng Daoxué teacher of Yánpíng 延平 in Fújiàn, *zì* Yuànzhōng 願中, conventionally Master Yánpíng. A pupil of Luó Cóngyàn 羅從彥, in the Yáng Shí [[楊時]] → Luó Cóngyàn → Lǐ Tóng → [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī transmission line of the Chéng-brothers' Luòxué that constitutes the canonical "*Dào tǒng*" 道統 inheritance. Lǐ Tóng is Zhū Xī's principal teacher; the *Yánpíng dáwèn* 延平答問 (Lǐ's letters to Zhū) is the primary record of the master-pupil relationship and was canonized in the early Southern-Sòng *Dàoxué* tradition.
