**Lǐ Chāohéng** 李超恆 of Shímén 石門 (Zhèjiāng) — Qīng publisher and annotator of [[汪樸齋]] Wāng Pǔzhāi's *Píngzhù chǎnkē xīnfǎ* ([[KR3ei069]]). In 1804 (Jiāqìng 9) he and his nephews "pooled funds and printed it for the public." His extensive 1804 introduction on the *jīdé* (merit-accumulation) and *jiéyù* (sexual continence) prerequisites for fertility transforms Wāng's clinical-pharmacological manual into a hybrid clinical-ethical compendium. Not in CBDB.
