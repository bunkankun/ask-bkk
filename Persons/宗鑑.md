Zōngjiàn 宗鑑 (fl. early 13th c.) was a Southern Sòng Tiāntái 天台 school monk active in the Sì-míng 四明 (Níngbō) region. The preface to his major work, the *Shìmén zhèngtǒng* 釋門正統 ([[KR6r0018]]), is dated 1237 (Jiāxī 1). His sobriquet was Liángzhǔ 良渚.

The *Shìmén zhèngtǒng* is an eight-juan universal Buddhist history that establishes the Tiāntái patriarchal succession as the orthodox lineage of Chinese Buddhism — directly responding, from the Tiāntái side, to the historiographical claims of the Chán lamp-record tradition. The work was composed in the broader context of the Sòng-period Tiāntái-Chán polemic and of the contemporary Confucian *zhèngtǒng* 正統 (legitimate-succession) debates. It served as the immediate model and predecessor for [[志磐]]'s much fuller *Fózǔ tǒngjì* ([[KR6r0012]]) of 1269.

[[宗鑑]] left the work apparently incomplete at his death; the surviving Xuzangjing recension may not represent his finished plan. No further substantial biographical data is preserved.
