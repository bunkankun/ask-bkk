Bǎo Huī 寶輝, *hào* Yù-shān 玉珊 (late-Qīng, *fl.* 1895–1901). The surname 寶 is rare in Hàn-Chinese genealogy and most commonly an abbreviated Manchu Bannerman name; Bǎo Huī was very likely a **Manchu Bannerman physician** active in the capital-region medical milieu, though the prefaces to his sole transmitted work give no native-place. Author of [[KR3eq092]] *Yī-yī xiǎo cǎo* 醫醫小草, an eclectic late-Qīng *yī-yī* (doctoring-the-doctors) clinical-doctrinal essay collection, with a printer's preface dated 1901. Not in CBDB.
