YuánMíng transitional scholar, *zì* Zìqiān 自謙, *hào* Shuōzhāi 說齋, of Yínxiàn 鄞縣 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). Son of [[舒天民]] (Shū Tiānmín). Annotated his father's *Liùyì gāngmù* 六藝綱目 [[KR1j0088]] (2 *juàn*) — providing the principal *zhù* layer of the work — which was issued in Zhìzhèng 24 / *jiǎchén* (1364) with the further *fùzhù* of fellow-townsman Zhào Yízhōng 趙宜中. CBDB cbdbId 117924; no firm lifedates. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that on the evidence of his father's grandson 舒睿's postface (dated Hóngwǔ 1 / *wùshēn* 1368) which does not yet mention Zhào Yízhōng's annotations, Zhào is likely a Míng addition.
