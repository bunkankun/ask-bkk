Yuán-Míng transitional scholar, *zì* Zì-qiān 自謙, *hào* Shuō-zhāi 說齋, of Yín-xiàn 鄞縣 (Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng). Son of [[舒天民]] (Shū Tiān-mín). Annotated his father's *Liù-yì gāng-mù* 六藝綱目 [[KR1j0088]] (2 *juàn*) — providing the principal *zhù* layer of the work — which was issued in Zhì-zhèng 24 / *jiǎ-chén* (1364) with the further *fù-zhù* of fellow-townsman Zhào Yí-zhōng 趙宜中. CBDB cbdbId 117924; no firm lifedates. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that on the evidence of his father's grandson 舒睿's postface (dated Hóng-wǔ 1 / *wù-shēn* 1368) which does not yet mention Zhào Yí-zhōng's annotations, Zhào is likely a Míng addition.
