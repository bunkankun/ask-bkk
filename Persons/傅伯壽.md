**Fù Bó-shòu** 傅伯壽 (fl. early-Southern Sòng), son of Fù Chá 傅察 [[傅察]] (the *Jìng-kāng*-eve martyr). Compiled and edited his father's writings as *Zhōng-sù jí* 忠肅集 [[KR4d0134]] in 3 *juǎn* — providing the principal channel of transmission. Held minor office; precise dates and own career undocumented beyond this editorial role.
