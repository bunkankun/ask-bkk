**Yáo Yǒng-jì** 姚永濟 (*zì* Rǔ-jí 汝楫, late Wàn-lì 萬曆), late-Míng physician. Author of the *Kǎo zhèng bìng yuán* 考證病源 ([[KR3er026]]), a four-*juǎn* clinical handbook on disease aetiology with a tightly aphoristic mnemonic-verse drug-formulary; the work's self-preface is dated *Wàn-lì dīng-yǒu* (1597). Yáo's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered from the source; the late-Wàn-lì dating is the secure evidence. No CBDB record.
