**Moriyuki** (Shikō) 師行 — Late-Heian or early-Kamakura Japanese Sanron monk; *in-shu* 院主 (abbot) of the **Tōnan-in** 東南院 of Tōdaiji, holder of the court rank *Ōkurakyō tokugō* 大藏卿得業 ("graduate of the [Esoteric / Sanron] examinations bearing the [honorary] court title Minister of the Treasury"). Compiler of *Sānlùnzōng lànshāng* 三論宗濫觴 ([[KR6t0003]], T70n2307B), a single-fascicle Sanron lineage narrative.

His only securely-attributed work is the lineage text, prepared for a *hōonkō* 報恩講 (debt-of-gratitude commemoration). The Tōnan-in was the Tōdaiji sub-temple that housed the Sanron-Shingon scholastic tradition of the late Heian (including Chinkai 珍海 and his successors). The latest patriarch named in his text is Kanri 觀理 (d. 1107), placing the work and its author somewhere between 1107 and the mid-13th century.
