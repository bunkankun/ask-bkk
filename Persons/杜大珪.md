Sòng compiler from Méishān 眉山 (modern Sìchuān), self-attributed in his preface as a *jìnshì*. The *Sìkù* editors note that his career is otherwise unverifiable. His preface to the *Míngchén bēizhuàn wǎnyǎn zhī jí* 名臣碑傳琬琰之集 ([[KR2g0025]]) is dated *Shàoxī jiǎyín* = 1194, fixing his floruit. He is principally known for that compilation: 107 *juàn* in three sections (27 + 55 + 25) — *shéndào bēi*, *zhìmíng / xíngzhuàng*, and *biézhuàn* — gathering biographical stelae and separate biographies for Northern-Sòng great officials from *Jiànlóng* (960) down to early Southern Sòng. His work is the principal Sòng-period anthology of biographical stele literature and a foundational source for Sòng prosopography.
