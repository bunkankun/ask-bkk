Sòng compiler from Méi-shān 眉山 (modern Sì-chuān), self-attributed in his preface as a *jìn-shì*. The *Sì-kù* editors note that his career is otherwise unverifiable. His preface to the *Míng-chén bēi-zhuàn wǎn-yǎn zhī jí* 名臣碑傳琬琰之集 ([[KR2g0025]]) is dated *Shào-xī jiǎ-yín* = 1194, fixing his floruit. He is principally known for that compilation: 107 *juàn* in three sections (27 + 55 + 25) — *shén-dào bēi*, *zhì-míng / xíng-zhuàng*, and *bié-zhuàn* — gathering biographical stelae and separate biographies for Northern-Sòng great officials from *Jiàn-lóng* (960) down to early Southern Sòng. His work is the principal Sòng-period anthology of biographical stele literature and a foundational source for Sòng prosopography.
