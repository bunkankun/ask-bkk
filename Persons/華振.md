**Huá Zhèn** 華振 (*zì* Qúshēng 劬生, *fl.* 1900s–1930s), Republican-era literatus-physician from **Háohú** 濠湖 (probably Háozhōu 濠州 region, Anhui). Originally trained as a Confucian scholar (*màocái* 茂才) before turning to medicine. Studied under his clan-relative Xí Yùxiān 席育先 (introduction via *zúrén* 族人 family-network). Author of the *Liúxiāngguǎn yīhuà* 留香館醫話 ([[KR3eq070]]) — a one-*juǎn* clinical-anecdotal compendium prefaced by Sūn Kuí 孫魁 in mid-autumn 1930. The work's distinctive *míngyī / yōngyī* (named-physician / pretender-physician) dichotomy material is a particularly useful late-Republican provincial primary source. Not in CBDB.
