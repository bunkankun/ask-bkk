Sēngqié-sīnà 僧伽斯那 (\*Saṃghasena, "Saṃgha-army"; lifedates unknown), Indic Buddhist compiler-figure named in the canonical signatures of two Chinese *jātaka* / *avadāna* anthologies: [[KR6b0002|*Púsà běn-yuán jīng* 菩薩本緣經 (T153)]], translated by [[支謙]] in the third century, and the *Bǎi-yú jīng* 百喻經 (T209) translated by Guṇavṛddhi 求那毘地 at Jiànkāng in the late fifth century. The two attributions are separated by some 250 years and modern scholarship (Mizuno Kōgen; Demiéville) generally regards them as distinct historical figures, or alternatively as a generic learned-compiler name attached to anthological *jātaka* literature. No biographical material survives.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6b0002]] *Púsà běn-yuán jīng* (T153, compiler-attribution).
