Xiè Yìngfāng 謝應芳 (1296–1392), *zì* Zǐlán 子蘭, *hào* Guīcháo 龜巢, was a YuánMíng transition Confucian classicist and *Lǐxué* moral-philosopher of Wǔjìn 武進 (Chángzhōu region, Jiāngsū). He never held office; lived to age 96, spanning the entire Yuán-period scholarly community and dying in early Míng (Hóngwǔ 25, 1392). Major works: the *Biàn huò biān* 辨惑編 ([[KR3a0073]], his celebrated anti-heterodox-customs polemic, composed in *Zhìzhèng* 1341–1370); the *Sī xián lù* 思賢錄 (Yuán *Lǐxué*-figure prosopography, in the SKQS); the *Guīcháo gǎo* 龜巢稿 (collected works, 20 juan). Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (Rúlín zhuàn). CBDB id 30337, dates 1296–1392.
