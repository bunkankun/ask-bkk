**Péng Hàogǔ** 彭好古 (b. 1552; CBDB index year 1552), *hào* **Xīlíng Yīhè jūshì** 西陵一壑居士 ("Reclusive One-Ravine of Xīlíng"); native of **楚黃** (Húběi — modern Huángzhōu / Wǔhàn region). Late-Wàn-lì-era Daoist exegete and inner-alchemist, signed his commentary on the *Jīnbì gǔwén lónghǔ shàng jīng* 金碧古文龍虎上經 ([[KR5i0029]]) at his Huìshuǐ retreat (*Huìshuǐ biéshù* 會水別墅) on **24 May 1599** (萬曆己亥季夏廿之四日). His *Xuánjiě* 玄解 reads the *Lónghǔ shàng jīng* as the foundational scripture of the *jīndān* tradition, parallel to the *Cān tóng qì*, and explicitly polemicises against the Sòng commentator Wáng Dào 王道. CBDB ID 207009 records his birth year; further life details are sparse. He is associated in late-Míng print catalogues with a circle of Húběi inner-alchemy editors who produced the major *Lónghǔ* and *Cāntóng* recensions of the period.
