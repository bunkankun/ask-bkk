Fù Fēi-qīng 傅飛卿 was a Southern-Sòng to Yuán *Jìng-míng* 淨明 master active in the Xī-shān 西山 lineage at Nánchāng 南昌. He is identified in the Daoist canon as *Zǐ-wēi yòu-diǎn-zhě Shǎo-wēi dū-lù Língbǎo jìng-míng yuàn sī-chá yòu-yǎn-jiào shǐ* 紫微右典者少微都錄靈寶淨明院司察右演教使 (Right-Codifier of the Zǐ-wēi Palace; Director-Registrar of Shǎo-wēi; Inspector and Right-Hand Propagator-of-Teaching of the Línbǎo Jìng-míng Cloister) — a Daoist *bīng-fǎ* 兵法 (administrative-bureaucratic) honorific marking his place as commentator on the *Huáng-sù shū* 黄素書. He is named as the *jiě* 解 (annotator) of [[KR5b0259]] (*Gāoshàng Yuè-gōng tài-yīn yuán-jūn xiào-dào xiān-wáng língbǎo jìng-míng huáng-sù shū*), the central scripture of the *Jìng-míng* tradition. No precise lifedates are recoverable from CBDB. Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1094) place him in the late Southern-Sòng to Yuán generation, in the same milieu as [[周方文]].
