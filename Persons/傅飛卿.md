Fù Fēiqīng 傅飛卿 was a Southern-Sòng to Yuán *Jìngmíng* 淨明 master active in the Xīshān 西山 lineage at Nánchāng 南昌. He is identified in the Daoist canon as *Zǐwēi yòudiǎnzhě Shǎowēi dūlù Língbǎo jìngmíng yuàn sīchá yòuyǎnjiào shǐ* 紫微右典者少微都錄靈寶淨明院司察右演教使 (Right-Codifier of the Zǐwēi Palace; Director-Registrar of Shǎowēi; Inspector and Right-Hand Propagator-of-Teaching of the Línbǎo Jìngmíng Cloister) — a Daoist *bīngfǎ* 兵法 (administrative-bureaucratic) honorific marking his place as commentator on the *Huángsù shū* 黄素書. He is named as the *jiě* 解 (annotator) of [[KR5b0259]] (*Gāoshàng Yuègōng tàiyīn yuánjūn xiàodào xiānwáng língbǎo jìngmíng huángsù shū*), the central scripture of the *Jìngmíng* tradition. No precise lifedates are recoverable from CBDB. Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1094) place him in the late Southern-Sòng to Yuán generation, in the same milieu as [[周方文]].
