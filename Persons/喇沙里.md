Lǎshālǐ 喇沙里 (Lasari, fl. early Kāngxī era) was a Manchu Hànlín lecturer-academician (*rìjiǎng qǐjūzhù guān* / *jīngyán jiǎngguān*) who served the young Kāngxī emperor as a daily-lecture instructor at the Hóngdédiàn 弘德殿. The 1677 *jìnchéngshū* memorial of the *Rìjiǎng Sìshū jiěyì* ([[KR1h0053]]) lists him at the head of the editorial group above Chén Tíngjìng 陳廷敬, ahead of Sèlěng 色冷, Yè Fāngǎi 葉方藹, Jiǎng Hóngdào 蔣弘道, Kùlènà 庫勒納, and Zhāng Yīng 張英 — the seven Hànlín academicians who compiled the imperial *Sìshū* lectures for Kāngxī. He is also recorded as having participated in the compilation of the *Huángyúbiǎo* 皇輿表 (with Kuíxù 揆敘). His specific lifedates, clan, and banner affiliation are not securely attested in widely available reference sources; CBDB has no entry under this name. Standard Qing references list him simply as 清·喇沙里, 奉敕編. The catalog meta gives only "Qīng" with no dates — followed here.
