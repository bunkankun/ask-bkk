Lǎshālǐ 喇沙里 (Lasari, fl. early Kāngxī era) was a Manchu Hànlín lecturer-academician (*rì-jiǎng qǐ-jū-zhù guān* / *jīng-yán jiǎng-guān*) who served the young Kāngxī emperor as a daily-lecture instructor at the Hóngdé-diàn 弘德殿. The 1677 *jìn-chéng-shū* memorial of the *Rì-jiǎng Sì-shū jiě-yì* (KR1h0053) lists him at the head of the editorial group above Chén Tíng-jìng 陳廷敬, ahead of Sè-lěng 色冷, Yè Fāng-ǎi 葉方藹, Jiǎng Hóng-dào 蔣弘道, Kù-lè-nà 庫勒納, and Zhāng Yīng 張英 — the seven Hànlín academicians who compiled the imperial *Sì-shū* lectures for Kāngxī. He is also recorded as having participated in the compilation of the *Huáng-yú-biǎo* 皇輿表 (with Kuíxù 揆敘). His specific lifedates, clan, and banner affiliation are not securely attested in widely available reference sources; CBDB has no entry under this name. Standard Qing references list him simply as 清·喇沙里, 奉敕編. The catalog meta gives only "Qīng" with no dates — followed here.
