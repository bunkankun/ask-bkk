# Huáng Hè 黃鶴

*Zì* Shū-sì 叔似. Native of *Yí-huáng xiàn* 宜黃 in *Fú-zhōu* 撫州 (modern *Yí-huáng* in central Jiāng-xī). Sòng-period scholar; son of Huáng Xī [[黃希]]. Floruit late 12th – early 13th century; the *Bǔ-zhù Dù shī* completion is dated *Jiā-dìng* 9 (1216).

His principal scholarly contribution is the completion of his father's *Bǔ qiān-jiā jí-zhù Dù gōng-bù shī shǐ* (= *Bǔ-zhù Dù shī*, [[KR4c0017]]). Hè worked on the text for some thirty years after his father's death, integrating the existing Sòng *Qiān-jiā zhù* book-stall edition, the *jí-zhù* of Wáng Zhū and Zhào Cì-gōng et al., and substantial supplementary annotations of his own (tagged *Hè yuē* 鶴曰) and his father's (tagged *Xī yuē* 希曰). He extended the *àn-nián biān-shī* (year-by-year arrangement) editorial program inherited from Huáng Bó-sī 黃伯思 and Lǔ Yìn 魯訔, producing the most rigorous chronological arrangement of the corpus to that date — a format inherited and further developed by the Qīng Qiū Zhào-áo [[仇兆鰲]] *Dù shī xiáng-zhù* [[KR4c0020]].

Hè's own collection, the *Běi-chuāng yù-yán jí* 北窗寓言集, is long lost. CBDB has no entry.
