# Huáng Hè 黃鶴

*Zì* Shūsì 叔似. Native of *Yíhuáng xiàn* 宜黃 in *Fúzhōu* 撫州 (modern *Yíhuáng* in central Jiāngxī). Sòng-period scholar; son of Huáng Xī [[黃希]]. Floruit late 12th – early 13th century; the *Bǔzhù Dù shī* completion is dated *Jiādìng* 9 (1216).

His principal scholarly contribution is the completion of his father's *Bǔ qiānjiā jízhù Dù gōngbù shī shǐ* (= *Bǔzhù Dù shī*, [[KR4c0017]]). Hè worked on the text for some thirty years after his father's death, integrating the existing Sòng *Qiānjiā zhù* book-stall edition, the *jízhù* of Wáng Zhū and Zhào Cìgōng et al., and substantial supplementary annotations of his own (tagged *Hè yuē* 鶴曰) and his father's (tagged *Xī yuē* 希曰). He extended the *ànnián biānshī* (year-by-year arrangement) editorial program inherited from Huáng Bósī 黃伯思 and Lǔ Yìn 魯訔, producing the most rigorous chronological arrangement of the corpus to that date — a format inherited and further developed by the Qīng Qiū Zhàoáo [[仇兆鰲]] *Dù shī xiángzhù* [[KR4c0020]].

Hè's own collection, the *Běichuāng yùyán jí* 北窗寓言集, is long lost. CBDB has no entry.
