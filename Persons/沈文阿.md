# Shěn Wén'ā 沈文阿

*Zì* Guówèi 國衛. Native of Wúkāng 武康 in Wúxīng 吳興 commandery (modern Zhèjiāng). Confucian classicist of the Liáng 梁 and Chén 陳 dynasties. He held *guózǐ bóshì* 國子博士 (Erudite of the Imperial Academy) and served as tutor to the heir-apparent under the Chén. During the Hóu Jǐng 侯景 disturbance (548–552) he attempted to take his own life by hanging from a tree rather than submit to the rebels, and was subsequently pardoned by Chén Bàxiān 陳霸先 (Chén Wǔdì).

His scholarly programme covered the *Sānlǐ* 三禮 (with a substantial *Lǐ yì* 禮義 in over eighty *juàn*), the *Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn* (the *Chūnqiū Zuǒshì zhuàn yìlüè* 春秋左氏傳義略, of which [[王元規]] Wáng Yuánguī's [[KR1e0125]] is the continuation), and a general handbook *Jīngdiǎn dàyì* 經典大義 in eighteen *juàn*. All these works are lost as integral books; surviving fragments are preserved chiefly in [[陸德明]] Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文, in the Táng *zhèngyì* 正義, and in *Yùhán shānfáng jíyì shū* and CHANT-tradition reconstructions. He died in 563 aged 61. Biography: *Chén shū* 陳書 *juàn* 33 (*Rúlín liè zhuàn*).
