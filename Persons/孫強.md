Táng-period philologist, of Fù-chūn 富春 (modern Fuyang, Zhejiang); active in the Shàng-yuán era (674–676) of Gāo-zōng. He produced the standard Táng-period augmentation of Gù Yěwáng's [[顧野王]] *Yùpiān* [[KR1j0022]] — a recension known historically as the *Shàng-yuán-běn* 上元本, which substantially enlarged the Liáng *Yùpiān*'s headword count and was the version most widely transmitted until the Northern-Sòng comprehensive revision of Chén Péng-nián [[陳彭年]] et al. (Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 6, 1013). Beyond his work on the *Yùpiān*, no biographical detail survives.
