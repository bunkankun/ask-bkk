# Shěn Yuē 沈約

*Zì* Xiūwén 休文; posthumous title Yǐn-hóu 隱侯 ("the Reclusive Marquis"). Native of Wǔ-kāng 武康 in Wú-xīng 吳興 (modern Dé-qīng 德清, Zhèjiāng). Member of the great southern Wú-xīng Shěn 吳興沈氏 lineage, four times "duke of the realm" across Sòng, Qí, Liáng.

Career: served all three southern dynasties of his lifetime. Under Liú-Sòng he held *Bù-bīng xiào-wèi* 步兵校尉 and *Tài-zǐ jiā lìng* 太子家令. Under Qí (479–502) he was *Hǎi-yán xiàn lìng* 海鹽縣令, then *Bù-bīng xiào-wèi* in the Wén-huì Crown Prince's 文惠太子 household, and after Wén-huì's death attached to the *Jìng-líng wáng* 竟陵王 Xiāo Zǐliáng 蕭子良 — at whose Jìng-líng salon (the *Jìng-líng bā yǒu* 竟陵八友, including Shěn, Xiè Tiǎo 謝朓, Wáng Róng 王融, Xiāo Yǎn 蕭衍 [later Liáng Wǔdì], Rèn Fǎng 任昉) the Yǒng-míng style of *gǔ-tǐ* poetry crystallised.

After Xiāo Yǎn's accession as Liáng Wǔdì in 502 — to which Shěn was a principal architect — he served as *Shàng-shū pú-shè* 尚書僕射, *Tài-zǐ shǎo-fù* 太子少傅, and *Shàng-shū lìng* 尚書令, dying *in officio* in 513.

His scholarly contributions are foundational. (1) The *Sòngshū* (KR2a0016), the standard dynastic history of the Liú-Sòng, in 100 *juǎn*, presented under Qí Wǔdì in *Yǒngmíng* 5 (487). (2) The *Sì-shēng* 四聲 (*Four-Tone*) theory of Chinese phonology — the recognition of *píng* 平, *shǎng* 上, *qù* 去, *rù* 入 as the four basic tonal classes — set out in his *Xiè Língyùn zhuàn lùn* 謝靈運傳論 (in the *Sòngshū*) and in his lost *Sì-shēng pǔ* 四聲譜. The doctrine is the foundation of all subsequent Chinese metrical and *yùn-shū* analysis. (3) The associated theory of *bā bìng* 八病 (Eight Faults) of regulated verse-prosody, governing the *Yǒng-míng tǐ* 永明體 ancestral to Tang regulated verse. (4) Substantial *fù*, *shī*, and *biǎo* — collected as the *Shěn Yǐn-hóu jí* 沈隱侯集.

His biographies are in *Liáng shū* 13 (KR2a0018) and *Nán shǐ* 57 (KR2a0024). CBDB id 134679 (lifedates 441–513 confirmed).
