# Shěn Yuē 沈約

*Zì* Xiūwén 休文; posthumous title Yǐnhóu 隱侯 ("the Reclusive Marquis"). Native of Wǔkāng 武康 in Wúxīng 吳興 (modern Déqīng 德清, Zhèjiāng). Member of the great southern Wúxīng Shěn 吳興沈氏 lineage, four times "duke of the realm" across Sòng, Qí, Liáng.

Career: served all three southern dynasties of his lifetime. Under LiúSòng he held *Bùbīng xiàowèi* 步兵校尉 and *Tàizǐ jiā lìng* 太子家令. Under Qí (479–502) he was *Hǎiyán xiàn lìng* 海鹽縣令, then *Bùbīng xiàowèi* in the Wénhuì Crown Prince's 文惠太子 household, and after Wénhuì's death attached to the *Jìnglíng wáng* 竟陵王 Xiāo Zǐliáng 蕭子良 — at whose Jìnglíng salon (the *Jìnglíng bā yǒu* 竟陵八友, including Shěn, Xiè Tiǎo 謝朓, Wáng Róng 王融, Xiāo Yǎn 蕭衍 [later Liáng Wǔdì], Rèn Fǎng 任昉) the Yǒngmíng style of *gǔtǐ* poetry crystallised.

After Xiāo Yǎn's accession as Liáng Wǔdì in 502 — to which Shěn was a principal architect — he served as *Shàngshū púshè* 尚書僕射, *Tàizǐ shǎofù* 太子少傅, and *Shàngshū lìng* 尚書令, dying *in officio* in 513.

His scholarly contributions are foundational. (1) The *Sòngshū* ([[KR2a0016]]), the standard dynastic history of the LiúSòng, in 100 *juǎn*, presented under Qí Wǔdì in *Yǒngmíng* 5 (487). (2) The *Sìshēng* 四聲 (*Four-Tone*) theory of Chinese phonology — the recognition of *píng* 平, *shǎng* 上, *qù* 去, *rù* 入 as the four basic tonal classes — set out in his *Xiè Língyùn zhuàn lùn* 謝靈運傳論 (in the *Sòngshū*) and in his lost *Sìshēng pǔ* 四聲譜. The doctrine is the foundation of all subsequent Chinese metrical and *yùnshū* analysis. (3) The associated theory of *bā bìng* 八病 (Eight Faults) of regulated verse-prosody, governing the *Yǒngmíng tǐ* 永明體 ancestral to Tang regulated verse. (4) Substantial *fù*, *shī*, and *biǎo* — collected as the *Shěn Yǐnhóu jí* 沈隱侯集.

His biographies are in *Liáng shū* 13 ([[KR2a0018]]) and *Nán shǐ* 57 ([[KR2a0024]]). CBDB id 134679 (lifedates 441–513 confirmed).
