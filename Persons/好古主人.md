**Hào Gǔ Zhǔrén** 好古主人 ("The Antiquarian Master") is the pen name attributed to the author of the Qīng-dynasty novel *Zhào Tàizǔ Sān Xià Nán Táng* 趙太祖三下南唐 ([[KR4k0288]]). No biographical details are known. The pseudonym is a common type in Qīng popular fiction and cannot be uniquely identified. No CBDB entry has been located. This pen name should not be confused with [[彭好古]] or [[王好古]], who are different historical persons.
