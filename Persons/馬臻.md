Yuán-period 元 Daoist priest and Sòng-loyalist *yí-mín* poet. *Zì* **Zhì-dào** 志道; *hào* **Xū-zhōng** 虛中 ("Empty-and-Composed"). Native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu, modern Zhè-jiāng). CBDB 28345 records *b.* 1254; death-date uncertain.

**Religious affiliation.** Took Daoist robes and resided at the **West Lake** as a retiring priest. Active in the early-Yuán *Xuán-jiào* / Quán-zhēn imperial-Daoist literary circle; participated in the **Dà-dé** *xīn-chǒu* (1301) imperial Daoist *nèi-jiào* 內醮 hosted at Yān (Beijing) by the **Forty-Second-Generation Tiān-shī Zhāng Yǔ-cái** 張與材 — recorded in Gōng Kāi's 龔開 preface to Mǎ's collection. Documented alongside fellow Daoist priest **Wáng Zǐ-yóu** 王子繇 in the same imperial event.

**Sòng-loyalist identity.** Identified by the Sìkù editors via the *Shù-huái* poem in his collection as "*Sòng-mò yí-lǎo jì-tuō huáng-guān*" — a Sòng-end surviving-elder who entrusted himself in the Daoist headdress; his "*háo-yì jùn-mài zhī qì*" (great-and-unrestrained transcendent-spirit) demonstrates Daoist retreat as Sòng-loyalist *yí-mín* statement, not merely as religious-formal posture.

**Literary network.** Exchange-friendship and preface-relationship with:
- **Qiú Yuǎn** [[仇遠]] (1247–1327) — composed the principal preface attesting to Mǎ's *qīng-xū* (clear-emptiness) retreat
- **Bái Tǐng** [[白珽]] (1248–1328)
- **Gōng Kāi** 龔開 (1222–1304) — major Sòng-loyalist Daoist; composed the Dà-dé-era imperial-Daoist event preface

**Poetic style.** Sìkù editors evaluate as *shén-gǔ xiù-qiān* (spirit-bone elegant-tall), *fēng-lì qiú-shàng* (style-strength firm-rising), with *láng-láng jīn-shí zhī yīn* (clarion-bright metallic-and-mineral sound).

**Transmission.** The Máo Jìn 毛晉 print of the *Xiá-wài shī-jí* placed Mǎ after Zhāng Yǔ (Bó-yǔ 伯雨); the Sìkù editors correct this — all extant prefaces are Dà-dé-era (1297+), placing Mǎ as Zhāng Yǔ's elder.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0475]] *Xiá-wài shī-jí* 霞外詩集 (撰).
