Yuán-period 元 Daoist priest and Sòng-loyalist *yímín* poet. *Zì* **Zhìdào** 志道; *hào* **Xūzhōng** 虛中 ("Empty-and-Composed"). Native of **Qiántáng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu, modern Zhèjiāng). CBDB 28345 records *b.* 1254; death-date uncertain.

**Religious affiliation.** Took Daoist robes and resided at the **West Lake** as a retiring priest. Active in the early-Yuán *Xuánjiào* / Quánzhēn imperial-Daoist literary circle; participated in the **Dàdé** *xīnchǒu* (1301) imperial Daoist *nèijiào* 內醮 hosted at Yān (Beijing) by the **Forty-Second-Generation Tiānshī Zhāng Yǔcái** 張與材 — recorded in Gōng Kāi's 龔開 preface to Mǎ's collection. Documented alongside fellow Daoist priest **Wáng Zǐyóu** 王子繇 in the same imperial event.

**Sòng-loyalist identity.** Identified by the Sìkù editors via the *Shùhuái* poem in his collection as "*Sòngmò yílǎo jìtuō huángguān*" — a Sòng-end surviving-elder who entrusted himself in the Daoist headdress; his "*háoyì jùnmài zhī qì*" (great-and-unrestrained transcendent-spirit) demonstrates Daoist retreat as Sòng-loyalist *yímín* statement, not merely as religious-formal posture.

**Literary network.** Exchange-friendship and preface-relationship with:
- **Qiú Yuǎn** [[仇遠]] (1247–1327) — composed the principal preface attesting to Mǎ's *qīngxū* (clear-emptiness) retreat
- **Bái Tǐng** [[白珽]] (1248–1328)
- **Gōng Kāi** 龔開 (1222–1304) — major Sòng-loyalist Daoist; composed the Dà-dé-era imperial-Daoist event preface

**Poetic style.** Sìkù editors evaluate as *shéngǔ xiùqiān* (spirit-bone elegant-tall), *fēnglì qiúshàng* (style-strength firm-rising), with *lángláng jīnshí zhī yīn* (clarion-bright metallic-and-mineral sound).

**Transmission.** The Máo Jìn 毛晉 print of the *Xiáwài shījí* placed Mǎ after Zhāng Yǔ (Bóyǔ 伯雨); the Sìkù editors correct this — all extant prefaces are Dà-dé-era (1297+), placing Mǎ as Zhāng Yǔ's elder.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0475]] *Xiáwài shījí* 霞外詩集 (撰).
