# Chén Wén-dào 陳聞道

Late-17th-century Qīng lay Buddhist and friend of [[澤|Rùn-guāng Zé]] 潤光澤. Lifedates unrecorded. Preface-writer for Rùn-guāng Zé's posthumous *Zǎo xuě jí* 澡雪集 [[KR6q0234]].
