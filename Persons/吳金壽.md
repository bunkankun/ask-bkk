**Wú Jīnshòu** 吳金壽 (*zì* Zǐmù 子木, *fl.* 1820s–1830s), Sūzhōu medical editor and publisher, disciple of Zhāng Wénxiè 張文燮. No CBDB record. Best known as the editor (*xiào* 校) responsible for the 1831 *Dàoguāng xīnmǎo* edition of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì's *Yīxiào mìchuán* 醫效秘傳 ([[KR3eh022]]), which he recovered as a manuscript from his fellow-student Xú Xuěxiāng 徐雪香 and printed alongside related Yè / Xuē / Miào case-record materials.

Wú belongs to the Sūzhōu *yīshū* 醫書 publishing milieu of the early Dàoguāng era — a circle of bibliophile-physicians who recovered, edited, and printed manuscript medical texts attributed to or associated with the Yè Tiānshì lineage. His preface to the *Yīxiào mìchuán* records the manuscript's chain of custody from Wēng Chūnyán 翁春岩's library through Xú Xuěxiāng to himself.
