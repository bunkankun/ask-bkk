# 徐蓮塘

Xú Liántáng 徐蓮塘, late-Qīng / early-Republican editor responsible for publishing the long-secret manuscript of [[KR3ep082]] *Xuēàn biànshū* — the anonymous Qīng critical commentary on [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's medical cases.
