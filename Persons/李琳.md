Lǐ Lín 李琳, an early-Yuán scholar; cited by the Sìkù *tiyao* to *Dà Yì jí shuō* [[KR1a0077]] as one of the contemporaries (alongside [[吳澄]] Wú Chéng) who praised Wáng Shēnzǐ's refined-thinking. Lifedates and biographical details are not securely attested in standard reference works.
