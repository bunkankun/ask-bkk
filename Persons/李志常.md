**Lǐ Zhìcháng** 李志常 (1193–1256) was a Yuán Quán zhēn patriarch, disciple and traveling companion of [[丘處機]] Qiū Chǔjī on his 1219–1223 journey to meet Chinggis Khan, successor to Yǐn Zhìpíng as head of the Quán zhēn order (1238–1256). Author of [[KR5g0238]] ***Cháng chūn zhēn rén xī yóu jì*** 長春真人西遊記 — **the indispensable travel-narrative of Qiū Chǔjī's journey to the Mongol court**, one of the major primary sources for 13th-century Central Asian history.
