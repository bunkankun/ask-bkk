# Yáng Qíxián 楊齊賢

*Zì* Zǐjiàn 子見. Native of *Chōnglíng* 舂陵 in *Yǒngzhōu* 永州 (modern *Níngyuǎn* 寧遠, southern Húnán). Sòng-period scholar; floruit late 12th – early 13th century by internal evidence (his commentary cites Sòng-Yuán transitional sources). His principal scholarly contribution is the *Lǐ Tàibái jí jízhù* 李太白集集注, the foundation of the Sòng-Yuán annotated tradition for Lǐ Bái's poetry, transmitted today only in Xiāo Shìyún's [[蕭士贇]] *shānbǔ* (Yuán-period revised) recension as [[KR4c0013]]. The Sìkù compilers note that Yáng's annotations follow the Sòng *kǎojù* tradition: extensive references to history and parallel poetry, conservative on textual judgments, with the limitations of one who could not always distinguish the Sòng *yuèfǔ* anthology titles from the *Hànshū yìwén zhì* originals. CBDB has no entry; no other works survive.
