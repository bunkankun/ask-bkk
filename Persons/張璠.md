# Zhāng Pán 張璠

Western Jìn 西晉 historian and *Yì* scholar. Author of *Hòu Hàn jì* 後漢紀 (a Western-Jìn history of the Eastern Hàn, lost as a whole but preserved in extensive citation in Péi Sōngzhī 裴松之's *Sānguó zhì* commentary and elsewhere) and of a *Zhōuyì jíjiě* 周易集解 (lost; cited by [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò in [[KR1a0008]]). Active during the late third / early fourth century.
