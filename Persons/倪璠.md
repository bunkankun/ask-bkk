**Ní Fán** 倪璠 (1672–1722, conventional dating), *zì* Lǔ-yù 魯玉, native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu), *jǔ-rén* of *Kāng-xī yǐ-yǒu* (1705), held office as *Nèi-gé zhōng-shū shè-rén* 內閣中書舍人 ("Drafter in the Secretariat of the Grand Secretariat"). He is the great early-Qīng *zhù*-edition annotator of Yǔ Xìn 庾信, completing his [[KR4b0018]] *Yǔ Zǐshān jí zhù* 庾子山集註 in 16 *juǎn* in 1689 (Kāngxī 28). His edition supersedes Wú Zhàoyí's earlier *jiān-zhù* ([[KR4b0017]]) by adding a *nián-pǔ* (chronological table) of Yǔ's life, broader source-citation, and key authorship corrections — notably transferring the *Péng-chéng gōng fū-rén Ěr-zhū-shì mù-zhì míng* and *Bó-mǔ Dōng-píng jùn fū-rén Lǐ-shì mù-zhì míng* stelae from Yǔ Xìn to Yáng Jiōng 楊炯 on the strength of *Wén yuàn yīng huá* evidence.
