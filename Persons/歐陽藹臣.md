Late-Qīng physician, friend and collaborator of [[袁仁賢]] Yuán Rén-xián. Named only in passing in 袁's *zì-xù* to [[KR3em034]] *Hóu-kē jīn-yuè quán-shū* as the discussion-partner with whom袁 "discussed throat-medicine syndromology and treatment in great detail" (與吾友歐陽藹臣討論喉門證治益精). No CBDB record; otherwise unknown.
