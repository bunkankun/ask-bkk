# Qiú Zhào'áo 仇兆鰲 (1638–1717)

*Zì* Cāngzhù 滄柱. Native of *Yínxiàn* 鄞縣 (modern *Níngbō*, Zhèjiāng). Qīng *Kāngxī*-period scholar-official; *jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 24 (1685), aged 47. Successively *biānxiū* 編修 of the *Hànlínyuàn*, *Hànlín shìjiǎng* 翰林侍講, and *lìbù shìláng* 吏部侍郎; retired *ca.* 1707, died 1717.

His monumental scholarly contribution is the *Dù shī xiángzhù* 杜詩詳註 [[KR4c0020]] — the standard pre-modern critical edition of Dù Fǔ [[杜甫]] — submitted to the Kāngxī court in 1693 in 25 *juǎn* (23 of poetry + 2 of prose), with a further 2-*juǎn Bǔzhù* completed in retirement. The book superseded the entire Sòng-Yuán *Qiānjiā zhù* tradition and remains the principal pre-modern reference; it is the base text of Stephen Owen's 6-volume 2016 English translation and of the Zhōnghuá 1979 standard modern reprint.

His parallel scholarly project, the *Lǎozǐ Héshànggōng zhù bǔzhù* 老子河上公註補注 (1707), is also influential in Qīng *Lǎozǐ* studies. CBDB has no entry, but the meta date 1638–1717 is the standard reference figure.
