# Qiú Zhào'áo 仇兆鰲 (1638–1717)

*Zì* Cāng-zhù 滄柱. Native of *Yín-xiàn* 鄞縣 (modern *Níng-bō*, Zhèjiāng). Qīng *Kāng-xī*-period scholar-official; *jìn-shì* of *Kāng-xī* 24 (1685), aged 47. Successively *biān-xiū* 編修 of the *Hàn-lín-yuàn*, *Hàn-lín shì-jiǎng* 翰林侍講, and *lì-bù shì-láng* 吏部侍郎; retired *ca.* 1707, died 1717.

His monumental scholarly contribution is the *Dù shī xiáng-zhù* 杜詩詳註 [[KR4c0020]] — the standard pre-modern critical edition of Dù Fǔ [[杜甫]] — submitted to the Kāng-xī court in 1693 in 25 *juǎn* (23 of poetry + 2 of prose), with a further 2-*juǎn Bǔ-zhù* completed in retirement. The book superseded the entire Sòng-Yuán *Qiān-jiā zhù* tradition and remains the principal pre-modern reference; it is the base text of Stephen Owen's 6-volume 2016 English translation and of the Zhōnghuá 1979 standard modern reprint.

His parallel scholarly project, the *Lǎo-zǐ Hé-shàng-gōng zhù bǔ-zhù* 老子河上公註補注 (1707), is also influential in Qīng *Lǎo-zǐ* studies. CBDB has no entry, but the meta date 1638–1717 is the standard reference figure.
