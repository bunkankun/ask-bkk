**Zhū Shíjìn** 朱時進 (*zì* Nánzhēn 南珍, fl. mid-Qián-lóng), Qīng physician of the Hīzhōu *Xīnān yīxué* 新安醫學 tradition. Author of the *Yī jiàn néng yī* 一見能醫 ([[KR3er025]], prefaced by Yè Fèngmáo 葉鳳毛 in *Qiánlóng* 34 = 1769) — an eight-*juǎn* mid-Qīng clinical-pedagogical handbook framed for the apprentice physician. Yè's preface describes Zhū as having "abandoned the Confucian classics for medicine" (去儒業醫) and as having "diligently and painstakingly studied" through personally hand-copying earlier physicians' secret texts. Zhū's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered. No CBDB record.
