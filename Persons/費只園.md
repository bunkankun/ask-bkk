Fèi Zhǐyuán 費只園 was a Republican-era popular fiction writer, author of *Qīngcháo Sānbǎinián Yànshǐ Yǎnyì* 清朝三百年豔史演義 ([[KR4k0311]]). The catalog entry for this text erroneously records the author as "豔史演義" (a genre label); the source file identifies the author as Fèi Zhǐyuán. No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1910s–1930s.
