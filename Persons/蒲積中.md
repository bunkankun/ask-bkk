# Pú Jī-zhòng 蒲積中 (fl. 1147)

A Southern-Sòng scholar of Méishān 眉山 (Sìchuān — the same prefecture as the Three Sūs and Sū Shùn-yú). His one securely-attested act is the compilation of the *Gǔ-jīn suì-shí zá-yǒng* 古今歲時雜詠 [[KR4h0035]] in 46 *juǎn* — an expansion of **Sòng Shòu** 宋綬's earlier 18- (or 20-) *juǎn* *Suì-shí zá-yǒng* of seasonal-festival poetry — with a self-preface dated *Shào-xīng dīng-mǎo* 紹興丁卯 (1147). Pú's contribution was to add Northern-Sòng poetry by Ōuyáng Xiū, Sū Shì, Huáng Tíng-jiān, Wáng Ān-shí (*Bàn-shān*), Méi Yáo-chén (*Wǎn-líng*), Zhāng Lěi (*Wén-qián*), and Chén Shī-dào (*Wú-jǐ*) to Sòng Shòu's original Han-through-Tang nucleus. The expanded anthology was titled *Gǔ-jīn suì-shí zá-yǒng* and is principally known by Pú's shortened title *Suì-shí zá-yǒng* 歲時雜詠. No CBDB entry.
