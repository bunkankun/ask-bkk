# Pú Jīzhòng 蒲積中 (fl. 1147)

A Southern-Sòng scholar of Méishān 眉山 (Sìchuān — the same prefecture as the Three Sūs and Sū Shùnyú). His one securely-attested act is the compilation of the *Gǔjīn suìshí záyǒng* 古今歲時雜詠 [[KR4h0035]] in 46 *juǎn* — an expansion of **Sòng Shòu** 宋綬's earlier 18- (or 20-) *juǎn* *Suìshí záyǒng* of seasonal-festival poetry — with a self-preface dated *Shàoxīng dīngmǎo* 紹興丁卯 (1147). Pú's contribution was to add Northern-Sòng poetry by Ōuyáng Xiū, Sū Shì, Huáng Tíngjiān, Wáng Ānshí (*Bànshān*), Méi Yáochén (*Wǎnlíng*), Zhāng Lěi (*Wénqián*), and Chén Shīdào (*Wújǐ*) to Sòng Shòu's original Han-through-Tang nucleus. The expanded anthology was titled *Gǔjīn suìshí záyǒng* and is principally known by Pú's shortened title *Suìshí záyǒng* 歲時雜詠. No CBDB entry.
