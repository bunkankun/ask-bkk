# Ruǎn Xiàoxù 阮孝緒

*Zì* Shìzōng 士宗. Liáng-dynasty 梁 recluse-scholar and bibliographer, native of Chénliú Wèishì 陳留尉氏 (modern Hénán). Author of the *Qīlù* 七錄 ("Seven Records"), an influential seven-fold bibliographic catalogue (preface 523) classifying the empire's books under the divisions *jīng* 經, *jì* 紀, *zǐ* 子, *wén* 文, *shù* 術, *fó* 佛, and *dào* 道. The work is lost as a whole but survives in extensive fragments and in the *Suíshū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 derivation. *Liáng shū* 梁書 (juan 51) and *Nán shǐ* 南史 (juan 76) give his biography.
