# Zhāng Wěi 張煒

Yuán-period lay scholar (*zì* Míngyuǎn 明遠) of Yúzhōng 嵎中; reassembled the [[KR6q0078]] *Bìyán lù* fragments and commissioned the 1300 reprint that became the basis of the received text.
