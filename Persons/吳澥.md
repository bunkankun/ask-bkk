# Wú Xiè 吳澥

Younger brother of [[吳沆]] Wú Hàng (fl. 1146), of Línchuān 臨川 in Fǔzhōu 撫州 (modern Jiāngxī). A commoner-scholar with no examination success or recorded office. In *Shàoxīng* 16 (1146) he and his elder brother went to the imperial residence at Hángzhōu to present writings: Xiè submitted *Yǔnèi biàn* 宇内辨 (a treatise on the inner-versus-outer political-administrative distinction) and *Lìdài jiāngyù zhì* 厯代疆域志 (a historical-geographic treatise). Neither of Xiè's works survives; the *Sìkù tíyào* on his brother's *Yì xuánjī* ([[KR1a0026]]) is the principal record of his existence.
