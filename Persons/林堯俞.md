Lín Yáoyú, *zì* Zī-bó 咨伯, *hào* Yōngzhāi 慵齋, was a native of Pútián 莆田 in Fújiàn. He took the *jìnshì* in Wànlì 17 (1589) and rose through the Hànlín Academy and the Ministry of Rites, eventually serving as Minister of Rites and as Hànlín Reader-in-Waiting. As Minister of Rites in the closing year of Wànlì and the brief Tàichāng reign, he was the official compiler-of-record (纂修) for the *Lǐbù zhìgǎo* 禮部志稿 ([[KR2l0010]]), the standing institutional history of the Ministry of Rites, completed in Tàichāng 1 (1620). After his retirement during the Tiānqǐ era he died at home; posthumous title 文簡. His birth year (1558) is given by various Míng biographical compilations and is the conventional figure adopted here.
