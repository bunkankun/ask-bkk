**Líng Hóngxiàn** 凌弘憲 (*zì* **Shū-dù** 叔度; *hào* **Tiān-chí jūshì** 天池居士, "Lay practitioner of the Heavenly Pool") — late-Ming Buddhist lay scholar and book-collector. Lifedates unrecorded. Native of **Wú-xīng** 吳興 (modern Hú-zhōu 湖州 in Zhèjiāng), the capital district of one of the most important Jiāngnán literati families of the late Ming. The Líng family of Wú-xīng was a major literati lineage with significant scholarly and printing activity in the Wàn-lì–Tiān-qǐ period, including the Líng family's celebrated colour-printed editions of literary classics; Líng Hóngxiàn belongs to this milieu.

His extant work is the **Lèngyán-jīng zhèngshū guǎngjiě** 楞嚴經證疏廣解 in 10 fascicles ([[KR6j0696]], X14 no. 288), a punctuated-and-glossed (*diǎn-shì* 點釋) recension of an earlier *Zhèng-shū* 證疏 (probably the late-Ming *zhèngshū* tradition deriving from the *Zhēng-mài shū* 正脉疏 lineage of [[真鑑]]). His own preface ("Engraving the *Lèngyán*") is dated **Tiān-qǐ 1 = 1621 CE** (天啟元年).
