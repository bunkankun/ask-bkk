# Chéngshí 成時 / Jiānmì Chéngshí 堅密成時 (1618–1678)

A leading late-Míng / early-Qīng Pure Land master, dharma heir of [[智旭]] **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655) — one of the **Four Great Monks of the Late Míng** — and the principal editor and transmitter of Ǒuyì's literary corpus. *Hào* **Jiānmì** 堅密 ("Firm-and-Dense"). His mature life spans the MíngQīng transition; he carried the Ǒuyì line through the political collapse of the Míng establishment into the early Qīng and was instrumental in the institutional consolidation of late-imperial Pure Land Buddhism.

His major editorial work is the **《淨土十要》** *Jìngtǔ shíyào* [[KR6p0067]] (X1164, 10 *juǎn*) — the **definitive late-imperial Pure Land anthology**, a curated selection of ten essential Pure Land texts (the "Ten Essentials") originally identified by his master Ǒuyì Zhìxù, with Chéngshí's editorial annotations (*píngdiǎn* 評點) and abridgement (*jiéyào* 節要). The *Shíyào* became the standard reading list of Pure Land doctrinal study in the Qīng dynasty and remains in active use in contemporary Chinese Pure Land circles. He also produced the *Jiānmì lǎorén yíshū* 堅密老人遺書 (his own collected writings) and various supplementary editions of Ǒuyì's works.
