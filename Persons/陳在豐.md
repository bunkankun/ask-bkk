# 陳在豐

Chén Zàifēng, mid-Qīng figure who in *Qiánlóng* 46 (1781) acquired the Yóu-clan throat-discipline manuscript [[KR3em022]] *Yóushì hóukē mìshū* 尤氏喉科秘書 from the Yóu family of Liángxī 梁溪 (Wúxī) for "fifty *jīn* of silver" and supplied the dated preface.
