# Guān-shuí 觀誰

Late-Míng Sichuan Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[圓䂐|Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo]] 字水圓䂐 (1605–c. 1640s, at [[KR6q0436]]). Head compiler (*děng lù* 等錄) of [[KR6q0436]] 《夔州臥龍字水禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B222, cut 1639). The *fǎ-huì* **觀誰** ("Observe-Who") is unusual — likely a Sichuan sub-branch variant or a personal rather than generational *fǎ-huì*. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
