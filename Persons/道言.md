Dào Yán 道言 (fl. Sòng) was a Sòng-dynasty Vinaya specialist known for his **Shì Sìfēn jièběn xù** 釋四分戒本序 ([[KR6k0147]]) — a one-fascicle commentary on the introductory matter (序 *xù*) of the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa*.
