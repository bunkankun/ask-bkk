**Chén Hùān** 陳笏庵 (*fl.* late 18th c. 清), Qiánlóng-era obstetrical specialist. Recorded as the author / compiler of *Tāi-chǎn mì-shū* 胎產秘書 ([[KR3ei054]]) on the strength of the published edition's attribution. The 1809 preface by Hé Róng 何榮 (古虞杏園) of Gǔ-yú reports that he received the secret manuscript from his teacher Chén Wù-ān 陳勿庵 — Hùān/Wù-ān phonetic correspondences suggest these may be variant transcriptions of the same person. No further biographical data located in standard reference works. Not in CBDB.
