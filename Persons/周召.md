Zhōu Zhào 周召 (Qīng) was a Mid-Qīng *biji* writer. Author of the *Shuāng-qiáo suí bǐ* 雙橋隨筆 (KR3a0113, 12 juan). Provenance and lifedates unrecorded. CBDB has multiple homonyms with no secure identification.
