# 王鐸

Wáng Duó (1592–1652)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng calligrapher of the highest renown. Senior Míng official who surrendered to the Qīng. Wrote the 1650 preface to [[無是道人]] Wúshì dàorén's [[KR6c0073]] *Jīngāng jīng rúshì jiě*, calling the author *xiàn zǎiguān shēn* 現宰官身 ("manifesting in the body of an official-administrator").
