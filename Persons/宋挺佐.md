Mid-Míng 明 scholar of Nán-yáng 南陽 (modern Hé-nán). Active in the early Jiā-jìng era (1520s). Compiler of the *Huán-shān yí-gǎo* [[KR4d0452]] — a 2-*juàn* gathered-fragments collection of the Yuán-period historian Yáng Huàn 楊奐 (1186–1255), whose original 60–120-*juàn* corpus had been almost entirely lost. The Sìkù editors specifically praise Sòng Tǐng-zuǒ's editorial method: each piece in the *yí-gǎo* is annotated with its provenance (which book it was gleaned from, which person preserved it, or where the stone inscription is located) — a level of philological care characteristic of substantial mid-Míng scholarship. The compilation also includes a *Kǎo-suì lüè* (Brief Chronological Examination, i.e. *nián-pǔ*) and an appended *fù-lù* of biographical sketches. No firm CBDB record located.
