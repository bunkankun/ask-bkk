Early-Qīng *jìnshì* (順治 4 / 1647) from **Jīntán** 金壇 (Jiāngsū). *Zì* Hǔchén 虎臣, *hào* Suí-ān 綏菴. Served briefly as a Hànlín Editor 翰林編修 and a *fù-zhǔ-kǎo* 副主考 in provincial examinations before abandoning office in the late 1650s and retreating to Mt. Éméi 峨嵋 in Sìchuān, where he lived as a Buddhist recluse and wrote the *Éméi-shān zhì* 峨眉山志 (the standard Qīng-period gazetteer of the mountain). Compiled the *Sòng Jǐng-lián wèi-kè jí* 宋景濂未刻集 [[KR4e0003]] in Shùnzhì 12 (1655) on the basis of the original Hóngwǔ cutting of Sòng Lián's collection preserved in the [[文徵明]] family. Died 1673.
