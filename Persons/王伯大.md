# Wáng Bódà 王伯大 (d. 1253)

Native of *Wú-zhōu* 婺州 *Yì-wū* 義烏 (modern *Yì-wū* in central Zhèjiāng). Sòng *Lǐ-zōng*-period scholar-official; *jìn-shì* of *Jiā-tài* 2 (1202). Successively *zhōng-shū shè-rén* 中書舍人 and *Hàn-lín xué-shì* 翰林學士 in the late Lǐ-zōng court. Died in 1253.

His principal scholarly contribution is the *Bié-běn Hán wén kǎo-yì* [[KR4c0044]] — the consolidated Sòng *kǎo-yì* edition of Hán Yù [[韓愈]] in 40 *juǎn*, in which Zhū Xī's [[朱熹]] originally separately printed *Yuán-běn Hán jí kǎo-yì* (= [[KR4c0043]]) is dispersed into the body of Hán Yù's collection underneath the relevant lines. The 40-*juǎn* arrangement preserves the original Lǐ Hàn 李漢 [[李漢]] (Tang-period) configuration. Wáng's editorial consolidation, completed *ca.* 1234–1244, became the dominant Sòng-Yuán-Míng circulation of the Hán-Yù corpus, almost completely displacing Zhū's separate *yuán-běn*.
