# Wáng Bódà 王伯大 (d. 1253)

Native of *Wúzhōu* 婺州 *Yìwū* 義烏 (modern *Yìwū* in central Zhèjiāng). Sòng *Lǐzōng*-period scholar-official; *jìnshì* of *Jiātài* 2 (1202). Successively *zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人 and *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士 in the late Lǐzōng court. Died in 1253.

His principal scholarly contribution is the *Biéběn Hán wén kǎoyì* [[KR4c0044]] — the consolidated Sòng *kǎoyì* edition of Hán Yù [[韓愈]] in 40 *juǎn*, in which Zhū Xī's [[朱熹]] originally separately printed *Yuánběn Hán jí kǎoyì* (= [[KR4c0043]]) is dispersed into the body of Hán Yù's collection underneath the relevant lines. The 40-*juǎn* arrangement preserves the original Lǐ Hàn 李漢 [[李漢]] (Tang-period) configuration. Wáng's editorial consolidation, completed *ca.* 1234–1244, became the dominant SòngYuánMíng circulation of the HánYù corpus, almost completely displacing Zhū's separate *yuánběn*.
