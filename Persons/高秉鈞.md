**Gāo Bǐngjūn** 高秉鈞 (*zì* Jǐntíng 錦庭, 1755–1827, 清), Qīng-period Wúxī 無錫 physician. Disciple of Fàn Shèngxué 范聖學 and Dù Yúnmén 杜雲門; trained over thirty years in both internal and external medicine, grounded in the *Nèijīng*. Author of [[KR3ek042]] ***Yángkē xīndé jí*** 瘍科心得集 (3 *juǎn* + appendix, self-prefaced Jiāqìng 10 / 1805) — a landmark Qīng *wàikē* work that, together with Chén Shígōng's [[KR3ek014]] *Wàikē zhèngzōng* (1617) and Wáng Hóngxù's [[KR3ek017]] *Wàikē quánshēng jí* (1740), forms the canonical triad of late-imperial *wàikē* schools. Gāo is the founder of the *xīndé* / *sānjiāo biànzhèng* / anatomy-by-region school of surgery, having pioneered the systematic application of Wēnbìng 溫病學 theory (acquired from the Sūzhōu school) to external pathology. His maternal nephew [[王旭高]] Wáng Xùgāo (1798–1862) trained with him in external medicine before transitioning to internal medicine; Wáng's [[KR3ek023]] *Wàikē zhèngzhì mìyào* preserves that family lineage. CBDB id 87354 carries lifedates 1755–1827, confirmed by the *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú niánbiǎo*.
