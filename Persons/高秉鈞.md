**Gāo Bǐng-jūn** 高秉鈞 (*zì* Jǐn-tíng 錦庭, 1755–1827, 清), Qīng-period Wú-xī 無錫 physician. Disciple of Fàn Shèng-xué 范聖學 and Dù Yún-mén 杜雲門; trained over thirty years in both internal and external medicine, grounded in the *Nèi-jīng*. Author of [[KR3ek042]] ***Yáng-kē xīn-dé jí*** 瘍科心得集 (3 *juǎn* + appendix, self-prefaced Jiā-qìng 10 / 1805) — a landmark Qīng *wài-kē* work that, together with Chén Shí-gōng's [[KR3ek014]] *Wài-kē zhèng-zōng* (1617) and Wáng Hóng-xù's [[KR3ek017]] *Wài-kē quán-shēng jí* (1740), forms the canonical triad of late-imperial *wài-kē* schools. Gāo is the founder of the *xīn-dé* / *sān-jiāo biàn-zhèng* / anatomy-by-region school of surgery, having pioneered the systematic application of Wēn-bìng 溫病學 theory (acquired from the Sūzhōu school) to external pathology. His maternal nephew [[王旭高]] Wáng Xù-gāo (1798–1862) trained with him in external medicine before transitioning to internal medicine; Wáng's [[KR3ek023]] *Wài-kē zhèng-zhì mì-yào* preserves that family lineage.
