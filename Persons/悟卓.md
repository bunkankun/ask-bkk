# Wù-zhuó 悟卓 (Pò-shí Wù-zhuó 破石悟卓)

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán master of Sichuan. *Hào* Pò-shí 破石 ("Broken Stone"). Lifedates 1609/11/15 – 1654/1/25 (Wànlì 37/10/19 – Shùnzhì 10/12/8, age 45, per the *xíng zhuàng* in his *Jǐn-píng Pò-shí Zhuó chánshī zá zhù* juan 1).

Active at Jǐn-píng-shān 錦屏山 ("Brocade-Screen Mountain") in Sichuan. Part of the mid-17th-century Sichuan Chán regional community.

Surviving work: the *Jǐn-píng Pò-shí Zhuó chánshī zá zhù* 錦屏破石卓禪師雜著 [[KR6q0228]] — a brief one-juan collection of poems and miscellaneous prose compositions, compiled by his attendant [[超常|Chāo-cháng]] with a biographical *xíng zhuàng* by [[發林|Fā-lín]].
