# Wùzhuó 悟卓 (Pòshí Wùzhuó 破石悟卓)

MíngQīng transitional Chán master of Sichuan. *Hào* Pòshí 破石 ("Broken Stone"). Lifedates 1609/11/15 – 1654/1/25 (Wànlì 37/10/19 – Shùnzhì 10/12/8, age 45, per the *xíng zhuàng* in his *Jǐnpíng Pòshí Zhuó chánshī zá zhù* juan 1).

Active at Jǐnpíngshān 錦屏山 ("Brocade-Screen Mountain") in Sichuan. Part of the mid-17th-century Sichuan Chán regional community.

Surviving work: the *Jǐnpíng Pòshí Zhuó chánshī zá zhù* 錦屏破石卓禪師雜著 [[KR6q0228]] — a brief one-juan collection of poems and miscellaneous prose compositions, compiled by his attendant [[超常|Chāocháng]] with a biographical *xíng zhuàng* by [[發林|Fālín]].
