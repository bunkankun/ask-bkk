**Lǐ Shìcái** 李士材 (= [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, *zì* Shìcái 士材, *hào* Niàné 念莪, 1588–1655), the same person as Lǐ Zhōngzǐ; the *zì* Shìcái is the more common identifier in late-Míng / early-Qīng medical literature. See [[李中梓]] for the full notice. Charged by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]] with having identified dysentery but missed its root.
