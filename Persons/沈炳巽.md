Shěn Bǐngxùn 沈炳巽 (fl. early 18th c.), *zì* Yìzhān 繹旃, of Guī-ān 歸安 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng), was a *kǎojù* scholar and the author of the *Shuǐjīng zhù jí-shì dìng-é* ([[KR2k0059]]), a 40-juan early-Qīng critical edition of Lì Dàoyuán's *Shuǐjīng zhù* compiled over nine years. He was the elder brother of the Yōngzhèng-era historiographer Shěn Bǐngzhèn 沈炳震 (1679–1737); his own dates are not recorded but place him squarely in the late Kāngxī through Yōngzhèng eras. Beyond the *Shuǐjīng zhù* edition, he is the author of the literary collection *Yìshān shīwén jí* 詩山詩文集.
