# Dámò 達默 (Qīng, mid-19th c.)

A Qīng Pure Land monk associated with the *Hóngluóshān* 紅螺山 (north of Beijing) — the Pure Land lineage of *Zīfúsì* 資福寺 / *Bìyuàn* 碧雲 that produced the late-Qīng / Republican Pure Land master Yìnguāng 印光. He is known principally as the author of the substantive *zàochāo* 造鈔 ("composed sub-commentary") on [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù's *Ēmítuó jīng yàojiě* 阿彌陀經要解 — the resulting composite work being the *Ēmítuó jīng yàojiě biànméngchāo* 阿彌陀經要解便蒙鈔 [[KR6p0025]]. The work was completed in three *juǎn* and circulated alongside Zhìxù's *Yàojiě* as the standard Qīng Pure Land textbook, jointly cut with [[達林]] Dámò's collaborator/co-edition's contributor Dálín 達林 (參訂).

His exact lifedates are not preserved. The *biànméngchāo* itself is datable to the mid-nineteenth century from the prefatory material; the title *biàn méng* 便蒙 ("convenient for the unenlightened") implies a popularising pedagogical aim consistent with the late-Qīng Pure Land revival sponsored by the Hóngluóshān lineage.
