**Shěn Hù-zhān** 沈岵瞻 (*fl.* mid-18th c., Xī-shān 錫山 / Wú-xī) — elder brother of the physician [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn-áo (1717–1776), of the same Wú-xī Shěn family. Author of the publication preface to his brother's *Zá-bìng yuán-liú xī-zhú* 雜病源流犀燭 ([[KR3er065]]), in which he characterises Shěn's first forty years as devoted to the Confucian curriculum and his second forty as devoted to medicine. Not in CBDB.
