**Shěn Hùzhān** 沈岵瞻 (*fl.* mid-18th c., Xīshān 錫山 / Wúxī) — elder brother of the physician [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīnáo (1717–1776), of the same Wúxī Shěn family. Author of the publication preface to his brother's *Zábìng yuánliú xīzhú* 雜病源流犀燭 ([[KR3er065]]), in which he characterises Shěn's first forty years as devoted to the Confucian curriculum and his second forty as devoted to medicine. Not in CBDB.
