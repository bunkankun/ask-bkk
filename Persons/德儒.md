Dé-rú 德儒, *zì* **Zhàn-qīng** 湛清 ("Pure-Clear"); referred to as **Zhàn-qīng Dé-rú** 湛清德儒. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Kōng-gǔ Dào-chéng** [[道澄]] (b. 1616). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*biān*) of his master's massive *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0583]] 《空谷道澄禪師語錄》 20 juan, J39 B471.

**Sources.** 《空谷道澄禪師語錄》 vol. 14 (X41nB471 p. 982a27).
