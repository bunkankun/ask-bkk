Tiānxīzāi 天息災 (probably Skt. *Devaśāntika*; DILA Authority A000146) was the original Chinese name of the North-Indian monk later renamed Fǎxián 法賢 in Yōngxī 雍熙 4 (987 CE) by Sòng Tàizōng. The name 天息災 is used in the bylines of his pre-987 translations; 法賢 in the post-987 ones; he is the same physical person. For full biographical details see [[法賢]]. Translations under the 天息災 byline include the [[KR6a0081|*Fēn-bié shàn-è bào-yìng jīng* 分別善惡報應經]] (T81). He died on 10 September 1000 CE and was given the posthumous name Huìbiàn 慧辯.
