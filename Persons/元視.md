Yuán-shì 元視, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Yuǎn-ān Běn-tǎn** [[本僼]] 遠菴本僼 (1622–1682) at **Ruì-yán-sì** in Níng-bō. Lead compiler of the 16-juan 《遠菴僼禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0511]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
