Yuánshì 元視, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Yuǎnān Běntǎn** [[本僼]] 遠菴本僼 (1622–1682) at **Ruìyánsì** in Níngbō. Lead compiler of the 16-juan 《遠菴僼禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0511]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
