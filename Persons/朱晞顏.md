Zhū Xīyán 朱晞顏 (Yuán), *zì* Jǐngyuān 景淵. There are two Yuán-period **朱晞顏**, both of whom must be carefully distinguished: (1) The 朱晞顏 of *Jīngbèiyín jí* [[KR4d0525]] — author of the **鯨背吟** maritime-travel poems — who was at one time identified with **宋無** (Sòng Wú [[宋無]]) of *Cuìhán jí* [[KR4d0498]] on the basis of Zhào Mèngfǔ's preface ("旧以晞顏字行" — "in early life he used Xīyán as his *zì*"), but whose case is left as *yí yǐ chuán yí* by the *Sìkù tíyào*. (2) The 朱晞顏 of *Piáoquán yíngǎo* [[KR4d0518]] — a man of Chángxīng 長興 (Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng), *zì* Jǐngyuān 景淵 — clearly a different person, attested in Wú Chéng's 吳澄 *mùbiǎo* for his father Wénjìn 文進. Career: initially *Píngyángzhōu Měnggǔyuàn* (because trained in the *guóshū*); then *Chánglín chéng sī zhǔ yánfù*; later *Jiāngxī Ruìzhōu jiānshuì* — terminated as a low-level *jùnyì bēilì*. The Chángxīng 朱晞顏 of [[KR4d0518]] is the principal subject of this page. The catalog meta for both work-IDs gives lifedates 1221–1279, which the *Sìkù tíyào* does **not** confirm (the *tíyào* leaves dates blank); 1221–1279 are not Yuán-era dates for either person and seem to be a catalog error. Standard Yuán-poetry reference (Yáng Lián, *Yuánshī shǐ*) treats both as flourishing in mid-Yuán.
