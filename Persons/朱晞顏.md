Zhū Xī-yán 朱晞顏 (Yuán), *zì* Jǐng-yuān 景淵. There are two Yuán-period **朱晞顏**, both of whom must be carefully distinguished: (1) The 朱晞顏 of *Jīng-bèi-yín jí* [[KR4d0525]] — author of the **鯨背吟** maritime-travel poems — who was at one time identified with **宋無** (Sòng Wú [[宋無]]) of *Cuì-hán jí* [[KR4d0498]] on the basis of Zhào Mèng-fǔ's preface ("旧以晞顏字行" — "in early life he used Xī-yán as his *zì*"), but whose case is left as *yí yǐ chuán yí* by the *Sìkù tíyào*. (2) The 朱晞顏 of *Piáo-quán yín-gǎo* [[KR4d0518]] — a man of Cháng-xīng 長興 (Hú-zhōu, Zhèjiāng), *zì* Jǐng-yuān 景淵 — clearly a different person, attested in Wú Chéng's 吳澄 *mù-biǎo* for his father Wén-jìn 文進. Career: initially *Píng-yáng-zhōu Měng-gǔ-yuàn* (because trained in the *guó-shū*); then *Cháng-lín chéng sī zhǔ yán-fù*; later *Jiāng-xī Ruì-zhōu jiān-shuì* — terminated as a low-level *jùn-yì bēi-lì*. The Cháng-xīng 朱晞顏 of [[KR4d0518]] is the principal subject of this page. The catalog meta for both work-IDs gives lifedates 1221–1279, which the *Sìkù tíyào* does **not** confirm (the *tíyào* leaves dates blank); 1221–1279 are not Yuán-era dates for either person and seem to be a catalog error. Standard Yuán-poetry reference (Yáng Lián, *Yuán-shī shǐ*) treats both as flourishing in mid-Yuán.
