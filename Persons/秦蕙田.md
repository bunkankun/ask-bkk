Qín Huìtián 秦蕙田 (1702–1764), *zì* Shù-fēng 樹峰, *hào* Wèi-jīng 味經, native of Jīn-kuì 金匱 (Wú-xī 無錫, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of Qián-lóng 1 (1736); held a series of senior offices including *Lǐ-bù shàngshū*, *Xíng-bù shàngshū*, and *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo* (Grand Guardian of the Heir-Apparent). His one major scholarly work — and one of the largest individual *Sānlǐ* compendia in the entire Chinese imperial bibliographic tradition — is the *Wǔlǐ tōngkǎo* 五禮通考 [[KR1d0087]] in 262 juan, composed across his late official career as a continuation of Xú Qiánxué's [[KR1d0051]] *Dú-lǐ tōng-kǎo* extending its mourning-ritual editorial framework to all five canonical-ritual classes (*jí, xiōng, jūn, bīn, jiā*). The catalog meta records the author's name as 泰蕙田 (a graphic typo for 秦蕙田); the standard biographical sources and the work's own preface confirm 秦蕙田. CBDB id 56839 (1702–1764). *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 304.
