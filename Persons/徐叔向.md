# 徐叔向 Xú Shūxiàng

**Xú Shūxiàng** 徐叔向 (Liú-Sòng to Liáng), early-medieval physician of the famous Xú-family hereditary medical lineage; reputed author of a *Míngtáng* 明堂 acupoint manual (now lost), cited in Edo Japanese acupuncture compendia such as [[原昌克]] Hara Masakatsu's *Jīngxué huìjiě* 經穴匯解 ([[KR3ee022]]).
