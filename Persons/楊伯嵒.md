Southern-Sòng *xiǎoxué* scholar and minor official, *zì* Yànsī 彥思 (or Yànzhān 彥瞻 in his preface), *hào* Yǒngzhāi 泳齋. Self-styled as a man of Dàijùn 代郡 (an old place-name then under Jīn rule) — i.e., the family ancestral place — but actually a Southern-Sòng official: under Chúnyòu (1241–1252), he served as *Gōngbù láng* and was *Qúzhōu shǒu* (Prefect of Qúzhōu). His one surviving *xiǎoxué* work is the *Jiǔjīng bǔyùn* 九經補韻 [[KR1j0063]] (1 *juàn*, prefaced Jiādìng 17 / 1224 / *jǐwàng* of the 10th month) — an attempt to supplement the official *Lǐbù yùnlüè* with c. 79 graphs from the Nine Classics not covered in the official rhyme-book, plus 88 additional graphs avoided by the *yùnlüè* on grounds of *sàngzhì* (mourning-rite associations) or *yīnyìfúshùn* (irregular reading). The author's preface explicitly states the supplement is for private use, not official petition. CBDB cbdbId 409354 has no firm lifedates.
