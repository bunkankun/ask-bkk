Southern-Sòng *xiǎo-xué* scholar and minor official, *zì* Yàn-sī 彥思 (or Yàn-zhān 彥瞻 in his preface), *hào* Yǒng-zhāi 泳齋. Self-styled as a man of Dài-jùn 代郡 (an old place-name then under Jīn rule) — i.e., the family ancestral place — but actually a Southern-Sòng official: under Chún-yòu (1241–1252), he served as *Gōng-bù láng* and was *Qú-zhōu shǒu* (Prefect of Qú-zhōu). His one surviving *xiǎo-xué* work is the *Jiǔ-jīng bǔ-yùn* 九經補韻 [[KR1j0063]] (1 *juàn*, prefaced Jiā-dìng 17 / 1224 / *jǐ-wàng* of the 10th month) — an attempt to supplement the official *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* with c. 79 graphs from the Nine Classics not covered in the official rhyme-book, plus 88 additional graphs avoided by the *yùn-lüè* on grounds of *sàng-zhì* (mourning-rite associations) or *yīn-yì-fú-shùn* (irregular reading). The author's preface explicitly states the supplement is for private use, not official petition. CBDB cbdbId 409354 has no firm lifedates.
