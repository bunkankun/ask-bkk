Xú Zhéshēn 徐哲身 was a popular Shanghai fiction writer active during the Republican period (ca. 1910s–1930s). He specialized in historical novels (*lìshǐ xiǎoshuō* 歷史小說) of the entertainment-popular variety, combining adventure, romance, and historical dramatization of Qīng dynasty events. His output was substantial and he composed a brief self-biography preserved in the preface to *Dàqīng Sānjié* 大清三傑 ([[KR4k0301]]). His known works in the Kanripo corpus include *Dàqīng Sānjié* 大清三傑 (Three heroes of the Qing, [[KR4k0301]]) and *Hàndài Gōngtíng Yànshǐ* 漢代宮廷豔史 (Scandalous history of the Hàn imperial court, [[KR4k0314]]). Precise birth and death dates have not been established.
