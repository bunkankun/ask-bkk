Lǐ Chāng-qí 李昌祺 (1376–1452), *míng* **Zhēn** 禎, *zì* **Chāng-qí** 昌祺 (he went by his *zì*), *hào* **Yùn-pì** 運甓 (the **Táo Kǎn** brick-carrying discipline). Native of **Lú-líng** 廬陵 (Jí-ān, Jiāng-xī). Yǒng-lè *jiǎ-shēn* (1404) *jìn-shì*; selected *shù-jí-shì* 庶吉士 (Lǐ-bù *kē*); appointed *Lǐ-bù láng-zhōng* 禮部郎中. Took part in the **Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn** compilation, particularly resolving obscure-book and doubtful-matter queries. Through office to **Guǎng-xī** and **Hé-nán** *zuǒ bù-zhèng-shǐ* 左布政使. *Míng shǐ* j. 161. Independently famous as the author of the **Jiǎn-dēng yú-huà** 剪燈餘話 (mid-Yǒng-lè *zhì-guài* / *chuán-qí* short-story collection, sequel to **Qú Yòu** 瞿佑's *Jiǎn-dēng xīn-huà*) — one of the principal literary documents of the early-Míng *zhì-guài* revival. The formal-canonical poetic-and-*cí* corpus survives as [[KR4e0096]] *Yùn-pì màn-gǎo*. CBDB id 34496 (1376–1452); also recorded under c_personid 67089 (1376–1452, *Lǐ Zhēn*).
