Lǐ Chāngqí 李昌祺 (1376–1452), *míng* **Zhēn** 禎, *zì* **Chāngqí** 昌祺 (he went by his *zì*), *hào* **Yùnpì** 運甓 (the **Táo Kǎn** brick-carrying discipline). Native of **Lúlíng** 廬陵 (Jíān, Jiāngxī). Yǒnglè *jiǎshēn* (1404) *jìnshì*; selected *shùjíshì* 庶吉士 (Lǐbù *kē*); appointed *Lǐbù lángzhōng* 禮部郎中. Took part in the **Yǒnglè dàdiǎn** compilation, particularly resolving obscure-book and doubtful-matter queries. Through office to **Guǎngxī** and **Hénán** *zuǒ bùzhèngshǐ* 左布政使. *Míng shǐ* j. 161. Independently famous as the author of the **Jiǎndēng yúhuà** 剪燈餘話 (mid-Yǒng-lè *zhìguài* / *chuánqí* short-story collection, sequel to **Qú Yòu** 瞿佑's *Jiǎndēng xīnhuà*) — one of the principal literary documents of the early-Míng *zhìguài* revival. The formal-canonical poetic-and-*cí* corpus survives as [[KR4e0096]] *Yùnpì màngǎo*. CBDB id 34496 (1376–1452); also recorded under c_personid 67089 (1376–1452, *Lǐ Zhēn*).
