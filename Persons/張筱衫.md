**Zhāng Xiǎo-shān** 張筱衫 (also written 筱衫; *míng* Zhèn-jūn 振鋆, originally Lǐ-quán 醴泉; *hào* Tì-lì-zǐ 惕厲子) was a late-Qīng physician of Bǎo-yìng 寶應 (modern Jiāngsū), active in Yáng-zhōu 揚州 and the surrounding Jiāngnán medical-network of the *Guāng-xù* era. His best-known work is the four-juan pediatric-massage manual [[KR3ee054]] *Lí-zhèng àn-mó yào-shù* 釐正按摩要術 (1888), an edited and reorganized recension of a transmitted Wàn-lì-era manuscript *Tuī-ná yào-jué* 推拿要訣 by **Zhōu Yú-fān** 周於蕃 of Hú-běi (recovered and given to Zhāng for editing by his namesake Zhāng Yán-lǐ 張言禮 / Xīn-qiáo 心樵 of Dān-tú 丹徒). Zhāng's prefaces and凡例 to that work describe a *Shù-gǔ-zhāi yī-shū sān-zhǒng* 述古齋醫書三種 — a three-work series of which the *Àn-mó yào-shù* is the first; the others (announced as *Shā-hóu zhèng-yì* 痧喉正義 in one juan and *Dòu-zhěn biàn-zhèng lù* 痘疹辨證錄 in four juan) followed in subsequent years. The Wáng Yí-zhèng 王儀鄭 preface to *Lí-zhèng àn-mó yào-shù* records that Zhāng treated the prefacer's two sons through an epidemic in 1890 (*gēng-yín* 庚寅) when both children recovered after his care — a glimpse of his clinical reputation in the Jiāngsū / Yáng-zhōu region.

CBDB entry **518977** lists the name 張振鋆 without dates; no birthyear, deathyear, or *fl.* range is recorded there, and the standard Republican / PRC pediatric-medicine surveys give only the 1888 self-preface date as a fixed point. He is therefore conventionally dated *fl.* 1888 (*Guāng-xù* 14, *wù-zǐ*).

He is also recorded under the *hào* **Tì-lì-zǐ** 惕厲子 (a *Yìjīng*-derived studio-name from the *Qián* 乾 hexagram's *zhōng-rì qián-qián, xī tì-rúo lì* 終日乾乾，夕惕若厲) — the signature he uses for his own original supplementary chapter in the *Lí-zhèng àn-mó yào-shù*.
