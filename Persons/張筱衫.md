**Zhāng Xiǎoshān** 張筱衫 (also written 筱衫; *míng* Zhènjūn 振鋆, originally Lǐquán 醴泉; *hào* Tìlìzǐ 惕厲子) was a late-Qīng physician of Bǎoyìng 寶應 (modern Jiāngsū), active in Yángzhōu 揚州 and the surrounding Jiāngnán medical-network of the *Guāngxù* era. His best-known work is the four-juan pediatric-massage manual [[KR3ee054]] *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù* 釐正按摩要術 (1888), an edited and reorganized recension of a transmitted Wàn-lì-era manuscript *Tuīná yàojué* 推拿要訣 by **Zhōu Yúfān** 周於蕃 of Húběi (recovered and given to Zhāng for editing by his namesake Zhāng Yánlǐ 張言禮 / Xīnqiáo 心樵 of Dāntú 丹徒). Zhāng's prefaces and凡例 to that work describe a *Shùgǔzhāi yīshū sānzhǒng* 述古齋醫書三種 — a three-work series of which the *Ànmó yàoshù* is the first; the others (announced as *Shāhóu zhèngyì* 痧喉正義 in one juan and *Dòuzhěn biànzhèng lù* 痘疹辨證錄 in four juan) followed in subsequent years. The Wáng Yízhèng 王儀鄭 preface to *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù* records that Zhāng treated the prefacer's two sons through an epidemic in 1890 (*gēngyín* 庚寅) when both children recovered after his care — a glimpse of his clinical reputation in the Jiāngsū / Yángzhōu region.

CBDB entry **518977** lists the name 張振鋆 without dates; no birthyear, deathyear, or *fl.* range is recorded there, and the standard Republican / PRC pediatric-medicine surveys give only the 1888 self-preface date as a fixed point. He is therefore conventionally dated *fl.* 1888 (*Guāngxù* 14, *wùzǐ*).

He is also recorded under the *hào* **Tìlìzǐ** 惕厲子 (a *Yìjīng*-derived studio-name from the *Qián* 乾 hexagram's *zhōngrì qiánqián, xī tìrúo lì* 終日乾乾，夕惕若厲) — the signature he uses for his own original supplementary chapter in the *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù*.
