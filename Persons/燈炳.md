Dēngbǐng 燈炳, *zì* **Héngshān** 衡山 (also **Chōnggǔ** 沖谷); often referred to as **Qìngyún Bǐng** 慶雲炳 from his second abbacy. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (in the Sìchuān **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line). Born **明萬曆辛亥四月十一日子時** = **1611-05-22** Gregorian; died **庚申六月十八日** = **1680-07-13**, age 70. Native of **Héjiāng** 合江 (Sìchuān).

**Formation.** As a youth visited the elder **Jùyún** 聚雲老人 = **Jùyún Chuīwàn** 聚雲吹萬 at **Bǎofēng** 寶峰. After Jùyún's death sought instruction from **Qìngzhōng** 慶忠 at **Zhǐjiāng** 枳江; became *dìyī zuò* (head monk) under him.

**Abbacies (two).** **Kuízhōufǔ Shízhù Jíxiáng chánsì** 夔州府石砫吉祥禪寺 (entered **順治壬辰七月望日** = **1652-08-19** on the invitation of patron **Sū Màoyǔ** 蘇茂宇 of 祇園檀越); **Qìngyún chánsì** 慶雲禪寺 (the second seat giving him his sobriquet **Qìngyún Bǐng**).

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* / lineage-figures include **Biéān** 別菴和尚 (active in **Pǔtuó** 普陀); the third-generation grandson **Jǐhuàn chánrén** 几幻禪人 carried the manuscript south for engraving. *Fǎsūn* compiler **Zōngwèi** [[宗位]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B438, compiled by *fǎsūn* **Zōngwèi** [[宗位]] *biān*; with two prefaces — by 龍池後裔閩莆 **Chāoyuán** [[超源]] (Mǐnpǔ Dànyá) dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; and by **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] (1649–1720), then *zhènshǒu Zhèjiāng děngchù dìfāng Dìnghǎi zǒngbīngguān zuǒ dūdū* (military commander of Dìnghǎi, Zhèjiāng), dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1.

**Sources.** 《衡山禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈塔銘〉; 《五燈全書》.
