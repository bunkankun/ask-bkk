Dēng-bǐng 燈炳, *zì* **Héng-shān** 衡山 (also **Chōng-gǔ** 沖谷); often referred to as **Qìng-yún Bǐng** 慶雲炳 from his second abbacy. Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line). Born **明萬曆辛亥四月十一日子時** = **1611-05-22** Gregorian; died **庚申六月十八日** = **1680-07-13**, age 70. Native of **Hé-jiāng** 合江 (Sì-chuān).

**Formation.** As a youth visited the elder **Jù-yún** 聚雲老人 = **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn** 聚雲吹萬 at **Bǎo-fēng** 寶峰. After Jù-yún's death sought instruction from **Qìng-zhōng** 慶忠 at **Zhǐ-jiāng** 枳江; became *dì-yī zuò* (head monk) under him.

**Abbacies (two).** **Kuí-zhōu-fǔ Shí-zhù Jí-xiáng chán-sì** 夔州府石砫吉祥禪寺 (entered **順治壬辰七月望日** = **1652-08-19** on the invitation of patron **Sū Mào-yǔ** 蘇茂宇 of 祇園檀越); **Qìng-yún chán-sì** 慶雲禪寺 (the second seat giving him his sobriquet **Qìng-yún Bǐng**).

**Disciples.** Major *fǎ-sì* / lineage-figures include **Bié-ān** 別菴和尚 (active in **Pǔ-tuó** 普陀); the third-generation grandson **Jǐ-huàn chán-rén** 几幻禪人 carried the manuscript south for engraving. *Fǎ-sūn* compiler **Zōng-wèi** [[宗位]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0554]] 《衡山禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B438, compiled by *fǎ-sūn* **Zōng-wèi** [[宗位]] *biān*; with two prefaces — by 龍池後裔閩莆 **Chāo-yuán** [[超源]] (Mǐn-pǔ Dàn-yá) dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; and by **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] (1649–1720), then *zhèn-shǒu Zhè-jiāng děng-chù dì-fāng Dìng-hǎi zǒng-bīng-guān zuǒ dū-dū* (military commander of Dìng-hǎi, Zhè-jiāng), dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1.

**Sources.** 《衡山禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈塔銘〉; 《五燈全書》.
