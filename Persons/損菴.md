# Sǔn'ān 損菴

Late-Míng Buddhist figure, mentioned by [[德清]] Hānshān Déqīng in his preface to *Xìngxiàng tōngshuō* ([[KR6n0102]]) as one of the compilers of the *Zhèngyì* 證義 commentary on the *Bāshí guījǔ* 八識規矩 — together with "Wáng tài-shǐ" 王太史 (a Hànlín Wáng) — in the Wànlì-era Yogācāra revival circle. Otherwise unidentified; no biographical material located.
