Zōkai 藏海 (Zàng-hǎi; also recorded as **Genkū Zōkai** 玄空藏海), Japanese Sanron 三論 monk of the early Heian period (dates not securely documented; conventionally placed in the 8th–9th century, roughly contemporary with or shortly after [[安澄]] Anchō, 763–814). Author of [[KR6m0011]] *Shí'èr-mén lùn shū wén-sī jì* 十二門論疏聞思記 (T65n2257), a single-fascicle lecture-record (*kikigaki*) on [[吉藏]] Jí-zàng's *Shí'èr-mén lùn shū* (T1825 = [[KR6m0009]]), preserving the early Japanese Sanron oral exegesis of Jí-zàng's preface. Probably affiliated with Daianji 大安寺 or Tōdaiji 東大寺. DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001887.

A separate twentieth-century Sōtō Zen monk also named 藏海 (DILA A037695, "大實藏海" Daijitsu Zōkai) is unrelated.
