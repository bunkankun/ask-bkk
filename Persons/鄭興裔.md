# Zhèng Xīngyì 鄭興裔 (1126–1199)

*Zì* Guāngxī 光錫. Original given name *Xīngzōng* 興宗 (later changed). Third-generation male-line great-grandson of Empress *Xiǎnsù* 顯肅 (Empress Wáng, principal wife of Huīzōng) — i.e., a member of the Sòng imperial *wàijiā* (maternal-affine) lineage. Posthumous *shì* Zhōngsù 忠肅 ("Loyal-and-Solemn"). CBDB id 15347, lifedates 1126–1199.

Career advanced through the *guānyì* (privileged-clan-recommendation) system characteristic of imperial-affine officials: from *Chéngzhōngláng* he rose through *Jiāngdōnglù qiánxiá* (commissioner of the Jiāngdōng circuit), *Jūnzhōu fángyùshǐ* (Defense Commissioner of Jūnzhōu), *Bǎojìngjūn jiédùshǐ* (Military Governor of the Bǎojìng army), recalled to *Wǔtàijūn jiédùshǐ* concurrent with charge of an Inner-Sacrificial office. Posthumously honored *Tàiwèi*. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* j. 465 (the *biographies of imperial affines*).

The principal documentary value of his career rests on his memorials, preserved in his collection [[KR4d0212]]: the famous *Qǐng qǐjū Chónghuágōng* memorial — requesting Guāngzōng to visit his retired father Xiàozōng — is one of the central documentary sources for the 1191–1194 filial-piety crisis that precipitated Guāngzōng's effective abdication and Hán Tuōzhòu's rise. Other prominent pieces document the Huáixī famine administration, the *zhébóqián* (cloth-equivalent currency) abuses, and recommendations of Lù Jiǔyuān 陸九淵, Gōng Míngzhī 龔明之, Yán Dù 顔度, and Chén Zào 陳造 for office.
