# Zhèng Xīngyì 鄭興裔 (1126–1199)

*Zì* Guāng-xī 光錫. Original given name *Xīng-zōng* 興宗 (later changed). Third-generation male-line great-grandson of Empress *Xiǎn-sù* 顯肅 (Empress Wáng, principal wife of Huī-zōng) — i.e., a member of the Sòng imperial *wài-jiā* (maternal-affine) lineage. Posthumous *shì* Zhōng-sù 忠肅 ("Loyal-and-Solemn"). CBDB id 15347, lifedates 1126–1199.

Career advanced through the *guān-yì* (privileged-clan-recommendation) system characteristic of imperial-affine officials: from *Chéng-zhōng-láng* he rose through *Jiāng-dōng-lù qián-xiá* (commissioner of the Jiāng-dōng circuit), *Jūn-zhōu fáng-yù-shǐ* (Defense Commissioner of Jūn-zhōu), *Bǎo-jìng-jūn jié-dù-shǐ* (Military Governor of the Bǎo-jìng army), recalled to *Wǔ-tài-jūn jié-dù-shǐ* concurrent with charge of an Inner-Sacrificial office. Posthumously honored *Tài-wèi*. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* j. 465 (the *biographies of imperial affines*).

The principal documentary value of his career rests on his memorials, preserved in his collection [[KR4d0212]]: the famous *Qǐng qǐ-jū Chóng-huá-gōng* memorial — requesting Guāng-zōng to visit his retired father Xiào-zōng — is one of the central documentary sources for the 1191–1194 filial-piety crisis that precipitated Guāng-zōng's effective abdication and Hán Tuō-zhòu's rise. Other prominent pieces document the Huái-xī famine administration, the *zhé-bó-qián* (cloth-equivalent currency) abuses, and recommendations of Lù Jiǔ-yuān 陸九淵, Gōng Míng-zhī 龔明之, Yán Dù 顔度, and Chén Zào 陳造 for office.
