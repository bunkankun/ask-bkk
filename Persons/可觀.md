Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquets **Yíwēng** 宜翁 / **Xuānwēng** 宣翁, *zì* **Jiěkōng** 解空, hermitage-name **Zhúān** 竹菴 (with full name **Zhúān Kěguān** 竹菴可觀; also **Cǎoān** 草菴 "Grass-Hermitage"). DILA Authority A000093.

Major works:
- **Shānjiā yìyuàn** 山家義苑 ([[KR6d0232]], X57n0956, 2 *juan*) — Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* doctrinal compendium, with **Zhìzēng** 智增 ([[智增]]) providing the *zhèng* 證 ("verification") editorial work.
- **Zhúān cǎolù** 竹菴艸錄 ([[KR6d0233]], X57n0957, 1 *juan*) — Kěguān's miscellaneous writings.
