Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquets **Yí-wēng** 宜翁 / **Xuān-wēng** 宣翁, *zì* **Jiě-kōng** 解空, hermitage-name **Zhú-ān** 竹菴 (with full name **Zhú-ān Kě-guān** 竹菴可觀; also **Cǎo-ān** 草菴 "Grass-Hermitage"). DILA Authority A000093.

Major works:
- **Shān-jiā yì-yuàn** 山家義苑 ([[KR6d0232]], X57n0956, 2 *juan*) — Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* doctrinal compendium, with **Zhì-zēng** 智增 ([[智增]]) providing the *zhèng* 證 ("verification") editorial work.
- **Zhú-ān cǎo-lù** 竹菴艸錄 ([[KR6d0233]], X57n0957, 1 *juan*) — Kě-guān's miscellaneous writings.
