# Míngzhé 明哲 (Xiàotáng Míngzhé 笑堂明哲)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, dharma-heir of [[通奇|Línyě Tōngqí]] 林野通奇 (1595/6–1652) in the Mìyún Yuánwù line. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Míngzhé** 明哲 (using 明 as a generation-character alternative to 行 in the Línyě sub-line's transition-level), *hào* **Xiàotáng** 笑堂 ("Laughter-Hall"). One of Línyě's 50 named dharma-heirs per the Mùchén Dàomǐn *tǎmíng* at [[KR6q0408]].

**Training.** Close attendant of Línyě at **Tiāntóngsì** in the 1650–52 period. Per the Chén Zhīshēn 1665 preface to [[KR6q0425]]: "*zhēnxiàn wèilèi jiáfǔ jiāogōng, jīnpíng wǎngdài* 疑關畏壘夾輔交攻巾瓶罔怠" ("at rocky-passes and windy-fortifications redoubled study; attended towel-and-vase without slack"). Received a *Qiūhuái* 秋懷 sealing-poem from Línyě as the earliest transmission-marker.

**Three abbacies.** (1) **Yúháng Bēidù** 餘杭杯渡. (2) **Sānjù** 三聚. (3) **Yúháng Wòlóngshān Guǎnghuà Chánsì** 餘杭臥龍山廣化禪寺 — central abbacy, opened **Shùnzhì 13 丙申冬** = 1656 winter. Held Guǎnghuà through the 1665 preface-period and presumably after.

Work: [[KR6q0425]] 《笑堂和尚語錄》 (1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J28 B206). Compiled by *shìzhě* [[超源]] Chāoyuán and edited by dharma-heir [[超晙]] Chāojùn (**Xiáqǐ**) at Bāoshān Língquánān. Two front-prefaces dated **Kāngxī 4 / 1665**.

Specific birthdate, lay surname, native place, and death-date unrecovered from the one-juan recension. A fuller biography (if it survives) would be in the Wǔdēng quánshū or regional Hang-zhōu monastic gazetteers.

Sources: [[KR6q0425]] juan 1 prefaces by **Chén Zhīshēn** (1665-09) and **Cáo Línyùn** (1665 autumn); [[KR6q0408]] Mùchén Dàomǐn *tǎmíng* (the 50-heir list).
