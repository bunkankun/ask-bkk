# Míng-zhé 明哲 (Xiào-táng Míng-zhé 笑堂明哲)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, dharma-heir of [[通奇|Lín-yě Tōng-qí]] 林野通奇 (1595/6–1652) in the Mìyún Yuán-wù line. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Míng-zhé** 明哲 (using 明 as a generation-character alternative to 行 in the Lín-yě sub-line's transition-level), *hào* **Xiào-táng** 笑堂 ("Laughter-Hall"). One of Lín-yě's 50 named dharma-heirs per the Mù-chén Dào-mǐn *tǎ-míng* at [[KR6q0408]].

**Training.** Close attendant of Lín-yě at **Tiān-tóng-sì** in the 1650–52 period. Per the Chén Zhī-shēn 1665 preface to [[KR6q0425]]: "*zhēn-xiàn wèi-lèi jiá-fǔ jiāo-gōng, jīn-píng wǎng-dài* 疑關畏壘夾輔交攻巾瓶罔怠" ("at rocky-passes and windy-fortifications redoubled study; attended towel-and-vase without slack"). Received a *Qiū-huái* 秋懷 sealing-poem from Lín-yě as the earliest transmission-marker.

**Three abbacies.** (1) **Yú-háng Bēi-dù** 餘杭杯渡. (2) **Sān-jù** 三聚. (3) **Yú-háng Wò-lóng-shān Guǎng-huà Chán-sì** 餘杭臥龍山廣化禪寺 — central abbacy, opened **Shùn-zhì 13 丙申冬** = 1656 winter. Held Guǎng-huà through the 1665 preface-period and presumably after.

Work: [[KR6q0425]] 《笑堂和尚語錄》 (1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B206). Compiled by *shì-zhě* [[超源]] Chāo-yuán and edited by dharma-heir [[超晙]] Chāo-jùn (**Xiá-qǐ**) at Bāo-shān Líng-quán-ān. Two front-prefaces dated **Kāng-xī 4 / 1665**.

Specific birthdate, lay surname, native place, and death-date unrecovered from the one-juan recension. A fuller biography (if it survives) would be in the Wǔ-dēng quán-shū or regional Hang-zhōu monastic gazetteers.

Sources: [[KR6q0425]] juan 1 prefaces by **Chén Zhī-shēn** (1665-09) and **Cáo Lín-yùn** (1665 autumn); [[KR6q0408]] Mù-chén Dào-mǐn *tǎ-míng* (the 50-heir list).
