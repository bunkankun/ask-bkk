# Zhāng Dūnrén 張敦仁

Style names *Zhònggāo* 仲篙 and *Gǔyú* 古餘. Native of Yáng-chéng 陽城 (Zé-zhōu 澤州 prefecture, Shān-xī). Born Qián-lóng 19 (1754); died Dào-guāng 14 (1834). CBDB id 80926 confirms the lifedates.

*Jìnshì* of Qián-lóng 43 (1778); served in successive provincial offices in Jiāng-xī, Shǎn-xī, and Sì-chuān, ultimately as *àn-chá-shǐ* 按察使 (Provincial Surveillance Commissioner) of Jiāng-níng-fǔ. After retirement in the early Jiā-qìng he settled at Yáng-zhōu and devoted himself to mathematical-philological work in the circle of [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún, [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì, and [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán.

The leading Jiā-Dào-period scholar of medieval Chinese algebra. His three principal mathematical works:

(1) **The *Qiú-yī suàn-shù* 求一算術** ([[KR3fc041]]) in 4 *juàn* — the systematic Qián-Jiā exposition of [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo's *Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù* 大衍求一術 (the Chinese Remainder Theorem), the first thorough Qīng exposition of this fundamental Sòng-Yuán algorithm.

(2) **A *xì-cǎo* on the *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng***  ([[KR3fc006]]) — detailed-procedure exposition of [[王孝通]] Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic-equation classic, presented as the *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng xì-cǎo* 緝古算經細草. Parallel to but independent of [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's [[KR3fc005]] *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng kǎo-zhù*.

(3) **The *Kāi-fāng bǔ-jì* 開方補記** — supplementary treatment of equation-solving methods, building on the *qiú-yī* and *kāi-fāng* lineage.

Zhāng was also a major classical philologist, with substantial editorial work on the *Yí-lǐ* 儀禮 and the *Hàn shū* 漢書. His mathematical work shares the methodological commitments of the Jiā-Dào *qiú-yī* / *kāi-fāng* recovery project: working out in full computational detail the procedures that the Sòng-Yuán algebraists had stated only schematically.
