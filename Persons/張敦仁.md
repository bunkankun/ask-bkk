# Zhāng Dūnrén 張敦仁

Style names *Zhònggāo* 仲篙 and *Gǔyú* 古餘. Native of Yángchéng 陽城 (Zézhōu 澤州 prefecture, Shānxī). Born Qiánlóng 19 (1754); died Dàoguāng 14 (1834). CBDB id 80926 confirms the lifedates.

*Jìnshì* of Qiánlóng 43 (1778); served in successive provincial offices in Jiāngxī, Shǎnxī, and Sìchuān, ultimately as *àncháshǐ* 按察使 (Provincial Surveillance Commissioner) of Jiāngníngfǔ. After retirement in the early Jiāqìng he settled at Yángzhōu and devoted himself to mathematical-philological work in the circle of [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún, [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì, and [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán.

The leading Jiā-Dào-period scholar of medieval Chinese algebra. His three principal mathematical works:

(1) **The *Qiúyī suànshù* 求一算術** ([[KR3fc041]]) in 4 *juàn* — the systematic Qián-Jiā exposition of [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo's *Dàyǎn qiúyī shù* 大衍求一術 (the Chinese Remainder Theorem), the first thorough Qīng exposition of this fundamental Sòng-Yuán algorithm.

(2) **A *xìcǎo* on the *Jígǔ suànjīng***  ([[KR3fc006]]) — detailed-procedure exposition of [[王孝通]] Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic-equation classic, presented as the *Jígǔ suànjīng xìcǎo* 緝古算經細草. Parallel to but independent of [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's [[KR3fc005]] *Jígǔ suànjīng kǎozhù*.

(3) **The *Kāifāng bǔjì* 開方補記** — supplementary treatment of equation-solving methods, building on the *qiúyī* and *kāifāng* lineage.

Zhāng was also a major classical philologist, with substantial editorial work on the *Yílǐ* 儀禮 and the *Hàn shū* 漢書. His mathematical work shares the methodological commitments of the Jiā-Dào *qiúyī* / *kāifāng* recovery project: working out in full computational detail the procedures that the Sòng-Yuán algebraists had stated only schematically.
