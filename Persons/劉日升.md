Liú Rìshēng 劉日升, Wànlì-era Míng official and editor. Together with [[陳道亨]] Chén Dàohēng collated and cut to woodblocks the Míng print-edition of [[楊簡]] Yáng Jiǎn's *Yángshì Yì zhuàn* ([[KR1a0037]]) that became the *Sìkù* base-text. The *Sìkù* editors infer from divergences against [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* record of two distinct shorter Yáng-Jiǎn *Yì* writings (the 10-juan *Cíhú Yì jiě* and 1-juan *Yǐ Yì*) that Liú Rìshēng and Chén Dàohēng substantially restructured the work in the late-Wànlì publishing context.
