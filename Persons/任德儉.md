# Rèn Déjiǎn 任德儉

Rèn Déjiǎn 任德儉, native of Jùnshuǐ 浚水 (location uncertain; possibly in northern Hénan or Héběi), Ming dynasty compiler. He is recorded only from the postface to the *Zhōngmì yuánběn* 中秘元本 ([[KR3k0071]]) written by [[呂柟]]: "右中秘元本二十卷，為浚水任德儉所輯" — "The *Zhōngmì yuánběn* in 20 *juan* was compiled by Rèn Déjiǎn of Jùnshuǐ." No further biographical record for this person has been located in CBDB, the *Míng shǐ*, or other standard sources. He compiled the classified literary-phrase reference *Zhōngmì yuánběn* from material found in or attributed to the imperial library (中秘) during the 正德 reign (1505–21).
