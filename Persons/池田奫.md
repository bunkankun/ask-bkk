**Ikeda Yin** 池田奫 (Edo-period Japan, late 18th – early 19th c.), son of [[池田瑞仙]] Ikeda Zuisen of the Bakufu Medical Academy 幕府醫學館 smallpox-instruction line. In Bunka 4 (1807) he produced the *Chóngjiào dòukē biànyào* 重校痘科辨要 — a critical re-collation of his father's *Dòukē biànyào* ([[KR3eg046]]) that restored the authentic text from the family draft and distinguished later student additions from the master's original.
