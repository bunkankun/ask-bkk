Raigen 賴驗 (also written 頼驗), Tendai *shōmyō* scholar of the **Kamakura period**. Precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works; he is conventionally placed in the period **c. 1200–1300** on the basis of doctrinal vocabulary in his *Onritsu shōke-shū* [[KR6t0427]]. The work is distinctive in **integrating Buddhist musical pitch-theory with esoteric Shingon-Tendai doctrine**, reading the pitch-pipe relations as expressions of the *dharma-realm* and the singer's intonation as devotional-cosmological practice. The DILA authority id is `A001261`.
