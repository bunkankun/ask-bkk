**Lǐ Chéngyín** 李承寅 (清庠生 / Qīng *xiùcái*) — postface-writer of [[高淑濂]] Gāo Shūlián's *Tāichǎn fāngàn* ([[KR3ei067]]), titled *Dú Gāoshì Tāichǎn fāngàn zhuìyán* 讀高氏胎產方案贅言. Frames Gāo's work in the Fàn Zhòngyān "if not a worthy minister, then a worthy physician" tradition. Not in CBDB.
