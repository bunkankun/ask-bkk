Mid-Qing philologist and antiquarian, *zì* Shān-fū 山夫, *hào* Bié-shān 別山, of Shānyáng 山陽 (modern Huái'ān, Jiāngsū). Held the rank of *lǐn-gòng-shēng* and served as instructor (*xùn-dǎo*) at Fèngyáng-xiàn. He was a serious epigraphist, especially on Hàn-Wèi seal-script and clerical-script materials, and a contributor to the Qing revival of *jīn-shí-xué* 金石學. His *Biéyǎ* [[KR1j0016]] is the standard Qing dictionary of *tōng-jiè-zì* 通假字 (loan-character pairs), arranged by rhyme, and was followed by his other major work, the epigraphic *Jīn-shí cún* 金石存.
