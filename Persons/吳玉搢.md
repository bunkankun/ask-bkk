Mid-Qing philologist and antiquarian, *zì* Shānfū 山夫, *hào* Biéshān 別山, of Shānyáng 山陽 (modern Huái'ān, Jiāngsū). Held the rank of *lǐngòngshēng* and served as instructor (*xùndǎo*) at Fèngyángxiàn. He was a serious epigraphist, especially on HànWèi seal-script and clerical-script materials, and a contributor to the Qing revival of *jīnshíxué* 金石學. His *Biéyǎ* [[KR1j0016]] is the standard Qing dictionary of *tōngjièzì* 通假字 (loan-character pairs), arranged by rhyme, and was followed by his other major work, the epigraphic *Jīnshí cún* 金石存.
