# Hóngyù 弘裕

Early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Yuánzuò 元祚. Dharma-heir of Xuějiàn Dàofèng 雪磵道奉 (one of [[元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái's named heirs, at Bóshān), thus *fǎsūn* 法孫 of [[元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái (1576–1630) — styled Bóshān Hóngyù 博山弘裕 for his position within the Bóshān Cáodòng succession. Co-editor (*tóngjí* 同集) of the *Wúyì Yuánlái chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0365]]; X72 n1435), with [[弘瀚]] Sùrú Hónghàn as principal compiler. Lifedates unrecorded. Per *Wǔdēng quánshū* *juan* 117, identified as "*Xìnzhōu Bóshān Yuánzuò Hóngyù chánshī* 信州博山元祚弘裕禪師," dharma-heir of Xuějiàn Dàofèng.
