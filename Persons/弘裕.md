# Hóng-yù 弘裕

Early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Yuán-zuò 元祚. Dharma-heir of Xuě-jiàn Dào-fèng 雪磵道奉 (one of [[元來]] Wú-yì Yuán-lái's named heirs, at Bó-shān), thus *fǎ-sūn* 法孫 of [[元來]] Wú-yì Yuán-lái (1576–1630) — styled Bó-shān Hóng-yù 博山弘裕 for his position within the Bó-shān Cáo-dòng succession. Co-editor (*tóng-jí* 同集) of the *Wú-yì Yuán-lái chánshī guǎng-lù* ([[KR6q0365]]; X72 n1435), with [[弘瀚]] Sù-rú Hóng-hàn as principal compiler. Lifedates unrecorded. Per *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* *juan* 117, identified as "*Xìn-zhōu Bó-shān Yuán-zuò Hóng-yù chánshī* 信州博山元祚弘裕禪師," dharma-heir of Xuě-jiàn Dào-fèng.
