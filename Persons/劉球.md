Liú Qiú 劉球 (1392–1443), *zì* **Qiúlè** 求樂 (later changed to **Tíngzhèn** 廷振), *hào* **Liǎngxī** 兩谿, posthumous title (early Jǐngtài) **Zhōngmǐn** 忠愍. Native of **Ānfú** 安福 (Jíān, Jiāngxī). Yǒnglè *xīnchǒu* (1421) *jìnshì*; appointed *Lǐbù zhǔshì* 禮部主事; recommended by **Yáng Shìqí** 楊士奇 ([[楊士奇]]) to attend the *jīngyán* (imperial lectures); reassigned *shìjiǎng* 侍講. After offending the great eunuch **Wáng Zhèn** 王振 by frank memorial in Zhèngtǒng 8 (1443), thrown into the *zhàoyù* and dismembered to death by Wáng Zhèn's confederate **Mǎ Shùn** 馬順. Posthumously gifted *Hànlín xuéshì* and rehabilitated in early Jǐngtài (1450–51) after the destruction of the Wáng Zhèn faction. *Míng shǐ* j. 162. Surviving works are [[KR4e0101]] *Liǎngxī wénjí* (24 *juǎn*), edited 28 years after his death by his sons **Liú Yuè** 劉鉞 and **Liú Yú** 劉釪. CBDB id 34501 (1392–1443).
