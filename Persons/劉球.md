Liú Qiú 劉球 (1392–1443), *zì* **Qiú-lè** 求樂 (later changed to **Tíng-zhèn** 廷振), *hào* **Liǎng-xī** 兩谿, posthumous title (early Jǐng-tài) **Zhōng-mǐn** 忠愍. Native of **Ān-fú** 安福 (Jí-ān, Jiāng-xī). Yǒng-lè *xīn-chǒu* (1421) *jìn-shì*; appointed *Lǐ-bù zhǔ-shì* 禮部主事; recommended by **Yáng Shì-qí** 楊士奇 ([[楊士奇]]) to attend the *jīng-yán* (imperial lectures); reassigned *shì-jiǎng* 侍講. After offending the great eunuch **Wáng Zhèn** 王振 by frank memorial in Zhèng-tǒng 8 (1443), thrown into the *zhào-yù* and dismembered to death by Wáng Zhèn's confederate **Mǎ Shùn** 馬順. Posthumously gifted *Hàn-lín xué-shì* and rehabilitated in early Jǐng-tài (1450–51) after the destruction of the Wáng Zhèn faction. *Míng shǐ* j. 162. Surviving works are [[KR4e0101]] *Liǎng-xī wén-jí* (24 *juǎn*), edited 28 years after his death by his sons **Liú Yuè** 劉鉞 and **Liú Yú** 劉釪. CBDB id 34501 (1392–1443).
