Late-Míng official from Ānfú 安福 in Jízhōu 吉州 (modern Jiāngxī), courtesy name Kěrèn 可任. CBDB id 82699 attests his lifedates as 1593–1667. He took the *jìnshì* in *Chóngzhēn* 1 (1628) and held office through the late Míng. With his fellow-townsman Lǐ Wéiyuè 李維樾 he co-edited the *Zhōngzhēn lù* 忠貞錄 ([[KR2g0013]]) on the *Jiànwén* martyr Zhuō Jìng 卓敬. After 1644 he reportedly remained loyal to the Míng cause and survived in retirement until 1667, well into the Qīng *Kāngxī* reign.
