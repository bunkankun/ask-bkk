# Zhāng Jǐngyuè 張景岳

Late-Míng physician (1563–1640), original name Zhāng Jièbīn 張介賓, *hào* Jǐngyuè 景岳, of Shānyīn 山陰 (modern Shàoxīng 紹興, Zhèjiāng). One of the four canonical late-Míng *wēnbǔ* 溫補 (warming-and-supplementing) physicians. Author of the *Lèijīng* 類經 (1624), the standard Míng-period thematic re-organisation of the *Nèijīng*, and of the *Jǐngyuè quánshū* 景岳全書 (printed 1700), an extensive systematic compendium of internal medicine, gynaecology, paediatrics, and *shānghán* doctrine. His doctrine of *mìngmén* 命門 and emphasis on *zhēnyīn* / *zhēnyáng* 真陰/真陽 supplementation is foundational for late-Míng to mid-Qīng internal medicine. The *Jǐngyuè quánshū* is among the most-cited late-imperial medical compendia.
