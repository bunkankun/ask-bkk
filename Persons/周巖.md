**Zhōu Yán** 周巖 (1832 – early-20th c.), *zì* Bódù 伯度, *hào* Lùqǐ Shānrén 鹿起山人. Native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). Late-Qīng *jīngfāng* 經方 physician. He passed the *jǔrén* in his thirties and held minor county-level posts in Húběi before withdrawing to private practice in his late forties. His pharmacological orientation belonged to the Jiāngnán *Běnjīng-Zhòngjǐng* revival of the mid-19th century: he was a generation younger than Zōu Shù [[鄒澍]] and Yáng Shítài [[楊時泰]] and continued their critique of Sòng-Yuán-Míng theoretical accretions. His major work, *Běncǎo sī biàn lù* 本草思辨錄 ([[KR3ec053]], 1904 Guāngxù imprint), is a *jīngfāng*-grounded substance-by-substance re-examination of the *Shānghán Jīnguì* pharmacopoeia. He also wrote the *Liù qì gǎn zhèng yāo yì* 六氣感證要義 on epidemic pathology. Two CBDB entries exist under the name 周巖 but neither has confident dates; no CBDB id assigned.
