# Tóng Pèi 童佩

*Zì* Zǐmíng 子鳴. Native of *Lóngyóu* 龍游 in *Qúzhōu* 衢州, Zhèjiāng — the same county where Yáng Jiǒng [[楊炯]] had served his last office as *Yíngchuān lìng* 盈川令. Late-Míng *Lóngqìng* 隆慶 / early *Wànlì* 萬曆 private scholar; a private literatus (no examination distinction recorded), described by his contemporary Huángfǔ Fāng 皇甫汸 as "addicted to reading to the point of pathology" (*dàn shūjí, dànjì wéi pǐ* 耽書籍淫嗜成癖).

His principal scholarly contribution is the recompilation of the *Yíngchuān jí* [[KR4c0004]], the surviving corpus of Yáng Jiǒng — undertaken as an act of *Lóngyóu* hometown commemoration since *Yíngchuān* was administratively part of *Lóngyóu* in the late Míng. He worked from the surviving SòngYuán *biéjí* manuscripts of the corpus, *Wén yuàn yīng huá* 文苑英華, *Yuèfǔ shījí* 樂府詩集, and the *lèishū*. His 10-*juǎn* recension is the basis of the Sìkù WYG print of the collection. CBDB does not record him.
