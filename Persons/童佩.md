# Tóng Pèi 童佩

*Zì* Zǐ-míng 子鳴. Native of *Lóng-yóu* 龍游 in *Qú-zhōu* 衢州, Zhèjiāng — the same county where Yáng Jiǒng [[楊炯]] had served his last office as *Yíng-chuān lìng* 盈川令. Late-Míng *Lóng-qìng* 隆慶 / early *Wàn-lì* 萬曆 private scholar; a private literatus (no examination distinction recorded), described by his contemporary Huáng-fǔ Fāng 皇甫汸 as "addicted to reading to the point of pathology" (*dàn shū-jí, dànjì wéi pǐ* 耽書籍淫嗜成癖).

His principal scholarly contribution is the recompilation of the *Yíng-chuān jí* [[KR4c0004]], the surviving corpus of Yáng Jiǒng — undertaken as an act of *Lóng-yóu* hometown commemoration since *Yíng-chuān* was administratively part of *Lóng-yóu* in the late Míng. He worked from the surviving Sòng-Yuán *bié-jí* manuscripts of the corpus, *Wén yuàn yīng huá* 文苑英華, *Yuè-fǔ shī-jí* 樂府詩集, and the *lèi-shū*. His 10-*juǎn* recension is the basis of the Sìkù WYG print of the collection. CBDB does not record him.
