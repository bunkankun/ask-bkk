Southern-Táng court philologist; held the offices *cháo-sǎn dàfū* 朝散大夫 and *Mì-shū-shěng jiào-shū láng* 祕書省校書郎. He provided the *fǎn-qiè* phonetic gloss layer for Xú Kǎi's [[徐鍇]] *Shuōwén xì-zhuàn* [[KR1j0019]], an independent Southern-Táng phonological dataset distinct from Sūn Miǎn's *Táng-yùn* (used by Xú Xuàn [[徐鉉]] in the *Dà-Xú-běn*). Zhū's readings consistently use the older *fǎn* 反 notation (rather than the post-Kāichéng *qiè* 切) and reflect Jiāngnán phonological practice of the mid-tenth century. CBDB carries one entry (#182208) with death-year 953, but this is uncertain; no further biographical detail can be recovered from the standard sources.
