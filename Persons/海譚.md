# Hǎi-tán 海譚

Northern-Sòng Chán junior-disciple (*xiǎo shī* 小師) of Bái-yún Shǒu-duān 白雲守端 (1025–1072). Recorder (*lù* 錄) of his master's *guǎng lù* [[KR6q0286]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
