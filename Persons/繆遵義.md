Miù Zūn-yì 繆遵義, *hào* Sōng-xīn 松心 (also *Tiān-shū* 天樞 in some sources), 1710–1793 (CBDB 91312, dynasty 20 = Qīng), native of Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Qián-lóng yǐ-chǒu* (1745) *jìn-shì*; held minor official posts before turning to medicine in middle age. A contemporary of Yè Tiān-shì in Sūzhōu and a meticulous practitioner of the Sūzhōu Yè-school light-and-precise prescribing style, with a clinical signature involving distinctive *Bái-shā fāng* (white-sand formulas), *Dàn-cài*, *Bā-xìng*, *Kǎn-qì*, and *Hǎi-gé-ké* preparations. Principal works: [[KR3ep073]] *Miù Sōng-xīn yī-àn* and [[KR3ep075]] *Sōng-xīn yī-àn bǐ-jì*. Several other entries under "繆遵義" in CBDB (353463) lack confirming data.
