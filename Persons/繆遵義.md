Miù Zūnyì 繆遵義, *hào* Sōngxīn 松心 (also *Tiānshū* 天樞 in some sources), 1710–1793 (CBDB 91312, dynasty 20 = Qīng), native of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Qiánlóng yǐchǒu* (1745) *jìnshì*; held minor official posts before turning to medicine in middle age. A contemporary of Yè Tiānshì in Sūzhōu and a meticulous practitioner of the Sūzhōu Yè-school light-and-precise prescribing style, with a clinical signature involving distinctive *Báishā fāng* (white-sand formulas), *Dàncài*, *Bāxìng*, *Kǎnqì*, and *Hǎigéké* preparations. Principal works: [[KR3ep073]] *Miù Sōngxīn yīàn* and [[KR3ep075]] *Sōngxīn yīàn bǐjì*. Several other entries under "繆遵義" in CBDB (353463) lack confirming data.
