# Kèwén 克文

Zhēnjìng Kèwén 真淨克文 (1025–1102), Northern-Sòng Línjì Huánglóng-line Chán master, dharma-heir of Huánglóng Huìnán; teacher of Zhàntáng Wénzhǔn and a major lineage node leading into Yuánwù and Dàhuì.
