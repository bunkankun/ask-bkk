Yáng Chángrú 楊長孺 (*zì* Bózǐ 伯子, *hào* Dōngshān 東山), eldest son of Yáng Wànlǐ [[楊萬里]]. Held office as *Shuàidōngguǎng* 帥東廣 (Pacification Commissioner of *Guǎngdōng*) — visible in the *Chéngzhāi jí* preface by Liú Wěishū. Compiled and edited his father's collected works [[KR4d0266]] *Chéngzhāi jí*, which was printed at *Tōngdé-xiāng* in *Duānpíng* 1–2 (1234–1235). CBDB id 10567 (lifedates not recorded). Author and editor.
