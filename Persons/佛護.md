Fóhù 佛護 (\*Buddha-rakṣa, "Protected-by-the-Buddha"; lifedates unknown) was a translator associated with [[道安|Dàoān]]'s late Yèsì 鄴寺 circle (Former Qín 符秦, c. 382). He is named in Dàoān's preface to [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (T1505) as one of the two oral translators (alongside [[竺佛念|Buddha-niàn 佛念]]) of [[婆素跋陀|Vasubhadra]]'s *Tridharmaka* abstract, with [[鳩摩羅佛提|Kumārabuddhi]] holding the Sanskrit. No other works are securely attributed to him.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (translation role).
