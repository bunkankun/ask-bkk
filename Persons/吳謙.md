**Wú Qiān** 吳謙 (*zì* Liùjí 六吉, *fl.* Qiánlóng early period, 清), Qīng-period imperial physician with the title Tàiyīyuàn yuànpàn 太醫院院判 (Director of the Imperial Medical Academy). Chief editor under imperial commission of the foundational Qīng-period imperial medical compendium ***Yùzuǎn Yīzōng jīnjiàn*** 御纂醫宗金鑑 ([[KR3e0090]], 90 juan, completed Qiánlóng 14 = 1749). Co-editor with [[劉裕鐸]] Liú Yùduó. The work was the official Qīng-period medical-curriculum text and the standard reference of Qīng imperial-medical practice.
