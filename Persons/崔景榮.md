Late-Míng official of Cháng-yuán 長垣 (Dà-míng 大名, Běi-zhí-lì); *jìn-shì* of *Wàn-lì* 11 / 1583. Served as *Bīng-bù shàng-shū* 兵部尚書 in the late Wàn-lì / Tiān-qǐ years and (briefly) *Lì-bù shàng-shū* 吏部尚書 under the Chóng-zhēn emperor, retiring after factional conflict with the Eunuch faction. He contributed one of the verse-eulogies in the paratextual matter of [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíng-xián's [[KR3er085]] *Jì-shì quán-shū* (1616), praising Gōng's clinical merit and his "complete eight works" (*zhù-shū bā zhǒng yú* 著書八種餘).
