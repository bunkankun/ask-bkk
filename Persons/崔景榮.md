Late-Míng official of Chángyuán 長垣 (Dàmíng 大名, Běizhílì); *jìnshì* of *Wànlì* 11 / 1583. Served as *Bīngbù shàngshū* 兵部尚書 in the late Wànlì / Tiānqǐ years and (briefly) *Lìbù shàngshū* 吏部尚書 under the Chóngzhēn emperor, retiring after factional conflict with the Eunuch faction. He contributed one of the verse-eulogies in the paratextual matter of [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián's [[KR3er085]] *Jìshì quánshū* (1616), praising Gōng's clinical merit and his "complete eight works" (*zhùshū bā zhǒng yú* 著書八種餘).
