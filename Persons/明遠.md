# Míng-yuǎn 明遠

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí (1296–1370); co-editor of his master's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
