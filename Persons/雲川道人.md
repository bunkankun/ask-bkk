**Yúnchuān Dàoren** 雲川道人 (also written 云川道人, "Cloud-River Daoist", *fl.* mid-Qiánlóng era), Qīng Daoist-affiliated literatus-physician active at his retreat the *Píngcuì shānfáng* 萍翠山房 ("Duckweed-Green Mountain-Cottage"). Author of the *Jiàngnáng cuōyào* 絳囊撮要 ([[KR3ed116]]), a 1-*juǎn* clinical formulary with a self-preface dated *Qiánlóng* 9 *jiǎzǐ* mid-summer 13th (= summer 1744). The preface's metaphysical apparatus — *xiāntiān* / *hòutiān*, *língmíng* / *xuèqì*, *qióngzhī yáocǎo* — is consistent with the inner-alchemy *nèidān* tradition and identifies the compiler as a Daoist-doctrinal physician. No securely-identified secular personal name; the *hào* is the only signature.
