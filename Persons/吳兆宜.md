**Wú Zhàoyí** 吳兆宜 (fl. mid-Kāng-xī era, ca. 1670–1700), *zì* Xiǎn-lìng 顯令, native of Wú-jiāng 吳江 (modern Sūzhōu), was a *zhū-shēng* (sub-licentiate) of the Kāng-xī era. He is known principally as the early-Qīng commentator on parallel-prose corpora: he produced *jiān-zhù* (interpolated annotations) on (a) [[KR4b0017]] *Yǔ Kāifǔ jí jiān-zhù* 庾開府集箋註 (Yǔ Xìn 庾信 collected works), assisted by Xú Shù-gǔ 徐樹穀 of Kūn-shān, building on the unfinished *jiān-zhù* by Hú Wèi 胡渭; (b) [[KR4b0019]] *Xú Xiào-mù jí jiān-zhù* 徐孝穆集箋註 (Xú Líng 徐陵 collected works), continued after Wú's death by his fellow countryman Xú Wén-bǐng 徐文炳; (c) the *Yùtái xīnyǒng* 玉臺新詠; (d) the *Cái-diào jí* 才調集; (e) the *Hán Wò shī jí* 韓偓詩集. Of these, only the Xú and Yǔ collections were printed and circulated; the others survived only as manuscripts at Qiánlóng-era cataloging time. Wú's commentaries on Yǔ Xìn were partly displaced by Ní Fán's 倪璠 *Yǔ Zǐshān jí zhù* (1689) but the *Sìkù* compilers preserve both for their pioneering value.
