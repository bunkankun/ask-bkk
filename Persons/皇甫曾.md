# Huángfǔ Zēng 皇甫曾

*Zì* Xiàocháng 孝常. Younger brother of Huángfǔ Rǎn [[皇甫冉]]. Native of *Āndìng* 安定 by family origin, but long-resident at *Wúxī* 無錫 in *Jiāngnán*. *Jìnshì* of *Tiānbǎo* 12 (753); successively *jiānchá yùshǐ* 監察御史 and *Yángzhōu sīmǎ* 陽州司馬. Lifedates not securely fixed; floruit *Tiānbǎo* through *Dàlì* (*ca.* 750–780). CBDB has no entry.

His extant verse — preserved as appendix to his elder brother's collection at [[KR4c0016]] — is in the same contemplative *shānshuǐ* idiom as Rǎn's. The two brothers are conventionally counted among the *Dàlì shí cáizǐ* 大曆十才子 ("Ten Talented Men of the Dàlì period"), but as a pair they constitute one of the few cases in the Táng of two siblings each producing a substantial *biéjí*.
