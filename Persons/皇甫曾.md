# Huángfǔ Zēng 皇甫曾

*Zì* Xiào-cháng 孝常. Younger brother of Huáng-fǔ Rǎn [[皇甫冉]]. Native of *Ān-dìng* 安定 by family origin, but long-resident at *Wú-xī* 無錫 in *Jiāng-nán*. *Jìn-shì* of *Tiān-bǎo* 12 (753); successively *jiān-chá yù-shǐ* 監察御史 and *Yáng-zhōu sī-mǎ* 陽州司馬. Lifedates not securely fixed; floruit *Tiān-bǎo* through *Dà-lì* (*ca.* 750–780). CBDB has no entry.

His extant verse — preserved as appendix to his elder brother's collection at [[KR4c0016]] — is in the same contemplative *shān-shuǐ* idiom as Rǎn's. The two brothers are conventionally counted among the *Dà-lì shí cái-zǐ* 大曆十才子 ("Ten Talented Men of the Dà-lì period"), but as a pair they constitute one of the few cases in the Táng of two siblings each producing a substantial *bié-jí*.
