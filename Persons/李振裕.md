Early-Qīng official and bibliographer, *zì* Wéiráo 維饒, *hào* Xǐngzhāi 醒齋, of Jí'ān 吉安 (Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of 1670; rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書 (Minister of Rites). His textual judgment, preserved in [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* 經義考, is the decisive resolution of the *Sòngshǐ* doubling of [[胡瑗]] Hú Yuán's *Yìjiě* and *Zhōuyì kǒuyì* as a single classroom-record work redacted by [[倪天隱]] Ní Tiānyǐn.
