Dōu Mù 都穆 (1458–1525), zì Xuánjìng 玄敬, hào Nánháo 南濠, of Wúxiàn (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū), was a Hóngzhì-era *jìnshì* (1499) and senior official, and a leading mid-Míng *jīnshí* connoisseur. He served in *gōngbù* and other capital posts and reached *yánsuǒ shùnfēng dàoyuán* 鹽所巡風道員 by his retirement. His major works are: *Jīnxiè línláng* 金薤琳琅 [[KR2n0030]] (a 20-juan *jīnshí* compendium of 63 stelae from Zhōu, Qín, Hàn, Suí, and Tang); *Rénwǔ gōngchén juéshǎng lù* 壬午功臣爵賞錄 (Yǒnglè-era 1402 reward register); *Tīngyǔ jì* 聽雨記; and the *Nánháo wénlüè* 南濠文略 (his collected literary works in 6 juan, the last 2 juan of which reproduce the *Jīnxiè línláng* colophons in fuller form). CBDB 34917 confirms 1458–1525; *Míngshǐ* j. 286 has his biography.
