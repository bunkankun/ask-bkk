**Zhōu Xuánzhēn** 周玄貞 was a Míng Daoist commentator, author of [[KR5h0009]] *Huáng jīng jí zhù* 皇經集註 — collected commentary on the *Gāo shàng Yù huáng běn xíng jí jīng* 高上玉皇本行集經 (Jade Emperor scripture).
