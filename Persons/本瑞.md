# Běnruì 本瑞 (Tiānqí Běnruì 天奇本瑞)

Mid-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Tiānqí 天奇 (var. 天琦), Qióngjué lǎorén 焭絕老人 / 煢絕老人 ("Solitary-Cut-Off Old-Man"). **Twenty-sixth-generation Línjì-orthodox patriarch** at Jīnlíng 金陵 (Nánjīng). Birth-year unrecorded; died Ming Wǔzōng Zhèngdé 戊辰 = Zhèngdé 3 = 1508 (first lunar month), per *Zōng tǒng biān nián* 宗統編年 juan 29.

Surviving Kanripo-canonical contribution: the *Qióngjué lǎorén Tiānqí zhí zhù Xuědòu Xiǎn héshàng sòng gǔ* 焭絕老人天奇直註雪竇顯和尚頌古 [[KR6q0250]] — his direct-commentary on the 100 classical *sòng gǔ* verses of [[重顯|Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn]].

Tiānqí's lineage-position as 26th-generation Línjì-orthodox patriarch places him as one of the late-Ming key intermediate masters whose lineage flowed forward into the late-Ming Yuánwù / Huányǒu Chán revival.
