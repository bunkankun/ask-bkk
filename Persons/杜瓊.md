Dù Qióng 杜瓊 (1396–1474), *zì* **Yòng-jiā** 用嘉, *hào* **Lù-guàn-dào-rén** 鹿冠道人 (also Dōng-yuán gēng-zhě 東原耕者), native of **Wú-xiàn** 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu). Mid-Míng literary scholar, painter, and biographer of the second-generation Sū-zhōu literary establishment — teacher of [[沈周]] **Shěn Zhōu** 沈周 (1427–1509), the foundational figure of the **Wú school** 吳派 of painting. Repeatedly refused office. Composer of the appended *Wáng Bàn-xuān zhuàn* 王半軒傳 attached to [[KR4e0047]] *Bàn-xuān jí*, the principal external biographical witness for [[王行]] Wáng Xíng; this biography is notable for its cautious decorum in handling the Lán Yù case implications.
