Dù Qióng 杜瓊 (1396–1474), *zì* **Yòngjiā** 用嘉, *hào* **Lùguàndàorén** 鹿冠道人 (also Dōngyuán gēngzhě 東原耕者), native of **Wúxiàn** 吳縣 (Sūzhōu). Mid-Míng literary scholar, painter, and biographer of the second-generation Sūzhōu literary establishment — teacher of [[沈周]] **Shěn Zhōu** 沈周 (1427–1509), the foundational figure of the **Wú school** 吳派 of painting. Repeatedly refused office. Composer of the appended *Wáng Bànxuān zhuàn* 王半軒傳 attached to [[KR4e0047]] *Bànxuān jí*, the principal external biographical witness for [[王行]] Wáng Xíng; this biography is notable for its cautious decorum in handling the Lán Yù case implications.
