# Hóu Guǒ 侯果

Also written 侯行果 Hóu Xíngguǒ in some sources. Mid-Táng *Yì* scholar, active in the Kāiyuán 開元 era. Held office as Director of the Imperial Academy (*Guózǐ jìjiǔ* 國子祭酒). His *Zhōuyì zhù* 周易註, in the *xiàngshù* 象數 line, is one of the most heavily cited Táng-period *Yì* commentaries preserved in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]); the original is otherwise lost.
