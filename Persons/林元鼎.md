Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Daoist disciple of [[蕭應叟|Xiāo Yīngsǒu]] 蕭應叟 (*hào* Guānfù xiānshēng 觀復先生, *fl.* 1226), *zì* Zhèngfū 正夫, *hào* Méiyán 梅巖 ("Plum-Cliff"). Known only from his authorship of [[KR5a0091|Fù nèiyì dānzhǐ gāngmù jǔyào 附內義丹旨綱目舉要]] (DZ 91), an appendix to his master Xiāo's [[KR5a0090|*Nèiyì* (DZ 90)]]. The appendix reconstructs the master's complex allegorical-alchemical interpretations of the *Dùrén jīng* into a single synoptic scheme — evidence that Xiāo's own text was already considered difficult within his immediate disciple-circle and required a pedagogical key. No CBDB record exists, and no other Kanripo-corpus work is attributed to him.
