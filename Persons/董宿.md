**Dǒng Sù** 董宿 (*fl.* mid-15th c.). Of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (modern Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Ming court physician. Held the office of *Tàiyī yuàn pàn* 太醫院判 (Deputy Director of the Imperial Medical Academy) under the Zhèngtǒng / Jǐngtài / Tiānshùn / Chénghuà reigns. Original compiler of the *Qíxiào liángfāng* 奇效良方 ([[KR3ed038]]), 69 *juǎn*, which was completed and edited after his death by his successor [[方賢]] Fāng Xián. Dǒng Sù has no separate biographical record in *Míng shǐ*; the main attestation is his named co-authorship on the *Qíxiào liángfāng*. Not in CBDB.
