**Liú Xīzhòng** 劉羲仲 (fl. late 11th c.), *zì* Zhuàngyú 壯輿. Native of Yúnzhōu 筠州 (modern Gāo'ān, Jiāngxī). Eldest son of [[劉恕]] (Liú Shù, 1032–1078), one of the three principal compilers of [[KR2j0001]] *Zīzhì tōngjiàn* 資治通鑑 under Sīmǎ Guāng; younger brother of the calendrical scholar Liú Xīsǒu 劉羲叟. (The form 義仲, found in transmitted texts of the *Sòngshǐ* and Zhōu Mì's 癸辛雜識, was identified by the *Sìkù* editors as a transmissional error and emended to 羲仲, the form preserved in the work itself.)

Liú Xīzhòng never sat for the *jìnshì*. His one recorded substantive appointment is the honorific *Jiāo-shè zhāiláng* 郊社齋郎 (Resident at the Suburban Sacrifices Hall) awarded to him after the *Zīzhì tōngjiàn*'s presentation in *Yuánfēng* 7 (1084), in posthumous recognition of his father's compilation labour — a benefice reserved for the descendants of meritorious officials. He was active in the *Yuányòu* and early *Shàoshèng* periods.

His one significant work is the [[KR2o0006]] *Tōngjiàn wènyí* 通鑑問疑 in 1 *juàn* — a compilation of his late father's recorded historiographical disputes with Sīmǎ Guāng over *Tōngjiàn* editorial decisions, supplemented by Liú Xīzhòng's own eight queries to [[范祖禹]] (Fàn Zǔyǔ) and Fàn's responses. The work is one of the principal first-hand testimonies to the *Tōngjiàn*'s internal editorial disputes — most importantly Liú Shù's lost argument for treating Shǔ-Hàn as legitimate succession, a position Zhū Xī revived in [[KR2o0020]] *Tōngjiàn gāngmù*.
