**Gōngsūn Lóng** 公孫龍 (*c.* 320 – *c.* 250 BCE) was one of the leading figures of the **School of Names** (*Míng jiā* 名家) in the Warring States, famous for logical-semantic paradoxes (*bái mǎ fēi mǎ* 白馬非馬 "a white horse is not a horse" being the most celebrated). Eponymous author of the ***Gōngsūn Lóng zǐ*** 公孫龍子 ([[KR5f0006]]).
