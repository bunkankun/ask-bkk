Mǎ Yǒngyì 馬永易 (fl. early 12th c.), *zì* Míngsǒu 明叟, native of Yángzhōu 揚州, was a Northern-Sòng minor official who held the post of *Wèi* (sheriff) of Shídài 石埭 in Chízhōu 池州 under Huīzōng 徽宗 (*r.* 1100–1125). He has no biography in the *Sòng shǐ*; the *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* and *Sòng shǐ · Yìwén zhì* preserve attributions to him of a *Táng zhílín* 唐職林, a *Yuánhé péngdǎng lù* 元和朋黨錄, and a *Shòuchūn zázhì* 夀春雜志 — all lost. His only work preserved (in fragmentary form, reconstructed by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*) is the *Shíbīn lù* 實賓錄 ([[KR3k0015]]), the principal Northern-Sòng compendium of *yìhào* (alternative sobriquets).
