Mǎ Yǒng-yì 馬永易 (fl. early 12th c.), *zì* Míng-sǒu 明叟, native of Yáng-zhōu 揚州, was a Northern-Sòng minor official who held the post of *Wèi* (sheriff) of Shí-dài 石埭 in Chí-zhōu 池州 under Huī-zōng 徽宗 (*r.* 1100–1125). He has no biography in the *Sòng shǐ*; the *Wén-xiàn tōng-kǎo* and *Sòng shǐ · Yìwén zhì* preserve attributions to him of a *Táng zhí-lín* 唐職林, a *Yuán-hé péng-dǎng lù* 元和朋黨錄, and a *Shòu-chūn zá-zhì* 夀春雜志 — all lost. His only work preserved (in fragmentary form, reconstructed by the *Sìkù* editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*) is the *Shí-bīn lù* 實賓錄 ([[KR3k0015]]), the principal Northern-Sòng compendium of *yì-hào* (alternative sobriquets).
