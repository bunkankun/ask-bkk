Wáng Wénsháo 王文韶 (*zì* Kuíshí 夔石; 1830–1908) was a senior Qīng statesman, Yúnnán-Guìzhōu governor-general (1889–1894), Zhílì governor-general, and eventually Grand Secretary. The grand-nephew of [[何其偉]] Hé Qíwěi, he sponsored the 1892 Yún-Guì-jiéshǔ printing of the *Yīxué miàodì* ([[KR3eh017]]) and contributed its principal preface.
