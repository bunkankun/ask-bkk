Bào Zhì 鮑至 (*fl.* Southern Qi–Liang dynasty, late 5th–early 6th century CE) was a geographic writer, author of a *Nán Yōngzhōu jì* 南雍州記 ([[KR2k0175]]), a record of the Nan Yongzhou region (modern northern Guangdong and western Guangxi). No further biographical details are attested; his work is known only from fragments preserved in medieval encyclopedias. He is distinct from the Eastern Han poet Bào Zhào 鮑照 (c. 414–466 CE) and from other homonymous figures.
