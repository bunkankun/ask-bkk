# Yíshān 怡山

Táng-era Chán master, traditionally identified as the author of the celebrated devotional vow-text *Yíshān lǐ fó fā yuàn wén* 怡山禮佛發願文 ("Vow-Text for Buddha-Worship"), which entered Chinese Buddhist ritual-liturgical usage and was widely employed across Chán, Vinaya, and Pure-Land contexts. Lifedates unrecorded.

Sources: the parent vow-text is the basis of the Qīng commentary [[KR6q0210]] *Yíshān lǐ fó fā yuàn wén lüè shì* by [[書玉|Yíjié Shūyù]].
