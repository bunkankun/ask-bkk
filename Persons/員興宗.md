Yún Xīngzōng 員興宗 (d. 1170, *zì* Xiǎndào 顯道, *hào* Jiǔhuázǐ 九華子), of *Sānyú* 三嵎 (modern *Rénshòu* 仁壽 in Sìchuān). Held office to *Zhùzuò láng* 著作郎 and *Shílù-yuàn jiǎntǎo-guān* 實錄院檢討官. Famous for upright remonstrance during early *Lóngxīng* / *Qiándào*: pointed out three matters as the court was about to act on them, and was driven from court without regret. Member of the corresponding network of Zhāng Shì [[張栻]] and Lù Jiǔyuān [[陸九淵]]. Antiquarian correspondent of Hóng Guā 洪适 on Hàn-stele epigraphy. Author of [[KR4d0259]] *Jiǔhuá jí* and the separately-transmitted *Cǎishí zhànshèng lù* 采石戰勝錄 (record of the 1161 *Cǎishí* battle). CBDB id 27397.
