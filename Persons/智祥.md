Zhì-xiáng 智祥, *zì* **Tīng-yún** 聽雲, *hào* **Pín-jí** 頻吉 (also **Pín-gǔ-fó** 頻古佛); often referred to as **Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng** 頻吉智祥. **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Yuán-jié Jìng-yíng** [[淨瑩]] (1612–1672). Born **明崇禎丙子腊月初十** = **1637-01-05** Gregorian; died **1709**, age c. 73. Native of **Huáng-zhōu Má-chéng** 黃州麻城 (Hú-běi); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** As a child whenever he saw a monk would become joyful. At 7 traveled with his father to **Gāo-shān Pú-tí-sì** 高山菩提寺 (in Xiāng-xiāng), met **Jí-ān** 吉庵律師 — refused to leave. **Jí-ān** ordered his disciple **Yī-shuǐ** 一水 to tonsure the boy. **順治乙未** = **1655** at age 19 went to **Shī-fēng** 獅峰 under **Fú-mù** 浮木 to take full ordination, then studied scriptures and treatises. Later went to **Jīn-líng** 金陵 to *cān* under **Làng-zhàng-rén** 浪杖人 = 覺浪道盛. Then to **Hú-zhōu Biàn-shān** 弁山 to *cān* under **Yún-yán Yuán-jié Jìng-yíng**. At 27 had an opening on hearing the *xiāng-bǎn* (incense board) sound. Served Jìng-yíng's *jīn-píng* (towel-and-jar) over a year. Subsequently *cān*-fǎng under **Yú-ān Yú** 愚庵盂, **Yù-lín Xiù** 玉林琇, **Fú-yán Róng** 福嚴容, **Qīng-lóng Sī** 青龍斯. **康熙壬寅** = **1662** assisted Jìng-yíng with the establishment of **Yún-yán** 雲岩.

**Abbacies.** **Hú-zhōu Lóng-huá chán-sì** 弁山龍華禪寺; **Héng-yáng Zǐ-yún-fēng Fó-guó-sì** 衡陽紫雲峰佛國寺; **Yún-yán Wú-zhù chán-sì** 雲巖無住禪寺.

**Death.** After his death, his body did not decay; hair and beard continued to grow.

**Disciples (53 *fǎ-sì*).** Major figures include **Huì-lóng Jí-ān Xūn** 會龍藉菴熏 and **Guǎng-zhōu Yù-rì Néng** 廣州浴日能, plus **Dé-néng** 德能 [[德能]] (lead compiler of his *yǔ-lù*).

**Works.**
- *Chán-lín bǎo-xùn bǐ shuō* 禪林寶訓筆說 [[KR6q0157]] (X64 n1266) — three-juan running-commentary on the [[KR6q0099]] *Chán-lín bǎo-xùn*.
- Lead compiler (*biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0567]] 《元潔瑩禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B453.
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0568]] 《頻吉祥禪師語錄》 15 juan, J39 B454 (+ 卍續藏).
- 《法華經授手》 (Lotus Sūtra commentary).
