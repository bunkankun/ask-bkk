Zhìxiáng 智祥, *zì* **Tīngyún** 聽雲, *hào* **Pínjí** 頻吉 (also **Píngǔfó** 頻古佛); often referred to as **Pínjí Zhìxiáng** 頻吉智祥. **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Yuánjié Jìngyíng** [[淨瑩]] (1612–1672). Born **明崇禎丙子腊月初十** = **1637-01-05** Gregorian; died **1709**, age c. 73. Native of **Huángzhōu Máchéng** 黃州麻城 (Húběi); lay surname **Lǐ** 李.

**Formation.** As a child whenever he saw a monk would become joyful. At 7 traveled with his father to **Gāoshān Pútísì** 高山菩提寺 (in Xiāngxiāng), met **Jíān** 吉庵律師 — refused to leave. **Jíān** ordered his disciple **Yīshuǐ** 一水 to tonsure the boy. **順治乙未** = **1655** at age 19 went to **Shīfēng** 獅峰 under **Fúmù** 浮木 to take full ordination, then studied scriptures and treatises. Later went to **Jīnlíng** 金陵 to *cān* under **Làngzhàngrén** 浪杖人 = 覺浪道盛. Then to **Húzhōu Biànshān** 弁山 to *cān* under **Yúnyán Yuánjié Jìngyíng**. At 27 had an opening on hearing the *xiāngbǎn* (incense board) sound. Served Jìngyíng's *jīnpíng* (towel-and-jar) over a year. Subsequently *cān*-fǎng under **Yúān Yú** 愚庵盂, **Yùlín Xiù** 玉林琇, **Fúyán Róng** 福嚴容, **Qīnglóng Sī** 青龍斯. **康熙壬寅** = **1662** assisted Jìngyíng with the establishment of **Yúnyán** 雲岩.

**Abbacies.** **Húzhōu Lónghuá chánsì** 弁山龍華禪寺; **Héngyáng Zǐyúnfēng Fóguósì** 衡陽紫雲峰佛國寺; **Yúnyán Wúzhù chánsì** 雲巖無住禪寺.

**Death.** After his death, his body did not decay; hair and beard continued to grow.

**Disciples (53 *fǎsì*).** Major figures include **Huìlóng Jíān Xūn** 會龍藉菴熏 and **Guǎngzhōu Yùrì Néng** 廣州浴日能, plus **Dénéng** 德能 [[德能]] (lead compiler of his *yǔlù*).

**Works.**
- *Chánlín bǎoxùn bǐ shuō* 禪林寶訓筆說 [[KR6q0157]] (X64 n1266) — three-juan running-commentary on the [[KR6q0099]] *Chánlín bǎoxùn*.
- Lead compiler (*biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0567]] 《元潔瑩禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B453.
- Own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0568]] 《頻吉祥禪師語錄》 15 juan, J39 B454 (+ 卍續藏).
- 《法華經授手》 (Lotus Sūtra commentary).
