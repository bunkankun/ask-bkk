Late Northern-Sòng grand councilor and ritual director under Huīzōng; native of Kāifēng. *Zì* Dáfū 達夫; posthumous name Wénzhèng 文正. Took the *jìnshì* in Yuán-fēng 8 (1085); rose through Huīzōng's court to *Zhī Shūmì-yuàn* and ultimately Senior Grand Secretary, serving in alternation with the Cài Jīng 蔡京 faction. Compiler-in-chief (under imperial commission) of the *Zhènghé wǔlǐ xīnyí* 政和五禮新儀 ([[KR2m0023]]) of 1111–1113, the only surviving complete Northern-Sòng imperial ritual code. He played a politically sensitive role in the diplomatic crisis with the Jīn in 1122–23, dying just before the *Jìngkāng* fall. CBDB confirms 1059–1123.
