Nakamura Gohyōe-no-jō 中村五兵衞尉 was the Keian-period (1651) Kyoto block-printer who produced the woodblock edition of [[源信]] Genshin's *Jùshě Lùn Sòngshū Zhèngwén* ([[KR6l0043]], T2253). His printing — based on the manuscript transcription chain through [[清澊]] Seijun (1468) and [[尊契]] Sonkei (1507) — is the immediate textual ancestor of the Taishō edition.
