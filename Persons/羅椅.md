Luó Yǐ 羅椅 (1213–after 1265, *zì* Zǐyuǎn 子遠, *hào* Jiàngǔ 澗谷), of *Lúlíng* 廬陵 (modern Jí'ān, Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Bǎoyòu* 4 (1256). Held office to *Bǐngyì-láng* as *Jiānglíng* education-officer; transferred to *Tánzhōu*; further *Zhī Gànzhōu Xìnfēng-xiàn*; promoted *Tíxiá quèhuò*. Discussed and dismissed at the start of *Déyòu* (1275). Zhōu Mì's 周密 *Qídōng yěyǔ* records that he attached himself to the *Dàoxué* faction during its flourishing and abandoned it after its decline — Zhōu's account is openly disapproving. Selected the *qiánjí* of [[KR4d0269]] *Jiàngǔ jīngxuǎn Lù Fàngwēng shījí* (also forming the *qiánjí* of [[KR4d0270]] *Fàngwēng shīxuǎn*), the principal Sòng-Yuán anthology of Lù Yóu's [[陸游]] poetry; first cut posthumously by his grandnephew Luó Jǐng 羅憼 at *Dàdé* 5 (1301). May Tang's *Qiánkūn qīngqì jí* records his *Yòuyú zhéchǐ gē* under early-Yuán authors, suggesting he was alive into the early Yuán. CBDB id 41857.
