Xiè Féngyuán 謝逢源, *hào* Quánshí shānrén 拳石山人 ("the Mountain Man of the Fist-shaped Rock"). Mid-19th-century Sū-zhōu-region literatus. Author of the *tící* (inscription-poem) prefacing [[KR3eq086]] *Shìyǐnlú yīxué zázhù*, dated *Guǐchǒu súfórì* 癸丑俗佛日 (1853, popular Buddha's-birthday). The poem invokes the *Wáng Shūhé family line* and frames Wáng Désēn's work in the *Yánnián* (Péng Yánnián 彭延年 longevity-cultivation) and *Fúyòu* (Mòjiā *Fúyòu biān* infant-rearing) traditions. No standard biographical record located.
