Xiè Féng-yuán 謝逢源, *hào* Quán-shí shān-rén 拳石山人 ("the Mountain Man of the Fist-shaped Rock"). Mid-19th-century Sū-zhōu-region literatus. Author of the *tí-cí* (inscription-poem) prefacing [[KR3eq086]] *Shì-yǐn-lú yī-xué zá-zhù*, dated *Guǐ-chǒu sú-fó-rì* 癸丑俗佛日 (1853, popular Buddha's-birthday). The poem invokes the *Wáng Shū-hé family line* and frames Wáng Dé-sēn's work in the *Yán-nián* (Péng Yán-nián 彭延年 longevity-cultivation) and *Fú-yòu* (Mò-jiā *Fú-yòu biān* infant-rearing) traditions. No standard biographical record located.
