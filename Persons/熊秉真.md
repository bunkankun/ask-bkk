# Xióng Bǐngzhēn 熊秉真

Contemporary historian (Academia Sinica / Chinese University of Hong Kong) specialising in the history of Chinese paediatrics, childhood, and the family. Author of *A Tender Voyage: Children and Childhood in Late Imperial China* (Stanford UP, 2005), the standard English-language monograph on Míng-Qīng paediatric medicine and childhood.
