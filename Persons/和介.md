# 和介 Wájiè

**Wájiè** 和介 (Japanese **Wakai**), 15th-century Japanese physician named in [[韓繼禧]] Hán Jìxǐ's 1474 preface to the Korean *Shényìng jīng* 神應經 ([[KR3ee029]]) as a transmitter of the Japanese *Yōngjū bāxué fǎ* 癰疽八穴法 needling method.
