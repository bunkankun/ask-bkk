Hashimoto Setsuzai 橋本節齋 (fl. mid-19th c.) was a late-Edo Japanese physician whose *Naika zensho* 內科全書 mapped the major biomedical-internal-medicine syndromes onto traditional therapy; the work was a principal source for [[大塚敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu's 1933 *Zhōngguó nèikē yījiàn* ([[KR3eh027]]).
