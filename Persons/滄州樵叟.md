Pseudonymous Southern-Sòng compiler, "the Cāng-zhōu Woodcutter" — the *nom-de-plume* of an otherwise unidentified scholar of the Lǐ-zōng (1224–1264) reign, attested in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* as the compiler of the *Qìng-yuán dǎng-jìn* 慶元黨禁 ([[KR2g0027]], a one-*juàn* documentary record of the 1196–1202 proscription of the *wěi-xué* faction under Hán Tuō-zhòu) and of the parallel *Shàoxīng zhèng-lùn* 紹興正論 (the corresponding record of the *Shàoxīng* proscription under Qín Huì); the *Sì-kù* editors note that the two works are by the same hand. He is sometimes identified by later scholarship with one of the proscribed faction's surviving descendants writing in safety after Hán's downfall, but no firm identification exists.
