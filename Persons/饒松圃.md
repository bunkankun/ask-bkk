# 饒松圃 Ráo Sōngpǔ

**Ráo Sōngpǔ** 饒松圃, late-Qīng Tóngzhì-era Beijing bookseller and friend of [[廖潤鴻]] Liào Rùnhóng; persuaded Liào to publish his *Zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* 針灸集成 ([[KR3ee007]], 1874).
