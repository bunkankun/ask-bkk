Sūn Zàifēng 孫在豐 (1644–1689), zì Qǐzhān 屺瞻, was an early-Kāngxī court official and editor from Déqīng 德清 (Húzhōu 湖州, Zhèjiāng 浙江). He passed the *jìnshì* examination in *Kāngxī* 9 = 1670 and rose through Hànlín appointments to Junior Vice-Minister of Works (Gōngbù shìláng 工部侍郎). He served on the imperial diary-lecture team and was in *Kāngxī* 19 (1680) commanded together with Niú Niǔ 牛鈕 ([[牛鈕]]) to compile the *Rì jiǎng Yìjīng jiěyì* 日講易經解義 ([[KR1a0116]]). His later career was tied to the supervision of waterworks (*hé wù* 河務) on the Yellow River, where in 1689 he died at his post in age 46.
