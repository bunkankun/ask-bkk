# 尤憲庚

Yóu Xiàngēng 尤憲庚 (late Qīng), physician from Tōngxǔ 通許 county, Hénán; practised at the Qǐxiàn 杞縣 City God Temple. Teacher of [[翟竹亭]] Zhái Zhútíng (b. 1875), in whose biographical preface to [[KR3ep104]] *Húyuè cūnsǒu yīàn* he is credited with instructing Zhái in pulse-theory and acupuncture during a five-year apprenticeship begun at age 16.
