Wèi Xiàn 魏峴 (fl. 1208–1242), of Yín County 鄞縣 (Sì-míng / modern Níngbō), was a Southern Sòng official who rose to *Cháo-fèng-láng* 朝奉郎 and Maritime Trade Superintendent of Fú-jiàn Circuit (*tí-jǔ Fú-jiàn lù shìbó* 提舉福建路市舶). He directed the Jiā-dìng-era reconstruction of the Tā-shān weir 它山堰 — the Táng Tài-hé 7 (833) hydraulic work of Wáng Yuán-wěi — and recorded the work in his *Sì-míng Tā-shān shuǐlì bèi-lǎn* ([[KR2k0062]]) in 2 *juan*, the earliest surviving monograph on any of the four still-functioning ancient Chinese major irrigation systems.
