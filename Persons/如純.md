Rú-chún 如純, *zì* **Shàn-yī** 善一 ("Sole Good"); often referred to as **Tiān-lóng Shàn-yī** 天龍善一 or **Yǔ-ān Tiān-lóng** 羽安天龍. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shàn-quán Dá-wèi** 善權達位 (1618–1684, cf. [[KR6q0581]]). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Qián Xí-ān** 黔習安 (Guì-zhōu); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Formation.** At 17 took tonsure at **Fǎ-hǎi-sì** 法海寺 under elder **Líng-guāng** 靈光. Two years in, met his real teacher **Quán hé-shàng** 權和尚 (= Dá-wèi), given the *wàn-fǎ guī yī* *gōng-àn*. Took full ordination at **Yún-jiù-shān** 雲鷲山 under **Dǐng-xiāng** 頂相和尚. *Cān* under **Tiān-tāi Yuè-fēng** 天台月峰. Final *yìn-zhèng* under **Shàn-quán Dá-wèi** at **Sōng-kuī** 松巋. After Quán's death (1684) traveled ten years in Zhè-jiāng. Then his *fǎ-shū* **Gǔ-yuán** 古源和尚 invited him to open dharma at Tiān-lóng.

**Abbacies.** **Tiān-lóng-shān Pǔ-dé chán-sì** 天龍山普德禪寺; **Sōng-kuī-shān Pǔ-guāng chán-sì** 松巋山普光禪寺 (three years); returned to Tiān-lóng.

**Major editorial work.** Compiler of [[KR6q0036]] 《黔南會燈錄》 *Qián-nán huì-dēng-lù* — the major regional Chán biographical anthology of Guì-zhōu, drawing on southwestern *yǔ-lù* he encountered while traveling. The 1673 preface dates an earlier draft; final compilation continued through the 1690s.

**Disciples.** **Xué-zhèng** [[學正]] (recorder of [[KR6q0580]]); **Míng-zhuǎn** 明轉.

**Works.**
