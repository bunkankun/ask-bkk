Rúchún 如純, *zì* **Shànyī** 善一 ("Sole Good"); often referred to as **Tiānlóng Shànyī** 天龍善一 or **Yǔān Tiānlóng** 羽安天龍. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Shànquán Dáwèi** 善權達位 (1618–1684, cf. [[KR6q0581]]). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Qián Xíān** 黔習安 (Guìzhōu); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Formation.** At 17 took tonsure at **Fǎhǎisì** 法海寺 under elder **Língguāng** 靈光. Two years in, met his real teacher **Quán héshàng** 權和尚 (= Dáwèi), given the *wànfǎ guī yī* *gōngàn*. Took full ordination at **Yúnjiùshān** 雲鷲山 under **Dǐngxiāng** 頂相和尚. *Cān* under **Tiāntāi Yuèfēng** 天台月峰. Final *yìnzhèng* under **Shànquán Dáwèi** at **Sōngkuī** 松巋. After Quán's death (1684) traveled ten years in Zhèjiāng. Then his *fǎshū* **Gǔyuán** 古源和尚 invited him to open dharma at Tiānlóng.

**Abbacies.** **Tiānlóngshān Pǔdé chánsì** 天龍山普德禪寺; **Sōngkuīshān Pǔguāng chánsì** 松巋山普光禪寺 (three years); returned to Tiānlóng.

**Major editorial work.** Compiler of [[KR6q0036]] 《黔南會燈錄》 *Qiánnán huìdēnglù* — the major regional Chán biographical anthology of Guìzhōu, drawing on southwestern *yǔlù* he encountered while traveling. The 1673 preface dates an earlier draft; final compilation continued through the 1690s.

**Disciples.** **Xuézhèng** [[學正]] (recorder of [[KR6q0580]]); **Míngzhuǎn** 明轉.

**Works.**
