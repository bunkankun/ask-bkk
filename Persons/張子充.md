**Zhāng Zǐchōng** 張子充, Northern-Sòng physician of the Xīnān 新安 (Huīzhōu) Zhāng house, great-great-uncle of [[張杲]] Zhāng Gǎo (author of [[KR3eq024]] *Yī shuō*); trained under [[范純仁]] Fàn Zhōngxuān and served at the Sòng capitals, founding the family medical tradition continued over three generations.
