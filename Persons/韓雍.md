Hán Yōng 韓雍 (1422–1478), *zì* **Yǒngxī** 永熙, posthumous title (Zhèngdé) **Xiāngyì** 襄毅. Native of **Wúxiàn** 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Zhèngtǒng *rénxū* (1442) *jìnshì*; rose to *yòu qiān dūyùshǐ* 右僉都御史 jointly *zǒngdū LiǎngGuǎng* 總督兩廣. Great mid-Míng frontier commander: in Chénghuà 1–7 (1465–1471) directed the **Dàténgxiá** 大藤峽 pacification campaigns against the **Yáo** 瑤 and **Zhuàng** 壯 peoples of Guǎngxī, with **Zhào Fǔ** 趙輔; from 1471–1477 consolidated central Míng authority in the LiǎngGuǎng. *Míng shǐ* j. 178. Surviving works are [[KR4e0107]] *Xiāngyì wénjí* (15 *juǎn*). CBDB id 34511 (gives 1422–1479; the lifedates 1422–1478 follow *Míng shǐ* and catalog meta).
