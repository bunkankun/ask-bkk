Hán Yōng 韓雍 (1422–1478), *zì* **Yǒng-xī** 永熙, posthumous title (Zhèng-dé) **Xiāng-yì** 襄毅. Native of **Wú-xiàn** 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). Zhèng-tǒng *rén-xū* (1442) *jìn-shì*; rose to *yòu qiān dū-yù-shǐ* 右僉都御史 jointly *zǒng-dū Liǎng-Guǎng* 總督兩廣. Great mid-Míng frontier commander: in Chéng-huà 1–7 (1465–1471) directed the **Dà-téng-xiá** 大藤峽 pacification campaigns against the **Yáo** 瑤 and **Zhuàng** 壯 peoples of Guǎng-xī, with **Zhào Fǔ** 趙輔; from 1471–1477 consolidated central Míng authority in the Liǎng-Guǎng. *Míng shǐ* j. 178. Surviving works are [[KR4e0107]] *Xiāng-yì wén-jí* (15 *juǎn*). CBDB id 34511 (gives 1422–1479; the lifedates 1422–1478 follow *Míng shǐ* and catalog meta).
