**Bìhé Guǎn Zhǔrén** 碧荷館主人 ("Master of the Jade-Green Lotus Studio") is the pen name of the author of the late Qīng science-fiction utopian novel *Xīn Jìyuán* 新紀元 ([[KR4k0259]]), published in 1908. The Internet Archive record for the novel's digital facsimile gives the author's dates as 1871–1919, but these cannot be confirmed from primary sources. The true identity behind the pseudonym has not been established in accessible scholarship. No CBDB record identified.
