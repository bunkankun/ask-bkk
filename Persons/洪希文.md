Yuán-period 元 Fú-jiàn-regional poet and educational official. *Zì* **Rǔzhì** 汝質; *hào* **Qùhuá** 去華. Native of **Pútián** 莆田 (Fújiàn). 1282–1366 (Wikipedia confirms; CBDB 34360 records but lacks dates).

**Career.** Held office as *xùndǎo* 訓導 (instructor / education official).

**Family.** Son of **Hóng Yánhǔ** [[洪巖虎]] (1239–1306), a Sòng-period *jiàoyú*. Hóng Yánhǔ's original *Xuānqú jí* 軒渠集 was destroyed in the YuánMíng transition; fragments survive only at the end of Hóng Xīwén's *Xù Xuānqú jí*.

**Collection.** *Xù Xuānqú jí* [[KR4d0480]] in 10 *juàn* — preserving Hóng Xīwén's own poetry plus his father's surviving fragments. Mid-Míng reprint by his seventh-generation descendant **Hóng Zhū** 洪珠 (*Shàoxīng zhīfǔ*), commissioned to **Cài Zōngyǎn** 蔡宗兗 of Shānyīn, cut at Jiājìng *guǐsì* (1533). The Sìkù editors evaluate Hóng's verse as purely Sòng-style — *qīngqiú jīzhuàng* (clear-firm, struggling-vigorous) — *luòluò dú xíng* (striding-alone uprightly), distinct from both late-Yuán florid style and Míng-period grand-stately register.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0480]] *Xù Xuānqú jí* 續軒渠集 (撰).
