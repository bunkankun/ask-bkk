Yuán-period 元 Fú-jiàn-regional poet and educational official. *Zì* **Rǔ-zhì** 汝質; *hào* **Qù-huá** 去華. Native of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Fú-jiàn). 1282–1366 (Wikipedia confirms; CBDB 34360 records but lacks dates).

**Career.** Held office as *xùn-dǎo* 訓導 (instructor / education official).

**Family.** Son of **Hóng Yán-hǔ** [[洪巖虎]] (1239–1306), a Sòng-period *jiào-yú*. Hóng Yán-hǔ's original *Xuān-qú jí* 軒渠集 was destroyed in the Yuán-Míng transition; fragments survive only at the end of Hóng Xī-wén's *Xù Xuān-qú jí*.

**Collection.** *Xù Xuān-qú jí* [[KR4d0480]] in 10 *juàn* — preserving Hóng Xī-wén's own poetry plus his father's surviving fragments. Mid-Míng reprint by his seventh-generation descendant **Hóng Zhū** 洪珠 (*Shào-xīng zhī-fǔ*), commissioned to **Cài Zōng-yǎn** 蔡宗兗 of Shān-yīn, cut at Jiā-jìng *guǐ-sì* (1533). The Sìkù editors evaluate Hóng's verse as purely Sòng-style — *qīng-qiú jī-zhuàng* (clear-firm, struggling-vigorous) — *luò-luò dú xíng* (striding-alone uprightly), distinct from both late-Yuán florid style and Míng-period grand-stately register.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0480]] *Xù Xuān-qú jí* 續軒渠集 (撰).
