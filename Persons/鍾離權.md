**Zhōnglí Quán** 鍾離權, styled **Zhèng yáng zhēn rén** 正陽真人, is a legendary Daoist immortal — traditionally the teacher of [[呂洞賓]] Lǚ Dòngbīn and the senior of the **Bā xiān** 八仙 (Eight Immortals). Hagiographies place him variously in the Hàn or Táng. A major figure in the **ZhōngLǚ** 鍾呂 inner-alchemical tradition, precursor to Sòng-era Quán zhēn. Attributed author of [[KR5f0025]] *Mì chuán Zhèng yáng zhēn rén Líng bǎo bì fǎ* 祕傳正陽真人靈寶畢法 and numerous alchemical writings.
