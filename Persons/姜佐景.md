Jiāng Zuǒjǐng 姜佐景, 1896–1968, Republican-period Shànghǎi physician and the senior disciple of [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐngfǔ. He is principally remembered as the compiler / editor and *ànyǔ* (commentary) author of [[KR3ep070]] *Jīngfāng shíyàn lù* — the single most-influential twentieth-century Chinese-medical casebook and the foundation document of the modern *jīngfāng* (classical-formula) revival in Shànghǎi, Taiwan, and Japan. After Cáo's death in 1937 he continued teaching at the Shànghǎi Chinese-Medical School and was a senior figure in the Shànghǎi medical-education establishment through the mid-twentieth century.
