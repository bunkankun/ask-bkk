Jiāng Zuǒ-jǐng 姜佐景, 1896–1968, Republican-period Shàng-hǎi physician and the senior disciple of [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐng-fǔ. He is principally remembered as the compiler / editor and *àn-yǔ* (commentary) author of [[KR3ep070]] *Jīng-fāng shí-yàn lù* — the single most-influential twentieth-century Chinese-medical casebook and the foundation document of the modern *jīng-fāng* (classical-formula) revival in Shàng-hǎi, Taiwan, and Japan. After Cáo's death in 1937 he continued teaching at the Shàng-hǎi Chinese-Medical School and was a senior figure in the Shàng-hǎi medical-education establishment through the mid-twentieth century.
