# Huì-bì 慧弼

Southern-Sòng Chán monk; dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) of Xuě-fēng Huì-kōng 雪峰慧空 (1096–1158). Lifedates unrecorded. Editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0281]].
