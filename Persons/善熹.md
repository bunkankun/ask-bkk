Shàn-xī 善熹 (active 12th c.), Southern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk and the disciple of [[師會]] Shī-huì 師會. After Shī-huì's death, Shàn-xī completed his master's unfinished commentary on [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's [[KR6e0074|*Wǔ jiào zhāng*]] — namely the [[KR6e0078|*Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng fù gǔ jì* 華嚴一乘教義分齊章復古記]] (X998), which Shī-huì had not finished before his death. Per the colophon to that work and to the *Wǔ jiào zhāng kē* 五教章科 (X997), Shàn-xī's contribution involved completing the section on *duàn huò fēn qí* and editing the whole. His birth and death dates are not preserved.
