# Dū Yù 都郁

*Zì* Zǐwén 子文. Father of [[都絜]] Dū Jié, of Dānyáng 丹陽 (modern Jiāngsū). A Northern- and early-Southern-Sòng local academy teacher (*jiàoguān*) of Huìzhōu 徽州 who devoted his life to *Yì*-learning. His own writings on the *Yì* are not preserved, but the *biàntǐ* hermeneutic systematized in his son's *Yì biàntǐ yì* 易變體義 ([[KR1a0027]]) is described by Dū Jié as based on what he had heard from his father. Lifedates not on record.
