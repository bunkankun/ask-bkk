Fàn Néngjùn 范能濬 (fl. *Kāngxī*, late 17th century), Qing-period *yìsūn* (descendant) of Fàn Chúnrén 范純仁 [[范純仁]] (1027–1101). On the basis of Fàn Néngjùn's *jiùběn* (old-recension) of the *Fàn Zhōngxuān gōng wénjí*, he re-collated and supplemented the *yíwén* (lost pieces) and *bǔbiān* (supplementary collection) — collecting Fàn Chúnrén's pieces (7), his younger brother Fàn Chúnlǐ 范純禮's pieces (2), and Fàn Chúncuì 范純粹's pieces (19) for the *yíwén* portion; and adding 12 pieces of *zhìcí*, *tíbá* and one *Chúnrén* letter for the *bǔbiān* portion. In *Kāngxī dīnghài* / 1707 his 20th-generation descendant Fàn Shíchóng 范時崇 issued the resulting *bǔbiān* edition jointly with Fàn Zhòngyān 范仲淹's collection.
