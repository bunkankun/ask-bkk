Fàn Néng-jùn 范能濬 (fl. *Kāng-xī*, late 17th century), Qing-period *yì-sūn* (descendant) of Fàn Chún-rén 范純仁 [[范純仁]] (1027–1101). On the basis of Fàn Néng-jùn's *jiù-běn* (old-recension) of the *Fàn Zhōng-xuān gōng wén-jí*, he re-collated and supplemented the *yí-wén* (lost pieces) and *bǔ-biān* (supplementary collection) — collecting Fàn Chún-rén's pieces (7), his younger brother Fàn Chún-lǐ 范純禮's pieces (2), and Fàn Chún-cuì 范純粹's pieces (19) for the *yí-wén* portion; and adding 12 pieces of *zhì-cí*, *tí-bá* and one *Chún-rén* letter for the *bǔ-biān* portion. In *Kāng-xī dīng-hài* / 1707 his 20th-generation descendant Fàn Shí-chóng 范時崇 issued the resulting *bǔ-biān* edition jointly with Fàn Zhòng-yān 范仲淹's collection.
