Major early-Qīng calligrapher and court official; *zì* Yúntāo 雲韜, *hào* Shùgōng 樹峰, of Wújiāng 吳江. Lifedates 1639–1708 (CBDB matches catalog meta). *Kāngxī* 24 (1685) *jìnshì*; rose through the Hanlin Academy to the post of Vice-Minister of Rites and a Reader-in-Waiting of the Southern Studio (*Nánshūfáng* 南書房). A favoured calligrapher of the Kāngxī emperor, he was the principal compiler of the imperially-authorised [[KR3h0061]] *Yùdìng Pèiwénzhāi shūhuà pǔ* 御定佩文齋書畫譜 (1708), the greatest single Chinese encyclopaedic compilation on calligraphy and painting, presented to the throne in the year of his death.
