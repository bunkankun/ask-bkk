# Zōngzhé 宗哲

Early-fourteenth-century Japanese novice (*shāmí* 沙彌) named in the 1313 Ryūshō-ji 龍翔寺 colophon of the *Xūtáng héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0065]]) as one of the lay donors who covered the cost of Sōtaku's reprint.
