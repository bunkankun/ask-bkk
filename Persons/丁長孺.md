**Dīng Chángrú** 丁長孺, late-Míng disciple-editor of [[繆希雍]] Miù Xīyōng who anthologised Miù's case-records — the editorial precedent invoked by [[周鑅]] Zhōu Yīng in his 1843 preface to [[KR3eq009]] *Huíchūn lù*, the case-records of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng.
