Jīnxiū 今羞. Cáodòng dharma-heir of **Qiānshān Shèngrén Hánkě** [[函可]] (1612–1660), one of the master's *gāozú* 高足 ("eminent disciples") named in his *xíngzhuàng*. Lifedates unknown. One of the *děnglù* (recorders) of [[KR6q0527]] 《千山剩人禪師語錄》, J38 B407.
