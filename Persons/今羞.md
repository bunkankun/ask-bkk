Jīn-xiū 今羞. Cáodòng dharma-heir of **Qiān-shān Shèng-rén Hán-kě** [[函可]] (1612–1660), one of the master's *gāo-zú* 高足 ("eminent disciples") named in his *xíng-zhuàng*. Lifedates unknown. One of the *děng-lù* (recorders) of [[KR6q0527]] 《千山剩人禪師語錄》, J38 B407.
