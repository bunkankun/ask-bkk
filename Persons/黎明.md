Contemporary Chinese specialist of Dūnhuáng Buddhist manuscripts and Táng commentary literature, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of Dàoyè's two-fascicle *Jìngmíng jīng jíjiě Guānzhōng shū* 淨名經集解關中疏 — the standard Táng *Vimalakīrti* commentary — published as [[KR6v0026]] (upper fascicle) and [[KR6v0032]] (lower fascicle), reconstructed from collation of more than thirty Beijing Library and dozens of British, French, and Russian Dūnhuáng witnesses.
