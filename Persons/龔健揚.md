**Gōng Jiànyáng** 龔健揚 — Qīng official and junior colleague of [[閻純璽]] Yán Chúnxǐ. Prefacer of Yán's *Tāichǎn xīnfǎ* ([[KR3ei058]]) in Yōngzhèng 3 (1725), while Yán was being sent on imperial commission to Guǎngxī. Not in CBDB.
