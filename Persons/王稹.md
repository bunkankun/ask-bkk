Wáng Zhěn 王稹 (also written 楨; mid-to-late 15th cent.), second son of the *Lìbù shàngshū* **Wáng Zhí** 王直 ([[王直]]) of **Tàihé** 泰和. In early Chénghuà supplemented his elder brother **Wáng Bǎo** 王䆅's 13-*juǎn* original recension of their father's collected works with a 37-*juǎn* *hòují* — material the original recension had not included plus everything composed after Wáng Zhí's 1457 retirement until his 1462 death — making the present 50-*juǎn* [[KR4e0095]] *Yìān wénjí*. No further biographical details preserved in standard sources.
