# Zǐhé 子和

Northern-Sòng Chán attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Huánglóng Bǎojué Zǔxīn 黃龍寶覺祖心 (1025–1100). Recorder (*lù* 錄) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0278]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
