**Xǔ Kèchāng** 許克昌 (*zì* Lúnshēng 倫聲, *fl.* late Qiánlóng – Dàoguāng, 清), Hézhōu 和州 (Ānhuī) National-Academy student (國學生). A reclusive scholar-physician who divided his time between mountain seclusion at home and itinerant practice in the lower Yangtze. The author of [[KR3ek001]] *Wàikē zhèngzhì quánshū* 外科證治全書 (5 *juǎn*, first printed Dàoguāng 11 = 1831 at the urging of the salt-tax commissioner Chéng Huáijǐng 程懷璟; reprinted 1865 and 1867), assembled out of more than twenty years of clinical practice supplementing the unfinished draft of his deceased friend [[畢法]] Bì Cānglín. Reported to be in his eighties at the time of the *editio princeps*, suggesting birth in the mid-1740s, but no firm lifedates are documented. Xǔ trained medically under the Dōngtái physician Zhōu Shūxuān 周舒軒 and previously published editions of Zhèng Xībīn's 鄭西賓 *Dòuzhèng bìdú* 痘證必讀 and a reconstruction of Gěng Rénzhāi's 耿仁齋 *Mákē míngjìng* 麻科明鏡. The local CBDB dump returns a 許克昌 (id 45531) listed as Sòng-dynasty — clearly not this Qīng physician — so no CBDB id is assigned.
