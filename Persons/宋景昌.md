# Sòng Jǐngchāng 宋景昌

Native of Yáng-zhōu. Active in the late Dào-guāng and early Xián-fēng decades (1830s–1850s). Birth and death years not securely recorded (CBDB id 125613 confirms the name and dynasty but supplies no dates).

A textual-critical scholar associated with the Yí-jiā-táng 宜稼堂 cóng-shū compilation of Yù Sōng-nián 郁松年 (Yáng-zhōu, c. 1842), which recovered and printed several previously-lost Sòng-Yuán mathematical works: [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo's [[KR3fc008]] *Shù-shū jiǔ-zhāng*, [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī's [[KR3fc010]] *Yáng Huī suàn-fǎ* and [[KR3fc011]] *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ*, and [[朱世傑]] Zhū Shìjié's [[KR3fc017]] *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn*. Sòng Jǐngchāng's principal scholarly contribution was the textual-critical apparatus *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ zhá-jì* 詳解九章算法札記 ([[KR3fc012]]), a running notebook of textual emendations and procedural verifications produced as he edited Yáng Huī's *Xiáng-jiě* for the cóng-shū. The work systematically collates the recovered Korean Chosŏn recension against the Sòng-fragment survivals in the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* and supplies emendations where the two lines diverge, accompanied by *cǎo* (procedural-working) verifications of each restored passage.

Sòng Jǐngchāng's role in the 1842 Yí-jiā-táng recovery was parallel to [[沈欽裴]] Shěn Qīnpéi's and [[羅士琳]] Luó Shìlín's roles in the parallel *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn* recovery project; the three together constitute the principal scholarly team responsible for the Dào-guāng-era re-introduction of Sòng-Yuán algebra to working Chinese mathematics.
