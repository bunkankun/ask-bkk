# 舒馳遠

Shū Chíyuǎn, Qīng *shānghán* school physician; author of *Shānghán jízhù* 傷寒集註 cited in [[KR3em032]] *Báihóu biànzhèng* 白喉辨證 as the source of a *yānchuāng* 咽瘡 formula using *dēngxīn* 燈心 burnt inside an emptied egg-shell.
