# Yóu Wànjùn 游萬儁

Late-Wànlì-era *Sānyī jiào* lay disciple (門人) of [[林兆恩]] Lín Zhàoēn at Pútián 莆田 in Fújiàn. Collator (校正) of Lín's *Bōrě xīnjīng gàilùn* 般若心經概論 ([[KR6c0164]]) and editor of several other Línzǐ writings.
