**Fù Yè** 傅燁 was a Sòng Daoist, author of [[KR5g0114]] ***Hù guó jiā jì Jiāng dōng wáng líng qiān*** 護國嘉濟江東王靈籤 — a divinatory stave-text for a regional cult of the Jiāngdōng King.
