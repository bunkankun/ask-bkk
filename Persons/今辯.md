Jīn-biàn 今辯 (also written 今辨), *zì* **Lè-shuō** 樂說 ("Joyful Discourse"). Cáodòng dharma-heir of **Tiān-rán Hán-shì** 天然函昰 [[函昰]] (1608–1685). Born **1637**, died **康熙乙亥 1/8** = **1695-02-20**, age **59**, *fǎ-là* **38**. Native of **Pān-yú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu); lay surname **Mài** 麥.

**Formation.** Initially trained under **Hǎi-fǎ** 海法禪師 in scripture and *fàn-xíng* (pure conduct). At 19 went to **Kuāng-lú** 匡廬 (= 廬山) to seek tonsure under **Tiān-rán**. Took full ordination at **Léi-fēng** 雷峰 in **順治庚子** = **1660**. When **Dàn-guī Jīn-shì** 澹歸今釋 opened **Dān-xiá** 丹霞, accompanied Tiān-rán there for several years. Received *fù-fǎ* (dharma-transmission) in **康熙丁未** = **1667** and *fēn-zuò* (co-abbacy) at Dān-xiá in **戊申** = **1668**. Successively held **Hǎi-zhuàng** 海幢, **Léi-fēng** 雷峰, **Yǒng-níng** 永寧, and **Cháng-qìng** 長慶 — hence the alternate appellation 海幢今辯.

**Works.** Re-compiler (*zhòng-biān* 重編) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0526]] 《廬山天然禪師語錄》 12 juan, J38 B406, printed in 1670 with a preface by **Lù Shì-kǎi** 陸世楷 (今亙). His own *yǔ-lù* survives in CBETA Jiā-xīng (J39 B450 *Lè-shuō Biàn chán-shī yǔ-lù*).

**Sources.** 《丹霞山志》 vol. 3; 《丹霞山志》 vol. 5 〈樂說辯禪師塔銘〉; 《西禪長慶寺志》 vol. 2.
