Jīnbiàn 今辯 (also written 今辨), *zì* **Lèshuō** 樂說 ("Joyful Discourse"). Cáodòng dharma-heir of **Tiānrán Hánshì** 天然函昰 [[函昰]] (1608–1685). Born **1637**, died **康熙乙亥 1/8** = **1695-02-20**, age **59**, *fǎlà* **38**. Native of **Pānyú** 番禺 (Guǎngzhōu); lay surname **Mài** 麥.

**Formation.** Initially trained under **Hǎifǎ** 海法禪師 in scripture and *fànxíng* (pure conduct). At 19 went to **Kuānglú** 匡廬 (= 廬山) to seek tonsure under **Tiānrán**. Took full ordination at **Léifēng** 雷峰 in **順治庚子** = **1660**. When **Dànguī Jīnshì** 澹歸今釋 opened **Dānxiá** 丹霞, accompanied Tiānrán there for several years. Received *fùfǎ* (dharma-transmission) in **康熙丁未** = **1667** and *fēnzuò* (co-abbacy) at Dānxiá in **戊申** = **1668**. Successively held **Hǎizhuàng** 海幢, **Léifēng** 雷峰, **Yǒngníng** 永寧, and **Chángqìng** 長慶 — hence the alternate appellation 海幢今辯.

**Works.** Re-compiler (*zhòngbiān* 重編) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0526]] 《廬山天然禪師語錄》 12 juan, J38 B406, printed in 1670 with a preface by **Lù Shìkǎi** 陸世楷 (今亙). His own *yǔlù* survives in CBETA Jiāxīng (J39 B450 *Lèshuō Biàn chánshī yǔlù*).

**Sources.** 《丹霞山志》 vol. 3; 《丹霞山志》 vol. 5 〈樂說辯禪師塔銘〉; 《西禪長慶寺志》 vol. 2.
