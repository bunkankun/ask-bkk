# Lǐ Chángmào 李長茂

An early-Qīng practical-mathematical scholar, *fl.* late Kāng-xī / early Yōng-zhèng (c. 1690–1730). Birth and death years not securely recorded. CBDB id 333582 confirms the name and dynasty.

His sole surviving major work is the *Suàn-hǎi xiáng-shuō* 算海詳說 ([[KR3fc034]]) in 11 *juàn* — a comprehensive practical-arithmetic compendium in the lineage of [[程大位]] Chéng Dàwèi's [[KR3fc027]] *Suàn-fǎ tǒng-zōng*, updated for the post-Kāng-xī mathematical environment. The work belongs to the early-Qīng popular-mathematical genre serving merchant-apprentice and elementary-school education, in parallel with the imperially-sponsored *Shù-lǐ jīng-yùn* 數理精蘊 (1722) and the *Méng-yǎng zhāi* mathematicians' more advanced productions.
