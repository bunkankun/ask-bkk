Huáng Shìhéng 黃士衡 was a Republican-era popular fiction writer, author of *Qíncháo Yěshǐ* 秦朝野史 ([[KR4k0308]]), a 96-chapter historical novel on the Qín dynasty. No biographical details have been located; active ca. 1900s–1930s.
