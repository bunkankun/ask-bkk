Dǒng Bǐngchún 董秉純 (1724–1794), *zì*/*hào* Xiǎoshān 曉山, native of Níngbō, Zhèjiāng. The senior disciple of [[全祖望]] Quán Zǔwàng and the principal editor of Quán's posthumous works. Dǒng prepared the clean-copy manuscript of Quán's *Jíqítíng jí* [[KR4f0054]] during Quán's lifetime; when Quán died in 1755 leaving the project unprinted, Dǒng spent the next half-century arranging for the imprint, eventually published as the standard 1804 (Jiāqìng 9) edition. He composed the front-matter *Quánshì shìpǔ* 全氏世譜 (clan lineage) and *Niánpǔ* 年譜 (chronological biography) of Quán Zǔwàng, on the model of Hú Zhù's *shìpǔ* prefacing the *Qiánxī jí* of Sòng Lián and Wàn Sīdà's *shìpǔ* prefacing Huáng Zōngxī's *Nánléi jí*. Dǒng died before the imprint was completed; the print run was carried through by Shǐ Mèngjiāo 史夢蛟 (*zì* Zhúfáng) of Yúyáo as collator. CBDB id 89485.
