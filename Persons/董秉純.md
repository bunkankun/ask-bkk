Dǒng Bǐngchún 董秉純 (1724–1794), *zì*/*hào* Xiǎo-shān 曉山, native of Níng-bō, Zhè-jiāng. The senior disciple of [[全祖望]] Quán Zǔ-wàng and the principal editor of Quán's posthumous works. Dǒng prepared the clean-copy manuscript of Quán's *Jí-qí-tíng jí* [[KR4f0054]] during Quán's lifetime; when Quán died in 1755 leaving the project unprinted, Dǒng spent the next half-century arranging for the imprint, eventually published as the standard 1804 (Jiāqìng 9) edition. He composed the front-matter *Quán-shì shì-pǔ* 全氏世譜 (clan lineage) and *Nián-pǔ* 年譜 (chronological biography) of Quán Zǔ-wàng, on the model of Hú Zhù's *shì-pǔ* prefacing the *Qián-xī jí* of Sòng Lián and Wàn Sī-dà's *shì-pǔ* prefacing Huáng Zōng-xī's *Nán-léi jí*. Dǒng died before the imprint was completed; the print run was carried through by Shǐ Mèng-jiāo 史夢蛟 (*zì* Zhú-fáng) of Yú-yáo as collator. CBDB id 89485.
