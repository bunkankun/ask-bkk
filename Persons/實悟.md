Shí-wù 實悟, *zì* **Dé-yún** 德雲 ("Virtuous Cloud"); often referred to as **Zhāo-jué Dé-yún Wù** 昭覺德雲悟 from his teacher's monastery. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhú-fēng Zhēn-xù** [[真續]] (b. 1660). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0591]] 《昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄》 6 juan, J40 B479.

A separate Qīng monk named **Shí-wù** 實悟 (DILA A046363), *zì* **Zōng-kāi** 宗開, fourth-generation revivalist. Not the same person.
