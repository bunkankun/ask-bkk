Southern-Sòng Chán monk, training disciple (*cān-xué* 參學) and co-editor of the *Yún-gǔ héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0384]]) of the Línjì master Yún-gǔ 雲谷. No further biographical information survives. Not to be confused with the Sixth Patriarch **Huì-néng** 慧能 (638–713), whose personal name was also 惠能 / 慧能 and whose dharma-name in some sources is transmitted as 惟能 as a variant orthography.
