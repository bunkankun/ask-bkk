**Xīwángmǔ** 西王母 (Queen Mother of the West). Mythological Daoist goddess whose audience with [[漢武帝]] Hàn Wǔdì on the seventh-month full-moon day of *Yuánfēng* 1 (110 BCE) is the canonical fable that opens *Jíjiù guǎngshēng jí* 急救廣生集 ([[KR3eo047]])'s exposition of *wù*-day seasonal taboos.
