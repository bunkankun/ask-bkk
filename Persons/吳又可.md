# Wú Yòukě 吳又可

*Zì* alternate of [[吳有性]] Wú Yǒuxìng (1582–1652), late-Míng physician of Jiāngsū, founder of the *wēnyì* 溫疫 (epidemic-warm) theory and author of the *Wēnyì lùn* 溫疫論 (1642). See [[吳有性]] for the primary entry.
