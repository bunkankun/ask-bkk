Tangut-period (Xī-Xià 西夏, 1038–1227) Esoteric Buddhist master, monk of **Hù-guó Rén-wáng-sì** 護國仁王寺 ("Country-Protecting King-of-Humanity Monastery") — one of the principal imperial monasteries of the Tangut state. Recompiler (重集 *zhòng-jí*) of the **Yú-jiā jí-yào yàn-kǒu shī-shí yí** 瑜伽集要燄口施食儀 [[KR6j0761]] (J19nB047), the Yàn-kǒu *food-bestowal* ritual recension that became the textual base of the Qing-period Tiāntái-school redaction by Shòu-dēng 受登. The Tangut empire under the Xià emperors (especially Rén-zōng 仁宗 / Rén-xiào 仁孝, r. 1139–1193) was a major site of Han-Tibet Buddhist exchange; Bù-dòng Jīn-gāng's recompilation interpolated the *Fó-dǐng zūn-shèng tuó-luó-ní* 佛頂尊勝陀羅尼 (Buddha-Crown Victorious dhāraṇī) into the food-bestowal sequence as a vehicle for *rebirth in Sukhāvatī*, integrating elements of the Tibetan Tantric mortuary tradition into the Han-Chinese Yàn-kǒu liturgy.

Life-dates and native place are not recorded in extant sources; he is referred to in the Shòudēng recension only as **Bùdòng shàngshī** 不動上師 ("Master Bùdòng"). Not in DILA Buddhist Studies Person Authority. Not the same person as the deity **Bùdòng Míngwáng** 不動明王 (Acala, also called 不動金剛明王) — note the homonymy.

Source: colophon and editorial notes of *Yújiā jíyào yànkǒu shīshí yí* J19nB047 [[KR6j0761]].
