Xióng Jié 熊節 (Sòng), *zì* Duāncāo 端操, was a direct disciple of Zhū Xī, native of Jiànyáng 建陽 (Fújiàn — Zhū Xī's local district). Held office to *Tōngzhí láng* 通直郎 with assignment as *Zhī Mǐnqīngxiàn* 知閩清縣 (Magistrate of Mǐnqīng, Fúzhōu). Compiler of the *Xìnglǐ qúnshū jùjiě* 性理群書句解 ([[KR3a0067]]), the first major Sòng *Lǐxué* topical anthology — predating the more-celebrated *Xìnglǐ dàquán* ([[KR3a0078]]) of Yǒnglè 1415. Annotated by his fellow Jiànyáng townsman Xióng Gāngdà 熊剛大 (a separate person, possibly kinsman). CBDB id 11820, no dates.
