Xióng Jié 熊節 (Sòng), *zì* Duāncāo 端操, was a direct disciple of Zhū Xī, native of Jiàn-yáng 建陽 (Fú-jiàn — Zhū Xī's local district). Held office to *Tōng-zhí láng* 通直郎 with assignment as *Zhī Mǐn-qīng-xiàn* 知閩清縣 (Magistrate of Mǐn-qīng, Fú-zhōu). Compiler of the *Xìng-lǐ qún-shū jù-jiě* 性理群書句解 (KR3a0067), the first major Sòng *Lǐxué* topical anthology — predating the more-celebrated *Xìng-lǐ dà-quán* (KR3a0078) of Yǒng-lè 1415. Annotated by his fellow Jiàn-yáng townsman Xióng Gāng-dà 熊剛大 (a separate person, possibly kinsman). CBDB id 11820, no dates.
