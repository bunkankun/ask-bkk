**Zhōu Dānchén** 周丹忱 of Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng) — late-Qīng prefacer of the 1856 (Xiánfēng *bǐngchén*) wood-block edition of [[單南山]] Shàn Nánshān's *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* ([[KR3ei053]]). His preface supplies the principal transmission history of the work — its origin as a manuscript under Shàn Nánshān's name, its circulation via [[錢升穟]] Qián Shēngsuì, and its eventual print at the Ōu family Zhōngxiàn dì mansion. No further biographical detail is recorded; not in CBDB.
