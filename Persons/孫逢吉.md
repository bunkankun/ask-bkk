**Sūn Féngjí** 孫逢吉 (*zì* Cóngzhī 從之, 1135?–1199?; Southern Sòng, dates uncertain) was the author of the *Zhǔnzhāi xīnzhì jǐlòu túshì* 準齋心制幾漏圖式 ([[KR3fa006]]), a treatise on the design and graduation of the *jǐ-lòu* 幾漏 (table-clepsydra / "table water-clock") with diagrams. He was associated with the Southern Sòng court's *Sī-tiān-jiān* 司天監 tradition of horological instrument-making. He is to be distinguished from the Northern Sòng *Sūn Féngjí* 孫逢吉 (*jìnshì* 1166 by some records) and from the Yuán figure of the same name; the present figure is the *Zhǔnzhāi* of the title, whose studio name is otherwise unrecorded.

Sūn Féng-jí 孫逢吉 (*zì* Yàn-tóng 彥同; ca. 1135 – ca. 1199), native of Fù-chūn 富春 (mod. Hāng-zhōu). Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* of *Lóng-xìng* 1 (1163); the *Sìkù tíyào* for the *Zhí-guān fēn-jì* establishes his career by chronological argument from the dates of his recorded posts. He served as *Mì-shū jiān* 秘書監, *Lì-bù shì-láng* 吏部侍郎, and *Zhī Tài-píng-zhōu* 知太平州 under Níng-zōng. In *Shào-xī* 5 (1194), after Zhū Xī 朱熹 was dismissed from the post of *Jīng-yán Zhí-jiǎng* 經筵直講, Sūn Féng-jí stepped in to lecture on the *Quán-yú* 權輿 chapter of the *Shī jīng*. His chief surviving work is the *Zhí-guān fēn-jì* 職官分紀 ([[KR3k0020]]) in 50 *juan*, the most comprehensive Southern-Sòng compendium of Chinese government office-history and prosopography. He is recorded in *Sòng shǐ* 397 (where the lifedates remain disputed in modern scholarship). The Qín Guān 秦觀 preface attached to the *Zhí-guān fēn-jì* dated 1092 cannot, on chronological grounds, be Qín Guān's — either misattributed or by a different Qín Guān.
