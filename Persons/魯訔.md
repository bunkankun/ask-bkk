Sòng official from Jiā-xīng 嘉興, courtesy name Jì-qīn 季欽. CBDB id 3628 attests his lifedates as 1100–1176. He served principally in *Fú-jiàn* and ended his career as *Fú-jiàn tí-diǎn xíng-yù gōngshì* 福建提點刑獄公事. His *zhì-yì* (epitaph) was written by Zhōu Bì-dà 周必大 and is preserved in the *Píng-yuán jí* 平園集. The *Sì-kù tíyào* notes that the various received accounts which give his courtesy name as *Jì-qīng* 季卿, his native place as Hǎi-yán 海鹽, or his final office as *Tài-fǔ-qīng* 大府卿 are all incorrect. He was a serious Du-studies scholar who annotated Dù Fǔ's poems and compiled the standard Sòng *Dù gōng-bù shī niánpǔ* 杜工部詩年譜 ([[KR2g0008]]) — the foundation of the later Du-studies *niánpǔ* tradition. Yáo Tóng-shòu's *Lè-jiāo sī-yǔ* notes that Lǔ Yín reorganized the order of Dù Fǔ's *Yóu Lóng-mén* through *Guò Dòng-tíng* poems to follow the poet's biographical chronology.
