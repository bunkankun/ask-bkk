Sòng official from Jiāxīng 嘉興, courtesy name Jìqīn 季欽. CBDB id 3628 attests his lifedates as 1100–1176. He served principally in *Fújiàn* and ended his career as *Fújiàn tídiǎn xíngyù gōngshì* 福建提點刑獄公事. His *zhìyì* (epitaph) was written by Zhōu Bìdà 周必大 and is preserved in the *Píngyuán jí* 平園集. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes that the various received accounts which give his courtesy name as *Jìqīng* 季卿, his native place as Hǎiyán 海鹽, or his final office as *Tàifǔqīng* 大府卿 are all incorrect. He was a serious Du-studies scholar who annotated Dù Fǔ's poems and compiled the standard Sòng *Dù gōngbù shī niánpǔ* 杜工部詩年譜 ([[KR2g0008]]) — the foundation of the later Du-studies *niánpǔ* tradition. Yáo Tóngshòu's *Lèjiāo sīyǔ* notes that Lǔ Yín reorganized the order of Dù Fǔ's *Yóu Lóngmén* through *Guò Dòngtíng* poems to follow the poet's biographical chronology.
