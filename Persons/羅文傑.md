Luó Wénjié 羅文傑, *hào* Zhǐyuán 止園 (late-Qīng / early-Republican; the catalog meta gives the dynasty as 清末民初). Republican-period physician of the **Sino-Western *huìtōng* 會通 (integration) school**. Author of [[KR3eq082]] *Zhǐyuán yīhuà* 止園醫話, a three-*juǎn* parallel-presentation textbook of Chinese-medical and Western-medical physiology and clinical practice, addressed to new-generation Chinese-medical students. The work is contemporaneous with Zhāng Xīchún's 張錫純 *Yīxué zhōngzhōng cānxī lù* and belongs to the same intellectual moment of Republican Sino-Western medical reconciliation. Not in CBDB.
