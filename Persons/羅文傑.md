Luó Wén-jié 羅文傑, *hào* Zhǐ-yuán 止園 (late-Qīng / early-Republican; the catalog meta gives the dynasty as 清末民初). Republican-period physician of the **Sino-Western *huì-tōng* 會通 (integration) school**. Author of [[KR3eq082]] *Zhǐ-yuán yī-huà* 止園醫話, a three-*juǎn* parallel-presentation textbook of Chinese-medical and Western-medical physiology and clinical practice, addressed to new-generation Chinese-medical students. The work is contemporaneous with Zhāng Xī-chún's 張錫純 *Yī-xué zhōng-zhōng cān-xī lù* and belongs to the same intellectual moment of Republican Sino-Western medical reconciliation. Not in CBDB.
