# Wāng Yuè 汪越

*Zì* Shītuì 師退. Native of Nán-líng 南陵 (in modern Anhuī, then under Tàipíng-fǔ 太平府). *Jǔrén* of *Kāngxī yǐyǒu* (1705). Floruit ca. 1700–1720.

A *kǎozhèng*-school scholar of the early Kāngxī era, principally remembered for the *Dú Shǐjì shí biǎo* 讀史記十表 (KR2a0005) — the first specialized monograph on the ten chronological tables of Sīmǎ Qiān's *Shǐjì*, supplemented during his lifetime by his fellow-villager Xú Kèfàn 徐克范. No other monograph by him is preserved. Cited as a forerunner of the great Qing tables-scholars Liáng Yùshèng 梁玉繩 and Qián Dàxīn 錢大昕.
