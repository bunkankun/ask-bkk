Wú Rènchén 吳任臣 (1628?–1689?), *zì* Zhìyī 志伊 (also Ěrqì 爾器), of Rénhé 仁和 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Early-Qīng polymath of unusually broad scholarly interests. He passed the special *bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 召試 examination of *Kāngxī* 康熙 18 (1679) — the early Qīng court's recruitment of distinguished Hàn-Chinese scholars — and was appointed *Hànlínyuàn jiǎntǎo* 翰林院檢討. He was a prolific author across history, philology, classical studies, and natural history. His major surviving works include the *Shíguó Chūnqiū* 十國春秋 [[KR2i0021]] in 114 juàn (the definitive synthesis on the Ten Kingdoms of the Five Dynasties), the *Shānhǎijīng guǎngzhù* 山海經廣注 [[KR3l0048]] (a major Qing commentary on the *Shānhǎi jīng*), and the *Zìhuì bǔ* 字彙補 (a supplement to Méi Yīngzuò's 梅膺祚 *Zìhuì*). CBDB id 66030 gives 1628–1689 with question-marks (the conventional dates from Qián Yíjí's 錢儀吉 *Bēizhuàn jí* 碑傳集); these are followed by the catalog meta and adopted here. CBDB id 351735 is a separate entry without dates. Wú belonged to the southern Hángzhōu intellectual world that produced [[毛奇齡]] Máo Qílíng, [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn, and [[查慎行]] Zhā Shènxíng; he and Zhū Yízūn corresponded extensively.
