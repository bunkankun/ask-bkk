Northern Sòng Vinaya scholar **Huáixiǎn** 懷顯 (also written 惟顯, the form preferred in some catalog meta), *hào* **Chíjìng dàshī** 持淨大師, native of *Qiántáng* 錢塘 (Hángzhōu) and resident of the *Xīhú* 西湖 monastic milieu during the Zhézōng 哲宗 (1085–1100) and Huīzōng 徽宗 (1100–1126) reigns. The catalog meta gives his name as 惟顯 but the source signatures of his own work consistently read 懷顯 (e.g. the closing list of *Vinaya*-school patriarchates in *juàn xià* of [[KR6k0255]] reads "*Qiántáng Huáixiǎn lǜshī lì wǔzǔ*" 錢唐懷顯律師立五祖). DILA A001078 likewise gives 懷顯 as the canonical form. Per DILA A001078: monk; *Chíjìng dàshī*; Northern Sòng; the principal datum is his self-preface to the *Lǜzōng xīnxué míngjù*, dated *Shàoshèng* 紹聖 1 *jiǎxū* 甲戌 (1094) at the *Fǎhuá lánruò* 法華蘭若.

Two works are recorded:
- **Lǜzōng xīnxué míngjù** 律宗新學名句 ([[KR6k0255]], X1107, 3 *juàn*) — a Vinaya-school terminological dictionary for novice students, organized by numerical category.
- **Qiántáng shèngjì jì** 錢塘勝跡記 (5 *juàn*, lost) — a gazetteer of Hángzhōu Buddhist sites, no longer extant.

Source: *Sòngsēngzhù* 宋僧著: 339; DILA A001078.
