Zhēn Déxiù 真德秀 (1178–1235), *zì* Jǐngyuán 景元 (later Xīyuán 希元), *hào* Xīshān 西山, posthumous title Wénzhōng 文忠, of Pǔchéng 浦城 in Jiànníng 建寧 (modern Fújiàn). Qìngyuán 5 (1199) *jìnshì*; *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 1205. The leading Southern-Sòng *Dàoxué* statesman of the post-Zhū-Xī generation. After the *Qìngyuán dǎngjìn* 慶元黨禁 (False-Learning Proscription, 1196–1202) lifted, he served in a sequence of increasingly senior posts, rising to *Cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 (Vice Grand Councillor) and *Zīzhèng diàn dàxuéshì* 資政殿大學士 under Lǐzōng. Through his *Dàxué yǎnyì* 大學衍義 (presented to the throne 1229) — a vast topical compendium of Confucian political-and-ethical doctrine grounded in the *Dàxué* — he made the post-Zhū-Xī *Dàoxué* programme operative as imperial-pedagogical doctrine. The *Sòngshǐ* (juan 437) gives him a substantial biography in the *Rúlín zhuàn*.
