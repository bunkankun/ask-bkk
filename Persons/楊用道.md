**Yáng Yòngdào** 楊用道 (*fl.* 金, mid-12th c.), Jīn-period physician and author of the third stratum of the ***Zhǒuhòu bèijí fāng*** 肘後備急方 ([[KR3e0010]]) — the ***Fù guǎng Zhǒuhòu fāng*** 附廣肘後方, which appends to Gé Hóng 葛洪's original Jìn-period text and Táo Hóngjǐng 陶弘景's Liáng-period supplement an additional layer of prescriptions drawn from Táng Shènwēi 唐慎微's ***Zhèng lèi běncǎo*** 證類本草, distributed under each symptom-pattern of the *Zhǒuhòu*. His additions are marked in the SKQS edition with an explicit *Fù fāng* 附方 header. Beyond his work on this text he has no transmitted biographical record.
