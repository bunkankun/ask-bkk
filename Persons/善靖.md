# Shàn-jìng 善靖

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[妙倫]] Duàn-qiáo Miào-lún (1201–1261); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0327]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A001346).
