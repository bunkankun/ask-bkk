# Shànjìng 善靖

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[妙倫]] Duànqiáo Miàolún (1201–1261); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0327]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A001346).
