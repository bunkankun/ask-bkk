Saeki Jōin 佐伯定胤 (Sino-Japanese reading: Zuǒbó Dìngyìn; 1867–1952) was a prominent Hossō 法相 (Yogācāra / Faxiang) master and Buddhist scholar of the late Meiji, Taishō, and early Shōwa eras in Japan. He served as abbot of Hōryū-ji 法隆寺 and was one of the chief figures in the modern Hossō revival, lecturing extensively on [[玄奘]]-school doctrine and editing biographical source-material on the patriarchs of the Chinese and Japanese Hossō tradition.

He co-edited with [[中野達慧]] (Nakano Tatsue) the *Xuánzàng sānzàng shīzī zhuàn cóngshū* ([[KR6r0048]]), a collected biographical compendium on Xuánzàng and his disciples preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing*.
