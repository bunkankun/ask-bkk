# Liángjià 良价

Dòngshān Liángjià 洞山良价 (807–869), Táng-period Chán master and one of the founding figures of the Cáodòng 曹洞 school. His Dharma name combined with that of his disciple Cáoshān Běnjì 曹山本寂 gave the school its name.
