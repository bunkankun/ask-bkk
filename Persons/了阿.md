# Ryōa Shōnin 了阿上人

Kamakura-period Japanese monk who relayed the 120 doubt-points of Saizan-Fukakusa's [[顯意]] Kenni to [[證忍]] Shōnin, prompting Shōnin's reply preserved as [[KR6f0079]] *Kangyō-gi kemmon gutōshō* (1285).
