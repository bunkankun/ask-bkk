Takuka 託何, senior cleric of the **Yūzū-nenbutsu-shū** 融通念佛宗 in the **mid-Edo period**, active in the early 18th c. The colophon to his *Kiboku-ron* [[KR6t0392]] gives him as *former resident of Mt. Ōdai* (黄臺山前住). Precise lifedates are not securely attested in modern reference works. He worked in the orbit of [[融觀]] **Yūkan** (1649–1716) — the abbot who reorganized the Yūzū-nenbutsu school under official Tokugawa recognition (Genroku 2 / 1689) — and his *Kiboku-ron* is the principal systematic-doctrinal companion to Yūkan's foundational *Yūzū enmon-shō* [[KR6t0391]]. The DILA authority id is `A001248`.
