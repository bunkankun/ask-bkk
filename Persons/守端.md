# Shǒuduān 守端

Northern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, conventionally styled Báiyún Shǒuduān 白雲守端 after his abbacy at Báiyún shān 白雲山 in Shūzhōu. Dharma-heir of [[方會]] Yángqí Fānghuì, having silently memorised Fānghuì's lectures during the period in which note-taking was forbidden — the transcript he later set down became the basis of the *Yángqí Fānghuì héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0056]]). His own principal heir was Wǔzǔ [[法演]] Fǎyǎn, through whom the Yángqí line came to dominate later Chán.
