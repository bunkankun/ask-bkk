**Yì Fèngzhù** 易鳳翥 (*zì* Dān-shān 丹山; *hào* Wú-xuān 梧軒, 1769–1844, 清), Qīng literatus-physician of Cháng-shā Zhì-shān 長沙峙山 (Húnán). Born to a Hàn-lín-academician grandfather in *Qián-lóng jǐ-chǒu* (1769), completed the Five Classics by age 9 and the Thirteen Classics by 16; repeatedly recommended in the provincial examinations but never obtained *jìn-shì*. Declined the patronage of the *zǒng-dū* Zhào Dí-lóu 趙笛樓 and turned to medicine in his fifties. Author of [[KR3ek026]] *Wài-kē bèi-yào* 外科備要 (4 *juǎn*) — a provincial-Húnán *wài-kē* handbook compiled over more than thirty years and built on the imperial [[KR3ek009]] *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Wài-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué*; substantially complete in his lifetime but published only after his death. At age 70 (1838) he burned most of his other medical manuscripts, instructing his descendants not to publish his collected works; the *Wài-kē bèi-yào* survived through his nephew Yì Rùn-tán 易潤壇, who used the manuscript during military service in Jiāngxī during the Tài-píng wars and arranged its eventual publication. Died Dào-guāng 24, second month, twenty-fifth day (1844), aged 76 *suì*. Lifedates and biography preserved in the family biography 誥贈奉直大夫易公丹山府君家傳 by Yì Rùn-tán, embedded as front matter in the published work. Not in CBDB.
