Lóng Duìshān 龍兌山, late-Qīng / early-Republican Hénán physician, the orphaned grandson of [[龍繪堂]] Lóng Huìtáng and the named addressee of [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔnzǐ yī* 蠢子醫. Per the 1914 postface, he and his uncle Jūnyóu 君由 established their clinical reputations on the basis of the verse-mnemonic *Chǔnzǐ yī*, and Duìshān provided oral explanations of obscure passages that the Republican-era editors recorded as marginal notes. No standard biographical record located.
