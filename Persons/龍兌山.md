Lóng Duì-shān 龍兌山, late-Qīng / early-Republican Hé-nán physician, the orphaned grandson of [[龍繪堂]] Lóng Huì-táng and the named addressee of [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔn-zǐ yī* 蠢子醫. Per the 1914 postface, he and his uncle Jūn-yóu 君由 established their clinical reputations on the basis of the verse-mnemonic *Chǔn-zǐ yī*, and Duì-shān provided oral explanations of obscure passages that the Republican-era editors recorded as marginal notes. No standard biographical record located.
