# Fǎchéng 法澄

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xīsǒu Shàotán (d. by 1279); principal named editor of the extended *guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0323]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
