# Fǎ-chéng 法澄

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[紹曇]] Xī-sǒu Shào-tán (d. by 1279); principal named editor of the extended *guǎng-lù* ([[KR6q0323]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
