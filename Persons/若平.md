# Rúpíng 若平

Early-Southern-Sòng Chán disciple of Yuánwù [[克勤]] Kèqín; per [[耿延禧]] Gěng Yánxǐ's 1133 preface to the *Yuánwù Fóguǒ chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0059]]), the immediate editor charged with assembling his teacher's recorded sayings for carving and publication.
