Pān Yǒng-yīn 潘永因 (fl. 17th cent.), an early-Qīng *yí-mín* (Míng loyalist who refused Qīng office) of Jīn-shān 金山 / Sōng-jiāng prefecture (the source attribution varies in Qīng-period gazetteers); no CBDB match has been located. His principal works are the *Sòng bài lèi chāo* 宋稗類鈔 ([[KR3k0065]]) — the 36-*juǎn* early-Qīng category-anthology of Sòng *bǐ-jì* (note-form prose) and *shī-huà* (poetry-talk), first printed 1669 — and the *Dú shǐ jīn dài* 讀史津逮 (separately catalogued in the *Sìkù*), a historiographical guide co-edited with his elder brother. The *Sòng bài lèi chāo* is the model for Xú Kē's 1916 *Qīng bài lèi chāo*, which adopts the same title-form and topical organization for late-Qīng materials.
