Early-Qīng physician of Yínxiàn 鄞縣 (Níngbō), Zhèjiāng, *zì* Shèn'ān 慎庵, active in the Kāngxī–Yōngzhèng transition. Pupil of the Confucian-physician Shěn Guǒlú 沈果廬. Author of *Sì zhěn jué wēi* 四診抉微 ([[KR3eb003]], pub. 1723), an eight-juan synthesis of the classical four diagnostics. The text records two of his disciples by name (Wú Guān 吳冠 and Zhāng Shàoyuǎn 張紹遠). No CBDB record.
