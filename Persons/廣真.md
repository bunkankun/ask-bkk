# Guǎngzhēn 廣真 (Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn 吹萬廣真)

Late-Míng Sichuan LínjìYángqí Chán master and founder of the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派 sub-lineage. *Hào* Chuīwàn 吹萬 ("Blowing the Ten-Thousand"); also Jùyún Guǎngzhēn 聚雲廣真 for his Jùyúnsì 聚雲寺 abbacy. Lifedates 1582 – 1639/8/28 (Wànlì 10 – Chóngzhēn 12/7/30, age 58, per his tomb-inscription *Chuīwàn chánshī tǎ míng*).

One of the founders of the distinctive Sichuan Chán regional tradition through his Jùyúnpài sub-lineage. Surviving works in the Kanripo corpus include:

- *Yī guàn bié chuán* 一貫別傳 [[KR6q0235]] — his five-juan Chán-Confucian integrative treatise on the unity of Confucian and Chán transmission-doctrines.
- *Chuīwàn chánshī yǔ lù* (further volume in the catalog — [[KR6q0236]] and possibly subsequent entries).

Part of the late-Míng / early-Qīng Sichuan Chán tradition alongside [[本善|Dúfēng Běnshàn]], [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]], [[悟卓|Pòshí Wùzhuó]], and others.
