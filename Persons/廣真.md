# Guǎng-zhēn 廣真 (Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn 吹萬廣真)

Late-Míng Sichuan Línjì-Yángqí Chán master and founder of the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派 sub-lineage. *Hào* Chuī-wàn 吹萬 ("Blowing the Ten-Thousand"); also Jù-yún Guǎng-zhēn 聚雲廣真 for his Jù-yún-sì 聚雲寺 abbacy. Lifedates 1582 – 1639/8/28 (Wàn-lì 10 – Chóngzhēn 12/7/30, age 58, per his tomb-inscription *Chuī-wàn chánshī tǎ míng*).

One of the founders of the distinctive Sichuan Chán regional tradition through his Jù-yún-pài sub-lineage. Surviving works in the Kanripo corpus include:

- *Yī guàn bié chuán* 一貫別傳 [[KR6q0235]] — his five-juan Chán-Confucian integrative treatise on the unity of Confucian and Chán transmission-doctrines.
- *Chuī-wàn chánshī yǔ lù* (further volume in the catalog — [[KR6q0236]] and possibly subsequent entries).

Part of the late-Míng / early-Qīng Sichuan Chán tradition alongside [[本善|Dú-fēng Běn-shàn]], [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]], [[悟卓|Pò-shí Wù-zhuó]], and others.
