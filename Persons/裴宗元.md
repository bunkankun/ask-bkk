**Péi Zōngyuán** 裴宗元 (*fl.* late 11th c., Northern Sòng). Senior medical officer of the *Tài-yī jú* 太醫局 and one of the three principal compilers — with [[陳承]] and [[陳師文]] — of the 1078–1085 redaction of the *Tàipíng huìmín héjì jú-fāng* 太平惠民和劑局方 ([[KR3ed011]]). Held the title 庫部郎中 (Storehouse Bureau Director, a court-medical administrative rank). The biographical record is sparse; he is not in CBDB. The principal evidence for his career is his presence as co-compiler on the *Héjì júfāng* redaction.
