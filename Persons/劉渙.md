# Liú Huàn 劉渙 (1000–1080)

*Zì* Níng-zhī 凝之, *hào* Xī-jiàn xiānshēng 西澗先生 ("Master of West Stream"). Native of Yún-zhōu 筠州 (modern Gāo'ān, Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Tiān-shèng* 8 (1030); served as **Magistrate of Yǐng-shàng** 潁上 (in modern Ānhuī). Retired as *Tài-zǐ zhōng-yǔn* 太子中允 and lived as a recluse on Mt. Lú (Lúshān), where he composed his *Xī-jiàn shī-jí*. He was famously visited by Sū Shì in his last decade and praised in Sū's verse. Father of **Liú Shù** [[劉恕]] (1032–1078), Sī-mǎ Guāng's principal collaborator on the *Zī-zhì tōng-jiàn*; grandfather of **Liú Xī-zhòng** 劉羲仲. The catalog meta gives Liú Huàn's dates as 1000–1064; CBDB and Wikidata give 1000–1080 (Sū Shì's *huáng-lóng dào-rén* visit in 1080 is the latest event), which is followed here. The principal preserved work of his is in the *Sān Liú jiā jí* [[KR4h0030]].
