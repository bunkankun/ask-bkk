# Liú Huàn 劉渙 (1000–1080)

*Zì* Níngzhī 凝之, *hào* Xījiàn xiānshēng 西澗先生 ("Master of West Stream"). Native of Yúnzhōu 筠州 (modern Gāo'ān, Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Tiānshèng* 8 (1030); served as **Magistrate of Yǐngshàng** 潁上 (in modern Ānhuī). Retired as *Tàizǐ zhōngyǔn* 太子中允 and lived as a recluse on Mt. Lú (Lúshān), where he composed his *Xījiàn shījí*. He was famously visited by Sū Shì in his last decade and praised in Sū's verse. Father of **Liú Shù** [[劉恕]] (1032–1078), Sīmǎ Guāng's principal collaborator on the *Zīzhì tōngjiàn*; grandfather of **Liú Xīzhòng** 劉羲仲. The catalog meta gives Liú Huàn's dates as 1000–1064; CBDB and Wikidata give 1000–1080 (Sū Shì's *huánglóng dàorén* visit in 1080 is the latest event), which is followed here. The principal preserved work of his is in the *Sān Liú jiā jí* [[KR4h0030]].
