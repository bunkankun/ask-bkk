# Jǐng-yuán 景元

Southern-Sòng Chán *mén-rén shì-zhě* 門人侍者 of [[了慧]] Xī-yán Liǎo-huì (1198–1262); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0324]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Northern-Sòng Yúnmén-school Fú-róng Jǐng-yuán 佛眼清遠 line monk of the same name.
