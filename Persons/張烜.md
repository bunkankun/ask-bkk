Zhāng Xuān 張烜 (fl. early 8th c.; CBDB id 93491), Táng *Jíxián diàn* 集賢殿 academician. Named in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* among the seven compilers of Xú Jiān's *Chūxué jì* 初學記 ([[KR3k0006]]) under Xuánzōng's commission of Kāiyuán 13 (725). No independent biography in the Two Táng Histories.
