Southern-Sòng *Yìxué* compiler, author of *Jí zhuàn jīng yì* 集傳精義 — a now-lost collation-commentary on the *Yì*. The *Sìkù* editors verify the reconstructed *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* text of [[司馬光]] Sīmǎ Guāng's *Wēngōng Yì shuō* ([[KR1a0013]]) against citations preserved in Chén Yǒuwén's *Jí zhuàn jīng yì*, [[馮椅]] Féng Yǐ's *Yìxué*, and [[胡一桂]] Hú Yīguì's *Huìtōng*.
