**Wēn Jiào** 溫嶠 (288–329, *zì* Tàizhēn 太真), Eastern Jìn general and statesman, native of Qíxiàn 祁縣 in Tàiyuán. He is referenced here as the protagonist of the *Jìn shū* 晉書 anecdote in which he lights a rhinoceros-horn torch (*ránxī* 燃犀) in the Niúzhǔ 牛渚 rapids to see the underwater monsters — the literary topos that names [[許志]] Xǔ Zhì's *Huòluàn ránxī shuō* ([[KR3eg042]]).
