Mínglín 明林, *hào* **Xiùyě** 秀野. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānmù / Qírán Chāozhì** [[超智]] 天目超智 (b. 1626) in the Línyě → Zìxián → Tiānmù sub-line. Lifedates not preserved.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0495]] 《奇然智禪師語錄》 — co-compiled with **Míngcàn** [[明燦]] his master Chāozhì's *yǔlù*.
- [[KR6q0496]] 《秀野林禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B357 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zuìzhèng** [[最正]] *děng biān* (whose own *yǔlù* is at [[KR6q0497]]).
