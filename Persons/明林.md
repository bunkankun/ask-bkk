Míng-lín 明林, *hào* **Xiù-yě** 秀野. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-mù / Qí-rán Chāo-zhì** [[超智]] 天目超智 (b. 1626) in the Lín-yě → Zì-xián → Tiān-mù sub-line. Lifedates not preserved.

**Works.**

- [[KR6q0496]] 《秀野林禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B357 — his own *yǔ-lù*, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zuì-zhèng** [[最正]] *děng biān* (whose own *yǔ-lù* is at [[KR6q0497]]).
