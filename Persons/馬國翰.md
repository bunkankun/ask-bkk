# Mǎ Guóhàn 馬國翰

*Zì* Cíxī 詞溪 (also 竹吾 Zhúwú). Qīng 清-period *kǎozhèng* 考證 scholar and *jíyì* 輯佚 compiler, native of Lìchéng 歷城 in Shāndōng. *Jìnshì* of 1832; held a succession of prefectural posts in Shǎnxī. His monumental *Yùhán shānfáng jíyì shū* 玉函山房輯佚書 — 594 reconstructed titles in roughly 700 *juàn*, compiled and printed posthumously (final form 1883) — is the most ambitious single Qīng *jíyì* compilation: it gathers fragments of lost canonical, philosophical, and historical works from quotations preserved in Táng *lèishū* 類書, *zhèngyì* 正義 sub-commentaries, and later citation collections. The *Yì* section alone reconstructs lost commentaries by [[卜商]] Bǔ Shāng (Zǐxià), [[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ, [[京房]] Jīng Fáng, [[陸績]] Lù Jì, and many other Hàn and post-Hàn masters.
