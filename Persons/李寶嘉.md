Lǐ Bǎojiā 李寶嘉, pen name Lǐ Bóyuán 李伯元, was born in 1867 in Wǔjìn 武進, Jiāngsū province, and died in 1906 (CBDB id 77664; dates confirmed by *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú nián biǎo* 《清代人物生卒年表》 #8520). He was a prolific journalist, editor, and fiction writer active in the late Qīng Shanghai literary world. After an unsuccessful attempt at the civil service examinations, he moved to Shanghai and became a major figure in the new commercial press. He founded *Shìjiè Fánhuá Bào* 世界繁華報 and edited *Xiù Xiàng Xiǎoshuō* 繡像小說, both instrumental in the development of the Shanghai fiction industry. He is best known as the author of *Guānchǎng Xiànxíng Jì* 官場現形記 ([[KR4k0119]]), one of the four canonical "exposé novels" (*qiǎnzé xiǎoshuō* 譴責小說) of the late Qīng. Wilkinson's *Chinese History: A New Manual* places him among the Shanghai settlement literati contributing to *Diǎnshí Zhāi Huàbào* 點石齋畫報 and the *Shēnbào* press.
