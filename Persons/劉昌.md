Mid-Míng official and anthologist. **CBDB id 132763, dates 1424–1480**. *Zì* **Qīnmó** 欽謨; native of **Wúxiàn** 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of **Zhèngtǒng *yǐchǒu* (1445)**. Served as **Hénán *tíxué fùshǐ*** (Vice Education Commissioner of Hénán) — during which tenure he compiled the **[[KR4h0101]]** *Zhōngzhōu míngxián wénbiǎo* 中州名賢文表 (30 *juǎn*), an anthology of six Yuán-era Central-Plain masters (Xǔ Héng, Yáo Suì, Mǎ Zǔcháng, Xǔ Yǒurén, Wáng Yún, FùZhūlǐ Chōng). Later promoted to **Guǎngdōng *cānzhèng***. His own preface mentions further *wàijí*, *zhèngjí*, *zájí* — none transmitted. The *Wénbiǎo* was reprinted in **Kāngxī 45 (1706)** by the celebrated Qīng official-bibliophile **Sòng Luò** 宋犖 with the engraver **Wāng Lìmíng** 汪立名 of Qiántáng — the recension that became the SKQS source.

Distinct from CBDB ids 20263 (id only), 132763 (Wúxiàn man 1424–1480 — the present), 201888 (1484– ), 145858 (Táng man 736–800), 60234 (?–1670). The Wúxiàn man is unambiguously the YuánMíng anthologist.
