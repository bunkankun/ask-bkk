Mid-Míng official and anthologist. **CBDB id 132763, dates 1424–1480**. *Zì* **Qīn-mó** 欽謨; native of **Wú-xiàn** 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of **Zhèng-tǒng *yǐ-chǒu* (1445)**. Served as **Hé-nán *tí-xué fù-shǐ*** (Vice Education Commissioner of Hé-nán) — during which tenure he compiled the **[[KR4h0101]]** *Zhōng-zhōu míng-xián wén-biǎo* 中州名賢文表 (30 *juǎn*), an anthology of six Yuán-era Central-Plain masters (Xǔ Héng, Yáo Suì, Mǎ Zǔ-cháng, Xǔ Yǒu-rén, Wáng Yún, Fù-Zhū-lǐ Chōng). Later promoted to **Guǎng-dōng *cān-zhèng***. His own preface mentions further *wài-jí*, *zhèng-jí*, *zá-jí* — none transmitted. The *Wén-biǎo* was reprinted in **Kāng-xī 45 (1706)** by the celebrated Qīng official-bibliophile **Sòng Luò** 宋犖 with the engraver **Wāng Lì-míng** 汪立名 of Qián-táng — the recension that became the SKQS source.

Distinct from CBDB ids 20263 (id only), 132763 (Wú-xiàn man 1424–1480 — the present), 201888 (1484– ), 145858 (Táng man 736–800), 60234 (?–1670). The Wú-xiàn man is unambiguously the Yuán-Míng anthologist.
