**Qīngxī Dàorén** 清溪道人 is a late-Míng pen name, identity unidentified. The author of [[KR4k0041]] *Chánzhēn Yìshǐ* 禪真逸史 (40 *huí*) and [[KR4k0042]] *Chánzhēn Hòushǐ* 禪真後史 (53 *huí*), both vernacular fiction novels with a syncretic Chan-Buddhist and Daoist-heroic ideology. Based on the prefaces and publication context, the author was active in the Wànlì to Chóngzhēn eras (ca. 1590–1644). No biographical data has been established.
