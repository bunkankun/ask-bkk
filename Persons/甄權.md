# 甄權 Zhēn Quán

**Zhēn Quán** 甄權 (541–643), Suí-Táng acupuncturist of Xǔzhōu 許州; brother of [[甄立言]] Zhēn Lìyán. Compiled a *Míngtáng rénxíng tú* 明堂人形圖 (now lost) on acupoint locations and clinical indications, which served as the basis for the Táng imperial acupoint atlas. Standard biography in *Jiùtángshū* and *Xīntángshū*.
