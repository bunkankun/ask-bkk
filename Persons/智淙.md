Zhì-zōng 智淙 (also written 智琮 in 《西河文集》 and DILA), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Liáng-tíng Jìng-tǐng** [[淨挺]] 俍亭淨挺 (1615–1684). One of Jìng-tǐng's thirty named heirs (per the *tǎ-zhì-míng* in *Xī-hé wén-jí* 冊 9). Lead compiler of the 18-juan 《雲溪俍亭挺禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0471]]), credited in the catalog meta as *Zhì-zōng děng biān* 智淙等編. No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
