**Lín Shìzhī** 林式之 (fl. mid-13th c.), *zì* **Zǐjìng** 子敬. A native of **Fú-qīng** 福清 (Fú-jiàn 福建), in the same clan-lineage as **Lín Xīyì** 林希逸 ([[林希逸]]), of whom he was a disciple and a younger second-cousin (*zài-cóng xiōng-zǐ* 再從兄子, per Lín Tóng's preface to [[KR4d0370]]).

**Career.** Held a post as **Magistrate-Recorder (*fǎ-cáo*-staff) of Pǔ-jūn** 莆軍法曹掾 in the same period when Lín Xīyì served at Xìng-huà 興化 / Pǔ. Later transferred to be **Vice-Prefect of Sān-yáng** 三陽通守 (modern Cháo-zhōu 潮州, Guǎngdōng), where in **Xián-chún 4 (1268)** he undertook to fund the cutting of his teacher's *xùjí* (continuation collection) — the present [[KR4d0370]] *Zhúxī Juànzhāi shíyī gǎo xùjí* in 30 *juàn*. Lín Tóng's preface explicitly records that Shìzhī had "in this office obtained a small income, and saving from his food-and-clothing supply, accumulating bit by bit, sought to fulfil his lifetime's unfulfilled wish" — that is, to support the printing of his teacher's writings.

**Role.** Lín Shìzhī is the **editorial compiler** of [[KR4d0370]]: the categorization into thirteen sections (poetry, miscellaneous prose, early works, *jì*, *xù*, *bá*, *sì-liù*, *shěng-tí shī*, *wǎn-shī*, *jì-wén*, *mù-zhì*, *xíng-zhuàng*, *xué-jì*) is his. He had earlier also overseen the cutting of the **last 10 *juàn* of Liú Kèzhuāng's** 劉克莊 *Hòu-cūn xiānshēng dà-quán-jí* 後村先生大全集 — his name appearing on the colophon of those *juàn* as **supervising-cutter**. Lín Shìzhī was therefore a recognized editorial-and-publishing figure in Fú-jiàn late-Sòng literary circles.

**Sources.** Lín Tóng's 1270 preface to [[KR4d0370]] is the principal contemporary source. CBDB person 11070 (with limited dating).
