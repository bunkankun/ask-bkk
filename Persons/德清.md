# Déqīng 德清

Sobriquet Hānshān 憨山 ("Foolish Mountain"). Late-Míng Chán-Huáyán-Pure-Land master, native of Quánjiāo 全椒 (Ānhuī). Lifedates 1546–1623. One of the *Wǎn Míng sì dàshī* 晚明四大師 ("Four Great Masters of the Late Míng"), alongside Yúnqī Zhūhóng 雲棲袾宏, Zǐbǎi Zhēnkě 紫柏真可, and Ǒuyì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭.

Restored Bǎolínsì 寶林寺 at Cáoxī 曹溪 — the site of the Sixth Patriarch Huìnéng's seat — and made it again a major Chán practice centre. Politically embattled: in 1595 he was prosecuted for unauthorised construction at Lǎoshān 嶗山 (his earlier base in Shāndōng), defrocked, and exiled to Léizhōu 雷州 in Guǎngdōng. Continued his teaching and writing throughout exile; partially rehabilitated in 1606.

Author of an immense corpus including the *Bāshíbā zǔ dàoyǐng zhuàn zàn* 八十八祖道影傳贊 ([[KR6q0045]]), commentaries on the *Lǎozǐ*, *Zhuāngzǐ*, *Lúnyǔ*, and *Zhōngyōng*, and Buddhist commentaries on the *Lèngyán jīng*, *Yuánjué jīng*, *Léngqié jīng*, and *Zhàolùn*. The standard English study is Sung-peng Hsu, *A Buddhist Leader in Ming China: The Life and Thought of Han-shan Te-ch'ing* (Pennsylvania State UP, 1979).
