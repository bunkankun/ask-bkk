# Xìnglěi 性磊

Early-Qīng Chán monk; supplementary editor (補輯) of the *NánSòng YuánMíng chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* 南宋元明禪林僧寶傳 ([[KR6q0042]]) under Zìróng 自融's principal authorship. Exact lifedates not transmitted.
