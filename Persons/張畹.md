Mid- to late-Qīng physician of Shàoxìng city (mod. Zhèjiāng), resident at the *Xǐmǎchí* 洗馬池 ("Horse-washing Pond") neighbourhood. Active clinically from c. 1840 to c. 1880. A literatus who turned to medicine as a young man; widely-read in the medical literature; reached the height of his clinical reputation in the *Tóngzhì* era. His son Shān xiānshēng 山先生 continued the family medical practice; his grandson Jīng-Qǐ 睛豈 preserved and brought to publication his manuscript clinical handbook and case-records as [[KR3er104]] *Yībìng jiǎnyào* 醫病簡要 (printed 1913 with a preface by Bāo Yuèhú 包越瑚). Not in CBDB.
