Mid- to late-Qīng physician of Shào-xìng city (mod. Zhè-jiāng), resident at the *Xǐ-mǎ-chí* 洗馬池 ("Horse-washing Pond") neighbourhood. Active clinically from c. 1840 to c. 1880. A literatus who turned to medicine as a young man; widely-read in the medical literature; reached the height of his clinical reputation in the *Tóng-zhì* era. His son Shān xiān-shēng 山先生 continued the family medical practice; his grandson Jīng-Qǐ 睛豈 preserved and brought to publication his manuscript clinical handbook and case-records as [[KR3er104]] *Yī-bìng jiǎn-yào* 醫病簡要 (printed 1913 with a preface by Bāo Yuè-hú 包越瑚). Not in CBDB.
