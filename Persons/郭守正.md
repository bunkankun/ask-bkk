Southern-Sòng *xiǎoxué* scholar, *zì* Zhèngjǐ 正己, *hào* Zǐyún shānmín 紫雲山民. *fl.* Lǐzōng era — known principally for two related rhyme-book projects of Jǐngdìng *jiǎzǐ* (1264). (1) The *Zēngxiū jiàozhèng yāyùn shìyí* [[KR1j0062]] — a re-augmentation of Ōuyáng Délóng's [[歐陽德隆]] *Yāyùn shìyí* of Shàodìng (1230), each *juàn* of which Guō added c. 1,000 *biànzhèng* corrections; this work alone is referred to in Yuán-Míng sources as *Zǐyún yùn* 紫雲韻 (after his *hào*) and is principally known through *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* citations. (2) An independent rhyme-book also titled *Zǐyún yùn*, separately catalogued in the *Sìkù*. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that the printed *Lǐbù yùnlüè* recensions later became conflated and that some prefaces and *tiáolì* were borrowed across editions. No CBDB entry.
