Southern-Sòng *xiǎo-xué* scholar, *zì* Zhèng-jǐ 正己, *hào* Zǐ-yún shān-mín 紫雲山民. *fl.* Lǐ-zōng era — known principally for two related rhyme-book projects of Jǐng-dìng *jiǎ-zǐ* (1264). (1) The *Zēng-xiū jiào-zhèng yā-yùn shì-yí* [[KR1j0062]] — a re-augmentation of Ōu-yáng Dé-lóng's [[歐陽德隆]] *Yā-yùn shì-yí* of Shào-dìng (1230), each *juàn* of which Guō added c. 1,000 *biàn-zhèng* corrections; this work alone is referred to in Yuán-Míng sources as *Zǐ-yún yùn* 紫雲韻 (after his *hào*) and is principally known through *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* citations. (2) An independent rhyme-book also titled *Zǐ-yún yùn*, separately catalogued in the *Sìkù*. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that the printed *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* recensions later became conflated and that some prefaces and *tiáo-lì* were borrowed across editions. No CBDB entry.
