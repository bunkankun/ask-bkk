Hóng Zīkuí 洪咨夔 (1176–1236), *zì* Shùn-yú 舜俞, *hào* Píng-zhāi 平齋, was a Southern Sòng official, remonstrator, and poet from Yú-qián 於潛 (Lín-ān prefecture, modern Líng'ān, Zhèjiāng). He passed the *jìn-shì* examination in *Jiā-tài* 2 (1202) — corrected by the *Sì-kù* editors against the *Sòngshǐ* "*Jiā-dìng* 2" reading on the basis of the cyclical *rén-xū* date in his own poetry. He served in a series of provincial and capital posts, became Censor (御史) and *cān-zhī zhèng-shì* 參知政事 under Lǐzōng, and is most celebrated for his unyielding remonstrances against the dominant chief councillor Shǐ Mí-yuǎn 史彌遠 — one of the few late-Sòng ministers willing to challenge Shǐ openly. His collected works are *Píngzhāi jí* 平齋集 [[KR4d0311]] (32 *juǎn*) and *Píngzhāi cí* 平齋詞 [[KR4j0046]] (1 *juǎn*); he also wrote a *Hóngshì Chūn-qiū shuō* 洪氏春秋說 [[KR1e0051]] (catalogued under his name with character variant 洪咨虁 in the krp index).
