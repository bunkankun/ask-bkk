Mid-Qīng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Shìsān** 示三. DILA Authority A001183. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the early-to-mid Qiánlóng era. Resident at the **Wǔyuán Jífúyuàn** 武源集福院.

In the Qiánlóng period, over four years, transcribed-and-supplemented (彙補 *huìbǔ*) SòngYuánMíng commentaries on Dìguān's *Sìjiào yí* into the comprehensive **Sìjiào yí zhù huìbǔ fǔhóng jì** 四教儀註彙補輔宏記 ([[KR6d0174]], X57n0980, 10 *juan*). The work was finalised in Kāngxī 44 (1705) — Xìngquán's own preface — but the most extensive editorial activity occurred in the Qiánlóng era. In the Jiāqìng era it was supplemented-and-corrected by the lay-disciple **Qián Yīān** 錢伊菴居士; in the Guāngxù era it was systematically arranged-and-printed by **Dìxián fǎshī** 諦閑法師.

Source: *Sìjiào yí zhù huìbǔ fǔhóng jì* *juan* 1; X57n0980; DILA A001183.
