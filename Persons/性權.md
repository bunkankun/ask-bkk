Mid-Qīng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Shì-sān** 示三. DILA Authority A001183. Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the early-to-mid Qiánlóng era. Resident at the **Wǔ-yuán Jí-fú-yuàn** 武源集福院.

In the Qiánlóng period, over four years, transcribed-and-supplemented (彙補 *huì-bǔ*) Sòng-Yuán-Míng commentaries on Dìguān's *Sì-jiào yí* into the comprehensive **Sì-jiào yí zhù huì-bǔ fǔ-hóng jì** 四教儀註彙補輔宏記 ([[KR6d0174]], X57n0980, 10 *juan*). The work was finalised in Kāngxī 44 (1705) — Xìng-quán's own preface — but the most extensive editorial activity occurred in the Qiánlóng era. In the Jiāqìng era it was supplemented-and-corrected by the lay-disciple **Qián Yī-ān** 錢伊菴居士; in the Guāngxù era it was systematically arranged-and-printed by **Dì-xián fǎshī** 諦閑法師.

Source: *Sì-jiào yí zhù huì-bǔ fǔ-hóng jì* *juan* 1; X57n0980; DILA A001183.
