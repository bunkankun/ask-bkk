Fǎdòng 法棟, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Fómǐn / Yìnxīn Jìnè** [[寂訥]] 印心佛敏寂訥 in the Cǎotáng / Zhàngxuě sub-line. Co-recorder with **Fǎtōng** [[法通]] of the 2-juan 《印心佛敏訥禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0509]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
