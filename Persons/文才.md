# Wén-cái 文才

Yuán-dynasty Chán *sì-fǎ mén-rén* 嗣法門人 of [[如砥]] Píng-shí Rú-dǐ (d. 1357); principal named co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Yuán-dynasty Huá-yán master Zhòng-chōng Wén-cái 仲仲文才 (1241–1302).
