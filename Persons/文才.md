# Wéncái 文才

Yuán-dynasty Chán *sìfǎ ménrén* 嗣法門人 of [[如砥]] Píngshí Rúdǐ (d. 1357); principal named co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Yuán-dynasty Huáyán master Zhòngchōng Wéncái 仲仲文才 (1241–1302).
