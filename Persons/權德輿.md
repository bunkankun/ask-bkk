**Quán Déyú** 權德輿 (761–818), *zì* Zài zhī 載之, was a mid-Táng statesman and prose-stylist, rising to Zǎi xiàng 宰相 (Chief Minister) under Táng Xiàn zōng. Native of Tiānshuǐ 天水 (Gān sù). Author of [[KR5d0076]] *Wú zūn shī zhuàn* 吳尊師傳 — a biography of the Daoist master [[吳筠]] Wú Yún.
