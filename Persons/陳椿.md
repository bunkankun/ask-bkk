Late-Yuán Salt-Bureau official; native of Tiāntái 天台 (Zhèjiāng). Active *fl.* 1334. Served as Salt-Bureau Director (*Yán-sī*) of the *Xiàshā-chǎng* 下砂場 salt-works near present-day Shànghǎi during the *Yuántǒng* era. Supplemented and completed the *Áobō tú* 熬波圖 ([[KR2m0045]])—the unique surviving Yuán-period illustrated manual on salt-manufacturing—building on an earlier draft by a former Director of the same office. Precise life-dates and biographical details not transmitted.
