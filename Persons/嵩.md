Sōng 嵩, *hào* **Ěrān** 耳庵 / **Xióngěrān** 熊耳菴, principal LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tiěméi Sānbāzhǎng Huìlì** [[慧麗]] 鐵眉三巴掌慧麗 (1586–1650) and bowl-and-robe inheritor at Tiěméi's death. *Shìshòu* **46** (per the closing 附記 of [[KR6q0453]]); precise lifedates not given but his career spans **1649** (first abbacy at **Guānyīnshān Xióngěrān** 觀音山熊耳庵 in 施州衛 / Húběi) through the early 1650s. Native of **Fēngdūxiàn** 酆都縣 in **Chóngqìngfǔ** 重慶府 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Máo** 毛 — same surname as **Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo** [[了]] (compare [[KR6q0447]]). Tonsured young at **Sānxìngān** 三姓庵 in his home county; described as *chōngdàn guǎ yánxiào, duō círěn* 沖澹寡言笑多慈忍.

**Formation.** Initial training in *jiàoshèng* 教乘 (sūtra-doctrinal study); converted to Chán on hearing of **Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]] at Jùyúnsì, *cānsuí duō zǎi* 參隨多載 — many years residence under Guǎngzhēn until the latter's 1639 death. Continued under **Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] at **Píngdū** 平都 *yǒu nián* 有年; finally consulted **Sānbāzhǎng Huìlì** at Bǎofēng / Shīwèi, who took him in as *jìshì* 記室 (secretary) and *shǒubǎn* 上板 (head-monk on the platform). At Tiěméi's death (1650), Ěrān received the *yīfú* 衣拂 (robe-and-whisk) transmission.

**Four abbacies.** **Fúhǔān** 伏虎庵 — **Xióngěrān** 熊耳菴 (Guānyīnshān, Shīzhōuwèi, **1649/10/15** entry per Tiěméi's testament with the lay-patrons **Chén Huàyǔ** 陳化宇 and *yuànzhǔ* **Wénzhǐ** 文旨) — **Huílóng Chányuàn** 迴龍禪院 (**Jīnfúshān**, **1650/9/15** entry with the *xuānwèisī qīzhǔ* **Rǎn Sīróng** 冉斯榮 of **Zhōnglù** 忠路) — **Tiānníng** 天寧 (final seat). Died at Tiānníng with the testamentary instruction to forward Tiěméi's *yīfá* 衣法 onward to **Sānshān** 三山 (= **Sānshān Dēnglái** [[燈來]]). Cremation-stūpa at **Tiānníng Wǔfēngshān zhī yáng** 天寧五峰山之陽.

**Work.** [[KR6q0453]] 《耳庵嵩禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B243, compiled by **Xìngkǎi** [[性愷]] with front-preface by **Xú Shàn** [[徐善]] of Zuìlǐ.
