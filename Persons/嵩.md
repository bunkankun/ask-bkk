Sōng 嵩, *hào* **Ěr-ān** 耳庵 / **Xióng-ěr-ān** 熊耳菴, principal Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiě-méi Sān-bā-zhǎng Huì-lì** [[慧麗]] 鐵眉三巴掌慧麗 (1586–1650) and bowl-and-robe inheritor at Tiě-méi's death. *Shì-shòu* **46** (per the closing 附記 of [[KR6q0453]]); precise lifedates not given but his career spans **1649** (first abbacy at **Guān-yīn-shān Xióng-ěr-ān** 觀音山熊耳庵 in 施州衛 / Hú-běi) through the early 1650s. Native of **Fēng-dū-xiàn** 酆都縣 in **Chóng-qìng-fǔ** 重慶府 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Máo** 毛 — same surname as **Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo** [[了]] (compare [[KR6q0447]]). Tonsured young at **Sān-xìng-ān** 三姓庵 in his home county; described as *chōng-dàn guǎ yán-xiào, duō cí-rěn* 沖澹寡言笑多慈忍.

**Formation.** Initial training in *jiào-shèng* 教乘 (sūtra-doctrinal study); converted to Chán on hearing of **Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]] at Jù-yún-sì, *cān-suí duō zǎi* 參隨多載 — many years residence under Guǎng-zhēn until the latter's 1639 death. Continued under **Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] at **Píng-dū** 平都 *yǒu nián* 有年; finally consulted **Sān-bā-zhǎng Huì-lì** at Bǎo-fēng / Shī-wèi, who took him in as *jì-shì* 記室 (secretary) and *shǒu-bǎn* 上板 (head-monk on the platform). At Tiě-méi's death (1650), Ěr-ān received the *yī-fú* 衣拂 (robe-and-whisk) transmission.

**Four abbacies.** **Fú-hǔ-ān** 伏虎庵 — **Xióng-ěr-ān** 熊耳菴 (Guān-yīn-shān, Shī-zhōu-wèi, **1649/10/15** entry per Tiě-méi's testament with the lay-patrons **Chén Huà-yǔ** 陳化宇 and *yuàn-zhǔ* **Wén-zhǐ** 文旨) — **Huí-lóng Chán-yuàn** 迴龍禪院 (**Jīn-fú-shān**, **1650/9/15** entry with the *xuān-wèi-sī qī-zhǔ* **Rǎn Sī-róng** 冉斯榮 of **Zhōng-lù** 忠路) — **Tiān-níng** 天寧 (final seat). Died at Tiān-níng with the testamentary instruction to forward Tiě-méi's *yī-fá* 衣法 onward to **Sān-shān** 三山 (= **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]]). Cremation-stūpa at **Tiān-níng Wǔ-fēng-shān zhī yáng** 天寧五峰山之陽.

**Work.** [[KR6q0453]] 《耳庵嵩禪師語錄》 1 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B243, compiled by **Xìng-kǎi** [[性愷]] with front-preface by **Xú Shàn** [[徐善]] of Zuì-lǐ.
