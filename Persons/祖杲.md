# Zǔ-gǎo 祖杲

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[得度]]) of *juan* 4 (*jǔ-gǔ* 舉古) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). The *Xíng-dào-jì* by Sòng Lián credits Zǔ-gǎo specifically with having requested that Qīng-yù compile a continuation of Xuě-dòu's *niān-gǔ* 拈古 — a request Qīng-yù initially refused but later fulfilled with a set of one hundred cases. Lifedates unrecorded.
