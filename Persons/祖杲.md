# Zǔgǎo 祖杲

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[得度]]) of *juan* 4 (*jǔgǔ* 舉古) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). The *Xíngdàojì* by Sòng Lián credits Zǔgǎo specifically with having requested that Qīngyù compile a continuation of Xuědòu's *niāngǔ* 拈古 — a request Qīngyù initially refused but later fulfilled with a set of one hundred cases. Lifedates unrecorded.
