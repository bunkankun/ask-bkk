# Xú Wúdǎng 徐無黨

Native of Yǒng-kāng 永康 (in modern Zhèjiāng). Floruit mid-Northern Sòng, *Bǎo-yuán* through *Huáng-yòu* eras (1030s–1050s). *Jìnshì* of *Qìng-lì* 6 (1046). CBDB id 46326 (no transmitted lifedates).

A younger scholar in Ōuyáng Xiū's intellectual circle. His sole transmitted work is the *zhù* (commentary) appended to Ōuyáng Xiū's *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* (KR2a0030); composed during Ōuyáng Xiū's lifetime as a working aid to his master's text. The Sìkù compilers find the commentary shallow but preserve it because of its long association with the *Xīn Wǔdài shǐ* tradition. No biography in the *Sòngshǐ*; mentioned only briefly in the *Sòng* bibliographic catalogues.
