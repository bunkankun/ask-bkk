# Cúnkōng 存空

Mid-seventeenth-century Buddhist abbot (*fǎzhǔ* 法主) at Kōfuku-ji 興福寺 in Nagasaki, Japan — the principal Sino-Japanese Buddhist contact point during the immediate pre-Ōbaku Míng-Qīng transition. Instigator and Japanese *kunten*-annotator of [[益證]] Yìzhèng Zìxīn's *Bōrě xīnjīng yīguàn shū* ([[KR6c0176]]), composed at Kōfuku-ji's west wing guest-residence in autumn 1648.
