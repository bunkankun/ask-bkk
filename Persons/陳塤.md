# Xí'ān Chén Xún 習菴陳塤

Southern Sòng scholar-official (style Xí'ān 習菴); composed the 1228 opening preface to [[KR6q0080]] *Wúmén guān*. Sometimes identified with [[陳垓]] Chén Gāi.
