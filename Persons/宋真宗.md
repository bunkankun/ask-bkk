# 宋真宗

Sòng Zhēnzōng 宋真宗 (personal name Zhào Héng 趙恆; 968–1022, r. 997–1022), third emperor of the Northern Sòng. He continued his father [[宋太宗]] Tàizōng's patronage of the imperial Translation Academy 譯經院 / 傳法院, sponsoring the translations of [[施護]] Shīhù (Dānapāla), [[法護]] Fǎhù, [[惟淨]] Wéijìng and others; also promoted the Daoist Lingbao 靈寶 tradition through the Tiānshū 天書 affair and the construction of the Yùqīng Zhāoyīng-gōng 玉清昭應宮.
