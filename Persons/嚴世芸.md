# 嚴世芸 Yán Shìyún

**Yán Shìyún** 嚴世芸 (b. 1940), contemporary Chinese historian of Chinese medicine and former president of the Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (上海中醫藥大學). Editor of the standard *Zhōngyī rénwù cídiǎn* 中醫人物詞典 (1988) and several major reference works on Sòng-period medical literature.
