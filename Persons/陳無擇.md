**Chén Wúzé** 陳無擇 (real name 陳言 Chén Yán, *zì* Wúzé, 1131–1189), Southern Sòng physician of Qīngtián 青田 (Zhèjiāng); author of *Sānyīn jí yī bìngzhèng fāng lùn* 三因極一病證方論 (1174) — the foundational *sānyīn* (three-causes) etiological treatise that classifies disease causation as *nèi yīn* (internal — the seven affects), *wài yīn* (external — the six excesses), and *bù nèi wài yīn* (neither — accident, dietary error, etc.). Cited by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng in [[KR3eh052]] on the Kāiyuán-coin standard for *dùliànghéng* 度量衡 conversion of HànTáng dose-equivalents.
