Mílè Púsà 彌勒菩薩 (Skt. **Maitreya** "the Friendly One") is the bodhisattva who in Mahāyāna cosmology dwells in the **Tuṣita** heaven awaiting his future descent as the next Buddha. The phonetic transliteration of *Maitreya* gives 彌勒; the translation is 慈氏 ([[慈氏菩薩]]). In Yogācāra doctrine, Maitreya is credited as the original revelation-source of the **Yogācārabhūmi** texts on the bodhisattva-path — including the **Pú-sà-jiè-běn** 菩薩戒本 ([[KR6k0121]], [[KR6k0123]]) on the bodhisattva-precepts and other texts in the *Yogācārabhūmi* corpus. The attribution is doctrinal-revelatory, with the texts mediated through the historical author **Asaṅga** 無著.
