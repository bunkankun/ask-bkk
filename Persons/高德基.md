Gāo Déjī 高德基 (*fl. Zhìzhèng*, mid-fourteenth century). Native of Píngjiāng 平江 (Sūzhōu). Late-Yuán official; held office as *Jiàndélù zǒngguǎn* (Prefectural Administrator of Jiàndélù, modern Jiàndé in Zhèjiāng). Author of the *Píngjiāng jìshì* 平江記事 ([[KR2k0123]]) — a late-Yuán *bǐjì* on Sūzhōu antiquities and folklore, composed during or after the *Zhìzhèng* era (after Yú Wénzhuàn's compilation of the three Yuán dynastic histories in 1344–1345). Otherwise undocumented; not in CBDB.
