Gāo Dé-jī 高德基 (*fl. Zhì-zhèng*, mid-fourteenth century). Native of Píng-jiāng 平江 (Sū-zhōu). Late-Yuán official; held office as *Jiàn-dé-lù zǒng-guǎn* (Prefectural Administrator of Jiàn-dé-lù, modern Jiàn-dé in Zhèjiāng). Author of the *Píngjiāng jìshì* 平江記事 ([[KR2k0123]]) — a late-Yuán *bǐ-jì* on Sū-zhōu antiquities and folklore, composed during or after the *Zhì-zhèng* era (after Yú Wén-zhuàn's compilation of the three Yuán dynastic histories in 1344–1345). Otherwise undocumented; not in CBDB.
