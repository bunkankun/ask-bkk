Běntǎn 本僼, *hào* **Yuǎnān** 遠菴 / **Wùān** 兀菴. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Mùchén Dàomín** [[道忞]] 木陳道忞 (1596–1674). Born **1622**; died **康熙壬戌 4** = **1682-05-07/06-05**, *shìshòu* **61**. Native of **Kūnshān** 昆山 (Sūzhōu prefecture); lay surname **Lù** 陸.

**Formation.** Tonsured at Tiāntóng. Studied under **Mùyún [Tōng-]mén** 牧雲門 first; final transmission from **Hóngjué Mín** = Mùchén Dàomín.

**Five abbacies.** **Chángshā Shéndǐng** 長沙神鼎; succeeded teacher at **Tiāntóng** 天童; subsequent **Ruìyán** 瑞巖 (Níngbō, the title-form 瑞巖遠菴), **Píngyáng** 平陽, **Báiyún** 白雲.

**Heir.** **Yīkuí Yuánkuí** [[元揆]] 一揆元揆 (cf. [[KR6q0513]]).

**Cross-reference.** Same Yuǎnān héshàng who organized the publication of [[KR6q0481]] 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 (his dharma-brother Yúnsǒu Běnzhù's *yǔlù*) at Ruìyánsì.

**Work.** [[KR6q0511]] 《遠菴僼禪師語錄》 16 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B386, compiled by *fǎsì* **Yuánshì** [[元視]] *děng biān*. Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 73; DILA A009287.
