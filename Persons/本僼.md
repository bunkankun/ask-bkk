Běn-tǎn 本僼, *hào* **Yuǎn-ān** 遠菴 / **Wù-ān** 兀菴. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mù-chén Dào-mín** [[道忞]] 木陳道忞 (1596–1674). Born **1622**; died **康熙壬戌 4** = **1682-05-07/06-05**, *shì-shòu* **61**. Native of **Kūn-shān** 昆山 (Sū-zhōu prefecture); lay surname **Lù** 陸.

**Formation.** Tonsured at Tiān-tóng. Studied under **Mù-yún [Tōng-]mén** 牧雲門 first; final transmission from **Hóng-jué Mín** = Mù-chén Dào-mín.

**Five abbacies.** **Cháng-shā Shén-dǐng** 長沙神鼎; succeeded teacher at **Tiān-tóng** 天童; subsequent **Ruì-yán** 瑞巖 (Níng-bō, the title-form 瑞巖遠菴), **Píng-yáng** 平陽, **Bái-yún** 白雲.

**Heir.** **Yī-kuí Yuán-kuí** [[元揆]] 一揆元揆 (cf. [[KR6q0513]]).

**Cross-reference.** Same Yuǎn-ān héshàng who organized the publication of [[KR6q0481]] 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 (his dharma-brother Yún-sǒu Běn-zhù's *yǔ-lù*) at Ruì-yán-sì.

**Work.** [[KR6q0511]] 《遠菴僼禪師語錄》 16 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B386, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Yuán-shì** [[元視]] *děng biān*. Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 73; DILA A009287.
