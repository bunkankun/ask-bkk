# Wáng Bówú 王伯無

Late-Wànlì lay-Buddhist (*jūshì*) of **Línhǎi** 臨海 (modern Zhèjiāng), patron of [[傳燈]] Yōuxī Chuándēng at the Yōuxī Gāomíngsì 幽溪高明寺 on Mt. Tiāntái; supported the completion of Chuándēng's *Lèngyán jīng yuántōng shū qiánmáo* ([[KR6j0705]]).
