Guō Zé 郭則 (pen name Yúncōng Huāyǐn 雲淙花隱; fl. 1819 or 1879) was a Qīng-dynasty author of *Hónglóu Zhēnmèng* 紅樓真夢, a 64-chapter sequel to *Hónglóu Mèng* 紅樓夢. His studio was named *Yìpǔ Zhúxuān* 逸圃竹軒. His preface demonstrates substantial literary learning, written in ornate parallel prose. No further biographical details are known. CBDB id 150978 returns no dates or other identifying information.
