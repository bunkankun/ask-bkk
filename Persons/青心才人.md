**Qīngxīn Cáirén** 青心才人 ("Talented Person of Pure Heart") is the pseudonymous author of *Jīn Yún Qiào Zhuàn* 金雲翹傳 ([[KR4k0156]]). The work is generally dated to the Shùnzhì–Kāngxī transitional period (c. 1641–1710) on internal and stylistic grounds, making Qīngxīn Cáirén a probable late-Míng or early-Qīng author. No independent biographical record has been found. The pseudonym is of the standard *cáirén* 才人 type common in late-Míng and early-Qīng popular fiction. No confident CBDB match exists.
