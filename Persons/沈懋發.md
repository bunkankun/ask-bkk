Mid-Qián-lóng-era Qīng physician, regional origin uncertain. Sole securely-attested work is [[KR3er093]] *Kuàicán piān* 鱠殘篇 (c. 1777), a one-*juǎn* pharmacological-clinical miscellany that defends a strict *zīyīn* 滋陰 (yin-supplementing) position against the contemporary vogue for stimulant *bǔhuǒ* 補火 (fire-supplementing) formulae. Not in CBDB. The text survives in Japanese-imprint recension repatriated through the *Hǎiwài huíliú zhōngyī gǔjí cóngshū* 海外回流中醫古籍叢書 series.
