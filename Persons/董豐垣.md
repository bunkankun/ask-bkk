Dǒng Fēngyuán 董豐垣 (*fl.* 1751; lifedates not securely known), *zì* Jútīng 菊町. Native of Wūchéng 烏程 in Húzhōu 湖州 (Zhèjiāng). Qiánlóng *xīnwèi* 16 (1751) *jìnshì*; served as magistrate of Dōngliú 東流 county in An-qìng 安慶 prefecture (An-huī). Compiler of the *Shí xiǎo biān* 識小編 ([[KR3j0079]]), a 2-juan mid-Qiánlóng-period evidential miscellany of 24 ritual-classics essays. Not in CBDB.
