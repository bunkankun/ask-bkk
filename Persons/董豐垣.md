Dǒng Fēngyuán 董豐垣 (*fl.* 1751; lifedates not securely known), *zì* Jú-tīng 菊町. Native of Wū-chéng 烏程 in Hú-zhōu 湖州 (Zhèjiāng). Qiánlóng *xīn-wèi* 16 (1751) *jìnshì*; served as magistrate of Dōng-liú 東流 county in An-qìng 安慶 prefecture (An-huī). Compiler of the *Shí xiǎo biān* 識小編 ([[KR3j0079]]), a 2-juan mid-Qiánlóng-period evidential miscellany of 24 ritual-classics essays. Not in CBDB.
