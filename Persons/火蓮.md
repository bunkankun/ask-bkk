# Huǒlián 火蓮

Early-Qīng Chán practitioner; compiler and printer (*jízǐ* 集梓) of the *Ǎn hēidòu jí* 揞黑豆集 ([[KR6q0025]]) under Xīnyuán 心圓's *niānbié*. Likely a lay disciple. Exact lifedates not transmitted.
