# Zhìrén 智仁

Táng Vinaya master of *Jīngzhào Guāngmíngsì* 京兆光明寺 (Cháng'ān). The first of the *liùshí jiā* 六十家 ("sixty houses") of Táng *Xíngshì chāo* sub-commentators, according to [[慧顯]] Huìxiǎn's catalogue ([[KR6k0170]] *Xíngshì chāo zhūjiā jì biāomù*), which records his ten-juan *Xíngshì chāo jì* (title not preserved). The work itself is lost; Zhìrén is reckoned the inaugural sub-commentator from whom the entire *liùshí jiā* tradition was counted outward.
