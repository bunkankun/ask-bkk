# Zhìshèng 智勝

Early-Míng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[普莊]] Dāiān Pǔzhuāng (1347–1403); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0351]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
