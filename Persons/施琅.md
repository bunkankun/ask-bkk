Early-Qīng admiral and architect of the 1683 conquest of Táiwān; *zì* Zūn-hóu 尊侯, *hào* Zhuó-gōng 琢公; native of Jìnjiāng 晉江, Quánzhōu, Fújiàn. Originally a subordinate of Zhèng Zhīlóng 鄭芝龍 and later Zhèng Chénggōng 鄭成功 (Koxinga), Shī Láng surrendered to the Qīng in 1646 after a quarrel with Zhèng that ended in the execution of his father and brother. He served the Qīng court as Fújiàn *Shuǐshī tídū* 福建水師提督 and led the imperial fleet that defeated the Zhèng régime at the battle of Pénghú 澎湖 (1683), bringing Táiwān under Qīng administration for the first time. He was enfeoffed *Jìnghǎi-hóu* 靖海侯 (Marquis Pacifier-of-the-Seas). CBDB (no. 56824) gives 1621–1696. The form 施烺 in the *Sìkù tíyào* of [[KR2k0152]] is a typographical slip for 施琅 (preserved here as it stands in the Wényuāngé text).
