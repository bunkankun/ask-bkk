Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Fǎchén** 法臣, sobriquet **Fúzōng** 扶宗 (also referenced as **Fúzōng Jìzhōng** 扶宗繼忠). DILA Authority A000924. Born 1012; died 6 November 1082, aged 71.

Major work: the **Fǎzhì yíbiān guānxīn èrbǎi wèn** 法智遺編觀心二百問 ([[KR6d0184]], T1935, 1 *juan*) — a compilation of **Sìmíng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮's two hundred *guānxīn* ("contemplating-the-mind") questions, edited by Jìzhōng. The work is one of the principal documentary records of Zhīlǐ's *shānjiā* doctrinal-meditative apparatus and demonstrates Jìzhōng's role as a major editor-transmitter of the Zhīlǐ corpus.

Sources: *Fózǔ tǒngjì* 佛祖統紀 (T2035, p. 217a24); DILA A000924; *Fóguāng* 6778.
