Mid-Qiánlóng Manchu noble, statesman, and *fùmǎ* 駙馬 (imperial son-in-law); of the Fùchá 富察 clan, son of Fùhéng 傅恒 and brother of Fùcháng'ān 福長安. Married Qiánlóng's fourth daughter, Princess Héjiā 和嘉公主. Held office as *Bīngbù shàngshū* and *Lǐbù shàngshū*, *Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì*. Directed the supplementary editing (*校補*) of the *Huángcháo lǐqì túshì* 皇朝禮器圖式 ([[KR2m0036]]) in 1766, succeeding Yùnlù. Co-author of the *Qīndìng Mǎnzhōu yuánliú kǎo* 欽定滿洲源流考. Died young in 1784, aged 38. Standard reference: *Qīng huàjiā shīshǐ* and the *Mǎnzú jiànshǐ*.
