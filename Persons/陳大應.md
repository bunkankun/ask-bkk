Southern-Sòng official, grandson of [[陳瓘]] Chén Guàn. [[馮椅]] Féng Yǐ reports having met Chén Dàyīng and seen a fuller version of his grandfather's *Yì jiě* 易解 — more than one juan and substantially *guàbiàn* 卦變 ("hexagram-change") oriented, in the manner of [[朱震]] Zhū Zhèn ([[KR1a0024]]). That fuller text is now lost; only the compressed *Liǎozhāi Yì shuō* ([[KR1a0018]]) printed by his cousin [[陳正同]] Chén Zhèngtóng survives.
