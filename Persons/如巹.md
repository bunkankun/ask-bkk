Mì-ān Rújǐn 蔤菴如巹 (b. 1425, death year unrecorded), Míng-dynasty Línjì-school 臨濟宗 Yángqí-branch 楊岐派 Chán monk, dharma-heir of Kōnggǔ Jǐnglóng 空谷景隆 (1393–1470). Native of Jiāhé 嘉禾 (Jiāxīng 嘉興, Jiāngnán), lay surname Jiāng 姜. *Hào* Mì-ān 蔤菴.

Initially studied under Héng Zōngjì 衡宗繼 at the Zhēnrú sì 真如寺 in Jiāhé. At Jǐngtài 1 (1450), aged 26 *sui*, travelled to the Xiūjí shān 修吉山 near Hángzhōu to study under Kōnggǔ Jǐnglóng, under whose instruction he received the dharma-seal; subsequently returned to Jiāhé to serve as *zhùchí* 住持 of the Zhēnrú sì. After Kōnggǔ's death (1470) Rújǐn turned to Pure Land practice; later studied under Wúzhù 無住 and had a minor awakening hearing crows calling in a courtyard tree.

Two major works. First, the *Zī mén jǐng xùn xù jí* 緇門警訓續集 ("Supplementary Compilation of Warning Instructions for the Black-Robed"), completed at Jiāhé in Chénghuà 6 (1470), originally in 2 juan; preface by Kōnggǔ Jǐnglóng the same year, the final year of Kōnggǔ's life. The text subsequently expanded to its received 10-juan form ([[KR6q0100]] T48 n2023) by later editorial hands in the late Ming. Second, the *Chánzōng zhèngmài* 禪宗正脈 ("Orthodox Veins of the Chán Lineage") in 10 juan, completed in Hóngzhì 2 (1489), a major Ming Línjì lineage-compendium ([[KR6q0014]]).
