Late-Qīng / early-Republican Húnán medical *kǎojù* scholar and physician; *zì* Zhòngshān 仲山. Lifedates 1868–1934 are conventional. Graduate of the Yuèlù Academy 嶽麓書院 in Chángshā. Author of the *Sūn shì yī xué cóng shū* 孫氏醫學叢書, an integrated set of critical editions of the early-medieval medical canon: the *Sùwèn jiào yì* 素問校義, the *Língshū jiào yì* 靈樞校義, the *Nànjīng zhāng jù* 難經章句, the *Shānghán lùn zhāng jù* 傷寒論章句, [[KR3eb029]] *Mài jīng chāo* (1909), and a *Huáng dì wài jīng* 黃帝外經 reconstruction. Doctrinally one of the most rigorous late-Qīng applications of Qing *kǎojù* methodology to medicine. No CBDB record.
