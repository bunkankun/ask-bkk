# Gāo Chǔfāng 高楚芳

Yuán-period scholar and commercial publisher; native of *Línjiāng* 臨江 *Xīnyú* 新喻 (modern *Xīnyú* in central Jiāngxī). Floruit late 13th – early 14th century. His principal scholarly contribution is the editing and printing of the *Jí qiānjiā zhù Dù gōngbù shī jí* [[KR4c0018]] (= *Fēnmén jízhù Dù gōngbù shī*) — the consummation of the Sòng *qiānjiā zhù* 千家註 tradition for Dù Fǔ [[杜甫]], reorganized into a 20-*juǎn fēnmén* (genre-arranged) anthology format that became the standard Dù Fǔ commentary edition for Yuán-Míng *kējǔ* preparation. CBDB has no entry; no other works are securely attributed to him. He was the kind of late-Sòng / early-Yuán publishing scholar — combining genuine commentary work with profit-seeking commercial printing — that the Sìkù tíyào catalogers describe with mixed approval.
