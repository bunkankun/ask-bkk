Cáo Dàozhèn 曹道振, Yánpíng *jìnshì* (Yánpíng = modern Nánpíng 南平, Fújiàn) of *Zhìzhèng* 3 (1343, late Yuán). Editor of the standard 17-*juǎn* recension of Luó Cóngyàn 羅從彥 [[羅從彥]]'s *Yùzhāng wénjí* [[KR4d0182]]. According to Cáo's own *yuánxù* (preserved in the *Sìkù* tíyào): the prefectural-resident Xǔ Yuántáng 許源堂 had earlier cut a 5-*juǎn* version of Luó's surviving collection; Cáo recently obtained a manuscript of 13 *juǎn* + 3 *juǎn* *fùlù* + 1 *juǎn* *wàijí* + 1 *juǎn* *niánpǔ* (= 18 *juǎn* total) from a fellow-townsman, Wú Shàozōng 吳紹宗. The Yuán recension circulated until lost; the Míng *Chénghuà* 8 (1472) reprint by Zhāng Tài 張泰 (with a *Jiājìng jiǎyín* = 1554 colophon by Xiè Luán 謝鸞) is the basis for all later prints, including the WYG.
