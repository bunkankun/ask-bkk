Cáo Dào-zhèn 曹道振, Yán-píng *jìn-shì* (Yán-píng = modern Nán-píng 南平, Fú-jiàn) of *Zhì-zhèng* 3 (1343, late Yuán). Editor of the standard 17-*juǎn* recension of Luó Cóng-yàn 羅從彥 [[羅從彥]]'s *Yù-zhāng wén-jí* [[KR4d0182]]. According to Cáo's own *yuán-xù* (preserved in the *Sì-kù* tíyào): the prefectural-resident Xǔ Yuán-táng 許源堂 had earlier cut a 5-*juǎn* version of Luó's surviving collection; Cáo recently obtained a manuscript of 13 *juǎn* + 3 *juǎn* *fù-lù* + 1 *juǎn* *wài-jí* + 1 *juǎn* *nián-pǔ* (= 18 *juǎn* total) from a fellow-townsman, Wú Shào-zōng 吳紹宗. The Yuán recension circulated until lost; the Míng *Chéng-huà* 8 (1472) reprint by Zhāng Tài 張泰 (with a *Jiā-jìng jiǎ-yín* = 1554 colophon by Xiè Luán 謝鸞) is the basis for all later prints, including the WYG.
