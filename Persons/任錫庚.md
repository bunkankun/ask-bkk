Rèn Xīgēng 任錫庚, late-Qīng physician of obscure biographical record. Author of [[KR3ep086]] *Wángshì yīàn yìzhù*, an eleven-*juǎn* annotated study-edition of the [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèngyīng *Wángshì yīàn* trilogy. The text circulated as a pedagogical reference in late-Qīng and early-Republican Chinese-medical schools, where Wáng's classical-medical density required commentary-mediation. Not in CBDB.
