**Kiyokawa Gendō** 清川玄道 (*zì* Nenso 念祖), early-Meiji Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician. Staff physician of the Hakusaidō 博濟堂 beriberi hospital in Tokyo from its founding in 1878, and co-author with Okada Masaharu [[岡田昌春]] of [[KR3eh054]] *Hakusaidō Kakke gairon* 博濟堂腳氣概論 (1879), under the direction of Asada Sōhaku [[淺田惟常]] (院長). Signs the joint preface dated Meiji 12 / 5 (May 1879). No detailed biographical material located.
