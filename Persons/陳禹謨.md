Chén Yǔ-mó 陳禹謨 (1548–1618; CBDB id 130879), native of Cháng-shú 常熟, was a Wàn-lì-period 萬曆 *jǔ-rén* and bibliophile-collator. His chief enduring contribution is the *bǔ-zhù* 補註 recension of Yú Shì-nán's *Běi-táng shū-chāo* 北堂書鈔 ([[KR3k0004]]) — for centuries the only complete version in circulation. The *Sìkù* editors hand down a mixed verdict: Chén did supplement large lacunae and marked his additions systematically with the character *bǔ* 補, allowing the Táng-era stratum to be read off the page; but he also silently amended what he found, drawing Qián Céng's and Zhū Yí-zūn's later complaints that he had "mixed in post-Zhēn-guān events and even Five-Dynasties Ten-Kingdoms titles, wholly losing the original look of the book". Several other Chén Yǔ-mó editions of Sòng and Yuán *lèishū* survive only in the *Sìkù*, all under the *bǔ* / *bǔ-zhù* designation.
