Chén Yǔmó 陳禹謨 (1548–1618; CBDB id 130879), native of Chángshú 常熟, was a Wàn-lì-period 萬曆 *jǔrén* and bibliophile-collator. His chief enduring contribution is the *bǔzhù* 補註 recension of Yú Shìnán's *Běitáng shūchāo* 北堂書鈔 ([[KR3k0004]]) — for centuries the only complete version in circulation. The *Sìkù* editors hand down a mixed verdict: Chén did supplement large lacunae and marked his additions systematically with the character *bǔ* 補, allowing the Táng-era stratum to be read off the page; but he also silently amended what he found, drawing Qián Céng's and Zhū Yízūn's later complaints that he had "mixed in post-Zhēn-guān events and even Five-Dynasties Ten-Kingdoms titles, wholly losing the original look of the book". Several other Chén Yǔmó editions of Sòng and Yuán *lèishū* survive only in the *Sìkù*, all under the *bǔ* / *bǔzhù* designation.
