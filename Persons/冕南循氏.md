**Miǎnnán Xúnshì** 冕南循氏 (style *Yúnshì*, *fl.* c. 1805), anonymous Qīng-period medical compiler. The younger brother of an unnamed elder-brother paediatric physician; redactor and 1805 postface-author of the *Dòuzhěn jīngxiáng* 痘疹精詳 ([[KR3ej059]]). The postface, dated *Jiāqìng shí nián zhòngchūn yuè shuò bā rì* 嘉慶十年仲春月朔八日 (8th day of the 2nd month of 1805), was composed at the *Shèndúxuān* 慎獨軒 studio at *Shífēng* 石峰. Personal name not preserved in the surviving frontmatter.
