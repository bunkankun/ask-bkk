Duàn Gōnglù 段公路 (*fl.* mid-to-late ninth century, *Xiántōng* era 860–874), of Línzī 臨淄 in Qízhōu (modern Shāndōng). Grandson of the Yuánhé / Chángqìng chief minister Duàn Wénchāng 段文昌 (773–835); held office as Magistrate-Aide (*xiànwèi*) of Wànnián 萬年 county in Jīngzhào. Travelled in Lǐngnán in or before *Xiántōng* 10 (869) and compiled the *Běihù lù* 北戶錄 ([[KR2k0106]]), a monograph on the natural products and customs of the South-of-the-Five-Ridges region. The Sìkù tíyào notes that the form "Gōnglù" with *lù* 璐 in the *Xuéhǎi lèibiān* is an error of *zì*. His career and dates are otherwise undocumented; the original preface to the *Běihù lù* by Lù Xīshēng 陸希聲 (*Yòu shíyí nèi gōngfèng*) places its composition in the late Yìzōng or early Xīzōng era.
