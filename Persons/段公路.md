Duàn Gōng-lù 段公路 (*fl.* mid-to-late ninth century, *Xián-tōng* era 860–874), of Lín-zī 臨淄 in Qí-zhōu (modern Shāndōng). Grandson of the Yuán-hé / Cháng-qìng chief minister Duàn Wén-chāng 段文昌 (773–835); held office as Magistrate-Aide (*xiàn-wèi*) of Wàn-nián 萬年 county in Jīng-zhào. Travelled in Lǐng-nán in or before *Xián-tōng* 10 (869) and compiled the *Běihù lù* 北戶錄 ([[KR2k0106]]), a monograph on the natural products and customs of the South-of-the-Five-Ridges region. The Sìkù tíyào notes that the form "Gōng-lù" with *lù* 璐 in the *Xué-hǎi lèi-biān* is an error of *zì*. His career and dates are otherwise undocumented; the original preface to the *Běihù lù* by Lù Xī-shēng 陸希聲 (*Yòu shí-yí nèi gōng-fèng*) places its composition in the late Yì-zōng or early Xī-zōng era.
