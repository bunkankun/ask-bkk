# Huìquán 惠泉

Northern-Sòng Chán monk of the Línjì / Huánglóng lineage. Native of Péngchéng 彭城 (present-day Xúzhōu, Jiāngsū). Took up residence at Nántái Gézi yuàn 南臺閣子院; characterised in the *Bǔxù gāosēng zhuàn* 補續高僧傳 as of austere temperament (*xìng gūjié* 性孤潔), not given to casual association (DILA A021573; X77n1524 p. 516c). Compiled (*jí* 集) the one-juan *Huánglóng Huìnán chánshī yǔlù* 黃龍慧南禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0055]]), the canonical recorded-sayings collection of his teacher [[慧南]] Huánglóng Huìnán (1002–1069). No independent dates are transmitted; his activity is therefore placed in the second half of the eleventh century on the basis of his teacher's life.
