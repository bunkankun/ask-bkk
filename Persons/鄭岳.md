# Zhèng Yuè 鄭岳 (Míng, fl. 1521)

A Zhèng-dé-era (1505–1521) Pú-tián 莆田 official. In *Zhèng-dé xīn-sì* (1521) he selected and edited the 9-*juǎn* + 1-*juǎn* recension of his fellow-villager Lín Guāng-cháo's [[林光朝]] *Ài-xuān jí* [[KR4d0225]] from a surviving manuscript copy after the Sòng-era 10- and 20-*juǎn* recensions had been lost. Bibliographic identifier only; not the same person as the more famous Míng *zhuàng-yuán* 鄭岳 (1468–1539, *zì* Rǔ-huá 汝華), with whom he is to be distinguished by context.
