Chéng Mǐn-zhèng 程敏政 (1445–1499), *zì* **Kè-qín** 克勤, *hào* **Huáng-dūn** 篁墩. Native of **Xiū-níng** 休寧 (Huī-zhōu, Ān-huī). A precocious *shén-tóng* (prodigy) — recommended to the *Tài-xué* at age 10; Chéng-huà 2 (1466) *jìn-shì*; reached *Lǐ-bù yòu shì-láng* 禮部右侍郎 jointly *Hàn-lín-yuàn xué-shì*. In Hóng-zhì 12 (1499) he was at the centre of a famous examination-leakage scandal — accused of leaking the metropolitan-examination questions; thrown into prison and forced into retirement (died shortly thereafter, possibly by suicide). *Míng shǐ* j. 286 (*Wén-yuàn 2*). Son-in-law of the Tiān-shùn-era Senior Grand Secretary **Lǐ Xián** 李賢 ([[李賢]]); edited his father-in-law's [[KR4e0104]] *Gǔ-ráng jí* (30 *juǎn*). Independently a great editor and *Lǐ-xué* author: edited [[KR4e0097]] *Táng Wén-fèng* *Wú-gāng jí* (and the *Táng-shì sān xiān-shēng jí* lineage compilation); edited [[KR4e0109]] *Yuè Zhèng* *Lèi-bó-gǎo*. Author of the major *Sòng-Yuán xué-àn*-precursor *Xīn-jīng fù-zhù* 心經附註 and other Lǐ-xué works. CBDB id 29602 (1445–1499).
