Chéng Mǐnzhèng 程敏政 (1445–1499), *zì* **Kèqín** 克勤, *hào* **Huángdūn** 篁墩. Native of **Xiūníng** 休寧 (Huīzhōu, Ānhuī). A precocious *shéntóng* (prodigy) — recommended to the *Tàixué* at age 10; Chénghuà 2 (1466) *jìnshì*; reached *Lǐbù yòu shìláng* 禮部右侍郎 jointly *Hànlínyuàn xuéshì*. In Hóngzhì 12 (1499) he was at the centre of a famous examination-leakage scandal — accused of leaking the metropolitan-examination questions; thrown into prison and forced into retirement (died shortly thereafter, possibly by suicide). *Míng shǐ* j. 286 (*Wényuàn 2*). Son-in-law of the Tiān-shùn-era Senior Grand Secretary **Lǐ Xián** 李賢 ([[李賢]]); edited his father-in-law's [[KR4e0104]] *Gǔráng jí* (30 *juǎn*). Independently a great editor and *Lǐxué* author: edited [[KR4e0097]] *Táng Wénfèng* *Wúgāng jí* (and the *Tángshì sān xiānshēng jí* lineage compilation); edited [[KR4e0109]] *Yuè Zhèng* *Lèibógǎo*. Author of the major *SòngYuán xuéàn*-precursor *Xīnjīng fùzhù* 心經附註 and other Lǐxué works. CBDB id 29602 (1445–1499).
