Zhāng Yīng 張英 (1637–1708), *zì* Dūnfù 敦復, *hào* Pǔwēng 圃翁, posthumously *Wénduān* 文端, was a leading early-Qīng Kāngxī-court official. Native of Tóng-chéng 桐城 (An-huī). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī liù-chén* (1667); rose to *Wén-huá-diàn dà-xué-shì*. Father of Zhāng Tíng-yù 張廷玉, the major Yōngzhèng-period official. Co-supervised the *Yù dìng xiào jīng yǎn yì* 御定孝經衍義 (KR3a0105). Major works include *Pǔ wēng xián bǐ* 圃翁閒筆 and a *Sōng-qī táng wén-jí* 松齊堂文集. CBDB id 56278.
