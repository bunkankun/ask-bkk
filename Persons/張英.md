Zhāng Yīng 張英 (1637–1708), *zì* Dūnfù 敦復, *hào* Pǔwēng 圃翁, posthumously *Wénduān* 文端, was a leading early-Qīng Kāngxī-court official. Native of Tóngchéng 桐城 (An-huī). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī liùchén* (1667); rose to *Wénhuádiàn dàxuéshì*. Father of Zhāng Tíngyù 張廷玉, the major Yōngzhèng-period official. Co-supervised the *Yù dìng xiào jīng yǎn yì* 御定孝經衍義 ([[KR3a0105]]). Major works include *Pǔ wēng xián bǐ* 圃翁閒筆 and a *Sōngqī táng wénjí* 松齊堂文集. CBDB id 56278.
