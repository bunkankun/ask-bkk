**Wāng Dōngtíng** 汪東亭 (*zì* Dōngtíng 東亭, *hào* sometimes given as Yīyǎn 一眼; full name variously transmitted as Wāng Qǐbīn 汪啟濱; *fl.* Guāngxù era, late 19th c.), late-Qīng inner alchemist of the **Xīpài** 西派 (Western Branch) transmission descending from Lǐ Xīyuè 李西月 (1806–1856). Active in Hànkǒu / Wǔhàn from at least the late 1880s. Disciple of "Wú Tiānzhìwēng" 吳天秩翁, from whom he received the *qīfǎn jiǔhuán jīnyè dà dān* 七返九還金液大丹 transmission; thereafter associated in Hànyáng with Kē Huáijīng 柯懷經, Lǐ Yúnlán 李云嵐, Zhōu Jùnfū 周俊夫 and Kē Zàishū 柯載書 in the Xīpài circle of teachers.

Principal work: *Xìngmìng yàozhǐ* 性命要旨 ([[KR3eo007]]), printed jointly with Kē Huáijīng's *Yǎngxìng biān* 養性編 in 1889 at Hànyáng under the editorial care of Chéng Shǒuyī 程守一 of Xīn'ān. Doctrinally distinctive for the Xīpài *xìngmìng shuāngxiū* sequence starting from *xìng* rather than *mìng*. Cited repeatedly by Chén Yīngníng 陳攖寧 (1880–1969) as one of his immediate-generation Xīpài predecessors. No CBDB record.
