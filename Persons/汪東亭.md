**Wāng Dōngtíng** 汪東亭 (*zì* Dōngtíng 東亭, *hào* sometimes given as Yī-yǎn 一眼; full name variously transmitted as Wāng Qǐbīn 汪啟濱; *fl.* Guāng-xù era, late 19th c.), late-Qīng inner alchemist of the **Xī-pài** 西派 (Western Branch) transmission descending from Lǐ Xī-yuè 李西月 (1806–1856). Active in Hàn-kǒu / Wǔ-hàn from at least the late 1880s. Disciple of "Wú Tiān-zhì-wēng" 吳天秩翁, from whom he received the *qī-fǎn jiǔ-huán jīn-yè dà dān* 七返九還金液大丹 transmission; thereafter associated in Hàn-yáng with Kē Huái-jīng 柯懷經, Lǐ Yún-lán 李云嵐, Zhōu Jùn-fū 周俊夫 and Kē Zài-shū 柯載書 in the Xī-pài circle of teachers.

Principal work: *Xìng-mìng yào-zhǐ* 性命要旨 ([[KR3eo007]]), printed jointly with Kē Huái-jīng's *Yǎng-xìng biān* 養性編 in 1889 at Hàn-yáng under the editorial care of Chéng Shǒu-yī 程守一 of Xīn'ān. Doctrinally distinctive for the Xī-pài *xìng-mìng shuāng-xiū* sequence starting from *xìng* rather than *mìng*. Cited repeatedly by Chén Yīng-níng 陳攖寧 (1880–1969) as one of his immediate-generation Xī-pài predecessors. No CBDB record.
