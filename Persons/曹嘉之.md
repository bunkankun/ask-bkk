Cáo Jiāzhī 曹嘉之 was a Jin-dynasty historian of whom little biographical information survives. He does not appear in the standard Jin biography sections. His *Jìnjì* 晉紀 is recorded in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志, which is the principal evidence for the work's existence. The *Jìnjì* covered at minimum the late Wèi through Western Jìn periods, with a focus on individual anecdotes and character portraits. After the Tang *Jìn shū* compilation (648 CE), the work was lost; [[湯球]] (Táng Qiú, 1804–1881) reconstructed the surviving fragments as [[KR4k0339]]. Not in CBDB.
