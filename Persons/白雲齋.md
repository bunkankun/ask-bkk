**Bái yún zhāi** 白雲齋 — the studio-name of the Míng bibliographer **Bái Yúnjì** 白雲霽, compiler of [[KR5g0239]] ***Dào zàng mù lù xiáng zhù*** 道藏目錄詳註 — a detailed annotated catalogue of the Dào zàng, the standard premodern catalog of the Daoist canon. Catalog meta has "Bái yún zhāi" as author.
