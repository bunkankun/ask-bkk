Late-Táng Chán monk, *cānxué ménrén* 參學門人 of **Zhàozhōu Cóngshěn** [[從諗]] (778–897). Recorder of the *Zhàozhōu héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0391]]). The preserved text preserves him only as the *jìlù* 記錄 name of record; no independent biography survives. Not to be confused with the Sòng-dynasty literatus Lǚshān Wényuǎn 廬山文遠 or with later monastic figures of the same name.
