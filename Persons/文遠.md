Late-Táng Chán monk, *cān-xué mén-rén* 參學門人 of **Zhào-zhōu Cóng-shěn** [[從諗]] (778–897). Recorder of the *Zhào-zhōu héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0391]]). The preserved text preserves him only as the *jì-lù* 記錄 name of record; no independent biography survives. Not to be confused with the Sòng-dynasty literatus Lǚ-shān Wén-yuǎn 廬山文遠 or with later monastic figures of the same name.
