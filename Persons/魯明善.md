Lǔ Míngshàn 魯明善 (fl. 1314–1330), Uyghur (*Wéi-wú-ér* 畏吾兒) Yuán official. Native name *Tiě-chén* 鐵僧 / *Tiě-chén* 鐵柱. Held office to *Tài Píng-lù dá-lǔ-huā-chì* 太平路達魯花赤. Author of the *Nóng-sāng yī-shí cuō yào* 農桑衣食撮要 (KR3d0004, dated *Yán-yòu* 1 / 1314) — the agricultural manual composed during his magistracy at Ān-fēng-lù.
