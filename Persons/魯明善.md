Lǔ Míngshàn 魯明善 (fl. 1314–1330), Uyghur (*Wéiwúér* 畏吾兒) Yuán official. Native name *Tiěchén* 鐵僧 / *Tiěchén* 鐵柱. Held office to *Tài Pínglù dálǔhuāchì* 太平路達魯花赤. Author of the *Nóngsāng yīshí cuō yào* 農桑衣食撮要 ([[KR3d0004]], dated *Yányòu* 1 / 1314) — the agricultural manual composed during his magistracy at Ānfēnglù.
