**Sūn Wén-yìn** 孫文胤 (*zì* Duì-wēi 對薇, late-Míng), Xīn-ān-born physician active in the Zhèn-jiāng 鎮江 region. His clinical practice spanned the lower Yangtze, with frequent consultative travel to Sūzhōu and Hángzhōu. Sūn's principal work is the *Dān-tái yù-àn* 丹臺玉案 ([[KR3er044]], composed c. 1625–1636), a six-*juǎn* late-Míng clinical compendium that self-consciously synthesises the Sòng-Jīn-Yuán four-master tradition with the *Líng-shū / Sù-wèn* canonical literature, the *Huáng-tíng nèi-jǐng* Daoist physiological tradition, and the *Mài-jīng* of Wáng Shū-hé. Sūn's biographical detail is otherwise sparse; he is recorded in some late-Míng / early-Qīng catalog records as a *Xīn-ān*-born physician of Zhèn-jiāng. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
