**Sūn Wényìn** 孫文胤 (*zì* Duìwēi 對薇, late-Míng), Xīn-ān-born physician active in the Zhènjiāng 鎮江 region. His clinical practice spanned the lower Yangtze, with frequent consultative travel to Sūzhōu and Hángzhōu. Sūn's principal work is the *Dāntái yùàn* 丹臺玉案 ([[KR3er044]], composed c. 1625–1636), a six-*juǎn* late-Míng clinical compendium that self-consciously synthesises the SòngJīnYuán four-master tradition with the *Língshū / Sùwèn* canonical literature, the *Huángtíng nèijǐng* Daoist physiological tradition, and the *Màijīng* of Wáng Shūhé. Sūn's biographical detail is otherwise sparse; he is recorded in some late-Míng / early-Qīng catalog records as a *Xīnān*-born physician of Zhènjiāng. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
