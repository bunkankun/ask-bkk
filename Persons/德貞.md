**John Dudgeon** 德貞 (Dé Zhēn, 1837–1901), Scottish Protestant medical missionary and surgeon of the London Missionary Society, active in Běijīng from 1864; first professor of anatomy and physiology at the *Tóngwén guǎn* 同文館 (1871–1894). Author of multiple Chinese-language Western-medical translations and the influential *Quán tǐ tōng kǎo* 全體通考 (anatomy, 1886). Cited by [[唐宗海]] Táng Zōnghǎi in [[KR3eh056]] alongside [[合信]] Hobson for mesenteric-inflammation autopsy findings on dysentery.
