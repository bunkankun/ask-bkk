# Dōngxī Fāngzé 東谿方澤

Mid-Míng Chán master, abbot of Jīngyánsì 精嚴寺 in Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). Principal dharma-heir of [[道濟|Fǎzhōu Dàojì]] 法舟道濟 (1487–1560). Authored a *xíng zhuàng* 行狀 biographical record of his teacher in 1563.
