# 孟准

Mèng Zhǔn

Mid-Míng layman of Bǎodǐxiàn 寶坻縣 (modern Tiānjīn area). With associates funded the 1551 printing of the *Xiāoshì Jīngāng jīng kēyí huìyào zhùjiě* ([[KR6c0055]]).
