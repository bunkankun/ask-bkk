# Zhèng Zhòng 鄭眾

*Zì* Zhòngshī 仲師. Eastern Hàn 東漢 classicist (d. 83 CE), son of the great Western-Hàn classicist Zhèng Xìng 鄭興 and forerunner (sometimes confused with) the later [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán. Held office as Grand Master of Ceremonial (*Tàicháng* 太常). A founding figure of the Eastern Hàn ancient-script (*gǔwén* 古文) school in *Shī*, *Sānlǐ*, *Chūnqiū Zuǒshì*, and the *Yì*. His *Yì* commentary is lost; fragments survive in [[史徵]] Shǐ Zhēng's *Zhōuyì kǒujué yì* ([[KR1a0009]]) and in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]). *Hòu Hànshū* (juan 36) gives his biography.
