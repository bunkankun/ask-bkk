**Wada Tōkaku** 和田東郭 (1742–1803), Edo-period Japanese physician, advocate of the *secchūha* 折衷派 syncretic approach combining *kohōha* 古方派 and *gosei-ha* 後世派 traditions. Cited in [[KR3eq038]] *Xiānzhé yīhuà jí*.
