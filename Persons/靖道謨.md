Jìng Dàomó 靖道謨 (fl. early 18th c.), a Húguǎng (Jiāng-líng 江陵) native, was a Kāngxī 60 (1721) *jìnshì*. He served as *zhīzhōu* of Yáo-zhōu 姚州 in Yúnnán under the Yōngzhèng emperor and was the principal *biānzuǎn* of two of the most important Yōngzhèng-cohort provincial gazetteers: the *Yúnnán tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0055]]) of 1736 and the *Guìzhōu tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0056]]) of 1741, both supervised by the Yún-Guì Governor-General [[鄂爾泰]]. His direct field experience in the Yún-Guì *gǎi-tǔ guī-liú* zone gave him an unusual qualification for documenting the transformation of the *tǔsī* system into regular bureaucratic administration.
