Tiān Chànshēng 天懺生 is a pen name used by the author of *Hóngxiàn Gōngwéi Yànshǐ Yǎnyì* 洪憲宮闈豔史演義 ([[KR4k0303]]), a Republican-era semi-fictional exposé of Yuán Shìkǎi's Hóngxiàn court (1915–1916). The real identity behind the pen name is unknown. Active after 1916.
