# Shījiǎo 師皎

Southern-Sòng Chán monk; *fǎsūn* 法孫 of [[淨端]] Wúshān Jìngduān (1031–1104). A century after the master's death, took on the editorial task of gathering Jìngduān's scattered *jìsòng* and correspondence and producing a two-juan *chóngbiān* 重編 recension of his *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0379]]; X73 n1449), with the assistance of the former Dàochǎngshān abbot Dìnglóng 定隆 (preface dated 1209). Lifedates unrecorded.
