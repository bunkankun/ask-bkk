# Shī-jiǎo 師皎

Southern-Sòng Chán monk; *fǎ-sūn* 法孫 of [[淨端]] Wú-shān Jìng-duān (1031–1104). A century after the master's death, took on the editorial task of gathering Jìng-duān's scattered *jì-sòng* and correspondence and producing a two-juan *chóng-biān* 重編 recension of his *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0379]]; X73 n1449), with the assistance of the former Dào-chǎng-shān abbot Dìng-lóng 定隆 (preface dated 1209). Lifedates unrecorded.
