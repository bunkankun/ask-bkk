Zhāng Wònán 張偓南 (fl. early 20th c.) was a physician of Wēixiàn 威縣, Héběi, who in 1915 acquired the manuscript scroll later known as the Dunhuang *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]) from a person said to have obtained it from [[王圓籙]] at Dūnhuáng. The scroll passed to his grandson [[張大昌]], who reconstructed the text from memory after the original's destruction in 1966.
