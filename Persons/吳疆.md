# Jiang Wu 吳疆

Jiang Wu (Wú Jiāng), contemporary scholar of Chinese Buddhism, professor at the University of Arizona. Author of *Enlightenment in Dispute: The Reinvention of Chan Buddhism in Seventeenth-Century China* (OUP, 2008), the standard study of seventeenth-century Línjì-Cáodòng polemics.
