Shìhéng 士衡 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* late 12th – early 13th c.), Southern-Sòng Tiāntāi 天台 monk, resident at **Yún-jiān** 雲間 (modern Sōng-jiāng 松江, in greater Shànghǎi). Designated 雲間沙門 ("monk of Yún-jiān") in his self-attribution at the head of [[KR6r0068]] *Tiāntāi jiǔ-zǔ zhuàn*.

He is the compiler of the **《天台九祖傳》** *Tiāntāi jiǔ-zǔ zhuàn* ([[KR6r0068]], T2069), a 1-juan biographical compendium of the **nine patriarchs of the Tiāntāi school** — Lóngshù 龍樹 (Nāgārjuna), Huì-wén 慧文, Huì-sī 慧思, Zhì-yǐ 智顗, Guàn-dǐng 灌頂, Zhì-wēi 智威, Huì-wēi 慧威, Xuán-lǎng 玄朗, and Zhàn-rán 湛然 — with an authorial preface (序) dated **Jiā-dìng** 嘉定 1 (1208). The colophon text gives Shìhéng's life nothing further than this Yún-jiān residence and Tiāntāi affiliation; he is otherwise unattested. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000050.
