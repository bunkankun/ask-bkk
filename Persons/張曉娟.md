Contemporary Chinese specialist of Dūnhuáng manuscripts and Buddhist commentary literature, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Co-editor (with [[李際寧]]) of *Fǎhuá jīng wénwài yì* 法華經文外義 ([[KR6v0027]]) — the precisely dated 545 CE Western-Wèi Lotus commentary recovered from Shanghai Museum's Dūnhuáng holdings.
