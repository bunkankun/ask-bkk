Wáng Zhēn 王禎 (fl. 1290s–1330s), *zì* Bóshàn 伯善, Yuán-period Chinese agricultural-encyclopedist and printing-pioneer. Native of Dōng-píng 東平 (Shān-dōng). Held office as Magistrate of Yǒng-fēng 永豐 (Jiāng-xī) and Xuān-zhōu 宣州 (An-huī). Major works: the *Nóng shū* 農書 (KR3d0005, his comprehensive agricultural encyclopedia in 22 juan, completed *Yán-yòu* 4 / 1313) and the appended *Zào huó zì yìn shū fǎ* 造活字印書法 (Method for Making Movable-Type Printing) — one of the earliest Chinese descriptions of wooden movable-type printing. CBDB has multiple homonyms 王禎; the catalog meta gives "fl. 1333" suggesting late-Yuán dating.
