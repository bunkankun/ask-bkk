Pseudonym of the author of [[KR4k0276]] *Yīngxióng Lèi* 英雄淚. The full pen name as given on the title page of the stone-print edition is 雞林冷血生著 — "Written by Lěngxuèshēng of Jīlín 雞林," where Jīlín (literally "Rooster Forest") is an archaic poetic name for Korea (from the ancient Silla kingdom). The self-preface is dated "mid-autumn of *gēngxū*" (庚戌仲秋, 1910).

A bibliographic note in the Kanripo source file raises the hypothesis that the author may be Chén Jǐnghán 陳景韓 (1878–1906), the journalist and editor of *Xiǎoshuō Shíbào* 小說時報, who used "冷血" as a pen name. However, Chén Jǐnghán died in 1906 — before the 1910 composition date given in the preface — and the identification remains uncertain. The Kanripo note records it "for further investigation" (*lù yǐ dài kǎo* 錄以待考). No CBDB record has been identified for the author of this work.
