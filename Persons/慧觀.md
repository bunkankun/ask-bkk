# Huì-guān 慧觀

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Liú-Sòng translator-monk Huì-guān 慧觀 (5th c.), a distinct figure.
