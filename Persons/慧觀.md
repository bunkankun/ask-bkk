# Huìguān 慧觀

Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuèjiāng Zhèngyìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with the Liú-Sòng translator-monk Huìguān 慧觀 (5th c.), a distinct figure.
