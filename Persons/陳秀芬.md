Chén Xiùfēn 陳秀芬 (Chen Hsiu-fen) is a contemporary Táiwān historian of Chinese medicine, with articles on Míng-Qīng *diānkuáng* 癲狂 nosology and the cultural history of dreams and mental disorder.
