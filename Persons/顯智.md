Kenchi 顯智 (1226–1310), Kantō Shinshū scholar of the **Takada** 高田 lineage, third abbot of the **Senju-ji** 専修寺 (the head temple of the Takada branch, in Mie). Pupil of **Shinbutsu** 真佛, who was himself a senior disciple of [[親鸞]] Shinran in the Kantō provinces. Kenchi is the most learned figure of the early Takada-ha and the principal scholastic systematizer of the Shinran doctrinal tradition outside the Hongan-ji line. His chief surviving work is the *Daimyōmoku* [[KR6t0382]], a doctrinal-lexical handbook of the Pure Land schools. He was a contemporary and rival of [[覺如]] **Kakunyo** (1270–1351), and the Takada-ha–Hongan-ji split begins with their respective generations. The DILA authority id is `A001242`.
