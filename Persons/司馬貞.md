# Sīmǎ Zhēn 司馬貞

*Zì* Zǐzhèng 子正. Active in the early to mid-eighth century; *Cháoyì dàfū* 朝議大夫 and *Hóngwén guǎn xuéshì* 弘文館學士 under Tang Xuánzōng. Native of Hénèi 河內 (modern Qìnyáng 沁陽, Hénán). Floruit ca. 720s–740s.

Author of the *Shǐjì suǒyǐn* 史記索隱 in 30 *juǎn* (KR2a0001 *Shǐjì*; separately KR2a0003 *Shǐjì suǒyǐn*), completed in the late 720s or early 730s. The *Suǒyǐn* concentrates on textual criticism, geographical and lexical glossing, and reconciliation of variant readings, and incorporates a vast array of Six Dynasties and early Tang commentators now otherwise lost. Until the Northern Sòng it circulated independently of the main text, in 30 *juǎn* against the *Shǐjì*'s 130, with a separate *xù* 序 and *fánlì* 凡例.

Sīmǎ Zhēn is also the author of the controversial *Sānhuáng běnjì* 三皇本紀 (Basic Annals of the Three Augusts), an attempt to supplement what he regarded as a defect in the original *Shǐjì*; the Ming Nán-jiàn edition placed it before the *Wǔdì běnjì* and the Sìkù editors strongly disapproved.
