# Fèiyǐn Tōngróng 費隱通容

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì 臨濟 Chán master (1593–1661), dharma-heir of Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟. Compiler of the polemical lamp record *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 ([[KR6q0018]]), which sharply revised earlier Cáodòng-line attributions in favour of the Línjì line.
