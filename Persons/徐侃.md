Xú Kǎn 徐侃, a Lǚ Zǔqiān disciple of the Wùzhōu Lìzhái-academy circle. Listed by the Sìkù editors among the candidates (with [[徐僑]] Xú Qiáo and Xú Zhuō) for the unidentified "Xú Zǒnggàn" author of *Yì chuán dēng* ([[KR1a0044]]). No further biographical data preserved.
