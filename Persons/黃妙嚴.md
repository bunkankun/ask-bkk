# 黃妙嚴

Huáng Miàoyán

Qiánlóng-era (1776) reprinter of the Yǒnglè *Jīngāng jīng zhùjiě* ([[KR6c0056]]) recovered from the Huáiyīn collector Chéng Qiūquán 程秋泉, with a fresh postface by Táo Xuéchūn 陶學椿 of Hángzhōu. This is the immediate exemplar behind the *Xùzàngjīng* reprint.
