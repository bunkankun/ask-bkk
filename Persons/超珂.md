Chāokē 超珂, *zì* **Yìgōng** 義公 ("Righteous Sir"); also styled **Kuíyīng** 揆英 (a *biéhào* given by **Jìqǐ** 繼起 at **Língyánshān** 靈巖). One of the major **female** LínjìYángqí Chán masters of mid-17th-century Jiāxīng. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Qíyuán Xínggāng** [[行剛]] (1597–1654), and second abbess of **Fúshī chányuàn** 伏獅禪院 at Méixī (Jiāxīng). Lifedates **1615 – 順治十八年二月初二日** = **1661-03-02**, age 47, *fǎlà* 39 (per the *xíngzhuàng* in juan 1 of her *yǔlù*). Native of **Méilǐ** 梅里 (Jiāxīng).

**Formation.** Tonsured at age 8. **順治三年** = **1646** invited her dharma-master **Qíyuán** 祇園 to Méilǐ to take residence at **Dǒngān** 董庵 (renamed **Fúshī chányuàn** on her arrival). At 34 began intensive *cān*-fǎng: visited **Shíyǔ** 石雨 at **Gāotíng** 皋亭 and **Jìqǐ** 繼起 at **Língyán** 靈巖 (where she received the secondary name *Kuíyīng*). **順治七年** = **1650** at **Nánxún Bōrěān** 南潯般若庵 during an intensive *qī* retreat, on the 9th day, kneeling before the Buddha-image, broke through on the *zhúbìzǐ* 竹篦子 *gōngàn* — "the bottom of the bucket fell out, and her whole body felt at ease." Returned as *jiānyuàn* of Fúshī, then sealed herself in *guān* 閉關 again at Bōrěān. **順治十一年** = **1654** received final *fùfǎ* from her teacher just before the latter's death and succeeded as second abbess.

**Sole *fǎsì*.** **Yīkuí Chāochēn** 一揆超琛 [[超琛]] 參同一揆.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0552]] 《伏獅義公禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B435, recorded by *fǎsūn* (dharma-grandson, more likely *fǎsì*) **Míngyuán** [[明元]] *jìlù*; preface by **Gāo Yǐyǒng** [[高以永]] dated **康熙戊午桂月** = **1678** (8th lunar month); *bá* (postface) by **Yīkuí Chāochēn** dated **康熙拾柒年蒲月** = **1678** (5th lunar month).
- Co-compiler / editor of her teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0428]] 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 2 juan, J28 B210.

Buried at **Cāntóngān** 參同庵 (Méixī).

**Sources.** 《伏獅義公禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行狀〉; 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 vol. 1.

**Secondary literature.** Beata Grant, *Eminent Nuns: Women Chan Masters of Seventeenth-Century China* (University of Hawaiʻi Press, 2009).
