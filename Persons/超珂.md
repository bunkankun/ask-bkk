Chāo-kē 超珂, *zì* **Yì-gōng** 義公 ("Righteous Sir"); also styled **Kuí-yīng** 揆英 (a *bié-hào* given by **Jì-qǐ** 繼起 at **Líng-yán-shān** 靈巖). One of the major **female** Línjì-Yángqí Chán masters of mid-17th-century Jiā-xīng. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qí-yuán Xíng-gāng** [[行剛]] (1597–1654), and second abbess of **Fú-shī chán-yuàn** 伏獅禪院 at Méi-xī (Jiā-xīng). Lifedates **1615 – 順治十八年二月初二日** = **1661-03-02**, age 47, *fǎ-là* 39 (per the *xíng-zhuàng* in juan 1 of her *yǔ-lù*). Native of **Méi-lǐ** 梅里 (Jiā-xīng).

**Formation.** Tonsured at age 8. **順治三年** = **1646** invited her dharma-master **Qí-yuán** 祇園 to Méi-lǐ to take residence at **Dǒng-ān** 董庵 (renamed **Fú-shī chán-yuàn** on her arrival). At 34 began intensive *cān*-fǎng: visited **Shí-yǔ** 石雨 at **Gāo-tíng** 皋亭 and **Jì-qǐ** 繼起 at **Líng-yán** 靈巖 (where she received the secondary name *Kuí-yīng*). **順治七年** = **1650** at **Nán-xún Bō-rě-ān** 南潯般若庵 during an intensive *qī* retreat, on the 9th day, kneeling before the Buddha-image, broke through on the *zhú-bì-zǐ* 竹篦子 *gōng-àn* — "the bottom of the bucket fell out, and her whole body felt at ease." Returned as *jiān-yuàn* of Fú-shī, then sealed herself in *guān* 閉關 again at Bō-rě-ān. **順治十一年** = **1654** received final *fù-fǎ* from her teacher just before the latter's death and succeeded as second abbess.

**Sole *fǎ-sì*.** **Yī-kuí Chāo-chēn** 一揆超琛 [[超琛]] 參同一揆.

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0552]] 《伏獅義公禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B435, recorded by *fǎ-sūn* (dharma-grandson, more likely *fǎ-sì*) **Míng-yuán** [[明元]] *jì-lù*; preface by **Gāo Yǐ-yǒng** [[高以永]] dated **康熙戊午桂月** = **1678** (8th lunar month); *bá* (postface) by **Yī-kuí Chāo-chēn** dated **康熙拾柒年蒲月** = **1678** (5th lunar month).
- Co-compiler / editor of her teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0428]] 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 2 juan, J28 B210.

Buried at **Cān-tóng-ān** 參同庵 (Méi-xī).

**Sources.** 《伏獅義公禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行狀〉; 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 vol. 1.
