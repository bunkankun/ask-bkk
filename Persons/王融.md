Wáng Róng 王融 (467–493 CE; *zì* Yuánzhǎng 元長) was a Southern Qí 南齊 *Yǒngmíng* era poet and one of the "Eight Friends" (*bā yǒu* 八友) of the Jǐnglíng wáng 竟陵王 Xiāo Zǐliáng 蕭子良. A leading practitioner of the new four-tone tonal prosody (*sì shēng* 四聲), he rose rapidly at court before being executed at age 27 for political miscalculation. Biography in *Nán Qí shū* juǎn 47 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 22. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0051]].
