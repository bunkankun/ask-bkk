**Yuán Tíngzhí** 袁庭植 was a Sòng Daoist, compiler of [[KR5g0059]] *Chōng xū tōng miào shì chén Wáng xiān sheng jiā huà* 沖虛通妙侍宸王先生家話 — recorded sayings of **Wáng Wénqīng** 王文卿 (1093–1153), senior patriarch of the Shén xiāo 神霄 Thunder-Rites tradition.
