**Jīn Júpǔ** 金菊圃 (also recorded as **Jīn Càizhōng** 金菜中, *fl.* c. 1801), Qīng-period Educational Official (*bǐngduó*) at Héngshān 衡山 (Húnán) who sponsored the first edition of [[文起]] Wén Qǐ's *Dòukē jíyào* 痘科輯要 ([[KR3ej060]]) in 1801. Author of one of the 1801 prefaces, which emphasises the *kuān xīn* (paternal solicitude) motivation for paediatric medicine and praises Wén Qǐ as having combined SòngYuán *sìdàjiā* learning with Niè Jiǔwú 聶久吾's *Huóyòu xīnfǎ* tradition. His son Jīn Zhújūn 金竹均 later donated the family copy for the 1829 reprint by [[毛鵬]] Máo Péng.
