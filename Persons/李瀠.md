**Lǐ Yíng** 李瀠 (*hào* Chángchūnzǐ 長春子, fl. early-mid Qīng, 17th–18th c., 清), Qīng-period channel-doctrine compiler. Author of the *Shēnjīng tōngkǎo* 身經通考 ([[KR3ee044]]), a four-juan catechistic treatise on channel-and-acupoint pathology. No further biographical record survives; not in CBDB.
