**Hé Yīng** 何英 (*fl.* late Qiánlóng – early Jiāqìng era, ca. 1780–1820), late-Qīng compiler of the *Wéntáng jíyàn fāng* 文堂集驗方 ([[KR3ed119]]) in 4 *juǎn* — a popular clinical formulary in the charity-pharmacy / merit-publication tradition, organised by syndrome with brief theoretical introductions before each formula-group. The *Wéntáng* 文堂 ("Literary Hall") is presumably the compiler's studio-name, but no further biographical detail is securely identifiable. The work's *fánlì* indicates that Hé was felled by serious illness during compilation, leaving the manuscript imperfectly proofed; he positioned the work as a freely-distributable merit-pharmacy text.
