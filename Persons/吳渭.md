**Wú Wèi** 吳渭 (fl. c. 1270–1290), *zì* **Qīngwēng** 清翁, sobriquet **Qiánzhāi** 潛齋 ("Hidden Studio"). Native of **Pújiāng** 浦江 in Wūzhōu 婺州 (Jīnhuá 金華, Zhèjiāng). Held the office of **Magistrate (xiànlìng) of Yìwū** 義烏 under the late Southern Sòng. After the Sòng surrender (1276) he refused all Yuán recruitment and retired to **Wúxī** 吳溪 in Pújiāng. There in **Zhìyuán 23–24 (1286–1287)** he founded the **Yuèquán yínshè** 月泉吟社 — the largest Sòng-loyalist poetry contest of the early Yuán — calling for submissions on the topic *Chūnrì tiányuán záxīng* 春日田園雜興 ("Mixed Inspirations on Spring-Day Farm-Garden", after Fàn Chéngdà's well-known series) in *wǔ-* and *qīyán lǜshī* form, distributed in the tenth month and harvested by the Lantern Festival of the following year. 2,735 *juǎn* (rolls) were submitted; **Fāng Fèng** ([[方鳳]]), **Xiè Áo** ([[謝翱]]), and **Wú Sīqí** ([[吳思齊]]) were brought in as judges; 280 entries were selected and posted on the third day of the third month. The resulting anthology [[KR4h0060]] preserves 60 of these entries and 74 poems together with the *shèyuē* (Society Pact), the *shìwén* (Oath), and the critical apparatus — making it a unique documentary witness to **Sòngyímín literary sociology**.
