**Zhāng Shēngchí** 張聲馳 of Huátíng 華亭 (Sōngjiāng), late-Qīng physician who in 1891 (Guāngxù 17) wrote the second postface to and re-printed [[KR3eq015]] *Yīyuán* of [[石壽棠]] Shí Shòutáng after receiving the manuscript through [[呂伯仁]] Lǚ Bórén and [[沈菊人]] Shěn Júrén.
