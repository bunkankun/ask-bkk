**Páng Yuánchéng** 龐元澄 (*hào* Wūchéng 烏程), late-Qīng Húzhōu 湖州 (Zhèjiāng) book-collector who in 1897 (Guāngxù 23) first printed [[陸以湉]] Lù Yǐtián's [[KR3eq005]] *Lěnglú yīhuà* and the *Zàixù míngyī lèiàn* 再續名醫類案 from manuscripts he had collected over a decade, having earlier purchased the printing blocks of Lù's *Lěnglú záshí* 冷廬雜識.
