Dīng Dù 丁度 (990–1053), *zì* Gōngyǎ 公雅, posthumously *Wénjiǎn* 文簡, was a Northern Sòng official and lexicographer. *Jìnshì* of *Dàzhōngxiángfú* 4 (1011); rose to *Shǐjì shǎojiàn* and *Hànlín shìzhì*. Major works: principal compiler of the *Jí yùn* 集韻 (the major Sòng rhyme-dictionary, completed 1037); co-compiler of the *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* 武經總要 ([[KR3b0012]]). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 292.
