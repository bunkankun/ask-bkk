Southern-Sòng *nèidān* 內丹 master in the *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇 commentarial tradition. *Lóngméi zǐ* 龍眉子 ("Master Dragon-Brow") is a religious *hào*; his real name, like those of many other authors in this lineage (Wúmíng zǐ 無名子, etc.), is unknown. Author of *[[KR5a0152|DZ 151 Jīnyè huándān yìnzhèng tú 金液還丹印證圖]]*, a chart-and-poem treatise on the cyclically transformed elixir, with preface dated 1218 and colophon dated *gēngzi* (TC: an error for *gēngchén* 庚辰 = 1220) but in fact ca. 1222. A second colophon, by Wáng Jǐngxuán 王景玄, claims that the pictures were drawn by Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾 and only the poems were by Lóngméi zǐ; since Wáng addresses Lóngméi as *zǔshī* 祖師 ("patriarch"), it is possible Wáng was the latter's disciple. Lóngméi zǐ's terminology aligns with Wúmíng zǐ's commentary on the *Wùzhēn piān* — placing him in the same circle of late Southern-Sòng *nèidān* practitioners (cf. *[[KR5a0142|DZ 141 Wùzhēn piān zhùshū]]*, DZ 142 *Sānzhù*, DZ 143 *Zhízhǐ xiángshuō sānchéng bìyào*, etc.).
