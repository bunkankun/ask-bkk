Fán Yīng 樊英 (d. 152), Eastern Hàn classical scholar. His biography in the *Hòu Hàn shū* lists the seven *wěi* 緯 texts including the *Yì wěi jī lǎn tú* at the head of the *Yì*-apocrypha — a foundational witness to the early structure of the *Yì wěi* tradition cited in the *Sìkù* notice on [[KR1a0164]].
