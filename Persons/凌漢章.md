# 凌漢章 Líng Hànzhāng

**Líng Hànzhāng** 凌漢章, mid-Míng Sūzhōu acupuncturist; mentioned in [[汪機]] Wāng Jī's *Zhēnjiǔ wènduì* 針灸問對 ([[KR3ee016]], 1530) preface as famous for needling "accurately through clothing" (隔衣針亦每中其穴).
