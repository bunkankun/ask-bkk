Shīzhèng 師正 (active Yuán), Yuán-period Buddhist scholar; the *pái kē* 排科 ("section-arranger") of [[KR6g0020|*Kē nán běn niè pán jīng*]] (X663) and the *fēn kē* 分科 ("section-divider") of [[KR6g0021|*Niè pán jīng huì shū jiě*]] (X664). Birth and death dates not preserved.
