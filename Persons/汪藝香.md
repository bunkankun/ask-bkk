Wāng Yìxiāng 汪藝香, late-Qīng physician of Liángxī 梁溪 (a literary name for Wúxī 無錫, Jiāngsū), *fl.* mid-to-late nineteenth century. Reputed in his time as "a fine physician of the late Qīng, broad in learning and discernment, decisive in life-and-death judgement"; most of his case-records were lost. His surviving cases were salvaged in an early-Republican medical-journal serialisation and now transmitted as [[KR3ep065]] *Wāng Yìxiāng xiānshēng yīàn*. Clinical signature places him within the Wāng Mèngyīng / Yè-school *wēnbìng* tradition. Not in CBDB.
