# Fǎrùn 法潤

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué; one of the section-compilers of juan 2 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0070]]), the *sònggǔ / niāngǔ* fascicle.
