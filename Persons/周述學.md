# Zhōu Shùxué 周述學

Style name *Yúnyuān* 雲淵, hence "Mr Yúnyuān" 雲淵先生. Native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Shàoxīng prefecture, Zhèjiāng); approximate dates Jiā-jìng-era to early Wànlì (c. 1518–c. 1582). No CBDB record located; biographical reconstruction derives from the Guāng-xù-period editor's preface preserved at the head of his *Shéndào dàbiān lìzōng tōngyì* and from the references to him in Huáng Zōngxī's polemical preface to [[KR3fb015]] *Lìxué jiǎrú*.

A Míng-period independent polymath who declined office under Qiú Luán 仇鸞 and "ended his days as a commoner" (以布衣終). His reputation in the Wànlì–Tiānqǐ era rested on three claims: (i) recovery of the *gūtiān gēyuán* 弧天割圜 (spherical-trigonometry) techniques underlying the Yuán *Shòushí lì* 授時曆 of Guō Shǒujìng 郭守敬, the technical chapters of which had been lost from circulation by the mid-Míng — Táng Shùnzhī 唐順之 of Wǔjìn and Gù Yìngxiáng 顧應祥 of Chángxìng had both sought the books in vain; (ii) integration of the western *Huíhuí lì* longitude-and-latitude (*jīngwěi*) and *língfàn* 凌犯 occultation procedures with native Chinese methods; and (iii) the production of the encyclopaedic *Shéndào dàbiān* 神道大編 in over a thousand *juàn* — covering calendrics, charts, the *Huángjí* 皇極, mathematical harmonics, the *Shānjīng* and *Shuǐjīng zhì*, geographical astrology (*fēnyě*), arithmetic, *Tàiyǐ* 太乙, *liùrén* / *dùnjiǎ* 壬遁, *yǎnqín* 演禽, *fēngjiǎo* 風角 ornithomancy, military *fúzhèn* 符陣, geomancy, *lùmìng* 祿命, *jiànchú* 建除, mortuary procedures, the Five Movements and Six Qi, and oceanic compass-needle navigation 海道鍼經. The first eighteen *juàn* of this encyclopaedia — the *Lìzōng tōngyì* 曆宗通議, here counted as 20 *juàn* — survive as [[KR3fb005]]; further surviving Míng blue-grid manuscripts include the *Suànhuì* 算會 in 15 *juàn* and the *Huátiān wǔxīng* 華天五星 in 9 *juàn*.

Huáng Zōngxī's preface to *Lìxué jiǎrú* names Zhōu as the source from whom Táng Shùnzhī obtained the *Shòushí* secrets — Táng then concealing the source — making Zhōu a key (and undervalued) hinge in the late-Míng transmission of Yuán mathematical astronomy. He is likewise cited at one remove in the polemical preface to [[KR3fb011]] Wèi Wénkuí's *Lì cè*. Modern surveys: Shí Yúnlǐ 石雲里, "Zhōu Shùxué jí qí lìxué chéngjiù" 周述学及其历学成就, *Zìrán kēxué shǐ yánjiū* (1998).
