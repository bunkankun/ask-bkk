# Zhōu Shùxué 周述學

Style name *Yúnyuān* 雲淵, hence "Mr Yúnyuān" 雲淵先生. Native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Shào-xīng prefecture, Zhèjiāng); approximate dates Jiā-jìng-era to early Wàn-lì (c. 1518–c. 1582). No CBDB record located; biographical reconstruction derives from the Guāng-xù-period editor's preface preserved at the head of his *Shén-dào dà-biān lì-zōng tōngyì* and from the references to him in Huáng Zōngxī's polemical preface to [[KR3fb015]] *Lì-xué jiǎ-rú*.

A Míng-period independent polymath who declined office under Qiú Luán 仇鸞 and "ended his days as a commoner" (以布衣終). His reputation in the Wàn-lì–Tiān-qǐ era rested on three claims: (i) recovery of the *gū-tiān gē-yuán* 弧天割圜 (spherical-trigonometry) techniques underlying the Yuán *Shòu-shí lì* 授時曆 of Guō Shǒu-jìng 郭守敬, the technical chapters of which had been lost from circulation by the mid-Míng — Táng Shùn-zhī 唐順之 of Wǔ-jìn and Gù Yìng-xiáng 顧應祥 of Cháng-xìng had both sought the books in vain; (ii) integration of the western *Huíhuí lì* longitude-and-latitude (*jīng-wěi*) and *líng-fàn* 凌犯 occultation procedures with native Chinese methods; and (iii) the production of the encyclopaedic *Shén-dào dà-biān* 神道大編 in over a thousand *juàn* — covering calendrics, charts, the *Huáng-jí* 皇極, mathematical harmonics, the *Shān-jīng* and *Shuǐ-jīng zhì*, geographical astrology (*fēn-yě*), arithmetic, *Tài-yǐ* 太乙, *liù-rén* / *dùn-jiǎ* 壬遁, *yǎn-qín* 演禽, *fēng-jiǎo* 風角 ornithomancy, military *fú-zhèn* 符陣, geomancy, *lù-mìng* 祿命, *jiàn-chú* 建除, mortuary procedures, the Five Movements and Six Qi, and oceanic compass-needle navigation 海道鍼經. The first eighteen *juàn* of this encyclopaedia — the *Lì-zōng tōng-yì* 曆宗通議, here counted as 20 *juàn* — survive as [[KR3fb005]]; further surviving Míng blue-grid manuscripts include the *Suàn-huì* 算會 in 15 *juàn* and the *Huá-tiān wǔ-xīng* 華天五星 in 9 *juàn*.

Huáng Zōngxī's preface to *Lì-xué jiǎ-rú* names Zhōu as the source from whom Táng Shùn-zhī obtained the *Shòu-shí* secrets — Táng then concealing the source — making Zhōu a key (and undervalued) hinge in the late-Míng transmission of Yuán mathematical astronomy. He is likewise cited at one remove in the polemical preface to [[KR3fb011]] Wèi Wénkuí's *Lì cè*. Modern surveys: Shí Yún-lǐ 石雲里, "Zhōu Shùxué jí qí lì-xué chéngjiù" 周述学及其历学成就, *Zì-rán kē-xué shǐ yán-jiū* (1998).
