# Zǐ-yì 子益

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Jiàn-guān 劍關. Native of Jiàn-zhōu 劍州 in Shǔ. Death Xiánchún 3 (1267) per DILA. Dharma-heir of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Three abbacies: Lóng-xìng Xīng-huà 興化 (from Jiāxī 3 / 1239), Yún-yán Shòu-níng 雲岩壽寧, and Fúzhōu Xī-chán Yí-shān Cháng-qìng 長慶 (from 1265 until his death). Recorded sayings as *Jiàn-guān Zǐ-yì chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0320]]). Named dharma-heir: Chóng-zé 崇澤.
