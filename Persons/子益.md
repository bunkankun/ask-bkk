# Zǐyì 子益

Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Jiànguān 劍關. Native of Jiànzhōu 劍州 in Shǔ. Death Xiánchún 3 (1267) per DILA. Dharma-heir of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Three abbacies: Lóngxìng Xīnghuà 興化 (from Jiāxī 3 / 1239), Yúnyán Shòuníng 雲岩壽寧, and Fúzhōu Xīchán Yíshān Chángqìng 長慶 (from 1265 until his death). Recorded sayings as *Jiànguān Zǐyì chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0320]]). Named dharma-heir: Chóngzé 崇澤.
