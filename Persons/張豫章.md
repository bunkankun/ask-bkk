Zhāng Yù-zhāng 張豫章 (active c. 1690–1715), early-Kāngxī Hàn-lín scholar. At the time of the *Yù-xuǎn Sòng-Jīn-Yuán-Míng sì-cháo shī* 御選宋金元明四朝詩 [[KR4h0143]] compilation (1709), he held office as *yuán-rèn yòu chūn-fāng yòu shù-zǐ jiān Hàn-lín-yuàn xiū-zhuàn* 原任右春坊右庶子兼翰林院修撰 (former Right *Shùzǐ* of the Right Spring-Office, concurrently Hàn-lín Compiler), and served as **principal *zuǎn-xuǎn guān*** (chief selection-compiler) on the project, jointly with Wèi Xué-chéng 魏學誠, Wú Bǐng 吳昺, Chén Zhì-yán 陳至言, Chén Zhāng 陳璋, and Wáng Jǐng-zēng 王景曾. CBDB id 345414 (no lifedates recorded). The standard biographies are thin; his name is principally documented in the imperial-anthology paratexts of the *Sì-cháo shī*.
