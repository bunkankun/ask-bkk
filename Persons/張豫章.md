Zhāng Yùzhāng 張豫章 (active c. 1690–1715), early-Kāngxī Hànlín scholar. At the time of the *Yùxuǎn SòngJīnYuánMíng sìcháo shī* 御選宋金元明四朝詩 [[KR4h0143]] compilation (1709), he held office as *yuánrèn yòu chūnfāng yòu shùzǐ jiān Hànlínyuàn xiūzhuàn* 原任右春坊右庶子兼翰林院修撰 (former Right *Shùzǐ* of the Right Spring-Office, concurrently Hànlín Compiler), and served as **principal *zuǎnxuǎn guān*** (chief selection-compiler) on the project, jointly with Wèi Xuéchéng 魏學誠, Wú Bǐng 吳昺, Chén Zhìyán 陳至言, Chén Zhāng 陳璋, and Wáng Jǐngzēng 王景曾. CBDB id 345414 (no lifedates recorded). The standard biographies are thin; his name is principally documented in the imperial-anthology paratexts of the *Sìcháo shī*.
