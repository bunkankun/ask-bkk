Wáng Shù 王澍 (1668–1743), zì Ruòlín 若霖 (also written 篛林), hào Xūzhōu 虛舟, of Jīntán 金壇 (Jiāngsū), was a Kāngxī-Yōngzhèng-era *jīnshí* and calligraphy scholar. *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 50 (1711), he served in the *Hànlínyuàn* and as *Lǐbù lángzhōng* 吏部郎中 before retiring. Renowned as one of the principal Kāngxī-era *zhuànlì* calligraphers and *fǎtiè* connoisseurs. His major works include the *Yǔgòng pǔ* 禹貢譜, the *Chúnhuà mìgé fǎtiè kǎozhèng* 淳化祕閣法帖考正 [[KR2n0044]] (12-juan critical work on the *Chúnhuà gétiè*, with a 1-juan *Gǔjīn fǎtiè kǎo* 古今法帖考 + 1-juan *Yúnfǎ* 筆法), and the *Zhúyún tíbá* 竹雲題跋 [[KR2n0045]] (4-juan colophons on calligraphy works he had personally examined and copied). The catalog meta gives 1668–1743; CBDB 69453 records 1668–1739, but the standard reference works (Hummel, *Qīngshǐ liézhuàn*) confirm 1668–1743 — followed here.
