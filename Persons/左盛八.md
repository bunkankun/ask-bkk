**Zuǒ Shèngbā** 左盛八 (*alias* 左修之 Zuǒ Xiūzhī, late Qīng / Republican period), Guìlín local figure named by [[羅哲初]] Luó Zhéchū as his teacher and source of the *Guìlín gǔběn* 桂林古本 manuscript of the *Shānghán zábìng lùn* ([[KR3ef002]]). Said in the transmission account to be the 46th-generation descendant of Zhāng Jī through the unbroken Zhāng-clan medical line of Guìlín. No independent biographical attestation outside Luó's preface.
