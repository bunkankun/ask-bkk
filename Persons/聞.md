# Wén 聞

Late-Míng Chán monk; disciple of [[深有|Wúniàn Shēnyǒu]] 無念深有 (1544–1627) at Huángbòshān 黃蘗山. Lifedates, native place, and full monastic name all unrecorded. Identified in the Kanripo catalog for the *Huángbò Wúniàn chánshī fù wèn* [[KR6q0186]] by the single character 聞 as *shān dìng* 刪定 ("culling and fixing") editor of the text — presumably the full monastic name, or an abbreviation thereof, would have been legible in a fuller preserved form of the colophon. The present person-note is a placeholder pending identification of his full name.
