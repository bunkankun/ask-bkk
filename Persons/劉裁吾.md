Republican-era physician, otherwise undocumented. Active *fl.* 1912 (preface dating); writes from his studio *Yǎng-shēng yuán* 養生園 in a Húnán / Húběi locality (the same county as Chén Bǎo 陳寶 and Xiè Yìnán 謝邑南, who wrote co-prefaces). No CBDB record.

Liú's preserved works:

1. *Zhì-nüè jī-yào* 治瘧機要 ([[KR3eh049]], 4 *juǎn*, 1912), a specialised treatise on *nüè* 瘧 (malaria-like fevers) arguing that *nüè* is fundamentally a *yíng-wèi* 榮衛 disorder rather than a six-channel disease, in continuity with the late-Míng *Jiè-nüè lùn shū* 痎瘧論疏 of [[盧之頤]] Lú Zhīyí. Engages biomedical quinine therapy and finds it inadequate.
2. *Jìng-bìng yǔ nǎo-mó-yán quán-shū* 痙病與腦膜炎全書 ([[KR3eg044]], "Complete Book on Convulsive Diseases and Meningitis"), a Sino-Western integrationist work that maps the indigenous *jìng-bìng* 痙病 (convulsive-disease) category onto the Western diagnostic category of meningitis.

The two works together place Liú in the broader Republican-era project of *zhōng-xī huì-tōng* 中西匯通 (Sino-Western synthesis) — translating Chinese-medical disease categories into Western clinical-diagnostic vocabulary while maintaining Chinese-medical pharmacology as the principal therapeutic instrument.
