Wáng Shǎng 王賞 (1103–1149), Southern-Sòng court historian and *Shílù xiūzhuàn* 實錄修撰 ("Veritable-Records Compiler") of the *Shàoxīng* 紹興 reign of Sòng Gāozōng 高宗, native of Méizhōu 眉州 (modern Méishān 眉山, Sìchuān). Father of the historian Wáng Chèn 王稱 (see [[王稱]]), to whom he transmitted the family's accumulated archival materials. CBDB id 1863, recording death year 1149 with first office (FL year) 1103. Mentioned in the *Sòngshǐ* and the Sìkù *tíyào* of his son's *Dōngdū shìlüè* ([[KR2d0007]]); his own writings have not survived as discrete works.
