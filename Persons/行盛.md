Běnchōng Xíngshèng 本充行盛 (b. early 17th century, d. 1671/2; birth year unrecorded), Qīng Línjì 臨濟 Yángqí-branch 楊岐派 Chán monk. *Zì* Běnchōng 本充; *hào* Yòuwēng 褎翁 and Nánhú lǎorén 南湖老人. Native of Quánzhōu fǔ 泉州府 (Fújiàn), lay surname Wú 吳.

Ordained at 28; studied under Jīnsù Róng (= [[通容|Fèiyǐn Tōngróng]] 費隱通容, 1593–1661) at Jīnsù shān 金粟山 and attained a sudden awakening and dharma-transmission. Became the 32nd-generation orthodox-Línjì patriarch (*Línjì zhèng zōng* dì sānshí'èr shì) and abbot of the Huìyún sì 慧雲寺.

Authored the *Chánlín bǎoxùn niān sòng* 禪林寶訓拈頌 ([[KR6q0155]], X64 n1264) during the Shùnzhì period, selecting 42 passages from the *Chánlín bǎoxùn* ([[KR6q0099]]) and composing 74 verses of commentary. His disciple Chāojì 超記 recorded the verses.

Also wrote prefaces for the *Chánlín bǎoxùn hé zhù* ([[KR6q0154]]) and the *Guān wúliàngshòu fó jīng tú sòng* 觀無量壽佛經圖頌.

Dharma-heirs include Jīxiōng Nián 機玄年, Kēyuè Liǎn 珂月璉, Yīzhēn Ēn 一真恩.

Died Kāngxī 10–11 (*xīnhài* 辛亥 = 1671 / early 1672), death date between 9 February 1671 and 29 January 1672.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0155]] *Chánlín bǎoxùn niān sòng* only.
