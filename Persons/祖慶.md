# Zǔ-qìng 祖慶 (Yún-ān Zǔ-qìng 雲庵祖慶)

Southern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Yún-ān 雲庵 ("Cloud-Hermitage"). Also Jìng-shān Zǔ-qìng 徑山祖慶 (for his Jìng-shān 徑山 abbacy). Lifedates unrecorded; active 1190 to post-1257. Seventeenth-generation Línjì descendant (*Dà-jiàn xià dì shí qī shì* 大鑑下第十七世).

Dharma-heir of **[[宗杲|Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo]]** 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163). Abbacies at Nán-yuán 南源, Dào-lín 道林, Zhōng-shān 鍾山, and Wú-jiāng Shèng-shòu 吳江聖壽 (from Bǎo-yòu 5 = 1257). Wrote the colophon to the *Pǔ-jué Zōng-gǎo chánshī yǔlù* in Shào-xī 1 = 1190.

Principal surviving Kanripo-canonical contribution: the *Niān bā fāng zhū yù jí* 拈八方珠玉集 [[KR6q0257]] — his re-edited three-juan *niān gǔ* anthology gathering prose-commentaries from across the Chán tradition.

Sources: *Jǐng-dé chuán dēng lù* 景德傳燈錄 juan continuations; *T51n2077_p0685b29*.
