# Zǔqìng 祖慶 (Yúnān Zǔqìng 雲庵祖慶)

Southern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Yúnān 雲庵 ("Cloud-Hermitage"). Also Jìngshān Zǔqìng 徑山祖慶 (for his Jìngshān 徑山 abbacy). Lifedates unrecorded; active 1190 to post-1257. Seventeenth-generation Línjì descendant (*Dàjiàn xià dì shí qī shì* 大鑑下第十七世).

Dharma-heir of **[[宗杲|Dàhuì Zōnggǎo]]** 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163). Abbacies at Nányuán 南源, Dàolín 道林, Zhōngshān 鍾山, and Wújiāng Shèngshòu 吳江聖壽 (from Bǎoyòu 5 = 1257). Wrote the colophon to the *Pǔjué Zōnggǎo chánshī yǔlù* in Shàoxī 1 = 1190.

Principal surviving Kanripo-canonical contribution: the *Niān bā fāng zhū yù jí* 拈八方珠玉集 [[KR6q0257]] — his re-edited three-juan *niān gǔ* anthology gathering prose-commentaries from across the Chán tradition.

Sources: *Jǐngdé chuán dēng lù* 景德傳燈錄 juan continuations; *T51n2077_p0685b29*.
