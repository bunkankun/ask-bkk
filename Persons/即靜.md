# Sokujō 即靜

Medieval Japanese Risshū (律宗) Vinaya scholar. Compiler of the analytical divisions (分會 *fēnhuì*) for the Tang–Sòng composite *Sìfēn lǜ hánzhù jièběn shū xíngzōng jì* 四分律含注戒本疏行宗記 ([[KR6k0146]]) combining Dàoxuān's parent commentary with Yuánzhào's sub-commentary, adapted for Japanese instructional use.
