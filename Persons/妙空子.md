# Miàokōngzǐ 妙空子

A late-Qīng lay-Buddhist (*jūshì* 居士) Pure Land scholar active in the Tóngzhì 同治 era (1862–1874). The studio designation *Bǎichéng yānshuǐ zhī jū* 百城煙水之居 ("Hundred-Cities-Mist-and-Water Dwelling") that appears in the colophon of his principal work suggests an itinerant or scholarly self-conception drawing on the *Huáyán* 華嚴 imagery of Sudhana's pilgrimage through one hundred and ten cities. Author of the **《蓮邦消息》** *Liánbāng xiāoxī* [[KR6p0112]] (X1193) — a doctrinal Pure Land treatise organised in ten *pǐn* (chapters), framing the *zhēnxīn běntǐ* / *yīniàn wúmíng* dialectic of the *Qǐxìn lùn* into a soteriological account of *niànfó* practice. Lifedates and lay name not preserved.
