# Miào-kōng-zǐ 妙空子

A late-Qīng lay-Buddhist (*jū-shì* 居士) Pure Land scholar active in the Tóng-zhì 同治 era (1862–1874). The studio designation *Bǎi-chéng yān-shuǐ zhī jū* 百城煙水之居 ("Hundred-Cities-Mist-and-Water Dwelling") that appears in the colophon of his principal work suggests an itinerant or scholarly self-conception drawing on the *Huá-yán* 華嚴 imagery of Sudhana's pilgrimage through one hundred and ten cities. Author of the **《蓮邦消息》** *Lián-bāng xiāo-xī* [[KR6p0112]] (X1193) — a doctrinal Pure Land treatise organised in ten *pǐn* (chapters), framing the *zhēn-xīn běn-tǐ* / *yī-niàn wú-míng* dialectic of the *Qǐ-xìn lùn* into a soteriological account of *niàn-fó* practice. Lifedates and lay name not preserved.
