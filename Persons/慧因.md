Huì Yīn 慧因 (fl. Sòng) was a Sòng-dynasty Buddhist scholar who composed the **Fànwǎng-jīng púsà-jiè zhù** 梵網經菩薩戒注 ([[KR6k0094]]) — a three-fascicle commentary on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts. The work is part of the Sòng-period revival of Vinaya scholarship that included Yuán-zhào's recovery of the Nánshān tradition.
