Shǐ Shéngzǔ 史繩祖 (*fl.* mid-thirteenth century), *zì* Qìng-cháng 慶長, of Méishān 眉山 in Méi-zhōu (Sìchuān). Disciple of Wèi Liǎowēng 魏了翁 (1178–1237), the great late-Sòng *Lǐ*-school philologist, whose *Lóng-shān jí* 龍山集 carries a colophon (*Tí Shǐ Shéngzǔ Kǎo jīng* 題史繩祖考經) for Shǐ's earlier and now lost *Kǎo jīng* 考經 collection. The dated preface (*Chúnyòu gēng-xū*, 1250) of his *Xué-zhāi zhān-bì* 學齋佔畢 ([[KR3j0052]]) is signed at the *Zǐ-cáo Jí-táng* 梓漕極堂, suggesting service in the Zǐzhōu fiscal circuit; Yáng Fāng's 楊枋 *Zì-xī jí* 字溪集 preserves a mourning poem in which he is styled *Cháo-qǐng dà-fū, zhí Huànzhāng gé, zhǔ-guǎn Chéngdū-fǔ Yùjú-guān* — a final retirement post as Daoist-temple superintendent in Chéngdū. The "*Qí-jùn* 齊郡 Shǐ" of the styling refers to the lineage's ancestral home, not his actual residence. CBDB id 10662 carries no dates.
