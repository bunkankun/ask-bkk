# Lǐ Zūnxù 李遵勗

*Zì* Gōngwǔ 公武. Northern Sòng lay Chán patron and imperial in-law (*fùmǎ dūwèi* 駙馬都尉), husband of a sister of the empress of Sòng Zhēnzōng 真宗. Native of Shàngdǎng 上黨 (modern Chángzhì 長治, Shānxī). Substantial lay practitioner in the Línjì 臨濟 line; studied with Gǔshān Shíjiǎn 谷山石鑑 and Gūyún Yuánwén 孤雲元文. Biography in *Sòng shǐ* 宋史 j. 464.

Compiler of the *Tiānshèng guǎngdēng lù* 天聖廣燈錄 ([[KR6q0004]]), 30 juan, presented to Sòng Rénzōng 仁宗 in 1029 and printed in 1036 — one of the five principal Sòng Chán lamp records and a rare case of lay-compiled Chán canonical historiography. Also credited with various occasional pieces on Chán matters preserved in later anthologies.

The name is sometimes written 李遵勖 in older sources; the two graphs 勗 and 勖 are variant forms of the same character.
