# Zhòngjiè 仲介

Northern-Sòng Chán disciple (*ménrén* 門人) of Huánglóng Bǎojué Zǔxīn 黃龍寶覺祖心. Re-editor (*chóng biān* 重編) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0278]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
