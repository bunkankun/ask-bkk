# Zhòng-jiè 仲介

Northern-Sòng Chán disciple (*mén-rén* 門人) of Huánglóng Bǎo-jué Zǔ-xīn 黃龍寶覺祖心. Re-editor (*chóng biān* 重編) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0278]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
