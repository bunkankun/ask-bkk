# Gāo Zhòngwǔ 高仲武 (fl. ca. 785)

Native of Bóhǎi 渤海 (Hé-běi). An obscure mid-Táng anthologist of the *Zhēn-yuán* era (785–805) about whom virtually nothing else is known. He is the compiler of the *Zhōngxìng jiānqì jí* 中興間氣集 [[KR4h0012]], a two-*juǎn* anthology of *Dà-lì* (766–779) poetry selecting 134 poems by 26 poets, each prefaced by a *píng-yǔ* 評語 in the style established by Yīn Pán [[殷璠]]. The preface is dated 785 (or, in some readings, *Zhēn-yuán* 9 = 793). His career and dates are not recoverable. No CBDB entry.
