Contemporary Chinese specialist of Dūnhuáng manuscripts and Yogācāra (*Wéishí* 唯識) literature, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of *Dàfāngguǎng Huáyán shí'è pǐn jīng* ([[KR6v0014]]) and of *Yújiā lùn juǎn dì shísì shǒu jì* 瑜伽論卷第十四手記 ([[KR6v0037]]). His editions are notable for thorough collation across the Beijing Library and British Library Dūnhuáng holdings.
