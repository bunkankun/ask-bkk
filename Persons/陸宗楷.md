# Lù Zōngkǎi 陸宗楷 (?–1773)

Qing scholar-official; native of Pínghú 平湖, Zhèjiāng. *Jìnshì* of Yōngzhèng 1 (1723). Held the offices of *Hànlínyuàn shìjiǎngxuéshì* 翰林院侍講學士 and *zhāolùnguān* 詔論官 under the Qiánlóng emperor.

In the *Sìkù quánshū* compilation Lù Zōngkǎi appears as the named *kǎozhèng* 考證 author appended to several of the *Sìshū* and *Wǔjīng* editions in the *jīngbù* 經部. His extant attestations in Kanripo are the *kǎozhèng* appendices to:
- [[KR1h0003]] *Mèngzǐ zhùshū* 孟子注疏 — *kǎozhèng* appendix.
- [[KR1h0007]] *Lúnyǔ zhùshū* 論語注疏 — *kǎozhèng* appendix (with [[呂熾]]).

The *kǎozhèng* genre, attached at imperial direction to the WYG copies, contains note-form remarks on textual variants, citation errors, and (where applicable) suggested emendations against earlier *zhùshū* editions; in volume it is small but it represents the *Sìkù*'s in-house philological labour.
