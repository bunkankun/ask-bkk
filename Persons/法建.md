# Fǎjiàn 法建

Late-Táng Chán monk styled Dàzhōu 大舟; disciple of [[裴休]] Péi Xiū to whom Péi entrusted his recorded Huángbò teachings (the *Chuánxīn fǎyào* / *Wǎnlíng lù* compilation) for verification by the elder monks of the Guǎngtáng sì 廣唐寺.
