**Tán Chǔduān** 譚處端 (1123–1185), *zì* Tōng zhèng 通正, *hào* Cháng zhēn zǐ 長真子, was **one of the Seven Perfected of Quán zhēn**, founder of the **Nán wú** 南無 branch. Author of [[KR5e0062]] *Shuǐ yún jí* 水雲集 (dated 1229 — posthumous publication).
