# Xiàng Míngdá 項名達

Style name *Méilǚ* 梅侶. Native of Rénhé 仁和 (modern Hángzhōu 杭州, Zhèjiāng). Born Qiánlóng 54 (1789); died Dàoguāng 30 (1850). *Jǔrén* in Dàoguāng 5 (1825); served briefly as district magistrate. CBDB c_personid 84043 confirms the lifedates.

The leading mathematician of the ZhèjiāngHángzhōu mathematical milieu of the second quarter of the nineteenth century, and one of the principal extenders of the Míng Āntú 明安圖 cyclotomic-series tradition. Xiàng Míngdá's mathematical work is concentrated on (a) the systematic theoretical extension of the cyclotomic infinite-series tradition initiated by [[明安圖]] Míng Āntú's *Gēyuán mìlǜ jiéfǎ* 割圈密率捷法 and continued by [[董佑誠]] Dǒng Yòuchéng's [[KR3fc068]] *Tújiě*; and (b) the procedural reformulation of the *gōugǔ* 勾股 (right-triangle) and *sānjiǎo* 三角 (triangle) problem-types.

His principal works preserved in the KR3fc series:

(1) **[[KR3fc069]] *Xiàngshù yīyuán* 象数一原** in 9 *juàn* — the principal theoretical synthesis of the cyclotomic-series tradition. Posthumously completed and corrected by his student [[戴煦]] Dài Xù.

(2) **[[KR3fc070]] *Gōugǔ liùshù* 勾股六術** in 2 *juàn* — systematic six-procedure exposition of the right-triangle problem-types.

(3) **[[KR3fc071]] *Sānjiǎo héjiào shù* 三角和較術** in 4 *juàn* — systematic exposition of the *sum-and-difference* methods in triangle geometry, working at the interface of indigenous *gōugǔ* and European spherical-and-plane trigonometry.

(4) **[[KR3fc072]] *Kāi zhūchéngfāng jiéshù* 開諸乘方捷術** — rapid procedure for higher-order root extraction.

Xiàng Míngdá's mathematical correspondence with [[戴煦]] Dài Xù is the principal documentary record of the early-Dào-guāng Zhèjiāng mathematical scene. After Xiàng Míngdá's death in 1850, Dài Xù completed the *Xiàngshù yīyuán* and ensured its publication. The Xiàng – Dài lineage is the principal Zhèjiāng counterpart to the Chángzhōu mathematical school of [[董佑誠]] Dǒng Yòuchéng and [[李善蘭]] Lǐ Shànlán.
