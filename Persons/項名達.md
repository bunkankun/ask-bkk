# Xiàng Míngdá 項名達

Style name *Méilǚ* 梅侶. Native of Rén-hé 仁和 (modern Háng-zhōu 杭州, Zhè-jiāng). Born Qián-lóng 54 (1789); died Dào-guāng 30 (1850). *Jǔ-rén* in Dào-guāng 5 (1825); served briefly as district magistrate. CBDB c_personid 84043 confirms the lifedates.

The leading mathematician of the Zhè-jiāng-Háng-zhōu mathematical milieu of the second quarter of the nineteenth century, and one of the principal extenders of the Míng Āntú 明安圖 cyclotomic-series tradition. Xiàng Míngdá's mathematical work is concentrated on (a) the systematic theoretical extension of the cyclotomic infinite-series tradition initiated by [[明安圖]] Míng Āntú's *Gē-yuán mì-lǜ jié-fǎ* 割圈密率捷法 and continued by [[董佑誠]] Dǒng Yòuchéng's [[KR3fc068]] *Tú-jiě*; and (b) the procedural reformulation of the *gōu-gǔ* 勾股 (right-triangle) and *sān-jiǎo* 三角 (triangle) problem-types.

His principal works preserved in the KR3fc series:

(1) **[[KR3fc069]] *Xiàng-shù yī-yuán* 象数一原** in 9 *juàn* — the principal theoretical synthesis of the cyclotomic-series tradition. Posthumously completed and corrected by his student [[戴煦]] Dài Xù.

(2) **[[KR3fc070]] *Gōu-gǔ liù-shù* 勾股六術** in 2 *juàn* — systematic six-procedure exposition of the right-triangle problem-types.

(3) **[[KR3fc071]] *Sān-jiǎo hé-jiào shù* 三角和較術** in 4 *juàn* — systematic exposition of the *sum-and-difference* methods in triangle geometry, working at the interface of indigenous *gōu-gǔ* and European spherical-and-plane trigonometry.

(4) **[[KR3fc072]] *Kāi zhū-chéng-fāng jié-shù* 開諸乘方捷術** — rapid procedure for higher-order root extraction.

Xiàng Míngdá's mathematical correspondence with [[戴煦]] Dài Xù is the principal documentary record of the early-Dào-guāng Zhè-jiāng mathematical scene. After Xiàng Míngdá's death in 1850, Dài Xù completed the *Xiàng-shù yī-yuán* and ensured its publication. The Xiàng – Dài lineage is the principal Zhè-jiāng counterpart to the Cháng-zhōu mathematical school of [[董佑誠]] Dǒng Yòuchéng and [[李善蘭]] Lǐ Shànlán.
