**Takashima Hisaya** 高島久也 (*imina* Sukekei 祜啟), early-Meiji Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician of the Hakusaidō 博濟堂 beriberi hospital cohort. Signed the postscript to [[KR3eh054]] *Kakke gairon* 腳氣概論 (1879), in which he records his own European travels and his observation that beriberi was unknown to European clinicians at the time. To be distinguished from his elder near-namesake [[高島久貫]] Takashima Hisanuki, author of the cholera tract *Sha-eki shinron* 瀉疫新論 ([[KR3eg045]]). No further biographical material located.
