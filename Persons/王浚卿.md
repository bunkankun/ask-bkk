**Wáng Jùnqīng** 王浚卿, late-Qīng fiction writer, author of the social novel *Lěngyǎn Guān* 冷眼觀 [[KR4k0172]]. No reliable biographical details have been located. The CBDB does not contain a matching entry for this name at dates consistent with a late-Qīng authorship. The pen name or courtesy name Jùnqīng may be a *hào* (literary sobriquet) rather than a formal name, which has complicated identification. He belongs to the generation of late Guangxu–Xuantong era fiction writers who produced social satire in the tradition of the "four great denunciation novels" (*wǎn Qīng sì dà qiǎnzé xiǎoshuō* 晚清四大譴責小說).
