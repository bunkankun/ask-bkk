**Chén Zhòng-yuǎn** 陳仲遠 (fl. mid-late 18th century), Daoist priest (*yǔ-kè* 羽客) of **Yún-fēng** 雲峯 in **Wǔ-yáng** 武陽 (Sì-chuān). Compiler-editor of two important DZJY items: [[KR5i0094]] *Yǎ yí jí* (preface dated 1779 by Zhāng Xiǎn) and [[KR5i0106]] *Qīng xuán jì liàn tiě guàn hú shí* (= the *Guǎng chéng yí zhì tiě guàn hú shí quán jí*) — i.e., the standard liturgical compendium for the *jì liàn* 濟煉 (salvation-refinement) ritual of feeding-the-hungry-ghosts in the *Guǎng chéng yí zhì* codification of late-imperial Sì-chuān Daoism. Chén's editorial work is the principal Sì-chuān contribution to the post-1809 DZJY supplement, and represents the active *cóng-lín* practice of Sì-chuān Lóng-mén Daoism in the late-Qián-lóng era. Standard reference: Olles, *Ritual Words* (Harrassowitz 2013) on Sì-chuān late-imperial Daoist ritual texts.
