**Chén Zhòngyuǎn** 陳仲遠 (fl. mid-late 18th century), Daoist priest (*yǔkè* 羽客) of **Yúnfēng** 雲峯 in **Wǔyáng** 武陽 (Sìchuān). Compiler-editor of two important DZJY items: [[KR5i0094]] *Yǎ yí jí* (preface dated 1779 by Zhāng Xiǎn) and [[KR5i0106]] *Qīng xuán jì liàn tiě guàn hú shí* (= the *Guǎng chéng yí zhì tiě guàn hú shí quán jí*) — i.e., the standard liturgical compendium for the *jì liàn* 濟煉 (salvation-refinement) ritual of feeding-the-hungry-ghosts in the *Guǎng chéng yí zhì* codification of late-imperial Sìchuān Daoism. Chén's editorial work is the principal Sìchuān contribution to the post-1809 DZJY supplement, and represents the active *cónglín* practice of Sìchuān Lóngmén Daoism in the late-Qián-lóng era. Standard reference: Olles, *Ritual Words* (Harrassowitz 2013) on Sìchuān late-imperial Daoist ritual texts.
