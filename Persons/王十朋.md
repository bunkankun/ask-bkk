Wáng Shí-péng 王十朋 (1112–1171), *zì* Guī-líng 龜齡, *hào* Méixī 梅溪, posthumous title Zhōng-wén 忠文; native of Lè-qīng 樂清 in Wēn-zhōu (modern Zhèjiāng). Famous Southern-Sòng official, poet, and *Chūn-qiū* scholar. Optimus (*zhuàng-yuán*) of *Shào-xīng* 27 (1157) at age 46. A close associate of Zhāng Jùn 張浚 in the war-party and one of the most outspoken Southern-Sòng critics of Qín Guì. Held senior posts as *qiān-pàn* of Shào-xīng (1163–1166), Prefect of Hú-zhōu, of Quán-zhōu, of Sì-zhōu, of Kuí-zhōu (1167–1168), of Hú-zhōu again, and as *Lóng-tú-gé xué-shì*. Author of the *Méixī xiānsheng wén-jí* 梅溪先生文集 ([[KR4d0121]]), the *Méixī wáng xiānsheng *Chūn-qiū* xiángshuō* 梅溪王先生春秋詳說 (lost), and the *Kuài-jī sān-fù* 會稽三賦 ([[KR2k0113]]). His *Bǎi-jiā zhù-bīn Dù Gōng-bù shī jí* 百家注分類補注李太白詩集 was a major contribution to Tang-poetry annotation. Biography in *Sòng-shǐ* 387. CBDB id 10598.
