Wáng Shípéng 王十朋 (1112–1171), *zì* Guīlíng 龜齡, *hào* Méixī 梅溪, posthumous title Zhōngwén 忠文; native of Lèqīng 樂清 in Wēnzhōu (modern Zhèjiāng). Famous Southern-Sòng official, poet, and *Chūnqiū* scholar. Optimus (*zhuàngyuán*) of *Shàoxīng* 27 (1157) at age 46. A close associate of Zhāng Jùn 張浚 in the war-party and one of the most outspoken Southern-Sòng critics of Qín Guì. Held senior posts as *qiānpàn* of Shàoxīng (1163–1166), Prefect of Húzhōu, of Quánzhōu, of Sìzhōu, of Kuízhōu (1167–1168), of Húzhōu again, and as *Lóngtúgé xuéshì*. Author of the *Méixī xiānsheng wénjí* 梅溪先生文集 ([[KR4d0121]]), the *Méixī wáng xiānsheng *Chūnqiū* xiángshuō* 梅溪王先生春秋詳說 (lost), and the *Kuàijī sānfù* 會稽三賦 ([[KR2k0113]]). His *Bǎijiā zhùbīn Dù Gōngbù shī jí* 百家注分類補注李太白詩集 was a major contribution to Tang-poetry annotation. Biography in *Sòngshǐ* 387. CBDB id 10598.
