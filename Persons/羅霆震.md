**Luó Tíngzhèn** 羅霆震 was a Yuán Daoist author, compiler of [[KR5c0363]] *Wǔ dāng jì shèng jí* 武當紀勝集 — a verse-collection on the scenic and sacred sites of **Wǔ dāng shān** 武當山.
