**Tóng Nánhéng** 童南恆 of Wǔlín 武林 (Hángzhōu), late-Míng figure whose case of brain abscess and death after self-administration of *fùzǐ* 附子 (aconite) and warming-drug regimen is preserved as a cautionary tale in [[KR3eq020]] *Shàngchí záshuō* of [[馮時可]] Féng Shíkě.
