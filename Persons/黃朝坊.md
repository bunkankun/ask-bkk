**Huáng Cháofāng** 黃朝坊 (*fl.* late Qīng), physician of the classical-formula-school (*jīngfāngpài*) lineage. Author of the medical compendium *Jīnguì qǐyuè* 金匱啟鑰 ("Opening the Lock of the Golden Cabinet"), of which the gynecological section is preserved as [[KR3ei038]]. The work belongs to the late-Qīng *wēnbǔ* (warming-tonifying) tendency in gynecology and is polemically opposed to routine prescription of *biǎo*-resolving formulae (*Wǔjī sǎn*, *Jiǔwèi qiānghuó tāng*) for cold-syndrome conditions.
