**Huáng Cháofāng** 黃朝坊 (*fl.* late Qīng), physician of the classical-formula-school (*jīng-fāng-pài*) lineage. Author of the medical compendium *Jīn-guì qǐ-yuè* 金匱啟鑰 ("Opening the Lock of the Golden Cabinet"), of which the gynecological section is preserved as [[KR3ei038]]. The work belongs to the late-Qīng *wēn-bǔ* (warming-tonifying) tendency in gynecology and is polemically opposed to routine prescription of *biǎo*-resolving formulae (*Wǔ-jī sǎn*, *Jiǔ-wèi qiāng-huó tāng*) for cold-syndrome conditions.
