Xú Shù-gǔ 徐樹穀 (*zì* Yì-chū 藝初), of *Kūn-shān* 崑山 (Jiāng-sū). *Kāng-xī yǐ-chǒu* (1685) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Shān-dōng dào jiān-chá yù-shǐ* (Investigating Censor for the *Shān-dōng* Circuit). Brother of **Xú Jiǒng** 徐炯 [[徐炯]] (*Kāng-xī rén-xū* 1682 *jìn-shì*).

Xú Shù-gǔ's principal work in the corpus is the *jiān* (philological gloss) component of the *Lǐ Yì-shān wén-jí jiān-zhù* [[KR4c0076]] — the Xú brothers' Qīng critical edition of **Lǐ Shāng-yǐn**'s [[李商隱]] prose, with Xú Jiǒng providing the *zhù* (commentary). Xú Shù-gǔ's *jiān* attended to historical-event verification, while his brother handled allusion-tracing.

The collaboration produced one of the principal works in the Qīng recovery of *Wǎn-Táng* prose. Subsequent **Féng Hào** 馮浩 (mid-Qián-lóng) corrected several attributional and interpretive errors in the Xú edition, but kept it as the base text.
