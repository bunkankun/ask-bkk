Xú Shùgǔ 徐樹穀 (*zì* Yìchū 藝初), of *Kūnshān* 崑山 (Jiāngsū). *Kāngxī yǐchǒu* (1685) *jìnshì*; rose to *Shāndōng dào jiānchá yùshǐ* (Investigating Censor for the *Shāndōng* Circuit). Brother of **Xú Jiǒng** 徐炯 [[徐炯]] (*Kāngxī rénxū* 1682 *jìnshì*).

Xú Shùgǔ's principal work in the corpus is the *jiān* (philological gloss) component of the *Lǐ Yìshān wénjí jiānzhù* [[KR4c0076]] — the Xú brothers' Qīng critical edition of **Lǐ Shāngyǐn**'s [[李商隱]] prose, with Xú Jiǒng providing the *zhù* (commentary). Xú Shùgǔ's *jiān* attended to historical-event verification, while his brother handled allusion-tracing.

The collaboration produced one of the principal works in the Qīng recovery of *WǎnTáng* prose. Subsequent **Féng Hào** 馮浩 (mid-Qián-lóng) corrected several attributional and interpretive errors in the Xú edition, but kept it as the base text.
