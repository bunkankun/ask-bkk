Zhāng Shìpèi 張士佩 (1531–1609), Wàn-lì-era *Sìchuān xúnfǔ* (Sichuan Governor) and editor of the definitive 81-*juǎn* recension of **Yáng Shèn** ([[楊愼]])'s *Shēngān jí* ([[KR4e0171]]). The compilation was formed by **selecting from** Yáng's enormous *bǐjì* corpus (*Dānqiān lù*, *Tányuàn tíhú* and others) plus the *biéjí*: 11 *juǎn* of *fù* and miscellaneous prose; 29 *juǎn* of poetry; 41 *juǎn* of *zájì* (selected miscellaneous-notes). CBDB id 130320, 1531–1609.
