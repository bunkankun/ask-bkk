Zhāng Shì-pèi 張士佩 (1531–1609), Wàn-lì-era *Sì-chuān xún-fǔ* (Sichuan Governor) and editor of the definitive 81-*juǎn* recension of **Yáng Shèn** ([[楊愼]])'s *Shēng-ān jí* ([[KR4e0171]]). The compilation was formed by **selecting from** Yáng's enormous *bǐ-jì* corpus (*Dān-qiān lù*, *Tán-yuàn tí-hú* and others) plus the *bié-jí*: 11 *juǎn* of *fù* and miscellaneous prose; 29 *juǎn* of poetry; 41 *juǎn* of *zá-jì* (selected miscellaneous-notes). CBDB id 130320, 1531–1609.
