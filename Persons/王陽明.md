**Wáng Yángmíng** 王陽明 (1472–1529, Míng). The principal Míng-period philosopher of the *xīnxué* "School of Mind"; demoted to Guìzhōu in 1506 and active in Jiāngxī during the *Zhèngdé* era contemporaneous with [[王蔡]]'s *Xiūzhēn mìyào*.
