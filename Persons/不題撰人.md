**Bù Tí Zhuànrén** 不題撰人 ("Author not named") is a conventional catalog designation for an anonymous author in Qīng-dynasty popular fiction. The *Jīnshí Yuán* 金石緣 ([[KR4k0154]]) provides no identifying information about its author in the preface or body text.
