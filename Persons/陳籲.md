Chén Yù 陳籲 (Yuán, fl. mid-14th c.), son of Chén Lǚ [[陳旅]] and editor of his father's *Ān-yǎ-táng jí* 安雅堂集 [[KR4d0516]]. Two preface dates (1349 by Zhāng Zhù 張翥 [[張翥]], 1351 by Lín Quán-shēng 林泉生) fix his editorial activity to the period 1349–1351. No separate biographical material located.
