# 虞氏 Yúshì

**Yúshì** 虞氏 ("Mr. Yú"), Táng-era commentator on the *Nànjīng* 難經; cited in [[楊繼洲]] Yáng Jìzhōu's *Zhēndào yuánliú* essay in the *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng* ([[KR3ee027]]). The commentary is lost; personal name otherwise unknown.
