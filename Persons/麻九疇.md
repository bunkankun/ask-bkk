Má Jiǔchóu 麻九疇 (*zì* Zhījī 知幾; 1183–1232) was a late-Jīn polymath, *jìnshì* 1226, scholar, mathematician, and physician. He and his circle preserved the *Sānxiāo lùn* ([[KR3eh047]]) of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù; the surviving textual witness was prepared from manuscripts obtained via Má from Liú's descendants.
