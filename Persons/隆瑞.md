# Lóngruì 隆瑞

Late-Míng *bhikṣu* of *Huángbòshān Wànfúsì* 黃檗山萬福寺 in Fújiàn. Recorded as the final compiler / printer of [[性祇]] Xìng Qí's *Píní rìyòng lù* 毗尼日用錄 ([[KR6k0258]], X1114) in *Chóngzhēn* 崇禎 *guǐyǒu* 癸酉 (1633). In *Chóngzhēn* 2 (1629) Lóngruì was instrumental in bringing [[圓悟]] Mìyún Yuánwù (1567–1642) to the abbacy of Huángbò, locating his career in the late-Míng Línji-Vinaya integrative milieu that subsequently produced the *Ōbakushū* 黃檗宗 emigration to Japan. Per DILA A003779.
