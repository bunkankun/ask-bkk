Early-to-mid Ming physician of Qí-zhōu 蘄州 (Húběi); *zì* Yuè-chí 月池, *hào* Yuè-chí Wēng 月池翁. Father of Lǐ Shízhēn 李時珍. Held the office of *lǐ-mù* 吏目 in the Tài-yī Yuàn 太醫院 (Imperial Medical Academy) under the Jiājìng emperor. Active in the first two-thirds of the 16th century. Author of an eight-juan *Sì zhěn fā míng* 四診發明 (mentioned in Lǐ Shízhēn's [[KR3eb014]] preface but not preserved) and of [[KR3eb033]] *Mài xué lèi biān* / *Sì yán jǔ yào lèi biān* — a classified rearrangement of Cuī Jiāyàn's Southern Sòng pulse mnemonic. No CBDB record.
