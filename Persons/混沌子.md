**Hùn-dùn-zǐ** 混沌子 ("the Master of Primordial Chaos"), pseudonymous late-Míng inner-alchemical author. The sole transmitted attribution is to the *Jǐn-shēn jī-yào* 錦身機要 ([[KR3eo010]]). The sobriquet is drawn from the *Zhuāng-zǐ* 應帝王 fable of Emperor Hùn-dùn and is a recurrent Daoist self-presentation across the Yuán-Míng inner-alchemical literature; the present author's true identity is unrecovered. The work's doctrinal alignment with the Lǚ Dòng-bīn 呂洞賓 transmission and its *xìng-mìng shuāng-xiū* exclusivism place the author in the late-Míng (15th–early 17th c.) popular-Daoist milieu.
