**Xú Yòngchéng** 徐用誠 (*zì* Yànchún 彥純, *fl.* late Yuán to early Míng), Yuán-to-early-Míng physician of Huìjī 會稽 (modern Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). A *sīshū* 私淑 (private-disciple, by reading) of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng's Yīn-deficiency school. Author of the original ***Yī xué zhézhōng*** 醫學折衷 — a 17-category clinical treatise — which was later expanded by Liú Chún 劉純 into the ***Yùjī wēiyì*** 玉機微義 ([[KR3e0068]], 50 juan, 50 categories). Yáng Shìqí's preface to the work locates the school of Xú Yòngchéng and Liú Chún in the *Northern* Zhāng Yuánsù → Lǐ Gǎo → Wáng Hǎogǔ transmission and the *Southern* Zhū Zhènhēng line — a syncretic placement the SKQS editors note as inaccurate (Xú and Liú are clearly Dān-xī-school disciples, not heirs of the Yìshuǐ / Pí-Wèi school).
