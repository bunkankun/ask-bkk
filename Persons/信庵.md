# Xìnān 信庵

A late-Qīng *Wǔlín* 武林 (Hangzhou) monk active in the late Tóngzhì 同治 / early Guāngxù 光緒 reigns. Author of the **《修西輯要》** *Xiūxī jí yào* [[KR6p0122]] (X1203), a single-*juǎn* Pure Land devotional anthology that selects and arranges the principal pastoral and doctrinal passages of the late-imperial Pure Land patristic tradition. The work was prefaced and edited for publication in *Guāngxù* 10 (1884) by his colleague **Běnpǔ** 本普, who frames Xìnān's editorial choices as foregrounding the *tālì* 他力 (other-power) doctrine of [[智顗]] **Tiāntái Zhìyǐ**'s *Shí yí lùn* [[KR6p0008]] over the more *zìlì* (self-power) Pure Land anthological alternatives. Lifedates not preserved.
