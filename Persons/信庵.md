# Xìn-ān 信庵

A late-Qīng *Wǔ-lín* 武林 (Hangzhou) monk active in the late Tóng-zhì 同治 / early Guāng-xù 光緒 reigns. Author of the **《修西輯要》** *Xiū-xī jí yào* [[KR6p0122]] (X1203), a single-*juǎn* Pure Land devotional anthology that selects and arranges the principal pastoral and doctrinal passages of the late-imperial Pure Land patristic tradition. The work was prefaced and edited for publication in *Guāng-xù* 10 (1884) by his colleague **Běn-pǔ** 本普, who frames Xìn-ān's editorial choices as foregrounding the *tā-lì* 他力 (other-power) doctrine of [[智顗]] **Tiāntái Zhì-yǐ**'s *Shí yí lùn* [[KR6p0008]] over the more *zì-lì* (self-power) Pure Land anthological alternatives. Lifedates not preserved.
