# 胡鑫

Hú Xīn, late-Qīng pupil of [[陳葆善]] Chén Bǎoshàn at the Wēnzhōu *Lǐjīng yīyuàn* 利濟醫院 and son-in-law of [[陳虯]] Chén Qiú. Recast Chén Bǎoshàn's manuscript into the fifteen *tiáobiàn* form of [[KR3em020]] *Báihóu tiáobiàn* 白喉條辨 (published 1898).
