# 袁枚

Yuán Méi 袁枚 (*zì* Jiǎnzhāi 簡齋, *hào* Suíyuán 隨園, 1716–1798), major Qīng poet, essayist, and literary critic of Qiántáng 錢塘. Author of the *Suíyuán shíhuà* 隨園詩話 and of the *Biography of Master Língtāi* (徐靈胎傳) attesting to Xú Dàchūn's clinical reputation.
