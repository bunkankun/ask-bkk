# 惟珪

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of Yuè-lín Shī-guān 月林師觀 (1143–1217); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0288]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
