Liú Yùsōn 劉玉蓀, Qīng-dynasty pupil of [[李光地]] Lǐ Guāngdì (1642–1718), known as one of the figures of the Lǐ Guāngdì school of *Yìxué* alongside [[楊名時]] Yáng Míngshí; specific biographical details not preserved in the *Sìkù* notice.
