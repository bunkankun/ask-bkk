**Zhōng Xīnqīng** 鍾心青 is a literary pseudonym used by the author of the late Qīng social fiction *Xīn Cháhuā* 新茶花 ([[KR4k0258]]), published by the Shēnjiāng Xiǎoshuō Shè 申江小說社 (Shanghai Fiction Press), ca. 1904–1907. The true identity of the author behind this pen name has not been definitively established in accessible scholarship. Zhōng Xīnqīng is also tentatively associated with a subsequent work on Chinese women's emancipation (*Wénmíng Zhī Guāng* 文明之光, ca. 1911). Biographical details — native place, examination history, official career — are unknown. No CBDB record identified.
