# Běn-guāng 本光

Late Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[如珙]] Héng-chuān Rú-gǒng (1222–1289); abbot of Yàndàng-shān Luóhàn chán-sì 鴈蕩山羅漢禪寺 and principal editor of his master's two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0344]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
