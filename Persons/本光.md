# Běnguāng 本光

Late Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[如珙]] Héngchuān Rúgǒng (1222–1289); abbot of Yàndàngshān Luóhàn chánsì 鴈蕩山羅漢禪寺 and principal editor of his master's two-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0344]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
