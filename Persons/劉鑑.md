Yuán-period (mid-14th-century) phonologist, *zì* Shìmíng 士明; self-styles as a man of Guānzhōng 闗中 — a region too large to identify the prefecture or county. *fl.* Yuántǒng era. Author of the *Jīngshǐ zhèngyīn qièyùn zhǐnán* 經史正音切韻指南 [[KR1j0067]] (1 *juàn*, completed Hòuzhìyuán 2 / *bǐngzǐ* / 10th month / 1336 — per the author's own preface). The work is the most influential of all post-Sòng *děngyùn* (phonological-grade chart) books: it adopts Sīmǎ Guāng's [[司馬光]] *Qièyùn zhǐzhǎngtú* [[KR1j0058]] as its base, supplemented by the *Sìshēng quánxíng děngzǐ* [[KR1j0066]] (anonymous Yuán), and adds the so-called "Wáng-Yúshī ménfǎ" 玉鑰匙門法 — a set of formal rules of *fǎnqiè* spelling — which became the standard pedagogical framework for *děngyùn* studies down to the Qīng. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that Liú Jiàn's later supplementary additions (the *Yùyàoshí* and the *Yúyú zhízhǐ ménfǎ*) are by the early-Míng monk Shì Zhēnkōng 釋真空 and later editors, not by Liú himself.
