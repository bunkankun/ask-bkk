Yuán-period (mid-14th-century) phonologist, *zì* Shì-míng 士明; self-styles as a man of Guān-zhōng 闗中 — a region too large to identify the prefecture or county. *fl.* Yuán-tǒng era. Author of the *Jīng-shǐ zhèng-yīn qiè-yùn zhǐ-nán* 經史正音切韻指南 [[KR1j0067]] (1 *juàn*, completed Hòu-zhì-yuán 2 / *bǐng-zǐ* / 10th month / 1336 — per the author's own preface). The work is the most influential of all post-Sòng *děng-yùn* (phonological-grade chart) books: it adopts Sī-mǎ Guāng's [[司馬光]] *Qiè-yùn zhǐ-zhǎng-tú* [[KR1j0058]] as its base, supplemented by the *Sì-shēng quán-xíng děng-zǐ* [[KR1j0066]] (anonymous Yuán), and adds the so-called "Wáng-Yú-shī mén-fǎ" 玉鑰匙門法 — a set of formal rules of *fǎn-qiè* spelling — which became the standard pedagogical framework for *děng-yùn* studies down to the Qīng. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that Liú Jiàn's later supplementary additions (the *Yù-yào-shí* and the *Yú-yú zhí-zhǐ mén-fǎ*) are by the early-Míng monk Shì Zhēn-kōng 釋真空 and later editors, not by Liú himself.
