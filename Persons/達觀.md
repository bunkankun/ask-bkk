# 達觀

Zǐbǎi Dáguān 紫柏達觀 (1543–1603, lay name Zhēnkě 真可), late-Míng Chán master. One of the *Míngmò sìdà gāosēng* 明末四大高僧 ("four great monks of the late Míng") together with [[祩宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng, [[德清]] Hānshān Déqīng, and [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù. Principal sponsor of the *Jiāxīngzàng* 嘉興藏 Buddhist canon publishing project; died in prison in Beijing during the *yāoshū* affair.
