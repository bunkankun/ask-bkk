Wāng Guǎngqī 汪廣期 (fl. early-mid 18th c.) was the elder brother of [[汪文綺]] Wāng Wénqǐ and a Wùyuán physician whose clinical practice informed Wáng's *Zázhèng huìxīn lù* ([[KR3eh007]]).
