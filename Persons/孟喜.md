# Mèng Xǐ 孟喜

Western Hàn 西漢 *Yì* master, *zì* Zhǎngqīng 長卿. One of the principal heads of the Hàn *xiàngshù* 象數 ("image-and-number") *Yì* school, recorded in the *Hànshū Rúlín zhuàn* 漢書·儒林傳 as a student of Tián Wángsūn 田王孫 (himself a disciple of [[田何]] Tián Hé). His school developed *guàqì* 卦氣 ("hexagram-breath") calendrical-cosmological correlations of the sixty-four hexagrams; the system was carried further by [[京房]] Jīng Fáng. No independent work by Mèng survives; his readings are preserved as quotations in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* 周易集解 ([[KR1a0008]]) and in Qīng *jíyì* 輯佚 reconstructions.
