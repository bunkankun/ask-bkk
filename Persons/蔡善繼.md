# Cài Shànjì 蔡善繼

Late-Míng literatus and lay Buddhist associate of [[張守約|Méicūn jūshì Zhāng Shǒuyuē]] 梅村居士張守約. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Second preface-writer for Zhāng's *Nǐ Hánshān shī* [[KR6q0217]]. His preface notes the substantial scope of Zhāng's larger poetic corpus (several thousand pieces imitating a wide range of classical poets beyond the preserved Hánshān imitations).

**Note**: The Kanripo catalog labels 蔡善繼's dynasty as 唐 (Táng), which appears to be a catalog error propagated from the adjacent 守禮 entry — Cài Shànjì is a Míng figure.
