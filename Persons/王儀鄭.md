# Wáng Yízhèng 王儀鄭

Late-Qīng physician of Xūyí 盱眙 (modern Jiāngsū); preface-writer to [[KR3ee054]] *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù* (1888), signing as *yīnyúdì* 姻愚弟 (in-law junior brother) to [[張筱衫]] Zhāng Xiǎoshān.
