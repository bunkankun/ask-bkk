Late-Qing physician of Húizhōu 徽州 (Anhui); *zì* Guǎng-ān 廣庵. Conventional lifedates 1825–c.1900 are not securely fixed. Son of Wāng Kūnjiān 汪坤堅 (*zì* Yìzhāng 義彰, *hào* Shíchéng-wēng 石城翁), a literatus-physician of the previous generation. Author of [[KR3eb050]] *Wàng zhěn zūn jīng* (1875), one of the most systematic Qing treatises on inspection diagnosis. No CBDB record.
