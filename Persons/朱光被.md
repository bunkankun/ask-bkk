# 朱光被 Zhū Guāngbèi

Early-Qīng physician, author of *Jīnguì zhèng yì* 金匱正義, a *Jīnguì yàoluè* commentary cited as a supplementary source in [[丹波元堅]] Tamba no Motokata's *Jīnguì yùhán yàoluè shùyì* ([[KR3ef085]]).
