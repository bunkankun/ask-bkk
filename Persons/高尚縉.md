**Gāo Shàngjìn** 高尚縉 (*fl.* early 20th c.) — late-Qīng prefacer of the 1906 stone-lithograph edition of [[倪枝維]] Ní Zhīwéi's *Chǎnbǎo* ([[KR3ei057]]) printed by [[黃子畬]] Huáng Zǐyú. Not in CBDB.
