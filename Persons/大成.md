# Dà-chéng 大成

Míng-Qīng transitional Cáodòng-lineage Chán monk; senior dharma-heir and editor of [[道盛|Jué-làng Dào-shèng]] 覺浪道盛 (1593–1659). Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Co-editor (*děng jiào* 等較, "et al. compared and corrected") of his master's massive *Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Shèng chánshī quán lù* 天界覺浪盛禪師全錄 [[KR6q0221]] in 33 juan, alongside his dharma-brother [[大然|Dà-rán]] 大然. Thirty-fourth-generation Cáodòng-orthodox descendant.
