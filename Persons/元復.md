Yuánfù 元復 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* late Southern Sòng), Southern-Sòng monk; *hào* Xiū-ān 休菴. According to the prefatory matter of [[KR6r0064]] *Wǔ-lín Xī-hú gāosēng shì-lüè* and to [[陳起]]'s *Liǔ-táng wài-jí* (j. 3), he was a monk of **Dōng-jiā** 東嘉 (Wenzhou).

Together with the Hángzhōu lecturer [[元敬]] he co-compiled the **《武林西湖高僧事略》** *Wǔ-lín Xī-hú gāosēng shì-lüè* ([[KR6r0064]], X77 no. 1526) — a brief one-juan compendium of biographical sketches of 30 eminent Buddhist masters associated with the West Lake establishments of Hángzhōu (Wǔ-lín). The biographies are paired with verse-eulogies (頌) attached to each. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000134.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6r0064]] *Wǔ-lín Xī-hú gāosēng shì-lüè*.
