**Zhèng Dé'ān** 鄭德安, *hào* Chōngxū miàojìng Níngzhēn zǐ 冲虛妙靜寧真子 ("Master Empty-Profound, Marvellous-Quiet, Tranquil-Real"), was a Southern-Sòng Daoist *nèidān* 內丹 practitioner of the Southern lineage that traced its descent through Zhāng Bóduān 張伯端 (whom Zhèng calls his *zǔshī* 祖師, "patriarch"). He produced the *Jīnyè dàdān kǒujué* 金液大丹口訣 ([[KR5a0292]], DZ 280), claiming to have received the formula from a "Daoist clad in white from the Tàiwēi cavern-heaven" (太微洞天白衣道者). The work is internally undated; its quotation of a half-verse from Xuē Dàoguāng's 薛道光 *Huándān fùmìng piān* 還丹復命篇 places it no earlier than the late twelfth century. Beyond the work itself, no biographical record survives.
