# Jiāochénrú 憍陳如

Skt. Ājñāta-Kauṇḍinya. The first of the *pañcavargīya* (五比丘 *wǔ bǐqiū*) — the five ascetics who became the Buddha's first disciples at the Deer Park (Mṛga-dāya / Sārnāth) when the Buddha set in motion the Wheel of the Dharma (*Dharmacakra-pravartana*). His attainment of *srotaāpatti* (stream-entry) on hearing the first sermon is recorded in all schools of the early Buddhist tradition.
