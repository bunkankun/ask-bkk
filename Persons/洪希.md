Hóngxī 洪希, LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yězhú Fúhuì** [[福慧]] 野竹福慧 in the PòshānSìchuānYúnnán sub-line. Lead compiler of the 8-juan 《益州嵩山野竹禪師後錄》 ([[KR6q0466]]), signing the juan-1 colophon as *zhù Jīzú Dàjuésì sìfǎ ménrén Hóngxī biān* 住雞足大覺寺嗣法門人洪希編 — i.e. abbot of **Jīzúshān Dàjuésì** 雞足山大覺寺 (Yúnnán Dàlǐ) at the time of compilation. The juan-2 colophon credits a co-compiler **Guǎngzhì** 廣智 *zhù Jīzú Shízhōngsì* 住雞足石鐘寺. No independent *yǔlù* located.
