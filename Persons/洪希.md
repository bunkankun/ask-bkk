Hóng-xī 洪希, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yě-zhú Fú-huì** [[福慧]] 野竹福慧 in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān-Yún-nán sub-line. Lead compiler of the 8-juan 《益州嵩山野竹禪師後錄》 ([[KR6q0466]]), signing the juan-1 colophon as *zhù Jī-zú Dà-jué-sì sì-fǎ mén-rén Hóng-xī biān* 住雞足大覺寺嗣法門人洪希編 — i.e. abbot of **Jī-zú-shān Dà-jué-sì** 雞足山大覺寺 (Yún-nán Dà-lǐ) at the time of compilation. The juan-2 colophon credits a co-compiler **Guǎng-zhì** 廣智 *zhù Jī-zú Shí-zhōng-sì* 住雞足石鐘寺. No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
