# Zhēnběn 真本 (Xiàng Zhēnběn 項真本)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng lay Chán practitioner and layman-teacher. Lay surname Xiàng 項, lay given name Qiān 謙. *Hào* Mù-dàozhě 牧道者 ("Herdsman Daoist"), Mù-gōng 牧公 ("Herdsman Duke"), Cì-ān 次庵 ("Second Hermitage"), Cí-ān 茨庵. Birth year 1618/1/17 (Wànlì 45/12/21 noon, per the biographical chapter in his *Jiū xīn lù*). Death year unrecorded.

Self-described as "a Daoist-monk who is not a [fully ordained] monk, a Daoist who is not a [fully secular] layman" (*dào sēng bù sēng, dào sú bù sú* 道僧不僧道俗不俗) — the distinctive hybrid lay-monastic position of late-imperial serious lay Chán practitioners. Active at Gǔ-píng-shān 古瓶山 ("Old-Bottle Mountain"; precise location not yet identified).

One major surviving work: the *Gǔ-píng-shān Mù-dào-zhě jiū xīn lù* 古瓶山牧道者究心錄 [[KR6q0202]] in three juan, his collected sermons and teachings. Also appears as a harmonizing-verse contributor (under the designation *Mù-gōng dàorén Xiàng Zhēn-běn* 牧公道人項真本) in the ox-herding compilations [[KR6q0161]], [[KR6q0163]], and [[KR6q0164]], evidencing his integration into the mid-17th-century Línjì-Yángqí lay-Chán network around [[嚴大參|Yán Dàcān]] 嚴大參 and the Pǔmíng-sì revival.

Author of the front-preface to **[[KR6q0455]]** 《嘉興退菴斷愚智禪師語錄》, signed *Píng-shān fǎ-dì Zhēn-běn Xiàng Qiān* 瓶山法弟真本項謙 and dated **康熙庚戌仲秋巳亥望旦 = 1670/8/15 Gregorian**. The preface refers to the master **Yǒng-zhèng Yī-lǎo héshàng** 永正一老和尚 as *fǎ-shū* 法叔 — placing Zhēn-běn as a *fǎ-zhí* 法姪 of Yǒng-zhèng-Yī (heir of [[普明真用]]), and *fǎ-dì* 法弟 of [[智|Tuì-ān Duàn-yú Zhì]] in the Hǔ-qiū → Jìng-shān → Duàn-qiáo Lún → Pǔ-míng-Yòng → Yǒng-zhèng-Yī sub-line. The preface establishes Zhēn-běn's formal lineage-affiliation more precisely than the earlier *Mù-dào-zhě* materials, which avoided explicit *fǎ-shū* / *fǎ-dì* designations.
