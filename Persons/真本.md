# Zhēnběn 真本 (Xiàng Zhēnběn 項真本)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng lay Chán practitioner and layman-teacher. Lay surname Xiàng 項, lay given name Qiān 謙. *Hào* Mùdàozhě 牧道者 ("Herdsman Daoist"), Mùgōng 牧公 ("Herdsman Duke"), Cìān 次庵 ("Second Hermitage"), Cíān 茨庵. Birth year 1618/1/17 (Wànlì 45/12/21 noon, per the biographical chapter in his *Jiū xīn lù*). Death year unrecorded.

Self-described as "a Daoist-monk who is not a [fully ordained] monk, a Daoist who is not a [fully secular] layman" (*dào sēng bù sēng, dào sú bù sú* 道僧不僧道俗不俗) — the distinctive hybrid lay-monastic position of late-imperial serious lay Chán practitioners. Active at Gǔpíngshān 古瓶山 ("Old-Bottle Mountain"; precise location not yet identified).

One major surviving work: the *Gǔpíngshān Mùdàozhě jiū xīn lù* 古瓶山牧道者究心錄 [[KR6q0202]] in three juan, his collected sermons and teachings. Also appears as a harmonizing-verse contributor (under the designation *Mùgōng dàorén Xiàng Zhēnběn* 牧公道人項真本) in the ox-herding compilations [[KR6q0161]], [[KR6q0163]], and [[KR6q0164]], evidencing his integration into the mid-17th-century Línjì-Yángqí lay-Chán network around [[嚴大參|Yán Dàcān]] 嚴大參 and the Pǔmíngsì revival.

Author of the front-preface to **[[KR6q0455]]** 《嘉興退菴斷愚智禪師語錄》, signed *Píngshān fǎdì Zhēnběn Xiàng Qiān* 瓶山法弟真本項謙 and dated **康熙庚戌仲秋巳亥望旦 = 1670/8/15 Gregorian**. The preface refers to the master **Yǒngzhèng Yīlǎo héshàng** 永正一老和尚 as *fǎshū* 法叔 — placing Zhēnběn as a *fǎzhí* 法姪 of Yǒngzhèng-Yī (heir of [[普明真用]]), and *fǎdì* 法弟 of [[智|Tuìān Duànyú Zhì]] in the Hǔqiū → Jìngshān → Duànqiáo Lún → Pǔmíng-Yòng → Yǒngzhèng-Yī sub-line. The preface establishes Zhēnběn's formal lineage-affiliation more precisely than the earlier *Mùdàozhě* materials, which avoided explicit *fǎshū* / *fǎdì* designations.
