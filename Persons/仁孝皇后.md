# Rénxiào huánghòu 仁孝皇后

Empress consort of the **Yǒnglè** 永樂 emperor of the Míng (Zhū Dì 朱棣); née Xú 徐, eldest daughter of the Míng founding general Xú Dá 徐達. Posthumously titled **Rénxiào wén huánghòu** 仁孝文皇后 (1362–1407).

Credited as the recipient of the dream-revelation of the *Mènggǎn fóshuō xīyǒu gōngdé jīng* 夢感佛說希有功德經 (1420). This claimed dream-text was expelled from the Buddhist canon by the Yǒnglè 18 catalog supervisor [[一如]] Yīrú with the verdict *huāngtáng zhī yán, bùkě rùzàng* 荒唐之言不可入藏 ("absurd words, must not enter the canon"). The episode is discussed in [[受登]] Shòudēng's editorial note in [[KR6j0761]].
