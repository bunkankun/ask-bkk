**Yáng Jìqīng** 楊霽青 of Dāntú 丹徒, late-Qīng / early-Republican intermediary through whom the Shàoxīng Medical Society editor [[袁焯]] Yuán Zhuó came upon a manuscript copy of [[莫枚士]] Mò Méishì's [[KR3eq014]] *Yánjīng yán* for the 1916 *Yīxué cóngshū* 醫學叢書 print run.
