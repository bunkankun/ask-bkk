# 高仲密
Gāo Zhòngmì (503–547)

Eastern Wèi military commander (Piàoqí dàjiāngjūn 驃騎大將軍) and patron of Buddhist scholastic translation. Sponsored the Yè 鄴-city translation circle that produced [[毘目智仙]] Vimokṣaprajñā-ṛṣi and [[瞿曇般若流支]] Gautama Prajñāruci's joint Mahāyāna *upadeśa* renderings ([[KR6h0035]], [[KR6h0030]]).
