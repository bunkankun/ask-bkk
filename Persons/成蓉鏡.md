# Chéng Róngjìng 成蓉鏡

Style name *Fúqīng* 芙卿. Native of Bǎoyìng 寶應 (Yángzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). Born Jiāqìng 21 (1816), died Guāngxù 9 (1883). A noted classicist of the Yángzhōu / Chángzhōu evidential-learning lineage, working in the late Qiánjiā tradition that ran via Liú Wéngí 劉文淇 and Liú Bǎonán 劉寶楠 of nearby Bǎoyìng. CBDB id 128850 (dynasty Qing, no birth/death years entered).

His scholarly range covered the *Shàngshū* and *Shījīng* (commentaries and prefatory studies), historical phonology (*Bǎoyìng Liúshì jí xí Chéngshì jiāfǎ* 寶應劉氏及襲成氏家法), classical-period chronology, and ancient-calendar reconstruction. The present catalog preserves his *Hàn Tàichū lì kǎo* 漢太初曆考 ([[KR3fb002]]), in which he argues — against the standard view that the *Shǐjì* "Lìshù jiǎzǐ piān" 厤術甲子篇 is a Chǔ Shàosūn 褚少孫 supplement based on the lost *Yīn lì* 殷曆 — that the chapter in fact embodies the *Tàichū lì* / *Sāntǒng lì* itself, and that the discrepancies it presents are textual corruptions rather than evidence of a different system. His other extant philological writings are collected in *Sījì jí* 思齋集; he was a frequent correspondent of the Yángzhōu *jīnwén* circle (Liǔ Xìngēng 柳興恩, Liú Yǔsōng 劉毓崧).
