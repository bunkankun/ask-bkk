# Jìngzhù 淨柱 (Yuǎnmén Jìngzhù 遠門淨柱)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng-lineage Chán monk. *Hào* Yuǎnmén 遠門 ("Distant Gate"). Lifedates unrecorded. Senior dharma-heir of [[明方|Shíyǔ Míngfāng]] 石雨明方 (1593–1648), whom he succeeded in the Cáodòng line tracing back through [[圓澄|Zhànrán Yuánchéng]] 湛然圓澄 to Dàjué Niàn 大覺念.

Major editorial works:
- *Wǔdēng huìyuán xùlüè* 五燈會元續略 [[KR6q0013]] — a four-juan supplement to the *Wǔdēng huìyuán*.
- *Shíyǔ chánshī fǎ tán* 石雨禪師法檀 [[KR6q0201]] — his master Míngfāng's collected *yǔlù* in twenty juan, including an extensive biographical *xíng zhuàng* of the master as juan 20.

Active in the final decades of the Míng and early Qīng.
