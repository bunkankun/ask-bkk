Xú Yǎng-tián 徐養恬, late-Qīng physician (CBDB 402790, dynasty 20 = Qīng, dates not specified in CBDB). Author of [[KR3ep066]] *Xú Yǎng-tián fāng-àn*, a four-*juǎn* casebook with an unusual empirical-formula appendix that includes opium-quitting prescriptions — dating the work to the second half of the nineteenth century. No further securely transmitted biographical record.
