# Zhìzàng 智藏

Liáng-dynasty Buddhist scholar-monk (458–522), one of the three great Liáng Lotus exegetes alongside [[法雲]] Fǎyún (467–529) and [[僧旻]] Sēngmín (467–527). The triad's southern Lotus exegesis represented the principal pre-Tiāntái Sinitic engagement with the Lotus Sūtra and provided much of the doctrinal apparatus that [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ later systematised in his Tiāntái productions.
