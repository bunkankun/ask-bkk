Kaigetsu 戒月 was a Japanese Vinaya monk of the Edo era; lifedates not preserved. He is remembered solely for his *gǎilù* 改錄 ("revised edition") of **Huìxiǎn**'s ([[慧顯]]) **Xíngshì chāo zhūjiā jì biāomù** 行事鈔諸家記標目 ([[KR6k0170]], X44n0741). His editorial intervention preserved the original Sòng compilation through the Tokugawa Manji-canon recension. Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000509.
