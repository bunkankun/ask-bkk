Móhē Jiāyè 摩訶迦葉 / Mahākāśyapa — one of the principal disciples of the Buddha; in the *honji-suijaku* mapping of Anchō's *Zhōng lùn shū jì* identified as the Buddhist identity of Lǎozǐ 老子.
