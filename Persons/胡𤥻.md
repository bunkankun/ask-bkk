Hú Tǐng 胡𤥻 / 胡珽 (b. 1821, d. uncertain), late-Qīng Buddhist lay-devotee; *zì* Tǐngchén 挺臣 / Xīnyún 心耘, *hào* Liánguī jūshì 蓮歸居士. Layman of the Pure-Land tradition, principal compiler of the **《淨土聖賢錄續編》** *Jìngtǔ shèngxián lù xùbiān* ([[KR6r0081]], X78 no. 1550, 4 juan) — the continuation of [[彭希涑]]'s *Jìngtǔ shèngxián lù* ([[KR6r0080]]).

The *Xùbiān* (compiled in the **Dàoguāng** 道光 reign-period, c. 1830s–1850s) brings the Pure-Land hagiographical record forward from the death of Péng Xīsù in 1793 through to the early-mid nineteenth century. It supplements [[KR6r0080]] with biographies of Qing monks, nuns, lay-men, and lay-women — particularly emphasising the late-Qián-lóng / Jiāqìng / Dàoguāng layperson devotional networks of the Jiāng-Zhè region. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A018442 (the same person is also catalogued as A000864 with less detail).

The character 𤥻 (= U+24B7B) is a variant orthography of 珽; both are attested in the source and in catalog tradition.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6r0081]] *Jìngtǔ shèngxián lù xùbiān*.
