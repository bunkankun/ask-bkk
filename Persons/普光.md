Pǔguāng 普光 (also known as Shìguāng 釋光 and Dàchéng Guāng 大乘光 "Great Vehicle Light"; DILA A000060; fl. mid-to-late 7th century CE) was one of the most prominent disciples of Xuanzang 玄奘 ([[玄奘]]) and served for approximately twenty years as his *bǐshòu* 筆受 (brush-receiver, scribe for dictated translations). He was present at Xuanzang's major translation projects and is mentioned in colophons for several Taisho texts. The preface to 圓暉's commentary ([[KR6l0036]]) by the Táng official Jiǎ Céng 賈曾 records that 普光 ("大乘光法師") was "親承密誨，初傳正釋" — "directly received the inner teaching and first transmitted the correct exposition" of the *Kośa*.

His principal works include the comprehensive commentary 俱舍論記 ([[KR6l0034]], T41n1821, 30 juan) and the brief systematic treatise 俱舍論法宗原 ([[KR6l0038]], X53n837, 1 juan). He is also listed among the proof-readers (*zhèng yì* 證義) for several of Xuanzang's translations of the Yogācāra corpus.
