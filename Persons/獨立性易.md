# Dúlì Xìngyì 獨立性易

Buddhist name of [[戴笠]] Dài Lì (1596–1672), late-Míng *jǔrén* of Hángzhōu who took ordination in Japan under the Ōbaku 黃檗 patriarch [[隱元隆琦]] Yǐnyuán Lóngqí. See [[戴笠]] for the primary entry.
