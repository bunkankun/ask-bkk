**Shí Tài** 石泰 (trad. 1022–1158), *zì* Dé zhī 得之, styled Xìng lín zhēn rén 杏林真人, of Cháng zhōu 常州 (Jiāngsū). **Second patriarch of the Southern Lineage** (*Nán zōng* 南宗) of Daoist inner alchemy, direct disciple of Zhāng Bóduān and teacher of Xuē Dàoguāng. Author of [[KR5d0114]] *Huán yuán piān* 還源篇.
