Míng 明, *zì* **Huànzhù** 幻住 ("Illusion-Dwelling"); often referred to as **Huànzhù Míng** 幻住明 or **Huìlíng Huànzhù** 會靈幻住明 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěrdú Dàotài** 耳毒道泰 (in the Pòshān Hǎimíng line). Lifedates unknown — active **1660–1694**. Native of **Lúzhōu** 瀘州 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Zēng** 曾.

**Abbacies (four).** **Línshuǐxiàn Xiānrénshān Guānyīn chányuàn** 鄰水縣仙人山觀音禪院 (entered **順治庚子冬** = **1660** winter); **Dàzhúxiàn Wànfēngshān Tàipíng chánsì** 大竹縣萬峰山太平禪寺; **Wànxiàn Xīnglóng chánsì** 萬縣興隆禪寺; **Shèhóng Huìlíngsì** 射洪會靈寺 (the principal seat giving him his sobriquet **Huìlíng Huànzhù**).

**Disciples.** **Tiānrán** 天然 and **Shuǐyìng** 水映 *fǎsì* (named in the *liánfāngjì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0549]] 《幻住明禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B432, compiled by *fǎsì* **Qīngshàng** [[清尚]] *děng biān*; preface dated **康熙甲戌歲冬杪除日** = **1694-12** by an anonymous lay donor at **Fánlíng** 樊陵.

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 14; 《幻住明禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈聯芳偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 105.

- - - - -

The dharma-name **Míng** 明 is shared by many monks in the Buddhist canon. This entry is specifically for **Huànzhù Míng** 幻住明 (DILA A019619), a Sìchuān-Lúzhōu Línjì-Yángqí monk active 1660–1694. Should not be confused with **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666), with whom he is in the same line of descent (Pòshān → Ěrdú Dàotài → Huànzhù Míng) — see also DILA A009652 for the latter.
