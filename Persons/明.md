Míng 明, *zì* **Huàn-zhù** 幻住 ("Illusion-Dwelling"); often referred to as **Huàn-zhù Míng** 幻住明 or **Huì-líng Huàn-zhù** 會靈幻住明 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěr-dú Dào-tài** 耳毒道泰 (in the Pò-shān Hǎi-míng line). Lifedates unknown — active **1660–1694**. Native of **Lú-zhōu** 瀘州 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Zēng** 曾.

**Abbacies (four).** **Lín-shuǐ-xiàn Xiān-rén-shān Guān-yīn chán-yuàn** 鄰水縣仙人山觀音禪院 (entered **順治庚子冬** = **1660** winter); **Dà-zhú-xiàn Wàn-fēng-shān Tài-píng chán-sì** 大竹縣萬峰山太平禪寺; **Wàn-xiàn Xīng-lóng chán-sì** 萬縣興隆禪寺; **Shè-hóng Huì-líng-sì** 射洪會靈寺 (the principal seat giving him his sobriquet **Huì-líng Huàn-zhù**).

**Disciples.** **Tiān-rán** 天然 and **Shuǐ-yìng** 水映 *fǎ-sì* (named in the *lián-fāng-jì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0549]] 《幻住明禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B432, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Qīng-shàng** [[清尚]] *děng biān*; preface dated **康熙甲戌歲冬杪除日** = **1694-12** by an anonymous lay donor at **Fán-líng** 樊陵.

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 14; 《幻住明禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈聯芳偈〉; 《五燈全書》 vol. 105.

- - - - -

The dharma-name **Míng** 明 is shared by many monks in the Buddhist canon. This entry is specifically for **Huàn-zhù Míng** 幻住明 (DILA A019619), a Sì-chuān-Lú-zhōu Línjì-Yángqí monk active 1660–1694. Should not be confused with **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666), with whom he is in the same line of descent (Pò-shān → Ěr-dú Dào-tài → Huàn-zhù Míng) — see also DILA A009652 for the latter.
