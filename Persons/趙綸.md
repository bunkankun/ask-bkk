# 趙綸 Zhào Lún

**Zhào Lún** 趙綸, Southern-Sòng official who issued the Shàodìng 4 (1231) reprint of [[王執中]] Wáng Zhízhōng's *Zhēnjiǔ zīshēng jīng* 針灸資生經 ([[KR3ee020]]).
