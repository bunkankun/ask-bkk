# 朱汝功 Zhū Rǔgōng

**Zhū Rǔgōng** 朱汝功 (1913–2005), modern Chinese acupuncturist of Shanghai; wife and collaborator of [[陸瘦燕]] Lù Shòuyàn in establishing the Shanghai school of acupuncture and in numerous clinical and historical studies.
