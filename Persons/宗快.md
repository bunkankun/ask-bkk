Shūkai 宗快 (1276–1340), Tendai *shōmyō* master and scholar of the **Ōhara Raigō-in** liturgical-music lineage in the Kamakura / Nanboku-chō period. His principal works are the *Gyo-san mokuroku* [[KR6t0425]] (a systematic catalogue of the Ōhara *shōmyō* repertoire) and the *Nyohōkyō genshū sahō* [[KR6t0441]] (a ritual manual for the *Nyohōkyō* sūtra-copying observance, with attention to its musical accompaniment). Together these provide the principal documentary record of the medieval Japanese Buddhist musical tradition as it existed at the Ōhara performance center in the early 14th c. The DILA authority id is `A001260`. He died at Raigō-in in **Ryakuō 3 / 1340**, age 65.
