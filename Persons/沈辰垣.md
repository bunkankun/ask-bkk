Shěn Chényuán 沈辰垣 (b. 1655, d. after c. 1715), Qīng scholar-official of the *Kāngxī* era. Joint *fèngchì* (imperially commissioned) chief editor, with Wáng Yìqīng [[王奕清]], of the great *Yùxuǎn lìdài shīyú* [[KR4j0074]] (1685–1707) of 120 *juǎn* — the imperially-decreed comprehensive *cí* anthology of the *Kāngxī* reign. CBDB id 79646 gives a birth-year of 1655; his death-year is not securely recorded but must postdate the 1707 completion of the *Lìdài shīyú*. No further substantive biographical detail.
