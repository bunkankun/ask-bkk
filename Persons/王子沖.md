**Wáng Zǐchōng** 王子沖 of Zhèjiāng (*jìnshì*) — Míng prefacer of the 1535 (Jiājìng 14) *Jiājìng yǐwèi* print of *Jìshēng chǎnbǎo* ([[KR3ei068]]), the Sòng obstetrical formulary edited by [[徐明善]] Xú Míngshàn and brought to press by [[雷鳴震]] Léi Mínzhèn. Praised the work's clinical value for both pregnant women and physicians. Not in CBDB.
