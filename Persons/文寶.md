# Wén-bǎo 文寶

Late-Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Fāng-shān 方山. Dharma-heir of [[妙倫]] Duàn-qiáo Miào-lún (1201–1261); co-editor (as 侍者) of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0327]]), and in his own right subject of the *Fāng-shān Wén-bǎo chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0328]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000187).
