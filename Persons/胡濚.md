**Hú Yíng** 胡濚 (1375–1463) — early-Míng official of Pí-líng 毗陵 (Cháng-zhōu 常州, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of *Jiàn-wén* 2 / 1400. Long career through Hóng-wǔ to Tiān-shùn reigns: famed for his decades-long undercover mission, ordered by the *Yǒng-lè* emperor, to search the realm for the deposed *Jiàn-wén* emperor; later *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū* 禮部尚書 (Minister of Rites) and *tài-zǐ bīn-kè* 太子賓客. Author of the *Zhèng-tǒng* 8 / 1443 publication preface to [[戴思恭]]'s *Mì-chuán zhèng-zhì yào-jué jí lèi-fāng* ([[KR3er068]]). CBDB id 246855.
