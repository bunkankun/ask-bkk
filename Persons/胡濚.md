**Hú Yíng** 胡濚 (1375–1463) — early-Míng official of Pílíng 毗陵 (Chángzhōu 常州, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Jiànwén* 2 / 1400. Long career through Hóngwǔ to Tiānshùn reigns: famed for his decades-long undercover mission, ordered by the *Yǒnglè* emperor, to search the realm for the deposed *Jiànwén* emperor; later *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書 (Minister of Rites) and *tàizǐ bīnkè* 太子賓客. Author of the *Zhèngtǒng* 8 / 1443 publication preface to [[戴思恭]]'s *Mìchuán zhèngzhì yàojué jí lèifāng* ([[KR3er068]]). CBDB id 246855.
