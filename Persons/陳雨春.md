# 陳雨春

Chén Yǔchūn, Húnán physician of the 1830s credited with standardising the diagnostic binome *báihóu* 白喉 (diphtheria) in Chinese medical writing, building on the *hóujiān fābái* doctrine of [[鄭宏綱]] Zhèng Hónggāng (cf. [[KR3em017]] *Chónglóu yùyuè* 重樓玉鑰 and [[KR3em020]] *Báihóu tiáobiàn* 白喉條辨).
