Southern-Sòng Zhèjiāng Daoist scholar and inner-alchemy writer, *zì* Zōngyù 宗禹, *hào* Yúnfēng sǎnrén 雲峰散人. Active in the Lǐnán-fǔ 臨安府 area in the 1220s. He studied inner alchemy under an (unnamed) adept on Mount Héng 衡山 and was a prolific writer on *nèidān* 內丹 topics. His surviving works in the Daoist canon include:

- [[KR5a0110|Huángdì yīnfú jīng jiǎngyì 黃帝陰符經講義]] (DZ 110, preface 1226, postface 1227) — an inner-alchemical discussion-commentary on the *Yīnfú jīng* 陰符經.
- [[KR5a0146|Zǐyáng zhēnrén Wùzhēn piān jiǎngyì 紫陽真人悟真篇講義]] (DZ 146) — a commentary on Zhāng Bóduān's *Wùzhēn piān*.
- The lost *Yúnfēng jīndān sānshū* 雲峰金丹三書 ("Three Books on the Gold Elixir"), partial traces preserved in juan 4 of [[KR5a0110|DZ 110]].
- A no-longer-extant commentary on the *Cuīgōng rùyào jīng* 崔公入藥鏡.

Xià Yuándǐng is a Representative figure of the Southern-Sòng Zhèjiāng inner-alchemy circle, drawing explicitly on the Mount Héng adept-transmission and integrating *Yīnfú jīng*, *Wùzhēn piān*, and *Rùyào jīng* traditions into a unified *nèidān* apparatus. No CBDB record was found.
