# Zōnghuì 宗會

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn (1178–1249); principal named co-editor (with [[智折]]) of the Qīngliáng abbacy record in juan 1 of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0315]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000593).
