Yìnyúxuān Fàng 蔭餘軒放 is a pen name used by the author of *Mínguó Yěshǐ* 民國野史 ([[KR4k0305]]), a popular fictionalized history of the early Chinese Republic (1911–1917). The real identity is unknown. Active ca. 1920s–1930s.
