**Gě Shèngzhòng** 葛勝仲 (1072–1144), *zì* Lǔqīng 魯卿, of Dānyáng 丹陽 (Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Shàoshèng* 4 / 1097; further passed the *xuéguān shì* and the *cíkē* — both *first-place*. Held office to *Huáwéngé dàizhì*, *Zhī Húzhōu*. *Shàoxīng* 1 / 1131 retired to *Tíjǔ Tàipíngguān*; died *Shàoxīng* 14 / 1144. Posthumously canonised *Wénkāng* 文康. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 445 (Wényuán). Author of *Dānyáng jí* 丹陽集 [[KR4d0143]] originally in 80 *juǎn* + *wàijí* 20 (per his son-in-law Zhāng Zòng's *xíngzhuàng*); *Sìkù* reconstruction is 24 *juǎn*. Praised for vigorous remonstrance: refused Shèng Zhāng 盛章's solicitation; refused Lǐ Yàn 李彥's tax-extortion; refused Zhū Miǎn 朱勔's request for white-sparrow / mandarin-duck tribute. Defended Húzhōu against rebels in his second prefectural tenure. Composed three discourses (*lùn rén*, *lùn xiào*, *lùn xué*) for the imperial heir; wrote 300-juǎn continuation of Ōuyáng Xiū's *Tàicháng yīngé lǐ*.
