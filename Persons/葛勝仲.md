**Gě Shèng-zhòng** 葛勝仲 (1072–1144), *zì* Lǔ-qīng 魯卿, of Dān-yáng 丹陽 (Cháng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of *Shào-shèng* 4 / 1097; further passed the *xué-guān shì* and the *cí-kē* — both *first-place*. Held office to *Huá-wén-gé dài-zhì*, *Zhī Hú-zhōu*. *Shào-xīng* 1 / 1131 retired to *Tí-jǔ Tài-píng-guān*; died *Shào-xīng* 14 / 1144. Posthumously canonised *Wén-kāng* 文康. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 445 (Wén-yuán). Author of *Dān-yáng jí* 丹陽集 [[KR4d0143]] originally in 80 *juǎn* + *wài-jí* 20 (per his son-in-law Zhāng Zòng's *xíng-zhuàng*); *Sìkù* reconstruction is 24 *juǎn*. Praised for vigorous remonstrance: refused Shèng Zhāng 盛章's solicitation; refused Lǐ Yàn 李彥's tax-extortion; refused Zhū Miǎn 朱勔's request for white-sparrow / mandarin-duck tribute. Defended Hú-zhōu against rebels in his second prefectural tenure. Composed three discourses (*lùn rén*, *lùn xiào*, *lùn xué*) for the imperial heir; wrote 300-juǎn continuation of Ōu-yáng Xiū's *Tài-cháng yīn-gé lǐ*.
