# Yún 筠

Chán monk of the tenth century, disciple at Zhāoqìng-yuàn 招慶院 in Quánzhōu 泉州 (Fújiàn) under the abbot Wéndēng 文僜 (884–972). Together with his colleague Jìng 靜, co-compiler of the *Zǔtáng jí* 祖堂集 ([[KR6q0002]]) in 952 (Southern Táng *Bǎodà* 10).

No other biographical data is transmitted; the compilers' names are preserved only as single characters in the work's colophon.
