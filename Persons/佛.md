# Fó 佛 (Hái-chū Guāng-fó 還初光佛)

Catalog-form short name **佛** = single-character abbreviation of [[光佛]] **Hái-chū Guāng-fó** 還初光佛 (b. 1631), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of [[德玉]] 聖可德玉 at **Chóng-qìng Huá-yán Chán-yuàn** 重慶華巖禪院. The catalog meta of [[KR6q0518]] uses the bare-character form; full biographical information is at the [[光佛]] person-note. (Distinct from the homonymous Buddha-symbol 佛 used in many compounds.)
