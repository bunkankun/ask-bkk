Ŭijŏk 義寂 (Korean *Ŭijŏk*; fl. mid-7th c.) was a Silla-dynasty 新羅 Buddhist exegete who studied in Tang China under **Xuánzàng** 玄奘 ([[玄奘]]) and **Kuījī** 窺基 (the principal Yogācāra master). His extant works include the **Pú-sà-jiè-běn shū** 菩薩戒本疏 ([[KR6k0082]]) — a two-fascicle commentary on the bodhisattva-precepts of the *Fànwǎng jīng* — and major commentaries on the *Lotus Sūtra*, the *Sukhāvatī-vyūha*, and other Mahāyāna scriptures. As a Silla scholar working in the Tang Yogācāra tradition, he is one of the principal channels by which Chinese Mahāyāna scholarship was transmitted to Korean Buddhism. The lifedates are uncertain: traditional sources give activity in the 660s–680s.
