# Yìlín 義林

Japanese (Edo-period) Sōtō elder of Yúnlóng 雲龍; held one of the two manuscript recensions of [[KR6q0071]] *Rújìng héshàng yǔlù* that [[禪山]] Zenzan supplied to [[卍山道白]] Manzan Dōhaku in 1680 for collation.
