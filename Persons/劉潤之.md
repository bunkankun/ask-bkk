# Liú Rùnzhī 劉潤之 (fl. mid-16th c.)

A late-Míng scholar of Hézhōng 河中 (modern southwestern Shānxī). He is the compiler of the *Èr Huángfǔ jí* 二皇甫集 [[KR4h0015]], the joint collection of the *Tiānbǎo*–*Dàlì* poets Huángfǔ Rǎn 皇甫冉 and Huángfǔ Zēng 皇甫曾, prefaced by Wáng Tíngxiāng 王廷相 (1474–1544) and with a colophon by Yáng Shèn 楊慎 (1488–1559) — placing his editorial activity in the first half of the sixteenth century. No further biographical detail is recoverable.
