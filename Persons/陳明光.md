Contemporary Chinese specialist of the *Dàzú shíkū* 大足石窟 / Dàzú cave-temple complex (Chongqing) and its Buddhist stone-inscription corpus, contributing editor of the *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* series. Author of *Dàzú shíkū jīngwén jí qí yánjiū* 大足石窟經文及其研究 (Chongqing: Chóngqìng chūbǎnshè, 1996). Principal *zhěnglǐ* 整理 editor of the Dàzú stone-inscription cluster in *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 4: [[KR6v0045]] (*Shǒu hù dà-qiān guótǔ jīng*), [[KR6v0046]] (*Liù dào lúnhuí tú jì*), [[KR6v0047]] (*Fù-mǔ ēn zhòng jīng biàn*), [[KR6v0048]] (*Dà fāngbiàn fó bào'ēn jīng biàn*), [[KR6v0049]] (*Sān-shèng yùzhì fóyá zàn*), [[KR6v0050]] (*Guān-jīng biàn xiàng*), [[KR6v0051]] (*Dìyù biàn jīngwén jì-sòng*) — together constituting one of the principal modern editions of the inscriptional Buddhist literature of the Southern Sòng Dàzú project of [[趙智鳳]].
