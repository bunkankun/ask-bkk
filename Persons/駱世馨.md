Late-Tóng-zhì-era Sì-chuān literatus of Shuāng-liú 雙流 (Chéng-dū prefecture); a neighbour and close acquaintance of the physician-poet [[劉仕廉]] Liú Shì-lián, with whom he conversed on poetry and medical doctrine. His sole documented role is as the preface-writer (*Tóng-zhì* 13 / 1874) of Liú's [[KR3er098]] *Yī-xué jí-chéng* 醫學集成; the preface dates the publication and identifies Liú as the actual author of the work. The catalog metadata for KR3er098 erroneously lists Luò himself as the author.
