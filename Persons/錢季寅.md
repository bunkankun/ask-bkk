**Qián Jìyín** 錢季寅, early-Republican Shànghǎi medical colleague of [[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi; commissioned by Qín to extract the medical sections of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Yùgāngzhāi bǐzhǔ* for the 1926 cut [[KR3eq045]] *Yùgāngzhāi yīxué bǐzhǔ*.
