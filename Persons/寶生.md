# Bǎo-shēng 寶生

Yuán Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with Ratnasambhava (寶生佛), the dhyāni-buddha of the South.
