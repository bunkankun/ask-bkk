# Dào-lín 道霖

Ming Chán monk; disciple of [[本瑞|Tiān-qí Běn-ruì]] 天奇本瑞 (d. 1508). Lifedates unrecorded. Co-compiler (with [[性福|Xìng-fú]] 性福) of his master's *Zhí zhù Xuě-dòu sòng gǔ* [[KR6q0250]] under the joint attribution "道霖性福編集".
