**Zhēnjiè** 真界, *hào* **Yuètáng** 悅堂 and **Huànjū** 幻居. Late-Ming Buddhist exegete and Dharma-heir of **Yuètíng Míngdé** 月亭明得 (1531 – 1588). Native of **Zuìlǐ** 檇李 (modern Jiāxìng 嘉興 in Zhèjiāng). Resident at **Zuìlǐ** and at the **Chuányī ān** 傳衣庵 of **Jìngshān** 徑山 (the great Línjì Chán monastery in Hangzhou prefecture). Lifedates unrecorded; productive period in the late Wànlì era. Within the lineage diagram he is a "high disciple of **Qiānsōng**" 千松之高足 — Qiānsōng being a sobriquet of his teacher Yuètíng Míngdé's broader school.

His extensive commentarial corpus includes:
- **Lèngyán-jīng zuǎn-zhù** 楞嚴經纂註 in 10 fascicles ([[KR6j0708]], X15 no. 300) — synoptic compilation-and-annotation on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*.
- **Qǐxìn lùn zuǎnzhù** 起信論纂註 in 2 fascicles — synoptic compilation on the *Awakening of Faith in the Mahāyāna*.
- **Jīngāng zhíjiě** 金剛直解 — direct exegesis of the *Diamond Sūtra*.
- **Yīn-míng rù zhèng-lǐ lùn jiě** 因明入正理論解 in 1 fascicle — commentary on Dignāga / Śaṅkarasvāmin's *Nyāyapraveśa*.
- **Wù bùqiān lùn biànjiě** 物不遷論辯解 in 1 fascicle — commentary on Sēngzhào's 僧肇 *Wù bùqiān lùn* 物不遷論.
