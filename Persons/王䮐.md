Wáng Yú 王䮐, clansman of [[王宗傳]] Wáng Zōngchuán. Resident at Wǔlíng 武陵 (modern Chángdé, Húnán) in *Kāixǐ* 1 (1205), at which time he wrote to [[林焞]] Lín Tūn (then magistrate of Kāihuàxiàn 開化縣 in Qúzhōu) requesting a preface for the impending publication of Wáng Zōngchuán's *Tóngxī Yì zhuàn* 童溪易傳 ([[KR1a0047]]) by [[劉日新]] Liú Rìxīn.
