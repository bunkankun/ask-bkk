**Nakagawa Shigeaki / Seishō** 中川成章 (late-Edo Kanpō practitioner, *fl.* 1840s–1860s), Japanese physician active in the closing decades of the Tokugawa period. Lifedates poorly attested in standard reference works; not in CBDB.

His one preserved Chinese-medical work catalogued here is the *Zhèng-zhì zhāi-yào* 證治摘要 ([[KR3eh026]]), a digest of Chinese internal-medicine *zhèng-zhì* 證治 (syndrome-and-treatment) literature compiled as a teaching condensation for Japanese students of *kanpō* 漢方. The work's editorial method — stripping Chinese sources to clinical essentials in the manner of the Edo *koihō* 古醫方 movement — places Nakagawa within the late-Edo programme of recovering Han-formula therapy as the doctrinal anchor of *kanpō* practice.
