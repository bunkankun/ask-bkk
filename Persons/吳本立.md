**Wú Běnlì** 吳本立, mid-Qīng physician; compiler of [[痢症彙參]] *Lì zhèng huì cān*, a comprehensive late-Qīng collation of opinion on dysentery. Criticised by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]] for collecting earlier opinions without filtering them.
