# Dài Xī 戴溪

*Zì* Xiàowàng / Shàowàng 肖望 / 少望, *hào* Mín'yǐn 岷隱 (self-styled after the Mín'gāng 岷崗 retreat shared with his friend Wáng Nán 王楠), posthumous title Wénduān 文端. Native of Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (modern Wēnzhōu 溫州, Zhèjiāng); grand-nephew of Dài Shù 戴述, one of the *Yǒngjiā jiǔ xiānsheng* 永嘉九先生.

Topped the *shěngshì* 省試 of Chúnxī 5 (1178) — the same cohort as Yè Shì 葉適, who placed second in the *tíngshì* 廷試 and later wrote to console Dài, calling him "*shàowàng tiānxià qícái*" 少望天下奇才. Served as 山長 (head) of the Shígǔ Academy 石鼓書院 in Héngzhōu 衡州 (Húnán) in 1185; later as 太學學錄 (1195), 兵部侍郎 (1206), 國子祭酒 兼 太子詹事 兼 秘書監 (1207), 太子右諭德 (1208), 權工部尚書 (1211), retiring as 龍圖閣學士 in 1215.

Birth year is given as 1144 (Shàoxīng *jiǎzǐ* 紹興甲子) in CBDB-aligned records, though some secondary sources give 1141; death year 1215 is firmly fixed (Jiādìng 8 嘉定八年).

Surviving works in Kanripo: *Shígǔ Lúnyǔ dáwèn* 石鼓論語答問 ([[KR1h0019]], 3 juan, WYG); *Xù Lǚshì jiāshú dúshī jì* 續呂氏家塾讀詩記 (KR1c0018, 4 juan, WYG); *Chūnqiū jiǎngyì* 春秋講義 (KR1e0046, 4 juan, WYG). Other attested titles not in Kanripo: *Yì zǒnglùn* 易總論, *Shū shuō* 書說, *Lǐjì kǒuyì* 禮記口義, *Mèngzǐ dáwèn* 孟子答問, *Tōngjiàn bǐyì* 通鑑筆議, *Jiāngjiàn lùnduàn* 將鑒論斷, *Fùchóu duì* 復仇對, *Qīngyuán zhì* 清源志, and a poetry collection *Mín'yǐn shīwén jí* 岷隱詩文集.
