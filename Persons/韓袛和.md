**Hán Zhīhé** 韓袛和 (*fl.* Yuán-yòu 元祐 – Shào-shèng 紹聖 reigns, ca. 1086–1093, 北宋), Northern-Sòng physician and the author of the *Shānghán wēi-zhǐ lùn* 傷寒微旨論 ([[KR3ef008]]) — the earliest post-Sòng-bureau theoretical treatise on the *Shānghán* canon. His native place and official career are not preserved in the standard histories; he is known almost exclusively through this single work and through references in Chéng Wúyǐ's 成無己 *Shānghán míng-lǐ lùn* 傷寒明理論 ([[KR3ef040]]) and in Hán's near-contemporary the *Chóng-wén zǒng-mù* 崇文總目. The work attests to a vibrant Northern-Sòng *Shānghán* commentarial culture that emerged immediately on the heels of the 1065 校正醫書局 collation. No CBDB record.
