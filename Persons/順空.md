**Zōzan Junkū** 藏山順空 (1233–1308) — Kamakura-period Japanese **Rinzai-Zen** master, posthumous title **Enkan Zenji** 圓鑑禪師 ("Round-Mirror Zen Master"). DILA gives his birth-year as **1233** (estimated from records).

A disciple of the Tōfuku-ji lineage / Shōichi-ha. His single-fascicle Recorded Sayings — *Enkan Kokushi goroku* 圓鑑國師語錄 ([[KR6t0256]], T80n2550) — were compiled by an unnamed *jisha* 侍者 (attendant) at his temple.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001884.
