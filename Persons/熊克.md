# Xióng Kè 熊克

Southern Sòng historian and minor official. *Zì* Zǐfù 子復. Native of Jiànyáng 建陽 (modern Fú-jiàn). *Jìnshì* of *Shào-xīng* 27 / 1157. Lifedates conventionally given as 1132–1204 (CBDB id 11818 has no dates filled in).

Career: held a series of provincial magistracies; under Xiào-zōng rose to *Qǐ-jū láng* 起居郎 in the Hàn-lín *xué-shì-yuàn* 學士院 secretariat; later served as prefect of Tāi-zhōu 台州. His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 445 (*Wén-yuàn zhuàn*).

Two principal historical works:
- *Zhōng-xīng xiǎo jì* 中興小紀 ([[KR2b0018]]) in 40 *juǎn* (the WYG transmits the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* recovery in nominally 10 *juǎn*) — annalistic chronicle of the Southern Sòng restoration from the *Jiàn-yán* abdication (*dīng-wèi* / 1127) to the end of the *Shàoxīng* era (*rén-wǔ* / 1162). Written in his private capacity (*xiǎo jì* — "small notes" — as opposed to the official court *rì jì* and *shí lù*) but invaluable as a source closer in time to the events than Lǐ Xīn-chuán's later *Jiànyán yǐ-lái xìnián yào lù*. Yuè Kē's *Téng shǐ* and Chén Zhènsūn both criticised the work for terseness and occasional contradiction; the Sìkù editors note these limits but defend its early-witness value.
- *Jiǔ-cháo tōng lüè* 九朝通略 in 168 *juǎn* — a synoptic chronicle of the Northern Sòng nine reigns (Tài-zǔ to Zhézōng); now lost except for 11 fragmentary juan recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*.

Member of the late-Southern-Sòng informal historical-research circle around Lǐ Xīn-chuán 李心傳; the latter explicitly draws on Xióng Kè in compiling the *Jiànyán yǐ-lái xìnián yào lù* ([[KR2b0024]]).
