Jì-fēng 際鋒, *zì* **Cáng-ān** 藏庵 ("Hidden Hut"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-yì Jù-xiáng** [[巨翔]] (1663–1695). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0589]] 《天翼翔禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B477.
