Jìfēng 際鋒, *zì* **Cángān** 藏庵 ("Hidden Hut"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānyì Jùxiáng** [[巨翔]] (1663–1695). Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0589]] 《天翼翔禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B477.
