Early-Yuán 元 Hàn-Confucian censor-official, one of the founding-generation institutional architects of Khubilai-era Mongol-Yuán administration. *Zì* **Tài-chū** 太初; *hào* **Qīng-yá** 青崖 ("Green-Cliff"). Native of **Shùn-shèng** 順聖 in **Hóng-zhōu** 宏州 (modern Shǎn-xī / Shānxī region).

**Family background.** Adopted son of his cousin-grandfather **Wèi Pán** 魏璠 — the eminent Jīn-end *Hàn-lín xiū-zhuàn* 翰林修撰 whose principled directness so impressed Khubilai Khan that Khubilai summoned Pán to the Mongol court at Hé-lín 和林 with high honors. Pán being childless, Wèi Chū was raised as his successor.

**Career.** Early entry to the *Zhōng-shū-shěng* (Imperial Secretariat) as a clerk; resigned and returned. Re-recommended; the emperor (Khubilai) inquired and learned Wèi was Pán's son — therefore appointed *Guó-shǐ-yuàn biān-xiū* 國史院編修. Promoted to **Censor** (*yù-shǐ* 御史); ultimately *Nán-tái yù-shǐ zhōng-chéng* 南臺御史中丞 (Vice-Censor-in-Chief at the Southern Censorate).

**Literary lineage.** Disciple of **Yuán Hǎo-wèn** 元好問 (1190–1257); the *Qīng-yá jí* preserves Wèi's record that he and **Jiāng Yù** 姜彧 had together studied under Yuán Hǎo-wèn, and that when their teacher came to Yān (Beijing) Wèi morning-and-evening attended his staff-and-sandals.

**Memorial-documentation.** Wèi's *zòu-yì* corpus is the principal historical contribution of his collection — a body of Khubilai-era administrative memorials that the Sìkù editors explicitly valued as supplementing the *Yuán-shǐ*'s lacunae. Notable memorials include:
- *Qǐng dìng fǎ-lìng* 請定法令 (legal codification)
- *Qǐng sù cháo-yí* 請肅朝儀 (court ritual reform)
- *Qǐng huǎn zhuāng-pèi yán-huò* 請緩椿配鹽貨 (salt-trade matter)
- *Qǐng jìn diāo-dèng kè-mǐ* 請禁刁蹬客米 (rice-trade)
- *Qǐng yōu-hù rú-hù* 請優護儒戶 (Confucian-household protections)
- *Qǐng jīng Zhèng Jiāng sǐ-jié* 請旌鄭江死節 (commemoration of martyrs)
- *Qǐng xiū Mèng-zǐ miào* 請修孟子廟 (restoration of the Mencius shrine — a major Yuán institutional-Confucian act)
- *Qǐng hé gù gōng-jiàng* 請和顧工匠 (artisan policy)
- *Qǐng bà Hé-nán qiān-jūn* 請罷河南簽軍 (Hé-nán demobilization)
