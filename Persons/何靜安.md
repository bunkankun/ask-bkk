Hé Jìng'ān 何靜安 (fl. mid-19th c.) was the grandson of [[何彤園]] Hé Tóngyuán who, together with the grand-nephew [[何星浦]], published the *Dàfāngmài* ([[KR3eh020]]) posthumously in 1855.
