**Hànfēn** 翰芬 (*fl.* late 19th c., 清), late-Qīng literatus. Wrote the 光緒十三年丁亥暮春 (1887) preface to [[陳其瑞]]'s *Běncǎo cuōyào* 本草撮要 ([[KR3ec055]]), comparing Chén's pharmacopoeia method to military strategy. No further biographical detail securely attested; no CBDB record.
