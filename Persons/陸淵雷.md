# Lù Yuānléi 陸淵雷

Republican-period Chinese-medicine reformer and educator (1894–1955), based in Shànghǎi. Co-founder of the Shànghǎi guóyī xuéyuàn 上海國醫學院 and the Shànghǎi zhōngguó yīxuéyuàn 上海中國醫學院 and one of the chief proponents of *kēxué huà* 科學化 (scientisation) of Chinese medicine. Author of the *Shānghán lùn jīnshì* 傷寒論今釋 (1931) and *Jīnguì yàolüè jīnshì* 金匱要略今釋 (1934), influential textbooks attempting to integrate Western biomedical concepts with the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* tradition.
