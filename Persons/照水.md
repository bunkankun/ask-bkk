Zhàoshuǐ 照水, *zì* **Xǐxīn** 洗心 ("Mind-Washing") — also styled **Chūntái** 春臺 ("Spring Terrace") in some prefatorial roles; often referred to as **Xiāngyán Xǐxīn** 香巖洗心. **Tonsure-teacher**: **Zhèngjué Rùnguāng Zé** 正覺潤光澤 (cf. [[KR6q0234]]); **dharma-transmission teacher** (*fùfǎ*): **Báiyún Xiànglín Běnzhēn** [[本真]] (1620–1691, cf. [[KR6q0571]]). Lifedates unknown — was 30 in **癸亥** = **1683** (the *fùfǎ* year), age 45 in **1698**. Native of **Jiànkāng Jiāngníng** 建康江寧 (Nánjīng); lay surname **Liú** 劉.

**Formation.** At 20 dreamt of a five-colored lotus and an adorned stupa with himself sitting alone on top, surrounded by chanting monks; on waking he took a knife to his hair vowing to leave the world — but his parents refused. At 24 both parents died, and he took tonsure at **Zhèngjuésì** 正覺寺 under **Rùn** 潤和尚 (= 潤光澤), who placed him under **Bǎiān Shī** 柏菴師 as direct teacher. Took up the *niànfó shì shuí* 念佛是誰 *gōngàn*. One winter midnight on walking *jīngxíng*, suddenly had insight. Took full ordination at **Míngshāzhōu** 鳴沙州. While at **Luóshān** 螺山 heard an elder discuss the **Línjì zài Huángbò chù chībàng** 臨濟在黃檗處喫棒 *gōngàn* — broke through and his earlier doubt-cluster fell away. Returned to Zhèngjué; sent south to *cān*-fǎng (Xìngshàn, Jiànfú, Fēngxué, Jīnsù). Finally a year under **Báiyún Xiànglín Běnzhēn** received final *fùfǎ* in **癸亥春** = **1683** at age 30.

**Abbacies (four).** **Huáyánsì** 華嚴, **Bǎoqìngsì** 寶慶, **Guānyīnsì** 觀音, **Xiāngyánsì** 香巖 (the principal seat giving him his sobriquet **Xiāngyán Xǐxīn**).
