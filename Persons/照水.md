Zhào-shuǐ 照水, *zì* **Xǐ-xīn** 洗心 ("Mind-Washing") — also styled **Chūn-tái** 春臺 ("Spring Terrace") in some prefatorial roles; often referred to as **Xiāng-yán Xǐ-xīn** 香巖洗心. **Tonsure-teacher**: **Zhèng-jué Rùn-guāng Zé** 正覺潤光澤 (cf. [[KR6q0234]]); **dharma-transmission teacher** (*fù-fǎ*): **Bái-yún Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** [[本真]] (1620–1691, cf. [[KR6q0571]]). Lifedates unknown — was 30 in **癸亥** = **1683** (the *fù-fǎ* year), age 45 in **1698**. Native of **Jiàn-kāng Jiāng-níng** 建康江寧 (Nán-jīng); lay surname **Liú** 劉.

**Formation.** At 20 dreamt of a five-colored lotus and an adorned stupa with himself sitting alone on top, surrounded by chanting monks; on waking he took a knife to his hair vowing to leave the world — but his parents refused. At 24 both parents died, and he took tonsure at **Zhèng-jué-sì** 正覺寺 under **Rùn** 潤和尚 (= 潤光澤), who placed him under **Bǎi-ān Shī** 柏菴師 as direct teacher. Took up the *niàn-fó shì shuí* 念佛是誰 *gōng-àn*. One winter midnight on walking *jīng-xíng*, suddenly had insight. Took full ordination at **Míng-shā-zhōu** 鳴沙州. While at **Luó-shān** 螺山 heard an elder discuss the **Lín-jì zài Huáng-bò chù chī-bàng** 臨濟在黃檗處喫棒 *gōng-àn* — broke through and his earlier doubt-cluster fell away. Returned to Zhèng-jué; sent south to *cān*-fǎng (Xìng-shàn, Jiàn-fú, Fēng-xué, Jīn-sù). Finally a year under **Bái-yún Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** received final *fù-fǎ* in **癸亥春** = **1683** at age 30.

**Abbacies (four).** **Huá-yán-sì** 華嚴, **Bǎo-qìng-sì** 寶慶, **Guān-yīn-sì** 觀音, **Xiāng-yán-sì** 香巖 (the principal seat giving him his sobriquet **Xiāng-yán Xǐ-xīn**).
