**Hóng Yìngmíng** 洪應明, *zì* Zì chéng 自誠, *hào* Huán chū dào rén 還初道人, was a late-Míng syncretic writer best known for the ***Cài gēn tán*** 菜根譚 (c. 1590), a foundational collection of aphorisms. Compiler of [[KR5h0034]] *Xiāo yáo xū jīng* 消搖墟經.
