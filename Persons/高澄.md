# 高澄
Gāo Chéng (521–549)

Son of [[高歡]] Gāo Huān and chancellor of the Eastern Wèi; continued the patronage of the Buddhist translation institute. Posthumously honored as Emperor Wénxiāng 文襄 of the Northern Qí after that dynasty's founding.
