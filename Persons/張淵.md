# Zhāng Yuān 張淵

A late-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar of *Yǎnnán* 剡南 (the southern Yǎn region in modern *Shèngzhōu* 嵊州, Zhèjiāng), *hào* **Qiánshìshì** 潛室氏 ("Master of the Hidden Chamber"). Author of the **《念佛起緣彌陀觀偈直解》** *Niànfó qǐyuán Mítuó guānjì zhíjiě* [[KR6p0114]] (X1195), a 1874 line-by-line *zhíjiě* exegesis of the popular eight-line *bājù guānjì* 八句觀偈 contemplation verse, intended to render Pure Land doctrinal content accessible to remote-village lay practitioners. He works within a regional Yǎn-region Pure Land literati circle that included the editorial collaborators *Zhāng Mèngxiān* 章夢仙 (*Lùyóushì* 鹿遊氏), *Lóu Rén* 樓仁 (*Shēnjìngshānshì* 深靜山氏), *Zhào Hóngwén* 趙洪文 (*Xǔzhāishì* 許齋氏), and the monk *Jièsù Liánfāng* 戒肅蓮芳. Lifedates not preserved.

The CBDB has multiple persons of the name 張淵 across various dynasties (e.g. CBDB id 25611 Sòng; id 199034 Míng with *bie-hao* 1429); none of these can be confidently matched to the *Yǎnnán* late-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar of this entry, who is presumably absent from CBDB.

Not to be confused with: the early Sòng *Zhāng Yuān* (CBDB 106636, b. 1264); the Yuán-Míng-era *Zhāng Yuān* of CBDB 199034 (b. 1429); the various other homonyms in CBDB.
