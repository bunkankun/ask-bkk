Liǎoyuán 了圓 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* mid-Míng), Míng-dynasty Buddhist monk, self-designated **Guān-shí shā-mén** 觀識沙門 ("monk of contemplative discernment"). According to the editorial preface of the *Fǎ-huá líng-yàn zhuàn* and the catalog records, he extracted the most striking miracle-narratives from three earlier *Lotus*-cult collections — [[惠詳]]'s 10-juan *Hóng-zàn Fǎ-huá zhuàn* (T2067 = [[KR6r0066]]), [[宗曉]]'s 4-juan *Fǎ-huá xiàn-yìng lù* 法華現應錄 (= [[KR6r0071]] *Fǎ-huá-jīng xiǎn-yìng lù*), and the 4-juan **真淨國師** Zhēn-jìng-guó-shī's *Hǎi-dōng chuán-hóng lù* 海東傳弘錄 — and edited them into the 2-juan **《法華靈驗傳》** *Fǎ-huá líng-yàn zhuàn* ([[KR6r0070]], X78 no. 1539). Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000038.
