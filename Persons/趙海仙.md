# 趙海仙

Zhào Hǎixiān 趙海仙 (late Qīng), physician of Xīnghuà 興化, northern Jiāngsū. Reckoned one of the "Three Great Physicians of Northern Jiāngsū in the late Qīng" alongside [[余奉仙]] Yú Fèngxiān of Fùníng and [[張子坪]] Zhāng Zǐpíng of Huáiān, as recorded in the biographical preface to [[KR3ep105]] *Yú Wúyán yīàn*.
