# 慧暉 (唐, Abhidharma scholar)

Huìhuī 慧暉 (DILA A001732; fl. mid-to-late 8th century CE) was a Tang dynasty Abhidharma monk and student of 圓暉 ([[圓暉]]). He lived at Xīmíng-sì 西明寺 in Chang'an and composed the 俱舍論頌疏義鈔 ([[KR6l0040]], X53n839, 3 juan), a sub-commentary on 圓暉's *Sòng Shū*. His biographical details are otherwise unrecorded.

# 慧暉 (宋, Chán master)

Huì-huī 慧暉 (alternate names: 自得慧暉, Zì-dé Huì-huī; also styled 淨慈慧暉; DILA A012808; 1097–1183)

Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Zì-dé 自得. 1097 – 22 December 1183 (lunar 淳熙 10.11.29), *shì-shòu* 87, *sēng-là* 75. Dharma-heir of **Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué** 宏智正覺 (1091–1157); thus dharma-grandson of [[子淳]] Dān-xiá Zǐ-chún and dharma-cousin of [[清了]] Zhēn-xiē Qīng-liǎo. Native of Shàng-yú 上虞 (Huì-jī 會稽), lay surname Zhāng 張 (though the *tǎ-míng* in *juan* 6 of his own *yǔlù* gives a divergent reading: Jīn-zhōu Ān-chéng 金州安城, lay surname Wáng 王 — an unresolved textual crux).

Tonsured in childhood by Chéng-zhào Dào-níng 澄照道凝; ordained at twelve-thirteen and took full precepts. At twenty went to Cháng-lú 長蘆 to consult Zhēn-xiē Qīng-liǎo and attained an initial opening; on return to his home region met Hóng-zhì, who after testing him declared him "a true son in the chamber" 室中真子 and conferred dharma-transmission.

Abbacies in sequence: Bǔ-tuó 補陀 (from Shào-xīng 7 / Dīng-sì / 1137), Wàn-shòu 萬壽, Jí-xiáng 吉祥, Xuě-dòu 雪竇. In Chún-xī 3 (1176) summoned by imperial edict to preside at Lín'ān Jìng-cí-sì 淨慈寺 — the premier court-patronised Chán seat of Southern-Sòng Hángzhōu — whence his abbatial style Jìng-cí Huì-huī. Retired in the autumn of Chún-xī 7 (1180) back to Xuě-dòu 雪竇 where he died three years later.

Four named dharma-heirs: **Xuě-dòu Dé-yún** 雪竇德雲 (DILA A040021), **Zhàng-xī Chóng-jiān** 仗錫崇堅 (A040034), **Huá-zàng Huì-zuò** 華藏慧祚 (A009618), **Xuě-dòu Huàn** 雪竇煥 (A040105). Principal work: his own six-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0360]]), edited by his dharma-brother [[了廣]] Zǎn-xī Liǎo-guǎng with *xià-yǔ* 下語 commentary by another dharma-brother [[明總]] Shí-shuāng Míng-zǒng — an unusually cooperative editorial arrangement reflecting the doctrinal solidarity of the Hóng-zhì / Cáo-dòng circle.
