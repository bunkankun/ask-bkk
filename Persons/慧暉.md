# 慧暉 (唐, Abhidharma scholar)

Huìhuī 慧暉 (DILA A001732; fl. mid-to-late 8th century CE) was a Tang dynasty Abhidharma monk and student of 圓暉 ([[圓暉]]). He lived at Xīmíngsì 西明寺 in Chang'an and composed the 俱舍論頌疏義鈔 ([[KR6l0040]], X53n839, 3 juan), a sub-commentary on 圓暉's *Sòng Shū*. His biographical details are otherwise unrecorded.

# 慧暉 (宋, Chán master)

Huìhuī 慧暉 (alternate names: 自得慧暉, Zìdé Huìhuī; also styled 淨慈慧暉; DILA A012808; 1097–1183)

Southern-Sòng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *zì* Zìdé 自得. 1097 – 22 December 1183 (lunar 淳熙 10.11.29), *shìshòu* 87, *sēnglà* 75. Dharma-heir of **Hóngzhì Zhèngjué** 宏智正覺 (1091–1157); thus dharma-grandson of [[子淳]] Dānxiá Zǐchún and dharma-cousin of [[清了]] Zhēnxiē Qīngliǎo. Native of Shàngyú 上虞 (Huìjī 會稽), lay surname Zhāng 張 (though the *tǎmíng* in *juan* 6 of his own *yǔlù* gives a divergent reading: Jīnzhōu Ānchéng 金州安城, lay surname Wáng 王 — an unresolved textual crux).

Tonsured in childhood by Chéngzhào Dàoníng 澄照道凝; ordained at twelve-thirteen and took full precepts. At twenty went to Chánglú 長蘆 to consult Zhēnxiē Qīngliǎo and attained an initial opening; on return to his home region met Hóngzhì, who after testing him declared him "a true son in the chamber" 室中真子 and conferred dharma-transmission.

Abbacies in sequence: Bǔtuó 補陀 (from Shàoxīng 7 / Dīngsì / 1137), Wànshòu 萬壽, Jíxiáng 吉祥, Xuědòu 雪竇. In Chúnxī 3 (1176) summoned by imperial edict to preside at Lín'ān Jìngcísì 淨慈寺 — the premier court-patronised Chán seat of Southern-Sòng Hángzhōu — whence his abbatial style Jìngcí Huìhuī. Retired in the autumn of Chúnxī 7 (1180) back to Xuědòu 雪竇 where he died three years later.

Four named dharma-heirs: **Xuědòu Déyún** 雪竇德雲 (DILA A040021), **Zhàngxī Chóngjiān** 仗錫崇堅 (A040034), **Huázàng Huìzuò** 華藏慧祚 (A009618), **Xuědòu Huàn** 雪竇煥 (A040105). Principal work: his own six-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0360]]), edited by his dharma-brother [[了廣]] Zǎnxī Liǎoguǎng with *xiàyǔ* 下語 commentary by another dharma-brother [[明總]] Shíshuāng Míngzǒng — an unusually cooperative editorial arrangement reflecting the doctrinal solidarity of the Hóngzhì / Cáodòng circle.
