Wú Tóng 吳侗, late-Míng Buddhist *jūshì* (lay practitioner), associated with the **Huángbòshān** 黃檗山 monastic community in **Fúqīng** 福清, Fújiàn. Lifedates not transmitted; floruit early 17th c. He collected the materials that **Mùchén Dàomín** 木陳道忞 (1596–1674) edited as the **《禪燈世譜》** *Chándēng shìpǔ* ([[KR6r0110]], X1601, 9 juan, prefaced 1632) — a tabular LínjìCáodòng lineage-genealogy. He is also the author of a 7-律 poem "**Spring stroll among Mt Huángbò**" 春日散步黃檗山中, preserved in the *Huángbò shānzhì* 黃檗山誌. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000456.
