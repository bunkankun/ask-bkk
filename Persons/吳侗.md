Wú Tóng 吳侗, late-Míng Buddhist *jū-shì* (lay practitioner), associated with the **Huáng-bò-shān** 黃檗山 monastic community in **Fú-qīng** 福清, Fú-jiàn. Lifedates not transmitted; floruit early 17th c. He collected the materials that **Mù-chén Dào-mín** 木陳道忞 (1596–1674) edited as the **《禪燈世譜》** *Chán-dēng shì-pǔ* ([[KR6r0110]], X1601, 9 juan, prefaced 1632) — a tabular Línjì-Cáo-dòng lineage-genealogy. He is also the author of a 7-律 poem "**Spring stroll among Mt Huáng-bò**" 春日散步黃檗山中, preserved in the *Huáng-bò shān-zhì* 黃檗山誌. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000456.
