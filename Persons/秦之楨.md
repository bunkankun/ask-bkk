**Qín Zhīzhēn** 秦之楨 (*zì* Huángshì 皇士, *fl.* early-mid Kāngxī, ca. 1700s–1720s, 清), Sūzhōu-region physician. Author of the *Shānghán dà bái* 傷寒大白 ([[KR3ef047]], 4 juan, 1714) — a clear pedagogical commentary on the *Shānghán lùn*. His lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
