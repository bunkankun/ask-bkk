Mid-Qīng physician of Shàoyáng 邵陽 in Húnán; *zì* Jīngwēi 荊威, *hào* Mèngjué Dàorén 夢覺道人. Conventionally given the lifedates 1771–1834. A Daoist-Buddhist syncretist medical writer whose work draws extensively on *Chán* and *qìgōng* / inner-alchemy idioms in describing pulse phenomenology. Author of [[KR3eb021]] *Sān zhǐ chán* (1827, 3 juan); also (according to the *Sān zhǐ chán* preface) of a *Shù mài jiě* 數脈解 (treatise on the rapid pulse) and a *Sān jiāo biàn* 三焦辨 (treatise on the three burners), composed in the same Daoist-mystical mode. No CBDB record. His teaching was influential in late-Qīng Hunanese medical pedagogy.
