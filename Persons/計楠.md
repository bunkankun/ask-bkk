**Jì Nán** 計楠 (*hào* **Shòuqiáo** 壽喬), Qīng physician of the late Qiánlóng / early Jiāqìng era (*fl.* 1780s–1820s by internal evidence from the 1804 Shěn Tāo postface to his **[[KR3eq010]]** *Kèchén yīhuà*), member of the Suzhou-Hangzhou *wénrén yīshēng* "literatus-physician" milieu. Practised at the *Géxiānlǐng* 葛仙嶺 site in Hángzhōu (the Daoist promontory associated with Gě Hóng 葛洪 / Bàopǔzǐ 抱朴子) and was scheduled to take up a *guǎngwén* 廣文 educational appointment in the second month of Jiāqìng 9 (1804). The Shěn Tāo postface attests a twenty-year friendship and a literary-medical circle including the now-deceased Xuěquán 雪泉, Shífān 石帆, and Lígǔ 梨谷. Not in CBDB; not in the standard biographical dictionaries of Qīng physicians (e.g. Zhōngguó yījí dàcídiǎn).
