**Jì Nán** 計楠 (*hào* **Shòu-qiáo** 壽喬), Qīng physician of the late Qián-lóng / early Jiā-qìng era (*fl.* 1780s–1820s by internal evidence from the 1804 Shěn Tāo postface to his **[[KR3eq010]]** *Kè-chén yī-huà*), member of the Suzhou-Hangzhou *wén-rén yī-shēng* "literatus-physician" milieu. Practised at the *Gé-xiān-lǐng* 葛仙嶺 site in Hángzhōu (the Daoist promontory associated with Gě Hóng 葛洪 / Bào-pǔ-zǐ 抱朴子) and was scheduled to take up a *guǎng-wén* 廣文 educational appointment in the second month of Jiā-qìng 9 (1804). The Shěn Tāo postface attests a twenty-year friendship and a literary-medical circle including the now-deceased Xuě-quán 雪泉, Shí-fān 石帆, and Lí-gǔ 梨谷. Not in CBDB; not in the standard biographical dictionaries of Qīng physicians (e.g. Zhōng-guó yī-jí dà-cí-diǎn).
