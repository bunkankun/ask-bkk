# Wáng Tíng 王庭

Early-Qīng lay scholar of Zuìlǐ 檇李 (Jiāxīng 嘉興). Preface-writer (Kāngxī 11 = 1672) and co-sponsor of the publication of [[KR6q0215]] *Xūzhōu chánshī zhù Bā shí guī jǔ sòng*. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.
