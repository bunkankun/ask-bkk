**Lǐ Péng** 李彭 (fl. *Yuán-yòu*–*Xuān-hé*, c. 1090–1130), *zì* Shāng-lǎo 商老, of Nán-kāng-jūn 南康軍 Jiàn-chāng 建昌 (Jiāng-xī). Per Chén Zhèn-sūn's *Zhí-zhāi shū-lù jiě-tí*, *cóng-sūn* (great-nephew) of Lǐ Gōng-zé 李公擇 (Lǐ Cháng 李常). Wáng Míng-qīng's *Huī-zhǔ-lù* gives a different identification — grandson of Lǐ Dìng 李定 (the *Zhòng-qiú* censor of the *Wū-tái shī-àn*) — both attributions uncertain. Lǐ Péng held no recorded office; the *Sòng shǐ* has no biography. Listed by Lǚ Běn-zhōng 呂本中 in the *Jiāng-xī shī-shè zōng-pài tú*, fifteenth in rank, immediately after Hán Jū. Author of *Rì shè-yuán jí* 日涉園集 [[KR4d0120]] in 10 *juǎn* (originally; *Sìkù* reconstruction). Correspondents include Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]], Zhāng Lěi 張耒 [[張耒]], and Liú Xī-zhòng 劉羲仲. Liú Kè-zhuāng's *Hòu-cūn shī-huà* praises Lǐ Péng's broad reading and strong memory but criticises his narrow poetic register.
