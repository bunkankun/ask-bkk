**Lǐ Péng** 李彭 (fl. *Yuányòu*–*Xuānhé*, c. 1090–1130), *zì* Shānglǎo 商老, of Nánkāngjūn 南康軍 Jiànchāng 建昌 (Jiāngxī). Per Chén Zhènsūn's *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí*, *cóngsūn* (great-nephew) of Lǐ Gōngzé 李公擇 (Lǐ Cháng 李常). Wáng Míngqīng's *Huīzhǔlù* gives a different identification — grandson of Lǐ Dìng 李定 (the *Zhòngqiú* censor of the *Wūtái shīàn*) — both attributions uncertain. Lǐ Péng held no recorded office; the *Sòng shǐ* has no biography. Listed by Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 in the *Jiāngxī shīshè zōngpài tú*, fifteenth in rank, immediately after Hán Jū. Author of *Rì shèyuán jí* 日涉園集 [[KR4d0120]] in 10 *juǎn* (originally; *Sìkù* reconstruction). Correspondents include Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]], Zhāng Lěi 張耒 [[張耒]], and Liú Xīzhòng 劉羲仲. Liú Kèzhuāng's *Hòucūn shīhuà* praises Lǐ Péng's broad reading and strong memory but criticises his narrow poetic register.
