# Dàofēng 道峯

Táng monk of *Dàjiànfúsì* 大薦福寺 in Cháng'ān. Known principally as the author of the preface to [[太賢]] Tàixián's *Púsà jièběn zōngyào* 菩薩戒本宗要 ([[KR6k0193]]), which praises Tàixián as "Dharma-master Dàxián of the Eastern Country" and identifies him with the *Yújiā zǎn* 瑜伽纂 Yogācāra compilation tradition. Beyond this preface no biographical information is preserved.
