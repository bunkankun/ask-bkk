# Zhāng Xún 張恂

Qīng lay Buddhist; editor (*biān yuè* 編閱) of [[如乾|Hān-xiū Rú-qián]] 憨休如乾's *Qiāo kōng yí xiǎng* 敲空遺響 [[KR6q0229]]. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Part of the late-17th / early-18th century Sichuan lay-Buddhist community that supported regional Chán publishing.
