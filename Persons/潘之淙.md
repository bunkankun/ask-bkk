Late-Míng calligrapher and theorist; *zì* Wúshēng 無聲, *hào* Dá-zhāi 達齋, of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). Active in the late *Wànlì* through *Chóngzhēn* decades (fl. 1627 per catalog meta). With his elder brother Pān Zhīqí 潘之淇 (*zì* Ěr-zhān 爾瞻) both noted as calligraphers. Author of [[KR3h0052]] *Shūfǎ líguō* 書法離鉤 (10 *juàn*), a late-Míng systematic calligraphic compendium taking its title from the Chán paradox of "detachment from method" — calligraphic method must be entered, transcended, and then re-entered.
