**Minamoto Tokuyō** 源德輿 (Edo-period Japan, late 18th – early 19th c.), son of [[源元凱]] Minamoto Genkai and collaborator on the doctrinal innovations of *Wēnbìng zhī yánjiū* ([[KR3eg025]]), the substantial Edo *kanpō* monograph on warm-disease that grew out of the Tenmei 8 (1788) Kyoto epidemic.
