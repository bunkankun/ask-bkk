Guō Xiángzhèng 郭祥正 (fl. *Xīníng*–*Yuányòu*, c. 1035–c. 1113), *zì* Gōngfǔ 功父, self-styled Zuìyín xiānshēng 醉吟先生 (after Bái Jūyì) — and later Zhāngnán làngshì 漳南浪士 (after his *Zhāngzhōu* internment). Of *Dāngtú* 當塗 (modern An-huī Mǎānshān). *Jìnshì* (date uncertain). Held *Tōngpàn Tīngzhōu* and as *zhī Duānzhōu*. Famously praised by Wáng Ānshí 王安石 [[王安石]] as *Tàibái hòushēn* (Lǐ Bái's reincarnation) — the *Sòngshǐ* records that Mèi Yáochén 梅堯臣 [[梅堯臣]] saw Guō's first-month-old infant verses and declared him so. Guō's loyalty to Wáng Ānshí is preserved in his three poems offered at Wáng's tomb at *Jiāngníng* (recorded in *Qīngshān jí*); he had been a Wáng-recommended officer dismissed early in his career on factional fall-out. *Yuánfēng* 5 / 1082, while *zhī Héféi*, demoted to *Zhāngnán* internment for three years (*Yuánfēng* 5 / 1082 to *Yuányòu* 1 / 1086) — confirmed by his own *Làngshì gē* and *Zhāngnán shūshì*, which the *Sìkù* editors note supplements *Sòngshǐ*'s lacuna here. Eventually retired to *Qīngshān* near *Dāngtú* (the *Qīngshān* in his collection's title commemorates Lǐ Bái's *Qīngshān*-burial site). Two collections: the *Qīngshān jí* 青山集 in 30 *juǎn* (= [[KR4d0096]]; directory missing in /home/Shared/krp/) and the *Qīngshān xùjí* 青山續集 [[KR4d0097]] in 7 *juǎn*. *Sòngshǐ* 444.
