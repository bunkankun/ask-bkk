Guō Xiáng-zhèng 郭祥正 (fl. *Xī-níng*–*Yuán-yòu*, c. 1035–c. 1113), *zì* Gōng-fǔ 功父, self-styled Zuì-yín xiān-shēng 醉吟先生 (after Bái Jū-yì) — and later Zhāng-nán làng-shì 漳南浪士 (after his *Zhāng-zhōu* internment). Of *Dāng-tú* 當塗 (modern An-huī Mǎ-ān-shān). *Jìn-shì* (date uncertain). Held *Tōng-pàn Tīng-zhōu* and as *zhī Duān-zhōu*. Famously praised by Wáng Ān-shí 王安石 [[王安石]] as *Tài-bái hòu-shēn* (Lǐ Bái's reincarnation) — the *Sòng-shǐ* records that Mèi Yáo-chén 梅堯臣 [[梅堯臣]] saw Guō's first-month-old infant verses and declared him so. Guō's loyalty to Wáng Ān-shí is preserved in his three poems offered at Wáng's tomb at *Jiāng-níng* (recorded in *Qīng-shān jí*); he had been a Wáng-recommended officer dismissed early in his career on factional fall-out. *Yuán-fēng* 5 / 1082, while *zhī Hé-féi*, demoted to *Zhāng-nán* internment for three years (*Yuán-fēng* 5 / 1082 to *Yuán-yòu* 1 / 1086) — confirmed by his own *Làng-shì gē* and *Zhāng-nán shū-shì*, which the *Sìkù* editors note supplements *Sòng-shǐ*'s lacuna here. Eventually retired to *Qīng-shān* near *Dāng-tú* (the *Qīng-shān* in his collection's title commemorates Lǐ Bái's *Qīng-shān*-burial site). Two collections: the *Qīng-shān jí* 青山集 in 30 *juǎn* (= KR4d0096; directory missing in /home/Shared/krp/) and the *Qīng-shān xù-jí* 青山續集 [[KR4d0097]] in 7 *juǎn*. *Sòng-shǐ* 444.
