**Taki Mototane** 多紀元簡 (also written 丹波元簡; *zì* Renpu 廉夫, *hào* Rekusō 櫟窗 / Rekuin Setsusha 櫟蔭拙者 / Keizan 桂山; 1755–1810), Edo-period hereditary physician of the Taki / Tamba 丹波 shogunal medical house. Eldest son of Taki Motonori 多紀元徳 (Ranke 藍渓); grandson of Taki Mototaka 多紀元孝 (founder of the *Seijukan* 躋壽館 private academy in 1765); **father** of Taki Genin 多紀元胤 and Taki Genken 多紀元堅. Head of the shogunal *Igaku-kan* 醫學館 (the renamed *Seijukan*, brought under direct shogunal administration in 1791 during his father's tenure) and the principal architect of the late-Edo Japanese *kǎojù*-medical philological school. Author of the canonical critical commentaries *Sùwèn shí* 素問識 (on the *Sùwèn*), *Língshū shí* 靈樞識 (on the *Língshū*), *Shānghán lùn jíyì* 傷寒論輯義 (on the *Shānghán lùn*), *Jīnguì yàolüè jíyì* 金匱要略輯義, *Màijīng* commentary, and the *Yī shèng* 醫賸 ([[KR3eq052]], *c.* 1803). His *kǎojù*-medical method — combining philological rigor, encyclopedic command of Chinese textual evidence, and openness to Korean *Yīfāng lèijù* 醫方類聚 and Dutch *rangaku* anatomical findings — set the standard for late-Edo and Meiji-era classical-medical scholarship. He also began the *Yījí kǎo* 醫籍考 ([[KR3es003]]), which was substantially drafted and completed posthumously by his son [[丹波元胤]] (Taki Motoin). Cataloged under 江戶 rather than the catalog's 清.
