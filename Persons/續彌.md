Xùmí 續彌 (also written **Xùmí** 續禰; the catalog and DILA give the variants 續彌 / 續禰 / 續稱). *Hào* **Méichè** 梅徹 ("Plum-Penetrating"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānzé Jīnéng** [[機能]] (b. 1618). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Luótián** 羅田 (Húběi), in the Yíshuǐ region. Co-recorder (*tóng lù*, with [[道崇]]) of [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B417, prefaced 1677. As Jīnéng's principal *fǎsì*, registered in 《五燈全書》 vol. 108.
