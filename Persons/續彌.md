Xù-mí 續彌 (also written **Xù-mí** 續禰; the catalog and DILA give the variants 續彌 / 續禰 / 續稱). *Hào* **Méi-chè** 梅徹 ("Plum-Penetrating"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-zé Jī-néng** [[機能]] (b. 1618). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Luó-tián** 羅田 (Hú-běi), in the Yí-shuǐ region. Co-recorder (*tóng lù*, with [[道崇]]) of [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B417, prefaced 1677. As Jī-néng's principal *fǎ-sì*, registered in 《五燈全書》 vol. 108.
