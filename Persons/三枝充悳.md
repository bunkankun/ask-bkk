Sānzhī Chōngdé 三枝充悳 / Saigusa Mitsuyoshi (1923–2010) — modern Japanese Buddhologist and philosopher; author of standard works on Indian Buddhism and an annotated Japanese translation of the *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*.
