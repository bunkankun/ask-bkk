**Luó Zhītì** 羅知悌 (*hào* Tàiwú 太無), Yuán physician of Hángzhōu in the [[劉完素]] Liú Héjiān transmission line; the master under whom [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng studied in the early 1320s and through whom Zhū received the synthesis of the SòngJīnYuán Four Masters' doctrines. Named in Zhū's self-preface to [[KR3eq027]] / [[KR3e0060]] *Gézhì yúlùn*.
