Late-Qīng (Guāng-xù-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Guàn-sān** 貫三, sobriquet **Pí-líng jū-shì** 毗陵居士 (Pí-líng = Cháng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). Lifedates unrecorded. Author of two-juan *Jīn-gāng jīng yì-jiě* 金剛經易解 ([[KR6c0098]], X25 no. 510), self-prefaced **光緒十五年歲次己丑三月之望 = mid-spring 1889**, with sponsoring prefaces by **Táng Yǎng-yú** 唐養愚 (sobriquet **Lái-yún-zǐ** 來雲子) and **Liú Shào-nán** 劉紹南 (sobriquet **Yuán-jué jū-shì** 圓覺居士). Also composed a parallel *Bān-ruò xīn-jīng yì-jiě* 般若心經易解, on the model that the *Heart Sūtra* is the *Vajracchedikā*'s essence in compact form.
