Late-Qīng (Guāng-xù-era) Buddhist layman, *zì* **Guànsān** 貫三, sobriquet **Pílíng jūshì** 毗陵居士 (Pílíng = Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū). Lifedates unrecorded. Author of two-juan *Jīngāng jīng yìjiě* 金剛經易解 ([[KR6c0098]], X25 no. 510), self-prefaced **光緒十五年歲次己丑三月之望 = mid-spring 1889**, with sponsoring prefaces by **Táng Yǎngyú** 唐養愚 (sobriquet **Láiyúnzǐ** 來雲子) and **Liú Shàonán** 劉紹南 (sobriquet **Yuánjué jūshì** 圓覺居士). Also composed a parallel *Bānruò xīnjīng yìjiě* 般若心經易解, on the model that the *Heart Sūtra* is the *Vajracchedikā*'s essence in compact form.
