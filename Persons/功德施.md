# 功德施

Gōngdéshī (Skt. Guṇadā)

Indian Yogācāra-Mādhyamika commentator on the *Vajracchedikā*, the author of *Jīngāng bānruò bōluómì jīng pò qǔzháo bù huài jiǎmíng lùn* 金剛般若波羅蜜經破取著不壞假名論 ([[KR6c0036]], T no. 1515). His commentary is characterized as 洞燭空假之致 ("illuminating the tendency of the empty-and-conventional") and is one of the principal substrate references used by [[曾鳳儀]] Zēng Fèngyí in [[KR6c0059]].
