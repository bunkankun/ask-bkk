**Fàn Qìng** 範慶 (*zì* **Cúnsuǒ** 存所, of Jiànjiāng 劍江), mid-Míng official; author of the 1548 preface to [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's *Nǚkē cuōyào* 女科撮要 ([[KR3ei009]]), commissioned by the *Dàsīmǎ Zhōngchéng Yuēānwēng* 大司馬中丞約菴翁 to print Xuē's collected medical case-records (*Jiājū yīlù* 家居醫錄).
