Dèng Shēn 鄧深 (fl. 1140s–1170s), *zì* Zīdào 資道 (per *Gǔ Luó zhì*) and Shēnbó 紳伯 (per the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* original-attribution), *hào* Dàyǐn 大隱 (whence the collection title and the Sòng-poetry-anthology *Wànxìng tōngpǔ*'s erroneous *Yuán*-dynasty attribution corrected by the *Sìkù* editors). Native of Xiāngyīn 湘陰 (Húnán); *Shàoxīng*-period *jìnshì*. Tested for *Jiàoguān*; entered as *Tàifǔ chéng* 太府丞; in *lúnduì* (audience-rotation) memorialised on Jīngxī, Húnánběi household-and-scholar-officials' folkways — Gāozōng accepted-and-praised. *Tíjǔ Guǎngxī shìbó* (Maritime Trade Commissioner of Guǎngxī); for-aged-parents requested convenient-prefecture; Prefect of Héngzhōu — at *Chálíng*, *Ānrén*, the *Xīdòng zhī dào* (mountain-valley bandits) on hearing-of-his-name *tiēxī* (settled-down). Promoted to *Tóngchuān cáo*; the salt-and-wine *xūé* (false-quotas) had-long been the people's-affliction; petitioned the court to remit the Sìchuān *yǐn* of 470,000.

Yú Yǔnwén 虞允文 wrote-letter to him: *bùwèi qiángyù; sījì sīmín; tǐngrán zhī cāo, wèijiàn jìnbǐ* (not-fearing the strong; thinking-to-aid the masses; resolutely-upright in conduct; not-yet-seen in recent-times). Late retired with *Cháoqǐng dàfū*. Loved his Dōnghú villa where he built the *Míngxiù* pavilion. His prose-collection in 10 *juǎn* recorded by *Gǔ Luó zhì*; the present *Sìkù* version is 2 *juǎn* of poetry only.
