Dèng Shēn 鄧深 (fl. 1140s–1170s), *zì* Zī-dào 資道 (per *Gǔ Luó zhì*) and Shēn-bó 紳伯 (per the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* original-attribution), *hào* Dà-yǐn 大隱 (whence the collection title and the Sòng-poetry-anthology *Wàn-xìng tōng-pǔ*'s erroneous *Yuán*-dynasty attribution corrected by the *Sì-kù* editors). Native of Xiāng-yīn 湘陰 (Hú-nán); *Shào-xīng*-period *jìn-shì*. Tested for *Jiào-guān*; entered as *Tài-fǔ chéng* 太府丞; in *lún-duì* (audience-rotation) memorialised on Jīng-xī, Hú-nán-běi household-and-scholar-officials' folkways — Gāozōng accepted-and-praised. *Tí-jǔ Guǎng-xī shì-bó* (Maritime Trade Commissioner of Guǎng-xī); for-aged-parents requested convenient-prefecture; Prefect of Héng-zhōu — at *Chá-líng*, *Ān-rén*, the *Xī-dòng zhī dào* (mountain-valley bandits) on hearing-of-his-name *tiē-xī* (settled-down). Promoted to *Tóng-chuān cáo*; the salt-and-wine *xū-é* (false-quotas) had-long been the people's-affliction; petitioned the court to remit the Sì-chuān *yǐn* of 470,000.

Yú Yǔn-wén 虞允文 wrote-letter to him: *bù-wèi qiáng-yù; sī-jì sī-mín; tǐng-rán zhī cāo, wèi-jiàn jìn-bǐ* (not-fearing the strong; thinking-to-aid the masses; resolutely-upright in conduct; not-yet-seen in recent-times). Late retired with *Cháo-qǐng dà-fū*. Loved his Dōng-hú villa where he built the *Míng-xiù* pavilion. His prose-collection in 10 *juǎn* recorded by *Gǔ Luó zhì*; the present *Sì-kù* version is 2 *juǎn* of poetry only.
