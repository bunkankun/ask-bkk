Yáng Zhìpú 楊志樸 (self-styled Yún-xī xián-lǎo 雲溪閑老, "Idle Elder of Cloud-Stream," of Wèi-nán 魏南) was a Jīn-era Daoist who authored the short preface to the Quánzhēn *nèidān* anthology *Huìzhēn jí* 會真集 ([[KR5a0248]] / DZ 247), in which he records meeting its author Wáng Jíchāng 王吉昌 (Chāorán zǐ 超然子) at the Qīng-yì Qīng-shén Temple 青義清神觀 and receiving the text from two of Wáng's disciples for prefatory composition.
