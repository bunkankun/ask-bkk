# Chéngzhèng 成正

Late-Míng Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master, *shǒuzuò* 首座 under [[元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái (1576–1630) at Bóshān; recorder (*lù* 錄) of the six-juan digest **Bóshān Wúyì dàshī yǔlù jíyào** 博山無異大師語錄集要 ([[KR6q0366]]; Jiāxīng J27 no. 197, Xuzangjing X72 no. 1436 preserves only the preface apparatus) and compiler (*jí* 集) of Yuánlái's widely-circulated practice manual **Bóshān cānchán jǐngyǔ** 博山參禪警語 ([[KR6q0148]]; X63 n1257). Lifedates unrecorded. DILA A000374.
