# Chéng-zhèng 成正

Late-Míng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán master, *shǒu-zuò* 首座 under [[元來]] Wú-yì Yuán-lái (1576–1630) at Bó-shān; recorder (*lù* 錄) of the six-juan digest **Bó-shān Wú-yì dà-shī yǔlù jí-yào** 博山無異大師語錄集要 ([[KR6q0366]]; Jiāxīng J27 no. 197, Xuzangjing X72 no. 1436 preserves only the preface apparatus) and compiler (*jí* 集) of Yuán-lái's widely-circulated practice manual **Bó-shān cān-chán jǐng-yǔ** 博山參禪警語 ([[KR6q0148]]; X63 n1257). Lifedates unrecorded. DILA A000374.
