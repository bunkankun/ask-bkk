Late-Míng (*Wàn-lì* 萬曆 era, *c.* 1573–1620) scholar of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (modern Zhèjiāng). With [[屠喬孫]] Tú Qiáo-sūn, Xiàng Lín co-produced the 100-juàn recompilation of the lost *Shíliù-guó Chūnqiū* 十六國春秋 [[KR2i0005]] of [[崔鴻]] Cuī Hóng (478–525). Beyond this single shared editorial work, no biographical detail survives. CBDB has no entry.
