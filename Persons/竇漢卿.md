# 竇漢卿 Dòu Hànqīng

**Dòu Hànqīng** 竇漢卿 (given name **Dòu Mò** 竇默, *zì* Zǐshēng 子聲, 1196–1280), Jīn-Yuán transitional acupuncturist and Confucian scholar of Guǎngpíng 廣平 (modern Hébei). Author of the *Zhēn jīng zhǐnán* 針經指南 ([[KR3ee017]] = [[KR3e0107]]) including the influential *Biāoyōu fù* 標幽賦 acupuncture rhapsody. Served at the Yuán court under Khubilai Khaghan. The *Biāoyōu fù* is one of the most-commented didactic acupuncture rhapsodies in the entire tradition.
