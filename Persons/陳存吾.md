Mid-Qīng (Jiā-qìng-era) Buddhist layman of **GǔMǐn** 古閩 (Fújiàn), formal name **Chén Yùpiān** 陳玉篇, *zì* **Cúnwú** 存吾 (under which he typically signs his Buddhist works), sobriquet **Báiyún shānrén** 白雲山人 ("White-Cloud Mountain-Dweller"). Local *xiāng jìnshì* (regional jìn-shì-equivalent). Lifedates unrecorded. Compiled the two-juan *Jīngāng jīng chǎnshuō* 金剛經闡說 ([[KR6c0096]], X25 no. 508) with self-preface dated **嘉慶二十一年秋七月上浣 = autumn 1816**; the work was reprinted with an additional preface by **Hóng Shòuchūn** 洪壽椿 of Táiyáng (Taiwan) dated **同治六年丁卯梅夏 = early summer 1867**, marking it as one of the earlier *Vajracchedikā* commentaries to circulate substantially in Taiwan.
