**Péi Xuàn** 裴鉉 was a Táng Daoist author, associated with the Zhōngnán shān 終南山, who submitted the memorial (*jìn biǎo* 進表) accompanying the **延壽赤書** ([[KR5c0274]] / [[KR5c0275]]) — a Táng life-prolonging scripture — to the throne. He styled himself "lín chén" 林臣 (mountain-forest subject), signalling recluse status.
