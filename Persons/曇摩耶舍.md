Tánmóyēshě 曇摩耶舍 (Skt. *Dharmayaśas* "Renowned for the Dharma"; DILA Authority A001796) was a Kaśmīra (*Jìbīn* 罽賓) Buddhist monk-translator who worked during the Yao Qin (姚秦, 384–417 CE). He collaborated with Dharmagupta 曇摩崛多 ([[曇摩崛多]]) on the translation of the *Śāriputrābhidharma* 舍利弗阿毘曇論 ([[KR6l0013]], T1548, 30 juan), the canonical Abhidharma text of the Dharmaguptaka school. His alternate name 大毘婆沙 may refer to his expertise in Vibhāṣā literature. His precise dates and further biographical details are not recorded in the surviving sources.
