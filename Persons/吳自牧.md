Wú Zì-mù 吳自牧 (*fl.* 1274), late-Southern-Sòng author, native of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu). Otherwise entirely undocumented. Composed the *Mèng-liáng lù* 夢梁錄 ([[KR2k0118]]) — a 20-juan monograph on the urban culture of the Southern-Sòng capital Lín-ān — with autograph preface dated Mid-Autumn day of *jiǎ-xū* (= *Xián-chún* 10 = 1274), two years before the Yuán capture of Hángzhōu in 1276. The Sìkù tíyào notes that Wáng Shìzhēn 王士禎, working from a manuscript that lacked the preface, did not know the work's authorship; the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* citations confirm the attribution. The catalog meta erroneously gives the author's *floruit* as 1670 — a transcription error for 1270s; corrected here.
