Míng-guǎng 明廣. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sūn* (dharma-grandson) of **Mì-xíng Jì-rěn** [[寂忍]] (?–?). Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with co-disciple [[明珍]] of his dharma-grandfather's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0545]] 《密行忍禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B428, prefaced 1694.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Míng-dynasty **Míng-guǎng** 明廣 (DILA A044246), 118th abbot of **Jìng-cí-sì** 淨慈寺 (Hángzhōu); native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘, lay surname **Zhū** 諸. Recorded in 《敕建淨慈寺志》 vol. 9.
