# 僧茂

Sēngmào (僧茂) was a Buddhist monk in Cháng'ān 長安 active in 384–385. He is named in [[道安]] Dàoān's preface to the *Ekottara-āgama* [[KR6a0126]] (T125) as one of those who helped check the translation produced by [[曇摩難提]] Dharmanandi for omissions. Dates are not recorded.
