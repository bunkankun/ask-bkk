**Wáng Dàqīng** 王大卿 (*fl.* 1711), early-Qīng literatus of *Éhú* 鵝湖 (Jiāngxī). Author of the 1711 (*Kāngxī xīnmǎo* 康熙辛卯) reprint preface to [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián's *Xiǎo'ér tuīná fāngmài huóyīng mìzhǐ quánshū* 小兒推拿方脈活嬰秘旨全書 ([[KR3ej069]]). Wáng worked with Shū Shíqīng 舒時卿 (of *Hóngdū* 洪都) and Zhāng Kāiwēng 張開翁 to re-edit and re-print the work from a surviving manuscript after its original printing-blocks were lost in the MíngQīng transition.
