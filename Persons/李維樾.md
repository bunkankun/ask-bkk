Late-Míng official from Ānfú 安福 in Jízhōu 吉州 (modern Jiāngxī), courtesy name Yìnchāng 蔭昌. CBDB id 77867 attests his death-date as 1654. With his fellow-townsman Lín Zēngzhì 林增志 he co-edited the *Zhōngzhēn lù* 忠貞錄 ([[KR2g0013]]), a late-Míng commemorative compilation of the writings and posthumous documentation of the *Jiànwén* 建文 martyr Zhuō Jìng 卓敬, also of Ānfú. The work is part of the wider Wàn-lì-onward project of rehabilitating and commemorating the *Jiànwén yíchén* 建文遺臣.
