Late-Míng official from Ān-fú 安福 in Jí-zhōu 吉州 (modern Jiāngxī), courtesy name Yìn-chāng 蔭昌. CBDB id 77867 attests his death-date as 1654. With his fellow-townsman Lín Zēng-zhì 林增志 he co-edited the *Zhōng-zhēn lù* 忠貞錄 ([[KR2g0013]]), a late-Míng commemorative compilation of the writings and posthumous documentation of the *Jiànwén* 建文 martyr Zhuō Jìng 卓敬, also of Ān-fú. The work is part of the wider Wàn-lì-onward project of rehabilitating and commemorating the *Jiànwén yí-chén* 建文遺臣.
