Yuánjìng 元敬 (lifedates unknown, *fl.* late Southern Sòng), Southern-Sòng monk; *hào* Jiéān 節菴. According to the prefatory matter of the *Wǔlín Xīhú gāosēng shìlüè* and the same author's biographical entry in [[陳起]]'s *Liǔtáng wàijí* 柳塘外集 (j. 3, *Xīhú gāosēng zhuàn xù* 西湖高僧傳序), he was lecturer (*jiǎngshī* 講師) at the **Mǎnǎo Yuàn** 瑪瑙院 on Xīhú 西湖 in Hángzhōu (Wǔlín 武林), one of the principal Tiāntāi-tradition study-houses in the Southern-Sòng capital.

Together with the East-Wenzhou monk [[元復]] (Yuánfù, *hào* Xiūān 休菴) he co-compiled the **《武林西湖高僧事略》** *Wǔlín Xīhú gāosēng shìlüè* ([[KR6r0064]], X77 no. 1526), a 1-juan biographical sketch-anthology of 30 eminent monks of the Hángzhōu Xīhú establishments — the foundational compendium for the West-Lake Buddhist tradition. The text was prefaced by [[陳起]] Chén Qǐ in the late Southern Sòng. Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000128.
