**Liáng Zhāngjù** 梁章鉅 (1775–1849, *zì* Chǎilín 茝林, *hào* Tuìān 退庵), Qīng official and prolific literary compiler of Fújiàn, *jìnshì* 1802 (Jiāqìng 7), provincial governor of Guǎngxī and Jiāngsū. Author of *Tuìān suíbǐ* 退庵隨筆, *Wénfāng zájì* 文房雜記, *Tuìān shīchāo* 退庵詩鈔, *Chēng wèi lù* 稱謂錄, and many other miscellanies; cited in [[KR3eq007]] *Yǒuyúzhāi yīhuà*.
