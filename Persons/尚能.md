Shàng-néng 尚能, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Zhì-tán Zuì-zhèng** [[最正]] in the Lín-yě → Zì-xián → Tiān-mù → Xiù-yě → Zhì-tán line. Co-compiler with **Shàng-yíng** [[尚瑩]] of the 1-juan 《智覃正禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0497]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
