Shàngnéng 尚能, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Zhìtán Zuìzhèng** [[最正]] in the Línyě → Zìxián → Tiānmù → Xiùyě → Zhìtán line. Co-compiler with **Shàngyíng** [[尚瑩]] of the 1-juan 《智覃正禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0497]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
