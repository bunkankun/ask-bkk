**Péng Zhìzhōng** 彭致中 was a Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist compiler, of [[KR5d0123]] *Míng hè yú yīn* 鳴鶴餘音 — a collection of inner-alchemical poetry (*cí* 詞 lyrics) compiled 1347.
