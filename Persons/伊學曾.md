**Yī Xuézēng** 伊學曾 (*fl.* mid-late 18th c.), Qīng obstetrician originally from Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng). In *Qiánlóng guǐsì* (1773) he acquired the manuscript *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* ([[KR3ei053]]) of [[單南山]] Shàn Nánshān via [[錢升穟]] Qián Shēngsuì, and through its application became famous as an obstetrician in his native district. His own clinical case-notes were posthumously appended to the 1856 print of the work as *Nǚkē yīàn* 女科醫案 (7 *juǎn*). Manuscript inherited by his nephew [[蔡益亭]] Cài Yìtíng. Not in CBDB.
