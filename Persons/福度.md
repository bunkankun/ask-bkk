Fú-dù 福度, *zì* **Méi-xī** 梅溪 ("Plum Stream"); often referred to as **Dōng-shān Méi-xī** 東山梅溪 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén** [[印文]]. Born **明崇禎丁丑正月三十寅時** = **1637-02-24** Gregorian; died **康熙四十年正月** = **1701-02-08–1701-03-09** (lunar 1), age 63, *sēng-là* 47. Native of **Xī-Shǔ Yǒng-chuān** 西蜀永川 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Zhāng** 張. His mother née **Wú** 吳 carried him into Guì-zhōu as a child.

**Formation.** At 16 took tonsure under **Dōng-shān Zhī-rú** 東山知如和尚. At 20 sought the ten precepts under **Kuò-làng dà-shī** 闊浪大師. At 21 went to **Diān-chéng** 滇城 (Yún-nán) to study under **Líng-yǐn** 靈隱禪師. **己亥春** = **1659** spring left his teacher; spent a year at **Dōng-shān** 東山 in Guì-zhōu. **庚子** = **1660** winter built a hut with his friend **Zhào-miàn-shān** 照面山. **辛丑冬** = **1661** winter Líng-yǐn arrived at **Dòu-bǎo Guān-yīn-sì** 竇堡觀音寺 in Guì-zhōu and convened a *jié-zhì* — Fú-dù served as *wéi-nuó* and on the **Buddha's enlightenment day** (lunar 12/8) received *fù-fǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Ān-shùn Qīng-yuán** 安順清源, **Ān-lóng Huá-guāng** 安籠華光, **Guǎng-xī Yù-huáng** 廣西玉皇, **Méng-huà Děng-jué** 蒙化等覺, **Yún-zhōu Yù-gé** 雲州玉閣, **Zōng-zhōu Guān-yīn** 宗州觀音, **Pǔ-ān Yuán-tōng** 普安圓通, **Péng-lái Yǒng-xīng** 蓬萊永興 — i.e. wide circulation across **Yún-nán / Guì-zhōu / Guǎng-xī**.

**Disciples (ten or more).** **Huì-yǐng Qìng-xù** 慧穎慶緒 [[慶緒]] (compiler of his *yǔ-lù*), **Shí-lín Dào-huī** 石林道輝, **Xiá-zhāng Hǎi-wěi** 霞章海偉, **Shào-nán Zhēn-jiě** 紹南真解, **Jì-ěr Xìng-jiān** 繼爾性堅, **Fǎ-yǔ Zhào-rùn** 法雨照潤.

**Death.** Returned to **Tiān-tóng** to clean Mì-zǔ's stupa; on the way back stopped at **Hàn-yáng** 漢陽; fell ill there. "When the moon was at zenith — *yī yuè zhèng zhōng tiān* — he set down his brush and died." Disciples **Shào-nán** and **Fǎ-yǔ** cremated him; relics were taken back to Dōng-shān for stupa-burial.

**Works.**
- *Fù biān* (re-compiler) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0560]] 《靈隱文禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B446 (1698).
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0561]] 《東山梅溪度禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B447, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Huì-yǐng Qìng-xù** [[慶緒]].
