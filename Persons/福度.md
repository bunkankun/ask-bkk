Fúdù 福度, *zì* **Méixī** 梅溪 ("Plum Stream"); often referred to as **Dōngshān Méixī** 東山梅溪 from his abbacy. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Língyǐn Yìnwén** [[印文]]. Born **明崇禎丁丑正月三十寅時** = **1637-02-24** Gregorian; died **康熙四十年正月** = **1701-02-08–1701-03-09** (lunar 1), age 63, *sēnglà* 47. Native of **XīShǔ Yǒngchuān** 西蜀永川 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Zhāng** 張. His mother née **Wú** 吳 carried him into Guìzhōu as a child.

**Formation.** At 16 took tonsure under **Dōngshān Zhīrú** 東山知如和尚. At 20 sought the ten precepts under **Kuòlàng dàshī** 闊浪大師. At 21 went to **Diānchéng** 滇城 (Yúnnán) to study under **Língyǐn** 靈隱禪師. **己亥春** = **1659** spring left his teacher; spent a year at **Dōngshān** 東山 in Guìzhōu. **庚子** = **1660** winter built a hut with his friend **Zhàomiànshān** 照面山. **辛丑冬** = **1661** winter Língyǐn arrived at **Dòubǎo Guānyīnsì** 竇堡觀音寺 in Guìzhōu and convened a *jiézhì* — Fúdù served as *wéinuó* and on the **Buddha's enlightenment day** (lunar 12/8) received *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacies.** **Ānshùn Qīngyuán** 安順清源, **Ānlóng Huáguāng** 安籠華光, **Guǎngxī Yùhuáng** 廣西玉皇, **Ménghuà Děngjué** 蒙化等覺, **Yúnzhōu Yùgé** 雲州玉閣, **Zōngzhōu Guānyīn** 宗州觀音, **Pǔān Yuántōng** 普安圓通, **Pénglái Yǒngxīng** 蓬萊永興 — i.e. wide circulation across **Yúnnán / Guìzhōu / Guǎngxī**.

**Disciples (ten or more).** **Huìyǐng Qìngxù** 慧穎慶緒 [[慶緒]] (compiler of his *yǔlù*), **Shílín Dàohuī** 石林道輝, **Xiázhāng Hǎiwěi** 霞章海偉, **Shàonán Zhēnjiě** 紹南真解, **Jìěr Xìngjiān** 繼爾性堅, **Fǎyǔ Zhàorùn** 法雨照潤.

**Death.** Returned to **Tiāntóng** to clean Mìzǔ's stupa; on the way back stopped at **Hànyáng** 漢陽; fell ill there. "When the moon was at zenith — *yī yuè zhèng zhōng tiān* — he set down his brush and died." Disciples **Shàonán** and **Fǎyǔ** cremated him; relics were taken back to Dōngshān for stupa-burial.

**Works.**
- *Fù biān* (re-compiler) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0560]] 《靈隱文禪師語錄》 3 juan, J39 B446 (1698).
- Own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0561]] 《東山梅溪度禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B447, compiled by *fǎsì* **Huìyǐng Qìngxù** [[慶緒]].
