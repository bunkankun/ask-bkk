Zhāng Xí 張習 (Míng, b. 1433, *fl.* Hóng-zhì *bǐng-chén* = 1496). Hóng-zhì-era editor and *zhòng-dìng* (re-arranger) of Zhèng Yuán-yòu [[鄭元祐]]'s *Qiáo-Wú jí* [[KR4d0545]]. Combined the original *Suì-chāng shān-rén jí* and *Qiáo-Wú jí* (which had much overlap) into a 12-*juǎn* selection, retaining only the "精純者" (essence-pure compositions). The Hóng-zhì *bǐng-chén* (1496) *bá* fixes the recension. No other biographical details located.
