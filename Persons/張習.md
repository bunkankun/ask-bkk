Zhāng Xí 張習 (Míng, b. 1433, *fl.* Hóngzhì *bǐngchén* = 1496). Hóng-zhì-era editor and *zhòngdìng* (re-arranger) of Zhèng Yuányòu [[鄭元祐]]'s *Qiáo Wú jí* [[KR4d0545]]. Combined the original *Suìchāng shānrén jí* and *Qiáo Wú jí* (which had much overlap) into a 12-*juǎn* selection, retaining only the "精純者" (essence-pure compositions). The Hóngzhì *bǐngchén* (1496) *bá* fixes the recension. No other biographical details located.
