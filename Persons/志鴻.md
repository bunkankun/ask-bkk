Zhì-hóng 志鴻 (lay name **Qián Yǎn** 錢儼) was a mid-to-late Tang Vinaya specialist of the **Nán-shān** 南山 lineage. Native of **Xià-ruò** 下若 (placeName id PL000000013087); resident at the Shuāng-lín-sì 雙林寺 in Wú prefecture (modern Sūzhōu region). He studied the *Nán-shān chāo* under **Dào-héng** 道恆 律師 of Bǎo-mào-yuàn 保茂苑. Posthumous title **Cháng-shòu dà-shī** 長壽大師 ("Great Master of Long Life") — a name reportedly given because he reached the age of one hundred and eight.

His chief Vinaya work is the **Sìfēn-lǜ sōu-xuán lù** 四分律搜玄錄 ([[KR6k0162]]) in twenty *juan* (only three *juan* survive), which gathers the essentials of more than forty *jì* and *chāo* commentaries by *Dà-cí* 大慈, *Líng-è* 靈崿, and others; the **fourth Huá-yán patriarch Chéng-guān** 澄觀 (738–839) wrote a preface for the work during the *Dà-lì* era (766–779), which fixes Zhì-hóng's mature career to the second half of the eighth century. Sources: *Fó-rén* 233; *Fó-guāng* 2889; DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000504.
