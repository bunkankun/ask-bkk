Zhìhóng 志鴻 (lay name **Qián Yǎn** 錢儼) was a mid-to-late Tang Vinaya specialist of the **Nánshān** 南山 lineage. Native of **Xiàruò** 下若 (placeName id PL000000013087); resident at the Shuānglínsì 雙林寺 in Wú prefecture (modern Sūzhōu region). He studied the *Nánshān chāo* under **Dàohéng** 道恆 律師 of Bǎomàoyuàn 保茂苑. Posthumous title **Chángshòu dàshī** 長壽大師 ("Great Master of Long Life") — a name reportedly given because he reached the age of one hundred and eight.

His chief Vinaya work is the **Sìfēnlǜ sōuxuán lù** 四分律搜玄錄 ([[KR6k0162]]) in twenty *juan* (only three *juan* survive), which gathers the essentials of more than forty *jì* and *chāo* commentaries by *Dàcí* 大慈, *Língè* 靈崿, and others; the **fourth Huáyán patriarch Chéngguān** 澄觀 (738–839) wrote a preface for the work during the *Dàlì* era (766–779), which fixes Zhìhóng's mature career to the second half of the eighth century. Sources: *Fórén* 233; *Fóguāng* 2889; DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000504.
