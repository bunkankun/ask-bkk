Jiāng Gāi 姜垓 (1614–1653), *zì* **Rú-xū** 如須, *hào* **Hòu-ān** 後菴 and **Fèng-ē shān-qiáo** 鳳阿山樵; native of **Lái-yáng** 萊陽 (Shān-dōng), of the famous Lái-yáng Jiāng clan. Younger brother of the *Chóng-zhēn* *jìnshì* and Southern-Míng *Bīng-bù shàng-shū* Jiāng Yuē 姜垓〔elder brother〕(actually his elder brother was **Jiāng Yuán 姜垍** the *jìnshì* and **Jiāng Cài 姜埰**, both martyrs). *Jìnshì* of *Chóng-zhēn* 13 / *gēng-chén* (1640). Disciple of [[徐汧]] of Cháng-zhōu; in collaboration with his teacher he edited the surviving writings of [[凌義渠]] as the *Líng Zhōng-jiè-gōng jí* ([[KR4e0241]]) after Líng's suicide in 1644. He remained loyal to the Míng, joined the southern resistance, and after 1645 lived in retirement in Hú-zhōu. CBDB 85253.
