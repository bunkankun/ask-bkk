Jiāng Gāi 姜垓 (1614–1653), *zì* **Rúxū** 如須, *hào* **Hòuān** 後菴 and **Fèngē shānqiáo** 鳳阿山樵; native of **Láiyáng** 萊陽 (Shāndōng), of the famous Láiyáng Jiāng clan. Younger brother of the *Chóngzhēn* *jìnshì* and Southern-Míng *Bīngbù shàngshū* Jiāng Yuē 姜垓〔elder brother〕(actually his elder brother was **Jiāng Yuán 姜垍** the *jìnshì* and **Jiāng Cài 姜埰**, both martyrs). *Jìnshì* of *Chóngzhēn* 13 / *gēngchén* (1640). Disciple of [[徐汧]] of Chángzhōu; in collaboration with his teacher he edited the surviving writings of [[凌義渠]] as the *Líng Zhōngjiègōng jí* ([[KR4e0241]]) after Líng's suicide in 1644. He remained loyal to the Míng, joined the southern resistance, and after 1645 lived in retirement in Húzhōu. CBDB 85253.
