Early-Míng grand minister under the Yǒnglè 永樂 and Hóngxī 洪熙 emperors; *zì* Kèshēng 克聲, of Líntóng 臨潼 in Shǎnxī. Lifedates 1365–1426 per CBDB and *Míngshǐ lièzhuàn*; the catalog meta gives "fl. 1386–1428" to accommodate the 1428 *Xuāndé dǐngyí pǔ* preface — but Lǚ would already have been dead two years at that date. *Hóngwǔ* 23 (1390) *jǔrén*; rose rapidly under the Yǒnglè emperor through the Beijing court reorganisation, serving in turn as Vice-Minister of Personnel, Vice-Minister of Punishments, Minister of Personnel and finally *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書 (Minister of Rites) under both the Yǒnglè and the *Hóngxī* / *Xuāndé* courts. Author (with collaborators) of [[KR3h0088]] *Xuāndé dǐngyí pǔ* 宣德鼎彝譜 (preface dated 1428), the principal document of the *Xuāndé* imperial bronze-recasting programme — though the actual compilation extends posthumously after Lǚ's 1426 death. Famous in the early-Míng court for his extraordinary administrative memory; *Míngshǐ lièzhuàn* records that he could recite all the *Lǐbù* archives from memory.
