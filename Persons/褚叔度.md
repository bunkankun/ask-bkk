# Chǔ Shūdù 褚叔度

Eastern Jìn official, Right Guard General (右衛將軍), who together with [[孟顗]] Mèng Yǐ (Inner Historian of Wú Commandery) served as principal lay donor (檀越, *dānapati*) for [[佛馱跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra's 418–420 translation of the 60-fascicle *[[KR6e0001|Dà fāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng 大方廣佛華嚴經]]* at the Dàochǎngsì 道場寺 in Jiànkāng. His patronage is recorded in the closing colophon of the work. Lifedates unrecorded.
