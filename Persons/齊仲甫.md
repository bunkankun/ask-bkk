**Qí Zhòngfǔ** 齊仲甫 (Southern Sòng, fl. early 13th c.), the name-form preferred in *Sòngshǐ yìwén zhì* and other early bibliographies for the author transmitted in later editions as [[宋仲甫]] Sòng Zhòngfǔ; author of the *Nǚkē bǎiwèn* 女科百問 ([[KR3ei031]]). The *Qí*/*Sòng* surname variant likely reflects scribal corruption in the medieval transmission.
