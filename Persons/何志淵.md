**Hé Zhìyuān** 何志淵 was a Southern-Sòng Daoist, compiler of [[KR5d0078]] *Chún yáng zhēn rén hùn chéng jí* 純陽真人渾成集 — a collection of poems attributed to [[呂洞賓]] Lǚ Dòngbīn (Chún yáng zǐ 純陽子), preface dated 1251.
