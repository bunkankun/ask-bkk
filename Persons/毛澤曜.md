**Máo Zéyào** 毛澤曜 (*zì* Nánfǔ 南甫), late-Qīng official from Wǎnjiāng 皖江 / Anhui. Author of the 1896 preface to [[徐延祚]] Xú Yánzuò's *Yī cuì jīng yán* 醫粹精言 ([[KR3eq060]]), recording his friendship with Xú during Máo's official assignment to Guǎngdōng in winter 1895 and his witness to the work's composition at Guangzhou.
