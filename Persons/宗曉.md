Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master and editor; *zì* **Dáxiān** 達先, sobriquet **Shízhī** 石芝 (also referenced as **Shízhī Zōngxiǎo** 石芝宗曉, **Sìmíng Shízhī** 四明石芝). DILA Authority A000487. Born 1151; productive into the early thirteenth century. Affiliated with the Sìmíng (Níngbō) Tiāntái scholastic establishment.

Major editorial work: the **Sìmíng zūnzhě jiàoxíng lù** 四明尊者教行錄 ([[KR6d0186]], T1937, 7 *juan*) — the most extensive collection of materials by and about **Sìmíng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]]), including correspondence, doctrinal essays, biographical materials, and stele inscriptions. The work is the principal documentary source for the modern reconstruction of Zhīlǐ's life and the Northern-Sòng *shānjiā* tradition's institutional history.

Other works include various Tiāntái-Pure Land synthesis productions characteristic of late-Sòng Tiāntái scholastic activity.

Note: distinct from the Japanese Tendai monk **Zhōngshān Zōngxiǎo** 鐘山宗曉 (a separate DILA entry).
