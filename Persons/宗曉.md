Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master and editor; *zì* **Dá-xiān** 達先, sobriquet **Shí-zhī** 石芝 (also referenced as **Shí-zhī Zōng-xiǎo** 石芝宗曉, **Sì-míng Shí-zhī** 四明石芝). DILA Authority A000487. Born 1151; productive into the early thirteenth century. Affiliated with the Sì-míng (Níngbō) Tiāntái scholastic establishment.

Major editorial work: the **Sì-míng zūn-zhě jiào-xíng lù** 四明尊者教行錄 ([[KR6d0186]], T1937, 7 *juan*) — the most extensive collection of materials by and about **Sì-míng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]]), including correspondence, doctrinal essays, biographical materials, and stele inscriptions. The work is the principal documentary source for the modern reconstruction of Zhīlǐ's life and the Northern-Sòng *shān-jiā* tradition's institutional history.

Other works include various Tiāntái-Pure Land synthesis productions characteristic of late-Sòng Tiāntái scholastic activity.

Note: distinct from the Japanese Tendai monk **Zhōng-shān Zōng-xiǎo** 鐘山宗曉 (a separate DILA entry).
