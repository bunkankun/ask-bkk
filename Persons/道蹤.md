# Mùzhōu Dàozōng 睦州道蹤

Late-Táng Chán master of Mùzhōu; teacher under whom [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃 (864–949) had his initial awakening (the *Qín shí luò lì zuān* 秦時𨍏轢鑽 precedent), as recorded in the *xínglù* preserved in [[KR6q0073]].
