Mò Xiūfú 莫休符 (*fl.* 899). Late-Táng official whose only documented post is *Jiǎnjiào sànqí chángshì* and Prefect (*cìshǐ*) of Róngzhōu 融州 (modern Róngshuǐ Miáo Autonomous County in Guǎngxī). Composed the *Guìlín fēngtǔ jì* 桂林風土記 ([[KR2k0107]]) in retirement after his term, dating his preface to the twenty-third day of the ninth month of *Guānghuà* 2 (899). Otherwise undocumented; not recorded in *Xīn Tángshū* or *Jiù Tángshū*. The lone surviving work is the principal late-Táng documentary monograph on Guìlín and the surrounding Lǐngnán *xīdào* region.
