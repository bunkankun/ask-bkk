Mò Xiū-fú 莫休符 (*fl.* 899). Late-Táng official whose only documented post is *Jiǎn-jiào sàn-qí cháng-shì* and Prefect (*cì-shǐ*) of Róng-zhōu 融州 (modern Róng-shuǐ Miáo Autonomous County in Guǎng-xī). Composed the *Guìlín fēng-tǔ jì* 桂林風土記 ([[KR2k0107]]) in retirement after his term, dating his preface to the twenty-third day of the ninth month of *Guāng-huà* 2 (899). Otherwise undocumented; not recorded in *Xīn Táng-shū* or *Jiù Táng-shū*. The lone surviving work is the principal late-Táng documentary monograph on Guì-lín and the surrounding Lǐng-nán *xī-dào* region.
