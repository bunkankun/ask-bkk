**Zhào Zhēnguān** 趙貞觀 (*fl.* late Ming – early Qīng; precise dates and native place unrecorded). Compiler of the *Jiàngxuě dānshū* 絳雪丹書 ([[KR3ed032]]), a specialised gynaecological / postpartum formulary in 1 *juǎn*. His work transmits a substantial portion of the *Zhúlínsì* (竹林寺) Buddhist monastic gynaecological tradition centred at Hángzhōu, with the *Five Acute Nineteen Critical* (五急十九危) postpartum-emergency classification. Not in CBDB; no other biographical record.
