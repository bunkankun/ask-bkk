蹉跎子 (*Cuōtuó Zǐ*, "The Stumbling One"), also known as Xīnyáng Cuōtuó Zǐ 新陽蹉跎子, is the pen name used by the author of [[KR4k0293]] *Zuìxīn Nǚjiè Guǐyù Jì* 最新女界鬼域記 (1909). The fuller form of the pen name identifies the author as from Xīnyáng 新陽, a district in Hunan province. A preface associated with the novel records that the author's family name was Wú 吳 (or 五 in some transcriptions) and that he came from a distinguished literary family of Jiāngsū. Beyond these traces, the author's identity has not been established. No CBDB entry is known.
