Liú Yǔn-péng 劉允鵬 (originally Liú Jì-xiān 劉繼先; *zì* Jìng-xū 敬虛; fl. mid-16th c.) was a native of Wǔ-dìng 武定 (modern Shāndōng) and a *jǔ-rén* of Jiā-jìng *xīn-mǎo* 嘉靖辛卯 = 1531. His name change is preserved only in the *Sìkù tíyào* for the *Lóng-jīn fèng-suǐ pàn*. His attested works are a *Xù Shì-lèi fù* 續事類賦 (a continuation of Wú Shū's [[KR3k0011]]; not transmitted) and the *zhù* 注 commentary on Zhāng Zhuó's *Lóng-jīn fèng-suǐ pàn* ([[KR3k0005]]), the only annotated tradition of that text. The *Sìkù* editors retained Liú's notes only by default, judging them detailed but verbose.
