Cháo Yuèzhī 晁說之 (1059–1129), *zì* Yǐdào 以道, *hào* Jǐngyū 景迂 (and *Jǐngyū-shēng* 景迂生 — taken in admiration of Sīmǎ Guāng's late *hào* *Yū sǒu* 迂叟; "Jǐng" = admire, "Yū" = Sīmǎ's pedant-style), was a Northern–Southern Sòng transition classicist of Jù-yě 鉅野 (Shān-dōng). *Jìnshì* of Yuán-fèng 5 (1082); was recommended by Sū Shì for the *Zhù-shù-kē* 著述科 in the Yuán-yòu period, and exiled to *xié-děng* 邪等 in Yuán-fú by *Xīn-fǎ* / Cài Jīng faction action. Recalled at the start of Jìng-kāng (1126) as *Zhù-zuò láng*, then *Zhōng-shū shè-rén* with concurrent *Tài-zǐ zhān-shì*; promoted at the start of Jiànyán to *Huī-yóu-gé dài-zhì*. Demoted by Gāozōng for his *Fēi Mèngzǐ* 非孟子 (against the canonisation of *Mèngzǐ*) and forced to retire. His *Jǐngyū-shēng jí* 景迂生集 (collected works) preserves substantial corpus; the present *Rú-yán* 儒言 (KR3a0036) circulated separately. He is also the textual critic responsible for diagnosing the received *Zǐxià Yìzhuàn* (KR1a0001) as a forgery by Zhāng Hú 張弧 (Tang). Member of the Cháo lineage that produced Cháo Bǔzhī 晁補之 (his cousin). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444 (Wén-yuán zhuàn). CBDB id 3029, dates 1059–1129.
