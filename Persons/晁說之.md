Cháo Yuèzhī 晁說之 (1059–1129), *zì* Yǐdào 以道, *hào* Jǐngyū 景迂 (and *Jǐngyūshēng* 景迂生 — taken in admiration of Sīmǎ Guāng's late *hào* *Yū sǒu* 迂叟; "Jǐng" = admire, "Yū" = Sīmǎ's pedant-style), was a Northern–Southern Sòng transition classicist of Jùyě 鉅野 (Shāndōng). *Jìnshì* of Yuánfèng 5 (1082); was recommended by Sū Shì for the *Zhùshùkē* 著述科 in the Yuányòu period, and exiled to *xiéděng* 邪等 in Yuánfú by *Xīnfǎ* / Cài Jīng faction action. Recalled at the start of Jìngkāng (1126) as *Zhùzuò láng*, then *Zhōngshū shèrén* with concurrent *Tàizǐ zhānshì*; promoted at the start of Jiànyán to *Huīyóugé dàizhì*. Demoted by Gāozōng for his *Fēi Mèngzǐ* 非孟子 (against the canonisation of *Mèngzǐ*) and forced to retire. His *Jǐngyūshēng jí* 景迂生集 (collected works) preserves substantial corpus; the present *Rúyán* 儒言 ([[KR3a0036]]) circulated separately. He is also the textual critic responsible for diagnosing the received *Zǐxià Yìzhuàn* ([[KR1a0001]]) as a forgery by Zhāng Hú 張弧 (Tang). Member of the Cháo lineage that produced Cháo Bǔzhī 晁補之 (his cousin). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444 (Wényuán zhuàn). CBDB id 3029, dates 1059–1129.
