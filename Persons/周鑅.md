**Zhōu Yīng** 周鑅, Qīng-era colleague of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng at the *Cháng-shān* 長山 medical circuit in the Dào-guāng era, *fl.* 1843. The signature of his postface to **[[KR3eq009]]** *Huí-chūn lù* 回春錄 (signed *yú-dì* 愚弟 "your foolish younger brother", Dào-guāng 23 = 1843) identifies him as a coeval-or-junior friend and clinical collaborator of Wáng during the latter's early Hángzhōu years. Zhōu served as the **editor-anthologist** of Wáng's first case-record compilation, taking Dīng Cháng-rú 丁長孺's edition of Miù Xī-yōng's [[繆希雍]] cases as his explicit model.
