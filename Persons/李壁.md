Lǐ Bì 李壁 (1159–1222), *zì* Jìzhāng 季章, *hào* Yànhú jūshì 雁湖居士, posthumous *Wényì* 文懿. Of *Méishān* (Sìchuān) — third son of the great Southern-Sòng historian Lǐ Tāo 李燾 [[李燾]] (compiler of *Xù Zīzhì tōngjiàn chángbiān* [[KR2b0028]]); his elder brothers were Lǐ Hòu 李垕 and Lǐ Shú 李塾 (the *tǔ*-radical naming pattern fixes the character — *Sòngshǐ* sometimes prints *Bì* as 璧 with the *yù* radical, but the brothers' naming pattern is conclusive). First entered office through paternal *yīn*; later took the *jìnshì*. Under *Níngzōng* rose through *Lǐbù shàngshū*, *Cānzhīzhèngshì*, and *Tóngzhī shūmìyuànshì*. Under Hán Tuōzhòu 韓侂胄's 1206 northern-recovery campaign Lǐ acted as the *fùshǐ* envoy to the front (after Zhāng Sìgǔ 張嗣古's first envoying ran counter to Hán's purpose); his concord with Hán led to his promotion. Yè Shàowēng 葉紹翁's *Sìcháo wénjiàn lù* records this — the *Sìkù* editors note that Lǐ's collaboration with Hán's failed northern campaign cost the family reputation. Demoted to *Línchuān* upon Hán's 1207 fall and the disastrous campaign's reckoning; in *Línchuān* exile composed the *Wáng Jīnggōng shīzhù* 王荊公詩注 [[KR4d0074]] in 50 *juǎn* — assisted by Zēng Jí 曾極 (*zì* Jǐngjiàn 景建); preface by Wèi Liǎowēng 魏了翁 ([[魏了翁]]) (the *Hèshān* / *Hèxiān*). *Sòngshǐ* 398.
