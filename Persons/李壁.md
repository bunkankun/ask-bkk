Lǐ Bì 李壁 (1159–1222), *zì* Jì-zhāng 季章, *hào* Yàn-hú jū-shì 雁湖居士, posthumous *Wén-yì* 文懿. Of *Méi-shān* (Sì-chuān) — third son of the great Southern-Sòng historian Lǐ Tāo 李燾 [[李燾]] (compiler of *Xù Zī-zhì tōng-jiàn cháng-biān* [[KR2b0028]]); his elder brothers were Lǐ Hòu 李垕 and Lǐ Shú 李塾 (the *tǔ*-radical naming pattern fixes the character — *Sòng-shǐ* sometimes prints *Bì* as 璧 with the *yù* radical, but the brothers' naming pattern is conclusive). First entered office through paternal *yīn*; later took the *jìn-shì*. Under *Níng-zōng* rose through *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*, *Cān-zhī-zhèng-shì*, and *Tóng-zhī shū-mì-yuàn-shì*. Under Hán Tuō-zhòu 韓侂胄's 1206 northern-recovery campaign Lǐ acted as the *fù-shǐ* envoy to the front (after Zhāng Sì-gǔ 張嗣古's first envoying ran counter to Hán's purpose); his concord with Hán led to his promotion. Yè Shào-wēng 葉紹翁's *Sì-cháo wén-jiàn lù* records this — the *Sìkù* editors note that Lǐ's collaboration with Hán's failed northern campaign cost the family reputation. Demoted to *Lín-chuān* upon Hán's 1207 fall and the disastrous campaign's reckoning; in *Lín-chuān* exile composed the *Wáng Jīng-gōng shī-zhù* 王荊公詩注 [[KR4d0074]] in 50 *juǎn* — assisted by Zēng Jí 曾極 (*zì* Jǐng-jiàn 景建); preface by Wèi Liǎo-wēng 魏了翁 ([[魏了翁]]) (the *Hè-shān* / *Hè-xiān*). *Sòng-shǐ* 398.
