Later-Hàn scholar of *Shānyīn* 山陰 (in Kuàijī commandery, modern Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng); *zì* Cháng-jūn 長君. The standard form of his name is 趙曄, but the Kanripo catalog meta and several Sòng-Yuán bibliographic references write the variant 燁 (the two graphs share the reading *yè* and are interchangeable; cf. CBDB id 166022 for 趙曄, distinct from id 26139 for a homonymous Sòng official). His brief biography appears in *Hòu-Hàn shū* 後漢書 79B (*Rúlín-zhuàn*): in his youth he resented being a clerk for the Kuàijī commandery, abandoned his post, and travelled to Zī-chuān 資中 (Sìchuān) where he studied the *Hán-shī* 韓詩 with Dù Fǔ 杜撫 for over twenty years, returning home only to find his family had buried him in absentia. Apart from the *Wú Yuè Chūnqiū* [[KR2i0001]], the *Hòu-Hàn shū* attributes to him a *Shī xì-lì shén-yuān* 詩細歷神淵 (lost) which Cài Yōng 蔡邕 reportedly judged superior to Wáng Chōng's 王充 *Lùn-héng* 論衡. He also figures in early traditions of the *Hán-shī* school of *Shī* exegesis. CBDB has no precise lifedates; the catalog meta of *fl.* 40–80 reflects the standard placement of his teaching career under Emperors Guāng-wǔ 光武 (r. 25–57) and Míng 明 (r. 57–75).
