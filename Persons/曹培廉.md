Early Qīng-dynasty Shànghǎi 上海 editor and bibliophile. In Kāng-xī *guǐsì* 癸巳 (1713) he prepared a re-edited and re-collated version of **Ní Zàn** 倪瓚's collected works as the *Qīngmì-gé quánjí* 清閟閣全集 in 12 *juǎn* — augmenting from earlier recensions: the 1460 Jiǎn Xī Tiānshùn print, the *Wànlì* re-cut by Ní's eighth-generation descendant Ní Chéng 倪珵 (in 15 *juǎn*, by then much damaged), and **Máo Jìn** 毛晉's *Jí-gǔ-gé* *shí Yuán-rén jí* recension. He further drew on **Zhū Cún-lǐ** 朱存理's *Lóu-jū zázhù* — which had collected scattered pieces, particularly from Ní's Wú travels — adding an *Wài jí* (Outer Collection) of one *juǎn*. The *Sìkù* WYG *Qīngmì-gé jí* ([[KR4d0576]]) is the Cáo Péi-lián recension, the most comprehensive Ní-Zàn collected-works edition through the Qīng period. The catalog meta records his function as 編 (compiler) for this work.
