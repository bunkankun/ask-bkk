**Zhèng Xiāoyán** 鄭肖岩 (1856–1934), *zì* Yánfǔ 巖甫, *hào* Yǐnjǐng Shānrén 飲井山人 ("the Recluse who Drinks from a Well"). Late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of southeastern Fújiàn (Fúníng prefecture, "海嶠"). Spent seventeen years compiling the original *Wěi yào tiáo biàn* 偽藥條辨 (1901) — a pharmacognostic monograph identifying about 110 drugs commonly counterfeited in the late-Qīng market and giving organoleptic criteria for distinguishing genuine from fake. The work was later expanded by Cáo Bǐngzhāng [[曹炳章]] as the *Zēngdìng wěi yào tiáo biàn* ([[KR3ec071]], 1928). Zhèng's postface to the 1928 expanded edition was written in 1917 when he was 69.
