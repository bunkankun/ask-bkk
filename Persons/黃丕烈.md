# Huáng Pīliè 黃丕烈

Qīng bibliophile (1763–1825, *hào* Fùwēng 復翁, also 蕘圃 Yáopǔ) of Sūzhōu 蘇州. The pre-eminent collector and connoisseur of Sòng and Yuán imprints of his generation; owner of the **Shìlǐjū** 士禮居 library. His *bátí* 跋題 colophons on rare Sòng-Yuán imprints, prized for their bibliographic precision, are collected in the *Bǎisòng yīchán fù* 百宋一廛賦 and *Shìlǐjū cángshū tíbá* 士禮居藏書題跋. Wrote the 1811 colophon to a defective Yuán *Zhìyuán jiǎwǔ* (1294) copy of *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]]).
