# Wēngǔ 溫古

Tang-dynasty Buddhist monk, disciple of Śubhakarasiṃha [[善無畏]] and co-disciple with [[一行]] Yīxíng. He prepared the posthumous fourteen-fascicle revised recension of Yīxíng's *Dàrì jīng yìshì* ([[KR6j0734]]) and authored its preface.
