# 張子坪

Zhāng Zǐpíng 張子坪 (late Qīng; surname also written 張子萍 in some sources), physician of Huáiān 淮安, northern Jiāngsū. Reckoned one of the "Three Great Physicians of Northern Jiāngsū in the late Qīng" alongside [[余奉仙]] Yú Fèngxiān of Fùníng and [[趙海仙]] Zhào Hǎixiān of Xīnghuà, as recorded in the biographical preface to [[KR3ep105]] *Yú Wúyán yīàn*.
