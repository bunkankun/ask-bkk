Yú Wénxī 于文煕 (also written 于文熙; b. 1546; CBDB id 206490), a mid- to late-Míng official, held the post of *Dàmíngfǔ bīngbèi fùshǐ* 大名兵備副使 (Military-Defence Assistant of Dàmíng prefecture). He supplemented and patched [[崔銑]]'s (Cuī Xiǎn, 1478–1541) *Xíng lù* 行錄 — the 1-*juàn* biographical compilation on [[劉安世]] (Liú Ānshì, 1048–1125) — which was then appended to the recension of the *Yuánchéng yǔ lù jiě* ([[KR3j0102]]) preserved in the *Sìkù quán shū*. Other biographical details have not surfaced.
