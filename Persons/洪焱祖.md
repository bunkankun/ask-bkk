Yuán-period local official and philologist, *zì* Qiánfū 潛夫, of Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Huīzhōu, modern Anhui — the same place as Luó Yuàn [[羅願]]). Held the magistracy of Xiūníng 休寧. He provided the standard *yīnshì* 音釋 phonetic glosses to Luó Yuàn's *Ěryǎ yì* [[KR1j0012]] — preserved as the inner commentary of the WYG and most subsequent printed editions — together with a postface itemizing the work's structure (six topical divisions across thirty-two *juàn*). His own collected works are *Xìngtíng zhāigǎo* 杏庭摘稿. CBDB gives lifedates 1267–1329 (*fl.* 1292), consistent with his Xiūníng magistracy and the dates of his postface.
