Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Luóxī** 螺溪 (with full name **Luóxī Yuánwù** 螺溪元悟). DILA Authority A001054. Lifedates unrecorded. Resident at the Tiāntáishān **Chuánjiàoyuàn** 傳教院 (the same institution at which Goryeo's Dìguān had earlier transmitted the Tiāntái texts back to China; see [[諦觀]]).

Compiler of the **Luóxī zhènzǔ jí** 螺溪振祖集 ([[KR6d0223]], X56n0946, 1 *juan*) — a Southern-Sòng compilation documenting the Luóxī Chuánjiàoyuàn's Tiāntái patriarchal lineage.
