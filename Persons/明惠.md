Míng-huì 明惠, *zì* **Kǒng-yì** 孔意. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** [[超自]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler (*děng biān*) with **Míng-xiū** [[明修]] and **Míng-zhì** [[明智]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0575]] 《何一自禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B462.
