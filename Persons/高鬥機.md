Gāo Dòujī 高鬥機, mid-19th-century. Eldest son of [[高鼎汾]] Gāo Dǐngfén (*hào* Shàngchí 上池) of **Wúxī** 無錫, grandson of [[高秉鈞]] Gāo Bǐngjūn (Jǐntíng 錦亭). The named addressee and second-person interlocutor of [[KR3eq090]] *Yīxué kèér cè* 醫學課兒策 (1843), Gāo Dǐngfén's pedagogical *cèwèn* (examination-question) text. The work was written to instruct him in clinical internal medicine in the family tradition. No standard biographical record located.
