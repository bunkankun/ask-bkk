Gāo Dòu-jī 高鬥機, mid-19th-century. Eldest son of [[高鼎汾]] Gāo Dǐng-fén (*hào* Shàng-chí 上池) of **Wú-xī** 無錫, grandson of [[高秉鈞]] Gāo Bǐng-jūn (Jǐn-tíng 錦亭). The named addressee and second-person interlocutor of [[KR3eq090]] *Yī-xué kè-ér cè* 醫學課兒策 (1843), Gāo Dǐng-fén's pedagogical *cè-wèn* (examination-question) text. The work was written to instruct him in clinical internal medicine in the family tradition. No standard biographical record located.
