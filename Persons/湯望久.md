**Tāng Wàngjiǔ** 湯望久 (Qīng physician, fl. mid-eighteenth century), redactor of the *Màiyīnzhèngzhì* 脈因證治 ([[KR3er010]]) attributed to Zhū Dānxī 朱震亨, in the recension brought to print by Miù Zūnyì 繆遵義 of Sūzhōu in 1775. No CBDB record. Tāng's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered from the source; his editorial work on the Dānxī material places him in the mid-Qīng Sūzhōu medical milieu that produced a number of similar editorial reconstructions of YuánMíng clinical doctrine.
