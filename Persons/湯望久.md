**Tāng Wàng-jiǔ** 湯望久 (Qīng physician, fl. mid-eighteenth century), redactor of the *Mài-yīn-zhèng-zhì* 脈因證治 ([[KR3er010]]) attributed to Zhū Dān-xī 朱震亨, in the recension brought to print by Miù Zūn-yì 繆遵義 of Sūzhōu in 1775. No CBDB record. Tāng's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered from the source; his editorial work on the Dān-xī material places him in the mid-Qīng Sūzhōu medical milieu that produced a number of similar editorial reconstructions of Yuán-Míng clinical doctrine.
