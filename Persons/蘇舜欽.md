*Qìnglì* generation *gǔwén* poet, *zì* Zǐměi 子美, of *Tóngshān* 銅山 (Zǐzhōu, Sìchuān) origin; later resided at *Kāifēng* and finally Sūzhōu. Grandson of Sū Tài 蘇耆, great-grandson of Sū Yìjiǎn 蘇易簡 (the *jǐngdé*-era councillor). *Jìnshì* of *Jǐngyòu* 1 / 1034. Married to Dù Yǎn's 杜衍 daughter, placing him at the center of the *Qìnglì* reform faction. Held *Jíxián xiàolǐ*; dismissed in *Qìnglì* 4 / 1044 in the *Zòuyuàn* scandal — a partisan attack from the anti-reform faction targeting his connection with Dù Yǎn. Retired to Sūzhōu, built the *Cānglàng tíng* 滄浪亭 on ruined Wú-Yuè garden ground, lived there as a private literatus. Died *Qìnglì* 8 / 1048 age 41. Bracketed with Méi Yáochén [[梅堯臣]] as "*Sū-Méi*" 蘇梅 in Ōuyáng Xiū's classic formulation — the principal pre-Ōuyáng innovators of *gǔwén* poetry. Author of the canonical *Cānglàng tíng jì* 滄浪亭記 and the *Mù Cānjūn āi cí* (lament for Mù Xiū). Surviving collection: *Sū xuéshì jí* [[KR4d0034]] (16 *juǎn*, 1698 Sòng Luò reprint). CBDB id 7064.
