*Qìng-lì* generation *gǔ-wén* poet, *zì* Zǐ-měi 子美, of *Tóng-shān* 銅山 (Zǐ-zhōu, Sìchuān) origin; later resided at *Kāi-fēng* and finally Sū-zhōu. Grandson of Sū Tài 蘇耆, great-grandson of Sū Yì-jiǎn 蘇易簡 (the *jǐng-dé*-era councillor). *Jìn-shì* of *Jǐng-yòu* 1 / 1034. Married to Dù Yǎn's 杜衍 daughter, placing him at the center of the *Qìng-lì* reform faction. Held *Jí-xián xiào-lǐ*; dismissed in *Qìng-lì* 4 / 1044 in the *Zòu-yuàn* scandal — a partisan attack from the anti-reform faction targeting his connection with Dù Yǎn. Retired to Sū-zhōu, built the *Cāng-làng tíng* 滄浪亭 on ruined Wú-Yuè garden ground, lived there as a private literatus. Died *Qìng-lì* 8 / 1048 age 41. Bracketed with Méi Yáo-chén [[梅堯臣]] as "*Sū-Méi*" 蘇梅 in Ōuyáng Xiū's classic formulation — the principal pre-Ōuyáng innovators of *gǔ-wén* poetry. Author of the canonical *Cāng-làng tíng jì* 滄浪亭記 and the *Mù Cān-jūn āi cí* (lament for Mù Xiū). Surviving collection: *Sū xué-shì jí* [[KR4d0034]] (16 *juǎn*, 1698 Sòng Luò reprint). CBDB id 7064.
