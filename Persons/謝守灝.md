Southern-Sòng 南宋 Daoist scholar-hagiographer, **1134–1212** (CBDB 52414). Author of a coordinated trilogy of works on the **Lǎojūn / Tài shàng Lǎo jūn** 太上老君 hagiographic tradition — one of the most systematic late-Southern-Sòng treatments of the Daoist divinised-Lǎozǐ tradition.

**Works** (the Lǎojūn trilogy):

1. ***[[KR5c0166|Hùn yuán shèng jì]]*** 混元聖紀 (DZ 770, 1193, 9 *juàn*) — the comprehensive chronicle of Lǎojūn's cosmic origin and successive manifestations.
2. ***[[KR5c0167|Tài shàng Lǎo jūn nián pǔ yào lüè]]*** 太上老君年譜要略 (DZ 771) — the summary chronological table.
3. ***[[KR5c0169|Tài shàng hùn yuán Lǎo zǐ shǐ lüè]]*** 太上混元老子史略 (DZ 773) — the summary history.

The three works together constitute a **comprehensive hagiographic apparatus** for the Daoist Lǎojūn tradition.

**Dating.** 1134–1212. Active principally in the late-Southern-Sòng period, contemporary with Zhū Xī (1130–1200) and the broader Southern-Sòng intellectual landscape.
