Jíjīn Yíyīng 吉津宜英 / Yoshizu Yoshihide (1943–2014) — modern Japanese Buddhologist; specialist on Huáyán 華嚴 and Chan thought; author of *Kegonzen no shisō-shiteki kenkyū* (1985).
