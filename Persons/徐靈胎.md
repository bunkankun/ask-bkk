# Xú Língtāi 徐靈胎

Qīng-period physician and medical critic (1693–1771), original name Xú Dàchūn 徐大椿, *zì* Língtāi 靈胎, *hào* Huíxī lǎorén 洄溪老人, of Wújiāng 吳江 (Jiāngsū). One of the most influential mid-Qīng medical thinkers; author of the *Yīxué yuánliú lùn* 醫學源流論 (1757), *Nànjīng jīngshì* 難經經釋, *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng bǎizhǒng lù* 神農本草經百種錄, and the *Shānghán lèifāng* 傷寒類方. His polemical writings advocate strict return to the classical canon and severely criticise contemporary practice, including the *wēnbǔ* school and the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn*.
