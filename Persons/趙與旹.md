Zhào Yǔshí 趙與旹 (1175–1231), *zì* Xíng-zhī 行之 (the *zì* Dé-xíng 德行 given in Chén Zōnglǐ's 陳宗禮 1257 preface may reflect a second *zì* or a transmission slip), was a Sòng imperial clansman, a descendant of Tàizǔ in the seventh generation, of *dài-cì* 待次 residence at Guī-ān 歸安 in Húzhōu 湖州. Never passed the *jìnshì*; served as granary-superintendent in Wù, Tài, and Qú prefectures, then as supervisor of the *xíng-zài cǎo-liào chǎng* 行在草料場 and the imperial arms office; appointed *chéng* 丞 (magistrate) of Lìshuǐ 麗水 just before his death; the *tōng-zhí láng* 通直郎 commission arrived posthumously. Studied under Yáng Jiǎn 楊簡 (*Cí-hú xiānshēng* 慈湖先生). Author of the *Bīn-tuì lù* 賓退錄 ([[KR3j0051]]) and a verse collection *Jiǎwǔ cún-gǎo* 甲午存藁 (not transmitted). The catalog meta gives 1172–1228; the *Sìkù tíyào* (citing Zhào Mèngjiān's 趙孟堅 tomb-inscription) and CBDB id 15109 both give 1175–1231, followed here.
