Late Yuán painting connoisseur; *zì* Shìliáng 士良, with clan-seat from Wúxīng 吳興 but resident in Sōngjiāng 松江 (modern Shànghǎi region). Active in the *Zhì-zhèng* 至正 era (1341–1368); friend and protégé of Yáng Wéizhēn 楊維楨, the dominant Wú literatus of the period. The family held a substantial painting collection. Author of [[KR3h0041]] *Túhuì bǎojiàn* 圖繪寶鑑 (1365), the standard biographical dictionary of Chinese painters from antiquity through the Yuán — 1,500+ entries with critical evaluations, the most comprehensive painting reference work produced before the Qīng *Pèiwén-zhāi shū-huà pǔ* [[KR3h0061]].
