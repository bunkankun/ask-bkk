**Lùn Zhìhuàn** 論志煥 was a JīnYuán Quán zhēn Daoist disciple, compiler of [[KR5d0082]] *Pán shān Qī yún Wáng zhēn rén yǔ lù* 盤山棲雲王真人語錄 — the recorded sayings of **Wáng Zhìjǐn** 王志謹 (1178–1263), a second-generation Quán zhēn patriarch who resided at Pán shān 盤山 and took the *hào* Qī yún 棲雲 ("Roost-in-Cloud"). Preface dated 1247.
