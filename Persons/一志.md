# Yīzhì 一志

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[元皓]]) of *juan* 1 (Kāifú abbacy) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
