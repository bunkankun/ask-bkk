# 明則

Míngzé 明則 (*fl.* 615/616 CE), Suí-dynasty monk member of the six-man translation team at the Shànglín-yuán fānjīng-guǎn 上林園翻經館 in Luòyáng 洛陽, working with [[慧矩]] Huìjǔ and [[達摩笈多]] Dámó Jíduō (Dharmagupta) on the retranslation of the *Yàoshī rúlái běnyuàn jīng* 藥師如來本願經 ([[KR6i0047]], T449) completed in Dà-yè 大業 12 (616 CE).
