Liú Yī-rén 劉一仁, otherwise undocumented physician of the Míng-Qīng transition (mid-17th c.). Conventionally named as author of [[KR3eq079]] *Yī-xué chuán-xīn lù* 醫學傳心錄, transmitted as a *chuán-xīn* (heart-transmitted) lineage didactic-mnemonic compendium by [[錢樂天]] Qián Lè-tiān. Not in CBDB. The attribution is reported in late-Imperial bibliographies but cannot be independently verified.
