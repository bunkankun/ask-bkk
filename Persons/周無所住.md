**Zhōu Wúsuǒzhù** 周無所住 (literally "Zhōu of No-Abiding-Place") was a Southern-Sòng Daoist author, of [[KR5d0095]] *Jīn dān zhí zhǐ* 金丹直指 — a direct-instruction treatise on the gold elixir, preface dated 1250. The pen-name ("No Abiding Place") echoes the Buddhist *Vajracchedikā* phrase *yīng wú suǒ zhù ér shēng qí xīn* 應無所住而生其心.
