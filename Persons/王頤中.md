**Wáng Yízhōng** 王頤中 was a Jīn-Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist disciple, compiler of [[KR5d0080]] *Dān yáng zhēn rén yǔ lù* 丹陽真人語錄 — the recorded sayings of **Mǎ Dān yáng** 馬丹陽 (= Mǎ Yù 馬鈺, 1123–1184), one of the **Seven Perfected** (*qī zhēn* 七真) of Quán zhēn and chief disciple of Wáng Chóngyáng.
