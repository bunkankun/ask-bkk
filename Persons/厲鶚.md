# Lì È 厲鶚

*Zì* Tàihóng 太鴻; *hào* Fánxiè 樊榭. Native of Qiántáng 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jǔrén* of *Kāngxī gēngzǐ* (1720). CBDB id 33481; lifedates 1692–1752.

One of the leading mid-Qiánlóng kǎozhèng scholars and a central member of the Hángzhōu *Wǔlín* literary circle. Repeatedly attempted but did not pass the metropolitan examination (*jìnshì*); spent his career as a private scholar in Hángzhōu, supported by the patronage of the great Yángzhōu salt merchants. A vigorous bibliophile and scholar of Sòng poetry.

Principal works: (1) the *Sòng shī jì shì* 宋詩紀事 (100 *juǎn*, 1746) — the canonical biographical anthology of Sòng poetry, the principal source for late-imperial knowledge of Sòng-era poets and their works; (2) the *Liáo shǐ shíyí* 遼史拾遺 ([[KR2a0034]], 24 *juǎn*) — the principal Qing-era supplement to the *Liáo shǐ*; (3) the *Dōngchéng zá jì* 東城雜記 — a Hángzhōu local-history miscellany; (4) the *Fánxiè shānfáng jí* 樊榭山房集 — his collected poetry and prose. He was also one of the founders of the *Zhèpài* 浙派 of Qing *cí* poetry.

His biography is in *Qīng shǐ gǎo* 485 ([[KR2a0039]]) under the *Wényuàn* category.
