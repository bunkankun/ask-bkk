# 許敬愚

Xǔ Jìngyú

Mid-Míng Sūzhōu layman, disciple of the monk Dàníng 大寧. Inherited annotated *Xiāoshì Jīngāng kēyí* materials from his late teacher and proposed printing them, the project that became [[KR6c0055]] in the 1551 recension.
