# Qióng-lín 瓊林

Southern-Sòng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[普度]] Xū-zhōu Pǔ-dù (1199–1280); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0340]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
