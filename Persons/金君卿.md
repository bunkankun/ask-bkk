Mid-Northern-Sòng official, *zì* Zhèng-shū 正叔, of *Fú-liáng* 浮梁 (Ráo-zhōu, Jiāng-xī). *Jìn-shì* of *Qìng-lì* (early 1040s); *Mì-shū chéng* in *Huáng-yòu* 2 / 1050; *Tài-cháng bó-shì* in *Huáng-yòu* 5 / 1053; held *zhī Lín-chuān*, acted as *Jiāng-xī tí-xíng*, then *Dù-zhī láng-zhōng* (per Hóng Mài's *Yí-jiān zhì*). One of four *jǔ jìn-shì* sons of Wèi-wèi sì-chéng Jīn Wēn-sǒu 金温叟 (whose *mù-zhì-míng* by Zēng Gǒng [[曾鞏]] is preserved in *Yuán-fēng lèi-gǎo*). Skilled in *Yì*. Surviving collection: *Jīn-shì wén jí* [[KR4d0043]] (2 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* against the original 15-*juǎn* recension by Jiāng Míng-zhòng with Fù Lín preface).
