Mid-Northern-Sòng official, *zì* Zhèngshū 正叔, of *Fúliáng* 浮梁 (Ráozhōu, Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Qìnglì* (early 1040s); *Mìshū chéng* in *Huángyòu* 2 / 1050; *Tàicháng bóshì* in *Huángyòu* 5 / 1053; held *zhī Línchuān*, acted as *Jiāngxī tíxíng*, then *Dùzhī lángzhōng* (per Hóng Mài's *Yíjiān zhì*). One of four *jǔ jìnshì* sons of Wèiwèi sìchéng Jīn Wēnsǒu 金温叟 (whose *mùzhìmíng* by Zēng Gǒng [[曾鞏]] is preserved in *Yuánfēng lèigǎo*). Skilled in *Yì*. Surviving collection: *Jīnshì wén jí* [[KR4d0043]] (2 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* against the original 15-*juǎn* recension by Jiāng Míngzhòng with Fù Lín preface).
