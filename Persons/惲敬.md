Yùn Jìng 惲敬 (1757–1817), *zì* Zǐ-jū 子居, *hào* Jiǎn-tāng 簡堂, native of Yáng-hú 陽湖, Cháng-zhōu, Jiāng-sū. With [[張惠言]] Zhāng Huì-yán, co-founder of the Yáng-hú 陽湖 school of *gǔwén* prose — the principal early-nineteenth-century rival to the Tóngchéng school. *Jǔ-rén* of Qiánlóng 48 (1783) but never advanced through the *jìnshì* examination. Served as sub-prefect and county magistrate in Jiāng-xī (Wǔ-níng 武寧 and Nán-fēng 南豐), known for vigorous administration. His prose program — distinctive from Tóngchéng's [[姚鼐]] in integrating *pián* (parallel-prose) into the *gǔwén* line, on the historical ground that Hàn-Wèi prose was *pián-sàn jiān-xíng* 駢散兼行 — provided the late-Jiāqìng / Dàoguāng theoretical basis for the parallel-prose revival. Major works: *Dà-yún-shān-fáng wén-gǎo* in three sub-collections (*chū-jí* [[KR4f0063]] / *èr-jí* / *yán-shì*); the *Sān dài yīn-yáng wǔ-xíng shuō* 三代陰陽五行說 (a *kǎo-zhèng* reconstruction of pre-Qín cosmology); the *Tōng-lì* 通例 (a systematic bibliographic-formal manual for *bié-jí* compilation, prefixed to the *chū-jí*). *ECCP* 962–963 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 65949.
