Yùn Jìng 惲敬 (1757–1817), *zì* Zǐjū 子居, *hào* Jiǎntāng 簡堂, native of Yánghú 陽湖, Chángzhōu, Jiāngsū. With [[張惠言]] Zhāng Huìyán, co-founder of the Yánghú 陽湖 school of *gǔwén* prose — the principal early-nineteenth-century rival to the Tóngchéng school. *Jǔrén* of Qiánlóng 48 (1783) but never advanced through the *jìnshì* examination. Served as sub-prefect and county magistrate in Jiāngxī (Wǔníng 武寧 and Nánfēng 南豐), known for vigorous administration. His prose program — distinctive from Tóngchéng's [[姚鼐]] in integrating *pián* (parallel-prose) into the *gǔwén* line, on the historical ground that Hàn-Wèi prose was *piánsàn jiānxíng* 駢散兼行 — provided the late-Jiāqìng / Dàoguāng theoretical basis for the parallel-prose revival. Major works: *Dàyúnshānfáng wéngǎo* in three sub-collections (*chūjí* [[KR4f0063]] / *èrjí* / *yánshì*); the *Sān dài yīnyáng wǔxíng shuō* 三代陰陽五行說 (a *kǎozhèng* reconstruction of pre-Qín cosmology); the *Tōnglì* 通例 (a systematic bibliographic-formal manual for *biéjí* compilation, prefixed to the *chūjí*). *ECCP* 962–963 (Tu Lien-che); CBDB id 65949.
