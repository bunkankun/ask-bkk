# 黃雲海

**Huáng Yúnhǎi** (*fl.* early Xiánfēng), Zhèjiāng literatus, friend of [[趙蘭亭]] Zhào Lántíng and [[管頌聲]] Guǎn Sòngshēng; named in Guǎn's preface to the 1852 *editio princeps* of [[KR3el014]] *Jiùshāng mìzhǐ* as the original preface-writer for the volume. No further biographical record located; likely a variant transcription of, or the same person as, [[黃鐎]] Huáng Jiāo of Cāngxī 蒼溪, whose 1851-dated preface is preserved in the same edition.
