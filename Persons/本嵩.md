Běn-sōng 本嵩 (active late 11th – early 12th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; author of the *[[KR6e0102|Zhù huáyán jīng tí fǎ jiè guān-mén sòng 註華嚴經題法界觀門頌]]* (T1885), a verse-hymn (*sòng* 頌) on the title of the *Avataṃsaka* and the doctrine of the Dharma-realm contemplation. Working under the Northern-Sòng Huì-yīn-sì 慧因寺 Huáyán revival programme that had been established by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán; his commentator [[琮湛]] Cóng-zhàn was responsible for the prose annotation accompanying the verse-hymn. Born and death dates are not preserved.
