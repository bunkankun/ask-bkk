# Shun'oku Myōha 春屋妙葩

Japanese Rinzai Zen monk (1311–1388), prominent Gozan literary figure; abbot of Tenryū-ji 天龍寺 and overseer of the 1349 Gozan-ban edition of Shàotán's *Wǔjiā zhèngzōng zàn* 五家正宗贊.
