# Wéikāng 惟康

Southern-Sòng Chán junior-disciple (*xiǎo shī* 小師) of Wúwén Dàocàn 無文道燦. Compiler of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0306]]. Lifedates unrecorded.

Note: a disciple also named 惟康 appears as one of the 27 editors of the *yǔlù* of Huáihǎi Yuánzhào ([[KR6q0304]]); the relationship (if any) to the present Wéikāng is unclear.
