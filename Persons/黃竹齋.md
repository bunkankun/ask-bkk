**Huáng Zhúzhāi** 黃竹齋 (1886–1960, 民國), Republican-period physician and *Shānghán* specialist, the principal collator and editor of the 1939 Báiyúnshān shè 白雲山舍 print of the *Guìlín gǔběn* 桂林古本 recension of the *Shānghán zábìng lùn* ([[KR3ef002]]). His collation notes against the *Sòngběn* and the *Jīnguì yàoluè* were the foundation of the 1939 and 1948 prints.
