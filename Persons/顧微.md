Gù Wēi 顧微 (*fl.* Eastern Jin dynasty, 317–420 CE) was a geographic writer, author of a *Guǎngzhōu jì* 廣州記 ([[KR2k0184]]), a record of the Guangzhou (Canton) region. He belongs to the prominent Wú-region Gù 顧 clan (the same Gù family as Gù Kǎizhī 顧愷之 the painter). His *Guǎngzhōu jì* is cited in Tang-Song encyclopedias and is sometimes conflated with the homonymous work by Péi Yuān 裴淵 ([[裴淵]]). No further biographical details are attested.
