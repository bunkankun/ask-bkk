# Wú Kèjǐ 吳克巳

Sòng-period lay Buddhist editorial patron, sobriquetted **Kǎiān** 鎧菴 ("Armor Hut"). Author of the *Chóngkān Shāndìng zhǐguān xù* 重刊刪定止觀序, the re-engraving preface to [[梁肅]] Liáng Sù's *Shāndìng zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0134]], X55n0915), which framed the work as a Tiāntái counter to the Chán-school *jiàowài biéchuán* 教外別傳 slogan. Lifedates and further biographical details not recovered.
