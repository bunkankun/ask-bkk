**Nagatomi Dokushōan** 永富獨嘯庵 (1732–1766), Edo-period Japanese physician of the *kohōha* 古方派 (Ancient-Formula School), pupil of [[山脇東洋]] Yamawaki Tōyō and teacher of [[吉益東洞]] Yoshimasu Tōdō's circle. His principal work is *Manbyō chiyō* 漫遊雜記 / *Tōiroku* 吐方考. Cited in [[KR3eq038]] *Xiānzhé yīhuà jí*.
