Zhào Shànyù 趙善譽 (1143–1189), *zì* Jìng-zhī 靜之, *hào* Shù-zhāi 恕齋. A member of the Sòng imperial clan (*zōng-shì* 宗室). First place in the *Lǐ-bù shì* 禮部試 (Ministry-of-Rites examination) of *Qián-dào* 5 (1169) under Xiào-zōng. Held *Dà-lǐ chéng* 大理丞 (Court of Judicial Review compiler), *Tóng-chuān-lù tí-xíng* 潼川路提刑 (Surveillance Commissioner of the Tóng-chuān circuit, eastern Sìchuān), and *zhuǎn-yùn pàn-guān* 轉運判官 (Fiscal Vice-Commissioner). At the time of the 1186 Qín Yù 秦焴 reprint of his *Yì shuō*, he had transferred to a *xiáng-xíng* 祥刑 / criminal-justice oversight position in *Dōng-Shǔ* 東蜀 (eastern Sìchuān).

Biography in *Sòngshǐ*; references in Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū-lù jiě-tí*. CBDB id 50966 records the imperial-clan affiliation and the death year (1189) without an explicit birth year. The catalog 1143 birth-year is a defensible inference from the 1169 *Lǐ-bù shì* first-place result (typical examination-success age 25–30) and is followed here.

Praised by Zhū Xī ("able to extend what earlier *rú* had not yet made clear") and drawn on extensively by Féng Yǐ 馮椅's *Yìxué* 易學 ([[KR1a0044]]).

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0039]] *Zhào-shì Yì shuō* 趙氏易說 (also titled *Shù-zhāi Yì shuō* 恕齋易說 in the 1186 print) — a 4-juan reconstruction by the Sìkù editors from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* citations of an originally 2-juan work (*Sòngshǐ Yìwén-zhì*) presented at court during his Tóng-chuān fiscal-commissionership.
