Zhào Shànyù 趙善譽 (1143–1189), *zì* Jìngzhī 靜之, *hào* Shùzhāi 恕齋. A member of the Sòng imperial clan (*zōngshì* 宗室). First place in the *Lǐbù shì* 禮部試 (Ministry-of-Rites examination) of *Qiándào* 5 (1169) under Xiàozōng. Held *Dàlǐ chéng* 大理丞 (Court of Judicial Review compiler), *Tóngchuānlù tíxíng* 潼川路提刑 (Surveillance Commissioner of the Tóngchuān circuit, eastern Sìchuān), and *zhuǎnyùn pànguān* 轉運判官 (Fiscal Vice-Commissioner). At the time of the 1186 Qín Yù 秦焴 reprint of his *Yì shuō*, he had transferred to a *xiángxíng* 祥刑 / criminal-justice oversight position in *Dōng-Shǔ* 東蜀 (eastern Sìchuān).

Biography in *Sòngshǐ*; references in Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*. CBDB id 50966 records the imperial-clan affiliation and the death year (1189) without an explicit birth year. The catalog 1143 birth-year is a defensible inference from the 1169 *Lǐbù shì* first-place result (typical examination-success age 25–30) and is followed here.

Praised by Zhū Xī ("able to extend what earlier *rú* had not yet made clear") and drawn on extensively by Féng Yǐ 馮椅's *Yìxué* 易學 ([[KR1a0044]]).

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0039]] *Zhàoshì Yì shuō* 趙氏易說 (also titled *Shùzhāi Yì shuō* 恕齋易說 in the 1186 print) — a 4-juan reconstruction by the Sìkù editors from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* citations of an originally 2-juan work (*Sòngshǐ Yìwénzhì*) presented at court during his Tóngchuān fiscal-commissionership.
