Táo Bǎolián 陶保廉 (1862–1938) was the son of the Qīng official and scholar Táo Mó 陶模 (governor-general of Liǎng-Guǎng). A late-Qīng / Republican-era literatus, he is remembered in the present catalog as the recorder (*lù* 錄) of his master [[梁玉瑜]] Liáng Yùyú's medical *dáwèn* 答問 ([[KR3eh030]]). His best-known independent work is *Xīnmǎo shìxíng jì* 辛卯侍行記 (1891), the travel diary of his service in the Xīběi.
