# Zhái Fèngxiáng 翟鳳翔

Late-Qīng to early-Republican collator (*zì* Zhǎnchéng 展成), associated with the Wǔchāng yī xué guǎn 武昌醫學館 of [[柯逢時]] Kē Féngshí. Collated the *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]]) 1910 critical edition together with [[蕭延平]] Xiāo Yánpíng. Lifedates unrecorded.
