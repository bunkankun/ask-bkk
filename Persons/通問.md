Ruò'ān Tōngwèn 箬庵通問 (1604–1655) was a late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì (Yángqí-branch) Chán master, native of **Sōng-líng** 松陵 (Wú-jiāng 吳江, Sū-zhōu). He was a principal dharma-heir of **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** [[圓修]] 天隱圓修 (1575–1635) of **Qìng-shān** 磬山 (Yíxīng), alongside **Línggāo Tōngyù** 林臯通豫, **Yùlín Tōngxiù** 玉林通琇, and **Shāncí Tōngjì** 山茨通際. His principal abbacy was at **Nán-jiàn Lǐ-ān-sì** 南澗理安寺 at Nán-jiàn south of Hángzhōu, whence the epithet *Nán-jiàn* 南澗; in old age he took the sobriquet *Lǚ-bó lǎo-rén* 旅泊老人 ("Old Man of the Lodger's Anchorage"). He died on Shùnzhì 12.9.27 (26 October 1655), aged 52. Tōngwèn functioned as a major compiler and dharma-historiographer of his generation: as principal editor (*dìngbiān* 等編) of his teacher's *Tiānyǐn héshàng yǔlù* 天隱和尚語錄 ([[KR6q0397]]), and as redactor (*biāndìng* 編定) of the *Xùdēng cúngǎo* 續燈存稿 ([[KR6q0030]]). Biographical source: 《布水臺集》卷14〈南澗箬菴問禪師塔銘〉; *Zōng-tǒng biān-nián* 宗統編年 juan 32; *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書 juan 66.
