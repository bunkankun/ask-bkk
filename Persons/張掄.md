# Zhāng Lún 張掄

Southern-Sòng lay Buddhist, poet, and song-writer (*cí* 詞 writer), styled Liánshè jūshì 蓮社居士 ("Layman of the Lotus Society"), active in the Chúnxī 淳熙 and Shàoxī 紹熙 eras (1170s–1190s) under Xiàozōng and Guāngzōng. Author of a small but historically important *cí* corpus (preserved in the *Shào'ān lèbiān* 紹庵樂編) of songs that were performed at court, and known among Dàhuì-tradition sources as a patron of Chán. Wrote the 1183 (Chúnxī 10.4.15) preface to the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī niánpǔ* ([[KR6q0063]]) compiled by [[祖詠]] Zǔyǒng — noting therein that the preface was requested through Jìng'ān Huáng Rǔlín 敬菴黃汝霖, whose ancestor had studied in Dàhuì's room.
