# Yán Péixī 嚴培西

A late-Míng / early-Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar of the **Yúnqī Zhūhóng** 雲棲袾宏 [[袾宏]] circle, identified in the colophons of his work as a *jūshì* 居士. Lifedates not preserved with precision. He collaborated with [[虞執西]] **Yú Zhíxī** 虞執西 on the compilation of the **《雲棲淨土彙語》** *Yúnqī jìngtǔ huìyǔ* [[KR6p0089]] — an anthology of Yúnqī's Pure Land sayings. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
