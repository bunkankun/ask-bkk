# 陳順庵

Chén Shùnān 陳順庵, early-nineteenth-century Sūzhōu collector / literatus at whose eastern-suburb residence [[葉萬青]] Yè Wànqīng saw a 110-case extract of Yè-school material in Dàoguāng xīnmǎo (1831), as recorded in the preface to [[KR3ep079]] *Yèshì yīàn cúnzhēn*.
