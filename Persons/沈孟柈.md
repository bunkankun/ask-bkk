# Shěn Mèngbàn 沈孟柈

Southern-Sòng lay Buddhist; narrator (*xù shù* 敘述) of the *Jìdiān Dàojì chánshī yǔlù* 濟顛道濟禪師語錄 [[KR6q0295]] — the early text on the "Crazy Jì" (Ji Gong) Chán-eccentric legend. Lifedates unrecorded.
