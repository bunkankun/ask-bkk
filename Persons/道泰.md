# Dàotài 道泰

Yuán-dynasty Chán monk. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*jí* 集) of the *Chán lín lèi jù* 禪林類聚 [[KR6q0248]] — a twenty-juan Yuán Chán classified-reference anthology organising SòngYuán Chán source-materials by topical category.

Distinct from at least two earlier DILA-recorded monks of the same name 道泰: the LiúSòng 涼州 Dàotài (DILA A001526) and the BěiWèi Dàotài (A006803). The Yuán Dàotài who compiled *Chán lín lèi jù* is not separately registered in the DILA authority database.
