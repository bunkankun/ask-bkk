# Dào-tài 道泰

Yuán-dynasty Chán monk. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*jí* 集) of the *Chán lín lèi jù* 禪林類聚 [[KR6q0248]] — a twenty-juan Yuán Chán classified-reference anthology organising Sòng-Yuán Chán source-materials by topical category.

Distinct from at least two earlier DILA-recorded monks of the same name 道泰: the Liú-Sòng 涼州 Dào-tài (DILA A001526) and the Běi-Wèi Dào-tài (A006803). The Yuán Dào-tài who compiled *Chán lín lèi jù* is not separately registered in the DILA authority database.
