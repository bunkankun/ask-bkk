聖法 Shèng-fǎ ("Sage Dharma") was a Táng-period or Five-Dynasties-period Chinese Buddhist monk associated with the Esoteric (密教) commentarial tradition on the [[KR6o0084|*Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn* 釋摩訶衍論]]. He is known only as the author/compiler of the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn jì* 釋摩訶衍論記 ([[KR6o0085]]), a brief notes-and-extract compendium on the parent treatise. The colophon "沙門聖法鈔" identifies him only by *shamen* (śramaṇa) and the verb 鈔 ("to extract / to copy"). He is not attested in the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* 宋高僧傳, *Jǐng-dé chuán-dēng lù* 景德傳燈錄, or other standard biographical sources; internal evidence places him loosely in the late Tang to Five Dynasties period. His work survived only through Japanese manuscript transmission in the *Manji-zokuzōkyō* 卍續藏經.
