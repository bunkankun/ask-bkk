# 孫璧峰瑛

Sūn Bìfēng Yǐng, grandson of the mid-Qīng ophthalmologist [[黃庭鏡]] Huáng Tíngjìng; recovered Huáng's manuscript after the pirate-edition by Dèng Xuélǐ and arranged the corrected Jiāqìng 23 (1818) family edition of [[KR3em006]] *Mùjīng dàchéng* 目經大成.
