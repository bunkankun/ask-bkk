Àixīn-juéluó Hónglì 愛新覺羅弘曆 (1711–1799), reign-name *Qiánlóng* 乾隆 (r. 1735–1796), temple name *Gāozōng* 高宗. Fourth Manchu Qīng emperor to rule over China; longest de facto reign in Chinese history. Sponsor of the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 imperial compilation (1772–1782), the *Sìkù tíyào* 四庫提要 prefaces, and numerous *Yùtí* 御題 / *Yùzhì* 御製 imperial-composition poems and prefaces attached to individual works (e.g. *Yì chuán dēng* [[KR1a0044]], *Zhōuyì zǒng yì* [[KR1a0048]]). Under his patronage [[紀昀]] Jǐ Yún, [[陸錫熊]] Lù Xīxióng, and [[孫士毅]] Sūn Shìyì served as General Compilers of the *Sìkù*; [[陸費墀]] Lù Fèichí as General Reviser.
