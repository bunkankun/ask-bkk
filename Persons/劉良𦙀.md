# Liú Liángshū 劉良𦙀

Southern-Sòng medical scholar of Shòuchūn 壽春. Known from Yè Dàlián's 1186 postface to the *Yèshì lùyàn fāng* ([[KR3ed130]]) as one of the two collators (alongside Xǔ Yáochén of Sānshān) of the family-transmitted formulary, prepared for the Lóngshū jùnzhāi woodblock printing.
