# Zhèngjì 正寂

Late-Míng Tiāntái-affiliated Pure Land monk active in the **Huátíng** 華亭 region (modern Sōngjiāng 松江, Shànghǎi). Lifedates not recorded with precision; he was active in the late Wànlì / early Tiānqǐ period, immediately after the death of [[傳燈]] **Yōuxī Chuándēng** 幽溪傳燈 (1554–1628), and his major work — the **《淨土生無生論註》** *Jìngtǔ shēng wúshēng lùn zhù* [[KR6p0070]] (X1167, 1 *juǎn*) — is an annotated commentary on Chuándēng's foundational Tiāntái-Pure Land treatise [[KR6p0056]]. The annotation is the earliest sustained commentary on Chuándēng's text and is the principal seventeenth-century reading apparatus for the *Shēng wúshēng lùn*. Beyond this work no substantial biographical record survives.
