**Yáo Mèng-yù** 姚孟昱 (fl. late Wàn-lì), *hào* **Jìn-yě-zǐ** 近野子 — Míng *jìn shì* (CBDB index year 1559) who served as Diplomatic Envoy in the *Xíng-rén sī* 行人司, the Hàn-lín-style envoy bureau of the Míng outer court. He is known as a fellow-townsman and intellectual associate of [[王一清]] (Tǐ-wù-zǐ) and contributed the dated preface (萬曆丁酉春上元 = 4 February 1597) to Wáng's *Dào dé jīng shì cí* ([[KR5i0007]]), in which he describes Wáng's enforced retirement to the Tài-hé cliffs and praises the resulting commentary as restoring the lost broad-political reading of the *Lǎo zǐ*.
