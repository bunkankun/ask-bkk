**Dài Zǔqǐ** 戴祖啟 (Qiánlóng era, 清), grandson of [[戴天章]] Dài Tiānzhāng. In Qiánlóng 43 (1778) he reprinted the authentic family-line text of *Guǎng wēnyì lùn* ([[KR3eg023]]) with a colophon correcting the pirated-edition story (the late-Qiánlóng circulation of the work under the false title *Wēnyì míngbiàn* attributed to [[鄭奠一]] Zhèng Diànyī).
