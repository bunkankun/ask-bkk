Wáng Xǐ 王喜 (fl. mid-14th c.), a Yuán-era hydraulic engineer of unrecorded native place. The Sìkù tíyào notes only that "his rank and place are unknown." His sole surviving work is the *Zhì-hé tú-lüè* ([[KR2k0064]]), a 1-juan illustrated memorial on Yellow River management, presented to the throne in connection with the Zhì-zhèng-era (1341–1370) Yellow River crisis — most likely shortly after the Bái-máo dyke breach of Zhì-zhèng 4 (1344) and before Jiǎ Lǔ's restoration in 1351. CBDB has multiple homonymous entries for 王喜 across the Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng range; no confident match for the Yuán hydraulic engineer.
