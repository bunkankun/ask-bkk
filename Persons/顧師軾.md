Gù Shīshì 顧師軾 (1799–1884), late-Qīng scholar from the Sūzhōu / Tàicāng region, principal compiler of the *Wú Méicūn xiānshēng nián pǔ* 吳梅村先生年譜 (1841) — the foundational chronological biography of [[吳偉業]] Wú Wěiyè (1609–1672) — and editor of the *fùlù* 坿錄 to the *Méicūn jiācáng gǎo* 梅村家藏藁 ([[KR4f0013]]), which assembles biographical and bibliographic apparatus around Wú's collected works. The CBDB entry (id 86282) confirms his Dào-Guāng to Guāngxù lifespan (1799–1884), placing him exactly mid-19th-century Suzhou literati culture. His editorial work on the Méicūn corpus appeared in the 1841 *gēngxū* recension printed at Tàicāng, the basis for the Republican-era SBCK reprint with collation by Sūn Yùxiū.
