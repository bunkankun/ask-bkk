Xú Jiǒng 徐炯 (*zì* Zhāng-zhòng 章仲), of *Kūn-shān* 崑山 (Jiāng-sū). *Kāng-xī rén-xū* (1682) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Zhí-lì xún-dào* (Inspector of *Zhí-lì*). In *Kāng-xī gēng-wǔ* (1690) he served as *jǔ-rén* examiner in *Fú-jiàn*, where he obtained from **Lín Jí** 林佶 the manuscript base of **Zhū Hè-líng**'s incomplete prose-collation of **Lǐ Shāng-yǐn** [[李商隱]]; this became the seed for the **Xú brothers'** complete edition. Brother of **Xú Shù-gǔ** 徐樹穀 [[徐樹穀]].

Xú Jiǒng's contribution to the *Lǐ Yì-shān wén-jí jiān-zhù* [[KR4c0076]] is the *zhù* (commentary) component, focused on allusion-tracing and *xùn-gǔ* (etymology / classical glossing); his brother's *jiān* attended to historical-event verification. Xú Jiǒng also supplemented the base text from the *Wén-yuàn yīng-huá* (especially for the missing *zhuàng* genre) and added the *Chóng-yáng tíng míng* — though this last attribution was later questioned by Féng Hào on philological grounds (Yáng Shèn forgery suspected).
