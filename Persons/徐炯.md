Xú Jiǒng 徐炯 (*zì* Zhāngzhòng 章仲), of *Kūnshān* 崑山 (Jiāngsū). *Kāngxī rénxū* (1682) *jìnshì*; rose to *Zhílì xúndào* (Inspector of *Zhílì*). In *Kāngxī gēngwǔ* (1690) he served as *jǔrén* examiner in *Fújiàn*, where he obtained from **Lín Jí** 林佶 the manuscript base of **Zhū Hèlíng**'s incomplete prose-collation of **Lǐ Shāngyǐn** [[李商隱]]; this became the seed for the **Xú brothers'** complete edition. Brother of **Xú Shùgǔ** 徐樹穀 [[徐樹穀]].

Xú Jiǒng's contribution to the *Lǐ Yìshān wénjí jiānzhù* [[KR4c0076]] is the *zhù* (commentary) component, focused on allusion-tracing and *xùngǔ* (etymology / classical glossing); his brother's *jiān* attended to historical-event verification. Xú Jiǒng also supplemented the base text from the *Wényuàn yīnghuá* (especially for the missing *zhuàng* genre) and added the *Chóngyáng tíng míng* — though this last attribution was later questioned by Féng Hào on philological grounds (Yáng Shèn forgery suspected).
