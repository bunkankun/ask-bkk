Xìng-liàng 性亮, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Tiě-méi Huì-lì** [[慧麗]] 鐵眉慧麗 (1586–1650) in the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派. Co-compiler with **Xìng-yǎng** [[性養]] of the 1-juan 《鐵眉三巴掌禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0452]]). No independent biography or *yǔ-lù* located.
