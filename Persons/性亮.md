Xìngliàng 性亮, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Tiěméi Huìlì** [[慧麗]] 鐵眉慧麗 (1586–1650) in the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派. Co-compiler with **Xìngyǎng** [[性養]] of the 1-juan 《鐵眉三巴掌禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0452]]). No independent biography or *yǔlù* located.
