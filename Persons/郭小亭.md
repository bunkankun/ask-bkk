**Guō Xiǎotíng** 郭小亭 is the name under which the Qīng-dynasty popular novel *Jìgōng Quánzhuàn* 濟公全傳 ([[KR4k0151]]) is attributed. Whether "郭小亭" represents a real personal name, a pen name (*bǐmíng* 筆名), or a publisher's attribution is unclear; no independent biographical record has been identified for this person in standard Qīng sources or in CBDB. The work is generally dated to the Guāngxù period (c. 1898) on the basis of bibliographical evidence from late-Qīng popular fiction catalogs. No confident CBDB match exists.
