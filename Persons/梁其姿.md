# Liáng Qízī 梁其姿

Contemporary historian of Chinese medicine and society (Hong Kong; formerly Academia Sinica); director of the Hong Kong Institute for the Humanities and Social Sciences, University of Hong Kong. Author of *Leprosy in China: A History* (Columbia, 2009), *Charity and Moral Transformation: Benevolent Societies in Ming and Qing China* (in Chinese, 1997; English, 2021), and "'Variolation' and Vaccination in Late Imperial China" (in *History of Vaccine Development*, Springer, 2011); the principal English-language authority on the social history of medicine in late-imperial China.
