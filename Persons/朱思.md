**Zhū Sī** 朱思 (fl. early–mid Southern Sòng), of Sū-zhōu 蘇州, *cóng-sūn* 從孫 (great-nephew) of Zhū Cháng-wén 朱長文 [[朱長文]]. Held office as *Zhī Hàn-yáng-jūn* 知漢陽軍 (Prefect of Hàn-yáng-jūn). After the *Jīng-kāng* / *Jiàn-yán* military disaster destroyed his great-uncle's *Lè-pǔ jí* (originally 100 *juǎn*), Zhū Sī collected the surviving fragments — 163 poems, 5 *jì*, 6 *xù*, 7 *qǐ*, 5 *muzhi*, 6 miscellaneous prose — and edited them into *Lè-pǔ yú-gǎo* 樂圃餘藁 [[KR4d0106]] in 10 *juǎn* + 1 appendix-*juǎn* of biographical materials, cutting the woodblocks for transmission. The edition is the principal channel for Zhū Cháng-wén's surviving literary works.
