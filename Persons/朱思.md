**Zhū Sī** 朱思 (fl. early–mid Southern Sòng), of Sūzhōu 蘇州, *cóngsūn* 從孫 (great-nephew) of Zhū Chángwén 朱長文 [[朱長文]]. Held office as *Zhī Hànyángjūn* 知漢陽軍 (Prefect of Hànyángjūn). After the *Jīngkāng* / *Jiànyán* military disaster destroyed his great-uncle's *Lèpǔ jí* (originally 100 *juǎn*), Zhū Sī collected the surviving fragments — 163 poems, 5 *jì*, 6 *xù*, 7 *qǐ*, 5 *muzhi*, 6 miscellaneous prose — and edited them into *Lèpǔ yúgǎo* 樂圃餘藁 [[KR4d0106]] in 10 *juǎn* + 1 appendix-*juǎn* of biographical materials, cutting the woodblocks for transmission. The edition is the principal channel for Zhū Chángwén's surviving literary works.
