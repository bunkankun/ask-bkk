Chéng Shīgōng 程師恭, *zì* Shūcái 叔才, late-Kāngxī Anhui (Wǎn) literati, author of the principal commentary on [[陳維崧]] Chén Wéisōng's parallel-prose corpus — the *Chén Jiǎn-tǎo sì-liù zhù* 陳檢討四六注, completed in Kāngxī 32 (1693, *guǐyǒu*). No CBDB record. Chéng is identified in [[王士禛]] Wáng Shìzhēn's *Gǔ-fū-yú tíng zá lù* 古夫于亭襍録 as having "by sole devotion of his ability annotated the parallel-prose of his fellow-townsman whom he had never met in person" — Chéng was a *shēng-yuán* in Wǎn (Anhui), not Yí-xìng (Jiāngsū) Chén's native place, and never met Chén Wéisōng but undertook the annotation after Chén's death and after the Jiǎng Jǐng-qí editorial *cǎo-táng* recension. The *Sìkù tíyào* of [[KR4f0031]] criticizes Chéng's annotations on multiple points of allusion-recognition but credits his philological labor for the parallel-prose's transmission, since *sì-liù* parallel-prose without annotation is largely unreadable for posterity.
