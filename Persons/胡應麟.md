Hú Yìnglín 胡應麟 (1551–1602), *zì* originally Yuánruì 元瑞, changed to Míngruì 明瑞, *hào* Shǎoshì shānrén 少室山人 / Shíyángshēng 石羊生, of Lánxī 蘭溪 (Zhèjiāng). Took the *jǔrén* in Wànlì 4 (1576); never *jìnshì*. Travelled in the **Hòu Qī Zǐ** orbit of **Lǐ Pānlóng** and **Wáng Shìzhēn**, becoming the leading second-generation theoretical voice. He is the author of the *Shīsǒu* 詩藪 — the most influential single Míng *shīhuà* — and of the *Sìbù zhèngé* 四部正譌 (Corrections to Errors in the Four Divisions), foundational for Míng *biànwèi* (forgery-detection) scholarship. His *jìsòng yānbó* (broad memorization) was widely praised in the *Sìkù* tíyào. His literary collection is the *Shǎoshì shānfáng jí* [[KR4e0218]] — a 120-*juǎn* consolidation by **Jiāng Zhànrán** of about 20 separately-circulated sub-collections, with appended **Wáng Shìzhēn**-authored *Shíyángshēng zhuàn*.
