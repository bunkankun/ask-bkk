Huìlì 慧力 was a late-fourth-century Chinese monk who served as one of the scribes (筆受) for the Former Qín translation of the *Āpítán Bā Jiāndù Lùn* 阿毘曇八犍度論 ([[KR6l0008]], T1543, the *Aṣṭagrantha* / *Jñāna-prasthāna*) under [[僧伽提婆]] Sēngjiātípó (*Saṃghadeva*) and [[竺佛念]] Zhú Fóniàn, with [[道安]] Dào'ān as patron. He is named only in Dào'ān's preface to that work and is otherwise undocumented.
