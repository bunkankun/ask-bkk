# Chāocháng 超常

MíngQīng transitional Chán monk of Sichuan; attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[悟卓|Pòshí Wùzhuó]] 破石悟卓 (1609–1654) at Jǐnpíngshān 錦屏山. Lifedates unrecorded.

Compiler (*jì lù* 記錄) of his master's *Jǐnpíng Pòshí Zhuó chánshī zá zhù* 錦屏破石卓禪師雜著 [[KR6q0228]] — Wùzhuó's brief one-juan poetic-miscellany.
