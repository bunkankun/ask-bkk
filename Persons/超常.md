# Chāo-cháng 超常

Míng-Qīng transitional Chán monk of Sichuan; attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[悟卓|Pò-shí Wù-zhuó]] 破石悟卓 (1609–1654) at Jǐn-píng-shān 錦屏山. Lifedates unrecorded.

Compiler (*jì lù* 記錄) of his master's *Jǐn-píng Pò-shí Zhuó chánshī zá zhù* 錦屏破石卓禪師雜著 [[KR6q0228]] — Wù-zhuó's brief one-juan poetic-miscellany.
