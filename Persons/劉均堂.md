Liú Jūntáng 劉均堂, an early-Yuán scholar with the rank *Biānxiū* 編修 (Compiler at the imperial historiographical office). Cited as a teacher of [[胡震]] Hú Zhèn (auto-preface to *Zhōuyì yǎn yì* [[KR1a0075]], dated 1305). Lifedates and biographical details are not securely attested in CBDB or standard reference works.
