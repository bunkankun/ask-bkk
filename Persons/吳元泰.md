**Wú Yuántài** 吳元泰 (fl. late 16th century) is a late-Míng author known only as the compiler of [[KR4k0046]] *Dōngyóu Jì* 東游記 (*Journey to the East*), a novel of the Eight Immortals. No biographical detail is available; no CBDB record has been located. The work was published in the late Wànlì era.
