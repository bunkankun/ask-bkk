Shào Tóng 邵銅 (active mid-15th c.), native of **Fúqīng** 福清 (Fújiàn) — Lín Hóng's fellow-townsman. Tiān-shùn-era (1457–1464) *yùshǐ* 御史 who, per **Yè Shèng** 葉盛's *Shuǐdōng rìjì* 水東日記, on a matter of memorial offended a powerful court figure and was demoted to *zhīxiàn*; later rose to *Wēnzhōu zhīfǔ* 溫州知府. Editor of his townsman [[林鴻]] Lín Hóng's verse collection [[KR4e0044]] *Míngshèng jí* 鳴盛集 in the early Chénghuà era (1465+). The *Sìkù* editors fault him for appending an apocryphal *Zhāng Hóngqiáo chànghé* 張紅橋唱和 sequence drawn from Míng-era *xiǎoshuō* to Lín Hóng's authentic manuscript — judging that as an upright *qìjié* official Shào was unfamiliar with proper literary-collection editorial form. CBDB id 128553 records the name without dates.
