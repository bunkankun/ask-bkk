Shào Tóng 邵銅 (active mid-15th c.), native of **Fú-qīng** 福清 (Fú-jiàn) — Lín Hóng's fellow-townsman. Tiān-shùn-era (1457–1464) *yù-shǐ* 御史 who, per **Yè Shèng** 葉盛's *Shuǐ-dōng rì-jì* 水東日記, on a matter of memorial offended a powerful court figure and was demoted to *zhī-xiàn*; later rose to *Wēn-zhōu zhī-fǔ* 溫州知府. Editor of his townsman [[林鴻]] Lín Hóng's verse collection [[KR4e0044]] *Míng-shèng jí* 鳴盛集 in the early Chéng-huà era (1465+). The *Sìkù* editors fault him for appending an apocryphal *Zhāng Hóng-qiáo chàng-hé* 張紅橋唱和 sequence drawn from Míng-era *xiǎo-shuō* to Lín Hóng's authentic manuscript — judging that as an upright *qì-jié* official Shào was unfamiliar with proper literary-collection editorial form. CBDB id 128553 records the name without dates.
