# Zhī Dùn 支遁 (Zhī Dàolín 支道林)

Eastern Jìn 東晉 Buddhist monk, *qīngtán* 清談 philosopher, and one of the central interpreters of *prajñāpāramitā* in Chinese in the mid-fourth century. Lifedates 314–366 CE. Personally associated with the *xuánxué* literati circle of Wáng Xīzhī 王羲之, Xǔ Xún 許詢, and Sūn Chuò 孫綽. Author of an influential commentary on the *Xiāoyáo yóu* 逍遙遊 chapter of the *Zhuāngzǐ* 莊子 and of the early Chinese formulation of the *jíse zōng* 即色宗 ("Identity-with-Form") school of Mahāyāna philosophy. Biography in *Gāosēng zhuàn* 高僧傳 (T2059) juan 4.
