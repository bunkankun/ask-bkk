**Chén Guāngsōng** 陳光淞 (late Qīng / early Republican), physician and medical philologist active in the 1900s–1910s. Author of the *Wēnrè lùn jiānzhèng* 溫熱論箋正 ([[KR3eg016]]), a critical-annotation edition of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì's *Wēnrè lùn* ([[KR3eg001]]), within the broader Shàoxīng-school late-Qīng / Republican *wēnbìng* editorial project led by [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén and his circle.
