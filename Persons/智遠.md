Zhì-yuǎn 智遠, *zì* **Fǎ-chuáng** 法幢 ("Dharma-Banner"); often referred to as **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** 法幢智遠. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Chún-bèi Guǎng-dé** [[廣德]]. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Shǔ-dōng Wàn-xiàn** 蜀東萬縣 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Sòng** 宋.

**Abbacy.** **Yí-líng-zhōu Huáng-líng chán-yuàn** 彝陵州黃陵禪院 (the same seat his teacher had held).

**Disciple.** **Ān-shí Tōng-huì** 安石通慧 [[通慧]] (sole *fǎ-sì*, compiler of his *yǔ-lù*).

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0550]] 《純備德禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B433.
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0551]] 《法幢遠禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B434.

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 15 (X85 n1590 p. 198c17).
