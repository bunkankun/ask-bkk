Zhìyuǎn 智遠, *zì* **Fǎchuáng** 法幢 ("Dharma-Banner"); often referred to as **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** 法幢智遠. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Chúnbèi Guǎngdé** [[廣德]]. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Shǔdōng Wànxiàn** 蜀東萬縣 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Sòng** 宋.

**Abbacy.** **Yílíngzhōu Huánglíng chányuàn** 彝陵州黃陵禪院 (the same seat his teacher had held).

**Disciple.** **Ānshí Tōnghuì** 安石通慧 [[通慧]] (sole *fǎsì*, compiler of his *yǔlù*).

**Works.**
- Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0550]] 《純備德禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B433.
- Own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0551]] 《法幢遠禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B434.

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 15 (X85 n1590 p. 198c17).
