Republican-era physician, otherwise undocumented. Author of *Qiūnüè zhǐnán* 秋瘧指南 ([[KR3eg049]]), a clinical handbook on **autumn-malaria** (*qiūnüè* 秋瘧). No detailed biographical material located in standard reference works. No CBDB record. (Not to be confused with the modern Taiwan surgeon Lin Tien-You 林天祐, 1912–1997.)
