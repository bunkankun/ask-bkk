Northern-Sòng *Hàn-lín xué-shì chéng-zhǐ* and lay Buddhist devotee, *zì* Míng-yuǎn 明遠, posthumous *Wén-yuán* 文元, of *Chán-zhōu Qīng-fēng* 澶州清豐 (family had moved to *Péng-mén* 彭門). *Jìn-shì* of Tài-píng xīng-guó 5 / 980. Pupil of Wáng Yǔ-chēng [[王禹偁]]. Held *Yòu-zhèng-yán*, *Zhí Shǐ-guǎn*, *Zhī-zhì-gào*, *Hàn-lín xué-shì*, *Hàn-lín chéng-zhǐ*; in *Tiān-xǐ* served as *pàn Xī-jīng liú-sī yù-shǐ-tái* and retired with the title *Tài-zǐ shào-bǎo*. Author of the *Fǎ-zàng suì-jīn lù* [[KR4d0007]] (10 *juǎn*, 1027–1031) and *Dào-yuàn jí yào* [[KR4d0008]] (3 *juǎn*, edited by Wáng Gǔ [[王古]]) — both expressions of his syncretic *Chán*-Confucian-Daoist devotion. Founder of the *Cháo* lineage of Northern-Sòng scholarship; the bibliographer Cháo Gōng-wǔ 晁公武 was his fifth-generation descendant. CBDB id 3012, lifedates 951–1034.
