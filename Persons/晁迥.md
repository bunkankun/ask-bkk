Northern-Sòng *Hànlín xuéshì chéngzhǐ* and lay Buddhist devotee, *zì* Míngyuǎn 明遠, posthumous *Wényuán* 文元, of *Chánzhōu Qīngfēng* 澶州清豐 (family had moved to *Péngmén* 彭門). *Jìnshì* of Tàipíng xīngguó 5 / 980. Pupil of Wáng Yǔchēng [[王禹偁]]. Held *Yòuzhèngyán*, *Zhí Shǐguǎn*, *Zhīzhìgào*, *Hànlín xuéshì*, *Hànlín chéngzhǐ*; in *Tiānxǐ* served as *pàn Xījīng liúsī yùshǐtái* and retired with the title *Tàizǐ shàobǎo*. Author of the *Fǎzàng suìjīn lù* [[KR4d0007]] (10 *juǎn*, 1027–1031) and *Dàoyuàn jí yào* [[KR4d0008]] (3 *juǎn*, edited by Wáng Gǔ [[王古]]) — both expressions of his syncretic *Chán*-Confucian-Daoist devotion. Founder of the *Cháo* lineage of Northern-Sòng scholarship; the bibliographer Cháo Gōngwǔ 晁公武 was his fifth-generation descendant. CBDB id 3012, lifedates 951–1034.
