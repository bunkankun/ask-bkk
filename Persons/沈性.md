Shěn Xìng 沈性 (Míng, fl. Tiān-shùn era = c. 1457-64), *zhī-fǔ* of Níng-guó in the early Míng. Native of Kuài-jī 會稽 (Shào-xīng). Compiler of the present 10-*juǎn* (+ *bǔ-yí* 1 *juǎn*) recension of Gōng Shī-tài [[貢師泰]]'s *Wán-zhāi jí* [[KR4d0540]] from Hóng-wǔ-era / Sòng-Lián / Yú-Què / Huáng-Jìn / Liú-Qīn-edited fragmentary collections. No CBDB lifedates registered; the Tiān-shùn-era *Níng-guó-shǒu* identification is the principal biographical evidence.
