Shěn Xìng 沈性 (Míng, fl. Tiānshùn era = c. 1457-64), *zhīfǔ* of Níngguó in the early Míng. Native of Kuàijī 會稽 (Shàoxīng). Compiler of the present 10-*juǎn* (+ *bǔyí* 1 *juǎn*) recension of Gōng Shītài [[貢師泰]]'s *Wánzhāi jí* [[KR4d0540]] from Hóng-wǔ-era / Sòng-Lián / Yú-Què / Huáng-Jìn / Liú-Qīn-edited fragmentary collections. No CBDB lifedates registered; the Tiān-shùn-era *Níngguóshǒu* identification is the principal biographical evidence.
