Guō Jiàn 郭薦 (fl. ca. 1296–1298), Yuán-period local school official. The Sìkù *tíyào* notes that "his native place is unascertained." He held office as *jiàoyù* 教諭 (Instructor) of Yīn-xiàn 鄞縣 school (a constituent county of Qìngyuán-lù 慶元路 / modern Níngbō) in the late Dàdé era under Yuán Chéngzōng. He was the principal working compiler of the *Chāngguó-zhōu túzhì* ([[KR2k0024]]) (1298), serving as Féng Fújīng's editorial executor under Féng's supervision. He is named alongside Féng in the Sìkù *tíyào* but does not appear in the catalog's principal authorship attribution.
