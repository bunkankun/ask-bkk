**Liǔ Bǎoyí** 柳寶詒 (1842–1901, CBDB 84838), *zì* Gǔsūn 谷孫 (also given 冠群), late-Qīng physician of Jiāngyīn 江陰 (Jiāngsū). One of the principal late-Qīng exponents of the **latent-qi** (*fúqì* 伏氣) theory of *wēnbìng* and a major figure in the Jiāngyīn medical tradition.

His best-known work is the *Wēnrè féngyuán* 溫熱逢源 ([[KR3eg003]]) in 3 *juǎn*, a systematic exposition of latent-qi warm-disease theory grounded in close re-reading of the *Nèijīng* and *Shānghán lùn*. The book did not see print during Liǔ's lifetime but was included in the *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 collectanea edited by Qiū Qìngyún 裘慶元 in the Republican period.

Other works include *Liǔ Xuǎnqìng yīàn* 柳選慶醫案 and *Wēnrè cǎn jiē* 溫熱讖解.
