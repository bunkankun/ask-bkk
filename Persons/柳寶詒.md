**Liǔ Bǎoyí** 柳寶詒 (1842–1901, CBDB 84838), *zì* Gǔ-sūn 谷孫 (also given 冠群), late-Qīng physician of Jiāng-yīn 江陰 (Jiāngsū). One of the principal late-Qīng exponents of the **latent-qi** (*fú-qì* 伏氣) theory of *wēn-bìng* and a major figure in the Jiāng-yīn medical tradition.

His best-known work is the *Wēn-rè féng-yuán* 溫熱逢源 ([[KR3eg003]]) in 3 *juǎn*, a systematic exposition of latent-qi warm-disease theory grounded in close re-reading of the *Nèi-jīng* and *Shāng-hán lùn*. The book did not see print during Liǔ's lifetime but was included in the *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 collectanea edited by Qiū Qìng-yún 裘慶元 in the Republican period.

Other works include *Liǔ Xuǎn-qìng yī-àn* 柳選慶醫案 and *Wēn-rè cǎn jiē* 溫熱讖解.
