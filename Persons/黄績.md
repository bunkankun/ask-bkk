# 黄績

Huáng Jì 黄績 of Pútián 莆田 (Fújiàn), late-Southern-Sòng scholar. Collaborator with [[趙以夫]] Zhào Yǐfū on the *Yì tōng* 易通 ([[KR1a0051]]); per the *Mǐn shū* 閩書, he engaged Zhào Yǐfū "back-and-forth on its discussions" and helped finalize the surviving recension.
