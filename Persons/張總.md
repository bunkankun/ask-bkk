Modern Chinese Buddhist studies scholar, Institute of World Religions of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences (中國社會科學院世界宗教研究所). Specialist in *Dìzàng* 地藏 (*Kṣitigarbha*) studies and Chinese Buddhist iconography. Author of *Dìzàng púsà yán-jiū* 地藏菩薩研究 (Beijing: Zōngjiào wénhuà, 2003). Edited the eight recensions of the popular *Dìzàng púsà shí-zhāi-rì* 地藏菩薩十齋日 ([[KR6v0077]] through [[KR6v0084]]) for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 7.
