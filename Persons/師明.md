# Shī-míng 師明

Southern-Sòng Chán monk. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Compiler (*jí* 集) of the *Xù gǔ zūn sù yǔ yào* 續古尊宿語要 [[KR6q0265]] — the six-juan continuation of the earlier *Gǔ zūn sù yǔ yào* compilation tradition.
