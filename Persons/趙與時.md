Southern-Sòng *zōngshì* (imperial-clansman) writer, *zì* Xíngzhī 行之. (His name is sometimes given in Sòng-Yuán printings with the variant graphs 峕 / 旹 for 時.) Author of the *bǐjì* miscellany *Bīntuì lù* 賓退錄 [[KR3j0051]] in 10 *juǎn*, a respected anecdote-and-textual-criticism collection drawing on his wide reading and his memories of court and literary life. Lifedates conventionally given as 1175–1231 in modern reference works.
