# 伯牙

Spring-and-Autumn-period qín-player; together with [[鍾子期]] Zhōng Zǐqī forms the proverbial pair of *zhīyīn* 知音 ("knowing the sound") — the perfect mutual interpretive understanding between musician and listener. The metaphor furnishes the title of [[如愚]] Rúyú's late-Míng Lotus Sūtra commentary *Fǎhuá jīng zhīyīn* 法華經知音 ([[KR6d0074]]).
