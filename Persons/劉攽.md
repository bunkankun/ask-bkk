Mid-Northern-Sòng historian and *Hàn-shū* specialist, *zì* Gòng-fù 貢父, *hào* Gōng-fēi 公非, of *Xīn-yú* 新喻 (Jiāng-xī, family registered at *Pēng-chéng*). Younger brother of Liú Chǎng [[劉敞]]; *jìn-shì* of *Qìng-lì* 6 / 1046 (the same examination as his brother). The principal *Hàn-shū* / *Sān-guó* historian of his generation: assigned by Sī-mǎ Guāng [[司馬光]] to draft the entire *Hàn* and *Sān-guó* portion of the *Zī-zhì tōng-jiàn* during the 1067–1085 compilation. Refused to enforce the *Xī-níng* New Policies and was demoted to monitor of the *Héng-zhōu* salt warehouses; restored under Zhé-zōng as *zhī Xiāng-zhōu*, *Mì-shū shǎo-jiān*, finally *Zhōng-shū shè-rén*. Author of the *Dōng-Hàn kān-wù* 東漢刊誤 (errata to the *Hòu Hàn-shū*; survives only as appendix to the *Běi-jiān-běn Hòu Hàn-shū*); the *Hàn-guān yí* 漢官儀; the *Wǔ-dài chūn-qiū*; and the influential *Zhōng-shān shī-huà* 中山詩話 [[KR4j0021]]. Died *Yuán-yòu* 4 / 1089 age 67. Surviving collection: *Péng-chéng jí* [[KR4d0045]] (40 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* against the original 50-*juǎn* recension). CBDB id 1220 (no birth/death year recorded; standard 1023–1089 follows *Sòng-shǐ* j. 319). The catalog meta gives 1022–1088, which is followed in the catalog frontmatter; CBDB and *Sòng-shǐ* support 1023–1089 — the discrepancy is one *suì*.
