Mid-Northern-Sòng historian and *Hànshū* specialist, *zì* Gòngfù 貢父, *hào* Gōngfēi 公非, of *Xīnyú* 新喻 (Jiāngxī, family registered at *Pēngchéng*). Younger brother of Liú Chǎng [[劉敞]]; *jìnshì* of *Qìnglì* 6 / 1046 (the same examination as his brother). The principal *Hànshū* / *Sānguó* historian of his generation: assigned by Sīmǎ Guāng [[司馬光]] to draft the entire *Hàn* and *Sānguó* portion of the *Zīzhì tōngjiàn* during the 1067–1085 compilation. Refused to enforce the *Xīníng* New Policies and was demoted to monitor of the *Héngzhōu* salt warehouses; restored under Zhézōng as *zhī Xiāngzhōu*, *Mìshū shǎojiān*, finally *Zhōngshū shèrén*. Author of the *DōngHàn kānwù* 東漢刊誤 (errata to the *Hòu Hànshū*; survives only as appendix to the *Běijiānběn Hòu Hànshū*); the *Hànguān yí* 漢官儀; the *Wǔdài chūnqiū*; and the influential *Zhōngshān shīhuà* 中山詩話 [[KR4j0021]]. Died *Yuányòu* 4 / 1089 age 67. Surviving collection: *Péngchéng jí* [[KR4d0045]] (40 *juǎn*, *Sìkù* reconstitution from *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* against the original 50-*juǎn* recension). CBDB id 1220 (no birth/death year recorded; standard 1023–1089 follows *Sòngshǐ* j. 319). The catalog meta gives 1022–1088, which is followed in the catalog frontmatter; CBDB and *Sòngshǐ* support 1023–1089 — the discrepancy is one *suì*.
