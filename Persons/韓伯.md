Hán Bó 韓伯 (Hán Kāngbó 韓康伯, 332–380), Eastern Jìn *Yìjīng* commentator. Composed the *Xìcí*-*Shuōguà* notes that complete [[王弼]] Wáng Bì's incomplete *Zhōuyì zhù* and have circulated together with it as the canonical received apparatus. The Táng commentator Guō Jīng 郭京 controversially attributed his *Zhōuyì jǔ zhèng* 周易舉正 emendations to a fictitious Wáng-Hán old recension — a fabrication the *Sìkù* editors note in the notice on [[KR1a0162]].
