Jiāng Shàoyú 江少虞 (*fl. Shàoxīng*, c. 1131–1162). The catalog meta and Sìkù tiyao record him as *Zuǒ cháoqǐng dàfū*, *quán fāqiǎn Jízhōu jūnzhōu shì* (Acting Prefect of Jízhōu, Jiāngxī). The *Jiāngxī tōngzhì* does not record his career and the Sìkù editors note that his further career-record cannot be investigated. Native place uncertain. Compiler of the *Shì shí lèi yuàn* 事實類苑 ([[KR3j0181]]) in 63 *juàn*, a major Northern-Sòng institutional-history *lèishū* compiled over fourteen years and completed in *Shàoxīng* 15 (1145), drawing on more than sixty source-works. Wáng Shìzhēn's *Jūyì lù* praised it as "the most comprehensive of Sòng-men's *shuōbù*, useful for history."
