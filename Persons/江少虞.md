Jiāng Shào-yú 江少虞 (*fl. Shào-xīng*, c. 1131–1162). The catalog meta and Sìkù tiyao record him as *Zuǒ cháo-qǐng dà-fū*, *quán fā-qiǎn Jí-zhōu jūn-zhōu shì* (Acting Prefect of Jí-zhōu, Jiāng-xī). The *Jiāng-xī tōng-zhì* does not record his career and the Sìkù editors note that his further career-record cannot be investigated. Native place uncertain. Compiler of the *Shì shí lèi yuàn* 事實類苑 ([[KR3j0181]]) in 63 *juàn*, a major Northern-Sòng institutional-history *lèi-shū* compiled over fourteen years and completed in *Shào-xīng* 15 (1145), drawing on more than sixty source-works. Wáng Shìzhēn's *Jū-yì lù* praised it as "the most comprehensive of Sòng-men's *shuō-bù*, useful for history."
