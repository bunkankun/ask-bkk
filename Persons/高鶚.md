Gāo È 高鶚 (courtesy name Láshǔ 蘭墅, also known as Yúnshì 雲士; c. 1758–c. 1814/1815) was a Qīng-dynasty official and man of letters from a Hànjūn bannerman (*Hànjūn qírén* 漢軍旗人) family. He passed the *jìnshì* 進士 examination in Qiánlóng 53 (1788) and served in various official posts including the Board of Revenue.

Gāo È is chiefly known for his role in completing and publishing *Hónglóu Mèng* 紅樓夢. Together with Chéng Wěiyuán 程偉元 (the printer and publisher), he produced the first printed 120-chapter edition of the novel in 1792 (Qiánlóng 57, the so-called *Chéng-Jiǎ* 程甲 edition, followed shortly by the *Chéng-Yǐ* 程乙 edition). The preface claims they assembled fragmentary manuscripts for the final 40 chapters; whether Gāo È composed, edited, or collated these chapters remains the most contested question in *Hóngxué* 紅學 scholarship.

CBDB id 87277 records his birth year as 1758 (乾隆 23), with the entry noting scholarly disagreement about the exact birth year (proposals ranging from Qiánlóng 3 to Qiánlóng 28) and death year (various sources give 1814 or 1815; see Zhang Shucai 張書才, "Gāo È shēngzú nián kǎoshí," *Wénxiàn* 1989.4).
