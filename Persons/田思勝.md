**Tián Sīshèng** 田思勝, modern PRC editor; chief editor of the *Féng Zhàozhāng yīxué quánshū* 馮兆張醫學全書 (Beijing: Zhōngguó Zhōngyīyào chūbǎnshè, 1999), a comprehensive modern edition of the works of [[馮兆張]] Féng Zhàozhāng.
