Lǐ Xuéyù 李學裕, Kāngxī-period pupil of [[胡煦]] Hú Xù (1655–1736). Took the manuscript of Hú's monumental 99-juàn *Zhōuyì hán shū* away for editing but died before completion; the manuscript was thereby scattered and lost. The surviving 18-juàn *Zhōuyì hán shū yuē cún* ([[KR1a0145]]) was reconstructed by Hú's son [[胡季堂]] Hú Jìtáng from extracted essential material.
