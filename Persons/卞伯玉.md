Liú Sòng-period (5th c.) *Yì jīng* exegete. Author of a now-lost *Zhōu Yì* commentary, surviving only in fragments preserved in [[陸德明]] Lù Démíng's *Jīng diǎn shì wén* 經典釋文 and quoted in connection with the readings of [[明僧紹]] Míng Sēngshào and [[桓玄]] Huán Xuán. Brief biographical notice in *Sòng shū* 宋書 56 / *Nán shǐ* 南史 21. No CBDB record at this date.
