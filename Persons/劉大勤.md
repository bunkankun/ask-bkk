Early-Qīng disciple of Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]]; precise birth and death dates are not recorded in the standard biographical sources and no CBDB record can be confidently matched. He is known almost exclusively as the compiler of the *Shī-yǒu shī-zhuàn xù lù* 師友詩傳續錄 (the second *juǎn* of [[KR4i0059]]), recording his exchanges of poetic questions and answers with Wáng Shì-zhēn alone (where Láng Tíng-huái [[郎廷槐]]'s first *juǎn* had questioned three masters of the Shāndōng circle, Liú's continuation is restricted to Wáng). His questions cover prosody (the *qīng-zhuó* and *yì-yáng* distinctions), the technique of five- and seven-syllable old-style verse, and Wáng's *shén-yùn* programme as expressed in the *Táng-xián sān-mèi jí* 唐賢三昧集.
