Northern- or early-Southern-Sòng scholar-official, titled *jìshì zhōng* 給事中 ("supervising secretary"). Author of [[KR5a0117|Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註]] (DZ 117), an eight-folio *nèidān*-oriented commentary on the *Yīnfú jīng*. Cited in *Tōngzhì* "Yìwén lüè" 5.4b.
