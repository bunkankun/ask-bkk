Chéngrú 成如, *zì* **Zǐyōng** 子雍 ("Childlike Harmony"); often referred to as **Zǐyōng Chéngrú** 子雍成如 or **Bìxiá Zǐyōng** 碧霞子雍 / **Yǒngqìng Zǐyōng** 永慶子雍 from her abbacies. **Female** Chán master (*nǐ* 尼) — one of the few female-authored *yǔlù* in the Jiāxīng Canon, alongside [[KR6q0428]] (Xínggāng), [[KR6q0552]] (Chāokē), [[KR6q0553]] (Chāochēn). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔlǜ Fàn** 古律範禪師. Born **順治五年** = **1648/49** Gregorian (per the *bǐngzǐ* 1696 verse in juan 1: "*sìshí jiǔ nián chūn*" 四十九年春 = age 49 in 1696, hence b. ~1648); death year unknown. Native of **Jīngménzhōu** 荊門州 (Húběi); lay surname **Zhōu** 周.

**Formation.** Reserved in speech and laughter from childhood. Took tonsure under **Bǔrén** 補仁和尚. Practiced *cānjiū* over 20 years, visiting many famous teachers; finally received *yìnzhèng* (sealing) from **Gǔlǜ** 古律.

**Abbacies (three).** **Yǒngān** 永安, **Hóngēn** 洪恩, **Yǒngqìng** 永慶 (the principal seat — possibly also called **Yǒngshòu** 永壽 in the *yǔlù* serial subtitle).

**Disciple / recorder.** **Zǔyuán** [[祖圓]] (recorder), **Fózhèng** 佛證 (*fǎsì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0578]] 《子雍如禪師語錄》 4 juan, J39 B465, recorded by **Zǔyuán** [[祖圓]] *jìlù*.

**Sources.** 《子雍如禪師語錄》 vol. 4 〈行實〉.
