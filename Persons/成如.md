Chéng-rú 成如, *zì* **Zǐ-yōng** 子雍 ("Childlike Harmony"); often referred to as **Zǐ-yōng Chéng-rú** 子雍成如 or **Bì-xiá Zǐ-yōng** 碧霞子雍 / **Yǒng-qìng Zǐ-yōng** 永慶子雍 from her abbacies. **Female** Chán master (*nǐ* 尼) — one of the few female-authored *yǔ-lù* in the Jiā-xīng Canon, alongside [[KR6q0428]] (Xíng-gāng), [[KR6q0552]] (Chāo-kē), [[KR6q0553]] (Chāo-chēn). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔ-lǜ Fàn** 古律範禪師. Born **順治五年** = **1648/49** Gregorian (per the *bǐng-zǐ* 1696 verse in juan 1: "*sì-shí jiǔ nián chūn*" 四十九年春 = age 49 in 1696, hence b. ~1648); death year unknown. Native of **Jīng-mén-zhōu** 荊門州 (Húběi); lay surname **Zhōu** 周.

**Formation.** Reserved in speech and laughter from childhood. Took tonsure under **Bǔ-rén** 補仁和尚. Practiced *cān-jiū* over 20 years, visiting many famous teachers; finally received *yìn-zhèng* (sealing) from **Gǔ-lǜ** 古律.

**Abbacies (three).** **Yǒng-ān** 永安, **Hóng-ēn** 洪恩, **Yǒng-qìng** 永慶 (the principal seat — possibly also called **Yǒng-shòu** 永壽 in the *yǔ-lù* serial subtitle).

**Disciple / recorder.** **Zǔ-yuán** [[祖圓]] (recorder), **Fó-zhèng** 佛證 (*fǎ-sì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0578]] 《子雍如禪師語錄》 4 juan, J39 B465, recorded by **Zǔ-yuán** [[祖圓]] *jì-lù*.

**Sources.** 《子雍如禪師語錄》 vol. 4 〈行實〉.
