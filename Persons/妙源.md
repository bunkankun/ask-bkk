# Miàoyuán 妙源

Late-Southern-Sòng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán monk, dharma-student (*cānxué* 參學) of [[智愚]] Xūtáng Zhìyú (1185–1269). Exact lifedates not transmitted. Lead editor of the ten-juan *Xūtáng héshàng yǔlù* 虛堂和尚語錄 ([[KR6q0065]]), compiled together with a collective of twenty-five further Xūtáng disciples named in the headings of the *Xuzangjing* recension (X1380): Kěxuān 可宣, Wúyǐn 無隱, Fànyuè 梵閱, Déyì 德溢, Wéijùn 惟俊, Fǎyún 法雲, Jìngtán 淨覃, and so on. Mentioned in connection with the *yǔlù* at the opening of each juan as "*cānxué Miàoyuán biān*" 參學妙源編. No independent biographical record.

Not to be confused with A015350 寶葉妙源 Bǎoyè Miàoyuán / 寶業道源 Bǎoyè Dàoyuán, a different Xūtáng dharma-heir and abbot.
