# Fóyǎn Qīngyuǎn 佛眼清遠 (1067–1120)

Northern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, one of the so-called "three Buddhas" (sān fó 三佛) — disciples of [[五祖法演]] Wǔzǔ Fǎyǎn (d. 1104) along with Yuánwù Kèqín 圜悟克勤 and Tàipíng Huìqín 太平慧懃. Native of Línqióng 臨邛 in Sìchuān, lay surname Lǐ 李. Recorded *yǔlù* preserved in the canon. To be distinguished from the southern-Sòng Huáyán scholar [[清遠]] Qīngyuǎn ([[KR6i0561]]).
