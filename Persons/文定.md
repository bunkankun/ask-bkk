# Wéndìng 文定

Míng-period Buddhist monk who signed the Chénghuà 5 (1469) reprint preface (*Chóngkè Zhēn xīn zhí shuō xù*) to the Chinul-attributed *Zhēn xīn zhí shuō* ([[KR6q0095]]).
