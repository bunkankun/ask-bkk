Mid- to late-Qiánlóng Manchu official; of the Ihirgen Gioro 伊爾根覺羅 clan. Served as Liǎng-Jiāng Governor-General (1779–1786, succeeding Gāo Jìn 高晉) and as director of the river-conservancy program in his Liǎng-Jiāng tenure. Continued the editorial work on the *Qīndìng Nánxún Shèngdiǎn* 欽定南巡盛典 ([[KR2m0039]]) after Gāo Jìn's death and into Qiánlóng's fifth and sixth southern tours. Died in office in 1786. Birth-year not transmitted.
