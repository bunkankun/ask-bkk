Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* transmitter, *zì* Tǐngzhī 挺之. A pupil of Mù Xiū [[穆修]] and the immediate teacher of Shào Yōng [[邵雍]]. He served as a county-level official under Rénzōng and is principally remembered for transmitting the *Xiāntiān tú* 先天圖 ("Prior Heaven Chart") and the *guà biàn* 卦變 ("hexagram-transformation") teachings of the Chén Tuán [[陳摶]] line to Shào Yōng. Lǐ's role is canonized in Zhū Zhèn [[朱震]]'s *Hànshàng Yìzhuàn biǎo*, which gives the lineage: Chén Tuán → Mù Xiū → Lǐ Zhīcái → Shào Yōng.
