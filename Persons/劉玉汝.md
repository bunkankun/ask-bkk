# Liú Yùrǔ 劉玉汝

*Zì* Chéngzhī 成之. Native of Lúlíng 廬陵 (modern Jí'ān, Jiāngxī). Lifedates not recoverable; CBDB has no confirmed entry. The catalog meta gives fl. 1341–1373.

The Sìkù editors trace his identity through Zhōu Tíngzhèn 周霆震's *Shíchū jí* 石初集: Liú Yùrǔ wrote a preface to the *Shíchū jí*, dated *Hàn Wǔ guǐchǒu* (per the editors' citation, though the era-name *Hàn Wǔ* here is irregular and probably reflects a Yuán-Míng-transition usage); since the *Shíchū jí* circulated into the early Míng, Liú Yùrǔ was still alive at the start of the Míng. He had passed the *xiānggòng jìnshì* but never held national-level office.

Author of *Shī zuǎn xù* 詩纘緒 in 18 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0032]]) — a Zhū-Xī-school sub-commentary on the *Shī jí zhuàn*, recovered by the Sìkù editors from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. The work is unrecorded in any YuánMíng bibliography; transmission was apparently lost between the early Míng and the *Yǒnglè* compilation, and revived only by Sìkù editorial intervention.
