Sēngqiéluóchā 僧伽羅叉 (\*Saṅgharakṣa, "Saṅgha-protected"), Indian Sarvāstivāda *yogācāra* master of Gandhāra. He is the author of the *Yogācārabhūmi* whose Chinese rendering survives as [[KR6i0243|*Xiūxíng dàodì jīng* 修行道地經 (T606)]] translated by [[竺法護]] in the Western Jin. Sēngruì's preface to [[KR6i0251|*Zuòchán sānmèi jīng* (T614)]] lists him among the Sarvāstivāda meditation masters whose *yoga* writings were drawn upon by [[鳩摩羅什]]. No biographical material survives.
