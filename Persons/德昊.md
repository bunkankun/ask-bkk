Déhào 德昊, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-disciple of **Hánsōng Zhìcāo** [[智操]] 寒松智操 (1626–1688) in the Bǎi-yú-line. Lead compiler of the 20-juan 《寒松操禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0517]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
