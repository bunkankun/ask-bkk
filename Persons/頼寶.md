**Raihō** 頼寶 / 賴寶 (1279–1330) — Late-Kamakura Japanese **Shingon** 真言 scholar-monk of **Mount Kōya** 高野山 and the principal late-Kamakura systematiser of the Shingi-Shingon doctrinal-encyclopaedic tradition. He represents the third generation of Shingi scholarship after [[覺鑁]] (Kakuban, 1095–1144) and the early-Kamakura post-Kakuban scholastic generation.

His principal surviving canonical works are three substantial Shingon doctrinal compilations preserved in the Taishō:

- **Shì mó-hē-yǎn lùn kān-zhù** 釋摩訶衍論勘注 ([[KR6o0097]], T69n2290) — a twenty-four-fascicle critical-annotation compendium on Nāgārjuna's *Shì mó-hē-yǎn lùn* [[KR6o0084]]: the largest single commentary on this *Awakening of Faith*-related text and the standard Shingi reference for its interpretation.
- **Zhū-fǎ fēn-bié chāo** 諸法分別抄 (T2448) — comprehensive Shingon doctrinal-analytical compendium.
- **Zhēnyán míngmù** 真言名目 (T2449) — Shingon technical-terminology lexicon.

His *Shì mó-hē-yǎn lùn kān-zhù* at 24 fascicles is the most extensive medieval Japanese engagement with the *Shakuron* and the principal documentary witness to the late-Kamakura Shingi-Shingon scholastic synthesis on Nāgārjuna's text.
