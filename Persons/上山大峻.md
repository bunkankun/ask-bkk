Japanese specialist of Tibetan-period Dūnhuáng Buddhism (b. 1936), professor at Ryūkoku University 龍谷大學. His principal monograph *Tonkō bukkyō no kenkyū* 敦煌仏教の研究 (Kyōto: Hōzōkan, 1990) is the standard treatment of the cross-cultural Sino-Tibetan Buddhist culture of Dūnhuáng during the Tibetan-imperial occupation (786–848). Co-editor (with [[袁德領]]) of *Púsà zǒngchí fǎ* ([[KR6v0030]]) — the *Nánzōng* Chán treatise from the Tibetan-period Pelliot 3777 scroll.
