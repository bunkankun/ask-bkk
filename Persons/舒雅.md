Shū Yǎ 舒雅 (d. 1009; CBDB id 49210), *zì* Zǐ-zhèng 子正, originally a Southern-Táng official, entered Northern-Sòng service after the 975 conquest. *Jìnshì* of the Southern Táng. At the time of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* commission he held *Guó-zǐ jiān chéng* 國子監丞 and was one of the fourteen named compilers. He was also one of the compilers of the *Wén-yuàn yīng-huá*. His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 441 (in the *Wén-yuàn* section).
