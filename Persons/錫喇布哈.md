Xī-lǎ-bù-hā 錫喇布哈 (Mong. *Siribuqa*), *zì* Yuán-pǔ 元普, *jìnshì* of Tài-dìng 4 (1327). Sè-mù scholar and disciple of Jiē Xī-sī [[揭傒斯]]; compiler of the *Wén-ān jí* [[KR4d0497]], his teacher's collected works. The form **錫喇布哈** is the Qiánlóng-era *Sìkù-guǎn* sinicization of the original Yuán-period transcription **燮理普化** Xiè-lǐ pǔ-huà (爕理普化), regularized as part of the systematic 1781 re-romanization of Mongol and Mongolian-period non-Hàn personal names; the *Sìkù tíyào* explicitly records the alteration ("錫喇布哈 原作 爕理普化 今改正"). The fourteenth-century *jìnshì* lists and Jiē Xī-sī's own *Sòng Xī Yuán-pǔ xù* 送錫元普序 (preserved as *juǎn* 9 of the *Wén-ān jí*) confirm the pre-Qiánlóng form. The Qiánlóng sinicization is followed here for consistency with the catalog meta but is recognized as a post-fact editorial replacement.
