# Shōen 照遠

Medieval Japanese Risshū (律宗) Vinaya scholar (likely 14th century). Compiler of the twenty-eight-fascicle *Zīxíng chāo* 資行鈔 ([[KR6k0130]]), an extensive sub-commentary on the combined Dàoxuān + Yuánzhào *Sìfēn lǜ xíngshìchāo* + *Zīchíjì* framework, preserved in the Japanese Daizōkyō supplement.
