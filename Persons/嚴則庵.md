**Yán Zé'ān** 嚴則庵 (*fl.* late Qīng, 19th c., 清), minor Qīng physician. Author of the *Shānghán jié jué* 傷寒捷訣 ([[KR3ef035]], 1 juan) and a *Yī xué qiǎn shuō* 醫學淺說. His lifedates and biographical details are not preserved in the standard reference works. No CBDB record.
