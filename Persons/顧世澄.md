**Gù Shìchéng** 顧世澄 (also Gù Chéng 顧澄; *zì* Liàn-jiāng 練江; *hào* Jìng-zhāi 靜齋, *fl.* Qián-lóng, 清), third-generation hereditary physician of Wú-hú 蕪湖 (Ān-huī), practising in Yáng-zhōu 揚州. Heir to a three-generation medical family (grandfather Níng-huá 寧華 and father Qīng-yán 青岩 were physicians). His magnum opus is the forty-*juǎn* [[KR3ek013]] *Yáng-yī dà-quán* 瘍醫大全 — completed in Qián-lóng 25 (1760), with additional prefaces in 1762 and 1773 (the printing date) — the largest single-author illustrated Chinese surgical compendium and the standard Qīng *wài-kē* desk reference into the Republican period. The work was conceived as a *wài-kē* counterpart to the imperial [[KR3ek009]] *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* (1742) and was financially sponsored by fellow Huái-region physicians. Gù speaks of forty-plus years of practice in his 1760 preface, suggesting birth in the early 18th century; no firm lifedates documented. Not in CBDB.
