# Yǔ Jìcái 庾季才

Native of Xīn-yě 新野 (Hé-nán). Active under the Northern Zhōu and early Sui; flourished c. 540–600. Birth and death years not securely recorded.

The principal Northern-Zhōu / early-Sui imperial astronomer-and-astrologer. Held the office of *Tài-shǐ Zhōng dà-fū* 太史中大夫 (Senior Grand Master of the Bureau of Astronomy) under the Northern Zhōu. The Suí-shū *Jīng-jí zhì* records his works in 115 *juàn*; the *Zhōu-shū* (Northern-Zhōu History) lists 110 *juàn*. The principal surviving work is the *Líng-tái mì-yuàn* 靈臺祕苑 ([[KR3g0017]]), originally in 110-or-115 *juàn* but reduced to 15 *juàn* by Northern-Sòng imperial-decree-revision under Wáng Ānlǐ 王安禮 (Wáng Ānshí's brother) and the Bureau of Astronomy staff.

The work is a major imperial-court astronomical-and-divinatory compendium: stellar identifications and computations (*bù-tiān gē* and diagrams), region-allotment (*fēn-yě tǔ-guī* — astrological-geographical correspondences), wind-thunder-cloud-pneumatic divination, and topical divination by sun, moon, planets, and the three-walls / 28-mansions. Through the Northern-Sòng revision, the work became one of the principal late-imperial divinatory-astronomical references.
