# Huìān 慧安

Sōngshān Huìān 嵩山慧安 (c. 582–709), also called Lǎo'ān 老安 ("Old An"). Northern-School Chán master, dharma-heir of [[弘忍|Hóngrěn]]. Active on Sōngshān 嵩山 and at the Táng court. Teacher of the lay practitioner [[慧光|Huìguāng]] (Dàzhào jūshì 大照居士) and a notable second-generation figure in the East-Mountain (Dōngshān) tradition.
