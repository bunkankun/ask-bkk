# Běnjì 本寂

Cáoshān Běnjì 曹山本寂 (840–901), late-Táng Chán master, principal dharma-heir of Dòngshān Liángjiè; the figure after whom the "Cáo" of Cáodòng 曹洞 is named. Codified the *wǔwèi* 五位 ("five positions") doctrinal system that became the signature apparatus of the Cáodòng school; his recorded sayings survive in the parallel recensions [[KR6q0068]] and [[KR6q0069]].
