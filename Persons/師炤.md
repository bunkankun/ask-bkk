# Shīzhào 師炤

Late-17th-century Qīng Buddhist nun (*bǐqiūní* 比丘尼); senior disciple of [[符|Língruì Fúānzhǔ]] 靈瑞符菴主 (DILA catalog-name Fú 符 nun) in the Hóngchú teaching-community at Sūzhōu / Wújùn. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Lead compiler-editor (*děng jì* 等記 "et al. recorded") of Fú-nun's *Língruì ní Zǔkuí Fú chánshī Miàozhàn lù* 靈瑞尼祖揆符禪師妙湛錄 [[KR6q0224]] — Fú-nun's five-juan solo *yǔlù*. Shīzhào's position as principal compiler indicates her status as Fú-nun's senior dharma-successor.

Discussed in Grant, Beata. 2008. *Eminent Nuns: Women Chan Masters of Seventeenth-Century China*.
