# Shī-zhào 師炤

Late-17th-century Qīng Buddhist nun (*bǐ-qiū-ní* 比丘尼); senior disciple of [[符|Líng-ruì Fú-ān-zhǔ]] 靈瑞符菴主 (DILA catalog-name Fú 符 nun) in the Hóng-chú teaching-community at Sū-zhōu / Wú-jùn. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Lead compiler-editor (*děng jì* 等記 "et al. recorded") of Fú-nun's *Líng-ruì ní Zǔ-kuí Fú chánshī Miào-zhàn lù* 靈瑞尼祖揆符禪師妙湛錄 [[KR6q0224]] — Fú-nun's five-juan solo *yǔlù*. Shī-zhào's position as principal compiler indicates her status as Fú-nun's senior dharma-successor.

Discussed in Grant, Beata. 2008. *Eminent Nuns: Women Chan Masters of Seventeenth-Century China*.
