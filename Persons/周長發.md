# Zhōu Chángfā 周長發

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as the *kǎozhèng* compiler for the *Liáo shǐ* ([[KR2a0033]]) in the *Sìkù quánshū*. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
