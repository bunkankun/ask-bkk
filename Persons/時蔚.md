Shíwèi 時蔚, *zì* **Wànfēng** 萬峰 ("Ten Thousand Peaks"); often referred to as **Wànfēng Shíwèi** 萬峰時蔚 or **Dèngwèi Shíwèi** 鄧尉時蔚 from his abbacy. **Yuán → early-Míng** LínjìYángqí Chán master. Born **元成宗大德七年癸卯八月廿九日午時** = **1303-10-18** Gregorian; died **明洪武十四年辛酉正月廿九日** = **1381-03-03**, age 79. Native of **Lèqīng** 樂清 (Zhèjiāng); lay surname **Jīn** 金.

**Formation.** At 13 took tonsure under **Shēngjiǎngzhǔ** 昇講主 at **Yǎnqìngsì** 演慶寺. At 16 became a fully-ordained monk; at 19 took full precepts. Visited **Hǔpǎo Zhǐyán** 虎跑止巖, given the *sān bù shì* 三不是 *gōngàn*. Returned to **Míngzhōu Dápéng Fójìshān** 明州達蓬佛跡山 for solitary practice. Later met **Qiānyán Yuáncháng** 千巖元長 (1284–1357) and received final *yìnkě*.

**Abbacy.** **Sūzhōu Dèngwèishān Shèngēnsì** 蘇州鄧蔚山聖恩寺 (= 鄧尉, in late-Yuán/early-Míng — opened *fǎ* there for thirty years, the famous "Wànfēng" mountain).

**Disciples.** Major *fǎsì* include **Wúniàn Shèngxué** 無念勝學, **Bǎocáng Pǔchí** 寶藏普持, **Hǎizhōu Pǔcí** 海舟普慈.

**Work.** [[KR6q0598]] 《萬峰和尚語錄》 1 juan, J40 B492, compiled by *fǎsì* **Pǔshòu** [[普壽]] *jí*. Despite the catalog dynasty 明, the master is more accurately described as a Yuán-Míng-transition figure.

**Sources.** 《祖燈大統》 vol. 88; 《雲外錄》 vol. 12; 《補續高僧傳》 vol. 15; 《佛光大辭典》 p. 4124; 《道影》 vol. 2.
