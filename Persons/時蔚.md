Shí-wèi 時蔚, *zì* **Wàn-fēng** 萬峰 ("Ten Thousand Peaks"); often referred to as **Wàn-fēng Shí-wèi** 萬峰時蔚 or **Dèng-wèi Shí-wèi** 鄧尉時蔚 from his abbacy. **Yuán → early-Míng** Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. Born **元成宗大德七年癸卯八月廿九日午時** = **1303-10-18** Gregorian; died **明洪武十四年辛酉正月廿九日** = **1381-03-03**, age 79. Native of **Lè-qīng** 樂清 (Zhèjiāng); lay surname **Jīn** 金.

**Formation.** At 13 took tonsure under **Shēng-jiǎng-zhǔ** 昇講主 at **Yǎn-qìng-sì** 演慶寺. At 16 became a fully-ordained monk; at 19 took full precepts. Visited **Hǔ-pǎo Zhǐ-yán** 虎跑止巖, given the *sān bù shì* 三不是 *gōng-àn*. Returned to **Míng-zhōu Dá-péng Fó-jì-shān** 明州達蓬佛跡山 for solitary practice. Later met **Qiān-yán Yuán-cháng** 千巖元長 (1284–1357) and received final *yìn-kě*.

**Abbacy.** **Sū-zhōu Dèng-wèi-shān Shèng-ēn-sì** 蘇州鄧蔚山聖恩寺 (= 鄧尉, in late-Yuán/early-Míng — opened *fǎ* there for thirty years, the famous "Wàn-fēng" mountain).

**Disciples.** Major *fǎ-sì* include **Wú-niàn Shèng-xué** 無念勝學, **Bǎo-cáng Pǔ-chí** 寶藏普持, **Hǎi-zhōu Pǔ-cí** 海舟普慈.

**Work.** [[KR6q0598]] 《萬峰和尚語錄》 1 juan, J40 B492, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Pǔ-shòu** [[普壽]] *jí*. Despite the catalog dynasty 明, the master is more accurately described as a Yuán-Míng-transition figure.

**Sources.** 《祖燈大統》 vol. 88; 《雲外錄》 vol. 12; 《補續高僧傳》 vol. 15; 《佛光大辭典》 p. 4124; 《道影》 vol. 2.
