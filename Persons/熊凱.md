Xióng Kǎi 熊凱, *zì* Shùnfū 舜夫, of Nánchāng 南昌, known in his lifetime as Yáoxī xiānshēng 遥溪先生. A Yuán-period *míngjīng* private-school teacher who instructed in the Classics for some forty years; teacher of his fellow townsman [[熊良輔]] Xióng Liángfǔ (the author of *Zhōuyì běnyì jíchéng* [[KR1a0079]]). Author of an *Yì zhuàn jí shū* 易傳集疏, no longer extant; this work was confused by Huáng Yújì 黃虞稷's *Qiānqǐngtáng shūmù* with Xióng Liángfǔ's *Jíchéng*, an attribution corrected by the Sìkù editors. Lifedates not securely recorded.
