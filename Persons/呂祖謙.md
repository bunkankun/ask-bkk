Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙 (1137–1181), *zì* Bó-gōng 伯恭, *hào* Dōng-lái 東萊, posthumous title *Chéng-gōng* 成公. Of Wùzhōu 婺州 / Jīnhuá 金華 (modern Jīnhuá, Zhèjiāng). One of the *Dōng-nán sān xiānshēng* 東南三先生 ("Three Masters of the Southeast") with Zhū Xī 朱熹 (1130–1200) and [[張栻]] (Zhāng Shì, 1133–1180).

Descendant of the great Northern-Sòng Lǚ-clan scholarly lineage (his great-great-grandfather was the chief minister Lǚ Yíjiǎn 呂夷簡; his great-grandfather Lǚ Gōngzhù 呂公著; collateral kinsman Lǚ Bénzhōng 呂本中 of the *Tóng-méng xùn* 童蒙訓 fame). *Jìnshì* of *Lóng-xìng* 1 (1163), and successful in the same year's *Bó-xué hóng-cí* 博學宏詞 (Polymath-and-Versatile-Composition) examination — an unusual double-success.

Founded the Wùzhōu Lì-zhái Academy 麗澤書院 with his brother Lǚ Zǔjiǎn 呂祖儉; this was the venue of the famous *É-hú zhī-huì* 鵝湖之會 (Goose-Lake Convocation, 1175) which he convened to mediate between Zhū Xī's *Dào-xué* circle and Lù Jiǔyuān's emerging *xīn-xué* circle. Held *Tài-xué bóshì* 太學博士 (Imperial Academy Professor); *Bì-shū-shěng zhèng-zì* 秘書省正字 (Imperial Library Orthographer); editorial work on the imperial *Huì-yào* 會要 compilation.

Death in *Chún-xī* 8 (1181), aged 45 *suì* (44 Western years), preceded the *Qìng-yuán dǎng-jìn* of 1196 — sparing him the proscriptive reaction that fell on Zhū Xī's later disciples.

CBDB id 7055 corroborates lifedates 1137–1181. *Sòngshǐ* biography in juan 434.

His scholarly project was distinctive within the *Dào-xué* mainstream for integrating *jīng-shǐ-zhī-xué* 經史之學 (Classics-and-history scholarship) with *Dào-xué* doctrine — methodologically prefiguring the Yǒngjiā / Yǒngkāng schools' historical-realist *jīng-shì* 經世 program. Compiled extensively across genres: the *Dōng-lái Bó-yì* 東萊博議 (essays on the *Zuǒ zhuàn*); *Lì-dài zhì-dù xiáng-shuō* 歷代制度詳說; the *Sòng wén-jiàn* 宋文鑑 anthology; the *Jìn-sī lù* 近思錄 (with Zhū Xī).

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the editor of [[KR1a0043]] *Gǔ Zhōuyì* 古周易 — the *gǔ-yì* (ancient-*Yì*) recension that became the textual base for Zhū Xī's *Zhōuyì běn-yì* and thereby the operative *Yì*-text-form for the entire post-Sòng tradition.
