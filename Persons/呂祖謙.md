Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙 (1137–1181), *zì* Bógōng 伯恭, *hào* Dōnglái 東萊, posthumous title *Chénggōng* 成公. Of Wùzhōu 婺州 / Jīnhuá 金華 (modern Jīnhuá, Zhèjiāng). One of the *Dōngnán sān xiānshēng* 東南三先生 ("Three Masters of the Southeast") with Zhū Xī 朱熹 (1130–1200) and [[張栻]] (Zhāng Shì, 1133–1180).

Descendant of the great Northern-Sòng Lǚ-clan scholarly lineage (his great-great-grandfather was the chief minister Lǚ Yíjiǎn 呂夷簡; his great-grandfather Lǚ Gōngzhù 呂公著; collateral kinsman Lǚ Bénzhōng 呂本中 of the *Tóngméng xùn* 童蒙訓 fame). *Jìnshì* of *Lóngxìng* 1 (1163), and successful in the same year's *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學宏詞 (Polymath-and-Versatile-Composition) examination — an unusual double-success.

Founded the Wùzhōu Lìzhái Academy 麗澤書院 with his brother Lǚ Zǔjiǎn 呂祖儉; this was the venue of the famous *Éhú zhīhuì* 鵝湖之會 (Goose-Lake Convocation, 1175) which he convened to mediate between Zhū Xī's *Dàoxué* circle and Lù Jiǔyuān's emerging *xīnxué* circle. Held *Tàixué bóshì* 太學博士 (Imperial Academy Professor); *Bìshūshěng zhèngzì* 秘書省正字 (Imperial Library Orthographer); editorial work on the imperial *Huìyào* 會要 compilation.

Death in *Chúnxī* 8 (1181), aged 45 *suì* (44 Western years), preceded the *Qìngyuán dǎngjìn* of 1196 — sparing him the proscriptive reaction that fell on Zhū Xī's later disciples.

CBDB id 7055 corroborates lifedates 1137–1181. *Sòngshǐ* biography in juan 434.

His scholarly project was distinctive within the *Dàoxué* mainstream for integrating *jīngshǐzhīxué* 經史之學 (Classics-and-history scholarship) with *Dàoxué* doctrine — methodologically prefiguring the Yǒngjiā / Yǒngkāng schools' historical-realist *jīngshì* 經世 program. Compiled extensively across genres: the *Dōnglái Bóyì* 東萊博議 (essays on the *Zuǒ zhuàn*); *Lìdài zhìdù xiángshuō* 歷代制度詳說; the *Sòng wénjiàn* 宋文鑑 anthology; the *Jìnsī lù* 近思錄 (with Zhū Xī).

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the editor of [[KR1a0043]] *Gǔ Zhōuyì* 古周易 — the *gǔyì* (ancient-*Yì*) recension that became the textual base for Zhū Xī's *Zhōuyì běnyì* and thereby the operative *Yì*-text-form for the entire post-Sòng tradition.
