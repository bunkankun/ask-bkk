**Shí Kǎi** 石楷 of **Dōnghǎi** 東海 (a Liányúngǎng 連雲港 / Hǎizhōu 海州 prefectural-name), early-Qīng *xuézhě* / physician *fl.* late seventeenth century, principal Kāng-xī-era editor-publisher of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jǐngyuè's **[[KR3eq016]]** *Zhìyí lù* 質疑錄 (Kāngxī 26 *dīngmǎo* = 1687 cut). His preface to the *Zhìyí lù* indicates his self-positioning as a moderate Jǐng-yuè-school inheritor who valued Zhāng's late self-revisions as authoritative for the "warming-tonifying" tradition's mature self-criticism. He further notes a project to print other unpublished Jǐngyuè manuscripts (*Zázhèngmó*, *Shānghán diǎn*, *Fùrén guī*, *Chuánzhōng lù*, *Běncǎo lèikǎo*), but the eventual *Jǐngyuè quánshū* recension supplanted this programme. Not in CBDB.
