**Shí Kǎi** 石楷 of **Dōng-hǎi** 東海 (a Lián-yún-gǎng 連雲港 / Hǎi-zhōu 海州 prefectural-name), early-Qīng *xué-zhě* / physician *fl.* late seventeenth century, principal Kāng-xī-era editor-publisher of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jǐng-yuè's **[[KR3eq016]]** *Zhì-yí lù* 質疑錄 (Kāng-xī 26 *dīng-mǎo* = 1687 cut). His preface to the *Zhì-yí lù* indicates his self-positioning as a moderate Jǐng-yuè-school inheritor who valued Zhāng's late self-revisions as authoritative for the "warming-tonifying" tradition's mature self-criticism. He further notes a project to print other unpublished Jǐng-yuè manuscripts (*Zá-zhèng-mó*, *Shāng-hán diǎn*, *Fù-rén guī*, *Chuán-zhōng lù*, *Běn-cǎo lèi-kǎo*), but the eventual *Jǐng-yuè quán-shū* recension supplanted this programme. Not in CBDB.
