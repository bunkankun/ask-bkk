Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Sìmíng** 四明 (with full name **Sìmíng Rújí** 四明如吉, also referenced as **Yīnguǒ Rújí** 因果如吉). DILA Authority A000282. Lifedates unrecorded; affiliated with the Sìmíng *shānjiā* tradition.

Compiler of the **Chóngbiān Tiāntái zhūwén lèijí** 重編天台諸文類集 ([[KR6d0228]], X57n0952, 1 *juan*) — a re-compiled topical anthology of Tiāntái-tradition documents.

Source: DILA A000282.
