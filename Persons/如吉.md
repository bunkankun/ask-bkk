Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Sì-míng** 四明 (with full name **Sì-míng Rú-jí** 四明如吉, also referenced as **Yīn-guǒ Rú-jí** 因果如吉). DILA Authority A000282. Lifedates unrecorded; affiliated with the Sì-míng *shān-jiā* tradition.

Compiler of the **Chóng-biān Tiāntái zhū-wén lèi-jí** 重編天台諸文類集 ([[KR6d0228]], X57n0952, 1 *juan*) — a re-compiled topical anthology of Tiāntái-tradition documents.

Source: DILA A000282.
