**Qiú** 球 (full surname unrecovered, *hào* Gōu-Wú būrén 勾吳逋人 "the recluse of Gōu-Wú", fl. mid-Qián-lóng era), Qīng-period physician-editor of the *Zhōu Shènzhāi yíshū* 周慎齋遺書 ([[KR3er004]]). Qiú's editor's preface, signed *Gōu-Wú būrén shū yú Xuéyì cǎolú* 勾吳逋人書於學易草廬 ("the recluse of Gōu-Wú writes in his Studio of Studying the *Yì*"), is dated *yǐyǒu shēnyuè* 乙酉申月 (most plausibly the seventh month of 1765 = *Qiánlóng* 30, on the basis of internal consistency with the Wáng Zhuóyá / Zhào Shùyuán publication chronology of 1774). Qiú reports that he began medical study at fourteen, devoted nearly thirty years to clinical practice, and produced commentaries on the *Yìjīng* (now lost) and the *Jīnguì yùhán jīng jiě* 金匱玉函經解 (also lost) before turning to the editorial recension of Zhōu Shènzhāi's clinical doctrine that he framed as a wing-appendix (翼) to Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Jīnguì*. Qiú's surname is not recovered from the source. The single-character given-name follows the source: "勾吳逋人名球者" — "the recluse of Gōu-Wú, named Qiú".
