Lǐ Xuánzhēn 李玄真, native of Liáodōng 遼東, was a late-eighth-century Daoist master (*dàoshì* 道士) active at the Chóngxuán guàn 崇玄觀 on Lúshān 廬山 — one of the state-sponsored Daoist abbeys founded by Emperor Xuánzōng 玄宗 in the second year of Tiānbǎo (743). He styles himself *Běiyuè yóuxuán xiānshēng* 北岳游玄先生 ("Master Who Wanders in the Arcane of the Northern Peak"), a Liù-cháo and Táng honorific designating *dàoshì* who had received ordination and held the Língbǎo registers. His one surviving work, the *Shàngqīng jīnmǔ qiúxiān shàngfǎ* [[KR5b0075]] (DZ 391), was taught to his disciple Wáng Yànzhēn 王彥真 and others during a seven-year period from a *gēngwǔ* 庚午 year to a *bǐngzǐ* 丙子 year, corresponding most plausibly to 790–796 CE. No CBDB record; the lifedates are undocumented beyond the mid- to late-790s period of his activity at Lúshān.
