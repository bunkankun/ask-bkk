**Chéng Jiè** 程玠 (*hào* Sōng-yá 松厓 / 松崖, *jìn-shì* of *Chéng-huà* 11 = 1475, mid-Míng), Hī-zhōu Xīn-ān physician and *jìn-shì*-bearing scholar-official. Chéng was a hereditary Hī-zhōu physician who combined his medical practice with a brief Hàn-lín *biān-xiū* 編修 career; the unusual dual track of physician-and-official is reflected in the framing of his medical writing as *rú-yī* 儒醫 (Confucian-physician) literature. His principal work is the [[KR3er032]] *Sōng-yá yī-jìng* 松崖醫徑 (c. 1487), a compact two-*juǎn* clinical handbook framed for the apprentice physician. He also produced an *Yī-lùn jí-yào* 醫論集要.
