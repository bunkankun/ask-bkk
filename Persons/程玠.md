**Chéng Jiè** 程玠 (*hào* Sōngyá 松厓 / 松崖, *jìnshì* of *Chénghuà* 11 = 1475, mid-Míng), Hīzhōu Xīnān physician and *jìnshì*-bearing scholar-official. Chéng was a hereditary Hīzhōu physician who combined his medical practice with a brief Hànlín *biānxiū* 編修 career; the unusual dual track of physician-and-official is reflected in the framing of his medical writing as *rúyī* 儒醫 (Confucian-physician) literature. His principal work is the [[KR3er032]] *Sōngyá yījìng* 松崖醫徑 (c. 1487), a compact two-*juǎn* clinical handbook framed for the apprentice physician. He also produced an *Yīlùn jíyào* 醫論集要.
