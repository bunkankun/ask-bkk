Early Northern Sòng art historian and minor literatus active at Chéngdū 成都; *zì* Guīběn 歸本. Lǐ Tián 李畋's preface to the *Yìzhōu mínghuà lù* describes him as "Mister Huáng of Jiāngxià 江夏," but the Sìkù editors note that the "Jiāngxià" placement appears only on later editions and may be a guess on the basis of Lǐ Tián's preface; Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí* assumed him a Shǔ man on the strength of his familiarity with local affairs. Author of [[KR3h0016]] *Yìzhōu mínghuà lù* 益州名畫錄 (1006), the foundational record of Chéngdū painting and the work that established the *yì-pǐn* 逸品 as the supreme grade in Chinese painting taxonomy. Also author of the *Máotíng kèhuà* 茅亭客話, a *bǐjì* on Shǔ affairs.
