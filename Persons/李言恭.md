Mid-Qīng physician, otherwise undocumented in standard biographical sources. Sole identified work is [[KR3er123]] *Yīshī mìjí* 醫師秘笈, dated by the *Jìchéng* tradition to 1736 (composition) and *Jiāqìng* 17 / 1812 (earliest extant print).

**Not to be confused with**: the late-Míng *Wànlì*-era Lǐ Yángōng 李言恭 (1541–1599), Marquis of Línhuái 臨淮侯, literatus-administrator and author of the *Rìběn kǎo* 日本考 — a different and earlier figure of the same name, with no medical writings to his credit. The catalog attribution of [[KR3er123]] to a Qīng-era *Lǐ Yángōng* is therefore a different person sharing the same name. Disambiguation would require consultation of the *Zhōngguó zhōngyī gǔjí zǒngmù*.
