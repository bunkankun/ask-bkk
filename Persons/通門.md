# Tōngmén 通門 (Mùyún Tōngmén 牧雲通門)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Wòān 臥菴 ("Reclining Hermitage"); commonly called Gǔnánmén 古南門 or Gǔnán héshàng 古南和尚 (for the Gǔnánsì 古南寺); self-deprecating sobriquet Chùsǒu 樗叟 ("Useless-Tree Old-Man"). Lay surname Zhāng 張; native of Chángshú 常熟 (Jiāngsū). Lifedates 1599/1/27 – 1671/12/15 (Wànlì 27 – Kāngxī 10/11/15, age 73, per *Tiāntóngsì zhì* 天童寺志 juan 3).

**Note on name-variants**: The canonical DILA form is 通門. The Kanripo catalog's 通明 for [[KR6q0212]] is a transcription variant (the characters 門 and 明 are often confused in late-imperial manuscript transmission). The catalog-form is retained as an alternate-name.

Tonsured at 20 under Pòshān Dòngwén Chéng héshàng 破山洞聞乘和尚. Initial Chán-training with Bóshān 博山; received dharma-transmission from [[圓悟|Mìyún Yuánwù]] 密雲圓悟 at Jīnsù 金粟. 31st-generation Línjì descendant.

Abbacies: Sìmíng Qīzhēn 四明棲真, Jiāhé Méixī 嘉和梅溪, Pòshān 破山, Jīngjiāng Hèlín 鎮江鶴林, Tiāntóng 天童, and Xiùfēng 秀峯. Died at Gǔnán in Shíhú 石湖 seated-meditation retreat.

Dharma-heirs: ten named masters including Yúnxíng Wěi 雲行瑋, Chóngyǐ Jùn 崇已峻, and Nányīn Yán 南音言.

Works: *Qī huì yǔ lù* 七會語錄 (his formal *yǔlù* from seven successive abbacies); *Lǎnzhāi bié jí* 嬾齋別集 [[KR6q0212]] (his literary supplementary-collection). Also contributed a harmonizing-verse set to the ox-herding compilation [[KR6q0164]].

Reputation: "Good at calligraphy, skilled in poetry and prose" (*shàn shū, gōng shīwén* 善書工詩文), per the *Dào yǐng* 道影 juan 2 biographical notice.

Sources: *Tiāntóngsì zhì* 天童寺志 juan 3; *Xù zhǐ yuè lù* 續指月錄 juan 19; *Wǔ dēng quán shū* 五燈全書 juan 67.
