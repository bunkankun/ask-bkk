# Tōng-mén 通門 (Mù-yún Tōng-mén 牧雲通門)

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Wò-ān 臥菴 ("Reclining Hermitage"); commonly called Gǔ-nán-mén 古南門 or Gǔ-nán héshàng 古南和尚 (for the Gǔ-nán-sì 古南寺); self-deprecating sobriquet Chù-sǒu 樗叟 ("Useless-Tree Old-Man"). Lay surname Zhāng 張; native of Cháng-shú 常熟 (Jiāng-sū). Lifedates 1599/1/27 – 1671/12/15 (Wànlì 27 – Kāngxī 10/11/15, age 73, per *Tiān-tóng-sì zhì* 天童寺志 juan 3).

**Note on name-variants**: The canonical DILA form is 通門. The Kanripo catalog's 通明 for [[KR6q0212]] is a transcription variant (the characters 門 and 明 are often confused in late-imperial manuscript transmission). The catalog-form is retained as an alternate-name.

Tonsured at 20 under Pò-shān Dòng-wén Chéng héshàng 破山洞聞乘和尚. Initial Chán-training with Bó-shān 博山; received dharma-transmission from [[圓悟|Mìyún Yuán-wù]] 密雲圓悟 at Jīn-sù 金粟. 31st-generation Línjì descendant.

Abbacies: Sì-míng Qī-zhēn 四明棲真, Jiā-hé Méi-xī 嘉和梅溪, Pò-shān 破山, Jīng-jiāng Hè-lín 鎮江鶴林, Tiān-tóng 天童, and Xiù-fēng 秀峯. Died at Gǔ-nán in Shí-hú 石湖 seated-meditation retreat.

Dharma-heirs: ten named masters including Yún-xíng Wěi 雲行瑋, Chóng-yǐ Jùn 崇已峻, and Nán-yīn Yán 南音言.

Works: *Qī huì yǔ lù* 七會語錄 (his formal *yǔlù* from seven successive abbacies); *Lǎn-zhāi bié jí* 嬾齋別集 [[KR6q0212]] (his literary supplementary-collection). Also contributed a harmonizing-verse set to the ox-herding compilation [[KR6q0164]].

Reputation: "Good at calligraphy, skilled in poetry and prose" (*shàn shū, gōng shī-wén* 善書工詩文), per the *Dào yǐng* 道影 juan 2 biographical notice.

Sources: *Tiān-tóng-sì zhì* 天童寺志 juan 3; *Xù zhǐ yuè lù* 續指月錄 juan 19; *Wǔ dēng quán shū* 五燈全書 juan 67.
