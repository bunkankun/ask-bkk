Late-Qián-lóng / early-Jiā-qìng-era Qīng physician of *Fēngshān* 楓山 in the Hángzhōu region (Rénhé 仁和 prefecture), identified through Qián Méi's postface to the [[KR3er095]] *Yīxué sānxìn biān* 醫學三信編 as Máo's elderly neighbour-physician. He produced two documented works: the formulary *Yǎngshēng jīngyàn héjí* 養生經驗合集 (compiled earlier in his career) and the doctrinal *Yīxué sānxìn biān* (composed when over eighty, postfaced by [[錢枚]] Qián Méi who died in 1804). Máo's medical position is a Qiánlóng Chinese parallel to the contemporary Japanese *kohō-ha* return to pre-Sòng sources: a *gǔfāng* 古方 / *gǔfǎ* 古法 emphasis grounded in the *Sùwèn* and the Chángshā 長沙 [Zhòngjǐng] formulary. Not in CBDB.
