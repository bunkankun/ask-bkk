Late-Qián-lóng / early-Jiā-qìng-era Qīng physician of *Fēng-shān* 楓山 in the Háng-zhōu region (Rén-hé 仁和 prefecture), identified through Qián Méi's postface to the [[KR3er095]] *Yī-xué sān-xìn biān* 醫學三信編 as Máo's elderly neighbour-physician. He produced two documented works: the formulary *Yǎng-shēng jīng-yàn hé-jí* 養生經驗合集 (compiled earlier in his career) and the doctrinal *Yī-xué sān-xìn biān* (composed when over eighty, postfaced by [[錢枚]] Qián Méi who died in 1804). Máo's medical position is a Qián-lóng Chinese parallel to the contemporary Japanese *kohō-ha* return to pre-Sòng sources: a *gǔ-fāng* 古方 / *gǔ-fǎ* 古法 emphasis grounded in the *Sù-wèn* and the Cháng-shā 長沙 [Zhòng-jǐng] formulary. Not in CBDB.
