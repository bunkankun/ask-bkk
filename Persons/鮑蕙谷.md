# 鮑蕙谷

Bào Huìgǔ 鮑蕙谷 (mid-19th c.), nephew-in-law of [[呂慎庵]] Lǚ Shènān; transmitted a copy of Yú Zhèn's *Gǔjīn yīàn àn* ([[KR3ep071]]) to [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng in spring 1853, occasioning Wáng's selection [[KR3ep072]].
