**Zhào Lián** 趙濂 (*zì* Zhúquán 竹泉, late-Qīng, fl. late 19th c., 清), JiāngsūDāntú 丹徒 (Zhènjiāngfǔ) physician. He spent decades in clinical practice with a special interest in traumatology and *jīnchuāng* wound-medicine, and is best known for his ***Shāngkē dàchéng*** 傷科大成 ([[KR3el005]], completed *Guāngxù* 17 = 1891), one of the most systematic late-Qīng traumatology compendia. The book went through multiple lead-type reprints between 1929 and 1955 and has remained continuously in print in modern PRC reprint series. Zhào's exact birth and death years are not preserved in the standard bibliographies; the active period is inferred from the dated publications of his works. No CBDB record. He is sometimes confused with the YuánMíng official Zhào Lián 趙濂 (a different person) in older bibliographic catalogues.
