**Zhào Lián** 趙濂 (*zì* Zhú-quán 竹泉, late-Qīng, fl. late 19th c., 清), Jiāngsū-Dāntú 丹徒 (Zhèn-jiāng-fǔ) physician. He spent decades in clinical practice with a special interest in traumatology and *jīn-chuāng* wound-medicine, and is best known for his ***Shāng-kē dà-chéng*** 傷科大成 ([[KR3el005]], completed *Guāng-xù* 17 = 1891), one of the most systematic late-Qīng traumatology compendia. The book went through multiple lead-type reprints between 1929 and 1955 and has remained continuously in print in modern PRC reprint series. Zhào's exact birth and death years are not preserved in the standard bibliographies; the active period is inferred from the dated publications of his works. No CBDB record. He is sometimes confused with the Yuán-Míng official Zhào Lián 趙濂 (a different person) in older bibliographic catalogues.
