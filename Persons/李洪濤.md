Lǐ Hóngtāo 李洪濤 is a contemporary editor of Chinese medical philological texts; he produced the standard punctuated and annotated edition of the *Zábìng guǎngyào* ([[KR3eh008]]) (Beijing: Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè, 2002).
