# 李瑋西 Lǐ Wěixī

Qīng-dynasty physician cited (alongside [[高世栻]] Gāo Shìshì and [[李彣]] Lǐ Wén) in the *Yī zōng jīn jiàn* ([[KR3ef086]]) as a *Jīnguì yàoluè* commentator; mentioned in [[丹波元堅]] Tamba no Motokata's *Jīnguì yùhán yàoluè shùyì* foreword ([[KR3ef085]]).
