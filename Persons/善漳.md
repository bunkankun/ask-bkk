Early-Qīng Yogācāra scholar-monk, dharma-heir of [[性起]] Miào-dé Xìng-qǐ. Lifedates unrecorded. Otherwise unattested except as one of the disciples (along with others) who recorded the master's oral exposition of the *Bā shí guī-jǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌, producing [[KR6n0140]] *Bā shí guī-jǔ lùn-yì* 八識規矩論義 (X55n0898; "Xìng-qǐ doctrinally explicating, Shàn-zhāng et al. recording"). The function-marker *děng lù* 等錄 ("[Shàn-zhāng] and others recording") in the Kanripo meta indicates that the work is a collaborative recension of the master's lectures rather than the work of a single editor.
