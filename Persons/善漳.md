Early-Qīng Yogācāra scholar-monk, dharma-heir of [[性起]] Miàodé Xìngqǐ. Lifedates unrecorded. Otherwise unattested except as one of the disciples (along with others) who recorded the master's oral exposition of the *Bā shí guījǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌, producing [[KR6n0140]] *Bā shí guījǔ lùnyì* 八識規矩論義 (X55n0898; "Xìngqǐ doctrinally explicating, Shànzhāng et al. recording"). The function-marker *děng lù* 等錄 ("[Shànzhāng] and others recording") in the Kanripo meta indicates that the work is a collaborative recension of the master's lectures rather than the work of a single editor.
