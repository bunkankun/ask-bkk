**Zhāng Mù** 章穆 (*zì* Xìng-yún 杏雲, *fl.* late Qiánlóng to mid Dào-guāng era, c. 1770s–1830s), Qīng polymath-physician from a Jiāng-zhè township. A literati scholar of substantial classical learning (deep in *jīng-shù* 經術), with a particular interest in astronomy, calendrical reckoning, and natural history, alongside active rural medical practice — he attended even the poorest patients in person and was regarded as a "living Buddha" (*shēng-fó*) by his contemporaries. Author of the *Tiáo-jí yǐn-shí biàn* 調疾飲食辯 ([[KR3eo045]]) — published in two stages, half during his lifetime, half posthumously through an anonymous benefactor. No CBDB record.
