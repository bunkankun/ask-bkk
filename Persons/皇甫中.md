**Huáng-fǔ Zhōng** 皇甫中 (*hào* Yún-zhōu 雲洲, *fl.* late 16th c., Wàn-lì 萬曆 era) — late-Míng physician of Sū-jùn 蘇郡 (Suzhou), described by [[許士柔]] Xǔ Shì-róu's 1622 preface to the *Míng-yī zhǐ-zhǎng* ([[KR3er053]]) as a hereditary "third-generation good physician" (三世良醫) of his locality. The *Míng-yī zhǐ-zhǎng* is his sole surviving work and was revised and supplemented for the press in 1622 by [[邵行甫]] Shào Xíng-fǔ a generation after Huáng-fǔ's death. Not in CBDB.
