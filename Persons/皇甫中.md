**Huángfǔ Zhōng** 皇甫中 (*hào* Yúnzhōu 雲洲, *fl.* late 16th c., Wànlì 萬曆 era) — late-Míng physician of Sūjùn 蘇郡 (Suzhou), described by [[許士柔]] Xǔ Shìróu's 1622 preface to the *Míngyī zhǐzhǎng* ([[KR3er053]]) as a hereditary "third-generation good physician" (三世良醫) of his locality. The *Míngyī zhǐzhǎng* is his sole surviving work and was revised and supplemented for the press in 1622 by [[邵行甫]] Shào Xíngfǔ a generation after Huángfǔ's death. Not in CBDB.
