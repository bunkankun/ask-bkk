Míng-mǎn 明滿. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Jiàn-táng Yī** [[一|Jiàn-táng Yī]] of **Cháng-lè chán-sì** 常樂禪寺. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Míng-huì** [[明慧]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0594]] 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B486.
