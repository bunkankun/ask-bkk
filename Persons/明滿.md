Míngmǎn 明滿. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Jiàntáng Yī** [[一|Jiàntáng Yī]] of **Chánglè chánsì** 常樂禪寺. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Mínghuì** [[明慧]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0594]] 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B486.
