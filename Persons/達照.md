Modern Chinese Buddhist scholar, monk, and editor. Specialist in Chinese Buddhist *lǐ-chàn* 禮懺 ("worship-and-confession") literature and *Vajracchedikā* exegesis. Edited the cluster of Dūnhuáng *Vajracchedikā lǐ-chàn* manuals — [[KR6v0071]] *Yáo héshàng Jīngāng wǔ-lǐ yī-běn*, [[KR6v0072]] *Jīngāng wǔ-lǐ*, [[KR6v0073]] *Jīngāng-lǐ* (by *Tōnglǐ*) — and a second cluster of *Jīngāng-jīng-zàn* and *Jīngāng-jīng-sòng* witnesses — [[KR6v0093]] through [[KR6v0100]] — for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vols 7 and 9. Author of *Jīngāng-jīng zàn* yán-jiū* 金剛經讚研究 (Beijing: Zōngjiào wénhuà, 2002).
