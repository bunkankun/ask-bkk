Modern Chinese Buddhist scholar, monk, and editor. Specialist in Chinese Buddhist *lǐchàn* 禮懺 ("worship-and-confession") literature and *Vajracchedikā* exegesis. Edited the cluster of Dūnhuáng *Vajracchedikā lǐchàn* manuals — [[KR6v0071]] *Yáo héshàng Jīngāng wǔlǐ yīběn*, [[KR6v0072]] *Jīngāng wǔlǐ*, [[KR6v0073]] *Jīngānglǐ* (by *Tōnglǐ*) — and a second cluster of *Jīngāngjīngzàn* and *Jīngāngjīngsòng* witnesses — [[KR6v0093]] through [[KR6v0100]] — for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vols 7 and 9. Author of *Jīngāngjīng zàn* yánjiū* 金剛經讚研究 (Beijing: Zōngjiào wénhuà, 2002).
