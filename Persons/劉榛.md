Liú Zhēn 劉榛 (1635–1690), *zì* Shānwèi 山蔚, *hào* Xūzhōu 虛舟, of Shāngqiū 商丘 (Hénán). A Kāng-xī-era *jǔrén* and minor official, but principally a *gǔwén* writer in the **Hóu Fāngyù** 侯方域 lineage of late-Míng / early-Qīng Hénán prose. He is best known to *Sìkù* scholarship as the Kāng-xī-era editor who reconstructed **Shěn Lǐ**'s *Yìyùtáng gǎo* [[KR4e0213]] in 1690 from fragments after the Wànlì cutting had been destroyed at the end of the Míng. The reconstruction preserves the prose but not the poetry of Shěn Lǐ's original. CBDB 74852.
