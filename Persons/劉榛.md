Liú Zhēn 劉榛 (1635–1690), *zì* Shānwèi 山蔚, *hào* Xūzhōu 虛舟, of Shāngqiū 商丘 (Hé-nán). A Kāng-xī-era *jǔrén* and minor official, but principally a *gǔ-wén* writer in the **Hóu Fāng-yù** 侯方域 lineage of late-Míng / early-Qīng Hé-nán prose. He is best known to *Sìkù* scholarship as the Kāng-xī-era editor who reconstructed **Shěn Lǐ**'s *Yì-yù-táng gǎo* [[KR4e0213]] in 1690 from fragments after the Wàn-lì cutting had been destroyed at the end of the Míng. The reconstruction preserves the prose but not the poetry of Shěn Lǐ's original. CBDB 74852.
