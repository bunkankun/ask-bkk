# Zhìbǎo 至寶

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[智及]] Yúān Zhìjí (1311–1378); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with Hǔqiū Nánchēn **Zhìbǎo** 智寶 (DILA A024985), another of Zhìjí's dharma-heirs.
