# Zhì-bǎo 至寶

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[智及]] Yú-ān Zhì-jí (1311–1378); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Not to be confused with Hǔ-qiū Nán-chēn **Zhì-bǎo** 智寶 (DILA A024985), another of Zhì-jí's dharma-heirs.
