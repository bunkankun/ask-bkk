**Sītǎn** 思坦, *hào* **Tóng-zhōu** 桐洲 (also referenced as **Tóng-zhōu Tǎn** 桐洲坦). Sòng (or Yuán-transition) Buddhist exegete; lifedates unrecorded. His sole transmitted work is the **Lèngyán-jīng jí-zhù** 楞嚴經集註 in 10 fascicles ([[KR6j0676]], X11 no. 268) — a *jí-zhù* ("collected-annotations") compilation on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*. The DILA Person Authority assigns the dynasty as 宋, and the work is conventionally placed in the late Southern-Sòng commentarial efflorescence on the *Lèngyán*; however, the prefatory matter to the printed edition records that the manuscript was carried north and made available for printing in **Huáng-qìng *rén-zǐ* = 1312 CE** (皇慶壬子歲) by the Yuan-period **Bān-ruò shì-lì** 般若室利 (a *Gāo-chāng* 高昌 Uyghur monk), placing the *editio princeps* in the early Yuán under the patronage of the Yuán court's Tibetan-Uyghur Buddhist establishment.

The work is sometimes referenced in later Lèngyán commentarial literature as the **Tóngzhōu Lèngyán jízhù** 桐洲楞嚴集註 to distinguish it from the various *jízhù* compilations on the same sūtra by other compilers.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A000832; *Lèngyánjīng jízhù* preface (X11n0268).
