Míng-dynasty sixth-generation descendant of the Yuán recluse-scholar **Jīn Juān** 金涓 of Yìwū. Together with his seventh-generation descendant kinsman Jīn Jiāng 金江, he gathered the scattered remnants of his ancestor's 40-*juǎn* literary collection (lost by the early Míng) and compiled the surviving one-*juǎn* *Qīngcūn yígǎo* 青村遺稿 ([[KR4d0556]]). Other biographical details are not documented in the catalog or *tíyào*.
