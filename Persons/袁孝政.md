**Yuán Xiàozhèng** 袁孝政 was a Táng commentator, author of the commentary on the ***Liú zǐ*** 劉子 ([[KR5d0053]]) — a late-Six-Dynasties philosophical miscellany attributed variously to Liú Xié 劉勰 or Liú Zhòu 劉晝.
