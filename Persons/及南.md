# 及南 Jínán

Northern-Sòng Chán monk; "former resident of the Fúzhōu Shàngshēng chányuàn 福州上生禪院, succeeding-patriarchal *śramaṇa*" (前住福州上生禪院嗣祖沙門). Author of the preface to [[戒環]] Jièhuán's *Fǎhuá jīng yàojiě* ([[KR6d0067]]) and collator-editor of the work: Jínán's collation began in Xuānhé *jǐhài* (1119) and final revisions on the subcommentary were discussed with Jièhuán at Nánshān in *bǐngwǔ* (1126).
