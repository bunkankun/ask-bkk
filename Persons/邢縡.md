# Xíng Zǎi 邢縡

Mid-Táng court figure (d. 752), son of [[邢璹]] Xíng Shù. Implicated in the 752 (Tiānbǎo 11) rebellion plot also involving Wáng Hàn 王銲 (brother of [[王鉷]] Wáng Hóng); executed when the plot was uncovered. The execution is the occasion on which the *Tángshū* (in the *Wáng Hóng zhuàn* 王鉷傳) records that his father Xíng Shù ended his career as *Hónglú shǎoqīng* 鴻臚少卿 — the only piece of biographical evidence we have for Xíng Shù's late career.
