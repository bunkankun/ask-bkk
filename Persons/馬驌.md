Mǎ Sù 馬驌 (1621–1673), zì Cōngyù 驄御, also zì Wǎnsī 宛斯, native of Zōupíng 鄒平 (Shāndōng 山東). 順治 *jǐhài* (1659) *jìnshì*; he served as judicial commissioner (推官) of Huái'ān 淮安 prefecture and ended his career as magistrate of Língbì 靈璧 county. He was nicknamed Mǎ Sāndài 馬三代 ("Three-Dynasties Mǎ") for his command of the high-antiquity literature, especially the *Zuǒ zhuàn* 左傳 and the materials on the Three Dynasties. Wáng Shìzhēn 王士禎 in his *Chí běi ǒu tán* 池北偶談 praises him as 博雅嗜古, especially in *Zuǒshì* studies. The catalog gives 1620–1673 but CBDB and Wilkinson (*Chinese History: A New Manual*, p. 706) give the birth year as 1621, followed here. His major works in the Kanripo corpus are *Zuǒ zhuàn shì wěi* 左傳事緯 ([[KR1e0101]]) and the antiquity-period compilation *Yì shǐ* 繹史 (in KR2 division).
