# Dúgū Zhènglún 獨孤正倫

Early-Táng author named in one tradition as the composer of the tomb-inscription for the Third Chán Patriarch [[僧璨]] Sēngcàn at the Shūzhōu Shāngǔ sì 舒州山谷寺, preserved in *Quán Táng wén* juan 390 (the other tradition attributes it to [[法琳]] Fǎlín).
