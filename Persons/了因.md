# Liǎo-yīn 了因

Yuán-dynasty Chán *sì-fǎ mén-rén* 嗣法門人 of [[如砥]] Píng-shí Rú-dǐ (d. 1357); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
