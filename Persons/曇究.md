Tánjiū 曇究 (lifedates unknown), monk attested in [[道安|Dàoān]]'s preface to [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (T1505) as one of three scribes (*bǐshòu* 筆受) — alongside [[僧導|Sēngdǎo]] and [[僧叡|Sēngruì]] — for the 382 CE translation of [[婆素跋陀|Vasubhadra]]'s *Tridharmaka* abstract at the Yèsì 鄴寺 under Former Qín 符秦. No independent biographical material is preserved.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6a0157]] *Sìāhánmù chāo jiě* (scribal role).
