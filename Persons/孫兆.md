**Sūn Zhào** 孫兆 (*fl.* 1057–1067, 宋), Northern Sòng official-physician and a member of the imperial **Bureau for Editing Medical Books** (*Jiàozhèng yīshū jú* 校正醫書局) under [[林億]]. He served as Jiàng-shì láng shǒu Diàn-zhōng chéng 將仕郎守殿中丞 with the bureau title 重校誤 ("Senior Collator of Errata"). His most prominent independent contribution was the editorial leadership of the校正醫書局's edition of the ***Wài-tái mì-yào*** ([[KR3e0015]]), presented to the throne in Zhìpíng 4 (1067); the surviving Sòng print of the *Wài-tái* under the printing-block colophon names him as the principal collator. He participated in the bureau's collation of the *Sùwèn* ([[KR3e0001]]) and other foundational classics. He has no transmitted free-standing biographical record.
