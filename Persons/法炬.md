Fǎjù 法炬 (also written 法巨, 帛法巨, 帛法炬; DILA Authority A000707) was a Western Jìn 西晉 monk and translator. The 帛 ("Bó") variant of his name suggests Yuezhi or Kuchean ancestry — the same conventional surname-marker borne by [[白法祖|Bó Fǎzǔ]] / 帛遠. He is securely attested in 308 CE (Yǒngjiā 永嘉 2) as one of the scribes (筆受) on Dharmarakṣa's translation of the *Lalitavistara* (the *Pǔ-yào jīng* 普曜經, T186), and as joint translator with [[法立]] (Fǎlì) of three substantial works in the late Western Jìn: the *Fǎjù pìyù jīng* 法句譬喻經 (T211), the *Fútián jīng* 福田經 (T683) and the [[KR6a0023|*Dà lóu-tàn jīng* 大樓炭經]] (T23). He is also credited individually with the *Fó shuō héng-shuǐ jīng* 佛說恒水經 (T33), the *Fǎhǎi jīng* 法海經 (T34), the *Fó shuō dǐng-shēng wáng gùshì jīng* 佛說頂生王故事經 (T39), the *Fó shuō qiú-yù jīng* 佛說求欲經 (T49), the *Fó shuō kǔ-yīn yīn-shì jīng* 佛說苦陰因事經 (T55), and several other short works of the *Āgama* and *Avadāna* types. His exact dates of birth and death are unrecorded.
