Tamba no Mototane 多紀元胤 (Taki Mototane; 1789–1827) was an Edo physician of the great Taki / Tamba medical-philological lineage, author of the *Zhōngguó yījí kǎo* 中國醫籍考 (*Iseki kō*, 1819) — the standard catalogue raisonné of Chinese medical literature compiled in the Bakumatsu *kōshōgaku* tradition. He was the elder brother of [[丹波元堅]] Tamba Motokata ([[KR3eh008]]).
