# 姚寶炘

Yáo Bǎoxīn, late-Qīng prefacer; contributed a 1901 preface to a later reissue of the *Qǐméng zhēndì* 啟蒙真諦 combined edition pairing [[KR3em009]] *Yīcǎotíng mùkē quánshū* 一草亭目科全書 with [[KR3em013]] *Yìshòu yǎnkē* 異授眼科.
