# 周謝庵

Zhōu Xièān 周謝庵, early-nineteenth-century Sūzhōu literatus / collector. [[葉萬青]] Yè Wànqīng saw the *Tiānyuán yīàn* 天元醫案 — a Yè-school casebook — at Zhōu's studio in Jiāqìng bǐngzǐ (1816), as noted in the preface to [[KR3ep079]] *Yèshì yīàn cúnzhēn*.
