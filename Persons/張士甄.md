**Zhāng Shìzhēn** 張士甄 (1624–1693) — early-Qīng official of Lùhé 潞河 (Tōngzhōu 通州, Zhílì / north of Běijīng); *jìnshì* of *Shùnzhì* 6 / 1649; served in various capacities at the *Hànlín* and the Six Boards through the *Kāngxī* era. Author of the 1686 (*Kāngxī bǐngyín*) publication-preface to [[馮兆張]] Féng Zhàozhāng's *Féngshì jǐnnáng mìlù* ([[KR3er070]]), composed at his Yāntái 燕臺 (Běijīng) residence. CBDB id 80213.
