**Zhāng Shì-zhēn** 張士甄 (1624–1693) — early-Qīng official of Lù-hé 潞河 (Tōng-zhōu 通州, Zhí-lì / north of Běijīng); *jìnshì* of *Shùn-zhì* 6 / 1649; served in various capacities at the *Hàn-lín* and the Six Boards through the *Kāng-xī* era. Author of the 1686 (*Kāng-xī bǐng-yín*) publication-preface to [[馮兆張]] Féng Zhào-zhāng's *Féng-shì jǐn-náng mì-lù* ([[KR3er070]]), composed at his Yān-tái 燕臺 (Běijīng) residence. CBDB id 80213.
