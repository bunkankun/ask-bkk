**Wáng Yǒng** 王永 (*fl.* mid-to-late Míng, ca. 1550–1600), Míng literatus-compiler of the *Jǐshì zhēnbǎo* 濟世珍寶 ([[KR3ed126]]) — a popular-clinical formulary with an unusually substantive appended *Guǎngsì yàoyǔ* (fertility-and-progeny treatise) of [[俞橋]] (Yú Qiáo). Sparse biographical record; the homonym is reasonably common in Míng-period materials and the *Jǐshì zhēnbǎo* compiler is not securely disambiguated. (Distinct from [[王永吉]], the late-Míng / early-Qīng official 1600–1659.)
