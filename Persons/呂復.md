# 呂復 Lǚ Fù

**Lǚ Fù** 呂復 (*hào* Yǎngshēngzhǔ 養生主), late-Yuán to early-Míng physician of Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō, Zhèjiāng); contributed the influential 1364 preface to [[滑壽]] Huá Shòu's *Shísì jīng fāhuī* 十四經發揮 ([[KR3ee026]]). Recorded a *Cún cún lùn* 存存論 ("Treatise on Preservation") and other medical works listed in Yuán-Míng bibliographies.
