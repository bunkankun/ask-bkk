# Sēng-jiàn 僧鑑

Late-17th-century Chán monk of Nán-cūn 南村. Preface-writer for the *Wúyī dàorén lù* 無依道人錄 [[KR6q0192]], dated *Dà-Qīng Kāngxī suìcì dīng-wèi qī yuè jì wàng* 大清康熙歲次丁未七月既望 = 16th day of the seventh month of Kāngxī 6 = 1667. The preface-signature identifies him as *sì-zǔ shāmén* 嗣祖沙門 ("dharma-succession monk"), indicating membership in a recognised Chán master-lineage, though his specific lineage-affiliation is not noted.

Beyond this preface-contribution, no independent biographical material is preserved.
