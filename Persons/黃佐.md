Huáng Zuǒ 黃佐 (1490–1566), *zì* Cáibó 才伯, *hào* Tàiquán 泰泉, native of Xiāngshān 香山 (modern Zhōngshān, Guǎngdōng). *Jìnshì* of Jiājìng 1 (1521); held a series of senior provincial offices including *Hànlínyuàn xiūzhuàn*, *Guǎngxī tíxué qiānshì* (Guǎngxī Education Commissioner), and ultimately *Nánjīng Guózǐ jiān jìjiǔ* (Director of the Southern Imperial Academy at Nánjīng). A staunch Cheng-Zhu Confucian who debated Wáng Yángmíng on *zhīxíng héyī* and was respected by Wáng for his *zhíliàng* (directness) without becoming a Wáng-school adherent. Major works: *Tàiquán xiānglǐ* 泰泉鄉禮 [[KR1d0090]] (community-ritual handbook, 7 juan); *Yuè diǎn* 樂典 (music compendium); *Hànlín jì* 翰林記 (history of the Míng Hànlínyuàn); *Guǎngzhōu rénwù zhì* 廣州人物志 (Guǎngzhōu local biographical compendium); *Huángjí jīngshì shū jiě* 皇極經世書解 (commentary on Shào Yōng's cosmological treatise — the Sìkù *tíyào* dismisses this as too speculative). *Míng shǐ* j. 287; CBDB id 30674 (1490–1566).
