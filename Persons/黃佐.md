Huáng Zuǒ 黃佐 (1490–1566), *zì* Cái-bó 才伯, *hào* Tài-quán 泰泉, native of Xiāng-shān 香山 (modern Zhōng-shān, Guǎng-dōng). *Jìnshì* of Jiā-jìng 1 (1521); held a series of senior provincial offices including *Hàn-lín-yuàn xiū-zhuàn*, *Guǎng-xī tí-xué qiān-shì* (Guǎng-xī Education Commissioner), and ultimately *Nán-jīng Guó-zǐ jiān jì-jiǔ* (Director of the Southern Imperial Academy at Nán-jīng). A staunch Cheng-Zhu Confucian who debated Wáng Yáng-míng on *zhī-xíng hé-yī* and was respected by Wáng for his *zhí-liàng* (directness) without becoming a Wáng-school adherent. Major works: *Tài-quán xiānglǐ* 泰泉鄉禮 [[KR1d0090]] (community-ritual handbook, 7 juan); *Yuè diǎn* 樂典 (music compendium); *Hàn-lín jì* 翰林記 (history of the Míng Hàn-lín-yuàn); *Guǎng-zhōu rénwù zhì* 廣州人物志 (Guǎng-zhōu local biographical compendium); *Huáng-jí jīng-shì shū jiě* 皇極經世書解 (commentary on Shào Yōng's cosmological treatise — the Sìkù *tíyào* dismisses this as too speculative). *Míng shǐ* j. 287; CBDB id 30674 (1490–1566).
