Southern-Sòng grand councillor and paleographer, *zì* Yàn-fā 彥發, of Jiā-xìng 嘉興 (modern Zhejiang). Granted *jìn-shì* in Qián-dào 2 (1166); under Níng-zōng he rose through senior posts to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū jiān gěi-shì-zhōng quán zhī Shū-mì-yuàn shì jiān Tài-zǐ bīn-kè jìn cān-zhī zhèng-shì*; on retirement *Tí-jǔ Dòng-xiāo-gōng*. His career is in his *Sòng-shǐ* biography (juàn 393). Two surviving philological monuments in the Sìkù: the *Bān-Mǎ zì-lèi* [[KR1j0032]] (c. 1184), a topical glossary of archaic graphs in the *Shǐjì* and *Hàn-shū*; and the *Hàn-lì zì-yuán* [[KR1j0033]] (c. 1190s), a comprehensive paleographic compendium of Hàn-Wèi-Jìn clerical-script forms drawn from 309 Hàn stelae and 31 Wèi-Jìn stelae. Together they make him one of the principal Southern-Sòng philologists. CBDB lifedates 1133–1211, in agreement with the *Sòng-shǐ* biography and the catalog meta.
