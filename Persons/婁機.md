Southern-Sòng grand councillor and paleographer, *zì* Yànfā 彥發, of Jiāxìng 嘉興 (modern Zhejiang). Granted *jìnshì* in Qiándào 2 (1166); under Níngzōng he rose through senior posts to *Lǐbù shàngshū jiān gěishìzhōng quán zhī Shūmìyuàn shì jiān Tàizǐ bīnkè jìn cānzhī zhèngshì*; on retirement *Tíjǔ Dòngxiāogōng*. His career is in his *Sòngshǐ* biography (juàn 393). Two surviving philological monuments in the Sìkù: the *Bān-Mǎ zìlèi* [[KR1j0032]] (c. 1184), a topical glossary of archaic graphs in the *Shǐjì* and *Hànshū*; and the *Hànlì zìyuán* [[KR1j0033]] (c. 1190s), a comprehensive paleographic compendium of Hàn-Wèi-Jìn clerical-script forms drawn from 309 Hàn stelae and 31 Wèi-Jìn stelae. Together they make him one of the principal Southern-Sòng philologists. CBDB lifedates 1133–1211, in agreement with the *Sòngshǐ* biography and the catalog meta.
