Gōng Míngzhī 龔明之 (1091–1182), *zì* Xīzhòng 希仲, *hào* Wǔxiū jūshì 五休居士; native of Kūnshān 崑山 in Sūzhōu (Píngjiāng). Northern-and-Southern-Sòng minor official and local *bǐjì* author. By prefectural-tribute and palace-examination in the *Shàoxīng* era he was given the post of *Gāozhōu wénxué*; under early *Chúnxī* nominated as *jīngmíng xíngxiū* and given honorary *Xuānjiào láng* on retirement. Lived to age 92; in his final year (*Chúnxī* 9 = 1182), at his lodge *Qīyí táng* (the "Hall of Reaching Centenarian Age"), he dictated the *ZhōngWú jìwén* 中吳紀聞 ([[KR2k0114]]) to his son Yù 昱 — the principal late-twelfth-century *bǐjì* on Sūzhōu literati biography and local culture. Note: the catalog meta gives 襲 as the surname, but the Sìkù tíyào and CBDB confirm 龔; followed here. CBDB id 10199.
