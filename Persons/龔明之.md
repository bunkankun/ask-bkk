Gōng Míng-zhī 龔明之 (1091–1182), *zì* Xī-zhòng 希仲, *hào* Wǔ-xiū jū-shì 五休居士; native of Kūn-shān 崑山 in Sū-zhōu (Píng-jiāng). Northern-and-Southern-Sòng minor official and local *bǐ-jì* author. By prefectural-tribute and palace-examination in the *Shào-xīng* era he was given the post of *Gāo-zhōu wén-xué*; under early *Chún-xī* nominated as *jīng-míng xíng-xiū* and given honorary *Xuān-jiào láng* on retirement. Lived to age 92; in his final year (*Chún-xī* 9 = 1182), at his lodge *Qī-yí táng* (the "Hall of Reaching Centenarian Age"), he dictated the *Zhōng-Wú jìwén* 中吳紀聞 ([[KR2k0114]]) to his son Yù 昱 — the principal late-twelfth-century *bǐ-jì* on Sū-zhōu literati biography and local culture. Note: the catalog meta gives 襲 as the surname, but the Sìkù tíyào and CBDB confirm 龔; followed here. CBDB id 10199.
