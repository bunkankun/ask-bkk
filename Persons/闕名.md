*Quēmíng* 闕名 ("name omitted") is the conventional placeholder for "anonymous" in the catalog meta of the Kanripo / SKQS corpus. This person-note exists as a wikilink target for works whose authorship is unknown or unrecorded; it groups multiple unrelated anonymous works without implying any single author.

Works in KR3a attributed to *Quēmíng*:

- *Jiā-shān tú-shū* 家山圖書 (KR3a0071) — Sòng-Yuán transition Confucian pedagogical chart-and-text. Per Qián Zēng's *Dúshū mǐn-qiú jì*, the work is from "an *Huì-ān* private-disciple's hand" (*Huì-ān sī-shū dì-zǐ zhī wén* 晦菴私淑弟子之文); transmitted from Xǔ Héng's pupil Liú-mǒu to Lǐ Huì 李晦. Sòng-Yuán transition.
