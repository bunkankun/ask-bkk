Liánshēng 聯昇, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Yúnfù Dàozhì** [[道智]] 雲腹道智 (1613–1673). Co-recorder with **Yuèxián** [[嶽賢]] of the 2-juan 《雲腹智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0480]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
