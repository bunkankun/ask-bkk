Lián-shēng 聯昇, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Yún-fù Dào-zhì** [[道智]] 雲腹道智 (1613–1673). Co-recorder with **Yuè-xián** [[嶽賢]] of the 2-juan 《雲腹智禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0480]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
