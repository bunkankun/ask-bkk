Jiāng Zé-zhī 江澤之, late-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician, *fl.* mid-to-late nineteenth century. Author of [[KR3ep055]] *Jiāng Zé-zhī yī-àn*, a single-*juǎn* casebook in the *wēn-bìng* / *fú-qì* (lurking-pathogen) tradition of Yè Tiān-shì and Wáng Mèng-yīng. No further transmitted biographical record beyond the textual evidence. Not in CBDB.
