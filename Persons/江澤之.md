Jiāng Zézhī 江澤之, late-Qīng Jiāngnán physician, *fl.* mid-to-late nineteenth century. Author of [[KR3ep055]] *Jiāng Zézhī yīàn*, a single-*juǎn* casebook in the *wēnbìng* / *fúqì* (lurking-pathogen) tradition of Yè Tiānshì and Wáng Mèngyīng. No further transmitted biographical record beyond the textual evidence. Not in CBDB.
