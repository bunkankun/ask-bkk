# Yuán-jìng 元靖

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[普巖]] Yùn'ān Pǔ-yán (1156–1226); principal co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0313]]) and recorder of the opening Dàshèng Pǔ-zhào abbacy record. Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000131).
