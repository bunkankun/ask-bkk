# Yuánjìng 元靖

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[普巖]] Yùn'ān Pǔyán (1156–1226); principal co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0313]]) and recorder of the opening Dàshèng Pǔzhào abbacy record. Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000131).
