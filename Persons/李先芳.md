# Lǐ Xiānfāng 李先芳

*Zì* Bóchéng 伯承, *hào* Běishān 北山. Native of Jiānlì 監利 (modern Húběi), with registered residence in Púzhōu 濮州. *Jìnshì* of *Jiājìng dīngwèi* (1547); held office through *Shàngbǎo sī shàoqīng*. Lifedates not recoverable.

The *Míngshǐ Wényuàn zhuàn* records that Wáng Shìzhēn 王世貞 included him among the *Guǎng wǔ zǐ* 廣五子 (the "Expanded Five Masters" of the late-Jiājìng / Lóngqìng literary circle); Lǐ Xiānfāng was one of these. Author of *Dú Shī sī jì* 讀詩私記 in 5 *juǎn* ([[KR1c0037]]), completed in *Lóngqìng* 4 (1570). Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* records a *Máoshī kǎo zhèng* 毛詩考正 by Lǐ Xiānfāng (no *juǎn*-count, "*not seen*") but not the *Sī jì*; the Sìkù editors speculate that Zhū Yízūn either had two distinct works or was confused on the title — uncertainty unresolved.
