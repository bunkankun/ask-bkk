Late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Hǔ-xī** 虎溪 (with full name **Hǔ-xī Huái-zé** 虎谿懷則 / 虎溪懷則). DILA Authority A001746. Native of **Shàng-yú** 上虞 (modern Zhèjiāng). Lifedates unrecorded.

Major work: the **Tiāntái chuán Fó xīn-yìn jì** 天台傳佛心印記 ([[KR6d0187]], T1938, 1 *juan*) — a Yuán-period Tiāntái treatise on the transmission of the Buddha's heart-seal through the Tiāntái lineage. The work is one of the most institutionally significant Yuán Tiāntái productions and articulates the Tiāntái claim to authentic transmission of the Buddha's awakening.

Note: distinct from the Tang-period **Huái-zé** 懷則 of Dà-yǔ-sì 大禹寺 (DILA A006612, disciple of Dào-àn 道岸, 654–717), and from the Tang **Hé-zhōng Huái-zé** 河中懷則 (DILA A007036, disciple of Mǎ-zǔ Dào-yī 馬祖道一).
