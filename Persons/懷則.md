Late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Hǔxī** 虎溪 (with full name **Hǔxī Huáizé** 虎谿懷則 / 虎溪懷則). DILA Authority A001746. Native of **Shàngyú** 上虞 (modern Zhèjiāng). Lifedates unrecorded.

Major work: the **Tiāntái chuán Fó xīnyìn jì** 天台傳佛心印記 ([[KR6d0187]], T1938, 1 *juan*) — a Yuán-period Tiāntái treatise on the transmission of the Buddha's heart-seal through the Tiāntái lineage. The work is one of the most institutionally significant Yuán Tiāntái productions and articulates the Tiāntái claim to authentic transmission of the Buddha's awakening.

Note: distinct from the Tang-period **Huáizé** 懷則 of Dàyǔsì 大禹寺 (DILA A006612, disciple of Dàoàn 道岸, 654–717), and from the Tang **Hézhōng Huáizé** 河中懷則 (DILA A007036, disciple of Mǎzǔ Dàoyī 馬祖道一).
