**Odai Sengaku** 尾臺淺岳 — Edo-period Japanese physician of the Odai house; teacher and adoptive father of [[尾臺榕堂]] Odai Yōdō (1799–1870), to whom he transmitted the Odai house's medical practice in Edo. Affiliated with the Yoshimasu Tōdō *kohōha* 古方派 lineage via the Mine 岑氏 succession.
