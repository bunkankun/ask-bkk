Zhāng Xù-chū 張旭初 (fl. *Chóng-zhēn* era, 1628–1644), younger brother of Zhāng Chǔ-shū [[張楚叔]] (the editor of the *Wú-sāo hé-biān* [[KR4j0069]]). Edited / revised and proofed the *Bái-xuě-zhāi xuǎn-dìng yuè-fǔ Wú-sāo hé-biān* under his elder brother's general editorship. No biographical detail beyond the family relationship is securely recorded; the brothers are otherwise known only through the *Wú-sāo* (吳騷) series of late-Míng *qǔ* anthologies. Not in CBDB.
