Zhāng Xùchū 張旭初 (fl. *Chóngzhēn* era, 1628–1644), younger brother of Zhāng Chǔshū [[張楚叔]] (the editor of the *Wúsāo hébiān* [[KR4j0069]]). Edited / revised and proofed the *Báixuězhāi xuǎndìng yuèfǔ Wúsāo hébiān* under his elder brother's general editorship. No biographical detail beyond the family relationship is securely recorded; the brothers are otherwise known only through the *Wúsāo* (吳騷) series of late-Míng *qǔ* anthologies. Not in CBDB.
