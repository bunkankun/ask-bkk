# Xiàng Zhōng 項忠

Mid-Míng general (1421–1502); posthumous title **Xiāngyì** 襄毅, hence styled *Xiāngyì gōng* 襄毅公. Best known for surviving the **Tǔmù** 土木 incident of 1449 (the Mongol capture of the Zhèngtǒng emperor). Lifelong *Cuṇḍā* dhāraṇī devotee from youth, who attributed his survival of that incident to the protection of the *Cuṇḍā* mantra.

Mentioned in his descendant [[項謙]] Xiàng Qiān's 1652 preface to the *Zhǔntí fénxiū xīdì chànhuǐ xuánwén* ([[KR6j0760]]) as the Xiàng-family ancestor who established the *Cuṇḍā* devotional tradition transmitted through the family.
