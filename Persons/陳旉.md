Chén Fū 陳旉 (1076–1154), Northern–Southern Sòng transition agricultural-treatise writer. Self-styled *Xīshānyǐnjūquánzhēnzǐ* 西山隱居全真子. Native of Yángzhōu 揚州 region, retired to the *Xīshān* (Western Mountains) of Yízhēn 儀真. Author of the *Nóng shū* 農書 ([[KR3d0002]], 3-juan agricultural manual focused on Lower-Yangzi rice cultivation, completed *Shàoxīng* 19 / 1149).
