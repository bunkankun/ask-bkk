Chén Fū 陳旉 (1076–1154), Northern–Southern Sòng transition agricultural-treatise writer. Self-styled *Xīshān-yǐnjū-quánzhēn-zǐ* 西山隱居全真子. Native of Yáng-zhōu 揚州 region, retired to the *Xī-shān* (Western Mountains) of Yí-zhēn 儀真. Author of the *Nóng shū* 農書 (KR3d0002, 3-juan agricultural manual focused on Lower-Yangzi rice cultivation, completed *Shào-xīng* 19 / 1149).
