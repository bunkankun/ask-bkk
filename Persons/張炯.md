Zhāng Jiǒng 張炯, native of Xuānchéng 宣城 (Anhuī), *fl.* late 18th century. He is known almost exclusively as the author of the 1799 (嘉慶四年) preface to the Sūn Xīngyǎn / Sūn Féngyì reconstruction of the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* [[KR3ec001]], which he signs "撰於瞻園之灌術莊." The preface defends Shénnóng as a "great Confucian of antiquity" and explicitly criticises Lǐ Shízhēn's [[李時珍]] *Běncǎo gāngmù* ([[KR3ec025]]) for cutting up the textual tradition without philological care.

CBDB lists three homonymous 張炯 entries (ids 106556, 292388, 456453), all without lifedates, none unambiguously matchable to the *Běncǎo* prefator from Xuānchéng. No id assigned here pending further evidence.
