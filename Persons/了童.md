Liǎotóng 了童 (active Míng), Míng-period Buddhist scholar styled *Gǔlíng Liǎotóng* 古靈了童; the *bǔ zhù* 補註 ("supplementary annotator") of *[[KR6g0047|Yí jiào jīng bǔ zhù]]* (X665). Birth and death dates not preserved.
