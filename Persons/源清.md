Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Fèngxiān** 奉先 (also referenced as **Fèngxiān zūnzhě** 奉先尊者, **Fèngxiān Yuánqīng** 奉先源清). DILA Authority A001049. Lifedates uncertain; died Xiánpíng 咸平 3 (= 13 February 1000 – 2 February 1001).

Major work: the **Fǎhuá shí miào bùèr mén shìzhū zhǐ** 法華十妙不二門示珠指 ([[KR6d0160]], X56n0926, 2 *juan*) — a Northern-Sòng commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* and the related Tiāntái doctrinal apparatus.

Source: *Sòngsēng zhù* 宋僧著 36; DILA A001049.
