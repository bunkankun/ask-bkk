Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Fèng-xiān** 奉先 (also referenced as **Fèng-xiān zūn-zhě** 奉先尊者, **Fèng-xiān Yuán-qīng** 奉先源清). DILA Authority A001049. Lifedates uncertain; died Xián-píng 咸平 3 (= 13 February 1000 – 2 February 1001).

Major work: the **Fǎhuá shí miào bù-èr mén shì-zhū zhǐ** 法華十妙不二門示珠指 ([[KR6d0160]], X56n0926, 2 *juan*) — a Northern-Sòng commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* and the related Tiāntái doctrinal apparatus.

Source: *Sòng-sēng zhù* 宋僧著 36; DILA A001049.
