# 呂廣 Lǚ Guǎng

**Lǚ Guǎng** 呂廣 (Three-Kingdoms-era Wú), tàiyī 太醫 of the Wú court; author of the earliest known commentary on the *Nànjīng* 難經 (only fragments preserved). Cited in [[楊繼洲]] Yáng Jìzhōu's *Zhēndào yuánliú* essay in the *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng* ([[KR3ee027]]).
