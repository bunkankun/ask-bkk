Lǚ Kūn 呂坤 (1536–1618), *zì* Shūjiǎn 叔簡, *hào* Xīnwú 新吾 / Bàodú jūshì 抱獨居士, was a Late-Míng *Lǐxué*-aligned official, statecraft writer, and one of the most-cited Late-Míng moral-philosophical voices. Native of Níng-líng 寧陵 (Hé-nán). *Jìnshì* of *Wàn-lì rén-wǔ* (1582); rose to *Xíng-bù shì-láng* and *Bīng-bù shì-láng*; resigned in disgust with the late-Wàn-lì court and devoted himself to scholarship and local affairs. Major works: the *Shēn yín yǔ* 呻吟語 (the major *yǔ-lù* notebook in 4 juan, completed *Wàn-lì rén-chén* / 1592 with Guō Zǐzhāng's preface; a 2-juan abridgement *Shēn yín yǔ zhāi* 呻吟語摘 in *Wàn-lì bǐng-chén* / 1616, prepared by Lǚ in his late life with his son Lǚ Zhī-wèi 呂知畏 — KR3a0096 is the abridgement); the *Sì lǐ yí* 四禮疑 (in the SKQS); the *Shí zhèng lù* 實政錄 (statecraft anthology); the *Guī fàn* 閨範 (women's pedagogy); the *Quē-lǐ tú* 闕里圖. Biography: *Míng shǐ* j. 226. CBDB id 125516, dates 1536–1618.
