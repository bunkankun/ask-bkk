Yú Xī 余熂 (active late 14th c.), native of **Xīnān** 新安 (Huīzhōu prefecture, modern Ānhuī). Pupil of [[殷奎]] **Yīn Kuí** 殷奎 — Yú travelled from his native Xīnān to **Kūnshān** 崑山 to study under Yīn at the county *rúxué*. After Yīn's death in 1376, Yú compiled his teacher's literary collection [[KR4e0058]] *Qiángzhāi jí* 強齋集 (nine *juǎn* of Yīn's verse and prose plus one *juǎn* of memorial materials). Later examined and rose to *jìnshì* in the metropolitan examinations, served in *jìnshì* (near-court) posts. The *Qiángzhāi jí* original preface notes Yú's later official career and his repeated mourning leaves during which he returned home and supplemented the compilation.
