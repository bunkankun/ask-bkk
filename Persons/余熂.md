Yú Xī 余熂 (active late 14th c.), native of **Xīn-ān** 新安 (Huī-zhōu prefecture, modern Ān-huī). Pupil of [[殷奎]] **Yīn Kuí** 殷奎 — Yú travelled from his native Xīn-ān to **Kūn-shān** 崑山 to study under Yīn at the county *rú-xué*. After Yīn's death in 1376, Yú compiled his teacher's literary collection [[KR4e0058]] *Qiáng-zhāi jí* 強齋集 (nine *juǎn* of Yīn's verse and prose plus one *juǎn* of memorial materials). Later examined and rose to *jìn-shì* in the metropolitan examinations, served in *jìn-shì* (near-court) posts. The *Qiáng-zhāi jí* original preface notes Yú's later official career and his repeated mourning leaves during which he returned home and supplemented the compilation.
