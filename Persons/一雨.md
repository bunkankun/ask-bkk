# Yīyǔ 一雨

Late-Wànlì / Tiānqǐ-era Buddhist master active around 1620–1621, known from the preface to [[通容]] Fèiyǐn Tōngróng's *Bōrě xīnjīng zhuólún jiě* ([[KR6c0167]]) as a lecturer on the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* at the time Tōngróng sought his correction. Biographical details otherwise sparse.
