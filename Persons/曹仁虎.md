Mid-Qiánlóng official, classical scholar, and editor; native of Jiādìng 嘉定 (modern Shànghǎi). *Zì* Láiyīn 來殷; *hào* Xí'ān 習庵. Took the *jìnshì* in Qiánlóng 26 (1761) and entered the Hanlin Academy. Member of the editorial board of the imperial *Sìkù quánshū* and one of the principal working editors of the Qiánlóng-era *Shítōng* continuations: *Xù Wénxiàn Tōngkǎo* ([[KR2m0014]]), *Xù Tōngdiǎn* ([[KR2m0016]]), and the parallel *Xù Tōngzhì*. His own work is the *Qīshíèr fēng cǎo táng wénjí* 七十二峰艸堂文集. He died in service in Qiánlóng 52 (1787); CBDB gives the dates 1731–1787, followed here.
