**Zhuāng Zàitián** 莊在田 (*fl.* 清), Qīng-era physician known for warming-tonifying (*wēnbǔ* 溫補) approaches to childhood emergencies. Cited by [[王春亭]] Wáng Chūntíng in the *Jìshēng jí* 濟生集 ([[KR3ei014]]) as a doctrinal authority for paediatric warming-supplementing therapeutics.
