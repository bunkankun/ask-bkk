The catalog meta form *Mǎ Lóng-shù* 馬隆述 conflates the personal name and the function: 馬隆 is the personal name (Mǎ Lóng, Western Jìn period official, *Xī-píng tài-shǒu*); *shù* 述 is the function "expounded, narrated". The catalog should read separately: name 馬隆, function 述讚 (expounding-and-eulogising). 馬隆 (d. 285?) was a Western Jìn military officer. He provided the *zàn* (eulogies) attached to the *Wò-jī jīng* (KR3b0001) — the SKQS *tíyào* notes that the *zàn* are also of doubtful authenticity, as the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* does not record them. CBDB has multiple homonyms 馬隆 with no confirmed match.
