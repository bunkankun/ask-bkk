# 張渙 Zhāng Huàn

**Zhāng Huàn** 張渙, Southern-Sòng military-rank physician with the title *Tuánliànshǐ* 團練使; author of the emergency-moxibustion handbook *Bèijí jiǔfǎ* 備急灸法 ([[KR3ee049]]). Likely identical with the Sòng paediatrician 張渙 (author of *Jǐshēng tángshì xiǎo'ér fāng* 濟生堂氏小兒方) named in some catalogues, but the identification is not secure.
