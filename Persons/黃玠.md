**Huáng Jiè** 黃玠 (fl. early Southern Sòng), grandson of Huáng Cháng 黃裳 [[黃裳]], of Yán-píng 延平 (Fú-jiàn). Compiled and printed his grandfather's *Yǎn-shān jí* 演山集 [[KR4d0109]] in 60 *juǎn* — the principal channel of transmission. Precise dates and own career undocumented beyond this editorial role. Not to be confused with the Yuán-period scholar Huáng Jiè 黃玠 (*hào* Bì-chuān 弁川) of Cí-xī.
