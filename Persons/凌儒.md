Líng Rú 凌儒 (fl. late 16th cent.), *hào* Hǎilóu 海樓, native of Hǎilíng 海陵 (Tàizhōu 泰州, Jiāngsū). A late-Míng literary figure of the Lower Yangzi region whose principal surviving trace is the *Wànlì yǐwèi* (1595) preface to [[彭大翼]]'s *Shāntáng sìkǎo* 山堂肆考 ([[KR3k0052]]). He is listed among the *cānjiào xìngshì* (collation contributors) of that work alongside [[焦竑]] and other Nánjīng / Lower-Yangzi literati. CBDB id 356556.
