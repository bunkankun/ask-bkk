Líng Rú 凌儒 (fl. late 16th cent.), *hào* Hǎi-lóu 海樓, native of Hǎi-líng 海陵 (Tài-zhōu 泰州, Jiāng-sū). A late-Míng literary figure of the Lower Yangzi region whose principal surviving trace is the *Wàn-lì yǐ-wèi* (1595) preface to [[彭大翼]]'s *Shān-táng sì-kǎo* 山堂肆考 ([[KR3k0052]]). He is listed among the *cān-jiào xìng-shì* (collation contributors) of that work alongside [[焦竑]] and other Nán-jīng / Lower-Yangzi literati. CBDB id 356556.
