**Shōzon** 性尊 (Chinese: Xìngzūn, *fl.* mid-12th c., 平安末), Japanese Buddhist scholar associated with the Daigo-ji 醍醐寺 monastic milieu. Wrote the preface to the standard transmitted text of the *Kōyōshō* 香要抄 ([[KR3ec075]]), conventionally dated to the Hōgen 保元 era (1156–1159). Japanese scholar; not in CBDB.
