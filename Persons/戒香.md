# Jiè-xiāng 戒香

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active in the *Tóng-zhì* 同治 era (1862–1874), known by the sobriquet **Lián-gēn dà-shī** 蓮根大師 ("Master Lotus-Root"). Author of the **《淨土極信錄》** *Jìng-tǔ jí xìn lù* [[KR6p0113]] (X1194), a single-*juǎn* exhortative treatise that takes the *Ē-mí-tuó jīng* 阿彌陀經 line by line and draws from each passage exhortations to the *xìn-yuàn-xíng* 信願行 (faith-vow-practice) triad. The work was prefaced in 1872 by **Jìng-tài** 境泰 (*hào* Ruò-yú shān-rén 若愚山人) at the *Lóng-yán* 龍巖 monastery. Lifedates and native place not preserved.
