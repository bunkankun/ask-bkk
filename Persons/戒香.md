# Jièxiāng 戒香

A late-Qīng Pure Land monk active in the *Tóngzhì* 同治 era (1862–1874), known by the sobriquet **Liángēn dàshī** 蓮根大師 ("Master Lotus-Root"). Author of the **《淨土極信錄》** *Jìngtǔ jí xìn lù* [[KR6p0113]] (X1194), a single-*juǎn* exhortative treatise that takes the *Ēmítuó jīng* 阿彌陀經 line by line and draws from each passage exhortations to the *xìnyuànxíng* 信願行 (faith-vow-practice) triad. The work was prefaced in 1872 by **Jìngtài** 境泰 (*hào* Ruòyú shānrén 若愚山人) at the *Lóngyán* 龍巖 monastery. Lifedates and native place not preserved.
