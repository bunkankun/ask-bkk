Rú-xīng 如惺. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of **Qīng-lóng Dé-fēng** [[德風]] (b. 1622), lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0531]] 《德風禪師般若語錄》 6 juan, J38 B411. Lifedates unknown, late 17th century.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Míng-dynasty **Rú-xīng** 如惺 (DILA A000348, dharma-heir of **Xiàng-xiān Zhēn-qīng** 象先真清, resident at Tiān-tāi Cí-yún-sì), who reissued the *Fó-fǎ jīn-tāng biān* 佛法金湯編 in 1600 and compiled the *Dà-míng gāo-sēng zhuàn* 大明高僧傳 (T2062) in **Wàn-lì 45** = **1617**, plus the *Dé-yù Lóng-huá xiū-zhèng chàn-yí* 得遇龍華修證懺儀 (X1488). The two are easily confused on filename and require disambiguation by context.
