Rúxīng 如惺. Personal attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of **Qīnglóng Défēng** [[德風]] (b. 1622), lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0531]] 《德風禪師般若語錄》 6 juan, J38 B411. Lifedates unknown, late 17th century.

- - - - -

Not to be confused with the Míng-dynasty **Rúxīng** 如惺 (DILA A000348, dharma-heir of **Xiàngxiān Zhēnqīng** 象先真清, resident at Tiāntāi Cíyúnsì), who reissued the *Fófǎ jīntāng biān* 佛法金湯編 in 1600 and compiled the *Dàmíng gāosēng zhuàn* 大明高僧傳 (T2062) in **Wànlì 45** = **1617**, plus the *Déyù Lónghuá xiūzhèng chànyí* 得遇龍華修證懺儀 (X1488). The two are easily confused on filename and require disambiguation by context.
