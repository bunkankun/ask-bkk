Late-Míng official, classicist and connoisseur; *zì* Wénróng 文融, *hào* Yuè-fēng 月峰, of Yúyáo 餘姚 in Zhèjiāng. Lifedates 1542–1613 (CBDB). *Wànlì jiǎ-xū* 甲戌 (1574) *jìnshì*. Career: rose to *Nán-jīng Bīngbù shàngshū* 南京兵部尚書; posthumous title *Wén-jiǎn* 文簡. Famed in his time for examination essays (*zhì-yì* 制義); his retreat to scholarship in his last years produced [[KR3h0047]] *Shū-huà bá-bá* 書畫跋跋 (6 *juàn*), a critical commentary on Wáng Shìzhēn 王世貞's colophons on calligraphy and painting, transmitted in manuscript until *Qián-lóng gēng-shēn* (1740). Also author of an important *Chūnqiū kǎo* 春秋考 and commentaries on the *Shī*. Note: not to be confused with the homonymous Míng Sūn Kuàng (CBDB 347812) of *Wànlì* who held lesser office.
