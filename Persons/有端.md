# Yǒuduān 有端

Yuán / early-Míng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[惟一]] Liǎotáng Wéiyī; co-editor (with [[宗義]]) of *juan* 1 (Yánqìng abbacy, 1330) of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0350]]). The Kanripo source text at this point shows a variant graph 省端, which DILA (A000382) regularises to 有端; the catalog meta follows DILA. Lifedates unrecorded.
