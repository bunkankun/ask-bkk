Cài Kān 蔡戡 (b. 1141, *zì* Dìngfū 定夫, *hào* Dìngzhāi 定齋), great-great-grandson of the Northern-Sòng calligrapher and statesman Cài Xiāng 蔡襄 (1012–1067) of *Xiānyóu* 仙遊 in *Xīnghuàjūn* 興化軍 (modern Fújiàn). His grandfather Cài Shēn 蔡紳 settled the family at *Wǔjìnxiàn* 武進縣 in *Chángzhōu* 常州 (modern Jiāngsū) during *Shàoxīng*. *Jìnshì* of *Qiándào bǐngxū* (1166), passing in *jiǎkē*. Held office to *Bǎomógé zhí xuéshì*, with substantial regional administrative service: *Jīngxī yùnpàn*, *Guǎngdōng yùnpàn*, *Húběi xiàn*, *Jīngzhào yǐn*, *Húnán* and *Huáixī zǒnglǐng*, and *Guǎngxī jīnglüè*. *Sòngshǐ* did not give him a biography — the *Sìkù* editors regarded this as an omission and reconstructed his career from his collection [[KR4d0258]]. CBDB id 3860 (death year not recorded; alive at least until close to 1230, when his collection was first cut). Author of [[KR4d0258]] *Dìngzhāi jí*.
