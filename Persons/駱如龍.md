**Luò Rúlóng** 駱如龍 (sobriquet **Qiánān** 潛庵 — *Lurking-Hermitage Master*) was an early-Qīng (Kāngxī era) paediatric massage (*tuīná* 推拿) specialist whose *Yòukē tuīná mìshū* 幼科推拿秘書 ([[KR3ej068]]) is one of the foundational early-Qīng *tuīná* manuals, conventionally dated 1691 (Kāngxī 30). The work survives in the *hxwd* recension without preface or biographical colophon; no other biographical data are recoverable.

CBDB has no entry for Luò Rúlóng. The work itself is the only surviving source of biographical attribution.
