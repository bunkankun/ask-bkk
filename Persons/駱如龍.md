**Luò Rú-lóng** 駱如龍 (sobriquet **Qián-ān** 潛庵 — *Lurking-Hermitage Master*) was an early-Qīng (Kāng-xī era) paediatric massage (*tuī-ná* 推拿) specialist whose *Yòu-kē tuī-ná mì-shū* 幼科推拿秘書 ([[KR3ej068]]) is one of the foundational early-Qīng *tuī-ná* manuals, conventionally dated 1691 (Kāng-xī 30). The work survives in the *hxwd* recension without preface or biographical colophon; no other biographical data are recoverable.

CBDB has no entry for Luò Rú-lóng. The work itself is the only surviving source of biographical attribution.
