Late Northern Sòng / early Southern Sòng art historian; a man of Shuāngliú 雙流 in Shǔ 蜀 (modern Sìchuān). Active in the *Shàoxīng* through *Qiándào* decades (c. 1127–1170s). Grandson of Dèng Xúnwǔ 鄧洵武 (d. 1121), who served Huī-zōng as *Zhī Shū-mì-yuàn shì* during the *Zhènghé* era when imperial patronage of painting was at its height; the family inherited a substantial portion of the Huī-zōng academy's painting record. Author of [[KR3h0030]] *Huà-jì* 畫繼 (1167), the third installment in the great Chinese art-historical succession after Zhāng Yànyuǎn [[KR3h0009]] and Guō Ruòxū [[KR3h0017]], covering the period 1074–1167 in 219 painters. The work is the principal source for the painters of the Huī-zōng academy and the early Southern Sòng *Lín'ān* court. Dèng Chūn's son or grandson Dèng Gōng 鄧公 (or 鄧公夀) and Dèng's family painting tradition continued in Shǔ.
