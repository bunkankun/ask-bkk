Xíng-líng 行凌, *zì* **Shí-guān** 石關 ("Stone Pass"); also referred to as **Pǔ-míng Shí-guān** 普明石關 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Born **明萬曆四十二年** = **1614** (癸丑冬圓花甲 — 60 in 1673 winter). Death year unknown — last fixed datum: alive at 60 in **康熙癸丑** = **1673**. Native of **Jiā-xīng Xiù-shuǐ** 秀水; lay surname **Gù** 顧.

**Formation.** As a child loved poetry and study but came from a poor family. His parents, recognizing his attraction to monks, sent him to **Jìng-shān Sōng-yuán-ān** 徑山松源菴 to enter the order. At 19 went to **Zhēn-jì-sì** 真寂寺 to take tonsure under **Wén-gǔ dà-shī** 聞谷大師 and to take full ordination. *Cān*-fǎng under **Xuě-guān** 雪關和尚 and **Yǒng-jué** 永覺和尚, later **Jué-làng** 覺浪和尚. One night while sitting under a glass-globe lamp, suddenly a monk emerged from the *xiǎo-qīng* (privy) — at the sound of the door opening, his "tight enclosure of doubt" broke open and he could neither see his body within nor the world without. Final *cān* under **Fèi-yǐn** and **Wàn-rú** 萬如. **丁酉春** = **1657** spring lectured on the *Fǎ-huá jīng* at **Fèng-míng-sì** 鳳鳴寺. Received final *fù-fǎ* under Fèi-yǐn at Jìng-shān.

**Abbacies.** **Pán-lóng-sì** 蟠龍寺, **Kāi-fǎ-sì** 開法寺, **Pǔ-míng-sì** 普明寺 (the principal abbacy giving him his sobriquet **Pǔ-míng Shí-guān**).

**Disciple.** **Xiāng-yán Fāng-guì** [[芳桂]] 香嚴芳桂 (1631–1708), compiler of this *yǔ-lù* and author of his own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0543]].

**Work.** [[KR6q0542]] 《石關禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B423, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fāng-guì** [[芳桂]] *děng biān*.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 71; 《石關禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈行實〉.
