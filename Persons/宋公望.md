Sòng Gōng-wàng 宋公望 (*fl.* early 16th c.), *zì* Tiān-mín 天民; native of Huá-tíng 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng). Son of [[宋詡]] (Sòng Xǔ) and father of [[宋懋澄]] (Sòng Mào-chéng). Contributed two of the five sections of the family work *Zhú yǔ shān fáng zá bù* 竹嶼山房雜部 ([[KR3j0171]]): *Zhòng-zhí bù* (Planting, 10 *juàn*) and *Zūn-shēng bù* (Reverencing Life, 10 *juàn*). No further biographical record survives beyond what the *Zhú yǔ shān fáng zá bù* preserves.
