Sòng Gōngwàng 宋公望 (*fl.* early 16th c.), *zì* Tiānmín 天民; native of Huátíng 華亭 (Sōngjiāng). Son of [[宋詡]] (Sòng Xǔ) and father of [[宋懋澄]] (Sòng Màochéng). Contributed two of the five sections of the family work *Zhú yǔ shān fáng zá bù* 竹嶼山房雜部 ([[KR3j0171]]): *Zhòngzhí bù* (Planting, 10 *juàn*) and *Zūnshēng bù* (Reverencing Life, 10 *juàn*). No further biographical record survives beyond what the *Zhú yǔ shān fáng zá bù* preserves.
