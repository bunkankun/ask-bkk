Southern-Sòng Buddhist monk; lifedates unrecorded. DILA Authority A001636. Resident at the **Qián-táng Huò-shān Guǎng-zhào-sì** 錢塘霍山廣照寺 (modern Háng-zhōu). Author of the **Fǎhuá jīng jù-jiě** 法華經句解 ([[KR6d0070]], X30n0604, 8 *juan*) — a Lotus Sūtra phrase-by-phrase running commentary, with a preface dated Jǐng-dìng 景定 2 (= 1261 CE).

The *jù-jiě* 句解 ("phrase-explanation") genre is a popular Sòng pedagogical form providing accessible phrase-by-phrase reading of canonical scriptures, intermediate in technical scholastic depth between the *yào-jiě* abridgements and the full *shū* (*jì*) subcommentary tradition.

Sources: DILA A001636; X30n0604; *Mínguó Fójiào qíkān wénxiàn jíchéng* 民國佛教期刊文獻集成 48, p. 402 (1933 article on Wéndá).
