Lǐ Kǎi 李鍇 (1686–1755), *zì* Tiějūn 鐵君, *hào* Méishān 眉山 (also Jiāomíng-zǐ 焦明子). A Hàn-bannerman of the Plain White Banner (Xiāng-bái-qí Hàn-jūn 鑲白旗漢軍); his ancestral home was Xiāng-píng 襄平 (the Hàn-period commandery seat of Liáodōng 遼東, modern Liáo-yáng 遼陽 in Shèngjīng 盛京) — he signs his preface to the *Shàng-shǐ* "of Xiāng-píng," reflecting his family's ancestry in the Liáodōng region. A privately-supported scholar throughout his life — he held no office under either Kāngxī or Yōngzhèng — Lǐ Kǎi is principally known for the *Shàng-shǐ* 尚史 ([[KR2d0024]]), his 107-*juǎn* re-cast of Mǎ Sù's 馬驌 *Yì-shǐ* 繹史 (1670) into *jì-zhuàn* form. He records in his preface that he began the project in *Yōngzhèng* 8 (*gēng-xū*, 1730) and completed it in *Qiánlóng* 10 (*yǐ-chǒu*, 1745), sixteen years of labour; he completed editorial revisions in his last decade. His other works include the *Hán-yè cǎo* 寒夜草 (poetry collection) and the *Liáo-shǐ jí-yào* 遼史輯要. CBDB id 125471 (dates 1686–1755 confirmed). Biography: *Qīngshǐ liè-zhuàn* 清史列傳 *juǎn* 71 (*Wényuàn*).
