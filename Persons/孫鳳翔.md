**Sūn Fèngxiáng** 孫鳳翔 (*zì* / *hào* Dú-shān 犢山, *fǔ* 甫), late-Qīng scholar of Jiāng-dū 江都 (Yáng-zhōu). Postfaced Zhāng Xiǎo-shān's *Yùyīng tíyàoshuō* 鬻嬰提要說 ([[KR3ej019]]) in 1889 (*Guāng-xù* 15 *jǐ-chǒu* mid-summer). Lifedates and other biographical detail not securely recorded.
