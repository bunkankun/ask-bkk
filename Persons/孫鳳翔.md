**Sūn Fèngxiáng** 孫鳳翔 (*zì* / *hào* Dúshān 犢山, *fǔ* 甫), late-Qīng scholar of Jiāngdū 江都 (Yángzhōu). Postfaced Zhāng Xiǎoshān's *Yùyīng tíyàoshuō* 鬻嬰提要說 ([[KR3ej019]]) in 1889 (*Guāngxù* 15 *jǐchǒu* mid-summer). Lifedates and other biographical detail not securely recorded.
