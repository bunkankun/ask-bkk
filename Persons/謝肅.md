Yuán–Míng transitional poet and *gǔwén* essayist, *zì* **Yuángōng** 原功, native of **Shàngyú** 上虞 (Kuàijī 會稽, Zhèjiāng). Studied under **Gòng Shītài** 貢師泰 at the *Yǎnggāo tíng* 仰高亭 on Wúshān 吳山, accompanying him by ship to Hǎichāng 海昌 during Gòng's grain-transport mission to Fújiàn and Guǎngdōng; later printed Gòng's collected works. Close friend of **Dài Liáng** 戴良 (1317–1383), who wrote two prefaces for his *Mì'ān gǎo* / *Mì'ān jí*. At the end of Yuán Zhìzhèng he sought audience with **Zhāng Shìchéng** 張士誠 at Wú with a strategy of demobilization and rest for the people; rebuffed, he withdrew to Yuè. Under Hóngwǔ raised by the *míngjīng* 明經 examination and appointed *Fújiàn ànchá sī qiānshì* 福建按察司僉事; subsequently implicated in a case, imprisoned, and died in confinement (CBDB 28452, citing genealogical sources, gives age at death as 53). His travels through Shāndōng, Shānxī, and Hénán in the 1360s produced an extensive corpus of *jìxíng* 紀行 poetry. Author of [[KR4e0021]] *Mì'ān jí* 密庵集.
