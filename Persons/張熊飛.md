# 張熊飛

Zhāng Xióngfēi, early-Qīng physician of Jiāngzuǒ 江左 (Jiāngnán); transmitted a manuscript on geriatric ophthalmology to [[馬化龍]] Mǎ Huàlóng in *bǐngchén* (1676), absorbed into [[KR3em008]] *Yǎnkē chǎnwēi* 眼科闡微.
