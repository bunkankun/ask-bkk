Wáng Xíng 王行 (1331–1395), *zì* **Zhǐ-zhòng** 止仲, *hào* **Bàn-xuān** 半軒, native of **Cháng-zhōu** 長洲 (Sū-zhōu prefecture). Member of the Sū-zhōu literary circle of [[高啟]] Gāo Qǐ (named in **Lǐ Zhì-guāng**'s biography of Gāo as one of Gāo's closest *jié-jiāo* friends). Began as a private tutor at the Qí-mén 齊門 (Sū-zhōu's north gate); served as local school-master in early Hóngwǔ; from c. 1383 was household tutor (*jiā-shī*) at the mansion of **Lán Yù** 藍玉 (*Liáng-guó-gōng* 涼國公), the foremost Hóngwǔ general. Distinguished by a serious lifelong interest in military strategy: held tactical discussions with Lán Yù and had close conversations with [[姚廣孝]] **Yáo Guǎng-xiào** (Dào-yǎn 道衍), advising the latter "to wait" for the right moment (a remark later interpreted in light of Yáo's role in the Jǐng-nán 靖難 rebellion of 1399–1402). Implicated in the **Lán Yù** treason case of Hóngwǔ 26 (1393) and executed in 1395. His collection is [[KR4e0047]] *Bàn-xuān jí* 半軒集; the appended *Wáng Bàn-xuān zhuàn* 王半軒傳 by [[杜瓊]] (Dù Qióng) is the principal external biographical witness. CBDB id 28689: 1331–1395, confirmed.
