Fāyù 發育, *hào* **Xiàocāng** 笑蒼. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jìguāng Yìnhuò** [[豁]] 寂光印豁 (b. 1619) in the Pòshān-line. Lead compiler of the 6-juan 《寂光豁禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0504]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
