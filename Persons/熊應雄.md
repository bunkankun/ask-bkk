**Xióng Yīngxióng** 熊應雄 (style **Yùnyīng** 運英) was an early-Qīng (Kāngxī era) paediatric massage (*tuīná* 推拿) practitioner of *Xīshǔ* 西蜀 (Sìchuān). His self-prefaced *Xiǎo'ér tuīná guǎngyì* 小兒推拿廣意 ([[KR3ej067]]), published 1676 (*Kāngxī* 15) during a military-administrative tenure at *Qīngyì* 青邑 (Zhèjiāng), is one of the foundational early-Qīng paediatric *tuīná* manuals.

He combined medical practice with military / administrative service, as was common for Qīng paediatric *tuīná* practitioners. He was associated through publication with **Chén Yùnyīng** 陳運英 (the Zhèjiāng-East prefect who urged Xióng to publish), but the relationship is professional / patronage rather than family.
