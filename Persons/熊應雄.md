**Xióng Yīng-xióng** 熊應雄 (style **Yùn-yīng** 運英) was an early-Qīng (Kāng-xī era) paediatric massage (*tuī-ná* 推拿) practitioner of *Xī-shǔ* 西蜀 (Sì-chuān). His self-prefaced *Xiǎo'ér tuī-ná guǎng-yì* 小兒推拿廣意 ([[KR3ej067]]), published 1676 (*Kāng-xī* 15) during a military-administrative tenure at *Qīng-yì* 青邑 (Zhèjiāng), is one of the foundational early-Qīng paediatric *tuī-ná* manuals.

He combined medical practice with military / administrative service, as was common for Qīng paediatric *tuī-ná* practitioners. He was associated through publication with **Chén Yùn-yīng** 陳運英 (the Zhèjiāng-East prefect who urged Xióng to publish), but the relationship is professional / patronage rather than family.
