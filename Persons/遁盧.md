**Dùnlú** 遁盧 (The Hermit's Cottage) is the pseudonym of the otherwise unidentified author of *Sīwén Biànxiàng* 斯文變相 [[KR4k0230]], a late-Qīng satirical novel in 10 chapters set in the Yángzhōu region. No biographical information has been located and CBDB contains no entry for this pen name.
