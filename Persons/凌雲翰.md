Yuán-Míng transition poet, native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Zì* Yàn-chōng 彥翀; *hào* Zhè-xuān 柘軒 ("Mulberry Studio"). Studied under the Jīn-huá-school scholar **Chéng Yǐ-wén** 程以文 at **Yī-nán** 黟南 (Huīzhōu); the Sìkù tíyào of his collected work [[KR4e0019]] explicitly corrects **Zhū Yízūn**'s Míng-era misattribution to Chén Lǚ 陳旅 (*zì* Zhòng-zhòng). Yuán *xiāng-shì* graduate of **Zhì-zhèng 19** (1359); appointed *shān-zhǎng* of the **Shào-xīng Lán-tíng shū-yuàn** 紹興蘭亭書院 but did not take up the post. In **Hóngwǔ 14** (*xīn-yǒu* 辛酉, 1381) recommended on grounds of talent and appointed *Sì-chuān Chéng-dū jiào-shòu* 四川成都教授, in which post he died. His verse and prose were assembled by his grandson in **Yǒng-lè 3** (1405) as the five-*juǎn* *Zhè-xuān jí* 柘軒集 [[KR4e0019]]. Lifedates not preserved in the standard biographies; the conventional bracket *c.* 1330 – *c.* 1395 is followed by modern scholarship. No confident CBDB match.
