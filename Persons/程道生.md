# Chéng Dàoshēng 程道生

Style name *Kěshēng* 可生. Native of Hǎi-níng 海寧 (Zhèjiāng). Active in the late Míng. Birth and death years not securely recorded.

His sole Sìkù-preserved work is the *Dùn-jiǎ yǎn-yì* 遁甲演義 ([[KR3g0048]]) in 4 *juàn* — a systematic exposition of the *Dùn-jiǎ* 遁甲 (Hidden-Trunk) cosmological-divinatory tradition, one of the *Sān-shì* (Three-Form) divination systems alongside *Tài-yī* and *Liù-rén*.
