# Ní Sī 倪思

*Zì* Zhèngfǔ 正甫; posthumous title Wénjié 文節. Native of Guī'ān 歸安 in Húzhōu 湖州 (modern Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Qiándào* 2 (1166) at age 19. Floruit Southern Sòng, second half of the twelfth century to the early thirteenth.

Career: served at successive court posts under Sòng Xiàozōng, Guāngzōng, and Níngzōng. Held *Lǐbù shàngshū* 禮部尚書, *Lìbù shàngshū* 吏部尚書, *Bǎowéngé xuéshì* 寶文閣學士. Briefly impeached and rusticated for his opposition to Hán Tuōzhòu's 韓侂胄 *Qìngyuán dǎngjìn* 慶元黨禁 (the proscription of the Dàoxué party, 1196–1202); rehabilitated after Hán's fall in 1207. Posthumous title Wénjié 文節, conferred under Lǐzōng.

Principal works: the *Bān-Mǎ yìtóng* 班馬異同 ([[KR2a0008]]) — the first systematic comparative-textual study of the *Shǐjì* / *Hànshū* relationship; the *Jīngchútáng zázhì* 經鉏堂雜誌 (a *bǐjì*-style miscellany of philological and ethical reflections); a substantial corpus of memorials and *fù* preserved as the *Wénjié jí* 文節集. His biography is in *Sòngshǐ* 398 ([[KR2a0032]]).
