**Hǎitiān Dú Xiào Zǐ** 海天獨嘯子 ("Solitary Howler at the Sky over the Sea") is the pen name of the author of *Nǚwā Shí* 女媧石 ([[KR4k0190]]), a late-Qīng feminist and political novel. The preface to the novel, written by "Wòhǔ Làngshì" 臥虎浪士, describes the author as a student who composed the novel during an examination recess. The *fán lì* notes indicate he came from a border region (*biān chuí* 邊陲) and wrote in Mandarin despite native dialect influences. The real name behind this pen name has not been identified. No CBDB record exists for this author.
