# Cóngyuè 從悅

Dòushuài Cóngyuè 兜率從悅 (1044–1091), Northern-Sòng Línjì Huánglóng-line Chán master, dharma-heir of Zhēnjìng Kèwén; subject of the closing narrative of the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī zōngmén wǔkù* ([[KR6q0061]]) on his training under Yúngài Zhìshàn.
