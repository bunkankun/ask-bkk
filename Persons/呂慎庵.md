**Lǚ Shènān** 呂慎庵 (mid-19th century, Qīng). The patron and friend who invited [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng to lodge at the *Bùkuīyuán* in *Méijīng* during the Tàipíng war, where Wáng composed his *Suíxījū yǐnshí pǔ* 隨息居飲食譜 ([[KR3eo043]]) in summer 1861.
