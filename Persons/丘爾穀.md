Qiū Ěr-gǔ 丘爾穀 (Tiān-qǐ era, fl. 1620s), descendant of the Hóng-zhì-era Senior Grand Secretary **Qiū Jùn** 丘濬 ([[丘濬]]) of **Qióng-zhōu**. In the Tiān-qǐ era selected 20% of the Jiā-jìng-era *Lèi-gǎo* 類稿, augmented it with 30% of the Jiā-jìng-era *Huì-gǎo* 會稿 (compiled by Zhèng Tíng-hú 鄭廷鵠), and combined with the original *Yín-gǎo* 吟稿 (compiled by Qiū Jùn's pupil Jiǎng Miǎn 蔣冕) into the present 24-*juǎn* [[KR4e0115]] *Chóng-biān Qióng-tái huì-gǎo* — the late-Míng curated subset of his ancestor's writings that became the WYG base text. No further biographical detail preserved.
