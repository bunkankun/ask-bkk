Qiū Ěrgǔ 丘爾穀 (Tiānqǐ era, fl. 1620s), descendant of the Hóng-zhì-era Senior Grand Secretary **Qiū Jùn** 丘濬 ([[丘濬]]) of **Qióngzhōu**. In the Tiānqǐ era selected 20% of the Jiā-jìng-era *Lèigǎo* 類稿, augmented it with 30% of the Jiā-jìng-era *Huìgǎo* 會稿 (compiled by Zhèng Tínghú 鄭廷鵠), and combined with the original *Yíngǎo* 吟稿 (compiled by Qiū Jùn's pupil Jiǎng Miǎn 蔣冕) into the present 24-*juǎn* [[KR4e0115]] *Chóngbiān Qióngtái huìgǎo* — the late-Míng curated subset of his ancestor's writings that became the WYG base text. No further biographical detail preserved.
