# Pǔzhào 普照

Southern Sòng *cānxué sìzǔ bǐqiū* 參學嗣祖比丘, immediate disciple of [[克勤|Yuánwù Kèqín]] 圜悟克勤 present at the Jiāshān Bìyányuàn lectures; composed the 1128 preface and led the *dàoyǒu* compilation of the original [[KR6q0078]] *Bìyán lù*.
