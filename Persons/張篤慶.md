Early-Qīng poet of the Shāndōng *Yú-yáng* circle, *zì* Lì-yǒu 歷友 (also written 厯友), *hào* Kūn-lún-shān-rén 崑崙山人; native of Zī-chuān 淄川 (Shāndōng). The nephew of Wáng Shì-zhēn [[王士禎]] (his mother was Wáng Shì-zhēn's elder sister). Standard biographical sources give his lifedates as 1642–1715. A close friend of Pú Sōng-líng 蒲松齡 (1640–1715, author of *Liáozhāi zhì-yì*); the two were classmates and members of the same Zī-chuān literary society. Failed repeatedly in the Provincial Examinations and held no official post, supporting himself by tutoring. His poetic positions, recorded in juǎn 1 of Láng Tíng-huái [[郎廷槐]]'s *Shī-yǒu shī-zhuàn lù* 師友詩傳錄 ([[KR4i0059]]) alongside Wáng Shì-zhēn and Zhāng Shí-jū [[張實居]], align with Wáng's *shén-yùn* programme but show more direct engagement with Sòng poetry (especially Lù Yóu 陸游 and the Jiāng-xī school). Collections: *Kūn-lún-shān-fáng jí* 崑崙山房集.
