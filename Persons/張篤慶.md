Early-Qīng poet of the Shāndōng *Yúyáng* circle, *zì* Lìyǒu 歷友 (also written 厯友), *hào* Kūnlúnshānrén 崑崙山人; native of Zīchuān 淄川 (Shāndōng). The nephew of Wáng Shìzhēn [[王士禎]] (his mother was Wáng Shìzhēn's elder sister). Standard biographical sources give his lifedates as 1642–1715. A close friend of Pú Sōnglíng 蒲松齡 (1640–1715, author of *Liáozhāi zhìyì*); the two were classmates and members of the same Zīchuān literary society. Failed repeatedly in the Provincial Examinations and held no official post, supporting himself by tutoring. His poetic positions, recorded in juǎn 1 of Láng Tínghuái [[郎廷槐]]'s *Shīyǒu shīzhuàn lù* 師友詩傳錄 ([[KR4i0059]]) alongside Wáng Shìzhēn and Zhāng Shíjū [[張實居]], align with Wáng's *shényùn* programme but show more direct engagement with Sòng poetry (especially Lù Yóu 陸游 and the Jiāngxī school). Collections: *Kūnlúnshānfáng jí* 崑崙山房集.
