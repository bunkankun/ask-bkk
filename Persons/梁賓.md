**Liáng Bīn** 梁賓 (fl. mid-18th c., Qiánlóng era), native of Jǐ-yáng Kǎo-chéng 濟陽考城 (modern Lán-kǎo 蘭考 in Hénán) — the same county as Jiāng Yān 江淹 — is known as the editor of the *Qiánlóng* 23 (= 1758, *wù-yín*) collation of [[KR4b0015]] *Jiāng Wéntōng jí* 江文通集 in 4 *juǎn*, the *Sìkù*-WYG base text. His editorial method — collating the Wāng Shì-xián 汪士賢, Zhāng Pǔ 張溥, and Tāng Bīn 湯斌 family copies, restoring multiple omitted pieces and missing lines, recording all variants — earned high praise from the *Sìkù* compilers. CBDB does not register him; no separate biographical record survives. He should not be confused with the Yuán-period painter of the same name 梁賓 (fl. 14th c., not the same figure).
