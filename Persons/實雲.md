Shí-yún 實雲 (catalog form; the *yǔ-lù* itself reads 實雪 Shí-xuě). Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* (or *shì-zhě*) of **Xǐ-xīn Zhào-shuǐ** [[照水]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler with **Hǎi-qīng** [[海清]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0572]] 《香巖洗心水禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B459. The catalog and DILA disagree on the character (實雲 / 實雪) — likely a typesetting variant for the same person.
