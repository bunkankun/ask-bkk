Shíyún 實雲 (catalog form; the *yǔlù* itself reads 實雪 Shíxuě). Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* (or *shìzhě*) of **Xǐxīn Zhàoshuǐ** [[照水]]. Lifedates unknown. Co-compiler with **Hǎiqīng** [[海清]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0572]] 《香巖洗心水禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B459. The catalog and DILA disagree on the character (實雲 / 實雪) — likely a typesetting variant for the same person.
