Late-Míng Buddhist layman of **Hǎi-yáng** 海陽 (Cháo-zhōu, Guǎng-dōng), sobriquet **Mèng-huá** 夢華 (Dream-of-Glory). Lifedates unrecorded. His *bǔ-zhù* (supplementary annotation) layer of *Jīn-gāng jīng bǔ-zhù* 金剛經補註 ([[KR6c0057]], X24 no. 469) supplemented the *jí-jiě* compilation of [[韓巖]] (Hè-xuān jū-shì); the joint work was prefaced in **1626** (Tiānqǐ 6) by **Zhā Yìng-guāng** 查應光 (Tài-shēng jū-shì 汰生居士) and is preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng*. The text gives the work-title as a function-pair on the title-page (集解 by 韓巖; 補註 by 程衷懋), suggesting two distinct lay editorial hands rather than a single author with assistant.
