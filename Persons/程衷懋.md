Late-Míng Buddhist layman of **Hǎiyáng** 海陽 (Cháozhōu, Guǎngdōng), sobriquet **Mènghuá** 夢華 (Dream-of-Glory). Lifedates unrecorded. His *bǔzhù* (supplementary annotation) layer of *Jīngāng jīng bǔzhù* 金剛經補註 ([[KR6c0057]], X24 no. 469) supplemented the *jíjiě* compilation of [[韓巖]] (Hèxuān jūshì); the joint work was prefaced in **1626** (Tiānqǐ 6) by **Zhā Yìngguāng** 查應光 (Tàishēng jūshì 汰生居士) and is preserved in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The text gives the work-title as a function-pair on the title-page (集解 by 韓巖; 補註 by 程衷懋), suggesting two distinct lay editorial hands rather than a single author with assistant.
