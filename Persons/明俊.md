Míngjùn 明俊. Female disciple-attendant of **Yīkuí Chāochēn** [[超琛]] (1625–1679) at **Cāntóngān** 參同庵 (Méixī, Jiāxīng). Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*lù*) of her master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0553]] 《參同一揆禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B436, jointly with co-disciple [[普明]] (*biān*).
