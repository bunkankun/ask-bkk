Míng-jùn 明俊. Female disciple-attendant of **Yī-kuí Chāo-chēn** [[超琛]] (1625–1679) at **Cān-tóng-ān** 參同庵 (Méi-xī, Jiā-xīng). Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*lù*) of her master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0553]] 《參同一揆禪師語錄》 1 juan, J39 B436, jointly with co-disciple [[普明]] (*biān*).
