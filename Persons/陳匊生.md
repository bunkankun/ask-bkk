Chén Jū-shēng 陳匊生 (匊 = 掬 *jū* "to scoop with both hands") — late-Qīng physician, kinsman of the preface-writer Chén Shù-rěn 陳述忍 and member of a literati family with a transmitted medical practice. The conventional dating places him in the Tóng-zhì / Guāng-xù era (1860s–1890s). His [[KR3ep030]] *Zhěn-yú jǔ-yú lù* 診餘舉隅錄 (two *juǎn*) is a minor but representative late-Qīng provincial-literati casebook with a substantial parallel-prose preface. CBDB has no entry; biographical data beyond the work and the preface are not available.
