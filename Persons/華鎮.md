**Huà Zhèn** 華鎮 (fl. *Yuán-fēng* 1078– *Shào-xīng* c. 1140s), *zì* Ān-rén 安仁, *hào* Yún-xī jūshì 雲溪居士, of Kuài-jī 會稽 (*Shào-xīng*). *Jìnshì* of *Yuán-fēng* 2 / 1079. Held office to *Cháo-fèng dài-fū* and *Zhī Zhāng-zhōu jūn-shì* (Prefect of Zhāng-zhōu). Author of *Yún-xī jūshì jí* 雲溪居士集 [[KR4d0108]] originally in 100 *juǎn* + supplements (totalling 117 *juǎn*); also wrote *Yáng Zǐ Fǎ-yán xùn-jiě* (training-explanation of *Yáng Zǐ Fǎ-yán*) in 10 *juǎn*, *Shū shuō* in 3 *juǎn*, and the noted *Kuài-jī lǎn-gǔ shī* 會稽覽古詩 (103 poems on Kuài-jī antiquities) — the last is his most-cited piece. Career is undocumented in the *Sòng shǐ*; the principal biographical source is the lost preface by Lóu Zhào 樓炤 (preserved in the WYG recension of the *bié-jí*) and his son Huà Chū-chéng 華初成's *yuán-biǎo* of *Shào-xīng* 13 / 1143. The collection was lost from the Yuán onward; the *Sìkù* recension (30 *juǎn*) is reconstructed from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*. CBDB has no entry under this name with secure dates; lifedates remain undetermined. Known to have been associated with the Cài Jīng / Zhāng Dūn faction (per the *Sìkù* tíyào) — a political cloud that contributed to his obscurity.
