**Huà Zhèn** 華鎮 (fl. *Yuánfēng* 1078– *Shàoxīng* c. 1140s), *zì* Ānrén 安仁, *hào* Yúnxī jūshì 雲溪居士, of Kuàijī 會稽 (*Shàoxīng*). *Jìnshì* of *Yuánfēng* 2 / 1079. Held office to *Cháofèng dàifū* and *Zhī Zhāngzhōu jūnshì* (Prefect of Zhāngzhōu). Author of *Yúnxī jūshì jí* 雲溪居士集 [[KR4d0108]] originally in 100 *juǎn* + supplements (totalling 117 *juǎn*); also wrote *Yáng Zǐ Fǎyán xùnjiě* (training-explanation of *Yáng Zǐ Fǎyán*) in 10 *juǎn*, *Shū shuō* in 3 *juǎn*, and the noted *Kuàijī lǎngǔ shī* 會稽覽古詩 (103 poems on Kuàijī antiquities) — the last is his most-cited piece. Career is undocumented in the *Sòng shǐ*; the principal biographical source is the lost preface by Lóu Zhào 樓炤 (preserved in the WYG recension of the *biéjí*) and his son Huà Chūchéng 華初成's *yuánbiǎo* of *Shàoxīng* 13 / 1143. The collection was lost from the Yuán onward; the *Sìkù* recension (30 *juǎn*) is reconstructed from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. CBDB has no entry under this name with secure dates; lifedates remain undetermined. Known to have been associated with the Cài Jīng / Zhāng Dūn faction (per the *Sìkù* tíyào) — a political cloud that contributed to his obscurity.
