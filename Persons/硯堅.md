Early-Yuán literatus of Mángchéng 邙城 (Luòyáng region, Hénán). He wrote the principal doctrinal preface to [[羅天益]] Luó Qiānfǔ's *editio princeps* of [[李杲]] Lǐ Dōngyuán's [[KR3er120]] *Dōngyuán shìxiào fāng* in *Zhìyuán* 3 / 1266. The preface gives the most substantial early commentary on the work's pedagogical method, emphasising that Lǐ explicitly cautioned Luó against teaching formulae alone — students must master the *materia medica* first, and only afterwards study formula-composition. Yàn warns that the *Shìxiào fāng* should be read together with Lǐ's doctrinal works (*Yīxué fāmíng*, *Píwèi lùn*, *Nèiwài shāng biàn*, *Yàoxiàng lùn*), not as a standalone formula-collection.
