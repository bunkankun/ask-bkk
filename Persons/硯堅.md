Early-Yuán literatus of Máng-chéng 邙城 (Luò-yáng region, Hé-nán). He wrote the principal doctrinal preface to [[羅天益]] Luó Qiān-fǔ's *editio princeps* of [[李杲]] Lǐ Dōng-yuán's [[KR3er120]] *Dōng-yuán shì-xiào fāng* in *Zhì-yuán* 3 / 1266. The preface gives the most substantial early commentary on the work's pedagogical method, emphasising that Lǐ explicitly cautioned Luó against teaching formulae alone — students must master the *materia medica* first, and only afterwards study formula-composition. Yàn warns that the *Shì-xiào fāng* should be read together with Lǐ's doctrinal works (*Yī-xué fā-míng*, *Pí-wèi lùn*, *Nèi-wài shāng biàn*, *Yào-xiàng lùn*), not as a standalone formula-collection.
