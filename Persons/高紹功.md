Gāo Shàogōng 高紹功, Southern-Sòng court physician of Gāozōng's Hànlín Medical Bureau (翰林醫官局), *fl.* mid-12th century. He served as one of the four named editors of the *Shàoxīng běncǎo* 紹興本草 ([[KR3ec014]]) of 1159 under the chief editor Wáng Jìxiān [[王繼先]]. No independent biography survives; he is known only from the editorial colophon preserved in the Kunaichō manuscript of the *Shàoxīng běncǎo*.
