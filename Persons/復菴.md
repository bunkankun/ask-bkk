Fù-ān 復菴 (active late 11th – early 12th c.), Northern Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk; author of the [[KR6e0118|*Huáyán jīng lún guàn* 華嚴經綸貫]] (X220), a *lún guàn* ("through-thread") synopsis of the *Avataṃsaka*. Birth and death dates not preserved. Fù-ān is referenced in the preface to [[KR6e0016|Chéngguān's *Qī chù jiǔ huì sòng shì zhāng*]] (T1738) as the author of an alternative *Lún guàn* on the *Lotus Sūtra* — the Chán-master version that the preface-writer of T1738 found "sharp and direct" but "not convenient for those who study the doctrinal teaching." Conventional scholarship places him in the late-Northern-Sòng Chán milieu.
