**Xǔ Shìróu** 許士柔 (1587–1642) — late-Míng official of Sūjùn 蘇郡 (Suzhou). *Jìnshì* of *Tiānqǐ* 1 / 1621; entered the *Hànlín* academy as *shùjíshì* 庶吉士. Later promoted to *Hànlín shìjiǎng* 侍講 and to *Lǐbù shìláng* 禮部侍郎. He authored the publication-preface of [[皇甫中]]'s *Míngyī zhǐzhǎng* ([[KR3er053]]) in 1622 while serving as *shùjíshì* in Běijīng.
