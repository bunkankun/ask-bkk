**Xǔ Shì-róu** 許士柔 (1587–1642) — late-Míng official of Sū-jùn 蘇郡 (Suzhou). *Jìnshì* of *Tiān-qǐ* 1 / 1621; entered the *Hàn-lín* academy as *shù-jí-shì* 庶吉士. Later promoted to *Hàn-lín shì-jiǎng* 侍講 and to *Lǐ-bù shì-láng* 禮部侍郎. He authored the publication-preface of [[皇甫中]]'s *Míng-yī zhǐ-zhǎng* ([[KR3er053]]) in 1622 while serving as *shù-jí-shì* in Běijīng. CBDB id 123920.
