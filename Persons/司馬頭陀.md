# Sīmǎ tóuduó 司馬頭陀

Itinerant Táng-period *tóuduó* 頭陀 (ascetic) with a reputation for geomantic insight; in the [[KR6q0075]] *yǔlù* narrative he identifies Dàguīshān 大溈山 in Tánzhōu as a Chán monastery seat and selects [[靈祐|Wéishān Língyòu]] 溈山靈祐 as its rightful occupant.
