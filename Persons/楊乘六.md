**Yáng Chéng-liù** 楊乘六 (*zì* Yùn-hóng 蘊洪, *fl.* late 17th – early 18th c., Wū-chéng 烏程 / Hú-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng) — early-Qīng physician of the Zhè-běi 浙北 region; the principal editorial compiler of the *Yī-zōng jǐ-rèn biān* 醫宗己任編 ([[KR3er067]]), which gathers and systematises the clinical case-records and doctrinal essays of [[高斗魁]] Gāo Gǔ-fēng (1623–1670). Yáng's eight-*juǎn* edition was the first form in which Gāo's medical work reached the printed tradition. Not in CBDB.
