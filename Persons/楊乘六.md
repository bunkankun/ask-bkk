**Yáng Chéngliù** 楊乘六 (*zì* Yùnhóng 蘊洪, *fl.* late 17th – early 18th c., Wūchéng 烏程 / Húzhōu, Zhèjiāng) — early-Qīng physician of the Zhèběi 浙北 region; the principal editorial compiler of the *Yīzōng jǐrèn biān* 醫宗己任編 ([[KR3er067]]), which gathers and systematises the clinical case-records and doctrinal essays of [[高斗魁]] Gāo Gǔfēng (1623–1670). Yáng's eight-*juǎn* edition was the first form in which Gāo's medical work reached the printed tradition. Not in CBDB.
