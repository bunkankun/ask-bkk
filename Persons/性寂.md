**Shōjaku** 性寂 (fl. early 18th c.) — Japanese Shingon monk known from a colophon dated Hōei 5 (1708) recording his collation of the *Shī zhū èguǐ yǐnshí jí shuǐ fǎ* 施諸餓鬼飲食及水法 ([[KR6j0546]], T21n1315).
