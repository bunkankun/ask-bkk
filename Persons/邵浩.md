# Shào Hào 邵浩 (b. ca. 1141)

*Zì* Shū-yì 叔義. Native of Jīn-huá 金華 (modern Zhèjiāng). Southern-Sòng official: he was just under twenty in *Shào-xīng wù-yín* (1158) and a *jìnshì* in *Lóng-xīng* 1 (1163, *guǐ-wèi*); these dates suggest a birth *ca.* 1141. By *Chún-xī jǐ-yǒu* (1189) he was serving in the *jī-mù* office at Yùzhāng (Hóngzhōu). His principal scholarly work is the *Pō-mén chóu-chàng jí* 坡門酬唱集 [[KR4h0033]], a 23-*juǎn* mutual-rhyme anthology of the **Sū Shì school** (the *Sū-mén liù jūnzǐ* and the two Sūs themselves) completed at Línjiāng in *Shào-xī gēng-xū* (1190). No firm death-date; no CBDB entry.
