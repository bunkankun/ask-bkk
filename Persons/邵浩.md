# Shào Hào 邵浩 (b. ca. 1141)

*Zì* Shūyì 叔義. Native of Jīnhuá 金華 (modern Zhèjiāng). Southern-Sòng official: he was just under twenty in *Shàoxīng wùyín* (1158) and a *jìnshì* in *Lóngxīng* 1 (1163, *guǐwèi*); these dates suggest a birth *ca.* 1141. By *Chúnxī jǐyǒu* (1189) he was serving in the *jīmù* office at Yùzhāng (Hóngzhōu). His principal scholarly work is the *Pōmén chóuchàng jí* 坡門酬唱集 [[KR4h0033]], a 23-*juǎn* mutual-rhyme anthology of the **Sū Shì school** (the *Sūmén liù jūnzǐ* and the two Sūs themselves) completed at Línjiāng in *Shàoxī gēngxū* (1190). No firm death-date; no CBDB entry.
