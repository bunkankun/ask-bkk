Sūn Jì-liáng 孫季良 (fl. early 8th c.), Táng *Jí-xián diàn* 集賢殿 academician. Named in the *Xīn Táng shū · Yìwén zhì* among the seven compilers of Xú Jiān's *Chū-xué jì* 初學記 ([[KR3k0006]]) under Táng Xuán-zōng's commission of Kāi-yuán 13 (725). No independent biography in the Two Táng Histories survives. Several Sòng *bǐ-jì* sources describe him as a poet whose work was admired by Wáng Wéi 王維, but no surviving collection bears his name.
