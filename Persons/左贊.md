Míng *Chéng-huà* 成化-era official, of *Nán-chāng* (Jiāng-xī). Active editorially in the late fifteenth century. Compiled and recut the 37-*juǎn* recension of Lǐ Gòu's [[李覯]] *Xū-jiāng jí* [[KR4d0042]] from family papers, expanding the original 12-*juǎn* Sòng arrangement.
