**Lù Jiǔzhī** 陸九芝 (*běnmíng* 陸懋修 Lù Màoxiū; *zì* Jiǔzhī 九芝; *hào* Dìngpǔ 定圃; 1815–1887), late-Qīng Sūzhōu 蘇州 physician-scholar of the **Wēnbìng** 溫病 school. Active in the Dàoguāng — Guāngxù period. Author of the major *Shìbǔzhāi yīshū* 世補齋醫書 (33 *juǎn*, 1884) — a doctrinal-textual compendium combining *Shānghán lùn* commentary, *Wēnbìng* doctrine, *Sùwèn* commentary, and case-records — and of the present *Yīlín suǒyǔ* 醫林瑣語 ([[KR3eq059]]) clinical-aphoristic miscellany. His doctrinal position is integrative, defending Yè Tiānshì / Wú Jūtōng *Wēnbìng* against rigorous *Shānghán*-school orthodoxy while preserving the classical *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* clinical method as the diagnostic-therapeutic substrate. One of the principal late-Qīng transmitter-figures between Wú Jūtōng (cf. [[KR3eq041]]) and the early-Republican Mènghé school. Not in CBDB.
