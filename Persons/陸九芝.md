**Lù Jiǔ-zhī** 陸九芝 (*běn-míng* 陸懋修 Lù Mào-xiū; *zì* Jiǔ-zhī 九芝; *hào* Dìng-pǔ 定圃; 1815–1887), late-Qīng Sū-zhōu 蘇州 physician-scholar of the **Wēn-bìng** 溫病 school. Active in the Dào-guāng — Guāng-xù period. Author of the major *Shì-bǔ-zhāi yī-shū* 世補齋醫書 (33 *juǎn*, 1884) — a doctrinal-textual compendium combining *Shānghán lùn* commentary, *Wēn-bìng* doctrine, *Sùwèn* commentary, and case-records — and of the present *Yī-lín suǒ-yǔ* 醫林瑣語 ([[KR3eq059]]) clinical-aphoristic miscellany. His doctrinal position is integrative, defending Yè Tiān-shì / Wú Jū-tōng *Wēn-bìng* against rigorous *Shānghán*-school orthodoxy while preserving the classical *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* clinical method as the diagnostic-therapeutic substrate. One of the principal late-Qīng transmitter-figures between Wú Jū-tōng (cf. [[KR3eq041]]) and the early-Republican Mèng-hé school. Not in CBDB.
