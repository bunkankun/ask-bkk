Pú Dào-yuán 蒲道源 (1260–1336; catalog meta gives the death year 1366 but CBDB and the *Sìkù tíyào* together support 1336, age 76 — the catalog meta figure is followed here as a flag for inspection but the inferred 1336 is preferred), *zì* Dé-zhī 得之, *hào* Shùn-zhāi 順齋. Family origins from Qīng-shén 青神 in Méi-zhōu (Sì-chuān), relocated to Xìng-yuán 興元 (Hàn-zhōng, Shǎn-xī). Initially served as *jùn xué-zhèng* in Xìng-yuán; resigned and returned. Recommended in Huáng-qìng (1312–13) as *Guó-shǐ-yuàn biān-xiū-guān*; promoted to *Guó-zǐ bó-shì* at age 60. After a year, retired again on illness. Ten years later (Tài-dìng era) summoned as *Shǎn-xī rú-xué tí-jǔ* but declined. Lived most of his life *xián-jū* (retired) — whence the collection title *Xián-jū cóng-gǎo* [[KR4d0507]]. Huáng Jìn [[黃溍]]'s preface to the collection characterizes the Huáng-qìng-Yán-yòu era as a moment when "the prosperous court-and-pavilion prose was applied to the inner content of *xìng-lǐ zhī xué*" — Pú representing a *zhèn-pǔ* ("real and unadorned") current.
