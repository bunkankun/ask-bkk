Pú Dàoyuán 蒲道源 (1260–1336; catalog meta gives the death year 1366 but CBDB and the *Sìkù tíyào* together support 1336, age 76 — the catalog meta figure is followed here as a flag for inspection but the inferred 1336 is preferred), *zì* Dézhī 得之, *hào* Shùnzhāi 順齋. Family origins from Qīngshén 青神 in Méizhōu (Sìchuān), relocated to Xìngyuán 興元 (Hànzhōng, Shǎnxī). Initially served as *jùn xuézhèng* in Xìngyuán; resigned and returned. Recommended in Huángqìng (1312–13) as *Guóshǐyuàn biānxiūguān*; promoted to *Guózǐ bóshì* at age 60. After a year, retired again on illness. Ten years later (Tàidìng era) summoned as *Shǎnxī rúxué tíjǔ* but declined. Lived most of his life *xiánjū* (retired) — whence the collection title *Xiánjū cónggǎo* [[KR4d0507]]. Huáng Jìn [[黃溍]]'s preface to the collection characterizes the Huángqìng-Yányòu era as a moment when "the prosperous court-and-pavilion prose was applied to the inner content of *xìnglǐ zhī xué*" — Pú representing a *zhènpǔ* ("real and unadorned") current.
