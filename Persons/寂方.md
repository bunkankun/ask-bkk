Jìfāng 寂方, *zì* **Zhìqióng** 至卭 ("Reaching Qióngzhōu"); also referred to as **Zīfú Fāng** 資福方 from his teacher's monastery. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zīfú Língjī Xíngguān** [[行觀]] (1616–1681). Native of **Mǐn Fúqīng** 閩之福清 (Fújiàn); lay surname **Xǔ** 許. Lifedates unknown.

**Abbacy.** **Húzhōu Xiángfèng Zīfúsì** 湖州翔鳳資福寺 (succeeded his teacher).

**Work.** Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0562]] 《靈機禪師語錄》 6 juan, J39 B448.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 91 (X82 n1571 p. 506a22).
