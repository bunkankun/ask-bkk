# Zhào Zhēnjí 趙貞吉

Míng-dynasty official and Confucian thinker (1508–1576), posthumous title Wénsù 文肅; associated with Tàizhōu-school syncretism and the diagrammatic *Hùnyuán tú* and *Wànzì lúnxiàng tú* discussed in the preface to *Zhǔntí jìngyè* ([[KR6j0750]]).
