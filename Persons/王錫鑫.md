**Wáng Xī-xīn** 王錫鑫 (*zì* Yà-zhuō 亞拙, *hào* Yú-quán 魚泉, *fl. Dào-guāng* era, mid-19th c.), Qīng-period paediatrician of the upper-Yáng-zǐ region, active in Chóng-qìng (*Yú-chéng* 渝城). Pupil of one **Jué-lái xiān-shēng** 覺來先生. Author of *Yòu-kē qiè-yào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]]), completed in 1847 (*Dào-guāng* 27 *dīng-wèi*) at Chóng-qìng with the patronage of his Chóng-qìng friends Fù Yí-rú 傅怡如, Aì Xū-táng 艾頊堂, and Qiū Dōng-shān 邱東山. The work was prefaced by Dù Jià-lù 杜價祿 (*zì* Bīn-wǔ 賓五), a *lǐn-shēng* 廩生 (stipended licentiate) of Jiāng-yīn 江陰. No CBDB record; lifedates not securely recorded.
