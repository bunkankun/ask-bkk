**Wáng Xīxīn** 王錫鑫 (*zì* Yàzhuō 亞拙, *hào* Yúquán 魚泉, *fl. Dàoguāng* era, mid-19th c.), Qīng-period paediatrician of the upper-Yáng-zǐ region, active in Chóngqìng (*Yúchéng* 渝城). Pupil of one **Juélái xiānshēng** 覺來先生. Author of *Yòukē qièyào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]]), completed in 1847 (*Dàoguāng* 27 *dīngwèi*) at Chóngqìng with the patronage of his Chóngqìng friends Fù Yírú 傅怡如, Aì Xūtáng 艾頊堂, and Qiū Dōngshān 邱東山. The work was prefaced by Dù Jiàlù 杜價祿 (*zì* Bīnwǔ 賓五), a *lǐnshēng* 廩生 (stipended licentiate) of Jiāngyīn 江陰. No CBDB record; lifedates not securely recorded.
