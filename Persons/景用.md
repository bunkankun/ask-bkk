# Jǐng-yòng 景用

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[普寧]] Wù-ān Pǔ-níng (1200–1276); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0337]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
