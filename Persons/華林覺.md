# Huálín Jué 華林覺

Táng-period monk and *dǐzuò* 第座 under [[懷海|Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi]] 百丈懷海; failed Sīmǎ tóuduó's water-pitcher test in the Dàguīshān foundation narrative preserved in [[KR6q0075]] *Wéishān Língyòu yǔlù*.
