Late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Gǔ-yún** 古雲 (also referenced as **Gǔ-yún Cuì** 古雲粹). DILA Authority A001053. Native of **Tiáo-xī** 苕溪 (Húzhōu). Lifedates unrecorded; productive period documented from Jiā-dìng 嘉定 7 (1214) through Jiā-dìng 17 (1224) and beyond.

Dharma-disciple of **Běi-fēng Zōng-yìn** 北峰宗印 (1148–1213). In 1224 became abbot of the **Chóng-shòu-sì** 崇壽寺, where he transmitted the Tiāntái doctrinal and contemplative tradition (*shí-fāng Tiāntái jiào-guān* 十方天台教觀).

Major works:
- **Yuán-jué jīng jí-zhù** 圓覺經集註 (2 *juan*) — *Yuán-jué* (Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment) annotated commentary, with own preface dated 1214.
- **Sì-jiào yí bèi-shì** 四教儀備釋 ([[KR6d0170]], X57n0977, 2 *juan*) — Yuán-period subcommentary on Dìguān's *Tiāntái sì-jiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]]).
