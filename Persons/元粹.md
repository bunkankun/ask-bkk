Late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquet **Gǔyún** 古雲 (also referenced as **Gǔyún Cuì** 古雲粹). DILA Authority A001053. Native of **Tiáoxī** 苕溪 (Húzhōu). Lifedates unrecorded; productive period documented from Jiādìng 嘉定 7 (1214) through Jiādìng 17 (1224) and beyond.

Dharma-disciple of **Běifēng Zōngyìn** 北峰宗印 (1148–1213). In 1224 became abbot of the **Chóngshòusì** 崇壽寺, where he transmitted the Tiāntái doctrinal and contemplative tradition (*shífāng Tiāntái jiàoguān* 十方天台教觀).

Major works:
- **Yuánjué jīng jízhù** 圓覺經集註 (2 *juan*) — *Yuánjué* (Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment) annotated commentary, with own preface dated 1214.
- **Sìjiào yí bèishì** 四教儀備釋 ([[KR6d0170]], X57n0977, 2 *juan*) — Yuán-period subcommentary on Dìguān's *Tiāntái sìjiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]]).
