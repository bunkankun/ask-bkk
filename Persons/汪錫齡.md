**Wāng Xīlíng** 汪錫齡 (fl. early Qīng), *zì* **Mèngjiǔ** 夢九 — Kāngxī to early-Yōng-zhèng-era official, *Tōngyì dàifū Hénán quánshěng hédào fùshǐ* (Vice-Commissioner of River-Works for the Whole Province of Hénán). His career took him through Sìchuān (*Jiànnán*), Yúnnán (*Yǒngběi*), and Cháozhōu (*Cháo*), with sufficient encounter with the Daoist heritage of Sìchuān to bring him to Zhāng Sānfēng materials. Compiler and prefacer of [[KR5i0071]] **Zhāng Sānfēng xiānshēng quán jí**, dated **雍正元年小陽之月 = 10th lunar month, 1723** (Yōngzhèng 1). Wāng's preface narrates a personal-cult relation to Zhāng Sānfēng, with Zhāng appearing to him in dream-encounters and instructing him; the resulting compilation includes thirty-some "manifest-trace" anecdotes attributed to Zhāng. The preface is signed *gào shòu Tōngyì dàifū Hénán quánshěng hédào fùshǐ Mèngjiǔ Wāng Xīlíng*. No CBDB record located.
