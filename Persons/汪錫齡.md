**Wāng Xī-líng** 汪錫齡 (fl. early Qīng), *zì* **Mèng-jiǔ** 夢九 — Kāng-xī to early-Yōng-zhèng-era official, *Tōng-yì dài-fū Hé-nán quán-shěng hé-dào fù-shǐ* (Vice-Commissioner of River-Works for the Whole Province of Hé-nán). His career took him through Sì-chuān (*Jiàn-nán*), Yún-nán (*Yǒng-běi*), and Cháo-zhōu (*Cháo*), with sufficient encounter with the Daoist heritage of Sì-chuān to bring him to Zhāng Sān-fēng materials. Compiler and prefacer of [[KR5i0071]] **Zhāng Sān-fēng xiān-shēng quán jí**, dated **雍正元年小陽之月 = 10th lunar month, 1723** (Yōng-zhèng 1). Wāng's preface narrates a personal-cult relation to Zhāng Sān-fēng, with Zhāng appearing to him in dream-encounters and instructing him; the resulting compilation includes thirty-some "manifest-trace" anecdotes attributed to Zhāng. The preface is signed *gào shòu Tōng-yì dài-fū Hé-nán quán-shěng hé-dào fù-shǐ Mèng-jiǔ Wāng Xī-líng*. No CBDB record located.
