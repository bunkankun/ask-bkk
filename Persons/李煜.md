# Lǐ Yù 李煜

Last ruler of the Southern Táng 南唐 (937–978, r. 961–975); established the Bàocí yuàn 報慈院 where [[文益|Fǎyǎn Wényì]] 法眼文益's disciple [[言導|Xuánjué Yándǎo]] 玄覺言導 opened dharma, and presided over Wényì's posthumous re-styling as Dàzhìzàng dàdǎoshī 大智藏大導師.
