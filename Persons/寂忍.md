Jìrěn 寂忍, *zì* **Mìxíng** 密行 ("Secret Practice"); often referred to as **Kāifēng Mìxíng** 開峰密行 from his last abbacy at **Kāifēng Nányún chányuàn** 開峰南雲禪院 in Húguǎng. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Lifedates uncertain — DILA's 《疑年錄》 gives world-age 58 but no birth/death years. Native of **Yúnnán Yíliáng** 宜良; lay surname **Gǔ** 谷.

**Formation.** Tonsured at 14 at **Zhú-zǐ-shān Zhú-lín-sì** 竹子山竹林寺 under **Shùn-yǔ** 順語老師 in Yún-nán. At 17 entered **Diān-nán Yún-tái-shān** 滇南雲臺山 to bow daily before the *Śūraṅgama* title (拜楞嚴經題). At 19 the impermanence-and-death feeling stirred up; meeting a wandering Chán monk who gave him the *wú* 無 *gōng-àn*, he could find no entry. Later under **Jì-fán** 濟凡禪師 had a "rhinoceros lost its nose" (打失鼻孔) breakthrough. Followed **Yàn-jū** 燕居大師 to **Xīn-qī** 新漆 to take precepts. Sequential *cān*-fǎng under several teachers; final *fù-fǎ* at **Shuāng-guì** 雙桂 (= Pò-shān Hǎi-míng) in **順治丙申** = **1656**.

**Abbacies.** **Xùzhōu Xǐyù** 敘州喜獄, **Luòyuán Xīngguó** 雒源興國 (entered **順治丁酉** = **1657**), **Bǎoqìng Dàbēi** 寶慶大悲 (Húnán), **Kāifēng Nányún chányuàn** 開峰南雲禪院 (Húguǎng / Héngzhōu — the seat from which his Yúnnán dharma-heir [[學蘊]] received instructions, cf. [[KR6q0523]]).

**Disciples (twenty-two listed in the *Jǐnjiāng chándēng*).** Major figures include **Sìdēng Yuányìn** 嗣燈圓胤, **Xīnzhuō Yuánhuái** 心拙圓懷, **Hánzhuō Chéngzǔ** 含拙成祖, **Zhīkōng Xuéyùn** 知空學蘊 [[學蘊]] (author of [[KR6q0523]]), **Wàndé Fókāi** 萬德佛開, and others.

**Work.** [[KR6q0545]] 《密行忍禪師語錄》 3 juan, J38 B428, compiled by *fǎsūn* (dharma-grandsons) **Míngguǎng** [[明廣]] and **Míngzhēn** [[明珍]] *děng biān*; preface by **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] of Qīngchéngshān (cf. [[KR6q0544]]) dated **康熙甲戌蒲月** = **1694** (Kāngxī 33, lunar 5).

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 10; 《密行忍禪師語錄》 vol. 3 〈行實〉; 《新續高僧傳四集》 vol. 21.
