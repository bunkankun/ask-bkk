**Sūn Jìng'ān** 孫靜庵 is the compiler-editor (*biān* 編) of *Jīnwū Mèng* 金屋夢 ([[KR4k0155]]). According to the novel's own editorial note (*shíyǔ* 識語), Sūn purchased an old manuscript attributed to "Mèng Bǐ Shēng" 夢筆生 from a book dealer, lightly edited it, and published it. No independent biographical record for Sūn Jìng'ān has been found in CBDB or standard Qīng sources. No confident CBDB match exists.
