Jì-wù 濟悟, *hào* **Hè-fēng** 鶴峰 ("Crane Peak"), also **Hè-shān** 鶴山 / **Lóng-tán Hè-fēng** 龍潭鶴峰 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Líng-yǐn Jù-dé Hóng-lǐ** 靈隱具德弘禮 (1600–1667). Born **明天啟丙寅 9/20** = **1626-11-08** Gregorian; died **康熙丁卯 9/11** = **1687-10-16**, age 62, *jiè-là* 44. Native of **Wú-mén** 吳門 (i.e. Wú-xiàn / Sūzhōu); lay surname **Fèi** 費.

**Formation.** Wished to leave home from age 12; tonsured at **Sōng-líng Luó-hàn-sì** 松陵羅漢寺 under **Ruì-zhī Guāng** 瑞芝光律師. Took full ordination at age 20 under **Pōu-shí** 萬峰剖石. *Cān*-fǎng under **Líng-yǐn Hóng-lǐ** on the *běn-lái miàn-mù* 本來面目 *gōng-àn* — one evening, seeing a lamp-flower spark, had an opening. Later, while sitting *qī* with **Bò-ān** 蘗菴和尚 at **Dà-bēi-táng** 大悲堂, was overheard receiving a *jǐng-cè* (warning slap) from his teacher *Jù lǎo-rén* 具老人 to a fellow monk and broke through. Final *fù-fǎ* under Hóng-lǐ.

**Abbacies (six).** **Lóng-tán Fú-shàn-sì** 龍潭福善寺, **Gū-sū Bǎo-shù-sì** 姑蘇寶樹寺, **Dāng-hú Zhú-yǐn-sì** 當湖竺隱寺, **Tóng-xī Jì-zhào-sì** 桐溪寂照寺, **Pú-shuǐ Fú-shàn-sì** 濮水福善寺, **Fú-shòu chán-lín** 福壽禪林.

**Sole *fǎ-sì*.** [[上震]] *Shàng-zhèn*.

**Work.** [[KR6q0540]] 《鶴峰禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B421, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Shàng-zhèn** [[上震]] *děng biān*; with three prefaces dated **康熙壬申** = **1692** (by 安山同門弟 濟日 + 法弟仲弘道) and **康熙乙亥** = **1695** (by 靈隱同門弟 慧輅).

**Sources.** 《鶴峰禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈行狀〉, 〈塔銘〉.
