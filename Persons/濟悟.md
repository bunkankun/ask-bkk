Jìwù 濟悟, *hào* **Hèfēng** 鶴峰 ("Crane Peak"), also **Hèshān** 鶴山 / **Lóngtán Hèfēng** 龍潭鶴峰 from his abbacy. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Língyǐn Jùdé Hónglǐ** 靈隱具德弘禮 (1600–1667). Born **明天啟丙寅 9/20** = **1626-11-08** Gregorian; died **康熙丁卯 9/11** = **1687-10-16**, age 62, *jièlà* 44. Native of **Wúmén** 吳門 (i.e. Wúxiàn / Sūzhōu); lay surname **Fèi** 費.

**Formation.** Wished to leave home from age 12; tonsured at **Sōnglíng Luóhànsì** 松陵羅漢寺 under **Ruìzhī Guāng** 瑞芝光律師. Took full ordination at age 20 under **Pōushí** 萬峰剖石. *Cān*-fǎng under **Língyǐn Hónglǐ** on the *běnlái miànmù* 本來面目 *gōngàn* — one evening, seeing a lamp-flower spark, had an opening. Later, while sitting *qī* with **Bòān** 蘗菴和尚 at **Dàbēitáng** 大悲堂, was overheard receiving a *jǐngcè* (warning slap) from his teacher *Jù lǎorén* 具老人 to a fellow monk and broke through. Final *fùfǎ* under Hónglǐ.

**Abbacies (six).** **Lóngtán Fúshànsì** 龍潭福善寺, **Gūsū Bǎoshùsì** 姑蘇寶樹寺, **Dānghú Zhúyǐnsì** 當湖竺隱寺, **Tóngxī Jìzhàosì** 桐溪寂照寺, **Púshuǐ Fúshànsì** 濮水福善寺, **Fúshòu chánlín** 福壽禪林.

**Sole *fǎsì*.** [[上震]] *Shàngzhèn*.

**Work.** [[KR6q0540]] 《鶴峰禪師語錄》 2 juan, J38 B421, compiled by *fǎsì* **Shàngzhèn** [[上震]] *děng biān*; with three prefaces dated **康熙壬申** = **1692** (by 安山同門弟 濟日 + 法弟仲弘道) and **康熙乙亥** = **1695** (by 靈隱同門弟 慧輅).

**Sources.** 《鶴峰禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈行狀〉, 〈塔銘〉.
