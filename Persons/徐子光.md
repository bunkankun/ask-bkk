Xú Zǐguāng 徐子光 (fl. 12th c.) was a Northern–Southern Sòng commentator known only for his *Méngqiú jízhù* 蒙求集註 ([[KR3k0010]]), the *Collected Notes* on Lǐ Hàn's foundational rhymed primer of historical *gùshí*. Chén Zhènsūn's *Zhízhāi shūlù jiětí* records the commentary in 8 *juan*; later editions consolidated it to 2 *juan*. No biographical material on Xú survives in the *Sòng shǐ* or in *bǐjì* literature; his native place and offices are unknown. The *Sìkù tíyào* critiques several specific slips in his notes — citing Zhū Yì's *Yījué liáo zájì* — but concludes that overall the work is broadly conversant and serves as a "ferry-raft for beginners".
