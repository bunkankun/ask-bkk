Bǎoguì 寶貴 (DILA Authority A001927; Suí period, fl. 597) was a śramaṇa of the Dà-xīngshàn-sì 大興善寺 in Cháng'ān during the reign of the founding Sui emperor Wén-dì 文帝. The DILA authority record cross-references his entry in Dàoxuān's *Xù gāosēng zhuàn* 續高僧傳 (T2060) under the biography of 道安 (the early Sui Dào'ān, distinct from the famous fourth-century master), where 寶貴 is identified as a disciple of that Dào'ān. He is remembered for a single editorial undertaking of major significance: the *Hé-bù Jīn-guāngmíng jīng* 合部金光明經 in eight fascicles ([[KR6i0302]], T664), which he assembled in Kāihuáng 開皇 17 (597) by inserting four chapters from the supplementary translations of Paramārtha and Yaśogupta into [[曇無讖]]'s shorter Northern Liáng version. The composite recension circulated alongside Dharmakṣema's text until it was finally superseded by [[義淨]]'s long translation of 703.
