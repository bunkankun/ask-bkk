Xìngyǎng 性養, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Tiěméi Huìlì** [[慧麗]] 鐵眉慧麗 (1586–1650) in the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派. Co-compiler with **Xìngliàng** [[性亮]] of the 1-juan 《鐵眉三巴掌禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0452]]), signing the colophon as *sìfǎsūn Xìngyǎng Xìngliàng tóng biān* 嗣法孫性養性亮仝編. The *xìng* generation indicates Xìngyǎng was an heir of one of Huìlì's *juéshēng* dharma-heirs (the closing 行狀 names **Xióngěrān** 熊耳菴 as the bowl-and-robe inheritor). No independent biography or *yǔlù* located.
