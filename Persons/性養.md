Xìng-yǎng 性養, *xìng*-generation Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Tiě-méi Huì-lì** [[慧麗]] 鐵眉慧麗 (1586–1650) in the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派. Co-compiler with **Xìng-liàng** [[性亮]] of the 1-juan 《鐵眉三巴掌禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0452]]), signing the colophon as *sì-fǎ-sūn Xìng-yǎng Xìng-liàng tóng biān* 嗣法孫性養性亮仝編. The *xìng* generation indicates Xìng-yǎng was an heir of one of Huì-lì's *jué-shēng* dharma-heirs (the closing 行狀 names **Xióng-ěr-ān** 熊耳菴 as the bowl-and-robe inheritor). No independent biography or *yǔ-lù* located.
