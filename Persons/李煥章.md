# 李煥章

Lǐ Huànzhāng (*zì* Xiàngxiān 象先), born 1614, of Qiānshèng 千乘 (Shāndōng). Late-Míng / early-Qīng literatus and prefacer; supplied the second preface to [[KR3em004]] *Yǎnkē mìjué* 眼科秘訣, vouching for the work's pseudepigraphic Sūn-Zhēnrén transmission.
