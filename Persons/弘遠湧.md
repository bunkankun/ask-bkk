# Hóngyuǎn Yǒng 弘遠湧

An early-Qīng visiting Dharma-master "of the East", described as deeply skilled in *zōng* and *shuō*, who joined in supporting publication of [[續法]] Xùfǎ's *Guānzìzài púsà rúyìxīn tuóluóní jīng lüèshū* ([[KR6j0737]]).
