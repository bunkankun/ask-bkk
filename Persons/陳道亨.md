Chén Dàohēng 陳道亨 (1542–1616), *zì* Yuánshēng 元升, *hào* Huánjiāng 環江, of Xīnjiàn 新建 in Jiāngxī. Wànlì-era *jìnshì* (1577); rose to *Bīngbù shàngshū* 兵部尚書 (Minister of War). With [[劉日升]] Liú Rìshēng, collated and cut to woodblocks the late-Wànlì print of [[楊簡]] Yáng Jiǎn's *Yángshì Yì zhuàn* ([[KR1a0037]]) that became the *Sìkù* base-text.
