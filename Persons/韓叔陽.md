**Hán Shūyáng** 韓叔陽 (*fl.* early 15th c.) was an early-Míng physician of the Wú-medical lineage descending from [[戴原禮]] Dài Yuánlǐ via [[王賓]] Wáng Bīn; with [[盛啟東]] Shèng Qǐdōng he transmitted Dānxī ([[朱震亨]]) doctrine into the early-Míng imperial 御藥院, the institutional setting invoked in [[顧從德]] Gù Cóngdé's postface to the 1550 facsimile of the Sòng校正 *Sùwèn* ([[KR3ea001]]).
