**Cháo Qiān-zhī** 晁謙之 (fl. early–mid Southern Sòng), of the Jù-yě 鉅野 Cháo lineage. Compiled and printed the *Jī-lèi jí* 雞肋集 [[KR4d0105]] of his clan-uncle Cháo Bǔ-zhī 晁補之 [[晁補之]] in 70 *juǎn* during the *Shào-xīng* period — the textual basis for the surviving recension. Held minor office; precise dates and biography are not securely established beyond his role as family editor.
