**Zhāng Guóxiáng** 張國祥 (*d.* 1611) was the **50th Celestial Master** (五十代天師) of the Lóng hǔ shān Zhèng yī lineage, chief Daoist figure during the Wàn lì reign, and general editor of the **Wàn lì Xù Dào zàng** 萬曆續道藏 (1607). Author of [[KR5h0032]] *Hàn tiān shī shì jiā* 漢天師世家 — the official genealogy of the Celestial Masters lineage from Zhāng Dàolíng to his own time.
