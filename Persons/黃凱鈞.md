**Huáng Kǎijūn** 黃凱鈞 (*zì* **Hètíng** 鶴亭, *hào* **Yǒuyú** 友漁), Qīng physician of **Jiāshàn** 嘉善 (Zhèjiāng), active in the Jiāqìng — early Dàoguāng era (c. 1790s–1820s by internal evidence — citing late-Qián-lóng ginseng-price collapse, the 1820s *Tuìān shīchāo* 退庵詩鈔, and a clinical career past middle age). Author of the **[[KR3eq007]]** *Yǒuyúzhāi yīhuà* 友漁齋醫話 (eight *zhǒng* 種 in approximately eight *juǎn*) — a collected handbook of clinical-theoretical reflections including the **practical materia-medica** treatise *Yàolóng xiǎopǐn* 藥籠小品, the **pulse-and-diagnosis** discussion *Lùnmài xiǎopǐn* 論脈小品, and a series of department-specific sketches (paediatrics, gynaecology, *shānghán* practice, case-records). Doctrinally a temperate Wēn-bìng-school inheritor close to Yè Tiānshì [[葉桂]]; his pharmacopoeial reflections are notable for treating drugs in the *zhìrén* "biographical-sketch" mode rather than the orthodox channel-classification grid. Not in CBDB.
