Ráo Jièzhī 饒介之 (Yuán), son-in-law of Dīng Fù [[丁復]] and compiler of the *Kuàitíngjí qiánjí* 檜亭集前集 [[KR4d0499]]. Confined to this editorial role in the surviving record; the *Sìkù tíyào* documents his collection-work but provides no further biographical detail. Not to be confused with the better-attested Yuán-Míng calligrapher Ráo Jiè 饒介 (d. 1367), Línchuān, whose given name is single 介; the *zhī* 之 is in this case the second character of a two-character given name. Independent identification is hampered by absent CBDB and *Yuánshǐ* records.
