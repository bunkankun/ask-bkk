**Zhào Qíngchū** 趙晴初, late-Qīng physician of the Hángzhōu medical circle (active in the Guāngxù era), author of **[[KR3eq023]]** *Cúncúnzhāi yīhuà gǎo* 存存齋醫話稿 (4 *juǎn*) — a late-Qīng *yīhuà* in the *Wēnbìng*-school tradition. The 1936 *Zhōngguó yīxué dàchéng* recension adds Shěn Zhòngguī's *Wúshān sǎnjì* 吳山散記 supplement. Not in CBDB; not in the standard biographical dictionaries available locally. The studio-name 存存齋 ("Cúncún Studio") echoes the Yìjīng 易經 *jǐcí* 繫辭 phrase 存存者 "those who preserve-and-preserve" — a conventional late-Qīng *wénrén* studio-name.
