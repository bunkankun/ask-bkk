**Zhào Qíng-chū** 趙晴初, late-Qīng physician of the Hángzhōu medical circle (active in the Guāng-xù era), author of **[[KR3eq023]]** *Cún-cún-zhāi yī-huà gǎo* 存存齋醫話稿 (4 *juǎn*) — a late-Qīng *yī-huà* in the *Wēn-bìng*-school tradition. The 1936 *Zhōng-guó yī-xué dà-chéng* recension adds Shěn Zhòng-guī's *Wú-shān sǎn-jì* 吳山散記 supplement. Not in CBDB; not in the standard biographical dictionaries available locally. The studio-name 存存齋 ("Cún-cún Studio") echoes the Yìjīng 易經 *jǐ-cí* 繫辭 phrase 存存者 "those who preserve-and-preserve" — a conventional late-Qīng *wén-rén* studio-name.
