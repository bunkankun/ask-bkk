# 梶井元鴻

Kajii Genkō of Nagasaki 崎陽, late-Edo Japanese kanpō physician and disciple of [[本荘普一]] Honjō Fuichi. Author of the closing colophon to [[KR3em005]] *Yǎnkē jǐnnáng* 眼科錦囊.
