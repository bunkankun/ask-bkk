# 熊希齡 Xióng Xīlíng

**Xióng Xīlíng** 熊希齡 (*zì* Bǐngsān 秉三, 1870–1937), prominent Húnán late-Qīng / Republican-era politician and reformer; Premier of the Republic of China (1913–14). Closely associated with the 1898 Húnán reform movement and the Nánxuéhuì 南學會 circle. Wrote the 1927 colophon to [[譚志光]] Tán Zhìguāng's *Zhēnjiǔ wèndá* 針灸問答 ([[KR3ee035]]).
