Zhāng Jiǎn 張儉 (Míng, fl. Jiā-jìng *xīn-mǎo* = 1531), co-editor (with Yú Shǒu-yú 虞守愚) of the Jiā-jìng-era *Wén-xiàn jí* 文獻集 [[KR4d0502]] of Huáng Jìn [[黃溍]]. His preface laments the defective state of the older recension and the inclusion of materials Huáng would not have endorsed; the recension he prepared from a "good copy at the family seat" together with Huáng's *Rì-sǔn-zhāi bǐ-jì* [[KR3j0058]] was printed at Jiàn-ǒu under *xiàn-yǐn* Shěn Bì 沈璧 and Chén Guī 陳珪. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes that the present 10-*juǎn* WYG block, which carries his name at the *juǎn*-head, was further-edited by Zhāng Wéi-shū 張維樞 and Wáng Tíng-zēng 王廷曾, and is therefore not literally Zhāng Jiǎn's recension. No CBDB id and no separate biography traced.
