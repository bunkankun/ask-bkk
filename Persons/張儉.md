Zhāng Jiǎn 張儉 (Míng, fl. Jiājìng *xīnmǎo* = 1531), co-editor (with Yú Shǒuyú 虞守愚) of the Jiā-jìng-era *Wénxiàn jí* 文獻集 [[KR4d0502]] of Huáng Jìn [[黃溍]]. His preface laments the defective state of the older recension and the inclusion of materials Huáng would not have endorsed; the recension he prepared from a "good copy at the family seat" together with Huáng's *Rìsǔnzhāi bǐjì* [[KR3j0058]] was printed at Jiànǒu under *xiànyǐn* Shěn Bì 沈璧 and Chén Guī 陳珪. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes that the present 10-*juǎn* WYG block, which carries his name at the *juǎn*-head, was further-edited by Zhāng Wéishū 張維樞 and Wáng Tíngzēng 王廷曾, and is therefore not literally Zhāng Jiǎn's recension. No CBDB id and no separate biography traced.
