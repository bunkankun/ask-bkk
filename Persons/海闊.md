Hǎikuò 海闊, LínjìYángqí dharma-disciple of **Zǐsù Chāoyuǎn** [[超遠]] 子肅超遠 (1631–1689). Co-recorder with **Xìngmíng** [[性明]] of the 1-juan 《終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0521]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
