Hǎi-kuò 海闊, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Zǐ-sù Chāo-yuǎn** [[超遠]] 子肅超遠 (1631–1689). Co-recorder with **Xìng-míng** [[性明]] of the 1-juan 《終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0521]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
