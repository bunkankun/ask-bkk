# 延慶 Enkei

Japanese Tendai 天台 monk who travelled to Sòng China to study with [[法照]] Huìyán Fǎzhào at Hángzhōu Shàngtiānzhúsì. Together with [[海順]] Kaijun, he had Fǎzhào's portrait painted and brought it back to Japan, where Fǎzhào's *Fǎhuá jīng sāndàbù dújiào jì* ([[KR6d0062]]) was subsequently studied in the medieval Tendai tradition.
