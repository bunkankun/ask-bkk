**Chén Shǒuzhēn** 陳守真, Republican-period paediatrician of Mǐnhóu 閩侯 (Fújiàn), active in Hànkǒu 漢口 (Wǔhàn). Author of *Érkē cuìjīng* 兒科萃精 ([[KR3ej012]]), an eight-juan paediatric textbook completed in October 1929 (民國十八年十月) at his medical office in the French Concession of Hànkǒu (漢口法租界三德里之醫室), prefaced in December 1929 by Lǐ Bórén 李博仁, then *Hànkǒu tèbiéshì wèishēngjú zhǎng* 漢口特別市衛生局長 (Director of the Hànkǒu Special Municipality Public Health Bureau). His preface frames the work as a popular-pedagogical paediatric reference; he criticises both *gǔfāng pài* 古方派 antiquarian rigidity and contemporary *qiáng* 剽 (plagiaristic) practice.
