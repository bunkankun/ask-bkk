**Chén Shǒuzhēn** 陳守真, Republican-period paediatrician of Mǐn-hóu 閩侯 (Fú-jiàn), active in Hàn-kǒu 漢口 (Wǔ-hàn). Author of *Ér-kē cuì-jīng* 兒科萃精 ([[KR3ej012]]), an eight-juan paediatric textbook completed in October 1929 (民國十八年十月) at his medical office in the French Concession of Hàn-kǒu (漢口法租界三德里之醫室), prefaced in December 1929 by Lǐ Bórén 李博仁, then *Hàn-kǒu tè-bié-shì wèi-shēng-jú zhǎng* 漢口特別市衛生局長 (Director of the Hàn-kǒu Special Municipality Public Health Bureau). His preface frames the work as a popular-pedagogical paediatric reference; he criticises both *gǔ-fāng pài* 古方派 antiquarian rigidity and contemporary *qiáng* 剽 (plagiaristic) practice. No CBDB record; lifedates not securely documented.
