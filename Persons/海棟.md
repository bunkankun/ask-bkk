Hǎidòng 海棟, *hào* **Fǎzhù** 法柱 ("Pillar of the Dharma"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Chángmíng Xìngjiǒng** [[性炅]] (?–1668). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Lǐzhōu Shímén** 澧洲石門 (Húnán); lay surname **Chén** 陳.

**Formation.** Left home at **25** out of grief that he could not repay parental kindness; tonsured at **Cílì Qīxīngshān** 慈利七星山 under his teacher Chángmíng. Three years' close training on the *kōan* "三不是". Took full ordination at **Lóngshān** under **Yǐngxiù** 穎秀和尚, then accompanied Chángmíng to **Yuánzhōu Míngshān** 沅州明山, attending him with *jīnpíng* (towel-and-jar service) for seven years and receiving *jìbié* (sealing).

**Abbacy.** **Chénzhōufǔ Tiānyúnshān Pǔfēngsì** 辰州府天雲山浦峰寺.

**Works.**
- Compiler of his teacher's *yǔlù*: [[KR6q0524]] 《浦峰長明炅禪師語錄》 1 juan, J37 B401.
- Own *yǔlù*: [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 2 juan, J37 B402.

**Disciples.** Twelve *fǎsì* including **Shūlún Xiāng** 殊倫相, **Chóngjiàn Shèng** 崇鑑晟, **Duànyí Zhì** 斷疑智.
