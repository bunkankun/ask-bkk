Hǎi-dòng 海棟, *hào* **Fǎ-zhù** 法柱 ("Pillar of the Dharma"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Cháng-míng Xìng-jiǒng** [[性炅]] (?–1668). Lifedates unknown. Native of **Lǐ-zhōu Shí-mén** 澧洲石門 (Húnán); lay surname **Chén** 陳.

**Formation.** Left home at **25** out of grief that he could not repay parental kindness; tonsured at **Cí-lì Qī-xīng-shān** 慈利七星山 under his teacher Cháng-míng. Three years' close training on the *kōan* "三不是". Took full ordination at **Lóng-shān** under **Yǐng-xiù** 穎秀和尚, then accompanied Cháng-míng to **Yuán-zhōu Míng-shān** 沅州明山, attending him with *jīn-píng* (towel-and-jar service) for seven years and receiving *jì-bié* (sealing).

**Abbacy.** **Chén-zhōu-fǔ Tiān-yún-shān Pǔ-fēng-sì** 辰州府天雲山浦峰寺.

**Works.**
- Compiler of his teacher's *yǔ-lù*: [[KR6q0524]] 《浦峰長明炅禪師語錄》 1 juan, J37 B401.
- Own *yǔ-lù*: [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 2 juan, J37 B402.

**Disciples.** Twelve *fǎ-sì* including **Shū-lún Xiāng** 殊倫相, **Chóng-jiàn Shèng** 崇鑑晟, **Duàn-yí Zhì** 斷疑智.
