Late-Qīng physician, otherwise undocumented in standard biographical sources. Sole identified work is [[KR3er117]] *Jiǎn-míng Zhōng-Xī huì-cān yī-xué tú-shuō* 簡明中西匯參醫學圖說, a late-Qīng integrative medical treatise in the *Zhōng-Xī huì-tōng* 中西匯通 (Sino-Western integration) tradition. The work is held in modern Chinese collections including the Academia Sinica Institute of History and Philology Medical-History Research Room. Not in CBDB.
