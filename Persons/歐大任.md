Mid-Míng Cantonese scholar from Shùndé 順德 in Guǎngdōng, courtesy name Zhēnbó 楨伯. CBDB id 33901 attests his lifedates as 1516–1595. He was elected *suìgòng* in *Jiājìng rénxū* (1562) and held a series of educational offices: *Jiāngdū xùndǎo* 江都訓導, *Guāngzhōu xuézhèng* 光州學正, *Guózǐxué bóshì* 國子博士, ending as *Nánjīng Hùbù lángzhōng* 南京戶部郎中. He was a member of the *Nányuán wǔzǐ* 南園五子 poets' group of Cantonese literati and a leading Cantonese cultural figure of his generation. He is principally known for the *Bǎiyuè xiānxián zhì* 百越先賢志 ([[KR2g0040]]) — a 4-*juàn* / 120-man collective biography of the worthies of the entire *Bǎiyuè* (southern coastal China) region from the Eastern Hàn down, completed in *Jiājìng jiǎyín* (1554) and printed in *Wànlì rénchén* (1592).
