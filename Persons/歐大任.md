Mid-Míng Cantonese scholar from Shùn-dé 順德 in Guǎng-dōng, courtesy name Zhēn-bó 楨伯. CBDB id 33901 attests his lifedates as 1516–1595. He was elected *suì-gòng* in *Jiā-jìng rén-xū* (1562) and held a series of educational offices: *Jiāng-dū xùn-dǎo* 江都訓導, *Guāng-zhōu xué-zhèng* 光州學正, *Guó-zǐ-xué bó-shì* 國子博士, ending as *Nán-jīng Hù-bù láng-zhōng* 南京戶部郎中. He was a member of the *Nán-yuán wǔ-zǐ* 南園五子 poets' group of Cantonese literati and a leading Cantonese cultural figure of his generation. He is principally known for the *Bǎi-yuè xiān-xián zhì* 百越先賢志 ([[KR2g0040]]) — a 4-*juàn* / 120-man collective biography of the worthies of the entire *Bǎi-yuè* (southern coastal China) region from the Eastern Hàn down, completed in *Jiā-jìng jiǎ-yín* (1554) and printed in *Wàn-lì rén-chén* (1592).
