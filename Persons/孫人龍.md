# Sūn Rénlóng 孫人龍

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīng-diàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as the *kǎozhèng* compiler for several of the Six-Dynasties *zhèngshǐ* in the *Sìkù quánshū*: the *Jìnshū* (KR2a0015), the *Liáng shū* (KR2a0018), the *Chénshū* (KR2a0019), the *Wèishū* (KR2a0020), and others. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
