Xún Yuè 荀悅 (148–209), *zì* Zhòngyù 仲豫, was a late-Eastern-Hàn classicist of Yǐng-yīn 潁陰 (Yǐngchuān 潁川). Nephew of Xún Shuǎng 荀爽 (128–190) and cousin of Xún Yù 荀彧 (163–212), he served Xiàndì 獻帝 (r. 189–220) as Palace Attendant Lecturer (*shìjiǎng* 侍講). His biography is *Hòu Hàn shū* j. 62 (荀韓鍾陳列傳). According to the *Hòu Hàn shū*, observing power passing into Cáo hands and finding his counsel disregarded, he composed the *Shēn jiàn* 申鑒 in five 篇 (KR3a0011) and presented it to the throne. He is also the author of the major work of late-Eastern-Hàn historiography, the *Hàn jì* 漢紀 in thirty juan, a chronologically-arranged condensation of the *Hàn shū* commissioned by Xiàndì in Jiàn'ān 3 (198) and completed in 200.
