Lú Qí 盧琦 (d. 1362), *zì* Xīhán 希韓, *hào* Lìzhāi 立齋. Native of Huìān 惠安 (Quánzhōu, Fújiàn). Guīfēng 圭峯 was the place where he lived (a small hill near Huìān). Zhìzhèng 2 (1342) *jìnshì*; appointed *Dàxiàozhōu lùshì*. Transferred *Yǒngchūn xiànyǐn*; reassigned *Níngdé xiànyǐn*; rose through *Cáosī tíjǔ*; appointed *Píngyángzhōu* — died before taking up the post. Career in *Yuánshǐ liánglì zhuàn*. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes that the modern Huìān edition of the *Guīfēng jí* (by *Zhuāng Hùbù Zhēngfǔ* of Huìān) had erroneously folded in 60+ pieces by Sàdūlā [[薩都拉]]; the *Sìkùguǎn* edition by Chén Chéngzhōng 陳誠中 (Wànlì early, ed. Zhū Yīlóng 朱一龍, Dǒng Yìngjǔ 董應舉) was earlier but already heavily corrupted; the *Sìkùguǎn* deleted 32 confirmed Sàdūlā misattributions to restore the genuine Lú corpus.
