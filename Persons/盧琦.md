Lú Qí 盧琦 (d. 1362), *zì* Xī-hán 希韓, *hào* Lì-zhāi 立齋. Native of Huì-ān 惠安 (Quán-zhōu, Fú-jiàn). Guī-fēng 圭峯 was the place where he lived (a small hill near Huì-ān). Zhì-zhèng 2 (1342) *jìnshì*; appointed *Dà-xiào-zhōu lù-shì*. Transferred *Yǒng-chūn xiàn-yǐn*; reassigned *Níng-dé xiàn-yǐn*; rose through *Cáo-sī tí-jǔ*; appointed *Píng-yáng-zhōu* — died before taking up the post. Career in *Yuán-shǐ liáng-lì zhuàn*. The *Sìkù tíyào* notes that the modern Huì-ān edition of the *Guī-fēng jí* (by *Zhuāng Hù-bù Zhēng-fǔ* of Huì-ān) had erroneously folded in 60+ pieces by Sà-dū-lā [[薩都拉]]; the *Sìkù-guǎn* edition by Chén Chéng-zhōng 陳誠中 (Wàn-lì early, ed. Zhū Yī-lóng 朱一龍, Dǒng Yìng-jǔ 董應舉) was earlier but already heavily corrupted; the *Sìkù-guǎn* deleted 32 confirmed Sà-dū-lā misattributions to restore the genuine Lú corpus.
