Fǎ-xiù 法秀, *hào* **Yì-táng** 寱堂. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fó-yǔ Chè-yù** [[徹御]] 佛語徹御 (d. 1674) at **Chóng-qìng Xiāng-guó-sì** 重慶香國寺. Sole named *fǎ-sì* of Chè-yù in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Co-compiler with **Fǎ-jié** [[法傑]] of the 1-juan 《佛語御禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0487]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* located.
