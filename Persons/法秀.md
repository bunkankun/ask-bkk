Fǎxiù 法秀, *hào* **Yìtáng** 寱堂. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fóyǔ Chèyù** [[徹御]] 佛語徹御 (d. 1674) at **Chóngqìng Xiāngguósì** 重慶香國寺. Sole named *fǎsì* of Chèyù in the Pòshān-Sìchuān sub-line. Co-compiler with **Fǎjié** [[法傑]] of the 1-juan 《佛語御禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0487]]). No independent *yǔlù* located.
