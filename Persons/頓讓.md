# Dùnràng 頓讓

Early-Qīng Chán monk styled Shuāngjì 雙髻 ("twin-topknot"); responsible for reviewing for print the *Dìwáng wèndào lù* fascicle ([[KR6q0052]]) excerpted from Guō Níngzhī's *Xiānjué zōngshèng* and issued separately in the Jiāxīng canon, ca. 1665.
