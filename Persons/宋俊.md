Sòng Jùn 宋俊 (*fl.* late 17th c.), *zì* Cháng-bái 長白; native of Shàn-yīn 山陰 in Shào-xīng (Zhèjiāng). Early-Qīng minor scholar; supplied 7 supplementary entries to Wú Qǐ's *Lǐng-nán fēng-wù jì* 嶺南風物記 ([[KR2k0129]]), most notably the discussion of Mǐ Fú's prized stones — distinguishing the original *tuō-shā* of Hán-kuāng county from the later "*Yīng-dé shí*" sold under that name after Hán-kuāng was merged into Yīng-dé prefecture. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 475707 has no birth or death year.
