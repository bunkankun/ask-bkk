Sòng Jùn 宋俊 (*fl.* late 17th c.), *zì* Chángbái 長白; native of Shànyīn 山陰 in Shàoxīng (Zhèjiāng). Early-Qīng minor scholar; supplied 7 supplementary entries to Wú Qǐ's *Lǐngnán fēngwù jì* 嶺南風物記 ([[KR2k0129]]), most notably the discussion of Mǐ Fú's prized stones — distinguishing the original *tuōshā* of Hánkuāng county from the later "*Yīngdé shí*" sold under that name after Hánkuāng was merged into Yīngdé prefecture. Otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 475707 has no birth or death year.
