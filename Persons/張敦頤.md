Zhāng Dūn-yí 張敦頤 (*fl. Shào-xīng*; *jìnshì* 1138, CBDB 15495 with index year 1108), *zì* Yǎng-zhèng 養正, native of Wù-yuán 婺源 in Huī-zhōu (modern Jiāng-xī). Southern-Sòng official, philologist, and historian. *Shào-xīng* 8 *jìnshì*; friend of Zhū Sōng 朱松 (Zhū Xī's father). Held office as Education-Officer of Nán-jiàn-zhōu (1142–1148), Prefect of Shū-zhōu, Prefect of Héng-zhōu, and *Zuǒ fèng-yì láng chōng Jiāng-nán dōng-lù ān-fǔ-sī gàn-bàn gōng-shì* (in 1160, when he composed the present work); retired on pension thereafter. Author of: the *Hán Liǔ wén yīn-biàn* 韓柳文音辨 — a phonological-philological commentary on the prose of Hán Yù and Liǔ Zōng-yuán; the *Liù-cháo shìjì biān-lèi* 六朝事跡編類 ([[KR2k0112]]) on the historical topography of Nánjīng; and several chronological-history works. Note: CBDB has multiple entries for "張敦頤"; only id 15495 is the Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* of 1138 — the remaining entries (80929, 345057, 400924) are Qīng-period homonyms.
