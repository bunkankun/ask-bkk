Zhāng Dūnyí 張敦頤 (*fl. Shàoxīng*; *jìnshì* 1138, CBDB 15495 with index year 1108), *zì* Yǎngzhèng 養正, native of Wùyuán 婺源 in Huīzhōu (modern Jiāngxī). Southern-Sòng official, philologist, and historian. *Shàoxīng* 8 *jìnshì*; friend of Zhū Sōng 朱松 (Zhū Xī's father). Held office as Education-Officer of Nánjiànzhōu (1142–1148), Prefect of Shūzhōu, Prefect of Héngzhōu, and *Zuǒ fèngyì láng chōng Jiāngnán dōnglù ānfǔsī gànbàn gōngshì* (in 1160, when he composed the present work); retired on pension thereafter. Author of: the *Hán Liǔ wén yīnbiàn* 韓柳文音辨 — a phonological-philological commentary on the prose of Hán Yù and Liǔ Zōngyuán; the *Liùcháo shìjì biānlèi* 六朝事跡編類 ([[KR2k0112]]) on the historical topography of Nánjīng; and several chronological-history works. Note: CBDB has multiple entries for "張敦頤"; only id 15495 is the Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* of 1138 — the remaining entries (80929, 345057, 400924) are Qīng-period homonyms.
