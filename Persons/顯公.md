# Xiǎngōng 顯公

Southern-Sòng abbot of the Gūsū Dìnghuìsì 姑蘇定慧寺 in Sūzhōu who, around the Qiándào era (c. 1170–1172), brought Xiánhuī's Shǒulèngyán jīng yìhǎi ([[KR6j0727]]) to the attention of the imperial preface-writer for the canonical preface.
