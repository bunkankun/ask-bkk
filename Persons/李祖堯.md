Lǐ Zǔ-yáo 李祖堯, *xué-shì* (Academician, late Sòng), pupil of Sūn Dí 孫覿 [[孫覿]] and editor-and-annotator of Sūn's letter-collection *Nèi-jiǎn chǐ-dú* 內簡尺牘 [[KR4d0180]] in 10 *juǎn*. CBDB does not preserve clear lifedates for him.

The *Sì-kù* editors specifically praise Lǐ's annotation as superior to other late-imperial commentary owing to his personal contact with Sūn — comparable in this respect to Rén Yuān 任淵's annotation of Chén Shī-dào and Huáng Tíng-jiān (where Rén had personal contact). The *Sì-kù* editors also note that Lǐ's annotation often draws on Sūn's own poetry-and-prose for cross-reference, and these citations preserve material missing from the surviving *Hóng-qìng jū-shì jí* recension.
