Lián Wén-fèng 連文鳳 (lifedates uncertain; CBDB gives fl. through 1280; *Bǎi-zhèng jí* internal evidence shows him still alive in 1300), *zì* Bǎi-zhèng 百正, *hào* Yìng-shān 應山, a "Sān-shān" 三山 (Fú-zhōu) man. Had served in some minor Sòng capacity before 1276 (his *Mù-qiū zá-xìng* poem mentions "[my] name [was] removed from the official roster"). After the Sòng fall he refused all Yuán recruitment. In Zhì-yuán *bǐng-xū* (1286) his seven contributions under the pseudonym **Luó Gōng-fú** 羅公福 took the top place in the *Yuè-quán yín-shè* 月泉吟社 — the largest Sòng-loyalist poetry contest of the early Yuán, organized by Wú Wèi 吳渭 at Pú-jiāng in collaboration with Xiè Áo 謝翺 and Fāng Fèng [[方鳳]]. His original Sòng-loyalist collection *Bǐng-zǐ-gǎo* 丙子稿 (named for the *bǐng-zǐ* year 1276 of the Sòng surrender) was lost by the Qián-lóng era; the surviving material was reassembled from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* as the *Bǎi-zhèng jí* [[KR4d0404]].
