Lián Wénfèng 連文鳳 (lifedates uncertain; CBDB gives fl. through 1280; *Bǎizhèng jí* internal evidence shows him still alive in 1300), *zì* Bǎizhèng 百正, *hào* Yìngshān 應山, a "Sānshān" 三山 (Fúzhōu) man. Had served in some minor Sòng capacity before 1276 (his *Mùqiū záxìng* poem mentions "[my] name [was] removed from the official roster"). After the Sòng fall he refused all Yuán recruitment. In Zhìyuán *bǐngxū* (1286) his seven contributions under the pseudonym **Luó Gōngfú** 羅公福 took the top place in the *Yuèquán yínshè* 月泉吟社 — the largest Sòng-loyalist poetry contest of the early Yuán, organized by Wú Wèi 吳渭 at Pújiāng in collaboration with Xiè Áo 謝翺 and Fāng Fèng [[方鳳]]. His original Sòng-loyalist collection *Bǐngzǐgǎo* 丙子稿 (named for the *bǐngzǐ* year 1276 of the Sòng surrender) was lost by the Qiánlóng era; the surviving material was reassembled from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* as the *Bǎizhèng jí* [[KR4d0404]].
