**Xiàng Yánghuì** 項揚惠 (*fl.* early Republican period, c. 1920s–1930s), one of the four named modern editors of the *Dá-mó xǐ-suǐ yì-jīn jīng* 達摩洗髓易筋經 ([[KR3eo037]]) as transmitted in the 1930 lithograph edition. No further biographical data available. No CBDB record.
