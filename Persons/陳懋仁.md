Chén Màorén 陳懋仁, *zì* Wúgōng 無功, of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). Late-Míng scholar and bibliographic editor active in the *Wànlì* 萬曆 (1573–1620) and *Tiānqǐ* 天啟 (1621–1627) eras; precise lifedates not recorded. Held office as *zhōupàn* 州判 of Quánzhōu 泉州 (Fújiàn). Best known as a productive commentator and miscellaneous compiler: his *Quánnán zázhì* 泉南雜志 (records of Quánzhōu) is preserved in several Míng *cóngshū*; he annotated Rèn Fǎng's [[任昉]] *Wénzhāng yuánqǐ* [[KR4i0004]] — the annotations marked *zhù* 註 (as distinct from the *bǔzhù* 補注 added later by Fāng Xióng [[方熊]] of the Qing); and he produced a handful of small philological notes on early *cí* and HànWèi *fù*. CBDB records him at id 565556 without biographical detail. The relevant standard Míng biographical compilations (Tán Qiān 譚遷's *Guóquè*, the *Míngshǐ* *Yìwénzhì*) preserve only this thin layer of office and locality.
