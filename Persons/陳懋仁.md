Chén Mào-rén 陳懋仁, *zì* Wú-gōng 無功, of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhè-jiāng). Late-Míng scholar and bibliographic editor active in the *Wàn-lì* 萬曆 (1573–1620) and *Tiān-qǐ* 天啟 (1621–1627) eras; precise lifedates not recorded. Held office as *zhōu-pàn* 州判 of Quán-zhōu 泉州 (Fú-jiàn). Best known as a productive commentator and miscellaneous compiler: his *Quán-nán zá-zhì* 泉南雜志 (records of Quán-zhōu) is preserved in several Míng *cóng-shū*; he annotated Rèn Fǎng's [[任昉]] *Wén-zhāng yuán-qǐ* [[KR4i0004]] — the annotations marked *zhù* 註 (as distinct from the *bǔ-zhù* 補注 added later by Fāng Xióng [[方熊]] of the Qing); and he produced a handful of small philological notes on early *cí* and Hàn-Wèi *fù*. CBDB records him at id 565556 without biographical detail. The relevant standard Míng biographical compilations (Tán Qiān 譚遷's *Guó-què*, the *Míng-shǐ* *Yì-wén-zhì*) preserve only this thin layer of office and locality.
