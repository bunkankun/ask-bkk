# Zhēn-pǔ 真樸

Early-Qīng Chán monk; senior dharma-heir and editorial-compiler of [[道忞|Mù-chén Dào-mín]] 木陳道忞 (1596–1674) at Tiān-tóng-sì 天童寺. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Accompanied Dào-mín on his 1659 imperial journey to the Shùnzhì court in Beijing, and subsequently edited and arranged the resulting teaching-materials into the *Tiāntóng Hóng-jué Mín chánshī běi yóu jí* 天童弘覺忞禪師北遊集 [[KR6q0199]], identified in the text as *mén-rén Zhēn-pǔ biān cì* 門人真樸編次 ("edited and sequenced by the disciple Zhēn-pǔ"). No independent biographical material is preserved.
