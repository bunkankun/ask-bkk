**Yè Zhìshēn** 葉志洗 (some sources 葉志詵, fl. 1820s–1850s, the catalog meta gives 葉志洗 — likely a graphical variant of 詵), *biéhào* Fēngwēng Dōngqīng 封翁東卿 ("the venerable Mr Dōngqīng"). Late-Qīng official-physician of Hànyáng 漢陽 (Húběi); served as a *zhōngchéng* (中丞 = lieutenant-governor-grade) in Guǎngdōng province, where he retired in his later years ("就養於粵東節署"). His pharmacological work, the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng zàn* 神農本草經贊 ([[KR3ec069]], printed 道光三十年 = 1850), is a four-character rhymed-verse *zàn* (praise-verse) on each substance of the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* as recovered by Sūn Xīngyǎn [[孫星衍]] and Sūn Féngyì [[孫馮翼]] (= [[KR3ec001]] in the catalog's reconstruction). The work is consciously modelled on Guō Pú 郭璞's *Shānhǎi jīng tú zàn* and represents a late-Qīng literary-pharmacological hybrid. Not in CBDB.
