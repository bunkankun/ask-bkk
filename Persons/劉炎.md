Liú Yán 劉炎 (Sòng), *zì* Zǐxuān 子宣, was a Southern Sòng *Lǐxué* commentator of Sōng-yáng 松陽 (Zhè-jiāng). His self-preface to the *Ěr yán* 邇言 (KR3a0055) is dated Jiā-tài *jiǎ-zǐ* 嘉泰甲子 (1204) — placing him in the immediate post-*Qìng-yuán dǎng-jìn* generation. He held no recorded official position. His major work is the *Ěr yán* in 12 *zhāng*, drawing on the Chéng-school tradition. CBDB has multiple homonyms named 劉炎; the *Ěr yán* author has no confidently identified id, with id 38005 (fl. 1200) the most chronologically plausible candidate.
