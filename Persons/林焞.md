Lín Tūn 林焞, *zì* Bǐngshū 炳叔, of Níngdé 寧德 in Fújiàn. *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī* 8 (*xīnchǒu*, 1181) — the same district, same school, same examination year as [[王宗傳]] Wáng Zōngchuán. Later magistrate of Kāihuàxiàn 開化縣 in Qúzhōu 衢州. Wrote the *Kāixǐ* 1 (1205) preface to Wáng Zōngchuán's *Tóngxī Yì zhuàn* 童溪易傳 ([[KR1a0047]]) at the request of Wáng Zōngchuán's clansman [[王䮐]] Wáng Yú. The preface — preserved at the head of the surviving recension — is the principal source for Wáng Zōngchuán's biographical detail, including the *fùlíngzhě* 負苓者 anecdote and the *xìng běn wú shuō* doctrinal formula.
