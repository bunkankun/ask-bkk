Xíng Tóng 邢侗 (1551–1612), *zì* **Zǐyuàn** 子愿, *hào* **Láiqínshēng** 來禽生 (after his Láiqín Hall *zhāi*) and **Zhīwú** 知吾; native of **Línyì** 臨邑 (Shāndōng). Wànlì 2 / *jiǎxū* (1574) *jìnshì*; served as **Nángōngzhīxiàn** and rose to *Shǎnxī xíng tàipúsìqīng*; retired early to his estate at Línyì where he devoted himself to calligraphy and to the editing of *fǎtiè* model-book compendia (notably the *Láiqínguǎn fǎtiè* 來禽館法帖). One of the **Wànlì sì jiā** 萬曆四家 of late-Míng calligraphy — paired with Dǒng Qíchāng 董其昌, Mǐ Wànzhōng 米萬鍾, and Zhāng Ruìtú 張瑞圖 — celebrated especially for his *xíngcǎo* in the Wáng Xīzhī manner. As a poet he was associated with the late Wànlì *fùgǔ* movement. His *Diào Sòngsǒu shī xù* 弔宋叟詩序 is the principal contemporary documentary witness to the death and self-understanding of the *bùyī* poet [[宋登春]] (*c.* 1515 – *c.* 1586), see [[KR4e0238]]. *Míngshǐ* lacks a dedicated biography; the standard source is the *Línyì xiànzhì* and the prefatory matter of his own *Láiqínguǎn jí* 來禽館集.
