Xíng Tóng 邢侗 (1551–1612), *zì* **Zǐ-yuàn** 子愿, *hào* **Lái-qín-shēng** 來禽生 (after his Lái-qín Hall *zhāi*) and **Zhī-wú** 知吾; native of **Lín-yì** 臨邑 (Shān-dōng). Wàn-lì 2 / *jiǎ-xū* (1574) *jìnshì*; served as **Nán-gōng-zhī-xiàn** and rose to *Shǎn-xī xíng tài-pú-sì-qīng*; retired early to his estate at Lín-yì where he devoted himself to calligraphy and to the editing of *fǎ-tiè* model-book compendia (notably the *Lái-qín-guǎn fǎ-tiè* 來禽館法帖). One of the **Wàn-lì sì jiā** 萬曆四家 of late-Míng calligraphy — paired with Dǒng Qí-chāng 董其昌, Mǐ Wàn-zhōng 米萬鍾, and Zhāng Ruì-tú 張瑞圖 — celebrated especially for his *xíng-cǎo* in the Wáng Xī-zhī manner. As a poet he was associated with the late Wàn-lì *fù-gǔ* movement. His *Diào Sòng-sǒu shī xù* 弔宋叟詩序 is the principal contemporary documentary witness to the death and self-understanding of the *bùyī* poet [[宋登春]] (*c.* 1515 – *c.* 1586), see [[KR4e0238]]. *Míng-shǐ* lacks a dedicated biography; the standard source is the *Lín-yì xiàn-zhì* and the prefatory matter of his own *Lái-qín-guǎn jí* 來禽館集.
