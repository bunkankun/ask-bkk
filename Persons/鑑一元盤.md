# Jiànyī Yuánpán 鑑一元盤

A late-Míng Buddhist commentator associated with Xīhú 西湖 (Hángzhōu); credited by [[行策]] Jiéliú Xíngcè with the *Xīhú Jiànyī shū* on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*, a commentary Xíngcè singled out as best exemplifying the principle of "abandoning self and following the sūtra."
