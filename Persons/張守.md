**Zhāng Shǒu** 張守 (1084–1145), *zì* Quán-zhēn 全真, of Cháng-zhōu 常州 Pí-líng 毗陵 (Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of *Chóng-níng* 1 / 1102. Held office under Huīzōng, Qīnzōng, and Gāozōng. *Jiàn-yán* mid-period: as *Cān-zhī zhèng-shì* (Vice-Chancellor, deputy chief-minister); engaged repeatedly in remonstrance against the *hé-yì* (peace-faction) of Huáng Qián-shàn and Wāng Bó-yàn. Author of *Pí-líng jí* 毗陵集 [[KR4d0144]] in 15 *juǎn*. Catalog gives composition date 1102 (referring to the *jìnshì* date). Lifedates 1084–1145 are conventionally given but not securely fixed.
