**Zhāng Shǒu** 張守 (1084–1145), *zì* Quánzhēn 全真, of Chángzhōu 常州 Pílíng 毗陵 (Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Chóngníng* 1 / 1102. Held office under Huīzōng, Qīnzōng, and Gāozōng. *Jiànyán* mid-period: as *Cānzhī zhèngshì* (Vice-Chancellor, deputy chief-minister); engaged repeatedly in remonstrance against the *héyì* (peace-faction) of Huáng Qiánshàn and Wāng Bóyàn. Author of *Pílíng jí* 毗陵集 [[KR4d0144]] in 15 *juǎn*. Catalog gives composition date 1102 (referring to the *jìnshì* date). Lifedates 1084–1145 are conventionally given but not securely fixed.
