# 劉橘泉 Liú Júquán

Late-Yuán / early-Míng physician, father of [[劉純]] Liú Chún and a direct student of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī. Conduit for the Dānxī-school clinical tradition into the early-Míng lineage that produced the *Yùjī wēiyì* ([[KR3e0068]]) and *Shānghán zhì lì* ([[KR3ef072]]).
