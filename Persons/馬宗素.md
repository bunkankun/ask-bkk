**Mǎ Zōngsù** 馬宗素 (*fl.* early-to-mid Yuán, ca. 1290s–1320s, 元), Yuán-period Hé-jiān-school physician in the lineage of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù. Author of two short *Shānghán* works:

- *Liú Hé-jiān Shānghán yī jiàn* 劉河間傷寒醫鑑 ([[KR3ef037]], 1 juan).
- *Shānghán qián fǎ* 傷寒鈐法 ([[KR3ef048]], 1 juan) — a calendar-based prognostic Shānghán treatise.

His biographical details are not preserved beyond his role as a Hé-jiān-school transmitter in the Yuán. No CBDB record.
