Shěn Zōngjìng 沈宗敬 (1669–1735; CBDB id 79687), *zì* Kètíng 恪庭, *hào* Nánléi 南雷 / Shīfēng 獅峰, was a Kāngxī-period scholar-official-painter from Huátíng 華亭 (Sōngjiāng prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 27 (1688); served as *Tàichángsì shàoqīng* and as *Hànlínyuàn tídū sìyìguǎn*. In the Kāngxī imperial *lèishū* programme he served as principal *fēnzuǎn* (compiler) on the *Pián zì lèibiān* 駢字類編 ([[KR3k0056]]), the *Fēn lèi zì jǐn* 分類字錦 ([[KR3k0057]]), and the *Zǐshǐ jīnghuá* 子史精華 ([[KR3k0058]]). He is independently noted as a landscape painter in the *Lóu Dōng* 婁東 school manner of Wáng Yuánqí 王原祁; his paintings survive in major museum collections.
