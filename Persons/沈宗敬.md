Shěn Zōng-jìng 沈宗敬 (1669–1735; CBDB id 79687), *zì* Kè-tíng 恪庭, *hào* Nán-léi 南雷 / Shī-fēng 獅峰, was a Kāngxī-period scholar-official-painter from Huá-tíng 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng prefecture, Jiāng-sū). *Jìnshì* of *Kāngxī* 27 (1688); served as *Tài-cháng-sì shào-qīng* and as *Hàn-lín-yuàn tí-dū sì-yì-guǎn*. In the Kāngxī imperial *lèishū* programme he served as principal *fēn-zuǎn* (compiler) on the *Pián zì lèi-biān* 駢字類編 ([[KR3k0056]]), the *Fēn lèi zì jǐn* 分類字錦 ([[KR3k0057]]), and the *Zǐ-shǐ jīng-huá* 子史精華 ([[KR3k0058]]). He is independently noted as a landscape painter in the *Lóu Dōng* 婁東 school manner of Wáng Yuán-qí 王原祁; his paintings survive in major museum collections.
