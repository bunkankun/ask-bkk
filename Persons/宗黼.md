# Zōng-fǔ 宗黼

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán dharma-heir (*sì-fǎ cān-xué* 嗣法參學) of [[無慍]] Shù-zhōng Wú-yùn (1309–1386); former abbot of Chāng-guó Wàn-shòu chán-sì 昌國萬壽禪寺. Heads the editorial line for *juan* 1 (Xiàng-shān Líng-yán) of Wú-yùn's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0349]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
