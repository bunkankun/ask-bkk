# Zōngfǔ 宗黼

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Chán dharma-heir (*sìfǎ cānxué* 嗣法參學) of [[無慍]] Shùzhōng Wúyùn (1309–1386); former abbot of Chāngguó Wànshòu chánsì 昌國萬壽禪寺. Heads the editorial line for *juan* 1 (Xiàngshān Língyán) of Wúyùn's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0349]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
