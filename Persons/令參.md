# Lìngcān 令參

Five Dynasties Chán master, abbot of the Cuìyán 翠巖 monastery (also referred to as Cuìyán Yǒngmíng Lìngcān 翠巖永明令參), the preceptor under whom [[延壽]] Yǒngmíng Yánshòu was tonsured.
