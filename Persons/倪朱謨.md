Ní Zhūmó 倪朱謨 (*zì* Chúnyǔ 純宇), native of Hángzhōu 杭州 (Zhèjiāng), *fl.* late Wànlì through Chóngzhēn (*c.* 1580 – *c.* 1640). Layman scholar-physician in the Hángzhōu late-Míng medical-literary network. Author of the *Běncǎo huì yán* 本草彙言 ([[KR3ec033]]) in 20 *juǎn*, compiled 1624–1629. Other works: *Líng-shū jīng zhù* 靈樞經注 (a commentary on the *Língshū*) and a clinical casebook *Xī jí yī àn* 析疑醫案 (preserved fragmentarily). His teacher was Miù Xīyōng [[繆希雍]]. No CBDB id located.
