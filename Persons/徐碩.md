Xú Shuò 徐碩 (fl. ca. 1286–1288), Yuán-period official and gazetteer compiler. Native place and detailed career are unrecorded; the Sìkù *tíyào* explicitly notes that "his native place is unascertained, and his career details are likewise without source." At the time of compiling the *Zhìyuán Jiāhé zhì* ([[KR2k0023]]) — the principal extant Yuán prefectural gazetteer of Jiāxīnglù 嘉興路 — he held the office *jiàoshòu* 教授 (Director of Studies) of the Confucian school of Jiāxīnglù, having been commissioned by the *jīnglì* 經歷 (Administrative Aide) Shàn Qìng 單慶 to take up Guān Shì's 闗栻 unfinished Sòng-era prefectural gazetteer (then five juan extant) and bring it to completion. He expanded the rubric scheme to 43 categories in 32 juan and added a substantial 11-juan epigraphy section. CBDB index year 1238 (CBDB id 29868) reflects the date of his floruit attached to a Sòng-Yuán transition cohort; his actual lifedates are not fixable from received sources.
