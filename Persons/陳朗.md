Chén Lǎng 陳朗 (fl. 乾隆 period, 18th century), style name Xiǎoshān 曉山, pen name Jìnghú Yìsǒu 鏡湖逸叟 ("Leisured Old Man of Mirror Lake"), is the author of the Qīng-dynasty novel [[KR4k0266]] *Xuě Yuè Méi* 雪月梅 (1775). From his self-preface, it is known that he was a widely traveled man who had journeyed north through the Yān 燕 and Qí 齊 regions and south into Fújiàn 閩 and Guǎngdōng 粵, composing the novel in his old age at the Sōngyuè Shānfáng 松月山房 in Gǔjūnyáng 古鈞陽 (present-day Jūn county area, Hénán). The postface author Dǒng Jìmián 董寄綿 (of Gǔdìngyáng 古定陽) addresses him as "Xiǎoshān Chénzǐ 曉山陳子." No further biographical details are known.
