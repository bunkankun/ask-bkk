**Lǚ Bórén** 呂伯仁, late-Qīng student of [[沈菊人]] Shěn Júrén who inherited a manuscript of [[石壽棠]] Shí Shòutáng's [[KR3eq015]] *Yīyuán* and transmitted it to [[張聲馳]] Zhāng Shēngchí for the 1891 print run.
