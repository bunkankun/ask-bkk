**Shěn Zhīyán** 沈知言 was a late-Táng Daoist alchemist, author of [[KR5c0342]] *Tōng xuán mì shù* 通玄祕術 ("Secret Techniques for Communicating with the Mystery"). An earlier version is attested from the late 9th century.
