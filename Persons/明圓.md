Míngyuán 明圓, *hào* **Bīnglún** 冰輪 ("Ice-Wheel"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔsù Zūn** [[尊]] 古宿尊 in the Mìyún → Yuǎnwēng → Gǔsù sub-line. Lead compiler of the 6-juan 《古宿尊禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0512]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
