Míng-yuán 明圓, *hào* **Bīng-lún** 冰輪 ("Ice-Wheel"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔ-sù Zūn** [[尊]] 古宿尊 in the Mì-yún → Yuǎn-wēng → Gǔ-sù sub-line. Lead compiler of the 6-juan 《古宿尊禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0512]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
