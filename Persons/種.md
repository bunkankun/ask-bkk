Zhǒng 種 (DILA-standardized **Zhìzhǒng** 智種), *hào* **Nèishào** 內紹 ("Inner Successor"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hànyuè Sānfēng** sub-line.

**Background.** Initially trained in **Tiāntái-school doctrinal study** under **Jízhōng fǎshī** 即中法師 (a Tiāntái master) — *tòu jìn Táizōng xuánào* 透盡台宗玄奧 — with reputation for doctrinal-debate prowess. Heard about Hóngchǔ at his Guóqīngsì abbacy and crossed lineages to Sānfēng Línjì transmission with decisive *xǐng* on a *zhǎn duàn báiyún guīlù* 截斷白雲歸路 *jì*-verse exchange. The Tiāntái → Línjì conversion is significant: Jízhōng fǎshī himself, in his last instruction, transferred his Tiāntái lineage-robe to Zhǒng despite the cross-school issue.

**Abbacy.** **Sūzhōu Fǔlǐ Yuèzhǎng Chányuàn** 蘇州甫里月掌禪院 (Wújiāng region) — whence the title-form 月掌種.

**Works.** [[KR6q0478]] 《內紹種禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B306, recorded by *shìzhě* **Zhàowéi** [[照維]] *děng jì*. Also 《內紹禪師得閑堂頌谷》 1 juan (separately preserved).

Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 85; DILA authority A019286.
