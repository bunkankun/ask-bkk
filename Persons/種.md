Zhǒng 種 (DILA-standardized **Zhì-zhǒng** 智種), *hào* **Nèi-shào** 內紹 ("Inner Successor"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng** sub-line.

**Background.** Initially trained in **Tiāntái-school doctrinal study** under **Jí-zhōng fǎ-shī** 即中法師 (a Tiāntái master) — *tòu jìn Tái-zōng xuán-ào* 透盡台宗玄奧 — with reputation for doctrinal-debate prowess. Heard about Hóng-chǔ at his Guó-qīng-sì abbacy and crossed lineages to Sān-fēng Línjì transmission with decisive *xǐng* on a *zhǎn duàn bái-yún guī-lù* 截斷白雲歸路 *jì*-verse exchange. The Tiāntái → Línjì conversion is significant: Jí-zhōng fǎ-shī himself, in his last instruction, transferred his Tiāntái lineage-robe to Zhǒng despite the cross-school issue.

**Abbacy.** **Sū-zhōu Fǔ-lǐ Yuè-zhǎng Chán-yuàn** 蘇州甫里月掌禪院 (Wú-jiāng region) — whence the title-form 月掌種.

**Works.** [[KR6q0478]] 《內紹種禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B306, recorded by *shì-zhě* **Zhào-wéi** [[照維]] *děng jì*. Also 《內紹禪師得閑堂頌谷》 1 juan (separately preserved).

Sources: 《五燈全書》 j. 85; DILA authority A019286.
