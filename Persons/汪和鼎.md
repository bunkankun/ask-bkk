**Wāng Hédǐng** 汪和鼎 (*fl.* late-19th c. 清), Qīng-era physician. Compiled the *Yílín cè* 宜麟策 ([[KR3ei072]]) by extracting and abridging the *Yílín cè* sections of **Zhāng Jièbīn** 張介賓 (1563–1640)'s *Jǐngyuè quánshū* 景岳全書 — specifically the *Fùrén guī* 婦人規 fertility-treatise. The work as preserved represents Wāng's editorial reorganisation of Zhāng's materials into a compact stand-alone treatise; Wāng himself contributes no original doctrinal content but functions as anthologist-redactor. Not in CBDB.
