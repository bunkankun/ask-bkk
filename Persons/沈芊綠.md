# 沈芊綠 Shěn Qiānlǜ

Qīng-dynasty physician, author of a *Jīnguì yàoluè* commentary (the *Shěn Qiānlǜ Jīnguì zhù*) cited as a principal late-Qīng *yángmíng* / *Jīnguì* reference in [[陸懋修]] Lù Màoxiū's *Shānghán lùn yángmíng bìng shì* ([[KR3ef070]]).
