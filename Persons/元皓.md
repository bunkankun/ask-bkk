# Yuán-hào 元皓

Late-Yuán Chán *cān-xué bǐ-qiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[一志]]) of *juan* 1 (Kāi-fú abbacy) of Qīng-yù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Distinct from Yuán-hào 元浩 of Píng-jiāng Yǒng-shòu (DILA A000120), co-editor of [[KR6q0345]].
