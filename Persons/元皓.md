# Yuánhào 元皓

Late-Yuán Chán *cānxué bǐqiū* 參學比丘 of [[清欲]] Liǎoān Qīngyù (1292–1367); co-editor (with [[一志]]) of *juan* 1 (Kāifú abbacy) of Qīngyù's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0347]]). Lifedates unrecorded. Distinct from Yuánhào 元浩 of Píngjiāng Yǒngshòu (DILA A000120), co-editor of [[KR6q0345]].
