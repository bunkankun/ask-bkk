**Shin'en** 親圓 — Mid-Heian Japanese **Kegon** 華嚴 (Avataṃsaka-school) monk of **Tōdaiji** 東大寺. His sole canonical work, *Huā-yán-zōng zhǒng-xìng yì chāo* 華嚴宗種性義抄 ([[KR6t0024]], T72n2328), is precisely dated by its colophon to **Kannin 3 (= 1019), 2nd month, 7th day**, at the *Tōdaiji North Hall West Second Subordinate-Officials Room* (東大寺北室西第二小吏房). The work is a Kegon-school compendium on the doctrine of *gotra* (inherent seed-natures) treated through the five-fold Kegon teaching-classification — an exemplary product of the Tōdaiji Kegon scholarly tradition at the height of the *Fujiwara regency*.
