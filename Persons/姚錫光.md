# 姚錫光

Yáo Xīguāng 姚錫光 (號 Shíquán 石荃), late-Qīng *jǔrén* and reform-era official who served as *guānchá* (intendant) in the northern capital region. Elder brother of the physician [[姚龍光]] Yáo Lóngguāng (author of [[KR3ep094]]).
