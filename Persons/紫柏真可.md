# Zǐbǎi Zhēnkě 紫柏真可

Zǐbǎi Zhēnkě 紫柏真可 (1543–1604), *zì* Dáguān 達觀; lay surname Shěn 沈. Native of Wújiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu). One of the **Four Eminent Monks of the late Míng** (*Wǎn Míng sì dà gāosēng* 晚明四大高僧) alongside [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng, [[德清]] Hānshān Déqīng, and [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù.

Vigorous promoter of Buddhist textual scholarship and a leading sponsor of the **Jiāxīngzàng** 嘉興藏 (Jingshan canon) project; itinerant teacher with a strong ascetic and Chán orientation. Implicated in the politically charged 1603 "*yāoshū* 妖書" affair, he was imprisoned and died under interrogation in 1604. His collected writings circulate as *Zǐbǎi zūnzhě quánjí* 紫柏尊者全集.
