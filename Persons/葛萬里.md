Gě Wàn-lǐ 葛萬里 (fl. early-to-mid Qīng), *hào* Mèng-háng 夢航 ("Dream-Voyaging"), of Kūn-shān 崑山 (Sū-zhōu, Jiāng-sū). No CBDB record located; biographical details are sparse. His sole recorded work is the *Bié hào lù* 別號錄 ([[KR3k0064]]) — the principal Qīng-period rhyme-indexed reference for the *bié hào* (alternate sobriquets) of Sòng-Jīn-Yuán-Míng literary figures. The brief author's preface signed *Mèng-háng jū-shì* gives the framing argument that *bié hào* "flourished in the Southern Sòng and grew rampant in the Míng" — making the work an indispensable usability-aid for Míng reading practice. *Sìkù* date markers place his active period in the early 18th century at the latest, before the Qiánlóng *Sìkù* recension review (1781).
