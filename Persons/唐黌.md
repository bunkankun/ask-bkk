**Táng Hóng** 唐黌 (*zì*/*hào* Qín-zhōu 芹洲, *fl.* Qián-lóng, 清), Kūn-shān 崑山 (Jiāngsū) amateur rural physician, not formally trained. Author of [[KR3ek015]] *Wài-kē xuǎn-yào* 外科選要 (2 *juǎn*, Qián-lóng 41 = 1776), a concise didactic distillation of the three great Míng-Qīng surgical compendia (Chén Shí-gōng's [[KR3ek014]] *Wài-kē zhèng-zōng*, Qí Kūn's [[KR3ek011]] *Wài-kē dà chéng*, and Wáng Kěn-táng's [[KR3ek027]] *Yáng-yī zhǔn-shéng*) for use by non-specialists in his locality. He describes himself in his preface as a self-taught village practitioner working from the imperial [[KR3ek009]] *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*. Should not be confused with the separate, larger *Wài-kē xuǎn-yào* in 6 *juǎn* by Xú Zhēn-jiè 徐真銈 (1843). Not in CBDB.
