**Táng Hóng** 唐黌 (*zì*/*hào* Qínzhōu 芹洲, *fl.* Qiánlóng, 清), Kūnshān 崑山 (Jiāngsū) amateur rural physician, not formally trained. Author of [[KR3ek015]] *Wàikē xuǎnyào* 外科選要 (2 *juǎn*, Qiánlóng 41 = 1776), a concise didactic distillation of the three great MíngQīng surgical compendia (Chén Shígōng's [[KR3ek014]] *Wàikē zhèngzōng*, Qí Kūn's [[KR3ek011]] *Wàikē dà chéng*, and Wáng Kěntáng's [[KR3ek027]] *Yángyī zhǔnshéng*) for use by non-specialists in his locality. He describes himself in his preface as a self-taught village practitioner working from the imperial [[KR3ek009]] *Yīzōng jīnjiàn*. Should not be confused with the separate, larger *Wàikē xuǎnyào* in 6 *juǎn* by Xú Zhēnjiè 徐真銈 (1843). Not in CBDB.
