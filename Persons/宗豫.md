Zōngyù 宗豫 (active 9th c.), Tang Buddhist scholar-monk; author of the *[[KR6e0104|Zhù huáyán fǎ jiè guān kē wén 註華嚴法界觀科文]]* (X1008), an alternative *kē wén* outline of [[宗密]] Zōngmì's *[[KR6e0101|Zhù fǎ jiè guānmén]]* (T1884) — distinct from Zōngmì's own outline (X1007). Birth and death dates not preserved; conventional scholarship places him as a contemporary of, or slightly later than, Zōngmì in the late-Tang Cǎotángsì circle.
