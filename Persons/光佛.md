Guāngfó 光佛, *zì* **Háichū** 還初. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] 聖可德玉 (1628–1701) at **Huáyánsì** 華嚴寺 in Chóngqìng (Sìchuān). Born **崇禎四 = 1631-02-01/1632-02-19**; alive in **康熙乙丑 = 1685** at age 55; death-date not preserved. Native of **Fùshùnxiàn** 富順縣 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Gāo** 高.

Tonsured at 25 (1655) under **Shèngkě Déyù**. Pilgrimage to **Língfēng** 靈峰, **Yúnfēng Tǐzōng** 雲峰體宗, and **Zhāojué Zhàngxuě** 昭覺丈雪 ([[通醉]]); returned to Déyù for transmission. Sole abbacy at **Chóngqìng Huáyán Chányuàn** 重慶華巖禪院 (succeeding his master).

**Heirs.** **Yúnshí Jìxiá** 雲石寂遐; **Xīlín Tōngliàng** 西臨通量 [[通量]] (compiler of [[KR6q0518]]).

**Works.**
- [[KR6q0488]] 《華嚴聖可禪師語錄》 5 juan — compiled his master Déyù's *yǔlù*.
- [[KR6q0518]] 《華巖還初佛禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B393 — his own *yǔlù*, compiled by *fǎsì* **Xīlín Tōngliàng** [[通量]] *děng biān*.
