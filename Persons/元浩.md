# Yuánhào 元浩

Yuán Chán *xiǎoshī* 小師 (junior disciple) of [[清茂]] Gǔlín Qīngmào (1262–1329); abbot of Píngjiānglù Yǒngshòu chánsì 平江路永壽禪寺. Editor of *juan* 1 (Tiānpíng and Kāiyuán abbacies) of Qīngmào's five-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0345]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
