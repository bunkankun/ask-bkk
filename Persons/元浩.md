# Yuán-hào 元浩

Yuán Chán *xiǎo-shī* 小師 (junior disciple) of [[清茂]] Gǔ-lín Qīng-mào (1262–1329); abbot of Píng-jiāng-lù Yǒng-shòu chán-sì 平江路永壽禪寺. Editor of *juan* 1 (Tiān-píng and Kāi-yuán abbacies) of Qīng-mào's five-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0345]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
