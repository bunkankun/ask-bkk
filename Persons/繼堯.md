# Jì-yáo 繼堯

Qīng Chán monk of Sichuan; disciple of [[如乾|Hān-xiū Rú-qián]] 憨休如乾. Lifedates unrecorded. Collator (*jiào dìng* 校訂) of his master's *Qiāo kōng yí xiǎng* 敲空遺響 [[KR6q0229]].
