**Lǐ Jìfāng** 李紀方 (*zì* Lún-qīng 倫青), late-Qīng Hú-nán 湖南 physician of Héng-shān 衡山, working in the Lóng-mén Shān-fáng 龍門山房 study. No CBDB record; lifedates are not securely fixed, but he was active over the period c. 1860–1882, on the testimony of his own preface to [[KR3em027]] *Bái-hóu quán-shēng jí* 白喉全生集 (dated *Guāng-xù* 8, 1882).

He reports his maternal grandfather Yǐn Shèn-huī 尹慎徽, a learned all-round physician, as his principal teacher; he studied with him for over twenty years before compiling the present work, in collaboration with his cousin Zhào Zhòng-biāo 趙仲標, into the most important late-Qīng monograph on *bái-hóu* 白喉 (diphtheria). His doctrinal achievement is the systematic critique of the contemporary tendency to misidentify *bái-hóu* with epidemic-pestilence (*yì* 疫) and to apply cooling drugs uniformly: he insists that the syndrome must be classified into cold-form, heat-form, and mixed cold-heat sub-forms before treatment.
