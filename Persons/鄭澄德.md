# Zhèng Chéngdé 鄭澄德 (style 學川 Xuéchuān, late Qīng)

A late-Qīng lay-Buddhist (*jūshì* 居士) who, together with his brother [[鄭澄源]] Zhèng Chéngyuán (style 學烺 Xuélàng), produced the *Ē-mí-tuó jīng zhù* 阿彌陀經註 [[KR6p0027]], a Pure Land devotional manual completed in 咸豐 11 (1861) and printed shortly thereafter. The brothers signed jointly as 淨業學人菩薩戒鄭澄德(學川)鄭澄源(學烺) ("Pure-Karma students who have received the bodhisattva precepts"). Lifedates not preserved.

The brothers belonged to the late-Qīng *Zōng-jìng-táng* 宗鏡堂 lay-Buddhist publishing circle in Hangzhou (named for [[永明延壽]] Yǒng-míng Yán-shòu's *Zōng-jìng lù*); the preface to [[KR6p0027]] by Xǔ Yuè-shēn 許樾身 of *Rén-hé* 仁和 records that Zhèng Chéngdé compiled a forty-eight-volume Pure Land-oriented collectanea (the *Zōng-jìng-táng cóng-shū* 宗鏡堂叢書), of which the *Ē-mí-tuó jīng zhù* with its accompanying daily-practice schedule (*Xī dìng kè* 西定課) was the first item to be cut and circulated. The manual is part of the late-Qīng lay-Buddhist devotional movement that includes [[彭際清]] Péng Jì-qīng's *Yuē-lùn* trilogy and [[魏源]] Wèi Yuán's *Jìngtǔ sì jīng*.
