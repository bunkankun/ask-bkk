**Shèng Qǐdōng** 盛啟東 (1375–1441), personal name Shèng Yín 盛寅, was an early-Míng Sūzhōu physician of the Wú-medical lineage descending from [[戴原禮]] Dài Yuánlǐ via [[王賓]] Wáng Bīn. He served as imperial physician under the Yǒnglè and Xuāndé emperors and held a senior post in the 太醫院; together with [[韓叔陽]] Hán Shūyáng he is one of the two named transmitters of Dānxī (朱震亨) doctrine into the early-Míng court medical establishment, the lineage to which [[顧從德]] Gù Cóngdé's family belonged (see [[KR3ea001]]).
