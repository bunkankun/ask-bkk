Cài Guózhēn 蔡國珍 (1530–1609), *zì* Rǔpìn 汝聘, *hào* Fèngpǔ 鳳浦, of Xīnyú 新喻 in Jiāngxī. Jiājìng *jìnshì* (1565); rose to *Lìbù shàngshū* 吏部尚書 (Minister of Personnel) under Wànlì. Author of the principal Míng-print preface to [[楊簡]] Yáng Jiǎn's *Yángshì Yì zhuàn* ([[KR1a0037]]), framing the work alongside [[蘇軾]] Sū Shì's *Dōngpō Yì zhuàn* ([[KR1a0019]]) as the twin heterodox-but-canonical "wing-feathers" of the Sòng *Yì*-tradition.
