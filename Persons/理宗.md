# 理宗 Lǐzōng

Fifth emperor of the Southern Sòng (r. 1224–1264), personal name **Zhào Yún** 趙昀. Imperial patron of Buddhist and Neo-Confucian institutions; conferred honorific titles on several major Buddhist masters, including the title *Fóguāng fǎshī* 佛光法師 on the Tiāntái master [[法照]] Huìyán Fǎzhào and imperial calligraphy of the two large characters *Huìyán* 晦岩. His long reign coincided with the late-Southern-Sòng renaissance of Tiāntái and Chán scholastic productive culture at Hángzhōu.
