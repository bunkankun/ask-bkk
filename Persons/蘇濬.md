Sū Jùn 蘇濬 (1542–1599), *zì* Jūnyǔ 君禹, *hào* Zǐxī 紫溪, of Jìnjiāng 晉江 in Quánzhōu 泉州 (modern Fújiàn). Wànlì-era *jìnshì* (1583); held office to Guǎngxī *àncháshǐ* 廣西按察使 (Surveillance Commissioner of Guǎngxī). Author of the *Yìjīng míngmíngpiān* 易經冥冥篇 ("Chapter of the Deep Dark of the *Yìjīng*"), the *Yì* commentary that the *Sìkù* editors flag as the late-Míng *Yì-Chán* synthesis at its most explicit, culminating the *xīnxué Yì* line that runs from [[王宗傳]] Wáng Zōngchuán and [[楊簡]] Yáng Jiǎn ([[KR1a0037]]).
