**Cài Yìtíng** 蔡益亭 (*míngjīng* 明經 graduate; *fl.* late 18th – early 19th c.) — nephew of [[伊學曾]] Yī Xuézēng. After Yī's death he inherited the manuscript of *Tāichǎn zhǐnán* ([[KR3ei053]]) and circulated copies among colleagues; his protégé [[歐孔章]] Ōu Kǒngzhāng ultimately commissioned the 1856 wood-block edition. Not in CBDB.
