13th-century Daoist author (pseudonym), active c. 1200–1300. *Hào* **Yǐn zhī** 隱芝 ("Hidden Mushroom", evoking the *zhī* 芝 magical mushroom of immortality-lore plus *yǐn* 隱 Daoist retreat). Author of the commentary on the *Yuán dào zhēn jīng* preserved as [[KR5c0157|DZ 761]].

**Activity.** 13th century. No precise dates. No CBDB record.
