**Wàn Quán** 萬全 (1499–1582), *zì* Shìjuǎn 事卷 (in some sources 事權), *hào* **Mìzhāi** 密齋 (by which he is universally known: **Wàn Mìzhāi** 萬密齋), was a major mid-Míng paediatrician and general physician of Luótián 羅田 (Húběi, modern E-zhōu), bearer of the principal Míng-period Wàn-family medical lineage. His grandfather Wàn Lán 萬蘭 and father Wàn Xiǎozhāi 萬筱齋 were renowned local paediatricians; Wàn Quán continued and substantially expanded the family practice, becoming one of the foremost specialists in paediatrics, gynaecology, and family medicine of the mid-Míng. His *cóngshū* **《萬密齋醫書十種》** (Ten Works of Wàn Mìzhāi) includes the *Yòu kē fā huī* 幼科發揮, *Bǎo yīng zī yāo* 保嬰擇要, *Fù rén kē* 婦人科 (gynaecology), *Yǎng shēng sì yào* 養生四要, and the *Shānghán zhāi jǐn* 傷寒摘錦 ([[KR3ef071]], 2 juan), a clinically-organized digest of the *Shānghán* canon for general practitioners. He died at Luótián in 1582, aged 83. The Wàn-family lineage continued through Wàn Quán's descendants for several more generations and is one of the principal documented Míng-Qīng family medical practices.
