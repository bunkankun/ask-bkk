Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Yuánshí** 元實, sobriquets **Běifēng** 北峰 (with full name **Běifēng Zōngyìn** 北峰宗印 / 北峯宗印), **Huìxíng fǎshī** 慧行法師 / **Huìxíng fǎshī** 惠行法師. DILA Authority A000571. Born 1148; died 1213.

Author of the **Běifēng jiàoyì** 北峰教義 ([[KR6d0239]], X57n0963, 1 *juan*) — Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* doctrinal treatise. Teacher of **Huìyán Fǎzhào** 晦巖法照 ([[法照]], 1185–1273), the Southern-Sòng abbot of Hángzhōu Shàngtiānzhúsì and author of the *Sāndàbù dújiào jì* ([[KR6d0062]]); and of **Gǔyún Yuáncuì** 古雲元粹 ([[元粹]], the author of [[KR6d0170]]).

Note: distinct from the Míng monk **Zōngyìn** 宗印 (DILA A008066, *zì* Xǐtián 璽田, d. c. 1632) of Jiǔhuáshān.
