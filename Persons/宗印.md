Southern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; *zì* **Yuán-shí** 元實, sobriquets **Běi-fēng** 北峰 (with full name **Běi-fēng Zōng-yìn** 北峰宗印 / 北峯宗印), **Huì-xíng fǎshī** 慧行法師 / **Huì-xíng fǎshī** 惠行法師. DILA Authority A000571. Born 1148; died 1213.

Author of the **Běi-fēng jiào-yì** 北峰教義 ([[KR6d0239]], X57n0963, 1 *juan*) — Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* doctrinal treatise. Teacher of **Huìyán Fǎzhào** 晦巖法照 ([[法照]], 1185–1273), the Southern-Sòng abbot of Háng-zhōu Shàng-tiānzhú-sì and author of the *Sān-dà-bù dú-jiào jì* ([[KR6d0062]]); and of **Gǔ-yún Yuán-cuì** 古雲元粹 ([[元粹]], the author of [[KR6d0170]]).

Note: distinct from the Míng monk **Zōng-yìn** 宗印 (DILA A008066, *zì* Xǐ-tián 璽田, d. c. 1632) of Jiǔhuá-shān.
