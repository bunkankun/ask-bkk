# Yuánwù 圓悟 (Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟)

Late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Zì* Juéchū 覺初; *hào* Mìyún 密雲 ("Dense Clouds"); also Tiāntóng 天童 (for Tiāntóng-sì 天童寺 in Míng-zhōu 明州, where he was abbot), Jīnsù 金粟 (for Jīnsù-sì 金粟寺 in Hǎiyán 海鹽). Imperial title *Huì-dìng chánshī* 慧定禪師. Lay surname Jiǎng 蔣. Lifedates 1567/1/5 (Jiājìng 45 / 11/16) – 1642/8/2 (Chóngzhēn 15 / 7/7), age 76 (per the *Mìyún chánshī yǔlù* 密雲禪師語錄 juan 12 annalistic chart). Native of Yíxīng 宜興 (Jiāngsū).

The dominant mid-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master, whose lineage-line came to constitute the most prolific branch of late-Míng / early-Qīng Chán. Dharma-heir of Huàn-yǒu Zhèngchuán 幻有正傳 of Lóngchí 龍池. Received *fù fǎ* 付法 in 1611; abbacies at Lóngchí 龍池, Tiāntóng 天童, Yùwáng 育王, Jīnsù 金粟, and Tōng-xuán-sì 通玄寺 (also in Jiāngsū). His dharma-heirs formed one of the most important teaching networks of seventeenth-century Chinese Buddhism, including [[法藏|Hànyuè Fǎzàng]] 漢月法藏 (of the controversial Sānfēng sub-lineage), Fèi-yǐn Tōngróng 費隱通容, Mù-chén Dào-mín 木陳道忞 (spiritual advisor to the Shùnzhì emperor), Pò-shān [[海明|Hǎimíng]] 破山海明 (the Sichuan Chán reviver), and many others.

Major works:
- *Mìyún chánshī yǔlù* 密雲禪師語錄 (12 juan, J10 B150) — his collected Chán sermons and annals.
- *Pì wàng jiù lüè shuō* 闢妄救略說 [[KR6q0168]] — his 1638 polemical rebuttal of Fǎzàng's *Wǔ zōng yuán*, central to the major seventeenth-century Chán controversy.
- Several shorter treatises and sermon collections.

Historical significance: the master-lineage of nearly every prominent early-Qīng Chán master traces back to Yuánwù; his polemic against Fǎzàng's *Wǔ zōng yuán* constituted the "official" pro-direct-transmission position that the Yōngzhèng emperor's 1733 *Jiǎn mó biàn yì lù* would codify as imperial orthodoxy.

# Yuánwù 圓悟 (Kū-yá Yuán-wù 枯崖圓悟)

Late-Southern-Sòng Línjì Chán master; *hào* Kū-yá 枯崖 ("Dry Cliff"). Native of **Fú-qīng** 福清 in Fú-zhōu 福州 (modern Fujian). According to the prefatory matter of his work, he returned to **Jìng-shān Méng-táng** 徑山蒙堂 in **Jǐng-dìng** 景定 4 (1263), where he assembled the materials he had collected over years of monastic travel — accounts of awakening-experiences (*rù-dào jī-yuán* 入道機緣) of senior monks, sermon-transcripts (*shì-zhòng fǎ-yǔ* 示眾法語), and fragmentary inscriptions of figures whose names had not yet been registered in the *dēng-lù* 燈錄 lineage-records — into a 2-juan compendium titled **《枯崖漫錄》** *Kū-yá màn-lù* ([[KR6r0096]], X87 no. 1613). In **Xián-chún** 咸淳 8 (1272) he was installed as abbot of **Quán-nán Xīng-fú-sì** 泉南興福寺 in his home prefecture.

Per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A008261. Distinct from the late-Míng Línjì master Mìyún Yuán-wù 密雲圓悟 (1567–1642, DILA A003688) above; also distinct from **Yuán-wù Kè-qín** 圓悟克勤 (1063–1135, DILA A003687), the great Northern-Sòng Línjì master and author of the *Bì-yán lù* 碧巖錄.

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6r0096]] *Kū-yá màn-lù*.
