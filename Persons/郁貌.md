# 郁貌

Yù Mào of Wújiāng 吳江, mid-Míng medical student; wrote the *Jiājìng dīngwèi* 嘉靖丁未 (1547) prefatory colophon to [[KR3em039]] *Kǒuchǐ lèiyào* 口齒類要, recording a case in which [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ ("Lìzhāi wēng" 立齋翁) successfully treated his uncle Zhīyán's *chǐgēn fúzhǒng*.
