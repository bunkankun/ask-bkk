Yuán-zhào 元照 (1048–1116), styled *Dà-zhì lǜshī* 大智律師 ("Great-Wisdom Vinaya Master"), Northern-Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk best known as the principal Sòng *Lǜ-zōng* 律宗 (Vinaya School) revivalist and a major Pure Land commentator. Resident at the Líng-zhī-sì 靈芝寺 in Hángzhōu. Author of the [[KR6f0077|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng yì shū* 觀無量壽佛經義疏]] (T1754) — the *Vinaya-school* reading of the Pure Land *Visualization Sūtra* — and many other Vinaya and Pure Land commentaries.
