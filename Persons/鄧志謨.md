Dèng Zhìmó 鄧志謨 (*fl.* 1596–1626) was a Jiànyáng 建陽 / Fújiàn vernacular writer, encyclopaedist, and editor in the late-Wànlì commercial publishing scene. His seal-line on the surviving prefaces of *Tánhuǒ diǎnxuě* ([[KR3eh040]]) fixes the late-Wànlì to Tiānqǐ era as the principal period of composition.
