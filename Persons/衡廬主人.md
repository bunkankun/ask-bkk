**Héng-lú zhǔ-rén** 衡廬主人 ("the Master of the Héng-lú studio", probably evoking Héng-shān 衡山 in Húnán) is the *hào* of an unidentified Republican-period physician-editor. According to the preface by Shū-xiāng jū-shì 蔬香居士 to the 1925 reprinting of Zhōu Zhèn's *Yòu-kē zhǐ-nán* 幼科指南 ([[KR3ej016]]), Héng-lú zhǔ-rén had spent twenty years in *fēng-chén* 風塵 (dusty travels) in government administration and judicial service, then in frustration retired to medical study, composing his own *Fù-kē zhǐ-nán* 婦科指南 and *Yòu-kē zhǐ-nán* (the latter being the reprinting of Zhōu Zhèn's work). The personal name has not been identified.
