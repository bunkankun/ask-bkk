**Hénglú zhǔrén** 衡廬主人 ("the Master of the Hénglú studio", probably evoking Héngshān 衡山 in Húnán) is the *hào* of an unidentified Republican-period physician-editor. According to the preface by Shūxiāng jūshì 蔬香居士 to the 1925 reprinting of Zhōu Zhèn's *Yòukē zhǐnán* 幼科指南 ([[KR3ej016]]), Hénglú zhǔrén had spent twenty years in *fēngchén* 風塵 (dusty travels) in government administration and judicial service, then in frustration retired to medical study, composing his own *Fùkē zhǐnán* 婦科指南 and *Yòukē zhǐnán* (the latter being the reprinting of Zhōu Zhèn's work). The personal name has not been identified.
