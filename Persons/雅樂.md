Yǎ-lè 雅樂, the eldest son of the late-Qīng physician [[蘭陔先生]] Lán-gāi xiānshēng. He transcribed and edited his father's clinical cases into the fifteen-*juǎn* compilation [[KR3ep067]] *Shèn-wǔ-táng zhì-yàn lù*, following his father's instruction that the cases be preserved "with all year-month-locality-prescription detail intact." His editorial preface is dated to the compilation period 1862–1879 (Tóng-zhì 1 to Guāng-xù 5). No further transmitted biographical record.
