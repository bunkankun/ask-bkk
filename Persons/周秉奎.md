**Zhōu Bǐngkuí** 周秉奎 (*zì* Yízhēn 頤貞), late-Qīng physician of three decades' clinical practice who in 1861 (Xiánfēng 11, *xīnyǒu*) wrote the first postface to [[KR3eq015]] *Yīyuán* of [[石壽棠]] Shí Shòutáng, urging its first print run.
