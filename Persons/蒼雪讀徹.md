# Cāngxuě Dútǐ 蒼雪讀徹

Late-Míng / early-Qīng Yúnnán Huáyán master, native of Yúnnán, active in the Jiāngnán Buddhist circles. Lifedates 1586–1656. To be distinguished from the contemporary Línjì-line Chán master Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695), with whom the *hào*-element 雪 leads to occasional confusion.
