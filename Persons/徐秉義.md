**Xú Bǐngyì** 徐秉義 (*zì* Guǒtíng 果亭, 1633–1711), early-Qīng Kūnshān 崑山 (Sūzhōu) high-ranking official and *kǎojù* scholar. *Tànhuā* (third place in the 1673 *jìnshì* examination); served as *zuǒ shìláng* of the Ministry of Personnel and as one of the principal compilers of the *Míngshǐ* 明史 in the late seventeenth century. Younger brother of the much more famous Xú Qiánxué 徐乾學 and the great *kǎojù* scholar Xú Yuánwén 徐元文 — the three *Xú* brothers of Kūnshān ("崑山三徐") were one of the dominant early-Qīng *kǎojù* families of JiāngNán. In 1691 (Kāngxī 30) Xú Bǐngyì supplied the prefatory framing of [[李用粹]] Lǐ Yòngcuì's *Zhèngzhì huìbǔ* 證治彙補 ([[KR3er015]]), positioning the work as the medical counterpart to the *kǎojù* scholarly programme. Lifedates 1633–1711 follow the consensus of Qīng-period biographical reference works.
