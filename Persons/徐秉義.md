**Xú Bǐng-yì** 徐秉義 (*zì* Guǒ-tíng 果亭, 1633–1711), early-Qīng Kūn-shān 崑山 (Sūzhōu) high-ranking official and *kǎo-jù* scholar. *Tàn-huā* (third place in the 1673 *jìn-shì* examination); served as *zuǒ shì-láng* of the Ministry of Personnel and as one of the principal compilers of the *Míng-shǐ* 明史 in the late seventeenth century. Younger brother of the much more famous Xú Qián-xué 徐乾學 and the great *kǎo-jù* scholar Xú Yuán-wén 徐元文 — the three *Xú* brothers of Kūn-shān ("崑山三徐") were one of the dominant early-Qīng *kǎo-jù* families of Jiāng-Nán. In 1691 (Kāng-xī 30) Xú Bǐng-yì supplied the prefatory framing of [[李用粹]] Lǐ Yòng-cuì's *Zhèng-zhì huì-bǔ* 證治彙補 ([[KR3er015]]), positioning the work as the medical counterpart to the *kǎo-jù* scholarly programme. Lifedates 1633–1711 follow the consensus of Qīng-period biographical reference works.
