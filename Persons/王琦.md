# Wáng Qí 王琦 (fl. *ca.* 1740–1770)

*Zì* Zhuóyái 琢崖. Native of *Qiántáng* 錢塘 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). Qīng *Qiánlóng* 乾隆-period commercial-publishing scholar and editor of Táng poetic *biéjí*. The catalog meta dates him to "fl. 1760," consistent with the publication dates of his major works (the *Lǐ Chángjí gēshī huìjiě* 李長吉歌詩彙解 of *Qiánlóng* 12 = 1747 and the *Lǐ Tàibái jí zhù* of *Qiánlóng* 23 = 1758).

Wáng's principal scholarly contribution is the standard Qīng critical and annotated edition of Lǐ Bái's [[李白]] collected works — the *Lǐ Tàibái jí zhù* [[KR4c0014]] in 36 *juǎn* (30 of text + 6 of appendix) — which integrates the Sòng-Yuán annotations of Yáng Qíxián [[楊齊賢]] and Xiāo Shìyún [[蕭士贇]] (= [[KR4c0013]]) with substantial supplementary *kǎojù*. This edition has remained the foundational modern reference for Lǐ Bái: every modern scholarly edition (Hàn Wèi 韓威 et al., 1977; Yú Xiánhào 郁賢皓) is built directly on it.

His parallel *Lǐ Chángjí gēshī huìjiě* 李長吉歌詩彙解 — the standard Qīng annotated edition of the late-Táng poet Lǐ Hè 李賀 (790–816) — is similarly the basis of all modern Lǐ Hè scholarship. He is sometimes confused with the slightly older Míng-period Wáng Qí 王琦 of the *Sān cái túhuì* 三才圖會 (1607); these are different individuals. CBDB has no entry.

Wáng also took editorial interest in medical and other scholarly publications: his 1764 (*Qiánlóng jiǎshēn* 乾隆甲申) postface to [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jǐngyuè's **[[KR3eq016]]** *Zhìyí lù* 質疑錄 — signed *Qiánjiāng Wáng Qí* 錢江王琦 (a Qiántáng / Hángzhōu variant) — is one of the better mid-Qīng medical-historical analyses, identifying the *Zhìyí lù* as Zhāng's "晚年未定稿" (late-years unfinalised draft) and adjudicating Zhāng's late revisions against the earlier *Lèijīng* / *Jǐngyuè quánshū* statements with characteristic Qián-lóng-era *kǎojù* care. His 1769 (*Qiánlóng jǐchǒu* 乾隆己丑) postface to [[張志聰]] Zhāng Yǐnān's **[[KR3eq018]]** *Lǚshāntáng lèibiàn* 侶山堂類辯 is signed *Xūshān lǎorén* 胥山老人 ("Old Man of Xūshān") and adds important supplementary evidence on the late-Míng / early-Qīng Hángzhōu medical-circle traditions.
