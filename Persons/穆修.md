Early-Northern-Sòng *gǔwén* polemicist and *Yìxué* transmitter, *zì* Bócháng 伯長, of *Wènyáng* 汶陽 (Shāndōng). *Jìnshì* of *Dàzhōngxiángfú* 2 / 1009 (Liáng Gù 梁固's class). First office *Tàizhōu sīlǐ cānjūn* (whence the conventional appellation "Aide-de-Camp Mù" *Mù cānjūn* 穆參軍); demoted to *Chízhōu* on a slander charge, then to literary clerkships of *Yǐng* and *Cài*. Died of illness *Míngdào* 1 / 1032. Carried the *gǔwén* program from Liǔ Kāi [[柳開]] to Yǐn Zhū [[尹洙]] (his pupil) and thence to Ōuyáng Xiū — the second link of the canonical Sòng *gǔwén* lineage. Studied *shùxué* under Chén Tuán's tradition (probably via Lǐ Zhīcái 李之才) and is a hinge in the transmission of the *Xiāntiān tú* 先天圖 numerological-cosmological diagrams that reach Shào Yōng 邵雍. Privately printed Hán Yù 韓愈 and Liǔ Zōngyuán 柳宗元 collected works — one of the earliest documented private *gǔwén* publishing programs of the Sòng. Surviving collection: *Mù cānjūn jí* [[KR4d0014]] (3 *juǎn* + 1 *juǎn yíshì*, edited by Zǔ Wúzé [[祖無擇]] in 1043). CBDB id 27759, lifedates 979–1032.
