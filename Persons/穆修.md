Early-Northern-Sòng *gǔ-wén* polemicist and *Yì-xué* transmitter, *zì* Bó-cháng 伯長, of *Wèn-yáng* 汶陽 (Shāndōng). *Jìn-shì* of *Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú* 2 / 1009 (Liáng Gù 梁固's class). First office *Tài-zhōu sī-lǐ cān-jūn* (whence the conventional appellation "Aide-de-Camp Mù" *Mù cān-jūn* 穆參軍); demoted to *Chí-zhōu* on a slander charge, then to literary clerkships of *Yǐng* and *Cài*. Died of illness *Míng-dào* 1 / 1032. Carried the *gǔ-wén* program from Liǔ Kāi [[柳開]] to Yǐn Zhū [[尹洙]] (his pupil) and thence to Ōuyáng Xiū — the second link of the canonical Sòng *gǔ-wén* lineage. Studied *shù-xué* under Chén Tuán's tradition (probably via Lǐ Zhī-cái 李之才) and is a hinge in the transmission of the *Xiān-tiān tú* 先天圖 numerological-cosmological diagrams that reach Shào Yōng 邵雍. Privately printed Hán Yù 韓愈 and Liǔ Zōng-yuán 柳宗元 collected works — one of the earliest documented private *gǔ-wén* publishing programs of the Sòng. Surviving collection: *Mù cān-jūn jí* [[KR4d0014]] (3 *juǎn* + 1 *juǎn yí-shì*, edited by Zǔ Wú-zé [[祖無擇]] in 1043). CBDB id 27759, lifedates 979–1032.
