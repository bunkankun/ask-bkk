**Zhāng Xī-liáng** 張希良, early-Qīng scholar-official of Huáng-ān 黃安 (Hú-běi). Served as *sī-duó* 司鐸 (Provincial Education Officer) of Jiāng-xià 江夏 (Wǔ-hàn). A former Hanlin academic (*mù-tiān qīng-lěng zhī guān* 木天清冷之官) with responsibility over the *shì-xí* 士習 (scholarly mores) of Zhèjiāng. Prefaced Chéng Yúnpéng 程雲鵬's *Cí-yòu xīnshū* 慈幼新書 ([[KR3ej022]]) in the late seventeenth century; the preface is one of the principal biographical witnesses for Chéng's career and his association with the Qīng scholar Wèi Xǐ 魏禧.
