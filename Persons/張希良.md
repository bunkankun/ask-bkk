**Zhāng Xīliáng** 張希良, early-Qīng scholar-official of Huángān 黃安 (Húběi). Served as *sīduó* 司鐸 (Provincial Education Officer) of Jiāngxià 江夏 (Wǔhàn). A former Hanlin academic (*mùtiān qīnglěng zhī guān* 木天清冷之官) with responsibility over the *shìxí* 士習 (scholarly mores) of Zhèjiāng. Prefaced Chéng Yúnpéng 程雲鵬's *Cíyòu xīnshū* 慈幼新書 ([[KR3ej022]]) in the late seventeenth century; the preface is one of the principal biographical witnesses for Chéng's career and his association with the Qīng scholar Wèi Xǐ 魏禧.
