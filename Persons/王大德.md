**Wáng Dàdé** 王大德 (*zì* Fānchuān 帆川, *fl.* Yōngzhèng, 清), early-Qīng physician and book-collector active at Jīntáng 金堂 (Sìchuān). Transmitter of two related early-Qīng surgical manuscripts: [[KR3ek019]] *Qīngnáng mìjué* 青囊秘訣 (preface dated Yōngzhèng 1 *guǐmǎo qiǎoyuè* = autumn 1723), and [[KR3ek031]] *Fùshì wàikē* 傅氏外科 (parallel transmission of overlapping material). In both prefaces Wáng records the same narrative — borrowing the manuscript from a friend (in the *Qīngnáng mìjué* case, ultimately from the Daoist Zhāng Shìxué 張士學 of the temple at Jìyuán 濟源, Huáiqìng 懷慶, Hénán), copying it under summer-heat conditions, and testing the methods clinically on *duìkǒu* 對口 (nape carbuncles) and *zhìlòu* 痔漏 (anal fistulas) with consistent success. Wáng's transcripts are the earliest dated witnesses to the surgical material conventionally attributed to [[傅山]] Fù Shān. Not in CBDB.
