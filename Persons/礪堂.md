**Lìtáng** 礪堂 (*fl.* 1786), editorial pseudonym ("Mr Lìtáng of Yuètián at Yéxī" 耶溪月田礪堂氏) of the late-*Qiánlóng* compiler-editor of the *Níngkūn mìjí* 寧坤秘笈 ([[KR3ei013]]). The pseudonym situates the compiler in the Yéxī / Zhūjì / Shàoxīng area of Zhèjiāng. No further biographical identification located.
