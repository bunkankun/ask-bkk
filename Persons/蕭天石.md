**Xiāo Tiānshí** 蕭天石 (1908–1986), 20th-century Daoist scholar and editor; compiler of the *Dàozàng jīnghuá* 道藏精華 series (Táiběi, 1956 ff.) in which Wāng Dōngtíng's *Xìngmìng yàozhǐ* is anthologised.
