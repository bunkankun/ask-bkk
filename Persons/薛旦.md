Xuē Dàn 薛旦 (Sòng), nephew of Xuē Jìxuān [[薛季宣]] and editor of the latter's *Lànɡyǔ jí*. Held office as *Zhī Fǔzhōu* 知撫州 in *Bǎoqìng* 2 (1226), during which year he edited and printed his uncle's collection [[KR4d0264]] with his own postface (preserved in the WYG). Not to be confused with the Míng-Qīng dramatist Xuē Dàn 薛旦 (b. 1617, CBDB id 691036), a different figure. CBDB does not record this Sòng Xuē Dàn separately.

Xuē Dàn 薛旦 (1617–after 1702, Míng-Qīng playwright). Active in the late Míng and early Qīng. Author of dramatic works including *Cháohuā mèng* 朝華夢. Distinct from the Sòng-era editor of [[KR4d0264]]. CBDB id 691036.
