Narendrayaśas (Nà-lián-tí-yē-shè 那連提耶舍, "King-of-Men Glory"), Northern-Indian Buddhist translator who arrived in Northern-Qí China c. 556 and worked there and subsequently in the Suí period until his death in 589. Native of *Wū-cháng* 烏萇 (Uḍḍiyāna). Per the *Xù gāosēng zhuàn* (T2060), his most consequential translation is the [[KR6g0026|*Dà bēi jīng* 大悲經]] (T0380, 5 fasc.), the *Karuṇā-puṇḍarīka-sūtra*.
