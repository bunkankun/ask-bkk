Southern-Sòng official, grandson of [[張根]] Zhāng Gēn. He composed the postface to the *Wúyuán Zhōuyì jiě* ([[KR1a0019]]) — the principal biographical witness for Zhāng Gēn's now-lost wider corpus of Five Classics commentaries and the *Sòngcháo biānnián* 宋朝編年.
