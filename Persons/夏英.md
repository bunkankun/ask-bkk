**Xià Yīng** 夏英 (*zì* Shíyàn 時彥, fl. mid-15th c., 明), early-Míng physician of Rén-hé 仁和 (Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). A hereditary literatus-physician ("studied medicine as a Confucian scholar"). Author of the *Língshū jīng-mài yì* 靈樞經脈翼 ([[KR3ee024]]), a *fāhuī*-style expansion of Huá Shòu's *Shísì jīng fāhuī* ([[KR3ee026]]). No further biographical record survives; CBDB has no entry that can be matched with confidence to this Ming physician.
