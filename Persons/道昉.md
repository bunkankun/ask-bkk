Dào Fǎng 道昉 (fl. late Míng) was a disciple-redactor of **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕩益智旭 ([[智旭]], 1599–1655). His role was the editorial revision (訂 *dìng*) of Zhìxù's *Fànwǎng-jīng xuán-yì* ([[KR6k0096]]) and *Fànwǎng-jīng hé-zhù* ([[KR6k0097]]). He is one of the principal lineage transmitters of the late-Míng Vinaya revival within the Línjì-Tiāntái synthesis represented by Zhìxù.
