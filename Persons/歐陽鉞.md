Ōuyáng Yuè 歐陽鉞, Míng *Wànlì*-period editor, twentieth-generation descendant of Ōuyáng Chè 歐陽澈 [[歐陽澈]]. Compiled the *Wànlì jiǎyín* (1614) recutting of his ancestor's *Ōuyáng Xiūzhuàn jí* [[KR4d0189]], with preface by Wú Dàonán 吴道南. CBDB has no clearly-matching entry.

The *Sìkù* tíyào of [[KR4d0189]] lists his contribution as appendix-edition (*juǎn* 8 — the section containing Chén Dōng's memorials) — this appendix-*juǎn* is missing from the WYG recension; the *Sìkù* editors fix the count at 7 *juǎn*.
