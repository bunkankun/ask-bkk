Ōu-yáng Yuè 歐陽鉞, Míng *Wàn-lì*-period editor, twentieth-generation descendant of Ōu-yáng Chè 歐陽澈 [[歐陽澈]]. Compiled the *Wàn-lì jiǎ-yín* (1614) recutting of his ancestor's *Ōu-yáng Xiū-zhuàn jí* [[KR4d0189]], with preface by Wú Dào-nán 吴道南. CBDB has no clearly-matching entry.

The *Sì-kù* tíyào of [[KR4d0189]] lists his contribution as appendix-edition (*juǎn* 8 — the section containing Chén Dōng's memorials) — this appendix-*juǎn* is missing from the WYG recension; the *Sì-kù* editors fix the count at 7 *juǎn*.
