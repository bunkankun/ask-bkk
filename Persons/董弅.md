# Dǒng Fén 董弅 (fl. 1133–1162)

Native of Dōng-píng 東平 (Shāndōng). Son of the Northern-Sòng courtier **Dǒng Yóu** 董逌 (compiler of the *Guǎng-chuān shū-bá* 廣川書跋 and *Guǎng-chuān huà-bá* 廣川畫跋). Dǒng Fén styled himself *Guǎng-chuān* to claim descent from the Western Hàn classicist Dǒng Zhòng-shū. During the *Shào-xīng* era he served as **Prefect of Yán-zhōu** 嚴州 (modern Jiàn-dé in western Zhèjiāng), where he produced the *Yán-líng jí* 嚴陵集 [[KR4h0036]] in 9 *juǎn* — the principal Sòng anthology of literature on Yán-zhōu / Yán-líng (the realm of the Eastern-Hàn recluse Yán Guāng), prefaced in *Shào-xīng* 9 (1139). He worked on the project with his subordinate the *jiào-shòu* Shěn Sù 沈愫 and drew on a private library held by the local Yú Yàn-xiān 喻彥先. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
