# Dǒng Fén 董弅 (fl. 1133–1162)

Native of Dōngpíng 東平 (Shāndōng). Son of the Northern-Sòng courtier **Dǒng Yóu** 董逌 (compiler of the *Guǎngchuān shūbá* 廣川書跋 and *Guǎngchuān huàbá* 廣川畫跋). Dǒng Fén styled himself *Guǎngchuān* to claim descent from the Western Hàn classicist Dǒng Zhòngshū. During the *Shàoxīng* era he served as **Prefect of Yánzhōu** 嚴州 (modern Jiàndé in western Zhèjiāng), where he produced the *Yánlíng jí* 嚴陵集 [[KR4h0036]] in 9 *juǎn* — the principal Sòng anthology of literature on Yánzhōu / Yánlíng (the realm of the Eastern-Hàn recluse Yán Guāng), prefaced in *Shàoxīng* 9 (1139). He worked on the project with his subordinate the *jiàoshòu* Shěn Sù 沈愫 and drew on a private library held by the local Yú Yànxiān 喻彥先. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
