**Jikkei** 實慶 — Early-to-mid Edo-period Japanese Sanron-school monk; *ājari* 阿闍梨 of **Sonkō-in** 尊光院, a sub-temple of **Tōdaiji Shingon-in** 東大寺眞言院 at Nara. His sole canonical work is *Sān-lùn-zōng chū-xīn chū-xué chāo* 三論宗初心初學鈔 ([[KR6t0004]], T70n2308), a single-fascicle introductory primer for novice Sanron students; the work survives only in a 1685 transcript made from Jikkei's own manuscript draft (草) at the Shingon-in South-One Cell.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001614 records him solely through this canonical attribution; no biographical sources beyond the work's transmission colophons survive. The 1685 *terminus ante quem* and the surviving draft state of the manuscript suggest a working life in the second-third quarter of the 17th century. He is part of the small Edo-period **Sonkō-in Sanron-Shingon revivalist tradition** centred at Tōdaiji, and was succeeded in transmission by **Jūkei** 重慶 律師, who copied the manuscript and passed it to Shōken 性憲 in 1692.
