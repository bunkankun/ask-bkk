**Zhāng Shítài** 張時泰 (Míng, fl. late 15th c.). Author of the *Guǎngyì* 廣義 (broad explications) appended to Shāng Lù's [[KR2o0022]] *Xù Zīzhì tōngjiàn gāngmù* 續資治通鑑綱目. Like Zhōu Lǐ (the *Fāmíng* commentator), Zhāng was a *Chénghuà*-Hóngzhì-era Wù-Zhē *Gāngmù*-school scholar working in the orbit of Shāng Lù; biographical details and lifedates are not preserved. CBDB has two undated homonym records (ids 59704, 227402); neither can be confidently identified with this commentator. The *Sìkù* tiyao groups the *Guǎngyì* with Zhōu Lǐ's *Fāmíng* in its critical verdict on the *Xù biān*'s commentaries.
