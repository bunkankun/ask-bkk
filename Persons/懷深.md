# Huái-shēn 懷深

Late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng Yún-mén-zōng 雲門宗 Chán master, *zì* Cí-shòu 慈受, *hào* Pǔ-zhào 普照. Dharma-heir of Jìng-zhào Chóng-xìn 淨照崇信. Native of Liù-ān 六安 (Shòu-chūn), lay surname Xià 夏. 1077 – 14 May 1132 (lunar 紹興 2.4.20), *shì-shòu* 56, *sēng-là* 36. For a short tenure abbot of the imperial-court Huì-lín chán-yuàn 慧林禪院 in the Sòng capital, in successor-line to [[宗本]] Yuán-zhào Zōng-běn.

Six abbacies (per Hán Jū's 1133 preface, *sān fèng tiān-zǐ zhī mìng* 三奉天子之命 "three-times receiving imperial command"): Yí-zhēn Zī-fú 資福 (from Zhèng-hé era c. 1111), Jiāo-shān-sì 焦山寺 (by imperial edict, four years), Dōng-jīng Huì-lín 慧林, Líng-yán 靈巖, Jiǎng-shān 蔣山 (briefly, by imperial edict), Dòng-tíng Bāo-shān 洞庭包山 (retreat), and finally Yuán-jué-sì 圓覺寺 as inaugural abbot. Died in 1132.

His four-juan *guǎng-lù* 廣錄 ([[KR6q0381]]; X73 n1451) was compiled by four of his *shì-zhě* — [[善清]], [[善隨]], [[宗先]], [[普紹]] (a named dharma-heir). Seven named dharma-heirs total per *Jiā-tài pǔ-dēng-lù* *juan* 9, led by Jì-shì Huì-guāng 寂室慧光 and Chuí-cí Pǔ-shào 垂慈普紹.
