**Shèng Jǐng-yún** 盛景雲 (*fl.* mid-to-late Qīng), Qīng literatus-physician of moderate clinical learning. Compiler of the *Yìshì jīngyàn liángfāng* 益世經驗良方 ([[KR3ed133]]) in 1 *juǎn* — a Qīng popular-clinical formulary drawing primarily on the *Lǐ Shízhēn Běncǎo gāngmù* with supplementary tested-formulas. The compiler is identified in the surviving preface only as the preface-writer's *zú* (lineage-member). Sparse further biographical record.
