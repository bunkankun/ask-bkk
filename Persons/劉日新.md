Liú Rìxīn 劉日新, late-Sòng publisher who in *Kāixǐ* 1 (1205) issued the first cutting of [[王宗傳]] Wáng Zōngchuán's *Tóngxī Yì zhuàn* 童溪易傳 ([[KR1a0047]]), some twenty years after the work's completion (1184–1186). Solicited a preface for the printing through Wáng Zōngchuán's clansman [[王䮐]] Wáng Yú, who in turn approached [[林焞]] Lín Tūn.
