Dùnlín 遁麟 (also known as 富春沙門 "the monk from Fùchūn [in Zhejiang]"; DILA A001581; fl. mid-to-late 8th century CE) was a Tang dynasty Abhidharma monk and student of 圓暉 ([[圓暉]]). He composed the 俱舍論頌疏記 ([[KR6l0041]], X53n841, 29 juan), a sub-commentary on 圓暉's *Jùshě lùn sòng shū* ([[KR6l0036]]). The designation "富春沙門" situates him geographically in Fùchūn 富春, Wúxīng 吳興 area (present-day Zhejiang). Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6l0041]] 俱舍論頌疏記.
