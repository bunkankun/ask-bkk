# Pǔ Tiānxiáng 朴天祥 (Pak Ch'ŏnsang)

Korean lay devotee, named in the *bá* 跋 colophon of [[延壽]] Yánshòu's *Shòu púsà jiè fǎ* 受菩薩戒法 ([[KR6k0251]], X1088-A) as the filial-son sponsor of a Koryŏ–Chosŏn private printing commemorating his deceased mother of the Lǐ 李 lineage. The colophon also names a *Xìngānzháizhǔ* 興安宅主 and a *Xiānfù Shùnpíngjūn* 順平君 (the latter a Korean noble title; *Xiānfù* "deceased father"). The colophon postdates Yánshòu by several centuries and is the principal witness to the work's circulation as a posthumous private-print devotional item on the Korean peninsula. No further biographical information preserved.
