Sūn Tíngquán 孫廷銓 (1613–1674), *zì* Bódù 伯度, also *zì* Méixiān 枚先, *hào* Zhǐtíng 沚亭; posthumous title *Wéndìng* 文定. Native of Yìdū 益都 in Shāndōng — specifically of the Yánshénzhèn 顏神鎮 / modern Bóshān 博山 region. Late-Míng / early-Qīng official and *bǐjì* author. *Chóngzhēn gēngchén jìnshì* (1640) under the Míng. Entered Qīng service by recommendation; under Shùnzhì served as *Hénánfǔ tuīguān*, then promoted *Lìbù zhǔshì*; under early Kāngxī rose to *Lìbù shàngshū* and finally Grand Secretary of the *Nèi mìshūyuàn* (Inner Secret Council). Retired in *Kāngxī* 5 (1666); during retirement at Yánshénzhèn composed the *Yánshān zájì* 顏山雜記 ([[KR2k0128]]) — a literarily distinguished and technologically important *bǐjì* on his home district, prized by Wáng Shìzhēn — and the *Nánzhēng jìlüè* 南征記略 (a memoir of his southern campaigns). Biography in *Qīngshǐ gǎo* 264. CBDB id 57021.
