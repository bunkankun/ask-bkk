Sūn Tíng-quán 孫廷銓 (1613–1674), *zì* Bó-dù 伯度, also *zì* Méi-xiān 枚先, *hào* Zhǐ-tíng 沚亭; posthumous title *Wén-dìng* 文定. Native of Yì-dū 益都 in Shāndōng — specifically of the Yán-shén-zhèn 顏神鎮 / modern Bó-shān 博山 region. Late-Míng / early-Qīng official and *bǐ-jì* author. *Chóngzhēn gēng-chén jìnshì* (1640) under the Míng. Entered Qīng service by recommendation; under Shùn-zhì served as *Hé-nán-fǔ tuī-guān*, then promoted *Lì-bù zhǔ-shì*; under early Kāng-xī rose to *Lì-bù shàng-shū* and finally Grand Secretary of the *Nèi mì-shū-yuàn* (Inner Secret Council). Retired in *Kāng-xī* 5 (1666); during retirement at Yán-shén-zhèn composed the *Yán-shān zájì* 顏山雜記 ([[KR2k0128]]) — a literarily distinguished and technologically important *bǐ-jì* on his home district, prized by Wáng Shìzhēn — and the *Nán-zhēng jì-lüè* 南征記略 (a memoir of his southern campaigns). Biography in *Qīng-shǐ gǎo* 264. CBDB id 57021.
