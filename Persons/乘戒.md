# Chéngjiè 乘戒

A late-Qīng monk, dharma-name pair **Chéng-jiè / Dìng-huì** 乘戒／定慧 ("vehicle-and-precepts / *samādhi*-and-*prajñā*"), self-designated *Hǎi-yǒng Xiǎo Bái-huā Hǎi-àn shān-rén* 海湧小白花海岸山人 ("Mountain-Wayfarer of the Sea-Surge Little White-Flower Coast"). The studio-name plausibly indicates a coastal-Zhèjiāng or *Pǔ-tuó-shān* 普陀山 association — *Bái-huā* 白花 ("White-Flower") being a standard literary periphrasis for *Pǔ-tuó-shān* (the bodhisattva-Avalokiteśvara island). Author of the **《唯心集》** *Wéi-xīn jí* [[KR6p0126]] (X1208), a short Pure Land devotional anthology in the *chán-jìng shuāng-xiū* 禪淨雙修 (Chán-Pure Land integration) tradition. Lifedates not preserved.
