**Léi Yìxiān** 雷逸仙 (early-to-mid 19th c., 清), father of [[雷豐]] Léi Fēng and a Qúzhōu 衢州 physician. Disciple of [[程芝田]] Chéng Zhītián. Known principally through Léi Fēng's *Shíbìng lùn* ([[KR3eg002]]) preface as a second-generation member of the Sānqú 三衢 (Qúzhōu) medical family.
