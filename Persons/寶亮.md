Bǎo-liàng 寶亮 (444–509), Liáng-period Buddhist scholar-monk; the principal Liáng *Niè-pán*-school authority and editor of the foundational *[[KR6g0004|Dà bān niè pán jīng jí jiě 大般涅槃經集解]]* (T1763, 71 fascicles) — the great compiled commentary on the *Mahāparinirvāṇa* drawing together the work of multiple earlier Niè-pán-school authorities. Native of Dōng-guǎn 東莞 (in modern Shāndōng).
