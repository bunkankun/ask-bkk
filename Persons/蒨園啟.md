# Qiànyuánqǐ 蒨園啟

Late-Edo Japanese member of the Tanba 丹波 medical-philological lineage; described in [[丹波元胤]] Tanba Genin's 1820 postface to *Huóyòu kǒuyì* 活幼口議 ([[KR3ej024]]) as the family member who acquired the complete copy of the work from the [[人見友雪]] Hitomi Yūsetsu (Rénjiàn Yǒuxuě) collection. Identity (alias of Tanba Genken or another family member) unclear.
