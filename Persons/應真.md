# Dānyuán Yìngzhēn 耽源應真

Mid-Táng Chán monk, *yícháng bùquē* chief disciple of the National Master [[慧忠|Nányáng Huìzhōng]] 南陽慧忠; transmitted the ninety-seven *yuán xiāng* 圓相 round-image teaching to the young [[慧寂|Yǎngshān Huìjì]] 仰山慧寂 as recorded in [[KR6q0076]].
