**Yè Fèngmáo** 葉鳳毛 (Qián-lóng-era Hīzhōu physician, fl. 1769), the prefacer of [[朱時進]] Zhū Shíjìn's *Yī jiàn néng yī* 一見能醫 ([[KR3er025]], 1769). Yè's preface, dated *Qiánlóng* 34 (1769), frames Zhū's clinical doctrine in the *rúyī* 儒醫 (Confucian-physician) tradition, polemicising against Zhāng Jièbīn's universal-vacuity-cold doctrine and against an unnamed contemporary anti-ginseng polemicist. Yè's biographical detail is otherwise unrecovered. No CBDB record.
