**Qí Ěrchéng** 祈爾誠 (*fl.* late Qīng), Qīng popular-medical editor; intermediary in the transmission of the manuscript source-text used for the *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē gēkuò* 傅青主女科歌括 ([[KR3ei021]]), supplied by [[韓寶臣]] Hán Bǎochén.
