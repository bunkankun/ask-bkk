Shǐ Yáobì 史堯弼 (*zì* Tángyīng 唐英, *hào* Liánfēng 蓮峯), of *Méizhōu* 眉州 (modern Sìchuān). *Sìchuān* literary prodigy: noticed at age 14 (per Zhōu Mì's *Hàorán-zhāi yǎtán*) for poetry composed at the *Lùmíng yàn*, where he answered Lǐ Tāo's 18-year-old *jiěyuán* by improvising on the rhyme-character *jiàn* with the line "*Four [years of] age, still less than Fáng Xuánlíng / Seven steps, not yet yielding to Cáo Zǐjiàn*". Studied with Zhāng Jùn 張浚 (who declared him "*greatly resembling Sū Dōngpō*") and with Zhāng Shì [[張栻]] at *Tánzhōu*. *Jìnshì* of *Shàoxīng dīngchǒu* (1157, with his younger brother Shǐ Yáowén 史堯文). Died young, before holding substantive office. CBDB id 10640. Author of [[KR4d0279]] *Liánfēng jí*.
