Sīmǎ Ráng-jū 司馬穰苴, original family name *Tián* 田 (Tián Ráng-jū 田穰苴), was a Spring-and-Autumn-era (the catalog dates -722 to -481 indicating the *Chūn-qiū* period) military commander of Qí 齊. Served Duke Jǐng 景公 of Qí (r. 547–490 BCE); raised in office to *Dà sī-mǎ* 大司馬 (Grand Marshal) — hence the family-house byname *Sīmǎ*. Biography: *Shǐjì* j. 64 (Sīmǎ Ráng-jū liè-zhuàn). Traditional author of the *Sīmǎ fǎ* 司馬法 (KR3b0005) — though the received text is most likely a Warring States compilation under his name, edited and contributed to during the King Wēi 威王 period of Qí (4th c. BCE) when the Qí court ordered Tián's military principles compiled.
