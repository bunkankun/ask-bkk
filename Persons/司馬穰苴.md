Sīmǎ Rángjū 司馬穰苴, original family name *Tián* 田 (Tián Rángjū 田穰苴), was a Spring-and-Autumn-era (the catalog dates -722 to -481 indicating the *Chūnqiū* period) military commander of Qí 齊. Served Duke Jǐng 景公 of Qí (r. 547–490 BCE); raised in office to *Dà sīmǎ* 大司馬 (Grand Marshal) — hence the family-house byname *Sīmǎ*. Biography: *Shǐjì* j. 64 (Sīmǎ Rángjū lièzhuàn). Traditional author of the *Sīmǎ fǎ* 司馬法 ([[KR3b0005]]) — though the received text is most likely a Warring States compilation under his name, edited and contributed to during the King Wēi 威王 period of Qí (4th c. BCE) when the Qí court ordered Tián's military principles compiled.
