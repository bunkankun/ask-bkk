**Qìng Shù** 慶恕 (*hào* Yún-gé 雲閣, *fl.* late *Guāng-xù* 光緒) — late-Qīng Manchu *zōng-shì* 宗室 (imperial-clan) official; *tài-shǒu* 太守 (prefect) in successive Gān-sù provincial postings during the 1890s; active in Běijīng *bù-cáo* 部曹 (capital-bureau) consulting practice during 1894 (*Guāng-xù jiǎ-wǔ*). Author of *Yī-xué zhāi-cuì* 醫學摘粹 ([[KR3er069]]), composed over 20+ years of clinical practice and first printed in 1897 under [[明保]] Míng-bǎo's preface. Not in CBDB.
