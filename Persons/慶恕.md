**Qìng Shù** 慶恕 (*hào* Yúngé 雲閣, *fl.* late *Guāngxù* 光緒) — late-Qīng Manchu *zōngshì* 宗室 (imperial-clan) official; *tàishǒu* 太守 (prefect) in successive Gānsù provincial postings during the 1890s; active in Běijīng *bùcáo* 部曹 (capital-bureau) consulting practice during 1894 (*Guāngxù jiǎwǔ*). Author of *Yīxué zhāicuì* 醫學摘粹 ([[KR3er069]]), composed over 20+ years of clinical practice and first printed in 1897 under [[明保]] Míngbǎo's preface. Not in CBDB.
