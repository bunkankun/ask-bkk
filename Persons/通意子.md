Tōngyìzǐ 通意子 (also signing as Xuánjī dòngzhǔ 璇璣洞主), pseudonymous late-Qīng scholar-physician, born c. 1824/25 (75 *suì* in 1899 per his self-preface to [[KR3ep062]] *Guànwéi jí*). The Daoist-coloured literary signatures (*Master of the Mystic-Mechanism Grotto*, *Master of Penetrating Intentionality*) cover an unrecoverable personal identity. His clinical activity spanned c. 1849–1899 and worked from a strong teacher-lineage; his single surviving casebook combines a *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* doctrinal foundation with a Yè-school light-descending prescription style. Not in CBDB.
