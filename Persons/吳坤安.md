**Wú Kūn'ān** 吳坤安 (*zì* Bì-Huá 璧華, *fl.* late Qiánlóng, 1750s–1796, 清), Tóngxiāng 桐鄉 (Zhèjiāng) physician. Author of the *Shānghán zhǐzhǎng* 傷寒指掌 ([[KR3ef032]], 4 juan, 1796), a pedagogical handbook synthesizing the *Shānghán* and *wēnbìng* clinical traditions, and the *Lèi zhèng pǔ jì běn shì fāng* 類證普濟本事方. His lifedates are not preserved. No CBDB record.
