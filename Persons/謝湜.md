Northern-Sòng *Yìxué* scholar, *zì* Chízhèng 持正. Pupil and correspondent of [[程頤]] Chéng Yí; the surviving letter from Chéng to Xiè Shí — preserved in [[邵伯溫]] Shào Bówēn's *Wénjiàn qián lù* 聞見前錄 — gives the locus classicus of Chéng's instruction on the *Yì*: "Reading the *Yì*, one should first study the three masters — [[王弼]] Wáng Bì, [[胡瑗]] Hú Yuán, and [[王安石]] Wáng Ānshí."
