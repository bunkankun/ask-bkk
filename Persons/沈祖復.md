Late-Qīng / early-Republican-period physician of Wúxī 無錫 (梁溪), Jiāngsū; ancestry traced to Húzhōu 湖州 (浙江), where the Sòng *jìnshì* ancestor Shěn Yǒukāi 沈有開 had served as 太常博士. *Zì* Lǐān 禮庵, *hào* Fèngjiāng 奉江, *biéshǔ* (later) Táiwēng 鮐翁 and Zhèshēng 蔗生 (the latter adopted after his recovery from cholera in 1902 via sugar-cane juice). Studied at the gate of the Mènghé 孟河 court physician Mǎ Péizhī 馬培之 (馬文植), after abandoning his examination career around 1894 with the slogan *dà zhàng fū bù wéi liáng xiàng dìng wéi liáng yī* 大丈夫不為良相定為良醫. From the late Guāngxū through the Republican period he practised in the Chóng'ān Sì 崇安寺 *Zhōng yǐn yuàn* 中隱院 in Wúxī. Co-founded the local *Zhōng yī yǒu yì huì* 中醫友誼會 and the journal *Yī zhōng* 醫鍾. The medical-case compilation [[KR3eb009]] *Yī yàn suí bǐ* was edited by his disciple Zhōu Féngrú 周逢儒 in 1924. His other writings — *Yī tōng* 醫通 (1 juan), *Chújīngtáng wén gǎo* 鋤經堂文稿, *Dìhuā jí* 棣華集 — are preserved in family editions but not in this catalog. No CBDB record.

The catalog meta for [[KR3eb009]] erroneously names "張文藻" as the author; the text itself is unambiguous in identifying Shěn as the clinician.
