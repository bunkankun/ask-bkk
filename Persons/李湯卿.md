**Lǐ Tāngqīng** 李湯卿 (*fl.* late Yuán, c. 1280–1368), Yuán medical theorist. Biographical data unrecovered: the sole transmitted attribution is to the *Xīnyìn gànzhū jīng* 心印紺珠經 ([[KR3eo021]]). Doctrinally aligned with the **Jīn-Yuán sìdà jiā** 金元四大家 tradition, particularly the *yùnqì* doctrine of Lǐ Gāo (Dōngyuán). The work was referenced in the early-Míng *Yīshǐ* 醫史 of Lǐ Lián 李濂 (1530), which provides the principal historical anchor for placing its composition in the late Yuán. No CBDB record.
