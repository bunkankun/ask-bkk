# Shìguī 士珪

Northern–Southern Sòng Chán master Zhúān Shìguī 竹菴士珪 (1083–1146); during his shared Jiāngxī residence with [[宗杲|Dàhuì Zōnggǎo]] in the 1130s–1140s the two jointly compiled the original *Chánlín bǎoxùn* ([[KR6q0099]]).
