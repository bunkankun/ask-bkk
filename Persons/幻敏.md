Huàn-mǐn 幻敏, *hào* **Zhú-fēng** 竺峰, was a Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668) in the Jù-yún-pài / Zhì-píng sub-line. Died **Kāng-xī 丁亥 3/12 卯時** = **1707/4/13 Gregorian** aged 70 *suì* (*sēng-là* 65) — yielding approximate birth-year **1638**. Native of **Fēng-líng** 酆陵 (= Fēng-dū 酆都, Sì-chuān), lay surname **Xú** 徐. Tonsured at **10 *suì*** with his fellow-disciple **Wàn-fēng Shàn chán-shī** 萬峰善禪師 under his maternal uncles **Fó-xǐ** 佛喜 and **Yě-yún** 野雲; full precepts at **Chóng-shèng** 崇聖; further training at **Wǔ-yún** 五雲; returned to receive transmission (*fù-fǎ* 付法) from Tiě-bì at Qìng-zhōng.

Long service under Tiě-bì — **twenty-one years** (事忠二十一年) — alongside his close associate the lay-disciple **Yuán Bǎo-shàn** 袁寶善. After Tiě-bì's death in 1668 the community invited him to succeed to **Zhì-píng** 治平; successive seats at **Qìng-zhōng-sì** 慶忠寺 (Liáng-shān), **Shí-gǔ** 石鼓, **Zhōng-zhōu Zhèn-zōng Chán-sì** 振宗禪寺 (the *chì-jiàn* imperially-bestowed seat), **Jiā-xīng Léng-yán Chán-sì** 楞嚴禪寺 (Zhè-jiāng — the publishing center for the Jiā-xīng Canon).

**Works.**
- Lead compiler of [[KR6q0451]] 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 20 juan, J29 B241, marked *chóng-biān*.
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0592]] 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 6 juan (+ 2 juan *hòu-lù*), J40 B483, compiled by four *fǎ-sì*: **Xìng-jù** [[性鉅]], **Xìng-zhàn** [[性湛]], **Xìng-héng** [[性珩]], **Xìng-jì** [[性濟]].
- 《募慶佛誕引》 for the Buddha's-birthday fund-raising on lunar 4/8 at Qìng-zhōng.

Thirty-three dharma-heirs (**Jiàn-táng** 鑑堂, **Qìng-táng** 慶堂, **Jué-táng** 覺堂, et al.) plus eighteen lay-disciples. Burial stūpa to the right of Tiě-bì's at Qìng-zhōng-sì.
