Huànmǐn 幻敏, *hào* **Zhúfēng** 竺峰, was a Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668) in the Jùyúnpài / Zhìpíng sub-line. Died **Kāngxī 丁亥 3/12 卯時** = **1707/4/13 Gregorian** aged 70 *suì* (*sēnglà* 65) — yielding approximate birth-year **1638**. Native of **Fēnglíng** 酆陵 (= Fēngdū 酆都, Sìchuān), lay surname **Xú** 徐. Tonsured at **10 *suì*** with his fellow-disciple **Wànfēng Shàn chánshī** 萬峰善禪師 under his maternal uncles **Fóxǐ** 佛喜 and **Yěyún** 野雲; full precepts at **Chóngshèng** 崇聖; further training at **Wǔyún** 五雲; returned to receive transmission (*fùfǎ* 付法) from Tiěbì at Qìngzhōng.

Long service under Tiěbì — **twenty-one years** (事忠二十一年) — alongside his close associate the lay-disciple **Yuán Bǎoshàn** 袁寶善. After Tiěbì's death in 1668 the community invited him to succeed to **Zhìpíng** 治平; successive seats at **Qìngzhōngsì** 慶忠寺 (Liángshān), **Shígǔ** 石鼓, **Zhōngzhōu Zhènzōng Chánsì** 振宗禪寺 (the *chìjiàn* imperially-bestowed seat), **Jiāxīng Léngyán Chánsì** 楞嚴禪寺 (Zhèjiāng — the publishing center for the Jiāxīng Canon).

**Works.**
- Lead compiler of [[KR6q0451]] 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 20 juan, J29 B241, marked *chóngbiān*.
- Own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0592]] 《竺峰敏禪師語錄》 6 juan (+ 2 juan *hòulù*), J40 B483, compiled by four *fǎsì*: **Xìngjù** [[性鉅]], **Xìngzhàn** [[性湛]], **Xìnghéng** [[性珩]], **Xìngjì** [[性濟]].
- 《募慶佛誕引》 for the Buddha's-birthday fund-raising on lunar 4/8 at Qìngzhōng.

Thirty-three dharma-heirs (**Jiàntáng** 鑑堂, **Qìngtáng** 慶堂, **Juétáng** 覺堂, et al.) plus eighteen lay-disciples. Burial stūpa to the right of Tiěbì's at Qìngzhōngsì.
