Late-Míng Chán monk, personal attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of **Hānshān Déqīng** [[德清]] from 1587 onward. Lay surname Hú 胡. His father, *zhōngchéng* Húgōng 胡中丞, had retired from office; his *qīn-zi* 親之子 (adopted / nephew) was sent to become Déqīng's attendant and given the dharma-name Fúshàn. He is the original *rìlù* 日錄-keeper — *shìzhě* who recorded Déqīng's daily teaching — whose records were subsequently edited into the *Hānshān lǎorén mèngyóu jí* ([[KR6q0386]]) by **Tōngjiōng** [[通炯]]. Also compiler of the *Hānshān dàshī niánpǔ shūzhù* 憨山大師年譜疏註 (B0085 in the Jiāxīng Canon).

Western-Jìn figure — the slave purchased by the Wú-era foreign monk **Ānhóu dàorén** 安矦道人 in the late-Wú / early-Jìn period (c. 270–280). Mentioned in the *Gāosēng zhuàn* 梁高僧傳 (juan 1, in the life of Ān Shìgāo 安世高 = Ānqīng 安清) as a minor prosopographical detail. Not the Míng-era monastic figure.
