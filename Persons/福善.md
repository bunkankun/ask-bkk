Late-Míng Chán monk, personal attendant (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of **Hān-shān Dé-qīng** [[德清]] from 1587 onward. Lay surname Hú 胡. His father, *zhōng-chéng* Hú-gōng 胡中丞, had retired from office; his *qīn-zi* 親之子 (adopted / nephew) was sent to become Déqīng's attendant and given the dharma-name Fú-shàn. He is the original *rì-lù* 日錄-keeper — *shì-zhě* who recorded Déqīng's daily teaching — whose records were subsequently edited into the *Hānshān lǎorén mèng-yóu jí* ([[KR6q0386]]) by **Tōng-jiōng** [[通炯]]. Also compiler of the *Hān-shān dà-shī nián-pǔ shū-zhù* 憨山大師年譜疏註 (B0085 in the Jiā-xīng Canon).

Western-Jìn figure — the slave purchased by the Wú-era foreign monk **Ān-hóu dào-rén** 安矦道人 in the late-Wú / early-Jìn period (c. 270–280). Mentioned in the *Gāo-sēng zhuàn* 梁高僧傳 (juan 1, in the life of Ān Shì-gāo 安世高 = Ān-qīng 安清) as a minor prosopographical detail. Not the Míng-era monastic figure.
