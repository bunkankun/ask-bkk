# Hǎi-shòu 海壽

Mid-14th-century Japanese Chán / Rinzai-Zen monk, *hào* Chúntíng 椿庭 (Chintei), self-style Mù-bēi dào-rén 木杯道人. Yángqí-branch Línjì disciple of [[梵僊]] Zhú-xiān Fàn-xiān 竺仙梵僊 (Jikusen Bonsen, 1292–1348), the founder of the Chikusen-ha 竺仙派 who had crossed to Japan as a dharma-heir of [[清茂]] Gǔ-lín Qīng-mào. Resident at Nán-chán-sì 南禪寺 (Nanzen-ji) in Kyōto; editor (編次) of the two-juan *Gǔ-lín Qīng-mào chánshī shí-yí jì-sòng* 古林清茂禪師拾遺偈頌 ([[KR6q0346]]), recovering some 338 additional *jì-sòng* of Qīng-mào from manuscript remnants reaching Japan from Jiāng-nán via Koryŏ and Kyūshū. DILA also records his editorial involvement in the *Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù chánshī yǔlù* (X71 n1414). Lifedates unrecorded.
