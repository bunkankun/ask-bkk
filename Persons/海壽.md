# Hǎishòu 海壽

Mid-14th-century Japanese Chán / Rinzai-Zen monk, *hào* Chúntíng 椿庭 (Chintei), self-style Mùbēi dàorén 木杯道人. Yángqí-branch Línjì disciple of [[梵僊]] Zhúxiān Fànxiān 竺仙梵僊 (Jikusen Bonsen, 1292–1348), the founder of the Chikusen-ha 竺仙派 who had crossed to Japan as a dharma-heir of [[清茂]] Gǔlín Qīngmào. Resident at Nánchánsì 南禪寺 (Nanzen-ji) in Kyōto; editor (編次) of the two-juan *Gǔlín Qīngmào chánshī shíyí jìsòng* 古林清茂禪師拾遺偈頌 ([[KR6q0346]]), recovering some 338 additional *jìsòng* of Qīngmào from manuscript remnants reaching Japan from Jiāngnán via Koryŏ and Kyūshū. DILA also records his editorial involvement in the *Liǎoān Qīngyù chánshī yǔlù* (X71 n1414). Lifedates unrecorded.
