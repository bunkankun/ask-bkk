# Shǒuquán 守詮

Southern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí Chán monk; dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) of Yīngān Tánhuá 應菴曇華 (1103–1163). Lead-editor of his master's ten-juan *yǔlù* [[KR6q0293]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
