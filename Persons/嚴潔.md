**Yán Jié** 嚴潔 (b. 1678, fl. 1761), *zì* Xītíng 西亭. Mid-Qīng physician of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). With his fellow Hángzhōu physicians Shī Wén [[施雯]] (*zì* Dàn-níng 澹寧) and Hóng Wěi-quán [[洪煒全]] he co-compiled the *Dé pèi běncǎo* 得配本草 ([[KR3ec058]]), printed in Qiánlóng 26 (乾隆辛巳, 1761). The work's distinctive contribution is the systematic exposition of drug *pèi-wǔ* 配伍 (combinatorial pairing): how each substance behaves when paired with other drugs in compound formulae — a doctrine implicit in Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *jīngfāng* but not previously made explicit in a pharmacopoeia. CBDB 76275 has a birth year of 1678 with no death year; this matches the conventional date for the *Dé pèi* author and the dynasty context. He is otherwise not richly documented.
