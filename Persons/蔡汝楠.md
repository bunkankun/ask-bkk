**Cài Rǔnán** 蔡汝楠 (*zì* Zǐmù 子木, *hào* Báishí 白石; 1516–1565; Míng) was a Jiā-jìng-era scholar-official from Dégé 德格 in Sìchuān (some sources give Déqīng 德清 in Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of 1538 (嘉靖十七年). His career was as a regional official, eventually rising to *Yòu fùdūyùshǐ* 右副都御史 and *Nánjīng gōngbù shàngshū* 南京工部尚書 in Wàngjí 1564. He was a member of the *Yángmíng* school of Wáng Shǒurén's followers (he studied under Zōu Shǒuyì 鄒守益 and Ōuyáng Dé 歐陽德), and contributed to mid-Míng *xīnxué* 心學 philosophy as well as to technical literature. Author of the *Tiānwén lüè* 天文略 ([[KR3fa010]]) and of a substantial *wénjí*, the *Cài Báishí xiānsheng wénjí* 蔡白石先生文集. CBDB id 132583.
