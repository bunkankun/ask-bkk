Ní Fù 倪復, *zì* Rǔxīn 汝新, of Sìmíng 四明 (Níngbō 寧波, modern Zhèjiāng). Mid-Míng music-theorist, *fl.* Jiājìng era. The principal evidence for his life is the preface that his fellow-prefectural Zhāng Bāngqí 張邦奇 wrote in winter Jiājìng 5 (1526) for his *Zhōnglǜ tōngkǎo* 鐘律通考 ([[KR1i0008]]) — completed in autumn of the same year. Zhāng's preface notes that Ní had given his life to a deep study of music classics and of Cài Yuándìng's *Lǜlǚ xīnshū*, but had been long unable to obtain office. Beyond the *Zhōnglǜ tōngkǎo* he is otherwise obscure.

CBDB id 255664 records him under dynasty 明 with index year 1405 — too early to be reconciled with the *Zhōnglǜ tōngkǎo*'s 1526 completion; the index-year is unreliable, likely an inference from an unrelated 倪復 in the *Mǐng* prefectural records. The conventional flourishing-period 1500–1530 should be preferred.
