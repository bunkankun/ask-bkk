Ní Fù 倪復, *zì* Rǔxīn 汝新, of Sì-míng 四明 (Níng-bō 寧波, modern Zhèjiāng). Mid-Míng music-theorist, *fl.* Jiā-jìng era. The principal evidence for his life is the preface that his fellow-prefectural Zhāng Bāngqí 張邦奇 wrote in winter Jiā-jìng 5 (1526) for his *Zhōng-lǜ tōngkǎo* 鐘律通考 ([[KR1i0008]]) — completed in autumn of the same year. Zhāng's preface notes that Ní had given his life to a deep study of music classics and of Cài Yuán-dìng's *Lǜ-lǚ xīn-shū*, but had been long unable to obtain office. Beyond the *Zhōng-lǜ tōngkǎo* he is otherwise obscure.

CBDB id 255664 records him under dynasty 明 with index year 1405 — too early to be reconciled with the *Zhōng-lǜ tōng-kǎo*'s 1526 completion; the index-year is unreliable, likely an inference from an unrelated 倪復 in the *Mǐng* prefectural records. The conventional flourishing-period 1500–1530 should be preferred.
