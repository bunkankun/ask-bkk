Xīzhòng 熙仲 (fl. 1336) was a mid-Yuán 元 Buddhist monk; the preface to his sole surviving major work, the *Lìcháo shìshì zījiàn* 歷朝釋氏資鑑 ([[KR6r0021]]), is dated 1336 (Zhìyuán *Bǐngzǐ* 至元丙子, Zhìyuán 2 of the second Zhìyuán reign-period under Emperor Shùn 順 of the Yuán). The work is a twelve-juan Buddhist universal history, modelled formally on [[司馬光]]'s *Zīzhì tōngjiàn*. No further biographical data is preserved beyond what can be inferred from the preface, which presents him as a monk active in the southern Yuán Buddhist establishment.
