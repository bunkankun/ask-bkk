志福 Zhì-fú ("Will-Blessing") was a Khitan Liáo 遼 dynasty Chinese Buddhist scholastic, fl. late eleventh century, of the Dōng-shān Chóng-xiān-sì 東山崇仙寺. He was active during the reign of Liáo Dào-zōng 道宗 (Tiān-yòu emperor, r. 1055–1101), under whose patronage he composed the *Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn tōng-xuán chāo* 釋摩訶衍論通玄鈔 ([[KR6o0090]]) — a four-*juǎn* sub-commentary on the [[KR6o0084|*Shì Móhē-yǎn lùn*]] commissioned by the emperor and prefaced by an imperial composition. The emperor's preface describes him as "of Vulture-Peak lineage (鷲嶺) and Garuda-voice (鵬聲) virtue", high doctrinal honorifics. He is otherwise poorly attested in standard biographical sources, reflecting the general gap in the Buddhist biographical record for the Khitan-Liáo period; no firm dates are available.
