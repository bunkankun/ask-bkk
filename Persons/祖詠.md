# Zǔyǒng 祖詠

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, disciple of [[宗杲]] Dàhuì Zōnggǎo (1089–1163). Exact lifedates not transmitted. Resided some years at Xīngshàn sì 興善寺 in Yuè (Yuèzhōu 越州, present-day Shàoxīng); subsequently at Línān 臨安, where he assembled the one-juan *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī niánpǔ* 大慧普覺禪師年譜 ([[KR6q0063]]), the standard chronological biography of his teacher. Prefaced by the lay Buddhist poet Zhāng Lún 張掄 in Chúnxī 10 (1183) and afterwarded — with sixty-plus corrections — by Huázàng Zōngyǎn 華藏宗演 in Kāixǐ 1 (1205). Mentioned in *Yúnwò jìtán* 雲臥紀譚 juan 2.
