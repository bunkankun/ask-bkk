# Míng-fán 明凡

Late-Míng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán *mén-rén* 門人 of [[圓澄]] Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng (1561–1627); recorder (*lù* 錄) of his master's eight-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0374]]), with *biān* 編 editing by the lay literati [[丁元公]] and [[祁駿佳]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
