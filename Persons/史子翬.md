# 史子翬

Shǐ Zǐhuī 史子翬, fl. mid-thirteenth-century Sòng. Author of the back-colophon (dated 1248) to [[稅與權]] Shuì Yúquán's *Yì xué qǐméng xiǎo zhuàn* ([[KR1a0057]]); developed the *QiánKūn nàjiǎ* 乾坤納甲 numerology argument that "the number 9 is concealed within 6" by way of 1+3+5 = 9.
