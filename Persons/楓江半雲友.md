Pseudonym of the author of [[KR4k0273]] *Yǐn Fēng Xiāo* 引風簫. The pen name means "A friend who is half-hidden in the clouds above the Maple River" — *fēngjiāng* 楓江 (maple-tree river) evokes Jiangnan, possibly Suzhou or the Hangzhou area; *bànyún* 半雲 (half-cloud) implies a hermit or reclusive literatus. No further biographical information is recorded. The name does not appear in CBDB or standard biographical dictionaries of Qing authors.
