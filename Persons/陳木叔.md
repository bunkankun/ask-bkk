# Chén Mùshū 陳木叔

Late-Míng literatus who used "Hánshān" 寒山 as his *hào* (in homage to the Táng eccentric-poet). Mentioned in the preface to [[KR6q0216]] *Tiāntái sān shèng shī jí hé yùn* as a literati figure associated with the Hánshān poetic-reception tradition. Lifedates and further biographical details unrecorded.
