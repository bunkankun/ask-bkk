Major eighteenth-century Qīng court painter and theorist of flower-painting; *zì* Xiǎo-shān 小山, *hào* Ràng-xiāng 讓鄉, of Wúxí 無錫. Lifedates 1686–1772 (CBDB matches catalog meta). *Yōngzhèng dīng-wèi* 5 (1727) *jìnshì*; rose through the Hanlin and the Southern Studio to *Nèi-gé xué-shì* 內閣學士 with the rank of Right Vice-Minister of Rites (*Lǐ-bù shì-láng* 禮部侍郎). A favoured court flower painter under the Qiánlóng emperor, he was commanded in *Qiánlóng* 21 (1756) to paint 36 species of *yáng-jú* 洋菊 (western chrysanthemum) — a key episode in the history of botanical illustration at the eighteenth-century court. Married into the Yùn 惲 family of Chángzhōu — son-in-law of Yùn Shòu-píng 惲壽平 (1633–1690), the great Cháng-zhōu-school *mò-jīng* 沒骨 flower-painting master, from whom he received the family painting transmission. Author of [[KR3h0071]] *Xiǎo-shān huà-pǔ* 小山畫譜, the principal Qīng theoretical handbook on flower-painting, distilling the *Bā-fǎ Sì-zhī* (Eight Methods and Four Knowings) framework.
