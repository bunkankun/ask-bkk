Gù Bóhuá 顧伯華 (1916–1993) was a senior 20th-century Shànghǎi-school *wàikē* 外科 surgeon and Chinese-medicine philologist; he edited the standard punctuated critical edition of [[汪綺石]] Wāng Qǐshí's *Lǐxū yuánjiàn* ([[KR3eh042]]) (Beijing: Rénmín wèishēng, 1959).
