# Fàn Níng 范寧

Variant graphic form of the personal name 范甯 / 范寧. Both forms refer to the same Eastern-Jìn classicist (339–401), author of the *Chūnqiū Gǔliáng zhuàn jí jiě* 春秋穀梁傳集解.

For full biography see [[范甯]].

The Kanripo catalog meta uses 范寧 in the WYG/Sìkù frontmatter for [[KR1e0010]] and 范甯 in the SBCK frontmatter for [[KR1e0009]]. The two pages here cross-reference one another; future cataloguers should add new biographical material to [[范甯]] only.
