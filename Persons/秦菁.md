Qín Jīng 秦菁 (*fl.* Jìn) is a Jìn-period figure recorded in the early bibliographic tradition only as the author of the *Qínzǐ* 秦子, a *zǐbù* treatise listed in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 in 3 *juàn*. No biography survives in the standard histories. His exact birth and death years are not preserved; he is conventionally placed in the Jìn period. CBDB has no entry.

In the Kanripo corpus he is the attributed author of [[KR3a0150]] *Qínzǐ* 秦子.
