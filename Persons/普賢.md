# 普賢
Pǔxián (Sanskrit: Samantabhadra)

Mahāyāna bodhisattva of "Universal Worthiness", patron of meditative practice and the ten great vows. A principal interlocutor in the Mahāyāna sūtra corpus, paired in iconography with [[文殊]] Mañjuśrī.
