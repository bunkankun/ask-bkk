**Xī-fāng-zǐ** 西方子 ("Master from the West"), pseudonymous compiler of the ***Míng-táng jiǔ jīng*** 明堂灸經 ([[KR3e0018]]) — the eight-juan Sòng-or-Yuán moxibustion treatise printed at Píng-yáng-fǔ 平陽府 in Shǎnxī. The pen-name suggests either a Western Regions origin or a Daoist self-styling; the SKQS editors note that they cannot identify Xī-fāng-zǐ historically. The book is dated by its Píng-yáng-fǔ printing context to the late Northern Sòng or Jīn-Yuán period (the prefectural printing industry in Píng-yáng was active from the late 11th into the 14th c.). The work is exclusively on moxibustion — no acupuncture content — and includes side-and-supine human-figure illustrations that are more detailed than those of the *Tóng-rén* tradition. Xī-fāng-zǐ has no transmitted biographical record beyond this work.
