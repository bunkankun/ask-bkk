**Qīngpǔ zhū jūnzǐ** 青浦諸君子 ("the various worthy gentlemen of Qīng-pǔ") — collective authorial designation under which the late-Qīng *Shòu-shì biān* 壽世編 ([[KR3eo017]]) was compiled. **Qīng-pǔ** 青浦 was an administrative county of Sōng-jiāng prefecture 松江府 in southeastern Jiāng-sū (modern Shàng-hǎi western suburb). The designation refers to a group of literati gentlemen associated with the Qīng-pǔ community-charitable / *shàn-shū* publication economy of the second half of the 19th century, in which family prescription collections and household medical recipes were pooled and printed for free distribution, often under the editorial coordination of local "*Lè-shàn-táng*" 樂善堂 establishments. Individual identities are not preserved in the transmitted text.
