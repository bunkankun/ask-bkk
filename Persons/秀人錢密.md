# Xiù-rén Qián Mì 秀人錢密

Northern-Sòng lay Buddhist; preface-writer (*xù* 序) for the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* 黃龍四家錄 [[KR6q0276]]. Lay surname Qián 錢, given name Mì 密; *Xiù-rén* 秀人 ("Refined Person") is a sobriquet. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.
