Republican-era Mèng-hé 孟河-tradition physician of Wúxī 無錫; disciple of Shěn Zǔfù 沈奉江. A member of the Wúxī Zhōu medical family ("秉承小農先生家學" — i.e., heir to Zhōu Xiǎonóng 周小農's family medicine). Signed as 周源 (本名) and 周逢儒 (字). Compiled and edited [[KR3eb009]] *Yī yàn suí bǐ* (1924) from his teacher's clinical practice; the postface to that work is also his. No CBDB record.
