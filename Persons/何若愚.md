# 何若愚 Hé Ruòyú

**Hé Ruòyú** 何若愚, Five-Dynasties Nán-Táng physician; author of the (lost) *Zhǐwēi lùn* 指微論 (3 juan) and *Zhǐwēi zhēn fù* 指微針賦 — the immediate doctrinal source for [[閻明廣]] Yán Míngguǎng's *Zǐwǔ liúzhù zhēn jīng* 子午流注針經 ([[KR3ee045]], 1153) and thus the deeper origin of the *zǐwǔ liúzhù* chronoacupuncture system.
