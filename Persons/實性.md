Shíxìng 實性 (variant **Bǎoxìng** 寶性). Female disciple-attendant (*nǐ* 尼) of **Lǎngkōng Shùn** [[順]] at **Yǒngshèngān** 永盛菴. Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*jìlù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0585]] 《盤山朗空順禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B473. Co-engraver with co-disciple **Shíxiáng** 實祥 ("弟子實性尼實祥等同刊").
