Shí-xìng 實性 (variant **Bǎo-xìng** 寶性). Female disciple-attendant (*nǐ* 尼) of **Lǎng-kōng Shùn** [[順]] at **Yǒng-shèng-ān** 永盛菴. Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*jì-lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0585]] 《盤山朗空順禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B473. Co-engraver with co-disciple **Shí-xiáng** 實祥 ("弟子實性尼實祥等同刊").
