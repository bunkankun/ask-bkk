Wáng Shēnzǐ 王申子 (lifedates not securely recorded; fl. late-13th / early-14th century), *zì* Xùnqīng 巽卿, of Qióngzhōu 邛州 in Sìchuān (modern Qiónglái 邛崍, Chéngdū metropolitan region). CBDB id 101134.

A Sòng-loyalist recluse-scholar who, after the Mongol conquest, withdrew to Cílìzhōu Tiānménshān 慈利州天門山 (modern Cílì county, Húnán) — one of the more remote Yuán-period scholarly retreats. Composed his *Yì*-commentary over nearly thirty years at this mountain retreat.

Within the Kanripo corpus he is the author of [[KR1a0077]] *Dà Yì jí shuō* 大易緝說 (10 juan, plus a 1-juan appendix *Xù kān Dà Yì jí shuō shǐmò* 續刊大易緝說始末; composed c. 1275–1303). Contemporary praise from [[吳澄]] Wú Chéng and Lǐ Lín 李琳; the 1301 Wáng Lǚ 王履 preface and 1303 Chéng Wénhǎi 程文海 preface are preserved at the head of the Sìkù base.

Methodologically a *Cài-Yuán-dìng-orthodox xiàng-shù-cosmologist*: the 10-*Hé tú* / 9-*Luò shū* assignment, *fēn wěi* (warp-division) charting of the *Xiāntiān*, and *cuòzōng* (zigzag-arrangement) charting of the *Hòutiān*. The 30-year mountain-retreat composition discipline places him in the lineage of Yuán-period scholarly recluses parallel to Yú Yǎn ([[俞琰]]) and Léi Sīqí ([[雷思齊]]).
