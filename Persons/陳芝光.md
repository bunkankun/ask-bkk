Chén Zhīguāng 陳芝光 (fl. early 18th c., *zì* Wèijiǔ 蔚九), of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng Hángzhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È; contributed 100 poems to the collaborative *NánSòng záshì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]]. Remained at *zhūshēng* (school-student) level; no major independent works survive. No CBDB record. Information drawn from the *Sìkù tíyào* of [[KR4h0166]].
