Chén Zhī-guāng 陳芝光 (fl. early 18th c., *zì* Wèi-jiǔ 蔚九), of Rén-hé 仁和 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōng-zhèng Háng-zhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È; contributed 100 poems to the collaborative *Nán-Sòng zá-shì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]]. Remained at *zhū-shēng* (school-student) level; no major independent works survive. No CBDB record. Information drawn from the *Sìkù tíyào* of [[KR4h0166]].
