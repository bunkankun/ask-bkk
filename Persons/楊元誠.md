**Yáng Yuánchéng** 楊元誠 (*fl.* mid-Yuán), Yuán civil official, former Director of the Astronomical Service (太史令 Tài-shǐ-lìng). At an earlier point in his career he served as prefect (*jùn-mù* 郡牧) of the home prefecture of Wāng Rǔmào 汪汝懋 in Tóng-jiāng 桐江, where he composed an earlier (now-lost) *Shān-jū* yǎng-shēng compendium which Wāng Rǔmào subsequently expanded into the surviving four-juan *Shān-jū sì-yào* 山居四要 ([[KR3eo013]]). No CBDB record; biographical details derive solely from Liú Rén-běn's 1360 preface to Wāng's expansion.
