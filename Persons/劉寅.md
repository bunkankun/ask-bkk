Liú Yín 劉寅 (Míng), early-Míng Hóng-wǔ-period scholar. Author of the *Sān lüè zhí jiě* 三略直解 (KR3b0008, dated *Hóng-wǔ* 4 / 1371) — the standard Míng-period commentary on the *Huáng-shí-gōng sān lüè*. Provenance and lifedates unrecorded. CBDB has multiple homonyms 劉寅 with no confirmed match.
