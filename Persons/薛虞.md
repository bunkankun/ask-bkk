# Xuē Yú 薛虞

Han-period *Yì* scholar of obscure dates. According to [[司馬貞]] Sīmǎ Zhēn's account (cited in the *Sìkù* *tiyao* of the *Zǐxià Yìzhuàn* [[KR1a0002]]), in the form of the *Zǐxià Zhuàn* that circulated in [[王儉]] Wáng Jiǎn's day the *Qīzhì* 七志 attribution of the text to [[韓嬰]] Hán Yīng had been dropped and notes by Xuē Yú were attached instead. No independent work survives.
