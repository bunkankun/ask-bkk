**Huáng Jì-zhī** 黃濟之 (late-Míng official, fl. *Wàn-lì* era), magistrate of Yì-wū 義烏 (Wù-zhōu 婺州, Zhèjiāng) from *bǐng-chén* (1616) onward, with concurrent service as magistrate of Wú-níng 吳寧 in *jǐ-wèi* (1619). The 1621 editor of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dān-xī's *Dān-xī shǒu-jìng* 丹溪手鏡 ([[KR3er042]]) and the companion *Qiáo-yǐn* 樵隱, both recovered from the Dān-xī family in autumn 1621 (*xīn-yǒu zhī qiū mèng*) — a remarkable transmission story narrated in his preface. Huáng's preface to the *Shǒu-jìng* is one of the principal early-Míng historiographical sources for the framing of Dān-xī as a Confucian *yǐn* 隱 (hidden one) who applied himself to medicine in a politically untenable situation at the end of the Yuán. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
