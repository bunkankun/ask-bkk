**Huáng Jìzhī** 黃濟之 (late-Míng official, fl. *Wànlì* era), magistrate of Yìwū 義烏 (Wùzhōu 婺州, Zhèjiāng) from *bǐngchén* (1616) onward, with concurrent service as magistrate of Wúníng 吳寧 in *jǐwèi* (1619). The 1621 editor of [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī's *Dānxī shǒujìng* 丹溪手鏡 ([[KR3er042]]) and the companion *Qiáoyǐn* 樵隱, both recovered from the Dānxī family in autumn 1621 (*xīnyǒu zhī qiū mèng*) — a remarkable transmission story narrated in his preface. Huáng's preface to the *Shǒujìng* is one of the principal early-Míng historiographical sources for the framing of Dānxī as a Confucian *yǐn* 隱 (hidden one) who applied himself to medicine in a politically untenable situation at the end of the Yuán. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
