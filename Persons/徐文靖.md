Mid-Qīng 清 historical-geographical scholar, native of Dāngtú 當塗 in Tàipíng 太平 prefecture (modern Ānhuī, on the Yangtze just north of Nanjing). Zì Wèishān 位山; hào Tuìwēng 蛻翁. Lifedates 1668–1756 (CBDB id 62026 gives 1668; the catalog meta and standard sources give 1667 — both acceptable; the catalog's 1667 is followed in the work-note frontmatter).

*Jǔrén* of Yōngzhèng 1 / 1723 (癸卯). In Qiánlóng 1 / 1736 he was nominated for the *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 special examination but did not pass; in Qiánlóng 17 / 1752, aged 85 *suì*, he was nominated for the special *Jīng xué* 經學 ("Canonical Learning") imperial examination and was specially appointed *Hànlín-yuàn jiǎntǎo* 翰林院檢討 — an unusual late-life imperial recognition for the *Yǔ gòng* commentary that had been the labor of his lifetime.

Major works: (1) *Yǔ gòng huì jiān* 禹貢會箋 ([[KR1b0058]]) in 12 *juǎn*, the standard Qing-period continuation of [[胡渭]]'s *Yǔ gòng zhuīzhǐ* (KR1b0053); (2) *Zhúshū jìnián tǒng jiān* 竹書紀年統箋 in 12 *juǎn* (separately in the *Sìkù*), the standard Qing-period investigation of the *Bamboo Annals* and one of the foundational Qing *kǎojù* contributions to early-Chinese chronological history; (3) *Mǎn-tíng zǎ lǎn* 滿庭雜覽 (a miscellany, also in the *Sìkù*); (4) extensive poetry and prose. He is one of the few Qing classical scholars whose specialty in early-Chinese geographical-chronological scholarship was directly recognized by imperial appointment to the Hànlín-yuàn.
