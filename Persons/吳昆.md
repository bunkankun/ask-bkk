Late-Wàn-lì master physician of Xīn'ān 新安 (Hùizhōu, modern Anhui Shèxiàn 歙縣); *zì* Shānfǔ 山甫, *hào* Hègāo 鶴皋, also Cānhuángzǐ 參黃子. Conventional lifedates 1551–1620+. After ten years of unsuccessful civil examinations, turned to medicine on the *liáng xiàng / liáng yī* model; trained itinerantly through Suzhou, Zhejiang, Hubei-Hunan, and Hebei over thirty years before returning home to write. Author of the late-Ming Xīn'ān-school synthesis:

- *Yī fāng kǎo* 醫方考 (1584) — a critical formulary, widely reprinted.
- *Mài yǔ* 脈語 ([[KR3eb040]], 1584) — pulse manual in dialogue form.
- *Huáng dì nèi jīng Sùwèn Wú zhù* 黃帝內經素問吳注 (1594) — *Sùwèn* commentary.
- *Zhēn fāng liù jí* 針方六集 (1618) — acupuncture compendium.

Doctrinally aligned with the Lǐ Shízhēn / Dài Qǐzōng critique of the pseudo-Wáng-Shūhé *Mài jué*. No CBDB record.
