**Lǚ Zhènmíng** 呂震名 (*zì* Yǒngyuán 永元, *hào* Wàngzhāi 望齋, 1799–1858, 清), Hángzhōu-region Dàoguāng-Xiánfēng period physician. *Jǔrén* of 1822; held minor official posts in Zhèjiāng before turning to medicine in mid-career. Author of the *Shānghán xún yuán* 傷寒尋源 ([[KR3ef039]], 3 juan, 1856) and a *Wàngzhāi yī àn* 望齋醫案 (case records). His clinical lineage was in the late-Qīng Zhèjiāng *Shānghán* synthesis tradition. Lifedates 1799–1858 from conventional reference works. No CBDB record.
