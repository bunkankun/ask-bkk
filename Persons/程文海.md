Chéng Wénhǎi 程文海 (1249–1318), *zì* Jùfū 鉅夫, *hào* Xuělóu 雪樓; sometimes also known as Chéng Jùfū 程鉅夫 (avoiding the taboo on Yuán *Wǔzōng*'s name). An early-Yuán statesman and *Hànlín* scholar from Jiànchāng 建昌 (modern Jiāngxī). Sent by Khubilai to the south to recruit Sòng-loyalist talent, he was instrumental in bringing [[趙孟頫]] Zhào Mèngfǔ, [[吳澄]] Wú Chéng, and other notable southern scholars into Yuán court service. Author of the *Xuělóu jí* 雪樓集. Wrote prefaces for several Yuán *Yì*-works including Wáng Shēnzǐ's *Dà Yì jí shuō* [[KR1a0077]] (1303).
