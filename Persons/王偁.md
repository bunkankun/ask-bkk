Early Míng official and editor, son of the late-Yuán loyalist **Wáng Hàn** 王翰 (1333–1378). Style-name **Mèngyáng** 孟敭; sobriquet **Xūzhōu** 虛舟. He was entrusted by his father to **Wú Hǎi** 吳海 just before Wáng Hàn's suicide in 1378, and grew up in Wú's household. He passed the Yǒnglè *zhì-shì* metropolitan examination as a *gòng-shēng*, served as a *jiànshàn* 檢討 in the Hànlín Yuàn and later as compiler. He fell foul of the Yǒnglè-era *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* compilation politics and died in prison c. 1415. He compiled his father's surviving poems as *Yǒushí shānrén yígǎo* 友石山人遺稿 ([[KR4d0551]]) and edited Wú Hǎi's prose-and-verse collection *Wén-guò-zhāi jí* 聞過齋集 ([[KR4d0552]]). He has his own *bié-jí*, the *Xū-zhōu jí* 虛舟集.
