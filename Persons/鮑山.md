Bào Shān 鮑山 (Late Míng), botanical-edibility writer. Provenance and lifedates unrecorded. Author of the *Yě cài bó lù* 野菜博錄 (KR3d0009, 4-juan wild-vegetable identification manual extending the *Jiù huāng běn cǎo* tradition).
