Qián Yuánchāng 錢元昌 (b. 1676), a Chángshú 常熟 (Sūzhōu) native, was a Kāngxī 60 (1721) *jìnshì*. He served as the principal *biānzuǎn* 編纂 of the Yōngzhèng-era *Guǎngxī tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0054]]) of 1733 under the supervision of Governor [[金鉷]]. His official career included service as a county magistrate in Guǎngxī, where he gathered direct field knowledge of the *gǎitǔ guīliú* reforms whose documentation is the principal scholarly contribution of the gazetteer.
