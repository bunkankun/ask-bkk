# Rénān 仁安

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě* 侍者) of [[心月]] Shíxī Xīnyuè (d. 1254); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
