# Zé 澤 (Rùn-guāng Zé 潤光澤)

Qīng Chán master, abbot of Zhèng-jué-sì 正覺寺. Catalog-name 澤 (single-character dharma-name per Kanripo convention for some masters); *hào* Rùn-guāng 潤光 ("Moist-Light"), Rùn-wēng 潤翁 ("Moist Old-Man"). Lifedates unrecorded; died by approximately 1684 based on the internal dating of his disciple's compilation-preface.

Surviving work: the *Zhèng-jué Rùn-guāng Zé chánshī zǎo xuě jí* 正覺潤光澤禪師澡雪集 [[KR6q0234]] — his posthumous one-juan poetic-miscellany, re-edited by his dharma-heir [[照水|Zhào-shuǐ]] 照水 and prefaced by Chén Wén-dào 陳聞道.
