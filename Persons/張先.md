Northern-Sòng *cí*-poet, *zì* Zǐyě 子野, of Wūchéng 烏程 (Húzhōu 湖州, modern Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Tiānshèng* 8 / 1030; held a long series of provincial appointments (Sùzhōu 宿州, Wújiāng 吳江, Jiāhé 嘉禾, Yǒngxīng 永興, etc.) and reached *Tàicháng bóshì* and *Dūguān lángzhōng* before retirement to Hángzhōu. Famous for his longevity (lived to the age of 88 *suì*, dying in 1078 by Chinese count) and his late-life associations — notably with Sū Shì 蘇軾, who composed several poems for him in old age — and for the literary nickname *Zhāng sān yǐng* 張三影 ("Zhāng Three-Shadows") earned from three favourite *cí* lines containing the word *yǐng* 影 (shadow). With Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] he is one of the principal early-Sòng pioneers of the *màncí* 慢詞 (long-tune *cí*), expanding the form beyond the *xiǎolìng* of Yàn Shū [[晏殊]]. His own *cí*-collection survives as the *Ānlù jí* [[KR4j0004]] (1 *juǎn*, WYG). Wrote the preface (now lost) to Yàn Shū's *Zhūyù cí* [[KR4j0001]]. Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 444 (under "Wényì" 文藝); CBDB.
