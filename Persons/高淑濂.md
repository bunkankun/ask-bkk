**Gāo Shūlián** 高淑濂 (*fl.* late 19th – early 20th c.), Tàiān 泰安 (Shāndōng) physician. Father of **Gāo Zōngyuè** 高宗岳 (the work's editor and printer), and trained him in medicine in 1908 (*Guāngxù wùshēn*) after the son's labour-pains caused him to abandon physical labour for medical study. Gāo Shūlián's *Tāichǎn fāngàn* 胎產方案 ([[KR3ei067]]) was the family's principal obstetrical manuscript. Originally titled *Tāichǎn mìshū* 胎產秘書, the work was renamed *Fāngàn* (Prescriptional Cases) in 1934 and printed by his son's *Yī Dài shānfáng* 依岱山房 in Tàiān. The author specialised in the obstetrics-and-gynaecology branch of clinical practice for over thirty years; the work distils his lifelong clinical experience. Not in CBDB.
