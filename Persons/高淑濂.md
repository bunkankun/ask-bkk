**Gāo Shū-lián** 高淑濂 (*fl.* late 19th – early 20th c.), Tài-ān 泰安 (Shān-dōng) physician. Father of **Gāo Zōng-yuè** 高宗岳 (the work's editor and printer), and trained him in medicine in 1908 (*Guāng-xù wù-shēn*) after the son's labour-pains caused him to abandon physical labour for medical study. Gāo Shū-lián's *Tāi-chǎn fāng-àn* 胎產方案 ([[KR3ei067]]) was the family's principal obstetrical manuscript. Originally titled *Tāi-chǎn mì-shū* 胎產秘書, the work was renamed *Fāng-àn* (Prescriptional Cases) in 1934 and printed by his son's *Yī Dài shān-fáng* 依岱山房 in Tài-ān. The author specialised in the obstetrics-and-gynaecology branch of clinical practice for over thirty years; the work distils his lifelong clinical experience. Not in CBDB.
