# Zhù-xiǎn 住顯

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of [[心月]] Shí-xī Xīn-yuè (d. 1254); principal named editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
