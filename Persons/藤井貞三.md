# 藤井貞三 Téngjǐng Zhēnsān / Fujii Teizō

**Fujii Teizō** 藤井貞三, mid-Edo Japanese kanpō physician; member of the acupoint-transmission lineage from [[宮本春仙]] Miyamoto Shunsen through [[中島元春]] Nakajima Genshun and [[良益]] Ryōeki to [[小阪營升]] Kosaka Genyū's *Jīngxué zuǎnyào* 經穴纂要 ([[KR3ee043]]).
