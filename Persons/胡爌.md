Hú Kuàng 胡爌 (late Míng, lifedates and birthplace not securely known). A private *kǎo-zhèng* scholar of the late-Wàn-lì / Tiān-qǐ / Chóng-zhēn period. Two works survive: the *Jiā guī jí yào* 家規輯要 (ancestral-rules compilation, separately catalogued in the SKQS) and the *Shí yí lù* 拾遺錄 ([[KR3j0068]]), whose 10-juan original was lost when the blocks burned during the dynastic collapse and which survives only as a one-juan partial recovery from a damaged exemplar gathered by a descendant. Not in CBDB.
