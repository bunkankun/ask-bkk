Lǐ Tíngzhōng 李廷忠 (*zì* Jūhòu 居厚, *hào* Júshān 橘山), of *Yúqián* 於潛 (modern Hángzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī* 8 (1181). Held office as *Wúwéi jiàoguān*, *Zhī Jīngdé-xiàn*; ended at *Kuízhōu tōngpàn*. *Sòngshǐ* has no biography. Lifedates unknown. Famous in Sòng times for *sìliù* parallel-prose; also author of a now-lost poetry collection *Dòngxiāo shījí* 洞霄詩集. CBDB id 33317. Author of [[KR4d0288]] *Júshān sìliù*.
