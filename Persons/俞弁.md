**Yú Biàn** 俞弁 (*zì* **Zǐróng** 子容), mid-Míng Sūzhōu 蘇州 physician-scholar, active in the Jiājìng era (*fl.* 1520s–1540s by internal evidence). Author of **[[KR3eq025]]** *Xù yī shuō* 續醫說 (10 *juǎn*), a self-conscious continuation of the late-Sòng [[張杲]] *Yī shuō*. Documented as a contemporary of the Sūzhōu literary-medical circle including **Huáng Shěngzēng** 黃省曾 (1490–1540) and **Lù Càn** 陸粲 (1494–1551), both of whom contributed prefaces to the *Xù yī shuō*. A bibliophile and *bǐjì* compiler in addition to his clinical practice; the Lù Càn preface records his manuscript-copying of *bàiguān xiǎoshuō* 稗官小說 reaching "several hundreds and thousands of *juǎn*" — a mark of the mid-Míng literary-medical *wénrén* style. Not in CBDB.
