# Dào-jì 道濟 (Fǎ-zhōu Dào-jì 法舟道濟)

Mid-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Fǎ-zhōu 法舟 ("Dharma-Boat"). Native of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhè-jiāng). Lifedates 1487–1560 (died Jiājìng 39 / autumn, age 74, per *Shì jiàn jī gǔ lüè xù jí* 釋鑑稽古略續集 juan 3).

Abbot of **Tiān-níng-sì** 天寧寺 in Jiā-xīng. Principal dharma-heir: Dōng-xī Fāng-zé 東谿方澤 of Jīngyán-sì 精嚴寺, who authored a *xíng zhuàng* biographical record of Dào-jì in 1563.

Surviving Kanripo-canonical work: *Tiān-níng Fǎ-zhōu Jì chánshī shèng yǔ* 天寧法舟濟禪師剩語 [[KR6q0238]], compiled by his dharma-heirs led by [[如淵|Rú-yuān]] 如淵.

Sources: *Shì jiàn jī gǔ lüè xù jí* 釋鑑稽古略續集 juan 3; *Wǔ dēng huì yuán xù lüè* 五燈會元續略; various lamp-records.
