# Dàojì 道濟 (Fǎzhōu Dàojì 法舟道濟)

Mid-Míng LínjìYángqí Chán master. *Hào* Fǎzhōu 法舟 ("Dharma-Boat"). Native of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). Lifedates 1487–1560 (died Jiājìng 39 / autumn, age 74, per *Shì jiàn jī gǔ lüè xù jí* 釋鑑稽古略續集 juan 3).

Abbot of **Tiānníngsì** 天寧寺 in Jiāxīng. Principal dharma-heir: Dōngxī Fāngzé 東谿方澤 of Jīngyánsì 精嚴寺, who authored a *xíng zhuàng* biographical record of Dàojì in 1563.

Surviving Kanripo-canonical work: *Tiānníng Fǎzhōu Jì chánshī shèng yǔ* 天寧法舟濟禪師剩語 [[KR6q0238]], compiled by his dharma-heirs led by [[如淵|Rúyuān]] 如淵.

Sources: *Shì jiàn jī gǔ lüè xù jí* 釋鑑稽古略續集 juan 3; *Wǔ dēng huì yuán xù lüè* 五燈會元續略; various lamp-records.
