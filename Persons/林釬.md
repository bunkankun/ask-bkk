Lín Hán 林釬 (1578–1636), *zì* **Shífǔ** 實甫, *hào* **Hètái** 鶴臺 and **Jiǔgāo jūshì** 九皋居士, posthumous title **Wénmù** 文穆. Born on Jīnmén 金門 island, registered as a native of **Lóngxī** 龍溪 (Zhāngzhōu prefecture, Fújiàn) where he was raised after his mother's remarriage. *Tànhuā* (third-place *jìnshì*) of Wànlì 44 / *bǐngchén* (1616); served as *Hànlín biānxiū*, *Guózǐjiān sīyè* and *Guózǐjiān jìjiǔ*. He confronted Wèi Zhōngxián directly when the latter sought to commandeer the *Guózǐjiān* bronze tripods for re-coinage and likewise objected to Wèi's effort to be ceremonially paired with Confucius; he retired in protest. Under *Chóngzhēn* he was recalled and rose to **Dōnggé dàxuéshì** (Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion) before his death in 1636. His preface to **[[施邦曜]]**'s edition of the *Yángmíng xiānshēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) is one of the four prefatory pieces preserved in the SBCK redaction (signing himself *Mǐn Jiǔgāo jūshì hòuxué Lín Hán*, the *Mǐn-* place-element marking his Fújiàn registration).
