Lín Hán 林釬 (1578–1636), *zì* **Shí-fǔ** 實甫, *hào* **Hè-tái** 鶴臺 and **Jiǔ-gāo jū-shì** 九皋居士, posthumous title **Wén-mù** 文穆. Born on Jīn-mén 金門 island, registered as a native of **Lóng-xī** 龍溪 (Zhāng-zhōu prefecture, Fú-jiàn) where he was raised after his mother's remarriage. *Tàn-huā* (third-place *jìnshì*) of Wàn-lì 44 / *bǐng-chén* (1616); served as *Hàn-lín biān-xiū*, *Guó-zǐ-jiān sī-yè* and *Guó-zǐ-jiān jì-jiǔ*. He confronted Wèi Zhōng-xián directly when the latter sought to commandeer the *Guó-zǐ-jiān* bronze tripods for re-coinage and likewise objected to Wèi's effort to be ceremonially paired with Confucius; he retired in protest. Under *Chóng-zhēn* he was recalled and rose to **Dōng-gé dà-xué-shì** (Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion) before his death in 1636. His preface to **[[施邦曜]]**'s edition of the *Yáng-míng xiān-shēng jí yào* ([[KR4e0245]]) is one of the four prefatory pieces preserved in the SBCK redaction (signing himself *Mǐn Jiǔ-gāo jū-shì hòu-xué Lín Hán*, the *Mǐn-* place-element marking his Fú-jiàn registration).
