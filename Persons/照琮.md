Zhào-cōng 照琮, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-disciple of **Chǔ-lín Shàng-ruì** [[上睿]] 楚林上睿 at **Jīng-dū Bào-ēn-sì** 京都報恩寺. Lead recorder of the 6-juan 《北京楚林禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0515]]). No independent *yǔ-lù* or biography located.
