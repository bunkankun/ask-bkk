Northern-Sòng Tiāntái 天台 master; sobriquets **Shén-wù** 神悟 (with full name **Shén-wù Chǔ-qiān** 神悟處謙) and **Chōng-wù dà-shī** 沖悟大師. DILA Authority A001141. Born 1011; died 28 April 1075, aged 65.

Major work: the **Fǎhuá xuán-jì shí bù-èr mén xiǎn-miào** 法華玄記十不二門顯妙 ([[KR6d0163]], X56n0929, 1 *juan*) — Northern-Sòng Tiāntái commentary on the *Shí bù-èr mén* tradition.

Source: DILA A001141.
