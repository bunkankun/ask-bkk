**Hé Jìgāo** 何繼高 (fl. late Wànlì), of **Huìjī** 會稽 (Shàoxīng) — late-Míng Daoist sympathiser-and-editor, presider (*shǒu zuò* 首座) of the *Lóngshā* assembly and *jiàozōng* (cult-master) of the *Yuè-de* assembly (presumably the *Lóngshā / Yuè-de* lay-Daoist *huì* of late-Wàn-lì Jiāngzhè — a Wú-dāng-style devotional fellowship). Compiler and prefacer of [[KR5i0060]] *Qióng guǎn Bái zhēn rén jí*, **dated 1594** (萬曆甲午 端陽 = end of *duānyáng* day, 1594), the principal late-Wàn-lì anthology of the southern Sòng *Nánzōng* fifth-patriarch Bái Yùchán. Hé's preface narrates his own travels through Bái Yùchán's two principal residences (*Linzhi-ge* / Línzhī gé and *Yùsì*) and his consultation with named *yìrén* (extraordinary masters) at the imperial residence and Línjiāng.
