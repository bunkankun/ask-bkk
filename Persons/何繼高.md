**Hé Jì-gāo** 何繼高 (fl. late Wàn-lì), of **Huì-jī** 會稽 (Shào-xīng) — late-Míng Daoist sympathiser-and-editor, presider (*shǒu zuò* 首座) of the *Lóng-shā* assembly and *jiào-zōng* (cult-master) of the *Yuè-de* assembly (presumably the *Lóng-shā / Yuè-de* lay-Daoist *huì* of late-Wàn-lì Jiāng-zhè — a Wú-dāng-style devotional fellowship). Compiler and prefacer of [[KR5i0060]] *Qióng guǎn Bái zhēn rén jí*, **dated 1594** (萬曆甲午 端陽 = end of *duān-yáng* day, 1594), the principal late-Wàn-lì anthology of the southern Sòng *Nán-zōng* fifth-patriarch Bái Yù-chán. Hé's preface narrates his own travels through Bái Yù-chán's two principal residences (*Linzhi-ge* / Lín-zhī gé and *Yù-sì*) and his consultation with named *yì-rén* (extraordinary masters) at the imperial residence and Lín-jiāng.
