Fú Zēng 符曾 (1688–1764, *zì* Yòulǔ 幼魯, *hào* Yàolín 藥林), of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Of the seven collaborators on the *Nán Sòng záshì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]] (c. 1729), Fú is the only one to have held substantial office: he entered service through **recommendation** (rather than examination), eventually rising to *Hùbù lángzhōng* 戶部郎中 (Vice-Director of a Bureau in the Ministry of Revenue). Otherwise a member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng Hángzhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È. Produced the *Chūnfēngtíng cí* 春鳳亭詞 (his collected *cí*). CBDB id 88866, lifedates 1688–1764.
