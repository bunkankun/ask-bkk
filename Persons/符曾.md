Fú Zēng 符曾 (1688–1764, *zì* Yòu-lǔ 幼魯, *hào* Yào-lín 藥林), of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu prefecture, Zhèjiāng). Of the seven collaborators on the *Nán-Sòng zá-shì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]] (c. 1729), Fú is the only one to have held substantial office: he entered service through **recommendation** (rather than examination), eventually rising to *Hù-bù láng-zhōng* 戶部郎中 (Vice-Director of a Bureau in the Ministry of Revenue). Otherwise a member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōng-zhèng Háng-zhōu literary circle around [[厲鶚]] Lì È. Produced the *Chūn-fēng-tíng cí* 春鳳亭詞 (his collected *cí*). CBDB id 88866, lifedates 1688–1764.
