**Reiha** 靈波 — Medieval Japanese **Kegon** 華嚴 (Avataṃsaka) monk, identifiable only as the author of *Huā-yán wǔ-jiào zhāng jiàn-wén chāo* 華嚴五教章見聞鈔 ([[KR6t0038]], T73n2342), an eight-fascicle *jiàn-wén* (lecture-record) sub-commentary on Fa-zang's *Wǔ jiào zhāng*. The work's format and citational patterns place him in the post-Gyōnen Tōdaiji-Kegon tradition of the 14th–15th centuries.

DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001981. No further biographical detail is preserved.
