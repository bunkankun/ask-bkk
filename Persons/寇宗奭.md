Northern-Sòng 北宋 medical-pharmacological scholar, active c. 1116. Author of the celebrated **Běn cǎo yǎn yì** 本草衍義 ("Extended Meaning of the Materia Medica", 1116) — one of the most important Northern-Sòng critical works on the *běn cǎo* 本草 pharmacopoeia tradition.

**Office.** Held minor pharmacological-administrative positions in the Northern Sòng government.

**Works.**

1. **Běn cǎo yǎn yì** 本草衍義 (1116, 20 *juàn*) — his major work, refining and extending the *Jīng shǐ zhèng lèi běi jí běn cǎo* 經史證類備急本草 of Táng Shèn wēi 唐慎微. A landmark of Northern-Sòng pharmacology.

2. **[[KR5c0164|*Tú jīng jí zhù yǎn yì běn cǎo xù lì*]]** 圖經集註衍義本草序例 (DZ 768) — the preface and general principles to his major *Běn cǎo yǎn yì*, preserved in the Daozang with Xǔ Hóng's 1208 revision.

3. **[[KR5c0165|*Běn cǎo yǎn yì* (DZ 769)]]** — preserved in the Daozang as KR5c0165; the main *Běn cǎo yǎn yì* work.

**Dating.** Active 1116 (attested). No precise lifedates. No CBDB record identified.

**Significance.** Kòu Zōngshì's *Běn cǎo yǎn yì* is a major document of Northern-Sòng medical-scientific culture and Daoist *yǎng shēng* 養生 scholarship.
