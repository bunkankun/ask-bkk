Zhēn-yǎn 真衍, *hào* **Ǒu-ān** 藕菴 (also **Zhú-ān** 竹庵). Línjì-Yángqí (Hǔ-qiū → Wú-zhǔn → Duàn-qiáo Lún → 14 generations → Pǔ-míng Yòng → Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo → Zhēn-yǎn) dharma-heir of **Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo** 金明介老 — same teacher as **Píng-shān Cì-ān Zhēn-běn** [[真本]] (cf. [[KR6q0202]], [[KR6q0455]] preface) and **Yǒng-zhèng Yī-lǎo** 永正一老 (cf. [[KR6q0455]]). Zhēn-yǎn is therefore *fǎ-dì* dharma-brother to those figures within the Jīn-míng circle.

**Abbacy.** **Sū-zhōu Zhú-ān** 蘇州竹庵 (whence the title-form 蘇州竹庵衍).

**Work.** [[KR6q0490]] 《蘇州竹庵衍禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B345, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Jī-rú** [[機如]] 機如 (= **Gǔ-lín Jī-rú** of [[KR6q0491]]). Sources: [[KR6q0490]] juan 1 *xù*; cross-reference to [[KR6q0455]] *xù* lineage-statement.
