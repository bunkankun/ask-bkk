Zhēnyǎn 真衍, *hào* **Ǒuān** 藕菴 (also **Zhúān** 竹庵). Línjì-Yángqí (Hǔqiū → Wúzhǔn → Duànqiáo Lún → 14 generations → Pǔmíng Yòng → Jīnmíng Jièlǎo → Zhēnyǎn) dharma-heir of **Jīnmíng Jièlǎo** 金明介老 — same teacher as **Píngshān Cìān Zhēnběn** [[真本]] (cf. [[KR6q0202]], [[KR6q0455]] preface) and **Yǒngzhèng Yīlǎo** 永正一老 (cf. [[KR6q0455]]). Zhēnyǎn is therefore *fǎdì* dharma-brother to those figures within the Jīnmíng circle.

**Abbacy.** **Sūzhōu Zhúān** 蘇州竹庵 (whence the title-form 蘇州竹庵衍).

**Work.** [[KR6q0490]] 《蘇州竹庵衍禪師語錄》 2 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J36 B345, compiled by *fǎsì* **Jīrú** [[機如]] 機如 (= **Gǔlín Jīrú** of [[KR6q0491]]). Sources: [[KR6q0490]] juan 1 *xù*; cross-reference to [[KR6q0455]] *xù* lineage-statement.
