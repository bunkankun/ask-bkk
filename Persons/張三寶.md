Zhāng Sānbǎo 張三寶, early-Republican Hénán literatus from **Xiàngchéng** 項城 (now in Hénán). Author of the 1914 (*Jiǎyín* mid-winter) postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔnzǐ yī*, written at the *Zhōubīn Zhāngshì biéshù xiànzhì jú* (Zhāng family villa, county-gazetteer office) while serving in the Xiàngchéng county gazetteer-compilation effort. No standard biographical record located.
