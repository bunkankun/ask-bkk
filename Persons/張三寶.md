Zhāng Sān-bǎo 張三寶, early-Republican Hé-nán literatus from **Xiàng-chéng** 項城 (now in Hé-nán). Author of the 1914 (*Jiǎ-yín* mid-winter) postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔn-zǐ yī*, written at the *Zhōu-bīn Zhāng-shì bié-shù xiàn-zhì jú* (Zhāng family villa, county-gazetteer office) while serving in the Xiàng-chéng county gazetteer-compilation effort. No standard biographical record located.
