Late-Táng official-historian (*shǐguān* 史官) active in the *Dàzhōng* 大中 reign (847–860). CBDB id 184585 gives his death-date as 843 — but his preface to the *Lǐ xiàngguó lùnshì jí* ([[KR2g0005]]) is dated *Dàzhōng* 5 (851), tenth month, which is irreconcilable with a 843 death-date; the inconsistency may reflect either an error in the CBDB record or that the preface date is the deposit date in the *Dōngguān* 東觀 imperial archive, with Jiǎng's own writing some years earlier. He was active in the historiographical apparatus of the *Dàzhōng* court under Xuānzōng 宣宗 and is best known for editing Lǐ Jiàng's 李絳 memorials and remonstrances into the seven-*piān* *Lǐ xiàngguó lùnshì jí* on the basis of materials supplied by the Censor Xiàhóu 夏侯. The work is the principal Táng *jiànlù* of the *Yuánhé* reign and one of the foundational specimens of the Táng-period *zhuànjì* (more strictly *jiànlù*) genre.
