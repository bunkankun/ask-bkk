Late-Táng official-historian (*shǐ-guān* 史官) active in the *Dà-zhōng* 大中 reign (847–860). CBDB id 184585 gives his death-date as 843 — but his preface to the *Lǐ xiàng-guó lùn-shì jí* ([[KR2g0005]]) is dated *Dà-zhōng* 5 (851), tenth month, which is irreconcilable with a 843 death-date; the inconsistency may reflect either an error in the CBDB record or that the preface date is the deposit date in the *Dōng-guān* 東觀 imperial archive, with Jiǎng's own writing some years earlier. He was active in the historiographical apparatus of the *Dà-zhōng* court under Xuānzōng 宣宗 and is best known for editing Lǐ Jiàng's 李絳 memorials and remonstrances into the seven-*piān* *Lǐ xiàng-guó lùn-shì jí* on the basis of materials supplied by the Censor Xià-hóu 夏侯. The work is the principal Táng *jiàn-lù* of the *Yuán-hé* reign and one of the foundational specimens of the Táng-period *zhuàn-jì* (more strictly *jiàn-lù*) genre.
