Wēn Déyù 溫德裕, Kāngxī-period official at Yǎnchéng 郾城 (Hénán) whose office hosted [[李塨]] Lǐ Gōng (1659–1733) during the autumn 1704 revision of his *Zhōuyì zhuàn zhù* ([[KR1a0140]]). No further biographical details preserved.
