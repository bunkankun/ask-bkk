Lù Qīnlì 逯欽立 (1911–1973) was a twentieth-century Chinese literary historian and philologist, a professor at Jílín University 吉林大學. He is best known as the compiler of *Xiān-Qín Hàn Wèi Jìn Nánběicháo shī* 先秦漢魏晉南北朝詩 ([[KR4h0177]]), the most authoritative modern anthology of pre-Tang poetry (*shī* and *yuèfǔ*), completed in 1965 and published posthumously by Zhōnghuá Shūjú 中華書局 in three volumes in 1983. The work arranges material chronologically by dynasty and by individual poet, providing short biographical notices. A companion index (*Xiān-Qín Hàn Wèi Jìn Nánběicháo shī zuòzhě piānmù suǒyǐn* 先秦漢魏晉南北朝詩作者篇目索引) was published by Zhōnghuá in 1988.
