Zhìzhí 智質, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Shēnfēng Xiàn** [[憲]] 屾峰憲 (in turn dharma-heir of 三宜明盂). Co-compiler with **Zhìyuán** [[智原]] of the 10-juan 《屾峰憲禪師語錄》 ([[KR6q0472]]). No independent *yǔlù* or biography located.
