# 裴兆期

Péi Zhàoqí, late-Qīng physician whose *báihóu* case-record is included in the *Bǔlù míngàn* 補錄名案 of [[KR3em032]] *Báihóu biànzhèng* 白喉辨證 as evidence for the use of *fùguì* warming prescriptions in cold-deficient diphtheria.
