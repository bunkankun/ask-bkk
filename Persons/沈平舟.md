Shěn Píngzhōu 沈平舟 (fl. mid-18th c.) was a Sūzhōu pupil of [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí who preserved Yóu's unpublished manuscript of the *Jīnguì yì* ([[KR3eh003]]) until its first printing in 1768.
