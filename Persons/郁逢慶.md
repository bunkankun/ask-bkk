Late-Míng connoisseur of calligraphy and painting; *zì* Shū-yù 叔遇, *hào* Shuǐxī dàorén 水西道人, of Jiāxīng 嘉興 in Zhèjiāng. Active in the *Chóngzhēn* 崇禎 decade (1628–44). Editor of [[KR3h0054]] *Shū-huà tí-bá jì* 書畫題跋記 in 12 *juàn* with a *xù* 續 in 12 *juàn* — a transcription record of inscriptions and colophons on famous calligraphies and paintings he had seen and (in part) personally owned. Self-colophon dated *Chóngzhēn* 7 (1634). Otherwise undocumented in standard biographical sources.
