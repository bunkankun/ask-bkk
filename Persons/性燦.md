# Xìng-cǎn 性燦

Late-17th-century Qīng Chán monk; dharma-heir of [[燈映|Yě-yún Dēng-yìng]] 野雲燈映. Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of his master's *Yě-yún Yìng chánshī zōng tǒng sòng* 野雲映禪師宗統頌 [[KR6q0237]].
