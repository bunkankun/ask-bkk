# Xìngcǎn 性燦

Late-17th-century Qīng Chán monk; dharma-heir of [[燈映|Yěyún Dēngyìng]] 野雲燈映. Lifedates unrecorded. Compiler (*lù* 錄) of his master's *Yěyún Yìng chánshī zōng tǒng sòng* 野雲映禪師宗統頌 [[KR6q0237]].
