# Yuèlín Shīguān 月林師觀

Southern Sòng Línjì-Yángqí-branch Chán master; dharma-transmission teacher of [[慧開|Wúmén Huìkāi]] 無門慧開, under whom Huìkāi awakened on the Zhàozhōu *wú* 趙州無 turning-phrase.
