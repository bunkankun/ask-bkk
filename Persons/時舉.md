Sòng-period Tiāntái 天台 monk; lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. DILA Authority A000934. Author of the **Jīngāngbēi lùn shìwén** 金剛錍論釋文 ([[KR6d0181]], X56n0937, 3 *juan*) — a Sòng-period running commentary on Zhànrán's *Jīngāngbēi* ([[KR6d0175]]). Subsequently collated (會 *huì*) by the late-Míng monk **Hǎiyǎn** 海眼 ([[海眼]]).

Catalog note: the catalog meta gives the name as `時擧` (with the older/alternative form 擧 of the character 舉); both forms are used in the source corpus.

Source: DILA A000934; X56n0937.
