Yuè Zhèng 岳正 (1418–1472), *zì* **Jìfāng** 季方, *hào* **Méngquán** 蒙泉 (also **Méngwēng** 蒙翁). Native of **Zhāngdé** 漳德 (modern Wǎnpíng *xiàn*, Bei-jīng vicinity). In early Tiānshùn entered the cabinet on a brief tenure; tried to break the **Cáo Jíxiáng** 曹吉祥 / **Shí Hēng** 石亨 post-coup alliance; the scheme failed and he was banished twice (first to the southern wastes, then to the western garrisons). Even after the Duómén faction was destroyed, blocked from cabinet by **Lǐ Xián** 李賢 ([[李賢]])'s *màojí* (jealousy) and *lúnluò yǐ zhōng* (sank and died). Posthumously rehabilitated in early Jiājìng with the title *Tàichángsì qīng*. Teacher and father-in-law of **Lǐ Dōngyáng** 李東陽 ([[李東陽]]). In late life took up the *Huángjí jīngshì shū* 皇極經世書 (Shào Yōng's cosmology) and composed *Lèiyán* essays expounding it; his poetry purely in the *Jīrǎng jí* mode. *Míng shǐ* j. 176. Surviving works are [[KR4e0109]] *Lèibó gǎo* (compiled by Lǐ Dōngyáng); the separately catalogued *Lèibó záyán* 類博雜言 (ShàoYōng cosmological essays) circulates elsewhere. CBDB id 34513 (1418–1472).
