Yuè Zhèng 岳正 (1418–1472), *zì* **Jì-fāng** 季方, *hào* **Méng-quán** 蒙泉 (also **Méng-wēng** 蒙翁). Native of **Zhāng-dé** 漳德 (modern Wǎn-píng *xiàn*, Bei-jīng vicinity). In early Tiān-shùn entered the cabinet on a brief tenure; tried to break the **Cáo Jí-xiáng** 曹吉祥 / **Shí Hēng** 石亨 post-coup alliance; the scheme failed and he was banished twice (first to the southern wastes, then to the western garrisons). Even after the Duó-mén faction was destroyed, blocked from cabinet by **Lǐ Xián** 李賢 ([[李賢]])'s *mào-jí* (jealousy) and *lún-luò yǐ zhōng* (sank and died). Posthumously rehabilitated in early Jiā-jìng with the title *Tài-cháng-sì qīng*. Teacher and father-in-law of **Lǐ Dōng-yáng** 李東陽 ([[李東陽]]). In late life took up the *Huáng-jí jīng-shì shū* 皇極經世書 (Shào Yōng's cosmology) and composed *Lèi-yán* essays expounding it; his poetry purely in the *Jī-rǎng jí* mode. *Míng shǐ* j. 176. Surviving works are [[KR4e0109]] *Lèi-bó gǎo* (compiled by Lǐ Dōng-yáng); the separately catalogued *Lèi-bó zá-yán* 類博雜言 (Shào-Yōng cosmological essays) circulates elsewhere.
