Bōlún 波崙 was a Tang-period *śramaṇa* of the **Fúshòusì** 福壽寺 (Felicity-and-Longevity Monastery), known only from his preface to the *Bùkōng juànsuǒ tuóluóní jīng* 不空羂索陀羅尼經 ([[KR6j0304]], T20n1096), the translation of Lǐ Wúchǎn ([[李無諂]]) completed in Shènglì 3 (700). The preface — *不空羂索陀羅尼經序，福壽寺沙門波崙撰* — celebrates the doctrinal scope of the Amoghapāśa cycle and provides a condensed introduction to the cult, but supplies no biographical information beyond Bōlún's monastic affiliation and his travels between the two capitals (兩京) of the Tang.
