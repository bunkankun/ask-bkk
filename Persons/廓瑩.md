Kuòyíng 廓瑩 (Jp. Kakuei) was a late-seventeenth-century Jōdo-shū 浄土宗 master at Ryōzen-ji 霊山寺 in Edo who gave dharma transmission to [[湛慧]] Tan'e in 1692 (when Tan'e was 17). He is otherwise undocumented in the principal Jōdo-shū biographical sources.
