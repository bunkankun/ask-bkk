Zōngjiān 宗堅, *hào* **Bùlín** 不磷 ("Unyielding"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yězhú Fúhuì** [[福慧]] 野竹福慧 in the **Pòshān → Zìshuǐ Yuánliáo → Shānhuī Bì** [[璧]] **→ Yězhú Fúhuì → Zōngjiān** Sìchuān-Yúnnán sub-line. Native of **Qíyáng** 蘄陽 (Húběi); ministered in **Lùnánzhōu** 路南州 (Yúnnán) and at the **Sōngshān** 嵩山 seat (Yúnnánfǔ, formerly his teacher Fúhuì's principal seat). Per the [[KR6q0467]] preface: *fèn jì Qíyáng, huà xíng Nánzhào* 奮跡蘄陽化行南詔 — born and trained in Qíyáng / Húběi, mission ministry in Yúnnán's Nánzhào region. Subsequently took up the Sōngshān seat (*jì xí Sōngshān* 繼席嵩山) succeeding Fúhuì. The Qián Guāngxiù preface emphasizes Bùlín's distinctive lineage-claim back through *fù Yángqí ér zǔ Shíshuāng cái Míngjiào ér xué Juéfàn* 父楊岐而祖石霜才明教而學覺範.

**Work.** [[KR6q0467]] 《兜率不磷堅禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J33 B285, recorded by *fǎsì* **Miàoshèng** [[妙聖]]. Front-preface by **Qián Guāngxiù Shèngyuè** 錢光繡聖月 *zhéān yěsǒu* of **Yín** 鄞 (Níngbō), dated **康熙癸丑除夕前三日** = **1674/02/01 Gregorian** (just-before lunar new year of 甲寅).
