Zōng-jiān 宗堅, *hào* **Bù-lín** 不磷 ("Unyielding"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yě-zhú Fú-huì** [[福慧]] 野竹福慧 in the **Pòshān → Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo → Shān-huī Bì** [[璧]] **→ Yě-zhú Fú-huì → Zōng-jiān** Sì-chuān-Yún-nán sub-line. Native of **Qí-yáng** 蘄陽 (Hú-běi); ministered in **Lù-nán-zhōu** 路南州 (Yún-nán) and at the **Sōng-shān** 嵩山 seat (Yún-nán-fǔ, formerly his teacher Fú-huì's principal seat). Per the [[KR6q0467]] preface: *fèn jì Qí-yáng, huà xíng Nán-zhào* 奮跡蘄陽化行南詔 — born and trained in Qí-yáng / Hú-běi, mission ministry in Yún-nán's Nán-zhào region. Subsequently took up the Sōng-shān seat (*jì xí Sōng-shān* 繼席嵩山) succeeding Fú-huì. The Qián Guāng-xiù preface emphasizes Bù-lín's distinctive lineage-claim back through *fù Yáng-qí ér zǔ Shí-shuāng cái Míng-jiào ér xué Jué-fàn* 父楊岐而祖石霜才明教而學覺範.

**Work.** [[KR6q0467]] 《兜率不磷堅禪師語錄》 3 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B285, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Miào-shèng** [[妙聖]]. Front-preface by **Qián Guāng-xiù Shèng-yuè** 錢光繡聖月 *zhé-ān yě-sǒu* of **Yín** 鄞 (Níng-bō), dated **康熙癸丑除夕前三日** = **1674/02/01 Gregorian** (just-before lunar new year of 甲寅).
