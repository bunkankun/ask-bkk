Liú Bīn 劉賓 of Fùyuán 敷原, late-Southern-Sòng collator (校正) of the 1265 (*Xiányán yǐchǒu*) Jiǔjiāng print of [[朱熹]] Zhū Xī's *Yuán běn Zhōuyì běnyì* ([[KR1a0031]]) commissioned by [[吳革]] Wú Gé. His collation mark at the end of each juan is one of the philological signs by which the Sìkù editors identified this print as the textually clean archetype of Zhū Xī's original twelve-juan recension.
