Qīng-era physician of uncertain regional affiliation, otherwise undocumented in standard biographical sources. Sole documented work is [[KR3er106]] *Cūnjū jiùjí fāng* 村居救急方, a seven-*juǎn* rural-emergency formulary preserved through the 1925 *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 edition (edited by [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jíshēng with supplementary editing by [[曹炳章]] Cáo Bǐngzhāng). Internal evidence (the *Bìnán quányīng fǎ* refugee-protection technique referring to the *Tàipíng* disorder) suggests composition between the late 18th c. and mid-19th c. Not in CBDB.
