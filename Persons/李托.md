# Lǐ Tuō 李托

Southern Hàn court official (*xiùhuá gōngshǐ tèjìn* 秀華宮使特進); the agent whose dream-summoned report prompted the 965/966 imperial tomb-opening that discovered [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃's incorrupt body and the conferral of his posthumous title, as recorded in the *xínglù* in [[KR6q0073]].
