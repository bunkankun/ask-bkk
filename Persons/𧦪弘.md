**𧦪弘** (Tang, lifedates unknown) — obscure Tang-period Esoteric editor. The unusual character 𧦪 (a rare graphic variant) renders the name difficult to identify with certainty in the standard biographical literature. His sole attested compilation is the **Dǐng-lún-wáng dà màntúluó guàndǐng yíguǐ** 頂輪王大曼荼羅灌頂儀軌 ([[KR6j0136]], T19n0959) — a one-fascicle *abhiṣeka* ritual manual on the Crown-Wheel-King mandala.

No further biographical data are preserved in the standard authority databases. His name does not appear in DILA Buddhist Person Authority. The work is conventionally placed in the late-Tang Cháng-ān Esoteric tradition based on its content and stylistic features.

Source: T19n0959 colophon.
