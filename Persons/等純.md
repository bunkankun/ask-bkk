# Děng-chún 等純

Early-Qīng Cáo-dòng 曹洞 Chán *dì-zǐ* 弟子 of [[道霈]] Wèi-lín Dào-pèi (1615–1702); co-recorder of his master's four-juan *Wèi-lín chánshī lǚ-bó-ān gǎo* ([[KR6q0372]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
