# Shītǎn 師坦

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*ménrén* 門人 / *shìzhě* 侍者) active around the mid-thirteenth-century Jìngshān / Lín'ān Línjì circle; named among the fifteen co-editors of the *Wújùn Shīfàn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0315]]) and, with [[了覺]], principal editor of the *Shítián Fǎxūn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0319]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000912).
