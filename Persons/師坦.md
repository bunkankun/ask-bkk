# Shī-tǎn 師坦

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*mén-rén* 門人 / *shì-zhě* 侍者) active around the mid-thirteenth-century Jìng-shān / Lín'ān Línjì circle; named among the fifteen co-editors of the *Wújùn Shīfàn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0315]]) and, with [[了覺]], principal editor of the *Shí-tián Fǎ-xūn chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0319]]). Lifedates unrecorded (DILA A000912).
