Jiāng Zhànrán 江湛然 (Wàn-lì-era, fl. early 17th c.), of Shèrén 歙人 (Huìzhōu, Ānhuī). As *Jīnhuá tōngpàn* in **Wànlì *wùwǔ*** (1618), he edited and cut the 120-*juǎn* consolidated edition of **Hú Yìnglín**'s *Shǎoshì shānfáng jí* [[KR4e0218]] — bringing together about 20 of Hú's separately-circulated sub-collections into the form that became the WYG-base text.
