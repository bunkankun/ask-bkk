Jiāng Zhànrán 江湛然 (Wàn-lì-era, fl. early 17th c.), of Shè-rén 歙人 (Huì-zhōu, Ān-huī). As *Jīn-huá tōng-pàn* in **Wàn-lì *wù-wǔ*** (1618), he edited and cut the 120-*juǎn* consolidated edition of **Hú Yìnglín**'s *Shǎo-shì shān-fáng jí* [[KR4e0218]] — bringing together about 20 of Hú's separately-circulated sub-collections into the form that became the WYG-base text.
