Chéng Dàzhōng 程大中 (1721–?, *zì* Quánshí 拳時, *hào* Shìān 是菴), of Yīngchéng 應城 (Húběi). A mid-Qiánlóng-period private *kǎojù* scholar working in the early-Qīng *Sìshū kǎozhèng* tradition descended from Yán Ruòqú. His principal extant work is the *Sìshū yìjiān* 四書逸箋 ([[KR1h0066]]), in six *juàn*, dated 1757 in the catalog meta — submitted to the Sìkù programme during the Qiánlóng era. The *Sìshū yìjiān* is a *kǎojù* miscellany on points where the Zhū Xī *Jízhù* misquotes or fails to cite source-texts in the *Sìshū*; the Sìkù editors pair it explicitly with Yán Ruòqú's *Sìshū shìdì* ([[KR1h0063]]) as a *kǎojù*-mode complement. CBDB id 89932 records his birth-year as 1721; his death-year is not given. Standard reference works name him only as a *Húběi*-region scholar of *Sìshū kǎojù*; biographical detail beyond this is not preserved in widely-available sources.
