Chéng Dàzhōng 程大中 (1721–?, *zì* Quán-shí 拳時, *hào* Shì-ān 是菴), of Yīng-chéng 應城 (Hú-běi). A mid-Qiánlóng-period private *kǎo-jù* scholar working in the early-Qīng *Sì-shū kǎozhèng* tradition descended from Yán Ruò-qú. His principal extant work is the *Sì-shū yì-jiān* 四書逸箋 (KR1h0066), in six *juàn*, dated 1757 in the catalog meta — submitted to the Sìkù programme during the Qián-lóng era. The *Sì-shū yì-jiān* is a *kǎo-jù* miscellany on points where the Zhū Xī *Jí-zhù* misquotes or fails to cite source-texts in the *Sì-shū*; the Sìkù editors pair it explicitly with Yán Ruò-qú's *Sì-shū shì-dì* (KR1h0063) as a *kǎo-jù*-mode complement. CBDB id 89932 records his birth-year as 1721; his death-year is not given. Standard reference works name him only as a *Hú-běi*-region scholar of *Sì-shū kǎo-jù*; biographical detail beyond this is not preserved in widely-available sources.
