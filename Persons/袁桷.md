Yuán Juè 袁桷 (1266–1327), *zì* Bózhǎng 伯長, *hào* Qīngróng jūshì 清容居士, posthumous title Wénqīng 文清, native of Qìngyuán-lù 慶元路 (modern Níngbō 寧波, Zhèjiāng). One of the most important literary figures of the late Yuán court establishment. Great-grandson of the Southern Sòng *zhī Shūmìyuàn shì* (Vice State Councillor for Military Affairs) Yuán Sháo 袁韶 — the family was one of the principal "old households of inheritance of the southeast" (*dōngnán gùjiā yíxiàn* 東南故家遺獻).

In youth he served as *shānzhǎng* (head) of the Lìzé shūyuàn 麗澤書院 in Wúzhōu 婺州 (modern Jīnhuá 金華), the academy founded in honour of Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙. On recommendation he transferred to the Hànlín Guóshǐyuàn 翰林國史院 as *jiǎnyuè-guān* 檢閱官, rising by promotions to *Hànlín shìjiǎng xuéshì* 翰林侍講學士. He retired and was posthumously appointed *cānzhī zhèngshì* 參知政事 of the Jiāng-Zhè *xíngshěng* and enfeoffed *Chénliú-jùn-gōng* 陳留郡公.

His extant works include the substantial literary collection *Qīngróng jūshì jí* 清容居士集 in fifty juan ([[KR4d0473]] in the Sìkù; SBCK base preserves complete text), and the *Yánjiā Sìmíng zhì* ([[KR2k0025]]) compiled in 1320 at the request of *zǒngguǎn* Mǎ Zé. His *Yìshuō* 易説 and *Chūnqiū-shuō* 春秋説, recorded in Sū Tiānjué's 蘇天爵 *mùzhì-míng* (epitaph) for him, are no longer extant. His *Yuán shǐ* biography appears at *Yuán shǐ* j. 172.
