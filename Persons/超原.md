Chāoyuán 超原 (also written **Chāoyuán** 超原 — note the catalog-form variant for the same person also appearing as preface-author for several other Sì-chuān-related *yǔlù* in J38–J39, signed 莆陽 / 龍池後裔閩莆 *Dànyá*; cf. [[超源]] for the homophonic 超源 entries). *zì* **Dànyá** 澹崖 ("Bland Cliff"); often referred to as **Tōngtiān Dànyá** 通天澹崖. LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tōngtiān Yìsǒu Xínggāo** [[行高]] (1616–1669). Born **明崇禎庚午十一月十一日** = **1630-12-14** Gregorian; died **康熙戊寅四月十八日丑時** = **1698-05-27**, age 69, *sēnglà* 36. Native of **Xīnghuà Pútián** 興化莆田 (Fújiàn); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Formation.** As a youth studied Confucian texts. One day reading the *Diamond Sūtra* at the line "*yīqiè yǒuwéi fǎ rú mèng huàn pào yǐng*" (一切有為法 如夢幻泡影) felt the urge to leave the world. After 33 went to **Huángbòshān** 黃檗山 to **Xūbái** 虛白和尚 to take tonsure and full ordination; given the **Bǎishùzǐ** 柏樹子 *gōngàn*. One day before the main hall, looking at an old pine, the obstruction in his chest fell away. Sought out **Yìsǒu**; received final *fùfǎ* on **康熙己酉正月元日** = **1669-02-01**.

**Abbacy.** **Púyáng Tōngtiān chánsì** 蒲陽通天禪寺 (succeeded his teacher) for one year; then retired to travel **Lúshān** 廬嶽 and **Jīnlíng** 金陵 sites.

**Disciples.** Thirteen *fǎsì* including **Xiùyuán Pǔ** 秀源溥, **Guàntōng Zhào** 貫通照, **Jùnfēng Dé** 峻峰德, **Huáfēng Róng** 華峰容, **Xuězhū Yíng** 雪珠瑩.

**Works.**
- *Xínglüè* author of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0569]] 《通天逸叟高禪師語錄》.
- Own *yǔlù* [[KR6q0570]] 《通天澹崖原禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B456, compiled by *fǎsūn* **Míngdé** [[明德]] *děng biān*.
- Author of preface to the *Biéān chánshī tóngmén lù* 別菴禪師同門錄.

**Sources.** 《通天澹崖原禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈行實〉, 〈聯芳偈〉.

- - - - -

There is a separate 17th-century Mǐnpǔ Lóngchí line **Chāoyuán** 超源/超原 *Dànyá* who wrote prefaces to [[KR6q0554]], [[KR6q0557]], and [[KR6q0559]] in **1695–1696** — see [[超源]] for the cross-reference. The two are easily conflated since they share the *Dànyá* sobriquet, but the dating of the dual *yǔlù* of [[行高]] (d. 1669) and [[超原]] (d. 1698) makes this Tōngtiān Chāoyuán's authorship of those Sì-chuān-related prefaces in 1695–1696 plausible.
