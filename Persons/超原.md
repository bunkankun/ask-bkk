Chāo-yuán 超原 (also written **Chāo-yuán** 超原 — note the catalog-form variant for the same person also appearing as preface-author for several other Sì-chuān-related *yǔ-lù* in J38–J39, signed 莆陽 / 龍池後裔閩莆 *Dàn-yá*; cf. [[超源]] for the homophonic 超源 entries). *zì* **Dàn-yá** 澹崖 ("Bland Cliff"); often referred to as **Tōng-tiān Dàn-yá** 通天澹崖. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tōng-tiān Yì-sǒu Xíng-gāo** [[行高]] (1616–1669). Born **明崇禎庚午十一月十一日** = **1630-12-14** Gregorian; died **康熙戊寅四月十八日丑時** = **1698-05-27**, age 69, *sēng-là* 36. Native of **Xīng-huà Pú-tián** 興化莆田 (Fú-jiàn); lay surname **Zhāng** 張.

**Formation.** As a youth studied Confucian texts. One day reading the *Diamond Sūtra* at the line "*yī-qiè yǒu-wéi fǎ rú mèng huàn pào yǐng*" (一切有為法 如夢幻泡影) felt the urge to leave the world. After 33 went to **Huáng-bò-shān** 黃檗山 to **Xū-bái** 虛白和尚 to take tonsure and full ordination; given the **Bǎi-shù-zǐ** 柏樹子 *gōng-àn*. One day before the main hall, looking at an old pine, the obstruction in his chest fell away. Sought out **Yì-sǒu**; received final *fù-fǎ* on **康熙己酉正月元日** = **1669-02-01**.

**Abbacy.** **Pú-yáng Tōng-tiān chán-sì** 蒲陽通天禪寺 (succeeded his teacher) for one year; then retired to travel **Lú-shān** 廬嶽 and **Jīn-líng** 金陵 sites.

**Disciples.** Thirteen *fǎ-sì* including **Xiù-yuán Pǔ** 秀源溥, **Guàn-tōng Zhào** 貫通照, **Jùn-fēng Dé** 峻峰德, **Huá-fēng Róng** 華峰容, **Xuě-zhū Yíng** 雪珠瑩.

**Works.**
- *Xíng-lüè* author of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0569]] 《通天逸叟高禪師語錄》.
- Own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0570]] 《通天澹崖原禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B456, compiled by *fǎ-sūn* **Míng-dé** [[明德]] *děng biān*.
- Author of preface to the *Bié-ān chán-shī tóng-mén lù* 別菴禪師同門錄.

**Sources.** 《通天澹崖原禪師語錄》 vol. 2 〈行實〉, 〈聯芳偈〉.

- - - - -

There is a separate 17th-century Mǐn-pǔ Lóng-chí line **Chāo-yuán** 超源/超原 *Dàn-yá* who wrote prefaces to [[KR6q0554]], [[KR6q0557]], and [[KR6q0559]] in **1695–1696** — see [[超源]] for the cross-reference. The two are easily conflated since they share the *Dàn-yá* sobriquet, but the dating of the dual *yǔ-lù* of [[行高]] (d. 1669) and [[超原]] (d. 1698) makes this Tōng-tiān Chāo-yuán's authorship of those Sì-chuān-related prefaces in 1695–1696 plausible.
