Guǎn Shí-mǐn 管時敏 (active 1378–after 1403), native of **Yún-jiān** 雲間 (= Sōng-jiāng, now Shànghǎi). Twenty-five-year-long household-official of the **Chǔ-wáng** 楚王 (Princely Establishment of Chǔ at Wǔ-chāng, under **Zhū Zhēn** 朱楨, Hóngwǔ's sixth son): promoted in succession from *jǐ-shàn* 紀善 to *zuǒ cháng-shǐ* 左長史 (head of the household-officials). The Yǒng-lè 1 (1403) preface to his collection [[KR4e0051]] *Yǐn-qiào jí* 蚓竅集, written by his fellow Chǔ-fǔ official **Hú Cuì-zhōng** 胡粹中 (then *Chǔ-fǔ yòu cháng-shǐ* 楚府右長史), is the principal external biographical witness. The collection was printed by imperial command of the Chǔ-wáng in Yǒng-lè 1 (1403). Birth-date probably 1340s; death-date post-1403. CBDB id 34390 records the name and Míng dynasty but no dates.
