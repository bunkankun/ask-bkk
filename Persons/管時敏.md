Guǎn Shímǐn 管時敏 (active 1378–after 1403), native of **Yúnjiān** 雲間 (= Sōngjiāng, now Shànghǎi). Twenty-five-year-long household-official of the **Chǔwáng** 楚王 (Princely Establishment of Chǔ at Wǔchāng, under **Zhū Zhēn** 朱楨, Hóngwǔ's sixth son): promoted in succession from *jǐshàn* 紀善 to *zuǒ chángshǐ* 左長史 (head of the household-officials). The Yǒnglè 1 (1403) preface to his collection [[KR4e0051]] *Yǐnqiào jí* 蚓竅集, written by his fellow Chǔfǔ official **Hú Cuìzhōng** 胡粹中 (then *Chǔfǔ yòu chángshǐ* 楚府右長史), is the principal external biographical witness. The collection was printed by imperial command of the Chǔwáng in Yǒnglè 1 (1403). Birth-date probably 1340s; death-date post-1403. CBDB id 34390 records the name and Míng dynasty but no dates.
