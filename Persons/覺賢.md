*Juéxián* 覺賢 ("Realised Worthy") is the standard Chinese rendering of the Sanskrit name **Buddhabhadra**; the same person also appears under the phonetic transliterations 佛陀跋陀羅 and 佛陀跋陀. For the full biography see [[佛陀跋陀羅]] (Buddhabhadra, 359–429): Kapilavastu-born Sarvāstivāda meditation-master, translator at Yángzhōu Dàochǎngsì of the 60-fascicle *Huāyán jīng* (T9n278) and (with [[法顯]]) of the *Móhēsēngqí lǜ* ([[KR6k0006]], T22n1425).
