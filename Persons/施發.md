Southern-Sòng physician of Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (Wēnzhōu). *Zì* Zhèngqīng 政卿, *hào* Guìtáng 桂堂. He turned to medicine in middle age after abandoning the examination track. Author of *Chá bìng zhǐ nán* 察病指南 ([[KR3eb002]], 1241), an early illustrated pulse manual with thirty-three pulse-image diagrams; also of a lost *Xù yì jiǎn fāng* 續易簡方 and a *Běn cǎo biàn yì* 本草辨異. Lifedates 1190–1268 are conventional, derived from his own preface dating; no CBDB record.
