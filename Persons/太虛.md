# Tàixū 太虛

Republican-era Chinese Buddhist reformer, founder of the *rénshēng fójiào* 人生佛教 ("Buddhism for Human Life") movement and a principal driver of twentieth-century Chinese Buddhist modernisation. Lay name Lǚ Gànsēn 呂淦森; *zì* Wéixīn 唯心. Educator, prolific writer, and organiser of the *Wǔchāng Buddhist Studies Institute* 武昌佛學院 and the *Sea-of-Tide Sound* 海潮音 journal. His reforms ("*three revolutions*" of doctrine, system, and property) shaped twentieth-century Chinese Buddhist institutional life.
