**Nagao Sōjō** 長尾藻城, early-twentieth-century Japanese physician-editor active in the Meiji-Taishō transition (*fl.* 1900s–1930s by internal evidence), principal compiler of **[[KR3eq038]]** *Xiān-zhé yī-huà jí* 先哲醫話集 — a one-*juǎn* anthology of approximately one hundred and thirty clinical maxims drawn from the entire range of Edo-period Japanese *kohōha* and *gosei-ha* medical literature. The compilation is modelled on Asada Sōhaku's [[KR3ep002]] *Xiān-zhé yī-huà* 先哲醫話 (1879) but with a substantially broader source-base extending to Meiji-era authors.
