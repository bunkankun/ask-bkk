**Gě Qiánsūn** 葛乾孫 (*zì* Kějiǔ 可久, 1305–1353, CBDB 109807), late-Yuán physician of Sūzhōu 蘇州 (the *gū-sū chūn-xiān-táng* 姑蘇春先堂 of his self-preface). Gě is a representative of the late-Yuán Sūzhōu medical milieu, contemporary with the great consolidation of the Dānxī tradition by [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng (1281–1358) just to the south.

His one famous work is the *Shíyào shénshū* 十藥神書 ([[KR3eh041]] / its versified mnemonic [[KR3eh044]]) — a famously concise treatise on consumption (*láo-zhài* 癆瘵 / pulmonary wasting) built around ten signature prescriptions in sequenced order: hemostasis (*Shí-huī sǎn* 十灰散, *Huā-ruǐ-shí sǎn* 花蕊石散), ginseng restoration (*Dú-shēn tāng* 獨參湯), sustaining lung-yīn (*Bǎo-hé tāng* 保和湯, *Bǎo-zhēn tāng* 保真湯, *Tài-píng wán* 太平丸), phlegm-heat purgation (*Chén-xiāng xiāo-huà wán* 沉香消化丸), lung lubrication (*Rùn-fèi gāo* 潤肺膏, *Bái-fèng gāo* 白鳳膏), and final marrow-replenishment (*Bǔ-suǐ dān* 補髓丹). The author's own preface is dated *Zhì-zhèng wù-zǐ* = 1348.

The work entered a long Ming-Qing commentary tradition, with major editorial layers from Zhōu Yángjùn 周揚俊 (1687 preface), Chén Niànzǔ 陳念祖 (1753–1823, the standard 註), Pān Wěi 潘霨 (1879 expanded edition), and Lín Shòuxuān 林壽萱 (1857 *jué* / 歌訣 supplement).
