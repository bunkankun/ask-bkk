Wēng Chūnyán 翁春岩 (fl. late 18th – early 19th c.) was a Sūzhōu physician through whose library the manuscript of the *Yīxiào mìchuán* ([[KR3eh022]]) passed before reaching [[吳金壽]] Wú Jīnshòu for the 1831 first printing.
