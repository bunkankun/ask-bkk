# Lín Hóng-yǎn 林弘衍

Late-Míng Fú-jiàn lay Buddhist editor, *zì* Dé-shān 得山 (as 林得山居士 in contemporary prefaces). Lifedates unrecorded. Active in the late-Wàn-lì / Tiān-qǐ era on the editorial recovery and temple-restoration of the late-Táng Fú-jiàn masters of the Xuě-fēng line. Editor (*biān-cì* 編次) of the Míng recension of the *Xuě-fēng Yì-cún chánshī yǔlù* 雪峰義存禪師語錄 / *Zhēn-jué chánshī yǔlù* 真覺禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0271]]) and of the parallel three-juan Míng recension of the *Xuán-shā Shī-bèi chánshī yǔlù* 玄沙師備禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0376]]). Also undertook the physical restoration of the derelict Xuán-shā temple-site in Fú-zhōu; [[圓澄]] Zhàn-rán Yuán-chéng's 1626 preface to [[KR6q0376]] praises his work.
