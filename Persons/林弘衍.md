# Lín Hóngyǎn 林弘衍

Late-Míng Fújiàn lay Buddhist editor, *zì* Déshān 得山 (as 林得山居士 in contemporary prefaces). Lifedates unrecorded. Active in the late-Wàn-lì / Tiānqǐ era on the editorial recovery and temple-restoration of the late-Táng Fújiàn masters of the Xuěfēng line. Editor (*biāncì* 編次) of the Míng recension of the *Xuěfēng Yìcún chánshī yǔlù* 雪峰義存禪師語錄 / *Zhēnjué chánshī yǔlù* 真覺禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0271]]) and of the parallel three-juan Míng recension of the *Xuánshā Shībèi chánshī yǔlù* 玄沙師備禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0376]]). Also undertook the physical restoration of the derelict Xuánshā temple-site in Fúzhōu; [[圓澄]] Zhànrán Yuánchéng's 1626 preface to [[KR6q0376]] praises his work.
