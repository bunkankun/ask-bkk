**Yáng Sùyuán** 楊素園, Qīng-period official of Hángzhōu; requested a transcription of [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng's *Nǚkē jíyào* manuscript ([[KR3ei010]]), which prompted [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng (Mèngyīng) to undertake the 1850 printed edition.
