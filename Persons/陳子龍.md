Chén Zǐ-lóng 陳子龍 (1608–1647), *zì* **Rén-zhōng** 人中 (later changed to **Wò-zǐ** 臥子), *bié-zì* **Yì-fú** 軼符, *hào* **Dà-zūn** 大樽 / **Hǎi-shì** 海士, posthumous title **Zhōng-yù** 忠裕 (granted by the Southern-Míng Lú-wáng court). Native of **Huá-tíng** 華亭 (modern Sōng-jiāng 松江, Shàng-hǎi). *Jìnshì* of Chóng-zhēn 10 / *dīng-chǒu* (1637). One of the dominant younger literary voices of the late Míng — the leading figure of the **Yún-jiān shī-pài** 雲間詩派 and a chief organiser of the **Jǐ-shè** 幾社 (the literary society parallel to the Suzhōu-based *Fù-shè*). With Xià Yǔn-yí 夏允彝, Xú Fú-yuǎn 徐孚遠 and others, he compiled the great late-Míng statecraft encyclopaedia *Huáng Míng jīng-shì wén-biān* 皇明經世文編 (1638). After 1644 he joined the Southern-Míng resistance, served briefly under the Hóng-guāng court, and after the fall of Sōng-jiāng plotted a rising with the Lú-wáng régime; captured in 1647 by Qīng forces, he drowned himself en route to Nán-jīng (or, in another tradition, in a river escape). The Wàn-lì-and-after revival of *zhuàng-yǔ* (the *cí* lyric) is largely to his credit, in particular his anthology *Yún-jiān èr Chén-zǐ shī-yú* 雲間二陳子詩餘 (with Chén Pèi 陳沛). His preface to the *Ní Wén-zhēn jí* ([[KR4e0240]]) dates from late 1642 / *rén-wǔ* and records his three years' attendance on [[倪元璐]] at Yuè-zhōng. CBDB 29584. *Míng-shǐ* j. 277.
