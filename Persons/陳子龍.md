Chén Zǐlóng 陳子龍 (1608–1647), *zì* **Rénzhōng** 人中 (later changed to **Wòzǐ** 臥子), *biézì* **Yìfú** 軼符, *hào* **Dàzūn** 大樽 / **Hǎishì** 海士, posthumous title **Zhōngyù** 忠裕 (granted by the Southern-Míng Lúwáng court). Native of **Huátíng** 華亭 (modern Sōngjiāng 松江, Shànghǎi). *Jìnshì* of Chóngzhēn 10 / *dīngchǒu* (1637). One of the dominant younger literary voices of the late Míng — the leading figure of the **Yúnjiān shīpài** 雲間詩派 and a chief organiser of the **Jǐshè** 幾社 (the literary society parallel to the Suzhōu-based *Fùshè*). With Xià Yǔnyí 夏允彝, Xú Fúyuǎn 徐孚遠 and others, he compiled the great late-Míng statecraft encyclopaedia *Huáng Míng jīngshì wénbiān* 皇明經世文編 (1638). After 1644 he joined the Southern-Míng resistance, served briefly under the Hóngguāng court, and after the fall of Sōngjiāng plotted a rising with the Lúwáng régime; captured in 1647 by Qīng forces, he drowned himself en route to Nánjīng (or, in another tradition, in a river escape). The Wàn-lì-and-after revival of *zhuàngyǔ* (the *cí* lyric) is largely to his credit, in particular his anthology *Yúnjiān èr Chénzǐ shīyú* 雲間二陳子詩餘 (with Chén Pèi 陳沛). His preface to the *Ní Wénzhēn jí* ([[KR4e0240]]) dates from late 1642 / *rénwǔ* and records his three years' attendance on [[倪元璐]] at Yuèzhōng. CBDB 29584. *Míngshǐ* j. 277.
