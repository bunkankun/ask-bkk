Wáng Yún 王筠 (472–549 CE; *zì* Yuánlǐ 元禮) was a Liáng 梁 official, calligrapher, and poet from Pénchéng 彭城. He rose to Shàngshū lìng 尚書令 near the end of his life. Admired as a calligrapher and as a court poet in the Palace Style circle alongside Liú Xiàochuò 劉孝綽 and Liú Xiàowēi 劉孝威. His Buddhist piety is attested in Buddhist letters preserved in the *Guǎng hóng míng jí*. Biography in *Liángshū* juǎn 33 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 22. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0060]].
