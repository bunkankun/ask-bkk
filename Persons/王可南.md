Wáng Kěnán 王可南, a late-Míng / early-Qīng householder of Máchéng 麻城 in whose household [[包儀]] Bāo Yí at last obtained a copy of [[邵雍]] Shào Yōng's *Huáng jí jīng shì* 皇極經世 in 1669, after eighteen years of failed searches — the precipitating event of [[KR1a0135]] *Yì yuán jiù zhèng*. No further biographical details preserved.
