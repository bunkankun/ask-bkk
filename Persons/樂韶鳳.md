Early-Míng official, *zì* Zhì-hé 致和, of Quán-jiāo 全椒 (Anhui). Joined Zhū Yuán-zhāng 朱元璋 in Lóng-jiāng (Nán-jīng) in Yuán Zhì-zhèng 15 (1355) and accompanied the founder through the dynastic foundation. Held senior posts under Hóng-wǔ: *Bīng-bù shàng-shū*, *Hàn-lín shì-jiǎng xué-shì*, *Yú-shǐ dà-fū*. Died c. Hóng-wǔ 15 (1382). The principal-named compiler (alongside Sòng Lián [[宋濓]], Wāng Guǎng-yáng, Chén Níng et al.) of the *Hóng-wǔ zhèng-yùn* [[KR1j0068]] (16 *juàn*) on imperial command in Hóng-wǔ 8 (1375) — the new official rhyme-book of the Míng. The book aimed to replace Lù Fǎ-yán's [[陸法言]] *Qiè-yùn* lineage (vilified as Wú dialect — actually a confusion with the earlier Shěn Yuē 沈約 work) with a "Central Plain" (*Zhōng-yuán*) rhyme-system, reducing the inherited 206 rhymes to 76 (22+22+22+10) and re-glossing on the basis of Máo Huǎng's [[毛晃]] *Zēng-yùn* [[KR1j0061]].
