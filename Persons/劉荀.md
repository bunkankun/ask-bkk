Liú Xún 劉荀 (Sòng) was a Southern Sòng official and classicist of Dōngpíng 東平 (Shāndōng). Grandson of *Shàngshū zuǒ púshè* Liú Zhì 劉摯 (1030–1097), the Yuán-yòu-faction Chancellor; his work refers to Liú Zhì as *Xiān Wénsù gōng* 先文肅公. Held office under Xiàozōng as *Zhī Xūyí jūn* 知盱眙軍. His career is otherwise unrecoverable. His major surviving work is the *Míng běn shì* 明本釋 ([[KR3a0050]]), a *biji* of his *jiǎngxué* discussions in 33 sections drawing on the Yuán-yòu-faction tradition (his grandfather Liú Zhì's *jiāshū*), the Chéng-school (Chéng Yí, Yáng Shí, Hú Ānguó's son Hú Hóng, Zhāng Jiǔchéng), and Zhū Xī.
