Liú Xún 劉荀 (Sòng) was a Southern Sòng official and classicist of Dōng-píng 東平 (Shān-dōng). Grandson of *Shàng-shū zuǒ pú-shè* Liú Zhì 劉摯 (1030–1097), the Yuán-yòu-faction Chancellor; his work refers to Liú Zhì as *Xiān Wén-sù gōng* 先文肅公. Held office under Xiào-zōng as *Zhī Xū-yí jūn* 知盱眙軍. His career is otherwise unrecoverable. His major surviving work is the *Míng běn shì* 明本釋 (KR3a0050), a *biji* of his *jiǎng-xué* discussions in 33 sections drawing on the Yuán-yòu-faction tradition (his grandfather Liú Zhì's *jiā-shū*), the Chéng-school (Chéng Yí, Yáng Shí, Hú Ān-guó's son Hú Hóng, Zhāng Jiǔchéng), and Zhū Xī.
