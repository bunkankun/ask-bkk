**Kaidō Rinjō** 快道林常 (1751–1810) — Edo-period Japanese Buzan 豐山 Shingi-Shingon 新義真言 scholar-monk active at the Buzan Aizen-in 愛染院; signatory of "上陽沙門" ("śramaṇa of Jōyō"). He prepared critical editions of several early-Heian Hossō *yīnmíng* 因明 texts, most importantly [[明詮]] (Myōsen)'s *Dàoshō* 大疏噵 ([[KR6o0012]]) and its parallel *Lǐshū* 大疏裏書 ([[KR6o0013]]) at the Buzan Aizen-in. His Kansei 4 (1792) colophon on the *Dàoshō* documents his collation of the *Bekuroku* 別録 and *Dō-hon* 噵本 manuscript witnesses, recording his method as "from the two manuscripts together verified, following the more reasonable reading" (兩本雙撿以從理長者). Kaidō was a key figure in the late-Edo Buzan Shingi-Shingon editorial movement that produced critical editions of much of the early-Heian Hossō *inmyō* corpus.
