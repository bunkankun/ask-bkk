# Císhòu Huáishēn 慈受懷深 (1077–1132)

Northern-Sòng / early Southern-Sòng Yúnmén-school Chán master, abbot at *Císhòu* 慈受 and other Zhèjiāng monasteries. Author of the *Císhòu chánshī guǎnglù* 慈受懷深禪師廣錄 and a substantial corpus of doctrinal verse. The line *shìjiān duō shājié, hú-bīng dào* 世間多殺劫，胡兵到 ("there is much killing in the world, and so the kalpa-of-the-blade arrives") attributed to him is cited at the head of [[周夢顏]] Zhōu Mèngyán's *Jièshā sìshíbā wèn* 戒殺四十八問 ([[KR6k0262]], X1138) as the doctrinal frame for the work's anti-killing apologetic.
