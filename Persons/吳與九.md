**Wú Yǔjiǔ** 吳與九 (style **Shuāngxī** 雙溪, mid-19th c. 清), Jiāngnán medical-publisher and son of the physician [[吳硯丞]] Wú Yànchéng. Author of the 1853 preface to his father's *Mázhěn bèiyào fānglùn* 麻疹備要方論 ([[KR3ej054]]). Wú Yǔjiǔ arranged the printing of this work as a companion volume to the *Shénjiǔ jīnglún* 神灸經綸 acupuncture-moxibustion work that Wú Yànchéng had previously edited. CBDB has no entry.
