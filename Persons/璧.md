# Bì 璧 (Shān-huī Bì 山暉璧 / Cháng-sōng Bì 長松璧)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master, **the principal Qīng-era mission-founder of institutional Chán in Guì-zhōu** (southwestern frontier). Dharma-heir of [[圓䂐|Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo]] 字水圓䂐 (1605–c. 1640s, at [[KR6q0436]]) in the **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** 破山海明 sub-line of the Mìyún Yuán-wù tradition; additional training residence at **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** 玉林通琇's **Bào-ēn-sì** 報恩寺. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Bì** 璧, *hào* **Shān-huī** 山暉 / **Cháng-sōng** 長松.

**Training.** First encountered Yuán-liáo at **Qī-líng-sì** 棲靈寺 (Kāi-xiàn, Sichuan) in the early Chóng-zhēn era (1630s). Followed Yuán-liáo to **Kuí-mén Wò-lóng** 夔門臥龍 in **1638**; tonsured **1638 lunar 11.14** (= ~20 December 1638). Accompanied Yuán-liáo on the **1639** Jīng-nán grave-sweeping journey. Additional training at Jiāng-zhè and Bào-ēn.

**Guì-zhōu mission (1651 onwards).** **辛卯 1651**: settled at **Méi-é-shān** 㠟峨山 in Guì-zhōu. Five-abbacy network: **Píng-yuè-fǔ Lóng-mén Hù-guó-yuàn** 平越府龍門護國; **Píng-yuè-fǔ Xīng-qìng Wàn-shòu-yuàn** 興慶萬壽; **Wèng-ān-xiàn Chuān-yún Shèng-ēn-yuàn** 甕安川雲聖恩; **Huáng-píng-zhōu Gāo-lán Cháng-sōng-yuàn** 黃平高藍長松 (giving his alternate *hào* **Cháng-sōng**); **Yáng-zhōu-fǔ Gāo-yóu Fú-hǎi-yuàn** 揚州府高郵福海 (late-career Jiāng-nán seat).

**Historical significance.** Shān-huī's 5-abbacy Guì-zhōu network is the **first documented substantial Chán-institutional establishment in the Guì-zhōu frontier**. The Chóng-qìng disciple **Fú-huì** 福慧's preface explicitly compares him to **Wáng Yáng-míng** 王陽明 during the latter's 1508–10 Lóng-chǎng exile (which introduced Confucian learning to Guì-zhōu): Shān-huī brought the same-scale religious-institutional impact to the province for Chán.

Birth and death dates not preserved in accessible portions; active 1638–c. 1670s.

Work: [[KR6q0437]] 《山暉禪師語錄》 (12 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B223, compiled by [[如崇]] Rú-chóng and [[宗上]] Zōng-shàng; two undated front-prefaces by **Xiāo Yuán-huì** 蕭元會 and **Fú-huì** 福慧).

Sources: [[KR6q0437]] juan 1 prefaces; scattered dated references in juan 6 (tonsure-date 1638.11.14), juan 11 (Wò-lóng residence 1638), juan 12 (1639 Jīng-nán trip and Qī-líng-sì early-attendance); **Qí Lǐ-jiā** 齊立家, 《貴州佛教史》.
