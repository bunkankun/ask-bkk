# Bì 璧 (Shānhuī Bì 山暉璧 / Chángsōng Bì 長松璧)

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán master, **the principal Qīng-era mission-founder of institutional Chán in Guìzhōu** (southwestern frontier). Dharma-heir of [[圓䂐|Zìshuǐ Yuánliáo]] 字水圓䂐 (1605–c. 1640s, at [[KR6q0436]]) in the **Pòshān Hǎimíng** 破山海明 sub-line of the Mìyún Yuánwù tradition; additional training residence at **Yùlín Tōngxiù** 玉林通琇's **Bàoēnsì** 報恩寺. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Bì** 璧, *hào* **Shānhuī** 山暉 / **Chángsōng** 長松.

**Training.** First encountered Yuánliáo at **Qīlíngsì** 棲靈寺 (Kāixiàn, Sichuan) in the early Chóngzhēn era (1630s). Followed Yuánliáo to **Kuímén Wòlóng** 夔門臥龍 in **1638**; tonsured **1638 lunar 11.14** (= ~20 December 1638). Accompanied Yuánliáo on the **1639** Jīngnán grave-sweeping journey. Additional training at Jiāngzhè and Bàoēn.

**Guìzhōu mission (1651 onwards).** **辛卯 1651**: settled at **Méiéshān** 㠟峨山 in Guìzhōu. Five-abbacy network: **Píngyuèfǔ Lóngmén Hùguóyuàn** 平越府龍門護國; **Píngyuèfǔ Xīngqìng Wànshòuyuàn** 興慶萬壽; **Wèngānxiàn Chuānyún Shèngēnyuàn** 甕安川雲聖恩; **Huángpíngzhōu Gāolán Chángsōngyuàn** 黃平高藍長松 (giving his alternate *hào* **Chángsōng**); **Yángzhōufǔ Gāoyóu Fúhǎiyuàn** 揚州府高郵福海 (late-career Jiāngnán seat).

**Historical significance.** Shānhuī's 5-abbacy Guìzhōu network is the **first documented substantial Chán-institutional establishment in the Guìzhōu frontier**. The Chóngqìng disciple **Fúhuì** 福慧's preface explicitly compares him to **Wáng Yángmíng** 王陽明 during the latter's 1508–10 Lóngchǎng exile (which introduced Confucian learning to Guìzhōu): Shānhuī brought the same-scale religious-institutional impact to the province for Chán.

Birth and death dates not preserved in accessible portions; active 1638–c. 1670s.

Work: [[KR6q0437]] 《山暉禪師語錄》 (12 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J29 B223, compiled by [[如崇]] Rúchóng and [[宗上]] Zōngshàng; two undated front-prefaces by **Xiāo Yuánhuì** 蕭元會 and **Fúhuì** 福慧).

Sources: [[KR6q0437]] juan 1 prefaces; scattered dated references in juan 6 (tonsure-date 1638.11.14), juan 11 (Wòlóng residence 1638), juan 12 (1639 Jīngnán trip and Qīlíngsì early-attendance); **Qí Lǐjiā** 齊立家, 《貴州佛教史》.
