# 章次公 Zhāng Cìgōng

Republican-era and PRC-era Shanghai-school physician (1903–1959); student of [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐngfǔ at the Shanghai TCM Specialist College; later a leading PRC clinician known for his integration of *jīngfāng* and modern pharmacology.
