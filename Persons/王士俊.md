Wáng Shìjùn 王士俊 (1694–1756), *zì* Zhuósān 灼三, *hào* Xīchuān 犀川, native of Píngyuèzhōu 平越州 (modern Guìzhōu). *Jìnshì* of Kāngxī 60 (1721); rose through Hénán postings and inherited the Hédōng Governor-Generalship from Tián Wénjìng on the latter's death (1732), serving in Hénán until Qiánlóng 1 (1736). It was Wáng who completed the *Hénán tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0047]]) in 1735 and presented it to the throne. Subsequently held Húběi governorship and LiǎngJiāng governor-generalship.
