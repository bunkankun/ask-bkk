**Lú Jué-yú** 盧覺愚 (*hào* Jué-lú 覺廬, *fl.* 1900s–1940s), Republican-era Cantonese physician based in Hong Kong / Guǎngzhōu. Originally a student at the **Hong Kong Government English-language School at Sai Ying Pun** 西營盤官立英文學校 (a mark of his English-language educational formation, distinctive among Republican-era Chinese-medical authors). Turned to medical study at age 17 after his mother's iatrogenic-failure death from over-treatment with warming-tonifying drugs. Studied under the laicized Guǎng-xī Buddhist monk Fāng Bó-zhāi 方博齋 of Guì-lín 桂林 and the physician Léi Dān-fēng 雷丹峰. Author of *Jué-lú yī-huà lù-cún* 覺廬醫話錄存 ([[KR3eq073]]). Not in CBDB.
