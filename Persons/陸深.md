Lù Shēn 陸深 (1477–1544), *zì* **Zǐyuān** 子淵, *hào* **Yǎnshān** 儼山, posthumous *shì* **Wényù** 文裕, of **Shànghǎi** 上海 (Sōngjiāngfǔ, Jiāngsū). Hóngzhì 18 / *yǐchǒu* (1505) *jìnshì*; rose to *Zhānshìfǔ zhānshì* concurrent *Hànlínyuàn xuéshì*. *Míngshǐ Wényuàn zhuàn*. Youth-companion of **Xú Zhēnqīng** ([[徐禎卿]]). Master calligrapher (after **Lǐ Yōng** 李邕 and **Zhào Mèngfǔ** 趙孟頫); *shǎngjiàn bóyǎ* (connoisseurship broad-and-elegant) — *cíchén guàn* (first among literary courtiers). Multiple-province administrator: *Shǎnxī tíxué* in Shānxī; *cānfān* in Húguang; *xúnxuān* in Sichuan — meritorious wherever sent. The collection is *Yǎnshān jí* in 100 *juǎn* + *Xùjí* in 10 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0165]]) — edited and printed by his son the *Guózǐjiàn shēng* **Lù Jí** 陸楫. Additional *Wàijí* 40 *juǎn* of *bǐjì zázhù* circulates separately. CBDB id 33841, 1477–1544.
