Lù Shēn 陸深 (1477–1544), *zì* **Zǐ-yuān** 子淵, *hào* **Yǎn-shān** 儼山, posthumous *shì* **Wén-yù** 文裕, of **Shàng-hǎi** 上海 (Sōng-jiāng-fǔ, Jiāng-sū). Hóng-zhì 18 / *yǐ-chǒu* (1505) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Zhān-shì-fǔ zhān-shì* concurrent *Hàn-lín-yuàn xué-shì*. *Míng-shǐ Wén-yuàn zhuàn*. Youth-companion of **Xú Zhēn-qīng** ([[徐禎卿]]). Master calligrapher (after **Lǐ Yōng** 李邕 and **Zhào Mèng-fǔ** 趙孟頫); *shǎng-jiàn bó-yǎ* (connoisseurship broad-and-elegant) — *cí-chén guàn* (first among literary courtiers). Multiple-province administrator: *Shǎn-xī tí-xué* in Shānxī; *cān-fān* in Húguang; *xún-xuān* in Sichuan — meritorious wherever sent. The collection is *Yǎn-shān jí* in 100 *juǎn* + *Xù-jí* in 10 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0165]]) — edited and printed by his son the *Guó-zǐ-jiàn shēng* **Lù Jí** 陸楫. Additional *Wài-jí* 40 *juǎn* of *bǐ-jì zá-zhù* circulates separately. CBDB id 33841, 1477–1544.
