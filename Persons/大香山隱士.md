*Dà Xiāng-shān yǐn shì* 大香山隱士 ("Hermit of Great Fragrant Mountain"), an unidentified Tang-period Buddhist editor; the compiler (*jí* 輯) of [[KR6e0162|*Jiànná biāohē yī chéng xiū xíng zhě mì mì yì jì* 健拏標訶一乘修行者秘密義記]] (Fáng-shān F1084), a transcription of esoteric Huáyán-school doctrinal material attributed in part to [[法藏]] Fǎzàng. The "Hermit of Great Fragrant Mountain" pseudonym is unidentified; *Dà Xiāng-shān* 大香山 refers to a mountain in the Cháng'ān region. Birth and death dates not preserved.
