# Shīkǎi 師楷

Yuán-dynasty Chán *sìfǎ ménrén* 嗣法門人 of [[如砥]] Píngshí Rúdǐ (d. 1357); co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0326]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
