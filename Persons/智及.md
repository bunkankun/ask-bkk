# Zhì-jí 智及

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *zì* Yǐ-zhōng 以中, 別號 Xī-lù 西麓, *hào* Yú-ān 愚菴; imperial title (from the Yuán Dì-shī) Míng-biàn Zhèng-zōng Guǎng-huì chánshī 明辨正宗廣慧禪師. 53rd *liè-zǔ* of Jìng-shān. Native of Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu), lay surname Gù 顧. 1311 – 3 October 1378 (lunar 洪武 11.9.4), *shì-shòu* 68. Dharma-heir of [[行端]] Yuán-sǒu Xíng-duān (1255–1341); co-generational with [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí, [[祖銘]] Gǔ-dǐng Zǔ-míng, [[曇噩]] Mèng-táng Tán-è, and [[法林]] Zhú-quán Fǎ-lín.

Entered religion at the Qióng-lóng Hǎi-yún-yuàn 穹窿海雲院 in his childhood; studied *Huáyán* under Xián-shǒu 賢首 (hearing lectures on the *Dharma-dhātu Contemplation* 法界觀). First visited Xiào-yǐn Dà-xīn 笑隱大訢 at Jiàn-yè; attained a first opening on seeing autumn leaves fall onto the courtyard. Went to Jìng-shān, met Xíng-duān, presented his attainment, and received *yìn-kě*.

Abbacies in sequence: Chāng-guó Lóng-jiào 昌國隆教 (from Zhì-zhèng 2 / 1342), Pǔ-cí 普慈, Hángzhōu Jìng-cí Bào-ēn 淨慈報恩 (from 1358 on invitation of Chéng-xiàng Dá-shī Tiě-mù-ěr 達失帖木爾), then two terms at Jìng-shān. In 1375 he returned to his home temple Hǎi-yún; died there three years later.

Named dharma-heirs: [[觀通]] Ruì-guāng Xī-yuǎn Guān-tōng 瑞光希遠觀通, [[希顏]] Tiān-tóng Yòng-yú Xī-yán 天童用愚希顏, [[忻悟]] Líng-yǐn Kōng-sǒu Xīn-wù 靈隱空叟忻悟, [[寶盈]] Xùn-zhōng Bǎo-yíng 巽中寶盈, **Dú-ān Dào-yǎn** 獨庵道衍 (aka Yáo Guǎng-xiào 姚廣孝, 1335–1418 — key strategist of the Yǒng-lè usurpation), Hǔ-qiū Nán-chēn Zhì-bǎo 虎丘南琛智寶.

Own ten-juan *yǔlù* survives as [[KR6q0354]] (X71 n1421), with preface by Sòng Lián 宋濂 dated Hóng-wǔ 8 / 1375.
