Sūn Yuánmíng 孫元明 was a mid-thirteenth-century figure in the Jiāngnán Zhōngxiào 忠孝 / Jìngmíng 淨明 Daoist circle surrounding the Yǒngwù dàoyuàn 勇悟道院. He is known solely through his 1247 (*dīngwèi* 丁未) postface to DZ 448 *Xīshān Xǔ zhēnjūn bāshíwǔ huà lù* [[KR5b0132]]. In that postface he reports receiving a premonitory dream, after which a member of the circle, Jiǎ Shǒuchéng 賈守澄, transmitted the edition of the work to him. No further biographical details survive.
