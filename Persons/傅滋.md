Mid-Míng (*Hóng-zhì* / *Zhèng-dé*-era) physician of Yì-wū 義烏 (Jīn-huá prefecture, Zhè-jiāng); disciple of Yú Tuán 虞摶 (1438 – c. 1517) in the post-Dān-xī Yì-wū school. His principal extant work is [[KR3er126]] *Xīn-kān yī-xué jí-chéng* 新刊醫學集成 (*editio princeps* *Zhèng-dé* 11 / 1516), one of the principal early-Míng Dān-xī-school general clinical compendia, distinguished by its systematic eightfold *biǎo-lǐ xū-shí yīn-yáng hán-rè* organisation of *Shāng-hán* patterns — the textual antecedent of the later canonical *bā-gāng biàn-zhèng* 八綱辨證 doctrine.
