Mid-Míng (*Hóngzhì* / *Zhèngdé*-era) physician of Yìwū 義烏 (Jīnhuá prefecture, Zhèjiāng); disciple of Yú Tuán 虞摶 (1438 – c. 1517) in the post-Dān-xī Yìwū school. His principal extant work is [[KR3er126]] *Xīnkān yīxué jíchéng* 新刊醫學集成 (*editio princeps* *Zhèngdé* 11 / 1516), one of the principal early-Míng Dān-xī-school general clinical compendia, distinguished by its systematic eightfold *biǎolǐ xūshí yīnyáng hánrè* organisation of *Shānghán* patterns — the textual antecedent of the later canonical *bāgāng biànzhèng* 八綱辨證 doctrine.
