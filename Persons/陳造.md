Chén Zào 陳造 (1133–1203, *zì* Tángqīng 唐卿, *hào* Jiānghú zhǎngwēng 江湖長翁), of *Gāoyóu* 高郵 in modern Jiāngsū. *Jìnshì* of *Chúnxī* 2 (1175). Held office to *Huáihǎi tíjǔ*. Substantial poetic-correspondence with three of the four great Southern-Sòng poets ([[范成大]], Yóu Mào 尤袤, and [[楊萬里]]). His self-cognomen *Jiānghú zhǎngwēng* establishes his identity as a senior figure of the *Jiānghú* cultural complex centered on lower *Yángzǐ* / *Huáihǎi* — a different, earlier constellation than the formal late-Sòng *Jiānghúpài* of [[戴復古]].
