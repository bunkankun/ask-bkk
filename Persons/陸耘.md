**Lù Yùn** 陸耘 (*hào* Shēngzhōu 生洲), early-Qīng Sūzhōu physician, of a slightly-later generation than [[凌新甫]] / [[葉啟東]]; listed in [[唐大烈]] Táng Dàliè's preface to [[KR3eq017]] *Wúyī huìjiǎng*.
