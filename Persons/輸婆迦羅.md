**輸婆迦羅** (Shū-pó-jiā-luó) is the direct phonetic transliteration of *Śubhakarasiṃha*; the same translator is canonically known as **善無畏** (Shàn Wúwèi, 637–735) — see [[善無畏]] for the full biography. The orthography 輸婆迦羅 appears in the colophon of [[KR6j0379]] (T1158, *Dìzàng pú-sà yí-guǐ*); 善無畏 is the Chinese semantic-paraphrase form ("Good–Without–Fear" = *Śubha-akara* + *siṃha*) used in most other colophons.

Source: DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001342 (where 輸婆迦羅 is listed as an alternative *persName* for the canonical 善無畏 entry).
