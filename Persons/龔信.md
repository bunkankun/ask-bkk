**Gōng Xìn** 龔信 (*zì* Ruìzhī 瑞之, *hào* Xīyuán 西園, mid-Míng), hereditary physician of Jīnxī 金溪 (Fǔzhōu 撫州, Jiāngxī), and father of the much better-known [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián (Yúnlín). Gōng Xìn served at the Imperial Academy of Medicine (*Tàiyī yuàn* 太醫院) and was the originator of the family clinical doctrine that his son continued. His principal work is the *Gǔjīn yījiàn* 古今醫鑑 ([[KR3er037]] / [[KR3er038]]), completed by him in *Wànlì* 4 (1576) and brought to print in *Wànlì* 17 (1589) by his son with substantial editorial expansion; the two-recension survival of the work (eight *juǎn chūkān* / sixteen *juǎn* expanded) preserves a rare witness to the father-son textual evolution.
