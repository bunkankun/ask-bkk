**Gōng Xìn** 龔信 (*zì* Ruì-zhī 瑞之, *hào* Xī-yuán 西園, mid-Míng), hereditary physician of Jīn-xī 金溪 (Fǔ-zhōu 撫州, Jiāngxī), and father of the much better-known [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíng-xián (Yún-lín). Gōng Xìn served at the Imperial Academy of Medicine (*Tài-yī yuàn* 太醫院) and was the originator of the family clinical doctrine that his son continued. His principal work is the *Gǔ-jīn yī-jiàn* 古今醫鑑 ([[KR3er037]] / [[KR3er038]]), completed by him in *Wàn-lì* 4 (1576) and brought to print in *Wàn-lì* 17 (1589) by his son with substantial editorial expansion; the two-recension survival of the work (eight *juǎn chū-kān* / sixteen *juǎn* expanded) preserves a rare witness to the father-son textual evolution. No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
