Shěn Lǐlóng 沈李龍 (*zì* Lóngyù 龍御, *hào* Yīshān shānrén 易山山人), *fl.* late 17th c. (Kāngxī era), native of Sūzhōu 蘇州 (Jiāngsū). Private scholar-physician in the Sūzhōu commercial-publishing networks. Author of the *Shíwù běncǎo huì zuǎn* 食物本草會纂 ([[KR3ec037]]), the principal Qīng dietetic pharmacopoeia, completed in 1689 and printed in 1691.
