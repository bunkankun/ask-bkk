**Luó Màodēng** 羅懋登 (fl. late 16th – early 17th century) is a Míng vernacular fiction author known almost exclusively through his single major work, the *Sānbǎo Tàijiān Xīyáng Jì Tōngsú Yǎnyì* 三寶太監西洋記通俗演義 [[KR4k0062]], first published in 1597 (Wànlì 25th year). Wilkinson (§54.4.2) identifies him as a "16th/17th century" author and describes the novel as "a late Ming novel based on the exploits of Zhèng Hé." No independent biographical details are recoverable; his native place, examination history (if any), and death date are unknown. CBDB (id 691011) records him by name without further data.
