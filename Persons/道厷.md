# Dàoshū 道厷

Liáo-dynasty Buddhist monk; lifedates not preserved. Author of the *Xiǎnmì yuántōng chéngfó xīnyào jí* 顯密圓通成佛心要集 ([[KR6j0019]]), the master synthesis text of *Cuṇḍā* practice that articulates the *xiǎnxiū / mìxiū* 顯修/密修 (exoteric / esoteric cultivation) framework. The work became the foundational reference for the subsequent *Cuṇḍā* devotional literature of the Yuán, Míng, and Qīng.
