Fǎtōng 法通 (DILA-standardized as **Tōngfǎ** 通法; the catalog meta of [[KR6q0462]] uses the reversed-character form *法通*), *hào* **Bùhuì** 不會 ("Not-Knowing"). Born **天啟元年辛酉 10/22 辰時** = **1621-12-04 Gregorian**; native of **Nánchōng** 南充 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊. LínjìYángqí Yáng-qí-school dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666) at **Shuāngguìsì** 雙桂寺.

**Formation.** At 16 secretly fled home to **Péngxī Zhìlínsì** 蓬溪智林寺 to take up monastic life; returned when his mother nearly starved on his absence. Met **Tiānyǐn dàshī** 天隱大師 who set him on the *wànfǎ guīyī yī guī héchù* 萬法歸一一歸何處 *gōngàn*. After both parents' deaths, formally tonsured at **age 26** (= **1646**) under **Xīngchū fǎshī** 惺初法師. Left Sìchuān for **Hànshàng** 漢上 (Húběi) and a four-year *shānjū* under **Shìyě Jié héshàng** 侍埜詰和尚, where he received full precepts. Pilgrimage circuit: **Jìngshān Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 徑山費隱通容; **Tiānjiè Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] 天界覺浪道盛; **Tiāntóng Mùchén Dàomín** [[道忞]] 天童木陳道忞. Final transmission from **Shuāngguì Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] in c. **1666**.

**Bùhuì phase.** Age 45 (**1666**, coinciding with Pòshān's death-year): *nì jì Shèjiāng Qīngguǒ Chányuàn* 匿跡射江清果禪院 — retreated to **Qīngguǒ Chányuàn** 清果禪院 in **Shèjiāng** 射江 (Sìchuān). The [[KR6q0462]] *kāitáng shū* 開堂疏 confirms this Qīngguǒ residence with two preceding *shū* invitation-petitions: the first by lay-officials and *xiāngshēn* dated **康熙十年十月初一日 = 1671-11-02 Gregorian**; the second by lay-Chán *fǎdìzǐ* circles. Death-date and *shìshòu* not located.

**Work.** [[KR6q0462]] 《不會禪師語錄》 10 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J32 B276, compiled by *fǎsì* **Xìnglíng** [[性靈]] *děng*. **Heirs**: **Xìnglíng** 性靈 (compiler), **Tōngdā** 通耷, **Cíkūn Fǎyù** 慈昆法玉 (named in the *fùfǎ* of j. 7), **Qìngfǎ** 慶法, et al.
