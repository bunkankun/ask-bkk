Fǎ-tōng 法通 (DILA-standardized as **Tōng-fǎ** 通法; the catalog meta of [[KR6q0462]] uses the reversed-character form *法通*), *hào* **Bù-huì** 不會 ("Not-Knowing"). Born **天啟元年辛酉 10/22 辰時** = **1621-12-04 Gregorian**; native of **Nán-chōng** 南充 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Línjì-Yángqí Yáng-qí-school dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666) at **Shuāng-guì-sì** 雙桂寺.

**Formation.** At 16 secretly fled home to **Péng-xī Zhì-lín-sì** 蓬溪智林寺 to take up monastic life; returned when his mother nearly starved on his absence. Met **Tiān-yǐn dà-shī** 天隱大師 who set him on the *wàn-fǎ guī-yī yī guī hé-chù* 萬法歸一一歸何處 *gōng-àn*. After both parents' deaths, formally tonsured at **age 26** (= **1646**) under **Xīng-chū fǎ-shī** 惺初法師. Left Sì-chuān for **Hàn-shàng** 漢上 (Húběi) and a four-year *shān-jū* under **Shì-yě Jié hé-shàng** 侍埜詰和尚, where he received full precepts. Pilgrimage circuit: **Jìng-shān Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 徑山費隱通容; **Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] 天界覺浪道盛; **Tiān-tóng Mù-chén Dào-mín** [[道忞]] 天童木陳道忞. Final transmission from **Shuāng-guì Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] in c. **1666**.

**Bù-huì phase.** Age 45 (**1666**, coinciding with Pòshān's death-year): *nì jì Shè-jiāng Qīng-guǒ Chán-yuàn* 匿跡射江清果禪院 — retreated to **Qīng-guǒ Chán-yuàn** 清果禪院 in **Shè-jiāng** 射江 (Sì-chuān). The KR6q0462 *kāi-táng shū* 開堂疏 confirms this Qīng-guǒ residence with two preceding *shū* invitation-petitions: the first by lay-officials and *xiāng-shēn* dated **康熙十年十月初一日 = 1671-11-02 Gregorian**; the second by lay-Chán *fǎ-dì-zǐ* circles. Death-date and *shì-shòu* not located.

**Work.** [[KR6q0462]] 《不會禪師語錄》 10 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J32 B276, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-líng** [[性靈]] *děng*. **Heirs**: **Xìng-líng** 性靈 (compiler), **Tōng-dā** 通耷, **Cí-kūn Fǎ-yù** 慈昆法玉 (named in the *fù-fǎ* of j. 7), **Qìng-fǎ** 慶法, et al.
