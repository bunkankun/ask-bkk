Qīnglián Shì Zhǔrén 青蓮室主人 ("Master of the Blue Lotus Chamber"; fl. c. 17th–18th century) is the pseudonym of the author of *Hòu Shuǐhǔ Zhuàn* 後水滸傳, a 45-chapter sequel to *Shuǐhǔ Zhuàn* 水滸傳. The work was published by the "Tiānhuā Cáng" 天花藏 imprint, a well-known commercial publisher of vernacular fiction in the Qīng dynasty. The author's real identity is unknown; no CBDB entry has been located.
