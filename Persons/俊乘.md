**Shunjō** 俊乘 (fl. mid-12th c.) — Japanese monk known from a colophon dated 永曆元年 (1160) recording his transcription of the *Móhē Pílúzhēnà rúlái dìnghuì jūnděng rù sānmèiyēshēn shuāngshēn Dàshèng Huānxǐtiān púsà xiūxíng mìmì fǎ yíguǐ* ([[KR6j0502]], T21n1271) from a Tō-ji 東寺 manuscript.
