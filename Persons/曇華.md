# Tánhuá 曇華

Yìng'ān Tánhuá 應庵曇華 (1103–1163), Southern-Sòng Yángqí-line Línjì master, dharma-heir of Yuánwù Kèqín's heir Hǔqiū Shàolóng; teacher of Mì'ān Xiánjié, under whom Mì'ān had his sudden awakening as recorded in the *Mì'ān héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0064]]).
