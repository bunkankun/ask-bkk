Modern Chinese Buddhist scholar/monk who edited the *Huá-yán lüè-shū juǎn dì-yī* 華嚴略疏卷第一 [[KR6v0087]] — a Northern-Zhōu period (557–574) Dūnhuáng *Huá-yán* commentary fragment, possibly by *Huì-guāng* 慧光 (467–537) of Northern Qí or *Sēng-měng* 僧猛 (508–590) of Northern Zhōu — for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 8.
