Cuī Bào 崔豹 (Western Jìn, fl. *c.* 290–306; *zì* Zhèng Xióng 正熊 by his own self-naming as preserved in the *Gǔjīn zhù* superscription, *Zhèng Néng* 正能 according to Liú Xiàobiāo 劉孝標's *Shìshuō xīnyǔ zhù* 世說新語注). The Sòng-era postface to *Gǔjīn zhù* by Lǐ Tāo 李燾 (1115–1184) identifies him, on the basis of family-line investigation, as a descendant of the Eastern-Hàn Cuī clan of Ān-píng 安平 — most plausibly of the line of Cuī Yuàn 崔瑗 (78–143) and Cuī Shí 崔寔 (*c.* 103–170). Native place therefore most plausibly Ān-píng (Hé-běi). Career attested only by his office of *Tài-fù chéng* 太傅丞 (Assistant to the Grand Tutor) under Jìn Huì-dì 晉惠帝 (r. 290–306), recorded in the superscription of *Gǔjīn zhù* itself ("晉太傅丞崔豹字正熊"). The *Jìn shū* 晉書 contains no biography. Author of [[KR3j0025]] *Gǔjīn zhù* 古今注, the principal early *míngwù* 名物 / antiquarian compendium of the Western Jìn, transmitted continuously since the Sòng (and entangled in the bibliographical record with the Hòu-Táng *Zhōnghuá gǔjīn zhù* 中華古今注 of Mǎ Gǎo 馬縞, on which see [[KR3j0025]]).
