Mínghuì 明慧. LínjìYángqí *fǎsì* of **Jiàntáng Yī** [[一|Jiàntáng Yī]] of **Chánglè chánsì** 常樂禪寺. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Míngmǎn** [[明滿]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0594]] 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B486.

- - - - -

Several other monks bear the name **Mínghuì** 明慧, including the LiúSòng monk at **Báimǎsì** 白馬寺 in Beijing (DILA A003471, 5th-century, famous for *fànbài* and scripture-recitation), and the late-Míng monk **Wújì Mínghuì** 無際明慧 (DILA A003566) of **Háiníng** 海寧, twenty-seventh-generation Línjì-lineage holder at **Huìyīnsì** 惠因寺. Neither is the present co-recorder.
