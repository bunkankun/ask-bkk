Míng-huì 明慧. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Jiàn-táng Yī** [[一|Jiàn-táng Yī]] of **Cháng-lè chán-sì** 常樂禪寺. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Míng-mǎn** [[明滿]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0594]] 《鑑堂一禪師語錄》 1 juan, J40 B486.

- - - - -

Several other monks bear the name **Míng-huì** 明慧, including the Liú-Sòng monk at **Bái-mǎ-sì** 白馬寺 in Beijing (DILA A003471, 5th-century, famous for *fàn-bài* and scripture-recitation), and the late-Míng monk **Wú-jì Míng-huì** 無際明慧 (DILA A003566) of **Hái-níng** 海寧, twenty-seventh-generation Línjì-lineage holder at **Huì-yīn-sì** 惠因寺. Neither is the present co-recorder.
