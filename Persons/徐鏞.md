**Xú Yōng** 徐鏞 (Qīng physician, *zì* and lifedates unrecovered), mid-Qīng author of the *Yīxué jǔyào* 醫學舉要 ([[KR3er006]]). The work cites both [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì's *Línzhèng zhǐnán yīàn* (1764) and [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn's *Yīxué yuánliú lùn* (1757), bracketing Xú Yōng's medical practice and the composition of his work to the second quarter of the nineteenth century. CBDB returns multiple homonymous 徐鏞 entries; CBDB 61782 (Qīng, *c_index_year* 1779) is the most plausible candidate, though the work's substantive evidence (developed mid-late Qīng *Shānghán*-revisionist programme drawing on Chéng Jiāoqiàn and Kē Yùnbó) suggests a later flourishing — probably the 1830s–1860s. Without further documentary evidence Xú Yōng remains undatable with precision.
