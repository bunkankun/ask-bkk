# Yuètíng Míngdé 月亭明得

Late-Míng Línjì-Chán monk (1531 – 1588) of the **Qiānsōng** 千松 sub-school associated with Mt. Tài. Dharma-teacher of [[真界]] Yuètáng Zhēnjiè, compiler of the *Lèngyán jīng zuǎnzhù* ([[KR6j0708]]).
