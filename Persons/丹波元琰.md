**Tamba no Motoyoshi** 丹波元琰 (*Dānbō Yuányǎn*, late Edo period, 江戶), grandson of [[丹波元簡]] Tamba no Mototane and member of the bakufu's hereditary medical lineage at the *Igaku-kan* 醫學館, contributor to the family's continuing program of philological collation on the Chinese medical canon.
