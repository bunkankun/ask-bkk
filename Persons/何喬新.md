Hé Qiáoxīn 何喬新 (1427–1502), *zì* **Tiānfǔ** 天甫, *hào* **Jiāoqiū** 椒邱. Native of **Guǎngchāng** 廣昌 (Jíān, Jiāngxī). Successive offices in inner-and-outer mostly with administrative achievement; suppressed by the late-Chéng-huà **Wàn Ān** 萬安 / **Liú Jí** 劉吉 cabinet faction because of his vigour-and-integrity, never granted senior office. Reached *Xíngbù shàngshū* 刑部尚書 in late Hóngzhì. Famous for his personal library — 30,000+ *zhì* (volumes), all personally collated. *Míng shǐ* j. 183. Surviving works are [[KR4e0117]] *Jiāoqiū wénjí* (34 *juǎn*; edited by **Yú Yíng** 余瑩 of **Wùyuán** 婺源); also the *Zhōulǐ jízhù* 周禮集註 separately catalogued. CBDB id 34522 (1427–1502).
