# Déguāng 德光

Southern-Sòng Línjì-school Chán master, dharma-heir of Dàhuì [[宗杲]] Zōnggǎo; styled Fózhào 佛照 by imperial bestowal. Abbot at Yùwáng shān 育王山 and Jìngshān 徑山. Famously summoned by emperor Xiàozōng for repeated audiences and dharma-talks at court, an exchange anthologised in the *Dìwáng wèndào lù* selection of *Xiānjué zōngshèng* ([[KR6q0052]]).
