Gù Kuíguāng 顧奎光 (1719–1764), zì Xīngwǔ 星五, native of Wúxī 無錫 (Cháng-zhōu 常州 prefecture, Jiāngsū). Held office as magistrate of Lú-xī 瀘溪 county. He is the author of *Chūnqiū suí bǐ* 春秋隨筆 ([[KR1e0121]]) and the larger miscellany *Rán yí lù* 然疑錄, both *Chūnqiū*-focused; the *Sìkù* tiyao notes that the *Chūnqiū* essays in the two works overlap, and judges *Suí bǐ* to contain the cream of his thinking. Catalog dates 1719–1764 confirmed by CBDB.
