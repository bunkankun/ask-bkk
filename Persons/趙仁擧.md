Zhào Rén-jǔ 趙仁擧, biography unrecorded, is known only as the early-Yuán annotator of **Fán Zōng-shī**'s [[樊宗師]] *Jiàng-shǒu jū-yuán-chí jì* (= [[KR4c0063]]). His work supplied the base commentary later supplemented by Wú Shī-dào [[吳師道]] (1313, with 22 additions and 60 corrections) and Xǔ Qiān [[許謙]] (1320, with 41 further additions). Zhào's annotation responded to a specific bibliographic gap: the two Táng commentaries on Fán Zōng-shī's piece by Wáng Shèng 王晟 and Liú Chén 劉忱 — recorded by Lǐ Zhào in his *Guó-shǐ bǔ* — were already lost by the early Yuán.

No other works recorded under his name; CBDB has no matching entry. The *Sì-kù* editors note only that he was clearly active before Wú Shī-dào's *Huáng-qìng* 2 (1313) supplement.
