Zhào Rénjǔ 趙仁擧, biography unrecorded, is known only as the early-Yuán annotator of **Fán Zōngshī**'s [[樊宗師]] *Jiàngshǒu jūyuánchí jì* (= [[KR4c0063]]). His work supplied the base commentary later supplemented by Wú Shīdào [[吳師道]] (1313, with 22 additions and 60 corrections) and Xǔ Qiān [[許謙]] (1320, with 41 further additions). Zhào's annotation responded to a specific bibliographic gap: the two Táng commentaries on Fán Zōngshī's piece by Wáng Shèng 王晟 and Liú Chén 劉忱 — recorded by Lǐ Zhào in his *Guóshǐ bǔ* — were already lost by the early Yuán.

No other works recorded under his name; CBDB has no matching entry. The *Sìkù* editors note only that he was clearly active before Wú Shīdào's *Huángqìng* 2 (1313) supplement.
