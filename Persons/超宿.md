# Chāosù 超宿 (Yírán Chāosù 怡然超宿)

Mid-seventeenth-century female Línjì Chán master (nun 尼), dharma-heir of [[行剛|Qíyuán Xínggāng]] 祇園行剛 (1597–1654) in the **Fúshī Chányuàn** 伏獅禪院 female-lineage. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Chāosù** 超宿 (超-generation, the 34th-generation Línjì tier); *hào* **Yírán** 怡然 ("Harmonious-Thus"). Listed second among Xínggāng's seven named dharma-heirs in the *xíngzhuàng* close. Co-compiler of [[KR6q0428]] 《伏獅祇園禪師語錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J28 B210).

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered. No independent works survive under her name.

Sources: [[KR6q0428]] juan-head signature line; juan 2 *xíngzhuàng*.
