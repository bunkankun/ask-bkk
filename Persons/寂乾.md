Jìqián 寂乾. Personal attendant of **Tiānyì Jùxiáng** [[巨翔]] (1663–1695) at **Dà-Wéishān Mìyìnsì** 大溈山密印禪寺. Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*lù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0589]] 《天翼翔禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B477.

- - - - -

Several other Buddhist monks also bear the name **Jìqián** 寂乾, including **Yuánchū Jìqián** 元初寂乾 (DILA A008066), Wàn-lì-era abbot of Pǔtuóshān, founder of **Xīzīān** 西資庵; and **Zhèngwén Jìqián** 正聞寂乾 (DILA A019190), of Huīzhōufǔ. Neither is the present recorder.
