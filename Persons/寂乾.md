Jì-qián 寂乾. Personal attendant of **Tiān-yì Jù-xiáng** [[巨翔]] (1663–1695) at **Dà-Wéi-shān Mì-yìn-sì** 大溈山密印禪寺. Lifedates unknown. Recorder (*lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0589]] 《天翼翔禪師語錄》 2 juan, J40 B477.

- - - - -

Several other Buddhist monks also bear the name **Jì-qián** 寂乾, including **Yuán-chū Jì-qián** 元初寂乾 (DILA A008066), Wàn-lì-era abbot of Pǔtuó-shān, founder of **Xī-zī-ān** 西資庵; and **Zhèng-wén Jì-qián** 正聞寂乾 (DILA A019190), of Huī-zhōu-fǔ. Neither is the present recorder.
