Lǐ Mù 李穆 (928–984; CBDB id 8080), *zì* Mèngyōng 孟雍, native of Zhèngzhōu 鄭州, a Northern-Sòng *jìnshì* (954) and *Hànlín xuéshì* under Sòng Tàizōng. He served as *Zhī zhìgào* 知制誥 at the time of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* commission and was among the fourteen named compilers. His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 263.
