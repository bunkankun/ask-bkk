Lǐ Mù 李穆 (928–984; CBDB id 8080), *zì* Mèng-yōng 孟雍, native of Zhèng-zhōu 鄭州, a Northern-Sòng *jìnshì* (954) and *Hàn-lín xuéshì* under Sòng Tài-zōng. He served as *Zhī zhì-gào* 知制誥 at the time of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* commission and was among the fourteen named compilers. His biography is in *Sòng shǐ* 263.
