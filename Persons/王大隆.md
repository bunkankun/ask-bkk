Wáng Dàlóng 王大隆 (1901–1966), *zì* Xīnfū 欣夫, leading Republican / early-PRC bibliographic philologist and SBCK series editor. Native of Wú-jiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). Disciple of the late-Qīng-Republican literatus Wú Méi 吳梅 in *cí*-lyric studies, and senior bibliographer at the Commercial Press's Sì-bù cóng-kān 四部叢刊 publishing program in Shanghai through the 1920s-1940s.

Wáng's SBCK editorial contributions include the *jí-wài shī-wén* (recovery of poems and prose not in the original recension) supplements to multiple late-Míng / early-Qīng *bié-jí* recensions, including the *Jū-yì-táng jí* 居易堂集 of [[徐枋]] ([[KR4f0021]]) where Wáng supplied the supplementary apparatus drawn from later Qīng-Republican manuscript witnesses of Xú's writings that had circulated outside the original Pān-Lěi recension. Wáng's most influential single work is the *Wén-xiàn xué jiǎng-yì* 文獻學講義 — the foundational Republican-era textbook of Chinese bibliographical-philological method, posthumously published in 1986.
