**Xǔ Guózhēn** 許國禎 (*zì* **Jìnzhī** 進之, *fl.* mid-13th c.), of Cízhōu 磁州 (modern Cíxiàn 磁縣, Héběi). Senior Yuán court physician. *Yuán shǐ* j. 168 records that Xǔ was a Han-Chinese physician who served Khubilai Khan and his consort Chabi 察必 as personal physician; he is credited with treating Empress Chabi's serious illness, after which Khubilai conferred on him the directorship of the imperial medical office. Held the office of *Tài-yī tí-diǎn* 太醫提點 (Director of the Imperial Medical Office) and was honoured with the senior rank *Róng-lù dàifū* 榮祿大夫.

Co-compiler of the *Yù-yào-yuàn fāng* 御藥院方 ([[KR3ed025]]) — the 1267 imperial-pharmacy formulary of the early Yuán, a textual consolidation of Sòng *Júfāng*, Jurchen Jin *Yù-yào-yuàn* (1242 *rén-yín* edition), and newly-collected Mongol-Chinese recipes. He is not in CBDB. The principal biographical source is *Yuán shǐ* j. 168, where he is described as a confidant of Khubilai and as treating Mongol royal-family members successfully across his career.
