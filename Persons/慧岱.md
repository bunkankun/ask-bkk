Huìdài 慧岱, *hào* **Zhōngshān** 中山. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎzhù Hǎidòng** [[海棟]] (35th generation Línjì). Lifedates unknown, active late 17th century. One of twelve dharma-heirs of Hǎidòng formally invested with verse-bestowal (*liánfāngjì* 聯芳偈) — Huìdài served as *shūjì* 書記 (clerk) at Pǔfēngsì. Co-compiler of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 — *lù* (transcription), printed in Jiāxīng Canon J37 B402 in 1691.
