Huì-dài 慧岱, *hào* **Zhōng-shān** 中山. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fǎ-zhù Hǎi-dòng** [[海棟]] (35th generation Línjì). Lifedates unknown, active late 17th century. One of twelve dharma-heirs of Hǎi-dòng formally invested with verse-bestowal (*lián-fāng-jì* 聯芳偈) — Huì-dài served as *shū-jì* 書記 (clerk) at Pǔ-fēng-sì. Co-compiler of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0525]] 《浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄》 — *lù* (transcription), printed in Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B402 in 1691.
