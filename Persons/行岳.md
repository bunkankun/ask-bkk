Xíngyuè 行岳, *zì* **Qīyún** 棲雲 ("Cloud-Dwelling"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yùlín Tōngxiù** [[通琇]] (1614–1675). Born **1614**; died **康熙五年十月廿七日** = **1666-11-23**, age 53. Lead compiler (*biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0605]] 《大覺普濟能仁琇國師語錄》 7 juan, Qiánlóng-canon No. 1641. Note that Xíngyuè predeceased his master Tōngxiù by nearly a decade — meaning the *biān*-work was done during the master's lifetime and the canonical edition includes posthumous additions by other heirs.

**Sources.** 《正源略集》.
