# Shírén 實仁

Southern-Sòng Chán attendant-disciple (*shìzhě ménrén* 侍者門人) of Huáihǎi Yuánzhào 淮海原肇 (1189–1275). Lead-editor of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0304]]. Lifedates unrecorded.
