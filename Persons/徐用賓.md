Xú Yòngbīn 徐用賓 (fl. late 10th c.), Northern-Sòng *Guānglù sì chéng* 光禄寺丞 at the time of the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* commission of 977, named among the fourteen compilers. No independent biography survives in the *Sòng shǐ*.
