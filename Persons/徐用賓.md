Xú Yòng-bīn 徐用賓 (fl. late 10th c.), Northern-Sòng *Guāng-lù sì chéng* 光禄寺丞 at the time of the *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* commission of 977, named among the fourteen compilers. No independent biography survives in the *Sòng shǐ*.
