**Takashima Hisanuki** 高島久貫 (Edo-period / mid-19th-century Japanese *kanpō* 漢方 physician). Author of *Xièyì xīnlùn* / *Sha-eki shinron* 瀉疫新論 ([[KR3eg045]]), a *kanpō* monograph on **purging-epidemic** (i.e. cholera-like diarrhoeal epidemic disease). The work was prompted by the 19th-century cholera pandemics that reached Japan in 1822 (Bunsei 5) and again in 1858 (Ansei 5). It was later transmitted to China and entered the *Huáng Hàn yīxué cóngshū* 皇漢醫學叢書 channel. No detailed biographical material located.
