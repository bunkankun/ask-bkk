# 定慧

Dìnghuì (no firm dates), a Tang-period monk of Mt Sōngshān 嵩山 Huìshàn-monastery credited in the anonymous *Fóshuō Léngqié jīng chánmén xītán zhāng* ([[KR6i0335]]) with translating the *xītán-zhāng* 悉談章 (siddham primer) into Chinese phonological verse aligned with Kumārajīva's *lǔ-liú* (魯留) phonology. The figure may be conflated or pseudonymous; he features in the early-Chán *Léngqié*-school pedagogical tradition.
