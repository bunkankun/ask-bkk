Zhèng Xuàn 鄭炫 (fl. Wànlì), great-grandson of **Zhèng Yuè** ([[鄭岳]]) of Pútián. Editor of the *Shānzhāi wénjí* in 24 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0151]]), which he recovered and re-printed in Wànlì from the fire-damaged remains of his ancestor's writings.
