Zhèng Xuàn 鄭炫 (fl. Wàn-lì), great-grandson of **Zhèng Yuè** ([[鄭岳]]) of Pú-tián. Editor of the *Shān-zhāi wén-jí* in 24 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0151]]), which he recovered and re-printed in Wàn-lì from the fire-damaged remains of his ancestor's writings.
