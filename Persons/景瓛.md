# Jǐnghuán 景瓛

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Yíngzhōng 瑩中. Principal dharma-heir of [[梵琦]] Chǔshí Fànqí (1296–1370); co-editor of his master's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]), and the person who commissioned Sòng Lián 宋濂 to supply its preface. Abbot of Sūzhōu Wànshòu 蘇州萬壽. Per *Zēngjí Xùchuándēnglù* *juan* 5, he became Fànqí's student after having received the full precepts — a somewhat unusual trajectory — choosing Fànqí (at Xīzhāi / Tiānníng) over his earlier training. Lifedates unrecorded.
