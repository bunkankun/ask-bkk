# Jǐng-huán 景瓛

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán master, *hào* Yíng-zhōng 瑩中. Principal dharma-heir of [[梵琦]] Chǔ-shí Fàn-qí (1296–1370); co-editor of his master's twenty-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0353]]), and the person who commissioned Sòng Lián 宋濂 to supply its preface. Abbot of Sū-zhōu Wàn-shòu 蘇州萬壽. Per *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 5, he became Fàn-qí's student after having received the full precepts — a somewhat unusual trajectory — choosing Fàn-qí (at Xī-zhāi / Tiān-níng) over his earlier training. Lifedates unrecorded.
