# 賈得道 Jiǎ Dédào

**Jiǎ Dédào** 賈得道 (1907–1996), modern Chinese physician and historian of medicine at the Shānxī Academy of Traditional Chinese Medicine; author of *Zhōngguó yīxué shǐlüè* 中國醫學史略 (1979) and other influential surveys of the history of Chinese medicine.
