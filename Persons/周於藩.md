# Zhōu Yúfān 周於藩

Late-Qīng paediatric *tuīná* 推拿 practitioner; author of *Xiǎoér tuīná zhílù* 小兒推拿直錄, one of the foundational late-Qīng manuals of paediatric medical massage, frequently cited together with the *Tuīná dàiyào fù* of [[夏鼎]] Xià Dǐng's *Yòukē tiějìng* 幼科鐵鏡 ([[KR3ej011]]) and the *Xiǎoér tuīná guǎngyì* 小兒推拿廣意 of [[熊應雄]] Xióng Yīngxióng ([[KR3ej067]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
