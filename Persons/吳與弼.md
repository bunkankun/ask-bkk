Wú Yǔ-bì 吳與弼 (1391–1469), *zì* **Zǐ-fù** 子傅 (also written 子傳), *hào* **Kāng-zhāi** 康齋. Native of **Lín-chuān** 臨川 (Fǔ-zhōu, Jiāng-xī). The great mid-Míng *Lǐ-xué* teacher: in Tiān-shùn 1 (1457) summoned to the capital by the recommendation of **Zhōng-guó-gōng Shí Hēng** 石亨; appointed *zuǒ Chūn-fáng zuǒ yù-dé* 左春坊左諭德; declined to take office; the throne ordered envoys to escort him home. Foundational teacher of the **mid-Míng *Lǐ-xué* turn**: pupil **Chén Xiàn-zhāng** 陳獻章 ([[陳獻章]]) opened the **Bái-shā xué-pài** 白沙學派 — and through Chén's pupil's pupil-line eventually contributed to the emergence of **Wáng Yáng-míng**'s *Yáo-jiāng* school; pupil **Hú Jū-rén** 胡居仁 opened the **Yú-gàn xué-pài** 餘干學派 (the orthodox-Zhū wing). The *Sìkù* historiographic verdict: *the Míng dynasty's two [Lǐ-xué] schools' successive transmissions all came from Yǔ-bì's advocacy*. Doctrinal innovation: *jiān-cǎi Zhū Lù zhī cháng* (combined the strengths of **Zhū Xī** and **Lù Jiǔ-yuān**), with *kè-kǔ zì-lì* (rigorous self-effort). *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (*Rú-lín zhuàn*). Surviving works are [[KR4e0122]] *Kāng-zhāi jí* (12 *juǎn*; the Chóng-zhēn *rén-shēn* / 1632 printing by **Chén Wéi-xīn** 陳維新, *Jiāng-nán tí-xué fù-shǐ*). CBDB id 34529 (1391–1469).
