Wú Yǔbì 吳與弼 (1391–1469), *zì* **Zǐfù** 子傅 (also written 子傳), *hào* **Kāngzhāi** 康齋. Native of **Línchuān** 臨川 (Fǔzhōu, Jiāngxī). The great mid-Míng *Lǐxué* teacher: in Tiānshùn 1 (1457) summoned to the capital by the recommendation of **Zhōngguógōng Shí Hēng** 石亨; appointed *zuǒ Chūnfáng zuǒ yùdé* 左春坊左諭德; declined to take office; the throne ordered envoys to escort him home. Foundational teacher of the **mid-Míng *Lǐxué* turn**: pupil **Chén Xiànzhāng** 陳獻章 ([[陳獻章]]) opened the **Báishā xuépài** 白沙學派 — and through Chén's pupil's pupil-line eventually contributed to the emergence of **Wáng Yángmíng**'s *Yáojiāng* school; pupil **Hú Jūrén** 胡居仁 opened the **Yúgàn xuépài** 餘干學派 (the orthodox-Zhū wing). The *Sìkù* historiographic verdict: *the Míng dynasty's two [Lǐxué] schools' successive transmissions all came from Yǔbì's advocacy*. Doctrinal innovation: *jiāncǎi Zhū Lù zhī cháng* (combined the strengths of **Zhū Xī** and **Lù Jiǔyuān**), with *kèkǔ zìlì* (rigorous self-effort). *Míng shǐ* j. 282 (*Rúlín zhuàn*). Surviving works are [[KR4e0122]] *Kāngzhāi jí* (12 *juǎn*; the Chóngzhēn *rénshēn* / 1632 printing by **Chén Wéixīn** 陳維新, *Jiāngnán tíxué fùshǐ*). CBDB id 34529 (1391–1469).
