Tānmèng Dàorén 貪夢道人 ("The Dreamer-Glutton Daoist") is the pseudonym of the author of *Péng Gōng'àn* 彭公案 ([[KR4k0192]]), a 225-chapter gōng'àn/wǔxiá novel published in the late Qīng dynasty (likely 1890s). The real identity behind this pen name remains unidentified. The author has been loosely associated in bibliographic tradition with the circle around Shí Yùkūn 石玉崑, the storyteller credited with *Sān Xiá Wǔ Yì* / *Qī Xiá Wǔ Yì*, but this connection is unverified. No CBDB record has been found.
