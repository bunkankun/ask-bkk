**Cáo Bǐngzhāng** 曹炳章 (*zì* Chìdiàn 赤電, 1878 – 5 March 1956), Republican-period Chinese physician, bibliophile, and editor; native of Yín-xiàn 鄞縣 (i.e. Sì-míng 四明 = modern Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng), resident from age fourteen in Shào-xīng 紹興.

Studied pharmacology with his father and clinical medicine under Fāng Xiǎo-ān 方曉安; began clinical practice in **1902**. Co-founded the **Shào-xīng Medical Society** 紹興醫學會 with [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén in **1908** and was a chief editor of its journal *Shào-xīng yī yào xué bào* 紹興醫藥學報. Founded the **Hé-jì Pharmacy** 和濟藥局 in Shào-xīng in **1913**.

From **1935** onward served as chief editor of the great Republican medical anthology ***Zhōngguó yī xué dà chéng*** 中國醫學大成 — a 365-title collection of Chinese medical works from the Hàn through the Qīng, comprising over 1,000 fascicles, the largest single Republican-era compilation of Chinese medical literature. In **1952** he donated more than 3,400 volumes from his personal medical library to the state. Author and editor of dozens of monographs, of which the best known are:

- **辨舌指南** ([[KR3eb056]], 6 juan, preface 1920) — comprehensive tongue-diagnostic compendium synthesising the entire Qīng tradition with early-Republican Western anatomical-physiological framing.
- *Biàn shé xīn biān* 辨舌新編 — earlier monograph on tongue diagnosis serialised in the *Shào-xīng yī bào*.
- *Cáo shì yī yào lùn cóng* 曹氏醫藥論叢 — selected medical essays.
