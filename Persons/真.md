Zhēn 真 (full reference form **Qǐ-zōng Zhēn** 起宗真), *zì* **Qǐ-zōng** 起宗 ("Inception of the School"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of two teachers: **Wéi-shān Gǔ-méi Dìng-liè** [[定冽]] (cf. [[KR6q0576]]) and **Huá-shān Dìng-ān Dé-jī** 華山定菴德基律師. Born **明崇禎戊寅十月初二** = **1638-11-07** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 63 in **康熙三十九年** = **1700**. Native of **Zhēn-zhōu-sī** 真州司; lay surname **Hú** 胡.

**Formation.** At 11 his mother died and he resolved to leave the world, but his father raged and prevented him. **庚午** = **1690** (at age 53!) finally gave up his lay life, entered a hermitage, invited **Wéi-shān Gǔ-méi** to perform the tonsure on him at the *ān*. The same year went to **Huá-shān** 華山 for full ordination, then returned and received *fù-fǎ* from **Wéi-shān**. After Wéi-shān's death, returned to Huá-shān to thank for the precepts, and there received a second *fù-fǎ* from **Dìng-ān Dé-jī**.

**Abbacy.** **Zhēn-zhōu Huá-yán chán-sì** 真州華嚴禪寺 (in his hometown).

**Disciples.** **Lì-rú** 力如 (*jì-shì* 記室), **Lì-guǎng** [[力廣]] (compiler).

**Work.** [[KR6q0577]] 《華嚴起宗真禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B464, recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Lì-guǎng** [[力廣]] *děng lù*.

**Sources.** 《華嚴起宗真禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈起宗真禪師行實〉.
