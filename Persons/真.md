Zhēn 真 (full reference form **Qǐzōng Zhēn** 起宗真), *zì* **Qǐzōng** 起宗 ("Inception of the School"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of two teachers: **Wéishān Gǔméi Dìngliè** [[定冽]] (cf. [[KR6q0576]]) and **Huáshān Dìngān Déjī** 華山定菴德基律師. Born **明崇禎戊寅十月初二** = **1638-11-07** Gregorian; death year unknown — was 63 in **康熙三十九年** = **1700**. Native of **Zhēnzhōusī** 真州司; lay surname **Hú** 胡.

**Formation.** At 11 his mother died and he resolved to leave the world, but his father raged and prevented him. **庚午** = **1690** (at age 53!) finally gave up his lay life, entered a hermitage, invited **Wéishān Gǔméi** to perform the tonsure on him at the *ān*. The same year went to **Huáshān** 華山 for full ordination, then returned and received *fùfǎ* from **Wéishān**. After Wéishān's death, returned to Huáshān to thank for the precepts, and there received a second *fùfǎ* from **Dìngān Déjī**.

**Abbacy.** **Zhēnzhōu Huáyán chánsì** 真州華嚴禪寺 (in his hometown).

**Disciples.** **Lìrú** 力如 (*jìshì* 記室), **Lìguǎng** [[力廣]] (compiler).

**Work.** [[KR6q0577]] 《華嚴起宗真禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B464, recorded by *fǎsì* **Lìguǎng** [[力廣]] *děng lù*.

**Sources.** 《華嚴起宗真禪師語錄》 vol. 1 〈起宗真禪師行實〉.
