**Zhāng Zōngliáng** 張宗良 (*zì* Liúxiān 留仙, mid-Qīng), Sūzhōu (吳郡) physician specializing in laryngology (*hóukē* 喉科). No CBDB record; lifedates are not securely fixed. He was established as an experienced laryngology specialist by the late *Qiánlóng* period, when his fellow-Sūzhōu and *Yōngzhèng* 5 (1727) *zhuàngyuán* [[彭啟豐]] Péng Qǐfēng wrote the preface (1757) to his *Hóukē zhǐzhǎng* ([[KR3em024]]). The work — six *juǎn*, with the *liùwèi tāng* 六味湯 as base prescription — became the most influential of the Qīng laryngology monographs and the foundation of the so-called "Zhāng-clan" *hóukē* lineage.

A second work, [[KR3em026]] *Yānhóu mìjí* 咽喉秘集, is catalogued under the orthographic variant 張宋良; modern specialist scholarship treats this as a typographic slip for the present 張宗良 (the same individual). The variant is preserved in the [[KR3em026]] frontmatter and flagged there in prose.
