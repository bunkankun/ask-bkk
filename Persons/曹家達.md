Cáo Jiādá 曹家達 (better known as Cáo Yǐngfǔ 曹穎甫; 1866–1938) was a leading Republican-era Jiāngyīn 江陰 Shānghán-school physician and teacher; he contributed a preface to the *Fēngláogǔgé sìdà zhèngzhì* ([[KR3eh038]]) reconstruction project.
