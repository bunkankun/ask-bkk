Northern-Sòng official of the *Bīngbù yuánwài láng* 兵部員外郎 office. According to [[沈括]] Shěn Kuò's *Mèngxī bǐtán* 夢溪筆談, Qín Jiè requested instruction in the *xiāntiān* 先天 *Yì*-numerology from [[邵雍]] Shào Yōng but was refused — paired in this rebuff with [[鄭夬]] Zhèng Guài (whose subsequent theft of [[王天悅]] Wáng Tiānyuè's manuscript is the central narrative of [[邵伯溫]] Shào Bówēn's *Yìxué biànhuò* ([[KR1a0017]])).
