Wáng Xuětái 王雪苔 (1925–2008) was a historian of Chinese medicine and a director of the Zhōngguó Zhōngyī yánjiū yuàn 中國中醫研究院. He sponsored and supervised [[馬繼興]]'s philological vetting of the *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào* ([[KR3eh001]]) and is the author of the standard critical edition *Fǔxíng jué zàngfǔ yòngyào fǎyào jiàozhù kǎozhèng* 輔行訣臟腑用藥法要校注考證 (Beijing: Rénmín jūnyī chūbǎnshè, 2008).
