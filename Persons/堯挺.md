# Yáotǐng 堯挺

Late-Wànlì period lay-Buddhist scholar; full lay name **Shī Yáotǐng** 施堯挺. Native of **Mǐn** 閩 (Fújiàn). Lifedates not preserved; floruit Wànlì 40s (1610s).

Author of the **Zhǔntí xīnyào** 準提心要 ([[KR6j0751]], X59 no. 1078), a one-fascicle lay-Buddhist devotional handbook on the *Cuṇḍā* 準提 dhāraṇī practice. The work is signed *Mǐnzhōng Fó dìzǐ Shī Yáotǐng xūnmù bàizàn* 閩中佛弟子施堯挺薰沐拜讚 ("Buddhist disciple of Mǐn, Shī Yáotǐng, having ritually bathed and bowed in praise"). The principal preface is dated **Tiānyùn *rénzǐ* 壬子, *zhǎngzhì rì* 長至日 = winter solstice 1612** (i.e., the Wànlì 40 winter solstice). A supplementary editorial note records additional revisions in **Tiānyùn *guǐchǒu* 癸丑 = 1613**.

Yáotǐng's *Xīnyào* belongs to the cluster of late-Wànlì lay-Buddhist *Cuṇḍā* dhāraṇī manuals (with [[謝于教]]'s *Zhǔntí jìngyè* [[KR6j0750]]) that document the period's substantial Esoteric–Pure-Land devotional revival.
