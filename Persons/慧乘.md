# 慧乘
Huìchéng (555–630)

Sui-Táng dynasty monk who served as a *zhèngyì* (verifier of meaning) in [[波羅頗蜜多羅]] Prabhākaramitra's translation team for the *Bǎoxīng tuóluóní jīng* 寶星陀羅尼經 ([[KR6h0006]]) at Dàxīngshàn monastery in 629–630.
