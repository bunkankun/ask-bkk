Late-Yuán / early-Míng literatus of **Yíxīng** 宜興 (Chángzhōu). *Zì* **Xiàocháng** 孝常. **CBDB birth: 1322**, death unrecorded. Close friend of [[周砥]] Zhōu Dǐ and [[高棅]] Gāo Qǐ (the early-Míng Wúzhōng poet). During the *Zhāng Shìchéng* rebellions (1353–1355) he sheltered with Zhōu Dǐ at the Zhōu-family Jīngnán 荊南 estate in Yíxīng, and the two composed the **[[KR4h0091]]** *Jīngnán chànghé shījí* (1 *juǎn*).

After the Míng founding he served as **Jiànchāng tóngzhī** 建昌同知 (Assistant Prefect of Jiànchāng). He transmitted the hand-copied *chànghé* anthology to Gāo Qǐ, who passed it through **Lǚ Mǐn** 呂敏 — Lǚ wrote a postscript and **Xú Bēn** 徐賁 added a *tící* — eventually returning to the Mǎ family. The collection was first printed in the **Chénghuà period (1465–1487)** by the Yíxīng man **Lǐ Tíngzhī** 李廷芝, edited by **Lǐ Yìngzhēn** 李應禎 and **Zhāng Bì** 張弼.

His own *biéjí* does not survive; his verse is preserved only in this collection.
