Late-Yuán / early-Míng literatus of **Yí-xīng** 宜興 (Cháng-zhōu). *Zì* **Xiào-cháng** 孝常. **CBDB birth: 1322**, death unrecorded. Close friend of [[周砥]] Zhōu Dǐ and [[高棅]] Gāo Qǐ (the early-Míng Wú-zhōng poet). During the *Zhāng Shì-chéng* rebellions (1353–1355) he sheltered with Zhōu Dǐ at the Zhōu-family Jīng-nán 荊南 estate in Yí-xīng, and the two composed the **[[KR4h0091]]** *Jīng-nán chàng-hé shī-jí* (1 *juǎn*).

After the Míng founding he served as **Jiàn-chāng tóng-zhī** 建昌同知 (Assistant Prefect of Jiàn-chāng). He transmitted the hand-copied *chàng-hé* anthology to Gāo Qǐ, who passed it through **Lǚ Mǐn** 呂敏 — Lǚ wrote a postscript and **Xú Bēn** 徐賁 added a *tí-cí* — eventually returning to the Mǎ family. The collection was first printed in the **Chéng-huà period (1465–1487)** by the Yí-xīng man **Lǐ Tíng-zhī** 李廷芝, edited by **Lǐ Yìng-zhēn** 李應禎 and **Zhāng Bì** 張弼.

His own *bié-jí* does not survive; his verse is preserved only in this collection.
