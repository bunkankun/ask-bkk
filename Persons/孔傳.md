Sòng official, forty-seventh-generation descendant of Confucius, courtesy name Shìwén 世文. CBDB record 15942 lists his floruit as 1127–1134; standard biographies place him broadly in the late Northern Sòng to early Southern Sòng. With his cousin Kǒng Duānyǒu 孔端友 (holder of the *Yǎnshènggōng* 衍聖公 title) he fled south at the Jurchen sack of Kāifēng in *Jiànyán* 1 (1127) and settled in Qúzhōu 衢州 in modern Zhèjiāng, where the Southern Sòng court duplicated the *Yǎnshènggōng* enfeoffment for the Kǒng family in exile. He rose under the Southern Sòng to *Yòucháoyìdàfū* 右朝議大夫, prefect of Fǔzhōu 撫州 with concurrent commission as Promoter of Agriculture in the locality, and was enfeoffed as *Xiānyuánxiàn kāiguónán* 仙源縣開國男. He is principally known as the compiler of the *Dōngjiā zájì* 東家雜記 ([[KR2g0002]]), an insider's topical account of the Kǒng descent line and of the cult-site at Qūfù, completed in *Shàoxīng* 4 (1134).
