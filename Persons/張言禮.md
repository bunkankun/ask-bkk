# Zhāng Yánlǐ 張言禮

*Hào* Xīnqiáo 心樵. Late-Qīng physician of Dāntú 丹徒 (modern Jiāngsū). Recovered the Wàn-lì-era manuscript *Tuīná yàojué* [[推拿要訣]] of [[周於蕃]] Zhōu Yúfān that had circulated unpublished in a *Zhāng* family for some twenty years, and in 1887 (*dīnghài* 丁亥) gave the manuscript to his namesake [[張筱衫]] Zhāng Zhènjūn (Zhāng Xiǎoshān) for editorial reorganization, illustration, and printing; the result was [[KR3ee054]] *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù*. His own preface to the work is one of five preserved in the front-matter.
