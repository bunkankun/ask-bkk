Early-Kāngxī master physician of Sūzhōu; *zì* Lùyù 路玉, *hào* Shíwán Lǎorén 石頑老人. Lifedates conventionally 1617–c.1700 (b. late Wànlì; alive in 1695 to complete the *Yī tōng*; death usually placed in 1700 or shortly after). Counted with Yú Jiāyán 喻嘉言 and Wú Qiān 吳謙 as one of the "Three Great Physicians of the Early Qīng." Author of:

- *Yī tōng* 醫通 (later 張氏醫通, 16 juan, 1695) — [[KR3e0095]] — his magnum opus, an internal-medicine synthesis.
- *Zhěn zōng sān mèi* 診宗三昧 ([[KR3eb022]], 1689) — pulse-and-diagnosis treatise.
- *Shānghán zuǎn lùn* 傷寒纘論 — Shānghán commentary.
- *Shānghán xù lùn* 傷寒緒論 — Shānghán supplement.
- *Běn jīng féng yuán* 本經逢原 (1695) — *Shénnóng běn cǎo jīng* exposition.

His son Zhāng Yǐróu 張倚柔 (字 自茹) and grandson Zhāng Yībāng 張以邦 continued the *Yī tōng* lineage. No CBDB record.
