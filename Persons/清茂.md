# Qīng-mào 清茂

Late-Southern-Sòng / Yuán Yángqí-branch Línjì (Sōng-yuán lineage) Chán master, *zì* Gǔ-lín 古林, *hào* Jīn-gāng-chuáng 金剛幢, late-life self-style Xiū-jū-sǒu 休居叟; imperial title Fú-zōng Pǔ-jué Fó-xìng chánshī 扶宗普覺佛性禪師, conferred by Yuán Rénzōng (r. 1311–20). Native of Lè-qīng 樂清 (Wēn-zhōu), lay surname Lín 林 — the same surname (and village, traditionally) as his teacher. Dharma-heir of [[如珙]] Héng-chuān Rú-gǒng 橫川如珙 (1222–1289, [[KR6q0344]]); principal dharma-founder of the "Gǔ-lín-pài" 古林派 of late-Yuán / early-Míng Línjì, and, through his Japanese students, of the Chikusen-ha 竺仙派 — one of the twenty-four schools of Japanese Chán / Zen.

Born on the 1st of the 8th month of Jǐngdìng 3 (24 August 1262). Entered religion at ten after hearing the "Miào-zhuāng-wáng pǐn" 妙莊嚴王品 of the *Lotus Sūtra*, serving Tiān-tái Guó-qīng Gū-yán-qǐ 孤巖啟 and receiving the precepts under Wēn Róng 溫容. Studied with Shí-lín Gǒng 石林鞏 (Nán-píng Shí-lín) at Jìng-cí, attained a first opening on the "Nán-shān bamboo-shoots / East-Sea cuttlefish" turn of phrase, and — after Shí-lín's death — went on to Yàndàng-shān, where he attained full awakening under Rú-gǒng at the Néng-rén Fàng-mù-liáo 能仁放牧寮.

Held four (five by the standard count) major abbacies: Píng-jiāng-fǔ Tiān-píng-shān Bái-yún chán-sì 天平山白雲禪寺 (from Dàdé 2 / 1298), Píng-jiāng Kāi-yuán chán-sì 開元禪寺 (two terms), Ráo-zhōu Yǒng-fú chán-sì 永福禪寺 (from Yánhòu 2 / 1315) — where he completed the *Zōng-mén tǒng-yào xù-jí* 宗門統要續集 — and finally Jīn-líng Bǎo-níng chán-sì 保寧禪寺, where he spent his later years and earned the appellation Bǎo-níng Mào 保寧茂. Died at Bǎo-níng on the 22nd of the 11th month of Tiān-lì 2 (20 December 1329), *shì-shòu* 68, *sēng-là* 55.

Named dharma-heirs include [[清欲]] Liǎo-ān Qīng-yù 了庵清欲 (1292–1367, DILA A001089), Zhòng-móu Liáng-yóu 仲謀良猷 (DILA A026108), Xiǎo-mào 小茂 (A015527), Qīng-hǎi 清海 (A021447), Dà-fāng Yīn 大方因 (A015541), the Japanese-resident [[梵僊]] Zhú-xiān Fàn-xiān 竺仙梵僊 (1292–1348, A000765, founder of the Chikusen-ha), Shí-shì Shàn-jiǔ 石室善玖 (A027658), and Yuè-lín Dào-jiǎo 月林道皎 (A027739). His *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0345]]) and *shí-yí jì-sòng* ([[KR6q0346]]) both survive, the latter compiled by the Japanese student Hǎi-shòu 海壽; the first Japanese printing of the *yǔlù* was cut at Kyōto's Nán-chán-sì in Kōei 1 (1342) by another Japanese follower, the monk Sēn 森, with assistance from Kokan Shiren's etc. circle. Principal biographical sources: *Zēng-jí Xù-chuándēng-lù* *juan* 5; the *Xíng-shí* and *bēi* in [[KR6q0346]] *juan* 2; *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* *juan* 52.
