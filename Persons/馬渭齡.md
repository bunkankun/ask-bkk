Mid-Qīng physician active in the early-*Dào-guāng* period (his postface, written by his son [[馬傳鈺]], dates his compilation of the work to summer *Dào-guāng* 16 / *bǐng-shēn* 丙申 / 1836; he is referred to in the postface as 先父, "my late father", so he had died by the time the work was committed to print). Author of [[KR3em033]] *Hóu-kē dà-chéng* 喉科大成, a four-*juǎn* synthesis of throat-medicine assembling the standard Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng canon (《活人》, 《金匱》, 《本事》, 《三因》, 《和劑》, 《綱目》, 《入門》, 《準繩》, 《金鑑》, 《心悟》, 《尊生》, 《徐峨峰瘍醫》) with family-tradition secret formulae. His professional position is inclusive rather than partisan — both cooling and warming protocols are kept side-by-side in his formulary — and the work covers the full throat-and-mouth-and-dental field, not merely the *bái-hóu* tract literature. No CBDB record; place of origin not recoverable from the postface.
