**Ní Zhīwéi** 倪枝維 (*zì* Pèiyù 佩玉, *hào* Fèngbīn 鳳賓), early-Qīng obstetrical specialist of Pújiāng 浦江 (Zhèjiāng). *Yōngzhèng* 6 = 1728: completed the *Chǎnbǎo* 產寶 ([[KR3ei057]]). The work circulated only as manuscript for over a century. According to Xǔ Lián 許槤 of Hǎichāng's preface of 1842 (*Dàoguāng rényín*), the manuscript was acquired from Zhào Wèi 趙魏, a friend of Xǔ, and lay in Xǔ's box for some twenty-five years before being prepared for printing. Xǔ's 1842 printing was the first; Huáng Zǔjīng 黃祖經 of Shíyáng 石陽 prepared a re-cut edition in 1871 (*Tóngzhì xīnwèi*); Huáng Zǐyú 黃子畬 prepared a stone-lithograph edition in 1906 (*Guāngxù bǐngwǔ*). Not in CBDB.
