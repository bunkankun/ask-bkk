**Ní Zhī-wéi** 倪枝維 (*zì* Pèi-yù 佩玉, *hào* Fèng-bīn 鳳賓), early-Qīng obstetrical specialist of Pú-jiāng 浦江 (Zhèjiāng). *Yōng-zhèng* 6 = 1728: completed the *Chǎn-bǎo* 產寶 ([[KR3ei057]]). The work circulated only as manuscript for over a century. According to Xǔ Lián 許槤 of Hǎi-chāng's preface of 1842 (*Dào-guāng rén-yín*), the manuscript was acquired from Zhào Wèi 趙魏, a friend of Xǔ, and lay in Xǔ's box for some twenty-five years before being prepared for printing. Xǔ's 1842 printing was the first; Huáng Zǔ-jīng 黃祖經 of Shí-yáng 石陽 prepared a re-cut edition in 1871 (*Tóng-zhì xīn-wèi*); Huáng Zǐ-yú 黃子畬 prepared a stone-lithograph edition in 1906 (*Guāng-xù bǐng-wǔ*). Not in CBDB.
