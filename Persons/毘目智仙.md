Pí-mù-zhì-xiān 毘目智仙 (active mid-6th c.), Northern-Wèi / Eastern-Wèi-period Indian translator working in Yè 鄴 (the Eastern-Wèi capital). Per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145), he collaborated with [[般若流支]] Prajñāruci on multiple translations including [[KR6f0033|*Shèng shàn-zhù-yì tiān-zǐ suǒ wèn jīng* 聖善住意天子所問經]] (T0341). Birth and death dates not preserved.
