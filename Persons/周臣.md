**Zhōu Chén** 周臣 (*zì* Zǐzhōng 子忠, *hào* Yòngzhuō 用拙 and Zàishān 在山; b. 1505), mid-Míng civil official. Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu) native, registered in Bāzhōu 霸州 of Shùntiānfǔ 順天府. Nephew of Zhōu Shēn 周紳 (Vice-Director of the Board of Works, *jǔrén* of *Zhèngdé* 8 = 1513). *Jǔrén* of *Jiājìng* 7 = 1528 (no. 24 in the Shùntiānfǔ provincial examination); *jìnshì* of *Jiājìng* 8 = 1529 (second rank, no. 38). Served as Sub-prefect of Jīnzhōu 金州 in Shǎnxī, then Sub-prefect of Gāotángzhōu 高唐州 in Shāndōng. From *Jiājìng* 28 = 1549 Prefect of Qúzhōu 衢州 in Zhèjiāng, where during a confinement to the prefectural studio caused by an outbreak of sores he composed the *Hòushēng xùnzuǎn* 厚生訓纂 ([[KR3eo009]]) on the urging of his Vice-Prefect Zhōu Yǔtánshí. Eventually rose to Provincial Administration Vice-Commissioner of Shǎnxī 陝西布政司參議.

**Not** the painter Zhōu Chén 周臣 (*zì* Shùnqīng 舜卿, *hào* Dōngcūn 東村, c. 1460s–1535, teacher of Táng Yín and Qiú Yīng) — a homonym from the same Wúxiàn lineage but a distinct person. The catalog meta `data/catalogs/meta/KR3eo.yaml` records the author of *Hòushēng xùnzuǎn* as 周涇 — a transcription error for 周臣. CBDB lists no clear entry for this Zhōu Chén distinct from the painter (128800 with dates 1460–1535 is the painter).

There is also a Míng painter **Zhōu Chén** 周臣 (*zì* Shùnqīng 舜卿, *hào* Dōngcūn 東村), c. 1460s–1535, Wúxiàn (Sūzhōu) native, teacher of Táng Yín 唐寅 and Qiú Yīng 仇英, prolific in landscape and figure painting. CBDB 128800 (with dates 1460–1535). This is **not** the author of *Hòushēng xùnzuǎn*.
