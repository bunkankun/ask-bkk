**Zhōu Chén** 周臣 (*zì* Zǐ-zhōng 子忠, *hào* Yòng-zhuō 用拙 and Zài-shān 在山; b. 1505), mid-Míng civil official. Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sū-zhōu) native, registered in Bā-zhōu 霸州 of Shùn-tiān-fǔ 順天府. Nephew of Zhōu Shēn 周紳 (Vice-Director of the Board of Works, *jǔ-rén* of *Zhèng-dé* 8 = 1513). *Jǔ-rén* of *Jiā-jìng* 7 = 1528 (no. 24 in the Shùn-tiān-fǔ provincial examination); *jìn-shì* of *Jiā-jìng* 8 = 1529 (second rank, no. 38). Served as Sub-prefect of Jīn-zhōu 金州 in Shǎn-xī, then Sub-prefect of Gāo-táng-zhōu 高唐州 in Shān-dōng. From *Jiā-jìng* 28 = 1549 Prefect of Qú-zhōu 衢州 in Zhèjiāng, where during a confinement to the prefectural studio caused by an outbreak of sores he composed the *Hòu-shēng xùn-zuǎn* 厚生訓纂 ([[KR3eo009]]) on the urging of his Vice-Prefect Zhōu Yǔ-tán-shí. Eventually rose to Provincial Administration Vice-Commissioner of Shǎn-xī 陝西布政司參議.

**Not** the painter Zhōu Chén 周臣 (*zì* Shùn-qīng 舜卿, *hào* Dōng-cūn 東村, c. 1460s–1535, teacher of Táng Yín and Qiú Yīng) — a homonym from the same Wú-xiàn lineage but a distinct person. The catalog meta `data/catalogs/meta/KR3eo.yaml` records the author of *Hòu-shēng xùn-zuǎn* as 周涇 — a transcription error for 周臣. CBDB lists no clear entry for this Zhōu Chén distinct from the painter (128800 with dates 1460–1535 is the painter).

There is also a Míng painter **Zhōu Chén** 周臣 (*zì* Shùn-qīng 舜卿, *hào* Dōng-cūn 東村), c. 1460s–1535, Wú-xiàn (Sū-zhōu) native, teacher of Táng Yín 唐寅 and Qiú Yīng 仇英, prolific in landscape and figure painting. CBDB 128800 (with dates 1460–1535). This is **not** the author of *Hòu-shēng xùn-zuǎn*.
