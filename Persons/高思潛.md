Early Republican-era physician of Héxiàn 和縣 (Ānhuī). Working with the *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 editor [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jíshēng, Gāo carried out a philological investigation (*kǎo* 考) in c. 1925 of the textual history of [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhīlán's *Yījīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (1662 — [[KR3er103]]), confirming that the previously circulating *Yījīng mìzhǐ* 醫經秘旨 ([[KR3er105]]) was a re-titled pirated extract from the first ten essays of the Jiāng original. Gāo's *kǎo* is the principal modern philological recovery of Jiāng's authorship; the *Sānsān yīshū* edition of the *Yījīn yī fá* is the textus receptus of modern Chinese-medical study of the work. Gāo's other principal scholarly contribution is documented in this collaboration with Qiú Jíshēng's series.
