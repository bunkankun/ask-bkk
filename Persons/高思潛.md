Early Republican-era physician of Hé-xiàn 和縣 (Ān-huī). Working with the *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 editor [[裘吉生]] Qiú Jí-shēng, Gāo carried out a philological investigation (*kǎo* 考) in c. 1925 of the textual history of [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhī-lán's *Yī-jīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (1662 — [[KR3er103]]), confirming that the previously circulating *Yī-jīng mì-zhǐ* 醫經秘旨 ([[KR3er105]]) was a re-titled pirated extract from the first ten essays of the Jiāng original. Gāo's *kǎo* is the principal modern philological recovery of Jiāng's authorship; the *Sān-sān yī-shū* edition of the *Yī-jīn yī fá* is the textus receptus of modern Chinese-medical study of the work. Gāo's other principal scholarly contribution is documented in this collaboration with Qiú Jí-shēng's series.
