**Wú Mài-shū** 吳邁書 (mid-Qián-lóng physician, fl. 1756), of Gǔ-Hī Fēng-nán 古歙豐南 (Hī-zhōu / Ān-huī). Compiler of the *Fāng-zhèng huì-yào* 方症會要 ([[KR3er022]], preface dated *Qián-lóng bǐng-zǐ* = 1756) — a four-*juǎn* clinical formulary based on the manuscript notebooks of his late father, a hereditary Hī-zhōu physician. Wú narrates in the preface that his own medical study was driven by his weak constitution; he tested his father's formulae against his own complaints and brought the family clinical inheritance to print after the father's death. Wú belongs to the productive mid-Qīng *Xīn-ān yī-xué* 新安醫學 (Hī-zhōu medical) tradition that produced the *Yī-shù* 醫述 of [[程杏軒]] Chéng Xìng-xuān ([[KR3er058]]) and a number of similar regional clinical compilations.
