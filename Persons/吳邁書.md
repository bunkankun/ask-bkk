**Wú Màishū** 吳邁書 (mid-Qián-lóng physician, fl. 1756), of GǔHī Fēngnán 古歙豐南 (Hīzhōu / Ānhuī). Compiler of the *Fāngzhèng huìyào* 方症會要 ([[KR3er022]], preface dated *Qiánlóng bǐngzǐ* = 1756) — a four-*juǎn* clinical formulary based on the manuscript notebooks of his late father, a hereditary Hīzhōu physician. Wú narrates in the preface that his own medical study was driven by his weak constitution; he tested his father's formulae against his own complaints and brought the family clinical inheritance to print after the father's death. Wú belongs to the productive mid-Qīng *Xīnān yīxué* 新安醫學 (Hīzhōu medical) tradition that produced the *Yīshù* 醫述 of [[程杏軒]] Chéng Xìngxuān ([[KR3er058]]) and a number of similar regional clinical compilations.
