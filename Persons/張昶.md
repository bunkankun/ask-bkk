**Zhāng Chǎng** 張昶, late-Míng physician of Dàliáng 大梁 (Kāifēng, Hénán). His preface to *Xiǎoér zhūzhèng bǔyí* 小兒諸證補遺 ([[KR3ej032]]) is signed *Chóngzhēn jiǔnián suìcì bǐngzǐ júyuè shàng huàn wǔrì Dàliáng qīshísì suì lǎorén Zhāng Chǎng* 崇禎九年歲次丙子菊月上浣五日大梁七十四歲老人張昶序 — *Chóngzhēn* 9 (1636) chrysanthemum-month (ninth lunar month) fifth day, by the Dàliáng seventy-four-year-old Zhāng Chǎng. The age statement (74 in 1636) places his birth in 1563 (*Jiājìng* 42). The preface positions the work as a companion supplement to his earlier *Wènduì* 問對 (Question-and-Reply, an adult medical *yīàn*), specifying particular paediatric disorders not covered there.
