**Zhāng Chǎng** 張昶, late-Míng physician of Dà-liáng 大梁 (Kāi-fēng, Hé-nán). His preface to *Xiǎo-ér zhū-zhèng bǔ-yí* 小兒諸證補遺 ([[KR3ej032]]) is signed *Chóng-zhēn jiǔ-nián suì-cì bǐng-zǐ jú-yuè shàng huàn wǔ-rì Dà-liáng qī-shí-sì suì lǎo-rén Zhāng Chǎng* 崇禎九年歲次丙子菊月上浣五日大梁七十四歲老人張昶序 — *Chóng-zhēn* 9 (1636) chrysanthemum-month (ninth lunar month) fifth day, by the Dà-liáng seventy-four-year-old Zhāng Chǎng. The age statement (74 in 1636) places his birth in 1563 (*Jiā-jìng* 42). The preface positions the work as a companion supplement to his earlier *Wèn-duì* 問對 (Question-and-Reply, an adult medical *yī-àn*), specifying particular paediatric disorders not covered there.
