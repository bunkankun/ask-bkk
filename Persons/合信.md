**Benjamin Hobson** 合信 (Hé Xìn, 1816–1873), British Protestant medical missionary of the London Missionary Society, active in Macao, Guǎngzhōu, and Shànghǎi from 1839 to 1859. Author of the five so-called *Hobson medical books* in classical Chinese — *Quán tǐ xīn lùn* 全體新論 (anatomy, 1851), *Bóqiáng yī lùn* 博物醫論 (general medicine), *Xī yī lüè lùn* 西醫略論, *Fù yīng xīn shuō* 婦嬰新說, *Nèi kē xīn shuō* 內科新說 — the principal late-Qīng Chinese-language conduit for Western anatomical and clinical knowledge before the Boxer era. Cited by [[唐宗海]] Táng Zōnghǎi in [[KR3eh056]] for mesenteric-inflammation autopsy findings on dysentery.
