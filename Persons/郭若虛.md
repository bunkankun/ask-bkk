Northern Sòng art historian and painting connoisseur. From a Tàiyuán 太原 family of imperial in-laws (his grandmother was Liú-shì 劉氏, an aunt of empress Liú of Zhēn-zōng's reign); his grandfather the *Sītú-gōng* and his father had assembled one of the great painting collections of the early Sòng, alongside those of Dīng Wèi 丁謂 (*Jìngōng*) and Mǎ Zhījié 馬知節 (*Zhèng-huì*). Active in the *Xīnínɡ* 熙寧 (1068–1077) decade; held diplomatic appointments to the Liáo. Author of [[KR3h0017]] *Túhuà jiànwén zhì* 圖畫見聞誌 (c. 1074–1080), the indispensable continuation of Zhāng Yànyuǎn's [[KR3h0009]] *Lìdài mínghuà jì*. The first *juàn*'s sixteen theoretical essays — especially the *qìyùn fēi shī* 氣韻非師 dictum and the Northern / Southern landscape genealogy — are among the most consequential pieces of Northern Sòng painting theory.
