Wàn Shàng-liè 萬尚烈 (fl. late 16th–early 17th c.), late-Míng Jiāng-xī Confucian, disciple of Zhāng Huáng 章潢. His sole attested contribution to the catalog is the biographical *xíng-zhuàng* (account of conduct) for Zhāng Huáng appended to Zhāng's *Tú-shū biān* 圖書編 ([[KR3k0050]]) in the *Sìkù* recension — the principal contemporary biographical source for Zhāng. No independent works survive under his name.
