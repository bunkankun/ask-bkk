# Wàn Chéngcāng 萬承蒼

Floruit Qiánlóng era, second half of the eighteenth century. A Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 editor, principally documented as the *kǎozhèng* compiler for the *Sòngshū* ([[KR2a0016]]) and the *Nán shǐ* ([[KR2a0024]]) in the *Sìkù quánshū*. Detailed biographical particulars not preserved; not in CBDB.
