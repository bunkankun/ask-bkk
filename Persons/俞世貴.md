**Yú Shì-guì** 俞世貴 (*zì* Guì-tíng 桂庭, *fl.* early-to-mid Dào-guāng period), Qīng-period literatus and editorial-medical scholar; maternal uncle of the great late-Qīng *Wēn-bìng*-school physician Wáng Shì-xióng 王士雄 (1808–1868). Did not practise medicine himself but read widely in the *Xuán-qí* 軒岐 medical literature. Performed substantial editorial work on Shǐ Diǎn's *Yuàn-tǐ yī-huà* 願體醫話 ([[KR3eq064]]) — eliminating one *yīn-zhèng* entry and adding supplementary formulary material. The edited manuscript came to Wáng Shì-xióng after Yú's death and was eventually printed by Wáng in 1853 or shortly after. Not in CBDB.
