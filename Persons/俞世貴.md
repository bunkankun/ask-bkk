**Yú Shìguì** 俞世貴 (*zì* Guìtíng 桂庭, *fl.* early-to-mid Dàoguāng period), Qīng-period literatus and editorial-medical scholar; maternal uncle of the great late-Qīng *Wēnbìng*-school physician Wáng Shìxióng 王士雄 (1808–1868). Did not practise medicine himself but read widely in the *Xuánqí* 軒岐 medical literature. Performed substantial editorial work on Shǐ Diǎn's *Yuàntǐ yīhuà* 願體醫話 ([[KR3eq064]]) — eliminating one *yīnzhèng* entry and adding supplementary formulary material. The edited manuscript came to Wáng Shìxióng after Yú's death and was eventually printed by Wáng in 1853 or shortly after. Not in CBDB.
