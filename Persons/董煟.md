Late-Southern-Sòng official and famine-relief specialist; native of Póyáng 鄱陽 (modern Jiāngxī). *Zì* Jìxìng 季興. Took the *jìnshì* in *Shàoxī* 5 (1194); served as magistrate of Ruì'ān 瑞安 (modern Wēnzhōu). His principal work is the *Jiùhuāng huómín shū* 救荒活民書 ([[KR2m0044]]), the seminal Chinese treatise on famine relief and the foundation of the genre—drawn upon by every later Yuán-Míng-Qīng compilation in the field. Precise life-dates not transmitted; *fl.* late 12th–early 13th century.
