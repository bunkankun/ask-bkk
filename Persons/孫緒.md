Sūn Xù 孫緒 (fl. Hóngzhì), *zì* **Chéngfǔ** 誠甫, self-styled **Shāxī** 沙溪, of **Gùchéng** 故城 (Jìnánfǔ, BěiZhílì). Hóngzhì 12 / *jǐwèi* (1499) *jìnshì*; rose to *Tàipúsì qīng*. His *biéjí* the *Shāxī jí* in 23 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0156]]) contains the unusual *Wúyòng xiántán* 無用閒談 (Useless Idle Talk) in 6 *juǎn* — a *bǐjì*-style critical-prose work explicitly arguing against the **Lǐ Mèngyáng** *QiánQīzǐ* archaicist program. Originally co-cut with **Mǎ Zhōngxí** 馬中錫's *Dōngtián jí*; the *Sìkù* separates them on the *xuéwèn bǐlì jiē shèng Zhōngxí* (his learning-and-pen-force surpass Zhōngxí) judgement and preserves Sūn while reducing Mǎ to *cúnmù* (catalogue-only).
