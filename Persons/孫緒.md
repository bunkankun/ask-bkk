Sūn Xù 孫緒 (fl. Hóng-zhì), *zì* **Chéng-fǔ** 誠甫, self-styled **Shā-xī** 沙溪, of **Gù-chéng** 故城 (Jì-nán-fǔ, Běi-Zhí-lì). Hóng-zhì 12 / *jǐ-wèi* (1499) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Tài-pú-sì qīng*. His *bié-jí* the *Shā-xī jí* in 23 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0156]]) contains the unusual *Wú-yòng xián-tán* 無用閒談 (Useless Idle Talk) in 6 *juǎn* — a *bǐ-jì*-style critical-prose work explicitly arguing against the **Lǐ Mèng-yáng** *Qián-Qī-zǐ* archaicist program. Originally co-cut with **Mǎ Zhōng-xí** 馬中錫's *Dōng-tián jí*; the *Sìkù* separates them on the *xué-wèn bǐ-lì jiē shèng Zhōng-xí* (his learning-and-pen-force surpass Zhōng-xí) judgement and preserves Sūn while reducing Mǎ to *cún-mù* (catalogue-only).
