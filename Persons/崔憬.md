# Cuī Jǐng 崔憬

Early Táng 唐 *Yì* scholar. His *Tàn xuán* 探玄 (also titled *Zhōuyì tàn xuán* 周易探玄) is the most heavily cited Táng *Yì* commentary in [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* ([[KR1a0008]]) after [[侯果]] Hóu Guǒ. He works in the *xiàngshù* 象數 tradition but interpolates philosophical glosses; the work as a whole is lost outside the *Jíjiě* citations.
