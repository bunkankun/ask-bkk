**Liáng Yuándì** 梁園棣 (*hào* Língshí 靈石, mid-Qīng official, fl. *Dàoguāng* era), holder of the *zhīzhōu xián* 知州銜 honorary rank, and three-time magistrate of Xīnghuà 興化縣 (Jiāngsū). Liáng's preface to [[趙術堂]] Zhào Shùtáng's *Yīxué zhǐguī* 醫學指歸 ([[KR3er050]]) provides the principal biographical evidence for Zhào and reflects Liáng's role as both Zhào's patron and the local gazetteer-compiler (he was simultaneously compiling the *Xīnghuà xiàn zhì* 興化縣志 in which Zhào would be honoured). No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
