**Liáng Yuán-dì** 梁園棣 (*hào* Líng-shí 靈石, mid-Qīng official, fl. *Dào-guāng* era), holder of the *zhī-zhōu xián* 知州銜 honorary rank, and three-time magistrate of Xīng-huà 興化縣 (Jiāng-sū). Liáng's preface to [[趙術堂]] Zhào Shù-táng's *Yī-xué zhǐ-guī* 醫學指歸 ([[KR3er050]]) provides the principal biographical evidence for Zhào and reflects Liáng's role as both Zhào's patron and the local gazetteer-compiler (he was simultaneously compiling the *Xīng-huà xiàn zhì* 興化縣志 in which Zhào would be honoured). No CBDB record. Lifedates undetermined.
