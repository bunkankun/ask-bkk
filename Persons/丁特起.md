丁特起 Dīng Tèqǐ was a Northern Song official (fl. 1126–1127; CBDB id 439011) who was present in Kaifeng during the Jīngkāng crisis — the Jin siege and capture of the Northern Song capital. His sole surviving work is the *Jìngkāng Jìwén* 靖康紀聞 ([[KR4k0028]]), a diary-style account of the siege, fall of the city, and Jin occupation. His dates and further biographical details are unknown.
