Liú Xiǎobīn 劉小斌 is a contemporary historian of medicine at the Guǎngzhōu Zhōngyīyào Dàxué 廣州中醫藥大學, principal author of *Lǐngnán yīxué shǐ* 嶺南醫學史 (Guǎngzhōu, 2005).
