# 位卿公

**Wèiqīnggōng** ("Honourable Sir Wèiqīng", *fl.* Jiāqìng era), Shàngyì 上邑 physician, father of [[胡青崑]] Hú Qīngkūn; received the traumatology manuscript from his uncle [[啟萬公]] Qǐwàngōng, copied it into three *jí* ("collections") under the title *Huíshēng*, and so produced the proximate exemplar that Hú Qīngkūn printed in 1856 as [[KR3el016]] *Diēdǎ sǔnshāng huíshēng jí*. Abandoned the *kējǔ* examinations to take up medicine on the maxim "if one cannot be a good minister, one can be a good physician to benefit the world." Personal name not recoverable from the surviving preface, which refers to him only by this respect-title.
