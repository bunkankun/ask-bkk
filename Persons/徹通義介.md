# Tettsū Gikai 徹通義介

Japanese Sōtō Zen master (1219–1309). Third abbot of Eihei-ji 永平寺 after [[道元|Dōgen]] 道元 and [[懷奘|Ejō]] 懷奘. Teacher of [[瑩山|Keizan Jōkin]] 瑩山紹瑾 (1264–1325). Subsequently founded Daijō-ji 大乘寺.
