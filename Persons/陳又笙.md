**Chén Yòushēng** 陳又笙 (mid- to late-Qīng), renowned obstetrical specialist; transmitter of the *Hēishéndān* 黑神丹 (Black-Spirit Pill) formula included as a supplement in the *Zhúlín nǚkē zhèngzhì* 竹林女科證治 ([[KR3ei026]]).
