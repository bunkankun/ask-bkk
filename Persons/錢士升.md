Qián Shì-shēng 錢士升 (1575–1652), *zì* **Yì-zhī** 抑之, *hào* **Yù-lěng** 御冷 and **Sài-ān** 塞庵, posthumous title **Wén-yì** 文毅 (Southern-Míng); native of **Jiā-shàn** 嘉善 (Zhè-jiāng). *Zhuàng-yuán* of Wàn-lì 44 / *bǐng-chén* (1616, first-place *jìnshì*). Rose to *Lǐ-bù shì-láng* under *Chóng-zhēn*, then *Dōng-gé dà-xué-shì* (Grand Secretary of the Eastern Pavilion) in *Chóng-zhēn* 8 / 1635, but was forced out by *Wēn Tǐ-rén* 溫體仁 in 1637 and returned home to Jiā-shàn. He survived the Míng–Qīng transition and lived on as a *yí-mín* until 1652. A close *Dōng-lín* sympathiser, he supplied a preface (now in the editions descending from Qián Fēn's first impression) to the *Máo-yán jí* ([[KR4e0243]]) of [[魏學洢]]. The *Sìkù* tiyao criticises that preface — written by Qián and others — for praising Wèi Xué-yī as one who '*died for his father*' (*yǔn-shēn xùn-fù*) while *suppressing* the more painful *zhuī-bǐ* / pursuit-for-restitution that actually drove him to his end. His son [[錢棻]] edited and printed the *Máo-yán jí* in its first edition. CBDB 86453. *Míng-shǐ* j. 251.
