Lǐ Jīnyōng 李今庸 (1925–2022) was a senior modern Chinese-medicine philologist and *guóyī dàshī* 國醫大師. He led the edition of *Wáng Kěntáng yīxué quánshū* 王肯堂醫學全書 (Beijing: Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè, 1999).
