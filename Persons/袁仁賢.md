Late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of Liúyáng 瀏陽, eastern Húnán; courtesy name 潤齊 *Rùnqí*, *hào* 悟真山人 *Wùzhēn shānrén*. Fifth-generation descendant of an eighteenth-century 神醫 *shényī* ancestor 諱翯公 *huì Hègōng* who is recorded as having received a 乾隆朝 imperial-bestowed plaque "Divine Doctor" 神醫 during the JiāngHuái 江淮 plague of his time and whose unpublished family medical compendium 《醫學一貫》 was transmitted within the袁 family for five generations. Studied under his fellow-villager Liú Xīncén 劉薪岑, then performed *jūnshì yóu* 軍事遊 (military advisory service) in 嶺南 (GuǎngdōngGuǎngxī), where he engaged Western medicine critically. Collaborated with [[歐陽藹臣]] Ōuyáng Ǎichén on laryngology. In winter *jǐyǒu* 己酉 / 1909 retired to Jīnlíng (Nanjing); in mid-summer *Xuāntǒng* 3 / *xīnhài* / 1911 completed and printed [[KR3em034]] *Hóukē jīnyuè quánshū* 喉科金鑰全書 with sponsoring prefaces by [[陸潤庠]] and [[勞乃宣]]. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
