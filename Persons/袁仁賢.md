Late-Qīng / early-Republican physician of Liú-yáng 瀏陽, eastern Húnán; courtesy name 潤齊 *Rùn-qí*, *hào* 悟真山人 *Wù-zhēn shān-rén*. Fifth-generation descendant of an eighteenth-century 神醫 *shén-yī* ancestor 諱翯公 *huì Hè-gōng* who is recorded as having received a 乾隆朝 imperial-bestowed plaque "Divine Doctor" 神醫 during the Jiāng-Huái 江淮 plague of his time and whose unpublished family medical compendium 《醫學一貫》 was transmitted within the袁 family for five generations. Studied under his fellow-villager Liú Xīn-cén 劉薪岑, then performed *jūn-shì yóu* 軍事遊 (military advisory service) in 嶺南 (Guǎngdōng-Guǎngxī), where he engaged Western medicine critically. Collaborated with [[歐陽藹臣]] Ōu-yáng Ǎi-chén on laryngology. In winter *jǐ-yǒu* 己酉 / 1909 retired to Jīn-líng (Nanjing); in mid-summer *Xuān-tǒng* 3 / *xīn-hài* / 1911 completed and printed [[KR3em034]] *Hóu-kē jīn-yuè quán-shū* 喉科金鑰全書 with sponsoring prefaces by [[陸潤庠]] and [[勞乃宣]]. No CBDB record; lifedates not recoverable.
