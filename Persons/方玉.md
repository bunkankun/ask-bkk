# 方玉

Fāng Yù

Late-Míng layman of Sūzhōu (Wúmén). Disciple of [[德清]] Hānshān Déqīng who oversaw the 1616 Wúmén printing of Déqīng's *Jīngāng jīng juéyí* ([[KR6c0062]]).
