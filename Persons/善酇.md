Shànzàn 善酇, *hào* **Yìān** 翼菴 ("Wing-Hermit"). LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] (1605–1672) in the Hànyuè Sānfēng line. Listed in the [[弘儲]] DILA roster among Hóngchǔ's *fǎsì* alongside **Zìyún Zōng** 字雲蹤, **Yuèhán Zǐ** 月函子, **Tóngshuò Hóng** 童碩宏, and the nun **Zǔfú** 祖符. Lifedates not preserved.

**Work.** [[KR6q0519]] 《翼菴禪師語錄》 8 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J37 B394 — catalog meta does not credit a separate compiler, suggesting the text may have been self-edited or the compilers' names were lost.
