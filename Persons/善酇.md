Shàn-zàn 善酇, *hào* **Yì-ān** 翼菴 ("Wing-Hermit"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] (1605–1672) in the Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng line. Listed in the [[弘儲]] DILA roster among Hóng-chǔ's *fǎ-sì* alongside **Zì-yún Zōng** 字雲蹤, **Yuè-hán Zǐ** 月函子, **Tóng-shuò Hóng** 童碩宏, and the nun **Zǔ-fú** 祖符. Lifedates not preserved.

**Work.** [[KR6q0519]] 《翼菴禪師語錄》 8 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B394 — catalog meta does not credit a separate compiler, suggesting the text may have been self-edited or the compilers' names were lost.
