Qí Bǐnghuì 齊秉慧, *hào* Yǒutáng 有堂, c. 1764 – after 1806, Sìchuān 四川 physician. According to his 1806 self-preface to [[KR3ep074]] *Qíshì yīàn*, he was a self-cured former invalid who turned to medicine through study of *Xuēshì yīàn* and formal training under **Huáng Chāofán** 超凡黃公 (a disciple of Yù Chāng's school via [[舒馳遠]] Shū Chíyuǎn) at Wǔchāng (Húběi) from Jiāqìng dīngsì (1797) at age 33. His clinical work was based principally in Sìchuān and explicitly grounded in the Yù-school *Shànglùn* / *Yùyì cǎo* doctrines. Not in CBDB.
