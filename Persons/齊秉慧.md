Qí Bǐng-huì 齊秉慧, *hào* Yǒu-táng 有堂, c. 1764 – after 1806, Sì-chuān 四川 physician. According to his 1806 self-preface to [[KR3ep074]] *Qí-shì yī-àn*, he was a self-cured former invalid who turned to medicine through study of *Xuē-shì yī-àn* and formal training under **Huáng Chāo-fán** 超凡黃公 (a disciple of Yù Chāng's school via [[舒馳遠]] Shū Chí-yuǎn) at Wǔ-chāng (Hú-běi) from Jiā-qìng dīng-sì (1797) at age 33. His clinical work was based principally in Sì-chuān and explicitly grounded in the Yù-school *Shàng-lùn* / *Yù-yì cǎo* doctrines. Not in CBDB.
