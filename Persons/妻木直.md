# 妻木直 Tsumaki Naoki

Late-Edo southern-Japanese (南越) medical officer (*fl.* late 18th c.); author of the *Kansei* 9 (1797) postface to [[川越正淑]] Kawagoe Masayoshi's *Shānghán yòng yào yán jiū* ([[KR3ef069]]).
