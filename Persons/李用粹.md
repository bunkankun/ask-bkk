**Lǐ Yòng-cuì** 李用粹 (*zì* Xiū-zhī 修之, *hào* Xìng-ān 惺菴, fl. Kāng-xī era), Qīng physician of Shàng-hǎi 上海. His principal work, the *Zhèng-zhì huì-bǔ* 證治彙補 ([[KR3er015]]), is an eight-*juǎn* internal-medicine synthesis organised under an eight-section / ten-rubric (八門十事) framework that became one of the most-used mid-Qīng didactic medical handbooks. The work was prefaced in 1691 by the Kūn-shān *kǎo-jù* scholar [[徐秉義]] Xú Bǐng-yì, who placed Lǐ in the world of high-Qīng *kǎo-jù* scholarship rather than the practising-physician milieu. No CBDB record. Lǐ's lifedates are not securely datable but the 1691 preface and the developed *zhèng-zhì* nosology of the work itself bracket his flourishing to c. 1687–1691, with a probable birth in the 1620s–1640s and death in the early eighteenth century.
