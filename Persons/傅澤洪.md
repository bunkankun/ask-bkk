Fù Zé-hóng 傅澤洪 (fl. 1725), *zì* Zhì-jūn 穉君, of the Hàn-jūn Bordered-Red Banner 鑲紅旗漢軍, served as *Fēn-xún Huái-Yáng dào àn-chá-sī fù-shǐ* (Vice-Provincial Surveillance Commissioner of the Huái-Yáng Circuit). His principal work, completed in Yōngzhèng 3 (1725), is the *Xíng-shuǐ jīn-jiàn* ([[KR2k0078]]) in 175 *juan* — the most extensive Chinese hydraulic-engineering compendium ever compiled and the principal documentary archive of pre-1722 Chinese water-management. Quán Zǔwàng's epitaph for [[鄭元慶]] (Zhèng Yuán-qìng) advances the unverified hypothesis that the actual editorial labor was Zhèng's, working as Fù's *mù-bīn*.
