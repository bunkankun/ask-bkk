Early-Míng anthologist of **Níng-bō** 寧波 (Zhèjiāng). Catalog meta records him as *fl.* **1384**. Compiler of the **[[KR4h0093]]** *Yuán-yīn* 元音 (12 *juǎn*), an author-organised anthology of Yuán poetry (176 named poets, brief biographical headnotes) — the central early-Míng curator of Yuán dynasty verse. The work was prefaced by **Wū Sī-dào** 烏斯道 of Sì-míng (Hóng-wǔ 17 = 1384) and edited / engraved by Chén Mèng-chéng (Gǔ-tián magistrate) and Zhāng Zhōng-dá (Dìng-hǎi vice-magistrate) before its final post-colophon of *Xīn-sì* 1401. No other works under his name are transmitted.
