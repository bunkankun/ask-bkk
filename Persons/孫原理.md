Early-Míng anthologist of **Níngbō** 寧波 (Zhèjiāng). Catalog meta records him as *fl.* **1384**. Compiler of the **[[KR4h0093]]** *Yuányīn* 元音 (12 *juǎn*), an author-organised anthology of Yuán poetry (176 named poets, brief biographical headnotes) — the central early-Míng curator of Yuán dynasty verse. The work was prefaced by **Wū Sīdào** 烏斯道 of Sìmíng (Hóngwǔ 17 = 1384) and edited / engraved by Chén Mèngchéng (Gǔtián magistrate) and Zhāng Zhōngdá (Dìnghǎi vice-magistrate) before its final post-colophon of *Xīnsì* 1401. No other works under his name are transmitted.
