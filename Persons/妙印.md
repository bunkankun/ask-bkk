# Miào-yìn 妙印

Mid-Qīng Línjì Chán monk, co-recorder (*děng lù* 等錄) of [[KR6q0427]] 《永濟融禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B209) in the Liáo-dōng assembly of [[融|Yǒng-jì Róng]] 永濟融, working alongside [[師住]] Shī-zhù. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0427]] juan-head signature line "*shū-jì Shī-zhù Miào-yìn lù* 書記師住妙印錄".
