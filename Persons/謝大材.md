# Xiè Dà-cái 謝大材

Early-Qīng lay disciple (*yōu-pó-sāi* 優婆塞) of [[道霈]] Wèi-lín Dào-pèi (1615–1702) during the master's Bái-yún-shān 白雲山 retreat-period; named first among the three lay recorders of the *Wèi-lín chánshī yún-shān fǎ-huì lù* ([[KR6q0371]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
