# Xiè Dàcái 謝大材

Early-Qīng lay disciple (*yōupósāi* 優婆塞) of [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi (1615–1702) during the master's Báiyúnshān 白雲山 retreat-period; named first among the three lay recorders of the *Wèilín chánshī yúnshān fǎhuì lù* ([[KR6q0371]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
