Wáng Lètíng 王樂亭, late-Qīng Sūzhōu / Wúmén physician (*fl.* mid-to-late 19th century). Author of [[KR3eq094]] *Wáng Lètíng zhǐyào* 王樂亭指要 (4 *juǎn*), a clinical casebook in the standard Wúmén *zuǒ/yòu* convention, doctrinally aligned with the *Wēnbìng* synthesis of Yè Tiānshì and [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèngyīng. Apart from this work he is unattested in standard biographical sources; he had no relationship with the much-better-known modern Běijīng acupuncturist Wáng Lètíng 王樂亭 (1895–1984, founder of the *Wǔlóng zhēnfǎ* lineage), who is an entirely separate figure.
