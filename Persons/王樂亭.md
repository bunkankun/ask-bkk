Wáng Lè-tíng 王樂亭, late-Qīng Sū-zhōu / Wú-mén physician (*fl.* mid-to-late 19th century). Author of [[KR3eq094]] *Wáng Lè-tíng zhǐ-yào* 王樂亭指要 (4 *juǎn*), a clinical casebook in the standard Wú-mén *zuǒ/yòu* convention, doctrinally aligned with the *Wēn-bìng* synthesis of Yè Tiān-shì and [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèng-yīng. Apart from this work he is unattested in standard biographical sources; he had no relationship with the much-better-known modern Běi-jīng acupuncturist Wáng Lè-tíng 王樂亭 (1895–1984, founder of the *Wǔ-lóng zhēn-fǎ* lineage), who is an entirely separate figure.
