# Dèng Zhōngchén 鄧忠臣 (fl. 1080s)

A Northern-Sòng *Yuán-yòu* (1086–1094)-era official whose name appears in the *Yuán-yòu dǎng-jí* 元祐黨籍 (the proscribed-faction roster of *Chóng-níng* 1, 1102), placing him in the Sū Shì / Liú Zhì circle. Compiler and principal participant of the *Tóng-wén-guǎn chàng-hé shī* 同文館唱和詩 [[KR4h0026]], the exchange-anthology produced when nineteen junior officials including Zhāng Lěi 張耒, Cháo Bǔ-zhī 晁補之, Cài Zhào 蔥肇, Kǒng Wǔ-zhōng 孔武仲 served as examination supervisors at the *Tóng-wén-guǎn* in *Yuán-yòu* 2 (1087). Dèng's own poem in the collection has the self-note "*Dīng-mǎo sì-yuè huán cháo*" (returned to court, *Yuán-yòu* 2, 4th month, 1087), anchoring the dating. His individual collection, the *Yù-chí jí* 玉池集, is lost; the *Tóng-wén-guǎn chàng-hé shī* is the principal surviving witness to his work. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
