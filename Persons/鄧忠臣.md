# Dèng Zhōngchén 鄧忠臣 (fl. 1080s)

A Northern-Sòng *Yuányòu* (1086–1094)-era official whose name appears in the *Yuányòu dǎngjí* 元祐黨籍 (the proscribed-faction roster of *Chóngníng* 1, 1102), placing him in the Sū Shì / Liú Zhì circle. Compiler and principal participant of the *Tóngwénguǎn chànghé shī* 同文館唱和詩 [[KR4h0026]], the exchange-anthology produced when nineteen junior officials including Zhāng Lěi 張耒, Cháo Bǔzhī 晁補之, Cài Zhào 蔥肇, Kǒng Wǔzhōng 孔武仲 served as examination supervisors at the *Tóngwénguǎn* in *Yuányòu* 2 (1087). Dèng's own poem in the collection has the self-note "*Dīngmǎo sìyuè huán cháo*" (returned to court, *Yuányòu* 2, 4th month, 1087), anchoring the dating. His individual collection, the *Yùchí jí* 玉池集, is lost; the *Tóngwénguǎn chànghé shī* is the principal surviving witness to his work. No firm lifedates; no CBDB entry.
