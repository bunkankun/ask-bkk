# Chāo-jù 超巨

Mid-late seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[謐|Èr-yǐn Xíng-mì]] 二隱行謐 (c. 1606–1665) in the Mìyún → Lín-yě Tōng-qí line. Co-compiler (*děng biān* 等編) of [[KR6q0430]] 《二隱謐禪師語錄》 (Jiā-xīng Canon J28 B212). Based at **Jiāng-nán Yáng-zhōu Gāo-shā Tiān-wáng-sì** 江南揚州高沙天王禪寺. The 超-generation character is standard for the 34th-generation Línjì / 35th-generation Cáoxī level below Xíng-mì's 行-generation.

Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.

Sources: [[KR6q0430]] juan-head signature line "*sì-fǎ mén-rén Chāo-jù Chāo-xiù děng biān* 嗣法門人超巨超秀等編".
