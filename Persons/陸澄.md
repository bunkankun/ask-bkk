# Lù Chéng 陸澄

*Zì* Yànyuān 彥淵. Southern Qí 南齊 court official and classicist, native of Wú prefecture (modern Sūzhōu). Held the office of Director of the Imperial Secretariat (*Mìshū jiàn* 祕書監). Known especially for his learning in the *Yì*: his letter to [[王儉]] Wáng Jiǎn arguing that [[王弼]] Wáng Bì's *Yì* commentary depends on the [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán school is preserved in the *Sìkù* *tiyao* tradition. *Nán Qí shū* 南齊書 (juan 39) and *Nán shǐ* 南史 give his biography.
