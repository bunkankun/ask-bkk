Chéng Lóng 程龍, Sòng-period commentator on the *Yì wěi* 易緯 tradition. Cited in the *Sìkù* notice on [[KR1a0163]] for the observation that after the Suí persecution of *chènwěi* 讖緯 only the *Qián záo dù* and *Kūn záo dù* texts (of the *Yì wěi* corpus) circulated in his day.
