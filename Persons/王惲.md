Foundational Khubilai-era 元 Hàn-Confucian civil servant and senior Hàn-lín literary office-holder; one of the principal poetic-and-prose figures of the early Yuán state. *Zì* **Zhòng-móu** 仲謀; *hào* **Qiū-jiàn** 秋澗 ("Autumn-Ravine"). Native of **Jí-zhōu** 汲州 (Wèi-huī 衛輝, modern Hé-nán).

**Career.** Long career in the Yuán imperial bureaucracy spanning the Khubilai through early-Chéng-zōng reigns. Rose to *Hàn-lín xué-shì chéng-zhǐ* 翰林學士承旨 — the senior Hàn-lín literary office; concurrent *Tài-zhōng dà-fū* 太中大夫. Composer of substantial Khubilai-era imperial *zhì-zhào* and *zòu-yì*.

**Literary lineage.** Disciple of **Yuán Hǎo-wèn** 元好問 (1190–1257) — the Jīn-Yuán transition master. Inheritor of the Yí-shān 遺山 poetic tradition of Hán-Wèi-Táng modeling.

**Network.** Close associate of **Hǎo Jīng** 郝經 (1223–1275, the great Khubilai-era Hàn-Confucian envoy); colleague of the **Bù-hū-mù** 不忽木 Khangli-Confucian senior advisor and other early-Yuán literati.

**Principal contributions.** The 100-*juàn* *Qiū-jiàn xiān-shēng dà-quán wén-jí* [[KR4d0464]] preserves: (1) the *Zhōng-tǒng shén-wǔ sòng* — Khubilai-imperial-founding praise-piece (Zhōng-tǒng era, 1260); (2) substantial *fù* and antiquarian poetry preserving Tang-Song site documentation; (3) the *Nǐ Hán-zǐ qiū huái* and *Jiǔ-rì hé Yuān-míng* poetic-imitation cycles; (4) the *Bài-diàn Lǔ Wén-xiàn-wáng miào* and *Bài-diàn Xuān-shèng-lín mù* Confucian-pilgrimage records; (5) substantial Khubilai-era *zòu-yì* memorial-archive material.

**Reference.** *Yuán-shǐ* j. 167.
