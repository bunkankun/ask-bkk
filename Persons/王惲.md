Foundational Khubilai-era 元 Hàn-Confucian civil servant and senior Hànlín literary office-holder; one of the principal poetic-and-prose figures of the early Yuán state. *Zì* **Zhòngmóu** 仲謀; *hào* **Qiūjiàn** 秋澗 ("Autumn-Ravine"). Native of **Jízhōu** 汲州 (Wèihuī 衛輝, modern Hénán).

**Career.** Long career in the Yuán imperial bureaucracy spanning the Khubilai through early-Chéng-zōng reigns. Rose to *Hànlín xuéshì chéngzhǐ* 翰林學士承旨 — the senior Hànlín literary office; concurrent *Tàizhōng dàfū* 太中大夫. Composer of substantial Khubilai-era imperial *zhìzhào* and *zòuyì*.

**Literary lineage.** Disciple of **Yuán Hǎowèn** 元好問 (1190–1257) — the Jīn-Yuán transition master. Inheritor of the Yíshān 遺山 poetic tradition of Hán-Wèi-Táng modeling.

**Network.** Close associate of **Hǎo Jīng** 郝經 (1223–1275, the great Khubilai-era Hàn-Confucian envoy); colleague of the **Bùhūmù** 不忽木 Khangli-Confucian senior advisor and other early-Yuán literati.

**Principal contributions.** The 100-*juàn* *Qiūjiàn xiānshēng dàquán wénjí* [[KR4d0464]] preserves: (1) the *Zhōngtǒng shénwǔ sòng* — Khubilai-imperial-founding praise-piece (Zhōngtǒng era, 1260); (2) substantial *fù* and antiquarian poetry preserving Tang-Song site documentation; (3) the *Nǐ Hánzǐ qiū huái* and *Jiǔrì hé Yuānmíng* poetic-imitation cycles; (4) the *Bàidiàn Lǔ Wénxiànwáng miào* and *Bàidiàn Xuānshènglín mù* Confucian-pilgrimage records; (5) substantial Khubilai-era *zòuyì* memorial-archive material.

**Within the Kanripo corpus.** [[KR4d0464]] *Qiūjiàn jí* 秋澗集 (撰).

**Reference.** *Yuánshǐ* j. 167.
