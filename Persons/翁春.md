High-Qīng (Qián-lóng-era) Buddhist layman, born **1736** (乾隆 1, *qián-yī*). *Zì* **Shǔ-jiū** 曙鳩, sobriquets **Biàn-táng** 辨堂 and **Dàn-shēng** 澹生. Co-compiler with **Wáng Xī-guǎn** [[王錫琯]] of the *Jīn-gāng jīng qiǎn-jiě* 金剛經淺解 ([[KR6c0078]], X25 no. 490) — a one-juan accessible *Vajracchedikā* commentary in the *qiǎn-jiě* ("shallow explanation") devotional-pedagogy genre, prefaced for the printed edition in **Kāng-xī 20 = 1681** (康熙辛酉; the dating of the printed edition pushes the original composition to before Wēng's birth — the recipient line credits the two compilers, but the date suggests an antedated authorship convention or that the work was actually by an earlier hand transmitted under their names — see entry note for [[KR6c0078]]). Standard biographical reference: *Qīng-dài rén-wù shēng-zú nián-biǎo* p. 657.
