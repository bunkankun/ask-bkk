High-Qīng (Qián-lóng-era) Buddhist layman, born **1736** (乾隆 1, *qiányī*). *Zì* **Shǔjiū** 曙鳩, sobriquets **Biàntáng** 辨堂 and **Dànshēng** 澹生. Co-compiler with **Wáng Xīguǎn** [[王錫琯]] of the *Jīngāng jīng qiǎnjiě* 金剛經淺解 ([[KR6c0078]], X25 no. 490) — a one-juan accessible *Vajracchedikā* commentary in the *qiǎnjiě* ("shallow explanation") devotional-pedagogy genre, prefaced for the printed edition in **Kāngxī 20 = 1681** (康熙辛酉; the dating of the printed edition pushes the original composition to before Wēng's birth — the recipient line credits the two compilers, but the date suggests an antedated authorship convention or that the work was actually by an earlier hand transmitted under their names — see entry note for [[KR6c0078]]). Standard biographical reference: *Qīngdài rénwù shēngzú niánbiǎo* p. 657.
