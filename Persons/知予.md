# 知予 Zhī Yǔ

**Zhī Yǔ** 知予 ("the one who knows me", evidently a literary pseudonym), mid-Míng figure who contributed a preface to [[夏英]] Xià Yīng's *Língshū jīngmài yì* 靈樞經脈翼 ([[KR3ee024]]); plausibly the printer of the work.
