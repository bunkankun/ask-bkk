# Zhēn Yì-yuàn 真益願

A mid-Qīng lay Pure Land practitioner, principally remembered as compiler (*zuǎn-shù* 纂述) of the short Pure Land exhortative tract **《勸修淨土切要》** *Quàn xiū jìng-tǔ qiè-yào* [[KR6p0105]] (X1186). He self-designates as **Jìng-yè xíng-rén** 淨業行人 ("a practitioner of pure karma"), the standard lay-pious self-designation. According to the *Xián-fēng yǐ-mǎo* 咸豐乙卯 (1855) preface by his cousin **Pān Zēng-yíng** 潘曾瑩 (1808–1878) of the Sūzhōu Pān 潘 lineage, his lay sobriquet was **Gēng-táng** 耕塘 ("Plough-Pond"). The work was already in circulation by 1855, when Pān reprinted it; a further reprinting (*chóng-kè* 重刻) followed in *Xián-fēng xīn-yǒu* 咸豐辛酉 (1861). Original lifedates not preserved.

The connection to the prominent Sūzhōu Pān lineage — Pān Zēng-yíng was a senior official and brother of the celebrated bibliophile **Pān Zǔ-yīn** 潘祖蔭 (1830–1890) — situates Zhēn Yì-yuàn within the high-status mid-nineteenth-century literati Pure Land tradition.
