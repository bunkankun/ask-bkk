# Zōnghóng 宗宏

Mid-seventeenth-century Línjì Chán monk, dharma-heir of [[福慧|Sōngshān Yězhú Fúhuì]] 嵩山野竹福慧 in the Pòshān / Yuánliáo / Shānhuī / Fúhuì Yúnnán mission sub-line. Head recorder (*lù* 錄) of [[KR6q0438]] 《嵩山野竹禪師錄》 (Jiāxīng Canon J29 B224). The 宗-generation character is shared with his dharma-brothers [[宗上]] and [[宗堅仝]] (co-editors of the same volume), constituting the 35th-generation Línjì tier in the Shānhuī / Fúhuì Guì-Yún sub-line lineage-poem. Native place, lay surname, and lifedates unrecovered.
