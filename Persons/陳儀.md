Chén Yí 陳儀 (1670–1742), *zì* Zǐhuì 子翽, *hào* Yīwú 一吾, of Wénān 文安 (Bǎodìng, Zhílì), was a Kāngxī 54 (1715) *jìnshì* who rose to *Hànlínyuàn shìjiǎng xuéshì* (Reading-in-Waiting at the Hànlín Academy). In Yōngzhèng 4 (1726) he was appointed *Bàzhōu děngchù yíngtián guāncháshǐ* (Inspector for Military-Agricultural Reclamation in Bàzhōu et al.) — the principal field-directing official of the Yōngzhèng-era *yíngtián* hydraulic-and-agricultural reclamation program in Héběi. The administrative direction of the program belonged to the Yīqīnwáng Yǔnxiáng 怡親王允祥. Chén's principal monograph from this office is the *Zhílì héqú zhì* ([[KR2k0077]]) in 1 *juan*, the principal early-Qīng documentary record of the Hé-běi-plain river-and-canal network.
