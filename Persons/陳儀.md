Chén Yí 陳儀 (1670–1742), *zì* Zǐ-huì 子翽, *hào* Yī-wú 一吾, of Wén-ān 文安 (Bǎo-dìng, Zhí-lì), was a Kāng-xī 54 (1715) *jìnshì* who rose to *Hàn-lín-yuàn shì-jiǎng xué-shì* (Reading-in-Waiting at the Hànlín Academy). In Yōngzhèng 4 (1726) he was appointed *Bà-zhōu děng-chù yíng-tián guān-chá-shǐ* (Inspector for Military-Agricultural Reclamation in Bà-zhōu et al.) — the principal field-directing official of the Yōngzhèng-era *yíng-tián* hydraulic-and-agricultural reclamation program in Hé-běi. The administrative direction of the program belonged to the Yī-qīn-wáng Yǔn-xiáng 怡親王允祥. Chén's principal monograph from this office is the *Zhí-lì hé-qú zhì* ([[KR2k0077]]) in 1 *juan*, the principal early-Qīng documentary record of the Hé-běi-plain river-and-canal network.
