Zhōu Mì 周密 (1232–1298), *zì* Gōng-jǐn 公謹, *hào* Cǎo-chuāng 草窗, also Píng-zhōu 蘋洲 and Biàn-yáng lǎo-rén 弁陽老人. Native of Jǐ-nán 濟南 by descent; the family had moved south with Gāo-zōng's crossing and was registered at Hú-zhōu 湖州 (his great-grandfather settled there). One of the most distinguished Sòng-loyalist (*yí-mín*) scholars of the Sòng-Yuán transition. Held office under the late Sòng as Magistrate of Yì-wū 義烏 (in *Chún-yòu*) and other minor posts; after the fall of the Southern Sòng in *Dé-yòu* 2 (1276) he refused to serve the Yuán and lived in seclusion at Hángzhōu's *Guǐ-xīn* street, where he produced his best-known works. Author of the *Wǔlín jiùshì* 武林舊事 ([[KR2k0119]]), the *Guǐ-xīn zá-shí* 癸辛雜識 ([[KR3j0093]]), the *Qí-dōng yě-yǔ* 齊東野語 ([[KR3j0021]]), the *Yún-yān guò-yǎn lù* 雲煙過眼錄 (an important early connoisseurship treatise), the *Hào-rán zhāi yǎ-tán* 浩然齋雅談, the *Cǎo-chuāng jí* 草窗集, and the major Sòng-loyalist anthologies *Jué-miào hǎo-cí* 絕妙好詞 (Sòng *cí* anthology) and *Yāng tán* 鞅潭. His combined output makes him the principal literary monument to the Sòng-Yuán transition.
