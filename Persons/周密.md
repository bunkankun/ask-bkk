Zhōu Mì 周密 (1232–1298), *zì* Gōngjǐn 公謹, *hào* Cǎochuāng 草窗, also Píngzhōu 蘋洲 and Biànyáng lǎorén 弁陽老人. Native of Jǐnán 濟南 by descent; the family had moved south with Gāozōng's crossing and was registered at Húzhōu 湖州 (his great-grandfather settled there). One of the most distinguished Sòng-loyalist (*yímín*) scholars of the SòngYuán transition. Held office under the late Sòng as Magistrate of Yìwū 義烏 (in *Chúnyòu*) and other minor posts; after the fall of the Southern Sòng in *Déyòu* 2 (1276) he refused to serve the Yuán and lived in seclusion at Hángzhōu's *Guǐxīn* street, where he produced his best-known works. Author of the *Wǔlín jiùshì* 武林舊事 ([[KR2k0119]]), the *Guǐxīn záshí* 癸辛雜識 ([[KR3j0093]]), the *Qídōng yěyǔ* 齊東野語 ([[KR3j0134]]), the *Yúnyān guòyǎn lù* 雲煙過眼錄 (an important early connoisseurship treatise), the *Hàorán zhāi yǎtán* 浩然齋雅談, the *Cǎochuāng jí* 草窗集, and the major Sòng-loyalist anthologies *Juémiào hǎocí* 絕妙好詞 (Sòng *cí* anthology) and *Yāng tán* 鞅潭. His combined output makes him the principal literary monument to the SòngYuán transition.
