# Shí Jì 石繼

Northern Sòng *Yì* scholar; mentioned in the Sìkù *tiyao* of [[KR1a0010]] *Zhōuyì jǔzhèng* (alongside [[趙汝楳]] Zhào Rǔméi) as one of the Sòng readers who denounced the *Jǔzhèng* as a forgery relying on the Wáng-Hán authorial names to alter the canonical text. No other secure biographical information has been located; the form of the name may itself be a variant.
