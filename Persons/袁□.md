# Yuán □ 袁□ (also known as *Bǎisuì lǎorén* 百歲老人, "Centenarian")

The unnamed Sòng author of [[KR3l0056]] *Fēngchuāng xiǎodú* 楓窗小牘. The *Sìkù* compilers, following the Míng-era preface of Yáo Shìlín [[姚士粦]], could establish only the following:

- **Surname Yuán 袁**, by the author's own quotation of a *Xiān sānlǎo* 先三老 entry which can be cross-checked against the Hàn-stele *Yuán Liáng bēi* 袁良碑 preserved in Hóng Shì 洪适's *Lìshì* 隸釋 — the stele belongs to a Yuán-clan ancestor whom the author claims as his own.
- **Native of Dàliáng 大梁** (i.e., *Biànjīng* 汴京 / *Kāifēng*); the work uses the phrase "*as a youth I grew up in Dàliáng*".
- **Resident of Línān 臨安** after the 1126/27 fall of *Biànjīng* — the opening passage of *juàn* 1 records his hasty river-crossing and refugee residence in the Línān mountains.
- **Of an old Biànjīng official family**: his great-grandfather was *zànshàn dàifū* 贊善大夫 Yànfāng 彥方 (named in a Sū Shì 蘇軾 letter); his father held a near-palace official rank and composed an *Àiyuèhuátái sòng* 萼綠華臺頌 for the *Gènyuè* 艮嶽 imperial garden; his great-grandmother (*zēng wáng dàmǔ*) was honoured *Xiànjūn* 縣君; an uncle (*cóngzǔ*) served as *cǎn* of Zhèngzhōu; and a cousin (*zǐ*) bore the style *Bóshì Yí* 博士頤.
- **Long-lived**: the old colophon names him *Bǎisuì lǎorén* 百歲老人 (Centenarian). Internal references span *Chóngníng* 5 (1106) court fashion to *Jiātài* 2 (1202) lunar eclipse — a span of 97 years — corroborating the title. His birth therefore falls c. *Yuánfēng* — *Yuányòu* (i.e. early 1080s), with death not before *Jiātài* 2 (1202).

His given name remains unknowable. Zhā Shènxíng 查慎行 (1650–1727), in his commentary on Sū Shì's *Láihètíng* 來鶴亭 poem, identifies the author with the Míng-era Sūzhōu publisher Yuán Jiǒng 袁褧 (CBDB id 35257, Míng dynasty, fl. 16th century, founder of the *Jiāqùtáng* 嘉趣堂 press) — but as the *Sìkù tíyào* observes, this is dynastically impossible and the basis of Zhā's claim is uncertain. Despite this, Zhā's identification has propagated through later catalogs and is the form one will most often encounter in modern bibliographic references; the catalog meta of the Kanripo corpus retains `袁□` as the honest reading.

For confirmed Yuán-surname Sòng *bǐjì* authors of comparable date, see [[袁說友]], [[袁采]], [[袁文]]; the *Fēngchuāng xiǎodú* author is not identifiable with any of them.
