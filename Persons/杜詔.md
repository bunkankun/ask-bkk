Dù Zhào 杜詔 (1666–1736; lifedates not in CBDB but well attested in literary biographies), *zì* Zǐlún 紫綸, *hào* Yúnchuān 雲川, native of Wúxī 無錫 (ChángZhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). *Jǔrén* of Kāngxī 38 (1699); subsequently *jìnshì* of Kāngxī 51 (1712). Entered the Hanlin and rose to *jiǎntǎo* 檢討. A noted poet and *cí* writer, included in the imperial poetic compilation *Yùzhì Pèiwénzhāi shīwén jí* and recommended for the *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學鴻詞 examination of Kāngxī 18 (though not selected). Engaged as principal *biānzuǎn* of the *Shāndōng tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0048]]) under Yuè Jùn in 1729; carried the project through to its near-completion in 1735–1736. His own *Yúnchuān gé jí* 雲川閣集 survives in 12 *juan*. CBDB id 56144 (no dates given); lifedates from *Qīngdài shīwén jí huìbiān* (Shanghai gǔjí, 2010) entry.
