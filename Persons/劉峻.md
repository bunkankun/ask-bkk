Liú Jùn 劉峻 (462–521 CE; *zì* Xiàobiāo 孝標) was a Liáng 梁 dynasty scholar from Píngcāng 平昌. Captured by Northern Wei as a child, he returned south and devoted himself to erudition, producing the authoritative commentary on the *Shìshuō xīnyǔ* 世說新語 — the *Shìshuō xīnyǔ zhù* 世說新語注 — which cites some 400 now-lost sources. His 〈廣絕交論〉 is a celebrated literary essay. He was repeatedly denied official appointment despite his learning. Biography in *Liángshū* juǎn 50 and *Nán shǐ* juǎn 49. His reconstructed collected works are [[KR4b0054]].
