**Zǔ Táizhī** 祖台之 (late 4th century), *zì* Yuánchén 元辰, native of Fànyáng 范陽 Suìchéng 遒城. His biography stands in *Jìn shū* 75 (列傳 45). Of the Fànyáng Zǔ clan — the same lineage as the great early-medieval astronomer-mathematicians Zǔ Chōngzhī 祖沖之 (429–500) and Zǔ Gēng 祖暅 — he served on the staff of Sīmǎ Dàozǐ 司馬道子 (Prince of Kuàijī, dominant Eastern-Jìn courtier of the 380s–390s) as *Cānjūn* 參軍 and *Zhōngshū láng* 中書郎, eventually rising to *Shàngshū* 尚書 in the closing years of the Eastern Jìn. His official career thus spans roughly 380–410. He is credited in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* with a *Zhìguài* 志怪 (also styled *Zhìguài lù* 志怪錄, sometimes *Zǔ Táizhī Zhìguài* 祖台之志怪) in 2 *juàn* — a *zhìguài* collection now surviving only in fragments preserved in the standard Sòng *léishū*, and reconstructed in Lǔ Xùn's *Gǔ xiǎoshuō gōuchén*. He has no CBDB id in the current dump. Within the Kanripo corpus he is the credited author of [[KR3l0163]].
