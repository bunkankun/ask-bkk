Lóng Huì-táng 龍繪堂, late-Qīng physician of **Yáng-xià** 陽夏 (= Tài-kāng 太康, in Kāi-fēng prefecture, Hé-nán). Attested only through the 1914 postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔn-zǐ yī* 蠢子醫, which reports him deceased by that date and the *Chǔn-zǐ yī* his "*wǎn-nián*" (late-year) work — a four-*juǎn* clinical pedagogical compendium in unmetered popular verse, composed to teach his orphaned grandson Duì-shān 兌山. The work is unusually localised to the Hé-nán late-Qīng regional medical voice, otherwise under-represented in the Chinese medical canon. Not in CBDB.
