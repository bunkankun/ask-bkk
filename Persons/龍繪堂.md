Lóng Huìtáng 龍繪堂, late-Qīng physician of **Yángxià** 陽夏 (= Tàikāng 太康, in Kāifēng prefecture, Hénán). Attested only through the 1914 postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔnzǐ yī* 蠢子醫, which reports him deceased by that date and the *Chǔnzǐ yī* his "*wǎnnián*" (late-year) work — a four-*juǎn* clinical pedagogical compendium in unmetered popular verse, composed to teach his orphaned grandson Duìshān 兌山. The work is unusually localised to the Hénán late-Qīng regional medical voice, otherwise under-represented in the Chinese medical canon. Not in CBDB.
