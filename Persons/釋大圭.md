Shì Dàguī 釋大圭 (Yuán, *fl.* Zhìzhèng 1355), *zì* Héngbái 恆白. Lay surname Liào 廖. Native of Jìnjiāng 晉江 (Quánzhōu, Fújiàn). In Zhì-zhèng-era resided at the Zǐyúnsì 紫雲寺 in Quánzhōu. Original collection 24 *juǎn*: 1 *juǎn Mèngfǎ* + 1 *juǎn Mèngjì* + 1 *juǎn Mèngshì* (all *zōngmén yǔlù*) + 6 *juǎn shī* + 15 *juǎn wén*. The *Sìkùguǎn* removed the *zōngmén yǔlù* (as not properly belonging to a *biéjí*) and most of the *qīngcí shūyǐn* prose (*Shìjiā běnsè* — properly Buddhist liturgy not literature), retaining only the 6 *juǎn shī* (reduced to 5 in the WYG block). Wú Jiàn's 吳鑒 original *xù* characterizes Dàguī's verse as "*yīnghuá shíxiāngfù — cí dá ér yì dào — bùdiāolòu ér gōng — qù zǎnzǔ ér lì — píng gēngchú ér xiù*." The *Sìkù tíyào* compares the surviving verse to Fàn Chéngdà (*Shíhú*) and Lù Yóu (*Jiànnán*) "*Shíhú Jiànnán zhī yúfēng*" — preserved "*yú fāngyǐwài*" (in the *fāngyǐwài* = outside-the-cardinal-quarters = monastic) realm.
