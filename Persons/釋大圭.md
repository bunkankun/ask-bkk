Shì Dà-guī 釋大圭 (Yuán, *fl.* Zhì-zhèng 1355), *zì* Héng-bái 恆白. Lay surname Liào 廖. Native of Jìn-jiāng 晉江 (Quán-zhōu, Fú-jiàn). In Zhì-zhèng-era resided at the Zǐ-yún-sì 紫雲寺 in Quán-zhōu. Original collection 24 *juǎn*: 1 *juǎn Mèng-fǎ* + 1 *juǎn Mèng-jì* + 1 *juǎn Mèng-shì* (all *zōng-mén yǔ-lù*) + 6 *juǎn shī* + 15 *juǎn wén*. The *Sìkù-guǎn* removed the *zōng-mén yǔ-lù* (as not properly belonging to a *bié-jí*) and most of the *qīng-cí shū-yǐn* prose (*Shì-jiā běn-sè* — properly Buddhist liturgy not literature), retaining only the 6 *juǎn shī* (reduced to 5 in the WYG block). Wú Jiàn's 吳鑒 original *xù* characterizes Dà-guī's verse as "*yīng-huá shí-xiāng-fù — cí dá ér yì dào — bù-diāo-lòu ér gōng — qù zǎn-zǔ ér lì — píng gēng-chú ér xiù*." The *Sìkù tíyào* compares the surviving verse to Fàn Chéng-dà (*Shí-hú*) and Lù Yóu (*Jiàn-nán*) "*Shí-hú Jiàn-nán zhī yú-fēng*" — preserved "*yú fāng-yǐ-wài*" (in the *fāng-yǐ-wài* = outside-the-cardinal-quarters = monastic) realm.
