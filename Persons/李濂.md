Lǐ Lián 李濂 (1488–1566), *zì* Chuān-fù 川父, *hào* Sōng-zhǔ 嵩渚, of Xiáng-fú 祥符 (Kāi-fēng), was a Míng-era local historian of his native Kāifēng. *Jìnshì* of Zhèng-dé 12 (1517); served as *Tài-pú-sì shǎo-qīng* (Junior Vice-Chamberlain of the Imperial Stud) and other minor offices before retirement. In retirement at Kāifēng he produced extensive Kāifēng local-historical scholarship: the *Biàn-jīng yí-jì zhì* ([[KR2k0097]]) — a 24-juan documentary monograph on Northern-Sòng Biàn-jīng — is his principal work; he also produced the *Xiáng-fú xiān-xián zhuàn* (catalogued elsewhere in the Sìkù) and the *Yī-shǐ* 醫史 (a medical-history monograph).
