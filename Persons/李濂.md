Lǐ Lián 李濂 (1488–1566), *zì* Chuānfù 川父, *hào* Sōngzhǔ 嵩渚, of Xiángfú 祥符 (Kāifēng), was a Míng-era local historian of his native Kāifēng. *Jìnshì* of Zhèngdé 12 (1517); served as *Tàipúsì shǎoqīng* (Junior Vice-Chamberlain of the Imperial Stud) and other minor offices before retirement. In retirement at Kāifēng he produced extensive Kāifēng local-historical scholarship: the *Biànjīng yíjì zhì* ([[KR2k0097]]) — a 24-juan documentary monograph on Northern-Sòng Biànjīng — is his principal work; he also produced the *Xiángfú xiānxián zhuàn* (catalogued elsewhere in the Sìkù) and the *Yīshǐ* 醫史 (a medical-history monograph).
