Táng-dynasty Tiāntái 天台 master and Lotus Sūtra exegete. DILA Authority A001281. Lifedates unrecorded. Author of two surviving subcommentaries on Zhìyǐ's *Miào-fǎ liánhuá jīng wén-jù* ([[KR6d0014]], T1718): the *Miào-jīng wén-jù sī-zhì zhū-pǐn yào-yì* 妙經文句私志諸品要義 ([[KR6d0018]], X29n0595, 2 *juan*) and the much larger *Miào-jīng wén-jù sī-zhì jì* 妙經文句私志記 ([[KR6d0019]], X29n0596, 14 *juan*) — together among the most substantial Táng-period subcommentaries on the *Wén-jù* outside Zhànrán's tradition. The *sī-zhì* 私志 ("private record") in both titles is conventional, indicating that the work is not an official school transmission but a personal exegetical study.

Note: distinct from another Suí-period **Zhì-yún** 智雲 / **Wū-yún** 烏雲 (DILA A007547), a monk of the Huì-rì-dào-cháng 慧日道場 in Luòyáng who died in the Rén-shòu 仁壽 era (601–605) at age 50.
