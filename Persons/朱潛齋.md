Zhū Qiánzhāi 朱潛齋, early-Republican county magistrate (*xiànzhǎng* 縣長) of **Yúshān** 榆山 in Hénán. Named in the 1914 postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔnzǐ yī* as the official who recommended that Lóng Huìtáng's manuscript be prepared for publication. No standard biographical record located. Not confidently disambiguated from other *Qiánzhāi*-styled persons (which include the more famous late-Qīng *Wēnbìng* physician Wáng Mèngyīng, *hào* Qiánzhāi 潛齋; this Zhū is a separate, early-Republican figure).
