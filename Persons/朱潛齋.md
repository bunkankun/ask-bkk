Zhū Qián-zhāi 朱潛齋, early-Republican county magistrate (*xiàn-zhǎng* 縣長) of **Yú-shān** 榆山 in Hé-nán. Named in the 1914 postface to [[KR3eq093]] *Chǔn-zǐ yī* as the official who recommended that Lóng Huì-táng's manuscript be prepared for publication. No standard biographical record located. Not confidently disambiguated from other *Qián-zhāi*-styled persons (which include the more famous late-Qīng *Wēn-bìng* physician Wáng Mèng-yīng, *hào* Qián-zhāi 潛齋; this Zhū is a separate, early-Republican figure).
