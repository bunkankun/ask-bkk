# Hé Xīfàn 何希範

Prefect of Sháozhōu 韶州 in the early tenth century; issued the *qǐng shū* 請疏 inviting [[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃 to take the Língshù 靈樹 abbacy, preserved in [[KR6q0073]].
