# 王維德

Wáng Wéidé, mid-Qīng external-medicine specialist; author of *Wàikē zhèngzōng* 外科證宗 (1740), which introduced the *Yánghé tāng* 陽和湯 / *Yánghé wán* warming-dissolving lineage into general Qīng surgical practice — subsequently imported into the *hóukē* corpus by [[方成培]] Fāng Chéngpéi in [[KR3em018]] *Chónglóu yùyuè xùbiān* 重樓玉鑰續編.
