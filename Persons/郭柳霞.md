Guō Liǔ-xiá 郭柳霞, modern PRC editor / collator (*diǎn-jiào* 點校). Co-collator (with [[張紹舜]] Zhāng Shào-shùn) of the *hxwd* (*Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī gǔ-jí cóng-shū*) edition of [[KR3eq082]] *Zhǐ-yuán yī-huà* 止園醫話. No standard biographical entry located.
