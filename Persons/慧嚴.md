Huì-yán 慧嚴 (363–443), Liú-Sòng-period Buddhist scholar; one of the principal disciples of [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva at the Cháng'ān bureau, and later a major Liú-Sòng court Buddhist scholar. Per the *Gāosēng zhuàn* (T2059), he and Huì-guān 慧觀 were the leaders of the editorial team that produced [[KR6g0003|the *Southern Recension* of the Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra]] (T0375, 36 fascicles) by re-editing [[曇無讖]] Dharmakṣema's [[KR6g0001|Northern Recension]] (T0374, 40 fasc.) in collaboration with [[佛馱跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra's earlier Fǎ-xiǎn-collaborative *Pre-Niè-pán* materials. Native of Yú-háng 餘杭 (in modern Zhèjiāng).
