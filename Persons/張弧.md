**Zhāng Hú** 張弧 was a Táng Daoist-Confucian syncretist thinker. Author of *Sùlǚzǐ* 素履子 — catalogued in Kanripo both as [[KR3a0019]] (Confucian *Rújiā* placement, following the SKQS) and as [[KR5d0050]] (Daoist placement). The official title given on the work itself is Court Gentleman for Trial in the Bureau of Metropolitan Affairs (將仕郎試大理寺評事 *Jiāngshìláng shì Dàlǐsì píngshì*); his native place is unrecorded. He has no biography in either Táng history. Cháo Yuèzhī 晁說之 (Sòng) attributed to him the forgery of the received *Zǐxià Yìzhuàn* 子夏易傳 ([[KR1a0001]]), one of the major *Yìjīng* spurious transmissions.
