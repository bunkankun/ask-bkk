# Pǔ-shào 普紹

Early-Southern-Sòng Yún-mén-zōng 雲門宗 Chán master, *hào* Chuí-cí 垂慈. Named dharma-heir of [[懷深]] Cí-shòu Huái-shēn (1077–1132) per *Jiā-tài pǔ-dēng-lù* *juan* 9; also served as *shì-zhě* 侍者 and co-editor of his master's four-juan *guǎng-lù* ([[KR6q0381]]; X73 n1451). Lifedates unrecorded.
