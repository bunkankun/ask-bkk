# Pǔshào 普紹

Early-Southern-Sòng Yúnménzōng 雲門宗 Chán master, *hào* Chuící 垂慈. Named dharma-heir of [[懷深]] Císhòu Huáishēn (1077–1132) per *Jiātài pǔdēnglù* *juan* 9; also served as *shìzhě* 侍者 and co-editor of his master's four-juan *guǎnglù* ([[KR6q0381]]; X73 n1451). Lifedates unrecorded.
