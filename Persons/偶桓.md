Early-Míng anthologist. Life-dates and native place not well recorded; CBDB has him as Míng-era (id 131128, no dates). Compiler of the **[[KR4h0092]]** *Qiánkūn qīngqì* 乾坤清氣 (14 *juǎn*), an early-Míng anthology of Yuán poetry organised by verse-form. The work belongs to the early-Hóng-wǔ recuperation of Yuán literary heritage, alongside Sūn Yuán-lǐ's [[KR4h0093]] *Yuán-yīn* (1384) and Sòng Gōng-chuán's [[KR4h0100]] *Yuán shī tǐ-yào*. No other works under his name are transmitted.
