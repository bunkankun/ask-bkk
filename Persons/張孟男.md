Mid- to late-Míng official of Zhōng-móu 中牟 (Hé-nán); *jìn-shì* of *Jiā-jìng* 38 / 1559. Held a long succession of capital and provincial posts culminating in *Nán-jīng hù-bù shàng-shū* 南京戶部尚書 and *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo* 太子太保. He provided the principal celebratory preface (*Hè Yún-lín Gōng-jūn róng-shòu Lǔ-fǔ ēn-cì yī-lín zhuàng-yuán xù*) for [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíng-xián's [[KR3er085]] *Jì-shì quán-shū* (1616), commemorating Gōng's 1593 award of the *Yī-lín zhuàng-yuán* 醫林狀元 title by the Lǔ-fán court. The preface is the chief biographical document for Gōng's Hé-nán-period career.
