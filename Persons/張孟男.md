Mid- to late-Míng official of Zhōngmóu 中牟 (Hénán); *jìnshì* of *Jiājìng* 38 / 1559. Held a long succession of capital and provincial posts culminating in *Nánjīng hùbù shàngshū* 南京戶部尚書 and *Tàizǐ tàibǎo* 太子太保. He provided the principal celebratory preface (*Hè Yúnlín Gōngjūn róngshòu Lǔfǔ ēncì yīlín zhuàngyuán xù*) for [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián's [[KR3er085]] *Jìshì quánshū* (1616), commemorating Gōng's 1593 award of the *Yīlín zhuàngyuán* 醫林狀元 title by the Lǔfán court. The preface is the chief biographical document for Gōng's Hé-nán-period career.
