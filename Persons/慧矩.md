# 慧矩

Huìjǔ 慧矩 (*fl.* Kāihuáng 開皇 17 [597 CE] – Dà-yè 大業 12 [616 CE]), Suí-dynasty scholar-monk who studied Sanskrit and worked at the Shànglín-yuán fānjīng-guǎn 上林園翻經館 in the eastern capital Luòyáng 洛陽. He wrote the preface (and supervised the bureau) for the 615/616 retranslation of the *Yàoshī rúlái běnyuàn jīng* 藥師如來本願經 ([[KR6i0047]], T449) under [[達摩笈多]] Dámó Jíduō (Dharmagupta), with [[法行]], [[明則]], [[長順]] and [[海馭]] as collaborators.
