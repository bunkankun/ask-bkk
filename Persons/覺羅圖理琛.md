Tulišen 覺羅圖理琛 / 圖理琛 (1667–1740), *zì* Yīn-zhōu 因周; of the *Ā-yán Jué-luó* 阿顏覺羅 clan (Manchu *Ayan Gioro*), originally of the *Yè-hè* 葉赫 lineage. Manchu Bordered-Yellow Banner official under Kāng-xī and Yōng-zhèng. Rose through *kǎo-qǔ* (selection-by-examination) to *Nèi-gé zhōng-shū* (Cabinet Drafter), *Nèi-gé shì-dú* (Cabinet Reading-in-Waiting), *Bīng-bù zhí-fāng-sī láng-zhōng* (Section Chief at the Bureau of Operations); under Yōng-zhèng held senior offices including *Lǐ-fān-yuàn shì-láng* (Vice-Director of the Court of Colonial Affairs). His name is preserved in history through his service as principal envoy of the Qīng diplomatic mission to the Tórgūt (Volga Kalmyk) khanate in 1712–1715, which he recorded in the *Yì-yù lù* 異域錄 ([[KR2k0151]]) — a parallel Manchu/Chinese text (Manchu original: *Lakcaha Jecen-de Takūraha Babe Ejehe Bithe*) that is the principal pre-modern Chinese-language documentary record of the Russian Empire, the Tórgūt khanate, and the Siberian and Central-Asian land routes. The mission preceded by 56 years the famous Tórgūt return to China of 1771. CBDB id 487009.
