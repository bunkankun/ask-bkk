Cháng-sūn Zī 長孫滋 (*zì* Jù-zé 巨澤, also styled *Wángwū-shān-qiáo* 王屋山樵, "Woodcutter of Mt. Wángwū") was a Táng-period Daoist of the *Xuán-zhū* 玄珠 transmission, identified in the Daoist canon as the transmitter (*chuán* 傳) of [[KR5b0280]] (*Xuán-zhū xīn-jìng zhù*); the chapter-by-chapter annotation (*zhāng-jù* 章句) is by his contemporary [[王損之]] (棲真子, Qī-zhēn-zǐ). The textual occasion is given as the strange biographical tale of Cuī Shào-xuán 崔少玄, daughter of Cuī Gōng 崔恭 (prefect of Fén-zhōu 汾州), married to Lú Chuí 盧陲 (a *cóng-shì* 從事 in Fú-jiàn) — a Heavenly-Maid-fallen-to-earth narrative that the commentary uses as a doctrinal hook. The catalog meta of Kanripo records the name as 長孫紫 — almost certainly a copyist's slip; the source text of KR5b0280 itself reads 長孫滋. No CBDB record located.
