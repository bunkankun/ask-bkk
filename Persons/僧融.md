# 僧融

Sēngróng, monk-disciple of [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva at Chang'an in the early 5th century. Counted, together with [[僧肇]] Sēngzhào, [[僧叡]] Sēngruì, and [[竺道生]] Zhú Dàoshēng, among the four principal disciples of Kumārajīva's translation seminar.
