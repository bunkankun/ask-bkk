# Sìzōng 嗣宗

Southern-Sòng Chán monk, author of the preface to the *Chánglú Jué héshàng sònggǔ niāngǔ jí* 長蘆覺和尚頌古拈古集 (juan 2 of the *Hóngzhì chánshī guǎnglù* [[KR6q0070]]), dating the *sònggǔ niāngǔ* material to Jiànyán 3 (1129).
