# Ānán 阿難 (Ānanda)

Canonical cousin and personal attendant of the Buddha Śākyamuni; in the Mahāyāna tradition, the rememberer (*bhāṇaka*) of the sūtra-piṭaka, conventionally invoked by the formula *rúshì wǒ wén* 如是我聞 ("Thus have I heard") that opens almost every Buddhist sūtra. Foremost in *bahuśruta* (extensive learning).
