Yuánhóng Yírùn 源洪儀潤 (lifedates unrecorded, active early 19th century), Qing-dynasty Buddhist monk-editor and commentator. *Zì* Yuánhóng 源洪. Abbot of the Huílóng Zhēnjì sì 迴龍真寂寺 (hence the alternate name Zhēnjì Yírùn 真寂儀潤).

Known principally through his editorial and commentarial work on monastic regulations and lay-precept material. Major published works:

- *Bǎizhàng qīng guī zhèng yì jì* 百丈清規證義記 ([[KR6q0135]], X63 n1244, 9 juan) — authoritative Qing commentary on Déhuī's Yuán *Chì xiū Bǎizhàng qīng-guī* ([[KR6q0102]]); self-preface dated Dàoguāng 3 (1823);
- *Zàijiā lǜ yào guǎng jí* 在家律要廣集 (X60 n1123, 3 juan) — expansion of Ōu-yì Zhìxù's earlier compilation on lay precepts; self-preface dated Dàoguāng 4 (1824);
- *Fǔhóng jì bǔ dìng xù* 輔宏記補訂序 within the *Sìjiāo yí zhù huìbǔ fǔhóng jì* 四教儀註彙補輔宏記 (X57 n980);
- *Shuǐlù yíguī* 水陸儀規 — compilation of the water-and-land rites manual;
- *Dìyú míngmù* 地輿名目 (geographical gazetteer, preserved in the National Library of China).

Works in the Kanripo corpus: [[KR6q0135]] *Bǎizhàng qīng guī zhèng yì jì* only.
