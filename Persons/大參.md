Late-Míng Chán lay-practitioner (*dào-rén* 道人), self-styled Lín-lì dào-rén 轆轢道人. Collator (*zhòng-jiào* 重校) of the Míng-era reprint of the *Zhào-zhōu héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0391]]). No further biographical data located; "Dà-cān" is likely an ordination-name (*fǎ-míng* 法名) or dharma-nickname rather than a Confucian style.
