Late-Míng Chán lay-practitioner (*dàorén* 道人), self-styled Línlì dàorén 轆轢道人. Collator (*zhòngjiào* 重校) of the Míng-era reprint of the *Zhàozhōu héshàng yǔlù* ([[KR6q0391]]). No further biographical data located; "Dàcān" is likely an ordination-name (*fǎmíng* 法名) or dharma-nickname rather than a Confucian style.
