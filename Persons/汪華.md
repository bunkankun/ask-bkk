# 汪華

Wāng Huá 汪華 (586–649), Yuèguó Gōng 越國公, Suí–early-Táng founding ancestor of the Huīzhōu 徽州 Wāng lineage from which the Xīnān-school physician [[汪機]] Wāng Jī descended.
