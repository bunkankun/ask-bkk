# Lí Méi 黎眉

Late-Míng editor of the Chán anthology *Jiàowài biéchuán* 教外別傳 ([[KR6q0026]]), working together with other co-editors. Active in the late sixteenth to early seventeenth century; exact lifedates and native place not transmitted.
