Lìguǎng 力廣. Personal disciple-attendant of **Qǐzōng Zhēn** [[真]] (b. 1638) at **Huáyán chánsì** 華嚴禪寺 (Zhēnzhōu). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0577]] 《華嚴起宗真禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B464.
