Lì-guǎng 力廣. Personal disciple-attendant of **Qǐ-zōng Zhēn** [[真]] (b. 1638) at **Huá-yán chán-sì** 華嚴禪寺 (Zhēn-zhōu). Lifedates unknown. Lead recorder (*děng lù*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0577]] 《華嚴起宗真禪師語錄》 2 juan, J39 B464.
