Modern Japanese Buddhist studies scholar (大内 文雄), specialist in apocryphal Chinese Buddhist texts (*yí-wěi-jīng* 疑偽經) and Dūnhuáng manuscripts. Co-edited (with [[齊藤隆信]] Saitō Takanobu) the critical text of the *Jìngdù sānmèi jīng* 淨度三昧經 [[KR6v0076]] for *Zàngwài fójiào wénxiàn* vol. 7, drawing on the *Nanatsu-dera* 七寺 manuscript canon of Nagoya (which preserves the only complete witness of the three-fascicle recension of this otherwise canon-rejected sūtra).
