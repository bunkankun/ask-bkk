# Fǎrú 法如

Fǎrú 法如 (638–689), Northern-School Chán master, dharma-heir of [[弘忍|Hóngrěn]]. Held the abbacy at Sōngyuè 嵩岳 Shàolín sì 少林寺 from ca. 681 until his death. Presented in [[杜朏|Dù Fěi]]'s *Chuán fǎ bǎo jì* [[KR6q0110]] as the senior fifth-generation successor (joint with [[神秀|Shénxiù]]), a position not reflected in the mature Northern-School tradition; effectively displaced in the later Southern-School lineage construction.
