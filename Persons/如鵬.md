Rúpéng 如鵬, *zì* **Yìyún** 翼雲 ("Wing-Cloud"). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qīngchéng Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] (b. 1634). Native of **Shǔxī Hànān** 蜀西漢安 (Sìchuān); lay surname **Féng** 馮. Lifedates unknown. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0544]] 《青城竹浪生禪師語錄》 7 juan, J38 B427.

**Sources.** 《錦江禪燈》 vol. 15 (X85 n1590 p. 196a13).
