*Héng-yuè zhēn-zǐ* 衡嶽真子 ("Perfected Master of Mount Héng") is the Daoist pseudonym of a tenth-century commentator from the southern sacred-mountain tradition (Mount Héng / Nán-yuè 南嶽 in present-day Húnán). He is named in the Daoist canon as the *zhù* 註 (annotator) of one of the two parallel commentaries on the *Xuán-zhū xīn-jìng* 玄珠心鏡 corpus, transmitted in the Daozang as [[KR5b0279]]; he draws extensively on the *Lǎo-jūn xī-shēng jīng* 老君西昇經 and the *Lǎo-jūn nèi-guān jīng* 老君內觀經 as scriptural foundations for the doctrine of the *yī-zhī yuán* 一之元 (the source of the One). The companion commentary at [[KR5b0280]] is by the Táng-period figure [[王損之]] (棲真子) transmitting the work of 長孫滋. No CBDB record is locatable; the figure is known only by his pseudonym.
