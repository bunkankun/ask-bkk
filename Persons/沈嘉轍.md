Shěn Jiā-zhé 沈嘉轍 (fl. early 18th c., *zì* Luán-chéng 欒城), of Qián-táng 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu prefecture). Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōng-zhèng Háng-zhōu literary circle that included [[厲鶚]] Lì È, [[趙昱]] Zhào Yù, [[趙信]] Zhào Xìn, [[符曾]] Fú Zēng, [[吳焯]] Wú Zhuō, and [[陳芝光]] Chén Zhī-guāng. Co-author with these six others of the *Nán-Sòng zá-shì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]] in 7 *juǎn* — the *yǒng-shǐ* (history-poems) of Southern-Sòng Háng-zhōu, with each of the seven contributors producing 100 poems on Southern-Sòng historical anecdotes drawn from the Háng-zhōu setting. Each poem comes with its source citations annotated below. The compilation was published c. 1729 and is the **most celebrated Háng-zhōu local history-poem cycle** of the Qīng period. Shěn Jiā-zhé remained a *zhū-shēng* (county-school student); no further substantial works survive. No CBDB record.
