Shěn Jiāzhé 沈嘉轍 (fl. early 18th c., *zì* Luánchéng 欒城), of Qiántáng 錢塘 (Hángzhōu prefecture). Member of the early-Kāngxī / Yōngzhèng Hángzhōu literary circle that included [[厲鶚]] Lì È, [[趙昱]] Zhào Yù, [[趙信]] Zhào Xìn, [[符曾]] Fú Zēng, [[吳焯]] Wú Zhuō, and [[陳芝光]] Chén Zhīguāng. Co-author with these six others of the *NánSòng záshì shī* 南宋雜事詩 [[KR4h0166]] in 7 *juǎn* — the *yǒngshǐ* (history-poems) of Southern-Sòng Hángzhōu, with each of the seven contributors producing 100 poems on Southern-Sòng historical anecdotes drawn from the Hángzhōu setting. Each poem comes with its source citations annotated below. The compilation was published c. 1729 and is the **most celebrated Hángzhōu local history-poem cycle** of the Qīng period. Shěn Jiāzhé remained a *zhūshēng* (county-school student); no further substantial works survive. No CBDB record.
