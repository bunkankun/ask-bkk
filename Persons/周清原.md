Zhōu Qīngyuán 周清原, early-Qīng official; *Gōngbù shìláng* 工部侍郎 (Vice-Minister of Works) under the Kāngxī emperor. He was the first principal compiler designated by Kāngxī to undertake the imperial *Lìdài jìshì niánbiǎo* 歷代紀事年表 ([[KR2d0019]]) project, working from the *bùyī* draft of Gōng Shìjiǒng 龔士炯. He died with the work unfinished; the project was thereafter transferred to *Nèigé xuéshì* Wáng Zhīshū 王之樞 (see [[王之樞]]), who set up the compilation bureau at Zhōu's family residence and brought Zhōu's son Zhōu Jiāzhēn 周嘉楨 in as *xié-xiū* (associate compiler). The work was completed in 1712. CBDB id 373052; no other particulars recorded.
