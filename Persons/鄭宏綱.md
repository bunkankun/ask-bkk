**Zhèng Hóng-gāng** 鄭宏綱 (*zì* Jì-mù 紀慕, *hào* Méi-jiàn 梅澗, 1727–1787), Qīng physician of Xī-pǔ 西埔 in Shè-xiàn 歙縣 (Xīn'ān 新安, modern Anhui). Hereditary laryngologist (*hóu-kē* 喉科) of four-or-more generations; senior figure of the Xīn'ān 新安 medical school. CBDB id 684510 (no dates recorded in the CBDB row; lifedates from the standard local biographical sources and modern TCM-history reference works are unanimous: 1727–1787).

Zhèng is the author of the foundational Qīng laryngology classic *Chóng-lóu yù-yuè* 重樓玉鑰 ([[KR3em017]]), in two *juǎn*, completed in manuscript during his active practice and posthumously printed in Sūzhōu in 1839 under the editorial supervision of Sūn Xué-shī 孫學詩. His younger brothers Zhèng Chéng-hàn 鄭承翰 (*hào* Shū-fú 樞扶) and Zhèng Jì-jūn 鄭既均 are his clinical co-workers and the editors of the text; the *Shū-fú-shì yuē* 樞扶氏曰 commentary throughout *juǎn shàng* is Chéng-hàn's. The *Méi-jiàn yī-yǔ* 梅澗醫語 coda in *juǎn xià* is Zhèng's own statement; it constitutes the earliest secure clinical identification in pre-modern Chinese medical literature of the epidemic later called *bái-hóu* 白喉 (diphtheria) as a distinct disease entity arising from latent heat in the *Shǎo-yīn* kidney channel, and supplies the canonical formula *Yǎng-yīn qīng-fèi tāng* 養陰清肺湯 together with twelve contraindicated materia medica. The discipline of Qīng-Republic *hóu-kē* takes its received vocabulary, three-formula core (*Zǐ-zhèng-sǎn*, *Dì-huáng-sǎn*, *Bīng-péng-sǎn*) and treatment paradigm directly from this text; the immediate supplement is [[KR3em018]] by Fāng Chéng-péi 方成培 (1838), and a critical refinement is [[KR3em020]] by Chén Bǎo-shàn 陳葆善 (1898).
