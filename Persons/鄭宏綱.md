**Zhèng Hónggāng** 鄭宏綱 (*zì* Jìmù 紀慕, *hào* Méijiàn 梅澗, 1727–1787), Qīng physician of Xīpǔ 西埔 in Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Xīn'ān 新安, modern Anhui). Hereditary laryngologist (*hóukē* 喉科) of four-or-more generations; senior figure of the Xīn'ān 新安 medical school. CBDB id 684510 (no dates recorded in the CBDB row; lifedates from the standard local biographical sources and modern TCM-history reference works are unanimous: 1727–1787).

Zhèng is the author of the foundational Qīng laryngology classic *Chónglóu yùyuè* 重樓玉鑰 ([[KR3em017]]), in two *juǎn*, completed in manuscript during his active practice and posthumously printed in Sūzhōu in 1839 under the editorial supervision of Sūn Xuéshī 孫學詩. His younger brothers Zhèng Chénghàn 鄭承翰 (*hào* Shūfú 樞扶) and Zhèng Jìjūn 鄭既均 are his clinical co-workers and the editors of the text; the *Shūfúshì yuē* 樞扶氏曰 commentary throughout *juǎn shàng* is Chénghàn's. The *Méijiàn yīyǔ* 梅澗醫語 coda in *juǎn xià* is Zhèng's own statement; it constitutes the earliest secure clinical identification in pre-modern Chinese medical literature of the epidemic later called *báihóu* 白喉 (diphtheria) as a distinct disease entity arising from latent heat in the *Shǎoyīn* kidney channel, and supplies the canonical formula *Yǎngyīn qīngfèi tāng* 養陰清肺湯 together with twelve contraindicated materia medica. The discipline of Qīng-Republic *hóukē* takes its received vocabulary, three-formula core (*Zǐzhèngsǎn*, *Dìhuángsǎn*, *Bīngpéngsǎn*) and treatment paradigm directly from this text; the immediate supplement is [[KR3em018]] by Fāng Chéngpéi 方成培 (1838), and a critical refinement is [[KR3em020]] by Chén Bǎoshàn 陳葆善 (1898).
