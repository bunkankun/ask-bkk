Màizhù 邁柱 (Manchu: Hitara Maizhu; 1670–1738), of the Hitara 喜塔臘 clan, of the Plain Yellow Banner (Manchu). His Yōngzhèng-era career: Húguǎng Governor-General (Yōngzhèng 6 = 1728 – Yōngzhèng 13 = 1735), in which capacity he supervised the *Húguǎng tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0046]]) from 1730 through to its completion in 1733. CBDB id 59432, listed under his full clan-personal name 喜塔臘邁柱; lifedates per *Qīngdài rénwù shēng-zú niánbiǎo* (#3935; index_addr 20055 = 滿洲鑲黃旗 Manchu Bordered Yellow Banner — note CBDB and *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 295 differ on banner affiliation; banner-roll evidence favours the Bordered Yellow). *Qīng shǐ gǎo* j. 295 has his biography. Posthumous title 文恭 Wéngōng.
