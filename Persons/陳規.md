Chén Guī 陳規 (1071–1141), *zì* Yuánzé 元則, posthumously *Zhōnghuì* 忠惠, was a Northern–Southern Sòng transition military commander famed for his successful defence of Déān 德安 against Jīn (Jurchen) and rebel forces during the Jīngkāng / Jiànyán years. Native of Mìzhōu 密州 (Shāndōng). His major work is the *Shǒu chéng lù* 守城錄 ([[KR3b0015]], the canonical Chinese siege-defence manual based on his Déān defence experience). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 377.
