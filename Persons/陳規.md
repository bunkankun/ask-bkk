Chén Guī 陳規 (1071–1141), *zì* Yuánzé 元則, posthumously *Zhōnghuì* 忠惠, was a Northern–Southern Sòng transition military commander famed for his successful defence of Dé-ān 德安 against Jīn (Jurchen) and rebel forces during the Jīng-kāng / Jiànyán years. Native of Mì-zhōu 密州 (Shān-dōng). His major work is the *Shǒu chéng lù* 守城錄 (KR3b0015, the canonical Chinese siege-defence manual based on his Dé-ān defence experience). Biography: *Sòng shǐ* j. 377.
