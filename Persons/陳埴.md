Chén Zhí 陳埴 (Sòng), *zì* Qìzhī 器之, was a Southern Sòng *Lǐxué* commentator of Yǒngjiā 永嘉 (Wēnzhōu, Zhèjiāng). *Jìnshì*; awarded *Tōngzhí láng* on retirement. A direct disciple of Zhū Xī's; the Yǒng-lè-period *Wǔ jīng dàquán* refers to him as *Qiánshì Chénshì* 潛室陳氏 (Mr. Chén of the Hidden Chamber). His major work is the *Mù zhōng jí* 木鍾集 ([[KR3a0056]]), a *yǔlù*-style classical commentary in 11 juan. The catalog meta date 1214 places him in the early Jiādìng era.
