Chén Zhí 陳埴 (Sòng), *zì* Qìzhī 器之, was a Southern Sòng *Lǐxué* commentator of Yǒng-jiā 永嘉 (Wēn-zhōu, Zhè-jiāng). *Jìnshì*; awarded *Tōng-zhí láng* on retirement. A direct disciple of Zhū Xī's; the Yǒng-lè-period *Wǔ jīng dà-quán* refers to him as *Qiánshì Chén-shì* 潛室陳氏 (Mr. Chén of the Hidden Chamber). His major work is the *Mù zhōng jí* 木鍾集 (KR3a0056), a *yǔ-lù*-style classical commentary in 11 juan. The catalog meta date 1214 places him in the early Jiā-dìng era.
