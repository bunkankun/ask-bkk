# Xiāo Shìyún 蕭士贇

*Zì* Cuì-kě 粹可. Native of *Níng-dū* 寧都 (modern *Níng-dū xiàn* in southern Jiāng-xī). Yuán-period scholar and accomplished poet; on close terms with Wú Chéng 吳澄 (1249–1333). Floruit early 14th century. The Sìkù compilers describe him as "*dǔ-xué gōng-shī* 篤學工詩" — devoted to learning and skilled at poetry. His principal scholarly contribution is the *shān-bǔ* (revised and supplemented) edition of Yáng Qí-xián's [[楊齊賢]] *Lǐ Tài-bái jí jí-zhù* — the resulting text is the *Lǐ Tài-bái jí fēn-lèi bǔ-zhù* [[KR4c0013]], the principal Sòng-Yuán annotated Lǐ Bái edition.

His own works — a *Shī píng* 詩評 in some 20 *piān* and a personal collection *Bīng-yái jí* 冰崖集 — are lost; only the Lǐ Bái *zhù* survives. The Sìkù tíyào singles out his judgment that the *Guǎng-wǔ zhàn-cháng huái-gǔ* 廣武戰場懷古 in the transmitted Lǐ Bái corpus is non-genuine — a judgment generally accepted in modern scholarship — as a piece of unusually clear-eyed editorial *kǎo-jù*.
