# Xiāo Shìyún 蕭士贇

*Zì* Cuìkě 粹可. Native of *Níngdū* 寧都 (modern *Níngdū xiàn* in southern Jiāngxī). Yuán-period scholar and accomplished poet; on close terms with Wú Chéng 吳澄 (1249–1333). Floruit early 14th century. The Sìkù compilers describe him as "*dǔxué gōngshī* 篤學工詩" — devoted to learning and skilled at poetry. His principal scholarly contribution is the *shānbǔ* (revised and supplemented) edition of Yáng Qíxián's [[楊齊賢]] *Lǐ Tàibái jí jízhù* — the resulting text is the *Lǐ Tàibái jí fēnlèi bǔzhù* [[KR4c0013]], the principal SòngYuán annotated Lǐ Bái edition.

His own works — a *Shī píng* 詩評 in some 20 *piān* and a personal collection *Bīngyái jí* 冰崖集 — are lost; only the Lǐ Bái *zhù* survives. The Sìkù tíyào singles out his judgment that the *Guǎngwǔ zhàncháng huáigǔ* 廣武戰場懷古 in the transmitted Lǐ Bái corpus is non-genuine — a judgment generally accepted in modern scholarship — as a piece of unusually clear-eyed editorial *kǎojù*.
