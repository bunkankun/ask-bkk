# Zhōng Shǐshēng 鍾始聲

**Lay name of [[智旭|Ǒuyì Zhìxù]] 蕅益智旭** (1599–1655), under which he published works directed at non-monastic audiences — particularly the *Pì xié jí* 闢邪集 [[KR6q0190]], his 1640s anti-Christian polemic.

The full self-designation sometimes includes *zì* Zhèn-zhī 振之 ("Zhōng Zhèn-zhī"), *zì* Jì-míng 際明 ("Zhōng Jì-míng"), or the original name Zhōng Dà-lǎng 鍾大朗 ("Zhōng Dà-lǎng"; cf. also [[大朗]]). See [[智旭]] for full biographical details.
