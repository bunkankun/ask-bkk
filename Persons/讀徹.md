Dúchè 讀徹 (active late Míng), Míng Buddhist scholar; one of the editorial collaborators on the late-Míng recension of [[KR6e0150|*Huáyán jīng hǎi yìn dào chǎng chàn yí* 華嚴經海印道場懺儀]] (X1470). Per the colophon to that work, his function was *cān yuè* 參閱 ("collation-and-review"). Birth and death dates not preserved.
