Sòng-period monk credited as compiler of the *Dìzàng cíbēi jiùkǔ jiànfú lìshēng dào-chǎng yí* 地藏慈悲救苦薦福利生道場儀 [[KR6v0064]], a four-fascicle Buddhist liturgical manual based on the *Dìzàng púsà běn-yuàn jīng* 地藏菩薩本願經 (T13n0412, trans. *Śikṣānanda* 實叉難陀). Note: this attribution is *not* secure — the famous Sòng *Vinaya* master *Língzhī Yuán-zhào* 靈芝元照 (1048–1116, of the *Tiāntái-Lǜ* school) is widely known but not closely associated with *Dìzàng*-rituals; the catalog meta gives only "Sòng" without further qualification. The ritual was preserved and used in the *Āzhālì* 阿吒力 ritual lineage of Yúnnán from at least the early Qīng (the surviving witness is a *Kāngxī* 康熙 37 = 1698 manuscript copy held by Zhào Wénhuàn 趙文煥). Whether this *Yuán-zhào* is the famous Vinaya master, a different Sòng monk, or a pseudo-attribution from the Yúnnán *Āzhālì* lineage to lend canonical authority to a regionally-composed liturgy is unresolved.

Famous Northern-Sòng Buddhist Vinaya master of the *TiāntáiLǜ* 天台律 school (the later *Lǜjiāo* 律教). Native of *Yúháng* 餘杭 (modern Hángzhōu). Initially studied under the Vinaya master *Yǔnkān* 允堪 (1005–1061) of the *Nánshānlǜ* 南山律 lineage; later established his own teaching at *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 in Hángzhōu (whence his style *Língzhī Yuánzhào* 靈芝元照). Granted the title *Dàzhì* 大智 by Sòng *Huīzōng* 徽宗. His major works on Vinaya — *Sìfēn lǜ xíngshìchāo zīchí jì* 四分律行事鈔資持記 (T40n1805's *jí*) and *Sìfēn lǜ jiémó shū jì* 四分律羯磨疏記 (T40n1808's *jí*) — became canonical for late-imperial Chinese Buddhist Vinaya scholarship. He is also a key figure in the *Pure Land* (*jìngtǔ* 淨土) revival of the late-Northern-Sòng. Whether the *Dìzàng* ritual [[KR6v0064]] is genuinely his is uncertain.
