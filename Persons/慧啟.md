# Huìqǐ 慧啟

Early-Míng Chán *ménrén* 門人 of [[普莊]] Dāiān Pǔzhuāng (1347–1403); heads the editorial byline of his master's eight-juan *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0351]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
