# 扁鵲 Biǎn Què

**Biǎn Què** 扁鵲 (legendary name; given name **Qín Yuèrén** 秦越人, c. 407 BCE – c. 310 BCE), legendary Warring-States itinerant physician of Bóhǎi 渤海 region (modern Hébei); credited as one of the founders of the Chinese pulse-diagnosis (*qiè mài* 切脈) tradition. The *Shǐjì* biography (*Biǎn Què Cāng Gōng lièzhuàn* 扁鵲倉公列傳) is the principal classical source. Many later medical works — including the *Nànjīng* 難經 and the *Biǎnquè shényìng zhēnjiǔ Yùlóng jīng* 扁鵲神應針灸玉龍經 ([[KR3ee008]]) — are pseudepigraphically attributed to him.
