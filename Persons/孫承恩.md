Sūn Chéngēn 孫承恩 (1481–1561; CBDB 129727 gives 1481, preferred over the catalog meta's 1485), *zì* **Zhēnfǔ** 貞甫, *hào* **Yìzhāi** 毅齋, posthumous *shì* **Wénjiǎn** 文簡, of **Huátíng** 華亭 (Sōngjiāngfǔ, Jiāngsū). Zhèngdé 6 / *xīnwèi* (1511) *jìnshì*; rose to *Lǐbù shàngshū jiā Tàizǐ tàibǎo*. *Míng shǐ* main biography. Zhèngdé era, in Hànlínyuàn; Jiājìng *jīngyán jiǎngguān*. As *zōngbó* (*Lǐbù shàngshū*) refused to *huángguān* (don the yellow-cap) for the *zhāigōng shèjiào* (palace-fasting-altar offerings) under Shìzōng's Daoist-rites obsession — therefore unlike **Yán Sōng** and others who used *qīngcí* (blue-prayer Daoist verse) to *zìmèi* (curry imperial favour). Cleanly retired. *Yǔ Mǎ Wénjiǎngōng* (with **Mǎ Lǐ** 馬理 of Guānzhōng, also a *Wénjiǎn*) jointly *èr zōngbó* (two ritual-ministers). With **Xú Jiē** 徐階 and **Wén Zhēngmíng** 文徵明 in the Sōngjiāng / Sūzhōu literary network. *Lù Shùshēng* 陸樹聲 wrote the preface. His writings are the *Wénjiǎn jí* in 58 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0173]]) — printed posthumously by his son **Sūn Kèhóng** 孫克弘 (Hànyáng *zhīfǔ*). CBDB id 129727, 1481–1561.
