Sūn Chéng-ēn 孫承恩 (1481–1561; CBDB 129727 gives 1481, preferred over the catalog meta's 1485), *zì* **Zhēn-fǔ** 貞甫, *hào* **Yì-zhāi** 毅齋, posthumous *shì* **Wén-jiǎn** 文簡, of **Huá-tíng** 華亭 (Sōng-jiāng-fǔ, Jiāng-sū). Zhèng-dé 6 / *xīn-wèi* (1511) *jìn-shì*; rose to *Lǐ-bù shàng-shū jiā Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo*. *Míng shǐ* main biography. Zhèng-dé era, in Hàn-lín-yuàn; Jiā-jìng *jīng-yán jiǎng-guān*. As *zōng-bó* (*Lǐ-bù shàng-shū*) refused to *huáng-guān* (don the yellow-cap) for the *zhāi-gōng shè-jiào* (palace-fasting-altar offerings) under Shì-zōng's Daoist-rites obsession — therefore unlike **Yán Sōng** and others who used *qīng-cí* (blue-prayer Daoist verse) to *zì-mèi* (curry imperial favour). Cleanly retired. *Yǔ Mǎ Wén-jiǎn-gōng* (with **Mǎ Lǐ** 馬理 of Guān-zhōng, also a *Wén-jiǎn*) jointly *èr zōng-bó* (two ritual-ministers). With **Xú Jiē** 徐階 and **Wén Zhēng-míng** 文徵明 in the Sōng-jiāng / Sū-zhōu literary network. *Lù Shù-shēng* 陸樹聲 wrote the preface. His writings are the *Wén-jiǎn jí* in 58 *juǎn* ([[KR4e0173]]) — printed posthumously by his son **Sūn Kè-hóng** 孫克弘 (Hàn-yáng *zhī-fǔ*). CBDB id 129727, 1481–1561.
