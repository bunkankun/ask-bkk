# Zì-chéng 自成

Yuán Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[正印]] Yuè-jiāng Zhèng-yìn; co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0342]]), named in *juan* 3. DILA records the teacher-student relation explicitly (A009687 → A000396). Lifedates unrecorded.
