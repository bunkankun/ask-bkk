A foreign monk known only from his attribution as the translator of the *Shíliù dà luóhàn yīnguǒ shíjiàn sòng* ([[KR6r0006]]). Modern Sanskrit reconstructions of the name vary: most often *Jñānatāra* or *Jñānatāka*. No biographical data, dates, or other translations are securely associated with him; some modern scholarship has suspected that the work is a Chinese composition disguised as a translation, in which case 闍那多迦 may be a fictive persona.
