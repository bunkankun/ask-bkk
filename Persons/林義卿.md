# 林義卿 Lín Yìqīng / Hayashi Yoshiqing

**Hayashi Yoshiqing** 林義卿 (*hào* Tōmei 東溟), mid-Edo-period Japanese kanpō physician and scholar; wrote the 1766 preface to [[管周桂]] Sugahara Shūkei's *Zhēnjiǔ xué gāngyào* 針灸學綱要 ([[KR3ee040]]). Likely connected with the *kogaku* ancient-learning current in Edo medicine.
