# Huáng Chūn 黃櫄

*Zì* Shífū 實夫. Native of Lóngxī 龍溪 (modern Zhāngzhōu 漳州, Fújiàn). Twelfth-century *Shī* scholar; lifedates not recoverable. In Chúnxī 淳熙 (1174–89) he sat the imperial *shèxuǎn* 舍選 selection examination and rose through both *jìnshì* grades; appointed Instructor of Nánjiàn-zhōu 南劍州 prefecture; final office *Xuānjiào láng* 宣教郎.

Author of *Shī jiě* 詩解 in 20 *juǎn* and a *Zǒnglùn* 總論 in 1 *juǎn*, surviving as the second layer of [[KR1c0012]] *Máoshī Lǐ-Huáng jíjiě* 毛詩李黃集解 (paired with [[李樗]] Lǐ Chū's larger *Máoshī xiángjiě* 毛詩詳解). On the *small preface* he takes the position of Wáng Ānshí 王安石 and the Chéng brothers — that the *xù* could only have been made by a sage and is therefore wholly authoritative — diametrically opposed to Lǐ Chū's Sū-Zhé-derived layered-*xù* hypothesis. The two commentaries thus complement each other in form but disagree fundamentally on the central question of Sòng *Shī* exegesis.
