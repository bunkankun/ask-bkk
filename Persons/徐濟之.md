Xú Jǐzhī 徐濟之, self-styled **"the wood-gatherer of Fāyájīng"** 發牙經樵, from *Píngyì Jīnzhōu xiāng* 平易金舟鄉. Late-Qīng Zhèjiāng literatus. Author of the 1890-dated *bá* (colophon-inscription) in elaborate parallel prose appended to [[KR3eq091]] *Yīdé jí* by **[[心禪僧]] Xīnchánsēng**, written at a *Húlín* (Hángzhōu) traveller's lodge. No standard biographical record located.
