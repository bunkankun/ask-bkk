Xú Jǐ-zhī 徐濟之, self-styled **"the wood-gatherer of Fā-yá-jīng"** 發牙經樵, from *Píng-yì Jīn-zhōu xiāng* 平易金舟鄉. Late-Qīng Zhèjiāng literatus. Author of the 1890-dated *bá* (colophon-inscription) in elaborate parallel prose appended to [[KR3eq091]] *Yī-dé jí* by **[[心禪僧]] Xīn-chán-sēng**, written at a *Hú-lín* (Háng-zhōu) traveller's lodge. No standard biographical record located.
