# 李鴻章 Lǐ Hóngzhāng

Late-Qīng statesman and reformer (1823–1901), leading figure of the *Yángwù yùndòng* 洋務運動 self-strengthening movement; younger brother of [[李瀚章]] Lǐ Hànzhāng.
