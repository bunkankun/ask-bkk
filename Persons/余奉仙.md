# 余奉仙

Yú Fèngxiān 余奉仙 (late Qīng), physician of Fùníng 阜寧 (northern Jiāngsū); father of [[余無言]] Yú Wúyán (1900–1963). Practised for sixty years and was reckoned, alongside [[趙海仙]] Zhào Hǎixiān of Xīnghuà 興化 and [[張子坪]] Zhāng Zǐpíng of Huáiān 淮安, one of the "Three Great Physicians of Northern Jiāngsū in the late Qīng". His clinical apprenticeship of his son is recorded in the biographical preface to [[KR3ep105]] *Yú Wúyán yīàn*.
