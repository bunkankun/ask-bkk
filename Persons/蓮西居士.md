# Liánxī jūshì 蓮西居士

Late-Qīng *Xiánfēng* 咸豐 era (1851–1861) lay Buddhist editor (*jūshì* 居士 = lay practitioner) of an emended printing of [[周夢顏]] Zhōu Mèngyán's *Jièshā sìshíbā wèn* 戒殺四十八問 ([[KR6k0262]], X1138). The pseudonymous lay style *Liánxī jūshì* ("Lay Householder of the West [of the] Lotus") is preserved in a parenthetical *Xiánfēng*-era anecdote interpolation in the *Xùcáng* exemplar — postdating Zhōu's death by over a century. Personal name not preserved.
