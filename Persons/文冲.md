Wénchōng 文冲 (active early-to-mid 18th century), Qing-dynasty Chán monk, *shàmén* of the Dà-yúnfēng chánsì 大雲峯禪寺 on Mount Yǒngpíng Dàoshān 永平道者山, styled Jū-huàn 居幻. Known through his editorial service as *chóng xiào biānjí* 重校編集 (re-collating and re-compiling editor) of the *Zhìjué chánshī zì xíng lù* 智覺禪師自行錄 ([[KR6q0124]], X63 n1232), the Qing-dynasty recension of [[延壽|Yǒngmíng Yánshòu]]'s 904–975 daily-practice compendium. The re-compilation was completed c. 1745 (Qiánlóng 10), under the patronage of the deceased *Dà-sīkòu* Zhāng Gōng 大司寇張公 and completed by his son Zhāng Bógēng 張伯耕.

No independent biographical material survives. No DILA authority entry. Distinct from other Chinese Buddhist figures of the name 文冲.
