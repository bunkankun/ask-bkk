Sòng-period Tiāntái 天台 monk; sobriquet **Xiāndū** 仙都 (with full name **Xiāndū Pǔróng** 仙都普容). DILA Authority A001351. Lifedates unrecorded.

Compiler of the **Tāi-zōng jīng-yīng jí** 台宗精英集 ([[KR6d0242]], X57n0966, 4 *juan*; per DILA originally 5 *juan*) — a Sòng *shān-jiā* anthology of essential Tiāntái doctrinal materials.

Note: distinct from the Yuán **Tài-xū Pǔ-róng** 太虛普容 (DILA, b. 1251) and the Qing **Yī-lún Pǔ-róng** 一輪普容 (DILA, fl. post-1661) of Pǔ-tuó-shān.
