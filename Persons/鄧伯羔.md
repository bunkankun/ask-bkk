Dèng Bógāo 鄧伯羔 (late Míng, lifedates and biographical details not known). A private Wàn-lì-era philological author. Two works survive: the *Jīn yì quán* 今易筌 (a study of the *Yìjīng*) and the *Yì gòu* 藝彀 plus *Gòu bǔ* 彀補 ([[KR3j0061]]), a six-juan evidential miscellany. The *Sìkù* editors place him in the late Wàn-lì period as a representative of solid late-Míng *kǎo-zhèng* against the dominant *kuáng Chán* 狂禪 intellectual current. Not in CBDB.
