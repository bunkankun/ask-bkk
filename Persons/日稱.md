Rì-chēng 日稱 (active mid-11th c.), Northern-Sòng Buddhist scholar-monk attached to the imperial Buddhist translation bureau (*Yìjīng-yuàn* 譯經院) in Kāifēng. Per DILA, he produced about a dozen translations during his Sòng career, including the [[KR6f0012|*Fù zǐ hé jí jīng* 父子合集經]] (T0320) — *Father-and-Son Joint Compilation Sūtra* — in 20 fascicles, in collaboration with other bureau translators. His birth and death dates are not preserved.
