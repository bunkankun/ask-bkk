**Zhāng Rǔbì** 張汝璧 (Qīng; dates uncertain, *fl.* mid-to-late Qīng) is the attributed author of the *Tiānguān tú* 天官圖 ([[KR3fa012]]), a one-*juǎn* set of star-map diagrams. He is otherwise undocumented; CBDB id 562474 has no associated dates. The Qing *Sìkù wèishōu shū jíkān* lists him as the compiler without further biographical data.
