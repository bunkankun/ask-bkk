# Xīyán 希顏

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎsì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yúān Zhìjí (1311–1378); *hào* Yòngyú 用愚, abbot of Sìmíng Tiāntóng Jǐngdé chánsì 天童景德禪寺. Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
