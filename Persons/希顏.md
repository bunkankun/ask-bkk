# Xī-yán 希顏

Late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì Chán dharma-heir (*fǎ-sì* 法嗣) of [[智及]] Yú-ān Zhì-jí (1311–1378); *hào* Yòng-yú 用愚, abbot of Sì-míng Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé chán-sì 天童景德禪寺. Co-editor of his master's *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0354]]). Lifedates unrecorded.
