Yuánfǎ 圓法, *zì* **Zhǔfēng** 主峰 ("Master Peak"), often referred to as **Sāntǎ Zhǔfēng** 三塔主峰 from his abbacy. Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jièān Wùjìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673, of the Yuānhú Miàoyòng line). Birth year unknown; died **己酉秋月** = **1669** (DILA narrows: between 1669-07-28 and 1669-08-25, Kāngxī 8). Native of **Huīzhōufǔ** 徽州府 (Ānhuī); a descendant of the **Yìngtiān Mù Guógōng** 應天沐國公 line — i.e. the **Mù Yīng** 沐英 (1344–1392) family of Míng marquises of Yúnnán. Held the rank of *lángjiāng* (郎將, military officer) before tonsure; "his mind-flower suddenly burst forth and he shaved his head." Trained under his teacher **Jīnmíng Wùjìn** 金明悟進 (= 介菴), receiving *fùfǎ*.

**Abbacy.** Sole abbacy at **Jiāxīng Sāntǎ Jǐngdé chánsì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺 (entered **癸卯二月十二日** = **1663-03-20**); death there in **1669**.

**Disciple.** **Huáiān Xīnzōng Chéngrán** 淮安心宗誠然 (DILA A041874, sole *fǎsì*).

**Work.** [[KR6q0532]] 《三塔主峰禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B412, recorded (*lù*) by his attendant **Juéhǎi** [[覺海]] *shìzhě* and prefaced by the *qǐngqǐ* (invitation petition) signed by **Tán Zhēnmò** [[譚貞默]] and other Jiāxīng gentry.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 81; 《徑石滴乳集》 vol. 5; 《介菴進禪師語錄》 (J29 B233).
