Lǐ Qiān-zhī 李謙之 (Yuán), disciple of Dīng Fù [[丁復]] and compiler of the *Kuài-tíng-jí xù-jí* 檜亭集續集 [[KR4d0499]]. The *Sìkù tíyào* writes the name **李謹之** Lǐ Jǐn-zhī, evidently a variant or scribal slip for 謙 / 謹 (both possible characters in the recommendation rosters). Confined to this editorial role in the surviving record; no separate biography.
