Wú Bǎozhī 吳寶芝 (fl. early-to-mid 18th cent.; CBDB id 578568), *gòngshēng* (tribute-student) of Shímén 石門 (in modern Tóngxiāng 桐鄉, Zhèjiāng). His sole recorded work is the *Huā mù niǎo shòu jí lèi* 花木鳥獸集類 ([[KR3k0063]]) — a 3-*juǎn* aesthetic compendium of flora and fauna allusions in the Táo Gǔ *Qīng yì lù* tradition. The work's frontispiece *chén Wú Bǎozhī gōng zuǎn* (your servant Wú Bǎozhī respectfully compiled) inscription indicates a presentation copy intended for the throne. No further biographical record has been located.
