**Sūn Zhìhóng** 孫志宏 (*hào* Táishí 臺石, *fl.* late Wànlì 萬曆 – Chóngzhēn 崇禎) — late-Míng physician active in the *Sān-Wú liǎng-Zhè* 三吳兩淛 (Suzhou-Hangzhou metropolitan) region, with his principal clinical practice at Wǔyuán 武原 / Hǎiyán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng). Disciple of Guìyán *xiān-sheng* 桂岩先生 (otherwise unidentified). Author of the eight-*juǎn* *Jiǎnmíng yī gòu* 簡明醫彀 ([[KR3er056]]), conventionally dated to *Chóngzhēn* 2 / 1629. Known only through [[施梁]] Shī Liáng's postface; not in CBDB.
