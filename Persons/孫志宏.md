**Sūn Zhì-hóng** 孫志宏 (*hào* Tái-shí 臺石, *fl.* late Wàn-lì 萬曆 – Chóng-zhēn 崇禎) — late-Míng physician active in the *Sān-Wú liǎng-Zhè* 三吳兩淛 (Suzhou-Hangzhou metropolitan) region, with his principal clinical practice at Wǔ-yuán 武原 / Hǎi-yán 海鹽 (Zhè-jiāng). Disciple of Guì-yán *xiān-sheng* 桂岩先生 (otherwise unidentified). Author of the eight-*juǎn* *Jiǎn-míng yī gòu* 簡明醫彀 ([[KR3er056]]), conventionally dated to *Chóng-zhēn* 2 / 1629. Known only through [[施梁]] Shī Liáng's postface; not in CBDB.
