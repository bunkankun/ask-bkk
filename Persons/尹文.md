**Yǐn Wén** 尹文 (*fl.* 4th century BCE) was a Warring-States thinker of the Jì xià 稷下 academy in Qí 齊, associated with the Huáng-Lǎo 黃老 tradition and the *Míng-jiā* ("School of Names"). Eponymous author of the ***Yǐn wén zǐ*** 尹文子 ([[KR5f0007]]).
