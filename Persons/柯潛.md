Kē Qián 柯潛 (1423–1473), *zì* **Mèng-shí** 孟時, *hào* **Zhú-yán** 竹巖. Native of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Quán-zhōu, Fú-jiàn). Jǐng-tài *xīn-wèi* (1451) *jìn-shì dì-yī* (*zhuàng-yuán*); office reaching *Zhān-shì-fǔ shào zhān-shì* 詹事府少詹事. In the *Hàn-lín-yuàn* compound built the **Qīng-fēng-tíng** 清風亭 and personally planted two cypresses; both gradually became canonical features of the *Hàn-lín* compound and entered cultural memory as **Kē-tíng** 柯亭 and **xué-shì-bǎi** 學士栢, the *Kē-tíng* surviving into the Qiánlóng era and entering the imperial visit-poem on the *Hàn-lín-yuàn*. *Míng shǐ* j. 153. Surviving works are [[KR4e0110]] *Zhú-yán jí* (a fragmentary manuscript-and-supplement reconstruction by the *Sìkù* editors). CBDB id 34515 (1423–1473).
