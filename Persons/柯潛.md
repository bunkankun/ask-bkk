Kē Qián 柯潛 (1423–1473), *zì* **Mèngshí** 孟時, *hào* **Zhúyán** 竹巖. Native of **Pútián** 莆田 (Quánzhōu, Fújiàn). Jǐngtài *xīnwèi* (1451) *jìnshì dìyī* (*zhuàngyuán*); office reaching *Zhānshìfǔ shào zhānshì* 詹事府少詹事. In the *Hànlínyuàn* compound built the **Qīngfēngtíng** 清風亭 and personally planted two cypresses; both gradually became canonical features of the *Hànlín* compound and entered cultural memory as **Kētíng** 柯亭 and **xuéshìbǎi** 學士栢, the *Kētíng* surviving into the Qiánlóng era and entering the imperial visit-poem on the *Hànlínyuàn*. *Míng shǐ* j. 153. Surviving works are [[KR4e0110]] *Zhúyán jí* (a fragmentary manuscript-and-supplement reconstruction by the *Sìkù* editors). CBDB id 34515 (1423–1473).
