Zàishēn Hóngzàn 在犙弘贊 (1612–1686), late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng 曹洞宗 monk, best known as a major Vinaya specialist and prolific author of precept-observance, Pure Land devotion, and scholastic-commentarial works. *Hào* Zàishēn 在犙 ("Residing-in-Sanity"). Native of Xīnhuì 新會 (Guǎngdōng), lay surname Zhū 朱.

Initially a Confucian scholar — Hóngzàn was a student of the *gōngshēng* 貢生 Yú Jíshēng 余集生 and *xian* 縣 *xué* 學 *shēng-yuán* 生員 before the Ming fall. After the 1644 Ming-Qing transition Hóngzàn retreated into monastic life, studying Chán under Dǐnghú Dàoqiū 鼎湖道邱 and receiving the Cáodòng transmission. First residency at the Bǎoxiàng-lín 寶象林 in Guǎngzhōu; subsequently succeeded Dàoqiū as abbot of the Dǐnghú-shān Qìngyún sì 鼎湖山慶雲寺 in Zhàoqìng 肇慶, which became his principal residence for the remainder of his life.

Hóngzàn's doctrinal orientation was strongly critical of the late-Ming / early-Qing factionalised Chán scene, which he viewed as given over to "*fú-kuā bù-shí* 浮誇不實" ("frothy boasting without substance"). He accordingly refused to teach Chán-polemical material and devoted himself to Vinaya observance and monastic-regulatory discipline. His enormous written corpus — several dozen titles — is centred on precept and ritual observance:

- *Sì fēn jiè běn rú shì* 四分戒本如釋 (12 juan) — detailed commentary on the *Sìfēn lǜ* precept text;
- *Guī jiè yào jí* 歸戒要集 (3 juan) — essentials of precept-taking;
- *Bā guān zhāi fǎ* 八關齋法 — manual on the eight-precept lay retreat;
- *Lǐ fó yí shì* 禮佛儀式 — Buddha-worship ritual manual;
- *Qī jù zhī Fó-mǔ suǒ shuō Zhǔn tí tuóluóní jīng huì shì* 七俱胝佛母所說準提陀羅尼經會釋 (3 juan) — commentary on the *Cuṇḍā dhāraṇī sūtra*;
- *Xīn jīng lùn* 心經論 — commentary on the *Heart Sūtra*;
- *Dǐnghú-shān Mù-rén jū Zàishēn chánshī cà gǎo* 鼎湖山木人居在犙禪師剎稿 (5 juan) — his collected miscellaneous prose;
- *Wéishān jǐngcè jù shì jì* 溈山警策句釋記 ([[KR6q0132]], X63 n1240) — his commentary on Wéishān Língyòu's *Guīshān jǐngcè*.

Died on Kāngxī 25.5.22 (12 July 1686), aged 75.
