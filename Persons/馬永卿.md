Mǎ Yǒngqīng 馬永卿 (*fl.* 1109; CBDB id 33866), *zì* Dànián 大年, self-styled *Lǎnzhēnzǐ* 嬾真子, was a Northern–Southern Sòng-transition official and *bǐjì* author. A native of Yángzhōu 揚州, he later took up residence in Yánshān 鉛山 (modern Jiāngxī). *Jìnshì* of *Dàguān* 3 (1109) per the *Guǎngxìnfǔ zhì*. He served as *chéng* of Jiāngdū 江都, *lìng* of Zhèchuān 浙川, *lìng* of Xiàxiàn 夏縣, and an unrecorded office in Guānzhōng (the office-record list reconstructed by the *Sìkù* editors from his own *Lǎnzhēnzǐ*). On taking up the *zhǔbù* of Yǒngchéng 永城 county in late 1109 he became the principal disciple-recorder of the Northern Sòng remonstrance official [[劉安世]] (Liú Ānshì, 1048–1125), then in retirement at Yǒngchéng. He attended on Liú for more than a year, and continued to visit him after Liú moved to Nánjīng. His two surviving works are the *Yuánchéng yǔ lù* 元城語錄 ([[KR3j0102]]; the recension passed down with [[王崇慶]]'s Míng *jiě* commentary as *Yuánchéng yǔ lù jiě*), composed *c.* 1135 (preface dated *Shàoxīng* 5 = 1135), and the *Lǎnzhēnzǐ* 嬾真子 ([[KR3j0103]]) in 5 *juàn*, completed at *Shàoxīng* 6 = 1136. He is one of the principal late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng transmitters of Sīmǎ Guāng's school via Liú Ānshì.
