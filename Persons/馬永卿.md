Mǎ Yǒng-qīng 馬永卿 (*fl.* 1109; CBDB id 33866), *zì* Dà-nián 大年, self-styled *Lǎn-zhēn-zǐ* 嬾真子, was a Northern–Southern Sòng-transition official and *bǐjì* author. A native of Yáng-zhōu 揚州, he later took up residence in Yán-shān 鉛山 (modern Jiāng-xī). *Jìnshì* of *Dà-guān* 3 (1109) per the *Guǎng-xìn-fǔ zhì*. He served as *chéng* of Jiāng-dū 江都, *lìng* of Zhè-chuān 浙川, *lìng* of Xià-xiàn 夏縣, and an unrecorded office in Guān-zhōng (the office-record list reconstructed by the *Sì-kù* editors from his own *Lǎn-zhēn-zǐ*). On taking up the *zhǔbù* of Yǒng-chéng 永城 county in late 1109 he became the principal disciple-recorder of the Northern Sòng remonstrance official [[劉安世]] (Liú Ān-shì, 1048–1125), then in retirement at Yǒng-chéng. He attended on Liú for more than a year, and continued to visit him after Liú moved to Nán-jīng. His two surviving works are the *Yuán-chéng yǔ lù* 元城語錄 ([[KR3j0102]]; the recension passed down with [[王崇慶]]'s Míng *jiě* commentary as *Yuán-chéng yǔ lù jiě*), composed *c.* 1135 (preface dated *Shào-xīng* 5 = 1135), and the *Lǎn-zhēn-zǐ* 嬾真子 ([[KR3j0103]]) in 5 *juàn*, completed at *Shào-xīng* 6 = 1136. He is one of the principal late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng transmitters of Sī-mǎ Guāng's school via Liú Ān-shì.
