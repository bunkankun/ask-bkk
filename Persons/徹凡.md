Chè-fán 徹凡. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎ-sì* of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** [[超見]] (1614–1688) at **Pán-shān** 盤山. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Xīn-yuè** [[心月]] of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0586]] 《盤山了宗禪師語錄》 4 juan, J40 B474.
