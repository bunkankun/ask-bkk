Chèfán 徹凡. Línjì-Yángqí *fǎsì* of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** [[超見]] (1614–1688) at **Pánshān** 盤山. Lifedates unknown. Co-recorder (*děng lù*) with **Xīnyuè** [[心月]] of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0586]] 《盤山了宗禪師語錄》 4 juan, J40 B474.
