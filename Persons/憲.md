# Xiàn 憲 (Shēnfēng Xiàn 屾峰憲)

*Hào* **Shēnfēng** 屾峰 (also written 艸峰 in DILA orthography). Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665) — same teacher as [[淨挺]] Liángtíng Jìngtǐng (at [[KR6q0471]]) and [[淨啟]] Jìngqǐ (at [[KR6q0459]]). Lifedates not preserved. Native and lay-life background not located.

**Five abbacies.** **Zuìlǐ Gǔ Zīshèngsì** 檇李古資聖寺 (Jiāxīng); **Qiánxī Gǔ Qíchāngsì** 前溪古齊昌寺; **Dōngshān Mìaojuéyuàn** 東山妙覺院 (Hángzhōu); **Guānyīnyuàn** 觀音院; **Yúnmén Xiǎnshèngsì** 雲門顯聖寺. **Heirs**: **Zhìzhí** [[智質]] and **Zhìyuán** [[智原]] — co-compilers of the *yǔlù*.

**Work.** [[KR6q0472]] 《屾峰憲禪師語錄》 10 juan, Jiāxīng Canon J34 B298, compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìzhí** [[智質]] + **Zhìyuán** [[智原]] together. Sources: [[KR6q0472]] juan 1–5 *passim*; juan 9 *Yúān lǎohéshàng xiàng* 愚菴老和尚像 verse referencing his teacher Sānyí; DILA authority A001774.
