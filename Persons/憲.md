# Xiàn 憲 (Shēn-fēng Xiàn 屾峰憲)

*Hào* **Shēn-fēng** 屾峰 (also written 艸峰 in DILA orthography). Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665) — same teacher as [[淨挺]] Liáng-tíng Jìng-tǐng (at [[KR6q0471]]) and [[淨啟]] Jìng-qǐ (at [[KR6q0459]]). Lifedates not preserved. Native and lay-life background not located.

**Five abbacies.** **Zuì-lǐ Gǔ Zī-shèng-sì** 檇李古資聖寺 (Jiā-xīng); **Qián-xī Gǔ Qí-chāng-sì** 前溪古齊昌寺; **Dōng-shān Mìao-jué-yuàn** 東山妙覺院 (Háng-zhōu); **Guān-yīn-yuàn** 觀音院; **Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng-sì** 雲門顯聖寺. **Heirs**: **Zhì-zhí** [[智質]] and **Zhì-yuán** [[智原]] — co-compilers of the *yǔ-lù*.

**Work.** [[KR6q0472]] 《屾峰憲禪師語錄》 10 juan, Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B298, compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-zhí** [[智質]] + **Zhì-yuán** [[智原]] together. Sources: [[KR6q0472]] juan 1–5 *passim*; juan 9 *Yú-ān lǎo-héshàng xiàng* 愚菴老和尚像 verse referencing his teacher Sān-yí; DILA authority A001774.
