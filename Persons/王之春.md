# 王之春

Wáng Zhīchūn 王之春 (1842–1906), of Qīngquán 清泉 (Húnán). Late-Qīng official and reform-era statesman who served as governor of Ānhuī and Guǎngxī. Author of the preface to [[KR3ep094]] *Chóngshítáng yīàn*.
