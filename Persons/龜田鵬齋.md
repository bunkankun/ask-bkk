**Kameda Bōsai** 龜田鵬齋 (also Kameda Hōsai; given name 長興 Chōkō; 1752–1826), Edo-period Japanese Confucian scholar, calligrapher, and *kǎozhèng*-philological teacher. Founder of the Kameda line of philological-Confucian instruction in Edo. Trained the young [[尾臺榕堂]] Odai Yōdō in classical-Confucian textual scholarship, alongside Odai's medical study under the Yoshimasu Tōdō *kohōha* lineage. A leading figure of late-Edo *eclectic* Confucian scholarship rejecting both Sōrai 徂徠 and Sōng-Xué orthodoxies.
