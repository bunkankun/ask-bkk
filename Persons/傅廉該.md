**Fù Liángāi** 傅廉該, Qīng-period official, Provincial Censor of Qīnghé 清河, elder colleague of [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ and instrumental in the publication of Chén's *Nǚkē yàozhǐ* 女科要旨 ([[KR3ei008]]); requested Chén's supplementary section on *yīntǐng* 陰挺 (uterine prolapse).
