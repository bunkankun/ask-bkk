# 金保三

Jīn Bǎosān of Wúzhōng 吳中 (Sūzhōu), late-Qīng physician who acquired the manuscript of [[KR3em023]] *Jiāoshì hóukē zhěnmì* 焦氏喉科枕秘 and arranged its *editio princeps* in *Tóngzhì* 7 (1868) with the assistance of [[孫雲齋]] Sūn Yúnzhāi.
