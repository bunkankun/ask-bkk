Dào-chóng 道崇. Personal attendant (*shì-zhě* 侍者) of **Tiān-zé Jī-néng** [[機能]] (b. 1618). Co-recorder (*tóng lù*, with [[續彌]]) of [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B417, prefaced 1677. Lifedates unknown.

- - - - -

Several other Línjì-Yángqí monks also bear the name **Dào-chóng** 道崇:
- **Tiān-yǐn Dào-chóng** 天隱道崇 (DILA A026288), Línjì-Yángqí, dharma-heir of **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** 敏樹如相 [[如相]] (1603–1672), native of **Diàn-jiāng** 墊江 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Bì** 畢; styled **Nán-bīn-zōng** 南濱宗. Lead compiler (*biān*) of his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0564]] 《敏樹禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B450.
- **Míng-yuè Dào-chóng** 明月道崇 (DILA A027040), Táng-dynasty Wěi-yǎng 溈仰-school monk, dharma-heir of **Yǎng-shān Huì-jì** 仰山慧寂.
