Dàochóng 道崇. Personal attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) of **Tiānzé Jīnéng** [[機能]] (b. 1618). Co-recorder (*tóng lù*, with [[續彌]]) of [[KR6q0536]] 《天則能禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B417, prefaced 1677. Lifedates unknown.

- - - - -

Several other Línjì-Yángqí monks also bear the name **Dàochóng** 道崇:
- **Tiānyǐn Dàochóng** 天隱道崇 (DILA A026288), Línjì-Yángqí, dharma-heir of **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** 敏樹如相 [[如相]] (1603–1672), native of **Diànjiāng** 墊江 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Bì** 畢; styled **Nánbīnzōng** 南濱宗. Lead compiler (*biān*) of his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0564]] 《敏樹禪師語錄》 10 juan, J39 B450.
- **Míngyuè Dàochóng** 明月道崇 (DILA A027040), Táng-dynasty Wěiyǎng 溈仰-school monk, dharma-heir of **Yǎngshān Huìjì** 仰山慧寂.
