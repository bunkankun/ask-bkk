**Zhāng Xūgǔ** 章虛谷 (Jiāqìng–Dàoguāng era, 清), *míng* Nán 楠, mid-Qīng physician and annotator of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì's *Wēnrè lùn* ([[KR3eg001]]) and [[薛雪]] Xuē Shēngbái's *Shīrè tiáobiàn* ([[KR3eg020]]). His commentary was incorporated into the late-Qīng *Nánbìng biéjiàn* ([[KR3eg029]]) by [[宋兆淇]] Sòng Zhàoqí; he is also known for the *Yīmén bàng hézhì* 醫門棒喝 (Qīng pedagogical anthology).
