**Zhōu Yángjùn** 周揚俊 (*zì* Yǔ-zǎi 禹載, *fl.* late seventeenth century, 清), early-Qīng physician of Sū-zhōu 蘇州. A *gòng-shēng* 貢生 turned physician, Zhōu was active in the Kāngxī era and is best known for his contribution to the **Yuán-Míng-Qīng *Jīnguì* commentary line**: he supplemented the earlier Yuán-period **Zhào Yǐdé** 趙以德 *Jīnguì yùhán jīng yǎn yì* 金匱玉函經衍義 commentary with additional annotations, producing the *Jīnguì yùhán jīng èr zhù* 金匱玉函經二註 ([[KR3ef083]], "Two Annotators on the *Jīnguì yùhán jīng*", printed 1687) — the "two annotators" being Zhào and Zhōu themselves. Zhōu was also the author of *Wēn rè shǔ yì quán shū* 溫熱暑疫全書 (a treatise on warm-disease and epidemic disorders) and *Sì míng xīn yào* 四明心要. Detailed lifedates are not preserved. He is one of the principal early-Qīng Sū-zhōu physicians and a major influence on the [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì *wēn-bìng* synthesis.
