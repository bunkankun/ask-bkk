**Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ** 蔣予蒲 (1756–1819), *zì* **Yuán-tíng** 元庭, *hào* **Mèng-yīn** 夢因 — native of **Suī-yáng** 睢陽 (modern Shāng-qiū, Hé-nán). Mid-Qīng *jìn-shì* (1779), official rising to *Tài-pú-sì shǎo-qīng* 太僕寺少卿, and **the principal compiler of the *Dào zàng jí yào* 道藏輯要** in its first edition (1809). Jiǎng was a deeply-committed Lóng-mén Daoist who developed the DZJY editorial project in collaboration with the **Èr xiān ān** 二仙菴 in Chéng-dū, drawing on the planchette-revealed corpus of the Lǚ-zǔ-circle (cf. [[柳守元]], [[劉體恕]]). His *Dào zàng jí yào* (1809) is the principal vehicle through which the late-imperial Lǚ-zǔ-cult planchette literature entered the Daoist canonical record. As editor-reviewer he appears frequently in DZJY scriptures as *Suī-yáng Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ Mèng-yīn chóng dìng* ("Suī-yáng Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ Mèng-yīn re-set"), e.g. at [[KR5i0085]] *Yuán huáng dà dào zhēn jūn jiù jié bǎo jīng* and [[KR5i0087]] *Yīn zhì wén zhù*. Standard reference: Mori Yuria, "*Daozang jiyao* and Quanzhen Daoism in the Qing Dynasty"; Esposito, *Facets of Qing Daoism*. CBDB ID 59100.
