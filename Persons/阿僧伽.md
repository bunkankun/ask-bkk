# Āsēngjiā 阿僧伽 (= [[無著菩薩]])

A direct transliteration of Sanskrit *Asaṅga* (\*"Unattached"); this is the same Indian Yogācāra master conventionally rendered as Wú-zhuó púsà 無著菩薩 in the Xuánzàng-Cí'ēn translation tradition. The transliteration *Āsēngjiā* 阿僧伽 is preferred in the earlier Yè / Luòyáng translation circle, especially in the [[KR6n0058]] *Shè dà-shèng lùn* 攝大乘論 by [[佛陀扇多]] (T31n1592, c. 531), where the Indian-name form was retained rather than calqued into Chinese.

For the full biography see [[無著菩薩]] (per DILA Buddhist Person Authority A001022).
