# Lǐ Qí 李琪

Style name *Kāibó* 開伯. Native of Wújùn 吳郡 (modern Sūzhōu). Active in the late Southern Sòng (Jiādìng era, c. 1208–1224). Held the post of *Guózǐ sīyè* 國子司業 (Vice Director of the Imperial Academy). The SKQS *tíyào* (under [[KR1e0049]]) gives no further career details. CBDB id 3473 records his floruit as 1196–1211.

His one surviving work is the *Chūnqiū wáng bà liè guó shì jì biān* 春秋王霸列國世紀編 ([[KR1e0049]]), completed in Jiādìng *xīnwèi* (1211). The work is a state-by-state historical reorganisation of the *Chūnqiū* with implicit contemporary political application — particularly to the Sòng-Jīn relationship and the failure of the *běiyuán* restoration programme. The SKQS editors are explicitly cool toward the work's interpretive merits, registering it as a documentary witness to "the Southern Sòng's progressive weakness, the literati class still leaning on classical texts to manufacture rhetoric for self-justification — a sign of the dynasty's secular decline."
