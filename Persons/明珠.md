Míngzhū 明珠 (1635–1708), of the Yèhè Nàlán 葉赫那拉 (Plain-Yellow Banner) Manchu clan, leading Kāngxī-period statesman and Grand Secretary; father of the *cí*-poet [[納喇性德]] Nàlǎ Xìngdé 性德 (1655–1685). The *Sìkù* notice on [[KR1a0139]] explicitly invokes the late-Kāngxī Míngzhū faction as the political context in which [[徐乾學]] Xú Qiánxué attached his *Tōngzhì táng jīngjiě* compilation to Míngzhū's son's name as a vehicle of patronage-currying.
