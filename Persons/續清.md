Xù-qīng 續清, *zì* **Zhàn-yī** 湛一 ("Single Translucent"); also referred to as **Yíng-ēn Qīng** 迎恩清 from his abbacy at **Huì-chuān Yíng-ēn-sì** 會川迎恩寺. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tǐ-zōng Dào-níng** [[寧]] (1598–1669) at **Yún-fēng-sì** 雲峰寺. Lifedates unknown. Native of **Shǔ-nán Yǒng-níng** 蜀南永寧 (Sì-chuān); lay surname **Wáng** 王. Lead compiler (*děng biān*) of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0548]] 《雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄》 1 juan, J38 B431.

**Sources.** 《五燈全書》 vol. 88 (X82 n1571 p. 492a22); 《錦江禪燈》 (X85 n1590 p. 189c05).
