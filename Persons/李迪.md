Lǐ Dí 李迪 (fl. early 18th century; lifedates not transmitted in the standard sources). Engaged by the Gānsù governor Xǔ Róng 許容 as principal *biānzuǎn* of the foundational *Gānsù tōngzhì* ([[KR2k0051]]), serving from approximately 1729 through to the work's completion in Qiánlóng 1 (1736). CBDB id 358316 — one of several Lǐ Dí entries in CBDB; this is the only one tagged for the Qīng dynasty. Not to be confused with the Northern-Sòng grand councillor Lǐ Dí 李迪 (971–1047, CBDB 1096), with whom there is no connection beyond name.

Lǐ Dí 李迪 (971–1047), *zì* Fùgǔ 復古, native of Pú-chéng 濮城. Northern-Sòng grand councillor, *zhuàngyuán* of Jǐng-dé 4 (1007); a leading figure of Zhēnzōng's and Rénzōng's reigns, twice serving as chief minister. *Sòng shǐ* j. 310 has his biography. CBDB id 1096; lifedates standard. Distinguished here from the Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng *Gānsù tōngzhì* compiler above.
