# Zhōu Chí 周馳

Yuán-period lay scholar of Liáochéng, style *Yùcén xiūxiū jūshì* 玉岑休休居士; supplied the 1305 second Yuán preface to the [[KR6q0078]] *Bìyán lù* and assisted [[張煒]] Zhāng Wěi's recutting of the woodblocks.
