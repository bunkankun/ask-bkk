**Wáng Fěng** 王諷 (*hào* **Yù-fēng-zǐ** 玉峰子), mid-Míng *xuě-zhě* / physician-scholar of **Yù-fēng** 玉峰 (a place-name in southern Jiāngsū, plausibly Kūn-shān / Yù-fēng-zhèn), *fl.* mid-1530s. Author of the supplementary preface (1534) to [[戴思恭]]'s **[[KR3eq026]]** *Tuī-qiú shī-yì* 推求師意, with the doctrinal positioning of the work as the legitimate transmission line *Zhū Dānxī → Dài Yuán-lǐ → [[汪機]] Wāng Shí-shān → Wáng Yù-fēng*. The preface is one of the better mid-Míng statements of the Dānxī-school transmission narrative and of the *míng huì* 冥會 ("mysterious-comprehension") discipular hermeneutic. Not in CBDB.
