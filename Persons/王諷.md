**Wáng Fěng** 王諷 (*hào* **Yùfēngzǐ** 玉峰子), mid-Míng *xuězhě* / physician-scholar of **Yùfēng** 玉峰 (a place-name in southern Jiāngsū, plausibly Kūnshān / Yùfēngzhèn), *fl.* mid-1530s. Author of the supplementary preface (1534) to [[戴思恭]]'s **[[KR3eq026]]** *Tuīqiú shīyì* 推求師意, with the doctrinal positioning of the work as the legitimate transmission line *Zhū Dānxī → Dài Yuánlǐ → [[汪機]] Wāng Shíshān → Wáng Yùfēng*. The preface is one of the better mid-Míng statements of the Dānxī-school transmission narrative and of the *míng huì* 冥會 ("mysterious-comprehension") discipular hermeneutic. Not in CBDB.
