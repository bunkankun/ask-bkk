**Lǐ Zhōuhàn** 李周翰 (fl. 718) was a Tang scholar, one of the five compilers of the *Wǔchén jízhù Wén xuǎn* 五臣集註文選 assembled under Lǚ Yánzuò 呂延祚 and presented to Tang Xuánzōng in Kāiyuán 6 (718). His commentary appears in [[KR4h0003]]. He is otherwise unattested in the standard histories or biographical sources; CBDB has no entry. Nothing further of his life is known.
