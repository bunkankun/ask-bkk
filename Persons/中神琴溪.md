**Nakagami Kinkei** 中神琴溪 (1744–1833), late-Edo Kyoto physician of the Yoshimasu Tōdō 吉益東洞 *kohōha* 古方派 lineage. Founder of the *Shēngshēngtáng* 生生堂 clinic in Kyoto and one of the principal second-generation *kohōha* teachers, training pupils from across Japan. Author of the *Shēngshēngtáng zhìyàn* 生生堂治驗 ([[KR3eq055]]) — clinical case-records compiled by his pupil Ono Son. His teaching is notable for the methodologically flexible application of *kohōha* principles, including the willingness to use emetic and warming therapies inverted from the standard Yoshimasu-school purgative default. Cataloged under 江戶.
