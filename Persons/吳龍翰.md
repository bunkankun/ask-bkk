Wú Lónghàn 吳龍翰 (1233–1293), *zì* Shìxián 式賢, *hào* Gǔméi 古梅 (after an old plum at his Shèxiàn residence), native of Shèxiàn 歙縣 in Huīzhōu 徽州. He passed the local *xiānggòng* in the Sòng Xiánchún era and was appointed by recommendation as *biānjiào* (collator) of writings in the Imperial Historiographical Office and the Veritable Records Office. After the Sòng surrender in 1276, the Huīzhōu prefectural school invited him to serve as Instructor; he soon resigned and lived out his life as a private literatus. A *jiānghú* poet attached to the circle of Liú Kèzhuāng [[劉克莊]], Fāng Yuè 方岳 (*hào* Qiūyá 秋崖) and Fāng Huí [[方回]], with marked interest in inner-and-outer alchemy (compared by the *Sìkù* editors to Yú Yǎn 俞琰). He is conventionally credited as the first to scale the Tiāndū peak of Huángshān (1242, with Bào Yúnlóng 鮑雲龍 and Sòng Fù 宋復) — recorded in his preface to the *Huángshān jìyóu*. His surviving works are collected in the *Gǔméi yígǎo* [[KR4d0397]]. CBDB person 35283.
