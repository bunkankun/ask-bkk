# 北峰宗印
Běifēng Zōngyìn (1148–1213)

A late-Southern-Sòng Tiāntái master, dharma-teacher of Gǔyún Yuáncuì 古雲元粹 ([[元粹]]) — the author of the *Sìjiào yí bèishì* ([[KR6d0170]]) — and a significant transmitter of the late-Sòng *shānjiā* Tiāntái scholastic tradition.
