Dàjiàn 大建 (lifedates unrecorded), Ming-dynasty Buddhist monk-editor, compiler-collator (*jiào* 較) of the *Chánlín bǎoxùn yīn yì* 禪林寶訓音義 ([[KR6q0153]], X64 n1262) — a *yīn yì* 音義 ("sound-and-meaning") philological apparatus for the Southern-Sòng pastoral-admonition compilation [[KR6q0099]] *Chánlín bǎoxùn*.

No independent biographical material survives; no DILA authority entry identified under this specific identity. Editorial role on [[KR6q0153]] only.
