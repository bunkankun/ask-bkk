# Xuē Jiǎn 薛簡

Táng court official; dispatched by emperor Zhōngzōng (or per other accounts Zé Tiān) to convey an imperial summons to the Sixth Patriarch [[惠能]] Huìnéng at Cáoxī, the encounter forming a familiar set-piece in the *Liùzǔ tán jīng* and reworked in the *Dìwáng wèndào lù* selection of *Xiānjué zōngshèng* ([[KR6q0052]]).
