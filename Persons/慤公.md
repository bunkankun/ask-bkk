# Quègōng 慤公

Tang-period monk Chóngfú Quègōng 崇福慤公 (likely Wéiquè 惟慤), traditionally regarded as the author of the first known commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra, transmitted from the original translation circle of Fáng Róng 房融.
