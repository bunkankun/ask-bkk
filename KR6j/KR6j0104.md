# Jīngāngdǐng jīng yújiā guān-zì-zài wáng rúlái xiūxíng fǎ 金剛頂經瑜伽觀自在王如來修行法
*Method of Yoga-Practice of the Avalokiteśvara-King Tathāgata from the Vajraśekhara-sūtra*
by [[金剛智]] (Vajrabodhi, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *yoga* practice manual by **Vajrabodhi** ([[金剛智]]) for the **Avalokiteśvara-King** (Vajradhātu Lotus-family principal) practice. Companion to KR6j0103 (Amoghavajra's later version of the same practice), this Vajrabodhi text is the **earlier Tang** rendering of the practice.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text establishes Vajrabodhi's earlier Tang version of the Vajradhātu Avalokiteśvara-King practice. The two recensions (Vajrabodhi's KR6j0104 and Amoghavajra's KR6j0103) document the development of the Tang Esoteric Avalokiteśvara practice tradition through two generations of master-translators.

The translation dates from Vajrabodhi's Cháng-ān period (720–741).
