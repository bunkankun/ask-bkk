# Wúliàngshòu rúlái guānxíng gòngyǎng yíguǐ 無量壽如來觀行供養儀軌
*Visualisation-Practice and Offering Ritual Manual for Amitāyus Tathāgata*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the practice of **Amitāyus** 無量壽 (literally "Limitless-Life" — the cosmic-life aspect of *AmitābhaAmitāyus*, the Buddha of the western Pure Land *Sukhāvatī*). The text presents the Esoteric framework for the popular Pure Land devotional practice.

### Prefaces


### Abstract

The text establishes the Esoteric framework for the Amitāyus practice: the *mudrā*-mantra-visualisation triplets, the offering sequences, the *japa* of the Amitāyus *mūla-mantra* (the *Wúliàngshòu chéngfó zhēnyán* — Skt. *amṛta-bhava* mantra) and ancillary mantras, and the **visualisation of Amitāyus and the Sukhāvatī Pure Land**. The text is a foundational work in the **Esoteric-Pure Land synthesis** that became widely influential in East Asian Buddhism — integrating the Pure Land devotional cult (centred on rebirth in Sukhāvatī) with the Esoteric Vajra-mandalic framework.

The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774).
