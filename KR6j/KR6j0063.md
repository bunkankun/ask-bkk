# Sūxīdì jiéluó gòngyǎng fǎ 蘇悉地羯羅供養法
*Method of Offering of the Susiddhikara* (recension A, 3 fascicles)
by [[善無畏]] (Śubhakarasiṃha, 譯)

## About the work

A three-fascicle ritual offering manual by **Śubhakarasiṃha** ([[善無畏]]) accompanying the *Susiddhikara-sūtra* ([[KR6j0061]], T18n0893a). The text codifies the *gōngyǎng* (offering / *pūjā*) sequences for the *Susiddhikara* tradition. It is one of two parallel recensions of the same ritual codification (companion to T894b = [[KR6j0064]] in two-fascicle form).

### Prefaces


### Abstract

The *Gōngyǎng fǎ* gives the systematic offering-ritual structure for the *Susiddhikara* practice — the *kriyā*-tantra offering sequence covering the preparation of substances, the *mudrā*-mantra-visualisation sequences for each offering, and the merit-dedication. The text is a working ritual manual paired with the foundational *Susiddhikara-sūtra* itself.

The translation dates from Śubhakarasiṃha's mature Cháng-ān period (724–735).
