# Fódǐng zūnshèng tuóluóní jīng 佛頂尊勝陀羅尼經 (Dù Xíngyǐ recension)
*Sūtra of the Buddha-Crown Victorious Dhāraṇī* (Skt. *Uṣṇīṣavijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra*)
by [[杜行顗]] (Dù Xíngyǐ, 譯), with [[地婆訶羅]] (Divākara) and Dùpó 度婆 as collaborators

## About the work

A one-fascicle parallel translation of the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra* by the Tang lay translator **Dù Xíngyǐ** ([[杜行顗]]) at Cháng-ān in **679 CE** (Yífèng 4 / 1st month) — preceding Buddhapāli's translation (KR6j0144) by four years. The translation was a collaborative work involving the Indian *bhikṣu* **Dùpó** 度婆 and the Tripiṭaka-master **Divākara** ([[地婆訶羅]]) as Sanskrit-verifier.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The Dù Xíngyǐ recension is the **earliest** of the four Tang Chinese translations of the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra*. According to the *Kāiyuán shìjiào lù*, the work was undertaken by imperial decree of Emperor Gāozōng after Buddhapāli had presented the Sanskrit manuscript at the imperial court. The Dù Xíngyǐ recension and the later Buddhapāli recension (T967) are the two principal early Tang witnesses; the later Divākara translations (T969 = KR6j0146, T970 = KR6j0147) and Yìjìng's translation (T971 = KR6j0148) round out the Tang witnesses.

The translation date is **Yífèng 4 = 679 CE**.
