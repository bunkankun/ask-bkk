# Zuìshèng fódǐng tuóluóní jìng-chú yèzhàng zhòu jīng 最勝佛頂陀羅尼淨除業障呪經
*Sūtra of the Most-Victorious Buddha-Crown Dhāraṇī Mantra for Purifying Karmic Obstructions* (recension B of Divākara's *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra*)
by [[地婆訶羅]] (Divākara, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle parallel translation by **Divākara** ([[地婆訶羅]]), companion to his own KR6j0146 (T969). The title elaborates the *karma-purifying* function of the *dhāraṇī*.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

T970 is Divākara's second translation of the same Sanskrit *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī-sūtra* tradition. The two parallel Divākara translations may reflect successive editorial passes by the translator; together with Buddhapāli's T967, Dù Xíngyǐ's T968, and Yìjìng's T971, they constitute the **five canonical Tang translations** of this most-popular *dhāraṇī*. The translation dates from Divākara's Cháng-ān period (676–688).
