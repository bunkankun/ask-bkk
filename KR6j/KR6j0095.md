# Yàoshī rúlái niànsòng yíguǐ 藥師如來念誦儀軌 (recension B)
*Recitation Ritual Manual for the Medicine-Master Tathāgata* (recension B)
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

The parallel recension B to KR6j0094 (T924A) — the alternate Amoghavajra recension of the Bhaiṣajyaguru ritual manual.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

T924B preserves a parallel arrangement of the same Tang Esoteric Bhaiṣajyaguru ritual material as T924A, with editorial differences in the specific *mudrā*-mantra prescriptions. Both recensions remain canonical and were preserved through the manuscript transmission as legitimate variant traditions.

The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
