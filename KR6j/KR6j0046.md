# Jīngāngdǐng yújiā sānshíqīzūn lǐ 金剛頂瑜伽三十七尊禮
*Liturgy of Homage to the Thirty-Seven Worthies of the Vajraśekhara-yoga*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A short one-fascicle liturgical-homage text by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the 37 Vajradhātu deities — companion to [[KR6j0045]] (the *lǐchàn wén*). Where [[KR6j0045]] is a full penitential liturgy with confession-and-homage components, [[KR6j0046]] is a focused **homage-only liturgy** (*lǐ* 禮) — a shorter daily devotional text addressed sequentially to the 37 deities.

### Abstract

The *Sānshíqīzūn lǐ* gives a sequential homage-formula for the 37 Vajradhātu deities — the practitioner makes prostration to each deity in turn, with the deity's epithet, *bīja*, and brief invocation. The text is the shortest of the Vajradhātu liturgical compositions and was used as a portable daily-devotion text alongside the longer [[KR6j0045]] *lǐchàn*.

The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774). The text became a standard daily-practice text in Japanese Shingon and Tendai-Esoteric traditions.
