# Fó shuō dàshèng shèng wúliàngshòu juédìng guāngmíng wáng rúlái tuóluóní jīng 佛說大乘聖無量壽決定光明王如來陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Buddha's Speaking of the Great-Vehicle Holy Limitless-Life Decisively-Illuminating-King Tathāgata Dhāraṇī*
by [[法天]] Fǎtiān (Dharmadeva, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric *Aparimitāyuḥ-sūtra* by **Fǎtiān** ([[法天]]) — a Sòng-period parallel to [[KR6j0108]] (Fǎchéng's Táng-period *Aparimitāyuḥ-sūtra*). The two translations represent successive Chinese renderings of related Indian *Aparimitāyuḥ* materials.

### Abstract

The text presents the *Aparimitāyuḥ-sūtra* in expanded form, integrating the basic Limitless-Life cult with the additional title-element *juédìng guāngmíng wáng* ("Decisively-Illuminating-King") — emphasizing the cosmic-illuminating aspect of the Buddha. The translation dates from Fǎtiān's career at the Sòng Yìjīngyuàn (980–1001).
