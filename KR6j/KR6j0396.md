# Jīn-gāng-dǐng chāo-shèng sān-jiè jīng shuō Wén-shū wǔ-zì zhēn-yán shèng-xiàng 金剛頂超勝三界經說文殊五字真言勝相
*Excellent Marks of Mañjuśrī's Five-Syllable Mantra Expounded in the Vajraśekhara Trailokyavijaya Sūtra*
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Tang Esoteric ritual text translated by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]], 705–774). Sanskrit title (per CANWWW): *Trailokyavijayamahākalparāja*. Alternate title 文殊五字真言勝相. Colophon: *開府儀同三司特進試鴻臚卿肅國公食邑三千戶賜紫贈司空諡大鑒正號大廣智大興善寺三藏沙門不空奉詔譯*. The text expounds the *shèng-xiàng* (excellent marks / superior characteristics) of Mañjuśrī's Five-Syllable mantra in the *Trailokyavijaya* (超勝三界, "Conqueror-of-the-Three-Realms") section of the *Vajraśekhara*-mahā-kalpa-rāja.

### Abstract

The text opens with **Vajrapāṇi Bodhisattva** (金剛手菩薩摩訶薩) and the other bodhisattvas in the great-assembly of *Vairocana Buddha*, each propounding their own *heart-mantra-and-mudrā* (心真言印). **Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva** then rises and addresses the Buddha: *I, in order to benefit all sentient beings of the future, will expound the swift-attainment-of-Mahā-Prajñā-Pāramitā method: whoever recites it once acquires the merit of reciting the eight-thousand-verse...* — i.e. the assertion that one recitation of the five-syllable Mañjuśrī mantra equals one recitation of the *Aṣṭasāhasrikā Prajñāpāramitā*. The text thus emphasises the **mnemonic-equivalence** valuation of the formula: the five-syllable *bīja*-cluster compresses the entire *prajñāpāramitā* corpus into recitable form, making it operative for laity and monks alike. The text belongs with [[KR6j0395]], [[KR6j0397]], [[KR6j0398]], [[KR6j0399]], and [[KR6j0400]] in the Mañjuśrī-Five-Syllable cycle. The frame-citation of the *Trailokyavijaya* portion of the *Vajraśekhara* situates it doctrinally within the *Yogatantra* — *Sarva-tathāgata-tattva-saṃgraha* tradition.
