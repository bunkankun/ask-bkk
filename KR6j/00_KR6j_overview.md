# KR6j — Esoteric Buddhism (密教 *Mìjiào* / Tantric Buddhism)

## Scope

The KR6j division of the Kanripo catalog comprises **762 texts** of **East Asian Esoteric Buddhism** (Chinese *Mìjiào* 密教 / *Mìzōng* 密宗; Japanese *Mikkyō*) — the Mahāyāna-Buddhist current that emerged in India in the 7th–8th centuries as **Vajrayāna** / **Mantrayāna** / *kriyā-caryā-yoga-tantra*, was systematically transmitted to China during the Tang dynasty (8th–9th centuries) by the **Three Great Tang Esoteric Masters** (*kāi-yuán sān dà-shì* 開元三大士), and from there transmitted to Japan as the **Shingon** 真言 and **Tendai-Esoteric** (Taimitsu) 台密 traditions.

The division's scriptural-textual scope covers: (i) the **Tang Esoteric corpus** of translations from Sanskrit, including the foundational scriptures and their ritual codifications; (ii) the **Sòng-period continuations** translated at the imperial Yìjīng-yuàn 譯經院 (982–1017+) by Dānapāla, Tiānxīzāi-Fǎxián, Fǎtiān, and Fǎhù; (iii) the **Yuán-Qīng Tibetan-Chinese transmission** by Sherap, Gönpojab, and Ngawang Tashi; (iv) the **Japanese Shingon and Tendai-Esoteric exegetical literature** (Heian to Edo period) preserved in Taishō volumes 58, 61.

## Doctrinal Framework

The Tang Esoteric tradition is built on **two scriptural pillars**:

1. **Mahāvairocana-sūtra** (大日經, *Dàrì jīng*; *Dà Pílúzhēnà chéngfó shénbiàn jiāchí jīng* [[KR6j0001]], T18n0848) — the foundational scripture of the **Garbhadhātu** (胎藏界, "Womb-Realm") tradition, translated 724–725 by **Śubhakarasiṃha** 善無畏 ([[善無畏]]) with **Yīxíng** 一行 ([[一行]]).

2. **Vajraśekhara-sūtra** (金剛頂經, *Jīngāngdǐng jīng*; *Sarvatathāgatatattvasaṃgraha-tantra*) — the foundational scripture of the **Vajradhātu** (金剛界, "Vajra-Realm" / "Diamond-Realm") tradition, with three principal Chinese translations: Vajrabodhi's 4-fascicle T866 ([[KR6j0030]], 720–741), Amoghavajra's standard 3-fascicle T865 ([[KR6j0024]], 753–774), and Dānapāla's complete 30-fascicle T882 ([[KR6j0049]], ca. 1010–1015).

Together these form the **dual-mandala** (兩部曼荼羅 *liǎng-bù màntúluó*; Jp. *ryōbu*) tradition that defines East Asian Esoteric Buddhism. Supplementary scriptural-doctrinal frameworks include:

- The **Sanmidsuji** *kriyā-tantra* corpus translated by Śubhakarasiṃha — *Mahāvairocana* (KR6j0001) + *Susiddhikara* (KR6j0061) + *Subāhu-paripṛcchā* (KR6j0065 / 0066) — with **Ennin** 圓仁 ([[圓仁]])'s extensive commentaries.
- The **Buddha-Crown / Uṣṇīṣa** (*Buddhoṣṇīṣa* 佛頂) tradition with the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī* and the *Sitātapatra* (*Śūraṅgama-mantra*) cult, with the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* itself ([[KR6j0118]]) by Pāramiti.
- The **Yoga-uttara-tantra** late-Indian materials (Sòng translations): *Hevajra-tantra* by Fǎhù, the *Sarva-durgati-pariśodhana* by Fǎxián, and the various Dānapāla *yoga-uttara* translations.
- The **Pure Land Esoteric** synthesis, integrating the Amitābha / Amitāyus cult with the Vajradhātu Lotus-family practice.
- The **Bhaiṣajyaguru** (Medicine-Buddha) cult, with both the Tang Esoteric corpus and the Yuán-Qīng Tibetan-Esoteric extensions to the **seven-Buddha** form.

## Lineage Genealogy

The principal Tang Esoteric lineage:

- **Śubhakarasiṃha** 善無畏 (637–735) — Garbhadhātu transmission.
- **Vajrabodhi** 金剛智 (669/671–741) — Vajradhātu transmission.
- **Amoghavajra** 不空 (705–774) — Vajrabodhi's principal disciple; the most prolific Tang Esoteric translator (over 100 translations).
- **Yīxíng** 一行 (683–727) — Śubhakarasiṃha's principal Chinese disciple; bridge between Garbhadhātu and Vajradhātu.
- **Huìguǒ** 惠果 (746–805) — seventh patriarch; consolidator of the **Jīn-tāi bú-èr** dual-mandala doctrine; teacher of **Kūkai** ([[空海]]) of Japan.
- **Yìcāo** 義操 (fl. 805–824) — Huìguǒ's successor; teacher of **Fǎquán** 法全 ([[法全]], fl. 824–859), who in turn taught the Japanese pilgrim-monks **Ennin** ([[圓仁]]), **Enchin** ([[圓珍]]), and **Shūei** 宗叡.

In Japan, this Tang transmission was institutionalised in the **Shingon** (Tōmitsu 東密) school by Kūkai (transmitting from Huìguǒ in 805) at **Tō-ji** 東寺 and **Mount Kōya** 高野山, and in the **Tendai-Esoteric** (Taimitsu 台密) school by Saichō / Ennin / Enchin / Annen ([[安然]]) at **Mount Hiei** and **Onjō-ji** 園城寺 (Mii-dera).

## Texts Cataloged in This Run (1–150)

This first run covers the **first 150 of 762 entries** in KR6j, comprising:

- **KR6j0001–0050**: The *Dà-rì jīng* (Mahāvairocana-sūtra) and Garbhadhātu corpus + the *Vajraśekhara-sūtra* and the canonical Vajradhātu corpus + their major commentaries.
- **KR6j0051–0100**: The Sòng Yìjīng-yuàn Esoteric translations (Dānapāla, Fǎxián, Fǎtiān, Fǎhù) including the *Hevajra-tantra*, the *Sarva-durgati-pariśodhana*, and other late-Indian *yoga-uttara* materials; the Tang Esoteric *kriyā-tantra* corpus; and the Bhaiṣajyaguru tradition (Tang–Yuán–Qīng).
- **KR6j0101–0150**: The *Buddha-Crown / Uṣṇīṣa* tradition (Sitātapatra, Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya, Cakra-rāja, Tejaprabha), the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* and its commentaries, and the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King family.

The remaining KR6j0151–0762 entries are not covered in this run and will be cataloged in subsequent runs.
