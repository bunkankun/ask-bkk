# Fó shuō jīngāng-chǎng zhuāngyán bōrě bōluómìduō jiào zhōng yī-fēn 佛說金剛場莊嚴般若波羅蜜多教中一分
*A Section from the Teaching of the Vajra-Maṇḍala-Adornment Prajñāpāramitā* (Skt. *Vajramaṇḍa-alaṃkāra*?)
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric translation by **Dānapāla** ([[施護]]) — a single section (*yī-fēn* 一分) extracted from a longer **Vajra-maṇḍala adornment Prajñāpāramitā** scripture, integrating the Esoteric *vajra*-mandala framework with the *Prajñāpāramitā* doctrinal tradition.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.* The Taishō text gives the standard Sòng editorial header.

### Abstract

The text exemplifies the late-tantric synthesis of *Prajñāpāramitā* (perfection-of-wisdom) doctrine with Esoteric *vajra*-mandalic ritual — a synthesis characteristic of the late Indian *yoga-* and *yoga-uttara-tantra* literature. The single-section excerpt suggests the longer parent text was not fully translated by the Sòng Institute.

The translation dates from Dānapāla's career at the Sòng Yìjīng-yuàn (982–1017).
