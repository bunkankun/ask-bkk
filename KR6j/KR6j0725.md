# Lèngyán jīng zhèng-jiàn 楞嚴經正見
*"Right View" Commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[濟時]] (述)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle (10卷) **Línjì-school commentary** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]) by **Xìng-fú Huì-gōng Jì-shí** 興福會公濟時 [[濟時]], abbot of the *Gǔ Xìng-fú Chán-sì* 古興福禪寺 at Lóu-dōng 婁東 (Tài-cāng, Jiāng-sū). The author identifies himself in the colophon as *Zhù Lóu-dōng Gǔ Xìng-fú Chán-sì shā-mén Jì-shí shù* 住婁東古興福禪寺沙門濟時述. Preserved as **X16 no. 317** in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The title — *zhèng-jiàn* 正見, "right view" — frames the work as a corrective to the proliferating sectarian *Lèngyán* commentarial divisions.

### Prefaces

A preface by **Shèng Fú-shēng** 盛符升 (a Kūnshān co-disciple, then 89 *suì*, dated **Kāngxī 38 *jǐ-mǎo* 己卯 winter [1699/early-1700]**) records the genesis of the work. In *wù-yín* 戊寅 spring (Kāngxī 37 = 1698), Jì-shí completed his earlier *Jīn-gāng jīng tí-yào* 金剛提要 (Vajracchedikā compendium); soon thereafter he produced the present *Lèngyán zhèng-jiàn*. Shèng Fú-shēng provides a substantial historiographical introduction to the *Lèngyán* commentarial tradition: from Chóng-fú **Què-gōng** 崇福慤公 (the first commentary, traced from the original *fáng-xiāng* — Fáng Róng's — translation); through Guǎn-táo **Zhèn-gōng** 振公 (eight-section / seven-topic structural division); through Zī-zhōng **Yuán-gōng** 資中沅公 (the source of Tiāntái-school *sān-fǎ sān-guān* method, continued by **Gū-shān Yuán-gōng** 孤山圓公); through Cháng-qìng **Yǎn-gōng** 長慶巘公 (origin of the Chán-school commentary tradition); to Sòng-period **Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán** 長水璿公 (synthesis of Xián-shǒu and Tiāntái); and on through the Sòng-Yuán "differentiation" of the post-Sòng commentarial schools. Shèng Fú-shēng concludes that Jì-shí's work, though firmly Línjì-school in lineage, recovers the pre-Sòng synthetic spirit.

### Abstract

The *Zhèng-jiàn* is the principal **Línjì-school commentary** of the late seventeenth century, complementing Yuánxián's Cáodòng-school *Lüè-shū* ([[KR6j0710]]), the Tiāntái-school *Dìng-jiě* ([[KR6j0714]]), the Huá-yán-school *Bǎo-jìng-shū* ([[KR6j0724]]) and *Zhǐ-zhǎng-shū* ([[KR6j0716]]). It is described as the product of three years' "withered sitting" (*kū-zuò sān-nián* 枯坐三年) followed by a single night's awakening, after which Jì-shí "fused them together within the single mind / threefold contemplation" (*róng-huì yú yī-xīn sān-guān* 融會於一心三觀). Composition: 1698–1699.
