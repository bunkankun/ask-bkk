# Fó shuō Zhì-guāng miè yīqiè yèzhàng tuóluóní jīng 佛說智光滅一切業障陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Dhāraṇī of the Light of Knowledge for the Extinction of All Karmic Obstructions*

by [[施護]] (譯)

## About the work

A short single-juan dhāraṇī-sūtra translated at the Sòng 譯經院 by [[施護]] Shīhù (*Dānapāla*, d. 1017). CANWWW restores the Sanskrit title as *Jñānolkā-nāma-dhāraṇī-sarvadurgati-pariśodhanī(sūtra)* — exactly the same Sanskrit base text underlying [[KR6j0629]] (T1397) by [[提雲般若]] Devaprajñā some three centuries earlier. The Sòng version is a fresh translation of the same Indian *vidyā*. CANWWW alt-title 智光滅業障經.

### Abstract

The Buddha is in the *Sun-and-Moon Devaputra*'s palace (日月天子宮 — i.e. the Sūrya/Candra-deva palace), with multiple Tathāgatas present: *Zhì-guāng* 智光 (Skt. *Jñāna-prabha* / *Jñānolkā*), *Zhēn-shí* 真實, *Jīn-guāng-jù* 金光聚, *Shèn-shēn shēng-wáng* 甚深聲王, *Fèn-nù* 忿怒, *Bǎo-yuè* 寶月; together with the bodhisattvas Samantabhadra, Mañjuśrī, *Chí-dì-wáng* 持地王, *Vajrapāṇi*, and others. The Buddha pronounces the *Jñānolkā-dhāraṇī*, which expunges all karmic obstacles and purifies beings of all unwholesome destinies. The Sanskrit *Jñānolkā-nāma-dhāraṇī* survives in Sanskrit manuscript and in Tibetan canonical translation. Recorded in the *Dà-zhōng-xiángfú fǎbǎo lù*; Nanjio N0835. The dating window 982–1017 covers Shīhù's translation career at the Sòng 譯經院.

## Other points of interest

The pair T1397 / T1398 = [[KR6j0629]] / this work documents the Tang–Sòng re-translation pattern: a single Indian *vidyā* re-translated centuries apart by Devaprajñā (Luòyáng, 689) and Shīhù (Kāifēng, 982 ×). Comparison of the two Chinese versions yields useful evidence for the historical philology of the *Jñānolkā* recensions.
