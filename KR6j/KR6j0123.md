# Qítè zuìshèng jīn-lún fódǐng niànsòng yíguǐ fǎ-yào 奇特最勝金輪佛頂念誦儀軌法要
*Essential Ritual Manual for the Recitation of the Wondrous Most-Victorious Golden-Wheel Buddha-Crown* (translator unknown 失譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric ritual manual on the Golden-Wheel Buddha-Crown practice. Companion to KR6j0122 (Amoghavajra's *Jīn-lún wáng fódǐng yàolüè niànsòng fǎ*).

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text presents an alternative anonymous recension of the Golden-Wheel Buddha-Crown ritual practice. The *qítè* ("wondrous") and *zuìshèng* ("most-victorious") epithets emphasize the practice's powerful cosmic-protective efficacy. Anonymous; conventionally dated to the late Tang period.
