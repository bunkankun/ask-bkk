# Sān-zhǒng xīdì pò-dìyù zhuǎn-yèzhàng chū-sānjiè mìmì tuóluóní fǎ 三種悉地破地獄轉業障出三界祕密陀羅尼法
*Esoteric Dhāraṇī Method of the Three Siddhi for Destroying Hells, Transforming Karmic Obstructions, and Transcending the Three Realms*
by [[善無畏]] (Śubhakarasiṃha, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *dhāraṇī*-ritual text attributed to **Śubhakarasiṃha** ([[善無畏]]). The title encodes the soteriological function: the three *siddhi*-attainments are achieved through *dhāraṇī*-recitation that (i) destroys the hells, (ii) transforms karmic obstructions, and (iii) transcends the three realms (kāma-, rūpa-, ārūpya-dhātu).

### Abstract

The text presents an Esoteric *dhāraṇī*-cum-*sādhana* practice oriented to **soteriological transformation** — the *dhāraṇī*-practice as the means of breaking through the karmic conditioning of *saṃsāra* and attaining the three *siddhi* of pacification (*śāntika*), augmentation (*pauṣṭika*), and transcendence (*lokottara* / out-of-the-three-realms). The work is one of three Śubhakarasiṃha-attributed Esoteric *dhāraṇī*-rituals on this theme (KR6j0076–KR6j0078), each presenting a slightly different framework.

The translation dates from Śubhakarasiṃha's Cháng-ān period (716–735).
