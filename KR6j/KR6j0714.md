# Lèngyán jīng guān-xīn dìng-jiě 楞嚴經觀心定解
*"Mind-Contemplation Settled Explanation" of the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[靈耀]] (述)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle (10卷) **Tiāntái-school commentary** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]) by **Líng-yào Quán-zhāng** 靈耀全彰 [[靈耀]], abbot of the **Lèngyán-jiǎng-sì** 楞嚴講寺 in Jiā-hé 嘉禾 (Jiāxīng, Zhèjiāng); the author identifies himself as *Zhù Jiā-hé Lèngyán-jiǎng-sì Tiāntái bǐ-qiū Líng-yào shù* 住嘉禾楞嚴講寺天台比丘靈耀述. Preserved as **X15 no. 306** in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The work is the main book of which [[KR6j0712]] (科) and [[KR6j0713]] (大綱) are the structural outline and main-outline summary.

### Abstract

The *Dìng-jiě* is a substantial late-Kāngxī-period reaffirmation of the **Tiāntái-school exegetical orthodoxy** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*, written in explicit opposition to the Wàn-lì-period commentator **Jiāo-guāng Zhēn-jiàn** 交光真鑑's *Lèngyán-jīng zhèng-mài shū* ([[KR6j0703]]), which had attempted to dispense with the six consciousnesses (六識) and the three contemplations (三觀) as exegetical categories. Líng-yào restores the classical Tiāntái apparatus — the threefold structural division (*sān-fēn fēn-wén* 三分分文: 序 / 正宗 / 流通); the *sì-xī-tán* 四悉檀 (four siddhāntas) for interpreting the *rú-shì* 如是 incipit; the *yuè-jiāo* 約教 system of doctrinal classification (Tripiṭaka / *tōng* / *bié* / *yuán*) — and uses it to read every section of the sūtra. The opening exegesis of the *rú-shì wǒ-wén* 如是我聞 already shows the method: Líng-yào takes the formulaic incipit and gives it a layered Tiāntái-school exposition through *sì-xī-tán* and *yuè-jiāo*. Composition: Kāngxī 19 / 1680, contemporaneous with the *Dà-gāng*.

The *Dìng-jiě* is the principal eighteenth-century Tiāntái-school *Śūraṃgama* commentary and an important counterweight to the Chán/Xián-shǒu-leaning commentaries dominant in late-Míng Buddhism.
