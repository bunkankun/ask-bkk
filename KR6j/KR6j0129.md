# Yī-zì dǐng-lún-wáng yújiā jīng 一字頂輪王瑜伽經
*Yoga Sūtra of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric scripture in the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King family, attributed to **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). The text is preserved in the *Fójiào dàzàng-jīng* (Buddhist Canon) Vol. 052 No. 1222 — a CBETA-canon source rather than the standard Taishō location.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text gives an Esoteric *yoga* (yoga-tantra) practice for the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King in compact one-fascicle form. The composition is conventionally attributed to Amoghavajra and dated to his mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
