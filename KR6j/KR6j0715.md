# Lèngyán jīng zhǐzhǎng shū xuánshì 楞嚴經指掌疏懸示
*Preliminary Indications of the "Pointing-to-the-Palm" Commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[通理]] (述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **prolegomenon** (*xuánshì* 懸示, "preliminary indications") to **Dátiān Tōnglǐ** 達天通理 [[通理]]'s ten-fascicle *Lèngyánjīng zhǐzhǎng shū* 楞嚴經指掌疏 ([[KR6j0716]]). Tōnglǐ identifies himself as *Jīngdū Niānhuásì Xiánzōng hòuxué Dátiān Tōnglǐ jìngshù* 京都拈華寺賢宗後學達天通理敬述 ("respectfully composed by Dátiān Tōnglǐ, junior disciple of the Xiánshǒu school at the Niānhuásì in the capital"), with editorial assistance from his Dharma-heirs **Xìngzōng Zǔwàng** 興宗祖旺, **Téngqīng Huáirén** 騰清懷仁, and **Zǔyù** 祖毓. Preserved as **X16 no. 307** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The *Xuán-shì* lays out Tōng-lǐ's programmatic framework for reading the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*, **explicitly modeled on Qīng-liáng Chéng-guān** 清涼澄觀 (the Táng Huá-yán patriarch). He divides the sūtra into **four parts** corresponding to the *Sì-zhǒng-fēn* 四種分 of the Huá-yán-school: (1) *Kāi-shì shē-mó chéng-xìn fēn* 開示奢摩成信分 ("Opening-and-revealing of *Śamatha*: bringing forth Faith"), (2) *Kāi-shì sān-mó chéng-jiě fēn* 開示三摩成解分 ("...of *Samādhi*: bringing forth Understanding"), (3) *Kāi-shì chán-nà chéng-xiū fēn* 開示禪那成修分 ("...of *Dhyāna*: bringing forth Cultivation"), (4) *Kāi-shì lèngyán chéng-zhèng fēn* 開示楞嚴成證分 ("...of *Śūraṃgama*: bringing forth Realization"). The *Xuán-shì* surveys the major Tang-Sòng *Lèngyán* commentarial tradition — Yuán-zhào, Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán, Wú-jìn-zàng Wén-shū — and explicitly differentiates Tōng-lǐ's reading from **Jiāo-guāng Zhēn-jiàn** 交光真鑑's *Zhèng-mài shū* ([[KR6j0703]]) — whose departures from Qīng-liáng's Huá-yán *jiā-fǎ* 家法 Tōng-lǐ regards as unacceptable. Composition: 1763 (癸未) — 1782 (Tōng-lǐ's death).
