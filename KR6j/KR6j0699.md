# Lèngyán jīng zhízhǐ 楞嚴經直指
*Direct Pointing on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[圅昰]] (疏)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle (10卷) **Cáodòng-Chán "direct-pointing" commentary** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]) by **Tiān-rán Hán-shì** 天然圅昰 [[圅昰]] (1608 – 1685), the principal late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng master of the **Lǐngnán** 嶺南 region. Together with the accompanying *Zhí-zhǐ kē-wén* ([[KR6j0698]]), the work forms Hánshì's complete *Lèngyán* exegetical apparatus. Preserved as **X14 no. 291** in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The catalog meta classes the dynasty as 明; the work was actually completed in the very early Qīng period, but Hánshì's persona as a *yí-mín* 遺民 ("Míng-loyalist remnant") makes this Míng-classification idiomatic.

### Prefaces

The work is prefaced by a substantial **Lèngyán zhí-zhǐ xù** 首楞嚴直指敘 by a disciple: "*Dà-jué néng-rén* (Great-Awakened Capable Sage), having attained the Way ten days, immediately taught the *Avataṃsaka*: with the *zhù-dì fán-nǎo* (abode-ground afflictions) of sentient beings as the buddhas' *bù-dòng zhì* (immovable wisdom). Like directly handing the precious throne to the ordinary common — therefore not what the three-vehicle stream can fully believe. The *śrāvakas* in attendance saw not, heard not. Yet at the final raising-the-flower [Vulture-Peak], then *jīn-sè tóu-tuó* [Mahākāśyapa] alone received the special transmission. *Qìng-xǐ* [Ānanda] succeeded him as the second *zǔ* (patriarch) of the Western Heaven. Although he ascended-stage at the time of the *chà-gān* (banner-pole) being laid, his awakening was at the bell-striking testing-of-permanence moment. Therefore the single sūtra of the *Śūraṃgama* is also a Dharma-seal of the lineage-school. From ancient times to the present, the commentaries on this all surpassed in their own [strengths]. My master, **Tiān-rán Shì hé-shàng**, sat in stillness at *Dān-xiá*; in three months he completed the *Zhí-zhǐ*. Just as it reached the *birth*-occasion …" (大覺能仁。成道十日。即說華嚴。以眾生住地煩惱。為諸佛不動智。如將寶位直授凡庸。故非三乘之流所能諦信。當時聲聞在坐。不見不聞。然而最後拈華。則金色頭陀。獨得別傳之囑。慶喜繼之。為西天二祖。雖登位於剎竿倒却之時。而發悟在擊鐘驗常之際。則楞嚴一經。又宗門之法印也。古今疏此。俱擅所長。吾師天然昰和上。宴坐丹霞。以三月成直指。適屆示生之 …).

### Abstract

The preface attests that Hánshì composed the *Zhízhǐ* in three months while in seated retreat at **Dānxiásì** 丹霞寺 (in Guǎngdōng) — most plausibly during one of his Lǐngnán residency periods between his early establishment at Léifēng (1640s) and his late-life Lúshān period (1670s–1685). The genre name *zhízhǐ* 直指 ("direct-pointing") signals a **Chán-tradition reading** that prioritizes the sūtra's teaching as a Dharma-seal for direct mind-pointing, in contrast to the *Tiāntái-doctrinal* reading of [[傳燈]]'s *Yuántōng shū* ([[KR6j0689]]) and the *philological-doctrinal* reading of [[真鑑]]'s *Zhèngmài shū* ([[KR6j0683]]).

Hánshì was one of the leading figures of the **Lǐngnán Cáodòng revival** of the late-Míng / early-Qīng transition; together with [[弘麗]] Hónglì and [[弘贊]] Hóngzàn (his fellow Lǐngnán Cáodòng commentators) he forms the principal exegetical triad of the regional school. The *Zhízhǐ* is the most ambitious *Lèngyán* commentary from this triad and one of the principal Chán-school readings of the *Lèngyán* in the seventeenth century.

The dating bracket is set to **1640 – 1685**, the productive Cáodòng-school career of Hánshì.
