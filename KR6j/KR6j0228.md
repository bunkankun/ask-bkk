# Guān zì-zài dà-bēi chéng-jiù yú-jiā liánhuá-bù niàn-sòng fǎ-mén 觀自在大悲成就瑜伽蓮華部念誦法門
*Method of the Lotus-Family Recitation Yoga for Accomplishing the Great Compassion of Avalokiteśvara*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the practice of the *Liánhuá-bù* 蓮華部 (Lotus-family) yoga, oriented toward Avalokiteśvara as the central deity. CANWWW alt-title: *Liánhuá-bù xīn-sòng fǎ-mén* 蓮華部心誦法門. The text is one of the principal liturgical manuals of the *Liánhuá-bù* — one of the three (later five) "families" of the Tang Esoteric *Vajraśekhara* maṇḍala system.

### Abstract

The Esoteric *kula*-system (Chinese *bù* 部, Sanskrit *kula* "family") organises the deities of the maṇḍala into three (later five) families, each headed by a Buddha and including subsidiary bodhisattvas, *vajra*-deities, and protector-figures. The **Lotus-family** (*Padma-kula* / 蓮華部) is the *prajñā* (wisdom) family, headed by **Amitābha** with **Avalokiteśvara** as its central *bodhisattva*. The present manual provides the ritual practice for the Lotus-family yoga, focused on Avalokiteśvara as the embodiment of *mahā-karuṇā* (great compassion). It belongs to a series of Avalokiteśvara-focused ritual manuals that Amoghavajra produced — including [[KR6j0229]], [[KR6j0230]], and [[KR6j0231]] — that together form the principal Tang-period ritual literature of the Avalokiteśvara cult within the Esoteric framework. Listed at Nanjio 1413.
