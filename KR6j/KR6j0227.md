# Miàofǎ liánhuá sānmèi mìmì sānmóyē jīng 妙法蓮華三昧祕密三摩耶經
*Sūtra of the Secret Samaya of the Lotus-Sūtra Samādhi*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric sūtra-manual preserved only in the Manji Xùzàngjīng (X02 no. 204), attributed to **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). The text presents the **Lotus-Sūtra samādhi** as an Esoteric *samaya* (sacred-vow / sacred-bond) — i.e. recasts the Lotus-sūtra meditation tradition within the Tang-period Esoteric *yoga-tantra* framework of *samaya*-vows linking practitioner to *kula* (Buddha-family).

### Abstract

Amoghavajra produced a substantial corpus of texts integrating the *Lotus-sūtra* tradition with Esoteric ritual; his most influential such work is the *Dàlè jīngāng bùkōng zhēnshí sānmóyē jīng* ([[KR6c0120]] T243), the *Adhyardhaśatikā Prajñāpāramitā* in Tantric guise. The present *Miàofǎ liánhuá sānmèi mìmì sānmóyē jīng* extends that programme to the *Lotus*-tradition proper. Like [[KR6j0226]] it is preserved only via the Xùzàngjīng supplementary line; the authenticity of the Amoghavajra attribution is therefore uncertain. The text presupposes a developed *yoga-tantra* ritual framework and reads as a late-eighth-century or later production within Amoghavajra's school. The Japanese Tendai and Shingon traditions both draw on this text in their respective Lotus-meditation practices.
