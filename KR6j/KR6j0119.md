# Dà fódǐng jīng kāití 大佛頂經開題
*Introductory Exegesis on the Great-Buddha-Crown Sūtra (Śūraṅgama)* (Jp. *Daibutchō-gyō kaidai*)
by [[空海]] (Kūkai, 撰)

## About the work

Kūkai's introductory exegesis (*kāití* / *kaidai*) on the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]], T19n0945) — alongside his *kāití* on the *Mahāvairocana-sūtra* ([[KR6j0002]]) and the *Vajraśekhara-sūtra* ([[KR6j0025]], [[KR6j0026]]).

### Prefaces


### Abstract

Kūkai's *Daibutchō kaidai* presents the Esoteric reading of the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra*'s title and opening sections. The title element *Dà fódǐng* 大佛頂 ("Great-Buddha-Crown") is unfolded as the cosmic *Buddhoṣṇīṣa* — the Esoteric *uṣṇīṣa* doctrine that identifies the Buddha's *uṣṇīṣa* with the cosmic *dharmakāya*. The *Śūraṅgama-mantra* itself is read as the cosmic *dhāraṇī* of the *dharmakāya*. The composition dates from Kūkai's mature Tō-ji / Mt. Kōya period (815–835).
