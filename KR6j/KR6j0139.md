# Bǎo xīdì chéngfó tuóluóní jīng 寶悉地成佛陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Jewel-Siddhi Buddhahood-Attainment Dhāraṇī*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *dhāraṇī* scripture by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) on the **Jewel-Siddhi** (*bǎo xīdì* 寶悉地 — Skt. *ratna-siddhi*) practice for Buddhahood-attainment.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text presents the *Jewel-Siddhi dhāraṇī*-practice oriented to the soteriological *siddhi* (accomplishment) of awakening. The practice integrates the Jewel-family iconography of the Vajradhātu mandala (centred on Ratnasambhava) with the *dhāraṇī*-recitation framework of the *kriyā* and *yoga* tantra traditions. Composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
