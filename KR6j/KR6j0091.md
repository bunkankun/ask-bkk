# Fóxīn jīng 佛心經
*Sūtra of the Buddha-Heart* (Skt. *Buddhahṛdaya-sūtra*?)
by [[菩提流志]] (Bodhiruci II, 譯)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Esoteric *dhāraṇī* scripture translated by **Bodhiruci II** 菩提流志 ([[菩提流志]], 572?–727?), the great Tang Indian-pilgrim translator at the Wǔ-Zhōu / Kāiyuán courts. The text presents a *Buddha-heart* (*fó-xīn* 佛心) doctrinal-Esoteric scripture in the *dhāraṇī*-class tradition.

### Prefaces


### Abstract

The *Fó-xīn jīng* presents a doctrinal-Esoteric scripture organised around the *Buddha-heart* concept — the cosmic-essence of all Buddhas as their unitary "heart" (*hṛdaya*). The two-fascicle structure covers the doctrinal exposition and the *dhāraṇī*-recitation practice. The translation dates from Bodhiruci's documented Cháng-ān period (693–727).
