# Lèngyán jīng xù zhǐ-wèi shū 楞嚴經序指味疏
*"Pointing to the Flavour" Commentary on the Preface of the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[諦閑]] (述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **Tiāntái-school commentary** by **Dì-xián Gǔ-xū** 諦閑古虛 [[諦閑]] (1858–1932) on the **prefatory section** alone of the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]). The author identifies himself as *Huáng-yán Tái-zōng yì-sūn bǐ-qiū Dì-xián shù-shū* 黃巖台宗裔孫比丘諦閑述疏 ("monk Dì-xián, lineal heir of the Tiāntái school in Huáng-yán, composed the commentary"), with a colophon by his disciple Huā-shān 華山. Preserved as **X16 no. 313** in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The title's metaphor — *zhǐ-wèi* 指味, "pointing to the flavour" — signals an exegesis that aims to bring out the doctrinal taste of the prefatory section rather than analyze it line by line.

### Prefaces

The author's own preface is dated **Guāngxù 19 *guǐ-sì* 癸巳, summer fifth month, Dragon-Boat Festival (5 May 1893)** and signed *Tái-zōng wǎn-xué Zhuó-sān bǐ-qiū Dì-xián* 台宗晚學卓三比丘諦閑 ("Junior Tiāntái student Zhuó-sān, monk Dì-xián"). Disciple Huā-shān's postface is dated *Guāngxù 28 rén-yín* 壬寅, spring (1902), at Yàn-nán 雁南.

### Abstract

The work is the most prominent late-Qīng Tiāntái-school treatment of the *Śūraṃgama* prefatory section: the author limits himself to the opening *xù* 序 (the canonical narrative of the sūtra's setting, audience, and *yīn-yuán* causation), reading it through a fivefold *jiě-tí* 解題 system (合釋 / 離釋 / 釋別題 [釋大佛頂 / 釋首楞嚴] / 釋通題) and then through a four-part *shì-wén* 釋文 covering the *yī-jīng dà-zhǐ* 一經大旨 ("cardinal purport of the entire sūtra"), the *shí-zhóu wén-yì* 十軸文義 ("textual sense of the ten fascicles"), the *míng-yì yīn-guǒ* 名義因果 ("naming-and-meaning, cause-and-effect"), and the *lǐ-xíng xìng-xiū* 理行性修 ("principle/practice, nature/cultivation"). The work is firmly within the Tiāntái-school tradition begun by [[長水子璿]] in the Sòng and continued by [[靈耀]] in the early Qīng (whose *Guān-xīn dìng-jiě* [[KR6j0714]] Dì-xián studied), but represents a more concentrated and pedagogical late-Qīng restatement. Composition: 1893; editorial preparation through 1902.

## Other points of interest

The *Xù zhǐ-wèi shū* is the seed-work of Dì-xián's larger *Lèngyán-jīng zhǐ-wèi shū* 楞嚴經指味疏 commentarial project; the early-Republican *Dì-xián dà-shī yí-jí* 諦閑大師遺集 contains the full project, but only this *xù*-fascicle was prepared for the *Xùzàngjīng* compilation. It is therefore one of the few late-nineteenth-century Tiāntái commentaries to find its way into the *Xùzàng* as an independent canonical work.
