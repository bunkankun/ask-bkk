# Yī-zì qítè fódǐng jīng 一字奇特佛頂經
*Sūtra of the One-Syllable Wondrous Buddha-Crown*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Esoteric scripture by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) on the **One-Syllable Wondrous Buddha-Crown** practice — companion to KR6j0124 (T950) and the wider Buddha-Crown / *uṣṇīṣa* corpus.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text presents an elaborated form of the One-Syllable Buddha-Crown practice with the additional epithet *qítè* ("wondrous, marvellous"), emphasizing the practice's powerful soteriological efficacy. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
