# Fó shuō Dà-jí-xiáng tiān-nǚ shí-èr míng-hào jīng 佛說大吉祥天女十二名號經
*Sūtra of the Twelve Names of the Great Auspicious Goddess, Spoken by the Buddha* (parallel rendering)
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A second one-fascicle parallel rendering of the same Mahāśrī-sūtra material treated above in [[KR6j0480]] (T1252a). Both renderings are attributed to **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) and bear the identical title, but they differ in textual detail. T1252b reflects the line of transmission in 【大】【大→宋】【大→元】【大→明】 (the principal printed Tripiṭaka witnesses); T1252a reflects an alternate witness in CBETA (likely from a single manuscript or block-printed edition).

### Abstract

The same Twelve-Names structure as T1252a, with variations in: (a) the transliteration of the Sanskrit names; (b) the Chinese-glossed translations of certain epithets; (c) some details of the rite-explanation. As with most parallel-recension pairs in the Esoteric corpus, both were retained because the Indian recensions diverged enough that one could not stand for the other. Date: Amoghavajra's Cháng-ān activity, 746–774.
