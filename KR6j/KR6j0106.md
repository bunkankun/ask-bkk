# Fó shuō wúliàng gōngdé tuóluóní jīng 佛說無量功德陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Buddha's Speaking of the Limitless-Merit Dhāraṇī*
by [[法賢]] (Fǎxián, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric *dhāraṇī* scripture by **Fǎxián** ([[法賢]]) on a *dhāraṇī* whose recitation produces "limitless merit" (*wúliàng gōngdé*).

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

A short *dhāraṇī*-recitation scripture in the genre of *gōngdé* ("merit-producing") *dhāraṇī* texts that became popular in the Sòng-period devotional environment. The translation dates from Fǎxián's career at the Sòng Yìjīng-yuàn (987–1001).
