# Lèngyán jīng yìshū zhùjīng kē 楞嚴經義疏注經科
*Sectional Outline of the Annotated Commentary on the Significance of the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[子璿]] (述)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle (1卷) **sectional-outline** (*kē* 科) accompanying [[子璿]]'s ten-fascicle *Yì-shū zhù-jīng* 義疏注經 ([[KR6j0673]], T39 no. 1799) — the Northern-Sòng Huá-yán-Chán commentary on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*. The *kē* genre is a Sòng-Yuán Buddhist exegetical apparatus consisting solely of a hierarchical sectional outline (*fēn-kē* 分科) of the parent commentary, providing the topical "skeleton" by which the commentary's argument can be read at a glance. It is the pedagogical complement to the running prose commentary, used in lecture-room teaching and private study.

The opening of the text exhibits the standard *kē* layout: "Explanation of the sūtra is divided into two: 1. The title (*Dà-fódǐng…*); 2. The body of the text (divided into three: 1. Preface section; 2. Main section; 3. Transmission section)" (釋經分(二)：一題目(大佛)；二本文(三)：一序說分(二) …). The structure recapitulates Zǐ-xuán's analytical hierarchy across the entire *Yì-shū* and is the textual precursor to the more elaborate Míng-period *kē* outlines (e.g., [[KR6j0681]] of [[真鑑]] for the *Zhèng-mài shū*, and [[KR6j0686]] of [[德清]]).

### Abstract

Authorship is securely attributed to **Cháng-shuǐ Zǐ-xuán** 長水子璿 by the colophon (長水沙門子璿述) and by the obvious correspondence of the outline to the parent *Yì-shū* commentary. Composition is bracketed at **1024 – 1038** — the productive period of Zǐ-xuán's *Yì-shū* and his death year. The work is preserved as **X10 no. 265** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.
