# Shìjiā wénní fó jīngāng yī-shèng xiūxíng yíguǐ fǎ-pǐn 釋迦文尼佛金剛一乘修行儀軌法品
*Chapter on the Vajra-Eka-Yāna Practice Ritual of Śākyamuni Buddha* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric ritual manual for the practice of **Śākyamuni** in the *vajra-eka-yāna* (Vajra-One-Vehicle) tradition — i.e., the cosmic-Esoteric form of the historical Buddha as integrated with the Vajra-mandalic framework.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text presents the Esoteric framework for Śākyamuni-practice — the identification of the historical Buddha (in his *nirmāṇakāya*) with his cosmic *dharmakāya* expression in the Vajradhātu mandala. The *yī-shèng* (One-Vehicle) doctrine of the *Saddharmapuṇḍarīka* tradition is here integrated with the Vajra-mandalic framework. The text is anonymously transmitted and conventionally dated to the late Tang period.
