# Qǐng guān-yīn jīng shū chǎn-yì chāo 請觀音經疏闡義鈔
*Sub-Commentary "Explicating the Meaning" of the Commentary on the Qǐng-guān-yīn jīng*
by [[智圓]] (述)

## About the work

A four-fascicle (4卷) **sub-commentary** by **Gū-shān Zhì-yuán** 孤山智圓 [[智圓]] (976–1022), the principal Northern-Sòng *shān-wài* 山外-school Tiāntái master, on Tiāntái Zhì-yǐ's *Qǐng guān-yīn jīng shū* ([[KR6j0729]]). Preserved as **T39 no. 1801** in the *Taishō*. The sub-commentary recovers and re-transmits Zhì-yǐ's *Shū*, which in the post-Táng period had largely fallen out of active study.

### Prefaces

The opening **Qǐng guān-yīn jīng shū chǎn-yì chāo xù** 請觀音經疏闡義鈔序 records Zhì-yuán's motivation: "From the time the *Shū* was expounded by the Wise One [Zhì-yǐ] and recorded by Zhāng-ān, no later master has been heard to praise or expound it. With the transmission's voice ceased, later scholars have often been unaware of its name, or knowing the name have never opened the scrolls. This text is reduced to a single thread; that I, unworthy as I am, feel pained for it." Zhì-yuán recovered the text, organized its meaning into sections, and wrote it into a *chāo* 鈔 (sub-commentary) of two (or in the received recension, four) fascicles, "in order to clarify the cardinal purport and to enlighten the unschooled."

### Abstract

The *Chǎn-yì chāo* is the principal **Sòng-period sub-commentary on Zhì-yǐ's *Qǐng guān-yīn jīng shū*** and the textual vehicle by which Zhì-yǐ's commentary entered the Sòng-Yuán Tiāntái curriculum. Zhì-yuán's *shān-wài* doctrinal orientation (emphasizing *guān-xīn* 觀心) shapes the reading of the *xiāo-fú dú-hài* 消伏毒害 dhāraṇī liturgy: he emphasizes the *Avalokiteśvara* invocation as a vehicle for *guān-xīn* contemplation rather than as a primarily apotropaic ritual instrument. The work is a substantial document of the **Sòng-period Tiāntái devotional revival** centered on the *Qǐng guān-yīn* and *Lèngyán* sūtras. Composition window: c. 1000 (Zhì-yuán's mature period at Gū-shān) – 1022 (his death).
