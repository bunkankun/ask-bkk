# Jīngāngdǐng jīng jìshì 金剛頂經偈釋
*Verse-Explication of the Vajraśekhara-sūtra* (Jp. *Kongōchō-gyō geshaku*)
by [[賴尊]] (Raison, 撰)

## About the work

A short verse-explication (*jì-shì* 偈釋, "explication of the *gāthās*") of the *Vajraśekhara-sūtra* ([[KR6j0024]], T18n0865) by the Japanese Esoteric scholar **Raison** 賴尊 ([[賴尊]]). The text focuses specifically on the verse-passages of the *Vajraśekhara* — the *gāthās* (頌) embedded in the prose narrative that articulate the principal doctrinal content — providing line-by-line Esoteric exegesis.

### Abstract

The *Jì-shì* genre is a sub-category of Buddhist commentary devoted specifically to the verse-passages of a scripture, distinct from the full prose commentary (*shū* 疏). For the *Vajraśekhara-sūtra*, whose prose narrative is heavily punctuated with doctrinally-loaded *gāthās* (including the foundational verses on the *pañcābhisaṃbodhi* — the five-stage Vajradhātu attainment of Buddhahood), the verse-passages bear particular doctrinal weight, and Raison's selective focus on these verses produces a focused doctrinal exegesis.

The composition dates and lifedates of Raison are uncertain; the text is conventionally placed in the **late Heian to Kamakura** period (12th–13th c.) based on the manuscript tradition and stylistic features. The text is one of the medium-length Heian-Kamakura Japanese commentaries on the *Vajraśekhara*.
