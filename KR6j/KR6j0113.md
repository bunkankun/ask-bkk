# Shìjiāmóunī fó chéngdào zài pútí-shù xiángmó zàn 釋迦牟尼佛成道在菩提樹降魔讚
*Verse-Praise of Śākyamuni Buddha's Awakening Beneath the Bodhi Tree, Subduing Māra*

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous *zàn* (verse-praise) text on Śākyamuni's defeat of Māra and attainment of awakening beneath the Bodhi tree — a key episode in the canonical Buddha-biography. The text gives a verse-rendering of the standard narrative for liturgical recitation.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The *zàn* genre — Buddhist verse-praise — adapted the Sanskrit *stotra* tradition to Chinese liturgical use. This particular *zàn* commemorates the climactic moment of the Buddha-biography (the defeat of Māra and the attainment of awakening) for use in liturgical recitation. The text is anonymous and conventionally dated to the late Tang to Sòng period.
