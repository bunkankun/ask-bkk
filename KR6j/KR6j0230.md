# Yú-jiā liánhuá-bù niàn-sòng fǎ 瑜伽蓮華部念誦法
*Yoga Recitation-Method of the Lotus Family*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) — a more compact / condensed *niàn-sòng fǎ* counterpart to [[KR6j0228]] (T1030 *Guān zì-zài dà-bēi chéng-jiù yú-jiā liánhuá-bù niàn-sòng fǎ-mén*). CANWWW alt-title: *Liánhuá-bù niàn-sòng fǎ* 蓮華部念誦法. Listed at Nanjio 1429.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The *Yú-jiā liánhuá-bù niàn-sòng fǎ* is a focused practice-text dedicated to the daily liturgical performance of the Lotus-family yoga. Where T1030 ([[KR6j0228]]) provides the doctrinal-ritual framework and the broader contextualisation of the practice, the present T1032 supplies the streamlined daily-recitation procedure suitable for routine liturgical use. This pairing of expanded-ritual-manual with condensed-practice-manual is characteristic of Amoghavajra's compositional method: his Esoteric ritual literature often comes in matched pairs of "full" and "abbreviated" forms.
