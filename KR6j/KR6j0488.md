# Mó-lì-zhī pú-sà lüè niàn-sòng fǎ 摩利支菩薩略念誦法
*Abridged Recitation Method of Mārīcī Bodhisattva*
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual abridgement (略 = "abridged") by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) — the practitioner's-handbook companion to the longer Mārīcī materials [[KR6j0483]] (T1254) and [[KR6j0484]]–[[KR6j0485]] (T1255a/b). The text supplies a compressed daily-practice format suitable for the practitioner who cannot perform the full elaborated rite.

### Abstract

The text gives the abbreviated Mārīcī-recitation programme: brief opening invocation, mantra-recitation, the essential mudrās, the *bīja* visualisation, and the dismissal — in a form that can be performed in a brief sitting. The dating bracket follows Amoghavajra's Cháng-ān activity (746–774).
