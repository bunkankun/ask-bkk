# Fó shuō yīqiè rúlái jīngāng sānyè zuìshàng mìmì dàjiàowáng jīng 佛說一切如來金剛三業最上祕密大教王經
*Sūtra of the Buddha's Speaking of the Highest-Esoteric Great-Teaching-King of the Vajra Three-Karma of All Tathāgatas* (Skt. *Sarvatathāgata-vajra-trikāya-mahā-guhya-paramatantra-rāja*?)
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A seven-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric translation by **Dānapāla** ([[施護]]) on the **Esoteric Vajra-trikāya** (the three Vajra-bodies / three Vajra-acts of body-speech-mind) doctrine. The text is one of Dānapāla's substantial Vajra-tantra translations.

### Prefaces


### Abstract

The text presents an advanced *yoga-uttara-tantra*-class scripture organised around the doctrine of the **Vajra Three-Karma** (*Jīngāng sānyè* 金剛三業) — the Esoteric *vajra*-form of the standard Buddhist Three-Karma (body-speech-mind) framework, identified with the **Three Mysteries** (三密 *sānmì*) of Esoteric practice. The seven fascicles cover the doctrinal apparatus, the ritual practices, and the *sādhana* prescriptions for the Vajra Three-Karma yoga.

The translation dates from Dānapāla's career at the Sòng Yìjīngyuàn (982–1017).
