# Jīn-gāng guāng-yàn zhǐ fēng-yǔ tuóluóní jīng 金剛光焰止風雨陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Vajra-Flame Dhāraṇī for Quelling Wind and Rain (Recension B)*
by [[菩提流志]] (Bodhiruci II, 譯)

## About the work

The Recension B (Taishō-canon recension) of Bodhiruci II's *Vajra-jvāla* weather-control dhāraṇī sūtra. The principal entry, with the doctrinal and historical discussion, is at [[KR6j0221]] (T1027A); the present T1027B preserves a parallel transmission descending through the Sòng (大→宋) and Korean (大→甲) editions distinct from the *orig* line of Recension A.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The two recensions T1027A and T1027B of the *Jīn-gāng guāng-yàn zhǐ fēng-yǔ tuóluóní jīng* document a textual divergence within the Chinese transmission of Bodhiruci's weather-control dhāraṇī. The variants are concentrated in the dhāraṇī-orthography (Recension B preserves a Sòng-line spelling normalisation) and in the ritual-prescription appendices, which differ in their specific *mudrā* and *maṇḍala* details. Both recensions descend from Bodhiruci II's eighth-century original; neither is a re-translation. For the full discussion see [[KR6j0221]].
