# Yàoshī rúlái niànsòng yíguǐ 藥師如來念誦儀軌
*Recitation Ritual Manual for the Medicine-Master Tathāgata* (recension A)
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the **Bhaiṣajyaguru** practice — companion to Vajrabodhi's earlier KR6j0093 in the Bhaiṣajyaguru ritual corpus. Recension A of two parallel recensions (T924A and T924B = KR6j0094 and KR6j0095), both bearing the same title and attributed to Amoghavajra.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text gives Amoghavajra's codification of the Bhaiṣajyaguru *sādhana*, complementing Vajrabodhi's earlier version (KR6j0093) and establishing the canonical Tang Esoteric Bhaiṣajyaguru practice. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
