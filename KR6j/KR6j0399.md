# Jīn-gāng-dǐng jīng yú-jiā Wén-shū-shī-lì pú-sà gòng-yǎng yí-guǐ 金剛頂經瑜伽文殊師利菩薩供養儀軌
*Offering Ritual Manual of Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva from the Yoga of the Vajraśekhara-sūtra*
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Tang Esoteric ritual manual translated by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]], 705–774). Alternate title 文殊五字念誦法. Colophon: full Amoghavajra titulature with *大廣智大興善寺三藏沙門不空奉詔譯*. Taishō head-note: *No. 1175 [cf. No. 1171]*. The opening verse: *歸命童真妙吉祥 我依瑜伽說念誦 身口意業金剛念* ("Homage to Mañjuśrī-kumāra; I rely on the Yoga to expound the recitation; with body, speech, and mind in vajra-attention…").

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text presents the **offering-and-recitation manual** for Mañjuśrī, opening with the homage-verse to *Mañjuśrī-kumāra* (童真妙吉祥) and announcing the Yoga-based recitation programme. The text proceeds to expound the full ritual sequence: bodhicitta-arousal, ten-direction-Buddha-anumodanā, mandala-construction, deity-visualisation (Mañjuśrī as five-syllable *kumāra*-form), *mudrā*-cycle, *mantra*-cycle, internal and external offerings (*pūjā*), and dedication. The manual provides the operative-ritual side of the Mañjuśrī-Pañcākṣarī corpus, complementing the doctrinal-and-narrative T1171 ([[KR6j0395]]) and the verse-summary T1174 ([[KR6j0398]]). CANWWW pairs this directly with T1171 as the principal ritual-companion text.

The dating bracket follows Amoghavajra's translation activity at Cháng'ān (746–774).
