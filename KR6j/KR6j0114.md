# Shìjiā fó zàn 釋迦佛讚
*Verse-Praise of Śākyamuni Buddha*
by [[薩穆丹達爾吉]] (Samten Targye, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Qīng-period Tibetan-Esoteric verse-praise of Śākyamuni Buddha translated by **Samten Targye** ([[薩穆丹達爾吉]]). The text is the Tibetan-Esoteric counterpart to the Tang/Sòng-period anonymous *Shìjiāmóunī fó* *zàn* (KR6j0113).

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The *Shìjiā fó zàn* presents a Tibetan-canonical *stotra* (verse-praise) of Śākyamuni in Chinese translation. The text is short and devotional — a standard form of Tibetan-Buddhist liturgical practice adapted for Qīng-court Chinese-language use. The translation dates from Samten Targye's Qiánlóng to early Jiāqìng career (1736–1820).
