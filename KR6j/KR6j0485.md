# Fó shuō Mólìzhītiān jīng 佛說摩利支天經
*Sūtra of Mārīcī-deva, Spoken by the Buddha* (parallel rendering)
by [[不空]] (Bùkōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A second parallel rendering of the Mārīcī-dhāraṇī material treated above in [[KR6j0484]] (T1255a). Both are attributed to **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]); they differ in textual detail (mantra-form, narrative-frame, iconographic detail), and both were retained in the Taishō because the divergences could not be reduced. Date: Amoghavajra's Cháng-ān activity, 746–774.

### Abstract

Same Mārīcī-dhāraṇī material as T1255a, but in a slightly different recension. Comparison shows mainly orthographic and phonetic variations in the transliterated mantra-syllables, plus minor compression of the framing-narrative. As elsewhere in the Esoteric corpus, parallel recensions of the same Sanskrit *kalpa*-material were retained because the Indian sources themselves diverged.
