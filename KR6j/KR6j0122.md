# Jīn-lún wáng fódǐng yàolüè niànsòng fǎ 金輪王佛頂要略念誦法
*Essential-Abridged Recitation Method of the Golden-Wheel-King Buddha-Crown*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the **Golden-Wheel-King Buddha-Crown** (*Jīn-lún wáng fódǐng* — Skt. *Suvarṇacakrarāja-buddhoṣṇīṣa*) practice — a particular Buddha-Crown / *uṣṇīṣa* deity associated with the cosmic *cakravartin*-imperial iconography.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text gives the abridged daily-practice recitation method for the Golden-Wheel-King Buddha-Crown — the cosmic-imperial form of the Buddha-Crown deity. Like other *yàolüè* (essential-abridged) ritual manuals, it provides a portable daily-practice form of the longer Esoteric *sādhana*. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
