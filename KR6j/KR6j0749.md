# Tāi-jīn liǎng-jiè xuè-mài 胎金兩界血脈
*Blood-Line Lineages of the Garbhadhātu and Vajradhātu Realms*
by [[造玄]] (增補)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **Esoteric lineage-record** in the *xuè-mài* 血脈 ("blood-line") genre, compiled and supplemented (*zēng-bǔ* 增補) by **Zàoxuán** 造玄 [[造玄]], a late-Tang Esoteric ācārya at the **Cí-ēn-sì** 慈恩寺 in the Cháng-ān **Zuǒ-jiē** 左街. Preserved as **X59 no. 1074** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Prefaces

The opening colophon precisely dates the work: *Dà Táng Xián-tōng liù nián bā yuè shí qī rì Cháng-ān-chéng zuǒ-jiē Cí-ēn-sì Zàoxuán ā-shé-lí fù-shǔ shī-zī xuè-mài* 大唐咸通六年八月十七日長安城左街慈恩寺造玄阿闍梨付屬師資血脉 — "Great Tang, Xián-tōng 6, eighth month, seventeenth day, Cháng-ān-city Left-Ward Cí-ēn-sì, ācārya Zàoxuán entrusts the master-and-disciple blood-line." This corresponds to **8 September 865 CE**.

### Abstract

The *Xuè-mài* presents the parallel lineages of the two Esoteric schools:

- **Garbhadhātu (Tāi-zàng-jiè 胎藏界):** beginning with **Dà-rì** 大日 (Mahāvairocana) at the source.
- **Vajradhātu (Jīn-gāng-jiè 金剛界):** beginning with the parallel transmission.

The work parallels [[KR6j0748]] *Jīn-tāi liǎng-jiè shī-zī xiāng-chéng* by [[海雲]] but with the substantial advantage of an explicit transmission-date (865 CE), making it one of the most precisely-datable Tang Esoteric lineage documents. The format — *fù-shǔ shī-zī xuè-mài* "entrusted master-and-disciple blood-line" — is the formal Esoteric ritual document of lineage-transmission: each named master in the chain has officially "entrusted" his lineage to the next, and the document constitutes the institutional record of that transmission.

## Other points of interest

The 865 CE date places the *Xuè-mài* near the very end of the active Tang Esoteric school's institutional life, before the **Huì-chāng persecution** of 845 had scattered the school's residential establishment (though the Cháng-ān Cí-ēn-sì had survived as one of the principal institutional refuges). Zàoxuán's 865 lineage record is therefore one of the last major documents from the late-Tang Esoteric establishment.
