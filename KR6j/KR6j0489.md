# Mólìzhītiān yīyìn fǎ 摩利支天一印法
*Single-Mudrā Method of Mārīcī-deva*
by anonymous compiler

## About the work

A very short anonymous Esoteric ritual text on **Mārīcī**, isolating a single core mudrā (一印 *yīyìn*) and its associated mantra. Catalog meta and canwww (T21N1259) record no translator and no dynasty. The text is the most compressed practical instruction in the Mārīcī cluster ([[KR6j0483]]–[[KR6j0489]]).

### Abstract

The text gives the *yīyìn* (single fundamental mudrā) of the Mārīcī rite with its associated mantra and abbreviated visualisation. The format reflects an Indian *kalpa* convention by which a deity's full ritual programme can be reduced to a single core mudrā for daily or emergency use; this is the smallest unit of Mārīcī practice. The dating bracket is conservative.
