# Jīngāngdǐng jiàngsānshì dà yíguǐ fǎwáng jiàozhōng Guānzìzài púsà xīn zhēnyán yīqiè rúlái liánhuá dà màntúluó pǐn 金剛頂降三世大儀軌法王教中觀自在菩薩心真言一切如來蓮華大曼荼羅品
*Lotus Mahā-Maṇḍala Chapter on the Heart-Mantra of Avalokiteśvara, from the Trailokya-vijaya Mahā-vidhi Dharma-rāja Teaching of the Vajraśekhara*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual chapter extracted by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) from the larger *Vajraśekhara-tantra* (金剛頂經) corpus, specifically from the *Trailokyavijaya-mahā-kalpa-rāja* (降三世大儀軌法王教 — "Vidhi-Dharma-rāja teaching of the Conqueror of the Three Worlds"). The chapter focuses on the *Liánhuá dàmàntúluó* 蓮華大曼荼羅 (Lotus Great Maṇḍala) ritual centred on **Avalokiteśvara's heart-mantra**.

### Abstract

The *Vajraśekhara-tantra* (Skt. *Sarva-tathāgata-tattva-saṃgraha*) is the great *yoga-tantra* maṇḍala-encyclopaedia that Amoghavajra brought back from Sri Lanka in 746 and translated piecemeal in the following decades. The *Trailokyavijaya* section presents the wrathful aspect of the *Vajraśekhara* programme, in which the deities of the maṇḍala manifest in their wrathful forms to subdue the demons of the *trailokya* (three worlds). The present text extracts from this section the specific Lotus-family chapter centred on Avalokiteśvara — i.e. it gives the procedure for the construction and visualization of Avalokiteśvara's *Liánhuá dàmàntúluó* (Lotus Great Maṇḍala) within the wrathful *Trailokyavijaya* framework. This is one of the more technical of Amoghavajra's *yoga-tantra* extractions and presupposes substantial prior familiarity with the *Vajraśekhara* maṇḍala system.
