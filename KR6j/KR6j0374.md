# Jīn-gāng-dǐng yú-jiā zuì-shèng mì-mì chéng-fó suí-qiú jí-dé shén-biàn jiā-chí chéng-jiù tuó-luó-ní yí-guǐ 金剛頂瑜伽最勝祕密成佛隨求即得神變加持成就陀羅尼儀軌
*Vajraśekhara-Yoga Most-Excellent Secret Buddhahood-Realisation Following-Wishes-and-Immediately-Attaining Spirit-Transformation Empowerment-Accomplishment Dhāraṇī Ritual Manual*
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Tang Esoteric ritual manual translated by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]], 705–774). Colophon: *特進試鴻臚卿大興善寺三藏沙門大廣智不空奉詔譯*. The text functions as the *yí-guǐ* (ritual procedure) companion to the *Mahāpratisarā* sūtra-cycle [[KR6j0371]] (T1153) and [[KR6j0373]] (T1154), specifying the visualisations, *mudrās*, *mantras*, and procedural steps for the operative rite.

### Abstract

The text opens with **Cuī-è-qù Bodhisattva** (滅惡趣菩薩, *Apāya-vidhvaṃsana-bodhisattva*, "Bodhisattva-Who-Destroys-the-Evil-Destinies") rising from his seat in the great assembly of *Vairocana Buddha* (毘盧遮那佛) and addressing the Buddha: he wishes to expound, on behalf of the beings of evil destinies in the future *latter-dharma defiled-mixture world* (當來末法雜染世界惡趣眾生), a *dhāraṇī of sin-destroying-and-Buddhahood-realising* (滅罪成佛陀羅尼). The Buddha grants permission. The text then presents the full ritual sequence: enthronement of *Mahāpratisarā* in the *Vajraśekhara* mandala system, the eight *mudrā*-cycle, the *mantra* sequence, the visualisation-procedure, and the *siddhi*-application for both worldly fulfilment and supramundane Buddhahood-realisation. The text systematically integrates the *Mahāpratisarā* formula into the *Vajraśekhara*-mandala framework that defines Amoghavajra's mature Esoteric synthesis. It belongs with [[KR6j0371]], [[KR6j0373]], [[KR6j0375]], [[KR6j0376]] and [[KR6j0377]] in the cluster of Tang–Liáo–Sòng material on the *Mahāpratisarā* cycle.
