# Dà-bēi xīn tuó-luó-ní xiū-xíng niàn-sòng lüè-yí 大悲心陀羅尼修行念誦略儀
*Abridged Manual for the Cultivation and Recitation of the Great-Compassion-Heart Dhāraṇī*
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle abridged *yí-guǐ* 儀軌 setting out the recitation-practice for the *Mahā-karuṇā-dhāraṇī* of [[KR6j0260]]. The colophon names the translator as **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]), 唐北天竺三藏沙門大廣智. Although the *yí-guǐ* genre is best categorised as a translated *kalpa* manual, in practice this abridgement was prepared at the *Dà-xìng-shàn-sì* 大興善寺 in the late Tángmì period as a compact recitation-practice handbook based on the *Vajra-śekhara* method.

### Abstract

The opening ("依灌頂道場經，說修陀羅尼法門") explicitly grounds the manual in the *Guàn-dǐng dào-cháng* — the *Abhiṣeka-maṇḍala* — apparatus of the Tangmí *Vajra-śekhara* lineage, situating the *Mahā-karuṇā-dhāraṇī* recitation within the *yoga-tantra* sādhanā framework rather than as a stand-alone popular spell. The procedure prescribed: (1) entry into the bodhi-citta and *saṃvara-śīla* of the Buddhas; (2) abhiṣeka into the maṇḍala; (3) self-generation as Avalokiteśvara through visualisation; (4) recitation of the *Mahā-karuṇā-dhāraṇī* together with the forty-two principal mudrās of the deity; (5) closing dedication and *visarjana*. The work belongs with [[KR6j0269]] (Amoghavajra's full *Mahā-karuṇā* translation) and [[KR6j0273]] (Amoghavajra's full maṇḍala-manual) as the *yí-guǐ* triad of the Tángmì *Sahasrabhuja* cycle. CANWWW (T20N1066) places the work alongside [[KR6j0260]] as the parent text and the maṇḍala-manuals.
