# Jiànlì màntúluó jí jiǎnzé dìfǎ 建立曼荼羅及揀擇地法
*Method of Mandala-Construction and Site-Selection*
by [[慧琳]] (Huìlín, 集 — compiled)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual compiled by **Huìlín** 慧琳 ([[慧琳]], 737–820), the great Tang Buddhist lexicographer and disciple of Amoghavajra. The text covers the twin preparatory rites of **mandala-construction** (*jiànlì màntúluó* 建立曼荼羅) and **site-selection** (*jiǎnzé dìfǎ* 揀擇地法).

### Abstract

The text is one of the few Esoteric ritual works by Huìlín — better known for his great philological work the *Yīqiè jīng yīnyì* 一切經音義 ([[KR6s0013]], T2128). His Esoteric training under Amoghavajra is reflected in this short ritual manual, which combines the *mandala-construction* prescriptions with the *site-selection* prescriptions into a single working manual. The text supplements the more general anonymous *Fàntiān zédì fǎ* ([[KR6j0081]]) with a more specifically Esoteric framework drawing on the Amoghavajra tradition.

The composition dates from Huìlín's mature career, conventionally bracketed 770–820 (his death year).
