# Qiān-shǒu Guān-yīn zào cì-dì fǎ yí-guǐ 千手觀音造次第法儀軌
*Manual on the Method of Producing the Thousand-Hand Guān-yīn Image, in Sequenced Stages*
by [[善無畏]] (Shàn-wú-wèi, Śubhakarasiṃha, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle iconographic *yí-guǐ* 儀軌 on the construction (造) of the **Sahasrabhuja-Avalokiteśvara** image, attributed to **Śubhakarasiṃha** ([[善無畏]]) of central India (中天竺國三藏). The text functions as a craftsman's manual coupled with a ritual-consecration manual: it gives the iconographic specifications for sculpting, painting, or modelling the deity, ordered in a definite sequence (次第).

### Abstract

The opening describes the central image: 上首正體身大黃色 — "the principal embodied figure is golden-yellow in color" — seated cross-legged on a great jewelled lotus pedestal, the lotus having thirty-two principal petals each with smaller leaves between them, each leaf adorned with countless mahā-maṇi gems. The chief face of the deity is then described, with the supplementary heads (eleven-headed iconography), the forty-two principal arms each with their attribute (the *vajra*, the *cintāmaṇi*, the *padma*, the *cakra*, the bow and arrow, the willow branch, the noose, etc.), the colors and *mudrā* of each, and the maṇḍala-attendants. The text is the locus classicus for the iconographic specification of the Sahasrabhuja deity in Tángmì practice and represents the **Śubhakarasiṃha** lineage's approach — distinct from but harmonious with the **Vajrabodhi** / **Amoghavajra** lineage manuals ([[KR6j0255]], [[KR6j0273]]). It is one of the most-cited *yí-guǐ* in subsequent Japanese Shingon sculptural traditions concerning the *Senjū-Kannon* (千手觀音).
