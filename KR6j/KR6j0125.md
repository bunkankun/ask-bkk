# Pútíchǎng jīng lüèyì shì 菩提場經略義釋
*Brief Doctrinal Explanation of the Bodhi-Maṇḍa Sūtra* (Jp. *Bodaijō-gyō ryakugi-shaku*)
by [[圓珍]] Yuánzhēn (Enchin, 撰)

## About the work

A five-fascicle commentary by **Enchin** ([[圓珍]]) on the *Bodhi-Maṇḍa Sūtra* — i.e., the *Pútíchǎng suǒshuō yīzì dǐnglúnwáng jīng* ([[KR6j0124]], T19n0950) — Amoghavajra's Esoteric scripture on the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King.

### Abstract

Enchin's commentary is the principal Heian Tendai-Esoteric (Taimitsu) treatment of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King practice, integrating the *Buddhoṣṇīṣa-cakra-rāja* tradition with the broader Tendai-Esoteric doctrinal apparatus. The five-fascicle scope is substantial — comparable to Enchin's other major commentaries.

The composition dates from Enchin's post-Táng period at Onjō-ji (858–891).
