# Fó shuō Mólìzhītiān púsà tuóluóní jīng 佛說摩利支天菩薩陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Dhāraṇī of Mārīcī-deva-bodhisattva, Spoken by the Buddha* (Skt. *Mārīcī-dhāraṇī-sūtra*)
by [[不空]] (Bùkōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric dhāraṇī-sūtra on **Mārīcī** by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]); the second of the Mārīcī cluster ([[KR6j0483]]–[[KR6j0489]], T1254–T1259) and the first of the two parallel renderings preserved as T1255a/b. Korean Tripiṭaka K1354; Nanjio 0846. Alternate title 摩利支天經.

### Abstract

A more compact rendering of the Mārīcī-dhāraṇī material than the full *Huāmán* sūtra of [[KR6j0483]] (T1254): the framing-narrative is shorter, the iconographic exposition compressed, and the focus is on the dhāraṇī itself together with its essential ritual conditions. The text is thus the **practitioner's-handbook** counterpart of T1254. Date: Amoghavajra's Chángān activity, 746–774.
