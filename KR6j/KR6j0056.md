# Yīqiè mìmì zuìshàng míngyì dàjiào-wáng yíguǐ 一切祕密最上名義大教王儀軌
*Ritual Manual of the Highest-Esoteric Great-Teaching-King of All Secret Names and Meanings*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric ritual manual translated by **Dānapāla** ([[施護]]). The text gives the *yíguǐ* (ritual procedure) for the deity-system of the **Highest-Esoteric Great-Teaching-King** doctrine, with the principal mantras and their *míngyì* (names-and-meanings).

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.* The Taishō text gives the standard Sòng editorial header.

### Abstract

The work is a working Esoteric ritual manual paired with KR6j0055 (the *Wú-èr píngděng zuìshàng yújiā dàjiào-wáng jīng*) — a *yíguǐ* (ritual codification) accompanying the doctrinal *yoga-uttara-tantra* scripture. The two-fascicle structure covers preparatory rites and central *sādhana* sequence.

The translation dates from Dānapāla's career at the Sòng Yìjīng-yuàn (982–1017).
