# Dà shèng miàojíxiáng púsà shuō chúzāi jiàolìng fǎlún 大聖妙吉祥菩薩說除災教令法輪
*Dharma-Wheel of Teaching-Command for Calamity-Removal Spoken by the Great-Holy Wonderful-Auspicious Bodhisattva (Mañjuśrī)* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric *dharma-wheel* (*fǎ-lún* 法輪) text spoken by **Mañjuśrī** (大聖妙吉祥 *Dà shèng Miào-jíxiáng* — "Great-Holy Wonderful-Auspicious") for the **removal of calamities**. The text identifies Mañjuśrī as the principal Bodhisattva of cosmic-protective wisdom in the Esoteric framework.

### Prefaces


### Abstract

The text combines the popular Mañjuśrī devotional tradition with the calamity-elimination ritual framework typical of the Esoteric *dhāraṇī*-class literature. The "*jiào-lìng fǎ-lún*" (Dharma-Wheel of Teaching-Command) is an Esoteric protective formula attributed directly to Mañjuśrī. Anonymous; conventionally dated to the late Tang to Sòng period.
