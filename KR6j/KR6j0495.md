# Fó shuō Ráng-wú-lí tóng-nǚ jīng 佛說穰麌梨童女經
*Sūtra of the Maiden Jāṅgulī, Spoken by the Buddha* (parallel rendering)
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A second one-fascicle parallel rendering of the **Jāṅgulī** material treated above in [[KR6j0494]] (T1264a), also by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). Zhōnghuá H1421. CANWWW preserves this under T21N1264B alongside T21N1264A.

### Abstract

The same Jāṅgulī-dhāraṇī material as T1264a, with variations in mantra-form, framing-narrative, and the *anuśaṃsā* catalogue. The two renderings were both retained because the divergences in the Indian source-recensions could not be reduced. Date: Amoghavajra's Cháng-ān activity, 746–774.
