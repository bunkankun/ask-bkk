# Yī-zì dǐng-lún-wáng niànsòng yíguǐ 一字頂輪王念誦儀軌 (recension B)
*Recitation Ritual Manual of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King* (recension B, anonymous)

## About the work

The anonymous parallel recension B of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King recitation ritual manual (companion to KR6j0130 = T954A by Amoghavajra).

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

T954B is anonymously transmitted as a parallel recension of the same Tang Esoteric ritual material. Its preservation in the Taishō reflects the multiple parallel manuscript traditions for this widely-practiced ritual.
