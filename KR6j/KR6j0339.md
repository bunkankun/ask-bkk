# Pǔ-xián jīn-gāng sà-duǒ lüè yú-qié niàn-sòng yí-guǐ 普賢金剛薩埵略瑜伽念誦儀軌
*Abridged Yoga Recitation Ritual Manual of Samantabhadra-Vajrasattva*
by [[不空]] (Bù-kōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A short one-fascicle Tang Esoteric **abridged** *yí-guǐ* on **Samantabhadra-Vajrasattva** (普賢金剛薩埵 *Pǔ-xián Jīn-gāng-sà-duǒ*) — i.e., Vajrasattva in his Samantabhadra-emanation form, the central figure of the *Mahāsukha-Vajrasattva* cycle. Translated by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). The opening line declares the text's purpose: "I now expound the *Samantabhadra-bodhisattva-kāya-vāk-citta-vajra-japa-fǎ*..." (我今說普賢菩薩身口意金剛念誦法 — "the *vajra*-recitation-method of the body-speech-mind of Samantabhadra"). The compactness (*lüè* 略) of this text makes it useful as a portable practice manual, complementing the longer Samantabhadra *yí-guǐ* of [[KR6j0337]] and [[KR6j0338]].

### Abstract

The text expounds the operative procedure in the *Pañca-vajra-japa* (五金剛念誦, "five vajra-recitations") framework, organised by the **three mysteries** (三密, *tri-guhya*: body, speech, mind) of Samantabhadra-Vajrasattva. The *kāya-vajra* (body-vajra) is the deity-visualisation; the *vāk-vajra* (speech-vajra) is the *mūla-mantra* recitation; the *citta-vajra* (mind-vajra) is the contemplative-meditative absorption in the deity-form. The text guides the practitioner through the *japa* (recitation) of all three vajras, integrating them into a unified daily practice. The *Pañca-vajra-japa* framework is one of the most important Tang Esoteric soteriological apparatuses, deriving from the doctrine of the **Three Mysteries** (三密) introduced in the *Mahāvairocana-sūtra* and developed into the *Vajraśekhara* yoga-tantra.
