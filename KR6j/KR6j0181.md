# Rénwáng hùguó bōrě bōluómìduō jīng tuóluóní niànsòng yíguǐ 仁王護國般若波羅蜜多經陀羅尼念誦儀軌
*Dhāraṇī-Recitation Ritual Manual for the Sūtra of the Humane Kings' Protection of the State, in Mahā-Prajñāpāramitā*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the Esoteric *dhāraṇī*-recitation practice associated with his retranslation of the *Rénwáng jīng* 仁王經 ([[KR6f0019]] T246, the *Sūtra for Humane Kings* in his 765 recension). The text gives the *yoga-tantra*-grade ritual frame for the *Rénwáng* dhāraṇīs, providing the ceremonial liturgical context for the state-protection rites. It is the central liturgical companion to Amoghavajra's *Rénwáng* translation programme and was the principal reference for the Tang court rites of imperial protection.

### Abstract

The *Rénwáng jīng* state-protection cult was systematically reformulated by Amoghavajra under emperors Sùzōng (r. 756–762), Dàizōng (r. 762–779), in close alignment with the Tang court's response to the An Lushan rebellion. Amoghavajra translated his expanded *yoga-tantra*-grade *Rénwáng jīng* (T246) in 765, and produced this companion ritual manual T994 to support the state-protection rites that he and his disciples performed at the Cháng-ān and Luòyáng court complexes through the 765–774 period. The companion texts include [[KR6j0184]] (*Rénwáng bōrě niànsòng-fǎ* T995) and [[KR6j0185]] (*Rénwáng bōrě tuóluóní shì* T996), forming a tight cluster of three Esoteric extensions to the *Rénwáng* corpus. The principal study is Charles Orzech's *Politics and Transcendent Wisdom* (1998), which provides the comprehensive analysis of the *Rénwáng*-state-protection ritual programme and Amoghavajra's central role in it.
