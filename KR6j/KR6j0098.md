# Yàoshī liúli-guāng wáng qī fó běnyuàn gōngdé jīng niànsòng yíguǐ gōngyǎng fǎ 藥師琉璃光王七佛本願功德經念誦儀軌供養法
*Offering-Method for the Recitation Ritual of the Seven Bhaiṣajyaguru Buddhas*
by [[沙囉巴]] (Sherap, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Yuán-period offering-ritual manual translated from Tibetan by **Sherap** ([[沙囉巴]]), companion to KR6j0097 — the offering-component (*gōngyǎng fǎ*) of the seven-Bhaiṣajyaguru ritual cycle.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text gives the systematic offering procedures (*pūjā*) for the seven-Bhaiṣajyaguru ritual: the preparation of offering substances, the eight standard offerings (*aṣṭa-pūjā*: water for drinking, water for washing the feet, flowers, incense, lamps, perfume, food, music), and the merit-dedication. The translation dates from Sherap's career at the Yuán court (ca. 1290–1314).
