# Āchù rúlái niànsòng gòngyǎng fǎ 阿閦如來念誦供養法
*Method of Recitation and Offering to Akṣobhya Tathāgata*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the practice of **Akṣobhya** 阿閦 (the Vajradhātu eastern Buddha — *Imperturbable*) — the principal deity of the eastern Vajra-family in the Vajradhātu mandala.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The text gives the daily-practice manual for a practitioner whose *iṣṭa-devatā* is Akṣobhya — covering the *mudrā*-mantra-visualisation triplets for the central Akṣobhya practice, the offering sequences, and the *japa* of the Akṣobhya *mūla-mantra* and ancillary mantras. Akṣobhya is associated with the **mirror-wisdom** (*mahā-ādarśa-jñāna* / *dà-yuán-jìng-zhì* 大圓鏡智) and the **eastern direction** in the Vajradhātu mandala; the practice removes karmic obstructions by purifying the *ādāna* (storehouse-consciousness).

The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng-ān period (746–774).
