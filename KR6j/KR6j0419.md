# Miàojíxiáng píngděng yújiā mìmì guānshēn chéngfó yíguǐ 妙吉祥平等瑜伽祕密觀身成佛儀軌
*Ritual Handbook for Becoming-Buddha-in-this-Body through the Secret Body-Visualization of the Mañjuśrī Equality-Yoga*
by [[慈賢]] (Cíxián, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle ritual handbook (yíguǐ) extracted from the **Mahā-samaya / Mañjuśrī Equality** cycle, paired with [[KR6j0418]] (T1192, the parent sūtra) and [[KR6j0420]] (T1194, the *homa* extract). Translated by **Cíxián** ([[慈賢]]) under the Liáo court. Colophon: 宋大契丹國師中天竺摩竭陀國三藏法師慈賢譯.

### Abstract

The text is the practical *vidhi* for the central visualization of the *Mahāsamaya* / Mañjuśrī Equality cycle: **becoming-Buddha-in-this-very-body** (*sōku-shin-jōbutsu* / *jí-shēn chéng-fó*) through the secret *aṅga-yoga* of identifying the practitioner's body with Mañjuśrī's *jñāna-sattva*. It opens with the **Eight Great Vows** (八大願) and the corresponding refuge-and-vow mantra:

唵 沒騰達囉𤚥 左 僧伽左 怛哩囉怛曩誐囉 麼努怛𠻴 母兔唧黨 迦嚕弭也扇 娑嚩鉢囉刺闥…

(*oṃ buddha-traṇaṃ ca saṃghaṃ ca tri-ratna-graṃ manotaranaṃ mūla-cittam karoti me sarva-prabhāvataḥ…* — a refuge-vow formula in the *Mahā-samaya* register).

Following the refuge-vow, the text gives the practical sequence:

1. **Pre-rite preparation** — purification of altar, water, incense; deity-summons;
2. **Body-mandala generation** (*kāya-maṇḍala-utpatti*) — the practitioner generates Mañjuśrī's body in his own through a series of *bīja*-syllable visualizations;
3. **Three secrets** (三密) — the integration of body (mudrā), speech (mantra), and mind (visualization);
4. **Self-identification** as Mañjuśrī — the *deva-yoga* climax of the rite;
5. **Dissolution** and dedication of merit.

The text is the most *practical* of the three Cíxián Mañjuśrī-Equality translations and the primary working manual for the cycle. It reflects the same *Anuttara-yoga-tantra* milieu as [[KR6j0418]] and shares the parent sūtra's deity-list and mantra-cycle.

The dating bracket follows Cíxián's Liáo-period activity (1030–1080).
