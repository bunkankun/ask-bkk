# Bōbānnǎng jiéshǐbō jīngāng niànsòng yí 播般曩結使波金剛念誦儀
*Recitation-Manual of the Pañcakeśa-Vajra*
by anonymous compiler

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric recitation-manual whose title transliterates a Sanskrit deity-name — best parsed as *Pañcakeśa-vajra* (or a similar five-tuft-haired Vajra-Kumāra) — though the precise form is ambiguous and the catalog meta gives no translator. Within the Taishō Vol. 21 sequence, the text falls in the **Mahā-cakra-Vajra** cycle ([[KR6j0458]]–[[KR6j0460]]), and is paired editorially with those manuals as the third item.

### Abstract

The text gives the *niànsòng yí* 念誦儀 (recitation-manual) for the deity: visualisation, *bīja* and full *mantra*, *mudrā* sequence, and the abbreviated daily-practice format. As with most short un-attributed Tang Esoteric recitation-manuals, the text functions as a portable practitioner's handbook complementing a longer scripture. The dating bracket is conservative.
