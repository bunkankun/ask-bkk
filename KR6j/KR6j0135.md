# Jīngāngdǐng jīng yīzì dǐnglúnwáng yíguǐ yīnyì 金剛頂經一字頂輪王儀軌音義
*Phonological-Semantic Glossary on the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King Ritual Manual of the Vajraśekhara*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle *yīnyì* 音義 (phonological-semantic glossary) on the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King ritual manuals by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). The *yīnyì* genre — pronunciation and semantic glossing of obscure terms — is a Tang Chinese Buddhist scholarly tradition particularly important for Esoteric texts, where Sanskrit transliterations and *bīja*-syllables require special phonetic attention.

### Prefaces


### Abstract

The text glosses the technical terms, Sanskrit transliterations, and *bīja*-syllables in the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King ritual manuals ([[KR6j0124]], [[KR6j0130]], [[KR6j0132]], [[KR6j0134]]). It is a working philological reference for practitioners of these ritual texts. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774).
