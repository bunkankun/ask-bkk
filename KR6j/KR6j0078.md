# Fódǐng zūnshèng xīn pò-dìyù zhuǎn-yèzhàng chū-sānjiè mìmì tuóluóní 佛頂尊勝心破地獄轉業障出三界祕密陀羅尼
*Esoteric Dhāraṇī of the Buddha-Crown Victorious-Heart for Destroying Hells, Transforming Karmic Obstructions, and Transcending the Three Realms*
by [[善無畏]] (Śubhakarasiṃha, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *dhāraṇī* text attributed to **Śubhakarasiṃha** ([[善無畏]]), companion to KR6j0076 and KR6j0077. The text gives the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī* itself with brief ritual instructions — distinct from KR6j0077 which gives the full ritual manual.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

The three-text *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya* sub-corpus (KR6j0076–0078) gives the Tang Esoteric tradition's framework for the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya* practice in three layers of detail: the doctrinal-soteriological framework (KR6j0076), the full ritual manual (KR6j0077), and the *dhāraṇī*-text proper with brief notes (KR6j0078). The structure suggests these are three editorial layers of the same practice tradition, preserved together in the Tang Esoteric corpus.

The translation dates from Śubhakarasiṃha's Cháng-ān period (716–735).
