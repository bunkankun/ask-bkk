# Lèngyán jīng bǎo-jìng shū kē-wén 楞嚴經寶鏡疏科文
*Outline of the "Precious-Mirror" Commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[溥畹]] (撰)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **structural outline** (*kē-wén* 科文) accompanying **Lán-gǔ Pǔ-wǎn** 蘭谷溥畹 [[溥畹]]'s ten-fascicle *Lèngyán-jīng bǎo-jìng shū* 楞嚴經寶鏡疏 ([[KR6j0724]]). Pǔ-wǎn identifies himself as *Dà-Qīng qīn-cì Yún-nán Fǎ-jiè-sì jiǎng-jīng Guǎng-líng shā-mén Pǔ-wǎn zhuàn* 大清欽賜雲南法界寺講經廣陵沙門溥畹撰 ("of the Great Qīng, by imperial bestowal Sūtra-Lecturer at the Fǎ-jiè-sì in Yún-nán, monk of Guǎng-líng [Yáng-zhōu]"). Preserved as **X16 no. 314** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The *Kē-wén* sets out the hierarchical structural outline of the *Bǎo-jìng-shū*: the conventional threefold division (*xù* 序 / *zhèng-zōng* 正宗 / *liú-tōng* 流通), with each section subdivided according to the Xián-shǒu (Huá-yán) school's *shí-mén* 十門 method. The work is the navigational apparatus to the main commentary; together with the prefatory volume *Bǎo-jìng shū xuán-tán* ([[KR6j0723]]) and the main *Bǎo-jìng shū* ([[KR6j0724]]), it forms Pǔ-wǎn's complete three-volume *Lèngyán* commentarial apparatus. Composition: presumably 18th century (Kāng-xī through Qián-lóng era), contemporaneous with the *Bǎo-jìng-shū* itself.
