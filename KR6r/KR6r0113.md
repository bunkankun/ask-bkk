# Fǎ-mén chú-guǐ 法門鋤宄
*Hoeing Out the Brigands from the Dharma Gate*

written by [[淨符]] (Jìng-fú / Wèi-zhōng 位中, fl. mid-17th c., 著)

## About the work

A 1-juan early-Qīng Cáo-dòng-school polemical work by **Jìng-fú** 淨符 (號 **Wèi-zhōng** 位中 / **Bái-yán Wèi-zhōng** 白巖位中) — a Cáo-dòng monk of the **Shí-yǔ Míng-fāng** 石雨明方 (1593–1648) dharma-line, abbot of **Bái-yán Zūn-shèng-sì** 白巖尊聖寺 in Qián-táng. Per DILA, the work is dated to **Kāng-xī 6** = **1667** by Jìng-fú himself; an additional preface attributes a further enlargement to a Japanese reprint of **Genroku** 元祿 3 (庚午 = **1690**), prepared as a companion volume to the Japanese editor's own **《五家辨正》** *Wǔ-jiā biàn-zhèng*.

### Abstract

The title *Fǎ-mén chú-guǐ* — "hoeing out the brigands from the Dharma gate" — explicitly frames the work as a **polemical clean-up** of the contemporary Chán scene. The reprint preface compares the targets of the work to "**Liáng-shàng jūn-zǐ**" 梁上君子 ("gentlemen on the rafters" — i.e., thieves) and praises Jìng-fú for "leaving them no foothold" — even at the cost of relegating the *Yán-dēng* 嚴燈 and *Chán-dēng* 禪燈 compilations to mere "sea-mirage cities."

The polemic is principally directed against:
1. **Bogus lineage-claims** — figures who appropriate Chán transmission without genuine dharma-succession;
2. **The Línjì-side appropriation of Cáo-dòng materials** — Jìng-fú, as a Cáo-dòng partisan, defends the integrity of his own line against figures who treat the Cáo-dòng revival as derivative from Línjì transmissions (cf. the contrary argument in [[KR6r0110]] *Chán-dēng shì-pǔ*).
3. **Specific historical revisions** — including a critical re-reading of the canonical Mǎ-zǔ–**Yào-shān** 藥山 transmission narrative.

The work circulates alongside Jìng-fú's larger **《祖燈大統》** *Zǔ-dēng dà-tǒng* (98 juan + 2-juan index, completed Kāng-xī 11 = 1672) and his *Zōng-mén niān-gǔ huì-jí* 宗門拈古彙集 (45 juan, X1296, X66) as part of the same Cáo-dòng-side polemical-historiographical project.

## Other points of interest

The work is a representative document of the **early-Qīng Cáo-dòng / Línjì lineage controversy** — a controversy in which both sides produced mutually contradictory tabular lineage-genealogies, each with implicit doctrinal commitments. Jìng-fú's *Fǎ-mén chú-guǐ* is one of the principal **Cáo-dòng-side** documents.
