# Guān-yīn cí-lín jí 觀音慈林集
*Anthology of the Compassionate Forest of Avalokiteśvara*

compiled by [[弘贊]] (Hóng-zàn / Zài-shēn 在犙, 1612–1686, 輯)

## About the work

A 3-juan early-Qīng anthology of materials relating to the **cult of Avalokiteśvara** (Guān-yīn 觀音 / Guān-shì-yīn 觀世音), compiled by the Cáo-dòng *vinaya*-master **Zài-shēn Hóng-zàn** 在犙弘贊 (1612–1686) at his residence on **Dǐng-hú-shān** 鼎湖山 in Guǎngdōng. The work is conceived as a **comprehensive textual and biographical resource for Avalokiteśvara devotion**, gathering in three juan (1) the foundational *sūtra*-passages on Avalokiteśvara from across the Chinese Buddhist canon, (2) historical **gǎn-yìng** miracle-tales of Avalokiteśvara's intervention, and (3) Avalokiteśvara-related **liturgical and devotional materials**. The "compassionate forest" (*cí-lín* 慈林) of the title refers both to the wide range of canonical and devotional sources gathered and to Avalokiteśvara's own quality as the embodiment of *mahā-karuṇā*. Transmitted in the Xù-zàng-jīng as X1644.

### Prefaces

The work's table of contents organises the material under two main rubrics — **Upper Juan: the *sūtras* (上卷諸經)** and **Middle and Lower Juan: the miracle tales (中下卷感應傳)** — with a brief framing preface that situates Avalokiteśvara within the wider Mahāyāna soteriological scheme.

### Abstract

The work is structured into three juan as follows:

1. **Upper Juan — the canonical *sūtras***: a comprehensive listing of the principal Chinese Buddhist canonical texts treating Avalokiteśvara, with extracts of the foundational passages from each:
   - The **《觀音普門品玄義》** *Guān-yīn pǔ-mén pǐn xuán-yì* of Zhì-yǐ 智顗 (Tiāntái commentary on the *Lotus*-Avalokiteśvara chapter).
   - The **《悲華經》** *Bēi-huá jīng* (the *Karuṇā-puṇḍarīka-sūtra*).
   - The **《觀音勢至授記經》** *Guān-yīn Shì-zhì shòu-jì jīng*.
   - The **《大悲陀羅尼經》** *Dà-bēi tuó-luó-ní jīng*.
   - The **Saddharmapuṇḍarīka** *Pǔ-mén pǐn* itself.
   - The **《大佛頂首楞嚴經》** *Shǒu-léng-yán jīng*'s Avalokiteśvara passage (the famous "**24 sense-faculty** practitioners' exposition culminating in Avalokiteśvara's *yuán-tōng* through *érgēn* / hearing").
   - And further sūtras with substantial Avalokiteśvara content.

2. **Middle Juan — *gǎn-yìng* tales**: historical miracle-tales of Avalokiteśvara's compassionate intervention, drawn from the entire Chinese Buddhist tradition from the Six Dynasties through the late Míng. Major sources include the early *Guān-yīn yìng-yàn* anthologies by **Fù Liàng** 傅亮 (374–426), **Zhāng Yǎn** 張演 (5th c.), and **Lù Gǎo** 陸杲 (459–532) — the foundational Six Dynasties Avalokiteśvara-miracle anthologies; together with later Tang, Sòng, and Míng miracle-tales drawn from the standard *gāo-sēng zhuàn* tradition, the **《冥報記》** *Míng-bào jì*, the **《法苑珠林》** [[KR6s0002]] *Fǎ-yuàn zhū-lín*, and contemporary materials.

3. **Lower Juan — devotional and liturgical material**: Avalokiteśvara *zàn* 贊 (eulogies), **vow-inscriptions**, **icon-dedicatory pieces**, and selections of late-Míng / early-Qīng devotional poetry and prose addressed to Avalokiteśvara.

The work is the **most comprehensive single-volume Avalokiteśvara devotional handbook** of the early Qīng period, and supplied a model for subsequent late-imperial Avalokiteśvara anthologies. It is also a major source for the recovery of the **early Six Dynasties Avalokiteśvara-miracle anthologies** of Fù Liàng, Zhāng Yǎn, and Lù Gǎo, which survive only fragmentarily in their original form but are preserved in substantial extract in Hóng-zàn's compilation.

The dating bracket — **1640 to 1686** — covers Hóng-zàn's productive period; together with the **《兜率龜鏡集》** [[KR6r0165]] (Maitreya devotion) and the **《六道集》** [[KR6r0167]] (the Six Realms moral-pedagogical anthology), the *Guān-yīn cí-lín jí* forms the central panel of Hóng-zàn's three-fold devotional anthological project.

## Translations and research

- Chün-fang Yü, *Kuan-yin: The Chinese Transformation of Avalokiteśvara* (New York: Columbia Univ. Press, 2001) — the standard Western-language monograph on the Chinese Avalokiteśvara cult, drawing extensively on the *Guān-yīn cí-lín jí* and the related anthological literature.
- Robert Campany, *Strange Writing: Anomaly Accounts in Early Medieval China* (Albany: SUNY Press, 1996) — covers the Six Dynasties Avalokiteśvara-miracle anthologies extensively, with discussion of their later anthological transmission through works like the *Cí-lín jí*.
- 牧田諦亮, 《六朝古逸觀世音應驗記の研究》(Kyōto: Heiraku-ji shoten, 1970) — the standard textual-philological study of the early Avalokiteśvara-miracle anthologies, including their transmission through Hóng-zàn.
- 釋長慈, 《在犙弘贊禪師研究》, MA thesis, 國立中央大學, 2007 — biographical and bibliographical context.
- 廖肇亨, 〈晚明清初觀音信仰〉, in 《佛教思想與藝術》(Táiběi: Lián-jīng, 2010) — late-Míng / early-Qīng Avalokiteśvara cult.
