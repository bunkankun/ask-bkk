# Zūn-shàng jīng 尊上經
*Sūtra of the Most-Venerable* (the *Loma-saka-bhaddiya-sūtra*; parallel to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 166, the *Shì-jiàn jīng* 釋見經)
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Zūn-shàng jīng* is a single-fascicle Western Jìn 西晉 translation of a discourse on the qualities of the truly venerable disciple — the one who is foremost among brahmins, foremost among kṣatriyas, foremost among gods, foremost among humans. The Chinese parallel is T26[166] (the *Shì-jiàn jīng* 釋見經 of the *Madhyama-āgama*).

The text opens at the Jetavana monastery in Śrāvasti, with the Buddha (here addressed by the archaic 婆伽婆) addressing the monks on the qualities of the foremost.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉月氏國三藏竺法護譯」.

### Abstract

T77 was produced during Dharmarakṣa's Western Jìn career (266–313 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
