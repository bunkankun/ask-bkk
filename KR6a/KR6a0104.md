# Fó shuō fǎ-yìn jīng 佛說法印經
*Sūtra of the Dharma-Seal* (parallel to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 80, and to T103)
by [[施護]] (Shīhù / Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fó shuō fǎ-yìn jīng* is a single-fascicle Northern-Sòng translation of the Dharma-seals discourse, a parallel of [[KR6a0103|T103]] (Dharmarakṣa). The Chinese title 法印 ("Dharma-seal") is the standard term that displaced the older 聖法印.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Sòng-court translator's signature at the head: 「西天譯經三藏朝奉大夫試鴻臚卿傳法大師臣施護奉詔譯」.

### Abstract

T104 is one of Shīhù's series of Sòng-Institute renderings; the defensible bracket 982–1017 is recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
