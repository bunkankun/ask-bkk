# Zhǎngzhě shī-bào jīng 長者施報經
*Sūtra of the Householder's Reward for Giving* (the *Velāma-sūtra*; parallel to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 155, the *Xū-dá-duō jīng* 須達哆經, to T72 / T73, and to *Ekottara-āgama* 27.3)
by [[法天]] (Fǎtiān / Dharmadeva, 譯)

## About the work

The *Zhǎngzhě shī-bào jīng* is a single-fascicle Northern-Sòng translation of the *Velāma-sūtra*, the latest of the four-fold Chinese cluster (T26[155], T72, T73, T74). The Chinese title 長者施報 ("the householder's reward for giving") emphasises the discourse's central concern with the merit-fruit (*phala*) of householder generosity.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Sòng-court translator's signature at the head: 「西天譯經三藏朝散大夫試鴻臚卿傳教大師臣法天奉詔譯」.

### Abstract

T74 was produced during Fǎtiān's Sòng-Institute career; the defensible bracket 973–1001 is recorded in the frontmatter (cf. [[KR6a0002]]). Together with T26[155], T72 and T73, T74 provides a four-witness diachronic series for the *Velāma-sūtra* in Chinese.
