# Fó shuō Yāngjuéjì jīng 佛說鴦崛髻經
*Sūtra of Aṅgulimāla* (alternative form of *Aṅgulimāla*'s name; parallel to T118 / T120)
by [[法炬]] (Fǎjù, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Western Jìn 西晉 translation of the *Aṅgulimāla-sūtra*, doublet of [[KR6a0118|T118]]. The Chinese title 鴦崛髻 *Yāngjuéjì* is an alternative phonetic transcription of *Aṅgulimāla* differing slightly from the form 鴦掘摩 used in T118.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉沙門法炬譯」.

### Abstract

T119 was produced during Fǎjù's Western Jìn translation period (290–311 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost. The relationship to T118 (Dharmarakṣa) is closely parallel: the two translations are nearly contemporaneous (T118 c. 266–313, T119 c. 290–311) and may render the same Indic Vorlage from different angles.
