# Fó shuō Dà-ài-dào bānníhuán jīng 佛說大愛道般泥洹經
*Sūtra of the Parinirvāṇa of Mahāprajāpatī Gautamī* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 52.1, and to T149)
by [[白法祖]] (Bó Fǎzǔ, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Western Jìn translation by Bó Fǎzǔ of the canonical narrative of the *parinirvāṇa* of Mahāprajāpatī Gautamī 大愛道 — the Buddha's foster-mother and foundress of the bhikkhunī sangha. The Chinese parallels are T125[52.1] and [[KR6a0149|T149]] (Huìjiǎn). The Pāli parallel is in *Apadāna* and the Therīgāthā commentary tradition.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉河內沙門白法祖譯」.

### Abstract

T148 was produced during Bó Fǎzǔ's Western Jìn career (290–305 CE; cf. [[KR6a0005]]), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost. T148 is, alongside [[KR6a0005|T5]], one of only a handful of texts securely attributable to Bó Fǎzǔ.
