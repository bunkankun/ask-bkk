# Fó shuō bùzìshǒuyì jīng 佛說不自守意經
*Sūtra on the Unguarded Mind* (parallel to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 277)
by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fó shuō bù-zì-shǒu-yì jīng* is a single-fascicle Three-Kingdoms Wú 吳 translation of a discourse on the dangers of an unguarded mind and on the practice of mind-restraint. The Chinese parallel is T99[277] (the *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 277).

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Wú-period translator's signature at the head: 「吳月支優婆塞支謙譯」.

### Abstract

T107 was produced during Zhī Qiān's Wú-court translation period (222–253 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
