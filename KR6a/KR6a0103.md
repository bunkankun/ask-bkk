# Fó shuō shèngfǎyìn jīng 佛說聖法印經
*Sūtra of the Sacred Dharma-Seals* (Skt. *Ārya-dharma-mudrā-sūtra*; parallel to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 80, and to T104)
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fó shuō shèng-fǎ-yìn jīng* — sub-titled "天竺名阿遮曇摩文圖" ("the Indian name is Ārya-dharma-mudrā [-mudrā?]") — is a single-fascicle Western Jìn 西晉 translation of a discourse on the three "Dharma-seals" (*dharma-mudrā*): all conditioned phenomena are impermanent, all phenomena are without self, and *nirvāṇa* is peace. The Chinese parallels are T99[80] (the *Saṃyukta-āgama* parallel) and [[KR6a0104|T104]] (Shīhù).

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉月氏國三藏竺法護譯」.

### Abstract

T103 was produced during Dharmarakṣa's Western Jìn career (266–313 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost. The discourse is one of the foundational sources for the doctrine of the three Dharma-seals, which would become a fundamental classification of Buddhist doctrine in later East Asian scholasticism.
