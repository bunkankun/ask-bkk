# Fó shuō fàng-niú jīng 佛說放牛經
*Sūtra of the Cowherd* (the *Mahāgopālaka-sūtra*; parallel to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 1248, *Ekottara-āgama* 49.1, and to T124)
by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fó shuō fàng-niú jīng* is a single-fascicle Yáo-Qín 後秦 translation by Kumārajīva of the *Mahāgopālaka-sūtra*, the discourse comparing the eleven qualities of a good cowherd with the eleven qualities of a good monk. The Pāli parallel is MN 33 *Mahāgopālaka-sutta*; the Chinese parallels are T99[1248] (*Saṃyukta-āgama*), [[KR6a0124|T124]] (Xuánzàng), and *Ekottara-āgama* 49.1 (T125).

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Yáo-Qín translator's signature at the head: 「後秦龜茲國三藏鳩摩羅什譯」.

### Abstract

T123 was produced during Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān period (401–413 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. Like [[KR6a0035|T35]], T123 is one of the relatively few Āgama-extracts in Kumārajīva's principally Mahāyāna corpus. The Indic source is presumed lost.
