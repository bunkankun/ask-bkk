# Fómǔ bānníhuán jīng 佛母般泥洹經
*Sūtra of the Parinirvāṇa of the Buddha's Mother* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 52.1, and to T148)
by [[慧簡]] (Huìjiǎn, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Liú-Sòng translation of the *parinirvāṇa* of Mahāprajāpatī, parallel to [[KR6a0148|T148]]. The text is conventionally placed at the same date as Huìjiǎn's other 457 productions.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the LiúSòng translator's signature at the head: 「宋沙門慧簡譯」.

### Abstract

T149 was produced by Huìjiǎn at the Lùyěsì in 457 CE (cf. [[KR6a0043]]); the date is recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
