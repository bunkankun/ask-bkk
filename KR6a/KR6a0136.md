# Pínpísuōluó-wáng yì-fó gòng-yǎng jīng 頻毘娑羅王詣佛供養經
*Sūtra on King Bimbisāra's Visit to Make Offerings to the Buddha* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 34.5)
by [[法炬]] (Fǎjù, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Western Jìn translation of a discourse on King Bimbisāra of Magadha's visit to the Buddha to make offerings. The Chinese parallel is T125[34.5].

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉沙門釋法炬譯」.

### Abstract

T136 was produced during Fǎjù's Western Jìn translation period (290–311 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
