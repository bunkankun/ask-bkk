# Fó shuō Ānán tóng-xué jīng 佛說阿難同學經
*Sūtra of Ānanda's Fellow-Disciple* (sub-titled "出增一阿含經"; an extract from the *Ekottara-āgama*)
by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Eastern Hàn translation, sub-titled "出增一阿含經" ("excerpted from the *Ekottara-āgama*"), on the conversion of a fellow-disciple of Ānanda named Jué-duō 掘多. The text predates the Chinese *Ekottara-āgama* itself by 200 years; the sub-title is the cataloguer's later identification.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the canonical translator's signature at the head: 「後漢安息國三藏安世高譯」.

### Abstract

T153 was produced during Ān Shìgāo's Luòyáng period (148–170 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
