# Shòu xīn-suì jīng 受新歲經
*Sūtra of the Receiving of the New Year* (the *Pavāraṇā-sūtra*; parallel to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 121, to T62 / T63, to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 1212, to T100 sūtra 228, and to *Ekottara-āgama* 32.5)
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Shòu xīn-suì jīng* is a single-fascicle Western Jìn 西晉 translation of the *Pavāraṇā-sūtra* — a parallel of [[KR6a0050|T50]] (the *Shòu-suì jīng* 受歲經, also by Dharmarakṣa). The two are closely related but distinct: T50 corresponds to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 89, while T61 corresponds to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 121. The two cover related but not identical material on the *pavāraṇā* (the closing-of-the-rains-retreat) ceremony. The Taishō head-note flags T61 as the parallel of T62, T63, and the *Saṃyukta-āgama* / *Ekottara-āgama* parallels.

The text opens at the Mṛgāramātṛ Hall (鹿母園 = *Migāra-mātupāsāda*) in the East Park (東苑) of Śrāvasti, with the Buddha and the saṅgha gathered for the *pavāraṇā*.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉月氏國三藏竺法護譯」.

### Abstract

T61 was produced during Dharmarakṣa's Western Jìn career (266–313 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost. T61 belongs to a four-fold cluster (T61, T62, T63, T26[121]) all rendering essentially the same Indic *pavāraṇā* discourse from different angles.
