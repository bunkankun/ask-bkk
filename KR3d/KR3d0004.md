# Nóng-sāng yī-shí cuō yào 農桑衣食撮要
*Essentials of Agriculture, Sericulture, Clothing, and Food*
by [[魯明善]] (Lǔ Míngshàn, fl. 1314–1330, 元)

## About the work

A 2-juan Yuán-period agricultural manual by Lǔ Míngshàn, the Uyghur Yuán official, composed at Ān-fēng-lù during his magistracy in *Yán-yòu* 1 (1314). The work organises the agricultural year by lunar month, providing for each month the appropriate agricultural-and-sericultural tasks. The frontmatter brackets to 1314–1330.

### Abstract

The *Nóng-sāng yī-shí cuō yào* is one of two principal Yuán-period agricultural manuals (with the *Nóng-sāng jí yào*, KR3d0003). The work is methodologically distinguished by its *yuè-lìng* (monthly-ordinance) format. The bibliographic record: *Yuán shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Nóng-jiā lèi*.
