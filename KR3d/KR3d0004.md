# Nóngsāng yīshí cuō yào 農桑衣食撮要
*Essentials of Agriculture, Sericulture, Clothing, and Food*
by [[魯明善]] (Lǔ Míngshàn, fl. 1314–1330, 元)

## About the work

A 2-juan Yuán-period agricultural manual by Lǔ Míngshàn, the Uyghur Yuán official, composed at Ānfēnglù during his magistracy in *Yányòu* 1 (1314). The work organises the agricultural year by lunar month, providing for each month the appropriate agricultural-and-sericultural tasks. The frontmatter brackets to 1314–1330.

### Abstract

The *Nóngsāng yīshí cuō yào* is one of two principal Yuán-period agricultural manuals (with the *Nóngsāng jí yào*, [[KR3d0003]]). The work is methodologically distinguished by its *yuèlìng* (monthly-ordinance) format. The bibliographic record: *Yuán shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Nóngjiā lèi*.
