# Nóng zhèng quán shū 農政全書
*Complete Treatise on Agricultural Administration*
by [[徐光啟]] (Xú Guāngqǐ, 1562–1633, 明)

## About the work

A 60-juan Late-Míng comprehensive agricultural encyclopedia by Xú Guāngqǐ, the major Late-Míng Confucian-Christian polymath. The work was substantially complete at Xú's death in 1633 but was published posthumously in 1639 with editorial work by Chén Zǐlóng 陳子龍 (1608–1647). The work synthesises pre-modern Chinese agricultural literature (*Qí mín yào shù*, the two *Nóng shū*, *Nóng-sāng jí yào*, etc.) with substantial new material drawn from Xú's own field experience and from Jesuit-introduced Western agricultural-technical material. Distinguished by its substantial *Bèi huāng* 備荒 (famine-preparedness) section and its incorporation of *Jiù huāng běn cǎo* (KR3d0006) materials. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1620–1639.

### Abstract

The *Nóng zhèng quán shū* is the most comprehensive pre-modern Chinese agricultural encyclopedia and the principal Late-Míng agricultural-administrative reference. The work has had unbroken Qīng-period reception and is the canonical pre-modern Chinese agricultural-administrative treatise. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Nóng-jiā lèi*.
