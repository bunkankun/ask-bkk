# Nóng-sāng jí yào 農桑輯要
*Essentials of Agriculture and Sericulture*
imperially commissioned and compiled by [[司農司]] (Sī-nóng-sī, the Yuán Office of Agricultural Affairs)

## About the work

A 7-juan Yuán imperial agricultural manual, commissioned by Khubilai Khan and prepared by the *Sī-nóng-sī* (Office of Agricultural Affairs). The work was distributed for use by local officials throughout the empire from *Zhì-yuán* 10 (1273) onward; the standard recension dates to *Zhì-yuán* 23 (1286). The work synthesises the *Qí mín yào shù* (KR3d0001) and Sòng-period agricultural literature with Yuán-period field experience. The frontmatter brackets to 1273–1286.

### Abstract

The *Nóng-sāng jí yào* is the principal Yuán-imperial agricultural manual. The bibliographic record: *Yuán shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Nóng-jiā lèi*.
