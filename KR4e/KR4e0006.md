# Tài-shī Chéng-yì-bó Liú Wén-chéng gōng jí 太師誠意伯劉文成公集
*The Collected Works of Sir Liú Wén-chéng, Grand Preceptor and Earl of Sincere Resolve*
by [[劉基]] (撰)

## About the work
The SBCK Lóngqìng-era (1572) recension of the collected works of **Liú Jī** 劉基 [[劉基]] (1311–1375), titled with his full set of honorifics: *Tài-shī* 太師 (Grand Preceptor — a posthumous honour), *Chéng-yì-bó* 誠意伯 (his Hóngwǔ-era fief), *Wén-chéng* 文成 (his posthumous title). The collection contains substantially the same body of material as the WYG-Sìkù *Chéng-yì-bó wén jí* [[KR4e0005]] — Liú's six component works (*Yù-lí zǐ*, *Fù-bù jí*, *Xiě-qíng jí*, *Lí-méi jí*, *Chūn-qiū míng-jīng*, *Yì-yùn lù*) — but reorganized and re-cut by **Xiè Tíng-jié** 謝廷傑 (the *Qiú-fēng* 虬峯 of the postface), Patrol Censor of the Two Zhèjiāng, in Lóngqìng 6 (壬申, 1572). The transmission line is independent of the Chénghuà *Zhèjiāng Cháyuàn* line that underlies the WYG.

### Prefaces
The collection opens with a long postface by the *Pī-yuè* 闢嶽 (named **Wáng Liè** 王烈 in the text), submitted to Xiè Tíng-jié on the completion of the recutting. Wáng's postface explains the transmission history: the first cutting was by **Wáng Jǐng-hòu** 王景後 (the *Wáng tài-shǐ* 王太史) of the early Yǒnglè period (c. 1403–1424), after which there had been three further cuttings, all gradually obscured by time and editorial disorder. In Jiājìng 38 (己未, 1559) the senior local elder **Fán Dǒu-shān** 樊斗山 had reprinted the collection at the *Běi-jī xún-yuàn* 北畿巡院, but copies had remained scarce in the Southeast and that recension had moreover dropped the *Chūn-qiū míng-jīng* fascicle, making it not a complete collection. The Lóngqìng 6 reissue under Xiè Tíng-jié therefore re-collated from multiple base texts, restored the dropped fascicle, and produced the complete recension printed here. The opening preface by [[宋濂]] 宋濂 to the original *Fù-bù jí* (Hóngwǔ-era) is also preserved.

### Abstract
This SBCK collection is the canonical Wàn-lì-era (more precisely, late Lóngqìng) transmission line of Liú Jī's collected works, parallel and complementary to the Chénghuà-based line that underlies the WYG-Sìkù [[KR4e0005]]. The postface of Wáng Liè dates the recutting to Lóngqìng 6, third month (i.e. spring 1572). The Patrol Censor **Xiè Tíng-jié** 謝廷傑 of **Yùzhāng** 豫章 (Nánchāng, Jiāngxī) is the principal sponsor; the actual editorial work is credited to Wáng Liè (a local administrator of Chǔzhōu 處州, Liú's home region). The contents are: the *Yù-lí zǐ* 郁離子 (Liú's most famous work, the Yuán-period political-allegorical fable collection of *c.* 1356–1358); the *Fù-bù jí* 覆瓿集 (his *gǔ-wén*); the *Xiě-qíng jí* 寫情集 and *Lí-méi jí* 犁眉集 (his verse); the *Chūn-qiū míng-jīng* 春秋明經 (his classical-exegetical writing on the *Chūn-qiū*); and the *Yì-yùn lù* 翊運錄 (the post-mortem documentary record compiled by Liú's grandson [[劉廌]] from imperial documents related to Liú Jī's fief and posthumous honour).

The text-historical significance of the SBCK recension is that it preserves the *Wàn-lì*-era restoration of the *Chūn-qiū míng-jīng* — a fascicle that the *Sìkù* editors found in a related Lóngqìng line and that the Chénghuà-era *Cháyuàn* line had also retained, but the intervening Jiājìng *Běi-jī xún-yuàn* reprint had dropped. For all matters of Liú's biography, school-lineage, and modern scholarship, see the [[劉基]] person note and the parallel entry at [[KR4e0005]].

## Other points of interest
This is the parallel SBCK transmission to the WYG-Sìkù [[KR4e0005]]; the two should be read together, since differences in selection and ordering bear directly on the editorial politics of Wàn-lì-era literary canon-formation.
