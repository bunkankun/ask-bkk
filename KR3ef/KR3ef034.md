# Héjiān Shānghán xīn yào 河間傷寒心要
*Hé-jiān's Essential Heart of Cold Damage*
by [[鎦洪]] (Liú Hóng, *fl.* late Jīn / early Yuán, 1200s–1270s)

## About the work

A short Jīn / Yuán treatise in one juan compiling and summarizing the doctrines of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù's "fire-and-heat" *Shānghán* school, by Líu Hóng 鎦洪 — a Hé-jiān-school disciple of Liú Wánsù's lineage. The work is a key vehicle for the transmission of Liú Wánsù's *Shānghán* doctrine into the Yuán-period medical curriculum.

### Abstract

The composition window 1200–1280 brackets the late Jīn and early Yuán activity of Liú's school. Liú Hóng (note: the surname character 鎦 is a less-common form of 劉) is mentioned in the standard JīnYuán medical bibliography but his exact biographical details are not preserved beyond his role as a *Héjiān*-school commentator. The work is preserved within the *Héjiān liù shū* 河間六書 corpus and is sometimes treated as supplementary to Liú Wánsù's *Shānghán biāoběn xīnfǎ lèicuì* ([[KR3ef009]]).
