# Shānghán zuǎn lùn 傷寒纘論
*Compiled Discourse on Cold Damage*
by [[張璐]] (Zhāng Lù, *zì* Lùyù 路玉, *hào* Shíwán Lǎorén 石頑老人, 1617–1700, 清)

## About the work

A two-juan early-Qīng commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* by the great Sūzhōu physician Zhāng Lù 張璐, completed 1667. The *Zuǎn lùn* is paired with Zhāng's *Shānghán xù lùn* 傷寒緒論 in a unified set published as the *Shānghán zuǎn-xù lùn* ([[KR3ef021]]); the *Zuǎn lùn* presents Zhāng's annotation of the canonical *Shānghán* text, while the *Xù lùn* (Threads) extends the commentary to ancillary clinical problems. Zhāng Lù is counted with [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng and [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān as one of the "Three Great Physicians of the Early Qīng."

### Abstract

The composition date 1667 is given by Zhāng's preface, signed Kāng-xī 6. Zhāng follows the *Cháng-shā fù-gǔ* (ancient-text-restoration) line of Fāng Yǒuzhí and Yú Chāng but tempers the polemical edge of those works, retaining selected glosses from Chéng Wúyǐ. The *Zuǎn lùn* is the first work of Zhāng's mature medical œuvre, preceding his great *Yī tōng* 醫通 ([[KR3e0095]], 1695) by nearly thirty years.
