# Shānghán lùn shù yì 傷寒論述義
*Exposition of the Meaning of the Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[丹波元堅]] (Tamba no Motokata / Dānbō Yuánjiān, 1795–1857, 江戶)

## About the work

A five-juan late-Edo philological-doctrinal commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* by Tamba no Motokata 丹波元堅 at the Igaku-kan 醫學館 in Edo. The *Shù yì* is the doctrinal companion to his father [[丹波元簡]] (Mototane)'s textual edition *Shānghán lùn jí yì* 傷寒論輯義: where *Jí yì* restores the canonical text by collating SòngYuán recensions, *Shù yì* expounds the canonical meaning, drawing on and adjudicating among the major SòngQīng commentary traditions.

### Abstract

Composition window 1827–1840 is bracketed by Motokata's emergence as senior figure at the Igaku-kan after his brother [[丹波元胤]] (Motoin)'s death in 1827 and the work's published circulation by the mid-Tenpō era. The text takes the structure of the received Sòng-edition *Shānghán lùn* (六經 conformation chapters) and supplies, clause-by-clause, a philologically rigorous exposition of canonical meaning that combines the *Shānghán* textual-restoration concerns of the Tamba school with the syndrome-discrimination concerns of the Qīng *Shānghán* tradition (Kē Qín, Xú Dàchūn, Yóu Yí). The work is the second of Motokata's three great *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* contributions, alongside the encyclopedic [[KR3ef051]] *Shānghán guǎng yào* and the *Jīnguì yùhán yào lüè shù yì* ([[KR3ef085]]).

The *Shù yì* is the principal Japanese commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* in the philological tradition founded by Mototane, and remains an indispensable reference for any text-critical work on the *Shānghán* canon.
