# Shānghán lùn biànzhèng guǎng zhù 傷寒論辯證廣註
*Discriminating-Syndrome Extensive Commentary on the Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[汪琥]] (Wāng Hù, *zì* Yìnxí 苓友, *hào* Yìnxí Lǎorén 苓友老人, *fl.* late Kāngxī, 清)

## About the work

A fourteen-juan early-Kāng-xī (1680) extensive commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* by the Sūzhōu physician Wāng Hù 汪琥. The work — emphasizing the precise discrimination of clinical syndrome (辯證) as the foundation of *Shānghán* practice — is paired with Wāng's companion *Zhōnghán lùn biànzhèng guǎng zhù* ([[KR3ef046]]) on the parallel "*zhōnghán*" (central / true cold-damage) tradition.

### Abstract

Composition date 1680 (Kāngxī 19) given by Wāng's preface. Wāng was a Sūzhōu contemporary of [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí and the broader early-Kāng-xī Jiāngnán medical circle. His commentary is moderately revisionist — accepting some of the Fāng Yǒuzhí / Yú Chāng restoration line while retaining selected Chéng Wúyǐ glosses — and is distinguished by the depth of its clinical exemplification: each section pairs canonical text with detailed clinical case-discussion. The work circulated in Qīng prints through the 18th century and was reissued in Republican-era medical anthologies.
