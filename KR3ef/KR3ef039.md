# Shānghán xún yuán 傷寒尋源
*Tracking the Source of Cold Damage*
by [[呂震名]] (Lǚ Zhènmíng, *zì* Yǒngyuán 永元, *hào* Wàng-zhāi 望齋, 1799–1858, 清)

## About the work

A three-juan late-Qīng (1856) *Shānghán* commentary by the Hángzhōu physician Lǚ Zhènmíng 呂震名 (1799–1858). The work — characteristically titled "Tracking the Source" — is in the *Cháng-shā fù-gǔ* (ancient-text-restoration) line of Fāng Yǒuzhí and Yú Chāng but with the more synthetic and harmonizing approach typical of Dàoguāng-Xiánfēng-period clinical writing.

### Abstract

Composition date 1856 is given by Lǚ's preface (Xián-fēng 6). Lǚ was *jǔ-rén* of 1822, holding minor official appointments in Zhèjiāng. He turned to medicine in mid-career and wrote the *Xún yuán* as a contribution to the late-Qīng *Shānghán* synthesis movement. The work is preserved in modest 19th-century prints and was reprinted in early Republican anthologies.
