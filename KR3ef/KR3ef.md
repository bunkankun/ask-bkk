## Scope and scholarly tradition

`KR3ef` 傷寒金匱 (*Shānghán Jīnguì*, "Treatises on Cold Damage and the Golden Casket") is the sixth sub-division of `KR3e` 醫家類 (Masters — Medicine) and gathers the entire commentarial and pedagogical tradition built around the two canonical clinical works attributed to the Eastern-Hàn physician [[張機]] Zhāng Jī (Zhòngjǐng 仲景, *c.* 150–219): the *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 ([[KR3ef001]]) and the *Jīnguì yào lüè* 金匱要略 ([[KR3ef079]]). The two are halves of a single original work, the *Shānghán zá-bìng lùn* 傷寒雜病論 — the *Shānghán lùn* covering acute externally-contracted "cold damage" febrile disease in a six-channel (*liù-jīng* 六經) progression, the *Jīnguì yào lüè* covering chronic and miscellaneous internal disorders (*zá-bìng* 雜病). Together they constitute the foundational clinical canon of the entire late-imperial Chinese medical tradition: as Endymion Wilkinson (*Chinese History: A New Manual*, §41.3.1 #3–4) registers, the *Shānghán lùn* and *Jīnguì yào lüè* are the two pre-Táng titles around which the post-Sòng curriculum of practical medicine was organized, alongside the *Huángdì nèijīng* and the *Nàn-jīng* (themselves in [[KR3ea]]). All 93 entries are drawn from the 華夏文獻 (*hxwd*) supplementary database rather than the Wényuāngé *Sìkù quánshū*: while several major Qīng commentaries (Xú Bīn [[KR3ef092]], Wèi Lìtóng [[KR3ef084]], the imperial *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn* sections [[KR3ef022]] [[KR3ef086]]) were absorbed into 子部·醫家 of the *Sìkù*, the bulk of the late-Míng / Qīng / Republican / Edo commentarial literature was excluded as technical and survives only through *hxwd*.

Chronologically the division spans nearly eighteen centuries. The anchor is the late-Hàn *Shānghán zá-bìng lùn* (composed *c.* 196–220 during the Jiàn'ān epidemic that killed two thirds of Zhāng's clan), recovered and re-edited as two separate works by the Western-Jìn physician [[王叔和]] Wáng Shū-hé (*c.* 210–285), and definitively stabilized by [[林億]] Lín Yì and his Sòng校正醫書局 (Bureau for Editing Medical Texts) colleagues in 1065 ([[KR3ef001]] [[KR3ef004]] [[KR3ef079]] [[KR3ef080]]). The Northern Sòng then produced the first wave of free-standing commentary ([[韓袛和]] [[KR3ef008]], [[龐安時]] [[KR3ef049]], [[朱肱]] [[KR3ef010]]); the Southern Sòng produced [[許叔微]] Xǔ Shūwēi's foundational case-record and mnemonic works ([[KR3ef044]] [[KR3ef061]] [[KR3ef063]]) and [[郭雍]] Guō Yōng's philological supplementation ([[KR3ef062]]). Jīn-Yuán doctrine is decisively shaped by [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ's 1144 *Zhù jiě Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef005]] [[KR3ef040]]) — the first sustained line-by-line commentary, standard until the late Míng — and by the Hé-jiān 河間 fire-and-heat re-reading of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù ([[KR3ef009]] [[KR3ef011]]) and his Yuán transmitters. The late-Míng / early-Qīng *cuòjiǎn chóng-dìng* 錯簡重訂 ("re-ordering the disordered slips") revisionist movement, opened by [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí ([[KR3ef015]], 1593) and amplified by [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng ([[KR3ef026]] [[KR3ef027]], 1648), inaugurated a century of competing high-Qīng reorganizations: [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù's moderated restoration ([[KR3ef020]] [[KR3ef021]]), [[柯琴]] Kē Qín's "by-syndrome" *Lái-sū jí* set ([[KR3ef013]] [[KR3ef014]] [[KR3ef007]] [[KR3ef033]] [[KR3ef042]]), [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí's "by-treatment-method" *Guàn-zhū jí* ([[KR3ef019]]) and [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn's "by-prescription-type" *Lèi fāng* ([[KR3ef064]]). The Qiánlóng court fixed an imperial harmonization in [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān's *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn · Dìng-zhèng Zhòngjǐng quán-shū* ([[KR3ef022]] [[KR3ef086]] [[KR3ef067]], 1742). The 19th century brought [[黃元御]] Huáng Yuányù's "Four Sages" cosmological synthesis ([[KR3ef016]] [[KR3ef054]] [[KR3ef088]]) and its Sì-chuān continuation in [[鄭壽全]] Zhèng Qīn'ān's 火神派 ([[KR3ef060]]); the Chén Xiūyuán pedagogical line ([[KR3ef066]] [[KR3ef090]]); and the late-Qīng 中西匯通 Sino-Western synthesis of [[唐宗海]] Táng Zōnghǎi ([[KR3ef028]] [[KR3ef091]]). The Edo *Igaku-kan* 醫學館 produced under the [[丹波元簡]] Tamba-Mototane / [[丹波元堅]] Tamba-Motokata philological lineage ([[KR3ef018]] [[KR3ef051]] [[KR3ef053]] [[KR3ef082]] [[KR3ef085]]) the modern East-Asian *textus receptus* of the *Shānghán* canon, paralleled by the *kohō* 古方 critical-reading tradition of Yamada Seichin and Nakanishi Iyū ([[KR3ef025]] [[KR3ef029]] [[KR3ef052]] [[KR3ef069]] [[KR3ef073]] [[KR3ef074]]). The Republican period closes the division with the Shanghai *jīng-fāng* 經方 revival of [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐngfǔ ([[KR3ef093]]), the Japanese back-transmission of [[湯本求真]] Yumoto Kyūshin's *Huáng Hàn yī xué* ([[KR3ef059]]), and the Zhèjiāng *tōng-sú* line of [[俞根初]] Yú Gēnchū and [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén ([[KR3ef031]] [[KR3ef036]]).

## Important texts and text clusters

**The Hàn canonical works and their early editors.** Two textual events frame everything that follows: Wáng Shū-hé's late-3rd-century recovery and bifurcation of Zhāng Jī's original 16-juan *Shānghán zá-bìng lùn*, and the校正醫書局 stabilization of 1065 under Lín Yì.
- *Shānghán lùn (Sòng běn)* 傷寒論（宋本） [[KR3ef001]] — Zhāng Jī, *c.* 196–220; Sòng校正 recension 1065. Ten juan, 22 chapters; the textual basis of every subsequent commentary, preserved through the Zhào Kāiměi 趙開美 1599 facsimile.
- *Shānghán zá-bìng lùn jí jiě* 傷寒雜病論集解 [[KR3ef004]] — Zhāng Jī, ed. Lín Yì et al., 1065. Synthetic Sòng-bureau reunification edition of cold-damage and miscellaneous-disease material.
- *Jīnguì yùhán jīng* 金匱玉函經 [[KR3ef080]] — Zhāng Jī, *c.* 196–220; rec. [[何焯]] Hé Zhuō 1712. Eight-juan Sòng校正 parallel recension recovered in Kāngxī 51 and preserving variant readings against the *Shānghán lùn (Sòng běn)*.
- *Jīnguì yào lüè fāng lùn* 金匱要略方論 [[KR3ef079]] — Zhāng Jī, ed. Wáng Shū-hé; Sòng校正 reconstruction by Wáng Zhū 王洙 / Lín Yì *c.* 1066. Three juan, recovered from worm-eaten *guǎn gé* 館閣 (palace-library) fragments; the foundational canonical text for *zá-bìng* 雜病.

**Republican "old-text" reconstructions.** Three twentieth-century recensions claim to preserve pre-Sòng readings of Zhāng Jī's original 16-juan work — none are accepted by mainstream textual criticism, but all are influential in modern East-Asian *jīng-fāng* 經方 practice.
- *Shānghán zá-bìng lùn (Guìlín gǔ-běn)* 桂林古本 [[KR3ef002]] — [[羅哲初]] Luó Zhéchū transmission, alleged 46th-generation Zhāng-clan source, surfaced 1909–1939. 16 juan; mainstream judgment is Republican reconstruction.
- *Gǔ-běn Kāngpíng Shānghán lùn* 古本康平傷寒論 [[KR3ef003]] — manuscript with Heian colophon dated Kāngpíng 3 (1060), collated [[大塚敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu (Shōwa 12, 1937). Central to modern Japanese Kanpō 漢方; the most respected of the three "old-text" lines.

**Northern Sòng commentaries.** The first wave of independent commentary, opening in the immediate aftermath of the 1065 bureau collation.
- *Shānghán wēi-zhǐ lùn* 傷寒微旨論 [[KR3ef008]] — [[韓袛和]] Hán Zhīhé, 1086–1093. Earliest free-standing theoretical *Shānghán* work; articulates the 陽虛傷寒 yáng-deficiency doctrine.
- *Shānghán zǒng-bìng lùn* 傷寒總病論 [[KR3ef049]] — [[龐安時]] Páng Ānshí, 1100, preface by [[黃庭堅]] Huáng Tíngjiān. Six juan; integrates *Shānghán* with the *yùn qì* 運氣 cosmological apparatus.
- *Lèi-zhèng huó-rén shū* 類證活人書 [[KR3ef010]] — [[朱肱]] Zhū Hóng, 1107–1118. 22 juan; reorganization as 101 dialogic questions; the most-circulated Sòng *Shānghán* derivative and the dominant Northern-Sòng vehicle for the *Shānghán* curriculum down to the Yuán.

**Southern Sòng — Xǔ Shūwēi and Guō Yōng.** Two Southern-Sòng physicians redirect commentary toward case-narrative and textual supplementation.
- *Shānghán jiǔ-shí lùn* 傷寒九十論 [[KR3ef044]] — [[許叔微]] Xǔ Shūwēi, *c.* 1132. The 90 clinical case-narratives that found the entire later *yī-àn* 醫案 (case-record) genre in Chinese medicine.
- *Shānghán bǎi zhèng gē* 傷寒百證歌 [[KR3ef061]] — Xǔ Shūwēi, *c.* 1133–1154. The most celebrated versified mnemonic of *Shānghán* syndromes.
- *Shānghán fā wēi lùn* 傷寒發微論 [[KR3ef063]] — Xǔ Shūwēi, *c.* 1133–1154. Discursive essay companion to the *Bǎi zhèng gē*.
- *Zhòngjǐng Shānghán bǔ wáng lùn* 仲景傷寒補亡論 [[KR3ef062]] — [[郭雍]] Guō Yōng, 1181–1182. 20 juan; the first Sòng commentary to treat the *Shānghán lùn* as a philologically incomplete text requiring supplementation from parallel sources.

**Chéng Wúyǐ and the standard pedagogical commentary (Jīn).** The Jīn-period text-and-doctrine synthesis that dominates the curriculum from the late 12th century until the late Míng.
- *Zhù jiě Shānghán lùn* 註解傷寒論 [[KR3ef005]] — [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ, 1144. Ten juan; the first sustained line-by-line commentary on the *Shānghán lùn*, grounding each clinical prescription in *Sùwèn* / *Língshū* doctrine. Standard until Fāng Yǒuzhí and Yú Chāng.
- *Shānghán míng-lǐ lùn* 傷寒明理論 [[KR3ef040]] — Chéng Wúyǐ, 1156. Four juan; 50 topical essays plus a *Lùn fāng* 論方 covering 20 prescriptions — the first sustained Chinese essay-form exposition of *Shānghán* doctrine, paired with the line-by-line commentary [[KR3ef005]].
- *Zhāng Qīngzǐ Shānghán lùn* 張卿子傷寒論 [[KR3ef006]] — [[張遂辰]] Zhāng Suìchén (Qīngzǐ, 1589–1668), 1640–1668. Seven juan; the principal vehicle by which Chéng Wúyǐ's commentary persisted in the Hángzhōu *wéi-hù jiù-lùn* 維護舊論 ("preserve the received text") line through the early Qīng.

**The Hé-jiān 河間 fire-and-heat school (Jīn-Yuán).** [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù's Jīn re-reading of *Shānghán* through fire-and-heat (*huǒ-rè* 火熱) doctrine, against Chéng Wúyǐ's strict cold-damage line.
- *Shānghán zhí-gé* 傷寒直格 [[KR3ef011]] — Liú Wánsù, 1172–1200. Three juan; the principal vehicle of fire-and-heat reinterpretation, promoting 白虎湯, 涼膈散 and 承氣 as first-line treatments.
- *Shānghán biāo-běn xīn-fǎ lèi-cuì* 傷寒標本心法類萃 [[KR3ef009]] — Liú Wánsù (attributed), 1172–1200. Two juan; root-and-branch (*biāo-běn* 標本) framework for the Hé-jiān doctrine.
- *Hé-jiān Shānghán xīn yào* 河間傷寒心要 [[KR3ef034]] — [[鎦洪]] Líu Hóng, 1200–1280. Compact summary of Liú Wánsù doctrine for the Yuán curriculum.
- *Liú Hé-jiān Shānghán yī jiàn* 劉河間傷寒醫鑑 [[KR3ef037]] — [[馬宗素]] Mǎ Zōngsù, *c.* 1297–1320. Yuán transmission of Liú Wánsù's *Shānghán* doctrine.
- *Shānghán qián fǎ* 傷寒鈐法 [[KR3ef048]] — Mǎ Zōngsù, *c.* 1297–1320. The most distinctive Yuán contribution — a calendrical-prognostic system fusing cosmological-correlative reasoning with *Shānghán* clinical decision.
- *Shānghán xīn jìng bié jí* 傷寒心鏡別集 [[KR3ef056]] — [[張從正]] Zhāng Cóngzhèng (Zǐ-Hé, 1156–1228), rec. by disciple [[常德]] Cháng Dé. The 攻邪 *gōng-xié* ("attacking-pathogens") school's *Shānghán* application.

**Pre-revisionist Míng works.** Before Fāng Yǒuzhí, the Míng produced four important integrative compendia.
- *Shānghán liù shū* 傷寒六書 [[KR3ef038]] — [[陶華]] Táo Huá (Jié-ān), 1445. Six juan; foundational mid-Míng synthesis of Chéng Wúyǐ with Hé-jiān adjustments.
- *Shānghán kuò yào* 傷寒括要 [[KR3ef041]] — [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ (Shìcái, 1588–1655), 1649. Late-Míng pedagogical compendium.
- *Shānghán zhì lì* 傷寒治例 [[KR3ef072]] — [[劉純]] Liú Chún (Zōnghòu), 1370–1412. Early-Míng symptom-category handbook in the Zhū Dānxī lineage; precursor to the Qīng *fāng-lèi* approach.
- *Shānghán zhāi jǐn* 傷寒摘錦 [[KR3ef071]] — [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (Mì-zhāi, 1499–1582), 1549–1582. Family-medicine *Shānghán* digest oriented to paediatric and gynaecological practice.
- *Lèi biān Shānghán huó rén shū kuò zhǐ-zhǎng tú lùn* 類編傷寒活人書括指掌圖論 [[KR3ef077]] — [[李知先]] Lǐ Zhīxiān (Sòng), illus. [[熊宗立]] Xióng Zōnglì (Jiàn-yáng 鐘德堂, 1409–1482), Míng. Illustrated palm-pointing mnemonic recasting of Zhū Hóng's *Huó rén shū kuò*.

**The 錯簡重訂派 / *Cháng-shā fù-gǔ* movement (late-Míng / early-Qīng revisionism).** The single most consequential interpretive movement in the post-Yuán division. Its proponents argue that Wáng Shū-hé's bifurcation and reordering of Zhāng Jī's text "scrambled the bamboo slips" (錯簡 *cuòjiǎn*) and must be undone to restore the "Cháng-shā ancient" 長沙古本 sequence.
- *Shānghán lùn tiáo biàn* 傷寒論條辨 [[KR3ef015]] — [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí, 1589–1593, printed 1593. Eight juan; the foundational document of the late-imperial *Shānghán* revisionist tradition and the first sustained "Cháng-shā fù-gǔ" argument against the Wáng / Chéng received text.
- *Shàng lùn piān* 尚論篇 [[KR3ef026]] — [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng (Jiāyán, 1585–1664), 1648. Eight juan; reorganizes the *Shānghán* into 397 *fǎ* 法; the principal early-Qīng restoration commentary. (Subject of [[林起龍]] Lín Qǐlóng's 1674 plagiarism charge against Fāng Yǒuzhí.)
- *Shàng lùn hòu piān* 尚論後篇 [[KR3ef027]] — Yú Chāng, 1648–1664. Four juan; continuation covering 溫疫, 風疫, 運氣 and convalescence.
- *Shānghán zuǎn lùn* 傷寒纘論 [[KR3ef020]] — [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù (Shíwán Lǎorén, 1617–1700), 1667. Two juan; the moderated-revisionist line by one of the "Three Great Physicians of Early Qīng."
- *Shānghán zuǎn lùn · Xù lùn* 傷寒纘論_傷寒緒論 [[KR3ef021]] — Zhāng Lù, 1667. Full four-juan set with the *Xù lùn* covering 溫病, paediatric and pregnancy *Shānghán*, and convalescence.
- *Shānghán sù-yuán jí* 傷寒溯源集 [[KR3ef012]] — [[錢潢]] Qián Huáng (Tiānlái), 1707. Ten juan; harmonizes Yú Chāng / Fāng Yǒuzhí revisionism with the Chéng Wúyǐ pedagogical tradition.
- *Shānghán lùn biàn-zhèng guǎng zhù* 傷寒論辯證廣註 [[KR3ef045]] — [[汪琥]] Wāng Hù (Yìnxí), 1680. 14 juan; Sūzhōu moderate-revisionist commentary with rich clinical exemplification.
- *Zhōng-hán lùn biàn-zhèng guǎng zhù* 中寒論辯證廣註 [[KR3ef046]] — Wāng Hù, 1680–1689. Three juan; companion treatise drawing the doctrinal distinction between 中寒 (true cold-attack) and 傷寒 (external cold-damage).
- *Shānghán jīng jiě* 傷寒經解 [[KR3ef023]] — [[姚球]] Yáo Qiú (Yùlán), 1727. Ten juan; early-Yōngzhèng Wú-mén commentary in the ancient-text-restoration line.

**The Kē Qín *Lái-sū jí* set — the "channel-pattern" 以證類證 school.** [[柯琴]] Kē Qín's reorganization of the *Shānghán lùn* by 證 (syndrome) rather than by Wáng Shū-hé's six-channel sequence is the single most influential Qīng commentarial project after Yú Chāng.
- *Shānghán lùn zhù lái sū jí* 傷寒論註來蘇集 [[KR3ef014]] — Kē Qín, 1669–1674. Four juan; the core annotation portion.
- *Shānghán lái sū jí* 傷寒來蘇集 [[KR3ef013]] — Kē Qín, 1669–1674. Eight juan; the full *Lái-sū jí* set; one of the "four most-cited Qīng commentaries."
- *Shānghán lùn yì* 傷寒論翼 [[KR3ef007]] — Kē Qín, 1669–1674. Two juan; 13 doctrinal essays underpinning the *Lái-sū jí*.
- *Shānghán fù yì* 傷寒附翼 [[KR3ef033]] — Kē Qín, 1669–1684. Two juan; supplementary pharmacology and dosage volume.
- *Shānghán fǎ zǔ* 傷寒法祖 [[KR3ef042]] — Kē Qín, posthumous *c.* 1735–1780, printed by disciple [[任越庵]] Rèn Yuè'ān. Two juan; posthumous compilation of Kē Qín's late essays.

**Other "four most-cited Qīng commentaries."** Two further Qīng commentaries join Yú Chāng's *Shàng lùn piān* and Kē Qín's *Lái-sū jí* in the standard high-Qīng canon.
- *Shānghán guàn-zhū jí* 傷寒貫珠集 [[KR3ef019]] — [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí (*c.* 1650–1749), 1729. Eight juan; organizes the *Shānghán* by 治法 (eight treatment-methods) rather than by syndrome or channel; the *cuò-jiǎn* line at its most philologically restrained.
- *Shānghán lùn lèi fāng* 傷寒論類方 [[KR3ef064]] — [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (Língtāi, 1693–1771), 1759. Four juan; reorganization by *fāng-lèi* 方類 (prescription-type) rather than six-channel. Convergent with Yoshimasu Tōdō's contemporary Edo *Ruijuhō* 類聚方 and the textual foundation of the modern *jīng-fāng* 經方 movement.

**The imperial Qīng curriculum — *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn* (1742).** [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān (*c.* 1689–1748), as chief editor of the imperial 太醫院 textbook commissioned by Qián-lóng, harmonized the major commentary lines into a state-sanctioned standard.
- *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn · Dìng-zhèng Zhòngjǐng quán-shū · Shānghán lùn zhù* 醫宗金鑑·訂正仲景全書傷寒論註 [[KR3ef022]] — Wú Qiān, [[劉裕鐸]] Liú Yùduó et al. (奉敕撰), 1742–1749. 17 juan; the imperial-curriculum *Shānghán* commentary.
- *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn · Dìng-zhèng Zhòngjǐng quán-shū · Jīnguì yào lüè zhù* 醫宗金鑑·訂正仲景全書金匱要略註 [[KR3ef086]] — Wú Qiān et al., 1742. Eight juan; the matching imperial *Jīnguì* edition.
- *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn · Shānghán xīn-fǎ yào jué* 醫宗金鑑·傷寒心法要訣 [[KR3ef067]] — Wú Qiān et al., 1742. Five juan; the versified mnemonic 歌訣 *Shānghán* digest, standard examination text from 1749.

**The Huáng Yuányù "Four Sages" 四聖 cosmological school and its Sì-chuān 火神 continuation.** A second 18th-century reorganizing line, doctrinally distinct from the *cuò-jiǎn* revisionists.
- *Shānghán xuán jiě* 傷寒懸解 [[KR3ef016]] — [[黃元御]] Huáng Yuányù (1705–1758, Shāndōng), 1748–1753. 14 juan; reads the *Shānghán lùn* through *zhōng-qì* 中氣, *fú-chén* 浮沈, and *Yì-jīng* 坎離 (Kǎn-Lí) framework.
- *Shānghán shuō yì* 傷寒說意* [[KR3ef054]] — Huáng Yuányù, 1748–1758. Ten juan; pedagogical clinical companion to the *Xuán jiě* with spleen-stomach-centred reading.
- *Jīnguì xuán jiě* 金匱懸解 [[KR3ef088]] — Huáng Yuányù, 1748–1758. 22 juan; the *Jīnguì* member of the "Four Sages" *xuán-jiě* set.
- *Shānghán shěn zhèng biǎo* 傷寒審證表 [[KR3ef065]] — [[包誠]] Bāo Chéng (Xīngyán), 1870, preface by [[李漢章]] Lǐ Hànzhāng. Tabulated chart of Huáng Yuányù's *Xuán jiě*.
- *Shānghán héng lùn* 傷寒恆論 [[KR3ef060]] — [[鄭壽全]] Zhèng Qīn'ān (Shòuquán, 1804–1901, Sìchuān), 1874–1894. Ten juan; the foundational text of the Sì-chuān 火神派 ("Fire-spirit School"); applies the *Yì-jīng* Kǎn-Lí cosmology in a continuous living tradition into the present.

**Mid- to late-Qīng commentaries.** A range of further high-Qīng works elaborate, refine, or critique the major commentarial schools.
- *Shānghán dà bái* 傷寒大白 [[KR3ef047]] — [[秦之楨]] Qín Zhīzhēn (Huáng-shì), 1714. Four juan; moderate Kāng-xī pedagogical exposition.
- *Shānghán jiān zhèng xī yì* 傷寒兼證析義 [[KR3ef057]] — [[張倬]] Zhāng Zhuó (son of Zhāng Lù), 1671–1715. One juan; specialist dialogue-format treatise on cold-damage with comorbidities.
- *Shānghán lùn dú* 傷寒論讀 [[KR3ef030]] — [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng (Yáo-fēng), 1764–1796. Four juan; mid-Qiánlóng pedagogical re-arrangement by clinical pattern.
- *Shānghán lùn gāng mù* 傷寒論綱目 [[KR3ef017]] — [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn'áo (1717–1776), 1774–1775. 16 juan; component of *Shěn shì zùnshēng shū* 沈氏尊生書 in synthetic outline-and-subordinate format.
- *Shānghán zhí zhǐ* 傷寒直指 [[KR3ef075]] — [[強健]] Qiáng Jiàn (the catalog's attribution to Chéng Wúyǐ is erroneous), 1680–1720. 16 juan; manuscript-only for 240 years; sharp critique of Yú Chāng's "Chán" rhetoric, incorporating Lín Lán 林瀾's *Zhé zhōng* 折中.
- *Shānghán jié jué* 傷寒捷訣 [[KR3ef035]] — [[嚴則庵]] Yán Zé'ān comp., ed. [[裘慶元]] Qiú Qìngyuán (1873–1947), Qīng. One-juan versified mnemonic preserved in *Sān-sān yī shū* 三三醫書.
- *Shānghán lùn yáng míng bìng shì* 傷寒論陽明病釋 [[KR3ef070]] — [[陸懋修]] Lù Mào-xiū (Jiǔ-zhī, 1818–1886), 1875–1884. Four juan; single-conformation monograph integrating *Shānghán* yáng-míng with *wēn-bìng* qì-fēn doctrine.
- *Shānghán bǔ lì* 傷寒補例 [[KR3ef043]] — [[周學海]] Zhōu Xuéhǎi (Jiànchí, 1856–1906, *jìnshì* 1885), 1882–1906. Two juan; 88 supplementary clinical case-records.
- *Shānghán xún yuán* 傷寒尋源 [[KR3ef039]] — [[呂震名]] Lǚ Zhènmíng (1799–1858), 1856. Three juan; late-Qīng synthesis in the ancient-text-restoration line.

**The Chén Xiūyuán *qiǎn-zhù* line and the *zhōng-Xī huì-tōng* 中西匯通 extension.** [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ (Xiūyuán) and his successor [[唐宗海]] Táng Zōnghǎi (the same lineage continues in [[KR3ea044]] for the *Nèijīng*).
- *Shānghán yī jué chuàn jiě* 傷寒醫訣串解 [[KR3ef066]] — Chén Niànzǔ (1753–1823), with juan 6 by nephew [[陳道著]] Chén Dàozhù, 1815–1856. Six juan; continuous-exposition clinical primer.
- *Shānghán lùn qiǎn-zhù bǔ zhèng* 傷寒論淺註補正 [[KR3ef028]] — Chén Niànzǔ (淺注), Táng Zōnghǎi (補正, 1846–1897), 1893. Seven juan; key document of late-Qīng 中西匯通 (Chinese-Western synthesis) school.
- *Jīnguì yào lüè qiǎn zhù* 金匱要略淺註 [[KR3ef090]] — Chén Niànzǔ with *Jīnguì gē kuò* by son [[陳靈石]] Chén Língshí, 1810–1823. Ten juan; foundational late-Qīng pedagogical *Jīnguì* text, with a famous preface by [[林則徐]] Lín Zéxú (1830).
- *Jīnguì yào lüè qiǎn zhù bǔ zhèng* 金匱要略淺註補正 [[KR3ef091]] — Chén Niànzǔ + Táng Zōnghǎi (補正) + [[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi (重校), 1884–1893. Nine juan; four-layer 中西匯通 edition.

**The Qīng *Jīnguì yào lüè* commentaries.** Independent of the imperial harmonization and the *qiǎn-zhù* line, the Qīng produced a sustained sequence of high-quality *Jīnguì* commentaries.
- *Jīnguì yào lüè lùn zhù* 金匱要畧論註 [[KR3ef092]] — [[徐彬]] Xú Bīn (Zhōngkě, disciple of Yú Chāng), 1671. 24 juan; the earliest extant Qīng *Jīnguì* commentary; absorbed into the *Sìkù quánshū*.
- *Jīnguì yùhán jīng èr zhù* 金匱玉函經二註 [[KR3ef083]] — [[趙以德]] Zhào Yǐdé (Yuán-Míng, Zhū Dānxī tradition) + [[周揚俊]] Zhōu Yángjùn (Yǔ-zǎi, early-Qīng supplement), 1687. 22 juan; the "Two Annotators" foundational commentary, base text for all subsequent Qīng *Jīnguì* exegesis.
- *Jīnguì yào lüè guǎng zhù* 金匱要略廣註 [[KR3ef087]] — [[李彣]] Lǐ Wén (Zhèn-shēng, Xī-yuán), 1682. Eight juan; distinctively broad citation including the military classic *Hǔ qián jīng* 虎鈐經.
- *Jīnguì yào lüè xīn diǎn* 金匱要略心典 [[KR3ef089]] — [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí, 1729–1749. Three juan; preface by [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn arguing that the *Jīnguì* corrects the misconception that Zhōng-jǐng was a *Shānghán* specialist.
- *Gāo zhù Jīnguì yào lüè* 高註金匱要略 [[KR3ef081]] — [[高學山]] Gāo Xuéshān (Hàn-zhì, Yú-yáo), 1720–1770. Six juan; notable for moving the canonical opening passage and applying a *wǔ-cháng* Five-Phases framework.
- *Jīnguì yào lüè fāng lùn běn yì* 金匱要略方論本義 [[KR3ef084]] — [[魏荔彤]] Wèi Lìtóng (Niàntíng), 1720–1725. 22 juan; "original meaning" commentary in *kǎo-zhèng* 考證 style; absorbed into the *Sìkù*.

**The Tamba 丹波 Edo philological circle at the *Igaku-kan* 醫學館.** The single most consequential textual project in the post-1700 division: Tokugawa shogunal-physician scholarship that produced the modern East-Asian *textus receptus* of the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* canon.
- *Shānghán lùn jí yì* 傷寒論輯義 [[KR3ef018]] — [[丹波元簡]] Tamba no Mototane 多紀元簡 (1755–1810, head of the *Igaku-kan*), 1800–1810. Seven juan; defines the modern East-Asian *textus receptus* of the *Shānghán* canon.
- *Shānghán guǎng yào* 傷寒廣要 [[KR3ef051]] — [[丹波元堅]] Tamba no Motokata 多紀元堅 (1795–1857), preface by [[丹波元胤]] Tamba no Motoin (Mototane's elder son), 1825–1827. 12 juan; bibliography of 150 commentators — the densest pre-Qīng *Shānghán* commentary survey in any language.
- *Shānghán lùn shù yì* 傷寒論述義 [[KR3ef053]] — Tamba no Motokata, 1827–1840. Five juan; doctrinal companion to his father's *Jí yì* [[KR3ef018]].
- *Jīnguì yùhán yào lüè jí yì* 金匱玉函要略輯義 [[KR3ef082]] — Tamba no Mototane, 1800–1810. Six juan; Edo philological *Jīnguì* edition, preserving verbatim the Sòng校正 preface of Lín Yì.
- *Jīnguì yùhán yào lüè shù yì* 金匱玉函要略述義 [[KR3ef085]] — Tamba no Motokata, 1842. Three juan; supplementary commentary collated against the Korean *Yī fāng lèi jù* 醫方類聚, transmitting otherwise-lost Sòng-Yuán readings.

**The Edo *kohō* 古方 critical-reading tradition.** A second Edo line, philologically critical and clinically pragmatic, distinct from the Tamba *Igaku-kan*'s text-establishment programme.
- *Shānghán lùn jí chéng* 傷寒論集成 [[KR3ef025]] — [[山田正珍]] Yamada Seichin 山田正珍 (1749–1787), 1789. Ten juan; late-Edo *koihō* 古意方 harmonization of Chinese commentary lines with the Yoshimasu Tōdō tradition.
- *Shānghán zhī yán-jiū* 傷寒之研究 [[KR3ef029]] — [[中西惟忠]] Nakanishi Iyū 中西惟忠 (1724–1803), 1789–1812. Ten juan; second-generation *kohō* response in clinical-pragmatic mode.
- *Shānghán lùn gāng yào* 傷寒論綱要 [[KR3ef052]] — [[橘南谿]] Tachibana Nankei 橘南谿 (1753–1805, pupil of Yoshimasu Tōdō), 1780–1805. One juan; brief *kohō* binary yáng / yīn schema.
- *Shānghán yòng yào yán jiū* 傷寒用藥研究 [[KR3ef069]] — [[川越正淑]] Kawagoe Masayoshi 川越正淑, 1790–1797. Three juan; *kohō* pharmacological study, postface by [[妻木直記]] Tsumaki Naoki.
- *Shānghán mài zhèng shì* 傷寒脈證式 [[KR3ef073]] — Kawagoe Masayoshi, 1790–1830. Three juan; an extraordinary skeptical attack on the Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng attribution, proposing that the *Shānghán* is structured on *Yì-jīng* hexagrams — anticipating modern textual criticism by nearly two centuries.
- *Shāng fēng yuē yán* 傷風約言 [[KR3ef074]] — [[後藤省]] Gotō Sei 後藤省, 1740–1800. One juan; sharp *kohō* polemic against contemporary Edo eclectic pedagogy.
- *Shānghán lùn Liú shì zhuàn* 傷寒論劉氏傳 [[KR3ef078]] — [[劉棟田良]] Ryū Tōden 劉棟田良 (a sinicized Edo *kanpō* author), 1750–1900. One juan; bibliographically obscure; source file empty.

**The Republican-era synthesis (Chinese).** The 1920s–1930s Shanghai *jīng-fāng* 經方 revival and the Zhèjiāng *tōng-sú* 通俗 line jointly carry the imperial-era tradition into the 20th century.
- *Cáo-shì Shānghán Jīnguì fā wēi hé kān* 曹氏傷寒金匱發微合刊 [[KR3ef093]] — [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐngfǔ (1866–1937), preface by [[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi, 1924–1937. Two juan; foundational document of the Shanghai-school *jīng-fāng* movement and teaching text of the Shanghai TCM Specialist College.
- *Huáng Hàn yī xué* 皇漢醫學 [[KR3ef059]] — [[湯本求真]] Yumoto Kyūshin 湯本求真 (1876–1941), 1927–1928. Three juan; watershed early-20th-century Japanese revival document; back-translated into Chinese in the 1930s and decisive for the Shanghai *jīng-fāng* movement (the principal influence on Cáo Yǐngfǔ).
- *Shānghán lùn huì-zhù jīng-huá* 傷寒論匯注精華 [[KR3ef024]] — [[惲鐵樵]] Yùn Tiěqiáo (1878–1935), 1923. Eight juan; Shanghai pedagogical synthesis anthology.
- *Zhòng-dìng tōng-sú Shānghán lùn* 重訂通俗傷寒論 [[KR3ef036]] — [[俞根初]] Yú Gēnchū (1734–1799), re-edited by [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén, 1934. 12 juan; definitive posthumous re-edition; foundational Zhèjiāng-school *tōng-sú* text.
- *Zēng-dìng tōng-sú Shānghán lùn* 增訂通俗傷寒論 [[KR3ef031]] — Hé Liánchén (1861–1929), 1916. 12 juan; expansion of Yú Gēnchū's vernacular treatise.
- *Gǎn zhèng bǎo fá* 感症寶筏 [[KR3ef055]] — [[吳坤安]] Wú Kūn'ān, re-edited by [[何炳元]] Hé Bǐngyuán (= Hé Liánchén), 1911. Six juan; 1911 retitling of Wú Kūn'ān's *Shānghán zhǐ zhǎng* [[KR3ef032]] integrating 六經 with 三焦 frameworks.
- *Shānghán zhǐ-zhǎng* 傷寒指掌 [[KR3ef032]] — Wú Kūn'ān (Bì-Huá), 1796. Four juan; widely-reprinted pedagogical handbook integrating *Shānghán* with *wēn-bìng*.
- *Shānghán wēn-yì tiáo biàn* 傷寒瘟疫條辨 [[KR3ef050]] — [[楊濬]] Yáng Jùn (Lì-shān, 1705–1795), 1784. Six juan; major late-Qiánlóng synthesis of 傷寒 with the 溫疫 tradition, integrating Wú Yòukě and Yè Tiānshì.
- *Shānghán biàn yào jiān jì* 傷寒辨要箋記 [[KR3ef058]] — [[程門雪]] Chéng Ménxuě (1902–1972, founding president of the Shanghai TCM University), 1930–1972. One juan; notebook-style commentary; source file empty.
- *Shānghán jiě dú liáo fǎ* 傷寒解毒療法 [[KR3ef068]] — [[聶雲臺]] Niè Yúntái (1880–1953), 1935–1948. One juan; synthesis of *Shānghán* with modern bacteriology and vitamin biochemistry.

## Topics

The division is organized around a small number of long-running debates.

**錯簡重訂 vs. 維護舊論 (re-ordering vs. preserving the received text).** The single defining controversy in the post-Yuán *Shānghán* tradition is whether Wáng Shū-hé's late-3rd-century bifurcation and reordering of Zhāng Jī's original text was a faithful recovery or a "scrambling of the bamboo slips" (*cuò-jiǎn* 錯簡). The 錯簡重訂派 (Fāng Yǒuzhí [[KR3ef015]], Yú Chāng [[KR3ef026]] [[KR3ef027]], Zhāng Lù [[KR3ef020]] [[KR3ef021]], Qián Huáng [[KR3ef012]], Wāng Hù [[KR3ef045]], Yáo Qiú [[KR3ef023]]) argued that the received Sòng-bureau text required reorganization to restore the "Cháng-shā ancient" 長沙古本 sequence. The 維護舊論派 (Zhāng Suìchén [[KR3ef006]], the Tamba *Igaku-kan* [[KR3ef018]] [[KR3ef051]] [[KR3ef053]], Qiáng Jiàn [[KR3ef075]]) insisted on the integrity of the received Sòng校正 text. Lín Qǐlóng's 1674 plagiarism charge against Fāng Yǒuzhí — accusing Yú Chāng of secretly recycling Fāng's *Tiáo biàn* — is the defining episode in the polemical history of the movement.

**Principles of *Shānghán* reorganization.** Within the *cuò-jiǎn* line, four competing logical principles for restructuring the text emerged, each producing a distinct major commentary: by 397 *fǎ* (Yú Chāng [[KR3ef026]]); by 證 syndrome (Kē Qín [[KR3ef013]] [[KR3ef014]]); by 治法 eight treatment-methods (Yóu Yí [[KR3ef019]]); by 方類 prescription-type (Xú Dàchūn [[KR3ef064]] — convergent with Yoshimasu Tōdō's contemporary Edo *Ruijuhō*).

**The "four most-cited Qīng commentaries."** Yú Chāng's *Shàng lùn piān* [[KR3ef026]], Kē Qín's *Lái-sū jí* [[KR3ef013]], Yóu Yí's *Guàn-zhū jí* [[KR3ef019]], and Xú Dàchūn's *Lèi fāng* [[KR3ef064]] — four works, four organizing principles, jointly constitute the high-Qīng *Shānghán* canon and the immediate source-base for the imperial *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn*.

**Six-channel doctrine and its cosmological reinterpretations.** Zhāng Jī's six-channel (*liù-jīng* 六經) framework — 太陽 → 陽明 → 少陽 → 太陰 → 少陰 → 厥陰 — is the structural backbone of the *Shānghán* canon. Successive cosmological reinterpretations recast it: Liú Wánsù's fire-heat ([[KR3ef009]] [[KR3ef011]]), Mǎ Zōngsù's *qián fǎ* calendrical-prognostic ([[KR3ef048]]), Huáng Yuányù's *zhōng-qì* / Kǎn-Lí ([[KR3ef016]] [[KR3ef054]] [[KR3ef088]]), and Zhèng Qīn'ān's 火神 ([[KR3ef060]]) all reorganize clinical reasoning by superimposing a separate cosmological structure on the six-channel sequence.

**傷寒 vs. 溫病 vs. 中寒 (cold damage, warm disease, true cold-attack).** A fundamental nosological controversy through the high and late Qīng. The boundary between *Shānghán* (externally-contracted febrile disease) and the late-Míng / Qīng 溫病 *wēn-bìng* (warm-disease) and 溫疫 *wēn-yì* (epidemic warm-disease) traditions is contested across the division. Wāng Hù argued for a doctrinally distinct 中寒 *zhōng-hán* ("true cold-attack") category [[KR3ef046]]; Yáng Jùn integrated 溫疫 with *Shānghán* [[KR3ef050]]; Wú Kūn'ān integrated 三焦 (warm-disease) with 六經 frameworks [[KR3ef032]] [[KR3ef055]]; Lù Mào-xiū unified yáng-míng with qì-fēn [[KR3ef070]]; the Zhèjiāng *tōng-sú* line (Yú Gēnchū / Hé Liánchén [[KR3ef031]] [[KR3ef036]]) produced the most thorough Republican-era synthesis.

**The authenticity of the Hàn attribution.** Three twentieth-century "old-text" recensions (Guìlín [[KR3ef002]], Kāngpíng [[KR3ef003]], Cháng-shā [non-catalogued]) all claim to preserve pre-Sòng — even pre-Wáng Shū-hé — readings of the original 16-juan work. Mainstream textual criticism treats Guìlín as a Republican reconstruction and Kāngpíng as a Heian-period Japanese descendant of the Sòng line; Kawagoe Masayoshi's *Shānghán mài zhèng shì* [[KR3ef073]] (*c.* 1790–1830) is the most remarkable pre-modern skeptical argument, proposing on internal grounds that the *Shānghán lùn* is structured on *Yì-jīng* hexagrams and not authored by Zhāng Zhòngjǐng — anticipating modern critical philology by two centuries.

**The Edo philological project.** The Tamba *Igaku-kan* under Mototane and Motokata ([[KR3ef018]] [[KR3ef051]] [[KR3ef053]] [[KR3ef082]] [[KR3ef085]]) produced the definitive modern East-Asian *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì textus receptus*. Their *Shānghán guǎng yào* [[KR3ef051]] surveying 150 commentators remains the densest single bibliography of the entire pre-modern commentarial corpus, and their *Jīnguì yùhán yào lüè shù yì* [[KR3ef085]] transmits readings from the Korean *Yī fāng lèi jù* otherwise lost to the Chinese tradition. The parallel *kohō* tradition (Yamada [[KR3ef025]], Nakanishi [[KR3ef029]], Tachibana [[KR3ef052]], Kawagoe [[KR3ef069]] [[KR3ef073]], Gotō [[KR3ef074]]) approaches the same canon polemically rather than philologically.

**經方 (jīng-fāng) — the prescription-tradition movement.** Centered on Xú Dàchūn's prescription-type reorganization [[KR3ef064]], paralleled by Yoshimasu Tōdō's Edo *Ruijuhō* (referenced in [[KR3ef025]] [[KR3ef069]]), revived by Yumoto Kyūshin in the 1920s [[KR3ef059]], and operationalized for the Shanghai TCM curriculum by Cáo Yǐngfǔ [[KR3ef093]]: the persistent claim that the *Shānghán* prescriptions themselves — not their doctrinal apparatus — constitute the true canonical content of the tradition. This is the principal currently-living *Shānghán* commentarial movement.

**中西匯通 (Sino-Western synthesis).** Táng Zōnghǎi's expansion of Chén Niànzǔ's commentaries with Western anatomical reference [[KR3ef028]] [[KR3ef091]], paralleled by his *Nèijīng* programme at [[KR3ea044]] and continued by Yumoto Kyūshin (Western pharmacology, [[KR3ef059]]) and Niè Yúntái (bacteriology and vitamin biochemistry, [[KR3ef068]]). The acumoxa-side analogue is [[KR3ee034]].
