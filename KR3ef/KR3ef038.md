# Shānghán liù shū 傷寒六書
*Six Books on Cold Damage*
by [[陶華]] (Táo Huá, *zì* Shàng-wén 尚文, *hào* Jié-ān 節庵, *fl.* early-mid Míng, 明)

## About the work

A six-juan early-Míng (1445) compendium on *Shānghán* clinical practice by the Hángzhōu physician Táo Huá 陶華 (字 Shàng-wén 尚文, 號 Jié-ān 節庵). The work — comprising six independent treatises bound as a set — is one of the foundational Míng-period *Shānghán* works and was central to the Míng medical curriculum prior to the *Tiáo biàn* / *Shàng lùn piān* revisionist movement of the late 16th and early 17th centuries.

### Abstract

The six constituent treatises are: *Shānghán suǒ yǐn* 傷寒鎖隱, *Shānghán jiā mì* 傷寒家秘, *Shānghán liù shū jué* 傷寒六書訣, *Yī yào liào liè* 殺車槌法, *Yī yào yāo zhǐ* 一提金, and *Tì Tóng jué* 截江網. Composition date 1445 is given by Táo's preface. Táo's work synthesizes Chéng Wúyǐ's *Shānghán* doctrine with Jīn-Yuán adjustments from Liú Wánsù's school, producing a unified clinical handbook that was widely reprinted through the Míng and into the early Qīng.
