# Shānghán qián fǎ 傷寒鈐法
*Calendar-Based Methods of Cold Damage*
by [[馬宗素]] (Mǎ Zōngsù, *fl.* early-mid Yuán, 元)

## About the work

A short Yuán-period treatise in one juan presenting a calendrical-prognostic system for cold-damage syndromes, by Mǎ Zōngsù 馬宗素 — a member of the Hé-jiān-school tradition in the lineage of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù. The "*qián fǎ*" 鈐法 (literally "seal-method") is a system whereby the timing of a *Shānghán* episode (calculated against the patient's natal calendrical configuration and the seasonal influences active at illness onset) determines the appropriate prescription. The work is a distinctive Yuán-period attempt to integrate cosmological-correlative reasoning with *Shānghán* canonical doctrine.

### Abstract

The composition window 1297–1320 brackets the early-mid Yuán activity of the Héjiān school. The *Qián fǎ* method circulated in YuánMíng medical practice but was largely abandoned in mainstream commentarial scholarship from the Wànlì period onward, when the *Chángshā fùgǔ* (ancient-text-restoration) movement turned away from cosmological-correlative reasoning in *Shānghán* practice. The work is preserved primarily through reprintings in Yuán and early-Míng collections.
