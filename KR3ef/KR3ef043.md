# Shānghán bǔ lì 傷寒補例
*Supplementary Cases on Cold Damage*
by [[周學海]] (Zhōu Xuéhǎi, *zì* Chèngzhī 澂之, *hào* Jiànchí 健之, 1856–1906, 清)

## About the work

A two-juan late-Qīng *Shānghán* supplement by the Ān-huī physician Zhōu Xuéhǎi 周學海 (1856–1906), a *jìnshì* of 1885 who turned to medicine after his official career. The work presents 88 supplementary clinical cases organized around problematic *Shānghán* syndromes, with detailed Zhōu commentary. Zhōu is also known for his comprehensive *Mài jiǎn* 脈簡 (pulse handbook) and for editing the *Zhōu shì yī xué cóng shū* 周氏醫學叢書 (a major late-Qīng medical reprint program).

### Abstract

Composition window 1882–1906 reflects Zhōu's medical activity from his early thirties to his death. The *Bǔ lì* draws on Zhōu's own clinical practice supplemented with cases from earlier authorities; its distinctive feature is the systematic attempt to match canonical *Shānghán* prescriptions to clinical scenarios that the *Shānghán lùn* itself treats only briefly. The work is one of the principal late-Qīng *Shānghán* supplements and was part of the broader Qīng synthesis movement.
