# Shàng lùn hòu piān 尚論後篇
*Later Esteemed-Discourse Chapters*
by [[喻昌]] (Yú Chāng, *zì* Jiāyán 嘉言, 1585–1664, 清)

## About the work

A four-juan continuation of the *Shàng lùn piān* ([[KR3ef026]]), extending Yú Chāng's reconstruction-commentary to the *Shānghán*-ancillary clinical problems not covered in the principal *Shàng lùn piān*: *wēn-yì* 溫疫 (warm epidemic), *fēng-yì* 風疫 (wind epidemic), *yùn qì* 運氣 (climatic-influence) considerations, and post-*Shānghán* convalescence problems. The work is structurally a sequel rather than a separate independent commentary.

### Abstract

The work was composed in the years following 1648 and refined through Yú's last years before his death in 1664. It is best read together with the *Shàng lùn piān* as a unified commentarial program. In some Qīng prints the two are bound together as a single twelve-juan set; in others they circulate separately, as here.
