# Shānghán lùn huì-zhù jīng-huá 傷寒論匯注精華
*Essential Refinements from the Gathered Annotations on the Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[惲鐵樵]] (Yùn Tiěqiáo, *zì* Shùjué 樹珏, 1878–1935, 民國) — compiler

## About the work

An eight-juan early-Republican-period anthology of *Shānghán* commentarial materials compiled by the Shàng-hǎi Republican-era educator and physician Yùn Tiěqiáo 惲鐵樵 (1878–1935), published 1923 by the Shanghai *Sì xué shè* 四學社. The work presents the canonical *Shānghán lùn* with selected and edited annotations drawn from a wide range of Sòng-Qīng commentaries (Chéng Wúyǐ, Zhū Hóng, Fāng Yǒuzhí, Yú Chāng, Kē Qín, Yóu Yí, Wú Qiān's *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn*, Tamba no Mototane, etc.), with Yùn's own editorial notes situating each tradition within the modern Chinese medical curriculum.

### Abstract

The catalog meta provides no author; modern Republican-period medical bibliography (Mǎ Jìxīng) attributes the work to Yùn Tiěqiáo, whose Shanghai medical publishing program in the 1920s produced a series of similar synthetic *Shānghán* and *Jīnguì* anthologies aimed at modernizing the Chinese-medicine curriculum in response to Western medical institutional pressure. The composition date 1923 is given by the printer's record. The work is a representative Republican-period synthesis: pedagogically organized, eclectic in commentary sourcing, with no strong polemical alignment, aiming for institutional standardization rather than scholarly originality.
