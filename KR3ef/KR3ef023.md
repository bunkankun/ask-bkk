# Shānghán jīng jiě 傷寒經解
*Canonical Explanation of Cold Damage*
by [[姚球]] (Yáo Qiú, *zì* Yùlán 玉嵐, *fl.* early Yōngzhèng, 清)

## About the work

A ten-juan early-Yōng-zhèng commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* by the Sūzhōu / Cháng-shú-region physician Yáo Qiú 姚球, completed 1727. The work — sometimes attributed in late-Qīng prints to "Wúmén" 吳門 (Sūzhōu) anonymous authors — presents the *Shānghán* materials with running annotation drawing on the *Chángshā fùgǔ* (ancient-text-restoration) line, supplemented with Yáo's own clinical adjustments.

### Abstract

The catalog meta provides no author; modern textual scholarship (Mǎ Jìxīng 1991) attributes the work to Yáo Qiú 姚球 on the basis of the preface in late-Qīng witnesses. Yáo is a minor early-Qīng physician known almost exclusively through this single work; his exact lifedates are not preserved. The composition date 1727 is given by the preface (Yōngzhèng 5). The work circulated in modest editions through the late Qīng and is preserved in the *Hxwd* corpus as a representative second-tier Qīng *Shānghán* commentary.
