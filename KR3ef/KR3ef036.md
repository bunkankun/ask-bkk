# Chóngdìng tōngsú Shānghán lùn 重訂通俗傷寒論
*Re-edited Vernacular Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[俞根初]] (Yú Gēnchū, *zì* Tóngtián 桐田, 1734–1799, 清) — original; [[何廉臣]] (Hé Liánchén, 1861–1929, 民國) — re-editor

## About the work

The definitive Republican-era re-edition of [[俞根初]] Yú Gēnchū's late-18th-century Shàoxīng vernacular *Shānghán* treatise, the *Tōngsú Shānghán lùn* 通俗傷寒論. The work is one of the most important integrations of the *Shānghán* and *wēnbìng* traditions in late-Qīng Zhèjiāng medicine, with substantial editorial expansion by Hé Liánchén. The 1934 edition was published posthumously by the *Shàoxīng yīyào xué bào* 紹興醫藥學報 press, edited by [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén.

### Abstract

Yú Gēnchū's original *Tōngsú Shānghán lùn* was composed in QiánlóngJiāqìng times (late 18th century), at Shàoxīng. It circulated in manuscript and in modest local prints for over a century. Hé Liánchén's *Zēngdìng* (Expanded) edition of 1916 ([[KR3ef031]]) was the first thoroughly re-edited modern version; the *Chóngdìng* (Re-edited) version published posthumously in 1934 by Hé's son [[何聲山]] Hé Shèngshān represents Hé's final corrections and additions. The work integrates Shàoxīng-school clinical doctrine with the broader Qīng *Shānghán* and *wēnbìng* commentarial traditions; it is a foundational document of modern Zhèjiāng-school medicine.
