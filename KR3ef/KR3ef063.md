# Shānghán fā wēi lùn 傷寒發微論
*Treatise Bringing to Light the Subtleties of Cold Damage*
by [[許叔微]] (Xǔ Shūwēi, *zì* Zhīkě 知可, ca. 1080–ca. 1154, 南宋)

## About the work

A two-juan Southern-Sòng *Shānghán* essay-collection by **Xǔ Shūwēi** 許叔微 (Xǔ Xué-shì 許學士), companion to his *Shānghán bǎi zhèng gē* ([[KR3ef061]]). The work is also bibliographically attested under the alternative title *Yì Shānghán lùn* 翼傷寒論 (Wing to the *Shānghán lùn*). It consists of clinical essays on a range of *Shānghán* doctrinal and therapeutic points — pulse-and-syndrome differentiation, pathogen-progression, and the limits of canonical prescription application — taking up topics that the strict *zhèng-fāng* (syndrome-prescription) format of the canonical text leaves under-developed.

### Abstract

Composition window 1133–1154 is bracketed by Xǔ's *Lèi-zhèng pǔ-jì běn-shì fāng* ([[KR3e0032]], 1133) and his death. The work circulates with the *Bǎi zhèng gē* as a single set in the Yuán-edition reprintings under the binding title 《新編張仲景注解傷寒百證歌五卷·發微論二卷》 (preserved in the introductory matter of the received text). The bibliographic survey embedded in this introductory matter — itself a major early-modern *Shānghán* bibliographic source — names the principal Sòng-Yuán *Shānghán* commentary tradition (Wáng Shūhé 王叔和, Chéng Wúyǐ 成無己, Lǐ Chēng 李檉, …) and identifies the *Fā wēi lùn* as the *Yì Shānghán lùn* of 《書錄解題》.

The Yuán-edition was rare in the Qīng — Qián Zūn-wáng 錢遵王's *Dú-shū mǐn-qiú jì* is one of the few catalog entries — and the modern reprintings derive from the Yuán block in 錢's collection (later in the hands of the Hǎi-yú 海虞 collector who wrote the bibliographic introduction).

The work, while bibliographically less famous than the *Bǎi zhèng gē*, is the more discursive of the two and is the principal vehicle for Xǔ Shūwēi's own doctrinal positions on the *Shānghán* tradition.

## Other points of interest

The 1820s-era bibliographic preface (preserved in Kanripo source under the title "重雕元刻傷寒百證歌發微論敘") is one of the most-cited early-modern records of Xǔ Shūwēi's lost works (the *Nǐ Shānghán gē*, *Zhì-fǎ* 81 piān, *Sānshí-liù tú*, *Yì Shānghán lùn*, *Biàn lèi*) and the textual transmission of the surviving pair.
