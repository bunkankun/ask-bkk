# Shānghán míng-lǐ lùn 傷寒明理論
*Treatise on Elucidating the Principles of Cold Damage*
by [[成無己]] (Chéng Wúyǐ, b. ca. 1063, *fl.* into 1156, 金)

## About the work

A four-juan companion treatise to Chéng Wúyǐ's annotated *Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef005]]), presenting in original-essay form the doctrinal apparatus implicit in his commentary. The work — three juan plus an appended *Lùn fāng* 論方 fourth juan — is the first sustained Chinese systematic exposition of *Shānghán* clinical doctrine in essay (rather than commentary) form, and was central to the Jīn-Yuán *Shānghán* curriculum.

### Abstract

The composition date 1156 is given by Yán Qìzhī's 嚴器之 preface (Shào-xīng 26), the principal early biographical witness to Chéng. The three principal juan are organized as fifty entries each addressing one *Shānghán*-relevant clinical-doctrinal topic (e.g. *fā-rè* 發熱 fever, *zhān-yǔ* 譫語 delirious speech, *biàn* 便 elimination). The appended *Lùn fāng* fourth juan presents 20 of the principal *Shānghán* canonical formulas with detailed pharmacological-doctrinal analysis. Together with the *Zhù jiě Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef005]]), the *Míng-lǐ lùn* constitutes Chéng's unified commentary-and-treatise program on the *Shānghán* canon. Chéng's catalog meta dynasty is given as 宋 here (correctly indicating the work was issued under Jīn-controlled territory in the Sòng calendar tradition); see [[成無己]] for the contextual note.
