# Shānghán zuǎn lùn / Shānghán xù lùn 傷寒纘論_傷寒緒論
*Compiled Discourse / Threads of Discourse on Cold Damage*
by [[張璐]] (Zhāng Lù, *zì* Lùyù 路玉, *hào* Shíwán Lǎorén 石頑老人, 1617–1700, 清)

## About the work

The combined four-juan recension of [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù's *Shānghán* commentary, presenting both the *Zuǎn lùn* 纘論 (compiled annotations on the canonical text, 2 juan; see [[KR3ef020]]) and the *Xù lùn* 緒論 (threads of further discourse on ancillary clinical problems, 2 juan) as a unified set. The combined edition is the standard form in Qīng prints.

### Abstract

Same composition date (1667). The *Xù lùn* component takes up subjects that the canonical *Shānghán* lùn treats only briefly or implicitly: *wēnbìng* 溫病 (warm-disease) interactions with *Shānghán*, the management of pediatric *Shānghán*, *Shānghán* in pregnancy, post-*Shānghán* convalescence problems, and the diagnostic-pulse adjustments specific to *Shānghán* clinical assessment. Together with the *Zuǎn lùn*, the set constitutes Zhāng's full *Shānghán* commentary.
