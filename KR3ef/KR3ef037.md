# Liú Héjiān Shānghán yī jiàn 劉河間傷寒醫鑑
*Liú Hé-jiān's Mirror of Cold-Damage Medicine*
by [[馬宗素]] (Mǎ Zōngsù, *fl.* early-mid Yuán, 元)

## About the work

A short Yuán-period treatise in one juan continuing the Hé-jiān-school *Shānghán* doctrine of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù, by Mǎ Zōngsù 馬宗素. The work — preserved in the *Héjiān liù shū* 河間六書 corpus — presents Liú's *fire-and-heat* doctrine in a more concise pedagogical form, with selected canonical *Shānghán* passages glossed through the Héjiān doctrinal lens.

### Abstract

The composition window of 1297–1320 brackets the early-mid Yuán activity of the Héjiān school. Mǎ Zōngsù is also the author of the *Shānghán qián fǎ* 傷寒鈐法 ([[KR3ef048]]), a parallel calendar-based prognostic Shānghán treatise. He is known almost exclusively as a Hé-jiān-school transmitter; his exact biographical details are not preserved.
