# Shānghán lái sū jí 傷寒來蘇集
*Collection of Reviving from Cold Damage*
by [[柯琴]] (Kē Qín, *zì* Yùnbó 韻伯, *fl.* late 17th c., 清)

## About the work

The comprehensive *Shānghán* compilation of the great early-Kāng-xī commentator Kē Qín 柯琴, in eight juan, traditionally bound together with the *Shānghán lùn yì* 傷寒論翼 ([[KR3ef007]]) and the *Shānghán fù yì* 傷寒附翼 ([[KR3ef033]]) as a four-part set under the *Lái sū jí* umbrella title. This is the foundational text of Kē Qín's "channel-pattern" (以證類證) school of *Shānghán* exegesis: rather than following Wáng Shūhé's manuscript sequence, Kē reorganizes the *Shānghán* materials by clinical syndrome (證), then prescription. This organizational principle has dominated Chinese-medicine pedagogy from the late Qīng through the present.

### Abstract

The composition window is the late Kāngxī period 1669–1674. Kē Qín — a native of Cíxī 慈溪 in Zhèjiāng who relocated to Yúshān 虞山 (Chángshú, Jiāngsū) — argued that Wáng Shūhé's editorial sequence misrepresented Zhāng Jī's clinical intent, and that the *Shānghán* materials should be re-grouped by symptomatic pattern. The result was a "*Shānghánlùn zhù*" (commentarial Cold-Damage Treatise) in 4 juan and an "*Yì*" (Wings) in 2 juan, together comprising the principal *Láisū jí*. The *Fùyì* (附翼, [[KR3ef033]]) — supplementary essays on key clinical-doctrinal problems — was added by Kē in his last years, and the *Shānghán fǎ zǔ* 傷寒法祖 ([[KR3ef042]]) is a posthumous arrangement by his disciple Rén Yuèān 任越庵 of unfinished material.

The *Láisū jí* is one of the four most-cited Qīng *Shānghán* commentaries, alongside [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng's *Shànglùn piān*, [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí's *Shānghán guànzhū jí*, and [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn's *Shānghán lùn lèifāng*. Modern critical editions are based on the *Si bù bèi yào* 四部備要 (Shanghai, 1936) line.
