## About the work

A bibliographically obscure short *Shānghán* monograph attributed in the Kanripo catalog to **Liú Dòng Tiánliáng** 劉棟田良. The catalog form of the name strongly suggests a sinicized Edo *kanpō* physician's pseudonym rather than a Chinese author; the dynasty assignment in the catalog (清) probably reflects approximate chronological contemporaneity with mid-Qīng China rather than authentic Chinese provenance. The title "*Liúshì zhuàn*" 劉氏傳 ("Transmission of the Liú Family") could indicate that the work represents Liú-school *Shānghán* clinical tradition, or that "Liú" is a sinicized surname.
