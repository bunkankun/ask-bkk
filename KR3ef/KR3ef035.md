# Shānghán jié jué 傷寒捷訣
*Quick Mnemonic of Cold Damage*
by [[嚴則庵]] (Yán Zé'ān, 清) — compiler; [[裘慶元]] (Qiú Qìngyuán, 1873–1947, 民國) — modern editor

## About the work

A one-juan mnemonic-handbook of *Shānghán* clinical practice, presented in verse for memorization. Compiled originally by the late-Qīng physician Yán Zé'ān 嚴則庵, the text was later re-edited by [[裘慶元]] Qiú Qìngyuán (1873–1947) and included in his Republican-era medical anthology *Sān-sān yī shū* 三三醫書. The work is representative of the rich Qīng / Republican vernacular tradition of versified medical mnemonics.

### Abstract

Yán Zé'ān is a minor late-Qīng physician about whom little is preserved beyond authorship of this single work and a *Yī xué qiǎn shuō* 醫學淺說. Qiú Qìngyuán's *Sān-sān yī shū* — a 99-title anthology published in three series (1924, 1925, 1936) — is one of the principal Republican-era preservation vehicles for short and minor Qīng medical texts; the *Jié jué* is preserved primarily through it. The composition window (Qīng / Republican) reflects this two-stage production.
