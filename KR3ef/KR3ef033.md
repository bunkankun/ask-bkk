# Shānghán fù yì 傷寒附翼
*Supplementary Wings to [the Treatise on] Cold Damage*
by [[柯琴]] (Kē Qín, *zì* Yùnbó 韻伯, *fl.* late 17th c., 清)

## About the work

The supplementary "Wings" (附翼) volume to Kē Qín's *Lái sū jí* set, comprising essays and pharmacological notes that complement the main commentary and the *Shānghán lùn yì* ([[KR3ef007]]). The work is in two juan; it covers ancillary topics — pharmacology of canonical *Shānghán* formulas, dosage relations, prescription combinations — that the main *Lùn zhù* and *Yì* did not address systematically.

### Abstract

Composition window late-Kāng-xī (1669–1684) follows Kē's continued editorial activity in his later years. In modern editions the *Fù yì* is bound with the *Lùn zhù* ([[KR3ef014]]), the *Yì* ([[KR3ef007]]), and posthumously with the *Fǎ zǔ* ([[KR3ef042]]) to form the complete Kē Qín *Shānghán* set under the *Lái sū jí* umbrella.
