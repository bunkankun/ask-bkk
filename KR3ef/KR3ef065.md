# Shānghán shěn zhèng biǎo 傷寒審證表
*Tabulated Examination of Cold-Damage Syndromes*
by [[包誠]] (Bāo Chéng, *zì* Xīngyán 興言, *fl.* mid-late 19th c., 清)

## About the work

A one-juan late-Qīng tabulated handbook of *Shānghán* syndrome-discrimination by the Jīngyì 涇邑 (Jīngxiàn 涇縣, Ānhuī) physician **Bāo Chéng** 包誠 (字 Xīngyán 興言). The work — its principal innovation is the tabulated format (*biǎo* 表) — re-presents [[黃元御]] (Huáng Yuányù)'s *Shānghán xuán jiě* 傷寒懸解 in chart form, with the canonical syndrome-clauses, pulse signs, and prescriptions arranged in a unified grid that "*證狀畢呈如掌如貌*" (presents the entire array of syndrome-features at a glance, as on the palm of the hand).

### Abstract

Composition date 1870, given by the preface — "同治九年歲在庚午冬十有二月朔" (Tóngzhì 9 / 1870, twelfth-month new-moon) by Lǐ Hànzhāng 李瀚章 of Héféi (合肥), then governor-general of Húguǎng (督楚使者). Lǐ — the elder brother of Lǐ Hóngzhāng 李鴻章 — records that Bāo Chéng called upon him while serving in a clerical post 鄂中 (in Húběi), expounded Huáng Yuányù's doctrine at length, and presented this *Shěn zhèng biǎo* "均依黃氏編第者一冊" (entirely organized according to Huáng's editorial schema) and asked Lǐ to fund its block-printing. Lǐ — keen to provide medical relief to the war-stricken populace of post-Tàipíng Húběi — wrote the preface and underwrote the printing.

A second preface (序傷寒論一書晉時已無善本…) gives the textual-historical rationale: the *Shānghán lùn* had been rearranged by Wáng Shūhé 王叔和 in the Jìn period, redacted by Lín Yì 林億 in the Sòng *Jiàoxhèng Yī shū jú* 校正醫書局 collation, and obscured by the accumulation of conflicting commentaries; Huáng Yuányù's *Xuán jiě* restored the canonical sequence; Bāo's *Shěn zhèng biǎo* makes Huáng's restoration accessible to the working clinician.

The work is one of the principal vehicles of the **Huáng Yuányù 黃元御 school** in the late Qīng — its only well-known transmission outside Sìchuān and Shāndōng — and an important document of late-Qīng provincial medical patronage.

## Other points of interest

The preface by Lǐ Hànzhāng 李瀚章 (1821–1899) is an unusually distinguished example of late-Qīng official patronage of medical publishing: Lǐ was at this time governor-general of HúbeiHúnán (Húguǎng zǒngdū 湖廣總督) and the elder brother of the more famous Lǐ Hóngzhāng. The preface records Lǐ's explicit linkage of medical relief to the post-Tàipíng reconstruction project ("致治之術與良醫治疾將無同").
