# Xǔ zhēn jūn yù xiá jì 許真君玉匣記

*Perfected Xǔ's Jade-Coffer Record*

attributed to [[許遜]] (Xǔ Xùn)

A Míng **divination almanac** attributed to Xǔ Xùn — among the most widely-circulated popular Daoist divination handbooks of the late imperial era.

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
