# Tài shàng zhōng dào miào fǎ lián huā jīng 太上中道妙法蓮花經

*Most High Middle-Way Lotus of the Wondrous Dharma Scripture*

Anonymous; Míng

**Opening text of the Xù Dào zàng** 續道藏 (Wàn lì Supplementary Canon, 1607). A Daoist scripture consciously modelled on the Buddhist *Saddharma Puṇḍarīka* (*Lotus Sūtra*, *Miào fǎ lián huā jīng* 妙法蓮華經). Preserved as **DZ 1432** / CT 1432 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
