# Yì lín shàng xià jīng 易林上下經

*Forest of the Yì, Upper and Lower Classics (= Jiāo-shì Yì lín)*

by [[焦贛]] (Jiāo Gòng / Jiāo Yánshòu)

**The *Jiāo-shì Yì lín*** 焦氏易林 — **the great Western-Hàn *Yì jīng* divination compendium**, providing 4,096 four-line divinatory verses, one for each ordered pair of the 64 hexagrams. One of the most important early Hàn *Yì* tradition works. Catalogued here from other sources (not preserved in the Dào zàng proper).

**Dating.** Western Hàn. Dynasty: 西漢.
