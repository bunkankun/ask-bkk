# Tiān huáng zhì dào tài qīng yù cè 天皇至道太清玉冊

*Heaven-Emperor Supreme-Way Tài-qīng Jade-Register*

by [[朱權]] (Zhū Quán, 1378–1448) — Míng Prince Níng

**Prince Níng Zhū Quán's comprehensive Daoist compendium** in 8 *juàn* — a major mid-Míng summa of Daoist doctrine, ritual, history, and practice. Important both as a Daoist text and as a work of Míng princely patronage. Preserved as **DZ 1483** / CT 1483 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Early to mid-Míng. Dynasty: 明.
