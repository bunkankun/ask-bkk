# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn kāi tiān jīng 太上老君開天經

*Most High Lord Lǎo's Heaven-Opening Scripture*

attributed to [[張泮]] (Zhāng Pàn)

A Six-Dynasties **Daoist cosmogonic scripture** narrating Lǎo jūn's opening of the heavens and origins of the cosmos. Preserved as **DZ 1437** / CT 1437 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
