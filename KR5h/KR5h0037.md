# Xú xiān hàn zǎo 徐仙翰藻

*Literary Treasures of the Immortal Xú*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng collection devoted to **Xú Shénwēng** 徐神翁 (1033–1108) — the Sòng thaumaturge. Preserved as **DZ 1468** / CT 1468 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
