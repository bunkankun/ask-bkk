# Huáng Míng ēn mìng shì lù 皇明恩命世錄

*Record of the Imperial Míng Grace-Commands across the Generations*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng **record of imperial commands and favours** bestowed on the Zhèng yī Celestial-Masters lineage at Lóng hǔ shān. Preserved as **DZ 1462** / CT 1462 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
