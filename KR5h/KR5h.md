## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR5h collects the **Xù Dàozàng** 續道藏, the supplement to the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (1445) compiled by the 50th Celestial Master **Zhāng Guóxiáng** 張國祥 (*d.* 1611) and printed in 1607 under imperial sponsorship by the Wànlì 萬曆 emperor. The supplement adds 32 *hán* 函 (case-units) of further material, numbered **DZ 1432–1487** (CT 1432–1487) in Schipper's *Concordance du Tao-tsang*, and is preserved in modern reprints as the 三家本 (Sānjiā běn, 1988) and the *Zhōnghuá Dàozàng* 中華道藏 (ZHDZ, 2004) editions. All 57 works in this division carry both `SJB` and `ZHDZ` in their `editions` frontmatter — the supplement was never separately printed in WYG 文淵閣四庫全書 form, since the Sìkù compilers regarded the Daoist Canon as outside the orthodox 子部 categorization.

Although KR5h sits inside Kanripo's **KR5 Masters** tree, the division is materially a Daoist-canon block rather than a *zhū-zǐ* 諸子 block. The works range from genuine early-medieval Shàng-qīng 上清 and Líng-bǎo 靈寶 scriptures preserved long after their composition, through Sòng–Yuán liturgies of the Shénxiāo 神霄 / Qīngwēi 清微 thunder-rite (*léifǎ* 雷法) traditions, to a large body of Míng popular-cult scriptures (Wáng Líng-guān 王靈官, Bì-xiá yuán-jūn 碧霞元君, Lǚ-zǔ 呂祖, Zhēn-wǔ 真武, Huá-guāng 華光, the city-god *chéng-huáng* 城隍), and finally to a set of late-Míng anthologies and *Classics* commentaries (Jiāo Hóng's 焦竤 *Lǎo-zǐ yì* and *Zhuāng-zǐ yì*) that mark the formal close of the canon. The supplement is therefore not a doctrinal corpus but an editorial container — its principle of selection was what the Wànlì-era court and the 龍虎山 Lóng-hǔ shān Celestial-Masters compilers judged worth preserving outside the 1445 canon, including older material that had been overlooked, newer scriptures generated by the popular pantheon, and the institutional self-documentation of the Zhāng lineage itself ([[KR5h0031]], [[KR5h0032]]).

## Important texts and text clusters

- **The Daoist *Lotus Sūtra* and Peacock-King cluster** — *Tàishàng zhōngdào miàofǎ liánhuā jīng* 太上中道妙法蓮花經 [[KR5h0001]] (the opening text of the supplement, a conscious Daoist re-modelling of the Buddhist *Saddharma-puṇḍarīka*), and the three-text Peacock-King block [[KR5h0002]], [[KR5h0003]], [[KR5h0004]] (a Daoist appropriation of Buddhist *Mahāmāyūrī* 孔雀明王). These texts set the supplement's signature register of Buddhist-Daoist syncretism.
- **Early-medieval Shàng-qīng and Líng-bǎo scriptures preserved through late inclusion** — *Shàng-qīng yuán-shǐ biàn-huà bǎo-zhēn shàng-jīng* 上清元始變化寶真上經 [[KR5h0005]] (六朝), *Tàishàng Lǎo-jūn kāitiān jīng* 太上老君開天經 [[KR5h0006]] (六朝 cosmogony attributed to 張泮), *Gāo-shàng yù-chén yōu-lè zhāng* 高上玉宸憂樂章 [[KR5h0026]] (東晉), and the Táng Shàng-qīng block [[KR5h0007]], [[KR5h0024]], [[KR5h0025]], [[KR5h0027]], [[KR5h0028]], [[KR5h0029]] — pre-Sòng material the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* had not absorbed.
- **Wàn-lì-era popular-cult scriptures** — the Wáng-líng-guān *Tàishàng Yuán-yáng shàng-dì… Huǒ-chē Wáng-líng-guān zhēn-jīng* 王靈官真經 [[KR5h0012]], the Medicine-King *Yào-wáng* [[KR5h0013]], the Tài-shān fertility goddess *Bì-xiá yuán-jūn* [[KR5h0014]], the Guān-Yǔ-associated *Lǎng-líng shàng-jiāng* [[KR5h0015]], the city-god scripture [[KR5h0016]], and the *Huá-guāng dà-dì* / Mǎ-zǔ scripture [[KR5h0017]]. Together this cluster documents the late-imperial pantheon as the canon-compilers found it.
- **Liturgical *kē* and repentance texts** — *Zhōng-tiān Zǐ-wēi xīng-zhēn bǎo-chàn* 中天紫微星真寶懺 [[KR5h0019]], *Zǐ-huáng liàn-dù xuán-kē* 紫皇鍊度玄科 [[KR5h0020]], the Dǒu-mǔ / Marīcī liturgy *Xiāntiān Dǒumǔ zòugào xuánkē* 先天斗母奏告玄科 [[KR5h0021]] (the most fully analysed liturgical *kē* in the supplement, diagnostic of the Shénxiāo / Qīngwēi thunder-rite synthesis), *Cháo-zhēn fā-yuàn chàn-huǐ wén* [[KR5h0022]], and the Líng-bǎo *shī-shí* hungry-ghost-feeding ritual [[KR5h0023]].
- **The Celestial-Masters institutional dossier** — *Huáng-Míng ēn-mìng shì-lù* 皇明恩命世錄 [[KR5h0031]] (a generational record of imperial favours to Lóng-hǔ shān) and *Hàn tiān-shī shì-jiā* 漢天師世家 [[KR5h0032]] (Zhāng Guóxiáng's own genealogy of the Celestial-Masters lineage from Zhāng Dào-líng 張道陵 onward), both fundamental to the institutional history of Zhèng-yī 正一 Daoism.
- **Hagiographies and *xiān-zhuàn* compendia** — *Xú-xiān hàn-zǎo* 徐仙翰藻 [[KR5h0037]] and *Xú-xiān zhēn-lù* 徐仙真錄 [[KR5h0039]] (both centred on the Sòng thaumaturge Xú Shén-wēng 徐神翁), *Hóng-dào lù* 弘道錄 [[KR5h0033]] (Shào Jīng-bāng's 25-*juàn* Daoist *Lives*), the syncretic *Xiāo-yáo xū jīng* 消搖墟經 [[KR5h0034]], and *Lǚ-zǔ zhì* 呂祖志 [[KR5h0054]] (the Lǚ-Dòng-bīn cult compendium).
- **Princely synthesis and sacred-geography** — *Tiān-huáng zhì-dào Tài-qīng yù-cè* 天皇至道太清玉冊 [[KR5h0053]] by the Míng Prince Níng **Zhū Quán** 朱權 (1378–1448), a comprehensive 8-*juàn* mid-Míng *summa* of Daoist doctrine, ritual, history and practice, and *Dài shǐ* 岱史 [[KR5h0041]], the 1586 gazetteer of Tài-shān 泰山 (the chief of the Five Sacred Peaks and centre of the Dōng-yuè 東嶽 cult).
- **Divination and almanac handbooks** — *Xǔ zhēn-jūn yù-xiá jì* 許真君玉匣記 [[KR5h0050]] (attributed to Xǔ Xùn 許遜, with its own table of contents [[KR5h0049]]), *Fǎ-shī xuǎn-zé jì* 法師選擇記 [[KR5h0051]], *Xuán-tiān shàng-dì bǎi-zì shèng-hào* 玄天上帝百字聖號 [[KR5h0052]] (a Zhēn-wǔ divination text), and *Zǐ-wēi dǒu-shù* 紫微斗數 [[KR5h0055]], the foundational handbook of the still-practised Purple-Pole star-astrology system traditionally ascribed to Chén Tuán 陳摶.
- **The terminal *Classics*-commentary block** — *Yì-yīn shàng-xià jīng* 易因上下經 [[KR5h0042]] by the iconoclast **Lǐ Zhì** 李贄 (1527–1602), the anonymous *Gǔ-yì kǎo-yuán* 古易考原 [[KR5h0043]], the Western-Hàn *Jiāo-shì Yì-lín* 焦氏易林 [[KR5h0044]] (4 096 four-line divinatory verses for paired hexagrams), and **Jiāo Hóng's** 焦竤 (1540–1620) *Lǎo-zǐ yì* 老子翼 [[KR5h0056]] and *Zhuāng-zǐ yì* 莊子翼 [[KR5h0057]] — the closing pair of the entire Zhèng-tǒng + Xù canon, and the standard late-Míng collected commentaries on the two great *zǐ* texts.
- **Two narrative and philosophical inclusions** — the Eastern-Jìn *Sōu-shén jì* 搜神記 [[KR5h0045]] (the foundational *zhì-guài* 志怪 collection of **Gān Bǎo** 干寶) and Tán Qiào's 譚峭 Five-Dynasties *Huà-shū* 化書 [[KR5h0047]] (a second recension of the work better known from [[KR5d0067]]), both included for their canonical status in the Daoist literary tradition.

## Important persons

- [[張國祥]] (Zhāng Guóxiáng, *d.* 1611) — 50th Celestial Master, **compiler of the Xù Dàozàng itself** and author of *Hàn tiān-shī shì-jiā* [[KR5h0032]]; defines the editorial perspective of the entire division.
- [[焦竤]] (Jiāo Hóng, 1540–1620) — late-Míng polymath and Hànlín academician; his *Lǎo-zǐ yì* [[KR5h0056]] and *Zhuāng-zǐ yì* [[KR5h0057]] form the terminal text-block of the canon and remain standard reference editions.
- [[朱權]] (Zhū Quán, 1378–1448) — Míng Prince Níng, son of the Hóngwǔ emperor; his *Tài-qīng yù-cè* [[KR5h0053]] is the major mid-Míng princely synthesis of Daoist doctrine and a key witness to early-Míng court patronage of Daoism.
- [[李贄]] (Lǐ Zhì, 1527–1602) — late-Míng iconoclast; his *Yì-yīn* [[KR5h0042]] is the single most striking philosophical voice in the division.
- [[邵經邦]] (Shào Jīng-bāng, 1491–1565) — Mid-Míng compiler of *Hóng-dào lù* [[KR5h0033]], a 25-*juàn* prosopography linking Confucian-Daoist *dào-tǒng* 道統 transmission.
- [[周玄貞]] (Zhōu Xuán-zhēn) — compiler of *Huáng-jīng jí-zhù* [[KR5h0009]], the standard Míng collected-commentary on the Jade-Emperor scripture.
- [[洪應明]] (Hóng Yìng-míng) — late-Míng syncretic writer; editor of *Xiāo-yáo xū jīng* [[KR5h0034]], better known for *Cài-gēn tán* 菜根譚 outside this canon.
- [[查志隆]] (Zhā Zhì-lóng, *jìnshì* 1558) — author of the 1586 *Dài shǐ* [[KR5h0041]], the most thorough late-Míng gazetteer of Tài-shān.
- [[黃恒]] (Huáng Héng) — compiler of *Xú-xiān zhēn-lù* [[KR5h0039]], printed 1443.
- [[譚峭]] (Tán Qiào, *fl.* ca. 880–950) — Five-Dynasties Daoist philosopher; *Huà-shū* [[KR5h0047]] is reprinted here as a second recension.
- [[焦贛]] (Jiāo Gòng / Jiāo Yán-shòu, end of the 1st century BCE) — Western-Hàn *Yì*-divination master; the *Jiāo-shì Yì-lín* [[KR5h0044]] is the great early-Hàn divinatory companion to the *Yì jīng*.
- [[干寶]] (Gān Bǎo, *fl.* 317–350) — Eastern-Jìn historian and *zhì-guài* anthologist; the Dàozàng recension of *Sōu-shén jì* [[KR5h0045]] is a shorter version of his foundational supernatural-narrative collection.
- [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòng-bīn) and [[許遜]] (Xǔ Xùn) — though not authors in any historical sense, they are the **two most prominent attribution-figures** of the division: Lǚ-zǔ as the subject of [[KR5h0054]], Xǔ Xùn as the cult-figure standing behind the *Yù-xiá jì* divination tradition [[KR5h0049]], [[KR5h0050]].
- [[張泮]] (Zhāng Pàn) — Six-Dynasties figure to whom the cosmogonic *Kāi-tiān jīng* [[KR5h0006]] is attributed.

## Topics

- **Buddhist–Daoist syncretism as constitutive register.** The supplement opens with an explicit Daoist *Lotus Sūtra* ([[KR5h0001]]) and continues through a Peacock-King block ([[KR5h0002]]–[[KR5h0004]]) and the Dǒu-mǔ / Marīcī liturgy ([[KR5h0021]]). The borrowing is not incidental but the editorial posture of the whole division: by 1607 the canon-compilers treat Buddhist deities and ritual forms as Daoist material when transposed into the appropriate idiom.
- **The pantheon of the late-imperial popular cult.** A substantial bloc of the supplement is dedicated to deities whose cults had matured between the Sòng and the Wànlì period — Wáng Líng-guān (the Míng court-promoted exorcist-general), Bì-xiá yuán-jūn (the Tài-shān fertility goddess), Lǚ Dòng-bīn (the *quán-zhēn* 全真 patriarch turned popular saviour), Zhēn-wǔ 真武 / Xuán-tiān shàng-dì, Huá-guāng dà-dì, the city-god *chéng-huáng*, and the Dōng-yuè 東嶽 cult of Tài-shān. The division is therefore a primary source for the religious culture of late-Míng China at large, well beyond the institutional Daoist priesthood.
- **Thunder-rite (*léifǎ* 雷法) and the Shénxiāo / Qīngwēi liturgical synthesis.** Several of the *kē* texts ([[KR5h0019]], [[KR5h0020]], [[KR5h0021]]) sit firmly inside the post-Sòng *léifǎ* tradition descended from Wáng Wén-qīng 王文卿 and Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾, with the canonical Dèng–Xīn–Zhāng 鄧辛張 thunder-marshal triad and the lineage of Zhāng Xū-jìng 張虛靖. The supplement preserves the most analytically tractable late-imperial witnesses to this ritual lineage.
- **The institutional history of the Celestial Masters at Lóng-hǔ shān.** The pairing of [[KR5h0031]] (imperial-edict record) and [[KR5h0032]] (Zhāng-family genealogy by the compiler himself) makes the supplement a deliberately self-documenting institutional dossier — the Zhāng lineage placing its own credentials inside the canon they were entrusted to expand.
- **Inner alchemy (*nèi-dān* 內丹) and life-prolongation (*yǎng-shēng* 養生).** Smaller-scale but present throughout: [[KR5h0035]], [[KR5h0036]], [[KR5h0046]], plus the *Zǐ-wēi dǒu-shù* astrological corpus [[KR5h0055]] for cosmological body-cosmos correspondence.
- **The *Yì jīng* as Daoist-canonical scripture.** The terminal cluster ([[KR5h0042]], [[KR5h0043]], [[KR5h0044]]) reflects the Wànlì-era understanding that the *Yì* belongs as much to the Daoist as to the Confucian curriculum — culminating in the inclusion of Jiāo Hóng's anthological commentaries on the *Lǎo-zǐ* and *Zhuāng-zǐ* ([[KR5h0056]], [[KR5h0057]]) as the canon's closing word.
- **Editorial principle of the *Xù*.** Across the division, the supplement collects three kinds of material: (i) early-medieval texts the 1445 canon had missed (the Six-Dynasties and Táng block), (ii) late-imperial scriptures and liturgies generated since 1445, and (iii) late-Míng scholarly works the compilers wanted to canonize alongside earlier material. Distinguishing these three editorial layers is the principal interpretive task when working with KR5h.

## Timeline

- c. 50 BCE–50 CE — *Yì-lín shàng-xià jīng* 易林上下經 [[KR5h0044]] — Western-Hàn divinatory companion to the *Yì jīng*, attributed to Jiāo Gòng; oldest stratum in the division (received recension).
- c. 300–400 — *Sōu-shén jì* 搜神記 [[KR5h0045]] — Gān Bǎo's foundational *zhì-guài* collection; a shorter Daoist-canon recension.
- c. 317–420 — *Gāo-shàng yù-chén yōu-lè zhāng* 高上玉宸憂樂章 [[KR5h0026]] — Eastern-Jìn Shàng-qīng verse-scripture.
- c. 317–589 — *Shàng-qīng yuán-shǐ biàn-huà bǎo-zhēn shàng-jīng* 上清元始變化寶真上經 [[KR5h0005]] and *Tàishàng Lǎo-jūn kāitiān jīng* 太上老君開天經 [[KR5h0006]] — Six-Dynasties Shàng-qīng material, the latter a cosmogony attributed to [[張泮]].
- c. 618–907 — Táng-era cluster: *Tàishàng Lǎo-jūn xū-wú zì-rán běn-qǐ jīng* [[KR5h0007]], *Tàishàng shuō tōng-zhēn Gāo-huáng jiě-yuān jīng* [[KR5h0018]], *Tàiwēi dì-jūn èr-shí-sì shén huí-yuán jīng* [[KR5h0024]], *Běidǒu jiǔ-huáng yǐn-huì jīng* [[KR5h0025]], *Tàishàng dòng-zhēn huí-xuán zhāng* [[KR5h0027]], *Shàng-qīng jīn-zhāng shí-èr piān* [[KR5h0028]], *Tàishàng dòng-xuán jì-zhòng jīng* [[KR5h0029]] — astral, Shàng-qīng, Líng-bǎo, and inner-body scriptures preserved through late inclusion.
- c. 930–960 — *Huà-shū* (Tán-zǐ *Huà-shū*) 化書 [[KR5h0047]] — Five-Dynasties philosophical text by Tán Qiào, second recension.
- c. 1127–1279 — *Tàishàng sān-yuán cì-fú shè-zuì jiě-è xiāo-zāi yán-shēng bǎo-mìng miào-jīng* 太上三元賜福… [[KR5h0011]] — Southern-Sòng popular liturgy for the Three-Origins festivals.
- c. 1127–1607 — *Yuán-shǐ tiān-zūn shuō Dōng-yuè huà-shēn…* [[KR5h0010]] — Sòng–Míng Dōng-yuè cult scripture (wide bracket).

- c. 1400–1448 — *Tiān-huáng zhì-dào Tài-qīng yù-cè* 天皇至道太清玉冊 [[KR5h0053]] — [[朱權]] Prince Níng's mid-Míng Daoist *summa* (1444 序).
