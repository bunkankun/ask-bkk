## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR5h collects the **Xù Dàozàng** 續道藏, the supplement to the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (1445) compiled by the 50th Celestial Master **Zhāng Guóxiáng** 張國祥 (*d.* 1611) and printed in 1607 under imperial sponsorship by the Wànlì 萬曆 emperor. The supplement adds 32 *hán* 函 (case-units) of further material, numbered **DZ 1432–1487** (CT 1432–1487) in Schipper's *Concordance du Tao-tsang*, and is preserved in modern reprints as the 三家本 (Sānjiā běn, 1988) and the *Zhōnghuá Dàozàng* 中華道藏 (ZHDZ, 2004) editions. All 57 works in this division carry both `SJB` and `ZHDZ` in their `editions` frontmatter — the supplement was never separately printed in WYG 文淵閣四庫全書 form, since the Sìkù compilers regarded the Daoist Canon as outside the orthodox 子部 categorization.

Although KR5h sits inside Kanripo's **KR5 Masters** tree, the division is materially a Daoist-canon block rather than a *zhūzǐ* 諸子 block. The works range from genuine early-medieval Shàngqīng 上清 and Língbǎo 靈寶 scriptures preserved long after their composition, through Sòng–Yuán liturgies of the Shénxiāo 神霄 / Qīngwēi 清微 thunder-rite (*léifǎ* 雷法) traditions, to a large body of Míng popular-cult scriptures (Wáng Língguān 王靈官, Bìxiá yuánjūn 碧霞元君, Lǚzǔ 呂祖, Zhēnwǔ 真武, Huáguāng 華光, the city-god *chénghuáng* 城隍), and finally to a set of late-Míng anthologies and *Classics* commentaries (Jiāo Hóng's 焦竤 *Lǎozǐ yì* and *Zhuāngzǐ yì*) that mark the formal close of the canon. The supplement is therefore not a doctrinal corpus but an editorial container — its principle of selection was what the Wànlì-era court and the 龍虎山 Lónghǔ shān Celestial-Masters compilers judged worth preserving outside the 1445 canon, including older material that had been overlooked, newer scriptures generated by the popular pantheon, and the institutional self-documentation of the Zhāng lineage itself ([[KR5h0031]], [[KR5h0032]]).

## Important texts and text clusters

- **The Daoist *Lotus Sūtra* and Peacock-King cluster** — *Tàishàng zhōngdào miàofǎ liánhuā jīng* 太上中道妙法蓮花經 [[KR5h0001]] (the opening text of the supplement, a conscious Daoist re-modelling of the Buddhist *Saddharma-puṇḍarīka*), and the three-text Peacock-King block [[KR5h0002]], [[KR5h0003]], [[KR5h0004]] (a Daoist appropriation of Buddhist *Mahāmāyūrī* 孔雀明王). These texts set the supplement's signature register of Buddhist-Daoist syncretism.
- **Early-medieval Shàngqīng and Língbǎo scriptures preserved through late inclusion** — *Shàngqīng yuánshǐ biànhuà bǎozhēn shàngjīng* 上清元始變化寶真上經 [[KR5h0005]] (六朝), *Tàishàng Lǎojūn kāitiān jīng* 太上老君開天經 [[KR5h0006]] (六朝 cosmogony attributed to 張泮), *Gāoshàng yùchén yōulè zhāng* 高上玉宸憂樂章 [[KR5h0026]] (東晉), and the Táng Shàngqīng block [[KR5h0007]], [[KR5h0024]], [[KR5h0025]], [[KR5h0027]], [[KR5h0028]], [[KR5h0029]] — pre-Sòng material the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* had not absorbed.
- **Wàn-lì-era popular-cult scriptures** — the Wánglíngguān *Tàishàng Yuányáng shàngdì… Huǒchē Wánglíngguān zhēnjīng* 王靈官真經 [[KR5h0012]], the Medicine-King *Yàowáng* [[KR5h0013]], the Tàishān fertility goddess *Bìxiá yuánjūn* [[KR5h0014]], the Guān-Yǔ-associated *Lǎnglíng shàngjiāng* [[KR5h0015]], the city-god scripture [[KR5h0016]], and the *Huáguāng dàdì* / Mǎzǔ scripture [[KR5h0017]]. Together this cluster documents the late-imperial pantheon as the canon-compilers found it.
- **Liturgical *kē* and repentance texts** — *Zhōngtiān Zǐwēi xīngzhēn bǎochàn* 中天紫微星真寶懺 [[KR5h0019]], *Zǐhuáng liàndù xuánkē* 紫皇鍊度玄科 [[KR5h0020]], the Dǒumǔ / Marīcī liturgy *Xiāntiān Dǒumǔ zòugào xuánkē* 先天斗母奏告玄科 [[KR5h0021]] (the most fully analysed liturgical *kē* in the supplement, diagnostic of the Shénxiāo / Qīngwēi thunder-rite synthesis), *Cháozhēn fāyuàn chànhuǐ wén* [[KR5h0022]], and the Língbǎo *shīshí* hungry-ghost-feeding ritual [[KR5h0023]].
- **The Celestial-Masters institutional dossier** — *HuángMíng ēnmìng shìlù* 皇明恩命世錄 [[KR5h0031]] (a generational record of imperial favours to Lónghǔ shān) and *Hàn tiānshī shìjiā* 漢天師世家 [[KR5h0032]] (Zhāng Guóxiáng's own genealogy of the Celestial-Masters lineage from Zhāng Dàolíng 張道陵 onward), both fundamental to the institutional history of Zhèngyī 正一 Daoism.
- **Hagiographies and *xiānzhuàn* compendia** — *Xúxiān hànzǎo* 徐仙翰藻 [[KR5h0037]] and *Xúxiān zhēnlù* 徐仙真錄 [[KR5h0039]] (both centred on the Sòng thaumaturge Xú Shénwēng 徐神翁), *Hóngdào lù* 弘道錄 [[KR5h0033]] (Shào Jīngbāng's 25-*juàn* Daoist *Lives*), the syncretic *Xiāoyáo xū jīng* 消搖墟經 [[KR5h0034]], and *Lǚzǔ zhì* 呂祖志 [[KR5h0054]] (the LǚDòngbīn cult compendium).
- **Princely synthesis and sacred-geography** — *Tiānhuáng zhìdào Tàiqīng yùcè* 天皇至道太清玉冊 [[KR5h0053]] by the Míng Prince Níng **Zhū Quán** 朱權 (1378–1448), a comprehensive 8-*juàn* mid-Míng *summa* of Daoist doctrine, ritual, history and practice, and *Dài shǐ* 岱史 [[KR5h0041]], the 1586 gazetteer of Tàishān 泰山 (the chief of the Five Sacred Peaks and centre of the Dōngyuè 東嶽 cult).
- **Divination and almanac handbooks** — *Xǔ zhēnjūn yùxiá jì* 許真君玉匣記 [[KR5h0050]] (attributed to Xǔ Xùn 許遜, with its own table of contents [[KR5h0049]]), *Fǎshī xuǎnzé jì* 法師選擇記 [[KR5h0051]], *Xuántiān shàngdì bǎizì shènghào* 玄天上帝百字聖號 [[KR5h0052]] (a Zhēnwǔ divination text), and *Zǐwēi dǒushù* 紫微斗數 [[KR5h0055]], the foundational handbook of the still-practised Purple-Pole star-astrology system traditionally ascribed to Chén Tuán 陳摶.
- **The terminal *Classics*-commentary block** — *Yìyīn shàngxià jīng* 易因上下經 [[KR5h0042]] by the iconoclast **Lǐ Zhì** 李贄 (1527–1602), the anonymous *Gǔyì kǎoyuán* 古易考原 [[KR5h0043]], the Western-Hàn *Jiāoshì Yìlín* 焦氏易林 [[KR5h0044]] (4 096 four-line divinatory verses for paired hexagrams), and **Jiāo Hóng's** 焦竤 (1540–1620) *Lǎozǐ yì* 老子翼 [[KR5h0056]] and *Zhuāngzǐ yì* 莊子翼 [[KR5h0057]] — the closing pair of the entire Zhèngtǒng + Xù canon, and the standard late-Míng collected commentaries on the two great *zǐ* texts.
- **Two narrative and philosophical inclusions** — the Eastern-Jìn *Sōushén jì* 搜神記 [[KR5h0045]] (the foundational *zhìguài* 志怪 collection of **Gān Bǎo** 干寶) and Tán Qiào's 譚峭 Five-Dynasties *Huàshū* 化書 [[KR5h0047]] (a second recension of the work better known from [[KR5d0067]]), both included for their canonical status in the Daoist literary tradition.

## Important persons

- [[張國祥]] (Zhāng Guóxiáng, *d.* 1611) — 50th Celestial Master, **compiler of the Xù Dàozàng itself** and author of *Hàn tiānshī shìjiā* [[KR5h0032]]; defines the editorial perspective of the entire division.
- [[焦竤]] (Jiāo Hóng, 1540–1620) — late-Míng polymath and Hànlín academician; his *Lǎozǐ yì* [[KR5h0056]] and *Zhuāngzǐ yì* [[KR5h0057]] form the terminal text-block of the canon and remain standard reference editions.
- [[朱權]] (Zhū Quán, 1378–1448) — Míng Prince Níng, son of the Hóngwǔ emperor; his *Tàiqīng yùcè* [[KR5h0053]] is the major mid-Míng princely synthesis of Daoist doctrine and a key witness to early-Míng court patronage of Daoism.
- [[李贄]] (Lǐ Zhì, 1527–1602) — late-Míng iconoclast; his *Yìyīn* [[KR5h0042]] is the single most striking philosophical voice in the division.
- [[邵經邦]] (Shào Jīngbāng, 1491–1565) — Mid-Míng compiler of *Hóngdào lù* [[KR5h0033]], a 25-*juàn* prosopography linking Confucian-Daoist *dàotǒng* 道統 transmission.
- [[周玄貞]] (Zhōu Xuánzhēn) — compiler of *Huángjīng jízhù* [[KR5h0009]], the standard Míng collected-commentary on the Jade-Emperor scripture.
- [[洪應明]] (Hóng Yìngmíng) — late-Míng syncretic writer; editor of *Xiāoyáo xū jīng* [[KR5h0034]], better known for *Càigēn tán* 菜根譚 outside this canon.
- [[查志隆]] (Zhā Zhìlóng, *jìnshì* 1558) — author of the 1586 *Dài shǐ* [[KR5h0041]], the most thorough late-Míng gazetteer of Tàishān.
- [[黃恒]] (Huáng Héng) — compiler of *Xúxiān zhēnlù* [[KR5h0039]], printed 1443.
- [[譚峭]] (Tán Qiào, *fl.* ca. 880–950) — Five-Dynasties Daoist philosopher; *Huàshū* [[KR5h0047]] is reprinted here as a second recension.
- [[焦贛]] (Jiāo Gòng / Jiāo Yánshòu, end of the 1st century BCE) — Western-Hàn *Yì*-divination master; the *Jiāoshì Yìlín* [[KR5h0044]] is the great early-Hàn divinatory companion to the *Yì jīng*.
- [[干寶]] (Gān Bǎo, *fl.* 317–350) — Eastern-Jìn historian and *zhìguài* anthologist; the Dàozàng recension of *Sōushén jì* [[KR5h0045]] is a shorter version of his foundational supernatural-narrative collection.
- [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòngbīn) and [[許遜]] (Xǔ Xùn) — though not authors in any historical sense, they are the **two most prominent attribution-figures** of the division: Lǚzǔ as the subject of [[KR5h0054]], Xǔ Xùn as the cult-figure standing behind the *Yùxiá jì* divination tradition [[KR5h0049]], [[KR5h0050]].
- [[張泮]] (Zhāng Pàn) — Six-Dynasties figure to whom the cosmogonic *Kāitiān jīng* [[KR5h0006]] is attributed.

## Topics

- **Buddhist–Daoist syncretism as constitutive register.** The supplement opens with an explicit Daoist *Lotus Sūtra* ([[KR5h0001]]) and continues through a Peacock-King block ([[KR5h0002]]–[[KR5h0004]]) and the Dǒumǔ / Marīcī liturgy ([[KR5h0021]]). The borrowing is not incidental but the editorial posture of the whole division: by 1607 the canon-compilers treat Buddhist deities and ritual forms as Daoist material when transposed into the appropriate idiom.
- **The pantheon of the late-imperial popular cult.** A substantial bloc of the supplement is dedicated to deities whose cults had matured between the Sòng and the Wànlì period — Wáng Língguān (the Míng court-promoted exorcist-general), Bìxiá yuánjūn (the Tàishān fertility goddess), Lǚ Dòngbīn (the *quánzhēn* 全真 patriarch turned popular saviour), Zhēnwǔ 真武 / Xuántiān shàngdì, Huáguāng dàdì, the city-god *chénghuáng*, and the Dōngyuè 東嶽 cult of Tàishān. The division is therefore a primary source for the religious culture of late-Míng China at large, well beyond the institutional Daoist priesthood.
- **Thunder-rite (*léifǎ* 雷法) and the Shénxiāo / Qīngwēi liturgical synthesis.** Several of the *kē* texts ([[KR5h0019]], [[KR5h0020]], [[KR5h0021]]) sit firmly inside the post-Sòng *léifǎ* tradition descended from Wáng Wénqīng 王文卿 and Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾, with the canonical Dèng–Xīn–Zhāng 鄧辛張 thunder-marshal triad and the lineage of Zhāng Xūjìng 張虛靖. The supplement preserves the most analytically tractable late-imperial witnesses to this ritual lineage.
- **The institutional history of the Celestial Masters at Lónghǔ shān.** The pairing of [[KR5h0031]] (imperial-edict record) and [[KR5h0032]] (Zhāng-family genealogy by the compiler himself) makes the supplement a deliberately self-documenting institutional dossier — the Zhāng lineage placing its own credentials inside the canon they were entrusted to expand.
- **Inner alchemy (*nèidān* 內丹) and life-prolongation (*yǎngshēng* 養生).** Smaller-scale but present throughout: [[KR5h0035]], [[KR5h0036]], [[KR5h0046]], plus the *Zǐwēi dǒushù* astrological corpus [[KR5h0055]] for cosmological body-cosmos correspondence.
- **The *Yì jīng* as Daoist-canonical scripture.** The terminal cluster ([[KR5h0042]], [[KR5h0043]], [[KR5h0044]]) reflects the Wànlì-era understanding that the *Yì* belongs as much to the Daoist as to the Confucian curriculum — culminating in the inclusion of Jiāo Hóng's anthological commentaries on the *Lǎozǐ* and *Zhuāngzǐ* ([[KR5h0056]], [[KR5h0057]]) as the canon's closing word.
- **Editorial principle of the *Xù*.** Across the division, the supplement collects three kinds of material: (i) early-medieval texts the 1445 canon had missed (the Six-Dynasties and Táng block), (ii) late-imperial scriptures and liturgies generated since 1445, and (iii) late-Míng scholarly works the compilers wanted to canonize alongside earlier material. Distinguishing these three editorial layers is the principal interpretive task when working with KR5h.

- c. 300–400 — *Sōushén jì* 搜神記 [[KR5h0045]] — Gān Bǎo's foundational *zhìguài* collection; a shorter Daoist-canon recension.
- c. 317–420 — *Gāoshàng yùchén yōulè zhāng* 高上玉宸憂樂章 [[KR5h0026]] — Eastern-Jìn Shàngqīng verse-scripture.
- c. 317–589 — *Shàngqīng yuánshǐ biànhuà bǎozhēn shàngjīng* 上清元始變化寶真上經 [[KR5h0005]] and *Tàishàng Lǎojūn kāitiān jīng* 太上老君開天經 [[KR5h0006]] — Six-Dynasties Shàngqīng material, the latter a cosmogony attributed to [[張泮]].
- c. 618–907 — Táng-era cluster: *Tàishàng Lǎojūn xūwú zìrán běnqǐ jīng* [[KR5h0007]], *Tàishàng shuō tōngzhēn Gāohuáng jiěyuān jīng* [[KR5h0018]], *Tàiwēi dìjūn èrshísì shén huíyuán jīng* [[KR5h0024]], *Běidǒu jiǔhuáng yǐnhuì jīng* [[KR5h0025]], *Tàishàng dòngzhēn huíxuán zhāng* [[KR5h0027]], *Shàngqīng jīnzhāng shíèr piān* [[KR5h0028]], *Tàishàng dòngxuán jìzhòng jīng* [[KR5h0029]] — astral, Shàngqīng, Língbǎo, and inner-body scriptures preserved through late inclusion.
