# Tài shàng Yuán shǐ tiān zūn shuō Bǎo yuè guāng Huáng hòu shèng mǔ tiān zūn kǒng què míng wáng jīng 太上元始天尊說寶月光皇后聖母天尊孔雀明王經

*Daoist Scripture of the Peacock King-of-Light*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng **Daoist borrowing of the Buddhist Peacock-King (*Mahāmāyūrī*) deity** into Daoist ritual. Preserved as **DZ 1433** / CT 1433 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
