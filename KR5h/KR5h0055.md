# Zǐ wēi dǒu shù 紫微斗數

*Purple-Pole Star-Astrology*

Anonymous; Míng

**The foundational text of *zǐ wēi dǒu shù*** 紫微斗數 — the Chinese star-astrology system centred on the **Zǐ wēi** 紫微 (Purple-Pole) constellation, traditionally attributed to Chén Xī yí 陳希夷 (Chén Tuán 陳摶) of the early Sòng but the received handbook is a Míng compilation. Remains the most widely-practised Chinese horoscope system. Preserved as **DZ 1485** / CT 1485 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
