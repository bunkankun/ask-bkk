# Tài shàng Yuán yáng shàng dì wú shǐ tiān zūn shuō Huǒ chē Wáng líng guān zhēn jīng 太上元陽上帝無始天尊說火車王靈官真經

*Scripture of the Fire-Chariot Wáng Líng-guān*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng scripture of the **Wáng Líng-guān** 王靈官 cult — the Míng court-promoted Daoist exorcist-general and one of the most popular late-imperial deities. Preserved as **DZ 1443** / CT 1443 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
