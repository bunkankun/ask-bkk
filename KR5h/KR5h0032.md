# Hàn tiān shī shì jiā 漢天師世家

*Hereditary-House of the Hàn Celestial Master*

by [[張國祥]] (Zhāng Guóxiáng, *d.* 1611) — 50th Celestial Master

**The official genealogy** of the **Celestial Masters (Zhāng family) lineage** from Zhāng Dàolíng (2nd century CE) to Zhāng Guóxiáng's own time — an essential primary source for the institutional history of Zhèng yī Daoism. Preserved as **DZ 1463** / CT 1463 (續道藏).

**Dating.** 1607–1611. Dynasty: 明.

## Translations and research
