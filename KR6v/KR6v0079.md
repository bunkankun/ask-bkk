# Dìzàng púsà shí-zhāi-rì 地藏菩薩十齋日
*The Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva Ten Fast-Days (Recension II)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[張總]] (整理)

## About the work
*Recension II* of the *Shí-zhāi-rì* cluster — a short Dūnhuáng witness preserving a slightly different sequence of buddha/bodhisattva invocations and a different mapping of *zhāi-rì* to inspecting deities. See [[KR6v0077]] for the work's full context.

### Abstract
*Recension II* differs from *Recension I* primarily in the assignment of buddha-names to the ten *zhāi-rì*: *Recension II* gives a more complete list of *Mahāyāna* buddhas (including *Bhaiṣajyaguru* 藥師 and *Amitābha* 阿彌陀) where *Recension I* uses more generic appellations. Zhāng Zǒng treats *Recension II* as a slightly later refinement that integrates more fully with the *Pure Land* and *Bhaiṣajyaguru* devotional cults of the later 8th–9th c.
