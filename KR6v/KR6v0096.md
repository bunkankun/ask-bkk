# Jīngāng-jīng zhù sòng shì 金剛經注頌釋
*Annotational Commentary, Hymn-Praises, and Explanation of the Vajracchedikā*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[達照]] (整理)

## About the work
A middle-type witness of the *Jīngāng-jīng zàn* cycle — see [[KR6v0093]] for the cluster context. Title and structure differ markedly from the early-type *Jīngāng-jīng zàn*: this recension layers three textual registers — *zhù* 注 (interlinear annotation), *sòng* 頌 (verse-praise), and *shì* 釋 (prose explanation) — around the *Vajracchedikā* text.

### Abstract
The middle-type witness represents an intermediate stage between the bare 50-verse *zàn*-cycle of [[KR6v0093]]–[[KR6v0095]] and the fully-developed *Liáng-cháo Fù dàshì sòng Jīngāng-jīng* of [[KR6v0098]]–[[KR6v0100]]. It expands the *zàn*-cycle to function as a continuous reading-companion (*kē-pàn* 科判) for *Vajracchedikā* recitation. The composition window is mid-to-late Táng.
