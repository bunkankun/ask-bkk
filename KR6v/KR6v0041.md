# Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng 佛說相好經
*The Buddha's Spoken Sūtra on the Major and Minor Marks (recension d, system 2)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[黃霞]] (整理)

## About the work
The second system-2 recension of the *Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng* — see [[KR6v0038]] for the full description. This recension includes both the *shùn-guān nì-guān* 順觀逆觀 instructions and the "*sān niàn fó*" 三念佛 rubric after each mark, but lacks the "*bāshí yì jié shēngsǐ zhī zuì*" salvific-promise block, replacing it with a different closing passage ("*ruò yǒu xíngzhě… zhù shì rén yǔ*" 若有行者…注是人語).
