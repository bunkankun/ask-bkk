# Dìzàng púsà shí-zhāi-rì 地藏菩薩十齋日
*The Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva Ten Fast-Days (Recension IV)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[張總]] (整理)

## About the work
*Recension IV* of the *Shí-zhāi-rì* cluster. See [[KR6v0077]] for the work's full context.

### Abstract
*Recension IV* preserves an expanded form, integrating prefatory invocation of *Dìzàng-bóusà* 地藏菩薩 (Kṣitigarbha) with the *zhāi-rì* schedule. The prominence given to *Dìzàng* (rather than the more common pan-buddha mode of Recensions I–II) suggests that *Recension IV* belongs to the later strata of the textual tradition, when the cult of *Dìzàng* — institutionally consolidating in the late Táng — had attached itself to the *zhāi-rì* practice. The fully-developed title *Dìzàng-bóusà shí-zhāi-rì* (which gave the cluster its received name) is itself a *Recension IV* form.
