# Dìzàng púsà shí-zhāi-rì 地藏菩薩十齋日
*The Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva Ten Fast-Days (Recension VI)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[張總]] (整理)

## About the work
*Recension VI* of the *Shí-zhāi-rì* cluster — see [[KR6v0077]] for full context.

### Abstract
*Recension VI* contains substantial *gāthā* expansions and intercalated *Dà-shèng sì-dà zhāi-rì* 大乘四大齋日 ("Mahāyāna four-great fast days") material — a parallel system of four fast-days (the four solstices/equinoxes) that overlapped with but was structurally distinct from the *shí-zhāi-rì*. *Recension VI* is therefore a syncretic form combining two ritual calendars.
