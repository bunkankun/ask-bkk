# Jīngāngjīng sòng (nǐ) 金剛經頌（擬）
*Vajracchedikā Hymn-Praise (provisionally titled)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[達照]] (整理)

## About the work
A second middle-type witness of the *Jīngāngjīng zàn* cycle — see [[KR6v0093]] for the cluster context. The *(nǐ)* marker indicates that the manuscript lacks original title-cartouches.

### Abstract
The text develops the *zàn*-form into pure *sòng* 頌 (hymn-praise) recital, dropping the integrated *Vajracchedikā* passage-quotation found in [[KR6v0094]]–[[KR6v0096]]. This minimised form is a step toward the autonomous *zàn*-cycle as performance-text. Composition window mid-to-late Táng.
