# Dìzàng púsà shí-zhāi-rì 地藏菩薩十齋日
*The Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva Ten Fast-Days (Recension III)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[張總]] (整理)

## About the work
*Recension III* of the *Shí-zhāi-rì* cluster, distinct from Recensions I and II principally in title-form and chapter-organisation. See [[KR6v0077]] for the full discussion of the work.

### Abstract
*Recension III* is sometimes titled *Xuán-zàng fǎshī lǐ-bài zhú-yuè yǒu shí-zhāi-rì* 玄奘法師禮拜逐月有十齋日 — a pseudo-attribution to the famous Táng pilgrim *Xuán-zàng* 玄奘 (ca. 602–664). The attribution is anachronistic (the *Shí-zhāi-rì* system fits its own internal evidence to the late-Táng popular-Buddhist environment) but reflects the medieval Chinese practice of attaching famous translator-names to popular ritual texts to lend them authority.
