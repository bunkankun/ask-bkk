# Dà bān nièpán jīng Fómǔ pǐn 大般涅槃經佛母品
*Chapter on the Buddha's Mother in the Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra (recension b)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[李際寧]] (整理)

## About the work
The *second* of four Dūnhuáng recensions of the apocryphal *Fómǔ jīng* 佛母經 — see [[KR6v0018]] for full literary description and bibliographic context. This recension is edited from Beijing Library *Běi* 6628 (月 43). The narrative is fragmentary at the head but preserves the standard six-dream-omens sequence: (1) Mt. Sumeru collapsing and the four oceans drying up, (2) a lion biting her body with her breasts spontaneously yielding milk, (3) fierce fire burning her body, etc.

### Abstract
See [[KR6v0018]] for the textual-critical and bibliographic discussion. Recension *b* is principally distinguished from *a* in narrative compression and a slightly different ordering of the dream-list. Lǐ Jìníng's edition uses *Běi* 6628 alone, with no other collateral.
