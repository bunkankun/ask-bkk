# Yáng-kē gāng-yào 瘍科綱要
*A Framework for Ulcer Medicine*
by [[張壽頤]] (Zhāng Shòuyí, *hào* Shān-léi 山雷, 1873–1934)

## About the work

One of the most influential Republican-era TCM textbooks for external medicine, in 2 *juǎn* (卷上 / 卷下). Zhāng Shòuyí (*hào* Shān-léi 山雷) was a founding teacher of the modern Chinese-medicine school system; this work was used as classroom material at the Lán-xī Zhōng-yī Zhuān-mén Xué-xiào 蘭谿中醫專門學校 (Zhèjiāng), one of the first formal TCM colleges. The work marks the modernisation of the *yáng-kē* genre — moving from miscellany-of-formulae to systematic clinical reasoning — and is one of the principal Republican-era responses to the perceived need to professionalise traditional surgery against Western competition. First published 1917; circulated in revised form into the late 1920s.

### Abstract

There is no *_000.txt* (no front-matter file) in the Kanripo digitisation; the WYG-editions field does not apply to this Republican text. The work opens directly with *juǎn* shàng 第一章 *Wài-yáng zǒng lùn* 外瘍總論, which functions as its programmatic preface. Zhāng polemically rejects Wáng Hóng-xù [[王洪緒]] (cf. [[KR3ek017]] *Wài-kē zhèng-zhì quán-shēng jí*) for equating *yōng* 癰 with yang and *jū* 疽 with yin, arguing instead that *yōng* simply means "blockage" (*yōng* 壅) and *jū* "obstruction" (*jǔ* 沮 / *zǔ* 阻), and that both must be analysed by careful application of yīn-yáng along multiple axes simultaneously (location, depth, speed of progression, virulence, hardness, pain quality). He aligns himself doctrinally with the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Wài-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué* [[KR3ek009]] tradition against the *quán-shēng pài*'s simpler colour-based dichotomy.

The two *juǎn* form a systematic *wài-kē* handbook combining classical theory with modern textbook organisation. Topical sections cover the general theory of external lesions (yīn-yáng, swelling, pain, itch, pus, fistula formation, sequelae), then specific lesion classes (*yōng-jū* 癰疽, *liú-zhù* 流注, *luǒ-lì* 瘰癧, *tán-hé* 痰核, *yáng-méi* 楊梅, *dīng-dú* 疔毒). Strong didactic and critical orientation throughout; explicitly written as classroom teaching material.

Zhāng Shòuyí (*hào* Shān-léi 山雷; *zì* Shòufǔ 壽甫) was born in Jiā-dìng 嘉定 (modern Shàng-hǎi) in 1873 and died in 1934. After the 1911 revolution he taught at the Lán-xī medical college, where he trained a generation of Mínguó physicians. His major works include the *Běn-cǎo zhèng-yì* 本草正義 ([[KR3ec063]], printed 1932), the *Zhōng-fēng kǎo-quán* 中風斠詮, and the *Shān-léi yī-xué cóng-shū* 山雷醫學叢書. The *Běn-cǎo zhèng-yì* is the principal Republican-era critical pharmacopoeia and the immediate predecessor of the modern PRC TCM materia medica curriculum.

## Other points of interest

The work's explicit pedagogical orientation makes it one of the clearest documents of the early-Republican TCM-college transformation of *wài-kē*: the lesion-by-lesion miscellany of late-imperial surgical handbooks gives way to a textbook structured around general theory, then disease classes, then differential and prescription. Compare *Tán-lì fǎ-mén* [[KR3ek033]], a contemporary work that retains the older family-lineage *jiā-chuán* form.
