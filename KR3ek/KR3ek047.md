# Chún-yì-lú jí (chuāng-yáng) 純懿廬集（瘡瘍）
*Anthology of the Hall of Pure Virtue (Sores and Ulcers)*
anonymous; a chapter / fascicle extracted from a larger *Chún-yì-lú* 純懿廬 collection of unknown overall provenance.

## About the work

A concise late-Qīng *wài-kē* fascicle on common superficial skin sores, extracted from the larger anonymous *Chún-yì-lú* (the *Chún-yì-lú* being a private studio name; the larger collection's authorship has not been recovered). Internal evidence — the text cites the imperial *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* [[KR3ek009]] (1742) — fixes the composition as post-1742; the stylistic register is later-Qīng. Defensible composition bracket: 1742–1911. Listed in UNCERTAIN.md.

### Abstract

There is no preface in the source file; the text opens directly with the *zhèng-wén* 正文. *No tí-yào / preface located in source.*

The work is a tightly organised theoretical-clinical fascicle on superficial skin sores classified under four headings — *shī chuāng* 濕瘡 (damp sores), *shā chuāng* 砂瘡 (gritty sores), *nóng-kē chuāng* 膿窠瘡 (boil-nest sores), *zuò-bǎn chuāng* 坐板瘡 (sitting-board sores, i.e. perineal dermatitis) — with aetiology in liver-spleen disequilibrium and *fēng-rè* / *shī-rè* 風熱 / 濕熱 invasion. The author proceeds in *Nèi-jīng*-style aphoristic *wèn-dá* 問答 pairs, each clinical sub-pattern followed by a tailored formula: *jīng-qiāng sǎn* 荊羌散, *bò-hé kū-cǎo tāng* 薄荷枯草湯, *cāng-zhú dāng-guī tāng* 蒼朮當歸湯, *cāng-zhú yín-huā tāng* 蒼朮銀花湯, *shēng-qí chuān-xiōng tāng* 生耆川芎湯, *dú-huó shēng-dì tāng* 獨活生地湯, *dǎn-cǎo shēng-dì tāng* 膽草生地湯, *shú-dì yín-huā tāng* 熟地銀花湯, *shú-dì ròu-guì tāng* 熟地肉桂湯, *rǔ-xiāng jiè-suì tāng* 乳香芥穗湯, *yuán-huā rǔ-xiāng tāng* 芫花乳香湯, *kǔ-shēn dì-yú tāng* 苦參地榆湯, and others. The text cites the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* on *zuò-bǎn chuāng* = *fēng gān* 風疳.

The work is distinctive in its compression and its pulse-and-pattern-driven (rather than formulary-driven) presentation — characteristic of the Jiāng-Zhè scholar-physician strand of late-Qīng medicine. It is a useful concise late-Qīng *wài-kē* monograph on common skin disorders, valuable for its tight pulse–pattern–formula trinity and for evidence of the integration of *zá-bìng* 雜病 / *shāng-hán* 傷寒 pulse logic into *wài-kē*.

The *Chún-yì-lú* collection is little studied and the work's authorship has not been recovered; further bibliographic work is needed. Authorial absence in catalog meta is preserved.
