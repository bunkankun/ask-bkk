# Lǐjì Fàn-shì yīn 禮記范氏音

*Master Fàn's Phonological Commentary on the* Lǐjì

by [[范宣]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[范宣]] Fàn Xuān's (Eastern-Jìn, *fl.* mid-fourth century) lost *Lǐjì Fàn-shì yīn* 禮記范氏音 — a phonological commentary on the *Lǐjì* ([[KR1d0049]]) in the same Eastern-Jìn *yīn-yì* tradition as the parallel [[徐邈]] [[KR1d0128]] *Lǐjì Xú-shì yīn* and [[劉昌宗]] [[KR1d0129]] *Lǐjì Liú-shì yīn*. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1102) is drawn from Lù Démíng 陸德明's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 citations.

### Abstract

The text follows the canonical 49-*piān* organisational structure of the *Lǐjì*, with each *piān* opening with phonological glosses on difficult readings.

The opening preserved fragments cover:

(i) **\<Qǔlǐ xià\> 曲禮下** — gloss *zhǔ pèi yǐ yú qǐ fǎn* 主佩倚於綺反 (the host's pendant — *yī*, glossed *yú qǐ fǎn* in *fǎn-qiè*).

(ii) **\<Yuèlìng\> 月令** — phonological glosses on calendrical and ritual-cycle vocabulary.

The text continues through the canonical *Lǐjì* *piān*-sequence with characteristic Fàn-school *fǎn-qiè* readings, often paired with citations from Xú Miǎo and Liú Chāngzōng to mark Fàn Xuān's particular preferred reading.

Fàn Xuān is also the author of [[KR1d0102]] *Lǐlùn dáwèn* — his Eastern-Jìn private-school ritual question-and-answer compilation. The *Lǐjì Fàn-shì yīn* derives from the same Yùzhāng 豫章 private-academy teaching context.

The dating bracket (330–410) is approximate, covering Fàn Xuān's documented mid-fourth-century teaching activity. He died sometime after 402 (cf. [[KR1d0102]] note).

## Other points of interest

The *Lǐjì Fàn-shì yīn* is the smallest of the four reconstructed Eastern-Jìn / Northern-Wèi *Lǐjì yīn* commentaries in this corpus, paired with the more substantial [[徐邈]] [[KR1d0128]] *Lǐjì Xú-shì yīn* and [[劉昌宗]] [[KR1d0129]] *Lǐjì Liú-shì yīn*. The three works together constitute the principal pre-Lù-Démíng phonological apparatus to the *Lǐjì*.
