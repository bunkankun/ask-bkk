# Zhōulǐ Xú-shì yīn 周禮徐氏音

*Master Xú's Phonological Commentary on the* Zhōulǐ

by [[徐邈]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[徐邈]] Xú Miǎo's (344–397) lost *Zhōulǐ Xú-shì yīn* 周禮徐氏音 — a fully phonological-only commentary (*yīn* 音) on the *Zhōulǐ* / *Zhōuguān* (cf. [[KR1d0001]]), preserving *fǎn-qiè* spellings for difficult words. Xú Miǎo, *zì* Xiānmín 仙民, native of Dōnghǎi Tán-xiàn 東海郯縣, was the leading Eastern-Jìn phonological-glossator of his generation, with parallel phonological commentaries on the *Wǔjīng* (e.g. [[KR1d0128]] *Lǐjì Xú-shì yīn*) and the *Shàngshū*. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1073) is drawn exclusively from Lù Démíng 陸德明's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文.

### Abstract

The text is reconstructed from Lù Démíng's named-author citations within his comprehensive *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* phonological apparatus for the *Zhōulǐ*. The structure follows the canonical *Liù-guān* (six-office) order of the *Zhōulǐ* — *Tiānguān zhǒngzǎi* (Heaven-office, Treasurer), *Dìguān sītú* (Earth-office, Director of Multitudes), *Chūnguān zōngbó* (Spring-office, Director of Rites), *Xiàguān sīmǎ* (Summer-office, Director of Horse), *Qiūguān sīkòu* (Autumn-office, Director of Punishments), and *Kǎogōng jì* (Records of Examination of Crafts — replacing the lost Winter-office). The opening preserved fragments cover the *biàn fāng zhèng wèi* 辨方正位 opening line of the *Tiānguān zhǒngzǎi*, with *biàn* glossed *fāng-miǎn fǎn* 方免反 (*fǎn-qiè* for the dental-onset reading); the *Lǚ xià-shì sānshí yǒu èr rén* 旅下士三十有二人 line with *èr* glossed *yīn èr* 音二.

The text is a high-density phonological gloss-collection rather than a substantive ritual-commentary; its scholarly importance is for the reconstruction of Eastern-Jìn-period pronunciation of difficult-character readings in the canonical *Zhōulǐ* text.

The dating bracket (339–397) reflects Xú Miǎo's documented lifespan in *Jìn shū* 91 (列傳 61). The *Zhōulǐ Xú-shì yīn* is conventionally placed in his late-Tài-yuán period.

## Other points of interest

The *Zhōulǐ Xú-shì yīn* is one of the two major Eastern-Jìn freestanding *Zhōulǐ yīn-yì* works (alongside [[KR1d0112]] *Zhōulǐ Liú-shì yīn* by Liú Chāng-zōng) — the two together constitute the principal Lù-Démíng-period phonological apparatus to the *Zhōulǐ* and are key sources for the historical phonology of Middle Chinese.
