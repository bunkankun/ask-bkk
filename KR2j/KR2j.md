# KR2j 時令類 (Shílìng lèi) — Histories: Seasonal Ordinances

## Scope and scholarly tradition

The *Shílìng* 時令 ("Seasonal Ordinances") category of the *Sìkù quánshū* gathers compilations on the calendar year, the festivals (*suìshí* 歲時 / *jiérì* 節日), the phenological signs (*wùhòu* 物候), and the ritual-administrative *yuèlìng* 月令 prescriptions that govern court and countryside through the twelve months. The genre's textual ancestry runs from the *Yuèlìng* 月令 chapter of the *Lǐjì* (itself drawn from the *Lǚshì chūnqiū* 呂氏春秋), through Cài Yōng's 蔡邕 and Kǒng Yǐngdá's 孔穎達 exegesis, through the Hàn agro-festival manuals (Cuī Shí's 崔寔 *Sìmín yuèlìng* 四民月令, late 2nd c.), the medieval almanacs (*Sìshí zuǎnyào* 四時纂要), and into the Sòng festival anthologies and late-Míng household encyclopedias. As an independent *Sìkù* category, *shílìng* is a relatively late editorial gesture: as Wilkinson notes (§39.15.1), the category was a Sòng innovation, with such works having previously been folded into agricultural and miscellaneous treatises in the *zǐbù*.

The *Sìkù* editors admitted only two titles to this category in the canon proper — a Southern Sòng compilation and a Kāngxī imperial revision of a late-Míng work. Together they bracket nearly five centuries of *yuèlìng* anthologizing and represent the genre's two principal modes: the eclectic *bàiguān* festival compendium of late-Sòng literati culture (*Suìshí guǎngjì* [[KR2j0001]]) and the imperially-supervised, classically-anchored court compilation (*Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* [[KR2j0002]]). The division is therefore unusually small but conceptually self-contained — Wilkinson's seasonal-calendar discussion (§9.12, §39.15) treats the two as the matching late-Sòng and high-Qīng landmarks of the genre.

## Important texts and text clusters

- *Suìshí guǎngjì* 歲時廣記 [[KR2j0001]] — Chén Yuánjìng's 陳元靚 four-juan late-Southern-Sòng anthology of festival lore arranged by month (春 46 / 夏 50 / 秋 32 / 冬 38 entries); the principal Sòng vehicle for the partially-lost *Jīng Chǔ suìshí jì* 荊楚歲時記 of Zōng Lǐn 宗懍, and the work in which the late-imperial Chinese festival year takes its received shape (Wilkinson §9.12.1).
- *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* 御定月令輯要 [[KR2j0002]] — the Kāngxī court's 24-juan re-edition of Féng Yìngjīng's 馮應京 late-Míng *Yuèlìng guǎngyì* 月令廣義, with imperial preface dated Kāngxī 54.4.9 (12 May 1715); the standard imperial-Qīng *yuèlìng* repository, organized in a four-tier annual / seasonal / monthly / sub-monthly hierarchy and rebuilt on the editorial template *biéqǐ yìlì* 別起義例 dictated by Kāngxī himself.

The two titles together also mark a methodological contrast that is visible across the category: the Sòng compilation favours full quotation from informal narrative literature (*bàiguān* 稗官) and folk practice, while the Qīng compilation enforces the descending citation hierarchy classics → histories → philosophers → regional sources that organized the Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿 imperial publishing programme.

## Important persons

- [[陳元靚]] — late-Southern-Sòng *jūshì* of Fújiàn / Mǐnběi book-trade origin, compiler of *Suìshí guǎngjì* [[KR2j0001]] and of the foundational *rìyòng lèishū* 日用類書 *Shìlín guǎngjì* 事林廣記; the only attributed Sòng author in the category.
- [[朱鑑]] (1190–1258) — eldest grandson of Zhū Xī 朱熹, *zhī Wúwéijūn Cháoxiàn shì*, author of the preface that anchors the *Suìshí guǎngjì* in Lǐzōng's reign and provides the principal evidence for the work's dating.
- [[李光地]] (1642–1718) — *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì* and chief director (*zǒng yuè* 總閱) of the *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* [[KR2j0002]]; the senior figure in the late-Kāngxī Hànlín editorial cohort that produced the imperial revision.
- [[王掞]] — *Wényuāngé dàxuéshì jiān Lǐbù shàngshū*, co-director of [[KR2j0002]].
- [[何焯]] (1661–1722) — the foremost philological textual critic of the Kāngxī Hànlín, member of the proof-collation team for [[KR2j0002]]; his presence on the editorial roll is the principal sign of the work's bibliographic seriousness.
- [[張廷玉]] (1672–1755), [[蔣廷錫]] (1669–1732), [[陳邦彥]] (1678–1752), [[楊名時]] (1661–1736), [[張照]] (1691–1745), [[陳鵬年]] (1663–1723) — junior Hànlín collators on [[KR2j0002]] who would go on to dominate the early Yōngzhèng and Qiánlóng courts; their participation locates the category's flagship work at the heart of the late-Kāngxī Hànlín *xuétáng* pipeline that staffed the Wǔyīngdiàn imperial-publishing programme.

## Topics

- **The *yuèlìng* lineage.** The *Lǐjì* *Yuèlìng* chapter and the *Lǚshì chūnqiū*, mediated through Cài Yōng and Kǒng Yǐngdá, anchor every work in the category. The compilers of [[KR2j0002]] make the lineage explicit in the imperial preface, naming *Sìmín yuèlìng* 四民月令, *Sìshí zuǎnyào* 四時纂要, *Qiānjīn yuèlìng* 千金月令, *Jǐndài* 錦帶, *Suìhuá jìlì* 歲華紀麗, and *Suìshí zá jì* 歲時雜記 as the genre's medieval and Sòng exemplars.
- **Phenology and the calendar of the husbandman.** The four-tier *suìlìng / yuèlìng / jiéxù / hòu* time-grid of [[KR2j0002]] and the month-by-month structure of [[KR2j0001]] both serve a practical agronomic and ritual function: phenological signs (*hòu* 候) signal the times for sowing, harvesting, sacrifice, and state observance.
- **Correlative cosmology.** The illustrated *juànshǒu* of [[KR2j0002]] (18 diagrams: *Tàijí* — *Liǎngyí* — *Sìxiàng* — *Bāguà*, the 12 *bì* hexagrams, the 24 *qì*, the 72 *hòu*, the *lǜlǚ* 律呂 pitch-pipes and five-tones, the *Tàisuì* stem-and-branch tables) embeds the *yuèlìng* tradition in the Sòng *Yìxué* (Shào Yōng, Zhū Xī) and Hàn correlative *yīnyáng wǔxíng* schemes — and includes the *tǔ wáng* 土王 (earth-phase interregnum) juan absent from the *Lǐjì Yuèlìng* but central to Hàn cosmology.
- **Festival history and the late-imperial canon.** [[KR2j0001]] is the textual point at which the late-imperial Chinese festival year takes recognizable form: the firm attachment of Qū Yuán 屈原 to the Duānwǔ 端午, the centralization of dragon-boat racing as *lóngzhōu jìngdù* 龍舟競渡, the linking of the Chóngyáng 重陽 hill-climbing custom to Fèi Chángfáng 費長房 (Wilkinson §9.12.4).
- **Canonical calendar vs. operative calendar.** The *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* makes explicit at j. 24 that its phenological table follows the Jesuit-reformed Qīng *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 and "differs slightly from the *Yuèlìng*" — a methodological tension between the canonical-correlative *yuèlìng* and the operative civil calendar that runs through the entire category.
- **Citation hierarchy.** The Sòng compilation draws heavily on informal narrative literature (*bàiguān* 稗官) and is criticized by the *Sìkù* editors for under-using *Ěryǎ* 爾雅 and *Huáinánzǐ* 淮南子; the Qīng compilation enforces a descending priority of canonical exegesis → histories and transmissions → philosophers, regional records, gazetteers. The shift mirrors the moral-philological orientation of the late-Kāngxī court.
- **Transmission and editorial loss.** The *Sìkù*-edition of [[KR2j0001]] descends from Cáo Róng's 曹溶 *Xuéhǎi lèibiān* 學海類編 recension and has lost the prefatory diagrams (*tú shuō* 圖說) described in Qián Zēng's 錢曾 *Dúshū mǐnqiú jì* 讀書敏求記 — a reminder that the *Sìkù* recensions are not always the fullest available witnesses.

## Timeline

- 1220–1265 — *Suìshí guǎngjì* 歲時廣記 [[KR2j0001]] — late-Southern-Sòng (Lǐzōng's reign) anthology of festival lore by month; the bibliographic landmark at which the late-imperial festival year takes its received form.
- 1700–1715 — *Yùdìng yuèlìng jíyào* 御定月令輯要 [[KR2j0002]] — imperially-supervised Kāngxī court compilation, preface dated 12 May 1715, name-list promulgated April 1716; the standard imperial-Qīng *yuèlìng* repository, succeeding and re-editing Féng Yìngjīng's late-Míng *Yuèlìng guǎngyì*.
