# Chén Xīn-tián wài-kē fāng-àn 陳莘田外科方案
*Surgical Case-and-Prescription Records of Chén Xīn-tián*
by [[陳莘田]] Chén Xīn-tián 陳莘田 (mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgeon).

## About the work

A six-*juǎn* *wài-kē* 外科 (external-medicine / surgical) casebook of the mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgical specialist Chén Xīn-tián. The opening of the hxwd `_000.txt` is a long mnemonic-verse compendium of *wài-kē* prescriptions (*Wài-kē bèi-yòng zhū-fāng* 外科備用諸方 — "all the prescriptions for surgical use") in seven-syllable rhyming couplets — a standard Qīng pedagogical format for committing prescription compositions to memory. The first prescription is *Zhēn-rén huó-mìng yǐn* 真人活命飲 — the foundational late-Míng / Qīng surgical formula for *yōng-jū* 癰疽 (abscesses and carbuncles) — with the mnemonic: *"Zhēn-rén huó-mìng yòng yín-huā, Fáng-bái guī chén cǎo-jié jiā, bèi-mǔ tiān-huā jiā jiān rǔ-mò, chuān-shān jiǎo-cì jiǔ jiān jiā"* (Real-person life-saving uses *jīn-yín-huā*; add *fáng-fēng*, *bái-zhǐ*, *dāng-guī*, *chén-pí*, *gān-cǎo jié*; *bèi-mǔ*, *tiān-huā fěn* added with *rǔ-xiāng* and *mò-yào*; *chuān-shān-jiǎ*, *zào-jiǎo cì*, wine-decocted, excellent). The verse-mnemonic format extends through more than thirty prescriptions covering the entire Qīng *wài-kē* repertoire.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` opens directly with the *Wài-kē bèi-yòng zhū-fāng* mnemonic-verse compendium; no standalone preface precedes it. *No formal preface in `_000.txt`.*

### Abstract

Chén Xīn-tián 陳莘田 — mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgeon, conventionally dated to the early-to-mid nineteenth century (Dào-guāng / Xián-fēng era). Biographical data are thin; he is treated in modern Chinese surveys as one of the principal Sūzhōu *wài-kē* practitioners of his generation. CBDB has no entry. The composition window 1820–1880 reflects his conventional floruit.

The work is one of several mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgical casebooks — a regional specialism of the Sūzhōu medical tradition that descends from Chén Shí-gōng's 陳實功 Míng-period *Wài-kē zhèng-zōng* 外科正宗 (1617) and from the Qián-lóng-era imperially-commissioned *Wài-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué* 外科心法要訣 of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* 醫宗金鑑. The verse-mnemonic compendium at the head of the work is a pedagogical companion to the cases proper, allowing students to commit the standard formulas to memory.
