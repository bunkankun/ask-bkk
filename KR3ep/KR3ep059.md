# Wáng Zhòng-qí yī-àn 王仲奇醫案
*Medical Case Records of Wáng Zhòng-qí*
by [[王仲奇]] Wáng Zhòng-qí (1881–1945), of Shè-xiàn 歙縣 (Ān-huī).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* casebook of the early-twentieth-century Xīn-ān 新安 school physician Wáng Zhòng-qí — one of the most influential Shàng-hǎi practitioners of the 1920s–1930s. The hxwd source preserves the case-records without preface, the patient-name and address given in canonical Shàng-hǎi street form (汪，唐家弄; 王，十六浦; etc.) — confirming the Shàng-hǎi setting.

### Abstract

Wáng Zhòng-qí 王仲奇 (1881–1945), *míng* Jīn-jié 金杰, of Shè-xiàn 歙縣 (the Huī-zhōu / Xīn-ān homeland in southern Ān-huī), descended from a long-established Xīn-ān 新安 medical lineage. He moved his practice to Shàng-hǎi in the early Republican period and built a major clinical reputation in the foreign-concession period, alongside [[丁甘仁]] Dīng Gānrén, [[陳蓮舫]] Chén Lián-fǎng, [[費繩甫]] Fèi Shéng-fǔ, and Cáo Yǐng-fǔ [[曹穎甫]].

The casebook represents Wáng's mature clinical signature: **Yè-school light-and-pacifying** prescribing infused with the Xīn-ān school's characteristic attention to *gān-yīn* / *shèn-yīn* (liver-yin / kidney-yin) deficiency. The opening cases address throat-pain with *xuán-yōng* descent (懸壅下垂) and liver-gall-bladder *qì*-transversal-blockage of stomach-descent, treated with light *hǎi-gé-fěn / chuān-bèi-mǔ / qīng-dài / shí-hú* combinations and *xuán-fù-huā / dài-zhě-shí / fǎ-bàn-xià* for the *gān-wèi* axis. This is a textbook Xīn-ān-meets-Yè-school clinical compendium.

The composition window 1900–1945 reflects Wáng's mature clinical decades through to his death. The cases were collected by his disciples; first major Republican printing in the early 1930s, later widely reproduced in twentieth-century Chinese-medical compilations.
