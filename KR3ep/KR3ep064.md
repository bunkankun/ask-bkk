# Lín-zhěn yī-àn 臨診醫案
*Bedside Medical Case Records*
Anonymous compiler.

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* anonymous mid-Qīng casebook from the Wú-sōng 吳淞 / Shàng-hǎi region. The cases are exactly dated; the opening case carries the precise date *Dào-guāng dīng-yǒu shí-yī yuè shí-sì rì* (Dào-guāng 17 = 14 November 1837) and is set on board the *Shī Cháng-shùn shā-chuán* 施長順沙船 (a sand-junk in the Wú-sōng / Yangtze-mouth coastal trade) — providing an unusually firm *terminus ante quem* and an unusual maritime clinical setting.

### Abstract

The casebook is set on the maritime trade route off Wú-sōng — the patient-list includes 朱象兄 (a passenger / shā-chuán client) at 35 *suì*, 浦大兄 of 吳淞南門外 (outside the south gate of Wú-sōng), 浦良兄 of 吳淞 (Wú-sōng), and 朱大兄 of 吳淞. The compiler is unnamed but is clearly a working clinician of the lower Yangtze coastal-trade community.

The clinical signature — careful pulse-and-tongue notation, light-aromatic-cleansing prescriptions for *dōng-wēn* (冬溫, winter-warmth), *xié-shí mēng-zǔ* (邪食蒙阻, blocked food-pathogens at middle-burner) — is characteristic of the late-Yè-school Jiāng-Zhè urban tradition. The 1837 date situates the text in the lower-Yangtze medical milieu of the Dào-guāng period, contemporary with [[李冠仙]] Lǐ Guàn-xiān ([[KR3ep060]]). The composition window 1837–1880 reflects an active clinical career from the dated opening case to a presumed mid-Tóng-zhì / Guāng-xù compilation.
