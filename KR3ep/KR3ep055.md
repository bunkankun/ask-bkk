# Jiāng Zé-zhī yī-àn 江澤之醫案
*Medical Case Records of Jiāng Zé-zhī*
by [[江澤之]] Jiāng Zé-zhī (late Qīng).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* casebook of the late-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician Jiāng Zé-zhī. The text is preserved without preface or compiler-colophon in the hxwd source; only the case-material is transmitted. Cases are arranged by syndrome, opening with 瘧疾 (intermittent fevers) and proceeding through 痢疾 (dysentery) and other internal-medicine categories.

### Abstract

Jiāng Zé-zhī's biographical record is meagre. Internal evidence — vocabulary (子母瘧, 一甲煎, 募原), drug repertoire (柴胡, 黃芩, 半夏, 杏仁, 西洋參, 牡蠣, 鱉甲, 老米, 烏梅), and the framing of *fú-xié* (伏邪, "lurking pathogen") — places the work within the late-Qīng *wēn-bìng* / *fú-qì* school, in the lineage of Yè Tiān-shì ([[葉桂]]) and Wáng Mèng-yīng ([[王士雄]]). The frequent use of *Xiǎo Chái-hú jiā jiǎn* (modified Lesser Bupleurum decoction) plus *yī-jiǎ jiān* (one-shell decoction) signals a Sòng-Yáo-school inflected clinical practice. The text was likely composed in the second half of the nineteenth century.

The casebook is meticulous about disease-mechanism reasoning (*biàn-zhèng*) and unusually clear in noting therapeutic intent — "speedy resolution then auspicious" (速解乃吉), "adjust harmonisation supporting the upright" (扶正和解) — which makes it pedagogically useful and suggests it may have been compiled for didactic purposes.
