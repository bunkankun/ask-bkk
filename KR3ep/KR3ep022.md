# Zēng-bǔ píng-zhù Liǔ-xuǎn yī-àn 增補評注柳選醫案
*Supplemented and Critically Annotated Edition of the Liǔ-Selected Case Records*
edited and supplemented by later hands (Republican-era), based on [[柳寶詒]] Liǔ Bǎo-yí 柳寶詒 (1842–1901).

## About the work

An eight-*juǎn* supplemented edition of [[KR3ep021]] *Liǔ-xuǎn sì-jiā yī-àn* — the late-Qīng anthology of cases from four early-to-mid-Qīng Jiāngsū physicians (Cáo Cún-xīn [[曹存心]], Wáng Jiǔ-fēng [[王九峰]], Gāo Bǐng-jūn 高炳鈞 = Gāo Jǐn-tíng, Cài Lěng-ān 蔡冷庵) edited by Liǔ Bǎo-yí — with further annotations and supplementary case material added by Republican-era editors. The supplementing tradition began with Dīng Gānrén 丁甘仁 ([[丁甘仁]]) and other Shànghǎi-Méng-hé physicians shortly after Liǔ's death and continued through the early Republican period. The result is the most heavily-annotated of all the late-Qīng casebook anthologies and a primary teaching text of the early Republican-era Shànghǎi medical schools.

### Abstract

For Liǔ Bǎo-yí's biographical background and the structure of the underlying selection see [[KR3ep021]]. The present `Zēng-bǔ píng-zhù` ("supplemented and critically annotated") edition reflects the post-1904 reception of Liǔ's anthology in the early-Republican Shànghǎi medical-school world. The composition window 1904–1920 reflects the first printing of Liǔ's original anthology by his disciples (1904) and the principal supplementing-and-annotating phase of the early Republic. The work is closely associated with the Shànghǎi Tóng-jì 同濟 and Shànghǎi Zhōng-yī Zhuān-mén Xué-xiào 上海中醫專門學校 curricula of the 1910s–1920s.

The annotations in the supplemented edition draw heavily on the Méng-hé clinical synthesis as it had matured by the early twentieth century, with extensive cross-references to the *Lín-zhèng zhǐ-nán yī-àn* [[KR3ep010]] tradition, the *Wáng-shì yī-àn* [[KR3ep083]] series of Wáng Mèng-yīng = [[王士雄], and the *Zhāng Yù-qīng yī-àn* [[KR3ep006]]. The result is one of the most thoroughly cross-referenced of all Chinese-medicine casebook anthologies and a key source for the late-Qīng / early-Republican consolidation of the Jiāng-nán medical canon.
