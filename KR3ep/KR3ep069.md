# Cáo Rénbó yīàn 曹仁伯醫案
*Medical Case Records of Cáo Rén-bó*
by [[曹存心]] Cáo Cúnxīn (*zì* Rénbó 仁伯, 1767–1834), of Chángshú 常熟.

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* selection from the case-records of Cáo Cúnxīn 曹存心 — the leading Sūzhōu / Chángshú physician of the late-Qiánlóng to Jiāqìng period — arranged by syndrome, opening with 風溫 (wind-warmth). This is a separate, compact selection from the larger casebooks already represented in the KR3ep series at [[KR3ep025]] *Cáo Rénbó yīàn lùn* and [[KR3ep095]] *Yánlíng dìzǐ jìyào*.

### Abstract

Cáo Cúnxīn 曹存心 (Rénbó, 1767–1834), of Chángshú 常熟 in the Sūzhōu prefecture, was the most influential Sūzhōu-school physician of the late-Qiánlóng / Jiāqìng generation. He held the rank of imperially-summoned court physician and operated a major teaching clinic at Chángshú from which the so-called *Yánlíng* (延陵) lineage of Sūzhōu *yīxué* spread across the lower Yangtze. His clinical signature integrates Yè Tiānshì's *wēnbìng* doctrine with strict *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* prescription-rigour and a robust *biànzhèng* (syndrome-discrimination) methodology.

The present selection opens with a six-day *fēngwēn* case in which Cáo successively deploys *Gégēn jiějī tāng*, *Huánglián jiědú tāng*, and *Xījiǎo dìhuáng tāng* across three clinical visits as the disease moves from surface through *yángmíng* into the *yíng* layer — a textbook demonstration of his successive-layer (*wèiqìyíngxuè*) prescribing methodology and an excellent teaching specimen.

The composition window 1790–1834 brackets Cáo's mature clinical career; the present compilation appears to be a mid-nineteenth-century editorial selection from his larger surviving manuscripts.
