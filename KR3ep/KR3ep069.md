# Cáo Rén-bó yī-àn 曹仁伯醫案
*Medical Case Records of Cáo Rén-bó*
by [[曹存心]] Cáo Cún-xīn (*zì* Rén-bó 仁伯, 1767–1834), of Cháng-shú 常熟.

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* selection from the case-records of Cáo Cún-xīn 曹存心 — the leading Sūzhōu / Cháng-shú physician of the late-Qiánlóng to Jiā-qìng period — arranged by syndrome, opening with 風溫 (wind-warmth). This is a separate, compact selection from the larger casebooks already represented in the KR3ep series at [[KR3ep025]] *Cáo Rén-bó yī-àn lùn* and [[KR3ep095]] *Yán-líng dì-zǐ jì-yào*.

### Abstract

Cáo Cún-xīn 曹存心 (Rén-bó, 1767–1834), of Cháng-shú 常熟 in the Sūzhōu prefecture, was the most influential Sūzhōu-school physician of the late-Qiánlóng / Jiā-qìng generation. He held the rank of imperially-summoned court physician and operated a major teaching clinic at Cháng-shú from which the so-called *Yán-líng* (延陵) lineage of Sūzhōu *yī-xué* spread across the lower Yangtze. His clinical signature integrates Yè Tiān-shì's *wēn-bìng* doctrine with strict *Shāng-hán* / *Jīn-guì* prescription-rigour and a robust *biàn-zhèng* (syndrome-discrimination) methodology.

The present selection opens with a six-day *fēng-wēn* case in which Cáo successively deploys *Gé-gēn jiě-jī tāng*, *Huáng-lián jiě-dú tāng*, and *Xī-jiǎo dì-huáng tāng* across three clinical visits as the disease moves from surface through *yáng-míng* into the *yíng* layer — a textbook demonstration of his successive-layer (*wèi-qì-yíng-xuè*) prescribing methodology and an excellent teaching specimen.

The composition window 1790–1834 brackets Cáo's mature clinical career; the present compilation appears to be a mid-nineteenth-century editorial selection from his larger surviving manuscripts.
