# Miù Sōng-xīn yī-àn 繆松心醫案
*Medical Case Records of Miù Sōng-xīn*
by [[繆遵義]] Miù Zūn-yì (Sōng-xīn 松心, 1710–1793), of Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* casebook of the mid-Qīng Sūzhōu physician Miù Zūn-yì (*hào* Sōng-xīn 松心), organised by syndrome. The text overlaps in clinical signature and patient-source-area with the closely related compilation [[KR3ep075]] *Sōng-xīn yī-àn bǐ-jì* and may represent an independent transcription of overlapping case-material.

### Abstract

Miù Zūn-yì 繆遵義 (CBDB 91312, 1710–1793), *hào* Sōng-xīn 松心 (also *Tiān-shū* 天樞 in some sources), of Wú-xiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). A *Qián-lóng yǐ-chǒu* (1745) *jìn-shì*; held minor official posts before turning to medicine in middle age. He was a contemporary of Yè Tiān-shì in Sūzhōu and is sometimes grouped with the other major Sūzhōu late-Yè-school masters.

The cases proceed through 喘 (gasping respiration), 不寐 (insomnia), and other internal-medicine categories. The prescribing signature — *Dàn-cài / Bā-xìng / Kǎn-qì / Mài-dōng / Chuān-bèi*; *Bái-shā fāng* (white-sand formula of *Hǎi-gé-ké*, *Bái fú-shí*, *Chán-tuì*, *Jì-yú-dǎn* with silver) — is highly distinctive and identifies Miù as a meticulous practitioner of the Sūzhōu Yè-school light-and-precise prescribing style. The opening *Chuǎn* case explicitly invokes the *Nèi-jīng* yáng-qiáo / *bù-mèi* (yáng-vessel insomnia) doctrine — a textbook display of canonical-doctrinal anchoring of Yè-school clinical practice.

The composition window 1740–1793 reflects Miù's mature clinical decades through to his death.
