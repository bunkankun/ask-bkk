# Sōng-xīn yī-àn bǐ-jì 松心醫案筆記
*Notebook of Sōng-xīn's Medical Case Records*
by [[繆遵義]] Miù Zūn-yì (Sōng-xīn 松心, 1710–1793), as transcribed by [[董受芝]] Dǒng Shòu-zhī (Republican period).

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* manuscript transcription of further cases of [[繆遵義]] Miù Sōng-xīn (1710–1793), recovered by the Jiā-xīng 嘉禾 physician **Dǒng Shòu-zhī** 董受芝 from a manuscript he obtained "from a Sūzhōu book-dealer," and prepared for publication with a preface by **Zhāng Hé-fēn** 張龢芬 of Cí-xī 慈溪 dated *guǐ-chǒu dōng-rì* (1913, winter).

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` carries a preface signed *Cí-xī Zhāng Hé-fēn shū yú Hǎi-* (the rest cut off in the preview) dated *guǐ-chǒu dōng-rì* (1913). The preface frames the work in the medical-historiographical lineage of *Gǔ-jīn yī-àn* (Yú Zhèn), *Míng-yī lèi-àn* (Jiāng Guàn), *Yè-shì zhǐ-nán* (Yè Tiān-shì), *Wú-jí Sān-jiā yī-àn* (Wú Jīn-shòu's compilation = [[KR3ep020]]), *Wú-mén zhì-yàn lù*, and the works of Wáng Shì-xióng of Qián-zhāi 潛齋 and Liǔ Bǎo-yí 柳寶詒 of Jiāng-yīn 江陰 ([[KR3ep021]], [[KR3ep022]]). Zhāng praises the recovered Miù manuscript as a "secret formula of the dragon palace" 龍宮秘方, with discussion as penetrating as Yù Jiā-yán's and clinical agility as alive as Yè Tiān-shì's, and notes that "the *àn* (cases) mention 'my own clan brother *Zhòng-chún* 仲醇'," confirming the author's surname as **Miù** 繆 — the same family as the Míng physician Miù Xī-yōng 繆希雍 (字 Zhòng-chún 仲醇, 1546–1627).

### Abstract

The text is a Republican-period salvage publication of mid-Qīng case-material — Miù Zūn-yì's clinical practice (c. 1740–1793) finally reaching print in 1913 through Dǒng Shòu-zhī's manuscript acquisition. The composition window 1740–1913 brackets the original clinical period through the Republican-period editorial assembly.

Together with [[KR3ep073]] *Miù Sōng-xīn yī-àn*, the present work constitutes the principal documentary record of Miù Zūn-yì's clinical practice. The two texts overlap in some cases but each preserves material not found in the other; collation of the two yields the most complete Miù-Sōng-xīn casebook.
