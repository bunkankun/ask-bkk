# Shào-shì fāng-àn 邵氏方案
*Prescriptions and Cases of the Shào Family*
Anonymous compiler (Shào-family physician).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* late-Qīng casebook of an unnamed member of the Shào 邵 medical family, transmitted without preface in the hxwd source. The cases are arranged in *Lǐ* 禮 / *Yuè* 樂 (and presumably *Shè* 射 / *Yù* 御) sub-sections following the *liù-yì* 六藝 ordering — a literary studio-organisation. The first section opens with 虛勞 (consumptive depletion) and proceeds through 表虛 (surface-deficiency / spontaneous sweating).

### Prefaces

*No transmitted preface in the hxwd source.* The `_000.txt` is absent; the text opens directly into the case-material as 卷之禮一、虛勞.

### Abstract

The Shào 邵 surname is associated with several late-Qīng medical families in Jiāng-nán; the *fāng-àn* in this compilation cannot be confidently attributed without additional internal evidence. The clinical signature — light *Yù-píng-fēng* (*Jade-Screen*) for surface-deficiency, *Gù-běn tāng* (*Fortify-the-Root* decoction) for *Sān-yīn jiāo-kuī* (triple-yin co-deficiency), and graded use of *yáng-shēn / ē-jiāo / chuān-bèi-mǔ / bǎi-hé* for consumptive *qiè* 怯 — places the text within the late-Qīng Yè-school *xū-láo* (consumptive depletion) treatment tradition. Decimal-precise dosing in *qián* and *fēn* and the unusual presence of *Xiyáng-shēn / Lì-shēn* (Western and Korean ginsengs) as alternatives suggest a late-Qīng to early-Republican composition window in a Jiāng-Zhè urban context.

There are at least three other "Shào" casebooks in the KR3ep series — [[KR3ep004]] *Shào Lán-sūn yī-àn*, [[KR3ep034]] *Shào-shì yī-àn* (Shào Lán-shēng), [[KR3ep057]] (the present text) — none of which appears to be by the same individual. The present text is most likely from the Shào family of Shào-xīng 紹興 / Hú-zhōu 湖州 medical lineage, but cannot be securely attributed.
