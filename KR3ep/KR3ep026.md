# Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng yī-àn 張畹香醫案
*Medical Case Records of Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng*
by [[張畹香]] Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng 張畹香 (late-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician).

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* clinical casebook of the late-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng. The hxwd directory for `KR3ep026` contains only two files (`_000.txt` and `_001.txt`); the `_000.txt` is essentially empty / header-only, with the substantive text in `_001.txt`. The work is conventionally dated to the mid-to-late nineteenth century by internal stylistic markers and by the catalog meta's dynasty assignment (清); biographical data on the author are thin.

### Abstract

Zhāng Wǎn-xiāng 張畹香 (CBDB 569619 records the name without dates) is a late-Qīng Jiāng-nán physician about whom little is known beyond the surviving casebook. He is identified in some modern reference works as Zhāng Hùn-zhāi 張混齋 of Shàoxīng 紹興, active in the mid-to-late nineteenth century — possibly within the Shàoxīng warm-disease tradition (compare [[KR3ep004]] Shào Lán-sūn and the Shàoxīng *wēn-bìng bā-jiā* 紹興溫病八家 grouping). The exact identification is not secure. The composition window 1850–1900 reflects the conventional placement of his floruit in the mid-to-late Qīng. The work is transmitted only in late-Qīng manuscript copies and was repatriated through the hxwd project.

The clinical material is conservative-classical in style, with case-presentations using the *Shāng-hán lùn* prescription repertoire and the Sūzhōu *wēn-bìng* light-dispersing aesthetic. As a Shàoxīng tradition document the work parallels [[KR3ep004]] *Shào Lán-sūn yī-àn* without belonging to the same precise editorial line.
