# Sān-yì dòng-jī 三易洞璣
*Penetrating-Pivot of the Three *Yì*-Traditions*
by [[黃道周]] (Huáng Dàozhōu, 1585–1646, 明, *zhuàn* 撰)

## About the work

Huáng Dàozhōu's distinctive 16-juan cosmological-numerological-astronomical synthesis treating the *Yìjīng* tradition as a system for measuring the heavens (the *jī-héng* 璣衡 = armillary sphere serving as the metaphor for the work's title). The "Three Yì" of the title are the *Yì* of Fú-xī (the *xiān-tiān* 先天 / Pre-Heaven tradition), the *Yì* of King Wén (the canonical *Zhōu Yì*), and the *Yì* of Confucius (the *Yì*-with-the-*Shí-yì* 十翼 commentary appendices).

Structure (per the 提要):

- ***Mì-tú jīng-wěi*** 宓圖經緯 (3 *juàn*) — the Fú-xī *xiān-tiān* arrangement (the Chén-Shào tradition's diagrammatic system)
- ***Wén-tú jīng-wěi*** 文圖經緯 (3 *juàn*) — the King-Wén *Zhōu Yì* hexagram-sequence
- ***Kǒng-tú jīng-wěi*** 孔圖經緯 (3 *juàn*) — the Confucius *Shuō-guà*'s "*Heaven-and-Earth set in their positions*" trigram-arrangement
- ***Zá-tú jīng-wěi*** 雜圖經緯 (3 *juàn*) — the *Zá-guà zhuàn* (Mixed-Hexagrams Treatise) arrangement
- ***Yú-tú jīng-wěi*** 餘圖經緯 (1 *juàn*) — derived from the *Zhōu-guān Tài-bǔ* (Zhōulǐ Grand-Diviner) reaching to the *Six-Dreams* divination, the *Ten-Effulgences* divination, and the *Qí-mén Tài-yī* astrological systems
- ***Zhēn-tú jīng-wěi*** 貞圖經緯 (3 *juàn*) — corresponding to the new diagrams, with *héng* (level), *yǐ* (standing), and *huán* (encircling) variations

Huáng Dàozhōu's intellectual project, per his own self-statement: integrating astronomy-and-calendrics-and-pitch-pipes-and-*Yì* into a single unified cosmological synthesis — "*the heaven-and-earth-and-man's images-and-numbers all are present in the* Yì*; spreading [them] out makes the calendar; arranging [them] makes pitch-pipes; uniting [them] makes the* Yì*. Removing the *Yì*'s diagrams and yarrow-stalks, distinguishing its substance and emptiness, makes the *Chūn-qiū* and the *Shī**". The work is thus a comprehensive cosmological-historical-philosophical system, modeled on the *Tài-xuán*-tradition's encompassing-ambition.

The Sìkù 提要 articulates the work's intellectual lineage: it builds on Zhèng Xuán's 鄭玄 Eastern-Hàn *Yì*-with-stellar-asterism interpretation; develops Jīng Fáng's *guà-qì zhí-rì* 卦氣值日 (hexagram-pneuma duty-day) tradition; extends Yī Xíng's 一行 *tuī-cè dìng-lì* 推策定厯 (computation-determining-the-calendar) tradition. Through Huáng Dàozhōu, this cosmological synthesis is brought to its late-Míng / pre-Western-impact culmination — a remarkable synthesis just before the Jesuit-mediated mathematical-astronomical revolution would transform Chinese cosmology entirely.

The 提要's verdict is balanced: "*[the work] sometimes flows into the divinatory and falls into the mixed-and-irregular; however the *Yì*-Way is broad-and-great, neither obstructed in number nor restricted to one corner — but also not omitting any one corner. Pulling-and-pushing it horizontally and vertically, each [angle] has its principle*". The work is preserved as "*one school of learning*" rather than as canonical *Yì*-exposition.

## Other points of interest

The 提要's preservation of the prophetic-anecdote (Huáng Dàozhōu's notebook predicting his own death) is a striking moment of late-Qīng editorial recognition that the work's *substantive* claims about cosmological-divinatory penetration may have been honored, even if its formal content is "*not the canonical correct meaning*". The editors' decision to preserve the work in the *shù-shù* division while withholding it from the canonical *Jīng-bù Yì* division is one of the more interesting *Sìkù* boundary-decisions.
