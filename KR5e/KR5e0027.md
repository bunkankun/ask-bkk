# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo sān dòng fèng dào kē jiè yíng shǐ 洞玄靈寶三洞奉道科戒營始

*Three-Cavern Initiation of Regulations, Precepts, and Establishment for Venerating the Way*

attributed to [[金明七真]] (Jīn míng qī zhēn); early Táng

A major early-Táng **regulatory-liturgical compendium** codifying the norms, precepts, and ritual establishment of Daoist monasticism across the Three Caverns. Fundamental source for reconstructing Táng Daoist institutional practice. Preserved as **DZ 1125** / CT 1125 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Early Táng. Dynasty: 唐初期.
