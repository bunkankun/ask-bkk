# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo zuǒ xuán lùn 洞玄靈寶左玄論

*Dòngxuán Língbǎo Discourse of the Left-Mystery*

Anonymous; 7th century or earlier

An early-Táng Líng bǎo 靈寶 **philosophical treatise** on the "left mystery" (*zuǒ xuán* 左玄). Preserved as **DZ 1136** / CT 1136 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 7th century or earlier. Dynasty: 唐.
