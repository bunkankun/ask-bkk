# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo běn xíng sù yuán jīng 太上洞玄靈寶本行宿緣經

*Most High Dòng-xuán Líng-bǎo Scripture of Original Practice and Karmic Prior-Causes*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Líng bǎo 靈寶 scripture on **karmic causation** (*sù yuán* 宿緣, "prior causes") and the original cultivation (*běn xíng* 本行) of the Daoist adept — reflecting the Líng bǎo absorption of Buddhist karmic vocabulary. Preserved as **DZ 1114** / CT 1114 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
