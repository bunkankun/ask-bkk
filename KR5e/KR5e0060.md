# Chóng yáng zhēn rén shòu Dān yáng èr shí sì jué 重陽真人授丹陽二十四訣

*Twenty-Four Instructions Transmitted by the Perfected Chóngyáng to Dānyáng*

Anonymous; 12th century

A collection of **twenty-four instructions** transmitted by Wáng Chóngyáng to his senior disciple Mǎ Dānyáng. Preserved as **DZ 1158** / CT 1158 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 12th century. Dynasty: 金.
