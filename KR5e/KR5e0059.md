# Mǎ Zìrán jīn dān kǒu jué 馬自然金丹口訣

*Oral Instructions on the Gold Elixir by Mǎ Zìrán*

attributed to [[馬自然]] (Mǎ Zìrán) — Northern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

An *nèi dān* oral-instruction text attributed to Mǎ Zìrán (distinct from the Quán zhēn Mǎ Yù). Preserved as **DZ 1157** / CT 1157 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Northern Sòng (probably). Dynasty: 北宋.
