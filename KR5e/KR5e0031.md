# Dào jiào yì shū 道教義樞

*Pivot of Daoist Doctrinal Meanings*

by [[孟安排]] (Mèng Ānpái) — late 7th-century Táng Daoist scholar

**One of the major Táng systematic doctrinal encyclopedias of Daoism** — providing authoritative definitions for the categories and concepts of Daoist teaching. Mèng's work reorganizes material from the earlier *Xuán mén dà yì* ([[KR5e0026]]) into a systematic 10-*juàn* reference. An indispensable tool for understanding Táng Daoist theological vocabulary. Preserved as **DZ 1129** / CT 1129 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Late 7th / early 8th century. Dynasty: 唐.
