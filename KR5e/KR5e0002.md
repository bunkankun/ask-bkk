# Tài píng jīng 太平經

*The Scripture of Great Peace*

Anonymous; Eastern Hàn core, Six-Dynasties expansion

**One of the foundational scriptures of Chinese Daoism** — the cornerstone text of the earliest organized Daoist movement, traditionally associated with the Yellow Turban / Five-Pecks-of-Rice context of the late Eastern Hàn. The ***Tài píng jīng*** is extensively quoted in the *Hòu Hàn shū* as a source of the revolutionary Tài píng dào 太平道 movement of Zhāng Jué 張角 (184 CE Yellow Turban rebellion). Later re-edited and expanded in the Six Dynasties.

## Abstract

The received text is defective — the transmitted corpus covers only 57 of an original 170 *juàn*. The three items DZ 1101 a/b/c provide:

- The **Táng epitome** ([[KR5e0001]] *Tài píng jīng chāo*, 10 *juàn*) — preserving the scripture's doctrinal content in condensed form.
- The **main surviving text** (this entry, [[KR5e0002]], 57 *juàn*) — fragmentary remains of the full scripture.
- The **preface** ([[KR5e0003]] *Tài píng jīng fù wén xù*) — an introduction.

The scripture's doctrinal core concerns **Great Peace** (*tài píng* 太平) — a utopian millennial social-cosmic order under a sage-ruler, aided by scriptural revelations that diagnose the world's illness and prescribe corrective moral and ritual action. Central concepts include *chéng fù* 承負 (inherited moral-cosmic burden across generations), *sān qì* 三氣 (the three cosmic pneumas that must be harmonized), and a prototype of Daoist ritual confession and self-cultivation.

**Dating.** Core Eastern Hàn (2nd century); extensive Six-Dynasties revision. Dynasty: 東漢-六朝.
