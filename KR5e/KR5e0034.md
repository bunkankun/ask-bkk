# Shàng qīng dào lèi shì xiāng 上清道類事相

*Shàng-qīng Classified Realia of the Way*

by [[王懸河]] (Wáng Xuánhé) — Táng Shàng qīng scholar

A Táng **Shàng qīng 上清 topical reference work** — one of Wáng Xuánhé's systematic classifications of Shàng qīng material by topic. Preserved as **DZ 1132** / CT 1132 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Late 7th / early 8th century. Dynasty: 唐.
