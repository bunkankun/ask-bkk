# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo sān shí èr tiān zūn yìng hào jīng 太上洞玄靈寶三十二天尊應號經

*Most High Dòng-xuán Líng-bǎo Scripture on the Responsive-Titles of the Thirty-Two Heaven-Worthies*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Líng bǎo 靈寶 scripture on the **thirty-two Heaven-Worthies** (*sān shí èr tiān zūn* 三十二天尊) of the Daoist cosmology of thirty-two heavens. Preserved as **DZ 1121** / CT 1121 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
