# Chóng yáng fēn lí shí huà jí 重陽分梨十化集

*Chóng-yáng's Ten-Conversions through the Divided-Pear*

by [[王嚞]] (Wáng Chóngyáng, 1113–1170)

A collection centered on Wáng Chóngyáng's celebrated "divided-pear" (*fēn lí* 分梨) conversion-episode with Mǎ Dānyáng and Sūn Bù'èr, narrating ten successive teachings. Preface 1183. Preserved as **DZ 1155** / CT 1155 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Preface 1183. Dynasty: 金.
