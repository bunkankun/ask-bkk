# Dào mén jīng fǎ xiāng chéng cì xù 道門經法相承次序

*Mutually-Transmitted Sequence of Scriptures and Dharma in the Gate of the Way*

by [[潘師正]] (Pān Shīzhèng, 586–684) — Eleventh Shàng qīng Patriarch

Pān Shīzhèng's systematic **ordering of the Daoist scriptural transmission** — essential source for reconstructing the canonical order and lineage of Shàng qīng and related scriptures in the 7th century. Preface preserves his famous exchange with Emperor Táng Gāo zōng. Preserved as **DZ 1128** / CT 1128 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** *c.* 680. Dynasty: 唐.
