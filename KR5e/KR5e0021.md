# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo xuán yī zhēn rén shuō shēng sǐ lún zhuǎn yīn yuán jīng 洞玄靈寶玄一真人說生死輪轉因緣經

*Dòng-xuán Líng-bǎo Scripture of the Mysterious-One Perfected on the Cause-Conditions of Birth-and-Death Transmigration*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Líng bǎo scripture explicitly adopting the Buddhist concept of **transmigration** (*lún zhuǎn* 輪轉) — one of the clearest loci of Daoist-Buddhist doctrinal absorption. Preserved as **DZ 1119** / CT 1119 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
