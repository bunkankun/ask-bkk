# Sān dòng zhū náng 三洞珠囊

*Pearl-Sack of the Three Caverns*

by [[王懸河]] (Wáng Xuánhé) — Táng Shàng qīng scholar

A **Táng Daoist encyclopedia** compiled by Wáng Xuánhé, collecting material from the Three Caverns of the Daoist canon organized by topic. A major primary source for Six-Dynasties to early-Táng Daoist material. Preserved as **DZ 1139** / CT 1139 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** *c.* 680. Dynasty: 唐.
