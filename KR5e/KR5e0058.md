# Chóng yáng zhēn rén jīn guān yù suǒ jué 重陽真人金關玉鎖訣

*Perfected Chóng-yáng's Instructions on the Gold-Barrier and Jade-Lock*

by [[王嚞]] (Wáng Chóngyáng, 1113–1170)

**Wáng Chóngyáng's principal instructional text on inner-alchemical practice** — the gold-barrier (*jīn guān* 金關) and jade-lock (*yù suǒ* 玉鎖) are physiological landmarks in the Quán zhēn inner-alchemical body. Preserved as **DZ 1156** / CT 1156 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Within Wáng's active life. Dynasty: 金.

## Translations and research
