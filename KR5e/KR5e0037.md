# Tài píng liǎng tóng shū 太平兩同書

*Book of the Great Peace Dual Concord*

by [[羅隱]] (Luó Yǐn, 833–910) — late-Táng poet-essayist

A late-Táng political-philosophical treatise on *tài píng* 太平 (Great Peace), organized by paired categories of Confucian and Daoist thought. Preserved as **DZ 1135** / CT 1135 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Late Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
