# Tài shàng Líng bǎo shǒu rù Jìng míng sì guī míng jiàn jīng 太上靈寶首入淨明四規明鑑經

*Most High Líng-bǎo Illumining-Mirror Scripture on the Four Rules for First Entering Pure Illumination*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Jìng míng 淨明 initiation-scripture on the "four rules" (*sì guī* 四規) for first entering the Jìng míng path. Preserved as **DZ 1107** / CT 1107 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
