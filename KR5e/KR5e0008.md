# Tài shàng Líng bǎo Jìng míng tiān zūn shuō yù wēn jīng 太上靈寶淨明天尊說禦瘟經

*Most High Líng-bǎo Pure-Illumination Heaven-Worthy's Scripture on Warding-off Plague*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Jìng míng 淨明 **anti-epidemic scripture** — a genre of Daoist texts invoking deities (here, the Pure Illumination Heaven-Worthy) to ward off plague (*wēn* 瘟). Preserved as **DZ 1106** / CT 1106 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
