# Tài shàng Líng bǎo Jìng míng jiǔ xiān shuǐ jīng 太上靈寶淨明九仙水經

*Most High Líng-bǎo Pure-Illumination Water Scripture of the Nine Immortals*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Jìng míng 淨明 scripture on the "water of the nine immortals" (*jiǔ xiān shuǐ* 九仙水). Preserved as **DZ 1108** / CT 1108 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
