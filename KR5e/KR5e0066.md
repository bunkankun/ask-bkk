# Jí jiù xiān fāng 急救仙方

*Immortals' Recipes for Emergency Relief*

Anonymous; early Míng

An early-Míng **Daoist medical-recipe collection** for emergency situations, 11 *juàn*. Compiled for inclusion in the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng, 1444–1445. Preserved as **DZ 1164** / CT 1164 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 1444–1445. Dynasty: 明.
