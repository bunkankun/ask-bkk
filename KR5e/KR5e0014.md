# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo bā xiān wáng jiào jiè jīng 太上洞玄靈寶八仙王教誡經

*Most High Dòngxuán Língbǎo Scripture of Instructions from the Eight Immortal-Kings*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Líng bǎo 靈寶 scripture of moral instructions (*jiào jiè* 教誡) from the **Eight Immortal-Kings** (*bā xiān wáng* 八仙王). Preserved as **DZ 1112** / CT 1112 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
