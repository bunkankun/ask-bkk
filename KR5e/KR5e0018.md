# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo tài shàng zhēn rén wèn jí jīng 洞玄靈寶太上真人問疾經

*Dòng-xuán Líng-bǎo Scripture of the Most High Perfected Inquiring-after-Illness*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Líng bǎo 靈寶 scripture — structured as a dialogue on illness and its religious treatment, echoing the Buddhist *Vimalakīrti-nirdeśa* pattern. Preserved as **DZ 1116** / CT 1116 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
