# Shàng fāng jūn tiān yǎn fàn zhēn jīng 上方鈞天演範真經

*Perfect Scripture of the Higher-Realm Jūn-tiān Expanding-Norms*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A Daoist scripture on the "Jūn-tiān" (one of the nine heavens) expounding cosmic norms. Preserved as **DZ 1134** / CT 1134 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
