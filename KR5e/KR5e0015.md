# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo guó wáng xíng dào jīng 太上洞玄靈寶國王行道經

*Most High Dòng-xuán Líng-bǎo Scripture on the State-Sovereign Practising the Way*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Líng bǎo 靈寶 scripture on the **state-sovereign (*guó wáng* 國王) practising the Way** — instructions for the ruler. Preserved as **DZ 1113** / CT 1113 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
