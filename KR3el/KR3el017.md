# Shāng-kē fāng-shū 傷科方書
*Prescription-Treatise on Traumatology*
by [[江考卿]] Jiāng Kǎo-qīng (*zì* Guó-xīng 國興, *hào* Ruì-píng 瑞屏, 清).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* parallel recension of [[KR3el007]] *Jiāng-shì shāng-kē xué* 江氏傷科學, both transmitted under the abbreviated alternate title *Shāng-kē fāng-shū* 傷科方書 (the full long title is 江氏傷科方書, "Jiāng family prescription-treatise on traumatology"). The author is **Jiāng Kǎo-qīng** 江考卿 (*zì* Guó-xīng 國興, *hào* Ruì-píng 瑞屏, alt. name 江祥; b. ca. 1770), a Wù-yuán 婺源 (then Huī-zhōu prefecture, now Jiāngxī) traumatology specialist of Qīng-huá 清華 township, who completed his manuscript in *Dào-guāng* 20 (1840) at age seventy *suì*. The manuscript circulated only in hand-copy during the author's lifetime and was first formally printed in 1924 by Qiū Jì-shēng 裘吉生 in the *Sān-sān yī-bào* 三三醫報 journal. The hxwd-recension of [[KR3el017]] (30 kB) is slightly longer than the [[KR3el007]] recension (28 kB) and may preserve a marginally fuller witness of the original manuscript.

### Prefaces

No preface is preserved in the hxwd recension. The text opens directly with the *Duàn-sǐ zhèng mì-jué* 斷死證秘訣 doggerel — the same verse that opens [[KR3el007]].

### Abstract

This entry is a parallel-recension companion to [[KR3el007]]; for the full bibliographic and content discussion see [[KR3el007]]. The two recensions transmit essentially the same work; minor variations of orthography and order do not amount to substantive textual divergence. The two parallel KR-entries reflect the Kanripo corpus's policy of including both surviving recensions of the same work under separate ids.

Both recensions open with the verse *Duàn-sǐ zhèng mì-jué* 斷死證秘訣 (the "secret formula for diagnosing mortal injuries": *jīn-shāng shēn-sǔn yǎn-pí qīng, dìng zhǔ shēn-wáng nán-jiù mìng* — "if metal-wound or bodily-damage shows green/blue eyelid, certainly the patient is dying and the life cannot be saved"; *rén-zhōng zhòu-mǎn chún yòu qīng, sān rì xū-zhī mìng bì-qīng* — "if the *rén-zhōng* area is fully swollen and the lips are green, know that within three days life will be lost"). The fifteen-item *Mì-shòu bù-zhì fǎ* 秘受不治法 list of untreatable conditions follows in identical wording to [[KR3el007]]. The body of the text contains the same numbered-prescription protocol (no. 20 *Hēi-lóng sǎn*; no. 14 *Táo-huā sǎn*; no. 24 *Zhǐ-xuè sǎn*; no. 18 *Tiē-gāo*; no. 6 *Jiē-gǔ dān*; no. 26 *Hēi-lóng sǎn* alternate). The closing materia-medica appendix on the *Jiē-dǎ sǔn-shāng gāo* 跌打損傷膏 plaster formula and the *Mì-zhì zhū-shā gāo* 秘製硃砂膏 cinnabar plaster is identical between the two recensions.

In light of this textual identity, the present entry adopts the same composition date (*Dào-guāng* 20 = 1840) as [[KR3el007]] and the same author attribution.
