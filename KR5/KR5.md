# KR5 道藏

KR5: Daoist Canon division.

- [[KR5a]] 洞真部 *Dòngzhēn bù* — Daoist Canon — Cavern of Perfection. Shangqing and related scriptures, revelations, hagiography, and early Daoist doctrinal materials. 332 texts.
- [[KR5b]] 洞玄部 *Dòngxuán bù* — Daoist Canon — Cavern of Mystery. Lingbao scriptures, ritual texts, cosmology, and associated Daoist canonical works. 324 texts.
- [[KR5c]] 洞神部 *Dòngshén bù* — Daoist Canon — Cavern of Divinity. Three Caverns materials, ritual manuals, talismans, and divinity-oriented texts. 392 texts.
- [[KR5d]] 太玄部 *Tàixuán bù* — Daoist Canon — Great Mystery. The first of the Four Supplements, with mixed scriptures, ritual, and doctrinal works. 123 texts.
- [[KR5e]] 太平部 *Tàipíng bù* — Daoist Canon — Great Peace. Great Peace texts and related canonical materials from the supplemental Daoist canon. 68 texts.
- [[KR5f]] 太清部 *Tàiqīng bù* — Daoist Canon — Great Clarity Section. External alchemy, ritual, and Great Clarity texts. 25 texts.
- [[KR5g]] 正一部 *Zhèngyī bù* — Daoist Canon — Orthodox Unity. Zhengyi liturgical, ritual, register, and Celestial Master materials. 244 texts.
- [[KR5h]] 續道藏 *Xù Dàozàng* — Masters — Wànlì Supplement to the Daoist Canon. The 1607 supplement to the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng*. 57 texts.
- [[KR5i]] 道藏輯要 *Dàozàng jíyào* — Masters — Essentials of the Daoist Canon. Qing-era abridgment and expansion of the Daoist canon. 109 texts.
