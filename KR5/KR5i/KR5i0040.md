# Tóng fú tiě quàn 銅符鐵券

*The Bronze Tally and Iron Charter*

compiled by [[彭好古]] (Péng Hàogǔ, *hào* 一壑居士 Yīhè jūshì), dated **己亥仲夏 = mid-summer 1599**

A late-Wàn-lì-era compendium of *fúzhuàn* 符篆 (talismanic seals) and *jīndān* operational formulas assembled by **Péng Hàogǔ** — the same Húběi inner-alchemy editor responsible for [[KR5i0029]] *Lónghǔ shàng jīng xuán jiě*. The work takes its title from the legend (preserved in the *Liè xiān zhuàn*) that **Lán Gōng** 蘭公 of Qūfù, in cultivating filial piety, was visited by an Imperial Star Perfected styling himself **Xiàotì wáng** 孝悌王, who descended to his house and gave him a *Jīn dān bǎo jīng* 金丹寶經, *Tóng fú* 銅符 (bronze tally), and *Tiě quàn* 鐵券 (iron charter) — to be transmitted onward to **Chénmǔ** Dānyáng-Huángtáng, and from her to the Perfected Lord Xǔ Xùn 許祖. Péng's compilation re-presents the legend's named scripture under the *Tóng fú tiě quàn* title and arranges it under the standard alchemical headings of *sān yuán yào wù huǒ hòu* 三元藥物火候 ("Three-Origin medicine-substance and fire-phasing").

### Prefaces

**Title-poem (Péng Hàogǔ, 己亥 mid-summer 1599 / *Yīhè jūshì*).** "Considering the *Liè xiān zhuàn*: Lángōng of Qūfù in his perfected cultivation of filial conduct stirred the *Dòu zhōng zhēn rén* (Dipper-Inner Perfected) to descend, calling himself the *Xiàotì wáng*. Coming down to his house, he gave him the *Jīndān bǎo jīng*, the *Tóng fú*, and the *Tiě quàn*, and ordered him to transmit them down to *Dān yáng huáng táng Chénmǔ*, who gave them to the Perfected Lord *Xǔzǔ*. — Now I view this book: it is laid out with *sān yuán yàowù huǒhòu*, every one provided, like a treasure-scripture for the seal-tally — though it has no *fúquàn* with it. — I have collected far afield the *fúzhuàn* numinous-texts and have spontaneously made it into volumes; I wished to add adjustments and corrections, but this book has been transmitted long, and I cannot wantonly disturb the immortal-scripture by my own will. So the book retains the *fúquàn* old name, and the *fúzhuàn* numinous-texts are placed afterward as a separate appendix to be referred to. — The various elixir-scriptures only speak the principle; they do not yet match this book's directly-pointing-to-truth-formula, with no even the slightest taboo. Whoever heart-comprehends, having gained the formula, can establish the *shéndān* on the spot. The formula — how easily can one speak it? Of those seeking the formula by heart-attainment, nine in ten get it; of those seeking by mouth-attainment, five in ten; of those seeking by paper-attainment, none. — I have collected this book truly seeking the formula in paper; but later there will be one who, by way of this, will catch the fish-and-rabbit, and then it may yet not but be his bamboo-trap and rabbit-snare. — *Jǐhài mid-summer*, *Yīhè jūshì titled.*"

### Abstract

A late-Wàn-lì compendium of inner-alchemical operational instructions framed under the legendary *Tóngfú tiěquàn* of Lán Gōng 蘭公 and Xǔ Xùn — i.e., located within the **Jìngmíng zhōngxiào dào** 淨明忠孝道 (Pure-Bright Loyalty-and-Filiality) tradition centred on Xǔ Xùn (cf. [[KR5i0041]] *Tài shàng líng bǎo jìng míng zōng jiào lù*). The collection's distinctive feature is its programmatic willingness to publish the operational formula in plain language, against the conventional alchemical rule of secrecy: Péng explicitly defends "the formula is sought by heart-attainment, nine in ten get it; by paper-attainment, none — yet I have collected it in paper, hoping that one student will catch the fish-and-rabbit and let go the bamboo-trap." Composition dated *jǐhài* mid-summer = **summer 1599**, the same season as Péng's preface to [[KR5i0029]]. The two are companion productions of his Húběi alchemical-editorial circle.

For Péng Hàogǔ's broader project see [[彭好古]].

## Other points of interest

The alignment with the *Jìngmíng zhōngxiào dào* of Xǔ Xùn is deliberate: Péng presents inner-alchemical practice as the technical complement to the Pure-Bright tradition's moral programme of *zhōngxiào* (loyalty-and-filiality), uniting *xìngmìng* cultivation with the family ethics of the Pure-Bright cult. This is a notable late-Míng synthesis.
