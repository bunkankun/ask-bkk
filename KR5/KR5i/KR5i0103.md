# Qīng chéng shān jì 青城山記

*Records of Mount Qīngchéng*

anonymous Sìchuān local-record / shānzhì gazetteer of *Qīngchéngshān* 青城山 — the Daoist sacred mountain of western Sìchuān, traditionally the place of Zhāng Dàolíng's first revelation in 142 CE

A two-juàn local-record / *shān zhì* 山志 of *Qīngchéngshān*, organised under the standard *shān zhì* topical headings: (juàn 1) *Yuán shǐ jì* (origins), *Sì zhì jì* (boundaries), *Zhū shān jì* (peaks), *Gōng guàn jì* (palaces and belvederes), *Gǔ jī jì* (ancient traces); (juàn 2) *Shì shí jì shàng* (events upper — *yǐn yì* / recluses) and *Shì shí jì xià* (events lower — *fāng jì* / arts and techniques), with *shí yí* (left-overs) and *yì shì* (anecdotes) sections. The work is the canonical Qīng-era *Qīngchéngshān* gazetteer and is the principal local-history source for the Daoist heritage of the mountain.

### Abstract

A standard *shān zhì* gazetteer of Qīngchéngshān in 2 juàn, anonymous, undated. Composition is c. 1700–1809; the work is incorporated into DZJY as part of the broader Sìchuān Daoist institutional-history materials (with [[KR5i0100]] *Èr xiān ān bēi jì* and [[KR5i0101]] *Qīng yáng gōng bēi jì*). Qīngchéngshān is one of the most important Daoist mountains of late-imperial China, traditionally the place of *Zhāng Dàolíng* (Tiānshī)'s 142-CE revelation; its gazetteer is a fundamental source for the cult-geography of southwestern Daoism.

For Qīngchéngshān in late-imperial Daoism see Olles, *Ritual Words* (2013); Esposito, *Facets of Qing Daoism*.
