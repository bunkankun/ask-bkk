# Lǚ dì wén jí 呂帝文集

*Collected Prose Writings of the Imperial Lord Lǚ*

planchette-attributed prose of [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòngbīn / Chúnyáng dìjūn / Fúyòu shàngdì) — the prose half of the *Lǚzǔ quán shū* compendium

A two-juàn collection of *fújī*-revealed prose writings attributed to Lǚ Dòngbīn — companion to the *Lǚdì shī jí* poetry collection at [[KR5i0050]] — assembled within the same Liú Tǐshù / Liǔ Shǒu-yuán-circle Lǚ-zǔ-cult editorial work that also produced [[KR5i0045]] *Lǚ zǔ běn zhuàn*. The contents include: *xù* (prefaces) — the *Dà dòng yù jīng xù*, *Yù huáng běn xíng jí jīng zhí jiě xù*, *Wénchāng dìjūn xiào jīng xù*, *Xuándì qī pǐn jīng xù*, *Xīhé sàzǔ jiùkǔ jīng xù*, etc. — i.e., spirit-written prefaces by Lǚzǔ to the various scriptures gathered in DZJY; *jì* (records) — *Zhōngnán dé dào jì*, *Jiāngzhōu tíng zìjì*, *Dōngyuè miào bēi jì*, etc.; *fù* (rhapsodies) — *Bā xiān yán fù*, *Qún xiān gāo huì fù*, *Nèi dān fù*, *Dēng xiān fù*; *lùn* (treatises) — *Rén dào lùn*, *Cān tóng lùn*, *Lì xíng gōng guò lùn*; and *zìxù* of his other works.

### Prefaces

The collection opens directly with the table of contents; no separate framing preface is preserved at the head, the *Wénchāng* and Liǔ Shǒuyuán prefaces operating at the level of the broader compendium.

### Abstract

The prose component of the mid-eighteenth-century **Lǚzǔ quán shū** compendium. The texts are spirit-written products of the Húběi / Sìchuān planchette network of the early-Qián-lóng era, c. 1700–1750. The prose collection is a useful repository: many of its component prefaces are the **same texts** that appear at the head of other DZJY scriptures — e.g., the *Yù huáng běn xíng jí jīng zhí jiě xù* corresponds to Lǚzǔ's preface preserved at [[KR5i0015]]; the *Wénchāng xiào jīng xù* matches the preface at [[KR5i0084]] etc. The collection is thus a useful internal index to Lǚzǔ's scripture-prefatorial corpus within DZJY.
