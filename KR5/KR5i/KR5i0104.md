# Tài shàng wú jí dà dào yán shòu jí fú xiāo jié bǎo chàn 太上無極大道延壽集福消劫寶懺

*Treasure-Repentance of the Most-High Limitless Great-Way for Extending Life, Gathering Fortune, and Dispelling Calamity*

anonymous Daoist liturgical *bǎo chàn* (treasure-repentance)

A liturgical repentance text addressed to the *Three-Pure境* (*Sān qīng jìng*), structured in the standard Daoist *kē yí* sequence: opening *zàn* (encomium), *kāi chàn zàn* (beginning-of-repentance encomium), *fú yǐ* (formal-statement), *qǐ shī* (initiating sequence), *xiàng shèng* (turning-toward-the-saints) — with the petitioner's *bāi sān qīng yìng huà tiān zūn / Jīn quē Yù huáng tiān zūn* etc. The text covers the standard themes of life-extension, fortune-gathering, and calamity-dispelling — the standard *yángshēng* trinity of Daoist devotion.

### Prefaces

The text bears no separate preface; it opens directly with the *kāi chàn zàn* (opening encomium): "*Reverently bow! / Three-Pure境's Limitless Three-境 Honoured-One: today I raise the orthodox Way and accept this slight sincerity. — Encomium for opening the repentance: the essence of the Tàijí, the numen of the Two-and-Five, *yīnyáng* combining-and-merging, naturally the Real comes-into-being; emerging-from-the-Mystery and entering-the-Pìn (female), wonderfully combined-and-condensed; *qiánkūn* once-set, the Two Forms shining-clear; mountain-river-grass-tree-man-thing all together generated, all possessing the Way; the Way is hard to forcibly name; Heaven-Earth's beginning-ancestor, the Three-境 Three-Pure…*"
