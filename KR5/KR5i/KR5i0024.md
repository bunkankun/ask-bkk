# Jiǔ huáng dòu mǔ jiè shā yán shēng zhēn jīng 九皇斗姥戒殺延生真經

*True Scripture of the Nine August Ones and the Mother of the Dipper, on the Forbidding of Killing and the Extending of Life*

Anonymous Qīng planchette scripture; Dòu-mǔ-cult liturgical text

A devotional-liturgical scripture on the Dòu mǔ ("Mother of the Dipper", = Mārīcī Daoised; cf. [[KR5i0023]]) and the Nine August Ones (九皇 — the nine stars of the Northern Dipper deified), centred on a moral imperative against the killing of life. The text opens with the standard preliminary purification spells (淨咒八則 — eight purifications: 淨天地, 淨八方, 淨壇, 淨身, 淨心, 淨口, 淨意, 淨業), each in 4-character pentastichs of liturgical verse, before proceeding to the scripture proper.

### Prefaces

No external preface; the text is a self-contained liturgical scripture without authorial signature.

### Abstract

A Qīng planchette-revealed scripture in the Dòumǔ / Nine-August cult, presenting an anti-slaughter doctrine 戒殺 in liturgical-recitation form. The opening *jìng zhòu* sequence is the standard front-matter for Daoist scripture-recitation; the substantive scripture combines a discourse on the Dòu mǔ's universal compassion with a directive on the karmic consequences of killing animals (the inherited *kalpa*-cycle of *jié* 劫 calamities, coordinated with the catalogue at [[KR5i0004]]).

The work belongs to the same DZJY Lóngmén planchette milieu as [[KR5i0023]] and is often bound with it as a paired Dòumǔ liturgy.
