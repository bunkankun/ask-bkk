# Sūn Bùèr yuán jūn chuán shù dān dào mì shū sān juàn 孫不二元君傳述丹道祕書三卷

*The Three-Juàn Secret Book of the Elixir-Way as Transmitted-and-Narrated by the Primal Lady Sūn Bùèr*

planchette-attributed to [[孫不二]] (Sūn Bùèr) and to **紫光天母** (Zǐguāng tiānmǔ, the Heavenly-Mother of Purple-Light)

A three-juàn pseudepigraphical Quánzhēn / *nǚdān* compendium, transmitting under Sūn Bùèr's name three distinct base scriptures: (i) **Yù qīng tāi yuán nèi yǎng zhēn jīng** 玉清胎元内養真經 (transmitted by *Bǎo shén yuán tái zhēn jūn* 寶神元臺真君); (ii) **Yù qīng wú shàng nèi jǐng zhēn jīng** 玉清無上内景真經 (transmitted by *Dà luó zhēn tiān yuán tiān dà shèng hòu Zǐ guāng tiān mǔ* 大羅真天元天大聖后紫光天母); (iii) a third revelation-scripture (the title less prominently displayed). The framing fiction is cosmological: in primordial time *Yuán shǐ shàng dì* 元始上帝 instructs the Tàishàng Lǎojūn on the cosmology of *jīngqìshén* (essence-pneuma-spirit) and on the salvation of the lower realm; the Tàishàng then transmits in turn to the Zǐguāng tiānmǔ; and Zǐguāng tiānmǔ in turn descends through Sūn Bùèr into the world.

### Prefaces

No external preface; the text begins directly with the *Yù qīng tāi yuán nèi yǎng zhēn jīng* opening and proceeds through the three scriptures.

### Abstract

A pseudepigraphical Qīng-era Quánzhēn / *nǚdān* compendium, paired with [[KR5i0063]] *Sūn Bùèr yuán jūn fǎ yǔ* — the two together comprising the principal *Sūn Bùèr* canonical pair. Composition is c. late Míng / early Qīng (1500–1750); the cosmology is that of the established Quánzhēn pantheon as it stood in the post-*Yún jí qī qiān* period.

The framing scripture's hierarchy of revelation — Yuánshǐ → Tàishàng → Zǐguāng tiānmǔ → Sūn Bùèr — is a standard late-imperial cosmological pedigree-claim, embedding the *nǚdān* tradition in the highest planchette-pantheon authority chain.
