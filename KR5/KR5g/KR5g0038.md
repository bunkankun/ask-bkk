# Quán zhēn zuò bō jié fǎ 全真坐鉢捷法

*Quánzhēn Swift Dharma for Sitting in the Almsbowl*

Anonymous; Yuán

A Yuán Quán zhēn **meditation-instruction text**. "Sitting in the almsbowl" (*zuò bō* 坐鉢) alludes to a specific Quán zhēn contemplative posture. Preserved as **DZ 1229** / CT 1229 (正一部).

**Dating.** Yuán. Dynasty: 元.
