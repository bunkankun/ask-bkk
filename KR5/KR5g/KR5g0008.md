# Shàng qīng Tài xiāo yǐn shū Yuán zhēn dòng fēi èr jǐng jīng 上清太霄隱書元真洞飛二景經

*Shàngqīng Scripture of the Tàixiāo Hidden Writing on the Yuánzhēn Cavern-Flying Two Lights*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Shàng qīng **esoteric visualization scripture** on the "two lights" (*èr jǐng* 二景, sun and moon). Preserved as **DZ 1199** / CT 1199 (正一部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
