# Shì dì chén Dōng huá shàng zuǒ sī mìng Yáng jūn zhuàn jì 侍帝晨東華上佐司命楊君傳記

*Biography of Lord Yáng, Attending-Thearch, Eastern-Floriate, Superior-Assistant, and Destiny-Controller*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng **biography of [[楊羲]] Yáng Xī** (330–386) — the visionary medium of the original Shàng qīng revelation. A vital primary source for the early Shàng qīng tradition. Preserved as **DZ 1428** / CT 1428 (正一部).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
