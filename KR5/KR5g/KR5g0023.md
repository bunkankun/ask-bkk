# Gāo shàng dà dòng Wén chāng sī lù Zǐ yáng bǎo lù 高上大洞文昌司祿紫陽寶籙

*High-and-Supreme Great-Cavern Wénchāng Salary-Controller Purple-Yáng Precious Register*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng ordination-register for **Wén chāng** 文昌 (god of literature and examinations) officiants. Preserved as **DZ 1214** / CT 1214 (正一部).

**Dating.** 15th century. Dynasty: 明.
