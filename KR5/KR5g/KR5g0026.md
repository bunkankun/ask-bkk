# Tài shàng Zhèng yī jiě wǔ yīn zhòu zǔ mì lù 太上正一解五音咒詛祕籙

*Most High Zhèngyī Secret Register for Dispelling Five-Tone Curses and Maledictions*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Zhèng yī **curse-dispelling register**, counteracting maledictions cast on the five phonetic tones. Preserved as **DZ 1217** / CT 1217 (正一部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
