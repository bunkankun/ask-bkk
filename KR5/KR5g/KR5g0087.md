# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo wǔ gǎn wén 洞玄靈寶五感文

*Dòngxuán Língbǎo Text of Five Responsive-Feelings*

by [[陸修靜]] (Lù Xiūjìng, 406–477)

A **foundational Líng bǎo treatise** by the patriarch Lù Xiūjìng on the "Five Responsive-Feelings" (*wǔ gǎn* 五感). Preserved as **DZ 1278** / CT 1278 (正一部).

**Dating.** Within Lù Xiūjìng's lifetime. Dynasty: 南朝.
