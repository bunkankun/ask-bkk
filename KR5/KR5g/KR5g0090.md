# Wǔ yuè zhēn xíng xù lùn 五嶽真形序論

*Preface-Discourse on the True Shapes of the Five Sacred Peaks*

Anonymous; late Six Dynasties

A late-Six-Dynasties **cosmographic-sacred-geography text** on the True Shapes (*zhēn xíng* 真形) of the Five Sacred Peaks — a foundational visual-ritual image in Daoist sacred cartography. Preserved as **DZ 1281** / CT 1281 (正一部).

**Dating.** Late Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝後期.

## Translations and research

- Chavannes, Édouard. *Le T'ai Chan*. Paris, 1910. Classic study of sacred-peaks cult.
