# Dào fǎ huì yuán 道法會元

*Origins Assembled: The Great Compendium of Daoist Ritual*

Anonymous; early Míng

**The single largest ritual compendium in the Dào zàng** — 268 *juàn*, collecting the ritual corpus of the **Thunder Rites** (*léi fǎ* 雷法) movements: Shén xiāo 神霄, Qīng wēi 清微, Tóng chū 童初, Tiān xīn 天心, and allied traditions. Compiled from Sòng-Yuán ritual sources during the early Míng for the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng. An indispensable primary source for the Sòng-Míng Daoist thunder-magic ritual traditions. Preserved as **DZ 1220** / CT 1220 (正一部).

**Dating.** Early Míng; incorporating Sòng-Yuán materials. Dynasty: 明.
