# Tài shàng sān wǔ Zhèng yī méng wēi lù 太上三五正一盟威籙

*Most High Three-and-Five Zhèngyī Covenant-Authority Register*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

**The core ordination-register** of the **Zhèng yī / Celestial Masters tradition** — the foundational *Sān wǔ méng wēi lù* 三五盟威籙 (the "Register of Three-and-Five Covenant-Authority") whose transmission defined entry into the Celestial-Masters priesthood. Preserved as **DZ 1208** / CT 1208 (正一部) in 6 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Core material early; received recension Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
