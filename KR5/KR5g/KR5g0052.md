# Zhèng yī fǎ wén tài shàng wài lù yí 正一法文太上外籙儀

*Zhèngyī Dharma-Text Most-High Liturgy for Outer Registers*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties **Celestial-Masters ordination liturgy** for the **outer registers** (*wài lù* 外籙 — lower-rank lay registers). An important source for reconstructing early Celestial-Masters ordination. Preserved as **DZ 1243** / CT 1243 (正一部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
