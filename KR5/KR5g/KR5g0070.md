# Liù gēn guī dào lùn 六根歸道論

*Discourse on the Six Sense-Roots Returning to the Way*

Anonymous; Yuán

A Yuán Daoist-Buddhist syncretic treatise on the **Six Sense-Roots** (*liù gēn* 六根 — a Buddhist term) returning to the Daoist Way. Preserved as **DZ 1261** / CT 1261 (正一部).

**Dating.** Yuán. Dynasty: 元.
