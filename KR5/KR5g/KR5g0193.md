# Shàng qīng Tài yī dì jūn tài dān yǐn shū jiě bāo shí èr jié jié tú jué 上清太一帝君太丹隱書解胞十二結節圖訣

*Shàngqīng Tàiyī Thearch-Lord's Diagrammed Instructions on Releasing the Twelve Knots of the Placenta*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Shàng qīng **embryonic-release scripture** on "releasing the twelve knots" (*jiě bāo shí èr jié* 解胞十二結) that bind the prenatal body. Preserved as **DZ 1384** / CT 1384 (正一部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
