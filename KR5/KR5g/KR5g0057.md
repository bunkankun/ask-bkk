# Sān dòng qún xiān lù 三洞群仙錄

*Record of the Multitude of Immortals from the Three Caverns*

by [[陳葆光]] (Chén Bǎoguāng)

**A major 20-*juàn* hagiographical encyclopedia** of Daoist immortals, compiled from Three-Cavern sources. Preserved as **DZ 1248** / CT 1248 (正一部).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
