# Sì shèng zhēn jūn líng qiān 四聖真君靈籤

*Numinous Oracle-Slips of the Four Sage Perfected-Lords*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Daoist **divinatory oracle-slip collection** (*líng qiān* 靈籤) for the Four Sage Perfected-Lords (Four Daoist guardian-generals of the Northern Dipper). Preserved as **DZ 1298** / CT 1298 (正一部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
