# Qīng hé zhēn rén Běi yóu yǔ lù 清和真人北遊語錄

*Recorded Sayings of Perfected Qīnghé's Northern Journey*

by [[尹志平]] (Yǐn Zhìpíng, 1169–1251) — Quán zhēn Patriarch

**Recorded sayings of Yǐn Zhìpíng** (*hào* Qīng hé zǐ 清和子), second-generation Quán zhēn patriarch and successor to [[丘處機]] Qiū Chǔjī. Preserved as **DZ 1310** / CT 1310 (正一部).

**Dating.** 1228–1251. Dynasty: 金-元.
