# Zhèng yī tiān shī gào Zhào Shēng kǒu jué 正一天師告趙昇口訣

*Oral Instructions of the Zhèngyī Celestial Master Instructing Zhào Shēng*

Anonymous; Eastern Jìn

An Eastern-Jìn Zhèng yī **oral-instruction text** framed as Zhāng Dàolíng 張道陵 instructing his disciple Zhào Shēng 趙昇. Preserved as **DZ 1273** / CT 1273 (正一部).

**Dating.** Eastern Jìn. Dynasty: 東晉.
