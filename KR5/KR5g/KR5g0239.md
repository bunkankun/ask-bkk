# Dào zàng mù lù xiáng zhù 道藏目錄詳註

*Detailed Annotated Catalogue of the Dào zàng*

by [[白雲齋]] (Bái yún zhāi = Bái Yúnjì) — Míng bibliographer

**The standard pre-modern catalogue of the Dào zàng**, completed 1626 — providing text-by-text bibliographical summaries of the canon. Fundamental reference for all subsequent Dàozàng scholarship until Schipper & Verellen's *Taoist Canon* (2004). Not preserved in the Dào zàng proper (post-dates the 1445 compilation).

**Dating.** 1626. Dynasty: 明.

## Translations and research

- Boltz, Judith M. *A Survey of Taoist Literature*. 1987. Makes extensive use.
- Schipper, Kristofer M., and Franciscus Verellen, eds. *The Taoist Canon*. Chicago UP, 2004. The modern replacement.
