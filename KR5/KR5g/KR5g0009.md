# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo tài shàng liù zhāi shí zhí shèng jì jīng 洞玄靈寶太上六齋十直聖紀經

*Dòngxuán Língbǎo Most High Sage-Record Scripture on the Six Abstinences and Ten Direct-Days*

Anonymous; 8th–10th century

A Líng bǎo scripture on the **six abstinence-days** (*liù zhāi* 六齋) and **ten direct-days** (*shí zhí* 十直) — the regular Daoist ritual calendar of merit-days, patterned on Buddhist *uposatha* days. Preserved as **DZ 1200** / CT 1200 (正一部).

**Dating.** 8th–10th century. Dynasty: 唐-五代.
