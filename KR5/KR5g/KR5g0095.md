# Lú shān Tài píng xīng guó gōng cǎi fǎng zhēn jūn shì shí 盧山太平興國宮採訪真君事實

*Record of the Perfected-Lord at the Tàipíng Xīngguó Palace on Mount Lú*

by [[葉義問]] (Yè Yìwèn, 1098–1170)

A Southern-Sòng local-Daoist record of the cult of the **Cǎi fǎng zhēn jūn** 採訪真君 at the Tài píng xīng guó gōng palace on Mount Lú (Jiāngxī). Preserved as **DZ 1286** / CT 1286 (正一部).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
