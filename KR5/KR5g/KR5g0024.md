*Most High Northern-Pole Demon-Subduing Divine-Incantation Demon-Slaying Register*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng **exorcistic register** for officiants of the Northern-Pole demon-subduing cult. Preserved as **DZ 1215** / CT 1215 (正一部).
