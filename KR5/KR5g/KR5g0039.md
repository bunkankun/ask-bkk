# Tài píng yù lǎn 太平御覽

*Imperial Readings of the Tàipíng Era*

excerpts compiled by [[李昉]] (Lǐ Fǎng, 925–996) and colleagues; 983

A selection of **Daoist-relevant materials from the great Northern-Sòng imperial encyclopedia *Tài píng yù lǎn*** (1000 *juàn*, completed 983). The Dào zàng entry in 10 *juàn* presents extracts of Daoist interest; the full *Tài píng yù lǎn* is a general imperial *leishu* (encyclopedia of classified quotations) — one of the Four Great Books of the Sòng (*Sòng sì dà shū*). Preserved as **DZ 1230** / CT 1230 (正一部).

**Dating.** 983. Dynasty: 北宋.
