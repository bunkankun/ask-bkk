# Shàng qīng qióng gōng líng fēi liù jiǎ lù 上清瓊宮靈飛六甲籙

*Shàngqīng Numinous-Flying Six-Jiǎ Register of the Jasper Palace*

Anonymous; Eastern Jìn

An Eastern-Jìn Shàng qīng **Six-Jiǎ** register for invoking the six *jiǎ* spirits of the sexagenary cycle. Preserved as **DZ 1391** / CT 1391 (正一部).

**Dating.** Eastern Jìn. Dynasty: 東晉.
