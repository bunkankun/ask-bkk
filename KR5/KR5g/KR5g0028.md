# Gāo shàng Shén xiāo yù qīng zhēn wáng zǐ shū dà fǎ 高上神霄玉清真王紫書大法

*High-and-Supreme Divine-Empyrean Jade-Clarity Perfect-King Great Dharma of the Purple Writ*

Anonymous; Míng

**A major Míng Shén xiāo 神霄 ritual compendium** in 12 *juàn* — collecting the Thunder-Rites liturgy of the Divine-Empyrean tradition that originated in the Northern-Sòng court of Huī zōng. Preserved as **DZ 1219** / CT 1219 (正一部).

**Dating.** Early Míng. Dynasty: 明.
