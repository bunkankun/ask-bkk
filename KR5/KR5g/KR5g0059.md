# Chōng xū tōng miào shì chén Wáng xiān sheng jiā huà 沖虛通妙侍宸王先生家話

*Household Conversations of Master Wáng, Attendant to the Throne*

compiled by [[袁庭植]] (Yuán Tíngzhí); sayings of **Wáng Wénqīng** 王文卿 (1093–1153)

Recorded sayings of **Wáng Wénqīng** 王文卿 — the founding patriarch of the **Shén xiāo** 神霄 ("Divine Empyrean") Thunder-Rites lineage, which achieved imperial prominence under the Sòng Huī zōng court. Preserved as **DZ 1250** / CT 1250 (正一部).

**Dating.** Mid to late Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
