# Dòng zhēn tài shàng bā sù zhēn jīng jīng yào sān jǐng miào jué 洞真太上八素真經精耀三景妙訣

*Dòngzhēn Eight-Plain Perfect-Scripture Wondrous Instructions on Essence-Radiance and Three Lights*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Shàng qīng **Eight-Plain** (*bā sù* 八素) scripture on meditation on the "three lights" (sun/moon/stars). Preserved as **DZ 1320** / CT 1320 (正一部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
