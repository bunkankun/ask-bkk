# Shàng qīng Líng bǎo dà fǎ (yī) 上清靈寶大法(一)

*Shàngqīng Língbǎo Great Dharma (Recension I)*

by [[王契真]] (Wáng Qìzhēn) — Southern-Sòng ritualist

A 44-*juàn* **Southern-Sòng ritual manual** synthesizing Shàng qīng and Líng bǎo liturgies — the first of two Dào zàng recensions (see [[KR5g0032]] for Jīn Yǔnzhōng's recension). Preserved as **DZ 1221** / CT 1221 (正一部).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
