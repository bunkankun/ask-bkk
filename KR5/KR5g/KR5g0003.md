# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo tiān zūn shuō Luó tiān dà jiào shàng pǐn miào jīng 太上洞玄靈寶天尊說羅天大醮上品妙經

*Most High Dòngxuán Língbǎo Heaven-Worthy's Wondrous Scripture of the Superior-Grade Luótiān Great Offering*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Sòng-Yuán Líng bǎo / Zhèng yī ritual scripture for the **Luó tiān dà jiào** 羅天大醮 — the largest and most prestigious of the Daoist imperial rituals. Preserved as **DZ 1194** / CT 1194 (正一部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
