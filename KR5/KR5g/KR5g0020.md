# Zhèng yī fǎ wén chuán dū gōng bǎn yí 正一法文傳都功版儀

*Zhèngyī Dharma-Text Liturgy for Transmitting the Chief-Merit Tablet*

Anonymous; early Táng

An early Táng Zhèng yī **ordination liturgy** for transmitting the "chief-merit tablet" (*dū gōng bǎn* 都功版). Preserved as **DZ 1211** / CT 1211 (正一部).

**Dating.** Early Táng. Dynasty: 唐初期.
