# Chóng yáng lì jiào shí wǔ lùn 重陽立教十五論

*Chóngyáng's Fifteen Discourses on Establishing the Teaching*

by [[王嚞]] (Wáng Chóngyáng, 1113–1170)

**Wáng Chóngyáng's foundational doctrinal manifesto** — fifteen short chapters establishing the programmatic teaching of the new Quán zhēn school (precepts, austerities, meditation, the nature of the mind). Preserved as **DZ 1233** / CT 1233 (正一部).

**Dating.** Within Wáng's active life. Dynasty: 金.

## Translations and research

- Eskildsen, Stephen. *The Teachings and Practices of the Early Quanzhen Taoist Masters*. SUNY Press, 2004. Fundamental text.
