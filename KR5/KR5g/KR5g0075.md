# Dèng tiān jūn xuán líng bā mén bào yìng nèi zhǐ 鄧天君玄靈八門報應內旨

*Lord Dèng's Inner Purport of the Eight-Gate Responsive Retribution of Mysterious Efficacy*

Anonymous; Sòng-Míng

A Daoist **retribution-scripture** invoking **Lord Dèng** 鄧天君 (Dèng Bó wēn 鄧伯溫, one of the Thunder-Rites generals). Preserved as **DZ 1266** / CT 1266 (正一部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Míng. Dynasty: 宋-明.
