# Dà Míng Dào zàng jīng mù lù 大明道藏經目錄

*Catalogue of the Scriptures of the Great Míng Dào zàng*

Anonymous; Míng

**The official catalogue** of the **Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng** 正統道藏 of 1445, listing all 1,476 texts of the canon as compiled under imperial commission. An indispensable primary reference for the Dào zàng's structure. Preserved as **DZ 1431a** / CT 1431a (正一部).

**Dating.** 1444–1445. Dynasty: 明.
