# Lǎo zǐ wēi zhǐ lì lüè 老子微旨例略

*Abridged Examples of Lǎozǐ's Subtle Purport*

by [[王弼]] (Wáng Bì, 226–249)

**Wáng Bì's systematic introduction to the *Dào dé jīng*** — a companion to his commentary proper. A foundational work of Wèi-Jìn *xuán xué* 玄學 metaphysics. Preserved as **DZ 1255** / CT 1255 (正一部).

**Dating.** Within Wáng Bì's lifetime (226–249). Dynasty: 魏.
