# Sān shí dài tiān shī Xū jìng zhēn jūn yǔ lù 三十代天師虛靖真君語錄

*Recorded Sayings of the Thirtieth Celestial Master, the Perfected-Lord Xūjìng*

by [[張繼先]] (Zhāng Jìxiān, 1092–1127) — Thirtieth Celestial Master

**The recorded teachings of the 30th Celestial Master** Zhāng Jìxiān, a key figure in the Northern-Sòng court-Daoist synthesis under Huī zōng. Preserved as **DZ 1249** / CT 1249 (正一部).

**Dating.** Within Zhāng's lifetime (1092–1127). Dynasty: 北宋-南宋.
