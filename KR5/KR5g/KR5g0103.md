# Shàng qīng huáng shū guò dù yí 上清黃書過度儀

*Shàngqīng Yellow-Writing Transition-Crossing Liturgy*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

**A controversial early Celestial-Masters sexual-ritual text** — the *Huáng shū* 黃書 corpus describes the *guò dù* 過度 ("passage through") rites combining male-female ritualized intercourse with astral-numerical procedures. Later Daoist reformers (notably Kòu Qiānzhī) sought to suppress these practices. Preserved as **DZ 1294** / CT 1294 (正一部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
