# Cháng chūn zhēn rén xī yóu jì 長春真人西遊記

*Record of the Perfected Chángchūn's Journey to the West*

by [[李志常]] (Lǐ Zhìcháng, 1193–1256)

**The travel-narrative of [[丘處機]] Qiū Chǔjī's 1219–1223 overland journey to meet Chinggis Khan** in the Hindu Kush, recorded by his disciple Lǐ Zhìcháng. **One of the major primary sources for 13th-century Central Asian history and Mongol-period religious-political relations.** Preserved as **DZ 1429** / CT 1429 (正一部).

**Dating.** 1228. Dynasty: 元.
