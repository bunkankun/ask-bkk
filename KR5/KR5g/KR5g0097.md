# Yuán chén zhāng jiào lì chéng lì 元辰章醮立成曆

*Established Calendar for Original-Hour Memorial Offerings*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Daoist **astrological-ritual calendar** for determining the correct days for memorial-offering rites. Preserved as **DZ 1288** / CT 1288 (正一部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
