# Yì shèng bǎo dé zhuàn 翊聖保德傳

*Account of the Assisting-Sage Protecting-Virtue [Deity]*

by [[王欽若]] (Wáng Qīnruò, 962–1025)

A Northern-Sòng **imperially-sponsored Daoist hagiographical-political text** on the new cult of **Yì shèng bǎo dé zhēn jūn** 翊聖保德真君 — one of the Daoist deities whose worship Wáng Qīnruò instituted to legitimize Sòng Zhēn zōng's imperial-Daoist program. Preserved as **DZ 1285** / CT 1285 (正一部).

**Dating.** Early Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋.
