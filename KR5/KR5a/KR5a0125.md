# Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhùjiě 黃帝陰符經註解

*Commentary on the "Yīnfú jīng" by Zōu Xīn (Zhū Xī)*

Southern-Sòng commentary attributed to Zōu Xīn 鄒訢 — a pseudonym for Zhū Xī 朱熹 (1130–1200) — printed c. 1305 by Huáng Ruìjié 黃瑞節; ten folios; preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0125 / CT 125 = TC 124), 洞真部 本文類; also in the *Sìkù quánshū* as *Yīnfú jīng kǎoyì* 陰符經考異

## About the work

A ten-folio commentary attributed in the Daozang heading to Zōu Xīn 鄒訢, a Daoist from Kōngtóng 崆峒 (where the Yellow Emperor reportedly discoursed with Guǎngchéngzǐ 廣成子 on the meaning of the *Yīnfú jīng*; cf. [[KR5a0120|DZ 120 preface]]). Zōu Xīn is a pseudonym of Zhū Xī 朱熹 (1130–1200): during the Warring States period, Zōu 鄒 was the name of a state earlier called Zhū 朱; and *xīn* 訢 is synonymous with *xī* 熹 (cf. *[[KR5a1002|DZ 1001 Zhōuyì cāntóng qì]]* 3.8a). The present work is identical with the commentary in Zhū Xī's *Yīnfú jīng kǎoyì*.

The real authorship is revealed by Huáng Ruìjié 黃瑞節 in his compilation *Zhūzǐ chéngshū* 朱子成書 (preface dated 1305): the explanations are not by Zhū Xī himself (he only revised the text) but by his friend and collaborator Cài Yuándǐng 蔡元定 (1135–1198). Cài used the long version of the *Yīnfú jīng* (446 characters), dividing it into three *piān* 篇; the disputed final part is left uncommentated. Huáng Ruìjié's supplementary notes quote additional sources and point out textual variants in four different *Yīnfú jīng* versions (8a).

This same commentary-text appears separately in the Daozang at *[[KR5a0118|DZ 118 Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù (Cài shì)]]*.
