# Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註

*Commentary on the "Huángdì yīnfú jīng"*

Táng commentary by the celebrated Daoist Zhāng Guǒ 張果 (first half of the eighth century, d. beginning of the Tiānbǎo era, 742–756), eleven folios, preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0113 / CT 113 = TC 112), 洞真部 本文類

## About the work

An eleven-folio Táng commentary on the [[KR5a0031|*Huángdì yīnfú jīng*]] by the celebrated Daoist Zhāng Guǒ 張果, recorded in [[KR5a0295|DZ 295 *Xù xiān zhuàn*]] 2.4b–6a as having died at the beginning of the Tiānbǎo period (742–756). Since the commentary mentions neither the title nor *hào* that were bestowed on Zhāng by the emperor in 734 (cf. *Jiù Táng shū* 8.200), it was probably written before that date. Zhāng Guǒ's biography in *Jiù Táng shū* 191 records that he wrote the *Yīnfú jīng xuánjiě* 陰符經玄解; in the Sòng catalogues, the present text is listed under the title *Yīnfú jīng tàiwú zhuàn* 陰符經太無傳 (see VDL 140).

Zhāng mentions in his preface that several commentaries to the *Yīnfú jīng* existed already; he wrote his specifically to refute Lǐ Quán 李筌's [[KR5a0109|DZ 109 *Jízhù*]] — making this commentary an early example within the Daozang of direct commentarial polemic between contemporary Táng Daoist authorities.

### Prefaces

*Preface by Zhāng Guǒ* surveying existing *Yīnfú jīng* commentaries and announcing his refutation of Lǐ Quán.

### Abstract

Florian C. Reiter, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:693 (§3.A.1), dates the commentary to before 734 (since it does not mention Zhāng's 734 imperial title).
