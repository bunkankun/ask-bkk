# Xiūzhēn tàijí hùnyuán zhǐxuán tú 修真太極混元指玄圖

*Diagram Pointing to the Mystery of the Cultivation of Truth, of the Supreme Pole, and of Primordial Chaos*

by [[金筌子]] (撰序)

## About the work

A nine-folio Sòng *nèidān* 內丹 ("inner alchemy") chart-text of the ZhōngLǚ 鍾呂 tradition, comprising nine diagrams together with a collection of short formulas (*jué* 訣) — the *Mìchuán tāixī jué* 祕傳胎息訣 in five sections, the *Zhēn lónghǔ jiāogòu nèidān jué tú* 真龍虎交媾內丹訣圖, the *Mìchuán nèidān jué* 祕傳內丹訣 in seven sections (*zǐjīn dān*, *jīndān*, *lónghǔ nèidān*, *qīngjīn dān*, *hóngjīn dān*, *xīhuá dān*, *tàixuán dān*), the *Zhōutiān huǒhòu jué tú* 周天火候訣圖, and others. The treatise, like its companion [[KR5a0150|DZ 149 *Xiūzhēn tàijí hùnyuán tú*]], illustrates the methods set out in the *Xiūzhēn zhǐxuán piān* 修真指玄篇 of [[KR5c1017|DZ 1017 *Dàoshū*]] 19.9a–22a, but supplies only the names of the techniques without practical instructions. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0150 / CT 150 = TC 150), 洞真部 靈圖類.

### Prefaces

*The text's own preface, by Jīn Quánzǐ 金筌子, zì Zhúpō 竹坡, of Hángzhōu, is misplaced in the Dàozàng at the head of the preceding work [[KR5a0150|DZ 149]]; in it Jīn describes encountering Shī Jiānwú in Nánjīng and receiving the diagrammatic transmission. No further preface is in the source.*

### Abstract

Farzeen Baldrian-Hussein, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:804 (§3.A.4, *Nèidān* and *Yǎngshēng*), explains that Jīn Quánzǐ's preface, which the Dàozàng editors inadvertently bound at the start of [[KR5a0150|DZ 149]], in fact belongs to the present work. A near-identical preface, with minor variations, is ascribed to Lǐ Sōng 李竦 — a disciple of Shī Jiānwú and the compiler of [[KR5a0247|DZ 246 *Xīshān qúnxiān huìzhēn jì*]] — in [[KR5a1248|DZ 1248 *Sāndòng qúnxiān lù*]] 14.22a, a work dated 1154. Whereas Jīn places his encounter with Shī in *Nánjīng* simply, Lǐ has *Nánjīng Yīngtiān fǔ* 南京應天府, a name in use in 1008–1016 (*Dàzhōng xiángfú* 大中祥符 era). The present text can therefore be placed between this period and 1154. One Jīn Zhúpō 金竹坡 of the Táng (618–907) is named as the author of [[KR5c0923|DZ 923 *Dàdān qiāngōng lùn*]], but that work is now recognised as a Southern Sòng forgery, and the present Jīn Quánzǐ should not be conflated with it. The frontmatter brackets composition notBefore 1008 / notAfter 1154.
