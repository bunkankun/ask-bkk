# KR5a 洞真部 *Dòngzhēn bù* — Daoist Canon → Cavern of Perfection

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR5a 洞真部 (*Dòngzhēn bù*, "Cavern of Perfection") collects 328 works (DZ 0001 – DZ 0316 in the Schipper / Verellen *Concordance du Tao-tsang* numbering) — the first of the *Sāndòng* 三洞 ("Three Caverns") under which the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (1445) is arranged. Wilkinson describes the *sāndòng* scheme as inherited from the Southern Dynasties: the three Caverns are notionally *schools* — 洞真 with the Shàngqīng 上清 revelations, 洞玄 with the Língbǎo 靈寶 corpus, 洞神 with the Sānhuáng 三皇 tradition — while the further subdivision of each Cavern into 十二類 ("twelve sections": 本文, 神符, 玉訣, 靈圖, 譜錄, 戒律, 威儀, 方法, 衆術, 記傳, 讚頌, 表奏) supplies a *genre* axis cutting across them (Wilkinson §29.4.3, §29.4.4). In practice, by the time the canon was fixed in the Ming the school boundaries had long collapsed: the *Dòngzhēn bù* opens not with a Shàngqīng text but with the Língbǎo *Dùrén jīng* 度人經 [[KR5a0001]], placed at DZ 0001 because the Northern Sòng court under Huīzōng 徽宗 (r. 1100–1126) had elevated it to canonical primacy, and runs on through Táng pseudepigrapha, Sòng-Yuán internal-alchemy commentaries, Yuán hagiographies, and Ming ritual liturgy.

The chronological span of the collected works runs from the pre-Qin *Mù tiānzǐ zhuàn* 穆天子傳 [[KR5a0303]] (c. 300 BCE), included for its mythography and for Guō Pú's 郭璞 Jìn-era commentary; through the *Liè xiān zhuàn* 列仙傳 [[KR5a0306]], the earliest extant collection of immortal-biographies; through the foundational Six-Dynasties strata of *Old Shàngqīng* (~317–420) and *Old Língbǎo* (~395–402) revelation-scriptures; an extensive Táng layer of court-sponsored and pseudo-revealed scriptures; the great Northern Sòng / Huīzōng-era reorganization driven by the Shénxiāo 神霄 movement around Lín Língsù 林靈素 (1076–1120); the Southern Sòng explosion of internal-alchemy (內丹 *nèidān*) commentary on Zhāng Bóduān's *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇 and the Southern-Lineage (南宗) writings of Bái Yùchán; the Yuán hagiographic synthesis in Zhào Dàoyī's *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn* 歷世真仙體道通鑑 [[KR5a0308]]; and a late stratum of Ming liturgy, with works compiled or revised under the forty-third Tiānshī Zhāng Yǔchū 張宇初 (1361–1410) reaching down to the 1410s. Date distribution by composition midpoint is sharply trimodal: a Six-Dynasties stratum (4th–6th c., ~40 works), a major Táng layer (7th–8th c., ~65 works), and a dominant Sòng-Yuán mass (11th–14th c., ~200 works); the late-imperial liturgy is comparatively thin.

## Important texts and text clusters

**The opening *Dùrén jīng* corpus.**
- *Língbǎo wúliàng dùrén shàngpǐn miàojīng* 靈寶無量度人上品妙經 [[KR5a0001]] — *the* opening text of the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* (DZ 0001), the flagship scripture of the Língbǎo tradition. Juan 1 is the late-fourth-century core, juan 2–61 a Northern-Sòng expansion produced under Huīzōng in the Shénxiāo circle around Lín Língsù.
- *Yuánshǐ wúliàng dùrén shàngpǐn miàojīng zhíyīn* 元始無量度人上品妙經直音 [[KR5a0002]] — Sòng-Yuán phonetic guide to the same text.
- The Sòng-Yuán *Dùrén jīng* commentary cluster: *Sìzhù* 四註 [[KR5a0087]], *Zhù* 註 [[KR5a0088]], *Tōngyì* 通義 [[KR5a0089]], *Nèiyì* 內義 [[KR5a0090]], *Zhùjiě* 註解 ([[KR5a0092]], [[KR5a0093]]), *Jīngfǎ* 經法 [[KR5a0094]], *Dàfàn yǐnyǔ shūyì* 大梵隱語疏義 [[KR5a0095]], *Jué yīnyì* 訣音義 [[KR5a0096]] — the densest exegetical layer attached to any single Daoist scripture, plus the diagrammatic *Fútú* 符圖 [[KR5a0148]] and *Pángtōng tú* 旁通圖 [[KR5a0149]] and the *Dàfǎ* 大法 ritual codification [[KR5a0220]].

**Old Shàngqīng revelations (~317–420).** Originating in the visionary séances of Yáng Xī 楊羲 and the Xǔ family at Mount Máo 茅山 and codified two centuries later by Táo Hóngjǐng [[陶弘景]]:
- *Shàngqīng huángqì yángjīng sāndào shùnxíng jīng* 上清黃氣陽精三道順行經 [[KR5a0033]], *Dòngzhēn tàijí Běidì Zǐwēi shénzhòu miàojīng* 洞真太極北帝紫微神咒妙經 [[KR5a0049]], *Shàngqīng qiónggōng língfēi liùjiǎ zuǒyòu shàngfú* 上清瓊宮靈飛六甲左右上符 [[KR5a0084]], *Tàiwéi língshū zǐwén xiānjì zhēnjì shàngjīng* 太微靈書紫文仙忌真記上經 [[KR5a0180]], *Tàiwéi língshū zǐwén lánggān huádān shénzhēn shàngjīng* 太微靈書紫文琅玕華丹神真上經 [[KR5a0256]], *Tàishàng yùpèi jīndāng tàijí jīnshū shàngjīng* 太上玉珮金璫太極金書上經 [[KR5a0056]], *Báiyǔ hēihé língfēi yùfú* 白羽黑翮靈飛玉符 [[KR5a0083]] — together the densest concentration of fourth-century Shàngqīng material in the canon.

**Old Língbǎo revelations (~395–402).** The "ancient Língbǎo scriptures" (*gǔ Língbǎo jīng* 古靈寶經) ascribed to Gě Cháofǔ 葛巢甫 and canonized in Lù Xiūjìng's 陸修靜 *Língbǎo jīngmù* 靈寶經目 (437):
- *Yuánshǐ wǔlǎo chìshū yùpiān zhēnwén tiānshū jīng* 元始五老赤書玉篇真文天書經 [[KR5a0022]] — the *Wǔpiān zhēnwén* 五篇真文, number 1 in Lù Xiūjìng's catalogue.
- *Tàishàng zhūtiān língshū dùmìng miàojīng* 太上諸天靈書度命妙經 [[KR5a0023]], *Tàishàng língbǎo zhūtiān nèiyīn zìrán yùzì* 太上靈寶諸天內音自然玉字 [[KR5a0098]], *Língbǎo zìrán jiǔtiān shēngshén sānbǎo dàyǒu jīnshū* 靈寶自然九天生神三寶大有金書 [[KR5a0166]] — the cosmogonic core of the early Língbǎo system.

**The *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng* tradition.**
- *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng* 上清大洞真經 [[KR5a0006]] — the central scripture of Shàngqīng inner practice, transmitted as a 31-chapter recitation cycle; the received recension descends through Zhū Zìyīng's 朱自英 1024 Máoshān preface, Jiǎng Zōngyíng's 蔣宗瑛 Sòng-Yuán editorial work, and Zhāng Yǔchū's early-Ming colophon.
- *Dàdòng yùjīng* 大洞玉經 [[KR5a0007]] — Ming reworking by Gōng Détóng 龔德同.
- *Tàishàng wújí zǒngzhēn Wénchāng dàdòng xiānjīng* 太上無極總真文昌大洞仙經 [[KR5a0005]] and its commentary *Yùqīng wújí zǒngzhēn Wénchāng dàdòng xiānjīng zhù* 玉清無極總真文昌大洞仙經註 [[KR5a0104]] — the Wénchāng-cult retake on the *Dàdòng* genre.
- *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng yùjué yīnyì* 上清大洞真經玉訣音義 [[KR5a0105]] — phonetic-and-glossary aid.

**The *Yùhuáng* (Jade Emperor) scriptures.** Late-Northern-Sòng to early-Yuán composite, framed as the speech of the *Yùhuáng dàdì* 玉皇大帝:
- *Gāoshàng Yùhuáng běnxíng jíjīng* 高上玉皇本行集經 [[KR5a0010]] [[KR5a0011]] — the long *vita* of the Jade Emperor.
- *Gāoshàng Yùhuáng běnxíng jīngsuǐ* 高上玉皇本行經髓 [[KR5a0012]] (Liú Chùyuán's 劉處源 distillation), *Gāoshàng Yùhuáng xīnyìn jīng* 高上玉皇心印經 [[KR5a0013]], *Gāoshàng Yùhuáng tāixí jīng* 高上玉皇胎息經 [[KR5a0014]] — the inner-cultivation short scriptures of the Yùhuáng cycle.

**The 雷霆 *Léitíng* / Shénxiāo storm-magic corpus.** Composed under and around the Shénxiāo movement of the Huīzōng court:
- *Wúshàng jiǔxiāo Yùqīng Dàfàn Zǐwēi xuándū léitíng yùjīng* 無上九霄玉清大梵紫微玄都雷霆玉經 [[KR5a0015]], *Jiǔtiān yìngyuán léishēng pǔhuà tiānzūn yùshū bǎojīng* 九天應元雷聲普化天尊玉樞寶經 [[KR5a0016]] and its *Jízhù* 集註 [[KR5a0100]], *Tàishàng shuō cháotiān xièléi zhēnjīng* 太上說朝天謝雷真經 [[KR5a0017]] — together the doctrinal scaffolding of the Sòng-Yuán thunder-rite (*léifǎ* 雷法) tradition.

**The *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇 commentary cluster.** Zhāng Bóduān's [[張伯端]] (c. 987–1082) verse-treatise on internal alchemy, canonized in the Sòng-Yuán Southern Lineage (南宗) and surrounded in the *Dòngzhēn bù* by the densest concentration of *nèidān* exegesis in the canon:
- *Zǐyáng zhēnrén Wùzhēn piān zhùshū* 紫陽真人悟真篇註疏 [[KR5a0142]] — Wēng Bǎoguāng's 翁葆光 (fl. 1173) commentary as re-edited by Dài Qǐzōng 戴起宗 (c. 1335–1340).
- *Wùzhēn piān sānzhù* 悟真篇三註 [[KR5a0143]], *Zhíxiǎng xiángshuō sānchéng mìyào* 直指詳說三乘秘要 [[KR5a0144]], *Shíyí* 拾遺 [[KR5a0145]], *Zhùshì* 註釋 [[KR5a0146]], *Jiǎngyì* 講義 [[KR5a0147]] — five further received commentaries, plus the *Xiūzhēn shíshū* recension [[KR5a0268]].

**The *Xiūzhēn shíshū* 修真十書 collection.** A ten-text Sòng-Yuán anthology of Southern-Lineage and proto-Quánzhēn material, gathered into a single ten-juan collection in the Yuán:
- [[KR5a0265]] *Jīndān dàchéng jí* 金丹大成集 (Xiāo Tíngzhī 蕭廷芝), [[KR5a0266]] *Zhōng Lǚ chuándào jí* 鍾呂傳道集 (the foundational Zhōnglí Quán 鍾離權 / Lǚ Dòngbīn 呂洞賓 dialogues), [[KR5a0267]] *Zázhù jiéjìng* 雜著捷徑, [[KR5a0268]] *Wùzhēn piān*, [[KR5a0269]] *Yùlóng jí* 玉隆集 and [[KR5a0270]] *Shàngqīng jí* 上清集 and [[KR5a0271]] *Wǔyí jí* 武夷集 (the three Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾 collected-writings volumes), [[KR5a0272]] *Pánshān yǔlù* 盤山語錄 (Wáng Zhìjǐn 王志謹's Quánzhēn discourses), [[KR5a0273]] *Huángtíng nèijǐng wǔzàng liùfǔ tú* 黃庭內景五藏六府圖, [[KR5a0274]] *Huángtíng nèijǐng yùjīng zhù* 黃庭內景玉經注 — together one of the great preserved compendia of Sòng-Yuán inner-alchemical practice.

**Hagiography and biography (記傳類).**
- *Mù tiānzǐ zhuàn* 穆天子傳 [[KR5a0303]] — the late-Warring-States travel romance of King Mù's encounter with the Queen Mother of the West, with Guō Pú's Jìn commentary; chronologically the oldest text in the division.
- *Liè xiān zhuàn* 列仙傳 [[KR5a0306]] — earliest extant Daoist hagiography, pseudepigraphically ascribed to Liú Xiàng 劉向 (77 – 6 BCE).
- *Hàn Wǔdì nèizhuàn* 漢武帝內傳 [[KR5a0304]] — Six-Dynasties Shàngqīng-flavoured fiction on the Hàn emperor's reception of the Queen Mother.
- *Zǐyáng zhēnrén nèizhuàn* 紫陽真人內傳 [[KR5a0315]] — Eastern-Jìn Shàngqīng *vita* of Zhōu Yìshān 周義山.
- *Zhōu shì míngtōng jì* 周氏冥通記 [[KR5a0314]] — Táo Hóngjǐng's 515–516 record of his disciple Zhōu Zǐliáng's 周子良 night-time encounters with the Perfected; the single best-preserved document of fifth-century Shàngqīng séance practice.
- *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn* 歷世真仙體道通鑑 [[KR5a0308]] (Zhào Dàoyī 趙道一, 53 juan, c. 1294), with continuation [[KR5a0309]] and *Hòují* 後集 [[KR5a0310]] — the great Yuán Daoist hagiographical synthesis, more than 900 biographies; long the principal Daoist counterpart to the Buddhist *Gāosēng zhuàn* 高僧傳.
- *Máoshān zhì* 茅山誌 [[KR5a0316]] (Liú Dàbīn 劉大彬, 1328) — the standard Yuán gazetteer of the central Shàngqīng mountain.

**Genealogies and ranking (譜錄類).**
- *Dòngxuán língbǎo zhēnlíng wèiyè tú* 洞玄靈寶真靈位業圖 [[KR5a0168]] — Táo Hóngjǐng's ranked diagram of the entire Daoist pantheon across seven celestial levels; foundational for later Daoist liturgy and the principal source for the Six-Dynasties imagination of the heavens.

**The Lǐ Dàochún 李道純 syncretic *nèidān* corpus.** A late-Yuán cluster gathering the writings of the Quánzhēn-Southern-Lineage syncretist (fl. 1290) — six titles in the division, the densest single-author concentration after the *Dùrén* and *Wùzhēn* commentary clusters.

## Important persons

- [[陶弘景]] (Táo Hóngjǐng, 456–536) — patriarch of the Máoshān Shàngqīng tradition; compiler of the *Zhēnlíng wèiyè tú* [[KR5a0168]] and editor of the *Zhōu shì míngtōng jì* [[KR5a0314]]; the indispensable transmitter of Yáng Xī's fourth-century revelations.
- [[陸修靜]] (Lù Xiūjìng, 406–477) — Liú-Sòng codifier of the Língbǎo corpus, whose 437 *Língbǎo jīngmù* fixed the canonical sequence of the *Yuánshǐ wǔlǎo chìshū* [[KR5a0022]] and its companion scriptures, and whose *Sāndòng* classification became the architecture of every subsequent Daoist canon — including the one this division opens.
- [[郭璞]] (Guō Pú, 276–324) — Jìn polymath whose commentary on the *Mù tiānzǐ zhuàn* [[KR5a0303]] is co-transmitted with the text and is its principal reading aid.
- [[劉向]] (Liú Xiàng, 77 – 6 BCE, attributed) — pseudepigraphic compiler of the *Liè xiān zhuàn* [[KR5a0306]], the prototype of the immortal-biography genre.
- [[寇謙之]] (Kòu Qiānzhī, 365–448) — Northern-Wèi Tiānshī reformer whose Old-Tiānshī materials underlie several pseudo-archaic ritual texts in the division.
- [[朱自英]] (Zhū Zìyīng, 976–1029) — 23rd Máoshān patriarch; his 1024 preface anchors the received [[KR5a0006]] *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng*.
- [[閭丘方遠]] (Lǘqiū Fāngyuǎn, d. 902) — late-Táng Shàngqīng master whose editorial work fixed the received text of the *Zhēnlíng wèiyè tú* [[KR5a0168]].
- [[陳景元]] (Chén Jǐngyuán, 1024–1094) — Northern-Sòng Shàngqīng exegete; commentator on the *Dùrén jīng* and contributor to the early *Dòngzhēn* commentarial stream.
- [[張伯端]] (Zhāng Bóduān, c. 987–1082) — author of the *Wùzhēn piān*, founder of the Southern Lineage (南宗) of *nèidān*; his work generates the seven *Wùzhēn piān* texts [[KR5a0142]]–[[KR5a0147]] and [[KR5a0268]] and is, by sheer volume of derivative commentary, the single most influential text in the division.
- [[翁葆光]] (Wēng Bǎoguāng, fl. 1173) — principal early commentator on the *Wùzhēn piān* [[KR5a0142]] [[KR5a0143]] [[KR5a0144]] [[KR5a0145]].
- [[白玉蟾]] (Bái Yùchán, c. 1194–1229) — fourth Patriarch of the Southern Lineage; the *Yùlóng* [[KR5a0269]], *Shàngqīng* [[KR5a0270]], and *Wǔyí* [[KR5a0271]] collections in the *Xiūzhēn shíshū* are his collected writings, and many of the late-Sòng thunder-rite and *Léitíng* texts in the division pass through his school.
- [[李道純]] (Lǐ Dàochún, fl. 1290) — Yuán syncretist who fused Quánzhēn and Southern-Lineage *nèidān*; the most prolific single late-Yuán author in the division.
- [[趙道一]] (Zhào Dàoyī, late 13th c.) — compiler of the *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn* [[KR5a0308]]–[[KR5a0310]], the principal Yuán hagiographical synthesis.
- [[蔣宗瑛]] (Jiǎng Zōngyíng, fl. 1250) — Sòng-Yuán transitional editor of the *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng* [[KR5a0006]].
- [[苗善時]] (Miáo Shànshí, fl. 1310) — Yuán hagiographer of the Lǚ Dòngbīn cult; author of [[KR5a0317]] *Chúnyáng dìjūn shénhuà miàotōng jì* 純陽帝君神化妙通紀.
- [[張宇初]] (Zhāng Yǔchū, 1361–1410) — 43rd Celestial Master; editorial overseer of the early-Ming preparations for the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* whose colophons are visible on [[KR5a0006]] and elsewhere in the division.
- [[路時中]] (Lù Shízhōng, fl. 1120) — Northern-Sòng Tiānxīn 天心 ritual codifier whose materials filter into the *Dòngzhēn* ritual texts.
- [[王玠]] (Wáng Jiè, fl. 1330) — late-Yuán commentator on the *Dùrén jīng* and *Wùzhēn piān*; one of the principal voices of post-Bái-Yùchán Southern-Lineage exegesis.
- [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhìxū, c. 1289 – after 1335) — Yuán syncretic *nèidān* author and *Wùzhēn piān* commentator [[KR5a0143]].
- [[夏元鼎]] (Xià Yuándǐng, fl. 1225) — Southern Sòng *Wùzhēn piān* commentator.
- [[元陽子]] (Yuánzǐ, Táng) — pseudonymous Táng alchemical author whose *Yīnfú jīng* hymn-commentary [[KR5a0323]] enters the division's *zànsòng* 讚頌 layer.
- [[文昌帝君]] (Wénchāng dìjūn) — divine "author" of the Wénchāng-cult scriptures including [[KR5a0005]] and [[KR5a0029]], reflecting the absorption of regional cult literature into the Daoist canon from the late Sòng onward.

Lín Língsù 林靈素 (1076–1120), founder of the Shénxiāo school under Huīzōng and chief architect of the 60-juan expansion of [[KR5a0001]], and Gě Cháofǔ 葛巢甫 (fl. c. 400), to whom the *Old Língbǎo* revelation cycle of [[KR5a0022]] [[KR5a0023]] [[KR5a0098]] [[KR5a0166]] is ascribed, would belong on this list; person notes for both remain to be created.

## Topics

- **The *sāndòng* 三洞 scheme and the school / genre cross-classification.** The notional division of the canon by school (Shàngqīng / Língbǎo / Sānhuáng) cuts against the genre subdivision into 十二類 — and by the time of the Ming compilation the two axes have ceased to cohere: in *Dòngzhēn bù* the opening text is Língbǎo, the largest cluster (the *Yùhuáng* and Léitíng scriptures) is post-revelation Sòng material, and many texts placed in 本文 are Sòng or Yuán pseudo-revelations rather than ancient scripture. Wilkinson (§29.4.4) notes the same drift across the canon as a whole.

- **Old Shàngqīng vs. Old Língbǎo.** The two principal Six-Dynasties revelation cycles preserve sharply different theological tones — Shàngqīng inner visualization and astral travel ([[KR5a0033]] [[KR5a0049]] [[KR5a0084]] [[KR5a0180]] [[KR5a0256]]) versus the Mahāyāna-influenced Língbǎo soteriology of universal salvation in the *Yuánshǐ wǔlǎo chìshū* [[KR5a0022]] and *Zhūtiān língshū dùmìng* [[KR5a0023]]. The two traditions are interleaved without comment in the *Dòngzhēn* 本文 layer.

- **The *Dùrén jīng* and the Huīzōng-Shénxiāo expansion.** A 4th–5th-century Língbǎo scripture of about one juan is grown in the early twelfth century into a sixty-one-juan compendium — the founding intervention of the Shénxiāo movement, and the editorial reason the Ming canon places this text at DZ 0001. The commentary cluster [[KR5a0087]]–[[KR5a0096]] is the single densest exegetical archipelago around any scripture in the canon.

- **Internal alchemy (*nèidān* 內丹) and the Southern Lineage.** The arrival of Zhāng Bóduān's *Wùzhēn piān* in the late eleventh century reorganizes Daoist soteriology around the symbolic alchemical refinement of *jīng* 精 — *qì* 氣 — *shén* 神. The *Dòngzhēn bù* preserves the densest commentary cluster on the *Wùzhēn piān* in any surviving collection ([[KR5a0142]]–[[KR5a0147]], [[KR5a0268]]) and the three-volume Bái Yùchán corpus inside [[KR5a0269]]–[[KR5a0271]], together with the *Zhōng Lǚ chuándào jí* [[KR5a0266]] that fixes the eponymous teacher-student dialogue framing of the entire Southern Lineage.

- **Thunder-rites and Shénxiāo / Léitíng theology.** A distinctive Northern-Sòng strand of Daoist ritual centred on the *Léishēng pǔhuà tiānzūn* 雷聲普化天尊 (the Heavenly Worthy of the Universal Transformation of Thunder), institutionally crystallizing in the Shénxiāo school. The principal scriptural texts [[KR5a0015]] [[KR5a0016]] [[KR5a0017]] and the *Jízhù* [[KR5a0100]] are flanked in the canon by the matching *Léitíng* repentance liturgies [[KR5a0196]] [[KR5a0197]].

- **Cult-literature absorption: Wénchāng, Yùhuáng, Zhēnwǔ.** Three regional cults — the Sichuan god of literature Wénchāng (texts [[KR5a0005]] [[KR5a0028]] [[KR5a0029]] [[KR5a0104]]), the Jade Emperor (the *Yùhuáng* group [[KR5a0010]]–[[KR5a0014]]), and the northern martial god Zhēnwǔ ([[KR5a0027]]) — enter the Daoist canon in the Sòng-Yuán-Ming centuries through scriptures framed as the speech of these gods themselves. The pattern is characteristic of the late imperial canon's openness to non-revelation-school literature.

- **Hagiographical synthesis.** A continuous tradition from the *Liè xiān zhuàn* [[KR5a0306]] through the *Hàn Wǔdì* romances [[KR5a0304]] [[KR5a0305]], the targeted single-figure biographies ([[KR5a0312]] *Huáyáng Táo yǐnjū nèizhuàn* on Táo Hóngjǐng; [[KR5a0315]] *Zǐyáng zhēnrén nèizhuàn*), and finally the great Yuán synthesis of Zhào Dàoyī ([[KR5a0308]] [[KR5a0309]] [[KR5a0310]]), to which the gazetteers ([[KR5a0316]] *Máoshān zhì*; [[KR5a0318]] *Tàihuá xīyí zhì*; [[KR5a0319]] *Xīyuè Huáshān zhì*) attach.

## Timeline

- **c. 300–250 BCE** — *Mù tiānzǐ zhuàn* 穆天子傳 [[KR5a0303]] — pre-Qin travel romance; Jìn commentary by [[郭璞]] (276–324).
- **1st c. CE (Hòu Hàn)** — *Liè xiān zhuàn* 列仙傳 [[KR5a0306]] — earliest Daoist hagiography, pseudepigraphically ascribed to [[劉向]].
- **2nd–3rd c.** — *Huángdì lóngshǒu jīng* 黃帝龍首經 [[KR5a0295]], *Tàiqīng zhēnrén luòmìng jué* 太清真人絡命訣 [[KR5a0133]] — early Hàn-Wèi divinatory pseudepigrapha.
- **317–420 (Eastern Jìn)** — Yáng Xī's Shàngqīng revelations: [[KR5a0033]] [[KR5a0049]] [[KR5a0056]] [[KR5a0083]] [[KR5a0084]] [[KR5a0180]] [[KR5a0256]] — the foundational stratum of the division.
- **c. 395–402 (Eastern Jìn)** — Gě Cháofǔ's Old Língbǎo cycle: [[KR5a0022]] *Yuánshǐ wǔlǎo chìshū*, [[KR5a0023]] *Zhūtiān língshū dùmìng*, [[KR5a0098]] *Zhūtiān nèiyīn zìrán yùzì*, [[KR5a0166]] *Jiǔtiān shēngshén sānbǎo dàyǒu jīnshū*; the *Dùrén jīng* [[KR5a0001]] core also dates from this window.
- **420–589 (Six Dynasties)** — Eastern-Jìn Shàngqīng/Língbǎo material consolidates: [[KR5a0037]] [[KR5a0055]] [[KR5a0060]] [[KR5a0082]] [[KR5a0129]] [[KR5a0134]] [[KR5a0167]] [[KR5a0185]] [[KR5a0188]]; *Hàn Wǔdì nèizhuàn* [[KR5a0304]] composed in the 6th c.
- **c. 500 (Liáng)** — [[陶弘景]] compiles the *Zhēnlíng wèiyè tú* [[KR5a0168]]; subsequently re-edited by [[閭丘方遠]] before 893.
- **515–517 (Liáng)** — [[陶弘景]] compiles the *Zhōu shì míngtōng jì* [[KR5a0314]] from his disciple Zhōu Zǐliáng's séance diaries.
- **618–907 (Táng)** — Large Táng layer of court-sponsored and pseudo-revealed scriptures: [[KR5a0004]] *Wúshàng nèibì zhēnzàng jīng*, [[KR5a0009]] *Hǎikōng zhìzàng jīng*, [[KR5a0019]] *Shēngxuán xiāozāi hùmìng miàojīng*, [[KR5a0021]] *Chángshēng yánshòu jífúdé jīng*, [[KR5a0024]] *Shēngtiān dédào jīng*, the *Yuándū lǜwén* 玄都律文 [[KR5a0189]] and many shorter scriptures; *Hàn Wǔdì wàizhuàn* [[KR5a0305]] reaches its received form by the mid-Táng.
- **1024 (N. Sòng)** — [[朱自英]]'s Máoshān preface canonizes the received [[KR5a0006]] *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng*.
- **c. 1075–1082 (N. Sòng)** — [[張伯端]] composes the *Wùzhēn piān* in Sichuan (now read through the Sòng-Yuán commentary cluster [[KR5a0142]]–[[KR5a0147]] [[KR5a0268]]).
- **c. 1080–1103 (N. Sòng, Huīzōng era)** — Shénxiāo / Léitíng scriptures emerge: [[KR5a0016]] *Yùshū bǎojīng* and [[KR5a0015]] *Léitíng yùjīng*.
- **c. 1100–1125 (Huīzōng era)** — Lín Língsù and the Shénxiāo school expand the *Dùrén jīng* to sixty-one juan [[KR5a0001]].
- **1166–1294** — [[李嘉謀]] and Zhāng Shànyuān commentary cluster on the *Yuánshǐ xiāntiān dàodéjīng zhùjiě* [[KR5a0003]].
- **1173 (S. Sòng)** — [[翁葆光]] composes his *Wùzhēn piān* commentary, later re-edited as [[KR5a0142]].
- **1180–1240 (S. Sòng)** — Compilation of the *Yùhuáng běnxíng jíjīng* corpus [[KR5a0010]] [[KR5a0011]] and the *Yùhuáng xīnyìn* / *tāixí* short scriptures [[KR5a0013]] [[KR5a0014]].
- **c. 1194–1229 (S. Sòng)** — [[白玉蟾]]'s teaching gathered into the three Bái Yùchán collections [[KR5a0269]] [[KR5a0270]] [[KR5a0271]] of the *Xiūzhēn shíshū*.
- **1225–1229** — Compilation of the *Xiūzhēn shíshū* *Yùlóng jí* [[KR5a0269]]; the full ten-volume corpus assembled by the late Sòng / early Yuán.
- **c. 1280–1294 (Yuán)** — [[趙道一]] compiles the *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn* [[KR5a0308]] and *Hòují* [[KR5a0310]].
- **c. 1290 (Yuán)** — [[李道純]]'s syncretic *nèidān* writings; six titles in the division.
- **1328 (Yuán)** — [[劉大彬]]'s *Máoshān zhì* [[KR5a0316]].
- **c. 1335–1340 (Yuán)** — [[戴起宗]]'s edition of [[翁葆光]]'s *Wùzhēn piān* commentary [[KR5a0142]].
- **1300–1450 (Yuán–early Ming)** — *Lìshì zhēnxiān tǐdào tōngjiàn xùbiān* [[KR5a0309]] attributed to Zhào Dàoyī.
- **1368–1644 (Ming)** — Late additions: [[KR5a0027]] *Zhēnwǔ miàojīng*; [[KR5a0042]] *Yùqīng yuánshǐ xuánhuáng jiǔguāng zhēnjīng*; [[KR5a0078]] *Sāndòng shénzhòu* twelve-juan compilation.
- **1361–1410** — [[張宇初]] supervises the editorial preparation that becomes the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng*, fixing the form of [[KR5a0006]] and other texts in the division.
- **1445** — Promulgation of the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng*, in which the *Dòngzhēn bù* opens the entire canon.
