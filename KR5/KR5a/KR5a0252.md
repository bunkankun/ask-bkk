# Quánzhēn jí xuán bìyào 全真集玄祕要

*Secret Essentials of the Collected Quánzhēn Mysteries*

by [[李道純]] (撰, hào Qīngān 清庵, also Yíngchánzǐ 瑩蟾子, d. 1306)

## About the work

A one-juan Quánzhēn 全真 *nèidān* 內丹 commentary by Lǐ Dàochún 李道純 (d. 1306), preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0251 / CT 251 = TC 250), 洞真部 方法類. The work is composed of two distinct commentaries. The first (1a–6a), titled *Zhū dú Zhōuyì cāntóng qì* 註讀周易參同契 ("Reading-Notes on the *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì*"), is Lǐ's gloss on a section of Zhāng Bóduān's 張伯端 *Wùzhēn piān* 悟真篇 known as *Zhú dú Zhōuyì cāntóng qì* (cf. [[KR5c0141|DZ 141]] 8.1 and [[KR5c0145|DZ 145]] 3.3a) — Lǐ states it concerns the principles of fire-phasing (*huǒhòu* 火候). The second part (6a–18b) is a commentary on Zhōu Dūnyí's 周敦頤 (1017–1073) *Tàijí tú jiě* 太極圖解 ("Explanations on the Diagram of the Great Ultimate"). Throughout, Lǐ deploys a remarkably integrated *sānjiào* 三教 ("Three-Teachings") synthesis: he reads each successive line of Zhōu's *Tàijí* essay as encoding a stage of the *nèidān* process, drawing equally from the *Yìjīng* 易經, the *Lǎozǐ*, the Neo-Confucian thinkers Zhōu Dūnyí and Shào Yōng 邵雍, the *Wùzhēn piān*, and the *Cāntóng qì* 參同契. The work shows Lǐ's signature "Central Branch" hermeneutic of locating Quánzhēn *nèidān* on a continuum with the philosophical *Yìjīng* tradition of the Northern Sòng.

### Prefaces

*No preface in the source.* The text opens directly with its title-couplet "*Quánzhēn jí xuán bìyào* — by Qīngān Lǐ Dàochún composed."

### Abstract

Catherine Despeux, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:1175–1176 (§3.B.9, The Quánzhēn Order), identifies the work as comprising two parts: a commentary on a section of the *Wùzhēn piān* called *Zhú dú Zhōuyì cāntóng qì* (the principles of fire-phasing), and a commentary on Zhōu Dūnyí's *Tàijí tú jiě*. The author draws on the *Yìjīng*, the *Lǎozǐ*, the Neo-Confucian thinkers Zhōu Dūnyí and Shào Yōng, the *Wùzhēn piān*, and the *Cāntóng qì*. The work bears no internal date but must fall within Lǐ Dàochún's mature period of activity at the Hermitage of Central Harmony in Nánjīng; the frontmatter brackets composition ca. 1280–1306. The text complements Lǐ's *[[KR5a0251|Sāntiān yìsuǐ]]* (DZ 250) and *[[KR5a0250|Zhōnghé jí]]* (DZ 249) in articulating the *Yìjīng*-based *nèidān* hermeneutic of Lǐ's "Central Branch" (*Zhōngpài* 中派).
