# Huángdì yīnfú jīng zhù 黃帝陰符經註

*Commentary on the "Huángdì yīnfú jīng"*

Northern-Sòng court-Daoist commentary on the *Yīnfú jīng* by Huáng Jūzhēn 黃居真 (*hào* Xūjìng dàshī 虛靖大師), twelve folios, preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0116 / CT 116 = TC 115), 洞真部 本文類

## About the work

A twelve-folio commentary by the court Daoist Huáng Jūzhēn 黃居真 (*hào* Xūjìng dàshī 虛靖大師). According to the preface, the Queen Mother of the West (*Xī wángmǔ* 西王母), wishing to help humanity, revealed these instructions to the Yellow Emperor (Huángdì), who shaped them into the present book; *yīnfú* ("hidden correspondences or contracts") designates the consensus that characterised the meeting of Xī wángmǔ and Huángdì. The scripture is given in the short version (c. 300 words) without subdivision, and the three subtitles appear as integral parts of the commentary. The commentary provides brief definitions of the terminology.

### Abstract

Florian C. Reiter, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:695–696 (§3.A.1), dates the commentary to Huáng Jūzhēn's Northern-Sòng court-Daoist activity.
