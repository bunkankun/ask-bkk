# Tāixī jīng zhù 胎息經註

*Commentary on the Book of Embryonic Breathing*

by [[幻真先生]] (注)

## About the work

A three-folio late-Táng commentary on the one-folio [[KR5a0014|DZ 14 *Gāoshàng yùhuáng tāixī jīng*]], the "Book of Embryonic Breathing," transmitted as the work of Huànzhēn xiānshēng 幻真先生 ("Master Illusion-True"), a ninth-century specialist on respiratory cultivation whose real identity is unknown. The text is preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0130 / CT 130), 洞真部 玉訣類, and is appended in the same Dàozàng fascicle as two companion pieces, [[KR5a0132|DZ 131 *Tāixī bìyào gējué*]] and [[KR5a0133|DZ 132 *Tàiqīng zhēnrén luòmìng jué*]].

### Prefaces

*No preface in the source; the text opens directly with the root-scripture line 胎從伏氣中結 and its commentary, and closes with a short verse-inscription (胎息銘).*

### Abstract

The compilers of the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào* 四庫全書總目提要 considered Huànzhēn xiānshēng to be the author of the root scripture as well, but the *Tāixī jīng* also circulates independently (see [[KR5a0014|DZ 14]]) and in a slightly variant form in *Yúnjí qīqiān* 雲笈七籤 60.27a–28b; the commentary itself is found at YJQQ 60.14a–27a. Huànzhēn xiānshēng is generally identified with the author of [[KR5a0828|DZ 828 *Huànzhēn xiānshēng fúnà yuánqì jué*]] (the surface character 幼 in the DZ edition being a scribal error for 幻), a major Táng treatise on embryonic breathing; an attached preface dates that work to the Tiānbǎo era (742–756), but the same preface circulates with [[KR5a0824|DZ 824]] under a Dàlì era (766–779) date, casting doubt on the Tiānbǎo attribution. Farzeen Baldrian-Hussein, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 1:367–368 (§2.A.4.b, Respiratory Techniques), observes that the equation *tāixī* 胎息 = *nèidān* 內丹 on 1a and 3b, and the borrowing at 3a from [[KR5a0825|DZ 825 *Yánlíng xiānshēng jí xīnjiù fúqì jīng*]] 17b, point to late-Táng or even post-Táng compilation, probably before the *Yúnjí qīqiān*. The frontmatter brackets composition notBefore 850 / notAfter 900.
