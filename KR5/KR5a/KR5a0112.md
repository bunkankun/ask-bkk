# Huángdì yīnfú jīng jíjiě 黃帝陰符經集解

*Collected Explanations of the "Huángdì yīnfú jīng"*

Northern-Sòng collected-commentaries edition of the *Yīnfú jīng* by Cáo Xīyùn 曹希蘊 (*fl.* 11th c.), three juan, preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0112 / CT 112 = TC 111), 洞真部 本文類; also in *Dàozàng jíyào* JY115 under the title *Huángdì yīnfú jīng shízhēn jíjiě* 黃帝陰符經十真集解

## About the work

A three-juan Northern-Sòng collected-commentaries edition of the *[[KR5a0031|Huángdì yīnfú jīng]]* compiled by Cáo Xīyùn 曹希蘊 (*fl.* eleventh century), the notable Sòng poetess-and-Daoist. The text presents the short version of the scripture (c. 300 words) in three paragraphs. Under the *Dàozàng jíyào* title *Shízhēn jíjiě* 十真集解 ("Collected Explanations by the Ten Perfected") it presents commentary-strata attributed to ten Daoist authorities.

### Abstract

Florian C. Reiter, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:694 (§3.A.1), dates the compilation to Cáo Xīyùn's Northern-Sòng *floruit*. The frontmatter brackets composition notBefore 1000 / notAfter 1100, with dynasty 北宋. Cáo Xīyùn is the sole catalog-meta person wikilinked.
