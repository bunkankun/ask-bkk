# Tàishàng língbǎo yùguì míngzhēn dàzhāi chànfāng yí 太上靈寶玉匱明真大齋懺方儀
*Liturgy of Confession-by-Direction in the Great-High Língbǎo Jade-Casket Luminous-Perfected Great Fast*

by [[杜光庭]] (修)

## About the work

The "great-fast" (*dàzhāi* 大齋) expansion of the *Yùguì míngzhēn chànfāng yí* (cf. [[KR5b0222]]), again attributed to **Dù Guāngtíng** 杜光庭. In the canonical bundling the two share one physical fascicle (*二儀同卷化三*). Where [[KR5b0222]] gives the abbreviated everyday version, the present work is the full-scale directional confession for the climactic *dàzhāi* of the *Yùguì míngzhēn* programme.

### Abstract

The work is organised by direction: *dōngfāng* 東方, *nánfāng* 南方, *xīfāng* 西方, *běifāng* 北方, *shàngfāng* 上方, *xiàfāng* 下方, *dōngběi*, *dōngnán*, *xīnán*, *xīběi* — the ten directions of the Daoist mandala. For each direction the rite invokes by name and title the local infinite-Tàishàng-Língbǎo tiānzūn, the local *yǐdédào dàshèngzhòng zhìzhēn zūnshén* 巳得道大聖衆至眞尊神, and a long enumerated pantheon of subordinate deities — beginning with the *Tàiqīng Xuányuán wúshàng sāntiān wújí dàdào Wúshàng Xuányuán lǎojūn* 太清玄元無上三天無極大道無上玄元老君, the *Tàishàng zhàngrén* 太上丈人, the *Huángshàng lǎojūn* 皇上老君, the *Huángshàng zhàngrén* 皇上丈人, the *Qīnglíng shàngzhēn tiāndìjūn* 青靈上眞天帝君, etc. — and offers the confessional declaration. The east-facing first section names the *Hòushèng Dìjūn* 後聖帝君, the *Qīngtóngjūn* 青童君, the *Jīnquē hòushèng Dìjūn* 金闕後聖帝君, and the *Língbǎo jiǔxiānjūn* 靈寳九仙君 among others — a roster characteristic of Dù's *Língbǎo* synthesis. Per Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1083, John Lagerwey, DZ 520), the work is structurally the largest and ritually most elaborate of the three *Yùguì míngzhēn* liturgies.
