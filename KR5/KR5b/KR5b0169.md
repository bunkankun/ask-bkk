# Jīnlù dàzhāi qǐméng yí 金籙大齋啟盟儀
*Vow-Initiation Liturgy for the Great Golden-Register Fast*

## About the work

The third of the Dù Guāngtíng **Jīnlù** 金籙 cycle ([[KR5b0167]]–[[KR5b0181]]), traditionally transmitted with [[KR5b0168]] and [[KR5b0170]] as "three liturgies in one fascicle." The *qǐméng yí* 啟盟儀 ("vow-initiation liturgy") is the **opening vow ceremony**, in which the patron and the celebrants formally enter into the *méng* 盟 (covenant) with the gods that frames the multi-day rite. The text opens with the high-priest's seating, the *qǐtáng fǎshì* 啟堂法事 (hall-opening rite), the burning of the *Fúlí guónèi Yùjīngshān* 浮黎國内玉京山 incense, the *yīzhù* 一炷 of incense rising upward, and the formal *qǐméng* announcement to the *Sānqīng sānjìng shíjí shíhuá* — the Three Pure Ones, Three Realms, Ten Ultimates, and Ten Magnificents.

### Abstract

The *qǐméng* establishes the binding vow between human and divine that constitutes the metaphysical foundation of the *zhāi* rite proper. Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 994–996, entry by John Lagerwey) note that the *méng* 盟 concept in Daoism descends from the original *Zhèngyī méngwēi* 正一盟威 covenant attributed to Zhāng Dàolíng — the celestial-human compact at the foundation of Daoist religion — and is here renewed in the form appropriate to imperial high-state liturgy. The text is procedural and supplies the precise formulae of vow.

The text concludes with the formal call-down of the *Jīnlù dàzhāi tán yìnggǎn zhēnzǎi* 金籙大齋壇應感真宰 — the responsive deities of the Golden-Register altar, who will witness and validate the vow.
