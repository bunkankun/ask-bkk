# Shàngqīng pèi fúwén jiàngquàn jué 上清佩符文絳卷訣
*Instructions Concerning the Crimson-Register Talismans of the Upper Clarity*

## About the work

A four-folio Táng-era anthology of Shàngqīng protective talismans under the "crimson register" (*jiàngquàn* 絳卷) category. Third of the five-part *Pèi fúwén* series (DZ 412–416 = [[KR5b0096]]–[[KR5b0100]]), transmitted together in a composite *juàn*.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.*

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Schipper (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 614–615, group entry for DZ 412–416). See [[KR5b0096]] for the full anthology context: the colour *crimson* is associated in the Five-Agents scheme with the South and Fire, although as throughout this series the organisation-by-colour does not strictly govern the selection of the excerpts. The shortest of the five booklets.
