# Tàishàng shénzhòu yánshòu miàojīng 太上神咒延壽妙經
*Marvelous Scripture of the Divine Incantations for Prolonging Life, of the Most High*

## About the work

A short two-folio Táng apotropaic-invocatory text on prolonging the life-count (*yánshòu* 延壽) through the invocation of the stellar generals. Transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* with DZ 357, DZ 359, and DZ 360 ([[KR5b0041]], [[KR5b0043]], [[KR5b0044]]).

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with the Yuánshǐ tiānzūn's salvific formula and carries no author preface or transmission colophon.

### Abstract

Schmidt (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 2: 561, DZ 358) dates the scripture to the Táng and demonstrates that it is an abridged and popularised recension of DZ 650 *Tàishàng lǎojūn shuō chángshēng yìsuàn miàojīng* 太上老君說長生益算妙經 or DZ 672 *Tàishàng lǎojūn shuō yìsuàn shénfú miàojīng* 太上老君說益算神符妙經. Unlike the parent scriptures, the present text does not enumerate the generals of the six cyclical *jiǎ* 甲 combinations individually, and the *fú* 符 that are elaborately described and drawn in DZ 650 / DZ 672 are here only briefly mentioned. Emphasis falls instead on the efficacy of simple recitation of the short present text — a characteristic late-Táng popularisation strategy that trades procedural complexity for reproducible scriptural merit.
