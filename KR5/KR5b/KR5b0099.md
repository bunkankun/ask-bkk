# Shàngqīng pèi fúwén hēiquàn jué 上清佩符文黑卷訣
*Instructions Concerning the Black-Register Talismans of the Upper Clarity*

## About the work

A three-folio Táng-era anthology of Shàngqīng protective talismans under the "black register" (*hēiquàn* 黑卷) category. Fourth of the five-part *Pèi fúwén* series (DZ 412–416 = [[KR5b0096]]–[[KR5b0100]]), transmitted together in a composite *juàn*.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.*

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Schipper (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 614–615, group entry for DZ 412–416). See [[KR5b0096]] for the full anthology context: the colour *black* is associated in the Five-Agents scheme with the North and Water. The excerpts are drawn from major Shàngqīng scriptures.
