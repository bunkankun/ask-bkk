# Yùlù dàzhāi dìsānrì zǎocháo yí 玉籙大齋第三日早朝儀
*Morning-Audience Liturgy of the Third Day of the Great Jade-Register Fast*

## About the work

Seventh of the eight-part **Yùlù dàzhāi** programme (DZ 505 a–h). The morning audience of day 3 opens the concluding day of the fast, the day on which the cumulative merit of the preceding two days of *zhuǎnjīng* is dedicated to the deceased and the rite is concluded with the *jìyōu pànhú* 濟幽判斛 closing salvation ([[KR5b0209]]).

### Abstract

The third-day morning audience follows the established frame (*fālú*, *qǐngchēng fǎwèi*, three *niǎnxiāng*) but with significant new ritual elements directed to the closing of the rite: the *xuānbǎng* 宣牓 (public proclamation of the rite's accomplishment), the *zhāozhào* 召召 summoning of the named *gūyōu* by name, and a preparatory *kāiyù* 開獄 (opening of the underworld prisons) sequence that anticipates the final *pànhú* judgment. The merit of the day is dedicated specifically to the release of the named souls and to the imperial *zōngtiào*; the orientation is by this point fully toward salvific deliverance.
