# Shàngqīng pèi fúwén báiquàn jué 上清佩符文白卷訣
*Instructions Concerning the White-Register Talismans of the Upper Clarity*

## About the work

An eleven-folio Táng-era anthology of Shàngqīng protective talismans under the "white register" (*báiquàn* 白卷) category. Second of the five-part *Pèi fúwén* series (DZ 412–416 = [[KR5b0096]]–[[KR5b0100]]), transmitted together in a composite *juàn*.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.*

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Schipper (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 614–615, group entry for DZ 412–416). See [[KR5b0096]] for the full anthology context: the five *Pèi fúwén* booklets together form an anthology of excerpts from the major Shàngqīng scriptures concerning protective talismans. The color-organisation (here *white*, associated in the Five-Agents scheme with the West and Metal) does not in fact correspond to any tight functional or directional criterion within the excerpts. Originally probably accompanied by talisman drawings, now mostly lost.
