# Shàngqīng zǐwēi dìjūn nánjí yuánjūn yùjīng bǎojué 上清紫微帝君南極元君玉經寶訣
*Precious Instructions of the Jade Scripture by the Zǐwēi dìjūn and the Nánjí yuánjūn, of the Upper Clarity*

## About the work

A ten-folio Táng Shàngqīng text almost entirely composed of quotations from earlier Shàngqīng scriptures. Transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* with DZ 407 ([[KR5b0091]]).

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with its quoted materials and carries no author preface or transmission colophon.

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Schipper (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 614, DZ 406). Almost the entire text consists of quotations from earlier Shàngqīng scriptures — principally DZ 405 *Shàngqīng zǐjīngjūn huángchū zǐlíng dàojūn dòngfáng shàngjīng* 4a–12a, the immediately preceding text in the *Dàozàng*. Only the sub-title (7a) and the closing few lines of instructions for the ritual of homage on entering the Pure Chamber (*jìngshì* 靜室) are original additions by the Táng compiler.

The work is one of a cluster of late-Shàngqīng / early-Táng summary-compendia of the Shàngqīng cult-practices — serving as a memorandum-book for the practitioner rather than as an independent revealed scripture.
