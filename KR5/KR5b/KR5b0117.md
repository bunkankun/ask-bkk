# Qīyù xiūzhēn zhèngpǐn tú 七域修真證品圖
*Diagram Demonstrating the Hierarchy of Degrees of the Practice of the True [Dào] and of the Seven Regions [of the Immortals]*

## About the work

An eight-folio Táng Shàngqīng diagrammatic chart of the spiritual-hierarchical *pǐn* 品 grades of Daoist practitioners, correlated with the *Qīyù* 七域 ("Seven Regions") of the celestial immortals. Transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* (*èr tú tóng juàn* 二圖同卷) with DZ 434 *Xuánlǎn Rénniǎo shān jīngtú* ([[KR5b0118]]).

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with the hierarchical schema.

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Robinet (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 617, DZ 433). The text (3a–5b) incorporates a list of Shàngqīng methods taken from DZ 1016 *Zhēn'gào* 5.3a–b, to which it couples a hierarchy of the spiritual degrees of practitioners of the Shàngqīng school. The description of the degrees follows the pattern established in DZ 426 *Shàngqīng tàishàng bāsù zhēnjīng* 4a–5b and 8a–b ([[KR5b0110]]) and in DZ 428 *Tàishàng fēixíng jiǔchén yùjīng* 2b–3a ([[KR5b0112]]). The Seven Regions are:

1. the Yùqīng 玉清 Heaven;
2. the Shàngqīng 上清 Heaven;
3. the Tàijí 太極 Heaven;
4. the Tàiqīng 太清 Heaven;
5. the Nine Palaces of the celestial immortals;
6. the Nine Palaces of the terrestrial immortals;
7. the cavern-heavens (*dòngtiān* 洞天).

Categories 6 and 7 are additions to the four- and seven-way schemata of DZ 426 and DZ 428, characteristic of late-Táng Shàngqīng systematisation.
