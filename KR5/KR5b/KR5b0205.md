# Yùlù dàzhāi dìèrrì wǔcháo yí 玉籙大齋第二日午朝儀
*Noon-Audience Liturgy of the Second Day of the Great Jade-Register Fast*

## About the work

Fifth of the eight-part **Yùlù dàzhāi** programme (DZ 505 a–h). The noon audience of day 2 stands at the geometric centre of the three-day, nine-audience fast — the *zhōngyuán* 中元 of the *zhōngrì* 中日 — and is correspondingly identified in the *Yùlù* liturgical theology with the apex of the salvific deliverance, the moment at which the merit of all preceding *huí* is consolidated and offered up.

### Abstract

Following the standard noon framework (*fālú*, declaration of ritual rank, three *niǎnxiāng*), the body of this fascicle carries the central three *huí* (4–6) of the nine-fold *zhuǎnjīng* sequence introduced at the morning audience ([[KR5b0204]]). The cosmological orientation is to the four corners — northeast, southeast, southwest, northwest — completing with the morning's three a six-direction grid; the final ninth *huí* (the central column) is reserved for the evening audience ([[KR5b0206]]). The *huíxiàng* of this audience is the most expansive of the cycle, dedicating the cumulative merit not only to the named beneficiary but to all *sānjiè* 三界 sentient beings.
