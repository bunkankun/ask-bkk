# Shàngqīng gāoshàng yùzhēn zhòngdào zōngjiān bǎohuì 上清高上玉真眾道綜監寶諱
*Comprehensive List of Precious Taboo Names of the Exalted Jade Perfected of the Upper Clarity*

## About the work

A seven-folio Táng Shàngqīng onomastic register (*bǎohuì* 寶諱 = precious taboo-names) of the celestial deities, transmitted with an excerpt from the *Purple Phoenix Register* (*Zǐfèng lù* 紫鳳籙) and regulations for transmission. Transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* with DZ 442 ([[KR5b0126]]).

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with the list of taboo-names and carries no author preface.

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Schipper (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 2: 680, DZ 443). The text opens with a list of taboo-names of various Shàngqīng deities (Liù jiǎ jūn 六甲君, Nányuè jūn 南嶽君, Běihuáng shū jūn 北黃書君, Tàijí jūn 太極君, Wànfú jūn 萬福君, Chìjiè jūn 赤界君, Qīngtóng jūn 青童君, Zhēndǒu jūn 真斗君, Dàdù jūn 大度君, etc.), followed by an excerpt from a *Zǐfèng lù* 紫鳳籙. Both the title of the book and the *Zǐfèng lù* citation indicate a relationship to the Shàngqīng tradition — especially to DZ 1372 *Shàngqīng gāoshàng yùchén fēngtái qǔsù shàngjīng* and to the *Zǐfèng chìshū* 紫鳳赤書, mentioned in the *Jiǔzhēn míngkē* (DZ 1314 *Dòngzhēn tàishàng sùlíng dòngyuán dàyǒu miàojīng* 51b, 52a) and cited in DZ 412 *Shàngqīng pèi fúwén qīngquàn jué* 2a ([[KR5b0096]]) and *Yúnjí qīqiān* 7.7a, 9.11b. Sòng catalogues list a *Dòngzhēn lóngjīng jiǔwén zǐfèng chìshū* in one *juàn* (VDL 122).

In the present text, however, the *Zǐfèng lù* is said to have been transmitted along with the *Bāwēi cèwén* 八威策文 — a text originally associated with the Língbǎo tradition (cf. DZ 352 *Tàishàng dòngxuán língbǎo chìshū yùjué miàojīng* 1.24b–26a = [[KR5b0036]]). The next section (4a–6b) is reported as deriving from chapter 75 of the *Jīnguāng míng jīng* of the *Sānhuáng tiānwén dàzì* 三皇天文大字. The closing account rehearses the regulations for the transmission of the book.

The text is characteristic of Táng Shàngqīng scholastic onomastic-compendium production — a blending of registers from the three *Sāndòng* traditions into an integrated esoteric-name inventory.
