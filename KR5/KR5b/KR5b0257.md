# Língbǎo jìngmíng Tiānshūyuàn dūsī fǎyuàn xūzhī fǎwén 靈寶淨明天樞院都司法院須知法文
*Method-Text to Be Known by the Law-Court of the Director-General's Office of the Língbǎo Jìng-míng Heavenly-Pivot Bureau*

## About the work

A short single-juǎn ritual handbook concluding the four-text *Tiānshūyuàn* cluster of *Jìngmíng* 淨明 manuals (DZ 549 / [[KR5b0253]] – DZ 553). Where the preceding *Lìng* ([[KR5b0255]]) and *Gé* ([[KR5b0256]]) fix the Bureau's administrative statutes and penal regulations, this manual gives the practical *fǎwén* 法文 — the methodical text of the rite — by which an officer of the *Dūsī* internally cultivates his office.

### Abstract

The opening section, *Tiānshū dūlù xíngchí fǎ* 天樞都録行持法 ("Procedure for the Maintenance of the Office, by the Director-Registrar of the Heavenly-Pivot"), declares the bureaucratic ethos of the Bureau: as *Tiānménsī yuàn* 天門司院, "Court-Office of the Heaven-Gate", it speaks for Heaven (*shàngtiān hóushé* 上天喉舌), superintends the seventy-three sub-offices plus the seven *lùshì* (so "seventy-four offices" *qīshísì sī* 七十四司), and audits the *yīnyáng* good-and-evil records of the Three Worlds. The text then turns to the internal-cultivation methods proper: the *yǎngshén fǎ* 養神法 (sunrise visualisation of the sun's red qì entering the crown and pairing with the moon's white qì in the breast to form an inner cinnabar pellet), the *bìqì* 閉炁 visualisations involving the Western Queen Mother and the *Yínhàn* 銀漢 (Silver River = Milky Way), and so on.

The text shares with [[KR5b0254]] (DZ 550) the *Shàngqīng* inner-anatomy vocabulary — *níwán* 泥丸, *jiànggōng* 絳宮 — and provides what Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1092–1093, John Lagerwey) describe as the technical *liànjīng* 鍊精 underpinning of the Bureau's executive office. The whole forms one of the most explicit accounts of how a Sòng-Yuán *fǎshī* 法師 was expected to internalise the celestial bureaucratic role in his own body.
