# Tàishàng dòngxuán sāndòng kāitiān fēngléi yǔbù zhìmó shénzhòu jīng 太上洞玄三洞開天風雷禹步制魔神咒經
*Scripture of Divine Incantations and the Paces of Yǔ for Opening Heaven, Marshalling Wind and Thunder, and Dominating Demons: A Liturgical Three-Caverns Text of the Cavern-Mystery Division of the Most High*

## About the work

A four-folio early Daoist thunder-magic scripture on the Thunder Gods (*léishén* 雷神) and their role in exorcism. Transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* with DZ 386 ([[KR5b0070]]). A major early witness to the pre-*wǔléi* 五雷 stratum of Daoist thunder-rites.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with Yùhuáng's address to Shuǐfǔ fúsāng dàdì and carries no author preface or transmission colophon.

### Abstract

Dated tentatively to the Táng (618–907) by Schipper (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 2: 572, DZ 385). The text is spoken by Yùhuáng 玉皇 (*Hàotiān yùhuáng shàngdì* 昊天玉皇上帝) to Shuǐfǔ fúsāng dàdì 水府扶桑大帝 in the presence of all the *bodhisattva* (*zhūtiān púsà* 諸天菩薩), with Yùhuáng rehearsing his own legend at 1b–2a. Despite the title's mention of the Paces of Yǔ (*Yǔbù* 禹步), the received text does not actually describe them; it offers instead a spell to conjure the Thunder Gods and *yakṣa* (*yèchā* 夜叉, 2b). The combination of Yùhuáng–*Hàotiān* theology, the Thunder-Gods pantheon, and the *bodhisattva* audience together indicate a Táng date.

The text is re-used and recast in the Southern Sòng *Tàiyī tiānzhāng jīléi pīlì jīng* 太乙天章紀雷霹靂經, preserved in DZ 1220 *Dàofǎ huìyuán* 140.10a–14b under the title *Hàotiān yùhuáng shàngdì bìshòu yǔbù zhìléi shénzhòu jīng* 昊天玉皇上帝祕授禹步制雷神咒經, to match the Five-Thunder Magic (*wǔléi fǎ* 五雷法) of the mature *Tàiyī tiānzhāng yánglèi pīlì dàfǎ*. The present DZ 385 is therefore one of the key earlier witnesses to the vocabulary of later Daoist thunder-rites.
