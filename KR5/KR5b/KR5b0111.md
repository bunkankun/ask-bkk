# Shàngqīng xiūxíng jīng jué 上清修行經訣
*Formulary for Shàngqīng Practices*

## About the work

A twenty-nine-folio Táng Shàngqīng anthology of practical prescriptions — an authentic formulary in which each practice is preceded by a title with a gloss indicating the source chapter.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with the first exercise — *Jū sānhún fǎ* 拘三魂法 ("Method for Binding the Three Souls"), with a source-gloss attributing it to the *Shàngjīng língshū zǐwén* 上經靈書紫文 — and carries no author preface.

### Abstract

Dated to the Táng by Robinet (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 616, DZ 427). The work is an explicit source-catalogue: each practice bears its source attribution in a small-character note. With the exception of one passage (10b–11b) drawn from a Língbǎo scripture (DZ 352 *Chìshū yùjué miàojīng* 1.25b and 24a), every other text in the collection comes from Shàngqīng sources:

- DZ 1016 *Zhēn'gào* 真誥 (the first page — without attribution);
- DZ 639 *Huángtiān shàngqīng jīnquè dìjūn língshū zǐwén shàngjīng*;
- DZ 1313 *Dòngzhēn gāoshàng yùdì dàdòng cíyī yùjiǎn wǔlǎo bǎojīng*;
- DZ 1380 *Shàngqīng tàishàng huángsù sìshísì fāng jīng*;
- DZ 1315 *Dòngzhēn shàngqīng qīngyào zǐshū jīngēn zhòngjīng*;
- DZ 1314 *Dòngzhēn tàishàng sùlíng dòngyuán dàyǒu miàojīng*;
- DZ 1378 *Shàngqīng jīnzhēn yùguāng bājǐng fēijīng*;
- DZ 1379 *Shàngqīng yùdì qīshèng xuánjī huítiān jiǔxiāo jīng*.

The final paragraph (25b to the end) cites a *Xiāomó jīng* 消魔經 — a citation not found in any extant work of that title but paralleled in *Yúnjí qīqiān* 47.10b–11a.

The text is a valuable specimen of Táng Shàngqīng scholastic anthologising: a working practitioner's reference-book grouping key exercises with precise source-attribution.
