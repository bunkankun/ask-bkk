# Shàngqīng tāijīng jì jiějié xíngshì jué 上清胎精記解結行事訣
*Formula for the Practice of Untying the Knots from the Records of Embryonic Essence, of the Upper Clarity*

## About the work

A fourteen-folio early-Shàngqīng ritual manual for the "untying of the embryonic knots" (*jiějié* 解結) — the loosening of the karmic bonds that form during gestation. Transmitted in the *Dàozàng* in a composite *juàn* with DZ 409 ([[KR5b0093]]). Position 20 of the *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng mù*.

### Prefaces

*No prefaces in the source.* The text opens directly with the ritual sequence and carries no author preface or transmission colophon.

### Abstract

Dated by Robinet (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 165–166, DZ 408) to the Eastern Jìn — within or immediately after the Yáng Xī revelations (364–370) of the original Shàngqīng stratum. The text is closely cognate with DZ 1382 *Shàngqīng jiǔdān shànghuà tāijīng zhōngjì jīng* 上清九丹上化胎精中記經, of which it reproduces the essential passages: 1a–12b, 12b–13a, and 13b–14 of the present text correspond respectively to 3a–15b, 22a–b, and 26a of DZ 1382. Both texts represent position 20 of the *Shàngqīng dàdòng zhēnjīng mù* list.

The ritual's substance is the undoing, through visualisation and *qì*-circulation, of the twelve "knots" (*jié* 結) that are considered to have formed during the twelve months of gestation — these knots being the somatic manifestation of karmic bonds inherited from previous existences and the source of later disease and death. The *Jiějié* rite is one of the cornerstones of Shàngqīng internal liturgy and remains central to the later Sòng *liàndù* 鍊度 mortuary tradition.
