# Shàngqīng tàishàng bāsù zhēnjīng 上清太上八素真經
*True Scripture of the Eight Purities, of the Most High Upper Clarity*

## About the work

A twenty-eight-folio Shàngqīng scripture on the eight female celestial deities called the Eight Purities (*bāsù* 八素). The received text is incomplete and appears originally to have formed part of DZ 1323 *Dòngzhēn tàishàng bāsù zhēnjīng jūshì rìyuè huánghuā jué* 洞真太上八素真經居室日月皇華訣.

### Prefaces

The work opens with a brief transmission genealogy titled *Tàishàng yǐnshū* 太上隱書: the *Bāsù zhēnjīng* is the Way of the Xuánqīng Yùhuáng 玄清玉皇; it was first transmitted by the *Shànghuáng tiāndì* 上皇天帝 to the *Tàiwēi tiāndì jūn* 太微天帝君, the *Sānyuán zǐjīng dàojūn* 三元紫精道君, and the *Zhēnyáng yuánlǎo jūn* 真陽元老君; thence to the Tàishàng dàojūn 太上道君, the Jīnquè hòushèng Lǐ jūn 金闕後聖李君, and so on.

### Abstract

Dated to the Eastern Jìn by Robinet (Schipper & Verellen, *Taoist Canon* 1: 142–143, DZ 426) within the original Shàngqīng revelation period. The stanzas titled *Tāngē jiǔzhāng* 湯歌九章 (cf. 13b), found in *Wúshàng bìyào* 20.4b–6b (partially corresponding to DZ 303 *Zǐyáng zhēnrén nèizhuàn* 17a–18a), once formed part of this work but are missing from the received text.

The present text divides into three parts. The first (4a–9b) presents a hierarchical list of Shàngqīng texts, indicating to which celestial eminences and heavens their practice gives access; this same list is found in *Yúnjí qīqiān* 6.6b–8a and in DZ 428 *Tàishàng fēixíng jiǔchén yùjīng* 2b–4a ([[KR5b0112]]). The work is a foundational document of the Shàngqīng tradition's internal hierarchical self-understanding of its scriptural corpus.
