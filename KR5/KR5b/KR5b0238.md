# Tàishàng língbǎo xiàyuán shuǐguān xiāoqiān mièzuì chàn 太上靈寶下元水官消愆滅罪懺
*Confession-Liturgy of the Lower Prime Watery Bureau for the Effacement of Offences and Extinction of Faults in the Great-High Língbǎo*

## About the work

Third and final member of the *Sānguān chàn* triad (cf. [[KR5b0236]], [[KR5b0237]]); the three texts share a single physical fascicle. The work addresses the *Xiàyuán shuǐguān* 下元水官, the Daoist Bureau of the Watery realm.

### Abstract

The opening narrative places the revelation in the *Jīn-líng dòng-yīn zhī guǎn* 金靈洞隂之舘 (Pavilion of the Golden Numen and Cavernous Yin), where *Yuán-shǐ tiān-zūn* expounds the teaching. A *zhēn-rén* named *Míng Luó Yuè* 明羅越 — a deity unattested elsewhere, possibly a Daoist Sinicisation of a Buddhist *yakṣa* or *deva* name — comes forward and addresses the Tiān-zūn, asking for instruction on the "great and small nine-and-six calamities" (*dà-xiǎo jiǔ-liù zhī zāi* 大小九六之災) that have not yet been resolved. The Tiān-zūn replies that "the four seasons revolve, the yin-yang and *lǜlǚ* circulate — all this is entrusted to the *Tiān-dì-shuǐ sān-guān* 天地水三官. As for the four seas, three rivers, mountain streams, springs, ponds, lakes, banks, embankments, isles, sluices, streams, fountains, the aquatic creatures — fish, dragons, river-turtles, alligators, sea-turtles, soft-shell turtles, all the moisture-dwelling species — and the annual harvests, the people's joys and sorrows, all are committed to the *Xiàyuán shuǐguān* for review and judgment."

The body of the work is the confession addressed to the Watery Bureau, performed on *xiàyuán* 下元 (15th day of the 10th month), the bureau's day in the calendar.

Per Schipper & Verellen (*Taoist Canon* 2: 1024–1025, John Lagerwey, DZ 535), the watery-bureau jurisdiction over aquatic life and harvests is a distinctively Daoist development of the older *Sānguān* schema.
