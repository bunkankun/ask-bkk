# Sōu shén jì 搜神記

*In Search of Spirits*

Anonymous (traditional attribution Gān Bǎo 干寶); Eastern Jìn

The great Eastern-Jìn ***Sōu shén jì*** 搜神記 of **Gān Bǎo** 干寶 (fl. 317–350) — the foundational collection of *zhì guài* 志怪 ("records of anomalies") tales, foundational for the Chinese supernatural narrative tradition. The Dàozàng text is a shorter recension. Preserved as **DZ 1476** / CT 1476 (續道藏). (DZ 1475 omitted in the canonical sequence.)

**Dating.** Eastern Jìn. Dynasty: 東晉.
