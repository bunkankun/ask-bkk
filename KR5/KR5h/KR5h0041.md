# Dài shǐ 岱史

*History of Mount Dài (Tàishān)*

by [[查志隆]] (Zhā Zhìlóng)

A Míng comprehensive gazetteer of **Tài shān** 泰山 — the chief of the Five Sacred Peaks, centre of the Dōng yuè 東嶽 cult. Preserved as **DZ 1472** / CT 1472 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
