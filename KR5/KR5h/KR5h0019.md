# Zhōng tiān zǐ wēi xīng zhēn bǎo chàn 中天紫微星真寶懺

*Precious Repentance-Liturgy of the Central-Heaven Purple-Pole Star*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng repentance liturgy (*chàn* 懺) for the **Zǐ wēi** 紫微 constellation (Purple-Pole). Preserved as **DZ 1450** / CT 1450 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
