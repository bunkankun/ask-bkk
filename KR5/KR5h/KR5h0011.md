# Tài shàng sān yuán cì fú shè zuì jiě è xiāo zāi yán shēng bǎo mìng miào jīng 太上三元賜福赦罪解厄消災延生保命妙經

*Most High Three-Origins' Wondrous Scripture for Bestowing Blessings, Pardoning Sins, Dissolving Calamities, Dispelling Disasters, Prolonging Life, and Protecting Destiny*

Anonymous; Southern Sòng

A widely-used popular Daoist scripture for the **Three Origins** (*sān yuán* 三元 — Upper, Middle, Lower Origin festivals) invoked for blessings. Preserved as **DZ 1442** / CT 1442 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
