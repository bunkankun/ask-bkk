# Rú mén chóng lǐ zhé zhōng kān yú wán xiào lù 儒門崇理折衷堪輿完孝錄

*Record of Perfected-Filial Geomancy by the Confucian Gate's Principle-Reverence*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng **Confucian-Daoist geomantic-filial compendium** — a syncretic work combining Confucian filial ethics with Daoist geomancy (*kān yú* 堪輿 = *fēng shuǐ*). Preserved as **DZ 1471** / CT 1471 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
