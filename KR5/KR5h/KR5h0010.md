# Yuán shǐ tiān zūn shuō Dōng yuè huà shēn jì shēng dù sǐ bá zuì jiě yuān bǎo mìng xuán fàn gào zhòu miào jīng 元始天尊說東嶽化身濟生度死拔罪解冤保命玄範誥咒妙經

*Yuánshǐ's Scripture on the Dōngyuè Transformation-Body for Saving Life, Crossing-Over the Dead, Uprooting Sins, Dissolving Grievances, and Protecting Destiny*

Anonymous; Sòng-Míng

A **Dōng yuè** 東嶽 (Eastern Peak / Tài shān) cult scripture for salvation of the living and dead. Preserved as **DZ 1441** / CT 1441 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Sòng-Míng. Dynasty: 宋-明.
