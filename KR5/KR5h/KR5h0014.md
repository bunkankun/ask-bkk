# Bì xiá yuán jūn hù guó bì mín pǔ jì bǎo shēng miào jīng 碧霞元君護國庇民普濟保生妙經

*Scripture of Bìxiá Yuánjūn (Princess of Azure-Dawn) Protecting State and Sheltering People*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng scripture of **Bì xiá yuán jūn** 碧霞元君 — the fertility-goddess of Tài shān, one of the most popular late-imperial female deities. Preserved as **DZ 1445** / CT 1445 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
