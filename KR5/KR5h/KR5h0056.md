# Lǎo zǐ yì 老子翼

*Wings to the Lǎo zǐ*

by [[焦竤]] (Jiāo Hóng, 1540–1620)

**The standard late-Míng collected-commentary edition of the *Lǎo zǐ*** — Jiāo Hóng's synthetic anthology assembling the chief commentarial traditions. A fundamental reference for premodern *Lǎo zǐ* scholarship. Preserved as **DZ 1486** / CT 1486 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Late Míng. Dynasty: 明.
