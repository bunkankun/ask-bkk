# Tài shàng dà shèng Lǎng líng shàng jiāng hù guó miào jīng 太上大聖朗靈上將護國妙經

*Most High Great-Sage Lǎnglíng Supreme-General Protecting-State Wondrous Scripture*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng scripture of the **Lǎng líng Supreme General** 朗靈上將 — traditionally identified with Guān Yǔ 關羽. Preserved as **DZ 1446** / CT 1446 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
