# Yuán shǐ tiān zūn shuō Yào wáng jiù bā shí yī nàn zhēn jīng 元始天尊說藥王救八十一難真經

*Yuánshǐ's Scripture of the Medicine-King Rescuing from Eighty-One Calamities*

Anonymous; Míng

A scripture of the **Yào wáng** 藥王 (Medicine-King, often identified with Sūn Sīmiǎo) cult. Preserved as **DZ 1444** / CT 1444 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
