# Xiāo yáo xū jīng 消搖墟經

*Scripture of the Carefree Ruins*

by [[洪應明]] (Hóng Yìngmíng) — late-Míng syncretic writer

A late-Míng Daoist-syncretic anthology. Title alludes to the *Zhuāng zǐ* "Xiāo yáo yóu" 逍遙遊. Preserved as **DZ 1465** / CT 1465 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Late Míng. Dynasty: 明.
