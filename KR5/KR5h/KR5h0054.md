# Lǚ zǔ zhì 呂祖志

*Record of Patriarch Lǚ*

Anonymous; Míng

A Míng **hagiographical compilation** of [[呂洞賓]] Lǚ Dòngbīn — reflecting the late-imperial Lǚ zǔ cult's enormous popularity. Preserved as **DZ 1484** / CT 1484 (續道藏).

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.

## Translations and research

- Katz, Paul R. *Images of the Immortal: The Cult of Lü Dongbin at the Palace of Eternal Joy*. University of Hawai'i Press, 1999.
