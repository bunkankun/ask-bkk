# Yǐn wén zǐ 尹文子

*Master Yǐn Wén*

attributed to [[尹文]] (Yǐn Wén)

A classical **Míngjiā** (School of Names) / HuángLǎo text attributed to Yǐn Wén. The received 2-chapter text is likely a Hàn compilation. Preserved as **DZ 1173** / CT 1173 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Warring-States to Hàn. Dynasty: 戰國-漢.
