# Tiān yuán fā wēi 天原發微

*Subtle Exposition of the Heaven-Origin*

by [[鮑雲龍]] (Bào Yúnlóng, 1226–1296)

A Southern-Sòng / Yuán **cosmological-diagrammatic treatise** on the origin of heaven, drawing on *Yì jīng*, Shào Yōng, and Zhū Xī. Preserved as **DZ 1182** / CT 1182 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Late 13th century. Dynasty: 南宋-元.
