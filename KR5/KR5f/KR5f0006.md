# Gōngsūn Lóng zǐ 公孫龍子

*Master Gōngsūn Lóng*

attributed to [[公孫龍]] (Gōngsūn Lóng)

**The foundational text of the Chinese School of Names** (*Míng jiā* 名家) — Gōngsūn Lóng's treatise on logical-semantic paradoxes. Contains the famous "**white horse is not horse**" (*bái mǎ fēi mǎ* 白馬非馬) and "**pointing to non-pointing**" (*zhǐ wù lùn* 指物論) arguments. The received 6-chapter text is a Hàn compilation. Preserved as **DZ 1172** / CT 1172 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Core Warring-States, received recension Hàn. Dynasty: 戰國-漢.
