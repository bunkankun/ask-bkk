# Hán fēi zǐ 韓非子

*Master Hán Fēi*

attributed to [[韓非]] (Hán Fēi, c. 280–233 BCE)

**The foundational text of Chinese Legalism** — Hán Fēi's systematic synthesis of the Warring-States *Fǎ jiā* 法家 traditions. Fifty-five essays on law, statecraft, and political position, including celebrated philosophical-literary chapters (*Jiě Lǎo* 解老, *Yù Lǎo* 喻老, *Wǔ dù* 五蠹, *Nán sān* 難三, *Shuì nán* 說難). Preserved as **DZ 1177** / CT 1177 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Core essays late Warring-States; compilation Hàn. Dynasty: 戰國-漢.
