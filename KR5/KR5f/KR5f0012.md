# Huáng shí gōng sù shū (yī) 黃石公素書(一)

*Huángshígōng's Plain Book (Recension I)*

commentary by [[魏魯]] (Wèi Lǔ) — Sòng commentator

The ***Sù shū*** 素書 is a Sòng-era military-strategic classic pseudepigraphically ascribed to the Hàn immortal **Huáng shí gōng** 黃石公 — traditional transmitter of the *Tài gōng Liù tāo* 太公六韜 to Zhāng Liáng 張良 at the Pǐ qiáo 圯橋. The text is almost certainly a Sòng fabrication by Zhāng Shāngyīng (see [[KR5f0013]]). First of two Dào zàng recensions. Preserved as **DZ 1178** / CT 1178 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Sòng (received text). Dynasty: 宋.
