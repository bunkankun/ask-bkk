# Zhū zhēn gē sòng 諸真歌頌

*Hymns of Sundry Perfected*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **collection of hymns** (*gē sòng* 歌頌) of various Daoist perfected (*zhū zhēn* 諸真). Preserved as **DZ 980** / CT 980 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
