# Xǔ zhēn jūn shí hán jì 許真君石函記

*Record of Perfected Xǔ's Stone Casket*

attributed to [[許遜]] (Xǔ Xùn, Xǔ zhēn jūn, trad. 239–374)

A Southern-Sòng Daoist **alchemical-ritual text** pseudepigraphically attributed to **Xǔ Xùn** 許遜 (Xǔ zhēn jūn), the Eastern-Jìn immortal venerated as patriarch of the Jìng míng 淨明 school at Xī shān 西山 (Jiāngxī). The "stone casket" (*shí hán* 石函) framing device represents the scripture as recovered from a hidden receptacle. Preserved as **DZ 951** / CT 951 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類) in 2 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
