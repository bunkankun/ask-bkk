# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn nián pǔ yào lüè 太上老君年譜要略

*Summary Chronological Table of the Most High Lord Lǎo*

by [[謝守灝]] (Xiè Shǒuhào, 1134–1212); companion to [[KR5c0166]]

A **chronological-table** (*nián pǔ* 年譜) summary of Lǎojūn's manifestations and deeds across the ages. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 771** / CT 771, and in the Qīng *Dàozàng jíyào* as JY085. Companion work to *[[KR5c0166|Hùn yuán shèng jì]]* (DZ 770).

## About the work

The *Nián pǔ yào lüè* provides a **condensed chronological presentation** of the fuller *Hùn yuán shèng jì* material — giving dates, events, and key hagiographic episodes in tabular-summary form. It functions as a **reference companion** to the main chronicle.

**Dating.** Within Xiè Shǒuhào's active career (1191–1212). Dynasty: 宋.
