# Běi jí Zhēn wǔ yòu shèng zhēn jūn lǐ wén 北極真武佑聖真君禮文

*Ritual-Text for the North-Polar Zhēn wǔ, the Sage-Protecting Perfected Lord*

Anonymous; SòngYuán

A Zhēn wǔ ritual-text (*lǐ wén* 禮文) using his post-1108 imperial title **佑聖真君** *Yòu shèng zhēn jūn* ("Sage-Protecting Perfected Lord", conferred by Sòng Huīzōng in 1108 — see [[KR5c0172]]). Preserved as **DZ 816** / CT 816.

**Dating.** Post-1108. Dynasty: 宋-元.
