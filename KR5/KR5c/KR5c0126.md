# Zhuāngzǐ 莊子

*Master Zhuāng* — the classical Daoist text

attributed to [[莊周]] (Zhuāng Zhōu, second half of the fourth century BCE) and his followers

A text-edition of the ***Zhuāngzǐ*** 莊子 in the canonical 33-chapter Guō Xiàng 郭象 ([[郭象]]) redaction, sourced from the **CHANT** (Chinese Ancient Text database, CUHK) critical edition. Parallel edition to [[KR5c0051|[[KR5c0051]] *Nánhuá zhēn jīng*]] (the Daozang canonical edition); this [[KR5c0126]] entry serves primarily as the linguistically-indexed digital resource.

### Abstract

See [[KR5c0051]] for the full treatment. Summary: The *Zhuāngzǐ* in the received 33-chapter Guō Xiàng redaction (c. 290–312 CE) is the second of the three foundational Daoist classics (with the *Dàodé jīng* and the *Yìjīng*). The inner chapters (*Nèi piān*, chs. 1–7) are widely attributed to Zhuāng Zhōu himself (fl. late 4th cent. BCE); the outer (*Wài piān*) and miscellaneous (*Zá piān*) chapters are the work of his followers and later tradition.
