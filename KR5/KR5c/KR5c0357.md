# Gǔ Lóu guān zǐ yún yǎn qìng jí 古樓觀紫雲衍慶集

*Collection of Purple-Cloud Blessings Unfolding at the Ancient Lóuguān*

by [[朱象先]] (Zhū Xiàngxiān) — Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist at Lóu guān tái

A three-*juàn* collection of **inscriptions and records** connected with **Gǔ Lóu guān** 古樓觀 (the Ancient Lóu guān), the traditional site where Lǎo zǐ delivered the *Dào dé jīng* to Yǐn Xǐ 尹喜 — understood as the oldest Daoist monastic establishment. Compiled shortly after 1308. Preserved as **DZ 957** / CT 957 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Shortly after 1308. Dynasty: 元.
