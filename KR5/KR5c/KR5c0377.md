# Dào dé piān zhāng xuán sòng 道德篇章玄頌

*Mysterious Hymns on the Chapters of the Dào dé jīng*

by [[宋鸞]] (Sòng Luán) — Táng Daoist author

A Táng **verse-commentary** on the *Dào dé jīng*, providing a hymn (*sòng* 頌) for each chapter. Preserved as **DZ 977** / CT 977 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類) in 2 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
