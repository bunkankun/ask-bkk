# Yīn zhēn jūn jīn shí wǔ xiāng lèi 陰真君金石五相類

*Perfected Yīn's Metals-and-Minerals Five-Aspects-Class*

attributed to [[陰長生]] (Yīn Chángshēng)

A Five-Dynasties **alchemical text** attributed to the Hàn immortal Yīn Chángshēng (Yīn zhēn jūn 陰眞君), treating the "five aspects" (*wǔ xiāng* 五相) of metals and minerals. Cognate with [[KR5c0302]] and [[KR5c0303]]. Preserved as **DZ 906** / CT 906 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
