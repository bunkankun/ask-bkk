# Zhèng yī zhǐ jiào zhāi qīng dàn xíng dào yí 正一旨教齋清旦行道儀

*Zhèng yī Purpose-Teaching Fasting-Retreat Dawn Practice Liturgy*

Anonymous; Táng

A companion to [[KR5c0195|DZ 798]] — the **dawn-practice** (*qīng dàn xíng dào* 清旦行道) component of the Zhèng yī fasting-retreat ritual. Preserved as **DZ 799** / CT 799 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text provides the **morning-session liturgy** of the fasting-retreat cycle — the first of the day's three sessions (*sān shí xíng dào* 三時行道 — "three times of the day practising the Way"). Middle and evening sessions would be addressed in parallel texts.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
