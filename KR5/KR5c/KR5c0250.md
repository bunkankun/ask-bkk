# Yǎng shēng biàn yí jué 養生辯疑訣

*Instructions for Resolving Doubts on Nourishing Life*

by [[施肩吾]] (Shī Jiānwú; mid-Táng Daoist poet); c. 820s–840s

A Táng *yǎng shēng* manual by **Shī Jiānwú** 施肩吾 (*zì* Xī shèng 希聖, *jìnshì* 820, *hào* Dōng zhāi 棟齋 or Huā yáng zhēn rén 華陽真人) — a major mid-Táng Daoist poet and *nèi dān* practitioner. Preserved as **DZ 853** / CT 853.

## About the work

Shī Jiānwú was one of the significant mid-Táng Daoist poet-practitioners, *jìnshì* of 820 who retreated to a Daoist life after his official career and composed poetry and *yǎng shēng* texts. His collected works include ***Xī shèng jí*** 希聖集 — preserving much of his Daoist-themed poetry.

**Dating.** Mid-Táng (c. 820–860). Dynasty: 唐.
