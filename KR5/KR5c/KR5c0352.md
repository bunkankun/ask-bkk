# Jiǔ zhuǎn liú zhū shén xiān jiǔ dān jīng 九轉流珠神仙九丹經

*Divine-Immortals' Nine-Elixir Scripture of the Nine-Cycle Flowing-Pearl*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **alchemical scripture in 2 *juàn*** on the **nine-cycle flowing-pearl** (*jiǔ zhuǎn liú zhū* 九轉流珠) elixir — treating nine elixirs (*jiǔ dān* 九丹) in the divine-immortal tradition. Preserved as **DZ 952** / CT 952 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
