# Shén xiān yǎng shēng mì shù 神仙養生祕術

*Secret Arts of Life-Nourishment from the Divine Immortals*

by [[陳顯微]] (Chén Xiǎnwēi) — Southern-Sòng Daoist

A Southern-Sòng **life-nourishing manual** (*yǎng shēng mì shù* 養生祕術) by Chén Xiǎnwēi, the *Cān tóng qì* commentator and important Southern-Lineage alchemist. Preserved as **DZ 948** / CT 948 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
