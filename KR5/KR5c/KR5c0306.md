# Shàng qīng jiǔ zhēn zhōng jīng nèi jué 上清九真中經內訣

*Inner Instructions on the Shàng qīng Nine-Perfected Central Scripture*

attributed to [[赤松子]] (Chì sōng zǐ)

An early-Six-Dynasties **Shàng qīng 上清** esoteric-instruction text on the *Jiǔ zhēn zhōng jīng* 九眞中經 — a core Shàng qīng meditation-scripture on the **Nine Perfected** (*jiǔ zhēn* 九眞). Attributed to the legendary rain-master Chì sōng zǐ. Preserved as **DZ 908** / CT 908 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Early Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝早期.
