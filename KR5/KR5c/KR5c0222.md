# Yán líng xiān shēng jí xīn jiù fú qì jīng 延陵先生集新舊服氣經

*Master Yán líng's Collected Scriptures of New and Old Pneumatic-Consumption*

compiled by [[桑榆子]] (Sāng yú zǐ); Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties **compilation of old and new *qì* 氣-cultivation texts** by **Sāng yú zǐ** 桑榆子 (*hào* "Master Mulberry Elm"), nominally presenting the teachings of **Yán líng xiān shēng** 延陵先生. Preserved as **DZ 825** / CT 825 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties (907–979). Dynasty: 五代.
