# Xuán tiān shàng dì qǐ shèng líng yì lù 玄天上帝啟聖靈異錄

*Record of Numinous Marvels in the Sage-Revelations of the Dark-Heaven Supreme Thearch*

Anonymous; Míng

A short Míng **hagiographical supplement** to [[KR5c0358]] *Xuán tiān shàng dì qǐ shèng lù*, collecting additional numinous-response (*líng yì* 靈異) narratives for **Xuán tiān shàng dì** 玄天上帝 (Zhēn wǔ). Preserved as **DZ 961** / CT 961 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
