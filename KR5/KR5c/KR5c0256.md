# Tài shàng tōng xuán líng yìn jīng 太上通玄靈印經

*Most High Mystery-Communicating Numinous-Seal Scripture*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Sòng

A Daoist scripture on the **numinous seal** (*líng yìn* 靈印) — the ritual seals used in Daoist exorcistic-liturgical practice. Preserved as **DZ 859** / CT 859.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Sòng. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
