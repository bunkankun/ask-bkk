# Zhèng yī chū guān zhāng yí 正一出官章儀

*Zhèng yī Liturgy for Dispatching Officers and Petitions*

Anonymous; Táng

A Zhèng yī 正一 liturgical manual for the **dispatching of celestial officers** (*chū guān* 出官) and the **submission of petitions** (*zhāng* 章) — key rituals of the Daoist priest's communication with the celestial bureaucracy. Preserved as **DZ 795** / CT 795 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The **dispatching of officers** (*chū guān*) — the mobilisation of the celestial officials who inhabit the Daoist priest's body for ritual purposes — and the **submission of petitions** (*shàng zhāng* 上章) are central operations of the Zhèng yī ritual system. The text provides the step-by-step procedure for these operations.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
