# Zhèng yī wēi yí jīng 正一威儀經

*Scripture of Zhèng yī Ritual Deportment*

Anonymous; 8th century

A Táng-era (8th-century) **ritual-deportment scripture** (*wēi yí* 威儀 = "awe-inspiring ritual conduct") of the Zhèng yī 正一 tradition. Preserved as **DZ 791** / CT 791 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text prescribes the **proper ritual conduct** of Zhèng yī Daoist clergy — the formal *wēi yí* (deportment-dignity) appropriate to various ritual contexts. This includes:

1. **Bodily postures** in rituals (standing, kneeling, prostration).
2. **Vocal formulas** (*zhòu* 咒, prayers, invocations).
3. **Ritual gestures** (incense-offering, implement-handling).
4. **Hierarchical protocols** for engagement with superior and inferior officiants.

**Dating.** 8th century per catalog. Dynasty: 唐.
