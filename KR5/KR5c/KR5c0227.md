# Fú qì jīng yì lùn 服氣精義論

*Treatise on the Essential Meaning of Pneumatic-Consumption*

by [[司馬承禎]] (Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn, 646–735) — Twelfth Shàng qīng Patriarch

A Táng *qì*-cultivation treatise by the great **Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn** 司馬承禎 (646–735) — the Twelfth Shàng qīng Patriarch and one of the most influential Táng Daoist masters (mentor of Táng Xuánzōng and author of [[DZ 1035]] *Dào tǐ lùn* and DZ 1036 *Zuò wàng lùn*). Preserved as **DZ 830** / CT 830.

## About the work

The treatise provides a **systematic theoretical-practical manual** for the *fú qì* 服氣 ("pneumatic consumption") practice — one of the foundational Shàng qīng meditation techniques.

**Dating.** Within Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn's active career (680s–735). Dynasty: 唐.

## Translations and research

- Engelhardt, Ute. *Die Klassische Tradition der Qi-Übungen*. Stuttgart: Steiner, 1987. Major German study of the *qì*-cultivation tradition including this work.
