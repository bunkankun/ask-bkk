# Tài shàng Zhèng yī cháo tiān sān bā xiè zuì fǎ chàn 太上正一朝天三八謝罪法懺

*Most High Zhèng yī Heavenly-Audience Three-Eights Apology-Repentance Dharma*

Anonymous; TángYuán

A Daoist **sin-apology / repentance liturgy** (*xiè zuì fǎ chàn* 謝罪法懺) of the Zhèng yī tradition, centred on the "Three Eights" (*sān bā* 三八 = twenty-four; or a specific ritual schedule of 3 × 8 = 24 prostrations / invocations). Preserved as **DZ 813** / CT 813 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Táng to Yuán. Dynasty: 唐-元.
