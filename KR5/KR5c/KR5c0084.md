# Dé jīng yì tóng zì 德經異同字

*Same-and-Different Characters in the Dé Scripture* (concordance of textual variants)

Anonymous; no source text files available

The companion volume to [[KR5c0083|*Dào jīng yì tóng zì* 道經異同字]] — a catalog entry for what appears to be a **Daozang concordance of textual variants in the *Dé jīng*** (the lower half of the *Dàodé jīng*, chs. 38–81). Like the parallel [[KR5c0083]], only a Readme.org title-stub is preserved in the Kanseki Repository; no source text files are available locally, and the work is not separately identified in Schipper & Verellen's *Taoist Canon* (2004) under this title.

## About the work

The Kanripo catalog meta gives only the title 德經異同字 and catalogs it as SJB and ZHDZ. No author, date, or dynasty is recorded. The work is the evident counterpart to [[KR5c0083]] ([[KR5c0083|*Dào jīng yì tóng zì*]]), extending the variant-concordance from the *Dào piān* 道篇 (chs. 1–37) to the *Dé piān* 德篇 (chs. 38–81). Together the two titles form a **two-part philological concordance** of textual variants across the whole 81-chapter *Dàodé jīng*.
