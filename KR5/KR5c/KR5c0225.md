# Yòu zhēn xiān shēng fú nèi yuán qì jué 幼真先生服內元氣訣

*Master Yòu zhēn's Instructions on Consuming the Inner Primordial Pneuma*

attributed to **Yòu zhēn xiān shēng** 幼真先生 ("Master Youthful Perfection"); Táng

A Táng-era *qì* cultivation manual on the **consumption of inner primordial *qì*** (*fú nèi yuán qì* 服內元氣) — the internal variant of the more common *fú qì* 服氣 practice. Preserved as **DZ 828** / CT 828.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
