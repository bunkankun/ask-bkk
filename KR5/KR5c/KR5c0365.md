# Sòng Dōng Tài yǐ gōng bēi míng 宋東太乙宮碑銘

*Stele-Inscription for the Sòng Eastern Tàiyǐ Palace*

by [[扈蒙]] (Hù Méng, 915–986) — Northern-Sòng court scholar

A single **stele-inscription** by Hù Méng for the **Dōng Tài yǐ gōng** 東太乙宮 (Eastern Tàiyǐ Palace), one of three Tài yǐ 太乙 temples — East, West, Central — established by the Sòng court for imperial worship of **Tài yǐ** 太乙 (the supreme cosmic deity, the One). Dated 984 (Yōng xī 1). Preserved as **DZ 965** / CT 965 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** 984. Dynasty: 北宋.
