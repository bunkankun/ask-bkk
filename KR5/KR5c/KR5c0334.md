# Tài bái jīng 太白經

*Scripture of Great White*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A short Daoist **alchemical-astral scripture** named after **Tài bái** 太白 (Venus, the "Great White" planet), governing metal-processes in Chinese cosmology. Preserved as **DZ 934** / CT 934 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
