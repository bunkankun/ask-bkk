# Xuán pǐn lù 玄品錄

*Record of the Daoist Ranks*

by [[張天雨]] (Zhāng Tiān yǔ; Yuán Daoist); dated **1335**

A **Yuán-era Daoist hagiographic-biographical compilation** listing and ranking Daoist immortals, masters, and adepts across the ages, by the Yuán Daoist **Zhāng Tiān yǔ** 張天雨 (also transcribed **Zhāng Yǔ** 張雨, 1283–1350). Dated **1335**. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 781** / CT 781 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類).

## About the work

### Author

**Zhāng Tiān yǔ / Zhāng Yǔ** 張雨 (1283–1350) was a major Yuán Daoist master, poet, and painter — a disciple of the 38th Celestial Master [[張嗣成]] Zhāng Sìchéng's circle. He was active as a Daoist patriarch at Máo shān 茅山 and was widely celebrated for his literati-scholar accomplishments.

### Structure

The *Xuán pǐn lù* ranks Daoist figures into categories (*pǐn* 品) corresponding to levels of realisation:

1. **Highest-rank Perfected** (*zhēn rén* 真人).
2. **Celestial immortals** (*tiān xiān* 天仙).
3. **Earthly immortals** (*dì xiān* 地仙).
4. Various lesser categories.

For each figure, the *Xuán pǐn lù* provides brief biographical / hagiographic information and assigns them to one of the ranks.

### Abstract

A characteristic Yuán-era Daoist hagiographic compilation, systematising the Daoist pantheon and master-lineage in hierarchical terms.

**Dating.** 1335. Dynasty: 元.
