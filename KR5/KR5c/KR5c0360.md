# Yù zhì Zhēn wǔ miào bēi 御制真武廟碑

*Imperially-Commissioned Stele-Inscription for the Zhēnwǔ Temple*

by [[成祖]] (Míng Chéng zǔ, the Yǒng lè emperor, r. 1403–1424)

A single **imperially-commissioned stele-inscription** (*yù zhì* 御制) by the Yǒng lè emperor for a **Zhēn wǔ** 真武 temple, composed 1415. Part of the Yǒng lè court's sweeping patronage of the Zhēn wǔ cult, which centered on **Wǔ dāng shān** 武當山 (Húběi) where the emperor simultaneously funded the construction of a monumental Daoist complex. Preserved as **DZ 960** / CT 960 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** 1415. Dynasty: 明.
