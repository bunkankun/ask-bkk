# Zǐ tuán dān jīng 紫團丹經

*Alchemical Scripture of the Purple Cluster*

Anonymous; Southern Sòng

A Southern-Sòng **inner-alchemical (*nèi dān* 內丹) manual**, attributed in the text to the "Zǐ tuán zhēn rén" 紫團眞人 ("Purple-Cluster Perfected"). The doctrine is **heart-kidney union** (*xīnshèn jiāo gòu* 心腎交媾) through *cún shén* 存神 meditation; the text explicitly cites Zhāng Bóduān's *Wù zhēn piān* 悟眞篇, placing it in the Southern-Sòng *Nán zōng* 南宗 alchemical tradition. Preserved as **DZ 878** / CT 878 (*Dòngshén bù, Fāngfǎ lèi* 洞神部方法類).

**Dating.** Southern Sòng. Dynasty: 南宋.
