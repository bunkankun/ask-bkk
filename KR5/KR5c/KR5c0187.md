# Nǚ qīng guǐ lǜ 女青鬼律

*Demon-Disciplinary Rules of Nǚ qīng*

Anonymous; probably 3rd century (catalog: "3rd cent?")

An important **early Celestial Master demon-disciplinary code** in 6 *juàn*. The title's **Nǚ qīng** 女青 refers to a supreme celestial administrative authority that supervises the rules governing demons. Preserved as **DZ 790** / CT 790 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

### Historical importance

The *Nǚ qīng guǐ lǜ* is one of the **foundational documents of early Daoist demonology**. Its 6-*juàn* presentation of rules governing demons — their classification, powers, and the ritual-disciplinary responses required of the Daoist practitioner — established the basic framework of subsequent Chinese demonological literature.

### Contents

The text includes:

1. **Systematic classification** of various demon-types.
2. **Specification of demonic names** — enabling the practitioner to invoke, summon, or subdue specific demons by name.
3. **Disciplinary rules** prescribing proper response to demonic attacks.
4. **Eschatological framework** — the demons act within a larger cosmic-administrative order overseen by *Nǚ qīng*.

### Dating

Per catalog: "3rd century?". Consistent with the early Celestial Master demonological tradition. Frontmatter gives **250–350** as a broad window covering 3rd and early-4th centuries. Dynasty: 三國-晉.
