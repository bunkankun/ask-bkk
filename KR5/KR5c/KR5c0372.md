# Gōng guàn bēi jì 宮觀碑記

*Stele-Records of Palaces and Abbeys*

Anonymous; early Míng

A Míng **collection of stele-inscriptions** from Daoist *gōng* 宮 (palaces) and *guàn* 觀 (abbeys), compiled for inclusion in the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng. (Source's title ends with 誌 rather than 記; both signify "record".) Preserved as **DZ 972** / CT 972 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Early Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
