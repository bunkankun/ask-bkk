# Yuán yáng zǐ wǔ jiǎ lùn 元陽子五假論

*Master Yuán yáng's Treatise on the Five Falses*

attributed to **Yuán yáng zǐ** 元陽子; undated

A short Daoist treatise on the **Five Falses** (*wǔ jiǎ* 五假) — five fundamental deceptions that obscure the true Way. Preserved as **DZ 864** / CT 864.

**Dating.** Undated. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
