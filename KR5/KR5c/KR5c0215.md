# Tài qīng dǎo yǐn yǎng shēng jīng 太清導引養生經

*Scripture of Great-Clarity Gymnastic-Breathing and Life-Nourishing*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties or Táng

A foundational **Daoist gymnastic-breathing** (*dǎo yǐn* 導引) and **life-nourishing** (*yǎng shēng* 養生) **manual**. Preserved as **DZ 818** / CT 818 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

### Dǎo yǐn tradition

***Dǎo yǐn*** 導引 ("guiding and pulling") is the classical Daoist system of **physical exercises** for health and longevity — combining stretching, breathing coordination, and visualisation. It has roots in the pre-Hàn *Yǐn shū* 引書 (now recovered from Zhāng jiā shān 張家山 archaeological findings) and is systematically codified in Hàn-Six-Dynasties texts.

### Contents

The text provides specific *dǎo yǐn* exercises, typically:

1. **Seasonal / diurnal-cyclic practices** aligned with *yīnyáng* and *wǔ xíng* cycles.
2. **Organ-specific exercises** addressing the *wǔ zàng* 五藏.
3. **Breathing techniques** (*tǔ nà* 吐納).
4. **Meditative visualisations** accompanying the physical practice.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties / Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
