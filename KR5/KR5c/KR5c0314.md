# Huán dān zhǒu hòu jué 還丹肘後訣

*Instructions on the Return-Elixir to Keep at One's Elbow*

Anonymous; late Táng to early Sòng

A **handy reference-manual** on the **return-elixir** (*huán dān* 還丹) in 3 *juàn* — "elbow-keepings" (*zhǒu hòu* 肘後) is a conventional Chinese title marker for compact, ready-to-hand technical manuals (cf. Gě Hóng's *Zhǒu hòu bèi jí fāng* 肘後備急方). Preserved as **DZ 915** / CT 915 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Late Táng or early Sòng (10th–11th century). Dynasty: 唐-宋.
