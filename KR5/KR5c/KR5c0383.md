# Cuì shàn lù 萃善錄

*Record of Gathered Goodness*

Anonymous; early Míng

A Míng **collection** (*cuì* 萃 — gathered) of memorials and petitions of "gathered goodness" — ritual compositions of the *biǎo zòu* 表奏 genre. Preserved as **DZ 983** / CT 983 (*Dòngshén bù, Biǎo zòu lèi* 洞神部表奏類) in 2 *juàn*. This is the final text in the *Dòngshén bù* 洞神部 of the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng.

**Dating.** Early Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
