# Sān dòng shū jī zá shuō 三洞樞機雜說

*Various Discourses on the Axial Mechanism of the Three Caverns*

Anonymous; Táng

Táng-era miscellaneous discourses on the **axial mechanism** (*shū jī* 樞機) of the Three Caverns (*sān dòng* 三洞) — the foundational revelation-structure of the Daoist canon. Preserved as **DZ 839** / CT 839.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
