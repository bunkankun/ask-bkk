# Shàng qīng Sī mìng Máo zhēn jūn xiū xíng zhǐ mí jué 上清司命茅真君修行指迷訣

*Instructions for Dispelling Delusions in Cultivation-Practice, by the Shàng qīng Perfected Lord Máo, Director of Destinies*

attributed to **Máo zhēn jūn** 茅真君 (the canonical Three Brothers Máo of Máo shān); Táng

A Táng cultivation-manual attributed to **Máo zhēn jūn** 茅真君 — the Three Máo Brothers who are the canonical patron-deities of the Shàng qīng 上清 tradition at **Máo shān** 茅山 (one of the three, **Máo Gù** 茅固, holds the title *Sī mìng* 司命 "Director of Destinies"). Preserved as **DZ 832** / CT 832.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
