# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn jīn shū nèi xù 太上老君金書內序

*Inner Preface to the Most High Lord Lǎo's Golden Book*

Anonymous

A short Daoist text providing a **framing inner preface** to a "Golden Book" (*jīn shū* 金書) of Lǎojūn revelations. Preserved as **DZ 772** / CT 772 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類).

## About the work

The text is short and prefatorial in character — likely an introductory framing document for a larger now-lost or separately-transmitted Lǎojūn revelation-book. Its placement in the *Pǔ lù* 譜錄 ("Genealogies and Records") subcategory alongside Xiè Shǒuhào's works suggests hagiographic-genealogical content.

**Dating.** Undated; range from Six Dynasties to Sòng. Frontmatter gives **500–1200** as a broad window.
