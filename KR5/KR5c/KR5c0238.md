# Sūn zhēn rén shè yǎng lùn 孫真人攝養論

*Treatise on Regulating Life, by Perfected Sūn*

attributed to [[孫思邈]] (Sūn Sīmiǎo, c. 581–682)

A Daoist-medical *shè yǎng lùn* 攝養論 ("Treatise on Regulating Life") attributed to Sūn Sīmiǎo. Preserved as **DZ 841** / CT 841. One of Sūn Sīmiǎo's attributed Daoist-medical trilogy alongside [[KR5c0231]] and [[KR5c0232]].

**Dating.** Traditional Sūn Sīmiǎo dates. Dynasty: 隋-唐.
