# Tài shàng dòng shén xuán miào bái yuán zhēn jīng 太上洞神玄妙白猿真經

*Most High Cavern-Spirit Mysterious-Marvelous White-Ape True Scripture*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Sòng

A Daoist **White-Ape Scripture** of the *Dòng shén* 洞神 tradition — associated with a legendary white-ape master of ancient cultivation-lore. Preserved as **DZ 858** / CT 858.

**Dating.** Undated. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
