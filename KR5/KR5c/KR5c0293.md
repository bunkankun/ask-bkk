# Yīn yáng jiǔ zhuǎn chéng zǐ jīn diǎn huà huán dān jué 陰陽九轉成紫金點化還丹訣

*Instructions on the Yīn-Yáng Nine-Cycles Forming Purple Gold for Transmutation into the Return-Elixir*

Anonymous; late Táng / early Sòng

A short **alchemical instruction-text** on the **"purple-gold transmutation" return-elixir** (*zǐ jīn diǎn huà huán dān* 紫金點化還丹) — a nine-cycle elixir produced by alternating yīnyáng firing phases. Preserved as **DZ 895** / CT 895 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Late Táng or early Sòng (10th century?). Dynasty: 唐-宋.
