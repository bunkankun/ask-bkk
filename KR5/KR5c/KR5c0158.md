# Tài shàng tài qīng tiān tóng hù mìng miào jīng zhù 太上太清天童護命妙經註

*Commentary on the Marvelous Scripture of the Most High and Great Clarity of the Celestial Youth Protecting Life*

by [[侯善淵]] (Hóu Shànyuān; Jīn-dynasty Quán zhēn Daoist, late 12th cent.)

A brief **Jīn-dynasty Quán zhēn commentary** on the Daoist scripture **Tiān tóng hù mìng miào jīng** 天童護命妙經 — a minor text of the *hù mìng* 護命 ("protect life") scripture-category invoking celestial youths (*tiān tóng* 天童) for personal protection. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 632** / CT 632 (*Dòngzhēn bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞真部玉訣類).

## About the work

### Author

**Hóu Shànyuān** 侯善淵 (see [[侯善淵]]) — the Jīn-dynasty Quán zhēn master active in the late 12th century. This is one of several short scripture-commentaries that Hóu Shànyuān produced.

### Scripture

The **Tiān tóng hù mìng miào jīng** is a short Daoist scripture of the *hù mìng* 護命 protective-ritual genre. The scripture addresses *Tài qīng* 太清 (Great Clarity) celestial youth-deities who provide personal protection against demonic influences and illness.

### Abstract

A minor but well-attested Jīn-era Quán zhēn commentary on a scripture of the protective-ritual genre.

**Dating.** Hóu Shànyuān's active period: c. 1150–1200. Dynasty: 金.
