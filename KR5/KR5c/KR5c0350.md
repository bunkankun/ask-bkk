# Shàng dòng xīn dān jīng jué 上洞心丹經訣

*Instructions on the Upper-Cavern Heart-Elixir Scripture*

Anonymous; Northern Sòng

A Northern-Sòng Daoist **alchemical instruction-text in 3 *juàn*** on the **upper-cavern heart-elixir** (*shàng dòng xīn dān* 上洞心丹) — an inner-alchemical (*nèi dān* 內丹) formulation centered on the heart. Preserved as **DZ 950** / CT 950 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋.
