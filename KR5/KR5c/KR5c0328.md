# Xuán jiě lù 懸解錄

*Record of Suspended Release*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **alchemical dialogue-text** titled after the Zhuāng zǐ phrase *xuán jiě* 懸解 ("release from suspension" = the Daoist liberation from life-and-death bondage). Dated by its preface to 855 (Dà zhōng 9, under Táng Xuān zōng). Preserved as **DZ 928** / CT 928 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 855 (preface). Dynasty: 唐.
