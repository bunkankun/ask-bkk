# Shàng xuán gāo zhēn yán shòu chì shū 上玄高真延壽赤書

*Red Book for Prolonging Life, from the Most High Mystery and Lofty Perfected*

compiled by [[裴鉉]] (Péi Xuàn) — Táng Zhōngnánshān Daoist

The **scripture proper** accompanying Péi Xuàn's memorial (see [[KR5c0274]]) — a Táng collection of **longevity and visualization techniques** gathered from the "high perfected" (*gāo zhēn* 高真), centring on the visualization of sun/moon pairs (*yù yí* 鬱儀 and *jié lín* 結璘) in the eyes, and the heart-sun visualization of the Southern Ultimate Dame (*Nán jí fū rén* 南極夫人). Preserved as **DZ 877b** / CT 877b (*Dòngshén bù, Fāngfǎ lèi* 洞神部方法類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
