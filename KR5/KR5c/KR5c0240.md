# Yǎng shēng yǒng xuán jí 養生詠玄集

*Collected Songs in Praise of the Mysterious for Nourishing Life*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng *yǎng shēng* 養生 **verse-collection** (*yǒng xuán jí* 詠玄集 = "collected songs in praise of the Mysterious") — poems on life-nourishing themes in classical Daoist register. Preserved as **DZ 843** / CT 843.

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
