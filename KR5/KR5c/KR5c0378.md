# Dào dé zhēn jīng sòng 道德真經頌

*Hymns to the Perfect Scripture of the Way and its Virtue*

by [[蔣融庵]] (Jiǎng Róng'ān) — Míng Daoist author

A Míng **verse-response** to the *Dào dé jīng* — paralleling Sòng Luán's [[KR5c0377]]. Preserved as **DZ 978** / CT 978 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類).

**Dating.** Early Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
