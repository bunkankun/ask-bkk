# Xuán shuāng zhǎng shàng lù 玄霜掌上錄

*Palm-Held Record of Mysterious Frost*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **alchemical notebook-text** on the **mysterious-frost elixir** (*xuán shuāng* 玄霜) — a white crystalline elixir product — in "palm-held" (*zhǎng shàng* 掌上) handbook format. Preserved as **DZ 945** / CT 945 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
