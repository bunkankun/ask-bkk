# Tōng xuán zhēn jīng zuǎn yì shì yīn 通玄真經纘義釋音

*Phonetic Glosses to the "Continuation of the Meaning of the True Scripture of Communion with the Mystery"*

by [[杜道堅]] (Dù Dàojiān, 1237–1318); companion to [[KR5c0143]]

A short **phonetic-gloss apparatus** (*shì yīn* 釋音) to [[杜道堅]] Dù Dàojiān's *Wénzǐ* commentary ([[KR5c0143]] *Tōng xuán zhēn jīng zuǎn yì*, 1310). Per Schipper & Verellen (2004, 2:3106): the phonetic explanations that conclude the work are "clearly by Dù Dàojiān himself".

## About the work

### Function

The *shì yīn* 釋音 is a brief **phonetic-gloss apparatus** providing:

1. ***Fǎnqiè*** 反切 pronunciations for difficult characters in the *Wénzǐ*.
2. **Homophone glosses** (in the *zhí yīn* 直音 convention) using common characters to indicate rare ones.
3. **Occasional brief philological notes**.

It is a reader-aid of the same genre as [[KR5c0094]] (Liáng Jiǒng's 1098 *Dàodé zhēn jīng jí zhù shì yīn*) and [[KR5c0132]] (Jiǎ Shànxiáng's 1086 *Nán huá zhēn jīng zhí yīn*) — part of the standard Sòng-Yuán apparatus attached to major Daoist commentaries.

### Abstract

The *Shì yīn* is a minor but useful component of the comprehensive Dù Dàojiān *Wénzǐ* scholarly apparatus (parent work at [[KR5c0143]]).

**Dating.** Contemporary with the parent work [[KR5c0143]], so **1310**. Dynasty: 元.
