# Tài shàng hùn yuán Lǎo zǐ shǐ lüè 太上混元老子史略

*Summary History of the Most High Chaotic-Origin Lǎozǐ*

by [[謝守灝]] (Xiè Shǒuhào, 1134–1212); third work in his Lǎojūn trilogy

The third of [[謝守灝]] Xiè Shǒuhào's Lǎojūn-hagiographic trilogy (alongside [[KR5c0166|DZ 770 *Hùn yuán shèng jì*]] and [[KR5c0167|DZ 771 *Nián pǔ yào lüè*]]). Preserved as **DZ 773** / CT 773 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類) in 3 *juàn*.

## About the work

A **summary history** (*shǐ lüè* 史略) of the Lǎojūn tradition — more discursive than the tabular *Nián pǔ yào lüè* but more concise than the comprehensive *Shèng jì*. The three works together constitute Xiè Shǒuhào's coordinated hagiographic programme.

**Dating.** Within Xiè Shǒuhào's active career (1191–1212). Dynasty: 宋.
