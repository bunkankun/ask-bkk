# Tài shàng yáo tái yì suàn bǎo jí yán nián chàn 太上瑤臺益算寶籍延年懺

*Most High Jade-Terrace, Life-Quota-Increasing, Precious-Register, Year-Extending Repentance*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A **Daoist longevity-repentance liturgy** integrating the **Jade Terrace** (*yáo tái* 瑤臺, celestial paradise) and **life-quota-increasing** (*yì suàn* 益算) themes — the latter familiar from the talismanic-longevity tradition of [[KR5c0053]] *Yì suàn shén fú jīng*. Preserved as **DZ 812** / CT 812 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
