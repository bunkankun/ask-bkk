# Tài qīng jīn yè shén qì jīng 太清金液神氣經

*Great-Clarity Scripture of the Divine-Pneuma of Gold Liquor*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties **Tài qīng 太清** alchemical scripture in 3 *juàn*, treating the **divine-pneuma** (*shén qì* 神氣) of the gold-liquor elixir — part of the classical Tài qīng alchemical corpus. Preserved as **DZ 882** / CT 882 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
