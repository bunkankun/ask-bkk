# Táng Yè zhēn rén zhuàn 唐葉真人傳

*Biography of the Táng Perfected Lord Yè*

Anonymous; c. 1250

A short **biographical / hagiographic** account of a Táng-era Daoist immortal named **Yè** 葉 (uncertain which specific Yè — possibly **Yè Fǎ shàn** 葉法善, the famous Táng Daoist master of Xuánzōng's court). Preserved as **DZ 780** / CT 780 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類). Catalog dates to c. 1250.

## About the work

The text is a brief hagiographic biography of the Táng Perfected Lord Yè. If identified as **Yè Fǎ shàn** 葉法善 (616–720) — the famous Táng court Daoist master who served Táng Gāozōng, Wǔ zé tiān, and Xuánzōng — the work would be an extended commemoration of one of the most influential early-Táng Daoists.

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán period (c. 1200–1300 per catalog). Dynasty: 宋-元.
