# Tài shàng jīng jiè 太上經戒

*Precepts of the Most High Scripture*

Anonymous; Táng 唐

A short **Táng-era precepts compilation** preserved as **DZ 787** / CT 787 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text compiles Daoist disciplinary precepts from the *Tài shàng* 太上 (Lǎojūn) scriptural tradition. It belongs to the mature Táng-era codification of Daoist *jiè lǜ* 戒律 discipline, alongside the efforts of [[金明七真]] Jīnmíng Qīzhēn (DZ 1125 *Sān dòng fèng dào kē jiè*) and others.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
