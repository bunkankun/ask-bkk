# Tài qīng tiáo qì jīng 太清調氣經

*Scripture of Great-Clarity Pneumatic-Regulation*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties / Táng

A classical **breath-regulation** (*tiáo qì* 調氣) manual of the Tài qīng tradition. Preserved as **DZ 820** / CT 820. Companion to [[KR5c0216]] *Tāi xí qì jīng* and other early Daoist breath-cultivation texts.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties / Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
