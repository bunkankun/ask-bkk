# Jù shèng gē 巨勝歌

*Song of Giant-Victory Sesame*

by [[柳沖用]] (Liǔ Chōngyòng)

A short **alchemical verse** on **black sesame** (*jù shèng* 巨勝 = *hú má* 胡麻), an important longevity and elixir ingredient in the classical Chinese materia medica. Preserved as **DZ 931** / CT 931 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
