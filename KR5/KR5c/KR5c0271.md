# Zǐ tíng nèi mì jué xiū xíng fǎ 紫庭內祕訣修行法

*Secret Instructions of the Purple Court for the Dharma of Cultivation*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **meditation-manual** on the visualization of the **Three Origins** (*sān yuán* 三元) — the three *dāntián* 丹田 (upper/middle/lower) together with their resident deities (Chìzǐ 赤子, Zhēn rén 眞人, etc.), following the *Huáng tíng jīng* 黃庭經 framework of inner-body gods. Preserved as **DZ 874** / CT 874 (*Dòngshén bù, Fāngfǎ lèi* 洞神部方法類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
