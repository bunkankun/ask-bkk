# Cān tóng qì wǔ xiāng lèi mì yào 參同契五相類祕要

*Secret Essentials of the Five-Aspects-Class Cān tóng qì*

attributed to [[魏伯陽]] (Wèi Bóyáng)

Companion-text to [[KR5c0302]]: a Sòng **alchemical extract** of the "five-aspects" (*wǔ xiāng* 五相) teaching from the *Cān tóng qì* tradition. Preserved as **DZ 905** / CT 905 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
