# Sān dòng fǎ fú kē jiè wén 三洞法服科戒文

*Code of Precepts on Dharma-Robes of the Three Caverns*

by [[張萬福]] (Zhāng Wànfú, *fl.* 713) — early-Táng Daoist master

A Táng **code of precepts on Daoist monastic robes** (*fǎ fú* 法服) by the early-Táng Daoist master **Zhāng Wànfú** 張萬福 (*fl.* 713). The **Three Caverns** (*sān dòng* 三洞) frame — referring to the three foundational revelation-categories of the Daoist canon (*Dòng zhēn* 洞真 Shàngqīng, *Dòng xuán* 洞玄 Língbǎo, *Dòng shén* 洞神 Sānhuáng) — structures the robes-and-precepts discussion. Preserved as **DZ 788** / CT 788 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

### Contents

The text prescribes:

1. **Daoist monastic-clerical robes** (*fǎ fú* 法服) appropriate to each of the Three Caverns and to each rank of ordination.
2. **Precepts and rules** associated with the wearing of the robes — proper conduct, ritual usage, maintenance.
3. **Hierarchical rank-system** of Daoist ordinands and their visual-ritual distinctions through robe-type and colour.

### Author

**Zhāng Wànfú** 張萬福 (*fl.* 713) — early-Táng Daoist patriarch active under Xuánzōng 玄宗. One of the major codifying-scholarly figures of Táng Daoism, contemporary with and complementary to Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn 司馬承禎. Author of several liturgical and disciplinary texts preserved in the Daozang.

**Dating.** Active 713 (attested). Dynasty: 唐.
