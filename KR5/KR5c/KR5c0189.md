# Xuán mén shí shì wēi yí 玄門十事威儀

*The Ten Matters of Ritual Deportment of the Daoist Gate*

Anonymous; Táng

A short Táng-era **ritual-deportment guide** listing ten key matters (*shí shì* 十事) of proper Daoist ritual conduct. Preserved as **DZ 792** / CT 792 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The "ten matters" covered:

1. **Entrance to sanctuaries** (*rù shān mén* 入山門).
2. **Incense offering** (*jìn xiāng* 進香).
3. **Prostrations** (*lǐ bài* 禮拜).
4. **Reading scriptures** (*sòng jīng* 誦經).
5. **Meditation** (*cún sī* 存思).
6. **Rising and sitting** (*qǐ jū* 起居).
7. **Eating** (*chī fàn* 吃飯).
8. **Sleeping** (*shuì mián* 睡眠).
9. **Clothing** (*yī fú* 衣服).
10. **Going out and returning** (*chū rù* 出入).

These are the basic routines of Daoist monastic life, codified in a simple instructional format.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
