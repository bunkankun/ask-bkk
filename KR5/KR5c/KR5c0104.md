# Dàodé zhēn jīng cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān kāi tí kē wén shū 道德真經藏室纂微篇開題科文疏

*An Analysis of the Introduction to the "Subtleties Culled from the Storehouse of the Way and Its Virtue"*

by [[薛致玄]] (Xuē Zhìxuán, *zì* Yǒng qì 永期, *hào* Tài xiá lǎo rén 太霞老人); preface 1249

A **late-Southern-Sòng subcommentary on Chén Jǐngyuán's [[KR5c0103|DZ 714]]** *Dàodé zhēn jīng cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān*, in five *juàn*, by **Xuē Zhìxuán** 薛致玄. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as DZ 715 / CT 715 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類). Three prefaces are dated to **1249**. This is one of two subcommentary-works by Xuē Zhìxuán on Chén Jǐngyuán's commentary — the companion piece being **DZ 716 *Dàodé zhēn jīng cáng shì zuǎn wēi shǒu chāo*** 道德真經藏室纂微手鈔 (2 *juàn*, [[KR5c0105]]).

## About the work

Isabelle Robinet's notice in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004, 2:1325–42, DZ 715 / 716) gives the authoritative modern framing.

### Structure

The work is divided into two parts:

1. **DZ 715 *Kāi tí kē wén shū*** 開題科文疏 — "An Analysis of the Introduction". 5 *juàn*. Primarily deals with the **mythology of Lǎozǐ** — his birth, his reincarnations, and his career — complementing Chén Jǐngyuán's own *kāi tí* ([[KR5c0102]]). Includes a substantial **biography of Chén Jǐngyuán himself** at 1.3b–8b.
2. **DZ 716 *Shǒu chāo*** 手鈔 — "A Hand Copy". 2 *juàn*. Principally identifies the **sources of the classical quotations** that Chén Jǐngyuán makes in his commentary. Contains only the second half of the *Dàodé jīng* (the *Dé jīng*); the first half's *Shǒu chāo* is missing.

### Prefaces

**DZ 715 *Kāi tí kē wén shū*** is preceded by **three prefaces**, all dated **1249**:

1. **By Lǐ Tīng** 李霆 from Fù yáng 富陽 (Zhè jiāng).
2. **By Guō Shìzhōng** 郭士忠, dated 1249.
3. **By Féng Fù** 馮福, dated 1249.

These prefaces state that both parts (DZ 715 and DZ 716) had been printed together.

### Philosophical content

The work is principally a **scholastic-devotional apparatus** to Chén Jǐngyuán's *kāi tí* and main commentary:

- The **mythology of Lǎozǐ** is elaborated beyond Chén Jǐngyuán's own treatment, with substantial new narrative material on Lǎozǐ's reincarnations (*zài shì* 在世) and cosmic career.
- **Chén Jǐngyuán's own biography** (1.3b–8b) is a valuable Song-era biographical document of the important 11th-century Daoist master.
- The work continues the scholarly tradition of treating Chén Jǐngyuán's commentary as the definitive Sòng reading of the *Dàodé jīng*.

### Abstract

The *Kāi tí kē wén shū* is a late-Southern-Sòng scholastic apparatus to the Northern-Sòng Chén Jǐngyuán tradition, preserving:

1. Elaborate **Lǎozǐ hagiography** that extends Chén Jǐngyuán's *kāi tí*.
2. A **biography of Chén Jǐngyuán** — one of the most detailed Sòng biographies of this important Daoist master.
3. A scholarly-devotional framework for reading the *Dàodé jīng*.

**Dating.** Three prefaces dated 1249; composition therefore completed by that year. Per the project's dating rule, the frontmatter gives **1249** as the composition date. Dynasty: 宋.

**Author.** Xuē Zhìxuán 薛致玄 is otherwise known primarily through his Chén Jǐngyuán-subcommentary work. *Zì* **Yǒng qì** 永期; *hào* **Tài xiá lǎo rén** 太霞老人 ("Old Man of the Great Roseate Cloud"). No lifedates are recorded; he was presumably a Daoist scholar active in Zhè jiāng in the mid-13th century.

## Other points of interest

Xuē Zhìxuán's two-part subcommentary represents the **late-Southern-Sòng scholarly reception** of the Northern-Sòng Chén Jǐngyuán tradition. The sustained scholarly-devotional treatment — extending across five decades between Chén Jǐngyuán's death (1094) and Xuē Zhìxuán's 1249 work — confirms Chén Jǐngyuán's enduring status as the authoritative Northern-Sòng Daoist commentator.

The **preservation of Chén Jǐngyuán's biography** at DZ 715 1.3b–8b is particularly valuable — providing one of the fullest single-source Sòng biographies of any 11th-century Daoist master, complementary to the briefer notice in the *Lì shì zhēn xiān tǐ dào tōng jiàn* 歷世真仙體道通鑑 (LZTT) 49.5a.
