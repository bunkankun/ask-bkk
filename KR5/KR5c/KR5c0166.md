# Hùn yuán shèng jì 混元聖紀

*Sagely Chronicle of the Chaotic Origin*

by [[謝守灝]] (Xiè Shǒuhào, 1134–1212); preface **1193**

A major **Southern-Sòng hagiographic-biographical chronicle** of **Lǎojūn / Tài shàng Lǎo jūn** 太上老君 — chronicling his cosmic origin, his manifestations across the ages, and his relationship with the Daoist religious tradition. In 9 *juàn*. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 770** / CT 770 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類), and in the Qīng *Dàozàng jíyào* as JY086.

## About the work

### Author

**Xiè Shǒuhào** 謝守灝 (1134–1212, CBDB 52414) was a major Southern-Sòng Daoist scholar-hagiographer. His biographical labour on the Lǎojūn tradition produced a coordinated trilogy of works:

1. **[[KR5c0166|*Hùn yuán shèng jì*]]** (DZ 770, 1193, 9 *juàn*) — the present comprehensive chronicle.
2. **[[KR5c0167|*Tài shàng Lǎo jūn nián pǔ yào lüè*]]** 太上老君年譜要略 (DZ 771) — a chronological table / summary.
3. **[[KR5c0169|*Tài shàng hùn yuán Lǎo zǐ shǐ lüè*]]** 太上混元老子史略 (DZ 773) — a summary history.

### Contents

The chronicle narrates:

1. **Lǎojūn's cosmic origin** from the Chaotic Origin (*hùn yuán* 混元).
2. **His successive manifestations** across the ages — beginning from pre-cosmic times, through the Three Sovereigns (*sān huáng* 三皇), the Five Emperors (*wǔ dì* 五帝), the Xià 夏, Shāng 商, and Zhōu 周 dynasties.
3. **His appearance as Lǎozǐ** in the Zhōu and his transmission of the *Dàodé jīng* to Yǐn Xǐ 尹喜.
4. **His subsequent reincarnations and revelations** through the Hàn, Wèi-Jìn, Táng, and Sòng periods.
5. **His integration** into the Daoist pantheon as *Tài shàng Lǎo jūn* 太上老君.

### Abstract

A foundational Southern-Sòng hagiographic document, systematising the Daoist Lǎojūn tradition in a comprehensive chronicle.

**Dating.** Preface 1193. Dynasty: 宋.
