# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng jīng zhù 太上老君說常清靜經註 (Bái Yùchán / Wáng Yuánhuī)

*Commentary on the Scripture Spoken by the Most High Lord Lǎo on Constant Clarity and Stillness* — attributed to Bái Yùchán with Wáng Yuánhuī's commentary

compiled by [[葛長庚]] (Gě Chángēng, i.e. **Bái Yùchán** 白玉蟾, d. 1229); commentary by [[王元暉]] (Wáng Yuánhuī); dated **1312**

A third Daozang commentary on the **Cháng qīng jìng jīng** 常清靜經 ([[DZ 620]]), catalogued with the *Bái Yùchán* attribution but more accurately the work of **Wáng Yuánhuī** 王元暉 (1312). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 757** / CT 757 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類). Catalog disambiguator "(三)" distinguishes from [[KR5c0151]] (Lǐ Dàochún's) and [[KR5c0152]] (anonymous).

## About the work

### Attribution

The catalog lists **Bái Yùchán** 白玉蟾 ([[葛長庚]], d. 1229) as compiler and **Wáng Yuánhuī** 王元暉 as commentator. The **1312 date** (post-dating Bái Yùchán's death by 83 years) makes the Bái Yùchán attribution pseudepigraphic — Bái Yùchán's name has been attached to the compilation in the Southern Lineage (*Nán zōng* 南宗) *nèi dān* tradition's practice of attributing new commentary to revered patriarchs. The substantive commentary is **Wáng Yuánhuī's** (1312).

### Wáng Yuánhuī

**Wáng Yuánhuī** 王元暉 is otherwise little-documented. His 1312 commentary represents a mature Yuán Southern-Lineage *nèi dān* reading of the Lǎojūn meditation-scripture.

### Philosophical character

The commentary:

1. **Places the Cháng qīng jìng jīng in the Southern-Lineage *nèi dān* framework** — drawing heavily on Zhāng Bóduān's 張伯端 alchemical vocabulary.
2. **Integrates Chán Buddhist terminology** with Daoist-alchemical reading.
3. **Provides practical meditative instructions** alongside scholarly-philosophical exposition.

### Abstract

The commentary is a minor but characteristic Yuán Southern-Lineage *nèi dān* reading of the Lǎojūn meditation-scripture. Its pseudo-Bái Yùchán attribution reflects the Southern Lineage's practice of invoking patriarchal authority.

**Dating.** 1312 per the Wáng Yuánhuī authorial date. Per the project's dating rule, the frontmatter gives **1300–1312** as a conservative window. Dynasty: 元.
