# Péng lái shān Xī zào huán dān gē 蓬萊山西竈還丹歌

*Songs of the Return-Elixir from the West Furnace of Pénglái Mountain*

by [[黃玄鍾]] (Huáng Xuánzhōng) — Táng Daoist alchemist

A Táng collection of **alchemical verses** (*gē* 歌) on elixir preparation, framed as issuing from the "West Furnace" (*xī zào* 西竈) of the mythical **Péng lái Mountain** 蓬萊山 — one of the three immortal isles of the eastern sea. Preserved as **DZ 916** / CT 916 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類) in 2 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
