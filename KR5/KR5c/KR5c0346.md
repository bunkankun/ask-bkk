# Tài jí zhēn rén zá dān yào fāng 太極真人雜丹藥方

*Miscellaneous Elixir and Drug Recipes of the Tàijí Perfected*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **miscellany of elixir and drug recipes** (*zá dān yào fāng* 雜丹藥方), attributed to the Tài jí zhēn rén 太極真人. Preserved as **DZ 946** / CT 946 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
