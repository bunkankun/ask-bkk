# Xuán tiān shàng dì qǐ shèng lù 玄天上帝啟聖錄

*Record of the Sage-Revelations of the Dark-Heaven Supreme Thearch*

Anonymous; Yuán

A major **Yuán hagiography** of **Xuán tiān shàng dì** 玄天上帝 (also called **Zhēn wǔ** 真武 / Xuán wǔ 玄武), the Dark Warrior of the North, in 8 *juàn*. Compiled in support of the rapidly expanding Zhēn wǔ cult centered at **Wǔ dāng shān** 武當山 (Húběi), which had received systematic imperial patronage from the Sòng onward and would receive spectacular Míng patronage under the Yǒng lè emperor. Preserved as **DZ 958** / CT 958 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** Yuán. Dynasty: 元.
