# Xuān yuán Huáng dì shuǐ jīng yào fǎ 軒轅黃帝水經藥法

*Xuānyuán Yellow-Thearch's Drug-Methods of the Water Classic*

Anonymous; Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties Daoist **alchemical text** on "water-classic drug-methods" (*shuǐ jīng yào fǎ* 水經藥法) — liquid elixir preparations — pseudepigraphically attributed to the **Xuānyuán Yellow Thearch** (Huáng dì 黃帝). Preserved as **DZ 929** / CT 929 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
