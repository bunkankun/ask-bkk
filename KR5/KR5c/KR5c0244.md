# Tài shàng zhǒu hòu yù jīng fāng 太上肘後玉經方

*Most High Jade-Scripture Prescriptions Behind the Elbow*

by [[盧遵元]] (Lú Zūn yuán); Táng

A Táng **pocket-recipe collection** (*zhǒu hòu* 肘後 = "behind the elbow", a conventional term for a short portable recipe-book) by **Lú Zūn yuán** 盧遵元. Preserved as **DZ 847** / CT 847.

## About the work

### *Zhǒu hòu* genre

The ***zhǒu hòu*** genre of Chinese medical literature comprises **portable emergency recipe-books** carried on one's person (literally "behind the elbow" in a sleeve-pocket) for rapid reference. The most famous is **Gé Hóng's** 葛洪 *Zhǒu hòu bèi jí fāng* 肘後備急方. The present DZ 847 is a Daoist-medical example of the genre.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
