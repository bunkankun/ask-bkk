# Sōng shān Tài wú xiān shēng qì jīng 嵩山太無先生氣經

*Scripture of Master Tài wú of Mt Sōng*

attributed to **Master Tài wú** 太無先生 ("Master Great Void") of **Mt Sōng** 嵩山; Táng-era

A Táng *qì* 氣-cultivation manual attributed to a **Mt Sōng 嵩山 immortal-master** named **Tài wú** 太無先生 ("Great Void"). Preserved as **DZ 824** / CT 824.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
