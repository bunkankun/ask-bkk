# Dān fáng xū zhī 丹房須知

*What Must Be Known about the Elixir Chamber*

by [[吳悞]] (Wú Wù) — Southern-Sòng Daoist alchemist

A Southern-Sòng **practical manual of alchemical laboratory practice** — the single most technical text on *wài dān* equipment in the Dào zàng. Covers siting, construction, and layout of the elixir chamber (*dān fáng* 丹房); types of furnaces (*lú* 爐), crucibles (*dǐng* 鼎), and vessels; ingredients and their preparation; firing schedules. Extensively illustrated. Dated by its preface of 1163. Preserved as **DZ 900** / CT 900 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 1163 (preface). Dynasty: 南宋.
