# Huáng dì jiǔ dǐng shén dān jīng jué 黃帝九鼎神丹經訣

*Instructions on the Yellow Thearch's Nine-Tripod Divine-Elixir Scripture*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Táng

A major **alchemical compilation in 20 *juàn*** surrounding the ancient ***Huáng dì jiǔ dǐng shén dān jīng*** 黃帝九鼎神丹經 (Yellow Thearch's Nine-Tripod Divine-Elixir Scripture) — one of the cornerstone texts of classical *wài dān* 外丹 mentioned already by Gě Hóng in the *Bào pǔ zǐ*. The "nine tripods" (*jiǔ dǐng* 九鼎) refers to the nine separate elixirs taught in the scripture. *Juàn* 1 preserves the core scripture; *juàn* 2–20 are prolix commentarial and technical instructions (*jué* 訣) accumulated from Six Dynasties through Táng. Preserved as **DZ 885** / CT 885 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Core scripture Six Dynasties; commentarial layers through Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
