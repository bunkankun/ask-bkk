# Tài qīng dào dé xiǎn huà yí 太清道德顯化儀

*Liturgy for Manifest Transformation of the Great Clarity Way and Virtue*

Anonymous; Míng dynasty

A Míng-era **liturgical text** for manifesting the Way and Virtue (*xiǎn huà* 顯化 = "manifest transformation"). Preserved as **DZ 793** / CT 793 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text provides a **ritual-liturgical procedure** for the formal cultivation of the Daoist Way-and-Virtue through collective recitation and visualization. It belongs to the late Daoist ritual-liturgical literature that proliferated in the Míng period under imperial and popular patronage.

**Dating.** Míng (1368–1644). Frontmatter gives **1368–1644** as the dynasty bracket. Dynasty: 明.
