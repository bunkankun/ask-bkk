# Tài shàng dòng shén tài yuán hé tú sān yuán yǎng xiè yí 太上洞神太元河圖三元仰謝儀

*Upward-Thanking Liturgy of the Most High Cavern-Spirit, Great Origin, River-Chart, and Three Origins*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Dòng shén 洞神 liturgy for the **Upward-Thanking** (*yǎng xiè* 仰謝) — a ritual of gratitude to celestial authorities, integrating the *Tài yuán* 太元 (Great Origin), *Hé tú* 河圖 (River Chart), and *Sān yuán* 三元 (Three Origins) cosmological-ritual frameworks. Preserved as **DZ 805** / CT 805 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
