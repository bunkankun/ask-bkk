# Jīn huá yù yè dà dān 金華玉液大丹

*The Gold-Flower Jade-Liquor Great Elixir*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng Daoist **alchemical text** on the **gold-flower jade-liquor great elixir** (*jīn huá yù yè dà dān* 金華玉液大丹). Preserved as **DZ 910** / CT 910 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
