# Zhòng zhī cǎo fǎ 種芝草法

*Methods for Cultivating the Numinous Mushroom*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **horticultural-alchemical manual** on the cultivation of **zhī** 芝 — the numinous fungi / magic mushrooms (*Ganoderma* and related species) that are the paramount botanical *materia medica* of the Daoist longevity tradition, catalogued already in Gě Hóng's *Bào pǔ zǐ nèi piān*. Preserved as **DZ 933** / CT 933 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
