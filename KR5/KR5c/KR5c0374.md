# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng jīng sòng zhù 太上老君說常清靜經頌註

*Verse-Commentary on the Most High Lord Lǎo's Scripture of Constant Purity and Stillness*

by [[劉通微]] (Liú Tōngwēi, c. 1167–1196) — Jīn-Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist, Wáng Chóngyáng disciple

A **verse-commentary** (*sòng zhù* 頌註) on the core Táng Daoist meditation-scripture *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* 常清靜經 — pairing expository prose with versified responses. Authored by Liú Tōngwēi, a direct disciple of **Wáng Chóngyáng** (founder of Quán zhēn). Preserved as **DZ 974** / CT 974 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類).

**Dating.** Within Liú's lifetime (c. 1167–1196). Dynasty: 金-元.
