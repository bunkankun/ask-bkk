# Sòng Zhōng Tài yǐ gōng bēi míng 宋中太乙宮碑銘

*Stele-Inscription for the Sòng Central Tàiyǐ Palace*

by [[呂惠卿]] (Lǚ Huìqīng, 1032–1111) — Northern-Sòng reformist statesman

Completing the triad with [[KR5c0365]] and [[KR5c0366]]: the stele-inscription for the **Zhōng Tài yǐ gōng** 中太乙宮 (Central Tàiyǐ Palace). Dated 1074 (Xī níng 7). Its author Lǚ Huìqīng was one of Wáng Ān shí's senior associates in the Xī níng reforms. Preserved as **DZ 967** / CT 967 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** 1074. Dynasty: 北宋.
