# Zhèng yī jiào zhái yí 正一醮宅儀

*Zhèng yī House-Offering Liturgy*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Zhèng yī **household-protection liturgy** — a *jiào* 醮 (offering-ritual) performed at a house to purify it, dispel demons, and invoke celestial protection for the residents. Preserved as **DZ 801** / CT 801 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

A standard Daoist domestic-protection ritual. Related to the household-talisman tradition of [[KR5c0054]] *Hùn yuán sān bù fú* and similar texts.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
