# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn xuán miào zhěn zhōng nèi dé shén zhòu jīng 太上老君玄妙枕中內德神咒經

*Most High Lǎo jūn Mysterious-Marvelous Pillow-Interior Inner-Virtue Divine-Spell Scripture*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **spell-scripture** (*shén zhòu jīng* 神咒經) attributed to Tài shàng Lǎo jūn, with the "**pillow-interior**" (*zhěn zhōng* 枕中) format signalling a personal / private meditative-ritual text. Preserved as **DZ 872** / CT 872.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
