# Wǔ dāng fú dì zǒng zhēn jí 武當福地總真集

*Complete Collection of Perfected [Beings] at the Blessed Site of Wǔdāng*

compiled by [[劉道明]] (Liú Dàomíng) — Yuán Daoist at Wǔ dāng shān

A major **Yuán gazetteer-hagiography** of **Wǔ dāng shān** 武當山 (Húběi), the principal mountain-cult site of **Zhēn wǔ** 真武 / **Xuán tiān shàng dì** 玄天上帝. Compiled 1291, 3 *juàn*. Covers the site's geography, sacred history, resident perfected-beings, and miracles. A primary source for the Sòng-Yuán Wǔ dāng cult before Míng Chéng zǔ's monumental patronage of the mountain. Preserved as **DZ 962** / CT 962 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** 1291. Dynasty: 元.
