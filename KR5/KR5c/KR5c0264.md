# Guǐ Gǔ zǐ tiān suǐ líng wén 鬼谷子天髓靈文

*Master Guǐ Gǔ's Heavenly-Marrow Numinous-Writings*

attributed to [[鬼谷子]] (Guǐ Gǔ zǐ — legendary Warring-States master); actual composition later

A **later Daoist pseudepigraphon** attributed to **Guǐ Gǔ zǐ** 鬼谷子 — the legendary Warring-States master-sage of the *Guǐ gǔ zǐ* 鬼谷子 strategic-rhetorical treatise. The attribution is pseudepigraphic; actual composition is much later (Sòng-Yuán). Preserved as **DZ 867** / CT 867.

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán (per catalog "later"). Dynasty: 宋-元.
