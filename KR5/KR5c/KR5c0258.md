# Tài shàng liù rén míng jiàn fú yīn jīng 太上六壬明鑑符陰經

*Most High Six-Rén Bright-Mirror Talisman-Shade Scripture*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Sòng

A Daoist scripture on the **Liù rén** 六壬 divinatory tradition — one of the three main classical Chinese divinatory-astrological systems (alongside *Tài yǐ* and *Dùn jiǎ*). The integration of *liù rén* divination with talismans (*fú* 符) reflects the mature Daoist ritual-divinatory synthesis. Preserved as **DZ 861** / CT 861.

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Sòng. Dynasty: 六朝-宋.
