# Zhāng zhēn rén jīn shí líng shā lùn 張真人金石靈砂論

*Perfected Zhāng's Treatise on Metals, Minerals, and Numinous Cinnabar*

by [[張隱居]] (Zhāng yǐn jū, *fl.* 742–770) — mid-Táng Daoist alchemist

A mid-Táng **alchemical treatise** on *jīn shí* 金石 (metals and minerals) with emphasis on **numinous cinnabar** (*líng shā* 靈砂). Composed in the Tiān bǎo to Dà lì reigns (742–770). Preserved as **DZ 887** / CT 887 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** 742–770. Dynasty: 唐.
