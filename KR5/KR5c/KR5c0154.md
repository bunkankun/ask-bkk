# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn shuō cháng qīng jìng jīng zhù 太上老君說常清靜經註 (Hóu Shànyuān)

*Commentary on the Cháng qīng jìng jīng* — Hóu Shànyuān's commentary

by [[侯善淵]] (Hóu Shànyuān; Jīn-dynasty Quán zhēn Daoist, late 12th cent.); preface by [[毛麾]] (Máo Huī, *fl.* 1187)

A **Jīn-dynasty Quán zhēn commentary** on the **Cháng qīng jìng jīng** 常清靜經 ([[DZ 620]]) by **Hóu Shànyuān** 侯善淵 — a well-attested Quán zhēn master. Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 758** / CT 758 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類). Preface by **Máo Huī** 毛麾 (*fl.* 1187) — providing a chronological anchor.

## About the work

### Author

**Hóu Shànyuān** 侯善淵 is a well-documented Jīn-dynasty 金 Quán zhēn 全真 Daoist master, active in the late 12th century. He composed **multiple commentaries on Daoist scriptures**, of which several are preserved in the Daozang:

- **[[KR5c0154|DZ 758]]** *Cháng qīng jìng jīng zhù* (present work).
- **[[KR5c0158|DZ 632]]** *Tài qīng tiān tóng hù mìng miào jīng zhù* 太上太清天童護命妙經註.
- Several other short commentaries.

### Commentary character

Hóu Shànyuān's commentary reflects the mature Jīn-era Quán zhēn interpretive tradition:

1. **Pedagogical clarity** — colloquial register suitable for disciples.
2. ***Nèi dān*** 內丹 alchemical framing.
3. **Integration with *Dàodé jīng*** vocabulary.
