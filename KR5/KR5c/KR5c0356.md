# Zhōng nán shān Shuō jīng tái lì dài zhēn xiān bēi jì 終南山說經臺歷代真仙碑記

*Stele-Records of the Immortal Perfected through the Ages at the Shuōjīng Terrace of Zhōngnán Mountain*

by [[朱象先]] (Zhū Xiàngxiān)

A late-Yuán collection of **stele-inscriptions** (*bēi jì* 碑記) documenting the Daoist perfected associated with the **Shuō jīng tái** 說經臺 (Platform of Scripture-Expounding) at **Zhōng nán shān** 終南山 — the traditional site where Lǎo zǐ expounded the *Dào dé jīng* to Yǐn Xǐ. Preserved as **DZ 956** / CT 956 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** End of 13th century. Dynasty: 元.
