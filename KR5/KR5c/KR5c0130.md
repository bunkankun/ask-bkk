# Nán huá zhēn jīng zhāng jù yú shì 南華真經章句餘事

*Remnant Matters on the Chapters and Phrases of the True Scripture of the Southern Florescence*

by [[陳景元]] (Chén Jǐngyuán, *hào* Bìxūzǐ 碧虛子; 1024–1094)

A **supplementary apparatus** to Chén Jǐngyuán's main *Zhuāngzǐ* philological work [[KR5c0129|DZ 736 *Nán huá zhēn jīng zhāng jù yīn yì*]]. Preserved as **DZ 737** / CT 737 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類). As its title indicates (*yú shì* 餘事 = "remnant matters"), this work collects miscellaneous philological-scholarly notes and observations that did not fit into the main apparatus — including additional variant-readings, textual-critical cross-references, and bibliographical addenda.

## About the work

### Function

The *Yú shì* supplement:

1. Records **additional philological observations** not included in the main *Yīn yì* apparatus.
2. Provides **a further list of pre-Sòng *Zhuāngzǐ* commentators** (expanding the list in the main work).
3. Offers **miscellaneous scholarly notes** on the *Zhuāngzǐ*'s reception and interpretation.

The list of commentators at the end of DZ 737 (at 20b) was cited by Chǔ Bóxiù ([[褚伯秀]]) in his 1265 DZ 734 ([[KR5c0127]]) compilation as one of his principal bibliographical sources.

### Prefaces

No substantive authorial preface is preserved in the received DZ 737 text.

### Abstract

The *Yú shì* is a minor but philologically-valuable supplement to Chén Jǐngyuán's main *Zhuāngzǐ* apparatus. Together with [[KR5c0129]], it constitutes the basic scholarly toolkit that Chén Jǐngyuán developed for reading the *Zhuāngzǐ* in the late Northern-Sòng period.

**Dating.** Undated. Composition must fall within Chén Jǐngyuán's active period (1060s–1094), probably contemporary with or shortly after the 1084 main apparatus ([[KR5c0129]]). Per the project's dating rule, the frontmatter gives **1080–1094** as a conservative window. Dynasty: 宋.
