# Tài shàng bǎo zhēn yǎng shēng lùn 太上保真養生論

*Most High Treatise on Preserving the True and Nourishing Life*

Anonymous; Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties *yǎng shēng* treatise on **preserving the True** (*bǎo zhēn* 保真 — preserving one's innate nature) alongside nourishing life. Preserved as **DZ 852** / CT 852.

**Dating.** Five Dynasties (907–979). Dynasty: 五代.
