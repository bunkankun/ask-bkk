# Zhèng yī jiě è jiào yí 正一解戹醮儀

*Zhèng yī Liturgy for Dispelling Misfortune*

Anonymous; early Táng

A **Zhèng yī** 正一 liturgical manual (*jiào yí* 醮儀) for the ritual **dispelling of misfortune** (*jiě è* 解戹) — illness, accident, bad luck, calamity. Preserved as **DZ 794** / CT 794 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

## About the work

The text provides the step-by-step procedure for a Daoist *jiào* 醮 (offering-ritual) aimed at averting or dispelling various forms of misfortune. Standard in the mature Zhèng yī liturgical repertoire.

**Dating.** Early Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
