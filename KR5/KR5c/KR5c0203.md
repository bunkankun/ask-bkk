# Tài shàng jīn shū yù dié bǎo zhāng yí 太上金書玉牒寶章儀

*Precious Petitions Liturgy of the Most High Golden-Book and Jade-Tablet*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng liturgy for the submission of **precious petitions** (*bǎo zhāng* 寶章) to celestial authorities, invoking the authority of the **Golden-Book and Jade-Tablet** (*jīn shū yù dié* 金書玉牒) — the highest-rank heavenly records. Preserved as **DZ 806** / CT 806 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
