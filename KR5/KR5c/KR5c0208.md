# Tài shàng jīn guì yù jìng yán shēng dòng xuán zhú yōu chàn 太上金櫃玉鏡延生洞玄燭幽懺

*Most High Golden-Cabinet Jade-Mirror Life-Extension, Cavern-Mystery, Candle-in-Darkness Repentance*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A **Daoist confession-repentance liturgy** (*chàn* 懺) with multiple cumulative title-elements evoking light-giving talismanic symbols — the Golden Cabinet and Jade Mirror being traditional metaphors for heavenly records; the "Candle in Darkness" (*zhú yōu* 燭幽) being the ritual's metaphorical illumination of hidden transgressions. Preserved as **DZ 811** / CT 811 (*Dòngshén bù, Jiè lǜ lèi* 洞神部戒律類).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
