# Qì fǎ yào miào zhì jué 氣法要妙至訣

*Essential Marvelous Supreme Instructions on Pneumatic Methods*

Anonymous; late Táng

A late-Táng compilation of **essential instructions on *qì* methods** — gathering key teachings on breath-cultivation from various sources. Preserved as **DZ 831** / CT 831.

**Dating.** Late Táng (*Táng hòu qī* 唐後期). Dynasty: 唐.
