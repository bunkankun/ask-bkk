# Dà dān jì 大丹記

*Record of the Great Elixir*

attributed to [[魏伯陽]] (Wèi Bóyáng)

An **alchemical record** of elixir preparation attributed to the Eastern-Hàn alchemical patriarch Wèi Bóyáng. The attribution is pseudepigraphic; the received text is most plausibly Táng-Sòng. Preserved as **DZ 899** / CT 899 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
