# Dì zhǐ shàng jiàng Wēn tài bǎo zhuàn bǔ yí 地衹上將溫太保傳補遺

*Supplementary Materials to the Biography of the Supreme General of Earth-Spirits, Grand Protector Wēn*

by [[黃公瑾]] (Huáng Gōngjǐn); dated **1274**; companion to [[KR5c0176]]

A short **supplementary** (*bǔ yí* 補遺 = "gleaning of remnants") apparatus to [[KR5c0176|DZ 780 *Wēn tài bǎo zhuàn*]], also by Huáng Gōngjǐn (1274). Preserved as **DZ 781** / CT 781 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類).

## About the work

The *Bǔ yí* collects **additional hagiographic material** on Wēn qióng / Wēn tài bǎo that did not fit into the main DZ 780 biography — additional episodes, genealogical material, ritual-liturgical information.

**Dating.** 1274. Dynasty: 宋.
