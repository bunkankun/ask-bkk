# Tài qīng xiū dān mì jué 太清修丹祕訣

*Secret Instructions on Refining the Elixir according to Great-Clarity*

Anonymous; Táng

A short Táng Daoist **alchemical oral-instruction text** on elixir refinement in the **Tài qīng** 太清 tradition. Preserved as **DZ 884** / CT 884 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
