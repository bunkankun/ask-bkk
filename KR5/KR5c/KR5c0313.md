# Jīn huá chōng bì dān jīng mì zhǐ 金華沖碧丹經祕旨

*Secret Purport of the Gold-Flower Soaring-Azure Elixir Scripture*

transmitted by [[白玉蟾]] (Bái Yùchán, 1194–1229)

The **scripture proper** accompanying Bái Yùchán's transmission ([[KR5c0312]]) — a Southern-Lineage (*Nán zōng* 南宗) alchemical text. The pair is jointly preserved as **DZ 914** / CT 914 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類) in 2 *juàn*.

**Dating.** 1225. Dynasty: 南宋.
