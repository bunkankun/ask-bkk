# Hùn sú yí shēng lù 混俗頤生錄

*Record of Nourishing Life amid the Vulgar World*

by [[劉詞]] (Liú Cí); Five Dynasties

A Five-Dynasties *yǎng shēng* manual "for the vulgar world" (*hùn sú* 混俗) — i.e., for the lay practitioner rather than the professional Daoist monk. By **Liú Cí** 劉詞. Preserved as **DZ 848** / CT 848.

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
