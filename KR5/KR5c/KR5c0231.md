# Cún shén liàn qì míng 存神鍊氣銘

*Inscription on Preserving Spirit and Refining Pneuma*

attributed to [[孫思邈]] (Sūn Sīmiǎo, c. 581–682) — the famous SuíTáng physician

A short **inscription** (*míng* 銘) on the meditation practice of preserving *shén* 神 and refining *qì* 氣, attributed to the great SuíTáng physician **Sūn Sīmiǎo** 孫思邈 (traditional dates 581–682, though his actual dates are disputed — various sources give birth c. 541, 581, or later; generally agreed to have lived into the mid-7th century). Preserved as **DZ 834** / CT 834.

## About the work

### Sūn Sīmiǎo's Daoist corpus

Several short Daoist-medical texts are attributed to Sūn Sīmiǎo in the Daozang:

1. **[[KR5c0231|*Cún shén liàn qì míng*]]** (DZ 834) — the present text.
2. **[[KR5c0232|*Bǎo shēng míng*]]** 保生銘 (DZ 835).
3. **[[KR5c0238|*Sūn zhēn rén shè yǎng lùn*]]** 孫真人攝養論 (DZ 841).

These supplement his famous medical works: ***Qiān jīn fāng*** 千金方 ("Thousand-Gold Prescriptions") and ***Qiān jīn yì fāng*** 千金翼方.

**Dating.** Traditional Sūn Sīmiǎo dates (581–682). Dynasty: 隋-唐.
