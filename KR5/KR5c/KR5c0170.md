# Yóu lóng zhuàn 猶龍傳

*Biography of the "As If a Dragon"*

by [[賈善翔]] (Jiǎ Shànxiáng, *zì* Hóng jǔ 鴻舉; Northern Sòng); composed between 1086 and 1100

A major **Northern-Sòng hagiography of Lǎojūn** in six *juàn* by **Jiǎ Shànxiáng** 賈善翔. The title **Yóu lóng** 猶龍 ("As if a dragon") derives from Confucius's famous description of Lǎozǐ in *Shǐjì* 63: *lǎo zǐ qí yóu lóng xié* 老子其猶龍邪 ("Lǎozǐ — is he not like a dragon?"). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 774** / CT 774 (*Dòngshén bù, Pǔ lù lèi* 洞神部譜錄類), and in the Qīng *Dàozàng jíyào* as JY083.

## About the work

### Content

The *Yóu lóng zhuàn* provides one of the **most elaborate medieval Chinese biographical-hagiographic treatments of Lǎozǐ**. The text narrates:

1. **Lǎozǐ's cosmic origin** — his pre-cosmic existence and descent.
2. **His birth and early life** — the elaborate narrative of his mother's 81-year gestation, his birth at Bó zhōu 亳州, his physiognomic marks, his career at the Zhōu archives.
3. **His teaching activity** — especially his transmission of the *Dàodé jīng* to Yǐn Xǐ 尹喜 at the Hángǔ pass.
4. **His successive manifestations** across the ages — incarnations and revelations from the Three Sovereigns through the Táng imperial cult.
5. **His divinisation as Tài shàng Lǎo jūn** in the religious Daoist tradition.

### Significance

The *Yóu lóng zhuàn* is **one of the principal Northern-Sòng sources** for Xiè Shǒuhào's subsequent hagiographic trilogy ([[KR5c0166]], [[KR5c0167]], [[KR5c0169]]) and for the general late-imperial Chinese hagiographic tradition of Lǎozǐ. Its 6-*juàn* scope is unusually extensive for a pre-Sòng Lǎozǐ biography.

### Dating

Composed during Jiǎ Shànxiáng's active period, c. 1086–1100. Dynasty: 宋.
