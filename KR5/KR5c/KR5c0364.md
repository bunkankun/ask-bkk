# Xī chuān Qīng yáng gōng bēi míng 西川青羊宮碑銘

*Stele-Inscription for the Blue-Ram Palace of Xīchuān*

by [[樂朋龜]] (Yuè Péngguī) — late-Táng prose-stylist

A Táng **stele-inscription** (*bēi míng* 碑銘) for the famous **Qīng yáng gōng** 青羊宮 (Blue-Ram Palace) in **Xī chuān** 西川 (Chéngdū, Sìchuān) — the temple marking where Lǎo zǐ is said to have reappeared to Yǐn Xǐ leading a green/blue-horned ram. Dated 884. Preserved as **DZ 964** / CT 964 (*Dòngshén bù, Jì chuán lèi* 洞神部記傳類).

**Dating.** 884. Dynasty: 唐.
