# Dà dān wèn dá 大丹問答

*Catechism of the Great Elixir*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **alchemical catechism** (*wèn dá* 問答) on the great elixir, cast in question-and-answer form. Preserved as **DZ 939** / CT 939 (*Dòngshén bù, Zhòng shù lèi* 洞神部眾術類).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
