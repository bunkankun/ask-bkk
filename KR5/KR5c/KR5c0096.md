# Dàodé zhēn jīng jí zhù shì wén 道德真經集註釋文

*Textual Glosses to the Collected Commentaries on the True Scripture of the Way and Its Virtue*

by [[彭耜]] (Péng Sì, *hào* Hè lín zhēn yì 鶴林真逸); composed 1229 as companion to [[KR5c0095]] (DZ 707)

A **philological apparatus** (1 + 21 folios) to Péng Sì's *[[KR5c0095|DZ 707 Dàodé zhēn jīng jí zhù]]*, preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as **DZ 708** / CT 708 (*Dòngshén bù, Yù jué lèi* 洞神部玉訣類), and in the Qīng *Dàozàng jíyào* as JY050. This is one of three complementary works by Péng Sì on the *Dàodé jīng* — together with DZ 707 *Jí zhù* ([[KR5c0095]]) and DZ 709 *Jí zhù zá shuō* — all dating to 1229.

## About the work

Isabelle Robinet's notice in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004, 2:1166, DZ 708) gives the authoritative modern framing. Péng Sì **compares textual variations and glosses** in the *Dàodé jīng* text, drawing on:

- **Lù Démíng's** 陸德明 (c. 550–630) *Jīng diǎn shì wén* 經典釋文 — the classical Táng philological reference.
- **Chén Jǐngyuán's** 陳景元 (1025–1094) DZ 714 *Dàodé zhēn jīng cáng shì zuǎn wēi piān*.
- **Data taken from DZ 707 *Jí zhù*** ([[KR5c0095]]) — internal cross-reference with the main anthology.

The *shì wén* provides more substantial philological treatment than Liáng Jiǒng's earlier *shì yīn* ([[KR5c0094]], 1098), which was primarily a pronunciation aid. Péng Sì's DZ 708 is a full **critical-edition apparatus**, integrating variant readings, etymological notes, and parallel citations.

### Abstract

The *shì wén* is an indispensable complement to Péng Sì's main anthology. Where the DZ 707 *Jí zhù* collects and cites the commentators, the DZ 708 *Shì wén* addresses the underlying textual-philological issues that the commentators themselves often presuppose. Together they form one of the most comprehensive single pre-Míng critical-scholarly treatments of the *Dàodé jīng*.

**Dating.** Contemporary with DZ 707, so **1229**. Dynasty: 宋.
