# Shàng qīng jīn shū yù zì shàng jīng 上清金書玉字上經

*Superior Scripture of Gold-Writ and Jade-Graphs of Shàng qīng*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties (Shàng qīng tradition)

A Six-Dynasties **Shàng qīng 上清** scripture transmitted through the **Fāngzhū gōng Qīngtóng jūn** 方諸宫青童君 (Lord Blue-Lad of the Fāngzhū Palace), one of the chief teachers of the Shàng qīng revelation. The text belongs to the inner-esoteric Shàng qīng talismanic tradition of "gold-writ and jade-graphs" (*jīn shū yù zì* 金書玉字). Preserved as **DZ 879** / CT 879 (*Dòngshén bù, Fāngfǎ lèi* 洞神部方法類).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
