# Xiān tiān xuán miào yù nǚ tài shàng shèng mǔ zī chuán xiān dào 先天玄妙玉女太上聖母資傳仙道

*Resources for Transmission of the Immortal Way, from the Pre-Celestial Mysterious-Marvelous Jade Maiden, the Most High Sagely Mother*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist scripture attributed to the **Xuán miào yù nǚ** 玄妙玉女 ("Mysterious-Marvelous Jade Maiden") — the mother of Lǎojūn per Daoist hagiographic tradition. Preserved as **DZ 868** / CT 868.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
