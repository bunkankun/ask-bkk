# Běi dǒu qī yuán jīn xuán yǔ zhāng 北斗七元金玄羽章

*Feather-Stanzas of the Gold and Mystery of the Seven Origins of the Northern Dipper*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A short **hymnal** (*yǔ zhāng* 羽章 — "feather-stanzas," ritual hymns) for the **Seven Origins of the Northern Dipper** (*Běi dǒu qī yuán* 北斗七元). Preserved as **DZ 975** / CT 975 (*Dòngshén bù, Zàn sòng lèi* 洞神部讚頌類).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
