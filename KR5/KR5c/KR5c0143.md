# Tōng xuán zhēn jīng zuǎn yì 通玄真經纘義

*Continuation of the Meaning of the True Scripture of Communion with the Mystery*

by [[杜道堅]] (Dù Dàojiān, 1237–1318) — the Zhèng yī 正一 Daoist master; preface c. 1310

A major early-Yuán commentary on the *Wénzǐ* / *Tōng xuán zhēn jīng* ([[KR5c0118]]) by **Dù Dàojiān** 杜道堅 (1237–1318). Preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* as part of the DZ 746–747 *Tōng xuán zhēn jīng* cluster (the editorial detail is complex; see Schipper & Verellen 2004, 2:2900–2950). The same work is preserved independently (in partial form) in the Wén yuān gé *Sìkù quánshū* as **[[KR5c0119]] *Wénzǐ zuǎn yì*** — where the WYG version derives from *Yǒng lè dà diǎn* 永樂大典 extracts and lacks 5 *piān*.

## About the work

The [[KR5c0143]] Daozang edition is the **more complete version** of Dù Dàojiān's *Zuǎn yì* commentary on the *Wénzǐ* — containing all 12 *piān*, where the WYG [[KR5c0119]] is missing 5 *piān* (*Dào yuán*, *Jiǔ shǒu*, *Dào dé*, *Shàng rén*, *Shàng lǐ*).

See [[KR5c0119]] for the comprehensive treatment of the work — its editorial method (collecting pre-Sòng commentators including Xú Língfǔ), its philosophical character (extending Dù Dàojiān's *Dàodé jīng* work to the *Wénzǐ*), its date (c. 1310), and its significance for *Wénzǐ* scholarship. The two catalog entries refer to the same work in two different transmission witnesses; [[KR5c0143]] is the Daozang version (complete), [[KR5c0119]] the WYG version (partially reconstructed from *Yǒng lè dà diǎn*).

### Complementarity with the Daozang

Per Schipper & Verellen (2004, 2:3091): A Daozang version of Dù Dàojiān's commentary on the *Wénzǐ* "includes a brief biography... Quánjiè (dated 1310), Huáng Shíwēng 黃時翁 (1310), and Dù Dàojiān himself" (prefaces). This matches the [[KR5c0143]] edition.

### Prefaces

The Daozang edition preserves:
1. **Wáng Quán jiè** 王全節 preface (1310).
2. **Huáng Shíwēng** 黃時翁 preface (1310).
3. **Dù Dàojiān's** own preface.

These prefaces — not included in the WYG reconstruction — confirm the 1310 composition date and the Daoist-scholarly circle within which Dù Dàojiān's commentary was produced.

### Phonetic explanations

The Daozang edition concludes with **phonetic explanations** — apparently by Dù Dàojiān himself (Schipper & Verellen 2004, 2:3106: "clearly by Dù Dàojiān himself, as are the phonetic explanations that conclude the...").

### Abstract

[[KR5c0143]] is the **primary and most complete** edition of Dù Dàojiān's *Wénzǐ zuǎn yì*. Together with [[KR5c0119]] (the WYG reconstruction), it constitutes our evidence for one of the most important Yuán commentaries on the *Wénzǐ*.

**Dating.** 1310. Per the project's dating rule, the frontmatter gives **1310** as the composition date. Dynasty: 元.
