# Hé tú 河圖

*The Yellow-River Chart*

Anonymous (Léi Sīqí redaction); Sòng-Yuán

A companion to Léi Sīqí's ([[KR5d0034]]) *Yì tú tōng biàn* — treating the **Hé tú** 河圖 (Yellow-River Chart), the classical mathematical-cosmological diagram said to have emerged from the Yellow River in antiquity. Preserved as **DZ 1013** / CT 1013 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
