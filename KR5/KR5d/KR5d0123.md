# Míng hè yú yīn 鳴鶴餘音

*Lingering Notes of the Calling Crane*

compiled by [[彭致中]] (Péng Zhìzhōng) — Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist

The final text of the **Tài xuán bù** 太玄部 of the Dào zàng. A 9-*juàn* Yuán **Quán zhēn poetry anthology** — inner-alchemical *cí* 詞 lyrics by Quán zhēn patriarchs and disciples, compiled by Péng Zhìzhōng in 1347. One of the most important collections of *nèi dān* poetic literature. Preserved as **DZ 1100** / CT 1100 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1347. Dynasty: 元.
