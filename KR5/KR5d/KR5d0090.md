# Shàng yáng zǐ jīn dān dà yào 上陽子金丹大要

*Master Shàngyáng's Essentials of the Gold Elixir*

by [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhìxū, 1290–1343)

**Chén Zhìxū's principal inner-alchemical treatise** — a 16-*juàn* systematic exposition of *nèi dān* synthesizing Southern-Lineage and Northern-Lineage (Quán zhēn) teachings. Accompanied by illustrations [[KR5d0091]], biographies [[KR5d0092]], and lineage tables [[KR5d0093]]. Preserved as **DZ 1067** / CT 1067 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** *c.* 1331. Dynasty: 元.
