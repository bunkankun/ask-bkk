# Yì wài bié zhuàn 易外別傳

*Separate Transmission Outside the Yì*

by [[俞琰]] (Yǔ Yǎn, 1245–1314)

Yǔ Yǎn's treatise on **what lies "outside" the *Zhōu yì*** — specifically the alchemical and meditative uses of *Yì jīng* cosmology. An essential companion to his *Cān tóng qì* studies. Preserved as **DZ 1009** / CT 1009 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** c. 1284. Dynasty: 宋-元.
