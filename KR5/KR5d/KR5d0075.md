# Zōng xuán xiān sheng Xuán gāng lùn 宗玄先生玄綱論

*Master Zōngxuán's Discourse on the Fundamental Principles of the Mystery*

by [[吳筠]] (Wú Yún, *d.* 778)

**Wú Yún's systematic philosophical synthesis** — the most celebrated of his works, expounding the metaphysics, cosmology, and practice of the Way in 33 short sections (*zhāng* 章). A masterpiece of mid-Táng Daoist synthesis, combining Chóng xuán 重玄 metaphysics with a graduated cultivation path. Preserved as **DZ 1052** / CT 1052 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Within Wú Yún's lifetime. Dynasty: 唐.
