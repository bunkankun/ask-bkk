# Yī chuān jī rǎng jí 伊川擊壤集

*The Yīchuān Ground-Striking Collection*

by [[邵雍]] (Shào Yōng, 1011–1077)

**Shào Yōng's poetry collection** — *jī rǎng* 擊壤 ("striking the ground") evokes the carefree songs of the peasant in the legendary *Jī rǎng gē* 擊壤歌, signaling Shào's recluse-philosopher persona. The verses integrate his cosmological system with everyday reflection. Preserved as **DZ 1042** / CT 1042 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Shào's active lifetime. Dynasty: 北宋.
