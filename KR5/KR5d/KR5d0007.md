# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo jiǔ zhēn rén wǔ fù sān guī xíng dào guān mén jīng 洞玄靈寶九真人五復三歸行道觀門經

*Dòngxuán Língbǎo Scripture of the Nine Perfected, Five-Fold Reversal, Three Refuges, and Dào-Practice Gate of Contemplation*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Líng bǎo 靈寶 meditation-scripture combining multiple doctrinal-numerological schemes — the Nine Perfected (*jiǔ zhēn rén* 九真人), the Five Reversals (*wǔ fù* 五復), the Three Refuges (*sān guī* 三歸), and the Gate-of-Contemplation for Practising the Way (*xíng dào guān mén* 行道觀門). Preserved as **DZ 990** / CT 990 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
