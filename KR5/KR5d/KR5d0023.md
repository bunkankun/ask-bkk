# Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù 周易參同契分章註

*Chapter-Divided Commentary on the Cān tóng qì*

by [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhìxū, 1290–1343) — Yuán Daoist alchemist, "Shàng yáng zǐ"

Chén Zhìxū's three-*juàn* chapter-divided commentary on the *Cān tóng qì*. Chén was one of the central Yuán synthesizers of Southern-Lineage (Zhāng Bóduān) and Northern-Lineage (Quán zhēn) *nèi dān*.

## Tiyao

The Sìkù editors write: "The *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù*, three *juàn*, by Chén Zhìxū 陳致虛 of the Yuán. Zhìxū's *zì* was Guān wú 觀吾, and his self-styled *hào* was Shàng yáng zǐ 上陽子. At the age of forty he began to study under Zhào Yǒuqīn 趙友欽..."

## Abstract

Chén's commentary reads the *Cān tóng qì* within his Yuán inner-alchemical synthesis, emphasizing the scripture's congruence with the Southern-Lineage *Wù zhēn piān* and the Northern-Lineage Quán zhēn teachings. Composed during Chén's active period in the 1320s-30s.

**Dating.** Within Chén Zhìxū's active life (c. 1330–1343). Dynasty: 元.
