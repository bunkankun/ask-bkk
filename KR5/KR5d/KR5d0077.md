# Nán tǒng dà jūn nèi dān jiǔ zhāng jīng 南統大君內丹九章經

*Scripture of the Nine Stanzas on Inner Elixir from the Great Lord of the Southern Unity*

attributed to [[吳筠]] (Wú Yún)

A Five-Dynasties Daoist **inner-alchemical scripture in nine stanzas**, attributed to the Táng Wú Yún but likely post-dating him. Preserved as **DZ 1054** / CT 1054 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 五代.
