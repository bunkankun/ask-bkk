# Jīn suǒ liú zhū yǐn 金鎖流珠引

*Gold-Lock Flowing-Pearl Induction*

attributed to [[李淳風]] (Lǐ Chúnfēng, 602–670) — early-Táng astronomer and polymath

A **massive 29-*juàn* Daoist ritual-astrological compendium** pseudepigraphically attributed to Lǐ Chúnfēng. Covers astral ritual, Dipper-pacing (*bù gāng* 步罡), exorcistic techniques, and divination. Preserved as **DZ 1015** / CT 1015 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Received recension Táng to Sòng; the attribution to Lǐ Chúnfēng is pseudepigraphic. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
