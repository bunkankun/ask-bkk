# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo Tiān guān jīng 太上洞玄靈寶天關經

*Most High Dòngxuán Língbǎo Scripture of the Heavenly Barrier*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Daoist **scripture on the Heavenly Barrier** (*tiān guān* 天關) — the cosmological threshold between the mortal and celestial realms. Preserved as **DZ 987** / CT 987 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
