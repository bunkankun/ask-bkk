# Zhōu yì cān tóng qì kǎo yì 周易參同契考異 — "Recension III"

*Textual Criticism of the Zhōu yì cān tóng qì*

by [[朱熹]] (Zhū Xī, 1130–1200) — under the pseudonym Zōu Xīn 鄒訢

**Zhū Xī's** philological study of the *Cān tóng qì*, the **third of the three Dào zàng recensions**. Originally titled *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì kǎo yì* 周易參同契考異 ("Textual Variants"), Zhū published it pseudonymously as "Kōng tóng Dàoshì Zōu Xīn" 空同道士鄒訢 in 1197, signaling scholarly distance from open Confucian engagement with a Daoist alchemical classic. Zhū's edition has been enormously influential in the philological reception of the *Cān tóng qì*. Preserved as **DZ 1001** / CT 1001 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1197. Dynasty: 南宋.

## Translations and research

- Chan Wing-tsit. *Chu Hsi: New Studies*. University of Hawai'i Press, 1989. Includes treatment of Zhū Xī's engagement with Daoist thought.
- Pregadio, Fabrizio. *The Seal of the Unity of the Three*. 2011. Discusses all three Dào zàng recensions.
