# Shān hǎi jīng 山海經

*The Classic of Mountains and Seas*

commentary by [[郭璞]] (Guō Pú, 276–324)

The ***Shān hǎi jīng*** 山海經 — the foundational **Chinese classic of mythical geography and zoology**, recording strange peoples, beasts, plants, minerals, deities, and spirits organized by cardinal directions. Compiled from pre-Qīn oral and ritual sources through the Western Hàn, receiving its canonical 18-*juàn* shape at the hands of Liú Xiāng 劉向 and Liú Xīn 劉歆; Guō Pú's Eastern-Jìn commentary is the standard received commentary preserved in the Dào zàng. Preserved as **DZ 1031** / CT 1031 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Core text Warring-States through Hàn; Guō Pú commentary early 4th century. Dynasty: 戰國-晉.
