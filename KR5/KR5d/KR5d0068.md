# Hǎi kè lùn 海客論

*Discourse of the Sea-Guest*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A short Daoist philosophical treatise cast as a discourse by a "sea-guest" (*hǎi kè* 海客 — conventional trope for a cosmopolitan wanderer). Preserved as **DZ 1045** / CT 1045 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
