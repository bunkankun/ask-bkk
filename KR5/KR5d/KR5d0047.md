# Huáng dì bā shí yī Nán jīng zuǎn tú jù jiě 黃帝八十一難經纂圖句解

*Huángdì Eighty-One Nánjīng with Compiled Diagrams and Phrase-by-Phrase Exegesis*

by [[李駉]] (Lǐ Jiōng) — Southern-Sòng medical scholar

A Southern-Sòng **diagrammed phrase-by-phrase commentary** on the ***Nán jīng*** 難經 ("Classic of Difficulties") — the companion-classic to the *Huáng dì nèi jīng*, probably Eastern-Hàn in date, arranging 81 medical "difficulties" (*nán* 難) of pulse-taking, viscera, meridians, and acupuncture. Preface dated 1269. Preserved as **DZ 1024** / CT 1024 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1269 (preface). Dynasty: 南宋.
