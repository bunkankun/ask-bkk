# Dān yáng zhēn rén yǔ lù 丹陽真人語錄

*Recorded Sayings of the Perfected Dānyáng*

compiled by [[王頤中]] (Wáng Yízhōng); sayings of Mǎ Yù 馬鈺 (Dān yáng zǐ 丹陽子, 1123–1184)

The **recorded sayings of Mǎ Yù** 馬鈺 (1123–1184), *hào* Dān yáng zǐ 丹陽子 — one of the **Seven Perfected** (*qī zhēn* 七眞) of Quán zhēn, the chief disciple of Wáng Chóngyáng 王重陽, and senior patriarch of the **Yù xiān** 遇仙 branch of Quán zhēn. Compiled by his disciple Wáng Yízhōng. Preserved as **DZ 1057** / CT 1057 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Late 12th century. Dynasty: 金.
