# Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fā huī 周易參同契發揮

*Exegesis of the Cān tóng qì*

by [[俞琰]] (Yǔ Yǎn, 1245–1314) — Sòng-loyalist Yì jīng and Cān tóng qì scholar

**Yǔ Yǎn's monumental 9-*juàn* commentary on the *Cān tóng qì*** — **perhaps the single most learned pre-modern commentary on the text**. Preface dated 1284. Yǔ Yǎn brings his exceptional mastery of the *Zhōu yì* together with the Cān tóng qì's own hexagrammatic scheme. Preserved as **DZ 1005** / CT 1005 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1284. Dynasty: 宋-元.

## Translations and research

- Pregadio, Fabrizio. *The Seal of the Unity of the Three*. Golden Elixir Press, 2011. Yǔ Yǎn's commentary is a principal source.
