# Jìn zhēn rén yǔ lù 晉真人語錄

*Recorded Sayings of the Perfected Jìn*

Anonymous (compiler); sayings of **Jìn zhēn rén** 晉真人

A JīnYuán Quán zhēn **recorded-sayings** collection, transmitting the teachings of **Jìn Yùshòu** 晉玉壽 (a Quán zhēn master from the generation of Qiū Chǔjī 丘處機's disciples). Preserved as **DZ 1056** / CT 1056 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 13th century. Dynasty: 金-元.
