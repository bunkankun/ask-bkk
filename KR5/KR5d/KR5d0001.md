# Xuán jīng bì xiá Líng bǎo jù xuán jīng 玄精碧匣靈寶聚玄經

*Líng bǎo Scripture of Mystery-Accumulation from the Azure Casket of the Mysterious Essence*

Anonymous; Sòng

The opening scripture of the **Tài xuán bù** 太玄部 (Great Mystery Section) of the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng — one of the four supplementary sections of the canon, this one supplementing the **Dòngzhēn bù** 洞真部 / Shàng qīng 上清 tradition. A three-*juàn* Sòng ritual-alchemical scripture in the Líng bǎo 靈寶 current. Preserved as **DZ 984** / CT 984.

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
