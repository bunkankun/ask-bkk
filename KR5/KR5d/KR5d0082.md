# Pán shān Qī yún Wáng zhēn rén yǔ lù 盤山棲雲王真人語錄

*Recorded Sayings of the Perfected Wáng Qīyún of Pánshān*

compiled by [[論志煥]] (Lùn Zhìhuàn); sayings of Wáng Zhìjǐn 王志謹 (1178–1263)

The recorded sayings of **Wáng Zhìjǐn** 王志謹 (1178–1263), *hào* Qī yún 棲雲 — a major second-generation Quán zhēn patriarch who resided at Pán shān 盤山. Preface dated 1247. Preserved as **DZ 1059** / CT 1059 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1247 (preface). Dynasty: 元.
