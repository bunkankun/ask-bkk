# Shàng qīng Wú yīng zhēn tóng hé yóu nèi biàn yù jīng 上清無英真童合遊內變玉經

*Shàngqīng Jade-Scripture on the Inner Transformations of the Wúyīng Perfected and the Perfect-Lad Journeying Together*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Táng

A Shàng qīng 上清 **inner-meditation scripture** on the joint visualization of **Wú yīng** 無英 and **Zhēn tóng** 真童 (the "Blossomless [Youth]" and the "Perfect Lad") — deities of the inner bodily pantheon in the classical Shàng qīng tradition. Preserved as **DZ 988** / CT 988 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
