# Huán dān fù mìng piān 還丹復命篇

*Treatise on the Return-Elixir for Restoring One's Destiny*

by [[薛道光]] (Xuē Dàoguāng, 1078–1191) — Third Patriarch of the Nán zōng

**Xuē Dàoguāng's principal inner-alchemical treatise**, preface dated 1126. One of the foundational texts of Southern-Lineage (*Nán zōng*) *nèi dān*. Preserved as **DZ 1088** / CT 1088 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1126. Dynasty: 北宋.
