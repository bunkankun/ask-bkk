# Shàng yáng zǐ jīn dān dà yào liè xiān zhì 上陽子金丹大要列仙誌

*Record of the Ranked Immortals in Master Shàngyáng's Essentials of the Gold Elixir*

by [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhìxū, 1290–1343)

**Companion hagiographical volume** to [[KR5d0090]] *Jīn dān dà yào* — biographies of inner-alchemical masters in Chén Zhìxū's lineage, including his teacher Zhào Yǒuqīn 趙友欽. Preserved as **DZ 1069** / CT 1069 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** *c.* 1331. Dynasty: 元.
