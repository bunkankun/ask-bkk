# Guǐ gǔ zǐ 鬼谷子

*Master Ghost-Valley*

attributed to [[鬼谷子]] (Guǐ gǔ zǐ)

The ***Guǐ gǔ zǐ*** is a short pre-Qīn / Hàn **rhetorical-diplomatic treatise** on political persuasion — nominally attributed to the legendary teacher of the Warring-States diplomats Sū Qín and Zhāng Yí. The text expounds the arts of *bài hé* 捭闔 (opening and closing), *fǎn yìng* 反應 (responsive reaction), and related techniques of persuasion-by-contextual-calibration. Preserved as **DZ 1025** / CT 1025 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Core text Warring-States to early Hàn; received recension Hàn. Dynasty: 戰國-漢.
