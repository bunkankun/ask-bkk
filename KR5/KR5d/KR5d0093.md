# Shàng yáng zǐ jīn dān dà yào xiān pài 上陽子金丹大要仙派

*Immortal Lineage in Master Shàngyáng's Essentials of the Gold Elixir*

by [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhìxū, 1290–1343)

**Lineage-chart volume** accompanying [[KR5d0090]] *Jīn dān dà yào* — tabular presentation of Chén's synthesis of Southern-Lineage (Zhāng Bóduān 張伯端) and Northern-Lineage (Wáng Chóngyáng 王重陽 / Quán zhēn) inner-alchemical lineages. Preserved as **DZ 1070** / CT 1070 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** *c.* 1331. Dynasty: 元.
