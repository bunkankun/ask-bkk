# Sù wèn rù shì yùn qì lùn ào 素問入式運氣論奧

*Mystery-Discourse for Entering the System of Movements-and-Pneumas in the Sùwèn*

by [[劉溫舒]] (Liú Wēnshū) — Northern-Sòng medical scholar

A Northern-Sòng treatise on the ***Five Movements and Six Pneumas*** (*wǔ yùn liù qì* 五運六氣) cosmological-medical system — the calendrical-astrological framework whereby illness-patterns correlate with year-stems, branches, and atmospheric cycles. The system was elaborated in Wáng Bīng's supplementary chapters of the *Sù wèn*. Dated 1099. Preserved as **DZ 1022** / CT 1022 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1099. Dynasty: 北宋.
