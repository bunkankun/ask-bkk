# Wú zūn shī zhuàn 吳尊師傳

*Biography of the Venerable Master Wú*

by [[權德輿]] (Quán Déyú, 761–818) — mid-Táng statesman

A Táng **biography of [[吳筠]] Wú Yún** (*d.* 778), composed by the mid-Táng statesman Quán Déyú. Important primary source for Wú Yún's life. Preserved as **DZ 1053** / CT 1053 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Within Quán Déyú's active lifetime. Dynasty: 唐.
