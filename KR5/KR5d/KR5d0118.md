# Zhèng dào gē 證道歌

*Song of Verifying the Way*

by [[左掌子]] (Zuǒ zhǎng zǐ) — Northern-Sòng Daoist

A short Northern-Sòng **Daoist verse-text** echoing the title of Yǒngjiā Xuánjué's 永嘉玄覺 Chán classic *Zhèng dào gē*. Preserved as **DZ 1095** / CT 1095 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋.
