# Kàng cāng zǐ 亢倉子

*Master Kàngcāng*

composed by [[王士源]] (Wáng Shìyuán, 742); pseudepigraphically attributed to [[庚桑楚]] (Gēngsāng Chǔ)

A Táng pseudepigraphon attributed to the Warring-States figure **Gēngsāng Chǔ** 庚桑楚 (a disciple of Lǎo dān mentioned in the *Zhuāng zǐ*). Actually composed by Wáng Shìyuán 王士源 in 742 (Tiān bǎo 1), who acknowledges the fabrication in his preface. The text plays a double role in the Táng canon: it was one of the **four apocryphal Daoist classics** promoted by Táng Xuán zōng and studied for the *dào jǔ* 道舉 Daoist examinations. Catalogued in the Kanripo as a separate text from the matching Dào zàng entry [[KR5c0050]]. Not preserved in the Kanseki mirror source tree (catalog entry only).

**Dating.** 742. Dynasty: 唐.
