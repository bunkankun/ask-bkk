# Tài xū xīn yuān piān 太虛心淵篇

*Treatise on the Heart-Abyss of Great Emptiness*

Anonymous; SòngYuán

A Daoist **inner-alchemical meditation treatise** on the "heart-abyss" (*xīn yuān* 心淵) within the "great emptiness" (*tài xū* 太虛). Preserved as **DZ 1047** / CT 1047 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** SòngYuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
