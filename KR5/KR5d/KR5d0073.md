# Huá yáng Táo yǐn jū jí 華陽陶隱居集

*Collected Works of the Huáyáng Recluse Táo*

by [[陶弘景]] (Táo Hóngjǐng, 456–536) — Ninth Shàng qīng Patriarch

**The collected literary works of Táo Hóngjǐng** — prose, poetry, and letters. "Huá yáng yǐn jū" 華陽隱居 ("Recluse of Huáyáng") was Táo's self-styled *hào* after he retired to Máo shān 茅山 (where the Huá yáng 華陽 grotto was understood to be located). The 2-*juàn* collection preserved in the Dào zàng is a later selection, not the complete *wén jí* that circulated in Táo's own time. Preserved as **DZ 1050** / CT 1050 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táo Hóngjǐng's lifetime (456–536). Dynasty: 南朝.
