# KR5d 太玄部 *Tàixuán bù* — Daoist Canon → Great Mystery

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR5d 太玄部 (*Tàixuán bù*, "Great Mystery") collects 123 works (DZ 984 – DZ 1100 in the Schipper / Verellen *Concordance du Tao-tsang* numbering) — the first of the *sì fǔ* 四輔 ("Four Supplements") of the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (1445), which sit after the *sāndòng* 三洞 ("Three Caverns" — [[KR5a]] 洞真, [[KR5b]] 洞玄, [[KR5c]] 洞神). In the classical *sì fǔ* scheme, *Tàixuán* is the supplement attached to the *Dòngzhēn bù* and to the Shàngqīng 上清 tradition: it was originally the textual home of the Lǎozǐ-and-its-commentaries — that being the senior pre-revelation classical layer Shàngqīng claimed as its philosophical ancestry. Wilkinson §29.4.3–4 notes that the *sāndòng* are *schools* and the cross-cutting *shí'èr lèi* 十二類 are *genres*, with the four supplements wedged in between — and that by the Míng compilation the school-axis had ceased to cohere with the actual contents of each section. Nowhere is the dislocation sharper than here: the Lǎozǐ corpus that *Tàixuán* was theoretically built around was in the event placed in [[KR5c]] *Dòngshén bù* during the Sòng-era catalog merger Schipper & Verellen reconstruct (*Taoist Canon* 1: 30–40), and what fills the surviving *Tàixuán bù* of the Míng canon is what the editors had left over once the three Caverns and the Lǎozǐ block had been fixed: a heterogeneous archive of (i) a tail of supplementary Shàngqīng / Língbǎo scriptures the *Dòngzhēn* and *Dòngxuán* blocks did not absorb, (ii) the entire ***Zhōuyì cāntóngqì*** 周易參同契 commentary tradition — the foundational scripture of Chinese alchemy, structurally tied to the *Yì jīng* and to the Lǎozǐ but not at home in either, (iii) the great Daoist anthologies *Yún jí qī qiān* 雲笈七籤 [[KR5d0055]] and *Dào shū* 道樞 [[KR5d0039]], (iv) the Daoist-affiliated medical classics *Huáng dì nèi jīng* and *Nán jīng* with their canonical TángSòng commentaries [[KR5d0040]]–[[KR5d0047]], (v) the Tao Hongjing flagship *Zhēn gào* 真誥 [[KR5d0036]] — the foundational document of Shàngqīng, here because *Tàixuán* is technically the Shàngqīng supplement, (vi) a *zhūzǐ* 諸子 (masters) cluster of Lǎo-Zhuāng-orbit pseudepigraphic works — *Guǐ gǔ zǐ* [[KR5d0048]], *Kàng cāng zǐ* [[KR5d0037]] [[KR5d0038]], *Tiān yǐn zǐ* [[KR5d0049]], *Sù lǚ zǐ* [[KR5d0050]], *Wú néng zǐ* [[KR5d0051]], *Xuán zhēn zǐ* [[KR5d0052]], *Liú zǐ* [[KR5d0053]] — and the *Shān hǎi jīng* 山海經 [[KR5d0054]] with [[郭璞]]'s commentary, (vii) the Quánzhēn 全真 *yǔlù* 語錄 ("recorded sayings") corpus that documents the early Northern-Lineage patriarchs [[KR5d0079]]–[[KR5d0083]], and (viii) a dense Southern-Sòng / Jīn / Yuán *nèidān* 內丹 commentary archive culminating in [[陳致虛]]'s *Shàng yáng zǐ jīn dān dà yào* [[KR5d0090]]–[[KR5d0093]] and the Yuán Quánzhēn *cí*-anthology *Míng hè yú yīn* 鳴鶴餘音 [[KR5d0123]] that closes the section. The *Tàixuán bù* is therefore best read not as a coherent textual school but as the *Dàozàng*'s structural overflow basin for major Daoist literature that the three-Cavern scheme could not house.

The chronological span runs from the late-Warring-States *Shān hǎi jīng* core (c. 400 BCE, [[KR5d0054]]) and the *Guǐ gǔ zǐ* (Warring States – Hàn, [[KR5d0048]]) through to the early-Míng *Suí jī yìng huà lù* 隨機應化錄 of [[何道全]] (1401, [[KR5d0099]]). Date distribution by composition midpoint is sharply skewed to the SòngYuán: a thin pre-Táng nucleus (5–6 titles: the *Shān hǎi jīng*, *Guǐ gǔ zǐ*, *Líng qí běn zhāng zhèng jīng* [[KR5d0064]], *Liú zǐ* [[KR5d0053]], *Zhēn gào* [[KR5d0036]], *Huá yáng Táo yǐn jū jí* [[KR5d0073]]); a substantial Táng layer (~15 titles, anchored by [[王冰]]'s 762 *Sù wèn* recension [[KR5d0040]] and the [[司馬承禎]] / [[吳筠]] philosophical-Daoist cluster); a sharp Five-Dynasties peak with the 947 [[彭曉]] *Cān tóng qì* commentary [[KR5d0019]] [[KR5d0020]] and [[譚峭]]'s *Huà shū* [[KR5d0067]]; a major Northern-Sòng wave driven by [[張君房]]'s *Yún jí qī qiān* and [[邵雍]]'s cosmological-historical corpus; the dominant **Southern-Sòng / Jīn / Yuán mass (~80 titles, c. 1100–1400)** that contains the entire *Cān tóng qì* commentary archipelago, the Quánzhēn *yǔlù* cluster, the [[俞琰]] and [[雷思齊]] Yì-cosmology clusters, and the late-Yuán *nèidān* synthesis of [[陳致虛]] and his successors; and a sparse early-Míng tail terminating at [[何道全]] (1401) and [[彭致中]] (1347). The section therefore reads in practice as the great SòngYuán Daoist-syncretic archive — bracketed by a token classical residue at one end and a Quánzhēn-poetry coda at the other.

## Important texts and text clusters

**The opening Shàngqīng / Língbǎo residue (本文類, [[KR5d0001]]–[[KR5d0011]]).** Eleven short scriptures that the *Dòngzhēn* and *Dòngxuán* compilers did not absorb, falling onto the *Tàixuán* supplement as overflow from the senior Shàngqīng tradition. The cluster includes [[KR5d0001]] *Xuán jīng bì xiá Líng bǎo jù xuán jīng*, [[KR5d0002]] *Tài shàng Dòngxuán Líng bǎo sān yī wǔ qì zhēn jīng*, the *Shàng qīng* short scriptures [[KR5d0005]] [[KR5d0006]] [[KR5d0010]] [[KR5d0011]], and the Líng bǎo / Dòngxuán short scriptures [[KR5d0003]] [[KR5d0004]] [[KR5d0007]] [[KR5d0008]] [[KR5d0009]]. Most are Six-Dynasties – Táng in core date with substantial Sòng-era editorial layers; the cluster is the principal reason the section is *technically* still the Shàngqīng supplement.

**The *Zhēn gào* and the Shàngqīng documentary anchor.**
- *Zhēn gào* 真誥 [[KR5d0036]] — [[陶弘景]] (456–536), finished form 499. **The foundational compilation of the Shàngqīng revelation**, gathering the séance records of Yáng Xī 楊羲 and the Xǔ 許 family (364–370) with Táo's philological apparatus including the celebrated marginal notes on autograph-handwriting identification — at once the central document of fourth-century Shàngqīng visionary practice and a masterpiece of medieval textual criticism.
- *Huá yáng Táo yǐn jū jí* 華陽陶隱居集 [[KR5d0073]] — [[陶弘景]]'s collected works in 2 *juàn*, edited by Fù Xiāo 傅霄 (d. 1159) and Chén Jué 陳桷 (fl. 1131–1162); the principal source for Táo's prose and verse outside the *Zhēn gào*.

**The *Zhōuyì cāntóngqì* 周易參同契 commentary archipelago — the densest single exegetical cluster in the section.** The foundational alchemical scripture, traditionally ascribed to the Eastern-Hàn [[魏伯陽]] (Wèi Bóyáng) — the **Urtext of both *wài dān* 外丹 (external alchemy) and SòngYuán *nèi dān* 內丹 (internal alchemy)**, structurally a synthesis of *Yì jīng* cosmology, HuángLǎo metaphysics, and alchemical practice. The *Tàixuán bù* preserves five distinct received recensions of the text plus its principal traditional commentaries:
- *Recension I*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì* (一) [[KR5d0016]] — Táng recension with commentary ascribed to the Hàn immortal [[陰長生]] (Yīn Chángshēng), pseudepigraphical.
- *Recension II*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì zhù* (二) [[KR5d0017]] — twelfth-century or later anonymous commentary.
- *Recension III*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì kǎo yì* 考異 [[KR5d0018]] — **[[朱熹]] (Zhū Xī, 1130–1200)**'s 1197 philological collation, the principal Neo-Confucian engagement with the alchemical classic and an unusual instance of a major Chéng-Zhū-school text entering the Daoist canon.
- *Recension IV*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì zhù* (四) [[KR5d0021]] — Five-Dynasties anonymous.
- *Recension V*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì zhù* (五) [[KR5d0027]] — Southern Sòng commentary by [[儲華谷]].
- *Péng Xiǎo's commentary*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fēn zhāng tōng zhēn yì* [[KR5d0019]] + *Dǐng qì gē míng jìng tú* [[KR5d0020]] — **[[彭曉]] (Péng Xiǎo, d. 955)**, 947, **the single most influential traditional commentary on the *Cān tóng qì***; Péng's structural division into chapters and his coherent alchemical reading furnish the interpretive framework inherited by every later commentator, including Zhū Xī.
- *Yú Yǎn's commentary*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fā huī* 發揮 [[KR5d0022]] (1284) + *Shì yí* 釋疑 [[KR5d0024]] (1281) — [[俞琰]] (Yú Yǎn, 1258–1314), the principal SòngYuán transition commentary.
- *Chén Xiǎnwēi's commentary*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì jiě* 解 [[KR5d0025]] (1234) — [[陳顯微]], Southern Sòng nèidān reading.
- *Chén Zhìxū's commentary*: *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù* 分章註 [[KR5d0023]] (Yuán) — [[陳致虛]].
- *Jiǎng Yībiāo's collection*: *Gǔ wén Cān tóng qì jí jiě* [[KR5d0026]] (Míng) — [[蔣一彪]] (fl. 1614)'s late-Míng compendium.

**The "Old-Text Dragon-Tiger Classic" satellite cluster.** A late-Hàn / Táng pseudepigraphic alchemical scripture treated by Sòng commentators as either a source-text of, or a parallel rewriting of, the *Cāntóng qì* itself:
- *Gǔ wén Lóng hǔ jīng zhù shū* 古文龍虎經註疏 [[KR5d0013]] — [[王道]] (Sòng).
- *Gǔ wén Lóng hǔ shàng jīng zhù* [[KR5d0014]] and *Dú Lóng hǔ jīng* [[KR5d0015]] — anonymous Sòng companion commentaries.
- *Lóng hǔ yuán zhǐ* 龍虎元旨 [[KR5d0106]] — [[青霞子]] (late Táng – Five Dynasties), the philosophical-cosmological reading.
- *Lóng hǔ huán dān jué sòng* [[KR5d0105]] ([[林太古]], N. Sòng) and *Lóng hǔ huán dān jué* [[KR5d0107]] ([[李真人]], N. Sòng) — the operative-alchemy gloss tradition.

**The Yú Yǎn — Léi Sīqí Yì-cosmology cluster.** Two SòngYuán figures who reread the *Yì jīng* through Daoist-cosmological lenses, jointly responsible for placing Yì-numerology inside the canon:
- [[俞琰]] (Yú Yǎn, 1258–1314): the *Cāntóng qì* commentaries above plus *Yì wài bié zhuàn* 易外別傳 [[KR5d0028]] (the Yìjīng-as-alchemical-key reading, c. 1284), *Xí shàng fǔ tán* 席上腐談 [[KR5d0029]] (philosophical miscellany), *Xuán pìn zhī mén fù* 玄牝之門賦 [[KR5d0030]] — Yú Yǎn alone accounts for **six titles** in the section, the most prolific single author in *Tàixuán bù*.
- [[雷思齊]] (Léi Sīqí, 1223–1301, "Kōng shān xiān sheng" 空山先生): *Yì shì tōng biàn* 易筮通變 [[KR5d0031]], *Hé tú* 河圖 [[KR5d0033]], *Yì tú tōng biàn* 易圖通變 [[KR5d0034]] (1330); with prefatory piece [[KR5d0032]] (1300) — the principal Daoist *Yì*-diagrammatics tradition of the Yuán, parallel to the Confucian *Hé tú / Luò shū* speculation of [[邵雍]].

**[[邵雍]]'s cosmological-historical corpus.** The Sòng Yìxué master enters the Daoist canon through his two principal works:
- *Huáng jí jīng shì* 皇極經世 [[KR5d0063]] — [[邵雍]] (Shào Yōng, 1011–1077), the great cosmological-historical treatise; the entire history of the cosmos and of human civilization charted on the 元 (yuán) – 會 (huì) – 運 (yùn) – 世 (shì) grid yielding world-cycles of 129,600 years. Together with the *Cān tóng qì*, the foundational reference of East Asian speculative numerology.
- *Yī chuān jī rǎng jí* 伊川擊壤集 [[KR5d0065]] — Shào's poetry collection in 20 *juàn*, the principal source for his philosophical *shī* and the textual record of his Luòyáng circle (Sī Mǎguāng, Chéng Yí, Zhāng Zǎi). The presence in *Tàixuán bù* of two major works by a figure conventionally listed as a Neo-Confucian *Yìxué* master is one of the section's striking editorial decisions.

**The great Northern Sòng / Southern Sòng Daoist anthologies.**
- *Yún jí qī qiān* 雲笈七籤 [[KR5d0055]] — [[張君房]] (Zhāng Jūnfáng, fl. 1008–1025), c. 1028–1029, 122 *juàn*. **The single most important pre-modern Daoist encyclopedia**, distilled from the Northern-Sòng *Dà Sòng tiān gōng bǎo zàng* 大宋天宮寶藏 (1019) which Zhāng had supervised. Preserves substantial material — early hagiographies, Six-Dynasties Shàngqīng meditation texts, Táng alchemical and dietetic literature — not transmitted elsewhere, since the Northern Sòng canon Zhāng was excerpting was lost in the JīnYuán destructions. The first reference consulted for any Táng or pre-Táng Daoist text.
- *Dào shū* 道樞 [[KR5d0039]] — [[曾慥]] (Zēng Zào, d. 1155), c. 1151, 42 *juàn*. The great Southern-Sòng topical anthology of Daoist meditative, alchemical, and *yǎngshēng* literature, organized under 100+ thematic headings; many cited passages derive from texts otherwise lost.
- *Zhì yán zǒng* 至言總 [[KR5d0056]] — [[范翛然]] (late Táng), 5 *juàn*; smaller topical anthology of the same genre.

**The Daoist-medical cluster — the *Huáng dì nèi jīng* and *Nán jīng*.** A striking concentration of medical classics inside the Daoist canon, reflecting the Tang-Song understanding of medicine as a branch of Daoist cosmological practice:
- *Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn bǔ zhù shì wén* 黃帝內經素問補註釋文 [[KR5d0040]] — [[王冰]] (Wáng Bīng, c. 710 – c. 805) commentary, 762, with the Sòng supplements of [[林億]] (Lín Yì) and [[孫兆]] (Sūn Zhào) c. 1078. **The standard received recension of the *Sù wèn***, including Wáng Bīng's restoration of the lost "Seven Great Treatises" (七篇大論) on cosmological correspondences.
- *Huáng dì nèi jīng Líng shū lüè* 黃帝內經靈樞略 [[KR5d0041]] — short companion-text to the *Líng shū*.
- *Huáng dì Sù wèn Líng shū jí zhù* 黃帝素問靈樞集註 [[KR5d0042]] — [[史崧]] (Shǐ Sōng, fl. 1155), 23 *juàn*; the standard received *Líng shū*, transmitting Shǐ Sōng's 1155 collation of the previously lost text.
- *Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn yí piān* [[KR5d0043]] — Sòng supplement.
- *Sù wèn rù shì yùn qì lùn ào* 素問入式運氣論奧 [[KR5d0044]] — [[劉溫舒]] (1099); the Northern Sòng systematization of *Sù wèn* "yùnqì" 運氣 (climatic-cycle) cosmology.
- *Sù wèn liù qì xuán zhū mì yǔ* 素問六氣玄珠密語 [[KR5d0045]] — [[王冰]] attributed.
- *Huáng dì bā shí yī Nán jīng* commentary cluster: *zhù yì tú xù lùn* [[KR5d0046]] and *zuǎn tú jù jiě* [[KR5d0047]] (1269) — both by [[李駉]] (Lǐ Jiǒng), the Southern-Sòng received-text commentary on the *Eighty-One Difficulties Classic* 難經.

**The *zhūzǐ* 諸子 (masters) cluster.** Pre-Hàn–Táng pseudepigraphic master-texts in the LǎoZhuāng orbit that the Sòng catalogers placed here rather than alongside the *Lǎozǐ* corpus in [[KR5c]] *Dòngshén bù*:
- *Guǐ gǔ zǐ* 鬼谷子 [[KR5d0048]] — attributed to the Warring-States teacher of Sū Qín 蘇秦 and Zhāng Yí 張儀; rhetorical-diplomatic treatise on persuasion-by-contextual-calibration.
- *Kàng cāng zǐ* 亢倉子 [[KR5d0037]] (text) + *zhù* [[KR5d0038]] (commentary) — fabricated 742 under [[李隆基]] (Táng Xuánzōng)'s patronage to round out the canonical Daoist classics, ascribed to *Zhuāngzǐ* ch. 23 figure [[庚桑楚]]; the companion-fabrication to the *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0050]].
- *Tiān yǐn zǐ* 天隱子 [[KR5d0049]] — [[司馬承禎]] (Sī Mǎ Chéngzhēn, 647–735), 12th Máoshān patriarch; a foundational Táng meditation manual organized in eight stages (*qì shàn* 齋戒, *ān chù* 安處, *cún xiǎng* 存想, *zuò wàng* 坐忘, *shén jiě* 神解, etc.).
- *Sù lǚ zǐ* 素履子 [[KR5d0050]] — [[張弧]] (Zhāng Hú, Táng), philosophical miscellany.
- *Wú néng zǐ* 無能子 [[KR5d0051]] — anonymous, 887; a late-Táng political-Daoist treatise on the failure of social institutions, contemporaneous with the Huáng Cháo 黃巢 rebellion.
- *Xuán zhēn zǐ wài piān* 玄真子(外篇) [[KR5d0052]] — [[張志和]] (Zhāng Zhìhé, fl. 757–762), the lyric-philosophical writings of the famous fisherman-poet.
- *Liú zǐ* 劉子 [[KR5d0053]] — with [[袁孝政]]'s commentary; a Liáng – Táng compendium of moral-political maxims.
- *Shān hǎi jīng* 山海經 [[KR5d0054]] — with [[郭璞]] (Guō Pú, 276–324)'s Jìn commentary; the late-Warring-States mythical-geographical encyclopedia, here entering the Daoist canon as a source of *xiānjìng* 仙境 cosmography. Chronologically the oldest text in the section.

**The Sī Mǎ Chéngzhēn — Wú Yún Táng-Daoist philosophical cluster.**
- *Zuò wàng lùn* 坐忘論 [[KR5d0059]] — [[司馬承禎]] (647–735), the classical Táng treatise on the practice of "sitting and forgetting" (*zuò wàng*) drawn from *Zhuāngzǐ* ch. 6; the foundational text of Táng Daoist meditation theory. Together with [[KR5d0049]] *Tiān yǐn zǐ*, anchors the 上清 / 茅山 meditation tradition.
- *Zōng xuán xiān sheng wén jí* 宗玄先生文集 [[KR5d0074]] — [[吳筠]] (Wú Yún, d. 778)'s collected works in 3 *juàn*; one of the few Táng Daoist literary collections to survive intact.
- *Zōng xuán xiān sheng Xuán gāng lùn* 玄綱論 [[KR5d0075]] — Wú Yún's principal philosophical-Daoist treatise, a systematic exposition of the "Mystic Net" cosmology in 33 essays.
- *Wú zūn shī zhuàn* 吳尊師傳 [[KR5d0076]] — [[權德輿]] (Quán Déyú, 759–818)'s biography of Wú Yún, the principal Táng-era source on his life.
- *Nán tǒng dà jūn nèi dān jiǔ zhāng jīng* [[KR5d0077]] — pseudepigraphic Five-Dynasties scripture ascribed to Wú Yún.

**The Quánzhēn 全真 *yǔlù* 語錄 ("recorded sayings") cluster.** The principal documentation of the first two generations of the Northern-Lineage Quánzhēn patriarchs — [[王重陽]] (Wáng Chóngyáng, 1113–1170) and his "Seven Perfected" (*qī zhēn* 七眞) disciples — recorded by their immediate followers:
- *Jìn zhēn rén yǔ lù* 晉真人語錄 [[KR5d0079]] — sayings of Jìn Zhīqī 晉志契 (Jin Zhenren), Jin-Yuan transition.
- *Dān yáng zhēn rén yǔ lù* 丹陽真人語錄 [[KR5d0080]] — compiled by [[王頤中]]; the sayings of [[馬鈺]] (Mǎ Yù, 1123–1184, 丹陽子), the senior of Wáng Chóngyáng's seven disciples and chief patriarch of the Yùxiān 遇仙 branch.
- *Wú wéi qīng jìng cháng shēng zhēn rén zhì zhēn yǔ lù* 無為清靜長生真人至真語錄 [[KR5d0081]] — [[劉處玄]] (Liú Chùxuán, 1147–1203, *Chángshēng zǐ*); 1202 preface; the sayings of the third disciple, founder of the Suíshān 隨山 branch.
- *Pán shān Qī yún Wáng zhēn rén yǔ lù* 盤山棲雲王真人語錄 [[KR5d0082]] — compiled by [[論志煥]] 1247; the sayings of [[王志謹]] (Wáng Zhìjǐn, 1178–1263), the great second-generation Quánzhēn teacher whose Pánshān 盤山 discourses are the most philosophically developed of the early-Quánzhēn *yǔlù* corpus (the same text appears as [[KR5a0272]] in the *Xiūzhēn shíshū*).
- *Qīng ān Yíng chán zǐ yǔ lù* 清庵瑩蟾子語錄 [[KR5d0083]] — 1288 preface, compiled by [[柴元皋]]; the sayings of [[李道純]] (Lǐ Dàochún, fl. 1290), the great late-Yuán Quánzhēn-Southern syncretist.

**The Lǚ Dòngbīn / Wáng Yù late-Sòng – Yuán syncretic cluster.**
- *Chún yáng zhēn rén hùn chéng jí* 純陽真人渾成集 [[KR5d0078]] — compiled by [[何志淵]] 1251; verse and prose ascribed to [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòngbīn, Chúnyáng zhēnrén), the central figure of the Zhōng–Lǚ 鍾呂 nèidān lineage and the patron-saint of late-imperial Daoism.
- *Shàng qīng tài xuán jí* 上清太玄集 [[KR5d0084]] — [[侯善淵]] (Hóu Shànyuān, Jīn), the principal Quánzhēn-flavoured Jīn-era collected writings.
- *Xuán jiào dà gōng àn* 玄教大公案 [[KR5d0088]] — [[苗善時]] (1324); the Quánzhēn "great gōngàn" 公案 — Daoist appropriation of the Chán *kōan* form.
- *Xuán zōng zhí zhǐ wàn fǎ tóng guī* 玄宗直指萬法同歸 [[KR5d0089]] — [[牧常晁]] (fl. 1294–1320), the Yuán Quánzhēn syncretic synthesis under the slogan "the ten thousand methods all converge".
- *Dào chán jí* 道禪集 [[KR5d0096]] — [[王志担]] (1200–1272), explicit Daoist-Chán synthesis.

**[[陳致虛]]'s *Jīn dān dà yào* corpus.** The principal late-Yuán *nèidān* systematic synthesis, all dated c. 1331:
- *Shàng yáng zǐ jīn dān dà yào* 上陽子金丹大要 [[KR5d0090]] — 16 *juàn*; the systematic treatise integrating Southern-Lineage and Quánzhēn nèidān doctrine.
- *Jīn dān dà yào tú* [[KR5d0091]] — illustrated supplement.
- *Jīn dān dà yào liè xiān zhì* [[KR5d0092]] — companion hagiography.
- *Jīn dān dà yào xiān pài* [[KR5d0093]] — lineage tables documenting the genealogy of Chén Zhìxū's transmission from [[張伯端]] forward.
- *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù* [[KR5d0023]] — Chén Zhìxū's parallel *Cān tóng qì* commentary, intellectually continuous with the *Dà yào*.

**The *Wùzhēn piān* / Southern Lineage *nèidān* commentary tradition.** The *Wùzhēn piān* itself sits in [[KR5a]] [[KR5a0142]]–[[KR5a0147]]; the *Tàixuán bù* preserves the further satellite literature:
- *Jīn dān sì bǎi zì* 金丹四百字 [[KR5d0104]] — attributed to [[張伯端]] (Zhāng Bóduān, 984–1082, founder of the Southern Lineage 南宗), the 400-character verse-summary of the *Wùzhēn piān* with [[黃自如]]'s 1241 commentary.
- *Huán dān fù mìng piān* 還丹復命篇 [[KR5d0111]] — [[薛道光]] (Xuē Dàoguāng, 1078–1191), 1126 preface; the third patriarch of the Southern Lineage. Together with [[KR5d0114]] *Huán yuán piān* 還源篇 ([[石泰]] Shí Tài, d. 1158, second patriarch) the section preserves the patriarchal succession-texts of the Southern Lineage.
- *Cuì xū piān* 翠虛篇 [[KR5d0113]] — [[陳楠]] (Chén Nán, d. 1213, fourth patriarch); compiled by Wáng Sīchéng 1217.
- *Yuán qīng zǐ zhì mìng piān* 爰清子至命篇 [[KR5d0112]] — [[王慶升]] (1249).
- *Yú zhuāng xiè hòu lù* 漁莊邂逅錄 [[KR5d0109]] — [[吳悞]] (Wú Wù, fl. 1187).
- *Jīn dān zhí zhǐ* 金丹直指 [[KR5d0095]] — [[周無所住]] (1250).
- A constellation of shorter SòngYuán *nèidān* essays and verse: *Zhèng dào gē* [[KR5d0118]], *Chén xiān sheng nèi dān jué* [[KR5d0119]], *Dòng yuán zǐ nèi dān jué* [[KR5d0120]], *Nèi dān huán yuán jué* [[KR5d0121]], *Cháng shēng zhǐ yào piān* [[KR5d0122]], *Jīn yè dà dān shī* [[KR5d0117]], *Huán dān zhì yào piān* [[KR5d0115]], *Dǎn jiǎ jí* [[KR5d0116]], *Nèi dān mì jué* [[KR5d0108]], *Jīn dān zhēng zōng* [[KR5d0110]], *Jīn dān zhēn yī lùn* [[KR5d0103]], *Zhēn rén Gāo Xiàngxiān jīn dān gē* [[KR5d0102]] (1014).

**[[王玠]] (Wáng Jiè, fl. 1330s–1392) and the early-Ming Quánzhēn coda.**
- *Huán zhēn jí* 還真集 [[KR5d0097]] — Wáng Jiè, 1392 preface (with foreword by 43rd Celestial Master [[張宇初]]); the late-Yuán Quánzhēn-Southern syncretic capstone.
- *Dào xuán piān* 道玄篇 [[KR5d0098]] — Wáng Jiè, parallel collection.
- *Yuán yáng zǐ fǎ yǔ* 原陽子法語 [[KR5d0094]] — [[趙宜真]] (Zhào Yízhēn, d. 1382), edited by his disciple [[劉淵然]] (1351–1432); the late-Yuán / early-Ming Pure Brightness 淨明 / Quánzhēn synthesis.
- *Suí jī yìng huà lù* 隨機應化錄 [[KR5d0099]] — [[何道全]] (Hé Dàoquán, d. 1399), 1401 preface; the closing testimony of the YuánMíng Quánzhēn transition.

**The closing Quánzhēn poetry anthology.** *Míng hè yú yīn* 鳴鶴餘音 [[KR5d0123]] — compiled by [[彭致中]] in 1347, with preface by [[虞集]] (Yú Jí, 1272–1348); 9 *juàn* of *cí* 詞 lyric poems by Quánzhēn patriarchs and disciples — the most important collection of *nèidān* poetic literature. **The final text of *Tàixuán bù* and of the entire DZ-numbered range 1001–1100.**

**Miscellaneous further titles.**
- *Yuān yuán dào miào dòng zhēn jì piān* [[KR5d0012]] — [[李景元]] (N. Sòng), Shàngqīng-flavoured 3-juan supplement.
- *Jīn suǒ liú zhū yǐn* 金鎖流珠引 [[KR5d0035]] — pseudepigraphically attributed to [[李淳風]] (Lǐ Chúnfēng, 602–670); 29 *juàn* on stellar-Daoist meditation and astral ritual.
- *Líng qí běn zhāng zhèng jīng* 靈棋本章正經 [[KR5d0064]] — with [[顏幼明]] (E. Jìn) commentary; the canonical Daoist divinatory casting-the-lots scripture, formally cognate with the *Yì jīng* but operationally distinct.
- *Tài xuán bǎo diǎn* 太玄寶典 [[KR5d0057]], *Dào tǐ lùn* 道體論 [[KR5d0058]], *Dà dào lùn* 大道論 [[KR5d0060]], *Xīn mù lùn* 心目論 [[KR5d0061]], *Sān lùn yuán zhǐ* 三論元旨 [[KR5d0062]], *Xuán zhū lù* 玄珠錄 [[KR5d0071]] ([[王暉]], 626–697), *Tài shàng xiū zhēn xuán zhāng* [[KR5d0066]], *Yún gōng fǎ yǔ* [[KR5d0072]] (1298), *Hǎi kè lùn* [[KR5d0068]], *Wù xuán piān* [[KR5d0069]] (1229), *Tài xū xīn yuān piān* [[KR5d0070]], *Xiū liàn xū zhī* [[KR5d0100]], *Yù shì jīng* [[KR5d0101]], *Dòng yuān jí* [[KR5d0086]] ([[李思聰]], c. 1050), *Dòng yuān jí* (再) [[KR5d0087]] ([[長筌子]], JīnYuán), *Jìn dòng tiān hǎi yuè biǎo* [[KR5d0085]] (1050) — together making up the discursive-essay and short-cosmological-treatise tail of the section.

## Important persons

- [[陶弘景]] (Táo Hóngjǐng, 456–536) — Shàngqīng patriarch and editor of the *Zhēn gào* [[KR5d0036]]; his collected works [[KR5d0073]] also live here. The Six-Dynasties textual anchor of *Tàixuán bù* (his role across the canon as Shàngqīng codifier is more fully treated in [[KR5a]]).
- [[魏伯陽]] (Wèi Bóyáng, attributed, Eastern Hàn) — pseudo-author of the *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì*; the foundational alchemical authority around whose text the central commentary archipelago [[KR5d0016]]–[[KR5d0027]] of the section is built.
- [[彭曉]] (Péng Xiǎo, d. 955) — Later-Shǔ alchemist; author of the most influential traditional commentary on the *Cāntóng qì* [[KR5d0019]] + [[KR5d0020]] (947), the structural framework for every subsequent reading. The single most consequential author in the central alchemical cluster.
- [[朱熹]] (Zhū Xī, 1130–1200) — Southern-Sòng Neo-Confucian patriarch; his *Cāntóng qì kǎo yì* [[KR5d0018]] (1197) is the principal Chéng-Zhū-school engagement with Daoist alchemy and an unusual instance of a major Confucian *Daoxué* figure entering the Daoist canon. (His role in [[KR1a]] *Yì lèi* and elsewhere in the *Jīng* division is more central.)
- [[俞琰]] (Yú Yǎn, 1258–1314) — SòngYuán transitional polymath; author of six titles in the section ([[KR5d0022]] [[KR5d0024]] [[KR5d0028]] [[KR5d0029]] [[KR5d0030]] plus other commentary) including the principal SòngYuán *Cāntóng qì* commentary and the *Yì wài bié zhuàn*. The most prolific single author in *Tàixuán bù*.
- [[雷思齊]] (Léi Sīqí, 1223–1301) — Yuán Daoist *Yìxué* master; author of the *Yì shì tōng biàn* / *Hé tú* / *Yì tú tōng biàn* cluster [[KR5d0031]]–[[KR5d0034]], the principal Daoist counterpart to the Confucian *Hé tú / Luò shū* speculation of [[邵雍]].
- [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhìxū, 1290–1343) — late-Yuán Quánzhēn-Southern syncretist; author of the *Shàng yáng zǐ jīn dān dà yào* cluster [[KR5d0090]]–[[KR5d0093]] (c. 1331) and a parallel *Cāntóng qì fēn zhāng zhù* [[KR5d0023]]. The principal systematic *nèidān* author in the section.
- [[張君房]] (Zhāng Jūnfáng, fl. 1008–1025) — Northern-Sòng Daoist editor; compiler of the *Yún jí qī qiān* [[KR5d0055]], the foundational pre-modern Daoist encyclopedia. His earlier supervision of the Northern-Sòng *Dà Sòng tiān gōng bǎo zàng* (1019) makes him the single most consequential Daoist bibliographer of the Sòng.
- [[曾慥]] (Zēng Zào, d. 1155) — Southern-Sòng literatus; compiler of the *Dào shū* [[KR5d0039]] anthology, the principal Southern-Sòng counterpart to the *Yún jí qī qiān*.
- [[邵雍]] (Shào Yōng, 1011–1077) — Northern-Sòng cosmologist; author of *Huáng jí jīng shì* [[KR5d0063]] and *Yī chuān jī rǎng jí* [[KR5d0065]]. His placement in the Daoist canon, despite his Neo-Confucian standing, registers the Sòng catalogers' read of his numerological *Yìxué* as continuous with the Daoist cosmological tradition.
- [[司馬承禎]] (Sī Mǎ Chéngzhēn, 647–735) — 12th Máoshān Shàngqīng patriarch; author of *Tiān yǐn zǐ* [[KR5d0049]] and *Zuò wàng lùn* [[KR5d0059]], the two foundational Táng treatises on Shàngqīng meditation. The principal Táng-era theoretician of *zuò wàng* sitting-and-forgetting practice.
- [[吳筠]] (Wú Yún, d. 778) — Táng court Daoist; author of *Wén jí* [[KR5d0074]] and *Xuán gāng lùn* [[KR5d0075]]; the principal Táng literary-philosophical Daoist after Sī Mǎ Chéngzhēn.
- [[王冰]] (Wáng Bīng, c. 710 – c. 805) — mid-Táng physician; author of the 762 *Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn* recension [[KR5d0040]] including the restored "Seven Great Treatises" on cosmological-medical correspondences. The single most important commentator on the Chinese medical classic and the principal bridge between Táng Daoism and classical medicine.
- [[譚峭]] (Tán Qiào, fl. 940s) — Five-Dynasties Daoist philosopher; author of *Huà shū* 化書 [[KR5d0067]], a foundational work of speculative philosophy whose meditation on cosmic and social transformation cuts across Daoist metaphysics and political critique.
- [[馬鈺]] (Mǎ Yù, 1123–1184) — Quánzhēn first-generation patriarch; chief disciple of [[王重陽]]; subject of the *Dān yáng zhēn rén yǔ lù* [[KR5d0080]] (compiled by his disciple [[王頤中]]). The principal documented voice of the founding generation of Quánzhēn.
- [[劉處玄]] (Liú Chùxuán, 1147–1203) — Quánzhēn first-generation patriarch; subject of [[KR5d0081]]; founder of the Suíshān 隨山 branch.
- [[王志謹]] (Wáng Zhìjǐn, 1178–1263) — Quánzhēn second-generation patriarch; subject of *Pán shān Qī yún Wáng zhēn rén yǔ lù* [[KR5d0082]] (also [[KR5a0272]] in the *Xiūzhēn shíshū* recension); the most philosophically developed Quánzhēn teacher of the JīnYuán transition.
- [[李道純]] (Lǐ Dàochún, fl. 1290) — Yuán Quánzhēn-Southern syncretist; subject of *Qīng ān Yíng chán zǐ yǔ lù* [[KR5d0083]] (1288). His commentarial and doctrinal corpus is principally housed in [[KR5a]] and [[KR5c]]; *Tàixuán bù* preserves the *yǔlù* witness.
- [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòngbīn, attributed) — the principal patron-saint of late-imperial Daoism; the central figure of the *ZhōngLǚ* 鍾呂 nèidān lineage; his Chúnyáng-zhēnrén-attributed verse corpus is preserved here as [[KR5d0078]].
- [[張伯端]] (Zhāng Bóduān, c. 984–1082) — founder of the Southern Lineage of nèidān; pseudo-author of the *Jīn dān sì bǎi zì* [[KR5d0104]]. His central work *Wùzhēn piān* sits in [[KR5a]]; here his presence is through this satellite verse summary and through the patriarchal succession-texts of his lineage.
- [[薛道光]] (Xuē Dàoguāng, 1078–1191), [[石泰]] (Shí Tài, d. 1158), [[陳楠]] (Chén Nán, d. 1213) — the second, third, and fourth patriarchs of the Southern Lineage; their *Huán dān fù mìng piān* [[KR5d0111]], *Huán yuán piān* [[KR5d0114]], and *Cuì xū piān* [[KR5d0113]] together preserve the Southern Lineage patriarchal succession-texts.
- [[彭致中]] (Péng Zhìzhōng, fl. 1347) — Yuán Quánzhēn anthologist; compiler of *Míng hè yú yīn* [[KR5d0123]], the closing text of the section.
- [[王玠]] (Wáng Jiè, fl. 1330s–1392) — late-Yuán / early-Míng Quánzhēn-Southern syncretist; author of [[KR5d0097]] and [[KR5d0098]] (and active in [[KR5a]]).
- [[趙宜真]] (Zhào Yízhēn, d. 1382) — late-Yuán / early-Míng Pure Brightness / Quánzhēn syncretist; subject of [[KR5d0094]] (compiled by his disciple [[劉淵然]]).
- [[何道全]] (Hé Dàoquán, d. 1399) — early-Míng Daoist; author of *Suí jī yìng huà lù* [[KR5d0099]], the closing testimony of the YuánMíng Quánzhēn transition.

- [[張弧]] (Zhāng Hú), [[張志和]] (Zhāng Zhìhé, fl. 757–762) — Táng *zhūzǐ* writers preserved here as [[KR5d0050]] and [[KR5d0052]].
- [[王士源]] (Wáng Shìyuán, Táng) and [[何璨]] (Hé Càn, Táng) — fabricator and commentator of the *Kàng cāng zǐ* [[KR5d0037]] [[KR5d0038]], composed 742 under [[李隆基]]'s patronage to round out the canonical Daoist classics.
- [[史崧]] (Shǐ Sōng, fl. 1155) — Southern-Sòng physician; the recoverer of the previously lost *Líng shū* [[KR5d0042]].

## Topics

- **The *sì fǔ* 四輔 (Four Supplements) and the *Tàixuán* paradox.** The notional scheme of the *Dàozàng* pairs each of the *sāndòng* with a textual supplement: *Tàixuán* with *Dòngzhēn* (Shàngqīng), *Tàipíng* with *Dòngxuán* (Língbǎo), *Tàiqīng* with *Dòngshén* (Sānhuáng), plus *Zhèngyī* spanning all three. Wilkinson §29.4.4 notes that this scheme cuts against the *shí'èr lèi* 十二類 genre axis and that by the Míng compilation the school/genre cross-classification had collapsed. *Tàixuán bù* registers the collapse: the Lǎozǐ corpus that the section was originally built around lives in [[KR5c]] (the Schipper-Verellen reconstruction is that the *Tàixuán* and *Dòngshén* sections merged in a Sòng-era catalog reform), and what fills *Tàixuán bù* in 1445 is the *Dàozàng*'s editorial overflow basin: alchemical, Yì-cosmological, medical, anthological, and Quánzhēn material that did not fit cleanly inside any Cavern.

- **The *Cāntóng qì* commentary tradition and the SòngYuán nèidān synthesis.** Twelve of the section's titles ([[KR5d0016]]–[[KR5d0027]]) are commentaries on or recensions of the *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì* — the densest single exegetical archipelago in *Tàixuán bù* and one of the densest in the whole canon. The cluster includes the foundational [[彭曉]] commentary (947), the Neo-Confucian [[朱熹]] *kǎo yì* (1197), and the SòngYuán nèidān readings of [[陳顯微]], [[儲華谷]], [[俞琰]], [[陳致虛]], and [[蔣一彪]]. From the late twelfth century onward the *Cāntóng qì* is increasingly read through the lens of [[張伯端]]'s *Wùzhēn piān* and the Southern Lineage's symbolic alchemy of *jīng* — *qì* — *shén*; this reading culminates in [[陳致虛]]'s *Jīn dān dà yào* corpus [[KR5d0090]]–[[KR5d0093]].

- **Daoist *Yìxué* 易學 and speculative numerology.** A distinctive strand of Daoist *Yì*-reading runs through the section: [[邵雍]]'s *Huáng jí jīng shì* [[KR5d0063]] grids the cosmos and human history on a system of 元-會-運-世 cycles; [[俞琰]]'s *Yì wài bié zhuàn* [[KR5d0028]] reads the *Yì* as an alchemical key; [[雷思齊]]'s *Yì tú tōng biàn* [[KR5d0031]]–[[KR5d0034]] develops the Daoist counterpart to the Confucian *Hé tú / Luò shū* diagrammatics. The presence of these works inside the Daoist canon — and the absence from [[KR1a]] *Yì lèi* of Shào Yōng's main treatise — registers the SòngYuán understanding of numerology as a Daoist-cosmological domain rather than a Confucian-classical one.

- **The medical-Daoist cluster.** Eight titles ([[KR5d0040]]–[[KR5d0047]]) preserve the *Huáng dì nèi jīng* and *Nán jīng* in their canonical TángSòng commentaries — the only major concentration of Chinese medical classics inside the Daoist canon. The cluster reflects the Táng understanding of medicine (especially the *yùnqì* 運氣 cosmological-medical doctrine [[劉溫舒]] systematizes in [[KR5d0044]]) as a branch of Daoist cosmological practice. [[王冰]]'s 762 *Sù wèn* recension is the textual centre of all subsequent Chinese medicine; the *Tàixuán bù* is one of its two principal transmissional carriers (the other being the Sòng imperial editorial line of [[林億]] and [[孫兆]]).

- **The *zhūzǐ* 諸子 supplementary classics.** The Sòng catalogers placed several Lǎo-Zhuāng-orbit pseudepigraphic master-texts here rather than alongside the *Lǎozǐ* corpus in [[KR5c]] *Dòngshén bù*: the *Guǐ gǔ zǐ* [[KR5d0048]], *Kàng cāng zǐ* [[KR5d0037]] [[KR5d0038]] (the Táng court fabrication that parallels the *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0050]]), *Tiān yǐn zǐ* [[KR5d0049]], *Sù lǚ zǐ* [[KR5d0050]], *Wú néng zǐ* [[KR5d0051]], *Xuán zhēn zǐ* [[KR5d0052]], *Liú zǐ* [[KR5d0053]], plus the *Shān hǎi jīng* [[KR5d0054]] with [[郭璞]]'s commentary. The grouping registers a Sòng editorial distinction between the *LǎozǐZhuāngzǐLièzǐGēngsāngzǐ* canonical four (placed in *Dòngshén*) and a second tier of *zhūzǐ* placed here.

- **The Quánzhēn *yǔlù* genre.** Five of the section's titles ([[KR5d0079]]–[[KR5d0083]]) preserve the recorded sayings of the founding generation of the Northern-Lineage Quánzhēn movement — [[馬鈺]], [[劉處玄]], [[王志謹]], [[李道純]], plus a parallel *Jìn zhēn rén yǔ lù*. The *yǔlù* form, modelled on the Chán *yǔlù* tradition with which Quánzhēn was in intense conversation through the JīnYuán period, becomes a principal genre of late-medieval Daoist literature. The closing *Míng hè yú yīn* [[KR5d0123]] (1347) anthology converts the lineage's prose teaching into *cí* lyric verse.

- **Daoist anthologies and the preservation of lost texts.** The *Yún jí qī qiān* [[KR5d0055]] and *Dào shū* [[KR5d0039]] — the two great pre-modern Daoist anthologies — between them preserve a great mass of Six-Dynasties, Táng, and Sòng material otherwise lost. The *Yún jí qī qiān* in particular, drawn from the now-lost Northern-Sòng *Dà Sòng tiān gōng bǎo zàng* (1019), is the single most consulted reference work for the early Daoist textual tradition.

- **The Six-Dynasties / Táng Shàngqīng inheritance.** Although the bulk of Shàngqīng material lives in [[KR5a]], *Tàixuán bù* preserves the foundational documentary anchor of the tradition — [[陶弘景]]'s *Zhēn gào* [[KR5d0036]] — together with the Táng meditation-theory cluster of [[司馬承禎]] ([[KR5d0049]] [[KR5d0059]]), the Wú Yún literary-philosophical corpus ([[KR5d0074]] [[KR5d0075]]), and the eleven-title opening cluster [[KR5d0001]]–[[KR5d0011]] of supplementary Shàngqīng / Língbǎo scriptures. Together these establish *Tàixuán* as the section's residual identity as the Shàngqīng supplement.

## Timeline

- **c. 400–250 BCE (late Warring States)** — *Shān hǎi jīng* 山海經 [[KR5d0054]] core composed; *Guǐ gǔ zǐ* 鬼谷子 [[KR5d0048]] core composed. The two pre-Hàn classics of the section.
- **c. 142 – 220 CE (Eastern Hàn, traditional attribution)** — *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì* 周易參同契 attributed to [[魏伯陽]] takes shape; the textual core is contested and may be later (post-Hàn WèiJìn), but the tradition's terminus ante quem is c. 4th c.
- **276–324 (Western Jìn)** — [[郭璞]] composes the surviving commentary on the *Shān hǎi jīng* [[KR5d0054]].
- **3rd–4th c.** — *Líng qí běn zhāng zhèng jīng* [[KR5d0064]] (with [[顏幼明]] E. Jìn commentary) takes shape; the canonical Daoist casting-the-lots scripture.
- **364–370 (Eastern Jìn)** — Yáng Xī's Shàngqīng séance revelations to the Xǔ family at Máoshān — the documentary base of the *Zhēn gào*.
- **456–536 (Liáng)** — [[陶弘景]] active; *Zhēn gào* [[KR5d0036]] finished form 499; collected works [[KR5d0073]] survive in a later Sòng edition by Fù Xiāo / Chén Jué.
- **550–650 (Six Dynasties – early Táng)** — *Liú zǐ* [[KR5d0053]] takes received form with [[袁孝政]] commentary.
- **6th–7th c.** — Anonymous Six-Dynasties / Táng supplementary ShàngqīngLíngbǎo scriptures [[KR5d0001]]–[[KR5d0011]] compiled; *Tài shàng Dòngxuán Líng bǎo sān yī wǔ qì zhēn jīng* [[KR5d0002]] is the earliest stratum.
- **626–697 (Táng)** — [[王暉]] compiles *Xuán zhū lù* [[KR5d0071]] (preserved in his disciple [[王大霄]]'s recension).
- **647–735 (Táng)** — [[司馬承禎]] active; *Tiān yǐn zǐ* [[KR5d0049]] and *Zuò wàng lùn* [[KR5d0059]] — the foundational Táng treatises on Shàngqīng meditation.
- **742 (Táng, Tiānbǎo 1)** — [[李隆基]] (Táng Xuánzōng) commissions the *Kàng cāng zǐ* [[KR5d0037]] fabrication by [[王士源]], with [[何璨]]'s commentary [[KR5d0038]]; parallel to the *Dòng líng zhēn jīng* [[KR5c0050]] fabrication and part of the same project to round out the canonical Daoist classics.
- **757–762 (Táng)** — [[張志和]] active; *Xuán zhēn zǐ wài piān* [[KR5d0052]] composed.
- **762 (Táng)** — [[王冰]] completes the *Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn* recension [[KR5d0040]] including the restored "Seven Great Treatises" on cosmological correspondences; *Sù wèn liù qì xuán zhū mì yǔ* [[KR5d0045]] is the parallel attributed text.
- **c. 750–778 (Táng)** — [[吳筠]] composes the *Zōng xuán xiān sheng wén jí* [[KR5d0074]] and *Xuán gāng lùn* [[KR5d0075]]; biography [[KR5d0076]] follows from [[權德輿]] (790s–810s).
- **800–907 (late Táng)** — *Zhì yán zǒng* [[KR5d0056]] ([[范翛然]]) compiled; *Wú néng zǐ* [[KR5d0051]] composed in 887 contemporaneously with the Huáng Cháo rebellion.
- **c. 850 – 950 (late Táng / Five Dynasties)** — *Lóng hǔ yuán zhǐ* [[KR5d0106]] ([[青霞子]]) composed; the Old-Text Dragon-Tiger Classic tradition emerges.
- **930–960 (Five Dynasties)** — [[譚峭]]'s *Huà shū* [[KR5d0067]] composed; *Sān lùn yuán zhǐ* [[KR5d0062]]; *Nán tǒng dà jūn nèi dān jiǔ zhāng jīng* [[KR5d0077]] (Wú Yún-attributed Five-Dynasties forgery).
- **947 (Latter Shǔ, Guǎngzhèng 10)** — [[彭曉]] dates by colophon his foundational commentary [[KR5d0019]] on the *Cāntóng qì*, with illustrated supplement [[KR5d0020]]; *Cāntóng qì zhù* (四) [[KR5d0021]] dates from the same Five-Dynasties window.
- **1014 (N. Sòng)** — [[高先]] composes *Zhēn rén Gāo Xiàngxiān jīn dān gē* [[KR5d0102]], the earliest dated Sòng nèidān poem in the section.
- **c. 1028–1029 (N. Sòng)** — [[張君房]] compiles the *Yún jí qī qiān* [[KR5d0055]] from the just-completed *Dà Sòng tiān gōng bǎo zàng* (1019); dedicated to Sòng Rénzōng shortly after his 1022 accession.
- **1040–1077 (N. Sòng)** — [[邵雍]] composes the *Huáng jí jīng shì* [[KR5d0063]] and *Yī chuān jī rǎng jí* [[KR5d0065]].
- **1050 (N. Sòng)** — [[李思聰]] composes *Dòng yuān jí* [[KR5d0086]] and *Jìn dòng tiān hǎi yuè biǎo* [[KR5d0085]].
- **1078–1158 (Southern Lineage early)** — [[石泰]] (d. 1158) composes *Huán yuán piān* [[KR5d0114]]; the second patriarch of the Southern Lineage.
- **1099 (N. Sòng)** — [[劉溫舒]] composes *Sù wèn rù shì yùn qì lùn ào* [[KR5d0044]], the Sòng systematization of *yùnqì* medical cosmology.
- **c. 1100–1200 (Sòng catalog reform)** — Schipper-Verellen's reconstructed merger of the *Tàixuán* and *Dòngshén* sections; the Lǎozǐ corpus migrates to *Dòngshén bù*, and *Tàixuán bù* becomes the *Dàozàng*'s textual overflow basin.
- **1126 (N. Sòng)** — [[薛道光]] composes the *Huán dān fù mìng piān* [[KR5d0111]]; the third patriarch of the Southern Lineage.
- **c. 1140–1155 (S. Sòng)** — [[曾慥]] compiles the *Dào shū* [[KR5d0039]].
- **1155 (S. Sòng)** — [[史崧]] collates and publishes the *Líng shū* in *Huáng dì Sù wèn Líng shū jí zhù* [[KR5d0042]], recovering the previously lost text.
- **1180–1213 (S. Sòng)** — [[陳楠]] composes the *Cuì xū piān* [[KR5d0113]]; the fourth patriarch of the Southern Lineage.
- **1187–1234 (S. Sòng)** — *Yú zhuāng xiè hòu lù* [[KR5d0109]] ([[吳悞]], 1187); *Wù xuán piān* [[KR5d0069]] ([[余洞真]], 1229); *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì jiě* [[KR5d0025]] ([[陳顯微]], 1234).
- **1197 (S. Sòng)** — [[朱熹]] composes *Zhōuyì cāntóng qì kǎo yì* [[KR5d0018]], the Neo-Confucian collation.
- **1202 (Jīn)** — [[劉處玄]]'s *yǔlù* compiled [[KR5d0081]].
- **c. 1150–1200 (Jīn)** — [[馬鈺]]'s *yǔlù* compiled [[KR5d0080]] by his disciple [[王頤中]].
- **1241–1250 (S. Sòng – Yuán)** — [[黃自如]] commentary on *Jīn dān sì bǎi zì* [[KR5d0104]] (1241); *Jīn dān zhí zhǐ* [[KR5d0095]] (1250); *Cháng shēng zhǐ yào piān* [[KR5d0122]] ([[林自然]], 1250); *Chún yáng zhēn rén hùn chéng jí* [[KR5d0078]] ([[何志淵]], 1251).
- **1247 (Yuán)** — [[論志煥]] compiles [[王志謹]]'s *Pán shān yǔ lù* [[KR5d0082]].
- **1269 (S. Sòng)** — [[李駉]] completes the *Nán jīng zuǎn tú jù jiě* [[KR5d0047]].
- **1281–1300 (S. Sòng – Yuán)** — [[俞琰]]'s six titles compiled: *Cāntóng qì shì yí* [[KR5d0024]] (1281), *Cāntóng qì fā huī* [[KR5d0022]] (1284), *Yì wài bié zhuàn* [[KR5d0028]] (c. 1284), *Xuán pìn zhī mén fù* [[KR5d0030]] (1284–1296), *Xí shàng fǔ tán* [[KR5d0029]], plus parallel commentary; [[雷思齊]]'s *Yì shì tōng biàn* [[KR5d0031]] / *Hé tú* [[KR5d0033]] (1280s); *Yì tú tōng biàn* preface [[KR5d0032]] (1300).
- **1288 (Yuán)** — [[柴元皋]] compiles [[李道純]]'s *Qīng ān Yíng chán zǐ yǔ lù* [[KR5d0083]].
- **1294–1320 (Yuán)** — [[牧常晁]] composes *Xuán zōng zhí zhǐ wàn fǎ tóng guī* [[KR5d0089]]; [[汪可孫]] composes *Yún gōng fǎ yǔ* [[KR5d0072]] (1298).
- **1324 (Yuán)** — [[苗善時]]'s *Xuán jiào dà gōng àn* [[KR5d0088]].
- **1330 (Yuán)** — [[雷思齊]] composes *Yì tú tōng biàn* [[KR5d0034]].
- **c. 1331 (Yuán)** — [[陳致虛]]'s *Jīn dān dà yào* corpus [[KR5d0090]]–[[KR5d0093]] composed; parallel *Cāntóng qì fēn zhāng zhù* [[KR5d0023]].
- **1347 (Yuán)** — [[彭致中]] compiles *Míng hè yú yīn* [[KR5d0123]], with preface by [[虞集]]; the closing text of the section.
- **1368–1399 (early Míng)** — Late additions: [[趙宜真]]'s *Yuán yáng zǐ fǎ yǔ* [[KR5d0094]] (compiled by [[劉淵然]]); [[王玠]]'s *Huán zhēn jí* [[KR5d0097]] (1392, with [[張宇初]] preface) and *Dào xuán piān* [[KR5d0098]]; [[蔣一彪]]'s *Gǔ wén Cān tóng qì jí jiě* [[KR5d0026]] (Míng, fl. 1614 — chronologically the latest commentary in the *Cāntóng qì* cluster).
- **1401 (early Míng)** — [[何道全]]'s *Suí jī yìng huà lù* [[KR5d0099]], with preface by [[靈通子]], compiled by [[賈道玄]]. The latest dated text in *Tàixuán bù*.
- **1445** — Promulgation of the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng*; the *Tàixuán bù* fixed at 123 works (DZ 984 – DZ 1100) in its present arrangement.
