# Xí shàng fǔ tán 席上腐談

*Decaying-Mat Discussions*

by [[俞琰]] (Yǔ Yǎn, 1245–1314)

Yǔ Yǎn's **miscellaneous reading-notes** (*zhá jì* 札記) — the upper *juàn* devoted to philological examinations of names and objects, the lower to broader discussions. The title (literally "decaying [talk] on the mat") is a self-deprecating humility-formula. Preserved in the WYG edition.

## Tiyao

The Sìkù editors note: "*Xí shàng fǔ tán*, two *juàn*, by Yǔ Yǎn of the Sòng. This work consists of his miscellaneous reading-notes. The first few dozen entries of the upper *juàn* are investigations of objects and nomenclature..."

**Dating.** Within Yǔ Yǎn's active life (c. 1284–1314). Dynasty: 宋-元.
