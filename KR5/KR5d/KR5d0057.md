# Tài xuán bǎo diǎn 太玄寶典

*Precious Canon of Great Mystery*

Anonymous; Sòng

A Sòng Daoist **philosophical-alchemical compendium**, three *juàn*, loosely structured as a "precious canon" (*bǎo diǎn* 寶典). Preserved as **DZ 1034** / CT 1034 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
