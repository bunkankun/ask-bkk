# Xuán zhēn zǐ (wài piān) 玄真子(外篇)

*Master of Mysterious Perfection (Outer Chapters)*

by [[張志和]] (Zhāng Zhìhé, c. 730–810)

The received (**"outer chapters"**) of Zhāng Zhìhé's Daoist philosophical-cosmological treatise, in three *juàn* (*Bì xū* 碧虛, *Yín suǒ* 鸑鷟, *Tāo xū* 濤之語). The **"inner chapters"** (*nèi piān* 內篇) are lost. Preserved as **DZ 1029** / CT 1029 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Within Zhāng's active life. Dynasty: 唐.
