*Discourse on the Gold Elixir and the One-of-Truth*

by [[百玄子]] (Bǎi xuán zǐ)

A late Northern-Sòng Daoist alchemical treatise by the pseudonymous Bǎi xuán zǐ. Preserved as **DZ 1080** / CT 1080 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Late Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋後期.
