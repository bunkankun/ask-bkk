# Huán yuán piān 還源篇

*Treatise on Returning to the Source*

by [[石泰]] (Shí Tài, 1022–1158) — Second Patriarch of the Nán zōng

**Shí Tài's principal *nèi dān* treatise** — the founding treatise of Southern-Lineage teaching after Zhāng Bóduān's *Wù zhēn piān*. Preserved as **DZ 1091** / CT 1091 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Within Shí's active lifetime. Dynasty: 北宋-南宋.
