# Tài shàng qīng jìng yuán dòng zhēn wén yù zì miào jīng 太上清靜元洞真文玉字妙經

*Most High Wondrous Scripture of the Perfect-Script Jade-Graphs of the Pure-Still Original Cavern*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A short **talismanic scripture** featuring **perfect-script jade-graphs** (*zhēn wén yù zì* 真文玉字) — one of the "heavenly-script" talismanic categories of Shàng qīng 上清 / Líng bǎo 靈寶 Daoism. Preserved as **DZ 986** / CT 986 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
