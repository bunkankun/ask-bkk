# Tài jí zuǒ xiān gōng shuō shén fú jīng 太極左仙公說神符經

*Scripture of Divine Talismans as Expounded by the Great-Ultimate Left-Immortal-Duke*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Táng

A Daoist **talismanic scripture** attributed to the **Tài jí zuǒ xiān gōng** 太極左仙公 (Gě Xuán 葛玄, the great-uncle of Gě Hóng and nominal transmitter of the Líng bǎo scriptures). Preserved as **DZ 1117** / CT 1117 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
