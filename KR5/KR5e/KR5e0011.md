# Tài shàng Líng bǎo Jìng míng zhōng huáng bā zhù jīng 太上靈寶淨明中黃八柱經

*Most High Língbǎo Pure-Illumination Scripture of the Central-Yellow Eight Pillars*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A Jìng míng 淨明 meditation-scripture on the **"Central Yellow"** (*zhōng huáng* 中黃, the central dāntián) and the **Eight Pillars** (*bā zhù* 八柱, symbolic cosmological supports). Preserved as **DZ 1109** / CT 1109 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
