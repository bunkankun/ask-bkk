# Shàng fāng Líng bǎo wú jí zhì dào kāi huà zhēn jīng 上方靈寶無極至道開化真經

*Perfect Scripture of the Higher-Realm Língbǎo, the Limitless Supreme Way Opening-and-Transforming*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A Daoist Líng bǎo 靈寶 scripture on the "limitless supreme Way" and its cosmic transforming action. Preserved as **DZ 1133** / CT 1133 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
