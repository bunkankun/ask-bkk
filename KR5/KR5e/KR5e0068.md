# Fǎ hǎi yí zhū 法海遺珠

*Pearls Left-Behind in the Sea of Dharma*

Anonymous; early Míng

**The concluding text of the Tài píng bù** — a 46-*juàn* early-Míng **ritual compendium** collecting Daoist ritual practices of the **Thunder Rites** (*léi fǎ* 雷法) tradition and allied currents. Preserved as **DZ 1166** / CT 1166 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Early Míng, by 1445. Dynasty: 明.
