# Sūn zhēn rén bèi jí qiān jīn yào fāng 孫真人備急千金要方

*Perfected Sūn's Emergency Thousand-Gold Essential Prescriptions*

by [[孫思邈]] (Sūn Sīmiǎo) — early-Táng physician; with Sòng editing

**The foundational clinical medical encyclopedia of the Táng**, composed by the great physician [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sīmiǎo c. 650, revised by Sòng editors in the 11th century (the Dào zàng copy follows the Sòng recension in 93 *juàn*). Covers diagnostics, obstetrics-pediatrics, internal medicine, acupuncture, materia medica, and emergency treatment. "Thousand-gold" in the title reflects Sūn's dictum that human life is worth more than a thousand gold pieces. Preserved as **DZ 1163** / CT 1163 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Core Táng (c. 650), Sòng redaction 11th century. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
