# Chóng yáng Quán zhēn jí 重陽全真集

*Chóngyáng's Quánzhēn Collection*

by [[王嚞]] (Wáng Chóngyáng, 1113–1170) — Founder of the Quán zhēn School

**The principal literary corpus of Wáng Chóngyáng** — 13 *juàn* of poetry, *cí* lyrics, and prose, the foundational literary expression of the Quán zhēn teaching. Preface dated 1188 (posthumous). Preserved as **DZ 1153** / CT 1153 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Preface 1188. Dynasty: 金.
