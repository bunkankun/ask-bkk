# Tài shàng Líng bǎo shēng xuán nèi jiào jīng zhōng hé pǐn shù yì shū 太上靈寶昇玄內教經中和品述議疏

*Most High Língbǎo Scripture of Ascending-Mystery Inner Teaching: Exegetical Sub-commentary on the Chapter of Centered Harmony*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

**Sub-commentary (*shù yì shū* 述議疏)** on the ***Shēng xuán jīng*** 昇玄經 ("Ascending-Mystery Scripture"), specifically its chapter on **Centered Harmony**. The *Shēng xuán jīng* was a major early-medieval Daoist scripture preserved only fragmentarily (mostly via Dūnhuáng 敦煌 manuscripts). Preserved as **DZ 1122** / CT 1122 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
