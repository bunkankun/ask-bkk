# Chóng yáng jiào huà jí 重陽教化集

*Chóngyáng's Teaching-and-Transformation Collection*

by [[王嚞]] (Wáng Chóngyáng, 1113–1170)

A collection of Wáng Chóngyáng's **conversion-exchanges** with his disciples and with prospective converts (*jiào huà* 教化, "teaching and transforming"). Preface 1183. Preserved as **DZ 1154** / CT 1154 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Preface 1183. Dynasty: 金.
