# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo fēi xíng sān jiè tōng wēi nèi sī miào jīng 太上洞玄靈寶飛行三界通微內思妙經

*Most High Dòngxuán Língbǎo Wondrous Scripture on Flying through the Three Realms with Penetrating-Subtle Inner Visualization*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Líng bǎo 靈寶 meditation-scripture on **astral flight** through the *sān jiè* 三界 (Three Realms) via inner visualization. Preserved as **DZ 1118** / CT 1118 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
