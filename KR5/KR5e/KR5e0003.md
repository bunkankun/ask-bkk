# Tài píng jīng fù wén xù 太平經復文序

*Preface to the Reduplicated Texts of the Tài píng jīng*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Táng

The **preface** to the *Tài píng jīng* transmission, the third of the three Dào zàng items (DZ 1101 a/b/c) preserving the *Tài píng jīng*. Preserved as **DZ 1101c** / CT 1101c (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
