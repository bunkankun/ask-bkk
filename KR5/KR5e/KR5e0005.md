# Tài shàng Líng bǎo Jìng míng dòng shén shàng pǐn jīng 太上靈寶淨明洞神上品經

*Most High Língbǎo Pure-Illumination Dòngshén Superior-Grade Scripture*

Anonymous; SòngYuán

The opening scripture of the ***Jìng míng*** 淨明 ("Pure Illumination") revelation-corpus within the Dào zàng — texts of the **Jìng míng zhōng xiào dào** 淨明忠孝道 ("Way of Pure Illumination, Loyalty, and Filiality"), a Daoist movement centered on the cult of [[許遜]] Xǔ Xùn at Xī shān 西山 (Jiāngxī), attaining its mature form in the 12th–14th centuries. Preserved as **DZ 1103** / CT 1103 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** SòngYuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
