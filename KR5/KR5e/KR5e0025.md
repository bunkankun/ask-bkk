# Yī qiè dào jīng yīn yì miào mén yóu qǐ 一切道經音義妙門由起

*Pronunciations and Meanings of All Daoist Scriptures: Origins of the Wondrous Gate*

compiled by [[史崇]] (Shǐ Chóng) — Táng Daoist scholar

A **Táng Daoist philological reference** — providing **pronunciations** (*yīn* 音) and **glosses** (*yì* 義) of technical terms across the Daoist canon. Modeled on the Buddhist *Yī qiè jīng yīn yì* 一切經音義. Compiled 712–713 under imperial commission. The present Dào zàng copy preserves only the "miào mén yóu qǐ" 妙門由起 introductory section; the full lexical work is largely lost. Preserved as **DZ 1123** / CT 1123 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 712–713. Dynasty: 唐.
