# Tài píng jīng chāo 太平經鈔

*Abridged Tàipíng Scripture*

Anonymous; Táng

A **Táng epitome** of the great *Tài píng jīng* 太平經 — preserving the scripture's doctrinal content in condensed form across 10 *juàn* arranged by the sexagenary 甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸 ordering. Together with the fragmentary main text [[KR5e0002]] and preface [[KR5e0003]], the three Dào zàng items (DZ 1101 a/b/c) constitute the surviving transmission of the *Tài píng jīng*. The opening work of the **Tài píng bù** 太平部 — the third of the four supplementary sections of the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng, named after the foundational *Tài píng jīng*.

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
