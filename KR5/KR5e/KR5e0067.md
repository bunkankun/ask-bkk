# Xiān chuán wài kē mì fāng 仙傳外科祕方

*Immortal-Transmitted Secret Recipes of External Medicine*

by [[趙宜真]] (Zhào Yízhēn) — late-Yuán / early-Míng Daoist

A Yuán-Míng **Daoist secret-recipe collection on external medicine** (*wài kē* 外科 — surgery, topical treatments, trauma, sores) by the Jìng míng / Qīng wēi patriarch Zhào Yízhēn. Preserved as **DZ 1165** / CT 1165 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Late Yuán to early Míng. Dynasty: 元-明.
