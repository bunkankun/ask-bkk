# Jìng míng zhōng xiào quán shū 淨明忠孝全書

*Complete Book of Pure-Illumination, Loyalty and Filiality*

compiled by [[黃元吉]] (Huáng Yuánjí, 1270–1324) — Yuán Jìng míng patriarch

**The foundational compilation of the Jìng míng 淨明 school** — the Yuán Jìng míng movement's authoritative anthology of scripture, ritual, doctrine, and hagiography, organized around the cult of **[[許遜]] Xǔ Xùn** (Xǔ zhēn jūn 許眞君) at Xī shān 西山 (Jiāngxī). The title emphasizes the school's distinctive **Confucian-Daoist synthesis**: *zhōng xiào* 忠孝 (loyalty and filiality) as the road to *jìng míng* 淨明 (pure illumination). Introduction dated 1327, after Huáng Yuánjí's death. Preserved as **DZ 1110** / CT 1110 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 1327 (introduction). Dynasty: 元.
