# Tài shàng Líng bǎo Jìng míng dào yuán zhèng yìn jīng 太上靈寶淨明道元正印經

*Most High Língbǎo Pure-Illumination Scripture of the Orthodox Seal of the Dào-Origin*

Anonymous; SòngYuán

A Jìng míng 淨明 scripture on the "orthodox seal of the Dào-origin" (*dào yuán zhèng yìn* 道元正印). Preserved as **DZ 1105** / CT 1105 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** SòngYuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
