# Pán xī jí 磻溪集

*Pánxī Collection*

by [[丘處機]] (Qiū Chǔjī, 1148–1227) — one of the Seven Perfected; founder of the Lóng mén branch

Qiū Chǔjī's own Quán zhēn poetry collection (title after the stream at Pán xī 磻溪 in Shǎn xī where Qiū practised austerities). Preserved as **DZ 1159** / CT 1159 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 1186–1208 (preface range). Dynasty: 金.
