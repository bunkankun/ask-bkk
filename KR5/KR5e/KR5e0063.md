# Tài gǔ jí 太古集

*Great-Antiquity Collection*

by [[郝大通]] (Hǎo Dàtōng, 1140–1212) — one of the Seven Perfected

Hǎo Dàtōng's Quán zhēn *Yì jīng*-cosmological collection, with numerous diagrams. Preserved as **DZ 1161** / CT 1161 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** 1178–1236. Dynasty: 金-元.
