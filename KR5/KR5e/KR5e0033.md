# Tài shàng miào fǎ běn xiāng jīng 太上妙法本相經

*Most High Wondrous-Dharma Scripture of Original Appearance*

Anonymous; early Táng

An early-Táng Daoist **doctrinal-soteriological scripture** on the "original appearance" (*běn xiāng* 本相) in the wondrous Dharma. Preserved as **DZ 1131** / CT 1131 (Tài píng bù 太平部).

**Dating.** Early Táng. Dynasty: 唐初期.
