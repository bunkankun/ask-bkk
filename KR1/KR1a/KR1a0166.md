# Zhōuyì Qián záo dù 周易乾鑿度
*The Chiseled Way of the Qián [Hexagram] of the Zhōuyì*
nominally by [[鄭玄]] (註)

## About the work
The fourth of the eight *Yì wěi* 易緯 texts gathered as appendix to the *Yì*-class of the *Sìkù quánshū*; see [[KR1a0163]] for the broader contextualization. The *Zhōuyì Qián záo dù* is the *Qián*-hexagram half of the *Qián Kūn záo dù* ([[KR1a0163]]) cycle and is preserved here in its independent two-juàn recension. The pairing of the two halves (*Qián záo dù* + *Kūn záo dù*) was conventional in the medieval transmission; the *Sìkù* preserves both as separate cataloged entries while noting their shared genre.

### Tiyao
*See [[KR1a0163]] for the joint contextualization. The* Sìkù tíyào *of the Qián záo dù *separately notes that this text was the more widely transmitted of the two záo dù halves, with separate editions circulating throughout the medieval period.*

### Abstract
The work is the *Qián* counterpart to the *Kūn záo dù* portion of [[KR1a0163]]. As with the broader *Yì wěi* corpus, the substantive content concerns Hàn-period (or post-Hàn-but-medieval) divinatory-cosmographic apparatus; the Zhèng Xuán-commentary attribution is conventional and not secure.
