# Zēngbǔ Zhèng shì Zhōuyì 增補鄭氏周易

*The Augmented Zhèng-Family Zhōuyì*

original commentary by [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán (*zì* Kāngchéng 康成, 127–200, Hàn);
recompiled by [[王應麟]] Wáng Yīnglín (1223–1296, Sòng);
augmented and corrected by [[惠棟]] Huì Dòng (*zì* Dìngyǔ 定宇, 1697–1758, Qīng)

## About the work

A Qīng *kǎozhèng* 考證 augmentation, in three *juàn*, of [[王應麟]] Wáng Yīnglín's late-Sòng one-*juàn* reconstruction of [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán's lost *Zhōuyì zhù* 周易註 (= [[KR1a0003]]). Where Wáng had assembled the surviving fragments without consistent source-citation and not always in canonical sequence, [[惠棟]] Huì Dòng of Chángzhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu) traced each Wáng-recorded passage back to its specific source text, reordered everything to follow the received *Zhōuyì*, and combed further sources to add 92 new fragments — 28 for the upper canon, 16 for the lower canon, 14 for the *Xìcí zhuàn* 繫辭傳, 22 for the *Shuōguà zhuàn* 說卦傳, 7 for the *Xùguà zhuàn* 序卦傳, and 5 for the *Záguà zhuàn* 雜卦傳. He moved Wáng's prefatory *Zhèng shì Zhōuyì zàn* 鄭氏周易贊 to the head of the work, deleted the eight illustrative *hùtǐ* 互體 citations Wáng had appended, and added at the end two diagrams reconstructed from Zhèng's lost commentaries on the *Zhōulǐ Tàishī* 周禮太師 (a twelve-month *yáochén* 爻辰 chart) and the *Yuè lìng* 月令 (a *yáochén*-and-twenty-eight-mansions chart). The added diagrams are aimed explicitly against [[朱震]] Zhū Zhèn's *Hànshàng Yìzhuàn* 漢上易傳 ([[KR1a0024]]).

This is the canonical Qīng-evidential reconstruction of Hàn-Yì Zhèng *Yì*; it is the form in which Zhèng Xuán's *Yì* fragments are normally cited in modern scholarship. The WYG *tiyao* and the Kanripo source file `KR1a0004_000.txt` print the work under the Sìkù-imposed title *Xīnběn Zhèng shì Zhōuyì* 新本鄭氏周易 ("New Edition Zhèng-Family Zhōuyì"); the Kanripo catalog and most secondary literature use Huì Dòng's own title *Zēngbǔ Zhèng shì Zhōuyì* 增補鄭氏周易 ("Augmented..."). The dating window 1730–1758 reflects the assumed mature-period composition span of Huì Dòng's reconstruction. The catalog dynasty field is 漢 by the Sìkù convention recorded in the *tiyao* of [[KR1a0003]] (recompiled lost works are filed under the original author's dynasty).

### Tiyao

We respectfully submit that *Xīnběn Zhèng shì Zhōuyì* in three *juàn* was edited by [[惠棟]] Huì Dòng of the present dynasty. Dòng (*zì* Dìngyǔ 定宇, native of Chángzhōu 長洲): [[王應麟]] Wáng Yīnglín had originally recompiled Zhèng Yuán's [Zhèng Xuán's] *Yì* commentary in one *juàn*, and his descendants printed it as an appendix to the *Yùhǎi* 玉海. Although it was made of broken passages and stray phrases, it still gave a fair outline of Hàn learning and rendered considerable service to the canon. However, none of the entries was attributed to a source-text; the order, head-to-tail, did not always match the canonical sequence; and there were omissions.

Dòng, taking that older edition as base, has thoroughly reset and corrected it. For every entry already in Yīnglín's compilation he has consulted the original source and noted under each one the specific book it comes from, making it clear where the evidence stands and verified — extremely meticulous in detail. The order, front to back, has likewise been reset to follow the canonical text in full. He has further searched broadly through the various corpora and supplementarily added: twenty-eight items for the upper canon, sixteen for the lower canon, fourteen for the *Xìcí zhuàn*, twenty-two for the *Shuōguà zhuàn*, seven for the *Xùguà zhuàn*, and five for the *Záguà zhuàn*. He has moved Yīnglín's appended *Yì Zàn* 易贊 to the head of the volume; he has cut out the eight passages, drawn from the various canonical *zhèngyì* commentaries on *hùtǐ*, that Yīnglín had quoted; and, working separately from Zhèng Yuán's [lost] *Zhōulǐ Tàishī* commentary, he has constructed a twelve-month *yáochén* 爻辰 chart, and from Zhèng Yuán's [lost] *Yuè lìng* commentary a *yáochén*-and-twenty-eight-mansions chart, appending both at the end of the volume — the express purpose being to refute the errors of [[朱震]] Zhū Zhèn's *Hànshàng Yìzhuàn*. Although his work proceeds upon another man's foundation, in research-and-collation precision it is in fact superior to the original. Yīnglín may justly be called a meritorious servant to Zhèng's school; this edition by Dòng may equally be called a meritorious servant to Wáng's edition.

Respectfully revised and submitted, third month of the forty-fifth year of Qiánlóng [1780].

General Compilers: [[紀昀]] Jǐ Yún, [[陸錫熊]] Lù Xīxióng, [[孫士毅]] Sūn Shìyì.
General Reviser: [[陸費墀]] Lù Fèichí.

### Abstract

[[惠棟]] Huì Dòng (1697–1758), of Yuánhé 元和 / Chángzhōu 長洲 (Sūzhōu), the founding figure of the Wú-school 吳派 of Qīng *Hàn xué* 漢學 and the most rigorous of the early evidentialists on the *Yì*. The recompilation here was part of his programmatic project to recover the Hàn *Yì* ([[孟喜]] Mèng Xǐ, [[京房]] Jīng Fáng, [[虞翻]] Yú Fān, [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán) against the Wáng Bì–Chéng Yí–Zhū Xī line that had dominated since the Northern Sòng. His other principal *Yì* monographs are *Zhōuyì shù* 周易述 (= [[KR1a0105]]) and *Yì Hàn xué* 易漢學 (= [[KR1a0106]]), with related material in *Yì lì* 易例 (= [[KR1a0107]]) — all of them pendants to the present source-collection.

The text-critical method is exact: every Wáng-Yīnglín fragment is verified back against its source (notably [[李鼎祚]] Lǐ Dǐngzuò's *Zhōuyì jíjiě* [[KR1a0008]], [[陸德明]] Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén*, the *zhèngyì* of *Shī*, *Sānlǐ*, *Chūnqiū*, and the *Hòu Hànshū* and *Wénxuǎn* commentaries), and the source is cited inline — a methodological standard not seen in Yīnglín's original. Huì Dòng's 92 newly added fragments are mostly drawn from Sòng *lèishū* 類書 and from the early Qīng's improving access to *Hòu Hànshū* and *Sānlǐ* sub-commentaries. The two appended *yáochén* diagrams are reconstructions from extant fragments of Zhèng's lost *Sānlǐ* sub-commentaries: they reify Zhèng's correlative-cosmological method in graphic form and are the basis on which Huì Dòng adjudicates the Hàn-versus-Sòng dispute over Hàn *Yì* diagrams (esp. against Zhū Zhèn's reduction of *yáochén* to a Hé-Luò 河洛 numerology).

The work was complete by Huì Dòng's death in 1758, well before the Sìkù 四庫 commission was constituted (1772). The *Sìkù* *tiyao* retitled it *Xīnběn Zhèng shì Zhōuyì* (the title it bears in the WYG print) in order to distinguish it editorially from Wáng Yīnglín's original "old edition"; secondary literature follows Huì Dòng's own title *Zēngbǔ Zhèng shì Zhōuyì*. The two are the same work.

## Translations and research

No European-language translation. The work is a research instrument rather than a primary text.

- Lín Zhōngjūn 林忠軍, *Zhōuyì Zhèng shì xué chǎnwēi* 周易鄭氏學闡微 (Shànghǎi gǔjí, 2005) — uses Huì Dòng's recompilation as base text and assesses its corrections of Wáng Yīnglín.
- Wú Pèi 吳沛 et al. (eds.), *Huì Dòng Yìxué jí* 惠棟易學集 (multivolume modern reprint with collation) — gathers all of Huì Dòng's *Yì* writings together; the modern reference set.
- Benjamin A. Elman, *From Philosophy to Philology: Intellectual and Social Aspects of Change in Late Imperial China* (Harvard, 1984; rev. 2001) — chapter on the Wú school positions Huì Dòng's *Yì* recompilations within the broader *kǎozhèng* turn.
- Hú Wéigé 胡渭 *Yìtú míng biàn* 易圖明辨 (1706, [[KR1a0150]]) — historical predecessor; Huì Dòng silently builds on Hú's anti-*tú* argument while restoring Hàn *yáochén* diagrams.
- Zhū Bóqūn 朱伯崑, *Yìxué zhéxué shǐ* 易學哲學史, vol. 4 (Huáxià, rev. ed. 1995) — substantive treatment of Huì Dòng within the Qīng *Hàn-Yì* revival.

## Other points of interest

The two appended *yáochén* charts are the most contested element of the work: Huì Dòng presents them as Zhèng Xuán's own diagrams reconstructed from his lost *Sānlǐ* commentaries, but the inferential leap from prose-fragment to chart is non-trivial, and several Qīng evidential successors (notably [[張惠言]] Zhāng Huìyán in his *Yú shì Yì* 虞氏易) treat the charts as Huì Dòng's own *expansion* rather than as attested Zhèng Xuán material. Modern scholarship generally agrees with Zhāng.

The work is the second of the *Sìkù* trio of *jíyì* titles for Hàn *Yì* placed in immediate sequence ([[KR1a0003]] Wáng Yīnglín's Zhèng, [[KR1a0004]] Huì Dòng's Zhèng, [[KR1a0005]] Yáo Shìlín's Lù Jì); the editorial pairing dramatizes the Sòng-versus-Qīng comparison in *jíyì* method.
