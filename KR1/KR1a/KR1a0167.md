# Yì wěi tōng guà yàn 易緯通卦驗
*The Apocryphal Yì on Penetrating-Hexagrams and Verifying*
nominally by [[鄭玄]] (註)

## About the work
The fifth of the eight *Yì wěi* 易緯 texts gathered as appendix to the *Yì*-class of the *Sìkù quánshū*; see [[KR1a0163]] for the broader contextualization. Two juàn. The work systematizes the *guàqì* 卦氣 calendrical-divinatory cycle that assigns hexagrams to days/months/seasons (also developed in [[KR1a0164]] *Jī lǎn tú*) and elaborates a *yàn* 驗 (verification) procedure for confirming or correcting the divinatory predictions against observed phenomena.

### Tiyao
*See [[KR1a0163]] for the joint contextualization of the eight* Yì wěi *texts.*

### Abstract
The *Tōng guà yàn* together with the *Jī lǎn tú* ([[KR1a0164]]) constitutes the principal *Yì wěi* documentation of the Hàn-period *guàqì* divinatory tradition. Where the *Jī lǎn tú* lays out the calendrical-cosmographic apparatus, the *Tōng guà yàn* details the verification-procedure by which the apparatus is to be tested against actual phenomena.
