# KR1 經部

KR1: the Classics division.

- [[KR1a]] 易類 *Yì lèi* — Classics — Book of Changes. The *Yìjīng* / *Zhōuyì* canon and its divinatory, diagrammatic, and philosophical commentarial traditions. 174 texts.
- [[KR1b]] 書類 *Shū lèi* — Classics — Book of Documents. The *Shàngshū* canon, old/new text debates, forged chapters, and documentary exegesis. 61 texts.
- [[KR1c]] 詩類 *Shī lèi* — Classics — Book of Poetry. The *Shījīng* / *Máoshī* corpus and its Mao, Zheng, Zhu Xi, and later interpretive lineages. 70 texts.
- [[KR1d]] 禮類 *Lǐ lèi* — Classics — Rituals. Ritual classics and scholarship on ceremony, institutions, etiquette, and classical ritual order. 136 texts.
- [[KR1e]] 春秋類 *Chūnqiū lèi* — Classics — Spring and Autumn Annals. The *Chūnqiū* and its Zuozhuan, Gongyang, Guliang, and later exegetical traditions. 126 texts.
- [[KR1f]] 孝經類 *Xiàojīng lèi* — Classics — Classic of Filial Piety. The *Xiàojīng*, commentaries, and closely related loyalty and filiality texts. 15 texts.
- [[KR1g]] 五經總義類 *Wǔjīng zǒngyì lèi* — Classics — General Works on the Five Classics. Cross-canonical studies treating more than one Confucian classic. 38 texts.
- [[KR1h]] 四書類 *Sìshū lèi* — Classics — Four Books. The *Daxue*, *Zhongyong*, *Lunyu*, and *Mengzi* as organized by Song and later learning. 66 texts.
- [[KR1i]] 樂類 *Yuè lèi* — Classics — Music. Ritual music, pitch systems, instruments, court repertoire, and music-theoretical classicism. 21 texts.
- [[KR1j]] 小學類 *Xiǎoxué lèi* — Classics — Philology and Lexicography. Character studies, phonology, glossaries, dictionaries, and textual philology. 109 texts.
