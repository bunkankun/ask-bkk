# Zuǒshì zhuàn shuō 左氏傳說

*Discussions of the Zuǒ-tradition*

by [[呂祖謙]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Zuǒshì zhuàn shuō* 左氏傳說 in twenty juan is one of three principal *Zuǒzhuàn* works of Lǚ Zǔqiān [[呂祖謙]] (1137–1181), known as *Dōnglái xiānshēng* 東萊先生. The other two are [[KR1e0040]] *Zuǒshì zhuàn xù shuō* and [[KR1e0041]] *Zuǒshì bó yì*; together with the lost *Zuǒzhuàn lèi biān* 左傳類編 (in 19 categories) they constitute Lǚ's lifelong *Zuǒzhuàn* programme. The present work is rich and detailed expository discussion organised by *zhuàn*-passage, with extensive analytical comment. Originally 30 juan (per Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí*); the present text is 20 juan, with the additional 10 juan being the separate *Xù shuō* recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. The Sìkù base is the WYG copy.

### Tiyao

The Sìkù *tíyào* (text from the Kyoto Zinbun digital *Sìkù tíyào*):

By Lǚ Zǔqiān of Sòng. Zǔqiān has the *Gǔ Zhōuyì* registered. His lifelong study of the *Zuǒzhuàn* produced three works: (1) the *Zuǒzhuàn lèi biān*, dividing the *Zuǒshì*'s text into 19 categories — long without transmitted exemplar, scattered in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* with little to draw from; (2) the *Bó yì*, raising matters and establishing meanings to evaluate gains and losses; (3) the present work, whose argument is roughly the same as the *Bó yì* but with much fuller exposition.

Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí* says: "On the *Zuǒshì*'s text, much elucidation; not formal prose; like a moment's lecture, transcribed by his disciples." Chén's view is sound. Zhū Zǐ's *Yǔ lù* also calls the work "exceedingly broad and detailed; but the deployment of language and intent rather suffers from cleverness."

Examining Lǚ's own *Dà shì jì* 大事記 (Great Events Record), Zhū Zǐ also says it has "places of fine cleverness" but praises Lǚ's pointing out the Hàn-period Gōngsūn Hóng 公孫弘 and Zhāng Tāng's 張湯 villainy as "all stated to humiliate them." So Zhū's "clever" refers to Lǚ's pen-edge being keen — every targeting penetrating to expose without reserve — not to clever debate or to inversion of right and wrong.

The *Shū lù jiětí* gives this work as 30 juan. The present text is only 20. Examining Zhāng Xuān's 張萱 Míng *Nèigé shūmù* 內閣書目: it lists *Zhuàn shuō* in 4 *cè* and additionally *Xù shuō* in 4 *cè* — making clear that Chén's "30 juan" includes the *Xù shuō* in 10 juan. We have separately gathered the *Xù shuō* from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* into a complete text, and (since its arrangement constitutes its own unit) keep it separately registered ([[KR1e0040]]).

### Abstract

The *Sìkù tíyào* makes the principal points: that this is one of Lǚ Zǔqiān's three principal *Zuǒzhuàn* works, the rich and detailed expository version; that the work is closely related to the *Bó yì* [[KR1e0041]] but with fuller exposition; that Chén Zhènsūn's "30 juan" total combines the present 20 juan plus the 10-juan *Xù shuō* [[KR1e0040]], the latter recovered separately from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*; that Zhū Xī's verdict — "clever" prose targeting Hàn-period villainy — captures Lǚ's polemical edge but does not impugn his judgement.

The work belongs to the Lǚ-school *Chūnqiū* and *Zuǒzhuàn* line that had begun with Lǚ Hàowèn, continued via Lǚ Běnzhōng [[KR1e0035]] and Lǚ Zǔqiān's senior teacher Lín Zhīqí 林之奇 (1112–1176), and culminated in Lǚ Zǔqiān himself. The school's signature is exhaustive coverage with precise source-attribution.

## Other points of interest

Lǚ Zǔqiān co-edited the seminal *Jìn sī lù* 近思錄 ("Reflections on Things at Hand") with Zhū Xī — the principal anthology of *Dàoxué* doctrine — in 1175, the same period in which the present *Zuǒzhuàn* lectures were being delivered. The intellectual closeness with Zhū Xī (who delivered the rather warm but qualified verdict on this work) is the central political-intellectual hinge of Lǚ's career.
