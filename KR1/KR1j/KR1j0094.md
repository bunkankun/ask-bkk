# Ěryǎ Gù-shì yīn 爾雅顧氏音
*Master Gù's Phonological Commentary on the* Ěryǎ
by [[顧野王]] (撰)

## About the work

A modern reconstruction of [[顧野王]] Gù Yěwáng's lost *Ěryǎ yīn* 爾雅音, the principal Liáng-period phonological apparatus to the *Ěryǎ* 爾雅 ([[KR1j0002]]). Gù Yěwáng — better known as the compiler of the *Yùpiān* 玉篇 [[KR1j0022]] — also produced this companion *yīn* (phonological gloss) for the *Ěryǎ* corpus. The original is recorded in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 (*xiǎoxué*) and was lost between the late Táng and Sòng; the CHANT reconstruction (CH2f1204) collates citations from Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 and the *Ěryǎ shū* 爾雅疏 of [[邢昺]] Xíng Bǐng ([[KR1j0003]]).

### Abstract

The Gù-shì *yīn* belongs to a small cluster of Liáng-period *Ěryǎ* phonological commentaries — [[KR1j0094]] (Gù Yěwáng), [[KR1j0095]] (Shī Qián 施乾), and [[KR1j0096]] (Xiè Qiáo 謝嶠) — that together represent the late southern *xiǎoxué* engagement with the *Ěryǎ* before the Suí *Qièyùn* synthesis. They are routinely cited together by Lù Démíng (frequently as *Gù, Shī, Xiè* 顧、施、謝, or in pairs).

Representative surviving fragment: at *Ěryǎ* 〈Shìgǔ 釋詁〉 first chapter, *jiǎn-zhào bǎn-zhì* 簡箌昄晊 — Gù reads *zhào* as *dū-jué qiè* 都角切 with the gloss "*Shuōwén* says *cǎo dà yě* 草大也; *Hán-shī* says *zhào bǐ fǔ-tián* 箌彼甫田" (cited via Xíng Bǐng's *shū*; Lù Démíng's *shìwén* parallel reads "切" as "反", reflecting the historical spelling-shift from *qiè* to *fǎn*). Other entries: *zhì* 晊 read *yīn-bǎn* 音板 or *fǎn-qiè pǔ-jiān*/*pǔ-liàn* 普姦、普練二反.

The reading-method shows Gù Yěwáng's *Yùpiān*-trained phonological apparatus applied directly to the *Ěryǎ* corpus — *fǎn-qiè* spellings often with multiple alternative readings and with explicit citation of *Shuōwén* 說文 and *Hán-shī wài-zhuàn* 韓詩外傳 attestations.

Dating: the bracket reflects Gù Yěwáng's whole career (519–581); the work is conventionally dated to the period immediately following his completion of the *Yùpiān* (543), but no firm internal date is preserved.

*No substantial dedicated Western-language secondary literature located.* The standard treatments are:
- Rén Dàchūn 任大椿, *Xiǎoxué gōuchén* 小學鉤沈.
- Mǎ Guóhàn 馬國翰, *Yùhánshānfáng jíyìshū* 玉函山房輯佚書.

## Other points of interest

The clustering of three Liáng-period *Ěryǎ* *yīn* in this CHANT trio (Gù, Shī, Xiè) — all preserved chiefly through Lù Démíng — confirms that the *Ěryǎ* received a coordinated phonological re-reading by the southern Liáng *xiǎoxué* milieu, of which Gù Yěwáng's *Yùpiān* was the principal monument.
