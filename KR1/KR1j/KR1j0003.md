# Ěryǎ shū 爾雅疏
*Sub-commentary on the* Ěryǎ
by [[邢昺]] (Xíng Bǐng) and colleagues, by imperial command (奉勅撰)

## About the work
A ten-*juàn* sub-commentary (*shū* 疏) on Guō Pú's annotated *Ěryǎ*, prepared by Xíng Bǐng 邢昺 (932–1010) and a board of court scholars at the order of Sòng Zhēnzōng. It is the standard *zhèngyì* 正義-style sub-commentary on the *Ěryǎ* and the version that became canonical when the *Ěryǎ* was incorporated into the Thirteen Classics of the *Shísānjīng zhùshū* 十三經注疏.

### Abstract
Sòng Zhēnzōng commissioned the work because the existing *Ěryǎ* sub-commentaries by Sūn Yán 孫炎 and Gāo Lián 高璉 — both pre-Táng — were judged "shallow and incomplete" (淺略). Xíng Bǐng was Hànlín *shìjiǎng xuéshì* and *zhíjiǔ* of the Imperial Academy when the project was undertaken; the team included Dù Hào 杜鎬 (drafting officer), Sūn Shì 孫奭 and others. Their preface (preserved here in *juàn* 0) states that they took the *Classics* as the documentary base and Guō Pú as the master commentator. The work was presented in Xiánpíng 2 (999) and printed at the National Academy in Xiánpíng 4 (1001); thus the standard date range for completion is 999–1010 (Xíng Bǐng's death). Together with Guō Pú's commentary, this *shū* circulates as the core of the standard *Ěryǎ zhùshū* 爾雅注疏 transmitted in the Sìbù congkān facsimile from a Sòng print. The composition reflects the early-Sòng campaign of imperially-sponsored sub-commentaries which also produced Xíng Bǐng's *Lúnyǔ shū*, *Xiàojīng shū* and *Lǐjì shū* under the same general program.
