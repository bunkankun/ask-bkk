# Yīn pǔ 音譜
*A Phonological Compendium*
by [[李概]] (撰)

## About the work

A modern reconstruction of [[李概]] Lǐ Gài's lost *Yīn pǔ* 音譜 — one of several brief Northern-Qí 北齊 rhyme-books circulating in the immediate pre-*Qièyùn* generation. *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records the work in four *juàn*. It was lost in the Sòng; the CHANT reconstruction (CH2f1225) draws principally from Shì Xuányìng 釋元應's *Yīqièjīng yīnyì* 一切經音義, the *Guǎngyùn* 廣韻, and Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén*.

### Abstract

The *Yīn pǔ* is a Northern-Qí phonological compendium in the *Shēng lèi* / *Yùn jí* lineage — a relatively brief work (four *juàn*) contributing to the development of the rhyme-book tradition that immediately precedes the Suí *Qièyùn* (601).

Representative entries: *chù* 憷 = "*tòng yě* 痛也, pain"; *jié* 䠨 = "*mǎ jí-xíng* 馬急行, a horse moving in haste"; another graph (rendered 𩅼) = "*yǐ wù nà shuǐ-zhōng* 以物內水中, to immerse an object in water." These are short paraphrastic glosses without explicit *fǎn-qiè* spellings preserved in the fragment-sample.

The Northern-Qí rhyme-book cluster ([[KR1j0112]] *Yīn pǔ* of Lǐ Gài, [[KR1j0113]] *Yùn lüè* of [[陽休之]] Yáng Xiūzhī) represents the northern parallel to the southern Liáng *xiǎoxué* phonological work of Gù Yěwáng. The convergence of these northern and southern Six-Dynasties rhyme traditions in the Lù Fǎyán *Qièyùn* conference (Rénshòu 1, 601) is the structural moment that produces the Suí-Táng standard.

Dating bracket (550–577): Northern-Qí span; no firmer internal evidence.
