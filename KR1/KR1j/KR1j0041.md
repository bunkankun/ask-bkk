# Liùshū zhèngé 六書正譌
*Correcting the Errors of the Six-Script Doctrine*
by [[周伯琦]] (Zhōu Bóqí, 撰)

## About the work
A five-*juàn* orthographic dictionary by Zhōu Bóqí 周伯琦 (1298–1369), companion to his *Shuōwén zìyuán* [[KR1j0040]] in 1 *juàn*. Internally arranged by *Lǐbù yùnlüè* 206 rhyme classes, with *xiǎozhuàn* small-seal as head graph; first the structural rationale of the graph is given, and below "*lìzuò X-X, súzuò X-X*" (clerical writes X-X, vulgar writes X-X) for distinguishing — broadly modeled on Zhāng Yǒu's [[張有]] *Fùgǔ biān* [[KR1j0031]].

### Abstract
The *Liùshū zhèngé* is the second of Zhōu Bóqí's two paired late-Yuán paleographic monuments. Where the *Shuōwén zìyuán* re-arranges the 540 *Shuōwén* radicals to bring cognates into adjacency, the *Liùshū zhèngé* re-organizes the actual graph corpus by rhyme class, in the manner of Zhāng Yǒu's *Fùgǔ biān*. The work distinguishes *zhèngtǐ*, *lì*-script, and *sútǐ* forms for each lemma. Sìkù compilers' overall judgment: more precise than Yáng Huán [[楊桓]] but less so than Zhāng Yǒu.
