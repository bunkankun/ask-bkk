# Wénzì jí lüè 文字集略
*Compact Compilation on Graphs*
by [[阮孝緒]] (撰)

## About the work

A modern reconstruction of [[阮孝緒]] Ruǎn Xiàoxù's lost *Wénzì jí lüè* 文字集略, the Liáng-period bibliographer-recluse's character glossary. Ruǎn Xiàoxù — better known as the compiler of the *Qīlù* 七錄 ("Seven Records") bibliography that underlies the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* classification system — also produced this short *xiǎoxué* compilation; the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* records the *Wénzì jí lüè* in six *juàn*; lost by the Sòng. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2f1219) draws principally from Buddhist *yīnyì* literature (the *Yīqièjīng yīnyì* of Shì Xuányìng / Huìlín, the *Fó běn-xíng jí jīng yīnyì* 佛本行集經音義).

### Abstract

The *Wénzì jí lüè* is a *compact compilation* in the strict sense — a short paraphrastic glossary of difficult graphs, selected for practical reading rather than for paleographic comprehensiveness.

Representative entries: *jiǎn* 瞼 = "*yǎn-wài pí yě* 眼外皮也, the outer eyelid" (cited via Shì Xuányìng's *Yuè-dēng sān-mèi jīng yīnyì* 月燈三昧經音義); *míng-xiāng* 名相 = "*zhào-mù yě* 詔目也, the listing of names" (cited from the *Fó běn-xíng jí jīng yīnyì*, where the gloss appears as a citation from the parallel-title *Zì lüè* 字略 [[KR1j0108]] of [[宋世良]] Sòng Shìliáng).

The note in the source — that the *Fó běn-xíng jí jīng yīnyì* citation appears under the title *Zì lüè* rather than *Wénzì jí lüè* — is the principal textual-bibliographic problem of the surviving fragments: the abbreviated title *Zì lüè* in Táng Buddhist *yīnyì* potentially refers either to Ruǎn Xiàoxù's *Wénzì jí lüè* or to Sòng Shìliáng's separate Northern-Wèi *Zì lüè* [[KR1j0108]], and CHANT preserves both attributions while noting the conflict.

Dating bracket (502–536): Ruǎn Xiàoxù's documented adulthood (he died in 536); the *Qīlù* preface dates to 523, anchoring his mature scholarly period.

## Other points of interest

The shared abbreviated-title problem with Sòng Shìliáng's [[KR1j0108]] *Zì lüè* is methodologically instructive: it shows the limits of Táng-period *yīnyì* citation as a reconstruction source, where two pre-Suí works with overlapping titles cannot always be disentangled. The CHANT editor's decision to preserve both attributions rather than silently assign each fragment to a single work is the correct philological response.
