# Zá zì jiě gǔ 雜字解詁
*Miscellaneous Graphs Explained and Glossed*
by [[周成]] (撰)

## About the work

A modern reconstruction of [[周成]] Zhōu Chéng's lost *Zá zì jiě gǔ* 雜字解詁, the larger of his two Jìn-period character glossaries (with [[KR1j0102]] *Zhōuchéng nán zì*). *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records this work in four *juàn*. Both works were lost in the Sòng; the CHANT reconstruction (CH2f1212) collects surviving citations principally from Yú Shìnán 虞世南's *Běitáng shūchāo* 北堂書鈔, *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽, and the *Shǐjì suǒyǐn* 史記索隱 of Sīmǎ Zhēn 司馬貞.

### Abstract

The *Zá zì jiě gǔ* is a *miscellaneous graph* glossary — the title declares it to compile graphs not fitting the standard *Cāngjié*-tradition lemma sequence (i.e., rare graphs and graphic curiosities) and to supply both a *jiě* (etymological / structural explanation) and a *gǔ* (paraphrastic gloss) for each.

Representative entries: *xiāo* 霄 = "*mā-tiān chì-qì yě* 摩天赤氣也, red vapor that grazes the sky" (cited from *Běitáng shūchāo* 150 and *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 8); an archaic graph (rendered &-; in the source) read *yīn jì* 音忌, cited from Sīmǎ Zhēn's *Shǐjì suǒyǐn* on the *Gāozǔ gōng-chén hòu nián-biǎo* 高祖功臣侯年表.

The work occupies the middle ground between the Wèi *Cāngjié* supplements (Zhāng Yī's [[KR1j0098]] *Pí cāng* + [[KR1j0099]] *Gǔjīn zì gǔ*; [[KR1j0100]] *Guǎng cāng*) and the later Liù-Cháo paleographic compendia ([[KR1j0109]] *Zì tǒng*, etc.) — a Jìn-period transitional witness to the diversification of *zìshū* genres after the Hàn–Wèi character primer.

The dating bracket (280–420) follows the broad Western-and-Eastern Jìn span; no internal dating evidence in the surviving fragments narrows this further. Zhōu Chéng has no documented court office and is essentially a name attached to a pair of catalog entries.
