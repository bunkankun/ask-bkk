# Xiǎoxué piān 小學篇
*The Primer of* Lesser Learning
by anonymous compiler (傳本佚)

## About the work

A modern reconstruction of an anonymous *Xiǎoxué piān* 小學篇 — a Six-Dynasties character primer in the *Cāngjié* / *Jíjiù* tradition. The title — "Primer of *Xiǎoxué*" — refers explicitly to the bibliographic category itself, and the work is one of the principal practical *xiǎoxué* primers of the Northern dynasties. *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records the work (anonymously) in one *juàn*; lost in the Sòng. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2f1217) draws principally from Buddhist *yīnyì* literature (Shì Xuányìng 釋元應's *Yīqièjīng yīnyì* 一切經音義 with extensive Vinaya and treatise citations), from [[顏之推]]'s *Yánshì jiāxùn ‧ Shū-zhèng* 顏氏家訓‧書證, and from the Táng-period *Běi-hù lù* 北戶錄.

### Abstract

The *Xiǎoxué piān* is the only attested primer in the pre-Suí *xiǎoxué* corpus to bear the title 小學 (rather than the more usual 字書, 字林, 字統, 字略, 字統, 字典 etc. titles) — a self-conscious naming that aligns the work directly with the *Sìkù* bibliographic category as it would later be formalized.

Representative entries: *bì* 篦 = "*shuā yě* 刷也, comb-and-brush" (cited from Shì Xuányìng's *yīnyì* on the Vinaya *Sì-fēn lǜ* 19, *Sēng-qí lǜ* 34, the *Chéng-shí lùn* 4, and the *Fó běn-xíng zàn jīng* 4 — a typical clustered Buddhist attestation); *zhèn* 陣 (cited in [[顏之推]]'s *Yánshì jiāxùn ‧ Shū-zhèng*); *wù-jīng* 芴菁 (cited from *Běi-hù lù*).

The work is anonymous: no author is preserved in any catalog, and the CHANT reconstruction does not assign one. The northern-dynasties dating is conventional, based on the Buddhist *yīnyì* citation pattern and the early Táng reception in *Yánshì jiāxùn* and *Běi-hù lù*.

The work has no relation to Zhū Xī 朱熹's late-Sòng *Xiǎoxué* 小學 (an ethics-and-ritual primer in the Neo-Confucian curriculum); the two share only the generic title.

Dating bracket (386–581): the broad Six-Dynasties northern span. The *Yánshì jiāxùn* citation supplies a *terminus ante quem*.

## Other points of interest

The unique self-reflexive title — a work *about xiǎoxué* called *Xiǎoxué piān* — points to a moment in pre-Suí intellectual history when the bibliographic category and the practical primer-genre converged in title. The naming is not repeated until [[KR1j0089]] Duàn Yùcái's 段玉裁 explicit Qīng programme some twelve centuries later.
