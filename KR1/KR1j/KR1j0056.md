# Dàguǎng yìhuì Yùpiān 大廣益會玉篇
*The Greatly-Expanded* Yùpiān
by [[陳彭年]] (Chén Péngnián, 961–1017) et al., by imperial command, Sòng Dàzhōngxiángfú 6 (1013)

## About the work
The *Sìbù cóngkān* (SBCK) reprint of the imperially-revised Northern-Sòng *Yùpiān* — i.e., the same imperial recension treated under [[KR1j0022]] (*Chóngxiū Yùpiān*, WYG version) but bearing its alternative Sòng-period title *Dàguǎng yìhuì Yùpiān*. The dictionary covers 30 *juàn* under 540 *bùshǒu*, transmitting the Liáng-period *Yùpiān* (543) of Gù Yěwáng [[顧野王]] in the Northern-Sòng recension produced by Chén Péngnián, Wú Ruì 吳銳, Qiū Yōng [[丘雍]] and others on imperial command (Dàzhōngxiángfú 6 / 1013). The SBCK base is the Yuán-Míng print line preserving the Sòng *Dàguǎng yìhuì* title — distinct from Zhāng Shìjùn's later print which (per the Sìkù tíyào discussion under [[KR1j0022]]) wrongly claimed *Shàngyuán*-edition status while in fact transmitting the same Sòng *chóngxiū* text. The work is the second great Chinese character dictionary after the *Shuōwén jiězì*; for the full bibliographic entry — provenance, Liáng/Táng/Sòng layered transmission, Shén Gǒng's *Wǔyīn shēnglùn* / *fǎnniǔtú* appendices, modern critical editions — see [[KR1j0022]].

### Tiyao
*No tiyao in source.* The SBCK transmits the Sòng-period *Yùpiān* without the Sìkù imperial *tíyào* (the imperial preface to the Sòng work itself, by Chén Péngnián, is preserved). For the Sìkù tíyào on this work, see the WYG version [[KR1j0022]].

### Abstract
This entry is the SBCK transmission of the same *Yùpiān* recension treated more fully at [[KR1j0022]]. The SBCK base text descends from the Yuán-Míng print line which preserved the Northern-Sòng imperial title *Dàguǎng yìhuì Yùpiān* — referring to Chén Péngnián's [[陳彭年]] re-edition of Dàzhōngxiángfú 6 (1013) which substantially expanded the Liáng-period text of Gù Yěwáng [[顧野王]] (presented 543) and the Táng-period augmentation by Sūn Qiáng (Shàngyuán 1, 674). The Sìkù imperial editors recognised this version as the one preserved in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* and used by Yuán-period scholars. *notBefore* = *notAfter* = 1013, the year of Chén Péngnián's revision.

## Other points of interest
The presence of the Sòng-period title *Dàguǎng yìhuì* on the SBCK frontispiece is the signal that this is the post-1013 imperial revision — distinct from the lost original Liáng *Yùpiān* (only fragments of which survive in *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* citations and a sixth-century manuscript fragment from the Yùnzhèngyuàn 元正院 in Japan, the *Wǎnjuànzǐběn Yùpiān* 篇玉殘卷, edited by Hú Jí 胡吉宣 1989).
