# Lǐjì Liú-shì yīn 禮記劉氏音

*Master Liú's Phonological Commentary on the* Lǐjì

by [[劉昌宗]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[劉昌宗]] Liú Chāngzōng's lost *Lǐjì Liú-shì yīn* 禮記劉氏音 — the companion to his much more extensive parallel [[KR1d0112]] *Zhōulǐ Liú-shì yīn*. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1105) is drawn from Lù Démíng 陸德明's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 and Dīng Dù 丁度's Sòng-period *Jíyùn* 集韻 citations.

### Abstract

The opening preserved fragment is from \<Tángōng shàng\> 檀弓上: "*ěr wú cóng-cóng ěr*" 爾毋從從爾 with *cóng* glossed *cù-cōng qiē* 徂聰切 in *fǎn-qiè* — citation from *Jíyùn shàng-píng-shēng yī-dōng cóng-zì zhù* (*Jíyùn* upper-level-tone first-*dōng* rhyme, *cóng*-character annotation).

The Sòng-period *Jíyùn* cross-citation is significant: it confirms that Liú Chāngzōng's phonological apparatus to the *Lǐjì* — like his parallel *Zhōulǐ* apparatus — remained an active reference for Sòng rhyme-dictionary compilers four hundred years after composition.

The substantive content covers the canonical 49-*piān* *Lǐjì* with characteristic Liú Chāngzōng *fǎn-qiè* readings, often cited as alternative-to-Xú Miǎo and alternative-to-Lǐ Guǐ readings.

The dating bracket (317–419) reflects the broad Eastern-Jìn span; Liú Chāngzōng's exact lifespan is not preserved.

## Other points of interest

The pairing of [[劉昌宗]]'s [[KR1d0112]] *Zhōulǐ Liú-shì yīn* (2 *juàn*) and the present *Lǐjì Liú-shì yīn* (1 *juàn*) — both showing the *Jíyùn* Sòng-cross-citation — confirms his standing as the second principal Eastern-Jìn-period systematic phonological commentator on the *Sānlǐ* alongside [[徐邈]] Xú Miǎo.
