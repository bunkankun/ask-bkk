# Sāngfú shìyí 喪服釋疑

*Resolving Doubts on Mourning Dress*

by [[劉智]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[劉智]] Liú Zhì's (*c.* 245–305) lost *Sāngfú shìyí* 喪服釋疑 — Liú Zhì's principal *Sāngfú* (mourning-dress) treatise, distinct from his more general [[KR1d0094]] / [[KR1d0099]]-cited *Shìyí* 釋疑 (question-and-answer ritual rulings). Liú Zhì is the named interlocutor of [[虞喜]] Yú Xǐ in the [[KR1d0094]] *Tōngyí* fragment-record, where his *Shìyí* supplies first-rulings and Yú Xǐ's *Tōngyí* the complementary judgements. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1084) is drawn from Dù Yòu 杜佑's *Tōngdiǎn* 通典 citations.

### Abstract

The opening preserved fragment is a question on *Lǐjì-Wén-wáng shì-zǐ*: "*gōng sù-fú bù jǔ, wú qí lún zhī sāng, wú fú, qīn kū zhī*" 公素服不舉，無其倫之喪，無服。親哭之 — the duke wears the plain dress and ceases entertainment, but lacking a *lún* (collateral-relation) mourning-case, wears no mourning-dress and personally performs the wailing.

Liú Zhì's *Sāngfú shìyí* addresses the technical question of what mourning the duke (and by extension the Son of Heaven) should observe for mourning-cases outside the canonical five-grade *Sāngfú* schema — i.e. for relatives by *yì* (rightness) rather than *qīn* (blood-kinship), for foster-children, and for collateral cases not anticipated by the *Sāngfú zhuàn*. The principal Liú Zhì position throughout is restrictive: where the *Sāngfú* does not prescribe, no formal mourning-dress should be assumed, but personal wailing (*qīn kū*) should be observed as an emotional commitment.

The dating bracket (265–320) reflects Liú Zhì's documented Western- and early-Eastern-Jìn court career. The *Sāngfú shìyí* is conventionally placed in his middle-career *Yǔshǐ-zhōng-chéng* and *Sǎn-qí cháng-shì* period.

## Translations and research

*No substantial secondary literature located.* Treated in surveys of Western-Jìn ritual scholarship and in *Tōngdiǎn*-source-criticism.

## Other points of interest

Liú Zhì's restrictive position in the *Sāngfú shìyí* — that absent a canonical *Sāngfú* rule, no formal mourning-dress should be assumed — is the doctrinal counterweight to [[賀循]]'s more inclusive [[KR1d0117]] *Hè-shì sāngfú pǔ* approach. The two positions frame the Eastern-Jìn court's debates on cross-canonical mourning cases.
