# Jízhù sāngfú jīngzhuàn 集注喪服經傳

*Collected Annotation on the Mourning-Dress Classic and Tradition*

by [[裴松之]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[裴松之]] Péi Sōngzhī's (372–451) lost *Jízhù sāngfú jīngzhuàn* 集注喪服經傳 — a collected-annotation commentary on the canonical *Yílǐ-Sāngfú* and its *zhuàn*. Péi Sōngzhī is principally known to standard history as the author of the *Sānguó zhì zhù* 三國志注 (the indispensable Liù-cháo commentary on Chén Shòu's *Sānguó zhì*, [[KR2c0001]]), but his ritual scholarship is substantial. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1093) is drawn from Dù Yòu 杜佑's *Tōngdiǎn* 通典 citations.

### Abstract

The opening preserved fragment glosses the canonical *dà-gōng* protocol: *dà-gōng bù-cuī cháng, mǔ-má dié-yīng, bù dài* 大功布衰裳、牡麻絰纓、布帶 — i.e. the cloth-cut mourning-dress, male-hemp head-and-loin bands, cloth girdle. The collected-annotation form (*jí-zhù* 集注) shows Péi Sōngzhī's characteristic methodology: a base-text of canonical *Sāngfú* materials, then collected glosses drawn from Hàn-Wèi-Jìn ritual authorities (Zhèng Xuán, Wáng Sù, Dù Yù, Hè Xún) with Péi Sōngzhī's own judgement-comments.

The substantive content covers:

(i) **The *dà-gōng* (nine-month) protocol** with collected annotations from prior authorities.

(ii) **Mourning-grade transitions** — particularly the *dà-gōng* to *xiǎo-gōng* downgrade and the conditions under which it may be triggered.

(iii) **Female-line and post-marriage mourning** rulings.

The dating bracket (372–451) reflects Péi Sōngzhī's documented lifespan in *Sòng shū* 64.

The CHANT-listed title 集注喪服經傳 — also borne by [[KR1d0089]] *Sāngfú jīngzhuàn* (Kǒng Lún 孔倫) in CH2e1089 — is one of two same-title works in the corpus; Péi Sōngzhī's version (CH2e1093) is later and more substantive in its synthesis of the prior Hàn-Wèi-Jìn annotation traditions.

## Other points of interest

The collected-annotation (*jí-zhù*) format Péi Sōngzhī applied to the *Sāngfú* — a base-text plus surveyed prior glosses plus the editor's own judgement — is identical in method to his *Sānguó zhì zhù* approach, confirming the unity of his scholarly practice across history-classics and ritual-classics. The *Sāngfú jí-zhù* represents the Liú-Sòng court-ritual moment when prior Hàn-Wèi-Jìn *Sāngfú* scholarship was being systematically organized into a unified canonical reference.
