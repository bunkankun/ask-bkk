# Sāngfú shìxíng yàojì 喪服世行要記

*Essential Notes on Mourning Dress in Contemporary Practice*

by [[王逡之]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[王逡之]] Wáng Qūnzhī's (late-fifth-century, Southern Qí) lost *Sāngfú shìxíng yàojì* 喪服世行要記 — a Southern-Qí court compendium of practical (*shì-xíng*, "in-world-practice") *Sāngfú* rulings. Wáng Qūnzhī was a Qí *Shàngshū zuǒ-chéng* 尚書左丞 and *Zhuó-zuò láng* 著作郎; the surviving text records his court-correspondence with [[王儉]] Wáng Jiǎn on specific imperial mourning cases. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1097) is drawn from Dù Yòu 杜佑's *Tōngdiǎn* 通典 citations.

### Abstract

The opening preserved fragment records a court-question-and-response: *Wáng Qūnzhī* questions *Wáng Jiǎn* (then *Zuǒ Púyè*) on the mourning protocol for the *Huáng tài-zǐ* (heir-apparent) Mù-fēi 穆妃 — specifically: should the *Zhōng-jūn Nán-jùn Wáng* 中軍南郡王's *xiǎo-xiáng* (lesser felicitous-sacrifice) wait for *Wén-xī* (the *Wén-xī gōng*)? Mù-fēi died on the seventh-month twenty-fourth day; *Wén-xī gōng* opened the formal mourning-wailing on the eighth-month [date].

This documents the standard Southern-Qí court-mourning ruling-process: the *Shàngshū zuǒ-chéng* (Wáng Qūnzhī's office) issues the technical question; *Zuǒ Púyè* [[王儉]] returns the court's ruling. The substantive content of the *Sāngfú shìxíng yàojì* compiles these Wáng-Qūnzhī-to-Wáng-Jiǎn case-correspondences as a working reference for the Southern-Qí court.

The dating bracket (472–502) reflects the documented Southern-Qí dynasty span; Wáng Qūnzhī's specific biographical details are sparse, but his collaboration with Wáng Jiǎn places him squarely in the *Jiànyuán* / *Yǒngmíng* / *Yǒng-yuán* court-ritual circle.
