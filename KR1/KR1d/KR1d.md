# KR1d 禮類 — Rituals

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR1d 禮類 (*Lǐ lèi*, "Rituals") is the ritual section of the Classics branch (*jīngbù* 經部, KR1) of the *Sìkù quánshū* fourfold scheme. The division collects the three canonical ritual classics — *Zhōulǐ* 周禮 [[KR1d0001]], *Yílǐ* 儀禮 [[KR1d0025]], *Lǐjì* 禮記 [[KR1d0052]] — together with their pre-modern commentaries, sub-commentaries, abridgements, and topical recastings, and a sizeable terminal cluster of synthesizing *Sānlǐ* compendia, *zōnglèi* 總類 reference works (illustrated diagrams, mourning encyclopedias, the *Wǔlǐ tōngkǎo*), and the practical-ritual / family-ritual manuals that descend from Sīmǎ Guāng's *Shūyí* [[KR1d0088]] and Zhū Xī's *Jiālǐ* [[KR1d0089]]. The 92 texts span from a pair of pre-Hàn / Western-Hàn canonical recensions (the three *jīng* themselves and the *Dà-Dài Lǐjì* [[KR1d0076]]) through the great Eastern-Hàn / Táng layered editions of Zhèng Xuán 鄭玄 and Jiǎ Gōngyàn 賈公彥 / Kǒng Yǐngdá 孔穎達 (mid-7th c.), Northern-Sòng reformist re-readings (Wáng Ānshí's *Zhōuguān xīnyì* [[KR1d0004]]), Southern-Sòng / Yuán scholastic compendia (Wèi Shí 衞湜 *Lǐjì jíshuō* [[KR1d0057]]; Chén Hào 陳澔 *Lǐjì jíshuō* [[KR1d0059]]; the ambitious *Yílǐ jīngzhuàn tōngjiě* [[KR1d0085]] of Zhū Xī and his school), and the high-Qīng evidential / imperial flowering (the three *Qīndìng yìshū* [[KR1d0018]], [[KR1d0037]], [[KR1d0068]] of the Qiánlóng court; Qín Huìtián's 262-juan *Wǔlǐ tōngkǎo* [[KR1d0087]]).

The reader must keep three structural facts in mind. First, the three canonical classics are heterogeneous in genre — the *Zhōulǐ* is an idealised bureaucratic blueprint, the *Yílǐ* a manual of ceremonial procedure, the *Lǐjì* a heterogeneous Confucian-school anthology — and were welded into a unitary *Sānlǐ* 三禮 corpus only retrospectively, by Zhèng Xuán in the late 2nd c. CE (Wilkinson §28.4.3). Second, the *Zhōulǐ* was the principal Old-Text classic (*gǔwén* 古文) of the late Western Hàn and remained controversial throughout the imperial period: Wáng Ānshí used it as the constitutional grounding for the Xīnfǎ 新法 reforms, Kāng Yǒuwéi reignited the *jīnwén/gǔwén* dispute in 1891 with the claim that it was a Liú Xīn forgery, and Qīng evidential scholars devoted disproportionate energy to its philology (Wilkinson §28.7). Third, the *Lǐjì* is the only ritual classic whose materials migrated outside the corpus: its *Dàxué* 大學 and *Zhōngyōng* 中庸 chapters were extracted by the Chéng brothers and Zhū Xī to form half of the *Sì shū* 四書 (Wilkinson §28.4.4), which means that the per-text notes here cover only the *Lǐjì* qua ritual canon and its commentaries; the *Sì shū* literature on *Dàxué* and *Zhōngyōng* sits in KR1g.

## Important texts and text clusters

The principal works fall into four clusters plus a terminal compendium cluster.

**The *Zhōulǐ* cluster ([[KR1d0001]]–0024).** *Zhōulǐ* 周禮 [[KR1d0001]], the canonical text, anchors the cluster as the foundational idealised-bureaucracy classic in six "ministries" (*liù guān* 六官) corresponding to the seasons, with the lost *Dōngguān* 冬官 supplied by the artisanal *Kǎogōngjì* 考工記. *Zhōulǐ zhùshū* 周禮注疏 [[KR1d0003]] is the standard layered edition combining Zhèng Xuán's Eastern-Hàn annotation, Jiǎ Gōngyàn's Táng sub-commentary, and Lù Démíng's *yīnyì* — one of the cornerstones of the *Shísān jīng zhùshū*. *Zhōuguān xīnyì* 周官新義 [[KR1d0004]] is the *locus classicus* of Wáng Ānshí's politically charged Xīnfǎ reading of the classic. *Zhōulǐ dìngyì* 周禮訂義 [[KR1d0010]] of Wáng Yǔzhī 王與之 is the largest Sòng *Zhōulǐ* compendium (80 juan), preserving the bulk of mid-Sòng commentarial opinion. *Qīndìng Zhōuguān yìshū* 欽定周官義疏 [[KR1d0018]] is the Qiánlóng court's official synthesis. *Zhōulǐ yíyì jǔyào* 周禮疑義舉要 [[KR1d0024]] of Jiāng Yǒng 江永 is the most acute mid-Qīng evidential treatment of textual cruces.

**The *Yílǐ* cluster ([[KR1d0025]]–0051).** *Yílǐ* 儀禮 [[KR1d0025]], the canonical text in 17 *piān*, is the principal pre-Hàn source for actual ceremonial procedure (capping, marriage, archery, sacrifice, mourning); under the original short title *Lǐ* 禮 it was the only one of the three classics included in the Hàn *Wǔ jīng* (Wilkinson §28.4.3). *Yílǐ zhùshū* 儀禮注疏 [[KR1d0028]] is the parallel Zhèng-Jiǎ-Lù layered edition. *Yílǐ jíshuō* 儀禮集說 [[KR1d0035]] of Áo Jìgōng 敖繼公 is the most influential Yuán revisionist re-reading, displacing portions of the Zhèng commentary. *Yílǐ Zhèngzhù jùdòu* 儀禮鄭註句讀 [[KR1d0038]] of Zhāng Ěrqí 張爾岐 reopened the *Yílǐ* for early-Qīng study after generations of neglect, with Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武's high praise. *Qīndìng Yílǐ yìshū* 欽定儀禮義疏 [[KR1d0037]] and *Yílǐ jíbiān* 儀禮集編 [[KR1d0049]] of Shèng Shìzuǒ 盛世佐 are the great mid-Qīng synthetic editions. *Dúlǐ tōngkǎo* 讀禮通考 [[KR1d0051]] of Xú Qiánxué 徐乾學, a 120-juan encyclopedia of mourning ritual (*xiōnglǐ* 凶禮) organised around the *Yílǐ Sāngfú* 喪服 chapter, is the principal Qīng-period work on the largest specialised ritual category and the seed from which Qín Huìtián's later *Wǔlǐ tōngkǎo* grew.

**The *Lǐjì* cluster ([[KR1d0052]]–0075).** *Lǐjì* 禮記 [[KR1d0052]], the Xiǎo Dài 小戴 49-chapter recension, is one of the *Wǔ jīng* and the largest of the three ritual classics. *Lǐjì zhùshū* 禮記注疏 [[KR1d0053]] is the Zhèng-Kǒng-Lù layered edition; *Lǐjì zhèngyì cán* 禮記正義殘 [[KR1d0061]] preserves a fragmentary witness to the early state of Kǒng Yǐngdá's sub-commentary. *Lǐjì jíshuō* 禮記集說 [[KR1d0057]] of Wèi Shí 衞湜 in 160 juan is the largest Sòng-period *Lǐjì* compendium and an indispensable witness to lost Sòng commentary. *Lǐjì jíshuō* 禮記集說 [[KR1d0059]] of Chén Hào 陳澔 (1322) is the work that displaced all others: it became the official examination textbook on the *Lǐjì* from the Yuán to the end of the Qīng. *Lǐjì dàquán* 禮記大全 [[KR1d0060]] is the Yǒnglè-era Hú Guǎng 胡廣 compilation, one of the three *Dàquán* shoring up the Sòng-Yuán Chéng-Zhū orthodoxy. The five thematic monographs of Huáng Dàozhōu 黃道周 — *Yuèlìng míngyì* [[KR1d0062]], *Biǎojì jízhuàn* [[KR1d0063]], *Fāngjì jízhuàn* [[KR1d0064]], *Zīyī jízhuàn* [[KR1d0065]], *Rúxíng jízhuàn* [[KR1d0066]] — form a self-contained late-Míng project on individual *Lǐjì* chapters. *Qīndìng Lǐjì yìshū* 欽定禮記義疏 [[KR1d0068]] completes the imperial *Sānlǐ* trilogy.

**The *Dà-Dài* and *Sānlǐ* studies cluster ([[KR1d0076]]–0083).** *Dà-Dài Lǐjì* 大戴禮記 [[KR1d0076]] of Dài Dé 戴德, the parallel ritual-records anthology that survives only in fragments (39 *piān* out of an original 85), is the indispensable secondary witness to Western Hàn ritual literature. *Xià xiǎo zhèng Dàishì zhuàn* 夏小正戴氏傳 [[KR1d0077]] of Fù Sōngqīng 傅崧卿 isolates the *Xià xiǎo zhèng* (the earliest agrarian calendar) embedded in the *Dà-Dài*. *Sānlǐ tú jízhù* 三禮圖集注 [[KR1d0078]] of Niè Chóngyì 聶崇義 (Northern Sòng) is the standard illustrated companion to the three classics. *Xuélǐ zhíyí* 學禮質疑 [[KR1d0080]] of Wàn Sīdà 萬斯大 inaugurates the Qīng evidential critique of received ritual interpretation.

**The terminal Sānlǐ-compendium and family-ritual cluster ([[KR1d0084]]–0092).** Three Sòng-Qīng "great syntheses" frame the division: *Lǐshū* 禮書 [[KR1d0084]] of Chén Xiángdào 陳祥道 (Sòng, 150 juan); *Yílǐ jīngzhuàn tōngjiě* 儀禮經傳通解 [[KR1d0085]] of Zhū Xī 朱熹 and his disciples Huáng Gàn 黃榦 and Yáng Fù 楊復 (66-juan combined recension, organised topically — the most ambitious *Sānlǐ* re-edition of pre-modern China); and *Wǔlǐ tōngkǎo* 五禮通考 [[KR1d0087]] of Qín Huìtián 秦蕙田 (Qīng, 262 juan) — the largest single ritual compendium ever produced, covering all five *wǔlǐ* 五禮 categories. *Lǐshū gāngmù* 禮書綱目 [[KR1d0086]] of Jiāng Yǒng is its mid-Qīng companion. The family-ritual sub-cluster — *Shūyí* 書儀 [[KR1d0088]] of Sīmǎ Guāng 司馬光, *Jiālǐ* 家禮 [[KR1d0089]] of Zhū Xī (massively influential throughout East Asia, including Korea and Japan; Wilkinson §7.8.1), *Tàiquán xiānglǐ* [[KR1d0090]], *Zhūzǐ lǐzuǎn* [[KR1d0091]], and *Biàndìng jìlǐ tōngsú pǔ* [[KR1d0092]] — extends the canonical ritual tradition into the lived domain of the literati household.

## Important persons

- [[鄭玄]] (127–200) — Eastern-Hàn polymath, author of the *Sānlǐ zhù* 三禮注 (the annotations preserved in [[KR1d0002]], [[KR1d0026]], the underlying layer of [[KR1d0003]], [[KR1d0028]], [[KR1d0053]]). Zhèng's unifying annotation is what made the three texts a *Sānlǐ* corpus.
- [[賈公彥]] (fl. 650–655) — Táng court sub-commentator on the *Zhōulǐ* [[KR1d0003]] and *Yílǐ* [[KR1d0027]], [[KR1d0028]].
- [[孔穎達]] (574–648) — Táng director of the *Wǔ jīng zhèngyì* project; lead sub-commentator on the *Lǐjì* [[KR1d0053]], [[KR1d0061]].
- [[陸德明]] (ca. 550–630) — author of the *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文, the *yīnyì* layer integrated into all three Táng *zhùshū* editions.
- [[王安石]] (1021–1086) — Northern-Sòng chief councillor; his *Zhōuguān xīnyì* [[KR1d0004]] is the *locus classicus* of using the *Zhōulǐ* as constitutional warrant for state reform.
- [[朱熹]] (1130–1200) — central architect of Sòng-Yuán Neo-Confucian ritual scholarship; principal author of the *Yílǐ jīngzhuàn tōngjiě* [[KR1d0085]] and the *Jiālǐ* [[KR1d0089]] (the latter as received, with the Sòng attribution disputed since the late imperial period).
- [[黃榦]] (1152–1221) and [[楊復]] (fl. 1228) — Zhū Xī's principal disciples; completed his *Yílǐ jīngzhuàn tōngjiě* in the *sāng* and *jì* sections. Yáng Fù is also the author of the standard Sòng *Yílǐ tú* 儀禮圖 [[KR1d0032]].
- [[衞湜]] (Sòng) — compiler of the 160-juan *Lǐjì jíshuō* [[KR1d0057]], the central late-Sòng *Lǐjì* compendium.
- [[陳澔]] (1261–1341) — author of the *Lǐjì jíshuō* [[KR1d0059]] that served as the official examination textbook on the *Lǐjì* for nearly six centuries.
- [[吳澄]] (1249–1331) — Yuán philologist; produced both an *Yílǐ yìjīng* recovery effort [[KR1d0034]] and the influential rearranged *Lǐjì zuǎnyán* [[KR1d0058]].
- [[敖繼公]] (Yuán) — *Yílǐ jíshuō* [[KR1d0035]], the most influential Yuán Yílǐ commentary, willing to overturn Zhèng Xuán.
- [[魏了翁]] (1178–1237) — Sòng condenser; *Yílǐ yàoyì* [[KR1d0033]] and *Lǐjì yàoyì* [[KR1d0055]] are the standard medium-length digests of the Zhèng Jiǎ/Zhèng-Kǒng *zhùshū*.
- [[戴德]] (Western Hàn) — compiler of the *Dà-Dài Lǐjì* [[KR1d0076]] and uncle of Dài Shèng (the compiler of the Xiǎo Dài *Lǐjì*).
- [[聶崇義]] (10th c.) — author of the standard *Sānlǐ tú* iconography [[KR1d0078]] under the early Northern Sòng.
- [[陳祥道]] (1053–1093) — *Lǐshū* [[KR1d0084]], the first comprehensive Sòng *Sānlǐ* encyclopedia.
- [[黃道周]] (1585–1646) — late-Míng ritual specialist; the five-work *Lǐjì*-chapter series [[KR1d0062]]–[[KR1d0066]] is the most coherent Míng-period topical *Lǐjì* project.
- [[胡廣]] (1370–1418) — Yǒnglè court compiler of the *Lǐjì dàquán* [[KR1d0060]], one of the three *Dàquán* anchoring early-Míng orthodoxy.
- [[司馬光]] (1019–1086) — *Shūyí* [[KR1d0088]], a foundational Sòng family-ritual manual that prefigured Zhū Xī's *Jiālǐ*.
- [[江永]] (1681–1762) — the principal Huīzhōu evidential scholar of ritual; contributed across the *Zhōulǐ* [[KR1d0024]], *Yílǐ* [[KR1d0047]], *Lǐjì* [[KR1d0074]], [[KR1d0075]], and the comprehensive *Lǐshū gāngmù* [[KR1d0086]].
- [[方苞]] (1668–1749) — Tóng-chéng-school stylist and court ritualist; produced parallel commentaries across all three classics ([[KR1d0021]], [[KR1d0041]], [[KR1d0072]]).
- [[李光坡]] (1651–1723) — another all-Sānlǐ commentator ([[KR1d0019]], [[KR1d0040]], [[KR1d0071]]), brother of Lǐ Guāngdì.
- [[沈彤]] (1688–1752) — Suzhou evidential scholar; *Zhōuguān lùtián kǎo* [[KR1d0023]] and *Yílǐ xiǎoshū* [[KR1d0048]] are model Qīng monographic studies.
- [[張爾岐]] (1612–1678) — early-Qīng *Yílǐ* reviver; *Yílǐ Zhèngzhù jùdòu* [[KR1d0038]] reopened the previously-neglected *Yílǐ* for serious study.
- [[萬斯大]] (1633–1683) — *Yílǐ shāng* [[KR1d0039]] and *Xuélǐ zhíyí* [[KR1d0080]]; an early-Qīng polemical reviser of received ritual interpretation, with the Zhèjiāng-eastern school's *yìlǐ* 義理 sensibility.
- [[徐乾學]] (1631–1694) — *Dúlǐ tōngkǎo* [[KR1d0051]]; the great late-Kāng-xī mourning-ritual encyclopedist, supervisor of an editorial circle that included Wàn Sīdà and Yán Ruòjù.
- [[秦蕙田]] (1702–1764) — *Wǔlǐ tōngkǎo* [[KR1d0087]]; the great mid-Qián-lóng evidential synthesist of the entire ritual tradition.
- [[高宗弘曆]] (1711–1799) — Qiánlóng emperor; nominal compiler of the three *Qīndìng yìshū* [[KR1d0018]], [[KR1d0037]], [[KR1d0068]], the imperial-court synthesis on each of the three classics.

## Topics

- **The formation of the *Sānlǐ* corpus.** The three classics were welded together by Zhèng Xuán's late-Eastern-Hàn unified annotation, before which the canonical *Lǐ* was the *Yílǐ* alone (under the Hàn name *Shìlǐ* 士禮; Wilkinson §28.4.3). The cluster organisation of KR1d — first *Zhōulǐ*, then *Yílǐ*, then *Lǐjì*, finally synthesising compendia — reproduces Zhèng's hierarchy, not the historical order of the texts.
- **Ancient Script vs. Current Script in ritual scholarship.** The *Zhōulǐ* is the principal *gǔwén* 古文 classic in the Old-Text / New-Text controversy (Wilkinson §28.7.1), surfacing in the Western Hàn under Liú Xīn's sponsorship and never accepted by Current-Script court orthodoxy until the post-Hàn period. Kāng Yǒuwéi's late-Qīng claim that the *Zhōulǐ* was a Liú Xīn forgery is the modern terminus of this debate, rejected by mainstream twentieth-century scholarship but still informing readings of the text.
- **The political *Zhōulǐ*.** From Wáng Mǎng 王莽 (whose institutional programme was *Zhōulǐ*-based) through Wáng Ānshí [[KR1d0004]] to the Tàipíng Tiānguó, the *Zhōulǐ* repeatedly functioned as a reform constitution. The Sòng-Qīng *Zhōulǐ* commentaries in this division — particularly [[KR1d0004]], [[KR1d0005]], [[KR1d0009]], [[KR1d0010]] — should be read against this political-canonical backdrop (Elman and Kern, eds., *Statecraft and Classical Learning*, 2010; Wilkinson §58.6.3.1).
- **Procedure (*Yílǐ*) versus essay (*Lǐjì*) — and the imperial preference.** The *Yílǐ* presents itself as a sequence of step-by-step procedural scripts; the *Lǐjì* as a discursive Confucian-school anthology. The fortunes of the two diverged sharply at Yuán-Míng: the *Lǐjì* became central to the examination curriculum via Chén Hào's [[KR1d0059]], while the *Yílǐ* sank into relative neglect until Zhāng Ěrqí [[KR1d0038]] revived it in the Kāngxī era.
- **Dài Dé vs. Dài Shèng.** The Western Hàn produced two parallel Confucian-ritual anthologies under the same family-name lineage — the *Dà-Dài Lǐjì* [[KR1d0076]] (Dài Dé, the elder) and the canonical *Xiǎo Dài Lǐjì* [[KR1d0052]] (Dài Shèng, the younger). The *Xiǎo Dài* version was canonised; the *Dà-Dài* version survives in fragmentary state. The relationship between the two is the principal philological problem of pre-canonical Hàn ritual literature.
- **The 五禮 (Wǔlǐ) typology.** From the post-Hàn ritual codes onward, the canonical typology *jí lǐ* 吉禮 / *xiōng lǐ* 凶禮 / *jūn lǐ* 軍禮 / *bīn lǐ* 賓禮 / *jiā lǐ* 嘉禮 organises imperial ritual programmes. The terminal compendia [[KR1d0086]], [[KR1d0087]] follow this typology; the mourning-only [[KR1d0051]] treats one of the five.
- **Family ritual and the literati household.** Sīmǎ Guāng's *Shūyí* [[KR1d0088]] and Zhū Xī's *Jiālǐ* [[KR1d0089]] founded a parallel tradition of household-scale ritual codification that became the de facto practical norm of literati life through the Yuán, Míng, and Qīng, and exported to Korean and Japanese Neo-Confucian households (Wilkinson §7.8.1).
- **The 月令 (Monthly Ordinances) sub-tradition.** The *Yuèlìng* chapter of the *Lǐjì*, with its cosmological correlation of months, colours, sounds, and ritual obligations, generated its own commentarial micro-tradition — *Yuèlìng jiě* [[KR1d0056]] (Zhāng Xí), *Yuèlìng míngyì* [[KR1d0062]] (Huáng Dàozhōu).
- **Qiánlóng imperial codification.** The three *Qīndìng yìshū* [[KR1d0018]], [[KR1d0037]], [[KR1d0068]], all dated to 1736–1748, are the high-water mark of imperial ritual-classics standardisation and the proximate model for Qín Huìtián's [[KR1d0087]] synthesis.

## Timeline

- c. 300 – 100 BCE — *Zhōulǐ* 周禮 [[KR1d0001]] — late Warring States / early Hàn composition of the received text, with the *Kǎogōngjì* substituted for the lost *Dōngguān*.
- c. 300 – 100 BCE — *Yílǐ* 儀禮 [[KR1d0025]] — pre-Hàn ritual-procedure corpus of unknown date; the only ritual classic in the original Hàn *Wǔ jīng*.
- c. 200 – 50 BCE — *Lǐjì* 禮記 [[KR1d0052]] — Western Hàn Xiǎo Dài 小戴 redaction by Dài Shèng of an older anthology of Confucian-school ritual essays.
- c. 50 BCE – 50 CE — *Dà-Dài Lǐjì* 大戴禮記 [[KR1d0076]] — Western Hàn parallel anthology compiled by Dài Dé; survives in 39 of the original 85 *piān*.
- 127 – 200 — *Sānlǐ zhù* (Zhèng Xuán's annotations) preserved in *Zhōulǐ* [[KR1d0002]] and *Yílǐ* [[KR1d0026]] — the unifying annotation that made the three texts a *Sānlǐ* corpus.
- 642 – 653 — *Lǐjì zhèngyì cán* [[KR1d0061]] and *Lǐjì zhùshū* [[KR1d0053]] — Kǒng Yǐngdá's Táng official sub-commentary on the *Lǐjì*.
- 650 – 655 — *Zhōulǐ zhùshū* [[KR1d0003]], *Yílǐ shū* [[KR1d0027]], *Yílǐ zhùshū* [[KR1d0028]] — Jiǎ Gōngyàn's Táng official sub-commentaries on the *Zhōulǐ* and *Yílǐ*.
- 956 – 962 — *Sānlǐ tú jízhù* 三禮圖集注 [[KR1d0078]] — Niè Chóngyì's early Northern-Sòng illustrated *Sānlǐ* iconography.
- 1071 – 1075 — *Zhōuguān xīnyì* 周官新義 [[KR1d0004]] — Wáng Ānshí's reformist reading written during his chief-councillorship.
- 1075 – 1086 — *Shūyí* 書儀 [[KR1d0088]] — Sīmǎ Guāng's foundational family-ritual manual.
- 1080 – 1093 — *Lǐshū* 禮書 [[KR1d0084]] — Chén Xiángdào's 150-juan Sòng *Sānlǐ* encyclopedia.
- 1100 – 1126 — *Zhōulǐ xiángjiě* [[KR1d0005]] — Wáng Zhāoyǔ's Northern-Sòng successor to Wáng Ānshí's reading.
- 1110 – 1130 — *Xià xiǎo zhèng Dàishì zhuàn* [[KR1d0077]] — Fù Sōngqīng's recovery of the *Xià xiǎo zhèng* embedded in the *Dà-Dài*.
- 1170 – 1200 — *Jiālǐ* 家禮 [[KR1d0089]] — Zhū Xī's family-ritual manual (with attribution disputed in late imperial scholarship).
- 1172 – 1175 — *Yílǐ shíwù* [[KR1d0029]] — Zhāng Chún's pioneering Sòng *Yílǐ* textual criticism.
- 1172 – 1200 — *Zhōulǐ fùgǔ biān* [[KR1d0006]] — Yú Tíngchūn's "restore-the-ancient" *Zhōulǐ* recension.
- 1180 – 1240 — *Lǐjīng huìyuán* [[KR1d0007]] — Yè Shí's mid-Sòng *Zhōulǐ* anthology.
- 1190 – 1227 — *Yílǐ jīngzhuàn tōngjiě* 儀禮經傳通解 [[KR1d0085]] — Zhū Xī's most ambitious *Sānlǐ* project, completed by Huáng Gàn and Yáng Fù.
- 1190 – 1230 — *Zhōuguān zǒngyì* [[KR1d0009]] — Yì Fú's southern-Sòng exposition.
