# Sāngfú yàojí 喪服要集

*Essential Compilation on Mourning Dress*

by [[杜預]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[杜預]] Dù Yù's (222–285) lost *Sāngfú yàojí* 喪服要集. Dù Yù — the Western-Jìn general, *Zhèng-nán dàjiāngjūn* 鎮南大將軍, conqueror of Wú in 280, and the principal author of the standard *Chūnqiū Zuǒ-shì jīng-zhuàn jíjiě* 春秋左氏經傳集解 (cf. [[KR1m0010]]) — is principally a *Chūnqiū* scholar but also a major Sānlǐ ritualist. His *Sāngfú yàojí* is the principal Western-Jìn *Sāngfú* compendium, preserved in *Tōngdiǎn* citations and in the citation-chain through [[賀循]]'s [[KR1d0118]] *Hè-shì sāngfú yàojì*. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1082) is drawn from these two principal source-chains.

### Abstract

The text opens with a *zōng-pǔ* 宗譜 (clan-genealogy) section establishing the canonical doctrine that the *shǐ-fēng zhī jūn* (founding prince) names one *bié-zǐ* (collateral son) as *zǔ* (lineage-founder), with the *tài-zǔ* (great ancestor) thereafter receiving permanent shrine-worship.

The substantive content covers:

(i) **Lineage-founder and shrine-permanence rules.** The canonical *Lǐjì-Dà-zhuàn* doctrine of the *bié-zǐ wéi zǔ* — the foundational principle of the *zōng-fǎ* (lineage-law) system.

(ii) **Cross-generational collateral mourning.** Specific rulings on the *bó-shū-fù* / *cóng-mǔ* mourning-grades preserved in Dù Yòu's *Tōngdiǎn* 73.

(iii) **Cross-reference to [[賀循]]'s *Sāngfú yàojì*.** The CHANT note "*Dù Yòu Tōngdiǎn juàn 73 Chuán Chún wèn Hè Xún Yàojì yúnyún, yǐn Dù-shì, xún qí wén-yì*" (cited as: *Tōngdiǎn 73* records Chuán Chún 傳純 questioning Hè Xún on his *Yàojì*, where Hè Xún cites Dù [Yù] and follows his sense) preserves an important transmission-link: [[賀循]] in the early Eastern-Jìn period cited Dù Yù's *Sāngfú yàojí* as his authoritative base-text, then was in turn questioned on his interpretation by Chuán Chún.

The dating bracket (222–285) reflects Dù Yù's documented lifespan in *Jìn shū* 34. The *Sāngfú yàojí* is conventionally placed in his post-280 retirement period at Xiāngyáng, contemporaneous with the completion of his *Chūnqiū Zuǒ-shì jīng-zhuàn jíjiě*.

## Translations and research

*No substantial Western-language secondary literature located* on the *Sāngfú yàojí* as such. Dù Yù's broader scholarship is treated in:
- Anne Cheng, *Étude sur le confucianisme Han* (Paris, 1985) chapter on the late-Hàn / early-Jìn Confucian transition.

## Other points of interest

The Dù Yù → [[賀循]] → Chuán Chún citation-chain preserved in *Tōngdiǎn* 73 is one of the most important documented Western-to-Eastern-Jìn ritual-scholarship transmission lines, establishing Dù Yù's *Sāngfú yàojí* as a key intermediary between Wèi-Jìn Wáng Sù-school *Sāngfú* scholarship and the early Eastern-Jìn court-ritual programme.
