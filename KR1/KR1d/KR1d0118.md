# Hè-shì sāngfú yàojì 賀氏喪服要記

*Master Hè's Essential Notes on Mourning Dress*

by [[賀循]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[賀循]] Hè Xún's (260–319) lost *Sāngfú yàojì* 喪服要記 — the question-and-answer ruling-compendium that pairs with his [[KR1d0117]] *Sāngfú pǔ* (genealogical-chart commentary) to form the two-part Eastern-Jìn court-authoritative *Sāngfú* reference. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1086) is drawn from Dù Yòu 杜佑's *Tōngdiǎn* 通典 citations.

### Abstract

The opening preserved fragment addresses the canonical *zhàng-zhāng* (mourning-staff section) rule: "*chū jū lú, lùn chēng zhàng zhě bì lú, lú zhě bì tán*" 出居廬，論稱杖者必廬，廬者必禫 — i.e. those bearing the mourning-staff must reside in the *lú* (mourning-hut), and *lú*-residents must perform the *tán* (removal-sacrifice). Hè Xún's gloss: "this clarifies that within the *zhàng-zhāng*, *lú*-residence must culminate in *tán* — *lú* and *tán* are inseparable as the *zhàng-zhāng* internal sequence."

The substantive content addresses the practical-ritual implementation of the canonical *Sāngfú* schedule:

(i) **The *zhàng* / *lú* / *tán* sequence.** Detailed protocols for the three principal external markers of the deepest mourning-grade (staff, hut, removal-sacrifice), with their canonical inter-relations.

(ii) **The *liàn* (practice-sacrifice) and *xiáng* (felicitous-sacrifice) transitions.** Specific rulings on which ritual events trigger which mourning-dress changes.

(iii) **Citation-of-and-response-to [[杜預]]'s *Sāngfú yàojí*.** Throughout the surviving text, Hè Xún cites Dù Yù's earlier [[KR1d0115]] *Sāngfú yàojí* as an authoritative reference, then either follows or modifies. The interlocutor *Chuán Chún* 傳純 questions Hè Xún on these citations (cf. *Tōngdiǎn* 73 citation in [[KR1d0115]]) — a documented Eastern-Jìn-to-Western-Jìn citation chain.

(iv) **Court-specific *biàn-lǐ* cases.** Particular rulings for the Sīmǎ imperial-clan mourning protocols established under Jìn Yuándì.

The dating bracket (280–319) reflects Hè Xún's documented life-span; the work is conventionally placed in his Eastern-Jìn *Tài-cháng* period.

## Other points of interest

The *Sāngfú yàojì* and *Sāngfú pǔ* together formed the principal Eastern-Jìn court-authoritative *Sāngfú* reference and were the most-cited single-author *Sāngfú* compilation in the *Tōngdiǎn*. Their preservation of the [[杜預]] → [[賀循]] → Chuán Chún citation chain is the key documentation for the Western-to-Eastern-Jìn ritual-scholarship transmission line.
