# Hè-shì sāngfú pǔ 賀氏喪服譜

*Master Hè's Genealogical Chart of Mourning Dress*

by [[賀循]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juàn reconstruction of [[賀循]] Hè Xún's (260–319) lost *Sāngfú pǔ* 喪服譜 — his principal *Sāngfú* (mourning-dress) genealogical-chart commentary on the canonical *Yílǐ-Sāngfú* and *Sāngfú-zhuàn*. Hè Xún was the chief southern-school ritualist of the early Eastern Jìn court and the principal advisor to Jìn Yuándì on *Sānlǐ* matters; his ritual scholarship is the most substantial of the early-fourth-century court tradition. The CHANT reconstruction (CH2e1085) is drawn from Dù Yòu 杜佑's *Tōngdiǎn* 通典 citations.

### Abstract

The text opens with a *zōng-yì* 宗義 (lineage-doctrine) section establishing the canonical *zōng-fǎ* (lineage-law) framework: the *bié-zǐ* (collateral son) of an ancient *zhū-hóu* and the founding *dàfū* (great officer) of a state each became a *tài-zǔ* (great ancestor) with permanent shrine-worship. This sets the doctrinal foundation for the subsequent chart of mourning-grades through cross-generational and collateral kinship relations.

The substantive content covers the full *Sāngfú zhuàn* schedule:

(i) **The five-grade mourning-dress system** with detailed specifications for *zhǎn-cuī* (full mourning, three years), *zī-cuī* (one-year mourning), *dà-gōng* (nine-month), *xiǎo-gōng* (five-month), and *sī-má* (three-month) protocols.

(ii) **Cross-generational mourning protocols** for the *cóng-fù* (father's collateral siblings), *cóng-zǔ* (grandfather's collateral siblings), and their corresponding *cóng-mǔ* female relations.

(iii) **The post-marriage downgrading protocols** for the bride's natal kin and the bride's mourning for the husband's kin.

(iv) **The *bié-zǐ wéi zǔ* (collateral-son-as-founder) doctrine** and its implications for the *dà-zōng* / *xiǎo-zōng* succession.

The dating bracket (280–319) reflects Hè Xún's documented life-span in *Jìn shū* 68; the work is conventionally placed in his Eastern-Jìn *Tài-cháng* period (after 317), where it served as the court's authoritative *Sāngfú* reference.

## Translations and research

*No substantial Western-language secondary literature located* on the *Sāngfú pǔ* as such. Hè Xún's ritual scholarship is treated in:
- 楊志剛 Yáng Zhìgāng, *Sānlǐ xué shǐ* 三禮學史, chapters on Eastern-Jìn court ritualism.

## Other points of interest

The pairing of [[賀循]]'s *Sāngfú pǔ* (this work) and [[KR1d0118]] *Sāngfú yàojì* — a genealogical-chart commentary plus a question-and-answer ruling-compendium — became the standard two-part format for *Sāngfú* scholarship in the Six-Dynasties period, repeatedly imitated by later authors.
