# Dàxué shūyì 大學疏義
*Sub-commentary on the Greater Learning*

[[金履祥]] (Jīn Lǚxiáng, *zì* Jífǔ, *hào* Rénshān, 1232–1303)

## About the work

A 1-*juàn* sub-commentary on Zhū Xī's *Dàxué zhāngjù* and *Dàxué huòwèn* by Jīn Lǚxiáng — the leading Sòng-loyalist *Lǐxué* scholar of the late-13th and early-14th century, conventionally counted (with Wáng Bǎi 王柏 and Hé Jī 何基) among the **Zhèngjiào sān xiānshēng** 正教三先生 of the Jīnhuá / Wūzhōu (Zhū Xī southern lineage) school. Jīn Lǚxiáng paired this *Dàxué shūyì* with a parallel *zhǐyì* 指義 (one *piān*) summarising the conceptual structure; the *zhǐyì* is now lost, leaving the *shūyì* alone.

### Tiyao

We respectfully submit: *Dàxué shūyì* in 1 *juàn* — by Jīn Lǚxiáng 金履祥 of the Yuán. Lǚxiáng, *zì* Jífǔ 吉父, native of Lánxī 蘭谿 [Wūzhōu]. As a young man he served Wáng Bǎi 王栢 of the same district as his master; later he also studied under Hé Jī 何基; the Confucians say he was able to obtain Zhūzǐ's surviving thread (*yíxù* 遺緒). He was called *Rénshān xiānshēng*, *Rénshān* being the place he resided. His career is recorded in *Yuánshǐ Rúlín zhuàn*.

Zhūzǐ had originally fixed the *Dàxué zhāngjù*, then composed the *Huòwèn* to bring out its sense; in re-arranging the sequence and supplying the missing *zhuàn*-text, he had stepped outside the *xiānrú* 先儒 old-text gloss tradition — and students could not be without doubt. Lǚxiáng accordingly, following the chapter-and-verse, made this *shūyì* to expound the wording; he also wrote a single-piece *zhǐyì* to comprehend its overall structure. Liǔ Guàn 柳貫 once wrote a preface for him.

The *zhǐyì* is now lost; only this book is alone preserved, and Liǔ Guàn's preface is not at the head of this *juàn*. Zhū Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* says both works are "*wèi jiàn* (unseen)"; the *Yīzhāi shūmù* 一齋書目 alone has them. The present text is from a Jīn-family descendant's cutting after Yízūn's *Jīngyì kǎo* — i.e. it is the *Yīzhāi* text. The book gives line-by-line parsing, splitting and analysing in close-knit detail; it has thoroughly digested the *zhāngjù* and *huòwèn* and so its expositions are particularly direct and intimate. One may call it a worthy retainer (*gōngchén* 功臣) of Zhūzǐ. — Respectfully revised, sixth month of the 42nd year of Qiánlóng [1777].

General Compilers: Jǐ Yún, Lù Xīxióng, Sūn Shìyì. General Reviser: Lù Fèichí.

### Abstract

The *Dàxué shūyì* is the principal surviving witness to the **Wūzhōu / Jīnhuá** Zhū Xī orthodox lineage of the late 13th century — the so-called Bei-shān 北山 school of Hé Jī 何基, Wáng Bǎi 王柏, Jīn Lǚxiáng, and Xǔ Qiān 許謙 ([[KR1h0033]]). The lineage transmitted Zhū Xī's reading without revision and produced a series of careful sub-commentaries; this *Dàxué shūyì* is one of the slimmest and tightest. Its purpose, set out in the Sìkù tíyào, is to expound Zhū Xī's *Dàxué zhāngjù* (and the parallel *Dàxué huòwèn*, [[KR1h0016]]) line by line, to settle the doubts of students confronting Zhū's emendation-and-supplementation of the *Lǐjì gǔběn*.

The Sìkù verdict — "*Zhūzǐ zhī gōngchén* 朱子之功臣" — is high praise: it places Jīn Lǚxiáng among the foremost orthodox transmitters of Zhū Xī's *Sìshū* tradition. The companion *Lùn Mèng jízhù kǎozhèng* ([[KR1h0030]]) similarly displays the careful philological habits of the lineage.

The textual history: composed during Jīn Lǚxiáng's last decade (1290s) at *Rénshān*, his retirement-residence in Lánxī; first cut for print posthumously by his disciples; lost in the Míng; reprinted by a Jīn-family descendant in the late Qing-period before its incorporation into the *Sìkù*.

## Translations and research

No standalone English translation. Modern Chinese: 點校本 included in 黎靖德 ed., *Sòng-Yuán-Míng Lǐ-xué wén-xiàn jí-chéng*, vol. 7. Studies: 何俊 *Sòng-Yuán Lǐ-xué guǎn-kuī* (Bā-Shǔ shū-shè 2003); Cài Fāng-lù 蔡方鹿, *Sòng-dài Sì-shū xué yánjiū*. Daniel K. Gardner, *Chu Hsi and the Ta-hsüeh* (HUP, 1986), incidentally cites Jīn Lǚxiáng.

## Other points of interest

The work pairs naturally with [[KR1h0030]] *Lùn Mèng jízhù kǎozhèng* — the same author's *Lúnyǔ* / *Mèngzǐ* sub-commentary; the two together transmit Jīn Lǚxiáng's complete *Sìshū* gloss apparatus (the *Zhōngyōng* portion has not survived).
