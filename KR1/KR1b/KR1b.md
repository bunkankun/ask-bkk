# KR1b 書類 *Shū lèi* — Classics → Book of Documents

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR1b 書類 collects 60 works of *Shàngshū* 尚書 (also *Shū jīng* 書經) scholarship — the canonical anthology of speeches, decrees, and counsels traditionally ascribed to the Yú, Xià, Shāng, and Zhōu dynasties, together with the layered tradition of commentary, sub-commentary, paraphrase, and philological audit that grew up around it from the Hàn through the high Qīng. Within the Sìkù quánshū macro-arrangement the division stands second inside `KR1` 經部 (Classics), immediately after `KR1a` 易類 and reflecting the *Shū*'s position as second of the Five Classics. Wilkinson notes that the oldest parts of the *Shū* date from the tenth century BCE, that the received text as we now have it was assembled around 317 CE and entered the Kāichéng 開成 stone Classics in 837, and that the once-standard contrast between an "Ancient-script" (*gǔwén* 古文) and a "Modern-script" (*jīnwén* 今文) recension is itself a misunderstanding that recent scholarship has worked to dispel (Wilkinson §58.6.3.1).

The chronological span of the collected works runs from the Hàn *Shàngshū dà zhuàn* 尚書大傳 attributed to Fú Shēng 伏勝 ([[KR1b0059]]), through the imperially-canonical Táng *Shàngshū zhèngyì* 尚書正義 of Kǒng Yǐngdá 孔穎達 ([[KR1b0003]] / [[KR1b0004]]), the Northern-Sòng cosmological reading of the *Hóngfàn* 洪範 chapter inaugurated by Hú Yuán 胡瑗 ([[KR1b0005]]), Cài Shěn's 蔡沈 epoch-defining *Shū jīng jízhuàn* 書經集傳 of 1209 ([[KR1b0017]]) — the standard examination commentary for the next six centuries — the Yuán Cài-Shěn-centric subcommentaries, the Míng imperial *Shū jīng dàquán* 書經大全 of 1415 ([[KR1b0037]]), and the Qīng evidential demolition of the 25 "Old-Text" chapters by Yán Ruòqú 閻若璩 in his *Shàngshū gǔwén shū zhèng* 尚書古文疏證 ([[KR1b0048]]). By count the Sòng (≈ 21 works) and Qīng (≈ 14) layers dominate, with smaller but pivotal Hàn, Táng, Yuán, and Míng strata. The division thus tracks two intertwined trajectories: a long *yìlǐ* 義理 reading tradition culminating in Cài Shěn and the Míng *Dàquán*, and a textual-philological tradition that, beginning quietly with Méi Zhuó 梅鷟 in the early sixteenth century and erupting into method with Yán Ruòqú a hundred and fifty years later, dismantled half of the received classic.

## Important texts and text clusters

**Canonical text and Hàn layer.**
- *Shàngshū (zhèngwén)* 尚書(正文) [[KR1b0001]] — the canonical text itself, the starting point for every commentary in the division.
- *Shàngshū dà zhuàn* 尚書大傳 [[KR1b0059]] — attributed to Fú Shēng 伏勝, with Zhèng Xuán's 鄭玄 annotations and a Qīng-era reconstruction by Sūn Zhīlù 孫之騄; the principal Hàn-era hermeneutic work transmitted under the *Shū*.
- *Shàngshū* (Sòng-printed Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn*) [[KR1b0002]] — the received recension of the *Kǒng zhuàn* 孔傳 ascribed to Kǒng Ān'guó 孔安國 with the Táng *yīnyì* 音義 of Lù Démíng 陸德明; the textual base that Yán Ruòqú would later impeach.

**Táng official subcommentary.**
- *Shàngshū zhèngyì* 尚書正義 [[KR1b0003]] — Kǒng Yǐngdá's 孔穎達 imperially-commissioned subcommentary on the Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn*, completed during the Zhēnguān-Yǒnghuī years (642–653); part of the *Wǔjīng zhèngyì* synthesis and the textbook *Shū* of the next four centuries.
- *Shàngshū zhùshū* 尚書注疏 [[KR1b0004]] — the integrated edition combining the Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn*, Kǒng Yǐngdá's *zhèngyì*, Lù Démíng's *yīnyì*, and the Qīng evidential *kǎozhèng* of Qí Zhàonán 齊召南 and Chén Hào 陳浩 added on the imperial *Shíjīng* commission; effectively the layered scholarly *Shū* in its received Sìkù form.

**Northern Sòng — *Hóngfàn* cosmology and the rise of philosophical reading.**
- *Hóngfàn kǒuyì* 洪範口義 [[KR1b0005]] — Hú Yuán's 胡瑗 lecture-style reading of the single chapter that, more than any other, would license Sòng cosmological speculation about the Five Phases and Nine Categories.
- *Shū zhuàn* 書傳 [[KR1b0006]] — Sū Shì's 蘇軾 *Dōngpō Shū zhuàn*, the most distinguished northern-Sòng literary-philosophical commentary, paired in tone with his *Yìzhuàn* in [[KR1a0015]].

**Southern Sòng — the *Yǔgòng* sub-tradition and the road to Cài Shěn.**
- *Shàngshū quánjiě* 尚書全解 [[KR1b0007]] — Lín Zhīqí's 林之奇 capacious commentary, transmitted in revised form and repeatedly cited by Cài Shěn.
- *Yǔgòng zhǐnán* 禹貢指南 [[KR1b0008]], *Yǔgòng lùn* 禹貢論 [[KR1b0009]], *Yǔgòng shuō duàn* 禹貢說斷 [[KR1b0012]] — Máo Huàng 毛晃, Chéng Dàchāng 程大昌, and Fù Yín 傅寅 founding the historical-geographical study of the *Yǔgòng* 禹貢 chapter as a free-standing sub-genre.
- *Shàngshū jiǎngyì* 尚書講義 [[KR1b0010]], *Shàngshū xiángjiě* 尚書詳解 [[KR1b0011]] — Shǐ Hào 史浩 and Xià Sǒng 夏僎: representative high-Sòng reading commentaries.
- *Zēngxiū Dōnglái Shū shuō* 增修東萊書說 [[KR1b0013]] — Lǚ Zǔqiān's 呂祖謙 lecture commentary expanded by Shí Lán 時瀾, parallel to Lǚ's 古周易 textual reconstruction in [[KR1a0043]].
- *Shū jīng jízhuàn* 書經集傳 [[KR1b0017]] — Cài Shěn's 蔡沈 imperially-prescribed commentary, written 1199–1209 at Zhū Xī's 朱熹 deathbed commission; the single most consequential post-classical *Shū* work, governing the examination curriculum from the Yuán reform of 1313 through the late Qīng. The functional counterpart in this division to Zhū Xī's *Zhōuyì běnyì* in [[KR1a0031]].
- *Shàngshū jīngyì* 尚書精義 [[KR1b0018]], *Chén shì Shàngshū xiángjiě* 陳氏尚書詳解 [[KR1b0019]], *Róngtáng Shū jiě* 融堂書解 [[KR1b0020]] — Huáng Lún 黃倫, Chén Jīng 陳經, and Qián Shí 錢時: the dense Southern Sòng reading layer that surrounds and partly anticipates Cài Shěn.
- *Shàngshū yàoyì* 尚書要義 [[KR1b0022]] — Wèi Liǎowēng's 魏了翁 distillation, alongside his parallel *Yàoyì* compilations on the *Yì* and *Lǐ* classics.
- *Shū jízhuàn huòwèn* 書集傳或問 [[KR1b0023]] — Chén Dàyóu 陳大猷, the first sustained dialogic apparatus around Cài Shěn's *Jízhuàn*.
- *Shàngshū biǎo zhù* 尚書表注 [[KR1b0025]] — Jīn Lǚxiáng's 金履祥 annotated table-format reading; the late-Sòng / early-Yuán transition piece.

**Yuán — codification of Cài Shěn.**
- *Shū zuǎn yán* 書纂言 [[KR1b0026]] — Wú Chéng's 吳澄 paired companion to his *Yì zuǎn yán* in [[KR1a0071]].
- *Shū jízhuàn zuǎnshū* 書集傳纂疏 [[KR1b0027]] — Chén Lì's 陳櫟 systematic subcommentary on Cài Shěn, the model for the genre.
- *Dú shū cóngshuō* 讀書叢說 [[KR1b0028]] — Xǔ Qiān's 許謙 reading-notes commentary in the Jīn-Wáng (金華) Daoxue line.
- *Shū chuán jí lù zuǎn zhù* 書傳輯錄纂註 [[KR1b0029]], *Shū Cài shì zhuàn pángtōng* 書蔡氏傳旁通 [[KR1b0031]] — Dǒng Dǐng 董鼎 and Chén Shīkǎi 陳師凱: explicit Cài Shěn commentaries marketed for the new examination curriculum.
- *Shàngshū tōngkǎo* 尚書通考* [[KR1b0030]] — Huáng Zhènchéng 黃鎮成: a classified-topic compilation of *Shū* lore.
- *Dú shū guǎnjiàn* 讀書管見 [[KR1b0032]], *Shū yì jīnshì* 書義矜式 [[KR1b0060]] — Wáng Chōngyún's 王充耘 paired works, the latter explicitly an examination handbook.
- *Shū yì duàn fǎ* 書義斷法* [[KR1b0033]] — Chén Yuèdào 陳悅道, similarly examination-oriented.
- *Shàngshū zuǎn zhuàn* 尚書纂傳 [[KR1b0034]] — Wáng Tiānyǔ 王天與: the most ambitious Yuán synthetic commentary outside the Cài Shěn line.
- *Shàngshū jù jiě* 尚書句解 [[KR1b0035]] — Zhū Zǔyì 朱祖義: Yuán plain-style line-by-line gloss, designed for school use.

**Míng — official compendia and the first cracks in the *Gǔwén*.**
- *Shū chuán huìxuǎn* 書傳會選 [[KR1b0036]] — Liú Sānwú's 劉三吾 1394 imperially-commissioned compilation, a brief Hóngwǔ-era counterpart to the later *Dàquán*.
- *Shū jīng dàquán* 書經大全 [[KR1b0037]] — Hú Guǎng's 胡廣 1415 imperial compendium; canonical for the Míng-Qīng examination system, paired with [[KR1a0091]] and the parallel *Sìshū dàquán*.
- *Shàngshū kǎo yì* 尚書考異 [[KR1b0038]] — Méi Zhuó's 梅鷟 sixteenth-century textual audit; the first sustained scholarly suspicion that the *Gǔwén* chapters and the Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn* could not be authentic, anticipating Yán Ruòqú by a century and a half.
- *Shàngshū yíyì* 尚書疑義 [[KR1b0039]], *Shàngshū rìjì* 尚書日記 [[KR1b0040]], *Shàngshū biān Cài biān* 尚書砭蔡編 [[KR1b0041]], *Shàngshū zhù kǎo* 尚書注考 [[KR1b0042]], *Shàngshū shū yǎn* 尚書疏衍 [[KR1b0043]] — Mǎ Míngháng 馬明衡, Wáng Qiáo 王樵, Yuán Rén 袁仁, Chén Tàijiāo 陳泰交, and Chén Dì 陳第: a clutch of late-Míng works increasingly willing to take issue with Cài Shěn or to test the canonical text against epigraphic and phonological evidence — the Cài-shèn-critical strain among them anticipating the Qīng turn.
- *Hóngfàn míngyì* 洪範明義 [[KR1b0044]] — Huáng Dàozhōu's 黃道周 cosmological reading of the *Hóngfàn*, parallel to his late-Míng *Yì xiàng zhèng* in [[KR1a0110]] and written against the same backdrop of impending dynastic collapse.

**Qīng — imperial codification, and the demolition of the *Gǔwén*.**
- *Rì jiǎng Shū jīng jiě yì* 日講書經解義 [[KR1b0045]] — the Kāngxī imperial lecture-commentary completed by Kūlènà 庫勒納 and Yè Fāng'ài 葉方藹 in 1678–80, paralleling the *Yì* and *Shī* lecture-commentaries.
- *Qīndìng Shūjīng zhuànshuō huìzuǎn* 欽定書經傳說彙纂 [[KR1b0046]] — Wáng Xūlíng's 王頊齡 great Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng compilation, the *Shū* counterpart to [[KR1a0117]] *Yù zuàn Zhōuyì zhézhōng*.
- *Shàngshū bài shū* 尚書稗疏 [[KR1b0047]] — Wáng Fūzhī's 王夫之 austere *Shū* reading, pendant to his *Zhōuyì bài shū* in [[KR1a0120]].
- *Shàngshū gǔwén shū zhèng* 尚書古文疏證 [[KR1b0048]] — Yán Ruòqú's 閻若璩 evidential demolition of the 25 "Old-Text" chapters and the Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn*, in 128 numbered arguments; the founding monument of high-Qīng *kǎozhèng* method and arguably the single most consequential work of premodern Chinese philology. Decisively settled the question of the *Gǔwén Shàngshū* for all subsequent scholarship.
- *Gǔwén Shàngshū yuān cí* 古文尚書冤詞 [[KR1b0049]], *Shàngshū guǎngtīng lù* 尚書廣聽錄 [[KR1b0050]] — Máo Qílíng's 毛奇齡 vigorous and ultimately unsuccessful counter-attack defending the authenticity of the *Gǔwén*; despite its failure, a methodologically important rejoinder that forced Yán Ruòqú's followers to sharpen their case.
- *Shàngshū píchuán* 尚書埤傳 [[KR1b0051]], *Yǔgòng chángjiān* 禹貢長箋 [[KR1b0052]] — Zhū Hèlíng 朱鶴齡: a paired Cài Shěn supplement and *Yǔgòng* monograph from the early Qīng.
- *Yǔgòng zhuīzhǐ* 禹貢錐指 [[KR1b0053]] — Hú Wèi's 胡渭 systematic historical-geographical commentary on the *Yǔgòng*, the definitive monograph in the *Yǔgòng* sub-tradition; pendant to his demolition of the Sòng diagrams of the *Yì* in [[KR1a0138]].
- *Hóngfàn zhènglùn* 洪範正論 [[KR1b0054]] — Hú Wèi's parallel monograph on the *Hóngfàn*, similarly purgative of Sòng cosmological accretion.
- *Shàngshū qī piān jiěyì* 尚書七篇解義 [[KR1b0055]] — Lǐ Guāngdì's 李光地 selective commentary on seven core chapters; pendant to his role as director of [[KR1a0117]].
- *Shū jīng zhōnglùn* 書經衷論 [[KR1b0056]], *Shàngshū dìlǐ jīnshì* 尚書地理今釋 [[KR1b0057]], *Yǔgòng huì jiān* 禹貢會箋 [[KR1b0058]] — Zhāng Yīng 張英, Jiǎng Tíngxī 蔣廷錫, and Xú Wénjìng 徐文靖: the late-Kāngxī to mid-Qiánlóng layer, mostly historical-geographical or topical.

## Topics

The division's principal interpretive axes:

- **The 古文 / 今文 (*gǔwén* / *jīnwén*) controversy and the false Old Text.** The most consequential single problem in *Shū* studies. From the Eastern Jìn through the Yuán, the received *Shàngshū* circulated in 58 chapters comprising the 33 "Modern-script" chapters and 25 putatively "Old-Script" chapters with the *Kǒng zhuàn* — all four of which (chapters, recension, commentary, attribution) Yán Ruòqú demonstrated in [[KR1b0048]] to be an Eastern-Jìn fabrication. Méi Zhuó in [[KR1b0038]] anticipated the case by a century and a half; Máo Qílíng in [[KR1b0049]] [[KR1b0050]] mounted the principal counter-attack; Yán Ruòqú prevailed. Wilkinson now warns that the very *gǔwén / jīnwén* labels are themselves a misunderstanding, but the Qīng victory over the false Old Text remains the canonical philological success story of the division.

- **The Cài Shěn settlement.** Cài Shěn's *Shū jīng jízhuàn* [[KR1b0017]], composed under Zhū Xī's express deathbed commission, was prescribed as the standard examination *Shū* by the Yuán reform of 1313 and confirmed in the Míng *Dàquán* [[KR1b0037]]. The bulk of the Yuán production in the division is explicit Cài Shěn subcommentary ([[KR1b0027]] [[KR1b0029]] [[KR1b0031]]) or Cài-Shěn-tested reading ([[KR1b0026]] [[KR1b0028]] [[KR1b0034]]); the late-Míng layer increasingly criticizes him ([[KR1b0041]]); the Qīng imperial compilations ([[KR1b0045]] [[KR1b0046]]) reaffirm him.

- **The *Yǔgòng* 禹貢 sub-tradition.** A single chapter — the schematic geographical survey ascribed to Yǔ — generates a free-standing scholarly genre. Begun in the Sòng by Máo Huàng [[KR1b0008]], Chéng Dàchāng [[KR1b0009]], and Fù Yín [[KR1b0012]], extended by Zhū Hèlíng [[KR1b0052]], and culminating in Hú Wèi's *Yǔgòng zhuīzhǐ* [[KR1b0053]] and Xú Wénjìng's *Yǔgòng huì jiān* [[KR1b0058]]. The sub-genre is also the seedbed of premodern Chinese historical geography more broadly; James Legge's separate translation of "The tribute of Yü" (1865) confirms its singular status in nineteenth-century Western reception.

- **The *Hóngfàn* 洪範 sub-tradition.** A second chapter — the "Great Plan" with its Five Phases (*wǔxíng* 五行) and Nine Categories (*jiǔ chóu* 九疇) — generates a parallel cosmological-political sub-genre, beginning with Hú Yuán [[KR1b0005]], extended by Zhào Shànxiāng's *Hóngfàn tǒngyī* [[KR1b0021]], reaching late-Míng cosmological maturity in Huáng Dàozhōu [[KR1b0044]], and pruned of accretion in the Qīng by Hú Wèi [[KR1b0054]].

- **義理 *yìlǐ* vs. textual recovery.** As in the *Yì* division, an opposition between meaning-and-principle reading (Sòng-Yuán Daoxue, culminating in Cài Shěn) and philological textual audit (Méi Zhuó, Yán Ruòqú, Hú Wèi). Unlike the *Yì*, where the textual side recovers the lost Hàn 象數, in the *Shū* the textual side dispatches half the canon as forgery — a decisive rather than recuperative outcome.

- **The Sòng-printed Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn* and its philological status.** [[KR1b0002]] preserves the recension that Yán Ruòqú impeached; [[KR1b0004]] preserves it integrated with Kǒng Yǐngdá's subcommentary and the Qīng evidential overlay. Reading the four together — base text, *Kǒng zhuàn*, *zhèngyì*, *kǎozhèng* — is reading the entire layered history of the *Shū* on one page.

## Timeline

- c. 10th–6th c. BCE — *Shàngshū* (oldest layers) 尚書 [[KR1b0001]] — the canonical text itself in its pre-Qín stratum; received recension assembled c. 317 CE (Wilkinson §58.6.3.1).
- c. -150 to 200 — *Shàngshū dà zhuàn* 尚書大傳 [[KR1b0059]] — the principal Hàn-era hermeneutic work attributed to Fú Shēng, with Zhèng Xuán's annotations.
- c. 1st c. BCE – 7th c. CE — *Shàngshū* (Sòng-printed Kǒng Ān'guó *zhuàn* + Lù Démíng *yīnyì*) [[KR1b0002]] — the layered text whose authenticity Yán Ruòqú would impeach.
- 642–653 — *Shàngshū zhèngyì* 尚書正義 [[KR1b0003]] — Kǒng Yǐngdá's imperial subcommentary, fixing the standard reading for four centuries.
- 642–1773 — *Shàngshū zhùshū* 尚書注疏 [[KR1b0004]] — the integrated *zhuàn* + *zhèngyì* + *yīnyì* edition with the Qīng *kǎozhèng* added; the layered scholarly *Shū* in its received Sìkù form.
- 1020–1059 — *Hóngfàn kǒuyì* 洪範口義 [[KR1b0005]] — Hú Yuán inaugurates the Sòng cosmological *Hóngfàn*.
- 1080–1101 — *Shū zhuàn* 書傳 [[KR1b0006]] — Sū Shì's literary-philosophical commentary.
- 1140–1176 — *Shàngshū quánjiě* 尚書全解 [[KR1b0007]] — Lín Zhīqí's comprehensive Southern-Sòng reading.
- 1150–1180 — *Yǔgòng zhǐnán* 禹貢指南 [[KR1b0008]] — Máo Huàng inaugurates the *Yǔgòng* sub-genre.
- 1150–1194 — *Shàngshū jiǎngyì* 尚書講義 [[KR1b0010]] — Shǐ Hào's lecture commentary.
- 1170–1189 — *Shàngshū xiángjiě* 尚書詳解 [[KR1b0011]] — Xià Sǒng's reading commentary.
- 1170–1195 — *Yǔgòng lùn* 禹貢論 [[KR1b0009]] — Chéng Dàchāng's *Yǔgòng* monograph.
- 1175–1180 — *Shàngshū jīngyì* 尚書精義 [[KR1b0018]] — Huáng Lún's reading.
- 1175–1207 — *Zēngxiū Dōnglái Shū shuō* 增修東萊書說 [[KR1b0013]] — Lǚ Zǔqiān, expanded by Shí Lán.
- 1180–1215 — *Yǔgòng shuō duàn* 禹貢說斷 [[KR1b0012]] — Fù Yín's *Yǔgòng*.
- 1180–1225 — *Wǔ gào jiě* 五誥解 [[KR1b0015]] — Yáng Jiǎn's reading of the five royal proclamations.
- 1190–1213 — *Shàngshū shuō* 尚書說 [[KR1b0014]] — Huáng Dù's reading.
- 1199–1209 — *Shū jīng jízhuàn* 書經集傳 [[KR1b0017]] — Cài Shěn, by Zhū Xī's deathbed commission; the orthodox examination commentary.
- 1200–1230 — *Chén shì Shàngshū xiángjiě* 陳氏尚書詳解 [[KR1b0019]] — Chén Jīng.
- 1200–1231 — *Jiézhāi jiāshú Shū chāo* 絜齋家塾書鈔 [[KR1b0016]] — Yuán Xiè's family-school *Shū* notes.
- 1207 — *Hóngfàn tǒngyī* 洪範統一 [[KR1b0021]] — Zhào Shànxiāng's *Hóngfàn* monograph.
- 1210–1244 — *Róngtáng Shū jiě* 融堂書解 [[KR1b0020]] — Qián Shí's reading.
- 1230–1260 — *Shū jízhuàn huòwèn* 書集傳或問 [[KR1b0023]] — Chén Dàyóu's dialogic apparatus to Cài Shěn.
- 1233–1235 — *Shàngshū yàoyì* 尚書要義 [[KR1b0022]] — Wèi Liǎowēng's distillation.
- 1240–1279 — *Hú shì Shàngshū xiángjiě* 胡氏尚書詳解 [[KR1b0024]] — Hú Shìháng's late-Sòng commentary.
- 1283–1298 — *Shàngshū zuǎn zhuàn* 尚書纂傳 [[KR1b0034]] — Wáng Tiānyǔ's synthetic Yuán commentary.
- 1285–1303 — *Shàngshū biǎo zhù* 尚書表注 [[KR1b0025]] — Jīn Lǚxiáng's annotated tables.
- 1290–1330 — *Shū chuán jí lù zuǎn zhù* 書傳輯錄纂註 [[KR1b0029]] — Dǒng Dǐng's Cài Shěn subcommentary.
- 1300–1331 — *Shū zuǎn yán* 書纂言 [[KR1b0026]] — Wú Chéng's Yuán commentary.
- 1314–1334 — *Shū jízhuàn zuǎnshū* 書集傳纂疏 [[KR1b0027]] — Chén Lì's model Cài Shěn subcommentary, contemporaneous with the Yuán examination reform.
- 1318–1321 — *Shū Cài shì zhuàn pángtōng* 書蔡氏傳旁通 [[KR1b0031]] — Chén Shīkǎi's Cài Shěn supplement.
- 1320–1337 — *Dú shū cóngshuō* 讀書叢說 [[KR1b0028]] — Xǔ Qiān's reading-notes.
- 1320–1360 — *Shū yì duàn fǎ* 書義斷法* [[KR1b0033]] — Chén Yuèdào's examination handbook.
- 1325–1330 — *Shàngshū tōngkǎo* 尚書通考* [[KR1b0030]] — Huáng Zhènchéng's classified compilation.
- 1330–1360 — *Shàngshū jù jiě* 尚書句解 [[KR1b0035]] — Zhū Zǔyì's school-use gloss.
- 1333–1350 — *Dú shū guǎnjiàn* 讀書管見 [[KR1b0032]] — Wáng Chōngyún's reading-notes.
- 1334–1370 — *Shū yì jīnshì* 書義矜式 [[KR1b0060]] — Wáng Chōngyún's examination handbook.
- 1394 — *Shū chuán huìxuǎn* 書傳會選 [[KR1b0036]] — Liú Sānwú's Hóngwǔ-era imperial compilation.
- 1414–1415 — *Shū jīng dàquán* 書經大全 [[KR1b0037]] — Hú Guǎng's Yǒnglè imperial compendium; the textbook *Shū* of late-imperial examinations.
- 1520–1545 — *Shàngshū kǎo yì* 尚書考異 [[KR1b0038]] — Méi Zhuó's first sustained Míng audit of the *Gǔwén*.
- 1530–1542 — *Shàngshū yíyì* 尚書疑義 [[KR1b0039]] — Mǎ Míngháng's late-Míng critical commentary.
- 1540–1580 — *Shàngshū biān Cài biān* 尚書砭蔡編 [[KR1b0041]] — Yuán Rén's critique of Cài Shěn.
- 1580–1595 — *Shàngshū rìjì* 尚書日記 [[KR1b0040]] — Wáng Qiáo's reading-diary.
- 1580–1620 — *Shàngshū zhù kǎo* 尚書注考 [[KR1b0042]] — Chén Tàijiāo's audit.
- 1590–1617 — *Shàngshū shū yǎn* 尚書疏衍 [[KR1b0043]] — Chén Dì's phonology-informed reading.
- 1635–1637 — *Hóngfàn míngyì* 洪範明義 [[KR1b0044]] — Huáng Dàozhōu's late-Míng *Hóngfàn*.
- 1660–1683 — *Shàngshū píchuán* 尚書埤傳 [[KR1b0051]] — Zhū Hèlíng's Cài Shěn supplement.
- 1665–1683 — *Yǔgòng chángjiān* 禹貢長箋 [[KR1b0052]] — Zhū Hèlíng's *Yǔgòng* monograph.
- 1670–1692 — *Shàngshū bài shū* 尚書稗疏 [[KR1b0047]] — Wáng Fūzhī's austere reading.
- 1670–1704 — *Shàngshū gǔwén shū zhèng* 尚書古文疏證 [[KR1b0048]] — Yán Ruòqú's demolition of the *Gǔwén Shàngshū*; the founding monument of high-Qīng *kǎozhèng*.
- 1678–1680 — *Rì jiǎng Shū jīng jiě yì* 日講書經解義 [[KR1b0045]] — the Kāngxī imperial lecture-commentary.
- 1680–1682 — *Shū jīng zhōnglùn* 書經衷論 [[KR1b0056]] — Zhāng Yīng's reading.
- 1685–1705 — *Yǔgòng zhuīzhǐ* 禹貢錐指 [[KR1b0053]] — Hú Wèi's definitive *Yǔgòng* monograph.
- 1690–1710 — *Hóngfàn zhènglùn* 洪範正論 [[KR1b0054]] — Hú Wèi's parallel *Hóngfàn* monograph.
- 1690–1716 — *Gǔwén Shàngshū yuān cí* 古文尚書冤詞 [[KR1b0049]] — Máo Qílíng's defense of the *Gǔwén*.
- 1690–1718 — *Shàngshū qī piān jiěyì* 尚書七篇解義 [[KR1b0055]] — Lǐ Guāngdì's selective commentary.
- 1700–1716 — *Shàngshū guǎngtīng lù* 尚書廣聽錄 [[KR1b0050]] — Máo Qílíng's continuation.

- 1715–1730 — *Shàngshū dìlǐ jīnshì* 尚書地理今釋 [[KR1b0057]] — Jiǎng Tíngxī's geographical handbook.
