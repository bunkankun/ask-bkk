# KR4 集部

KR4:  Collected Works / Literature division.

- [[KR4a]] 楚辭類 *Chǔcí lèi* — Collected Works — Songs of Chu. The *Chǔcí* corpus and its commentarial, anthology, and literary afterlife. 9 texts.
- [[KR4b]] 別集類一 *Biéjí lèi yī* — Collected Writings — Individual Collections, Hàn through Suí. Individual literary collections from early and medieval writers. 111 texts.
- [[KR4c]] 別集類·唐五代 *Biéjí lèi · Táng Wǔdài* — Collected Works — Individual Writers of the Tang and Five Dynasties. Author collections from the Tang and Five Dynasties. 113 texts.
- [[KR4d]] 別集類·宋金元 *Biéjí lèi · Sòng Jīn Yuán* — Collected Works — Individual Writers of the Sòng, Jīn, and Yuán. Individual collections from the Song, Jin, and Yuan periods. 550 texts.
- [[KR4e]] 別集類·明 *Biéjí lèi · Míng* — Collected Works — Individual Writers of the Míng. Ming author collections and literary corpora. 228 texts.
- [[KR4f]] 別集類(清) *Biéjí lèi (Qīng)* — Belles-Lettres — Individual Collections of the Qīng. Qing individual literary collections. 67 texts.
- [[KR4h]] 總集類 *Zǒngjí lèi* — Collected Works — General Anthologies. Anthologies gathering works by multiple authors across periods or genres. 164 texts.
- [[KR4i]] 詩文評類 *Shīwén píng lèi* — Belles-Lettres — Literary Criticism. Poetry and prose criticism, poetics, literary judgement, and critical notes. 65 texts.
- [[KR4j]] 詞曲類 *Cíqǔ lèi* — Collected Works — Lyric Poetry and Songbooks. *Cí*, *qǔ*, songbooks, tune prosody, and lyric criticism. 88 texts.
- [[KR4k]] 逸史小說類 *Yìshǐ xiǎoshuō lèi* — Extra Historical and Narrative Works. Historical fragments, anecdotal records, transformations, tales, and other narrative texts preserved outside the regular KR4 summaries. 417 texts.
