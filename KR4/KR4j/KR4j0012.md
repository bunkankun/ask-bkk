# Gūxī cí 姑溪詞
 *Lyrics of Gū-xī*
by [[李之儀]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Gūxī cí* 姑溪詞 is the one-*juǎn* Sìkù *cí* collection of Lǐ Zhīyí [[李之儀]] (*fl.* 1085–*ca.* 1117; *zì* Duānshū 端叔, *hào* Gūxī jūshì 姑溪居士), a Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] follower best known in his lifetime for his *chǐdú* 尺牘 (letters). His *cí* — gathered by Máo Jìn [[毛晉]] into forty tune-titles and eighty-eight *cí* — are placed by the *Tíyào* "nearly equal to Qín Guān [[秦觀]]" in the *xiǎolìng* register, *qīngwǎn qiàoqiàn* 清婉峭蒨 (clear-restrained, sharply-bright). The collection's unusual editorial trait is that Lǐ's matching-*cí* with Chén Guàn 陳瓘, Hè Zhù 賀鑄, and Huáng Tíngjiān [[黃庭堅]] place the original *cí* of the dedicatee first and Lǐ's matching piece second — a practice borrowed (per the *Tíyào*) from the *Xiè Tiǎo jí*'s incorporation of Wáng Róng 王融's poems, and a useful resource for cross-collation.

### Tiyao

*Gūxī cí*, one *juǎn*, by Lǐ Zhīyí of the Sòng. Zhīyí has the *Gūxī jí* separately catalogued. The *Shūlù jiětí* records Zhīyí's *Gūxī cí* in one *juǎn*. The present text was cut by Máo Jìn: forty tune-titles, eighty-eight *cí* in total. Zhīyí is renowned for his *chǐdú*, and his *cí* are also fine, especially his *xiǎolìng* — clear-restrained, sharp and bright, scarcely below Qín Guān. Jìn's colophon says: "Huáng Shēng [[黃昇]]'s *Huāān cí xuǎn* [[KR4j0066]] did not pick him up — there is the lament of the abandoned pearl." But Huáng's selections are all of men after the southern crossing — hence "Marvellous *cí* since the *Zhōngxīng*" [[KR4j0067]]; Zhīyí's period is earlier; Jìn did not check. As for the lines Jìn quotes — *yuānqīn bàn yōng kōngchuáng yuè* 鴛衾半擁空床月, *bù lǎn qià xún chuángwò* 步懶恰尋床卧, *kàn yóusī dào dì cháng* 看遊絲到地長, *shíshí jìn shǒu xīntóu yùn, shòu jìn wúrén zhī chù liáng* 時時浸手心頭熨，受盡無人知處凉 — these still do not exhaust his strengths. His matching-*cí* for Chén Guàn, Hè Zhù, Huáng Tíngjiān: the original is given first, his own piece second; songs paired in mutual address — the example is from the *Xiè Tiǎo jí*'s appended Wáng Róng poems — letting the matching of feeling on both sides be seen; the *cí*-arrangement and intent thus becomes far better explained than in collections that omit it. The Huáng Tíngjiān *Hǎo shì jìn* 好事近 here closes *fù shífēn jiāoyè* 負十分蕉葉; today's *Shāngǔ cí* [[KR4j0006]] mis-prints 蕉 as 金 — a cross-verification asset.

### Abstract

The transmitted *Gūxī cí* descends through Máo Jìn [[毛晉]]'s *Jígǔgé* one-*juǎn* cutting, agreeing with Chén Zhènsūn [[陳振孫]]'s catalog form. Modern editions: the *Quán Sòng cí* of Táng Guīzhāng [[唐圭璋]] gives a corpus of around 92 *cí*. The collection contains Lǐ's most famous piece, *Bǔ suàn zǐ · Wǒ zhù Chángjiāng tóu* 卜算子·我住長江頭 — *wǒ zhù Chángjiāng tóu, jūn zhù Chángjiāng wěi, rìrì sī jūn bù jiàn jūn, gòng yǐn Chángjiāng shuǐ* 我住長江頭，君住長江尾，日日思君不見君，共飲長江水 ("I live at the head of the great river, you at its tail; day-on-day I long for you and do not see you, but we drink the same river-water") — composed for his second wife Yáng Shūyǔ 楊姝, a singing-girl whom Lǐ married at Tàipíngzhōu when banished there in the *Chóngníng* period; this *cí*, with its echo of the *Yuèfǔ* tradition, has remained the most-cited *xiǎolìng* of the early Sòng. Lǐ's *cí* divide neatly between the Biànjīng matching-*cí* with the *Sū mén* circle (the matching pieces to Chén Guàn, Hè Zhù, Huáng Tíngjiān documented above) and the southern exile period at Tàipíngzhōu.

## Other points of interest

The Sìkù compilers' note that Lǐ's matching-*cí* arrangement preserves the textual basis for cross-collating Huáng Tíngjiān's *Hǎo shì jìn* — proving *jiāo* 蕉 against the corrupted *jīn* 金 — is a classic of the *cí*-philology that emerged in the late Qiánlóng *Sìkù* phase. The popular reception of *Bǔ suàn zǐ · Wǒ zhù Chángjiāng tóu* — set to song by countless modern composers — has made Lǐ the most universally-known of the secondary Northern-Sòng *cí*-writers.
