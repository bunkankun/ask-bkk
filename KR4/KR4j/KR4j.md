# KR4j 詞曲類 — Lyric Poetry and Songbooks

## Scope and scholarly tradition

The *Cíqǔ lèi* 詞曲類 is the smallest of the five subdivisions of *Jíbù* 集部 (Collected Works) in the *Sìkù quánshū* arrangement, but bibliographically the most contested. It is the standing imperial admission that *cí* 詞 ("lyrics of unequal lines set to a tune") and *qǔ* 曲 ("aria") — genres that Sòng and Yuán catalogers had often hesitated to admit at all, on the grounds that they were *zhèngtǒng* 正統 *shī*-poetry's vernacular and "music-hall" descendants — deserve a legitimate place in the imperial library. Wilkinson (§30.2.1, Box 172) places *cí* and *qǔ* in the same dynastic-genre sequence as *shī* and *fù*, with *cí* "Tang developed; Song perfected, but lost the connection with songs," dated from the *Huājiān jí* 花間集 (940), and *qǔ* "reaching its height in the Yuan." The KR4j division covers exactly this arc: from the late-Táng / Five-Dynasties *Huājiān* corpus through the Sòng *cí* canon, the late-Sòng loyalist *yǒngwù* anthologies, the Yuán transition into *sǎnqǔ* 散曲, the Míng *cíhuà* 詞話 tradition, and the *Kāngxī*–*Qiánlóng* imperial canonization project that produced the *Yùxuǎn lìdài shīyú* [[KR4j0074]], the *Yùdìng cípǔ* [[KR4j0086]], and the *Yùdìng qǔpǔ* [[KR4j0089]].

The division is internally heterogeneous in three ways. First, by genre: it bundles *cí* (personal collections + anthologies + *cíhuà* + *cípǔ*), *sǎnqǔ* + *xìqǔ*-prosody (*Zhōngyuán yīnyùn* [[KR4j0090]], *qǔpǔ*, *qǔhuà*), and the *Kāngxī*-imperial canon-formation works under a single roof. Second, by chronology: it runs from 940 (*Huājiān jí*) to the late-Qián-lóng *Sìkù* apparatus (the 1782–1786 *Kǎozhèng* [[KR4j0091]] is bound at the division's tail). Third, by aesthetic axis: it crosses the two great stylistic poles of the *cí* — the *wǎnyuē* 婉約 (restrained, lyrical) line that runs from the *Huājiān* through Yàn Shū [[晏殊]], Zhōu Bāngyàn [[周邦彥]], Lǐ Qīngzhào [[李清照]], Jiāng Kuí [[姜夔]], and Wú Wényīng [[吳文英]]; and the *háofàng* 豪放 (bold, expansive) line opened by Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] and brought to its supreme statement by Xīn Qìjí [[辛棄疾]]. The *Sìkù tíyào* to the *Dōngpō cí* [[KR4j0005]] codifies the canonical historiography of this split: Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] was the Bái Jūyì of the *cí* form, Sū Shì its Hán Yù — opening the road for the entire Xīn Qìjí line that defines half the post-Sòng *cí* canon.

## Important texts and text clusters

- *Huājiān jí* 花間集 [[KR4j0062]] — the founding anthology of the *cí* (940, Hòu-Shǔ court of Mèng Chǎng), edited by Zhào Chóngzuò [[趙崇祚]]; 500 *cí* by 18 late-Táng / Five-Dynasties poets and the textual source of the early *cí* repertoire.
- The Northern-Sòng *xiǎolìng* core — *Zhūyù cí* 珠玉詞 of Yàn Shū [[KR4j0001]], *Liùyī cí* 六一詞 of Ōuyáng Xiū [[KR4j0002]], *Ānlù jí* 安陸集 of Zhāng Xiān [[KR4j0004]], *Xiǎoshān cí* 小山詞 of Yàn Jǐdào [[KR4j0010]] — the canonical entry-point to the polite *xiǎolìng* in the Féng Yánsì manner.
- *Yuèzhāng jí* 樂章集 [[KR4j0003]] — Liǔ Yǒng's lyric corpus; the foundational corpus of the *màncí* 慢詞 ("long-tune") form and the demotic urban *cí* register.
- *Dōngpō cí* 東坡詞 [[KR4j0005]] — Sū Shì's *cí*; the *biàngé* 變格 ("variant register") that opened the *háofàng* line and the locus classicus of *Niànnú jiāo · Chìbì huáigǔ*.
- *Piànyù cí* 片玉詞 [[KR4j0015]] — Zhōu Bāngyàn's prosodically supreme *cí*; head of the *Dàshèng yuèfǔ* 大晟樂府 reform and the model for the entire Southern-Sòng *gélǜ* 格律 school, with three matching-*cí* collections (Fāng Qiānlǐ [[KR4j0019]], Yáng Zémín, Chén Yǔnpìng) preserved alongside.
- *Shùyù cí* 漱玉詞 [[KR4j0027]] — Lǐ Qīngzhào's slim seventeen-piece collection, the most exalted single voice in the Chinese *cí* and the canonical *Yìān tǐ* 易安體.
- *Jiàxuān cí* 稼軒詞 [[KR4j0040]] — Xīn Qìjí's vast corpus (c. 626 *cí*), supreme *háofàng* monument and the canonical patriotic-loyalist *cí*-collection.
- *Báishí dàorén gēqǔ* 白石道人歌曲 [[KR4j0047]] — Jiāng Kuí's *cí* including 13 *zìzhìqǔ* 自製曲 (self-composed tunes) with the only surviving Sòng *cí* notation (*pángpǔ* 旁譜) — the single most musicologically important *cí* text.
- *Mèngchuāng gǎo* 夢窗稿 [[KR4j0048]] — Wú Wényīng's dense, opaque late-Southern-Sòng *cí*; the Lǐ Shāngyǐn 李商隱 of the *cí*-form, read against Jiāng Kuí as the prosodic-school co-pole.
- The late-Sòng anthology cluster — *Yuèfǔ yǎcí* 樂府雅詞 [[KR4j0065]] of Zēng Zào [[曾慥]], *Huāān cíxuǎn* 花菴詞選 [[KR4j0066]] and *Zhōngxīng yǐlái juémiào cíxuǎn* [[KR4j0067]] of Huáng Shēng [[黃昇]], *Juémiào hǎocí jiān* 絕妙好詞箋 [[KR4j0071]] of Zhōu Mì [[周密]] (annotated by the Qīng commentators Chá Wéirén [[查爲仁]] and Lì È [[厲鶚]]) — the principal Sòng-Yuán selections that constituted the *cí*-canon for late-imperial reading.
- *Yuèfǔ bǔtí* 樂府補題 [[KR4j0072]] — the politically supreme *yǒngwù* 詠物 anthology: 37 exchange-*cí* by 14 Sòng-loyalist *yímín* poets at five gatherings, 1278–1290; read since Wáng Guówéi as allegorical elegy for the desecrated Sòng imperial tombs at Shàoxīng.
- *Bìjī mànzhì* 碧鷄漫志 [[KR4j0078]] and *Yuèfǔ zhǐmí* 樂府指迷 [[KR4j0080]] — the two founding Sòng *cí*-treatises: Wáng Zhuó's historical-prosodic survey of the *cí* as song-form (1145–1149) and Shěn Yìfǔ's practical compositional handbook in the Wú Wényīng manner (1242–1250). The textual basis of all subsequent *cíhuà*.
- *Zhōngyuán yīnyùn* 中原音韻 [[KR4j0090]] — Zhōu Déqīng's 1324 phonological manual; the founding statement of modern *Mandarin* phonology (19 rhyme-groups; *yīn / yángpíng* division; *rù*-redistribution) and the indispensable reference for reading Yuán *běiqǔ*.
- The Yuán *sǎnqǔ* anthology pair — *Tàipíng yuèfǔ* 太平樂府 [[KR4j0081]] of Yáng Cháoyīng [[楊朝英]] (1351) and the *Líyuán ànshì yuèfǔ xīnshēng* 梨園按試樂府新聲 [[KR4j0082]] — the principal documentary sources for Yuán *sǎnqǔ*.
- The Qīng anthology and prosody triad — *Cízōng* 詞綜 [[KR4j0075]] of Zhū Yízūn [[朱彝尊]] (1678; founding charter of the *Zhèxīpài* 浙西派), *Shíwǔ jiā cí* 十五家詞 [[KR4j0076]] of Sūn Mò [[孫默]] (1670s, *Yángxiànpài* 陽羨派 representative), and *Cílǜ* 詞律 [[KR4j0087]] of Wàn Shù [[萬樹]] (1687) — together establishing the Qīng *cí* revival.
- The *Kāngxī* imperial canon — *Yùxuǎn lìdài shīyú* 御選歷代詩餘 [[KR4j0074]] (1707, c. 9,000 *cí* by c. 1,540 poets in 120 *juǎn*), *Yùdìng cípǔ* 御定詞譜 [[KR4j0086]] (1715, 826 tunes), *Yùdìng qǔpǔ* 御定曲譜 [[KR4j0089]] (1715, Northern + Southern *qǔ*). The three together constitute the institutionalized Qīng *cíqǔ* prosody.
- *Cíyuàn cóngtán* 詞苑叢談 [[KR4j0085]] of Xú Qiú [[徐釚]] — the most comprehensive of all Qīng *cíhuà*, organized into seven thematic *mén* (form, sound, criticism, anecdote, textual verification, jest, miscellany); the Qīng analytical encyclopedia of *cí*.
- *Kēxuě cí* 珂雪詞 [[KR4j0061]] of Cáo Zhēnjí [[曹貞吉]] — the only Qīng-period *cí* collection admitted to the *Sìkù* on its author's own merit, standing between *Yángxiàn* and *Zhèxī* aesthetic poles.

## Important persons

- [[趙崇祚]] — Hòu-Shǔ courtier; editor of the *Huājiān jí*, the founding *cí*-anthology of the tradition.
- [[晏殊]] — Northern-Sòng councillor; foundational *xiǎolìng* master in the Féng Yánsì line.
- [[柳永]] — first great expander of the *cí* into the *màn* (long-tune) form; demotic Northern-Sòng urban *cí* and the songs of the courtesan-quarters.
- [[蘇軾]] — supreme *biàngé* poet; opened the *háofàng* line and made the *cí* a fully literary form.
- [[周邦彥]] — head of the *Dàshèng yuèfǔ* Imperial Music Bureau under Huīzōng; the supreme musical theorist of the *cí* and parent of the Southern-Sòng *gélǜ* school.
- [[李清照]] — supreme woman *cí*-poet; survivor of the Sòng-Jīn rupture and author of the *Jīnshí lù hòu xù*; canonical *Yìāntǐ*.
- [[辛棄疾]] — supreme *háofàng* master; ex-Jīn-resistance fighter, sometime official, and the largest single *cí*-corpus of the Sòng.
- [[陸游]] — the great patriot poet, also represented by his *cí* [[KR4j0043]].
- [[姜夔]] — supreme prosodic-school master; only Sòng *cí*-writer whose own musical notation survives; ancestor of all subsequent musicological recovery of the Sòng *cí*.
- [[吳文英]] — supreme late-Southern-Sòng prosodist; the "Lǐ Shāngyǐn of the *cí*"; co-pole of the *gélǜ* line with Jiāng Kuí.
- [[蔣捷]] — *Sòng* loyalist *cí*-writer of the *Zhúshān cí* [[KR4j0053]]; canonized in the *Cízōng* as a *Zhèxī* auxiliary.
- [[周密]] — late-Sòng loyalist and connoisseur; compiler of the *Juémiào hǎocí* and a contributor to the *Yuèfǔ bǔtí*.
- [[黃昇]] — Southern-Sòng anthologist whose *Huāān cíxuǎn* preserved the basic life-data and corpus of 88 Southern-Sòng poets.
- [[王灼]] — Southern-Sòng theorist; author of the founding *cí*-treatise *Bìjī mànzhì*.
- [[沈義父]] — Southern-Sòng pupil of Wú Wényīng and author of the compositional handbook *Yuèfǔ zhǐmí*.
- [[周德清]] — Yuán phonologist; *Zhōngyuán yīnyùn*, the codification of modern Mandarin phonology for the *běiqǔ* singer.
- [[楊朝英]] — Yuán anthologist; *Tàipíng yuèfǔ* and *Yángchūn báixuě*, the principal Yuán *sǎnqǔ* anthologies.
- [[陳霆]] — Míng *cíhuà* compiler; *Zhūshāntáng cíhuà* preserves Sòng *yìpiān* otherwise lost.
- [[朱彝尊]] — supreme Qīng *cí*-scholar and founder of the *Zhèxīpài*; compiler of the *Cízōng*.
- [[萬樹]] — Qīng prosodist; *Cílǜ*, the rigorous private *cí*-prosody manual that became the model for all subsequent work.
- [[徐釚]] — Qīng *cí*-historian; *Cíyuàn cóngtán*, the comprehensive Qīng compendium of *cí*-historical material.
- [[聖祖玄燁]] — *Kāngxī* emperor; patron of the imperial *cíqǔ* canon-formation project.
- [[王奕清]] — *Hànlín* editor under *Kāngxī*; chief compiler of both the *Yùdìng cípǔ* and the *Yùdìng qǔpǔ*.
- [[曹貞吉]] — early-*Kāngxī cí*-revival poet; the only Qīng *cí*-author with a personal collection admitted to the *Sìkù*.

## Topics

- **Wǎnyuē 婉約 vs. háofàng 豪放.** The single most consequential interpretive axis of the *cí*-tradition. The *Huājiān*-line poets and Northern-Sòng *xiǎolìng* masters (Yàn Shū [[晏殊]], Zhāng Xiān [[張先]]) established the restrained-lyrical mode; Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] opened the demotic line; Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] then opened the *háofàng* line, brought to its supreme statement by Xīn Qìjí [[辛棄疾]] in the Southern Sòng. The *Sìkù tíyào* to *Dōngpō cí* [[KR4j0005]] is the canonical Qīng formulation: Liǔ as the Bái Jūyì of the *cí*, Sū as its Hán Yù. The Qīng *Zhèxīpài* of [[朱彝尊]] explicitly canonized the *wǎnyuē* line through Jiāng Kuí [[姜夔]]; the *Chángzhōupài* of Zhāng Huìyán in the early 19th century positioned itself in opposition. Every comprehensive anthology in the division — *Huāān cíxuǎn* [[KR4j0066]], *Cízōng* [[KR4j0075]], *Yùxuǎn lìdài shīyú* [[KR4j0074]] — takes a definite editorial position on this axis.

- **Cí as song-form vs. cí as literary form.** Whether the *cí* should be conceived primarily as a singable musical form or as a written literary form is the founding theoretical question of the genre, opened by Wáng Zhuó's *Bìjī mànzhì* [[KR4j0078]] (which mourns the loss of the Táng *gēfǎ* and treats the Sòng *cí* as a residual song-tradition) and by Shěn Yìfǔ's *Yuèfǔ zhǐmí* [[KR4j0080]] (which formalizes the *qùshēng* / *píngshǎngrù* substitution rule). The question becomes acute with the loss of Sòng *qǔpǔ* by the Yuán, after which *cí* could only be a literary form. Jiāng Kuí's *zìzhìqǔ* in [[KR4j0047]] — with surviving notation — remains the principal documentary anchor of the song-form reading. Wàn Shù's *Cílǜ* [[KR4j0087]] takes the literary form to its logical end with a *qūpǔ*-based prosody recovered by collation.

- **Yǎzhèng 雅正 as anthology principle.** The classifying principle that runs from Zēng Zào's *Yuèfǔ yǎcí* [[KR4j0065]] through Zhōu Mì's *Juémiào hǎocí* to Zhū Yízūn's *Cízōng* [[KR4j0075]] — *cí* must conform to *yǎzhèng* (the canonically refined and orthodox) and not to the *Huājiān* / *Cǎotáng* line of decorative pretty pieces. The *Yùxuǎn lìdài shīyú* [[KR4j0074]] institutionalizes the *yǎzhèng* preference as imperial taste.

- **Yǒngwù 詠物 as political allegory.** The Southern-Sòng *yǒngwù* mode — descriptive lyrics on objects — became, in the loyalist generation after 1276, a vehicle for political allegory. The *Yuèfǔ bǔtí* [[KR4j0072]] is the supreme example: five sets of exchange-*cí* on dragon-spittle perfume, white lotus, water-shield, cicada, and crab — read by Wáng Guówéi and modern scholars as figures for the desecrated Sòng imperial tombs. The *yǒngwù* mode shapes the late Wú Wényīng [[吳文英]] [[KR4j0048]] and the entire late-Southern-Sòng tail.

- **The Sòng-Yuán transition: cí into sǎnqǔ.** As Wilkinson's Box 172 sets out, *qǔ* reached its height in the Yuán as a successor genre to *cí*: the same musical-lyric impulse moving to a new prestige form. The transition is documented within the division by the late-Sòng-into-Yuán *cí*-writers Zhāng Yán, Bái Pǔ [[KR4j0059]], Zhāng Hùi [[KR4j0060]], and by the founding Yuán phonological and anthology works ([[KR4j0090]], [[KR4j0081]], [[KR4j0082]]). The *Yùdìng qǔpǔ* [[KR4j0089]] then institutionalizes the unified *cíqǔ* prosodic tradition.

- **North vs. South in qǔ.** The Yuán *běiqǔ* tradition (rooted in *Zhōngyuán yīnyùn* phonology [[KR4j0090]] and the *Tàipíng yuèfǔ* anthology [[KR4j0081]]) and the Míng *nánqǔ* tradition (running through the *Yōngxī yuèfǔ* [[KR4j0068]] and the *Wúsāo hébiān* [[KR4j0069]]) form the two great regional song-traditions of the Yuán-Míng period. The *Yùdìng qǔpǔ* [[KR4j0089]] codifies both in parallel registers.

- **Cí by women.** The *Cíqǔ lèi* admits two single-author collections by Sòng women: the *Shùyù cí* of Lǐ Qīngzhào [[KR4j0027]] and the *Duàncháng cí* 斷腸詞 of Zhū Shūzhēn [[KR4j0058]] — together the principal documentary basis of the Sòng-era *guīyīn* 閨音 ("women's voice") tradition. Chén Tíng's *Zhūshāntáng cíhuà* [[KR4j0083]] preserves Wáng Zhāoyí's *Mǎnjiānghóng*, the most-anthologized late-Sòng court-lady's *cí*.

- **Cípǔ and qǔpǔ as scholarly enterprise.** The Qīng *kǎojù*-era *cípǔ* / *qǔpǔ* project — Wàn Shù's *Cílǜ* [[KR4j0087]] privately and the imperial *Cípǔ* [[KR4j0086]] / *Qǔpǔ* [[KR4j0089]] institutionally — is the longest-running scholarly enterprise within the division. It reflects the wider *kǎojù*-era ambition to recover by philological-prosodic means what the loss of the Sòng *qǔpǔ* had taken away.

- 1687 — *Cílǜ* 詞律 [[KR4j0087]] — Wàn Shù's rigorous private *cí*-prosody manual.
- 1685–1707 — *Yùxuǎn lìdài shīyú* 御選歷代詩餘 [[KR4j0074]] — *Kāngxī*-commissioned comprehensive *cí* canon (120 *juǎn*).
- 1715 — *Yùdìng cípǔ* 御定詞譜 [[KR4j0086]] and *Yùdìng qǔpǔ* 御定曲譜 [[KR4j0089]] — the institutionalized Qīng prosodic system for *cí* and *qǔ*.
- 1782–1786 — *Qīndìng Sìkù quánshū kǎozhèng* 欽定四庫全書考證 [[KR4j0091]] — the textual-collation apparatus of the entire *Sìkù*, bound at the tail of the *Jíbù*.
