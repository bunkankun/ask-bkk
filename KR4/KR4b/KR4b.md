# Biéjí lèi yī 別集類一 — Individual Collections, Hàn through Suí

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR4b sits inside `KR4` 集部 (*jíbù*, Collected Writings) as the first of the four chronological bands into which the *Sìkù quánshū* sub-divides the *biéjí lèi* 別集類 — the collected writings of named individuals, as distinct from anthologies (*zǒngjí* 總集), *cí*-and-*qǔ* (*cíqǔ lèi* 詞曲類), and the *Chǔcí* group. The nineteen works gathered here run from the Western Hàn polymath Yáng Xióng 揚雄 (53 BCE – 18 CE) to the LiángChén stylist Xú Líng 徐陵 (507–583), spanning roughly six centuries and the entire arc the *Sìkù* compilers designated as the formative period of the Chinese authorial *jí*: late-Hàn classicists, the *Jiànān* 建安 generation, the *Zhúlín qī xián* 竹林七賢 / *WèiJìn* counterculture, the southern *Èr Lù* 二陸, the Eastern-Jìn / LiúSòng eremitic and *yuèfǔ* lines, the *Yǒngmíng* / *gōngtǐ* 永明–宮體 prosodists of the Southern Qí and Liáng, and the great Liáng / Northern-Zhōu *parallel-prose* (*piántǐ* 駢體) masters who fed directly into the Táng. The Suí is represented only by Yǔ Xìn's 庾信 final year (the *Xīn Chéng bēi wén* 辛成碑文 reburial of 581 places his last attested composition in the new dynasty); no native Suí *jí* appears in the division.

The defining textual fact for this band — flagged in almost every per-text *tíyào* — is that **scarcely any of the early collections survives in anything like its Hàn-to-Liáng form**. Wilkinson §61.3.10 records that even by the Suí the early *biéjí* corpus had been reduced to about 34 securely attested collections, against just under 1,000 known by title for the Táng (Tāo and Lǐ 2001); for the Sòng *Wén xiàn tōng kǎo* and the *Sìkù*, the situation upstream of the Sòng is more attrition than transmission. The *juǎn*-counts in *Suíshū jīngjí zhì*, the two *Tángshū yìwén zhì*, *Chóng wén zǒng mù*, and Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiě tí* document a near-continuous loss curve: Yáng Xióng's 5-*juǎn* original disappears in the Sòng and is replaced by a Wànlì re-aggregation (1595) of forty-some pieces gleaned from the *Hànshū*, *Gǔwén yuàn*, and *lèishū*; Kǒng Róng's 9–10 *juǎn* perish in the Sòng, leaving a Míng patchwork of 44 substantive fragments; Cài Yōng's Liáng 20-*juǎn* drops to a 10-*juǎn* Sòng version and finally a Yōngzhèng-era Chénliú-cut 6-*juǎn* recension; Cáo Zhí's 30-*juǎn* Suí text is the ancestor of a 1213 Sòng print whose 210 pieces in fact *over-shoot* the Wèi-court enumeration of "over a hundred" by absorbing *lèishū* fragments and music-bureau adjustments. The reader of KR4b is therefore never handling a Hàn or Wèi book directly — every text in the division is a SòngMíngQīng *reconstruction*, and the *tíyào* tradition (carried in this division almost exclusively by Jì Yún 紀昀, Lù Xīxióng 陸錫熊, Sūn Shìyì 孫士毅, and Lù Fèichí 陸費墀 between 1778 and 1781) does its most consequential work by tracking which layer of the editorial palimpsest each piece belongs to. Wilkinson §27.6 frames the same point from the other side: the late-Sòng / Míng *Bǎi sān jiā jí* 百三家集 of Zhāng Pǔ 張溥 and the parallel Wànlì *cǎijìn* prints are the principal recoverable filter through which Hàn-Wèi-Six-Dynasties writing reaches the modern reader.

## Important texts and text clusters

The division resolves into a small number of recognizable clusters, each of which crystallizes a moment in the long arc from Hàn classicism to Táng prosody.

- **Hàn classicists.** *Yáng Zǐyún jí* 揚子雲集 [[KR4b0001]] — the Wànlì Zhèng Pǔ 鄭樸 re-aggregation of Yáng Xióng's prose, *fù*, and three philosophical treatises, also the locus of the *Sìkù*'s major editorial defense of the *Hànshū* "*Mǎng dàfū* 莽大夫" verdict against Jiāo Hóng's 焦竑 chronological rehabilitation. *Cài Zhōngláng jí* 蔡中郎集 [[KR4b0002]] — the surviving signature of the supervisor of the *Xīpíng shí jīng* 熹平石經, transmitted in a Yōngzhèng Chénliú re-edition partial to its native son. *Kǒng Běihǎi jí* 孔北海集 [[KR4b0003]] — a one-*juǎn* Míng reconstruction preserving the satirical voice that cost Kǒng Róng his life under Cáo Cāo.
- **Jiànān 建安 and Wèi.** *Cáo Zǐjiàn jí* 曹子建集 [[KR4b0004]] — Sòng Jiādìng 6 (1213) print, the cornerstone of the *Jiànān* canon and the principal source for the *Luò shén fù* 洛神賦, *Qī āi* 七哀, *Zèng Bái Mǎ Wáng Biāo* 贈白馬王彪, and the seven *Zá shī* 雜詩. *Xí Zhōngsǎn jí* 嵇中散集 [[KR4b0005]] — the foundational corpus of *mínglǐ* 名理 argument (*Shēng wú āi lè lùn* 聲無哀樂論, *Yǎng shēng lùn* 養生論, *Shì sī lùn* 釋私論) and the two *jué jiāo shū* 絕交書 that secured Xí Kāng's iconic refusal of WèiJìn court compromise.
- **Western Jìn — the *Èr Lù* 二陸.** *Lù Shìhéng wén jí* 陸士衡文集 [[KR4b0006]] and *Lù Shìlóng wén jí* 陸士龍文集 [[KR4b0007]] — Xú Mínzhān's 徐民瞻 joint Sòng Qìngyuán 6 (1200) *Jìn èr jùn wén jí* 晉二俊文集, anchoring the *Wén fù* 文賦 (the first systematic *fù* on the writing process) and Lù Yún's extraordinary correspondence with his brother on the drafting of that very text — the most intimate compositional document in early-medieval Chinese letters.
- **Eastern Jìn — Táo Qián 陶潛.** *Táo Yuānmíng jí* 陶淵明集 [[KR4b0008]] — the Qiánlóng *Sìkù* eight-*juǎn* re-edition that, on explicit imperial endorsement, strips the *Wǔ xiào zhuàn* 五孝傳 and *Sìbā mù* 四八目 as Northern-Qí forgeries planted by Yáng Xiūzhī 陽休之 and restores a putatively Xiāo-Tǒng-original division. *Jiānzhù Táo Yuānmíng jí* 箋註陶淵明集 [[KR4b0009]] — the late-Sòng / Yuán Lǐ Gōnghuàn 李公煥 *jiānzhù* preserved in the SBCK as the Yuán *Cuìyánjīngshè* 翠巖精舍 cut, retaining the disputed paratexts and serving as the principal source for the SòngYuán reception (Sū Shì, Huáng Tíngjiān) that canonized the *Guī qù lái cí* 歸去來辭 and *Táo huā yuán jì* 桃花源記. The pairing of these two volumes is the single most instructive editorial dossier in the division.
- **Wǔgōng curiosity.** *Xuánjī tú shī dú fǎ* 璇璣圖詩讀法 [[KR4b0010]] — Kāng Wànmín 康萬民's late-Wànlì exegesis of the Former-Qín *huíwén* 回文 brocade-poem attributed to Sū Huì 蘇蕙, the canonical exemplar of constraint-based composition in the Chinese tradition; classed in *biéjí* on the strength of Sū Huì's putative authorship.
- **LiúSòng / YuánJiā 元嘉.** *Bào Míngyuǎn jí* 鮑明遠集 [[KR4b0011]] — the Yú Yán 虞炎 Southern-Qí recension preserved in a Míng *Zhèngdé* 5 (1510) Zhū Yìngdēng 朱應登 print, foundational for the seven-syllable *yuèfǔ* line (*Nǐ Xíng lù nán* 擬行路難) and the great ruins-*fù* *Wú chéng fù* 蕪城賦.
- **Southern Qí *Yǒngmíng tǐ* 永明體.** *Xiè Xuānchéng jí* 謝宣城集 [[KR4b0012]] — Lóu Zhào's 樓炤 Shàoxīng 28 (1158) cut, the five *fù*-and-*shī juǎn* surviving from the original ten (the prose half having been editorially discarded as "matters of a declining age"); the principal witness to the prosodic experiments that prepared Táng *jìntǐ shī* 近體詩.
- **Liáng court literature.** *Zhāomíng tàizǐ jí* 昭明太子集 [[KR4b0013]] — Xiāo Tǒng's 蕭統 own collection, a Míng aggregation whose textual integrity the *Sìkù tíyào* damages by identifying five poems as in fact by *Liáng Jiǎnwéndì* 簡文帝 (Xiāo Gāng) misattributed under the homonymous "*tàizǐ*" title. *Hé Shuǐbù jí* 何水部集 [[KR4b0014]] — the spare one-*juǎn* survival of the *Yǒngmíng* second-wave miniaturist whom Dù Fǔ 杜甫 invoked at every opportunity as a stylistic forebear. *Jiāng Wéntōng jí* 江文通集 [[KR4b0015]] and *Liáng Jiāng Wéntōng wén jí* 梁江文通文集 [[KR4b0016]] — twin recensions (a *Sìkù*-WYG four-*juǎn* Liáng Bīn 梁賓 collation of 1758 against an SBCK ten-*juǎn* Yè Shùlián 葉樹廉 conservative edition) of Jiāng Yān's writings, source of *Hèn fù* 恨賦, *Bié fù* 別賦, and the *Zá tǐ shī* 雜體詩 imitations.
- **Liáng–Northern-Zhōu *parallel-prose* masters.** *Yǔ Kāifǔ jí jiānzhù* 庾開府集箋註 [[KR4b0017]] and *Yǔ Zǐshān jí* 庾子山集 [[KR4b0018]] — the paired Kāngxī commentaries on Yǔ Xìn 庾信 (Wú Zhàoyí 吳兆宜's pioneering effort, and Ní Fán's 倪璠 1689 redo with the first systematic *niánpǔ* 年譜 and the *Wén yuàn yīng huá*–based transfer of two stelae to Yáng Jiōng 楊炯), centered on the *Āi Jiāngnán fù* 哀江南賢. *Xú Xiàomù jí jiānzhù* 徐孝穆集箋注 [[KR4b0019]] — Wú Zhàoyí's *jiānzhù* (completed by Xú Wénbǐng 徐文炳) on Yǔ Xìn's literary twin Xú Líng 徐陵, including the *Yùtái xīnyǒng xù* 玉臺新詠序 — the foundational document of the *gōngtǐ* 宮體 aesthetic. Together with the *Yǔ Xìn* pair, this volume closes the division on the threshold of the Táng *sìliù* 四六 tradition.

## Important persons

- [[揚雄]] — Western-Hàn classicist, *fù*-poet, and lexicographer; namesake of [[KR4b0001]] and a constant reference-point for late-Hàn and WèiJìn writers gathered in the division.
- [[蔡邕]] — Eastern-Hàn polymath, supervisor of the Xīpíng Stone Classics, authority on music and ritual; namesake of [[KR4b0002]].
- [[孔融]] — late-Hàn Confucian moral-political voice and one of the *Jiànān qī zǐ* 建安七子; namesake of [[KR4b0003]] and the martyr-case of frank speech under Cáo Cāo.
- [[曹植]] — third son of Cáo Cāo, posthumously *Chén Sī wáng* 陳思王; the canonical *fù*-poet of the *Jiànān* mode, central to [[KR4b0004]].
- [[嵇康]] — leader of the *Zhúlín qī xián* 竹林七賢, foundational *mínglǐ* essayist; subject of [[KR4b0005]] and patron-saint of WèiJìn iconoclasm.
- [[陸機]] and [[陸雲]] — the *Èr Lù* of Wújùn Huátíng; authors of the *Wén fù* and its drafting correspondence, jointly edited by Xú Mínzhān into [[KR4b0006]] and [[KR4b0007]].
- [[陶潛]] — Eastern-Jìn / LiúSòng poet of eremitic withdrawal, subject of two recensions ([[KR4b0008]], [[KR4b0009]]) that frame the editorial debate over the Northern-Qí additions.
- [[蕭統]] — Crown Prince of Liáng, compiler of the *Wén xuǎn* 文選, editor of [[KR4b0008]], and namesake of [[KR4b0013]]. The most influential single editorial intelligence in the division's transmission history.
- [[鮑照]] — LiúSòng poet whose seven-syllable *yuèfǔ* line is the structural ancestor of Lǐ Bái's *Xíng lù nán* 行路難; subject of [[KR4b0011]].
- [[謝朓]] — *Yǒngmíng tǐ* prosodist of the Southern Qí; subject of [[KR4b0012]] and Lǐ Bái's recurring poetic interlocutor.
- [[何遜]] — second-wave *Yǒngmíng* miniaturist; subject of [[KR4b0014]] and Dù Fǔ's openly avowed stylistic ancestor.
- [[江淹]] — late-Sòng / Qí / Liáng *fù*-master; subject of [[KR4b0015]] and [[KR4b0016]].
- [[庾信]] — the *bridge* poet of the Liáng-Northern-Zhōu transition, supreme master of medieval *piántǐ wén*; subject of [[KR4b0017]] and [[KR4b0018]].
- [[徐陵]] — Yǔ Xìn's literary twin and the *Yùtái xīnyǒng* compiler; subject of [[KR4b0019]].
- [[鄭樸]], [[虞炎]], [[樓炤]], [[徐民瞻]], [[李公煥]], [[黃省曾]], [[葉樹廉]], [[梁賓]], [[吳兆宜]], [[倪璠]] — the chain of SòngYuánMíngQīng editors whose successive selections, collations, and *jiānzhù* are the actual textual substance of the division: without their re-aggregations from *lèishū*, standard-history quotation, and *Wén yuàn yīng huá*, none of the named authors above would be readable in collected form.
- [[紀昀]], [[陸錫熊]], [[孫士毅]], [[陸費墀]] — the Qiánlóng *Sìkù* compilers (submissions 1778–1781) whose *tíyào* in this division systematically expose forgery layers (*Wǔ xiào zhuàn*, *Sìbā mù*, the misattributed Jiǎnwéndì poems in [[KR4b0013]]) and document editorial provenance with model *kǎozhèng* method.

## Topics

- **Reconstructed vs. transmitted text.** Every Hàn and early-Wèi collection in the division is a SòngMíngQīng reconstruction; the *biéjí* category here is therefore as much about *editorial archaeology* as about authorship. The signature philological move — exposing a re-editing layer via tell-tale graphic substitutions (e.g., *zhào* 照 / *zhāo* 昭 in [[KR4b0011]], the Wǔ Zétiān taboo), citation-introduction "*yuē* 曰" wrongly absorbed into the text (the Cáo Zhí *Yáo què fù* and *Biān fú fù*), or *jí zuò …* 集作 cross-reference glosses that betray a derivative recension — is the *Sìkù tíyào*'s recurring contribution.
- **Forgery, attribution, and the limits of canon.** The *Wǔ xiào zhuàn* / *Sìbā mù* deletion in [[KR4b0008]], the *Jǐn dài shū* and *yīng chū gǔ* anachronism in [[KR4b0013]], the *Xuánjī tú* WǔZétiān preface in [[KR4b0010]], the three Cáo-Cāo-praising six-syllable poems attributed to Kǒng Róng in [[KR4b0003]], and the transfer of two *mùzhì* from Yǔ Xìn to Yáng Jiōng in [[KR4b0018]] — all these establish a connected dossier on how the *Sìkù* compilers handled forgery, dating-by-anachronism, and homonym-driven misattribution.
- **From four-character verse to seven-character *yuèfǔ* to *gōngtǐ* / *Yǒngmíng* prosody.** The diachronic backbone of the division traces the development of poetic line: Yáng Xióng's *fù* and *zhēn* → the *Jiànān* four-and-five-character poets ([[KR4b0003]], [[KR4b0004]], [[KR4b0005]]) → Cáo Zhí's transitional five-character lyric → Lù Jī's *Wén fù*-anchored five-character imitations → Táo Qián's four-and-five-character pastoral → Bào Zhào's *Nǐ Xíng lù nán* seven-character *yuèfǔ* → Xiè Tiǎo's and Hé Xùn's tonal-rule poems → the *gōngtǐ* of Xú Líng and the late Yǔ Xìn's *Āi Jiāngnán fù*. Almost every signal innovation in pre-Táng prosody can be located on a single *jí* in this division.
- **Parallel prose (*piántǐ wén* 駢體文 / *sìliù wén* 四六文).** The other diachronic axis runs from Cài Yōng's stelae through Lù Jī's *fù* to Jiāng Yān's *HènBié* tradition and culminates in the *XúYǔ tǐ* 徐庾體 of [[KR4b0017]] / [[KR4b0018]] / [[KR4b0019]] — the immediate ancestor of the Táng *Sì jié* 四傑 and the canonical Táng *sìliù*.
- **Southern and northern voices.** The division registers the long migration of southern (Wú, Qí, Liáng, Chén) literary prestige into the unified northern court order — the *Èr Lù* 's move to Luòyáng, Yǔ Xìn's and Xú Líng's diplomatic captures by the Wèi and the Qí. Wilkinson §27 notes this as the formative period for the long-term southern colonization of court literary aesthetics, an arc this division documents in editorial detail.
- **Reception and the formation of authorial personae.** The SòngYuán reception layers (Sū Shì and Huáng Tíngjiān on Táo Qián; Lǐ Bái on Xiè Tiǎo; Dù Fǔ on Hé Xùn and Yǔ Xìn) are repeatedly carried forward by the editorial apparatus itself — preserved as *zǒng lùn* 總論 prefatory matter or worked into the *jiānzhù* — so that the persona of each author is in part the deposit of his later canonization. The division is therefore also a small archive of medieval-into-Sòng *reception* history.

## Timeline

- **c. 18 CE — *Yáng Zǐyún jí* 揚子雲集 [[KR4b0001]]** — the Hàn writings of [[揚雄]]; collection itself is the Wànlì 1595 Zhèng Pǔ re-aggregation, dated bracket of the recension, but the contents anchor the division's Western-Hàn floor.
- **c. 192 — *Cài Zhōngláng jí* 蔡中郎集 [[KR4b0002]]** — at the death of [[蔡邕]]; transmitted via successive recensions down to the Yōngzhèng-era (1723–1735) Chénliú cut.
- **c. 208 — *Kǒng Běihǎi jí* 孔北海集 [[KR4b0003]]** — at the death of [[孔融]]; surviving 44-piece Míng aggregation (compiled c. 1500–1644).
- **c. 232 — *Cáo Zǐjiàn jí* 曹子建集 [[KR4b0004]]** — at the death of [[曹植]]; received form is the Sòng Jiādìng 6 (1213) print, with 210 Sòng-aggregated pieces.
- **c. 262 — *Xí Zhōngsǎn jí* 嵇中散集 [[KR4b0005]]** — at the execution of [[嵇康]]; received form is the Míng Jiājìng 4 (1525) Huáng Shěngzēng collation.
- **c. 280–303 — *Lù Shìhéng wén jí* 陸士衡文集 [[KR4b0006]]** and ***Lù Shìlóng wén jí* 陸士龍文集 [[KR4b0007]]** — the writing life of the *Èr Lù* [[陸機]] and [[陸雲]]; received form is the joint Xú Mínzhān Sòng Qìngyuán 6 (1200) *Jìn èr jùn wén jí*.
- **365–427 — *Táo Yuānmíng jí* 陶淵明集 [[KR4b0008]]** — lifetime of [[陶潛]]; received form is the Xiāo Tǒng eight-*juǎn* recension, edited 526–531 in Liáng, restored by the *Sìkù* in 1781.
- **c. 380s — *Xuánjī tú shī dú fǎ* 璇璣圖詩讀法 [[KR4b0010]]** — date of the diagram itself; the exegetical compendium of Kāng Wànmín is late Wànlì (1573–1620).
- **c. 414–466 — *Bào Míngyuǎn jí* 鮑明遠集 [[KR4b0011]]** — lifetime of [[鮑照]]; received form is the Míng Zhèngdé 5 (1510) Zhū Yìngdēng print of the Yú Yán Southern-Qí (480–502) recension.
- **464–499 — *Xiè Xuānchéng jí* 謝宣城集 [[KR4b0012]]** — lifetime of [[謝朓]]; received form is Lóu Zhào's Shàoxīng 28 (1158) Xuānzhōu print.
- **444–505 — *Jiāng Wéntōng jí* 江文通集 [[KR4b0015]]** / **[[KR4b0016]]** — lifetime of [[江淹]]; received forms are the Qiánlóng 23 (1758) Liáng Bīn four-*juǎn* collation (WYG) and the ShùnzhìKāngxī Yè Shùlián ten-*juǎn* edition (SBCK).
- **c. 480–518 — *Hé Shuǐbù jí* 何水部集 [[KR4b0014]]** — lifetime of [[何遜]]; received form is the Míng Zhèngdé 12 (1517) Sōngjiāng Zhāng Hóng print.
- **501–531 — *Zhāomíng tàizǐ jí* 昭明太子集 [[KR4b0013]]** — lifetime of [[蕭統]]; received form is the Míng Yè Shàoqín Jiāxīng aggregation (compiled c. 1500–1644).
- **507–583 — *Xú Xiàomù jí jiānzhù* 徐孝穆集箋注 [[KR4b0019]]** — lifetime of [[徐陵]]; received form is the Kāngxī (1666–1722) Wú Zhàoyí *jiānzhù*, completed by Xú Wénbǐng.
- **513–581 — *Yǔ Kāifǔ jí jiānzhù* 庾開府集箋註 [[KR4b0017]]** and ***Yǔ Zǐshān jí* 庾子山集 [[KR4b0018]]** — lifetime of [[庾信]]; received forms are Wú Zhàoyí's Kāngxī *jiānzhù* (1662–1722) and Ní Fán's annotated edition of 1689.
