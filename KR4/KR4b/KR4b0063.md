# Yǔ Jiānwú jí 庾肩吾集
*Collected Works of Yu Jiangwu (Reconstructed)*
by [[庾肩吾]] (撰)

## About the work

A reconstructed collection (*jíyìběn* 輯佚本) of the literary writings of Yǔ Jiānwú 庾肩吾 (487–551 CE), Liáng 梁 court poet, literary compiler, and calligraphic critic, father of the great poet Yǔ Xìn 庾信. Organized in two *juǎn*, the fragments are consistently cited at *Shī jì* 詩紀 juǎn 80 and the *Wànhuā gǔ* 萬花谷 anthology (後卷 25) explicitly attributes a poem as "庾肩吾詩." The collection includes *yuèfǔ* 樂府 imitations, court occasional verse, *yìnglìng* 應令 (response-to-imperial-command) poems from the gatherings in the Xuányuán 玄圃 garden of Xiao Gang (Emperor Jianwen), and fragments of his *Shū pǐn* 書品 (Graded Evaluations of Calligraphers).

### Abstract

Yǔ Jiānwú 庾肩吾 (487–551 CE; *zì* Zǐshèn 子慎; CBDB: see [[庾肩吾]] for details) was a Liáng dynasty literary official from Nányáng 南陽 Xīn'yě 新野. He served as a literary official in the retinue of Xiao Gang (the future Jianwen Emperor 簡文帝), directing poetic gatherings in the Xuányuán garden and participating in the Palace Style (*gōngtǐ* 宮體) movement. He is the father of the famous poet Yǔ Xìn 庾信. See [[庾肩吾]] for full biography.

Yu Jiangwu is also known as the author of the *Shū pǐn* 書品 (Graded Evaluations of Calligraphers), a systematic ranking of 128 calligraphers from Hàn to Liáng in twelve grades modeled on Zhōng Róng's 鍾嶸 *Shīpǐn*. He compiled the *Yùtái xīnyǒng* 玉臺新詠 anthology? (No — that was Xú Líng 徐陵; Yu Jiangwu is sometimes confused with Xu Ling in this regard, but it is Xu Ling who compiled the anthology.) Yu Jiangwu's literary verse was included in Xu Ling's *Yùtái xīnyǒng*. The *Suíshū Jīngjízhì* records a *Yǔ Jiānwú jí* in twenty *juǎn*. Zhāng Pǔ 張溥 included a *Yǔ Zǐshèn jí* 庾子慎集 in the *Hàn Wèi Liùcháo bǎisān jiā jí* 漢魏六朝百三家集.
