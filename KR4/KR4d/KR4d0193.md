# Xuěxī jí 雪溪集
*Snow-Stream Collection*
by [[王銍]] (撰)

## About the work
*Xuěxī jí* 雪溪集 in 5 *juǎn* is the surviving poetic collection of Wáng Zhì 王銍 (fl. 1122–1144), Sòng *bǐjì* author (*Mòjì* 默記, *Bǔ shìér xiǎomíng lù bǔyí* 補侍兒小名錄補遺) of erudite reputation. The title takes Wáng's *hào* Xuěxī 雪溪. Originally 8 *juǎn* per Chén Zhènsūn [[KR3h0011]] and *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì*; 5 *juǎn* in the surviving WYG recension. The *Sìkù* editors place Wáng's poetic style close to Wēn Tíngyún and Lǐ Shāngyǐn (Late-Tang lyric).

### Tiyao
*Xuěxī jí* 5 *juǎn*, by Wáng Zhì of the Sòng. [Wáng] Zhì has the *Bù shìér xiǎomíng lù bǔyí* — already-recorded. This compilation is his poetry-collection. Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí* and *Sòng shǐ Yìwénzhì* both make-it 8 *juǎn*; this version is only 5 *juǎn*.

Examining the *Mòzhuāng mànlù* records [Wáng] Zhì's composed *Wáng Wénrú Qúān shī* one piece; further the *Shāncūn shī* one piece; the *Yuèyǒng* records [Wáng] Zhì's composed *Yúnménsì shī* one piece — today none-visible in the collection. Knowing what is now-transmitted is already-lost-its-3-*juǎn*; not-the-complete-fascicle.

[Wáng] Zhì's poetic-style close-to Wēn / Lǐ. Wáng Shìzhēn's *Jūyì lù* criticises his poetry as not-very skillful; but praises his appended *Lúshān sēng Kěhé héshī* one piece — appears not the *dǔlùn* (definitive judgment).

Only [Wáng] Zhì by *bóqià* (broad-comprehensive) named — yet within-the-collection's *Báitóu yín xù* did-not draw-on the *Xījīng zájì*, but drew-on Wú Jīng's *Yuèfǔ jiětí* — already missed-its-source. (Note: the *Xījīng zájì* though a false-book is before Wú Jīng — i.e. Wú's account itself draws-out from-it.) Further called the *Sòngzhì* records *Wénjūn shī* etc. — not-knowing the *Sòng shū yuèzhì*'s *Báitóu yín* really is *gǔcí*, not Wénjūn's. This is also a thousand-considerations' one-loss. *Xìn hū kǎozhèng zhī nán* (truly the difficulty of textual-research) indeed. Respectfully collated, *Qiánlóng* 42 (1777), 9th month.

### Abstract
The *Xuěxī jí* in 5 *juǎn* preserves about 5/8 of Wáng Zhì's original poetic corpus (with three identifiable losses cited from *Mòzhuāng mànlù* and *Yuèyǒng*). Wáng was a *bǐjì*-master of erudite reputation, and his *Mòjì* and *Bǔ shìér xiǎomíng lù bǔyí* are independently valuable. The *Sìkù* tíyào is unusually pointed in catching two philological errors in Wáng's own *Báitóu yín xù*: (a) the source-error (Wú Jīng's *Yuèfǔ jiětí* drawn-on instead of the original *Xījīng zájì*); (b) the attribution-error (claiming the *Sòngzhì* records Wénjūn's poem, when in fact the *Sòng shū yuèzhì* records *Báitóu yín* as anonymous). The *Sìkù* editors' generalising verdict — *kǎozhèng zhī nán* (the difficulty of philological research) — is methodologically self-aware.
