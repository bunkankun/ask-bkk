# Zhāng yòushǐ wénjí 張右史文集
*Collected Works of Right-Historian [Zhāng Lěi]*
by [[張耒]] (撰)

## About the work
*Zhāng yòushǐ wénjí* 張右史文集 (named from Zhāng Lěi 張耒 [[張耒]]'s *Zhùzuò zuǒláng* / *yòushǐ* official titles) is the SBCK reprint of the Sòng *Zhèxī cáotái* recension in 60 *juǎn* — one of the four parallel Southern-Sòng recensions of Zhāng Lěi's collection enumerated by Zhōu Zǐzhī 周紫芝 in his *Tàicāng tímǐ jí* postscript. The structure: 3 *juǎn fù*; 2 *juǎn yuèfǔ*; *gǔshī* and *jìntǐshī* through *juǎn* 28; thereafter *biǎozhuàngzházǐzòuyìshūjìxùbēizhìzàn* in standard *biéjí* genre-ordering. The KRP source files include the *Sòngshǐ Zhāng Lěi lièzhuàn* preserved at the head — providing the complete *Sòngshǐ Wényuàn zhuàn* version of Zhāng's biography. The recension is parallel to the *Sìkù* WYG 50-*juǎn* *Kēshān jí* [[KR4d0089]] but bibliographically distinct: Zhōu Zǐzhī's record clearly distinguishes the 70-*juǎn* *Zhāng yòushǐ jí* (probably this recension's antecedent) from the 100-*juǎn* *Tánjùn xiānshēng jí* (the most complete Sòng recension, lost). The 60-*juǎn* state of the present SBCK base is therefore a *zhāicuì* (selection-and-gathering) intermediate between the 70-*juǎn* and the lost 100-*juǎn* states.

### Tiyao
*The KRP source is the SBCK reprint, which does not include the Sìkù tíyào.* The work was not separately cataloged by the *Sìkù* — which received the *Kēshān jí* in 50 *juǎn* [[KR4d0089]] as the principal Zhāng Lěi *biéjí*. The *Sìkù* tíyào of *Kēshān jí* discusses the parallel recensions including the *Zhāng yòushǐ jí* explicitly.

### Abstract
*Zhāng yòushǐ wénjí* preserves Zhāng Lěi's complete extant output in a different recensional state than the *Sìkù*'s *Kēshān jí*. The 60-*juǎn* count places this recension in the upper-middle of the four-recension family enumerated by Zhōu Zǐzhī (10 / 30 / 70 / 100). For Zhāng-Lěi philological work the SBCK *Zhāng yòushǐ wénjí* is the principal control on the *Sìkù*'s 50-*juǎn* *Kēshān jí*; the modern Zhōnghuá *Zhāng Lěi jí* edition (1990) collates both. The collection's 2 *juǎn yuèfǔ* — preserving Zhāng's *Zhāng-Jí*-influenced ballads — is one of the more striking Sòng *yuèfǔ* corpora outside the strict *Jiāngxī*-school orbit. The *Sòngshǐ lièzhuàn* preserved at the head supplies the standard biographical frame: Zhāng's *Yuányòu*-coalition position, the *Sūmén* discipleship, the *Chóngníng* proscription for memorializing Sū Shì's death (the *Yǐngzhōu*-period *jǔāi xíngfú* incident is canonical), the *Chénzhōu* end-of-career, and the *Sòngshǐ* notice of his being the last surviving major *Sūxué* figure. Dating bracket: Zhāng's death (1114) to the SBCK reprint (1929).

## Other points of interest
The four-recension transmission state of Zhāng Lěi's collection — uniquely well-documented by Zhōu Zǐzhī's *Tàicāng* postscript and by Hú Yìnglín's *Bǐcóng* — makes this *biéjí* a textbook case of Sòng *biéjí* recensional fluidity. The SBCK and *Sìkù* recensions together preserve, between them, the surviving fraction of the lost 100-*juǎn* full state — but neither alone is complete.
