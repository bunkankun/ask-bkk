# Dàyǐn jūshì shījí 大隱居士詩集
*Great-Recluse Poetry Collection*
by [[鄧深]] (撰)

## About the work
*Dàyǐn jūshì shījí* 大隱居士詩集 in 2 *juǎn* is the surviving poetic collection of Dèng Shēn 鄧深 (fl. 1140s–1170s), Sòng *Tóngchuān cáo* and Maritime Trade Commissioner of Guǎngxī. The title takes Dèng's *hào* Dàyǐn 大隱. Reconstituted entirely from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* by the *Sìkù* editors. The original-attribution in *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* was *Dèng Shēnbó jí* — without specifying a name or period; the *Sìkù* editors' reconstruction of authorship from internal evidence (place-names, *zì*-references, *hào*-references) and external evidence (*Gǔ Luó zhì*, *Wànxìng tōngpǔ*) is exemplary philology. Dèng's father-and-mother loved-the-classics so deeply that the prose collection — recorded in *Gǔ Luó zhì* as 10 *juǎn* — is otherwise entirely lost.

### Tiyao
*Dàyǐn jūshì shījí* in 2 *juǎn*. Examining: the *Dàyǐn jūshì jí*'s *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* original-title is *Dèng Shēnbó jí*. Those-scattered-and-visible in the various *yùn* — gathered-and-arranged — still 2 *juǎn*. But the original-version did-not give his name, did-not give his time. Various-scholars' catalogs all did-not record this book.

Only the collection has *Yóu Luó Zhèngzhòng qìngzhǎo fēnyùn shī* — title says *Shēn dé yīzì*; the *Zhūrén jí Pínlèxuān shǎnghuā fēnyùn shī* — title says *Shēn dé bǎzì* — then his name should-be Dèng Shēn.

Examining the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* under the *Dèng* word *yùn* — citing the *Gǔ Luó zhì* says: Sòng Dèng Shēn, *zì* Zīdào, tested *Jiàoguān*; entered as *Tàifǔ chéng*; in *lúnduì* discussed Jīngxī, Húnánběi household-and-scholar-officials' folkways — Gāozōng accepted-and-praised. *Tíjǔ Guǎngxī shìbó*, for-aged-parents requested convenient-prefecture; *Zhī Héngzhōu* — at *Chálíng*, *Ānrén*, the *Xīdòng zhī dào* (mountain-valley bandits) on-hearing-of-his-name *tiēxī*. Promoted to *Tóngchuān cáo*; *yánjiǔ xūé jiǔ wéi mín hài; qǐng yú cháo, juān Chuānyǐn 47-wàn* (the salt-and-wine false-quotas long-pained the people; petitioned the court, remitting the Sìchuān *yǐn* 470,000); supervisors and county-magistrates *tānnüè* (greedy-cruel) — impeached. Yú Yǔnwén wrote-letter saying: *bùwèi qiángyù; sījì sīmín; tǐngrán zhī cāo, wèijiàn jìnbǐ*. Later with *Cháoqǐng dàfū* ended at home. Loved-residing at the eastern-lake's *shèng* (scenic spot); built pavilion called *Míngxiù*; had *wénjí* in 10 *juǎn*. Líng Dízhī's *Wànxìng tōngpǔ* also records *Dèng Shēn, Xiāngyīn person, *Shàoxīng*-period *jìnshì*; remainder same as *Gǔ Luó zhì*.

The collection has *village-people praying-for-rain-with-response-time visiting Wūshí*; further chants the *Lǐquán*, titled *Yánshí, mountain-stone, drum, presented-to Ráo Sīlǐ, parted-from-Cháng-shā-yì, ferried-across the Yùxū dòng* — these places are all close-to Héngzhōu. *Sùxiá sānyóudòng, Xiájiāng yànyù duī* — these places also all close-to Tóngchuān. With [Dèng] Shēn's office-tour *yīyī xiāngfú* (one-by-one matching) — then this collection composed by Dèng Shēn — verified.

Only Shēnbó's *zì* — different from what the *Gǔ Luó zhì* records — surely had-two *zì*. Huáng Yújì's *Qiānqǐngtáng shūmù* records there's a *Yuán Dèng Dàyǐn jūshì shījí*. The collection's *Dá shèyǒu shī* has line "*xiǎoxuān míng Dàyǐn*" (small-pavilion called Great-Recluse); further has *Zìfù Dàyǐn yīlǜ* (one regulated-poem self-composing the Great-Recluse) — matches it. But examining its poetry-line and headings, really a Sòng-person, not a Yuán-person. Suspect *Dàyǐn* is [Dèng] Shēn's *biéhào* — hence the collection accordingly named-thus. Yújì etc. by transmission-and-transcription, erred Sòng as Yuán.

Today specifically based-on-this — re-titling the collection name; while time-period still followed-from *Gǔ Luó zhì* — placed in Sòng. Respectfully collated, *Qiánlóng* 46 (1781), 12th month.

### Abstract
The *Dàyǐn jūshì shījí* in 2 *juǎn* is a *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*-recovered collection whose authorship and dynastic-attribution were both initially uncertain. The *Sìkù* editors' multi-pronged philological reconstruction — drawing on internal *zì*-tags (the *fēnyùn* poems whose *yùn* is the *Shēn* character — proof of name); on internal place-names (*Wūshí*, *Lǐquán*, *Chángshā*, *Xiájiāng* — proof of itinerary); on external evidence (*Gǔ Luó zhì*, *Wànxìng tōngpǔ*) — establishes Dèng Shēn as the author and the Sòng *Shàoxīng*–*Qiándào* period as the date-bracket. Huáng Yújì's *Qiānqǐngtáng shūmù*'s erroneous *Yuán*-attribution is corrected.

The collection is a record of Dèng's regional-administrative tour: Héngzhōu Prefect (Húnán), then Tóngchuān (Sìchuān). The Yú Yǔnwén commendation — *bùwèi qiángyù; sījì sīmín; tǐngrán zhī cāo* — preserved by the *Gǔ Luó zhì*, places Dèng among the *qīngzhèng* (clean-and-just) provincial officials of the Xiàozōng period.

## Other points of interest
- The *Sìkù* editors' philological reconstruction of authorship, *zì*, *hào*, dynasty, and itinerary from a fragmentary *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* collection is one of the more methodologically-interesting cases in the *jíbù biéjí* tíyào series.
- Distinguish from the very-different *Dàyǐn jí* [[KR4d0166]] by Lǐ Zhèngmín (also titled with *Dàyǐn*).
