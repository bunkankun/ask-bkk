# Qīngróng jūshì jí 清容居士集
*The Qīng-róng (Clear-and-Composed) Recluse-Layman Collection*
by [[袁桷]] (撰)

## About the work

The 50-*juàn* monumental collection of **Yuán Juè** 袁桷 (CBDB 10389, 1266–1327), *zì* **Bózhǎng** 伯長, *hào* **Qīngróng jūshì** 清容居士 ("Clear-Composed Recluse-Layman"), posthumous *shì* **Wénqīng** 文清, posthumously enfeoffed **Chénliú jùngōng** 陳留郡公. Native of **Qìngyuánlù** 慶元路 (modern Níngbō 寧波, Zhèjiāng). Great-grandson of the Southern-Sòng *zhī Shūmìyuàn shì* **Yuán Sháo** 袁韶 — the family one of the principal "southeastern old-households of [Sòng-Yuán] transmission of moral-and-cultural inheritance" (*dōngnán gùjiā yíxiàn* 東南故家遺獻). The SBCK base preserves the complete 50-*juàn* corpus from the Yuán-period cut.

The collection's structure is encyclopaedic, reflecting Yuán Juè's senior Hànlín career:
- **juàn 1**: 22 *fù* (rhapsodies) — including topographic-architectural pieces (*Yúnlín fù*, *Tóngbǎiguàn fù*, *Chūntáng fù*, *Rénshòutáng fù*, *Lèshuǐtú fù*, *Yújiānzhāi fù*, *Xīzhāi fù*, *Jiǔhuátái fù*, *Mòzhú fù*, *Sùxuān fù*, *Dōngwō fù*, *Róngxuān fù*, *Fùān fù*, *Yǐnjūtú fù*) — major Yuán antiquarian-and-architectural documentation
- **juàn 2**: *sāo / cí* — including the *Mǐnshì*, *Guǎngzhāo*, *Qīguān*, *Yuèlùtú cí*, *Yōurángé cí*, *Jīntí cí*, *Shítián shānfáng cí*, *Xuějiāngtú cí*, *Chuílúntíng cí*, *Jùfēng cí*, *Yúnzhōng cí*
- (subsequent *juàn*): substantial poetry of all forms; major prose (*xù*, *jì*, *bēimíng*, *mùzhìmíng*, *tíbá*, *shū*, *zhìgào*, *yuèfǔ*); *zòuyì* memorial-discussions

### Abstract

Yuán Juè (1266–1327) is the principal Yuán-period scholar-statesman of the southeastern Sòng-aristocratic family-tradition. Great-grandson of the Sòng Vice-Censor Yuán Sháo, Yuán Juè inherited the Qìngyuán (Níngbō) Yuánshì family's substantial Sòng-Yuán-transition library and ritual-cultural standing. His career trajectory traversed:

1. **Lìzé shūyuàn** 麗澤書院 at Wùzhōu (modern Jīnhuá) — *shānzhǎng* of the academy founded in honour of **Lǚ Zǔqiān** 呂祖謙 (the great Sòng Wùzhōu Confucian) — placing Yuán Juè in the direct Wùzhōu Sòng-orthodoxy lineage
2. **Hànlín Guóshǐyuàn** — recommended to the Hàn-lín-Historical Academy as *jiǎnyuèguān*; rose through senior offices to *Hànlín shìjiǎng xuéshì* 翰林侍講學士 (Hànlín Lecturer-in-Waiting Academician) — the principal late-Yuán imperial-historical-Confucian office
3. Retirement and posthumous promotion: *Cānzhī zhèngshì* of the Jiāng-Zhè *xíngshěng*; enfeoffed *Chénliújùngōng*; *shì* Wénqīng

The collection's significance:
- **The largest single Yuán-period imperial-literary corpus from the southeastern aristocratic perspective**, preserving the Sòng-Yuán transition through to the Yuán mid-period (1290–1327). 50 *juàn*.
- **Foundational mid-Yuán Hànlín state-papers**, particularly *bēimíng* and *mùzhìmíng* for the Yuán imperial and Sòng-remnant elite — including Yuán Juè's *mùzhìmíng* for Ān Xī [[安熙]] (preserved in 蘇天爵's *Yuán wénlèi*)
- **Major antiquarian-and-architectural *fù* corpus** (*juàn* 1)
- **Substantial gazetteer-historiographical context** for Qìngyuán / Sìmíng — Yuán Juè composed the *Yánjiā Sìmíng zhì* [[KR2k0025]] in 1320 at the request of *zǒngguǎn* Mǎ Zé 馬澤

CBDB 10389 (1266–1327 firm). *Yuánshǐ* j. 172 (Yuán Juè biography). His lost works (per Sū Tiānjué's *mùzhìmíng*) include the *Yì shuō* and *Chūnqiū shuō*.

## Translations and research

- *Yuán-shǐ* j. 172 (Yuán Juè biography) — the standard biography.
- Hok-lam Chan, "Chinese Official Historiography at the Yüan Court" — discusses Yuán Juè's role in Yuán imperial historiography.
- Various Sòng-Yuán transition studies treat Yuán Juè substantially.

## Other points of interest

The Yuánshì of Qìngyuán is one of the principal Sòng-Yuán transition family-archives — the family preserved its Sòng-aristocratic ritual-cultural identity through three generations of Yuán imperial service, maintaining the Wùzhōu Sòng-Confucian library and learning traditions while adapting to Yuán imperial-Confucian institutionality. Yuán Juè's editorial role in *Yánjiā Sìmíng zhì* (1320) makes him one of the principal Yuán-period gazetteer-compilers.
