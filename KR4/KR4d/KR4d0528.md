# Chúnbáizhāi lèigǎo 純白齋類稿
*The Chún-bái-zhāi (Pure-White Studio) Classified Drafts*
by [[胡助]] (撰)

## About the work
A 20-*juǎn* + 2 *juǎn fùlù* truncated recension of **Hú Zhù** 胡助 (b. 1278), originally 30 *juǎn* self-edited; lost in transmission for two centuries; re-assembled from scattered fragments by his sixth-generation descendant Hú Huái 胡淮 in mid-Zhèng-dé. The present recension preserves 1 *juǎn fù* + 16 *juǎn shī* + 3 *juǎn záwén* + 2 *juǎn fùlù* (contemporary *zèng* verse to Hú). Yú Jí's *bá* on the *Shàngjīng jìxíng jí* and Dèng Wényuán / Wú Chéng's *bá* on *Luánpō xiǎolù* and *Shēng xué jìqì wén* are documented in the *Sìkù tíyào* as lost — their pieces presumably among the 10 *juǎn* dropped from the original.

### Tiyao
*Chúnbáizhāi lèigǎo*, 20 *juǎn* + 2 *juǎn fùlù*. By Hú Zhù of the Yuán. Zhù *zì* Lǚxìn, *yīzì* Gǔyú. Native of Wūzhōu Dōngyáng. Initially raised by *Màocái*; *Jiànkānglù rúxué xuélù*; through *Měihuà shūyuàn shānzhǎng*, *Wēnzhōulù rúxué jiàoshòu*; by second recommendation again *Hànlín Guóshǐyuàn biānxiūguān*; retired with *Chéngxìnláng*, *Tàicháng bóshì*. The time was Zhìzhèng 5 (1345). This collection is what Zhù himself edited — originally 30 *juǎn*. Years long, *cánquē shīcì* (incomplete and out-of-order). In Míng Zhèngdé, his 6th-gen. descendant Huái gathered the scattered, re-edited it — only *fù* 1 *juǎn*, *shī* 16 *juǎn*, *záwén* 3 *juǎn* + *fùlù* 2 *juǎn* of contemporary *zèng* verse, retaining the *Chúnbáizhāi lèigǎo* title — *juǎn* count reduced by 1/3, no longer the original. Yú Jí once *bá* his *Shàngjīng jìxíng jí* — said his *Lóngmén yǐhòu shī yóu jiā*; *jīn yǐ sàn rù jí zhōng*. Dèng Wényuán and Wú Chéng each *bá* his *Luánpō xiǎolù* and *Shēng xué jìqì wén* — *cǐ běn bùzǎi* — must be in the lost 10 *juǎn*. Hú Zhù's *shīwén* are *píngyì jìnrén*, no *shēnzhàn qíjǐng* (deep, soak-deep, strange-and-alert) thinking; but also no *zhīlí pòsuì* (fragmentation and breakage) defect. Probably not lost as *zhōngshēng* (middle voice). Wú Chéng characterized his verse as: "*chūnlán zhuóyá — xiàzhú hántuò — lùzī yǔxǐ zhī yú — zhuózhuó yōumèi — juānjuān jìnghǎo*" — *xíngróng guòdāng* (over-stating the description) — *fǎn bù xiào qí pǐngé* (instead, doesn't match his *pǐngé*). Respectfully collated.

### Abstract
*Chúnbáizhāi lèigǎo* preserves the principal corpus of a Wūzhōu literary figure of the same Dōngyáng circle as the *Jīnhuá sìxiānshēng*. Hú is now best known for his preface to Wú Lái [[吳萊]]'s *Yuānyǐng jí*, supplying the "*dūlùn*" line on *qiǎnlòu* vs. *hóngbó*. The collection's progressive truncation (30 → 20 + 2 *juǎn*; 1/3 reduction during Zhèng-dé-era Hú-Huái re-edit) is a typical case of Yuán-Míng small-author transmission decay. Composition window: from Hú's earliest preserved compositions (post-c. 1310) to his Zhìzhèng 5 (1345) retirement.
