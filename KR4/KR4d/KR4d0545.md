# Qiáo Wú jí 僑吳集
*The Sojourning-in-Wú Collection*
by [[鄭元祐]] (撰), re-edited by [[張習]] (重編)

## About the work
A 12-*juǎn* (6 *juǎn wén* + 6 *juǎn shī*) re-edited collection of **Zhèng Yuányòu** 鄭元祐 (1292–1364). Title from Zhèng's 40-year *liúyù* (in transit-residence) at Píngjiāng (Wú = Sūzhōu) after relocation from Qiántáng. *Suìchāng shānrén* by *biéhào*. Original Yuán-Míng recension consisted of *Suìchāng shānrén jí* + *Qiáo Wú jí* with much overlap; the Hóngzhì *bǐngchén* (1496) *Zhāng Xí* 張習 *zhòngdìng* combined and selected the "精純者" 12-*juǎn* recension as the present text. The collection's principal anomaly — the *Zhāng Décháng shū* claiming a *jùn* post — is corrected by Sū Dànián's *mùzhì* and Lú Xióng's *Sūzhōu fǔzhì*, both attesting Zhèng held only *xiàoguān* (school-officer) posts.

### Tiyao
*Qiáo Wú jí*, 12 *juǎn*. By Zhèng Yuányòu of the Yuán. Yuányòu has *Suìchāng zálù* already in the catalog. Yuányòu's family-seat Suìchāng — relocated to Qiántáng — and *liúyù* at Píngjiāng 40 years — the longest period — so the *jí*'s name *Qiáo Wú* (Sojourning in Wú). Actually compositions at Hángzhōu are also in it; takes from the bulk. The original *jí* was Yuányòu's late-years' *zìdìng* — given to **Xiè Huī** 謝徽. The present version's back has Hóngzhì *bǐngchén* (1496) Zhāng Xí *bá*: "*Yuányòu had original Suìchāng shānrén jí*, with *Qiáo Wú jí* — much *fánwú chóngchū* (cluttered and repeated) — so I read both and obtained the *shīwén jīngchún zhě* — combined as 12 *juǎn* — still named *Qiáo Wú jí* — for woodblock printing." So this version is Xí *chóngdìng* — not Yuányòu's hand-edited version. *Wén* 6 *juǎn*; *shī* 6 *juǎn*. Among them *Zhāng Décháng shū* — has "*pú zàn jùn wúbǔ — cháng yí Léilǐ zhī yǔ*" — but Sū Dànián's *mùzhì* and Lú Xióng's *Sūzhōu fǔzhì* all say Yuányòu was by *dàfǔ* (provincial) recommended; twice *jiàoguān* — does not say also held other posts — does not match Yuányòu's *shìlǚ* (career). Could it be a composition for someone else, slipping the marking? — His prose *pō shūdàng yǒu qì* (rather free and powerful); verse also *cānggǔ*. Since Yuányòu was born *Zhìyuán mò* (end of Zhìyuán) — still saw the *Xiánchún* (Sòng-late) *yílǎo*; in the middle also saw Yú Jí et al. — got their *xùlùn*. End-years exchanged with — also all Gù Āyīng, Ní Zàn, Zhāng Yǔ et al. — *hù xiāng xūnrǎn* — his *qìyùn* not the same — also has *zìlái* (its source). Respectfully collated.

### Abstract
*Qiáo Wú jí* preserves the principal monument of a Suìchāng-Qiántáng-Sūzhōu literatus whose life spans the *Xiánchún yílǎo* (Sòng-late old men he met) → Yú Jí mid-Yuán → Gù Āyīng / Ní Zàn / Zhāng Yǔ late-Yuán arc — an unusually broad intellectual reach. Zhèng's role is principally as a Sūzhōu literary node — his *Yuán* circle includes the principal late-Yuán Wúzhōng figures. The Hóng-zhì-era Zhāng Xí re-editing (1496) is a typical Míng pruning of an Yuán *biéjí*. The catalog meta gives an editor function 重編 for Zhāng Xí — *zhòngbiān* exactly fits the 1496 *zhòngdìng*. Composition window: 1320 (mature work begins) to 1364 (death).
