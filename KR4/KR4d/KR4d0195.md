# Dōnglái shījí 東萊詩集
*Dōng-lái Master Poetry Collection*
by [[呂本中]] (撰)

## About the work
*Dōnglái xiānshēng shījí* 東萊先生詩集 in 20 *juǎn* is the poetic collection of Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 (1084–1145), the *Dà Dōnglái* (Great Dōnglái — i.e. the earlier of the two famous Dōng-lái-named Lǚ figures, distinguishing him from his great-grand-cousin Lǚ Zǔqiān). The title takes Lǚ's *hào*. Compiled by Lǚ's literary friend Shěn Gōngyǎ 沈公雅 (son of Shěn Zōngshī 沈宗師) at his Wújùn 吳郡 prefectural seat in *Qiándào yuánnián* (1165), with colophon by Zēng Jǐ 曾幾 [[曾幾]] (also born 1084, contemporary). The 20 *juǎn* are organised chronologically (the colophon: *cìdì suìyuè wéi èrshí tōng* — sequenced by years into 20 fascicles). Lǚ's principal historical importance — beyond this collection — is as the compiler of the *Jiāngxī shīpài tú* (the foundational text of the *Jiāngxī* school's self-canonisation), the *Tóngméng xùn* (KR3a series), and the *Zǐwēi shīhuà* (KR4h series).

### Tiyao
*The local source for [[KR4d0195]] is the SBCK reproduction of the* Dōnglái xiānshēng shījí, *with the Zēng Jǐ colophon preserved at the head.* Date is *Qiándào yuánnián* (1165) per the Zēng Jǐ colophon.

The *Sìkù* tíyào is reproduced at the end of WYG. Translated:

*Dōnglái shījí* in 20 *juǎn*, by Lǚ Běnzhōng of the Sòng. [Lǚ] Běnzhōng, *zì* Jūrén, *hào* Dōnglái xiānshēng. Career detailed in *Sòng shǐ* biography. The collection has at-the-end Zēng Jǐ's *bá* — saying: at *Qiándào yuánnián* (1165), Shěn Gōngyǎ from *Shàngshūláng* promoted to prefect at this commandery (= Wújùn). In leisure-day collected [Lǚ's] poetry — none-omitted; sequenced years into 20 fascicles; cut blocks placed in the prefectural studio. Surely [Lǚ's] friendship with the Shěn-family was deep — and-so the editing was complete.

[Zēng] Jǐ also was-known to [Lǚ] Běnzhōng. Hence Gōngyǎ requested-it to write the colophon. [Zēng] Jǐ self-recalled: with [Lǚ] both born in *Yuánfēng jiǎzǐ* (1084); further mutually-related by-marriage; *yǎ xiānghǎo* (elegantly-friends). At *Shàoxīng xīnhài* (1131), [Zēng] Jǐ was at Liǔzhōu, in *bìdì* (refuge) — etc.

[Lǚ] Běnzhōng's poetic-rule learned from his great-grandfather Lǚ Gōngzhù; the family-learning *yǔ Yuányòu zhūjūnzǐ wéi yīxí* (with the *Yuányòu* gentlemen one-fold). Compiled the *Jiāngxī shīpài tú* — listed Huáng Tíngjiān as *zǔ*; descending listed cohort-of-poets. (Hán Jū annoyed at the listing; Chén Yǔyì later than the cohort, hence not-included.) Together with his own poetic style, *géfǎ jìn yú Yùzhāng* (rhythm-rule close to Huáng Tíngjiān) — the standard *Jiāngxī*-school-stamp.

### Abstract
The 20-*juǎn* collection is the standard recension of Lǚ Běnzhōng's poetic corpus, edited by his literary friend Shěn Gōngyǎ at *Qiándào* 1 (1165) with Zēng Jǐ's colophon — twenty years after Lǚ's death. The chronological organisation is unusually careful for a Sòng poetic collection.

The collection is foundational for understanding the *Jiāngxī* school's self-articulation: Lǚ was the compiler of the *Jiāngxī shīpài tú*, and his own verse exemplifies the school's signature *géfǎ* (rhythmic-rule) of Dù Fǔ → Huáng Tíngjiān → *Jiāngxī* descent. Read together with [[KR4h0008]] (his *Zǐwēi shīhuà*) and the *Tóngméng xùn* (KR3a series) for the full Lǚ corpus.

CBDB id 10703 confirms 1084–1145.

## Translations and research
- 曾幾 *bá* (1165) — preserved at the end of SBCK.
- *Sòng shǐ* j. 376 — Lǚ Běn-zhōng biography.
- 朱熹, *Lǚ Dōng-lái nián-pǔ* — preserves the Lǚ family lineage in *Dào-xué* perspective.
- *Sòng yuán xué-àn* — Lǚ family lineage.
- *No dedicated Western-language monograph on the collection located.* Lǚ is treated in surveys of *Jiāng-xī*-school poetics and *Yuán-yòu*-circle survival.

## Other points of interest
- The *Sìkù* editors' tíyào of [[KR4d0153]] (Chén Yǔyì) discusses Chén's exclusion from the *Jiāngxī shīpài tú* — Chén being too young to have been included by Lǚ. The discussion provides the standard reading of the *tú*'s scope.
