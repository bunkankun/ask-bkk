# Biéběn Hán wén kǎoyì 別本韓文考異
*Alternative Edition: Hán Yù Collection with Critical Notes*
by [[韓愈]] (撰), [[朱熹]] (考異), [[王伯大]] (重編)

## About the work
*Biéběn Hán wén kǎoyì* 別本韓文考異 in 40 *juǎn* — also widely transmitted as *Zhū Wéngōng jiào Chánglí xiānshēng wén jí* 朱文公校昌黎先生文集 (the SBCK title) — is the **consolidated** form of the Zhū Xī HánYù critical apparatus: Wáng Bódà 王伯大's late-Sòng (*Chúnyòu* / *Bǎoyòu* period, *ca.* 1234–1244) editorial digest in which Zhū Xī's [[朱熹]] *kǎoyì* notes (originally separately printed as the 10-*juǎn* *Yuánběn Hán jí kǎoyì* = [[KR4c0043]]) are dispersed back into the body of the Hán Yù collection underneath the relevant lines. The 40-*juǎn* arrangement matches the standard Tang-period Lǐ Hàn 李漢 [[李漢]] table-of-contents form. Wáng's editorial consolidation made the *kǎoyì* far more usable as a reading text and as a result became the dominant SòngYuánMíng circulation of the HánYù corpus, almost completely displacing Zhū's separate *yuánběn* (= [[KR4c0043]]) for several centuries.

The SBCK file opens with Zhū Xī's substantial *Hán wén kǎoyì xù* 韓文考異序 — itself a major piece of Sòng critical bibliography, surveying the *Háng běn* 杭本 (*Xiángfú* period, *ca.* 1010), *Shǔ běn* 蜀本 (*Tiānxǐ*, *ca.* 1020), and *Jiāyòu Shǔ běn* 嘉祐蜀本 (1057), the *Guǎngé běn* 館閣本, and the principles by which Fāng Sōngqīng's [[方崧卿]] *Jǔzhèng* should be retained or corrected.

### Tiyao
*No tíyào in source.* The [[KR4c0044]] file is the SBCK base, which preserves Zhū Xī's preface but no Sìkù tíyào. The Sìkù WYG 40-*juǎn* tíyào (V1073.3) survives in the Zinbun digital *Sìkù tíyào*; it pairs the entry with [[KR4c0043]] and notes Wáng Bódà's editorial consolidation.

### Abstract
The Wáng Bódà recension is the principal pre-modern reading edition of Hán Yù — it consolidates the *jiàokān* foundation of Fāng Sōngqīng (= [[KR4c0042]]) and the critical refinement of Zhū Xī (= [[KR4c0043]]) into a single 40-*juǎn* working text, replicating the original Lǐ Hàn arrangement but enriched with Zhū's interlinear *kǎoyì*. From the late Sòng through the Míng this was the standard form of Hán Yù's collection on the *kējǔ* curriculum and in private libraries.

The Sòng *Tángshū yìwén zhì* records *Hán Chánglí jí* 韓昌黎集 in 40 *juǎn*; the 40-*juǎn* form is the original Lǐ Hàn (Hán Yù's son-in-law and his original editor in 824) configuration, preserved without break across the entire SòngYuánMíngQīng tradition.

Wáng Bódà (cbdbId 20099 in CBDB; meta gives no dates; CBDB has *fl.* 1214 and death 1253) was a *Wúzhōu* 婺州 *Yìwū* 義烏 native (modern *Yìwū* in central Zhèjiāng); *jìnshì* of *Jiātài* 2 (1202); successively *zhōngshū shèrén* 中書舍人 and *Hànlín xuéshì* 翰林學士 in the late Lǐzōng court. He died in 1253. The editorial consolidation of Zhū Xī's *kǎoyì* into the HánYù body text was undertaken in his middle years.

For Hán Yù himself, see the existing person note [[韓愈]]; for the collateral and downstream HánYù commentary tradition see [[KR4c0042]], [[KR4c0043]], [[KR4c0045]], [[KR4c0046]], [[KR4c0047]].

## Other points of interest
The SòngYuán dominance of Wáng Bódà's consolidated form is the principal reason Zhū Xī's separate *yuánběn* fell out of circulation; the *Sìkù*-editorial decision to print both [[KR4c0043]] and [[KR4c0044]] is therefore unusual and reflects the editors' commitment to recovering lost original forms even where a downstream consolidation had been the practical reading text.
