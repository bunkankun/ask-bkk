# Liǔ Hédōng jí 柳河東集
*Collected Works of Liǔ of Hé-dōng (Liǔ Zōng-yuán)*
by [[柳宗元]] (撰), [[韓醇]] (詁訓)

## About the work
*Gǔxùn Liǔ xiānshēng wénjí* 詁訓柳先生文集 in 45 *juǎn* (with appended *Wàijí* 外集 in 2 *juǎn* and *Xīnbiān wàijí* 新編外集 in 1 *juǎn*) — generally known as *Liǔ Hédōng jí* 柳河東集 — is the foundational Sòng annotated edition of Liǔ Zōngyuán [[柳宗元]]'s collected works, with running commentary (*gǔxùn* 詁訓 — glosses and explanations) by **Hán Chún** 韓醇 (*zì* Zhòngshào 仲韶, *Línqióng* 臨邛 / Sìchuān man, fl. *ca.* 1170–1180; not the same person as Hán Yù's son-in-law Hán Yù 韓郁). The 45-*juǎn* form descends from Liǔ Yǔxī's [[KR4c0051 author]] Liú Yǔxī 劉禹錫's original Tang-period editorial close (820s) — Liú Yǔxī, Liǔ Zōngyuán's lifelong friend, edited the collection at his death in 819 and supplied the substantial preface preserved here. Of the four Sòng circulating recensions identified by the Sìkù tíyào (a 33-*juǎn* *Yuánfú* period print, the *Zēng* prime-minister family copy, the *Yàn Yuánxiàn* family copy, and the present 45-*juǎn* — derived from the *Mù Xiū* 穆脩 family copy and identified in the Sòng tradition as Liú Yǔxī's original 45-*juǎn*), the present version is the most authoritative.

### Tiyao
*Gǔxùn Liǔ xiānshēng wénjí* in 45 *juǎn*, with *Wàijí* in 2 *juǎn* and *Xīnbiān wàijí* in 1 *juǎn* — composed by Liǔ Zōngyuán of the Táng, with *yīnshì* (phonetic and meaning notes) by Hán Chún of the Sòng. Chún's *zì* was Zhòngshào, a *Línqióng* man; his beginning and end are unknown. Zōngyuán's collection was edited by Liú Yǔxī. Subsequent *juǎnmù* additions and deletions by the Sòng produced four parallel recensions: (1) 33 *juǎn*, *Yuánfú* period (1098–1100) capital print; (2) the Zēng [Gǒng?] family copy; (3) the Yàn Yuánxiàn 晏元獻 (= Yàn Shū 晏殊) family copy; (4) the present 45-*juǎn* version, said to come from the Mù Xiū 穆脩 family and to be Liú Yǔxī's original recension.

Per Chén Zhènsūn's *Shūlù jiětí*: Liú Yǔxī's preface says he edited the prose into 32 *tōng* (sections), with the [Hán Yù] *zhì* and *jìwén* appended at the end of the first *tōng*; current circulating texts are all 45 *juǎn* and do not include Hán Yù's *zhìwén* — therefore not the original. Examining the present text: Liú Yǔxī's preface here gives 45 *tōng* not 32 — disagreeing with Chén's account; either later editors revised the preface to match the 45-*juǎn* circulating version, or the figure is not certain. ### Abstract
The 45-*juǎn* form is the principal Sòng recension of the Liǔ Zōngyuán corpus. Hán Chún's commentary represents the earlier of the two surviving major Sòng commentary traditions (the other being the *Liǔ Hédōng jí zhù* by Tóng Zōngshuō [[童宗說]], Zhāng Dūnyí 張敦頤, and Pān Wěi 潘緯 = [[KR4c0049]], and behind both, the *Wǔbǎijiā zhù* anthology of Wèi Zhòngjǔ = [[KR4c0050]]). Hán Chún's notes are principally philological — *yīnshì* phonetic glosses and source-tracing for allusions — without the philosophical interpretation found in the later Sòng *jízhù* tradition.

For Liǔ Zōngyuán himself see the existing person note [[柳宗元]]; for the parallel Liǔ commentary editions see [[KR4c0049]] and [[KR4c0050]]; for the Hán Yù counterpart commentary tradition see [[KR4c0042]] – [[KR4c0047]].

## Other points of interest
Hán Chún was also responsible for an annotated edition of Hán Yù's collection (a *Hán Chánglí jí jiàozhèng* mentioned in Sòng catalogs but not surviving in WYG); his pairing of the Hán-Liǔ commentary work places him in the broader Sòng *fùgǔ* commemorative scholarship of the *Lóngxìng* / *Qiándào* generation.
