# Hónglóu Zhēnmèng 紅樓真夢
*The True Dream of the Red Chamber*
by [[郭則]]

## About the work

*Hónglóu Zhēnmèng* 紅樓真夢 is a 64-chapter Qīng-dynasty sequel novel (*xùshū* 續書) to *Hónglóu Mèng* 紅樓夢 [[KR4k0131]], composed by Guō Zé 郭則 (pen name Yúncōng Huāyǐn 雲淙花隱). The text is distinguished among *Hónglóu Mèng* sequels by its markedly literary preface, written in ornate parallel prose (*piánwén* 駢文), which signals an author of considerable classical education. The work claims to offer the "true dream" (*zhēnmèng* 真夢) as opposed to other continuations, which the author dismisses as missing the spirit of the original.

### Abstract

Guō Zé 郭則, writing as Yúncōng Huāyǐn 雲淙花隱 at his studio *Yìpǔ Zhúxuān* 逸圃竹軒, composed a self-preface (*zìxù* 自序) dated "己卯小春" (tenth month of a *jǐmǎo* year). The two *jǐmǎo* years in the plausible Qīng range are Jiājìng 24 (1819) and Guāngxù 5 (1879). The CBDB record for Guō Zé 郭則 (id 150978) provides no dates. Given the sophisticated treatment of *Hónglóu Mèng* textual scholarship in the preface — noting the *Zhīyān Zhāi* 脂硯齋 commentated manuscripts, discussing debates about "索賈辨甄" (decoding Jiǎ and Zhēn), and criticizing rivals who "附會梅村贊佛之詩" (attribute the novel to Wú Wěiyè 吳偉業's Buddhist poetry) — the work appears to engage with mature *Hóngxué* scholarship, which suggests the later date (1879) is more probable, though 1819 cannot be excluded. Both dates are provided as the range.

The self-preface is a literary tour de force, deploying dense classical allusion to position the author's project as a response to personal and historical suffering (the preface mentions "板蕩" upheaval and "黍離" lament, possibly alluding to the Taiping destruction or other late-Qīng calamities). The author signals that he seeks to "抑釵揚黛" (demote Bǎochāi and elevate Dàiyù) and to "索賈辨甄" (distinguish the real Jiǎ from the false Zhēn) — positioning the work as a corrective to misreadings. The narrative continues from the 120-chapter ending, with Jiǎ Yǔcūn 賈雨村 meeting Zhēn Shìyǐn 甄士隱 at the ferry crossing (*Juémí Dùkǒu* 覺迷渡口), and traces the further adventures of surviving characters across 64 chapters, ending with chapter 64 "慶慈壽碧落會團欒 聚仙眷紅樓結因果."
