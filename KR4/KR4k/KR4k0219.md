# Sān Chūn Mèng 三春夢
*Dream of Three Springs*
by [[佚名]] (anonymous)

## About the work

*Sān Chūn Mèng* 三春夢 is an anonymous late Qīng novel in approximately 33 *huí* 回, set against the Ming-Qīng dynastic transition in Guangdong. This entry (KR4k0219) is a duplicate or near-duplicate of [[KR4k0215]], with an identical line count of 37,634 lines in the Kanripo edition. It should be read in conjunction with that entry.

### Abstract

This entry is almost certainly a second digitization of the same text as [[KR4k0215]], or a scan of a closely related exemplar. The title, anonymous authorship, line count (37,634 — identical to [[KR4k0215]]), and content all match. The Kanripo corpus catalog records both as separate items under slightly different reference keys (KR4k0215: `San_chun_meng`; KR4k0219: `San1_chun1_meng4`), which may reflect two different source copies or simply a cataloging duplicate.

For full description of the work — its preface lamenting the fall of the Ming, its cast of historical and fictional characters in the Cháozhōu 潮州 campaigns, and its late Qīng Han-nationalist framing — see the entry for [[KR4k0215]].

A collation of the two Kanripo copies to determine whether they represent the same or variant texts is recommended before any critical use of the Kanripo digitization.
