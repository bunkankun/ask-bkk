# Xiāngyáng Qíjiù Zhuàn 襄陽耆舊傳
*Biographies of Elders of Xiangyang*
by [[習鑿齒]]

## About the work

*Xiāngyáng Qíjiù Zhuàn* 襄陽耆舊傳 (also known as *Xiāngyáng Qíjiù Jì* 襄陽耆舊記) is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a local biographical collection recording notable elders (*qíjiù* 耆舊) of Xiāngyáng 襄陽 by [[習鑿齒]], the Eastern Jin historian and native of the city. At over 700 lines, it is one of the more substantial of the regional *qíjiù zhuàn* reconstructions.

### Abstract

Xí Zuòchǐ (fl. mid-4th c. CE), the author of the *Hàn Jìn Chūnqiū* 漢晉春秋 ([[KR4k0346]]), was Xiāngyáng's most famous son and its principal historian. His *Xiāngyáng Qíjiù Zhuàn* (recorded in the *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography in 5 *juǎn*) was a local history of his hometown's notable figures. The fragments include biographical notices on officials, scholars, and recluses from the Hàn through the Eastern Jin periods who were associated with Xiāngyáng — which, as a strategic city on the middle Hàn River, produced a disproportionate number of notable figures in the Three Kingdoms and Jin periods. The work complements the anonymous *Xiāngyáng Jì* 襄陽記 ([[KR4k0393]]) and provides an insider's view of the region by its most important historian.
