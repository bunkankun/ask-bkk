# Sòngdài Shíbā Cháo Gōngtíng Yànshǐ 宋代十八朝宮廷豔史
*Scandalous History of the Eighteen Reigns of the Song Imperial Court (Part 1)*
by [[李逸侯]] (撰)

## About the work

KR4k0320 preserves an alternate copy of Part 1 only (approximately 50 chapters, to the end of the Sòng Tài Zǔ reign narrative) of *Sòngdài Shíbā Cháo Gōngtíng Yànshǐ* 宋代十八朝宮廷豔史 by [[李逸侯]] (Lǐ Yìhóu). The combined edition (both Part 1 and Part 2) is preserved at [[KR4k0319]]. The content is identical to the Part 1 portion of KR4k0319 and represents a separate published edition of only the first installment of the novel. See [[KR4k0319]] for the full entry.
