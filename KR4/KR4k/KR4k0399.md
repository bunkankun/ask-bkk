# Xiàozǐ Zhuàn 孝子傳 (Wáng Xīn)
*Biographies of Filial Sons*, by Wang Xin
by [[王歆]]

## About the work

*Xiàozǐ Zhuàn* 孝子傳 by Wáng Xīn 王歆 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a lost collection of filial son biographies. The surviving fragments are very few — only a handful of lines — but represent a separate compilation from those of better-known authors in the genre such as [[蕭廣濟]] ([[KR4k0396]]).

### Abstract

Wáng Xīn is otherwise obscure; the *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography records a *Xiàozǐ Zhuàn* attributed to him. The surviving fragments include brief tales of filial devotion, recovered from the *Tàipíng Yùlǎn* 太平御覽 and other standard sources.
