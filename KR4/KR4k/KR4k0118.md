# Fúshēng Liù Jì 浮生六記
*Six Records of a Floating Life*
by [[沈復]] (撰)

## About the work

*Fúshēng Liù Jì* 浮生六記 (*Six Records of a Floating Life*) is the celebrated personal memoir by Shěn Fù 沈復 (courtesy name Sānbái 三白; 1763–after 1809) of Sūzhōu 蘇州. This entry represents a second catalog record in the Kanripo corpus for the same text, differing from [[KR4k0116]] only in the catalog reference tag (*Fusheng_liuji* here vs. *Fu2sheng1_liu4_ji4* for KR4k0116). Comparison of the two digital files confirms they are byte-for-byte identical. For full description see [[KR4k0116]].

### Abstract

See [[KR4k0116]] for the complete abstract. This Kanripo entry ([[KR4k0118]]) is a duplicate catalog record for the same textual witness. Both entries preserve the four surviving *juǎn* of *Fúshēng Liù Jì*:

1. *Guīfáng Jì Lè* 閨房記樂 (Joys of the Boudoir)
2. *Xiánqíng Jì Qù* 閑情記趣 (Pleasures of Leisure)
3. *Kǎnkě Jì Chóu* 坎坷記愁 (Sorrows of Hardship)
4. *Làng Yóu Jì Kuài* 浪遊記快 (Joys of Roaming)

The two remaining *juǎn* referenced in the title are absent from all known witnesses, as in [[KR4k0116]].
