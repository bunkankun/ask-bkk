# Xiàozǐ Zhuàn 孝子傳 (Yú Pányòu)
*Biographies of Filial Sons*, by Yu Panyou
by [[虞盤佑]]

## About the work

*Xiàozǐ Zhuàn* 孝子傳 by Yú Pányòu 虞盤佑 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a minor lost collection of filial son biographies. The surviving fragments are very few — about 50 lines — representing a separate compilation from the larger and better-known *xiàozǐ zhuàn* collections in this division.

*No tiyao found in source. This is a jíyìběn reconstruction.*

### Abstract

Yú Pányòu's name (sometimes written 虞盤佑 or 虞般佑) appears in the *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography as the author of a *Xiàozǐ Zhuàn*. He was likely a Jin-dynasty figure from the prominent Yú 虞 clan of Kuàijī 會稽 (one of many historians and scholars from that lineage). The surviving fragments include brief filial piety narratives, some overlapping with those in the collections of [[蕭廣濟]] ([[KR4k0396]]) and [[宋躬]] ([[KR4k0402]]), suggesting that these compilers drew on a common pool of circulating exempla.
