# Chǔguó Xiānxián Zhuàn 楚國先賢傳
*Biographies of Former Worthies of Chu*
anonymous

## About the work

*Chǔguó Xiānxián Zhuàn* 楚國先賢傳 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a lost regional biographical collection recording "former worthies" (*xiānxián* 先賢) of the ancient Chǔ 楚 region (roughly modern Húběi and Húnán). The title invokes the Warring States kingdom of Chǔ, but the biographies likely covered Hàn and post-Hàn figures from the central Yangtze region.

### Abstract

The Chǔ region had a distinctive cultural identity dating to the Warring States period, with its own poetic tradition (the *Chǔcí* 楚辭), religious practices, and scholarly lineages. The *Chǔguó Xiānxián Zhuàn* celebrated this regional heritage through biographies of local worthies. The surviving fragments, about 220 lines, include notices on Chǔ-region officials and scholars recognized for their conduct, learning, or service. The text was lost after the Táng.
