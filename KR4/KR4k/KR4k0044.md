# Dàtóng Jìshì 大同紀事
*Record of Events at Datong*
by [[韓邦奇]]

## About the work

A brief narrative account of the *Jiājìng jiǎshēn* 嘉靖甲申 (1524) mutiny at Dàtóng 大同 (in northern Shanxi), written by [[韓邦奇]] Hán Bāngqí (1479–1555; CBDB id 34652), a mid-Míng official and Confucian scholar. The text opens with the preface phrase: "Jiājìng jiǎshēn Dàtóng zhī biàn, Dūyùshǐ Zhāng Wénjǐn xúnfǔ dìfāng" 嘉靖甲申大同之變，都禦史張文錦巡撫地方 — establishing that the account concerns the Dàtóng mutiny that occurred while the Surveillance Commissioner Zhāng Wénjǐn 張文錦 was serving as Provincial Governor (*xúnfǔ* 巡撫). Hán Bāngqí appears to have had direct knowledge of these events through his official career.

### Abstract

韓邦奇 Hán Bāngqí (1479–1555; CBDB id 34652; see [[韓邦奇]] person note) was a *jìnshì* of 1502, serving in a series of provincial and central government posts including eventually Minister of War at Nánjīng. His scholarly work primarily concerns the *Yì* (Book of Changes), music theory, and mathematics.

The *Dàtóng Jìshì* is a minor work recording specific events during the Dàtóng garrison mutiny (*Dàtóng biàn* 大同變) of the Jiājìng jiǎshēn year (1524). The Dàtóng garrison area (*Dàtóng wèi* 大同衛) was a strategically critical military zone on the northern frontier, and mutinies and factional disputes among the garrison soldiers were a recurring problem in the mid-Míng. The account's value is as a firsthand or near-contemporary record of these frontier military events, which are otherwise only summarily treated in official history.

CBDB notes (id 34652): "MS, 201.5317-5319" for the *Míng shǐ* biography; death year also given as Jiājìng 36 (1557) in some sources per the Academia Sinica note.
