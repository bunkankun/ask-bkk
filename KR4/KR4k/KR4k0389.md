# Sānshí Guó Chūnqiū 三十國春秋 (Wǔ Mǐnzhī)
*Spring and Autumn of the Thirty States*, by Wu Minzhi
by [[武敏之]]

## About the work

*Sānshí Guó Chūnqiū* 三十國春秋 by Wǔ Mǐnzhī 武敏之 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a lost comprehensive history of the Sixteen Kingdoms and related regimes, organized in the annalistic *chūnqiū* 春秋 (spring and autumn annals) format. The "Thirty States" encompassed not only the Sixteen Kingdoms proper but also smaller or more transient polities of the period.

### Abstract

The *Sānshí Guó Chūnqiū* was a popular genre title in the Southern Dynasties; it was used by at least two authors for comprehensive histories of the Sixteen Kingdoms period. Wǔ Mǐnzhī's version is the less well-known of the two. The *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography records his *Sānshí Guó Chūnqiū* in 1 *juǎn*, suggesting it was a concise treatment. The more famous 30-*juǎn* version was by Xiāo Fāngděng 蕭方等 ([[KR4k0390]]). Wǔ Mǐnzhī's surviving fragments are modest, drawn from Táng and Sòng citations, and cover events from the early fourth through the early fifth centuries across the fragmented political landscape of North China.
