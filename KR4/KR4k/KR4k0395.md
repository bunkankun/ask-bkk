# Yìdū Qíjiù Zhuàn 益都耆舊傳
*Biographies of Elders of Yidu*
anonymous

## About the work

*Yìdū Qíjiù Zhuàn* 益都耆舊傳 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a lost local biographical collection recording notable elders of Yìzhōu 益州 (the ancient name for the Sìchuān basin, with its capital at Chéngdū 成都). The *qíjiù zhuàn* 耆舊傳 genre was particularly well-developed in the Sìchuān region, which had a strong local historiographical tradition.

### Abstract

Yìzhōu / Yìdū was one of the most culturally distinctive regions of the Hàn empire, home to famous literary figures such as Sīmǎ Xiàngrú 司馬相如 and Yáng Xióng 揚雄. The *Yìdū Qíjiù Zhuàn* was part of a broader Shǔ 蜀 local historiographical tradition that included Cháng Qú's 常璩 *Huáyáng Guó Zhì* 華陽國志 and Chén Shòu's 陳壽 *Yìbù Qíjiù Zhuàn* 益部耆舊傳 (a separate, fuller work recorded in the *Suí Shū*). The surviving fragments are very few — brief notices of local officials and scholars — recovered from Táng and Sòng citations.
