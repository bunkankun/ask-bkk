# Gǔ Shǐ Kǎo Xù 古史考序
*Preface to the Investigation of Ancient History*
by [[孫星衍]]; compilation by [[章宗源]]

## About the work

*Gǔ Shǐ Kǎo Xù* 古史考序 is [[孫星衍]]'s preface to the *Gǔ Shǐ Kǎo* 古史考 (Investigation of Ancient History), a jíyìběn compilation of pre-Qín and Hàn historical fragments assembled by his late friend [[章宗源]] (Zhāng Zōngyuán, styled Féngzhī 逢之, jǔrén 舉人 of the *Qiánlóng bǐngwǔ* year, 1786). The preface is dated the 21st day of the 6th month of *Jiāqìng* 11 (July 1806) and was written at Sūn Xīngyǎn's office in Āndé 安德, Shāndōng.

### Abstract

Sūn Xīngyǎn's preface provides a biographical sketch of Zhāng Zōngyuán, a devoted jíyìběn scholar who spent his life recovering lost texts listed in the *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography. Zhāng's work — filling over ten baskets (*qiè* 笈) — was nearly sold to a provincial official (Bì Dūbù 畢督部) but the transaction was interrupted by military disturbances in Húběi. Zhāng later fell into misfortune: he became involved with a corrupt Buddhist monk at Guǎnghuì Monastery 廣慧寺 whose bribery case implicated him; his examination degree was stripped by a powerful minister, and he died poor and dispirited after his students' employer burned the books he had assigned them to compile. Most of his manuscripts passed to a collector named Yè Jìwén 葉繼雯; a fraction (about 10–20%) reached Sūn Xīngyǎn, who edited and published the *Gǔ Shǐ Kǎo* as a memorial to his friend.

Sūn Xīngyǎn (1753–1818) was one of the most important Qīng-period philologists and bibliographers, a *jìnshì* of 1786 associated with the Yǎnzhōu 兗州 school of Han Learning. His preface is a significant source for the social history of jíyìběn scholarship in the Qiánlóng-Jiāqìng era, documenting the precarious conditions under which the texts reconstructed in this division were produced.
