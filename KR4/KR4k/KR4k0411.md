# Zǐtóng Shìnǚ Zhì 梓潼士女志
*Record of Gentlemen and Women of Zitong*
by [[常璩]]

## About the work

*Zǐtóng Shìnǚ Zhì* 梓潼士女志 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a local biographical record of Zǐtóng 梓潼 Commandery (in northern Sìchuān) by [[常璩]], the Eastern Jin author of the *Huáyáng Guó Zhì* 華陽國志. At over 700 lines, it preserves significant regional biographical material from the Shu-Han through Eastern Jin periods.

### Abstract

Cháng Qú's *Zǐtóng Shìnǚ Zhì* was structured as a paired record of notable local men (*shì* 士) and women (*nǚ* 女), a format that he also employed in the *Huáyáng Guó Zhì*. Zǐtóng, lying on the route between Chéngdū and the Hànzhōng basin, was an important region in the Chéng-Hàn and Eastern Jin periods. The fragments preserve biographical notices on Zǐtóng natives recognized for official service, scholarly achievement, moral conduct, or (in the case of women) exemplary chastity and domestic virtue. The text was lost after the Táng; the fragments were recovered from Táng and Sòng citations.
