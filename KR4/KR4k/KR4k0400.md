# Xiàozǐ Zhuàn 孝子傳 (Zhōu Jǐngshì)
*Biographies of Filial Sons*, by Zhou Jingshi
by [[周景式]]

## About the work

*Xiàozǐ Zhuàn* 孝子傳 by Zhōu Jǐngshì 周景式 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a lost collection of filial son biographies. The surviving fragments are modest, about 100 lines, but preserve several distinctive tales not found in the other *xiàozǐ zhuàn* collections in this division.

### Tiyao

*No tiyao found in source. This is a jíyìběn reconstruction.*

### Abstract

Zhōu Jǐngshì is otherwise unknown; the *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography records a *Xiàozǐ Zhuàn* attributed to him. The fragments include tales of filial children displaying extraordinary devotion and receiving supernatural recognition for their virtue. They were recovered from the *Tàipíng Yùlǎn* 太平御覽 and the *Yìwén Lèijù* 藝文類聚. The text was lost after the Táng.
