# Yàn Zhì 燕志
*Gazette of Yan*
by [[高閭]]

## About the work

*Yàn Zhì* 燕志 (also titled *Gāo Lǘ Yàn Zhì* 高閭燕志) is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a lost gazette or chronicle of the Yàn 燕 states by Gāo Lǘ 高閭 (styled Yànshì 彥士), a Northern Wei scholar-official. The surviving fragments are brief but were compiled by an important historian of the Northern Dynasties.

### Abstract

Gāo Lǘ was a prominent Northern Wei official and writer, a native of Yúyáng 漁陽 who served under several Northern Wei emperors. He compiled the *Yàn Zhì* as a record of the Mùróng 慕容 clan's Yàn states, which the Northern Wei had destroyed and absorbed. The *Suí Shū* 隋書 bibliography records the *Yàn Zhì* in 10 *juǎn*. Gāo Lǘ wrote from the perspective of the conquering power — the Northern Wei regarded the Mùróng enterprise as a predecessor that they had legitimately superseded. The surviving fragments, drawn from Táng-Sòng citations, preserve only a few lines. The text was lost after the Táng.
