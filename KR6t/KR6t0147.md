# Mìzōng jiàoxiàng chāo 祕宗教相鈔
*Compendium on the Doctrinal Aspect of the Esoteric School*
by [[重譽]] (撰)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle **doctrinal-disputation compendium** of the principal *kemmitsu* questions of medieval Shingon, organized as **forty-eight topical decisions** (*tíjué* 題決). The work is the principal scholastic-systematic statement of mid-late-Heian Shingon doctrinal-disputational consolidation by **Chōyo** 重譽.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Chōyo. The subtitle of the opening fascicle states explicitly: "*Concerning the Esoteric School's expounded matters, we hereby probe forty-eight topical decisions, the doubts within them.*" (就祕密宗所説且探四十八條題決其中疑慮)

**Date.** Within Chōyo's career, conventionally **12th century**.

**Content.** The work is organized as **forty-eight topical decisions**, each treating one principal doctrinal-disputational question of the medieval Shingon tradition. The opening topics:

1. **大日經所説三瑜祇行料簡第一** — *The expounded three-yogic practice of the Mahāvairocanasūtra: ascertainment, no. 1.* (with the marginal note: "*this gate ends with question-and-answer further ascertaining the abolition / establishment of [stages 8] *one-path-unconditioned* and [stage 9] *ultimate no-own-nature*, together with the practitioner's transition-from-apparent-to-esoteric stage-division*").
2. **大日經三瑜祇行顯密料簡第二** — *The* Mahāvairocanasūtra's *three-yogic practice: apparent-esoteric ascertainment, no. 2.*
3. **百六十心本惑唯取五鈍簡五利第三** — *The 160-mind fundamental delusions: taking only the five sluggish [delusions] and rejecting the five sharp, no. 3.*
4. (proceeding through to topic 48).

Each topic is treated in detailed Q&A format with extensive citation of canonical sources, *Mahāvairocana-sūtra* and *Vajraśekhara-sūtra* passages, Yixing's *Yìshì*, Kūkai's foundational treatises, and the medieval sub-commentaries of Saisen and others. The topical organization is **non-arbitrary**: it traces the progression of *kemmitsu* doctrinal articulation from the *Mahāvairocana-sūtra* foundational doctrines through the contested stage-progression questions, to the culminating *sokushinjōbutsu* and *kanjō* topics.

**Significance.** The *Mìzōng jiàoxiàng chāo* is one of the principal **mid-late-Heian Shingon scholastic-disputational compendia**, complementing Saisen's line-by-line sub-commentary corpus. The forty-eight-topic organization established a **canonical question-set** that subsequent medieval Shingon scholastic literature continued to address. The work is studied alongside Saisen's sub-commentaries in the medieval and Edo Shingon scholastic curriculum.
