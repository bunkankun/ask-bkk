# Sān-mèi-liú kǒu-chuán jí 三昧流口傳集
*Collected Oral Transmissions of the Sanmai-ryū*
by [[良祐]] (記)

## About the work

A two-fascicle compendium of the **Sanmai-ryū** 三昧流 (Samādhi-lineage) *kuden* (oral-transmission) corpus, compiled by **Ryōyū** 良祐 (active late 12th century). The Sanmai-ryū was a sub-lineage of the late-Heian Tendai-esoteric Kawa-ryū tradition, in the line of descent from Chōen 長宴 ([[長宴]]).

### Abstract

**Authorship.** The work bears no opening colophon, but the catalog meta and *Taishō* edition attribute it to Ryōyū consistently. The text opens directly with a numbered topic-index.

**Date.** Within Ryōyū's lifespan, conventionally **mid- to late 12th century**.

**Content.** The opening *mokuroku* table of contents lists the work's principal topics:

1. **Shén-gōng** 神供 — deity-offering ritual.
2. **Sì-zhǒng màn-tú-luó** 四種曼荼羅 — the four maṇḍala types (great, *samaya*, *dharma*, *karma*).
3. **Sān-zhǒng xī-dì** 三種悉地 — the three categories of *siddhi* attainment.
4. **Zūn-shèng jìng-shuǐ** 尊勝淨水 — Uṣṇīṣa-vijayā purified-water rite.
5. **Zūn-shèng jīn-gāng bō-luó-mì guān** 尊勝金剛波羅蜜觀 — Uṣṇīṣa-vijayā vajra-pāramitā contemplation.
6. **Chì-shèng-guāng màn-dá-luó** 熾盛光曼陀羅 — Tejaḥprabha maṇḍala (the blazing-light deity).
7. (further topics through to item 78, including individual deity rites, *homa* procedure, and ritual-procedural questions).

Each topic is presented in *kuden* format: a short procedural-doctrinal exposition followed by Ryōyū's record of his teacher's oral teaching, often with alternative readings, variant procedural details, and contrary opinions from other Taimitsu sub-lineages noted for comparison.

**Significance.** The work is one of the principal **Sanmai-ryū lineage statements** preserved in the *Taishō* corpus, and is a key documentary source for medieval Kawa-ryū diversification — alongside Chōen's [[KR6t0108]] *Sì-shí tiè jué* and Chōgō's *Sōji-shō* ([[KR6t0112]] of [[澄豪]]). The work was studied within the medieval Hiei-zan Taimitsu tradition and is cited in the encyclopedic [[KR6t0110]] *Keiran-shūyōshū*.
