# Jīn-gāng-jiè cì-dì shēng-qǐ 金剛界次第生起
*Structural Genesis of the Vajradhātu Liturgical Sequence*
by [[最圓]]

## About the work

A single-fascicle **structural-analytical exposition** of the Vajradhātu 金剛界 (Vajra-realm) liturgical sequence, by **Saien** 最圓 (1048–1102), Tendai esoteric master of the Kawa-ryū lineage. The work is the Vajradhātu counterpart to Kakuchō's Garbhadhātu *shōki* ([[KR6t0103]]) and develops the same analytical framework.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** The catalog meta attributes the work to **Saien** 最圓. The text opens directly without a colophon, stating the work's analytical premise:

> "*Now, when the Tathāgata established his teaching, there must be a sequence. The classifications in the various sūtras into chapters are not without reason. Even today, when we practice the mantra method, there must be a sequence. The intention of the sequence — how could it lack a structural-genesis? Therefore, I privately set this down for the essentials of recitation-discipline.*"

**Date.** Within Saien's lifespan (**1048–1102 CE**).

**Content.** The work proceeds step-by-step through the Vajradhātu sequence, beginning with the **preparatory contemplations** (*fáng-zhōng hù-jìng, guān-niàn* 房中護淨觀念 — chamber-purification and contemplation), the **entry into the practice hall** with **ground-purification** (淨地) followed by **body-purification** (淨身), and the **opening contemplation of the Buddhas filling all space** (觀於遍虚空佛). Each procedural step is explained by its *genetic* rationale: *if X were not done, then Y would not be possible*, framing the liturgical sequence as a logically necessary causal chain rather than an arbitrary ritual order.

The work is part of the **canonical mid-Heian Yokawa / Kawa-ryū Taimitsu** literature on procedural analysis. It is short (one fascicle) but tightly argued, and was studied alongside Kakuchō's Garbhadhātu *shōki* as a paired pedagogical introduction to the two principal Tendai esoteric liturgies.

In *Taishō* this work appears at T75n2406A; the next entry (T75n2406B) is the anonymous *Jǐn-dā jīn-gāng-jiè yí-wèn zǒng-lái shí tiáo* ([[KR6t0106]]), a ten-point Q&A on Vajradhātu liturgical doubts, treated as an editorial appendix to Saien's *shōki*.
