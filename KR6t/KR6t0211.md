# Zhōng-yuàn-liú shì 中院流事
*Matters of the Chū'in Lineage*
by [[宥快]]; recorded by [[成雄]] (記)

## About the work

A single-fascicle **Shingon *kuden* compendium** preserving the **Chū'in-ryū** 中院流 oral teachings transmitted by **Yūkai** 宥快 (1345–1416) to his disciple **Jōyū** 成雄. The text opens with the explicit attribution: "*Yūkai's oral [teachings]; Jōyū has recorded them.*" (宥快口成雄記之). Together with the companion [[KR6t0212]] *Chū'in-ryū daiji kikigaki*, the work is the principal documentary record of Yūkai's late-career codification of the Chū'in-ryū transmission.

### Abstract

Authorship and dating: composition window **ca. 1380–1416**, between Yūkai's reception of the Chū'in-ryū transmission ([[KR6t0209]], 1377) and his death. The text records: "*The master said: 'Of his school, although there is the* hōkō *transmission, we should take the orthodox to be those whose succession through Raishin 賴審 of the Kosaikō Boin 胝母院 [= ?] and Genkai 玄海 etc. is unbroken; their great-matter transmission is the principal one.*" Subsequent transmission records are signed: "**Yūsei** 宥勢 of *Nyoirin-ji* recorded the work."

Doctrinal content: the work opens with the **fundamental lineage matters** (許可) — the *kyoka* / *permission*, including the *dharma-transmission ordination-seal* (傳法印信), the *Yoga-Vehicle ordination-seal* in two layers (this being also called the *Tenchō ordination-seal*), the *third-layer ordination-seal*, the *Vairocana method's ordination-seal*, the *Great-Stūpa great-matter ordination-seal*, and the *post-Sōjō oral teachings* (recorded by Genkai). The text then sets out the **Chū'in three-great-matters** (中院三箇大事):
1. *Vaiśravaṇa great-matter*.
2. *Third-layer great-matter*.
3. *Nanzan (Kōyasan) Eight-Petal Peak great-matter*.

These are noted as appearing in Genkai's record. The work then traces a sub-transmission: "*The* Shin-Nanin Niren *received this transmission: the* permission-transmission-and-dharma-seal *which has two layers, and the* third-layer *seal — this is the Yoga-Vehicle great-matter, i.e. the* Tenchō *ordination-seal.*"

A particularly interesting closing note discusses the **textual transmission** of Kūkai's *Shōryō-shū* 性靈集: "*The last three fascicles of the* Shōryō-shū *are said to have been collected by Shinzei [眞濟]; but in the present age these are lost — those three fascicles are gone. The present version's last three fascicles are those collected by Saisen [濟暹]; this is why the last three fascicles are abundant.*" (一。性靈集末三卷ハ眞濟聚給ト見タリ。今程世ニ絶テ此三卷無之。今有之濟暹聚之云云仍末三卷多之也). This is a historically valuable note on the textual history of the *Shōryō-shū*.

The work is one of the principal mid-Muromachi documents of Kōyasan Chū'in-ryū transmission practice.
