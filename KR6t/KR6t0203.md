# Shí-guī chāo 實歸鈔
*Compendium of the Return-to-the-Real*
by [[深賢]] (集)

## About the work

A single-fascicle **Shingon ritual compendium** by **Jingen** 深賢 (also read *Jinken*; fl. early-to-mid 13th c.), a Kamakura-period master of the **Daigo-ji / Tōji** transmission line. CANWWW records the compilation-role as 集 ("collected"). The work draws together specialised *bessen-hō* materials from across the principal late-Heian and early-Kamakura masters, with particular attention to the orally-transmitted variants distinguishing the different *ryū*.

### Abstract

Authorship and dating: Jingen (CANWWW AUT01126) is recorded as a senior disciple in the early-13th-century Daigo tradition; composition window **ca. 1200–1262**. Two Edo-period copyists' colophons preserve the manuscript history: **Kan'ei 3 (1626), 2nd month, an auspicious day**, copied by **Arichika** 有以 — "*the master's autograph having been laboured upon by another hand*"; and **Kyōhō 7 (1722), 8th month, last day**, copied by **Vajra Buddha-disciple Chōei** 長詠 (age 33). An earlier internal colophon notes: "*Heard from the dharma-mouth, transmitted into the inner-treasury intention — passing through the various schools*" (殊口授聞奧藏意樂傳諸流而已), signed "**Saifu Taii** 西阜泰畏 (age 38)."

Doctrinal content: the table of contents covers over **fifty individual rituals** across the standard Shingon pantheon: from the principal Buddhas (*Amitābha*, *Mahā-Buddhoṣṇīṣa*, *Aparimitāyus*, the Six-syllable Sūtra, the Cintāmaṇi-cakra) and the *Vidyārājas* and *vajra-deities*, through the *protector-deva* rites (*Vaiśravaṇa* / *Tamonten*, *Daikoku-ten*, *Śrīmahādevī*, *Gaṇeśa*, *Twelve Devas*, *Varuṇa*, *Fifteen-Child-Sūtra*, *Ucchuṣma*), to highly specialised procedures (the *Karaṇḍa-mudrā Sūtra*, the *Mahā-Maṅgala* relic-rite, the *Snake-Liberation* method, the *Five-Esoteric* contemplation, the *Five-Ākāśagarbha*, the *preta-feeding* rite, the *Hidden Treasury method*, the *Water-Joy* offering). The work concludes with general-procedure topics — *separation from karmic activity* (離作業), *small-altar venue* (小壇所), *dwelling-stabilization* (鎭壇), *various empowerments* (諸加持), *two-realm presentation procedures* (兩界等授).

The text is a typical mid-Kamakura specialist's working compendium and reflects the increasingly fragmented and *kuden*-dependent nature of post-Hōgen Shingon scholarship.
