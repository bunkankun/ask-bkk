# Suàn-hǎi xiáng-shuō 算海詳說
 *Detailed Exposition of the Sea of Mathematics*
by [[李長茂]] (撰)

## About the work

[[李長茂]] Lǐ Chángmào's early-Qīng practical-arithmetic compendium in 11 *juàn*, in the lineage of [[程大位]] Chéng Dàwèi's [[KR3fc027]] *Suàn-fǎ tǒng-zōng* (1592). The work serves the early-Qīng merchant-apprentice and elementary-school audience, providing a comprehensive but accessible treatment of the standard arithmetic curriculum.

### Abstract

The work belongs to the popular-mathematical genre of the late-Kāng-xī / early-Yōng-zhèng era, parallel to but more elementary than the imperially-sponsored *Shù-lǐ jīng-yùn* 數理精蘊 (1722) and the more theoretically-sophisticated productions of the *Méng-yǎng zhāi* mathematicians. Lǐ Chángmào's title *Suàn-hǎi* (Sea of Mathematics) emphasizes the encyclopedic-reference character of the work, *xiáng-shuō* (Detailed Exposition) emphasizes its pedagogical mission.

The eleven *juàn* cover the standard topical sequence inherited from the [[KR3fc027]] *Tǒng-zōng* tradition: (1–2) basic abacus arithmetic and the multiplication-division mnemonics; (3–6) the rules of three and false position, applied to commercial calculation; (7–9) area-and-volume calculations and the right-triangle; (10) root extraction; (11) a closing miscellany of "curious" problems including indeterminate-equation and recreational mathematics.

The work's principal substantive innovation beyond the *Tǒng-zōng* lineage is the integration of selected European-derived mathematical content from the post-1607 Jesuit-translation corpus. Lǐ Chángmào incorporates the proportional-divider (*bǐ-lì-guī*) procedures from the Aleni 1630 translation, the basic Euclidean-geometric foundations from Ricci/Xú Guāngqǐ's *Jǐ-hé yuán-běn*, and some elements of the *jiè-gēn-fāng* (borrowed-root algebraic) method newly introduced under the Kāng-xī court. The integration is at the elementary-pedagogical level rather than the theoretical level; Lǐ Chángmào's intended audience is the working merchant rather than the imperial mathematician.

The work was repeatedly reprinted in the eighteenth century and served as the principal practical-arithmetic textbook in many provincial schools through the mid-Qīng. It is one of the better documentary records of the level of elementary-mathematical instruction in the early-Qīng provincial-merchant environment.

Dating: *NotBefore* set conservatively at 1690 (late-Kāng-xī productive period for Lǐ Chángmào); *notAfter* at 1730 (mid-Yōng-zhèng), the outer-bound for the work's plausible composition.
