# Bǔ-xiū Sòng Zhàn-tiān shù 補修宋占天術
 *Supplementary Reconstruction of the Sòng Zhàn-tiān Calendar*
by [[李銳]] (述并注)

## About the work

[[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's (1768–1817) supplementary evidential reconstruction in 1 *juàn* of the *Zhàn-tiān lì* 占天曆, one of the short-lived supplementary calendrical systems of the Northern Sòng. The *Zhàn-tiān* was designed in Chóng-níng 崇寧 4 (1106) by Yáo Shùn-fǔ 姚舜輔 and used briefly before being superseded by the *Jì-yuán lì* 紀元曆 of the same Yáo Shùn-fǔ in Chóng-níng 5 (1107).

### Abstract

The *Zhàn-tiān lì* is one of the more obscure Northern-Sòng calendars: it was promulgated only briefly, and the same designer immediately produced the superseding *Jì-yuán lì*. The procedural detail in the *Sòng-shǐ Lǜ-lì zhì* 宋史律曆志 is correspondingly thinner than for the major Sòng calendars. Lǐ Ruì's 1-*juàn* *bǔ-xiū* 補修 reconstruction is precisely a supplementary reconstruction — recovering the algorithmic content as far as the abbreviated source permits, with cross-reference to the *Fèng-yuán* (cf. [[KR3fc054]]) and the *Jì-yuán* for parallel evidence.

This is the fifth and final member of Lǐ Ruì's calendrical reconstruction sequence ([[KR3fc051]]–[[KR3fc055]]). The selection of texts is informative: rather than reconstructing the major and well-documented Sòng systems (*Tǒng-tiān*, *Jì-yuán*, *Shòu-shí*), Lǐ Ruì chose the less-well-preserved supplementary systems where his philological-mathematical method could make the most distinctive contribution. The pair *Fèng-yuán* / *Zhàn-tiān* fills two specific gaps in the Sòng calendrical record.

Dating: composed during Lǐ Ruì's mature productive period in [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán's patronage circle. *notBefore* 1795; *notAfter* 1817.
