# Bǔ-xiū Sòng Fèng-yuán shù 補修宋奉元術
 *Supplementary Reconstruction of the Sòng Fèng-yuán Calendar*
by [[李銳]] (述并注)

## About the work

[[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's (1768–1817) supplementary evidential reconstruction in 1 *juàn* of the *Fèng-yuán lì* 奉元曆, the official calendar of the Northern Sòng promulgated under Shén-zōng 神宗 in Xī-níng 熙寧 7 (1074), used for nineteen years until replaced by Wáng Sōng's 王宋 *Guān-tiān lì* 觀天曆 in 1093.

### Abstract

The *Fèng-yuán lì* was designed by Wèi Pǔ 衛朴 and a commission of imperial astronomers in response to the discovery of significant errors in the preceding Northern-Sòng calendrical system. It was adopted in Xī-níng 7 (1074). The system was technically conservative — incremental refinement of the *Chóng-tiān lì* 崇天曆 of 1024 — but introduced revised values for the fundamental mean-motion constants and revised eclipse procedures.

The *Fèng-yuán lì* survives in summary form in the *Sòng-shǐ Lǜ-lì zhì* 宋史律曆志, but the algorithmic procedures are less fully preserved than those of the leading Sòng calendars (the *Tǒng-tiān* 統天 of Yáng Zhōng-fǔ 楊忠輔 and the *Shòu-shí* 授時 of [[郭守敬]] Guō Shǒujìng). Lǐ Ruì's 1-*juàn* reconstruction is explicitly *bǔ-xiū* 補修 ("supplementary reconstruction") — filling out the algorithmic detail from the abbreviated *Sòng-shǐ* presentation, drawing on parallel evidence from the preceding *Chóng-tiān* and the following *Guān-tiān* systems. The companion 1-*juàn* reconstruction of the *Zhàn-tiān lì* 占天曆 (in [[KR3fc055]]) does the same for one of the supplementary Northern-Sòng calendars.

The pair is the Sòng tail of Lǐ Ruì's five-work calendrical sequence ([[KR3fc051]]–[[KR3fc055]]), extending his evidential-philological method from the Hàn calendrical foundations forward to the high-period Sòng systems. The systematic Qián-Jiā recovery of the historical Chinese calendrical tradition that the sequence represents was the technical-mathematical complement to the broader Qián-Jiā evidential project on the classical and historical canons.

Dating: composed during Lǐ Ruì's mature productive period in [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán's patronage circle. *notBefore* 1795; *notAfter* 1817.
