# KR3fc 算法 — Suàn-fǎ (Mathematical Methods)

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3fc 算法 (*Suàn-fǎ*) is the pure-mathematics sub-division of KR3f 天文算法類 (*Tiānwén suànfǎ lèi*), parallel to the asterism-and-observational [[KR3fa]] 天文 and the calendrical-method [[KR3fb]] 曆法. Where the Sìkù 算書之屬 sub-category is represented inside the imperial canon by the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* 算經十書 corpus and the Kāngxī–Qiánlóng compendia at [[KR3f0032]]–[[KR3f0056]], KR3fc is the Kyoto-University supplementary collection (refid prefixes `KX03-07-057` through `KX03-07-124`, plus `KXDLJ003*` items) drawn from the *Zhōngguó kēxué jìshù diǎnjí tōnghuì* 中國科學技術典籍通匯 and cognate cóng-shū reprints, gathering the indigenous Chinese mathematical literature in its full historical depth — including the Sòng-Yuán algebraic peak whose tradition the *Sìkù* could only partially document, and the dense Qīng *kǎo-zhèng* 考證 recovery effort that returned that tradition to active use after a four-century hiatus. Wilkinson §38.17 frames this corpus as one of the four principal scientific traditions of pre-modern China; the present sub-division is its most direct documentary witness.

The chronological span runs from the Hàn-Wèi-Jìn *Jiǔ-zhāng* 九章 redaction with [[劉徽]] Liú Huī's *Jǐng-yuán* 4 (263) commentary [[KR3fc001]] through the Suí-Táng curricular consolidation under [[李淳風]] Lǐ Chúnfēng (656), the Sòng-Yuán algebraic flowering of [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo (1247), [[李冶]] Lǐ Yě (1248), [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī (1261–75), and [[朱世傑]] Zhū Shìjié (1299–1303), the Yuán-Míng popular-arithmetic codification culminating in [[程大位]] Chéng Dàwèi's *Suàn-fǎ tǒng-zōng* 算法統宗 (1592, in the WYG line at [[KR3f0045]]; the cognate [[KR3fc027]] is the Kyoto recension), the early-Jesuit *Gōu-gǔ* 勾股 / *jǐ-hé* 幾何 encounter under [[徐光啟]] Xú Guāngqǐ (1606–33) and [[陳藎謨]] Chén Jìnmó (c. 1640), the long Qīng *kǎo-zhèng* recovery of the indigenous tradition centered on [[戴震]] Dài Zhèn's reconstitution of the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* from the *Yǒng-lè dàdiǎn* and the Yáng-zhōu–Sū-zhōu mathematical school of [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún, [[汪萊]] Wāng Lái, and [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì (c. 1795–1820), and culminating in the late-Qīng synthesis of [[李善蘭]] Lǐ Shànlán (1845–67), where the Sòng-Yuán *tiān-yuán* tradition meets the differential calculus on the verge of full Westernization. The Wilkinson §38.17.2 list of "the ten" — *Zhōubì*, *Jiǔzhāng*, *Sūnzǐ*, *Hǎidǎo*, *Wǔcáo*, *Wǔjīng suàn-shù*, *Xiàhóu Yáng*, *Zhāng Qiūjiàn*, *Jí-gǔ*, with *Shù-shù jì-yí* as auxiliary — is the indispensable framing concept; nearly half the works in KR3fc are commentaries, reconstructions, or extensions of texts in this list.

The intellectual posture of the division as a whole is captured by the studio names of its principal Qīng authors: [[戴震]] Dài Zhèn's *Wù'ān* 勿菴 (in his Anhui school), [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún's *Lǐ-táng* 里堂 (whence [[KR3fc042]] *Lǐ-táng xué-suàn jì* 里堂學算記), [[汪萊]] Wāng Lái's *Héng-zhāi* 衡齋 ([[KR3fc049]] *Héng-zhāi suàn-xué*), and above all [[李善蘭]] Lǐ Shànlán's *Zé-gǔ-xī zhāi* 則古昔齋 ([[KR3fc078]]) — "the studio for following the ancients". The studio names declare the *kǎo-zhèng* programme: to recover the lost Chinese mathematical tradition (Sòng-Yuán algebra; the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū*; the ancient calendrical systems) on its own terms, not as a tributary of European mathematics but as a coequal achievement to be brought back into productive scholarly use.

## Important texts and text clusters

The 78 cataloged works (the sequence runs `KR3fc001`–`KR3fc080` with `KR3fc049a` and `KR3fc077` absent in the present corpus) divide into nine readily recognisable clusters.

**Suàn-jīng shí-shū textual cluster.** The Kyoto recensions of the ancient mathematical classics, complementing the WYG / *Sìkù* line of [[KR3f0032]]–[[KR3f0040]].
- *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-jīng* 九章筭經 [[KR3fc001]] — the Liú Huī-and-Lǐ Chúnfēng *Jiǔ-zhāng* in its five-*juàn* Táng-canonical recension under the older orthography *suàn* 筭; the head of the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* curriculum.
- *Zhāng Qiūjiàn suàn-jīng* 張丘建算經 [[KR3fc004]] — the Northern-Wèi text with [[李淳風]]'s *zhù-shì*, principal source for indeterminate problems (the "hundred fowls" problem).
- *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng* — three Qīng commentaries on Wáng Xiàotōng's 王孝通 Táng text: [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's *Kǎo-zhù* [[KR3fc005]], [[張敦仁]] Zhāng Dūnrén's *Xì-cǎo* [[KR3fc006]], and [[劉衡]] Liú Héng's *Bǔ-zhù* [[KR3fc007]] — a textbook example of the dense Qīng commentarial work on a single short Táng classic.

**Lǐ Huáng's recovery program.** [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's (?–1812) magisterial xì-cǎo tú-shuō series, posthumously completed and edited by [[沈欽裴]] Shěn Qīnpèi, supplies the standard early-19th-century *kǎo-zhèng* readings for three of the canonical Ten.
- *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-shù xì-cǎo tú-shuō* 九章算術細草圖說 [[KR3fc002]] — the now-standard reading edition of the *Jiǔ-zhāng*.
- *Hǎi-dǎo suàn-jīng xì-cǎo tú-shuō* 海島算經細草圖說 [[KR3fc003]] — the parallel reading edition of Liú Huī's *Hǎi-dǎo* 海島.
- *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng kǎo-zhù* 緝古算經考注 [[KR3fc005]] — the parallel reading edition of Wáng Xiàotōng's *Jí-gǔ*.

**Sòng-Yuán algebraic peak.** The four monuments of medieval Chinese algebra, all preserved in the present sub-division.
- *Shù-shū jiǔ-zhāng* 數書九章 [[KR3fc008]] (1247) — [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo's 18-*juàn* / 81-problem treatise. Contains the first complete algorithmic statement of the Chinese Remainder Theorem (*Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù* 大衍求一術), systematic *tiān-yuán* algebraic equations, and a Horner-type method for polynomial evaluation. The Kyoto edition preserves the correct title against the *Sìkù*'s corrupted *Shù-xué jiǔ-zhāng* at [[KR3f0041]].
- *Cè-yuán hǎi-jìng xì-cǎo* 測圓海鏡細草 [[KR3fc015]] (1248) — [[李冶]] Lǐ Yě's 16-*juàn* treatise systematizing the *tiān-yuán-yī* 立天元一 method for setting up and solving polynomial equations in a single unknown, framed around the inscribed-circle problems of the right triangle. The parallel WYG entry is at [[KR3f0042]].
- *Suàn-xué qǐ-méng* 算學啟蒙 [[KR3fc016]] (1299) and *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn* 四元玉鑑 [[KR3fc017]] (1303) — [[朱世傑]] Zhū Shìjié's two principal works. The *Qǐ-méng* is the elementary primer that consolidated the entire Sòng-Yuán curriculum in 259 problems; the *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn* extends *tiān-yuán-yī* to systems of up to four unknowns (*tiān, dì, rén, wù*) via a rod-numeral two-dimensional notation, with the *xiāo-fǎ* 消法 elimination procedure (anticipating Bézout's resultant by four centuries) and the *zhāo-chā* 招差 finite-difference interpolation (anticipating Newton). The *Qǐ-méng* was lost in China after the Míng, recovered in 1839 from a Korean reprint and re-edited as [[KR3fc020]]; the *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn* itself was preserved continuously but became unintelligible by 1700 and was re-elucidated in the 1830s as [[KR3fc018]]–[[KR3fc019]].
- *Yáng Huī suàn-fǎ* 楊輝算法 [[KR3fc010]] and the cognate *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ* 詳解九章算法 [[KR3fc011]] and *Suàn-fǎ* 算法 [[KR3fc021]] — [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī's (fl. 1261–75) collected work. The *Xiáng-jiě* preserves [[賈憲]] Jiǎ Xiàn's eleventh-century method for *n*-th-root extraction (the "Yáng Huī triangle", anticipating Pascal by six centuries) and Liú Yì's lost *Yì-gǔ gēn-yuán* 議古根源 polynomial techniques.

**The post-Sòng-Yuán recovery of *tiān-yuán* and *sì-yuán*.** The works that took the lost algebraic tradition of the previous cluster and reconstructed it for nineteenth-century use.
- *Suàn-xué yuán-liú* 算學源流 [[KR3fc009]] — an anonymous Sòng-Yuán manuscript on the history and method of mathematics, transmitted through the *Yǒng-lè dàdiǎn*.
- *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ zhá-jì* 詳解九章算法札記 [[KR3fc012]] — [[宋景昌]] Sòng Jǐngchāng's c. 1840 *zhá-jì* notes that supply the running textual-critical apparatus for the Yí-jiā-táng reprint of Yáng Huī.
- *Xīn-biān sì-yuán yù-jiàn* 新編四元玉鑒 [[KR3fc018]], *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn xì-cǎo* 四元玉鑑細草 [[KR3fc019]], *Xīn-biān suàn-xué qǐ-méng / Zǒng-kuò* 新編算學啟蒙/總括 [[KR3fc020]] — [[沈欽裴]] Shěn Qīnpèi and [[羅士琳]] Luō Shìlín's 1820s–1830s editorial reconstructions of Zhū Shìjié, the works through which Sòng-Yuán algebra re-entered nineteenth-century Chinese mathematics.

**Yuán-Míng popular-arithmetic codification.** The corpus of practitioner manuals that transmitted basic computation from the abacus revolution through to the late Míng.
- *Dīng Jù suàn-fǎ* 丁巨算法 [[KR3fc013]] (1355) — early Yuán practitioner's handbook.
- *Xiáng-míng suàn-fǎ* 詳明算法 [[KR3fc014]] (1373) — [[安止齋]] Ān Zhǐzhāi and [[何平子]] Hé Píngzǐ's standard primer of the Yuán-Míng transition.
- *Suàn-fǎ quán-néng jí* 算法全能集 [[KR3fc022]] — [[賈亨]] Jiǎ Hēng's late-fourteenth-century compendium.
- *Jiǔ-zhāng xiáng-zhù bǐ-lèi suàn-fǎ dà-quán* 九章詳註比類算法大全 [[KR3fc023]] (1450) — [[吳敬]] Wú Jìng's massive Míng compendium, the dominant *Jiǔ-zhāng*-derivative textbook before Chéng Dàwèi.
- *Xīn-biān zhí-zhǐ suàn-fǎ tǒng-zōng* 新編直指算法統宗 [[KR3fc027]] (1592) — [[程大位]] Chéng Dàwèi's *Suàn-fǎ tǒng-zōng*, the work that codified abacus computation for two centuries of Míng-Qīng practice and that circulated throughout East Asia (the parallel WYG entry is at [[KR3f0045]]).
- *Shén-dào dà-biān lì-zōng suàn-huì* 神道大編歷宗算會 [[KR3fc024]] — [[周述學]] Zhōu Shùxué's encyclopedic mid-Míng mathematical-and-calendrical compendium.
- *Gōu-gǔ suàn-shù* 勾股算術 [[KR3fc025]] — [[顧應祥]] Gù Yīngxiáng's mid-Míng right-triangle textbook (also represented at [[KR3f0049]]); the same Gù Yīngxiáng whose loss of comprehension of the *tiān-yuán* notation in his Cè-yuán hǎi-jìng restatement [[KR3f0043]] dates the disappearance of Sòng-Yuán algebra in the mainstream Míng tradition.

**Early-Jesuit *gōu-gǔ* / *jǐ-hé* encounter.** The first Chinese mathematical works produced under the impact of Ricci and his successors. The *Sìkù* shelves the Jesuit translations themselves at [[KR3f0046]]–[[KR3f0047]] in KR3f; KR3fc preserves the *Chinese* responses.
- *Gōu-gǔ yì* 勾股義 [[KR3fc026]] (1606–08) — [[徐光啟]] Xú Guāngqǐ's reframing of right-triangle theory in Euclidean-deductive form after the *Jǐ-hé yuán-běn* 幾何原本 translation.
- *Dìng-fǎ píng-fāng suàn-shù* 定法平方算術 [[KR3fc029]] — Xú Guāngqǐ on systematic square-root extraction.
- *Dù-cè* 度測 [[KR3fc030]], *Dù-suàn jiě* 度算解 [[KR3fc031]], *Kāi-píng-fāng shuō* 開平方說 [[KR3fc032]], *Kāi-lì-fāng shuō* 開立方說 [[KR3fc033]] — [[陳藎謨]] Chén Jìnmó's quartet of late-Míng didactic treatises on surveying, sector-rod computation, and root extraction, the principal vehicle for the Jesuit *settore* (proportional compass) in Chinese mathematics.
- *Jiā-liàng suàn-jīng* 嘉量算經 [[KR3fc028]] — [[朱載堉]] Zhū Zǎiyù's late-Míng applied-mathematics treatise on the imperial standard measures (the same prince-mathematician whose calendrical and acoustical work appears at [[KR3f0007]] and [[KR4d0035]]).

**Méi Wéndǐng and the early-Qīng synthesis.** The dominant early-Qīng mathematical school. The principal Méi Wéndǐng works are in the WYG line at [[KR3f0026]]–[[KR3f0028]]; the present sub-division preserves the Anhui-school adjacents and Méi's auxiliary works.
- *Suàn-hǎi xiáng-shuō* 算海詳說 [[KR3fc034]] — [[李長茂]] Lǐ Chángmào's late-Kāngxī mathematical compendium.
- *Gǔ-suàn yǎn-lüè* 古算衍略 [[KR3fc035]] and *Chóu-suàn* 籌算 [[KR3fc036]] — [[梅文鼎]] Méi Wéndǐng's historical-mathematical essay and his treatise on Napier's rods, the principal Chinese channel for European logarithmic computation.
- *Gē-yuán mì-lǜ jié-fǎ* 割圈密率捷法 [[KR3fc037]] — [[安圖]] Ān-tú (Minggatu, c. 1692–1763), the Mongol mathematician of the *Qīntiān-jiān*, on trigonometric power-series expansions for sine, cosine and arc-functions, with [[陳際新]] Chén Jìxīn's posthumous completion. The single most original eighteenth-century Chinese mathematical work.
- *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì* 勾股割圓記 [[KR3fc038]] and the four-work compendium *Zhǔn-wàng jiǎn-fǎ / Gē-yuán hú-shǐ bǔ-lùn / Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán quán-yì tú / Fāng-yuán bǐ-lì shù-biǎo* [[KR3fc039]] — [[戴震]] Dài Zhèn's mathematical writings; Dài is also the figure who in the *Sì-kù* project (concurrent with these) recovered the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* from the *Yǒng-lè dàdiǎn* and so re-established the Hàn-Táng mathematical tradition as a primary text-base.
- *Tóng-dù jì* 同度記 [[KR3fc040]] — [[孔繼涵]] Kǒng Jìhán's treatise; Kǒng Jìhán was Dài Zhèn's son-in-law and the publisher of the foundational *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* edition.

**The Yáng-zhōu–Sū-zhōu school (high Qīng kǎo-zhèng mathematics).** The four-person Qián-Jiā high school: [[焦循]], [[汪萊]], [[李銳]], and [[張敦仁]]. The largest single cluster in the division.
- *Lǐ-táng xué-suàn jì* 里堂學算記 [[KR3fc042]] and its constituent treatises *Jiā-jiǎn chéng-chú shì* 加減乘除釋 [[KR3fc043]], *Tiān-yuán-yī shì* 天元一釋 [[KR3fc044]], *Shì-hú* 釋弧 [[KR3fc045]], *Shì-lún* 釋輪 [[KR3fc046]], *Shì-tuǒ* 釋橢 [[KR3fc047]], *Kāi-fāng tōng-shì* 開方通釋 [[KR3fc048]] — [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún's expository series, with the *Tiān-yuán-yī shì* the central document of the early-nineteenth-century recovery of Sòng-Yuán algebra.
- *Héng-zhāi suàn-xué* 衡齋算學 [[KR3fc049]] — [[汪萊]] Wāng Lái's 1796–1813 collected treatises on indeterminate equations, the theory of polynomial roots (Wāng anticipated Descartes' rule of signs in the Chinese context), and the algebraic logic of *kāi-fāng*.
- *Qiú-yī suàn-shù* 求一算術 [[KR3fc041]] — [[張敦仁]] Zhāng Dūnrén's full restatement of the *Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù* (Chinese Remainder Theorem) procedure of Qín Jiǔsháo.
- The Lǐ Ruì calendar-and-equation series [[KR3fc050]]–[[KR3fc060]] — [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's eleven treatises, the most prolific output of any single author in the division. The six "Hàn / Sòng calendrical reconstructions" — *Zhào-gào rì-míng kǎo* [[KR3fc050]], *Hàn Sān-tǒng shù* [[KR3fc051]], *Hàn Sì-fēn shù* [[KR3fc052]], *Hàn Qián-xiàng shù* [[KR3fc053]], *Bǔ-xiū Sòng Fèng-yuán shù* [[KR3fc054]], *Bǔ-xiū Sòng Zhàn-tiān shù* [[KR3fc055]] — return the ancient calendrical algorithms to fully computable form from the bare descriptions in the Standard-History *lì-zhì* 曆志; the parallel mathematical works *Rì-fǎ shuò-yú qiáng-ruò kǎo* [[KR3fc056]], *Fāng-chéng xīn-shù cǎo* [[KR3fc057]], *Gōu-gǔ suàn-shù xì-cǎo* [[KR3fc058]], *Hú-shǐ suàn-shù xì-cǎo* [[KR3fc059]], and the foundational *Kāi-fāng shuō* 開方說 [[KR3fc060]] (the high-water mark of pre-modern Chinese equation theory, treating the multiplicity of polynomial roots and the case-by-case behavior of higher-degree equations).

**Late-Qīng infinite-series and approach to Westernization.** The mid-to-late nineteenth-century tradition, where indigenous *gē-yuán* 割圓 (cyclotomic) series and *gōu-gǔ* methods, the late-Míng *jiè-gēn-fāng* 借根方 (algebraic-equation) absorption of European symbolic algebra, and the imminent full Western synthesis run in parallel.
- *Rú-jī yǐn-méng* 如積引蒙 [[KR3fc061]] — [[汪曰楨]] Wāng Yuē-zhēn's introduction to the *rú-jī* algebraic-equation method.
- [[劉衡]] Liú Héng's six-work *Liù-jiǔ xuān* compendium [[KR3fc062]]–[[KR3fc067]] together with the *Yì-yóu lù* 藝游錄 [[KR3fc080]] of [[駱騰鳳]] Luò Téngfèng — early-to-mid-nineteenth-century practitioners' digests covering surveying, root extraction, *jiè-gēn-fāng*, and the four-term proportion.
- *Gē-yuán lián-bǐ-lì shù tú-jiě* 割圈連比例術圖解 [[KR3fc068]] — [[董佑誠]] Dǒng Yòu-chéng's illustrated treatment of the cyclotomic continued-proportion (infinite-series) method.
- *Xiàng-shù yī-yuán* 象數一原 [[KR3fc069]], *Gōu-gǔ liù-shù* [[KR3fc070]], *Sān-jiǎo hé-jiào shù* [[KR3fc071]], *Kāi zhū-chéng-fāng jié-shù* [[KR3fc072]] — [[項名達]] Xiàng Míngdá's mathematical work, with [[戴煦]] Dài Xù's *Qiú-biǎo jié-shù* 求表捷術 [[KR3fc073]]: together the principal Chinese statement, on the eve of full Westernization, of an indigenous calculus of infinite series (sine, cosine, arctan, logarithm expansions, all derived inside the cyclotomic tradition).
- *Bǐ-suàn shuō-lüè* 筆算說略 [[KR3fc074]] — [[鄭復光]] Zhèng Fùguāng's exposition of European brush-and-pen notational arithmetic, the first dedicated Chinese textbook of Western algorithmic notation.
- *Cè-yuán mì-lǜ* 測圓密率 [[KR3fc075]] — [[徐有壬]] Xú Yǒurén's precision-ratio circle methods.
- *Zhì-qū shù / Zhì-qū tú-jiě* 致曲術/致曲圖解 [[KR3fc076]] — [[夏鸞翔]] Xià Luánxiáng's late treatise on curves.
- *Zé-gǔ-xī zhāi suàn-xué shí-sān zhǒng* 則古昔齋算學十三種 [[KR3fc078]] (1845–67) — [[李善蘭]] Lǐ Shànlán's collected thirteen treatises, the climax of indigenous-tradition Chinese mathematics: the *Duǒ-jī bǐ-lèi* 垛積比類 (finite differences), the *Fāng-yuán chǎn yōu* 方圓闡幽 (cyclotomic series), and *Hú-shǐ qǐ mì* 弧矢啓秘 (arc-sagitta series) all anticipate, in their own *gē-yuán* idiom, the central results of European real analysis. Lǐ's subsequent translation work at the Mò-hǎi Shū-guǎn (Newton, De Morgan, Loomis) marks the boundary at which the indigenous mathematical tradition becomes the modern Chinese mathematical tradition.
- *Qiú-yī shù tōng-jiě* 求一術通解 [[KR3fc079]] — [[黃遵憲]] Huáng Zūnxiàn's late-nineteenth-century general-exposition rewrite of the *Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù*, the work with which the long Qīng commentarial cycle on Qín Jiǔsháo's Chinese Remainder Theorem effectively closes.

## Important persons

The persons listed here are those whose careers shaped KR3fc specifically; many figure also in [[KR3fa]] (asterism / observational astronomy) and [[KR3fb]] (calendrical method) with distinct roles there.

- [[劉徽]] — Wèi-Jìn (mid-third-century) mathematician; the *Jiǔ-zhāng* commentator whose *Jǐng-yuán* 4 (263) preface and exegesis is the foundational document of Chinese mathematical reasoning and the chief witness to the inferential-justificatory style of Hàn-Wèi mathematics ([[KR3fc001]]; also at [[KR3f0032]]).
- [[李淳風]] — Táng (602–670); director of the *Tài-shǐ-jú* 太史局, editor of the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* and author of the *zhù-shì* sub-commentary attached to the *Jiǔ-zhāng*, *Zhāng Qiūjiàn*, *Jí-gǔ*, and other classics ([[KR3fc001]], [[KR3fc004]]).
- [[張邱建]] — Northern-Wèi (c. fifth century); compiler of the *Zhāng Qiūjiàn suàn-jīng* ([[KR3fc004]]), the principal early Chinese text on indeterminate problems.
- [[秦九韶]] — Sòng (1208–1268); author of *Shù-shū jiǔ-zhāng* (1247), the first complete algorithmic statement of the Chinese Remainder Theorem ([[KR3fc008]]; also at [[KR3f0041]]).
- [[楊輝]] — Sòng (fl. 1261–1275); the principal redactor of Liú Yì's lost polynomial-equation work and the transmitter of Jiǎ Xiàn's binomial-coefficient method ([[KR3fc010]]–[[KR3fc011]], [[KR3fc021]]).
- [[李冶]] — Jīn-Yuán (1192–1279); systematizer of the *tiān-yuán-yī* method via the *Cè-yuán hǎi-jìng* ([[KR3fc015]]; also at [[KR3f0042]]–[[KR3f0044]]).

## Topics

The principal subjects, debates, and interpretive problems running through KR3fc:

- **The Sòng-Yuán algebraic peak and its disappearance.** The *tiān-yuán-yī* method of [[李冶]] Lǐ Yě, the *sì-yuán shù* (four-unknown method) of [[朱世傑]] Zhū Shìjié, and the *Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù* (Chinese Remainder Theorem) of [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo were already incomprehensible to mainstream Míng mathematicians by 1500 (the most famous evidence: [[顧應祥]] Gù Yīngxiáng's openly puzzled *Cè-yuán hǎi-jìng* restatement [[KR3f0043]], which simply omits the rod-algebra of Lǐ Yě's procedures). The question *how a tradition this sophisticated was forgotten within two generations of the Yuán-Míng transition* is the central problem of Chinese mathematical historiography, and the Qīng *kǎo-zhèng* programme — culminating in [[羅士琳]] Luō Shìlín's reconstructions and [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún's *Tiān-yuán-yī shì* — is the long answer.

- **The Suàn-jīng shí-shū recovery.** [[戴震]] Dài Zhèn's recovery of eight of the Ten from the *Yǒng-lè dàdiǎn* during the *Sì-kù* project, [[孔繼涵]] Kǒng Jìhán's foundational edition, and [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's *xì-cǎo tú-shuō* reading editions ([[KR3fc002]]–[[KR3fc005]]) together constitute the textual-philological foundation of all modern study of pre-Sòng Chinese mathematics.

- **Gōu-gǔ 勾股 — the right triangle as organizing concept.** From the *Zhōubì* through the *Jiǔ-zhāng* chapter 9, [[徐光啟]]'s *Gōu-gǔ yì* [[KR3fc026]] (the Euclidean re-presentation), Méi Wéndǐng's Anhui-school treatments, Dài Zhèn's *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì* [[KR3fc038]], the cluster of "gōu-gǔ" titles in the Liú Héng and Xiàng Míngdá series [[KR3fc058]], [[KR3fc064]], [[KR3fc070]], and Lǐ Shànlán's [[KR3fc078]] — the right triangle is the single most recurrent figure of Chinese mathematical exposition across two thousand years.

- **Kāi-fāng 開方 — root extraction and the theory of equations.** The Hàn-Wèi square-and-cube-root algorithms in [[KR3fc001]], the Sòng generalization to *n*-th roots in [[楊輝]]'s *Xiáng-jiě* [[KR3fc011]] preserving Jiǎ Xiàn's binomial-coefficient triangle, the Qín-Jiǔsháo Horner-style polynomial evaluation in [[KR3fc008]], the Míng didactic reductions in [[KR3fc032]]–[[KR3fc033]] and Méi Wéndǐng's *Píng-fāng* tradition, [[焦循]]'s *Kāi-fāng tōng-shì* [[KR3fc048]], and culminating in [[李銳]]'s *Kāi-fāng shuō* [[KR3fc060]] — the most sophisticated pre-modern Chinese treatment of the theory of equations, including a systematic analysis of multiple roots.

- **The Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù 大衍求一術 (Chinese Remainder Theorem).** From the *Sūnzǐ suàn-jīng* problem 26 ([[KR3f0033]]) through Qín Jiǔsháo's full algorithm [[KR3fc008]], [[張敦仁]]'s *Qiú-yī suàn-shù* [[KR3fc041]] modernization, and [[黃遵憲]]'s late-Qīng *Qiú-yī shù tōng-jiě* [[KR3fc079]] — the principal Chinese contribution to number theory.

- **Cyclotomic / infinite-series methods (gē-yuán-shù 割圓術).** The medieval origin in Liú Huī's circle-cutting computation of π in [[KR3fc001]], the eighteenth-century *Gē-yuán mì-lǜ jié-fǎ* power-series of [[安圖]] Ān-tú / Minggatu [[KR3fc037]], the *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì* of [[戴震]] [[KR3fc038]], the [[項名達]] / [[戴煦]] series [[KR3fc069]]–[[KR3fc073]], and the *Fāng-yuán chǎn yōu* in Lǐ Shànlán's *Zé-gǔ-xī zhāi* [[KR3fc078]] — the indigenous Chinese form of analytic-series computation, developed largely independently of European calculus from the 1730s through the 1860s.

- **The Jesuit encounter and the jiè-gēn-fāng 借根方.** The translation of European algebraic notation into Chinese as the *jiè-gēn-fāng* ("borrowed-root method") in the *Shù-lǐ jīng-yùn* ([[KR3f0048]]) and the subsequent reckoning, by [[焦循]] in [[KR3fc044]] and successors, of the relationship between *jiè-gēn-fāng* (European) and *tiān-yuán-yī* (Chinese). Settling this equivalence is the conceptual hinge between indigenous and Westernized Chinese mathematics; the late-Qīng Liú Héng didactic series [[KR3fc066]]–[[KR3fc067]] and Wāng Yuē-zhēn's [[KR3fc061]] are practical fruits of the equivalence.

- **The kǎo-zhèng reconstruction of ancient calendrical algorithms.** The series of [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì reconstructions [[KR3fc051]]–[[KR3fc055]] — *Sān-tǒng*, *Sì-fēn*, *Qián-xiàng*, *Fèng-yuán*, *Zhàn-tiān* — converts the bare descriptions in the Standard-History *lì-zhì* into running computational procedures, recovering the technical content of Hàn-Sòng mathematical astronomy through pure textual scholarship. The parallel project on the calendrical side is in [[KR3fb]].

- **The transition to modern mathematics.** [[鄭復光]] Zhèng Fùguāng's *Bǐ-suàn shuō-lüè* [[KR3fc074]] (Western notational arithmetic), [[李善蘭]] Lǐ Shànlán's *Zé-gǔ-xī zhāi* [[KR3fc078]] as the closing monument of indigenous mathematics on the eve of Lǐ's Mò-hǎi Shū-guǎn translations of Newton and De Morgan — these works trace the actual moment of transition. The division ends, chronologically, at the boundary of the modern Chinese mathematical tradition.
