# Zhǔn-wàng jiǎn-fǎ / Gē-yuán hú-shǐ bǔ-lùn / Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán quán-yì tú / Fāng-yuán bǐ-lì shù-biǎo 準望簡法/割圈弧矢補論/勾股割圈全義圖/方圈比例數表
 *Four-Work Compendium: Simplified Surveying / Supplementary Discussion of Circle-Arc Functions / Diagrammatic Full Exposition of Right-Triangle Circle-Cutting / Numerical Tables of Square-and-Circle Proportions*
by [[戴震]] (撰)

## About the work

A four-work compendium by [[戴震]] Dài Zhèn, transmitted together as a 1-*juàn* unit complementing the larger [[KR3fc038]] *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì*. The constituent four works:

(1) **Zhǔn-wàng jiǎn-fǎ** 準望簡法 ("Simplified Surveying"). A condensed practical-surveying treatise applying the *gōu-gǔ* right-triangle methods to standard surveying problems.

(2) **Gē-yuán hú-shǐ bǔ-lùn** 割圈弧矢補論 ("Supplementary Discussion of Circle-Cutting Arc-and-Versed-Sine"). A theoretical supplement to the circle-cutting discussion in *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì*, treating in particular the arc-and-versed-sine (*hú-shǐ* 弧矢) function-relations.

(3) **Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán quán-yì tú** 勾股割圈全義圖 ("Diagrammatic Full Exposition of Right-Triangle Circle-Cutting"). The diagrammatic apparatus for the *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì*'s circle-cutting derivations, separately collected.

(4) **Fāng-yuán bǐ-lì shù-biǎo** 方圈比例數表 ("Numerical Tables of Square-and-Circle Proportions"). Numerical tables of the ratios between the inscribed/circumscribed polygons and the circle, supporting the circle-cutting π-computations.

### Abstract

The four-work compendium is a typical Dài-Zhèn-style scholarly production: a major principal treatise (the [[KR3fc038]] *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì*) supplemented by a cluster of related shorter pieces treating specific sub-topics and providing the auxiliary apparatus (tables, diagrams, alternative-form treatments). The arrangement is parallel to Dài Zhèn's philological practice — where his principal philological treatises are likewise accompanied by clusters of shorter related works.

Substantively, the four pieces fill out the *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì*'s framework:

- The *Zhǔn-wàng jiǎn-fǎ* provides the practical-surveying application that the principal treatise leaves implicit.
- The *Gē-yuán hú-shǐ bǔ-lùn* expands the trigonometric-function discussion to the *hú-shǐ* (arc-and-versed-sine) functions specifically, anticipating the [[安圖]] Mínggatu power-series treatment in [[KR3fc037]] *Gē-yuán mì-lǜ jié-fǎ*.
- The *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán quán-yì tú* provides the geometric-diagrammatic apparatus that the textual treatise leaves embedded in prose.
- The *Fāng-yuán bǐ-lì shù-biǎo* provides the numerical-tabular apparatus underlying the π-computations.

For Dài Zhèn's broader project and the *Gōu-gǔ gē-yuán jì*'s context see [[KR3fc038]].

Dating: the compendium is companion to [[KR3fc038]] and shares its *NotBefore* / *notAfter* bracket of 1755–1773.
