# Gōu-gǔ suàn-shù xì-cǎo 勾股算術細草
 *Detailed Working of Right-Triangle Arithmetic*
by [[李銳]] (撰)

## About the work

[[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's (1768–1817) procedural treatise in 2 *juàn* on the *gōu-gǔ* 勾股 (right-triangle) problem domain — the subject matter of the *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-shù* 九章算術 Chapter 9 *Gōu-gǔ* and the closely-related *Zhōu-bì suàn-jīng* 周髀算經 ([[KR3fc001]]'s companion classic). The title's *xì-cǎo* 細草 indicates the genre — worked-example exposition with step-by-step computational procedure.

### Abstract

The *gōu-gǔ* (literally "leg and thigh," i.e. the two legs of a right triangle, with the hypotenuse *xián* 弦 understood) is the indigenous Chinese formulation of the Pythagorean theorem and its associated problem-types: given two of {leg, leg, hypotenuse, perimeter, area, sum of two sides, difference of two sides}, find the remaining elements. The *Jiǔ-zhāng* Chapter 9 presents the basic procedures and a representative selection of problem-types; the *Zhōu-bì suàn-jīng* embeds the right-triangle theorem in an astronomical-cosmological framework as the basis of *gài-tiān* 蓋天 ("heaven-as-cover") cosmography.

Lǐ Ruì's 2-*juàn* *xì-cǎo* presents a modernised, systematic exposition of the *gōu-gǔ* problem domain. The first *juàn* presents the basic procedures with worked examples; the second extends to harder problem-types (involving simultaneous use of multiple *gōu-gǔ* relations, or the use of *gōu-gǔ* in combination with the area / perimeter / circular-segment apparatus). Where the *Jiǔ-zhāng* Chapter 9 presents the procedures tersely, Lǐ Ruì's exposition supplies the intermediate steps, the algebraic derivations of the procedures from the basic Pythagorean identity, and the geometric figures clarifying the configurations.

The treatise is the central member of Lǐ Ruì's three-work procedural-exposition sequence ([[KR3fc057]] *Fāng-chéng xīn-shù cǎo* — linear systems; the present text — right triangles; [[KR3fc059]] *Hú-shǐ suàn-shù xì-cǎo* — arcs and versed-sines). Together they constitute Lǐ Ruì's pedagogical project of making the recovered *Jiǔ-zhāng*-tradition procedures accessible to the educated mathematical reader of the Jiā-Dào era.

Dating: composed during Lǐ Ruì's mature productive period. *notBefore* 1795; *notAfter* 1817.
