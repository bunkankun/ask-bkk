# Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ zhá-jì 詳解九章算法札記
 *Critical Notebook on the Detailed Exposition of the Nine Chapters Mathematical Methods*
by [[宋景昌]] (撰)

## About the work

[[宋景昌]] Sòng Jǐngchāng's textual-critical notebook accompanying his 1842 editing of [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī's [[KR3fc011]] *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ* for the Yí-jiā-táng 宜稼堂 cóng-shū. Produced in the closing months of the 1842 cóng-shū editorial work — *notBefore* conservatively set at 1840 to allow for the work's preparation across the cóng-shū's preparation period, *notAfter* at the 1842 publication date.

### Abstract

The work belongs to the *zhá-jì* 札記 genre — a running scholarly notebook of textual emendations, procedural verifications, and editorial decisions made during the preparation of a critical edition. Sòng Jǐngchāng's specific task was the collation of the recovered Korean Chosŏn recension of Yáng Huī's *Xiáng-jiě* against the Sòng-fragment survivals in the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* and against the citations of Yáng Huī's work in subsequent Yuán-Míng mathematical literature.

The *zhá-jì* records, in entry-by-entry form, the textual problems encountered and their resolution: where the Korean and *Yǒng-lè* lines agree, the reading is straightforward; where they disagree, Sòng Jǐngchāng supplies a procedural *cǎo* (computation) to verify which reading produces the correct mathematical result, and emends the text accordingly. The methodology is sophisticated: where surface philological reasoning would be inadequate to choose between variant readings, Sòng Jǐngchāng's mathematical competence allows him to use the mathematical correctness of each reading as the decisive criterion.

The work is essential reading alongside the Yí-jiā-táng *Xiáng-jiě*, providing the running justification for the published critical text. Together with the parallel apparatus produced by [[沈欽裴]] Shěn Qīnpéi and [[羅士琳]] Luó Shìlín for the [[KR3fc017]] *Sì-yuán yù-jiàn* recovery (their *xì-cǎo* and *bǔ-shì*), the *Zhá-jì* establishes the Yí-jiā-táng cóng-shū as the first methodologically-rigorous edition of the four great Sòng-Yuán mathematical works.
