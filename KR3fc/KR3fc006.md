# Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng xì-cǎo 緝古算經細草
 *Detailed Procedural Exposition of the Continuing-the-Ancients Mathematical Canon*
by [[張敦仁]] (細草)

## About the work

Zhāng Dūnrén 張敦仁's *xì-cǎo* (detailed-procedure exposition) of [[王孝通]] Wáng Xiàotōng's Táng *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng* 緝古算經 ([[KR3f0040]]), the principal cubic-equation classic of pre-modern Chinese mathematics. Composed in the closing years of the eighteenth century or early Jiā-qìng, parallel to but independent of [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's [[KR3fc005]] *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng kǎo-zhù*. The catalog records the entry under the title *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng* (the parent-text title); the KX edition reproduces the parent text with Zhāng's interlinear and appended *xì-cǎo* working.

### Abstract

Zhāng Dūnrén 張敦仁 (1754–1834; CBDB id 80926) was among the principal mathematical philologists of the Jiā-Dào-period scholarly circle at Yáng-zhōu, working alongside [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún (1763–1820) and [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì (1768–1817). His *Qiú-yī suàn-shù* ([[KR3fc041]]) had recovered the procedural specifics of [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo's *Dà-yǎn qiú-yī shù* (the Chinese Remainder Theorem); the present *xì-cǎo* extended the same recovery method to Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic algorithms.

Zhāng's exposition differs methodologically from Lǐ Huáng's parallel project in two respects. First, Zhāng's *xì-cǎo* is more compact: rather than reconstructing every implicit geometric reduction and supplying full *tú-shuō* (figure-and-explanation), Zhāng concentrates on the numerical computation, presenting the cube-root extraction step-by-step in counting-rod or column-form notation. Second, where Lǐ Huáng tends to defer to the received text and emend only where the procedure-logic forces it, Zhāng more readily proposes textual corrections based on his own computational reconstruction.

The two Qīng *Jí-gǔ* expositions are conventionally read together: Lǐ Huáng for the geometric reduction and Zhāng Dūnrén for the computational *xì-cǎo*. Together they made Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic methods fully accessible to mid-Qīng working mathematicians for the first time since the late Sòng. The work is also cited under the variant title *Jí-gǔ suàn-jīng xì-cǎo* 緝古算經細草 in the bibliographies. Liú Héng 劉衡 ([[KR3fc007]]) subsequently added a *bǔ-zhù* 補注 to the work, extending Zhāng's reconstructions.

The catalog records the work without specified *juàn* count; the KX-imprint preserves it as 1 *juàn*. *NotBefore* is set conservatively at 1800 (allowing for the work to have been completed in the same decade as the *Qiú-yī suàn-shù*); *notAfter* at Zhāng's death-year 1834.
