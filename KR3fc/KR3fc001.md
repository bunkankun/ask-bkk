# Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-jīng 九章筭經
 *The Nine Chapters Mathematical Canon*
by [[劉徽]] (注), [[李淳風]] (等釋)

## About the work

A non-WYG recension of the *Jiǔ-zhāng* 九章 — the foundational mathematical classic of the Chinese tradition — under its Táng-canonical title *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-jīng* 九章筭經 ("Canon of the Nine Chapters" — note the older orthography 筭 for the later standard 算). The WYG edition of the same work is catalogued in nine *juàn* under its Hàn title *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-shù* 九章算術 ([[KR3f0032]]); the present KX entry preserves a five-*juàn* division of the text under the Táng-canonized title, with the [[劉徽]] commentary (preface dated *Jǐng-yuán* 4 = 263 CE, but the work itself begun before and continued after the Wèi-Jìn transition of 265) and the [[李淳風]] *zhù-shì* sub-commentary commissioned by Táng Gāo-zōng in 656 CE for the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* 算經十書 imperial mathematical curriculum.

The *Jiǔ-zhāng* contains 246 problems-with-solutions organized into nine topical chapters: (i) *Fāng-tián* 方田 (Square Fields — plane-area calculation, fractions, π); (ii) *Sù-mǐ* 粟米 (Grain-Rice — proportional exchange); (iii) *Cuī-fēn* 衰分 (Distribution by Proportion); (iv) *Shǎo-guǎng* 少廣 (Less-Width — square- and cube-root extraction); (v) *Shāng-gōng* 商功 (Construction — volume calculation); (vi) *Jūn-shū* 均輸 (Equitable Transport — weighted distribution); (vii) *Yíng bù-zú* 盈不足 (Surplus-and-Deficit — the Rule of False Position); (viii) *Fāng-chéng* 方程 (Rectangular Arrays — Gaussian elimination of linear systems, with the first systematic mathematical use of negative numbers in any tradition); and (ix) *Gōu-gǔ* 勾股 (Right-Triangle — the Pythagorean theorem and its applications).

### Abstract

The *Jiǔ-zhāng* is the single most influential pre-modern Chinese mathematical work. Internal evidence (the reference to the Shàng-lín 上林 imperial park at Cháng-ān, established under Hàn Wǔ-dì in the 130s BCE) fixes a *terminus post quem* for the received compilation in the late Western Hàn, with traditional attribution of the basic redaction to Zhāng Cāng 張蒼 and Gěng Shòuchāng 耿壽昌 in the early Hàn — almost certainly an idealizing back-projection. The book entered its received Táng-canonical form in three stages: the base compilation (c. 100 BCE), the Liú Huī commentary (begun 263 CE, with later supplements after the Wèi-Jìn transition), and the Lǐ Chúnfēng *zhù-shì* of 656 — at which point the work was retitled *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-jīng* to fit the *Suàn-jīng shí-shū* canonical series, with which it was promulgated as the head text of the Imperial-Academy mathematics curriculum.

The KX edition's five-*juàn* division most likely reflects an older recension line transmitted via the *Yǒng-lè dàdiǎn* 永樂大典 fragments and the Bāo Tíngbó 鮑廷博 *Zhī-bù-zú zhāi* 知不足齋 collated reprint of the Sòng *Mǎ Yán-jiā* 馬延家 imprint; the WYG line gives the same content in nine *juàn* (one per chapter) plus the Lǐ Jí 李籍 *Yīn-yì* 音義 supplementary *juàn*. The work's mathematical content is identical across recensions; for the substantive discussion of method, transmission, and modern scholarship see the WYG companion [[KR3f0032]]. The chief later commentaries — Lǐ Huáng's 李潢 detailed reading [[KR3fc002]] *Jiǔ-zhāng suàn-shù xì-cǎo tú-shuō*, Yáng Huī's 楊輝 [[KR3fc011]] *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng suàn-fǎ*, and Wú Jìng's 吳敬 [[KR3fc023]] *Jiǔ-zhāng xiáng-zhù bǐ-lèi suàn-fǎ dà-quán* — are all preserved in the present KR3fc series.

## Other points of interest

The five-*juàn* division used in the present KX edition preserves the older medieval recension structure attested in the *Suí shū Jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 ("*Jiǔ zhāng suàn-jīng* nine *juàn*, *yīn-yì* one *juàn*") only in the supplemental *Yīn-yì* count; the body division into five rather than nine *juàn* reflects the Sòng *Mǎ-shì* imprint's repagination. Comparison of this division with the WYG/SBCK nine-*juàn* division is a standard reference point for *Jiǔ-zhāng* textual scholarship.
