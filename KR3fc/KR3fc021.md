# Suàn-fǎ 算法
 *Mathematical Methods*
by [[楊輝]] (編)

## About the work

A brief mathematical compendium in 1 *juàn* attributed in the catalog to [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī. The work's bibliographic identification is uncertain: under the bare title *Suàn-fǎ* 算法 it could plausibly refer to (a) Yáng Huī's *Rì-yòng suàn-fǎ* 日用算法 of 1262 — a daily-use computational primer cited by later Yuán-Míng mathematical works but otherwise transmitted only in fragments — or (b) an abridged or alternative title for one of the constituent works of [[KR3fc010]] *Yáng Huī suàn-fǎ*. The single-*juàn* extent and the Sòng-dynasty placement match the *Rì-yòng suàn-fǎ* most closely.

### Abstract

The *Rì-yòng suàn-fǎ* 日用算法 (1262), as cited in the Yuán-Míng bibliographic literature, was the second of Yáng Huī's mathematical works — a daily-use computational primer occupying a middle position between his more substantive *Xiáng-jiě jiǔ-zhāng* ([[KR3fc011]], 1261) and the later compendium *Yáng Huī suàn-fǎ* ([[KR3fc010]], 1274–75). It systematized elementary arithmetic for practical commercial, taxation, and labor-distribution applications, providing the *kǒu-jué* 口訣 (oral-formula) mnemonics for multiplication and division shortcuts and a small selection of worked problems on each standard topic.

The work was largely lost in China by the late Míng; the KX recension presumably preserves a partial reconstruction from Korean and Japanese citations and from internal cross-references in the surviving Yáng Huī works. The work's significance is primarily transmissional: it represents the practical-arithmetic counterpoint to Yáng Huī's high-mathematical *Xiáng-jiě* commentary, and the pattern of one mathematician producing both high-theoretical and practical-pedagogical work in close succession is characteristic of the Southern-Sòng mathematical scene.

Dating: the *Rì-yòng suàn-fǎ* preface (preserved in citation) gives Xián-chún 8 (1262); the *Xù-gǔ zhāi-qí* of 1275 references the *Rì-yòng* explicitly. *NotBefore* set at 1262 if the present work is the *Rì-yòng*; *notAfter* at 1275 to bracket the alternative interpretation that the present work is an extracted portion of the *Yáng Huī suàn-fǎ* compendium.

For [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī's biographical context see his Person note.
