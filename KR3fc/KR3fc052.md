# Hàn Sìfēn shù 漢四分術
 *The Hàn-Dynasty Sì-fēn (Quarter-Day) Calendrical System*
by [[李銳]] (述并注)

## About the work

[[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's (1768–1817) evidential-philological reconstruction in 3 *juàn* of the *HòuHàn Sìfēn lì* 後漢四分曆, the official calendar of the Later Hàn (DōngHàn 東漢) dynasty from 85 CE through to the end of the Hàn. The *Sìfēn* system replaced [[劉歆]] Liú Xīn's *Sāntǒng* (cf. [[KR3fc051]]) and used the simpler "quarter-day" tropical-year approximation of 365 ¼ days that gives the system its name.

### Abstract

The *Sìfēn* ("quarter") calendar was promulgated in Yuánhé 元和 2 (85 CE) under the Hàn emperor Zhāngdì 章帝, replacing the *Sāntǒng lì* whose accumulated error had become embarrassingly visible. The new system reverted to the simpler tropical-year value 365 ¼ days (the same value embedded in the still-earlier *Tàichū* 太初 of 104 BCE), with revised metonic 19-year intercalation and revised constants for lunar motion. The system remained in use under successive minor revisions for the rest of the Later Hàn period.

The *Sìfēn lì* is preserved in the calendrical chapter (*Lǜlì zhì* 律曆志) of the *HòuHàn shū* 後漢書, in a procedural form somewhat clearer than that of the *Sāntǒng* in the *Hànshū*. Lǐ Ruì's 3-*juàn* reconstruction extracts the algorithmic content, organises it under the standard headings of a Chinese calendar treatise (fundamental constants, derived constants, new-moon procedure, solar-term procedure, eclipse and planetary procedures), and supplies a critical *zhù* 注 that emends corrupted figures where the algorithmic logic permits.

The work is the second member of Lǐ Ruì's quintet of Hàn-and-Sòng calendrical reconstructions ([[KR3fc051]]–[[KR3fc055]]). The pair *Sāntǒng* and *Sìfēn* together represent the foundational Hàn calendrical tradition; the *Qiánxiàng* 乾象 ([[KR3fc053]]) is the immediate SānGuó successor; the two Sòng reconstructions ([[KR3fc054]], [[KR3fc055]]) bring the lineage forward to the Northern Sòng. The whole sequence is the principal Qīng evidential-philological recovery of the indigenous Chinese calendrical tradition.

Dating: composed during Lǐ Ruì's mature productive period in [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán's patronage circle. *notBefore* 1795; *notAfter* 1817 (death year).
