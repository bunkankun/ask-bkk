# Wēn-rè lùn jiān-zhèng 溫熱論箋正
*Annotated Critical Edition of the Treatise on Warm-Heat Disorders*
by [[葉桂]] (Yè Guì, *zì* Tiān-shì 天士, 1666–1745) — base text; the annotation is by **Chén Guāng-sōng** 陳光淞 (late-Qīng / early Republican)

## About the work

The principal late-Qīng / early-Republican **critical-annotation edition** of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiān-shì's *Wēn-rè lùn* ([[KR3eg001]]). The base text is Yè's foundational warm-disease treatise; the annotation (*jiān-zhèng* 箋正 = "annotate-and-correct") is by Chén Guāng-sōng 陳光淞, a late-Qīng physician active in the 1900s–1910s. The catalog meta lists only Yè as author, since the *jiān-zhèng* genre conventionally subordinates the annotator's identity to the base text; in the *hxwd* collection format the annotation is the principal new contribution.

### Abstract

Chén Guāng-sōng's *jiān-zhèng* is one of a cluster of late-Qīng / Republican critical-annotation editions of the *Wēn-rè lùn*, a moment in which the doctrinal authority of the *Wēn-rè lùn* had become canonical and the textual-critical work shifted toward establishing the most authentic version of Yè's words. The principal textual issues addressed by Chén are: (a) the choice between the Táng Dàliè *Wú-yī huì-jiǎng* recension and the Huá Xiùyún *Lín-zhèng zhǐ-nán yī-àn* recension; (b) the doctrinal weight of disputed passages on *xié-zài-fèi* 邪在肺 versus *xié-zài-xīn-bāo* 邪在心包; (c) the relationship of the *Wēn-rè lùn* to the parallel *Sān-shí zhǒng wài-gǎn zhèng* 三十種外感證 text also ascribed to Yè.

The work falls within the broader Shàoxīng-school late-Qīng / Republican *wēn-bìng* editorial project, in which Hé Liánchén [[何廉臣]] and his circle systematically re-edited the canonical *wēn-bìng* texts. Dating is approximate; the work probably appeared in the late 1900s or early 1910s.

## Other points of interest

The *jiān-zhèng* genre is characteristic of late-Qīng / Republican medical-philological work — a Chinese-medical adaptation of the *Sì-kù* 提要-style critical-edition apparatus.
