# Shí-bìng lùn 時病論
*Treatise on Seasonal Disorders*
by [[雷豐]] (Léi Fēng, *zì* Sōng-cún 松存, *hào* Shào-yì 少逸, 1833–1888)

## About the work

Léi Fēng's *Shí-bìng lùn* in 8 *juǎn*, completed Guāng-xù 8 (1882) and printed the following year. A major late-Qīng *wēn-bìng* monograph organising the externally-contracted seasonal disorders (*shí-bìng* 時病) into a clear four-season framework, with the canonical *wēn-bìng*-school distinction between *fú-qì* 伏氣 (latent-qi) and *xīn-gǎn* 新感 (newly-contracted) varieties carefully maintained throughout. The text is one of the most clinically useful and pedagogically influential of the late-Qīng *wēn-bìng* treatises and has remained a standard reference in modern TCM training.

### Abstract

The preface is signed "Guāng-xù bā nián rén-wǔ zhòng-qiū qián yī rì, Sān-qú Léi Fēng Shào-yì shì zhì yú Yǎng-hè Shān-fáng" 光緒八年壬午仲秋前一日，三衢雷豐少逸氏識於養鶴山房, fixing both date (1882) and place (Yǎng-hè Shān-fáng 養鶴山房, Léi's studio in Qú-zhōu 衢州). Léi was a native of Sān-qú 三衢 (Qú-zhōu) and a third-generation physician — his father Léi Yì-xián 雷逸仙 had studied under Chéng Zhī-tiān 程芝田.

The work is organised by season: spring disorders (*chūn-wēn* 春溫, *fēng-wēn* 風溫, *wēn-yì* 溫疫, *wēn-dú* 溫毒); summer disorders (*shǔ-wēn* 暑溫, *shī-wēn* 濕溫, *xià-shǔ* 夏暑); autumn disorders (*qiū-zào* 秋燥, *fú-shǔ* 伏暑); winter disorders (*dōng-wēn* 冬溫, *dōng-hán* 冬寒, *tán-yǐn* 痰飲). For each disorder Léi presents the canonical *Nèi-jīng* / *Shāng-hán lùn* doctrinal background, the standard prescriptions, and a body of his own clinical cases. The clinical idiom is recognisably continuous with the southern (Sūzhōu–Hángzhōu) Yè–Wú–Wáng line of *wēn-bìng* practice, but with Léi's own emphasis on rigorous seasonal classification.

The work was reprinted many times in the late Qīng and Republican periods and remains in print as a standard *wēn-bìng* textbook.

## Other points of interest

The text is the principal exhibit for the late-Qīng "Zhèjiāng" wing of the *wēn-bìng* tradition, distinguished from the Sūzhōu mainstream by an explicit emphasis on the *fú-qì* / *xīn-gǎn* distinction and on the seasonal pathogenetic schema.
