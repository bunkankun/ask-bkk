# Wēn-rè féng-yuán 溫熱逢源
*Sources of Warm-Heat Disorders*
by [[柳寶詒]] (Liǔ Bǎoyí, 1842–1901)

## About the work

A 3-*juǎn* systematic treatise on warm-disease pathology composed in the second half of the 19th century by the Jiāng-yīn 江陰 physician Liǔ Bǎoyí. The text is the principal late-Qīng monograph on the **latent-qi** (*fú-qì* 伏氣) theory of warm disease, grounded in close re-reading of the canonical *Nèi-jīng*, *Nán-jīng*, and *Shāng-hán lùn* passages on cold-injury / warm-disease and supplemented with critical assessments of all the major preceding *wēn-bìng* monographs.

### Abstract

The work was not printed during Liǔ's lifetime; it circulated in manuscript and was first published as part of Qiū Qìng-yún's 裘慶元 *Sān-sān yī-shū* 三三醫書 (early Republican period, 1924). Dating is therefore approximate — written some time between Liǔ's establishment as a mature physician in the 1860s and his death in 1901.

The three *juǎn* are organised as follows: **upper juǎn** annotates the loci classici on latent-qi warm disease in the *Nèi-jīng*, *Nán-jīng*, and *Shāng-hán lùn*, together with selected passages on summer-heat contraction and damp-warm combined patterns; **middle juǎn** examines and corrects passages from Zhōu Yáng-jùn's *Wēn-rè shǔ-yì quán-shū* ([[KR3eg032]]), Liú Sōng-fēng / Liú Jí-rén's *Fú-xié piān* ([[KR3eg028]]), Zhāng Lù's *Shāng-hán xù-lùn*, and Wú Yòu-kě's *Wēn-yì lùn* ([[KR3eg004]]); **lower juǎn** presents Liǔ's own systematic exposition of latent-qi warm disease, its aetiology, transmission, and treatment principles, anchored in clinical experience.

The work is significant for explicitly polemicising against the dominant Sūzhōu (Yè–Wú–Wáng) line on the relative weight of latent-qi versus newly-contracted (*xīn-gǎn* 新感) pathogens. Liǔ argues for the priority of latent-qi explanations against the Sūzhōu mainstream emphasis on newly-contracted warm pathogens; this puts him in alignment with Léi Fēng ([[KR3eg002]]) and against Wú Jūtōng ([[KR3eg010]]).

## Other points of interest

The work is the most rigorous *Nèi-jīng* / *Nán-jīng*-anchored statement of late-Qīng latent-qi theory and a key witness to the Jiāng-yīn medical tradition.
