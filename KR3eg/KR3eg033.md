# Wēnyì míngbiàn gējué 溫疫明辨歌訣
*Versified Summary of "Clear Discrimination of Warm-Epidemic"*
by [[王旭高]] (Wáng Xùgāo, 1798–1862)

## About the work

A mid-19th-century **versified mnemonic** of Dài Tiānzhāng's *Guǎng wēnyì lùn* ([[KR3eg023]]) — which had circulated under the pirated title *Wēnyì míngbiàn* 瘟疫明辨 attributed to Zhèng Diànyī 鄭奠一 (see [[KR3eg023]] abstract). The catalog meta and surviving printings of this *gējué* refer to the "*Wēnyì míngbiàn*" base text by its pirated-edition title rather than by the authentic *Guǎng wēnyì lùn* title — a small but interesting witness to the persistence of the false attribution in the late-Qīng book trade.

### Abstract

The text is a third member of Wáng Xùgāo's *wēnbìng* mnemonic project, paired with [[KR3eg015]] (versifying Xuē Shēngbái's *Shīrè tiáobiàn*) and [[KR3eg024]] (versifying Wú Yǒuxìng's *Wēnyì lùn*). Together the three *gējué* / *gēkuò* distill the canonical Qīng *wēnbìng* monographs (Yè, Xuē, Wú Yǒuxìng, Dài Tiānzhāng) into mnemonic form for student memorisation.

The *Wēnyì míngbiàn gējué* renders each proposition of Dài Tiānzhāng's text into four- or seven-character verse, with prose annotations where needed for clinical clarification. The doctrinal substance is faithful to Dài's pestilential-qi framework and his **five-concomitants and ten-mixed-conditions** (五兼十夾) syndrome schema.

Dating is approximate, conventionally placed in Wáng's mature period (1840s–1850s) on the same grounds as the other *gējué*. The posthumous collected edition *Wáng Xùgāo yī shū liù zhǒng* 王旭高醫書六種 (1862) is the principal extant witness.

## Other points of interest

The persistence of the *Wēnyì míngbiàn* title in the *gējué* (rather than the authentic *Guǎng wēnyì lùn* title) demonstrates how the pirated-edition title outlived its authentic source-text in late-Qīng book culture — even after Dài Zǔqǐ's 1778 authentic reprint correcting the attribution.
