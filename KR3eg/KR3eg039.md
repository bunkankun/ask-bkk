# Sōngfēng shuō yì 松峰說疫
*Sōng-fēng's Treatise on Epidemic Disease*
by [[劉奎]] (Liú Kuí, *hào* Sōngfēng 松峰, 清)

## About the work

A 6-*juǎn* mid-Qīng comprehensive treatise on epidemic disease by the Shāndōng physician Liú Kuí, completed in the late Qiánlóng period. The principal preface by **Wáng Shūxiào** 王舒嘯 is dated Qiánlóng 50 *yǐsì* 乙巳 (1785); Liú Sīzōng 劉斯宗's preface is dated Qiánlóng 52 *dīngwèi* 丁未 *qīnghé yuè* (1787). The work is the major extension of Wú Yǒuxìng's *Wēnyì lùn* in the northern (Shāndōng) medical tradition — a parallel to Dài Tiānzhāng's southern (Jiāngníng) extension in *Guǎng wēnyì lùn* ([[KR3eg023]]).

### Abstract

The six juǎn systematically expand and clinicise Wú Yǒuxìng's pestilential-qi (*lìqì* 戾氣) doctrine for northern epidemic conditions. Liú is one of the Qīng physicians most explicitly engaged with Wú Yǒuxìng's theoretical framework — he is independently the author of *Wēnyì lùn lèibiān* 瘟疫論類編, a topical re-organisation of Wú's text.

Liú's distinctive **doctrinal contributions:**

1. Systematic extension of the *lìqì* framework to include **regional and seasonal variations** in pestilential presentation — the Wú Yǒuxìng / Jiāngnán material does not adequately address northern epidemic conditions, and Liú supplies the regional-medical adaptation.

2. Practical **public-health measures** — Liú gives substantial attention to the prevention and containment of epidemic outbreaks, including isolation, hygiene, and the use of fumigation. This material drew on Liú's experience accompanying his cousin Liú Yōng 劉墉 on official duties.

3. **Northern materia medica adaptation** — substitution of locally-available northern drugs for the southern-tradition prescriptions of Wú Yǒuxìng and Dài Tiānzhāng.

The work was an important reference for the Shāndōng / JīngJīn medical tradition and was widely reprinted in the late Qīng. Liú studied medicine in Běijīng under [[郭志邃]] Guō Zhìsuì (author of *Shāzhàng yùhéng* [[KR3eg037]]) and reportedly under Huáng Yuányù 黃元御, giving him direct access to both the Jiāngnán *shā* tradition and the Shāndōng classical tradition.

## Other points of interest

The Wú Yǒuxìng / Dài Tiānzhāng / Liú Kuí line constitutes the principal Qīng-period extension of late-Míng pestilential-qi doctrine into a fully clinicised apparatus — three texts in three generations, each adapting the framework to a specific regional and clinical context (Jiāngnán Wújiāng, Jiāngníng Nánjīng, Shāndōng Zhūchéng).
