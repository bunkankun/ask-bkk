# Sōng-fēng shuō yì 松峰說疫
*Sōng-fēng's Treatise on Epidemic Disease*
by [[劉奎]] (Liú Kuí, *hào* Sōng-fēng 松峰, 清)

## About the work

A 6-*juǎn* mid-Qīng comprehensive treatise on epidemic disease by the Shān-dōng physician Liú Kuí, completed in the late Qián-lóng period. The principal preface by **Wáng Shū-xiào** 王舒嘯 is dated Qián-lóng 50 *yǐ-sì* 乙巳 (1785); Liú Sī-zōng 劉斯宗's preface is dated Qián-lóng 52 *dīng-wèi* 丁未 *qīng-hé yuè* (1787). The work is the major extension of Wú Yǒu-xìng's *Wēn-yì lùn* in the northern (Shān-dōng) medical tradition — a parallel to Dài Tiān-zhāng's southern (Jiāng-níng) extension in *Guǎng wēn-yì lùn* ([[KR3eg023]]).

### Abstract

The six juǎn systematically expand and clinicise Wú Yǒu-xìng's pestilential-qi (*lì-qì* 戾氣) doctrine for northern epidemic conditions. Liú is one of the Qīng physicians most explicitly engaged with Wú Yǒu-xìng's theoretical framework — he is independently the author of *Wēn-yì lùn lèi-biān* 瘟疫論類編, a topical re-organisation of Wú's text.

Liú's distinctive **doctrinal contributions:**

1. Systematic extension of the *lì-qì* framework to include **regional and seasonal variations** in pestilential presentation — the Wú Yǒu-xìng / Jiāng-nán material does not adequately address northern epidemic conditions, and Liú supplies the regional-medical adaptation.

2. Practical **public-health measures** — Liú gives substantial attention to the prevention and containment of epidemic outbreaks, including isolation, hygiene, and the use of fumigation. This material drew on Liú's experience accompanying his cousin Liú Yōng 劉墉 on official duties.

3. **Northern materia medica adaptation** — substitution of locally-available northern drugs for the southern-tradition prescriptions of Wú Yǒu-xìng and Dài Tiān-zhāng.

The work was an important reference for the Shān-dōng / Jīng-Jīn medical tradition and was widely reprinted in the late Qīng. Liú studied medicine in Běi-jīng under [[郭志邃]] Guō Zhì-suì (author of *Shā-zhàng yù-héng* [[KR3eg037]]) and reportedly under Huáng Yuán-yù 黃元御, giving him direct access to both the Jiāng-nán *shā* tradition and the Shān-dōng classical tradition.

## Other points of interest

The Wú Yǒu-xìng / Dài Tiān-zhāng / Liú Kuí line constitutes the principal Qīng-period extension of late-Míng pestilential-qi doctrine into a fully clinicised apparatus — three texts in three generations, each adapting the framework to a specific regional and clinical context (Jiāng-nán Wú-jiāng, Jiāng-níng Nán-jīng, Shān-dōng Zhū-chéng).
