# Shǔ-zhèng fā-yuán 暑症發原
*Tracing the Origins of Summer-Heat Disorders*
by [[李識候]] (Lǐ Shí-hòu, 清)

## About the work

A late-Qīng monograph dedicated to **summer-heat disorder** (*shǔ-zhèng* 暑症 / *shǔ-bìng* 暑病), in 1 *juǎn*. The text takes its place in the cluster of Qīng *wēn-bìng*-tradition monographs on summer-heat — alongside the earlier base-text Zhāng Fèng-kuí's *Shāng-shǔ quán-shū* ([[KR3eg005]]) and the canonical treatment of *shǔ-wēn* in the *wēn-bìng* synthesis (cf. *Wēn-bìng tiáo-biàn*, *Wēn-rè jīng-wěi*).

### Abstract

Summer-heat is one of the four principal warm-disease categories in the Qīng *wēn-bìng* synthesis, alongside *fēng-wēn* 風溫, *shī-wēn* 濕溫, and *wēn-yì* 溫疫. Lǐ Shí-hòu's monograph systematically traces the doctrinal origins of summer-heat back through the canonical *Nèi-jīng* loci (*Sù-wèn* "Rè-lùn" 熱論 etc.) and provides a syndrome-discriminating clinical apparatus.

Dating and authorial detail are not preserved. The text is conventionally placed in the late Qīng on doctrinal grounds. No CBDB record exists for the author.
