# Wú Yòu-kě wēn-yì lùn gē-kuò 吳又可溫疫論歌括
*Versified Summary of Wú Yòu-kě's "Treatise on Warm-Epidemic Disorders"*
by [[王旭高]] (Wáng Xù-gāo, 1798–1862)

## About the work

The mid-19th-century **versified mnemonic** of Wú Yǒu-xìng's foundational *Wēn-yì lùn* ([[KR3eg004]]), composed by the Wúxī physician Wáng Xù-gāo as part of his pedagogical *gē-jué* / *gē-kuò* (rhymed-formula) project. The companion piece to Wáng's *Xuē shì shī-rè lùn gē-jué* 薛氏濕熱論歌訣 ([[KR3eg015]]): together the two *gē-jué* / *gē-kuò* distill the canonical Qīng *wēn-bìng* monographs into mnemonic form for student memorisation.

### Abstract

Each proposition of Wú Yǒu-xìng's original text is rendered into four- or seven-character verse, with prose annotations as needed for clinical clarification. The pedagogical idiom is recognisably continuous with Wáng's broader *gē-jué* output (the *Yùnqì zhèngzhì gē jué* 運氣證治歌訣 [[KR3ea019]] and several *Shāng-hán* *gē-jué*).

Dating is approximate. Wáng's posthumous collected edition *Wáng Xù-gāo yī shū liù zhǒng* 王旭高醫書六種 (1862) is the principal extant witness. The *gē-kuò* itself is generally dated to the 1840s–1850s during Wáng's most productive years in Wúxī.

The work is doctrinally a faithful summary of Wú's pestilential-qi doctrine and its *mó-yuán* / nine-transmission framework; its contribution is pedagogical rather than substantive. It served as part of the standard Wúxī-Sūzhōu *wēn-bìng* curriculum and was widely reprinted in late-Qīng *cóngshū*.

## Other points of interest

The versified mnemonic form (*gē-jué* / *gē-kuò*) is a characteristic late-Qīng pedagogical genre; Wáng Xù-gāo is the principal exponent within the *wēn-bìng* curriculum.
