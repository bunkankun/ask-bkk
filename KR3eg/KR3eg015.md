# Xuē shì shī-rè lùn gē-jué 薛氏濕熱論歌訣
*Versified Summary of Mr. Xuē's "Treatise on Damp-Heat Disorders"*
by [[王旭高]] (Wáng Xù-gāo, 1798–1862)

## About the work

A mid-19th-century **versified mnemonic** of [[薛雪]] Xuē Shēng-bái's *Shī-rè tiáo-biàn* ([[KR3eg020]]), composed by the Wúxī physician Wáng Xù-gāo as part of his pedagogical *gē-jué* 歌訣 (rhymed-formula) project. Each numbered proposition of Xuē's original treatise is rendered as a four- or seven-character verse for memorisation, with prose annotations as needed.

### Abstract

The *gē-jué* genre — versified medical mnemonics — is a characteristic late-Qīng pedagogical form, particularly associated with the Wúxī-Sūzhōu medical schools. Wáng Xù-gāo's *gē-jué* output is substantial; the *Shī-rè lùn gē-jué* is the principal *wēn-bìng*-tradition contribution. Its companion piece is the *Wú Yòu-kě wēn-yì lùn gē-kuò* 吳又可溫疫論歌括 ([[KR3eg024]]), versifying Wú Yǒu-xìng's *Wēn-yì lùn*; together they constitute Wáng's distillation of the canonical *wēn-bìng* monographs into mnemonic form.

The dating bracket reflects Wáng's principal active period — the *gē-jué* are typically dated to the 1840s–1850s, with the collected works *Wáng Xù-gāo yī shū liù zhǒng* 王旭高醫書六種 printed posthumously in 1862. No earlier preface specifically dates this *gē-jué*.

The text is doctrinally a faithful summary of Xuē's original; its independent contribution is pedagogical rather than substantive. It became part of the standard mid-Qīng *wēn-bìng* curriculum in the Wúxī-Sūzhōu medical milieu and was widely reprinted.

## Other points of interest

The genre of versified medical mnemonics — *gē-jué* / *gē-kuò* — flourished particularly in the mid-19th-century Sūzhōu-Wúxī medical world; Wáng Xù-gāo is its principal *wēn-bìng*-school exponent.
