# Wēn-zhèng zhǐ-guī 溫證指歸
*Pointing to the Heart of Warm-Disease Syndromes*
by [[周魁]] (Zhōu Kuí, 清)

## About the work

A late-Qīng warm-disease pedagogical treatise organised around syndrome-differentiation and prescription, in the late-Qīng *wēn-bìng*-mainstream idiom. The title — *zhǐ-guī* 指歸 ("pointing to the heart of") — locates the work in the broader late-Qīng pedagogical *wēn-bìng* genre, alongside Lóu Jié's *Wēn-bìng zhǐ-nán* ([[KR3eg007]]) and Mǎ Zōng-yuán's *Wēn-bìng biàn-zhèng* ([[KR3eg019]]).

### Abstract

Zhōu Kuí's biographical record has not been preserved in standard reference works, and the *Wēn-zhèng zhǐ-guī* lacks a securely dated preface in widely accessible editions. The text is conventionally dated to the late Qīng on grounds of doctrinal alignment — it engages closely with the Wú Jū-tōng (*Wēn-bìng tiáo-biàn*) and Wáng Mèng-yīng (*Wēn-rè jīng-wěi*) doctrinal apparatus, which fixes the *terminus a quo* at ca. 1852, with the late-Qīng terminus extending to the end of the dynasty.

The doctrinal substance is a systematic mid-late-Qīng *wēn-bìng* syndrome-differentiation organised by the canonical four-fen and three-burner staging frames, with the standard prescription apparatus. The text's contribution is pedagogical rather than doctrinally innovative.
