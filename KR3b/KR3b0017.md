# Zhèn jì 陣紀
*Records of Battle Formations*
by [[何良臣]] (Hé Liángchén, *zì* Jìmíng 際明, 明)

## About the work

A 4-juan Late-Míng military-formations treatise by Hé Liángchén, in the *wō-kòu* defence tradition of Táng Shùnzhī (KR3b0016) and Qī Jì-guāng (KR3b0019). Composition: bracketed by the *wō-kòu* crisis period (1550s–1590s). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1560–1600.

### Abstract

The *Zhèn jì* is one of the principal Late-Míng military-tactical treatises. The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Bīng-jiā lèi*.
