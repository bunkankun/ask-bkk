# Liàn bīng shí jì 練兵實紀
*Veritable Records of Troop Training*
by [[戚繼光]] (Qī Jì-guāng, 1528–1587, 明)

## About the work

A 9-juan Late-Míng military training manual by Qī Jì-guāng, composed during his northern-frontier command (1568–1582) at Jì-zhèn 薊鎮 — the strategic command guarding the eastern approaches to Beijing. The work parallels and extends Qī's earlier *Jì xiào xīn shū* (KR3b0019), with extensive adaptation for cavalry-and-firearms warfare appropriate to the northern frontier (versus the infantry-centric Zhè-Mǐn coastal context of the earlier work). Composition: bracketed by Qī's Jì-zhèn tenure (1568–1582). The frontmatter brackets to 1568–1582.

### Abstract

The *Liàn bīng shí jì* is Qī's mature military-doctrinal work, complementing the earlier *Jì xiào xīn shū* with the northern-frontier perspective. Together the two works are the principal Late-Míng military-doctrinal corpus.

The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Bīng-jiā lèi*.
