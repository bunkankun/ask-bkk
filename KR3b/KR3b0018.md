# Jiāngnán jīng lüè 江南經畧
*Strategic Survey of the Lower Yangzi*
by [[鄭若曾]] (Zhèng Ruòzēng, 1505–1580, 明)

## About the work

An 8-juan Late-Míng comprehensive Lower-Yangzi-defence treatise by Zhèng Ruòzēng, drawing on his extensive *wōkòu*-defence experience under Hú Zōngxiàn. The work covers the complete Lower-Yangzi region with detailed treatment of fortifications, communications, troop deployments, and naval defence. Composition: bracketed by Zhèng's mature *wōkòu*-defence career (1560s–1570s). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1560–1580.

### Abstract

The *Jiāngnán jīng lüè* is one of the principal Late-Míng regional-defence treatises and the major scholarly work on Lower-Yangzi *wōkòu* defence. Companion to Zhèng's *Chóu hǎi tú biān*. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Bīngjiā lèi*.
