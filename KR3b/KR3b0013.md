# Hǔ qián jīng 虎鈐經
*The Tiger-Seal Classic*
by [[許洞]] (Xǔ Dòng, *zì* Dòngfū 洞夫, 宋)

## About the work

A 20-juan early-Northern-Sòng comprehensive military treatise by Xǔ Dòng, completed in *Xián-píng* 3 (1000). The work covers the full range of pre-Sòng military classical theory plus extensive practical-administrative material on troop deployment, fortification, frontier strategy, and military astrology. Composition: precisely datable to 1000. The frontmatter brackets to 1000–1000.

### Abstract

The *Hǔ qián jīng* is the principal pre-*Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* (KR3b0012) Northern Sòng military treatise. Substantively superseded by the imperial *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* of 1044, but substantively important as a private-scholarly compilation of the same generation.

The bibliographic record: *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Bīng-jiā lèi*.
