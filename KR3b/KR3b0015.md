# Shǒu chéng lù 守城錄
*Records of Defending Cities*
by [[陳規]] (Chén Guī, 1071–1141, 宋)

## About the work

A 4-juan classical Chinese siege-defence manual by Chén Guī, based on his successful defence of Dé-ān during the Jīng-kāng / Jiànyán Sòng-Jīn wars. The work is one of the principal Sòng-period military-technical treatises. Composition: bracketed by Chén's mature military career, with the work probably composed in the 1130s during his Dé-ān tenure. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1130–1141.

### Abstract

The *Shǒu chéng lù* is the principal Sòng-period siege-defence treatise. The work is heavily cited in modern military-historical scholarship on Sòng-Jīn warfare.

The bibliographic record: *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Bīng-jiā lèi*.
