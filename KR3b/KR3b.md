### Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3b is the *bīng-jiā* 兵家 ("military experts") subdivision of the Masters branch (*zǐ-bù* 子部) in the Sìkù fourfold classification. Wilkinson (§26.5) treats this as a single coherent corpus running from the foundational Warring-States manuals (*Sūn-zǐ*, *Wú-zǐ*, *Sīmǎ fǎ*, *Wèi Liáo-zǐ*) through the Hàn aphoristic and apocryphal layer (*Sān lüè*, *Liù tāo*) to the Sòng-period synthesis. The decisive moment in the formation of this division is the canonisation of the *Wǔ jīng qī shū* 武經七書 ("Seven Military Classics") in c. 1078–1080 under Sòng Shén-zōng as the curriculum for the military examinations; six pre-Hàn / Hàn texts plus the Táng-Sòng *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì* were thereby fixed as the authoritative canon of the genre, and remained so until the end of the empire (Wilkinson §26.5.3).

Inside KR3b, the division therefore stratifies cleanly into three transmissional layers: (1) the Warring-States and Hàn nucleus of the *Wǔ jīng qī shū*, much of it now controlled by the Yín-què-shān 銀雀山 bamboo-strip find of 1972 (*Sūn-zǐ*, *Sūn Bìn bīng-fǎ*, *Wèi Liáo-zǐ*, etc.); (2) the Sòng compilation and pseudepigraphical efflorescence (the *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào*, the *Hǔ qián jīng*, the *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì* itself, the *Wò-jī jīng*, the *Sù shū*), a period in which the genre was both standardised (canon, examinations) and saturated by retroactively-attributed material; and (3) the Late-Míng training-and-defence corpus of Qī Jì-guāng 戚繼光 and his contemporaries, written in direct response to the *wō-kòu* 倭寇 crisis on the Lower-Yangzi and Zhè-Mǐn coasts. The Wèn-yuān-gé Sìkù preserves twenty principal works; three further entries (KR3b0021–0023) are *jí-yì* fragments and pseudepigrapha included in the broader Kanripo digitisation.

### Important texts and text clusters

The *Wǔ jīng qī shū* canon (six of the seven are inside KR3b; the seventh, *Sūn Bìn bīng-fǎ*, was already lost by the Sòng):
- *Sūn-zǐ* 孫子 [[KR3b0003]] — the foundational Chinese military classic, c. late 4th–early 3rd c. BCE; the unbroken commentary tradition from Cáo Cāo onward is the spine of the entire division.
- *Wú-zǐ* 吳子 [[KR3b0004]] — second of the Seven; received in 6 *piān*, sharply reduced from the 48 *piān* recorded in the *Hàn-shū yìwén zhì*.
- *Sīmǎ fǎ* 司馬法 [[KR3b0005]] — third of the Seven; Warring-States redaction in Qí of materials traceable to the Spring-and-Autumn general Sīmǎ Ráng-jū.
- *Wèi Liáo-zǐ* 尉繚子 [[KR3b0006]] — fourth of the Seven; Yín-què-shān fragments confirm a pre-Hàn nucleus.
- *Liù tāo* 六韜 [[KR3b0002]] — pseudepigraphically ascribed to *Tài-gōng* 太公 (Lǚ Wàng); one of the Seven; covers civil-government (*Wén tāo*) alongside strictly military materials.
- *Huáng-shí gōng sān lüè* 黃石公三畧 [[KR3b0007]] — Hàn-period aphoristic work, one of the Seven.
- *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì* 李衞公問對 [[KR3b0010]] — seventh of the Seven; a post-Táng / pre-1080 dialogue placed in the mouths of Tài-zōng and Lǐ Jìng, pseudepigraphic but canonical.

The Sòng imperial and private encyclopedias:
- *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* 武經總要 [[KR3b0012]] — the comprehensive Northern-Sòng imperial military encyclopedia, commissioned 1038, completed 1044; the decisive technical and institutional reference for the late-imperial military arts.
- *Hǔ qián jīng* 虎鈐經 [[KR3b0013]] — Xǔ Dòng's 1000 CE private military compendium, the principal pre-*Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* Northern Sòng treatise.

Sòng essayistic and siege-defence works:
- *Hé bóshì bèi lùn* 何博士備論 [[KR3b0014]] — Hé Qù-fēi's *jiào shǐ* (lessons-of-history) essays, an exemplar of the Sòng officer-scholar genre.
- *Shǒu chéng lù* 守城錄 [[KR3b0015]] — Chén Guī's siege-defence treatise, drawn from his own command experience in the Sòng-Jīn wars.

The *Huáng-shí gōng* / *Sān lüè* satellite cluster:
- *Huáng-shí gōng sù shū* 黃石公素書 [[KR3b0009]] — Sòng-period aphoristic pseudepigraphon attached to the *Sān lüè* tradition, with a standard Zhāng Shāng-yīng 張商英 commentary.
- *Sān lüè zhí jiě* 三畧直解 [[KR3b0008]] — the standard early-Míng commentary on the *Sān lüè*, composed 1371 by Liú Yín 劉寅.

The Sòng-Sòng pseudepigraphical formation-classics:
- *Wò-jī jīng* 握奇經 [[KR3b0001]] — a Late-Táng / Five-Dynasties / Northern-Sòng compilation, traceable through Dú-gū Jí's *Bā-zhèn tú jì* to the Zhū-gě Liàng *bā-zhèn fǎ* tradition; the canonical Sòng *jīng* / *jiě* / *zàn* form is itself diagnostic.
- *Xīn-shū* 心書 [[KR3b0023]] — a late-Sòng / Yuán pseudepigraphon ascribed to Zhū-gě Liàng; excluded from the SKQS *Bīng-jiā lèi* main collection but circulated widely in Míng-Qīng military *cóng-shū*.

The Táng treatises:
- *Tài-bái yīn jīng* 太白陰經 [[KR3b0011]] — Lǐ Quán's Mid-Táng (c. 759) treatise, written against the background of the post-An-Lù-shān reform debate.

The Late-Míng *wō-kòu* defence and training corpus (Wilkinson §26.5.4 treats this as the second great cluster of the division):
- *Jì xiào xīn shū* 紀效新書 [[KR3b0019]] — Qī Jì-guāng's foundational training manual (1559–1567), drafted on his Zhè-Mǐn coastal command.
- *Liàn bīng shí jì* 練兵實紀 [[KR3b0020]] — Qī's mature northern-frontier doctrinal companion to the *Jì xiào xīn shū*.
- *Wǔ biān* 武編 [[KR3b0016]] — Táng Shùn-zhī's mid-Míng military encyclopedia, the scholarly counterpart to Qī's practitioner manuals.
- *Jiāng-nán jīng lüè* 江南經畧 [[KR3b0018]] — Zhèng Ruò-zēng's regional-defence treatise on Lower-Yangzi *wō-kòu* defence, companion to his *Chóu hǎi tú biān*.
- *Zhèn jì* 陣紀 [[KR3b0017]] — Hé Liáng-chén's Late-Míng tactical treatise on formations and unit drill.

The eleven-commentary recension of *Sūn-zǐ*:
- *Shí-yī jiā zhù Sūn-zǐ* 十一家註孫子 (Mèng-shì 注 portion) [[KR3b0022]] — the standard post-Sòng critical recension of the *Sūn-zǐ*, compiled by Jí Tiān-bǎo c. late 11th century; the Mèng-shì stratum of commentary is conventionally placed in the Liáng (502–557).

The outlier:
- *Fú-zǐ* 苻子 [[KR3b0021]] — Fú Lǎng's late-4th-c. *xuán-xué* / Daoist anecdotal *zhū-zǐ* work, surviving as Qīng *jí-yì* fragments by Mǎ Guó-hàn and Yán Kě-jūn; the Sìkù classification of this work under *bīng-jiā* is unusual and reflects late-imperial editorial expedience rather than the work's own genre.

### Important persons

- [[孫武]] — putative author of the *Sūn-zǐ*; the foundational figure of the entire division, even though the received text is a generation or two later than the 6th-c.-BCE general to whom it is attributed.
- [[呂望]] (Tài-gōng) — pseudepigraphic author of the *Liù tāo*; central to the *Wǔ jīng qī shū* canon under his ascribed name even though no Sòng-and-later critic accepted the attribution.
- [[吳起]] — Warring-States general of Wèi and Chǔ, author of the *Wú-zǐ*.
- [[司馬穰苴]] — late Spring-and-Autumn Qí general, name-bearer of the *Sīmǎ fǎ*.
- [[尉繚]] — Warring-States military thinker, author of the *Wèi Liáo-zǐ*.
- [[黃石公]] — legendary patron-figure to whom both the *Sān lüè* and the *Sù shū* are attributed; the persona, not the historical individual, is what matters in this division.
- [[李靖]] (571–649) — Táng general and statesman, the *dramatis persona* of the *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì* and the standing exemplar of the early-Táng general-strategist.
- [[李筌]] (fl. 759) — Mid-Táng official and author of the *Tài-bái yīn jīng*.
- [[曾公亮]] (998–1078) and [[丁度]] — joint compilers, by imperial commission, of the *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào*; defining the institutional voice of Northern-Sòng military scholarship.
- [[許洞]] — Northern-Sòng private compiler of the *Hǔ qián jīng* (1000), the largest non-imperial military compendium of the period.
- [[何去非]] (fl. 1082–1090) — Northern-Sòng *jiào-shǐ* essayist (*Hé bóshì bèi lùn*), part of the cohort of military-examination scholar-officers.
- [[陳規]] (1071–1141) — Sòng-Jīn-war defender of De-ān, author of the *Shǒu chéng lù*; the principal voice of practical siege defence in the division.
- [[劉寅]] — early-Míng commentator on the *Sān lüè*; standard reference for the Hóng-wǔ-era reading of the work.
- [[張商英]] — Northern-Sòng official, standard commentator on the *Sù shū*.
- [[唐順之]] (1507–1560) — Mid-Míng polymath, author of the *Wǔ biān*.
- [[何良臣]] — Late-Míng tactical writer (*Zhèn jì*).
- [[鄭若曾]] (1505–1580) — Late-Míng cartographer and *wō-kòu*-defence specialist, author of the *Jiāng-nán jīng lüè* and (outside KR3b) the *Chóu hǎi tú biān*.
- [[戚繼光]] (1528–1587) — the dominant Late-Míng military figure, author of both the *Jì xiào xīn shū* and the *Liàn bīng shí jì*; the only Míng general whose technical writings entered the central Late-Imperial military curriculum (Wilkinson §26.5.4).
- [[諸葛亮]] — the Three-Kingdoms military icon under whose name the *Wò-jī jīng* (indirectly) and the *Xīn-shū* (directly) circulated.
- [[苻朗]] — late-4th-c. Former-Qín literatus and author of the *Fú-zǐ*; an outlier to the division.
- [[孟氏]] — anonymous Liáng-dynasty commentator on the *Sūn-zǐ*, known only from his stratum within the *Shí-yī jiā* recension.

### Topics

- **Canonisation and the examination curriculum.** The single most important fact about KR3b is that the genre was fixed by the c. 1078–1080 selection of seven works as the *Wǔ jīng qī shū* — the curriculum for the Sòng military examinations and, with adjustments, for every later military examination system. The Sìkù *Bīng-jiā lèi* organisation reflects this canon directly (Wilkinson §26.5.3).
- **Pseudepigraphy and the retro-attribution layer.** A high proportion of the works in the division are demonstrably or probably pseudepigraphic: the *Liù tāo* (ascribed to Lǚ Wàng), the *Sān lüè* and *Sù shū* (ascribed to Huáng-shí gōng), the *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì* (post-Táng, pre-1080), the *Wò-jī jīng* (Sòng, ascribed to the Hàn), the *Xīn-shū* (Sòng, ascribed to Zhū-gě Liàng). The pattern is structural to the genre: military authority required ancient names. The dating policy in this knowledgebase places these works at the date of the received recension, not at the attributed author's lifetime.
- **The commentary tradition on the *Sūn-zǐ*.** From Cáo Cāo through the Sòng *Shí-yī jiā* compilation by Jí Tiān-bǎo, the *Sūn-zǐ* commentary tradition is the longest continuous textual project in the genre. The Mèng-shì stratum [[KR3b0022]] is one of the eleven; the others (Cáo Cāo, Lǐ Quán, Du Mu, Méi Yáo-chén, Wáng Xī, Hé Yán-xī, Zhāng Yù, Jiǎ Lín, Du You, Chén Hào) are not separately extant in the SKQS *Bīng-jiā lèi*, only in the eleven-commentary edition.
- **Yín-què-shān 銀雀山 (1972) and 20th-c. critical scholarship.** The Hàn-tomb find recovered late-Western-Hàn versions of the *Sūn-zǐ*, *Sūn Bìn bīng-fǎ*, *Wèi Liáo-zǐ* and other military texts, decisively reshaping modern philological work on the corpus (Wilkinson §26.5.1.1, §26.5.3). Although the bamboo strips themselves lie outside the SKQS, every modern critical edition of the relevant KR3b texts uses them as principal collation source.
- **Civil-military separation under the Sòng.** The Sòng deliberate downgrading of the military profession (Wilkinson §26.6) is the political background against which the Sòng compilation programme — *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào*, *Hǔ qián jīng*, *Lǐ Wèi-gōng wèn duì*, the canonisation of the Seven — must be read: a state attempt to codify and control military expertise at exactly the moment when general-officer prestige was being squeezed out of the elite culture.
- **Late-Míng *wō-kòu* defence and military reform.** The Late-Míng cluster (KR3b0016–KR3b0020) is unified by the *wō-kòu* crisis on the Lower-Yangzi and Zhè-Mǐn coasts. Qī Jì-guāng's *Jì xiào xīn shū* is the central work; Táng Shùn-zhī's *Wǔ biān* and Zhèng Ruò-zēng's *Jiāng-nán jīng lüè* are the encyclopedic and regional-defence companions; Hé Liáng-chén's *Zhèn jì* is the tactical formal reference.
- **Siege warfare and the technical layer.** From Chén Guī's *Shǒu chéng lù* through the relevant chapters of the *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* and the *Hǔ qián jīng* to Qī Jì-guāng's training manuals, the division contains the principal pre-modern Chinese textual record of siege technology, fortifications, weapons and unit drill — a layer that is conceptually separable from the strategic-philosophical layer of the *Sūn-zǐ* tradition.

### Timeline

- c. -400 to -378 — *Wú-zǐ* 吳子 [[KR3b0004]] — Wú Qǐ's working life provides the upper bound.
- c. -400 to -300 — *Sīmǎ fǎ* 司馬法 [[KR3b0005]] — Warring-States Qí redaction of older Sīmǎ Ráng-jū materials.
- c. -350 to -200 — *Wèi Liáo-zǐ* 尉繚子 [[KR3b0006]] — Yín-què-shān strips establish a pre-Hàn nucleus.
