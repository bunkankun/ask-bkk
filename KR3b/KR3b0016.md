# Wǔ biān 武編
*Compendium of Military Affairs*
by [[唐順之]] (Táng Shùnzhī, *hào* Jīngchuān 荊川, 1507–1560, 明)

## About the work

A 10-juan Mid-Míng comprehensive military encyclopedia by Táng Shùnzhī, drawing on his experience in coastal defence against the *wō kòu* in the Jiā-jìng era. Composition: bracketed by Táng's working life, with the work likely composed in the 1540s–1550s during the height of the *wō kòu* crisis. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1540–1560.

### Abstract

The *Wǔ biān* is the major Mid-Míng military encyclopedia, complementing the slightly later *Jì xiào xīn shū* of Qī Jì-guāng (KR3b0019) — Táng's contemporary and fellow *wō-kòu* defender. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Bīng-jiā lèi*.
