# Dèng Xī-zǐ 鄧析子
*Master Dèng Xī*
attributed to [[鄧析]] (Dèng Xī, ca. 545–501 BCE in legend, 周)

## About the work

A one-juan *Míng-jiā* (School of Names) and *Fǎ-jiā* (Legalist) classical text in 2 篇 — *Wú hòu* 無厚 (Without Thickness) and *Zhuǎn cí* 轉辭 (Turning Words) — pseudepigraphically attributed to Dèng Xī, the legendary Spring-and-Autumn-era *Míng-jiā* thinker. The actual composition is universally Late Warring States or early Hàn. The work is brief but substantively important as one of the few pre-modern preservations of *Míng-jiā* technical terminology and argumentative method.

### Abstract

The *Dèng Xī-zǐ* is one of the principal pre-modern preservations of the *Míng-jiā* tradition. Composition window: late Warring States to early Hàn. The frontmatter brackets to ca. -300 to -100. The bibliographic record: *Hàn shū yìwén zhì*; *Suí shū jīngjí zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Fǎ-jiā lèi*.
