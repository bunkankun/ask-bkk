# Guǎn-zǐ bǔ zhù 管子補注
*Supplementary Commentary on the* Guǎn-zǐ
by [[劉績]] (Liú Jì, b. 1468, 明)

## About the work

A 24-juan Mid-Míng supplementary commentary on the *Guǎn-zǐ* (KR3c0001), composed by Liú Jì in 1490. The work supplements Fáng Xuánlíng's Táng-period commentary with additional glossing, textual collation, and explanatory remarks. Composition: precisely datable to 1490. The frontmatter brackets to 1490–1490.

### Abstract

The *Guǎn-zǐ bǔ zhù* is the principal Mid-Míng *Guǎn-zǐ* commentary, complementing Fáng Xuánlíng's Táng layer. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐ-bù — Fǎ-jiā lèi*.
