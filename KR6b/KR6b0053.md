# Zhōng běnqǐ jīng 中本起經
*Middle Origin-Sūtra (Continuation of the Buddha-Biography)*
by [[曇果]] (Tánguǒ) and [[康孟詳]] (Kāng Mèngxiáng), 譯

## About the work

A two-fascicle Eastern-Hàn Buddha-biography sūtra continuing the narrative from the Bodhi-tree onward — the *Zhōng* ("middle") of the title indicates the middle phase of the Buddha-biography after the awakening, complementing [[KR6b0040|*Xiūxíng běnqǐ jīng* 修行本起經 (T184)]] which covers the prior phases (the *běn* / "origin"). The Taishō head-note identifies the sūtra as 「次名四部僧」 ("next to be called the *Sūtra of the Four-fold Saṃgha*") and notes that it draws material from the *Dīrghāgama* (出長阿含). Translated by Tánguǒ 曇果 and Kāng Mèngxiáng 康孟詳. Signature: 「後漢西域沙門曇果共康孟詳譯」.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the standard *evaṃ mayā śrutam* formula in the archaic Hàn Buddhist register: 「阿難曰：吾昔從佛聞如是」. No separate preface or postface in the source file.

### Abstract

T196's translation can be dated to Jiànān 建安 12 = 207 CE based on the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* records. It is the post-awakening Buddha-biography continuing T184; the two together constitute the canonical Eastern-Hàn Chinese Buddha-biography cycle. The Indic source is conventionally identified with a now-lost *Madhyamāgama* / *Dīrghāgama*-class Buddha-biography of the Mahāsāṃghika or Sarvāstivāda tradition, with internal evidence (the head-note's reference to 出長阿含) suggesting a *Dīrghāgama*-related Indic exemplar.
