# Zá pìyù jīng 雜譬喻經 (T204)
*Sūtra of Miscellaneous Parables*
by [[支婁迦讖]] (Lokakṣema, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle *pìyù* (譬喻 = *avadāna* / *dṛṣṭānta*, "parable") anthology of some 11 short narratives, attributed in the Chinese canon to [[支婁迦讖]] Lokakṣema. Signature: 「後漢月支沙門支婁迦讖譯」.

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T204 is one of three closely-related *Zá pìyù jīng* anthologies in the Taishō (T204, [[KR6b0062|T205]], [[KR6b0064|T207]]), all titled identically but distinguished by attribution: T204 to Lokakṣema (Hàn), [[KR6b0062|T205]] anonymous Hàn-register, [[KR6b0064|T207]] compiled by [[道略]] Dàoluè (Latter-Qín). Modern scholarship (Mizuno; Nattier) is sceptical of the attribution of T204 to Lokakṣema: the linguistic register and content are inconsistent with Lokakṣema's better-attested Mahāyāna translations and the work is more likely a slightly later compilation that was retrospectively attached to his name in catalog tradition. The bracket 178–200 reflects Lokakṣema's documented active period at Luòyáng if the attribution is accepted.

The *Zá pìyù jīng* anthologies as a group are foundational sources for the East-Asian Buddhist *pìyù* genre — short *exempla* narratives used for popular preaching — and influenced the development of Chinese Buddhist popular literature.
