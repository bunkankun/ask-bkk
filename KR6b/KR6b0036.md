# Fóshuō Jiǔsè lù jīng 佛說九色鹿經 (T181b)
*Sūtra of the Nine-Coloured Deer (alternative recension)*
by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle alternative recension of Zhī Qiān's [[KR6b0035|*Jiǔsè lù jīng* (T181a)]], with slightly different textual transmission. Signature: 「吳月氏優婆塞支謙譯」. For the *Ruru-jātaka* narrative and bibliography, see [[KR6b0035]].

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T181b is the parallel-recension companion of T181a in the Sòng / Yuán canonical traditions. The translation is identical to T181a in substance; minor textual variation reflects manuscript transmission.
