# Yì-chū púsà běnqǐ jīng 異出菩薩本起經
*Variant Bodhisattva Origin-Sūtra*
by [[聶道真]] (Niè Dàozhēn, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Buddha-biography sūtra recounting the previous lives of Śākyamuni in compressed narrative. The title's 異出 ("variant version") signals that this is an alternative Buddha-biography presentation, complementing the translations of [[KR6b0040|T184]], [[KR6b0041|T185]], and [[KR6b0042|T186]]. Translated by Niè Dàozhēn 聶道真 in the Western Jìn. Signature: 「西晉居士聶道真譯」.

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T188 is one of Niè Dàozhēn's documented Western-Jìn translations, dated to his post-Dharmarakṣa active period in the late third / early fourth century. The Indic source is unidentified; the *yì-chū* ("variant version") title-element suggests the work was issued explicitly as an alternative recension of Buddha-biography material already available in earlier Chinese translation. The brief single-fascicle format places T188 in the *avadāna*-summary category of Buddha-biography rather than the full-length *Lalitavistara*-class genre.
