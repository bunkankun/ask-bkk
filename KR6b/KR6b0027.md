# Shǎnzǐ jīng 睒子經
*Sūtra of Śyāmaka*
by [[聖堅]] (Shèngjiān / \*Āryasthira, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Western-Qín version of the *Śyāmaka-jātaka*, by [[聖堅]] Shèngjiān. Signature: 「西秦沙門聖堅奉詔譯」. Together with the two further recensions [[KR6b0028|T175b]] and [[KR6b0029|T175c]] this constitutes the three Taishō witnesses to Shèngjiān's Chinese rendering of the *Śyāmaka* narrative — the same translation transmitted in slightly different textual recensions across the *Korean*, *Sòng*, *Yuán*, and *Míng* canonical traditions, distinguished in the Taishō by the suffix-letters 175a / 175b / 175c.

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical signature.

### Abstract

T175a is the *Korean*-canon recension of Shèngjiān's *Śyāmaka-jātaka* translation, datable to the Western-Qín period 388–431. The Indic source is the *Sāmajātaka* / *Śyāmaka-jātaka* (Pāli *Jātaka* no. 540). The doublet relationship between this recension and [[KR6b0026|the anonymous T174]] has been one of the standard test-cases for studying the catalog-history of *Śyāmaka*-translations into Chinese.

For broader context on the *Śyāmaka-jātaka* narrative and its East-Asian reception, see [[KR6b0026|T174]].
