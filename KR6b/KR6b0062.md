# Zá pìyù jīng 雜譬喻經 (T205)
*Sūtra of Miscellaneous Parables*
by [[失譯]] (anonymous translator, 譯; appended to the Hàn register)

## About the work

A two-fascicle *pìyù*-anthology of 32 narratives, anonymous translation appended to the Hàn register. Signature: 「失譯人名附後漢錄」. The opening parable: "A bodhisattva delivering beings is like a skilled wet-nurse caring for an infant: there are four duties — first, to bathe him clean; second, to feed him so he is full; third, to lay him to sleep peacefully; fourth, to protect him from harm…"

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical signature.

### Abstract

T205 is one of three Taishō *Zá pìyù jīng* anthologies (T204, T205, T207); see [[KR6b0061|T204]] for the full series-discussion. The Hàn-register attribution in the catalog is conventional rather than documented; modern scholarship (Mizuno) treats this anthology as one of several anonymous Hàn-period Chinese-language *pìyù* compilations whose actual translation date and provenance remain uncertain.
