# Xiūxíng běnqǐ jīng 修行本起經
*Sūtra of the Origins of Cultivation (Buddha-biography)*
by [[竺大力]] (Mahābala) and [[康孟詳]] (Kāng Mèngxiáng), 譯

## About the work

A two-fascicle Eastern-Hàn Buddha-biography sūtra recounting the lineage and youth of the future Buddha — his Tuṣita-heaven previous life, his descent, his birth in Kapilavastu, and the early phases of his renunciation. Translated jointly by Mahābala 竺大力 (Indian *śramaṇa*) and Kāng Mèngxiáng 康孟詳 (Hàn-Chinese collaborator) in the late Eastern Hàn. Signature: 「後漢西域三藏竺大力共康孟詳譯」.

T184 is one of the earliest stand-alone Buddha-biography texts in Chinese — a foundational document of the Hàn-period reception of Buddhist hagiography — and the source-text for many later sinophone Buddha-biography elaborations.

### Abstract

T184's translation is documented in the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 to Jiàn'ān 建安 2 = 197 CE — one of the most precisely-dated Hàn-period translations after Ān Shìgāo's. The Indic source corresponds to a *Lalitavistara*-class Buddha-biography in early-Mahāyāna form, though no exact Indic recension can be identified. The work is paired with [[KR6b0053|*Zhōng běnqǐ jīng* (T196)]] — translated jointly by Tánguǒ 曇果 and Kāng Mèngxiáng — which continues the Buddha-biography from the *bodhi*-tree onward; the two texts together constitute a complete Buddha-biography cycle in the Eastern-Hàn Chinese tradition.

The *Buddha-biography* genre played a foundational role in the Hàn-period sinophone formation of Buddhism: T184 and T196 together gave the Chinese reading public their first sustained narrative encounter with the life of the Buddha, and provided the imaginative-doctrinal framework within which Mahāyāna *bodhisattva*-doctrine and later *Buddhāvataṃsaka*-class scriptures could be received.
