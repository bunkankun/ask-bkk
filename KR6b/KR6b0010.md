# Dà-shèng běn-shēng xīn-dì-guān jīng qiǎn-zhù kē-wén 大乘本生心地觀經淺註科文
*Topical-Outline (Kē-wén) to the Shallow Notes on the Mahāyāna Mind-Ground Sūtra*
by [[來舟]] (Lái Zhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A single-fascicle *kē-wén* 科文 (topical-outline) accompanying Lái Zhōu's eleven-fascicle [[KR6b0011|*Qiǎn-zhù* 淺註 (X367)]] commentary on [[KR6b0008|T159]]. The *kē-wén* genre is the standard East-Asian Buddhist diagrammatic-tabular outline of a *sūtra*'s structure: each section, sub-section, and clause is labelled with a tree-form numbering (○九, 二, 三 …), giving a complete topical map of the *sūtra* organised hierarchically. The *kē-wén* is normally read alongside the line-by-line commentary, whose section-divisions correspond to the *kē-wén*'s nodes.

### Prefaces

The fascicle bears no separate preface; the author and commitment-of-method are inherited from the companion *Xuán-shì* [[KR6b0009]] and *Qiǎn-zhù* [[KR6b0011]]. The catalog meta does not name an author for KR6b0010 explicitly, but the work is universally treated as part of Lái Zhōu's commentarial set on T159 and is signed in the canonical apparatus accordingly.

### Abstract

The *Kē-wén* gives the explicit topical-outline structure that the line-by-line *Qiǎn-zhù* assumes; it is the navigational armature of the commentary. The outline divides the *sūtra* into its *xù* 序 / *zhèng* 正 / *liú-tōng* 流通 sections in the standard Chinese Buddhist exegetical scheme, then sub-divides each chapter and verse-paragraph into its constituent topical units. This work is therefore not exegetical in its own right but is the indispensable companion-text by which the *Qiǎn-zhù*'s commentarial divisions are read.
