# Zá pìyù jīng 雜譬喻經 (T207)
*Sūtra of Miscellaneous Parables (Dàoluè's Compilation)*
by [[道略]] (Dàoluè, 集)

## About the work

A single-fascicle *pìyù*-anthology of some 39 short narratives, compiled (集 *jí*) by the Latter-Qín *bhikṣu* Dàoluè 道略. Signature: 「比丘道略集」. The opening contents-list enumerates: 「雀離寺師將沙彌下喻、聖王生九百九十九子喻、兄弟二人共為沙門喻、伎兒作種種伎喻、比丘被擯喻、目連與弟子下耆闍崛…」 ("The Sparrow-Departure Monastery's *upādhyāya* with his *śrāmaṇera* parable; the Sage-King with 999 sons parable; the two brothers who became *śramaṇa* together parable…").

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; the only paratext is the compiler-signature.

### Abstract

T207 is the third Taishō *Zá pìyù jīng*; see [[KR6b0061|T204]] for the series-overview. T207 is distinguished by being a *jí* (editorial compilation) rather than a fresh translation: Dàoluè drew on the *pìyù* / *avadāna* material in Kumārajīva's translation circle and selected and arranged the narratives. The bracket 405–412 corresponds to Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān period under whose patronage Dàoluè worked. The companion compilation [[KR6b0065|T208 *Zhòngjīng zhuànzá pìyù*]] is also Dàoluè's, with similar editorial method.
