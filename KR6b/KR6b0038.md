# Fóshuō Lùmǔ jīng 佛說鹿母經 (T182b)
*Sūtra of the Mother-Deer (alternative recension)*
by [[竺法護]] (Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle alternative recension of Dharmarakṣa's [[KR6b0037|*Lù-mǔ jīng* (T182a)]], in slightly different textual state. Signature: 「西晉三藏法師竺法護譯」. For narrative content and bibliography, see [[KR6b0037]].

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T182b is the parallel-recension companion of T182a in the Sòng / Yuán canonical traditions; the translation is identical to T182a in substance.
