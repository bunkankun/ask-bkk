# Pìyù jīng 譬喻經
*Sūtra of Parables*
by [[義淨]] (Yìjìng, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle *pìyù* sūtra translated by Yìjìng 義淨 in the Tang. Signature: 「大唐三藏法師義淨譯」. The opening sets a Jetavana frame in Yìjìng's idiosyncratic Sanskritising orthography (室羅伐城逝多 = *Śrāvastī Jetavana*).

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T217 falls within Yìjìng's documented post-695 translation programme at Cháng'ān and Luòyáng (700–713). The Indic source corresponds to a Mūlasarvāstivādin Vinaya-narrative *avadāna* compatible with Yìjìng's principal Indic-source orientation.
