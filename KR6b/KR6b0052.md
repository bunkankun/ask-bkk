# Fó-shuō Shí'èr-yóu jīng 佛說十二遊經
*Sūtra of the Twelve-Year Wanderings of the Buddha*
by [[迦留陀伽]] (\*Kālodaka, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Buddha-biography text recounting the Buddha's twelve years of post-awakening travels and ministry. Translated by Kālodaka 迦留陀伽 in the Eastern Jìn. Signature: 「東晉西域沙門迦留陀伽譯」. The opening narrates the Bodhisattva's renunciation and his early-pilgrim study under the *brāhmaṇa* of Gautama-gotra (婆羅門, 姓瞿曇).

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T195 is one of the more compact Buddha-biographies in Chinese, focused not on the descent and birth (the *Lalitavistara*-genre concern) but on the post-awakening twelve-year ministry — i.e., a *vinaya*-narrative-class Buddha-biography. The text bridges Buddha-biography and *vinaya*-narrative literature: its concern with the Buddha's ministry and the *cāturmāsa* travel-cycle is consistent with a *Madhyamāgama* / *vinaya-vibhaṅga* type Indic source.
