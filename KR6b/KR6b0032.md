# Qián-shì sān-zhuǎn jīng 前世三轉經
*Sūtra on the Three Past-Life Transformations*
by [[法炬]] (Fǎjù, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle *jātaka* / *avadāna* on the doctrine of *karmic* transmigration through three previous-life states, framed as a Buddha-discourse at Jetavana to an assembly of five hundred *bhikṣu*-arhats. Translated by Fǎjù 法炬 (\*Dharma-bhāṇaka), a Western-Jìn translator. Signature (with Taishō apparatus glosses): 「西晉沙門法炬譯」.

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical translator-signature.

### Abstract

T178 is one of Fǎjù's documented Western-Jìn translations from the late third / early fourth century; Fǎjù's recorded translation period is c. 290–307 CE at Luòyáng. The Indic source is unidentified; the *jātaka* is a relatively short *karmic*-illustration narrative rather than a formally-named *avadāna* of the standard collections.
