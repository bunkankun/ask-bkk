# Yīnguǒ běnqǐ jīng 因果本起經
*Sūtra of the Origin of Causes-and-Effects*
by [[求那跋陀羅]] (Guṇabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Buddha-biography text preserved in the Fáng-shān shí-jīng 房山石經 ("Stone-Sutra of Mount Fáng") — the carved-stone Buddhist canon at Yúnjū-sì 雲居寺 in Fáng-shān, Hebei (a project initiated in the early seventh century and continuing through the Liáo and Jīn). The text is a Buddha-biography frame opening at Jetavana, with the Buddha hearing the *bhikṣu*s discussing past-life causal narratives and entering to discourse on the *yīn-guǒ* relationships. Attributed by the Fáng-shān catalog to Guṇabhadra 求那跋陀羅.

### Abstract

T-equivalent for [[KR6b0046]] is the *Fáng-shān shí-jīng* edition F02n0069. The text is closely related to and partly overlaps with [[KR6b0045|T189 *Guò-qù xiàn-zài yīnguǒ jīng*]] — the four-fascicle Liú-Sòng Buddha-biography that is its likely textual relative or a source-fragment. The catalog attribution to Guṇabhadra is conventional rather than independently documented; modern scholarship treats the text as an extracted or condensed witness of the *yīn-guǒ* Buddha-biography tradition into which T189 also belongs.

## Other points of interest

The Fángshān shíjīng preserves several texts not present in the standard Taishō or *Dūnhuáng* manuscript collections; [[KR6b0046]] is one of the more obscure of these. The Fángshān carving project provides a unique textual-archaeological witness to texts circulating in mid-Tang and Liáo-period Buddhist communities that have otherwise been lost.
