### Scope and scholarly tradition

*Wǔjīng zǒngyì* 五經總義 is the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 sub-category that collects works which range across more than one of the Confucian canon rather than expounding a single classic. Wilkinson treats it under §28.5 as one of the two branches the Qing bibliographers added to the *jīngbù* 經部 (the other being *Sì shū* 四書), correlating to the *Wǔjīng zhùshū* 五經注疏 / *Shísān jīng zhùshū* 十三經注疏 print tradition described under his §28.7. In practice, the division gathers four distinct kinds of writing: (1) Hàn–Táng compendia of glosses, pronunciations, and inter-school disagreements which treat the canon as a whole; (2) Sòng–Yuán *jīngshuō* 經說 essay collections produced under the Dàoxué reorientation; (3) text-critical, collation, and *zhíyīn* 直音 handbooks supporting block-printing of the *Classics*; and (4) the long Qing arc of *kǎozhèng* 考證 philology that ends with the apparatus criticus on which Ruǎn Yuán's 阮元 1815 *Shísān jīng zhùshū jiào kān jì* 十三經注疏校勘記 was built.

Two structural points govern the division. First, its chronological range runs from the second-century-CE exchange between Xǔ Shèn 許愼 and Zhèng Xuán 鄭玄 — Wilkinson §28.7.2 makes Lù Démíng's 陸德明 *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 (compiled c. 583, the second of the "two most influential annotated editions of the *Classics* up to the Song") the keystone of the early stratum — through to the late-eighteenth-century Sūzhōu school of Huì Dòng 惠棟 and his disciple Yú Xiāokè 余蕭客. Second, the *Sìkù* compilers consistently used this division to register methodological reorientations: the appearance of Liú Chǎng's 劉敞 *Gōngshì Qī jīng xiǎo zhuàn* 公是七經小傳 ([[KR1g0004]]) for the Sòng break with HànTáng *zhāngjù* 章句, of Sūn Jué's 孫瑴 *Gǔwēi shū* 古微書 ([[KR1g0032]]) for the pre-modern recovery of the *chènwěi* 讖緯 apocrypha (Wilkinson §28.6), and of Huì Dòng's *Jiǔ jīng gǔ yì* 九經古義 ([[KR1g0024]]) for the programmatic Hàn-learning turn. The single conspicuous gap in the present catalog is the *Qīndìng fānyì Wǔjīng Sìshū* 欽定繙譯五經四書 ([[KR1g0019]], the Qiánlóng-era Manchu translation), for which no per-text note exists yet.

### Important texts and text clusters

- *Bó Wǔjīng yìyì* 駁五經異義 [[KR1g0001]] and *Zhèng zhì* 鄭志 [[KR1g0002]] — the two Hàn–Wèi works (Xǔ Shèn → Zhèng Xuán → Zhèng Xiǎotóng 鄭小同) that put the new-text / old-text disputes of the Eastern Hàn into the canonical record; both survive only as fragment-reconstructions assembled by *Sìkù* editors.
- *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 [[KR1g0003]] — Lù Démíng's thirty-*juàn* *yīnyì* 音義 corpus for fourteen classics (the *Thirteen Classics* minus the *Mèngzǐ*, plus *Lǎozǐ* and *Zhuāngzǐ*); Wilkinson identifies it as the single philological keystone of the pre-Sòng canonical tradition, citing roughly 230 Hàn–Six-Dynasties authorities now otherwise lost.
- The **Northern Sòng break** — *Gōngshì Qī jīng xiǎo zhuàn* 公是七經小傳 [[KR1g0004]] (Liú Chǎng's conjectural-emendation programme, regularly named as the proximate precursor of Wáng Ānshí's 王安石 *Xīn jīngyì* 新經義) and *Chéng shì jīng shuō* 程氏經說 [[KR1g0005]] (Chéng Yí 程頤 on the classics outside the *Yì*, including the redacted *Dàxué* 大學 that Zhū Xī 朱熹 later took as the basis of his *Zhāngjù* 章句).
- The **Sòng *tú* / *zhíyīn* / *yán'gé* collation cluster** — *Liù jīng tú* 六經圖 [[KR1g0006]] (309 illustrated schemata, Yáng Jiǎ 楊甲 supplemented by Máo Bānghàn 毛邦翰); *Liù jīng zhèng wù* 六經正誤 [[KR1g0007]] (Máo Jūzhèng's 毛居正 1223 collator's notes, including engraver falsifications); *Jiǔ jīng sān zhuàn yán'gé lì* 九經三傳沿革例 [[KR1g0008]] (Yuè Kē's 岳珂 seven-principle editorial manifesto for the Xiāngtāi 相臺 cut); and the anonymous *Míngběn pái zì Jiǔ jīng zhí yīn* 明本排字九經直音 [[KR1g0012]] (a SòngYuán Míngzhōu 明州 commercial sound-gloss handbook, surviving in the 1287 cut).
- *Róngtáng Sì shū guǎn jiàn* 融堂四書管見 [[KR1g0009]] — Qián Shí's 錢時 LùCíhú 陸慈湖 *xīnxué* 心學 commentary on a heterodox "Sì shū" (*Lúnyǔ*, *Xiàojīng*, *Dàxué*, *Zhōngyōng*), pointedly excluding the *Mèngzǐ*; the principal pre-Yángmíng *xīnxué* counter-canon in the division.
- *Liù jīng ào lùn* 六經奧論 [[KR1g0011]] — an anonymous late-Sòng examination-prep compendium falsely ascribed to Zhèng Qiáo 鄭樵; included as the textbook *Sìkù* case-study in attribution criticism (the editors' six-strand demolition is a model of *kǎozhèng* method).
- The **Míng eclectic-philological cluster** — *Jiǎn duān lù* 簡端錄 [[KR1g0016]] (Shào Bǎo 邵寶, the Wúxī 無錫 ChéngZhū restorationist precursor to the Dōnglín 東林 movement), *Wǔ jīng jī yí* 五經稽疑 [[KR1g0017]] (Zhū Mùjié 朱睦㮮, from inside the Zhōu princely Wànjuàntáng 萬卷堂 library), and *Jīngdiǎn jī yí* 經典稽疑 [[KR1g0018]] (Chén Yàowén 陳耀文, whom the *Sìkù* editors call "a footstep in the empty valley" — the proximate Míng precursor of Qing *kǎozhèng*).
- *Qī jīng Mèngzǐ kǎo wén bǔ yí* 七經孟子考文補遺 [[KR1g0020]] — Yamai Kanae 山井鼎 (d. 1728) and Mononobe Mitsuyoshi 物觀, the Tokugawa-Japan critical collation against the Ashikaga School's Sòng cuts and Táng-line *kohon* 古本; the first systematic comparison with Japanese variants and a re-imported resource for high-Qing *kǎozhèng*.
- The **Qing *kǎozhèng* arc** — *Jiǔ jīng wù zì* 九經誤字 [[KR1g0021]] (Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武, founding-Qing erratum on the late-Míng Imperial-Academy and market cuts), *Jīng wèn* 經問 [[KR1g0022]] (Máo Qílíng 毛奇齡's polemical wing, naming Gù Yánwǔ, Yán Ruòqú 閻若璩, and Hú Wèi 胡渭 alongside virtually every major prior commentator), *Jiǔ jīng gǔ yì* 九經古義 [[KR1g0024]] (Huì Dòng's foundational Wú-school 吳派 Hàn-learning programme), *Qún jīng bǔ yì* 羣經補義 [[KR1g0028]] (Jiāng Yǒng 江永, the Wǎnpài 皖派 founder), *Shí sān jīng zhùshū zhèng zì* 十三經注疏正字 [[KR1g0026]] (Shěn Tíngfāng 沈廷芳, the proximate apparatus criticus ancestor of Ruǎn Yuán's 1815 *Jiào kān jì* 校勘記), and *Gǔ jīng jiě gōu chén* 古經解鉤沉 [[KR1g0031]] (Yú Xiāokè, Huì Dòng's disciple, the methodological model for the late-Qing fragment-recovery school of Mǎ Guóhàn 馬國翰 and Huáng Shì 黃奭).
- *Gǔwēi shū* 古微書 [[KR1g0032]] — Sūn Jué's 孫瑴 late-Míng compendium of Hàn-period *chènwěi* fragments, the foundational pre-modern recovery of the apocrypha (Wilkinson §28.6), placed by the *Sìkù* compilers at the end of *Wǔjīng zǒngyì* as a structural appendix.

### Important persons

- [[鄭玄]] — the figure around whom the division's Hàn stratum is organised, both as author of the *Bó Wǔjīng yìyì* and as the implied authority behind the *Zhèng zhì*.
- [[陸德明]] — compiler of the *Jīngdiǎn shìwén*, the indispensable Suí–Táng *yīnyì* corpus for the canon as a whole.
- [[劉敞]] — pivot from HànTáng *zhāngjù* to Sòng conjectural emendation.
- [[程頤]] — anchor of the ChéngZhū commentarial reorientation visible across the Sòng entries.
- [[岳珂]] — author of the most lucid surviving Sòng manifesto on text-critical principles for cutting the *Classics*.
- [[邵寶]] — the late-Míng Wúxī ChéngZhū restorationist whose marginalia anchor the Dōnglín-precursor lineage.
- [[山井鼎]] (Yamai Kanae) — Tokugawa philologist whose Japanese-variant collation, with [[物觀]] (Mononobe), entered the imperial library in 1731 and re-shaped late-Qing collation practice.
- [[顧炎武]] — opens the Qing arc; the *Jiǔ jīng wù zì* is the founding-Qing erratum of the canon.
- [[惠棟]] — programmatic Hàn-learning of the Wú school 吳派; the *Jiǔ jīng gǔ yì* is its methodological creed.
- [[江永]] — Wǎnpài founder; the *Qún jīng bǔ yì* is the prose model of the high-Qing Anhui method.
- [[毛奇齡]] — the polemical wing, indispensable for tracking inter-scholar disputes in early Qing.
- [[沈廷芳]] — the proximate apparatus-criticus ancestor of Ruǎn Yuán.
- [[余蕭客]] — Huì Dòng's disciple; model for late-Qing fragment recovery.
- [[孫瑴]] — sole representative of *chènwěi* fragment recovery within the division.

### Topics

- **Old-text vs. new-text (古文／今文) in the canon as a whole.** Wilkinson §28.7.1 frames the Hàn dispute over the *Shū* 書 and the *Gōngyáng* 公羊 commentary; in this division the question is anchored by the *Bó Wǔjīng yìyì* [[KR1g0001]] and the *Zhèng zhì* [[KR1g0002]], and re-opened by Huì Dòng's [[KR1g0024]] programmatic call to recover Hàn-era readings.
- ***Yīnyì* 音義 and *zhíyīn* 直音 — pronunciation and gloss as canonical apparatus.** Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* [[KR1g0003]] sets the technique; the anonymous SòngYuán *Míngběn pái zì Jiǔ jīng zhí yīn* [[KR1g0012]] popularises it for commercial print; Shěn Bǐngzhèn's *Jiǔ jīng biàn zì dú méng* [[KR1g0030]] formalises it into a twelve-fold typology of graphic and phonetic variation.
- **Conjectural emendation vs. *zhāngjù* deference.** Liú Chǎng [[KR1g0004]] opens the Sòng break with HànTáng *zhāngjù*; Chéng Yí [[KR1g0005]] consolidates it; Yuè Kē [[KR1g0008]] codifies its limits as a textual-critical practice.
- **The canon as illustrated diagram (*jīng tú* 經圖).** The *Liù jīng tú* [[KR1g0006]] preserves Sòng cosmological, ritual, institutional and chronological schemata in 309 charts.
- **Pre-modern collation philology and the line to Ruǎn Yuán.** Máo Jūzhèng [[KR1g0007]] → Yuè Kē [[KR1g0008]] → Yamai Kanae & Mononobe [[KR1g0020]] → Shěn Tíngfāng [[KR1g0026]] traces the methodological ancestry of the 1815 *Shísān jīng zhùshū jiào kān jì*.
- **The Sòng–Míng *xīnxué* 心學 counter-current.** Qián Shí [[KR1g0009]] supplies the most articulate pre-Yángmíng *xīnxué* reading of a heterodox "Sì shū" set; Huáng Zhòngyuán [[KR1g0010]] and the Yuán entries chart its survival under Mongol rule.
- ***Kǎozhèng* 考證 as method.** From Gù Yánwǔ [[KR1g0021]] through Huì Dòng [[KR1g0024]], Jiāng Yǒng [[KR1g0028]] and Yú Xiāokè [[KR1g0031]], the division registers the high-Qing turn to philological recovery — fragment by fragment, gloss by gloss.
- **Recovery of the apocrypha.** Sūn Jué's *Gǔwēi shū* [[KR1g0032]] is the principal pre-modern *chènwěi* recovery, structurally placed at the end of *Wǔjīng zǒngyì* (Wilkinson §28.6).

### Timeline

- 150–200 — *Bó Wǔjīng yìyì* 駁五經異義 [[KR1g0001]] — Eastern Hàn record of inter-school doctrinal disagreement; division's anchor in Hàn *jīng xué*.
- 200–254 — *Zhèng zhì* 鄭志 [[KR1g0002]] — Wèi-period compilation of Zhèng Xuán's exegetical replies; preserves Hàn-classroom pedagogy.
- 583–627 — *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文 [[KR1g0003]] — Suí–Táng *yīnyì* corpus; the philological keystone of the pre-Sòng canon.
- 1046–1068 — *Gōngshì Qī jīng xiǎo zhuàn* 公是七經小傳 [[KR1g0004]] — first Northern Sòng break with *zhāngjù* deference via conjectural emendation.
- 1080–1107 — *Chéng shì jīng shuō* 程氏經說 [[KR1g0005]] — Chéng Yí's pan-classical exegesis; foundation of the ChéngZhū reorientation.
- 1131–1165 — *Liù jīng tú* 六經圖 [[KR1g0006]] — Sòng illustrated *jīng tú* compendium.
- c. 1200–1279 — *Liù jīng ào lùn* 六經奧論 [[KR1g0011]] — late-Sòng anon. examination-prep compendium falsely ascribed to Zhèng Qiáo.
- c. 1200–1287 — *Míngběn pái zì Jiǔ jīng zhí yīn* 明本排字九經直音 [[KR1g0012]] — anonymous SòngYuán *zhíyīn* handbook surviving in the 1287 Míngzhōu cut.
- 1219–1242 — *Jiǔ jīng sān zhuàn yán'gé lì* 九經三傳沿革例 [[KR1g0008]] — Yuè Kē's seven-principle editorial manifesto for the Xiāngtāi cut.
- 1223–1225 — *Liù jīng zhèng wù* 六經正誤 [[KR1g0007]] — Máo Jūzhèng's collator's notes on the Imperial Academy re-cut.
- 1229–1238 — *Róngtáng Sì shū guǎn jiàn* 融堂四書管見 [[KR1g0009]] — pre-Yángmíng *xīnxué* counter-canon on a heterodox "Sì shū" set.
- 1271–1312 — *Sìrú jiǎng gǎo* 四如講稿 [[KR1g0010]] — Huáng Zhòngyuán's Sòng-loyalist lecture compilation.
- 1280–1310 — *Shí yī jīng wèn duì* 十一經問對 [[KR1g0014]] — Hé Yìsūn's early-Yuán Q&A on an idiosyncratic "Eleven Classics".
- 1280–1323 — *Wǔ jīng shuō* 五經說 [[KR1g0013]] — Xióng Pénglái's early-Yuán Sòng-orthodox compendium.
- 1360–1370 — *Wǔ jīng lí cè* 五經蠡測 [[KR1g0015]] — Jiǎng Tìshēng's Yuán–Míng transition compendium (printed 1538).
- 1485–1527 — *Jiǎn duān lù* 簡端錄 [[KR1g0016]] — Shào Bǎo's Chéng-Zhū-restorationist marginalia; Dōnglín precursor.
- 1577–1620 — *Jīngdiǎn jī yí* 經典稽疑 [[KR1g0018]] — Chén Yàowén's late-Míng preservation of pre-Sòng glosses; precursor of Qing *kǎozhèng*.
- 1583–1586 — *Wǔ jīng jī yí* 五經稽疑 [[KR1g0017]] — Zhū Mùjié's late-Wànlì doubt-investigations from inside Wànjuàntáng.
- 1610–1640 — *Gǔwēi shū* 古微書 [[KR1g0032]] — Sūn Jué's late-Míng *chènwěi* fragment recovery.
- 1660–1682 — *Jiǔ jīng wù zì* 九經誤字 [[KR1g0021]] — Gù Yánwǔ's founding-Qing erratum.
- 1680–1716 — *Jīng wèn* 經問 [[KR1g0022]] — Máo Qílíng's polemical lecture-record.
- 1720–1738 — *Jiǔ jīng biàn zì dú méng* 九經辨字瀆蒙 [[KR1g0030]] — Shěn Bǐngzhèn's twelve-fold *xiǎo xué* typology.
- 1720–1753 — *Jīng zhǐ* 經咫 [[KR1g0029]] — Chén Zǔfàn's moderate Sūzhōu exegesis.
- 1725 — *Zhū zǐ Wǔ jīng yǔ lèi* 朱子五經語類 [[KR1g0027]] — Chéng Chuān's topical re-extract of Zhū Xī's *yǔ lèi* sayings on the *Five Classics*.
- 1726–1731 — *Qī jīng Mèngzǐ kǎo wén bǔ yí* 七經孟子考文補遺 [[KR1g0020]] — Tokugawa Japanese collation against Sòng cuts and Táng *kohon*; entered imperial library 1731.
- 1730–1745 — *Shí sān jīng yì yí* 十三經義疑 [[KR1g0023]] — Wú Hào's Yōngzhèng-era *Shísān jīng* doubt-resolutions.
- 1730–1762 — *Qún jīng bǔ yì* 羣經補義 [[KR1g0028]] — Jiāng Yǒng's Wǎnpài *suí bǐ* compendium across nine classics.
- 1740–1758 — *Jiǔ jīng gǔ yì* 九經古義 [[KR1g0024]] — Huì Dòng's foundational Wú-school Hàn-learning programme.
- 1740–1779 — *Jīng bài* 經稗 [[KR1g0025]] — Zhèng Fāngkūn's mid-Qing *bǐjì*-aggregator compendium.
- 1750–1772 — *Shí sān jīng zhùshū zhèng zì* 十三經注疏正字 [[KR1g0026]] — Shěn Tíngfāng's apparatus criticus, proximate ancestor of Ruǎn Yuán's 1815 *Jiào kān jì*.
- 1759–1762 — *Gǔ jīng jiě gōu chén* 古經解鉤沉 [[KR1g0031]] — Yú Xiāokè's pre-Táng fragment recovery; model for the late-Qing fragment school.
