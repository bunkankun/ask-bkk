# 神農本草經百種錄

*Shénnóng běncǎo jīng bǎizhǒng lù* "Record of One Hundred Items from the Shennong Materia Medica Classic", 1736 critical commentary on 100 entries from the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng* by [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (1693–1771).
