# Bǎoyīng cuōyào 保嬰撮要

A mid-Míng paediatrics compendium compiled by [[薛鎧]] Xuē Kǎi and his father [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (1487–1559), one of the principal Míng paediatric synthesises. Often transmitted together with Xuē Jǐ's gynecological writings in the *Xuēshì yīàn* corpus.
