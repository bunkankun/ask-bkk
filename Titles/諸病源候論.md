# 諸病源候論

The canonical Suí-era medical encyclopaedia by Cháo Yuánfāng et al., completed in *Dàyè* 6 = 610. Unusual among classical medical texts in attaching *dǎoyǐn*, *xíngqì*, and *ànmó* therapeutic instructions to its discussion of each disease-syndrome, sourced from the now-lost *Yǎngshēng fāng*. Also at [[KR3e0012]] in WYG transmission.
