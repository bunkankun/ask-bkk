# Shānléi yīxué cóngshū 山雷醫學叢書

The collected medical writings of [[張壽頤]] Zhāng Shòuyí (*hào* Shānléi 山雷, 1873–1934), produced during his teaching career at the Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānménxuéxiào. Includes the *Shěnshì nǚkē jíyào jiānshū* ([[KR3ei041]]) among its principal medical-pedagogical components.
