# 校註婦人良方

*Collated and Annotated Good Formulas for Women* — [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's mid-Míng revision and commentary on [[陳自明]] Chén Zìmíng's Sòng-period *Fùrén dàquán liángfāng* 婦人大全良方 ([[KR3e0038]]); a standard reference cited in later gynecologies including [[KR3ei029]] *Nǚkē jīnglún*.
