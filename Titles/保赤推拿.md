**Bǎochì tuīná** 保赤推拿 by the late-Qīng pediatric physician [[夏英白]] Xià Yīngbái. One of the three principal Qīng pediatric-massage handbooks ancestral to [[塗蔚生]] Tú Wèishēng's [[KR3ee053]] *Tuīná juéwēi* (1928), where Tú adopts Xià's technical method as his own work's core while criticizing it for lacking proper diagnostic and pharmacotherapeutic apparatus. Not separately cataloged in the Kanripo corpus.
