# Chuán guāng lù 傳光錄

*Record of the Transmission of the Light* (*Denkōroku*) by [[瑩山|Keizan Jōkin]] 瑩山紹瑾 (1264–1325). A series of dharma-talks on the fifty-three Indian, Chinese and Japanese patriarchs of the Sōtō line from Śākyamuni through Dōgen. Not preserved in the Kanripo canonical corpus.
