# Dàfāngmài 大方脈

The general-internal-medicine section of [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán's mid-Qīng medical compendium *Tóngyuán yīshū* 彤園醫書 ([[彤園醫書]]). Identified by its compiler as the prerequisite primary reading for the *Fùrénkē* (gynecology) section ([[KR3ei037]]).
