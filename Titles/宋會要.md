# Sòng huì yào 宋會要

Sòng administrative-history compilation, the working title for the Sòng-dynasty *huì-yào* compilation projects (begun under Sòng Tàizōng in the late 10th century, continued in serial form through the dynasty). The original work was lost; the surviving form is the *Sòng huì yào jí gǎo* 宋會要輯稿, a Qīng-period reconstruction by Xú Sōng 徐松 (1781–1848) from quotations in the *Yǒnglè dà diǎn*. Indispensable source for Sòng-period institutional history, including the Northern-Sòng imperial Buddhist translation bureau (*Yìjīngyuàn* 譯經院) and its translator-personnel.
