# Běncǎo zhèngyì 本草正義

A 1932 critical *běncǎo* (materia medica) by [[張壽頤]] Zhāng Shòuyí (Shānléi 山雷, 1873–1934), produced during his teaching at the Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānménxuéxiào 蘭谿中醫專門學校. Catalogued as [[KR3ec063]].
