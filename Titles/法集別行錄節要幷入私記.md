# Fǎjí biéxíng lù jiéyào bìng rù sījì 法集別行錄節要幷入私記

Late Koryŏ doctrinal abridgement-with-commentary by [[知訥]] Pojo Chinul (1158–1210) of Zōngmì's *Fǎjí biéxíng lù* 法集別行錄, with Chinul's own interpretive notes; one of his three principal authentic works.
