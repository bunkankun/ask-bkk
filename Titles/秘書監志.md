# 秘書監志

*Mìshūjiān zhì* — "Gazetteer of the Imperial Library".

Yuán administrative compilation in 11 *juǎn* recording the regulations, personnel, holdings, and procedures of the Yuán *Mìshūjiān* 秘書監 (Imperial Library), compiled by Wáng Shìdiǎn 王士點 and Shāng Qǐwēng 商企翁 in the Zhìzhèng 至正 era (1340s). One of the principal documentary sources for Yuán imperial bureaucratic and bibliographic history, including the curricular organisation of the *Tàiyīyuàn* 太醫院 and the *shísān kē* 十三科.
