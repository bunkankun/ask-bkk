# 閨範

Late-Míng work (1590) of women's ethical instruction by [[呂坤]] Lǚ Kūn. Mentioned in *Xiānglián rùnsè* ([[KR3eo020]]) as the most famous member of the small subgenre of late-Míng publications on women's practice, alongside Hú Wénhuàn's cosmetic compendium.
