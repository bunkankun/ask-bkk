# Zōngmén liándēng huìyào 宗門聯燈會要

A thirty-juan Southern-Sòng Chán lamp-record (*dēnglù*) compiled by Huìwēng Wùmíng 晦翁悟明, completed in Chúnxī 10 (1183); a major secondary channel through which *gōng'àn* anecdotes from earlier Chán literature, including the *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī zōngmén wǔkù*, entered the standardised late-imperial Chán corpus.
