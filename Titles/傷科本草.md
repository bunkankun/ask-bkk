# 傷科本草

*Shāngkē běncǎo* — a specialised traumatology *běncǎo* compiled by [[胡廷光]] Hú Tíngguāng of Xiāoshān 蕭山 (Hángzhōu) and incorporated into juǎn 9–12 of [[KR3el011]] *Shāngkē huìzuǎn* (1815); does not survive as an independent work.
