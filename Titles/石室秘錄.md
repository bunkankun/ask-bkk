# Shíshì mìlù 石室秘錄
*Secret Records of the Stone Chamber*

Medical work by [[陳士鐸]] Chén Shìduó (1627–1707), published 1687, in the *spirit-revelation* genre of Chinese medical writing, ascribing prescriptions to sage-figures (Qítiānshī 岐天師, Léigōng 雷公) and to Fù Shān 傅山. Substantial portions overlap with the received *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē* 傅青主女科.
