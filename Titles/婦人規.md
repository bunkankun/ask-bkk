# Fùrén guī 婦人規
*Compass for Women (Gynecology)*

The gynecological portion of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn's *Jǐngyuè quánshū* 景岳全書 (1624/1640), widely circulated as a standalone work in late-Míng and Qīng *fùkē* literature. Stub for cross-reference purposes.
