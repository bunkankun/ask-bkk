**Lì zhèng huì cān** 痢症彙參, a mid-Qīng compilation on dysentery by [[吳本立]] Wú Běnlì, gathering pre-Qīng and Qīng opinion on the disease. Criticised by [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng in [[KR3eh053]] *Lìjí míngbiàn* as collecting opinions without filtering them.
