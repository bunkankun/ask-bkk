# Zhìyuán fǎbǎo kāntóng zǒnglù 至元法寶勘同總錄
*Comprehensive Concordance Catalog of the Dharma-Treasures of the Zhìyuán Era*

A Yuán-dynasty Buddhist canon-catalog compiled in the Zhìyuán 至元 era (1264–1294) by Qìngjíxiáng 慶吉祥 and others, comparing Chinese, Tibetan, and Tangut Buddhist canons. The catalog records the incorporation of imperial-bureau translations into the official canon and is a key witness to the canon-philological practice of the Yuán court.
