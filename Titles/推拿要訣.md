**Tuīná yàojué** 推拿要訣 (also called *Tuīná mìjué* 推拿秘訣), the Wàn-lì-era pediatric *tuīná* manuscript of [[周於蕃]] Zhōu Yúfān of Húběi (Chǔ 楚 region). Never separately printed in the Míng or Qīng. The manuscript was transmitted within one *Zhāng* family for some twenty years and recovered by [[張言禮]] Zhāng Yánlǐ of Dāntú in 1887; he gave it to [[張筱衫]] Zhāng Zhènjūn (Zhāng Xiǎoshān) for editorial reorganization, illustration, and printing, the result being [[KR3ee054]] *Lízhèng ànmó yàoshù* (1888). The text is otherwise lost in independent transmission. Not separately cataloged in the Kanripo corpus.
