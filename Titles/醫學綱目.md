**Yī xué gāng mù** 醫學綱目, an early-Míng comprehensive medical nosological synthesis in 40 *juǎn* by [[婁全善]] Lóu Quánshàn (c. 1320–1400), completed 1396 and first printed 1565. The work organises Chinese medicine successively by the *liù jīng* 六經 main illnesses, *záběng* 雜病, seasonal disorders, and women's-and-children's medicine — an architecture explicitly adopted by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng for [[KR3e0078]] *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* (cf. [[KR3eh052]]).
