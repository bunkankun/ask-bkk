# 楞嚴經翼解

*Lèngyán jīng yìjiě*

Two-fascicle prolegomenon to the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* drafted by [[元賢]] Yǒngjué Yuánxián in 1624 to "uncover the cardinal points" of the sūtra; later expanded by Yuánxián into his mature *Lèngyán jīng lüèshū* ([[KR6j0710]]).
