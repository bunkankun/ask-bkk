# Gǔjīn yīàn 古今醫案
*Medical Cases Ancient and Modern*

Major Qīng medical case-anthology by Yú Zhèn 俞震 (*Gǔjīn yīàn àn* 古今醫案按, 1778), one of the standard historiographical reference-points for Qīng *yīàn* compilation, cited in the preface to [[KR3ep075]].
