# Wángshì yīàn 王氏醫案
*Medical Case Records of Wáng*

Two-*juǎn* casebook of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng (Mèngyīng, 1808–1868) documenting his pre-war Hángzhōu clinical practice; first of the *Wángshì yīàn* trilogy. See [[KR3ep083]].
