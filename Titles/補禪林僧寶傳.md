# Bǔ Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn 補禪林僧寶傳

*Supplement to the Biographies of the Jewel Monks of the Chán Forest*

A one-juan supplement to Huìhóng's *Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* 禪林僧寶傳 ([[KR6q0040]]), attributed to the Northern Sòng monk [[舟峯菴慶老]] Zhōufēngān Qìnglǎo. Together with the *Línjì zōngzhǐ* 臨濟宗旨 ([[KR6q0126]]), it accounts for the difference between the 30-juan original recension and the 32-juan recension catalogued by the Sìkù editors.
