# 河間六書 Héjiān liù shū

Canonical six-work collection of [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù's (河間) writings, the principal vehicle for the JīnYuán Héjiān 河間 school's "fire-and-heat" 火熱 reinterpretation of Hàn-period medical canons. Comprises the *Shānghán zhígé* ([[KR3ef011]]), *Shānghán biāoběn xīnfǎ lèicuì* ([[KR3ef009]]), *Sùwèn xuánjī yuánbìng shì*, *Xuānmíng lùn fāng*, and related theoretical works.
