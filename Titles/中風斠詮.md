# Zhōngfēng jiàoquán 中風斠詮

A critical commentary on the *zhōngfēng* (wind-stroke / apoplexy) literature by [[張壽頤]] Zhāng Shòuyí (Shānléi 山雷, 1873–1934), a Republican-era contribution to the modernisation of Chinese-medicine pedagogy.
