# Sòng-Yuán Chánzōng shǐ 宋元禪宗史

Modern survey of Sòng- and Yuán-period Chán Buddhism by [[楊曾文]] Yáng Zēngwén, published by 中國社會科學 (2006). A principal Sinophone reference for the history of Chán in the Sòng-Yuán period.
