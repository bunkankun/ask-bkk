# Jiǎng Bǎosù yīlüè 蔣寶素醫略
*Jiǎng Bǎosù's Medical Outline*

Medical compendium of [[蔣寶素]] Jiǎng Bǎosù (1795–1873), senior disciple of [[王九峰]] Wáng Jiǔfēng. Preserved a manuscript of Wáng's case-records that [[嚴敦益]] Yán Dūnyì later used in his 1923 recovery-edition ([[KR3ep076]]).
