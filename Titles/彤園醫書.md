# Tóngyuán yīshū 彤園醫書

A comprehensive mid-Qīng medical compendium by [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán (*hào* Tóngyuánzhǔrén 彤園主人), organised by specialty. Includes the *Dàfāngmài* 大方脈 ([[大方脈]], general internal medicine) and *Fùrénkē* 婦人科 (gynecology, [[KR3ei037]]) sections, among others. Printed in the late *Jiāqìng* / *Dàoguāng* era.
