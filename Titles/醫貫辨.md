# 醫貫辨

*Critique of the Yīguàn* — a mid-Qīng critical essay by [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (Húixī 洄溪) attacking the doctrinal rigidity of [[趙獻可]] Zhào Xiànkě's *Yīguàn* 醫貫 ([[KR3ed015]]).
