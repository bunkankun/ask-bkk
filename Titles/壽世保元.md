# 壽世保元

*Shòushì bǎoyuán* "Protecting the Origin for a Long-Lived World", 1615 comprehensive clinical encyclopedia by [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián (1522–1619); a late companion to his *Wànbìng huíchūn* 萬病回春.
