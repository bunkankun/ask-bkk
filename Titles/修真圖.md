# 修真圖

Late-Míng illustrated inner-alchemical chart tradition that crystallised in *Chóngzhēn*-era recensions; methodologically downstream of the *Bǎoshēng xīnjiàn* 保生心鑑 ([[KR3eo041]]) and other illustrated *dǎoyǐn* manuals.
