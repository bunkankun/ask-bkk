# 萬病回春

*Wànbìng huíchūn* "Restoring Spring for the Myriad Diseases", 1587 comprehensive clinical encyclopedia by [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián (1522–1619); one of the most reprinted Chinese medical texts of the late Míng and a principal vehicle for the dissemination of Míng clinical doctrine into Japan and Korea.
