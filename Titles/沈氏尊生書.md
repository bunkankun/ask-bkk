# 沈氏尊生書

*The Shěn-Family Book of Cherishing-Life* — a 72-*juǎn* mid-Qiánlóng medical encyclopedia compiled by [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn'áo (1717–1776), completed in 1773. The compilation comprises *[[雜病源流犀燭]]* 雜病源流犀燭, *[[傷寒論綱目]]* 傷寒論綱目, *[[幼科釋謎]]* 幼科釋謎, *Fùkē yùchǐ* 婦科玉尺 ([[KR3ei023]]), *Màixiàng tǒnglèi* [[KR3eb027]], *Zhūmài zhǔbìng shī* [[KR3eb028]], and others.
