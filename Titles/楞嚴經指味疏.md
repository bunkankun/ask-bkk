# 楞嚴經指味疏

*Lèngyán jīng zhǐwèi shū*

The larger late-Qīng Tiāntái commentary project of Dìxián 諦閑 on the full Śūraṃgama-sūtra; only the prefatory fascicle ([[KR6j0721]] Lèngyán jīng xù zhǐwèi shū) was prepared for the Xùzàngjīng, while the complete project survives in the early-Republican Dìxián dàshī yíjí [[諦閑大師遺集]].
