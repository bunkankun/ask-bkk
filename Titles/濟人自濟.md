# Jìrén Zìjì 濟人自濟

A Qiánlóng-Jiāqìng era popular-charity medical compilation. Cited by Hé Yīng in the *fánlì* of his [[KR3ed119]] *Wéntáng jíyàn fāng* as a recent acquisition that anticipated his own work, with material widely augmented and corrected against it. Full bibliographic detail not yet verified.
