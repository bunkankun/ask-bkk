# 宋會要輯稿 *Sòng huìyào jígǎo*

Qing-period reconstruction of the lost Sòng-dynasty *huìyào* compilations of administrative documents and government records. Compiled by Xú Sōng 徐松 (1781–1848) from quotations preserved in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* 永樂大典 and other sources. A principal documentary source for Sòng institutional and religious history, including monastic registers, translation bureau activity, and court ceremonial records.
