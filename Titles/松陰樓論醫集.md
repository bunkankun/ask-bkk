**Sōngyīn lóu lùn yī jí** 松陰樓論醫集, a late-Qīng medical-discussion collection by [[王旭高]] Wáng Xùgāo, posthumously printed after 1881. Cites a *Wú Fǔtián* on dysentery — confirming the dating and identity of [[吳士瑛]] Wú Shìyīng (cf. [[KR3eh053]]).
