# Féngshì Jǐnnáng Mìlù 馮氏錦囊秘錄

A 1702 (Kāngxī 41) clinical encyclopedia by [[馮兆張]] (Féng Zhàozhāng). Cited as a *flowery-curated* popular-formulary precedent in the preface to [[KR3ed132]] *Yīfāng shíjǐn* (1873).
