# 三具足經 *Sānjùzú jīng*

Lost Mahāyāna scripture (Skt. *Trisamucchaya-sūtra*) on three perfecting-virtues — *shī* 施 (giving / *dāna*), *jiè* 戒 (moral discipline / *śīla*), and *wén* 聞 (hearing-of-the-Dharma / *śruta*) — said by the Buddha to comprehensively encompass the bodhisattva practice. No Chinese translation of the source text survives; the Vasubandhu *upadeśa* preserved in [[KR6i0593]] *Sānjùzú jīng yōubōtíshè* is the principal surviving witness to the doctrine.
