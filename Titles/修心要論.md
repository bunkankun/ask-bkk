# Xiūxīn yào lùn 修心要論

The alternative title under which the East Mountain school meditation treatise [[KR6q0086]] *Zuìshàng chéng lùn* widely circulates in the Dūnhuáng manuscript tradition; attributed to [[弘忍]] Hóngrěn but more likely an early-Táng compilation by his disciples.
