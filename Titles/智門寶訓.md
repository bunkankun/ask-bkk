# 智門寶訓

A Línjì 臨濟 Chán doctrinal-polemical work by [[慧洪]] Juéfàn Huìhóng (1071–1128), part of his broader literary-doctrinal project. Mentioned alongside his *Línjì zōngzhǐ* ([[KR6q0126]]) as exemplifying his Chán *Chánjiào héyī* engagement; not currently included in the Kanripo corpus.
