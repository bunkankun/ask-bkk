# Huíshēng dān 回生丹

A famous Qīng-period popular formula for emergency obstetrical conditions (especially *zǐsǐ fùzhōng* 子死腹中, fetal death in utero), preserved with a discursive *lùn* 論 in [[何集庵]] Hé Jíān's compilation and circulated as an appendix to the *Wànshì nǚkē* recension ([[KR3ei040]]).
