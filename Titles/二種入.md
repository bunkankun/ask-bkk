# Èrzhǒng rù 二種入

Short Chán treatise on the "two entrances" (*lǐ rù* and *xíng rù*) and four practices, constituting the third gate of [[KR6q0084]] *Shǎoshì liù mén*; the most plausibly archaic Bodhidharma-circle material in the compilation, with parallels in the Dūnhuáng *Èrrù sìxíng lùn* 二入四行論.
