# Dōngzhēng fù 東征賦
*Rhapsody on an Eastward Journey*

A Han-dynasty *fù* rhapsody by [[班昭]] Bān Zhāo describing her journey eastward; preserved in the *Wén xuǎn* and traditionally drawn upon in medieval Chinese-Buddhist lexicographic apparatus.
