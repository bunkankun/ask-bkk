# Shānghán zhāijǐn 傷寒摘錦

A *Shānghán* (cold-damage) work by [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (1499–1582), part of his *Wànshì yīshū shízhǒng* ([[萬氏醫書十種]]) family-lineage corpus. Catalogued as [[KR3ef071]].
