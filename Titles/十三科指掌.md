# 十三科指掌

*Pointing-to-the-Palm in the Thirteen Specialties* — early-Qīng comprehensive medical pedagogical work by [[葉其蓁]] Yè Qízhēn covering the canonical thirteen sub-fields of clinical medicine; the gynecology section was extracted and printed independently as the *Nǚkē zhǐzhǎng* 女科指掌 ([[KR3ei027]]).
