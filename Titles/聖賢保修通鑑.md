# 聖賢保修通鑑

"Comprehensive Mirror of the Sages and Worthies' Methods of Preservation." The unidentified source-text encountered by [[鐵峰居士]] Tiěfēng Jūshì in autumn 1505, from which he transcribed the outline that became the *Bǎoshēng xīnjiàn* 保生心鑑 ([[KR3eo041]]).
