# 經驗良方

*Jīngyàn liángfāng* "Good Formulas of Clinical Experience", a common generic title used for collections of experiential medical prescriptions; here referring to the supplement appended to the 1786 [[KR3ei013]] *Níngkūn mìjí* compilation.
