# 靈感傳

*Línggǎn zhuàn* ("Records of Numinous Response"). A Buddhist hagiographical-tale collection cited by [[遵式]] Zūnshì in his *Guānyīn jīng pǔmén pǐn chóngsòng* ([[KR6d0056]]) for the heavenly-voice tradition associated with [[道宣]] Dàoxuān of Nánshān, asserting that Kumārajīva (an "eighth-stage bodhisattva") omitted the *Pǔmén* verses from his Lotus Sūtra translation. Not preserved as an independent text in the Kanripo.
