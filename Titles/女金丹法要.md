# Nǚ jīndān fǎyào 女金丹法要

A Míng-Qīng-era consolidation of the female internal-alchemy (*nǚdān*) tradition, treating the cultivation of the *jīndān* 金丹 (golden elixir) in the female body. One of the principal late-imperial texts in the *nǚdān* sub-genre of internal alchemy.
