# Zōngmén huòwèn 宗門或問
*Questions and Answers on the [Chán] School*

A late-Míng Chán dialogical apologetic by Zhànrán Yuánchéng 湛然圓澄 ([[圓澄]]; 1561–1626) of the Cáodòng 曹洞 lineage. Not separately listed in the Kanripo title list; not transmitted in CBETA; cited as part of his polemical-reformist corpus alongside the *Kǎigǔ lù* 慨古錄 ([[KR6q0171]]).
