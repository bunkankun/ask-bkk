# 法華經大窾要旨

A late-Míng "essential meaning" condensation associated with [[通潤]] Tōngrùn's *Fǎhuá jīng dàkuǎn* ([[KR6d0080]]); cited by [[大義]] Dàyì in the compilation history of his *Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng* ([[KR6d0085]]). Not held under a separate KR identifier.
