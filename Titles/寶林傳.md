# Bǎolín zhuàn 寶林傳

Brief stub for a title mentioned in [[KR6q0001]] but not in the main krp-titles catalog. An early-Táng (ca. 801) Chán transmission record compiled at Bǎolínsì 寶林寺, an important precursor of the Sòng lamp-record genre; preserved only in fragmentary form.
