# Bā shí guī jǔ sòng 八識規矩頌

*Verses on the Patterns of the Eight Consciousnesses*

A short mnemonic-verse text traditionally attributed to [[玄奘|Xuánzàng]] 玄奘 (602–664), summarising the Yogācāra doctrine of the eight consciousnesses in twelve four-line verses (three verses each for: the five sense-consciousnesses, the 6th *yìshí* 意識 mental consciousness, the 7th *mònà* 末那 defiled-mind, and the 8th *ālàiyé* 阿賴耶 storehouse-consciousness). The verses capture the consciousnesses' characteristics, levels of defilement and purification, and association with the four kinds of wisdom (*sì zhì* 四智). Not separately catalogued in the Kanripo corpus; transmitted within commentarial expositions such as [[KR6q0215]] *Xūzhōu chánshī zhù Bā shí guī jǔ sòng* by [[行省|Xūzhōu Xíngxǐng]].
