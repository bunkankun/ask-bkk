# Wúshì Jièchén chánshī yǔlù 無示介諶禪師語錄

Recorded-sayings (*yǔlù*) collection of the Southern-Sòng Línjì-Huánglóng Chán master Wúshì [[介諶]] Jièchén (1080–1148); cited by Kōhō [[東晙]] Tōshun as one of the source texts for his Edo-period *xùbǔ* supplement to the *Huánglóng Huìnán chánshī yǔlù* ([[KR6q0055]]).
