# Xuèmài lùn 血脈論

Mid-to-late-Táng (or early-Sòng) Chán treatise constituting the sixth gate of [[KR6q0084]] *Shǎoshì liù mén*; closes with the famous transmission-gatha *yī huā kāi wǔ yè* 一華開五葉, the canonical textual warrant for the Five Houses of Chán self-identification.
