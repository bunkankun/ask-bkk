# Yíshān lǐ fó fā yuàn wén 怡山禮佛發願文

*Vow-Text for Buddha-Worship of Chán Master Yíshān*

A classical devotional vow-text by the Táng-era Chán master [[怡山|Yíshān chánshī]] 怡山禪師, used widely in Chinese Buddhist *lǐ fó* 禮佛 (Buddha-worship) liturgical practice across Chán, Vinaya, and Pure-Land contexts. Not separately catalogued in the Kanripo corpus; transmitted within liturgical compilations and commentarial expositions. The principal Qīng commentary is [[KR6q0210]] *Yíshān lǐ fó fā yuàn wén lüè shì* by [[書玉|Yíjié Shūyù]] (1713).
