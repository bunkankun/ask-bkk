**Xūláo shīxuè jí yào** 虛勞失血集要, a Qīng medical treatise on internal depletion and blood-loss by [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī (Húnán); the second half of Cài's projected diptych whose first half is [[傷寒瘟疫抉要]] *Shānghán wēnyì jué yào* (printed 1817). The *Xūláo* half remained in manuscript until a fresh subscription in 1823 enabled its printing combined with the first half as [[KR3eh051]] *Nèishāng jí yào*.
