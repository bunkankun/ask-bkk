# Chǎnbǎo 產寶

A Táng-period obstetrical compendium traditionally attributed to Zàn Yīn 昝殷 (mid-9th c.), one of the earliest extant Chinese works dedicated to obstetrics and the postpartum care of women. Cited extensively in later Sòng and Míng gynecological literature.
