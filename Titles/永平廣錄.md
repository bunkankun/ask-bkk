# Yǒngpíng guǎnglù 永平廣錄

The *Eihei kōroku*, the recorded sayings of the Japanese Sōtō founder [[道元]] Dōgen (1200–1253); a principal Japanese-side source for citations of his Chinese teacher [[如淨|Tiāntóng Rújìng]] 天童如淨 and a witness to the now-lost fuller *guǎnglù* of Rújìng.
