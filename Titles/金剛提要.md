# 金剛提要

*Jīngāng tíyào*

Late-seventeenth-century Vajracchedikā (Jīngāng jīng) compendium by the Línjì-school monk [[濟時]] Jìshí of Xìngfúsì 興福寺 at Lóudōng, completed in Kāngxī 37 (1698) just before his Lèngyán zhèng-jiàn ([[KR6j0725]]).
