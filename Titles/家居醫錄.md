# Jiājū yīlù 家居醫錄
*Medical Records from Home Life*

A collection of clinical case-records (*yīàn* 醫案) assembled by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (1487–1559) during his retirement from the Imperial Medical Academy. Printed in 1548 with a preface by [[範慶]] Fàn Qìng. The work later contributed to the *Xuēshì yīàn* 薛氏醫案 ([[KR3e0070]]) and contains the case-records that form the basis of the *Nǚkē cuōyào* 女科撮要 ([[KR3ei009]]).
