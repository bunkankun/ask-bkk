**Shānghán wēnyì jué yào** 傷寒瘟疫抉要, a Qīng medical treatise by [[蔡貽績]] Cài Yíjī (Húnán), printed Jiāqìng 22 (1817) with funds raised by [[易麗昭]] Yì Lìzhāo and others; the externally-contracted-disease half of Cài's projected diptych, the depletion / blood-loss half being [[虛勞失血集要]] *Xūláo shīxuè jí yào*. The two halves were eventually recombined and reprinted as [[KR3eh051]] *Nèishāng jí yào* in 1823.
