# Sēngbǎo zhuàn 僧寶傳

A generic title used by the Sìkù tíyào of the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* to refer to the *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* tradition of Chán "Jewels of the Saṅgha" biographies. The principal works in this tradition are *Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* 禪林僧寶傳 ([[KR6q0040]]) by Huìhóng 惠洪 and *Nán-Sòng Yuán Míng Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* 南宋元明禪林僧寶傳 ([[KR6q0042]]) by Zìróng 自融.
