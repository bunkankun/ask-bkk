# 蒙文真珠

Yuán-period Mongolian-Sanskrit-Chinese transcription manual associated with the official *zhìyīn* 直音 ("direct phonology") method used by the Mongol court for rendering Tibetan and Sanskrit Buddhist material into Chinese.
