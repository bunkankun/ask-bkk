# Huáyán lùn jiéyào 華嚴論節要

Koryŏ-period (compiled c. 1207) abridgement by [[知訥]] Pojo Chinul of [[李通玄]] Lǐ Tōngxuán's *Xīn Huáyán jīng lùn* 新華嚴經論 ([[KR6c0009]]); one of Chinul's principal undoubtedly-authentic works.
