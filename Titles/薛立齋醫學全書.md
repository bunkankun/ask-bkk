# Xuē Lìzhāi yīxué quánshū 薛立齋醫學全書

A modern collected edition of the medical works of [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (*hào* Lìzhāi 立齋, 1487–1559), the prolific late-Míng imperial physician. The 16-work corpus is gathered in the Míng-period *Xuēshì yīàn* 薛氏醫案 ([[KR3e0070]]).
