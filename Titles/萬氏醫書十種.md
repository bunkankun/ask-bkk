# Wànshì yīshū shízhǒng 萬氏醫書十種

The ten-work medical-family corpus of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (Mìzhāi 密齋, 1499–1582) of Luótián 羅田 (Húběi). Includes works on paediatrics, infant-care, gynecology ([[KR3ei040]] *Wànshì nǚkē*), constitutional health, and *Shānghán* ([[KR3ef071]]).
