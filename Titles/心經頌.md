# Xīn jīng sòng 心經頌

A Táng-period verse-by-verse gathic commentary on the *Mahā-prajñāpāramitā-hrdaya-sūtra* (Heart Sūtra), constituting the first gate of [[KR6q0084]] *Shǎoshì liù mén*, traditionally attributed to Bodhidharma but post-dating Xuánzàng's 649 translation of the Heart Sūtra.
