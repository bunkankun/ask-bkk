# Guǎngsì Yàoyǔ 廣嗣要語

A mid-Míng treatise on fertility methodology by [[俞橋]] (Yú Qiáo, *zì* Sùhuízǐ 溯洄子), combining Daoist *nèidān* (inner-alchemy) doctrine with practical clinical-prescription material on producing descendants (*guǎngsì*). The text circulated independently in at least two earlier editions and is preserved as an appendix to [[KR3ed126]] *Jǐshì zhēnbǎo* (Wáng Yǒng's late-Míng compilation).
