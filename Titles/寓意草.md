# Yùyì cǎo 寓意草
*Notes on Lodged Intentions*

Famous medical casebook of [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng (Jiāyán 嘉言, 1585–1664), one of the foundational early-Qīng *yīàn* collections; transmitted in this knowledgebase as [[KR3ep012]].
