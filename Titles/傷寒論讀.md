# Shānghán lùn dú 傷寒論讀
*Readings on the Treatise on Cold Damage*

A Qīng-period *Shānghán lùn* study text by [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng (*fl.* 1750–1780), composed ca. 1764 as one of three coordinated *dú* ("readings") works alongside the *Yījīng dú* 醫經讀 ([[KR3ea043]]) and the *Nǚkē jíyào* 沈氏女科輯要 ([[KR3ei010]]).
