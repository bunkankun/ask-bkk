# 清朝文獻通考

*Qīngcháo wénxiàn tōngkǎo* — "Comprehensive Examination of [Qing] Dynasty Documents and Cultural Works".

Imperially commissioned Qīng-dynasty encyclopaedic compendium in 300 *juǎn*, continuing the *Wénxiàn tōngkǎo* 文獻通考 of Mǎ Duānlín 馬端臨; begun under Qiánlóng 乾隆 (initiated 1747) and presented in 1787. Often referenced together with the *Xù wénxiàn tōngkǎo* 續文獻通考 and *Huángcháo wénxiàn tōngkǎo* 皇朝文獻通考 of the same project. The *Tàiyīyuàn* 太醫院 section records the eventual deletion of *zhùyóu* 祝由 from the imperial medical curriculum under the Qīng.
