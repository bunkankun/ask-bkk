**Ton'ishō** 頓醫抄, the first major Japanese-vernacular medical compendium in 50 *kan* by [[梶原性全]] Kajiwara Shōzen, completed 1304. Predecessor to the same author's later *kanbun* [[萬安方]] *Man'an-hō*.
