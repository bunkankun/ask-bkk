# Jìyīn gāngmù 濟陰綱目
*Outline of Saving Yīn (Women's Medicine)*

A late-Míng systematic gynecology by [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng (1620), one of the principal Míng-period *fùkē* reference works. Not present in the Kanripo title list as a standalone entry; stub for cross-reference purposes.
