# Xiǎo zhǐguān jué 小止觀訣

A name for the compact Tiāntái meditation manual attributed to [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ (538–597), commonly known as the *Xiǎo zhǐguān* 小止觀 (cf. *Xiūxí zhǐguān zuòchán fǎyào* 修習止觀坐禪法要, [[KR6d0143]]); cited as the genre-precedent for short *jué* 訣 instructional texts in Yánshòu's *Wéi xīn jué* ([[KR6q0094]]).
