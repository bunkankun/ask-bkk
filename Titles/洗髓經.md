# 洗髓經

"Classic of Washing the Marrow", companion text to the *Yìjīn jīng* in the Bodhidharma cycle of Shàolín internal-cultivation literature, pseudepigraphically attributed to Bodhidharma; late-Míng / early-Qīng in its received form.
