# Yèshì zhǐnán 葉氏指南
*Yè's Guide*

Short title for *Línzhèng zhǐnán yīàn* 臨證指南醫案 ([[KR3ep010]]), the canonical Yè-school casebook of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì compiled by 華岫雲 Huá Xiùyún in 1764. Cited in the preface to [[KR3ep075]].
