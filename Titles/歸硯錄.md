# Guīyàn lù 歸硯錄
*Returning-Inkstone Records*

Late case-and-discourse compilation of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng (Mèngyīng 孟英, 1808–1868). Together with the *Wángshì yīàn* trilogy ([[KR3ep083]], [[KR3ep084]], [[KR3ep085]]) it forms the principal source-material for [[KR3ep078]] *Wáng Mèngyīng yīàn*, the 1921 topical rearrangement by [[陸士諤]] Lù Shìè.
