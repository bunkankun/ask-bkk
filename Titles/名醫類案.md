# Míngyī lèiàn 名醫類案
*Classified Cases of Famous Physicians*

Foundational Míng medical case-anthology by Jiāng Guàn 江瓘 (1503–1565), the principal model for later syndrome-classified *yīàn* compilations. Listed in Kanripo as KR3e0075. Cited in the preface to [[KR3ep075]].
