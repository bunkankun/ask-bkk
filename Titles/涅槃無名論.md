# Nièpán wúmíng lùn 涅槃無名論
*Treatise on the Namelessness of Nirvāṇa*

The fourth of the four treatises of [[僧肇]] Sēngzhào's *Zhào lùn* 肇論, expounding the doctrine that nirvāṇa transcends conceptual designation. Frequently cited in Chinese-Buddhist exegesis to elucidate the Mādhyamaka doctrine of emptiness.
