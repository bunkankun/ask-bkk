# 勸發真信文

*Quàn fā zhēn xìn wén*

A Pure Land devotional tract by [[行策]] Jiéliú Xíngcè (1628–1682), the tenth Patriarch of the Chinese Pure Land lineage; reads alongside his *Shìzhì zhāng jiě* ([[KR6j0720]]) as the doctrinal-practical scriptural ground for early-Qīng Pure Land institutionalization.
