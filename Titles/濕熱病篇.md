# Shīrè bìng piān 濕熱病篇
*Treatise on Damp-Heat Diseases*

Foundational *wēnbìng* text on *shīrè* (damp-heat) disorders by [[薛雪]] Xuē Xuě (Shēngbái 生白, 1681–1770), Sūzhōu Yè-school contemporary of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì. Cited as Xuē's principal doctrinal contribution in [[KR3ep080]].
