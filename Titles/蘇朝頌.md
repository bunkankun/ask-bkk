# 蘇朝頌

A *sòng* 頌 (eulogy) by [[張商英]] Wújìn jūshì Zhāng Shāngyīng (1043–1121) commemorating [[蘇軾]] Sū Shì (1037–1101), as cited in the biographical literature on Zhāng's Buddhist productive corpus. Not currently included in the Kanripo corpus.
