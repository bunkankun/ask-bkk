# 外台秘要

Táng-era medical encyclopedia by [[王燾]] Wáng Tāo (752), a principal repository of emergency-medicine prescriptions drawn upon by Qīng compilers such as [[程鵬程]] Chéng Péngchéng in *Jíjiù guǎngshēng jí* 急救廣生集 ([[KR3eo047]]).
