# Wúmén zhìyàn lù 吳門治驗錄
*Record of Clinical Successes in the Wú Region*

A Qīng-period Sūzhōu (Wúmén 吳門) clinical case-compilation, cited in the preface to [[KR3ep075]] *Sōngxīn yīàn bǐjì* as part of the Jiāng-Zhè *yīàn* lineage.
