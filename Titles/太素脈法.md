# 太素脈法

Late-Sòng / Yuán-Míng-transition tradition of divinatory pulse-reading, pseudepigraphically attributed to [[張太素]] Zhāng Tàisù. The principal pre-Hu Wenhuan transmission of the *Tàisù mài* doctrine. Underlying basis of *Tàisù màijué mìshū* ([[KR3eo018]]) and *Tàisù xīnyào* ([[KR3eo019]]) as edited by [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn.
