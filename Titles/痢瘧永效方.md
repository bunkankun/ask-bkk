# 痢瘧永效方

*Lìnüè yǒngxiào fāng* "Permanent-Effective Formulas for Dysentery and Malaria", a short work on dysentery and malaria attributed to the early-Qīng physician [[倪涵初]] Ní Hánchū, often appended to gynecological compendia (e.g. as *xià-juǎn* supplement to the *Níngkūn mìjí* [[KR3ei013]]).
