# Zhànrán jūshì wénjí 湛然居士文集

The 14-juan collected writings of the Mongol-Yuán statesman [[耶律楚材]] Yēlü Chǔcái (1190–1244), preserved in the Sìbù cóngkān; contains extensive autobiographical reference to the composition of [[KR6q0079]] *Cóngróng lù* and Yēlü's own matching-verses on a subset of its hundred cases.
