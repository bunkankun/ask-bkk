# 洞神玄訣

Daoist commentary tradition associated with the *Tàiqīng zhōnghuáng zhēnjīng* ([[KR5c0214]]). Cited in [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn's *Lèixiū yàojué* 類修要訣 ([[KR3eo011]]) as the basis of the brief commentary keyed to Hú's abbreviated reprint of the *Tàiqīng zhōnghuáng* doctrine.
