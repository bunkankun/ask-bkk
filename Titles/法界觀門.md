# 法界觀門

*Fǎjiè guānmén* — the "Contemplation-Gate of the Dharma-Realm," attributed to [[杜順]] Dùshùn, first patriarch of the Chinese Huáyán school; the foundational text of Tang Huáyán contemplative practice, surviving only embedded in [[宗密]] Zōngmì's later commentary *[[KR6e0101|Zhù huáyán fǎjiè guānmén]]* (T1884).
