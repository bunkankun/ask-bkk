# Sānlùn yàowén jí 三論要文集
*Collected Essential Writings of the Three-Treatise School*

An Insei-period Tōdaiji Sanron-school anthology of doctrinal extracts, used as a major source for the historical reconstruction of the Heian-period Sanron tradition.
