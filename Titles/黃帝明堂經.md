**Huángdì Míngtáng jīng** 黃帝明堂經, the lost Hàn / Six-Dynasties acupoint canon of the *Míngtáng* 明堂 tradition. Foundational source for the entire Chinese acumoxa-cataloging tradition: the principal text from which [[皇甫謐]] Huángfǔ Mì drew his 349-acupoint catalog in [[KR3ee005]] *Zhēnjiǔ jiǎyǐ jīng*; the textual ancestor of the surviving Táng *Huángdì Míngtáng jiǔjīng* [[KR3ee018]] and of the Yuán *Xīfāngzǐ Míngtáng jiǔjīng* [[KR3ee023]]. The original is otherwise lost in independent transmission and survives only in citation and through these derivative works. Not separately cataloged in the Kanripo corpus.
