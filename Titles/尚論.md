# Shànglùn 尚論
*Esteeming the Discussions (of Shānghán lùn)*

Short title for *Shànglùn piān* 尚論篇 (KR3e0091), the influential *Shānghán lùn* commentary by [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng (Jiāyán 嘉言, 1585–1664), founding text of the Xīchāng-school *Shānghán* exegesis.
