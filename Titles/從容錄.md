# Cóngróng lù 從容錄

*Record of Composure* (also titled 從容庵錄 *Cóngróng ān lù*). Major Cáodòng-line gōng'àn collection: a Sòng-era anthology of Hóngzhì Zhèngjué 宏智正覺's verses on one hundred cases, with later commentary by Wànsōng Xíngxiù 萬松行秀 (preface 1224). Forms with the *Bìyán lù* ([[KR6q0078]]) the principal pair of Sòng Chán case anthologies.
