# Shíméntíng lù 石門提錄

A Northern-Sòng scholarly-Chán miscellany by [[慧洪]] Juéfàn Huìhóng (1071–1128), companion to his *Línjiān lù* in the establishment of the lexicographical-scholarly paradigm for Chán study.
