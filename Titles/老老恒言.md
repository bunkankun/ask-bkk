# 老老恒言

Qiánlóng-era elder-care manual (1773) by Cáo Tíngdòng 曹庭棟; with Wāng Áng's *Yǎngshēng zǒngyào* and Xú Wénbì's *Shòushì chuánzhēn*, one of the canonical late-Qiánlóng triad of household *yǎngshēng* manuals.
