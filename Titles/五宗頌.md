# Wǔ zōng sòng 五宗頌

Gathic praise-verses on each of the Five Houses of Chán, composed by the Northern-Sòng layman [[楊無為]] Yáng Wúwéi and preserved in juan 6 of the *Rén tiān yǎnmù*.
