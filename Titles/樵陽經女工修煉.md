# Qiáoyáng jīng nǚgōng xiūliàn 樵陽經女工修煉

A female internal-alchemy (*nǚdān* 女丹) Daoist text describing the distinctive practice of "*zhǎn chìlóng*" 斬赤龍 ("beheading the red dragon" = cessation of menstruation through internal alchemical cultivation). Opens the *Nǚdān hébiān xuǎnzhù* 女丹合編選註 ([[KR3ei036]]) anthology compiled by [[賀龍驤]] Hè Lóngxiāng in the late Qīng.
