# 傷寒論類方

*Shānghán lùn lèifāng* "Classified Formulas of the *Shānghán lùn*", reorganisation of [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 by prescription-type rather than by syndrome, by [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (1693–1771).
