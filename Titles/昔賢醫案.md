# Xī xián yīàn 昔賢醫案
*Medical Case Records of Former Worthies*

A four-volume manuscript compilation assembled in Dàoguāng 30 (1850) by [[王文書]] Wáng Wénshū at the Shěn family's Tàinán villa, gathering scattered case-records of [[俞震]] Yú Zhèn and [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng acquired through book-traders. Not transmitted as an independent printed work; its preface survives via [[KR3ep051]] *ShěnYú yīàn héchāo*.
