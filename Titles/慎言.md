# 慎言

Major philosophical work of [[王廷相]] Wáng Tíngxiāng (1474–1544), articulating his *qì*-monist position. Mentioned in *Shèshēng yàoyì* ([[KR3eo015]]) as part of Wáng's unified philosophical project alongside [[雅述]] *Yǎshù*.
