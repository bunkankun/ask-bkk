# Chánzōng zhèngmài 禪宗正脈

A 10-juan Míng-period Línjì lineage-compendium by [[如巹]] Mìān Rújǐn, completed in Hóngzhì 2 (1489); typically read alongside the same compiler's *Zī mén jǐng xùn* ([[KR6q0100]]).
