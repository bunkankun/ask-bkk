# Ānxīn fǎmén 安心法門

Northern-School Táng Chán dialogue-treatise centred on the celebrated Bodhidharma-Huìkě "pacify-the-mind" exchange; constitutes the fourth gate of [[KR6q0084]] *Shǎoshì liù mén* and is also preserved in the *Zōngjìng lù* and *Zhèngfǎ yǎnzàng*.
