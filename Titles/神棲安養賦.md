# Shén qī ān yǎng fù 神棲安養賦

A short prose-rhapsody (*fù* 賦) by [[延壽]] Yǒngmíng Yánshòu (904–975) in praise of Pure Land rebirth in Sukhāvatī ("the realm of peaceful cultivation"); one of his shorter doctrinal-devotional compositions.
