# Zhěnjiā zhèngyǎn 診家正眼
*Correct Eye for Diagnosticians*

Pulse-diagnostic manual by [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ (Shìcái 士材, 1588–1655), part of the *Shìcái sānshū* 士材三書 trilogy. A canonical pedagogical text in the Qīng medical curriculum; referenced in [[KR3ep081]] *Lǐzhōng yīàn*.
