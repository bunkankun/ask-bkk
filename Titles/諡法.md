# Shìfǎ 諡法
*Code of Posthumous Names*

A traditional Chinese reference work on the conventions of posthumous naming (*shì*), most famously attributed to the Western-Zhōu statesman Zhōugōng or to Shǐ Yì; cited in medieval Sino-Japanese Buddhist exegesis as part of the classical-Chinese lexicographic apparatus used to elucidate doctrinal terminology.
