# 法華經科註

The *Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù* (Sectional-Analysis-and-Annotation Subcommentary on the Lotus Sūtra) title is borne by three distinct Lotus Sūtra commentaries preserved in the *Xùzàngjīng*:

- *Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù* by [[守倫]] (Sòng, 10 juan, X30n0605) — [[KR6d0071]]
- *Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù* by [[徐行善]] (Yuán, 8 juan, X31n0606) — [[KR6d0072]]
- *Fǎhuá jīng kēzhù* by [[一如]] (Míng, 7 juan, X31n0607) — [[KR6d0073]]
