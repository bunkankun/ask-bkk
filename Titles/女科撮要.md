# 女科撮要

*Essentials Picked from Women's Medicine* — mid-Míng gynecology in two *juǎn* by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (1487–1559), widely cited in later *fùkē* literature including [[KR3ei029]] *Nǚkē jīnglún*.
