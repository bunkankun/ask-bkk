# 陳氏接骨書

*Chénshì jiēgǔ shū* — a manuscript bone-setting treatise of the Chén family, transmitted in the Xiāoshān 蕭山 (Hángzhōu) Hú household and used by [[胡廷光]] Hú Tíngguāng as one of the three foundational sources of [[KR3el011]] *Shāngkē huìzuǎn* (1815), alongside [[KR3el018]] *Zhèngtǐ lèiyào* and [[KR3el015]] *Zhènggǔ xīnfǎ yàozhǐ*. No independent transmission survives; the Chén-family material is preserved only as the *Chénshì jiēgǔ gē* 陳氏接骨歌 mnemonic verses cited in juǎn 3 of *Shāngkē huìzuǎn*.
