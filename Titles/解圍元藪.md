# Jiěwéi yuánsǒu 解圍元藪
*Original Marsh of Releasing the Siege*

A mid-Míng leprosy / *fēng* 風 formulary in 4 *juǎn* attributed to Shěn Zhīwèn 沈之問 (preface dated *Jiājìng jiǎyín* = 1554). One of the earliest specialised Chinese monographs on *dàmáfēng* (Hansen's disease and cognate skin scourges); a principal source for late-imperial leprosy compilations including the anonymous *Mìchuán dàmáfēng fāng* ([[KR3eh043]]).
