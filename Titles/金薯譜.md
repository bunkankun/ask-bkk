# Jīnshǔ pǔ 金薯譜
*Treatise on the Golden Tuber (Sweet Potato)*

An agricultural-medical treatise by [[陳世元]] Chén Shìyuán (1768) documenting the cultivation, properties, and reputed longevity-effects of the sweet potato (*gānshǔ* 甘薯) in coastal Fújiàn. Cited in late-Qīng medical literature including [[陳念祖]] Chén Niànzǔ's *Nǚkē yàozhǐ* 女科要旨 ([[KR3ei008]]).
