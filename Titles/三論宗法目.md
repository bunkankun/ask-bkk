# Sānlùnzōng fǎmù 三論宗法目
*Catalogue of Sanron-School Dharma-Items*

A traditional Japanese Sanron-school bibliographical-doctrinal index used as a teaching reference in Tōdaiji and other Sanron-affiliated monasteries; cited in modern Japanese scholarship (e.g. in connection with Chinkai's biography).
