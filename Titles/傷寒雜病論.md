# 傷寒雜病論

*Treatise on Cold Damage and Miscellaneous Diseases* — Late-Hàn medical treatise by [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng (c. 150–c. 219), the foundational text of clinical Chinese medicine; transmitted in its two reconstructed halves as the *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 ([[KR3e0008]]) and the *Jīnguì yàolüè* 金匱要略 ([[KR3e0007]]). One of the three original *juǎn* was devoted to women's medicine.
