# 西湖鑑一疏

*Xīhú Jiànyī shū*

A *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* commentary by [[鑑一元盤]] Jiànyī Yuánpán of Xīhú 西湖 (Hángzhōu), singled out by [[行策]] Jiéliú Xíngcè as best exemplifying the principle of "abandoning self and following the sūtra."
