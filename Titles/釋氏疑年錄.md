# Shìshì yílǜ 釋氏疑年錄

A modern reference work by [[陳垣]] Chén Yuán, compiling birth and death dates for Chinese Buddhist clerics, with critical evaluation of conflicting source attestations. A foundational tool for the historiography of Chinese Buddhism.
