# 醫林集要

*Essentials from the Forest of Medicine* — a Míng-period medical compendium; cited as a successor in the *wēnbǔ* 溫補 (warming-tonifying) tradition of [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn's *Fùrén guī* 婦人規 ([[KR3ei028]]).
