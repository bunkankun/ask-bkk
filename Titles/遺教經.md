# 遺教經

*Yíjiào jīng* — "Sūtra of the Bequeathed Teaching" (Skt. roughly *Buddha-parinirvāṇa-mahāsūtra*; T389). Traditionally attributed in Chinese tradition to the translator Kumārajīva (鳩摩羅什). It records the Buddha's final teaching delivered to his disciples on the night of his *parinirvāṇa* and became, with the *Sìshíèrzhāng jīng* 四十二章經 ([[KR6i0483]]) and the *Bā dàrén jué jīng* 八大人覺經 ([[KR6i0476]]), one of the Sòng-grouped "Three Sūtras of the Buddha-Patriarchs" (佛祖三經).
