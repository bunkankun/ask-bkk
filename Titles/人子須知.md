# 人子須知

*Rénzǐ xūzhī* (*What a Son Must Know*) — Confucian/genealogical handbook of filial duty, here referenced as the foundational primer given by Hú Tíngguāng's father in his autobiographical *Fánlì* to [[KR3el011]] *Shāngkē huìzuǎn*. Most commonly identifies the Míng work of 徐善繼 / 徐善述 (a Jiāngxī kānyú / geomantic-genealogical treatise of *Wànlì* era); other Qīng titles by the same name also circulated as household instructional texts.
