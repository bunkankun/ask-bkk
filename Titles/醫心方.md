**Ishinpō** 醫心方, the foundational Japanese medical encyclopaedia in 30 *kan* by [[丹波康賴]] Tamba no Yasuyori (912–995), submitted to the imperial court in Eikan 2 (984). The largest single source for now-lost Suí and Táng Chinese medical texts; canonical for the Tamba 多紀 / Taki line of Japanese *kōshōgaku* medical philology. Cited in [[KR3eh054]] for its dedicated *kakke* chapter.
