# 永覺元賢禪師廣錄

*Yǒngjué Yuánxián chánshī guǎnglù*

The collected recorded sayings (*guǎnglù*) of [[元賢]] Yǒngjué Yuánxián (1578 – 1657), late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng-line Chán master of Gǔshān Yǒngquánsì; preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon.
