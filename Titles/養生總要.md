# 養生總要

Late-17th-century *yǎngshēng* compendium attributed to Wāng Áng 汪昂; with Cáo Tíngdòng's *Lǎolǎo héngyán* and Xú Wénbì's *Shòushì chuánzhēn*, one of the canonical late-Qiánlóng triad of household *yǎngshēng* manuals.
