# Yùshì lǜlì 喻氏律例
*Master Yù's Rules and Examples*

Collective reference to the *lǜ* (rules) and *lì* (examples) corpus by [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng (Jiāyán 嘉言, 1585–1664), notably the *Yīmén fǎlǜ* 醫門法律. Cited as foundational doctrinal source by Yù-school transmitters such as [[舒馳遠]] Shū Chíyuǎn and his disciple [[齊秉慧]] Qí Bǐnghuì ([[KR3ep074]]).
