# 隆興編年通論

Southern Sòng chronological-annalistic Buddhist history compiled by [[祖琇]] Zǔxiù in 1164 (*Lóngxīng* 2). A juan-based annalistic survey of Buddhist history from the Hàn through the Northern Sòng.
