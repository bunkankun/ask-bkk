# 徐靈胎醫書

*Xú Língtāi yīshū* "The Medical Books of Xú Língtāi", collected edition of the medical writings of [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (*zì* Língtāi 靈胎, 1693–1771), issued in the late 18th and 19th centuries.
