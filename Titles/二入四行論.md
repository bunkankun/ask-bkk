# Èrrù sìxíng lùn 二入四行論

Early-Chán treatise on the "two entrances" and four practices preserved in the Dūnhuáng manuscript tradition; the principal pre-compilation witness for the *Èrzhǒng rù* material that became gate 3 of [[KR6q0084]] *Shǎoshì liù mén*. Standard critical edition by Yanagida Seizan in his *Daruma no goroku* (1969).
