# Wéimó shū jì 維摩疏記
*Notes on the Commentary to the Vimalakīrti-sūtra*

A Tiantai-school sub-commentary on the *Vimalakīrti-nirdeśa* (維摩經) tradition; cited in medieval Japanese Sanron exegesis as part of the inherited lexicographic and doctrinal apparatus used to gloss Jízàng's *Sānlùn xuányì*.
