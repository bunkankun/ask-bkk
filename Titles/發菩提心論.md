# 發菩提心論

*Fā pútí xīn lùn* — the "Treatise on Generating the Mind of Awakening" (T1665), traditionally attributed to Nāgārjuna but in the East Asian transmission associated with Amoghavajra (Bùkōng 不空), one of the principal Esoteric-Buddhist treatments of the *bodhicitta* doctrine.
