# 牟子理惑論

*Móuzǐ lǐhuò lùn* — "Master Móu's Treatise on the Settling of Doubts."

An early Chinese apologetic treatise on Buddhism in dialogue form, traditionally ascribed to a late-Hàn lay literatus Móu Zǐ 牟子 (Mou Bo 牟博) of Jiāozhōu 交州. It is preserved as the opening text of the *Hóngmíng jí* 弘明集 ([[KR6r0042]]); its dating and authorship remain debated, with proposals ranging from the late Eastern Hàn through the early Six Dynasties.
