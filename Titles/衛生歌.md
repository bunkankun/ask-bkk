# 衛生歌

"Song of Protecting Life", a *yǎngshēng* verse traditionally attributed to Sūn Sīmiǎo 孫思邈 (hence *Sūn zhēnrén wèishēng gē*), in its received form a Sòng compilation; opening piece of Mǎ Qí's *Yǎngshēng mìzhǐ*.
