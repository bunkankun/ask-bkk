**Man'an-hō** 萬安方, a Kamakura-period Japanese *kanbun* medical encyclopaedia in 62 *kan* by [[梶原性全]] Kajiwara Shōzen, compiled ca. 1315 and presented to the Hōjō regency. The first major Japanese-compiled Sinitic medical encyclopaedia, drawing heavily on Sòng sources (*Shèngjì zǒnglù*, *Tàipíng shènghuì fāng*); cited in [[KR3eh054]] for its *kakke* prescriptions.
