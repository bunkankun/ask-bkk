# 普賢十大願

*Pǔxián shí dà yuàn* — the "Ten Great Vows of Samantabhadra," a foundational devotional formula derived from the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn pǐn* of the *Avataṃsaka sūtra*; the standard liturgical articulation of bodhisattva vows in East Asian Mahāyāna practice.
