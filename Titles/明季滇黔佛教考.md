# Míngjì Diān-Qián Fójiào kǎo 明季滇黔佛教考

*Study of Buddhism in Yúnnán and Guìzhōu during the Late Míng*

A foundational modern study (1940) by the historian [[陳垣]] Chén Yuán on the development of Buddhism — and particularly Chán — in the southwestern provinces of Yúnnán 雲南 and Guìzhōu 貴州 during the late Míng and Míng-Qīng transition period.
