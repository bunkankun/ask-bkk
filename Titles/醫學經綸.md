# 醫學經綸

*The Warp and Woof of Medicine* — early-Qīng general-medicine encyclopedia by [[蕭塤]] Xiāo Xūn covering 160-plus *zázhèng* 雜症 categories with over 7,000 citations from earlier authorities; companion to his *Nǚkē jīnglún* 女科經綸 ([[KR3ei029]]).
