# Tiānyuán yīàn 天元醫案
*Tiānyuán Medical Case Records*

A Yè-school casebook of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì seen by [[葉萬青]] Yè Wànqīng at [[周謝庵]] Zhōu Xièān's studio in 1816 and used as a collation source for [[KR3ep079]] *Yèshì yīàn cúnzhēn*.
