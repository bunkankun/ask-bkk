# 仲景全書 Zhòngjǐng quánshū

Wànlì 27 (1599) compilation by [[趙開美]] Zhào Kāiměi, gathering the principal Zhāng Jī 張機 (Zhòngjǐng) canonical works — most importantly a facsimile reproduction of a Northern-Sòng print of the *Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef001]]) — and serving as the principal manuscript witness to the Sòng校正醫書局 recension. Includes the *Sòng* *Shānghán lùn*, *Zhù-jiě Shānghán lùn* ([[KR3ef005]]), and the *Jīnguì yàoluè* materials.
