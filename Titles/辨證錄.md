# Biànzhèng lù 辨證錄
*Record of Pattern Differentiation*

Medical work by [[陳士鐸]] Chén Shìduó (1627–1707), published 1687, in the *spirit-revelation* genre. Together with the *Shíshì mìlù* 石室秘錄, it constitutes the principal clinical synthesis from which substantial portions of the received *Fù Qīngzhǔ nǚkē* 傅青主女科 derive.
