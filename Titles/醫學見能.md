**Yī xué jiàn néng** 醫學見能, a popularising late-Qīng didactic medical handbook by [[唐宗海]] Táng Zōnghǎi (1847–1897), companion piece to his *Sānzì jué* didactic monographs (cf. [[KR3eh056]]). One of the principal pedagogical works of the *Zhōng-Xī huìtōng* 中西匯通 movement.
