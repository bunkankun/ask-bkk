# Běncǎo cóngxīn 本草從新
*Materia Medica Renewed*

A mid-Qīng *materia medica* compiled by [[吳儀洛]] Wú Yíluò (1757), revising and updating earlier *běncǎo* compendia. A widely-circulated pharmacological reference in late-Qīng Chinese-medicine practice.
