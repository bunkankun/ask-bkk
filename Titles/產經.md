# Chǎnjīng 產經

An early Chinese obstetrical work, traditionally attributed to the Six Dynasties period and associated with Dé Zhēnchángzhě 德貞常者 (alternate attributions exist). Lost in China but partially preserved through citations in the Japanese *Ishinpō* 醫心方 and other later compilations.
