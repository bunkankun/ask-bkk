# Qiánzhāi yīyào cóngshū 潛齋醫藥叢書

A late-Qīng *cóngshū* of the [[王孟英]] Wáng Mèngyīng school (Qiánzhāi 潛齋 being Wáng's *biéhào*). Late-Qīng stone-print editions transmitted the Wáng-school commentaries on Shěn Yòupéng's *Nǚkē jíyào* ([[KR3ei010]]) that [[張壽頤]] Zhāng Shòuyí later re-set and annotated in his *Shěnshì nǚkē jíyào jiānshū* ([[KR3ei041]]).
