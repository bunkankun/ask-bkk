# 脈鏡

*Mirror of Pulse-Diagnosis* — early-Qīng pulse-diagnosis work by [[葉其蓁]] Yè Qízhēn, companion-volume to his *Shísānkē zhǐzhǎng* 十三科指掌 and *Nǚkē zhǐzhǎng* 女科指掌 ([[KR3ei027]]).
