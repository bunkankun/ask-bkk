# Zhōu yì cān tóng qì dǐng qì gē míng jìng tú 周易參同契鼎器歌明鏡圖

*Tripod-Vessel Ode and Luminous-Mirror Diagram for the Cān tóng qì*

by [[彭曉]] (Péng Xiǎo, *d.* 955) — Five-Dynasties Latter-Shǔ Daoist alchemist

**Péng Xiǎo's supplementary volume** accompanying his [[KR5d0019]] *Fēn zhāng tōng zhēn yì* — containing (1) the versified ***Dǐng qì gē*** 鼎器歌 on the alchemical tripod-vessel and (2) the ***Míng jìng tú*** 明鏡圖 (Luminous-Mirror Diagram) charting the alchemical process. Dated by colophon to 947. Preserved as **DZ 1003** / CT 1003 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 947. Dynasty: 五代.
