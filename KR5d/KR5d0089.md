# Xuán zōng zhí zhǐ wàn fǎ tóng guī 玄宗直指萬法同歸

*Direct Pointing of the Mysterious Lineage: The Ten-Thousand Dharmas Return to One*

by [[牧常晁]] (Mù Chángzhào) — Yuán Daoist

A Yuán Daoist **syncretic treatise** arguing for the convergence of all dharmas/ways in a unified cultivation. Post-1294. Preserved as **DZ 1066** / CT 1066 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** After 1294. Dynasty: 元.
