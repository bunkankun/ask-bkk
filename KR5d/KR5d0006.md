# Shàng qīng shén bǎo dòng fáng zhēn huì shàng jīng 上清神寶洞房真諱上經

*Shàng-qīng Superior Scripture of the Divine-Treasure Cavern-Chamber and Perfected Secret-Names*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Táng

A Shàng qīng 上清 scripture on the **dòng fáng** 洞房 ("cavern-chamber" — the central *dān tián* cavity in the head) and the **zhēn huì** 真諱 (secret ritual names of the perfected). Preserved as **DZ 989** / CT 989 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
