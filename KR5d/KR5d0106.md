# Lóng hǔ yuán zhǐ 龍虎元旨

*Original Purport of the Dragon-Tiger*

by [[青霞子]] (Qīng xiá zǐ)

A late-Táng to Five-Dynasties Daoist alchemical treatise attributed to Qīng xiá zǐ (= Sū Yuánlǎng 蘇元朗). Preserved as **DZ 1083** / CT 1083 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 9th–10th century. Dynasty: 晚唐-五代.
