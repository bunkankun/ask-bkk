# Xuán zhū lù 玄珠錄

*Record of the Mysterious Pearl*

by [[王暉]] (Wáng Huī); likely = Wáng Xuánlǎn 王玄覽 (626–697)

A Táng **Chóng xuán 重玄 philosophical-aphoristic collection** — nearly certainly recording the lectures of **Wáng Xuánlǎn** 王玄覽 (626–697), one of the most original early-Táng Chóng-xuán philosophers, whose disciples compiled his oral teachings. The Dào zàng attribution to "Wáng Huī" 王暉 is likely a corruption. Preserved as **DZ 1048** / CT 1048 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Late 7th century. Dynasty: 唐.
