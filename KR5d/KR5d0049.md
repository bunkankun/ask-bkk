# Tiān yǐn zǐ 天隱子

*Master of Heavenly Concealment*

by [[司馬承禎]] (Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn, 647–735) — Twelfth Shàng qīng patriarch

A short Táng Daoist **meditative treatise** in eight sections, expounding a graduated ascent of meditative practice: 齋戒 *zhāi jiè* (fasting and precepts), 安處 *ān chǔ* (settled abode), 存想 *cún xiǎng* (visualization), 坐忘 *zuò wàng* ("sit-and-forget"), 神解 *shén jiě* (spiritual release). Traditionally attributed to Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn and closely paralleling his [[KR5d0059]] *Zuò wàng lùn*. Preserved as **DZ 1026** / CT 1026 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Within Sīmǎ Chéngzhēn's active life (*c.* 700–735). Dynasty: 唐.
