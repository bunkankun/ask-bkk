# Yì tú tōng biàn 易圖通變

*Penetrating the Transformations of the Yì Diagrams*

by [[雷思齊]] (Léi Sīqí) — "Kōng shān xiān sheng"

Léi Sīqí's five-*juàn* **cosmological-diagrammatic treatise on the *Yì jīng***, dated 1330 by preface. Companion-work to [[KR5d0031]] *Yì shì tōng biàn*. Preserved as **DZ 1014** / CT 1014 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部), with prefatory pieces [[KR5d0032]] and [[KR5d0033]].

**Dating.** 1330. Dynasty: 元.
