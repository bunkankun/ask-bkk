# Huáng dì nèi jīng Líng shū lüè 黃帝內經靈樞略

*Abridged Huángdì Nèijīng Língshū*

Anonymous; TángSòng

An **abridged** (*lüè* 略) recension of the *Líng shū* 靈樞 — the second half of the *Huáng dì nèi jīng*. Preserved as **DZ 1019** / CT 1019 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部). See [[KR5d0042]] for the Southern-Sòng Shǐ Sōng recension.

**Dating.** TángSòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
