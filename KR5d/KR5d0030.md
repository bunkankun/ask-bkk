# Xuán pìn zhī mén fù 玄牝之門賦

*Rhapsody on the Gate of the Mysterious Feminine*

by [[俞琰]] (Yǔ Yǎn, 1245–1314)

Yǔ Yǎn's short ***fù*** rhapsody expounding the *Dào dé jīng* chapter 6 phrase **玄牝之門** "the Gate of the Mysterious Feminine" — a central trope of inner-alchemical meditation on the origin of cosmos and body. Preserved as **DZ 1010** / CT 1010 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1284–1296. Dynasty: 宋-元.
