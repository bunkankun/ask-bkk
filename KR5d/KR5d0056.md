# Zhì yán zǒng 至言總

*Compendium of Supreme Sayings*

compiled by [[范翛然]] (Fàn Xiāorán) — late-Táng Daoist

A late-Táng Daoist **philosophical-meditative anthology**, collecting "supreme sayings" (*zhì yán* 至言) on cultivation. Preserved as **DZ 1033** / CT 1033 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部) in 5 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Late Táng. Dynasty: 唐後期.
