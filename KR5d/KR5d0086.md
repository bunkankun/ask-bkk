# Dòng yuān jí 洞淵集

*Cavern-Abyss Collection*

by [[李思聰]] (Lǐ Sīcōng) — Northern-Sòng Daoist

A Northern-Sòng Daoist **poetic-philosophical collection**, *c.* 1050. The Dào zàng preserves two anthologies under this title — this one by Lǐ Sīcōng, the other ([[KR5d0087]]) by Cháng quán zǐ 長筌子. Preserved as **DZ 1063** / CT 1063 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** *c.* 1050. Dynasty: 北宋.
