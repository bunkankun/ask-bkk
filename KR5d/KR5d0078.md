# Chún yáng zhēn rén hùn chéng jí 純陽真人渾成集

*Collection of Untempered-Completeness of the Perfected Pure-Yáng*

compiled by [[何志淵]] (Hé Zhìyuān); verses attributed to [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòngbīn, Chún yáng zǐ)

A Southern-Sòng **collection of verses** attributed to the Daoist immortal **Lǚ Dòngbīn** 呂洞賓 (Chún yáng zǐ 純陽子), compiled by Hé Zhìyuān with preface dated 1251. Preserved as **DZ 1055** / CT 1055 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1251. Dynasty: 南宋.
