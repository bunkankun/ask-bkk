# Dào tǐ lùn 道體論

*Discourse on the Body of the Way*

by [[通玄先生]] (Tōng xuán xiān sheng) — 10th-century Daoist

A short Daoist **philosophical treatise** on the **body / substance of the Way** (*dào tǐ* 道體) — i.e., the metaphysical constitution of the Way. Preserved as **DZ 1035** / CT 1035 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 10th century. Dynasty: 五代-宋.
