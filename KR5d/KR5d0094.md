# Yuán yáng zǐ fǎ yǔ 原陽子法語

*Dharma-Sayings of Master Yuán-yáng*

by [[趙宜真]] (Zhào Yízhēn) — late-Yuán / early-Míng Jìng míng and Qīng wēi Daoist

Dharma-sayings of Zhào Yízhēn 趙宜真 (Yuán yáng zǐ 原陽子), a leading Jìng míng 淨明 / Qīng wēi 清微 patriarch active in the Yuán-Míng transition. Preserved as **DZ 1071** / CT 1071 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Late Yuán to early Míng. Dynasty: 元-明.
