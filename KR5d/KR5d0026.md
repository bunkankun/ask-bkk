# Gǔ wén Cān tóng qì jí jiě 古文參同契集解

*Collected Explanations of the Ancient-Script Cān tóng qì*

compiled by [[蔣一彪]] (Jiǎng Yībiāo) — Míng alchemical commentator

A Míng collected-commentary edition of the "ancient-script" (*gǔ wén* 古文) recension of the *Cān tóng qì* — an alternate recension distinct from the mainstream Péng Xiǎo / Zhū Xī textual tradition. Not preserved in the Dào zàng proper; listed in the Kanseki catalog from other sources. (Note: source directory absent from the Kanseki mirror.)

**Dating.** Míng. Dynasty: 明.
