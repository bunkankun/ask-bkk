# Tài shàng dòng xuán Líng bǎo sān yī wǔ qì zhēn jīng 太上洞玄靈寶三一五氣真經

*Most High Dòngxuán Língbǎo Perfect Scripture of the Three-One and Five Pneumas*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Daoist **meditation-scripture** on the **Three-One** (*sān yī* 三一 — the three deities of the three *dān tián* as a unified trinity) and the **Five Pneumas** (*wǔ qì* 五氣 — the five-phase pneumas of the viscera). Preserved as **DZ 985** / CT 985 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
