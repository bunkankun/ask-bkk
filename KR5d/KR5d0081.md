# Wú wéi qīng jìng cháng shēng zhēn rén zhì zhēn yǔ lù 無為清靜長生真人至真語錄

*Supreme-Perfect Recorded Sayings of the Non-Acting, Pure-Still, Long-Lived Perfected*

by [[劉處玄]] (Liú Chùxuán, 1147–1203) — one of the Seven Perfected of Quán zhēn

The recorded sayings of **Liú Chùxuán** 劉處玄 (1147–1203), *hào* Cháng shēng zǐ 長生子, one of the Seven Perfected of Quán zhēn and founder of the Suí shān 隨山 branch. Preface dated 1202. Preserved as **DZ 1058** / CT 1058 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1202 (preface). Dynasty: 金.
