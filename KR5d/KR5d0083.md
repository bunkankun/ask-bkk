# Qīng ān Yíng chán zǐ yǔ lù 清庵瑩蟾子語錄

*Recorded Sayings of Qīng-ān, Master Yíng-chán*

compiled by [[柴元皋]] (Chái Yuángāo); sayings of [[李道純]] (Lǐ Dàochún)

The recorded sayings of **[[李道純]] Lǐ Dàochún** (Qīng ān 清庵, Yíng chán zǐ 瑩蟾子), the great Yuán *nèi dān* synthesizer who bridged the Southern Lineage and Quán zhēn traditions. Preface dated 1288. Preserved as **DZ 1060** / CT 1060 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1288. Dynasty: 元.
