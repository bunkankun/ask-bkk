# Gǔ wén Lóng hǔ jīng zhù shū 古文龍虎經註疏

*Commentary and Sub-Commentary on the Ancient-Script Dragon-Tiger Scripture*

by [[王道]] (Wáng Dào) — Sòng Daoist alchemical exegete

A Sòng three-*juàn* **commentary-and-sub-commentary** on the ***Gǔ wén Lóng hǔ jīng*** 古文龍虎經 — an alchemical classic closely affiliated with the *Cān tóng qì* 參同契, extant only through citations and this commentary. Preserved as **DZ 996** / CT 996 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
