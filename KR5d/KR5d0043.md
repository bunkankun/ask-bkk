# Huáng dì nèi jīng Sù wèn yí piān 黃帝內經素問遺篇

*The Lost Chapters of the Huáng-dì Nèi-jīng Sù-wèn*

Anonymous; Sòng

The **"lost chapters"** (*yí piān* 遺篇) of the *Sù wèn* — chapters missing from Wáng Bīng's 762 redaction, recovered (or reconstructed) during the Sòng and appended. Preserved as **DZ 1021** / CT 1021 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
