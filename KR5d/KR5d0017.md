# Zhōu yì cān tóng qì zhù (èr) 周易參同契注(二)

*Commentary on the Cān tóng qì (Recension II)*

Anonymous; 12th century or later

The **second of the three Dào zàng recensions** of the *Cān tóng qì* — an anonymous Sòng-era commentary in 3 *juàn*. Preserved as **DZ 1000** / CT 1000 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 12th century or later. Dynasty: 宋.
