# Xuán jiào dà gōng àn 玄教大公案

*Great Gōng-àn of the Mysterious Teaching*

by [[苗善時]] (Miáo Shànshí) — Yuán Quán zhēn Daoist

A Yuán Daoist collection of ***gōng àn*** 公案 (lit. "public cases" — Chan-style problematic questions/exchanges applied to Daoist practice). Preface dated 1324. Preserved as **DZ 1065** / CT 1065 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1324. Dynasty: 元.
