# Sù lǚ zǐ 素履子

*Master Plain-Treading*

by [[張弧]] (Zhāng Hú) — Táng Daoist-Confucian syncretist

A Táng Daoist-Confucian **moral treatise** organized in fifteen sections on moral virtues — *lǚ dào* 履道 (treading the Way), *lǚ xué* 履學 (treading learning), *lǚ liǎn* 履廉 (treading integrity), etc. The title (from *Yì jīng* hexagram 10 *Lǚ* 履) signals a fusion of Confucian ethics and Daoist cultivation. Preserved as **DZ 1027** / CT 1027 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
