# Zhōu yì cān tóng qì shì yí 周易參同契釋疑

*Resolution of Doubts about the Cān tóng qì*

by [[俞琰]] (Yǔ Yǎn, 1245–1314)

Yǔ Yǎn's **companion-text to his [[KR5d0022]] *Fā huī***, handling specific philological and interpretive difficulties in the *Cān tóng qì*. Preface dated 1281, preceding the full *Fā huī* (1284). Preserved as **DZ 1006** / CT 1006 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1281. Dynasty: 宋-元.
