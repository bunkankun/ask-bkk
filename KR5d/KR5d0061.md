# Xīn mù lùn 心目論

*Discourse on Mind and Eye*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A short Daoist philosophical treatise on the relation of **mind** (*xīn* 心) and **eye** (*mù* 目) — the inner faculty of awareness and its outer organ of perception. Preserved as **DZ 1038** / CT 1038 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
