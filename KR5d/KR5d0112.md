# Yuán qīng zǐ zhì mìng piān 爰清子至命篇

*Master Yuán-qīng's Treatise on Reaching-Destiny*

by [[王慶升]] (Wáng Qìngshēng, Yuán qīng zǐ)

A Southern-Sòng *nèi dān* treatise on the cultivation of "destiny" (*mìng* 命), the physiological dimension of inner alchemy. Dated 1249. Preserved as **DZ 1089** / CT 1089 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1249. Dynasty: 南宋.
