# Sù wèn liù qì xuán zhū mì yǔ 素問六氣玄珠密語

*Secret-Speech of the Mysterious-Pearl on the Six Pneumas of the Sù-wèn*

attributed to [[王冰]] (Wáng Bīng, *c.* 710 – *c.* 805)

A Táng medical-cosmological treatise on the ***Six Pneumas*** (*liù qì* 六氣) system of the *Sù wèn*, pseudepigraphically attributed to Wáng Bīng (who styled himself Qǐ xuán zǐ 啟玄子 — hence "mysterious-pearl" *xuán zhū* 玄珠). Preserved as **DZ 1023** / CT 1023 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Received recension Táng; the attribution to Wáng Bīng is plausible but insecure. Dynasty: 唐.
