# Jīn dān sì bǎi zì 金丹四百字

*Four Hundred Characters on the Gold Elixir*

attributed to [[張伯端]] (Zhāng Bóduān, 987–1082) — Founding Patriarch of the Nán zōng

A short **versified treatise** on the gold elixir in exactly four hundred characters — a compact companion to the larger *Wù zhēn piān* 悟真篇. Pseudepigraphically attributed to Zhāng Bóduān; the extant recension is dated 1241. (Catalog meta renders Zhāng's name as 張伯瑞 — a common variant of 張伯端.) Preserved as **DZ 1081** / CT 1081 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Attributed to Zhāng Bóduān (*fl.* 1070s); received recension Southern Sòng, 1241. Dynasty: 北宋-南宋.
