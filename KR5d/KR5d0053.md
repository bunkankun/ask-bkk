# Liú zǐ 劉子

*Master Liú*

anonymous (commentary by [[袁孝政]] Yuán Xiàozhèng, Táng)

The ***Liú zǐ*** is a late-Six-Dynasties philosophical-ethical miscellany traditionally (but uncertainly) attributed either to **Liú Xié** 劉勰 (author of the *Wén xīn diāo lóng* 文心雕龍) or to **Liú Zhòu** 劉晝 (514–565). In 55 short sections covering self-cultivation, governance, and moral psychology. The standard commentary is by Yuán Xiàozhèng 袁孝政 of the Táng. Preserved as **DZ 1030** / CT 1030 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Core text late Six Dynasties; commentary Táng. Dynasty: 南朝-唐.
