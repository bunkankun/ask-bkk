# Tài shàng cháng wén dà dòng Líng bǎo yōu xuán shàng pǐn miào jīng 太上長文大洞靈寶幽玄上品妙經

*Most High Long-Text Great-Cavern Língbǎo Obscure-Mystery Wondrous Scripture of the Superior Grade*

Anonymous; TángSòng

A Daoist **Líng bǎo** 靈寶 superior-grade (*shàng pǐn* 上品) scripture in the "long-text" (*cháng wén* 長文) genre of the *Dà dòng* 大洞 tradition. Preserved as **DZ 991** / CT 991 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部), with companion exegesis [[KR5d0009]].

**Dating.** TángSòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
