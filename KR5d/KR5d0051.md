# Wú néng zǐ 無能子

*Master Unable*

Anonymous; late Táng (887)

A late-Táng **Daoist-naturalist philosophical treatise** in 34 short chapters, by an anonymous author who styles himself Wú néng zǐ 無能子 ("Master Unable"). Dated by internal reference to 887 (Guāng qǐ 3), the late Huáng Cháo 黃巢 rebellion period. The text advocates a radical primitivism, denying cultural artifice and advocating a return to unconditioned nature. Preserved as **DZ 1028** / CT 1028 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 887. Dynasty: 唐.
