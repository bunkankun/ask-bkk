# Huáng dì Sù wèn Líng shū jí zhù 黃帝素問靈樞集註

*Collected Commentary on the Huángdì Sùwèn and Língshū*

by [[史崧]] (Shǐ Sōng) — Southern-Sòng physician

**The standard received recension of the *Líng shū*** 靈樞 — second half of the *Huáng dì nèi jīng*. Shǐ Sōng's 1155 edition (Shào xīng 25) with collected commentary has been the base text of the *Líng shū* ever since. Preserved as **DZ 1020** / CT 1020 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** 1155. Dynasty: 南宋.

## Translations and research

- Unschuld, Paul U. *Huang Di Nei Jing Ling Shu: The Ancient Classic on Needle Therapy*. University of California Press, 2016. Standard English translation.
