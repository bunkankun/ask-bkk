# Dòng yuán zǐ nèi dān jué 洞元子內丹訣

*Master Dòng-yuán's Instructions on Inner Elixir*

Anonymous; Northern Sòng

A Northern-Sòng *nèi dān* instruction-text attributed to the pseudonymous Dòng yuán zǐ 洞元子 ("Master of the Cavern-Origin"). Preserved as **DZ 1097** / CT 1097 (Tài xuán bù 太玄部).

**Dating.** Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋.
