# KR4f 別集類(清) — Biéjí lèi (Qīng)
*Belles-Lettres — Individual Literary Collections of the Qīng Dynasty*

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR4f sits within KR4 集部 *Jíbù* (the fourth of the four 四部 divisions of the SKQS classification — *Jīng, Shǐ, Zǐ, Jí*) and gathers individual literary collections (*biéjí* 別集 — a single author's collected prose, poetry, and lyric, as distinct from the *zǒngjí* 總集 anthologies of multiple authors) of the Qīng dynasty (清, 1644–1911). All 69 catalog entries in KR4f are Qīng works, including a handful of Míng-Qīng-transition *yímín* 遺民 (Ming-loyalist) figures conventionally classified under their Qīng-dynasty productive years. The Qīng *biéjí* corpus is the largest in Chinese bibliographic history: Wilkinson §66.4.9 records that Ke Yuchun's 2002 *Qīngrén shīwénjí zǒngmù tíyào* lists some 40,000 collected works of over 19,700 Qīng literati, of which only a fraction were printed and a still smaller fraction transmitted to modern collections. The 69 works in KR4f therefore represent a doubly-filtered canon.

Two distinct curatorial filters shape the selection. The *Sìkù quánshū* 文淵閣 selection (1773–1782) admitted Qīng authors approved by the imperial editorial board — Hānlín officials, high-provincial figures, Cheng-Zhu orthodox writers, plus the Kāngxī-Yōngzhèng-Qiánlóng imperial *yùzhì* 御製 collections — and systematically excluded Ming-loyalist authors on political grounds, so that [[顧炎武]], [[黃宗羲]], [[王夫之]], and [[錢謙益]] are absent from WYG. The *Sìbù cóngkān* 四部叢刊 selection (1919–1936) restored these *yímín* collections and added important later figures — [[姚鼐]], [[全祖望]], [[戴震]], [[錢大昕]], [[阮元]], [[張惠言]], [[龔自珍]], [[曾國藩]] — whose *biéjí* circulated in the Qīng only in family imprints or Tóng-zhì-era post-Tài-píng restoration reprints. Together the two filters preserve roughly complementary canons of Qīng literature.

Three principal Qīng literary-theoretical traditions structure the division: the *Shén-yùn* 神韻 school of poetic indirection centered on [[王士禛]]; the Tóng-chéng 桐城 school of *gǔwén* 古文 prose, descending from [[方苞]] through [[劉大櫆]] to [[姚鼐]] (the Yáng-hú 陽湖 offshoot of [[張惠言]] and [[惲敬]] partly broke with this synthesis — Wilkinson, Box 173); and the *Cháng-zhōu* 常州 and *Zhè-xī* 浙西 *cí* 詞 schools of allusive lyric, descending from [[陳維崧]] and [[朱彝尊]] in the early Qīng through [[厲鶚]] to [[張惠言]]'s late-imperial *Cí xuǎn* 詞選 program. Cross-cutting these is the great *kǎo-zhèng* 考證 (evidential research) movement of the late Qiánlóng / early Jiāqìng generation — [[戴震]], [[錢大昕]], [[盧文弨]], [[孫星衍]], [[洪亮吉]], [[汪中]], [[阮元]] — whose *biéjí* preserve the polemical, biographical, and methodological apparatus surrounding the technical monographs catalogued elsewhere in KR1–KR3. The late-Qīng *Gōng-yáng* 公羊 New-Text Confucian tradition that runs from [[張惠言]] through [[龔自珍]] to the Hundred-Days Reform generation supplies the diagnostic vocabulary of dynastic decline that closes the division.

## Important texts and text clusters

**Early-Qīng *yímín* prose and verse.** The three great early-Qīng Confucians — [[顧炎武]] in [[KR4f0006]] *Tínglín shī-jí* 亭林詩集 and [[KR4f0007]] *Tínglín yú-jí* 亭林餘集; [[黃宗羲]] in [[KR4f0008]] *Nánléi wén-àn* 南雷文案; [[王夫之]] in [[KR4f0009]] *Jiāng-zhāi shī-wén-jí* 薑齋詩文集 — were excluded by the *Sìkù* as politically inadmissible and survive in pre-modern print only via the SBCK. To these belong [[KR4f0010]] / [[KR4f0011]] *Mù-zhāi chū-xué jí* / *Yǒu-xué jí* 牧齋初學集 / 牧齋有學集 of [[錢謙益]] (the surrendered late-Míng grand secretary whose poetics shaped the *Yú-shān* 虞山 tradition); [[KR4f0021]] *Jū-yì-táng jí* 居易堂集 of [[徐枋]] (a recluse who refused all Qīng patronage); and [[KR4f0069]] *Míng-zhāi jí* 茗齋集 of [[彭孫貽]].

**Imperial *yùzhì* collections.** [[KR4f0001]] *Shèngzǔ rén huángdì yùzhì wénjí* 聖祖仁皇帝御製文集 (the Kāngxī emperor, 176 juan); [[KR4f0002]] *Shìzōng xiàn huángdì yùzhì wénjí* 世宗憲皇帝御製文集 (the Yōngzhèng emperor, 30 juan); [[KR4f0003]] *Yùzhì lè-shàn-táng quán-jí dìng-běn* 御製樂善堂全集定本 (the Qiánlóng emperor's prince-period writings, re-edited under his reign); [[KR4f0004]] *Yùzhì wén-jí* 御製文集 and [[KR4f0005]] *Yùzhì shī-jí* 御製詩集 (Qiánlóng's reign-era prose and 454-juan poetry — the single largest text in KR4f). These four imperial corpora are the principal documentary record of high-Qīng court intellectual culture and of the imperial editorial program that produced the *Sìkù quánshū* itself.

**Early-Qīng Hànlín literary circle.** [[KR4f0012]] / [[KR4f0013]] *Méicūn jí* / *Méicūn jiā-zàng-gǎo* 梅村集 / 梅村家藏藁 of [[吳偉業]] Wú Wěiyè (Ming-loyalist who later took Qīng office); [[KR4f0019]] *Jīng huá lù* 精華錄 of [[王士禛]] Wáng Shìzhēn (founder of *Shén-yùn*); [[KR4f0020]] *Yáo-fēng wén-chāo* 堯峯文鈔 of [[汪琬]]; [[KR4f0022]] *Wǔ-tíng wén-biān* 午亭文編 of [[陳廷敬]]; [[KR4f0025]] *Pù-shū-tíng jí* 曝書亭集 of [[朱彝尊]] Zhū Yízūn (founder of the *Zhè-xī* *cí* school); [[KR4f0029]] *Wén-duān jí* 文端集 of [[張英]]; [[KR4f0030]] *Xī-hé jí* 西河集 of [[毛奇齡]] Máo Qílíng (the most prolific early-Qīng polemicist, 189 juan — the largest single-author *biéjí* in the division); [[KR4f0031]] *Chén Jiǎn-tǎo sì-liù* 陳檢討四六 and [[KR4f0032]] *Chén Jiā-líng wén-jí* 陳迦陵文集 of [[陳維崧]] (founder of the *Yáng-xiàn* *cí* school).

**Cheng-Zhu Lǐ-xué officials.** [[KR4f0014]] *Tāng-zǐ yí-shū* 湯子遺書 of [[湯斌]]; [[KR4f0015]] *Jiān-jì-táng wén-jí* 兼濟堂文集 of [[魏裔介]]; [[KR4f0039]] *Róng-cūn jí* 榕村集 of [[李光地]] (Kāngxī's principal Sòng-school adviser); [[KR4f0040]] *Sān-yú-táng wén-jí* 三魚堂文集 of [[陸隴其]]; [[KR4f0043]] *Èr-xī-táng wén-jí* 二希堂文集 of [[蔡世遠]]. The documentary record of the Qīng *Lǐ-xué* establishment that anchored the dynasty's Confucian-orthodox ideological program.

**Tóng-chéng prose tradition.** [[KR4f0045]] *Wàng-xī jí* 望溪集 of [[方苞]] Fāng Bāo (the founder) and [[KR4f0052]] *Xī-bào-xuān wénjí* 惜抱軒文集 of [[姚鼐]] Yáo Nài (the second-generation synthesizer of *yì-lǐ*, *kǎo-jù*, *cí-zhāng*) together define Tóng-chéng doctrine. Yáo's parallel anthology *Gǔwén-cí lèi-zuǎn* 古文辭類纂 — not in KR4f — became the standard composition textbook of late-imperial China and the Republican period.

**Mid-Qīng *Zhè-xī* lyric and Sòng-poetic line.** [[KR4f0044]] *Jìng-yè-táng shī-jí* 敬業堂詩集 of [[查慎行]] (the leading early-Qīng Sòng / Lù Yóu line poet); [[KR4f0049]] *Fán-xiè shān-fáng jí* 樊榭山房集 of [[厲鶚]] (third in the canonical *Zhè-xī* *cí* line after Zhū Yízūn, also the foundational mid-Qīng Sòng-poetry historian via his *Sòng shī jì shì* 宋詩紀事).

**The *kǎo-zhèng* generation.** [[KR4f0053]] *Dài Dōng-yuán jí* 戴東原集 of [[戴震]] (the central Qián-Jiā intellectual figure); [[KR4f0054]] *Jí-qí-tíng jí* 鮚埼亭集 and [[KR4f0055]] *Jí-qí-tíng shī-jí* 鮚埼亭詩集 of [[全祖望]] (the *Zhè-dōng* historiographer who continued Huáng Zōngxī's *Sòng-Yuán xué-àn*); [[KR4f0058]] *Bào-jīng-táng wén-jí* 抱經堂文集 of [[盧文弨]]; [[KR4f0059]] *Qián-yán-táng wén-jí* 潛研堂文集 of [[錢大昕]] (foremost historical philologist of the generation); [[KR4f0056]] *Hóng Běi-jiāng shī-wén-jí* 洪北江詩文集 of [[洪亮吉]]; [[KR4f0057]] *Sūn Yuān-rú shī-wén-jí* 孫淵如詩文集 of [[孫星衍]]; [[KR4f0060]] / [[KR4f0061]] *Shù-xué nèi-piān* 述學內篇 / *Róng-fǔ xiānshēng yí-shī* 容甫先生遺詩 of [[汪中]]; [[KR4f0050]] *Guǒ-táng jí* 果堂集 of [[沈彤]]; [[KR4f0062]] *Yán-jīng-shì yī-jí* 揅經室一集 of [[阮元]] (the institutional architect of late-Qiánlóng *kǎo-zhèng*, founder of the Gū-shān 詁經精舍 and Xué-hǎi-táng 學海堂 academies). This is the largest single cluster in the division and supplies the literary-personal complement to the technical *kǎo-zhèng* monographs scattered through KR1, KR2, and KR3.

**Cháng-zhōu / Yáng-hú school and late-Qīng *Gōng-yáng*.** [[KR4f0063]] *Dà-yún shān-fáng wén-gǎo* 大雲山房文稿 of [[惲敬]] (Yáng-hú co-founder); [[KR4f0066]] *Míng-kē wén chū-biān* 茗柯文初編 and [[KR4f0067]] *Míng-kē wén bǔ-biān* 茗柯文補編 of [[張惠言]] (Yáng-hú co-founder, *Yú-shì Yì* 虞氏易 specialist, founder of the Cháng-zhōu *cí* school via the *Cí xuǎn xù* 詞選序); [[KR4f0064]] *Dìng-ān wén-jí* 定盦文集 and [[KR4f0065]] *Dìng-ān wén-jí bǔ-biān* 定盦文集補編 of [[龔自珍]] (the late-Cháng-zhōu *Gōng-yáng* prophet of Qīng decline; maternal grandson of [[段玉裁]], student of Liú Féng-lù 劉逢祿).

**Late-Qīng restoration.** [[KR4f0068]] *Zēng Wén-zhèng-gōng shī-jí* 曾文正公詩集 of [[曾國藩]] (Tóng-zhì restoration statesman, suppressor of the Tài-píng, principal Self-Strengthening leader, posthumously honored *Wén-zhèng* 文正).

## Important persons

- [[聖祖玄燁]] (1654–1722) — the Kāngxī emperor; his *yùzhì wénjí* is the documentary heart of early-high-Qīng imperial-literary culture and supplies the imperial side of debates carried elsewhere by his senior officials.
- [[顧炎武]] (1613–1682) — founder of Qīng *kǎo-zhèng*; his poetry in KR4f supplies the early-Qīng *yímín* lyric tradition.
- [[黃宗羲]] (1610–1695) — founder of the *Zhè-dōng* historical school; his prose carries the political-philosophical kernels later elaborated in the *Míng yí dài fǎng lù*.
- [[王夫之]] (1619–1692) — third of the three great early-Qīng Confucians; *yímín* poet-philosopher of Héng-yáng.
- [[錢謙益]] (1582–1664) — late-Míng grand secretary turned Qīng official; his *Mù-zhāi* collections and his preface to [[王士禛]]'s *Jīng huá lù* set the late-seventeenth-century poetic field.
- [[吳偉業]] (1609–1672) — Méicūn; the *yímín*-but-served *qīliù tǐ* 七律 (seven-character regulated verse) master of the early Qīng.
- [[王士禛]] (1634–1711) — founder of the *Shén-yùn* 神韻 poetic school and the dominant Kāngxī-era poet.
- [[朱彝尊]] (1629–1709) — founder of the *Zhè-xī* *cí* school; one of the great early-Qīng erudite poet-philologists.
- [[毛奇齡]] (1623–1716) — the most prolific and polemical early-Qīng Classics commentator; his *Xī-hé jí* is the largest *biéjí* in KR4f.
- [[陳維崧]] (1625–1682) — founder of the *Yáng-xiàn* *cí* school; one of the early-Qīng *cí* triumvirate with Zhū Yízūn and Nà-lán Xìng-dé.
- [[李光地]] (1642–1718) — Kāngxī's chief Cheng-Zhu *Lǐ-xué* adviser; principal editor of the imperially-commissioned *Zhū-zǐ quán-shū* and *Xìng-lǐ jīng-yì*.
- [[查慎行]] (1650–1727) — leading early-Qīng exponent of the Sòng / Lù Yóu poetic line.
- [[方苞]] (1668–1749) — founder of the Tóng-chéng *gǔwén* school; the most influential mid-Qīng prose theorist.
- [[厲鶚]] (1692–1752) — leading mid-Qiánlóng *Zhè-xī* *cí*-lyricist and Sòng-poetry historian.
- [[姚鼐]] (1732–1815) — second-generation Tóng-chéng master; the *Gǔwén-cí lèi-zuǎn* anthology defined late-imperial prose pedagogy.
- [[戴震]] (1724–1777) — the central intellectual figure of Qián-Jiā *kǎo-zhèng*; his presence in KR4f is via the *Dài Dōng-yuán jí*'s literary-philosophical essays.
- [[錢大昕]] (1728–1804) — foremost historical philologist of the Qián-Jiā generation; the *Qián-yán-táng wén-jí* is the literary complement to his *Èr-shí-èr-shǐ kǎo-yì* and other technical monographs.
- [[全祖望]] (1705–1755) — *Zhè-dōng* historiographer; continuator of Huáng Zōngxī's *Sòng-Yuán xué-àn*.
- [[阮元]] (1764–1849) — principal institutional patron and synthesizer of late-Qiánlóng to Dàoguāng *kǎo-zhèng*; founder of the Gū-shān and Xué-hǎi-táng academies.
- [[張惠言]] (1761–1802) — Cháng-zhōu polymath: *Yú-shì Yì* specialist, Yáng-hú prose co-founder, Cháng-zhōu *cí* school founder.
- [[龔自珍]] (1792–1841) — the late-Cháng-zhōu *Gōng-yáng* New-Text prophet; his prose articulates the late-Qīng diagnosis of dynastic decline and supplies the rhetorical model for the Hundred-Days Reform generation.
- [[曾國藩]] (1811–1872) — Tóng-zhì restoration statesman; his *biéjí* closes the division at the late-nineteenth-century turning point.

## Topics

- **The *Sìkù* / SBCK divide.** The Qīng *biéjí* corpus is uniquely shaped by two successive curatorial filters separated by 130 years. The *Sìkù quánshū* (1773–1782) excluded Ming-loyalist authors on political grounds; the *Sìbù cóngkān* (1919–1936) restored these and added important later figures whose collections circulated only in family imprints. The textual provenance of each KR4f entry (WYG vs. SBCK) is therefore the key bibliographic signal of its political-and-canonical status.

- **The *Shén-yùn* / Sòng-poetic divide.** [[王士禛]]'s *Shén-yùn* school of poetic indirection — derived from Wáng Wéi and Yán Yǔ's *Cāng-làng shī-huà* — dominated Kāngxī-Yōngzhèng critical taste; the principal counter-tradition was the Sòng-poetic line (Lù Yóu, Yuán Hào-wèn, Chén Shī-dào), represented by [[查慎行]] and by the *Zhè-xī* *cí*-and-Sòng-poetics school of [[朱彝尊]] and [[厲鶚]]. The *Sìkù* programmatic preference for *Shén-yùn* shaped the inclusion-and-evaluation language of many tíyào.

- **The Tóng-chéng / Yáng-hú dispute.** The Tóng-chéng program of [[方苞]] and [[姚鼐]] sought to unite *yì-lǐ* (Sòng moral metaphysics), *kǎo-jù* (Hàn-Qīng evidential philology), and *cí-zhāng* (literary craft) under a single *gǔwén* method. The Yáng-hú offshoot of [[張惠言]] and [[惲敬]] broke partly with this synthesis, accommodating *pián* parallel-prose and Cháng-zhōu *kǎo-zhèng* in a more eclectic mode (Wilkinson, Box 173). The dispute structures the entire second half of the division.

- **The *Cháng-zhōu cí* / *Zhè-xī cí* succession.** The early-Qīng *cí* revival had two principal regional traditions: the *Yáng-xiàn* of [[陳維崧]] and the *Zhè-xī* of [[朱彝尊]]. The *Zhè-xī* tradition descended through [[厲鶚]] to the early-Jiāqìng generation, where [[張惠言]]'s *Cí xuǎn xù* (preserved in [[KR4f0066]] *Míng-kē wén chū-biān*) inaugurated a new *Cháng-zhōu* program emphasizing allegorical depth — the dominant late-imperial *cí* doctrine through 1900.

- **The Qián-Jiā *kǎo-zhèng* personal record.** The mid- to late-Qīng *biéjí* serve as the personal-biographical apparatus of the evidential-research movement. Prefaces, friendship-letters, *bēi-zhì* 碑誌 epitaphs, *zhuàn* 傳 biographies, and *jì* 記 records here document the social networks among [[戴震]], [[錢大昕]], [[盧文弨]], [[孫星衍]], [[洪亮吉]], [[汪中]], [[段玉裁]], and [[阮元]] — while their technical *kǎo-zhèng* monographs are catalogued separately across KR1, KR2, and KR3.

- **The late-Qīng *Gōng-yáng* and dynastic-decline diagnosis.** From Liú Féng-lù through [[張惠言]] to [[龔自珍]], the Cháng-zhōu *Gōng-yáng* New-Text Confucian tradition supplied late-Qīng intellectuals with a critical idiom for diagnosing dynastic decline — the *sān shì* 三世 (three-age) and *yī tǒng* 一統 (unified-rule) doctrines, recast as periodization of Qīng crisis. The vocabulary descends to Kāng Yǒu-wéi's *Dà-tóng shū* and Liáng Qǐ-chāo's late-Qīng historiography. [[KR4f0064]] *Dìng-ān wén-jí* is the principal documentary node for this transition.

- **The Tài-píng / Tóng-zhì restoration filter.** Many late-Qīng *biéjí* in KR4f (the 1869 reprint of [[KR4f0066]] *Míng-kē wén* with [[曾國藩]]'s preface; the 1860s Wú Xù imprint of [[KR4f0064]] *Dìng-ān wén-jí*) are explicitly products of the post-Tài-píng restoration program — the Tóng-zhì-era project to recover the Jiāng-nán literary record after the destruction of 1850–1864. The prefaces of these editions are themselves a documentary record of the restoration.
