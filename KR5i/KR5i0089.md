# Guān shèng dì jūn běn zhuàn 關聖帝君本傳

*Original Biography of the Holy-Imperial Lord Guān*

biography of [[關帝]] (Guān shèng dì-jūn = Guān Yǔ); compiled by **崇德弟子** (Chóng-dé dì-zǐ — anonymous disciple), labelled "輯全書" (edited from a comprehensive book) in the source-line

The principal Guān-dì biographical narrative in the Lǚ-zǔ-circle Daoist register, complementary to the *Sān jiè fú mó* scripture at [[KR5i0088]]. The biography opens with the historical sketch from the *San guó zhì*: Guān Yǔ (*zì* Yún-cháng), of *Hé-dōng Jiě-liáng Bǎo-chí Xià-lǐ* *Féng* village (Shān-xī), descendant of the Xià dynasty *Guān Lóng-féng*; his early association with Liú Bèi 劉備 and Zhāng Fēi 張飛 (*Huán-hóu* 桓侯 here means *Hóng-dé Zhāng Fēi*); their bonds of brotherhood; the campaigns under various warlords; the famous incident of Guān Yǔ's "three conditions" with Cáo Cāo (1. surrender to Hàn but not to Cáo; 2. provide for the two ladies — Liú Bèi's wives; 3. depart instantly when Liú Bèi's whereabouts are known) — preserving Guān's loyalty intact through service under Cáo. The biography continues through Guān's death at Mài-chéng (220) and proceeds to the post-mortem cult-history.

### Prefaces

No external preface. The compiler is signed "崇德弟子" without further identification, and the credit "輯全書" indicates extraction from a more extensive source.

### Abstract

The Guān-dì biographical complement to the **Sān jiè fú mó** scripture, edited within the Lǚ-zǔ-circle planchette tradition. Composition c. 1700–1809; the work follows the Sòng-Yuán hagiographic tradition of the cult, with elements drawn from the *Sān guó zhì*'s *Guān Yǔ zhuàn* (which the biography reproduces almost verbatim) supplemented by post-mortem cult-history. The DZJY recension preserves the standard late-imperial form.

For the broader Guān-dì cult see [[關帝]] and ter Haar, *Guan Yu* (2017).
