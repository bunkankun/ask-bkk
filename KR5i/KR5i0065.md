# Jīn yè huán dān yìn zhèng tú shī 金液還丹印證圖詩

*Diagrammed and Versified Verifications of the Golden-Liquid Returning Elixir*

base teaching transmitted by [[白玉蟾]] (Bái Yù-chán, 1194–1229); narrated and versified by [[彭耜]] (Péng Sì, *hào* 龍眉子 Lóng-méi-zǐ); annotated by **涵蟾子** (Hán-chán-zǐ); dated **宋嘉定戊寅 = 1218** (Sòng Jiā-dìng 11)

A 20-chapter Southern-Sòng *nèi-dān* manual organised as a series of diagrams and verses (*tú shī* 圖詩) — nine chapters on the *outer* aspect (*wài fǎ xiàng* 外法象) of elixir-making (本原 / 乾坤 / 鼎器 / 鉛汞 / 和合 / 真土 / 採取 / 制度 / 輔佐), nine chapters on the *inner* aspect (*nèi fǎ xiàng* 内法象) of nourishing the elixir (服丹 / 九鼎 / 進火 / 退火 / 抽添 / 沐浴 / 金液 / 抱元 / 朝元), with two further chapters of warning. The work is the principal didactic compendium of the Bái Yù-chán → Péng Sì transmission and uses graphic-and-versified pedagogy — characteristic of the late-Sòng Nán-zōng — to make the *jīn-dān* curriculum systematically accessible.

### Prefaces

**Self-preface (Lóng-méi-zǐ Péng Sì), dated *Sòng Jiā-dìng wù-yín* (= **mid-winter 1218**, *zhòng dōng yuán rì*).** "*Reverently to whittle off death and fix on life, the formula is most simple — at the jade-tablet finding-master and asking-the-Way, the heart only matches with the elixir-book — good-and-evil reside in the man, ascent-and-fall by oneself. — Looking at the *Comprehensive Immortal-Biographies*, I now know that since antiquity those who have *chōng-jǔ* (ascended-vertically) are over a hundred-thousand, those who have *bá-zhái* (whisked-the-house-with-them) are over eight-thousand — could they all be of different endowment? It is by virtue of strenuous-study that they so became. Like the Yellow Emperor born and divinely-numinous, that is by Heaven-grant; like Xǔ Jīng-yáng cultivating-and-being-fully-prepared, is that not by human-effort? — Must wait till the evil-deeds are extinguished and only then good-conditions arise; some by guarding-a-mountain-pass meet [a master], some passing-the-sea encounter [one], some cross-the-demon's-test and the heart grows the firmer, some traverse-the-test and the will retreats not. — When the heart is hard-and-bitter, the cultivation must be precise-and-undivided. Refining at *Hóng-dū* assembly's centre; living-secluded under *Tài-huá Sōng-shān*; or scattered in the red-dust *Lǐ Tuō*'s 800-year-life-style, *Ān-qī*'s 3000-year; or rising-up into the bright-day, marvellous as *Zǐ-jìn* riding the *luán*, *Qín-gāo* dragging the carp; or, weary of the world, *shī-jiě* (corpse-deliverance); or remaining in the world, leaving the form behind; or master-and-disciple all ascending; or grandfather-and-grandson all attaining; some attaining-with-difficulty and accomplishing-with-ease; some attaining-with-ease and accomplishing-with-difficulty — the records preserved in the records, falsehoods cannot. — I from infancy have been firmly devoted to *qīng-xū* (the pure-and-empty); broadly seeking, right-or-wrong I could not distinguish; through three reckoning-eras my heart was kept; my hundred masters' fingers count out — what I learnt was all wrong; only on encounter did I understand. — Knowing therefore that hard-to-meet matters cannot easily be done. — To *kuà-hè zhī yóu* (ride the crane-tour) one must borrow the *yāo qián* (waist-money) for help. The lower-grade men hear and laugh out loud; the upper-sage therefore does not speak. To miss by a hair is to err by a thousand miles. If the Heaven-mechanism is lightly leaked, the patriarch's punishment-extends-and-arrives; if the supreme-Way is not transmitted, oneself is reproached-largely. — Wishing to speak again I would stop; wishing to stop I cannot. To say my hiding-secret-in-the-mystery-subtle would not be as good as to lay-out my outline. Therefore I narrate the master's pointing-and-instruct, and have made the diagrammed-chapters: nine chapters of outer *fǎ-xiàng* to exhaust the precision of *making* the elixir; nine chapters of inner *fǎ-xiàng* to article-out the detail of *nourishing* the elixir.* — *Sòng Jiā-dìng wù-yín mid-winter, original-day, Lóng-méi-zǐ inscribed.*"

### Abstract

The principal didactic compendium of the late Southern-Sòng **Nán-zōng** Daoist inner-alchemy, transmitted from Bái Yù-chán to his disciple Péng Sì (*hào* Lóng-méi-zǐ), authored by Péng on the basis of his master's oral instruction and dated 1218 (Sòng Jiā-dìng 11). The work is one of the most well-organised exposition-handbooks of the Nán-zōng tradition, with its 9 + 9 + 2 division providing a complete graphical-and-versified curriculum — and an *ad-hoc* commentary added later by **涵蟾子** (Hán-chán-zǐ — most likely a Yuán-Míng-era commentator). The DZJY recension is the basis for the ongoing transmission.

For the broader Bái Yù-chán → Péng Sì lineage see [[白玉蟾]] and [[彭耜]].
