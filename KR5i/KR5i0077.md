# Sān bǎo wàn líng fǎ chàn 三寶萬靈法懺

*Repentance-Method to the Three Treasures and the Myriad Numina*

reviewed (閱) by **下元道士王守上** (the *Xià-yuán* Daoist priest Wáng Shǒu-shàng)

A three-juàn liturgical repentance text, an item from the *Chàn fǎ dà guān* family ([[KR5i0076]]) extracted for separate printing. The text addresses the **Three Treasures** (三寶 = the *Sān-qīng* / Three Pure Ones — Yù-qīng, Shàng-qīng, Tài-qīng — and their respective heavenly-worthy retinues) and the **Myriad Numina** (萬靈 = the universal Daoist pantheon down to the local deities). The format is the standard Daoist *kē-yí* (ritual sequence): purification, *jìng kǒu* (mouth-purifying), *kāi chàn xiāng wén* (incense-text for opening the repentance), *bǎo gào* (treasure proclamations), the chants proper, *sān bǎo gào* (proclamation to the Three Treasures), and the closing dispatch.

### Prefaces

The text bears no separate preface; the *kāi chàn xiāng wén* (incense-text for opening the repentance) at the head opens directly with the Daoist's request to the spirits.

### Abstract

A standard Daoist liturgical *fǎ chàn* (repentance liturgy), reviewed for the 1809 DZJY by *Wáng Shǒu-shàng* under his title *Xià-yuán dào-shì* — the *Xià-yuán* (15th of 10th lunar month) being one of the three *Yuán* festivals. Composition of the text is c. 1700–1809; reception in the DZJY signals its acceptance into the planchette-circle's standard liturgical repertoire. The text is liturgically continuous with the [[KR5i0076]] *Chàn fǎ dà guān* tradition.
