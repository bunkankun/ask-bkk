# Cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù 參同契分章註

*Sectioned-by-Chapter Annotation of the Cān-tóng-qì*

by [[陳致虛]] (Chén Zhì-xū, *hào* 上陽子 Shàng-yáng-zǐ, *Lú-líng shān-rén* 廬陵山人; fl. 1289–1335)

The classical Yuán-era *Cān tóng qì* commentary by **Chén Zhì-xū**, with the canonical text re-divided into 35 named chapters (上篇 15 章, 中篇 15 章, 下篇 5 章) — the standard chapter-division for almost all subsequent recensions. Chén Zhì-xū is the principal northern-southern-fusion commentator of the late Yuán; his *Cān tóng qì zhù* (preserved as DZ 1006 *Zhōu yì cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù*) is one of the most influential pre-Míng readings of the scripture. The DZJY recension here re-prints the canonical 35-chapter text, each chapter followed by Chén's running commentary in *Shàng-yáng-zǐ yuē* 上陽子曰 form. The commentary is essentially complete textually but is here printed without separate preface or postface (the original Yuán self-preface preserved at DZ 1006 1.1a is omitted in DZJY). The chapter-titles — *Dà yì zǒng xù*, *Qián kūn shè wèi*, *Rì yuè xuán xiàng*, *Shèng rén shàng guān*, etc. — are Chén's own classification of the *Cān tóng qì* base text.

### Prefaces

No preface in the DZJY recension. The base commentary is dated by external evidence to 1335 (preserved in the Yuán *Dào shū yuán shén qì* compendium and elsewhere; cf. Boltz, *Survey*).

### Abstract

Chén Zhì-xū's *Cān tóng qì fēn zhāng zhù* is the standard Yuán recension of the alchemical canon, dividing the previously continuous text into 35 chapters with topical titles, a division that became normative in subsequent commentary. Chén is the principal Yuán *Quán-zhēn* / *Nán zōng* fusion master, integrating Wáng Chóng-yáng's northern Quán-zhēn lineage with Bái Yù-chán's southern *Nán zōng* alchemical theory. His broader project of *Cān tóng qì* / *Wù zhēn piān* harmonisation set the framework for all subsequent late-imperial inner-alchemy reception. For his life and broader work see [[陳致虛]] and Boltz, *Survey*.

The **DZJY recension** of this commentary appears in juxtaposition with [[KR5i0037]] Zhū Yuán-yù's *Chǎn yōu* — the two together provide the editorial pair Yuán + early-Qīng on the *Cān tóng qì* in DZJY.
