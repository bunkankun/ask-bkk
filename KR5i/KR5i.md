## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR5i collects the **Dàozàng jíyào** 道藏輯要, the principal Qīng-era abridgment-and-expansion of the Daoist Canon, in two superimposed strata: the **原版道藏輯要** (Yuán-bǎn DZJY, base-edition tag `YPP`), compiled by **Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ** 蔣予蒲 (= Jiǎng Yuán-tíng 蔣元庭, 1755–1819) and printed in Beijing in 1806/1809; and the **重刊道藏輯要** (Chóng-kān DZJY, base-edition tag `CK-KZ`), the substantially enlarged re-engraving completed at the **Èr-xiān-ān** 二仙菴 and **Qīng-yáng-gōng** 青羊宮 of Chéng-dū in 1906 under the abbacy of **Hé Lóng-xiāng** 賀龍驤. The Kanripo recension preserves all 109 works in DZJY's final received state, with the YPP / CK-KZ stratification visible in each per-text frontmatter. The division has no WYG (Wényuàn-gé Sìkù) counterpart: the Sìkù compilers excluded the Daoist Canon and its supplements from the 子部, so DZJY's entry into Kanripo is via its own block-print line, not via any imperial bibliographic sanction (cf. the parallel situation for [[KR5h]] *Xù Dàozàng*).

DZJY differs from the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* ([[KR5a]]–[[KR5g]]) and its Wànlì supplement ([[KR5h]]) in three editorial respects that shape the whole division. First, the selection is decisively **Lǚ-zǔ-centric and Quán-zhēn / Lóng-mén in orientation**: a large bloc of the texts are Lǚ Dòng-bīn 呂洞賓 *fú-jī* 扶乩 (planchette) productions ([[KR5i0049]], [[KR5i0050]], [[KR5i0051]], [[KR5i0053]], [[KR5i0054]], [[KR5i0055]], [[KR5i0047]], [[KR5i0048]], [[KR5i0045]]), and the institutional precept and liturgy corpus ([[KR5i0097]]–[[KR5i0099]], [[KR5i0090]]–[[KR5i0091]]) anchors the collection on the post-1656 Wáng Cháng-yuè revival at the Bái-yún-guàn 白雲觀. Second, **inner alchemy** (*nèi-dān* 內丹) is the dominant practice register — the *Cān-tóng-qì* and *Wù-zhēn-piān* lineages, the Wǔ-Liǔ pài 伍柳派 dossier, the women's-alchemy (*nǚ-dān*) corpus and the *Jīn-huá* / *Tài-yǐ jīn-huá zōng-zhǐ* tradition are all present in their late-imperial received form. Third, DZJY enacts an explicit **Three-Teachings synthesis** by canonizing several non-Daoist works — Zhōu Dūn-yí's 太極圖說 and 通書 with Zhū Xī's annotation ([[KR5i0079]]–[[KR5i0080]]), Shào Yōng's 皇極經世書 and 擊壤集 ([[KR5i0081]]–[[KR5i0082]]), and Zhū-gě Liàng's collected works ([[KR5i0078]]) — alongside Buddhist-register expositions like Wǔ Shǒu-yáng's *Xiān fó hé zōng* ([[KR5i0066]]). The geographic centre of gravity is **Sì-chuān**: the texts emerge from, or were re-canonized through, the Chéng-dū Lóng-mén network around the Èr-xiān-ān and the Sì-chuān Lǚ-zǔ planchette altars, making DZJY the principal documentary witness to nineteenth-century West-China Daoist institutional life.

## Important texts and text clusters

- **The Lǚ-zǔ planchette dossier (*Chún-yáng* corpus).** The single largest cluster in the division: *Lǚ-zǔ běn-zhuàn* 呂祖本傳 [[KR5i0045]], *Lǚ-dì wén-jí* 呂帝文集 [[KR5i0049]], *Lǚ-dì shī-jí* 呂帝詩集 [[KR5i0050]], *Chún-yáng sān-shū* 純陽三書 [[KR5i0051]], *Yǔ-lù dà-guān* 語錄大觀 [[KR5i0053]], *Wēi-yán zhāi-yào* 微言摘要 [[KR5i0054]], *Lǚ-dì shèng-jì jì-yào* 呂帝聖蹟紀要 [[KR5i0055]], *Tóng-cān jīng* 同參經 [[KR5i0047]], and *Wǔ-jīng hé-biān* 五經合編 [[KR5i0048]]. Together this bloc constitutes the **canonical anthology of the Lǚ-zǔ cult as it operated through Qīng planchette altars** — Liǔ Shǒu-yuán 柳守元 is the recurring editorial hand, and the corpus defines DZJY's editorial centre of gravity.
- **The Wǔ-Liǔ pài 伍柳派 inner-alchemy core.** *Tiān-xiān zhèng-lǐ zhí-lùn* 天仙正理直論 [[KR5i0067]] and its *Qiǎn-shuō* abridgement [[KR5i0068]], *Xiān-fó hé-zōng yǔ-lù* 仙佛合宗語錄 [[KR5i0066]], *Wǔ-zhēn-rén dān-dào jiǔ-piān* 伍真人丹道九篇 [[KR5i0070]], and *Jīn-dān yào-jué* 金丹要訣 [[KR5i0069]] — the foundational dossier of the **Wǔ Shǒu-yáng** 伍守陽 (c. 1574–1644) → Liǔ Huá-yáng 柳華陽 inner-alchemical lineage, which became the most widely-printed and most-translated late-imperial *nèi-dān* tradition (Cleary's *Daoist Yoga* renders its derivative form).
- **The *Cān-tóng-qì* / *Wù-zhēn-piān* commentarial tradition.** *Cān-tóng-qì chǎn-yōu* 參同契闡幽 [[KR5i0037]] and *Wù-zhēn-piān chǎn-yōu* 悟真篇闡幽 [[KR5i0059]], the paired commentaries of **Zhū Yuán-yù** 朱元育 (Kāng-xī era); *Cān-tóng-qì fēn-zhāng-zhù* 參同契分章註 [[KR5i0038]] by the Yuán master **Chén Zhì-xū** 陳致虛; the foundational *Jīn-dān sì-bǎi-zì* 金丹四百字 [[KR5i0058]] of **Zhāng Bó-duān** 張伯端 (c. 1075–1085); *Wǔ-piān líng-wén* 五篇靈文 [[KR5i0062]] (the Jīn-era short scripture of the Quán-zhēn founders' alchemical insight); and the Sòng *Rù-yào-jìng* 入藥鏡 [[KR5i0039]] of [[崔希範]] — collectively the classical inner-alchemical curriculum of late-imperial Daoism.
- **The *Jīn-huá* / *Golden Flower* and Qīng-huá-shàng-dì planchette compendia.** *Jīn-huá zōng-zhǐ* 金華宗旨 [[KR5i0046]] (the DZJY recension of the work that reached Europe in 1929 as Richard Wilhelm's *Das Geheimnis der Goldenen Blüte*); *Tiān-xiān jīn-dān xīn-fǎ* 天仙金丹心法 [[KR5i0056]]; *Chàng-dào zhēn-yán* 唱道真言 [[KR5i0030]] (the great Yōng-zhèng-era *Qīng-huá-shàng-dì* planchette compendium, datable to 3 May 1729); *Yǎng-zhēn jí* 養真集 [[KR5i0072]], *Yù-quán* 玉詮 [[KR5i0073]], and *Zhēn-quán* 真詮 [[KR5i0074]] — the late-imperial cultivation digests.
- **The women's alchemy (*nǚ-dān* 女丹) corpus.** *Sūn Bù-èr yuán-jūn fǎ-yǔ* 孫不二元君法語 [[KR5i0063]], *Sūn Bù-èr yuán-jūn chuán-shù dān-dào mì-shū* 孫不二元君傳述丹道祕書 [[KR5i0064]], and the late-Qīng *Nǚ-dān hé-biān* 女丹合編 [[KR5i0109]] — the principal vehicle of female inner-alchemy in the post-1700 Daoist tradition.
- **The Quán-zhēn / Lóng-mén institutional dossier.** The three-altar ordination block: *Sān-tán yuán-mǎn tiān-xiān dà-jiè lüè-shuō* 三壇圓滿天仙大戒略說 [[KR5i0097]], *Chū-zhēn jiè-lǜ* 初真戒律 [[KR5i0098]] (transmitted by **Wáng Cháng-yuè** 王常月 at the Bái-yún-guàn on 31 March 1656 — the founding moment of the Qīng Quán-zhēn institutional revival), and *Zhōng-jí jiè* 中極戒 [[KR5i0099]]. Paired daily-liturgy manuals: *Qīng-wēi hóng-fàn dào-mén gōng-kè* 清微宏範道門功課 [[KR5i0090]] and *Tài-shàng xuán-mén gōng-kè jīng* 太上玄門功課經 [[KR5i0091]]. Sacred-geography documentation of the DZJY's own institutional home: *Èr-xiān-ān bēi-jì* 二仙菴碑記 [[KR5i0100]], *Qīng-yáng-gōng bēi-jì* 青羊宮碑記 [[KR5i0101]], and *Qīng-chéng-shān jì* 青城山記 [[KR5i0103]].
- **The high-canonical scripture set.** *Yuán-shǐ dà-dòng yù-jīng* 元始大洞玉經 [[KR5i0001]] and its 1709 expanded recension [[KR5i0003]] (Shàng-qīng 大洞 tradition); *Gāo-shàng yù-huáng běn-xíng jí-jīng* 高上玉皇本行集經 [[KR5i0015]] with its 1809 Jiǎng-circle commentary [[KR5i0016]] (Jade-Emperor scripture); the cluster of *Gāo-shàng yù-huáng xīn-yìn* recensions [[KR5i0017]]–[[KR5i0020]]; the *Huáng-tíng* 黃庭 inner / outer scriptures [[KR5i0010]]–[[KR5i0012]]; and the *Yù-shū bǎo-jīng* 玉樞寶經 [[KR5i0035]] of the Sòng Shén-xiāo 神霄 thunder-rite tradition.
- **The *Tài-shàng gǎn-yìng piān* moral-merit anchor.** *Tài-shàng gǎn-yìng piān jiān-zhù* 太上感應篇箋注 [[KR5i0013]] and *Tài-shàng gǎn-yìng piān jí-zhù* 太上感應篇集注 [[KR5i0014]] — the two principal Qīng commentaries on the most-printed *shàn-shū* 善書 of late-imperial China, accompanied by *Yīn-zhì-wén zhù* 陰騭文註 [[KR5i0087]] and the merit-ledger pair *Shí-jiè gōng-guò gé* 十戒功過格 [[KR5i0095]] and *Jǐng-shì gōng-guò gé* 警世功過格 [[KR5i0096]].
- **The Wén-chāng cult dossier.** *Wén-chāng dì-jūn běn-zhuàn* 文昌帝君本傳 [[KR5i0083]], *Wén-dì xiào-jīng* 文帝孝經 [[KR5i0084]] (with Qiū Jùn's 1492 preface), *Wén-chāng yán-sì jīng* 文昌延嗣經 [[KR5i0086]] (dated Yōng-zhèng 12 = 1734), and the *Yù-qīng zàn-huà jiǔ-tiān yǎn-zhèng xīn-yìn* pair [[KR5i0021]]–[[KR5i0022]] — DZJY's witness to the late-imperial literature-god cult that mediated Daoism to the *shì-dà-fū* class.
- **The Guān-dì and Dòu-mǔ cults.** *Sān-jiè fú-mó Guān-shèng dì-jūn zhōng-xiào zhōng-yì zhēn-jīng* 三界伏魔關聖帝君忠孝忠義真經 [[KR5i0088]] and *Guān-shèng dì-jūn běn-zhuàn* 關聖帝君本傳 [[KR5i0089]] for the Guān-Yǔ cult; the Nine-August / Dòu-mǔ cluster [[KR5i0023]]–[[KR5i0026]].
- **The *Yīn-fú jīng* commentarial cluster.** The base text in two recensions [[KR5i0031]]–[[KR5i0032]]; the precisely-dated 1772 *Yīn-fú xuán-jiě* 陰符玄解 [[KR5i0033]] of **Fàn Yí-bīn** 范宜賓; and the *Tài-gōng Yīn-fú jīng* 太公陰符經 attribution [[KR5i0061]] — together documenting the eighteenth-century Liáo-níng / Sì-chuān Lóng-mén reading of the canonical *Yīn-fú*.
- **The Three-Teachings inclusions.** *Tài-jí tú-shuō* 太極圖說 [[KR5i0079]] and *Tōng-shū* 通書 [[KR5i0080]] of **Zhōu Dūn-yí** 周敦頤 (Sòng, with Zhū Xī's annotation); *Huáng-jí jīng-shì-shū* 皇極經世書 [[KR5i0081]] and *Jī-rǎng jí* 擊壤集 [[KR5i0082]] of **Shào Yōng** 邵雍 (Sòng); and *Hàn chéng-xiàng Zhū-gě Zhōng-wǔ-hóu jí* 漢丞相諸葛忠武侯集 [[KR5i0078]] of **Zhū-gě Liàng** 諸葛亮 (compiled 1632 by his 36th-generation descendant Zhū-gě Xī 諸葛羲). Together they enact DZJY's editorial commitment to *sān-jiào hé-yī* by canonizing Sòng *Lǐ-xué* and Hàn loyalty-and-martial figures *as Daoist material*.
- **The Lǎo-Zhuāng commentaries.** *Tài-shàng xuán-yuán Dào-dé jīng jiě* 太上玄元道德經解 [[KR5i0005]] (Lǚ-zǔ planchette commentary); *Tài-shàng Dào-dé bǎo-zhāng yì* 太上道德寶章翼 [[KR5i0006]] (Chéng Yǐ-níng 程以甯, paired with [[KR5i0036]]); *Dào-dé jīng shì-cí* 道德經釋辭 [[KR5i0007]] (Wáng Yī-qīng 王一清, 1592–1597, late-Míng Wǔ-dāng-shān milieu); and *Nán-huá zhēn-jīng zhù-shū* 南華真經註疏 [[KR5i0036]] (Chéng Yǐ-níng, *guǐ-yǒu* spring 1693). The latter is the high-water mark of inner-alchemical *Zhuāng-zǐ* hermeneutics in DZJY.
- **The Zhāng Sān-fēng compendium.** *Zhāng Sān-fēng xiān-shēng quán-jí* 張三丰先生全集 [[KR5i0071]] — the standard late-imperial collected works of the legendary Wǔ-dāng-shān patriarch, edited by Wāng Xī-líng 汪錫齡 (1723).
- **The Five-Dynasties and Sòng inclusions.** *Huà-shū* 化書 [[KR5i0042]] of **Tán Qiào** 譚峭 in its second canonical recension (cf. the *Xù Dàozàng* recension at [[KR5h0047]]); *Qióng-guǎn Bái zhēn-rén jí* 瓊琯白真人集 [[KR5i0060]] (the 1594 collected works of the Southern-Sòng *Nán-zōng* 南宗 patriarch **Bái Yùchán** 白玉蟾); and *Jīn-bì gǔ-wén Lóng-hǔ shàng-jīng* 金碧古文龍虎上經 [[KR5i0029]] in **Péng Hào-gǔ's** 彭好古 1599 critical recension.
- **The Jìng-míng dossier.** *Tài-shàng líng-bǎo jìng-míng zōng-jiào lù* 太上靈寶淨明宗教錄 [[KR5i0041]] — the Qīng-recension institutional record of the Xǔ Xùn-centred Jìng-míng 淨明 tradition out of Jiāng-xī.
- **The repentance and *zhāi-jiào* 齋醮 liturgical bloc.** *Chàn-fǎ dà-guān* 懺法大觀 [[KR5i0076]] (32-part repentance compendium), *Sān-bǎo wàn-líng fǎ-chàn* 三寶萬靈法懺 [[KR5i0077]], *Tài-shàng wú-jí dà-dào yán-shòu jí-fú xiāo-jié bǎo-chàn* [[KR5i0104]], and the *Tài-shàng wú-jí dà-dào sān-shí-liù-bù zūn-jīng* opening / commentary pair [[KR5i0107]]–[[KR5i0108]] — DZJY's witness to the Qīng performative-liturgical tradition out of which the *Èr-xiān-ān* engraving house operated. The hungry-ghost-feeding rite is in *Qīng-xuán jì-liàn tiě-guàn hú-shí* 青玄濟煉鐵罐斛食 [[KR5i0106]].

## Important persons

- [[蔣予蒲]] (Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ, *zì* Jiǎng Yuán-tíng 元庭, 1755–1819) — Qīng *jìnshì* and high official; **compiler of the original 1806/1809 *Dào-zàng jí-yào*** in Beijing, and the editor whose Lǚ-zǔ-circle affiliations define the division's selection principle. Cf. Mori Yuria, "*Daozang jiyao* and Quanzhen Daoism in the Qing Dynasty."
- [[賀龍驤]] (Hé Lóng-xiāng) — Sì-chuān Lóng-mén abbot at the **Èr-xiān-ān** 二仙菴 in Chéng-dū; principal editor of the 1906 **重刊道藏輯要**, the substantially expanded re-engraving from which most CK-KZ-tagged texts derive.
- [[呂洞賓]] (Lǚ Dòng-bīn) — the **dominant attribution-figure of the whole division**: 25+ works carry his name as planchette-revealer, commentator, or biographical subject. DZJY is, in editorial substance, the canonical Qīng anthology of the Lǚ-zǔ cult.
- [[柳守元]] (Liǔ Shǒu-yuán) — recurring editorial / compilatorial hand across the Lǚ-zǔ planchette corpus; appears as compiler-preface-writer on six DZJY entries and is the principal organising figure of the *Chún-yáng* dossier.
- [[伍守陽]] (Wǔ Shǒu-yáng, c. 1574–1644) — **founder of the Wǔ-Liǔ pài** 伍柳派; principal author of [[KR5i0066]], [[KR5i0067]], [[KR5i0068]], [[KR5i0070]]. The Wǔ-Liǔ corpus is the most widely-circulated late-imperial inner-alchemical exposition.
- [[伍守虛]] (Wǔ Shǒu-xū) — Wǔ Shǒu-yáng's brother; collator and annotator across the Wǔ-Liǔ dossier.
- [[王常月]] (Wáng Cháng-yuè, 1622–1680) — **Lóng-mén institutional founder**; transmitted the *Chū-zhēn jiè-lǜ* [[KR5i0098]] at the Bái-yún-guàn on 31 March 1656, instituting the three-altar ordination system that organizes [[KR5i0097]]–[[KR5i0099]]. His preface is one of the foundational documents of Qīng Quán-zhēn institutional history.
- [[朱元育]] (Zhū Yuán-yù) — Kāng-xī-era inner-alchemical commentator; author of the paired *Cān-tóng-qì chǎn-yōu* [[KR5i0037]] and *Wù-zhēn-piān chǎn-yōu* [[KR5i0059]], the most-cited Qīng commentaries on the two foundational *nèi-dān* classics.
- [[程以甯]] (Chéng Yǐ-níng) — Kāng-xī-era *Lǎo-Zhuāng* commentator from the same milieu; author of [[KR5i0006]] *Dào-dé bǎo-zhāng yì* and [[KR5i0036]] *Nán-huá zhēn-jīng zhù-shū* (*guǐ-yǒu* spring 1693), the latter the principal Qīng *Zhuāng-zǐ* commentary in DZJY.
- [[王一清]] (Wáng Yī-qīng, *hào* Tǐ-wù-zǐ 體無子, *fl.* 1592–1597) — late-Wàn-lì Wǔ-dāng-shān commentator; author of *Dào-dé jīng shì-cí* [[KR5i0007]] and of the 1592 critical recension of *Huà-shū* [[KR5i0042]] that established the modern attribution to Tán Qiào.
- [[彭好古]] (Péng Hào-gǔ) — late-Wàn-lì Hú-běi inner-alchemy editor; 1599 critical recension of *Jīn-bì gǔ-wén Lóng-hǔ shàng-jīng* [[KR5i0029]] is the clearest specimen of late-Míng Hú-běi inner-alchemical exegesis in DZJY.
- [[範宜賓]] / [[范宜賓]] (Fàn Yí-bīn, *hào* Yī-zhōng-zǐ) — Shěn-yáng-based mid-Qián-lóng Lóng-mén figure; 5 July 1772 *Yīn-fú xuán-jiě* [[KR5i0033]] documents the eighteenth-century Liáo-níng / Sì-chuān Lóng-mén commentarial network.
- [[張清夜]] (Zhāng Qīng-yè, *hào* Zì-mù dào-rén 自牧道人) — early-Qīng Chéng-dū *Zǐ-yáng-dòng* 紫陽洞 abbot; author of *Yīn-fú fā-mì* (the antecedent to [[KR5i0033]]) and a foundational figure of the Sì-chuān Lóng-mén network that produced DZJY itself.
- [[張伯端]] (Zhāng Bó-duān, 987–1082) — founder of the Sòng Nán-zōng 南宗 inner-alchemical lineage; author of *Jīn-dān sì-bǎi-zì* [[KR5i0058]] and the underlying *Wù-zhēn piān* whose Qīng commentary forms [[KR5i0059]].
- [[白玉蟾]] (Bái Yùchán, *fl.* early 13th c.) — Southern-Sòng Nán-zōng fifth patriarch; *Qióng-guǎn Bái zhēn-rén jí* [[KR5i0060]] is his standard Míng-Qīng collected works.
- [[譚峭]] (Tán Qiào, *fl.* c. 880–950) — Five-Dynasties Daoist philosopher; *Huà-shū* [[KR5i0042]] in this division's recension.
- [[孫不二]] (Sūn Bù-èr, 1119–1182) — Quán-zhēn Seven-Perfected female patriarch; principal attribution-figure of the *nǚ-dān* tradition in [[KR5i0063]], [[KR5i0064]].
- [[周敦頤]] (Zhōu Dūn-yí, 1017–1073) — Sòng Neo-Confucian founder; included here for *Tài-jí tú-shuō* [[KR5i0079]] and *Tōng-shū* [[KR5i0080]] under the Three-Teachings synthesis.
- [[邵雍]] (Shào Yōng, 1011–1077) — Sòng cosmologist and image-numerologist; *Huáng-jí jīng-shì-shū* [[KR5i0081]] and *Jī-rǎng jí* [[KR5i0082]].
- [[朱熹]] (Zhū Xī, 1130–1200) — annotator of Zhōu Dūn-yí's two works as preserved in [[KR5i0079]]–[[KR5i0080]] (canonical *jí-zhù*).
- [[諸葛亮]] (Zhū-gě Liàng, 181–234) — Hàn-Sānguó statesman; corpus at [[KR5i0078]], included on the Daoist tradition's claim that he alone among Hàn figures grasped the *Yīn-fú jīng*.
- [[諸葛羲]] (Zhū-gě Xī) — Zhū-gě Liàng's 36th-generation descendant; 1632 compiler of the *Wǔ-hóu jí*.
- [[張三丰]] (Zhāng Sān-fēng) — legendary Wǔ-dāng-shān patriarch; subject and attributed author of the *Zhāng Sān-fēng xiān-shēng quán-jí* [[KR5i0071]].
- [[文昌帝君]] (Wén-chāng dì-jūn) — deified literature-god and principal *fú-jī* speaker of the Wén-chāng cult dossier ([[KR5i0021]], [[KR5i0083]]–[[KR5i0084]], [[KR5i0086]], [[KR5i0087]]); the matrix of late-imperial *shàn-shū* moral-merit literature.
- [[關帝]] (Guān-dì = Guān-yǔ 關羽) — Hàn martial-loyalty figure deified as principal warrior-deity of the late-imperial state cult; principal speaker in [[KR5i0088]]–[[KR5i0089]].
- [[蔣國祚]] (Jiǎng Guó-zuò) — Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ's elder kinsman; co-annotator of the *Gǎn-yìng-piān* commentary [[KR5i0013]].
- [[朱珪]] (Zhū Guī, 1731–1807) — Jiā-qìng-era Grand Secretary; Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ's principal political patron, named as 敬校 on [[KR5i0013]] and elsewhere — his patronage is the political circumstance under which the 1806 DZJY could be printed at all.

## Topics

- **Spirit-writing (*fú-jī* 扶乩 / *fù-luán* 扶鸞) as canonization mechanism.** A defining feature of DZJY: a large bloc of the "Qīng" texts are planchette-revealed scriptures whose attributed speakers are deified Táng-Sòng patriarchs (Lǚ Dòng-bīn, Xǔ Xùn, Zhāng Sān-fēng, Zhōng-lí Quán) speaking centuries after their natural lives. The mechanism is not occasional but the principal mode by which new scripture entered the late-imperial Daoist canon. The Wén-chāng-cult bloc [[KR5i0021]] and the *Chàng-dào zhēn-yán* [[KR5i0030]] are paradigm cases; the dating policy across the division — *terminus a quo* = consolidation of the planchette cult (post-1700), *terminus ad quem* = inclusion in DZJY (1809 or 1906) — directly reflects this.
- **The Lǚ-zǔ cult as editorial centre.** No other figure even approaches Lǚ Dòng-bīn's dominance across DZJY's frontmatter. The Lǚ-zǔ-circle planchette altars of late-imperial Sì-chuān, Jiāng-nán, and the capital are the editorial matrix of the entire division; reading DZJY without that frame collapses much of the editorial logic.
- **Inner alchemy (*nèi-dān* 內丹) as central practice.** Where the Míng *Zhèng-tǒng Dào-zàng* gave roughly equal weight to ritual / liturgy and to inner cultivation, DZJY tilts decisively toward inner alchemy. The *Cān-tóng-qì* / *Wù-zhēn-piān* commentarial tradition, the Wǔ-Liǔ corpus, the women's-alchemy materials, and the *Jīn-huá* / *Tài-yǐ jīn-huá zōng-zhǐ* compendia together form the doctrinal core, and the standard late-imperial *nèi-dān* curriculum *is* the DZJY *nèi-dān* curriculum.
- **Three-Teachings synthesis (*sān-jiào hé-yī* 三教合一).** The most-distinctive editorial commitment: the Sòng Neo-Confucian classics ([[KR5i0079]]–[[KR5i0082]]) are canonized *as Daoist material*, with Zhū Xī as their annotator; Wǔ Shǒu-yáng's [[KR5i0066]] uses Chán Buddhist *yuán-jué* as a paired absolute with Daoist *xiān-tiān yī-qì*; and Zhū-gě Liàng's loyalty-and-martial corpus [[KR5i0078]] is incorporated as Daoist ancestry. This is not an accident of bricolage but a programmatic claim.
- **The Quán-zhēn / Lóng-mén institutional turn and the 1656 Bái-yún-guàn revival.** The precept-and-liturgy bloc ([[KR5i0090]]–[[KR5i0091]], [[KR5i0097]]–[[KR5i0099]]) anchors the division to the post-Wáng-Cháng-yuè Lóng-mén institutional revival. Sī-chuān (Èr-xiān-ān, Qīng-yáng-gōng, Qīng-chéng-shān, Zǐ-yáng-dòng) is the geographic centre, and the local-stele texts [[KR5i0100]], [[KR5i0101]], [[KR5i0103]] make DZJY itself a documentary witness to its own institutional home.
- **The popular-cult pantheon at the doorstep of the literati.** Wén-chāng, Guān-dì, the Jade-Emperor / 玉皇 cult, Dòu-mǔ, and the *Tài-shàng gǎn-yìng piān* moral-merit tradition all appear in DZJY in their state-of-the-art Qīng forms. The division is therefore a primary source for the religious culture of late-imperial gentry life, not only for monastic Daoism.
- **Editorial layering: YPP (1806/1809) vs. CK-KZ (1906).** Every per-text frontmatter records the base-edition tag. Reading the division by edition reveals two editorial moments separated by a century: Jiǎng Yǔ-pǔ's Beijing-court-supported selection of 1806/1809, vs. Hé Lóng-xiāng's Chéng-dū institutional re-engraving of 1906 (which absorbs the supplement-track materials including [[KR5i0104]], [[KR5i0107]]–[[KR5i0109]]). Most analytic claims about DZJY must be relativised to which stratum a text belongs in.
- **Women's alchemy as a recognised sub-tradition.** The *Sūn Bù-èr* materials [[KR5i0063]]–[[KR5i0064]] and the late-Qīng *Nǚ-dān hé-biān* [[KR5i0109]] establish *nǚ-dān* as a self-aware, lineage-documented sub-tradition with its own boundary-policing against "*yīn-yáng* schools" of sexual yoga ([[KR5i0109]] *Páng-mén lù* polemic).
- **The Sì-chuān provenance of the received text.** The 1906 re-engraving was produced at Chéng-dū institutions, and several DZJY-only items (the local-stele texts; the Sì-chuān provincial-Daoist anthology [[KR5i0094]]) point unambiguously to a Sì-chuān editorial home. The Beijing 1806 stratum and the Chéng-dū 1906 stratum jointly define the geography of late-imperial Daoist institutional Daoism in the period that immediately preceded the early-Republican Daoist reform.
