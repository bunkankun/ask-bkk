# Sūn Bù-èr yuán jūn chuán shù dān dào mì shū sān juàn 孫不二元君傳述丹道祕書三卷

*The Three-Juàn Secret Book of the Elixir-Way as Transmitted-and-Narrated by the Primal Lady Sūn Bù-èr*

planchette-attributed to [[孫不二]] (Sūn Bù-èr) and to **紫光天母** (Zǐ-guāng tiān-mǔ, the Heavenly-Mother of Purple-Light)

A three-juàn pseudepigraphical Quán-zhēn / *nǚ-dān* compendium, transmitting under Sūn Bù-èr's name three distinct base scriptures: (i) **Yù qīng tāi yuán nèi yǎng zhēn jīng** 玉清胎元内養真經 (transmitted by *Bǎo shén yuán tái zhēn jūn* 寶神元臺真君); (ii) **Yù qīng wú shàng nèi jǐng zhēn jīng** 玉清無上内景真經 (transmitted by *Dà luó zhēn tiān yuán tiān dà shèng hòu Zǐ guāng tiān mǔ* 大羅真天元天大聖后紫光天母); (iii) a third revelation-scripture (the title less prominently displayed). The framing fiction is cosmological: in primordial time *Yuán shǐ shàng dì* 元始上帝 instructs the Tài-shàng Lǎo-jūn on the cosmology of *jīng-qì-shén* (essence-pneuma-spirit) and on the salvation of the lower realm; the Tài-shàng then transmits in turn to the Zǐ-guāng tiān-mǔ; and Zǐ-guāng tiān-mǔ in turn descends through Sūn Bù-èr into the world.

### Prefaces

No external preface; the text begins directly with the *Yù qīng tāi yuán nèi yǎng zhēn jīng* opening and proceeds through the three scriptures.

### Abstract

A pseudepigraphical Qīng-era Quán-zhēn / *nǚ-dān* compendium, paired with [[KR5i0063]] *Sūn Bù-èr yuán jūn fǎ yǔ* — the two together comprising the principal *Sūn Bù-èr* canonical pair. Composition is c. late Míng / early Qīng (1500–1750); the cosmology is that of the established Quán-zhēn pantheon as it stood in the post-*Yún jí qī qiān* period.

The framing scripture's hierarchy of revelation — Yuán-shǐ → Tài-shàng → Zǐ-guāng tiān-mǔ → Sūn Bù-èr — is a standard late-imperial cosmological pedigree-claim, embedding the *nǚ-dān* tradition in the highest planchette-pantheon authority chain.
