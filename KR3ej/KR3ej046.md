# Mázhěn chǎnzhù 麻疹闡註
*Elucidatory Commentary on Measles*
by [[張霞谿]] (撰); preface by [[徐漸逵]] (1848)

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* mid-Qīng commentary on the measles (*má-zhěn* 麻疹) chapter of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* 醫宗金鑑 by **Zhāng Xiá-xī** 張霞谿 (*míng* Lián 廉, *zì* Tōng-yuán 通源) of Zhū-jì 諸暨 (Zhèjiāng). The work was published with a preface by **Xú Jiàn-kuí** 徐漸逵 (*hào* Xù-zhāi 煦齋) at the Lǐ-chéng *shì-yù* 蠡城試寓 (Lǐ-chéng examination lodge) in *Dào-guāng* 28 *wù-shēn* 戊申 *mèng-xià* 1848 fourth lunar month — posthumous publication arranged by Zhāng's family (clan-nephew Jí-yuán 輯園, nephew Huì-pō 蕙坡, brother Zǐ-tíng 紫庭, clan-cousin Chóng-gāo 重皋) under the editorial supervision of Lì-zǐ Fēi-ān 酈子非葊. The work's title *chǎn-zhù* 闡註 (elucidatory annotation) signals its commentarial-pedagogical genre: it provides paragraph-by-paragraph annotation of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* measles chapter to render its compressed verse-mnemonic format accessible to clinical practitioners.

### Prefaces

**Preface** by **Xú Jiàn-kuí** 徐漸逵 (*hào* Xù-zhāi 煦齋) at the Lǐ-chéng shì-yù, *Dào-guāng* 28 *wù-shēn mèng-xià* (fourth lunar month of 1848). Xú frames Zhāng's editorial motivation: standard measles references — the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* 醫宗金鑑, Zhào-shì's *Huì-bǔ* 匯補 commentary, and the Xiè-shì *qī-shí-èr zhèng* 七十二症 (Seventy-two Symptoms) work — should suffice, but Zhāng felt that the *Jīn-jiàn*'s compressed *jiǎn-yuē* 簡約 (concise) format, the *fán-suǒ* 煩瑣 (excessive) qualifications, and the variable *biàn-yì* 變易 (changes) of measles symptomatology rendered annotation necessary. The work also embeds a *xiān shì yǐ dú-shū zhī fǎ* 先示以讀書之法 (a preliminary discussion of how to read [medical books]). Xú characterises village physicians as treating *zhèng zhī píng-xiǎn* 症之平險 (mild and severe diseases) without distinction, *bù-kěn yǐ bù-zhī cí* 不肯以不知辭 (refusing to admit ignorance), and *rǔ-mǎng miè-liè* 鹵莽滅裂 (treating recklessly); the *chǎn-zhù* aims to remedy this through systematic explanation. Earlier Lì Fēi-ān had collated the work for publication but had not completed the print run; the publication was effectively completed by the Zhāng family during the *Dào-guāng* 28 spring-summer measles epidemic. **Author's preface** (敘張子, partial) characterises Zhāng as a *Zhū-jì lǎo-sù* 諸暨老宿 (an old worthy of Zhū-jì), *bó-tōng jīng-shǐ, gōng jǔ-yè, shàn gǔ-wén-cí* 博通經史，工舉業，善古文詞 (broadly conversant in canonical and historical literature, skilled in eight-legged-essay composition, accomplished in classical prose).

### Abstract

The *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* is a focused mid-Qīng paediatric specialty work — an annotation-commentary on the measles chapter of the standard imperial paediatric reference. The work treats: measles pathological doctrine (*má bǐng yú yáng ér chéng yú yīn* 麻稟於陽而成於陰 — measles inherits from yang but completes in yin), symptomatology, treatment by clinical phase (*chū-rè* 初熱 initial-fever, *jiàn-miáo* 見苗 eruption emerging, *qí-miáo* 齊苗 eruption fully out, *huí-tā* 回塌 receding, *huí-hòu* 回後 post-recovery), and the supplementary disorders (*hóu-tòng* 喉痛, *kě-sòu* 咳嗽, *xiè-lì*, *yán-hóu* 咽喉). The work's commentary supplements the *Jīn-jiàn* verses with extensive prescription-modification and clinical-pulse discussion. The 1848 publication date and the 1848 measles epidemic that occasioned the publication are documentary evidence for the social context of mid-Qīng paediatric clinical practice.
