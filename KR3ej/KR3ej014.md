# Yòukē lèicuì 幼科類萃
*Distilled Categories of Paediatrics*
by [[王鑾]] (撰)

## About the work

A 28-*juǎn* Míng-period paediatric encyclopaedia by **Wáng Luán** 王鑾 (*zì* Wén-róng 文融, *hào* Róng-hú 容湖) of Wū-chéng 烏程 (modern Wú-xīng 吳興, Zhèjiāng), bearer of a multi-generation family paediatric practice. The work was composed across the *Hóng-zhì* to *Jiā-jìng* periods (c. 1502–1534) and published with a preface by Lǐ Lián 李濂 of Sōng-zhǔ 嵩渚 dated *Jiā-jìng* 13 (1534). It is one of the principal mid-Míng paediatric works prior to the Wàn Quán family lineage. Its 28 *juǎn* are organised: *juǎn* 1–2 as general theoretical introduction (*zǒng-lùn* 總論), *juǎn* 3–28 as 26 disease-categories — neonatal disorders, *jīng-fēng* 驚風, the *gān* 疳 chronic malnutrition syndromes, *nüè* 瘧, *lì* 痢, *tù-xiè* 吐瀉, *ké-sòu* 咳嗽, *zhěn* 疹, *shuǐ-zhǒng* 水腫, *fù-zhàng* 腹脹, *xián* 癇 epilepsy, *Shānghán* 傷寒 (incorporated into paediatrics, a methodological innovation later expanded by Chén Fù-zhèng), *shāng-shí* 傷食, *fù-tòng* 腹痛, *jī-shāng* 積傷, *pǐ-jī* 痞積, *lín* 淋, *dān-dú* 丹毒 (erysipelas), *dǎn* 疸 jaundice, *zhū xuè-zhèng* 諸血證, *hàn-zhèng* 汗證, *hóu chǐ shé* 喉齒舌, *ěr mù kǒu bí* 耳目口鼻, *zá-zhèng* 雜證, and *dòu* 痘 smallpox.

### Prefaces

The *hxwd* `_000.txt` carries an unsigned postface (*bá* 跋) — actually the closing prose of a senior official commending the project. The postface frames the work in the Biǎn Què anecdote (specialisation by category) and contrasts the Confucian ideal of *shén-shèng gōng-qiǎo* 神聖工巧 medicine with contemporary practice degraded to *chuǎi-mò tān-qǔ* 揣抹探取 (palpation-and-guesswork). It then describes the work's coverage — from *shòu-tāi* 受胎 (conception) through neonatal nourishment, constitutional assessment, pulse-and-fingerprint diagnosis, and the principal disease categories — and praises Wáng Luán's editorial method of distilling the *qún-shū yào-fāng* 群書要方 (essential prescriptions of the corpus of medical works) into a single accessible synthesis. The postface closes by noting that the work was printed at the Kāi-fēng *gōng-shǔ* 開封公署 (provincial office) at the direction of **Liú Sōng-shí** 劉鬆石 (Liú Sōng-shí gōng 劉鬆石公), and notes that this same official had also commissioned the printing of the *Shānghán liù-shū* 傷寒六書. The Lǐ Lián 1534 preface itself is not present in the `_000.txt` here transmitted.

### Abstract

The *Yòukē lèicuì* is the principal Wū-chéng-Wáng-family Míng-period paediatric work. Its therapeutic frame integrates Qián Yǐ's *zàng-fǔ* doctrine with the Jīn-Yuán four-master synthesis (Liú Wán-sù, Zhāng Cóng-zhèng, Lǐ Dōng-yuán, Zhū Dān-xī) and especially with Zhāng Yuán-sù 張元素 (*Jié-gǔ* 潔古) prescription practice. It is notable for unifying acupuncture and decoction therapy under a single paediatric *biàn-zhèng* schema, and for an emphasis on the central role of *pí-wèi* 脾胃 (spleen-stomach, the post-natal *hòu-tiān* 後天 root) in paediatric pathology — a doctrine inherited from Lǐ Dōng-yuán and from Qián Yǐ's *zàng-fǔ* paediatric synthesis. The work was reprinted by Liú Sōng-shí at the Kāi-fēng provincial office in the early-to-mid *Jiā-jìng* period and circulated widely in Míng paediatric pedagogy. The 1984 photographic reprint (Zhōng-yī gǔ-jí chū-bǎn-shè) is from the Tiān-jīn Health Vocational Medical College's Míng woodblock edition.

## Other points of interest

The postface's reference to Liú Sōng-shí 劉鬆石 (a senior *Jiā-jìng*-era official) and the parallel printing of the *Shānghán liù-shū* 傷寒六書 at the Kāi-fēng provincial yamen is documentary evidence for the role of provincial-office printing in the dissemination of Míng-period medical works — a complement to the better-known commercial *fāng-kè* 坊刻 trade.
