# Yòukē fāhuī 幼科發揮
*Elaboration on the Paediatric Branch*
by [[萬全]] (撰)

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* paediatric treatise by **Wàn Quán** 萬全 (1499–1582, *hào* Mìzhāi 密齋), third-generation inheritor of the Wàn-family medical practice of Luótián 羅田 in Húběi. The work systematically expounds the principles of paediatric pathology and therapy along Qián Yǐ 錢乙 *zàngfǔ* lines, organising disorders by the Five Phases (*gānbìng* 肝病, *xīnbìng* 心病, *píbìng* 脾病, *fèibìng* 肺病, *shènbìng* 腎病). It functions as the theoretical companion to the author's earlier clinical handbook *Yùyīng jiāmì* 育嬰家秘 ([[KR3ej043]]) — as Wàn's own preface explains, *Jiāmì* preserves the family's prescriptions while *Fāhuī* "elaborates the underlying ideas" (*fāmíng jiāmì zhī yíyì* 發明家秘之遺意), so that learners can grasp the *yì* 意 (clinical reasoning) and not merely the *fǎ* 法 (formulary). The work circulated widely in the JiāngHuái, Jiāngnán, MǐnYuè, and WúYuè regions during the late Míng and became one of the most-cited paediatric reference works of the MíngQīng period.

### Prefaces

The author's own preface (in `_000.txt`) opens with a meditation on Heaven's compassion and the parent's anxious concern for the infant body, then traces the Wàn family's medical genealogy: grandfather Xìngchéng 杏城翁 of Yùzhāng 豫章 ("first generation") died young; father Júxuān 菊軒翁 (Wàn Xiǎozhāi 萬筱齋) inherited the practice and in the *Chénghuà gēngzǐ* year (1480) settled at Luótián, where he married a Chén woman and fathered the author ("second generation"); Wàn Quán himself trained with the local Confucian scholars Zhāng Yùquán 張玉泉 and Hú Liǔxī 胡柳溪, then compiled the family's transmitted teachings — collecting what had been scattered, supplying what had been deficient, pruning what was overgrown, and correcting errors — into the *Yùyīng jiāmì* 育嬰家秘. He notes with regret that none of his ten sons proved able to continue the practice, and that the *Fāhuī* was therefore written to make the underlying theory transmissible to disciples outside the lineage.

### Abstract

The *Yòukē fāhuī* is one of the principal paediatric works of the WànMìzhāi medical lineage and a foundational text of mid-to-late-Míng paediatric scholarship. Internal evidence dates it to after the *Yùyīng jiāmì* and before the author's death in 1582; conventional dating places it in the *Jiājìng* to *Wànlì* transition, c. 1549–1579. The hxwd recension here transmitted is in four *juǎn* — *juǎn* 1 (preface and general principles of infant care, *bǎoyīng* 保嬰), *juǎn* 2 (Five Phase pathology, especially *gānbìng* and *xīnbìng*), *juǎn* 3 (*píbìng*, *fèibìng*, *shènbìng*), and *juǎn* 4 (specific clinical entities including *jīngfēng* 驚風 convulsive seizures, *gān* 疳 chronic malnutrition syndromes, *xièlì* 瀉痢 diarrhoea-dysentery, and a substantial *dòuzhěn* 痘疹 smallpox-and-eruptive-fevers section). Wàn frames paediatric pathology in a QiánYǐ *zàngfǔ* model with characteristic family modifications: cooling and clearing for the over-heating *xīngān* organs of the child, supplementing for the constitutionally weak *píshèn* organs. The Wàn family lineage and its publications — *Yùyīng jiāmì* 育嬰家秘 ([[KR3ej043]]), *Piànyù xīnshū* 片玉心書 ([[KR3ej044]]), *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ* 痘疹心法 ([[KR3ej066]]) — are described by Hinrichs and Barnes (2013) as the most significant family-line paediatric tradition of late-imperial China.

## Translations and research

- Hsiung Ping-chen 熊秉真, *A Tender Voyage: Children and Childhood in Late Imperial China*. Stanford UP, 2005 — the standard English-language monograph on paediatric medicine and childhood in Míng-Qīng China; gives extensive attention to the Wàn family and the *Yòukē fāhuī*.
- Hinrichs, T. J. and Linda L. Barnes (eds.), *Chinese Medicine and Healing: An Illustrated History*. Harvard UP, 2013, pp. 145–146 — situates *Yùyīng jiā-mì* and *Yòukē fāhuī* in the Wàn family medical lineage.
- *Wàn Mì-zhāi yī-xué quán-shū* 萬密齋醫學全書, ed. Fù Pèi-yīng 傅沛膺 et al. Beijing: Zhōngguó zhōng-yī-yào chūbǎn-shè, 1999 — the standard modern collected edition of Wàn Quán's works, including a punctuated *Yòukē fāhuī*.
- Fù Pèi-yīng 傅沛膺, *Wàn Mì-zhāi yī-shū shí-zhǒng jiào-zhù* 萬密齋醫書十種校注. Beijing: Rénmín wèi-shēng chūbǎn-shè — critical edition.
