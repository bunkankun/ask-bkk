# Jīnguì qǐyuè · Yòukē 金匱啟鑰（幼科）
*Opening the Lock of the Golden Coffer — Paediatrics*
by [[黃朝坊]] (撰)

## About the work

The paediatric section of **Huáng Cháofāng** 黃朝坊's (*fl. Dào-guāng* era) collected medical *cóng-shū* the *Jīn-guì qǐ-yuè* 金匱啟鑰 (Opening the Lock of the Golden Coffer, c. 1820–1850), a multi-work paediatric, gynaecological, and internal-medicine collection. Huáng was a Qīng paediatric specialist of the *Dào-guāng* generation; his collected *cóng-shū* is one of the secondary Qīng-period regional-physician compendia. The paediatric volume covers the standard paediatric disease-range in six *juǎn* and is the principal modern witness to Huáng's paediatric doctrine.

### Abstract

The *Jīn-guì qǐ-yuè · Yòu-kē* is the paediatric section of Huáng Cháofāng's *cóng-shū*. The work's frame is the standard High-Qīng paediatric synthesis — Qián-Yǐ *zàng-fǔ* doctrine, *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* imperial orthodoxy, integration of *má-dòu* 麻痘 (measles-and-smallpox) chapters from the Wàn-family and Chén-Wén-zhōng traditions, and Sòng-Míng paediatric prescription practice. The six *juǎn* cover the conventional paediatric disease-range. The title *Jīn-guì qǐ-yuè* 金匱啟鑰 (Opening the Lock of the Golden Coffer) evokes the Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng *Jīn-guì yào-lüè* 金匱要略 canonical reference and frames the work as opening the inherited medical canon for paediatric application. The work circulated principally in regional Qīng paediatric practice and was preserved in late-Qīng *cóng-shū* recensions.
