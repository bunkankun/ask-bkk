# Yīngtóng lèicuì 嬰童類萃
*Distilled Categories on Infants and Children*
by [[王大綸]] (撰)

## About the work

A late-Míng paediatric treatise by **Wáng Dà-lún** 王大綸 (*hào* Yí-gāng 怡岡), bearer of a nine-generation family medical lineage. The work was completed and woodblock-printed in *Tiān-qǐ* 2 = 1622 *jú-yuè* (chrysanthemum month, ninth lunar month) as one component of Wáng's three-work *cóng-shū* the *Wáng-shì jiā-chāo* 王氏家抄 (Family Transcriptions of the Wáng), the others being *Dòu-shā xīn-fǎ* 痘痧心法 (Smallpox-and-eruption Heart-Method) and an external-medicine prescription appendix. The *hxwd* recension transmits the paediatric volume *Yīngtóng lèicuì* alone.

### Prefaces

The *_000.txt* carries the author's own **postface** (*bá* 跋) dated *Tiān-qǐ* 2 (1622) *jú-yuè* by *Yí-gāng* Wáng Dà-lún. The postface opens with a vocabulary classification of the age categories — *yuè-nèi wéi qiǎng-bǎo* 月內為襁褓 (newborn within first month: swaddled), *jí qī yuē yīng* 及期曰嬰 (first year: *yīng*), *sān suì wéi hái* 三歲為孩 (three years: *hái*), *qī tiáo bā chèn* 七齠八齔 (seven-and-eight tooth-shedding age), *shí suì zhì-zǐ* 十歲稚子 (ten: *zhì-zǐ*), *shí-liù suì wéi tóng* 十六歲為童 (sixteen: *tóng*) — and articulates the *yǎ-kē* (silent department) problem: the infant cannot speak, even the older child cannot speak with diagnostic precision; "embryos and infants entrust their lives entirely to the physician, and the physician is the substitute mother of all his charges" (*yīng-tóng dú tīng-mìng yú yī, ér yī dú wéi zhòng-yīng zhī mǔ* 嬰童獨聽命於醫，而醫獨為眾嬰之母). Cāng-gōng's 倉公 maxim *yī zhě yì yě* 醫者意也 (the physician is the *yì* — intuitive understanding) is invoked, with Hán Yù's *Yuán-dào* 原道 metaphor of *xīn jìn huǒ chuán* 薪盡火傳 (the firewood is consumed but the fire is transmitted) — i.e. medical knowledge transmitted through generations. Wáng then recounts his family's nine-generation medical lineage, and explains the three-work *jiā-chāo* publication.

### Abstract

The *Yīngtóng lèicuì* is one of several Wàn-lì / *Tiān-qǐ* late-Míng paediatric compendia (alongside Wáng Kěn-táng's *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng yòu-kē* of 1602 and Qín Chāng-yù's *Yòu-kē zhé-zhōng* of c. 1641). The work organises paediatric disorders into a *lèi-cuì* (categorial distillation) format derived from the earlier Wàn Quán family and Lǐ Shí-zhēn's *Běn-cǎo gāng-mù* taxonomic models. The work circulated in late-Míng and early-Qīng Jiāng-nán paediatric practice. The 1622 date is fixed by the author's own postface.
