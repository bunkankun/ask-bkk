## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3ej is the **paediatrics** (*yòu-kē* 幼科, also *ér-kē* 兒科, *xiǎo'ér-kē* 小兒科) subdivision of the Kanripo medicine class (KR3e 醫家類, *yī-jiā lèi*, "Masters → Medicine"). The catalog lists 72 texts, all of which are catalogued here; all entries come from the early-modern Chinese-medical *cóngshū* (catalog source `hxwd`) — the *Hǎi-wài huí-guī Zhōng-yī shàn-běn gǔjí cóngshū* 海外回歸中醫善本古籍叢書 and its companion compilations — that complements the gynecology and warm-disease subdivisions [[KR3ei]] and [[KR3eg]] by gathering the literature on infant and child medicine: prenatal and neonatal care, the developmental *biàn-zhèng* 變蒸 doctrine, the paediatric *zàng-fǔ* and Five-Phase pathology of Qián Yǐ 錢乙, *jīng-fēng* 驚風 convulsive disorders, *gān* 疳 chronic-malnutrition syndromes, *má-zhěn* 麻疹 measles and *dòu-zhěn* 痘疹 smallpox (and the eighteenth-century state-codified variolation *zhòng-dòu* 種痘), and paediatric *tuī-ná* 推拿 medical massage. The doctrinal canon under which the corpus organises itself — **infant constitution as *chún-yáng* 純陽** (pure yáng, kindled and rapidly developing), the **Qián-Yǐ paediatric *zàng-fǔ* doctrine** (*gān-yǒu-yú, pí-bù-zú, shèn-xū* 肝有餘脾不足腎虛 as the paediatric-specific adjustment of Five-Phase pathology), and the **standard paediatric symptom-cluster taxonomy** (*jīng-fēng / gān / xiè-lì / ké-sòu / dòu-zhěn-má-zhěn*) — is a stable late-imperial typology that runs from the Sòng founder Qián Yǐ through the Míng synthesisers and Qīng imperial standard texts to the Republican-era TCM-college curricula.

The chronological span runs from the legendary pre-Táng / early-Táng *Lúxìn jīng* ([[KR3ej023]], a *yǐn-xíng* compilation possibly incorporating Hàn-Táng material, *terminus ante quem* c. 1100) and Sūn Sī-miǎo's paediatric chapters in the *Qiān-jīn yào-fāng* ([[KR3ej070]], 650s–680s), through the Northern-Sòng foundation by [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ ([[KR3ej020]], compiled 1119 by [[閻孝忠]] Yán Xiào-zhōng), the great Sòng paediatric encyclopaedias of [[劉昉]] Liú Fǎng ([[KR3ej010]], 1150) and the anonymous *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* ([[KR3ej017]], c. 1127–1216), the Southern-Sòng smallpox-and-eruption foundation of [[陳文中]] Chén Wén-zhòng ([[KR3ej053]] and [[KR3ej056]]), the Yuán paediatrics of [[曾世榮]] Zēng Shìróng ([[KR3ej007]], [[KR3ej024]]), the great Míng synthesis (the [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ corpus, the Wàn-Mì-zhāi family lineage of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán, [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěn-táng's *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng yòu-kē*), the Qiánlóng imperial standard ([[KR3ej029]], [[KR3ej048]], [[KR3ej055]], 1742) and the dense Qīng popular and specialised literature (smallpox-and-measles monographs, *tuī-ná* manuals, regional family handbooks), and into the Edo-period Japanese paediatrics of [[下津壽泉]] Shimotsu Jusen ([[KR3ej041]], 1709) and the Republican-era TCM-college re-edition tradition ([[吳克潛]] Wú Kèqián's 1934 *Érkē yàolüè* [[KR3ej002]]; [[陳守真]] Chén Shǒuzhēn's 1929 *Érkē cuìjīng* [[KR3ej012]]; [[惲鐵樵]] Yùn Tiěqiáo's *Bǎochì xīnshū* [[KR3ej072]]; [[李聰甫]] Lǐ Cōng-fǔ's 1940 *Mázhěn zhuānlùn* [[KR3ej058]]), closed by the Shōwa-era Japanese *Zhōngguó érkē yījiàn* of [[大塜敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu ([[KR3ej042]]). Wilkinson §41.7.7 (Gynecology, Pediatrics) frames the secondary literature; the standard English-language monograph is Hsiung Ping-chen 熊秉真, *A Tender Voyage: Children and Childhood in Late Imperial China* (Stanford UP, 2005). Wilkinson §41.3.1 lists Qián Yǐ's *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhíjué* (item 9) and Chén Wén-zhòng's *Xiǎo'ér dòuzhěn fānglùn* (item 11) as canonical entries in the broader Chinese medical corpus. Hinrichs and Barnes, *Chinese Medicine and Healing* (2013) supplies the one-volume general reference, with explicit attention to the Wàn-family lineage (pp. 145–146, 153).

## Important texts and text clusters

**The pre-Sòng foundational stratum.** Two works pre-date the Sòng paediatric synthesis and supply the textual ground for everything that follows:
- *Lúxìn jīng* 顱顖經 ([[KR3ej023]], c. 600–1100) — the earliest extant Chinese paediatric monograph, a *yǐn-xíng* (hidden-author) compilation of Hàn-Táng paediatric material on *biàn-zhèng* developmental cycles, *jīng-fēng* nosology in proto-form, *gān* chronic-malnutrition syndromes, and the *qí-fēng* 臍風 (umbilical-wind, neonatal tetanus) emergency. Preserved through Sòng citation in [[KR3ej010]] and *Tài-píng shèng-huì fāng* 太平聖惠方, and as a standalone two-*juǎn* work in the *Sì-kù quán-shū*; the principal source from which Qián Yǐ rebuilt the canonical post-Sòng paediatric synthesis.
- *Shàoxiǎo yīngrú fāng* 少小嬰孺方 ([[KR3ej070]], 650s–680s) — the paediatric chapter of [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sī-miǎo's *Qiān-jīn yào-fāng* and *Qiān-jīn yì-fāng*, treated as a discrete *fāng-shū* by long-standing bibliographic convention. The foundational doctrinal articulation of paediatrics as a distinct medical specialty: children as *xuè-qì wèi shí* 血氣未實 (incompletely-developed in *qì*-blood) with *zàng-fǔ jiāo-nèn* 臟腑嬌嫩 (delicate and tender organs); the *fēng-qì-mìng* 風氣命 finger-vein examination; the *xìn-mén* 囟門 fontanelle palpation; carefully-reduced paediatric dosing.

**The Qián-Yǐ Sòng foundation.** The single most consequential cluster in the entire division — paediatrics as a distinct *kē* with its own theoretical doctrine begins here:
- *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhíjué* 小兒藥證直訣 ([[KR3ej020]], compiled 1119 by [[閻孝忠]] Yán Xiào-zhōng from [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ's clinical and theoretical material of c. 1060–1113) — the canonical foundation of Chinese paediatric medicine. Six historically epochal innovations: (1) the **paediatric *zàng-fǔ* doctrine** (Five-Phase pathology with paediatric-specific *gān-yǒu-yú pí-bù-zú shèn-xū* adjustment); (2) the **canonical paediatric prescription corpus** — *Liù-wèi dì-huáng wán* 六味地黃丸 (the founding *zī-shèn* formula, perhaps the single most influential modification in the history of Chinese prescription, derived from the *Jīn-guì Shèn-qì wán* by Qián Yǐ's removal of *fù-zǐ* and *guì-zhī*), *Dǎo-chì sǎn* 導赤散, *Xiè-bái sǎn* 瀉白散, *Yì-huáng sǎn* 益黃散, *Bái-zhú sǎn* 白朮散; (3) the **doctrine of *biàn-zhèng* 變蒸** (the 31-day developmental crisis cycle, ten cycles to 320 days); (4) the **doctrine of paediatric *chún-yáng* 純陽** with its therapeutic implications; (5) the **paediatric *yī-àn* 醫案 corpus** in *juǎn* 2 — one of the earliest in Chinese medicine; (6) **paediatric *jīng-fēng* nosology** in its threefold *jí-jīng / màn-jīng / màn-pí-fēng* form.
- *Qián-shì xiǎo'ér zhíjué* 錢氏小兒直訣 ([[KR3ej021]], 1119/Míng) — the *hxwd* recension of the same text annotated by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ in the mid-Míng; the principal channel through which Qián Yǐ's Sòng paediatric doctrine entered the late-imperial pedagogical curriculum.

**The Sòng paediatric encyclopaedias.** Two great Sòng compendia preserve the pre-Sòng paediatric prescription literature and codify the Qián-Yǐ synthesis:
- *Yòuyòu xīnshū* 幼幼新書 ([[KR3ej010]], 1150) — Liú Fǎng's forty-*juǎn* compendium completed posthumously by [[樓璹]] Lóu Shú with [[石才孺]] Shí Cáirú's preface; the largest comprehensive Chinese paediatric work prior to the Míng-Qīng era, integrating *Nèi-jīng / Nán-jīng* theory, *Qiān-jīn / Zhǒu-hòu / Wài-tái* prescription traditions, Qián Yǐ's *zàng-fǔ* doctrine, the *Tài-píng shèng-huì fāng* imperial corpus, and twelfth-century Jiāng-nán paediatric *jiā-fāng*. Its *cǎi-jí* compilation-from-sources method preserves substantial fragments of paediatric texts now otherwise lost.
- *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* 小兒衛生總微論方 ([[KR3ej017]], c. 1127–1216) — the anonymous twenty-*juǎn* Sòng encyclopaedia covering all the standard paediatric disease-categories including a dedicated paediatric *Shānghán* section; the post-1127 dynastic-collapse generation's *yǐn-jū* (hidden-author) practice. The 1489 Míng edition and the 1910 Kē Féng-shí–Xiāo Běi-chéng collation are the principal transmission witnesses.

**The Chén Wén-zhòng Southern-Sòng smallpox doctrine.** [[陳文中]] Chén Wén-zhòng (*fl.* 1169–1184), *Hé-ān-láng pàn Tài-yī-jú jiān Hàn-lín liáng-yī* of the Southern-Sòng court, founded the *wēn-bǔ* 溫補 (warm-supplementing) approach to smallpox in opposition to the cooling-purging mainstream — a doctrinal opposition that structures the entire subsequent Chinese smallpox literature:
- *Xiǎo'ér dòuzhěn fānglùn* 小兒痘疹方論 ([[KR3ej053]], c. 1170–1200) — the *sān-huì-yè-dú* 三穢液毒 (Three Foul-Liquid Poisons) etiology and the four-way *biǎo-lǐ xū-shí* clinical classification; the signature warm-supplementation formulary *shí-yī-wèi mù-xiāng sǎn*, *shí-èr-wèi yì-gōng sǎn*, *shēn-qí nèi-tuō sǎn*, *ròu-dòu-kòu wán*. Wilkinson §41.3.1 item 11.
- *Chén-shì xiǎo'ér dòuzhěn fānglùn* 陳氏小兒痘疹方論 ([[KR3ej056]]) — the recension carrying [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's mid-Míng *yú àn* 愚按 ("my note") interpolations, which mediate Chén's *wēn-bǔ* doctrine with Xuē's later *bǔ-tǔ* 補土 (earth-supplementation) school.

**The Yuán paediatrics of Zēng Shìróng.** [[曾世榮]] Zēng Shìróng of Héng-yáng (*fl.* 1290–1322) supplies the Yuán-period bridge between the Sòng foundation and the Míng synthesis through two complementary works:
- *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]], 1294) — systematic textbook in rhymed-mnemonic *kǒu-jué* format; aligns Qián Yǐ's *zàng-fǔ* paediatric framework with Chén Wén-zhòng's *dòu-zhěn* doctrines.
- *Huóyòu kǒuyì* 活幼口議 ([[KR3ej024]], 1290–1322) — the more discursive oral-discussion companion. The text's survival is one of the principal cases in the Sino-Japanese-Korean medical-philological circuit: lost in defective copies in China, preserved by the Korean *Yī-fāng lèi-jù* (1445), and reconstituted in early-nineteenth-century Edo by the Tanba family.

**The mid-Míng Xuē-shì 薛氏 cluster.** Three works by or edited by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (1487–1559), *Tài-yī-yuàn yuàn-shǐ* under the Jiājìng emperor, and his son-disciple [[薛鎧]] Xuē Kǎi:
- *Bǎoyīng cuōyào* 保嬰撮要 ([[KR3ej015]], 1556) — the canonical mid-Míng paediatric treatise and the principal paediatric component of the *Xuē-shì yī-àn* corpus; twenty *juǎn* organised around case-driven (*yī-àn*) pedagogy; the principal Míng-period source for the Wàn family, [[KR3ej037]] and [[KR3ej013]].
- *Bǎoyīng jīnjìng lù* 保嬰金鏡錄 ([[KR3ej030]], c. 1500–1559) — the visual-diagnostic facial-coloration handbook in the Qián-shì five-*zàng* tradition, expanded with Xuē Jǐ's interlinear prescription commentary; an earlier paediatric handbook edited and annotated for the *Xuē-shì yī-àn* compendium.
- *Qián-shì xiǎo'ér zhíjué* ([[KR3ej021]]) — Xuē Jǐ's mid-Míng critical edition of Qián Yǐ (see above).

**The Wàn-Mì-zhāi family lineage of Luótián.** [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (1499–1582, *hào* Mì-zhāi 密齋), third-generation inheritor of the Wàn-family medical practice of Luó-tián 羅田 (Húběi), is the most prolific Míng paediatrician-gynaecologist and supplies a complete five-work paediatric corpus:
- *Yùyīng jiā-mì* 育嬰家秘 ([[KR3ej043]], c. 1549–1582) — the family's transmitted clinical formulary.
- *Yòukē fāhuī* 幼科發揮 ([[KR3ej001]], c. 1549–1579) — the theoretical companion to *Jiā-mì*, expounding paediatric pathology along Qián-Yǐ *zàng-fǔ* lines organised by the Five Phases.
- *Wàn-shì mìchuán Piànyù xīnshū* 萬氏秘傳片玉心書 ([[KR3ej044]], c. 1549–1582) — the more public-pedagogical *xīn-jué* (heart-method mnemonic) companion.
- *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ* 痘疹心法 ([[KR3ej066]], c. 1549–1568) — the synthesising late-Míng masterwork of paediatric smallpox medicine; develops the *guī-shèn biàn* doctrine (poison residing in the Kidney *zàng*) and elaborates Chén Wén-zhòng's *tāi-dú* in three layers (organ-, membrane-, marrow-poison).
- *Piànyù dòuzhěn* 片玉痘疹 ([[KR3ej065]], 1577) — the family's specialised smallpox monograph, opening with a programmatic *fù* on *tāi-dú* aetiology and phased disease progression.

The family lineage and its publications are described by Hinrichs and Barnes (2013, p. 145) as the most significant family-line paediatric tradition of late-imperial China.

**The late-Míng systematic synthesis.** The principal late-Míng systematic paediatric works alongside the Wàn and Xuē corpora:
- *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng·Yòukē* 證治準繩·幼科 ([[KR3ej037]], 1607) of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng — the paediatric volume (9 *juǎn*) of his great 120-*juǎn* *Liù-kē Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* (cf. [[KR3eh011]], [[KR3eh052]], [[KR3ei030]]). Doctrinally even-handed late-Míng standard reference; Wáng explicitly notes assembling *píng-shēng jù má-dòu shū bǎi-shù-shí jiā* 平生聚麻痘書百數十家 (more than a hundred works on measles-and-smallpox) of which a subset of value.
- *Yòukē lèicuì* 幼科類萃 ([[KR3ej014]], 1502–1534) of [[王鑾]] Wáng Luán — the Wū-chéng-Wáng-family work integrating Qián Yǐ's *zàng-fǔ* doctrine with the Jīn-Yuán *sì-dà-jiā* synthesis, especially [[張元素]] Zhāng Yuán-sù *Jié-gǔ* prescription practice; unifies acupuncture and decoction therapy under a single paediatric *biàn-zhèng* schema.
- *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]], 1400–1506) of [[魯伯嗣]] Lǔ Bósì — early-Míng hundred-question paediatric compendium integrating *Qián-shì zàng-fǔ* theory with Táng-Sòng prescription literature; one of the principal sources for Míng-period paediatric *xuéyuàn* curricula.
- *Yòukē zhézhōng* 幼科折衷 ([[KR3ej018]], c. 1620–1641) of [[秦昌遇]] Qín Chāngyù — the eclectic-synthesis treatise integrating Qián Yǐ, Liú Wán-sù, Lǐ Dōng-yuán, Zhū Dān-xī, and Zhāng Jǐng-yuè; with the manuscript variant *Yòukē zhézhōng mìchuán zhēnběn* ([[KR3ej034]], recorded by [[孟作霖]] Mèng Zuòlín).
- *Rénduān lù* 仁端錄 ([[KR3ej071]], 1591–1645) of [[徐謙]] Xú Qiān — comprehensive 16-*juǎn* late-Míng paediatric monograph framing paediatric practice in the Confucian *rén* / *yī wéi rén-shù* 醫為仁術 tradition; included in the *Sì-kù quán-shū*.
- *Yīngtóng lèicuì* 嬰童類萃 ([[KR3ej026]], 1622) of [[王大綸]] Wáng Dàlún — *Wàn-lì / Tiān-qǐ* paediatric *lèi-cuì* compendium derived from Wàn-family and *Běn-cǎo gāng-mù* taxonomic models.
- *Yòuyòu jí* 幼幼集 ([[KR3ej040]], 1595) of [[孟繼孔]] Mèng Jì-kǒng, postfaced by [[胡文煥]] Hú Wén-huàn — instance of the late-Míng *yī-rú-shū* scholar-publisher–physician collaboration.
- *Yuányòu xīnfǎ* 原幼心法 ([[KR3ej031]], c. 1550–1644) of [[彭用光]] Péng Yòngguāng — mnemonic-verse paediatric prescription handbook.
- *Xiǎo'ér zhūzhèng bǔyí* 小兒諸證補遺 ([[KR3ej032]], 1636) of [[張昶]] Zhāng Chǎng — focused late-Míng supplement on neonatal disorders; *fl. Hé-nán* paediatric practice.
- *Xiǎo'ér tuīná fāngmài huóyīng mìzhǐ quánshū* 小兒推拿方脈活嬰秘旨全書 ([[KR3ej069]], 1604) of [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián — late-Míng integration of *tuī-ná* with *fāng* (prescription) and *mài* (pulse), founder of the early-Qīng paediatric *tuī-ná* canon.

**The Qīng imperial standard: the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* paediatric corpus.** [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān (c. 1689–1748) chief-edited the Qiánlóng imperial *Yùzuǎn Yīzōng jīn-jiàn* [[KR3e0090]] of 1742, which contains three paediatric sections that together became the principal late-imperial state-sanctioned paediatric curriculum, in seven-character rhymed mnemonic verses with prose commentary, used as the standard textbook for the *Tài-yī yuàn* 太醫院 examinations from 1749 onward:
- *Yòukē xīnfǎ yàojué* 幼科心法要訣 ([[KR3ej029]], 6 *juǎn*) — general paediatrics in five-*zàng* organisation following Qián Yǐ.
- *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ yàojué* 痘疹心法要訣 ([[KR3ej048]], 8 *juǎn*) — smallpox-and-measles, organised by the canonical four-phase clinical course (*fā-rè / jiàn-diǎn / qǐ-zhǎng-guàn-jiāng / huí-shōu*) plus the four standard variolation techniques (*dòu-yī fǎ*, *dòu-jiāng fǎ*, *hàn-miáo fǎ*, *shuǐ-miáo fǎ*); the principal documentary source for the eighteenth-century Chinese variolation tradition that Edward Jenner later modified into Western smallpox vaccination.
- *Yòukē zhǒngdòu xīnfǎ yàozhǐ* 幼科種痘心法要旨 ([[KR3ej055]], 1 *juǎn*) — the dedicated variolation chapter.

**The Qīng reformist polemic.** Two early- to mid-Qīng works develop a self-conscious polemical position against the Qián-Yǐ-inherited orthodoxy that the imperial *Jīn-jiàn* canonised:
- *Yòukē tiějìng* 幼科鐵鏡 ([[KR3ej011]], 1695) of [[夏鼎]] Xià Dǐng — Chǔ-Wǎn (Hú-běi / Ān-huī) regional reformist; codifies paediatric *tuī-ná* as primary therapy (*Tuī-ná dài-yào fù* 推拿代藥賦, 62 verses / 332 characters); attacks *sān-guān zhǐ-wén* 三關指紋 finger-vein diagnosis as *yī-jiā zhī yì-jiào* (heterodox); attacks *jīng-fēng* nosology in the *Pì zhū jīng míng zhī miù* 闢諸驚名之謬 section; promotes the *dēng-huǒ shí-sān jiāo* 燈火十三燋 (Thirteen Lamp-Fire Cauterisations) for *qí-fēng* (neonatal tetanus).
- *Yòuyòu jíchéng* 幼幼集成 ([[KR3ej013]], 1750) of [[陳復正]] Chén Fùzhèng — High-Qīng Lǐng-nán reformist; six *juǎn* with the *jīng-fēng / chù* reform polemic, Daoist *huǒ-gōng* (cauterisation) practice, and integration of *Shānghán* doctrine into paediatric reasoning; alongside [[KR3ej037]] and [[KR3ej029]] one of the three most influential Qīng paediatric works.

**The major Qīng general-paediatric monographs.** A dense literature of standard Qīng paediatric handbooks:
- *Yòukē shìmí* 幼科釋謎 ([[KR3ej004]], 1774) of [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn-áo — the paediatric component of the great mid-Qīng polymath's medical œuvre (cf. [[KR3ei023]] *Fùkē yùchǐ*); six *juǎn*, *píng-zhèng* (moderate) clinical orientation.
- *Yòukē zhǐnán* 幼科指南 ([[KR3ej016]], 1661–1679) of [[周震]] Zhōu Zhèn — early-Qīng standard handbook with the distinctive *Hǔ-kǒu mài-wén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 supplement to *sān-guān zhǐ-wén*.
- *Cíyòu xīnshū* 慈幼新書 ([[KR3ej022]], 1650–1700) of [[程雲鵬]] Chéng Yúnpéng — Hú-běi-regional paediatric work synthesising mainstream *zàng-fǔ* doctrine with the *Wǔ-hóu yòng-bīng* (Zhū-gě Liàng-as-strategist) metaphor of clinical reasoning.
- *Érkē xǐng* 兒科醒 ([[KR3ej008]], c. 1707–1796) of [[芝嶼樵客]] Zhī-yǔ Qiáo-kè (studio-pseudonym) — opinionated single-*juǎn* late-Qīng household paediatric handbook with strong *Nèi-jīng* theoretical framing.
- *Yīng'ér lùn* 嬰兒論 ([[KR3ej009]], c. 1700–1797) of [[周士禰]] Zhōu Shìní — Qīng discursive paediatric monograph.
- *Yòukē qièyào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]], 1847) of [[王錫鑫]] Wáng Xī-xīn — late-Qīng pressing-essentials handbook.
- *Tóngyuán yīshū·Xiǎo'érkē* 彤園醫書·小兒科 ([[KR3ej038]], 1796–1820) of [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán — the paediatric volume of the *Tóng-yuán* family-school compendium (cf. [[KR3ei037]]).
- *Jīnguì qǐyuè (Yòukē)* 金匱啟鑰（幼科） ([[KR3ej039]], 1820–1850) of [[黃朝坊]] Huáng Cháofāng — late-Qīng standard handbook (cf. [[KR3ei038]]).
- *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* 保幼新編 ([[KR3ej005]], 1500–1785) of [[盧光履]] Lú Guānglǚ — Korean-recension paediatric handbook, with *yùn-qì liú-xíng fǎ* cosmological qi-circulation methodology more characteristic of Korean than mainland-Chinese paediatric texts.
- *Yùyīng tíyàoshuō* 鬻嬰提要說 ([[KR3ej019]], 1889) of [[張筱衫]] Zhāng Xiǎoshān — late-Qīng essentials on infant nurture.
- *Yòukē jīngchù mén* 幼科驚搐門 ([[KR3ej061]], 1791) of [[樓擁千]] Lóu Yōngqiān — specialised mid-Qīng treatise on convulsive disorders.
- *Fù-shì érkē* 傅氏兒科 ([[KR3ej045]], 1820–1900) — late-Qīng Shān-xī paediatric handbook in the [[傅山]] Fù Shān pseudepigraphic stream (cf. the larger Fù-pài gynecological cluster at [[KR3ei001]], [[KR3ei021]], [[KR3ei042]], [[KR3ei043]]).
- Anonymous and minor: *Cíyòu biànlǎn* 慈幼便覽 ([[KR3ej027]]), *Chén-shì yòukē mìjué* 陳氏幼科秘訣 ([[KR3ej028]]), *Dàyī Mǎ-shì xiǎo'ér mài-zhēn kē* 大醫馬氏小兒脈珍科 ([[KR3ej033]]), *Chéngqiú jí* 誠求集 ([[KR3ej035]]), *Yòukē huìjué zhíjiě* 幼科匯訣直解 ([[KR3ej036]]).

**The Qīng smallpox-and-measles specialty cluster.** A substantial late-imperial *zhuān-shū* literature on *má* / *dòu* eruptive fevers — both the catastrophic smallpox *dòu-zhěn* (then endemic and high-mortality, the disease that took Shùnzhì and Tóngzhì and scarred Kāngxī and Xiánfēng — Wilkinson p. 17ff) and the related measles *má-zhěn*, scarlatina *shā-zhěn*, and other eruptive fevers — beyond the imperial *Jīn-jiàn* and the Wàn-family Míng synthesis:
- *Dòuzhì lǐbiàn* 痘治理辨 ([[KR3ej063]], 1531–1539) of [[汪機]] Wāng Jī — early-Míng *Xīn-ān*-school philosophical-medical treatise on smallpox; bridge between the Sòng-Yuán *sì-dà-jiā* tradition and the late-Míng synthesising schools; advocates moderate-supplementation against both cooling-purging excess and warming-supplementation extreme.
- *Zhěnkē lèibiān* 疹科類編 ([[KR3ej062]], 1625) of [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng (cf. [[KR3ei006]] for the same author's gynecological synthesis) — late-Míng three-section measles treatise (*fā-rè / jiàn-xíng / shōu-hòu*), the principal late-Míng measles monograph.
- *(Dòuzhěn) Shēngmín qièyào* （痘疹）生民切要 ([[KR3ej064]], 1640–1700) of [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng — early-Qīng smallpox monograph by the major *Shānghán*-school theorist (cf. [[KR3eh]] for his broader corpus).
- *Mákē huórén quánshū* 麻科活人全書 ([[KR3ej047]], 1748–1800) of [[謝玉瓊]] Xiè Yùqióng — most comprehensive Qīng specialty treatise on measles; 72 measles symptom-patterns matched to specific prescriptions; addresses regional variations in measles presentation.
- *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* 麻疹闡註 ([[KR3ej046]], 1800–1848) of [[張霞谿]] Zhāng Xiáxī — early-nineteenth-century elucidatory measles commentary.
- *Mázhěn bèiyào fānglùn* 麻疹備要方論 ([[KR3ej054]], 1853) of [[吳硯丞]] Wú Yànchéng — mid-nineteenth-century measles formulary.
- *Dòukē jíyào* 痘科輯要 ([[KR3ej060]], 1801) of [[文起]] Wén Qǐ — early-nineteenth-century smallpox compilation.
- *Shāzhěn jíyào* 痧疹輯要 ([[KR3ej049]], 1888–1890) of [[葉霖]] Yè Lín — late-Qīng monograph on *shā-zhěn* (scarlatina-type eruptive fever); systematic differentiation from *má*, *yǐn-zhěn*, *shí-má*, *fēng-zhěn*; integrates *zhōng-wài* (Chinese and Western) sources.
- *Zhuānzhì máshā chūbiān* 專治麻痧初編 ([[KR3ej051]], 1890) of [[凌德]] Líng Dé — late-Qīng Háng-zhōu regional measles-and-eruptive-fever synthesis; cf. the same author's [[KR3ei024]] in gynecology.
- *Yuáncù yàolùn* 原瘄要論 ([[KR3ej050]], 1700–1900) of [[鄞縣袁氏]] Yuán of Yín-xiàn — late-Qīng regional measles monograph foregrounding the *fèi* (lung) in eruptive-fever pathology.
- *Xīndìng dòuzhěn jìshì zhēnquán* 新訂痘疹濟世真詮 ([[KR3ej057]]) and *Dòuzhěn jīngxiáng* 痘疹精詳 ([[KR3ej059]]) — anonymous Qīng smallpox compilations.
- *Jīngyàn máke* 經驗麻科 ([[KR3ej052]]) — anonymous Qīng experiential measles manuscript.

**The early-Qīng paediatric *tuī-ná* canon.** Three works define the early-Qīng paediatric medical-massage canon, drawing on a common late-Míng oral-transmission tradition associating *tuī-ná* with the legendary [[九天玄女]] Jiǔ-tiān Xuán-nǚ (the Mysterious Lady of the Nine Heavens):
- *Xiǎo'ér tuīná fāngmài huóyīng mìzhǐ quánshū* 小兒推拿方脈活嬰秘旨全書 ([[KR3ej069]], 1604) of [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián — the late-Míng founder of the genre, integrating *tuī-ná* + *fāng* + *mài*.
- *Xiǎo'ér tuīná guǎngyì* 小兒推拿廣意 ([[KR3ej067]], 1676) of [[熊應雄]] Xióng Yīngxióng — clinical-pedagogical orientation for apprentice training; covers facial-color assessment, hand-and-finger meridian-and-point identification, the principal *tuī-ná* manipulations (*tuī / ná / mō / àn / róu / gǔn*), and a disorder-by-disorder treatment programme.
- *Yòukē tuīná mìshū* 幼科推拿秘書 ([[KR3ej068]], 1691) of [[駱如龍]] Luò Rúlóng — systematic rather than encyclopedic, with the unified pedagogical structure *Bǎo-yīng fù* + *Bǎo-shēng gē* (containing the famous maxim *yào dé xiǎo'ér ān, cháng dài jī yǔ hán* 要得小兒安，常帶飢與寒) + *Biàn-zhèng lùn* + clinical sections + technique sections.

**The Japanese paediatric contributions.** Two works document the East-Asian medical exchange in paediatric medicine:
- *Yòukē zhèngzhì dàquán* 幼科證治大全 ([[KR3ej041]], 1709) of [[下津壽泉]] Shimotsu Jusen — Edo-period systematic paediatric compendium in the *kobō-ha* style, organised by disorder with careful preservation of multiple variant recensions of each prescription (*Yī-lín*, *Xuē-shì*, *Yī-tǒng*, *Dà-quán*, *Dà-quán* Yáng-shì, and Japanese supplements); a useful primary source for the philology of late-Míng / early-Qīng paediatric prescription transmission.
- *Zhōngguó érkē yījiàn* 中國兒科醫鑑 ([[KR3ej042]], 1930–1980) of [[大塜敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu — Shōwa-era integration of modern Western paediatric pathology (the 10–11-day measles incubation period, *Koplik's spots* on the buccal mucosa, Celsius temperature ranges) with the *zhèng-zhì* paediatric measles formulary (*Shēng-má gé-gēn tāng*, *Yín-qiào sǎn*, *Sāng-jú yǐn*) and the Chén-Wén-zhōng / post-Qián-Yǐ paediatric traditions.

**The Republican-era TCM-college reform.** A coherent early- to mid-twentieth-century Chinese-medicine textbook tradition, composed for the new urban TCM colleges of Shànghǎi and the Republican-era *guó-yī* movement:
- *Érkē cuìjīng* 兒科萃精 ([[KR3ej012]], 1929) of [[陳守真]] Chén Shǒuzhēn — 36-*mén* paediatric handbook integrating classical *Qián-Yǐ* doctrine with twentieth-century vaccination practice (*zhǒng-dòu* in the modern sense).
- *Érkē yàolüè* 兒科要略 ([[KR3ej002]], 1934) of [[吳克潛]] Wú Kèqián — nine-chapter Shànghǎi-TCM-college textbook interleaving the Chinese paediatric tradition with selective borrowings from Western paediatrics.
- *Bǎochì xīnshū* 保赤新書 ([[KR3ej072]], 1924–1932) of [[惲鐵樵]] Yùn Tiěqiáo — the most influential Republican-era *guó-yī* polymath's paediatric monograph; one of the principal vehicles of the late-Qīng / Republican TCM-college reform.
- *Mázhěn zhuānlùn* 麻疹專論 ([[KR3ej058]], 1940) of [[李聰甫]] Lǐ Cōng-fǔ — Republican-era three-phase measles management treatise (*chū-qí / zhōng-qí / mò-qí*); composed at Xīn-huà under war-time displacement; includes the 1934 prophetic essay *Wēn-yì-bìng zhī shèng-xíng* on epidemic disease.
- *Yòukē gàilùn* 幼科概論 ([[KR3ej003]], 1912–1949) — anonymous early-Republican paediatric handbook in the *jiǎng-yì* lecture-text genre, polemic against the *jīng-fēng* nosological category.

## Topics

- **Children's bodies as *chún-yáng* 純陽 and *zhì-yīn* 稚陰.** The paediatric anthropology articulated by Qián Yǐ [[KR3ej020]] — the infant body as yáng-replete and rapidly developing, with the *yīn* (substance, fluid, structure) lagging behind — supplies the doctrinal premise from which all subsequent paediatric pathology and therapy are deduced. The standard formulation is *zàng-fǔ jiāo-nèn, xíng-qì wèi chōng* 臟腑嬌嫩，形氣未充 (organs are delicate and tender; physical form and *qì* are not yet replete), already articulated by [[KR3ej070]] Sūn Sī-miǎo.
- **The Qián-Yǐ paediatric *zàng-fǔ* doctrine.** The systematic application of the Five Phases to paediatric pathology, with the paediatric-specific adjustment *gān-yǒu-yú, pí-bù-zú, shèn-xū* 肝有餘脾不足腎虛 (the liver is over-supplied, the spleen is under-supplied, the kidney is depleted). Runs from [[KR3ej020]] through the Sòng encyclopaedias ([[KR3ej010]], [[KR3ej017]]) and the Yuán ([[KR3ej007]]) and Míng paediatric corpus ([[KR3ej015]], [[KR3ej001]], [[KR3ej014]], [[KR3ej037]]) to the imperial Qīng canonisation in [[KR3ej029]].
- **The doctrine of *biàn-zhèng* 變蒸.** The 31- (or 32-) day developmental crisis cycle, repeating ten times to *zàng-fǔ qì zú* organ-vitality completion, accompanied by mild fevers that should not be treated as pathological. Articulated in [[KR3ej023]], canonicalised in [[KR3ej020]], reaffirmed in [[KR3ej068]] *Biàn-zhèng lùn*. A central diagnostic question is when an infant fever is normal *biàn-zhèng* and when it is genuine paediatric pathology.
- **The *jīng-fēng* 驚風 nosological controversy.** The classification of paediatric convulsive disorders into *jí-jīng* 急驚 acute, *màn-jīng* 慢驚 chronic, *màn-pí-fēng* 慢脾風 chronic-spleen — articulated by [[KR3ej020]] and elaborated through the Míng paediatric mainstream — became the principal target of Qīng paediatric polemic. [[KR3ej011]] Xià Dǐng (*Pì zhū jīng míng zhī miù*) and [[KR3ej013]] Chén Fùzhèng (the *jīng / chù* reform) attempted to dissolve the *jīng-fēng* category into more analytically defensible diagnostic schemes; the polemic continues in [[KR3ej003]] and the Republican TCM-college textbooks.
- **The Qián-Yǐ vs Chén-Wén-zhòng smallpox doctrinal opposition.** The structural fault-line of Chinese smallpox medicine: cooling-purging following Qián Yǐ [[KR3ej020]] (and elaborated by Liú Wán-sù and Zhū Dānxī) versus warming-supplementation following Chén Wén-zhòng [[KR3ej053]] / [[KR3ej056]]. Mediating positions in [[KR3ej063]] Wāng Jī (moderate-supplementation), [[KR3ej066]] Wàn Quán's systematising masterwork, [[KR3ej071]] Xú Qiān, and the imperial canonisation in [[KR3ej048]].
- **Variolation (*zhòng-dòu* 種痘) and the eighteenth-century Chinese smallpox technology.** The four standard variolation techniques (*dòu-yī fǎ* clothes-method, *dòu-jiāng fǎ* lymph-method, *hàn-miáo fǎ* dry-scab-method, *shuǐ-miáo fǎ* wet-scab-method) were codified as state-sanctioned imperial-medical orthopraxy in [[KR3ej048]] *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ yàojué* (1742) and the dedicated [[KR3ej055]] (1742). This is the principal documentary source for the eighteenth-century Chinese variolation tradition that Edward Jenner later modified into Western smallpox vaccination. Variolation is presupposed as established orthopraxy by [[KR3ej008]] and the late-Qīng popular handbooks; vaccination in the modern sense enters the Chinese paediatric corpus only in the Republican-era [[KR3ej012]].
- **Smallpox as a Manchu political question.** Wilkinson p. 17 documents how the Manchu rulers (not immune to smallpox in their steppe homeland) developed elaborate *bì-dòu suǒ* 避痘所 smallpox isolation facilities at the Nán-yuàn 南苑 and elsewhere; Shùnzhì died of smallpox at 22 and Tóngzhì at 18, while Kāngxī and Xiánfēng survived attacks but bore facial scars. The political stakes of the Qīng *dòu-zhěn* literature ([[KR3ej048]], [[KR3ej055]], [[KR3ej047]]) cannot be separated from this dynastic-medical concern.
- **Measles (*má-zhěn* 麻疹) and scarlatina (*shā-zhěn* 痧疹) differentiation.** A characteristic late-Qīng concern, addressed in [[KR3ej047]] (72 measles symptom-patterns), [[KR3ej049]] (Yè Lín's systematic *má / yǐn-zhěn / shí-má / fēng-zhěn* differentiation with explicit *zhōng-wài* synthesis), [[KR3ej051]] (Líng Dé's Háng-zhōu regional reform), [[KR3ej062]] (Wǔ Zhīwàng's late-Míng three-section frame), and culminating in the Republican-era [[KR3ej058]] (Lǐ Cōng-fǔ's three-phase clinical protocol) and [[KR3ej042]] (Ōtsuka's Sino-Japanese-Western synthesis).
- **Paediatric *tuī-ná* 推拿 and the question of non-medication therapy.** The doctrinal premise that paediatric *tuī-ná* can serve as primary therapy (because the infant cannot tolerate decoction medicine; because *shǒu-zú lián-luò zàng-fǔ, nèi-yìng wài-tōng* 手足聯絡臟腑，內應外通) is articulated by [[KR3ej011]] (Xià Dǐng's *Tuī-ná dài-yào fù*) and [[KR3ej068]] (Luò Rúlóng); the early-Qīng *tuī-ná* canon [[KR3ej067]], [[KR3ej068]], [[KR3ej069]] codifies the genre. The legendary [[九天玄女]] Jiǔ-tiān Xuán-nǚ origin myth and the gender-differentiated *nán zuǒ nǚ yòu* diagnostic scheme are characteristic doctrinal furniture. The early-Qīng paediatric *tuī-ná* tradition is the direct ancestor of the contemporary PRC TCM paediatric massage curriculum.
- **The *zhǐ-wén* 指紋 (three-pass finger-vein) diagnostic controversy.** The paediatric-specific *sān-guān zhǐ-wén* 三關指紋 (three-pass finger-vein) diagnostic method, where the colour and length of the venous patterns visible through the infant's thumb-index web are read as paediatric pulse-equivalents, is a major target of [[KR3ej011]] Xià Dǐng's polemic. The supplementary *Hǔ-kǒu mài-wén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 of [[KR3ej016]] Zhōu Zhèn defends and refines the method; the modern PRC TCM paediatric curriculum continues to teach it.
- **The *fēng-qì-mìng* 風氣命 three-position scheme.** The paediatric pulse-equivalent system in which the three finger-vein positions correspond to *fēng-guān* (wind-pass, mild), *qì-guān* (qi-pass, moderate), and *mìng-guān* (life-pass, severe) — articulated already by [[KR3ej070]] Sūn Sī-miǎo and inherited through the Sòng-Míng paediatric corpus.
- **Paediatric *gān* 疳 chronic-malnutrition syndromes.** A characteristic paediatric-specific disease category covering chronic malnutrition, failure-to-thrive, and chronic digestive disorders; structured into multiple sub-types in the Sòng paediatric corpus ([[KR3ej017]]) and central in the Qīng standard ([[KR3ej029]]).
- **Confucian moral framing of paediatric practice.** The title *Rénduān lù* 仁端錄 ("Record of the Foundations of Humaneness") of [[KR3ej071]] Xú Qiān, and the *Yī wéi rén-shù* 醫為仁術 (medicine as the art of humaneness) framing, explicitly cast paediatric practice as a moral as well as technical undertaking. The Republican-era handbooks (*cíyòu* 慈幼 "compassionate childcare" — [[KR3ej022]], [[KR3ej027]] — and *bǎo-chì* 保赤 "protecting the newborn" — [[KR3ej072]]) preserve this rhetorical framing.
- **Family-school transmission and regional medical lineages.** The principal paediatric family lineages structure substantial portions of the division: the Luó-tián Wàn family (five works [[KR3ej001]], [[KR3ej043]], [[KR3ej044]], [[KR3ej065]], [[KR3ej066]]; cf. [[KR3ei040]], [[KR3ei074]] in gynecology); the Sūzhōu Xuē family (three paediatric works [[KR3ej015]], [[KR3ej021]], [[KR3ej030]]; cf. the gynecological [[KR3ei009]], [[KR3ei039]] and others); the Wū-chéng Wáng family ([[KR3ej014]]); the Tóng-yuán Zhèng family ([[KR3ej038]]; cf. [[KR3ei037]]). The Hú-běi Chǔ-Wǎn regional reformist tradition culminates in [[KR3ej011]]; the Lǐng-nán Daoist-influenced reformist tradition in [[KR3ej013]]; the Háng-zhōu *zhé-zhōng* tradition in [[KR3ej051]].
- **Republican-era TCM-college reform.** The early-twentieth-century *guó-yī* movement's project of presenting Chinese paediatric medicine as a systematic, modernised clinical discipline, integrating classical Chinese paediatric doctrine with selective borrowings from Western paediatrics. Represented by [[KR3ej002]], [[KR3ej003]], [[KR3ej012]], [[KR3ej058]], [[KR3ej072]] (and parallel to the gynecological reform of [[KR3ei041]]). The shared vocabulary draws on the late-Qīng *yī-huà* 醫話 idiom and the early-Republican *jiǎng-yì* 講義 lecture-text genre.
- **Sino-Japanese-Korean medical-philological transmission.** Several paediatric works document the East-Asian transmission circuit. [[KR3ej024]] *Huóyòu kǒuyì* was lost in defective copies in China, preserved by the Korean *Yī-fāng lèi-jù* (1445), and reconstituted in early-nineteenth-century Edo by the Tanba family. [[KR3ej005]] *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* is a Korean-recension paediatric handbook (cosmological-circulation Korean methodology). [[KR3ej041]] (Edo) preserves multiple variant Chinese paediatric prescription recensions. [[KR3ej042]] (Shōwa Japan) integrates Western paediatric pathology with the classical Chinese paediatric formulary. Korean and Japanese recensions are therefore not peripheral but central textual witnesses for the Chinese paediatric tradition.
- **Cross-divisional connections.** Several core paediatric works are extracts or re-organisations of works held elsewhere in the KR catalog: [[KR3ej029]], [[KR3ej048]], [[KR3ej055]] are *juǎn* 50–55, 56–63, and a separate chapter of [[KR3e0090]] *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*; [[KR3ej037]] is one of six sections of [[KR3e0078]] *Liù-kē Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng*; [[KR3ej038]] is the paediatric volume of the *Tóng-yuán yīshū* (cf. [[KR3ei037]]); [[KR3ej039]] is the paediatric volume of *Jīn-guì qǐ-yuè* (cf. [[KR3ei038]]). Like gynecology, paediatrics is constituted both through specialised monographs and through the systematic disaggregation of comprehensive medical encyclopaedias.
