## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3ej is the **paediatrics** (*yòukē* 幼科, also *érkē* 兒科, *xiǎo'érkē* 小兒科) subdivision of the Kanripo medicine class (KR3e 醫家類, *yījiā lèi*, "Masters → Medicine"). The catalog lists 72 texts, all of which are catalogued here; all entries come from the early-modern Chinese-medical *cóngshū* (catalog source `hxwd`) — the *Hǎiwài huíguī Zhōngyī shànběn gǔjí cóngshū* 海外回歸中醫善本古籍叢書 and its companion compilations — that complements the gynecology and warm-disease subdivisions [[KR3ei]] and [[KR3eg]] by gathering the literature on infant and child medicine: prenatal and neonatal care, the developmental *biànzhèng* 變蒸 doctrine, the paediatric *zàngfǔ* and Five-Phase pathology of Qián Yǐ 錢乙, *jīngfēng* 驚風 convulsive disorders, *gān* 疳 chronic-malnutrition syndromes, *mázhěn* 麻疹 measles and *dòuzhěn* 痘疹 smallpox (and the eighteenth-century state-codified variolation *zhòngdòu* 種痘), and paediatric *tuīná* 推拿 medical massage. The doctrinal canon under which the corpus organises itself — **infant constitution as *chúnyáng* 純陽** (pure yáng, kindled and rapidly developing), the **QiánYǐ paediatric *zàngfǔ* doctrine** (*gānyǒuyú, píbùzú, shènxū* 肝有餘脾不足腎虛 as the paediatric-specific adjustment of Five-Phase pathology), and the **standard paediatric symptom-cluster taxonomy** (*jīngfēng / gān / xièlì / késòu / dòuzhěnmázhěn*) — is a stable late-imperial typology that runs from the Sòng founder Qián Yǐ through the Míng synthesisers and Qīng imperial standard texts to the Republican-era TCM-college curricula.

The chronological span runs from the legendary pre-Táng / early-Táng *Lúxìn jīng* ([[KR3ej023]], a *yǐnxíng* compilation possibly incorporating HànTáng material, *terminus ante quem* c. 1100) and Sūn Sīmiǎo's paediatric chapters in the *Qiānjīn yàofāng* ([[KR3ej070]], 650s–680s), through the Northern-Sòng foundation by [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ ([[KR3ej020]], compiled 1119 by [[閻孝忠]] Yán Xiàozhōng), the great Sòng paediatric encyclopaedias of [[劉昉]] Liú Fǎng ([[KR3ej010]], 1150) and the anonymous *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* ([[KR3ej017]], c. 1127–1216), the Southern-Sòng smallpox-and-eruption foundation of [[陳文中]] Chén Wénzhòng ([[KR3ej053]] and [[KR3ej056]]), the Yuán paediatrics of [[曾世榮]] Zēng Shìróng ([[KR3ej007]], [[KR3ej024]]), the great Míng synthesis (the [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ corpus, the WànMìzhāi family lineage of [[萬全]] Wàn Quán, [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng yòukē*), the Qiánlóng imperial standard ([[KR3ej029]], [[KR3ej048]], [[KR3ej055]], 1742) and the dense Qīng popular and specialised literature (smallpox-and-measles monographs, *tuīná* manuals, regional family handbooks), and into the Edo-period Japanese paediatrics of [[下津壽泉]] Shimotsu Jusen ([[KR3ej041]], 1709) and the Republican-era TCM-college re-edition tradition ([[吳克潛]] Wú Kèqián's 1934 *Érkē yàolüè* [[KR3ej002]]; [[陳守真]] Chén Shǒuzhēn's 1929 *Érkē cuìjīng* [[KR3ej012]]; [[惲鐵樵]] Yùn Tiěqiáo's *Bǎochì xīnshū* [[KR3ej072]]; [[李聰甫]] Lǐ Cōngfǔ's 1940 *Mázhěn zhuānlùn* [[KR3ej058]]), closed by the Shōwa-era Japanese *Zhōngguó érkē yījiàn* of [[大塜敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu ([[KR3ej042]]). Wilkinson §41.7.7 (Gynecology, Pediatrics) frames the secondary literature; the standard English-language monograph is Hsiung Ping-chen 熊秉真, *A Tender Voyage: Children and Childhood in Late Imperial China* (Stanford UP, 2005). Wilkinson §41.3.1 lists Qián Yǐ's *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhíjué* (item 9) and Chén Wénzhòng's *Xiǎo'ér dòuzhěn fānglùn* (item 11) as canonical entries in the broader Chinese medical corpus. Hinrichs and Barnes, *Chinese Medicine and Healing* (2013) supplies the one-volume general reference, with explicit attention to the Wàn-family lineage (pp. 145–146, 153).

## Important texts and text clusters

**The pre-Sòng foundational stratum.** Two works pre-date the Sòng paediatric synthesis and supply the textual ground for everything that follows:
- *Lúxìn jīng* 顱顖經 ([[KR3ej023]], c. 600–1100) — the earliest extant Chinese paediatric monograph, a *yǐnxíng* (hidden-author) compilation of HànTáng paediatric material on *biànzhèng* developmental cycles, *jīngfēng* nosology in proto-form, *gān* chronic-malnutrition syndromes, and the *qífēng* 臍風 (umbilical-wind, neonatal tetanus) emergency. Preserved through Sòng citation in [[KR3ej010]] and *Tàipíng shènghuì fāng* 太平聖惠方, and as a standalone two-*juǎn* work in the *Sìkù quánshū*; the principal source from which Qián Yǐ rebuilt the canonical post-Sòng paediatric synthesis.
- *Shàoxiǎo yīngrú fāng* 少小嬰孺方 ([[KR3ej070]], 650s–680s) — the paediatric chapter of [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sīmiǎo's *Qiānjīn yàofāng* and *Qiānjīn yìfāng*, treated as a discrete *fāngshū* by long-standing bibliographic convention. The foundational doctrinal articulation of paediatrics as a distinct medical specialty: children as *xuèqì wèi shí* 血氣未實 (incompletely-developed in *qì*-blood) with *zàngfǔ jiāonèn* 臟腑嬌嫩 (delicate and tender organs); the *fēngqìmìng* 風氣命 finger-vein examination; the *xìnmén* 囟門 fontanelle palpation; carefully-reduced paediatric dosing.

**The QiánYǐ Sòng foundation.** The single most consequential cluster in the entire division — paediatrics as a distinct *kē* with its own theoretical doctrine begins here:
- *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhíjué* 小兒藥證直訣 ([[KR3ej020]], compiled 1119 by [[閻孝忠]] Yán Xiàozhōng from [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ's clinical and theoretical material of c. 1060–1113) — the canonical foundation of Chinese paediatric medicine. Six historically epochal innovations: (1) the **paediatric *zàngfǔ* doctrine** (Five-Phase pathology with paediatric-specific *gānyǒuyú píbùzú shènxū* adjustment); (2) the **canonical paediatric prescription corpus** — *Liùwèi dìhuáng wán* 六味地黃丸 (the founding *zīshèn* formula, perhaps the single most influential modification in the history of Chinese prescription, derived from the *Jīnguì Shènqì wán* by Qián Yǐ's removal of *fùzǐ* and *guìzhī*), *Dǎochì sǎn* 導赤散, *Xièbái sǎn* 瀉白散, *Yìhuáng sǎn* 益黃散, *Báizhú sǎn* 白朮散; (3) the **doctrine of *biànzhèng* 變蒸** (the 31-day developmental crisis cycle, ten cycles to 320 days); (4) the **doctrine of paediatric *chúnyáng* 純陽** with its therapeutic implications; (5) the **paediatric *yīàn* 醫案 corpus** in *juǎn* 2 — one of the earliest in Chinese medicine; (6) **paediatric *jīngfēng* nosology** in its threefold *jíjīng / mànjīng / mànpífēng* form.
- *Qiánshì xiǎo'ér zhíjué* 錢氏小兒直訣 ([[KR3ej021]], 1119/Míng) — the *hxwd* recension of the same text annotated by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ in the mid-Míng; the principal channel through which Qián Yǐ's Sòng paediatric doctrine entered the late-imperial pedagogical curriculum.

**The Sòng paediatric encyclopaedias.** Two great Sòng compendia preserve the pre-Sòng paediatric prescription literature and codify the QiánYǐ synthesis:
- *Yòuyòu xīnshū* 幼幼新書 ([[KR3ej010]], 1150) — Liú Fǎng's forty-*juǎn* compendium completed posthumously by [[樓璹]] Lóu Shú with [[石才孺]] Shí Cáirú's preface; the largest comprehensive Chinese paediatric work prior to the MíngQīng era, integrating *Nèijīng / Nánjīng* theory, *Qiānjīn / Zhǒuhòu / Wàitái* prescription traditions, Qián Yǐ's *zàngfǔ* doctrine, the *Tàipíng shènghuì fāng* imperial corpus, and twelfth-century Jiāngnán paediatric *jiāfāng*. Its *cǎijí* compilation-from-sources method preserves substantial fragments of paediatric texts now otherwise lost.
- *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēi lùnfāng* 小兒衛生總微論方 ([[KR3ej017]], c. 1127–1216) — the anonymous twenty-*juǎn* Sòng encyclopaedia covering all the standard paediatric disease-categories including a dedicated paediatric *Shānghán* section; the post-1127 dynastic-collapse generation's *yǐnjū* (hidden-author) practice. The 1489 Míng edition and the 1910 Kē Féngshí–Xiāo Běichéng collation are the principal transmission witnesses.

**The Chén Wénzhòng Southern-Sòng smallpox doctrine.** [[陳文中]] Chén Wénzhòng (*fl.* 1169–1184), *Héānláng pàn Tàiyījú jiān Hànlín liángyī* of the Southern-Sòng court, founded the *wēnbǔ* 溫補 (warm-supplementing) approach to smallpox in opposition to the cooling-purging mainstream — a doctrinal opposition that structures the entire subsequent Chinese smallpox literature:
- *Xiǎo'ér dòuzhěn fānglùn* 小兒痘疹方論 ([[KR3ej053]], c. 1170–1200) — the *sānhuìyèdú* 三穢液毒 (Three Foul-Liquid Poisons) etiology and the four-way *biǎolǐ xūshí* clinical classification; the signature warm-supplementation formulary *shíyīwèi mùxiāng sǎn*, *shíèrwèi yìgōng sǎn*, *shēnqí nèituō sǎn*, *ròudòukòu wán*. Wilkinson §41.3.1 item 11.
- *Chénshì xiǎo'ér dòuzhěn fānglùn* 陳氏小兒痘疹方論 ([[KR3ej056]]) — the recension carrying [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's mid-Míng *yú àn* 愚按 ("my note") interpolations, which mediate Chén's *wēnbǔ* doctrine with Xuē's later *bǔtǔ* 補土 (earth-supplementation) school.

**The Yuán paediatrics of Zēng Shìróng.** [[曾世榮]] Zēng Shìróng of Héngyáng (*fl.* 1290–1322) supplies the Yuán-period bridge between the Sòng foundation and the Míng synthesis through two complementary works:
- *Huóyòu xīnshū* 活幼心書 ([[KR3ej007]], 1294) — systematic textbook in rhymed-mnemonic *kǒujué* format; aligns Qián Yǐ's *zàngfǔ* paediatric framework with Chén Wénzhòng's *dòuzhěn* doctrines.
- *Huóyòu kǒuyì* 活幼口議 ([[KR3ej024]], 1290–1322) — the more discursive oral-discussion companion. The text's survival is one of the principal cases in the Sino-Japanese-Korean medical-philological circuit: lost in defective copies in China, preserved by the Korean *Yīfāng lèijù* (1445), and reconstituted in early-nineteenth-century Edo by the Tanba family.

**The mid-Míng Xuēshì 薛氏 cluster.** Three works by or edited by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (1487–1559), *Tàiyīyuàn yuànshǐ* under the Jiājìng emperor, and his son-disciple [[薛鎧]] Xuē Kǎi:
- *Bǎoyīng cuōyào* 保嬰撮要 ([[KR3ej015]], 1556) — the canonical mid-Míng paediatric treatise and the principal paediatric component of the *Xuēshì yīàn* corpus; twenty *juǎn* organised around case-driven (*yīàn*) pedagogy; the principal Míng-period source for the Wàn family, [[KR3ej037]] and [[KR3ej013]].
- *Bǎoyīng jīnjìng lù* 保嬰金鏡錄 ([[KR3ej030]], c. 1500–1559) — the visual-diagnostic facial-coloration handbook in the Qiánshì five-*zàng* tradition, expanded with Xuē Jǐ's interlinear prescription commentary; an earlier paediatric handbook edited and annotated for the *Xuēshì yīàn* compendium.
- *Qiánshì xiǎo'ér zhíjué* ([[KR3ej021]]) — Xuē Jǐ's mid-Míng critical edition of Qián Yǐ (see above).

**The WànMìzhāi family lineage of Luótián.** [[萬全]] Wàn Quán (1499–1582, *hào* Mìzhāi 密齋), third-generation inheritor of the Wàn-family medical practice of Luótián 羅田 (Húběi), is the most prolific Míng paediatrician-gynaecologist and supplies a complete five-work paediatric corpus:
- *Yùyīng jiāmì* 育嬰家秘 ([[KR3ej043]], c. 1549–1582) — the family's transmitted clinical formulary.
- *Yòukē fāhuī* 幼科發揮 ([[KR3ej001]], c. 1549–1579) — the theoretical companion to *Jiāmì*, expounding paediatric pathology along QiánYǐ *zàngfǔ* lines organised by the Five Phases.
- *Wànshì mìchuán Piànyù xīnshū* 萬氏秘傳片玉心書 ([[KR3ej044]], c. 1549–1582) — the more public-pedagogical *xīnjué* (heart-method mnemonic) companion.
- *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ* 痘疹心法 ([[KR3ej066]], c. 1549–1568) — the synthesising late-Míng masterwork of paediatric smallpox medicine; develops the *guīshèn biàn* doctrine (poison residing in the Kidney *zàng*) and elaborates Chén Wénzhòng's *tāidú* in three layers (organ-, membrane-, marrow-poison).
- *Piànyù dòuzhěn* 片玉痘疹 ([[KR3ej065]], 1577) — the family's specialised smallpox monograph, opening with a programmatic *fù* on *tāidú* aetiology and phased disease progression.

The family lineage and its publications are described by Hinrichs and Barnes (2013, p. 145) as the most significant family-line paediatric tradition of late-imperial China.

**The late-Míng systematic synthesis.** The principal late-Míng systematic paediatric works alongside the Wàn and Xuē corpora:
- *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng·Yòukē* 證治準繩·幼科 ([[KR3ej037]], 1607) of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng — the paediatric volume (9 *juǎn*) of his great 120-*juǎn* *Liùkē Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* (cf. [[KR3eh011]], [[KR3eh052]], [[KR3ei030]]). Doctrinally even-handed late-Míng standard reference; Wáng explicitly notes assembling *píngshēng jù mádòu shū bǎishùshí jiā* 平生聚麻痘書百數十家 (more than a hundred works on measles-and-smallpox) of which a subset of value.
- *Yòukē lèicuì* 幼科類萃 ([[KR3ej014]], 1502–1534) of [[王鑾]] Wáng Luán — the Wū-chéng-Wáng-family work integrating Qián Yǐ's *zàngfǔ* doctrine with the JīnYuán *sìdàjiā* synthesis, especially [[張元素]] Zhāng Yuánsù *Jiégǔ* prescription practice; unifies acupuncture and decoction therapy under a single paediatric *biànzhèng* schema.
- *Yīngtóng bǎiwèn* 嬰童百問 ([[KR3ej006]], 1400–1506) of [[魯伯嗣]] Lǔ Bósì — early-Míng hundred-question paediatric compendium integrating *Qiánshì zàngfǔ* theory with TángSòng prescription literature; one of the principal sources for Míng-period paediatric *xuéyuàn* curricula.
- *Yòukē zhézhōng* 幼科折衷 ([[KR3ej018]], c. 1620–1641) of [[秦昌遇]] Qín Chāngyù — the eclectic-synthesis treatise integrating Qián Yǐ, Liú Wánsù, Lǐ Dōngyuán, Zhū Dānxī, and Zhāng Jǐngyuè; with the manuscript variant *Yòukē zhézhōng mìchuán zhēnběn* ([[KR3ej034]], recorded by [[孟作霖]] Mèng Zuòlín).
- *Rénduān lù* 仁端錄 ([[KR3ej071]], 1591–1645) of [[徐謙]] Xú Qiān — comprehensive 16-*juǎn* late-Míng paediatric monograph framing paediatric practice in the Confucian *rén* / *yī wéi rénshù* 醫為仁術 tradition; included in the *Sìkù quánshū*.
- *Yīngtóng lèicuì* 嬰童類萃 ([[KR3ej026]], 1622) of [[王大綸]] Wáng Dàlún — *Wànlì / Tiānqǐ* paediatric *lèicuì* compendium derived from Wàn-family and *Běncǎo gāngmù* taxonomic models.
- *Yòuyòu jí* 幼幼集 ([[KR3ej040]], 1595) of [[孟繼孔]] Mèng Jìkǒng, postfaced by [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn — instance of the late-Míng *yīrúshū* scholar-publisher–physician collaboration.
- *Yuányòu xīnfǎ* 原幼心法 ([[KR3ej031]], c. 1550–1644) of [[彭用光]] Péng Yòngguāng — mnemonic-verse paediatric prescription handbook.
- *Xiǎo'ér zhūzhèng bǔyí* 小兒諸證補遺 ([[KR3ej032]], 1636) of [[張昶]] Zhāng Chǎng — focused late-Míng supplement on neonatal disorders; *fl. Hénán* paediatric practice.
- *Xiǎo'ér tuīná fāngmài huóyīng mìzhǐ quánshū* 小兒推拿方脈活嬰秘旨全書 ([[KR3ej069]], 1604) of [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián — late-Míng integration of *tuīná* with *fāng* (prescription) and *mài* (pulse), founder of the early-Qīng paediatric *tuīná* canon.

**The Qīng imperial standard: the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* paediatric corpus.** [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān (c. 1689–1748) chief-edited the Qiánlóng imperial *Yùzuǎn Yīzōng jīnjiàn* [[KR3e0090]] of 1742, which contains three paediatric sections that together became the principal late-imperial state-sanctioned paediatric curriculum, in seven-character rhymed mnemonic verses with prose commentary, used as the standard textbook for the *Tàiyī yuàn* 太醫院 examinations from 1749 onward:
- *Yòukē xīnfǎ yàojué* 幼科心法要訣 ([[KR3ej029]], 6 *juǎn*) — general paediatrics in five-*zàng* organisation following Qián Yǐ.
- *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ yàojué* 痘疹心法要訣 ([[KR3ej048]], 8 *juǎn*) — smallpox-and-measles, organised by the canonical four-phase clinical course (*fārè / jiàndiǎn / qǐzhǎngguànjiāng / huíshōu*) plus the four standard variolation techniques (*dòuyī fǎ*, *dòujiāng fǎ*, *hànmiáo fǎ*, *shuǐmiáo fǎ*); the principal documentary source for the eighteenth-century Chinese variolation tradition that Edward Jenner later modified into Western smallpox vaccination.
- *Yòukē zhǒngdòu xīnfǎ yàozhǐ* 幼科種痘心法要旨 ([[KR3ej055]], 1 *juǎn*) — the dedicated variolation chapter.

**The Qīng reformist polemic.** Two early- to mid-Qīng works develop a self-conscious polemical position against the Qián-Yǐ-inherited orthodoxy that the imperial *Jīnjiàn* canonised:
- *Yòukē tiějìng* 幼科鐵鏡 ([[KR3ej011]], 1695) of [[夏鼎]] Xià Dǐng — ChǔWǎn (Húběi / Ānhuī) regional reformist; codifies paediatric *tuīná* as primary therapy (*Tuīná dàiyào fù* 推拿代藥賦, 62 verses / 332 characters); attacks *sānguān zhǐwén* 三關指紋 finger-vein diagnosis as *yījiā zhī yìjiào* (heterodox); attacks *jīngfēng* nosology in the *Pì zhū jīng míng zhī miù* 闢諸驚名之謬 section; promotes the *dēnghuǒ shísān jiāo* 燈火十三燋 (Thirteen Lamp-Fire Cauterisations) for *qífēng* (neonatal tetanus).
- *Yòuyòu jíchéng* 幼幼集成 ([[KR3ej013]], 1750) of [[陳復正]] Chén Fùzhèng — High-Qīng Lǐngnán reformist; six *juǎn* with the *jīngfēng / chù* reform polemic, Daoist *huǒgōng* (cauterisation) practice, and integration of *Shānghán* doctrine into paediatric reasoning; alongside [[KR3ej037]] and [[KR3ej029]] one of the three most influential Qīng paediatric works.

**The major Qīng general-paediatric monographs.** A dense literature of standard Qīng paediatric handbooks:
- *Yòukē shìmí* 幼科釋謎 ([[KR3ej004]], 1774) of [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīnáo — the paediatric component of the great mid-Qīng polymath's medical œuvre (cf. [[KR3ei023]] *Fùkē yùchǐ*); six *juǎn*, *píngzhèng* (moderate) clinical orientation.
- *Yòukē zhǐnán* 幼科指南 ([[KR3ej016]], 1661–1679) of [[周震]] Zhōu Zhèn — early-Qīng standard handbook with the distinctive *Hǔkǒu màiwén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 supplement to *sānguān zhǐwén*.
- *Cíyòu xīnshū* 慈幼新書 ([[KR3ej022]], 1650–1700) of [[程雲鵬]] Chéng Yúnpéng — Hú-běi-regional paediatric work synthesising mainstream *zàngfǔ* doctrine with the *Wǔhóu yòngbīng* (Zhūgě Liàng-as-strategist) metaphor of clinical reasoning.
- *Érkē xǐng* 兒科醒 ([[KR3ej008]], c. 1707–1796) of [[芝嶼樵客]] Zhīyǔ Qiáokè (studio-pseudonym) — opinionated single-*juǎn* late-Qīng household paediatric handbook with strong *Nèijīng* theoretical framing.
- *Yīng'ér lùn* 嬰兒論 ([[KR3ej009]], c. 1700–1797) of [[周士禰]] Zhōu Shìní — Qīng discursive paediatric monograph.
- *Yòukē qièyào* 幼科切要 ([[KR3ej025]], 1847) of [[王錫鑫]] Wáng Xīxīn — late-Qīng pressing-essentials handbook.
- *Tóngyuán yīshū·Xiǎo'érkē* 彤園醫書·小兒科 ([[KR3ej038]], 1796–1820) of [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán — the paediatric volume of the *Tóngyuán* family-school compendium (cf. [[KR3ei037]]).
- *Jīnguì qǐyuè (Yòukē)* 金匱啟鑰（幼科） ([[KR3ej039]], 1820–1850) of [[黃朝坊]] Huáng Cháofāng — late-Qīng standard handbook (cf. [[KR3ei038]]).
- *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* 保幼新編 ([[KR3ej005]], 1500–1785) of [[盧光履]] Lú Guānglǚ — Korean-recension paediatric handbook, with *yùnqì liúxíng fǎ* cosmological qi-circulation methodology more characteristic of Korean than mainland-Chinese paediatric texts.
- *Yùyīng tíyàoshuō* 鬻嬰提要說 ([[KR3ej019]], 1889) of [[張筱衫]] Zhāng Xiǎoshān — late-Qīng essentials on infant nurture.
- *Yòukē jīngchù mén* 幼科驚搐門 ([[KR3ej061]], 1791) of [[樓擁千]] Lóu Yōngqiān — specialised mid-Qīng treatise on convulsive disorders.
- *Fùshì érkē* 傅氏兒科 ([[KR3ej045]], 1820–1900) — late-Qīng Shānxī paediatric handbook in the [[傅山]] Fù Shān pseudepigraphic stream (cf. the larger Fùpài gynecological cluster at [[KR3ei001]], [[KR3ei021]], [[KR3ei042]], [[KR3ei043]]).
- Anonymous and minor: *Cíyòu biànlǎn* 慈幼便覽 ([[KR3ej027]]), *Chénshì yòukē mìjué* 陳氏幼科秘訣 ([[KR3ej028]]), *Dàyī Mǎshì xiǎo'ér màizhēn kē* 大醫馬氏小兒脈珍科 ([[KR3ej033]]), *Chéngqiú jí* 誠求集 ([[KR3ej035]]), *Yòukē huìjué zhíjiě* 幼科匯訣直解 ([[KR3ej036]]).

**The Qīng smallpox-and-measles specialty cluster.** A substantial late-imperial *zhuānshū* literature on *má* / *dòu* eruptive fevers — both the catastrophic smallpox *dòuzhěn* (then endemic and high-mortality, the disease that took Shùnzhì and Tóngzhì and scarred Kāngxī and Xiánfēng — Wilkinson p. 17ff) and the related measles *mázhěn*, scarlatina *shāzhěn*, and other eruptive fevers — beyond the imperial *Jīnjiàn* and the Wàn-family Míng synthesis:
- *Dòuzhì lǐbiàn* 痘治理辨 ([[KR3ej063]], 1531–1539) of [[汪機]] Wāng Jī — early-Míng *Xīnān*-school philosophical-medical treatise on smallpox; bridge between the SòngYuán *sìdàjiā* tradition and the late-Míng synthesising schools; advocates moderate-supplementation against both cooling-purging excess and warming-supplementation extreme.
- *Zhěnkē lèibiān* 疹科類編 ([[KR3ej062]], 1625) of [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng (cf. [[KR3ei006]] for the same author's gynecological synthesis) — late-Míng three-section measles treatise (*fārè / jiànxíng / shōuhòu*), the principal late-Míng measles monograph.
- *(Dòuzhěn) Shēngmín qièyào* （痘疹）生民切要 ([[KR3ej064]], 1640–1700) of [[喻昌]] Yù Chāng — early-Qīng smallpox monograph by the major *Shānghán*-school theorist (cf. [[KR3eh]] for his broader corpus).
- *Mákē huórén quánshū* 麻科活人全書 ([[KR3ej047]], 1748–1800) of [[謝玉瓊]] Xiè Yùqióng — most comprehensive Qīng specialty treatise on measles; 72 measles symptom-patterns matched to specific prescriptions; addresses regional variations in measles presentation.
- *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* 麻疹闡註 ([[KR3ej046]], 1800–1848) of [[張霞谿]] Zhāng Xiáxī — early-nineteenth-century elucidatory measles commentary.
- *Mázhěn bèiyào fānglùn* 麻疹備要方論 ([[KR3ej054]], 1853) of [[吳硯丞]] Wú Yànchéng — mid-nineteenth-century measles formulary.
- *Dòukē jíyào* 痘科輯要 ([[KR3ej060]], 1801) of [[文起]] Wén Qǐ — early-nineteenth-century smallpox compilation.
- *Shāzhěn jíyào* 痧疹輯要 ([[KR3ej049]], 1888–1890) of [[葉霖]] Yè Lín — late-Qīng monograph on *shāzhěn* (scarlatina-type eruptive fever); systematic differentiation from *má*, *yǐnzhěn*, *shímá*, *fēngzhěn*; integrates *zhōngwài* (Chinese and Western) sources.
- *Zhuānzhì máshā chūbiān* 專治麻痧初編 ([[KR3ej051]], 1890) of [[凌德]] Líng Dé — late-Qīng Hángzhōu regional measles-and-eruptive-fever synthesis; cf. the same author's [[KR3ei024]] in gynecology.
- *Yuáncù yàolùn* 原瘄要論 ([[KR3ej050]], 1700–1900) of [[鄞縣袁氏]] Yuán of Yínxiàn — late-Qīng regional measles monograph foregrounding the *fèi* (lung) in eruptive-fever pathology.
- *Xīndìng dòuzhěn jìshì zhēnquán* 新訂痘疹濟世真詮 ([[KR3ej057]]) and *Dòuzhěn jīngxiáng* 痘疹精詳 ([[KR3ej059]]) — anonymous Qīng smallpox compilations.
- *Jīngyàn máke* 經驗麻科 ([[KR3ej052]]) — anonymous Qīng experiential measles manuscript.

**The early-Qīng paediatric *tuīná* canon.** Three works define the early-Qīng paediatric medical-massage canon, drawing on a common late-Míng oral-transmission tradition associating *tuīná* with the legendary [[九天玄女]] Jiǔtiān Xuánnǚ (the Mysterious Lady of the Nine Heavens):
- *Xiǎo'ér tuīná fāngmài huóyīng mìzhǐ quánshū* 小兒推拿方脈活嬰秘旨全書 ([[KR3ej069]], 1604) of [[龔廷賢]] Gōng Tíngxián — the late-Míng founder of the genre, integrating *tuīná* + *fāng* + *mài*.
- *Xiǎo'ér tuīná guǎngyì* 小兒推拿廣意 ([[KR3ej067]], 1676) of [[熊應雄]] Xióng Yīngxióng — clinical-pedagogical orientation for apprentice training; covers facial-color assessment, hand-and-finger meridian-and-point identification, the principal *tuīná* manipulations (*tuī / ná / mō / àn / róu / gǔn*), and a disorder-by-disorder treatment programme.
- *Yòukē tuīná mìshū* 幼科推拿秘書 ([[KR3ej068]], 1691) of [[駱如龍]] Luò Rúlóng — systematic rather than encyclopedic, with the unified pedagogical structure *Bǎoyīng fù* + *Bǎoshēng gē* (containing the famous maxim *yào dé xiǎo'ér ān, cháng dài jī yǔ hán* 要得小兒安，常帶飢與寒) + *Biànzhèng lùn* + clinical sections + technique sections.

**The Japanese paediatric contributions.** Two works document the East-Asian medical exchange in paediatric medicine:
- *Yòukē zhèngzhì dàquán* 幼科證治大全 ([[KR3ej041]], 1709) of [[下津壽泉]] Shimotsu Jusen — Edo-period systematic paediatric compendium in the *kobō-ha* style, organised by disorder with careful preservation of multiple variant recensions of each prescription (*Yīlín*, *Xuēshì*, *Yītǒng*, *Dàquán*, *Dàquán* Yángshì, and Japanese supplements); a useful primary source for the philology of late-Míng / early-Qīng paediatric prescription transmission.
- *Zhōngguó érkē yījiàn* 中國兒科醫鑑 ([[KR3ej042]], 1930–1980) of [[大塜敬節]] Ōtsuka Keisetsu — Shōwa-era integration of modern Western paediatric pathology (the 10–11-day measles incubation period, *Koplik's spots* on the buccal mucosa, Celsius temperature ranges) with the *zhèngzhì* paediatric measles formulary (*Shēngmá gégēn tāng*, *Yínqiào sǎn*, *Sāngjú yǐn*) and the ChénWénzhōng / post-Qián-Yǐ paediatric traditions.

**The Republican-era TCM-college reform.** A coherent early- to mid-twentieth-century Chinese-medicine textbook tradition, composed for the new urban TCM colleges of Shànghǎi and the Republican-era *guóyī* movement:
- *Érkē cuìjīng* 兒科萃精 ([[KR3ej012]], 1929) of [[陳守真]] Chén Shǒuzhēn — 36-*mén* paediatric handbook integrating classical *QiánYǐ* doctrine with twentieth-century vaccination practice (*zhǒngdòu* in the modern sense).
- *Érkē yàolüè* 兒科要略 ([[KR3ej002]], 1934) of [[吳克潛]] Wú Kèqián — nine-chapter Shànghǎi-TCM-college textbook interleaving the Chinese paediatric tradition with selective borrowings from Western paediatrics.
- *Bǎochì xīnshū* 保赤新書 ([[KR3ej072]], 1924–1932) of [[惲鐵樵]] Yùn Tiěqiáo — the most influential Republican-era *guóyī* polymath's paediatric monograph; one of the principal vehicles of the late-Qīng / Republican TCM-college reform.
- *Mázhěn zhuānlùn* 麻疹專論 ([[KR3ej058]], 1940) of [[李聰甫]] Lǐ Cōngfǔ — Republican-era three-phase measles management treatise (*chūqí / zhōngqí / mòqí*); composed at Xīnhuà under war-time displacement; includes the 1934 prophetic essay *Wēnyìbìng zhī shèngxíng* on epidemic disease.
- *Yòukē gàilùn* 幼科概論 ([[KR3ej003]], 1912–1949) — anonymous early-Republican paediatric handbook in the *jiǎngyì* lecture-text genre, polemic against the *jīngfēng* nosological category.

## Topics

- **Children's bodies as *chúnyáng* 純陽 and *zhìyīn* 稚陰.** The paediatric anthropology articulated by Qián Yǐ [[KR3ej020]] — the infant body as yáng-replete and rapidly developing, with the *yīn* (substance, fluid, structure) lagging behind — supplies the doctrinal premise from which all subsequent paediatric pathology and therapy are deduced. The standard formulation is *zàngfǔ jiāonèn, xíngqì wèi chōng* 臟腑嬌嫩，形氣未充 (organs are delicate and tender; physical form and *qì* are not yet replete), already articulated by [[KR3ej070]] Sūn Sīmiǎo.
- **The QiánYǐ paediatric *zàngfǔ* doctrine.** The systematic application of the Five Phases to paediatric pathology, with the paediatric-specific adjustment *gānyǒuyú, píbùzú, shènxū* 肝有餘脾不足腎虛 (the liver is over-supplied, the spleen is under-supplied, the kidney is depleted). Runs from [[KR3ej020]] through the Sòng encyclopaedias ([[KR3ej010]], [[KR3ej017]]) and the Yuán ([[KR3ej007]]) and Míng paediatric corpus ([[KR3ej015]], [[KR3ej001]], [[KR3ej014]], [[KR3ej037]]) to the imperial Qīng canonisation in [[KR3ej029]].
- **The doctrine of *biànzhèng* 變蒸.** The 31- (or 32-) day developmental crisis cycle, repeating ten times to *zàngfǔ qì zú* organ-vitality completion, accompanied by mild fevers that should not be treated as pathological. Articulated in [[KR3ej023]], canonicalised in [[KR3ej020]], reaffirmed in [[KR3ej068]] *Biànzhèng lùn*. A central diagnostic question is when an infant fever is normal *biànzhèng* and when it is genuine paediatric pathology.
- **The *jīngfēng* 驚風 nosological controversy.** The classification of paediatric convulsive disorders into *jíjīng* 急驚 acute, *mànjīng* 慢驚 chronic, *mànpífēng* 慢脾風 chronic-spleen — articulated by [[KR3ej020]] and elaborated through the Míng paediatric mainstream — became the principal target of Qīng paediatric polemic. [[KR3ej011]] Xià Dǐng (*Pì zhū jīng míng zhī miù*) and [[KR3ej013]] Chén Fùzhèng (the *jīng / chù* reform) attempted to dissolve the *jīngfēng* category into more analytically defensible diagnostic schemes; the polemic continues in [[KR3ej003]] and the Republican TCM-college textbooks.
- **The QiánYǐ vs ChénWénzhòng smallpox doctrinal opposition.** The structural fault-line of Chinese smallpox medicine: cooling-purging following Qián Yǐ [[KR3ej020]] (and elaborated by Liú Wánsù and Zhū Dānxī) versus warming-supplementation following Chén Wénzhòng [[KR3ej053]] / [[KR3ej056]]. Mediating positions in [[KR3ej063]] Wāng Jī (moderate-supplementation), [[KR3ej066]] Wàn Quán's systematising masterwork, [[KR3ej071]] Xú Qiān, and the imperial canonisation in [[KR3ej048]].
- **Variolation (*zhòngdòu* 種痘) and the eighteenth-century Chinese smallpox technology.** The four standard variolation techniques (*dòuyī fǎ* clothes-method, *dòujiāng fǎ* lymph-method, *hànmiáo fǎ* dry-scab-method, *shuǐmiáo fǎ* wet-scab-method) were codified as state-sanctioned imperial-medical orthopraxy in [[KR3ej048]] *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ yàojué* (1742) and the dedicated [[KR3ej055]] (1742). This is the principal documentary source for the eighteenth-century Chinese variolation tradition that Edward Jenner later modified into Western smallpox vaccination. Variolation is presupposed as established orthopraxy by [[KR3ej008]] and the late-Qīng popular handbooks; vaccination in the modern sense enters the Chinese paediatric corpus only in the Republican-era [[KR3ej012]].
- **Smallpox as a Manchu political question.** Wilkinson p. 17 documents how the Manchu rulers (not immune to smallpox in their steppe homeland) developed elaborate *bìdòu suǒ* 避痘所 smallpox isolation facilities at the Nányuàn 南苑 and elsewhere; Shùnzhì died of smallpox at 22 and Tóngzhì at 18, while Kāngxī and Xiánfēng survived attacks but bore facial scars. The political stakes of the Qīng *dòuzhěn* literature ([[KR3ej048]], [[KR3ej055]], [[KR3ej047]]) cannot be separated from this dynastic-medical concern.
- **Measles (*mázhěn* 麻疹) and scarlatina (*shāzhěn* 痧疹) differentiation.** A characteristic late-Qīng concern, addressed in [[KR3ej047]] (72 measles symptom-patterns), [[KR3ej049]] (Yè Lín's systematic *má / yǐnzhěn / shímá / fēngzhěn* differentiation with explicit *zhōngwài* synthesis), [[KR3ej051]] (Líng Dé's Hángzhōu regional reform), [[KR3ej062]] (Wǔ Zhīwàng's late-Míng three-section frame), and culminating in the Republican-era [[KR3ej058]] (Lǐ Cōngfǔ's three-phase clinical protocol) and [[KR3ej042]] (Ōtsuka's Sino-Japanese-Western synthesis).
- **Paediatric *tuīná* 推拿 and the question of non-medication therapy.** The doctrinal premise that paediatric *tuīná* can serve as primary therapy (because the infant cannot tolerate decoction medicine; because *shǒuzú liánluò zàngfǔ, nèiyìng wàitōng* 手足聯絡臟腑，內應外通) is articulated by [[KR3ej011]] (Xià Dǐng's *Tuīná dàiyào fù*) and [[KR3ej068]] (Luò Rúlóng); the early-Qīng *tuīná* canon [[KR3ej067]], [[KR3ej068]], [[KR3ej069]] codifies the genre. The legendary [[九天玄女]] Jiǔtiān Xuánnǚ origin myth and the gender-differentiated *nán zuǒ nǚ yòu* diagnostic scheme are characteristic doctrinal furniture. The early-Qīng paediatric *tuīná* tradition is the direct ancestor of the contemporary PRC TCM paediatric massage curriculum.
- **The *zhǐwén* 指紋 (three-pass finger-vein) diagnostic controversy.** The paediatric-specific *sānguān zhǐwén* 三關指紋 (three-pass finger-vein) diagnostic method, where the colour and length of the venous patterns visible through the infant's thumb-index web are read as paediatric pulse-equivalents, is a major target of [[KR3ej011]] Xià Dǐng's polemic. The supplementary *Hǔkǒu màiwén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 of [[KR3ej016]] Zhōu Zhèn defends and refines the method; the modern PRC TCM paediatric curriculum continues to teach it.
- **The *fēngqìmìng* 風氣命 three-position scheme.** The paediatric pulse-equivalent system in which the three finger-vein positions correspond to *fēngguān* (wind-pass, mild), *qìguān* (qi-pass, moderate), and *mìngguān* (life-pass, severe) — articulated already by [[KR3ej070]] Sūn Sīmiǎo and inherited through the SòngMíng paediatric corpus.
- **Paediatric *gān* 疳 chronic-malnutrition syndromes.** A characteristic paediatric-specific disease category covering chronic malnutrition, failure-to-thrive, and chronic digestive disorders; structured into multiple sub-types in the Sòng paediatric corpus ([[KR3ej017]]) and central in the Qīng standard ([[KR3ej029]]).
- **Confucian moral framing of paediatric practice.** The title *Rénduān lù* 仁端錄 ("Record of the Foundations of Humaneness") of [[KR3ej071]] Xú Qiān, and the *Yī wéi rénshù* 醫為仁術 (medicine as the art of humaneness) framing, explicitly cast paediatric practice as a moral as well as technical undertaking. The Republican-era handbooks (*cíyòu* 慈幼 "compassionate childcare" — [[KR3ej022]], [[KR3ej027]] — and *bǎochì* 保赤 "protecting the newborn" — [[KR3ej072]]) preserve this rhetorical framing.
- **Family-school transmission and regional medical lineages.** The principal paediatric family lineages structure substantial portions of the division: the Luótián Wàn family (five works [[KR3ej001]], [[KR3ej043]], [[KR3ej044]], [[KR3ej065]], [[KR3ej066]]; cf. [[KR3ei040]], [[KR3ei074]] in gynecology); the Sūzhōu Xuē family (three paediatric works [[KR3ej015]], [[KR3ej021]], [[KR3ej030]]; cf. the gynecological [[KR3ei009]], [[KR3ei039]] and others); the Wūchéng Wáng family ([[KR3ej014]]); the Tóngyuán Zhèng family ([[KR3ej038]]; cf. [[KR3ei037]]). The Húběi ChǔWǎn regional reformist tradition culminates in [[KR3ej011]]; the Lǐngnán Daoist-influenced reformist tradition in [[KR3ej013]]; the Hángzhōu *zhézhōng* tradition in [[KR3ej051]].
- **Republican-era TCM-college reform.** The early-twentieth-century *guóyī* movement's project of presenting Chinese paediatric medicine as a systematic, modernised clinical discipline, integrating classical Chinese paediatric doctrine with selective borrowings from Western paediatrics. Represented by [[KR3ej002]], [[KR3ej003]], [[KR3ej012]], [[KR3ej058]], [[KR3ej072]] (and parallel to the gynecological reform of [[KR3ei041]]). The shared vocabulary draws on the late-Qīng *yīhuà* 醫話 idiom and the early-Republican *jiǎngyì* 講義 lecture-text genre.
- **Sino-Japanese-Korean medical-philological transmission.** Several paediatric works document the East-Asian transmission circuit. [[KR3ej024]] *Huóyòu kǒuyì* was lost in defective copies in China, preserved by the Korean *Yīfāng lèijù* (1445), and reconstituted in early-nineteenth-century Edo by the Tanba family. [[KR3ej005]] *Bǎoyòu xīnpiān* is a Korean-recension paediatric handbook (cosmological-circulation Korean methodology). [[KR3ej041]] (Edo) preserves multiple variant Chinese paediatric prescription recensions. [[KR3ej042]] (Shōwa Japan) integrates Western paediatric pathology with the classical Chinese paediatric formulary. Korean and Japanese recensions are therefore not peripheral but central textual witnesses for the Chinese paediatric tradition.
- **Cross-divisional connections.** Several core paediatric works are extracts or re-organisations of works held elsewhere in the KR catalog: [[KR3ej029]], [[KR3ej048]], [[KR3ej055]] are *juǎn* 50–55, 56–63, and a separate chapter of [[KR3e0090]] *Yīzōng jīnjiàn*; [[KR3ej037]] is one of six sections of [[KR3e0078]] *Liùkē Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng*; [[KR3ej038]] is the paediatric volume of the *Tóngyuán yīshū* (cf. [[KR3ei037]]); [[KR3ej039]] is the paediatric volume of *Jīnguì qǐyuè* (cf. [[KR3ei038]]). Like gynecology, paediatrics is constituted both through specialised monographs and through the systematic disaggregation of comprehensive medical encyclopaedias.
