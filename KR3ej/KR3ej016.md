# Yòukē zhǐnán 幼科指南
*Compass for Paediatrics*
by [[周震]] (撰); 1925 reprint preface by [[蔬香居士]] for [[衡廬主人]]

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* early-Qīng paediatric handbook by **Zhōu Zhèn** 周震 (*hào* Shèn-zhāi 慎齋), composed in the *Shùn-zhì* 18 (1661) — *Kāng-xī* 18 (1679) range and first printed in 1789 by the Yí-xīng Dào-shēng-táng. The *hxwd* recension preserves a Republican-era 1925 reprinting with a fresh preface by **Shū-xiāng jū-shì** 蔬香居士 for the contemporary editor **Héng-lú zhǔ-rén** 衡廬主人 who had paired the work with his own *Fù-kē zhǐ-nán* 婦科指南 (gynaecology compass). The work covers paediatric four-diagnostic procedures (*sì-zhěn* 四診), pulse and finger-vein diagnosis, the five-*zàng* meridian disorders, *jīng-fēng* 驚風, and the principal miscellaneous paediatric disorders.

### Prefaces

The *hxwd* `_000.txt` carries the **1925 reprint preface** by **Shū-xiāng jū-shì** 蔬香居士, dated 民國十四年歲次乙丑孟夏月 = 1925 fourth lunar month. It frames paediatric medicine in *Lún-yǔ* 論語 idiom (the *yǎng* 養 / *yī* 醫 of *nǚ-zǐ yǔ xiǎo-rén* 女子與小人 being notoriously difficult), and recounts the biography of **Héng-lú zhǔ-rén** 衡廬主人 — twenty years of *fēng-chén* 風塵 (dusty travels) in government administration and judiciary, frustrated ambition, retirement into medical study, composition of the *Fù-kē zhǐ-nán* and *Yòu-kē zhǐ-nán*. The preface presents the *Yòu-kē zhǐ-nán* as the rediscovered and re-presented form of Zhōu Zhèn's Qīng original. After the preface, the body of the work opens with *Sì-zhěn zǒng-kuò* 四診總括 (general outline of the four diagnostic procedures), followed by separate sections on *chá-sè* 察色 (visual diagnosis from facial coloration), *tīng-shēng* 聽聲 (auscultation of the five vocal sounds correlated with the five *zàng*), *shěn-bìng / wèn* 審病問 (clinical questioning), and *qiè-mài* 切脈 (pulse-taking).

### Abstract

The *Yòukē zhǐnán* is one of the standard Qīng-period paediatric handbooks of the post-*Kāng-xī* era. Its *juǎn* 1 contains paediatric *gē-fù* 歌賦 (rhymed verses and rhapsodies) including the distinctive *Hǔ-kǒu mài-wén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 (Verses on the Tiger's-mouth Pulse Pattern) — a supplement to the conventional *sān-guān zhǐ-wén* 三關指紋 finger-vein diagnostic doctrine, identifying *qīng-zǐ* (blue-purple) patterns as *xīn-gān* (heart-liver) disorders, *huáng-bái* (yellow-white) as *gān-fèi* (liver-lung), and *hēi* (black) as *shèn* (kidney). *Juǎn* 2 covers miscellaneous paediatric disorders. *Juǎn* 3–4 cover the five-*zàng* meridian disorders (*xīn-gān-fèi-pí-shèn jīng* 心肝肺脾腎經) with case-histories appended. The work's *píng-zhèng* (moderate, balanced) approach to prescription distinguishes it from the more polemical works of the Wàn-family, Chén Fù-zhèng, and Xià Dǐng paediatric lineages. Standard modern edition: *Yòu-kē yī-xué zhǐ-nán* 幼科醫學指南 jiào-zhù, ed. Zhèng Chūn-sù 鄭春素 et al. (Zhōng-yī gǔ-jí chū-bǎn-shè, 2013, ISBN 9787513220149) based on the 1789 *Qián-lóng* Dào-shēng-táng base-text with collation against the 1789 Lì-yáng Bǎo-chì-táng Yù-shù-táng edition and the 1814 *Jiā-qìng* 19 Lì-yáng Bǎo-chì-táng edition.
