# KR1h 四書類 Sìshū lèi — Classics: Four Books

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR1h covers the *Sìshū* 四書 ("Four Books") branch of the Classics, the latest of the ten sub-categories of the *jīngbù* 經部 in the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 classification. As Wilkinson observes (Wilkinson § 28.4.4, § 28.5), the *Sìshū* — *Dàxué* 大學, *Zhōngyōng* 中庸, *Lúnyǔ* 論語, *Mèngzǐ* 孟子 — were first assembled as a curricular unit by Chéng Hào 程顥 and Chéng Yí 程頤 in the Northern Sòng and given their canonical form by Zhū Xī 朱熹, who issued them as a set in 1190 under the title *Sìzǐ* 四子 with his own commentaries. After the 1227 acceptance of Zhū's commentaries as the standard primer and especially after 1315, when the Yuán reinstituted the examination system on the basis of the *Sìshū* with Zhū Xī's notes, the four texts became the single most-memorized body of writing in late imperial East Asia, supplanting in pedagogical centrality even the *Wǔjīng* 五經 to which the *Classics* branch had originally been devoted.

The KR1h division therefore has a distinctive shape compared with the older *jīng* sub-categories: there is no pre-Hàn classic to which all later commentaries directly attach. The *Lúnyǔ* 論語 and *Mèngzǐ* 孟子 each function as a base text in their own right (and were not formally placed in the Classics branch until the Sòng — *Mèngzǐ* was moved out of the masters branch only at that point, Wilkinson § 28.5), while *Dàxué* and *Zhōngyōng* are short chapters extracted from the *Lǐjì* 禮記 whose independent transmission is essentially a Sòng-and-later phenomenon and is represented in KR1h through later commentarial collections rather than through a separate base recension. The bulk of the division is consequently medieval-to-Qīng: a Hàn–Táng floor of *Lúnyǔ* and *Mèngzǐ* commentary (Zhào Qí 趙岐, Hé Yàn 何晏, Huáng Kǎn 皇侃, Xíng Bǐng 邢昺), an enormous SòngYuán build-up of Dàoxué readings centred on Zhū Xī, the 1415 Ming imperial *Sìshū dàquán* 四書大全 that froze the orthodoxy for the next four centuries, and a Qīng response that ranges from Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其's defence of Zhū-school orthodoxy to Máo Qílíng 毛奇齡 and Yán Ruòqú 閻若璩's evidential-philology critique. The division thus traces the entire arc by which Neo-Confucianism took institutional shape (Wilkinson § 28.7.3) and then came under empirical scrutiny in the late empire.

## Important texts and text clusters

**Base texts.** The bare canonical texts on which the division depends:

- *Mèngzǐ (zhèngwén)* 孟子(正文) [[KR1h0001]] — the Mencius extracted from the RuǎnYuán 阮元 1816 reprint of the Sòng *Mèngzǐ zhùshū*; the principal base text for all the *Mèngzǐ*-related commentaries below.
- *Lúnyǔ (zhèngwén)* 論語(正文) [[KR1h0004]] — the Analects base text from the same series.

**Hàn–Táng commentarial floor on the *Lúnyǔ* and *Mèngzǐ*.** The pre-Sòng layer in the division:

- *Mèngzǐ* 孟子 (Zhào Qí zhù) [[KR1h0002]] and *Mèngzǐ zhùshū* 孟子注疏 [[KR1h0003]] — Zhào Qí's 趙岐 (d. 201) Hàn annotations, with the late-Sòng *yīnyì* of Sūn Shì 孫奭 attached; the only complete Hàn commentary on the *Mèngzǐ* to survive.
- *Lúnyǔ* (Hé Yàn jíjiě) 論語 [[KR1h0005]], *Lúnyǔ jíjiě yìshū* 論語集解義疏 [[KR1h0006]] (Huáng Kǎn 皇侃, c. 545), and *Lúnyǔ zhùshū* 論語注疏 [[KR1h0007]] (Hé Yàn / Xíng Bǐng) — the foundational layered commentary tradition on the *Analects* from CáoWèi through Northern Sòng. Huáng Kǎn's *yìshū*, transmitted in Japan and re-imported in the 18th c., is the principal southern-dynasties witness.
- *Lúnyǔ bǐjiě* 論語筆解 [[KR1h0008]] — the Hán Yù 韓愈 / Lǐ Áo 李翱 "pen-and-brush glosses," the earliest text in the division standing inside the late-Táng Confucian revival that Wilkinson treats as the genealogical root of Neo-Confucianism (§ 28.7.3).

**The Zhū Xī core and its immediate satellites.** The Southern Sòng cluster that fixed the Four Books as a unit:

- *Sìshū zhāngjù jízhù* 四書章句集注 [[KR1h0015]] — Zhū Xī's section-and-sentence commentary, the most consequential single work in the division and the textual basis of six centuries of examinations.
- *Sìshū huòwèn* 四書或問 [[KR1h0016]] — Zhū's companion question-and-answer volume defending and extending the *Jízhù*'s readings.
- *LùnMèng jīngyì* 論孟精義 [[KR1h0017]] — Zhū's earlier (1163–1172) gathering of Northern Sòng masters' (esp. Chéng-brothers) glosses on *Lúnyǔ* and *Mèngzǐ*, the workbook out of which the *Jízhù* grew.
- *Zhōngyōng jíluè* 中庸輯略 [[KR1h0018]] — Shí Zǐzhòng's 石𡼖 anthology cut and re-edited by Zhū Xī, fixing the Northern-Sòng readings of the *Zhōngyōng*.

**Sòng critiques and alternative readings.** Works that argue with, or partly against, the Zhū consensus:

- *Mèngzǐ yīnyì* 孟子音義 [[KR1h0009]] — Sūn Shì's 孫奭 (962–1033) pre-Zhū *yīnyì*, the principal Northern Sòng technical apparatus on the *Mèngzǐ*.
- *Lúnyǔ shíyí* 論語拾遺 [[KR1h0010]] / *Mèngzǐ jiě* 孟子解 [[KR1h0011]] — Sū Zhé 蘇轍's short interventions, characteristic of Shǔ-school 蜀學 alternatives to ChéngZhū.
- *Lúnyǔ quánjiě* 論語全解 [[KR1h0012]] — Chén Xiángdào 陳祥道's reading from inside the Wáng Ānshí 王安石 *Xīnxué* circle.
- *Zūn Mèng biàn* 尊孟辨 [[KR1h0014]] — Yú Yǔnwén 余允文's polemic against SīMǎ Guāng 司馬光's *Yí Mèng* 疑孟, the defining text of the 12th-c. controversy over Mencius' canonicity.
- *Guǐsì Lúnyǔ jiě* 癸巳論語解 [[KR1h0021]] and *Guǐsì Mèngzǐ shuō* 癸巳孟子說 [[KR1h0022]] — Zhāng Shì 張栻's pair, the major HúXiāng 湖湘 school readings and Zhū Xī's most important dialogue partner.
- *Shígǔ Lúnyǔ dáwèn* 石鼓論語答問* [[KR1h0019]] — Dài Xī 戴溪's classroom catechism from the Shígǔ shūyuàn 石鼓書院.

**Late-Sòng / Yuán Cheng-Zhu consolidation.** The big sub-commentarial compendia by which Zhū Xī's reading became scholastically codified:

- *Sìshū jíbiān* 四書集編 [[KR1h0024]] — Zhēn Déxiù's 眞德秀, the first major attempt to assemble Zhū's apparatus into a single book.
- *Sìshū zuǎnshū* 四書纂疏 [[KR1h0028]] — Zhào Shùnsūn 趙順孫's, completed in the last decades of the Sòng.
- *Sìshū jíyì jīngyào* 四書集義精要 [[KR1h0031]] — Liú Yīn 劉因's Yuán-period digest.
- *Sìshū tōng* 四書通 [[KR1h0034]] — Hú Bǐngwén 胡炳文's authoritative Yuán-period sub-commentary, often paired with…
- *Sìshū tōngzhèng* 四書通證 [[KR1h0035]] (Zhāng Cúnzhōng 張存中), *Sìshū zuǎnjiān* 四書纂箋 [[KR1h0038]] (Zhān Dàochuán 詹道傳) and *Dú Sìshū cóngshuō* 讀四書叢說 [[KR1h0033]] (Xǔ Qiān 許謙) as the canonical Yuán reference shelf.
- *Sìshū biànyí* 四書辨疑 [[KR1h0032]] — Chén Tiānxiáng 陳天祥's, a rare Yuán *critique* of Zhū's commentary written inside the same Cheng-Zhu world.
- *Sìshū jīngyí guàntōng* 四書經疑貫通* [[KR1h0037]] (1333) — Wáng Chōngyún 王充耘's, designed specifically for the *jīngyí* question-format of the Yuán-restored examinations.

**The Ming imperial frame.** The text that defines the Ming-Qīng orthodoxy:

- *Sìshū dàquán* 四書大全 [[KR1h0043]] — Hú Guǎng 胡廣 et al., 1415, the Yǒnglè-era imperial compendium that bound a curated set of SòngYuán sub-commentaries to Zhū Xī's *Jízhù* and made the result the sole reference for the examination essay for the next five centuries.

**Ming and Qīng critical and devotional readings.**

- *Sìshū méngyǐn* 四書蒙引 [[KR1h0044]] — Cài Qīng 蔡清, the principal mid-Ming examination-school exposition of the *Dàquán*.
- *Lúnyǔ lèikǎo* 論語類考 [[KR1h0047]] and *Mèngzǐ zájì* 孟子雜記 [[KR1h0048]] — Chén Shìyuán 陳士元 (1544), pioneering thematic-philological treatments.
- *Lúnyǔ xué'àn* 論語學案 [[KR1h0051]] — Liú Zōngzhōu 劉宗周, the late-Ming Dōnglín-adjacent 心學-tinged reading.
- *Mèngzǐ shīshuō* 孟子師說 [[KR1h0055]] — Huáng Zōngxī 黃宗羲's record of Liú Zōngzhōu's *Mèngzǐ* lectures, a key Ming–Qīng transition text. (The catalog gives the misspelling 黃宗義 for 黃宗羲, corrected here and in the per-text note.)
- *Rìjiǎng Sìshū jiěyì* 日講四書解義 [[KR1h0053]] — Lāshālǐ 喇沙里 et al., 1677, the Kāngxī court's authoritative imperial-lecture exposition.
- *Sìshū jiǎngyì kùnmiǎn lù* 四書講義困勉錄 [[KR1h0057]] and *Sōngyáng jiǎngyì* 松陽講義 [[KR1h0058]] — Lù Lǒngqí 陸隴其, the canonical Qīng defence of Zhū-school orthodoxy.
- *Lúnyǔ jīqiú piān* 論語稽求篇 [[KR1h0060]], *Sìshū shèngyán* 四書賸言 [[KR1h0061]] and *Dàxué zhèngwén* 大學證文 [[KR1h0062]] — Máo Qílíng 毛奇齡's polemical evidential critiques of Zhū Xī's readings, particularly of the *Dàxué* re-arrangement.
- *Sìshū shìdì* 四書釋地 [[KR1h0063]] — Yán Ruòqú 閻若璩's geographical-historical investigations of *Sìshū* references, a model exercise of Qīng *kǎozhèng* applied to the canon.
- *Xiāngdǎng túkǎo* 鄉黨圖考 [[KR1h0067]] — Jiāng Yǒng 江永's diagrammatic philological reconstruction of the ritual practices behind *Lúnyǔ* "Xiāngdǎng," one of the high points of Qīng evidential scholarship on the *Sìshū*.

## Important persons

- [[趙岐]] (d. 201) — author of the only complete extant Hàn commentary on the *Mèngzǐ*, the textual base of all later *Mèng* commentary.
- [[何晏]] (d. 249) — chief compiler of the *Lúnyǔ jíjiě*, the CáoWèi synthesis that displaced earlier Hàn readings and became the standard *Lúnyǔ* commentary through the Táng.
- [[皇侃]] (488–545) — author of the Liáng-dynasty *Lúnyǔ yìshū*, the principal Six-Dynasties subcommentary, lost in China but transmitted in Japan and re-imported in the Qīng.
- [[陸德明]] (c. 556–627) — *yīnyì* glossator whose work was absorbed into the standard *Lúnyǔ zhùshū*.
- [[邢昺]] (932–1010) — author of the Northern-Sòng *zhèngyì* on the *Lúnyǔ*, completing the *Shísānjīng zhùshū* layer for the *Analects*.
- [[孫奭]] (962–1033) — the *Mèngzǐ yīnyì* and the *zhèngyì* traditionally attached to Zhào Qí; the figure most responsible for the *Mèngzǐ*'s formal inscription into the canon in the early Northern Sòng.
- [[韓愈]] (768–824) and [[李翱]] (772–841) — joint authors of the *Lúnyǔ bǐjiě*, the bridge between the late-Táng Confucian revival and the Sòng *Sìshū*.
- [[蘇轍]] (1039–1112) — author of the *Lúnyǔ shíyí* and *Mèngzǐ jiě*; the Shǔ-school alternative to ChéngZhū inside the division.
- [[張栻]] (1133–1180) — the leading Hú-Xiāng-school reader of the *Sìshū*, Zhū Xī's most consequential intellectual interlocutor.
- [[朱熹]] (1130–1200) — the indispensable figure of the division. His *Sìshū zhāngjù jízhù*, *Sìshū huòwèn*, *LùnMèng jīngyì*, and *Zhōngyōng jíluè* between them define the textual unit, the standard reading, and the curricular form that all subsequent KR1h works react to.
- [[眞德秀]] (1178–1235) — *Sìshū jíbiān*; the prototype Sòng *Sìshū* sub-commentarial compendium.
- [[金履祥]] (1232–1303) — *Dàxué shūyì* and *LùnMèng jízhù kǎozhèng*; the most precise late-Sòng / early-Yuán philological exegete of Zhū Xī.
- [[胡炳文]] (1250–1333) — *Sìshū tōng*; the canonical Yuán sub-commentary, repeatedly cited in the 1415 *Dàquán*.
- [[許謙]] (1270–1337) — *Dú Sìshū cóngshuō*; the JīnHuá 金華 lineage reading.
- [[胡廣]] (1370–1418) — chief editor (*fèngchì zhuàn* 奉敕撰) of the *Sìshū dàquán*; through his compilation the division acquired the orthodoxy under which it was read until 1905.
- [[蔡清]] (1453–1508) — *Sìshū méngyǐn*; the most influential mid-Ming examination-essay handbook.
- [[劉宗周]] (1578–1645) — *Lúnyǔ xué'àn*; the major late-Ming devotional-philosophical reading of the *Analects*.
- [[黃宗羲]] (1610–1695) — *Mèngzǐ shīshuō*; preserver of Liú Zōngzhōu's *Mèngzǐ* lectures and pivotal Ming-Qīng transition figure.
- [[陸隴其]] (1630–1693) — *Sìshū jiǎngyì kùnmiǎn lù* and *Sōngyáng jiǎngyì*; the chief Qīng defender of strict Zhū-school orthodoxy.
- [[毛奇齡]] (1623–1716) — *Lúnyǔ jīqiú piān*, *Sìshū shèngyán*, *Dàxué zhèngwén*; the most aggressive Qīng critic of Zhū's *Sìshū* arrangements.
- [[閻若璩]] (1636–1704) — *Sìshū shìdì*; the model of geographic-historical *kǎozhèng* applied to the *Sìshū*.
- [[江永]] (1681–1762) — *Xiāngdǎng túkǎo*; the pinnacle of mid-Qīng evidential scholarship on the *Lúnyǔ*'s ritual material.

## Topics

- **Formation of the unit.** The historical question of how four originally heterogeneous texts — two pre-Hàn canonical books (*Lúnyǔ*, *Mèngzǐ*) and two chapters of a Hàn ritual anthology (*Dàxué*, *Zhōngyōng* from the *Lǐjì*) — were welded into a single curricular package by the Chéng brothers and Zhū Xī. The division's organization mirrors this history: there is no single base recension to which all commentaries attach, only the four-book *suite*. See Wilkinson § 28.4.4.

- **The *Mèngzǐ*'s elevation to canon.** Mencius' move from masters branch to *jīng* branch (Wilkinson § 28.5), the 12th-c. "doubting Mencius" (*Yí Mèng* 疑孟) controversy associated with SīMǎ Guāng, and Yú Yǔnwén's [[KR1h0014]] defence are landmark events in late-imperial Confucianism that the KR1h corpus tracks closely.

- **The structure of the *Dàxué*.** Whether Zhū Xī's emendation of the *Dàxué* — re-ordering the *jīng* / *zhuàn* structure, supplying a missing "investigation of things" (*géwù* 格物) paragraph — was textually defensible. This is the single most contested philological question in the division: Hú Wèi's *Dàxué yìzhēn* [[KR1h0056]] and Máo Qílíng's *Dàxué zhèngwén* [[KR1h0062]] are the two principal Qīng investigations on each side of the question.

- **Cheng-Zhu orthodoxy versus alternative readings.** The division stages the long contest between the Dàoxué reading (ChéngZhū, *lǐqì* 理氣, *xìnglǐ* 性理) and its critics: Northern-Sòng Shǔxué (Sū Zhé), HúXiāng (Zhāng Shì), Yuán-period internal critics (Chén Tiānxiáng [[KR1h0032]]), late-Ming *xīnxué*-inflected readings (Liú Zōngzhōu, Huáng Zōngxī), and Qīng evidential critique (Máo Qílíng, Yán Ruòqú).

- **The examination essay.** From 1315 onward almost every text in KR1h is shaped by the *Sìshū*'s role as the basis of the *kējǔ* 科舉 essay. Works specifically engineered for the exam — Wáng Chōngyún's *jīngyí* manual [[KR1h0037]], Cài Qīng's *Méngyǐn* [[KR1h0044]] — sit side by side with Lù Lǒngqí's lecture-hall expositions and the Kāngxī court's *Rìjiǎng* [[KR1h0053]]. See Wilkinson § 24.8 on the role of the *Sìshū* in late imperial education.

- **Geography, ritual realia, and *kǎozhèng*.** A characteristic Qīng turn: treating the *Sìshū* not as a metaphysical primer but as a historical-philological object. Yán Ruòqú on *Sìshū* geography [[KR1h0063]], Jiāng Yǒng on the *Xiāngdǎng* ritual code [[KR1h0067]], Chén Shìyuán's *lèikǎo* / *zájì* [[KR1h0047]], [[KR1h0048]].
