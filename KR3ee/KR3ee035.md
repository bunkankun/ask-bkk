# Zhēnjiǔ wèn-dá 針灸問答
*Questions and Answers on Acupuncture and Moxibustion*
by [[譚志光]]

## About the work
A single-juan Republican-period question-and-answer acupuncture manual by Tán Zhìguāng 譚志光 (*hào* Róng-yuán 容園), of Cháng-shā 長沙. The work's appended colophon — `KR3ee035_000.txt` — is by Xióng Xī-líng 熊希齡 (Xióng Bǐng-sān 秉三, the prominent Hú-nán reformer and Premier of the Republic of China in 1913–14), dated Mín-guó 16 (1927) summer. Xióng's colophon records meeting Tán at the Cháng-shā Xiāng-bào 湘報館 newspaper offices and at the Hú-nán Nán-xué-huì 南學會, in the company of Kāng Yǒu-wéi (Nán-hǎi), Liáng Qǐ-chāo (Xīn-huì), and Tán Sì-tóng (Líu-yáng) — placing the young Tán Zhìguāng in the inner circle of the 1898 reformist movement. In the Mín-guó 5 (1916) Chuán-shān xué-shè 船山學社 reunion, Liào Bǐ-táng 廖笏唐, Rén Shòu-guó 任壽國, Péng Shào-xiāng 彭少湘 and others rose and declared: "this Yongyuan Tán Zhìguāng has long made use of acupuncture to revive medical learning and save lives" (素以針灸之學啟振醫林、拯救人命者也). Xióng then describes the present work as "rooted in [Huángdì and] Qí-bó, tracing the source through [Huá] Tuó and Biǎn [Què], a grand composition" (根據軒岐，探源華扁，鴻篇鉅製).

### Abstract
The *Zhēnjiǔ wèn-dá* is a Hú-nán Republican-period acupuncture catechism — one of the principal local-school manuals through which the late-Qīng Hú-nán acupuncture lineage (Liào Rùnhóng [[KR3ee007]], [[KR3ee032]], [[KR3ee037]]) was transmitted into the Republican TCM curriculum. Tán Zhìguāng's question-and-answer form makes the work pedagogically accessible; the content focuses on clinical applications of acupuncture for the common Republican-era diseases (malaria, dysentery, neurasthenia, etc.) drawing on the *Língshū-Sùwèn* canonical doctrine and Tán's own Hú-nán clinical lineage. The work was issued by the Cháng-shā medical-press community in 1927.
