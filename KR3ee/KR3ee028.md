# Zhēnjiǔ jiéyào 針灸節要
*Essential Excerpts of Acupuncture and Moxibustion*
by [[高武]]

## About the work
A three-juan early-Jiā-jìng abridgement of the *NèijīngNànjīng* acupuncture doctrine, compiled by Gāo Wǔ 高武 of Sìmíng 四明 and printed in Zhèngdé 14 (1519). This is the earliest of Gāo Wǔ's three acupuncture works — the others being the *Zhēnjiǔ jùyīng* ([[KR3ee014]], 1529) and the *Zhēnjiǔ SùNàn yàozhǐ* ([[KR3ee021]], 1537). The *Jiéyào* selects the acupuncture-relevant passages from the *Sùwèn*, *Língshū*, and *Nànjīng*, organizes them topically (rather than by their original chapter-sequence), and provides minimal editorial connective prose. Gāo's stated method in the *Jùyīng* preface — "to recover the goodness of the methods that the ancients established" (古人立法之善) — is here applied for the first time: the *Jiéyào* is the source-text restoration on which the later *Jùyīng* (comparative-critical compendium) and *Yàozhǐ* (topical synthesis) build.

The work is registered in the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào cúnmù* under *Zǐbù · Yījiā lèi*; the SKQS compilers note that the *Jiéyào* "is the earliest of Gāo Wǔ's three acupuncture works and the methodological foundation for the *Jùyīng*" (在《聚英》之先). The work survives in a Zhèngdé block-print and several MíngQīng recensions.
