# Língshū jīngmài yì 靈樞經脈翼
*Wings to the Língshū Channel-Vessel Chapters*
by [[夏英]]

## About the work
A three-juan early-Míng exposition of the channel-vessel doctrine of the *Língshū jīngmài* 靈樞經脈 chapter, by Xià Yīng 夏英 (*zì* Shíyàn 時彥) of Rénhé 仁和 (Hángzhōu). Xià, a hereditary literatus-physician, set out to provide a "Wings" (翼) — a paraliterary expansion — to the *Língshūjīngmài* chapter, which he found difficult to follow because of its compressed style and textual confusion ("the language is old and deep, with intermixed and transposed lines, lacking commentary; later transmitters have not failed to lose the original meaning"). The work follows the structure of Huá Shòu 滑壽's *Shísì jīng fāhuī* 十四經發揮 ([[KR3ee026]]), treating each of the fourteen channels (twelve regular + *rèndū*) in turn. For each channel Xià gives (1) Huá Shòu's mnemonic verse from the *Shísì jīng fāhuī* with an expanded prose paraphrase; (2) the *Língshūjīngmài* original text below; (3) Huá's commentary; (4) Xià's own *zǒngkuò* 總括 ("overall summary") synthesizing the doctrine into clinical-application form. A concluding chapter on the eight extraordinary vessels (*Qíjīng bāmài*), with full-body diagrams, follows.

### Abstract
The *Língshū jīngmài yì* is an early-Míng "*fāhuī*-style" companion to Huá Shòu's seminal Yuán treatise; the preface by Zhī Yǔ 知予 ("the one who knows me", likely a literary pseudonym of the printer) frames the work as an accessible introduction-to-channels for students who find Huá's terse style too compressed. Xià's contribution is the systematic re-versification and prose-paraphrase apparatus, which made the *Língshūjīngmài* doctrine clinically useable. The work was printed in the mid-15th century and reprinted multiple times in the Míng; it is not in the SKQS but is registered in the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào* *Zǐbù cúnmù*. The base text in `hxwd` follows a late-Qīng reprint. The composition bracket 1456–1500 followed here corresponds to Xià's active period in Rénhé.
