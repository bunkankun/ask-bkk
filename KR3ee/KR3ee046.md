# Jiǔ gāohuāng shùxué fǎ 灸膏肓腧穴法
*Method of Moxibustion at the Gāo-huāng Acupoint*
by [[莊綽]]

## About the work
A short Southern-Sòng monograph by the literatus-physician Zhuāng Chuò 莊綽 (*hào* Jìyù 季裕, fl. early-Southern-Sòng, also known as the author of the *Jīlè biān* 雞肋編 miscellany) on the technique of locating and moxibusting the *gāohuāng* 膏肓 acupoint (the *BL-43* point at the Sòng standardization, lying between the shoulder-blades on the *Bladder* meridian's outer line). The work opens with citations of Sūn Sīmiǎo's *Qiānjīn fāng* (`KR3ee046_000.txt`): "the *Gāohuāngshù* acupoint has no disease it cannot treat — it principally cures wasting-and-weakness, deficiency-decline, nocturnal-emission, ascending-qi cough, derangement-and-forgetfulness." The work then gives the precise localization technique: "Have the patient sit upright with the spine arched, the two arms stretched with the elbows resting on the knees so that the arms are straight, the thumbs level with the knee-tops, prop the elbows with an object so the arms do not move; from the scapula's upper corner trace down the entire scapula to its lower end — there will be four ribs and three intercostal spaces; moxa the middle interspace." Variants of the technique for patients too weak to sit upright follow. The work then quotes Wáng Wéiyī's *Míngtáng tóngrén jiǔ jīng* ([[KR3ee056]]) on the same point and synthesizes the two traditions.

### Abstract
The *Jiǔ gāohuāng shùxué fǎ* is the principal Southern-Sòng monograph on a single acupoint and is the standard source for the technique of *gāohuāng* moxibustion in all subsequent Chinese, Korean, and Japanese practice. Zhuāng Chuò compiled the work from Sūn Sīmiǎo, Wáng Wéiyī, and his own clinical practice; the precision of the bone-landmark localization made the work indispensable. The composition window 1128–1148 corresponds to Zhuāng's known active period; the work circulated in Southern-Sòng print and is preserved in the *Sìkù quánshū* under *Zǐbù · Yījiā lèi*.
