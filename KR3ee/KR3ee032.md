# Kǎozhèng zhōushēn xué-fǎ gē 考正周身穴法歌
*Verified Whole-Body Acupoint Method Song*
by [[廖潤鴻]]

## About the work
A single-juan mnemonic-verse acupuncture handbook by Liào Rùnhóng 廖潤鴻, completed and printed in Tóngzhì 13 (1874, *jiǎ-xū*), winter. This is the stand-alone version of the verse-mnemonic that Liào appended to his *Zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* ([[KR3ee007]]) and to the *Miǎn-xué-táng zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* ([[KR3ee037]]); the prefaces of all three works (here as `KR3ee032_000.txt`) are virtually identical, recounting the same autobiography of frustration in Beijing officialdom, encounter with the *Zhēnjiǔ dàchéng* ([[KR3ee027]]), and re-versification of the acupoint material into five-syllable verse. The text proceeds channel-by-channel through the standard twelve regular channels plus *rèn* and *dū*, giving for each channel the acupoint sequence and a five-character-per-line mnemonic — e.g. for the *Lung* channel: "*Hand Tài-yīn Lung Channel*, in the *yīng* (chest) area, the *yáo* point — at *Zhōng-fǔ*..." (手太陰肺經，膺間中...).

### Abstract
This is the most-copied late-Qīng acupuncture-handbook in the Húnán region; Liào's five-character verses replaced the older *Zhōushēn jīng-xué fù* 周身經穴賦 as the standard memorization material in late-Qīng acupuncture schools. The work is bound together with reduced-scale woodblock reproductions of the *Tóngrén* (Bronze Figure) acupoint diagrams "for the convenience of the desktop reader" (以便案頭搜討). The 1874 first edition was issued by the bookseller Ráo Sōng-pǔ 饒松圃 of the Húnán Lù-jiāng 淥江 community; subsequent reprints appended it to either [[KR3ee007]] or [[KR3ee037]].
