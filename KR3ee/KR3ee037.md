# Miǎnxuétáng zhēnjiǔ jíchéng 勉學堂針灸集成
*Miǎn-xué-táng Compendium of Acupuncture and Moxibustion*
by [[廖潤鴻]]

## About the work
A four-juan late-Qīng acupuncture compendium by Liào Rùnhóng 廖潤鴻, issued from his "Miǎnxuétáng" 勉學堂 ("Strive-to-Learn Hall") in Tóngzhì 13 (1874, *jiǎxū*). The preface (`KR3ee037_000.txt`) is identical in substance to that of [[KR3ee007]] (also titled *Zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* and also dated 1874) — recounting Liào's frustration in Beijing officialdom, his encounter with the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* ([[KR3ee015]]) acupuncture-curriculum verses, and his project of re-versifying them as five-character mnemonics with reduced-scale *Tóngrén* acupoint diagrams. The relationship between [[KR3ee007]] and the present [[KR3ee037]] is that the two are alternate titles for the same compilation, with [[KR3ee007]] being the basic acupoint-verse-and-diagram handbook and [[KR3ee037]] being the expanded *Miǎnxuétáng* edition that adds full clinical-syndrome chapters drawn from the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn cìjiǔ xīnfǎ yàojué* ([[KR3ee015]]). Both share the *Kǎozhèng zhōushēn xuéfǎ gē* ([[KR3ee032]]) as the verse-mnemonic core.

### Abstract
The *Miǎnxuétáng zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* is the most-reproduced of Liào Rùnhóng's late-Qīng acupuncture handbooks; it was the standard portable acupuncture-textbook in the Húnán medical-education community from the 1870s through the Republican period. Its didactic structure — five-character verse mnemonic + reduced-scale acupoint atlas + clinical-syndrome indications — is the principal late-Qīng pedagogical model from which the post-1949 PRC TCM acupuncture textbooks derive. The base edition in `hxwd` follows the original 1874 block-print.
