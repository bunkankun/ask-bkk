# Zhuāng-shì suàn-xué 莊氏算學
*Mr. Zhuāng's Mathematical Studies*
by [[莊亨陽]] (Zhuāng Hēngyáng, 1686–1746, 清, *zhuàn* 撰); posthumously assembled by Zhuāng's descendants from his working notebooks

## About the work

Zhuāng Hēngyáng's 8-juan mathematical compendium, posthumously assembled by his descendants from his working notebooks (*cán-gǎo* 殘藁) prepared during his official service as a hydraulic-engineering Circuit Intendant. The work is methodologically synthetic-and-pedagogical, drawing principally on:

- The [[KR3f0048]] *Shù-lǐ jīng-yùn*'s line-surface-solid (*xiàn-miàn-tǐ*) categorical apparatus
- The [[KR3f0047]] *Jǐ-hé yuán-běn* (Euclid 6 books) for plane-geometric foundations
- [[梅文鼎]] Méi Wéndǐng's [[KR3f0026]] *Lì-suàn quán-shū* for the broader synthetic methodology
- The [[KR3f0013]] *Xīnfǎ suànshū* for the *Qī-zhèng bù-fǎ* (Seven-Regulator step-methods) section at the end

The Sìkù 提要 explicitly frames the work's value as *condensation-and-abridgment*: the [[KR3f0048]] *Shù-lǐ jīng-yùn*'s line-surface-solid sections alone exceed 30 *juàn*; the [[KR3f0047]] *Jǐ-hé yuán-běn* is 5 *juàn*; the [[KR3f0026]] *Lì-suàn quán-shū* is 60 *juàn*. The combined volume of these foundational works is too substantial for non-specialist learners to engage with. Zhuāng Hēngyáng's *Zhuāng-shì suàn-xué* abridges this material into 8 *juàn*, "*selecting-and-raising the refined-essentials, separately gathering-and-collecting, condensed-but-not-leaking, summarized-but-not-branching*" — providing an accessible entry-point.

The 提要's verdict: "*although [the work] cannot greatly have something to discover, as a beginner-enlightenment resource for learners — [it has] particular benefit*".

For the foundational works the *Zhuāng-shì suàn-xué* draws on, see [[KR3f0026]], [[KR3f0047]], [[KR3f0048]], [[KR3f0013]]. For Zhuāng Hēngyáng's biography, see [[莊亨陽]].
