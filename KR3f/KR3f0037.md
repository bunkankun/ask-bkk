# Wǔ-jīng suàn-shù 五經算術
*The Five Classics' Mathematical Methods*
by [[甄鸞]] (Zhēn Luán, Northern Zhōu, *zhuàn* 撰); imperially commissioned annotation by [[李淳風]] (Lǐ Chúnfēng, Tang, *zhù* 注)

## About the work

Zhēn Luán's 2-juan systematic mathematical commentary on the calculatable passages of the Five Classics — the foundational Confucian canon of *Shū-jīng*, *Shī-jīng*, *Yì-jīng*, *Lùn-yǔ* (and the related *Xiào-jīng*), *Sān-lǐ* (the three Ritual Classics), *Lǐ-jì*, and *Chūn-qiū* (with its *Zuǒ-zhuàn* commentary). For each passage in the classics that involves quantitative-and-computational reasoning, Zhēn Luán supplies the mathematical apparatus needed to verify or interpret the classical statement.

The work is structured topically by source-classic and by computational-problem-type:

**Upper *juàn***: solar-and-lunar conjunction methods (*tuī rì-yuè hé-sù fǎ*); 19-year intercalary cycle (*qiú shíjiǔ nián qī rùn fǎ*); the *Shàng-shū* / *Xiào-jīng* "myriad people" notation method (*zhào-mín zhù shù yuè cì fǎ*); the *Shī Fá-tán* differing Máo and Zhèng commentaries; the *Shī Fēng-nián* notation method; the *Zhōu-yì* yarrow-stalk numbers; the *Lùn-yǔ* "thousand-chariot state" calculation; the *Zhōu-guān* chariot-cover method; the *Yí-lǐ* mourning-garment/cord measurements; the *Lǐ-jì Wáng-zhì* "ancient hundred-*lǐ* equals present 121-*lǐ* 60-*bù* 4-*chǐ* 2-*cùn* 2-*fēn*" method (and similar Zhèng-Xuán commentary verifications).

**Lower *juàn***: *Lǐ-jì Yuè-lìng* *Huáng-zhōng* pitch-pipe methods; *Lǐ-jì Lǐ-yùn* commentary methods; the *Hàn-shū* terminus-at-*Nán-shì* method; *Lǐ-jì Tóu-hú* (pitch-pot game) calculation; *Chūn-qiū Lǔ Xī-gōng* 5th-year *zhèng-yuè xīn-hài shuò* (1st-month new-moon date) method; the related "next-month new-moon" derivation; the *Lǔ Xī-gōng* 5th-year winter-solstice computation; the related "next *qì*" derivation; *Lǔ Wén-gōng* 1st-year (*sì-wèi* / *yǐ-wèi*) intercalary-month placement reconstruction; *Lǔ Wén-gōng* 6th-year intercalary-month problem; *Lǔ Xiāng-gōng* 27th-year intercalary problem; the *Jiàng-xiàn* (Jìn) old man's age-of-450-*jiǎzǐ* problem; *Wén-gōng* 11th-year *Jiàng-xiàn* old-man's-birth-month problem; *Lǔ Zhāo-gōng* 19th-year intercalary-month placement; the related *jí-rì* derivation; *Lǔ Zhāo-gōng* 19th-year (*wù-yín*) intercalary placement; *Lǔ Zhāo-gōng* 19th and 20th year monthly-new-moon and winter-solstice problems.

The work is therefore both a mathematical handbook and a Confucian-classical commentary; its purpose is to demonstrate the mathematical-arithmetic skills required for proper exegesis of the Five Classics, and conversely to demonstrate the canonical-textual grounding of the *suàn-shù* (computational arts) tradition. The Sìkù 提要 makes the explicit point that the work is "*not only useful to the mathematical-arts family — it is also sufficient to clarify the Classics-and-Histories and to verify-and-correct doubtful meanings; for the *kǎo-jù* (evidential research) study it is especially meritorious*". This is one of the most explicit *Sìkù* editorial endorsements of the integration of mathematical-and-classical scholarship.

The Sìkù recension is recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* and reorganized into 2 *juàn* per the Tang *Yì-wén zhì*'s recorded juan-count.

### Abstract

Composition window: c. 560–575 CE. Zhēn Luán was active under the Northern-Zhōu *Wǔ-dì* (560-578); his death is dated c. 575. The work was composed during this productive Northern-Zhōu period, before Lǐ Chúnfēng's Tang-imperial annotation (c. 656).

The work's significance:

(a) **Mathematical-Confucian classical synthesis**: the *Wǔ-jīng suàn-shù* is the principal pre-modern Chinese work that systematically applies mathematical analysis to the Confucian classical canon. By demonstrating that proper classical exegesis requires mathematical apparatus, the work positions mathematics as an essential component of Confucian scholarship rather than as a separate technical specialty. This integration of mathematics-and-classical-scholarship would shape Chinese mathematical practice through the late imperial period.

(b) **Pitch-pipe and calendrical material as classical exegesis**: the work's substantial treatment of pitch-pipe (*lǜ-lǚ*) and calendrical (*lì*) computations demonstrates that these specialist domains are direct extensions of the canonical Confucian classical inheritance — the *Lǐ-jì Yuè-lìng* and the *Chūn-qiū* are the principal sources for both. This framing establishes the *lǜ-lì* tradition as part of mainstream Confucian scholarship, not a separate technical concern.

(c) **The Sìkù editorial endorsement**: the 提要's strong statement that the work is "*especially meritorious for kǎo-jù study*" — citing the work's preservation of pre-Tang readings of the *Hòu-Hàn-zhì* and *Lǐ-jì yì-shū* that have been corrupted in present-day transmission — places the *Wǔ-jīng suàn-shù* within the high-Qīng *kǎo-jù* (evidential research) project. This editorial framing is one of the more explicit endorsements of the integration of mathematical and philological scholarship in the *Sìkù tíyào* corpus.
