# Dù Yù jí 杜預集
*Collected Works of Du Yu (Reconstructed)*
by [[杜預]] (撰)

## About the work

A reconstructed collection (*jíyìběn* 輯佚本) of writings attributed to Dù Yù 杜預 (222–284 CE), the Western Jìn 西晉 general, hydraulic engineer, and commentator on the *Zuǒzhuàn* 左傳. The surviving fragments in a single *juǎn* include memorials and petitions such as 〈舉賢良方正表〉 (Memorial Recommending Worthy and Upright Men) and 〈表〉 citing administrative projects — notably irrigation and canal construction east of Luòyáng 洛陽 (the "chìmǎ zhōu" waterway 赤馬舟). The collection also contains fragments cited from the *Chūxué jì* 初學記 and *Wénxuǎn* 文選 annotations. A note in the collection cautions that Zhāng Pǔ 張溥 mistakenly incorporated one item from Lù Yún 陸雲's 〈薦張瞻文〉 into the *Dù Yù jí* in his *Bǎisān jiā jí* edition; the present reconstruction excludes it.

### Abstract

Dù Yù 杜預 (222–284; *zì* Yuánkǎi 元凱; CBDB id 21190; enfeoffed Marquis of Dāngyáng 當陽侯) was one of the most versatile officials of the Western Jìn, combining military command, administrative reform, and classical scholarship at a high level. His most famous contribution is the *Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn jí jiě* 春秋左傳集解 ([[KR1e0108]]), the standard commentary on the *Zuǒzhuàn* that remained authoritative throughout the imperial period; he also authored the *Chūnqiū shì lì* 春秋釋例, an analytical supplement. As general he commanded the conquest of Wú 吳 in 280, completing the unification of the empire under Jìn. His administrative memorials on river control and irrigation in the Jìng-Huái 荊淮 region are classics of hydraulic management.

The literary fragments in the present reconstruction are relatively few in number and predominantly official in register. The *Suíshū Jīngjízhì* records a *Dù Yù jí* 杜預集 in seventeen *juǎn*, already abbreviated from an earlier thirty-*juǎn* version; none survives. Zhāng Pǔ 張溥's reconstruction in the *Hàn Wèi Liùcháo bǎisān jiā jí* 漢魏六朝百三家集 erroneously included a text from Lù Yún 陸雲, corrected in the present edition. See also [[杜預]].
