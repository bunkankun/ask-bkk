# Dà-yǐn jū-shì shī-jí 大隱居士詩集
*Great-Recluse Poetry Collection*
by [[鄧深]] (撰)

## About the work
*Dà-yǐn jū-shì shī-jí* 大隱居士詩集 in 2 *juǎn* is the surviving poetic collection of Dèng Shēn 鄧深 (fl. 1140s–1170s), Sòng *Tóng-chuān cáo* and Maritime Trade Commissioner of Guǎng-xī. The title takes Dèng's *hào* Dà-yǐn 大隱. Reconstituted entirely from the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* by the *Sì-kù* editors. The original-attribution in *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* was *Dèng Shēn-bó jí* — without specifying a name or period; the *Sì-kù* editors' reconstruction of authorship from internal evidence (place-names, *zì*-references, *hào*-references) and external evidence (*Gǔ Luó zhì*, *Wàn-xìng tōng-pǔ*) is exemplary philology. Dèng's father-and-mother loved-the-classics so deeply that the prose collection — recorded in *Gǔ Luó zhì* as 10 *juǎn* — is otherwise entirely lost.

### Tiyao
*Dà-yǐn jū-shì shī-jí* in 2 *juǎn*. Examining: the *Dà-yǐn jū-shì jí*'s *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* original-title is *Dèng Shēn-bó jí*. Those-scattered-and-visible in the various *yùn* — gathered-and-arranged — still 2 *juǎn*. But the original-version did-not give his name, did-not give his time. Various-scholars' catalogs all did-not record this book.

Only the collection has *Yóu Luó Zhèng-zhòng qìng-zhǎo fēn-yùn shī* — title says *Shēn dé yī-zì*; the *Zhū-rén jí Pín-lè-xuān shǎng-huā fēn-yùn shī* — title says *Shēn dé bǎ-zì* — then his name should-be Dèng Shēn.

Examining the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* under the *Dèng* word *yùn* — citing the *Gǔ Luó zhì* says: Sòng Dèng Shēn, *zì* Zī-dào, tested *Jiào-guān*; entered as *Tài-fǔ chéng*; in *lún-duì* discussed Jīng-xī, Hú-nán-běi household-and-scholar-officials' folkways — Gāozōng accepted-and-praised. *Tí-jǔ Guǎng-xī shì-bó*, for-aged-parents requested convenient-prefecture; *Zhī Héng-zhōu* — at *Chá-líng*, *Ān-rén*, the *Xī-dòng zhī dào* (mountain-valley bandits) on-hearing-of-his-name *tiē-xī*. Promoted to *Tóng-chuān cáo*; *yán-jiǔ xū-é jiǔ wéi mín hài; qǐng yú cháo, juān Chuān-yǐn 47-wàn* (the salt-and-wine false-quotas long-pained the people; petitioned the court, remitting the Sì-chuān *yǐn* 470,000); supervisors and county-magistrates *tān-nüè* (greedy-cruel) — impeached. Yú Yǔn-wén wrote-letter saying: *bù-wèi qiáng-yù; sī-jì sī-mín; tǐng-rán zhī cāo, wèi-jiàn jìn-bǐ*. Later with *Cháo-qǐng dà-fū* ended at home. Loved-residing at the eastern-lake's *shèng* (scenic spot); built pavilion called *Míng-xiù*; had *wén-jí* in 10 *juǎn*. Líng Dí-zhī's *Wàn-xìng tōng-pǔ* also records *Dèng Shēn, Xiāng-yīn person, *Shào-xīng*-period *jìn-shì*; remainder same as *Gǔ Luó zhì*.

The collection has *village-people praying-for-rain-with-response-time visiting Wū-shí*; further chants the *Lǐ-quán*, titled *Yán-shí, mountain-stone, drum, presented-to Ráo Sī-lǐ, parted-from-Cháng-shā-yì, ferried-across the Yù-xū dòng* — these places are all close-to Héng-zhōu. *Sù-xiá sān-yóu-dòng, Xiá-jiāng yàn-yù duī* — these places also all close-to Tóng-chuān. With [Dèng] Shēn's office-tour *yī-yī xiāng-fú* (one-by-one matching) — then this collection composed by Dèng Shēn — verified.

Only Shēn-bó's *zì* — different from what the *Gǔ Luó zhì* records — surely had-two *zì*. Huáng Yú-jì's *Qiān-qǐng-táng shū-mù* records there's a *Yuán Dèng Dà-yǐn jū-shì shī-jí*. The collection's *Dá shè-yǒu shī* has line "*xiǎo-xuān míng Dà-yǐn*" (small-pavilion called Great-Recluse); further has *Zì-fù Dà-yǐn yī-lǜ* (one regulated-poem self-composing the Great-Recluse) — matches it. But examining its poetry-line and headings, really a Sòng-person, not a Yuán-person. Suspect *Dà-yǐn* is [Dèng] Shēn's *bié-hào* — hence the collection accordingly named-thus. Yú-jì etc. by transmission-and-transcription, erred Sòng as Yuán.

Today specifically based-on-this — re-titling the collection name; while time-period still followed-from *Gǔ Luó zhì* — placed in Sòng. Respectfully collated, *Qián-lóng* 46 (1781), 12th month.

### Abstract
The *Dà-yǐn jū-shì shī-jí* in 2 *juǎn* is a *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn*-recovered collection whose authorship and dynastic-attribution were both initially uncertain. The *Sì-kù* editors' multi-pronged philological reconstruction — drawing on internal *zì*-tags (the *fēn-yùn* poems whose *yùn* is the *Shēn* character — proof of name); on internal place-names (*Wū-shí*, *Lǐ-quán*, *Cháng-shā*, *Xiá-jiāng* — proof of itinerary); on external evidence (*Gǔ Luó zhì*, *Wàn-xìng tōng-pǔ*) — establishes Dèng Shēn as the author and the Sòng *Shào-xīng*–*Qián-dào* period as the date-bracket. Huáng Yú-jì's *Qiān-qǐng-táng shū-mù*'s erroneous *Yuán*-attribution is corrected.

The collection is a record of Dèng's regional-administrative tour: Héng-zhōu Prefect (Hú-nán), then Tóng-chuān (Sì-chuān). The Yú Yǔn-wén commendation — *bù-wèi qiáng-yù; sī-jì sī-mín; tǐng-rán zhī cāo* — preserved by the *Gǔ Luó zhì*, places Dèng among the *qīng-zhèng* (clean-and-just) provincial officials of the Xiào-zōng period.

## Other points of interest
- The *Sì-kù* editors' philological reconstruction of authorship, *zì*, *hào*, dynasty, and itinerary from a fragmentary *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* collection is one of the more methodologically-interesting cases in the *jí-bù bié-jí* tíyào series.
- Distinguish from the very-different *Dà-yǐn jí* [[KR4d0166]] by Lǐ Zhèng-mín (also titled with *Dà-yǐn*).
