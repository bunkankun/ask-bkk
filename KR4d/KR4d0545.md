# Qiáo-Wú jí 僑吳集
*The Sojourning-in-Wú Collection*
by [[鄭元祐]] (撰), re-edited by [[張習]] (重編)

## About the work
A 12-*juǎn* (6 *juǎn wén* + 6 *juǎn shī*) re-edited collection of **Zhèng Yuán-yòu** 鄭元祐 (1292–1364). Title from Zhèng's 40-year *liú-yù* (in transit-residence) at Píng-jiāng (Wú = Sūzhōu) after relocation from Qián-táng. *Suì-chāng shān-rén* by *bié-hào*. Original Yuán-Míng recension consisted of *Suì-chāng shān-rén jí* + *Qiáo-Wú jí* with much overlap; the Hóng-zhì *bǐng-chén* (1496) *Zhāng Xí* 張習 *zhòng-dìng* combined and selected the "精純者" 12-*juǎn* recension as the present text. The collection's principal anomaly — the *Zhāng Dé-cháng shū* claiming a *jùn* post — is corrected by Sū Dà-nián's *mù-zhì* and Lú Xióng's *Sū-zhōu fǔ-zhì*, both attesting Zhèng held only *xiào-guān* (school-officer) posts.

### Tiyao
*Qiáo-Wú jí*, 12 *juǎn*. By Zhèng Yuán-yòu of the Yuán. Yuán-yòu has *Suì-chāng zá-lù* already in the catalog. Yuán-yòu's family-seat Suì-chāng — relocated to Qián-táng — and *liú-yù* at Píng-jiāng 40 years — the longest period — so the *jí*'s name *Qiáo-Wú* (Sojourning in Wú). Actually compositions at Háng-zhōu are also in it; takes from the bulk. The original *jí* was Yuán-yòu's late-years' *zì-dìng* — given to **Xiè Huī** 謝徽. The present version's back has Hóng-zhì *bǐng-chén* (1496) Zhāng Xí *bá*: "*Yuán-yòu had original Suì-chāng shān-rén jí*, with *Qiáo-Wú jí* — much *fán-wú chóng-chū* (cluttered and repeated) — so I read both and obtained the *shī-wén jīng-chún zhě* — combined as 12 *juǎn* — still named *Qiáo-Wú jí* — for woodblock printing." So this version is Xí *chóng-dìng* — not Yuán-yòu's hand-edited version. *Wén* 6 *juǎn*; *shī* 6 *juǎn*. Among them *Zhāng Dé-cháng shū* — has "*pú zàn jùn wú-bǔ — cháng yí Léi-lǐ zhī yǔ*" — but Sū Dà-nián's *mù-zhì* and Lú Xióng's *Sū-zhōu fǔ-zhì* all say Yuán-yòu was by *dà-fǔ* (provincial) recommended; twice *jiào-guān* — does not say also held other posts — does not match Yuán-yòu's *shì-lǚ* (career). Could it be a composition for someone else, slipping the marking? — His prose *pō shū-dàng yǒu qì* (rather free and powerful); verse also *cāng-gǔ*. Since Yuán-yòu was born *Zhì-yuán mò* (end of Zhì-yuán) — still saw the *Xián-chún* (Sòng-late) *yí-lǎo*; in the middle also saw Yú Jí et al. — got their *xù-lùn*. End-years exchanged with — also all Gù Ā-yīng, Ní Zàn, Zhāng Yǔ et al. — *hù xiāng xūn-rǎn* — his *qì-yùn* not the same — also has *zì-lái* (its source). Respectfully collated.

### Abstract
*Qiáo-Wú jí* preserves the principal monument of a Suì-chāng-Qián-táng-Sūzhōu literatus whose life spans the *Xián-chún yí-lǎo* (Sòng-late old men he met) → Yú Jí mid-Yuán → Gù Ā-yīng / Ní Zàn / Zhāng Yǔ late-Yuán arc — an unusually broad intellectual reach. Zhèng's role is principally as a Sū-zhōu literary node — his *Yuán* circle includes the principal late-Yuán Wú-zhōng figures. The Hóng-zhì-era Zhāng Xí re-editing (1496) is a typical Míng pruning of an Yuán *bié-jí*. The catalog meta gives an editor function 重編 for Zhāng Xí — *zhòng-biān* exactly fits the 1496 *zhòng-dìng*. Composition window: 1320 (mature work begins) to 1364 (death).
