# Xuě-xī jí 雪溪集
*Snow-Stream Collection*
by [[王銍]] (撰)

## About the work
*Xuě-xī jí* 雪溪集 in 5 *juǎn* is the surviving poetic collection of Wáng Zhì 王銍 (fl. 1122–1144), Sòng *bǐ-jì* author (*Mò-jì* 默記, *Bǔ shì-ér xiǎo-míng lù bǔ-yí* 補侍兒小名錄補遺) of erudite reputation. The title takes Wáng's *hào* Xuě-xī 雪溪. Originally 8 *juǎn* per Chén Zhèn-sūn [[KR3h0011]] and *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén-zhì*; 5 *juǎn* in the surviving WYG recension. The *Sì-kù* editors place Wáng's poetic style close to Wēn Tíng-yún and Lǐ Shāng-yǐn (Late-Tang lyric).

### Tiyao
*Xuě-xī jí* 5 *juǎn*, by Wáng Zhì of the Sòng. [Wáng] Zhì has the *Bù shì-ér xiǎo-míng lù bǔ-yí* — already-recorded. This compilation is his poetry-collection. Chén Zhèn-sūn's *Shū-lù jiě-tí* and *Sòng shǐ Yì-wén-zhì* both make-it 8 *juǎn*; this version is only 5 *juǎn*.

Examining the *Mò-zhuāng màn-lù* records [Wáng] Zhì's composed *Wáng Wén-rú Qú-ān shī* one piece; further the *Shān-cūn shī* one piece; the *Yuè-yǒng* records [Wáng] Zhì's composed *Yún-mén-sì shī* one piece — today none-visible in the collection. Knowing what is now-transmitted is already-lost-its-3-*juǎn*; not-the-complete-fascicle.

[Wáng] Zhì's poetic-style close-to Wēn / Lǐ. Wáng Shì-zhēn's *Jū-yì lù* criticises his poetry as not-very skillful; but praises his appended *Lú-shān sēng Kě-hé hé-shī* one piece — appears not the *dǔ-lùn* (definitive judgment).

Only [Wáng] Zhì by *bó-qià* (broad-comprehensive) named — yet within-the-collection's *Bái-tóu yín xù* did-not draw-on the *Xī-jīng zá-jì*, but drew-on Wú Jīng's *Yuè-fǔ jiě-tí* — already missed-its-source. (Note: the *Xī-jīng zá-jì* though a false-book is before Wú Jīng — i.e. Wú's account itself draws-out from-it.) Further called the *Sòng-zhì* records *Wén-jūn shī* etc. — not-knowing the *Sòng shū yuè-zhì*'s *Bái-tóu yín* really is *gǔ-cí*, not Wén-jūn's. This is also a thousand-considerations' one-loss. *Xìn hū kǎo-zhèng zhī nán* (truly the difficulty of textual-research) indeed. Respectfully collated, *Qián-lóng* 42 (1777), 9th month.

### Abstract
The *Xuě-xī jí* in 5 *juǎn* preserves about 5/8 of Wáng Zhì's original poetic corpus (with three identifiable losses cited from *Mò-zhuāng màn-lù* and *Yuè-yǒng*). Wáng was a *bǐ-jì*-master of erudite reputation, and his *Mò-jì* and *Bǔ shì-ér xiǎo-míng lù bǔ-yí* are independently valuable. The *Sì-kù* tíyào is unusually pointed in catching two philological errors in Wáng's own *Bái-tóu yín xù*: (a) the source-error (Wú Jīng's *Yuè-fǔ jiě-tí* drawn-on instead of the original *Xī-jīng zá-jì*); (b) the attribution-error (claiming the *Sòng-zhì* records Wén-jūn's poem, when in fact the *Sòng shū yuè-zhì* records *Bái-tóu yín* as anonymous). The *Sì-kù* editors' generalising verdict — *kǎo-zhèng zhī nán* (the difficulty of philological research) — is methodologically self-aware.
