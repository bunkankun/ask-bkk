# Jiāng-yuè sōng-fēng jí 江月松風集
*Collection of River-Moon and Pine-Wind*
by [[錢惟善]] (撰)

## About the work
A twelve-*juǎn* poetry collection by **Qián Wéi-shàn** 錢惟善 (style-name Sīfù 思復), a Hángzhōu literatus who passed the 1341 Yuán provincial examination, declined service under **Zhāng Shì-chéng** 張士誠, and died in the early Hóngwǔ era. The collection is one of the most striking transmission cases in late-Yuán literature: the original was Qián's own holograph, kept in the Lù family of Liànchuān, then passed to the Jiā-xīng book-collector **Cáo Róng** 曹溶 (1613–1685); in the Kāng-xī era the bibliophile **Jīn Kǎn** 金侃 copied it from Cáo's library and cross-checked it with the Xǔ family of Fǔ-lǐ's copy, producing the present recension. Gù Sìlì's *Yuán shī xuǎn* drew on this same Jīn-Kǎn recension. Apart from Zhèng Yuán-yòu's parallel Wújiāng/Sūzhōu *liú-yù* group (see [[KR4d0545]]) this is one of the few Hángzhōu-anchored late-Yuán collections to come down in something approaching a complete form.

### Tiyao
*Jiāng-yuè sōng-fēng jí*, 12 *juǎn*. By Qián Wéi-shàn of the Yuán. Wéi-shàn, style-name Sīfù, self-styled Xīnbái dàorén, was a man of Qiántáng. He took the *Zhì-zhèng xīn-sì* (1341) provincial selection and rose to *fù tí-jǔ*. When Zhāng Shì-chéng occupied Wú he retired into seclusion at Wújiāng, then moved to Huá-tíng. He died in the early Hóngwǔ. Wéi-shàn was broadly learned and of fierce memory. When he first sat the provincial examination the topic was *Luó-chà jiāng fù*; of the three thousand in the locked compound none knew the source. Wéi-shàn alone cited Méi Shèng's *Qī-fā*, identifying the Qián-táng *Qū-jiāng* as the *Luó-chà jiāng*. (Note: Méi Shèng calls *Guǎnglíng zhī Qū-jiāng* — i.e. the tide-viewing should be at Yáng-zhōu. Later men, on the ground that Guǎnglíng in Hàn times had wide territory, assigned this to the Qián-táng tide — quite a forced equation. Wéi-shàn's *fù* in fact perpetuated the slip and is no firm authority. We append this correction here.) The chief examiner greatly praised him, and he became famous on that basis. His collection was not much in evidence in the Míng — Jiāo Hóng's *Guóshǐ jīngjí zhì* gathers Yuán poetry collections quite copiously but does not record Wéi-shàn's. What had transmitted to the world was only the nine poems collected in the *Liáng Dà-yǎ jí* of [Lài]. The present version was Wéi-shàn's own holograph, kept in the Lù family of Liànchuān; later it passed to Cáo Róng of Jiā-xīng. In the Kāng-xī era Jīn Kǎn copied from Cáo's library and cross-collated against the Fǔ-lǐ Xǔ-shì copy; only then was this volume in clean form put into circulation. Gù Sìlì's *Yuán shī xuǎn* relies on this collection for its excerpts. Respectfully collated, Qiánlóng forty-second (1777), fifth month. Compilers: Jì Yún, Lù Xī-xióng, Sūn Shì-yì; head proofreader: Lù Fèi-chí.

### Abstract
*Jiāng-yuè sōng-fēng jí* is the survival of Qián Wéi-shàn's verse output by an unusual route — through holograph transmission and an early-Qīng collector's salvage by Cáo Róng → Jīn Kǎn rather than through Míng printing. As such, the text is also a useful documentary case for early-Qīng bibliographic culture: the *Sìkù* compilers explicitly cite their reliance on the late-17th-century Jīn Kǎn copy, and credit the Cáo Róng library. Composition window: from the *Zhì-zhèng xīn-sì* (1341) examination through to Qián's death in the early Hóngwǔ era; the catalog gives no precise dates. The *fù* on the Luó-chà jiāng — Qián's signature piece — is unfortunately built on a textual misidentification that the *Sìkù* compilers correct in their *àn* parenthesis. The collection is also a major witness to the literary circle around Yáng Wéi-zhēn and the Zhèng Yuán-yòu Wújiāng cohort in the late-Yuán Hángzhōu / Sūzhōu axis.

## Translations and research
- Treated in studies of late-Yuán Hángzhōu / Sūzhōu literary culture; rarely the subject of dedicated monographs.
- Late-imperial bibliographic studies routinely cite this case for the Cáo Róng → Jīn Kǎn → *Sìkù* recovery route.

## Other points of interest
- The *Sìkù* compilers' parenthetical correction of Qián's geographic identification (*Luó-chà jiāng* = Hàn *Guǎnglíng Qū-jiāng* not Qiántáng) is a small but notable instance of the *tíyào* correcting an author cited approvingly within the same entry.
