# Chún-bái-zhāi lèi-gǎo 純白齋類稿
*The Chún-bái-zhāi (Pure-White Studio) Classified Drafts*
by [[胡助]] (撰)

## About the work
A 20-*juǎn* + 2 *juǎn fù-lù* truncated recension of **Hú Zhù** 胡助 (b. 1278), originally 30 *juǎn* self-edited; lost in transmission for two centuries; re-assembled from scattered fragments by his sixth-generation descendant Hú Huái 胡淮 in mid-Zhèng-dé. The present recension preserves 1 *juǎn fù* + 16 *juǎn shī* + 3 *juǎn zá-wén* + 2 *juǎn fù-lù* (contemporary *zèng* verse to Hú). Yú Jí's *bá* on the *Shàng-jīng jì-xíng jí* and Dèng Wén-yuán / Wú Chéng's *bá* on *Luán-pō xiǎo-lù* and *Shēng xué jì-qì wén* are documented in the *Sìkù tíyào* as lost — their pieces presumably among the 10 *juǎn* dropped from the original.

### Tiyao
*Chún-bái-zhāi lèi-gǎo*, 20 *juǎn* + 2 *juǎn fù-lù*. By Hú Zhù of the Yuán. Zhù *zì* Lǚ-xìn, *yī-zì* Gǔ-yú. Native of Wū-zhōu Dōng-yáng. Initially raised by *Mào-cái*; *Jiàn-kāng-lù rú-xué xué-lù*; through *Měi-huà shū-yuàn shān-zhǎng*, *Wēn-zhōu-lù rú-xué jiào-shòu*; by second recommendation again *Hàn-lín Guó-shǐ-yuàn biān-xiū-guān*; retired with *Chéng-xìn-láng*, *Tài-cháng bó-shì*. The time was Zhì-zhèng 5 (1345). This collection is what Zhù himself edited — originally 30 *juǎn*. Years long, *cán-quē shī-cì* (incomplete and out-of-order). In Míng Zhèng-dé, his 6th-gen. descendant Huái gathered the scattered, re-edited it — only *fù* 1 *juǎn*, *shī* 16 *juǎn*, *zá-wén* 3 *juǎn* + *fù-lù* 2 *juǎn* of contemporary *zèng* verse, retaining the *Chún-bái-zhāi lèi-gǎo* title — *juǎn* count reduced by 1/3, no longer the original. Yú Jí once *bá* his *Shàng-jīng jì-xíng jí* — said his *Lóng-mén yǐ-hòu shī yóu jiā*; *jīn yǐ sàn rù jí zhōng*. Dèng Wén-yuán and Wú Chéng each *bá* his *Luán-pō xiǎo-lù* and *Shēng xué jì-qì wén* — *cǐ běn bù-zǎi* — must be in the lost 10 *juǎn*. Hú Zhù's *shī-wén* are *píng-yì jìn-rén*, no *shēn-zhàn qí-jǐng* (deep, soak-deep, strange-and-alert) thinking; but also no *zhī-lí pò-suì* (fragmentation and breakage) defect. Probably not lost as *zhōng-shēng* (middle voice). Wú Chéng characterized his verse as: "*chūn-lán zhuó-yá — xià-zhú hán-tuò — lù-zī yǔ-xǐ zhī yú — zhuó-zhuó yōu-mèi — juān-juān jìng-hǎo*" — *xíng-róng guò-dāng* (over-stating the description) — *fǎn bù xiào qí pǐn-gé* (instead, doesn't match his *pǐn-gé*). Respectfully collated.

### Abstract
*Chún-bái-zhāi lèi-gǎo* preserves the principal corpus of a Wū-zhōu literary figure of the same Dōng-yáng circle as the *Jīn-huá sì-xiān-shēng*. Hú is now best known for his preface to Wú Lái [[吳萊]]'s *Yuān-yǐng jí*, supplying the "*dū-lùn*" line on *qiǎn-lòu* vs. *hóng-bó*. The collection's progressive truncation (30 → 20 + 2 *juǎn*; 1/3 reduction during Zhèng-dé-era Hú-Huái re-edit) is a typical case of Yuán-Míng small-author transmission decay. Composition window: from Hú's earliest preserved compositions (post-c. 1310) to his Zhì-zhèng 5 (1345) retirement.
