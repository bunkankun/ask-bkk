# Zhāng yòu-shǐ wén-jí 張右史文集
*Collected Works of Right-Historian [Zhāng Lěi]*
by [[張耒]] (撰)

## About the work
*Zhāng yòu-shǐ wén-jí* 張右史文集 (named from Zhāng Lěi 張耒 [[張耒]]'s *Zhù-zuò zuǒ-láng* / *yòu-shǐ* official titles) is the SBCK reprint of the Sòng *Zhè-xī cáo-tái* recension in 60 *juǎn* — one of the four parallel Southern-Sòng recensions of Zhāng Lěi's collection enumerated by Zhōu Zǐ-zhī 周紫芝 in his *Tài-cāng tí-mǐ jí* postscript. The structure: 3 *juǎn fù*; 2 *juǎn yuè-fǔ*; *gǔ-shī* and *jìn-tǐ-shī* through *juǎn* 28; thereafter *biǎo-zhuàng-zhá-zǐ-zòu-yì-shū-jì-xù-bēi-zhì-zàn* in standard *bié-jí* genre-ordering. The KRP source files include the *Sòng-shǐ Zhāng Lěi liè-zhuàn* preserved at the head — providing the complete *Sòng-shǐ Wén-yuàn zhuàn* version of Zhāng's biography. The recension is parallel to the *Sìkù* WYG 50-*juǎn* *Kē-shān jí* [[KR4d0089]] but bibliographically distinct: Zhōu Zǐ-zhī's record clearly distinguishes the 70-*juǎn* *Zhāng yòu-shǐ jí* (probably this recension's antecedent) from the 100-*juǎn* *Tán-jùn xiān-shēng jí* (the most complete Sòng recension, lost). The 60-*juǎn* state of the present SBCK base is therefore a *zhāi-cuì* (selection-and-gathering) intermediate between the 70-*juǎn* and the lost 100-*juǎn* states.

### Tiyao
*The KRP source is the SBCK reprint, which does not include the Sìkù tíyào.* The work was not separately cataloged by the *Sìkù* — which received the *Kē-shān jí* in 50 *juǎn* [[KR4d0089]] as the principal Zhāng Lěi *bié-jí*. The *Sìkù* tíyào of *Kē-shān jí* discusses the parallel recensions including the *Zhāng yòu-shǐ jí* explicitly.

### Abstract
*Zhāng yòu-shǐ wén-jí* preserves Zhāng Lěi's complete extant output in a different recensional state than the *Sìkù*'s *Kē-shān jí*. The 60-*juǎn* count places this recension in the upper-middle of the four-recension family enumerated by Zhōu Zǐ-zhī (10 / 30 / 70 / 100). For Zhāng-Lěi philological work the SBCK *Zhāng yòu-shǐ wén-jí* is the principal control on the *Sìkù*'s 50-*juǎn* *Kē-shān jí*; the modern Zhōng-huá *Zhāng Lěi jí* edition (1990) collates both. The collection's 2 *juǎn yuè-fǔ* — preserving Zhāng's *Zhāng-Jí*-influenced ballads — is one of the more striking Sòng *yuè-fǔ* corpora outside the strict *Jiāng-xī*-school orbit. The *Sòng-shǐ liè-zhuàn* preserved at the head supplies the standard biographical frame: Zhāng's *Yuán-yòu*-coalition position, the *Sū-mén* discipleship, the *Chóng-níng* proscription for memorializing Sū Shì's death (the *Yǐng-zhōu*-period *jǔ-āi xíng-fú* incident is canonical), the *Chén-zhōu* end-of-career, and the *Sòng-shǐ* notice of his being the last surviving major *Sū-xué* figure. Dating bracket: Zhāng's death (1114) to the SBCK reprint (1929).

## Other points of interest
The four-recension transmission state of Zhāng Lěi's collection — uniquely well-documented by Zhōu Zǐ-zhī's *Tài-cāng* postscript and by Hú Yìng-lín's *Bǐ-cóng* — makes this *bié-jí* a textbook case of Sòng *bié-jí* recensional fluidity. The SBCK and *Sìkù* recensions together preserve, between them, the surviving fraction of the lost 100-*juǎn* full state — but neither alone is complete.
