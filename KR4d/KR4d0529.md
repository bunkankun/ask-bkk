# Guīfēng jí 圭峯集
*The Guī-fēng (Jade-Tablet Peak) Collection*
by [[盧琦]] (撰)

## About the work
A 2-*juǎn* restored recension of **Lú Qí** 盧琦 (d. 1362), the HuìānFújiàn *liánglì* poet-administrator. The textual situation is one of the more dramatic in the *Sìkùguǎn*'s late-Yuán *biéjí* repertory: the Wànlì Chén Chéngzhōng edition (preceded by the more recent ZhuāngHùbù Huìān edition) had folded 60+ pieces from Sàdūlā [[薩都拉]]'s *Yànmén jí* [[KR4d0514]] into Lú Qí's collection — including, most flagrantly, Sà's *Xīxíng zhōngqiū wányuè* (whose self-preface explicitly names "*yú nǎi Sàshì zǐ*" — "I am of the Sà family") — re-titled in the corrupted Lú edition as "*rú yǒu Sàshì zǐ*" with the "Zhìyuán *dīngchǒu* (1337) mid-autumn" colophon deliberately deleted. The *Sìkùguǎn* deleted 32 such confirmed misattributions and restored the authentic 2-*juǎn* LúQí corpus. The collection also contains 3 *fù*, 6 *jì*, 2 *zhìmíng*, 1 *jìwén*, 3 *qǐ*, 9 *zázhù* — these *quèguān Lú zhī zhēnzuò* (definitely-genuine Lú-authentic compositions).

### Tiyao
*Guīfēng jí*, 2 *juǎn*. By Lú Qí of the Yuán. Qí *zì* Xīhán, *hào* Lìzhāi. Native of Huìān. Guīfēng is where he lived. The hand-copy version writes *Guīzhāi jí* — a transmission error. Zhìzhèng 2 (1342) [appointed] *Dàxiàozhōu lùshì*; transferred *Yǒngchūn xiànyǐn*; reassigned *Níngdé*; through *Cáosī tíjǔ*; appointed *Píngyángzhōu* — order came down but he died. Career fully in *Yuánshǐ liánglì zhuàn*. Xú Yúzǐ's 徐&#x242B9; *Bǐjīng* says: "*Guīfēng jí* — long not transmitted; recently Huìān *ZhuāngHùbù Zhēngfǔ* gathered and printed it." This version is by Chén Chéngzhōng of the Yuán; Míng Wànlì early, town-fellows Zhū Yīlóng of Huìān and Dǒng Yìngjǔ of Fúzhōu prefaced and printed it — preceding the Zhuāng version. But it had already many alien pieces folded in: *wǔyán gǔshī Chūnrì sī yuǎnyóu* is in Chén Lǚ's collection [[KR4d0516]]; *wǔyán gǔ Guòlǐng zhì Chóngān*, *Sòng Wú Fǔ zhì Yángzhōu*, *Tí Jiāoshān fāngzhàng bì*, *Qiūrì chíshàng dù Mǐnguān sù Táishānsì juédǐng zǎofā Huánghé* etc.; *qīyán gǔ Yǒu shì jū Yōngguān zǒubǐ zèng Mèng Lǐ Yuèlíngtái wàngyuè yèbó Diàotái Jiāngnán lè Jiāngnán yuàn Xuěshān cí Cuī Zhèn zǔ fēng Yóu Wúshān Tuófēng Zǐyángān Jiāngshàng wén dí Biéyǒu Hányè wén dí Àndàntān gē Qīnghú qū Hǎitáng qū Rú yǒu Sàshì zǐ* etc.; *qīyán lǜshī Gāoyóu chénglóu wǎnwàng Yān jiāngjūn chūliè jì Hèlín chánglǎo Hé Wáng Wéi xuéhǎi nánhuán yùn Sānqú shǒu suǒ tí Lànkē shíqiáo Dēng Zhènyáng Lóngxīngsì gé jì cānzhèng Xǔ Kěyòng Sòng qiānxiàn Wáng Jūnshí Jīnlíng dàozhōng zàiguò Zhōngshān Wànshòusì* etc. — total 32 — all in Sàdūlā [[薩都拉]]'s collection. As to Sàdūlā's *Xīxíng zhōngqiū wányuè* — self-preface "*yú nǎi Sàshì zǐ*" etc. — *bānbān kě kǎo*. This collection re-titled it "*Rú yǒu Sàshì zǐ*"; the preface-end "*Zhìyuán dīngchǒu* (1337) mid-autumn writing" cut off — particularly displaying *zuòwěi* (deliberate forgery) — cannot be called accidental misattribution. Probably at compilation time, to inflate *juǎnzhì* and to *kuā* (boast) wide search, hence the *zhēnyàn hùnxiáo* mix-up. Qí's office was not high, but among *liánglì*, *kě bùjiè shī ér chuán* (need not depend on verse to transmit). Even on poetic grounds, his *qīngcí yǎyùn* (clear-words, elegant rhymes) — also not below Chén Lǚ and Sàdūlā. The editor *yí jiǎ wéi yǐ* (taking *jiǎ* for *yǐ*) — also not without cause. The collection also has 3 *fù*, 6 *jì*, 2 *zhìmíng*, 1 *jìwén*, 3 *qǐ*, 9 *zázhù* — *quèguān Qí zuò*, not by *jiǎjiè*. Now we delete the falsely-recorded verse, combine with the prose — to preserve Qí's genuine work. Respectfully collated.

### Abstract
The *Guīfēng jí* is one of the principal textual-recovery feats of the *Sìkùguǎn* in the Yuán *biéjí* division — the systematic deletion of 32 confirmed Sàdūlā misattributions, with the *tíyào* itself providing the detailed list of titles deleted. The *Xīxíng zhōngqiū wányuè* deliberate-deletion of "*Sàshì zǐ*" → "*Sàshì zǐ*" (with date-colophon erased) is the *Sìkù tíyào*'s *fǎyī* exemplar of late-Míng Fújiàn *zuòwěi*. The textually-clean LúQí corpus (verse + 3 *fù* + 6 *jì* + 2 *zhìmíng* + others) is the principal monument of one of the *Yuánshǐ liánglì*. Composition window: 1342 (*jìnshì*) to 1362 (death before taking up Píngyángzhōu).

## Other points of interest
The Lú Qí / Sàdūlā misattribution case is one of the standard textbook cases in Chinese textual studies on the dangers of late-Míng *huìzǐ* (regional re-printing) practice without source-attribution discipline.
