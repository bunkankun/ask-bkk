# Guī-fēng jí 圭峯集
*The Guī-fēng (Jade-Tablet Peak) Collection*
by [[盧琦]] (撰)

## About the work
A 2-*juǎn* restored recension of **Lú Qí** 盧琦 (d. 1362), the Huì-ān-Fú-jiàn *liáng-lì* poet-administrator. The textual situation is one of the more dramatic in the *Sìkù-guǎn*'s late-Yuán *bié-jí* repertory: the Wàn-lì Chén Chéng-zhōng edition (preceded by the more recent Zhuāng-Hù-bù Huì-ān edition) had folded 60+ pieces from Sà-dū-lā [[薩都拉]]'s *Yàn-mén jí* [[KR4d0514]] into Lú Qí's collection — including, most flagrantly, Sà's *Xī-xíng zhōng-qiū wán-yuè* (whose self-preface explicitly names "*yú nǎi Sà-shì zǐ*" — "I am of the Sà family") — re-titled in the corrupted Lú edition as "*rú yǒu Sà-shì zǐ*" with the "Zhì-yuán *dīng-chǒu* (1337) mid-autumn" colophon deliberately deleted. The *Sìkù-guǎn* deleted 32 such confirmed misattributions and restored the authentic 2-*juǎn* Lú-Qí corpus. The collection also contains 3 *fù*, 6 *jì*, 2 *zhì-míng*, 1 *jì-wén*, 3 *qǐ*, 9 *zá-zhù* — these *quèguān Lú zhī zhēn-zuò* (definitely-genuine Lú-authentic compositions).

### Tiyao
*Guī-fēng jí*, 2 *juǎn*. By Lú Qí of the Yuán. Qí *zì* Xī-hán, *hào* Lì-zhāi. Native of Huì-ān. Guī-fēng is where he lived. The hand-copy version writes *Guī-zhāi jí* — a transmission error. Zhì-zhèng 2 (1342) [appointed] *Dà-xiào-zhōu lù-shì*; transferred *Yǒng-chūn xiàn-yǐn*; reassigned *Níng-dé*; through *Cáo-sī tí-jǔ*; appointed *Píng-yáng-zhōu* — order came down but he died. Career fully in *Yuán-shǐ liáng-lì zhuàn*. Xú Yú-zǐ's 徐&#x242B9; *Bǐ-jīng* says: "*Guī-fēng jí* — long not transmitted; recently Huì-ān *Zhuāng-Hù-bù Zhēng-fǔ* gathered and printed it." This version is by Chén Chéng-zhōng of the Yuán; Míng Wàn-lì early, town-fellows Zhū Yī-lóng of Huì-ān and Dǒng Yìng-jǔ of Fú-zhōu prefaced and printed it — preceding the Zhuāng version. But it had already many alien pieces folded in: *wǔ-yán gǔ-shī Chūn-rì sī yuǎn-yóu* is in Chén Lǚ's collection [[KR4d0516]]; *wǔ-yán gǔ Guò-lǐng zhì Chóng-ān*, *Sòng Wú Fǔ zhì Yáng-zhōu*, *Tí Jiāo-shān fāng-zhàng bì*, *Qiū-rì chí-shàng dù Mǐn-guān sù Tái-shān-sì jué-dǐng zǎo-fā Huáng-hé* etc.; *qī-yán gǔ Yǒu shì jū Yōng-guān zǒu-bǐ zèng Mèng Lǐ Yuè-líng-tái wàng-yuè yè-bó Diào-tái Jiāng-nán lè Jiāng-nán yuàn Xuě-shān cí Cuī Zhèn zǔ fēng Yóu Wú-shān Tuó-fēng Zǐ-yáng-ān Jiāng-shàng wén dí Bié-yǒu Hán-yè wén dí Àn-dàn-tān gē Qīng-hú qū Hǎi-táng qū Rú yǒu Sà-shì zǐ* etc.; *qī-yán lǜ-shī Gāo-yóu chéng-lóu wǎn-wàng Yān jiāng-jūn chū-liè jì Hè-lín cháng-lǎo Hé Wáng Wéi xué-hǎi nán-huán yùn Sān-qú shǒu suǒ tí Làn-kē shí-qiáo Dēng Zhèn-yáng Lóng-xīng-sì gé jì cān-zhèng Xǔ Kě-yòng Sòng qiān-xiàn Wáng Jūn-shí Jīn-líng dào-zhōng zài-guò Zhōng-shān Wàn-shòu-sì* etc. — total 32 — all in Sà-dū-lā [[薩都拉]]'s collection. As to Sà-dū-lā's *Xī-xíng zhōng-qiū wán-yuè* — self-preface "*yú nǎi Sà-shì zǐ*" etc. — *bān-bān kě kǎo*. This collection re-titled it "*Rú yǒu Sà-shì zǐ*"; the preface-end "*Zhì-yuán dīng-chǒu* (1337) mid-autumn writing" cut off — particularly displaying *zuò-wěi* (deliberate forgery) — cannot be called accidental misattribution. Probably at compilation time, to inflate *juǎn-zhì* and to *kuā* (boast) wide search, hence the *zhēn-yàn hùn-xiáo* mix-up. Qí's office was not high, but among *liáng-lì*, *kě bù-jiè shī ér chuán* (need not depend on verse to transmit). Even on poetic grounds, his *qīng-cí yǎ-yùn* (clear-words, elegant rhymes) — also not below Chén Lǚ and Sà-dū-lā. The editor *yí jiǎ wéi yǐ* (taking *jiǎ* for *yǐ*) — also not without cause. The collection also has 3 *fù*, 6 *jì*, 2 *zhì-míng*, 1 *jì-wén*, 3 *qǐ*, 9 *zá-zhù* — *quèguān Qí zuò*, not by *jiǎ-jiè*. Now we delete the falsely-recorded verse, combine with the prose — to preserve Qí's genuine work. Respectfully collated.

### Abstract
The *Guī-fēng jí* is one of the principal textual-recovery feats of the *Sìkù-guǎn* in the Yuán *bié-jí* division — the systematic deletion of 32 confirmed Sà-dū-lā misattributions, with the *tíyào* itself providing the detailed list of titles deleted. The *Xī-xíng zhōng-qiū wán-yuè* deliberate-deletion of "*Sà-shì zǐ*" → "*Sà-shì zǐ*" (with date-colophon erased) is the *Sìkù tíyào*'s *fǎ-yī* exemplar of late-Míng Fú-jiàn *zuò-wěi*. The textually-clean Lú-Qí corpus (verse + 3 *fù* + 6 *jì* + 2 *zhì-míng* + others) is the principal monument of one of the *Yuán-shǐ liáng-lì*. Composition window: 1342 (*jìnshì*) to 1362 (death before taking up Píng-yáng-zhōu).

## Other points of interest
The Lú Qí / Sà-dū-lā misattribution case is one of the standard textbook cases in Chinese textual studies on the dangers of late-Míng *huì-zǐ* (regional re-printing) practice without source-attribution discipline.
