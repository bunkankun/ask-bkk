# Dōng-lái shī-jí 東萊詩集
*Dōng-lái Master Poetry Collection*
by [[呂本中]] (撰)

## About the work
*Dōng-lái xiān-shēng shī-jí* 東萊先生詩集 in 20 *juǎn* is the poetic collection of Lǚ Běn-zhōng 呂本中 (1084–1145), the *Dà Dōng-lái* (Great Dōng-lái — i.e. the earlier of the two famous Dōng-lái-named Lǚ figures, distinguishing him from his great-grand-cousin Lǚ Zǔ-qiān). The title takes Lǚ's *hào*. Compiled by Lǚ's literary friend Shěn Gōng-yǎ 沈公雅 (son of Shěn Zōng-shī 沈宗師) at his Wú-jùn 吳郡 prefectural seat in *Qián-dào yuán-nián* (1165), with colophon by Zēng Jǐ 曾幾 [[曾幾]] (also born 1084, contemporary). The 20 *juǎn* are organised chronologically (the colophon: *cì-dì suì-yuè wéi èr-shí tōng* — sequenced by years into 20 fascicles). Lǚ's principal historical importance — beyond this collection — is as the compiler of the *Jiāng-xī shī-pài tú* (the foundational text of the *Jiāng-xī* school's self-canonisation), the *Tóng-méng xùn* (KR3a series), and the *Zǐ-wēi shī-huà* (KR4h series).

### Tiyao
*The local source for KR4d0195 is the SBCK reproduction of the* Dōng-lái xiān-shēng shī-jí, *with the Zēng Jǐ colophon preserved at the head.* Date is *Qián-dào yuán-nián* (1165) per the Zēng Jǐ colophon.

The *Sì-kù* tíyào is reproduced at the end of WYG. Translated:

*Dōng-lái shī-jí* in 20 *juǎn*, by Lǚ Běn-zhōng of the Sòng. [Lǚ] Běn-zhōng, *zì* Jū-rén, *hào* Dōng-lái xiān-shēng. Career detailed in *Sòng shǐ* biography. The collection has at-the-end Zēng Jǐ's *bá* — saying: at *Qián-dào yuán-nián* (1165), Shěn Gōng-yǎ from *Shàng-shū-láng* promoted to prefect at this commandery (= Wú-jùn). In leisure-day collected [Lǚ's] poetry — none-omitted; sequenced years into 20 fascicles; cut blocks placed in the prefectural studio. Surely [Lǚ's] friendship with the Shěn-family was deep — and-so the editing was complete.

[Zēng] Jǐ also was-known to [Lǚ] Běn-zhōng. Hence Gōng-yǎ requested-it to write the colophon. [Zēng] Jǐ self-recalled: with [Lǚ] both born in *Yuán-fēng jiǎ-zǐ* (1084); further mutually-related by-marriage; *yǎ xiāng-hǎo* (elegantly-friends). At *Shào-xīng xīn-hài* (1131), [Zēng] Jǐ was at Liǔ-zhōu, in *bì-dì* (refuge) — etc.

[Lǚ] Běn-zhōng's poetic-rule learned from his great-grandfather Lǚ Gōng-zhù; the family-learning *yǔ Yuán-yòu zhū-jūn-zǐ wéi yī-xí* (with the *Yuán-yòu* gentlemen one-fold). Compiled the *Jiāng-xī shī-pài tú* — listed Huáng Tíng-jiān as *zǔ*; descending listed cohort-of-poets. (Hán Jū annoyed at the listing; Chén Yǔ-yì later than the cohort, hence not-included.) Together with his own poetic style, *gé-fǎ jìn yú Yù-zhāng* (rhythm-rule close to Huáng Tíng-jiān) — the standard *Jiāng-xī*-school-stamp.

### Abstract
The 20-*juǎn* collection is the standard recension of Lǚ Běn-zhōng's poetic corpus, edited by his literary friend Shěn Gōng-yǎ at *Qián-dào* 1 (1165) with Zēng Jǐ's colophon — twenty years after Lǚ's death. The chronological organisation is unusually careful for a Sòng poetic collection.

The collection is foundational for understanding the *Jiāng-xī* school's self-articulation: Lǚ was the compiler of the *Jiāng-xī shī-pài tú*, and his own verse exemplifies the school's signature *gé-fǎ* (rhythmic-rule) of Dù Fǔ → Huáng Tíng-jiān → *Jiāng-xī* descent. Read together with [[KR4h0008]] (his *Zǐ-wēi shī-huà*) and the *Tóng-méng xùn* (KR3a series) for the full Lǚ corpus.

CBDB id 10703 confirms 1084–1145.

## Translations and research
- 曾幾 *bá* (1165) — preserved at the end of SBCK.
- *Sòng shǐ* j. 376 — Lǚ Běn-zhōng biography.
- 朱熹, *Lǚ Dōng-lái nián-pǔ* — preserves the Lǚ family lineage in *Dào-xué* perspective.
- *Sòng yuán xué-àn* — Lǚ family lineage.
- *No dedicated Western-language monograph on the collection located.* Lǚ is treated in surveys of *Jiāng-xī*-school poetics and *Yuán-yòu*-circle survival.

## Other points of interest
- The *Sì-kù* editors' tíyào of [[KR4d0153]] (Chén Yǔ-yì) discusses Chén's exclusion from the *Jiāng-xī shī-pài tú* — Chén being too young to have been included by Lǚ. The discussion provides the standard reading of the *tú*'s scope.
