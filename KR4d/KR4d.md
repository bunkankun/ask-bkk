# KR4d 別集類·宋金元 — Individual Collected Works of the Sòng, Jīn, and Yuán

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR4d sits inside the *Sìkù quánshū jí-bù* 集部 *bié-jí lèi* 別集類 (Collections → individual collected works), the Sìkù compilers' chronological *bié-jí* sequence, immediately after KR4c (Tang and Five Dynasties) and before KR4e (Ming) and KR4f (Qing). It is the **Sòng-Jīn-Yuán segment**: 415 Sòng, 5 Jīn, and 175 Yuán bié-jí, 595 entries in all — the second-largest dynastic block in KR4 after KR4f, and the largest dynastic block in KR4 if Yuán-into-early-Míng cataloging conventions are taken into account. The opening is Xú Xuàn's *Qíshěng jí* 騎省集 ([[KR4d0001]], 991 — a Five-Dynasties → Sòng transition figure whose Sòng dating places him in this division rather than KR4c) and the closing tail is the late-Yuán *Tiěyá* circle and the painter-poet Ní Zàn 倪瓚 ([[KR4d0575]], [[KR4d0576]], death 1374) on the cusp of the Míng.

The division is the **first dynastic *bié-jí* block where collected works survive in critical mass**. Wilkinson, *Chinese History: A New Manual* §30.4.3, gives the survival figures: of pre-Táng *bié-jí* only nineteen reach the Sìkù; of perhaps a thousand recorded Táng *bié-jí* roughly three hundred survive; but for the Sòng about **800 individuals' bié-jí survive** (≈ 20–30 % of those printed), and for Liáo / Jīn / Yuán together more than 250 survive. The reason is the printing revolution: from the Northern Sòng forward, *bié-jí* are printed during or shortly after their authors' lifetimes by family, disciples, and provincial offices, and are no longer dependent on a single manuscript line. Wilkinson §62.3.10 gives the headline figure that the Sìkù itself contains 355 Sòng *bié-jí*; the Kanripo extends slightly past that selection (415 Sòng entries) by including a handful of *Sìkù cún-mù* and post-Sìkù collections. For Yuán, Wilkinson §64.3.8 records about 250 extant *bié-jí*, of which 175 are catalogued here. The standard modern reference catalogues are Zhū Shàngshū 祝尚書 *Sòngrén bié-jí xùlù* 宋人別集敘錄 (rev. 2020) and Lù Jùnlíng 陸峻嶺 *Yuánrén wénjí piānmù fēnlèi suǒyǐn* 元人文集篇目分類索引 (1979).

Two structural points govern reading the division. First, the *bié-jí* form itself is functionally biographical: a Sòng or Yuán *bié-jí* contains both the literary output (*shī*, *fù*, *cí*, *jì*, *xù*, *bēi*) and the official documentary residue (*zòu-yì*, *gōng-dú*, *qǐ*, *zhuàng*, *zhì*, *biǎo*, *jiào-shū*) of a single life — making KR4d simultaneously the documentary archive of the Sòng-Yuán bureaucracy and the literary corpus of the same period (cf. §19.7: ~55 % of Táng-through-Qīng *bié-jí* are by *jìnshì* holders). Second, the Sòng innovated the now-standard arrangement of *bié-jí* contents — by genre at the top level, then chronologically within each genre — explicitly tied to the parallel Sòng establishment of the *niánpǔ* 年譜 (Wilkinson §30.4.1). This means KR4d collections can be read as ordered biographical sources in a way Táng *bié-jí* often cannot. A high proportion of KR4d entries — at least two dozen — survive only through *Sìkù* editorial reconstruction from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* 永樂大典, the most aggressive imperial recovery program of the eighteenth century.

For the period-level periodization Wilkinson is silent on the school-level subdivisions of Sòng poetry (*Xī-kūn tǐ* 西崑體, *Jiāngxī shī-pài* 江西詩派, *Yǒngjiā sì-líng* 永嘉四靈, *Jiānghú-pài* 江湖派), all of which are nevertheless basic to reading the division and for which the supplementary reference is Denecke et al., *Oxford Handbook of Classical Chinese Literature*.

## Important texts and text clusters

- **Early Northern Sòng *gǔwén* and reform-poetry pioneers (c. 990–1060).** [[KR4d0001]] *Qíshěng jí* 騎省集 (Xú Xuàn 徐鉉), [[KR4d0009]] *Xiǎoxù jí* 小畜集 (Wáng Yǔchèn 王禹偁), [[KR4d0012]] *Wǔyí xīnjí* 武夷新集 (Yáng Yì 楊億, the *Xī-kūn tǐ* anchor), [[KR4d0013]] *Lín Hé-jìng jí* 林和靖集 (Lín Bū 林逋), [[KR4d0014]] / [[KR4d0015]] *Mù cān-jūn jí* / *Hénán Mù-gōng jí* (Mù Xiū 穆修), [[KR4d0025]] *Fàn Wén-zhèng jí* 范文正集 (Fàn Zhòngyān), [[KR4d0028]] *Cúlái jí* 徂徠集 (Shí Jiè 石介), [[KR4d0034]] *Sū xué-shì jí* 蘇學士集 (Sū Shùnqīn 蘇舜欽), [[KR4d0056]] *Wǎn-líng jí* 宛陵集 (Méi Yáochén 梅堯臣). Together these document the decisive break from late-Táng / *Xī-kūn* aestheticism towards the *Qìng-lì* (1041–48) reform-period prose-and-verse program.
- **The Northern Sòng *bā dà-jiā* and the Sū circle.** Five of the six Sòng *Táng-Sòng bā dà-jiā* are present: Ōuyáng Xiū's principal *bié-jí* [[KR4d0068]] *Wén-zhōng jí* 文忠集 (153 *juǎn*, edited by Zhōu Bìdà 周必大, with a digest at [[KR4d0069]] *Ōuyáng wén-cuì*); [[KR4d0073]] *Línchuān wénjí* 臨川文集 + [[KR4d0074]] *Wáng Jīng-gōng shī-zhù* (Wáng Ānshí 王安石, with Lǐ Bì 李壁's 1207 commentary); [[KR4d0054]] *Yuán-fēng lèi-gǎo* 元豐類藁 (Zēng Gǒng 曾鞏); [[KR4d0072]] *Jiā-yòu jí* 嘉祐集 (Sū Xún 蘇洵); [[KR4d0076]] *Dōng-pō quán-jí* 東坡全集 (Sū Shì 蘇軾, the post-loss reconstruction by Cài Shì-yīng 蔡士英 in 115 *juǎn* under the Kāng-xī court) with its four-text Sòng / Yuán / Qīng commentary apparatus [[KR4d0077]]–[[KR4d0080]] and Zhā Shènxíng 查慎行's [[KR4d0081]] *Sūshī bǔ-zhù* (1702 — the only Qīng entry in the division, retained because it is part of the Sū-shī commentarial ecology); [[KR4d0082]] / [[KR4d0083]] *Luán-chéng jí* / *Yìng-zhào jí* (Sū Zhé 蘇轍).
- **The *Sū-mén sì xué-shì* 蘇門四學士 and the *Jiāngxī shī-pài*.** [[KR4d0085]] / [[KR4d0086]] *Shān-gǔ nèi-jí shī-zhù* / *wài-jí shī-zhù* (Huáng Tíngjiān 黃庭堅, with Rèn Yuān 任淵 / Shǐ Róng 史容's annotated editions, the founding text of the *Jiāngxī shī-pài*); [[KR4d0087]] / [[KR4d0088]] *Hòu-shān jí* / *Hòu-shān shī-zhù* (Chén Shīdào 陳師道, with Rèn Yuān again); [[KR4d0090]] *Zhāng yòu-shǐ wénjí* (Zhāng Lěi 張耒); [[KR4d0091]] *Huái-hǎi jí* 淮海集 (Qín Guān 秦觀); [[KR4d0105]] *Jī-lèi jí* 雞肋集 (Cháo Bǔzhī 晁補之). [[KR4d0195]] *Dōng-lái shī jí* (Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中) carries the *Jiāng-xī shī-pài tú* 江西詩派圖 — the canon-list that constituted the school as a literary identity. The principal Northern-Sòng → Southern-Sòng poet-bridge is [[KR4d0153]] *Jiǎn-zhāi jí* 簡齋集 (Chén Yǔyì 陳與義).
- **The conservative pole and the great Northern-Sòng official corpora.** [[KR4d0040]] *Chuán-jiā jí* 傳家集 (Sīmǎ Guāng 司馬光); [[KR4d0024]] *Ān-yáng jí* 安陽集 (Hán Qí 韓琦); [[KR4d0029]] *Duān-míng jí* 端明集 (Cài Xiāng 蔡襄); [[KR4d0035]] *Sū Wèi-gōng wénjí* (Sū Sòng 蘇頌, the future *Xīn-yí xiàng* astronomer); [[KR4d0061]] *Lù-gōng wénjí* (Wén Yànbó 文彥博); [[KR4d0044]] *Gōng-shì jí* + [[KR4d0045]] *Péngchéng jí* (the Liú 劉敞 / 劉攽 brothers).
- **The early *Dào-xué* and *Lǐ-xué* literary corpora.** Although the *yǔ-lù* and treatises live in KR3a / KR3c, the literary collections of the founding cohort are filed here: [[KR4d0062]] *Jī-rǎng jí* 擊壤集 (Shào Yōng 邵雍, with its distinctive *xiàng-shù*-inflected *yǒng-shǐ shī*); [[KR4d0065]] *Zhōu Yuán-gōng jí* (Zhōu Dūnyí 周敦頤); [[KR4d0136]] / [[KR4d0137]] *Guī-shān jí* / *xiānshēng yǔ-lù* (Yáng Shí 楊時); [[KR4d0182]] *Yù-zhāng wénjí* (Luó Cóngyàn 羅從彥, of the *Yán-píng sān xiānshēng*); [[KR4d0177]] *Píng-shān jí* (Liú Zǐhuī 劉子翬, one of Zhū Xī's teachers); and the central [[KR4d0226]] *Huì-ān jí* 晦菴集 (Zhū Xī 朱熹, 1265 Hú Bīng-wén edition), which Wilkinson §30.4.1 takes as the paradigmatic late-imperial *bié-jí*. The opposing *Xīn-xué* pole is [[KR4d0253]] *Xiàng-shān jí* 象山集 (Lù Jiǔyuān 陸九淵). The senior late-Sòng *Lǐ-xué* officialdom: [[KR4d0306]] *Xī-shān wénjí* 西山文集 (Zhēn Déxiù 真德秀) and [[KR4d0305]] *Hè-shān jí* 鶴山集 (Wèi Liǎowēng 魏了翁).
- **The *Jìng-kāng* trauma and Sòng patriotic literature.** A distinctive cluster running from the Jīn invasion through the early Southern-Sòng: [[KR4d0188]] *Shǎo-yáng jí* 少陽集 (Chén Dōng 陳東, executed 1127); [[KR4d0135]] *Zōng zhōng-jiǎn jí* 宗忠簡集 (Zōng Zé 宗澤); [[KR4d0138]] *Liáng-xī jí* 梁谿集 (Lǐ Gāng 李綱); [[KR4d0148]] *Zhōng-zhèng dé-wén jí* (Zhào Dǐng 趙鼎); [[KR4d0191]] *Yuè Wǔ-mù yí-wén* 岳武穆遺文 (the surviving prose of Yuè Fēi 岳飛, edited by Xú Jiē 徐階 in the Míng); [[KR4d0196]] *Dàn-ān wénjí* 澹菴文集 (Hú Quán 胡銓, of the famous 1138 anti-peace memorial). These collections are continuous in spirit with the late-Sòng loyalist cluster below and constitute the principal documentary basis for the historiography of the *Jìng-kāng* and *Shào-xīng* (1127–1162) reconstitution.
- **The *Zhōng-xīng sì dà shī-rén* 中興四大詩人.** The four canonical High Southern-Sòng poets: [[KR4d0265]] *Shí-hú shī-jí* (Fàn Chéngdà 范成大), [[KR4d0266]] *Chéng-zhāi jí* 誠齋集 (Yáng Wànlǐ 楊萬里), [[KR4d0229]] *Liáng-xī yí-gǎo* (Yóu Mào 尤袤), and the largest single-author cluster of the division at [[KR4d0267]]–[[KR4d0270]] (Lù Yóu 陸游's *Jiàn-nán shī-gǎo* 劎南詩槀, *Wèi-nán wénjí* 渭南文集, *Fàng-wēng shī-xuǎn*, and *Jiàn-gǔ jīng-xuǎn Lù Fàng-wēng*).
- **The *Yǒngjiā* and *Yǒngkāng* utilitarian schools and their Daoxue counterparts.** [[KR4d0264]] *Làng-yǔ jí* 浪語集 (Xuē Jìxuān 薛季宣, founder), [[KR4d0233]] *Zhǐ-zhāi jí* 止齋集 (Chén Fùliáng 陳傅良), [[KR4d0273]] *Shuǐ-xīn jí* 水心集 (Yè Shì 葉適, the mature *Yǒngjiā gōng-lì-xué* synthesis), [[KR4d0303]] *Lóng-chuān jí* 龍川集 (Chén Liàng 陳亮, the *Yǒngkāng* utilitarian and Zhū Xī's interlocutor in the famous *wáng-bà* exchange). Their constitutional-historical counterpart is [[KR4d0232]] *Dōng-lái jí* (Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙) and [[KR4d0284]] *Nán-xuān jí* (Zhāng Shì 張栻, edited by Zhū Xī).
- **The *Yǒngjiā sì-líng* 永嘉四靈 and the *Jiānghú-pài* 江湖派.** The reaction against *Jiāngxī*-school recondite verse: [[KR4d0297]] *Fāng-lán-xuān jí* (Xú Zhào 徐照) and [[KR4d0298]] *Èr-wēi-tíng shī-jí* (Xú Jǐ 徐璣), two of the four *Sì-líng* (the others — Zhào Shīxiù 趙師秀 and Wēng Juǎn 翁卷 — survive only outside KR4d). The *Jiānghú-pài* anchors are [[KR4d0278]] *Shí-píng shī-jí* 石屏詩集 (Dài Fùgǔ 戴復古) and [[KR4d0340]] *Hòu-cūn jí* 後村集 (Liú Kèzhuāng 劉克莊, also the principal *Jiāng-hú* anthologist).
- **The Sòng loyalist (*yí-mín* 遺民) corpus.** [[KR4d0365]] *Wén-shān jí* 文山集 + [[KR4d0366]] *Wén Xìn-guó jí Dù shī* (Wén Tiānxiáng 文天祥, including the *Yuán-dū* prison-cell *Jí Dù shī* assembled wholly from Dù Fǔ couplets); [[KR4d0367]] *Dié-shān jí* 叠山集 (Xiè Fāngdé 謝枋得); [[KR4d0395]] *Jì-shān wénjí* (Lín Jǐngxī 林景熙, of the famous *Mèng-mò shī* on the desecration of the Six Sòng tombs); [[KR4d0331]] *Sānshān Zhèng Jú-shān xiānshēng qīng-jùn jí* (attributed to Zhèng Sīxiào 鄭思肖); [[KR4d0382]] *Sì-míng wén-xiàn jí* (Wáng Yìnglín 王應麟); [[KR4d0406]] *Bó-yá qín* 伯牙琴 (Dèng Mù 鄧牧, anti-totalitarian remnant prose); [[KR4d0407]] *Cún-yǎ-táng yí-gǎo* (Fāng Fèng 方鳳); [[KR4d0374]] *Xū-xī jí* 須溪集 (Liú Chénwēng 劉辰翁, also the principal late-Sòng anthologist and commentator on Tang poetry).
- **The Jīn dynasty (5 entries).** [[KR4d0416]] *Zhuō-xuān jí* (Wáng Jì 王寂); [[KR4d0417]] *Fǔ-shuǐ jí* 滏水集 (Zhào Bǐngwén 趙秉文, the head of the late-Jīn *wén-rén*); [[KR4d0418]] *Hū-nán jí* 滹南集 (Wáng Ruòxū 王若虛); [[KR4d0419]] *Zhuāng-jìng jí* (Lǐ Jùnmín 李俊民); and the pivotal Jīn → Yuán figure [[KR4d0420]] *Yí-shān jí* 遺山集 (Yuán Hàowèn 元好問, the supreme Jīn poet whose *Zhōng-zhōu jí* preserves the rest of Jīn verse).
- **Early-Yuán northern statesmen.** [[KR4d0421]] *Zhàn-rán jū-shì jí* 湛然居士集 (Yē-lǜ Chǔcái 耶律楚材, the Khitan Buddhist who served Genghis Khan and Ögedei); [[KR4d0424]] *Líng-chuān jí* (Hè Jīng 郝經, who refused the southern Sòng seal-bearing mission and was held seventeen years); [[KR4d0464]] *Qiū-jiàn jí* (Wáng Yùn 王惲); [[KR4d0465]] *Mù-ān jí* (Yáo Suì 姚燧, the most distinguished early-Yuán prose stylist); [[KR4d0466]] *Xuě-lóu jí* (Chéng Jùfū 程鉅夫); [[KR4d0453]] *Lǔ-zhāi yí-shū* (Xǔ Héng 許衡, the northern *Lǐ-xué* master); [[KR4d0454]] *Jìng-xiū xiānshēng wénjí* (Liú Yīn 劉因, the model of non-cooperation).
- **The Sòng → Yuán literary bridge and the southern *Lǐ-xué*.** [[KR4d0431]] *Shàn-yuán wénjí* (Dài Biǎoyuán 戴表元, the principal stylistic bridge); [[KR4d0428]] *Tóng-jiāng xù-jí* (Fāng Huí 方回, surrendered Sòng prefect, compiler of the *Yíng-kuí lǜ-suǐ* 瀛奎律髓 and the principal Yuán theorist of the *Jiāngxī* tradition); [[KR4d0445]] *Sōng-xuě-zhāi jí* 松雪齋集 (Zhào Mèngfǔ 趙孟頫, calligrapher–painter–poet, the most consequential collaborating Hàn-rén of the early Yuán); [[KR4d0446]] *Wú Wén-zhèng jí* (Wú Chéng 吳澄, the southern *Lǐ-xué* master).
- **The *Yán-yòu* generation: the *Yuán shī sì dà jiā* 元詩四大家 and the *Rú-lín sì-jié* 儒林四傑.** The mature mid-Yuán imperial-academy literary establishment, crystallized after the 1313 restoration of the examinations: [[KR4d0493]] *Dào-yuán xué-gǔ lù* + [[KR4d0494]] *Dào-yuán yí-gǎo* (Yú Jí 虞集), [[KR4d0495]] *Yáng Zhòng-hóng jí* (Yáng Zǎi 楊載), [[KR4d0496]] *Fàn Dé-jī shī-jí* (Fàn Pēng 范梈), [[KR4d0497]] *Wén-ān jí* (Jiē Xī-sī 揭傒斯) — the four together = *Yuán shī sì dà jiā*. Add [[KR4d0502]] / [[KR4d0503]] *Wén-xiàn jí* / *Jīn-huá Huáng xiānshēng wénjí* (Huáng Jǐn 黃溍), [[KR4d0504]] *Guī-zhāi wénjí* (Ōuyáng Xuán 歐陽玄, chief compiler of the *Sòng-Liáo-Jīn shǐ*), [[KR4d0505]] *Dài-zhì jí* (Liǔ Guàn 柳貫), [[KR4d0473]] *Qīng-róng jū-shì jí* (Yuán Juè 袁桷); the four (Yú / Huáng / Liǔ / Ōu) = *Rú-lín sì-jié*. This is the documentary residue of the Tuō-tuō *guó-shǐ-yuàn* compilation enterprise.
- **The *sè-mù* literati operating in Chinese.** A genuine Yuán novelty: Khitan, Tangut, Uyghur, and Mongol authors with full *bié-jí*. [[KR4d0421]] (Yē-lǜ Chǔcái, Khitan Buddhist); [[KR4d0491]] *Shí-tián wénjí* 石田文集 (Mǎ Zǔcháng 馬祖常, of Önggüd Christian descent, the most distinguished *sè-mù* poet of the *Sì dà jiā* generation); [[KR4d0514]] *Yàn-mén jí* 雁門集 + [[KR4d0519]] *Sà Tiān-xī shī-jí* (Sà Dūlā 薩都拉, of Türkic / Tangut origin, the leading mid-late Yuán *sè-mù* poet).
- **The late-Yuán *Tiěyá* circle and the painter-poet bridge into the Míng.** [[KR4d0585]] *Dōng-wéi-zǐ jí* + [[KR4d0586]] *Tiě-yá gǔ yuè-fǔ* + [[KR4d0587]] *Fù-gǔ shī-jí* + [[KR4d0589]] *Lì-zé yí-yīn* (Yáng Wéizhēn 楊維楨, the transgressive *yuè-fǔ* revival); [[KR4d0575]] *Ní Yún-lín xiānshēng shī-jí* + [[KR4d0576]] *Qīng-bì-gé quánjí* (Ní Zàn 倪瓚, painter-poet of the *Yuán sì dà jiā* of painting, *Hóngwǔ*-era recluse); [[KR4d0539]] *Méi-huā dào-rén yí-mò* (Wú Zhèn 吳鎮); these mark the chronological tail.
- **The *shī-sēng* sub-corpus.** Roughly a dozen monk-poet collections distributed across the division: [[KR4d0031]] *Tán-jīn jí* 鐔津集 (Shì Qìsōng 釋契嵩); [[KR4d0093]] *Cān-liáo-zǐ shī-jí* (Shì Dàoqián 釋道潛, Sū Shì's monastic friend); [[KR4d0095]] *Shí-mén wén-zì-chán* 石門文字禪 (Shì Huìhóng 釋惠洪, the foundational *wén-zì-chán* corpus); [[KR4d0357]] *Běi-jiàn jí* (Shì Jūjiǎn 釋居簡); [[KR4d0372]] *Qián-shān jí* (Shì Wénxiàng 釋文珦). They are the documentary basis for the late-Sòng / Yuán *Chán* literary participation.

## Important persons

- [[蘇軾]] — central Northern Sòng poet, prose master, calligrapher; the most heavily commentated *bié-jí* in the division, with five-text apparatus.
- [[歐陽修]] — leader of the Sòng *gǔwén* movement and de facto Sòng literary-canon arbiter; both his own *Wén-zhōng jí* ([[KR4d0068]]) and Zhōu Bìdà's editorial residue shape the entire mid-Northern-Sòng prose ecology.
- [[王安石]] — *Xīn-fǎ* reformer; the *Línchuān wénjí* + Lǐ Bì commentary together define the Sòng *zhèng-shū* prose tradition.
- [[蘇轍]] — Sū Shì's brother and political voice; chief editor of the lost Sòng *Dōng-pō qī-jí*.
- [[蘇洵]] — eldest of the *sān Sū* 三蘇; *bā dà-jiā* essay-foundation.
- [[曾鞏]] — sixth of the *Táng-Sòng bā dà-jiā*; the model of disciplined Sòng prose.
- [[黃庭堅]] — founder of the *Jiāngxī shī-pài*; the densest Northern-Sòng commentary tradition after Sū.
- [[陳師道]] — *Sū-mén liù jūn-zǐ*; austere Sòng *gǔ-shī*.
- [[秦觀]], [[張耒]], [[晁補之]] — completing the *Sū-mén sì xué-shì* 蘇門四學士.
- [[司馬光]] — conservative pole, historian; *Chuán-jiā jí* is the literary residue of the *Zī-zhì tōng-jiàn* enterprise.
- [[范仲淹]] — *Qìng-lì* reformer; *Yuè-yáng-lóu jì* programmatic prose.
- [[梅堯臣]] and [[蘇舜欽]] — co-founders of the new mid-Sòng *gǔ-shī* style.
- [[周敦頤]] and [[邵雍]] — Dào-xué progenitors with literary collections in this division.

## Topics

- **The Sòng *gǔwén* continuation and the *Táng-Sòng bā dà-jiā* canon.** The Sòng leg of the *Táng-Sòng bā dà-jiā* — Ōuyáng Xiū, Zēng Gǒng, Wáng Ānshí, the three Sū — anchors the Sìkù conception of mature classical prose. The post-Sū Northern-Sòng *gǔwén* legacy then bifurcates into the Sòng *Dào-xué* prose tradition (Zhū Xī's letters and disquisitions) and the Yǒngjiā / Yǒngkāng utilitarian prose (Yè Shì, Chén Liàng); the trajectory continues through the Yuán in the Yú Jí / Huáng Jǐn / Liǔ Guàn / Ōuyáng Xuán generation as the model for state-document prose. KR4d is the principal documentary archive of this arc.
- **The *Xī-kūn* → reform-poetry → *Jiāngxī* → *Sì-líng* / *Jiānghú* axis of Sòng poetry.** The early-Sòng Yáng Yì–Liú Yún ornamental *Xī-kūn tǐ* is displaced in the 1040s by the Méi Yáochén / Sū Shùnqīn reform program; this matures into the Sū-Huáng *Dōng-pō* / *Shān-gǔ* style; the *Jiāngxī shī-pài* (Lǚ Běnzhōng's canonical list) carries it through the Northern-Southern Sòng transition; the *Yǒngjiā sì-líng* and the *Jiānghú-pài* mount a late-twelfth-century reaction recovering late-Tang plain-style verse; Yán Yǔ's *Cāng-làng shī-huà* codifies the resulting periodization. Almost every major *bié-jí* in KR4d either belongs to or argues against one of these positions.
- **Cí 詞 versus shī 詩 inside the *bié-jí*.** Many KR4d collections contain *cí* alongside their *shī* and *wén* (Sū Shì, Lù Yóu, Yáng Wànlǐ, Yè Mèngdé, Chén Liàng, etc.), and many of the canonical *cí* poets are present here only by their secondary genres ([[KR4d0216]] Zhāng Xiàoxiáng's *Yú-hú jí*; cf. *cí*-only collections in adjacent divisions). The *bié-jí* form thus preserves *cí* as a part of integrated authorial output rather than as a separate genre — important for the editorial history of the Sòng *cí* canon.
- **Dào-xué and Lǐ-xué literary practice.** A thread through the entire division: the founding Northern-Sòng cohort (Zhōu Dūnyí, Shào Yōng), the *Yán-píng* line (Yáng Shí, Luó Cóngyàn, Lǐ Tóng), Zhū Xī's massive *bié-jí*, the Sì-Lú-Wèi-Zhēn senior-officialdom set, and the southern / northern Yuán transmitters (Wú Chéng, Xǔ Héng). The *xīn-xué* counter-pole (Lù Jiǔyuān) and the utilitarian *Yǒngjiā* / *Yǒngkāng* alternatives are equally present. KR4d documents what *Dào-xué* looked like as a literary practice, not only as a philosophical school.
- **The *Jìng-kāng* trauma and the loyalist tradition.** Two parallel loyalist clusters: the high-Northern-Sòng-into-Southern-Sòng (Chén Dōng → Zōng Zé → Lǐ Gāng → Yuè Fēi → Hú Quán → Lù Yóu), and the late-Sòng-into-early-Yuán (Wén Tiānxiáng → Xiè Fāngdé → Lín Jǐngxī → Zhèng Sīxiào → Dèng Mù → Liú Chénwēng). Each is editorially distinctive: the first largely transmitted by family and by Sòng official offices; the second often surviving only in late-Míng or early-Qīng *jí-běn* recovery and in Sìkù *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* extraction. The pair frames KR4d as the principal documentary archive of pre-modern Chinese loyalist literary identity.
- **The Mongol-court Hàn-rén establishment and the *sè-mù* literati.** The integration of Khitan, Jurchen, Tangut, Uyghur, Önggüd, and Mongol authors into the Sòng-Yuán literary canon is a feature unique to KR4d in the entire Kanripo corpus. The Hàn-rén collaborators (Yē-lǜ Chǔcái, Xǔ Héng, Yú Jí, Zhào Mèngfǔ) and the *sè-mù* poets (Mǎ Zǔcháng, Sà Dūlā) collectively redefine what a "Chinese" *bié-jí* can be: Christian-descent, Tangut-Uyghur, and Mongol authors writing in Chinese on classical themes for a multi-ethnic court audience.
- **The *Yán-yòu* (1313) restoration of the examinations and the maturation of Yuán prose.** Most of the principal Yuán-period *bié-jí* date from after the 1313 *kē-jǔ* restoration, which produced the *Yuán shī sì dà jiā* and the *Rú-lín sì-jié*; their collections double as the documentary residue of the Tuō-tuō *guó-shǐ-yuàn* compilation effort that produced the *Sòng*, *Liáo*, and *Jīn shǐ*. The corpus is therefore not just a literary archive but a state-historiographical archive.
- **Editorial reconstruction and the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* recovery.** A high proportion of KR4d entries — including [[KR4d0004]] Pān Láng, [[KR4d0308]] Gě Shàotǐ, [[KR4d0322]], [[KR4d0332]], [[KR4d0342]] Hán Hù, [[KR4d0347]], [[KR4d0351]], [[KR4d0364]], [[KR4d0374]] Liú Chénwēng, [[KR4d0518]], [[KR4d0565]], [[KR4d0581]], [[KR4d0591]] — survive only because the eighteenth-century *Sìkù* editors extracted them from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*. KR4d is therefore also a record of what the Qīng imperial editorial enterprise judged worth recovering. The complementary editorial axis is the dense Sòng / Yuán / Míng / Qīng commentary apparatus around the canonical poets ([[KR4d0076]]–[[KR4d0081]] for Sū Shì, [[KR4d0085]]–[[KR4d0088]] for Huáng-Chén, [[KR4d0073]]–[[KR4d0074]] for Wáng Ānshí), structurally parallel to the KR4c Hán-Liǔ-Dù-Lǐ commentarial clusters.
