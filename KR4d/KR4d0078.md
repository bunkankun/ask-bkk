# Zēng-kān jiào-zhèng Wáng zhuàng-yuán jí-zhù fēn-lèi Dōng-pō xiān-shēng shī 增刊校正王狀元集註分類東坡先生詩
*Augmented and Collated, Top-Graduate Wáng's Combined Commentary, Classified Poems of Master Dōng-pō*
by [[蘇軾]] (撰), [[王十朋]] (注), [[傅藻]] (撰紀年錄)

## About the work
The *Zēng-kān jiào-zhèng Wáng zhuàng-yuán jí-zhù fēn-lèi Dōng-pō xiān-shēng shī* 增刊校正王狀元集註分類東坡先生詩 is the SBCK reprint of a Sòng *Jiàn-yáng* (Fú-jiàn) booksellers' classified-and-annotated edition of Sū Shì 蘇軾 [[蘇軾]]'s poetry, in 25 *juǎn*. The title's "*Wáng zhuàng-yuán*" is Wáng Shí-péng 王十朋 [[王十朋]] (the *Shào-xīng* 27 / 1157 *zhuàng-yuán*, top-graduate); the *jí-zhù fēn-lèi* signals the same *bǎi-jiā*-style classified-annotation as the parallel *Sìkù* recension [[KR4d0077]] but in a different (compressed: 25 *juǎn* vs. 32) edition — and crucially with Fù Zǎo 傅藻 [[傅藻]]'s *Jì-nián lù* (chronological record of composition) appended. This is the SBCK reprint, not in the *Sìkù* WYG corpus.

### Tiyao
*The KRP source is the SBCK reprint, which does not include the Sìkù tíyào.* As the *Sìkù* editors note in the parallel *Dōng-pō shī-jí zhù* tíyào [[KR4d0077]], the Wáng Shí-péng *bǎi-jiā* attribution is a *shū-sì* (booksellers') formulaic forgery — the chronological argument from the Zhào Kuí *xù* and the Wáng Shí-péng *Méi-xī jí* prefaces being decisive. The Fù Zǎo *Jì-nián lù* appended to this recension is, however, an authentic Southern-Sòng *biān-nián* (chronological) work, otherwise sparsely attested.

### Abstract
*Zēng-kān Wáng zhuàng-yuán fēn-lèi Dōng-pō shī* is a Sòng booksellers' commercial edition of the *Wáng Shí-péng bǎi-jiā* annotated Sū-shī, in compressed 25-*juǎn* format with Fù Zǎo's *Jì-nián lù* appended at the back. It is the SBCK-reprinted parallel of the *Sìkù*-cataloged *Dōng-pō shī-jí zhù* [[KR4d0077]] (in 32 *juǎn*) — both descending from the same *zēng-bǔ* (augmentation-and-supplementation) tradition. Bibliographically the principal interest is: (1) the SBCK preserved an earlier (and more compressed) recension than the *Sìkù* base, useful for collation; (2) the Fù Zǎo *Jì-nián lù* — absent from the *Sìkù* recension — is a significant Southern-Sòng *biān-nián* document on Sū's compositional chronology, one of the earliest such systematic accounts. The compressed 25-*juǎn* organization (vs. the *Sìkù* 32-*juǎn*) suggests an intermediate state in the recensional history. Dating bracket: late Sòng (Fù Zǎo *fl.*, post-1200) to the SBCK reprint (1929).

## Other points of interest
The Fù Zǎo *Jì-nián lù*, appended only to the SBCK recension and not preserved in the *Sìkù* base, is one of the few independent witnesses to Sū's compositional chronology before the major Qing annotators (Shào Cháng-héng, Zhā Shèn-xíng, Wáng Wén-gào) systematized the *biān-nián* approach.
