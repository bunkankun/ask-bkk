# Sìfēn jièběn yuēyì 四分戒本約義
*The Essential Meaning of the Sìfēn Prātimokṣa*
by [[元賢]] (Yuán Xián, 述)

## About the work

A four-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng abridged commentary on the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa* by **Yǒngjué Yuánxián** 永覺元賢 ([[元賢]], 1578–1657), the major Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master and Vinaya specialist. 約義 *yuēyì* "essential meaning" indicates a condensed exposition.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明永覺元賢述*.

### Abstract

Yuánxián's commentary represents the late-Míng Cáodòng-Chán synthesis of meditative practice with Vinaya observance. As a major Cáodòng Chán master, Yuánxián's involvement in Vinaya scholarship reflects the broader late-Míng integration of meditation and discipline pioneered by [[祩宏]] Zhūhóng, [[憨山德清]] Hānshān Déqīng, and [[智旭]] Zhìxù. The *Yuēyì* presents the *prātimokṣa* in a condensed form suited to Chán-monastic instruction.
