# Sàpóduō-bù pínì módélèjiā 薩婆多部毘尼摩得勒伽
*The Sarvāstivāda-Vinaya-Mātṛkā* (Index-Matrix of the Sarvāstivāda Vinaya)
by [[僧伽跋摩]] (Saṃghavarman, 譯)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle Chinese translation of the Sanskrit *Sarvāstivāda-vinaya-mātṛkā* — a systematic index/synopsis of the Sarvāstivāda Vinaya, expounding rule-categories, *karmavācanā* schemata, and inter-text cross-references. 摩得勒伽 *módélèjiā* transliterates *mātṛkā* ("matrix"). Translated by **Saṃghavarman** 僧伽跋摩 ([[僧伽跋摩]]) at Jiànkāng in 433 CE, contemporary with Guṇavarman's [[KR6k0015]].

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋元嘉年僧伽跋摩於長干寺譯*.

### Abstract

The *mātṛkā* genre — systematic abhidharmic-style indexes of doctrinal categories — is one of the principal exegetical genres of the early Sarvāstivāda. This Vinaya-*mātṛkā* applies the same systematic approach to the monastic discipline, treating the rules and procedures under fixed analytical headings (*saṃgha*-types, *karma*-types, *paripūrṇa*-types, etc.). The work is a critical complement to the *Sàpóduō pínì pípóshā* ([[KR6k0021]]) and the *Shísòng lǜ* ([[KR6k0016]]) for understanding the Sarvāstivāda Vinaya tradition.
