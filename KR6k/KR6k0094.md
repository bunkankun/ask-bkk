# Fànwǎngjīng púsàjiè zhù 梵網經菩薩戒注
*Annotations on the Bodhisattva Precepts of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[慧因]] (Huì Yīn, 註)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Sòng-dynasty annotated commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* bodhisattva-precepts by **Huì Yīn** 慧因 ([[慧因]]).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *宋慧因註*.

### Abstract

Huì Yīn's commentary belongs to the Sòng-period tradition of compact annotated *Fànwǎng* editions, designed for monastic instructional use. The work integrates earlier Tang scholarship into a streamlined annotation suitable for monastic teaching.
