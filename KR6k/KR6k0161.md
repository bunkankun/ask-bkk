# Sìfēn-lǜ shū 四分律疏
*Commentary on the Sìfēn-lǜ*
by [[法礪]] (Fǎ-lì, 撰述)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle Suí–early-Táng commentary on the *Sìfēn-lǜ* 四分律 ([[KR6k0001]]) by **Fǎ-lì** 法礪 ([[法礪]], 569–635), the founding patriarch of the **Xiāng-bù** 相部 school of Tang Vinaya exegesis. The work is the foundational treatise of one of the three competing Tang Vinaya schools (*Xiāng-bù* / *Nán-shān* / *Dōng-tǎ*).

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a *mù-cì* 目次 listing the contents of each *juan* (本/末 sub-divisions, "from the opening of the title to the explanation of the *fā-jiè* cause", etc.).

### Abstract

Fǎ-lì's *Shū* is the senior of the three principal Tang *Sìfēn-lǜ* commentaries, predating Dàoxuān's ([[道宣]]) *Xíng-shì chāo* and *Suí-jī jié-mó shū* by a generation. Where Dàoxuān's works are practical-pedagogical and oriented to monastic conduct, Fǎ-lì's *Shū* is a doctrinal-philosophical exposition keyed to the conceptual structure of the *Sìfēn* in the manner inherited from his teachers in the late-Suí Vinaya tradition. The *Xiāng-bù* school's distinctive doctrinal positions — including its analysis of the *jiè-tǐ* 戒體 ("substance of the precepts") in non-*Mahāyāna* terms, in contrast to Dàoxuān's *yuán-jiào* 圓教 reading — are systematically argued here. Fǎ-lì's chief disciple **Dìng Bīn** 定賓 ([[定賓]]) produced the principal sub-commentary, [[KR6k0163]] *Sìfēn-lǜ shū shì-zōng yì-jì*. The *Xiāng-bù* school flourished into the early Tang but was gradually displaced by the *Nán-shān* tradition; Fǎ-lì's *Shū* survives as our principal direct witness to the *Xiāng-bù* doctrinal position.
