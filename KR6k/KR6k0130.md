# Zīxíng chāo 資行鈔
*Sub-Commentary on the Practical Compendium and Its Resource Records*
by [[照遠]] (Shōen, 撰)

## About the work

A massive twenty-eight-fascicle Japanese sub-commentary on the Dàoxuān + Yuánzhào *Sìfēn lǜ xíngshì chāo* + *Zīchí jì* by the Japanese Risshū scholar **Shōen** 照遠 ([[照遠]]).

### Prefaces

Author: 照遠 (Shōen).

### Abstract

The *Zīxíng chāo* is one of the most extensive Japanese-Buddhist scholastic compendia on the Tang-Sòng *Sìfēn lǜ* tradition. As a Japanese work, it is preserved in the Japanese **Daizōkyō supplement** rather than the standard Chinese canon. Its 28 fascicles provide encyclopedic treatment of the DàoxuānYuánzhào framework adapted to medieval Japanese Risshū-Vinaya scholastic interests.
