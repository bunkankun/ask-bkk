# Zhī-yuán yí-biān 芝園遺編
*Posthumous Compilation from the Zhī-yuán* [Garden]
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 錄); [[道詢]] (Dào-xún, 集)

## About the work

A three-fascicle posthumous compilation of miscellaneous *Líng-zhī-sì* writings of **Yuán-zhào** ([[元照]], 1048–1116, the *Líng-zhī Dà-zhì lǜ-shī*), gathered by his Southern-Sòng disciple-successor **Dào-xún** 道詢 ([[道詢]], DILA A001543, dates not preserved). *Zhī-yuán* 芝園 ("Auspicious-Mushroom Garden") was the studio name of Yuán-zhào at *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 (lit. "Numinous-*Lingzhi*-Mushroom Monastery") in Hángzhōu; *yí-biān* 遺編 designates the residual writings not gathered into the principal *Zhī-yuán jí* 芝園集 ([[KR6k0217]]).

### Structural Division

The internal *mù-lù* gives:

- **Upper fascicle**: *Jiè-tǐ zhāng* 戒體章 (treatise on the *substance* of the precepts), *Chí-fàn tǐ zhāng* 持犯體章 (the substance of upholding-and-violating), *Chí-fàn jù-fǎ zhāng* 持犯句法章, *Lǜ-zōng yào* 律宗要 (essentials of the Vinaya school), *Dà xiǎo chéng lùn* 大小乘論 (on the great and lesser vehicles), *Shǐ-zhōng jiè yào* 始終戒要 (essentials of the precepts from beginning to end).
- **Middle fascicle**: *Shòu Dà-chéng pú-sà jiè yí* 授大乘菩薩戒儀 (rite for conferring the bodhisattva precepts), *Tì-fà yí-shì* 剃髮儀式 (tonsure rite), *Shòu-jiè fāng-biàn* 受戒方便 (preliminaries for ordination), *Jiàn Míng-zhōu Kāi-yuán-sì jiè-tán shì-wén bìng Zhèng chéng-xiàng bá* 建明州開元寺戒壇誓文(并鄭丞相跋) (founding-inscription for the *jiè-tán* 戒壇 ordination platform at *Kāi-yuán-sì* in Míng-zhōu, with the colophon by Chancellor *Zhèng* — i.e. Northern-Sòng *Zhèng Jū-zhōng* 鄭居中 or *Zhèng Jiè* 鄭挺?), *Jiè shā-mí wén* 誡沙彌文.
- **Lower fascicle**: *Wèi Jiǎng shū-mì kāi-jiǎng yào-yì (qǐng jiǎn-shī fù)* 為蔣樞密開講要義(請簡詩附) (essentials for the inaugural lecture sponsored by Privy-Counsellor *Jiǎng* — *Jiǎng Zhī-qí* 蔣之奇 (1031–1104), with the sponsorship-poem appended), *Wèi Gāo-lì sēng-tǒng Yì-tiān kāi-jiǎng yào-yì* 為高麗僧統義天開講要義 (essentials for the inaugural lecture given for the **Goryeo monk-supremo Ŭich'ŏn 義天** [1055–1101]), *Shàng Zhā-ān fǎ-shī shū* 上樝菴法師書 (letter to Master *Zhā-ān*), *Nán-shān lǜ-zōng zǔ-chéng tú-lù* 南山律宗祖承圖錄 (genealogical chart of the Nán-shān Vinaya patriarchate), *Nán-shān lǜ-shī zhuàn cháng-lù bìng bá* 南山律師撰長錄(并跋) (long catalogue of works by [Dào-xuān], with colophon).

### Prefaces

Yuán-zhào's own opening note for the *Jiè-tǐ zhāng* dates that piece precisely: *Xī-níng sān-nián, hòu ān-jū rì, shì Yuán-zhào yú Nán-shān zǔ-shī jié-mó-shū lù-chū jiè-tǐ zhāng* 熙寧三年。後安居日。釋元照於南山祖師羯磨疏錄出戒體章 — i.e. **1070 / Xī-níng 3**, day after the conclusion of the rains-retreat. This is the earliest dated piece in the collection.

### Abstract

The *Zhī-yuán yí-biān* is the **principal source for Yuán-zhào's *theological* writings on the precept-body** (*jiè-tǐ* 戒體) and his *occasional* compositions for major patrons. It contains his earliest dated independent treatise (*Jiè-tǐ zhāng*, 1070) and his two most historically resonant *kāi-jiǎng yào-yì* 開講要義 inaugural-lecture pieces — the one for *Jiǎng Zhī-qí* (Privy-Counsellor and biographer of Bái Jū-yì), and the one for *Ŭich'ŏn 義天* of Goryeo, who visited Hángzhōu in **1085** during his year of study in Sòng China. The Goryeo lecture-piece is among the more important documents of Sòng-Goryeo Buddhist contact. The *Jiàn Míng-zhōu Kāi-yuán-sì jiè-tán shì-wén* records the inscription Yuán-zhào composed for the founding of the *jiè-tán* ordination-platform at *Kāi-yuán-sì* in Míng-zhōu (Níng-bō), an important episode in the Sòng revival of formal *bhikṣu* ordination. Composition of the *yí-biān* itself is bracketed by the Xī-níng-3 piece (1070) and Yuán-zhào's death (1116); Dào-xún's posthumous *jí* 集 must postdate 1116. `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1070–1116 for the contents.

## Translations and research

- For the Yuán-zhào / Ŭich'ŏn contact: see Robert Buswell, "Ŭich'ŏn", in *Encyclopedia of Buddhism* (2004), s.v., and Sŏngch'ŏl Sŭnim's standard biographies of Ŭich'ŏn.
- For the *Jiè-tǐ zhāng* and Sòng *jiè-tǐ* doctrine: discussion in Stanley Weinstein and Ann Heirman's surveys of post-Tang Vinaya thought.

## Other points of interest

- The volume is the principal source for the chart-genealogy *Nán-shān lǜ-zōng zǔ-chéng tú-lù*, the canonical Sòng version of the Nán-shān Vinaya patriarchal lineage.
- The *Wèi Gāo-lì sēng-tǒng Yì-tiān kāi-jiǎng yào-yì* is one of the principal documentary witnesses to Sòng-Goryeo Buddhist contacts in the late 11th c.
