# Lǜchāo dìsān juǎn shǒujué 律抄第三卷手決
*Hand-Resolved Notes on the Third Fascicle of the Vinaya Excerpts* (Dunhuang)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Dunhuang fragment of "hand-resolved notes" (手決 *shǒujué* = oral-instruction notes recorded in writing) on the third fascicle of an unidentified Vinaya excerpts-collection.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The *shǒujué* genre — formal oral teaching recorded in writing — is an important late-Tang monastic-pedagogical genre. This fragment preserves the working oral instruction of an unidentified Dunhuang Vinaya master on the practical application of selected Vinaya excerpts.
