# Zhī-yuán jí 芝園集
*Collected Writings from the Zhī-yuán Garden*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 作)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Northern-Sòng *bié-jí* 別集 (literary collection) of **Yuán-zhào** ([[元照]], 1048–1116, the *Líng-zhī Dà-zhì lǜ-shī* of *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 in Hángzhōu). Where the companion *Zhī-yuán yí-biān* 芝園遺編 ([[KR6k0216]]) gathers his Vinaya-treatise fragments and lecture pieces, the *Zhī-yuán jí* preserves his **biographical, epitaphic and occasional prose**: stupa-inscriptions and life-records of contemporary monks, lay-funerary writings, and short essays. The set is the principal source for late-Northern-Sòng Hángzhōu Buddhist prosopography.

### Structural Division

**Upper fascicle** (14 pieces):
1. *Háng-zhōu Nán-píng-shān Shén-wù fǎ-shī tǎ-míng* 杭州南屏山神悟法師塔銘 — stupa-inscription for *Shén-wù fǎ-shī* of Nán-píng-shān;
2. *Háng-zhōu Xiáng-fú-sì Tōng-yì dà-shī tǎ-míng* — for *Tōng-yì dà-shī* of Xiáng-fú-sì;
3. *Xiù-zhōu Huá-tíng Chāo-guǒ-sì Zhào fǎ-shī tǎ-míng* — for *Zhào fǎ-shī* of Chāo-guǒ-sì;
4. *Táng Qí-zhōu Qīng-zhe fǎ-shī xíng-yè jì* — record of the Táng-period *Qīng-zhe fǎ-shī* of Qí-zhōu;
5. *Hú-zhōu Dōng-lín Chán-huì dà-shī xíng-yè lù* — for *Chán-huì dà-shī* of Dōng-lín-sì;
6. *Háng-zhōu Xiáng-fú-sì Jiǔ shé-lí zhuàn* — biography of *Jiǔ shé-lí*;
7. *Xiù-zhōu Chāo-guǒ-sì Wéi-zhàn fǎ-shī xíng-yè jì*;
8. *Háng-zhōu Léi-fēng Guǎng-cí fǎ-shī xíng-yè jì* — for *Guǎng-cí fǎ-shī* of Léi-fēng-tǎ;
9. *Háng-zhōu Xiáng-fú-sì Yīng fǎ-shī tǎ-míng*;
10. *Yuè-zhōu Yú-yáo Yì shé-lí tǎ-míng*;
11. *Yuè-zhōu Yú-pǔ Jìng-huì dà-shī tǎ-míng*;
12. *Wēn-zhōu Bāo fǎ-shī xíng-yè lù xù*;
13. *Hú-zhōu Bā-shèng-sì Jiàn sì-zhǔ zhuàn*;
14. *Wēn-zhōu Dū sēng-zhèng Chí-zhèng dà-shī xíng-yè jì*.

**Lower fascicle** (18 pieces): including *Xiù-zhōu Lǚ-shì líng-gǔ zàn* 秀州呂氏靈骨讚, *Kǎo-bǐ mù-zhì míng* 考妣墓誌銘 (epitaph for the author's own parents), several lay-funerary inscriptions, *Lùn zēng-jiè shū* 論增戒書, *Lùn Cí-mǐn sān-zàng jí shū* 論慈愍三藏集書 (a discourse-letter on the works of the Táng pilgrim-monk *Cí-mǐn Huì-rì* 慈愍慧日), *Sòng yī-bō shū* 送衣鉢書, *Chán yì* 讒議, *Bó-yì jiě* 博奕解, *Sì-zǐ yào-yán xù* 四子要言序, ***Gāo-lì Lǐ xiāng-gōng Lè-dào-jí xù*** 高麗李相公樂道集序 (preface for the *Lè-dào jí* of Chancellor Lǐ of Goryeo).

### Abstract

The *Zhī-yuán jí* is **the principal collection of Yuán-zhào's literary prose**, parallel to the *Zhī-yuán yí-biān* but covering biography and epitaph rather than treatise. It is one of the most important sources for the social history of Northern-Sòng Hángzhōu Buddhism: through Yuán-zhào's stupa-inscriptions and *xíng-yè jì* one can reconstruct the network of Sòng *jiào* 教 (Tiāntái-Huá-yán scholastic) abbots, their patrons, and their lineage relationships. The lower fascicle also preserves Yuán-zhào's *Gāo-lì Lǐ xiāng-gōng Lè-dào-jí xù* — his preface for the *Lè-dào jí* of a Goryeo Chancellor *Lǐ*, an important document of Sòng-Goryeo literary contact (paired with his *Wèi Gāo-lì sēng-tǒng Yì-tiān kāi-jiǎng yào-yì* preserved in [[KR6k0216]]). Composition is bracketed by Yuán-zhào's mature career and his death; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1080–1116.

## Translations and research

*No substantial secondary literature located* on the collection as such. Individual epitaphs are routinely cited in Sòng-Buddhist prosopographic studies.
