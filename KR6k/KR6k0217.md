# Zhīyuán jí 芝園集
*Collected Writings from the Zhīyuán Garden*
by [[元照]] (Yuánzhào, 作)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Northern-Sòng *biéjí* 別集 (literary collection) of **Yuánzhào** ([[元照]], 1048–1116, the *Língzhī Dàzhì lǜshī* of *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 in Hángzhōu). Where the companion *Zhīyuán yíbiān* 芝園遺編 ([[KR6k0216]]) gathers his Vinayatreatise fragments and lecture pieces, the *Zhīyuán jí* preserves his **biographical, epitaphic and occasional prose**: stupa-inscriptions and life-records of contemporary monks, lay-funerary writings, and short essays. The set is the principal source for late-Northern-Sòng Hángzhōu Buddhist prosopography.

### Structural Division

**Upper fascicle** (14 pieces):
1. *Hángzhōu Nánpíngshān Shénwù fǎshī tǎmíng* 杭州南屏山神悟法師塔銘 — stupainscription for *Shénwù fǎshī* of Nánpíngshān;
2. *Hángzhōu Xiángfúsì Tōngyì dàshī tǎmíng* — for *Tōngyì dàshī* of Xiángfúsì;
3. *Xiùzhōu Huátíng Chāoguǒsì Zhào fǎshī tǎmíng* — for *Zhào fǎshī* of Chāoguǒsì;
4. *Táng Qízhōu Qīngzhe fǎshī xíngyè jì* — record of the Táng-period *Qīngzhe fǎshī* of Qízhōu;
5. *Húzhōu Dōnglín Chánhuì dàshī xíngyè lù* — for *Chánhuì dàshī* of Dōnglínsì;
6. *Hángzhōu Xiángfúsì Jiǔ shélí zhuàn* — biography of *Jiǔ shélí*;
7. *Xiùzhōu Chāoguǒsì Wéizhàn fǎshī xíngyè jì*;
8. *Hángzhōu Léifēng Guǎngcí fǎshī xíngyè jì* — for *Guǎngcí fǎshī* of Léifēngtǎ;
9. *Hángzhōu Xiángfúsì Yīng fǎshī tǎmíng*;
10. *Yuèzhōu Yúyáo Yì shélí tǎmíng*;
11. *Yuèzhōu Yúpǔ Jìnghuì dàshī tǎmíng*;
12. *Wēnzhōu Bāo fǎshī xíngyè lù xù*;
13. *Húzhōu Bāshèngsì Jiàn sìzhǔ zhuàn*;
14. *Wēnzhōu Dū sēngzhèng Chízhèng dàshī xíngyè jì*.

**Lower fascicle** (18 pieces): including *Xiùzhōu Lǚshì línggǔ zàn* 秀州呂氏靈骨讚, *Kǎobǐ mùzhì míng* 考妣墓誌銘 (epitaph for the author's own parents), several lay-funerary inscriptions, *Lùn zēngjiè shū* 論增戒書, *Lùn Címǐn sānzàng jí shū* 論慈愍三藏集書 (a discourse-letter on the works of the Táng pilgrim-monk *Címǐn Huìrì* 慈愍慧日), *Sòng yībō shū* 送衣鉢書, *Chán yì* 讒議, *Bóyì jiě* 博奕解, *Sìzǐ yàoyán xù* 四子要言序, ***Gāolì Lǐ xiānggōng Lèdàojí xù*** 高麗李相公樂道集序 (preface for the *Lèdào jí* of Chancellor Lǐ of Goryeo).

### Abstract

The *Zhīyuán jí* is **the principal collection of Yuánzhào's literary prose**, parallel to the *Zhīyuán yíbiān* but covering biography and epitaph rather than treatise. It is one of the most important sources for the social history of Northern-Sòng Hángzhōu Buddhism: through Yuánzhào's stupa-inscriptions and *xíngyè jì* one can reconstruct the network of Sòng *jiào* 教 (Tiāntái-Huáyán scholastic) abbots, their patrons, and their lineage relationships. The lower fascicle also preserves Yuánzhào's *Gāolì Lǐ xiānggōng Lèdàojí xù* — his preface for the *Lèdào jí* of a Goryeo Chancellor *Lǐ*, an important document of Sòng-Goryeo literary contact (paired with his *Wèi Gāolì sēngtǒng Yìtiān kāijiǎng yàoyì* preserved in [[KR6k0216]]). Composition is bracketed by Yuánzhào's mature career and his death; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1080–1116.

## Translations and research

*No substantial secondary literature located* on the collection as such. Individual epitaphs are routinely cited in Sòng-Buddhist prosopographic studies.

## Other points of interest

- For the related Sòng-Goryeo material see [[KR6k0216]]'s *Wèi Gāolì sēngtǒng Yìtiān kāijiǎng yàoyì*.
