# Sìfēn lǜ xíngshì chāo zī-chí jì 四分律行事鈔資持記
*The Resource and Sustenance Records on the Sìfēn Vinaya Practical Compendium*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 撰)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Sòng-dynasty commentary on Dàoxuān's *Xíngshì chāo* ([[KR6k0128]]) by **Yuán-zhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116) of the Língzhī-yuàn 靈芝院. 資持 *zī-chí* "resource and sustenance" — the title indicates the work's role as the practical-pedagogical resource for the study of Dàoxuān's foundational compendium.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *宋餘杭沙門元照撰*.

### Abstract

Yuán-zhào's *Zī-chí jì* is the standard Sòng-period commentary on Dàoxuān's *Xíngshì chāo* and the principal medium through which the Tang Nánshān tradition was transmitted to the late-imperial Chinese Buddhist *saṅgha*. The work is foundational for understanding Dàoxuān's terminology, procedural details, and doctrinal positions, and was widely used as the working pedagogical text in late-Sòng and post-Sòng monastic instruction. Together with Dàoxuān's parent *Xíngshì chāo*, the *Zī-chí jì* constitutes the canonical East Asian practical Vinaya manual.
