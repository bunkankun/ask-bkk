# Sìfēnlǜ shū 四分律疏
*Commentary on the Sìfēn-lǜ*
by [[智首]] (Zhìshǒu, 撰)

## About the work

A surviving single fragment (originally many *juan*; only **juan 9** is preserved in the Manji canon) of the **Sìfēnlǜ shū** by **Zhìshǒu** 智首 ([[智首]], 567–635), the leading Sui–early-Táng Vinaya master and teacher of **Dàoxuān** 道宣 ([[道宣]], 596–667). The Manji edition prints the surviving fragment with the signature *沙門釋　智者　撰* (*Composed by the monk Zhìzhě*) — a graphic variant for *Zhìshǒu* common in some Manji witnesses (cf. DILA A001269); the catalog and the standard tradition correctly identify the author as Zhìshǒu.

### Prefaces

No preface in the surviving fragment.

### Abstract

Zhì-shǒu is conventionally regarded as the eighth patriarch of the Chinese *Sìfēn-lǜ* tradition; his lost commentary was the principal source from which Dàoxuān, his disciple, drew the foundational *Nán-shān-lǜ* programme. The surviving fragment treats the *yǔ-yù-yī jiè* 雨浴衣戒 ("rain-bathing garment precepts" — the rules in *prātimokṣa* nos. 27–28 governing acquisition and use of rain-cloth) and the *zhé-jiào-ní jiè* 輙教尼戒 ("rules on instructing nuns without authorization"). It analyses each rule under the formal scheme of *yuán-qǐ* 緣起 (occasion of institution), *fàn-xiāng* 犯相 (violation profile, given here as *wǔ-yuán* / *sì-yuán* 五緣/四緣 — the five or four conditions for full violation), *kāi-yuán* 開緣 (exemption clauses), and parallel-text comparison (with the *Sēng-qí lǜ* 僧祇律 and other recensions). The fragment is documentarily valuable as the only direct surviving witness to the early-Tang pre-Dàoxuān *Sìfēn-lǜ* exegesis from which the *Nán-shān* tradition emerged. The dating bracket is Zhì-shǒu's mature scholarly career.
