# Xīn shān-dìng sìfēn sēng-jièběn 新刪定四分僧戒本
*The Newly Edited Sìfēn Saṅgha Prātimokṣa*
by [[道宣]] (Dàoxuān, 刪定)

## About the work

A one-fascicle revised recension of the **Sìfēn *bhikṣu-prātimokṣa*** edited by **Dàoxuān** 道宣 ([[道宣]]) for the practical use of the Nánshān Vinaya school. 刪定 *shān-dìng* "edited and fixed" indicates the editorial method: Dàoxuān reviewed the existing transmission of the Dharmaguptaka *prātimokṣa* (KR6k0010, KR6k0011) and produced a new recension correcting transmissional errors and standardising the rule-formulations.

### Prefaces

Author's preface explains the editorial method: comparison of multiple manuscript witnesses, harmonisation with the parent *Sìfēn lǜ*, and standardisation of the rule-numbering.

### Abstract

The *Xīn shān-dìng* recension is a key witness to Dàoxuān's project of constructing a **standardised, authoritative *prātimokṣa* edition** for the canonical *Sìfēn* Vinaya tradition in early-Tang China. The work supplemented the older Buddhayaśas-period *prātimokṣa* extracts ([[KR6k0010]], [[KR6k0011]]) and provided the basis for Dàoxuān's own *Sìfēn lǜ bǐqiū hán-zhù jièběn* ([[KR6k0131]]) — the comprehensive *prātimokṣa*-with-commentary that became the universal *Sìfēn* recitation-text of East Asian Buddhism.
