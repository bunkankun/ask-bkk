# Sìfēn-lǜ kāi-zōng jì 四分律開宗記
*Records on Opening the Tenets of the Sìfēn-lǜ*
by [[懷素]] (Huái-sù, 撰)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle Tang commentary on the *Sìfēn-lǜ* 四分律 ([[KR6k0001]]) by **Huái-sù** 懷素 ([[懷素]], 624–697), the founding master of the **Dōng-tǎ** 東塔 ("Eastern Pagoda") *Sìfēn* school — the third of the three competing Tang Vinaya schools (alongside **Dàoxuān**'s *Nán-shān* and **Fǎ-lì**'s *Xiāng-bù*). *Kāi-zōng* 開宗 ("opening the school's tenets") signals the foundational doctrinal-systematic intent of the work.

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a *mù-cì* 目次 listing the contents of each *juan* in *běn / mò* 本/末 sub-divisions ("from the opening title-establishment to the explanation of the Tripiṭaka head-titles", etc.).

### Abstract

The *Kāi-zōng jì* is the principal surviving witness to the *Dōng-tǎ* 東塔 *Sìfēn-lǜ* tradition and the doctrinal counterpart to Huái-sù's celebrated *karman* compilations [[KR6k0047]] *Sēng jié-mó* and [[KR6k0048]] *Ní jié-mó*. The work mounts a systematic attack on the *Nán-shān* doctrinal positions of Dàoxuān ([[道宣]]), particularly on the *jiè-tǐ* 戒體 ("substance of the precepts") question; it represents the *Yogācāra*-influenced reading of Vinaya that follows from Huái-sù's earlier collaboration with **Xuán-zàng** 玄奘 on the *Yogācāra-bhūmi* translation. Although the *Dōng-tǎ* school did not displace the *Nán-shān* in the long run, the *Kāi-zōng jì* remained a landmark of Tang Vinaya scholarship and was studied in the Sòng-period *Lǜ-zōng* curriculum as the principal alternative voice to the *Nán-shān* synthesis. Composition belongs to Huái-sù's mature career (post-670, after his Vinaya specialisation under Dàoxuān).
