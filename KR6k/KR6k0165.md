# Sìfēnlǜ kāizōng jì 四分律開宗記
*Records on Opening the Tenets of the Sìfēnlǜ*
by [[懷素]] (Huáisù, 撰)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle Tang commentary on the *Sìfēnlǜ* 四分律 ([[KR6k0001]]) by **Huáisù** 懷素 ([[懷素]], 624–697), the founding master of the **Dōngtǎ** 東塔 ("Eastern Pagoda") *Sìfēn* school — the third of the three competing Tang Vinaya schools (alongside **Dàoxuān**'s *Nánshān* and **Fǎlì**'s *Xiāngbù*). *Kāizōng* 開宗 ("opening the school's tenets") signals the foundational doctrinal-systematic intent of the work.

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a *mùcì* 目次 listing the contents of each *juan* in *běn / mò* 本/末 sub-divisions ("from the opening title-establishment to the explanation of the Tripiṭaka head-titles", etc.).

### Abstract

The *Kāizōng jì* is the principal surviving witness to the *Dōngtǎ* 東塔 *Sìfēnlǜ* tradition and the doctrinal counterpart to Huáisù's celebrated *karman* compilations [[KR6k0047]] *Sēng jiémó* and [[KR6k0048]] *Ní jiémó*. The work mounts a systematic attack on the *Nánshān* doctrinal positions of Dàoxuān ([[道宣]]), particularly on the *jiètǐ* 戒體 ("substance of the precepts") question; it represents the *Yogācāra*-influenced reading of Vinaya that follows from Huáisù's earlier collaboration with **Xuánzàng** [[玄奘]] on the *Yogācārabhūmi* translation. Although the *Dōngtǎ* school did not displace the *Nánshān* in the long run, the *Kāizōng jì* remained a landmark of Tang Vinaya scholarship and was studied in the Sòng-period *Lǜzōng* curriculum as the principal alternative voice to the *Nánshān* synthesis. Composition belongs to Huáisù's mature career (post-670, after his Vinaya specialisation under Dàoxuān).
