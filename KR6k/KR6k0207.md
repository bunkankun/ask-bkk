# Nán-shān zǔ-shī lǐ-zàn wén 南山祖師禮讚文
*A Hymnody of Veneration for the Patriarch-Master of the Nán-shān (Vinaya) School*
by [[智圓]] (Zhì-yuán, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Northern-Sòng *lǐ-zàn wén* 禮讚文 (worship-and-eulogy liturgy) for the death-anniversary of **Nán-shān Dào-xuān** 南山道宣 (596–667), the *Chéng-zhào Dà-shī* 澄照大師, founder of the Sì-fēn-lǜ Nán-shān 四分律南山 (Four-Part Vinaya) school. Composed by **Gū-shān Zhì-yuán** 孤山智圓 ([[智圓]], 976–1022), the leader of the *Shān-wài* 山外 wing of Sòng Tiāntái. The author signature reads *Gū-shān shā-mén shì Zhì-yuán shù* 孤山沙門釋　智圓　述. The work opens the trio of Sòng *Nán-shān zǔ-shī lǐ-zàn wén* preserved as X1504–1506 (= [[KR6k0207]], [[KR6k0208]], [[KR6k0209]]); these three were brought together as a unit and prefaced (元符三年 = 1100) by **Yú-háng Yuán-zhào** 餘杭元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116), the *Líng-zhī Dà-zhì lǜ-shī* 靈芝大智律師.

### Prefaces

The 1100 *Jí Nán-shān lǐ-zàn xù* 集南山禮讚序 by Yuán-zhào (preserved at the head of the present X1504) reports the genesis: the *Gū-shān fǎ-shī* (Zhì-yuán) "first took up the brush, [his work] requested by *Lǜ-shī* of *Dōu-shuài Zé-wú* 兜率擇梧" (Dōu-shuài Zé-wú); next, the *Zhá-xī fǎ-shī* (i.e. *Jìng-jué Rén-yuè* 淨覺仁岳) added a metrical version because Zhì-yuán's lacked liturgical melody; and the *Tiāntái Zhēn-wù lǜ-shī* (i.e. *Yǔn-kǎn* 允堪) combined and re-extended the two. Yuán-zhào himself collated the three for liturgical use across the standard three-day *huì-rì* 諱日 (death-anniversary) cycle.

### Abstract

Zhì-yuán's *Nán-shān zǔ-shī lǐ-zàn wén* is the **earliest Sòng *zàn-yí* for the founder of the Nán-shān Vinaya school**, written between his entry into mature literary activity (ca. 1000) and his death in **1022**, in response to a request from a *lǜ-shī* at *Dōu-shuài-yuàn* in Mt Zé-wú. The text follows the standard *zàn-yí* structure: an opening *yī-xīn fèng-qǐng* 一心奉請 (one-mind invocation) of the master, prostrations, *zàn-cí* 讚詞 (eulogy stanzas) walking through the four-fold scheme of *Dào-xuān*'s biographical episodes (*Rì-yán jīng-shè* 日嚴精舍, transmission of the *vinaya*, the heavenly responses, the deathbed *fù-fǎ* 付法), and closing offerings. The prose preface (by Zhì-yuán himself) contains the formula *Nán-shān jiào-zhǔ Chéng-zhào Dà-shī lì-shì lì-yán dà-chǎn pí-ní zhī xué* 南山教主澄照大師立事立言大闡毗尼之學 — "the master of the Nán-shān, the Great Master Chéng-zhào, who *both established the deeds and established the words, greatly explicating the study of vinaya*." Composition is bracketed by Zhì-yuán's mature career at *Gū-shān* on West Lake; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are set 1000–1022. For the parallel later *zàn-yí* see [[KR6k0208]] (by Rén-yuè) and [[KR6k0209]] (by Yǔn-kǎn).
