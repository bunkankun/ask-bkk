# Fànwǎng púsàjièjīng yìshū fāyǐn 梵網菩薩戒經義疏發隱
*Bringing Forth the Hidden: Sub-Commentary on the Yìshū Commentary on the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[袾宏]] (Zhūhóng, 發隱)

## About the work

A five-fascicle late-Míng sub-commentary by **Yúnqī Zhūhóng** 雲棲袾宏 ([[袾宏]], 1535–1615) on ZhìyǐGuàndǐng's *Fànwǎng púsàjièjīng yìshū* ([[KR6k0084]]). 發隱 *fāyǐn* "bringing forth the hidden" expresses the genre — a sub-commentary that draws out the deeper meanings of the parent commentary.

### Prefaces

Author: 明雲棲袾宏 (Yúnqī Zhūhóng).

### Abstract

Zhūhóng's commentary is one of the foundational documents of the **late-Míng Vinaya revival**, in which Zhūhóng — together with **Hānshān Déqīng** 憨山德清 (1546–1623) and **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕩益智旭 ([[智旭]]) — sought to recover practical Vinaya scholarship after the long Míng decline of monastic discipline. Zhūhóng's emphasis on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts as the foundation of *both* monastic and lay Buddhist ethics shaped the late-Míng synthesis of Pure Land devotion, Chán meditation, and bodhisattva-discipline. Together with the auxiliary texts [[KR6k0087]] (*shìyì*) and [[KR6k0088]] (*wènbiàn*), this work constitutes Zhūhóng's complete *Fànwǎng* commentary.
