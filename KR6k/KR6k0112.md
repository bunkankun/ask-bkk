# Jì-tiáo-yīn suǒ-wèn jīng 寂調音所問經
*The Sūtra of the Inquiry of [the Bodhisattva] Quiet-Harmonious-Voice*
by [[法海]] (Fǎ Hǎi, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Mahāyāna *sūtra* in question-and-answer form, translated by **Fǎ Hǎi** 法海 ([[法海]]) under the Liú-Sòng. The bodhisattva *Jì-tiáo-yīn* 寂調音 ("Quiet-Harmonious-Voice") inquires of the Buddha about points of bodhisattva-practice and discipline.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋沙門法海譯*.

### Abstract

The text belongs to the *bodhisattva-paripṛcchā* genre — Mahāyāna *sūtras* in which a named bodhisattva poses doctrinal-disciplinary questions to the Buddha. The bodhisattva *Jì-tiáo-yīn* is otherwise unattested, suggesting either a localised cult or a generic naming. The text's inclusion in the Vinaya division reflects its precept-content.
