# Lǜ záchāo 律雜抄
*Miscellaneous Vinaya Excerpts* (Dunhuang fragment)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous miscellaneous Vinaya excerpts-collection, preserved in Dunhuang. 雜抄 *záchāo* "miscellaneous excerpts" indicates the genre — a compilation of selected Vinaya passages on various topics.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The text is one of the Dunhuang Vinaya manuscript fragments in Taishō volume 85, presenting a working monastic compilation of selected Vinaya passages on various practical-disciplinary points. Such miscellanies were typical of monastic-pedagogical use in late-Tang Buddhist communities.
