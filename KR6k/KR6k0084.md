# Fànwǎng púsàjièjīng yìshū 梵網菩薩戒經義疏
*Commentary on the Brahmajāla Bodhisattva Precept Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 記)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Sui-dynasty commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* by **Guàndǐng** 灌頂 ([[灌頂]], 561–632), the principal disciple-redactor of **Zhìyǐ** 智顗 ([[智顗]]). The relationship to [[KR6k0078]] (which is also attributed to Guàndǐng's redaction of Zhìyǐ's teaching) reflects the complex transmission of Tiāntái commentary literature: this independent work appears to be Guàndǐng's own commentary as distinct from his recording of Zhìyǐ's teaching.

### Prefaces

Compiler's colophon: *隋天台沙門灌頂記*.

### Abstract

Guàndǐng's independent commentary represents the **second-generation Tiāntái scholastic** treatment of the *Fànwǎng* tradition, building on Zhìyǐ's foundational teaching but extending the doctrinal exposition into more systematic scholastic form. Together with [[KR6k0078]], it is one of the foundational Tiāntái texts on the bodhisattva-precepts.
