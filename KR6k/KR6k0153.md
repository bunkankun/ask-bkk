# Pí-ní guān-yào shì-yì 毗尼關要事義
*The Essentials of Vinaya: Glosses on Affairs and Meanings*
by [[德基]] (Dé-jī, 輯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle glossary that serves as the lexical and historical companion to **Dìng-ān Dé-jī**'s ([[德基]], 1634–1700/01) sixteen-juan *Pí-ní guān-yào* ([[KR6k0152]]). Editorial signature: 清金陵寶華山律學沙門　德基　輯 (*Compiled by Dé-jī, Vinaya monk of Bǎo-huá-shān at Jīn-líng of the Qīng*). The work proceeds entry by entry through the names, places, dynasties, and technical terms cited in the *Guān-yào* and supplies short philological and historical glosses.

### Prefaces

No separate preface in the source; the work serves as a companion glossary to [[KR6k0152]] and shares its preface and editorial framing.

### Abstract

The *Shì-yì* is a working glossary written for the early-Qīng Bǎo-huá-shān curriculum. The opening entries gloss the dynastic names cited at the head of *Sìfēn-lǜ* translations (Yáo Qín 姚秦, Yíng Qín 嬴秦, Qǐfú Qín 乞伏秦, Fú Qín 符秦), distinguishing them historically and identifying the geographical referents (Jīn-chéng 金城 = Lín-táo Prefecture in Shǎn-xī; Yuàn-chuān 苑川 = Bǎo-jī county in Fèng-xiáng, Shǎn-xī, etc.). It then proceeds to the *wǔ-míng* 五明 (the five Indian sciences — *śabda*, *hetu*, *cikitsā*, *śilpa*, *adhyātma*) and the doctrinal vocabulary of the *prātimokṣa*. As a working monastic-school glossary, the *Shì-yì* is invaluable for reconstructing the standard pedagogical handling of *Sìfēn* terminology in the late-seventeenth-century Vinaya school. Composition is contemporary with [[KR6k0152]] (between 1679 and 1700/01).
