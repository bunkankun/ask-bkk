# Sìfēn bǐqiūnī chāo kē 四分比丘尼鈔科
*Structural Outline of the Compendium on Bhikṣuṇī Rules of the Sìfēn-lǜ*
by [[允堪]] (Yǔn Kān, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) of Dàoxuān's three-juan **Sìfēn bǐqiūnī chāo** 四分比丘尼鈔 ([[KR6k0155]]) by **Yǔn Kān** 允堪 ([[允堪]], 1005–1061), the leading Northern Sòng Vinaya master of the **Nán-shān** 南山 lineage at Hángzhōu's Línghuá-sì 靈花寺. *Kē* outlines were a standard genre of the Sòng Vinaya tradition: each major commentary by Dàoxuān received a numbered subdivision-tree from a Sòng successor as a study aid. Yǔn Kān's signature is *天台沙門　允堪　述* (*Composed by Yǔn Kān, monk of Tiāntái*).

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the *kē* opens directly with its three-tier subdivision (initial title, body, transmission).

### Abstract

The *Kē* divides Dàoxuān's [[KR6k0155]] *Sìfēn bǐqiūnī chāo* into a tree of nested topics keyed to the head-words of the source text, allowing a reader to navigate the work systematically. As Yǔn Kān's date can be securely placed within his lifetime (1005–1061), the work belongs to his mature Vinaya output, which forms the core of the eleventh-century Sòng Nán-shān-lǜ revival together with his commentaries on the [[KR6k0144]] *Sìfēn-lǜ hán-zhù jiè-běn shū* and on [[KR6k0156]] *Sìfēn-lǜ suí-jī jié-mó shū*.
