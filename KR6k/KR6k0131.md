# Sìfēn lǜ bǐqiū hánzhù jièběn 四分律比丘含注戒本
*The Bhikṣu Prātimokṣa with Embedded Annotations*
by [[道宣]] (Dàoxuān, 述)

## About the work

A three-fascicle *bhikṣu-prātimokṣa* with embedded interlinear annotations by **Dàoxuān** 道宣 ([[道宣]]). 含注 *hán-zhù* "embedded annotations" indicates the format — the parent *prātimokṣa* text is presented in full with explanatory notes inserted directly within the rule-text rather than as separate commentary.

### Prefaces

Author's preface explains the editorial method and the pedagogical purpose: to provide the *prātimokṣa* text in a form that teaches itself through embedded gloss.

### Abstract

The *Hán-zhù jièběn* is the third of the **Three Great Works of the Nánshān school** (along with [[KR6k0046]] *Suíjī jiémó* and [[KR6k0128]] *Xíngshì chāo*). After the imperial canonisation of the *Sìfēn lǜ* in 709 CE, this work became the universal **Sìfēn *prātimokṣa* recitation-text with commentary** for monastic instruction throughout East Asian Buddhism. Its embedded-gloss format proved especially effective for teaching new monks the practical meaning and application of each rule — making it the standard pedagogical text for the *Sìfēn* tradition.
