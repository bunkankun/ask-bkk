# Shāmínī lí-jiè wén 沙彌尼離戒文
*The Text of the Liberation Precepts for Novice Nuns*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous compilation of the ten precepts for *śrāmaṇerī* 沙彌尼 (novice nuns), framed as a recitation-text. Translator and date unknown.

### Prefaces

No translator-attribution survives.

### Abstract

The *Shāmínī lí-jiè wén* presents the *śrāmaṇerī* precepts in continuous recitation-format, similar in function to a *prātimokṣa* but for the novice rank. The title 離戒 *lí-jiè* "liberation precepts" reflects the Pāli *vimutti-sīla* concept — the ten precepts as conducive to liberation. Together with [[KR6k0063]] this text supplied the practical Vinaya for the early-medieval Chinese *śrāmaṇerī*-saṅgha.
