# Lǜ-yào hòu-jí 律要後集
*A Sequel-Anthology of Vinaya Essentials*
by [[智旭]] (Zhì-xù, 集)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng *vinaya* anthology by **Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù** ([[智旭]], 1599–1655), conceived as a supplement (*hòu-jí* 後集 "later collection") to his earlier *Zài-jiā lǜ-yào* (now preserved in the expanded form [[KR6k0243]] *Zài-jiā lǜ-yào guǎng-jí*) and his *Píní hòu-jí wèn-biàn* 毗尼後集問辯.

### Structural Division

The internal *mù-cì* gives the work's contents:

1. *Sòng huò běn* 誦或本 — note: *"taken from the *Dì-chí jiè-pǐn* in the *Pú-sà-jiè běn-jīng*; therefore here omitted"* (i.e. the user is referred to the canonical source).
2. ***Pú-sà-jiè jié-mó wén shì* 菩薩戒羯磨文釋** — a doctrinal-procedural commentary on the *karma-vācanā* of the bodhisattva-precept conferral (preserved in fascicle 61 of the Xùzàng-jīng).
3. ***Chóng-dìng shòu pú-sà-jiè fǎ* 重定授菩薩戒法** — Zhì-xù's revised procedure for the conferral of the bodhisattva precepts.
4. ***Xué pú-sà-jiè fǎ* 學菩薩戒法** — the practitioner's protocol for studying the bodhisattva precepts.
5. *Fàn-wǎng jīng chàn-huǐ xíng-fǎ* 梵網經懺悔行法 — note: "found in the *Zài-jiā lǜ-yào guǎng-jí*; therefore here omitted." (Cf. [[KR6k0245]], where this is also separately preserved.)
6. ***Pí-ní hòu-jí wèn-biàn* 毗尼後集問辯** — Zhì-xù's *vinaya* question-and-disputation collection.

### Abstract

The *Lǜ-yào hòu-jí* gathers Zhì-xù's late-stage ritual-and-question productions in *Vinaya*. The core item is *Chóng-dìng shòu pú-sà-jiè fǎ* — Zhì-xù's revised procedure for bodhisattva-precept conferral, in which the candidate first formally **invokes the bodhisattva-master**, prostrates to the past-and-future Buddhas of the four directions and the Three Jewels, recites the *guǎng-yuàn* 廣願 (great vows), and finally receives the *sān-jù jìng-jiè* 三聚淨戒 (three pure precepts: *zhēng-fó* 證佛, *shàn-fǎ* 善法, *shè-zhòng-shēng* 攝眾生). The procedure is independently designed (it does not follow Zhū-hóng's *Pú-sà-jiè-yì shū fā-yǐn* 菩薩戒義疏發隱) and supplied a benchmark for subsequent late-Míng / Qīng bodhisattva-precept conferrals. Composition is bracketed by Zhì-xù's mature commentarial period and his death in 1655; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are 1620–1655.
