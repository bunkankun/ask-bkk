# Fó shuō bìchú jiāshījiā shífǎ jīng 佛說苾芻迦尸迦十法經
*The Sūtra on the Ten Practices of the Bhikṣu of Kāśyapa*
by [[法天]] (Fǎtiān, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period translation by **Fǎtiān** 法天 ([[法天]]) of an Indian *sūtra* on the **ten practices** of the *bhikṣu* — likely associated with the Kāśyapīya school (迦尸迦 *jiā-shī-jiā* transliterates *kāśyapika*).

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋天竺三藏法天奉詔譯*.

### Abstract

The "ten practices" — like the "five practices" of [[KR6k0068]] — present a doctrinal-ethical framework for monastic life. The reference to **Kāśyapa** (Skt. *kāśyapika*) in the title may identify the text as belonging to the Kāśyapīya school tradition, parallel to the school's *prātimokṣa* preserved at [[KR6k0041]].
