# Fó shuō Dàshèng jiè jīng 佛說大乘戒經
*The Mahāyāna Precept Sūtra*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period translation of an Indian Mahāyāna precept sūtra by **Dānapāla** 施護 ([[施護]]) at the imperial Yìjīngyuàn 譯經院, late 10th to early 11th century.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋西天三藏施護奉詔譯*.

### Abstract

The text is one of the late-Sòng *bodhisattva-vinaya* translations from the Northern Sòng imperial translation project, recovering Indian Mahāyāna precept material absent from the older Chinese canon. Like the parallel Mūlasarvāstivāda translations of Yìjìng ([[KR6k0023]]–0040), these Sòng translations arrived too late to displace the established canonical literature, but they preserve significant Indian doctrinal-disciplinary material.
