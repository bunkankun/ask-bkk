# Sìfēn-lǜ shí pí-ní yì-chāo kē 四分律拾毗尼義鈔科
*Structural Outline of the Sìfēn-lǜ Compendium of Vinaya Meanings*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 錄)

## About the work

A single-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) of **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēn-lǜ shí pí-ní yì-chāo* 四分律拾毗尼義鈔 ([[KR6k0176]]) by **Yuán-zhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116). Author signature: *餘杭靈芝蘭若　元照　錄* (*Recorded by Yuán-zhào, of the Líng-zhī monastery in Yú-háng*).

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the *kē* opens directly with its two-fold structural division — *jiào-kān yì-chāo xù kē-fēn* 校勘義鈔序科分 ("structural division of the corrected preface to the *Yì-chāo*") — and proceeds *piān*-by-*piān* through Dàoxuān's lemma structure.

### Abstract

The *Kē* serves as the structural index for the parent text [[KR6k0176]] *Sìfēn-lǜ shí pí-ní yì-chāo* in Yuán-zhào's *jiào-kān* 校勘 (collated) recension. Yuán-zhào's preface to the *Yì-chāo* (preserved at [[KR6k0176]] No. 747-A) is here divided structurally; the two works together constitute a coherent late-eleventh-century *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 editorial project to recover and structurally analyse Dàoxuān's lesser-known Vinaya digest. Composition belongs to Yuán-zhào's mature period (post-1075).
