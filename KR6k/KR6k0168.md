# Sìfēn-lǜ xíng-shì chāo kē 四分律行事鈔科
*Structural Outline of the Sìfēn-lǜ Compendium of Practical Affairs*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 錄)

## About the work

A three-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) of **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēn-lǜ shān-fán bǔ-quē xíng-shì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]) by **Yuán-zhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116), the Northern Sòng *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 *Nán-shān-lǜ* revivalist. Author signature: *餘杭郡沙門釋　元照　錄* (*Recorded by Yuán-zhào, monk of Yú-háng commandery*). The *kē* serves as the structural index for Yuán-zhào's principal substantive sub-commentary on the *Xíng-shì chāo*, the *Zī-chí jì* 資持記 (in the Manji canon as X41 etc.).

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the *kē* opens directly with the three-fold structural division *xù yuán fā-qǐ* 敘緣發起 (narrative cause and origin) — *zhèng-míng zōng-tǐ* 正明宗體 (right exposition of school-substance) — *liú-tōng* 流通 (transmission), and proceeds *piān*-by-*piān* through every nested subdivision of the *Xíng-shì chāo*.

### Abstract

The *Kē* is the structural counterpart to Yuán-zhào's *Zī-chí jì* on the *Xíng-shì chāo*; together with the *Kē* on the *Suí-jī jié-mó shū* ([[KR6k0157]]) and the *Kē* on the *Hán-zhù jiè-běn shū* (in [[KR6k0144]]), it forms the late-eleventh-century *Líng-zhī-sì* programme of structural-philological work on Dàoxuān's three principal *Sìfēn-lǜ* commentaries. These structural outlines became the dominant pedagogical tool for the Sòng-period *Lǜ-zōng* and were exported to Japan and Korea as part of the Sòng Vinaya curriculum. Composition belongs to Yuán-zhào's mature period (post-1075).
