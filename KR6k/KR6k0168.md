# Sìfēnlǜ xíngshì chāo kē 四分律行事鈔科
*Structural Outline of the Sìfēn-lǜ Compendium of Practical Affairs*
by [[元照]] (Yuánzhào, 錄)

## About the work

A three-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) of **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēnlǜ shānfán bǔquē xíngshì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]) by **Yuánzhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116), the Northern Sòng *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 *Nánshānlǜ* revivalist. Author signature: *餘杭郡沙門釋　元照　錄* (*Recorded by Yuánzhào, monk of Yúháng commandery*). The *kē* serves as the structural index for Yuánzhào's principal substantive sub-commentary on the *Xíngshì chāo*, the *Zīchí jì* 資持記 (in the Manji canon as X41 etc.).

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the *kē* opens directly with the three-fold structural division *xù yuán fāqǐ* 敘緣發起 (narrative cause and origin) — *zhèngmíng zōngtǐ* 正明宗體 (right exposition of school-substance) — *liútōng* 流通 (transmission), and proceeds *piān*-by-*piān* through every nested subdivision of the *Xíngshì chāo*.

### Abstract

The *Kē* is the structural counterpart to Yuánzhào's *Zīchí jì* on the *Xíngshì chāo*; together with the *Kē* on the *Suíjī jiémó shū* ([[KR6k0157]]) and the *Kē* on the *Hánzhù jièběn shū* (in [[KR6k0144]]), it forms the late-eleventh-century *Língzhīsì* programme of structural-philological work on Dàoxuān's three principal *Sìfēnlǜ* commentaries. These structural outlines became the dominant pedagogical tool for the Sòng-period *Lǜzōng* and were exported to Japan and Korea as part of the Sòng Vinaya curriculum. Composition belongs to Yuánzhào's mature period (post-1075).
