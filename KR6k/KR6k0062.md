# Fó shuō shāmí shí-jiè yí-zé jīng 佛說沙彌十戒儀則經
*The Sūtra of the Ten Precepts and Procedural Norms for Novice Monks*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period translation of a Sanskrit *śrāmaṇera*-precepts sūtra by **Dānapāla** 施護 ([[施護]]) at the imperial Yìjīng-yuàn 譯經院, late 10th to early 11th century.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋西天三藏施護奉詔譯*.

### Abstract

The text is one of the late Sòng translations of Indian *śrāmaṇera*-precept manuals, supplementing the older Chinese translations (KR6k0060, KR6k0061). Dānapāla's translation reflects the Indian Mūlasarvāstivāda recension of the novice precepts as practised in 10th-century India. The text was used as a supplementary practical handbook for novice training in Sòng-period Chinese monasteries.
