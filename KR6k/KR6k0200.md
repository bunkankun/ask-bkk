# Wú-zhōng shí-fó xiàng-hǎo chàn-yí 吳中石佛相好懺儀
*A Repentance Liturgy for the Marks-and-Auspicious-Signs of the Stone Buddha in Wú*
by [[傳燈]] (Chuán-dēng, 集)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle late-Míng *chàn-yí* 懺儀 (repentance liturgy) compiled by **Yōu-xī Chuán-dēng** 幽溪傳燈 ([[傳燈]], 1554–1628), the major late-Míng Tiāntái 天台宗 master at Gāomíng-sì 高明寺 on Mount Tiāntái, for use at the **Wú-zhōng** 吳中 (Sūzhōu region) **stone-Buddha** site — likely the famous **Liáng-cháo** colossal stone Buddha at *Shí-fó-sì* 石佛寺 in Sūzhōu region. The full title in some witnesses is *Lǐ Wú-zhōng shí-fó qǐ-zhǐ yí-shì* 禮吳中石佛起止儀式 (*Procedural Manual for Reverencing the Stone Buddha at Wú-zhōng, from Beginning to End*) — the form preserved in the source-text head.

### Prefaces

The work opens directly with the liturgical opening formulas: *一切恭敬* ("complete respect"), *一心頂禮十方常住三寶* ("with one mind I prostrate to the abiding Three Jewels of the ten directions"), followed by the *yī-bài* 一拜 (one bow) and *hù-guì shāo-xiāng sàn-huā* 互跪燒香散華 ("kneeling alternately, burning incense, scattering flowers"). The opening *xiāng-huā gòng-yǎng* 香華供養 verse: *願此香華雲徧滿十方界* ("may this cloud of fragrance and flowers pervade the ten directions") establishes the standard *Tiāntái chàn-yí* opening.

### Abstract

The *Shí-fó xiàng-hǎo chàn-yí* is a specialised repentance liturgy designed for performance at the *Wú-zhōng* stone-Buddha site, focusing devotionally on the *xiàng-hǎo* 相好 (the *lakṣaṇa* and *anuvyañjana*, "the marks and the auspicious signs" of a Buddha-body). The liturgy belongs to Chuán-dēng's broader *chàn-yí* corpus — produced for specific local devotional sites in his Tiāntái-mountain orbit — and reflects the integrated Tiāntái-Pure-Land devotional programme he developed at Gāomíng-sì. Author signature: *天台山幽溪沙門　傳燈　集* (*Compiled by Chuán-dēng, monk of Yōu-xī on Mount Tiāntái*). Composition is bracketed by Chuán-dēng's residence at Gāomíng-sì from 1587 and his death in 1628.
