# Chóng-zhì pí-ní shì-yì jí-yào 重治毗尼事義集要
*Re-edited Compendium of Vinaya Affairs and Meanings*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 彙釋)

## About the work

A seventeen-fascicle revised digest of *Sìfēn-lǜ* 四分律 vinaya material compiled by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 ([[智旭]], 1599–1655), the late-Míng integrative Buddhist master who is one of the *Wǎn-Míng sì dà gāosēng* 晚明四大高僧 ("Four Great Masters of Late Míng Buddhism"). The work is a *chóng-zhì* 重治 ("re-edited") version of an earlier compilation by Zhìxù; the post-colophon note in the source records that the original ran to eighteen *juan*, with the eighteenth (*Jiè-dù lüè-shì* 犍度略釋) circulating separately and edited in this Manji recension as a stand-alone work ([[KR6k0174]]).

### Prefaces

The 卍 Xuzangjing edition opens with two prefaces: a *Chóng-zhì pí-ní shì-yì jí-yào xù* 重治毗尼事義集要敘 by the Vinaya monk **Zhì-tái** 智[台/十] of Jìn-zhōu 晉州 (the source preserves the unusual character form 台/十 in the name), recording that the editio princeps was prepared at the urging of **Jiǔ-shèng** 九聖, **Huì-shí** 慧石, **Fó-bì** 佛璧, and **Qiān-shòu** 千壽 with the unblemished Vinaya master **Wú-xiá** 無瑕 of Jìn-Liáo as the principal collator; and Zhìxù's autobiographical *xù* opening with his birth on the third day of the fifth month of Wàn-lì 27 = 1599. The author's terminal *bá* is dated *gēng-yín* 庚寅 8th month 8th day = **1650** (Shùnzhì 7), which therefore fixes the *terminus ad quem* of the present recension; the underlying first compilation was prepared in the late-Chóngzhēn (after 1632) when Zhìxù's Vinaya career was already mature.

### Abstract

The *Jí-yào* digests, classifies, and re-arranges the standard *Sìfēn* vinaya material — pātimokkha, *jiémó* 羯磨 (karmavācanā), *jiē-dù* 犍度 (skandhaka) — in a form designed for late-Míng monastic use. Zhìxù was active in Vinaya scholarship throughout his career: the work belongs together with his *Pí-ní shì-yì yào-lüè* 毗尼事義要略 and *Sìfēn-lǜ-zàng dà-xiǎo chí-jiè jiē-dù lüè-shì* ([[KR6k0174]]) as a coherent late-Míng programme of Vinaya synthesis. As a Tiāntái-Chán-Pure Land synthesist, Zhìxù was atypical among Vinaya commentators of his generation: his interest in the Vinaya was framed by the late-Míng concern (shared with Zhūhóng, Hānshān Déqīng, and the Bǎo-huá-shān revivalists) for restoring observant monastic discipline as the precondition for meditative and devotional practice. The text was eventually integrated into the *Bǎo-huá-shān* Vinaya curriculum and supplied with a follow-up gloss, *Pí-ní guān-yào* 毗尼關要 ([[KR6k0152]]), by **Dìng-ān Dé-jī** 定菴德基 ([[德基]], 1634–1700/01).
