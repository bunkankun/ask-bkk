# Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn sījì 梵網經菩薩戒本私記
*Personal Notes on the Brahmajāla Bodhisattva Precept-Text*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 造)

## About the work

A one-fascicle commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* bodhisattva-precepts by the great Silla-dynasty Korean Buddhist philosopher **Wǒnhyo** 元曉 ([[元曉]], 617–686). 私記 *sījì* "personal notes" indicates the genre — informal exegetical notes rather than a formal scholastic commentary.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *新羅元曉造*.

### Abstract

Wǒnhyo is the foundational systematic philosopher of Korean Buddhism, the author of the *Daśabhūmika-sūtra-vibhāṣā* commentary, the *Awakening of Faith* commentary (*Qǐ-xìn lùn shū* 起信論疏), and over seventy other works. His *Fànwǎng* commentary, though brief, is significant as one of the **three principal Silla** commentaries on the bodhisattva-precepts (alongside Ŭijŏk's [[KR6k0082]] and Taehyŏn's [[KR6k0083]]). Wǒnhyo's distinctive **harmonising** (和諍 *hwajaeng*) interpretive method — reconciling apparently contradictory doctrinal positions — is applied to the *Fànwǎng* precepts to integrate the bodhisattva-precept tradition with both the Yogācāra and Madhyamaka philosophical frameworks.
