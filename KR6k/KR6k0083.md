# Fànwǎng-jīng gǔ-jì jì 梵網經古迹記
*Records of the Old Traces on the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[太賢]] (Tài-xián / Taehyŏn, 集)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Silla-dynasty commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* by **Taehyŏn** 太賢 ([[太賢]]) — the major Silla Yogācāra scholar of the 8th century. *古迹* "old traces" indicates that the commentary preserves earlier exegetical traditions; the genre is a compendium that gathers and synthesises prior commentaries.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *新羅國青丘沙門太賢集*.

### Abstract

Taehyŏn's *Fànwǎng-jīng gǔ-jì jì* is one of the **three principal Silla commentaries** on the *Fànwǎng jīng*, alongside Ŭijŏk's [[KR6k0082]] and Wǒnhyo's [[KR6k0090]]. The text presents a synthetic Yogācāra-Cí'ēn reading drawing on Tang scholarship while preserving distinctly Korean exegetical traditions. Like Ŭijŏk's commentary, it is included in the Taishō canon as representative East Asian commentary literature.
