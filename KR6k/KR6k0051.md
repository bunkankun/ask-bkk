# Shìchāmónánī jièběn 式叉摩那尼戒本
*The Śikṣamāṇā Prātimokṣa* (rules for probationer-nuns)
by [[弘贊]] (Hóngzàn, 輯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle compendium of the rules for **śikṣamāṇā** 式叉摩那尼 (probationer nuns — *bhikṣuṇī*-aspirants undergoing a two-year preparatory training before full ordination), compiled by the Míng-dynasty Vinaya specialist **Hóngzàn** 弘贊 ([[弘贊]], 1611–1685). 式叉摩那 transliterates Skt. *śikṣamāṇā* "she who is being trained". The text supplies the practical rule-set for the intermediate stage between *śrāmaṇerī* (novice nun) and full *bhikṣuṇī*.

### Prefaces

Author's preface: *明弘贊輯*.

### Abstract

The *Shìchāmónánī jièběn* is the principal Chinese reference for the **probationary nun** stage in Dharmaguptaka practice. The *śikṣamāṇā* status is unique to the *bhikṣuṇī*-track in the Vinaya: female aspirants must spend two years as probationers, observing the six fundamental precepts (六法 *liù-fǎ*) before they may be ordained as *bhikṣuṇīs*. The rules are extracted from the parent *Sìfēn lǜ* and supplemented by Hóngzàn's commentary on traditional Vinaya practice. The text was important for the Míng-period revival of the Chinese *bhikṣuṇī*-saṅgha.
