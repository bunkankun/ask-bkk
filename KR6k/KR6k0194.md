# Pú-sà jiè-běn chí-fàn yào-jì 菩薩戒本持犯要記
*Essential Records on Keeping and Violating the Bodhisattva Precepts*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Silla bodhisattva-precept treatise by **Wǒnhyo** 元曉 ([[元曉]], 617–686), the foundational Korean Buddhist philosopher. Author signature: *新羅國沙門元曉述* (*Composed by the Silla monk Wǒnhyo*).

### Prefaces

The work opens with Wǒnhyo's distinctive doctrinal *xù*: *菩薩戒者。返流歸源之大津。去邪就正之要門也* ("The bodhisattva-precepts are the great ford for turning back the stream and returning to the source; the essential gate for forsaking the wrong and pursuing the right"). The preface introduces the work's central problematic — *xié-zhèng zhī xiàng yì làn* 邪正之相易濫 ("the marks of right and wrong are easily confused") — and proceeds to expound the gradations under four heads: outer-conduct conformity with hidden-internal violation, outer-action defilement with internal purity, action-merit producing major harm, action-conformity with deeper disharmony.

### Abstract

The *Chí-fàn yào-jì* is one of Wǒnhyo's most distinctive Vinaya works and presents his programmatic *intentionality-centred* analysis of the bodhisattva precepts: outer compliance is not sufficient; the precepts are *kept* and *violated* fundamentally at the level of inner intention (*xīn* 心) and contextual interpretation. The work is closely related to Wǒnhyo's broader *bodhisattva-bhūmi* commentarial programme and, alongside [[KR6k0197]] *Dà-shèng liù-qíng chàn-huǐ* on Mahāyāna repentance, represents his contribution to the ethical-meditational integration of Mahāyāna practice. Composition belongs to Wǒnhyo's mature productive period after his famous turn-back from the Tang pilgrimage with Ǔisang 義湘.
