# Gēnběn shuō-yīqiē-yǒu-bù pínàiyē nítuónà-mùdéjiā shèsòng 根本說一切有部毘奈耶尼陀那目得迦攝頌
*Summary Verses of the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya Nidāna-Mātṛkā*
by [[義淨]] (Yìjìng, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Chinese translation of the **summary verses** (攝頌 *shè-sòng* = Skt. *uddāna* / *piṇḍoddāna*) for the Mūlasarvāstivāda *Nidāna-mātṛkā* ([[KR6k0033]]). The verse-summaries serve as mnemonic indexes to the parent text. Translated by **Yìjìng** ([[義淨]]) in the early 8th century.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *大唐三藏義淨奉制譯*.

### Abstract

The *uddāna* genre — verse-summaries that catalogue the topics or *karman*-types covered in the parent prose work — is a standard feature of the Indian Vinaya literature, providing both mnemonic aids for memorisation and finding-aids for reference. The Mūlasarvāstivāda's elaborate *uddāna* systems are well-preserved across Sanskrit, Tibetan, and Chinese, and are an important resource for textual reconstruction. This compendium of summary verses was translated as a separate work by Yìjìng for use as an organizational aid to the parent *Nidāna-mātṛkā*.
