# Zhōng-nán jiā-yè 終南家業
*The Family Tradition of [Mount] Zhōng-nán*
by [[守一]] (Shǒu-yī, 述); [[行枝]] (Xíng-zhī, 編)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Southern-Sòng treatise-and-letter collection by **Sì-míng Tiě-wēng Shǒu-yī** 四明鐵翁守一 ([[守一]], DILA A011485, dates not preserved; resident from **1239** at *Tāi-zhōu Rì-shān* 台州日山), the late-Sòng restorer of the *Nán-shān* Vinaya school. Edited by his disciple **Xíng-zhī** 行枝 ([[行枝]]). The title *Zhōng-nán jiā-yè* — "The Family Inheritance of Mt Zhōng-nán" — designates the doctrinal heritage of *Nán-shān Dào-xuān* 南山道宣 (596–667), whose principal seat was Mt Zhōng-nán in Cháng'ān; Shǒu-yī, in lineage from *Rú-ān Liǎo-hóng* 如庵了宏, presents himself as the heir who must "keep up the family business." The full author signature reads *Sì-míng Tiě-wēng zōng-shī shù / mén-rén Xíng-zhī biān* 四明　鐵翁宗師　述／門人　行枝　編.

### Structural Division

**Upper fascicle:** *Jiào-guān cuō-yào* 教觀撮要 (essentials of doctrine and contemplation), *Sān-guān chén-lù* 三觀塵露 (the three contemplations: dust and dew), ***Dá Rì-běn Réng fǎ-shī jiào-guān zhū wèn*** 答日本芿法師教觀諸問 (replies to the Japanese monk **Réng** [Shunjō 俊芿, 1166–1227] on matters of *jiào-guān* — see [[KR6k0256]] *Lǜ-zōng wèn-dá*).

**Middle fascicle:** *Chóng-shòu jiè-wén* 重受戒文 (rite for re-receiving the precepts), *Jiè-tǐ zhèng-yì zhí-yán* 戒體正義直言, *Sì-zhèng yào-lùn* 四諍要論 (essential discussion of the four contentions), *Yī-zhì gé-yán* 衣制格言 (canonical maxims on the regulation of robes), *Lùn fēn-bù* 論分部, *Lùn sēng-tǐ* 論僧體, *Kē-shì Zá-xīn-lùn chū sān-yǒu duì wén* 科釋雜心論出三有對文, *Shòu-yuán chóng-kāi* 受緣重開.

**Lower fascicle:** *Lùn xīn-yòng shuāng chí-fàn jié zhì-zuì* 論心用雙持犯結制罪, *Biàn èr-zhǐ bìng bā-jiǔ míng-yì* 辨二止並八九名義, *Chóng shì Shì-chāo chí-fàn piān tōng-sāi wén* 重釋事鈔持犯篇通塞文, *Chóng dá Qīn-shī jìng-xiǎng wèn* 重答欽師境想問, *Xī-rán Mèng-ān chí-fàn sì nán* 㭊然夢庵持犯四難, *Zhēng-xiǎn dìng-dào èr-jiè* 徵顯定道二戒, *Lüè biàn zhèng-yòng xiāng-cóng* 略辨正用相從, *Lüè-yì dì-qī fēi-tǐ* 略議第七非體, *Biàn lüè-jiào jié-fàn* 辨略教結犯.

### Prefaces

The *jiào-guān cuō-yào* of fascicle 1 opens: *"Wú zǔ hóng lǜ, yǐ miào-guān wéi běn"* 吾祖弘律。以妙觀為本 — "Our patriarch promoted the *vinaya* by taking *miào-guān* 妙觀 (subtle contemplation) as foundation. This follows from the Vinaya itself, in which the Buddha enjoins the *bhikṣu* to *guān-niàn duì-* (contemplative attention)."

### Abstract

The *Zhōng-nán jiā-yè* is **the principal Southern-Sòng *Nán-shān-lǜ* doctrinal compendium**, written at *Tāi-zhōu Rì-shān* by Shǒu-yī after he settled there in 1239 (Jiā-xī 3). The collection is exegetically tied to *Yuán-zhào* 元照's *Líng-zhī* corpus a century earlier (see [[KR6k0167]] *Sì-fēn lǜ xíng-shì chāo zī-chí jì*) but engages on the technical level of Sòng *guān-xīn* 觀心 doctrine: the *jiè-tǐ* (precept-substance), *chí-fàn* (upholding-and-violation), and *sēng-tǐ* (the *substance* of the saṅgha) treatises in the middle fascicle revisit late-Sòng controversies. The most historically significant piece is in fascicle 1: *Dá Rì-běn Réng fǎ-shī jiào-guān zhū wèn* — Shǒu-yī's replies to the Japanese pilgrim-monk **Shunjō Réng** 俊芿 (1166–1227, founder of *Sennyū-ji* 泉涌寺 in Kyoto) on questions of *jiào* and *guān*. This is one of three Southern-Sòng *Lǜ-zōng-wèn-dá* groupings (the others survive as [[KR6k0256]]). Composition is bracketed by Shǒu-yī's residence at Rì-shān (from 1239) and a defensible upper bound for his floruit; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1239–1260. Companion volume: [[KR6k0220]] *Lǜ-zōng huì-yuán*.

## Translations and research

- For Shunjō Réng and Sòng-Japan Vinaya contacts: see Paul Groner, *Saichō: The Establishment of the Japanese Tendai School* (Honolulu, 2000) Appendix; and the *Sennyū-ji-shi* documentation. The *Dá Rì-běn Réng fǎ-shī jiào-guān zhū wèn* in this volume is a standard source for that history.

## Other points of interest

- Together with [[KR6k0256]] *Lǜ-zōng wèn-dá*, this volume constitutes the principal Southern-Sòng response-corpus to Japanese Vinaya pilgrims.
