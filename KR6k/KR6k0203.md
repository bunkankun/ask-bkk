# Cí-bēi dào-chǎng shuǐ-chàn-fǎ kē-zhù 慈悲道場水懺法科註
*A Sub-Section Commentary on the Compassion-Site Water-Repentance Liturgy*
by [[西宗]] (Xī-zōng, 集註)

## About the work

A three-fascicle late-Qīng *kē-zhù* 科註 (sub-section + interlinear commentary) on the Táng-period *Cí-bēi shuǐ-chàn-fǎ* 慈悲水懺法 (T1910 / [[KR6c0007]]) attributed to *Wù-dá guó-shī* Zhī-xuán 悟達國師知玄. Compiled by **Chéng-jiǎn Xī-zōng** 成簡西宗 ([[西宗]], DILA A000429, ?–1688), dharma-heir of *Lǚ-píng Zhì-xián fǎ-shī* 履平智賢法師 (1608–1673) at *Jīn'é-sì* 金峩寺 in Níng-bō, while resident at the *Lóng-zhù-sì* 龍住寺 in Xī-xiāng 西鄉. The author signature reads *Lóng-zhù shì Chéng-jiǎn Xī-zōng jí-zhù* 龍住釋　成簡西宗　集註.

### Prefaces

**(A)** *Cí-bēi dào-chǎng shuǐ-chàn yuán xù* 慈悲道場水懺原敘 — a recapitulation of the *yuán-qǐ* 緣起 of the parent *Shuǐ chàn*: the encounter between **Wù-dá guó-shī** 悟達國師 (Zhī-xuán 知玄) and the bodhisattva **Jiā-nuò-jiā zūn-zhě** 迦諾迦尊者 (Kanaka) under the Táng emperor Yì-zōng 懿宗, the affliction of the *rén-miàn chuāng* 人面瘡 (the "face-shaped boil") born from the karmic enmity of *Yuán Àng* 袁盎 and *Cháo Cuò* 晁錯 of the Western Hàn, and its washing by the bodhisattva's *sān-mèi shuǐ* 三昧水 (water of *samādhi*) at *Péng-zhōu Jiǔ-lóng-shān* 彭州九龍山 in Western Shǔ.

**(B)** *Yuán-qǐ wén* 緣起文 — Xī-zōng's own opening statement on the rite's purpose: "to turn the resentful into kin and to transform sin's morass into a field of merit."

A later editorial colophon clarifies that the master "followed the old block-edition's *kē* divisions and continued his interlinear notes [until] illness reached its crisis and he laid down the brush" (病篤而止) — the work was therefore **finished only as far as the master's strength held** before his death in early 1688 (Kāng-xī 26 / 27, 12th lunar month).

### Abstract

Xī-zōng's *Kē-zhù* parses the *Shuǐ chàn* sentence by sentence, supplementing the source-text with a *kē* 科 (section-tree) skeleton followed by Chán/Jiào doctrinal annotations. The *zhù* threads canonical citations (the *Jīn-gāng jīng*, *Niè-pán jīng*, the seven-Buddha *fù-fǎ jié* 付法偈, etc.) into a comprehensive devotional commentary. Composition is precisely bracketed: Xī-zōng was tonsured in middle age after a family disaster, kept a long period of canon-reading retreat under his master Lǚ-píng (d. 1673), then taught at Pǔ-qiū 普邱, Dìng-qiáo 定橋 and Jiāng-xīn 江心 monasteries; the *Kē-zhù* must have been completed in the period between his teacher's death in 1673 and his own in early 1688 — `notBefore` and `notAfter` are accordingly set 1670–1688. The work is preserved by the *Jīn'é-sì zhì* 金峩寺志, juan 5.
