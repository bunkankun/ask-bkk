# Cíbēi dàochǎng shuǐchànfǎ suíwén lù 慈悲道場水懺法隨聞錄
*A Listener's Record of the Compassion-Site Water-Repentance Liturgy*
by [[智證]] (Zhìzhèng, 錄)

## About the work

A three-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng commentary (*suíwén lù* 隨聞錄, "record of what was heard along with the text") on the famous Táng-period *Shuǐ chàn* 水懺 attributed to **Wùdá guóshī** 悟達國師 (i.e. *Cíbēi shuǐchànfǎ* 慈悲水懺法, T1910 / [[KR6c0007]]). Composed by **Zhòuzhōng Zhìzhèng** 晝中智證 ([[智證]]), a disciple of *Gāofēng Bàopú Dàlián chánshī* 高峰抱璞大蓮禪師 (?–1629), while resident at the *Qīngluóān* 青螺菴 in Gǔlóu 古婁 (Tàicāng 太倉). The commentary parses the *Shuǐ chàn*'s opening titulus and its sin-categories one phrase at a time, weaving in scriptural citations from both *zàng* 藏 collections, classical historical exempla (the袁盎/晁錯 backstory of Wùdá's *rénmiàn chuāng* 人面瘡 affair), and Tiāntái-school doctrinal annotations.

### Prefaces

The text opens with four prefatory pieces:

**(A)** *Xù* 序, signed *Guāngxù èrshíyī nián suìcì yǐwèi gōng sìyuè Fóyù rì Hángzhōu Gǔlíngjiù Yìwúsì zhùchí shāmén chuán Tiāntái jiàoguān yìsūn Ruìbīn jǐn xù* — i.e. **1895** (Guāngxù 21, Buddhabath day = 8th day of the 4th lunar month). Ruìbīn 瑞斌, abbot of Yìwúsì 義吾寺 at Gǔlíngjiù 古靈鷲 in Hángzhōu and a Tiāntái lineage-grandson, recounts how the woodblocks had perished in the *Tàipíng* military fires (兵燹板燬無存) and only fragments survived, until "Qīngyuán Shěn jūshì" 清源沈居士 produced a complete copy. He paid for fresh blocks.

**(B)** *Suíwén lù xù* 隨聞錄序, signed *Gǔtiáo Gāofēng Jìngmíngān Wǔrì zhèngdàorén Pántán shí* 古苕高峰淨名菴午日證道人槃譚識 — Pántán of the Jìngmíngān at Gāofēng on Mount Gǔtiáo (Wūchéng 烏程), a fellow practitioner who first sought out the woodcutters.

**(C)** *Chóngkè Shuǐchàn suíwén lù xù* 重刻水懺隨聞錄序, signed *Tiāntái jiàoguān hòuxué Hǎimén Yànjìng* 天台教觀後學海門彥淨, with a check-reading note (校對) by *Wǔlín hòuxué Chǎnzōng* 武林後學闡宗.

**(D)** *Zhòuzhōng chánshī shì Shuǐchàn xù* 晝中禪師釋水懺敘, the author's own opening *commentarial preface* paraphrasing the dharmaposition of the *Shuǐ chàn* and signed *yù Gǔlóu Qīngluóān shāmén Zhòuzhōng Zhìzhèng lù* 寓古婁青螺菴沙門　晝中智證　錄.

### Abstract

The *Suíwén lù* is a discursive Tiāntái-school *zhùshū* 註疏 on the perennially-popular *Shuǐ chàn*. Its principal contribution is its synthesis: Zhìzhèng "first collated the canonical prooftexts of the two *piṭakas*, then digested the explanations of the worthies, and selected what was reliable" (先覈兩藏之原文。次簡諸賢之疏釋。摭其確當). The work survived only patchily after the late-Qīng wars and was reissued in 1895 from a recovered manuscript by Ruìbīn at Yìwúsì in Hángzhōu — the woodblock edition reproduced in the Xùzàngjīng. The compiler Zhìzhèng's life-dates are not preserved ; since his teacher died in 1629, his floruit must lie in the second quarter of the 17th century, with the commentary very likely composed in the mid-Qīng — `notBefore` and `notAfter` are bracketed loosely (1620–1680) accordingly. For the parent text see [[KR6c0007]] (T1910 *Cíbēi shuǐchànfǎ*).
