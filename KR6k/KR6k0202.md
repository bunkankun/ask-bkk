# Cí-bēi dào-chǎng shuǐ-chàn-fǎ suí-wén lù 慈悲道場水懺法隨聞錄
*A Listener's Record of the Compassion-Site Water-Repentance Liturgy*
by [[智證]] (Zhì-zhèng, 錄)

## About the work

A three-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng commentary (*suí-wén lù* 隨聞錄, "record of what was heard along with the text") on the famous Táng-period *Shuǐ chàn* 水懺 attributed to **Wù-dá guó-shī** 悟達國師 (i.e. *Cí-bēi shuǐ-chàn-fǎ* 慈悲水懺法, T1910 / KR6c0007). Composed by **Zhòu-zhōng Zhì-zhèng** 晝中智證 ([[智證]], DILA A001300), a disciple of *Gāo-fēng Bào-pú Dà-lián chán-shī* 高峰抱璞大蓮禪師 (?–1629), while resident at the *Qīng-luó-ān* 青螺菴 in Gǔ-lóu 古婁 (Tài-cāng 太倉). The commentary parses the *Shuǐ chàn*'s opening titulus and its sin-categories one phrase at a time, weaving in scriptural citations from both *zàng* 藏 collections, classical historical exempla (the袁盎/晁錯 backstory of Wù-dá's *rén-miàn chuāng* 人面瘡 affair), and Tiāntái-school doctrinal annotations.

### Prefaces

The text opens with four prefatory pieces:

**(A)** *Xù* 序, signed *Guāng-xù èr-shí-yī nián suì-cì yǐ-wèi gōng sì-yuè Fó-yù rì Háng-zhōu Gǔ-líng-jiù Yì-wú-sì zhù-chí shā-mén chuán Tiāntái jiào-guān yì-sūn Ruì-bīn jǐn xù* — i.e. **1895** (Guāng-xù 21, Buddha-bath day = 8th day of the 4th lunar month). Ruì-bīn 瑞斌, abbot of Yì-wú-sì 義吾寺 at Gǔ-líng-jiù 古靈鷲 in Hángzhōu and a Tiāntái lineage-grandson, recounts how the woodblocks had perished in the *Tài-píng* military fires (兵燹板燬無存) and only fragments survived, until "Qīng-yuán Shěn jū-shì" 清源沈居士 produced a complete copy. He paid for fresh blocks.

**(B)** *Suí-wén lù xù* 隨聞錄序, signed *Gǔ-tiáo Gāo-fēng Jìng-míng-ān Wǔ-rì zhèng-dào-rén Pán-tán shí* 古苕高峰淨名菴午日證道人槃譚識 — Pán-tán of the Jìng-míng-ān at Gāo-fēng on Mount Gǔ-tiáo (Wū-chéng 烏程), a fellow practitioner who first sought out the woodcutters.

**(C)** *Chóng-kè Shuǐ-chàn suí-wén lù xù* 重刻水懺隨聞錄序, signed *Tiāntái jiào-guān hòu-xué Hǎi-mén Yàn-jìng* 天台教觀後學海門彥淨, with a check-reading note (校對) by *Wǔ-lín hòu-xué Chǎn-zōng* 武林後學闡宗.

**(D)** *Zhòu-zhōng chán-shī shì Shuǐ-chàn xù* 晝中禪師釋水懺敘, the author's own opening *commentarial preface* paraphrasing the dharma-position of the *Shuǐ chàn* and signed *yù Gǔ-lóu Qīng-luó-ān shā-mén Zhòu-zhōng Zhì-zhèng lù* 寓古婁青螺菴沙門　晝中智證　錄.

### Abstract

The *Suí-wén lù* is a discursive Tiāntái-school *zhù-shū* 註疏 on the perennially-popular *Shuǐ chàn*. Its principal contribution is its synthesis: Zhì-zhèng "first collated the canonical proof-texts of the two *piṭakas*, then digested the explanations of the worthies, and selected what was reliable" (先覈兩藏之原文。次簡諸賢之疏釋。摭其確當). The work survived only patchily after the late-Qīng wars and was reissued in 1895 from a recovered manuscript by Ruì-bīn at Yì-wú-sì in Hángzhōu — the woodblock edition reproduced in the Xùzàng-jīng. The compiler Zhì-zhèng's lifedates are not preserved (DILA A001300); since his teacher died in 1629, his floruit must lie in the second quarter of the 17th century, with the commentary very likely composed in the mid-Qīng — `notBefore` and `notAfter` are bracketed loosely (1620–1680) accordingly. For the parent text see [[KR6c0007]] (T1910 *Cí-bēi shuǐ-chàn-fǎ*).
