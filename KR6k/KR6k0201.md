# Shèlì chànfǎ 舍利懺法
*A Repentance Liturgy for the Buddha-Relics*
by [[繼僧]] (Jìsēng, 著)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Qīng-period repentance liturgy (*chàn-fǎ* 懺法) compiled by **Shēn-qī Jì-sēng** 深栖繼僧 (also known as 聲碧 Shēng-bì), a Tiāntái-mountain ascetic, at the *Fú-guó jīng-shè* 福國精舍 in Hán-jiāng 邗江 (Yáng-zhōu region) in the spring of 1718. The liturgy takes the *śarīra* (*shè-lì* 舍利, Buddha-relics) as the cardinal object of veneration and *dhāraṇī* recitation as its instrumental support: practitioners chant the *Dà bǎo guǎng-bó suí-xīn tuó-luó-ní* 大寶廣博隨心陀羅尼 across forty-nine days of altar service to "extinguish all sin and fulfil all wishes." Edited (校) by **Dìng-shān bǐ-qiū Yán-zhāng** 定山比丘嚴章.

### Prefaces

The work opens with three prefaces. **(A)** *Shè-lì líng-yìng xù* 舍利靈應序 ("Preface on the Numinous Response of the Relics"), signed *Dà-Qīng Kāng-xī wù-xū zhōng-qiū qián sān-rì Láo-shān Lǐ Zhào* 大清康熙歲次戊戌中秋前三日勞山李照拜手書 — i.e. three days before mid-autumn, **1718**. Lǐ Zhào recounts how, while visiting Fú-guó jīng-shè to consult *Jì-shēng chán-shī* 濟生禪師, he met Shēng-bì *hé-shàng* 聲碧和尚, who described how a *śarīra* had appeared at the tip of his brush while he was copying the *Huá-yán jīng* 華嚴經 in his own blood on Mount Tiāntái, and how a second auspicious cloud had appeared three days later. **(B)** *Shè-lì chàn yuán-qǐ xù* 舍利懺緣起序 ("Preface on the Origin of the Relic Repentance"), signed *Hán-jiāng Fú-guó Yuán-dù* 邗江福國元度. Yuán-dù (a fellow disciple of Jì-sēng's late teacher) describes Shēng-bì's blood-copying of the *Huá-yán*, *Fǎ-huá* 法華 and *Lèng-yán* 楞嚴, the manifestation of the relics at the brush-tip while writing the *Shí-xìn* 十信 chapter, and the establishment of the present *chàn-fǎ* by Shēng-bì at Fú-guó. **(C)** *Zì xù* 自序 ("Author's Preface"), signed *Gǔ-Wú Shēn-qī Jì-sēng zì-shù* 古吳深栖繼僧自述. The author dates his liturgical fast to a forty-nine-day altar held in 1718 (戊戌歲), at the conclusion of which (5th lunar month, full-moon day) the relics descended in answer to the assembly's prostrations.

### Abstract

The *Shè-lì chàn-fǎ* is a relic-cult repentance manual produced in response to a specific late-Kāng-xī devotional event at Fú-guó jīng-shè in Yáng-zhōu in 1718. The compiler **Jì-sēng** (DILA A001933, dates not precisely known; flourished early Qīng) had spent the years 1705–1718 secluded on Tiāntái-shān copying Mahāyāna sūtras in his own blood; the manifestation of relics in his brushwork during the writing of the *Huá-yán*'s *Shí-xìn* chapter became the occasion for him to compose a structured *chàn-fǎ* organising the relic-cult around the *suí-xīn dhāraṇī*. The work divides the rite into ten *kē* 科 (procedural sections). Composition is precisely datable to 1718 (Kāng-xī 57) on the basis of the three contemporary prefaces; both `notBefore` and `notAfter` are set to that year. The DILA Buddhist Person Authority (A001933) notes Jì-sēng's life-dates are not preserved beyond his own statements in this work's prefaces.
