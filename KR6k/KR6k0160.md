# Píní zuòchí xùshì 毗尼作持續釋
*A Continued Commentary on the Vinaya Rules of Doing*
by [[道宣]] (Dàoxuān, 撰集), [[讀體]] (Dútǐ, 續釋)

## About the work

A fifteen-fascicle early-Qīng commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]], 596–667) *Sìfēnlǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó* 四分律刪補隨機羯磨 ([[KR6k0019]]) by **Jiànyuè Dútǐ** 見月讀體 ([[讀體]], 1601–1679), the principal early-Qīng *Bǎohuáshān* 寶華山 Vinaya-school revivalist. *Zuòchí* 作持 ("rules of doing") indicates the *karman* / regulatory side of *vinaya* (paired with *zhǐchí* 止持, the rules of non-doing); *xùshì* 續釋 ("continued commentary") signals Dútǐ's editorial intent to revive the long-dormant Sòng *zuòchí* commentary tradition.

### Prefaces

Dú-tǐ's autograph *xù*, dated *Kāngxī yǐ-sì* (= **Kāngxī 4 = 1665**) "*qián ān-jū rì*" (the day before the summer retreat), composed at *Jīn-líng Bǎo-huá-shān Guān-xī xuān* 金陵寶華山觀西軒. The preface narrates the genesis of the work in extended detail. Dú-tǐ records that he received ordination at *Rùn-zhōu* 潤州 in *yǐ-hài* 乙亥 (= 1635) and served his *xiān lǎo-rén* (= **Sān-mèi Jì-guāng** 三昧寂光, 1580–1645) in supporting Vinaya transmission throughout the empire. He repeatedly raised the question of *zuò-chí* practice with Sān-mèi, who replied that the *Sìfēn-lǜ* 曇無德部刪補隨機羯磨 by *Nán-shān lǜ-zǔ* 南山律祖 (Dàoxuān) was the canonical authority but had been "submerged for ages and difficult to revive in force": Sān-mèi charged Dú-tǐ to "expound it in future generations for the saṃgha." After Sān-mèi's seclusion in *yǐ-yǒu* 乙酉 (1645), Dú-tǐ retreated to the mountain and produced the present *Xù-shì* over twenty years, "burning lampwick to follow the sun, exhausting the *Vinaya*-piṭaka's hidden subtleties, leading the saṃgha in personal practice, expounding the great norms of the *jié-mó*." The preface signature gives him as *bǐ-ní hòu-xué Diān-nán Dú-tǐ* 毘尼後學滇南讀體 ("the Vinaya-junior Dú-tǐ of Diān-nán" — Yún-nán in the south-west).

### Abstract

The *Xùshì* is the principal **early-Qīng Bǎohuáshān** *zuòchí* commentary and the practical complement to Dìngān Déjī's later *Píní guānyào* ([[KR6k0152]]) on the *zhǐchí* side. With its preface dated 1665 it is the earliest of the great Bǎohuáshān Vinaya works of the Kāngxī era and reflects Dútǐ's twenty-year recovery of Dàoxuān's karman tradition under conditions where (as Dútǐ explicitly notes in the preface) the *zuòchí* portion of the Vinaya had received "no expounder for several hundred years." The work supplies *guǎngqí shìfǎ shìqí yǐnwēi* 廣其事法釋其隱微 ("amplifying the affairs and rules, glossing the subtle and hidden") on each *jiémó* formula of the canonical *Suíjī jiémó*. The text is an indispensable witness to early-Qīng Vinaya pedagogy at its most institutionally consequential moment — the founding generation of the Bǎohuáshān Vinaya revival.
