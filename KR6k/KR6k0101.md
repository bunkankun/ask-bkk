# Fànwǎng-jīng zhí-jiě shì-yì 梵網經直解事義
*Auxiliary Notes on the Direct Exposition of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
(author/editor unknown — companion to KR6k0100)

## About the work

A one-fascicle auxiliary notes-collection accompanying [[寂光]]'s *Fànwǎng-jīng zhí-jiě* ([[KR6k0100]]). 事義 *shì-yì* "factual meaning" indicates the genre — supplementary lexical and historical clarifications.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The text supplies the auxiliary apparatus for Jì-guāng's principal *Zhí-jiě* commentary, providing definitions, identifications, and brief explanations for the technical terms and historical references in the parent text. Together with KR6k0100 it forms the complete Bǎohuá-shān commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng*.
