# Fànwǎngjīng zhíjiě shìyì 梵網經直解事義
*Auxiliary Notes on the Direct Exposition of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
(author/editor unknown — companion to [[KR6k0100]])

## About the work

A one-fascicle auxiliary notes-collection accompanying [[寂光]]'s *Fànwǎngjīng zhíjiě* ([[KR6k0100]]). 事義 *shìyì* "factual meaning" indicates the genre — supplementary lexical and historical clarifications.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The text supplies the auxiliary apparatus for Jìguāng's principal *Zhíjiě* commentary, providing definitions, identifications, and brief explanations for the technical terms and historical references in the parent text. Together with [[KR6k0100]] it forms the complete Bǎohuáshān commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng*.
