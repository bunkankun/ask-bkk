# Sìfēn lǜ hán-zhù jièběn shū xíng-zōng jì 四分律含注戒本疏行宗記
*Records on the Practice-Lineage Commentary on the Embedded-Annotation Prātimokṣa*
by [[道宣]] (Dàoxuān, 撰) and [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 述)

## About the work

A four-fascicle composite work combining Dàoxuān's parent commentary on the *Hán-zhù jièběn* ([[KR6k0131]]) with **Yuán-zhào's** 元照 ([[元照]]) sub-commentary. The Japanese monk **Jì-jìng** 即靜 (即靜) added analytical divisions (分會 *fēn-huì*) for instructional use.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐道宣撰，宋元照述，日本即靜分會*.

### Abstract

The composite Tang-Sòng-Japanese transmission of this text reflects the international scope of late-medieval East Asian Vinaya scholarship: the Tang commentary tradition (Dàoxuān) was extended in the Sòng (Yuán-zhào) and further refined for Japanese instructional use. The text became a standard Japanese Risshū reference.
