# Yōupósè wǔ-jiè wēi-yí jīng 優婆塞五戒威儀經
*The Sūtra on the Five Precepts and Decorums for Lay Followers*
by [[求那跋摩]] (Guṇavarman, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle *sūtra* on the five lay precepts and accompanying behavioural norms (*wēi-yí*), translated by **Guṇavarman** ([[求那跋摩]]) at Jiànkāng in 431 CE.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋罽賓沙門求那跋摩譯*.

### Abstract

Companion text to [[KR6k0065]] (*Yōupósè wǔ-jiè-xiāng jīng*), this work supplies the practical-behavioural elaboration of the five lay precepts. Guṇavarman's full lay-precept apparatus comprises both the *wǔ-jiè-xiāng* (the doctrinal-categorical text) and the present *wēi-yí* (the behavioural-procedural text), giving the early Chinese lay Buddhist communities the complete practical handbook for the *upāsaka pañca-śīla*.
