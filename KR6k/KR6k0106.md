# Běn-yè yīngluò-jīng shū 本業瓔珞經疏
*Commentary on the Yīngluò Běn-yè Sūtra* (Dunhuang fragment)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle commentary fragment on the *Púsà yīngluò běn-yè jīng* ([[KR6k0105]]), preserved at Dunhuang and included in volume 85 of the Taishō (the *Gǔ-yì-bù* 古逸部 "old/lost works").

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The fragment is one of the Dunhuang Buddhist commentary fragments in Taishō volume 85, attesting to active engagement with the *Yīngluò běn-yè jīng* in northwestern Tang Buddhist communities. Like the parallel *Fànwǎng-jīng shù-jì* fragment ([[KR6k0104]]), the fragmentary state limits utility but valuable as a witness to lost commentary traditions.
