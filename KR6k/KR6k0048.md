# Ní jiémó 尼羯磨
*Karmavācanā for Nuns* (Dōngtǎ school)
by [[懷素]] (Huáisù, 集)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Dharmaguptaka *karmavācanā* compendium for the **nuns' saṅgha**, compiled by **Huáisù** ([[懷素]]) of the *Dōng-tǎ* school of *Sìfēn lǜ* Vinaya. Companion text to the *Sēng jiémó* ([[KR6k0047]]) and parallel in approach. Composed at Cháng-ān in the second half of the 7th century.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐西太原寺沙門懷素集*.

### Abstract

The *Ní jiémó* presents the *karmavācanā* formulae for the *bhikṣuṇī*-saṅgha — *upasaṃpadā* (with the dual-saṅgha procedure), *uposatha*, *pravāraṇā*, the *aṣṭa-gurudharma* recitation, and the various *bhikṣuṇī*-specific procedures. The text supplied the practical handbook for the conduct of *bhikṣuṇī* legal acts in the Dōng-tǎ tradition. Like the parallel *Sēng jiémó*, it was eclipsed by Dàoxuān's lineage in the late Tang but retained authority in the Sòng under Yuán-zhào's recovery.
