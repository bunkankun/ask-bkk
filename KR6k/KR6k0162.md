# Sìfēn-lǜ sōu-xuán lù 四分律搜玄錄
*Records Searching the Mysteries of the Sìfēn-lǜ*
by [[志鴻]] (Zhì-hóng, 撰述)

## About the work

A surviving three-fascicle fragment (originally twenty *juan*) of a mid-Táng *jì* 記 sub-commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēn-lǜ shān-fán bǔ-quē xíng-shì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]) by the Sūzhōu Vinaya master **Zhì-hóng** 志鴻 ([[志鴻]]) of Shuāng-lín-sì 雙林寺. The work programmatically collects and digests the *jì* and *chāo* of more than forty earlier exegetes — including *Dà-cí* 大慈, *Líng-è* 靈崿, and others of the post-Dàoxuān generation — and is invaluable for the reconstruction of pre-Sòng *Nán-shān* Vinaya scholarship.

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a preface by **Chéng-guān** 澄觀 (738–839), the Fourth Patriarch of *Huā-yán*, here signed *京師大興唐寺沙門釋　澄觀* (*Chéng-guān, monk of Dà-xìng-táng-sì in the imperial capital*). The preface dates the work to the *Dà-lì* 大曆 era (766–779) by reference to "five hundred years after Buddha-day" reckoning and identifies Zhì-hóng as the *fǔ-sì* 輔嗣 ("auxiliary successor") of Dàoxuān's school.

### Abstract

The *Sōu-xuán lù* is the principal mid-Táng *Nán-shān* sub-commentary surviving in fragmentary form and is the chief witness to the diversity of post-Dàoxuān Vinaya exegesis between the second-half of the seventh century and the late eighth. Chéng-guān's preface confirms not only the date but the breadth of Zhì-hóng's reading; the preserved fragments are a critical resource for identifying lost Tang *jì* writers. Zhì-hóng died at the age of one hundred and eight (per the DILA biographical authority); his composition window must precede that and is fixed here by Chéng-guān's *Dà-lì* preface.
