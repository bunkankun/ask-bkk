# Nán-shān zǔ-shī lǐ-zàn wén 南山祖師禮讚文
*A Hymnody of Veneration for the Patriarch-Master of the Nán-shān (Vinaya) School*
by [[仁岳]] (Rén-yuè, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Northern-Sòng *lǐ-zàn wén* 禮讚文 for the death-anniversary of **Nán-shān Dào-xuān** 南山道宣 (596–667), composed by **Jìng-jué Rén-yuè** 淨覺仁岳 ([[仁岳]], 992–ca. 1064), the *Zhá-chuān shā-mén* 霅川沙門 — i.e. monk of *Zhá-xī* 霅谿 (Wú-xīng 吳興). Author signature: *Zhá-chuān shā-mén shì Rén-yuè shù* 霅川沙門釋　仁岳　述. The work is the **second** of the three Sòng *Nán-shān lǐ-zàn wén* preserved in the Xùzàng-jīng (X1504, [[KR6k0207]] = Zhì-yuán; X1505, this work = Rén-yuè; X1506, [[KR6k0209]] = Yǔn-kǎn).

### Prefaces

Yuán-zhào's later collected preface (preserved as the head of [[KR6k0207]]) explains Rén-yuè's compositional motive: Zhì-yuán's earlier *zàn* "lacked metrical-musical fitness" (*shì-yí shàng quē yīn-yùn* 事儀尚闕音韻), so Rén-yuè wrote his own to supply the lyrical idiom needed for chanted altar use. Rén-yuè's own opening prose preface (here in the body of the text) declares: *Wǔ-chéng qǐ-yùn wéi shēng-wén zuì xiān; sān-zàng liú-huī gù pí-ní wéi běn* 五乘啟運唯聲聞最先。三藏流輝故毗尼為本 — "Of the five vehicles in unfolding, the *śrāvaka* is foremost; of the three baskets in radiant transmission, *vinaya* is foundational" — and identifies Dào-xuān explicitly as the *Xī-míng lǜ-shī* 西明律師 (i.e. master of the Xī-míng-sì in Cháng'ān).

### Abstract

Rén-yuè's *Nán-shān zǔ-shī lǐ-zàn wén* is structurally parallel to Zhì-yuán's: an *yī-xīn fèng-qǐng* 一心奉請 invocation, prostrations, eulogy verses keyed to Dào-xuān's biographical episodes (the descent of relics into a precious casket at *Rì-yán jīng-shè* 日嚴精舍, the transmission of the four-part *vinaya*, etc.), with closing offerings. Where Zhì-yuán wrote the prototype, Rén-yuè's contribution is principally **liturgical**: he set the eulogy in chantable verse-and-prose alternation, and supplied the metrical *zàn* stanzas that became standard in Sòng-onward Vinaya-school anniversary services. Rén-yuè's life is bracketed 992–ca. 1064 (DILA A000165); the *zàn-wén* must postdate his break with *Sì-míng Zhīlǐ* 四明知禮 (after ca. 1020) and predate his death — `notBefore` and `notAfter` are accordingly set 1020–1064. Rén-yuè was best known for his *Shí bù-èr mén wén-xīn jiě* 十不二門文心解 ([[KR6d0162]], X928), the cardinal *shān-wài* tract of the Sòng Tiāntái doctrinal controversy.
