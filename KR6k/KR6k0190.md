# Shuō-zuì yào-xíng fǎ 說罪要行法
*The Essential Method of Confessing Offences*
by [[義淨]] (Yì-jìng, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Vinaya manual on the canonical practice of *shuō-zuì* 說罪 (confession of offences) at the monthly *poṣadha* by **Yì-jìng** 義淨 ([[義淨]], 635–713). Author signature: *唐三藏法師義淨撰*.

### Prefaces

The work opens directly: *每於半月月盡憶所犯罪。準法而說* ("every fortnight at the end of the month, recalling the offences committed, declare them according to the rule"). The opening enumeration lists the typical offences: *gù-wàng-yǔ* 故妄語 (intentional false speech), *yǐn-jiǔ* 飲酒 (consuming alcohol), *fēi-shí shí* 非時食 (eating outside permitted times), and others.

### Abstract

The *Shuō-zuì yào-xíng fǎ* completes the trio of Yì-jìng's short Vinaya manuals ([[KR6k0188]], [[KR6k0189]], [[KR6k0190]]) and is the most operational of the three: it presents the actual ritual formulary for the *poṣadha* confession. The CANWWW alternative-title *Bié shuō-zuì yào-xíng fǎ* 別說罪要行法 ("the *Distinct* Essential Method of Confessing Offences") is preserved in the canonical record (T45N1903 *alt* title) and indicates the manual's distinct status as a complement to the standard *poṣadha* karman recitation. Composition belongs to Yì-jìng's post-695 Luò-yáng / Cháng-ān decades.
