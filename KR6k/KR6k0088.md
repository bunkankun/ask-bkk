# Fànwǎng púsà-jiè-jīng yìshū fā-yǐn wèn-biàn 梵網菩薩戒經義疏發隱問辯
*Polemical Q&A on the Fā-yǐn Sub-Commentary*
by [[袾宏]] (Zhūhóng, 述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle polemical question-and-answer companion to Zhūhóng's *Fā-yǐn* sub-commentary ([[KR6k0086]]). 問辯 *wèn-biàn* "questions and disputations" indicates the genre — a defence of the principal commentary against potential objections.

### Prefaces

Author: 明雲棲袾宏.

### Abstract

The *wèn-biàn* preserves Zhūhóng's responses to questions and objections about his interpretation of the *Fànwǎng* precepts. Such polemical-defensive companion texts are characteristic of the late-Míng intellectual culture, in which doctrinal positions were frequently contested through formal Q&A exchanges. The disputed points include the relationship of the bodhisattva-precepts to the *prātimokṣa*, the requirements for the conferral of bodhisattva-precepts on lay practitioners, and the doctrinal status of the *Fànwǎng jīng* given its disputed authenticity.
