# Lǜ jiè-běn shū 律戒本疏
*Commentary on the Vinaya Prātimokṣa* (Dunhuang fragment)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Vinaya *prātimokṣa* commentary fragment, preserved in Dunhuang manuscript form (T85n2788).

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

One of the Dunhuang Vinaya commentary fragments in Taishō volume 85, attesting to the active Sìfēn scholarship at Dunhuang. The text comments on the *prātimokṣa* without specifying which Vinaya school's recension it follows.
