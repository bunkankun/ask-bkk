# Sìfēn-lǜ shān-bǔ suí-jī jié-mó shū kē 四分律刪補隨機羯磨疏科
*Structural Outline of the Commentary on the Karman-text*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 錄)

## About the work

A four-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) of **Dào-xuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēn-lǜ shān-bǔ suí-jī jié-mó shū* 四分律刪補隨機羯磨疏 ([[KR6k0019]]) by **Yuán-zhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116), the Northern Sòng *Líng-zhī-sì* 靈芝寺 Vinaya-school revivalist. Author signature: *餘杭郡沙門釋　元照　錄* (*Recorded by Yuán-zhào, monk of Yú-háng commandery*). The *kē* serves as the structural index for Yuán-zhào's own *Jì-yuán jì* 濟緣記 ([[KR6k0158]]) on the same parent text.

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the *kē* opens directly with the three-tier structural divisions — *疏序*, *題號*, *序文* — and proceeds through every nested subdivision of Dàoxuān's eight-chapter karman manual.

### Abstract

The *Kē* is the structural counterpart to Yuán-zhào's substantive sub-commentary [[KR6k0158]] *Sìfēn-lǜ shān-bǔ suí-jī jié-mó shū jì-yuán jì* and is studied alongside it in the Sòng *Nán-shān-lǜ* curriculum. Yuán-zhào's *kē* is more granular than Yǔn Kān's earlier *Zhèng-yuán jì* ([[KR6k0156]]), reflecting the late-eleventh-century maturation of the *Lǜ-zōng* sub-commentary tradition. Composition belongs to Yuán-zhào's mature Vinaya output (post-1075).
