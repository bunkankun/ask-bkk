# Sìfēnlǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó shū kē 四分律刪補隨機羯磨疏科
*Structural Outline of the Commentary on the Karman-text*
by [[元照]] (Yuánzhào, 錄)

## About the work

A four-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) of **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēnlǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó shū* 四分律刪補隨機羯磨疏 ([[KR6k0019]]) by **Yuánzhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116), the Northern Sòng *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 Vinaya-school revivalist. Author signature: *餘杭郡沙門釋　元照　錄* (*Recorded by Yuánzhào, monk of Yúháng commandery*). The *kē* serves as the structural index for Yuánzhào's own *Jìyuán jì* 濟緣記 ([[KR6k0158]]) on the same parent text.

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the *kē* opens directly with the three-tier structural divisions — *疏序*, *題號*, *序文* — and proceeds through every nested subdivision of Dàoxuān's eight-chapter karman manual.

### Abstract

The *Kē* is the structural counterpart to Yuánzhào's substantive sub-commentary [[KR6k0158]] *Sìfēnlǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó shū jìyuán jì* and is studied alongside it in the Sòng *Nánshānlǜ* curriculum. Yuánzhào's *kē* is more granular than Yǔn Kān's earlier *Zhèngyuán jì* ([[KR6k0156]]), reflecting the late-eleventh-century maturation of the *Lǜzōng* sub-commentary tradition. Composition belongs to Yuánzhào's mature Vinaya output (post-1075).
