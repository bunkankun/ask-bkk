# Sìfēnlǜzàng dàxiǎo chíjiè jiēdù lüèshì 四分律藏大小持戒犍度略釋
*Brief Commentary on the Skandhaka of Greater and Lesser Precept-keeping in the Sìfēn-lǜ-piṭaka*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 釋)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the *Chíjiè jiēdù* 持戒犍度 ("the skandhaka on keeping the precepts") of the *Sìfēnlǜ* 四分律 ([[KR6k0001]]) by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 ([[智旭]], 1599–1655). The author signs himself as *菩薩沙彌古吳智旭際明　釋* (*Commented by Bodhisattva-novice Zhìxù Jìmíng of GǔWú*) — using the bodhisattva-precept "novice" status that he adopted in the late Míng. The work is the eighteenth and final fascicle of his original *Píní jíyào* 毗尼集要 (subsequently re-edited as the seventeen-juan [[KR6k0151]] *Chóngzhì píní shìyì jíyào*); the post-colophon to [[KR6k0151]] explicitly notes that the original eighteenth juan, the *Jiēdù lüèshì*, was edited separately and circulates in the present recension as a stand-alone work.

### Prefaces

No separate front-matter; the work opens directly with the title-line and Zhìxù's exposition.

### Abstract

The work treats the *Chí-jiè jiē-dù* as the canonical site at which the *prātimokṣa* is grounded in *jiè-běn* 戒本 (the precepts as text), distinguishing under two heads: (1) *dāng-fēn míng-yì* 當分明義 ("clarification within division"), where *dà* 大 = *bǐ-qiū* 比丘 (fully ordained) and *xiǎo* 小 = *shā-mí* 沙彌 (novice), and (2) *kuà-jié míng-yì* 跨節明義 ("clarification across nodes"). Zhìxù's analysis re-classifies the precept-system into three orders: *bō-luó-tí-mù-chā jiè* 波羅提木叉戒 (the *prātimokṣa* proper), *chán-jiè* 禪戒 (precepts as the basis of meditative absorption — entering the four *dhyāna* through the abandonment of the five *nivāraṇa*), and *wú-lòu jiè* 無漏戒 (precepts as the basis of the five higher cognitions and the three liberating insights). This three-fold scheme reflects the late-Míng integrative hermeneutic. Composition belongs to Zhìxù's mature Vinaya period and is dated to his original *Jí-yào* compilation (post-1632, before his death in 1655).
