# Lǜ-zōng huì-yuán 律宗會元
*Convergence at the Source of the Vinaya School*
by [[守一]] (Shǒu-yī, 集)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Southern-Sòng anthology by **Tiě-wēng Shǒu-yī** ([[守一]], DILA A011485) at *Tāi-zhōu Rì-shān*, organising the *Nán-shān-lǜ* tradition under ten doctrinal gates (*shí-mén* 十門) with sub-section gloss. Companion to [[KR6k0219]] *Zhōng-nán jiā-yè*. The full title given in the source-text is *Lǜ-zōng huì-yuán (bìng shí-mén zǒng-mù suí-mén lüè-shì) juǎn dì-yī (zhǐ Yuán-jiào zhōng)* 律宗會元(并十門總目隨門略釋)卷第一(止原教終). Author signature: *Tāi-zhōu Rì-shān sì zǔ bǐ-qiū Shǒu-yī jǐn jí* 台州日山嗣祖比丘　守一　謹集.

### Structural Division

The ten gates are signalled in the *shí-mén zǒng-mù* embedded in the work:

1. *Yuán-jiào mén* 原教門 (origin of the [vinaya] teaching);
2. *Guān-fǎ mén* 觀法門 (contemplation-method gate);
3. (continuing) *Shòu-jiè mén* 受戒門, *Chí-fàn mén* 持犯門, *Yī-shí mén* 衣食門, *Sēng-jiā mén* 僧伽門, *Yú-fǎ mén* 餘法門, *Zhèng-yán mén* 證驗門, *Yǐn-shēng mén* 引申門 — and a closing *miscellany* gate. (Each gate carries Shǒu-yī's *suí-mén lüè-shì* 隨門略釋, "section-by-section brief gloss".)

### Abstract

The *Lǜ-zōng huì-yuán* organises the textual heritage of the Nán-shān Vinaya school as a topical anthology, drawing canonical and sub-canonical citations into a *huì-yuán* 會元 ("gathering-at-the-source") framework. The opening *Yuán-jiào mén* makes the work's principal doctrinal claim: while the *vinaya-piṭaka* nominally belongs to the *Hīnayāna* division of the *tripiṭaka*, *"the *Lotus Sūtra* opens-and-converges (*kāi huì* 開會) and the *Niè-pán Sūtra* sustains-and-discusses (*fú-tán* 扶談), so that the partial is identical to the round (*zhǐ piān jí yuán* 指偏即圓)" — i.e. the Nán-shān reading places Vinaya within the Mahāyāna *one-vehicle* (*yī Fó chéng* 一佛乘) doctrinally as well as ritually. This is the standard *Líng-zhī* + Tiāntái fusion. The second gate *Guān-fǎ mén* makes the corresponding methodological claim: Vinaya *requires* contemplation (*xí lǜ zhī xū guān* 習律之須觀), since the Buddha himself in the *vinaya-vibhaṅga* enjoins the *bhikṣu* to "uphold dignity in postures, in robes, medicines and food, in word and gesture, in rest and movement, ever with one mind." Composition is bracketed by Shǒu-yī's residence at Rì-shān (from 1239); `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1239–1260.

## Other points of interest

- The *huì-yuán* (gathering-at-the-source) format is a Southern-Sòng pedagogical genre, distinct from the *xíng-shì-chāo* 行事鈔 / *zī-chí jì* 資持記 commentary tradition; this work is an important example.
