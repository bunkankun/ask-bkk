# Sìfēn-lǜ shí pí-ní yì-chāo 四分律拾毗尼義鈔
*A Compendium of Vinaya Meanings Picked Up from the Sìfēn-lǜ*
by [[道宣]] (Dào-xuān, 撰)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Tang Vinaya digest by **Dào-xuān** 道宣 ([[道宣]], 596–667) preserving extracts and discussions of *Sìfēn-lǜ* 四分律 material that did not find a home in his three principal Vinaya commentaries (*Xíng-shì chāo* 行事鈔, *Jiè-běn shū* 戒本疏, *Suí-jī jié-mó shū* 隨機羯磨疏). *Shí* 拾 ("picking up") signals the *Yì-chāo*'s status as a supplement to the main *Sìfēn-lǜ* corpus.

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with **Yuán-zhào**'s ([[元照]]) editorial preface, *Jiào-kān yì-chāo xù* 校勘義鈔序 ("Preface to the Collated Yì-chāo"), signed *餘杭郡沙門釋　元照　述* — recording Yuán-zhào's eleventh-century recovery and collation of the work, which had become rare and corrupt in transmission ("*chéng-zhōng zhī rǔ rì jiàn guāi zhēn* 城中之乳日漸乖真" — "the milk in the city deviates daily from its true taste"). Yuán-zhào lists three goals of his recension: (1) clarifying the *zhuàn-lái yì* 撰來意 (authorial intent), (2) refuting variant readings, (3) collating the textual witnesses.

### Abstract

The *Shí pí-ní yì-chāo* is a less-studied but important Vinaya digest by Dàoxuān, supplying material that fell outside his three principal *Sìfēn* commentaries. Yuán-zhào's preface confirms that the work had been transmitted continuously since Dàoxuān's time but had become textually corrupt and scarce by the eleventh century; Yuán-zhào's *jiào-kān* recension is the form preserved in the canon. The companion sub-commentary [[KR6k0177]] *Sìfēn-lǜ shí pí-ní yì-chāo fǔ-yào jì* by **Yǔn Kān** 允堪 ([[允堪]]) further attests to the work's standing in the Sòng *Lǜ-zōng* curriculum. Composition belongs to Dàoxuān's mature Vinaya period at Zhōng-nán.
