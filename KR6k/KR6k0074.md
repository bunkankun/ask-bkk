# Fó shuō Mùlián wǔ-bǎi-wèn jīng lüè-jiě 佛說目連五百問經略解
*Concise Commentary on the Maudgalyāyana Five-Hundred Questions Sūtra*
by [[性祇]] (Xìng Qí, 述)

## About the work

A two-fascicle late-Míng abridged commentary on the *Mùlián wǔ-bǎi-wèn jīng* ([[KR6k0073]]) by **Xìng Qí** 性祇 ([[性祇]]). The commentary glosses the casuistic Vinaya questions of the parent sūtra, explaining the Buddha's adjudications and relating them to standard Vinaya doctrine.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明性祇述*.

### Abstract

Xìng Qí's commentary is one of two late-Míng treatments of the *Mùlián wǔ-bǎi-wèn* casuistic literature (the other being [[永海]]'s [[KR6k0075]]). The two commentaries reflect the late-Míng Vinaya revival under figures like **Yúnqī Zhūhóng** 雲棲袾宏 ([[袾宏]]) and **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕩益智旭 ([[智旭]]) — a movement to recover practical Vinaya scholarship after the long Míng decline of monastic discipline.
