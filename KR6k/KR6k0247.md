# Chuán-shòu sān-tán hóng-jiè fǎ-yí 傳授三壇弘戒法儀
*A Code for the Transmission of the Three-Tier Promotion of the Precepts*
by [[法藏]] (Fǎ-zàng, 撰集); [[超遠]] (Chāo-yuǎn, 檢錄)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng *sān-tán* 三壇 (three-tier) ordination manual, layered in two compositional stages: (i) the late-Míng original *zhuàn-jí* 撰集 by **Sān-fēng Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** 三峰漢月法藏 ([[法藏]], 1573–1635) at *Běi-chán-sì* 北禪寺 in the *Tiān-qǐ* 天啟 era (1621–1627); (ii) the early-Qīng *jiǎn-lù* 檢錄 (review-and-record) by **Zǐ-sù Chāo-yuǎn** 子肅超遠 ([[超遠]], 1631–1689) at *Pán-lóng chán-sì* 蟠龍禪寺 on Mt Zhōng-nán (Shǎn-xī). Author signature: *Míng Tiān-qǐ Běi-chán-sì hóng-jiè shā-mén Fǎ-zàng zhuàn-jí / Qīng Zhōng-nán-shān Pán-lóng chán-sì bǐ-qiū Chāo-yuǎn jiǎn-lù* 明天啟北禪寺弘戒沙門　法藏　撰集／清終南山蟠龍禪寺比丘　超遠　檢錄.

### Structural Division

**Upper section** — *Shòu shā-mí shí-jiè fǎ* 授沙彌十戒法 (the rite for conferring the ten *śrāmaṇera* precepts).

**Middle section** — *Shòu bǐ-qiū jù-zú jiè fǎ* 授比丘具足戒法 (the rite for conferring the *bhikṣu upasampadā*, employing the *bái-sì jié-mó* with the *sān-shī qī-zhèng* 三師七證 structure).

**Lower section** — *Shòu pú-sà jiè fǎ* 授菩薩戒法 (the rite for conferring the *Fàn-wǎng* bodhisattva precepts).

Each section walks through the rite step by step with rubrics for the *kāi-dǎo* 開導 (instructor), the *zhí-shì* 執事 (officials), the candidates, and the bell-and-drum signals.

### Abstract

The *Chuán-shòu sān-tán hóng-jiè fǎ-yí* is the **principal Sān-fēng-line *sān-tán* ordination manual**, produced by Fǎ-zàng during his abbacy at *Běi-chán-sì* in *Sū-zhōu* (in the Tiān-qǐ era of the late Míng) and subsequently *jiǎn-lù*-ed by his lineage-descendant **Chāo-yuǎn** at *Zhōng-nán-shān Pán-lóng-sì* in the early Qīng. **Chāo-yuǎn** had himself studied Vinaya under *Jiàn-yuè Dú-tǐ* at Bǎo-huá-shān before turning to the Línjì lineage (cf. [[KR6k0226]]), so his check-redaction of Fǎ-zàng's manual in 1670s–1680s reflects his rare cross-lineage fluency in both the Bǎo-huá-shān and Sān-fēng-line ordination traditions. Composition of the present *jiǎn-lù*-ed recension is bracketed by Chāo-yuǎn's accession to the *Pán-lóng-sì* abbacy in 1672 and his death in 1689; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1672–1689.

The work shares the proscribed status of [[KR6k0246]] *Hóng-jiè fǎ-yí* — both were targeted by the *Yōng-zhèng* emperor's 1733 anti-Sān-fēng polemic.

## Other points of interest

- The unusual cross-lineage status of Chāo-yuǎn (Bǎo-huá-shān-trained Vinaya, Línjì-Yáng-qí Chán) makes this manual one of the rare early-Qīng ordination texts that bridges the otherwise antagonistic *Sān-fēng* and *Bǎo-huá-shān* lines.
