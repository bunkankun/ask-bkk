# Fó shuō Mùlián wèn jièlǜ zhōng wǔ-bǎi qīng-zhòng shì jīng 佛說目連問戒律中五百輕重事經
*The Sūtra of Maudgalyāyana's Five-Hundred Inquiries* (recension b)
(translator unknown)

## About the work

A two-fascicle variant recension of the *Mùlián wǔ-bǎi-wèn* (cf. KR6k0072), preserved as Taishō T1483b. Translator and date unknown.

### Prefaces

No translator-attribution survives.

### Abstract

The two-recension transmission of the *Mùlián wǔ-bǎi-wèn* (T1483a vs. T1483b) reflects the textual variability characteristic of Vinaya-casuistic literature in early-medieval Chinese transmission. The "b" recension is fuller (two fascicles vs. one) and includes additional cases not in T1483a. Both recensions are commented on in the late-Míng works of [[性祇]] ([[KR6k0074]]) and [[永海]] ([[KR6k0075]]).
