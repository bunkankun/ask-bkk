# Sìfēnlǜ suíjī jiémó shū zhèngyuán jì 四分律隨機羯磨疏正源記
*Records on Restoring the Source: Notes on the Commentary on the Karman-text Adapted to Circumstances*
by [[允堪]] (Yǔn Kān, 述)

## About the work

An eight-fascicle Northern Sòng *jì* 記 sub-commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēnlǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó shū* 四分律刪補隨機羯磨疏 ([[KR6k0019]]) by **Yǔn Kān** 允堪 ([[允堪]], 1005–1061). *Zhèngyuán* 正源 ("rectifying the source") signals Yǔn Kān's editorial intent: to restore Dàoxuān's original karman-text commentary against the corruptions and lacunae of the eleventh-century Sòng witnesses.

### Prefaces

The Manji edition prints a structural index (*mùcì* 目次) at the head of *juan* 1 that reproduces Dàoxuān's own *xù* and *Suíjī jiémó xù* before launching into the eight-fascicle *jì*. No separate YǔnKān preface survives.

### Abstract

The *Zhèngyuán jì* is one of the principal Northern Sòng sub-commentaries on Dàoxuān's karman-text manual ([[KR6k0019]]) and forms — together with Yǔn Kān's *jì* on the *Sìfēnlǜ hánzhù jièběn shū* ([[KR6k0144]]) — the core of his contribution to the Sòng Vinaya revival. The work proceeds *piān*-by-*piān* through Dàoxuān's eight chapters (*jífǎ* 集法, *jiéjiè* 結界, *shòufǎ* 受法, *yīyào shòujìng* 衣藥受淨 etc.), reconstructing the text-history and supplying lemmatic glosses on each of Dàoxuān's *kē* divisions. Yǔn Kān's reconstructive philological work was decisive for the later Sòng *Lǜzōng*: his sub-commentary supplied the practical resource that allowed Yuánzhào ([[元照]]) and the Língzhīsì 靈芝寺 successors to revive the *Nánshān* tradition in the second half of the eleventh century. Composition cannot be precisely dated within Yǔn Kān's career; the bracket here gives his mature scholarly years (he reached majority around the late-1030s).
