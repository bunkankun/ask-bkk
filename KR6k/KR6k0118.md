# Fó shuō zhèng gōng-jìng jīng 佛說正恭敬經
*The Sūtra of Right Respect*
by [[佛陀扇多]] (Buddhaśānta, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Mahāyāna *sūtra* on the right-respect etiquette of Buddhist practice, translated by **Buddhaśānta** 佛陀扇多 ([[佛陀扇多]]) at Yè 鄴 of the Northern Wèi 元魏 in 525–539 CE. An alternate translation to KR6k0117 of essentially the same Sanskrit *sūtra*.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *元魏天竺三藏佛陀扇多譯*.

### Abstract

The text presents the same content as Jñānagupta's [[KR6k0117]] in an earlier Northern-Wèi recension. The double translation in two distinct Chinese renderings of the same Indian source-text indicates the importance of the Three-Jewels-etiquette tradition in early-medieval Chinese Buddhism.
