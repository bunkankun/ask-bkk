# Sìfēn lǜ hán-zhù jièběn shū kē 四分律含注戒本疏科
*Topical Outline for the Commentary on the Embedded-Annotation Prātimokṣa*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 錄)

## About the work

A four-fascicle topical outline (科 *kē*) for the commentary on Dàoxuān's *Hán-zhù jièběn* ([[KR6k0131]]), recorded (錄 *lù*) by **Yuán-zhào** 元照 ([[元照]]). The 科 *kē* genre — outlines / topical synopses — is a standard medieval Chinese Buddhist scholastic apparatus providing the analytical structure of a parent commentary.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *宋元照錄*.

### Abstract

Yuán-zhào's outline supplies the working analytical structure for the study of the Dàoxuān-period *Hán-zhù jièběn* commentary tradition. The outline format facilitated rapid reference and instructional use, making the parent text accessible to Sòng-period students of the *Sìfēn* Vinaya.
