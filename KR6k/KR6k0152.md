# Pí-ní guān-yào 毗尼關要
*The Essentials of Vinaya*
by [[德基]] (Dé-jī, 輯)

## About the work

A sixteen-fascicle early-Qīng commentary on the *Sìfēn bǐqiū jiè-běn* 四分比丘戒本 ([[KR6k0010]]) compiled by **Dìng-ān Dé-jī** 定菴德基 ([[德基]], 1634–1700/01), third-generation patriarch of **Bǎo-huá-shān Lǜ-zōng** 寶華山律宗 and dharma-heir of [[讀體]] Jiàn-yuè Dú-tǐ. *Guān-yào* 關要 ("the essential pivot") indicates a comprehensive practical study-manual rather than a strict textual commentary. The work supplies a thorough phrase-by-phrase exegesis of the *prātimokṣa* in the early-Qīng Bǎo-huá-shān manner and is paired with the companion glossary *Pí-ní guān-yào shì-yì* 毗尼關要事義 ([[KR6k0153]]).

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a *xù* by an unnamed admirer (the source preserves the prefatorial signature only as the contributor's *zì-shù*) recording that the work was completed after Dé-jī's "more than twenty summers" of Vinaya study, and naming the southern transmission **Sān-mèi → Jiàn-yuè → Dìng-ān** as the institutional chain at Bǎo-huá-shān. The *Tiānhuá lǎo-rén* 千華老人 (= Sān-mèi Jì-guāng) and *Nán-lái Jiàn-zhě héshàng* 南來見者和尚 (= Jiàn-yuè Dú-tǐ) are named as Dé-jī's lineage predecessors.

### Abstract

The *Pí-ní guān-yào* is the principal **early-Qīng Bǎo-huá-shān** *Sìfēn-lǜ* study manual, occupying for the early-Qīng monastic curriculum the position that Dàoxuān's *Sìfēn-lǜ shān-fán bǔ-quē xíng-shì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 had occupied for Tang and Sòng *Nán-shān* monks. It systematises the practical implementation of the *prātimokṣa* under the institutional conditions of the Bǎo-huá-shān ordination platform — the dominant Vinaya-school institution of early-Qīng China — and absorbs the late-Míng Vinaya scholarship of [[智旭]] (the [[KR6k0151]] *Chóng-zhì pí-ní shì-yì jí-yào*) into a Bǎo-huá-shān framework. The companion *Shì-yì* ([[KR6k0153]]) supplies the lexical and historical glosses for the names, places, and technical terms cited in the *Guān-yào*. Composition cannot be precisely dated; it must postdate Dé-jī's succession to the abbacy after 1679 (Dú-tǐ's death) but precede his own death in 1700/01.
