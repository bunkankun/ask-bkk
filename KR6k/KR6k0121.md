# Púsà-jiè jiémó-wén 菩薩戒羯磨文
*The Karmavācanā for the Bodhisattva Precepts*
by [[彌勒菩薩]] (Maitreya, 說) and [[玄奘]] (Xuánzàng, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle *karmavācanā* (procedural-formula) text for the conferral of the bodhisattva-precepts, attributed to **Maitreya Bodhisattva** 彌勒菩薩 ([[彌勒菩薩]]) and translated by **Xuánzàng** 玄奘 ([[玄奘]]) at Cháng-ān 645–664 CE. Excerpted from the *Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra* (T30n1579) bodhisattva-bhūmi section.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *大唐三藏法師玄奘奉詔譯*.

### Abstract

The *Púsà-jiè jiémó-wén* is the procedural-formula handbook for the **Yogācāra** tradition's conferral of the bodhisattva-precepts. Where the *Fànwǎng jīng* tradition presents the bodhisattva-precepts in narrative-cosmological form, the *Yogācārabhūmi*-derived tradition presents them as a systematic procedural rite parallel to the Vinaya *karmavācanā*. The text is foundational for the Yogācāra-Cí'ēn 慈恩 reading of the bodhisattva-precepts and is a key counterpart to the *Fànwǎng* tradition. The Sanskrit original is preserved in Asaṅga's *Yogācārabhūmi*.
