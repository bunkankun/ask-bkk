# Fànwǎng-jīng shùn-zhū 梵網經順硃
*The Vermilion-Marked Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[德玉]] (Dé Yù, 順硃)

## About the work

A two-fascicle early-Qīng commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* by **Dé Yù** 德玉 ([[德玉]]). 順硃 *shùn-zhū* "vermilion-marked" indicates a commentarial format in which the parent text is presented with red-ink (硃 = 朱) marginal annotations.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *清德玉順硃*.

### Abstract

The *shùn-zhū* genre — sūtra texts with red-ink commentarial annotation — was a popular early-Qīng format for the practical study of canonical texts. Dé Yù's *Fànwǎng* edition presents the bodhisattva-precept text with concise marginal explanations suited to instructional and devotional use.
