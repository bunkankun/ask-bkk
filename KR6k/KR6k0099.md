# Bàn-yuè sòng púsà-jiè yí-shì zhù 半月誦菩薩戒儀式註
*Annotated Liturgy for the Bi-monthly Bodhisattva-Precept Recitation*
by [[弘贊]] (Hóngzàn, 注)

## About the work

A one-fascicle annotated liturgical handbook for the **bi-monthly bodhisattva-precept recitation** (半月誦 *bàn-yuè sòng* — the bodhisattva counterpart to the *uposatha prātimokṣa* recitation), compiled by **Hóngzàn** 弘贊 ([[弘贊]]). Provides procedural instructions, formulaic recitations, and explanatory notes for the conduct of the ceremony.

### Prefaces

Author: 明弘贊.

### Abstract

The text is a practical liturgical handbook for the regular Mahāyāna bodhisattva-precept observance. The bi-monthly recitation of the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts paralleled the *prātimokṣa* recitation of the formal Vinaya, providing a public-communal renewal of the bodhisattva-vow. Hóngzàn's annotated version supplied the practical handbook used in Bǎohuá-shān and other late-imperial Chinese ordination centres.
