# Fànwǎngjīng púsàjiè lüèshū 梵網經菩薩戒略疏
*Concise Commentary on the Bodhisattva Precepts of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[弘贊]] (Hóngzàn, 述)

## About the work

An eight-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts by the Vinaya specialist **Hóngzàn** 弘贊 ([[弘贊]], 1611–1685). 略疏 *lüèshū* "concise commentary" — though the eight fascicles indicate that the work is concise only by comparison with the encyclopedic Tang commentaries.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明弘贊述*.

### Abstract

Hóngzàn's commentary is one of the principal late-Míng / early-Qīng commentaries on the *Fànwǎng jīng*, alongside Zhìxù's [[KR6k0097]] and Jì-guāng's [[KR6k0100]]. Hóngzàn approached the *Fànwǎng* from a Vinaya-specialist perspective, emphasising practical procedural-disciplinary application over against the doctrinal-philosophical readings of Zhūhóng and Zhìxù. The text is preserved in the Jiāxīng 嘉興 edition of the canon — a valuable Míng-Qīng compilation independent of the standard Buddhist Tripiṭaka.
