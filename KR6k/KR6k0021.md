# Sàpóduō pínì pípóshā 薩婆多毘尼毘婆沙
*The Sarvāstivāda-Vinaya-Vibhāṣā* (Commentary on the Sarvāstivāda Vinaya)
(translator unknown)

## About the work

A nine-fascicle Chinese rendering of the Sanskrit *Sarvāstivāda-vinaya-vibhāṣā* — a commentary on the *Shísòng lǜ* ([[KR6k0016]]) preserving Indian Sarvāstivāda exegetical tradition. 薩婆多 *Sàpóduō* transliterates *sarvāstivāda*; 毘尼 *pínì* transliterates *vinaya*; 毘婆沙 *pípóshā* transliterates *vibhāṣā* (commentary). Translator and date unknown; the catalog tradition assigns it to "lost translation" (失譯), but the text's diction and translation conventions place it in the late-Eastern Jìn / early-Liú-Sòng period (late 4th to early 5th c.).

### Prefaces

No translator-attribution survives. The text is documented as anonymous in the *Chū sānzàng jìjí* (T55n2145) and the *Kāiyuán shìjiào lù* (T55n2154).

### Abstract

The *Sàpóduō pínì pípóshā* is the principal **Sarvāstivāda Vinaya commentary** in the Chinese canon (parallel to the Dharmaguptaka *Pínì-mǔ jīng* [[KR6k0044]] and the Theravāda *Shàn-jiàn lǜ pípóshā* [[KR6k0043]]). The text systematically expounds the *bhikṣu-prātimokṣa* of the *Shísòng lǜ*, providing exegetical comment on the rule-formulations, with technical discussions of *karmavācanā* procedure, school identification, and inter-Vinaya comparison. It is one of the earliest examples of Indian *vinaya-vibhāṣā* exegetical literature preserved in Chinese, and a key witness for the early Sarvāstivāda exegetical tradition.
