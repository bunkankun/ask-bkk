# Zī-chí jì lì-tí shí-yì 資持記立題拾義
*Picking Up the Meanings of the Title-Establishment in the Zī-chí jì*
by [[道標]] (Dào-biāo, 出)

## About the work

A brief one-juan Sòng exposition of the title-line of **Yuán-zhào**'s ([[元照]], 1048–1116) *Zī-chí jì* 資持記 — Yuán-zhào's principal sub-commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēn-lǜ shān-fán bǔ-quē xíng-shì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]) — by **Dào-biāo** 道標 ([[道標]]) of *Mó-yún-táng* 摩雲堂. Author signature: *摩雲堂佛慧宗師　道標　出* (*Set forth by Dào-biāo, master Fó-huì of Mó-yún-táng*).

### Prefaces

The work itself opens with the lemmatic exposition of the title; no separate front-matter.

### Abstract

The *Lì-tí shí-yì* glosses each phrase of the *Zī-chí jì*'s long title (*Sìfēn-lǜ Xíng-shì chāo Zī-chí jì*), distinguishing the *tōng-tí* 通題 (general title) from the *bié-mù* 別目 (specific designation) and explaining the relation of *suǒ-jiě* 所解 (object of explanation) to *néng-jiě* 能解 (means of explanation). The opening passage explicitly cites Dàoxuān's *Jiè-shū* 戒疏 — "*Sìfēn* is the specific name of one of the five recensions and the general designation of one school" — and notes that more than fifty earlier *Xíng-shì chāo* commentators (*wǔ-shí yú jiā* 五十餘家) had already glossed the title in their own ways. Dating: the work must postdate Yuán-zhào (post-1116) and falls within the Southern-Sòng range.
