# Fànwǎng-jīng púsà-jiè-běn shū 梵網經菩薩戒本疏
*Commentary on the Bodhisattva Precept-Text of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[知周]] (Zhī Zhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A two-fascicle commentary on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts by the mid-Tang Cí'ēn-Yogācāra scholar **Zhī Zhōu** 知周 ([[知周]], 668–723).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐慈恩沙門知周述*.

### Abstract

Zhī Zhōu's commentary represents the third-generation Cí'ēn Yogācāra reading of the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts. Working within the lineage of Xuánzàng-Kuījī, Zhī Zhōu integrates the Yogācāra *bhūmi*-doctrine with the *Fànwǎng* precepts, treating each precept as an expression of the bodhisattva's progressive realisation of the *bhūmis*.
