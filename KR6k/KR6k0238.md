# Lǐ shè-lì-tǎ yí-shì 禮舍利塔儀式
*The Ritual Code for the Veneration of Relic-Stūpas*
by [[弘贊]] (Hóng-zàn, 編)

## About the work

A single-fascicle early-Qīng liturgical manual by **Hóng-zàn** ([[弘贊]], 1612–1686) for the formal worship of *śarīra*-stūpas. Author signature: *Yuè-dōng Dǐng-hú-shān shā-mén shì Hóng-zàn Zài-shēn biān* 粵東鼎湖山沙門釋　弘贊在犙　編.

### Opening doctrinal frame

The opening doctrinal note covers (i) the etymology of *shè-lì* 舍利 (Skt. *śarīra*; transliterations *shì-lì-luó* 室利羅, *shè-lì-luó* 設利羅 — variant Sanskrit registrations); (ii) the canonical taxonomy of relics — bone (white), hair (black), flesh (red); (iii) the doctrinal significance of relics — those of a Buddha cannot be broken when struck (*chuí-jī bù-pò* 槌擊不破), those of an *arhat* shatter on test, indicating the Buddha's *jiè-dìng-huì xūn-xiū* 戒定慧熏修 ("perfection by perfumation through the precepts, meditation and wisdom").

### Structural Division

The fascicle gives a complete worship-cycle, opening with *yī-xīn fèng-qǐng* 一心奉請 invocations of the *śarīra*-stūpa; the *xiāng-huā gòng-yǎng* 香華供養 (incense-and-flower offerings); a sequence of *zàn* 讚 (eulogies) drawn from the *Běn-xíng jīng* 本行經 and *Shàn-jiè jīng* 善戒經 on the merit of stūpa-veneration; and the closing dedication.

### Abstract

The *Lǐ shè-lì-tǎ yí-shì* is part of Hóng-zàn's **liturgical-procedural trilogy** for Cantonese-Buddhist devotional practice, alongside [[KR6k0239]] *Lǐ Fó yí-shì* (Buddha-veneration) and [[KR6k0240]] *Gòng zhū-tiān kē-yí* (offering to the *devas*). It is a polemically-conscious work: Hóng-zàn complains that "in the present time those who do reverence do not consult the canonical institution and contravene the standards; they only know how to call the names but do not know how to praise the virtues" (今時禮敬。罔諳教法。多乖儀則。唯知稱名。不曉讚德), citing the *Běn-xíng* on the superiority of even one four-line *zàn* over years of material support, as the work's polemical justification. Composition is bracketed by Hóng-zàn's mature career; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are 1660–1686.
