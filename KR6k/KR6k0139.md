# Sān-bù lǜ-chāo 三部律抄
*Excerpts from the Three Vinaya Schools* (Dunhuang)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Dunhuang Vinaya excerpts-collection drawing on three Vinaya schools (三部 *sān-bù*) — likely the *Sìfēn*, *Shísòng*, and *Sēngqí*.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The text presents comparative Vinaya material drawn from three of the four canonical Chinese Vinayas, providing the late-Tang Dunhuang reader with a synoptic resource for cross-school Vinaya comparison. Such cross-school Vinaya scholarship was characteristic of Dàoxuān's Nán-shān approach and is attested in this Dunhuang fragment as a wider feature of late-Tang Vinaya studies.
