# Fànwǎng púsàjièjīng shūzhù 梵網菩薩戒經疏註
*Commentarial Annotations on the Brahmajāla Bodhisattva Precept Sūtra*
by [[與咸]] (Yǔ Xián, 入疏箋經加註釋疏)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Sòng-dynasty annotated commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng*, with elaborate textual apparatus integrating the parent text, the major Tang commentary (Fǎzàng's [[KR6k0080]]), and explanatory annotations. Compiled by **Yǔ Xián** 與咸 ([[與咸]]) of the Sòng. The compositional method (入疏箋經加註釋疏) reflects an elaborate synoptic format combining sūtra, commentary, and gloss.

### Prefaces

Compiler's colophon: *宋與咸入疏箋經加註釋疏*.

### Abstract

The text is one of the more elaborate Sòng-period commentary compendia on the *Fànwǎng jīng*. It reflects the scholastic integration achieved by Sòng-period Buddhist exegetes, who combined the major Tang commentary lineages into multi-layered synoptic editions facilitating instructional use.
