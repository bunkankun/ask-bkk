# Fó shuō Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn jièlǜzhōng qīngzhòngshì jīngshì 佛說目連五百問戒律中輕重事經釋
*Commentary on the Maudgalyāyana Five-Hundred Questions Sūtra*
by [[永海]] (Yǒng Hǎi, 述)

## About the work

A two-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the *Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn* sūtra ([[KR6k0073]]) by **Yǒng Hǎi** 永海 ([[永海]]). Companion to **Xìng Qí's** [[KR6k0074]].

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明永海述*.

### Abstract

Yǒng Hǎi's commentary, like its companion by Xìng Qí, reflects the late-Míng Vinaya revival's interest in casuistic disciplinary literature. Where Xìng Qí's *lüèjiě* tends to abridge, Yǒng Hǎi's *jīngshì* tends to expand and contextualise the Buddha's adjudications, providing more discussion of doctrinal background and historical precedent. The two are typically read together.
