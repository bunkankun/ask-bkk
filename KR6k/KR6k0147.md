# Shì Sìfēn jièběn xù 釋四分戒本序
*Explanation of the Preface to the Sìfēn Prātimokṣa*
by [[道言]] (Dào Yán, 述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-dynasty commentary by **Dào Yán** 道言 ([[道言]]) on the introductory matter (序 *xù*) of the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa*.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *宋道言述*.

### Abstract

The text focuses specifically on the preface and introductory formulae of the *Sìfēn jièběn*, providing detailed exposition of the *uposatha*-opening formula and the standard liturgical preliminaries. Such focused commentary on prefatory material was a distinctive Sòng-period scholastic genre.
