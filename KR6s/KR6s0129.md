# Zhù-jìn Fǎ-xiàng zōng zhāng-shū 注進法相宗章疏
*The Annotated-and-Presented Fǎ-xiàng (Yogācāra) School's Chapters and Commentaries*
by [[藏俊]] (撰), = **Zōshun** 藏俊 (1104–1180)

## About the work

A single-juan **Heian-era Japanese Fǎ-xiàng (Yogācāra) school annotated bibliography**, by **Zōshun** 藏俊 (Japanese **Zōshun**, **1104–1180**) — the leading mid-12th-century Hossō-school scholar at **Kōfuku-ji** 興福寺 in Nara. The work is a **expanded and annotated** version of [[KR6s0128]], adding scholarly commentary and presentation-context to the bibliographic register. Preserved at T55 no. 2181. *notBefore* = **1100**, *notAfter* = **1200**. Catalog dynasty 日本.
