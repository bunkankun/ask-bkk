# Fú-zhōu Wēn-zhōu Tāi-zhōu qiú-dé jīng-lǜ-lùn shū-jì wài-shū děng mù-lù 福州溫州台州求得經律論疏記外書等目錄
*The Catalog of Sutras-Vinaya-Treatises-Commentaries-Records-and-Outer-Books Obtained at Fú-zhōu, Wēn-zhōu, and Tāi-zhōu*
by [[圓珍]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim importation catalog**, the second of **Enchin's** 圓珍 five importation catalogs (cf. [[KR6s0116]], [[KR6s0118]]–[[KR6s0120]]). This catalog documents materials acquired in three southeast-coastal Tang prefectures — **Fú-zhōu** 福州 (modern Fú-jiàn), **Wēn-zhōu** 溫州 and **Tāi-zhōu** 台州 (both in Zhèjiāng) — during Enchin's Tang study mission. Includes both Buddhist canonical materials (sutras, vinaya, treatises, commentaries, records) and **outer books** (*wài-shū* 外書 = secular literature). Preserved at T55 no. 2170. *notBefore* = **858**, *notAfter* = **858**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

See [[KR6s0116]] for substantive treatment.
