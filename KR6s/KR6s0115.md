# Huìyùn lǜshī shū mùlù 惠運律師書目錄
*The Book Catalog of the Vinaya Master Huì-yùn*
by [[惠運]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **companion catalog** to **Eun's** principal importation catalog ([[KR6s0114]]), focused specifically on **vinaya** (*lǜ* 律) materials brought back from Tang. Preserved at T55 no. 2168B.

### Abstract

Companion to [[KR6s0114]]. *notBefore* = **847**, *notAfter* = **847**. Catalog dynasty 日本. The work documents the **vinaya** subset of Eun's importation, separately catalogued for the Heian-court vinaya-establishment review.

See [[KR6s0114]] for substantive treatment of Eun.
