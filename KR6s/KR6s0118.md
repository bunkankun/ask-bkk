# Qīng-lóng-sì qiú-fǎ mù-lù 青龍寺求法目錄
*The Catalog of Dharma-Seeking at Qīng-lóng-sì*
by [[圓珍]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim Esoteric importation catalog** by **Enchin** 圓珍, third of his five catalogs, documenting Esoteric materials acquired at **Qīng-lóng-sì** 青龍寺 in Cháng-ān — the principal Esoteric monastery of the late Tang capital, where **Huì-guǒ** 惠果 (Kūkai's master) had earlier transmitted the dual-mandala lineage. Enchin received Esoteric initiation here from **Fǎ-quán** 法全 (a late-Tang Esoteric master). Preserved at T55 no. 2171. *notBefore* = **858**, *notAfter* = **858**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

See [[KR6s0116]] for substantive treatment.
