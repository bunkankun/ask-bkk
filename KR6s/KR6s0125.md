# Huāyán zōng zhāngshū bìng yīnmíng lù 華嚴宗章疏并因明錄
*The Catalog of the Huá-yán School's Chapters-and-Commentaries with Logic*
by [[圓超]] (錄), = **Enchō** 圓超

## About the work

A single-juan **Heian-era Japanese Buddhist sectarian-bibliographic catalog** of materials in the **Huáyán** 華嚴 (Japanese **Kegon**) school combined with **Yīnmíng** 因明 (Buddhist logic) materials. By **Enchō** 圓超 (Japanese, dates not preserved). Preserved at T55 no. 2177. *notBefore* = **950**, *notAfter* = **1100** (broadly mid-Heian). Catalog dynasty 日本.

One of the **seven sectarian bibliographies** ([[KR6s0125]]–[[KR6s0131]]) of the Heian Japanese Buddhist establishment that catalog the major doctrinal schools' textual holdings.
