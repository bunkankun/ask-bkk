# Rì-běn bǐ-qiū Yuán-zhēn rù-Táng qiú-fǎ mù-lù 日本比丘圓珍入唐求法目錄
*The Catalog of Tang-Pilgrimage Dharma-Seeking by the Japanese Bhikkhu Enchin*
by [[圓珍]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim importation catalog** by **Enchin** 圓珍, fourth of his five catalogs. The work is a **comprehensive** importation catalog summarizing Enchin's entire Tang acquisitions across his various study locations (Cháng-ān Qīng-lóng-sì, Cháng-ān Kāi-yuán-sì, Tāi-zhōu, Wēn-zhōu, Fú-zhōu) — the comprehensive form of the more focused individual catalogs ([[KR6s0116]]–[[KR6s0118]]). Preserved at T55 no. 2172. *notBefore* = **858**, *notAfter* = **858**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

See [[KR6s0116]] for substantive treatment.
