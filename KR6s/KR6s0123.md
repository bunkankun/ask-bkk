# Lù-wài jīng děng mù-lù 錄外經等目錄
*The Catalog of Sutras-Outside-the-Catalog*
anonymous (Heian-era Japanese Buddhist supplementary catalog)

## About the work

A single-juan **anonymous Heian-era Japanese supplementary catalog** of canonical sutras and other materials **not included in the principal canonical bibliographies** (*lù-wài* 錄外, "outside-the-catalog"). Preserved at T55 no. 2175. *notBefore* = **800**, *notAfter* = **1000** (broadly the Heian-era bracket). Catalog dynasty 日本.

The work documents materials acquired through *nittō hakke* importations or other channels but not formally entered into the standard Heian canonical-bibliographic apparatus. As a supplementary catalog, it is a primary witness to the **breadth of Heian-era Japanese Buddhist textual holdings beyond the canonical core**.
