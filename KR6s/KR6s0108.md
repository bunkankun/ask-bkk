# Gēn-běn dà-hé-shàng zhēn-jì cè-zǐ děng mù-lù 根本大和尚真跡策子等目錄
*A Catalog of the Authentic-Trace Notebooks etc. of the Foundational Great Master*
attributed to **Saichō** 最澄 (767–822)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tendai-school catalog** of authograph (*zhēn-jì* 真跡 = "authentic trace") notebook materials of the **Foundational Great Master** (*gēn-běn dà-hé-shàng* 根本大和尚) — almost certainly **Saichō** 最澄, the foundational master of Japanese Tendai. The Kanripo catalog meta does not specify an author, but the title's "**Foundational Great Master**" wording and the **804–822 dating bracket** (Saichō's Tang return through his death) align the text with Saichō's circle. Preserved at T55 no. 2162.

### Abstract

The work documents the autograph manuscript-notebooks (*cè-zǐ* 策子, "small bound notebooks" in Japanese-Heian convention) and other materials associated with Saichō. *notBefore* = **805**, *notAfter* = **822**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

The work is one of the principal **early-Heian Japanese Tendai documentary materials**, supplementing the canonical importation catalogs ([[KR6s0105]]–[[KR6s0106]]) with notebook-and-personal-document materials.
