# Wáng Fán-zhì shī jiào-zhù 王梵志詩校注
*Wáng Fán-zhì's Poems, Critically Annotated*

## About the work

The Kanripo catalog meta lists this entry as **title-only** (source: *krp-titles*) — there are no source files in the local Kanripo collection at `/home/Shared/krp/KR6s/KR6s0134/`. The reference is to the **modern scholarly critical edition** of the **Wáng Fán-zhì** poetry corpus — most authoritatively, **Xiàng Chǔ** 項楚, *Wáng Fán-zhì shī jiào-zhù* 王梵志詩校注 (Shàng-hǎi Gǔ-jí Chū-bǎn-shè, 1991, with successive expanded editions in the 2010s). Other modern editions exist; the *jiào-zhù* form-of-title is generic for critical editions.

The reference here is for cross-cataloging purposes: the actual poetic text is treated under [[KR6s0055]] *Wáng Fán-zhì shī jí* (T54n2863, the Taishō-canonical recension of the corpus). For substantive content see [[KR6s0055]] and [[Persons/王梵志]].
