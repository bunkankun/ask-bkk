# Xiūzhēn shíshū zázhù zhǐxuán piān 修真十書雜著指玄篇

*Ten Books on the Cultivation of Truth: Miscellaneous Writings Pointing to the Mystery*

by [[白玉蟾]] (撰, hào Hǎiqióngzǐ 海瓊子, 1194–1229) and others

## About the work

The first eight juan of the comprehensive Southern-lineage *Xiūzhēn shíshū* 修真十書 ("Ten Books on the Cultivation of Truth"), the standard collected edition of *Nán-zōng* 南宗 *nèidān* 內丹 literature, preserved in the *Zhèngtǒng Dàozàng* 正統道藏 (DZ 0263a / CT 263a, juan 1–8 = TC 262 part 1), 洞真部 方法類. The eight juan of the present block carry the section-title *Zázhù zhǐxuán piān* 雜著指玄篇 ("Miscellaneous Writings Pointing to the Mystery") and are attributed primarily to Bái Yùchán 白玉蟾 (born Gě Chánggēng 葛長庚, 1194–1229), the Fifth Patriarch of the Southern Lineage and disciple of Chén Nán 陳楠. The section assembles treatises and poems by Bái Yùchán, his teachers, and his disciples on the subject of inner alchemy. Most of the constituent texts can also be located elsewhere in the canon: juan 1 contains *Bó xiānshēng jīndān huǒhòu tú* 白先生金丹火候圖 (with explanations by Bái's disciples; cf. [[KR5a1309|DZ 1309 *Hǎiqióng chuándàojì*]]); juan 2 the *Huányuán piān* 還元篇 (cf. [[KR5a1091|DZ 1091]]); juan 3 the *Zǐtíng jīng* 紫庭經 (cf. [[KR5a1090|DZ 1090 *Cuìxū piān*]]) and a *Yīnfú suǒ* 陰符鎖 condensed from [[KR5a0119|DZ 119]]; juan 4 *Xiūxiān biànhuò lùn* 修仙辯惑論, *Gǔshén bùsǐ lùn* 谷神不死論, *Yīnyáng shēngjiàng lùn* 陰陽升降論, *Dānfáng fǎyǔ* 丹房法語; juan 5 *Jīndān sì-bǎi zì* 金丹四百字 (cf. [[KR5a1081|DZ 1081]]); juan 6 *Xiè Zhāng Zǐyáng shū* 謝張紫陽書 (Bái's letter of thanks to Zhāng Bóduān); juan 7 poems ascribed to Xǔ Jǐngyáng 許旌陽 and Zhōnglí Quán 鍾離權, plus *Dānsuǐ gē* 丹髓歌 (by Xuē Dàoguāng 薛道光); juan 8 *Wèishēng gē* 衛生歌 by *Xīshān xiānshēng* 西山先生.

### Prefaces

*No preface in the source.* The eight juan open directly with the title-page and proceed through the constituent texts; the longer *Xiūzhēn shíshū* in its complete form (38 juan = DZ 263) does carry separate prefaces for several of its component works, but the *_000.txt* frontmatter file of the present split contains only the bibliographic header.

### Abstract

Farzeen Baldrian-Hussein, in Schipper & Verellen eds., *The Taoist Canon* (2004) 2:839–840 (§3.A.4, *Nèidān* and *Yǎngshēng*), establishes that the *Zázhù zhǐxuán piān* is listed in the *Qiānqīngtáng shūmù* 千頃堂書目 16.25a as *Bái Yùchán zhǐxuán piān* 白玉蟾指玄篇 in eight juan. One of the texts in the present work is dated 1244 (1.10a), suggesting some component pieces were added after Bái's death in 1229. The *Zázhù zhǐxuán piān* is, however, *not* included in Yú Yǎn's 俞琰 (fl. 1284) list of Bái Yùchán's works (cf. *Xī-shàng fú-tán* 西上拂談 2.7b). The *Dàoyán nèi-wài bìjué* 道言內外祕訣 contains a related miscellany also under the name *Zǐqīng* [Bái Yùchán] *zhǐxuán jí* 紫清指玄集, and a parallel collection appears in [[KR5x1219|*Dàozàng jīnghuá lù*]] (*Shūmù lèibiān* 53.24074). The frontmatter brackets composition 1218 (Bái's adoption of his Hǎiqióng *hào*) to 1244 (the latest internal date), with most content composed during Bái's active years 1218–1229 and a few items added by his disciples after his death. In the Daoist canon's own internal organisation, the present *KR5a0264* corresponds to the first portion of *DZ 263 Xiūzhēn shíshū*, which is then followed in the same canon-fascicule by *KR5a0265 Jīndān dàchéng jí* 金丹大成集 (juan 9–13), [[KR5a0266|*Zázhù jiéjiào piān*]] 雜著捷徑篇 (juan 14–24), [[KR5a0267|*Sānzhù wùzhēn piān*]] 三註悟真篇 (juan 25–34), and so on, the whole forming the Sòng-Yuán Nán-zōng 南宗 corpus.
