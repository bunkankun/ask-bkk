# Yī-xué yuán-liú lùn 醫學源流論
*Essays on the Sources and Streams of Medical Learning*
by [[徐大椿]] Xú Dà-chūn (1693–1771).

## About the work

KR3eq012 is a **second hxwd-tradition exemplar** of Xú Dà-chūn's *Yī-xué yuán-liú lùn* (1757), the principal substantive entry for which is **[[KR3eq011]]**. The catalog meta records the title twice within the same KR3eq division because the modern hxwd repatriation series received two distinct manuscript-or-print transmissions of the same work from different Japanese-preserved exemplars, and the cataloguers assigned a separate KR id to each transmission to preserve the textual-witness distinction. The substantive content — the 99-essay arrangement, the 1757 self-preface, the doctrinal positions on the *Jīng-fāng* tradition and against the Jīn-Yuán Four Masters — is identical to KR3eq011 and is treated at length there.

### Prefaces

See [[KR3eq011]] for the dated 1757 self-preface signed at the Bàn-sōng shū-wū 半松書屋 on Wú-shān 吳山.

### Abstract

For the work's doctrinal significance — its founding role in the mid-to-late Qīng *kǎo-jù yī-xué* movement, its programmatic re-canonisation of the Hàn-Táng *Jīng-fāng* tradition, and its polemic against the Jīn-Yuán Four Masters — see the principal entry at [[KR3eq011]].

The present entry is preserved as a separate KR id only to register the textual-witness distinction within the hxwd repatriation tradition; the date-bracket and authorship-attribution follow the principal entry. CBDB records Xú Dà-chūn at id 61225 with lifedates 1693–1771.
