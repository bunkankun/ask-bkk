# Liǔ Bǎo-yí yī-lùn yī-àn 柳寶詒醫論醫案
*Liǔ Bǎo-yí's Medical Discussions and Case Records*
by [[柳寶詒]] Liǔ Bǎo-yí (*zì* Gǔ-sūn 谷孫, 1842–1901), late-Qīng physician of **Jiāng-yīn** 江陰 (Jiāngsū), a key figure of the **Mèng-hé** 孟河 medical school of the late nineteenth century.

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* compilation of clinical-theoretical discussions (*yī-lùn* 醫論) and case-records (*yī-àn* 醫案) by Liǔ Bǎo-yí, gathered posthumously from his clinical notes and published in the early-Republican era as part of the broader effort to preserve the Mèng-hé-school clinical-doctrinal corpus. The catalog meta supplies no personal author tag; the work's title and self-identification establish Liǔ as the author. The compilation falls in the standard Mèng-hé-school clinical-doctrinal genre: case-records prefaced by aetiological-doctrinal reasoning, with explicit cross-reference to the Yè Tiān-shì 葉天士 / Wú Tāng 吳瑭 *Wēn-bìng* canon and the Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng *Shāng-hán* classical-formulary tradition. Liǔ's distinctive doctrinal contribution is the **integration of the *Wēn-bìng* school's clinical methodology with a rigorous *jīng-fāng* engagement** — an integration that defined the Mèng-hé school's late-Qīng synthesis and shaped Republican-era Chinese internal medicine.

### Abstract

Liǔ Bǎo-yí 柳寶詒 (Gǔ-sūn, 1842–1901), Jiāng-yīn-region physician active in the late Qīng, is the founding figure of the **late-Qīng / Republican Mèng-hé branch** of the *Wēn-bìng* school. The catalog meta lists him conventionally as Qīng; the composition window 1870–1907 reflects (a) his maturing clinical career from the early 1870s and (b) the posthumous compilation date of the case-record collection. His other principal works are the *Wēn-rè féng-yuán* 溫熱逢源 — an important late-Qīng systematic treatise on warm-disease — and his contributions to the **Shàoxīng Medical Society** publications under Hé Lián-chén [[何廉臣]]. See person note [[柳寶詒]] for biographical detail.

Doctrinal significance: the work is one of the principal documents of the Mèng-hé school's late-Qīng synthesis between *Wēn-bìng* aetiological-doctrinal reasoning and classical-formulary *jīng-fāng* clinical method. The Mèng-hé school — centered on Cháng-zhōu 常州 / Jiāng-yīn 江陰 and including [[費伯雄]] Fèi Bó-xióng (1800–1879), [[馬培之]] Mǎ Péi-zhī (1820–1903), and the early-Republican [[丁甘仁]] Dīng Gān-rén (1865–1926) — was the most influential single late-Qīng medical lineage and the principal source of the Republican-era Chinese internal-medicine tradition.
