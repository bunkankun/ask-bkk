# Liǔ Bǎoyí yīlùn yīàn 柳寶詒醫論醫案
*Liǔ Bǎo-yí's Medical Discussions and Case Records*
by [[柳寶詒]] Liǔ Bǎoyí (*zì* Gǔsūn 谷孫, 1842–1901), late-Qīng physician of **Jiāngyīn** 江陰 (Jiāngsū), a key figure of the **Mènghé** 孟河 medical school of the late nineteenth century.

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* compilation of clinical-theoretical discussions (*yīlùn* 醫論) and case-records (*yīàn* 醫案) by Liǔ Bǎoyí, gathered posthumously from his clinical notes and published in the early-Republican era as part of the broader effort to preserve the Mèng-hé-school clinical-doctrinal corpus. The catalog meta supplies no personal author tag; the work's title and self-identification establish Liǔ as the author. The compilation falls in the standard Mèng-hé-school clinical-doctrinal genre: case-records prefaced by aetiological-doctrinal reasoning, with explicit cross-reference to the Yè Tiānshì 葉天士 / Wú Tāng 吳瑭 *Wēnbìng* canon and the Zhāng Zhòngjǐng *Shānghán* classical-formulary tradition. Liǔ's distinctive doctrinal contribution is the **integration of the *Wēnbìng* school's clinical methodology with a rigorous *jīngfāng* engagement** — an integration that defined the Mènghé school's late-Qīng synthesis and shaped Republican-era Chinese internal medicine.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` carries the standard front-matter prefaces of the work; the present digital exemplar's paratextual content varies by edition. Liǔ Bǎoyí's earlier preface to [[KR3eq006]] *Yèxuǎn yīhéng* (1894) is the principal datable self-statement of his doctrinal position.

### Abstract

Liǔ Bǎoyí 柳寶詒 (Gǔsūn, 1842–1901), Jiāng-yīn-region physician active in the late Qīng, is the founding figure of the **late-Qīng / Republican Mènghé branch** of the *Wēnbìng* school. The catalog meta lists him conventionally as Qīng; the composition window 1870–1907 reflects (a) his maturing clinical career from the early 1870s and (b) the posthumous compilation date of the case-record collection. His other principal works are the *Wēnrè féngyuán* 溫熱逢源 — an important late-Qīng systematic treatise on warm-disease — and his contributions to the **Shàoxīng Medical Society** publications under Hé Liánchén [[何廉臣]]. See person note [[柳寶詒]] for biographical detail.

Doctrinal significance: the work is one of the principal documents of the Mènghé school's late-Qīng synthesis between *Wēnbìng* aetiological-doctrinal reasoning and classical-formulary *jīngfāng* clinical method. The Mènghé school — centered on Chángzhōu 常州 / Jiāngyīn 江陰 and including [[費伯雄]] Fèi Bóxióng (1800–1879), [[馬培之]] Mǎ Péizhī (1820–1903), and the early-Republican [[丁甘仁]] Dīng Gānrén (1865–1926) — was the most influential single late-Qīng medical lineage and the principal source of the Republican-era Chinese internal-medicine tradition.
