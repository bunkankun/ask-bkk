# Yù-gāng-zhāi yī-xué bǐ-zhǔ 鬱岡齋醫學筆麈
*Medical "Talking-Whisk" from the Yù-Crag Studio*
by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěn-táng (*zì* Yǔ-tài 宇泰, 1549–1613), completed at the *Yù-gāng-zhāi* 鬱岡齋 in **Wàn-lì 30 (1602)**, with the modern editorial recension prepared by **[[秦伯未]] Qín Bó-wèi** (1901–1970, *zì* Zhī-jì 之濟) in 1926.

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* condensation by Qín Bó-wèi (the early-Republican-era Shàng-hǎi medical educator) of the medical-theoretical material in Wáng Kěn-táng's twelve-*juǎn* late-Míng *bǐ-jì* 筆麈 (talking-whisk-style miscellany) ***Yù-gāng-zhāi bǐ-zhǔ*** 鬱岡齋筆麈. The parent *bǐ-zhǔ* — covering literature, history, *yìjīng* 易經 studies, *yīn-yáng wǔ-xíng* cosmology, and medical questions in twelve *juǎn* — was preserved in the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 and is cited by the *Sìkù tíyào* editors as among the finest late-Míng *bǐ-jì* productions. Qín Bó-wèi's editorial intervention extracts the medical content (approximately three-tenths of the parent work) and presents it as a self-contained two-*juǎn* medical compendium with section-headings and apparatus suited to early-Republican Shàng-hǎi medical-school curriculum. The work is methodologically interesting as **Wáng Kěn-táng's pre-*Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* medical notes** — composed in 1602, two years before the 1604 completion of the *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng*'s [[KR3e0078]] cold-damage and surgical sections — and therefore preserves Wáng's medical thinking in its formative pre-encyclopaedic stage.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` opens with the preface by **Qín Bó-wèi** 秦伯未 of Shàng-hǎi (signed *Bǐng-yín bā-yuè shàng Shàng-hǎi Qín Zhī-jì Bó-wèi-fǔ jì* 丙寅八月上海秦之濟伯未甫記 = eighth month of *bǐng-yín* 丙寅 = 1926). Qín records the parent twelve-*juǎn* *bǐ-zhǔ*'s inclusion in the *Sìkù quánshū*, his discovery that approximately three-tenths of the parent work concerns medicine, his commissioning of his colleague Qián Jì-yín 錢季寅 to extract the medical sections for separate publication, the addition of *jiào-chóu* 校讎 (textual collation), small-section *xiǎo-mù* 小目 (sub-headings), and the rename to *Yī-xué bǐ-zhǔ* 醫學筆麈. Qín further notes that the parent *bǐ-zhǔ* was completed in *Wàn-lì sān-shí-nián rén-yín* 萬曆三十年壬寅 = 1602, while Wáng's *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* was completed in *Wàn-lì sān-shí-èr-nián jiǎ-chén* 萬曆三十二年甲辰 = 1604, so that the medical *bǐ-zhǔ* records "*[material] preserved separately as Wáng was compiling the Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng, with which it can be cross-referenced*".

### Abstract

The composition window 1602–1926 reflects the two-stage history: Wáng Kěn-táng's original 1602 composition of the parent *bǐ-zhǔ* (at age 53, mid-career), and Qín Bó-wèi's 1926 editorial extraction of the medical material into the present two-*juǎn* form. The work is the most useful surviving witness to **Wáng Kěn-táng's medical thinking in its pre-encyclopaedic form** — the doctrinal positions on the *xiāng-huǒ* / *mìng-mén* problem, the integration of *yìjīng* cosmology with clinical reasoning, and the engagement with the [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī yīn-supplementation tradition were all worked out in the 1602 *bǐ-zhǔ* before being systematised in the 1604 *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng*.

CBDB records Wáng Kěn-táng at 1549–1613. Qín Bó-wèi (1901–1970) is the early-Republican Shàng-hǎi medical educator best known for his role in the Shàng-hǎi *Zhōng-yī xué-yuàn* 中醫學院 founding and the *Zhōng-yī rù-mén* 中醫入門 textbook tradition.
