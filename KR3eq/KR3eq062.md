# Kè-chuāng ǒu-tán 客窗偶談
*Casual Conversations at the Guest-Window*
by [[沈明宗]] Shěn Míng-zōng (*zì* Mù-cún 目存, *hào* Qiū-chí 秋池, *fl.* late seventeenth / early eighteenth century, Kāng-xī period).

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* clinical-theoretical miscellany by Shěn Míng-zōng — the early-Qīng Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (浙江) physician of the **Lǐ-Zhū** 李朱 (Lǐ Dōng-yuán / Zhū Dān-xī) tradition who also produced the *Shānghán Líu-shū chán-jiě* 傷寒六書纏解, the *Jīnguì yào-lüè biān-zhù* 金匱要略編注 (1692), and the *Wēn-rè bìng piān* 溫熱病篇. The *Kè-chuāng ǒu-tán* is the **theoretical-doctrinal companion** to Shěn's classical-text commentary work — preserving his short doctrinal essays on the structure of Chinese medical theory. The work opens with a Confucian student-master dialogue on the inadequacy of the standard *shí-èr jīng-luò* / *wǔ-zàng-liù-fǔ* clinical schema and the necessity of integrating it with the *tiān-rén* 天人 *yùn-qì* 五運六氣 cosmological framework — establishing Shěn's signature integrative-doctrinal position.

### Abstract

Shěn Míng-zōng 沈明宗 (Mù-cún / Qiū-chí), early-Qīng Jiā-xīng physician, is one of the most theoretically sophisticated physicians of the post-Lǐ Shízhēn / pre-Wú Jū-tōng period — combining classical *Lǐ-Zhū* clinical doctrine with the *yùn-qì* cosmological framework and the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* classical-formulary tradition. His major works are the *Shānghán Liù-shū chán-jiě* (a commentary on the 'six books' of *Shānghán* commentary), the *Jīnguì yào-lüè biān-zhù* 金匱要略編注 (1692, a major early-Qīng commentary on the *Jīnguì*), and the present *Kè-chuāng ǒu-tán* miscellany. The composition window 1670–1710 reflects his documented productive period in the Kāng-xī era. The work entered Chinese circulation via the late-Qīng / Republican *Hǎi-wài huí-liú zhōng-yī gǔ-jí* (hxwd) repatriation programme.

Historiographical significance: Shěn Míng-zōng is one of the **early-Qīng theorist-physicians** between the Míng Lǐ-Zhū-school orthodoxy and the mature *Wēn-bìng*-school of the Qián-lóng — Jiā-qìng era. The *Kè-chuāng ǒu-tán*'s integrative doctrinal-cosmological position prefigures the **Mèng-hé-school** integrative method of the Dào-guāng — Xián-fēng period (cf. [[KR3eq049]]). Not in CBDB.
