# Huáyán jīng hǎi yìn dào chǎng jiǔ huì qǐng fó yí 華嚴經海印道場九會請佛儀
*Ritual for Inviting the Buddhas of the Nine Assemblies in the Ocean-Seal Bodhi-Maṇḍa of the Huáyán Scripture*
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text is a Buddhist ritual manual (*yí* 儀) for monastic *huáyán* services centred on the cosmological structure of the [[KR6e0010|*Avataṃsaka*]]'s seven-place / nine-assembly teaching framework. The ritual invites the cosmic Vairocana Buddha and the bodhisattvas of the nine assemblies into the *bodhi-maṇḍa* (道場) for devotional offering and contemplative practice. The *hǎi yìn* "ocean-seal" reference is to the [[KR6e0113|*hǎi yìn sānmèi* doctrine]].

### Abstract

The work is anonymous and undated; conventional scholarship places it broadly in the Tang-Sòng period of Chinese Buddhist liturgical literature. The bracket adopted here (800 – 1280) reflects this maximum window. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1469) collection in the *Huáyán* liturgical-ritual section.
