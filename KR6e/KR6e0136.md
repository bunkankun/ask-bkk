# Zhù tóng jiào wèn dá 註同教問答
*Annotated Question-and-Answer on the Shared-Teaching*
by [[師會]] (Shī-huì, 述) and [[善熹]] (Shàn-xī, 注)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text combines [[師會]] Shī-huì's question-and-answer treatment of the *tóng jiào* doctrine with [[善熹]] Shàn-xī's annotation. The work is part of the same Sòng Huáyán doctrinal-controversy literature on the *tóng jiào* / *bǐn jiào* distinction as [[KR6e0135|*Zhù huáyán tóng jiào yī chéng cè*]] (X1016).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1145 – 1180) reflects the period of [[師會]] Shī-huì and [[善熹]] Shàn-xī's master-disciple collaborative scholarship. The work belongs to the same Sòng Huáyán *tóng jiào* / *bǐn jiào* literature as the parent *cè* and reflects the ongoing scholastic culture around mature Huáyán doctrinal categories.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1017) collection.
