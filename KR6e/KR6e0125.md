# Huáyán jīng gāng mù guàn shè 華嚴經綱目貫攝
*Through-Threading and Outlining of the Huáyán Scripture*
by [[永光]] (Yǒng-guāng, 錄集 / recorded and compiled)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the late-Qīng outline-and-synopsis of the [[KR6e0010|80-fascicle *Avataṃsaka*]] by [[永光]] Yǒng-guāng of the *Suìníng Guǎngdé-sì* 遂寧廣德寺 in Sìchuān. Combines a *gāng mù* (structural outline) with *guàn shè* ("through-threading and embracing") synthesis, providing both navigational structure and doctrinal summary.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1900 – 1911) reflects the late-Qīng period of Yǒng-guāng's activity, with [[KR6e0126|the parallel *Sānshí jiǔ pǐn dà yì*]] dated to *Guāng-xù* 31 (1905). The work documents the continuing late-Qīng Chinese Buddhist scholarly engagement with the *Avataṃsaka* and the *gāng mù* + *guàn shè* genre's continuing vitality even at the very end of the imperial Buddhist publishing tradition.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X241) collection.
