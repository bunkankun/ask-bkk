# Zhù huáyán tóng jiào yī chéng cè 註華嚴同教一乘策
*Annotated Treatise on the Shared-Teaching One-Vehicle of the Huáyán*
by [[師會]] (Shīhuì, 述) and [[希迪]] (Xīdí, 註)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text combines [[師會]] Shīhuì's treatise on the *tóng jiào yī chéng* 同教一乘 ("shared-teaching One-Vehicle") doctrine — a key Huáyán-school doctrinal position concerning the relation of the *Avataṃsaka*'s One-Vehicle teaching to the Three-Vehicle teachings of the other Mahāyāna scriptures — with [[希迪]] Xīdí's annotation. Per DILA A000487, Xīdí's annotation is dated *Jiātài* 嘉泰 2 (1202).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The composite work covers the period from Shīhuì's mature activity (c. 1145 – 1170) through Xīdí's annotation (1202). The bracket adopted here (1145 – 1218) reflects this maximum window. The doctrinal substance is one of the most important controversies in mature Sòng Huáyán-school doctrinal study: the *bǐn jiào* 別教 ("separate-teaching One-Vehicle") versus *tóng jiào* 同教 ("shared-teaching One-Vehicle") distinction that Fǎzàng had introduced in [[KR6e0074|the *Wǔ jiào zhāng*]].

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1016) collection.
