# Wǔ xiàng zhì shí sòng 五相智識頌
*Verse on the Five Characteristics of Wisdom-Consciousness*
by anonymous

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Sòng-period text is a verse-treatise (*sòng* 頌) on the doctrine of the *wǔ xiàng zhì shí* — "Five Characteristics of Wisdom-Consciousness," a Yogācāra-Mahāyāna doctrinal apparatus integrated here into a Huáyán-school doctrinal framework. The 5-fold scheme corresponds to the standard Yogācāra schema of consciousness types but is here cast in *Avataṃsaka*-tradition idiom.

### Abstract

The work is anonymous and undated; conventional scholarship places it in the broad Southern-Sòng period (the bracket 1100 – 1280 CE adopted here). The work is one of the lesser items in the Sòng Huáyán-school polemical/doctrinal cycle preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng*.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1022) collection.
