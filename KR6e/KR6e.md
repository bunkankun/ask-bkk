# KR6e 華嚴部類 — Huáyán Corpus

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR6e gathers the Chinese-language literature of the *Huáyánjīng* 華嚴經 / *Buddhāvataṃsaka-sūtra* tradition: the three full Chinese translations of the sūtra, the early Hàn-through-Jìn partial translations and parallel texts (兜沙經, 菩薩本業經, 漸備一切智德經 etc.), the *Daśabhūmika* and *Gaṇḍavyūha* clusters that circulated independently, the foundational śāstras of Nāgārjuna 龍樹 and Vasubandhu 天親, the writings of the Táng "five patriarchs" (Dùshùn 杜順, Zhìyǎn 智儼, Fǎzàng 法藏, Chéngguān 澄觀, Zōngmì 宗密) and of the parallel layman-exegete Lǐ Tōngxuán 李通玄, the Sillan Huāǒm 華嚴 contributions of Wǒnhyo 元曉 and Ǔisang 義湘, the Northern-Sòng "restoration" centred on Jìngyuán 淨源 and the Liáo-Sòng *Wǔjiào zhāng* subcommentarial tradition, and the Yuán-Míng-Qīng synthetic and ritual works that close the corpus. The division spans roughly seventeen centuries, from Lokakṣema's 支婁迦讖 *Dōushā jīng* in the Later Hàn (c. 178–189) to Péng Jìqīng's 彭際清 *Huáyán niànfó sānmèi lùn* (c. 1770–1796).

In Taishō organisation, the heart of the corpus is the *Huáyánbù* 華嚴部, running from T277 to T309. KR6e is broader than that division: it pulls in the *Daśabhūmika-vibhāṣā* 十住毘婆沙論 and the *Daśabhūmika-sūtra-śāstra* 十地經論 from the Taishō treatise sections (T1521, T1522), Fǎzàng's and Chéngguān's exegetical works from the *Jīngshūbù* 經疏部 (T1731–T1740), the patriarchal essays from the *Zhūzōngbù* 諸宗部 (T1866–T1899), and Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng material from the *Xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏經. The Kanripo grouping thus reflects sectarian rather than strictly bibliographic logic: it is the textual archive of the Huáyán *school* (Huáyánzōng 華嚴宗, also known as Xiánshǒuzōng 賢首宗 after Fǎzàng's posthumous title) read as a continuous tradition.

## Important texts and text clusters

- **The three full translations of the *Huáyánjīng***.
  - *Liùshí Huáyán* 六十華嚴, [[KR6e0001]] — Buddhabhadra 佛馱跋陀羅's sixty-fascicle rendering completed at Yángzhōu in 418–420; the base text for Dùshùn, Zhìyǎn, Wǒnhyo and the early Huáyán synthesis.
  - *Bāshí Huáyán* 八十華嚴, [[KR6e0010]] — Śikṣānanda 實叉難陀's eighty-fascicle "new translation" of 695–699, sponsored by Wǔ Zétiān 武則天; supersedes the Liùshí Huáyán in Táng and later exegesis and is the version Fǎzàng (after revision), Chéngguān, and Lǐ Tōngxuán comment on.
  - *Sìshí Huáyán* 四十華嚴, [[KR6e0041]] — Prajñā 般若's forty-fascicle translation of 796–798, which renders only the *Gaṇḍavyūha* 入法界品 but adds the *Bhadracarī-praṇidhāna* / Pǔxián xíngyuàn 普賢行願 hymn that became the principal Huáyán liturgical text.
- **Early partial and parallel translations**. [[KR6e0028]] 兜沙經 (Lokakṣema, c. 178–189) is the oldest extant Chinese fragment of the corpus; [[KR6e0029]] 菩薩本業經 (Zhī Qiān 支謙, 222–253), [[KR6e0030]] 諸菩薩求佛本業經 (Niè Dàozhēn 聶道真), and [[KR6e0031]] 菩薩十住行道品 / [[KR6e0032]] 佛說菩薩十住經 form the Hàn-through-Jìn pre-history of the *Huáyán*'s reception in China.
- **The *Daśabhūmika* / 十地 cluster**. [[KR6e0033]] 漸備一切智德經 (Zhú Fǎhù 竺法護, 297), [[KR6e0034]] 十住經 (Kumārajīva 鳩摩羅什, c. 405), and [[KR6e0035]] 佛說十地經 (Śīladharma 尸羅達摩, 789–798) translate the same sūtra at three points; together with [[KR6e0059]] 十住毘婆沙論 (Nāgārjuna 龍樹 / Kumārajīva tr.) and [[KR6e0060]] 十地經論 (Vasubandhu / Bodhiruci 菩提流支, 508–511), they constitute the Indian doctrinal core that anchored the Northern Wèi Dìlùn 地論 school and through it the later Huáyán synthesis.
- **The *Gaṇḍavyūha* / 入法界品 cluster**. [[KR6e0043]] 羅摩伽經 (Shèngjiān 聖堅), [[KR6e0044]] 大方廣佛華嚴經入法界品 (Divākara 地婆訶羅), and the *Sìshí Huáyán* [[KR6e0041]] all transmit Sudhana's 善財 pilgrimage; this cluster underlies [[KR6e0115]] 文殊指南圖讚 (Wéibái 惟白) and the standard iconographic programme of the fifty-three teachers.
- **Tathāgata-realm sūtras** (Wǔ-Zhōu-era expansions of the corpus). [[KR6e0049]] 不思議佛境界分, [[KR6e0050]] 大方廣如來不思議境界經, [[KR6e0052]] 佛華嚴入如來德智不思議境界經, and [[KR6e0053]] 大方廣入如來智德不思議經 round out the Avataṃsaka literary horizon as it was received in early Táng.
- **The Táng patriarchal corpus**. Zhìyǎn's *Sōuxuán jì* 搜玄記 [[KR6e0003]] and *Wǔshí yào wèndá* [[KR6e0083]]; Fǎzàng's *Tànxuán jì* 探玄記 [[KR6e0004]], *Wénjì gāngmù* [[KR6e0005]], the *Wǔjiào zhāng* 五教章 (Huáyán yīchéng jiàoyì fēnqí zhāng) [[KR6e0074]], the *Zhǐguī* [[KR6e0085]], *Yìhǎi bǎimén* [[KR6e0089]], *Wàngjìn huányuán guān* [[KR6e0090]] and the *Jīnshīzǐ zhāng* 金獅子章 commentarial tradition [[KR6e0097]]–[[KR6e0098]]; Chéngguān's massive *Dàshū* [[KR6e0011]] and *Yǎnyìchāo* [[KR6e0012]] together with the *Fǎjiè xuánjìng* [[KR6e0100]] and *Sānshèng yuánróng guānmén* [[KR6e0099]]; and Zōngmì's *Zhù Fǎjiè guānmén* [[KR6e0101]] and *Yuánrén lùn* 原人論 [[KR6e0105]], the latter widely read as the school's most concise doctrinal statement.
- **Lǐ Tōngxuán's parallel tradition**. The *Xīn Huáyán jīng lùn* 新華嚴經論 [[KR6e0022]], the *Juéyí lùn* [[KR6e0024]] and *Shímíng lùn* [[KR6e0112]], later edited together with the sūtra as the *Huáyánjīng hélùn* 華嚴經合論 [[KR6e0066]], offered an alternative reading of the *Bāshí Huáyán* outside the patriarchal lineage and exerted strong influence on Sòng Chán reception.
- **Sillan Huāǒm**. Wǒnhyo 元曉's surviving *Huáyán jīng shū* fascicle [[KR6e0009]], Ǔisang 義湘's *Yīchéng fǎjiè tú* 一乘法界圖 [[KR6e0109]] with its later anthology [[KR6e0110]], Pyowǒn 表員's *Wényì yàojué wèndá* [[KR6e0121]], Gyǒndǔng 見登之's *Yīchéng chéngfó miàoyì* [[KR6e0114]] and Myǒnghyo 明皛's *Hǎiyìn sānmèi lùn* [[KR6e0113]] are the principal Korean contributions retained in the Chinese canon.
- **The *Wǔjiào zhāng* subcommentarial tradition**. Dàotíng 道亭's *Yìyuàn shū* [[KR6e0075]], Shīhuì 師會's *Fénxīn* [[KR6e0076]] and *Fùgǔ jì* [[KR6e0078]], and Xīdí 希迪's *Jíchéng jì* [[KR6e0079]] and *Píngfùgǔjì* [[KR6e0080]] form the Sòng debate over Fǎzàng's foundational five-teachings classification.
- **Sòng-Liáo-Yuán exegesis**. Jìngyuán 淨源's *Huányuánguān kē* [[KR6e0091]] and related ritual reconstructions of the school in the Northern Sòng; the Liáo monk Xiānyǎn 鮮演's *Tánxuán juézé* [[KR6e0119]]; the Yuán *Xuántán huìxuán jì* [[KR6e0120]] of Pǔruì 普瑞; Jièhuán 戒環's *Yàojiě* [[KR6e0122]] and Dàotōng 道通's *Tūnhǎi jí* [[KR6e0123]].
- **Late synthesis (Míng-Qīng)**. Chéngguān's *Gāngyào* (with Míng reorganisation) [[KR6e0124]], Xùfǎ 續法's *Xiánshǒu wǔjiào yí* [[KR6e0142]] and *Xiánshǒu wǔjiào yí kāiméng* [[KR6e0143]] codifying the Qīng-dynasty school curriculum, and Péng Jìqīng 彭際清's lay-Buddhist *Yīchéng juéyí lùn* [[KR6e0147]] and *Huáyán niànfó sānmèi lùn* [[KR6e0148]] integrating Huáyán with Pure-Land practice.
- **Ritual and contemplative material**. The *Fǎjiè guānmén* 法界觀門 attributed to Dùshùn (preserved only via [[KR6e0100]] and [[KR6e0101]]), [[KR6e0081]] 華嚴五教止觀, [[KR6e0082]] 華嚴一乘十玄門 (whose authenticity as Dùshùn's own work is contested), and the great repentance and offering manuals — [[KR6e0149]] 海印道場九會請佛儀, [[KR6e0150]] 海印道場懺儀, [[KR6e0152]]–[[KR6e0153]] 普賢行願修證儀, and Zōngmì's [[KR6e0155]] 圓覺經道場修證儀 — establish Huáyán as a practice-tradition, not only an exegetical one.

## Important persons

- [[佛馱跋陀羅]] (Buddhabhadra, 359–429) — translator of the *Liùshí Huáyán* [[KR6e0001]] and of the early *Wénshū shīlì fāyuàn jīng* [[KR6e0045]]; the figure through whom the *Avataṃsaka* first entered Chinese in its full form.
- [[實叉難陀]] (Śikṣānanda, 652–710) — Khotanese monk recruited by Wǔ Zétiān to retranslate the sūtra; produced the *Bāshí Huáyán* [[KR6e0010]] and the parallel inconceivable-realm sūtras [[KR6e0050]], [[KR6e0053]], [[KR6e0047]].
- [[般若]] (Prajñā, 744–810) — Kashmiri translator of the *Sìshí Huáyán* [[KR6e0041]] at the request of Emperor Dézōng 德宗; brought the *Bhadracarī-praṇidhāna* into the corpus.
- [[杜順]] (Dùshùn, 557–640) — traditional first patriarch; lifetime work survives only in attributions ([[KR6e0081]], [[KR6e0082]], [[KR6e0160]]) and through Chéngguān and Zōngmì's transmission of the *Fǎjiè guānmén* in [[KR6e0100]]–[[KR6e0101]].
- [[智儼]] (Zhìyǎn, 602–668) — second patriarch; in the *Sōuxuán jì* [[KR6e0003]] and the *Kǒngmù zhāng* [[KR6e0084]] he organised the *Liùshí Huáyán* into the analytical framework Fǎzàng would inherit.
- [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng, 643–712) — third patriarch and the systematiser of the school; sixteen distinct works in the division, anchoring the doctrinal core in the *Wǔjiào zhāng* [[KR6e0074]] and the meditative-metaphysical core in the *Wàngjìn huányuán guān* [[KR6e0090]] and *Yìhǎi bǎimén* [[KR6e0089]].
- [[澄觀]] (Chéngguān, 738–839) — fourth patriarch; author of the largest single body of writing in the division (seventeen works), centred on the *Dàshū* and *Yǎnyìchāo* commentaries on the *Bāshí Huáyán* [[KR6e0011]]–[[KR6e0012]]. The exegetical horizon of every later author rests on his work.
- [[宗密]] (Zōngmì, 780–841) — fifth patriarch and the bridge to Chán; his *Yuánrén lùn* [[KR6e0105]] is the most widely read short Huáyán text outside the school proper.
- [[李通玄]] (Lǐ Tōngxuán, 645–740) — Táng layman; his *Xīn Huáyán jīng lùn* [[KR6e0022]] and successor works developed a reading of the *Bāshí Huáyán* organised around the Wǔtáishān cosmology and the Sudhana pilgrimage, parallel and partly counter to the patriarchal tradition.
- [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 617–686) — Sillan exegete; his *Huáyán jīng shū* [[KR6e0009]] survives only in fragments but is the earliest extant Korean Huáyán commentary.
- [[義湘]] (Ǔisang, 625–702) — Sillan founder of Huāǒm; the *Yīchéng fǎjiè tú* [[KR6e0109]] is one of the school's iconic short doctrinal works.
- [[慧苑]] (Huìyuàn, fl. early 8th c.) — Fǎzàng's disciple; the *Xù-Huáyán-jīng lüèshū kāndìng jì* [[KR6e0065]] reorganised his master's commentarial scheme on the new translation and was sharply contested in later transmission.
- [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 1011–1088) — the Northern-Sòng "restorer of the Huáyán teaching" (中興教主); a cluster of his works ([[KR6e0091]], [[KR6e0092]], [[KR6e0152]], [[KR6e0153]], [[KR6e0156]], [[KR6e0157]]) reassembled the school's curriculum after the loss of major Táng texts.
- [[師會]] (Shīhuì, fl. mid-12th c.) — central voice in the Sòng *Wǔjiào zhāng* debate; [[KR6e0076]]–[[KR6e0078]] defend Fǎzàng's text against Huìyuàn's emendations and frame the later orthodox reading.
- [[續法]] (Xùfǎ, 1641–1728) — Qīng compiler and self-styled twenty-eighth Huáyán patriarch; his *Xiánshǒu wǔjiào yí* [[KR6e0142]] became the standard pedagogical entry into the school.
- [[彭際清]] (Péng Jìqīng / Péng Shàoshēng 彭紹昇, 1740–1796) — Qing lay scholar; his integration of Huáyán with Pure-Land practice in [[KR6e0148]] marks the final major intellectual moment of the corpus.

## Topics

- **The classification of teachings (*pànjiào* 判教)**. The school's signature project is the ranking of all Buddhist teachings into the *Wǔjiào* 五教 (Hīnayāna 小乘 / Elementary Mahāyāna 大乘始 / Final Mahāyāna 大乘終 / Sudden 頓 / Perfect 圓) and *Shízōng* 十宗 schemes laid out in Fǎzàng's *Wǔjiào zhāng* [[KR6e0074]]. The contested Sòng-era reception of that text ([[KR6e0075]]–[[KR6e0080]]) and Xùfǎ's Qīng codification [[KR6e0142]] map the boundaries of school orthodoxy across a millennium.
- **The Dharma-realm (*fǎjiè* 法界) and its four divisions**. Dùshùn's *Fǎjiè guānmén* — surviving only in Chéngguān's *Xuánjìng* [[KR6e0100]] and Zōngmì's *Zhù* [[KR6e0101]] — articulates the canonical fourfold scheme (*shìfǎjiè* 事法界 / *lǐfǎjiè* 理法界 / *lǐshì wúài fǎjiè* 理事無礙法界 / *shìshì wúài fǎjiè* 事事無礙法界). This is the doctrinal kernel from which all later Huáyán metaphysics is unfolded.
- **The Ten Mysterious Gates (*shíxuánmén* 十玄門) and the Six Marks (*liùxiàng* 六相)**. The cosmological apparatus that converts *fǎjiè* doctrine into the school's signature mereology of mutual interpenetration; older redaction in [[KR6e0082]], systematised by Zhìyǎn ([[KR6e0083]], [[KR6e0084]]) and given its definitive form by Fǎzàng (notably in [[KR6e0074]] and [[KR6e0089]]).
- **The *Daśabhūmika* and the legacy of the Dìlùn school**. Vasubandhu's *Shídì jīng lùn* [[KR6e0060]], translated in the Northern Wèi, was the doctrinal core of the Dìlùn 地論 school and a direct ancestor of Huáyán; the school's debates over *ālayavijñāna* and the relation of the *tathāgatagarbha* to the bodhisattva path enter Huáyán through Zhìyǎn's reading of [[KR6e0060]] together with [[KR6e0001]].
- **Patriarchal lineage versus layman exegesis**. The five-patriarch line (Dùshùn → Zhìyǎn → Fǎzàng → Chéngguān → Zōngmì) is itself a Sòng-era retrospective construction; the parallel and historically prior reading of Lǐ Tōngxuán ([[KR6e0022]], [[KR6e0066]], [[KR6e0112]]) provides a check on its self-presentation and exerted independent influence on Chán.
- **Cross-cultural transmission**. The Sillan contributions ([[KR6e0009]], [[KR6e0109]], [[KR6e0110]], [[KR6e0113]], [[KR6e0114]], [[KR6e0121]]) — produced under and after the Korean monks who studied with Zhìyǎn and Fǎzàng — and the Khotanese, Kashmiri, and Sogdian translators ([[KR6e0001]], [[KR6e0010]], [[KR6e0041]], [[KR6e0044]], [[KR6e0046]]) make the *Huáyán* the most internationally produced corpus in the Chinese canon outside the Vinaya.
- **Huáyán as Chán and as Pure Land**. Zōngmì's *Yuánrén lùn* [[KR6e0105]] and *Zhù Fǎjiè guānmén* [[KR6e0101]] frame Huáyán doctrine for a Chán readership; Péng Jìqīng's *Niànfó sānmèi lùn* [[KR6e0148]] reframes it for a Pure-Land one. These two ends of the doctrinal afterlife are essential to reading Sòng-Qīng intellectual history.
- **The *Pǔxián xíngyuàn* 普賢行願 cult**. The hymn appended to the *Sìshí Huáyán* [[KR6e0041]] generated its own commentarial micro-corpus ([[KR6e0069]], [[KR6e0070]], [[KR6e0071]], [[KR6e0072]], [[KR6e0073]]) and the ritual structures of [[KR6e0149]]–[[KR6e0153]]; the *Bhadracarī-praṇidhāna* is the daily liturgical text of East Asian Mahāyāna and originates here.
- **Pseudepigrapha and apocrypha**. Several works ascribed to Dùshùn (notably [[KR6e0082]]) are now thought to be Zhìyǎn's or later; the *Xíngyuàn pǐn biéxíng shūchāo* [[KR6e0073]] survives only in selection. Authenticity is a live problem across the early-Táng layer.

## Timeline

- **c. 178–189** — *Fó shuō Dōushā jīng* 佛說兜沙經 [[KR6e0028]] — Lokakṣema 支婁迦讖's earliest extant fragment of the *Avataṃsaka* horizon.
- **222–253** — *Fó shuō Pútísà běnyè jīng* 佛說菩薩本業經 [[KR6e0029]] — Zhī Qiān 支謙's Wú-era partial translation.
- **late 3rd c.** — *Zhū pútísà qiú fó běnyè jīng* 諸菩薩求佛本業經 [[KR6e0030]], *Pútísà shízhù xíngdào pǐn* [[KR6e0031]] (Zhú Fǎhù) — Western Jìn parallel translations.
- **297** — *Jiànbèi yīqiè zhìdé jīng* 漸備一切智德經 [[KR6e0033]] — Zhú Fǎhù's *Daśabhūmika* translation; the first independent rendering of what will become the *Shízhù pǐn*.
- **c. 290–313** — *Děngmù pútísà suǒwèn sānmèi jīng* [[KR6e0036]], *Fó shuō Rúlái xìngxiǎn jīng* [[KR6e0039]], *Dùshì pǐn jīng* [[KR6e0040]] (Zhú Fǎhù) — further Western Jìn pre-Buddhabhadra material.
- **c. 317–420** — *Fó shuō pútísà shízhù jīng* [[KR6e0032]] — Qíduōmì 祇多蜜 (Eastern Jìn) re-translation of the *Bodhisattva-daśabhūmika*.
- **402–409** — *Shízhù jīng* 十住經 [[KR6e0034]] — Kumārajīva's *Daśabhūmika* translation at Cháng'ān.
- **402–412** — *Shízhù pípóshā lùn* 十住毘婆沙論 [[KR6e0059]] — Nāgārjuna 龍樹's śāstra translated by Kumārajīva; foundational for Pure-Land theory through the "easy practice" chapter.
- **418–420** — *Liùshí Huáyán* 大方廣佛華嚴經 [[KR6e0001]] — Buddhabhadra 佛馱跋陀羅's sixty-fascicle translation; the canonical received text of the sūtra until the late Táng.
- **c. 420s** — *Wénshū shīlì fāyuàn jīng* [[KR6e0045]] (Buddhabhadra) — the earliest version of the *Praṇidhāna* hymn in Chinese.
- **c. 420** — *Luómóqié jīng* 羅摩伽經 [[KR6e0043]] — Shèngjiān 聖堅's Xī-Qín *Gaṇḍavyūha* fragment.
- **508–511** — *Shídì jīng lùn* 十地經論 [[KR6e0060]] — Bodhiruci 菩提流支's translation of Vasubandhu's *Daśabhūmika-śāstra*; founding text of the Northern Dìlùn school.
- **c. 510–540** — *Huáyán jīng yìjì* [[KR6e0008]] (Huìguāng 慧光); *Huáyán jīng lùn* [[KR6e0064]] (Língbiàn 靈辨, c. 510) — Northern Wèi commentaries that prepare the Suí-Táng synthesis.
- **c. 580–640** — *Huáyán wǔjiào zhǐguān* [[KR6e0081]], *Huáyán yīchéng shíxuán mén* [[KR6e0082]], *Xuánfù jì* [[KR6e0160]] — works ascribed to Dùshùn 杜順, the traditional first patriarch.
- **581–618** — *Huáyán yóuyì* [[KR6e0002]] (Jízàng 吉藏) — Suí-era exegesis from outside the nascent Huáyán lineage.
- **c. 590–610** — *Shídì lùn yìjì* [[KR6e0063]] (Huìyuǎn 慧遠) — Suí *Daśabhūmika*-śāstra commentary, the last major Dìlùn-school work before the Huáyán turn.
- **627–668** — *Sōuxuán jì* [[KR6e0003]], *Wǔshí yào wèndá* [[KR6e0083]], *Kǒngmù zhāng* [[KR6e0084]] — Zhìyǎn 智儼's commentarial corpus.
- **660–686** — *Huáyán jīng shū* fragment [[KR6e0009]] (Wǒnhyo 元曉, Silla).
- **668–670** — *Huáyán yīchéng fǎjiè tú* [[KR6e0109]] (Ǔisang 義湘, Silla).
- **670–712** — Fǎzàng 法藏's corpus: *Tànxuán jì* [[KR6e0004]], *Wǔjiào zhāng* [[KR6e0074]], *Zhǐguī* [[KR6e0085]], *Cèlín* [[KR6e0086]], *Yìhǎi bǎimén* [[KR6e0089]], *Wàngjìn huányuán guān* [[KR6e0090]], *Yóuxīn fǎjiè jì* [[KR6e0093]], *Fā pútíxīn zhāng* [[KR6e0094]], *Guānmài yìjì* [[KR6e0095]], etc.
- **680–687** — *Rù fǎjiè pǐn* 入法界品 [[KR6e0044]] (Divākara 地婆訶羅).
- **691–c. 720** — *Xìnlì rùyìn fǎmén jīng* [[KR6e0054]] (Dharmaruci 曇摩流支); *Dàfāngguǎng zǒngchí bǎoguāngmíng jīng* [[KR6e0048]] (Fǎtiān 法天, Northern Sòng but appended here).
- **695–699** — *Bāshí Huáyán* 大方廣佛華嚴經 [[KR6e0010]] — Śikṣānanda 實叉難陀's eighty-fascicle "new translation"; the canonical received text from this point on.
- **695–710** — *Rúlái bùsīyì jìngjiè* sūtra group [[KR6e0049]]–[[KR6e0053]] (Devaprajñā 提雲般若, Śikṣānanda).
- **c. 700–730** — *Xīn Huáyán jīng lùn* [[KR6e0022]] and successor works [[KR6e0023]]–[[KR6e0024]], [[KR6e0112]] of Lǐ Tōngxuán 李通玄.
- **c. 710–720** — *Xù Huáyán jīng lüèshū kāndìng jì* [[KR6e0065]] (Huìyuàn 慧苑); the disputed continuation of Fǎzàng.
- **784–838** — Chéngguān 澄觀's exegetical programme: *Dàshū* [[KR6e0011]] (784–787), *Yǎnyìchāo* [[KR6e0012]] (787–791), *Lüècè* [[KR6e0015]], *Xuántán* [[KR6e0019]], *Xíngyuàn pǐn shū* [[KR6e0069]], *Fǎjiè xuánjìng* [[KR6e0100]] (787–838), *Sānshèng yuánróng guānmén* [[KR6e0099]].
- **789–798** — *Fó shuō Shídì jīng* [[KR6e0035]] (Śīladharma 尸羅達摩).
- **796–798** — *Sìshí Huáyán* 大方廣佛華嚴經 [[KR6e0041]] — Prajñā 般若's *Gaṇḍavyūha*-only translation, adding the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn*.
- **c. 800–810** — *Pǔxián pútísà xíngyuàn zàn* [[KR6e0046]] (Amoghavajra 不空) — the Esoteric-school rendering of the *Praṇidhāna*.
- **820–841** — Zōngmì 宗密's writings: *Zhù Fǎjiè guānmén* [[KR6e0101]], *Yuánrén lùn* [[KR6e0105]], *Xíngyuàn pǐn shūkē* [[KR6e0070]], *Yuánjué jīng dàochǎng xiūzhèng yí* [[KR6e0155]].
- **c. 920–940** — *Shì Huáyán xuánfù jì* [[KR6e0161]] (Wéijìng 惟勁, Hòu-Táng) — annotated edition of Dùshùn's *Xuánfù jì*.
- **9th–10th c.** — *Huáyán mìyì jì* [[KR6e0162]] (attrib. Fǎzàng but ninth-century or later); *Yīchéng fǎjiè tú héshī yīyìn* [[KR6e0111]] (Yánfǎshī 儼法師) — Sillan-derived diagrammatic literature.
- **1011–1088** — Jìngyuán 淨源's corpus reassembling the school: [[KR6e0091]], [[KR6e0092]], [[KR6e0106]], [[KR6e0152]], [[KR6e0153]], [[KR6e0156]], [[KR6e0157]].
- **1080–1118** — *Tánxuán juézé* [[KR6e0119]] (Xiānyǎn 鮮演, Liáo) — the principal Khitan contribution to Huáyán exegesis.
- **1086–c. 1090** — *Yuánzōng wénlèi* [[KR6e0134]] (Ǔichǒn 義天) — Goryeo prince-monk's anthology of school documents.
- **1100–1127** — *Huáyánjīng hélùn* 華嚴經合論 [[KR6e0066]] — Northern-Sòng combined edition of the *Bāshí Huáyán* with Lǐ Tōngxuán's *Lùn*, edited by Zhìníng 志寧 and others.
- **c. 1120–1170** — *Wǔjiào zhāng* subcommentarial debate: Dàotíng 道亭 [[KR6e0075]]; Shīhuì 師會 [[KR6e0076]], [[KR6e0077]], [[KR6e0078]]; Xīdí 希迪 [[KR6e0079]], [[KR6e0080]].
- **c. 1130–1200** — *Huáyán jīng yàojiě* [[KR6e0122]] (Jièhuán 戒環); *Huáyán jīng tūnhǎi jí* [[KR6e0123]] (Dàotōng 道通); *Wénshū zhǐnán túzàn* [[KR6e0115]] (Wéibái 惟白).
- **1200–1250** — *Píng Jīngāng pī* [[KR6e0137]], *Biànfēi jí* [[KR6e0138]], *Chìmiù* [[KR6e0139]] (Shànxī 善熹) — Sòng polemics.
- **1300–1368** — *Xuántán huìxuán jì* [[KR6e0120]] (Pǔruì 普瑞, Yuán); *Huáyán yuánrén lùn jiě* [[KR6e0107]], *Huáyán yuánrén lùn héjiě* [[KR6e0108]] (Yuánjué 圓覺, Yuán).
- **c. 1500–1644** — *Huáyán dàyì* [[KR6e0141]] (Shànjiān 善堅, Míng); *Huáyánjīng hélùn jiǎnyào* [[KR6e0067]] (Lǐ Zhì-jiǎn 李贄簡, Míng); *Huáyánjīng hélùn zuǎnyào* [[KR6e0068]] (Fāng Zé 方澤, Míng); *Huáyán gāngyào* [[KR6e0124]] (final Míng editorial form, base text Chéngguān's).
- **1660–1715** — *Xiánshǒu wǔjiào yí* [[KR6e0142]] and the rest of Xùfǎ 續法's Qīng codification: [[KR6e0143]], [[KR6e0144]], [[KR6e0145]], [[KR6e0146]].
- **1740–1796** — *Yīchéng juéyí lùn* [[KR6e0147]] and *Huáyán niànfó sānmèi lùn* [[KR6e0148]] of Péng Jìqīng 彭際清; *Huáyánjīng gāngmù guànshè* [[KR6e0125]] and *Huáyánjīng sānshíjiǔ pǐn dàyì* [[KR6e0126]] of Yǒngguāng 永光 — the late-Qīng terminus of the corpus.
