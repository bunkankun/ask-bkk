# Wǔ yùn guān 五蘊觀
*Contemplation of the Five Aggregates*
by [[澄觀]] (Chéngguān, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's brief contemplative-doctrinal treatment of the *wǔ yùn* 五蘊 (the *Five Aggregates*: form, sensation, perception, mental formations, consciousness) — the foundational Buddhist doctrine of the *pañca-skandha* — as it is configured for *Avataṃsaka*-style contemplative practice. The doctrine is foundational to all Buddhist traditions, and Chéngguān's treatment integrates it into the mature Huáyán-school contemplative apparatus.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's mature period, c. 787 – 838 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1004) collection.
