# Xián-shǒu wǔ jiào duàn zhèng sān jué jiǎn làn tú 賢首五教斷證三覺揀濫圖
*Diagram for Selecting the Genuine from the Confused: The Cutting-Off-and-Verifying Three Awakenings of Xián-shǒu's Five Teachings*
by [[續法]] (Xù-fǎ, 集)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[續法]] Xù-fǎ is a diagrammatic-tabular exposition of the relations between Fǎzàng's *Five Teachings* doxography and the doctrines of *duàn* 斷 (cutting-off of defilements), *zhèng* 證 (verifying/realising), and *sān jué* 三覺 (the three awakenings). The *jiǎn làn tú* "diagram for selecting from the confused" format is intended to provide systematic clarity through visual-tabular presentation of doctrinal relations.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1660 – 1715) reflects [[續法]] Xù-fǎ's mature scholarly activity. The work is part of his late-imperial Huáyán-school doctrinal-publishing project. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1026) collection.
