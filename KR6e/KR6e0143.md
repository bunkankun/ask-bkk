# Xián-shǒu wǔ jiào yí kāi méng 賢首五教儀開蒙
*Introductory Manual to the Doctrinal Procedures of Xián-shǒu's Five Teachings*
by [[續法]] (Xù-fǎ, 集)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the introductory companion to [[續法]] Xù-fǎ's [[KR6e0142|*Wǔ jiào yí* 五教儀]] (X1024) — the *kāi méng* "introductory manual" providing accessible entry to the larger 6-fascicle parent work. The pair (X1024 + X1025) constitutes Xù-fǎ's primary textual apparatus for late-imperial Huáyán doctrinal teaching.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1660 – 1715) reflects [[續法]] Xù-fǎ's mature scholarly activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1025) collection.
