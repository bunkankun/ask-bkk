# Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng kē 華嚴一乘教義分齊章科
*Sectional Outline of the Treatise on the Doctrinal Categories of the One-Vehicle Teaching of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[師會]] (Shīhuì, 述)

## About the work

This one-fascicle text is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) by [[師會]] Shīhuì of [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's [[KR6e0074|*Wǔ jiào zhāng*]] (T1866). It serves as the navigational map for Shīhuì's own [[KR6e0078|*Fù gǔ jì*]] (X998) and, by extension, for the parent text of Fǎzàng. The hierarchical heading-format makes the conceptual structure of Fǎzàng's doxographical schema accessible at a glance.

### Prefaces

*The work opens with the title-line "華嚴一乘教義分齊章科 / 玉峯沙門　師會　述" — "Sectional outline of the *Wǔ jiào zhāng*. Composed by the *śramaṇa* Shī-huì of Yùfēng."*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1145 – 1170) reflects [[師會]] Shīhuì's mature scholarly period at the Yùfēng monastery. As with all *kē* outlines, the work's primary function is pedagogical and navigational: it provides the structural skeleton of the larger treatise, enabling students to track its doctrinal articulation. Shīhuì's particular *kē* of the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* preserves his own reading of the work's structure — sometimes differing in minor structural divisions from Dàotíng's earlier reading.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X997) collection.

## Other points of interest

- The *kē* outline format is one of the principal scholastic tools of Sòng Huáyán study; Shīhuì's outline of the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* was used as the standard navigation aid in subsequent SòngYuánMíng monastic study of Fǎzàng's treatise.
