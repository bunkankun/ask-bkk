# Wǔ jiào zhāng jí chéng jì 五教章集成記
*Compilation Notes on the Treatise on the Five Teachings*
by [[希迪]] (Xīdí, 述)

## About the work

This one-fascicle commentary on [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's [[KR6e0074|*Wǔ jiào zhāng*]] (T1866) is by [[希迪]] Xīdí, the Southern Sòng Huìyīnsì 慧因寺 abbot. The *jí chéng jì* ("compilation notes") format is more synthetic and integrative than [[師會]] Shīhuì's polemical *Fù gǔ jì*: rather than refuting [[道亭]] Dàotíng's earlier readings, Xīdí's commentary attempts to gather the best of multiple commentary traditions into a coherent reading.

### Prefaces

The work is preceded by a *Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng jí chéng jì xù* 華嚴一乘教義分齊章集成記序 ("Preface to the *Compilation Notes on the Wǔ jiào zhāng*").

### Abstract

The work is precisely datable per the DILA Authority entry (A000487) to *Jiādìng* 嘉定 11 (1218 CE), composed at the Wǔlín Huìyīnsì when Xīdí was abbot. The bracket adopted here (1218 – 1218) reflects this exact dating. The doctrinal substance is a synthetic exposition of Fǎzàng's *Five Teachings* schema drawing on the entire commentarial tradition (Dàotíng, Shīhuì, Shànxī).

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X999) collection.

## Other points of interest

- The Wǔlín / Hángzhōu Huìyīnsì where Xīdí worked was the same monastery that had been the centre of [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's eleventh-century Huáyán project — providing a continuity of Huáyán-school learning at one of the most important Buddhist publishing centres of the Sòng dynasty.
