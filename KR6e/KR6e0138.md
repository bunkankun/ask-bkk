# Biàn fēi jí 辨非集
*Compilation Discriminating Errors*
by [[善熹]] (Shànxī, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[善熹]] Shànxī is a polemical-critical compilation against errors in rival Buddhist commentarial traditions. The genre — *biàn fēi* "discriminating errors" — is one of the standard formats of Sòng-period Buddhist polemical literature, and Shànxī's contribution is part of his broader engagement with rival Buddhist doctrinal positions, paralleling his [[KR6e0137|*Píng jīn gāng pī*]] (X1018).

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1170 – 1200) reflects [[善熹]] Shànxī's mature scholarly activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1019) collection.
