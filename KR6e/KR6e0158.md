# Yī lèng yán jiū jìng shì chàn 依楞嚴究竟事懺
*Ultimate Phenomenal Repentance Following the Śūraṅgama [Sūtra]*
by [[禪修]] (Chánxiū, 述)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[禪修]] Chán-xiū is a late-Míng Buddhist repentance ritual (*shì chàn* 事懺 — "phenomenal repentance," distinct from *lǐ chàn* 理懺 "principial repentance") based on the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* tradition. Like its predecessor [[KR6e0157|*Shǒu lèng yán tán chǎng xiū zhèng yí*]] (X1477) by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán, the work treats the *Lèng yán* as part of the broader Huáyán-school ritual heritage.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1500 – 1644) reflects the broad late-Míng period during which Chánxiū was active. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1478) collection.
