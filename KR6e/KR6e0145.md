# Fǎ jiè zōng lián huā zhāng 法界宗蓮花章
*The Lotus Treatise of the Dharma-Realm Lineage*
by [[續法]] (Xù-fǎ, 集)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[續法]] Xù-fǎ is a doctrinal-devotional treatise on the *Avataṃsaka*'s teaching of the *fǎ jiè zōng* 法界宗 ("Dharma-Realm Lineage" — i.e. the Huáyán school) cast in the metaphor of the *lián huā* 蓮花 (lotus flower), an iconic Buddhist devotional image.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1660 – 1715) reflects [[續法]] Xù-fǎ's mature scholarly activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1027) collection.
