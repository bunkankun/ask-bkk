# Huáyán dào chǎng qǐ zhǐ dà lüè 華嚴道場起止大略
*General Outline of the Beginning-and-End of the Huáyán Bodhi-Maṇḍa [Service]*
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Míng-period text provides a general outline (*dà lüè*) of the *qǐ zhǐ* 起止 ("beginning-and-end") of a *huáyán dào chǎng* (Huáyán bodhi-maṇḍa) service — the multi-day monastic ritual centred on the *Avataṃsaka*'s cosmology. The work serves as a procedural manual for the abbots and ritual-leaders conducting the service.
