# Fā pútí xīn jiè 發菩提心戒
*The Precept of Generating the Mind of Awakening*
by [[志仙]] (Zhì-xiān, 記 / recorded)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by the Liáo monk [[志仙]] Zhì-xiān is a precept-text (*jiè* 戒) on the generation of *bodhicitta*, framed as the foundational vow underlying the bodhisattva path. The text — preserved in the Fáng-shān stone-canon (F1076) — is one of the few extant Liáo Buddhist devotional texts.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1000 – 1125) reflects the broad Liáo dynasty period (916 – 1125) during which the work would have been composed. The Fáng-shān stone-canon preservation provides exceptional textual security, with the text cut into stone alongside the great corpus of the Fáng-shān stone-cut Buddhist canon.

The work is preserved in the *Fáng-shān shí jīng* 房山石經 collection (F1076).
