# Píng fù gǔ jì 評復古記
*Critical Discussion of the "Restoring the Ancient" Notes* (alternative title: 扶焚薪 *Fú fén xīn*, "In Defence Against the *Fén xīn*")
by [[希迪]] (Xī-dí, 錄)

## About the work

This one-fascicle text is [[希迪]] Xī-dí's polemical response to [[師會]] Shī-huì's [[KR6e0078|*Fù gǔ jì*]] (X998) and, by extension, to Shī-huì's earlier [[KR6e0076|*Fén xīn*]] (X996). The alternative title *Fú fén xīn* 扶焚薪 ("In Defence Against the *Fén xīn*") makes explicit the polemical positioning: where Shī-huì had "burnt the firewood" of [[道亭]] Dào-tíng's earlier readings, Xī-dí here "supports / defends" against Shī-huì's own polemic.

### Prefaces

*The work opens directly with the title-line "評復古記(一名扶焚薪) / 希迪 謹錄" — "*Critical Discussion of the Fù gǔ jì* (alternatively *Fú fén xīn*). Respectfully recorded by Xī-dí."*

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to the period after Xī-dí's [[KR6e0079|*Jí chéng jì*]] of 1218 — i.e. to roughly the period 1218 – 1230 CE — when Xī-dí, having established his synthetic reading of the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* in the *Jí chéng jì*, turned to engage the rival Shī-huì commentary tradition critically. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work completes the cycle of Sòng Wǔ-jiào-zhāng commentary literature: Dào-tíng → Shī-huì (*Fén xīn* and *Fù gǔ jì*) → Xī-dí (*Jí chéng jì* and *Píng fù gǔ jì*) — five major commentaries in 130 years on a single 4-fascicle treatise, the most intensively studied work in Sòng Huáyán-school scholarship.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1000) collection.

## Other points of interest

- The five-stage Sòng commentary tradition on the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* (Dào-tíng → Shī-huì → Shàn-xī → Xī-dí, with Xī-dí writing in two distinct registers) is one of the most thoroughly documented internal scholastic controversies in Chinese Buddhist intellectual history, and provides exceptional material for studying the dynamics of Sòng monastic-doctrinal debate.
