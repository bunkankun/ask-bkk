# Huáyán fǎ jiè xuán jìng 華嚴法界玄鏡
*The Profound Mirror of the Dharma-Realm of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[澄觀]] (Chéngguān, 述)

## About the work

The *Huáyán fǎ jiè xuán jìng* in 2 fascicles is [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's elaborate treatise on the *Avataṃsaka*'s doctrine of the *dharma-realm* (法界 / *dharmadhātu*), drawing together the conceptual articulation of his master's master ([[杜順]] Dù-shùn's *Fǎjiè guān-mén* 法界觀門, preserved in [[宗密]] Zōngmì's later commentary T1884) with his own mature philosophical articulation. The "*xuán jìng*" / "profound mirror" metaphor figures the dharma-realm as a mirror that perfectly reflects all phenomena while remaining itself unobstructed.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's mature period, c. 787 – 838 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The doctrinal substance — the articulation of the four levels of the *dharma-dhātu* (*shì fǎjiè* 事法界 / phenomenal realm; *lǐ fǎjiè* 理法界 / principial realm; *lǐ shì wú'ài fǎjiè* 理事無礙法界 / principle-and-phenomenon non-obstructed realm; *shì shì wú'ài fǎjiè* 事事無礙法界 / phenomenon-and-phenomenon non-obstructed realm) — is the metaphysical apex of mature Huáyán doctrine and was the basis for all subsequent East Asian Buddhist articulations of the *Avataṃsaka*'s ontology.

The Taishō text (T1883) is established on the standard apparatus.

## Other points of interest

- The four-level *dharma-dhātu* schema (事法界 / 理法界 / 理事無礙 / 事事無礙) — formalised here in Chéngguān's mature articulation — became the canonical East Asian Buddhist metaphysical schema and was the conceptual foundation for the entire later East Asian Mahāyāna ontology.
