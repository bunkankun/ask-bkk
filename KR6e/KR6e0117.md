# Huáyán jīng rù fǎ jiè pǐn shí bā wèn dá 華嚴經入法界品十八問答
*Eighteen Questions and Answers on the Chapter on Entering the Dharma-Realm of the Huáyán Scripture*
by [[澄觀]] (Chéngguān, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's compact catechetical treatment of the [[KR6e0010|*Rù fǎjiè pǐn* 入法界品]] — the closing chapter of the 80-fascicle *Avataṃsaka* containing the Sudhana pilgrimage — in 18 question-and-answer pairs. The format addresses 18 doctrinal cruxes raised by the chapter, with each question framed as a doctrinal inquiry and the answer providing Chéngguān's mature reading.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's mature period, c. 787 – 838 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work is part of Chéngguān's broader pedagogical-commentarial corpus on the *Avataṃsaka*, paralleling his [[KR6e0015|*Lüè cè*]] (T1737, 42 questions) and [[KR6e0016|*Qī chù jiǔ huì sòng shì zhāng*]] (T1738) — multiple compact pedagogical works produced alongside the great commentaries.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X218) collection.
