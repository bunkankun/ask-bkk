# Huáyán jīng pǔxián guān xíng fǎ mén 華嚴經普賢觀行法門
*The Dharma-Gate of Samantabhadra's Contemplation-Practice in the Huáyán Scripture*
by [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[法藏]] Fǎzàng treats the contemplative practice associated with Samantabhadra in the *Avataṃsaka* — the *guān xíng* 觀行 ("contemplation-practice") that constitutes the bodhisattva's specifically *Samantabhadra*-style cultivation. The work supplements [[KR6e0094|Fǎzàng's *Fā pútí xīn zhāng*]] (T1878) and other doctrinal works by providing focused treatment of contemplative methodology.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's mature period, c. 670 – 712 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work is one of the smaller items in Fǎzàng's substantial corpus, supplementing the major commentaries with focused contemplative-practice material.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X991) collection.
