# Shì huáyán xuán fù jì 釋華嚴漩澓偈
*Explanation of the Huáyán Whirling-Eddy Verses*
by [[惟勁]] (Wéi-jìn, 釋 / explained)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[惟勁]] Wéi-jìn (active Later-Tang, c. 920 – 936 CE) is a commentary on [[杜順]] Dù-shùn's [[KR6e0160|*Xuán fù jì*]] (F1083). Preserved in the Fáng-shān stone-canon (F1082), it is one of the few extant Later-Tang Buddhist commentaries on a Tang Huáyán-school work.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (920 – 936) reflects the Later-Tang dynasty (後唐, 923 – 936) within which Wéi-jìn was active. The work documents the continuing tradition of Huáyán-school doctrinal study during the brief Later-Tang period, between the Tang collapse (907) and the Sòng founding (960).

The work is preserved in the *Fáng-shān shí jīng* 房山石經 collection (F1082).
