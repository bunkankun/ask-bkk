# Zhù huáyán jīng tí fǎ jiè guān mén sòng 註華嚴經題法界觀門頌
*Annotated Verse-Hymn on the Title of the Huáyán Scripture and the Contemplation-Gate of the Dharma-Realm*
by [[本嵩]] (Běnsōng, 述 / verse-author) and [[琮湛]] (Cóngzhàn, 註 / annotator)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text combines a verse-hymn (*sòng* 頌) by [[本嵩]] Běnsōng on the *Avataṃsaka*'s title and on the *Fǎ jiè guānmén* doctrine, with prose annotation by [[琮湛]] Cóngzhàn. The format — devotional verse plus explanatory prose — was popular in Northern Sòng Buddhist literature, providing accessible material for both monastic recitation and lay study.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1080 – 1130) reflects the late-Northern-Sòng period during which the *sòngzhù* genre flourished. The work is part of the wider Sòng Huìyīnsì Huáyán-school revival project (cf. [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's [[KR6e0091|*Huán yuán guān kē*]], [[KR6e0017|*Huáyán jīng shū kē*]], etc.), providing devotional-pedagogical material derived from the foundational Tang Huáyán doctrinal corpus.

The Taishō text (T1885) is established on the standard apparatus.
