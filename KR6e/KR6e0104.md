# Zhù huáyán fǎ jiè guān kē wén 註華嚴法界觀科文 (by Zōng-yù)
*Sectional Outline of the Annotated Edition of the Huáyán Dharma-Realm Contemplation* (alternative version)
by [[宗豫]] (Zōng-yù, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the alternative *kē wén* outline of [[宗密]] Zōngmì's [[KR6e0101|*Zhù fǎ jiè guān-mén*]] (T1884), composed by [[宗豫]] Zōng-yù — distinct from Zōngmì's own outline (preserved as [[KR6e0103|X1007]]). The existence of two parallel *kē wén* outlines of the same parent commentary attests the work's central pedagogical importance and the scholastic activity around it in the late-Tang Cǎo-táng-sì circle.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (830 – 900) reflects the period of late-Tang activity at the Cǎo-táng-sì 草堂寺 on Mt. Guī-fēng 圭峰山, which Zōng-yù is conventionally associated with as a contemporary or successor of Zōngmì. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1008).

## Other points of interest

- The doubled *kē wén* tradition (Zōngmì's own X1007 + Zōng-yù's parallel X1008) is unusual; it attests the active scholastic culture around the *Fǎ jiè guān-mén* in the late Tang Huáyán circle.
