# Huáyán huán yuán guān kē 華嚴還源觀科
*Sectional Outline of the Huáyán Contemplation of Returning to the Source*
by [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 刊正 / editorially-corrected)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) for [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's [[KR6e0090|*Huán yuán guān*]] (T1876), edited by the great Northern-Sòng Huáyán-school revivalist [[淨源]] Jìngyuán (1011–1088). Like Jìngyuán's other *kē* outlines (cf. [[KR6e0017|X1418]] for Chéngguān's *Shū*), the work serves as a navigational map for the parent contemplative-doctrinal text.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface; the work opens with the title-line and Jìngyuán's editorial signature.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067 – 1088) reflects the period of [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's mature editorial activity at the Huìyīnsì 慧因寺 in Hángzhōu. The *kē* outline is part of the wider Northern-Sòng Huáyán-school project to reconsolidate the Tang corpus through systematic editorial work; the *Huán yuán guān*'s contemplative significance for the school made it a natural object of such editorial attention.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X993) collection.

## Other points of interest

- Jìngyuán's editorial work on the *Huán yuán guān* — together with his sub-commentary [[KR6e0092|*Bǔ jiě* 補解]] (X994) — re-established the work as a foundational contemplative text in the Sòng Huáyán-school curriculum.
