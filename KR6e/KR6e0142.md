# Xián-shǒu wǔ jiào yí 賢首五教儀
*The Doctrinal Procedures of Xián-shǒu's Five Teachings*
by [[續法]] (Xù-fǎ, 集錄)

## About the work

This 6-fascicle work by the great Qīng Buddhist scholar [[續法]] Xù-fǎ (1641–1728) is the most ambitious early-Qīng commentary on [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's *Five Teachings* doxography (cf. [[KR6e0074|*Wǔ jiào zhāng*]]). Xù-fǎ — known in the East Asian Buddhist tradition as one of the most important late-imperial Huáyán-school authorities — restructures Fǎzàng's classical schema in a *yí* 儀 ("ritual procedures") format, providing systematic instruction for monastic-doctrinal study.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1660 – 1715) reflects [[續法]] Xù-fǎ's mature scholarly activity. The *Wǔ jiào yí* is part of Xù-fǎ's vast commentary corpus on the Huáyán-school doctrinal apparatus, which together (cf. [[KR6e0143|*Wǔ jiào yí kāi méng*]], [[KR6e0144|*Duàn zhèng sān jué jiǎn làn tú*]], etc.) constitutes the late-imperial Huáyán doctrinal curriculum.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1024) collection.
