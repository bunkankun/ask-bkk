# Huáyán yuán rén lùn fā wēi lù 華嚴原人論發微錄
*Record of Drawing Out the Subtleties of the Inquiry into the Origin of Humanity of the Huáyán [School]*
by [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 述)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text is [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's substantial Northern-Sòng commentary on [[宗密]] Zōngmì's [[KR6e0105|*Yuán rén lùn*]] (T1886). The title — *fā wēi* 發微 "drawing out the subtleties" — signals the work's exegetical aim: to make the implicit doctrinal structures of Zōngmì's brief but doctrinally dense original text fully explicit for Sòng readers.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's mature period at the Huìyīnsì 慧因寺 in Hángzhōu, c. 1067 – 1088 CE; the bracket adopted here reflects this window. As one of Jìngyuán's substantial editorial-commentarial projects, the work is part of his broader Northern-Sòng programme to consolidate the Tang Huáyán-school doctrinal corpus.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1031) collection.

## Other points of interest

- Jìngyuán's *Fā wēi lù* is the principal Sòng-period commentary on Zōngmì's *Yuán rén lùn* and is one of the most consequential Sòng-Buddhist doctrinal works of its kind; through it Zōngmì's hierarchical doxography entered the SòngYuánMíng monastic curriculum.
