# Chūnqiū bǐ shì 春秋比事

*Comparative Affairs of the Spring and Autumn Annals*

by [[沈棐]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Chūnqiū bǐ shì* 春秋比事 in twenty juan is a Southern-Sòng *Chūnqiū* topical compilation. The catalog meta gives the author as Shěn Bǐ 沈棐, but authorship is contested — the SKQS *tíyào* tentatively follows Chén Liàng's 陳亮 (1143–1194) preface, which calls the author Shěn Bǐ (*zì* Wén-bó 文伯, of Hú-zhōu 湖州, school official at Wù-zhōu 婺州). Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí*, however, identifies a "Shěn Wén-bó" of Hú-zhōu as Shěn Cháng-qīng 沈長卿 (sobriquet *Shěn-zhāi jū-shì* 審齋居士), prefect of Cháng-zhōu, exiled by Qín Guì 秦檜 to Huà-zhōu — *not* Shěn Bǐ. Dū Mù's 都穆 *Tīng yǔ jì tán* 聽雨紀談 cites a Jiā-dìng *xīn-wèi* 嘉定辛未 (1211) preface by Tán Yuè-qīng 譚月卿 of Lú-líng attributing the work to Liú Shuò 劉朔 of Pú-yáng 莆陽. The original title was *Chūnqiū zǒng lùn* 春秋總論; Chén Liàng renamed it. The Sìkù base is the Yuán Zhì-zhèng 至正-era Jīn-huá print, with a preface by Wáng Xiǎn-rén 王顯仁 of *Zhōng-xīng-lù rú-xué jiào-shòu* 中興路儒學教授.

### Tiyao

The Sìkù *tíyào* (text from the Kyoto Zinbun digital *Sìkù tíyào*):

Old text titled "by Shěn Bǐ of Sòng." Bǐ's career is unrecoverable. Only the work's preface by Chén Liàng calls his *zì* Wén-bó, of Hú-zhōu, formerly school-official of Wù-zhōu. Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí* says: "Among Hú-zhōu personalities is one Shěn Wén-bó, named Cháng-qīng 長卿, sobriquet *Shěn-zhāi jū-shì*, prefect of Cháng-zhōu, who antagonised Qín Guì and was demoted to Huà-zhōu — not 'Bǐ.' Why Tóng-fù [Chén Liàng's *zì*] says so is unclear. Was there another person named Bǐ with the *zì* Wén-bó? Then he is not from Hú-zhōu." Chén Zhènsūn's view is at odds with Chén Liàng. Dū Mù's *Tīng yǔ jì tán*, citing the Jiā-dìng 4 (1211) Lú-líng preface by Tán Yuè-qīng, calls the author Liú Shuò of Pú-yáng, claiming Tán saw the Liú-family copy. The present text does not include Tán's preface; we cannot tell what Dū Mù was relying on. Doubt is transmitted as doubt; we have no firm correction. Since Chén Liàng was close in time to Bǐ, we provisionally follow Chén Liàng's preface, retaining the name Shěn Bǐ.

The work places the various states in classified sequence first; afterwards it brings together comparable affairs of court attendance, sieges, and covenants — categorising and explaining each. The argument is rather balanced. The original was titled *Chūnqiū zǒng lùn* 春秋總論; Chén Liàng renamed it. In the Yuán Zhì-zhèng era it was cut at Jīn-huá; the woodblock was long destroyed, and the work rarely transmitted. Hence Zhū Yízūn's *Jīng yì kǎo* notes "lost." The present text has at the head a preface by *Zhōng-xīng-lù rú-xué jiào-shòu* Wáng Xiǎn-rén — apparently still based on the Yuán print.

### Abstract

The *Sìkù tíyào* makes the principal points: that the work's authorship is genuinely contested between Shěn Bǐ (per Chén Liàng's preface), Shěn Cháng-qīng (per Chén Zhènsūn's identification of "Shěn Wén-bó"), and Liú Shuò (per Dū Mù's record of Tán Yuè-qīng's preface); that the editors provisionally follow Chén Liàng's contemporary attribution; that the work's hermeneutical method is to organise *Chūnqiū* events thematically (court visits, sieges, covenants, etc.) rather than year-by-year, anticipating Gù Dònggāo's 顧棟高 *Chūnqiū dà-shì biǎo* 春秋大事表 by half a millennium; that the work was rare in transmission, recovered via the Yuán Zhì-zhèng print.

## Other points of interest

The thematic organisation of *Chūnqiū* events — by category of event rather than chronologically — is the precursor to the Qīng evidential-school *dà-shì biǎo* 大事表 form, of which Gù Dònggāo's mid-Qīng 50-juan *Chūnqiū dà-shì biǎo* is the most ambitious example.
