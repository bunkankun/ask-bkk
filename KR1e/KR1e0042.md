# Chūnqiū bǐ shì 春秋比事

*Comparative Affairs of the Spring and Autumn Annals*

by [[沈棐]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Chūnqiū bǐ shì* 春秋比事 in twenty juan is a Southern-Sòng *Chūnqiū* topical compilation. The catalog meta gives the author as Shěn Bǐ 沈棐, but authorship is contested — the SKQS *tíyào* tentatively follows Chén Liàng's 陳亮 (1143–1194) preface, which calls the author Shěn Bǐ (*zì* Wénbó 文伯, of Húzhōu 湖州, school official at Wùzhōu 婺州). Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí*, however, identifies a "Shěn Wénbó" of Húzhōu as Shěn Chángqīng 沈長卿 (sobriquet *Shěnzhāi jūshì* 審齋居士), prefect of Chángzhōu, exiled by Qín Guì 秦檜 to Huàzhōu — *not* Shěn Bǐ. Dū Mù's 都穆 *Tīng yǔ jì tán* 聽雨紀談 cites a Jiādìng *xīnwèi* 嘉定辛未 (1211) preface by Tán Yuèqīng 譚月卿 of Lúlíng attributing the work to Liú Shuò 劉朔 of Púyáng 莆陽. The original title was *Chūnqiū zǒng lùn* 春秋總論; Chén Liàng renamed it. The Sìkù base is the Yuán Zhìzhèng 至正-era Jīnhuá print, with a preface by Wáng Xiǎnrén 王顯仁 of *Zhōngxīnglù rúxué jiàoshòu* 中興路儒學教授.

### Tiyao

The Sìkù *tíyào* (text from the Kyoto Zinbun digital *Sìkù tíyào*):

Old text titled "by Shěn Bǐ of Sòng." Bǐ's career is unrecoverable. Only the work's preface by Chén Liàng calls his *zì* Wénbó, of Húzhōu, formerly school-official of Wùzhōu. Chén Zhènsūn's *Shū lù jiětí* says: "Among Húzhōu personalities is one Shěn Wénbó, named Chángqīng 長卿, sobriquet *Shěnzhāi jūshì*, prefect of Chángzhōu, who antagonised Qín Guì and was demoted to Huàzhōu — not 'Bǐ.' Why Tóngfù [Chén Liàng's *zì*] says so is unclear. Was there another person named Bǐ with the *zì* Wénbó? Then he is not from Húzhōu." Chén Zhènsūn's view is at odds with Chén Liàng. Dū Mù's *Tīng yǔ jì tán*, citing the Jiādìng 4 (1211) Lúlíng preface by Tán Yuèqīng, calls the author Liú Shuò of Púyáng, claiming Tán saw the Liú-family copy. The present text does not include Tán's preface; we cannot tell what Dū Mù was relying on. Doubt is transmitted as doubt; we have no firm correction. Since Chén Liàng was close in time to Bǐ, we provisionally follow Chén Liàng's preface, retaining the name Shěn Bǐ.

The work places the various states in classified sequence first; afterwards it brings together comparable affairs of court attendance, sieges, and covenants — categorising and explaining each. The argument is rather balanced. The original was titled *Chūnqiū zǒng lùn* 春秋總論; Chén Liàng renamed it. In the Yuán Zhìzhèng era it was cut at Jīnhuá; the woodblock was long destroyed, and the work rarely transmitted. Hence Zhū Yízūn's *Jīng yì kǎo* notes "lost." The present text has at the head a preface by *Zhōngxīnglù rúxué jiàoshòu* Wáng Xiǎnrén — apparently still based on the Yuán print.

### Abstract

The *Sìkù tíyào* makes the principal points: that the work's authorship is genuinely contested between Shěn Bǐ (per Chén Liàng's preface), Shěn Chángqīng (per Chén Zhènsūn's identification of "Shěn Wénbó"), and Liú Shuò (per Dū Mù's record of Tán Yuèqīng's preface); that the editors provisionally follow Chén Liàng's contemporary attribution; that the work's hermeneutical method is to organise *Chūnqiū* events thematically (court visits, sieges, covenants, etc.) rather than year-by-year, anticipating Gù Dònggāo's 顧棟高 *Chūnqiū dàshì biǎo* 春秋大事表 by half a millennium; that the work was rare in transmission, recovered via the Yuán Zhìzhèng print.

## Other points of interest

The thematic organisation of *Chūnqiū* events — by category of event rather than chronologically — is the precursor to the Qīng evidential-school *dàshì biǎo* 大事表 form, of which Gù Dònggāo's mid-Qīng 50-juan *Chūnqiū dàshì biǎo* is the most ambitious example.
