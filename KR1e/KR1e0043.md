# Dōng-lái Lǚ tài-shǐ Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn lèi biān 東萊呂太史春秋左傳類編

*Master Lǚ Dōng-lái's Categorised Reorganisation of the Spring and Autumn Annals' Zuǒ-tradition*

by [[呂祖謙]] (撰) · [[胡文楷]] (撰校勘記)

## About the work

The *Dōng-lái Lǚ tài-shǐ Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn lèi biān* 東萊呂太史春秋左傳類編 in six juan is the *Zuǒzhuàn* topical reorganisation of Lǚ Zǔqiān [[呂祖謙]] (1137–1181). It is the work whose existence the SKQS *tíyào* on [[KR1e0039]] notes (Lǚ "had three works on the *Zuǒzhuàn*: the *Lèi biān* dividing the text into nineteen categories, *the Bó yì* [[KR1e0041]] raising matters and establishing meanings, and the present work [the *Zhuàn shuō*]") and which the *tíyào* says is "long without transmitted exemplar, scattered in the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* with little to draw from." The present SBCK edition recovers the work in its 19-category form, here reorganised as 6 juan with categorical headings: *gāng lǐng* 綱領 (overview), Zhōu, Qí, Jìn, Chǔ, Wú-Yuè, *yí-dí* 夷狄, *fù-yōng* 附庸, *zhū-hóu zhì-dù* 諸侯制度, *fēng-sú* 風俗, *lǐ* 禮, *shì-zú* 氏族, *guān-zhì* 官制, *cái-yòng* 財用, *xíng* 刑, *bīng-zhì* 兵制, *dì-lǐ* 地理, *chūnqiū qián shì* 春秋前事, *lùn yì* 論議. The Republican-era critical apparatus (*jiào kān jì* 校勘記) is by Hú Wénkǎi 胡文楷.

### Tiyao

*No SKQS tíyào in the SBCK source.* The work was not registered in the SKQS, having been recovered only via the SBCK editorial work in the early twentieth century. The SKQS *tíyào* on [[KR1e0039]] *Zuǒshì zhuàn shuō* notes the existence of the *Lèi biān* in nineteen categories but says it was unrecoverable from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* fragments alone.

### Abstract

The *Lèi biān* takes the entire *Zuǒzhuàn* text and redistributes it under nineteen topical categories — by state (Zhōu, Qí, Jìn, Chǔ, Wú, Yuè, plus the barbarians and the small dependencies) and by institutional theme (rites, lineage, official system, taxation, military, geography, customs, etc.). It is the most ambitious Sòng-period topical reorganisation of the *Zuǒzhuàn* and one of the principal forerunners of the late-imperial *Chūnqiū dà-shì biǎo* 春秋大事表 form (Gù Dònggāo, mid-Qīng).

The work was used by Lǚ Zǔqiān as a teaching tool for his disciples at the Míng-zhāo-shān 明招山 academy and at the *Lì-zé shū-yuàn* 麗澤書院; the topical organisation made it possible to examine all *Zuǒ*-passages relating to a single institutional or political question simultaneously. The *Lèi biān*'s methodological commitment — to read the *Zuǒzhuàn* as a comprehensive evidentiary witness to early Chinese institutions — is the basis on which Lǚ's other *Zuǒzhuàn* works ([[KR1e0039]], [[KR1e0040]], [[KR1e0041]]) are constructed.

The Republican-era SBCK edition recovers the work via collation of late-Sòng print fragments that the SKQS editors did not have access to. Hú Wénkǎi 胡文楷's *jiào kān jì* documents the principal textual variants.

## Other points of interest

The Republican-era SBCK recovery of the *Lèi biān* is one of the principal classical-text recoveries of the early-twentieth-century philological renaissance — comparable in importance to the recovery of the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* fragments, but specific to texts the Qīng editors had not accessed. Hú Wénkǎi 胡文楷 (1894–1976) was a prolific Republican-era textual scholar; his *jiào kān jì* for this volume is one of his classical-philology contributions.
