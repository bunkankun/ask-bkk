# KR1e 春秋類 — Spring and Autumn Annals

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR1e 春秋類 (*Chūnqiū lèi*, "Spring and Autumn Annals") is the *Chūnqiū*-classics section of the Classics branch (*jīngbù* 經部, KR1) of the *Sìkù quánshū* fourfold scheme. The division collects the canonical *Chūnqiū* 春秋 itself, its three transmitted commentaries — *Zuǒzhuàn* 左傳, *Gōngyáng zhuàn* 公羊傳, *Gǔliáng zhuàn* 穀梁傳 (the *Sānzhuàn* 三傳) — and the entire received corpus of pre-modern exegesis, sub-commentary, polemic, topical reorganisation, calendrical and prosopographical reference works built around them. The 121 per-text notes in this directory span from a Western-Hàn standalone canonical text ([[KR1e0001]]) through the foundational Hàn-Jìn-Táng combined editions (Dù Yù 杜預, Hé Xiū 何休, Fàn Níng 范甯, Kǒng Yǐngdá 孔穎達), the Táng *Dàn–Zhào–Lù* 啖–趙–陸 radical revival, the Northern-Sòng *xīnyì* 新義 movement and Hú Ānguó's 胡安國 Southern-Sòng *Chūnqiū zhuàn* 春秋傳, the late-Sòng / Yuán radical-sceptical commentaries of Cuī Zǐfāng 崔子方, Chéng Duānxué 程端學 and Zhào Fǎng 趙汸, the Míng *xìn-jīng bù-xìn-zhuàn* 信經不信傳 reaction culminating in Lù Càn 陸粲 and the Dōnglín 東林 commentators, the Qīng imperial trilogy of Kāngxī and Qiánlóng, and the high-Qīng evidential summa of Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武, Gù Dònggāo 顧棟高, Huì Dòng 惠棟 and Jiāng Yǒng 江永.

The reader must keep four structural facts in mind. First, the *Chūnqiū* itself is a 17,000-character court chronicle of the state of Lǔ 魯 covering 722–481 BCE; for nearly 2,400 years most readers held it to have been edited by Confucius so as to convey his "lofty principles in subtle words" (*wēi-yán dàyì* 微言大義) (Wilkinson §48.1.1), and almost every commentary in this division is a position taken in the resulting hermeneutic — whether the *jīng* really transmits hidden *bāo-biǎn* 褒貶 (praise-and-blame) judgements, and if so, by what regulatory keys. Second, the three commentaries are radically different in character: the *Gōngyáng* and *Gǔliáng* are short *bāo-biǎn*-driven catechisms in question-and-answer form, while the *Zuǒzhuàn* — over ten times their combined length — is a fully-narrative pre-Hàn historical work that was attached to the *Chūnqiū* as commentary only secondarily, and is "the prime source on the entire period" (Wilkinson §48.1.1.1). Much of the division's history is the long oscillation between *Zuǒ*-primary historiographical reading and *Gōngyáng*/*Gǔliáng*-primary moral-hermeneutic reading. Third, the integrated *Sānzhuàn* edition with all three commentaries interlinear with the *Chūnqiū* is a Northern-Sòng product, and the standard *Shísān jīng zhùshū* 十三經注疏 form in which it is now read dates only from 1815 (Wilkinson §48.1.1.1); the foundational layered editions in this division ([[KR1e0004]], [[KR1e0006]], [[KR1e0010]]) descend from this stratification. Fourth, after Hú Ānguó's *Chūnqiū zhuàn* [[KR1e0036]] was made the official examination commentary in 1313 (and reaffirmed in the 1415 *Wǔ-jīng dà-quán* 五經大全 [[KR1e0074]]), it remained the orthodox state textbook for nearly six centuries, and a great many entries from the Yuán onward are best read as either elaborations of, or polemical attacks on, the Hú-school orthodoxy.

## Important texts and text clusters

The principal works fall into eleven clusters.

**The canonical text and Hàn-Jìn-Táng *Sānzhuàn* (KR1e0001–0011, plus [[KR1e0122]]).** *Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn (zhèngwén)* 春秋左傳(正文) [[KR1e0001]] is a standalone canonical edition of the *jīng* + *Zuǒ* without commentary. *Chūnqiū jīng-zhuàn jíjiě* 春秋經傳集解 [[KR1e0002]], composed by Dù Yù 杜預 in semi-retirement after the Jìn conquest of Wú in 280, is the foundational edition that combined the *Chūnqiū* and *Zuǒzhuàn* into a single integrated text — the move that made Dù Yù's reading the default *Zuǒ* tradition for the next 1,700 years; *Chūnqiū shìlì* 春秋釋例 [[KR1e0012]] is its systematic counterpart organised around Dù Yù's theory of the fifty *fán* 凡 (regulatory items). *Chūnqiū zhèngyì* 春秋正義 [[KR1e0003]] is Kǒng Yǐngdá's 孔穎達 official Táng sub-commentary, and *Chūnqiū Zuǒzhuàn zhùshū* 春秋左傳注疏 [[KR1e0004]] is the resulting layered Dù-Kǒng-Lù edition. The parallel *Gōngyáng* and *Gǔliáng* layered editions are *Chūnqiū Gōngyáng zhuàn zhùshū* 春秋公羊傳注疏 [[KR1e0006]] (Hé Xiū 何休's *Jiě gǔ* 解詁 + Xú Yàn 徐彥's Táng sub-commentary) and *Chūnqiū Gǔliáng zhùshū* 春秋穀梁注疏 [[KR1e0010]] (Fàn Níng 范甯's *Jí jiě* + Yáng Shìxūn 楊士勛's Táng sub-commentary). *Chūnqiū Gōngyáng jīng-zhuàn jiě-gǔ* 春秋公羊經傳解詁 [[KR1e0007]] is the standalone Hé Xiū work, fruit of seventeen years' labour and the polemical centre of Hàn *Gōngyáng* learning. *Zhēn gāohuāng* 箴膏肓 [[KR1e0011]] preserves Zhèng Xuán's 鄭玄 rebuttal of Hé Xiū in the long Hàn dispute over the relative authority of the three commentaries. *Chūnqiū fánlù* 春秋繁露 [[KR1e0122]] of Dǒng Zhòngshū 董仲舒 is the Western-Hàn *Gōngyáng*-school cosmological treatise that grafted *yīn-yáng wǔ-xíng* onto Confucian moral-political thought — the indispensable Hàn-period theoretical companion to the *Gōngyáng* tradition, even though the received text contains layers down to the Sòng.

**The Táng *Dàn–Zhào–Lù* radical school (KR1e0013–0015).** Lù Chún's 陸淳 trilogy — *Chūnqiū jí-zhuàn zuǎn lì* 春秋集傳纂例 [[KR1e0013]], *Chūnqiū jí-zhuàn wēi-zhǐ* 春秋集傳微旨 [[KR1e0014]], *Chūnqiū jí-zhuàn biàn-yí* 春秋集傳辨疑 [[KR1e0015]] — transmits the lost teachings of Dàn Zhù 啖助 and Zhào Kuāng 趙匡, the mid-Táng founders of the *Chūnqiū* "rejection-of-the-three-commentaries" tradition. The trilogy is the proximate methodological ancestor of the entire Northern-Sòng *xīnyì* movement; its *biàn-yí* is the polemical charge against the *Sānzhuàn* and the *zuǎn lì* the constructive replacement.

**Northern-Sòng *xīnyì* and ritual / regulatory commentaries (KR1e0017–0024, 0028–0031).** The cluster opens with Sūn Fù's 孫復 *Chūnqiū zūn-wáng fā-wēi* 春秋尊王發微 [[KR1e0018]], whose *yǒu-biǎn wú-bāo* 有貶無褒 doctrine — Confucius criticises but never praises — set the radical-rigorist tone of the whole. *Chūnqiū huáng-gāng lùn* 春秋皇綱論 [[KR1e0019]] of Wáng Xī 王晳 stands almost alone in resisting both Sūn Fù's rigorism and the Dàn-Zhào tendency. Liú Chǎng's 劉敞 four-work programme — *Chūnqiū quán-héng* 春秋權衡 [[KR1e0021]], *Liú-shì Chūnqiū zhuàn* 劉氏春秋傳 [[KR1e0022]], *Liú-shì Chūnqiū yìlín* 劉氏春秋意林 [[KR1e0023]], *Chūnqiū chuán-shuō lì* 春秋傳說例 [[KR1e0024]] — is the methodologically most rigorous Northern-Sòng *xīnyì* corpus, anchored in classical ritual. Cuī Zǐfāng's 崔子方 *Cuī-shì Chūnqiū jīng-jiě* 崔氏春秋經解 [[KR1e0028]] and *Chūnqiū běnlì* 春秋本例 [[KR1e0029]] developed the distinctive *rì-yuè lì* 日月例 (day-month item) theory — that the calendrical detail of each *Chūnqiū* entry encodes *bāo-biǎn*. Zhāng Dàhēng's 張大亨 *Chūnqiū wǔ-lǐ lì-zōng* 春秋五禮例宗 [[KR1e0030]] redistributes the entire *Chūnqiū* under the canonical *wǔ lǐ* 五禮 typology.

**The Hú Ānguó school and its institutional triumph (KR1e0036, [[KR1e0071]], [[KR1e0074]]).** Hú Ānguó's 胡安國 *Hú-shì Chūnqiū zhuàn* 胡氏春秋傳 [[KR1e0036]] — written under the shadow of the Jīn 金 invasions and reading the *Chūnqiū* as cipher for political-ethical judgement on contemporary frontier policy — became the foundational Southern-Sòng commentary; its later canonisation as the *fourth* official *Chūnqiū* tradition (alongside the *Sānzhuàn*) made it the longest-standing imperial orthodoxy in this division. *Chūnqiū Hú-zhuàn fù-lù zuǎn-shū* 春秋胡傳附錄纂疏 [[KR1e0071]] of Wāng Kèkuān 汪克寬 is its standard Yuán-period sub-commentary and the Yuán-Míng examination textbook. *Chūnqiū dà-quán* 春秋大全 [[KR1e0074]] of Hú Guǎng 胡廣, the Yǒnglè-era imperial compilation in the *Wǔ-jīng dà-quán* programme, codified Hú Ānguó's reading as Míng state orthodoxy.

**Southern-Sòng *Zuǒ*-primary, *Dàoxué*, and Yǒng-jiā commentaries (KR1e0032–0035, 0037–0048).** Yè Mèngdé's 葉夢得 tetrad — *Yè-shì Chūnqiū zhuàn* 葉氏春秋傳 [[KR1e0032]], *Chūnqiū kǎo* 春秋考 [[KR1e0033]], *Chūnqiū Zuǒ-zhuàn yàn* 春秋左傳讞 [[KR1e0034]] (the *Gōng-zhuàn yàn* and *Gǔ-zhuàn yàn* are lost) — sets the methodologically careful middle path between Sūn Fù and Sū Zhé. *Lǚ-shì Chūnqiū jíjiě* 呂氏春秋集解 [[KR1e0035]] of Lǚ Běnzhōng 呂本中 anthologises the major Northern-Sòng commentators. Lǚ Zǔqiān's 呂祖謙 *Zuǒ*-trilogy — *Zuǒ-shì zhuàn-shuō* 左氏傳說 [[KR1e0039]], *Zuǒ-shì zhuàn xù-shuō* 左氏傳續說 [[KR1e0040]], *Zuǒ-shì bó-yì* 左氏博議 [[KR1e0041]] — together with his topical recasting *Dōng-lái Lǚ Tài-shǐ Chūnqiū Zuǒ-zhuàn lèi-biān* 東萊呂太史春秋左傳類編 [[KR1e0043]] — makes Lǚ Zǔqiān the central Southern-Sòng *Zuǒ* exegete; *Zuǒ-shì bó-yì* in particular, with its 168 evaluative essays on *Zuǒ* episodes, became the most popular Sòng *Chūnqiū* book. *Chūnqiū hòu zhuàn* 春秋後傳 [[KR1e0038]] of Chén Fùliáng 陳傅良 is the leading work of the Yǒng-jiā 永嘉 statecraft school. *Chūnqiū jí-yì* 春秋集義 [[KR1e0047]] of Lǐ Míngfù 李明復 is the most comprehensive Southern-Sòng *Dàoxué*-school *Chūnqiū* compilation; *Zhāng-shì Chūnqiū jí-zhù* 張氏春秋集注 [[KR1e0048]] of Zhāng Qià 張洽 is the principal *Chūnqiū* commentary by a Zhū Xī disciple. *Chūnqiū fēn-jì* 春秋分記 [[KR1e0045]] of Chéng Gōng-shuō 程公說 is the most ambitious Southern-Sòng *Shǐjì*-modeled historiographical reorganisation of the *Chūnqiū*.

**Late-Sòng / Yuán radical-sceptical and synthetic commentaries (KR1e0049–0065).** *Chūnqiū tōng-shuō* 春秋通說 [[KR1e0050]] of Huáng Zhòng-yán 黃仲炎 makes the strong thesis that the *Chūnqiū* is a book of *teaching* and *admonition*, not *bāo-biǎn*. *Chūnqiū jīng-quán* 春秋經筌 [[KR1e0052]] of Zhào Péngfēi 趙鵬飛 — programmatically refusing to use any commentary at all — is the sharpest Sòng instance of the radical-sceptical tendency. Chéng Duānxué's 程端學 trilogy — *Chūnqiū běnyì* 春秋本義 [[KR1e0060]], *Chéng-shì Chūnqiū huò-wèn* 程氏春秋或問 [[KR1e0061]], *Sān-zhuàn biàn-yí* 三傳辨疑 [[KR1e0062]] — pushes the position to its limit, treating the *Zuǒzhuàn* as a forgery and the *Gōngyáng* and *Gǔliáng* as worthless; this is the late-Yuán radical centre. *Chūnqiū jí-zhuàn shìyì dà-chéng* 春秋集傳釋義大成 [[KR1e0056]] of Yú Gāo 兪皋 introduces the four-*zhuàn* parallel format (Zuǒ, Gōng, Gǔ, Hú under each lemma) that became standard. *Chūnqiū huì-tōng* 春秋會通 [[KR1e0064]] of Lǐ Lián 李廉 stakes out the synthetic middle.

**The Yuán Xīn'ān school and Zhào Fǎng's five-work programme (KR1e0066–0071).** *Chūnqiū jí-zhuàn* 春秋集傳 [[KR1e0066]], *Chūnqiū shī-shuō* 春秋師說 [[KR1e0067]], *Chūnqiū Zuǒ-shì zhuàn bǔ-zhù* 春秋左氏傳補註 [[KR1e0068]], *Chūnqiū jīn-suǒ-shi* 春秋金鎖匙 [[KR1e0069]], *Chūnqiū shǔ-cí* 春秋屬辭 [[KR1e0070]] together constitute Zhào Fǎng's 趙汸 *Chūnqiū* programme — the most coherent single-author *Chūnqiū* corpus of the entire pre-modern tradition, organised around the methodological key *cè-shū* / *bǐ-xuē* 策書/筆削 (court-record vs. Confucian editorial brush). *Chūnqiū shī-shuō* preserves the otherwise-lost teachings of his teacher Huáng Zé 黃澤. The shorter *Chūnqiū què-yí* 春秋闕欵 [[KR1e0065]] of Zhèng Yù 鄭玉 brings the late-Yuán *quē-yí* 闕疑 (acknowledged limit-of-knowledge) doctrine to its sharpest formulation.

**Míng *xìn-jīng bù-xìn-zhuàn* and Dōnglín commentaries (KR1e0072–0092).** *Chūnqiū chūn-wáng zhèng-yuè kǎo* 春秋春王正月考 [[KR1e0072]] of Zhāng Yǐníng 張以寧 (1370) opens the Míng with a calendrical refutation of Hú Ānguó's *yǐ Xià-shí guàn Zhōu-yuè* 以夏時冠周月 doctrine. *Zuǒ-zhuàn fù zhù* 左傳附注 [[KR1e0077]] and *Chūnqiū Hú-shì zhuàn biàn-yí* 春秋胡氏傳辨疑 [[KR1e0078]] of Lù Càn 陸粲 inaugurate the mid-Míng *kǎozhèng*-style philological revision that Gù Yánwǔ would build on. *Chūnqiū míngzhì lù* 春秋明志錄 [[KR1e0079]] of Xióng Guō 熊過 is the most systematic mid-Míng *xìn-jīng bù-xìn-zhuàn* monograph. *Chūnqiū kǒng-yì* 春秋孔義 [[KR1e0088]] of Gāo Pānlóng 高攀龍 is the principal Dōnglín-school *Chūnqiū* work; its *yǐ jīng jiě jīng* method ("explain the canon by the canon") is a key statement of late-Míng *Chūnqiū* hermeneutic. *Chūnqiū biàn-yì* 春秋辯義 [[KR1e0089]] of Zhuó Ěrkāng 卓爾康 is the largest single Míng *Chūnqiū* commentary, encyclopedic in form. *Zuǒ-zhuàn Dù-Lín hé-zhù* 左傳杜林合注 [[KR1e0092]] is the late-Míng composite student edition (Dù Yù annotation + Lín Yáosǒu 林堯叟 paraphrase) that became the standard handbook for several generations.

**The Qīng imperial trilogy (KR1e0093–0095).** *Rì-jiǎng Chūnqiū jiěyì* 日講春秋解義 [[KR1e0093]] is the Kāngxī court lecture-cycle, anchoring on Hú Ānguó while purging the most extreme readings; *Qīn-dìng Chūnqiū chuán-shuō huì-zuǎn* 欽定春秋傳說彙纂 [[KR1e0094]] (1721) is the Kāngxī-commissioned official synthesis, designed to fill the gap that Zhū Xī had left by composing no *Chūnqiū* commentary; *Yù-zuǎn Chūnqiū zhí-jiě* 御纂春秋直解 [[KR1e0095]] (1758) completes the trilogy under Qiánlóng, with a polemical critique of the *Dàn–Zhào–Lù* school.

**Qīng evidential *Zuǒ-zhuàn* philology and the Wú-pài / Wú-Jiāng lineages (KR1e0096–0117).** *Zuǒ-zhuàn Dù-jiě bǔ-zhèng* 左傳杜解補正 [[KR1e0096]] of Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武 is the foundational text of Qīng *Zuǒ-zhuàn* evidential research and the model for the entire Wú-Jiāng school; *Dú Zuǒ rì-chāo* 讀左日鈔 [[KR1e0100]] of Zhū Hèlíng 朱鶴齡 is its companion. *Chūnqiū bài-shū* 春秋稗疏 [[KR1e0097]] of Wáng Fūzhī 王夫之 supplies the geographic / calendrical / *shū-fǎ* substratum. *Zuǒ-zhuàn shì-wěi* 左傳事緯 [[KR1e0101]] of Mǎ Sù 馬驌 is genre-redefining, disaggregating and regrouping the *Zuǒ* under 108 topical headings. Máo Qílíng's 毛奇齡 ritualist trilogy — *Chūnqiū Máo-shì zhuàn* 春秋毛氏傳 [[KR1e0102]], *Chūnqiū jiǎn-shū kān-wù* 春秋簡書刋誤 [[KR1e0103]], *Chūnqiū shǔ-cí bǐ-shì jì* 春秋屬辭比事記 [[KR1e0104]] — is the polemical core of the early-Qīng anti-Hú reaction. The Wú-pài 吳派 lineage opens with Huì Shìqí's 惠士奇 *Huì-shì Chūnqiū shuō* 惠氏春秋說 [[KR1e0113]] and matures in his son Huì Dòng's 惠棟 *Huì-shì Chūnqiū Zuǒ-zhuàn bǔ-zhù* 惠氏春秋左傳補註 [[KR1e0116]], with Shěn Tóng's 沈彤 *Chūnqiū Zuǒ-zhuàn xiǎo shū* 春秋左傳小疏 [[KR1e0117]] as its specialist philological complement. *Sān-zhuàn zhé-zhū* 三傳折諸 [[KR1e0107]] of Zhāng Shàngyuàn 張尚瑗 is the magnum opus of the Wú-Jiāng *yǐ jīng zhèng zhuàn* programme.

**Qīng anti-Hú reaction, Tóngchéng school, and reference apparatus (KR1e0105–0121).** *Chūnqiū dìmíng kǎo lüè* 春秋地名考略 [[KR1e0105]] of Gāo Shìqí 高士奇 is the imperial-period historical-geographical lexicon. *Chūnqiū shìzú pǔ* 春秋世族譜 [[KR1e0111]] of Chén Hòuyào 陳厚耀 reconstructs *Chūnqiū* prosopography; *Chūnqiū chánglì* 春秋長歷 [[KR1e0112]] of the same author corrects Dù Yù's calendrical scheme by one intercalation and is the defining work of Qīng *Chūnqiū* calendrical reconstruction. *Sān-zhèng kǎo* 三正考 [[KR1e0119]] of Wú Nài 吳鼐 settles the *sān zhèng* 三正 problem in favour of the Zhōu calendar. *Chūnqiū dìlǐ kǎo-shí* 春秋地理考實 [[KR1e0118]] of Jiāng Yǒng 江永 is the most concise authoritative eighteenth-century gazetteer. *Chūnqiū dà-shì biǎo* 春秋大事表 [[KR1e0114]] of Gù Dònggāo 顧棟高, in fifty-fold tabular form, is the largest single-author *Chūnqiū* reference work in the Chinese tradition and remains, alongside Yáng Bójùn 楊伯峻's annotated edition, indispensable. The Tóngchéng-school 桐城派 line — *Chūnqiū tōng-lùn* 春秋通論 [[KR1e0110]] of Fāng Bāo 方苞 and *Chūnqiū jiū yí* 春秋究遺 [[KR1e0120]] of Yè Yǒu 葉酉 — reroutes orthodox-Zhū *Chūnqiū* hermeneutic into prose-and-meaning literary practice. *Chūnqiū què-rú biān* 春秋闕如編 [[KR1e0108]] of Jiāo Yuánxī 焦袁熹 is the most elegant statement of the early-Qīng reaction against harsh-moralist exegesis.

## Important persons

- [[左丘明]] (trad. 6th–5th c. BCE) — the blind Lǔ scribe to whom Sīmǎ Qiān attributed the *Zuǒzhuàn*; modern scholarship (since Gù Yánwǔ) treats the work as composed by several hands over the late Warring States period (Wilkinson §48.1.1.1).
- [[公羊高]] (trad. 5th c. BCE) and [[穀梁赤]] (trad. 5th c. BCE) — eponymous traditional founders of the *Gōngyáng* and *Gǔliáng* commentarial traditions. Both works appear in our textual record only in early Western Hàn ([[KR1e0006]], [[KR1e0008]]).
- [[董仲舒]] (179–104 BCE) — Western-Hàn *Gōngyáng*-school theorist whose *Chūnqiū fánlù* [[KR1e0122]] supplied the cosmological-political framework of Hàn imperial Confucianism.
- [[何休]] (129–182) — Eastern-Hàn *Gōngyáng* champion; author of *Chūnqiū Gōngyáng jīng-zhuàn jiě-gǔ* [[KR1e0007]] and the polemical works that triggered Zhèng Xuán's rebuttal in [[KR1e0011]].
- [[鄭玄]] (127–200) — Eastern-Hàn polymath; in this division represented by *Zhēn gāohuāng* [[KR1e0011]], the rebuttal of Hé Xiū's *Gōngyáng*-supremacist polemics.
- [[杜預]] (222–284) — Jìn-period commander and *Zuǒ*-scholar; *Chūnqiū jīng-zhuàn jíjiě* [[KR1e0002]] integrated the *Chūnqiū* into the *Zuǒzhuàn* and made Dù Yù's reading the default for the next 1,700 years; his *Chūnqiū shìlì* [[KR1e0012]] is the systematic regulatory companion.
- [[范甯]] (339–401) — Eastern-Jìn author of the *Gǔliáng jí jiě* [[KR1e0009]], the foundational extant commentary on the *Gǔliáng zhuàn*.
- [[陸德明]] (ca. 550–630) — author of the *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文; supplies the *yīn-yì* 音義 layer integrated into [[KR1e0002]], [[KR1e0004]], [[KR1e0006]], [[KR1e0007]], [[KR1e0009]], [[KR1e0010]].
- [[孔穎達]] (574–648) — Táng director of the *Wǔ-jīng zhèngyì* project; lead sub-commentator on the *Zuǒzhuàn* [[KR1e0003]], [[KR1e0004]].

## Topics

- **Did Confucius edit the *Chūnqiū*?** The presupposition that the canonical text encodes Confucius's *wēi-yán dàyì* drives almost the entire *bāo-biǎn* tradition (Wilkinson §48.1.1). Most pre-modern commentators in this division accept this premise; the radical sceptical line — Wáng Ān-shí's "fragmentary court gazette" remark, the Dàn-Zhào school, Zhào Péngfēi [[KR1e0052]], Chéng Duānxué [[KR1e0060]]–[[KR1e0062]], down through Lù Càn [[KR1e0078]] and Wáng Fūzhī [[KR1e0097]] — challenges it. The modern consensus that the *Chūnqiū*'s "omissions and variations are the result of natural causes" rather than cryptographic praise-and-blame (Kennedy 1942; Van Auken 2008; Wilkinson §48.1.1.1) is in effect a vindication of the radical sceptical line.
- **The relative authority of the three commentaries.** The Hàn-period dispute over which of the three commentaries to privilege (Hé Xiū vs. Zhèng Xuán in [[KR1e0007]] / [[KR1e0011]]) is the foundational version of a problem that recurs in every period: Dù Yù's Jìn-period commitment to *Zuǒ* primacy [[KR1e0002]], Lù Chún's Táng rejection of all three [[KR1e0013]]–[[KR1e0015]], the Sòng promotion of Hú Ānguó [[KR1e0036]] *as* a fourth tradition, the Qīng restoration of *Zuǒ*-primary historiography under the Wú-Jiāng school [[KR1e0096]] / [[KR1e0107]] / [[KR1e0116]].
- **The *Zuǒzhuàn* as historical narrative vs. as commentary.** Already in the Hàn the *Zuǒ* was understood to be qualitatively different from the *Gōngyáng* and *Gǔliáng* — a fully-narrative pre-Hàn historical work attached secondarily to the *Chūnqiū* (Wilkinson §48.1.1.1). The Sòng-Qīng tradition repeatedly mines this difference, either to elevate the *Zuǒ* as the prime source for the period (Lǚ Zǔqiān's *Zuǒ*-trilogy [[KR1e0039]]–[[KR1e0041]]; Mǎ Sù [[KR1e0101]]) or to attack it as a forgery interpolated to look like a commentary (Chéng Duānxué [[KR1e0060]]–[[KR1e0062]]).
- ***Bāo-biǎn* versus admonition / teaching.** The *yǒu-biǎn wú-bāo* programme of Sūn Fù [[KR1e0018]] sets one extreme; Huáng Zhòngyán's *Chūnqiū* as a book of *teaching and admonition* [[KR1e0050]] sets a different one; Lǚ Dàguī [[KR1e0053]] and Jiāo Yuánxī [[KR1e0108]] reject *bāo-biǎn* altogether. The political stakes — what the *Chūnqiū* authorises a sovereign to praise or condemn — make the question structural to imperial ritual reading, especially in the Hú-school readings under Jīn / Mongol / Qīng pressure.
- **Calendrical hermeneutics.** Cuī Zǐfāng's *rì-yuè lì* theory [[KR1e0028]]–[[KR1e0029]] holds that calendrical detail in each entry encodes *bāo-biǎn*; the Qīng rebuttal proceeds via independent calendrical reconstruction (Chén Hòuyào *Chūnqiū chánglì* [[KR1e0112]]), which corrects Dù Yù's scheme by one intercalation, and via the *sān zhèng* 三正 settlement of Wú Nài [[KR1e0119]] — clinching the case that the *Chūnqiū* uses the Zhōu calendar straight, without hermeneutic encoding. Zhāng Yǐníng [[KR1e0072]] had already used calendrical evidence against Hú Ānguó's *yǐ Xià-shí guàn Zhōu-yuè* doctrine.
- **Topical / categorical reorganisation.** A long sub-tradition recasts the *Chūnqiū* (or the *Zuǒzhuàn*) by topic rather than chronology: Zhāng Dàhēng under the *wǔ lǐ* [[KR1e0030]], Lǚ Zǔqiān under nineteen topical heads [[KR1e0043]], Chéng Gōng-shuō *Shǐjì*-style [[KR1e0045]], Qí Lǚqiān state-by-state [[KR1e0059]], Wú Chéng under seven cosmic-ritual heads [[KR1e0057]], Mǎ Sù under 108 headings [[KR1e0101]], Gù Dònggāo in fifty tables [[KR1e0114]]. This is one of the division's most productive genres and the proximate ancestor of modern reference works on the period.
- **Reference apparatus: prosopography, geography, calendar.** The Qīng evidential turn produced a triple reference apparatus: prosopography (Chén Hòuyào *Chūnqiū shìzú pǔ* [[KR1e0111]], Chéng Tíngzuò *Chūnqiū shíxiǎo lù* [[KR1e0115]]); historical geography (Gāo Shìqí [[KR1e0105]], Jiāng Yǒng [[KR1e0118]]); calendar (Chén Hòuyào *Chūnqiū chánglì* [[KR1e0112]], Wú Nài [[KR1e0119]]). Together these make the *Chūnqiū* corpus continuously usable as a primary historical source.
- **Hú Ānguó's six-century institutional reign.** From its 1313 elevation to the official examination commentary through the Yǒnglè *Dà-quán* [[KR1e0074]] to the high-Qīng, Hú Ānguó's *Chūnqiū zhuàn* [[KR1e0036]] was the orthodox state textbook. The Míng-Qīng landscape of the division is in large measure a landscape of stances toward Hú: Wāng Kèkuān *fù-lù zuǎn-shū* [[KR1e0071]], Lù Càn anti-Hú [[KR1e0078]], Yuán Rén *kǎo-wù* [[KR1e0085]], the Qīng imperial trilogy [[KR1e0093]]–[[KR1e0095]] keeping Hú as anchor while purging extremes, Xú Tíngyuán anti-Hú reaction [[KR1e0106]].
- **Dōnglín and Tóngchéng readings.** The late-Míng Dōnglín circle (Gāo Pānlóng [[KR1e0088]]) and the early-to-mid-Qīng Tóngchéng school (Fāng Bāo [[KR1e0110]], Yè Yǒu [[KR1e0120]]) supply two distinct moralist *Chūnqiū* hermeneutics that bridge between Sòng *yìlǐ* readings and Qīng evidential philology.
