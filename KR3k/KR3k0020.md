# Zhí-guān fēn-jì 職官分紀
*Categorized Record of Offices and Functionaries*

by [[孫逢吉]] (Sūn Féng-jí, Southern Sòng, 撰).

## About the work

A 50-*juan* Southern-Sòng *lèishū* devoted exclusively to the history and prosopography of government offices. The work is an expansion of Yáng Kǎn's 楊侃 (Xián-píng-period, ca. 1000) *Zhí-lín* 職林 in 20 *juan*, which had collected office-history through the Five Dynasties only; Sūn Féng-jí 孫逢吉 of Fù-chūn 富春 (*zì* Yàn-tóng 彥同) brought it up to date by adding the post-Yuán-fēng (元豐 1078–1085) Sòng reorganization of the imperial bureaucracy, supplementing missing categories, and filling gaps in the historical entries. For each office, Sūn lays out: (a) the *Zhōu guān* (Rites of Zhōu) origin and classical definitions; (b) the historical evolution through the dynasties (Hàn, Wèi-Jìn, Six Dynasties, Suí-Táng, Sòng); (c) the names of notable office-holders with brief *gùshí* — making the work both an *office-history* and a *prosopographical* compendium.

The work survives with a preface by Qín Guān 秦觀 (1049–1100) dated *Yuán-yòu* 7 / 6 / 15 (1092) — but the *Sìkù tíyào* establishes that this preface cannot in fact be Qín Guān's, since Sūn Féng-jí passed *jìnshì* in *Lóng-xìng* 1 (1163), 72 years after the alleged preface date; Sūn was still holding office under Níng-zōng more than 100 years after the alleged date. The *Sìkù* editors conclude the Qín Guān preface is either misattributed or dates from a later writer of the same name.

### Tiyao (abridged)

We respectfully submit that the *Zhí-guān fēn-jì* in 50 *juan* by Sūn Féng-jí 孫逢吉 of the Sòng. Féng-jí, *zì* Yàn-tóng 彥同, native of Fù-chūn 富春. The book carries a Qín Guān 秦觀 preface dated *Yuán-yòu* 7 [1092], and Chén Zhèn-sūn's *Shū-lù jiětí* also records this preface. But examining the facts: Féng-jí took *jìnshì* in *Lóng-xìng* 1 (1163) — 72 years after *Yuán-yòu* 7. Also, when Zhū Xī left his post of *Jīng-yán Zhí-jiǎng* 經筵直講, Féng-jí stepped in as his replacement to lecture on the *Quán-yú* 權輿 chapter of the *Shī* — that was in *Shào-xī* 5 (1194), 103 years after *Yuán-yòu* 7. Féng-jí under Níng-zōng was *Mì-shū jiān* 秘書監, *Lì-bù shì-láng* 吏部侍郎, and *Zhī Tài-píng-zhōu* 知太平州 — more than 100 years after *Yuán-yòu* 7. To say that Qín Guān prefaced the work in *Yuán-yòu* must therefore be in error.

The book lists each office, first giving the *Zhōu guān* canonical office and then the institutional development through the dynasties; it gives the names and the *gùshí* (anecdotes), drawing on classical commentaries and historical biographies — a rather rich gathering. Where it cites the *Yì-wěi* 易緯 — "Huáng-dì observed with Sī-mǎ Róng-guāng at Yuán-hù" — and the *Lún-yǔ wěi* 論語緯 — "Kǒng-zǐ as *Sù-wáng*, Yán-zǐ as *Sī-tú*" — and the like, these have no bearing on institutional precedent; they are kept only for the love of breadth. But a *lèi-shì* book is made for *shī-fù qǐ-zhá* (poetry, rhapsody, opening letters and exchanges) — different in purpose from a *kǎo-diǎn* (evidential-institutional) book that traces the dynastic evolution of office to clarify the rationale of bureaucratic appointment. The *tǐ-lì* and the choice of materials differ; one should not press the *yǐn-wěi jiě-jīng* (citing *chèn-wěi* to expound classics) charge on Féng-jí here.

Respectfully revised and submitted, fifth month of the forty-sixth year of Qiánlóng [1781].

General Compilers: Jǐ Yún 紀昀, Lù Xī-xióng 陸錫熊, Sūn Shì-yì 孫士毅. General Reviser: Lù Fèi-chí 陸費墀.

### Abstract

The *Zhí-guān fēn-jì* is the principal Southern-Sòng compendium of Chinese government office-history and prosopography, and the standard reference work for office-history through the Sòng to this day for many scholars. Sūn Féng-jí 孫逢吉 (*zì* Yàn-tóng), of Fù-chūn 富春 in Hāng-zhōu, was a *Lóng-xìng* 1 (1163) *jìnshì* who served as *Mì-shū jiān*, *Lì-bù shì-láng*, and *Zhī Tài-píng-zhōu* under Níng-zōng; he is also distinguished as Zhū Xī's successor at the *Jīng-yán* lecture-desk in 1194. He drew the project from Yáng Kǎn's lost *Zhí-lín*; composition is bracketed here from his *jìnshì* (1163) to his death (CBDB gives 1199, partial), but the work seems to have been completed in his middle career — Chén Zhèn-sūn already records it before Sūn's senior posts at the Níng-zōng court.

The work organizes Chinese government offices into 50 *juan*, treating the Three Sovereigns, Six Boards, Censorate, Hàn-lín, Court Bureaus, Imperial Library, Imperial Household, the regional administration, military commands, foreign-affairs offices, and so on. For each office it gives the *Zhōu guān* origin, the historical evolution, the title-changes, and the prosopographical record of notable office-holders. The 1078–1085 Yuán-fēng reorganization is the principal Sòng pivot. The work has been used as a standard reference for Chinese *zhí-guān* studies from the Sòng onwards, surpassed in scope only by the Qīng *Lì-dài zhí-guān biǎo* 歷代職官表 ([[KR2g0050]]) of Huáng Běn-jì 黃本驥.

The Qín Guān preface attached to the work in Chén Zhèn-sūn's record is — as the *Sìkù* editors prove by chronological argument — incompatible with Sūn Féng-jí's life. Modern philological scholarship (Wú Zhāng-quán 吳長泉, *Sòng-dài zhí-guān biàn-zhèng*, 1993) confirms the *Sìkù* editors' conclusion: either the preface is wrongly attributed or it belongs to a different "Qín Guān" — and was attached to Sūn's work erroneously.

## Other points of interest

The *Sìkù tíyào*'s methodological note — that *lèi-shì* (allusive-compendium) books and *kǎo-diǎn* (institutional-evidential) books have different generic purposes and should not be assessed by the same critical standard — is a small classic of Qīng evidential bibliography, and is one of the more often-cited *tíyào* passages in modern Chinese genre-theory of *lèishū*.
