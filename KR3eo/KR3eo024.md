# Yǎng-shēng yuè-lǎn 養生月覽
*Monthly Compendium for Nourishing Life*
compiled by [[周守忠]] Zhōu Shǒuzhōng (*fl.* Southern Sòng, *Jiā-dìng* era).

## About the work

A two-juan **month-by-month** *yǎng-shēng* anthology — the seasonal complement to the same author's much larger topical anthology *Yǎng-shēng lèi-zuǎn* ([[KR3eo022]]). The text proceeds calendrically from the first month through the twelfth, each month subdivided into entries for specific dates (the New Year's day, the *yuán-rì*, the *zǐ* hour of the first month, etc.) and seasonal observances. For each entry Zhōu cites a named source — *Yuè-lìng tú-jīng* 月令圖經, *Sì-shí zuǎn-yào* 四時纂要, *Zá Wǔ-xíng shū* 雜五行書, *Lǚ-gōng suì-shí zá-jì* 呂公歲時雜記, *Jīng-Chǔ suì-shí jì* 荊楚歲時記, *Suǒ-suì-lù* 瑣碎錄 of Wēn Gé 溫革, *Tài-píng yù-lǎn* 太平御覽, *Zhōu Chǔ Fēng-tǔ jì* 周處風土記, *Cuī-shí Sì-mín yuè-lìng* 崔實四民月令, *Yù-zhú bǎo-diǎn* 玉燭寶典 — many of these the principal pre-Sòng "seasonal almanac" (*yuè-lìng*) sources, several preserved chiefly through Zhōu's anthology.

### Prefaces

The hxwd reprint of the *Yuè-lǎn* preserves no separate xù; the work opens directly with *juàn shàng zhèng-yuè* 卷上正月 ("Upper Juan, First Month"). For the editorial *xù*-tradition see Hú Wénhuàn's joint preface to the *Yǎng-shēng lèi-zuǎn* ([[KR3eo022]]).

### Abstract

The *Yuè-lǎn* is one of the most important Southern-Sòng witnesses to the **early-medieval seasonal-almanac tradition**. Many of the specific *yuè-lìng* texts Zhōu Shǒuzhōng cites — particularly the *Yuè-lìng tú-jīng* 月令圖經, the *Sì-shí zuǎn-yào* 四時纂要 (the Táng / Five-Dynasties almanac attributed to *Hán È*), the *Lǚ-gōng suì-shí zá-jì* 呂公歲時雜記, the *Suǒ-suì-lù* 瑣碎錄, the *Zá xiū-yǎng shū* 雜修養書 — are partly or wholly preserved through Zhōu's anthology, making the *Yuè-lǎn* one of the principal modern sources for the reconstruction of these earlier traditions. The very first entry, citing the *Yuè-lìng tú-jīng* on the burning of dung at the *zǐ*-*chǒu* hour of the first lunar day, is itself a major piece of evidence for the *Yuè-lìng tú-jīng* — a text now lost except for fragments.

The work's organisation as a **regimen-by-date schedule** marks it as the prototype of the late-imperial popular almanac (*lì-shū* 曆書) tradition, in which the *yǎng-shēng* directives are tabulated against the days of the year. Zhōu's *Yuè-lǎn* effectively codified the **classical "yang-sheng calendar"** for subsequent Yuán-Míng compilers.

The dating bracket 1208–1220 reflects Zhōu's known active period in the *Jiā-dìng* era. The catalog meta records dynasty as 宋; the present record specifies 南宋.

## Other points of interest

The *Yuè-lǎn* is the principal medieval-Chinese *yǎng-shēng* almanac and the source of many of the calendrically-fixed observances (the *Tú-sū jiǔ* of the *yuán-rì*, the *jué-fǎn* 屠蘇酒 ritual of the New Year, the *zhú-yì* 桃逸 talismanic procedures, the *jí-bào-zhú* 庭前爆竹 firecracker tradition) that became part of the late-imperial popular calendar. The Hú Wénhuàn 1596 Qián-táng print made the work available again in late-Míng circulation, and the present hxwd recension is the most accessible modern reprint.
