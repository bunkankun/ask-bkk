# Chéng wéishí lùn liǎoyì dēng jì 成唯識論了義燈記
*Notes on the Lamp of Definitive Meaning of the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[智周]] (Zhìzhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle sub-commentary by [[智周]] on [[慧沼]]'s [[KR6n0030]] *Chéng wéishí lùn liǎoyì dēng* (T43n1832). Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X49n0812). The text glosses Huìzhāo's polemical commentary, supplementing the polemics against Yuáncè 圓測 and the Paramārtha-line *Shèlùn* doctrines with additional Cí'ēn-orthodox argumentation.

### Abstract

The work is one of three short sub-commentaries by [[智周]] on the Cí'ēn corpus on the *Chéng wéishí lùn* (alongside [[KR6n0035]] and [[KR6n0032]]). It belongs to the second generation of Cí'ēn polemic-against-rival-readings — the layer that consolidated the orthodox Cí'ēn position established by Huìzhāo against the Korean and Paramārthan alternatives. Together these works fixed the standard Cí'ēn reading of T1585 as it would be transmitted to Heian Hossō. The dating bracket (700–733) reflects Zhìzhōu's productive teaching period.
