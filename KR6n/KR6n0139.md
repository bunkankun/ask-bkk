# Bā shí guī-jǔ sòng zhù 八識規矩頌注
*Annotations on the Eight-Consciousnesses Verses*
by [[行舟]] (Xū-zhōu Xíng-zhōu, 註)

## About the work

A single-fascicle early-Qīng interlinear annotation on the *Bā shí guī-jǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌 by [[行舟]] **Xū-zhōu Xíng-zhōu**, companion piece to his [[KR6n0138]] *Bā shí guī-jǔ qiǎn-shuō*. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0897.

### Prefaces

No separate preface; the text opens directly with the first verse and Xíng-zhōu's running interlinear gloss in small-character notation: "*xìng-jìng xiàn-liàng tōng sān xìng* 性境現量通三性 (just because the *true nature* is unclear, all sorts of false natures arise)" — Xíng-zhōu's notes are characteristically Chán-flavoured rather than Cí'ēn-philological, treating each line as a meditation-prompt rather than as a doctrinal proposition.

### Abstract

The *Sòng zhù* is the more compact of Xíng-zhōu's pair on the *guī-jǔ*, providing a verse-by-verse annotation in interlinear small-character format. Each canonical verse-line is followed by a short Chán-style annotation that often takes the form of a doctrinal *gōng-àn* — pointing past the analytical content of the verse to the meditative situation it describes. This makes the *Sòng zhù* one of the more idiosyncratic *guī-jǔ* commentaries: closer to a Chán *yǔ-lù* in style than to the standard Cí'ēn philological commentary.

The dating window 1644–1700 brackets the early-Qīng productive period; lifedates of Xíng-zhōu are not securely attested.
