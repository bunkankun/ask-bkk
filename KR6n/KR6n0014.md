# Yúqié lùn jié-zhāng sòng 瑜伽論劫章頌
*Verses on the Kalpa-Sections of the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle versified summary by [[窺基]] (632–682) of the cosmological *kalpa* (劫) sections of the *Yúqié shī dì lùn* ([[KR6n0001]], T30n1579) — specifically the discussions of the eras of cosmic dissolution and reconstitution scattered through the *Běn-dì fēn* 本地分. The text condenses the relevant prose of the master treatise into mnemonic verse for memorisation by Cí'ēn-zōng / Fǎxiàng students. Preserved only in the *Xuzangjing* (X47n0794).

### Structural Division

CANWWW does not assign a TOC to X47n0794. The single fascicle consists of a continuous verse-text (*sòng* 頌) without subdivisions.

### Abstract

The *jié-zhāng* 劫章 ("kalpa sections") are the cosmological background to the path-discussions of the *Yogācārabhūmi*: the treatise's accounts of the *Saṃvarta-* and *Vivarta-kalpas*, of the levels of the desire-form-formless realms, and of the bodhisattva-careers extended over multiple aeons. Kuījī condensed this cosmological apparatus into versified form for ease of memorisation, complementing his much longer prose commentaries [[KR6n0008]] *Yúqié shī dì lùn lüèzuǎn* and the lost *Yúqié shī dì lùn shū*. The text is one of his three principal works on the *Yogācārabhūmi*.

The composition window adopted (660–682) covers Kuījī's mature teaching period at the Dà Cí'ēn-sì 大慈恩寺 in Cháng'ān. The text was transmitted to Sillan Korea and to Heian Japan, where it became a standard introductory verse-text for *Hossō* novices and is referenced in early Japanese Hossō works such as Zenju 善珠's *Yuga-ron-jichō-ke* 瑜伽論時頌科 (now lost).
