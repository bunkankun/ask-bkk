# Wéishí sānshí lùn zhí-jiě 唯識三十論直解
*Direct Exposition of the Thirty Verses on Consciousness-Only*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 解)

## About the work

A single-fascicle running commentary by the late-Míng Buddhist polymath [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 1599–1655) on Vasubandhu's *Triṃśikā* — the verse text preserved in Chinese as [[KR6n0022]] *Wéishí sānshí lùn sòng* 唯識三十論頌 (T31n1586). The "*zhí-jiě*" 直解 ("direct exposition") title indicates Zhìxù's preferred commentarial mode: lemma-by-lemma paraphrase in plain prose, without the apparatus of citation that characterises the Cí'ēn tradition. Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X51n0828).

Internally the text follows the standard Cí'ēn five-stage division of the *Triṃśikā* (the five stages of practice, *wǔ wèi* 五位: *zī-liáng wèi*, *jiā-xíng wèi*, *tōng-dá wèi*, *xiū-xí wèi*, *jiū-jìng wèi*) inherited from [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn*.

### Abstract

The opening of the work explicitly cites the Cí'ēn account ("護法等菩薩。約此三十頌造成唯識") of the genesis of the *Chéng wéishí lùn*, then proceeds verse by verse through Vasubandhu's thirty *kārikā*. Zhìxù's exegesis is markedly less polemical than the Cí'ēn tradition — he does not pursue the doctrinal disputes between Dharmapāla, Sthiramati, and the other "ten masters" that animate [[KR6n0016]] — but rather presents a clear pedagogical paraphrase, drawing on his syncretic position that integrates Tiāntái, Chán, Pure Land, and Yogācāra perspectives.

The *zhí-jiě* is one of three Yogācāra commentaries by Zhìxù alongside his works on the *Bǎi-fǎ míng-mén lùn* and his short Yogācāra lexicons. Together with [[KR6n0050]] *Wéishí sānshí lùn yuē-yì* 唯識三十論約意 by Míngyù 明昱 and the Cí'ēn-revival commentaries of Zhēn-jiè 真界 and Wáng Kěn-táng 王肯堂, it constitutes the late-Míng *Triṃśikā* commentarial corpus, marking a distinctive moment in Chinese Yogācāra scholarship after centuries of relative neglect.

The dating bracket adopted (1632–1655) covers the period from Zhìxù's first systematic doctrinal works to his death. The colophon does not give a precise date.
