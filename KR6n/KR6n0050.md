# Wéishí sānshí lùn yuēyì 唯識三十論約意
*Concise Meaning of the Thirty Verses on Consciousness-Only*
by [[明昱]] (Míngyù, 約意)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle commentary by the late-Míng Yogācāra revivalist [[明昱]] on [[KR6n0022]] *Wéishí sānshí lùn sòng* 唯識三十論頌 (T31n1586) — Vasubandhu's *Triṃśikā*. The "*yuē-yì*" 約意 ("compressed meaning") title indicates a concise verse-by-verse exposition designed for pedagogical use. Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X51n0827).

### Abstract

The work is one of [[明昱]]'s several Yogācāra commentaries (alongside [[KR6n0043]] *súquán* on [[KR6n0016]], [[KR6n0105]] *zhuì-yán* on the *Mahāyāna-śatadharma* [[KR6n0096]], [[KR6n0114]] *huì-shì* and [[KR6n0116]] *shì jì* on the *Ālambana-parīkṣā*, etc.). Together these constitute one of the principal late-Míng monuments of Yogācāra scholarship.

The *yuē-yì* differs from the standard Cí'ēn approach to the *Triṃśikā* in that it treats the verses as an independent text rather than as the verse-base of [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn*: Míngyù provides a stand-alone exegesis of each *kārikā*, drawing on his own reading of the *Triṃśikā* tradition rather than simply paraphrasing the *Chéng wéishí lùn* commentary. This is a methodological innovation of some interest — it is one of the very few late-imperial Chinese commentaries on the *Triṃśikā* as such, rather than as the *kārikā*-base of the longer treatise.

The dating bracket adopted (1582–1620) covers Míngyù's mature commentarial period.
