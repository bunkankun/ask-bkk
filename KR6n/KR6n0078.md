# Xiǎn-yáng shèng-jiào lùn sòng 顯揚聖教論頌
*Verses of the Treatise Manifesting the Holy Teaching*
by [[無著菩薩]] (Wúzhuó púsà = Asaṅga, 造) and [[玄奘]] (Xuánzàng, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle separate verse-text containing the *kārikā* of [[KR6n0077]] *Xiǎn-yáng shèng-jiào lùn* (T31n1602) extracted as a stand-alone aide-memoire. Translated by [[玄奘]] in 645–646, in the same campaign as the prose treatise. One fascicle.

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T31N1603) does not preserve internal structure. The single fascicle contains the *kārikā* of all eleven *pǐn* of the *Xiǎn-yáng* in a single continuous run.

### Abstract

The separate-verse-text format reflects standard Indian curricular practice of memorising the verse-base of a śāstra independently of its prose exposition. Although the verses reproduce material already present in [[KR6n0077]], they were extracted and translated separately by Xuánzàng's team for the convenience of advanced students.

The dating bracket follows the parallel translation of T1602 (645–646). The text is brief and is not cited extensively in the commentarial tradition — the prose work [[KR6n0077]] supplies the doctrinal substance and is the principal reference text.
