# Chéng wéishí lùn yīnxiǎng bǔyí kē 成唯識論音響補遺科
*Structural Outline of the Phonetic-and-Resonance Supplement to the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[智素]] (Zhìsù, 科)

## About the work

A two-fascicle topical-structural outline (*kē* 科, in the technical Chinese-Buddhist sense of a hierarchical *科判* "doctrinal sub-division") accompanying the same author's [[KR6n0049]] *Chéng wéishí lùn yīnxiǎng bǔyí* 成唯識論音響補遺 (X51n0826). The pair of works belongs to the early-Qīng continuation of the late-Míng Yogācāra revival. Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X51n0825).

### Abstract

The *kē* outline functions as a study-aid for the longer commentary [[KR6n0049]]: it sets out the topical organisation of the parent commentary in tabular form, which in turn tracks the structure of [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn*. The pairing of *kē* (outline) and main commentary is a standard late-Míng / early-Qīng commentarial convention, designed for the reading-practice of monastic study communities.

The dating bracket adopted (1644–1750) reflects the absence of internal evidence. Zhìsù is conventionally placed in the early-to-mid Qīng.
