# Chéng wéishí lùn shū chāo 成唯識論疏抄
*Excerpts on the Commentary on the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[靈泰]] (Língtài, 撰)

## About the work

A 16-fascicle sub-commentary by [[靈泰]] (Língtài), a direct disciple of [[窺基]], on Kuījī's [[KR6n0026]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shùjì* (T43n1830). Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X50n0819) and also in the late-Míng *Jiāxìng* canon supplement. The "*chāo*" 抄 ("excerpts") format indicates a topical extraction from the *shùjì* with elaborative comment — i.e. a study-aid for the principal Cí'ēn commentary rather than an independent work.

### Abstract

[[靈泰]]'s direct discipleship under [[窺基]] places him in the first generation of post-foundational Cí'ēn-sì commentators — earlier than [[慧沼]]'s mature output and contemporary with the early activity of Huìzhāo. The dating window adopted (680–720) covers the period from Kuījī's death in 682 (when Língtài began composing his independent works) through to the early eighth century. As a direct disciple, Língtài's transmission of Kuījī's reading is regarded as particularly reliable, and the text is repeatedly cited by later Cí'ēn commentators including [[智周]] in his [[KR6n0032]] *yǎn-mì*.

The *shū chāo* is one of the more substantial sub-commentaries on the *shùjì* and provides extensive material on the doctrinal cruces of T1585 / T1830 — particularly on the eight-consciousness scheme and on the bodhisattva-bhūmi correlations — that is not available in other extant first-generation Cí'ēn sources.
