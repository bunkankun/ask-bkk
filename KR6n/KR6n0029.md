# Chéng wéishí lùn zhǎngzhōng shūyào 成唯識論掌中樞要
*Essential Pivot in the Palm: Compendium of the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

A two-fascicle topical compendium by [[窺基]] on the *Chéng wéishí lùn* ([[KR6n0016]], T31n1585), distinct from the lemma-by-lemma [[KR6n0026]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shùjì* and from the lost full-length *shū* 疏. The "*zhǎng zhōng shū yào*" 掌中樞要 ("pivotal essentials in the palm of the hand") title indicates a portable doctrinal handbook: it presents the Cí'ēn-zōng's principal doctrinal positions (the eight consciousnesses, the three natures, the *amala-vijñāna* polemic, the bodhisattva-bhūmi system) in topical rather than textual order, designed for memorisation and oral exposition.

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T43N1831) lists [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* 成唯識論 (T31n1585) as the related text. The two *juǎn* are conventionally divided into upper and lower; internally they cover the six fundamental Yogācāra topics in encyclopedia form rather than tracking the master text.

### Abstract

The *zhǎngzhōng shūyào* is the third of [[窺基]]'s three principal commentaries on the *Chéng wéishí lùn*: alongside the lemma-by-lemma [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì* and the lost full *shū* 疏 (mentioned in [[KR2m0019]] *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* T2061, j. 4), the *shūyào* serves as the introductory and topical-encyclopedic counterpart. Together with [[KR6n0030]] *Chéng wéishí lùn liǎoyì dēng* (T1832) by Huìzhāo and [[KR6n0032]] *Chéng wéishí lùn yǎnmì* (T1833) by Zhìzhōu, it constitutes the canonical Cí'ēn "three commentaries" (*sān shū* 三疏) of the early Tang.

The dating window adopted (659–682) covers Kuījī's productive teaching period from the publication of the master text through to his death.

The work was a standard textbook for Hossō study in Heian Japan and is referenced in nearly every Japanese sub-commentary on the *Chéng wéishí lùn*. The companion text [[KR6n0035]] *Chéng wéishí lùn zhǎngzhōng shūyào jì* (X49n0810) by Zhìzhōu is the principal sub-commentary.
