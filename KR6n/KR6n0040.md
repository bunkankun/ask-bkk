# Zhù chéng wéishí lùn 註成唯識論
*Annotated Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*

## About the work

A single-fascicle anonymous annotated text of [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* — i.e. an interlinear commentary giving short glosses on the master text rather than a freestanding commentary. Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X50n0817) without attribution to a named author. The genre (*zhù* 註, "annotation") is the most basic form of commentarial text, providing the immediate semantic gloss on technical terms and difficult phrases.

### Abstract

The text is one of several anonymous Cí'ēn-tradition annotations on T1585 preserved in the *Xuzangjing*; on internal grounds it is generally placed in the Tang period, possibly in the late-Tang. The dating bracket adopted (700–900) reflects the absence of internal dating evidence. The work's principal interest is as evidence of the practical reading-tradition — the kind of marginal annotation a student would have used at the lecture-bench — that surrounded the formal Cí'ēn commentaries.
