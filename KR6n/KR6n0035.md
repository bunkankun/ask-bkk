# Chéng wéishí lùn zhǎng-zhōng shū-yào jì 成唯識論掌中樞要記
*Notes on the Essential Pivot in the Palm: Compendium of the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[智周]] (Zhìzhōu, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle sub-commentary by [[智周]] (678–733) on Kuījī's [[KR6n0029]] *Chéng wéishí lùn zhǎng-zhōng shū-yào* (T43n1831). Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X49n0810). The text glosses Kuījī's compendious presentation of the topical-doctrinal organisation of the *Chéng wéishí lùn*, providing additional citations from the master treatise itself ([[KR6n0016]]) and from the lemma-by-lemma [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì*.

### Abstract

The text is one of [[智周]]'s several short sub-commentaries on Kuījī's Cí'ēn corpus, alongside [[KR6n0036]] *Chéng wéishí lùn liǎo-yì dēng jì* on Huìzhāo's *liǎo-yì dēng* and [[KR6n0032]] *yǎn-mì* on the master treatise itself. Together these works trace the second-generation Cí'ēn reading of T1585: where Kuījī's commentaries set out the basic doctrinal framework, Zhìzhōu's sub-commentaries refine technical details and resolve emerging interpretive disputes within the school.

The dating bracket (700–733) follows Zhìzhōu's productive teaching period.
