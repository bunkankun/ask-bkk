# Dà-shèng fǎ-yuàn yì-lín zhāng bǔ-què 大乘法苑義林章補闕
*Filling the Gaps in the Compendium of Doctrinal-Forest Essays*
by [[慧沼]] (Zī-ēn-sì Huì-zhǎo, 撰)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Táng Cí'ēn-school sub-commentary by [[慧沼]] **Huì-zhǎo** (650–714), Kuījī's principal disciple and the second patriarch of the Cí'ēn-school, on his teacher's [[KR6n0124]] *Dà-shèng fǎ-yuàn yì-lín zhāng* 大乘法苑義林章. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0882 (= ZB6n0113). The function-marker *bǔ-què* 補闕 ("filling the gaps") indicates that the work supplies essays on topics where Kuījī's parent compendium was either silent or compressed beyond clarity.

### Structural Division

The text is structured as a series of additional doctrinal essays, the surviving fascicle 4 covering the *Sān-kē zhāng* 三科章 (essay on the three categories — *skandha*, *āyatana*, *dhātu*), *Dé-fēi-dé zhāng* 得非得章 (the essay on attainment and non-attainment), *Kōng-yì zhāng* 空義章 (the essay on emptiness), and *Shí-èr zhī zhāng* 十二支章 (the essay on the twelve links of dependent origination). Fascicles 7 and 8 of the source survival cover further specialised topics. The Manji recension preserves only fascicles 4, 7, and 8 — the bulk of the original three-fascicle structure.

### Prefaces

Authorship line: "*Dà-yún-sì shā-mén Huì-zhǎo zhuàn* 大雲寺沙門慧沼撰" — by the Dà-yún-sì monk Huì-zhǎo. The text opens directly with the additional essays; there is no separate authorial preface in the surviving recension.

### Abstract

The *Bǔ-què* is the single most important Cí'ēn-school sub-commentary on the *Yì-lín zhāng*, supplementing Kuījī's coverage with detailed expositions of doctrinal topics (the *abhidharma*-school traditional categories of *sān-kē*, the analytic of *dé* / *fēi-dé* in *abhidharma* psychology, the Yogācāra reading of the twelve-link *pratītya-samutpāda*) where Kuījī's text is either silent or compressed. The work is a key witness to the second-generation Cí'ēn-school's effort to consolidate Kuījī's compendium into a complete doctrinal reference.

[[慧沼]] Huì-zhǎo (650–714, *zì* Xuán-shū 玄樞, sobriquet **Zī-ēn-sì** 淄恩寺 / Sūshēng-sì 蘇生寺) was Kuījī's chief disciple and the second patriarch of the Cí'ēn-school, succeeding Kuījī at *Cí'ēn-sì* and later moving to *Dà-yún-sì* 大雲寺. His full corpus includes [[KR6n0127]] *Quàn-fā pú-tí xīn jí* 勸發菩提心集 (T45n1862) and [[KR6n0128]] *Néng xiǎn zhōng-biān huì-rì lùn* 能顯中邊慧日論 (T45n1863). The dating window 682 (Kuījī's death) to 714 (Huìzhǎo's death) is the period within which Huìzhǎo could have composed the *Bǔ-què*.
