# Bā shí guī-jǔ zhí-jiě 八識規矩直解
*Direct Explanation of the Eight-Consciousnesses Verses*
by [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 解)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the *Bā shí guī-jǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌 by [[智旭]] **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** (1599–1655). Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0895. Part of Zhìxù's *zhí-jiě* set of compact direct expositions of the late-Míng Yogācāra core curriculum, alongside [[KR6n0106]] *Bǎi-fǎ míng-mén lùn zhí-jiě*, [[KR6n0115]] *Guān suǒ-yuán-yuán lùn zhí-jiě*, [[KR6n0117]] *Guān suǒ-yuán-yuán lùn shì zhí-jiě*, and [[KR6n0145]] *Xiàng-zōng bā yào zhí-jiě*.

### Prefaces

Authorship line: "*Sān-zàng fǎ-shī Xuán-zàng zuò* 三藏法師玄奘作 (composed by Tripiṭaka Master Xuán-zàng) / *Ǒuyì shā-mén Zhì-xù jiě* 蕅益沙門智旭解 (explicated by the Ǒuyì monk Zhì-xù)." No separate preface; the text opens directly with the first verse ("*xìng-jìng xiàn-liàng tōng sān xìng* 性境現量通三性") and the running commentary.

### Abstract

The *Zhí-jiě* is the most concise and pedagogically polished of the late-Míng *guī-jǔ* commentaries — designed for direct use in teaching. Zhìxù reads each verse with a brief, doctrinally precise gloss, with the broader integrative *xìng-xiàng* synthesis kept in the background and the foreground occupied by clear definition and example. Where Hānshān's [[KR6n0135]] *Tōng-shuō* opens the text out into the *yī-xīn* doctrine and Pǔ-tài's [[KR6n0131]] *Bǔ-zhù* loads the verses with the full Cí'ēn-school technical apparatus, Zhìxù's *Zhí-jiě* finds the middle path: each verse is glossed precisely enough for the doctrine to be unambiguous, but compactly enough for the student to retain the verse as a mnemonic. This is the *guī-jǔ* commentary most frequently used in modern Chinese Buddhist seminaries.

The dating window 1630–1655 brackets Zhìxù's productive period after his major doctrinal turn around 1631.
