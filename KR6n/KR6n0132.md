# Bā shí guī-jǔ bǔ-zhù zhèng-yì 八識規矩補註證義
*Doctrinal Verification of the Supplementary Annotations on the Eight-Consciousnesses Verses*
by [[明昱]] (Gāo-yuán Míngyù, 證義)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng sub-commentary by [[明昱]] **Gāo-yuán Míngyù** on Pǔ-tài's [[KR6n0131]] *Bā shí guī-jǔ bǔ-zhù*. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0890. The function-marker *zhèng-yì* 證義 ("doctrinal verification") indicates a sub-commentary that *verifies* (in both the assertive and the philological senses) Pǔ-tài's doctrinal positions, citing supporting authority from the broader Cí'ēn-school corpus.

### Prefaces

The text opens with a substantial preface — *Bā shí guī-jǔ bǔ-zhù zhèng-yì xù* 八識規矩補註證義序 (No. 890-A) — that situates the entire *xìng-xiàng* problematic in the historical narrative of Indian and Chinese Buddhist thought: from the Buddha's *jué-huáng* 覺皇 (sovereign-of-awakening) original synthesis, through the post-*parinirvāṇa* Indian split between Mañjuśrī's and Maitreya's lineages (i.e. between *xìng* and *xiàng*), through Nāgārjuna and Vasubandhu's separate restorations, into the Chinese tradition where *xìng* and *xiàng* "share the cloud and the moon but each its own stream and mountain." Vasubandhu's *Bā-shí* synthesis "extracts the *Yújiā*" doctrine and condenses it into the eight-consciousness scheme; Pǔ-tài's *Bǔ-zhù* recovered this for late Míng practice; Míngyù's *Zhèng-yì* now verifies and corroborates Pǔ-tài's exposition.

### Abstract

The *Zhèng-yì* tracks Pǔ-tài's *Bǔ-zhù* verse by verse, supplying additional doctrinal authority from the *Chéng wéishí lùn*, the *Yújiā shī-dì lùn*, and the standard Cí'ēn-school glossary. Where Pǔ-tài's interpretation could be read in more than one way, Míngyù argues for the philologically more conservative and Cí'ēn-orthodox reading. The work is a key witness to Míngyù's principal late-Míng Yogācāra philological project: re-establishing the strict Cí'ēn-school reading of the *guī-jǔ* tradition as against the more ecumenical readings being developed in parallel by Hānshān ([[KR6n0135]]) and Zhìxù ([[KR6n0137]]).

The dating window 1590–1620 brackets Míngyù's productive Wàn-lì period.
