# Xiǎnshí lùn 顯識論
*Treatise on the Manifestation of Consciousness*
by [[真諦]] (Zhēndì / Paramārtha, 譯)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Yogācāra treatise translated by [[真諦]] in his late-life Guǎngzhōu period (557–569). Closely related in subject and translator to the [[KR6n0024]] *Zhuǎn shí lùn* 轉識論, the *Xiǎnshí lùn* expounds the Yogācāra doctrine of the *xiǎn shì* 顯識 ("manifest" or "appearing" consciousness) — i.e. the eight consciousnesses considered as the manifest products of the *ālaya*-storehouse rather than as independent entities. The Sanskrit original is not preserved.

### Abstract

[[真諦]] arrived in Guǎngzhōu in 557 after a complicated career across the Liáng-Chén transition, and during the dozen-odd years before his death in 569 he produced what is commonly considered the most important corpus of pre-Xuánzàng Yogācāra translations: the [[KR6n0006]] *Juédìngzàng lùn* 決定藏論, the [[KR6n0024]] *Zhuǎn shí lùn* 轉識論, the present [[KR6n0025]] *Xiǎnshí lùn* 顯識論, the [[KR6n0054]] *Dàshèng wéishí lùn* 大乘唯識論, the [[KR6n0059]] *Shè dàshèng lùn* 攝大乘論, and the [[KR6n0061]] *Shè dàshèng lùn shì* 攝大乘論釋. Together they constitute the textual basis of the *Shèlùn-zōng* 攝論宗 — the principal Chinese Yogācāra school of the late sixth and early seventh century, before its supersession by the Cí'ēn-zōng / Fǎxiàng of [[玄奘]] and [[窺基]].

The *Xiǎnshí lùn* is doctrinally closer to T1587 than to T1585: it presents Yogācāra in Paramārtha's distinctive idiom — using *amala-vijñāna* 阿摩羅識, the *zhuǎn yī* 轉依, and the doctrine of the eight consciousnesses as cyclic transformations of an underlying immaculate ninth consciousness. The text is one of the principal sources for the doctrinal reading of Paramārtha that was preserved in the *Tiāntái* and *Huáyán* traditions even after the Cí'ēn supersession.

The dynasty tag in the catalog is 陳 (Chén dynasty, 557–589), reflecting the political situation in Guǎngzhōu during Paramārtha's late translation work.
