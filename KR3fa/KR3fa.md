# KR3fa 天文 — Tiānwén (Astronomy)

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3fa 天文 (*Tiānwén*) is the astronomy sub-division of KR3f 天文算法類 (*Tiānwén suànfǎ lèi*), the sixth category of the 子部 (Masters) under the *Sìkù quánshū* classification — the category Wilkinson §38.17 and §39 identify as the institutional home of pre-modern Chinese mathematical-astronomical literature. Where the SKQS subdivision proper ([[KR3f0001]] *Zhōubì suànjīng*, [[KR3f0002]] *Xīn-yí xiàng fǎ yào*, [[KR3f0008]] *Gǔjīn lǜlì kǎo*, [[KR3f0009]] *Qián-kūn tǐ-yì*, etc.) gathers the canon-quality texts, KR3fa is the Kyoto-University supplementary corpus — works preserved through the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*, the *Sìkù wèi-shōu shū jí-kān*, and the modern *Zhōngguó kēxué jìshù diǎnjí tōnghuì* reprints (refid prefix `KX03-07-` and `KXDLJ003`) — that fill in the astronomical literature *around* the imperial canon: commentaries on the canonical *Zhōubì*, Táng-Sòng popular and operational texts, Yuán-Míng didactic and asterism handbooks, the Late-Míng natural-philosophical encounter with the Jesuit mission, and the long Qīng debate over the Tychonic-Keplerian reform of the Imperial Astronomical Bureau (Wilkinson §39.6.2, §39.17).

The chronological span runs from the Hàn *Gǔ Zhōubì suànjīng* with Zhào Shuǎng's commentary (the foundational document of the *gàitiān shuō* 蓋天說 cosmology, Wilkinson §39.1.1) through the Táng asterism-mnemonic tradition of the *Bù-tiān gē*, the Sòng *Sī-tiān-jiān* horological and *yī-jīng* compilation traditions, the Yuán-Míng *xīng-zhàn* and operational-calendar handbooks for the *Shòu-shí* / *Dà-tǒng lì* system, and culminating in the dense Qīng triangle of (i) imperial-bureau Jesuit astronomy (Verbiest, Grimaldi, the *Yíxiàng kǎochéng* sequence), (ii) Chinese-scholarly natural-philosophical responses to Western cosmology (Xióng Míngyù, Jiē Xuān, Wú Sùgōng, Jiāng Yǒng), and (iii) the *xī-xué zhōng-yuán* 西學中源 synthesist programme (Xǔ Guìlín, Jiāo Tínghù, Wáng Jiābì). The terminal works are the 1842–1844 *Yíxiàng kǎochéng xùbiān* — the last pre-modernisation Chinese-imperial star-catalogue — and the early-Tóngzhì-Guāngxù treatises that adapt Cassini-Lacaille European reference astronomy for a Chinese readership.

## Important texts and text clusters

The 38 cataloged works fall into seven readily recognisable clusters.

**Zhōubì commentarial cluster.** Three of the works are direct commentaries on the canonical *Zhōubì suànjīng* [[KR3f0001]], the *gàitiān shuō* foundation-text and first of the *Suànjīng shíshū* 算經十書.
- *Gǔ Zhōubì suànjīng* 古周髀算經 [[KR3fa002]] — Zhào Shuǎng's 趙爽 Hàn-Wèi commentary, the earliest substantive *Zhōubì* exegesis and the principal vehicle by which the gnomon-shadow cosmology was transmitted into the Suí-Táng curriculum.
- *Zhōubì suànjīng yīnyì* 周髀算經音義 [[KR3fa001]] — Lǐ Jí's 李籍 Sòng phonological-and-semantic apparatus, attached to every modern critical edition of the parent text.
- *Zhōubì suànjīng jiàokān jì* 周髀算經校勘記 [[KR3fa003]] — Ruǎn Yuán's 阮元 Qīng evidential-philological collation (1773–1812), part of his comprehensive *Suànjīng shíshū* recovery programme.

**Táng popular and asterism cluster.** Two short Táng works mark the genre's earliest popular-science register, both recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn*.
- *Xiàng yǔ shū* 相雨書 [[KR3fa004]] — Huáng Zǐfā's 黃子發 hundred-entry meteorological pocket-book, transmitting the empirical proto-meteorological tradition that runs parallel to the systematic *fēn-yě* astrological literature.
- *Bù-tiān gē* 步天歌 [[KR3fa005]] — Wáng Xīmíng's 王希明 Kāiyuán-era (713–741) seven-character verse-mnemonic for the three enclosures and twenty-eight lodges; the closely related Dūnhuáng star chart S.3326 is the oldest surviving graphical sky-map in any culture.

**Sòng horological cluster.** Two clepsydra treatises preserve the desk-top register of the *Sī-tiān-jiān* time-keeping tradition that culminates in [[蘇頌]] Sū Sòng's famous astronomical clock-tower at Kāifēng (1086–92).
- *Zhǔnzhāi xīnzhì jǐlòu túshì* 準齋心制幾漏圖式 [[KR3fa006]] — Sūn Féngjí's 孫逢吉 Southern-Sòng compensated-flow desktop clepsydra with diagrams.
- *Tóng-hú lòu-jiàn zhìdù* 銅壺漏箭制度 [[KR3fa007]] — anonymous Sòng companion text on the bronze-pot floating-indicator clepsydra.

**Sòng-Yuán classical and didactic cluster.** Two works that anchor the philological-classical and the mnemonic-pedagogical registers of the genre.
- *Liùjīng tiānwén biān* 六經天文編 [[KR3fa008]] — [[王應麟]] Wáng Yīnglín's classicising compilation of astronomical material from the Five Classics + *Yuèjīng*, gathering Hàn-Táng commentarial scholia on the canonical astronomical passages.
- *Tiānwén jīngyì fù* 天文精義賦 [[KR3fa009]] — Yuè Xīzài's 岳煕載 Yuán-period rhapsody on the essentials of astronomy, in the *fù* didactic-versified register.

**Yuán-Míng operational-calendar and star-chart cluster.** The largest single group in the division: handbooks, atlases and astrological encyclopedias supporting the *Shòu-shí* / *Dà-tǒng lì* system before its Jesuit-era displacement.
- *Jiāoshí / Rìshí / Yuèshí / Sìyú / Wǔxīng tōngguǐ* 交食通軌等五種 [[KR3fa017]] — Yuán Tǒng's 元統 1384–1390 operational eclipse-and-planetary computation manuals for the Hóng-wǔ promulgation of the renamed *Dà-tǒng lì*; the principal evidence for the practical operation of the [[郭守敬]] Guō Shǒujìng *Shòu-shí* apparatus.
- *Tiānwén lüè* 天文略 [[KR3fa010]] — Cài Rǔnán's 蔡汝楠 mid-Míng introductory survey.
- *Shéndào dàbiān xiàngzōng huátiān wǔxīng* 神道大編象宗華天五星 [[KR3fa011]] — Zhōu Yún's 周雲 eleven-*juǎn* late-Míng astrological encyclopaedia, packaging Sòng-Yuán *xīng-zhàn* material and the *Kāiyuán zhànjīng* tradition for court and élite-amateur readers.
- *Sānyuán qīzhèng èrshíbāxiù zhōutiān jīngjiàn* 三垣七政二十八宿周天精鑒 [[KR3fa013]] — Zhāng Shìchún's 章士純 Míng asterism-and-planet reference.
- *Tiānxiàng yí quántú* 天象儀全圖 [[KR3fa014]], *Tiānwén túshuō* 天文圖說 [[KR3fa015]], *Tiānwén sānshíliù quántú* 天文三十六全圖 [[KR3fa016]], *Tiānguān tú* 天官圖 [[KR3fa012]] (Qīng) — short illustrated atlases of celestial-instrument and asterism material.
- *Sānyuán lièshè rùxiù qùjí jí* 三垣列舍入宿去極集 [[KR3fa018]] — anonymous Míng table of right-ascensions and declinations for the three enclosures and twenty-eight lodges.
- *Rì-yuè-xīng guǐshì* 日月星晷式 [[KR3fa020]] — anonymous Míng treatise on sundials of the sun, moon and stars.

**Late-Míng to Qīng Jesuit-encounter cluster.** The largest substantive cluster in the division: a dense engagement between the imperial-bureau Jesuit astronomers, their Chinese natural-philosophical interlocutors, and the polemicists who rejected the reform.
- *Gé-zhì cǎo* 格致草 [[KR3fa019]] — Xióng Míngyù's 熊明遇 1628–1644 synthesis of Aristotelian-Ptolemaic-Tychonic European cosmology with Sòng-Míng *lǐ-xué* and *qì*-cosmology; the foundational Chinese natural-philosophical text of the Jesuit encounter.
- *Xīn-zhì línɡtái yíxiàng zhì* 新制靈臺儀象志 [[KR3fa021]] — [[南懷仁]] Verbiest's 1674 sixteen-*juǎn* technical-and-engineering manual for the six bronze instruments he constructed at the Beijing observatory; the principal operational document of the post-1645 *Xīyáng xīn-fǎ lìshū* 西洋新法曆書 imperial calendar.
- *Bùdé yǐ* 不得已 [[KR3fa028]] — [[楊光先]] Yáng Guāngxiān's 1664–1665 polemic against the Jesuit reform, written during his brief Bureau directorship after Schall's deposition; the principal contemporary articulation of the *anti*-reform position.
- *Xuánjī yíshù* 璇璣遺述 [[KR3fa029]] — [[揭暄]] Jiē Xuān's 1675–1695 *vortex* cosmology, an independent Chinese natural-philosophical response that absorbs the Tychonic geo-heliocentric system but rebuilds its mechanism on a *xuán-fēng* 旋風 ("whirling-wind") *qì*-theory, anticipating Cartesian-style positions developed without contact with Descartes.
- *Tiānguān kǎoyì* 天官考異 [[KR3fa022]] — Wú Sùgōng's 吳肅公 critical study of Bureau-tradition asterism discrepancies.
- *Fāngxīng tújiě* 方星圖解 [[KR3fa023]] — [[閔明我]] Claudio Filippo Grimaldi's 1694–1712 square ecliptic star-chart with explanatory text, one of the very few Jesuit-authored Chinese astronomical monographs of the early Kāng-xī period.
- *Tiānyuán lìlǐ quánshū* 天元歷理全書* [[KR3fa024]] — Xú Fā's 徐發 fifteen-*juǎn* Chinese mathematical-astronomical synthesis.
- *Tuībù fǎ jiě* 推步法解 [[KR3fa025]] — [[江永]] Jiāng Yǒng's six-*juǎn* exposition of imperial-bureau calculation procedures, in the high-Qián-lóng evidential-mathematical idiom of [[戴震]] and [[梅文鼎]].
- *Sāncái shíyì tiānjí* 三才實義天集 [[KR3fa027]] — Zhōu Yúqī's 周於漆 twenty-two-*juǎn* synthesist treatise.
- *Qīndìng tiānwén zhèngyì* 欽定天文正義 [[KR3fa030]] — anonymous eighty-one-*juǎn* high-Qīng imperial astronomical compendium, the most extensive Qīng official synthesis of the Schall-Verbiest *Xīyáng xīn-fǎ* apparatus.
- *Tiānxiàng yuánwěi* 天象源委 [[KR3fa031]] — Zhāng Yǒngzuò's 張永祚 sixteen-*juǎn* compendium.
- *Tiānxué chǎnwēi* 天學闡微 [[KR3fa036]] — Wáng Jiābì's 王家弼 ten-*juǎn* *shì-dàfū*-oriented synthesist digest.
- *Tiānwén mìzhǐ* 天文秘旨 [[KR3fa038]] — anonymous late-imperial *xīng-zhàn* / *xīng-mìng* digest.
- *Zhōutiān xīngwèi jīngwěi xiùdù kǎo* 周天星位經緯宿度考 [[KR3fa026]] — anonymous twenty-eight-*juǎn* Qīng star-coordinate reference table.

**Earth-sphericity and heliocentrism cluster.** Three works mark the transmission of post-1757 (Roman lifting of the prohibition on teaching heliocentrism) European astronomy into Chinese.
- *Dìqiú túshuō / Bǔ tú* 地球圖說/補圖 [[KR3fa032]] — Michel Benoist 蔣友仁's two-*juǎn* technical-and-cosmological treatise accompanying his 1767 Kūn-yú quán-tú 坤輿全圖, polished by [[何國宗]] and [[錢大昕]] and supplemented with diagrams by [[阮元]]; celebrated in modern historiography as the first Chinese-language text to set out and explicitly endorse the Copernican heliocentric system.
- *Dì yuán shuō* 地圓說 [[KR3fa033]] — Jiāo Tínghù's 焦廷琥 short Jiā-qìng-era treatise on the sphericity of the earth.
- *Xuān-xī tōng* 宣西通 [[KR3fa034]] (and digest version [[KR3fa035]]) — [[許桂林]] Xǔ Guìlín's late-Jiā-qìng programmatic statement of the *xī-xué zhōng-yuán* thesis: the *Hóngfàn*'s *xuán-jī yù-héng* already contains the celestial-equatorial coordinate system and the precession-of-the-equinoxes phenomenon, and the Jesuit-introduced data are the recovery of, not the replacement of, classical Chinese cosmology.

**Late-Qīng imperial atlas continuation.**
- *Qīndìng yíxiàng kǎochéng xùbiān* 欽定儀象考成續編 [[KR3fa037]] — the 1842–1844 Dào-guāng continuation of the 1744 *Yíxiàng kǎochéng*, the last pre-modernisation Chinese-imperial star-catalogue and the terminal point of the Qīntiān-jiān cataloging tradition.

## Important persons

- [[趙爽]] (Zhào Shuǎng, fl. 3rd c.) — the Hàn-Wèi commentator whose annotations to the *Zhōubì* fixed the *gàitiān* cosmology in the form transmitted into the Suí-Táng curriculum.
- [[王希明]] (Wáng Xīmíng, fl. 713–741) — the Táng-Xuánzōng Hànlín on-call attendant whose *Bù-tiān gē* anchors the entire mnemonic-asterism tradition of Chinese popular astronomy.
- [[王應麟]] (Wáng Yīnglín, 1223–1296) — the great Southern-Sòng philological encyclopaedist whose *Liùjīng tiānwén biān* fixes the classicising programme that anchors astronomy to the Confucian canon.
- [[元統]] (Yuán Tǒng, fl. 1384) — the first Míng-dynastic *Tài-shǐ lìng* whose *tōngguǐ* manuals are the principal evidence for the practical operation of the *Shòu-shí* / *Dà-tǒng* astronomical system at the late-imperial court.
- [[熊明遇]] (Xióng Míngyù, 1579–1649) — the Late-Míng *jìnshì* whose *Gé-zhì cǎo* is the founding Chinese natural-philosophical engagement with Jesuit-introduced Aristotelian-Ptolemaic-Tychonic cosmology.
- [[南懷仁]] (Ferdinand Verbiest, 1623–1688) — Flemish Jesuit, director of the Beijing Imperial Astronomical Bureau (1674–1688); his *Línɡtái yíxiàng zhì* is the operational manual of the imperial-Qīng Tychonic-Keplerian astronomy that determined the *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 calendar from 1645 to 1911 (Wilkinson §39.6.2).
- [[湯若望]] (Adam Schall von Bell, 1591–1666) — Verbiest's predecessor as Bureau director (1645–1664), author of the *Xīyáng xīn-fǎ lìshū* 西洋新法曆書, repeatedly invoked across the Qīng cluster as the originator of the imperial-Chinese Jesuit astronomy.
- [[楊光先]] (Yáng Guāngxiān, 1597–1669) — the principal anti-Jesuit polemicist whose *Bùdé yǐ* defines the conservative-Confucian critique of the reform.
- [[揭暄]] (Jiē Xuān, c. 1613–1695) — the Jiāng-xī recluse whose *Xuánjī yíshù* is the most ambitious independent Chinese cosmological synthesis of the Late-Míng / early-Qīng period, integrating Tychonic empirical results into an indigenous *qì*-vortex theory.
- [[閔明我]] (Claudio Filippo Grimaldi, 1638–1712) — Italian Jesuit, Bureau director after Verbiest; author of [[KR3fa023]], one of the few Jesuit-authored Chinese astronomical monographs of the early-Kāng-xī period.
- [[江永]] (Jiāng Yǒng, 1681–1762) — the Wǎn-pài evidential scholar whose *Tuībù fǎ jiě* exemplifies the high-Qián-lóng Confucian-mathematical absorption of imperial-bureau computation.
- [[蔣友仁]] (Michel Benoist, 1715–1774) — French Jesuit cartographer at the Qián-lóng court whose *Dìqiú túshuō* is the first explicit Chinese-language endorsement of Copernican heliocentrism, made possible by the 1757 Roman lifting of the prohibition.
- [[阮元]] (Ruǎn Yuán, 1764–1849) — the great Jiā-qìng-Dào-guāng *xué-zhèng* official-scholar whose evidential-philological work links the Han-mathematical recovery cluster (Zhōubì collation [[KR3fa003]]) to the late-imperial astronomical-cartographic synthesis (Benoist supplementary diagrams in [[KR3fa032]]).
- [[許桂林]] (Xǔ Guìlín, 1779–1821) — the late-Jiā-qìng *jǔrén* whose *Xuān-xī tōng* is the most rigorous philological-and-mathematical defence of the *xī-xué zhōng-yuán* thesis in the division.

## Topics

- ***Gàitiān*, *huntiān*, *xuānyè* and the *xuán-fēng* extension.** The classical tripartite cosmology (Wilkinson §39.1.1) — *gàitiān* 蓋天 (canopied sky), *huntiān* 渾天 (celestial sphere), *xuānyè* 宣夜 (infinite empty space) — is the framing problem of the division's classical commentaries (the *Zhōubì* cluster fixes *gàitiān*) and is taken up again by Jiē Xuān's [[KR3fa029]] vortex extension, an independent Chinese alternative to the Tychonic and the Cartesian models.
- **The three enclosures, the twenty-eight lodges, and the *fēn-yě* system.** The standard celestial-equatorial framework of Chinese astronomy (Wilkinson §39.1.2) runs across the entire division: as observational target in the operational and atlas works ([[KR3fa013]], [[KR3fa016]], [[KR3fa017]], [[KR3fa018]], [[KR3fa026]], [[KR3fa037]]), as mnemonic-pedagogical structure in the *Bù-tiān gē* tradition ([[KR3fa005]], [[KR3fa011]]), and as the explicitly classical anchor in [[KR3fa008]] and [[KR3fa034]].
- **Operational vs. theoretical astronomy.** Wilkinson §39.6 distinguishes the calendar-making mathematical-astronomical system from its theoretical apparatus; the division illustrates both — the *tōngguǐ* operational manuals [[KR3fa017]] and the *Yíxiàng zhì* / *Yíxiàng kǎochéng xùbiān* observational handbooks ([[KR3fa021]], [[KR3fa037]]) on one side; the *lǐ-xué*-style natural-philosophical treatises [[KR3fa019]], [[KR3fa024]], [[KR3fa029]] on the other.
- **The Jesuit reform of the imperial calendar.** The single dominant theme of the division's Qīng half: the Schall-Verbiest *Xīyáng xīn-fǎ lìshū* of 1635/1645, the 1664–1669 *lì-yù* 曆獄 in which Yáng Guāngxiān briefly displaced the Jesuits, Verbiest's 1669 restoration, and the resulting *Shíxiàn lì* calendar that ran the Qīng empire from 1645 to 1911 (Wilkinson §39.6.2–3). The division gives both sides of the dispute (Verbiest [[KR3fa021]] vs. Yáng Guāngxiān [[KR3fa028]]) and the Chinese natural-philosophical positions developed on either side of it ([[KR3fa019]], [[KR3fa029]], [[KR3fa022]]).
- **The *xī-xué zhōng-yuán* thesis.** The high-Qīng programme that all genuinely useful European astronomical results are the recovery of an originally Chinese astronomy lost in the Qín book-burning. The thesis runs through [[KR3fa027]], [[KR3fa036]], and reaches its most rigorous late-Jiā-qìng statement in [[KR3fa034]] / [[KR3fa035]].
- **Earth-sphericity and heliocentrism.** From Xióng Míngyù's [[KR3fa019]] acceptance of the spherical earth, through Verbiest's [[KR3fa021]] Tychonic system, to Benoist's [[KR3fa032]] explicit endorsement of Copernicus, and Jiāo Tínghù's [[KR3fa033]] short *Dì yuán shuō* — the division traces the staged Chinese reception of the European cosmological revolution.
- **Clepsydra and instrument design.** The Sòng *jǐ-lòu* tradition ([[KR3fa006]], [[KR3fa007]]) and the Qīng Jesuit bronze-instrument tradition ([[KR3fa021]]) bracket the division's two great horological-and-instrumental moments; the *Yíxiàng kǎochéng xùbiān* [[KR3fa037]] is their terminal-imperial synthesis.
