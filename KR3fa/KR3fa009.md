# Tiānwén jīngyì fù 天文精義賦
 *Rhapsody on the Essential Meaning of Astronomy*
by [[岳煕載]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Tiānwén jīngyì fù* is a Yuán-period verse treatise on astronomy in five *juǎn*, cast in the *fù* 賦 (rhapsody) form so that the technical content can be recited and memorised. The work is in the same broad family as the much earlier [[王希明]] *Bù-tiān gē* ([[KR3fa005]]): a versified pedagogical text that runs through the Chinese sky asterism by asterism, integrating Sòng-Yuán updates of the *xīng-guān* 星官 inventory and of stellar fortunes (*lǜ-mìng* 律命 — the astrological assignments of asterisms to terrestrial regions, persons and offices).

### Abstract

The work is attributed to Yuè Xīzài 岳煕載 of the Yuán dynasty; nothing further is reliably known of the author. The five-*juǎn* structure covers (a) the three *yuán* enclosures, (b) the twenty-eight lodges, (c) the seven *zhèng* (Sun, Moon, five planets), (d) eclipses and anomalies, and (e) astro-meteorological omens. Each section combines parallel-prose *fù* exposition with shorter *zàn* 贊 (eulogium) verses summarising the astrological significance of each asterism.

The work is not preserved in the Sòng-Yuán independent transmission; it survives in the Yuán-Míng *cóngshū* of astrological literature, notably the *Lù-shì zhēn-cáng* 陸氏珍藏 collection of Yuán *zǐ-bù* technical texts, and was incorporated into the Qīng *Sìkù quánshū* (zǐ-bù *tiānwén* sub-class). The work is one of a small number of Yuán-period astronomical texts in Chinese (rather than in the Persian-influenced *Huíhuí* 回回 calendrical tradition that the Yuán court also employed); for the latter see the works in the *Huíhuí lì* tradition such as the *Yuán-shǐ* 元史 chronicle records of Mǎhémóde 馬合謀的.

The composition window is the Yuán dynasty (1271–1368). No narrower bracket is recoverable.
