### Abstract

This is a duplicate entry of [[KR3ed076]] *Yīfāng jíjiě*, distinguished only by the differing **base-edition** of the underlying source file in the Kanripo TEI metadata (T-base vs. jc-base for KR3ed076). The intellectual content is the same: the 1682 formulary commentary of Wāng Áng of Xiūníng, organizing 374 principal formulas under 21 therapeutic categories.

The corpus convention of assigning separate KR ids to alternate base-editions of the same intellectual text is bibliographic, not philological: it permits each base-edition's pagination and reading to be cited independently, but the works are not considered distinct intellectual entities.

## Other points of interest

The two-id-per-work pattern arises occasionally in the Kanripo corpus for works that have circulated in multiple major recensions. For works of Wāng Áng's level of pedagogical influence — used by every village physician for two centuries — such variation is the rule rather than the exception. Comparative collation between the two base-editions could be productive for variant-readings research, but no such study is known.
