# Biàn Mài Píng Mài Zhāng Jù 辨脈平脈章句
*Phrase-by-Phrase Exposition of the "Biàn Mài" and "Píng Mài" Chapters*
by [[周學海]] (Zhōu Xuéhǎi, 字澂之, 號健霞, 1856–1906, 清)

## About the work

A two-juan close commentary on the *Biàn mài* 辨脈 and *Píng mài* 平脈 chapters of Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Shānghán lùn* 傷寒論 — the two introductory chapters that frame the pulse-doctrinal apparatus of the *Shānghán* tradition. Zhōu Xuéhǎi applies his usual *zhāngjù* 章句 method: each clause is quoted, glossed, and discussed in the light of the *Sùwèn*, *Língshū*, *Nànjīng*, and the prior commentarial tradition (Chéng Wújǐ 成無己, Zhāng Yǐnān 張隱菴, Zhāng Lìngshào 張令韶, Wèi Lìtóng 魏荔彤, Yú Jiāyán 喻嘉言, Zhāng Lùyù 張石頑, Huáng Kūnzài 黃坤載). The book is the fourth component of Zhōu's pulse cycle and reads as the *Shānghán*-applied counterpart to the more theoretical *Mài yì jiǎn mó* [[KR3eb036]].

### Prefaces

`KR3eb038_000.txt` carries Zhōu's preface dated *Guāngxù guǐsì shàng yuán hòu wǔ rì* 光緒癸巳上元后五日 = the fifth day after the Lantern Festival of **1893**, signed *Wǎnnán Jiàndé Zhōu Xuéhǎi Chéngzhī shū* 皖南建德周學海澄之書 ("Anhui southern, Jiàndé [Dōngzhì], Zhōu Xuéhǎi Chéngzhī signs"). The preface engages with the late-Qing dispute over the authenticity of the *Biàn mài* / *Píng mài* chapters — earlier commentators including Yú Jiāyán, Zhāng Lùyù, and Huáng Kūnzài having dismissed them as "Wáng Shūhé's spurious additions" — and Zhōu defends them as genuine to the *Shānghán lùn* corpus.

### Abstract

Zhōu's defence of the *Biàn mài* / *Píng mài* chapters is doctrinally consequential: these chapters provide the pulse-theoretic background to the body of the *Shānghán lùn*, and if they are dismissed (as Yú Jiāyán et al. dismissed them), the *Shānghán*'s pulse-clinical decisions become unanchored. Zhōu's *Zhāng jù* is the most systematic late-Qing reconstruction of the *Shānghán* pulse-doctrinal framework and a key contribution of the *Zhōu shì yī xué cóng shū* second series (1893). For Zhōu's biographical context and the broader cycle see [[KR3eb036]] and the person note.
