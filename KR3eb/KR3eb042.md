# Chóng Dìng Shí Xíng Fú Yīn Chú Yán 重訂時行伏陰芻言
*Revised Modest Words on the Seasonal Latent-Yīn Disorder*
by [[田宗漢]] (Tián Zōnghàn, *zì* Yúnchá 雲槎, *fl.* mid-19th c., 清), revised in Republican period

## About the work

A two-juan revised re-edition of the original *Shí xíng fú yīn lùn* 時行伏陰論 by Tián Zōnghàn (字 雲槎), a Qing physician active in the mid-19th century. The work concerns the differential diagnosis of a syndrome — *shí xíng fú yīn* 時行伏陰 ("seasonal latent yīn disorder") — that the author argues has been routinely misdiagnosed as cholera (*huò luàn* 霍亂), with disastrous clinical consequences when the appropriate cold-cooling treatment for cholera is given to a patient whose underlying pattern is *yīn*-cold-stagnation. The author's own preface emphasises that the symptom-complex superficially resembles cholera but is fundamentally a *yīn fú* 陰伏 (latent yīn) pattern requiring opposite treatment.

### Abstract

Tián Zōnghàn 田宗漢 (字 雲槎 Yúnchá) was an obscure mid-19th-century physician whose contribution rests entirely on this single short treatise. The doctrinal claim — that the cholera-pandemic-era misdiagnosis of *fú yīn* as *huò luàn* killed large numbers of patients through inappropriate cold treatment — is consequential and historically interesting; it places the work in dialogue with the larger Qing epidemiological debate about the *huò luàn* pandemics of the 1820s, 1840s, and 1860s. The 1922 republication reflects the continuing clinical relevance of the differential during the Republican-period plague seasons.
