# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Dìng-zhèng Zhòng-jǐng quán-shū Shānghán-lùn zhù 醫宗金鑑·訂正仲景全書傷寒論註
 *Corrected Commentary on the Shāng-hán lùn (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu017. The two listings reflect the *hxwd* reprint catalog's separation of the same 17-*juàn* imperial *Shāng-hán lùn* commentary into multiple sub-volume entries. See [[KR3eu017]] for the full description.

### Abstract

The *Dìng-zhèng Shānghán-lùn zhù* is the opening 17 *juàn* of the Qīng *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* — the imperial-orthodoxy corrected commentary on Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng's *Shāng-hán lùn*, codifying the *cuò-jiǎn chóng-dìng* 錯簡重訂 (revisionist) text-reorganisation initiated by [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí and developed by [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng. Composition window 1742–1749.

For the textual programme, the editorial method, the prescription apparatus, and the relationship to the Sìkù *tíyào* see [[KR3eu017]] and [[KR3e0090]].
