# Lì-yáng jī yào 癘瘍機要
 *Mechanism-Essentials of Leprous Ulcers*
by [[薛己]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Lì-yáng jī yào* 癘瘍機要 ("Mechanism-Essentials of *Lì-yáng* / Leprous Ulcers"), 3 *juàn*, is [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's specialist treatise on *lì-yáng* — the broad pre-modern Chinese disease-category covering leprosy in the modern Hansen's-disease sense together with several other chronic ulcerative skin diseases. It is the ninth sub-title of the *Xuē-shì yī-àn* corpus ([[KR3e0070]]); the work is the earliest specialist Chinese monograph devoted to this disease-cluster after Sòng general-medical treatments.

### Abstract

The three *juàn* are organised: (j. 1) general doctrine, aetiology and basic prescription strategy; (j. 2) detailed symptom-types and prescriptions; (j. 3) case records (醫案). Xuē frames *lì-yáng* as an internal-medicine problem with skin-surface manifestation rather than a purely external/skin disease — a doctrinal move characteristic of his integration of *yáng-yī* (surgery) into the broader Spleen-and-Stomach / *Mìng-mén* clinical frame. The recommended treatment couples external surgical management of the ulcers with systematic internal warming-tonifying regimens (*Liù-wèi wán*, *Bǔ-zhōng yì-qì tāng*, *Guī-pí tāng*) targeting the underlying *qì*-and-blood and kidney deficiencies. Composition window 1529–1554 is inferred from Xuē's mature-career chronology; the work is incorporated into the Sìkù *Xuē-shì yī-àn* recension.

The work's significance lies in being one of the most substantial premodern East-Asian treatments of leprosy and related chronic skin diseases; it was extensively cited by Edo Japanese kanpō dermatologists, including in [[片倉元周]] Katakura Motoshū's *Méi-lì xīn-shū* 黴癘新書 (cf. KR3eu058 in the present batch), which is a direct lineal descendant of Xuē's frame.
