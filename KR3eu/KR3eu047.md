# Shāng-hán lùn shù yì 傷寒論述義
 *Expounding the Meaning of the Shāng-hán lùn*
by [[丹波元堅]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Shāng-hán lùn shù yì* 傷寒論述義 ("Expounding the Meaning of the *Shāng-hán lùn*"), 5 *juàn*, by [[丹波元堅]] **Tamba no Motokata**, is the doctrinal-supplement to his father [[丹波元簡]] **Tamba no Mototane**'s philological collation of the *Shāng-hán lùn* — the *Shāng-hán lùn jí yì* 傷寒論輯義 (1812). Where Mototane's *Jí yì* fixes the critical text and gathers prior commentaries, Motokata's *Shù yì* offers his own integrating doctrinal exposition, working through the canonical six-channel chapters with attention to prescription-application logic and clinical-pattern correlation.

### Abstract

The work belongs to a paired *Shāng-hán* / *Jīn-guì* doctrinal-supplement set produced by Motokata: the *Shāng-hán lùn shù yì* paired with the *Jīn-guì yù-hán yào-lüè shù yì* 金匱玉函要略述義 (KR3eu049), both supplementing his father's two corresponding *jí yì* collations. The doctrinal stance is the orthodox literate-physician position of the Tamba circle: scrupulous attention to the canonical Sòng text-order, integration of all major post-Sòng commentaries (including the imperial *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn*), and a cautious clinical-application apparatus.

Composition window 1827–1857. For Motokata's biography and full publication list see his person note; for the companion works see [[KR3eu046]] *Shāng-hán guǎng yào* and [[KR3eu049]] *Jīn-guì yù-hán yào-lüè shù yì*.
