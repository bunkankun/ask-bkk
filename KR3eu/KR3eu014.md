# Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng · Nǚkē 證治準繩·女科
 *Plumb-Line for Pattern-and-Treatment — Women's Medicine*
by [[王肯堂]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* 女科準繩 ("Plumb-Line for Women's Medicine") is the women's-medicine treatise of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* — 5 *juàn* (one *cè* per *juàn* in the Wànlì print), completed in Wànlì 35 / dīngwèi (1607) together with the *Yòukē zhǔnshéng*. It systematises late-Míng women's-medicine practice through the same balanced, evidence-by-school framework that characterises the rest of the encyclopaedia. The major upstream sources are [[陳自明]] Chén Zìmíng's *Fùrén dàquán liáng fāng* ([[KR3e0038]], in Xuē Jǐ's annotated form — cf. KR3eu008) and [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's *Nǚkē cuō yào* 女科撮要; the structural plan follows Chén's eight categorical gates (menstrual regulation, miscellaneous diseases, seeking-offspring, prenatal education, pregnancy, the lying-in month, difficult births, post-natal care).

### Abstract

Wáng integrates the SòngMíng women's-medicine corpus through a clinical-categorical structure that selects from rather than is dominated by the warming-tonifying Xuē-school: each category presents both the cooling / blood-moving Sòng tradition and the Xuē-school warming-tonifying alternative, with case-and-prescription material from Wáng's own practice. Together with the *Yòukē zhǔnshéng* (the paediatric companion completed the same year — cf. KR3eu015), the *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* is the principal late-Míng synthesis of the reproductive-and-family-medicine literature and is heavily cited in the corresponding Qīng *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* chapters (cf. KR3eu021 / KR3eu032).

Composition window: 1607. For the parent compound work see [[KR3eu011]] / [[KR3e0078]].
