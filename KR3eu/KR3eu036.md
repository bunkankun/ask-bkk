# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Cì-jiǔ xīn-fǎ yào-jué 醫宗金鑑·刺灸心法要訣
 *Heart-Method Essentials for Acupuncture and Moxibustion (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu024 — the acupuncture-and-moxibustion chapter of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*, 8 *juàn*.

### Abstract

The chapter codifies the *shí-sì jīng* 十四經 (fourteen channels: the twelve regular plus *Rèn* and *Dū*) as the standard Qīng acupuncture-channel system, with mnemonic-verse acupoint listings and clinical-application chapters by symptom. It was the official Qīng imperial-academy textbook of acupuncture and remained in active institutional use into the 20th century. Composition window 1742–1749.

For full description and secondary literature see [[KR3eu024]].
