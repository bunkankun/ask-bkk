# Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Zábìng xīnfǎ yàojué 醫宗金鑑·雜病心法要訣
 *Heart-Method Essentials for Miscellaneous Diseases*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

The *Zábìng xīnfǎ yàojué* 雜病心法要訣 (5 *juàn*) is the internal-medicine *zábìng* (miscellaneous-diseases) section of the imperial *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016), within the larger *Zhūkē xīnfǎ yàojué* 諸科心法要訣 division. The work covers the standard internal-medicine clinical categories — *fēng* (wind / stroke), *hán* (cold injuries other than the canonical *Shānghán*), *shǔ* (summer-heat), *shī* (damp), *zào* (dryness), *huǒ* (fire), heart-and-spleen conditions, *qì*-and-blood deficiency, the major emotional-pattern syndromes, the *jījù* 積聚 accumulation-diseases, *fǎnwèi* / *yēgé* digestive obstructions, the *yǒngjū* internal-organ abscesses, sweating disorders, oedema, jaundice, etc. — in mnemonic verse-form with prose commentary.

### Abstract

The *xīnfǎ yàojué* 心法要訣 format — verse mnemonics (mostly seven-character regulated verse) followed by line-by-line prose explanation and selected prescription — is the signature pedagogical device of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn*. The *Zábìng* chapter integrates the late-Míng synthesis of internal-medicine doctrine (Wáng Kěntáng's *Zázhèng zhǔnshéng* (KR3eu011) and Zhāng Jièbīn's *Jǐngyuè quánshū* are major proximate sources) with the imperial-orthodoxy of balanced clinical reasoning — neither rigidly warming-tonifying nor rigidly cold-cooling.

Composition window 1742–1749. For the parent compendium see [[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016; cf. variant entry KR3eu031.
