# Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng · Yòukē 證治準繩·幼科
 *Plumb-Line for Pattern-and-Treatment — Paediatrics*
by [[王肯堂]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Yòukē zhǔnshéng* 幼科準繩 ("Plumb-Line for Paediatrics") is the paediatric treatise of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* — 9 *juàn* (the largest single sub-work in the encyclopaedia), completed in Wànlì 35 / dīngwèi (1607). It systematises late-Míng paediatric practice with the same balanced evidence-by-school method that characterises the rest of the *Zhǔnshéng*: the foundational [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ doctrine in Yán Xiàozhōng's *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhí jué* form, the Sòng *Xiǎo'ér wèishēng zǒngwēilùn* 小兒衛生總微論 / [[董汲]] Dǒng Jí line, the Yuán *Yòuyòu xīnshū* 幼幼新書, the Míng [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ pediatric trilogy, and Wáng's own clinical contributions are all incorporated by category.

### Abstract

The structural plan follows the established SòngMíng paediatric taxonomy: a doctrinal opening on paediatric *chúnyáng* 純陽 physiology and the diagnostic-difficulty principle inherited from [[錢乙]]; then the five-organ *gān* 疳 (chronic malnutrition) types, the eight kinds of childhood epilepsy (*xián* 癇), the acute and chronic *jīngfēng* 驚風 (convulsion) sequences, the smallpox / *dòuzhěn* 痘疹 specialist literature, and detailed prescriptions throughout. The work is the most comprehensive late-Míng paediatric synthesis after the *Pǔjì fāng · Yīnghái mén* (KR3eu001) and the Xuē corpus, and is one of the principal proximate sources for the corresponding Qīng *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* paediatric chapters (cf. KR3eu022 / KR3eu033 / KR3eu034).

Composition window: 1607. For the parent compound work see [[KR3eu011]] / [[KR3e0078]].
