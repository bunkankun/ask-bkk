# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Wài-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué 醫宗金鑑·外科心法要訣
 *Heart-Method Essentials for External Medicine*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

The *Wài-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué* 外科心法要訣 (16 *juàn*) is the external-medicine (surgical, dermatological, ulcerative-disease) chapter of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016). It systematises the Míng-era *Wài-kē zhèng-zōng* 外科正宗 of Chén Shí-gōng 陳實功 (1617) and the *Wài-kē dà-chéng* 外科大成 of Qí Kūn 祁坤 (1665) into the imperial mnemonic-verse-and-commentary format, covering *yōng-jū* 癰疽 (deep-seated abscesses and carbuncles) by anatomical region, *liú-zhù* 流注 (migratory abscesses), *liú-tán* 流痰 (cold-abscesses, including tuberculous bone disease), *yáng* 瘍 (ulcers), *xuǎn* 癬 (tinea-like dermatoses), *yīn-chuāng* 陰瘡 (genital ulcers), *gān-chuāng* 疳瘡 (syphilitic ulcers), *mài-fēng* 麻風 (leprosy), and pediatric *yán-mí* (eye-and-throat surface diseases).

### Abstract

The chapter is one of the most comprehensive Qīng-period external-medicine treatises and was the standard Qīng imperial-academy textbook of the specialty. The imperial editors integrate the late-Míng dual surgical tradition — Chén Shí-gōng's pragmatic *Wài-kē zhèng-zōng* and the doctrinal-integrative line of [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's external-medicine works (cf. KR3eu007) and Wáng Kěntáng's *Yáng-yī zhǔn-shéng* — into a single coherent reference, with detailed anatomical illustrations of lesion-types by body region and the surgical instruments required.

Composition window 1742–1749. For the parent compendium see [[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016.
