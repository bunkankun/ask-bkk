# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Dìng-zhèng Zhòng-jǐng quán-shū 醫宗金鑑·訂正仲景全書
 *Corrected Complete Works of Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

The *Dìng-zhèng Zhòng-jǐng quán-shū* 訂正仲景全書 of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* is the combined corrected-commentary on Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng's two foundational works — the *Shāng-hán lùn* (17 *juàn*; cf. KR3eu017 / KR3eu028) and the *Jīn-guì yàoluè* (8 *juàn*; cf. KR3eu029) — published in the present recension as a 25-*juàn* unit at the head of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*. The pairing follows the late-Míng / Qīng revisionist *cuò-jiǎn chóng-dìng* school's convention of treating Zhāng's two surviving works as a single editorial unit (the *Zhòng-jǐng quán-shū* 仲景全書), inheriting [[趙開美]] Zhào Kāi-měi's 1599 print of Zhào's *Shāng-hán lùn zhù jiě* + *Jīn-guì yào-lüè fāng-lùn* (the *Zhòng-jǐng quán-shū* of the late Míng).

### Abstract

For the *Shāng-hán lùn* commentary (17 *juàn*) see KR3eu017; for the *Jīn-guì* commentary (8 *juàn*) see KR3eu029. The combined work is the imperial-Qīng standard for both of Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng's foundational compositions and the principal channel through which the *Cháng-shā fù-gǔ* 長沙復古 (Back-to-Zhāng-of-Cháng-shā) revisionist programme — initiated by [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí (1592–1593) and developed by [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng (1648), [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù (*Shāng-hán zǒng-lùn*), and [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí (1729) — became codified as the Qīng official orthodoxy.

Composition window 1742–1749. For the parent compendium see [[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016.
