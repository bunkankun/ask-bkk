# Zhèng-tǐ lèi yào 正體類要
 *Categorical Essentials of Bone-Setting*
by [[薛己]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Zhèng-tǐ lèi yào* 正體類要 ("Categorical Essentials of Bone-Setting / Orthopaedic Medicine"), 2 *juàn*, is [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's specialist treatise on the treatment of fractures, dislocations and traumatic injury — the first specialist Chinese orthopaedic monograph after the Yuán *Shì-yī dé-xiào fāng* 世醫得效方 of [[危亦林]] Wēi Yìlín. The work is the seventh sub-title of the *Xuē-shì yī-àn* corpus ([[KR3e0070]]); the Sìkù *tíyào* of the *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn* ([[KR3e0090]]) specifically notes that the *Yīzōng jīn-jiàn* added a bone-setting section (*Zhèng-gǔ xīn-fǎ yào-zhǐ*) because Xuē's *Zhèng-tǐ lèi yào* had been the only specialist book in that field for almost two centuries.

### Abstract

The work pairs an upper *juàn* of clinical doctrine on injury patterns — including blunt trauma, fracture, dislocation, soft-tissue contusion, and the management of post-traumatic deficiency / blood-stasis / *qì*-stasis sequelae — with a lower *juàn* of representative case records illustrating Xuē's practice. As elsewhere in the Xuē corpus, the doctrinal core is the Spleen-and-Stomach school of [[李杲]] Lǐ Gǎo combined with the *Bā-wèi yuán* 八味元 / *Liù-wèi wán* 六味丸 strategy: post-traumatic *qì*-and-blood deficiency is treated with *Bǔ-zhōng yì-qì tāng* 補中益氣湯 and *Guī-pí tāng* 歸脾湯; underlying kidney-deficiency with *Liù-wèi* / *Bā-wèi*. The work's importance lies in being almost the unique Míng-period literate-physician treatment of an art that was otherwise the domain of the lower-status bone-setting craft (*zhèng-gǔ jiàng* 正骨匠); it bridges the literate-medical and craft-medical traditions and was the principal model for the Qīng imperial-edition bone-setting chapter.

Composition is conventionally dated to the second quarter of the 16th century — within Xuē's mature Sū-zhōu clinical period. For the parent compendium see [[KR3e0070]].
