# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Dòu-zhěn xīn-fǎ yào-jué 醫宗金鑑·痘疹心法要訣
 *Heart-Method Essentials for Smallpox and Measles (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu022, the smallpox-and-measles chapter of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* — 6 *juàn*. The two listings reflect the *hxwd* reprint catalog's separation of the same imperial chapter into multiple sub-volume entries.

### Abstract

The chapter consolidates the Sòng-Yuán-Míng smallpox / *dòu-zhěn* specialist literature and is the first official Qīng imperial work to incorporate a section on *rén-dòu* 人痘 (variolation) inoculation — a major medical-policy decision of Qián-lóng-era. The chapter is the principal channel through which 18th- and 19th-century Qīng practitioners learned variolation technique and was instrumental in the practice's spread throughout the Qīng empire. Composition window 1742–1749.

For full description, secondary literature, and the variolation discussion see [[KR3eu022]].
