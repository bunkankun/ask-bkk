# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Yǎn-kē xīn-fǎ 醫宗金鑑·眼科心法
 *Heart-Method for Ophthalmology (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu023 — the *Yǎn-kē xīn-fǎ yào-jué* 眼科心法要訣 ophthalmology chapter of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016) — under the slightly truncated title *Yǎn-kē xīn-fǎ*. The chapter is 2 *juàn*.

### Abstract

The ophthalmology chapter integrates the dual lineage of premodern Chinese eye-medicine — the Sòng *wǔ-lún bā-kuò* 五輪八廓 symptomatic schema and the literate-physician aetiology-first frame founded by [[倪維德]] Ní Wéi-dé's *Yuán-jī qǐ-wēi* (KR3eu005) — in the imperial mnemonic-verse format. Composition window 1742–1749.
