# Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn · Shān-bǔ míng-yī fāng-lùn 醫宗金鑑·刪補名醫方論
 *Edited Discussions on the Prescriptions of Famous Physicians (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu018. The two listings reflect the *hxwd* reprint catalog's separation of the same 8-*juàn* imperial formulary-and-discussion volume into multiple sub-volume entries.

### Abstract

The *Shān-bǔ míng-yī fāng-lùn* — 8 *juàn* — is the formulary-and-discussion volume of the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*, presenting selected prescriptions from Zhāng Zhòng-jǐng, the Sòng official compendia, the four Jīn-Yuán great masters and the major Míng physicians, accompanied by editorial *fāng-lùn* (prescription-discussions). The work is one of the most influential *fāng-lùn*-genre texts in any Chinese medical compendium and is the principal Qīng imperial-academy reference for the rationale (*fāng-yì* 方義) of standard prescriptions. Composition window 1742–1749.

For the full description, the immediate forerunners (Wú Kūn's *Yī-fāng kǎo* and Wāng Áng's *Yī-fāng jí-jiě*), and the secondary literature, see [[KR3eu018]] and [[KR3e0090]].
