# KR3fb 曆法 — Lì-fǎ (Calendrical Method)

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3fb 曆法 (*Lì-fǎ*) is the calendrical-method sub-division of KR3f 天文算法類 (*Tiānwén suànfǎ lèi*), parallel to the asterism-and-observational sub-division [[KR3fa]] 天文. Wilkinson is emphatic that *lì* 曆 means not "calendar" in the modern sense but "mathematical astronomy" — the computational apparatus whose practical output was the imperial almanac and whose periodic reform (*gǎi-lì* 改曆) was a recurring task of the Imperial Astronomical Bureau (*Qīntiān-jiān* 欽天監) from the Hàn down to 1911 (Wilkinson §39.6, Box 219). The thirty-and-more *gǎi-lì* episodes of pre-modern Chinese history each generated a body of procedural manuals, polemical defences, evidentiary reconstructions and hemerological commentaries; KR3fb is the Kyoto-University supplementary collection of these texts (refid prefixes `KX03-07-035` through `KX03-07-056`, plus two `KXDLJ003` items), drawn from the *Zhōngguó kēxué jìshù diǎnjí tōnghuì* 中國科學技術典籍通匯 and cognate reprint series, gathering the procedural-astronomical literature that the *Sìkù* editors excluded or that survived only in manuscript or rare-print form.

The chronological span runs from the Hóng-wǔ-period (1383) Chinese translation of the Khwarezmian *Huíhuí lì* 回回曆 manual — preserved through Bèi Lín's 貝琳 1477 Nán-jīng *Qīntiān-jiān* recension [[KR3fb003]] and its companion worked-examples volume [[KR3fb004]] — through the dense late-Míng calendar crisis around the failing *Dà-tǒng lì* 大統曆, the Jesuit-led *Chóng-zhēn lì-shū* 崇禎曆書 reform of 1629–35 and its 1645 promulgation as the Qīng *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 (Wilkinson §39.6.2), the dual Qīng tradition of (a) operational manuals for the Schall–Verbiest *Shíxiàn* system and (b) Chinese-scholarly defences of pre-Jesuit native methods, and culminating in the great 19th-century *kǎo-zhèng* 考證 reconstructions of the lost ancient astronomical systems — the Qín *Zhuān-xū lì* 顓頊曆, the Hàn *Tài-chū* 太初 / *Sān-tǒng* 三統, the Yuán *Shòu-shí lì* 授時曆 — and Wāng Yuē-zhēn's 汪曰楨 standard *shuò-rùn* 朔閏 chronological apparatus for the entire pre-modern span. Unlike [[KR3fa]] (which contains a single Hàn root-text and otherwise spans Hàn-to-Qīng broadly), KR3fb is almost entirely a Míng-Qīng corpus: it documents the long internal Chinese struggle to maintain, reform, and finally replace the *Dà-tǒng* / *Shòu-shí* mathematical-astronomical system in the face of Khwarezmian and then European competing systems (Wilkinson §39.6.2, §39.17.3.2).

## Important texts and text clusters

The 24 cataloged works fall into seven readily recognisable clusters.

**Islamic-calendar (*Huíhuí lì*) cluster.** Two works preserve the principal Míng-Qīng vehicle by which Khwarezmian Islamic astronomy entered Chinese practice, in parallel to the more theoretically ambitious *Qī-zhèng tuī-bù* 七政推步 [[KR3f0006]] in the SKQS proper.
- *Huí-huí lì-fǎ* 回回曆法 [[KR3fb003]] — Bèi Lín's 貝琳 1477 collated and printed recension of the Hóng-wǔ 16 (1383) court translation of the Islamic ephemeris-and-procedural manual by Mǎshā Yī-hēi 馬沙亦黑, Wú Bózōng 吳伯宗 and Lǐ Chōng 李翀; the Bureau's parallel "West-of-the-Western-Borders calendar" (*Xī-yù lì* 西域曆) used alongside the *Dà-tǒng* for eclipse cross-checking.
- *Huí-huí lì-fǎ shìlì* 回回曆法釋例 [[KR3fb004]] — Bèi Lín's companion worked-examples volume.

**Late-Míng *Dà-tǒng* operational and crisis cluster.** Six works document the operating state and progressive failure of the official *Dà-tǒng lì* 大統曆 (the Míng renaming of Guō Shǒu-jìng's 郭守敬 Yuán *Shòu-shí*) in the century before the Jesuit reform.
- *Dà-tǒng lì zhù* 大統歷注 [[KR3fb006]] — anonymous twelve-*juàn* practical hemerological commentary (*lì-zhù*) on the official almanac, in the *xuǎn-zé* 選擇 day-choosing genre.
- *Xīn-biān zūn-yī Sī-tiān-tái jīng-wěi lì-shū* 新編遵依司天臺經緯歷書 [[KR3fb007]] — Lù Wèi's 陸位 Wàn-lì private recension of an Imperial Astronomical Bureau ephemeris of the Seven Governors.
- *Shén-dào dà-biān lì-zōng tōng-yì* 神道大編曆宗通議 [[KR3fb005]] — Zhōu Shùxué's 周述學 mid-Míng commoner-scholar attempt (1545–1582) to recover the spherical-trigonometric core of the lost Yuán *Shòu-shí* method, fused with *Huíhuí* longitude-latitude procedures.
- *Zhézhōng lì-fǎ* 折衷曆法 [[KR3fb009]] — Zhū Zhòngfú's 朱仲福 sixteen-*juàn* "eclectic" reform attempt, framed by a sustained defence of private commoner mathematical-astronomical learning.
- *Wàn-nián lì bèi-kǎo* 萬年曆備考 [[KR3fb010]] — Zhū Zàiyù's 朱載堉 (1536–1611) supplementary evidentiary apparatus to his *Shèng-shòu wàn-nián lì* 聖壽萬年曆 [[KR3f0007]] reform proposal of 1595; a comparative survey of fifty principal historical calendar systems.
- *Wù-shēn lì-chūn kǎo-zhèng* 戊申立春考證 [[KR3fb012]] — Xíng Yún-lù's 邢雲路 focused 1608 empirical demonstration that the *Dà-tǒng*'s predicted *lì-chūn* node was hours away from the gnomon-shadow observation; the empirical centrepiece of the larger critique in his *Gǔ-jīn lǜ-lì kǎo* [[KR3f0008]].
- *Lì zhì* 歷誌 [[KR3fb008]] — anonymous fifteen-*juàn* late-Míng textbook of plane and spherical trigonometry adapted to astronomical computation, asserting that "trigonometry is the ridgepole of calendrical computation."

**Conservative anti-Jesuit (*Dōng-jú*) cluster.** Two works mount the principal Chinese-scholarly defence of native calendrical methods against the Jesuit reform.
- *Lì cè* 曆測 [[KR3fb011]] — Wèi Wén-kuí's 魏文魁 surviving five-*juàn* theoretical writing (1628–1635), supplying the intellectual basis for his *Dōng-jú* 東局 ("Eastern Bureau") rival reform programme established at court against Xú Guāng-qǐ's 徐光啟 and Lǐ Tiān-jīng's 李天經 *Xī-jú* 西局.
- *Dìng-lì yù-héng* 定歷玉衡 [[KR3fb021]] — Zhāng Yōng-jìng's 張雍敬 late-Kāng-xī twenty-one-*juàn* programmatic defence of native methods against the *Shíxiàn lì*, surveying fifty-six historical schools and identifying forty-four specific errors as the corrective "jade balance" of the title.

**Jesuit-era *Shíxiàn lì* operational and explanatory cluster.** Seven works document the imperial-Qīng Tychonic-Keplerian apparatus introduced by Adam Schall von Bell and Ferdinand Verbiest as the *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 of 1645 (Wilkinson §39.6.2, §39.6.3) — the calendar that ran the Qīng empire from 1645 to 1911.
- *Mín-lì pù-zhù jiě-huò* 民歷鋪註解惑 [[KR3fb013]] — [[湯若望]] Schall's three-*juàn* defensive apologia (1645–1666) for the editorial procedure he applied to the *pù-zhù* 鋪註 hemerological glosses of the reformed civil almanac, cast as a dialogue answering critics among the Yí-xīng 宜興 faction that would coalesce in the Yáng Guāng-xiān 楊光先 anti-Western prosecution of 1664–65 (see [[KR3fa028]] *Bù-dé yǐ*).
- *Jiāo-shí lì-shū* 交食曆書 [[KR3fb016]] — [[南懷仁]] Verbiest's 1683 single-*juàn* technical monograph on the *huáng-dào jiǔ-shí dù xiàn* 黃道九十度限 (the ninety-degree point of the ecliptic above the horizon, essential for solar-eclipse prediction), simplified from his *Jiāo-shí lì-zhǐ* 交食曆指 for working astronomers at the Bureau's Shèng-jīng (Mukden) sub-station.
- *Lì-xiàng běn-yào* 曆象本要 [[KR3fb017]] — Yáng Wén-yán's 楊文言 two-*juàn* Qīng primer of post-*Chóng-zhēn* / *Shíxiàn* Western-style astronomy, structured as a contrastive glossary of "new calendrical terms" (*xīn lì-fǎ míng* 新曆法名).
- *Qī-zhèng tái lì* 七政臺曆 [[KR3fb018]] — anonymous Qīng *Qīntiān-jiān* working planetary almanac of the Seven Governors (*qī-zhèng* 七政: sun, moon, five visible planets) in the post-*Shíxiàn* tradition.
- *Suàn qī-zhèng jiāo-shí líng-fàn fǎ* 算七政交食凌犯法 [[KR3fb019]] — anonymous Qīng practitioner's recipe-book for syzygies and planetary conjunctions/transits using the *Shíxiàn*-era tabular apparatus.
- *Dà-Qīng Shíxiàn-shū jiān-shì* 大清時憲書箋釋 [[KR3fb022]] — Miù Zhī-jìn's 繆之晉 three-*juàn* annotated commentary positioning itself between the official almanac and the popular *tōng-shū* 通書 hemerological handbooks.
- *Xiāo-xiāo-zǐ lì jìng* 囂囂子曆鏡 [[KR3fb020]] — Hú Xí-cān's 胡襲參 three-*juàn* diachronic "mirror" of historical calendrical systems for the use of contemporary post-Jesuit-era practitioners.

**Ming-loyalist *Shòu-shí* historical-philological cluster.** Two paired works recover the rationale of the Yuán system displaced by the Jesuit reform.
- *Shòu-shí lì gù* 授時曆故 [[KR3fb014]] — [[黃宗羲]] Huáng Zōng-xī's five-*juàn* exegesis of the technical procedures of the Yuán *Shòu-shí lì* (1280) of Wáng Xún 王恂 and [[郭守敬]] Guō Shǒu-jìng — the level/standing/fixed-difference (*píng-lì-dìng sān-chā* 平立定三差) interpolation and the *hú-shǐ gē-yuán* 弧矢割圓 spherical computations — recovered both for intrinsic value and as a Ming-loyalist scholarly defence against the Jesuit narrative of Chinese decline.
- *Lì-xué jiǎ-rú* 曆學假如 [[KR3fb015]] — Huáng's three-*juàn* pedagogical companion of model problems and worked examples, intended to break the *Shòu-shí* out of its closed-craft transmission and place it within the reach of literate-elite study.

**Qīng *kǎo-zhèng* reconstructive cluster.** Four 19th-century evidential-philological reconstructions of lost or imperfectly transmitted ancient systems, exemplifying the high *kǎo-zhèng* programme applied to mathematical astronomy.
- *Zhuān-xū lì kǎo* 顓頊曆考 [[KR3fb001]] — [[鄒漢勛]] Zōu Hàn-xūn's two-*juàn* reconstruction (1840–1853) of the Qín / early-Western-Hàn *Zhuān-xū lì* 顓頊曆 (in use c. 246–104 BCE), recovered by working backwards from dated histories using modern computation; consciously modelled on Dù Yù's 杜預 *Chūn-qiū cháng-lì* and intended as the first instalment of a planned continuation through to the Míng *Dà-tǒng*.
- *Hàn Tài-chū lì kǎo* 漢太初曆考 [[KR3fb002]] — Chéng Róng-jìng's 成蓉鏡 two-*juàn* evidentiary study (1840s) arguing that the *Lì-shù jiǎ-zǐ piān* 厤術甲子篇 of *Shǐjì* "Lì-shū" is a tabular embodiment of the *Tài-chū lì* 太初曆 of 104 BCE rather than the lost *Yīn lì* 殷曆, with corrupt readings to be emended against Liú Xīn's 劉歆 *Sān-tǒng lì* 三統曆.
- *Sān-tǒng-shù xiáng-shuō* 三統術詳說 [[KR3fb024]] — Chén Lǐ's 陳澧 five-*juàn* late-Qīng exegesis of Liú Xīn's *Sān-tǒng* system as transmitted in the *Hàn shū "Lǜ-lì zhì"* 律曆志, in dialogue with Liú Bān's 劉攽 Sòng commentary; the canonical reference work for the system that Cullen (2017) takes as one of the three foundation-systems of Chinese mathematical astronomy.
- *Lì-dài cháng-shù jí-yào* 歷代長術輯要 [[KR3fb023]] — Wāng Yuē-zhēn's 汪曰楨 fourteen-*juàn* standard 19th-century chronological apparatus: a year-by-year *shuò-rùn* 朔閏 reference from the 841 BCE *Gòng-hé* 共和 regency to the end of the Míng, computed using the calendrical system actually in use in each period; the abridged form of his lost fifty-three-*juàn* *Èr-shí-sì shǐ yuè-rì kǎo* 二十四史月日考.

## Important persons

- [[貝琳]] (Bèi Lín, fl. 1477) — Nán-jīng *Qīntiān-jiān* astronomer whose collated 1477 printed recension of the Hóng-wǔ *Huíhuí lì* translation [[KR3fb003]] [[KR3fb004]] is the principal Chinese vehicle of Khwarezmian-tradition Islamic astronomy in the Míng.
- [[周述學]] (Zhōu Shùxué, fl. 1545–1582) — Mid-Míng independent commoner-scholar whose *Lì-zōng tōng-yì* [[KR3fb005]] is the most substantial Míng attempt to recover the Yuán *Shòu-shí* spherical-trigonometric techniques outside the imperial Bureau.
- [[朱載堉]] (Zhū Zàiyù, 1536–1611) — Míng prince-scholar; his late corpus on mathematical harmonics and calendrics, of which the *Wàn-nián lì bèi-kǎo* [[KR3fb010]] is the evidentiary apparatus, frames the most ambitious commoner-elite reform proposal of the Wàn-lì period.
- [[邢雲路]] (Xíng Yún-lù, *jìnshì* 1580) — Late-Wàn-lì commoner-astronomer whose 1608 empirical demonstration [[KR3fb012]] of *Dà-tǒng* failure is the immediate trigger for the late-Míng reform campaign that culminated in the Jesuit-led *Chóng-zhēn lì-shū*.
- [[魏文魁]] (Wèi Wén-kuí, fl. 1628–1635) — Mán-chéng commoner-astronomer, leader of the conservative *Dōng-jú* 東局 ("Eastern Bureau") rival programme to the Jesuit reform; his *Lì cè* [[KR3fb011]] is the principal surviving theoretical statement of the anti-reform position in this division.
- [[湯若望]] (Adam Schall von Bell, 1591/2–1666) — German Jesuit, Director of the Imperial Astronomical Bureau 1645–1664 (Wilkinson §39.6.2); architect of the *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 itself, repeatedly invoked across the Qīng cluster as the originator of the imperial-Chinese Jesuit astronomy. His *Mín-lì pù-zhù jiě-huò* [[KR3fb013]] is his own defence of his hemerological editorial procedure.
- [[南懷仁]] (Ferdinand Verbiest, 1623–1688) — Flemish Jesuit, Schall's successor as Bureau director (1674–1688); his *Jiāo-shí lì-shū* [[KR3fb016]] supplies one of the few focused operational eclipse-computation monographs of the post-Schall imperial corpus.
- [[黃宗羲]] (Huáng Zōng-xī, 1610–1695) — The great Míng-loyalist scholar-philosopher; the paired *Shòu-shí lì gù* [[KR3fb014]] and *Lì-xué jiǎ-rú* [[KR3fb015]] are the most influential early-Qīng historical-philological recovery of the Yuán *Shòu-shí* system displaced by the *Shíxiàn* reform.
- [[張雍敬]] (Zhāng Yōng-jìng, fl. 1690–1709) — Late-Kāng-xī Xīn-chéng scholar whose *Dìng-lì yù-héng* [[KR3fb021]] is the most exhaustive Chinese-scholarly defence of native calendrical methods against the Jesuit *Shíxiàn lì*.
- [[鄒漢勛]] (Zōu Hàn-xūn, 1805–1853) — Xīn-huà *kǎo-zhèng* scholar whose *Zhuān-xū lì kǎo* [[KR3fb001]] is the foundational 19th-century reconstruction of the lost Qín / early-Hàn calendar; killed on the Tài-píng front before his planned diachronic continuation could be completed.
- [[陳澧]] (Chén Lǐ, 1810–1882) — Pān-yú Cantonese evidential scholar of the *Xué-hǎi-táng* 學海堂; his *Sān-tǒng-shù xiáng-shuō* [[KR3fb024]] is the standard late-Qīng exegesis of the Hàn *Sān-tǒng* system.
- [[汪曰楨]] (Wāng Yuē-zhēn, 1813–1881) — Wū-chéng historian-chronologist (Wilkinson at 9643 notes his sobriquet *Gāng-mù* 剛木); his *Lì-dài cháng-shù jí-yào* [[KR3fb023]] is the standard nineteenth-century *shuò-rùn* reference for the entire pre-modern span.
- [[繆之晉]] (Miù Zhī-jìn, fl. Qīng) — Compiler of the *Dà-Qīng Shíxiàn-shū jiān-shì* [[KR3fb022]], the principal mid-Qīng commentarial bridge between the official *Shíxiàn* almanac and popular *tōng-shū* hemerological practice.

## Topics

- **Reform of the astronomical system (*gǎi-lì* 改曆).** Wilkinson §39.6 / Box 219 emphasises that *gǎi-lì* refers not to "calendar reform" in the modern sense but to reform of the underlying mathematical-astronomical system. KR3fb is the corpus through which the late-imperial *gǎi-lì* cycle is most directly documented: the late-Míng failure of the *Dà-tǒng* ([[KR3fb010]], [[KR3fb012]]), the Jesuit reform that produced the *Shíxiàn* ([[KR3fb013]], [[KR3fb016]], [[KR3fb017]]), the conservative *Dōng-jú* response ([[KR3fb011]], [[KR3fb021]]), and the *kǎo-zhèng* recovery of earlier displaced systems ([[KR3fb001]], [[KR3fb002]], [[KR3fb014]], [[KR3fb024]]).
- **The *Shòu-shí* / *Dà-tǒng* mathematical core.** The level-standing-fixed-difference interpolation and spherical *hú-shǐ gē-yuán* arc-and-sagitta computations of Wáng Xún and [[郭守敬]] Guō Shǒu-jìng (Sivin 2009, cited in Wilkinson §39.17.2.1) run as a subterranean thread through the division: as living-but-decaying Bureau practice in the late Míng ([[KR3fb006]], [[KR3fb007]]), as the object of commoner-scholarly recovery efforts ([[KR3fb005]], [[KR3fb009]]), as the displaced ancestor of the *Shíxiàn* whose technical rationale Huáng Zōng-xī sought to preserve ([[KR3fb014]], [[KR3fb015]]), and as the implicit comparand against which conservative anti-Jesuit polemic measured the reformed system ([[KR3fb011]], [[KR3fb021]]).
- **The three-cornered confrontation: native, *Huí-huí*, and Western methods.** The division provides documentary evidence for all three poles of the late-Míng / Qīng astronomical pluralism: the *Huíhuí lì* of [[KR3fb003]] / [[KR3fb004]] inherited from the Yuán Muslim Bureau (Wilkinson §39.6.2); the Jesuit *Shíxiàn* of [[KR3fb013]] / [[KR3fb016]] / [[KR3fb022]]; and the native programme defended in [[KR3fb011]] / [[KR3fb021]] and recovered in [[KR3fb014]] / [[KR3fb015]].
- ***Pù-zhù* 鋪註 and hemerology.** The dense day-by-day glosses on the official almanac — auspicious / inauspicious activities, *jiàn-chú* 建除, *qí-shén fāng-wèi* 奇神方位 (Wilkinson §39.16) — are themselves a contested object: Schall's apologia for his redaction of them [[KR3fb013]], the anonymous Míng *Dà-tǒng lì zhù* [[KR3fb006]] continuing the inherited *xuǎn-zé* tradition, and Miù Zhī-jìn's [[KR3fb022]] positioning between official almanac and popular *tōng-shū*, all bear on the same question.
- **The Qīng *kǎo-zhèng* reconstruction of ancient mathematical-astronomical systems.** The 19th-century texts in the division ([[KR3fb001]], [[KR3fb002]], [[KR3fb023]], [[KR3fb024]]) embody a coherent evidential-philological programme: recover the procedures of the *Zhuān-xū*, *Tài-chū*, *Sān-tǒng* and *Shòu-shí* systems from the histories' fragmentary procedural specifications and the dated entries of the standard histories, computing back from modern *mì-shù* 密術 precise methods to identify both the constants in use and the textual corruptions in transmission. The systems concerned are precisely those that Cullen (2017) translates and Sivin (2009) studies as the foundation-corpus of Chinese mathematical astronomy.
- **Commoner-elite versus imperial-Bureau learning.** A striking proportion of the division's authors are scholars writing outside the *Qīntiān-jiān*: Zhōu Shùxué [[KR3fb005]], Zhū Zhòngfú [[KR3fb009]], Xíng Yún-lù [[KR3fb012]], Wèi Wén-kuí [[KR3fb011]], Huáng Zōng-xī [[KR3fb014]] / [[KR3fb015]], Zhāng Yōng-jìng [[KR3fb021]], Zōu Hàn-xūn [[KR3fb001]], Chéng Róng-jìng [[KR3fb002]], Chén Lǐ [[KR3fb024]] and Wāng Yuē-zhēn [[KR3fb023]] are all commoner or local-elite scholars; their writings collectively form the principal extra-Bureau body of mathematical-astronomical scholarship, in tension with the imperial monopoly that Wilkinson §39.6.3 documents.
- **Chronological apparatus and the use of past calendars.** The practical task of converting between the calendrical systems actually in use across two millennia — essential for reading the dated material in the standard histories — culminates in Wāng Yuē-zhēn's [[KR3fb023]] standard *shuò-rùn* reference, the canonical 19th-century answer to a problem implicit in the entire pre-modern Chinese historiographical apparatus.
