# Chóng-dìng chǎn-yùn jí 重訂產孕集
*Revised Collection on Pregnancy and Childbirth*
by [[張曜孫]] (Zhāng Yào-sūn, 1808–1863)

## About the work

Zhāng Yào-sūn's own revised redaction of his earlier *Chǎn-yùn jí* 產孕集 ([[KR3ei059]]), prepared in the late *Dào-guāng* / *Xián-fēng* period (1850s, before Zhāng's death in 1863). The revised edition incorporates clinical-experience additions from Zhāng's medical practice during his retreat at Wǔ-chāng during the Tài-píng disturbances, and reorganises the original tripartite *huái-tāi / lín-chǎn / chǎn-hòu* scheme with finer-grained subdivisions and a more extensive *jiā-jiǎn* (additions-and-subtractions) apparatus for each principal prescription.

### Abstract

The hxwd recension of KR3ei060 carries the base-edition indicator `BASEEDITION: T` (i.e. a *T-series* base) — i.e. derives from a Tóng-zhì / Guāng-xù era print rather than the earlier *Dào-guāng* witness. The catalog meta records only Zhāng Yào-sūn as author, identifying the work explicitly as a revised redaction (*chóng-dìng*) of [[KR3ei059]]. The composition window is bracketed to 1850–1863 — after the original *Chǎn-yùn jí* and before Zhāng's death.

The relationship between the two recensions is that of author's working-copy and final author-revised text. Both are preserved in the Hǎi-wài huí-guī series. The textual relationship between them has not been the object of detailed Chinese-medicine philological study, and is one of several outstanding tasks in late-Qīng obstetrical-literature criticism.
