# Chǎn-bǎo 產寶
*Obstetrical Treasury*
by [[倪枝維]] (Ní Zhī-wéi, *zì* Pèi-yù 佩玉, *hào* Fèng-bīn 鳳賓; *fl.* Yōng-zhèng era)

## About the work

A concise one-*juǎn* post-partum manual completed in 1728 (Yōng-zhèng 6) — a focused clinical compendium organised entirely around the post-partum complications, with *Shēng-huà tāng* 生化湯 as its master formula. The work's theoretical signature, stated in Xǔ Lián's 1842 preface, is the proposition that **"to regulate blood one must first regulate qi"** (治血必先理氣) — a deliberate rejection of the contemporary tendency to use *mò-yào / sān-léng / é-zhú* blood-breaking drugs in post-partum, which Xǔ Lián blames for the death of his own first wife in 1813.

### Abstract

Ní Zhī-wéi completed the work in 1728 but it was never printed in his lifetime — the manuscript circulated only privately. According to the preface of Xǔ Lián 許槤 of Hǎi-chāng (a Qīng official and bibliophile), the manuscript was given to him by his friend Zhào Wèi 趙魏 and lay in his box for some twenty-five years before he prepared the first wood-block edition in 1842 (*Dào-guāng rén-yín*) at his Píng-dù-zhōu official residence. Huáng Zǔ-jīng 黃祖經 of Shí-yáng prepared a re-cut at his family school in 1871 (*Tóng-zhì xīn-wèi*) after his father (Huáng's father, a *guāng-lù* official, had also intended to print). Huáng Zǐ-yú 黃子畬 prepared a stone-lithograph edition in 1906 (*Guāng-xù bǐng-wǔ*) at the Quán-shí-shān-fáng 拳石山房, with prefaces by Gāo Shàng-jìn 高尚縉 and the editor's family notes describing several first-hand clinical successes from the use of the book's prescriptions.

The dating of 1728 follows the explicit testimony of Xǔ Lián's preface: "*Ní Yōng-zhèng liù nián chéng shì shū*" — *Yōng-zhèng* 6 = 1728. The work's theoretical apparatus is anchored in three master formulae of Ní's design or popularisation: (i) *Shēng-huà tāng* 生化湯 (川芎 12 fēn, 當歸 5 qián, 炮黑 乾薑 5 fēn, 炙 甘草 3 fēn, 桃仁 11 粒) for *zhèng-huà* (the "transforming-and-generating" axis of post-partum blood-regulation); (ii) *Shēng-huà duó-mìng tāng* 生化奪命湯 for *blood-collapse fainting*; (iii) *Yǎng-zhèng tōng-yōu tāng* 養正通幽湯 for post-partum constipation. The book's distinctive contraindications — never use *má-huáng*, *dà-huáng*, *chéng-qì tāng*, *zhī-fù*, *bǎi-cǎo-shuāng*, *zōng-huī*, *jiào-bǎo*, *huáng-bǎi*, *huáng-lián*, *zhī-zǐ*, *huáng-bǎo* in post-partum — became canonical late-Qīng obstetrical orthodoxy.

The work is one of the most-clinically-influential single-*juǎn* obstetrical manuals of the Qīng. The 1906 prefaces record half a dozen documented case-successes using its protocols.
