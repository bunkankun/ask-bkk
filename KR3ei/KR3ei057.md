# Chǎnbǎo 產寶
*Obstetrical Treasury*
by [[倪枝維]] (Ní Zhīwéi, *zì* Pèiyù 佩玉, *hào* Fèngbīn 鳳賓; *fl.* Yōngzhèng era)

## About the work

A concise one-*juǎn* post-partum manual completed in 1728 (Yōngzhèng 6) — a focused clinical compendium organised entirely around the post-partum complications, with *Shēnghuà tāng* 生化湯 as its master formula. The work's theoretical signature, stated in Xǔ Lián's 1842 preface, is the proposition that **"to regulate blood one must first regulate qi"** (治血必先理氣) — a deliberate rejection of the contemporary tendency to use *mòyào / sānléng / ézhú* blood-breaking drugs in post-partum, which Xǔ Lián blames for the death of his own first wife in 1813.

### Abstract

Ní Zhīwéi completed the work in 1728 but it was never printed in his lifetime — the manuscript circulated only privately. According to the preface of Xǔ Lián 許槤 of Hǎichāng (a Qīng official and bibliophile), the manuscript was given to him by his friend Zhào Wèi 趙魏 and lay in his box for some twenty-five years before he prepared the first wood-block edition in 1842 (*Dàoguāng rényín*) at his Píngdùzhōu official residence. Huáng Zǔjīng 黃祖經 of Shíyáng prepared a re-cut at his family school in 1871 (*Tóngzhì xīnwèi*) after his father (Huáng's father, a *guānglù* official, had also intended to print). Huáng Zǐyú 黃子畬 prepared a stone-lithograph edition in 1906 (*Guāngxù bǐngwǔ*) at the Quánshíshānfáng 拳石山房, with prefaces by Gāo Shàngjìn 高尚縉 and the editor's family notes describing several first-hand clinical successes from the use of the book's prescriptions.

The dating of 1728 follows the explicit testimony of Xǔ Lián's preface: "*Ní Yōngzhèng liù nián chéng shì shū*" — *Yōngzhèng* 6 = 1728. The work's theoretical apparatus is anchored in three master formulae of Ní's design or popularisation: (i) *Shēnghuà tāng* 生化湯 (川芎 12 fēn, 當歸 5 qián, 炮黑 乾薑 5 fēn, 炙 甘草 3 fēn, 桃仁 11 粒) for *zhènghuà* (the "transforming-and-generating" axis of post-partum blood-regulation); (ii) *Shēnghuà duómìng tāng* 生化奪命湯 for *blood-collapse fainting*; (iii) *Yǎngzhèng tōngyōu tāng* 養正通幽湯 for post-partum constipation. The book's distinctive contraindications — never use *máhuáng*, *dàhuáng*, *chéngqì tāng*, *zhīfù*, *bǎicǎoshuāng*, *zōnghuī*, *jiàobǎo*, *huángbǎi*, *huánglián*, *zhīzǐ*, *huángbǎo* in post-partum — became canonical late-Qīng obstetrical orthodoxy.

The work is one of the most-clinically-influential single-*juǎn* obstetrical manuals of the Qīng. The 1906 prefaces record half a dozen documented case-successes using its protocols.
