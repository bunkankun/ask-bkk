# Shěnshì nǚkē jíyào jiānshū 沈氏女科輯要箋疏
*Annotated Commentary on the Shen Family's Compilation of Essentials in Women's Medicine*
original compilation by [[沈又彭]] (Shěn Yòupéng, mid-Qīng); critical annotation (*jiānshū*) by [[張壽頤]] (Zhāng Shòuyí = Zhāng Shānléi, 1873–1934)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* early-Republican-era critical annotated commentary on the *Shěnshì nǚkē jíyào* [[KR3ei010]] of Shěn Yòupéng 沈又彭 (Yáofēng 堯封, *fl.* mid-Qián-lóng), prepared by **Zhāng Shòuyí** 張壽頤 (*hào* Shānléi 山雷, 1873–1934) of the Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānménxuéxiào 蘭谿中醫專門學校 (Zhejiang). The work is one of the foundational Republican-era TCM-college textbooks. Zhāng preserves the full ShěnYòupéng base-text together with the Wáng Mèngyīng 王孟英 (1808–1868) 1850 annotations (introduced "*Wáng Mèngyīng àn*" 王孟英按), and adds his own *jiānshū* 箋疏 ("paratextual commentary") drawing on twenty-plus years of clinical experience and a critical reading of the entire late-Qīng gynecological literature.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` `_000.txt` opens with Zhāng Shòuyí's *Xiǎoyǐn* 小引 (small introduction) — a substantial doctrinal-historiographic opening. Zhāng surveys the principal gynecological literature: Chén Liángfǔ 陳良甫 (= Chén Zìmíng)'s *Dàquán liángfāng*, Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng·Nǚkē* (= [[KR3ei030]]), Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jìyīn gāngmù* (= [[KR3ei006]]) — and judges them all "*chénchén xiāngyīn, fǔqì mǎnzhǐ*" 陳陳相因，腐氣滿紙 ("repetitively derivative, decay reeking from every page"). He singles out the Shěn Yáofēng *Nǚkē jíyào* as exceptionally fresh, clinically sharp, and original, attributing his own training in gynecological practice to early study of this text. Zhāng notes that recent stone-print editions in the *Qiánzhāi yīyào cóngshū* 潛齋醫藥叢書 (a Wáng Mèngyīng-school cóngshū) have transcription errors and difficult-to-read passages; he therefore re-set the text and added his own commentary, taking the project as a teaching-text for his Lánxī TCM college courses.

### Abstract

Zhāng Shòuyí (1873–1934, see [[張壽頤]]) was one of the founding teachers of the modern Chinese-medicine college system: after the 1911 revolution he taught at the Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānménxuéxiào 蘭谿中醫專門學校 (Zhèjiāng), one of the first formal TCM colleges, where he trained a generation of Republican-era physicians. His major works include the *Běncǎo zhèngyì* 本草正義 (1932, see [[KR3ec063]]), the *Zhōngfēng kǎo* 中風斠詮, and the *Shānléi yīxué cóngshū* 山雷醫學叢書 — of which the *Shěnshì nǚkē jíyào jiānshū* is one of the principal medical-pedagogical components. Composition is dated within Zhāng's principal TCM-college teaching career: `notBefore` 1915 / `notAfter` 1933 brackets the relevant range.

The work is the **standard modern TCM gynecology textbook** in the Wáng Mèngyīng / Shěn Yòupéng lineage: it preserves Shěn's mid-Qián-lóng critical-pragmatic original, Wáng's 1850 *Wēnbìng*-school commentaries, and adds Zhāng's *jīngfāng*-leaning Republican-era critical apparatus. The work has remained in continuous PRC TCM-academy use since publication.
