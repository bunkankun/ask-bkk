# Nǚkē zhǐyào 女科旨要
*The Indicators and Essentials of Women's Medicine*
attributed to [[雪岩禪師]] (Xuěyán Chánshī, Qīng-period monastic attribution)

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* gynecological compendium attributed to the monk **Xuěyán Chánshī** 雪岩禪師 ("Snow-Cliff Meditation-Master"), one of the Buddhist-monastic lineage attributions used in late-imperial popular gynecology. The work follows the *fùkē* age-cohort organisation principle: each entry begins with "女子十三四歲" (girls aged 13–14), "十五六歲" (15–16), etc., proceeding through reproductive-age cohorts and into menopausal years. This *suìshùfāng* 歲數方 (formula-by-age-cohort) format is distinctive of Qīng popular gynecology — compare [[KR3ei005]] *Fùkē mìfāng* (which also uses age-cohort organisation under the Xúnxī Chénshì attribution). Each entry presents a typical clinical scenario for the cohort and a prescribed formula.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension `_001.txt` opens directly with "卷一 婦女逐年經症" ("*Juǎn* 1: Annual menstrual disorders by year"), with no separable author-preface preserved.

### Abstract

"Xuěyán Chánshī" is a monastic appellation; no historical individual of this name is documented in standard Chinese-Buddhist or Chinese-medicine reference works. The catalog meta dynasty 清 is correct; internal evidence (use of *Bāwù tāng* 八物湯, *Héqì sǎn* 和氣散, *Sìwù tiáojīng tāng* 四物調經湯 — the standard late-imperial formulary) confirms a Qīng-period composition; `notBefore` 1700 / `notAfter` 1900 is a defensible bracket. The work belongs to the broader corpus of Qīng popular gynecology literature framed under monastic-lineage authorship, alongside the Bamboo-Grove-Monastery (*Zhúlínsì*) corpus ([[KR3ei013]], [[KR3ei020]], [[KR3ei022]], [[KR3ei026]], [[KR3ei046]]) and the *Lúnyìn Chánshī* attribution ([[KR3ei022]]).

The *suìshùfāng* age-cohort organisation method positions the work in the same provincial-popular-practitioner tradition as [[KR3ei005]] *Fùkē mìfāng*.
