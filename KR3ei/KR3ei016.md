# Nǚkē zhǐyào 女科指要
*Indicators of Essentials in Women's Medicine*
by [[徐大椿]] (Xú Dàchūn = Xú Língtāi = Xú Huíxī, 1693–1771, mid-Qīng)

## About the work

A five-*juǎn* systematic gynecology treatise by the great Qīng polymath-physician Xú Dàchūn 徐大椿 (Língtāi 靈胎 / Huíxī 洄溪, 1693–1771) of Wújiāng 吳江 (Sūzhōu prefecture, Jiāngsū). The work is structured by topical *mén* 門: *jīnghòu* 經候 (menstrual cycle), *jīngbì* 經閉 (amenorrhoea), *bēnglòu* 崩漏, *dàixià* 帶下, *qiúzǐ* 求子, *tāiqián* 胎前, *línchǎn* 臨產, *chǎnhòu* 產後, and *zábìng* 雜病. Each entry follows Xú's distinctive doctrinal-clinical-pharmacological argumentation: a brief étiological statement from canonical sources, a *màifǎ* 脈法 (pulse-diagnosis) note, a *zhìfǎ* 治法 (therapeutic-method) statement specifying the *cardinal class* of drugs required, and one or more *xuǎnfāng* 選方 (selected formulas) with full drug-by-drug commentary on why each ingredient is included. The drug-by-drug *jiěfāng* 解方 (formula-explanation) is a signature of Xú's clinical-pharmacological pedagogy and the principal interest of the work.

### Abstract

Xú Dàchūn (1693–1771, CBDB 61225) is the foremost Qīng polymath-physician — author of the *Yīxué yuánliú lùn* 醫學源流論 (1757), *Lántái guǐfàn* 蘭台軌範 (1764), *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng bǎizhǒng lù* 神農本草經百種錄 (1736), *Shānghán lùn lèifāng* 傷寒論類方, and *Huíxī yīàn* 洄溪醫案. His scholarly orientation is the mature Qīng *kǎojù* 考據 (evidential research) tradition applied to medical texts: rigorous philological method, polemical independence (notably his sharp criticism of Lǐ Dōngyuán's *Bǔzhōng yìqì tāng* school and Zhū Dānxī's *xiānghuǒ* 相火 doctrine), and a return to *Shānghán lùn* / *Jīnguì yàoluè* canonical foundations.

The *Nǚkē zhǐyào* applies Xú's distinctive evidential-philological method to gynecology. The work refuses both the LǐZhū late-Míng tonifying-and-clearing synthesis and the popular FùQīngzhǔ *Wándài tāng* / *Shēnghuà tāng* formulary, returning instead to a canonical Zhāng Zhòngjǐng framework supplemented by SòngYuán authorised formulae (*Sìwù tāng*, *Bāzhēn tāng*, *Yùzhú sǎn* 玉燭散 = *Sìwù tāng* + *Tiáowèi chéngqì tāng* 調胃承氣湯, *Sānhé tāng* 三和湯). The work is therefore one of the principal Qīng *jīngfāng* 經方 (classical-formula) school gynecological reformulations, parallel in spirit to Xú's other *kǎojù* medical writings.

Composition is bracketed between Xú's first medical publication (*Shénnóng běncǎo jīng bǎizhǒng lù*, 1736) and his death in 1771; `notBefore` 1740 / `notAfter` 1771 is a defensible range. The work was included in the *Xú Língtāi yīshū* 徐靈胎醫書 collected editions issued in the late 18th and 19th centuries.

The work's distinctive *jiěfāng* 解方 method — drug-by-drug doctrinal commentary on each formula — is comparable to that of Wāng Áng 汪昂's *Yīfāng jíjiě* 醫方集解 (1682) but applied specifically to gynecology. It became a standard reference for late-Qīng gynecological teaching, particularly in the Sūzhōu medical lineages.

## Other points of interest

Xú Dàchūn's medical polemics in the *Yīxué yuánliú lùn* contain a famous critique of contemporary gynecology that informs the *Nǚkē zhǐyào*: he rejects the routine *bǔzhōng yìqì tāng* prescription for menstrual disorders, arguing that mid-Qīng physicians were applying Lǐ Dōngyuán's spleen-stomach doctrine indiscriminately without correct syndrome-discrimination. The *Nǚkē zhǐyào* exemplifies this corrective programme, returning to *Shānghán lùn* / *Jīnguì* canonical principles for gynecological practice.
