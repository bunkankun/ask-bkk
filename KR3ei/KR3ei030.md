# Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng·Nǚkē 證治準繩·女科
*Standard for Diagnosis and Therapy: Women's Section*
by [[王肯堂]] (Wáng Kěntáng = Wáng Yǔtài = Niànxī jūshì, 1549–1613, late Míng)

## About the work

A five-*cè* gynecological section of the late-Míng comprehensive medical encyclopedia *Liù-kē Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* 六科證治準繩 (or simply *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng*, [[KR3e0078]], 120 *juǎn*) by Wáng Kěntáng 王肯堂 (1549–1613). The *Nǚ-kē zhǔn-shéng* portion was completed in *Wàn-lì dīng-wèi* (1607) along with the *Yòu-kē zhǔn-shéng* (paediatrics). The work is one of the great late-Míng gynecological *summae*, providing comprehensive coverage of menstrual disorders, *bēng-lòu*, *dài-xià*, *qiú-zǐ*, *tāi-qián*, *lín-chǎn*, *chǎn-hòu*, and women's miscellaneous diseases. Its *gāng-mù* 綱目 (outline-and-detail) systematic organisation was the principal model adopted (with re-arrangement) by Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jì-yīn gāng-mù* (1620; see [[KR3ei006]]).

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` `_000.txt` carries Wáng's *zì-xù* 自序 (author's preface). Wáng narrates the historiographic context: gynecological specialisation begins with Biǎn-què's *dài-xià* medicine at Hándān; Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Shāng-hán zá-bìng lùn* devoted one of three *juǎn* to women; Sūn Sī-miǎo's *Qiān-jīn fāng* gave priority of place to women's medicine. Wáng traces the line through Zàn Yīn 昝殷's *Chǎn-bǎo* 產寶 (Táng), Lǐ Shī-shèng 李師聖's *Chǎn-lùn* 產論 and Guō Jì-zhōng 郭稽中's accompanying formulary, the *Chǎn-yù bǎo-qìng jí* 產育寶慶集 of Dù Qiú 杜荍, and to Chén Zì-míng 陳自明's *Fù-rén dà-quán liáng-fāng* 婦人大全良方 — the principal Sòng *fù-kē* synthesis — which Wáng then takes as the foundation he organises systematically in his own work. The preface is one of the most detailed late-Míng historiographies of the *fù-kē* sub-discipline available in any medical source.

### Abstract

Wáng Kěntáng (1549–1613, see [[王肯堂]]) was a late-Míng Confucian-medical (*rú-yī* 儒醫) literatus-physician of Jīn-tán 金壇 (Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of 1589, with imperial-academy and provincial-administrative posts in Fujian, he turned to medicine after retirement. The *Zhèng-zhì zhǔn-shéng* (120 *juǎn*) was completed in stages over a decade and a half: *Zá-zhèng zhǔn-shéng* and *Lèi-fāng* 1597–1598; *Shāng-hán zhǔn-shéng* and *Yáng-yī zhǔn-shéng* 1604; *Yòu-kē zhǔn-shéng* and *Nǚ-kē zhǔn-shéng* 1607. The whole corpus is praised by the *Sì-kù* editors as "*the medical school's standard reference*" (*yī-jiā zhī guī-niè* 醫家之圭臬). The *Nǚ-kē zhǔn-shéng* — 1607, in five *cè* — is the gynecology section.

Doctrinally Wáng is balanced-comprehensive: neither over-cooling (against the Zhū Dān-xī tradition) nor over-warming (against the Zhāng Jièbīn *wēn-bǔ* counter-tradition). His method is to assemble citations from the major Sòng-Yuán-Míng authorities under each clinical heading with critical evaluative commentary, rather than to advance a partisan doctrinal position. This even-handed encyclopedic method made the work the principal late-Míng *standard reference* (*zhǔn-shéng* 準繩 = "plumb-line-and-cord", i.e. standard of measurement) for gynecological practice. Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jì-yīn gāng-mù* (1620; see [[KR3ei006]]) is a critical re-organisation of Wáng's *Nǚ-kē zhǔn-shéng* into a tighter *gāng-mù* hierarchical structure — Wǔ explicitly acknowledged Wáng as his principal source.

The work has been continuously in print since 1607 and remains in active scholarly use in modern PRC TCM gynecology research.

## Other points of interest

The *Nǚ-kē zhǔn-shéng* is the **textual foundation** of both major late-Míng / early-Qīng systematic gynecologies: Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jì-yīn gāng-mù* (1620; condensation and re-organisation) and Xiāo Xūn's *Nǚkē jīng-lún* (1684; citation-and-commentary expansion, see [[KR3ei029]]). Together these three works form the **trilogy of late-Míng / early-Qīng systematic gynecology** that shaped all subsequent imperial and Republican-era *fù-kē* practice and teaching.
