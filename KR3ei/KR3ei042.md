# Fù-shì nǚkē 傅氏女科
*The Fù-Family's Gynecology*
attributed to [[傅山]] (Fù Shān = Fù Qīngzhǔ, 1607–1684)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* supplementary gynecological collection attributed to Fù Shān 傅山, presented as a *gleaning* of *Nǚkē* materials not included in the main *Fù-Qīng-zhǔ nǚkē* [[KR3ei001]]. The text's first paragraph explicitly states: "*The Master originally had a Nǚkē circulating in the world; these few entries are not included in that Nǚkē, hence preserved here*" (女科（先生本有《女科》傳於世，此數條《女科》未載故存之）). The entries cover *chǎn-hòu* 產後 (post-natal), *tāi-lòu tāi-dòng* 胎漏胎動 (pregnancy bleeding and threatened miscarriage), *zǐ-xuán* 子懸 (suspended foetus), *bái-dài* 白帶 (white vaginal discharge), and other specific clinical scenarios with the relevant formulas.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension carries no separable preface; the work opens with the parenthetical editorial note quoted above identifying itself as a supplementary collection to the main *Fù-Qīng-zhǔ nǚkē*.

### Abstract

The work belongs to the broader Fù-Qīng-zhǔ pseudepigraphic medical corpus (see [[KR3ei001]] *Fù-Qīng-zhǔ nǚkē*; [[KR3ei021]] *Fù-Qīng-zhǔ nǚkē gē-kuò*; [[KR3ed080]] *Dà-xiǎo zhū-zhèng fāng-lùn*; [[KR3ed047]] *Fù-shì zá-fāng*). The catalog meta records no specific author and dynasty unspecified; internal evidence places it firmly in the post-1827 Fù-Qīng-zhǔ literary tradition. `notBefore` 1827 (= the date of the main *Fù-Qīng-zhǔ nǚkē* imprint) / `notAfter` 1900 brackets the range. For the complex authorship problems of the Fù-Shān-attributed medical corpus see [[KR3ei001]] and [[KR3ed080]].

The work is a brief supplement rather than a full systematic treatise. The prescribed formulas — *Bǔ-qì xuè jiā-jiǎn* 補氣血加減 modifications for post-partum conditions, *Bā-zhēn tāng* 八珍湯 derivatives for *tāi-lòu*, and similar — fit recognisably within the Fù-Qīng-zhǔ formulary style.
