# Guǎngsì yàoyǔ 廣嗣要語
*Essential Words for Producing Heirs*
by [[俞橋]] (Yú Qiáo, *zì* Zǐmù 子木, *hào* Sùhuízǐ 溯洄子, *fl.* mid-Míng)

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* mid-Míng fertility-treatise by **Yú Qiáo** 俞橋 of Hǎiníng 海寧 (Zhèjiāng), one of the most influential Míng-period works on the *zhǒngzǐ* (producing-heirs / seed-planting) topic. The work establishes Yú's distinctive synthesis: a *xiāntiān / hòutiān* "pre-celestial / post-celestial" inner-alchemy theoretical framework, combined with a practical clinical-prescription pharmacology drawing on standard Míng obstetrical-gynaecological literature. Yú himself produced three sons in late life by applying the *forward-cultivation* (*shùnxiū* 順修) method of his own treatise — the experiential anchor for his teaching.

### Abstract

Composition window: mid-16th century, set on the basis of the *Jiājìng* dīnghài (1527) reference in the preface (*Jiājìng dīnghài, yú Guǎngxìn Zhèng Zhōngchéng zhái* — Yú obtained the *Wǔzǐ yǎnzōng wán* recipe at Guǎngxìn in 1527), and a *terminus ante quem* before 1570 based on the work's first appearance in late-Míng catalog records. We bracket 1540–1570 to cover the mature productive period of Yú's career after the 1527 acquisition.

The work has five constituent parts:
1. **Diàolǐ jīngxuè lùn** 調理精血論 — On regulating the *jīng* and *xuè*. The theoretical chapter establishing the *gūyáng bùshēng / dúyīn bùchéng* doctrine.
2. **Zhízhǐ zhēnyuán lùn** 直指真源論 — On pointing-directly to the true source. The chapter on the inner-alchemical *zhìzhěn* and the role of mental quietude in fertility.
3. **Nánnǚ fúyào lùn** 男女服藥論 — On male and female drug-administration. The pharmacological core, introducing the *Sīxiān dān* 思仙丹 for the male and the *Yùyào qǐróng wán* 玉鑰啟榮丸 for the female as the work's two signature formulas.
4. **The *Jiāohuì yíjì rì* table** — listing the *yí* (auspicious) and *jì* (forbidden) days for conception, drawing on the *Wǔyùn liùqì* and the *Bǔshì lì*.
5. **The *yīnyáng xūshí sìtú*** 陰陽虛實四圖 — Four diagrams of the *yīnyáng* virtual-and-real types, with their corresponding clinical-prescriptions.

The work was widely cited in the late-Míng and Qīng *zhǒngzǐ* literature. It is preserved as the appendix to [[KR3ed126]] *Jǐshì zhēnbǎo* ([[王永]] Wáng Yǒng's compilation) and also in independent printings (the *Wàn Lùyuán* 萬鹿園 edition of the Brocade-Robed-Guard officer Wànjūn, and the [[劉仲恆]] Liú Zhònghéng edition of a District Magistrate). The Hǎiwài huíguī recension preserves the standalone Míng print witness.

## Other points of interest

Yú Qiáo's fusion of inner-alchemical theory with practical clinical pharmacology is one of the most-cited models in the late-Míng *zhǒngzǐ* literature. The work's *Wǔzǐ yǎnzōng wán* 五子衍宗丸 (枸杞子, 菟絲子, 車前子, 五味子, 覆盆子) — first published with explicit transmission-history in this work — became one of the canonical Chinese-medicine fertility formulas, still in use in contemporary TCM.
