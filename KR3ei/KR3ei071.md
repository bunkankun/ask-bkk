# Guǎng-sì yào-yǔ 廣嗣要語
*Essential Words for Producing Heirs*
by [[俞橋]] (Yú Qiáo, *zì* Zǐ-mù 子木, *hào* Sù-huí-zǐ 溯洄子, *fl.* mid-Míng)

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* mid-Míng fertility-treatise by **Yú Qiáo** 俞橋 of Hǎi-níng 海寧 (Zhèjiāng), one of the most influential Míng-period works on the *zhǒng-zǐ* (producing-heirs / seed-planting) topic. The work establishes Yú's distinctive synthesis: a *xiān-tiān / hòu-tiān* "pre-celestial / post-celestial" inner-alchemy theoretical framework, combined with a practical clinical-prescription pharmacology drawing on standard Míng obstetrical-gynaecological literature. Yú himself produced three sons in late life by applying the *forward-cultivation* (*shùn-xiū* 順修) method of his own treatise — the experiential anchor for his teaching.

### Abstract

Composition window: mid-16th century, set on the basis of the *Jiā-jìng* dīng-hài (1527) reference in the preface (*Jiā-jìng dīng-hài, yú Guǎng-xìn Zhèng Zhōng-chéng zhái* — Yú obtained the *Wǔ-zǐ yǎn-zōng wán* recipe at Guǎng-xìn in 1527), and a *terminus ante quem* before 1570 based on the work's first appearance in late-Míng catalog records. We bracket 1540–1570 to cover the mature productive period of Yú's career after the 1527 acquisition.

The work has five constituent parts:
1. **Diào-lǐ jīng-xuè lùn** 調理精血論 — On regulating the *jīng* and *xuè*. The theoretical chapter establishing the *gū-yáng bù-shēng / dú-yīn bù-chéng* doctrine.
2. **Zhí-zhǐ zhēn-yuán lùn** 直指真源論 — On pointing-directly to the true source. The chapter on the inner-alchemical *zhì-zhěn* and the role of mental quietude in fertility.
3. **Nán-nǚ fú-yào lùn** 男女服藥論 — On male and female drug-administration. The pharmacological core, introducing the *Sī-xiān dān* 思仙丹 for the male and the *Yù-yào qǐ-róng wán* 玉鑰啟榮丸 for the female as the work's two signature formulas.
4. **The *Jiāo-huì yí-jì rì* table** — listing the *yí* (auspicious) and *jì* (forbidden) days for conception, drawing on the *Wǔ-yùn liù-qì* and the *Bǔ-shì lì*.
5. **The *yīn-yáng xū-shí sì-tú*** 陰陽虛實四圖 — Four diagrams of the *yīn-yáng* virtual-and-real types, with their corresponding clinical-prescriptions.

The work was widely cited in the late-Míng and Qīng *zhǒng-zǐ* literature. It is preserved as the appendix to [[KR3ed126]] *Jǐ-shì zhēn-bǎo* (Wáng Yǒng's compilation) and also in independent printings (the *Wàn Lù-yuán* 萬鹿園 edition of the Brocade-Robed-Guard officer Wàn-jūn, and the *Liú Zhòng-héng* 劉仲恆 edition of a District Magistrate). The Hǎi-wài huí-guī recension preserves the standalone Míng print witness.

## Other points of interest

Yú Qiáo's fusion of inner-alchemical theory with practical clinical pharmacology is one of the most-cited models in the late-Míng *zhǒng-zǐ* literature. The work's *Wǔ-zǐ yǎn-zōng wán* 五子衍宗丸 (枸杞子, 菟絲子, 車前子, 五味子, 覆盆子) — first published with explicit transmission-history in this work — became one of the canonical Chinese-medicine fertility formulas, still in use in contemporary TCM.
