# Nǚkē jīngyào 女科精要
*The Refined Essentials of Women's Medicine*
by [[馮兆張]] (Féng Zhàozhāng, early Qīng)

## About the work

A three-*juǎn* systematic gynecology work by the early-Qīng physician Féng Zhàozhāng 馮兆張 (*zì* Chǔ-zhān 楚瞻), forming an integral section of his major medical encyclopedia *Féng-shì jǐn-náng mì-lù* 馮氏錦囊秘錄 (*The Secret Records from Féng's Brocade Bag*, 50 *juǎn*, completed 1702). The work is structured by topical *mén* 門: *yuè-jīng mén* 月經門 (menstrual disorders), *jīng-bìng mén* 經病門 (menstrual-disease commentary), *bēng-lòu mén* 崩漏門, *dài-xià mén* 帶下門, *qiú-zǐ mén* 求子門, *tāi-qián mén* 胎前門, *lín-chǎn mén* 臨產門, *chǎn-hòu mén* 產後門. Each topical *mén* opens with a substantial *zhū-lùn* 諸論 (collected discussions) gathering classical and Sòng-Yuán-Míng authorities (frequently citing the *Sù-wèn*, Wáng Bīng 王冰, Lǐ Dōng-yuán, Zhū Dān-xī, and Sūn Sī-miǎo) before proceeding to formulary practice.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension carries the body of the work but no separable preface; that material is associated with the parent *Jǐn-náng mì-lù*. The opening of the *yuè-jīng mén* offers a substantial doctrinal opening establishing the Sòng *fù-rén yǐ xuè wéi běn* 婦人以血為本 framework with citations from the *Sù-wèn*, Wáng Bīng's commentary, and Lǐ Dōng-yuán's *Pí-wèi lùn* 脾胃論.

### Abstract

Féng Zhàozhāng 馮兆張 (*zì* Chǔ-zhān 楚瞻) was a Hǎi-yán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng) physician of the early Qīng who completed the *Féng-shì jǐn-náng mì-lù* 馮氏錦囊秘錄 in 50 *juǎn* in 1702 (*Kāng-xī* 41). The *Jǐn-náng mì-lù* is a major early-Qīng systematic medical encyclopedia, organised by sub-discipline: internal medicine, paediatrics, gynecology, *shāng-hán*, *wài-kē* (external medicine), pulse-diagnosis, and *materia medica*. The *Nǚkē jīng-yào* is the gynecology section, comprising approximately three *juǎn* of the parent work. The work survives both as part of the *Jǐn-náng mì-lù* parent compilation and as separately-extracted standalone editions, the latter circulated as `Nǚkē jīng-yào` from the late Kāng-xī era onwards.

Doctrinally Féng combines the Lǐ-Zhū *bǔ-zhōng* / *zī-yīn* synthesis with extensive engagement with the Sòng-Yuán canonical tradition, citing Wáng Bīng's *Sù-wèn* commentary, Sūn Sī-miǎo's *Qiān-jīn yào-fāng*, Chén Zì-míng's *Fù-rén dà-quán liáng-fāng*, and the *Hé-jì jú-fāng* directly. The work's distinctive feature is its emphasis on early-life female constitutional development: Féng argues that constitutional weakness from improperly nourished early adolescence is the principal long-term cause of adult gynecological disorders. The opening of the *jīng-bìng mén* offers detailed advice on the timing of marriage in relation to *tiān-guǐ* 天癸 onset.

The *Jǐn-náng mì-lù* was widely reprinted in the Qīng and Republican periods and remains a standard reference in modern PRC TCM curriculum. The *Nǚkē jīng-yào* is one of its most-extracted sections.

## Other points of interest

Féng's *Jǐn-náng mì-lù* is one of the earliest major Qīng systematic medical compilations and a key bridge between the late-Míng *gāng-mù* synthesis (Wáng Kěntáng, Wǔ Zhīwàng) and the imperially-sponsored *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn* of 1742. The work's wide circulation in popular Qīng medical book markets made it one of the principal channels by which late-Míng gynecological doctrine reached 18th-century practitioners.
