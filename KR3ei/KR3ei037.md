# Tóngyuán yīshū·Fùrénkē 彤園醫書·婦人科
*Tóngyuán's Medical Books: Women's Section*
by [[鄭玉壇]] (Zhèng Yùtán = Tóngyuán-zhǔ-rén 彤園主人, mid-Qīng)

## About the work

A six-*juǎn* gynecological section of Zhèng Yùtán 鄭玉壇's larger medical compendium *Tóng-yuán yī-shū* 彤園醫書. The work covers the canonical *fù-kē* topics — *tiáo-jīng*, *jīng-bì*, *bēng-lòu*, *dài-xià*, *jī-jù* 積聚, *zhēng-jiǎ* 癥瘕, *sì-yù* 嗣育, *zhǒng-zǐ*, *tāi-qián*, *chǎn-hòu*, *rǔ-jí* 乳疾 (breast conditions), *qián-yīn* 前陰 (genital conditions) — explicitly framed as a *zhé-zhōng qún-shū* 折衷群書 (compromise-synthesis of multiple sources) drawing on the Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng tradition.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension `_001.txt` opens with a *fù-kē zǒng-kuò* 婦科總括 (general summary of women's medicine), without a separable author-preface. The work explicitly references the editor's parent compilation *Dà-fāng-mài* 大方脈 ("*Major Pulse-and-Therapy*") as the prerequisite primary reading.

### Abstract

Zhèng Yùtán 鄭玉壇 (*hào* Tóng-yuán-zhǔ-rén 彤園主人) was a mid-Qīng physician who issued the *Tóng-yuán yī-shū* 彤園醫書, a comprehensive medical compendium organised by specialty. The *Tóng-yuán* (Vermillion-Garden) studio name appears in late-Jiā-qìng / Dào-guāng-era imprints. The work is best dated to the early 19th century; `notBefore` 1801 / `notAfter` 1850 is a defensible bracket. The catalog meta dynasty 清 is correct.

Doctrinally the work is *zhé-zhōng* 折衷 (compromise-synthesis), citing Kòu Zōng-shí 寇宗奭's "*better treat ten men than one woman*" maxim in the *zǒng-kuò* opening and drawing on the standard late-imperial gynecological synthesis (Chén Zì-míng, Xuē Jǐ, Wǔ Zhīwàng, Wáng Kěntáng, Zhāng Jièbīn, the *Yī-zōng jīn-jiàn*). The work belongs to the mid-Qīng popular practitioner-textbook tradition; it does not advance an original doctrinal position but provides a portable systematic synthesis of the major gynecological positions.
