# Tóngyuán yīshū·Fùrénkē 彤園醫書·婦人科
*Tóngyuán's Medical Books: Women's Section*
by [[鄭玉壇]] (Zhèng Yùtán = Tóngyuánzhǔrén 彤園主人, mid-Qīng)

## About the work

A six-*juǎn* gynecological section of Zhèng Yùtán 鄭玉壇's larger medical compendium *Tóngyuán yīshū* 彤園醫書. The work covers the canonical *fùkē* topics — *tiáojīng*, *jīngbì*, *bēnglòu*, *dàixià*, *jījù* 積聚, *zhēngjiǎ* 癥瘕, *sìyù* 嗣育, *zhǒngzǐ*, *tāiqián*, *chǎnhòu*, *rǔjí* 乳疾 (breast conditions), *qiányīn* 前陰 (genital conditions) — explicitly framed as a *zhézhōng qúnshū* 折衷群書 (compromise-synthesis of multiple sources) drawing on the SòngYuánMíngQīng tradition.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension `_001.txt` opens with a *fùkē zǒngkuò* 婦科總括 (general summary of women's medicine), without a separable author-preface. The work explicitly references the editor's parent compilation *Dàfāngmài* 大方脈 ("*Major Pulse-and-Therapy*") as the prerequisite primary reading.

### Abstract

Zhèng Yùtán 鄭玉壇 (*hào* Tóngyuánzhǔrén 彤園主人) was a mid-Qīng physician who issued the *Tóngyuán yīshū* 彤園醫書, a comprehensive medical compendium organised by specialty. The *Tóngyuán* (Vermillion-Garden) studio name appears in late-Jiā-qìng / Dào-guāng-era imprints. The work is best dated to the early 19th century; `notBefore` 1801 / `notAfter` 1850 is a defensible bracket. The catalog meta dynasty 清 is correct.

Doctrinally the work is *zhézhōng* 折衷 (compromise-synthesis), citing Kòu Zōngshí 寇宗奭's "*better treat ten men than one woman*" maxim in the *zǒngkuò* opening and drawing on the standard late-imperial gynecological synthesis (Chén Zìmíng, Xuē Jǐ, Wǔ Zhīwàng, Wáng Kěntáng, Zhāng Jièbīn, the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn*). The work belongs to the mid-Qīng popular practitioner-textbook tradition; it does not advance an original doctrinal position but provides a portable systematic synthesis of the major gynecological positions.
