# Nǚkē bǎiwèn 女科百問
*A Hundred Questions on Women's Medicine*
by [[宋仲甫]] (Sòng Zhòngfǔ = Qí Zhòngfǔ 齊仲甫, *fl.* 1220, Southern Sòng)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* (*shàng* / *xià*) Southern-Sòng gynecology by Sòng Zhòngfǔ 宋仲甫 (= Qí Zhòngfǔ 齊仲甫 — name variant in the transmitted tradition), structured as a sequence of one hundred numbered *wèn-dá* 問答 (question-and-answer) entries covering the canonical *fù-kē* topics: *jīng-shuǐ* 經水 (menstruation), *bēng-lòu* 崩漏, *dài-xià* 帶下, *qiú-zǐ* 求子, *tāi-qián* 胎前, *lín-chǎn* 臨產, *chǎn-hòu* 產後, and miscellaneous women's diseases. Each question articulates a clinical scenario and the corresponding answer provides étiological discussion, classical citation, and prescribed formula. The work opens with a remarkable opening question on "*jīng-xuè yǐ fēn nán-nǚ zhī běn-yuán*" 精血以分男女之本源 ("*essence and blood as the root-source by which men and women are differentiated*") — a foundational doctrinal opening that sets the work's *jīng-xuè* (essence-blood) framework.

### Abstract

Sòng Zhòngfǔ 宋仲甫 is identified in Sòng-Yuán medical-bibliographic literature as Qí Zhòngfǔ 齊仲甫 (the *Sòng-shǐ yì-wén zhì* and several early bibliographies use this form); the *Sòng/Qí* variant probably reflects scribal variation in the medieval transmission. He is a Southern-Sòng gynecologist active in the early 13th century. The *Nǚkē bǎi-wèn* is one of the major Sòng-period gynecological works alongside Chén Zì-míng's *Fù-rén dà-quán liáng-fāng* 婦人大全良方 (1237). The catalog's dating 南宋 is correct; the work is conventionally dated c. 1220–1230. The *bǎi-wèn* 百問 catechetical format would later be replicated in numerous Qīng popular gynecologies (compare [[KR3ei004]] *Fùkē wèndá* and [[KR3ei034]] *Fùkē bǎi-biàn*).

Doctrinally the work reflects the mature Northern-Song / early-Southern-Song synthesis: it integrates the *Sù-wèn* / *Líng-shū* canonical framework with Sūn Sī-miǎo's *Qiān-jīn yào-fāng*, Wáng Tāo's *Wài-tái mì-yào*, Chén Yán's *Sān-yīn fāng* (1174), and the *Hé-jì jú-fāng*. It is a major secondary witness to Northern-Sòng gynecological doctrine. The work was widely cited by Yuán-Míng-Qīng gynecologists and remains in active use in modern PRC TCM scholarship.
