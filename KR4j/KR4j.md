# KR4j 詞曲類 — Lyric Poetry and Songbooks

## Scope and scholarly tradition

The *Cí-qǔ lèi* 詞曲類 is the smallest of the five subdivisions of *Jí-bù* 集部 (Collected Works) in the *Sìkù quánshū* arrangement, but bibliographically the most contested. It is the standing imperial admission that *cí* 詞 ("lyrics of unequal lines set to a tune") and *qǔ* 曲 ("aria") — genres that Sòng and Yuán catalogers had often hesitated to admit at all, on the grounds that they were *zhèng-tǒng* 正統 *shī*-poetry's vernacular and "music-hall" descendants — deserve a legitimate place in the imperial library. Wilkinson (§30.2.1, Box 172) places *cí* and *qǔ* in the same dynastic-genre sequence as *shī* and *fù*, with *cí* "Tang developed; Song perfected, but lost the connection with songs," dated from the *Huā-jiān jí* 花間集 (940), and *qǔ* "reaching its height in the Yuan." The KR4j division covers exactly this arc: from the late-Táng / Five-Dynasties *Huā-jiān* corpus through the Sòng *cí* canon, the late-Sòng loyalist *yǒng-wù* anthologies, the Yuán transition into *sǎn-qǔ* 散曲, the Míng *cí-huà* 詞話 tradition, and the *Kāng-xī*–*Qián-lóng* imperial canonization project that produced the *Yù-xuǎn lì-dài shī-yú* [[KR4j0074]], the *Yù-dìng cí-pǔ* [[KR4j0086]], and the *Yù-dìng qǔ-pǔ* [[KR4j0089]].

The division is internally heterogeneous in three ways. First, by genre: it bundles *cí* (personal collections + anthologies + *cí-huà* + *cí-pǔ*), *sǎn-qǔ* + *xì-qǔ*-prosody (*Zhōng-yuán yīn-yùn* [[KR4j0090]], *qǔ-pǔ*, *qǔ-huà*), and the *Kāng-xī*-imperial canon-formation works under a single roof. Second, by chronology: it runs from 940 (*Huā-jiān jí*) to the late-Qián-lóng *Sìkù* apparatus (the 1782–1786 *Kǎo-zhèng* [[KR4j0091]] is bound at the division's tail). Third, by aesthetic axis: it crosses the two great stylistic poles of the *cí* — the *wǎn-yuē* 婉約 (restrained, lyrical) line that runs from the *Huā-jiān* through Yàn Shū [[晏殊]], Zhōu Bāng-yàn [[周邦彥]], Lǐ Qīng-zhào [[李清照]], Jiāng Kuí [[姜夔]], and Wú Wén-yīng [[吳文英]]; and the *háo-fàng* 豪放 (bold, expansive) line opened by Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] and brought to its supreme statement by Xīn Qì-jí [[辛棄疾]]. The *Sìkù tíyào* to the *Dōng-pō cí* [[KR4j0005]] codifies the canonical historiography of this split: Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] was the Bái Jū-yì of the *cí* form, Sū Shì its Hán Yù — opening the road for the entire Xīn Qì-jí line that defines half the post-Sòng *cí* canon.

## Important texts and text clusters

- *Huā-jiān jí* 花間集 [[KR4j0062]] — the founding anthology of the *cí* (940, Hòu-Shǔ court of Mèng Chǎng), edited by Zhào Chóng-zuò [[趙崇祚]]; 500 *cí* by 18 late-Táng / Five-Dynasties poets and the textual source of the early *cí* repertoire.
- The Northern-Sòng *xiǎo-lìng* core — *Zhū-yù cí* 珠玉詞 of Yàn Shū [[KR4j0001]], *Liù-yī cí* 六一詞 of Ōuyáng Xiū [[KR4j0002]], *Ān-lù jí* 安陸集 of Zhāng Xiān [[KR4j0004]], *Xiǎo-shān cí* 小山詞 of Yàn Jǐ-dào [[KR4j0010]] — the canonical entry-point to the polite *xiǎo-lìng* in the Féng Yán-sì manner.
- *Yuè-zhāng jí* 樂章集 [[KR4j0003]] — Liǔ Yǒng's lyric corpus; the foundational corpus of the *màn-cí* 慢詞 ("long-tune") form and the demotic urban *cí* register.
- *Dōng-pō cí* 東坡詞 [[KR4j0005]] — Sū Shì's *cí*; the *biàn-gé* 變格 ("variant register") that opened the *háo-fàng* line and the locus classicus of *Niàn-nú jiāo · Chì-bì huái-gǔ*.
- *Piàn-yù cí* 片玉詞 [[KR4j0015]] — Zhōu Bāng-yàn's prosodically supreme *cí*; head of the *Dà-shèng yuè-fǔ* 大晟樂府 reform and the model for the entire Southern-Sòng *gé-lǜ* 格律 school, with three matching-*cí* collections (Fāng Qiān-lǐ [[KR4j0019]], Yáng Zé-mín, Chén Yǔn-pìng) preserved alongside.
- *Shù-yù cí* 漱玉詞 [[KR4j0027]] — Lǐ Qīng-zhào's slim seventeen-piece collection, the most exalted single voice in the Chinese *cí* and the canonical *Yì-ān tǐ* 易安體.
- *Jià-xuān cí* 稼軒詞 [[KR4j0040]] — Xīn Qì-jí's vast corpus (c. 626 *cí*), supreme *háo-fàng* monument and the canonical patriotic-loyalist *cí*-collection.
- *Bái-shí dào-rén gē-qǔ* 白石道人歌曲 [[KR4j0047]] — Jiāng Kuí's *cí* including 13 *zì-zhì-qǔ* 自製曲 (self-composed tunes) with the only surviving Sòng *cí* notation (*páng-pǔ* 旁譜) — the single most musicologically important *cí* text.
- *Mèng-chuāng gǎo* 夢窗稿 [[KR4j0048]] — Wú Wén-yīng's dense, opaque late-Southern-Sòng *cí*; the Lǐ Shāng-yǐn 李商隱 of the *cí*-form, read against Jiāng Kuí as the prosodic-school co-pole.
- The late-Sòng anthology cluster — *Yuè-fǔ yǎ-cí* 樂府雅詞 [[KR4j0065]] of Zēng Zào [[曾慥]], *Huā-ān cí-xuǎn* 花菴詞選 [[KR4j0066]] and *Zhōng-xīng yǐ-lái jué-miào cí-xuǎn* [[KR4j0067]] of Huáng Shēng [[黃昇]], *Jué-miào hǎo-cí jiān* 絕妙好詞箋 [[KR4j0071]] of Zhōu Mì [[周密]] (annotated by the Qīng commentators Chá Wéi-rén [[查爲仁]] and Lì È [[厲鶚]]) — the principal Sòng-Yuán selections that constituted the *cí*-canon for late-imperial reading.
- *Yuè-fǔ bǔ-tí* 樂府補題 [[KR4j0072]] — the politically supreme *yǒng-wù* 詠物 anthology: 37 exchange-*cí* by 14 Sòng-loyalist *yí-mín* poets at five gatherings, 1278–1290; read since Wáng Guó-wéi as allegorical elegy for the desecrated Sòng imperial tombs at Shào-xīng.
- *Bì-jī màn-zhì* 碧鷄漫志 [[KR4j0078]] and *Yuè-fǔ zhǐ-mí* 樂府指迷 [[KR4j0080]] — the two founding Sòng *cí*-treatises: Wáng Zhuó's historical-prosodic survey of the *cí* as song-form (1145–1149) and Shěn Yì-fǔ's practical compositional handbook in the Wú Wén-yīng manner (1242–1250). The textual basis of all subsequent *cí-huà*.
- *Zhōng-yuán yīn-yùn* 中原音韻 [[KR4j0090]] — Zhōu Dé-qīng's 1324 phonological manual; the founding statement of modern *Mandarin* phonology (19 rhyme-groups; *yīn / yáng-píng* division; *rù*-redistribution) and the indispensable reference for reading Yuán *běi-qǔ*.
- The Yuán *sǎn-qǔ* anthology pair — *Tài-píng yuè-fǔ* 太平樂府 [[KR4j0081]] of Yáng Cháo-yīng [[楊朝英]] (1351) and the *Lí-yuán àn-shì yuè-fǔ xīn-shēng* 梨園按試樂府新聲 [[KR4j0082]] — the principal documentary sources for Yuán *sǎn-qǔ*.
- The Qīng anthology and prosody triad — *Cí-zōng* 詞綜 [[KR4j0075]] of Zhū Yí-zūn [[朱彝尊]] (1678; founding charter of the *Zhè-xī-pài* 浙西派), *Shí-wǔ jiā cí* 十五家詞 [[KR4j0076]] of Sūn Mò [[孫默]] (1670s, *Yáng-xiàn-pài* 陽羨派 representative), and *Cí-lǜ* 詞律 [[KR4j0087]] of Wàn Shù [[萬樹]] (1687) — together establishing the Qīng *cí* revival.
- The *Kāng-xī* imperial canon — *Yù-xuǎn lì-dài shī-yú* 御選歷代詩餘 [[KR4j0074]] (1707, c. 9,000 *cí* by c. 1,540 poets in 120 *juǎn*), *Yù-dìng cí-pǔ* 御定詞譜 [[KR4j0086]] (1715, 826 tunes), *Yù-dìng qǔ-pǔ* 御定曲譜 [[KR4j0089]] (1715, Northern + Southern *qǔ*). The three together constitute the institutionalized Qīng *cí-qǔ* prosody.
- *Cí-yuàn cóng-tán* 詞苑叢談 [[KR4j0085]] of Xú Qiú [[徐釚]] — the most comprehensive of all Qīng *cí-huà*, organized into seven thematic *mén* (form, sound, criticism, anecdote, textual verification, jest, miscellany); the Qīng analytical encyclopedia of *cí*.
- *Kē-xuě cí* 珂雪詞 [[KR4j0061]] of Cáo Zhēn-jí [[曹貞吉]] — the only Qīng-period *cí* collection admitted to the *Sìkù* on its author's own merit, standing between *Yáng-xiàn* and *Zhè-xī* aesthetic poles.

## Important persons

- [[趙崇祚]] — Hòu-Shǔ courtier; editor of the *Huā-jiān jí*, the founding *cí*-anthology of the tradition.
- [[晏殊]] — Northern-Sòng councillor; foundational *xiǎo-lìng* master in the Féng Yán-sì line.
- [[柳永]] — first great expander of the *cí* into the *màn* (long-tune) form; demotic Northern-Sòng urban *cí* and the songs of the courtesan-quarters.
- [[蘇軾]] — supreme *biàn-gé* poet; opened the *háo-fàng* line and made the *cí* a fully literary form.
- [[周邦彥]] — head of the *Dà-shèng yuè-fǔ* Imperial Music Bureau under Huīzōng; the supreme musical theorist of the *cí* and parent of the Southern-Sòng *gé-lǜ* school.
- [[李清照]] — supreme woman *cí*-poet; survivor of the Sòng-Jīn rupture and author of the *Jīn-shí lù hòu xù*; canonical *Yì-ān-tǐ*.
- [[辛棄疾]] — supreme *háo-fàng* master; ex-Jīn-resistance fighter, sometime official, and the largest single *cí*-corpus of the Sòng.
- [[陸游]] — the great patriot poet, also represented by his *cí* [[KR4j0043]].
- [[姜夔]] — supreme prosodic-school master; only Sòng *cí*-writer whose own musical notation survives; ancestor of all subsequent musicological recovery of the Sòng *cí*.
- [[吳文英]] — supreme late-Southern-Sòng prosodist; the "Lǐ Shāng-yǐn of the *cí*"; co-pole of the *gé-lǜ* line with Jiāng Kuí.
- [[蔣捷]] — *Sòng* loyalist *cí*-writer of the *Zhú-shān cí* [[KR4j0053]]; canonized in the *Cí-zōng* as a *Zhè-xī* auxiliary.
- [[周密]] — late-Sòng loyalist and connoisseur; compiler of the *Jué-miào hǎo-cí* and a contributor to the *Yuè-fǔ bǔ-tí*.
- [[黃昇]] — Southern-Sòng anthologist whose *Huā-ān cí-xuǎn* preserved the basic life-data and corpus of 88 Southern-Sòng poets.
- [[王灼]] — Southern-Sòng theorist; author of the founding *cí*-treatise *Bì-jī màn-zhì*.
- [[沈義父]] — Southern-Sòng pupil of Wú Wén-yīng and author of the compositional handbook *Yuè-fǔ zhǐ-mí*.
- [[周德清]] — Yuán phonologist; *Zhōng-yuán yīn-yùn*, the codification of modern Mandarin phonology for the *běi-qǔ* singer.
- [[楊朝英]] — Yuán anthologist; *Tài-píng yuè-fǔ* and *Yáng-chūn bái-xuě*, the principal Yuán *sǎn-qǔ* anthologies.
- [[陳霆]] — Míng *cí-huà* compiler; *Zhū-shān-táng cí-huà* preserves Sòng *yì-piān* otherwise lost.
- [[朱彝尊]] — supreme Qīng *cí*-scholar and founder of the *Zhè-xī-pài*; compiler of the *Cí-zōng*.
- [[萬樹]] — Qīng prosodist; *Cí-lǜ*, the rigorous private *cí*-prosody manual that became the model for all subsequent work.
- [[徐釚]] — Qīng *cí*-historian; *Cí-yuàn cóng-tán*, the comprehensive Qīng compendium of *cí*-historical material.
- [[聖祖玄燁]] — *Kāng-xī* emperor; patron of the imperial *cí-qǔ* canon-formation project.
- [[王奕清]] — *Hàn-lín* editor under *Kāng-xī*; chief compiler of both the *Yù-dìng cí-pǔ* and the *Yù-dìng qǔ-pǔ*.
- [[曹貞吉]] — early-*Kāng-xī cí*-revival poet; the only Qīng *cí*-author with a personal collection admitted to the *Sìkù*.

## Topics

- **Wǎn-yuē 婉約 vs. háo-fàng 豪放.** The single most consequential interpretive axis of the *cí*-tradition. The *Huā-jiān*-line poets and Northern-Sòng *xiǎo-lìng* masters (Yàn Shū [[晏殊]], Zhāng Xiān [[張先]]) established the restrained-lyrical mode; Liǔ Yǒng [[柳永]] opened the demotic line; Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] then opened the *háo-fàng* line, brought to its supreme statement by Xīn Qì-jí [[辛棄疾]] in the Southern Sòng. The *Sìkù tíyào* to *Dōng-pō cí* [[KR4j0005]] is the canonical Qīng formulation: Liǔ as the Bái Jū-yì of the *cí*, Sū as its Hán Yù. The Qīng *Zhè-xī-pài* of [[朱彝尊]] explicitly canonized the *wǎn-yuē* line through Jiāng Kuí [[姜夔]]; the *Cháng-zhōu-pài* of Zhāng Huì-yán in the early 19th century positioned itself in opposition. Every comprehensive anthology in the division — *Huā-ān cí-xuǎn* [[KR4j0066]], *Cí-zōng* [[KR4j0075]], *Yù-xuǎn lì-dài shī-yú* [[KR4j0074]] — takes a definite editorial position on this axis.

- **Cí as song-form vs. cí as literary form.** Whether the *cí* should be conceived primarily as a singable musical form or as a written literary form is the founding theoretical question of the genre, opened by Wáng Zhuó's *Bì-jī màn-zhì* [[KR4j0078]] (which mourns the loss of the Táng *gē-fǎ* and treats the Sòng *cí* as a residual song-tradition) and by Shěn Yì-fǔ's *Yuè-fǔ zhǐ-mí* [[KR4j0080]] (which formalizes the *qù-shēng* / *píng-shǎng-rù* substitution rule). The question becomes acute with the loss of Sòng *qǔ-pǔ* by the Yuán, after which *cí* could only be a literary form. Jiāng Kuí's *zì-zhì-qǔ* in [[KR4j0047]] — with surviving notation — remains the principal documentary anchor of the song-form reading. Wàn Shù's *Cí-lǜ* [[KR4j0087]] takes the literary form to its logical end with a *qū-pǔ*-based prosody recovered by collation.

- **Yǎ-zhèng 雅正 as anthology principle.** The classifying principle that runs from Zēng Zào's *Yuè-fǔ yǎ-cí* [[KR4j0065]] through Zhōu Mì's *Jué-miào hǎo-cí* to Zhū Yí-zūn's *Cí-zōng* [[KR4j0075]] — *cí* must conform to *yǎ-zhèng* (the canonically refined and orthodox) and not to the *Huā-jiān* / *Cǎo-táng* line of decorative pretty pieces. The *Yù-xuǎn lì-dài shī-yú* [[KR4j0074]] institutionalizes the *yǎ-zhèng* preference as imperial taste.

- **Yǒng-wù 詠物 as political allegory.** The Southern-Sòng *yǒng-wù* mode — descriptive lyrics on objects — became, in the loyalist generation after 1276, a vehicle for political allegory. The *Yuè-fǔ bǔ-tí* [[KR4j0072]] is the supreme example: five sets of exchange-*cí* on dragon-spittle perfume, white lotus, water-shield, cicada, and crab — read by Wáng Guó-wéi and modern scholars as figures for the desecrated Sòng imperial tombs. The *yǒng-wù* mode shapes the late Wú Wén-yīng [[吳文英]] [[KR4j0048]] and the entire late-Southern-Sòng tail.

- **The Sòng-Yuán transition: cí into sǎn-qǔ.** As Wilkinson's Box 172 sets out, *qǔ* reached its height in the Yuán as a successor genre to *cí*: the same musical-lyric impulse moving to a new prestige form. The transition is documented within the division by the late-Sòng-into-Yuán *cí*-writers Zhāng Yán, Bái Pǔ [[KR4j0059]], Zhāng Hùi [[KR4j0060]], and by the founding Yuán phonological and anthology works ([[KR4j0090]], [[KR4j0081]], [[KR4j0082]]). The *Yù-dìng qǔ-pǔ* [[KR4j0089]] then institutionalizes the unified *cí-qǔ* prosodic tradition.

- **North vs. South in qǔ.** The Yuán *běi-qǔ* tradition (rooted in *Zhōng-yuán yīn-yùn* phonology [[KR4j0090]] and the *Tài-píng yuè-fǔ* anthology [[KR4j0081]]) and the Míng *nán-qǔ* tradition (running through the *Yōng-xī yuè-fǔ* [[KR4j0068]] and the *Wú-sāo hé-biān* [[KR4j0069]]) form the two great regional song-traditions of the Yuán-Míng period. The *Yù-dìng qǔ-pǔ* [[KR4j0089]] codifies both in parallel registers.

- **Cí by women.** The *Cí-qǔ lèi* admits two single-author collections by Sòng women: the *Shù-yù cí* of Lǐ Qīng-zhào [[KR4j0027]] and the *Duàn-cháng cí* 斷腸詞 of Zhū Shū-zhēn [[KR4j0058]] — together the principal documentary basis of the Sòng-era *guī-yīn* 閨音 ("women's voice") tradition. Chén Tíng's *Zhū-shān-táng cí-huà* [[KR4j0083]] preserves Wáng Zhāo-yí's *Mǎn-jiāng-hóng*, the most-anthologized late-Sòng court-lady's *cí*.

- **Cí-pǔ and qǔ-pǔ as scholarly enterprise.** The Qīng *kǎo-jù*-era *cí-pǔ* / *qǔ-pǔ* project — Wàn Shù's *Cí-lǜ* [[KR4j0087]] privately and the imperial *Cí-pǔ* [[KR4j0086]] / *Qǔ-pǔ* [[KR4j0089]] institutionally — is the longest-running scholarly enterprise within the division. It reflects the wider *kǎo-jù*-era ambition to recover by philological-prosodic means what the loss of the Sòng *qǔ-pǔ* had taken away.

- 1324 — *Zhōng-yuán yīn-yùn* 中原音韻 [[KR4j0090]] — Zhōu Dé-qīng's phonological codification for *běi-qǔ*; founding statement of modern Mandarin phonology.
- c. 1287–1368 — *Tuì-yán cí* 蛻巖詞 [[KR4j0060]] and *Tiān-lài jí* 天籟集 [[KR4j0059]] — Zhāng Hùi's and Bái Pǔ's Yuán-era *cí*.
- 1351 — *Cháo-yě xīn-shēng tài-píng yuè-fǔ* 朝野新聲太平樂府 [[KR4j0081]] — Yáng Cháo-yīng's anthology of Yuán *sǎn-qǔ*.
- 1502 — *Zhū-shān-táng cí-huà* 渚山堂詞話 [[KR4j0083]] — Chén Tíng's Míng *cí-huà* preserving lost Sòng-Yuán pieces (dated by author's 1502 *jìn-shì*; window 1505–1540).
- 1550 — *Huā-cǎo cuì-biān* 花草稡編 [[KR4j0073]] — Chén Yào-wén's 24-*juǎn* Míng *cí* compendium (also the transmission-vehicle for *Yuè-fǔ zhǐ-mí*).
- c. 1660–1698 — *Kē-xuě cí* 珂雪詞 [[KR4j0061]] — Cáo Zhēn-jí's early-*Kāng-xī* revival *cí*; only Qīng author's collection admitted to the *Sìkù*.
- 1670s — *Shí-wǔ jiā cí* 十五家詞 [[KR4j0076]] — Sūn Mò's *Yáng-xiàn-pài* anthology.
- 1678 — *Cí-zōng* 詞綜 [[KR4j0075]] — Zhū Yí-zūn's founding *Zhè-xī-pài* anthology.
- 1680–1688 — *Cí-yuàn cóng-tán* 詞苑叢談 [[KR4j0085]] — Xú Qiú's seven-*mén* comprehensive Qīng *cí-huà*.
- 1687 — *Cí-lǜ* 詞律 [[KR4j0087]] — Wàn Shù's rigorous private *cí*-prosody manual.
- 1685–1707 — *Yù-xuǎn lì-dài shī-yú* 御選歷代詩餘 [[KR4j0074]] — *Kāng-xī*-commissioned comprehensive *cí* canon (120 *juǎn*).
- 1715 — *Yù-dìng cí-pǔ* 御定詞譜 [[KR4j0086]] and *Yù-dìng qǔ-pǔ* 御定曲譜 [[KR4j0089]] — the institutionalized Qīng prosodic system for *cí* and *qǔ*.
- 1782–1786 — *Qīn-dìng Sì-kù quán-shū kǎo-zhèng* 欽定四庫全書考證 [[KR4j0091]] — the textual-collation apparatus of the entire *Sìkù*, bound at the tail of the *Jí-bù*.
