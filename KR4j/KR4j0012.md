# Gū-xī cí 姑溪詞
 *Lyrics of Gū-xī*
by [[李之儀]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Gū-xī cí* 姑溪詞 is the one-*juǎn* Sìkù *cí* collection of Lǐ Zhī-yí [[李之儀]] (*fl.* 1085–*ca.* 1117; *zì* Duān-shū 端叔, *hào* Gū-xī jūshì 姑溪居士), a Sū Shì [[蘇軾]] follower best known in his lifetime for his *chǐ-dú* 尺牘 (letters). His *cí* — gathered by Máo Jìn [[毛晉]] into forty tune-titles and eighty-eight *cí* — are placed by the *Tíyào* "nearly equal to Qín Guān [[秦觀]]" in the *xiǎo-lìng* register, *qīng-wǎn qiào-qiàn* 清婉峭蒨 (clear-restrained, sharply-bright). The collection's unusual editorial trait is that Lǐ's matching-*cí* with Chén Guàn 陳瓘, Hè Zhù 賀鑄, and Huáng Tíng-jiān [[黃庭堅]] place the original *cí* of the dedicatee first and Lǐ's matching piece second — a practice borrowed (per the *Tíyào*) from the *Xiè Tiǎo jí*'s incorporation of Wáng Róng 王融's poems, and a useful resource for cross-collation.

### Tiyao

*Gū-xī cí*, one *juǎn*, by Lǐ Zhī-yí of the Sòng. Zhī-yí has the *Gū-xī jí* separately catalogued. The *Shūlù jiětí* records Zhī-yí's *Gū-xī cí* in one *juǎn*. The present text was cut by Máo Jìn: forty tune-titles, eighty-eight *cí* in total. Zhī-yí is renowned for his *chǐ-dú*, and his *cí* are also fine, especially his *xiǎo-lìng* — clear-restrained, sharp and bright, scarcely below Qín Guān. Jìn's colophon says: "Huáng Shēng [[黃昇]]'s *Huā-ān cí xuǎn* [[KR4j0066]] did not pick him up — there is the lament of the abandoned pearl." But Huáng's selections are all of men after the southern crossing — hence "Marvellous *cí* since the *Zhōng-xīng*" [[KR4j0067]]; Zhī-yí's period is earlier; Jìn did not check. As for the lines Jìn quotes — *yuān-qīn bàn yōng kōng-chuáng yuè* 鴛衾半擁空床月, *bù lǎn qià xún chuáng-wò* 步懶恰尋床卧, *kàn yóu-sī dào dì cháng* 看遊絲到地長, *shí-shí jìn shǒu xīn-tóu yùn, shòu jìn wú-rén zhī chù liáng* 時時浸手心頭熨，受盡無人知處凉 — these still do not exhaust his strengths. His matching-*cí* for Chén Guàn, Hè Zhù, Huáng Tíng-jiān: the original is given first, his own piece second; songs paired in mutual address — the example is from the *Xiè Tiǎo jí*'s appended Wáng Róng poems — letting the matching of feeling on both sides be seen; the *cí*-arrangement and intent thus becomes far better explained than in collections that omit it. The Huáng Tíng-jiān *Hǎo shì jìn* 好事近 here closes *fù shí-fēn jiāo-yè* 負十分蕉葉; today's *Shān-gǔ cí* [[KR4j0006]] mis-prints 蕉 as 金 — a cross-verification asset.

### Abstract

The transmitted *Gū-xī cí* descends through Máo Jìn [[毛晉]]'s *Jí-gǔ-gé* one-*juǎn* cutting, agreeing with Chén Zhèn-sūn [[陳振孫]]'s catalog form. Modern editions: the *Quán Sòng cí* of Táng Guī-zhāng [[唐圭璋]] gives a corpus of around 92 *cí*. The collection contains Lǐ's most famous piece, *Bǔ suàn zǐ · Wǒ zhù Cháng-jiāng tóu* 卜算子·我住長江頭 — *wǒ zhù Cháng-jiāng tóu, jūn zhù Cháng-jiāng wěi, rì-rì sī jūn bù jiàn jūn, gòng yǐn Cháng-jiāng shuǐ* 我住長江頭，君住長江尾，日日思君不見君，共飲長江水 ("I live at the head of the great river, you at its tail; day-on-day I long for you and do not see you, but we drink the same river-water") — composed for his second wife Yáng Shū-yǔ 楊姝, a singing-girl whom Lǐ married at Tài-píng-zhōu when banished there in the *Chóng-níng* period; this *cí*, with its echo of the *Yuè-fǔ* tradition, has remained the most-cited *xiǎo-lìng* of the early Sòng. Lǐ's *cí* divide neatly between the Biàn-jīng matching-*cí* with the *Sū mén* circle (the matching pieces to Chén Guàn, Hè Zhù, Huáng Tíng-jiān documented above) and the southern exile period at Tài-píng-zhōu.

## Other points of interest

The Sìkù compilers' note that Lǐ's matching-*cí* arrangement preserves the textual basis for cross-collating Huáng Tíng-jiān's *Hǎo shì jìn* — proving *jiāo* 蕉 against the corrupted *jīn* 金 — is a classic of the *cí*-philology that emerged in the late Qián-lóng *Sìkù* phase. The popular reception of *Bǔ suàn zǐ · Wǒ zhù Cháng-jiāng tóu* — set to song by countless modern composers — has made Lǐ the most universally-known of the secondary Northern-Sòng *cí*-writers.
