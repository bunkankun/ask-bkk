# Nánshān zǔshī lǐzàn wén 南山祖師禮讚文
*A Hymnody of Veneration for the Patriarch-Master of the Nánshān (Vinaya) School*
by [[智圓]] (Zhìyuán, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Northern-Sòng *lǐzàn wén* 禮讚文 (worship-and-eulogy liturgy) for the death-anniversary of **Nánshān Dàoxuān** 南山道宣 (596–667), the *Chéngzhào Dàshī* 澄照大師, founder of the Sìfēnlǜ Nánshān 四分律南山 (Four-Part Vinaya) school. Composed by **Gūshān Zhìyuán** 孤山智圓 ([[智圓]], 976–1022), the leader of the *Shānwài* 山外 wing of Sòng Tiāntái. The author signature reads *Gūshān shāmén shì Zhìyuán shù* 孤山沙門釋　智圓　述. The work opens the trio of Sòng *Nánshān zǔshī lǐzàn wén* preserved as X1504–1506 (= [[KR6k0207]], [[KR6k0208]], [[KR6k0209]]); these three were brought together as a unit and prefaced (元符三年 = 1100) by **Yúháng Yuánzhào** 餘杭元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116), the *Língzhī Dàzhì lǜshī* 靈芝大智律師.

### Prefaces

The 1100 *Jí Nánshān lǐzàn xù* 集南山禮讚序 by Yuánzhào (preserved at the head of the present X1504) reports the genesis: the *Gūshān fǎshī* (Zhìyuán) "first took up the brush, [his work] requested by *Lǜshī* of *Dōushuài Zéwú* 兜率擇梧" (Dōushuài Zéwú); next, the *Zháxī fǎshī* (i.e. *Jìngjué Rényuè* 淨覺仁岳) added a metrical version because Zhìyuán's lacked liturgical melody; and the *Tiāntái Zhēnwù lǜshī* (i.e. *Yǔnkǎn* 允堪) combined and reextended the two. Yuánzhào himself collated the three for liturgical use across the standard three-day *huìrì* 諱日 (death-anniversary) cycle.

### Abstract

Zhìyuán's *Nánshān zǔshī lǐzàn wén* is the **earliest Sòng *zànyí* for the founder of the Nánshān Vinaya school**, written between his entry into mature literary activity (ca. 1000) and his death in **1022**, in response to a request from a *lǜshī* at *Dōushuàiyuàn* in Mt Zéwú. The text follows the standard *zànyí* structure: an opening *yīxīn fèngqǐng* 一心奉請 (onemind invocation) of the master, prostrations, *zàncí* 讚詞 (eulogy stanzas) walking through the fourfold scheme of *Dàoxuān*'s biographical episodes (*Rìyán jīngshè* 日嚴精舍, transmission of the *vinaya*, the heavenly responses, the deathbed *fùfǎ* 付法), and closing offerings. The prose preface (by Zhìyuán himself) contains the formula *Nánshān jiàozhǔ Chéngzhào Dàshī lìshì lìyán dàchǎn píní zhī xué* 南山教主澄照大師立事立言大闡毗尼之學 — "the master of the Nánshān, the Great Master Chéngzhào, who *both established the deeds and established the words, greatly explicating the study of vinaya*." Composition is bracketed by Zhìyuán's mature career at *Gūshān* on West Lake; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are set 1000–1022. For the parallel later *zànyí* see [[KR6k0208]] (by Rényuè) and [[KR6k0209]] (by Yǔnkǎn).
