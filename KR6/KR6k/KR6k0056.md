# Fó shuō fànjiè zuìbào qīngzhòng jīng 佛說犯戒罪報輕重經 (variant)
*The Sūtra on the Light and Heavy Karmic Retributions for Violating the Precepts* (variant recension)
(translator unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle variant recension of [[KR6k0055]] (the *Fóshuō fànjiè zuìbào qīngzhòng jīng* attributed to An Shigao). The Taishō entry T1467 is divided into two sub-recensions (a and b); this is the variant transmission stream. Translator unknown.

### Prefaces

No separate translator-attribution survives.

### Abstract

The two-recension transmission of the *Fànjiè zuìbào* sūtra reflects the textual instability characteristic of short-form Vinaya-related *sūtras* in the early Chinese Buddhist tradition. The variant differs from [[KR6k0055]] in details of phrasing and in the order of the karmic consequences enumerated. Comparative philological work (Nattier 2008) treats both recensions as valuable witnesses to the earliest Chinese Vinaya-derived translations.
