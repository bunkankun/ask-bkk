# Púsàjièběn shū 菩薩戒本疏
*Commentary on the Bodhisattva Precept-Text*
by [[義寂]] (Ŭijŏk, 述)

## About the work

A two-fascicle commentary on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts by the Silla-dynasty Korean Buddhist scholar **Ŭijŏk** 義寂 ([[義寂]]). One of the **three principal Korean Silla commentaries** on the *Fànwǎng jīng*, alongside [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo's *Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn sījì* ([[KR6k0090]]) and [[太賢]] Tàixián's *Fànwǎngjīng gǔjì jì* ([[KR6k0083]]).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *新羅國海東沙門義寂述*.

### Abstract

Ŭijŏk's commentary represents the Silla-dynasty **Yogācāra-Cí'ēn 慈恩** reading of the bodhisattva-precepts. Working in the Tang Yogācāra tradition of [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng and [[窺基]] Kuījī, Ŭijŏk reads the *Fànwǎng* precepts within the Yogācāra framework of consciousness-only and the *bodhisattva-bhūmi* progression. The text is one of the principal documents of the Korean Buddhist exegetical tradition's deep engagement with Chinese Buddhist scholarship and is included in the **Taishō** canon as a representative of the East Asian Buddhist commentary tradition on the bodhisattva-precepts.
