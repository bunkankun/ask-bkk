# Chuánshòu sāntán hóngjiè fǎyí 傳授三壇弘戒法儀
*A Code for the Transmission of the Three-Tier Promotion of the Precepts*
by [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng, 撰集); [[超遠]] (Chāoyuǎn, 檢錄)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng *sāntán* 三壇 (three-tier) ordination manual, layered in two compositional stages: (i) the late-Míng original *zhuànjí* 撰集 by **Sānfēng Hànyuè Fǎzàng** 三峰漢月法藏 ([[法藏]], 1573–1635) at *Běichánsì* 北禪寺 in the *Tiānqǐ* 天啟 era (1621–1627); (ii) the early-Qīng *jiǎnlù* 檢錄 (review-and-record) by **Zǐsù Chāoyuǎn** 子肅超遠 ([[超遠]], 1631–1689) at *Pánlóng chánsì* 蟠龍禪寺 on Mt Zhōngnán (Shǎnxī). Author signature: *Míng Tiānqǐ Běichánsì hóngjiè shāmén Fǎzàng zhuànjí / Qīng Zhōngnánshān Pánlóng chánsì bǐqiū Chāoyuǎn jiǎnlù* 明天啟北禪寺弘戒沙門　法藏　撰集／清終南山蟠龍禪寺比丘　超遠　檢錄.

### Structural Division

**Upper section** — *Shòu shāmí shíjiè fǎ* 授沙彌十戒法 (the rite for conferring the ten *śrāmaṇera* precepts).

**Middle section** — *Shòu bǐqiū jùzú jiè fǎ* 授比丘具足戒法 (the rite for conferring the *bhikṣu upasampadā*, employing the *báisì jiémó* with the *sānshī qīzhèng* 三師七證 structure).

**Lower section** — *Shòu púsà jiè fǎ* 授菩薩戒法 (the rite for conferring the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva precepts).

Each section walks through the rite step by step with rubrics for the *kāidǎo* 開導 (instructor), the *zhíshì* 執事 (officials), the candidates, and the bell-and-drum signals.

### Abstract

The *Chuánshòu sāntán hóngjiè fǎyí* is the **principal Sānfēng-line *sāntán* ordination manual**, produced by Fǎzàng during his abbacy at *Běichánsì* in *Sūzhōu* (in the Tiānqǐ era of the late Míng) and subsequently *jiǎnlù*-ed by his lineage-descendant **Chāoyuǎn** at *Zhōngnánshān Pánlóngsì* in the early Qīng. **Chāoyuǎn** had himself studied Vinaya under *Jiànyuè Dútǐ* at Bǎohuáshān before turning to the Línjì lineage (cf. [[KR6k0226]]), so his check-redaction of Fǎzàng's manual in 1670s–1680s reflects his rare cross-lineage fluency in both the Bǎohuáshān and Sānfēng-line ordination traditions. Composition of the present *jiǎnlù*-ed recension is bracketed by Chāoyuǎn's accession to the *Pánlóngsì* abbacy in 1672 and his death in 1689; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1672–1689.

The work shares the proscribed status of [[KR6k0246]] *Hóngjiè fǎyí* — both were targeted by the *Yōngzhèng* emperor's 1733 anti-Sānfēng polemic.
