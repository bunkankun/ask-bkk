# Píní rìyòng qièyào xiāngrǔ jì 毗尼日用切要香乳記
*A Fragrant-Milk Annotated Record on the Crucial Essentials of Vinaya for Daily Use*
by [[書玉]] (Shūyù, 箋記)

## About the work

A two-fascicle late-Qīng running commentary on **Jiànyuè Dútǐ**'s *Píní rìyòng qièyào* ([[KR6k0224]]) by **Yíjié Shūyù** 宜潔書玉 ([[書玉]], 1645–1722) of *Zhāoqìngsì* 昭慶寺 in Hángzhōu — Dútǐ's principal personal-attendant disciple and the principal late-Qīng inheritor of the Bǎohuáshān Vinaya line. The title's *xiāngrǔ* 香乳 ("fragrant milk") metaphor designates the commentary's task: just as fragrant milk nourishes the *vinaya*-novice, the *jì* explicates the *gāthās* and *dhāraṇīs* of the parent text and supplies their full canonical context.

### Prefaces

**(A)** *Xù* 序, signed *Kāngxī wùyín jiǔyuè Hǎiníng Yáng Yōngjiàn Yǐzāi tí yú Fúguòxuān* 康熈戊寅九月海寧楊雍建以哉題於弗過軒 — i.e. **1698** (Kāngxī 37), 9th lunar month, by Yáng Yōngjiàn (1620?–1704), a Hǎiníng official-scholar. Yáng describes how *Zhāoqìngsì*'s **Yíjié lǜshī** (Shūyù) presented him a manuscript copy of the *Xiāngrǔ jì* during the *Tóngyīn dīlù, guìshú piāoxiāng* season (autumn). Yáng's reading-praise: *"Reading it through, I knew that he is profoundly versed in the *Brahmāpitaka* (i.e. Vinaya), specialised in the five *piān* and three *jù* (the *prātimokṣa* categories), enabling those of black robe and white to grasp this text — sustaining half a verse in each of the four postures, walking, sitting, lying, day after day without a break — and one shall see *maṇi*-jewel and *Brahmānet* radiance interreflecting; he truly does not betray the orthodox transmission of *Bǎohuáshān*."*

**(B)** *Xù* 序, a second longer doctrinal preface treating the universal scheme of *jīnglǜlùn* 經律論 (sūtra-vinaya-śāstra), the *zēngshàng* 增上 doctrines of precept-meditation-wisdom, and the standard *èrshèng sānzàng* 二乘三藏 framework.

### Abstract

The *Xiāngrǔ jì* is **the standard companion commentary** for *Píní rìyòng qièyào* and the principal pedagogical reading-aid for the Bǎohuá / Zhāoqìng Vinaya tradition. Where Dútǐ's parent text supplies the *gāthā* and *dhāraṇī* in skeletal form keyed to each daily activity, Shūyù's *jì* unpacks (i) the canonical source of each verse (most often the *Jìngxíng pǐn* of *Huáyán*); (ii) the meaning of each *dhāraṇī* in transliterated and transposed form; (iii) the doctrinal point of the *gāthā*'s phrasing; and (iv) attendant ritual rubrics. The work transformed the daily *gāthā* recitation from a memorised set of formulae into a programme of doctrinal-meditative formation. Composition is bracketed by Shūyù's tenure at *Zhāoqìngsì* (he was installed there shortly after Dútǐ's death in 1679 and remained there until his death in 1722) and the date of the 1698 Yáng preface; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1690–1698. The work is the principal late-Qīng witness to the *Píní rìyòng* tradition.

## Translations and research

*No substantial Western-language secondary literature located.* Shūyù's life and his Zhāoqìng abbacy are covered in the *Wǔlín Dàzhāoqìng lǜsì zhì* 武林大昭慶律寺志 *juan* 9, and discussed by Holmes Welch (*Practice of Chinese Buddhism*) in connection with the modern Chinese ordination tradition.

## Other points of interest

- The 1698 preface by **Yáng Yōngjiàn** 楊雍建 — a Hǎiníng *jìnshì* (1655) and high-ranking Qīng official — preserves a noted lay-Buddhist endorsement of the Bǎohuáshān Vinaya line.
