# Sìfēn jièběn rúshì 四分戒本如釋
*The Sìfēn Prātimokṣa, Thus Explained*
by [[弘贊]] (Hóngzàn, 繹)

## About the work

A twelve-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng commentary on the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa* by **Hóngzàn** 弘贊 ([[弘贊]], 1611–1685). 繹 *yì* "drawn out / unraveled" indicates the methodical-elucidatory commentary genre.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明弘贊繹*.

### Abstract

Hóngzàn's *Rúshì* is one of the most extensive late-Míng / early-Qīng commentaries on the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa*, providing systematic rule-by-rule exposition for monastic instruction. As a Vinaya specialist, Hóngzàn approached the *prātimokṣa* with particular attention to procedural-practical detail. Together with his *Fànwǎngjīng púsàjiè lüèshū* ([[KR6k0098]]), this work represents Hóngzàn's complete dual-Vinaya commentary apparatus (Sìfēn + Fànwǎng).
