# Púsà wǔfǎ chànhuǐ wén 菩薩五法懺悔文
*The Bodhisattva's Five-Fold Confession*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Mahāyāna confessional liturgy organised under five categories (五法 *wǔfǎ*). Translator unknown.

### Prefaces

No translator-attribution survives.

### Abstract

The text is a brief Mahāyāna confessional liturgy used in bodhisattva-precept observance. The "five categories" structure of confession became a standard schema in East Asian Mahāyāna repentance literature, paralleling the *prātimokṣa* recitation in the Vinaya tradition. Such confessional texts were used in *uposatha* gatherings and as preparation for major ordination ceremonies.
