# Chóngzhì píní shìyì jíyào 重治毗尼事義集要
*Re-edited Compendium of Vinaya Affairs and Meanings*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 彙釋)

## About the work

A seventeen-fascicle revised digest of *Sìfēnlǜ* 四分律 vinaya material compiled by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 ([[智旭]], 1599–1655), the late-Míng integrative Buddhist master who is one of the *Wǎn-Míng sì dà gāosēng* 晚明四大高僧 ("Four Great Masters of Late Míng Buddhism"). The work is a *chóngzhì* 重治 ("re-edited") version of an earlier compilation by Zhìxù; the post-colophon note in the source records that the original ran to eighteen *juan*, with the eighteenth (*Jièdù lüèshì* 犍度略釋) circulating separately and edited in this Manji recension as a stand-alone work ([[KR6k0174]]).

### Prefaces

The 卍 Xuzangjing edition opens with two prefaces: a *Chóngzhì píní shìyì jíyào xù* 重治毗尼事義集要敘 by the Vinaya monk **Zhìtái** 智[台/十] of Jìnzhōu 晉州 (the source preserves the unusual character form 台/十 in the name), recording that the editio princeps was prepared at the urging of **Jiǔshèng** 九聖, **Huìshí** 慧石, **Fóbì** 佛璧, and **Qiānshòu** 千壽 with the unblemished Vinaya master **Wúxiá** 無瑕 of Jìn-Liáo as the principal collator; and Zhìxù's autobiographical *xù* opening with his birth on the third day of the fifth month of Wànlì 27 = 1599. The author's terminal *bá* is dated *gēngyín* 庚寅 8th month 8th day = **1650** (Shùnzhì 7), which therefore fixes the *terminus ad quem* of the present recension; the underlying first compilation was prepared in the late-Chóngzhēn (after 1632) when Zhìxù's Vinaya career was already mature.

### Abstract

The *Jíyào* digests, classifies, and re-arranges the standard *Sìfēn* vinaya material — pātimokkha, *jiémó* 羯磨 (karmavācanā), *jiēdù* 犍度 (skandhaka) — in a form designed for late-Míng monastic use. Zhìxù was active in Vinaya scholarship throughout his career: the work belongs together with his *Píní shìyì yàolüè* 毗尼事義要略 and *Sìfēnlǜzàng dàxiǎo chíjiè jiēdù lüèshì* ([[KR6k0174]]) as a coherent late-Míng programme of Vinaya synthesis. As a Tiāntái-Chán-Pure Land synthesist, Zhìxù was atypical among Vinaya commentators of his generation: his interest in the Vinaya was framed by the late-Míng concern (shared with Zhūhóng, Hānshān Déqīng, and the Bǎohuáshān revivalists) for restoring observant monastic discipline as the precondition for meditative and devotional practice. The text was eventually integrated into the *Bǎohuáshān* Vinaya curriculum and supplied with a follow-up gloss, *Píní guānyào* 毗尼關要 ([[KR6k0152]]), by **Dìngān Déjī** 定菴德基 ([[德基]], 1634–1700/01).
