# Lǜyào hòují 律要後集
*A Sequel-Anthology of Vinaya Essentials*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 集)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng *vinaya* anthology by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** ([[智旭]], 1599–1655), conceived as a supplement (*hòují* 後集 "later collection") to his earlier *Zàijiā lǜyào* (now preserved in the expanded form [[KR6k0243]] *Zàijiā lǜyào guǎngjí*) and his *Píní hòují wènbiàn* 毗尼後集問辯.

### Structural Division

The internal *mùcì* gives the work's contents:

1. *Sòng huò běn* 誦或本 — note: *"taken from the *Dìchí jièpǐn* in the *Púsàjiè běnjīng*; therefore here omitted"* (i.e. the user is referred to the canonical source).
2. ***Púsàjiè jiémó wén shì* 菩薩戒羯磨文釋** — a doctrinal-procedural commentary on the *karmavācanā* of the bodhisattva-precept conferral (preserved in fascicle 61 of the Xùzàngjīng).
3. ***Chóngdìng shòu púsàjiè fǎ* 重定授菩薩戒法** — Zhìxù's revised procedure for the conferral of the bodhisattva precepts.
4. ***Xué púsàjiè fǎ* 學菩薩戒法** — the practitioner's protocol for studying the bodhisattva precepts.
5. *Fànwǎng jīng chànhuǐ xíngfǎ* 梵網經懺悔行法 — note: "found in the *Zàijiā lǜyào guǎngjí*; therefore here omitted." (Cf. [[KR6k0245]], where this is also separately preserved.)
6. ***Píní hòují wènbiàn* 毗尼後集問辯** — Zhìxù's *vinaya* question-and-disputation collection.

### Abstract

The *Lǜyào hòují* gathers Zhìxù's late-stage ritual-and-question productions in *Vinaya*. The core item is *Chóngdìng shòu púsàjiè fǎ* — Zhìxù's revised procedure for bodhisattva-precept conferral, in which the candidate first formally **invokes the bodhisattva-master**, prostrates to the past-and-future Buddhas of the four directions and the Three Jewels, recites the *guǎngyuàn* 廣願 (great vows), and finally receives the *sānjù jìngjiè* 三聚淨戒 (three pure precepts: *zhēngfó* 證佛, *shànfǎ* 善法, *shèzhòngshēng* 攝眾生). The procedure is independently designed (it does not follow Zhūhóng's *Púsàjièyì shū fāyǐn* 菩薩戒義疏發隱) and supplied a benchmark for subsequent late-Míng / Qīng bodhisattva-precept conferrals. Composition is bracketed by Zhìxù's mature commentarial period and his death in 1655; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are 1620–1655.
