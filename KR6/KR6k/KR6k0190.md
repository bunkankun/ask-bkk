# Shuōzuì yàoxíng fǎ 說罪要行法
*The Essential Method of Confessing Offences*
by [[義淨]] (Yìjìng, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle Vinaya manual on the canonical practice of *shuōzuì* 說罪 (confession of offences) at the monthly *poṣadha* by **Yìjìng** 義淨 ([[義淨]], 635–713). Author signature: *唐三藏法師義淨撰*.

### Prefaces

The work opens directly: *每於半月月盡憶所犯罪。準法而說* ("every fortnight at the end of the month, recalling the offences committed, declare them according to the rule"). The opening enumeration lists the typical offences: *gùwàngyǔ* 故妄語 (intentional false speech), *yǐnjiǔ* 飲酒 (consuming alcohol), *fēishí shí* 非時食 (eating outside permitted times), and others.

### Abstract

The *Shuōzuì yàoxíng fǎ* completes the trio of Yìjìng's short Vinaya manuals ([[KR6k0188]], [[KR6k0189]], [[KR6k0190]]) and is the most operational of the three: it presents the actual ritual formulary for the *poṣadha* confession. The CANWWW alternative-title *Bié shuōzuì yàoxíng fǎ* 別說罪要行法 ("the *Distinct* Essential Method of Confessing Offences") is preserved in the canonical record (T45N1903 *alt* title) and indicates the manual's distinct status as a complement to the standard *poṣadha* karman recitation. Composition belongs to Yìjìng's post-695 Luòyáng / Chángān decades.
