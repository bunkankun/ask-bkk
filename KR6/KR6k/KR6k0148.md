# Sìfēn jièběn yuánqǐ shìyì 四分戒本緣起事義
*Origin-Narratives and Explanatory Notes for the Sìfēn Prātimokṣa*
by [[廣莫]] (Guǎng Mò, 輯錄)

## About the work

A one-fascicle late-Míng compilation by **Guǎng Mò** 廣莫 ([[廣莫]]) of the *yuánqǐ* (origin-narratives) and *shìyì* (factual explanations) for the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa* rules — that is, the *vibhaṅga*-derived case-narratives explaining the institution of each rule, together with explanatory notes.

### Prefaces

Compiler's colophon: *明廣莫輯錄*.

### Abstract

The text assembles practical-pedagogical material on the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa* rules — origin-narratives + explanatory notes — for instructional use. The genre was popular in late-Míng monastic education as it provided the historical-narrative context that mere rule-recitation lacked.
