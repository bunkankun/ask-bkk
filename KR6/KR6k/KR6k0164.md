# Sìfēnlǜ shū 四分律疏
*Commentary on the Sìfēnlǜ*
by [[智首]] (Zhìshǒu, 撰)

## About the work

A surviving single fragment (originally many *juan*; only **juan 9** is preserved in the Manji canon) of the **Sìfēnlǜ shū** by **Zhìshǒu** 智首 ([[智首]], 567–635), the leading Sui–early-Táng Vinaya master and teacher of **Dàoxuān** 道宣 ([[道宣]], 596–667). The Manji edition prints the surviving fragment with the signature *沙門釋　智者　撰* (*Composed by the monk Zhìzhě*) — a graphic variant for *Zhìshǒu* common in some Manji witnesses (cf. DILA A001269); the catalog and the standard tradition correctly identify the author as Zhìshǒu.

### Prefaces

No preface in the surviving fragment.

### Abstract

Zhìshǒu is conventionally regarded as the eighth patriarch of the Chinese *Sìfēnlǜ* tradition; his lost commentary was the principal source from which Dàoxuān, his disciple, drew the foundational *Nánshānlǜ* programme. The surviving fragment treats the *yǔyùyī jiè* 雨浴衣戒 ("rain-bathing garment precepts" — the rules in *prātimokṣa* nos. 27–28 governing acquisition and use of rain-cloth) and the *zhéjiàoní jiè* 輙教尼戒 ("rules on instructing nuns without authorization"). It analyses each rule under the formal scheme of *yuánqǐ* 緣起 (occasion of institution), *fànxiāng* 犯相 (violation profile, given here as *wǔyuán* / *sìyuán* 五緣/四緣 — the five or four conditions for full violation), *kāiyuán* 開緣 (exemption clauses), and parallel-text comparison (with the *Sēngqí lǜ* 僧祇律 and other recensions). The fragment is documentarily valuable as the only direct surviving witness to the early-Tang pre-Dàoxuān *Sìfēnlǜ* exegesis from which the *Nánshān* tradition emerged. The dating bracket is Zhìshǒu's mature scholarly career.
