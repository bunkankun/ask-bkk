# Lǜ jièběn shū 律戒本疏 (variant)
*Commentary on the Vinaya Prātimokṣa* (Dunhuang fragment, variant recension)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous variant *prātimokṣa* commentary fragment, preserved in Dunhuang. The variant relationship with [[KR6k0134]] reflects the multiplicity of Vinaya commentary streams in Tang-period Dunhuang.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

Companion to [[KR6k0134]]; both are Dunhuang fragments of *Sìfēn prātimokṣa* commentaries in slightly variant manuscript transmissions. Together they testify to the diversity of Vinaya scholarship in northwestern Tang Buddhist communities.
