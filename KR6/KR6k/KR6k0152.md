# Píní guānyào 毗尼關要
*The Essentials of Vinaya*
by [[德基]] (Déjī, 輯)

## About the work

A sixteen-fascicle early-Qīng commentary on the *Sìfēn bǐqiū jièběn* 四分比丘戒本 ([[KR6k0010]]) compiled by **Dìngān Déjī** 定菴德基 ([[德基]], 1634–1700/01), third-generation patriarch of **Bǎohuáshān Lǜzōng** 寶華山律宗 and dharma-heir of [[讀體]] Jiànyuè Dútǐ. *Guānyào* 關要 ("the essential pivot") indicates a comprehensive practical study-manual rather than a strict textual commentary. The work supplies a thorough phrase-by-phrase exegesis of the *prātimokṣa* in the early-Qīng Bǎohuáshān manner and is paired with the companion glossary *Píní guānyào shìyì* 毗尼關要事義 ([[KR6k0153]]).

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a *xù* by an unnamed admirer (the source preserves the prefatorial signature only as the contributor's *zìshù*) recording that the work was completed after Déjī's "more than twenty summers" of Vinaya study, and naming the southern transmission **Sānmèi → Jiànyuè → Dìngān** as the institutional chain at Bǎohuáshān. The *Tiānhuá lǎorén* 千華老人 (= Sānmèi Jìguāng) and *Nánlái Jiànzhě héshàng* 南來見者和尚 (= Jiànyuè Dútǐ) are named as Déjī's lineage predecessors.

### Abstract

The *Píní guānyào* is the principal **early-Qīng Bǎohuáshān** *Sìfēnlǜ* study manual, occupying for the early-Qīng monastic curriculum the position that Dàoxuān's *Sìfēnlǜ shānfán bǔquē xíngshì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 had occupied for Tang and Sòng *Nánshān* monks. It systematises the practical implementation of the *prātimokṣa* under the institutional conditions of the Bǎohuáshān ordination platform — the dominant Vinaya-school institution of early-Qīng China — and absorbs the late-Míng Vinaya scholarship of [[智旭]] (the [[KR6k0151]] *Chóngzhì píní shìyì jíyào*) into a Bǎohuáshān framework. The companion *Shìyì* ([[KR6k0153]]) supplies the lexical and historical glosses for the names, places, and technical terms cited in the *Guānyào*. Composition cannot be precisely dated; it must postdate Déjī's succession to the abbacy after 1679 (Dútǐ's death) but precede his own death in 1700/01.
