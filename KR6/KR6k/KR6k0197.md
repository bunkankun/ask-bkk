# Dàshèng liùqíng chànhuǐ 大乘六情懺悔
*Mahāyāna Repentance for the Six Sense-faculties*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 撰)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Silla *chànhuǐ* 懺悔 (repentance) liturgy by **Wǒnhyo** 元曉 ([[元曉]], 617–686) for the **six sense-faculties** (*liùqíng* 六情 = *liùgēn* 六根: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind). Author signature: *釋元曉撰*.

### Prefaces

The work opens directly: *若依法界始遊行者。於四威儀無一唐遊。念諸佛不思議德。常思實相杇銷業障* ("Whoever begins to roam in accordance with the *dharma-dhātu*: in the four deportments not a single sojourn is in vain. He recalls the inconceivable virtues of all the Buddhas, constantly contemplates the *bhūtatathatā*, and dissolves karmic obstructions"). The opening passage establishes the work's framing as a continuous-practice repentance integrated with daily-life meditation.

### Abstract

The *Liùqíng chànhuǐ* is one of Wǒnhyo's most distinctive ritual-liturgical works and reflects his programmatic integration of *Tathāgatagarbha* doctrine, *Yogācāra* analysis, and *chànhuǐ* practice. The repentance is structured around the six sense-faculties as the locus of *karman*-production; the practitioner takes refuge in the ten-direction Buddhas, contemplates the *bhūtatathatā* of all phenomena, and uses meditative absorption to dissolve karmic accumulations. Together with [[KR6k0194]] *Púsà jièběn chífàn yàojì*, it represents Wǒnhyo's contribution to the East Asian Buddhist *practical-ethics* literature; the work is studied as the principal extant Silla *chànhuǐ* manual. Composition belongs to Wǒnhyo's mature productive years.
