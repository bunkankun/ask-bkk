# Shísòng jiémó bǐqiū yàoyòng 十誦羯磨比丘要用
*Essential Karmavācanā for Bhikṣus from the Ten-Recitation Vinaya*
by [[僧璩]] (Sēng Qú, 撰出)

## About the work

A one-fascicle compendium of essential *karmavācanā* formulae for daily monastic use, extracted (撰出) from the *Shísòng lǜ* ([[KR6k0016]]) by **Sēng Qú** 僧璩 ([[僧璩]]) of the Liú-Sòng dynasty. Sēng Qú was a second-generation disciple in the Sēngyè 僧業 lineage of *Shísòng* specialists at Jiànkāng.

### Prefaces

Compiler's colophon: *宋僧璩撰出*. The text is documented in the *Chū sānzàng jìjí* (T55n2145) j. 12.

### Abstract

The *Shísòng jiémó bǐqiū yàoyòng* is the **only Sarvāstivāda *karmavācanā* compendium** in the Chinese canon (parallel to the Dharmaguptaka *jiémó* compilations of Kāng Sēngkǎi [[KR6k0013]] and Tán Dì [[KR6k0014]]). Together with the parent *Shísòng lǜ*, it constituted the practical-procedural base of the *Shísòng* Vinaya school in southern China during the 5th–7th centuries. The text condenses the long *karmavācanā* sections of the parent Vinaya into a portable handbook for daily use, with the standard *uposatha*, *pravāraṇā*, robe-distribution, and *upasaṃpadā* formulae.
