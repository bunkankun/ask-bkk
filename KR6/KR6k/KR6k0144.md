# Sìfēn lǜ hánzhù jièběn shū fāhuī jì 四分律含注戒本疏發揮記
*Records Drawing Out the Meaning of the Commentary on the Embedded-Annotation Prātimokṣa*
by [[允堪]] (Yǔn Kān, 述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-dynasty sub-commentary by **Yǔn Kān** 允堪 ([[允堪]]) on the Tang-period commentary tradition for Dàoxuān's *Hánzhù jièběn* ([[KR6k0131]]).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *宋餘杭沙門允堪述*.

### Abstract

Yǔn Kān's *Fāhuī jì* "records drawing out the meaning" is a Sòng-period sub-commentary on the existing Tang commentary tradition, aimed at clarifying obscure points in the original Dàoxuān exposition. As a major Sòng Vinaya scholar, Yǔn Kān is responsible for transmitting the Tang Nánshān tradition to the later Sòng and post-Sòng *saṅgha*.
