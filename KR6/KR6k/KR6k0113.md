# Púsàzàng jīng 菩薩藏經
*The Bodhisattva-Piṭaka Sūtra*
by [[僧伽婆羅]] (Saṃghapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Mahāyāna *sūtra* on the foundational doctrines of the **Bodhisattva-piṭaka** (the Mahāyāna canonical-doctrinal scope), translated by **Saṃghapāla** 僧伽婆羅 ([[僧伽婆羅]]) at the Liáng court 506–520 CE. Not to be confused with the much larger *Da Bodhisattva-piṭaka Sūtra* translated by Xuánzàng (T11n0310 j. 35–54).

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *梁扶南三藏僧伽婆羅譯*.

### Abstract

The text is one of the early Mahāyāna scriptures on the doctrinal scope of the bodhisattva-path. Its inclusion in the Vinaya division is somewhat anomalous — the text is thematically more a doctrinal *sūtra* than a Vinaya — but reflects the Chinese canonical practice of grouping bodhisattva-precept materials with related doctrinal *sūtras* of the Mahāyāna ethical tradition.
