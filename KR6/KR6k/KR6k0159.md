# Jiémó jīng xùjiě 羯磨經序解
*Glosses on the Preface of the Karman-text*
by [[則安]] (Zéān, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Northern Sòng pedagogical commentary on the preface (*xù*) and incipit of **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) karman-text manual *Sìfēnlǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó* 四分律刪補隨機羯磨 ([[KR6k0019]]) by **Zéān** 則安 ([[則安]]), senior disciple of **Yuánzhào** 元照 ([[元照]], 1048–1116). Author signature: *門人　則安　述* ("composed by the disciple Zéān"), identifying him as Yuánzhào's *ménrén*.

### Prefaces

The work itself is the gloss on the preface; no separate front-matter.

### Abstract

The *Xùjiě* takes Dàoxuān's prefatorial twenty-five characters and the *zhuànzhùrénhào* 撰注人號 (the line identifying the author and his location) and supplies short philological glosses on each phrase: explanations of *Jīngzhào* 京兆 (the Tang metropolitan administrative unit, from *jīng* "great" and *zhào* "multitude"), *Chóngyìsì* 崇義寺 (the temple's name as derived from a marriage-bequest transformation), *shāmén* 沙門 (Skt. *śramaṇa*, "the diligent stiller of the passions"), *shì* 釋 (Skt. *Śākya*, the surname adopted by all ordained monks), and *Dàoxuān* 道宣 with his *fǎbiàn* 法徧. The work is short but documentarily valuable: it preserves the immediate-post-Yuánzhào pedagogical tradition of the *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 *Nánshānlǜ* school. It must postdate Yuánzhào's death (1116) — Zéān refers to Yuánzhào with the posthumous title *Dàzhì lǜshī* 大智律師 in his other works — but precise dating within the early twelfth century is not possible; the bracket here gives Zéān's likely active period.
