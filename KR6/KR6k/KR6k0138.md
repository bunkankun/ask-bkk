# Pínì xīn 毘尼心
*The Heart of the Vinaya* (Dunhuang)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Dunhuang fragment titled *Pínì xīn* "Heart of the Vinaya" — a synoptic-essential exposition of Vinaya doctrine.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The "heart" (心 *xīn*) genre — distillations of the essential meaning of a doctrinal corpus — was popular in late-Tang Chinese Buddhism. This Vinaya *xīn* presents the essential content of monastic discipline in compressed form for instructional use. As a Dunhuang manuscript, it preserves a working text from the late-Tang Dunhuang Buddhist communities.
