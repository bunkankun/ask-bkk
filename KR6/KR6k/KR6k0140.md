# Lǜchāo 律抄
*Vinaya Excerpts* (Dunhuang)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Dunhuang Vinaya excerpts-collection. Generic title (literally "Vinaya extracts") indicates an unattributed working compilation of Vinaya material for monastic use.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

One of the Dunhuang Vinaya manuscript fragments in Taishō volume 85, preserving a working monastic Vinaya selection from the late-Tang Dunhuang library.
