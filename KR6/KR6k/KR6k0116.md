# Fó shuō jìng yèzhàng jīng 佛說淨業障經
*The Sūtra on Purifying Karmic Obstructions*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Mahāyāna *sūtra* on the **purification of karmic obstructions** (淨業障 *jìng yèzhàng*) through Vinaya practice and confession.

### Prefaces

No translator-attribution survives.

### Abstract

The text presents the Mahāyāna doctrine that bodhisattva-precept observance, combined with proper confession (*deśanā-pāpa*), purifies the practitioner's karmic obstructions to bodhi. The doctrine is foundational for the East Asian Mahāyāna confessional tradition and shaped the practical liturgies of repentance (懺法 *chànfǎ*) developed in Tiāntái and Pure Land contexts.
