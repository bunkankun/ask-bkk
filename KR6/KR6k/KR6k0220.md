# Lǜzōng huìyuán 律宗會元
*Convergence at the Source of the Vinaya School*
by [[守一]] (Shǒuyī, 集)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Southern-Sòng anthology by **Tiěwēng Shǒuyī** ([[守一]]) at *Tāizhōu Rìshān*, organising the *Nánshānlǜ* tradition under ten doctrinal gates (*shímén* 十門) with subsection gloss. Companion to [[KR6k0219]] *Zhōngnán jiāyè*. The full title given in the source-text is *Lǜzōng huìyuán (bìng shímén zǒngmù suímén lüèshì) juǎn dìyī (zhǐ Yuánjiào zhōng)* 律宗會元(并十門總目隨門略釋)卷第一(止原教終). Author signature: *Tāizhōu Rìshān sì zǔ bǐqiū Shǒuyī jǐn jí* 台州日山嗣祖比丘　守一　謹集.

### Structural Division

The ten gates are signalled in the *shímén zǒngmù* embedded in the work:

1. *Yuánjiào mén* 原教門 (origin of the [vinaya] teaching);
2. *Guānfǎ mén* 觀法門 (contemplation-method gate);
3. (continuing) *Shòujiè mén* 受戒門, *Chífàn mén* 持犯門, *Yīshí mén* 衣食門, *Sēngjiā mén* 僧伽門, *Yúfǎ mén* 餘法門, *Zhèngyán mén* 證驗門, *Yǐnshēng mén* 引申門 — and a closing *miscellany* gate. (Each gate carries Shǒuyī's *suímén lüèshì* 隨門略釋, "sectionbysection brief gloss".)

### Abstract

The *Lǜzōng huìyuán* organises the textual heritage of the Nánshān Vinaya school as a topical anthology, drawing canonical and subcanonical citations into a *huìyuán* 會元 ("gathering-at-the-source") framework. The opening *Yuánjiào mén* makes the work's principal doctrinal claim: while the *vinayapiṭaka* nominally belongs to the *Hīnayāna* division of the *tripiṭaka*, *"the *Lotus Sūtra* opens-and-converges (*kāi huì* 開會) and the *Nièpán Sūtra* sustains-and-discusses (*fútán* 扶談), so that the partial is identical to the round (*zhǐ piān jí yuán* 指偏即圓)" — i.e. the Nánshān reading places Vinaya within the Mahāyāna *onevehicle* (*yī Fó chéng* 一佛乘) doctrinally as well as ritually. This is the standard *Língzhī* + Tiāntái fusion. The second gate *Guānfǎ mén* makes the corresponding methodological claim: Vinaya *requires* contemplation (*xí lǜ zhī xū guān* 習律之須觀), since the Buddha himself in the *vinayavibhaṅga* enjoins the *bhikṣu* to "uphold dignity in postures, in robes, medicines and food, in word and gesture, in rest and movement, ever with one mind." Composition is bracketed by Shǒuyī's residence at Rìshān (from 1239); `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1239–1260.

## Other points of interest

- The *huìyuán* (gathering-at-the-source) format is a Southern-Sòng pedagogical genre, distinct from the *xíngshìchāo* 行事鈔 / *zīchí jì* 資持記 commentary tradition; this work is an important example.
