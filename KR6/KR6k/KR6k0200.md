# Wúzhōng shífó xiànghǎo chànyí 吳中石佛相好懺儀
*A Repentance Liturgy for the Marks-and-Auspicious-Signs of the Stone Buddha in Wú*
by [[傳燈]] (Chuándēng, 集)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle late-Míng *chànyí* 懺儀 (repentance liturgy) compiled by **Yōuxī Chuándēng** 幽溪傳燈 ([[傳燈]], 1554–1628), the major late-Míng Tiāntái 天台宗 master at Gāomíngsì 高明寺 on Mount Tiāntái, for use at the **Wúzhōng** 吳中 (Sūzhōu region) **stone-Buddha** site — likely the famous **Liángcháo** colossal stone Buddha at *Shífósì* 石佛寺 in Sūzhōu region. The full title in some witnesses is *Lǐ Wúzhōng shífó qǐzhǐ yíshì* 禮吳中石佛起止儀式 (*Procedural Manual for Reverencing the Stone Buddha at Wúzhōng, from Beginning to End*) — the form preserved in the source-text head.

### Prefaces

The work opens directly with the liturgical opening formulas: *一切恭敬* ("complete respect"), *一心頂禮十方常住三寶* ("with one mind I prostrate to the abiding Three Jewels of the ten directions"), followed by the *yībài* 一拜 (one bow) and *hùguì shāoxiāng sànhuā* 互跪燒香散華 ("kneeling alternately, burning incense, scattering flowers"). The opening *xiānghuā gòngyǎng* 香華供養 verse: *願此香華雲徧滿十方界* ("may this cloud of fragrance and flowers pervade the ten directions") establishes the standard *Tiāntái chànyí* opening.

### Abstract

The *Shífó xiànghǎo chànyí* is a specialised repentance liturgy designed for performance at the *Wúzhōng* stone-Buddha site, focusing devotionally on the *xiànghǎo* 相好 (the *lakṣaṇa* and *anuvyañjana*, "the marks and the auspicious signs" of a Buddha-body). The liturgy belongs to Chuándēng's broader *chànyí* corpus — produced for specific local devotional sites in his Tiāntái-mountain orbit — and reflects the integrated Tiāntái-Pure-Land devotional programme he developed at Gāomíngsì. Author signature: *天台山幽溪沙門　傳燈　集* (*Compiled by Chuándēng, monk of Yōuxī on Mount Tiāntái*). Composition is bracketed by Chuándēng's residence at Gāomíngsì from 1587 and his death in 1628.
