# Sìfēn bǐqiū jièběn shū 四分比丘戒本疏
*Commentary on the Sìfēn Bhikṣu-Prātimokṣa* (Xiāngbù school)
by [[定賓]] (Dìng Bīn, 撰)

## About the work

A two-fascicle commentary on the *Sìfēn bhikṣu-prātimokṣa* by **Dìng Bīn** 定賓 ([[定賓]]) of the **Xiāngbù** 相部 school of *Sìfēn* Vinaya — one of the three competing Tang *Sìfēn* exegetical traditions, founded by **Fǎlì** 法礪 ([[法礪]], 569–635) at the Xiāngbù region.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐西明寺沙門定賓撰*.

### Abstract

The Xiāngbù school's interpretation of the *Sìfēn lǜ* differed in important details from Dàoxuān's Nánshān and Huáisù's Dōngtǎ readings, particularly on questions of the relation between the *Sìfēn* and the *Yogācārabhūmi* bodhisattva-precept tradition, the requirements for valid *upasaṃpadā*, and the doctrinal classification of certain *naiḥsargika* offences. Dìng Bīn's commentary is the principal extant Xiāngbù treatment of the *prātimokṣa*. After the eclipse of the Xiāngbù school in the late Tang, the work was preserved primarily as a doctrinal-historical reference.
