# Fó shuō qīngjìng pínàiyē zuìshàng dàshèng jīng 佛說清淨毘奈耶最上大乘經
*The Mahāyāna Sūtra of the Supreme Pure Vinaya*
by [[智吉祥]] (Zhì Jíxiáng, 等譯)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Sòng-period Mahāyāna *vinaya* sūtra, translated by **Zhì Jíxiáng** 智吉祥 ([[智吉祥]]). Preserved in the **Jīn Edition** 金藏 (the Sòng-period Jīn-dynasty 金 stone-canon), one of the alternative Sòng/Jīn-period Buddhist canons.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋智吉祥等譯*.

### Abstract

The text is a Mahāyāna sūtra on the bodhisattva-vinaya in its "supreme" Mahāyāna form, translated in the late Northern Sòng. Its preservation in the Jīn Edition rather than in the Taishō reflects the alternate manuscript-stream traditions that supplemented the dominant transmission. The Jīn 金 (Jurchen) canon, engraved 1148–1173 at Xièzhōu 解州 in present-day Shanxi, preserves a number of texts not in the standard Buddhist canon.
