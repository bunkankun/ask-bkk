# Fó shuō Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn jīng lüèjiě 佛說目連五百問經略解
*Concise Commentary on the Maudgalyāyana Five-Hundred Questions Sūtra*
by [[性祇]] (Xìng Qí, 述)

## About the work

A two-fascicle late-Míng abridged commentary on the *Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn jīng* ([[KR6k0073]]) by **Xìng Qí** 性祇 ([[性祇]]). The commentary glosses the casuistic Vinaya questions of the parent sūtra, explaining the Buddha's adjudications and relating them to standard Vinaya doctrine.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明性祇述*.

### Abstract

Xìng Qí's commentary is one of two late-Míng treatments of the *Mùlián wǔbǎiwèn* casuistic literature (the other being [[永海]] Yǒng Hǎi's [[KR6k0075]]). The two commentaries reflect the late-Míng Vinaya revival under figures like [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng 雲棲袾宏 and [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭 — a movement to recover practical Vinaya scholarship after the long Míng decline of monastic discipline.
