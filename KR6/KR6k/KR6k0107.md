# Yīngluò běnyèjīng shū 瓔珞本業經疏
*Commentary on the Yīngluò Běn-yè Sūtra*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 撰)

## About the work

A one-fascicle commentary on the *Púsà yīngluò běnyè jīng* ([[KR6k0105]]) by the great Silla-dynasty Korean Buddhist philosopher **Wǒnhyo** 元曉 ([[元曉]], 617–686).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *新羅元曉撰*.

### Abstract

Wǒnhyo's commentary applies his characteristic harmonising (和諍 *hwajaeng*) interpretive method to the *Yīngluò běnyè jīng's* bodhisattva-path schema. As a major work of Korean Silla scholasticism, it is preserved in the *Xùzàngjīng* 卍 supplementary canon and is one of the principal extant commentaries on this important Mahāyāna text.
