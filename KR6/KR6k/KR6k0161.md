# Sìfēnlǜ shū 四分律疏
*Commentary on the Sìfēnlǜ*
by [[法礪]] (Fǎlì, 撰述)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle Suí–early-Táng commentary on the *Sìfēnlǜ* 四分律 ([[KR6k0001]]) by **Fǎlì** 法礪 ([[法礪]], 569–635), the founding patriarch of the **Xiāngbù** 相部 school of Tang Vinaya exegesis. The work is the foundational treatise of one of the three competing Tang Vinaya schools (*Xiāngbù* / *Nánshān* / *Dōngtǎ*).

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a *mùcì* 目次 listing the contents of each *juan* (本/末 sub-divisions, "from the opening of the title to the explanation of the *fājiè* cause", etc.).

### Abstract

Fǎlì's *Shū* is the senior of the three principal Tang *Sìfēnlǜ* commentaries, predating Dàoxuān's ([[道宣]]) *Xíngshì chāo* and *Suíjī jiémó shū* by a generation. Where Dàoxuān's works are practical-pedagogical and oriented to monastic conduct, Fǎlì's *Shū* is a doctrinal-philosophical exposition keyed to the conceptual structure of the *Sìfēn* in the manner inherited from his teachers in the late-Suí Vinaya tradition. The *Xiāngbù* school's distinctive doctrinal positions — including its analysis of the *jiètǐ* 戒體 ("substance of the precepts") in non-*Mahāyāna* terms, in contrast to Dàoxuān's *yuánjiào* 圓教 reading — are systematically argued here. Fǎlì's chief disciple **Dìng Bīn** 定賓 ([[定賓]]) produced the principal sub-commentary, [[KR6k0163]] *Sìfēnlǜ shū shìzōng yìjì*. The *Xiāngbù* school flourished into the early Tang but was gradually displaced by the *Nánshān* tradition; Fǎlì's *Shū* survives as our principal direct witness to the *Xiāngbù* doctrinal position.
