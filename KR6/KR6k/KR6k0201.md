# Shèlì chànfǎ 舍利懺法
*A Repentance Liturgy for the Buddha-Relics*
by [[繼僧]] (Jìsēng, 著)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Qīng-period repentance liturgy (*chànfǎ* 懺法) compiled by **Shēnqī Jìsēng** 深栖繼僧 (also known as 聲碧 Shēngbì), a Tiāntái-mountain ascetic, at the *Fúguó jīngshè* 福國精舍 in Hánjiāng 邗江 (Yángzhōu region) in the spring of 1718. The liturgy takes the *śarīra* (*shèlì* 舍利, Buddha-relics) as the cardinal object of veneration and *dhāraṇī* recitation as its instrumental support: practitioners chant the *Dà bǎo guǎngbó suíxīn tuóluóní* 大寶廣博隨心陀羅尼 across forty-nine days of altar service to "extinguish all sin and fulfil all wishes." Edited (校) by **Dìngshān bǐqiū Yánzhāng** 定山比丘嚴章.

### Prefaces

The work opens with three prefaces. **(A)** *Shèlì língyìng xù* 舍利靈應序 ("Preface on the Numinous Response of the Relics"), signed *Dà-Qīng Kāngxī wùxū zhōngqiū qián sānrì Láoshān Lǐ Zhào* 大清康熙歲次戊戌中秋前三日勞山李照拜手書 — i.e. three days before mid-autumn, **1718**. Lǐ Zhào recounts how, while visiting Fúguó jīngshè to consult *Jìshēng chánshī* 濟生禪師, he met Shēngbì *héshàng* 聲碧和尚, who described how a *śarīra* had appeared at the tip of his brush while he was copying the *Huáyán jīng* 華嚴經 in his own blood on Mount Tiāntái, and how a second auspicious cloud had appeared three days later. **(B)** *Shèlì chàn yuánqǐ xù* 舍利懺緣起序 ("Preface on the Origin of the Relic Repentance"), signed *Hánjiāng Fúguó Yuándù* 邗江福國元度. Yuándù (a fellow disciple of Jìsēng's late teacher) describes Shēngbì's blood-copying of the *Huáyán*, *Fǎhuá* 法華 and *Lèngyán* 楞嚴, the manifestation of the relics at the brush-tip while writing the *Shíxìn* 十信 chapter, and the establishment of the present *chànfǎ* by Shēngbì at Fúguó. **(C)** *Zì xù* 自序 ("Author's Preface"), signed *Gǔ-Wú Shēnqī Jìsēng zìshù* 古吳深栖繼僧自述. The author dates his liturgical fast to a forty-nine-day altar held in 1718 (戊戌歲), at the conclusion of which (5th lunar month, full-moon day) the relics descended in answer to the assembly's prostrations.

### Abstract

The *Shèlì chànfǎ* is a relic-cult repentance manual produced in response to a specific late-Kāngxī devotional event at Fúguó jīngshè in Yángzhōu in 1718. The compiler **Jìsēng** (dates not precisely known; flourished early Qīng) had spent the years 1705–1718 secluded on Tiāntáishān copying Mahāyāna sūtras in his own blood; the manifestation of relics in his brushwork during the writing of the *Huáyán*'s *Shíxìn* chapter became the occasion for him to compose a structured *chànfǎ* organising the relic-cult around the *suíxīn dhāraṇī*. The work divides the rite into ten *kē* 科 (procedural sections). Composition is precisely datable to 1718 (Kāngxī 57) on the basis of the three contemporary prefaces; both `notBefore` and `notAfter` are set to that year. The DILA Buddhist Person Authority notes Jìsēng's life-dates are not preserved beyond his own statements in this work's prefaces.
