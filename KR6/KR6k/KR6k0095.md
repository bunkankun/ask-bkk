# Púsàjiè shū suíjiàn lù 菩薩戒疏隨見錄
*Records of Casual Observations on the Bodhisattva Precept Commentary*
by [[今釋]] (Jīn Shì, 造)

## About the work

A one-fascicle late-Míng / early-Qīng commentary-notes by the Chán master **Jīn Shì** 今釋 ([[今釋]]) on the bodhisattva-precepts. 隨見錄 *suíjiàn lù* "records of casual observations" indicates the genre — informal exegetical notes accumulated through reading.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *明今釋造*.

### Abstract

The text reflects the late-Míng tradition of Chán-Vinaya synthesis, integrating Chán meditative practice with bodhisattva-precept observance. Jīn Shì was associated with the Cáodòng 曹洞 lineage of Chán in the late Míng / early Qīng transition.
