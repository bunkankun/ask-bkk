# Sēng jiémó 僧羯磨
*Karmavācanā for Monks* (Dōngtǎ school)
by [[懷素]] (Huáisù, 集)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Dharmaguptaka *karmavācanā* compendium for the **monks' saṅgha**, compiled by **Huáisù** 懷素 ([[懷素]], 624–697) of the **Dōng-tǎ** 東塔 (Eastern Pagoda) school of Sìfēn Vinaya. Composed in the second half of the 7th century at Cháng'ān, the text presents an alternative — and in some traditions, a competing — *karmavācanā* compendium to Dàoxuān's *Sìfēn lǜ shānbǔ suíjī jiémó* ([[KR6k0046]]).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐西太原寺沙門懷素集*.

### Abstract

The *Sēng jiémó* represents the **Dōngtǎ school** of Sìfēn Vinaya — one of the three competing exegetical traditions of the Tang Sìfēn Vinaya, alongside Dàoxuān's *Nánshān* and Fǎlì's *Xiāngbù*. Where Dàoxuān gave priority to a synthesising approach combining multiple Vinaya traditions to clarify the *Sìfēn lǜ*, Huáisù's Dōngtǎ school took a strict-textualist approach, working only from the *Sìfēn lǜ* itself and rejecting external supplementation. The two *karmavācanā* compendia accordingly differ in detail. After the eclipse of the Dōngtǎ school in the late Tang, Dàoxuān's tradition became universal — but Huáisù's text remained a respected secondary authority, especially in the Sòng under the influence of **Yuánzhào**'s eclectic recovery.
