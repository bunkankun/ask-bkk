# Sìfēn jièběn shū 四分戒本疏 卷第一．第二．第三
*Commentary on the Sìfēn Prātimokṣa, Fascicles 1–3* (Dunhuang fragments)
(author unknown)

## About the work

Three fascicles of an anonymous Tang-period commentary on the *Sìfēn prātimokṣa*, preserved in **Dunhuang** manuscript form and included in volume 85 of the Taishō *Gǔyìbù* 古逸部.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The Dunhuang library cave preserved numerous Tang-period commentary fragments on the *Sìfēn lǜ* and its *prātimokṣa* — testimony to the active Vinaya scholarship at Dunhuang and other northwestern Chinese Buddhist centres in the late-Tang period. This three-fascicle fragment is one such commentary, complementing the major canonical commentaries ([[道宣]] Dàoxuān, [[懷素]] Huáisù, [[定賓]] Dìng Bīn) and providing evidence for the diversity of *Sìfēn* exegetical traditions in mid-late Tang Buddhism. [[KR6k0134]]–0142 are similar Dunhuang Vinaya commentary fragments.
