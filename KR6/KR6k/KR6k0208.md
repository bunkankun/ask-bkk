# Nánshān zǔshī lǐzàn wén 南山祖師禮讚文
*A Hymnody of Veneration for the Patriarch-Master of the Nánshān (Vinaya) School*
by [[仁岳]] (Rényuè, 述)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Northern-Sòng *lǐzàn wén* 禮讚文 for the death-anniversary of **Nánshān Dàoxuān** 南山道宣 (596–667), composed by **Jìngjué Rényuè** 淨覺仁岳 ([[仁岳]], 992–ca. 1064), the *Zháchuān shāmén* 霅川沙門 — i.e. monk of *Zháxī* 霅谿 (Wúxīng 吳興). Author signature: *Zháchuān shāmén shì Rényuè shù* 霅川沙門釋　仁岳　述. The work is the **second** of the three Sòng *Nánshān lǐzàn wén* preserved in the Xùzàngjīng (X1504, [[KR6k0207]] = Zhìyuán; X1505, this work = Rényuè; X1506, [[KR6k0209]] = Yǔnkǎn).

### Prefaces

Yuánzhào's later collected preface (preserved as the head of [[KR6k0207]]) explains Rényuè's compositional motive: Zhìyuán's earlier *zàn* "lacked metricalmusical fitness" (*shìyí shàng quē yīnyùn* 事儀尚闕音韻), so Rényuè wrote his own to supply the lyrical idiom needed for chanted altar use. Rényuè's own opening prose preface (here in the body of the text) declares: *Wǔchéng qǐyùn wéi shēngwén zuì xiān; sānzàng liúhuī gù píní wéi běn* 五乘啟運唯聲聞最先。三藏流輝故毗尼為本 — "Of the five vehicles in unfolding, the *śrāvaka* is foremost; of the three baskets in radiant transmission, *vinaya* is foundational" — and identifies Dàoxuān explicitly as the *Xīmíng lǜshī* 西明律師 (i.e. master of the Xīmíngsì in Cháng'ān).

### Abstract

Rényuè's *Nánshān zǔshī lǐzàn wén* is structurally parallel to Zhìyuán's: an *yīxīn fèngqǐng* 一心奉請 invocation, prostrations, eulogy verses keyed to Dàoxuān's biographical episodes (the descent of relics into a precious casket at *Rìyán jīngshè* 日嚴精舍, the transmission of the fourpart *vinaya*, etc.), with closing offerings. Where Zhìyuán wrote the prototype, Rényuè's contribution is principally **liturgical**: he set the eulogy in chantable verse-and-prose alternation, and supplied the metrical *zàn* stanzas that became standard in Sòngonward Vinayaschool anniversary services. Rényuè's life is bracketed 992–ca. 1064 ; the *zànwén* must postdate his break with *Sìmíng Zhīlǐ* 四明知禮 (after ca. 1020) and predate his death — `notBefore` and `notAfter` are accordingly set 1020–1064. Rényuè was best known for his *Shí bùèr mén wénxīn jiě* 十不二門文心解 ([[KR6d0162]], X928), the cardinal *shānwài* tract of the Sòng Tiāntái doctrinal controversy.
