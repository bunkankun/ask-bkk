# Fóāpítán jīng chūjiāxiàng pǐn 佛阿毘曇經出家相品
*The Buddha-Abhidharma Sūtra: Chapter on the Forms of Going-Forth*
by [[真諦]] (Paramārtha, 譯)

## About the work

A two-fascicle excerpt from a larger Indian abhidharmic-Vinaya work, presenting the chapter (品 *pǐn*) on the **forms of monastic ordination** (出家相 *chūjiā xiàng*). Translated by **Paramārtha** 真諦 ([[真諦]]) at the Chén court in 558–569 CE.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *陳天竺三藏真諦譯*.

### Abstract

The *Fóāpítán* is an abhidharmic-Vinaya hybrid work — combining doctrinal-systematic analysis (*abhidharma*) with monastic-disciplinary content (*vinaya*). The "chapter on the forms of going-forth" expounds the doctrinal taxonomy of *upasaṃpadā* — the various modes by which monastic ordination is conferred (the early "ehi-bhikṣu" form, the *tisaraṇa* form, the *karmavācanā* form, etc.). The text is one of the few Sammitīya / Sāṃmatīya-affiliated works preserved in Chinese (cf. [[KR6k0042]] *Lǜ èrshíèr míngliǎo lùn*); Paramārtha's preference for Sammitīya material reflects his Indian background at Vālabhī, the school's principal centre.
