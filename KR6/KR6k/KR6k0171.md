# Zīchí jì lìtí shíyì 資持記立題拾義
*Picking Up the Meanings of the Title-Establishment in the Zīchí jì*
by [[道標]] (Dàobiāo, 出)

## About the work

A brief one-juan Sòng exposition of the title-line of **Yuánzhào**'s ([[元照]], 1048–1116) *Zīchí jì* 資持記 — Yuánzhào's principal sub-commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēnlǜ shānfán bǔquē xíngshì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]) — by **Dàobiāo** 道標 ([[道標]]) of *Móyúntáng* 摩雲堂. Author signature: *摩雲堂佛慧宗師　道標　出* (*Set forth by Dàobiāo, master Fóhuì of Móyúntáng*).

### Prefaces

The work itself opens with the lemmatic exposition of the title; no separate front-matter.

### Abstract

The *Lìtí shíyì* glosses each phrase of the *Zīchí jì*'s long title (*Sìfēnlǜ Xíngshì chāo Zīchí jì*), distinguishing the *tōngtí* 通題 (general title) from the *biémù* 別目 (specific designation) and explaining the relation of *suǒjiě* 所解 (object of explanation) to *néngjiě* 能解 (means of explanation). The opening passage explicitly cites Dàoxuān's *Jièshū* 戒疏 — "*Sìfēn* is the specific name of one of the five recensions and the general designation of one school" — and notes that more than fifty earlier *Xíngshì chāo* commentators (*wǔshí yú jiā* 五十餘家) had already glossed the title in their own ways. Dating: the work must postdate Yuánzhào (post-1116) and falls within the Southern-Sòng range.
