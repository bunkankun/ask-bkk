# Sìfēnlǜ sōuxuán lù 四分律搜玄錄
*Records Searching the Mysteries of the Sìfēnlǜ*
by [[志鴻]] (Zhìhóng, 撰述)

## About the work

A surviving three-fascicle fragment (originally twenty *juan*) of a mid-Táng *jì* 記 sub-commentary on **Dàoxuān**'s ([[道宣]]) *Sìfēnlǜ shānfán bǔquē xíngshì chāo* 四分律刪繁補闕行事鈔 ([[KR6k0017]]) by the Sūzhōu Vinaya master **Zhìhóng** 志鴻 ([[志鴻]]) of Shuānglínsì 雙林寺. The work programmatically collects and digests the *jì* and *chāo* of more than forty earlier exegetes — including *Dàcí* [[大慈]], *Língè* [[靈崿]], and others of the post-Dàoxuān generation — and is invaluable for the reconstruction of pre-Sòng *Nánshān* Vinaya scholarship.

### Prefaces

The Manji edition opens with a preface by **Chéngguān** [[澄觀]] (738–839), the Fourth Patriarch of *Huāyán*, here signed *京師大興唐寺沙門釋　澄觀* (*Chéngguān, monk of Dàxìngtángsì in the imperial capital*). The preface dates the work to the *Dàlì* 大曆 era (766–779) by reference to "five hundred years after Buddha-day" reckoning and identifies Zhìhóng as the *fǔsì* 輔嗣 ("auxiliary successor") of Dàoxuān's school.

### Abstract

The *Sōuxuán lù* is the principal mid-Táng *Nánshān* sub-commentary surviving in fragmentary form and is the chief witness to the diversity of post-Dàoxuān Vinaya exegesis between the second-half of the seventh century and the late eighth. Chéngguān's preface confirms not only the date but the breadth of Zhìhóng's reading; the preserved fragments are a critical resource for identifying lost Tang *jì* writers. Zhìhóng died at the age of one hundred and eight (per the DILA biographical authority); his composition window must precede that and is fixed here by Chéngguān's *Dàlì* preface.
