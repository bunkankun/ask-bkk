# Fànwǎngjīng púsàjiè shū 梵網經菩薩戒疏
*Commentary on the Bodhisattva Precepts of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[法銑]] (Fǎ Xiǎn, 撰)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Tang-dynasty commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* bodhisattva-precepts by **Fǎ Xiǎn** 法銑 ([[法銑]]).

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐法銑撰*.

### Abstract

Fǎ Xiǎn's commentary is one of the more compact Tang-period treatments of the *Fànwǎng* precepts. It draws on the major preceding commentaries ([[智顗]] Zhìyǐ–[[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Púsàjiè yìshū* [[KR6k0078]], [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's *Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn shū* [[KR6k0080]]) while presenting a more summary exposition suited to instructional use.
