# Shàn gōngjìng jīng 善恭敬經
*The Sūtra of Proper Respect*
by [[闍那崛多]] (Jñānagupta, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Mahāyāna *sūtra* on the proper attitudes of respect (恭敬 *gōngjìng*) toward the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Saṅgha, translated by **Jñānagupta** 闍那崛多 ([[闍那崛多]]) at the Suí court late 6th century. Companion text to [[KR6k0118]] (*Zhèng gōngjìng jīng*).

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *隋天竺三藏闍那崛多譯*.

### Abstract

The text expounds the **etiquette of the Three Jewels** (Buddha, Dharma, Saṅgha): how a practitioner should approach, address, and honour each of the Three Jewels. Such etiquette texts supply the practical-behavioural foundation for monastic and lay Buddhist conduct.
