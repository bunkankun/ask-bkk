# Cíbēi dàochǎng shuǐchànfǎ kēzhù 慈悲道場水懺法科註
*A Sub-Section Commentary on the Compassion-Site Water-Repentance Liturgy*
by [[西宗]] (Xīzōng, 集註)

## About the work

A three-fascicle late-Qīng *kēzhù* 科註 (subsection + interlinear commentary) on the Táng-period *Cíbēi shuǐchànfǎ* 慈悲水懺法 (T1910 / [[KR6c0007]]) attributed to *Wùdá guóshī* Zhīxuán 悟達國師知玄. Compiled by **Chéngjiǎn Xīzōng** 成簡西宗 ([[西宗]], ?–1688), dharmaheir of *Lǚpíng Zhìxián fǎshī* 履平智賢法師 (1608–1673) at *Jīn'ésì* 金峩寺 in Níngbō, while resident at the *Lóngzhùsì* 龍住寺 in Xīxiāng 西鄉. The author signature reads *Lóngzhù shì Chéngjiǎn Xīzōng jízhù* 龍住釋　成簡西宗　集註.

### Prefaces

**(A)** *Cíbēi dàochǎng shuǐchàn yuán xù* 慈悲道場水懺原敘 — a recapitulation of the *yuánqǐ* 緣起 of the parent *Shuǐ chàn*: the encounter between **Wùdá guóshī** 悟達國師 (Zhīxuán 知玄) and the bodhisattva **Jiānuòjiā zūnzhě** 迦諾迦尊者 (Kanaka) under the Táng emperor Yìzōng 懿宗, the affliction of the *rénmiàn chuāng* 人面瘡 (the "faceshaped boil") born from the karmic enmity of *Yuán Àng* 袁盎 and *Cháo Cuò* 晁錯 of the Western Hàn, and its washing by the bodhisattva's *sānmèi shuǐ* 三昧水 (water of *samādhi*) at *Péngzhōu Jiǔlóngshān* 彭州九龍山 in Western Shǔ.

**(B)** *Yuánqǐ wén* 緣起文 — Xīzōng's own opening statement on the rite's purpose: "to turn the resentful into kin and to transform sin's morass into a field of merit."

A later editorial colophon clarifies that the master "followed the old block-edition's *kē* divisions and continued his interlinear notes [until] illness reached its crisis and he laid down the brush" (病篤而止) — the work was therefore **finished only as far as the master's strength held** before his death in early 1688 (Kāngxī 26 / 27, 12th lunar month).

### Abstract

Xīzōng's *Kēzhù* parses the *Shuǐ chàn* sentence by sentence, supplementing the source-text with a *kē* 科 (section-tree) skeleton followed by Chán/Jiào doctrinal annotations. The *zhù* threads canonical citations (the *Jīngāng jīng*, *Nièpán jīng*, the seven-Buddha *fùfǎ jié* 付法偈, etc.) into a comprehensive devotional commentary. Composition is precisely bracketed: Xīzōng was tonsured in middle age after a family disaster, kept a long period of canon-reading retreat under his master Lǚpíng (d. 1673), then taught at Pǔqiū 普邱, Dìngqiáo 定橋 and Jiāngxīn 江心 monasteries; the *Kēzhù* must have been completed in the period between his teacher's death in 1673 and his own in early 1688 — `notBefore` and `notAfter` are accordingly set 1670–1688. The work is preserved by the *Jīn'ésì zhì* 金峩寺志, juan 5.
