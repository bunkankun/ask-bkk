# Fó shuō púsà nèijiè jīng 佛說菩薩內戒經
*The Sūtra on the Inner Bodhisattva Precepts*
by [[求那跋摩]] (Guṇavarman, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Mahāyāna *sūtra* on the inner-mental aspects of bodhisattva-discipline, translated by **Guṇavarman** ([[求那跋摩]]) at Jiànkāng in 431 CE. 內戒 *nèi-jiè* "inner precepts" refers to the mental-attitudinal dimensions of the bodhisattva-vow, complementing the external behavioural precepts.

### Prefaces

Translator's colophon: *宋罽賓沙門求那跋摩譯*.

### Abstract

The text is part of Guṇavarman's foundational project of introducing **Mahāyāna bodhisattva-precept material** to early-medieval Chinese Buddhism. Its emphasis on the inner-mental dimensions of the bodhisattva-vow — the *bodhicitta* "mind of awakening" and the meditative-volitional aspects of bodhisattva-practice — anticipates the later Tiāntái and Chán readings of the bodhisattva-precepts as fundamentally mental-spiritual rather than merely behavioural commitments.
