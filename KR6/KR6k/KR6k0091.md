# Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn shùjì 梵網經菩薩戒本述記
*Recorded Exposition of the Bodhisattva Precept-Text of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[勝莊]] (Sŭngjang, 撰)

## About the work

A two-fascicle commentary on the *Fànwǎng* bodhisattva-precepts by the Silla-dynasty Korean scholar **Sŭngjang** 勝莊 ([[勝莊]]), composed during his Tang-period activity at Cháng'ān. The commentary represents a Yogācāra-Cí'ēn 慈恩 reading.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *西大律者勝莊撰*.

### Abstract

Sŭngjang's commentary preserves the Yogācāra-influenced Tang-Silla scholastic interpretation of the bodhisattva-precepts. Sŭngjang himself participated in [[義淨]] Yìjìng's translation projects at Cháng'ān, giving him direct access to the late-Indian Mahāyāna *bodhisattva-vinaya* tradition that informs the commentary.
