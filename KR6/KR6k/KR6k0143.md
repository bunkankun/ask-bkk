# Pínì zhēnjìng lù 毗尼珍敬錄
*Records of the Vinaya Treasured and Revered*
by [[廣承]] (Guǎng Chéng, 輯錄) and [[智旭]] (Zhìxù, 會補)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Vinaya selections-anthology compiled by **Guǎng Chéng** 廣承 ([[廣承]]) of the late Míng, supplemented and finalised by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 ([[智旭]]). 珍敬 *zhēnjìng* "treasured and revered" indicates the genre — a devotional-pedagogical anthology of Vinaya material.

### Prefaces

Compiler's colophon: *明廣承輯錄，智旭會補*.

### Abstract

The text is a working anthology of select Vinaya passages organised for late-Míng monastic instruction. Zhìxù's *huìbǔ* "compilation and supplementation" reflects his foundational role in the late-Míng Vinaya revival. The anthology format made the work suitable for daily monastic study and for non-specialist monastic education.
