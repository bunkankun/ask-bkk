# Sìbù lǜ bìng lùn yàoyòng chāo 四部律并論要用抄
*Practical Excerpts from the Four Vinayas and Treatises* (Dunhuang)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Dunhuang excerpts-compilation drawing on the four canonical Chinese Vinayas (四部律 *sìbù lǜ* = *Sìfēn*, *Wǔfēn*, *Sēngqí*, *Shísòng*) plus the associated Vinaya treatises (論 *lùn*). The most ambitious of the Dunhuang Vinaya excerpts-compilations.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The text is a substantial cross-Vinaya practical reference, drawing on all four canonical Chinese Vinayas plus the *vinaya-vibhāṣā* commentary tradition. The two fascicles testify to the depth of Vinaya scholarship at Dunhuang and provide important evidence for the active comparative-Vinaya tradition in northwestern Tang Buddhist communities.
