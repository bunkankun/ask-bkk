# Tiāntái Púsàjiè shū 天台菩薩戒疏
*The Tiantai Commentary on the Bodhisattva Precepts*
by [[明曠]] (Míngkuàng, 刪補)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Tang-dynasty edited and supplemented version of **Zhìyǐ's** *Púsàjiè yìshū* ([[KR6k0078]]) by the Tiāntái scholastic **Míngkuàng** 明曠 ([[明曠]]). 刪補 *shānbǔ* "edited and supplemented" indicates the redactional method.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐天台沙門明曠刪補*.

### Abstract

Míngkuàng's revised commentary represents the **mid-Tang Tiāntái scholastic consensus** on the bodhisattva-precepts. The work systematises Zhìyǐ's expository teaching, supplements it with intervening Tang-period scholarship (notably [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's *Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn shū* ([[KR6k0080]]) Huayan reading), and presents a synthesised Tiāntái interpretation that became the working text of the Tang–Sòng Tiāntái tradition.
