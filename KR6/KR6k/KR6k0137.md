# Zōng Sìfēn bǐqiū suímén yàolüè xíngyí 宗四分比丘隨門要略行儀
*Practical Decorum Manual for Bhikṣus following the Sìfēn Tradition* (Dunhuang)
(author unknown)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Dunhuang Vinaya manual on practical *bhikṣu* decorum, organised by topical "gates" (隨門 *suímén* "following the gates"). 宗四分 indicates allegiance to the Sìfēn (Dharmaguptaka) Vinaya tradition.

### Prefaces

No author identified.

### Abstract

The text is a working Dunhuang monastic *wēiyí* (decorum) handbook, providing practical behavioural guidance for monks following the *Sìfēn lǜ* tradition. Together with the parallel Dunhuang fragments ([[KR6k0133]]–0142), the text documents the practical Vinaya life at Dunhuang in the late-Tang period.
