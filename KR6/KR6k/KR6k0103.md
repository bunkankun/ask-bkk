# Fànwǎngjīng púsàjiè chūjīn 梵網經菩薩戒初津
*The First Ford to the Bodhisattva Precepts of the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[書玉]] (Shū Yù, 述)

## About the work

An eight-fascicle Qīng-dynasty commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* by **Shū Yù** 書玉 ([[書玉]]). 初津 *chūjīn* "first ford / initial crossing" is a metaphorical title — the work serves as the introductory study by which the practitioner first approaches the bodhisattva-precepts.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *清書玉述*.

### Abstract

Shū Yù's commentary is one of the more substantial Qīng-period treatments of the *Fànwǎng jīng*. Aimed at students entering the bodhisattva-precept tradition, the text provides systematic exposition with detailed coverage of practical procedural matters.
