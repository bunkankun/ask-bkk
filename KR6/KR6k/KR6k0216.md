# Zhīyuán yíbiān 芝園遺編
*Posthumous Compilation from the Zhīyuán [Garden]*
by [[元照]] (Yuánzhào, 錄); [[道詢]] (Dàoxún, 集)

## About the work

A three-fascicle posthumous compilation of miscellaneous *Língzhīsì* writings of **Yuánzhào** ([[元照]], 1048–1116, the *Língzhī Dàzhì lǜshī*), gathered by his Southern-Sòng disciple-successor **Dàoxún** 道詢 ([[道詢]], dates not preserved). *Zhīyuán* 芝園 ("Auspicious-Mushroom Garden") was the studio name of Yuánzhào at *Língzhīsì* 靈芝寺 (lit. "Numinous-*Lingzhi*-Mushroom Monastery") in Hángzhōu; *yíbiān* 遺編 designates the residual writings not gathered into the principal *Zhīyuán jí* 芝園集 ([[KR6k0217]]).

### Structural Division

The internal *mùlù* gives:

- **Upper fascicle**: *Jiètǐ zhāng* 戒體章 (treatise on the *substance* of the precepts), *Chífàn tǐ zhāng* 持犯體章 (the substance of upholding-and-violating), *Chífàn jùfǎ zhāng* 持犯句法章, *Lǜzōng yào* 律宗要 (essentials of the Vinaya school), *Dà xiǎo chéng lùn* 大小乘論 (on the great and lesser vehicles), *Shǐzhōng jiè yào* 始終戒要 (essentials of the precepts from beginning to end).
- **Middle fascicle**: *Shòu Dàchéng púsà jiè yí* 授大乘菩薩戒儀 (rite for conferring the bodhisattva precepts), *Tìfà yíshì* 剃髮儀式 (tonsure rite), *Shòujiè fāngbiàn* 受戒方便 (preliminaries for ordination), *Jiàn Míngzhōu Kāiyuánsì jiètán shìwén bìng Zhèng chéngxiàng bá* 建明州開元寺戒壇誓文(并鄭丞相跋) (foundinginscription for the *jiètán* 戒壇 ordination platform at *Kāiyuánsì* in Míngzhōu, with the colophon by Chancellor *Zhèng* — i.e. Northern-Sòng *Zhèng Jūzhōng* 鄭居中 or *Zhèng Jiè* 鄭挺?), *Jiè shāmí wén* 誡沙彌文.
- **Lower fascicle**: *Wèi Jiǎng shūmì kāijiǎng yàoyì (qǐng jiǎnshī fù)* 為蔣樞密開講要義(請簡詩附) (essentials for the inaugural lecture sponsored by Privy-Counsellor *Jiǎng* — *Jiǎng Zhīqí* 蔣之奇 (1031–1104), with the sponsorship-poem appended), *Wèi Gāolì sēngtǒng Yìtiān kāijiǎng yàoyì* 為高麗僧統義天開講要義 (essentials for the inaugural lecture given for the **Goryeo monk-supremo Ŭich'ŏn 義天** [1055–1101]), *Shàng Zhāān fǎshī shū* 上樝菴法師書 (letter to Master *Zhāān*), *Nánshān lǜzōng zǔchéng túlù* 南山律宗祖承圖錄 (genealogical chart of the Nánshān Vinaya patriarchate), *Nánshān lǜshī zhuàn chánglù bìng bá* 南山律師撰長錄(并跋) (long catalogue of works by [Dàoxuān], with colophon).

### Prefaces

Yuánzhào's own opening note for the *Jiètǐ zhāng* dates that piece precisely: *Xīníng sānnián, hòu ānjū rì, shì Yuánzhào yú Nánshān zǔshī jiémóshū lùchū jiètǐ zhāng* 熙寧三年。後安居日。釋元照於南山祖師羯磨疏錄出戒體章 — i.e. **1070 / Xīníng 3**, day after the conclusion of the rains-retreat. This is the earliest dated piece in the collection.

### Abstract

The *Zhīyuán yíbiān* is the **principal source for Yuánzhào's *theological* writings on the precept-body** (*jiètǐ* 戒體) and his *occasional* compositions for major patrons. It contains his earliest dated independent treatise (*Jiètǐ zhāng*, 1070) and his two most historically resonant *kāijiǎng yàoyì* 開講要義 inaugural-lecture pieces — the one for *Jiǎng Zhīqí* (Privy-Counsellor and biographer of Bái Jūyì), and the one for *Ŭich'ŏn 義天* of Goryeo, who visited Hángzhōu in **1085** during his year of study in Sòng China. The Goryeo lecture-piece is among the more important documents of Sòng-Goryeo Buddhist contact. The *Jiàn Míngzhōu Kāiyuánsì jiètán shìwén* records the inscription Yuánzhào composed for the founding of the *jiètán* ordination-platform at *Kāiyuánsì* in Míngzhōu (Níngbō), an important episode in the Sòng revival of formal *bhikṣu* ordination. Composition of the *yíbiān* itself is bracketed by the Xīníng-3 piece (1070) and Yuánzhào's death (1116); Dàoxún's posthumous *jí* 集 must postdate 1116. `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1070–1116 for the contents.

## Other points of interest

- The volume is the principal source for the chart-genealogy *Nánshān lǜzōng zǔchéng túlù*, the canonical Sòng version of the Nánshān Vinaya patriarchal lineage.
- The *Wèi Gāolì sēngtǒng Yìtiān kāijiǎng yàoyì* is one of the principal documentary witnesses to Sòng-Goryeo Buddhist contacts in the late 11th c.
