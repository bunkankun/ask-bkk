# Zhōngnán jiāyè 終南家業
*The Family Tradition of [Mount] Zhōngnán*
by [[守一]] (Shǒuyī, 述); [[行枝]] (Xíngzhī, 編)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Southern-Sòng treatise-and-letter collection by **Sìmíng Tiěwēng Shǒuyī** 四明鐵翁守一 ([[守一]], dates not preserved; resident from **1239** at *Tāizhōu Rìshān* 台州日山), the late-Sòng restorer of the *Nánshān* Vinaya school. Edited by his disciple **Xíngzhī** 行枝 ([[行枝]]). The title *Zhōngnán jiāyè* — "The Family Inheritance of Mt Zhōngnán" — designates the doctrinal heritage of *Nánshān Dàoxuān* 南山道宣 (596–667), whose principal seat was Mt Zhōngnán in Cháng'ān; Shǒuyī, in lineage from *Rúān Liǎohóng* 如庵了宏, presents himself as the heir who must "keep up the family business." The full author signature reads *Sìmíng Tiěwēng zōngshī shù / ménrén Xíngzhī biān* 四明　鐵翁宗師　述／門人　行枝　編.

### Structural Division

**Upper fascicle:** *Jiàoguān cuōyào* 教觀撮要 (essentials of doctrine and contemplation), *Sānguān chénlù* 三觀塵露 (the three contemplations: dust and dew), ***Dá Rìběn Réng fǎshī jiàoguān zhū wèn*** 答日本芿法師教觀諸問 (replies to the Japanese monk **Réng** [Shunjō 俊芿, 1166–1227] on matters of *jiàoguān* — see [[KR6k0256]] *Lǜzōng wèndá*).

**Middle fascicle:** *Chóngshòu jièwén* 重受戒文 (rite for re-receiving the precepts), *Jiètǐ zhèngyì zhíyán* 戒體正義直言, *Sìzhèng yàolùn* 四諍要論 (essential discussion of the four contentions), *Yīzhì géyán* 衣制格言 (canonical maxims on the regulation of robes), *Lùn fēnbù* 論分部, *Lùn sēngtǐ* 論僧體, *Kēshì Záxīnlùn chū sānyǒu duì wén* 科釋雜心論出三有對文, *Shòuyuán chóngkāi* 受緣重開.

**Lower fascicle:** *Lùn xīnyòng shuāng chífàn jié zhìzuì* 論心用雙持犯結制罪, *Biàn èrzhǐ bìng bājiǔ míngyì* 辨二止並八九名義, *Chóng shì Shìchāo chífàn piān tōngsāi wén* 重釋事鈔持犯篇通塞文, *Chóng dá Qīnshī jìngxiǎng wèn* 重答欽師境想問, *Xīrán Mèngān chífàn sì nán* 㭊然夢庵持犯四難, *Zhēngxiǎn dìngdào èrjiè* 徵顯定道二戒, *Lüè biàn zhèngyòng xiāngcóng* 略辨正用相從, *Lüèyì dìqī fēitǐ* 略議第七非體, *Biàn lüèjiào jiéfàn* 辨略教結犯.

### Prefaces

The *jiàoguān cuōyào* of fascicle 1 opens: *"Wú zǔ hóng lǜ, yǐ miàoguān wéi běn"* 吾祖弘律。以妙觀為本 — "Our patriarch promoted the *vinaya* by taking *miàoguān* 妙觀 (subtle contemplation) as foundation. This follows from the Vinaya itself, in which the Buddha enjoins the *bhikṣu* to *guānniàn duì-* (contemplative attention)."

### Abstract

The *Zhōngnán jiāyè* is **the principal Southern-Sòng *Nánshānlǜ* doctrinal compendium**, written at *Tāizhōu Rìshān* by Shǒuyī after he settled there in 1239 (Jiāxī 3). The collection is exegetically tied to *Yuánzhào* 元照's *Língzhī* corpus a century earlier (see [[KR6k0167]] *Sìfēn lǜ xíngshì chāo zīchí jì*) but engages on the technical level of Sòng *guānxīn* 觀心 doctrine: the *jiètǐ* (precept-substance), *chífàn* (upholding-and-violation), and *sēngtǐ* (the *substance* of the saṅgha) treatises in the middle fascicle revisit late-Sòng controversies. The most historically significant piece is in fascicle 1: *Dá Rìběn Réng fǎshī jiàoguān zhū wèn* — Shǒuyī's replies to the Japanese pilgrim-monk **Shunjō Réng** 俊芿 (1166–1227, founder of *Sennyūji* 泉涌寺 in Kyoto) on questions of *jiào* and *guān*. This is one of three Southern-Sòng *Lǜzōngwèndá* groupings (the others survive as [[KR6k0256]]). Composition is bracketed by Shǒuyī's residence at Rìshān (from 1239) and a defensible upper bound for his floruit; `notBefore`–`notAfter` are accordingly set 1239–1260. Companion volume: [[KR6k0220]] *Lǜzōng huìyuán*.

## Translations and research

- For Shunjō Réng and Sòng-Japan Vinaya contacts: see Paul Groner, *Saichō: The Establishment of the Japanese Tendai School* (Honolulu, 2000) Appendix; and the *Sennyūjishi* documentation. The *Dá Rìběn Réng fǎshī jiàoguān zhū wèn* in this volume is a standard source for that history.

## Other points of interest

- Together with [[KR6k0256]] *Lǜzōng wèndá*, this volume constitutes the principal Southern-Sòng response-corpus to Japanese Vinaya pilgrims.
