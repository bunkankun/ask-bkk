# Fànwǎngjīng jì 梵網經記
*Records on the Brahmajāla Sūtra*
by [[傳奧]] (Chuán Ào, 述)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Tang-dynasty commentary on the *Fànwǎng jīng* by **Chuán Ào** 傳奧 ([[傳奧]]). The work belongs to the broader Huāyán-influenced Tang exegetical tradition.

### Prefaces

Author's colophon: *唐傳奧述*.

### Abstract

Chuán Ào's commentary is a representative example of mid-Tang scholastic literature on the *Fànwǎng jīng*, working within the Huāyán-Cí'ēn 慈恩 synthesis that characterised the period after [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's *Fànwǎngjīng púsàjièběn shū* ([[KR6k0080]]) foundational commentary.
