# Zēngxiū Jiàoyuàn qīngguī 增修教苑清規
*Augmented-and-Revised Pure-Rules for Doctrinal-Garden [Tiāntái Monasteries]*
edited by [[自慶]] (Zìqìng / Yúnwài Zìqìng, 編述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* Yuán-period revision by **Yúnwài Zìqìng** 雲外自慶 of the *qīngguī* 清規 (pure-rules) monastic regulations for Tiāntái-school institutions (*jiàoyuàn* 教苑 — "doctrinal gardens," the Tiāntái-school monastic designation distinguishing them from Chán-school institutions).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within the Yuán productive period (1271–1368). The work belongs to the broader medieval Buddhist *qīngguī* tradition that includes the foundational *[[百丈清規]]* of Chán Buddhism and parallel monastic regulations for the various Buddhist schools.
