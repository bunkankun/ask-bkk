# Tiāntái Zhìzhě dàshī zhuànlùn 天台智者大師傳論
*Biographical Discourse on the Tiāntái Great Master Zhìyǐ*
by [[梁肅]] (Liáng Sù / Liáng Jìngzhī, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* mid-Táng biographical discourse on Zhìyǐ 智顗 (538–597) by **Liáng Sù** 梁肅 (753–793), the mid-Táng polymath and Tiāntái lay-promoter. Companion to Liáng Sù's *Shāndìng zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0134]]).

### Abstract

The work is one of the principal mid-Táng documentary records of Zhìyǐ's biography and demonstrates Liáng Sù's institutional role as a major lay-Buddhist promoter of the Tiāntái tradition at the late-Táng court. The composition is bracketed within Liáng Sù's productive period c. 780–793.
