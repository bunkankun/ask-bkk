# Shí bùèr mén zhǐyào chāo xiángjiě 十不二門指要鈔詳解
*Detailed Interpretation of the Pointing-the-Essentials Notes on the Ten Non-Dual Gates*
by [[可度]] (Kědù / Wújí Kědù, 祥解); divided-and-collated by [[正謐]] (Zhèngmì / Yǐxuán, 分會)

## About the work

A two-*juan* late-Sòng / early-Yuán detailed commentary by **Wújí Kědù** 無極可度 on Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐyào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]], T1928), with editorial *fēnhuì* 分會 ("division-and-combination") work by the late-Míng monk **Yǐxuán Zhèngmì** 以玄正謐 of Línhǎi 臨海. The work is the principal late-Sòng-Yuán-Míng subcommentary on Zhīlǐ's foundational *shānjiā* polemical work.

### Abstract

Kědù's *Xiángjiě* provides systematic late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái commentary on Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐyào chāo*, integrating two centuries of accumulated scholastic interpretation of the work into a unified detailed exposition. The Míng editorial work by Zhèngmì subsequently reorganised the text for late-imperial readership.

The dating of the original Kědù composition is bracketed within his productive period in the late Sòng / early Yuán transition (c. 1200–1300); the editorial finalisation by Zhèngmì extends the bracket to the late-Míng period (c. 1400–1600).
