# Shí bùèr mén yì 十不二門義
*The Meaning of the Ten Non-Dual Gates*
extracted-and-published by [[道邃]] (Dàosuì / Xīngdào, 錄出)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Táng record of Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* ([[KR6d0157]], T1927), extracted-and-published (錄出 *lùchū*) by **Dàosuì** 道邃 — the tenth Tiāntái patriarch and Saichō's Chinese teacher. The work preserves the *Shí bùèr mén* in a slightly different recension or with editorial framing distinct from the Taishō T1927 recension, demonstrating the post-Zhànrán textual transmission at Tiāntáishān.

### Prefaces

The text in the X56n0925 recension reproduces Zhànrán's text with editorial framing by Dàosuì.

### Abstract

Dàosuì's *Shí bùèr mén yì* belongs together with his *Zhǐguān jìzhōng yìyì* ([[KR6d0136]]) as the principal documentary witnesses to Dàosuì's role as the post-Zhànrán Tiāntái scholastic transmitter at Tiāntáishān. The work is consequently of substantial historiographical importance for the modern reconstruction of late-Táng Tiāntái scholastic activity.

The composition is bracketed within Dàosuì's productive period c. 800–850.
