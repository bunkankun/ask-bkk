# Táizōng shílèi yīngé lùn 台宗十類因革論
*Treatise on the Ten Categories of Continuity-and-Change in the Tiāntái School*
by [[善月]] (Shànyuè / Bóting Shànyuè, 述)

## About the work

A four-*juan* Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* doctrinal-historical treatise by **Bóting Shànyuè** 柏庭善月. The work systematically analyses ten categories of *yīngé* 因革 ("continuity-and-change") in the Tiāntái doctrinal tradition.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Shànyuè's productive period c. 1180–1240.
