# Fǎhuá jīng zhǐzhǎng shū xuánshì 法華經指掌疏懸示
*Hanging-Display of the Pointing-the-Palm Subcommentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[通理]] Tōnglǐ (述)

## About the work

The introductory exposition (*xuánshì* 懸示, "hanging-display") volume of [[通理]] Tōnglǐ's four-text *Fǎhuá jīng zhǐzhǎng shū* commentarial complex (see [[KR6d0095]]). Provides the doctrinal-historical introduction to the *Zhǐzhǎng shū* main commentary ([[KR6d0097]]).

### Prefaces

The text carries the standard front matter for the introductory exposition. The *xuánshì* genre is parallel to the *xuántán* of [[KR6d0092]] and to the broader Tiāntái *xuányì* 玄義 framework — providing the systematic doctrinal-historical preface that orients the reader to the main commentary.

### Abstract

The *Xuánshì* belongs together with [[KR6d0095]] (the *kēwén*) as the principal navigational-introductory apparatus to Tōnglǐ's *Zhǐzhǎng shū* main commentary ([[KR6d0097]]). The two together fulfill the function of the standard Tiāntái scholastic *Wǔzhòng xuányì* introductory framework, providing the structural and doctrinal orientation needed for productive engagement with the main commentary.
