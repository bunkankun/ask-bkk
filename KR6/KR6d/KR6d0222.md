# Bǎoyún zhènzǔ jí 寶雲振祖集
*Collection of the Bǎoyún Patriarchal-Promulgation*
edited by [[宗曉]] (Zōngxiǎo / Shízhī Zōngxiǎo, 編)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng compilation by **Shízhī Zōngxiǎo** 石芝宗曉 (1151–1214) of materials documenting the **Bǎoyúnsì** 寶雲寺 Tiāntái lineage — the institutional centre under **Bǎoyún Yìtōng** 寶雲義通 ([[義通]], 927–988) at which Sìmíng Zhīlǐ received his Tiāntái formation. Companion to Yuánwù's parallel *Luóxī zhènzǔ jí* ([[KR6d0223]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zōngxiǎo's productive period c. 1180–1214.
