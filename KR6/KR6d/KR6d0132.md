# Zhǐguān yìlì 止觀義例
*Examples and Cases of Cessation-and-Contemplation*
by [[湛然]] (Zhànrán / Jīngxī Zhànrán, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* (上 / 下) supplementary doctrinal work to [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ's *Móhē zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0130]], T1911) by [[湛然]] Zhànrán, organised as a sequence of seven *lì* 例 ("cases" / "examples") that elaborate selected doctrinal points of the *Móhē zhǐguān*. The work belongs to Zhànrán's broader project of systematic Tiāntái scholastic consolidation alongside his three great subcommentaries.

### Prefaces

The text in the Taishō recension carries the standard front matter; the body opens directly with the seven *lì* listing: "First: the Transmitted-Section Distinction case. Second: the Foundational-Doctrine case. Third: the Foundational-Exposition case. Fourth: the Great-Section General-Particular case. Fifth: the Mind-and-Realm Resolving-Doubt case. Sixth: the Interpretation-and-Practice Mutual-Aid case. Seventh: the Parable-Doubt Manifesting-Right case (one heading says: 'breaking-confusion'; please change [as needed])."

### Abstract

The *Zhǐguān yìlì* is a focused supplementary doctrinal work that addresses seven selected interpretive issues in the *Móhē zhǐguān*, providing systematic Tiāntái scholastic resolution of contested points. The work is consequently more analytically focused than the comprehensive *Zhǐguān fǔxíng* ([[KR6d0131]]) and was widely used in the Tiāntái scholastic curriculum for systematic doctrinal preparation.

The composition is bracketed within Zhànrán's mature productive period c. 750–782.
