# Fǎhuá jīng kēshí kē 法華經科拾科
*Sectional Analysis of the Sectional-Gleanings on the Lotus Sūtra*
sectional structure by [[佛閑]] Fóxián (立科); re-redacted by [[智一]] Zhìyī (重訂)

## About the work

The sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) volume of the three-text *Fǎhuá jīng kēshí* commentarial complex by [[佛閑]] Fóxián 佛閑 of Pǔdésì 普德寺 in Nánjīng, with re-redaction (*chóngdìng* 重訂) by [[智一]] Zhìyī 智一. The work is the navigational apparatus to the *Kēshí* main commentary ([[KR6d0094]]) and follows the standard Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng editorial pattern of providing structural-outline navigational aids for major commentarial productions.

### Prefaces

The text in the X33n0627 recension carries the standard front matter and consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kēpàn* sectional division of the *Kēshí* main commentary.

### Abstract

The *Kēshí kē* belongs together with the *Kēshí xuántán juànshǒu* ([[KR6d0092]]) as the auxiliary navigational apparatus to the *Kēshí* main commentary ([[KR6d0094]]). The dating is securely fixed by Zhìyī's 1705 preface (preserved in [[KR6d0092]]).
