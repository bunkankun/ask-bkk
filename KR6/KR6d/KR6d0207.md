# Chánmén zhāng 禪門章
*Chapter on the Meditation-Gate*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dàshī, 說)

## About the work

A single-*juan* short meditation manual by Zhìyǐ 智顗 on the foundational meditation-gates of Buddhist practice. Companion to the parallel *Chánmén yàolüè* ([[KR6d0208]]).

### Abstract

The *Chánmén zhāng* belongs to Zhìyǐ's broader meditation-manual corpus alongside the four principal Tiāntái meditation texts ([[KR6d0143]], [[KR6d0144]], [[KR6d0145]], [[KR6d0130]]). The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
