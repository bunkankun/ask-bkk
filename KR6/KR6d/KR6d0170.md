# Sìjiào yí bèishì 四教儀備釋
*Comprehensive Interpretation of the Four-Doctrines Manual*
by [[元粹]] (Yuáncuì / Gǔyún Yuáncuì, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* (上 / 下) Yuán-period commentary by **Gǔyún Yuáncuì** 古雲元粹 of Tiáoxī (Húzhōu) on Dìguān's *Tiāntái sìjiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931). Body attribution: *Tiáoxī shāmén Yuáncuì shù* 苕谿沙門　元粹　述. The composition is bracketed within Yuáncuì's productive period c. 1214–1290 (extending the bracket beyond his documented productive years to allow for late activity).

### Abstract

Yuáncuì's *Bèishì* belongs to the substantial Sòng-Yuán commentarial tradition on Dìguān's *Sìjiào yí*. As a dharma-disciple of [[北峰宗印]] Běifēng Zōngyìn (1148–1213), Yuáncuì represents the late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái scholastic tradition's continued engagement with the Tiāntái doctrinal-classification framework.
