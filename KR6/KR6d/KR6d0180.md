# Jīngāngbēi lùn yìjiě 金剛錍論義解
*Doctrinal Interpretation of the Vajra-Lance Treatise*
by [[善月]] (Shànyuè / Bóting Shànyuè, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng commentary on Zhànrán's *Jīngāngbēi* ([[KR6d0175]], T1932) by **Bóting Shànyuè** 柏庭善月 of Sìmíng. Body attribution: *Sìmíng shāmén Shànyuè shù* 四明沙門　善月　述. Companion to Shànyuè's other Lotus-tradition productions ([[KR6d0021]], [[KR6d0064]]).

### Abstract

Shànyuè's *Yìjiě* belongs to the substantial Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* commentarial tradition on Zhànrán's *Jīngāngbēi*, providing the orthodox *shānjiā* interpretation of the doctrine of the buddha-nature of insentient beings against the *shānwài* alternative interpretation preserved in Zhìyuán's *Xiǎnxìng lù* ([[KR6d0179]]).

The composition is bracketed within Shànyuè's productive period at the Yánqìngsì 延慶寺 in Sìmíng, c. 1180–1240.
