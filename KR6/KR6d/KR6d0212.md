# Tiāntái Zhìzhě dàshī fāyuàn wén 天台智者大師發願文
*Vow-Arousing Text by the Tiāntái Great Master Zhìyǐ*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dàshī, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* personal vow-arousing text by Zhìyǐ 智顗, paralleling Huìsī's *Lìshìyuàn wén* ([[KR6d0182]]) as one of the early Tiāntái tradition's personal-vow declarations.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
