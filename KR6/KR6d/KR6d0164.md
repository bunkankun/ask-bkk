# Shí bùèr mén shūyào 十不二門樞要
*Pivot-Essentials of the Ten Non-Dual Gates*
by [[了然]] (Liǎorán / Zhìyǒng Liǎorán / Hǔxī zūnzhě, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* Sòng-period commentary by **Liǎorán** 了然 (1077–1141) on Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* ([[KR6d0157]]). Companion to Liǎorán's other major Sòng *shānjiā* commentary, the *Dàshèng zhǐguān fǎmén zōngyuán jì* ([[KR6d0153]]) on Huìsī's *Dàshèng zhǐguān fǎmén*.

### Abstract

Liǎorán's *Shūyào* belongs to the late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng productive period of the *shānjiā* commentarial tradition. Together with the broader *Shí bùèr mén* commentarial corpus ([[KR6d0158]]–[[KR6d0165]]), it documents the mature Sòng Tiāntái scholastic engagement with Zhànrán's foundational doctrinal apparatus.

The composition is bracketed within Liǎorán's productive period c. 1095–1141.
