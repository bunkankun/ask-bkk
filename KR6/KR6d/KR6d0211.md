# Guānxīn sòngjīng fǎjì 觀心誦經法記
*Notes on the Method of Contemplating-the-Mind during Sūtra-Recitation*
spoken by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ, 說); recorded by [[湛然]] (Zhànrán of Táng, 述)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* method-and-notes work on the practice of contemplating the mind during sūtra-recitation, with the foundational teaching attributed to Zhìyǐ and the recording-and-elaboration to Zhànrán. Companion to [[KR6d0210]] (on contemplating-the-mind during eating) as part of the Tiāntái daily-practice meditative apparatus.

### Abstract

The dating bracket spans Zhìyǐ's foundational productive period (575–597) through Zhànrán's recording-and-elaboration period (750–782).
