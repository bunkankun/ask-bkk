# Fǎhuá jīng shòushǒu kē 法華經授手科
*Sectional Analysis of the Hand-Bestowal Commentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[智祥]] (Zhìxiáng / Pínjí Zhìxiáng / Tīngyún, 排 — sequential arranger)

## About the work

The sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) volume of [[智祥]] Zhìxiáng's three-part *Fǎhuá jīng shòushǒu* 法華經授手 commentarial complex (see [[KR6d0087]], [[KR6d0089]]). Compiled by Pínjí Zhìxiáng 頻吉智祥 (1637–1709) himself.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0622 recension carries Zhìxiáng's own editorial framing of the sectional analysis. The work consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kēpàn* sectional division of the *Shòushǒu* main commentary ([[KR6d0089]]).

### Abstract

The *Shòushǒu kē* provides the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Zhìxiáng's *Shòushǒu* main commentary, parallel to [[KR6d0084]] (the *Dàchéng kē*) on [[大義]] Dàyì's *Dàchéng*. The three-part Zhìxiáng *Shòushǒu* complex (front matter, sectional analysis, main commentary) follows the standard Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng editorial pattern of providing complete navigational apparatus for major commentarial productions.
