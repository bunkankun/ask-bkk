# Tiāntái chuán Fó xīnyìn jì zhù 天台傳佛心印記註
*Annotation on the Record of the Tiāntái Transmission of the Buddha's Heart-Seal*
by [[傳燈]] (Chuándēng / Yōuxī Chuándēng, 註)

## About the work

A two-*juan* late-Míng commentary by **Yōuxī Chuándēng** 幽溪傳燈 (1554–1628), the central figure of the late-Míng Tiāntái revival, on Huáizé's *Tiāntái chuán Fó xīnyìn jì* ([[KR6d0187]], T1938).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Chuándēng's productive period c. 1580–1628.
