# Shìjiā rúlái jiàngshēng lǐzàn wén 釋迦如來降生禮讚文
*Worship-Encomium Text for the Birth of Tathāgata Śākyamuni*
by [[仁岳]] (Rényuè / Jìngjué Rényuè, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng commemorative liturgy by **Rényuè** 仁岳 for the commemoration of Śākyamuni Buddha's birth. Companion to Rényuè's parallel *parinirvāṇa* commemoration ([[KR6d0197]]), together providing the *shānwài* tradition's contribution to the standard Sòng-period Buddhist commemorative liturgical cycle.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Rényuè's productive period c. 1010–1060.
