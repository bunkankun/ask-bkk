# Tiāntái bājiào dàyì 天台八教大意
*The Great Purport of the Tiāntái Eight Doctrines*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng / Zhāngān Guàndǐng, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* synoptic exposition of the Tiāntái *bājiào* 八教 (eight doctrinal classifications) by Guàndǐng 灌頂 (561–632), Zhìyǐ's principal disciple and the redactor of his major treatises. The *bājiào* combines the Tiāntái *huàyí sìjiào* 化儀四教 (four modes of teaching: sudden / gradual / secret / indeterminate) and *huàfǎ sìjiào* 化法四教 (four doctrinal contents: *zàng* / *tōng* / *bié* / *yuán*) into the comprehensive eight-fold Tiāntái doctrinal-classification system.

### Abstract

Guàndǐng's *Bājiào dàyì* is one of the principal early Tiāntái pedagogical productions, providing accessible synoptic introduction to the foundational *bājiào* doctrinal-classification framework. The work is parallel in genre to Dìguān's later *Sìjiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931) but earlier and more extensive in doctrinal coverage.

The composition is bracketed within Guàndǐng's productive period after Zhìyǐ's death (597) and before his own death (632).
