# Móhē zhǐguān yìlì kē 摩訶止觀義例科
*Sectional Analysis of the Examples-and-Cases of Mahā-Cessation-and-Contemplation*
by [[從義]] (Cóngyì / Shénzhì, 排)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) navigational apparatus to [[湛然]] Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān yìlì* ([[KR6d0132]], T1913) by [[從義]] Cóngyì. Companion to Cóngyì's longer *Yìlì zuǎnyào* ([[KR6d0139]], X56n0921) main commentary; together the two works constitute Cóngyì's complete two-text apparatus on the *Zhǐguān yìlì*.

### Prefaces

The text in the X56n0922 recension carries no separate translator's preface and consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams.

### Abstract

The *Yìlì kē* provides the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Cóngyì's *Yìlì zuǎnyào*, parallel to other Sòng-period *kē* productions on major commentarial works. The dating is bracketed with the main commentary in Cóngyì's productive period c. 1062–1091.
