# Chóngbiān Tiāntái zhūwén lèijí 重編天台諸文類集
*Re-Edited Topical Anthology of Tiāntái Texts*
edited by [[如吉]] (Rújí / Sìmíng Rújí, 編)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng topical anthology by **Sìmíng Rújí** 四明如吉 of selected Tiāntái-tradition documents, organised by topic. The work is one of the earliest Sòng-period Tiāntái topical compilations and demonstrates the systematic editorial activity that characterised the Northern-Sòng *shānjiā* tradition's institutional consolidation.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Rújí's productive period in the Northern Sòng (c. 1000–1100).
