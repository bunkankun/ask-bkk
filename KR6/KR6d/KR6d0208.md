# Chánmén yàolüè 禪門要略
*Brief Outline of the Meditation-Gate*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dàshī, 出)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* compact meditation outline by Zhìyǐ 智顗, providing the most synoptic of his various meditation manuals. Companion to [[KR6d0207]].

### Abstract

The *Chánmén yàolüè* belongs to the standard Tiāntái meditation-manual corpus and provides the most accessible compact introduction to Zhìyǐ's meditative apparatus. The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
