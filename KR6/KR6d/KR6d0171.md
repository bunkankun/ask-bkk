# Sìjiào yí jízhù kē 四教儀集註科
*Sectional Analysis of the Compiled-Annotations on the Four-Doctrines Manual*
by [[蒙潤]] (Méngrùn / Yùgāng, 排定)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional analysis (*kē* 科) by **Yùgāng Méngrùn** 玉岡蒙潤 (1276–1343) on the Yuán-period combined edition of Dìguān's *Tiāntái sìjiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931) with its principal Sòng-Yuán commentaries (the *Sìjiào yí jízhù*). The work belongs to the standard Yuán-period editorial pattern of pairing major commentarial works with their structural-outline navigational apparatus.

### Prefaces

The text opens with a preface by an unidentified Yuán editor: "*The Tiāntái Sìjiào yí is in truth a key way of the doctrinal-gate. From of old to the present, annotators have been many. Some [annotations are] brief and not complete; others are broad and excessively complex. Moreover [some] cut away the body-text and only mark the beginning and end. If not for those who memorise it carefully, none can read [these editions]. Now [I] gather all the parts' texts, annotated below; would this not benefit those casually consulting?*"

The composition is bracketed within Méngrùn's productive period c. 1300–1343.
