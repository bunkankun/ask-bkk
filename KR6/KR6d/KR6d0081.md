# Fǎhuá jīng lúnguàn 法華經綸貫
*Threading-Through Notes on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Míng synoptic Lotus Sūtra commentary by Ǒuyì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), one of the *Míngmò sìdà gāosēng* 明末四大高僧 and the principal late-Míng systematiser of Tiāntái doctrine. The work is the synoptic companion to Zhìxù's longer *Fǎhuá jīng huìyì* ([[KR6d0082]], X32n0616, 7 *juan*): the *Lúnguàn* providing the unifying synoptic framework, the *Huìyì* providing the comprehensive running commentary. The genre title — *lúnguàn* 綸貫 ("threading-and-stringing") — indicates a commentary that "threads" the structural and doctrinal elements of the sūtra together into a unified interpretive framework.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0615 recension carries Zhìxù's own framing of the *lúnguàn* genre. The work belongs together with Zhìxù's *Jiéyào* on the *Xuányì* ([[KR6d0010]]) as part of his comprehensive late-Míng pedagogical apparatus on the Tiāntái Lotus tradition.

### Abstract

Zhìxù's *Lúnguàn* applies a *yītǐ* 一體 ("unified-essence") synoptic framework to the Lotus Sūtra, presenting the entire text as a single integrated doctrinal architecture rather than as a sequence of chapters with distinct themes. The work's distinctive contribution is its harmonisation of the Tiāntái *kāiquán xiǎnshí* / *fājì xiǎnběn* hermeneutic with the Pure Land *yīniàn jíshēng* 一念即生 (single-thought immediate-rebirth) doctrine, characteristic of Zhìxù's broader Tiāntái-Pure Land synthesis.

The dating is bracketed within Zhìxù's mature productive period after his entry to Mount Jiǔhuá in 1641 and before his death in 1655; the work is most plausibly placed in the period 1645–1655 when Zhìxù produced the bulk of his major Tiāntái productions (*Fǎhuá lúnguàn*, *Fǎhuá huìyì*, *Yújiā jíyào*, *Línjì zōng pài bútú*, *Yuèzàng zhījīn*).

## Other points of interest

The *lúnguàn* genre is one of Zhìxù's distinctive scholastic productions: a synoptic interpretive framework that "threads" through the entire sūtra-text, allowing the reader to grasp the work's overall doctrinal architecture before engaging with the more detailed phrase-by-phrase commentary. Zhìxù applied the *lúnguàn* method also to other Mahāyāna sūtras in his corpus, demonstrating the consistency of his late-Míng pedagogical project.
