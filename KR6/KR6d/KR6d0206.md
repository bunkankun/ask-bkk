# Suízìyì sānmèi 隨自意三昧
*The Self-Following Samādhi*
by [[慧思]] (Huìsī / Nányuè Huìsī, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Chén-period meditation manual by **Huìsī** 慧思 (515–577), the second Tiāntái patriarch, on the *suízìyì sānmèi* 隨自意三昧 ("self-following *samādhi*") — a meditative practice that Huìsī articulated alongside the *Fǎhuá sānmèi* and the *Wúzhèng sānmèi*.

### Abstract

The *Suízìyì sānmèi* belongs together with Huìsī's other major surviving works ([[KR6d0151]], [[KR6d0152]], [[KR6d0156]]) as the documentary record of his pre-Zhìyǐ Sinitic Mahāyāna meditative-doctrinal synthesis. The composition is bracketed within Huìsī's productive period at Nányuè 南嶽 c. 560–577.
