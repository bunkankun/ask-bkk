# Fǎhuá shí miào bùèr mén shìzhū zhǐ 法華十妙不二門示珠指
*Showing-the-Pearl Indicators of the Lotus's Ten Wonders and Non-Dual Gates*
by [[源清]] (Yuánqīng / Fèngxiān Yuánqīng, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* Northern-Sòng commentary by Yuánqīng 源清 (d. 1000) on Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* ([[KR6d0157]], T1927) integrated with the broader Lotus *shí miào* 十妙 ("ten wonders") doctrinal apparatus of Zhìyǐ's *Xuányì*. The work is the earliest of the substantial Sòng *Shí bùèr mén* commentaries.

### Abstract

Yuánqīng's *Shìzhū zhǐ* belongs to the very early Northern-Sòng productive period (Yuánqīng died in 1000, twenty-eight years before Zhīlǐ), making it the earliest substantial Sòng-period commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* — predating Zhīlǐ's foundational *Zhǐyào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]]) by some quarter-century. The work is consequently of substantial historiographical interest as evidence for the pre-Zhīlǐ Sòng Tiāntái scholastic tradition.

The composition is bracketed within Yuánqīng's productive period c. 980–1000.
