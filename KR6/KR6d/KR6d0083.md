# Fǎhuá jīng zhuójiě 法華經卓解
*Distinguished Interpretation of the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[徐昌治]] (Xú Chāngzhì / Jìnzhōu / Wúyī dàorén, 著)

## About the work

A seven-*juan* late-Míng / early-Qīng Lotus Sūtra commentary by the lay Buddhist scholar [[徐昌治]] Xú Chāngzhì (sobriquet **Wúyī dàorén** 無依道人, "Master Without-Refuge"). The work is preserved as X32n0617 in the *Xùzàngjīng* and represents the early-Qīng continuation of the late-Wànlì literati-Buddhist commentarial tradition.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0617 recension carries Xú Chāngzhì's own framing of the *zhuójiě* 卓解 ("distinguished interpretation") genre, which indicates a distinctive personal-individual reading rather than a school-derivative exposition.

### Abstract

Xú Chāngzhì's *Zhuójiě* belongs to the early-Qīng continuation of the late-Wànlì literati-Buddhist commentarial tradition, parallel to [[焦竑]] Jiāo Hóng's *Pínglín* ([[KR6d0078]]) and the broader late-Míng tradition of distinctive personal commentaries on the Lotus Sūtra. The work's seven-*juan* extent and continuous-running structure place it among the more substantial late-imperial Lotus commentaries.

The dating is bracketed within the early-Qīng productive period c. 1644–1700.
