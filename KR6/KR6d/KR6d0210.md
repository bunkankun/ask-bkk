# Guānxīn shífǎ 觀心食法
*Method of Contemplating-the-Mind during Eating*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dàshī, 述)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* meditation manual by Zhìyǐ 智顗 on the practice of contemplating the mind during the daily eating ritual. Belongs to the Tiāntái *guānxīn* (contemplating-the-mind) tradition's elaboration of standard monastic ritual into meditative cultivation.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
