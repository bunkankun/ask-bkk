# Tiāntáizōng wèijué (fù shìyí) 天台宗未決（附釋疑）
*Unresolved Questions of the Tiāntái School (with Interpretive Resolutions)*
questions by [[源信]] (Genshin / Yuánxìn / Eshin Sōzu, 疑問); resolutions by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ / Sìmíng Zhīlǐ, 決答)

## About the work

A six-*juan* documentary record of the Sòng-Heian Tiāntái scholastic exchange between the Japanese Tendai master **Genshin** 源信 (942–1017) and the Northern-Sòng Tiāntái patriarch **Sìmíng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 ([[知禮]], 960–1028). Genshin sent doctrinal questions on contested points of Tiāntái doctrine to China; Zhīlǐ provided the *juédá* 決答 ("decisive answers"). The work is one of the principal documents of the Heian-Sòng international Tiāntái-Tendai scholastic dialogue.

**Catalog correction:** the catalog meta lists Zhīlǐ's dynasty as 唐 (Tang); this is incorrect — Zhīlǐ was a Northern-Sòng figure (960–1028). The corrected dynasty would be 宋.

### Abstract

The composition is dated to the period of the Genshin-Zhīlǐ correspondence c. 1000–1017 (Genshin's productive period before his death).
