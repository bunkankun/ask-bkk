# Dàshèng zhǐguān fǎmén shìyào 大乘止觀法門釋要
*Essential-Explanation of the Mahāyāna Cessation-and-Contemplation Dharma-Gates*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

A four-*juan* late-Míng commentary on Huìsī's *Dàshèng zhǐguān fǎmén* ([[KR6d0152]], T1924) by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), one of the *Míngmò sìdà gāosēng* 明末四大高僧. The work is the principal late-Míng commentary on Huìsī's foundational meditation manual, complementing Liǎorán's Sòng *Zōngyuán jì* ([[KR6d0153]]).

### Prefaces

The text in the X55n0905 recension carries the standard front matter and proceeds with the running commentary.

### Abstract

Zhìxù's *Shìyào* belongs to his late-Míng systematic Tiāntái pedagogical project, alongside his commentaries on the Lotus Sūtra ([[KR6d0010]], [[KR6d0081]], [[KR6d0082]]) and his other Tiāntái scholastic productions. The work integrates Huìsī's foundational meditation manual into Zhìxù's late-Míng Tiāntái-Pure Land synthetic apparatus, providing the standard late-Míng-and-Qīng accessible commentary on the work.

The composition is bracketed within Zhìxù's mature productive period c. 1641–1655.
