# Jīnguāngmíng chànfǎ bǔzhù yí 金光明懺法補助儀
*Auxiliary Apparatus for the Suvarṇaprabhāsa Repentance Liturgy*
compiled by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 集)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng auxiliary liturgical apparatus by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032) — the Tiāntái-Pure Land synthesis master of Háng-zhōu Tiānzhú-sì and the most prolific compiler of repentance liturgies in Sòng Buddhism — for the *Jīn-guāng-míng* (Suvarṇaprabhāsa-sūtra) repentance practice. Body attribution: *Sòng Tiāntái Dōngyè-shān shāmén Zūnshì jí* 宋天台東掖山沙門遵式集.

### Abstract

Zūnshì's *Bǔ-zhù yí* belongs to his comprehensive late-Northern-Sòng liturgical apparatus, alongside his other major repentance compilations (the *Wǎngshēng jìng-tǔ chàn-yuàn yí* 往生淨土懺願儀 T1984 and the *Pǔ-mén pǐn chóng-sòng* [[KR6d0056]]). Through Zūnshì's productive activity, the *Jīn-guāng-míng* (Suvarṇaprabhāsa) devotional cult became one of the standard institutional liturgies of Sòng Tiāntái-Pure Land devotional practice.
