# Sìjiào yí jízhù jiéyì 四教儀集註節義
*Selected-Meanings of the Compiled-Annotations on the Four-Doctrines Manual*
by [[靈耀]] (Língyào / Língyào Quánzhāng, 節)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Qing-period selective-extract (節 *jié*) commentary by **Língyào Quánzhāng** 靈耀全彰 on Méngrùn's *Sìjiào yí jízhù* — providing accessible Qing-period extracts of the Yuán combined-edition apparatus. Companion to Língyào's other Tiāntái productions ([[KR6d0012]], [[KR6d0057]], [[KR6d0142]]).

### Prefaces

The text opens with a *Sìjiào jízhù jiéyì bìng kē xù* 四教集註節義并科序 (preface to the joint *jiéyì* and *kē*).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Língyào's productive period c. 1644–1796 (early-to-mid Qīng era).
