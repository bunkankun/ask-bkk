# Lǐ Fǎhuájīng yíshì 禮法華經儀式
*Liturgical Procedure for Worshipping the Lotus Sūtra*
Anonymous (Táng to Northern Sòng).

## About the work

A single-*juan* anonymous liturgical procedure (*yíshì* 儀式) for the worship and recitation of the Lotus Sūtra, belonging to the standard Tiāntái Lotus liturgical apparatus alongside [[KR6d0191]]–[[KR6d0193]]. The work provides ritual instruction for the lay-and-monastic recitation-and-veneration practice of the Lotus Sūtra.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed loosely within the post-Zhànrán productive period for *Fǎhuá*-related liturgical material (c. 750–1100).
