# Pǔxián guān jīng yìshū kē 觀普賢菩薩行法經義疏科
*Sectional Analysis of the Pǔxián Contemplation Sūtra Subcommentary*
Anonymous (Sòng to Yuán).

## About the work

A single-*juan* anonymous sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) navigational apparatus to the *Pǔxián guān jīng yìshū* ([[KR6d0123]], X35n0650) — Shénzhào [[本如]] Běnrú's Sòng-period subcommentary on the *Pǔxián guān jīng* ([[KR6d0120]], T277).

### Prefaces

The text in the X35n0649 recension carries no separate translator's preface and consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kēpàn* sectional division of the *Yìshū* main commentary.

### Abstract

The *Yìshū kē* belongs together with the broader Sòng-Yuán-Míng tradition of providing structural-outline navigational apparatus for major commentarial works. Its preservation in the canonical apparatus alongside Běnrú's *Yìshū* ([[KR6d0123]]) demonstrates the editorial pattern of pairing major commentarial productions with their navigational-apparatus volumes.

The dating is bracketed within the Sòng-Yuán productive period for navigational apparatus on Běnrú's commentary (c. 1000–1300).
