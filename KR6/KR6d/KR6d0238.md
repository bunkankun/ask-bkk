# Bùkě chànà wú cǐjūn 不可剎那無此君
*[One] Cannot for a Moment Be without This Lord*
by [[義銛]] (Yìxiān / Pǔwēng Yìxiān, 述)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* Sòng-period treatise by **Pǔwēng Yìxiān** 朴翁義銛. The title is drawn from the famous bamboo-loving anecdote of [[王徽之]] Wáng Huīzhī (Eastern Jìn): "*One cannot for a moment be without this lord*" (referring to bamboo, *cǐjūn* 此君 = "this lord"). The work uses this literary metaphor as the framing for a Tiāntái doctrinal-meditative discussion.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Yìxiān's productive period in the Sòng (c. 1100–1250).
