# Luóxī zhènzǔ jí 螺溪振祖集
*Collection of the Luóxī Patriarchal-Promulgation*
edited by [[元悟]] (Yuánwù / Luóxī Yuánwù, 編)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng compilation by **Luóxī Yuánwù** 螺溪元悟 of materials documenting the Tiāntáishān **Luóxī Chuánjiàoyuàn** 螺溪傳教院 patriarchal lineage — the same institutional context at which Goryeo [[諦觀]] Dìguān had transmitted the recovered Tiāntái texts back to China. Companion to Zōngxiǎo's parallel *Bǎoyún zhènzǔ jí* ([[KR6d0222]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Yuánwù's productive period in the Southern Sòng (c. 1150–1250).
