# Jiàoguān cuōyào lùn 教觀撮要論
*Treatise Capturing-the-Essentials of Doctrine-and-Contemplation*
Anonymous (Sòng).

## About the work

A two-*juan* anonymous Sòng-period treatise on the Tiāntái *jiàoguān* 教觀 (doctrine-and-contemplation) tradition. The work belongs to the broader Sòng *shānjiā* doctrinal anthology tradition.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed loosely within the Sòng productive period (960–1279).
