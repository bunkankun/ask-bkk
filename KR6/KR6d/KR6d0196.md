# Jīnguāngmíng zuìshèng chànyí 金光明最勝懺儀
*Suvarṇaprabhāsa-uttama Repentance Liturgy*
compiled by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ / Sìmíng Zhīlǐ / Fǎzhì dàshī, 集)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng repentance liturgy by **Sì-míng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 (960–1028) for the *Jīn-guāng-míng zuì-shèng wáng jīng* 金光明最勝王經 ([[義淨]] Yìjìng's Tang translation of the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa-sūtra*). The work is parallel to [[遵式]] Zūnshì's *Bǔ-zhù yí* ([[KR6d0195]]) and demonstrates Zhīlǐ's substantial contribution to the Sòng Tiāntái liturgical tradition alongside his polemical-doctrinal output.

### Abstract

The *Zuì-shèng chàn-yí* belongs to Zhīlǐ's broader liturgical productive corpus and represents the *shān-jiā* tradition's contribution to the Sòng *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* devotional liturgy. The composition is bracketed within Zhīlǐ's mature productive period at the Yán-qìng-sì 延慶寺 in Sì-míng, c. 996–1028.
