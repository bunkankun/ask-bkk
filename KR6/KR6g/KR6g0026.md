# Dà bēi jīng 大悲經
*The Sūtra of Great Compassion (Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka)*
by [[那連提耶舍]] (Narendrayaśas, 譯)

## About the work

This 5-fascicle text by [[那連提耶舍]] Narendrayaśas is the *Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka-sūtra* — one of the major Mahāyāna sūtras of *karuṇā* (compassion) doctrine. Distinct from but doctrinally related to the *Mahā-karuṇā-puṇḍarīka* tradition.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Narendrayaśas's Northern-Qí period (556–568 CE).
