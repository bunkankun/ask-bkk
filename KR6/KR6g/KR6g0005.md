# Dà bān niè pán jīng xuán yì 大般涅槃經玄義
*The Profound Meaning of the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 撰)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle commentary on *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]) is by [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng (561–632), the principal disciple of [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ and the second patriarch of the Tiāntái school. The *xuán yì* "profound meaning" format provides a doctrinal preamble to the larger sūtra, in the standard Tiāntái scholastic genre.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (597–632) reflects Guàndǐng's mature Tiāntái scholarly activity, beginning with Zhìyǐ's death.
