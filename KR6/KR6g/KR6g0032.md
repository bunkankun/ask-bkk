# Lián huā miàn jīng 蓮華面經
*The Sūtra of the Lotus-Face*
by [[那連提耶舍]] (Narendrayaśas, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[那連提耶舍]] Narendrayaśas treats the bodhisattva *Liánhuāmiàn* 蓮華面 (Lotus-Face) and his interactions with the Buddha at the time of the *parinirvāṇa*. The text contains substantial *mò fǎ* (decline-of-Dharma) narrative material.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Narendrayaśas's late-life Suí period (582–589 CE).
