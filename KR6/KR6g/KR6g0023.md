# Dà bān niè pán jīng hòu fēn 大般涅槃經後分
*The Latter Section of the Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[若那跋陀羅]] (Jñānabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[若那跋陀羅]] Jñānabhadra is the *latter section* of the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* — additional Indic material continuing the narrative of the Buddha's last discourses beyond what is contained in Dharmakṣema's *Northern Recension* (*Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 [[KR6g0001]], T0374). The text provides further material on the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* and the disposition of his relics.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Jñānabhadra's Táng activity (660–700 CE).
