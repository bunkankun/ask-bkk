# Niè pán jīng yì jì 涅槃經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng-preserved text is a commentary on *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]). Preserved in Taishō volume 85 (the *Dūnhuáng yíshū*).

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500–900) reflects the broad pre-Táng to Táng period of Dūnhuáng-fragment composition.
