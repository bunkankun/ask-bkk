# Niè pán lùn 涅槃論
*Treatise on Nirvāṇa*
by [[天親菩薩]] (Vasubandhu, 造) and [[達磨菩提]] (Dharmabodhi, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the *Nirvāṇa-śāstra* attributed to [[天親菩薩]] Vasubandhu, translated by [[達磨菩提]] Dharmabodhi during his Northern-Wèi activity. The work is one of the principal Indian *śāstra*-commentaries on the Mahāyāna *parinirvāṇa* doctrine.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Dharmabodhi's Northern-Wèi period (508–535 CE).
