# Fó shuō fāng děng bān ní huán jīng 佛說方等般泥洹經
*The Vaipulya Sūtra of the Buddha's Parinirvāṇa*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle proto-Mahāyāna *Niè-pán* text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù is one of the earliest Chinese versions of the *Pre-Niè-pán* / *Mahāyāna Mahāparinirvāṇa* tradition.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266–313 CE).
