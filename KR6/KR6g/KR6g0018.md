# Niè pán jīng shū sī jì 涅槃經疏私記 (Dàoxiān)
*Private Notes on the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[道暹]] (Dàoxiān, 述)

## About the work

This 9-fascicle Táng Tiāntái-school sub-commentary on [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Dà bān niè pán jīng shū* 大般涅槃經疏 ([[KR6g0007]]) is by [[道暹]] Dàoxiān, paralleling Xíngmǎn's parallel *Sī jì* ([[KR6g0017]], X660). Together the two works document the mid-Táng Tiāntái-school *Nièpán* commentarial tradition.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (766–800) reflects Dàoxiān's mid-Táng activity per the *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn*.
