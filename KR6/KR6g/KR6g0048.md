# Yí jiào jīng jiě 遺教經解
*Explication of the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra*
by [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the *Yí jiào jīng* 遺教經 ([[KR6g0043]], T0389) is by [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù (1599–1655), one of the *Four Great Masters of the Late Míng*.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1620–1655) reflects Ǒuyì Zhìxù's mature scholarly period.
