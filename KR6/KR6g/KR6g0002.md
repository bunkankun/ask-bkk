# Dà bān niè pán jīng yì jì 大般涅槃經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[淨影慧遠]] Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn (述)

## About the work

This 10-fascicle commentary on *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]) is by [[淨影慧遠]] Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn (523–592). One of the most substantial Suí-period commentaries on the *Niè-pán jīng* and a foundational document of mature Chinese Buddha-nature doctrine.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580–592) reflects Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn's mature Suí period.
