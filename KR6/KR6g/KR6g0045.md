# Fó yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào 佛遺教經論疏節要
*Essential Abridgement of the Commentary on the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra Treatise*
by [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 節要) and [[袾宏]] (Zhūhóng, 補註)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle composite work combines [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's Northern-Sòng abridgement (*jié yào* 節要) of the *Yí jiào jīng lùn* 遺教經論 ([[KR6g0044]]) commentarial tradition with [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng's late-Míng supplementary annotation (*bǔ zhù* 補註).

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067–1615) reflects the period from Jìngyuán's Northern-Sòng activity to Zhūhóng's late-Míng supplementary work.
