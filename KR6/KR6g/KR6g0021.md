# Niè pán jīng huì shū jiě 涅槃經會疏解
*Explication of the Combined Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[師正]] Shīzhèng (分科) and [[圓澄]] Yuánchéng (會疏)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle Yuán-period composite work combines [[師正]] Shīzhèng's sectional outline with [[圓澄]] Yuánchéng's *huì shū* commentary on *Niè pán jīng huì shū* 涅槃經會疏 ([[KR6g0016]]). The "圓澄" here is most likely the late-Míng / Qīng Yúnmén-school Chán master *Hānshān Yuánchéng* 雲門圓澄 (1561–1626) or his Japanese namesake; the precise identification is uncertain.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1280–1368) reflects the Yuán dynasty period.
