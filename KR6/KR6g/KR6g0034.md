# Dà yún wú xiǎng jīng juàn dì jiǔ 大雲無想經卷第九
*The Mahāmegha No-Conceptions Sūtra, Fascicle Nine*
by [[竺佛念]] (Zhú Fóniàn, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text preserves the ninth fascicle of a longer (possibly originally 9- or 10-fascicle) Yáo-Qín *Dà yún* tradition translation by [[竺佛念]] Zhú Fóniàn, parallel to but distinct from [[曇無讖]] Dharmakṣema's *Dà fāng děng wú xiǎng jīng* 大方等無想經 ([[KR6g0033]], T0387). Only juan 9 survives.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Zhú Fóniàn's Yáo-Qín period (365–411 CE).
