# Niè pán jīng zhì dìng shū kē 涅槃經治定疏科
*Editorially-Established Sectional Outline of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra Commentary*
by [[智圓]] (Zhìyuán, 撰)

## About the work

This 10-fascicle text by [[智圓]] Zhìyuán is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) for [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Dà bān niè pán jīng shū* 大般涅槃經疏 ([[KR6g0007]], T1767). At 10 fascicles, the *kē* itself is one of the largest in the East Asian Buddhist tradition, reflecting the parent commentary's substantial scope.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (990–1022) reflects Zhìyuán's mature scholarly period.
