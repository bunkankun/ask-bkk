# Dà bān niè pán jīng 大般涅槃經 (Southern Recension)
*The Sūtra of the Great Parinirvāṇa* — the *Southern Recension* (南本 *Nán-běn*)
by [[慧嚴]] (Huìyán *et al.*, 等依泥洹經加之)

## About the work

The *Dà bān niè pán jīng* in 36 fascicles — the *Southern Recension* (*Nán-běn* 南本) — is the Liú-Sòng editorial revision of [[曇無讖]] Dharmakṣema's *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]], Northern Recension, T0374, 40 fasc.). Re-edited by [[慧嚴]] Huìyán and [[慧觀]] Huìguān in collaboration with the Liú-Sòng-court Buddhist establishment c. 433–443 CE, the *Southern Recension* combines Dharmakṣema's text with the earlier *Pre-Mahāparinirvāṇa* (*Níyuán jīng* 泥洹經, *Fó shuō dà bān ní huán jīng* 佛說大般泥洹經 [[KR6g0022]]) materials translated by [[法顯]] Fǎxiǎn and [[佛陀跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra, smoothing the text for southern Chinese readers and re-arranging the chapter divisions. The *Southern Recension* and the *Northern Recension* together constitute the canonical East Asian Mahāyāna *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* corpus.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (433–443) reflects the Liú-Sòng editorial period of Huìyán's collaboration with Huìguān.

The Taishō text (T0375) is established on a particularly rich apparatus including the *Liúbù běn* (Disseminated Edition), *Fú* 福 / *Fúyǐ* 福乙 (Fúzhōu) witnesses.

## Other points of interest

- The Northern / Southern recension distinction is one of the foundational data-points for Liú-Sòng-period Buddhist intellectual history; the two recensions co-existed and were studied together in subsequent East Asian Buddhist tradition, with the *Southern Recension* generally preferred in southern monastic establishments and the *Northern Recension* in northern ones.
