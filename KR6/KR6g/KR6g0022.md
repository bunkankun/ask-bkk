# Fó shuō dà bān ní huán jīng 佛說大般泥洹經
*The Sūtra of the Great Parinirvāṇa* (the *Pre-Niè-pán* recension)
by [[法顯]] (Fǎxiǎn, 譯)

## About the work

This 6-fascicle text is the *short* / *pre-Niè-pán* (*Ní huán* 泥洹) recension of the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra*, brought back from India by [[法顯]] Fǎxiǎn (337–422) on his great pilgrimage and translated into Chinese in collaboration with [[佛陀跋陀羅]] Buddhabhadra at the Dàochǎngsì 道場寺 in Jiànkāng. This shorter recension predated the full Dharmakṣema *Northern Recension* (*Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 [[KR6g0001]], T0374, 40 fasc.) by some years and was the basis for the *Pre-Niè-pán*-school doctrinal controversies of [[竺道生]] Dàoshēng's generation.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (416–418) reflects Fǎxiǎn's translation activity at the Dàochǎngsì.

The Taishō text (T0376) is established on a particularly rich apparatus.
