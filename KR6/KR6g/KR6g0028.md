# Jí yī qiè fú dé sānmèi jīng 集一切福德三昧經
*The Sūtra on the Samādhi of Gathering All Merit (Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi)*
by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text by [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva is the second Chinese version of the *Sarvapuṇya-samuccaya-samādhi-sūtra*, parallel to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's earlier *Děng jí zhòng dé sānmèi jīng* 等集眾德三昧經 ([[KR6g0027]], T0381).

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān period (402–412 CE).
