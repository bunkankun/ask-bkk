# Niè pán zōng yào 涅槃宗要
*The Essential Doctrinal Lineage of the Nirvāṇa [Sūtra]*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle compendium by [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo treats the doctrinal essence (*zōng yào*) of *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]) — paralleling Wǒnhyo's *Liǎng juàn wú liàng shòu jīng zōng yào* 兩卷無量壽經宗要 ([[KR6f0067]], T1747) and other *zōng yào* compendia.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (660–686) reflects Wǒnhyo's mature Silla period.
