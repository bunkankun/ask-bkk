# Niè pán jīng huì shū 涅槃經會疏
*Combined Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 撰), [[湛然]] (Zhànrán, 再治), [[本純]] (Honjun, 分會)

## About the work

This 36-fascicle composite work combines [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Dà bān niè pán jīng shū* 大般涅槃經疏 ([[KR6g0007]]) with [[湛然]] Zhànrán's Táng-period editorial revision (*zài zhì* 再治) and the Japanese editorial *fēn huì* of [[本純]] Honjun. The 36-fascicle scope matches the *Southern Recension* of the parent sūtra ([[KR6g0003]]) and provides line-by-line interlinear commentary.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (597–1700) reflects the maximum window from Guàndǐng's mature period to the Edo-Japanese editorial work.
