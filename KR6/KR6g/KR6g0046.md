# Fó yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào 佛遺教經論疏節要 (Yongle Northern Canon ed.)
*Essential Abridgement of the Commentary on the Bequeathed Teaching Sūtra Treatise* — Yǒnglè Northern-Canon edition
by [[淨源]] (Jìngyuán, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the Northern-Yǒnglè-canon edition of [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's *Fó yí jiào jīng lùn shū jié yào* 佛遺教經論疏節要, paralleling the Taishō version ([[KR6g0045]], T1820) but with editorial differences. Both versions document the Northern-Sòng-onwards commentarial tradition on the *Yí jiào jīng*.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067–1088) reflects Jìngyuán's mature scholarly activity. The work is preserved in the Northern Yǒnglè edition of the Buddhist canon (B1590).
