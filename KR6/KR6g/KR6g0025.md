# Sì tóng zǐ sānmèi jīng 四童子三昧經
*The Sūtra of the Samādhi of the Four Boys*
by [[闍那崛多]] (Jñānagupta, 譯)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text by [[闍那崛多]] Jñānagupta treats the *Sì tóng zǐ sānmèi* — the meditative *samādhi* of the four bodhisattva-boys — and is one of the major Suí-period Mahāyāna translations.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Jñānagupta's Suí-imperial-bureau activity (587–600 CE).
