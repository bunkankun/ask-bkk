# Mó hē mó yē jīng 摩訶摩耶經
*The Mahāmāyā Sūtra*
by [[曇景]] (Tánjǐng, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[曇景]] Tánjǐng is the *Mahāmāyā-sūtra* — the Mahāyāna sūtra concerning the Buddha's mother *Mahāmāyā* 摩訶摩耶, narrating the events surrounding the Buddha's parinirvāṇa from Mahāmāyā's perspective in the Trāyastriṃśa heaven and including a famous *prophecy of the decline of the Dharma* (the standard *mò fǎ* 末法 doctrine).

### Abstract

The translation belongs to Tánjǐng's Xiāo-Qí period (479–502 CE).
