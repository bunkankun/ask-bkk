# Dà bān niè pán jīng shū 大般涅槃經疏
*Commentary on the Great Parinirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 撰)

## About the work

The *Niè-pán jīng shū* in 33 fascicles is the great Suí-period Tiāntái commentary on *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]) by [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng. Together with *Dà bān niè pán jīng xuán yì* 大般涅槃經玄義 ([[KR6g0005]], T1765), it constitutes the canonical Tiāntái-school doctrinal apparatus on the *Niè-pán jīng*. The 33-fascicle scope makes it one of the most substantial single Tiāntái-school commentaries.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (597–632) reflects Guàndǐng's mature Tiāntái scholarly activity.
