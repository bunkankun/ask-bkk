# Niè pán jīng shū sān dé zhǐ guī 涅槃經疏三德指歸
*The Three-Virtue Direction-Pointing of the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[智圓]] (Zhìyuán, 述)

## About the work

This 19-fascicle Northern-Sòng sub-commentary on [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Dà bān niè pán jīng shū* 大般涅槃經疏 ([[KR6g0007]]) is by [[智圓]] Zhìyuán, the *Shānwài* Tiāntái-school master. The "three virtues" (*sān dé* 三德) of the title refers to the *Nièpán jīng*'s threefold doctrine of *fǎ shēn* / *bān ruò* / *jiě tuō* (Dharma-body, wisdom, liberation).

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (990–1022) reflects Zhìyuán's mature scholarly activity. The 19-fascicle scope makes this one of the largest Sòng-period sub-commentaries.
