# Niè pán jīng shū sī jì 涅槃經疏私記 (Xíngmǎn)
*Private Notes on the Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra*
by [[行滿]] (Xíngmǎn, 集)

## About the work

This 12-fascicle Táng Tiāntái-school sub-commentary on [[灌頂]] Guàndǐng's *Dà bān niè pán jīng shū* 大般涅槃經疏 ([[KR6g0007]], T1767) is by [[行滿]] Xíngmǎn. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X660).

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (766–800) reflects Xíngmǎn's mid-Táng activity. The work parallels Dàoxiān's *Sī jì* ([[KR6g0018]], X661).
