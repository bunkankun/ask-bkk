# Niè pán jīng shū 涅槃經疏
*Commentary on the Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng-preserved text is yet another fragmentary commentary on *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]), distinct from T2764A ([[KR6g0010]]) and T2764B ([[KR6g0011]]).

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500–900) reflects the broad Dūnhuáng-fragment period.
