# Niè pán jīng huì shū tiáo gě 涅槃經會疏條箇
*Combined-Commentary Item-Outline of the Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (anonymous)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 3-fascicle anonymous text is a *huì shū tiáo gě* — a *combined-commentary item-by-item outline* — of *Dà bān niè pán jīng* 大般涅槃經 ([[KR6g0001]]) commentaries. The format provides item-by-item navigation through the *huì běn* (combined edition) of the *Niè-pán* commentaries.

### Abstract

The work is anonymous and undated; conventionally placed in the broad late-Táng to Qīng period of *huì běn* publishing activity.
