# Ruíxīyē jīng 蕤呬耶經
*Sūtra of the Vehicle* (Skt. *Guhya-tantra*? — title transliteration uncertain)
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Esoteric scripture translated by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) — the title *Ruíxīyē* 蕤呬耶 transliterates a Sanskrit term whose precise identification has been debated; suggestions include *Guhya* (esoteric / secret) or a related Sanskrit term meaning "vehicle" or "carrier". The text is one of Amoghavajra's substantial Esoteric translations and is preserved in the standard Tang Esoteric corpus.

### Abstract

The text presents an Esoteric ritual-scriptural framework consistent with the Tang Esoteric *kriyā* / *caryā*-tantra horizon. The three fascicles cover the doctrinal framework, the *abhiṣeka* sequences, and the *sādhana* prescriptions for the principal deities of the text's mandalic system.

The translation dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774).
