*Great Dhāraṇī of the Great-Buddha-Crown* (anonymous parallel)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous parallel recension of the Buddha-crown *dhāraṇī* — companion to [[KR6j0116]] (Amoghavajra's *Sitātapatra-dhāraṇī* T944A). Preserved in the Taishō under T944B, it gives an alternative recension of the same Sitātapatra material.

### Abstract

The text is an anonymous parallel rendering of the Sitātapatra-dhāraṇī tradition — likely a manuscript-tradition variant of the same long *dhāraṇī* that travels under T944A (Amoghavajra) and other Táng/Sòng versions. Its preservation in the Taishō reflects the multiple parallel text-witnesses to this most-popular *dhāraṇī*.
