# Fóshuō liùzì shénzhòuwáng jīng 佛說六字神呪王經
*Sūtra of the Divine Six-Syllable Spell-King (Recension A)*
[anonymous translator, attributed to Eastern Jìn]

## About the work

The Recension A (Korean canon line) of an anonymous Eastern-Jìn six-syllable spell-text. The pair T1045A and T1045B (Recension A and B = [[KR6j0244]]) preserve two parallel transmissions of the same underlying *Liùzì shénzhòuwáng* text. For the broader *Liùzì* tradition see [[KR6j0242]].

### Abstract

This text is closely related to T1044 ([[KR6j0242]]) and T1046 ([[KR6j0245]]), all anonymous Eastern-Jìn translations of variants of the *ṣaḍ-akṣarī-vidyā* (six-syllable spell) tradition. The Recension A line preserves the Korean-canon (高麗藏) text, while Recension B ([[KR6j0244]]) preserves a parallel Sòng-canon transmission. The variants between A and B are concentrated in the dhāraṇī orthography and minor narrative-frame details. For the broader textual and doctrinal context see [[KR6j0242]].
