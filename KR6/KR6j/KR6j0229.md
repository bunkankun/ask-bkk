# Shèng Guānzìzài púsà xīn zhēnyán yújiā guānxíng yíguǐ 聖觀自在菩薩心真言瑜伽觀行儀軌
*Yoga-Contemplation Ritual Manual for the Heart-Mantra of Noble Avalokiteśvara*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) supplying the *yújiā guānxíng yíguǐ* (yoga-contemplation ritual procedure) for the heart-mantra of *Ārya-Avalokiteśvara* (聖觀自在 = Skt. *Ārya-Avalokiteśvara*, the *non-adorned* form of Avalokiteśvara — distinguished from the *Sahasra-bhuja-Avalokiteśvara* and other proliferated forms). CANWWW alt-titles: *Shèng Guānzìzài yíguǐ* 聖觀自在儀軌, *Yújiā guānxíng guǐyí* 瑜伽觀行軌儀.

### Abstract

The *Ārya-Avalokiteśvara* heart-mantra (*hṛdaya-mantra*) is one of the simplest and most-recited Avalokiteśvara mantras (typically in the form *oṃ aroḷika svāhā*) and the present text provides its full Esoteric ritual context: *mudrā*-sequence, *maṇḍala*-construction, *guānxíng* (visualisation) procedures, and the doctrinal frame within which the practice is to be undertaken. Together with [[KR6j0228]], [[KR6j0230]], and [[KR6j0231]] it forms the core of Amoghavajra's Avalokiteśvara liturgical corpus.
