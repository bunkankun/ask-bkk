# Lèngyán jīng zhèngjiàn 楞嚴經正見
*"Right View" Commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[濟時]] (述)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle (10卷) **Línjì-school commentary** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]) by **Xìngfú Huìgōng Jìshí** 興福會公濟時 [[濟時]], abbot of the *Gǔ Xìngfú Chánsì* 古興福禪寺 at Lóudōng 婁東 (Tàicāng, Jiāngsū). The author identifies himself in the colophon as *Zhù Lóudōng Gǔ Xìngfú Chánsì shāmén Jìshí shù* 住婁東古興福禪寺沙門濟時述. Preserved as **X16 no. 317** in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The title — *zhèngjiàn* 正見, "right view" — frames the work as a corrective to the proliferating sectarian *Lèngyán* commentarial divisions.

### Prefaces

A preface by **[[盛符升]] Shèng Fúshēng** 盛符升 (a Kūnshān co-disciple, then 89 *suì*, dated **Kāngxī 38 *jǐmǎo* 己卯 winter [1699/early-1700]**) records the genesis of the work. In *wùyín* 戊寅 spring (Kāngxī 37 = 1698), Jìshí completed his earlier *Jīngāng jīng tíyào* 金剛提要 [[金剛提要]] (Vajracchedikā compendium); soon thereafter he produced the present *Lèngyán zhèngjiàn*. Shèng Fúshēng provides a substantial historiographical introduction to the *Lèngyán* commentarial tradition: from Chóngfú **Quègōng** 崇福慤公 [[慤公]] (the first commentary, traced from the original *fángxiāng* — [[房融]] Fáng Róng's — translation); through Guǎntáo **Zhèngōng** 振公 [[振公]] (eight-section / seven-topic structural division); through Zīzhōng **Yuángōng** 資中沅公 [[沅公]] (the source of Tiāntái-school *sānfǎ sānguān* method, continued by **Gūshān Yuángōng** 孤山圓公 [[圓公]]); through Chángqìng **Yǎngōng** 長慶巘公 [[巘公]] (origin of the Chán-school commentary tradition); to Sòng-period **Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán** 長水璿公 [[子璿]] (synthesis of Xiánshǒu and Tiāntái); and on through the Sòng-Yuán "differentiation" of the post-Sòng commentarial schools. Shèng Fúshēng concludes that Jìshí's work, though firmly Línjì-school in lineage, recovers the pre-Sòng synthetic spirit.

### Abstract

The *Zhèngjiàn* is the principal **Línjì-school commentary** of the late seventeenth century, complementing [[元賢]]'s Cáodòng-school *Lüèshū* ([[KR6j0710]]), the Tiāntái-school *Dìngjiě* ([[KR6j0714]]), the Huáyán-school *Bǎojìng shū* ([[KR6j0724]]) and *Zhǐzhǎng shū* ([[KR6j0716]]). It is described as the product of three years' "withered sitting" (*kūzuò sānnián* 枯坐三年) followed by a single night's awakening, after which Jìshí "fused them together within the single mind / threefold contemplation" (*rónghuì yú yīxīn sānguān* 融會於一心三觀). Composition: 1698–1699.
