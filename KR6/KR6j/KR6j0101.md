# Jìngliúli jìngtǔ biāo 淨瑠璃淨土摽
*Iconographic Diagram of the Pure Lapis-Lazuli Pure Land* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous text on the **iconographic diagram** (*biāo* 摽 = *biāozhì* 標幟, "marker / sign") of the **Pure Lapis-Lazuli Pure Land** (*Jìngliúli jìngtǔ* 淨瑠璃淨土 = *Vaiḍūryanirbhāsa-buddhakṣetra*) — the eastern Pure Land presided over by Bhaiṣajyaguru. The text describes the iconographic-mandalic representation of the Pure Land for use in visualisation practice.

### Abstract

The text provides the iconographic-mandalic specifications for the Pure Lapis-Lazuli Pure Land: its central deity (Bhaiṣajyaguru with his retinue of two attendant Bodhisattvas, *Sūryaprabha* 日光 and *Candraprabha* 月光), the architectural features (the pavilions, gardens, jewel-trees), the cosmological setting, and the visualisation prescriptions. Anonymous transmission; conventionally dated to the late Táng period (8th–10th c.).
