# Zhū fó xīnyìn tuóluóní jīng 諸佛心印陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Heart-Seal Dhāraṇī of All Buddhas*
by [[法天]] (Fǎtiān, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric *dhāraṇī* scripture translated by **Fǎtiān** ([[法天]]). Companion to [[KR6j0089]] (Xuánzàng's *Zhū fó xīn tuóluóní jīng* T918) — the title's *xīnyìn* 心印 ("heart-seal") expands the earlier "heart-dhāraṇī" with the additional *yìn* (seal / *mudrā*) component.

### Abstract

The text is the Sòng-period parallel to T918, presenting a related but distinct *dhāraṇī* scripture in the same conceptual family. The translation dates from Fǎtiān's career at the Sòng Yìjīngyuàn (980–1001).
