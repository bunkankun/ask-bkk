# Dōubù tuóluóní mù 都部陀羅尼目
*General Index of Dhāraṇīs by Family*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle systematic catalogue of *dhāraṇīs* organised by **family** (*bù* 部 = the five Esoteric families: Buddha, Vajra, Lotus, Jewel, Karma) by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). The text is a working reference for the Esoteric *ācāryā* who needed to find *dhāraṇīs* by deity-family-affiliation.

### Abstract

The *Dōubù tuóluóní mù* organises the principal *dhāraṇīs* of the Tang Esoteric corpus by family — Buddha-family (with Mahāvairocana and the cosmic Buddhas), Vajra-family (with Vajrapāṇi and the Vajra-bodhisattvas), Lotus-family (with Avalokiteśvara and the Lotus deities), Jewel-family (with the Ratna-deities), and Karma-family (with the Karma deities). For each family, the text gives a sequential listing of the principal *dhāraṇīs* with brief identifying notes.

The text is one of Amoghavajra's working scholarly reference compositions; together with [[KR6j0073]] (the theoretical *Zǒngshì tuóluóní yì zàn*), it gives the Tang Esoteric tradition's twin theoretical-and-practical framework for *dhāraṇī* practice.

The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774).
