# Lèngyán jīng shìzhì yuántōng zhāng jiě 楞嚴經勢至圓通章解
*Commentary on the "Mahāsthāmaprāpta Universal-Penetration Chapter" of the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[行策]] (撰)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) commentary on the **Mahāsthāmaprāpta Universal-Penetration Chapter** (*Shìzhì yuántōng zhāng*) of the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]) by **Jiéliú Xíngcè** 截流行策 [[行策]] (1628–1682), traditionally counted as the **tenth Patriarch of the Chinese Pure Land lineage**. Preserved as **X16 no. 312** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Prefaces

The work opens with a substantial preface by Xíngcè himself. He situates the *Lèngyán* historiographically: composed and translated *after* the *Lotus*, the sūtra was unknown to [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ in his day — had Zhìyǐ seen it, he would have placed it in the *tíhú* 醍醐 (consummate) doctrinal tier without question. After the great Bodhisattva-commentators are gone, however, "the various commentary-masters have raised conflicting views" (*gǔjīn shūzhǔ gè xìng yìjiàn* 古今疏主各興異見) and the readers are at a loss. Some have wished to "abandon all commentaries and read only the bare sūtra" — but this is an excessive reaction. Xíngcè proposes a middle path: among the existing commentaries, the [[西湖鑑一疏]] *Xīhú Jiànyī shū* 西湖鑑一疏 (presumably [[鑑一元盤]] Jiànyī Yuánpán 鑑一元盤's *Lèngyán jīng zhèngjiàn* or similar — possibly [[KR6j0703]] or related material) most successfully "abandons self and follows the sūtra"; Xíngcè's own commentary follows that model.

### Abstract

The work belongs to the cluster of late-Míng / early-Qīng *Mahāsthāmaprāpta-chapter* commentaries — including [[KR6j0718]] (Zhèngxiàng) and [[KR6j0719]] (Xùfǎ) — that extracted the chapter from its *Lèngyán* setting and presented it as the principal Mahāyāna scripture for **Chán–Pure Land syncretism**. Xíngcè's reading places the *niánfó* gateway alongside the *zhǐguān* 止觀 of [[馬鳴]] Mǎmíng (Aśvaghoṣa) as one of the two *equal pillars* of Buddhist practice — a programmatic statement of his own institutional career, which led from intensive Línjì Chán practice through the Liánfū ān 蓮柎庵 founding (1663) to the Liánshè 蓮社 revival at Yùshān Pǔrén yuàn 虞山普仁院 (1670). Composition window: 1663 (his first major Pure Land establishment) – 1682 (his death).

## Other points of interest

The *Shìzhì zhāng jiě* is one of the canonical sources for the orthodox Pure Land lineage's recognition of Xíngcè as **tenth Patriarch**: the work sits programmatically alongside his [[勸發真信文]] *Quàn fā zhēn xìn wén* 勸發真信文 and *Qǐ yīxīn jīngjìn niànfó qī qī guīshì* 起一心精進念佛七期規式 ([[KR6p0094]]) as the doctrinal-practical scriptural ground for early-Qīng Pure Land institutionalization.
