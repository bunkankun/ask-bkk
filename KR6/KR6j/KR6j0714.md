# Lèngyán jīng guānxīn dìngjiě 楞嚴經觀心定解
*"Mind-Contemplation Settled Explanation" of the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[靈耀]] (述)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle (10卷) **Tiāntái-school commentary** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra* ([[KR6j0118]]) by **Língyào Quánzhāng** 靈耀全彰 [[靈耀]], abbot of the **Lèngyán jiǎngsì** 楞嚴講寺 in Jiāhé 嘉禾 (Jiāxīng, Zhèjiāng); the author identifies himself as *Zhù Jiāhé Lèngyán jiǎngsì Tiāntái bǐqiū Língyào shù* 住嘉禾楞嚴講寺天台比丘靈耀述. Preserved as **X15 no. 306** in the *Xùzàngjīng*. The work is the main book of which [[KR6j0712]] (科) and [[KR6j0713]] (大綱) are the structural outline and main-outline summary.

### Abstract

The *Dìngjiě* is a substantial late-Kāngxī-period reaffirmation of the **Tiāntái-school exegetical orthodoxy** on the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*, written in explicit opposition to the Wànlì-period commentator [[交光真鑑]] Jiāoguāng Zhēnjiàn 交光真鑑's *Lèngyán jīng zhèngmài shū* ([[KR6j0703]]), which had attempted to dispense with the six consciousnesses (六識) and the three contemplations (三觀) as exegetical categories. Língyào restores the classical Tiāntái apparatus — the threefold structural division (*sānfēn fēnwén* 三分分文: 序 / 正宗 / 流通); the *sìxītán* 四悉檀 (four siddhāntas) for interpreting the *rúshì* 如是 incipit; the *yuèjiāo* 約教 system of doctrinal classification (Tripiṭaka / *tōng* / *bié* / *yuán*) — and uses it to read every section of the sūtra. The opening exegesis of the *rúshì wǒwén* 如是我聞 already shows the method: Língyào takes the formulaic incipit and gives it a layered Tiāntái-school exposition through *sìxītán* and *yuèjiāo*. Composition: Kāngxī 19 / 1680, contemporaneous with the *Dàgāng*.

The *Dìngjiě* is the principal eighteenth-century Tiāntái-school *Śūraṃgama* commentary and an important counterweight to the Chán/Xiánshǒu-leaning commentaries dominant in late-Míng Buddhism.
