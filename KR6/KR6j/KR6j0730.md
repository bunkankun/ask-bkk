# Qǐng guānyīn jīng shū chǎnyì chāo 請觀音經疏闡義鈔
*Sub-Commentary "Explicating the Meaning" of the Commentary on the Qǐng guānyīn jīng*
by [[智圓]] (述)

## About the work

A four-fascicle (4卷) **sub-commentary** by **Gūshān Zhìyuán** 孤山智圓 [[智圓]] (976–1022), the principal Northern-Sòng *shānwài* 山外-school Tiāntái master, on Tiāntái [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ's *Qǐng guānyīn jīng shū* ([[KR6j0729]]). Preserved as **T39 no. 1801** in the *Taishō*. The sub-commentary recovers and re-transmits Zhìyǐ's *Shū*, which in the post-Táng period had largely fallen out of active study.

### Prefaces

The opening **Qǐng guānyīn jīng shū chǎnyì chāo xù** 請觀音經疏闡義鈔序 records Zhìyuán's motivation: "From the time the *Shū* was expounded by the Wise One [Zhìyǐ] and recorded by Zhāng'ān [[[灌頂]]], no later master has been heard to praise or expound it. With the transmission's voice ceased, later scholars have often been unaware of its name, or knowing the name have never opened the scrolls. This text is reduced to a single thread; that I, unworthy as I am, feel pained for it." Zhìyuán recovered the text, organized its meaning into sections, and wrote it into a *chāo* 鈔 (sub-commentary) of two (or in the received recension, four) fascicles, "in order to clarify the cardinal purport and to enlighten the unschooled."

### Abstract

The *Chǎnyì chāo* is the principal **Sòng-period sub-commentary on Zhìyǐ's *Qǐng guānyīn jīng shū*** and the textual vehicle by which Zhìyǐ's commentary entered the Sòng-Yuán Tiāntái curriculum. Zhìyuán's *shānwài* doctrinal orientation (emphasizing *guānxīn* 觀心) shapes the reading of the *xiāofú dúhài* 消伏毒害 dhāraṇī liturgy: he emphasizes the *Avalokiteśvara* invocation as a vehicle for *guānxīn* contemplation rather than as a primarily apotropaic ritual instrument. The work is a substantial document of the **Sòng-period Tiāntái devotional revival** centered on the *Qǐng guānyīn* and *Lèngyán* sūtras. Composition window: c. 1000 (Zhìyuán's mature period at Gūshān) – 1022 (his death).
