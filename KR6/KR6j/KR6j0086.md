# Shòu pútíxīn jiè yí 受菩提心戒儀
*Ritual for Receiving the Bodhicitta Precepts*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ordination ritual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the **Bodhicitta-precepts ceremony** (*pútíxīn jiè* 菩提心戒) — the Esoteric ordination by which the practitioner formally takes the *bodhicitta-saṃvara* (Bodhi-mind-vow) and is admitted to the Esoteric path.

### Abstract

The *bodhicitta-saṃvara* in the Esoteric tradition is distinct from the standard Mahāyāna *bodhisattva* precepts (*púsà jiè* 菩薩戒) — it is specifically the **Esoteric Bodhi-vow** that establishes the practitioner in the Esoteric *samaya* (pledge). The text gives the ritual procedure: the candidate's preparation, the *ācāryā*'s consecration, the formal taking of the vow, the *abhiṣeka*-water sprinkling, and the post-ordination practice instructions.

The text is a foundational Esoteric ordination ritual and was canonical in both the Tang Tángmì and the Japanese Shingon and Tendai-Esoteric traditions. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774).
