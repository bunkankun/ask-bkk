# Jīngāngdǐng jīng yújiā guānzìzài wáng rúlái xiūxíng fǎ 金剛頂經瑜伽觀自在王如來修行法
*Method of Yoga-Practice of the Avalokiteśvara-King Tathāgata from the Vajraśekhara-sūtra*
by [[金剛智]] Jīngāngzhì (Vajrabodhi, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *yoga* practice manual by **Vajrabodhi** ([[金剛智]]) for the **Avalokiteśvara-King** (Vajradhātu Lotus-family principal) practice. Companion to [[KR6j0103]] (Amoghavajra's later version of the same practice), this Vajrabodhi text is the **earlier Táng** rendering of the practice.

### Abstract

The text establishes Vajrabodhi's earlier Táng version of the Vajradhātu Avalokiteśvara-King practice. The two recensions (Vajrabodhi's [[KR6j0104]] and Amoghavajra's [[KR6j0103]]) document the development of the Táng Esoteric Avalokiteśvara practice tradition through two generations of master-translators.

The translation dates from Vajrabodhi's Cháng'ān period (720–741).
