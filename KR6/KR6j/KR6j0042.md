# Liánhuábù xīn niànsòng yíguǐ 蓮華部心念誦儀軌
*Lotus-Family Heart-Recitation Ritual Manual* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Lotus-family Esoteric ritual manual, paralleling the better-known Amoghavajra version ([[KR6j0039]], T18n0873). The Taishō header notes the cross-references "[cf. Nos. 873, 874]" — confirming this as a parallel recension within the Vajradhātu corpus.

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the ritual instructions, giving the sequence: 淨地 (purifying the site), 淨身 (purifying the body), and so on. No translator-attribution colophon survives.

### Abstract

The *Liánhuábù xīn niànsòng yíguǐ* (T875) is the **anonymous parallel** to Amoghavajra's *Jīngāngdǐng liánhuábù xīn niànsòng yíguǐ* (T873, [[KR6j0039]]). The two texts share the same ritual function — the Lotus-family *xīn niànsòng* (heart-recitation) practice for the *iṣṭa-devatā* of the Lotus-family (Amitāyus / Avalokiteśvara) — but the two recensions differ in the specific ritual prescriptions.

The text's anonymous transmission and short length suggest a working ritual handbook rather than a translated scripture; conventionally placed in the **late Tang** period (8th–9th c.).
