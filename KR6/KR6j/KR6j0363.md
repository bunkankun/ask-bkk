# Dàxūkōngzàng púsà niànsòng fǎ 大虛空藏菩薩念誦法
*Recitation Method of the Great Ākāśagarbha Bodhisattva*
by [[不空]] (Bùkōng, Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Tang Esoteric *niànsòngfǎ* manual translated by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]], 705–774). Colophon: *開府儀同三司特進試鴻臚卿肅國公食邑三千戶賜紫贈司空諡大鑒正號大廣智大興善寺三藏沙門不空奉詔譯*. Alternate title 虛空藏念誦法. The text explicitly cites the *Vajraśekhara* (瑜伽金剛頂經) as its doctrinal authority.

### Abstract

The text opens by stating its programme: *I now, basing myself on the Yogatantra Vajraśekhara, expound the Mantra-and-Method of Ākāśagarbha Bodhisattva of the Ratna-kula* (我今依瑜伽金剛頂經說寶部虛空藏菩薩真言教法). The intended audience is explicitly the householder and mountain-dwelling monastic of "thin merit and meagre virtue, lacking in resources" (在家山家薄福少德乏少資具者) — those whose worldly and supramundane wishes go unmet. The ritual programme purges karmic obstacles, expands merit, brings mental ease, and confirms unshakable bodhicitta. As an Amoghavajra Esoteric *niànsòng* manual, the text fills out the operative-ritual side of the Ākāśagarbha-cult complementarily with [[KR6j0362]] (the *qiúwénchí fǎ* mnemonic-retention manual). Together with [[KR6j0364]], [[KR6j0365]], [[KR6j0366]] and [[KR6j0367]], the present text is part of the broad late-Tang Ākāśagarbha cultic complex that developed around the *Vajraśekhara* and *Vajradhātu*-mandala traditions.
