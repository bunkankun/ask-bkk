# Guānzìzài púsà xīn zhēnyán yīyìn niànsòng fǎ 觀自在菩薩心真言一印念誦法
*One-Mudrā Recitation Method of the Heart-Mantra of Avalokiteśvara*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A short Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) presenting the *one-mudrā* (一印, i.e. abbreviated single-mudrā) form of the Avalokiteśvara heart-mantra recitation. The *yīyìn* ("one-mudrā") format is a deliberately abbreviated practice-form designed for daily liturgical use or for practitioners not yet ready for the full Esoteric *aṣṭa-mudrā* maṇḍala procedures.

### Abstract

The *yīyìn niànsòng fǎ* (one-mudrā recitation method) is one of Amoghavajra's recurring formats — a stripped-down ritual procedure using a single principal *mudrā* and the heart-mantra alone, suitable for routine daily liturgy or for practitioners who have not yet undergone full *abhiṣeka* (consecration) into the *Vajraśekhara* maṇḍala. The text accompanies the more extensive Avalokiteśvara ritual manuals ([[KR6j0228]]–[[KR6j0231]], [[KR6j0238]]) as the abbreviated practice-form of the same liturgical tradition. This format-pairing is characteristic of Amoghavajra's compositional method.
