# Liánhuá tāizàngjiè yíguǐ jiěshì 蓮華胎藏界儀軌解釋
*Explication of the Lotus-Womb-Realm Ritual Manual* (Jp. *Renge taizōkai giki kaishaku*)
by [[眞興]] (Shinkō, 撰)

## About the work

A doctrinal exegetical commentary by the Heian Hossō scholar **Shinkō** 眞興 ([[眞興]], 935–1004) on the principal Garbhadhātu ritual manual ([[KR6j0008]], T18n0852a) — Fǎquán's *Xuánfǎsì yíguǐ*. The commentary is one of the earliest substantial Heian doctrinal treatments of the Garbhadhātu ritual system from outside the strict Shingon (Tōmitsu) lineage.

### Abstract

Shinkō's *Jiěshì* (Jp. *Kaishaku* — "Explication") is a remarkable Heian-era cross-school engagement: a **Hossō-trained Kōfuku-ji scholar** producing a systematic doctrinal commentary on a **Tang Esoteric ritual manual**. The composition reflects the late-10th-century Heian intellectual environment in which Hossō (Yogācāra) doctrinal categories were being applied to the interpretation of Esoteric ritual texts — a methodology that would become characteristic of the Heian "doctrinal-Esoteric" (*kyō-mitsu* 教密) synthesis.

The commentary covers the principal sections of Fǎquán's ritual manual — the preparatory rites, the central Garbhadhātu *sādhana*, the *abhiṣeka* offerings, the *japa* sequences — and analyses each in terms of its doctrinal presuppositions about the *bodhicitta*, the *dharmakāya*, and the Yogācāra "consciousness-only" framework. Where Fǎquán's text is purely operative-ritual, Shinkō's *jiěshì* is doctrinal-interpretive; together they exemplify the Heian distinction between *ji* (practice) and *kyō* (doctrine) as the two complementary registers of Esoteric understanding.

The dating bracket (ca. 960 to Shinkō's death in 1004) reflects his documented mature period at Kōfuku-ji.
