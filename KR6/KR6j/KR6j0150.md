# Zūnshèng fódǐng xiū yújiā fǎ guǐyí 尊勝佛頂脩瑜伽法軌儀
*Yoga-Practice Ritual Manual for the Victorious Buddha-Crown*
by [[善無畏]] Shànwúwèi (Śubhakarasiṃha, 譯)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Esoteric *yoga* practice ritual manual by **Śubhakarasiṃha** ([[善無畏]]) for the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya* (*Zūnshèng fódǐng* — Victorious Buddha-Crown) practice. The text gives the elaborated *yoga-tantra* version of the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya* practice, integrating the *dhāraṇī*-recitation with the broader Esoteric *yoga*-mandala framework.

### Abstract

Śubhakarasiṃha's two-fascicle ritual manual is one of the principal Táng Esoteric codifications of the Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya practice. Where Amoghavajra's later T972 ([[KR6j0149]]) gives the daily-practice recitation manual, Śubhakarasiṃha's T973 gives the more elaborate *yoga* practice for advanced practitioners. The translation dates from Śubhakarasiṃha's Cháng'ān period (716–735).
