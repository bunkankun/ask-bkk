# Niànsòng jiéhùfǎ pǔtōng zhūbù 念誦結護法普通諸部
*General [Ritual] for Recitation, Boundary, and Protective Practice across All Families*
by [[金剛智]] (Vajrabodhi, 述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual *expounded* (*shù* 述) by **Vajrabodhi** 金剛智 ([[金剛智]]) on the general procedures for **recitation** (*niànsòng* 念誦), **boundary establishment** (*jiéjiè* 結界), and **protective practice** (*hùfǎ* 護法) across the principal Esoteric **families** (*zhūbù* 諸部 = the five families: Buddha, Vajra, Lotus, Jewel, Karma).

### Abstract

The text is a unified ritual manual covering the ritual elements common to all Esoteric *sādhana* practices regardless of the specific family or deity: the boundary-establishment that consecrates the practice space, the recitation procedures common to *kriyā* and *yoga* practices, and the protective practices that guard the practitioner. The text is a working **portable reference** for the Esoteric *ācāryā* engaged in any of the principal Esoteric ritual sequences.

The composition dates from Vajrabodhi's Chángān period (720–741).
