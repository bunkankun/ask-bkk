# Lèngyán jīng zhízhǐ kēwén 楞嚴經直指科文
*Sectional Outline of the Direct-Pointing Commentary on the Śūraṃgamasūtra*
by [[圅昰]] (作)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle (1卷) **sectional-outline** (*kēwén* 科文) accompanying [[圅昰]]'s *Lèngyánjīng zhízhǐ* 楞嚴經直指 ([[KR6j0699]], X14 no. 291). The catalog meta records no author for this entry; however, the work is the structural-outline companion to [[KR6j0699]] and is conventionally attributed to **Tiānrán Hánshì** 天然圅昰 [[圅昰]] (1608 – 1685), the late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng-school master of the Lǐngnán region. Preserved as **X14 no. 290** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The work opens with the standard *kēwén* layout: "Wholesūtra major outline (in three): / Initial: preface section (in two) / Initial: the various sūtras' general examples (in two) / Initial: speaking time-and-place …" (○全經大科(三)：初序分(二)：初諸經通例(二)：初說時處 …). The structure is the topical key to the parent *Zhízhǐ* commentary, providing the hierarchical sectional analysis necessary to follow Hánshì's distinctive Cáodòng-Chán reading of the *Lèngyánsūtra*.

The dating bracket is set to **1640 – 1685**, the productive Cáodòng-school career of Hánshì from his establishment as abbot at the **Léifēngsì** 雷峰寺 / **Hǎiyúnsì** 海雲寺 / **Lúshān Guīzōngsì** 廬山歸宗寺 through to his death.
