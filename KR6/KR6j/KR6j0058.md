# Fó shuō yújiā dàjiàowáng jīng 佛說瑜伽大教王經
*Sūtra of the Buddha's Speaking of the Yoga Great-Teaching-King*
by [[法賢]] (Fǎxián [= Tiānxīzāi after renaming], 譯)

## About the work

A five-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric *yoga-tantra* translation by **Fǎxián** ([[法賢]]) — the renamed Tiānxīzāi after 987. The text presents a Vajra-yoga *jiàowáng* (great-teaching-king) scripture parallel to the *Sarvatathāgatatattvasaṃgraha* tradition.

### Abstract

The *Yújiā dàjiàowáng jīng* is one of Fǎxián's principal Esoteric scripture translations, presenting an advanced *yoga-tantra* scripture organised around the Vajra-yoga doctrinal framework. The five fascicles cover the doctrinal exposition and ritual practice. The translation dates from Fǎxián's mature Yìjīngyuàn career (987–1001).
