# Fó shuō dàwēidé jīnlún fódǐng chìshèngguāng rúlái xiāochú yīqiè zāinán tuóluóní jīng 佛說大威德金輪佛頂熾盛光如來消除一切災難陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Calamity-Eliminating Dhāraṇī of the Blazing-Light Tathāgata, Great-Powerful Golden-Wheel Buddha-Crown* (translator unknown 失譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric *dhāraṇī* scripture parallel to [[KR6j0140]] (Amoghavajra's Blazing-Light *dhāraṇī*). The fuller title combines the **Golden-Wheel Buddha-Crown** and **Blazing-Light Tathāgata** epithets, integrating the two Esoteric protective traditions.

### Abstract

T964 is an anonymous parallel to T963 ([[KR6j0140]]), with the fuller title-formula. The text gives an alternative version of the Blazing-Light *dhāraṇī*-practice. Anonymous transmission; conventionally dated to the late Táng to Sòng period.
