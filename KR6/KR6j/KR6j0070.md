# Qīngjìng fǎshēn Pílúzhēnà xīndì fǎmén chéngjiù yīqiè tuóluóní sānzhǒng xīdì 清淨法身毘盧遮那心地法門成就一切陀羅尼三種悉地
*The Three Siddhi Accomplishments of All Dhāraṇī, the Mind-Ground Dharma-Gateway of the Pure Dharmakāya Mahāvairocana* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric text on the **three siddhi accomplishments** (三種悉地 *sānzhǒng xīdì*) of the *dhāraṇī*-practice tradition, attributed to the *xīndì fǎmén* (Mind-Ground Dharma-Gateway) of the pure *dharmakāya* Mahāvairocana. The title encodes the central Esoteric doctrine that the cosmic *dharmakāya* Mahāvairocana, in his pure form, is the source of all *dhāraṇī* practice and the goal of the three-fold *siddhi* (śāntika / pauṣṭika / abhicāra; or alternatively the three soteriological levels of *siddhi*).

### Abstract

The text presents an Esoteric exposition organised around (i) the doctrinal foundation of the *dharmakāya* Mahāvairocana as ground of all practice, (ii) the three *siddhi* attainments and their relationships to the threefold *karma* of body-speech-mind, and (iii) the *dhāraṇī* practices that lead to each *siddhi*-attainment. The text is anonymously transmitted and conventionally dated to the **late Tang to Sòng** period (8th–10th c.).
