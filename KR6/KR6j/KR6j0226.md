# Mìmì yàoshù fǎ 祕密要術法
*Method of the Essential Secret Techniques*
by [[阿謨伽]] (Amogha [= Amoghavajra], 譯)

## About the work

A short Esoteric ritual manual preserved only in the Manji Xùzàngjīng (卍 Xuzangjing, X02 no. 188), attributed to the Tang Esoteric translator under the name **Āmójiā** 阿謨伽 (= Sanskrit *Amogha* = abbreviated *Amoghavajra*; see [[阿謨伽]] for the name analysis). The text is not preserved in the Taishō main canon and exists only via the supplementary Xùzàngjīng line.

### Abstract

The text presents a compendium of *mìmì yàoshù* 祕密要術 ("essential secret techniques") — a collection of *mudrā*, *mantra*, and *maṇḍala*-instructions for various Esoteric rites. As a Xùzàngjīng-only text, it represents the supplementary stratum of Esoteric ritual manuals that circulated in late-Tang and Sòng monastic communities outside the principal canonical line. Attribution to **Āmójiā** (alternate name of **Amoghavajra** [[不空]]) places the text within his school but its authentic Amoghavajra-origin is uncertain — the Xùzàngjīng-only status and the name-form 阿謨伽 (rather than the standard 不空) suggest possible later compilation under his name. The catalog meta records the catalog form 阿謨伽; the prose treatment correlates this with [[不空]].
