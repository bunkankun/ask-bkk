# Dàlún jīngāng xiūxíng xīdì chéngjiù jí gòngyǎng fǎ 大輪金剛修行悉地成就及供養法
*Method for Cultivating the Great-Wheel Vajra Practice for Siddhi-Accomplishment and Offering*
by anonymous compiler

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous practitioner's manual on the **Great-Wheel Vajra** 大輪金剛 cycle, complementing the deity's principal scripture [[KR6j0458]] (T1230) and the recitation-manual [[KR6j0460]] (T1232). The catalog meta gives no translator and no dynasty; canwww (T21N1231) likewise records no responsible-statement.

### Abstract

The text is structured as a **xiūxíng** 修行 (cultivation) and **gòngyǎng** 供養 (offering / *pūjā*) manual: it lays out the daily-practice programme of the Mahā-cakra-Vajra rite — purification of the practitioner, construction and consecration of the practice-altar, *abhiṣeka*-self-empowerment, dhāraṇī-recitation, the four-period (晝夜四時) routine, the offering substances and their visualised transformation, and the *xīdì* 悉地 (Skt. *siddhi*) attainment that is the ritual's stated end. It functions as a how-to companion to the doctrinal exposition of T1230. The dating bracket is conservative — Tang to early Sòng — given inclusion in the late-Tang and Sòng canonical collections of the Mahā-cakra-Vajra cycle.
