# Jīngāngdǐng yújiā hùmó yíguǐ 金剛頂瑜伽護摩儀軌 (recension B)
*Vajraśekhara-yoga Homa Ritual Manual* (recension B)
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle alternative recension of the Vajradhātu *homa* ritual manual under the same title as [[KR6j0079]] (T908), preserved in the Taishō under T18n0909. The two recensions cover the same four-fold *karman* of *homa* practice but differ in the specific ritual prescriptions.

### Abstract

The two-recension preservation of the Vajradhātu *homa* ritual manual (T908 and T909, both attributed to Amoghavajra and both bearing the identical title) suggests parallel ritual traditions or editorial reorganisations within the Tang Esoteric *homa* corpus. Both recensions remain canonical and were used in the Japanese Shingon *homa* tradition, where multiple recensions of the same ritual are preserved as legitimate variants.

The translation dates from Amoghavajra's mature Chángān period (746–774).
