# Yīzì dǐnglúnwáng yújiā guānxíng yíguǐ 一字頂輪王瑜伽觀行儀軌
*Yoga-Visualization Ritual Manual of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King*
by [[不空]] Bùkōng (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *yoga* visualization-practice ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King — focused specifically on the *yújiā guānxíng* ("yoga-visualization") aspect of the practice, distinct from the more general *niànsòng* (recitation) manuals [[KR6j0130]] / [[KR6j0131]].

### Abstract

The text codifies the visualization-yoga sequences for advanced practitioners of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King — the visualization of the seed-syllable, the cosmic-imperial chariot iconography, and the integration with the practitioner's body via the *bīja*-cakra system. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng'ān period (746–774).
