# Lèngyán jīng guānxīn dìngjiě kē 楞嚴經觀心定解科
*Outline of the "Mind-Contemplation Settled Explanation" of the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[靈耀]] (述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **structural-outline** (*kē* 科) accompanying **Língyào Quánzhāng** 靈耀全彰 [[靈耀]]'s ten-fascicle *Lèngyán jīng guānxīn dìngjiě* 楞嚴經觀心定解 ([[KR6j0714]]). The author identifies himself as *Tiāntái bǐqiū Língyào Quánzhāng shù* 天台比丘靈耀全彰述 ("composed by the Tiāntái-school monk Língyào Quánzhāng"). Preserved as **X15 no. 304** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The *Kē* is the conventional *Sūtra-outline* paratext that accompanies a major Chinese Buddhist commentary: it lays out, in deeply nested headings, the structural division of the *Śūraṃgama* into *xù* 序 / *zhèngzōng* 正宗 / *liútōng* 流通 sections, with each scene and doctrinal step assigned to its place under a hierarchical numbered scheme. The outline is to be read alongside the *Dìngjiě* proper as its navigational apparatus and as the visible expression of the Tiāntái-school *fēnkē* 分科 method. Composition window: 1680 (the date of the *Dìngjiě dàgāng* preface) – early Kāngxī period.
