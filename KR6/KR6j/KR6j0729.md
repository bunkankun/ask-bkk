# Qǐng guānyīn jīng shū 請觀音經疏
*Commentary on the Qǐng guānyīn jīng (Sūtra of Inviting Avalokitêśvara)*
by [[智顗]] (說), [[灌頂]] (記)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) commentary on the **Qǐng guānshìyīn púsà xiāofú dúhài tuóluóní zhòujīng** 請觀世音菩薩消伏毒害陀羅尼呪經 ([[KR6d0288]] = T20n1043) by **Tiāntái Zhìyǐ** 智顗 [[智顗]] (538–597), recorded (*jì* 記) by his disciple **Zhāng'ān Guàndǐng** 章安灌頂 [[灌頂]] (561–632). Preserved as **T39 no. 1800** in the *Taishō*, **2** in the *Northern Yǒnglè canon* / *Jiāxīng zàng* circulation. The *Qǐng guānyīn jīng* (T20 n1043) is one of the canonical Tiāntái-school texts of *Avalokiteśvara* devotional practice, and Zhìyǐ's commentary establishes its place within the Tiāntái doctrinal scheme.

### Abstract

The *Shū* unfolds the sūtra's title and content under Zhìyǐ's standard Tiāntái scheme of *rén* 人 / *fǎ* 法 (person and method): the "person" is *Avalokiteśvara* (with *tōngbié* 通別 — generic and specific — aspects); the "method" is the dhāraṇī. Each is then unfolded under further sub-distinctions (*yòng* 用 / *tǐ* 體, *néng* 能 / *suǒ* 所), and the whole apparatus is read through the Tiāntái *sānguān* 三觀 method. The commentary is one of the foundational sources for **Tiāntái Avalokiteśvara devotional practice**, particularly for the *xiāofú dúhài* 消伏毒害 ("dissolving and conquering toxic harms") liturgy associated with the dhāraṇī. The *Shū* circulated widely in the Tiāntái lineage before falling into relative obscurity, until **Gūshān Zhìyuán** 孤山智圓 [[智圓]] (976–1022) revived it by composing his *Chǎnyì chāo* ([[KR6j0730]]). Composition: late sixth century, before Zhìyǐ's death in 597.
