# Fódǐng zūnshèng tuóluóní niànsòng yíguǐ fǎ 佛頂尊勝陀羅尼念誦儀軌法
*Recitation Ritual Manual for the Buddha-Crown Victorious Dhāraṇī*
by [[不空]] Bùkōng (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the recitation of the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī*. Where the five Táng translations [[KR6j0144]]–[[KR6j0148]] give the *sūtra* text containing the *dhāraṇī*, Amoghavajra's T972 gives the **ritual framework** within which the *dhāraṇī* is to be recited — the *mudrā*-mantra-visualisation triplets, the offering sequences, and the meritorial-soteriological dedication.

### Abstract

The text codifies the daily-practice and ceremonial-occasional *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya-dhāraṇī* recitation in the Táng Esoteric ritual framework. As a working liturgical manual, it became the standard reference for the *Uṣṇīṣa-vijaya* practice in both the Táng Tángmì tradition and the Japanese Shingon-Tendai derivatives. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng'ān period (746–774).
