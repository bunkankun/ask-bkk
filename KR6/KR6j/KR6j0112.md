# Fó shuō Dìshìyán mìmì chéngjiù yíguǐ 佛說帝釋巖祕密成就儀軌
*Sūtra-Ritual of Esoteric-Accomplishment at Indra's Cliff*
by [[施護]] Shīhù (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric ritual manual by **Dānapāla** ([[施護]]) on a practice associated with **Indra's Cliff** (*Dìshìyán* 帝釋巖 — Skt. *Indraśailaguhā*, the famous cliff-cave near Rājagṛha where Indra is said to have visited the Buddha and asked the questions of the *Sakkapañha-suttanta*).

### Abstract

The text gives an Esoteric ritual practice associated with the Indra's Cliff site — likely a *sādhana* invoking Indra and the cosmic-protective deities of the eastern direction in the Vajradhātu mandala. The translation dates from Dānapāla's Yìjīngyuàn period (985–1017).
