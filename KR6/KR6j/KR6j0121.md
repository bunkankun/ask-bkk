# Dà fódǐng rúlái fàngguāng xīdáduō bāndáluó dà shénlì dōushè yīqiè zhòuwáng tuóluóní jīng dà wēidé zuìshèng jīnlún sānmèi zhòupǐn 大佛頂如來放光悉怛多般怛羅大神力都攝一切呪王陀羅尼經大威德最勝金輪三昧呪品
*Sūtra-Chapter on the Mantra of the Great-Power Most-Victorious Golden-Wheel Samādhi of the Great-Buddha-Crown Sitātapatra Tathāgata's Universal-Compendium-of-Mantra-Kings Dhāraṇī* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric *dhāraṇī*/*mantra* chapter under the very long composite Sitātapatra / Buddhoṣṇīṣa-vijaya / Cakra-rāja title. The text is one of the working liturgical extracts of the Buddha-Crown *dhāraṇī* corpus, focused on the **golden-wheel** (*jīnlún* 金輪 = *cakra-rāja*) Esoteric practice.

### Abstract

The *Cakra-rāja* (Wheel-King) practice integrates the cosmic-imperial *cakravartin* (Wheel-Turning King) iconography with the Buddha-Crown / *Sitātapatra* protective tradition. The text gives the relevant mantras with brief ritual prescriptions. Anonymous; conventionally dated to the late Táng to Sòng period.
