# Jīngāngdǐng yīzì dǐnglúnwáng yújiā yīqiè shíchù niànsòng chéngfó yíguǐ 金剛頂一字頂輪王瑜伽一切時處念誦成佛儀軌
*Buddhahood-Attainment Ritual Manual of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King Yoga of the Vajraśekhara, for Recitation in All Times and Places*
by [[不空]] Bùkōng (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King practice in the *Vajraśekhara-yoga* framework — emphasizing the **all-times-and-places** (*yīqiè shíchù*) practical applicability of the practice. The text is one of the working portable Esoteric daily-practice manuals.

### Abstract

The text gives a generalised, portable form of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King practice, suitable for recitation at any time or place — distinct from the more elaborate context-specific ritual sequences of T954 / T955. The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng'ān period (746–774).
