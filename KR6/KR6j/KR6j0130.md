# Yīzì dǐnglúnwáng niànsòng yíguǐ 一字頂輪王念誦儀軌
*Recitation Ritual Manual of the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King* (recension A)
by [[不空]] Bùkōng (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric ritual manual by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) for the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King practice. Recension A of two parallel recensions (T954A = [[KR6j0130]] and T954B = [[KR6j0131]]).

### Abstract

The text gives the daily-practice recitation manual for the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King — covering the *mudrā*-mantra-visualisation triplets, offering sequences, and *japa* of the principal one-syllable mantra. Companion to the underlying scriptures [[KR6j0124]] (T950) and [[KR6j0128]] (T953). The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng'ān period (746–774).
