# Liùzì shénzhòuwáng jīng 六字神呪王經
*Sūtra of the Divine Six-Syllable Spell-King (Recension B)*
[anonymous translator, attributed to Eastern Jìn]

## About the work

The Recension B (Sòng-canon line) of the anonymous Eastern-Jìn *Liùzì shénzhòuwáng jīng*; companion to Recension A [[KR6j0243]] (T1045A). For the textual and doctrinal context see [[KR6j0242]].

### Abstract

The two recensions T1045A and T1045B descend through divergent Chinese transmission lines (Korean canon and Sòng canon respectively). Both preserve the same underlying *Liùzì shénzhòu* tradition with minor textual variants. For the broader *ṣaḍ-akṣarī* tradition and the cluster of related Eastern-Jìn six-syllable texts ([[KR6j0242]]–[[KR6j0245]]) see [[KR6j0242]].
