## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric scripture in the One-Syllable Crown-Wheel-King family, attributed to **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]). The text is preserved in the *Fójiào dàzàngjīng* (Buddhist Canon) Vol. 052 No. 1222 — a CBETA-canon source rather than the standard Taishō location.
