# Fó shuō pínàiyé jīng 佛說毘奈耶經
*Sūtra of the Buddha's Speaking of Vinaya (Esoteric)* (anonymous)

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric scripture under the title *Pínàiyé* 毘奈耶 (= Skt. *vinaya*, "discipline / regulations"), included in the Esoteric volume rather than in the *vinaya* (律藏) volumes — indicating that the *vinaya* of the title refers to the **Esoteric *samaya***-discipline of the *kriyā-tantra* tradition rather than the standard monastic *vinaya*.

### Abstract

The text presents the Esoteric *samaya* (vow / pledge) disciplinary framework — the rules and observances that bind a *kriyā*-tantra practitioner. The text functions as an Esoteric *vinaya*: not the monastic disciplinary code but the disciplinary framework for the *kriyā* practitioner who has received Esoteric *abhiṣeka*.

The text is anonymously transmitted and is conventionally dated to the Tang Esoteric period (8th–9th c.).
