# Lèngyán jīng bǎojìng shū kēwén 楞嚴經寶鏡疏科文
*Outline of the "Precious-Mirror" Commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[溥畹]] (撰)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **structural outline** (*kēwén* 科文) accompanying **Lángǔ Pǔwǎn** 蘭谷溥畹 [[溥畹]]'s ten-fascicle *Lèngyánjīng bǎojìng shū* 楞嚴經寶鏡疏 ([[KR6j0724]]). Pǔwǎn identifies himself as *Dà Qīng qīncì Yúnnán Fǎjièsì jiǎngjīng Guǎnglíng shāmén Pǔwǎn zhuàn* 大清欽賜雲南法界寺講經廣陵沙門溥畹撰 ("of the Great Qīng, by imperial bestowal Sūtra-Lecturer at the Fǎjièsì in Yúnnán, monk of Guǎnglíng [Yángzhōu]"). Preserved as **X16 no. 314** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The *Kēwén* sets out the hierarchical structural outline of the *Bǎojìngshū*: the conventional threefold division (*xù* 序 / *zhèngzōng* 正宗 / *liútōng* 流通), with each section subdivided according to the Xiánshǒu (Huáyán) school's *shímén* 十門 method. The work is the navigational apparatus to the main commentary; together with the prefatory volume *Bǎojìng shū xuántán* ([[KR6j0723]]) and the main *Bǎojìng shū* ([[KR6j0724]]), it forms Pǔwǎn's complete three-volume *Lèngyán* commentarial apparatus. Composition: presumably 18th century (Kāngxī through Qiánlóng era), contemporaneous with the *Bǎojìngshū* itself.
