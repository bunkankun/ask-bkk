# Dà Pílúshènà chéngfó shénbiàn jiāchí jīng liánhuá tāizàng bēishēng màntúluó guǎngdà chéngjiù yíguǐ 大毘盧舍那成佛神變加持經蓮華胎藏悲生曼荼羅廣大成就儀軌
*Compendium of Greater Accomplishment Ritual Procedures for the Lotus-Womb-Treasury Compassion-Born Mandala from the Mahāvairocanasūtra* (anonymous variant recension)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Garbhadhātu ritual codification, anonymously transmitted, printed in the Taishō under T18n0852b immediately after Fǎquán's *Xuánfǎsì yíguǐ* ([[KR6j0008]], T852a). The two texts share a near-identical title (with the variant Chinese transliteration *Pílúshènà* 毘盧舍那 in T852b vs. *Pílúzhēnà* 毘盧遮那 in T852a — both rendering the Sanskrit *Vairocana*) and overlapping ritual content. T852b is conventionally treated as a parallel or alternate recension of the same Garbhadhātu ritual tradition.

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the title and the chapter header "供養方便會第一" (*Compendium on the Means of Offering, Chapter 1*); no translator-attribution colophon survives. The opening verse is identical in form to that of T849 ([[KR6j0005]]) and T850 ([[KR6j0006]]), confirming the cognate ritual tradition.

### Abstract

The relationship between T852a ([[KR6j0008]], Fǎquán's *Xuánfǎsì yíguǐ*) and T852b (this text) is contested. The Taishō editors print them as parallel texts under the same number with different sub-letters, suggesting they regarded T852b as a variant recension of T852a but with sufficient independent textual identity to merit separate inclusion. Possibilities considered in modern scholarship (Ono Genmyō, Hodge, Orzech) include: (i) T852b as an earlier draft or alternative arrangement of Fǎquán's ritual codification; (ii) T852b as an independent post-Fǎquán Chángān or Tendai-Esoteric Japanese reworking; (iii) T852b as the Tōmitsu (Shingon) recension distinct from the Taimitsu (Tendai) recension preserved as T852a. The dating bracket here (824–859, late Tang) reflects the most likely period of redaction in light of the textual relations.

The text comes with the same ritual content as T852a — preparatory rites, central Garbhadhātu *sādhana*, *abhiṣeka* offerings, and merit-dedication — but with somewhat different *mantra*-mudrā arrangements and visualisation prescriptions in the central *sādhana*.
