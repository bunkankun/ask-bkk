# Yīqiè mìmì zuìshàng míngyì dàjiàowáng yíguǐ 一切祕密最上名義大教王儀軌
*Ritual Manual of the Highest-Esoteric Great-Teaching-King of All Secret Names and Meanings*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Sòng-period Esoteric ritual manual translated by **Dānapāla** ([[施護]]). The text gives the *yíguǐ* (ritual procedure) for the deity-system of the **Highest-Esoteric Great-Teaching-King** doctrine, with the principal mantras and their *míngyì* (names-and-meanings).

### Abstract

The work is a working Esoteric ritual manual paired with [[KR6j0055]] (the *Wúèr píngděng zuìshàng yújiā dàjiàowáng jīng*) — a *yíguǐ* (ritual codification) accompanying the doctrinal *yoga-uttara-tantra* scripture. The two-fascicle structure covers preparatory rites and central *sādhana* sequence.

The translation dates from Dānapāla's career at the Sòng Yìjīngyuàn (982–1017).
