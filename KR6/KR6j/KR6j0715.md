# Lèngyán jīng zhǐzhǎng shū xuánshì 楞嚴經指掌疏懸示
*Preliminary Indications of the "Pointing-to-the-Palm" Commentary on the Śūraṃgama-sūtra*
by [[通理]] (述)

## About the work

A one-fascicle (1卷) **prolegomenon** (*xuánshì* 懸示, "preliminary indications") to **Dátiān Tōnglǐ** 達天通理 [[通理]]'s ten-fascicle *Lèngyán jīng zhǐzhǎng shū* 楞嚴經指掌疏 ([[KR6j0716]]). Tōnglǐ identifies himself as *Jīngdū Niānhuásì Xiánzōng hòuxué Dátiān Tōnglǐ jìngshù* 京都拈華寺賢宗後學達天通理敬述 ("respectfully composed by Dátiān Tōnglǐ, junior disciple of the Xiánshǒu school at the Niānhuásì in the capital"), with editorial assistance from his Dharma-heirs [[興宗祖旺]] Xìngzōng Zǔwàng 興宗祖旺, [[騰清懷仁]] Téngqīng Huáirén 騰清懷仁, and [[祖毓]] Zǔyù 祖毓. Preserved as **X16 no. 307** in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The *Xuánshì* lays out Tōnglǐ's programmatic framework for reading the *Śūraṃgama-sūtra*, **explicitly modeled on Qīngliáng Chéngguān** 清涼澄觀 [[澄觀]] (the Táng Huáyán patriarch). He divides the sūtra into **four parts** corresponding to the *Sìzhǒngfēn* 四種分 of the Huáyán-school: (1) *Kāishì shēmó chéngxìn fēn* 開示奢摩成信分 ("Opening-and-revealing of *Śamatha*: bringing forth Faith"), (2) *Kāishì sānmó chéngjiě fēn* 開示三摩成解分 ("...of *Samādhi*: bringing forth Understanding"), (3) *Kāishì chánnà chéngxiū fēn* 開示禪那成修分 ("...of *Dhyāna*: bringing forth Cultivation"), (4) *Kāishì lèngyán chéngzhèng fēn* 開示楞嚴成證分 ("...of *Śūraṃgama*: bringing forth Realization"). The *Xuánshì* surveys the major Tang-Sòng *Lèngyán* commentarial tradition — [[圓照]] Yuánzhào, [[子璿]] Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán, [[無盡藏文殊]] Wújìnzàng Wénshū — and explicitly differentiates Tōnglǐ's reading from [[交光真鑑]] Jiāoguāng Zhēnjiàn 交光真鑑's *Zhèngmài shū* ([[KR6j0703]]) — whose departures from Qīngliáng's Huáyán *jiāfǎ* 家法 Tōnglǐ regards as unacceptable. Composition: 1763 (癸未) — 1782 (Tōnglǐ's death).
