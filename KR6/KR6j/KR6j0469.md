# Jiātuó jīngāng zhēnyán 伽馱金剛真言
*Mantra of Gada-Vajra*
by anonymous compiler

## About the work

A very short anonymous Esoteric mantra-text on **Gada-Vajra** 伽馱金剛 (also 迦馱金剛, *Gada-vajra*, the "Mace-Vajra" — a club-bearing wrathful Vajra-deity). Catalog meta and canwww (T21N1241) both record no translator and no dynasty. The text occupies a single page-block in the Taishō.

### Abstract

The text gives the *zhēnyán* 真言 ("true word", *mantra*) of Gada-Vajra, with brief notes on use. As an isolated short *zhēnyán* text — without framing-narrative or extended ritual instructions — it represents the most compressed form of an Esoteric Vajra-deity manual: a portable practitioner's *aide-mémoire* with no doctrinal exposition. The dating bracket is conservative.
