# Jiǔpǐn wǎngshēng Ēmítuó sānmódì jí tuóluóní jīng 九品往生阿彌陀三摩地集陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Compendium of Dhāraṇī of the Amitābha Samādhi for Rebirth in the Nine Grades*
by [[不空]] Bùkōng (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric scripture by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]) integrating the Pure Land **nine-grades-of-rebirth** doctrine (*jiǔpǐn wǎngshēng* 九品往生 — the Pure Land doctrine that rebirth in *Sukhāvatī* takes nine forms graded by the practitioner's level of attainment) with the Esoteric *samādhi*-*dhāraṇī* practice. The text is one of Amoghavajra's principal contributions to the **Esoteric-Pure Land synthesis**.

### Abstract

The text presents a comprehensive Esoteric framework for Pure Land rebirth practice, establishing how each of the nine grades of rebirth corresponds to specific *samādhi*-attainments and *dhāraṇī*-recitations. The synthesis integrates the popular Pure Land devotional cult — based on the nine-grades doctrine of the *Guān Wúliàngshòu fó jīng* 觀無量壽佛經 ([[KR6f0071]], T12n0365) — with the Esoteric *Vajradhātu* framework of the Lotus-family.

The composition dates from Amoghavajra's mature Cháng'ān period (746–774).
