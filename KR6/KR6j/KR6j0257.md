# Qiānyǎn qiānbì Guānshìyīn púsà tuóluóní shénzhòu jīng 千眼千臂觀世音菩薩陀羅尼神呪經
*Dhāraṇī Spell-Sūtra of the Thousand-Eye Thousand-Arm Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara* (parallel recension b)
by [[智通]] (Zhìtōng, 譯)

## About the work

The "b" recension of Taishō No. 1057, a parallel transmission of [[智通]] Zhìtōng's two-fascicle *Sahasrabhuja* dhāraṇī-sūtra (cf. [[KR6j0256]] for the "a" recension). Both recensions are ascribed to Zhìtōng of Zǒngchísì 總持寺 in the Zhēnguān 貞觀 (627–649) period; the divergent textual transmissions are preserved in different Tripiṭaka editions and were collated together by the Taishō editors as 1057a / 1057b.

### Abstract

Textually the "b" recension is closely parallel to the "a" recension ([[KR6j0256]]) but exhibits divergences in mantra-transcription, mudrā-name, and occasional ordering of the central twenty-five mudrā / spell pairs. The two recensions diverge in places where one transmission has preserved an older Sanskrit phonology and the other a Tang revisionist phonology, suggesting that one is an older copy and the other a partly-revised version that travelled separately into the canon. The Korean canonical recension (TK) preserves one of the two as part of K0292; the divergent copy entered through Sòng *kānjīng* 刊經 channels and was kept distinct because the editors could not safely collate them.

For the textual matter and the Structural Division refer to [[KR6j0256]]; CANWWW does not give a distinct *toc* for the "b" recension.
