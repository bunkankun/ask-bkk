# Fó shuō Xiāochú yīqiè zāizhàng Bǎojì tuóluóní jīng 佛說消除一切災障寶髻陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Ratnaśikhin (Jewel-Crested) Dhāraṇī for Removing All Calamitous Obstacles*

by [[法賢]] (譯)

## About the work

A short single-juan dhāraṇī-sūtra translated at the Sòng 譯經院 by [[法賢]] Fǎxián. *Bǎojì* 寶髻 = "jewel-topknot" / "jewel-crested" = Skt. *Ratnaśikhin* — both a Buddha-epithet and the name of a particular *vidyā*-deity, well-attested in Indian Buddhism (the *Ratnaśikhin-tathāgata* of the *Saṃdhinirmocana* and other Mahāyāna scripture-lists).

### Abstract

The Buddha addresses Ānanda. Long ago, when Indra (帝釋) was defeated and put to flight by the *asura*s in their cosmic war, he came in fear to the Buddha begging for protection. The Buddha (in his prior-birth identity) reassured Indra and recounted that in a past kalpa, in a Buddha-realm called *Guānzhào* 觀照 ("Vision-Light"), there was a Buddha *Guānzìzài rúlái* 觀自在如來 (= *Avalokiteśvara*-Tathāgata) who bestowed the *Bǎojì* dhāraṇī. This dhāraṇī, declared in unison by *koṭi*s of Buddhas, has the power to bring auspiciousness; one who recites or transcribes it gains protection from all calamities. The text exemplifies the Sòng-period dhāraṇī-genre's deep absorption of Indian *jātaka* / *avadāna* narrative-frames as authorising matter for the spell. Recorded in the *Dàzhōngxiángfú fǎbǎo lù*; Nanjio N0885.
