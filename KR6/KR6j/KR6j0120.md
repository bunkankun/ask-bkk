# Dà fódǐng guǎngjù tuóluóní jīng 大佛頂廣聚陀羅尼經
*Sūtra of the Broad-Collection of Dhāraṇīs of the Great-Buddha-Crown* (translator unknown 失譯)

## About the work

A four-fascicle anonymous Esoteric *dhāraṇī* compendium under the *Buddha-Crown* (*fódǐng* 佛頂 / *Buddhoṣṇīṣa*) family. The text presents a systematic collection of *dhāraṇīs* associated with the Buddha-Crown / *uṣṇīṣa* cult — the worship of the Buddha's cranial protuberance as the seat of cosmic-protective power.

### Abstract

The *guǎngjù* ("broad-collection") indicates a comprehensive collection of *Buddhoṣṇīṣa*-class *dhāraṇīs* including the *Sitātapatra*-, *Uṣṇīṣavijaya*-, and other Buddha-Crown *dhāraṇīs*. The text is anonymously transmitted and conventionally dated to the late Táng to Sòng period.
