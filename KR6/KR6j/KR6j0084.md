# Huǒhūm gòngyǎng yíguǐ 火𤙖供養儀軌
*Fire-Hūṃ Offering Ritual Manual*

## About the work

A one-fascicle anonymous Esoteric *homa* (fire-offering) ritual manual. The title's *huǒhūṃ* 火𤙖 — combining the Chinese 火 (fire) with the Sanskrit *bīja* hūṃ (𤙖, the seed-syllable of cosmic transformation in Esoteric practice) — designates the central element of the ritual: the *homa* fire transformed by the *hūṃ* mantra into the cosmic-transformative *agni-tantra* fire that consumes karmic obstructions.

### Abstract

The text gives a concise *homa* ritual manual focused on the central role of the *hūṃ* mantra in the fire-transformation. The four-fold *karman* sequence (śāntika / pauṣṭika / vaśikaraṇa / abhicāra) is present but compressed; the text emphasizes the soteriological-symbolic dimension of *homa* as cosmic-transformative ritual rather than the operational details. The text is anonymous and conventionally dated to the late Tang to early Sòng period.
