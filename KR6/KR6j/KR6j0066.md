# Sūpóhū tóngzǐ qǐngwèn jīng 蘇婆呼童子請問經 (recension B)
*Sūtra of the Questions of the Youth Subāhu* (alternative recension, 2 fascicles)
by [[善無畏]] (Śubhakarasiṃha, 譯)

## About the work

The two-fascicle alternative recension of the *Subāhu-paripṛcchā-sūtra* (companion to [[KR6j0065]] = T895a in three-fascicle form). The two recensions differ in length but share the core *kriyā-tantra* doctrinal-practical content.

### Abstract

The two-fascicle recension represents an alternative or abbreviated arrangement of the same Tang Esoteric *kriyā-tantra* material. The Korean Tripiṭaka and the standard Taishō witnesses confirm it as a separate text-tradition. The translation dates from Śubhakarasiṃha's Chángān period (724–735).
