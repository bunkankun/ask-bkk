# Dàfāngguǎng fó huáyán jīng rù fǎjièpǐn dùnzhèng Pílúzhēnà fǎshēn zìlún yújiā yíguǐ 大方廣佛花嚴經入法界品頓證毘盧遮那法身字輪瑜伽儀軌
*Yoga Ritual Manual on the Letter-Wheel for Sudden Realisation of the Vairocana Dharmakāya, from the Avataṃsaka's "Entry into the Dharma-Realm" Chapter*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

A one-fascicle Esoteric *yújiā yíguǐ* 瑜伽儀軌 (yoga ritual manual) by **Amoghavajra** ([[不空]]), forming the practice-companion to [[KR6j0212]] (T1019, the *guānmén* / contemplation-gate text). Where T1019 sets out the contemplative-doctrinal interpretation of the *Arapacana* forty-two-syllable mantra, the present T1020 supplies the *zìlún* 字輪 ("letter-wheel") liturgical procedure: the *maṇḍala* arrangement of the syllables in a circle for the practitioner to visualise and circumambulate-internally as the path to **sudden realisation** (*dùnzhèng* 頓證) of Vairocana's *dharmakāya*.

### Abstract

The *zìlún yújiā* ("letter-wheel yoga") is a distinctive Esoteric practice in which the 42 *Arapacana* syllables are visualised as the rim of a wheel surrounding the practitioner's body or the Vairocana figure at the centre of the *maṇḍala*; through one-pointed yogic concentration on this configuration the practitioner is said to attain *dùnzhèng* 頓證 — sudden, non-gradual realisation — of identity with the *Mahāvairocana dharmakāya*. The text is among the most theoretically important of Amoghavajra's syllabary-yoga writings and is paired with T1019 ([[KR6j0212]]) as the practical complement. Together they testify to the Tang Esoteric integration of Avataṃsaka cosmology with the *Vajraśekhara* yoga-tantra apparatus that Amoghavajra was systematising at Dàxìngshànsì.
