# Rù-Táng xīnqiú shèngjiào mùlù 入唐新求聖教目錄
*The Catalog of Newly-Sought Sage-Teaching from Tang*
by [[圓仁]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim importation catalog**, the **third** of **Ennin's** 圓仁 (794–864) four catalogs (cf. [[KR6s0111]]–[[KR6s0112]], [[KR6s0114]]). This is the **comprehensive importation catalog produced upon Ennin's final return to Japan** in **847 CE** after his nine-year Tang stay, documenting the totality of materials brought back. Preserved at T55 no. 2167.

### Abstract

Authorship and date: composed by **Ennin** in **847** upon his return from his **838–847** Tang study mission. *notBefore* = **847**, *notAfter* = **847**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

The catalog documents the largest and most consequential of the Heian *nittō hakke* importations — the totality of Ennin's Tang acquisitions. Ennin's Tang study spanned **Wǔ-tái-shān** (the Mañjuśrī sacred mountain), **Cháng'ān** (the Tang capital), and other major Tang Buddhist centers, and his importation included substantial **Tendai**, **Esoteric**, and devotional materials — providing the foundation for the post-Saichō Tendai institutional consolidation that Ennin himself led after his return.

See [[KR6s0111]] for principal substantive treatment of Ennin and his importation project.
