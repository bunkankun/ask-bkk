# Xù Dà Táng nèidiǎn lù 續大唐內典錄
*A Continuation of the Catalog of the Inner Canon of the Great Táng*
by [[道宣]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **continuation supplement** to **Dàoxuān's** main *Dà Táng nèidiǎn lù* ([[KR6s0088]], T2149, 664 CE), produced by **Dàoxuān** 道宣 (596–667) himself in the brief period between the main catalog's completion (664) and his death (667). The supplement adds further bibliographic and translation-history materials that came to Dàoxuān's attention after the main catalog's completion. Preserved at T55 no. 2150.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the **Xù Dà Táng nèidiǎn lù zànxù** 續大唐內典錄讚序. In paraphrase:

> *Now: the **great sage** in his substance distinguishes and is long-deep-and-still. Universally moistening the moving-and-the-still — together they ripen-the-*qì*-and-ascend. The dim-darkened are not bright; the able to be different transformed do not nourish. Not saying parents-and-relatives — returning in mark to the having-mark, the having-Way — perhaps storing in the lofty heights, dwelling in the rocks, expounding the **prajñā** double-grove…*

[The brief preface continues with concise doctrinal framing.]

### Abstract

Authorship and date: composed by **Dàoxuān** 道宣 in the years immediately after the 664 main catalog's completion, before his death in 667. *notBefore* = **664**, *notAfter* = **667**. Catalog dynasty 唐.

The work is a **brief supplement** containing additional bibliographic data that Dàoxuān gathered after completing the main catalog — particularly material on Tang-period translation activity that postdated the main catalog's bibliographic cutoff. It functions as the **author's own addendum** rather than an independent work; its single-juan extent makes it a slim supplement to the 10-juan main catalog.

The work is consequently of less independent bibliographic significance than [[KR6s0088]] but is essential for the **complete reconstruction of Dàoxuān's bibliographic project**. Modern scholarly use of the *Dà Táng nèidiǎn lù* tradition treats the main catalog and the supplement as a single combined documentary unit.

## Other points of interest

Dàoxuān's death in **Qiánfēng** 乾封 2, 10th month, 3rd day = 27 October **667** marks the close of his bibliographic work. The supplement's brief 1-juan extent reflects its production in the brief 3-year window between the main catalog's completion and his death — a poignant documentary witness to a major scholar's continued professional engagement up to the end of his life.
