# Zhū āshélí zhēnyán mìjiào bùlèi zǒnglù 諸阿闍梨真言密教部類總錄
*The General Catalog of the Mantra Esoteric Teaching Sections by Various Ācāryas*
by [[安然]] (集), = **Annen** 安然 (841–915?)

## About the work

A two-juan **Heian-era Japanese Tendai-Esoteric (Taimitsu) bibliographic compendium** by **Annen** 安然 (Japanese **Annen**, 841–915?) — the principal late-9th-century systematizer of Japanese Tendai-Esoteric (*Taimitsu* 台密) doctrine and bibliography. The work organizes Esoteric (*zhēnyán mìjiào* 真言密教 = mantra-esoteric-teaching) canonical materials by **ācārya** (*āshélí* 阿闍梨, the Esoteric "preceptor"-lineage transmitter) — making it both a bibliographic catalog and a documentary witness to the Tang Esoteric lineage transmissions that the *nittō hakke* had brought to Japan. Preserved at T55 no. 2176. *notBefore* = **885**, *notAfter* = **902** (Annen's principal scholarly productivity period). Catalog dynasty 日本.

The work is one of the most important **organizational** (rather than merely inventory-bibliographic) documents of the post-*nittō-hakke* Heian Esoteric establishment — making the lineage-transmission structure of Esoteric Buddhism explicit through its catalog organization.
