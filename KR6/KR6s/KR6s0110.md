# Yuánxíng lù 圓行錄
*The Catalog of Engyō*
by [[圓行]] (撰), = **Engyō** 圓行 (799–852)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim importation catalog** by **Engyō** 圓行 (Japanese **Engyō**, **799–852**), one of the *nittō hakke*. Engyō traveled to Tang in **838** (returning **839**) in the same Japanese embassy as **Jōgyō** 常曉 ([[KR6s0109]]) and **Ennin** 圓仁. His catalog documents the texts and ritual materials he brought back, with particular emphasis on **Esoteric** materials. Preserved at T55 no. 2164.

### Abstract

Authorship and date: by **Engyō** 圓行 (Japanese, **799–852**), produced upon his **839** return to Japan. *notBefore* = **839**, *notAfter* = **839**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

The work is one of the **eight foundational Heian-era Tang-importation catalogs**, supplementing the larger and more consequential Saichō, Kūkai, and Ennin importations with additional Esoteric materials.
