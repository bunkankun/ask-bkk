# Fǎxiàng zōng zhāngshū 法相宗章疏
*The Fǎ-xiàng (Yogācāra) School's Chapters and Commentaries*
by [[平祚]] (錄), = **Heiso** 平祚

## About the work

A single-juan **Heian-era Japanese Fǎxiàng (Yogācāra) school bibliographic catalog**, by **Heiso** 平祚 (Japanese, dates not preserved). The **Fǎxiàng** 法相 / *Hossō* school is the Japanese-Yogācāra institutional successor to the Tang **Xuánzàng** 玄奘–**Kuījī** 窺基 Yogācāra tradition. One of the **seven sectarian bibliographies** ([[KR6s0125]]–[[KR6s0131]]). Preserved at T55 no. 2180. *notBefore* = **950**, *notAfter* = **1100**. Catalog dynasty 日本.
