# Dà Míng Tàizōng Wénhuángdì yùzhì xùzàn wén 大明太宗文皇帝御製序讚文
*Great Míng Tài-zōng Wén-huáng-dì's Imperially Composed Prefaces and Encomia*
by [[太宗朱棣]] (撰), the **Yǒnglè** 永樂 emperor

## About the work

A single-juan collection of **ten** imperially-composed Buddhist prefaces and encomia (*xùzàn wén* 序讚文) by **Míng Tàizōng** 太宗 = the **Wénhuángdì** 文皇帝 = the **Yǒnglè** 永樂 emperor (**Zhū Dì** 朱棣, 1360–1424, r. 1402–1424). The ten pieces are imperial-prefaces and encomiastic compositions written for various Buddhist canonical and devotional texts during his reign; collected together as a single juan and prefixed to the **Northern Yǒnglè canon**. Preserved at P178 no. 1610. The first of three substantial Yǒng-lè-imperial Buddhist works in the Kanripo ([[KR6s0063]], [[KR6s0064]], [[KR6s0065]]).

### Prefaces

The text opens with a **table of the ten included pieces**:

- **Zàngjīng zàn** 藏經讚 — Encomium on the Buddhist Canon
- **Rúlái zhèngzōng dàjué miàojīng xù** 如來正宗大覺妙經序 — Preface to the *Sūtra of the Tathāgata's Right-Lineage Great-Awakening Wonderful Teaching*
- **Sìbù jīng xù** 四部經序 — Preface to the Four-Part Sutras
- **Shèng miào-jí-xiáng zhēn-shí-míng jīng xù** 聖妙吉祥真實名經序 — Preface to the *Sutra of the Holy Wonderful-Auspicious Real Name* (= *Mañjuśrī-nāma-saṅgīti*)
- **Xǐ jīngāng běnxù xù** 喜金剛本續序 — Preface to the *Hevajra-mūla-tantra*
- **Bō-rě-lùn zhōng-dào-lùn duì-lùn lǜ-lùn bǐ-liàng-lùn xù** 般若論中道論對論律論比量論序 — Preface to the *Prajñāpāramitā-śāstra*, *Madhyamaka-śāstra*, *Confrontation-Treatise*, *Vinaya-Treatise*, *Inference-Treatise*
- (and four further pieces)

### Abstract

Authorship and date: composed by the **Yǒnglè emperor** **Zhū Dì** 朱棣 (1360–1424) during his reign **1402–1424**. *notBefore* = **1402**, *notAfter* = **1424**. Catalog dynasty 明.

The collection is one of the principal documents of the **Yǒng-lè-era imperial Buddhist program**, which included:

- The printing of the **Northern Yǒnglè canon** (*Běizàng* 北藏, begun 1410 in Beijing, completed 1440) — the primary Yǒnglè canonical project of which this collection is a prefatory component.
- The production of the **Southern Yǒnglè canon** in Nanjing (begun 1372 under the founding emperor, completed 1399).
- Sponsorship of **Tibetan-Buddhist** translation and printing — including the imperial publication of the *Hevajra-mūla-tantra* and other Tantric texts (the **Xǐ jīngāng běnxù xù** in this collection).
- The construction of the **Báoēnsì** 報恩寺 (Pagoda of Báoēnsì, the famous "porcelain pagoda" of Nánjīng) and other major imperial Buddhist building projects.
- Sponsorship of the major early-Míng *fǎshù* compilation **Dà Míng sānzàng fǎshù** ([[KR6s0007]], 1419, by Yīrú).

The ten included pieces span the full range of Yǒng-lè's Buddhist patronage: orthodox Mahāyāna (canon, sutras, *prajñā*-tradition), Tibetan Tantric (Hevajra), and devotional encomia. The collection is therefore one of the principal documents of **early-Míng imperial Buddhist syncretism**, particularly in its integration of **Han-Chinese** and **Tibetan** Buddhist traditions under a single imperial program.

## Other points of interest

The Yǒnglè emperor's combined Han-Chinese and Tibetan Buddhist patronage was foundational for the **Ming-Qing imperial-Buddhist multi-traditional sponsorship pattern** that became the standard imperial-religious-policy model down to the end of the Qing. The inclusion of the **Hevajra-mūla-tantra** preface in this collection is one of the principal early-Míng evidences for **imperially-sponsored Tibetan-Buddhist printing in Chinese**.
