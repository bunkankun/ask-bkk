# Lǜ zōng zhāngshū 律宗章疏
*The Vinaya School's Chapters and Commentaries*
by [[榮穩]] (錄), = **Eion** 榮穩

## About the work

A single-juan **Heian-era Japanese Vinaya school bibliographic catalog**, by **Eion** 榮穩 (Japanese, dates not preserved). One of the **seven sectarian bibliographies** ([[KR6s0125]]–[[KR6s0131]]). Preserved at T55 no. 2182. *notBefore* = **950**, *notAfter* = **1100**. Catalog dynasty 日本.
