# Tiāntāi zōng zhāngshū 天台宗章疏
*The Tiāntái School's Chapters and Commentaries*
by [[玄日]] (錄), = **Gennichi** 玄日

## About the work

A single-juan **Heian-era Japanese Tendai school bibliographic catalog**, by **Gennichi** 玄日 (Japanese, dates not preserved). One of the **seven sectarian bibliographies** ([[KR6s0125]]–[[KR6s0131]]). Preserved at T55 no. 2178. *notBefore* = **950**, *notAfter* = **1100**. Catalog dynasty 日本.
