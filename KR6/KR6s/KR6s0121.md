# Xīn shūxiě qǐnglái fǎmén děng mùlù 新書寫請來法門等目錄
*The Catalog of Newly-Copied Imported Dharma-Gates*
by [[宗叡]] (撰), = **Shūei** 宗叡 (809–884)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim importation catalog** by **Shūei** 宗叡 (Japanese **Shūei**, **809–884**), the **last** of the *nittō hakke* — Tang-pilgrim Japanese monks. Shūei traveled to Tang in **862–865**, returning with the final substantial Heian importation of Tang Buddhist materials. Preserved at T55 no. 2174A. *notBefore* = **865**, *notAfter* = **865**. Catalog dynasty 日本.
