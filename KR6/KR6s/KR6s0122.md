# Chánlínsì Zōngruì sēngzhèng mùlù 禪林寺宗叡僧正目錄
*The Catalog of Sēng-zhèng Shūei of Chán-lín-sì*
by [[宗叡]] (撰)

## About the work

Companion catalog to [[KR6s0121]] by **Shūei** 宗叡 — produced under his Heian-court office of **sēngzhèng** 僧正 (the senior ecclesiastical-administrative title) at **Chánlínsì** 禪林寺 in Kyoto. Preserved at T55 no. 2174B. *notBefore* = **865**, *notAfter* = **865**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

See [[KR6s0121]] for substantive treatment.
