# Zhìzhèng dàshī qǐnglái mùlù 智證大師請來目錄
*The Importation Catalog of the Great Master Chishō*
by [[圓珍]] (撰)

## About the work

A single-juan **Japanese Tang-pilgrim importation catalog** by **Enchin** 圓珍 (= **Chishō Daishi** 智證大師), the fifth and last of his preserved catalogs (cf. [[KR6s0116]]–[[KR6s0119]]). The work uses Enchin's posthumous title **Chishō Daishi** in the catalog title — indicating either a posthumous-edition compilation by Tendai-Jimon successors, or (more likely) the canonical title-form adopted in subsequent Tendai-school transmission. Preserved at T55 no. 2173. *notBefore* = **858**, *notAfter* = **858**. Catalog dynasty 日本.

See [[KR6s0116]] for substantive treatment of Enchin and his importation project.
