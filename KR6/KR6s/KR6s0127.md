# Sānlùn zōng zhāngshū 三論宗章疏
*The Sān-lùn School's Chapters and Commentaries*
by [[安遠]] (錄), = **Anen** 安遠

## About the work

A single-juan **Heian-era Japanese Sānlùn (Mādhyamika) school bibliographic catalog**, by **Anen** 安遠 (Japanese, dates not preserved). The **Sānlùn** 三論 / *Sanron* school is the Japanese-Mādhyamika institutional successor to the Tang **Jízàng** 吉藏 (549–623) Sanron tradition. One of the **seven sectarian bibliographies** ([[KR6s0125]]–[[KR6s0131]]). Preserved at T55 no. 2179. *notBefore* = **950**, *notAfter* = **1100**. Catalog dynasty 日本.
