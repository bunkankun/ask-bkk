# KR6 佛藏

KR6:  Buddhist Canon division.

- [[KR6a]] 阿含部 *Āhán bù* — Buddhist Canon — Āgama Section. Early Buddhist discourse collections, parallels, cosmology, and Āgama-related treatises. 162 texts.
- [[KR6b]] 本緣部 *Běnyuán bù* — Buddhist Canon — Past-Life Narratives. Jātaka, avadāna, Buddha biography, parables, and Dharmapada-related texts. 76 texts.
- [[KR6c]] 般若部 *Bōrě bù* — Buddhist Canon — Prajñāpāramitā Literature. Perfection of Wisdom sūtras and major scholastic commentaries. 229 texts.
- [[KR6d]] 法華部 *Fǎhuá bù* — Buddhist Canon — Saddharmapuṇḍarīka Section. Lotus Sūtra texts, satellite scriptures, and Tiantai exegetical materials. 249 texts.
- [[KR6e]] 華嚴部類 *Huáyán bùlèi* — Buddhist Canon — Huáyán / Avataṃsaka Corpus. Avataṃsaka translations, partials, commentaries, and Huayan doctrinal literature. 161 texts.
- [[KR6f]] 寶積部 *Bǎojī bù* — Buddhist Canon — Ratnakūṭa Section. The *Mahāratnakūṭa* collection and related independent or parallel translations. 104 texts.
- [[KR6g]] 涅槃部 *Nièpán bù* — Buddhist Canon — Nirvāṇa Section. Mahāyāna *Mahāparinirvāṇa* scriptures and their exegetical tradition. 52 texts.
- [[KR6h]] 大集部 *Dàjí bù* — Buddhist Canon — Mahāsaṃnipāta Section. Great Assembly sūtras and related Mahāyāna collections. 35 texts.
- [[KR6i]] 經集部 *Jīngjí bù* — Buddhist Canon — Sūtra Collection. Mahāyāna sūtras not assigned to the named text cycles. 595 texts.
- [[KR6j]] 密教部 *Mìjiào bù* — Buddhist Canon — Esoteric Texts. Tantric scriptures, dhāraṇī, mantra, ritual manuals, and esoteric practice texts. 762 texts.
- [[KR6k]] 律部 *Lǜ bù* — Buddhist Canon — Vinaya. Monastic discipline, precepts, ordination, procedural texts, and Vinaya commentary. 263 texts.
- [[KR6l]] 毘曇部類 *Pídān bùlèi* — Buddhist Canon — Abhidharma Treatises. Sarvāstivāda and related Abhidharma texts, including Kośa materials. 45 texts.
- [[KR6m]] 中觀部 *Zhōngguān bù* — Buddhist Canon — Madhyamaka Treatises. Mādhyamaka and Sānlùn treatises by Nāgārjuna, Āryadeva, and later scholastics. 51 texts.
- [[KR6n]] 瑜伽部 *Yújiā bù* — Buddhist Canon — Yogācāra Works. Yogācāra, consciousness-only, and *Yogācārabhūmi* lineages. 146 texts.
- [[KR6o]] 論集部 *Lùnjí bù* — Buddhist Canon — Treatise Collection. Miscellaneous śāstras and doctrinal treatises outside the named schools. 145 texts.
- [[KR6p]] 淨土宗著述部 *Jìngtǔ zōng zhùshù bù* — Buddhist Canon — Pure Land School Compositions. Pure Land commentaries, liturgies, devotional texts, and biographies. 140 texts.
- [[KR6q]] 禪宗著述部 *Chánzōng zhùshù bù* — Buddhist Canon — Chán School Compositions. Chán records, sayings, transmission histories, and school writings. 609 texts.
- [[KR6r]] 史傳部 *Shǐzhuàn bù* — Buddhist Canon — History and Biography. Buddhist historiography, hagiography, apologetics, and miracle tales. 186 texts.
- [[KR6s]] 事彙部類 *Shìhuì bùlèi* — Buddhist Canon — Reference and Auxiliary Materials. Lexicons, catalogues, reference works, and subject compilations. 134 texts.
- [[KR6t]] 諸宗部類 *Zhūzōng bùlèi* — Buddhist Canon — Japanese Sectarian Writings. Japanese doctrinal, ritual, and school-specific Buddhist texts. 428 texts.
- [[KR6u]] 疑似部 *Yísì bù* — Buddhist Canon — Apocrypha. Doubtful and spurious scriptures, often composed in China under translation guise. 57 texts.
- [[KR6v]] 藏外部類 *Zàngwài bùlèi* — Buddhist Canon — Texts Outside the Tripitaka. Dunhuang, inscriptional, regional, and extra-canonical Buddhist materials. 103 texts.
