# Ēmítuó jīng yuēlùn 阿彌陀經約論
*Concise Treatise on the Smaller Amitābha-sūtra*
by [[彭際清]] (Péng Jìqīng, 述)

## About the work

A short single-*juǎn* doctrinal essay by the late-Qīng lay-Buddhist [[彭際清]] Péng Jìqīng (1740–1796) on the *Smaller Amitābhasūtra*, completing his three-part Pure Land *yuēlùn* trilogy alongside the *Wúliángshòu jīng qǐxìn lùn* [[KR6p0003]] and the *Guānwúliángshòu fó jīng yuēlùn* [[KR6p0013]]. The author's preface — signed *Púsà jiè dìzǐ Péng Jìqīng shù* 菩薩戒弟子彭際清述 — frames the work in four divisions: (1) the *yàolǐng* 要領 ("essential points") of the sūtra, (2) the *wǎngshēng lìyì* 往生利益 ("benefits of rebirth"), (3) special Q-and-A (*biè shēn wèndá* 別申問答), and (4) closing dedication-verses (*jiésòng* 偈) for the merit-transfer.

### Abstract

Péng's reading of the smaller sūtra is structured around five organising doctrinal pillars: *yīxīn* 一心 (one-mind) as the doctrinal core, *chímíng* 持名 (upholding the name) as the practice, *xìnyuàn* 信願 (faith and aspiration) as the orienting condition, *bù tuì* 不退 (non-retrogression) as the soteriological signpost, and *ēnòuduōluó sānmiǎo sānpútí* 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提 (*anuttarā samyak-sambodhi*) as the ultimate goal. The text's distinctive doctrinal claim is the identification of the *Smaller Sukhāvatīvyūma's* "one-pointed mind not distracted" (*yīxīn bù luàn* 一心不亂) with the Huáyán doctrine of *yīxīn fǎjiè* 一心法界 — making the practice of *niànfó* a direct manifestation of the totalising Dharma-realm, and Pure Land devotion a route to *immediate* (rather than gradually progressed) realisation of bodhi.

The *Yuēlùn* is more compact than Péng's earlier *Qǐxìn lùn* on the larger sūtra and shares the brevity of his *Yuēlùn* on the *Guānjīng*. Together the three texts constitute the late-Qīng lay-Buddhist commentary apparatus for the three principal Pure Land sūtras, the precedent that [[魏源]] Wèi Yuán's *Jìngtǔ sì jīng* would build on a generation later. Dating bracket: Péng's mature period, c. 1781–1796.
