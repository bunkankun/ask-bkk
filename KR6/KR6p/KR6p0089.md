# Yúnqī jìngtǔ huìyǔ 雲棲淨土彙語
*Anthology of [Yúnqī Zhūhóng's] Sayings on the Pure Land*
by [[虞執西]] (Yú Zhíxī, 錄) and [[嚴培西]] (Yán Péixī, 錄)

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* anthology of [[袾宏]] **Yúnqī Zhūhóng**'s Pure Land sayings (*jìngtǔ yǔ* 淨土語) collated from across Yúnqī's prose corpus by his lay-Buddhist disciples [[虞執西]] **Yú Zhíxī** and [[嚴培西]] **Yán Péixī** in the early seventeenth century, shortly after Yúnqī's death in 1615.

### Abstract

Yúnqī's Pure Land writings are scattered across many works — the *Ēmítuó jīng shūchāo* [[KR6p0028]], the *Jìngtǔ yíbiàn* [[KR6p0058]], the *Dá sìshíbā wèn* [[KR6p0063]], the *Xīfāng yuànwén* [[KR6p0064]], plus prefaces, letters, and miscellaneous prose pieces preserved in his collected works the *Yúnqī fǎhuì* 雲棲法彙. The *Huìyǔ* extracts and re-organises Yúnqī's most pointed and pastoral Pure Land sayings into a single accessible volume, organised thematically rather than by source-text: opening sections on the doctrinal foundations of Pure Land devotion; sections on practical *niànfó* practice; sections on the deathbed *zhìniàn*; sections of pastoral counsel directed to lay-Buddhist householders and women; sections of polemical responses to Chán objections.

The work belongs to the **post-mortem consolidation of Yúnqī's intellectual legacy** carried out by his lay-Buddhist circle in the decades after his death. Together with the collected *Yúnqī fǎhuì*, the *Wǎngshēng jí*, and the various individual Pure Land writings, the *Huìyǔ* establishes Yúnqī as the **doctrinal centre of late-Míng Pure Land Buddhism** — a status that the subsequent Qīng-period Pure Land masters (Xǐng'ān Shíxián [[實賢]], Hóngluó Dámò [[達默]], etc.) consistently reinforced.

The text is preserved in the *Xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏 (X1170). No preface fixes a precise composition date; the bracket adopted (1620–1660) covers the early to mid seventeenth century during which the Yúnqī lay-Buddhist circle was consolidating his legacy.
