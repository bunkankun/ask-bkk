# Jìngtǔ shíyào 淨土十要
*Ten Essentials of the Pure Land*
edited and annotated by [[成時]] (Jiānmì Chéngshí, 評點節要)

## About the work

The **definitive late-imperial Pure Land anthology** — a curated selection of ten essential Pure Land texts (the "Ten Essentials") with editorial annotations (*píngdiǎn* 評點) and abridgement (*jiéyào* 節要) by [[成時]] **Jiānmì Chéngshí** 堅密成時 (1618–1678), dharma heir of [[智旭]] **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655). The original selection of the ten texts was made by Ǒuyì Zhìxù; Chéngshí completed the editorial apparatus after Ǒuyì's death and circulated the work in its present form during his own abbacy, in the late-Shùnzhì / early-Kāngxī period.

### Abstract

The "ten essentials" comprise the canonical reading list of late-imperial Pure Land doctrinal study, drawn from the Tiāntái Pure Land tradition (the *Shíyí lùn* attributed to Zhìyǐ [[KR6p0036]]; the *Jìngtǔ shēng wúshēng lùn* of Chuándēng [[KR6p0056]]; the *Jìngtǔ huòwèn* of Tiānrú [[KR6p0053]]); the late-Sòng / Yuán synthesis (the *Lónshū jìngtǔ wén* of Wáng Rìxiū [[KR6p0050]]); the late-Míng synthesis (Yúnqī's *Dá sìshíbā wèn* [[KR6p0063]] and the *Xīfāng yuànwén* [[KR6p0064]]; Yuán Hóngdào's *Xīfāng hélùn* [[KR6p0057]]); and Ǒuyì's own *Ēmítuó jīng yàojiě* 阿彌陀經要解 (his masterwork of Pure Land doctrinal commentary). The selection is therefore a **canonical pedagogical map** of the doctrinal tradition: organised genealogically rather than thematically, the ten essentials together demonstrate the unity of the Pure Land tradition from Suí-Táng through late Míng.

Chéngshí's editorial work is substantial but unobtrusive. The *píngdiǎn* annotations supply doctrinal cross-references and brief explanatory glosses; the *jiéyào* abridgements remove material that Chéngshí (following Ǒuyì) judged less central, producing readable working editions of the selected texts. The result is a single compact anthology that can serve as a complete Pure Land doctrinal-pedagogical curriculum — the function it has continuously served in Chinese Pure Land seminary education from the seventeenth century to the present.

The text is preserved only in the *Xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏 (X1164). The dating bracket adopted (1655–1678) covers from the death of Ǒuyì Zhìxù (after which Chéngshí became the principal transmitter of his master's line) to Chéngshí's own death.

## Other points of interest

The *Shíyào* is the **single most important pedagogical anthology of late-imperial Chinese Pure Land Buddhism**. The Republican-era and post-1949 Pure Land revivals (Yìnguāng 印光, Tāixū 太虛, etc.) all worked from the *Shíyào* as their foundational reading list, and the work continues to define the doctrinal core of contemporary Chinese Pure Land study in monastic seminaries on both sides of the Strait. Chéngshí's editorial selection has, in this sense, shaped the doctrinal self-understanding of every subsequent generation of Chinese Pure Land Buddhists.
