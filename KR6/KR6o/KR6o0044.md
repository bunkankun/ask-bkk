# Típó púsà shì Léngqié jīng zhōng wài dào xiǎo shèng nièpán lùn 提婆菩薩釋楞伽經中外道小乘涅槃論
*Bodhisattva Āryadeva's Explanation of the Non-Buddhist and Hīnayāna Theories of Nirvāṇa Mentioned in the Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra*
by [[提波菩薩]] (Típó púsà / Āryadeva, 造) and [[菩提流支]] (Pútíliúzhī / Bodhiruci, 譯)

## About the work

The companion piece to [[KR6o0043]]: a short one-*juǎn* polemical treatise enumerating and refuting twenty non-Buddhist and Hīnayāna theories of *nirvāṇa* mentioned in the *Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra*. The same colophon "提波菩薩造，後魏北印度三藏菩提流支譯" attributes the work to Āryadeva (in the Wèi-period 提波 orthography) and the translation to [[菩提流支]] (Bodhiruci). Like its companion, the work is closely tied to the *Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra* and is best read as part of the early-sixth-century Wèi-Buddhist effort to systematise Mahāyāna polemics against rival schools.

### Structural Division

### Abstract

The text enumerates twenty rival theories of *nirvāṇa* — (1) the *māṭhara* theory (i.e. *liberation = absence of merit and demerit*); (2) the *jainamata* theory; (3) the Sāṃkhya theory; and so on — and refutes each in turn using Mādhyamika arguments rooted in the impossibility of substantial *nirvāṇa* if *saṃsāra* is itself empty. The structure parallels the *Laṅkāvatāra-sūtra* ch. 3 enumerations of these theories. The Sanskrit does not survive. The translation, like its companion, is from Bodhiruci's Wèi period; both texts are placed together in T32 (Nos. 1639–1640) and are conventionally treated as a single doctrinal unit. Translation date 508–535.
