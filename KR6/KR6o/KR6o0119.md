# Qǐxìn lùn shūjì huìyuè juànshǒu 起信論疏記會閱卷首
*Front Volume to the Combined Reading of the Commentary and Records on the Awakening of Faith*
by [[續法]] (Xùfǎ / Bótíng, 會編)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* prefatory volume (*juànshǒu* 卷首) to the larger 10-*juǎn* *Qǐxìn lùn shūjì huìyuè* [[KR6o0120]] (X45n768), produced by the early-Qing Huáyán-school revivalist [[續法]] Xùfǎ (Bótíng) (1641–1728). The catalog meta entry has no listed author for this front-matter volume separately, but the work is the integral *juànshǒu* of the larger compilation by Xùfǎ.

### Abstract

The volume contains the editorial apparatus for the *Huì-yuè*: a *fán-lì* 凡例 (editorial conventions), a *yuán-qǐ* 緣起 (origin), a *kē-pàn* 科判 (structural outline), a "biography of the five [Huáyán] patriarchs" (五祖傳), an abbreviated record of the treatise's author (論主略錄, i.e., Aśvaghoṣa) and of the principal commentator ([[法藏]] Fǎzàng — 記主略錄), a Dharma-characteristic numerical key (法相數), and a "True/False Arising-and-Perishing Dharma-Characteristic Diagram" (真妄生滅法相圖). The *kē-pàn* maps the structure of the composite *huì-yuè* across its 10 *juǎn*: *juǎn* 1 covers the prefaces and the title-analyses; *juǎn* 2–10 covers the running text of the *Qǐ-xìn lùn* divided according to the standard Huáyán scholastic *kē-wén*.

This *juànshǒu* is essentially the front matter required for orienting a student to the *huìyuè* as a study tool — a standard front-end for a major scholastic compilation in the Huáyán tradition. Composition window: Xùfǎ's mature career, 1670–1728.

## Other points of interest

Xùfǎ's project of producing a unified *huìyuè* of the *shū* (of [[法藏]]) and the *jì* (of [[子璿]]) for the *Qǐxìn lùn* is parallel to his other Huáyán-revival activities — he undertook similar editorial-comparative compilations across the entire Huáyán doctrinal corpus. The *juànshǒu* is part of that systematic project, providing students with a single integrated reading-key.
