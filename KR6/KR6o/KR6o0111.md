# Qǐxìn lùn yìshū 起信論義疏
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Awakening of Faith*
by [[曇延]] (Tányán, 撰)

## About the work

A short Sui-period commentary on the *Awakening of Faith*, in 1 *juǎn* (preserved with a 上 / 下 division), attributed to [[曇延]] (Tányán, 516–588), the *guótǒng* (State-Patriarch) of the early Suí Buddhist establishment and one of the most authoritative scholar-monks of the late sixth century. If genuine, this is among the very earliest extant commentaries on the *Qǐxìn lùn*.

### Abstract

The text is signed 曇延法師撰 ("composed by the Dharma-master Tányán"). It opens with a flowing prose-prologue in the same elegant style as the contemporary [[元曉]] *shū* and [[法藏]] *Yìjì*: "Probing the perfect motion which has no boundary, thought and reflection cannot mark its origin; the dark thread, vast and empty, sight and hearing do not know its limit. And yet that which makes this connection possible — is it not the True Thusness of the Mind? If there is a substance through which the dark connection passes, the light of wisdom pervades the entire dharma-realm…" The author proceeds to expound the *zhēnrú* 真如 (Tathatā) under three aspects of "greatness" (大): *tǐdà* 體大 (greatness of substance), *xiàngdà* 相大 (greatness of characteristic), *yòngdà* 用大 (greatness of function) — the canonical *sāndà* of the *Awakening of Faith*.

The attribution to Tányán is plausible on biographical grounds: he was active before Wǒnhyo and Fǎzàng, was a senior teacher under Suí Wéndì, and was a known student-line teacher of [[智愷]]'s circle, putting him close to the manuscript transmission of the *Qǐxìn lùn*. The composition window 560–588 covers his Cháng'ān period through to his death. However, the text is not registered in the Tang bibliographic catalogs and survives only in Japanese manuscript transmission through the Wànxùzàngjīng; some modern scholars treat the attribution as less than certain, while not directly challenging it.

The Xùzàngjīng base is collated by the Taishō editors against 大正藏 (the parent Awakening of Faith).
