# Shì mó hē yǎn lùn shū 釋摩訶衍論疏
*Commentary on the Shi Moheyan Lun*
by [[法敏]] (Fǎmǐn, 集)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* prose commentary on the [[KR6o0084|*Shì Móhēyǎn lùn* 釋摩訶衍論]] by the Táng or Five-Dynasties monk [[法敏]] (Fǎmǐn). The colophon "沙門法敏集" identifies the author as *shamen* and the verb 集 ("to assemble / collect"), suggesting partial compilation from earlier exegetical sources. The work is preserved only in the *Manji-zokuzōkyō* 卍續藏經 (X45). The text consists of "next-section-by-next-section" 次第屬當 commentary on the parent treatise, formatted with substantial extracts from the source text followed by Fǎmǐn's own analytical remarks.

### Abstract

The text opens "次第屬當釋本論文刪補三卷別行 / 疏上 / 釋摩訶衍論十卷 / 龍樹菩薩造 / 沙門法敏集". The first verse organises the doctrinal material into "ten treatises" (十論), the last of which is the *Dà chéng qǐxìn lùn* itself — reflecting the *Shì Móhēyǎn lùn*'s self-presentation as the comprehensive Mahāyāna doctrinal scheme of which the *Awakening of Faith* is the doctrinal-foundational summary. The author Fǎmǐn is otherwise unattested in standard biographical sources; internal evidence places him loosely in the Tang or Five Dynasties period. The work survived only through Japanese manuscript transmission.

## Other points of interest

The text is the second of the seven Chinese / Liao sub-commentaries on the *Shì Móhēyǎn lùn* preserved in the canon ([[KR6o0085]]–0090). Its identification of "ten treatises" within the parent text reflects the late Tang Esoteric numerological style.
