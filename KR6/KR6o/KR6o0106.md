# Dàshèng qǐxìn lùn yìjì biéjì 大乘起信論義記別記
*Separate Notes to the Record of the Meaning of the Mahāyāna Awakening of Faith*
by [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng / Xiánshǒu, 撰)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* supplement to [[法藏]]'s main *Yìjì* [[KR6o0105]] (T44n1846), preserving topical glosses on points the *Yìjì* either treated cursorily or did not engage. The colophon names the author as 崇福寺沙門法藏撰 ("the monk Fǎzàng of the Chóngfúsì"), placing the text at the Chóngfúsì 崇福寺 in Cháng'ān, another imperially-supported monastery where Fǎzàng resided in his mature years.

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T44N1847) lists no internal sub-divisions; the related-text pointers are to the parent text [[KR6o0078]] (T32n1666) and to the main *Yìjì* [[KR6o0105]] (T44n1846).

### Abstract

The text opens with a list of topics it intends to gloss — "the meaning of the title; the *jìng-yì* of the verses; the meaning of the Buddha-treasure; the meaning of the Dharma-treasure; the meaning of *yòng-dà wéi shàn* 用大唯善; the meaning of *jué-bù-jué* 覺不覺; the meaning of *suí-rǎn èr-xiàng* 隨染二相; the meaning of *běn-jué* 本覺 in its *běn-yǒu* and *xiū-shēng* aspects; the seven sub-divisions in *shēng-miè yīn-yuán* 生滅因緣; …" — and proceeds through them as a topical commentary. Several of these are points where Fǎzàng's *Yì-jì* needed amplification: in particular the *běn-jué* 本覺 / *shǐ-jué* 始覺 distinction (between the "original" and "initial" awakening) and the doctrine of *Tathāgatagarbha* as both the ground of *saṃsāra* and the locus of *nirvāṇa*, which became central topics of medieval and modern Buddhist debate.

Like the main *Yìjì*, the composition window is bracketed by Fǎzàng's career, c. 670–712. The Taishō establishes the text from the 高麗 base collated against 甲 (the Kōyasan / Daigo manuscript witness).

## Other points of interest

Fǎzàng's pairing of a main *jì* (記) with a *biéjì* (別記) follows the same pattern as Wǒnhyo's *shū* / *biéjì* on the same parent text ([[KR6o0101]] / [[KR6o0104]]); both are devices for separating sequential exposition from topical excurses. [[子璿]] Zǐxuán's later *Bǐxuē jì* [[KR6o0107]] (T44n1848) cross-references both Fǎzàng works heavily.
