# Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn yì zuǎn yào 因明入正理論義纂要
*Compiled Essentials of the Treatise on Entering Correct Reasoning of Hetuvidyā*
by [[慧沼]] (Huìzhǎo, 集)

## About the work

The second of [[慧沼]]'s three *yīnmíng* works (with [[KR6o0018]] and [[KR6o0022]]), this one *juǎn* offers a thematic digest of the most important interpretive points of the *Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn* [[KR6o0003]], compiled (*jí* 集) rather than composed in linear-commentary form. It opens by surveying five distinct interpretations (五釋) of the title-words *yīnmíng* found in earlier commentators, and proceeds to systematize the doctrine of the three causal-types (生因 productive, 了因 cognitive, 智因 intellectual) and the four sub-types of perception and inference. The work functions as a reference compendium intended to guide readers through the *Rù lùn* and Kuījī's *Dàshū* by topic rather than by passage, and is therefore especially useful for navigating disputed-question discussions.

### Abstract

The colophon "淄洲大雲寺沙門慧沼集" credits Huìzhǎo of the Dàyúnsì 大雲寺 in Zīzhōu 淄洲 as compiler. The work appears to have been written together with the *Yìduàn* [[KR6o0018]] in the same period of Huìzhǎo's mature exegetical activity (c. 690–714); modern scholarship treats them as a paired set, with the *Yìduàn* taking the form of disputed-question arbitration and the *Zuǎnyào* taking the form of topical synthesis. The *Zuǎnyào* is more selective than the *Yìduàn*: it concentrates on the interpretive cruxes that govern the structure of *yīnmíng* doctrine, especially the analyses of *parārtha* and *svārtha* operations and the canonical four-fold typology of contradiction (四相違). Internal evidence shows that Huìzhǎo regards Kuījī's *Dàshū* [[KR6o0008]] as the orthodox standard but is willing to depart from it on a small number of points where he claims to recover Xuánzàng's own oral teaching. The Taishō follows the Korean canon as base text, collated against the 甲 and 原 manuscripts.
