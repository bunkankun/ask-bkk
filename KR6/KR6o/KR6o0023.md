# Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn shū qián jì 因明入正理論疏前記
*Earlier Notes on the Commentary on the Treatise on Entering Correct Reasoning*
by [[智周]] (Zhìzhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A three-*juǎn* sub-commentary by [[智周]] (Zhìzhōu, 668–723), the leading third-generation master of the Cí'ēn 慈恩 / Fǎxiàng 法相 school after [[慧沼]], glossing the difficult points of [[窺基]] (Kuījī)'s *Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn shū* (the *Dàshū*) [[KR6o0008]]. Together with the same author's *Hòu jì* [[KR6o0024]] and the much shorter *Shū chāo* [[KR6o0025]] this forms what the tradition calls Zhìzhōu's "three records" (智周三記) on Kuījī's *Dàshū*. The *Qián jì* covers — by the convention of the title — the upper portion of Kuījī's *Dàshū*, with the *Hòu jì* taking up the lower portion. As sub-sub-commentary it both helps readers parse Kuījī's frequently elliptical phrasing and resolves the divergences between Kuījī and Huìzhǎo on contested points, generally siding with Huìzhǎo's interpretations.

### Structural Division

The Xuzangjing recension of this text is not in CANWWW. The work is a sub-commentary on Kuījī's commentary [[KR6o0008]] on the *Rù lùn* [[KR6o0003]].

### Abstract

The Xuzangjing edition prefixes a Sòng or early-Yuán "engraving preface" 刻因明入正理論疏前後二記序 narrating the cataloging history: it identifies Zhìzhōu as the author of the "two records" (前記後記) and praises Kuījī for the *Dàshū* and Huìzhǎo for the *Yìduàn* [[KR6o0018]] and the *Zuǎnyào* [[KR6o0019]], noting that the "*Dàshū* itself was not yet glossed at the level of detail required to make the work practically useful" — a need which Zhìzhōu's two records were composed to meet. Composition is normally placed in Zhìzhōu's mature period at the Pǔguāngsì 普光寺 in Cháng'ān, c. 700–723. The Xuzangjing transmits the text from a Japanese manuscript line; in China the text was little circulated after the late Táng. The internal structure follows Kuījī's *Dàshū* lemma by lemma; Zhìzhōu typically opens each unit with a "疏..." (Kuījī says...) lemma, followed by his gloss.

## Other points of interest

The triplet *Qián jì* [[KR6o0023]], *Hòu jì* [[KR6o0024]], and *Shū chāo* [[KR6o0025]] forms the late-Táng Cí'ēn school's standard sub-commentarial apparatus on Kuījī's *Dàshū*; their close interweaving with Huìzhǎo's three works ([[KR6o0018]], [[KR6o0019]], [[KR6o0022]]) constitutes the entire body of the "Cí'ēn three masters" (慈恩三家 — Kuījī, Huìzhǎo, Zhìzhōu) commentarial output on Buddhist logic.
