# Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn xù shū 因明入正理論續疏
*Continuation of the Commentary on the Treatise on Entering Correct Reasoning*
by [[慧沼]] (Huìzhǎo, 述)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* "continuation commentary" (續疏 *xù shū*) by [[慧沼]] (Huìzhǎo, 651–714) supplying a sustained gloss on the lower portion of the *Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn* [[KR6o0003]] — i.e. continuing where Kuījī's *Dàshū* [[KR6o0008]] breaks off mid-juǎn. The source text is explicitly headed "因明入正理論疏下，正等寺沙門慧沼續" — "Commentary on the *Rù lùn*, lower [juǎn]: continued by Huìzhǎo, monk of Zhèngděngsì 正等寺"; this is the third element of Huìzhǎo's three-part completion of his teacher Kuījī's logic project, alongside the *Yìduàn* [[KR6o0018]] (disputed-question arbitration) and the *Yìzuǎnyào* [[KR6o0019]] (topical synthesis).

### Structural Division

The Xuzangjing recension of this text is not in CANWWW. The work continues the commentary on the *Rù lùn* [[KR6o0003]] from where the *Dàshū* breaks off.

### Abstract

The opening lemma "能立法不成者至猶如極微" (i.e. the section on the fallacy of the *néng-lì-fǎ bù chéng* — failure to establish the property of the predicate — running through the example "as in the case of *paramāṇu* [極微 atoms]") shows that the *Xù-shū* takes up the *Rù lùn* in the latter portion of its section on fallacies of the reason (似因). Huìzhǎo's gloss is closely aligned with Kuījī's manner; he treats the same sequence of disputed questions, frequently citing Kuījī's *Dà-shū* by name and resolving its ambiguities in the same Cí'ēn idiom. The text functions in practice as the missing third *juǎn* of the *Dà-shū*: it covers exactly the territory the *Dà-shū* leaves untreated. The colophon attributes the work to Huìzhǎo's residence at Zhèngděng-sì 正等寺 — the imperial monastery in Cháng'ān — placing the composition in his late period after his transfer from Zīzhōu, conventionally given as c. 705–714. The Xuzangjing transmits the text from a Japanese manuscript line; the work was rare in the Chinese tradition and appears to have circulated mainly through the Japanese Hossō school.

## Other points of interest

The status of the *Xùshū* as a "third *juǎn*" continuation of Kuījī's *Dàshū* is a striking feature of the Cí'ēn *yīnmíng* tradition: rather than re-commenting from scratch, Huìzhǎo treats his teacher's interrupted work as authoritative and writes himself in directly where the *Dàshū* fails. This pattern — of a disciple producing literally a second half of a teacher's incomplete commentary — is unusual in East Asian Buddhist scholarship.
