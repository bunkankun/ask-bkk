# Yīnmíng rù zhèng lǐ lùn shū chāo 因明入正理論疏抄
*Excerpts from the Commentary on the Treatise on Entering Correct Reasoning*
by [[智周]] (Zhìzhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A short one-*juǎn* digest by [[智周]], compiled (per the colophon "定水寺沙門智周撰") at the Dìngshuǐsì 定水寺. The Xuzangjing prefixes a sub-title "因明疏抄(略記)" — "Excerpts from the *Yīnmíng* commentary (abbreviated record)". The work is a condensed extract from the *Qián jì* [[KR6o0023]] and *Hòu jì* [[KR6o0024]], retaining the most important annotations on Kuījī's *Dàshū* [[KR6o0008]] in a portable single-volume format.

### Structural Division

The Xuzangjing recension is not in CANWWW. The work is a digest of the *Qián jì* [[KR6o0023]] and *Hòu jì* [[KR6o0024]].

### Abstract

The text opens with the same Confucius / Kǒngsāng 空桑 lemma as the *Hòu jì* [[KR6o0024]] — citing Yú Bǎo 于寶 *Sōushén jì* 搜神記 — but in much abbreviated form. The body of the *Shū chāo* runs through Kuījī's *Dàshū* in linear order, retaining only the most pithy of Zhìzhōu's earlier glosses; it is best read alongside the *Qián jì* and *Hòu jì* for which it functions as a compact appendix. The Dìngshuǐsì colophon places the work at one of Zhìzhōu's later residences (he served at multiple Cháng'ān monasteries during his career); composition is in the same general window as the longer "two records", c. 700–723.
