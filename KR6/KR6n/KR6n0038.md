# Chéng wéishí lùn shū yìyǎn 成唯識論疏義演
*Compiled Elaboration of the Commentary on the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[如理]] (Rúlǐ, 集)

## About the work

A twelve-fascicle compilation by the late-Tang commentator [[如理]] (Rúlǐ) elaborating on [[窺基]]'s [[KR6n0026]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shùjì* (T43n1830) — i.e. a *sub-commentary* on Kuījī's principal commentary, glossing the *shùjì* itself rather than the master text [[KR6n0016]]. The "*jí*" 集 ("compiled") in the attribution indicates that Rúlǐ draws together multiple earlier sub-commentaries, including the second-generation Cí'ēn material of [[慧沼]] and [[智周]], to elucidate Kuījī's text.

### Abstract

The work is an important late-Tang representative of the third-generation Cí'ēn commentarial tradition. Where the second generation ([[慧沼]] and [[智周]]) produced sub-commentaries on the master text itself, the third generation increasingly produced sub-commentaries on Kuījī's *shùjì*, treating it as a quasi-canonical text in its own right. Rúlǐ's *yìyǎn* is the most substantial extant example of this pattern; together with his shorter [[KR6n0039]] *yǎnmì shì*, it constitutes the principal late-Tang Cí'ēn corpus on the *Chéng wéishí lùn* commentarial tradition.

The dating bracket adopted (750–850) reflects scholarly uncertainty: the work post-dates [[智周]] (whose *yǎnmì* it draws on systematically) and pre-dates the late-Tang collapse of Cí'ēn institutional infrastructure following the Huìchāng 會昌 persecution of 845. The work survived in the *Xuzangjing* line but not in the Taishō.
