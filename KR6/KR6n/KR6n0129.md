# Dàshèng rùdào cìdì 大乘入道次第
*The Stages of Entering the Path in the Mahāyāna*
by [[智周]] (Zhìzhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A single-fascicle systematic treatise by [[智周]] **Zhìzhōu** (668–723), the third patriarch of the Cí'ēn-school, laying out the bodhisattva-path in stages (*cìdì* 次第 = *krama*) within the Yogācāra-Cí'ēn doctrinal framework. Preserved in the *Taishō* at T45n1864. Functions as an introductory practice-manual to the bodhisattva-path complementing Huìzhǎo's [[KR6n0127]] *Quànfā pútí xīn jí*.

Opens with a dedication verse: "I bow my head to the unequalled good charioteer / Who can pull beings out of the sinking suffering of birth-and-death; / The nature and characteristic of [his] empty-space [wisdom] are without coming-or-going — / Like a shadow following a form, responding to those to be transformed; / The single-flavour true characteristic, the wondrous sweet-dew…"

### Abstract

The *Rùdào cìdì* is a compact didactic treatment of the Mahāyāna path, designed to be intelligible to monks beginning Yogācāra study. It systematically presents the *path* (*dào* = *mārga*) in its standard Yogācāra five-stage form — *zīliángdào* 資糧道 (path of accumulation), *jiāxíngdào* 加行道 (path of preparation), *jiàndào* 見道 (path of seeing), *xiūdào* 修道 (path of cultivation), *jiūjìngdào* 究竟道 (path of completion) — together with the analyses of the *èrzhàng* (twofold obscuration) cut at each, and the *èrkōng* (twofold emptiness) realised at each. The work is characteristic of the third-generation Cí'ēn-school's effort to consolidate the doctrinal apparatus of [[玄奘]] and [[窺基]] into pedagogically accessible practice manuals.

The dating window 700–723 brackets Zhìzhōu's productive period as third patriarch of the Cí'ēn-school. The work was sufficiently authoritative that the Héxī Cí'ēn-school monk [[曇曠]] composed an extended *kāijué* (decisional supplement) on it — [[KR6n0130]] *Dàshèng rùdào cìdì kāijué* — at Dūnhuáng in the third quarter of the eighth century.
