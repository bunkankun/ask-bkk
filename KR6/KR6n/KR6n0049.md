# Chéng wéishí lùn yīnxiǎng bǔyí 成唯識論音響補遺
*Phonetic-and-Resonance Supplement to the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[智素]] (Zhìsù, 補遺)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle commentary on [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* by the early-Qīng monk [[智素]] (Zhìsù), accompanied by the structural outline [[KR6n0048]] *kē*. The "*yīnxiǎng bǔyí*" 音響補遺 ("phonetic-and-resonance supplement") title indicates that the work specifically supplements the late-Míng commentaries (especially Zhìxù's [[KR6n0047]] *guānxīn fǎyào*) with phonetic glosses and additional doctrinal-resonance material that those commentaries had passed over. Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X51n0826).

### Abstract

The *yīnxiǎng bǔyí* extends the late-Míng Yogācāra revival into the Qīng period. Where the late-Míng commentaries focused on doctrinal-systematic exposition, Zhìsù's work pays significant attention to philological detail — pronunciation, archaic vocabulary, and obscure technical terms — that the late-Míng commentators had treated only in passing. This makes the *bǔyí* a useful complementary reference text for any study of T1585 in conjunction with the late-Míng commentaries.

The dating bracket adopted (1644–1750) covers the early-to-mid Qīng. The work is preserved only in the *Xuzangjing* and not in the principal Qīng-period canonical editions.
