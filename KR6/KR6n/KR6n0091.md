# Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū 大乘法界無差別論疏
*Commentary on the Mahāyāna Treatise on the Non-Differentiation of the Dharmadhātu*
by [[法藏]] (Fǎzàng = Xiánshǒu Fǎzàng, 撰)

## About the work

[[法藏]]'s (Xiánshǒu Fǎzàng, 643–712) single-fascicle commentary on [[KR6n0089]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn* 大乘法界無差別論 (T31n1626) — the short Tathāgatagarbha treatise attributed to Sāramati and translated by [[提雲般若]] (Devaprajñā) c. 690–691. Fǎzàng was himself part of Devaprajñā's translation team and presumably composed this commentary shortly after the translation campaign. The work is a foundational Hua-yan-school text on the Tathāgatagarbha doctrine.

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T44N1838) lists [[KR6n0089]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn* (T31n1626) as the related text — i.e. the parent / commented work. The single fascicle treats Sāramati's twelve-aspect doctrine of *bodhicitta* and the doctrine of the non-differentiated *dharmadhātu* through Fǎzàng's characteristic Hua-yan philosophical framework.

### Abstract

Fǎzàng (the third patriarch of the Hua-yan 華嚴 school) was the author of more than thirty doctrinal works, including the foundational *Huáyán jīng tànxuán jì* 華嚴經探玄記 (T1733), the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* 五教章 (T1866), and the parabolic *Jīnshīzǐ zhāng* 金獅子章 (T1880). His commentary on the *Dharmadhātu-aviśeṣa* short treatise is one of his shorter doctrinal works but is theologically central to the Hua-yan-school synthesis of Tathāgatagarbha and Avataṃsaka doctrines. Fǎzàng treats Sāramati's text as a key locus for the Hua-yan position that the *dharmadhātu* — the realm of all phenomena viewed in their ultimate inter-penetrating nature — is identifiable with the universal Buddha-nature.

The dating bracket adopted (691–712) covers the period from the completion of Devaprajñā's translation (in which Fǎzàng participated) to Fǎzàng's death. The colophons do not give a precise date.

The work is one of the principal Tang-dynasty Tathāgatagarbha-tradition commentaries and is the textual foundation for the Sòng-period sub-commentaries [[KR6n0092]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū lǐngyào chāo kē* and [[KR6n0093]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū lǐngyào chāo* by [[普觀]] of the Sòng dynasty.
