# Chéng wéishí lùn yìyùn 成唯識論義蘊
*Doctrinal Substance of the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[道邑]] (Dàoyì, 撰)

## About the work

A five-fascicle commentary on [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* by the late-seventh- or eighth-century Cí'ēn commentator [[道邑]] (Dàoyì). Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X49n0814). The "*yìyùn*" 義蘊 ("doctrinal substance / interior meaning") title indicates a substantive doctrinal commentary that goes beyond glossary into systematic exposition of the philosophical implications of T1585.

### Abstract

The text is one of several second-tier Cí'ēn commentaries on T1585 that survive only through the *Xuzangjing* and not the Taishō. Internal evidence suggests Dàoyì draws systematically on [[窺基]]'s [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì* but apparently not on [[智周]]'s [[KR6n0032]] *yǎnmì*; the work is therefore conventionally placed in the late seventh or early eighth century, perhaps roughly contemporary with [[慧沼]]. The dating bracket adopted (700–800) reflects this uncertainty.

The commentary is doctrinally orthodox-Cí'ēn but distinguishes itself by spending more time than Kuījī's *shùjì* on the *yīnmíng* 因明 (Buddhist logic / *hetuvidyā*) implications of the *Chéng wéishí lùn* arguments — possibly reflecting Dàoyì's institutional location in a Cí'ēn-affiliated *yīnmíng* lecture-tradition.
