# Chéng wéishí lùn liàojiǎn 成唯識論料簡
*Critical Selections from the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

A short two-fascicle work by [[窺基]] presenting selected critical issues from [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* in question-and-answer format. The "*liào jiǎn*" 料簡 ("critical selection / sifting of materials") title indicates a focused doctrinal-critical study of disputed points, complementing the more general [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì* and [[KR6n0029]] *zhǎngzhōng shūyào*. Preserved only in the *Xuzangjing* (X48n0806) and not in the Taishō.

### Abstract

The text is one of two short works by [[窺基]] preserved in the *Xuzangjing* but not in the Taishō — the other being [[KR6n0034]] *Chéng wéishí lùn bié chāo* 成唯識論別抄 (X48n0808). Both preserve material that did not enter the great commentaries (the *shùjì* and the lost full *shū*) but that Kuījī treated as worth recording for circulation. The dating bracket (659–682) follows from his teaching career at Cí'ēnsì.

The *liàojiǎn* and *biéchāo* together constitute the principal source for our understanding of how Kuījī conceived of the doctrinal cruces of the *Chéng wéishí lùn* outside the systematic commentary. Several positions taken in these works are at variance with — or substantially developed beyond — the corresponding passages in the *shùjì*, providing important evidence for the development of Kuījī's mature thought.
