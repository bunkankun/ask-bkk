# Chéng wéishí lùn yǎnmì shì 成唯識論演秘釋
*Exposition of the Esoteric Elaboration of the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[如理]] (Rúlǐ, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle sub-commentary by [[如理]] on [[智周]]'s [[KR6n0032]] *Chéng wéishí lùn yǎnmì* (T43n1833). Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X50n0816). Together with the longer [[KR6n0038]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shū yìyǎn* on Kuījī's *shùjì*, the *yǎnmì shì* belongs to the late-Tang third-generation Cí'ēn commentarial tradition.

### Abstract

The work glosses the more difficult passages of Zhìzhōu's *yǎnmì*, focusing on the technical-philosophical questions of the *manas*-*ālayavijñāna* relation, the *xiànxíng* / *bīja* doctrine, and the relations among the three natures. As with [[KR6n0038]], the dating window adopted (750–850) reflects the conventional placement of [[如理]] in the late-Tang Cí'ēn lineage between the second-generation patriarchs and the Huìchāng-era collapse.
