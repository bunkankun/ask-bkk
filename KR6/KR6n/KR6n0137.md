# Bā shí guījǔ zhíjiě 八識規矩直解
*Direct Explanation of the Eight-Consciousnesses Verses*
by [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 解)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng commentary on the *Bā shí guījǔ sòng* 八識規矩頌 by [[智旭]] **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** (1599–1655). Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0895. Part of Zhìxù's *zhíjiě* set of compact direct expositions of the late-Míng Yogācāra core curriculum, alongside [[KR6n0106]] *Bǎifǎ míngmén lùn zhíjiě*, [[KR6n0115]] *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn zhíjiě*, [[KR6n0117]] *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn shì zhíjiě*, and [[KR6n0145]] *Xiàngzōng bā yào zhíjiě*.

### Prefaces

Authorship line: "*Sānzàng fǎshī Xuánzàng zuò* 三藏法師玄奘作 (composed by Tripiṭaka Master Xuánzàng) / *Ǒuyì shāmén Zhìxù jiě* 蕅益沙門智旭解 (explicated by the Ǒuyì monk Zhìxù)." No separate preface; the text opens directly with the first verse ("*xìngjìng xiànliàng tōng sān xìng* 性境現量通三性") and the running commentary.

### Abstract

The *Zhíjiě* is the most concise and pedagogically polished of the late-Míng *guījǔ* commentaries — designed for direct use in teaching. Zhìxù reads each verse with a brief, doctrinally precise gloss, with the broader integrative *xìngxiàng* synthesis kept in the background and the foreground occupied by clear definition and example. Where Hānshān's [[KR6n0135]] *Tōngshuō* opens the text out into the *yīxīn* doctrine and Pǔtài's [[KR6n0131]] *Bǔzhù* loads the verses with the full Cí'ēn-school technical apparatus, Zhìxù's *Zhíjiě* finds the middle path: each verse is glossed precisely enough for the doctrine to be unambiguous, but compactly enough for the student to retain the verse as a mnemonic. This is the *guījǔ* commentary most frequently used in modern Chinese Buddhist seminaries.

The dating window 1630–1655 brackets Zhìxù's productive period after his major doctrinal turn around 1631.
