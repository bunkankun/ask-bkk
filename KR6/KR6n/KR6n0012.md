# Dìchí yìjì juǎn dìsì 地持義記卷第四
*Notes on the Meaning of the Bodhisattvabhūmi (Fascicle Four)*

## About the work

A single fragmentary fascicle (the surviving fourth *juǎn*) of an anonymous Sui-Tang exegetical commentary on the *Púsà dìchí jīng* ([[KR6n0003]], T30n1581) — Dharmakṣema's translation of the *Bodhisattvabhūmi*. Recovered from the Dūnhuáng cave-deposit and edited as Taishō No. 2803 in the Dūnhuáng-text volume (T85). No author is named in the manuscript and the work cannot be confidently associated with any known commentary tradition; on internal grounds it is sometimes ascribed to the *Dìlùn* 地論 / *Shèlùn* 攝論 milieu, possibly the school of [[淨影慧遠]] Suí Huìyuǎn (523–592), but the attribution is not secure.

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T85N2803) lists [[KR6n0003]] *Púsà dìchí jīng* 菩薩地持經 (T30n1581) as the parent text. The fragment covers material from the third or fourth *pǐn* of T1581.

### Abstract

The text is one of a small group of pre-Xuánzàng exegetical fragments on the *Bodhisattvabhūmi* recovered from Dūnhuáng (alongside Huìyuǎn's *Dìchí lùn yìjì* [[KR6n0013]] = X39n0704 and several smaller fragments). It is important as direct evidence of the early-medieval Northern Chinese reception of the *Bodhisattvabhūmi* tradition before this material was effectively superseded by [[玄奘]]'s complete translation of the *Yogācārabhūmi* ([[KR6n0001]]) in 648.

The dating bracket adopted here (600–800) reflects scholarly uncertainty: the work could be Sui (a contemporary of Huìyuǎn's *Dìchí lùn yìjì*) or it could be early Tang, predating or paralleling the introduction of Xuánzàng's superior version. The textual evidence does not permit a tighter bracket. The Dynasty tag 唐 is conventional rather than firmly attested.
