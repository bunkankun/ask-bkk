# Dàshèng fǎyuàn yìlín zhāng bǔquè 大乘法苑義林章補闕
*Filling the Gaps in the Compendium of Doctrinal-Forest Essays*
by [[慧沼]] (Zīēnsì Huìzhǎo, 撰)

## About the work

A three-fascicle Táng Cí'ēn-school sub-commentary by [[慧沼]] **Huìzhǎo** (650–714), Kuījī's principal disciple and the second patriarch of the Cí'ēn-school, on his teacher's [[KR6n0124]] *Dàshèng fǎyuàn yìlín zhāng* 大乘法苑義林章. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0882 (= ZB6n0113). The function-marker *bǔquè* 補闕 ("filling the gaps") indicates that the work supplies essays on topics where Kuījī's parent compendium was either silent or compressed beyond clarity.

### Structural Division

The text is structured as a series of additional doctrinal essays, the surviving fascicle 4 covering the *Sānkē zhāng* 三科章 (essay on the three categories — *skandha*, *āyatana*, *dhātu*), *Défēidé zhāng* 得非得章 (the essay on attainment and non-attainment), *Kōngyì zhāng* 空義章 (the essay on emptiness), and *Shíèr zhī zhāng* 十二支章 (the essay on the twelve links of dependent origination). Fascicles 7 and 8 of the source survival cover further specialised topics. The Manji recension preserves only fascicles 4, 7, and 8 — the bulk of the original three-fascicle structure.

### Prefaces

Authorship line: "*Dàyúnsì shāmén Huìzhǎo zhuàn* 大雲寺沙門慧沼撰" — by the Dàyúnsì monk Huìzhǎo. The text opens directly with the additional essays; there is no separate authorial preface in the surviving recension.

### Abstract

The *Bǔquè* is the single most important Cí'ēn-school sub-commentary on the *Yìlín zhāng*, supplementing Kuījī's coverage with detailed expositions of doctrinal topics (the *abhidharma*-school traditional categories of *sānkē*, the analytic of *dé* / *fēidé* in *abhidharma* psychology, the Yogācāra reading of the twelve-link *pratītyasamutpāda*) where Kuījī's text is either silent or compressed. The work is a key witness to the second-generation Cí'ēn-school's effort to consolidate Kuījī's compendium into a complete doctrinal reference.

[[慧沼]] Huìzhǎo (650–714, *zì* Xuánshū 玄樞, sobriquet **Zīēnsì** 淄恩寺 / Sūshēngsì 蘇生寺) was Kuījī's chief disciple and the second patriarch of the Cí'ēn-school, succeeding Kuījī at *Cí'ēnsì* and later moving to *Dàyúnsì* 大雲寺. His full corpus includes [[KR6n0127]] *Quànfā pútí xīn jí* 勸發菩提心集 (T45n1862) and [[KR6n0128]] *Néng xiǎn zhōngbiān huìrì lùn* 能顯中邊慧日論 (T45n1863). The dating window 682 (Kuījī's death) to 714 (Huìzhǎo's death) is the period within which Huìzhǎo could have composed the *Bǔquè*.
