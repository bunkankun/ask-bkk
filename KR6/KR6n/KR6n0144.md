# Xiàngzōng bā yào jiě 相宗八要解
*Explication of the Eight Essential [Texts] of the Characteristics-School*
by [[明昱]] (Gāoyuán Míngyù, 著)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng commentary by [[明昱]] **Gāoyuán Míngyù** on the eight foundational Yogācāra texts compiled by [[洪恩]] in [[KR6n0143]] *Xiàngzōng bā yào* 相宗八要. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0900. The function-marker *zhe* 著 ("authored") is here equivalent to *jiě* (explication); the work supplies a running commentary on each of the eight texts in the *Bā yào* curriculum.

### Structural Division

The text follows the canonical *Bā yào* sequence (cf. [[KR6n0143]]) — *Yīnmíng rù zhènglǐ lùn* → *Bǎifǎ míngmén lùn* → *Bā shí sòng* → *Wéishí sānshí lùn* → *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn* → *Liù líhé shì* → *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn shì* → *Zhēn wéishí liàng* — providing a detailed Cí'ēn-school philological reading of each.

### Prefaces

No separate authorial preface in the extant edition; the text opens directly into the running commentary on the first of the eight texts.

### Abstract

The *Jiě* is Míngyù's principal late-Míng *Bā yào* commentary, presenting the eight foundational Yogācāra texts as an integrated reading-course in the strict Cí'ēn-school philological tradition that he champions throughout his corpus. Where Zhìxù's parallel [[KR6n0145]] *Bā yào zhíjiě* tends to read the texts integratively in light of his Tiāntái-Yogācāra synthesis, Míngyù's *Jiě* sticks closely to the Cí'ēn-school orthodox readings transmitted through Pǔtài and consolidated by Míngyù's own work. The *Jiě* together with Zhìxù's *Zhíjiě* are the two principal commentaries on Hóngēn's *Bā yào* compilation.

The dating window 1590–1620 brackets Míngyù's productive Wànlì period.
