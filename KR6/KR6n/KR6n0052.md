# Wéishí sānshí lùn yàoshì 唯識三十論要釋
*Essential Exegesis of the Thirty Verses on Consciousness-Only*
(anonymous Dūnhuáng manuscript)

## About the work

A single-fascicle anonymous commentary on Vasubandhu's *Triṃśikā* — the verse base preserved as [[KR6n0022]] *Wéishí sānshí lùn sòng* 唯識三十論頌 (T31n1586) — recovered from Dūnhuáng and edited into volume 85 of the *Taishō shinshū daizōkyō* (T85n2804). The Taishō header notes "[cf. Nos. 1585, 1586]," signalling that the text relates simultaneously to the *Chéng wéishí lùn* and the verse base.

### Structural Division

CANWWW does not assign internal structure for T85N2804. The manuscript breaks off without colophon and is internally organised as a verse-by-verse paraphrase.

### Abstract

The *yàoshì* belongs to the cluster of Yogācāra commentaries surviving only in Dūnhuáng manuscripts (collectively gathered in T85, the *Gǔyìbù* 古逸部 / "ancient lost works" division). It opens with the standard Cí'ēn-style exposition of the four "*yīnyuán*" 因緣 (causes, conditions) for the composition of the *Triṃśikā*: refuting the various heterodox positions that absolutise either matter or mind, and establishing the consciousness-only doctrine through analysis of the eight consciousnesses, the three natures, and the three non-natures. The commentary follows the Xuánzàng-Cí'ēn doctrinal framework — citing the *Yújiā shī dì lùn* and *Xiǎnyáng shèngjiào lùn* explicitly — but is not by [[窺基]] himself, since it differs in technical vocabulary and citation pattern from the authentic Cí'ēn corpus.

The dating bracket (700–900) reflects the post-Xuánzàng / Cí'ēn-school technical vocabulary (*requiring* a *terminus post quem* in the late seventh century) combined with the typical date range for Dūnhuáng manuscript material in this textual class. The author was likely a Cí'ēn-school monk operating in the Héxī corridor or in central Cháng'ān during the high or middle Táng.

The text is one of several Dūnhuáng *Wéishí*-school commentaries that have substantially expanded modern understanding of the diffusion and reception of Cí'ēn Yogācāra in the centuries between [[窺基]] and the Sòng — see Ueyama Daishun 上山大峻 on Dūnhuáng Yogācāra materials.
