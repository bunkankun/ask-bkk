# Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū lǐngyào chāo kē 大乘法界無差別論疏領要鈔科
*Topical Outline for the "Notes Capturing the Essentials of the Commentary on the Mahāyāna Treatise on the Non-Differentiation of the Dharmadhātu"*
by [[普觀]] (Pǔguān, 錄)

## About the work

A single-fascicle structural-outline (*kē* 科) by the Sòng-dynasty scholar-monk [[普觀]] (Pǔguān, *fl.* 1100–1180), preliminary to his more substantial sub-commentary [[KR6n0093]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū lǐngyào chāo* 大乘法界無差別論疏領要鈔 (X46n0787). The "kē" 科 genre in Chinese Buddhist scholasticism is a topical-outline / synopsis text, providing a structural framework for reading the body of the commentary; here it summarises the structure of [[法藏]]'s [[KR6n0091]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū* 大乘法界無差別論疏 (T44n1838) and provides the scaffolding for [[普觀]]'s own *lǐngyào* commentary.

### Structural Division

CANWWW does not preserve a structural division for X46n0786. The single fascicle is itself an outline document — its content is the structural skeleton of [[KR6n0093]].

### Abstract

The *kē* + *chāo* (outline + extracts) is a standard Sòng-period scholastic format: the *kē* provides the topical structure, the *chāo* (notes) elaborates on each topical heading. This pair of texts ([[KR6n0092]] kē + [[KR6n0093]] chāo) is the principal Sòng-dynasty sub-commentary on Fǎzàng's *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū* and one of the few sustained Sòng-period engagements with the Tathāgatagarbha-tradition treatises in Chinese Buddhist scholarship.

The dating window adopted follows the established floruit for [[普觀]] (c. 1100–1180) — biographical details for him are sparse; the catalogs preserve only his honorific *Guāngyán Wújì dàshī* 光嚴無際大師.
