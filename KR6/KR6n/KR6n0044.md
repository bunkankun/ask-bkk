# Chéng wéishí lùn jíjiě 成唯識論集解
*Compiled Explanations of the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[通潤]] (Tōngrùn, 集解)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle commentary on [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* by the late-Míng Lotus-Sūtra-and-Yogācāra master [[通潤]] (Tōngrùn / Yīyǔ 一雨, 1565–1624). The "*jíjiě*" 集解 ("compiled explanations") title indicates a synthetic commentary drawing together the Tang Cí'ēn tradition (especially Kuījī's [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì*) with Tōngrùn's own elaborations. Preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X50n0821).

### Abstract

[[通潤]] is best known for his Lotus Sūtra commentary [[KR6d0080]] *Fǎhuá jīng dàkuǎn*, but his self-designation as "Master of the Two-Lèngyán [Sūtras]" 二楞主人 indicates that the *Lèngyán jīng* 楞嚴經 (Śūraṃgama-sūtra) was equally important to him — and through that text, he was inevitably engaged with the consciousness-only doctrine of the *Chéng wéishí lùn*. The *jíjiě* is the principal product of this Yogācāra engagement.

The dating bracket adopted (1605–1624) covers Tōngrùn's mature period, between the publication of his earliest Lotus-related commentaries and his death in 1624. The work belongs to the same late-Míng Yogācāra revival as Míngyù's [[KR6n0043]] *súquán* and Wáng Kěntáng's [[KR6n0045]] *zhèngyì*, and shares with them the program of restoring Cí'ēn doctrinal study in late-Míng Buddhism.

The *jíjiě* is somewhat more eclectic than the other late-Míng commentaries on T1585: Tōngrùn brings to the *Chéng wéishí lùn* the same broad-ranging late-Míng synthesis that characterises his Lotus-Sūtra and Śūraṃgama work, including significant Tiāntái-doctrinal material that the strictly Cí'ēn commentaries (e.g. Míngyù's) keep at arm's length.
