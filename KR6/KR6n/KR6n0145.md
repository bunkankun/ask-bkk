# Xiàngzōng bā yào zhíjiě 相宗八要直解
*Direct Explanation of the Eight Essential [Texts] of the Characteristics-School*
by [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 解)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng commentary by [[智旭]] **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** (1599–1655) on the eight foundational Yogācāra texts compiled by [[洪恩]] in [[KR6n0143]] *Xiàngzōng bā yào* 相宗八要. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X55n0901. The capstone text in Zhìxù's *zhíjiě* set on the late-Míng Yogācāra core curriculum, alongside [[KR6n0106]] *Bǎifǎ míngmén lùn zhíjiě*, [[KR6n0115]] *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn zhíjiě*, [[KR6n0117]] *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn shì zhíjiě*, and [[KR6n0137]] *Bā shí guījǔ zhíjiě*.

### Structural Division

The text follows the *Bā yào* sequence (cf. [[KR6n0143]]). The opening of the source text begins with section No. 901-8 — the *Liù líhé shì fǎshì lüèjiě* 六離合釋法式略解 ("Brief Explanation of the Pattern of the Six Compound-Word Analyses") — illustrating Zhìxù's distinctive approach: he often re-orders the *Bā yào* sequence to bring philological aids forward, opening with the *Liù líhé* compound-analysis which he treats as a propaedeutic technical aid for parsing the rest of the doctrine. The six analyses are: *yīzhǔ shì* 依主釋 (*tatpuruṣa*), *chíyè shì* 持業釋 (*karmadhāraya*), *yǒucái shì* 有財釋 (*bahuvrīhi*), *xiāngwéi shì* 相違釋 (*dvandva*), *dàishù shì* 帶數釋 (*dvigu*), *línjìn shì* 隣近釋 (proximity-modification — a sub-type of *bahuvrīhi*).

### Prefaces

Authorship line embedded in the section header: "*Liù líhé shì fǎshì lüèjiě* — *Ǒuyì Zhìxù lüèjiě* 蕅益智旭略解." No separate authorial preface; the work proceeds text-by-text through the eight texts of the *Bā yào* with Zhìxù's compact running explication.

### Abstract

Zhìxù's *Zhíjiě* on the *Bā yào* is the single most influential late-Míng Yogācāra teaching aid: by providing a unified compact commentary across the entire eight-text curriculum, Zhìxù effectively became the standard reference-author for late-Míng and Qīng Yogācāra study. His distinctive contribution is the integrative reading: each of the eight texts is presented in light of the others, with cross-references that build up an integrated picture of the Cí'ēn doctrinal system in its compact didactic form. The work is the principal vehicle by which Zhìxù's reading of the late-Míng Yogācāra revival reached the Qīng monastic educational system.

The dating window 1630–1655 brackets Zhìxù's productive period after his major doctrinal turn around 1631.
