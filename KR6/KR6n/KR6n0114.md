# Guān suǒyuányuán lùn huìshì 觀所緣緣論會釋
*Synthetic Commentary on the Investigation into the Object-Condition*
by [[明昱]] (Gāoyuán Míngyù, 會釋)

## About the work

A single-fascicle late-Míng commentary by [[明昱]] Gāoyuán Míngyù (the Wàn-lì-era Yogācāra revivalist) on Dignāga's [[KR6n0112]] *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn* 觀所緣緣論 (T31n1624, in [[玄奘]]'s translation). Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* 卍續藏 at X51n0830. The function-marker *huìshì* 會釋 ("synthetic explication") indicates a commentary that gathers together (*huì*) and reconciles the various exegetical lines that had developed around the text — both in the Indian Yogācāra (Dharmapāla, etc.) and in the post-Kuījī Cí'ēn-school tradition.

### Prefaces

Authorship line: "*Chénnà púsà* 陳那菩薩 **造**; *Táng sānzàng fǎshī Xuánzàng* 唐三藏法師玄奘 **譯**; *Míng Shǔ shāmén Míngyù* 明蜀沙門明昱 **會釋**." No separate preface; opens directly with the running commentary on the title. Míngyù glosses the title term-by-term: *guān* 觀 = "investigation"; *suǒyuán* 所緣 = "having the meaning of an object" (*yǒu jìng yì* 有境義); *yuán* 緣 = "having the meaning of being able to draw forth a perception" (*néng yǐnshēng shí yì* 能引生識義). The hyphen between the two *yuán* (which Chinese readers sometimes obscure) is the entire substance of the work: only what fulfils *both* meanings — being a real cognitive object *and* causally producing the perception that takes it as object — is properly called the *suǒyuányuán* (object-condition).

### Abstract

The *Huìshì* presents Dignāga's argument as a binary diagnostic: any candidate for the role of *suǒyuányuán* must satisfy both the *yǒujìng* (being-an-object) condition and the *yǐnshēng* (causally-generating) condition; the *paramāṇu* satisfies the second but not the first; the aggregated cluster satisfies the first but not the second; therefore neither external candidate works, and the *suǒyuányuán* must be the inner *xiāngfēn* 相分 (image-portion) of consciousness itself — the canonical Yogācāra *vijñapti-mātra* conclusion. Míngyù's commentary works through this symmetric refutation in detail and integrates the Cí'ēn-school treatment found in the *Chéng wéishí lùn* 成唯識論 (T31n1585) and in [[窺基]]'s lost *Shū* on the *Guān suǒyuányuán lùn*.

The dating window 1590–1620 brackets Míngyù's productive Wàn-lì-era period; this is one of his three commentaries on the *Guānsuǒyuán* corpus (alongside [[KR6n0116]] *Shìjì* — his commentary on Dharmapāla's *vṛtti* — and the *Bǎifǎ míngmén lùn zhuìyán* [[KR6n0105]]). Together they form Míngyù's principal contribution to the late-Míng Yogācāra revival's recovery of Dignāga's *pramāṇa* corpus.
