# Chéng wéishí lùn zìkǎo 成唯識論自攷
*Self-Examined Records on the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[大惠]] (Dàhuì, 錄)

## About the work

A ten-fascicle commentary on [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* by the late-Míng monk [[大惠]] (Dàhuì), preserved in the *Xuzangjing* (X51n0823). The "*zìkǎo*" 自攷 ("self-examined / personally collated") title and the *lù* 錄 ("recorded") attribution together indicate a commentary developed over an extended period of private study and only later released for wider circulation — a common pattern in late-Míng monastic scholarship.

### Abstract

The *zìkǎo* belongs to the late-Míng Yogācāra revival cluster alongside [[KR6n0043]] (Míngyù), [[KR6n0044]] (Tōngrùn), [[KR6n0045]] (Wáng Kěntáng), and [[KR6n0047]] (Zhìxù). Together these works constitute the principal monument of the late-Míng restoration of Cí'ēn doctrinal study, undertaken in the wake of the rediscovery of the canonical Cí'ēn corpus through Japanese Hossō and Korean reception.

The dating bracket adopted (1573–1620) covers the Wànlì era as a whole, since no precise internal dating evidence is available for the work. The text reads as a methodical lemma-by-lemma commentary, drawing principally on Kuījī's [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì* but supplementing this with material from Huìzhāo's [[KR6n0030]] *liǎoyì dēng* and Zhìzhōu's [[KR6n0032]] *yǎnmì*.
