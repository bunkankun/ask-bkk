# Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū lǐngyào chāo 大乘法界無差別論疏領要鈔
*Notes Capturing the Essentials of the Commentary on the Mahāyāna Treatise on the Non-Differentiation of the Dharmadhātu*
by [[普觀]] (Pǔguān, 述)

## About the work

A two-fascicle Sòng-dynasty sub-commentary by [[普觀]] on [[法藏]]'s [[KR6n0091]] *Dàshèng fǎjiè wú chābié lùn shū* (T44n1838) — the Tang Hua-yan-school commentary on Sāramati's *Dharmadhātu-aviśeṣa-śāstra* ([[KR6n0089]]). The companion *kē* (topical outline) is [[KR6n0092]] (X46n0786). Pǔguān's pair of works is one of the principal Sòng-period engagements with the Tathāgatagarbha treatises in Chinese Buddhist scholarship.

### Structural Division

CANWWW does not preserve a structural division for X46n0787. The two *juǎn* track [[法藏]]'s commentary section by section — using the topical structure outlined in the parallel [[KR6n0092]] *kē*.

### Abstract

The Sòng dynasty saw a partial revival of Hua-yan-school doctrinal scholarship after the late-Tang persecutions and the dispersal of the Tang Hua-yan-school institutional base. Pǔguān's sub-commentary belongs to this revival: it engages with [[法藏]]'s text not merely as historical doctrine but as living philosophical resource, applying Sòng-period scholastic methods (the *kē* + *chāo* paired format) to the *Dharmadhātu-aviśeṣa* tradition. The work is one of the few Sòng-dynasty sub-commentaries on the Tathāgatagarbha treatises (most Sòng Buddhist scholastic energy went into the Tian-tai and Chán traditions), and is a valuable witness to the continued Hua-yan-school doctrinal interest in the post-Tang period.

The dating window adopted (1100–1180) follows the conventional floruit for Pǔguān; specific composition dates are not preserved in the colophon. Pǔguān's other principal surviving work is the [[KR6i0368]] *Lánpén jīng shū huìgǔ tōngjīn jì* 蘭盆經疏會古通今記, also a sub-commentary in the *kē + jì* format.
