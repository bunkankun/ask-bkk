# Chéng wéishí lùn biéchāo 成唯識論別抄
*Separate Excerpts from the Demonstration of Consciousness-Only*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

A four-fascicle compilation by [[窺基]] of supplementary notes on [[KR6n0016]] *Chéng wéishí lùn* — material that, like that in the companion [[KR6n0033]] *liàojiǎn*, did not enter the principal commentary [[KR6n0026]] *shùjì* but was preserved in circulation. The "*bié chāo*" 別抄 ("separate excerpts") title indicates that the text is in part an aggregation of marginal notes and lecture-records assembled by Kuījī (or by his disciples) outside the formal commentarial framework.

### Abstract

The *biéchāo* is preserved only in the *Xuzangjing* (X48n0808). Like its companion [[KR6n0033]], it preserves Kuījī's working notes on doctrinal cruces of the *Chéng wéishí lùn* — particularly on questions of the *xiànxíng* / *bīja* relation, the *manas*-*ālayavijñāna* relation, and the bodhisattva-bhūmi correlations — that fell outside the topical organisation of the principal commentaries.

Modern Yogācāra scholarship has paid increasing attention to the *biéchāo* and *liàojiǎn* together as evidence for Kuījī's doctrinal development across his teaching career: positions taken in these working notes sometimes anticipate or refine those in the *shùjì*, suggesting (Yoshimura, 2013) that Kuījī continued revising his understanding of the *Chéng wéishí lùn* throughout the 660s and 670s. The dating window adopted (659–682) covers Kuījī's full teaching career.
