# Púsà shànjiè jīng 菩薩善戒經
*Sūtra on the Bodhisattva's Excellent Discipline (one-fascicle abridgment)*
by [[求那跋摩]] (Qiúnàbámó / Guṇavarman, 譯)

## About the work

A short, single-fascicle text traditionally counted as a separate work but in fact an extract from the larger nine-fascicle *Púsà shànjiè jīng* ([[KR6n0004]], T30n1582) of the same translator, [[求那跋摩]] (Guṇavarman, 367–431). The single fascicle preserves the *Púsàjiè běn* 菩薩戒本 — the bodhisattva-precept core — extracted from the larger translation for liturgical use in the Liú-Sòng monastic communities. It thus stands in the same relation to T1582 as Dharmakṣema's *Púsà jièběn* 菩薩戒本 ([[KR6k0020]], T30n1500) stands to the *Púsà dìchí jīng* ([[KR6n0003]], T30n1581).

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T30N1583) places the text alongside [[KR6n0001]], [[KR6n0003]], and [[KR6n0004]] as a related text in the *Bodhisattvabhūmi* group. The single fascicle has no formal subdivisions; its content is the recitation-text of the bodhisattva-precepts.

### Abstract

[[求那跋摩]] arrived in Jiànkāng 建康 in 431 CE (Yuánjiā 元嘉 8) and died there later the same year. The *Chū sānzàng jì jí* 出三藏記集 (T2145) reports both T1582 and T1583 as products of his Jǐyuánsì 祇洹寺 translation work in that single year. Tradition treats T1583 as the *jièběn* (precept-text) extracted for chanting at the bodhisattva-precept ceremony; the parallel later passage in T1582 is generally considered the textual source.

The text was widely used in the early Northern and Southern dynasties for the conferral of the bodhisattva-precepts and is repeatedly cited in early *Púsàjiè* commentaries. Its short length and standalone format made it the standard short *jièběn* for the Liú-Sòng and early Liáng monasteries; it was eventually superseded in normative status by the *Fànwǎng jīng* 梵網經 ([[KR6k0017]]) but never entirely replaced in the Northern transmission.

## Translations and research

*No substantial monographic study of T1583 in isolation; covered as an appendix to the literature on T1582 and the Bodhisattvabhūmi (see [[KR6n0004]]).*

## Other points of interest

The two *Púsà shànjiè jīng* texts (T1582 and T1583) together with Dharmakṣema's *Púsà jièběn* (T1500, [[KR6k0020]]) and Xuánzàng's later [[KR6k0121]] *Púsàjiè běn* (T1501) form the Yogācāra strand of bodhisattva-precept literature in the Chinese canon, parallel to and in productive tension with the *Fànwǎng jīng* tradition.
