# Wéishí èrshí lùn shùjì 唯識二十論述記
*Commentary on the Twenty Verses on Consciousness-Only*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

[[窺基]]'s lemma-by-lemma commentary on [[KR6n0055]] *Wéishí èrshí lùn* 唯識二十論 (T31n1590), [[玄奘]]'s translation of Vasubandhu's *Viṃśatikā-vṛtti*. Together with [[KR6n0026]] *Chéng wéishí lùn shùjì* (T43n1830) on the *Triṃśikā*, this *shùjì* completes Kuījī's two principal Cí'ēn commentaries on the canonical "two treatises" (*èr lùn* 二論) of Yogācāra *Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi*. Two fascicles, frequently subdivided into upper and lower halves.

### Structural Division

CANWWW (T43N1834) lists [[KR6n0055]] *Wéishí èrshí lùn* (T31n1590) as the related text. The two *juǎn* track the twenty *kārikā* of Vasubandhu's verse text in sequence, with Kuījī expanding each verse into an extended doctrinal analysis.

### Abstract

The *shùjì* is one of three principal works by [[窺基]] on the *Viṃśatikā* — alongside the lost full *shū* 疏 (mentioned in [[KR2m0019]] *Sòng gāosēng zhuàn* j. 4) and a topical compendium also lost. The surviving *shùjì* is the canonical Cí'ēn commentary and was transmitted to Heian Japan as a foundational Hossō study text.

Kuījī's exegesis draws extensively on Dharmapāla's [[KR6n0056]] *Chéng wéishí bǎoshēng lùn* (T31n1591) — in places nearly verbatim, suggesting Kuījī had access to the Dharmapāla commentary in some form before Yìjìng's later translation, perhaps via Xuánzàng's notes or through the parallel Sanskrit material brought back from India. The commentary expounds, on Vasubandhu's twenty verses, the standard Yogācāra refutation of external-object realism, the elaborate analysis of *vāsanā* (*xíqì* 習氣) as the operative cause of inter-stream agreement, and the technical Cí'ēn doctrine of the four *aspects* of cognition (*jiànfēn*, *xiāngfēn*, *zìzhèngfēn*, *zhèngzìzhèngfēn* 見相自證證自證分).

The dating bracket (661–682) covers Kuījī's productive teaching period from the publication of Xuánzàng's *Viṃśatikā* translation through to his death. The work is heavily cited in subsequent East Asian Yogācāra literature; the principal sub-commentary is [[KR6n0083]] *Zájí lùn shùjì* by Kuījī himself (on a related work) and the various Heian and Kamakura Hossō school sub-commentaries on the *èrshí lùn shùjì* (e.g. by Zenju 善珠).
