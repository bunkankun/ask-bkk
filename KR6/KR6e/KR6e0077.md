# Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng kē 華嚴一乘教義分齊章科
*Sectional Outline of the Treatise on the Doctrinal Categories of the One-Vehicle Teaching of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[師會]] (Shīhuì, 述)

## About the work

This one-fascicle text is the structural-outline (*kē* 科) by [[師會]] Shīhuì of [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's *[[KR6e0074|Wǔ jiào zhāng 五教章]]* (T1866). It serves as the navigational map for Shīhuì's own *[[KR6e0078|Fù gǔ jì 復古記]]* (X998) and, by extension, for the parent text of Fǎzàng. The hierarchical heading-format makes the conceptual structure of Fǎzàng's doxographical schema accessible at a glance.

### Prefaces

*The work opens with the title-line "華嚴一乘教義分齊章科 / 玉峯沙門　師會　述" — "Sectional outline of the Wǔ jiào zhāng 五教章. Composed by the śramaṇa Shīhuì of Yùfēng 玉峯."*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1145 – 1170) reflects [[師會]] Shīhuì's mature scholarly period at the Yùfēng 玉峯 monastery. As with all *kē* outlines, the work's primary function is pedagogical and navigational: it provides the structural skeleton of the larger treatise, enabling students to track its doctrinal articulation. Shīhuì's particular *kē* of the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* preserves his own reading of the work's structure — sometimes differing in minor structural divisions from [[道亭]] Dàotíng's earlier reading.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* 續藏經 (X997) collection.
