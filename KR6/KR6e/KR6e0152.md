# Huáyán pǔxián xíngyuàn xiūzhèng yí 華嚴普賢行願修證儀
*Cultivation-and-Verification Ritual of Samantabhadra's Practice-Vow of the Huáyán*
by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán (集)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán is the Northern Sòng Huáyán-school's ritual manual for cultivating and verifying the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn* — the *Practice-Vow of Samantabhadra*, the foundational devotional text of the *Avataṃsaka* tradition (cf. [[KR6e0041|the 40-fascicle *Sì shí Huáyán*]]). The *xiūzhèng yí* "cultivation-verification ritual" provides the liturgical framework for the practitioner's vow-cultivation, integrating recitation of the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn pǐn* with contemplative-doctrinal instruction.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067 – 1088) reflects [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's mature editorial activity at the Huìyīnsì in Hángzhōu. The work is part of his comprehensive Huáyán-school revival project; alongside the *Shū-Chāo* editorial work and the *Huán yuán guān* materials, the present ritual manual provided the practical-devotional framework for Sòng Huáyán monastic practice.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1472) collection.
