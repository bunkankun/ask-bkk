# Huáyán pǔxián xíngyuàn xiūzhèng yí 華嚴普賢行願修證儀 (alternative version)
*Cultivation-and-Verification Ritual of Samantabhadra's Practice-Vow of the Huáyán* (alternative recension)
by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán (集)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the alternative recension of [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's *[[KR6e0152|Pǔxián xíngyuàn xiūzhèng yí]]* (X1472), preserved as a parallel-but-distinct version in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1473). The two versions document slightly different transmission lines of the same essential ritual material.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067 – 1088) reflects [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's mature editorial activity. The parallel transmission of two versions of the same work is itself a piece of evidence for the active liturgical use of the *Pǔxián xíngyuàn* material in Northern-Sòng Huáyán-school monastic practice.
