# Píng jīn gāng pī 評金剛錍
*Critical Discussion of the "Diamond Scalpel"*
by [[善熹]] (Shànxī, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[善熹]] Shànxī is a critical Huáyán-school engagement with [[湛然]] Zhànrán's *[[KR6c0025|Jīngāng pī 金剛錍]]* (T1932) — the *Diamond Scalpel*, the famous Tiāntái-school treatise that argued for the doctrine of *fǎ rì zhī yǔ wúqíng* 法日之雨無情 ("the doctrine that even non-sentient beings have Buddha-nature"). The Huáyán-school challenge to Zhànrán's Tiāntái doctrine — articulated here by Shànxī — is one of the principal documents of mature Sòng Buddhist inter-school doctrinal disputation.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[善熹]] Shànxī's mature period, c. 1170 – 1200 CE; the bracket adopted here reflects this window. The doctrinal substance — the Tiāntái-versus-Huáyán dispute on Buddha-nature in non-sentients — is one of the principal Sòng-Yuán scholastic controversies between the two doctrinal traditions.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1018) collection.
