# Huáyán dà yì 華嚴大意
*The Main Idea of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[善堅]] (Shànjiān, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by the Míng Buddhist scholar [[善堅]] Shànjiān is a compact synopsis of the [[KR6e0010|*Avataṃsaka*]]'s main doctrinal-narrative content, suitable for late-Míng monastic and lay readers. The *dà yì* "main idea" format is one of the standard late-imperial East Asian Buddhist literary genres for accessible doctrinal introduction.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1500 – 1600) reflects the broad Míng period of [[善堅]] Shànjiān's activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1023) collection.
