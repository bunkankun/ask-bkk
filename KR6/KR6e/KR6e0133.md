# Fǎ jiè guān pī yún jí 法界觀披雲集
*"Parting-the-Clouds" Compilation on the Dharma-Realm Contemplation*
by [[道通]] (Dàotōng, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[道通]] Dàotōng is a compact treatise on the [[KR6e0101|*Fǎ jiè guānmén* 法界觀門]] tradition, framed as *pī yún* 披雲 ("parting the clouds" — i.e. clarifying the obscured doctrinal substance). The text supplements Dàotōng's other works ([[KR6e0123|*Tūn hǎi jí*]], [[KR6e0132|*Pán jié*]]) with a focused treatment of the foundational Huáyán contemplative-doctrinal apparatus.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1100 – 1280) reflects the Southern-Sòng period of [[道通]] Dàotōng's activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1014) collection.
