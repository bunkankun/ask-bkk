# Huáyán dàochǎng qǐzhǐ dàlüè 華嚴道場起止大略
*General Outline of the Beginning-and-End of the Huáyán Bodhi-Maṇḍa [Service]*
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Míng-period text provides a general outline (*dàlüè*) of the *qǐzhǐ* 起止 ("beginning-and-end") of a *huáyán dàochǎng* (Huáyán bodhi-maṇḍa) service — the multi-day monastic ritual centred on the *Avataṃsaka*'s cosmology. The work serves as a procedural manual for the abbots and ritual-leaders conducting the service.

### Abstract
