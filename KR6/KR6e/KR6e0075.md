# Huáyán yī chéng jiào yì fēn qí zhāng yì yuàn shū 華嚴一乘教義分齊章義苑疏
*The Yìyuàn Commentary on the Treatise on the Doctrinal Categories of the One-Vehicle Teaching of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[道亭]] (Dàotíng, 述)

## About the work

The *Yì yuàn shū* in 10 fascicles is the major Northern-Sòng commentary on [[法藏]] Fǎzàng's [[KR6e0074|*Wǔ jiào zhāng* 五教章]] (T1866). Composed by [[道亭]] Dàotíng (active late 11th c.) of the Zháxī Pǔjìngsì 霅溪普靜寺 in Wúxīng 吳興 / Húzhōu 湖州, the work is the earliest extant systematic commentary on Fǎzàng's foundational doxographical treatise.

### Prefaces

The work is preceded by a *Huáyán yī chéng fēn qí zhāng yì yuàn shū xù* 華嚴一乘分齊章義苑疏敘 ("Preface to the *Yìyuàn Commentary*"), composed by *Cháosàn láng* 朝散郎, *Shàngshū zhǔkè yuánwài láng* 尚書主客員外郎, *Qīngchē* 輕車 (Court-rank holder, an unidentified Sòng official). The work has a *hòu xù* 後序 (postface) by [[楊傑]] Yáng Jié dated *Yuányòu* 元祐 5 (1090).

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to the period 1080 – 1090 CE, the bracket up to the dated postface. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The *Yì yuàn shū* is the foundational document of the Sòng commentary tradition on the *Wǔ jiào zhāng*; it inaugurated a tradition of Huáyán-school commentarial scholarship that would continue through [[師會]] Shīhuì's [[KR6e0076|*Fén xīn* 焚薪]] (X996), [[KR6e0078|*Fù gǔ jì* 復古記]] (X998), and [[希迪]] Xīdí's [[KR6e0079|*Jí chéng jì* 集成記]] (X999) and [[KR6e0080|*Píng fù gǔ jì* 評復古記]] (X1000) — the most extensive Sòng commentary tradition on a single Huáyán-school text.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* 續藏經 (X995) collection.

## Other points of interest

- Dàotíng's commentary inaugurated the Sòng-period flourishing of Huáyán-school commentary on the *Wǔ jiào zhāng*; the subsequent commentaries (Shīhuì, Shànxī, Xīdí) all engage critically with his readings.
