# Quàn fā pútí xīn wén 勸發菩提心文
*An Exhortation to Generate the Mind of Awakening*
by [[裴休]] (Péi Xiū, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is a devotional-doctrinal exhortation by [[裴休]] Péi Xiū (797–870), the senior Tang lay Buddhist intellectual and prime minister, urging the cultivation of *bodhicitta* (the *mind of awakening*). Péi Xiū was the disciple of [[宗密]] Zōngmì and a major lay-Buddhist patron of the late Tang; his religious writings — including this work, the prefaces to several of Zōngmì's books, and the *Xīn fǎ yào* 心法要 (the *Essential Dharma of the Mind*) — are among the most distinguished examples of late-Tang lay-Buddhist literature.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[裴休]] Péi Xiū's mature period of Buddhist activity, c. 845 – 870 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The doctrinal substance — the *quàn fā pútí xīn* "exhortation to generate *bodhicitta*" — is one of the standard themes of Mahāyāna devotional literature, and Péi Xiū's contribution is distinguished by its literary elegance and its integration of Chán-Huáyán synthesis themes drawn from his teacher Zōngmì.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1010) collection.

## Other points of interest

- Péi Xiū's combination of high political office (he served as prime minister under Tang Xuānzōng 宣宗) and lay-Buddhist literary distinction makes him one of the most consequential lay-Buddhist intellectuals of the late Tang, and his writings are key documents of Tang Buddhist-Confucian intellectual synthesis.
