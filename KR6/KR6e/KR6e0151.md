# Huáyán Qīngliáng guóshī lǐ zàn wén 華嚴清涼國師禮讚文
*Worship-Eulogy of the National Preceptor Qīngliáng [Chéngguān] of the Huáyán [School]*
by [[智肱]] (Zhìgōng, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[智肱]] Zhìgōng is a Buddhist liturgical worship-eulogy (*lǐ zàn wén* 禮讚文) honoring [[澄觀]] Chéngguān (the *Qīngliáng guóshī*) — the fourth Huáyán patriarch and the most important Tang Huáyán-school authority. The text functions as a devotional liturgy used in Sòng-Yuán-Míng Huáyán monastic worship of the school's patriarchs.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1050 – 1100) reflects [[智肱]] Zhìgōng's mature Northern-Sòng activity in the lineage of *Chángshuǐ Zǐjùn*. The work documents the Sòng Huáyán-school's ritualisation of its own patriarchal lineage — the same impulse that produced the *qī zǔ xíng jì* 七祖行蹟 list of seven patriarchs ([[KR6e0073|J B005]]) and the related Sòng-period Huáyán-lineage literature.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1471) collection.
