# Shǒulèngyán tánchǎng xiūzhèng yí 首楞嚴壇場修證儀
*Cultivation-and-Verification Ritual of the Sūtra of the Heroic March's Bodhi-Maṇḍa*
by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán (編敘 / edited and prefaced)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán is the ritual manual for the *Shǒulèngyán jīng* 首楞嚴經 ([[KR6j0118|*Śūraṅgama-sūtra*]], T945), the foundational Mahāyāna scripture closely associated with the Huáyán-Chán synthesis tradition. While not technically a *huáyán* work, the *Shǒulèngyán* was treated by Jìngyuán's Sòng Huìyīnsì circle as part of the broader Huáyán-school doctrinal-ritual heritage; the present manual extends Jìngyuán's liturgical-publication project from the *Avataṃsaka*-tradition core to the closely-related *Lèngyán* tradition.
