# Yī Lèngyán jiūjìng shìchàn 依楞嚴究竟事懺
*Ultimate Phenomenal Repentance Following the Śūraṅgama [Sūtra]*
by [[禪修]] Chánxiū (述)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[禪修]] Chánxiū is a late-Míng Buddhist repentance ritual (*shìchàn* 事懺 — "phenomenal repentance," distinct from *lǐchàn* 理懺 "principial repentance") based on the *Śūraṅgama-sūtra* tradition. Like its predecessor *[[KR6e0157|Shǒulèngyán tánchǎng xiūzhèng yí]]* (X1477) by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán, the work treats the *Lèngyán* as part of the broader Huáyán-school ritual heritage.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1500 – 1644) reflects the broad late-Míng period during which Chánxiū was active. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1478) collection.
