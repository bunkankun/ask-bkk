# Yuánjué jīng dàochǎng xiūzhèng yí 圓覺經道場修證儀
*Cultivation-and-Verification Ritual of the Bodhi-Maṇḍa of the Sūtra of Perfect Awakening*
by [[宗密]] Zōngmì (述)

## About the work

This 18-fascicle text by [[宗密]] Zōngmì is the great Buddhist liturgical-ritual manual for the *Yuánjué jīng* 圓覺經 ([[KR6i0551|*Sūtra of Perfect Awakening*]], T0842) — the Mahāyāna scripture that, alongside the *Avataṃsaka*, was central to Zōngmì's Chán-Huáyán synthesis. The 18-fascicle scope makes the work one of the most extensive Buddhist ritual manuals of the Tang period, and the principal liturgical-ritual manual of the *Yuánjué jīng* tradition.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[宗密]] Zōngmì's mature period at the Cǎotángsì on Mt. Guīfēng, c. 820 – 841 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The 18-fascicle scope reflects the elaborate liturgical-cosmological architecture of the ritual, which Zōngmì developed alongside his major doctrinal work on the *Yuánjué jīng* (the [[KR6i0555|*Yuánjué jīng dà shū* 圓覺經大疏]]).

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1475) collection.
