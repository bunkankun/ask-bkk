# Huáyán yī chéng fǎ jiè tú jì 華嚴一乘法界圖記
*Notes on the Diagram of the One-Vehicle Dharma-Realm of the Huáyán [Sūtra]* (anonymous Korean commentary)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text is an anonymous Korean Hwaeom-school commentary on *[[KR6e0109|Ŭisang's Fǎ jiè tú 華嚴法界圖]]* (T1887A). Preserved together with the parent text in the Taishō (as T1887B), it represents one stratum of the rich Korean Hwaeom commentarial tradition that grew up around Ŭisang's foundational verse-diagram.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (800 – 1100) reflects the broad late-Silla to Goryeo period of Korean Hwaeom commentarial activity on the *Fǎ jiè tú*. Modern scholarship has not securely identified the author of this particular commentary, though it is conventionally placed within the post-Ŭisang Korean Hwaeom tradition of the eighth to eleventh centuries. The commentary works through Ŭisang's verse line by line, expounding the doctrinal content and explicating the structural logic of the *seal-diagram*.

The Taishō text (T1887B) is established on the standard apparatus.
