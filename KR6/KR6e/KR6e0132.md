# Huáyán fǎ xiàng pán jié 華嚴法相槃節
*"Tortoise-Joints" Outline of the Phenomenal Categories of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[道通]] (Dàotōng, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[道通]] Dàotōng — the same author as [[KR6e0123|*Tūn hǎi jí* 吞海集]] (X239) — is a compact treatise on the *fǎ xiàng* 法相 ("phenomenal categories" or "characteristics of dharmas") of the *Avataṃsaka*, organised in *pán jié* 槃節 ("tortoise-joints" — i.e. articulated structural divisions modeled on the joints of a tortoise's shell).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1100 – 1280) reflects the Southern-Sòng period of Dàotōng's activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1013) collection, alongside its companion [[KR6e0123|*Tūn hǎi jí*]] (X239) and [[KR6e0133|*Pī yún jí*]] (X1014).
