# Huáyán xīn yào fǎ mén zhù 華嚴心要法門註
*Annotated Edition of the Mind-Essential Dharma-Gate of the Huáyán [Sūtra]*
by [[澄觀]] (Chéngguān, 撰) and [[宗密]] (Zōngmì, 註)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text combines [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's *Huáyán xīn yào fǎ mén* 華嚴心要法門 — a brief treatise on the *mind-essential* (*xīn yào* 心要) of the *Avataṃsaka* — with [[宗密]] Zōngmì's annotation. The pairing is characteristic of Tang Huáyán scholastic transmission: master-and-disciple authorial-annotative collaboration, master Chéngguān providing the doctrinal substance and disciple Zōngmì supplying the explanatory annotation.

### Abstract

The composite work spans the Chéngguān-Zōngmì transmission: Chéngguān's original text dates to his mature period (787 – 838 CE) and Zōngmì's annotation to his mature period (820 – 841 CE). The bracket adopted here (787 – 841) covers both phases. The doctrinal substance — the *xīn yào* "mind-essential" of the *Avataṃsaka* — connects directly to the Chán-Huáyán synthesis that Zōngmì systematised, in which the *dharma-realm*'s ontological structure is correlated with the practitioner's mind-nature.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1005) collection.
