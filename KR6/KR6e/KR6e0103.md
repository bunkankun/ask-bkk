# Zhù huáyán fǎ jiè guān kē wén 註華嚴法界觀科文
*Sectional Outline of the Annotated Edition of the Huáyán Dharma-Realm Contemplation*
by [[宗密]] (Zōngmì, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the structural-outline (*kē wén* 科文) by [[宗密]] Zōngmì for his own *[[KR6e0101|Zhù fǎ jiè guānmén 註法界觀門]]* (T1884) — the navigational map for the parent annotated text of the *Fǎ jiè guānmén*. As with all *kē wén* works, the function is pedagogical and navigational: providing the hierarchical heading-table of the parent commentary.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (820 – 841) reflects [[宗密]] Zōngmì's mature scholarly activity at the Cǎotángsì on Mt. Guīfēng. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1007).
