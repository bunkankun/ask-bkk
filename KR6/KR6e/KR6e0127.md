# Huáyán róng huì yī chéng yì zhāng míng zōng jì 華嚴融會一乘義章明宗記
*Notes Clarifying the Lineage of the Treatise on the Doctrine of the Perfect-Integration One-Vehicle of the Huáyán*
by [[師會]] (Shīhuì, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[師會]] Shīhuì (active 12th c.) — the Southern-Sòng Yùfēng-monastery Huáyán-school authority — is a programmatic statement of the doctrine of *róng huì yī chéng* 融會一乘 ("perfect-integration of the One-Vehicle") and its lineage in the Huáyán-school doctrinal tradition.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The work is precisely datable through Shīhuì's biographical notice (DILA A000915) to *Qiándào* 乾道 7 (1171 CE). The bracket adopted here (1171 – 1171) reflects this exact dating. The doctrinal substance — the *róng huì yī chéng* doctrine and its lineage from Fǎzàng to the Sòng — places the work in the same intellectual milieu as Shīhuì's better-known commentaries on the *[[KR6e0074|Wǔ jiào zhāng]]* (*[[KR6e0076|Fén xīn]]*, *[[KR6e0077|Kē]]*, *[[KR6e0078|Fù gǔ jì]]*).

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X985) collection.
