# Xuánfù jì 漩澓偈
*The Whirling-Eddy Verses*
by [[杜順]] Dùshùn (撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text — preserved in the Fángshān stone-canon (F1083) — is a verse-treatise (*jì* 偈) attributed to [[杜順]] Dùshùn (557–640), the *first patriarch* of the Chinese Huáyán school. The title *xuánfù* 漩澓 — "whirling eddy" — figures the bodhisattva's progress through the dharma-realm as a whirling water-current that returns to its source while continuously moving forward.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 640) reflects [[杜順]] Dùshùn's mature period. The traditional ascription to Dùshùn — as with the closely-related [[KR6e0081|*Wǔjiào zhǐguān*]] (T1867) — is sometimes questioned by modern scholarship, but is accepted in the East Asian tradition. The Fángshān stone-canon preservation provides exceptional textual security.

The work is preserved in the *Fángshān shí jīng* 房山石經 collection (F1083) — the same collection in which [[KR6e0111|Ŭisang's *Yīchéng fǎjiè tú*]] is also preserved.
