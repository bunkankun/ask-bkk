# Huáyán jīng tán xuán jué zé 華嚴經談玄抉擇
*Selections of the Profound Discourses on the Huáyán Scripture*
by [[鮮演]] (Xiānyǎn, 述)

## About the work

This 5-fascicle text by [[鮮演]] Xiānyǎn (1049–1118), the senior Liáo-dynasty Buddhist scholar, is a substantial study of the *xuán tán* 玄談 ("profound discourses" — i.e. the doctrinal-preliminary essays) sections of [[澄觀]] Chéngguān's *[[KR6e0011|Huáyán jīng shū]]* (T1735). The work is one of the principal documents of Liáo-period (Khitan) Buddhist scholarship and one of the few extant Liáo Buddhist commentaries on a Tang Huáyán-school work.

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to [[鮮演]] Xiānyǎn's mature period, c. 1080 – 1118 CE; the bracket adopted here reflects this window. The Liáo Buddhist establishment was strongly committed to the Tang Huáyán doctrinal heritage; Xiānyǎn's work documents the school's continuing northern-Chinese transmission under Khitan rule, in parallel with but separate from the Northern-Sòng Huìyīnsì Huáyán-revival project of [[淨源]] Jìngyuán.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X235) collection.
