# Chì miù 斥謬
*Refutation of Errors*
by [[善熹]] (Shànxī, 書 / written)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[善熹]] Shànxī is a focused polemical refutation of doctrinal errors in rival Buddhist commentaries. Together with [[KR6e0137|*Píng jīn gāng pī*]] (X1018) and [[KR6e0138|*Biàn fēi jí*]] (X1019), it constitutes the trio of Shànxī's polemical works engaging Tiāntái and other rival traditions on behalf of the Huáyán-school doctrine.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1170 – 1200) reflects [[善熹]] Shànxī's mature scholarly activity. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1020) collection.
