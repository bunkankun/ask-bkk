# Huáyán yuán rén lùn jiě 華嚴原人論解
*Explication of the Inquiry into the Origin of Humanity of the Huáyán [School]*
by [[圓覺]] (Yuánjué, 述)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text is the Yuán-period commentary by [[圓覺]] Yuánjué on [[宗密]] Zōngmì's [[KR6e0105|*Yuán rén lùn*]] (T1886) — distinct from and post-dating [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's earlier Northern-Sòng [[KR6e0106|*Fā wēi lù*]] (X1031). Yuánjué's commentary takes a more systematic approach than Jìngyuán's, providing line-by-line philological-doctrinal exposition for Yuán readers.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1300 – 1368) reflects the Yuán dynasty period during which the work was composed. Yuánjué's commentary continued the Sòng-Yuán Buddhist tradition of intensive commentarial scholarship on Zōngmì's foundational doxographical text.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1032) collection.

- The continuous Sòng-Yuán-Míng commentary tradition on the *Yuán rén lùn* (Jìngyuán → Yuánjué → Míng abridgements) attests the work's central place in late-imperial East Asian Buddhist doctrinal study.
