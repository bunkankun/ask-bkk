# Huáyán jīng sānshí jiǔ pǐn dà yì 華嚴經三十九品大意
*The Main Idea of the Thirty-Nine Chapters of the Huáyán Scripture*
by [[永光]] (Yǒngguāng, 敬錄 / respectfully recorded)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the late-Qīng chapter-by-chapter synopsis of the 39 chapters of the [[KR6e0010|80-fascicle *Avataṃsaka*]] by [[永光]] Yǒngguāng. The format — *dà yì* "main idea" — provides a compact summary of the principal doctrinal-narrative content of each chapter, suitable for monastic and lay readers requiring an accessible introduction.

### Abstract

The work is precisely datable through the colophon to *Guāngxù* 31 / *yǐsì* 乙巳 (1905 CE), per DILA A000281. The bracket adopted here (1905 – 1905) reflects this exact dating. The work — produced just six years before the fall of the Qīng — is one of the latest pre-modern Chinese Buddhist commentaries on the *Avataṃsaka* and documents the continuing vitality of traditional Buddhist publishing at the end of the imperial period.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X242) collection.
