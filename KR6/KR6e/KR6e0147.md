# Yī chéng jué yí lùn 一乘決疑論
*Treatise on Resolving Doubts about the One-Vehicle*
by [[彭際清]] (Péng Jìqīng / Péng Shàoshēng, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[彭際清]] Péng Jìqīng (1740–1796), the principal late-Qīng lay-Buddhist intellectual, is a doctrinal treatise resolving doubts about the *yī chéng* 一乘 (One-Vehicle) doctrine — the foundational Mahāyāna teaching elaborated in the *Avataṃsaka* and the *Lotus*, and the doctrinal centre of the Huáyán-school's distinctive doxographical claim. The *jué yí* "resolving doubts" format is one of the standard Buddhist apologetic genres.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1770 – 1796) reflects Péng's mature Buddhist activity, leading up to his death. The work belongs to his synthesis of Huáyán doctrine and Pure Land devotional practice — alongside [[KR6e0148|*Huáyán niàn fó sānmèi lùn*]] (X1030) — that became the foundational corpus of late-Qīng / early-Republican lay-Buddhist intellectualism.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1029) collection.
