# Píng fù gǔ jì 評復古記
*Critical Discussion of the "Restoring the Ancient" Notes* (alternative title: 扶焚薪 *Fú fén xīn*, "In Defence Against the *Fén xīn*")
by [[希迪]] (Xīdí, 錄)

## About the work

This one-fascicle text is [[希迪]] Xīdí's polemical response to [[師會]] Shīhuì's [[KR6e0078|*Fù gǔ jì* 復古記]] (X998) and, by extension, to Shīhuì's earlier [[KR6e0076|*Fén xīn* 焚薪]] (X996). The alternative title *Fú fén xīn* 扶焚薪 ("In Defence Against the *Fén xīn*") makes explicit the polemical positioning: where Shīhuì had "burnt the firewood" of [[道亭]] Dàotíng's earlier readings, Xīdí here "supports / defends" against Shīhuì's own polemic.

### Prefaces

*The work opens directly with the title-line "評復古記(一名扶焚薪) / 希迪 謹錄" — "Critical Discussion of the Fù gǔ jì 復古記 (alternatively Fú fén xīn 扶焚薪). Respectfully recorded by Xīdí 希迪."*

### Abstract

The work is conventionally datable to the period after Xīdí's [[KR6e0079|*Jí chéng jì* 集成記]] of 1218 — i.e. to roughly the period 1218 – 1230 CE — when Xīdí, having established his synthetic reading of the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* 五教章 in the *Jí chéng jì*, turned to engage the rival Shīhuì commentary tradition critically. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work completes the cycle of Sòng *Wǔ jiào zhāng* commentary literature: Dàotíng → Shīhuì (*Fén xīn* and *Fù gǔ jì*) → Xīdí (*Jí chéng jì* and *Píng fù gǔ jì*) — five major commentaries in 130 years on a single 4-fascicle treatise, the most intensively studied work in Sòng Huáyán-school scholarship.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* 續藏經 (X1000) collection.

## Translations and research

## Other points of interest

- The five-stage Sòng commentary tradition on the *Wǔ jiào zhāng* (Dàotíng → Shīhuì → [[善熹]] Shànxī → Xīdí, with Xīdí writing in two distinct registers) is one of the most thoroughly documented internal scholastic controversies in Chinese Buddhist intellectual history, and provides exceptional material for studying the dynamics of Sòng monastic-doctrinal debate.
