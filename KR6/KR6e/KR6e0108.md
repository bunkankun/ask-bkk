# Huáyán yuán rén lùn hé jiě 華嚴原人論合解
*Combined Explication of the Inquiry into the Origin of Humanity of the Huáyán [School]*
by [[圓覺]] (Yuánjué, 解 / explication) and [[揚嘉祚]] (Yáng Jiāzuò, 刪合 / editorially merged)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text is the Míng-period merging-and-abridgement of [[圓覺]] Yuánjué's earlier 3-fascicle [[KR6e0107|*Yuán rén lùn jiě*]] (X1032), edited by [[揚嘉祚]] Yáng Jiāzuò. The result is a more compact and accessible version of Yuánjué's Yuán-period commentary, suitable for late-Míng monastic and lay study.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1370 – 1644) reflects the broad late-Yuán to late-Míng period during which the editorial merging took place. The work documents the late-imperial Chinese Buddhist tradition's continuing engagement with [[宗密]] Zōngmì's foundational *Yuán rén lùn*, which by then had been the subject of more than seven centuries of continuous commentarial activity.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1033) collection.
