# Yuánjué jīng dàochǎng lüèběn xiūzhèng yí 圓覺經道場略本修證儀
*Abbreviated Edition of the Cultivation-and-Verification Ritual of the Bodhi-Maṇḍa of the Sūtra of Perfect Awakening*
by [[淨源]] Jìngyuán (錄)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's compact version of [[宗密]] Zōngmì's [[KR6e0155|*Yuánjué jīng dàochǎng xiūzhèng yí*]] (X1475, 18 fasc.). The drastic reduction — from 18 fascicles to 1 — provides a portable practitioner's edition suitable for everyday monastic use, distinct from the elaborate full-length ritual.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1067 – 1088) reflects [[淨源]] Jìngyuán's mature editorial activity. The work is part of his comprehensive Northern-Sòng project of consolidating and abbreviating the Tang Buddhist liturgical heritage for Sòng monastic use.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X1476) collection.
