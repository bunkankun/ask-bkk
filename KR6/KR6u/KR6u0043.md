# Pǔxián púsà xíngyuàn wáng jīng 普賢菩薩行願王經
*Sūtra of the King of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's Vow-Practices*
Anonymous Chinese composition; Dūnhuáng-only verse recension of the *Bhadracaryāpraṇidhāna*.

## About the work
A short verse scripture in one fascicle, devoted to the *Bhadracaryāpraṇidhāna* — the famous "vow-practices" of Samantabhadra. The opening lines praise the Buddhas of the ten directions and the three times, take refuge with body, speech, and mind, and pledge to make universal homage with the power of Samantabhadra's vow-practices. The text is a verse-only recension; it has no narrative frame, no setting at Vulture Peak or any other locale, and no discursive prose. It thus differs from the canonical *Bhadracaryāpraṇidhāna* embedded in the *Avataṃsaka* (Buddhabhadra and Śikṣānanda recensions), and from the standalone *Pǔxián púsà xíngyuàn pǐn* (T293, by Prajña, late 8th century).

### Abstract
T85n2907 and its companion text [[KR6u0044]] (T85n2908) are two Dūnhuáng manuscript recensions of the *Bhadracaryāpraṇidhāna* that diverge slightly from each other and from the canonical T293 recension. Modern scholarship (Cao Ling 2011; Akira Yuyama, *Prajñā-pāramitā-ratna-guṇa-saṃcaya-gāthā* and parallel studies) treats them as alternate Chinese-circulating recensions, possibly representing earlier translations or earlier Tibetan-influenced retranslations of the *Bhadracaryāpraṇidhāna* — not as Chinese-composed apocrypha in the strong sense, but as non-canonical recensions of an originally Indic verse work. Their inclusion in the *Yiweibu* of T85 reflects the Tàishō editors' decision to group all non-canonical Dūnhuáng witnesses together rather than to integrate them with the *Avataṃsaka* canon.
