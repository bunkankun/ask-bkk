# Dàlè jīng xiǎnyì chāo 大樂經顯義抄
*Released-Meaning Commentary on the Mahāsukha (Adhyardhaśatikā-Prajñāpāramitā) Sūtra*
by [[濟暹]] (撰)

## About the work

A two-juan **scholastic doctrinal commentary** on the **Adhyardhaśatikā-prajñāpāramitā** *Mahāsukha-vajra-satya-samaya-sūtra* ([[KR6c0120]], T8n0243, the *Rishukyō*) by the late-Heian Shingon scholar **Saisen** 濟暹 ([[濟暹]], 1025–1115). The text presents itself in the colophon as a self-consciously **technical follow-up** to the foundational commentaries: *based on the deep intent of the* Lǐqùshì 理趣釋 *of Bùkōng* and *following the section-division and phrasing of the Zhēnshíjīng wénjù 眞實經文句* of Kūkai (= [[KR6c0122]]), with material drawn from both apparent and Esoteric teachings. It is the most substantial late-Heian Shingon doctrinal commentary on the *Rishukyō* and was originally composed for restricted school-internal circulation — the opening notice **此書頗可祕之** ("this work is to be kept rather secret") flags its esoteric, lineage-internal character. Preserved in Taishō Vol. 61, No. 2239.

### Abstract

The work is organized under the classical **three-gate** scheme of doctrinal exegesis: (1) **the occasion of the teaching's arising** *jiàoqǐyīn* 教起因; (2) **the title of the sūtra** *jīngtímù* 經題目; (3) **the body of the sūtra** *běnwén* 本文.

The opening section on the **occasion of the teaching's arising** is the most doctrinally distinctive. Saisen subdivides it into the *general* occasion of the rise of the Esoteric teaching (*zǒng* 總) and the *particular* occasion of the rise of *this* sūtra (*bié* 別). The general section operates entirely within the **kemmitsu 顯密** distinction Kūkai had established in the foundational *Bendō kenmitsu nikyō ron* 辨顯密二教論 — which Saisen had already commented on at length elsewhere. He quotes Kūkai's classical formulation: *yìnghuà kāishuō míng yuē xiǎnjiào; yán xiǎnlüè dòu jī, fǎfó tánhuà wèi zhī mìzàng* 應化開説名曰顯教，言顯略逗機，法佛談話謂之密藏 ("the *nirmāṇakāya* / *saṃbhogakāya* expositions are called the apparent teaching, abbreviated for the capacity of beings; the Dharma-Body's discourse is called the Esoteric store"). The apparent teaching is exposited within the standard three-vehicle / *liùdù* (six-pāramitā) framework "for the sake of teaching the *triple-great* cause and beyond"; the Esoteric is the **Dharmakāya's self-enjoyment teaching** (*zìshòu fǎlè* 自受法樂), the discourse the Dharmakāya gives to its own retinue in the Vajra-realm palace. Saisen anchors this in three principal Esoteric scriptural sources — the *Mìzàng bǎoyào* 祕藏寶鑰 of Kūkai, the *Yúqí jīng* 瑜祇經 (Vajra-summit *yoga* scripture), and the *Mahāvairocanasūtra* 大日經 with Yīxíng's 一行 commentary.

The **particular occasion** of the *Rishukyō* is then linked to Bùkōng's *Lǐqùshì*: the sūtra is said to have been spoken so that practitioners of yoga "would remain unstained while transmigrating, would broadly perform the welfare-causes for beings, would rapidly attain immeasurable *samādhi*-liberation wisdom, would rapidly gather merit-and-wisdom provisions, and would overcome Māra and the *vināyaka* hordes."

The middle section (**title-exposition**) follows Kūkai's *kaidai* template ([[KR6c0121]]) but in much expanded form: each constituent term — *vajra-summit*, *great-bliss*, *non-empty*, *true-real*, *samaya*, *Prajñāpāramitā*, *Rishukyō* — is given a doctrinal-philological exposition citing the full range of Esoteric scriptural authorities. The third section, the principal body of the work, is a **section-by-section gloss** of the sūtra's seventeen-chapter sequence, parallel to Kūkai's *wénjù* but considerably more developed, integrating Bùkōng's *Lǐqùshì*, the *Mahāvairocana*-tradition exegesis, the *Vajra-summit* doctrinal apparatus, and the standard early-Heian Shingon scholastic vocabulary.

Dating: Saisen was born 1025 and died 1115; his doctrinal-productive maturity falls roughly 1050–1115. Within that bracket the *Dairakukyō kengishō* is a mature work that presupposes his prior commentaries on the *Bendō kenmitsu nikyō ron* and the *Shì móhēyǎn lùn*, but a more precise dating is not securely established in the textual record.

## Other points of interest

The text is a key witness to the **late-Heian institutionalization of the Esoteric *prajñā* commentarial tradition**: by Saisen's generation the foundational Kūkai works ([[KR6c0121]], [[KR6c0122]]) had become canonical, and the next-generation commentarial labor consisted in *sub-commentary* on Kūkai integrated with Bùkōng's authoritative Tang source. The *kemmitsu* (apparent/Esoteric) opening section of the work is a model exposition of the Kūkai-Saisen doctrinal architecture and is frequently cited as a paradigmatic Shingon scholastic statement of the *zìshòu fǎlè* 自受法樂 ("Dharma-Body's self-enjoyment teaching") thesis. The opening *cǐ shū pō kě mì zhī* 此書頗可祕之 ("this work is to be kept rather secret") notice is significant evidence for the **restricted-circulation pedagogy** of late-Heian Shingon scholastic culture: the work is positioned not as a public exposition but as a within-lineage teaching aid for advanced disciples.
