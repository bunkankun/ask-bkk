# Jīngāng bānruò jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào kāndìngjì huìbiān 金剛般若經疏論纂要刊定記會編
*Combined Edition of the Diamond Sūtra Commentary, Sub-Commentary, and Sub-Sub-Commentary*
by [[宗密]] Zōngmì (述疏), [[子璿]] Zǐxuán (錄記), [[行策]] Xíngcè (會編)

## About the work

A ten-juan early-Qīng (Kāngxī 3 = 1664) *huìbiān* (combined-edition) recension by Zhùmèng Xíngcè 截流行策 (1628–1682) of the central Huáyán *Vajracchedikā* commentary apparatus: Guīfēng Zōngmì's (780–841) Táng-period *Jīngāng bānruò jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào* ([[KR6c0041]]) + Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán's (965–1038) Northern-Sòng *Kāndìngjì*. Distinct from the parallel *Xùzàngjīng* recension [[KR6c0079]] (X25 no. 491) which incorporates a Míng-period *kēhuì* (outline-synthesis) by Dàbīn 大璸 — the present text, preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon (J31 no. B269), is Xíngcè's own larger combined-edition without the Dàbīn layer, more coordinated for monastic-pedagogical use. The author's preface is dated *Kāngxī jiǎchén* zhōngxià = mid-summer 1664 (康熙甲辰歲仲夏之吉), composed *yú Wǔlín* 於武林 (in Hángzhōu). Cf. [[KR6c0080]] for the smaller Xíngcè condensation. *notBefore* = 824 (substrate Zōngmì commentary); *notAfter* = 1664 (Xíngcè's combined edition). Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

The text presents a strictly hierarchical *kēwén* outline at the head: *zuǎnyào shūwén fēn (sān): chū biāo tímù (èr): chū jīng shū míngtí; èr zuòzhě jiāhào* 纂要疏文分(三) / 初標題目(二) / 初經疏名題 / 二作者嘉號 — opening with the title-and-author module before proceeding to the sūtra-commentary body. Xíngcè's editorial method is the *huìbiān* combined-edition: under each phrase of the [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva *Vajracchedikā* he supplies first Zōngmì's *zuǎnyào* commentary, then Zǐxuán's *kāndìngjì* sub-commentary, in clearly demarcated layers. The 10-juan length accommodates the substantial Sòng *jì* (sub-commentary) layer in full rather than excerpting it, distinguishing this recension from the more compressed [[KR6c0080]]. The work was preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon (J31 nB269), a textual-witness independent of the *Xùzàngjīng* tradition.

## Translations and research

- For Zōngmì's *Vajracchedikā* commentary in its Huáyán hermeneutic context see Peter N. Gregory, *Tsung-mi and the Sinification of Buddhism* (Princeton, 1991; rep. Hawaii, 2002).
- For Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán's sub-commentary tradition see surveys of Sòng Buddhism, esp. on the Sìmíng / Chángshuǐ axis.
- For Xíngcè's combined-edition project and his Pure-Land patriarchate see modern surveys of Qīng Pure-Land Buddhism.

## Other points of interest

The Jiāxīng-canon preservation (J31 nB269) of Xíngcè's full *huìbiān*, distinct from the *Xùzàng* multi-stratum [[KR6c0079]] (which adds Dàbīn's outline), gives modern scholars two independent textual witnesses of the same Zōngmì + Zǐxuán combination — a useful redundancy for textual criticism of the Huáyán *Vajracchedikā* commentary tradition.
