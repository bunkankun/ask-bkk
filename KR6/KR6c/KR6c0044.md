# Jīngāng bōrě bōluómì jīng luèshū 金剛般若波羅蜜經略疏
*Brief Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[智儼]] (Zhìyǎn, 述)

## About the work

A two-juan Tang Huáyán-school commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra*, by Zhìyǎn 智儼 (602–668), the second patriarch of the Huáyán 華嚴 school (the foundational systematizer of Huáyán doctrine, between [[杜順]] Dùshùn the first patriarch and [[法藏]] Fǎzàng the third patriarch). *notBefore* = 640 (Zhìyǎn's mature scholarly period); *notAfter* = 668 (Zhìyǎn's death).

### Abstract

Zhìyǎn 智儼 (602–668) was the principal early Huáyán-doctrinal systematizer — establishing the framework of Huáyán *yuánjiào* doctrine that [[法藏]] Fǎzàng (643–712) would later bring to its mature systematic form. The present *Vajracchedikā* commentary applies early Huáyán doctrinal categories to the *prajñā* sūtra.
