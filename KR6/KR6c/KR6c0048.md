# Jīngāng jīng kǒujué 金剛經口訣
*Oral Decisions on the Diamond Sūtra*
attributed to [[慧能]] (Huìnéng, 說, attributed)

## About the work

A single-juan brief oral-instruction collection on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra*, attributed to Huìnéng 慧能 (638–713), the Sixth Patriarch of Chinese Chán. Like [[KR6c0047]] (the same author's *Jiěyì*), the Huìnéng attribution is modern-scholarly disputed — the text is plausibly a Sòng-period Chán-school composition. Preserved at X24 no. 460. *notBefore* = 700, *notAfter* = 900.

### Abstract

The "oral decision" (*kǒujué* 口訣) format reflects the Chán-school's emphasis on direct master-to-disciple oral transmission — making the work, whether or not authentically Huìnéng's, a documentary witness to Chán-school *Vajracchedikā* commentarial practice in the oral-instruction format.
