# Jīngāng jīng shū 金剛經疏 (T85 no. 2738)
*Diamond Sūtra Commentary*
[anonymous]

## About the work

A second **anonymous one-juan *Vajracchedikā* commentary** recovered from the **Dūnhuáng cache** and preserved as T85 no. 2738. Distinct from but companion to [[KR6c0109]] (T85 no. 2737) and [[KR6c0111]] (T85 no. 2739). Authorship not preserved. The surviving fragment treats the *kṣānti-pāramitā* (forbearance-perfection) section of the sūtra. *notBefore* / *notAfter* set very broadly (**600–900**) consistent with the Dūnhuáng-corpus dating range. Catalog dynasty 唐.

### Abstract

The surviving fragment opens on the *rěnrǔ pōluómì* 忍辱波羅蜜 (forbearance perfection) section of the sūtra. The commentator deploys a striking gendered-political typology of *forces*: *gù nǚrén yǐ chēn wéi lì, yù yǒu suǒ suǒ bì xiān chēn gù; guówáng yǐ jiāoháo wéi lì; luóhàn yǐ jīngjìn wéi lì; zhūfó yǐ dàbēi wéi lì; bǐqiū yǐ rěn wéi lì* 故女人以嗔為力，欲有所索必先嗔故；國王以嬌豪為力；羅漢以精進為力；諸佛以大悲為力；比丘以忍為力 ("women take anger as their force, since seeking something they must first anger; kings take haughtiness as their force; arhats take diligence as their force; buddhas take great compassion as their force; *bhikṣus* take forbearance as their force"). The commentator then cites the *Yíjiàojīng* 遺教經, the *Qīfó jì* 七佛偈, and other authoritative sources on *kṣānti*, before specifying that the *Vajracchedikā*'s *rěn* is to be understood as **non-arising-dharmas-forbearance** (*wúshēng fǎrěn* 無生法忍 — the *anutpattika-dharma-kṣānti* characteristic of non-retrogressing bodhisattvas). The work belongs to the same Dūnhuáng anonymous-commentary cluster as [[KR6c0109]] and [[KR6c0111]].
