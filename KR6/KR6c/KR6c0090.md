# Jīngāng jīng zhùjiǎng 金剛經註講
*The Diamond Sūtra: Annotated Discourse*
by [[行敏]] Xíngmǐn (述)

## About the work

A two-juan early-Qīng *Vajracchedikā* commentary by Duànméi Xíngmǐn 斷眉行敏, sister volume to [[KR6c0089]] *Jīngāng jīng rúshì jīngyì*. The catalog credit-line *shù* 述 ("expounded") and the title-formula *zhùjiǎng* ("annotation-and-discourse") identify the genre: a verse-by-verse commentary in the discoursive *jiǎng* (lecture-like) register, more expansive than [[KR6c0089]]'s structural reading. Together the two works form Xíngmǐn's coordinated *Vajracchedikā* pair — *rúshì jīngyì* as structural / hermeneutic, *zhùjiǎng* as expository / discoursive. Preserved as X25 no. 502. *notBefore* / *notAfter* = 1660 / 1700 (same window as the sister volume). Catalog dynasty 清.

### Abstract

The text opens with a particularly clean canonical opening: *jīngāng shì jīn zhōng zhī jīngjiān zhě* ("vajra is the essence-firmness of metal"); *bānruò zhě fányǔ, huá yán zhìhuì* ("prajñā is Sanskrit, in Chinese 'wisdom'"); *bōluómì zhě, dào bǐ'àn* ("pāramitā: reaching the other shore"). Then a substantial systematic exposition of the six pāramitās as a coordinated path: *yī bùshī, dù qiāntān; èr chíjiè, dù yínxié; sān rěnrǔ, dù chēnnù; sì jīngjìn, dù xiètuì; wǔ chándìng, dù sànluàn; liù zhìhuì, dù yúchī* — *prajñā* identified as that which *néng shēng bāwàn sìqiān zhìhuì* ("can give rise to the 84,000 wisdoms") and so subsumes the other five pāramitās. The body proceeds verse-by-verse through the [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva *Vajracchedikā* with annotated *jiǎng* exposition; the discoursive register and the systematic doctrinal anchoring (*Vajracchedikā* read as *the prajñā moment of the six-pāramitā path*) make this a useful Línjì pedagogical *jiǎng* version of the sūtra.
