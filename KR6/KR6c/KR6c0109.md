### Abstract

The surviving fragment opens in the middle of the commentary on the **second-fruit (Skt. *sakṛdāgāmin*)** passage of the sūtra: *Sùpútí, yú yì yún hé, sītuóhán néng zuò shì niàn, wǒ dé sītuóhán guǒ bù?* (Section 9 of the Liáng Zhāomíng division). The commentator parses the sūtra as a sequence of *Sùpútí*'s questions and the Buddha's responses, with each *fruit* (*xūtuóhuán*, *sītuóhán*, *ānuóhán*, *āluóhàn*) treated separately. The annotation is structural-doctrinal in the standard mid-Táng manner, using the *Sìfēn lǜ* schema for the four fruits and indexing each to the corresponding *fánnǎo* (afflictions) cut by it (*sītuóhán* = *bó tānchēn* 薄貪嗔, the diminishing of greed-anger, etc.). The work is one of three undated and anonymous Dūnhuáng *Jīngāng jīng shū* in T85 (cf. [[KR6c0110]] T85 no. 2738; [[KR6c0111]] T85 no. 2739, the latter an *interlinear annotation* edition).
