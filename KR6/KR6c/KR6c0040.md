# Jīngāng jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào (xuǎnlù) 金剛經疏論纂要（選錄「釋金剛經纂要疏科」）
*Selected Headings from the Compendious-Essentials Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra* (extract: "Explication of the Headings of the Diamond Sūtra Compendious-Essentials Commentary")
extracted by [[子璿]] (Zǐxuán, 錄)

## About the work

A single-juan extract of the outline-headings (*kē* 科) section from Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán's 長水子璿 *Jīngāng jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào* 金剛經疏論纂要 — the Northern-Sòng Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán's editorial reorganization of [[宗密]] Zōngmì's (780–841) *Diamond Sūtra* commentary [[KR6c0041]]. The byline reads 長水沙門子璿 ("*Chángshuǐ* śramaṇa Zǐxuán"). Preserved at P187 no. 1624.

### Abstract

Authorship: by Zǐxuán 子璿 (alternate persona-name Chángshuǐ Zǐxuán 長水子璿; 965–1038 CE), the Northern-Sòng Huáyán-school scholar based at Chángshuǐyuàn 長水院. Zǐxuán was the principal Sòng-period editorial-redactor of [[宗密]] Zōngmì's commentarial corpus, producing the standard Sòng recensions of Zōngmì's *Yuánjué jīng* 圓覺經 (T1795 / T1796), *Huáyán* commentary (with sub-commentary), and the present *Diamond Sūtra* commentary structuring.

*notBefore* = 990 (Zǐxuán's mature scholarly period); *notAfter* = 1038 (Zǐxuán's death).

The work is a structural / outline-extract of the [[KR6c0041]] *Jīngāng jīng shūlùn zuǎnyào* full commentary — providing the *kē* 科 (heading-outline / topic-tree) framework that allows readers to navigate the substantive commentary. As such, it is more a reading-aid index than an independent doctrinal work, but is preserved separately in the canonical tradition for its practical-pedagogical utility.
