# Jīngāng jīng huìjiě 金剛經會解
*A Combined Explanation of the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[善月]] (Shànyuè, 述)

## About the work

A two-juan Sòng Tiāntái-school synthesizing commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra*, by Shànyuè 善月 (1149–1241, *zì* Guāngyuǎn 光遠, sobriquet Bǎisōng 柏松), a leading late-Sòng Tiāntái master at the Yánqìngsì 演慶寺 in Sìmíng 四明 (modern Níngbō). The work is a "combined explanation" (*huìjiě* 會解) — synthesizing previous Tiāntái-school *Vajracchedikā* commentary (cf. [[KR6c0037]] of [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ) with Sòng-period developments. Preserved at X24 no. 462. *notBefore* = 1196 (Shànyuè's mature scholarly period); *notAfter* = 1241 (Shànyuè's death).

### Abstract

Shànyuè 善月 was a senior Sòng Tiāntái master active in the Tiāntái-school's late-Sòng *Sìmíng pài* 四明派 — the dominant Sòng-period Tiāntái lineage descending from [[知禮]] Sìmíng Zhīlǐ (960–1028).
