# Jīngāng jīng zhùshū 金剛經註疏
*Annotated Commentary on the Diamond Sūtra*
by [[慧淨]] (Huìjìng, 註)

## About the work

A three-juan early-Tang commentary on the *Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā-sūtra*, by Huìjìng 慧淨 (578–after 645), an early-Tang scholar-monk of Jìguósì 紀國寺 in Cháng'ān. Preserved in the Wànzì xùzàngjīng at X24 no. 456. *notBefore* = 580, *notAfter* = 645.

### Abstract

Huìjìng (lay surname Fáng 房; native of Chángshān 常山; 578 – after 645) was an early-Tang scholar-monk known particularly for his role in early-Tang Buddhist debates against Daoist polemicists. He produced commentaries on the *Vajracchedikā*, the *Heart Sūtra*, the *Wúliàngshòu jīng*, and other Mahāyāna canonical texts, plus a now-lost large-scale Mahāyāna doctrinal compendium.
