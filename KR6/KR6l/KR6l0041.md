# Jùshě Lùn Sòng Shū Jì 俱舍論頌疏記
*Notes on the Commentary on the Abhidharmakośa Verses*
by [[遁麟]] (述)

## About the work

The *Jùshě Lùn Sòng Shū Jì* 俱舍論頌疏記 (CBETA [[X53n841]]) is a Tang dynasty 29-juan sub-commentary (*jì* 記) on 圓暉's *Jùshě lùn sòng shū* ([[KR6l0036]], T1823), composed by the monk Dùnlín 遁麟 ([[遁麟]]), a student of 圓暉. The text is preserved in the Xuzangjing (卍新纂大日本續藏經) Vol. 53, No. 841, with a table of contents covering juan 1 through 29. The first juan includes a section titled 釋序文 (glosses on the preface) before proceeding to the main commentary content chapter by chapter.

### Prefaces

The first section (釋序文) is 遁麟's commentary on the preface to 圓暉's text (the [[賈曾]] Jiǎ Céng preface). The main commentary begins with 分別界品第一之一.

### Abstract

Dùnlín 遁麟 (also known as Fùchūn shāmén 富春沙門 "the monk from Fùchūn"; fl. mid-to-late 8th century CE) was a student of 圓暉 ([[圓暉]]) who composed this extensive sub-commentary covering nearly all 30 juan of 圓暉's *Sòng Shū*. The *Jì* genre (*jì* 記, "notes") represents a disciple's explanatory record of a teacher's commentary — a format central to the transmission of Chinese Buddhist scholasticism. At 29 juan, Dùnlín's *Jì* is the most substantial surviving sub-commentary on 圓暉's text and was particularly important for the transmission of the *Sòng Shū* tradition to Japan.
