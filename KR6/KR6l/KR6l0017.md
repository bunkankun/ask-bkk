# Zá Āpítán Xīn Lùn 雜阿毘曇心論
*Saṃyuktābhidharma-hṛdaya-śāstra: Mixed/Combined Heart of the Abhidharma*
by [[法救]] (造), [[僧伽跋摩]] (等譯)

## About the work

The *Zá Āpítán Xīn Lùn* 雜阿毘曇心論 (Skt. *Saṃyuktābhidharma-hṛdaya-śāstra*, CBETA [[T28n1552]]) is a Liu Song (劉宋) dynasty translation of the third and most elaborated text in the *Hṛdaya* tradition, 11 juan, attributed to Dharmatrāta 尊者法救 ([[法救]]) and translated by Saṃghavarman 僧伽跋摩 ([[僧伽跋摩]]) and others. The colophon identifies it as 宋天竺三藏僧伽跋摩等譯. The Taisho header "No. 1552 [cf. Nos. 1550, 1551]" marks its position in the *Hṛdaya* sequence.

### Abstract

The *Saṃyuktābhidharma-hṛdaya* ("Mixed Heart of the Abhidharma") is attributed to Dharmatrāta 法救 (*Dharmatrāta*), a prominent Sarvāstivāda master of the Kaśmīra Vaibhāṣika tradition, also cited in the *Mahāvibhāṣā* as one of the four major masters whose views it presents. Frauwallner (1995) identifies the *Saṃyuktābhidharma-hṛdaya* as the most comprehensive text of the *Hṛdaya* tradition, substantially expanding on Dharmaśreṣṭhin's base text with the addition of "mixed" (*saṃyukta*) material from related Abhidharma sources.

The translator Saṃghavarman 僧伽跋摩 (*Saṃghavarman*, "Protected by the Saṃgha") was a Central Indian (*Tiānzhú* 天竺) monk who worked in the Liu Song court, translating several texts including this one. He was active in the mid-5th century CE.

The *Saṃyuktābhidharma-hṛdaya* was an important study text in Chinese Abhidharma circles. Together with T1550 and T1551, it forms a progressive trilogy of increasingly elaborated presentations of the Sarvāstivāda doctrinal system in summary format. Vasubandhu's *Abhidharmakośa* ([[KR6l0023]]) may have been composed partly in response to and in dialogue with this tradition.
