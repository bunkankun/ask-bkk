# Fó shuō sì wèicéngyǒu fǎ jīng 佛說四未曾有法經
*Sūtra of the Four Wonderful Qualities* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 42.3, and to T138)
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Western Jìn translation of a discourse on the four wonderful qualities (*acchariya-abbhūta-dhamma*) of a Wheel-turning King — the qualities by which his subjects, his elephant-treasure, his horse-treasure, and his jewel-treasure are all delighted by the sight of him. The Chinese parallel is T125[42.3]; this is a doublet of [[KR6a0138|T138]] but covers a different sub-set of the *Ekottara* material on the same theme.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉三藏竺法護譯」.

### Abstract

T139 was produced during Dharmarakṣa's Western Jìn career (266–313 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
