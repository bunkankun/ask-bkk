# Fó shuō yuèyù jīng 佛說月喻經
*Sūtra of the Moon Simile* (parallel to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtra 1136, T100 sūtra 111)
by [[施護]] (Shīhù / Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fó shuō yuèyù jīng* is a single-fascicle Northern-Sòng translation of a discourse using the analogy of the moon — entering a dark place by night without disturbing it — for the proper attitude of a teaching monk approaching a layperson's house. The Pāli parallel is SN 16.3 *Candūpama-sutta*; the Chinese parallels are T99[1136] and T100[111].

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Sòng-court translator's signature at the head: 「西天譯經三藏傳法大師賜紫臣施護奉詔譯」.

### Abstract

T121 is one of Shīhù's series of Sòng-Institute renderings; the defensible bracket 982–1017 is recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
