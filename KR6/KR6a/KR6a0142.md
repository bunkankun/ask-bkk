# Fó shuō sì nílí jīng 佛說四泥犁經
*Sūtra of the Four Hells* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 50.5)
by [[竺曇無蘭]] (Zhú Tánwúlán, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Eastern Jìn translation of a discourse on the four hells corresponding to four kinds of evil action. The Chinese parallel is T125[50.5].

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Eastern-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「東晉西域沙門竺曇無蘭譯」.

### Abstract

T142 was produced during Zhú Tánwúlán's Yangdu translation period (381–395 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
