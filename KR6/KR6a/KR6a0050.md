# Shòusuì jīng 受歲經
*Sūtra of the Receiving of Years* (the *Pavāraṇā-sūtra*; parallel to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 89, the *Bǐqiū qǐng jīng* 比丘請經)
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Shòu-suì jīng* is a single-fascicle Western Jìn 西晉 translation of the *Pavāraṇā-sūtra*, the discourse on the *pavāraṇā* ceremony — the closing-of-the-rains-retreat ritual on which monks formally invite mutual correction from one another. The Chinese title 受歲 ("receiving of years") refers to the *vassa*-counting that begins with the *pavāraṇā* — each successive *vassa*-completion adding a year of seniority. The Pāli parallel is MN 67 *Cātumā-sutta* (and SN 8.7 *Pavāraṇā-sutta*); the Chinese parallel is T26[89] (the *Bǐqiū qǐng jīng* 比丘請經 of the *Madhyama-āgama*).

The text opens at the Veṇuvana Monastery (羅閱祇迦蘭陀竹園) at Rājagṛha, with the saṅgha completing the *vassa*. Mahāmoggallāna addresses the assembly: "Monks who are receiving the years (i.e. completing the rains-retreat), let each of you speak — I should speak, you should speak — admonishing and being admonished by one another." The body of the text is the systematic instruction on how mutual *pavāraṇā*-criticism should be received and given.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉三藏竺法護譯」 (cf. [[KR6a0047]]).

### Abstract

T50 was produced during Dharmarakṣa's Western Jìn career (266–313 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost. The principal interest of T50 is its preservation of the *Pavāraṇā* doctrine in late-third-century Chinese; the discourse is among the foundational sources for the Buddhist conception of saṅgha-internal accountability.
