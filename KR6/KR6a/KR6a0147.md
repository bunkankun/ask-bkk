# Yùyē jīng 玉耶經
*Sūtra of Sujātā* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 51.9, T144 / T145 / T146)
by [[竺曇無蘭]] (Zhú Tánwúlán, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Eastern Jìn translation of the *Sujātā* discourse on the seven kinds of wife. The Chinese parallels are T125[51.9], T144, T145, T146.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Eastern-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「東晉天竺三藏竺曇無蘭譯」.

### Abstract

T147 was produced during Zhú Tánwúlán's Yangdu translation period (381–395 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
