# Yì jīng 意經
*Sūtra of the Mind* (parallel to *Madhyama-āgama* sūtra 172, the *Xīn jīng* 心經)
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Yì jīng* is a single-fascicle Western Jìn 西晉 translation of a discourse on the centrality of mind (*citta* / *manas*) in the conditioning of action and consequence. The Chinese parallel is T26[172] (the *Xīn jīng* 心經 of the *Madhyama-āgama* — not to be confused with the more famous *Heart Sūtra* of the *Prajñāpāramitā*).

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Western-Jìn translator's signature at the head: 「西晉月氏國三藏竺法護譯」.

### Abstract

T82 was produced during Dharmarakṣa's Western Jìn career (266–313 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
