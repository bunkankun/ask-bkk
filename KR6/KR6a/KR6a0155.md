# Jiǔhéng jīng 九橫經
*Sūtra of the Nine Untimely Deaths*
by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Eastern Hàn translation of a discourse on the nine *untimely deaths* (橫死) — the death of those who eat what should not be eaten, who eat without restraint, who eat what disagrees with them, etc. — i.e., causes of death before one's allotted lifespan is exhausted. The text is bound with [[KR6a0154|T154]] in the Taishō (T150A and T150B).

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the canonical translator's signature at the head: 「後漢安息國三藏安世高譯」.

### Abstract

T155 was produced during Ān Shìgāo's Luòyáng period (148–170 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
