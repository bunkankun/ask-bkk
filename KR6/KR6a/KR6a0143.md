# Ānàbīndǐ huà qīzǐ jīng 阿那邠邸化七子經
*Sūtra of Anāthapiṇḍika Converting His Seven Sons* (parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 51.7)
by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

A single-fascicle Eastern Hàn translation of a discourse on Anāthapiṇḍika 阿那邠邸's conversion of his seven sons, who had no faith in the Buddha-Dharma until their father's reasoned exposition won them over. The Chinese parallel is T125[51.7].

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the canonical translator's signature at the head: 「後漢安息國三藏安世高譯」.

### Abstract

T143 was produced during Ān Shìgāo's Luòyáng period (148–170 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost.
