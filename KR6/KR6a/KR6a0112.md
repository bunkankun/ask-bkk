# Fó shuō bāzhèngdào jīng 佛說八正道經
*Sūtra of the Noble Eightfold Path* (parallel to *Saṃyukta-āgama* sūtras 784–785)
by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fó shuō bāzhèngdào jīng* is a single-fascicle Eastern Hàn 後漢 translation of a foundational discourse on the noble eightfold path. The Chinese parallel is T99[784–785].

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the canonical translator's signature at the head: 「後漢安息國三藏安世高譯」.

### Abstract

T112 was produced during Ān Shìgāo's Luòyáng period (148–170 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The Indic source is presumed lost. The text is one of the foundational early-Chinese sources for the eightfold path doctrine.
