# Xūmótínǚ jīng 須摩提女經 (T128b)
*Sūtra of Sumati* (the *Sumati-sūtra*; alternative recension; parallel to *Ekottara-āgama* 30.3, and to T128a / T131)
by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

T128b is an alternative recension of the *Sumati-sūtra*, attributed (like T128a, [[KR6a0129|T129]]) to Zhī Qiān. The Taishō prints both as T128a and T128b — a rare instance in the canon of two parallel recensions of the same work being given consecutive Taishō entries. The two differ in narrative detail but share the basic *Sumati* legend.

### Prefaces

The text bears no preface or postface. The only paratext is the Wú-period translator's signature at the head: 「吳月支優婆塞支謙譯」.

### Abstract

T130 was produced during Zhī Qiān's Wú-court translation period (222–253 CE), recorded in the frontmatter. The doublet relationship to T129 is one of the more curious cases in the early canon: both versions are transmitted under Zhī Qiān's name, suggesting either two genuinely separate translations from the same Vorlage by the same translator, or — more probably — a textual divergence in the manuscript transmission of a single original work.
