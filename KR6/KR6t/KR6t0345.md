# Jìngtǔ fǎmén dàtú míngmù 淨土法門大圖名目
*Topical-Headings to the Great Chart of the Doctrinal Gates of the Pure Land*
by [[行觀]] Gyōkan (撰)

## About the work

A **single-fascicle Seizan-school catechetical primer** by **Gyōkan** 行觀 (the Japanese Pure-Land monk, *not* the Chinese Chán master of the same name), structurally functioning as the gloss-edition / explanatory key (*meimoku*) to the parallel-genre [[KR6t0344]] *Jōdoshū hōmon dai-zu* by [[覺融]] Kakuyū. The work expands on the *hairyū-bō-shō-jo* (廢立傍正助 — *discard-and-establish, accompanying, correct, assisting*) doctrinal scheme of the Seizan school through detailed topical headings, each given a concise gloss.

### Abstract

The opening entry illustrates the method: "*The matter of "Discard-and-Establish, Accompanying, Correct-and-Assisting" (廢立傍正助三重之事): "Discard-and-Establish" means: *discard* the various practices of disciplined-good and dispersed-good; *establish* the one practice of nenbutsu...*" (廢立傍正助三重之事 / 廢立ト者。廢ハ定散之諸行。立ハ念佛ノ一行也). The body of the text proceeds through enumerated topical headings, with each topical entry giving:

1. The technical term in Sino-Japanese characters;
2. A brief etymological / scriptural gloss;
3. The Seizan-school doctrinal position on the term;
4. Where relevant, the contrast with the Chinzei or Shinshū reading.

The work is one of the principal pedagogical primers of the Seizan school in the early-modern period and was used in conjunction with [[KR6t0344]] (the underlying *daizu* diagrams) and with the doctrinal compendia of the Ken'i corpus [[KR6t0337]]–[[KR6t0342]].

**Date.** The Taishō text is the **Bunka 13 / 1816** woodblock edition prepared by **Chōzan** 超山 of Saikō-ji 西向寺 in west Kyoto (specifically: "*Bunka 13 / Ryūshū / 子 / 8th month, upper-third; reporting-the-grace, the * residence-disciple Chōzan śramaṇa, copied at the eastern-veranda of Saikō-ji of *Imazato* in west Kyoto*"). The underlying composition is undatable from internal evidence; a *15th–17th c.* date is plausible.
