# Huāyánzōng suǒlì wǔjiào shízōng dàyì lüèchāo 華嚴宗所立五教十宗大意略抄
*Brief Compendium on the Kegon School's Established Five Teachings and Ten Schools*
*Anonymous*

## About the work

An anonymous single-fascicle **Kegon doctrinal primer** on the *Avataṃsaka* school's foundational doctrinal-classification scheme: **the five teachings** (*wǔ jiào* 五教) and **the ten schools** (*shí zōng* 十宗). The five teachings are: (1) **Lesser Vehicle** (*xiǎo-shèng jiào* 小乘教 — the *Āgamas*, *Vinaya-piṭaka*, *Abhidharma*); (2) **Beginning Mahāyāna** (*dà-shèng shǐ jiào* 大乘始教 — the *Prajñāpāramitā* and Yogācāra); (3) **Final Mahāyāna** (*dà-shèng zhōng jiào* 大乘終教 — the *Tathāgatagarbha* tradition); (4) **Sudden Teaching** (*dùn jiào* 頓教 — Chan and the Sudden tradition); (5) **One-Vehicle Complete Teaching** (*yī-chéng yuán jiào* 一乘圓教 — the *Avataṃsaka* itself).

### Abstract

Authorship and dating: The work is **anonymous** in the canon; no transmission colophon survives. The doctrinal apparatus — directly drawn from Fa-zang's *Wǔ jiào zhāng* 華嚴五教章 [[KR6a0011]] and its standard medieval Japanese commentarial tradition — is the Tōdaiji-Kegon curriculum standard. The work's compact, didactic style places it within the Japanese Kegon-school pedagogical literature, plausibly composed between the late Heian and the early Muromachi periods. **notBefore = 1000, notAfter = 1500** is a conservative bracket.

Doctrinal content: the work systematically expounds the Lesser Vehicle's *sān-shēng liù-shí jié* (three-life, sixty-kalpa) path of the *śrāvaka*, with its three stages of *shùn jiětuō fēn* 順解脱分 (concordant-with-liberation), *shùn juédéfēn* 順決擇分 (concordant-with-decisive-analysis), and *jiàn dào* 見道 (path of vision). The treatment of the *Beginning Mahāyāna* covers the standard Yogācāra **five-stages** doctrine (*zī-liáng wèi* → *jiā-xíng wèi* → *jiàn dào wèi* → *xiū dào wèi* → *jiū-jìng wèi*). The remaining three teachings are treated more briefly in the standard Kegon-sectarian sequence.

The text serves as a **memorisation primer** for the foundational Kegon teaching-classification doctrine — comparable in pedagogical function to [[KR6t0030]] *Huāyánzōng dàyào chāo*. Its anonymity is consistent with its functional, didactic character.

## Other points of interest

The work is one of the very few **anonymous Kegon primers** preserved in the Taishō canon. It belongs to the same genre as [[KR6t0020]] *Qīshíwǔ fǎ míngmù* — both works are anonymous, didactic, and structurally tabular — and is a witness to the medieval Japanese tradition of producing **memorisation primers** for the foundational doctrinal categories of each school.
