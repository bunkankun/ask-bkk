# Huāyán wǔjiào zhāng míngmù 華嚴五教章名目
*Names and Categories of Fa-zang's Avataṃsaka Five-Teachings Treatise*
by [[喜海]] (撰)

## About the work

A three-fascicle **scholastic reference manual** on the doctrinal categories established in Fa-zang's *Wǔ jiào zhāng* 華嚴五教章 [[KR6a0011]], compiled by **Kikai** 喜海 (also **Girinbō** 義林房, 1178–1250), the principal disciple of **Myōe Kōben** ([[高辨]]) at **Kōzan-ji** 高山寺. The work is organised as a **ten-gate** (*shí mén* 十門) systematic enumeration of the doctrinal categories of Fa-zang's treatise: (1) *jiànlì chéng* 建立乘 (establishment of the vehicles); (2) *jiàoyì shèyì* 教義攝益 (the inclusion-and-benefit of teaching and meaning); (3) *xù gǔ jīn lì jiào* 叙古今立教 (recounting the past and present establishment of teachings); (4) *fēn jiào kāi zōng* 分教開宗 (dividing the teaching and opening the school); (5) *chéng jiào kāi hé* 乘教開合 (the opening and combining of vehicles and teachings); (6) *jiào qǐ qián hòu* 教起前後 (the prior-and-after of the teaching's arising); (7) *juézé qí yì* 決擇其意 (the decisive selection of its meaning); (8) *shīshè yìxiāng* 施設異相 (the manifestation of distinct characteristics) — these together comprising the upper volume; (9) *yìlǐ fēnjì* 義理分齊 (the proportions of doctrinal principle) — the middle volume; (10) *suǒquán chābié* 所詮差別 (the distinctions of what is conveyed) — the lower volume.

### Abstract

Authorship and dating: The catalog meta gives **Kikai** 喜海 as the author. A copying colophon in the Taishō text dates the manuscript transmission to "*Shōō 5 (= 1292), 6th month 12th day, copied at the **Bodaiin** 菩提院 South Hall by **Senpen** 宣遍*" — implying that the work was completed sometime before this 1292 transcription. **Kikai** (1178–1250; DILA A001182; Wikidata Q11419213) was the principal disciple and biographer of **Myōe** at Kōzan-ji, the central Kegon-revival monastery of Kamakura Japan. His other surviving works are the *Avataṃsaka* phonetic-glossary commentaries [[KR6e0014]] *Xīnyì Huáyán jīng yīnyì* (compiled 1227) and [[KR6e0042]] *Zhēnyuán Huáyán jīng yīnyì*. The *Wǔ jiào zhāng míng-mù* falls within his mature scholarly career: **notBefore = 1200, notAfter = 1250** (his death).

Doctrinal content: the work is organised topic-by-topic following Fa-zang's structure. Gate 1 *jiànlì chéng* opens with the standard Kegon doctrine of "*one-vehicle teaching-meaning, opened into two gates: (a) the **special teaching** (*bié jiào* 別教) — different from the three vehicles; (b) the **common teaching** (*tóng jiào* 同教) — identical with the three vehicles and others.*" Within the *bié jiào* there are further two gates: "*(i) the **nature-ocean fruit-portion** (*xìnghǎi guǒfēn* 性海果分), which is the meaning of the ineffable, i.e. the ten-buddha's own realm; (ii) the **dependent-arising cause-portion** (*yuánqǐ yīnfēn* 緣起因分), which is the speakable portion, i.e. the realm of Samantabhadra*." The Samantabhadra-gate then opens into two further gates: *fēnxiāng mén* 分相門 (gate of divided characteristics) and *gāishè mén* 該攝門 (gate of comprehensive inclusion). This recursive bifurcation-structure is the Kegon-school's distinctive **doctrinal-cataloguing apparatus** — and Kikai's work is its most compact medieval Japanese codification.

The CANWWW related-texts apparatus correctly identifies the work as keyed to [[KR6a0011]] *Wǔ jiào zhāng* (T45N1866) and to [[KR6t0035]] *Wǔjiào zhāng tōnglù jì* by [[凝然]] — confirming the canonical placement of the work as part of the **Tōdaiji-Kōzan-ji Wǔjiào zhāng commentarial apparatus**.

## Other points of interest

Kikai's status as **Myōe's principal Kōzan-ji disciple** (the compiler of the *Myōeshōnin gyōjō* and a Huayan philologist) makes this Kegon doctrinal-catalogue manual a unique witness to **the doctrinal curriculum at Kōzan-ji in the second generation** after Myōe's death. The work would have served as the standard introductory survey for new students at the monastery.
