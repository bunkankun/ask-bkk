# Guàndǐng sī jiànwén 灌頂私見聞
*Private kanjō Notes from What I Have Heard and Seen*
by [[了翁]] (御談)

## About the work

A single-fascicle **disciple's record of master Ryōō's teaching on Tendai esoteric *kanjō* procedure** — explicitly headed "*Ryōō's teaching*" 了翁御談 at the top of the text. The work is a *kuden* document of medieval Tendai-esoteric consecration practice.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** The work is presented as a disciple's record of **Ryōō's** 了翁 (13th-c.) oral teaching. The disciple is not named in the *Taishō* text; the genre is the *jiànwén* 見聞 ("what I have heard and seen") — a record kept by a personal disciple of a master's *kuden* on specific topics.

**Date.** Within the 13th century, in Ryōō's lifetime.

**Content.** The *mokuroku* lists the topics under the **Garbhadhātu** 胎 heading:

1. **灌頂梵語事** — On the Sanskrit term for *kanjō* (i.e., the Sanskrit *abhiṣeka* / *abhi-seca*, and its semantic field).
2. **七日作壇事** — On the seven-day altar-construction sequence.
3. **八色幡事** — On the eight-color banner arrangement (matching that prescribed in [[KR6t0107]] *Suíyào jì*).
4. **十二天事** — On the Twelve Devas (the protective deities of the eight directions plus heaven and earth).

(further topics on the **Vajradhātu** 金 side and *denpō* 傳法 transmission side, as preserved in the rest of the fascicle).

For each topic, Ryōō's *kuden* is recorded — the etymological-doctrinal interpretation, the procedural-ritual practice, the *kuden* variants from other Taimitsu sub-lineages, and Ryōō's own master-lineage's settled position. The work is short but doctrinally and procedurally compact, and provides important supplementary witness for the medieval Tendai-esoteric *kanjō* tradition.

**Significance.** The *Guàndǐng sī jiànwén* is one of many shorter *kuden*-record fascicles preserved in the Tendai-esoteric section of *Taishō*. Such works are key supplementary documents to the major encyclopedic compendia ([[KR6t0108]], [[KR6t0109]], [[KR6t0110]], [[KR6t0114]]), providing the **specific master-disciple transmission moment** that the compendia summarize and systematize.
