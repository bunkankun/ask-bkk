# Shìjiā Rúlái chéngdào jì zhù 釋迦如來成道記註
*Annotations to the Record of the Tathāgata Śākyamuni's Attainment of the Way*

annotated by [[道誠]] (Dàochéng, fl. early 11th c., 註)

## About the work

A two-juan annotated commentary on [[王勃]]'s short hagiography *Shìjiā Rúlái chéngdào jì* ([[KR6r0027]]), composed by the Sòng monk [[道誠]] (Dàochéng) and dated by his colophon to 1019 (Sòng *Tiānxǐ* 天禧 3). The catalog meta classifies this entry under the Táng dynasty by association with the parent text; the actual composition is Northern Sòng. [[道誠]] supplies, for each phrase of [[王勃]]'s dense parallel-prose text, the underlying scriptural reference together with an explanatory paraphrase, thereby making the text accessible to readers without a thorough scriptural background.

### Abstract

[[道誠]] is best known for his other compilation, the *Shìshì yàolǎn* 釋氏要覽 ([[KR6m0029]]) of 1019 — a major Buddhist encyclopaedic handbook — and the present commentary belongs to the same year and to the same broader project of making Buddhist learning accessible to a wider readership. Each segment of [[王勃]]'s prose is reproduced in the commentary, followed by [[道誠]]'s annotations identifying the source sūtra (the *Buddhacarita*, *Lalitavistara*, etc.) and clarifying the technical Buddhist allusions.

The 1019 dating is given in the colophon and is secure. The combination of [[王勃]]'s elegant Táng *piánwén* with [[道誠]]'s Sòng-period scriptural commentary made the *Shìjiā Rúlái chéngdào jì* one of the most readable handbooks on the Buddha's life for Sòng-period educated readers, and the annotated edition was reprinted in the Jiāxīngzàng 嘉興藏 of the late Míng.
