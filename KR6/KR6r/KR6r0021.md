# Lìcháo shìshì zījiàn 歷朝釋氏資鑑
*Mirror for Reference of the Śākyans Through the Successive Dynasties*

compiled by [[熙仲]] (Xīzhòng, fl. 1336, 集)

## About the work

A Yuán-dynasty Buddhist universal history in twelve juan, compiled by the monk [[熙仲]] (Xīzhòng) and dated by his preface to 1336 (Yuán *Zhìyuán* 至元 2 of the second Zhìyuán reign-period). The title — *zījiàn* 資鑑 ("mirror for reference") — places the work in the same Confucian-historiographical lineage as [[本覺]]'s [[KR6r0020]], i.e. as a Buddhist analogue to the *Zīzhì tōngjiàn* of [[司馬光]]. Preserved in the *Manji Xuzangjing* (X76 no. 1517).

### Abstract

[[熙仲]]'s preface explains that he undertook the compilation because the existing universal Buddhist histories — including [[志磐]]'s [[KR6r0012]], [[本覺]]'s [[KR6r0020]], and [[祖琇]]'s [[KR6r0017]] — had become difficult to consult and inconsistent in their chronological coverage. His own twelve-juan work follows a strict year-by-year annalistic scheme, drawing imperial annals from the standard histories and inserting Buddhist events alongside.

The work covers from the Hàn through the early Yuán. Like [[本覺]]'s earlier *Tōngjiàn*, it adopts a relatively even-handed treatment of the Buddhist schools without obvious Tiāntái, Chán, or Pure Land bias. Its principal value is as a comparative source for events covered also by [[KR6r0012]], [[KR6r0013]], and [[KR6r0014]]: where these works disagree, [[熙仲]]'s independent compilation provides a fourth witness.

[[熙仲]] himself is little documented beyond his preface. He was a Yuán-period monk active in the southern Buddhist establishment.
