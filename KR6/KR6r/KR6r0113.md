# Fǎmén chúguǐ 法門鋤宄
*Hoeing Out the Brigands from the Dharma Gate*

written by [[淨符]] (Jìngfú / Wèizhōng 位中, fl. mid-17th c., 著)

## About the work

A 1-juan early-Qīng Cáo-dòng-school polemical work by **Jìngfú** 淨符 (號 **Wèizhōng** 位中 / **Báiyán Wèizhōng** 白巖位中) — a Cáodòng monk of the **Shíyǔ Míngfāng** 石雨明方 (1593–1648) dharma-line, abbot of **Báiyán Zūnshèngsì** 白巖尊聖寺 in Qiántáng. Per DILA, the work is dated to **Kāngxī 6** = **1667** by Jìngfú himself; an additional preface attributes a further enlargement to a Japanese reprint of **Genroku** 元祿 3 (庚午 = **1690**), prepared as a companion volume to the Japanese editor's own **《五家辨正》** *Wǔjiā biànzhèng*.

### Abstract

The title *Fǎmén chúguǐ* — "hoeing out the brigands from the Dharma gate" — explicitly frames the work as a **polemical clean-up** of the contemporary Chán scene. The reprint preface compares the targets of the work to "**Liángshàng jūnzǐ**" 梁上君子 ("gentlemen on the rafters" — i.e., thieves) and praises Jìngfú for "leaving them no foothold" — even at the cost of relegating the *Yándēng* 嚴燈 and *Chándēng* 禪燈 compilations to mere "sea-mirage cities."

The polemic is principally directed against:
1. **Bogus lineage-claims** — figures who appropriate Chán transmission without genuine dharma-succession;
2. **The Línjì-side appropriation of Cáodòng materials** — Jìngfú, as a Cáodòng partisan, defends the integrity of his own line against figures who treat the Cáodòng revival as derivative from Línjì transmissions (cf. the contrary argument in [[KR6r0110]] *Chándēng shìpǔ*).
3. **Specific historical revisions** — including a critical re-reading of the canonical Mǎzǔ–**Yàoshān** 藥山 transmission narrative.

The work circulates alongside Jìngfú's larger **《祖燈大統》** *Zǔdēng dàtǒng* (98 juan + 2-juan index, completed Kāngxī 11 = 1672) and his *Zōngmén niāngǔ huìjí* 宗門拈古彙集 (45 juan, X1296, X66) as part of the same Cáo-dòng-side polemical-historiographical project.

## Other points of interest

The work is a representative document of the **early-Qīng Cáodòng / Línjì lineage controversy** — a controversy in which both sides produced mutually contradictory tabular lineage-genealogies, each with implicit doctrinal commitments. Jìngfú's *Fǎmén chúguǐ* is one of the principal **Cáo-dòng-side** documents.
