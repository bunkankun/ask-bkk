# Fó shuō huàn shì rén xián jīng 佛說幻士仁賢經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Magician Bhadra (Bhadramāyākāra-vyākaraṇa)*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù is the *Bhadramāyākāra-vyākaraṇa* — the Mahāyāna sūtra on the conversion of the magician Bhadra. The narrative-doctrinal structure presents a magician (the *huàn shì* 幻士) who is led from his stage-magic illusions to a deeper understanding of Buddhist *māyā* / illusion-doctrine.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE). The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work was incorporated into the *[[KR6f0001|Mahā-ratnakūṭa]]* tradition.
