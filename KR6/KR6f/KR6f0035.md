# Fó shuō tài zǐ shuā hù jīng 佛說太子刷護經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Prince Susukha*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù treats the bodhisattva-prince *Tài zǐ shuā hù* 太子刷護 (a Sanskrit-name reconstruction unclear). With [[KR6f0036|*Tài zǐ hé xiū jīng* 太子和休經]] (T0344) it forms a parallel-translation pair.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE). The Taishō text (T0343) is established on the standard apparatus.
