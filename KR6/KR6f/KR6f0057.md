# Shèng mán jīng yì jì 勝鬘經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Śrīmālā Sūtra*
by [[慧遠]] (Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn / Suí Yuǎn, 撰)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle commentary on the *[[KR6f0045|Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra]]* is by [[慧遠]] *Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn* (the Suí *Dìlùn*-school master, 523–592, distinct from the much earlier Lúshān Huìyuǎn of 334–416). The work belongs to Huìyuǎn's vast commentarial corpus on the major Mahāyāna scriptures and represents the canonical Suí-period reading of the *Śrīmālā*.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 592) reflects [[慧遠]] *Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn*'s mature scholarly period. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X351).
