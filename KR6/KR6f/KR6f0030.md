# Fó shuō lí gòu shī nǚ jīng 佛說離垢施女經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Maiden Vimaladattā*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù treats the bodhisattva-girl *Lí gòu shī* 離垢施 (Skt. *Vimaladattā*, "Stainless Bestowing"). With [[KR6f0031|*Dé wú gòu nǚ jīng* 得無垢女經]] (T0339) it forms a parallel-translation pair on the same essential material.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE). The Taishō text (T0338) is established on the standard apparatus.
