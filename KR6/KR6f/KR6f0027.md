# Fó shuō xū mó tí pútísà jīng 佛說須摩提菩薩經 (Kumārajīva)
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Bodhisattva Sumati* — Kumārajīva's translation
by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva's translation of the *Sumati-paripṛcchā*, parallel to [[KR6f0026|Zhú Fǎhù's T0334]] and [[KR6f0028|Bodhiruci's T0336]]. The triple parallel-translation tradition documents the early-medieval Chinese reception of the bodhisattva-girl Sumati narrative.
