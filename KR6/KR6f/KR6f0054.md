# Shèng mán jīng jì 勝鬘經記
*Notes on the Śrīmālā Sūtra* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng manuscript fragment is a commentary on the [[KR6f0045|*Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra*]] (T0353). Preserved in Taishō volume 85 — the *Dūnhuáng yíshū* 敦煌遺書 series.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao or preface in source.*

### Abstract

The work is anonymous and undated; conventional scholarship places it in the broad Tang period (600 – 900 CE) of Dūnhuáng manuscript production.

The Taishō text (T2761) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript witness.
