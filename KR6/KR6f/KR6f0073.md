# Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng shū 觀無量壽佛經疏 (Zhìyǐ)
*Commentary on the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ, 說 / lectured)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary by [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ (538–597, the founder of the Tiāntái 天台 school) is the Tiāntái-school reading of the [[KR6f0071|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng* T0365]]. The work integrates Pure Land contemplative practice with the Tiāntái-school *zhǐguān* (śamatha-vipaśyanā) framework.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 597) reflects [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ's mature scholarly period at the imperial Suí court and at Tiāntáishān 天台山.

The Taishō text (T1750) is established on the standard apparatus.
