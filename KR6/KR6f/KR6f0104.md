# Dà bǎo jī jīng shù 大寶積經述
*Account of the Mahā-ratnakūṭa Sūtra*
by [[徐鍔]] (Xú È, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by the Tang court official [[徐鍔]] Xú È is an *account* (*shù* 述) of the *[[KR6f0001|Mahā-ratnakūṭa T0310]]*, paralleling the imperial preface by *Tàishànghuáng* [[睿宗]] Ruìzōng. Xú È provides further documentary detail on the translation enterprise of [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci and the institutional setting of the work.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (706 – 720) reflects the period during which Xú È served as a Tang court official and was associated with the Bodhiruci translation team.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X264) collection.
