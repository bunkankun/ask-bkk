by anonymous translator

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text is the parallel translation of the same essential material as [[KR6f0035|*Tài zǐ shuā hù jīng* 太子刷護經]] (T0343, Zhú Fǎhù's version), preserved without translator-attribution. The Taishō apparatus places it in the Western-Jìn / Eastern-Jìn period bracket.

The bracket adopted here (266 – 420) reflects the broad Western-Jìn / Eastern-Jìn period in which the work was likely produced.
