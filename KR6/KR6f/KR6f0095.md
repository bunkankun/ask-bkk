# Ā mí tuó fó shuō zhòu 阿彌陀佛說呪
*The Spell Spoken by Amitābha Buddha* (anonymous)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text preserves a brief Pure Land *zhòu* (spell / dhāraṇī) attributed to Amitābha Buddha. The text functions as a Pure Land devotional spell, parallel to *[[KR6f0094|Bá yī qiè yè zhàng T0368]]*.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The text is anonymous and undated; conventional scholarship places it broadly in the medieval Tang-Sòng period of Pure Land devotional literature.

The Taishō text (T0369) is established on the standard apparatus.
