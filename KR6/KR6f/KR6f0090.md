## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the *[[KR6f0082|Smaller Sukhāvatī]]* is by the Silla master [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo. Distinct from *[[KR6f0067|Wǒnhyo's Two-Fascicle Larger Sukhāvatī Zōng yào]]* (T1747); the present commentary treats the *Smaller* parallel.
