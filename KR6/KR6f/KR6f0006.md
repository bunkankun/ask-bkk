# Fó shuō pǔ mén pǐn jīng 佛說普門品經 (recension a)
*The Sūtra of the Universal Gate Chapter (recension a)*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text — the first of two parallel Taishō recensions (T0315a and [[KR6f0007|T0315b]]) — is [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's translation of the *Pǔ mén pǐn jīng* (a chapter on the *Universal Gate* doctrine, a *bodhisattva*-path text). The text was incorporated into the *[[KR6f0001|Mahā-ratnakūṭa]]* as assembly 10 (*Wénshūshīlì pǔ mén huì* 文殊師利普門會).

### Abstract

The translation is conventionally datable to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's translation activity at Cháng'ān, c. 266 – 313 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The Taishō prints two recensions of the same translation as T0315a and T0315b; the *a* recension corresponds to the Korean *Tripiṭaka Koreana* witness, while T0315b is from another transmission line. The two preserve substantially the same translation in slightly different states.

The Taishō text (T0315a) is established on the Korean *Tripiṭaka Koreana* witness.
