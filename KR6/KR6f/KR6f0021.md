# Fó shuō xū lài jīng 佛說須賴經 (Zhī Shīlún)
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Surata (Surata-paripṛcchā)* — Former-Liáng version
by [[支施崙]] (Zhī Shīlún, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[支施崙]] Zhī Shīlún is the second Chinese version of the *Surata-paripṛcchā*, parallel to [[KR6f0020|Báiyán's earlier T0328]]. The two-translation tradition documents the early Chinese reception of the *paripṛcchā* sūtra and provides important comparative-philological material.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation is conventionally placed in the Former-Liáng period (314 – 376 CE). The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The Taishō text (T0329) is established on the standard apparatus.
