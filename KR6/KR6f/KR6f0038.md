# Fó shuō dà fāng guǎng shàn qiǎo fāng biàn jīng 佛說大方廣善巧方便經
*The Great-Vast Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Skillful Means (Mahāupāya-kauśalya)*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

This 4-fascicle text by [[施護]] Dānapāla is the Northern-Sòng version of the *upāya-kauśalya* (skillful-means) Mahāyāna doctrine, paralleling but distinct from [[KR6f0037|*Huì shàng pútísà wèn dà shàn quán jīng*]] (T0345). The work was incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] tradition.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[施護]] Dānapāla's Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau activity (980 – 1017 CE). The Taishō text (T0346) is established on the standard apparatus.
