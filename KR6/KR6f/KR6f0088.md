# Ā mí tuó jīng shū 阿彌陀經疏 (Kuījī)
*Commentary on the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0082|*Smaller Sukhāvatī*]] is by the great Tang Yogācāra-school master [[窺基]] Kuījī (632–682), [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng's principal disciple. The work presents the Yogācāra-school reading of the Pure Land sūtra, paralleling Kuījī's commentary on the [[KR6f0089|same sūtra in Yogācāra-traditional format]] (T1758).

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (660 – 682) reflects Kuījī's mature scholarly period.

The Taishō text (T1757) is established on the standard apparatus.
