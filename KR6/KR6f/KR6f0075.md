# Guān wú liàng shòu jīng yì shū 觀無量壽經義疏 (Jízàng)
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra*
by [[吉藏]] (Jízàng, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the *[[KR6f0071|Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng T0365]]* is by [[吉藏]] Jízàng. The Sānlùn-school reading of the *Visualization Sūtra*, paralleling Jízàng's parallel commentary on the *Larger Sukhāvatī* ([[KR6f0066|T1746]]).
