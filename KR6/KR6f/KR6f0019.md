# Fā jué jìng xīn jīng 發覺淨心經
*The Sūtra on Awakening the Pure Mind*
by [[闍那崛多]] (Jñānagupta, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[闍那崛多]] Jñānagupta is a Mahāyāna sūtra on the awakening of the *jué jìng xīn* 覺淨心 (Skt. *bodhi-citta*-purification) and its cultivation in the bodhisattva path. The work was incorporated into the *[[KR6f0001|Mahā-ratnakūṭa]]* tradition.
