# Fó shuō pútísà xiū xíng jīng 佛說菩薩修行經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Bodhisattva's Cultivation*
by [[白法祖]] (Bái Fǎzǔ, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[白法祖]] Bái Fǎzǔ is a Mahāyāna sūtra on the bodhisattva's cultivation (*xiū xíng* 修行) — i.e. the practical implementation of the bodhisattva path. The work was incorporated into the *[[KR6f0001|Mahā-ratnakūṭa]]* tradition.

### Abstract

[[白法祖]] Bái Fǎzǔ (active late 3rd / early 4th c.) was a Western-Jìn-period Buddhist translator working in the period 290 – 306 CE per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145). The bracket adopted here reflects this window. He is also credited with the *Wǔ kǔ zhāng jù jīng* 五苦章句經 and other shorter Buddhist translations; his translations were largely produced at the Western-Jìn capital Luòyáng.

The Taishō text (T0330) is established on the standard apparatus.
