# Fó shuō jué dìng pí ní jīng 佛說決定毘尼經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Decisive Vinaya (Vinaya-viniścaya-upāli-paripṛcchā)*
by [[燉煌三藏]] (Tripiṭaka of Dūnhuáng, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by the unidentified *Dūnhuáng sān zàng* ("Tripiṭaka of Dūnhuáng") is the *Vinaya-viniścaya-upāli-paripṛcchā* — the Mahāyāna sūtra on Upāli's inquiry concerning *vinaya* (monastic discipline) decisions, presented from a bodhisattva-yāna rather than a *śrāvaka*-yāna perspective. The work is incorporated into the *[[KR6f0001|Mahā-ratnakūṭa]]* as assembly 24 (*Yōubōlí huì* 優波離會).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation is conventionally placed in the Western-Jìn period (266 – 316 CE), the bracket of the *Dūnhuáng sān zàng* honorific. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The text is the most important Mahāyāna *vinaya*-related text in the early Chinese tradition, presenting the *bodhisattva-yāna* perspective on monastic-discipline decisions.

The Taishō text (T0325) is established on the standard apparatus.
