## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is a Dūnhuáng-preserved Buddhist commentary on the *[[KR6f0045|Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra]]* by an unidentified *Zhāo fǎshī* ("Master Zhāo"). The work is one of several pre-Tang and Tang fragmentary *Śrīmālā* commentaries preserved at Dūnhuáng.
