# Ā mí tuó jīng tōng zàn shū 阿彌陀經通贊疏
*Through-Eulogy Commentary on the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 撰)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle commentary on the *[[KR6f0082|Smaller Sukhāvatī]]* is the more substantial of [[窺基]] Kuījī's two commentaries on the work (alongside the more compact [[KR6f0088|T1757]]). The *tōng zàn* "through-eulogy" format includes verse-eulogies alongside the prose commentary.
