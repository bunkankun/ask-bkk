## About the work

This 3-fascicle commentary on the *[[KR6f0060|Wú liàng shòu jīng T0360]]* is by [[璟興]] Kyŏnghŭng — one of the major Silla-period Korean Pure Land scholars after [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo. The work documents the post-Wǒnhyo Silla Pure Land doctrinal tradition in its mature form.
