# Fóshuō Shǎnzǐ jīng 佛說睒子經 (T175c)
*Sūtra of Śyāmaka, spoken by the Buddha (third recension)*
by [[聖堅]] (Shèngjiān / \*Āryasthira, 譯)

## About the work

The third Taishō recension of [[聖堅]] Shèngjiān's *Śyāmaka-jātaka* translation; signature 「姚秦三藏法師釋聖堅譯」. The dynastic header — 姚秦 ("Yáo-Qín", i.e., the Latter Qín) — represents a catalog mis-attribution of Shèngjiān's translation to the Latter Qín rather than the Western Qín; modern scholarship treats Shèngjiān as a Western-Qín translator, and the 姚秦 attribution as a canonical-tradition variant introduced in later catalogs.

### Prefaces

No preface or postface; only the canonical signature.

### Abstract

T175c is the third of three Taishō recensions of [[聖堅]] Shèngjiān's *Śyāmaka-jātaka* translation; see [[KR6b0027|T175a]] and [[KR6b0028|T175b]]. For broader context on the *Śyāmaka* narrative, see [[KR6b0026|T174]].
