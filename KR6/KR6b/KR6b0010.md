# Dàshèng běnshēng xīndìguān jīng qiǎnzhù kēwén 大乘本生心地觀經淺註科文
*Topical-Outline (Kē-wén) to the Shallow Notes on the Mahāyāna Mind-Ground Sūtra*
by [[來舟]] (Lái Zhōu, 撰)

## About the work

A single-fascicle *kēwén* 科文 (topical-outline) accompanying Lái Zhōu's eleven-fascicle [[KR6b0011|*Qiǎnzhù* 淺註 (X367)]] commentary on [[KR6b0008|T159]]. The *kēwén* genre is the standard East-Asian Buddhist diagrammatic-tabular outline of a *sūtra*'s structure: each section, sub-section, and clause is labelled with a tree-form numbering (○九, 二, 三 …), giving a complete topical map of the *sūtra* organised hierarchically. The *kēwén* is normally read alongside the line-by-line commentary, whose section-divisions correspond to the *kēwén*'s nodes.

### Prefaces

The fascicle bears no separate preface; the author and commitment-of-method are inherited from the companion *Xuánshì* [[KR6b0009]] and *Qiǎnzhù* [[KR6b0011]]. The catalog meta does not name an author for [[KR6b0010]] explicitly, but the work is universally treated as part of Lái Zhōu's commentarial set on T159 and is signed in the canonical apparatus accordingly.

### Abstract

The *Kēwén* gives the explicit topical-outline structure that the line-by-line *Qiǎnzhù* assumes; it is the navigational armature of the commentary. The outline divides the *sūtra* into its *xù* 序 / *zhèng* 正 / *liútōng* 流通 sections in the standard Chinese Buddhist exegetical scheme, then sub-divides each chapter and verse-paragraph into its constituent topical units. This work is therefore not exegetical in its own right but is the indispensable companion-text by which the *Qiǎnzhù*'s commentarial divisions are read.
