translated by [[沮渠京聲]] Jǔqú Jīngshēng (譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Zhāntuóyuè guówáng jīng* (T518) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by [[沮渠京聲]] Jǔqú Jīngshēng. The protagonist *Zhāntuóyuè* 旃陀越 (also written 旃陀掘) is a phonetic transliteration possibly of *Caṇḍrāvatī* or another Indic toponym.
