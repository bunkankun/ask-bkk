# Fóshuō Shùtíjiā jīng 佛說樹提伽經
*Sūtra of Jyotiṣka*
translated by [[求那跋陀羅]] (Guṇabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Shùtíjiā jīng* (T540a) is the "a" recension of the *Jyotiṣka* sūtra, translated by Guṇabhadra 求那跋陀羅 ([[求那跋陀羅]]). *Shùtíjiā* 樹提伽 is a phonetic transliteration of *Jyotiṣka* — a wealthy lay Buddhist disciple. See [[KR6i0171]] (T540b) for the parallel "b" recension.
