# Zhù dàshèng rù léngqié jīng 注大乘入楞伽經
*Annotated [Sūtra of] Mahāyāna Entry into Laṅkā*
composed by [[寶臣]] (Bǎochén, 述)

## About the work

T1791 in ten fascicles is the Sòng-period commentary on [[實叉難陀]]'s *[[KR6i0329|Dàshèng rù léngqié jīng]]* (T672) — by far the most substantial classical commentary on the Tang *Laṅkāvatāra* and the principal medium through which the seven-fascicle version was studied in the Sòng. The genre is *zhù* 注 (running annotation): the parent sūtra is reproduced in full, with [[寶臣]]'s commentary inserted at each lemma.

### Abstract

The author [[寶臣]] (DILA Authority A001919) is identified in the colophon as a monk of the eastern capital (東都僧) — i.e. Kāifēng under the Northern Sòng. His biographical record is otherwise essentially empty; [[寶臣]] is known almost solely for T1791. Date bracket follows the conservative Northern-Sòng eastern-capital window (the Sòng moved capitals to Lín'ān after 1127); within that, the doctrinal vocabulary of the commentary is consistent with a date in the eleventh century. T1791 became the most commonly used Sòng commentary on T672 in the Yuán and Míng periods, and was the basis of the late-Míng commentaries by [[德清]] ([[KR6i0343|X326]], [[KR6i0344|X327]]), [[智旭]] ([[KR6i0345|X328]], [[KR6i0346|X329]]), [[通潤]] ([[KR6i0348|X331]]), and others.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0329]] (大乘入楞伽經 / T672); cognate Huáyán commentary [[KR6i0333]] (入楞伽心玄義 / T1790).
