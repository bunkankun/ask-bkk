# Wèilái xīngxiù jié qiān fómíng jīng 未來星宿劫千佛名經
*Sūtra of the Names of the Thousand Buddhas of the Future Starry Aeon* (recension a)
Translator unknown ([[失譯]])

## About the work

The *Wèilái xīngxiù jié qiān fómíng jīng* enumerates the thousand Buddhas of the future aeon, the "Starry Aeon" (*xīngxiù jié* 星宿劫), completing the "three aeons, three thousand Buddhas" (*sān jié sānqiān fó*) system alongside the past-aeon ([[KR6i0022]]–[[KR6i0023]]) and present-aeon ([[KR6i0024]]–[[KR6i0025]]) texts. Two recensions are preserved in the Taishō: T448a (this text) and T448b ([[KR6i0027]]). The text is anonymous; the "a" recension is supported by major canon editions.

### Abstract

T448a is the principal Taishō recension of the future-aeon thousand-Buddha-names list. The "Starry Aeon" (*xīngxiù jié* 星宿劫) is a Chinese Buddhist cosmological concept for the aeon following the present bhadrakalpa, in which a thousand more Buddhas will appear; the name suggests an aeon characterized by stars (*xīngxiù* 星宿, "lunar lodges / asterisms"). The three-aeon structure completes a devotional calendar that encompasses all of Buddhist time. This text completes the group; see [[KR6i0022]]–[[KR6i0025]] for the past- and present-aeon parallels.
