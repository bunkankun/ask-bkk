## About the work

The *Fóshuō Mòluó wáng jīng* (T517) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by [[沮渠京聲]] Jǔqú Jīngshēng. The protagonist *Mòluó wáng* 末羅王 may render Sanskrit *Malla-rāja* (king of the Malla clan, who hosted the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* at Kuśinagara).
