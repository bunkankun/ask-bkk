# Fóshuō Púsà Shì jīng 佛說菩薩逝經
*Sūtra of the Bodhisattva Shì*
translated by [[白法祖]] (Bái Fǎzǔ, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Púsà Shì jīng* (T528) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Bái Fǎzǔ 白法祖 ([[白法祖]]). It is a parallel translation to [[KR6i0157]] *Shì tóngzǐ jīng* (T527) by Zhī Fǎdù — both render the same Indic narrative about a boy named *Shì* (Sanskrit *Jiyaka*?) who is given Buddhahood-prediction.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

The two parallel translations [[KR6i0157]] and [[KR6i0158]] preserve the early Western Jìn Chinese reception of this narrative. Bái Fǎzǔ was a Bái 白 clan monk active in the early Western Jìn at Cháng'ān; he is recorded as the translator of about 23 short texts. Translation dated to ca. 290–306 CE.
