# Sīhēmèi jīng 私呵昧經
*Sūtra of Sīhēmèi*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Sīhēmèi jīng* (T532) is a one-fascicle short Mahāyāna sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). *Sīhēmèi* 私呵昧 transliterates an unidentified Sanskrit name (possibly *Siṃhamati*).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna text on the bodhisattva path. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
