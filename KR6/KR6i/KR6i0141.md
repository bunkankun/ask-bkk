# Fóshuō Píngshā wáng wǔyuàn jīng 佛說蓱沙王五願經
*Sūtra of the Five Vows of King Bimbisāra*
translated by [[支謙]] Zhī Qiān (譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Píngshā wáng wǔyuàn jīng* (T511) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by [[支謙]] Zhī Qiān. The protagonist *Píngshā* 蓱沙 is a Chinese phonetic transliteration of *Bimbisāra* — the king of Magadha and a major patron of the Buddha (also father of Ajātaśatru, cf. [[KR6i0137]]–[[KR6i0139]]). The text narrates Bimbisāra's five vows as a young prince, all of which were fulfilled — receiving the throne, meeting an enlightened teacher (the Buddha), receiving Dharma, etc.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A canonical narrative of King Bimbisāra's piety, demonstrating the principle that proper vow-making leads to the fulfillment of one's aspirations. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE). The text has parallels in the Pāli Vinaya and the *Saṃyutta-nikāya*.
