# Sīyì Fàntiān suǒwèn jīng jiǎnzhù 思益梵天所問經簡註
*Concise Notes on the Sūtra of the Questions of Brahmā Viśeṣacintin*
annotated by [[圓澄]] (Yuánchéng, 註)

## About the work

This is a four-fascicle Late-Ming "concise notes" (*jiǎnzhù* 簡註) commentary on Kumārajīva's [[KR6i0218]] *Sīyì Fàntiān suǒwèn jīng*, by the Cáodòng 曹洞 Chán master Zhànrán Yuánchéng 湛然圓澄 ([[圓澄]]; 1561–1626). The commentary is typical of late-Ming Chán-flavored sūtra exegesis: terse glosses on the canonical text, paired with brief Chán-style remarks on the doctrinal heart of each chapter.

### Prefaces

The Xùzàngjīng edition preserves Yuánchéng's own preface explaining his motive — to make the famously dense Kumārajīva translation accessible to Chán practitioners.

### Abstract

A late-Ming devotional and exegetical work attached to Kumārajīva's *Sīyì Fàntiān suǒwèn jīng*. Yuánchéng was a major Cáodòng revivalist active at Yúnmén 雲門 in Zhèjiāng and is better known for his Chán records (*Zhànrán Yuánchéng chánshī yǔlù* 湛然圓澄禪師語錄 ([[KR6q0374]]), *Kǎigǔ lù* 慨古錄 ([[KR6q0171]]), *Zōngmén huòwèn* 宗門或問 ([[宗門或問]])). The *Jiǎnzhù* offers a window onto how late-Ming Chán reread classical Mahāyāna scripture.
