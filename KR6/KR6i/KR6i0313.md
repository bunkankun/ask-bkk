# Jīnguāngmíng jīng wénjù wénjùjì huìběn 金光明經文句文句記會本
*Combined Edition of the Phrase-and-Sentence Commentary on the Sūtra of Golden Light with the Notes on the Phrase-and-Sentence Commentary*
expounded by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ, 說), recorded by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 錄), commented by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ, 述), collated by [[明得]] (Míngdé, 會)

## About the work

X358 is the eight-fascicle *huìběn* 會本 ("combined edition") of [[KR6i0306|*Wénjù* 文句]] (T1785) and its [[KR6i0307|*Wénjù jì* 文句記]] (T1786), the *wénjù* counterpart to [[KR6i0311]] (X356). Like its companion, the work interleaves the parent commentary by [[智顗]] / [[灌頂]] with the subcommentary by [[知禮]] at the corresponding lemmata. The longer extent (eight fascicles vs two for X356) reflects the greater length of the parent *wénjù* commentary and the *jì* subcommentary as compared with the *xuányì* / *shíyíjì* pair.

### Abstract

Compiled by [[明得]] in the late Ming, c. 1560–1588 (his mature productive period; cf. DILA A008323). The four-author attribution in the colophon — 隋天台智者大師說 / 唐章安灌頂大師錄 / 宋四明法智大師述 / 明月亭明得大師會 — is unusual and reflects the *huìběn*'s status as a single unified working text drawing the entire commentarial layering of the *wénjù* line into one book. As with X356, of pedagogical rather than original-doctrinal value, but the standard form in which Late-Míng and Qīng-dynasty Tiāntái students engaged the parent commentaries.

Related canonical texts: [[KR6i0306]] (金光明經文句 / T1785), [[KR6i0307]] (金光明經文句記 / T1786).
