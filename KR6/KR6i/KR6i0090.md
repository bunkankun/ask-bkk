# Wéimó jīng shū 維摩經疏
*Commentary on the Vimalakīrti Sūtra*
(anonymous Dunhuang manuscript commentary)

## About the work

The *Wéimó jīng shū* (T2771) is another anonymous one-fascicle Vimalakīrti commentary from Dunhuang, distinct from but adjacent to [[KR6i0089]] (T2770). The Taishō editorial team kept these as separate texts because the manuscript witnesses, paleography, and doctrinal content differ enough to indicate independent works.

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the commentary; the manuscript is fragmentary and lacks a preface or colophon.

### Abstract

T2771 is part of the Dunhuang Vimalakīrti commentary cluster. The commentary's exegetical style is direct and gloss-oriented, suggesting a teaching-text or *jiǎng yì* 講義 (lecture-notes) origin. The Tang dating bracket (600–850 CE) is established by manuscript paleography and doctrinal content; precise authorship and date are not recoverable.
