# Wǔmǔ zǐ jīng 五母子經
*Sūtra of the Five Mothers and the Son (Recension a)*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Wǔmǔ zǐ jīng* (T555a) is the "a" recension of a one-fascicle short sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). The narrative concerns a son who in successive past lives had five different mothers, illustrating the karmic ties of family relations across rebirths.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna* exemplifying the doctrine of inter-life karmic family ties. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
