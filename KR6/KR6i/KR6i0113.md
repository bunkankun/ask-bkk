## About the work

The *Bùsīyì guāng púsà suǒshuō jīng* (T484) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra translated by [[鳩摩羅什]] Kumārajīva at Cháng'ān. The protagonist is the bodhisattva *Bùsīyì guāng* 不思議光 ("Inconceivable Light"; Sanskrit *Acintyaprabhāsa*).
