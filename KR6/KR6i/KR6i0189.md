# Fóshuō Lóngshī nǚ jīng 佛說龍施女經
*Sūtra of the Nāga-Donor Maiden*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Lóngshī nǚ jīng* (T557) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). The protagonist *Lóngshī* 龍施 ("Nāga-Donor"; Sanskrit *Nāgadattā*) is a young nāga-girl who exemplifies female bodhisattva-realization. Cf. [[KR6i0190]] (T558, *Lóngshī púsà běn qǐ jīng*) translated by Dharmarakṣa.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

This is one of the "wise women" / female bodhisattva texts, doctrinally close to the Nāga-girl narrative in the *Lotus Sūtra*. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
