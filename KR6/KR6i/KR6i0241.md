# Fóshuō Chánxíng sānshíqī pǐn jīng 佛說禪行三十七品經
*Sūtra of the Thirty-Seven Factors of Meditation Practice*
attributed to [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Chánxíng sānshíqī pǐn jīng* (T604) is a one-fascicle short sūtra-text on the thirty-seven *bodhipakṣya-dharmas* (the 37 limbs of awakening) framed as items of *chánxíng* 禪行 ("meditation practice"), traditionally attributed to Ān Shìgāo 安世高 ([[安世高]]).

### Abstract

A short exposition of the standard early-Buddhist scheme of the 37 *bodhipakṣya-dharmas* (4 *smṛtyupasthāna*, 4 *samyakprahāṇa*, 4 *ṛddhipāda*, 5 *indriya*, 5 *bala*, 7 *bodhyaṅga*, 8-fold *mārga*). Traditional ascription to Ān Shìgāo gives the bracket of his Luòyáng activity (148–170 CE). Modern scholarship (Nattier 2008) classes the text among the "uncertain" Ān Shìgāo attributions; the conservative bracket is retained here.
