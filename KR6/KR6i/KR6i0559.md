# Yuánjué jīng lüèshū chāo 圓覺經略疏鈔
*Subcommentary on the Abridged Commentary to the Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment*
by [[宗密]] (Guīfēng Zōngmì, 於大疏略出, "extracted in summary form from the Great Commentary")

## About the work

The *Yuánjué jīng lüèshū chāo* in 12 fascicles is the line-by-line subcommentary by [[宗密]] Guīfēng Zōngmì (780–841) on his own [[KR6i0553]] *Yuánjué jīng lüèshū* 圓覺經略疏. It is the working-classroom companion to the *Lüèshū*, just as the [[KR6i0557]] *Dàshū chāo* is the companion to the [[KR6i0555]] *Dàshū*. The persons-line in the catalog meta — *於大疏略出*, "extracted in summary form from the Great Commentary" — gives the precise compositional history: the *Lüèshū chāo* is not an independent fresh subcommentary but a compressed re-arrangement of the much longer *Dàshū chāo*'s glosses, attached to the line-text of the *Lüèshū* and trimmed to fit it. The result is the most-used form of Zōngmì's *Yuánjué jīng* exegesis: substantial enough to give the doctrinal apparatus, short enough to be navigable.

### Abstract

The *Lüèshū chāo* is the *de facto* textbook by which the *Yuánjué jīng* has been read in East Asian Buddhism since the Sòng. The Korean and Japanese commentary traditions both build on it; the major Sòng *Yuánjué* commentaries by [[觀復]] ([[KR6i0560]]) and others are typically responses to, or summaries of, the *Lüèshū chāo* rather than independent expositions of the sūtra. Like the rest of Zōngmì's *Yuánjué* commentary apparatus, the *Lüèshū chāo* survives only through Korean and Japanese transmission. The Xùzàng text is an Edo Ōbaku-print derived from a Korean ancestor; the standard modern edition is X09 no. 248. The work is occasionally cataloged under titles such as *Yuánjué jīng shū chāo* 圓覺經疏鈔 or simply *Yuánjué jīng chāo*, but the *lüèshū chāo* form is the canonical one.
