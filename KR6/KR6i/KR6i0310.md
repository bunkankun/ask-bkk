# Jīnguāngmíng jīng xuányì kē 金光明經玄義科
*Topical Outline of the* Profound Meaning of the Sūtra of Golden Light
arranged by [[明得]] (Míngdé, 排定)

## About the work

X355 is a one-fascicle *kē* 科 (topical outline / structural divisions) of *[[KR6i0304|Jīnguāngmíng xuányì 金光明玄義]]* (T1783) — that is, a tabular skeleton of Zhìyǐ's commentary, organising its argument under nested rubrics. The genre serves as a study aid: each major and sub-division of the parent text is laid out as a hierarchical bullet-list. The compiler is identified in the Taishō witness as 雙徑沙門明得排定, "[[明得]] of Mt Shuāngjìng (= Jīngshān 徑山), arranged".

### Abstract

The work is one of [[明得]]'s four ancillary Tiāntái treatises on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* corpus, prepared together with [[KR6i0312]] (*Wénjù kē* on T1785) and the two *huìběn* collations [[KR6i0311]] and [[KR6i0313]]. The four are explicitly listed in 明得's DILA biography (A008323) and are paired with his three-year retreat at Língxiāo Peak on Mt Jīngshān and subsequent lecturing at the Chuányīān; the most defensible date bracket follows his mature Tiāntái productive period (c. 1560–1588). The treatise is essentially diagrammatic and adds little doctrinal content of its own, but is invaluable as a finding-aid for [[KR6i0304|T1783]] and as documentation of late-Míng Tiāntái pedagogical practice.

Related canonical texts: parent commentary [[KR6i0304]] (金光明經玄義 / T1783); paired *kē* [[KR6i0312]] (金光明經文句科 / X357).
