# Fó wèi niánshào bǐqiū shuō zhèngshì jīng 佛為年少比丘說正事經
*Sūtra of the Buddha Teaching Correct Matters to Young Monks*
translated by [[法炬]] Fǎjù (譯)

## About the work

The *Fó wèi niánshào bǐqiū shuō zhèngshì jīng* (T502) is a one-fascicle short sūtra translated by [[法炬]] Fǎjù. It contains the Buddha's pedagogical instruction to young monks (*nián-shào bǐqiū* 年少比丘) on the proper conduct of a *bhikṣu*.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Hīnayāna sūtra on monastic conduct, addressed to novice monks. Translation falls within Fǎjù's productive period (290–306 CE).
