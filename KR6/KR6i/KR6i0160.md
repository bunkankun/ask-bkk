# Fóshuō Xūmótí zhǎngzhě jīng 佛說須摩提長者經
*Sūtra of the Elder Sumati*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Xūmótí zhǎngzhě jīng* (T530) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). *Xūmótí* 須摩提 transliterates *Sumati* (one of the standard Buddhist personal names).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna* on an elder named Sumati. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
