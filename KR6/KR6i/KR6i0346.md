# Léngqié jīng yìshū 楞伽經義疏
*Doctrinal Commentary on the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
composed by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒuyì, 疏義)

## About the work

X329 in four fascicles is [[智旭]]'s phrase-by-phrase commentary on *[[KR6i0327|Léngqiéābáduōluó bǎo jīng 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經]]* (T670), paired with the *[[KR6i0345|xuányì]]* (X328). The pair structurally mirrors the Tiāntái-school's commentarial doublet on its principal sūtras (*xuányì* + *wénjù*); [[智旭]] adapted that pattern from the Tiāntái master tradition to the *Laṅkāvatāra*.

### Abstract

Date bracket follows [[智旭]]'s late productive period at Língfēngsì 靈峯寺 (1635–1655). The *yìshū* draws on the doctrinal programme laid out in the *xuányì* and applies it lemma-by-lemma to the parent sūtra. The commentary is doctrinally syncretic in [[智旭]]'s characteristic late-Míng manner — Yogācāra, Tathāgatagarbha, Chán, Tiāntái, and Pure-Land categories all engaged as the parent sūtra warrants — and is one of the most thorough monastic commentaries on T670 produced in the late imperial period.

X328 + X329 + Hānshān's [[KR6i0343|X326]] + [[曾鳳儀]]'s [[KR6i0347|X330]] + [[通潤]]'s [[KR6i0348|X331]] together represent the late-Míng *Laṅkāvatāra* commentarial canon, constituted within roughly one generation of monastic and lay scholars in the Wànlì–Chóngzhēn–early-Qīng transition.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670); paired commentary [[KR6i0345]] (楞伽經玄義 / X328).
