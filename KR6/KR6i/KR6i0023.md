# Guòqù zhuāngyán jié qiān fómíng jīng 過去莊嚴劫千佛名經
*Sūtra of the Names of the Thousand Buddhas of the Past Ornate Aeon* (recension b)
Translator unknown ([[失譯]])

## About the work

The *Guòqù zhuāngyán jié qiān fómíng jīng* (T446b) is the second recension of the Past Ornate Aeon thousand-Buddha-names text. It differs from recension a ([[KR6i0022]], T446a) in textual organization and may represent a different manuscript tradition or a supplementary list. The source text (T446b) carries a variant title: 過去莊嚴劫千佛名經(一名「集諸佛大功德山」), giving the alternative title "Collection of the Great Merit-Mountain of All Buddhas" (集諸佛大功德山). The "orig" edition note (in place of a named printed edition) suggests this recension was preserved in an earlier manuscript tradition not fully represented in the major printed canons. As with recension a, no translator is identified.

### Prefaces

No translator colophon; the body begins with the alternative title and the Buddha-name list.

### Abstract

T446b represents a parallel textual transmission of the thousand-Buddha-names for the past aeon, distinguished from T446a ([[KR6i0022]]) by its alternative title (集諸佛大功德山) and differing textual arrangement. The "a" and "b" recensions together constitute the full Taishō witness to this particular thousand-Buddha-names list. Both are anonymous, undatable with precision, and are best understood as products of the Chinese liturgical elaboration of the "three aeons, three thousand Buddhas" (*sān jié sānqiān fó* 三劫三千佛) devotional system. See also [[KR6i0022]], [[KR6i0024]]–[[KR6i0027]].
