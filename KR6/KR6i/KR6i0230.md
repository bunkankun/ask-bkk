# Fóshuō Tiānwáng tàizǐ Bìluó jīng 佛說天王太子辟羅經
*Sūtra of the Heavenly King's Heir Apparent Bìluó*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Tiānwáng tàizǐ Bìluó jīng* (T596) is a one-fascicle anonymous short sūtra. *Bìluó* 辟羅 transcribes Sanskrit \*Vipula or \*Pīla — the prince of a heavenly king, who serves as the protagonist of a brief conversion-narrative.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. No translator attribution survives.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna *avadāna* in the *devaputra*-prince genre. Catalogue assignment is to the post-Han / pre-Tang anonymous corpus. The conservative bracket 317–600 follows the standard *shīyì* horizon. Contents are typical for the genre: a divine prince approaches the Buddha, asks doctrinal questions, takes refuge.
