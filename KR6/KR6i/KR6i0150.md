# Fóshuō Sàluó guó jīng 佛說薩羅國經
*Sūtra of the Kingdom of Sàluó*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Sàluó guó jīng* (T520) is a one-fascicle short sūtra of unknown translation attribution. *Sàluó guó* 薩羅國 is likely a transcription of an Indic toponym, possibly *Śāla* or another minor kingdom.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. There is no surviving translator attribution.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative on a kingdom and its Buddhist conversion. The [[僧祐]] Sēngyòu *Chū sānzàng jì jí* lists the text as *shīyì* without dynasty assignment; the bracket of 200–600 CE reflects the standard Pre-Sui *shīyì* anonymous corpus.
