# Yuánjué jīng lüèshū xùzhù 圓覺經略疏序注
*Annotations on the Preface to the Abridged Commentary on the Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment*
by [[如山]] (Rúshān, 注)

## About the work

A single-fascicle annotated commentary on the preface — by [[裴休]] Péi Xiū 裴休 (787–860) — to [[宗密]] Guīfēng Zōngmì's [[KR6i0553]] *Yuánjué jīng lüèshū*. Composed by the Sòng-era monk [[如山]] Rúshān 如山 of the Zhāngjiāng Chán Yuàn 章江禪院 at Lóngshā 龍沙 (modern Nánchāng, Jiāngxī), it is the companion piece to [[KR6i0566]] *Yuánjué jīng xùzhù* (which treats Péi's preface to the sūtra itself) and shares its line-by-line annotative method.

### Abstract

Where the sister work treats the more general preface to the sūtra, the present *xùzhù* takes Péi Xiū's narrower technical preface to Zōngmì's *Lüèshū*. Péi's *Lüèshū* preface contains substantial autobiographical material — including his account of his discipleship under Zōngmì, his receipt of Zōngmì's writings, and the famous formulation 教也者，諸佛菩薩所留之言；禪也者，諸佛菩薩所傳之心 ("'Teaching' is the words bequeathed by the Buddhas and bodhisattvas; 'Chán' is the mind transmitted by the Buddhas and bodhisattvas") — and Rúshān's annotations are correspondingly oriented to biographical, lineage, and doctrinal-historical clarification. The work supplies extensive material on Zōngmì's biography and on the relationship between the *Lüèshū* and the larger *Dàshū* corpus.

Rúshān's lifedates and biographical details are not preserved; the catalog and the work itself simply identify him as *Lóngshā Zhāngjiāng Chányuàn shāmén* 龍沙章江禪院沙門. As with the *xùzhù*, the composition window is bracketed at 1100–1279 on the basis of internal evidence.

## Other points of interest

The two *xùzhù* texts together preserve a substantial Sòng-era reading of Péi Xiū's biography and lineage, including the *sānshēng* 三生 ("three lives") narrative connecting Péi to the Eastern-Jìn lay-Buddhist [[許玄度]] Xǔ Xuándù — material that is otherwise scattered through Sòng *gāosēng zhuàn* but here gathered into a compact commentarial format.
