# Bā dàrén jué jīng shū 八大人覺經疏
*A Subcommentary on the Sūtra of the Eight Great-Person Awakenings*
compiled by [[續法]] (Xùfǎ = Bǎitíng, 集)

## About the work

A one-fascicle commentary on the *Bā dàrén jué jīng* 八大人覺經 ([[KR6i0476]], T779) by the Qīng-dynasty Huáyán scholar-monk [[續法]] (Xùfǎ = Bǎitíng 柏亭, 1641–1728), one of the principal late-Qīng commentators on Huáyán-school texts. The work is preserved in the Manji *Zoku-zōkyō* (CBETA X37 No. 673).

### Abstract

Xùfǎ's *shū* (subcommentary / extended exegesis) is markedly fuller than [[智旭]] Zhìxù's shorter *Lüèjiě* ([[KR6i0477]]) and reflects the Qīng-period scholastic tendency toward systematic exposition with doctrinal cross-references. Xùfǎ approaches the sūtra through Huáyán-school doctrinal categories: he reads the eight awakenings as a graded sequence of contemplative practices culminating in the bodhisattva's universal vow, and he organises the exposition around the Huáyán *yīzhēn fǎjiè* 一真法界 (one-true-Dharma-realm) doctrine. The work draws on a wide range of canonical and exegetical sources, including the *Avataṃsaka-sūtra*, the *Mahā-prajñā-pāramitā-śāstra*, and the standard Tiāntái *zhì-guān* texts, providing the reader with comprehensive doctrinal context for each of the eight contemplations.

Xùfǎ also produced a much fuller five-fascicle subcommentary on the *Sìshí'èrzhāng jīng shūchāo* 四十二章經疏鈔 ([[KR6i0487]]), reflecting his programmatic engagement with the "Three Sūtras of the Buddha-Patriarchs" — the *Sìshí'èrzhāng jīng*, the *Bā dàrén jué jīng*, and the *Yíjiào jīng* — as foundational monastic curriculum texts.
