# Jīnguāngmíng jīng wénjù 金光明經文句
*Phrase-and-Sentence Commentary on the Sūtra of Golden Light*
expounded by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ, 說), recorded by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng, 錄)

## About the work

T1785 in six fascicles is the Tiāntái-school *wénjù* 文句 ("phrase-and-sentence") commentary on *[[KR6i0301|Jīnguāngmíng jīng 金光明經]]* (T663). The *wénjù* genre, paired with the *xuányì* 玄義, follows the parent sūtra phrase-by-phrase under the four-section *yīnyuán*, *yuējiào*, *běnjì*, *guānxīn* (因緣, 約教, 本迹, 觀心) interpretive grid characteristic of Zhìyǐ's exegetical method. Together with the *[[KR6i0304|xuányì (T1783)]]*, this is the second leg of the Tiāntái commentarial doublet on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa*.

### Abstract

The opening lines preserved in the Taishō text give the standard Tiāntái attribution: 隋天台智者大師說 / 門人灌頂錄. The dating bracket follows Zhìyǐ's lecturing period at Yùquánsì 玉泉寺 (585–597). The first folio of the work itself frames its reading of [[KR6i0301|T663]]'s 18 chapters as a critique of two prior exegetical traditions — the "Jiāngběi school" (江北諸師) and the "Jiāngnán school" (江南諸師) — both of which had divided the sūtra into a different *xùzhèngliútōng* 序正流通 (introduction / main-doctrine / circulation) tripartite frame. Zhìyǐ's reading rejects both and proposes a new three-part division anchored on the doctrinal centrality of the *Shòuliàng pǐn* 壽量品 (chapter 2 on Buddha's lifespan).

T1785 is foundational for the East Asian *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* commentarial tradition. [[知禮]]'s *[[KR6i0307|Wénjù jì 文句記]]* (T1786) is the principal subcommentary; [[從義]]'s *[[KR6i0315|Wénjù xīnjì 文句新記]]* is the rival but later assimilated Sòng commentary; and the Ming-period [[明得]] *[[KR6i0313|Wénjù wénjùjì huìběn 文句文句記會本]]* (X358) collates 智顗 + 灌頂 + 知禮 + 明得 into a single working text. Like T1783, T1785 also figured in the *guānxīn shì* dispute between *shānjiā* and *shānwài*.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0301]] (金光明經 / T663); subcommentary [[KR6i0307]] (金光明經文句記 / T1786).
