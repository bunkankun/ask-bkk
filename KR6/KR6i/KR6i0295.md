# Fóshuō huáshǒu jīng 佛說華手經
*Sūtra of the Flower-Hand as Spoken by the Buddha*
translated by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Jiūmóluóshí / Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

T657 (ten fascicles, alt. title 華首經; Sanskrit *Kuśalamūlasamparigraha-sūtra*, alt. *Kuśalamūlaparidhara*) is a major Mahāyāna *sūtra* on the bodhisattva's accumulation of meritorious roots (*kuśala-mūla*), translated by [[鳩摩羅什]] at Cháng'ān. The *huáshǒu* "Flower-Hand" of the title is a bodhisattva who serves as the principal interlocutor; the *sūtra* is built around his questions to the Buddha about how a bodhisattva should accumulate, protect, and dedicate the roots of merit.

### Structural Division

T657 is a long text in 35 chapters covering the entire bodhisattva path. Principal sections include 序品 (introductory), 神力品 (spiritual powers), 不退轉品 (irreversibility), 為法品 (acting for the Dharma), 法門品 (dharma-gates), 囑累品 (entrustment). The full chapter list is preserved in CANWWW (T16N0657-1 through T16N0657-35).

### Abstract

T657 is unanimously ascribed to [[鳩摩羅什]] in the canonical bibliographies. Date bracket follows his Cháng'ān period (401–413). The Sanskrit *Kuśalamūlasamparigraha-sūtra* is referenced in [[KR6c0005|the *Dà zhìdù lùn*]] and survives in fragments; a complete text is preserved in Tibetan. The Chinese translation is one of Kumārajīva's longer works and is stylistically continuous with the *Mahāprajñāpāramitā* group: long doctrinal expositions interleaved with verse summaries, *avadāna*-style narratives, and bodhisattva-encounter scenes. Together with [[KR6i0294|T656]] (Zhú Fóniàn's *Púsà yīngluò*), T657 is among the major source texts for Chinese conceptions of the *kuśala-mūla* — the "roots of good" that a bodhisattva must plant, protect from being uprooted (e.g. by anger), and dedicate to enlightenment.
