# Dàshèng dàogǎn jīng suítīng shū jué 大乘稻芉經隨聽疏決
*Resolution / Sub-commentary on the Lecture-Record Commentary on the Mahāyāna Śālistamba-sūtra*
author unknown

## About the work

T2783 in one fascicle is an anonymous sub-commentary or *jué* 決 ("resolution") on [[法成]]'s *Dàshèng dàogǎn jīng suítīng shū* ([[KR6i0405]] / T2782). It survives, like its parent text, only in the Dūnhuáng manuscripts and is preserved in Taishō volume 85 (*Gǔyìbù*). The author is not identified in the manuscript. The dating bracket is uncertain: the *jué* genre indicates a clarification of doctrinal points raised in the lecture-record, suggesting composition by a follower of [[法成]] at Dūnhuáng during the late Tang or early Five Dynasties period.

### Abstract

The *jué* 決 genre — "resolution" or "decision" — is a sub-commentarial form addressing specific doctrinal cruces in the parent lecture-record. The *Suítīng shū jué* poses and resolves questions about [[法成]]'s exposition of the twelve nidānas in [[KR6i0405]]. Where Chos-grub's commentary follows the sequence of the base sūtra ([[KR6i0404]]) line-by-line, the *jué* is selective, treating only the points where the master's exposition required further elaboration.

The text is a witness to the **active commentarial tradition that developed around [[法成]]'s lectures at Dūnhuáng** in the ninth and tenth centuries. The pairing of base text + lecture-record + *jué* (sūtra → shū → jué) is a three-tier structure characteristic of the systematic Buddhist scholarship Chos-grub introduced. Like its parent, the text never entered the printed canon and was recovered only with the opening of Dūnhuáng cave 17.
