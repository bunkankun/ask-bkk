# Āshéshì wáng shòujué jīng 阿闍世王授決經
*Sūtra of King Ajātaśatru Receiving the Buddhahood Prediction*
translated by [[法炬]] Fǎjù (譯)

## About the work

The *Āshéshì wáng shòujué jīng* (T509) is a one-fascicle sūtra translated by [[法炬]] Fǎjù. It narrates Ajātaśatru's reception of *vyākaraṇa* (*shòujué* 授決, prediction of future buddhahood) — a major Mahāyāna soteriological event in which the unrepentantly sinful king is nonetheless predicted to attain buddhahood, demonstrating the Mahāyāna doctrine that no being is excluded from the path.

### Abstract

The Ajātaśatru *vyākaraṇa* narrative is foundational for Chinese Mahāyāna doctrines of universal redemption. The narrative is treated extensively in the *Mahāparinirvāṇa-sūtra* tradition (T374, T375) and in Mahāyāna apocryphal narratives. Translation falls within Fǎjù's productive period (290–306 CE).
