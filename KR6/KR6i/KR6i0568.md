# Yuánjué jīng jízhù 圓覺經集註
*Collected Annotations on the Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment*
by [[元粹]] (Yuáncuì, 述)

## About the work

A 2-fascicle anthological commentary on the *Yuánjué jīng* ([[KR6i0551]]) compiled by the late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái master [[元粹]] Yuáncuì 元粹 (*hào* Gǔyún 古雲), styled in the work *Tiáoshuǐ shāmén* 苕水沙門. The work is in the standard *jízhù* 集註 ("collected annotations") format: each lemma of the sūtra is followed by a curated selection of glosses drawn from the principal earlier commentaries — chiefly [[宗密]] Zōngmì's [[KR6i0555]] *Dàshū* and [[KR6i0553]] *Lüèshū*, but also material from the southern-Sòng *Lèijiě* tradition (see [[KR6i0563]]) and Yuáncuì's own brief connecting remarks (*sīzhù* 私註). The result is a compact, classroom-oriented guide to the sūtra suitable for the Tiāntái doctrinal curriculum.

### Abstract

The autograph preface, preserved at the head of the work (X10 no. 257), is dated 時宋嘉定七年歲在甲戌九月九日 — i.e., the *chóngyáng* (9/9) day of Jiādìng 7 = 22 October 1214 CE (Sòng Níngzōng's reign), and signed *Tiáoshuǐ shāmén Yuáncuì* 苕水沙門元粹. Yuáncuì was a senior dharma-disciple of the Tiāntái master [[北峰宗印]] Běifēng Zōngyìn (1148–1213) and a native of Tiáoxī 苕溪 (Húzhōu); his other major work is the [[KR6d0170]] *Sìjiào yí bèishì* 四教儀備釋, a Yuán-period subcommentary on Dìguān's *Tiāntái sìjiào yí*.

The *Jízhù* is the principal Tiāntái-tradition commentary on the *Yuánjué jīng* and forms a counterpart to the Huáyán-tradition exegetical apparatus represented by the [[宗密]] Zōngmì corpus. Yuáncuì's reading domesticates the sūtra into the Tiāntái doctrinal framework: the threefold *guān* of the Bodhisattva Mighty-Virtue chapter is read in close alignment with the Tiāntái *zhǐguān* 止觀 doctrine, and the twelve-bodhisattva structure is mapped onto the Tiāntái fourfold-and-fivefold doctrinal classification.

## Other points of interest

The 1214 dating makes the *Jízhù* close in time to [[清遠]] Qīngyuǎn's [[KR6i0561]] *Yuánjué jīng shūchāo suíwén yàojiě* (1213) and to [[孝宗帝]] Sòng Xiàozōng's [[KR6i0562]] *Yùzhù Yuánjué jīng* (1183). The early-thirteenth century thus emerges from the Xùzàng record as the high-point of southern-Sòng *Yuánjué* commentary production, with three major works completed within thirty years.
