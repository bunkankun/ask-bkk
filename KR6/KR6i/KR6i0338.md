# Léngqié jīng shū 楞伽經疏
*Commentary on the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
anonymous

## About the work

X322 in two fascicles is an anonymous Tang-period commentary on *[[KR6i0327|Léngqiéābáduōluó bǎo jīng 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經]]* (T670). The catalog meta has no author entry, and the witness preserved in the *Xuzangjing* is similarly without an attribution-line. The doctrinal vocabulary is consistent with the late-Tang or early-Sòng phase of *Laṅkāvatāra* commentary; the treatment of the *yuánchéngshí* 圓成實 (parispanna) and the *wàngxiǎng zìxìng* 妄想自性 (parikalpita) categories suggests a Yogācāra-influenced commentator active after [[法藏]] and the assimilation of [[實叉難陀]]'s [[KR6i0329|T672]].

### Abstract

The work is one of the few extant Tang-period anonymous *Laṅkāvatāra* commentaries, preserved only in the Wànzì *Xuzangjing*. Date bracket conservatively follows the Tang span, with a more probable concentration in the eighth or ninth century (700–900). The text is fragmentary: only a *juànshàng* portion is preserved in the *Xuzangjing* compilation, with the second fascicle either lost or never extant. The opening lemma — on the *yuánchéngshí* nature being free of being-and-non-being, with the *wàngxiǎng* (parikalpita) constructed-nature giving rise to the duality of being-and-non-being — is consistent with a Yogācāra-affiliated reading of the *Laṅkāvatāra* in the Cíēn 慈恩 tradition.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670).
