# Shíshàn yèdào jīng jiéyào 十善業道經節要
*Essentials of the Sūtra of the Path of the Ten Wholesome Actions*
edited and abridged by [[智旭]] Ǒuyì Zhìxù (編訂)

## About the work

The *Shí-shàn yèdào jīng jiéyào* (X706) is a one-fascicle late-Ming / early-Qīng abridged-and-annotated edition by Ǒuyì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭 ([[智旭]]; 1599–1655) of [[KR6i0234]] *Shí-shàn yèdào jīng* 十善業道經 (T600), Śikṣānanda's translation of the *Daśakuśala-karmapatha-sūtra*.

### Prefaces

Ǒuyì's preface — preserved in the Xùzàngjīng — explains his motive: to extract from the canonical text its essential teaching for the use of laypeople and to make it serviceable as a daily-recitation text within his project of laicized Buddhist ethics.

### Abstract

An ethical-pedagogical condensation of T600 produced as part of Ǒuyì Zhìxù's broader late-Ming / early-Qīng program of synthetic *jiào-Chánjìng* (teachings-Chán-Pure-Land) Buddhist reform. Ǒuyì's many such *jiéyào* and *yìjì* writings constitute a parallel "lay canon" of essential Mahāyāna ethics.
