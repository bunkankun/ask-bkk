# Léngqié bǔyí 楞伽補遺
*Supplementary Notes to [the Commentary on] the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
composed by [[德清]] (Déqīng / Hānshān, 撰)

## About the work

X327 in one fascicle is [[德清]]'s short *bǔyí* 補遺 ("supplement") to his major commentary *[[KR6i0343|Guān Léngqié jīng jì]]* (X326). The genre signals an addendum: passages, glosses, or doctrinal observations that Hānshān had developed after the main commentary's completion and that he wished to preserve as a coda rather than to retro-fit into the body of X326.

### Abstract

Date bracket follows Hānshān's late productive period (post-1606 partial rehabilitation, pre-death 1623), and is presumably very late in that bracket since the *bǔyí* presupposes the completion of [[KR6i0343|X326]]. The work is a coda to X326 and should be read with that commentary; it is one of the smaller pieces in the Hānshān corpus but is a useful witness to the maturation of his *Laṅkāvatāra* exegesis in the years immediately preceding his death.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670); main commentary [[KR6i0343]] (觀楞伽經記).
