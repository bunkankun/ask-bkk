# Fóshuō Shāhé bǐqiū gōngdé jīng 佛說沙曷比丘功德經
*Sūtra of the Merits of the Monk Shāhé*
translated by [[法炬]] Fǎjù (譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Shāhé bǐqiū gōngdé jīng* (T501) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by [[法炬]] Fǎjù. The protagonist *Shāhé bǐqiū* 沙曷比丘 (the monk Shāhé) is a relatively obscure disciple whose merit-deeds are recounted as exemplary. The Sanskrit name behind 沙曷 is uncertain.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. The colophon attributes the translation to Fǎjù.

### Abstract

The text is a brief avadāna-style narrative on a monastic disciple's exemplary merit (*guṇa* 功德). Translation falls within Fǎjù's productive period (290–306 CE).
