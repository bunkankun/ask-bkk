# Lúzhì zhǎngzhě yīnyuán jīng 盧至長者因緣經
*Sūtra of the Causes and Conditions of the Elder Lúzhì*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Lúzhì zhǎngzhě yīnyuán jīng* (T539) is a one-fascicle anonymous *yīnyuán* (avadāna / *jātaka*-style) sūtra. *Lúzhì* 盧至 transliterates an Indic personal name (possibly *Roca* or *Lūca*).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short narrative on the karmic causes leading to the elder Lúzhì's encounter with the Buddha. Standard pre-Sui anonymous *shīyì* attribution.
