# Shēnguān jīng 身觀經
*Sūtra on Body-Contemplation*
translated by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Shēnguān jīng* (T612) is a one-fascicle short sūtra on body-contemplation (*kāyānupaśyanā*) translated by Dharmarakṣa 竺法護 ([[竺法護]]). The text complements his [[KR6i0248]] *Fǎguān jīng* on the *dharma-anupaśyanā*.

### Abstract

A miniature contemplation-sūtra on the body, treating the standard impurity-list (*aśubha*) and the four-elements analysis (*dhātu-vibhaṅga*). Datable to Dharmarakṣa's translation activity (266–308 CE).
