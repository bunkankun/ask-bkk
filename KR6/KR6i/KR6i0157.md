# Fóshuō Shì tóngzǐ jīng 佛說逝童子經
*Sūtra of the Boy Shì*
translated by [[支法度]] (Zhī Fǎdù, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Shì tóngzǐ jīng* (T527) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by the Western Jìn translator Zhī Fǎdù 支法度 ([[支法度]]). The protagonist *Shì tóngzǐ* 逝童子 ("the Boy Shì") is parallel to [[KR6i0158]] *Púsà Shì jīng* (T528).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative on a boy who attains bodhisattva-vyākaraṇa. Zhī Fǎdù was active around 290–306 CE in the late Western Jìn; this is one of his few surviving translations. Cf. [[KR6i0158]] for the parallel text by Bái Fǎzǔ.
