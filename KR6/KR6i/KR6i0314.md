# Jīnguāngmíng jīng xuányì shùnzhèng jì 金光明經玄義順正記
*Notes on the* Profound Meaning of the Sūtra of Golden Light *Conforming to the Correct [Reading]*
composed by [[從義]] (Cóngyì, 撰)

## About the work

X359 is [[從義]]'s three-fascicle Northern-Sòng subcommentary on Zhìyǐ's [[KR6i0304|*Xuányì* 玄義]] (T1783), parallel to but distinct from [[知禮]]'s [[KR6i0305|*Shíyí jì* 拾遺記]] (T1784). The title's *shùnzhèng* 順正 ("conforming to the correct") signals the work's polemical posture: it claims to be a *correction* of [[知禮]]'s reading at points where [[從義]] judged the *Sìmíng* master's account divergent from Zhìyǐ's intent.

### Abstract

The opening dialogue of the preface explicitly engages the existing exegetical landscape: "or someone asks: from of old, those who have written *jì* on this *xuányì* are many. And the masters of Qiántáng (i.e. the *shānwài* tradition) have all said that the single section of *guānxīn* is something later men brashly added — only Sìmíng Fǎzhì (= [[知禮]]) defended it as the master's own teaching, and to this day no one fails to transmit it. Why now do you alter and rewrite it? I fear no one will adopt your version, and it will be cast out by the road." [[從義]]'s reply is twofold: (i) he does not abandon the *guānxīn shì* but follows [[知禮]] in retaining it; (ii) he nonetheless judges that [[知禮]]'s sectional analysis ("分科釋義頗有疎失") and his analysis of the title (specifically the reading of 金光明 as a *single dharma-name* 單法為名) are flawed.

The work is therefore best understood as *intra-shān-jiā* polemic — [[從義]] working within [[知禮]]'s broad framework but correcting it at the level of philological detail. Date bracket follows [[從義]]'s mature productive period at the Tiāntái scholastic establishments (1070–1091; he died Yuányòu 6 = 1091, age 50). Together with [[KR6i0315|*Wénjù xīnjì* 文句新記]] (X360) on the *wénjù* side, X359 is one of the two principal post-[[知禮]] Sòng subcommentaries on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa*.

Related canonical texts: parent commentary [[KR6i0304]] (金光明經玄義 / T1783); paired commentary [[KR6i0315]] (金光明經文句新記 / X360); rival/predecessor [[KR6i0305]] (金光明經玄義拾遺記 / T1784).
