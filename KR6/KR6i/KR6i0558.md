# Yuánjué jīng lüèshū kē 圓覺經略疏科
*Outline (kē) for the Abridged Commentary on the Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment*
by [[宗密]] (Guīfēng Zōngmì, 製)

## About the work

A short single-fascicle outline-text by [[宗密]] Guīfēng Zōngmì (780–841), giving the topic-tree (*kē* 科) of his *Yuánjué jīng lüèshū* 圓覺經略疏 ([[KR6i0553]]). It is the working classroom counterpart of the more extensive *Dàshū chāo kē* ([[KR6i0556]]), bearing the same relation to the *Lüèshū* that the *Dàshū chāo kē* bears to the *Dàshū chāo*. Like all the Zōngmì *kē* texts it survives only through Korean and Japanese transmission and has reached the modern reader through the *Wànzì xùzàngjīng*.

### Abstract

The *Lüèshū kē* lays out the hierarchical outline of the *Lüèshū*: the threefold introductory matter (*xùfēn* 序分 / *zhèngzōngfēn* 正宗分 / *liútōngfēn* 流通分) and the twelve bodhisattva-dialogue chapters, each with its inner threefold-and-fourfold subdivision into question, response, gāthā, and concluding injunction. As with the *Dàshū chāo kē*, the *Lüèshū kē* is a navigational and didactic apparatus rather than a freestanding doctrinal exposition; its value to the modern reader is to make explicit the chapter-and-section architecture that Zōngmì had embedded in the marginal *kēmù* of his commentary. It is the pair to the *Lüèshū* in the same way that the *Dàshū chāo kē* is the pair to the *Dàshū chāo*.
