# Fǎguān jīng 法觀經
*Sūtra on the Contemplation of Dharmas*
translated by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fǎguān jīng* (T611) is a one-fascicle short meditation sūtra translated by Dharmarakṣa 竺法護 ([[竺法護]]). It presents a brief treatment of the *dharma-anupaśyanā* — the fourth of the four foundations of mindfulness (*satipaṭṭhāna* / *smṛtyupasthāna*).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A miniature contemplation-sūtra on the *dharma-anupaśyanā* foundation, datable to Dharmarakṣa's translation activity (266–308 CE). The text supplements his fuller meditation translations [[KR6i0243]] *Xiūxíng dàodì jīng* (T606) and [[KR6i0249]] *Shēnguān jīng* 身觀經 (T612).
