# Chányào jīng 禪要經
*Sūtra on the Essentials of Meditation*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Chányào jīng* (T609) is a one-fascicle anonymous meditation manual in sūtra form, transmitting a small set of meditation methods (most prominently the contemplation of impurity, *aśubha-bhāvanā*, and counting of breaths).

### Prefaces

No translator preface or attribution survives.

### Abstract

A brief anonymous *chán*-text in the early-medieval Chinese Sarvāstivāda meditation tradition. Catalog assignment is to the post-Han / pre-Tang anonymous corpus; the conservative bracket of 317–600 CE follows the standard *shīyì* horizon.
