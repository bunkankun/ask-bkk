# Tiānzhúguó Pútídámó chánshī lùn 天竺國菩提達摩禪師論
*Treatise of the Indian Chán Master Bodhidharma (recension a — P.2039)*
falsely ascribed to [[菩提達摩]]; critical edition by [[方廣錩]] (整理)

## About the work
The first of two Dūnhuáng recensions of the *Tiānzhúguó Pútídámó chánshī lùn* — see [[KR6v0002]] for the full discussion of the work, its content, and its place in early-Chán literature. [[KR6v0002]] published a *combined* edition collating both witnesses; [[KR6v0024]] publishes recension *a* alone, edited from P. 2039 (BnF) — the original Pelliot Dūnhuáng manuscript identified by Tanaka Ryōshō 田中良昭. This recension preserves the head- and tail-titles "*Tiānzhúguó Pútídámó chánshī lùn*" 天竺國菩提達摩禪師論 and "*Dámó chánshī lùn*" 達摩禪師論 respectively.

### Abstract
See [[KR6v0002]] for the textual-historical and bibliographic discussion. Fāng Guǎngchāng's decision to issue separate editions of the two witnesses (as [[KR6v0024]] and [[KR6v0025]], in addition to the combined critical edition of [[KR6v0002]]) reflects his programmatic methodology for handling apocryphal Chinese Buddhist texts that have evolved into substantively divergent recensions: rather than producing a single eclectic critical text, he aims to make each independent witness available as a textual document in its own right.
