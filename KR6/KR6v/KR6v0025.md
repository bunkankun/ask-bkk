# Tiānzhúguó Pútídámó chánshī lùn 天竺國菩提達摩禪師論
*Treatise of the Indian Chán Master Bodhidharma (recension b — Běi-xīn 1254)*
falsely ascribed to [[菩提達摩]]; critical edition by [[方廣錩]] (整理)

## About the work
The second of two Dūnhuáng recensions of the *Tiānzhúguó Pútídámó chánshī lùn* — see [[KR6v0002]] for the full literary description. This recension is edited from Beijing Library *Běixīn* 1254 alone (head damaged, tail intact; see also [[KR6v0003]] and [[KR6v0004]] which were copied on the same scroll). Recension *b* differs from recension *a* (P. 2039 / [[KR6v0024]]) in two principal ways: (1) the head is missing approximately 300 characters, but (2) the tail extends recension *a* by approximately 600 characters of summary plus a Mahāyāna / Hīnayāna *chán* dialogue not found in recension *a*, which Fāng treats as later interpolation.

### Abstract
See [[KR6v0002]] for the textual-historical and bibliographic discussion. Recension *b* is the principal evidence for treating [[KR6v0002]]–[[KR6v0004]] as fragments of a single *Chánzàng* 禪藏 scroll. Where recension *a* (P. 2039) probably represents an earlier or unexpanded text, recension *b* exhibits the typical pattern of late-Táng / Five-Dynasties Dūnhuáng manuscript-tradition: copying preserves the work but accretes summary and supplementary dialogue.
