# Jīngāngjīng zàn jí (nǐ) 金剛經讚集（擬）
*Collected Vajracchedikā Encomia (Recension I, Witness 3 — provisionally titled)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[達照]] (整理)

## About the work
The third early-type witness of the *Jīngāngjīng zàn* cycle — see [[KR6v0093]] for full discussion.

### Abstract
[[KR6v0095]] prints from Pelliot 2277 — head and tail damaged, no original title. Like *Fú* 323 ([[KR6v0094]]), P.2277 integrates the *zàn*-cycle with the *Vajracchedikā* sūtra-passages but does not include the *shíbā zhùchù* enumeration. The three early-type witnesses (P.2039-verso, *Fú* 323, P.2277) represent three genuinely independent recensions of the same Type-I *zàn*-cycle, suggesting widespread early-to-mid Táng circulation of this text-form.
