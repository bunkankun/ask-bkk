# Guānfó sānmèihǎi jīng běnxíng pǐn dìbā 觀佛三昧海經本行品第八
*The Eighth Original-Practice Chapter of the Sūtra on the Ocean of Buddha-Visualisation Samādhi (recension e, system 2)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[黃霞]] (整理)

## About the work
The third (and final) system-2 recension of the *Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng* — see [[KR6v0038]] for the full description. This recension has both the *shùnguān nìguān* 順觀逆觀 instructions and the salvific-promise block, but *omits* the "*sān niàn fó*" 三念佛 rubric after each mark — suggesting it represents a textually purer (less liturgically marked-up) state of the system-2 lineage.

### Abstract
See [[KR6v0038]] for the full discussion of the five-recension structure and witness-base. The *recension e* survives in a single witness; the precise manuscript number is given in Huáng Xiá's edition.

The *Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng* cluster ([[KR6v0038]]–[[KR6v0042]]) is one of the principal lab-cases for studying the textual proliferation of Chinese popular Buddhism: from a single Indian source (T15n0643's *Guān xiàng pǐn* + *Běnxíng pǐn*), a Chinese liturgical compiler produced a recitation manual; that manual then evolved through scribal accretion into five recensions in two systems, all preserved at Dūnhuáng. Comparable cases include the *Tiāngōng jīng* triad ([[KR6v0015]]–[[KR6v0017]]) and the *Fómǔ jīng* tetrad ([[KR6v0018]]–[[KR6v0021]]).
