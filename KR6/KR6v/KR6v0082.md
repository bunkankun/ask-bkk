# Dìzàng púsà shízhāirì 地藏菩薩十齋日
*The Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva Ten Fast-Days (Recension V)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[張總]] (整理)

## About the work
*Recension V* of the *Shízhāirì* cluster — see [[KR6v0077]] for full context.

### Abstract
*Recension V* belongs to a different *system* in Zhāng Zǒng's classification. It contains expanded *zàn* 讚 (praise-verses) attached to each fast-day's deity-invocation, integrating the *zhāirì* schedule with a more elaborate liturgical performance structure. This makes *Recension V* a transitional form between the bare *zhāirì* schedule of Recensions I–III and the fully-developed *biànwén* 變文 form found in the Bǎodǐngshān stone-engraving (cf. [[KR6v0051]]).
