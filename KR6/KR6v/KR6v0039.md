# Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng 佛說相好經
*The Buddha's Spoken Sūtra on the Major and Minor Marks (recension b, system 1)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[黃霞]] (整理)

## About the work
The *b* recension of the *Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng* / system 1 — see [[KR6v0038]] for the full literary description and the five-recension textual situation. This recension differs from recension *a* ([[KR6v0038]]) in lacking the *shùnguān nìguān* 順觀逆觀 (ascending/descending visualisation) instructions; otherwise the text is in the same system-1 group (no closing salvific-promise block).

### Abstract
Edited from P. 3593 *verso* alone (head damaged, tail intact). See [[KR6v0038]] for the full discussion.
