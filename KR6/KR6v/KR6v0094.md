# Jīngāngjīng zàn jí (nǐ) 金剛經讚集（擬）
*Collected Vajracchedikā Encomia (Recension I, Witness 2 — provisionally titled)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[達照]] (整理)

## About the work
The second early-type witness of the *Jīngāngjīng zàn* cycle — see [[KR6v0093]] for full discussion. The *(nǐ)* 擬 marker in the title indicates that this manuscript witness lacks an extant head- or tail-cartouche, and the editorial title is provisional.

### Abstract
[[KR6v0094]] prints from Russian Dūnhuáng *Fú* 弗 323 (Institute of Oriental Manuscripts, St. Petersburg) — head and tail damaged, no original title. The witness is distinctive in that it integrates the 50-verse *zàn*-cycle with the *Vajracchedikā* (Kumārajīva) text passage by passage, marking each *zàn*'s scriptural basis. It also adds a list of the *shí-bā zhù-chù* 十八住處 ("eighteen abiding-stations") — the third *yì-jù* 義句 of *Asaṅga*'s seven *yì-jù* doctrinal scheme, encompassing the eighteen stages from initial bodhicitta-arising to perfect Buddhahood. *Fú* 323 is therefore the most fully realised *Type-I* witness. The composition window inherits from the cluster: early-to-mid Táng.
