# Yīnyuán xīn lùn shì 因緣心論釋
*Pratītyasamutpāda-hṛdaya-vṛtti*
by [[龍樹]] (Nāgārjuna, 造); critical edition by [[華方田]] (整理)

## About the work
Nāgārjuna's prose auto-commentary (*svopajña-vṛtti*) on the verse-treatise *Yīnyuán xīn lùn sòng* ([[KR6v0033]]). The commentary expounds the *yuán-qǐ* 緣起 (dependent origination) doctrine in Madhyamaka *kōng-zōng* 空宗 framework, addressing the implications for *yīn-guǒ-lùn* 因果論 (theory of causation).

### Abstract
Like the verses, the *Vṛtti* was lost from the Chinese tradition and recovered from Dūnhuáng. The Taishō text (T32 no. 1654) was set from S. 1358 + S. 2462 with many errors. Huá Fāngtián's 1996 edition draws on eight Dūnhuáng witnesses (Beijing Library *Běi* 7257 [海 17] = base text; S. 4235, *Běi* 7255 [官 68], P. 4645, S. 1358, S. 2462, S. 1513 *verso*, *Bĕi-dà* 北大 D096) and is fully cross-collated with the verses ([[KR6v0033]]) and the secondary commentary ([[KR6v0035]]). See [[KR6v0033]] for the full discussion.
