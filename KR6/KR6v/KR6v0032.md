# Jìngmíng jīng jíjiě Guānzhōng shū juǎn xià 淨名經集解關中疏卷下
*The Guānzhōng Compendium-Commentary on the Vimalakīrti Sūtra, Lower Fascicle*
by [[道液]] (述); critical edition by [[黎明]] (整理)

## About the work
The lower fascicle of Dàoyè's 道液 *Jìngmíng jīng jíjiě Guānzhōng shū* — see [[KR6v0026]] for the full literary description and bibliographic discussion. The lower fascicle was composed at the *Pútí dàochǎng* 菩提道場 in Cháng'ān during *Yǒngtài chū sì* 永泰初祀 (765 CE), five years after the upper fascicle.

### Abstract
See [[KR6v0026]]. The lower fascicle is the textually larger half of the work and contains Dàoyè's commentary on chapters 7 ("*Guānzhòngshēng pǐn*" 觀眾生品) through 14 ("*Zhǔlèi pǐn*" 囑累品) of the *Vimalakīrti*. Lí Míng's edition is based on five Dūnhuáng witnesses: Beijing Library *Běixīn* 0293 (base text — complete both ends), S. 6870 (head intact, tail damaged), S. 6418 (complete), Beijing Library *Běixīn* 1274 + *Běixīn* 1143 (rejoined fragments of one scroll, head damaged tail intact), and Beijing Library *Běixīn* 0292 (head damaged, tail intact). Significantly, Lí's edition recovers approximately 10,000 characters that the Taishō text (T85n2777) had inadvertently omitted from the second half of chapter 5 through the first half of chapter 7 — making this the first complete critical edition of Dàoyè's work.

## Other points of interest
The 10,000-character lacuna in the Taishō text — silently passed over until Lí Míng's collation work — is one of the most consequential text-critical recoveries in modern Chinese Buddhist studies, restoring nearly half of Dàoyè's commentary on chapters 6 ("*Bùsīyì pǐn*" 不思議品) and the opening of "*Guānzhòngshēng pǐn*."
