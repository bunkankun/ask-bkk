# Dà bān nièpán jīng Fómǔ pǐn 大般涅槃經佛母品
*Chapter on the Buddha's Mother in the Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra (recension c)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[李際寧]] (整理)

## About the work
The *third* of four Dūnhuáng recensions of the *Fómǔ jīng* — see [[KR6v0018]] for full description. This recension is edited from P. 3919 (BnF) as base, collated against further witnesses from Beijing Library and the British Library. It carries the same head-title as recension *b*, but exhibits a textually distinct line-by-line tradition.

### Abstract
See [[KR6v0018]] for the textual-critical and bibliographic discussion. Recension *c* is text-critically the richest of the four because it has the largest number of available witnesses for collation; it represents a textually intermediate stage between recension *a* (the most elaborated) and recension *d* (the most compressed).
