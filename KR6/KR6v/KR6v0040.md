# Fó shuō Guānfó sānmèi hǎizàng jīng běnxíng pǐn dìbā 佛說觀佛三昧海藏經本行品第八
*The Eighth Original-Practice Chapter of the Buddha's Spoken Sūtra on the Ocean-Storehouse Samādhi of Buddha-Visualisation (recension c, system 2)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[黃霞]] (整理)

## About the work
The first system-2 recension of the *Fó shuō xiānghǎo jīng* — see [[KR6v0038]] for the full description. This recension belongs to the second textual system (which adds the closing *bāshí yì jié shēngsǐ zhī zuì* salvific-promise block and the *Fó gào Ānán* dialogue), but does *not* include the *shùnguān nìguān* 順觀逆觀 (ascending/descending visualisation) instructions present in the other system-2 recensions.
