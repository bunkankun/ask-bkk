# Dìzàng púsà shízhāirì 地藏菩薩十齋日
*The Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva Ten Fast-Days (Recension I, Witness 2)*
anonymous Chinese composition; critical edition by [[張總]] (整理)

## About the work
The second witness of *Recension I* of the *Dìzàngbóusà shízhāirì* — see [[KR6v0077]] for full discussion of the work and its eighteen-witness eight-recension transmission. [[KR6v0078]] prints a parallel Dūnhuáng manuscript witness for *Recension I*.

### Abstract
This second *Recension I* witness shows minor textual variation (mostly orthographic) from the base witness in [[KR6v0077]]; both are in the same lineage of the standardised *Shízhāirì* form that became dominant in mid-Táng Dūnhuáng. The *system 1 / recension 1* form titles itself "*Měiyuè yǒu shízhāirì*" 每月有十齋日 in head-cartouches.
