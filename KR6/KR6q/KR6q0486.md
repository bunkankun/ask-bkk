# Huìjué Yī chánshī yǔlù 慧覺衣禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huì-jué Yī*
by [[照衣]] (說), [[徹御]] (等編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Huìjué Zhàoyī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (**1605-04-08 – 1673-10-27**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Chèyù** [[徹御]] *děng biān*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J35 B340. Juan 3 includes the *xínglù* 行錄 self-biographical record.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[照衣]] note: native of **Qíjiāngxiàn** 綦江縣 (Yúzhōu, Sìchuān), lay surname **Xióng** 熊. Late-life ordination at age 30 under **Chángbái** at Huáyínshān; eight years' refuge at **Yèláng** during the 1644 dynastic transition. **1653/9**: transmission from Pòshān at Liángshān Jīnchéngsì + Shuāngguìsì.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1675** as a conservative bound for post-1673-decease compilation.

**Eight abbacies.** All Sì-chuān-region: Chóngqìng Chóngyīnsì → Húshān Zhìpíngsì → Tàigōngshān Shíbǎosì → Jiāngjīn Yuántōngsì → Zhūlín Hóngfúsì → Bāxiàn Bǎoliányuàn — a Sì-chuān-internal frontier-restoration ministry under the Pòshān-line aegis.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Huìjué Zhàoyī → six named heirs including **Pòfēng Zhòng** [[普重]] (cf. [[KR6q0492]]) and **Fóyǔ Chèyù** [[徹御]] (cf. [[KR6q0487]]).

## Other points of interest

- **Late-life monastic conversion at age 30.** Like several of his Sìchuān contemporaries (cf. [[KR6q0464]]'s Chuán, [[KR6q0465]]'s Xíngshěng), Zhàoyī's late-life ordination was triggered by the 1640s warfare-disruption rather than youthful religious aspiration — making the Pòshān-line Sìchuān population partly composed of mature converts from the late-Míng warfare-collapse generation.
