# Chángmù Diàn chánshī yǔlù 長目電禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cháng-mù Diàn*
by [[光電]] (說), [[悟本]] (編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Chángmù Guāngdiàn** [[光電]] (**1636-11-29 – 1685-08-27**, *shìshòu* 50), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Héngxiù Fālín** [[發林]] (cf. [[KR6q0494]]) in the Pòshān → Yúnhuàn → Héngxiù → Chángmù line. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Wùběn** [[悟本]]. *fǎfù* 1670; three Pòshān-line abbacies. *notBefore* = **1670** (transmission), *notAfter* = **1685** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B391.
