# Pánshān Liǎozōng chánshī yǔlù 盤山了宗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Liǎo-zōng of Pán-shān*
by [[超見]] (說), [[徹凡]] (等錄), [[心月]] (等錄)

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** [[超見]] 了宗超見 (**1614-10-17 – 1688**, age 75), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Dàbó Xíngqián** [[乾]] (1602–1673, cf. [[KR6q0584]]). Native of **Shùntiānfǔ Bǎodǐxiàn** 順天府寶坻縣 (Héběi), lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Chèfán** [[徹凡]] and **Xīnyuè** [[心月]] *děng lù*.

Documents Chāojiàn's eight major Héběi abbacies — most prominently the historic **Pánshān Gǔzhōngpán Zhèngfǎsì** 盤山古中盤正法寺 from which his "Pánshān" sobriquet derives. Other seats include **Nántāi** 南臺, **Lóngyún** 隆阭, **Wànshànsì** 萬善寺, **Fàngshēngyuàn** 放生院, **Pǔdùsì** 普渡寺, **Guānyīnsì** 觀音寺, **Rénshèngsì** 仁聖寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1675** (post-Dà-bó-death abbacy maturity); *notAfter* = **1688** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B474.

**Contents.** Juan 1–3: abbacy *yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*; *jīyuán*; *fǎyǔ*; *fóshì*. Juan 4: closing *xíngshí* preserving birth and death dates.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0584]] ([[乾]]), [[KR6q0586]] (his *fǎsì* [[超見]]), [[KR6q0573]] ([[昊]]), and [[KR6q0585]] ([[順]]) form a complete three-generation Línjì-Yángqí northern Pánshān lineage record: **Dàbó Xíngqián** (1602–1673) → **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** (1614–1688) → **Huīzhōu Hào** & **Lǎngkōng Shùn**. Together they document the consolidation of the **Mìyún → Wànrú → Pánshān** Línjì-Yángqí northern-Hé-běi establishment over three generations, with **Pánshān Zhèngfǎsì** as the principal anchor and **Wànshànsì** + **Tānzhèsì** as the secondary rotating-abbacy posts.
