# KR6q — *Chánzōng zhùshù bù* 禪宗著述部

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR6q gathers the writings of the Chán school 禪宗 as preserved across three textual reservoirs: the *Shǐchuán bù* 史傳部 and *Zhū-zōng bù* 諸宗部 of the Taishō canon (T47, T48, T51), the Xuzangjing 卍續藏 Chán *yǔlù* run (X64–X88), and the Jiāxīng Canon 嘉興藏 *yǔlù* expansion (J20–J40 and the Wàihán *bùyí* 補遺). The division therefore spans the full literary output of an indigenous Chinese Buddhist tradition: transmission-of-the-lamp histories (*dēnglù* 燈錄) and their *xù-* / *guǎng-* / *liánxù-* / *zēngjí-* derivatives; the recorded sayings (*yǔlù* 語錄) of individual masters; the kōan / "old-cases" literature (*gōng'àn* 公案, *sòng gǔ* 頌古, *niāngǔ* 拈古, *píngchàng* 評唱) and its commentarial superstructure; Buddhist biographical jewelry (*sēngbǎo zhuàn* 僧寶傳); Chán monastic codes (*qīngguī* 清規); the admonitory-pastoral genre (*jǐng cè* 警策, *bǎoxùn* 寶訓); doctrinal compendia and sectarian polemics on the *wǔ jiā qī zōng* 五家七宗 lineages; verse cycles (notably the *shíniú tú sòng* 十牛圖頌 and the *Yǒngjiā Zhèngdào gē* 永嘉證道歌); and the literary collected works (*biéjí* 別集) of Chán masters. The 609 entries make KR6q the single largest Chán-literature corpus assembled anywhere in modern catalogs and the densest documentation of any East-Asian Buddhist school.

Chronologically the corpus stretches from the conventionally-dated 6th-century *Sì xíng guān* 四行觀 attributed to Bodhidharma (KR6q0112) through to the early-19th-century *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* 正源略集 of Jìchéng 際澄 (KR6q0031–0033, 1820–1828). Quantitatively, however, the division is overwhelmingly weighted toward the 17th century: roughly 335 of the 609 entries — about 55% — are 17th-century Jiāxīng-Canon *yǔlù* of late-Míng and early-Qīng Chán abbots, reflecting both the print boom of the Wàn-Lì 萬曆 reform and the Línjì-school 臨濟宗 revival under Mìyún Yuánwù 密雲圓悟 and his dharma-heirs. For Taishō structural context the relevant CANWWW divisions are `div21.xml` (史傳部, hosting the foundational *dēnglù* set) and the Chán portion of `div23.xml` / `div24.xml` (諸宗部 Chán entries). Modern critical reference for the *dēnglù* tradition runs through Yanagida Seizan 柳田聖山, T. Griffith Foulk, Albert Welter, and Morten Schlütter; for the late-Míng / Qīng *yǔlù* explosion, through Jiang Wu 吳疆, Beata Grant, and Hasebe Yūkei 長谷部幽蹊.

## Important texts and text clusters

**The foundational *dēnglù* sequence ("Five Lamps" 五燈 and after).** This is the structural backbone of the division and the principal narrative source for Chinese Chán self-history.

- *Zǔtáng jí* 祖堂集 [[KR6q0002]] — the 952 Quánzhōu 泉州 compilation, the earliest extant true lamp record; lost in China and recovered via Korean transmission.
- *Jǐngdé chuándēng lù* 景德傳燈錄 [[KR6q0003]] — Dàoyuán's 1004–1011 work, the imperially-presented Sòng prototype that gave the genre its canonical shape.
- *Tiānshèng guǎngdēng lù* 天聖廣燈錄 [[KR6q0004]] — Lǐ Zūnxù's 1029–1036 expansion, the first lay-compiled lamp record.
- *Jiànzhōng jìngguó xùdēng lù* 建中靖國續燈錄 [[KR6q0007]] — Wéibái's 1101 continuation.
- *Liándēng huìyào* 聯燈會要 [[KR6q0008]] — Wùmíng's 1183 harmonization of the previous three.
- *Jiātài pǔdēng lù* 嘉泰普燈錄 [[KR6q0010]] — Zhèngshòu's 1204 broadening to laity and women.
- *Wǔdēng huìyuán* 五燈會元 [[KR6q0012]] — Pǔjì's 1252 *editio definitiva* synthesizing the five preceding lamp records into a single 20-juan corpus; the most influential single text of the genre.
- *Xù chuándēng lù* 續傳燈錄 [[KR6q0016]] — the early-Míng (1401–1402) Yǒnglè-era imperial continuation.
- *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 [[KR6q0019]] / *Jiěhuò biān* [[KR6q0020]] — Fèiyǐn Tōngróng's 1653–1654 partisan Línjì rewrite and its self-defence, the most notorious sectarian intervention in the *dēnglù* tradition.
- *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 [[KR6q0022]] — Jièlín Chāoyǒng's 120-juan Kāngxī-era (1697) compendium.
- *Zhǐyuè lù* 指月錄 [[KR6q0023]] — Qú Rǔjī's lay-compiled 1602 anthology.

**Kōan / old-cases tradition.** The *gōng'àn* literary tradition reframes encounter-dialogue narratives from the *dēnglù* as objects of literary commentary.

- *Bìyán lù* 碧巖錄 [[KR6q0078]] — Yuánwù Kèqín's *píngchàng* commentary (c. 1128) on Xuědòu Chóngxiǎn's 雪竇重顯 100 *sòng-gǔ* verses; the *locus classicus* of *wénzì chán* 文字禪.
- *Cóngróng ān lù* 從容庵錄 [[KR6q0079]] — Wànsōng Xíngxiù's 1223 commentary on Hóngzhì Zhèngjué's 宏智正覺 100 verses; Cáodòng counterpart to the *Bìyán lù*.
- *Wúmén guān* 無門關 [[KR6q0080]] — Wúmén Huìkāi's 1228 collection of 48 cases, the most internationally diffused kōan compendium.
- *Fóguǒ jījié lù* 佛果擊節錄 [[KR6q0249]], the Línquán 林泉 commentaries [[KR6q0251]] [[KR6q0252]], and the Tiānqí 天奇 directing commentaries [[KR6q0250]] [[KR6q0253]] continue the genre through the Yuán and Míng.

**Early Chán and the *Liù zǔ* 六祖 corpus.** A tight cluster of works either attributed to or recovered from the early Tang Chán milieu, much of it Dūnhuáng-attested.

- The two recensions of the *Liùzǔ tánjīng* 六祖壇經 (Dūnhuáng [[KR6q0082]]; the standard Yuán recension [[KR6q0083]]).
- *Shǎoshì liù mén* 少室六門 [[KR6q0084]] and the four short Bodhidharma treatises (*Xuèmài lùn*, *Wùxìng lùn*, *Pòxiàng lùn*, *Sì xíng guān*) at [[KR6q0112]]–[[KR6q0115]].
- *Xìnxīn míng* 信心銘 attributed to Sēngcàn 僧璨 [[KR6q0085]].
- *Zuìshàng shèng lùn* 最上乘論 attributed to Hóngrěn 弘忍 [[KR6q0086]].
- *Léngqié shīzī jì* 楞伽師資記 [[KR6q0109]] and *Chuán fǎ bǎo jì* 傳法寶記 [[KR6q0110]] — the principal Northern-school lineage records, recovered from Dūnhuáng.
- The Huángbò Xīyùn 黃檗希運 corpus recorded by Péi Xiū 裴休: *Chuánxīn fǎyào* [[KR6q0087]] and *Wǎnlíng lù* [[KR6q0088]].
- *Dùnwù rùdào yàomén lùn* 頓悟入道要門論 [[KR6q0117]] by Dàzhū Huìhǎi 大珠慧海.
- *Yǒngjiā jí* [[KR6q0089]] and *Yǒngjiā Zhèngdào gē* [[KR6q0090]].
- *Chányuán zhū quán jí dūxù* 禪源諸詮集都序 [[KR6q0091]] — Guīfēng Zōngmì's 圭峰宗密 doctrinal classification of Chán.

**Sòng-Chán masters' *yǔlù* — the "core canon".** The classical *yǔlù* of the five-house founders and the major Sòng Línjì / Cáodòng lines.

- *Línjì lù* 臨濟錄 [[KR6q0053]]; *Yúnmén guǎnglù* 雲門廣錄 [[KR6q0073]]; *Cáoshān yǔlù* (in [[KR6q0269]]); *Fǎyǎn yǔlù* (in [[KR6q0270]]).
- The Línjì-Yángqí 楊岐 line: *Fényáng* [[KR6q0054]], *Huánglóng* [[KR6q0055]], *Yángqí* [[KR6q0056]] [[KR6q0057]], *Fǎyǎn* (Wǔzǔ Fǎyǎn 五祖法演) [[KR6q0058]], *Yuánwù Kèqín* 圓悟克勤 [[KR6q0059]], *Dàhuì Zōnggǎo* 大慧宗杲 [[KR6q0060]]–[[KR6q0063]], *Mì'ān Xiánjié* 密菴 [[KR6q0064]], *Xūtáng Zhìyú* 虛堂智愚 [[KR6q0065]].
- The Cáodòng line: *Hóngzhì guǎnglù* 宏智廣錄 [[KR6q0070]], *Tiāntóng Rújìng yǔlù* 天童如淨 [[KR6q0072]], *Dòngshān* / *Cáoshān* in [[KR6q0066]]–[[KR6q0069]].
- *Gǔ zūnsù yǔlù* 古尊宿語錄 [[KR6q0261]] / [[KR6q0263]] and its continuations *Xù gǔ zūnsù yǔyào* [[KR6q0265]] — the Sòng anthologies of pre-five-house master sayings.
- *Sì jiā yǔlù* 四家語錄 [[KR6q0266]]–[[KR6q0269]] — the canonical Mǎ-zǔ 馬祖, Bǎizhàng 百丈, Huángbò, Línjì set.

**Doctrinal compendia and sectarian polemics.** The *zōngmén* 宗門 literature in which the Chán school theorized its own lineage and doctrinal claims.

- *Zōngjìng lù* 宗鏡錄 [[KR6q0092]] — Yǒngmíng Yánshòu's 永明延壽 100-juan synthesis of Chán with Huáyán-Tiāntái doctrine (c. 961); the most ambitious doctrinal compendium in the division. Companion works at [[KR6q0093]] *Wànshàn tóngguī jí* and [[KR6q0094]] *Wéixīn jué*.
- *Rén tiān yǎnmù* 人天眼目 [[KR6q0081]] — Zhìzhāo's 智昭 1188 systematic exposition of the *wǔ jiā* methods.
- *Wǔ jiā zhèngzōng zàn* 五家正宗贊 [[KR6q0005]] — Shàotán's 紹曇 1254 verse-encomium of the five houses.
- *Wǔ zōng yuán* 五宗原 [[KR6q0167]], *Pìwàng jiùlüè shuō* 闢妄救略說 [[KR6q0168]], *Wǔ jiā zōngzhǐ zuǎnyào* 五家宗旨纂要 [[KR6q0169]] — the mid-17th-century Línjì-school polemical trilogy around the Mìyún-Yuánwù dharma-heirs' rewriting of lineage history.
- *Chánjiā guījiàn* 禪家龜鑑 [[KR6q0146]] — Korean Sŏn introduction by Sŏsan Hyujŏng 西山休靜.

**Chán biographical compendia.**

- *Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* 禪林僧寶傳 [[KR6q0040]] — Juéfàn Huìhóng's 覺範慧洪 1119–1124 collection, the genre's prototype.
- *Sēngbǎo zhèngxù zhuàn* 僧寶正續傳 [[KR6q0041]].
- *Nán-Sòng Yuán-Míng chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* [[KR6q0042]] — Zìróng 自融 and Xìnglěi 性磊, 1654.
- *Huáng-Míng míngsēng jí lüè* 皇明名僧輯略 [[KR6q0043]] — Zhūhóng 袾宏 (Yúnqī).
- *Bāshíbā zǔ dàoyǐng zhuànzàn* 八十八祖道影傳贊 [[KR6q0045]] — Hānshān Déqīng 憨山德清.

**Monastic codes and pastoral-regulatory literature.**

- *Chìxiū Bǎizhàng qīngguī* 敕修百丈清規 [[KR6q0102]] — the 1335 Yuán imperial recension of the Chán monastic code attributed to Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi 百丈懷海.
- *Chányuàn qīngguī* 禪苑清規 [[KR6q0136]] — the 1103 Sòng recension by Cháng-lú Zōngzé 長蘆宗賾, the earliest surviving Chán code.
- *Chánlín bǎoxùn* 禪林寶訓 [[KR6q0099]] with its four Míng-Qīng commentaries [[KR6q0153]]–[[KR6q0157]].
- *Zī mén jǐngxùn* 緇門警訓 [[KR6q0100]] — Míng-period encyclopedic admonition anthology.
- *Chánguān cèjìn* 禪關策進 [[KR6q0101]] — Zhūhóng's 1600 practice-encouragement anthology.

**Comprehensive *guǎnglù* and Jiāxīng-Canon *biéjí*.** The late-Míng / Qīng print expansion. The principal monumental sets include *Yǒngjué Yuánxián chánshī guǎnglù* [[KR6q0367]], *Bù shuǐ tái jí* 布水臺集 [[KR6q0200]] of Mùchén Dàomín 木陳道忞 (with a preface by Qián Qiānyì 錢謙益), *Wúyì Yuánlái guǎnglù* [[KR6q0365]], the Hānshān 憨山 corpus *Mèngyóu jí* [[KR6q0386]] / *Quánjí* [[KR6q0389]], and the Zǐbǎi 紫柏 *Zūnzhě quánjí* [[KR6q0382]].

## Topics

- **The *dēnglù* and the construction of patriarchal lineage.** The *dēnglù* genre invented and then iteratively re-edited the canonical lineage chart of Chinese Chán: the *Xī tiān èr-shí-bā zǔ* 西天二十八祖 + *Dōng-tǔ liù zǔ* 東土六祖 + *wǔ jiā qī zōng* 五家七宗 schema. Every text in [[KR6q0001]]–[[KR6q0039]] is a re-articulation of this charter narrative, with sectarian rewrites (e.g. [[KR6q0019]] *Wǔdēng yántǒng*) using the genre to *redistribute* dharma-heir assignments between Cáodòng and Línjì.
- **Encounter-dialogue (*jīyuán wèndá* 機緣問答) as literary form.** The *yǔlù* and *dēnglù* together generate the encounter-dialogue paradigm — short, edited dialogues between master and disciple structured around live language, demotic speech, paradox, and physical action — which becomes the master genre of Chinese Buddhist literature and the proximate source-material for the kōan tradition.
- **The *gōng'àn* and *wénzì chán* / *kàn huà* / *mòzhào* debate.** The Sòng kōan tradition splits along three axes: literary commentary on edited cases (*Bìyán lù*, *Cóngróng lù*), *kàn huàtóu* introspection on a single critical phrase (Dàhuì Zōnggǎo, [[KR6q0060]]–[[KR6q0063]]), and *mòzhào chán* silent-illumination practice (Hóngzhì Zhèngjué, [[KR6q0070]] and Tiāntóng Rújìng, [[KR6q0072]]). The Sòng controversy between Dàhuì and Hóngzhì is the principal doctrinal debate documented in the division.
- **The *wǔ jiā qī zōng* doctrinal classification.** The *wǔ jiā* — Guī-yǎng 溈仰, Línjì, Cáodòng, Yúnmén, Fǎyǎn — and the *qī zōng* extension with the two Línjì sub-houses (Huánglóng 黃龍 and Yángqí 楊岐) provide the categorical grid against which every *dēnglù* and *zōngmén* compendium ([[KR6q0081]], [[KR6q0005]], [[KR6q0167]]–[[KR6q0169]]) organizes its material.
- **Sòng-Chán syncretism with Huáyán-Tiāntái doctrine and the *jiào-chán yī-zhì* 教禪一致 program.** Anchored by Zōngmì's *Chányuán zhū quán jí dūxù* [[KR6q0091]] and developed at scale in Yǒngmíng Yánshòu's *Zōngjìng lù* [[KR6q0092]], this syncretic stream argues against Chán-school anti-textualism and re-grounds the *patriarchal vehicle* in canonical doctrine.
- **Pure Land convergence (*chán-jìng shuāng-xiū* 禪淨雙修).** From Yánshòu onward, and intensified in the late-Míng synthesis of Zhūhóng, Hānshān, and Ǒuyì, the division documents the long convergence between Chán and Pure Land practice — visible in the *Wànshàn tóngguī jí* [[KR6q0093]], the *Wéixīn jué* [[KR6q0094]], and across the Zhūhóng / Hānshān / Ǒuyì corpora.
- **Late-Míng / Qīng lineage disputes and the print boom.** The mid-17th-century controversy between the Línjì-Mìyún descendants and the Cáodòng line is the most concentrated polemical episode in Chinese Buddhist history: [[KR6q0018]]–[[KR6q0020]], [[KR6q0167]]–[[KR6q0169]], and the *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* tradition [[KR6q0031]]–[[KR6q0033]] all participate. The Jiāxīng-Canon 嘉興藏 print enterprise (1589–c. 1712) is the medium through which the resulting *yǔlù* explosion reaches the canon.
- **Chán monastic codes and the institutional history of the school.** [[KR6q0102]], [[KR6q0136]], and the *qīngguī* continuations [[KR6q0139]]–[[KR6q0142]] track the institutional discipline of the Chán monastery from Sòng through Qīng — the basis for any historical reconstruction of *cónglín* 叢林 life.
- **Lay Buddhism and women practitioners.** The lamp records open progressively to lay and female practitioners across the 13th–17th centuries: *Jūshì fēndēng lù* 居士分燈錄 [[KR6q0039]] (lay), *Yōupóyí zhì* [[KR6q0051]] (laywomen), [[KR6q0273]] *Páng jūshì yǔlù* (the iconic Páng Yùn 龐蘊 family), and *yǔlù* of Buddhist nuns scattered in the late-Míng / Qīng Jiāxīng-Canon range.
- **Transmission to Korea and Japan.** The division preserves: the Koryŏ-Sŏn corpus of Chinul [[KR6q0095]]–[[KR6q0097]] and the Chosŏn corpus of Sŏsan Hyujŏng [[KR6q0146]]; the *Zǔtáng jí* itself, transmitted through Korea; Japanese Edo-period re-editions of the Cáodòng founders [[KR6q0066]]–[[KR6q0069]]; and Dōgen's 道元 *Zuòchán zhēn* 坐禪箴 [[KR6q0241]] in its Sino-Japanese textual form.
- **Chán authors writing on the Three Teachings.** The division also collects works by Chán authors on non-Buddhist canonical texts — most prominently Ǒuyì's *Zhōuyì chánjiě* 周易禪解 [[KR6q0184]], *Qī yuán zhǐtōng* (Zhuāngzǐ) [[KR6q0219]], and *Dàodé jīng shùn zhū* [[KR6q0227]] — a witness to the late-Míng *sān jiào* 三教 conversation as conducted from inside the *cónglín*.
