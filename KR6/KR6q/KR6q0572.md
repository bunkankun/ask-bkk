# Xiāngyán Xǐxīn Shuǐ chánshī yǔlù 香巖洗心水禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xǐ-xīn Shuǐ of Xiāng-yán [-sì]*
by [[照水]] (說), [[實雲]] (等編), [[海清]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Xǐxīn Zhàoshuǐ** [[照水]] 洗心照水 (lifedates unknown — was 30 in **1683**, age 45 in **1698**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiànglín Běnzhēn** [[本真]] (1620–1691, cf. [[KR6q0571]]). Native of **Jiànkāng Jiāngníng** 建康江寧 (Nánjīng), lay surname **Liú** 劉. Compiled by his attendants **Shíyún** [[實雲]] (catalog form; the source itself reads 實雪 Shíxuě) and **Hǎiqīng** [[海清]] *děng biān*.

Three prefaces are preserved: by lay scholar **Zhào Yùnxī** 趙運熙 (拔貢) dated **康熙丁卯歲春王上元日** = **1687-02-26** (lunar 1/15, *Yuánxiāo*); a **康熙丙寅** = **1686** verse-text; and by a former *副總兵 zuǒdūdū* of **Qúxié** 衢協 region (Zhèjiāng) dated **康熙乙亥秋九月** = **1695-10**. Documents teaching at four lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Huáyánsì** 華嚴寺 (entered **康熙癸酉中秋** = **1693-09**); **Bǎoqìng** 寶慶; **Guānyīnsì** 觀音寺 (entered **康熙甲戌元宵** = **1694-02**); **Xiāngyánsì** 香巖寺 (the principal seat). *notBefore* = **1683** (癸亥 *fùfǎ*); *notAfter* = **1698** (last attested year). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B459.

**Contents.** Two juan: prefaces; *shàngtáng* records at the four abbacies; *xiǎocān*; *jīyuán*; *fǎyǔ*; *jì*; *fóshì*; *xíngshí* (the autobiographical narration in juan 2 — primary source for his life).

## Other points of interest

The pairing with [[KR6q0571]] (Běnzhēn) shows a complete teacher-disciple sequence within the Línjì-Yángqí 35-generation series: tonsure under **Zhèngjué Rùnguāng Zé** 潤光澤 (cf. [[KR6q0234]]) → *fùfǎ* under **Báiyún Xiànglín Běnzhēn** [[本真]] in 1683 — Zhàoshuǐ's editorial work spans both lineage-lines. The 1695 preface by a Zhèjiāng military commander indicates substantial provincial-elite patronage, perhaps building on Zhàoshuǐ's Jiāngníng (Nánjīng) lay-Buddhist family background.
