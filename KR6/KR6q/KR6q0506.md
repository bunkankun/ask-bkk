# Zhèān Fàn chánshī yǔlù 蔗菴範禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhè-ān Fàn*
by [[淨範]] (說), [[智璋]] (等錄)

Thirty-juan *yǔlù* of **Wéizé / Zhèān Jìngfàn** [[淨範]] 蔗菴淨範 (**1620 – 1692**, *shìshòu* 73), Cáo-dòng-school principal dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665). Recorded by *fǎsì* **Zhìzhāng** [[智璋]] *děng lù*. The 30-juan extent reflects the long c. 1649–1692 preaching career across five major Zhèjiāng Cáodòng abbacies. Juan 30 *zìxù*. Stands alongside [[KR6q0459]], [[KR6q0471]], [[KR6q0472]] as the **fourth and most extensive** Sānyí Míng-yú-line *yǔlù* in the Jiāxīng Canon — Jìngfàn was first-listed among Míngyú's thirty heirs and lead compiler of his master's [[KR6q0411]] *yǔlù*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B369.
