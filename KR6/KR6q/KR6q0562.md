# Língjī chánshī yǔlù 靈機禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Líng-jī*
by [[行觀]] (說), [[寂方]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Zīfú Língjī Xíngguān** [[行觀]] 資福靈機行觀 (**1616-03-10 – 1681-11-08**, age 66, *sēnglà* 49), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Native of **Lóngxī** 龍溪 (Fújiàn), lay surname **Zhōu** 周. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Zhìqióng Jìfāng** [[寂方]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at two abbacies: **Húzhōu Wǔkāng Xiángfèngshān Xiǎnzhōng Chóngxiào Zīfú chánsì** 湖州武康翔鳳山顯忠崇孝資福禪寺 (**1654** abbacy / **1644** initial residence — the principal seat) and **Jiāxīngfǔ Shíménxiàn Fúyán chánsì** 嘉興府石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙辛亥** = **1671**). *notBefore* = **1654** (formal abbacy onset); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (the canon-edition is a *zhòngkè* 重刻 reissue with corrected materials, likely a couple of decades after the master's 1681 death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B448.

**Contents.** Juan 1: three prefaces (one labeled *zhòngkè yuángǎo xù* 重刻原稿序, another *kèzhèng yuángǎo xù* 刻正原稿序); *mùlù*; *Zīfúsì yǔlù*; *Fúyánsì yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*; *shìzhòng*. Juan 2: *jīyuán*; *chuíwèn wǔ zé* 垂問五則 (five suspended questions). Juan 3: *kānbiàn*; *zǎocān* 早參 (morning interviews); *wǎncān* 晚參 (evening interviews). Juan 4: *yuánliúsòng* 源流頌 (verses on the lineage stream). Juan 5: *sònggǔ*; *shūwèn* (epistolary, including 復海鹽張禹侯居士書). Juan 6: closing materials including the master's *xíngzhuàng* 行狀 (the principal source for his life) and *tǎmíng*.

## Other points of interest

The double-preface format (*zhòngkè* + *kèzhèng*) suggests an iterative editorial history: an initial publication during the master's lifetime, then a posthumous corrected reissue. The 源流頌 in juan 4 — "verses on the lineage stream" — is an unusual genre marker, presenting a verse-form patriarchal genealogy in continuation of his teacher Fèiyǐn's program of explicit Línjì-lineage articulation (cf. *Fèiyǐn chánshī jìzǔ dào yǐng* 費隱禪師繼祖道影).
