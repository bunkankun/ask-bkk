# Tiāntái Tōngxuánsì Dúlǎng chánshī yǔlù 天台通玄寺獨朗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dú-lǎng of Tōng-xuán-sì on Tiān-tái-shān*
by [[行曰]] (說), [[超心]] (等編), [[超香]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Dúlǎng Xíngrì** [[行曰]] 獨朗行日 (lifedates not preserved; *shìshòu* 66, *sēnglà* 43), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Chāoxīn** [[超心]] + **Chāoxiāng** [[超香]] *děng biān*. Two abbacies: **Yáojiāng Jīqìng** + **Tiāntái Tōngxuánsì** (death-seat). Juan 2 *tǎmíng*. *notBefore* / *notAfter*: c. 1655–1685. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B368.
