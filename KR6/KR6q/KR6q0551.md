# Fǎchuáng Yuǎn chánshī yǔlù 法幢遠禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fǎ-chuáng Yuǎn*
by [[智遠]] (說), [[通慧]] (等編), [[弘源]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** [[智遠]] 法幢智遠 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Chúnbèi Guǎngdé** [[廣德]] (cf. [[KR6q0550]]), thus a *fǎsūn* of **Ěrdú Dàotài** in the Pòshān Hǎimíng line. Native of **Shǔdōng Wànxiàn** 蜀東萬縣 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Sòng** 宋. Compiled by his sole *fǎsì* **Ānshí Tōnghuì** [[通慧]] *děng biān*. Preface by **Hāntáng Hóngyuán** [[弘源]] dated **康熙三十四年四月佛誕日** = **1695-05-21** at **Yílíng**. *notBefore* ≈ **1672** (post-master's Sìguī Fóxīng 1672 founding); *notAfter* = **1695** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B434.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *mùcì*; *Huánglíngyuàn yǔlù* (the principal *shàngtáng* record at the same Yangtze-gorge seat his teacher had held); *xiǎocān*; *shìzhòng*; *sònggǔ*; ten **牧頌** *mùsòng* (oxherding poems: *wèimù*, *chūtiáo*, *shòuzhì*, *huíshǒu*, *xúnfú*, *wúài*, *rènyùn*, *xiāngwàng*, *dúzhào*, *shuāngmián*); *liánfāng* (lineage-distribution).

## Other points of interest

The pairing of [[KR6q0550]] (Chúnbèi Guǎngdé) and [[KR6q0551]] (Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn) gives a teacher-disciple sequence of *yǔlù* documenting the Húběi Yangtze-gorge institutional rebuilding of late-Kāngxī Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism on the Pòshān → Ěrdú line. The *mùsòng* sequence in Zhìyuǎn's *yǔlù* uses a slightly non-standard ten-stage scheme (omitting the more familiar *kuòniú* / *fǎnběn* terminology in favor of *xúnfú* / *xiāngwàng* / *dúzhào* / *shuāngmián*).
