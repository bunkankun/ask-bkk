# Línquán lǎorén píngchàng Dānxiá Chún chánshī sònggǔ Xūtáng jí 林泉老人評唱丹霞淳禪師頌古虗堂集

*Old-Man Línquán's Evaluation-Singing on Master Dānxiá Chún's Sònggǔ: Empty-Hall Collection* — a six-juan companion-volume to [[KR6q0251]] *Kōnggǔ jí*, in which the Yuán Cáodòng master [[從倫|Línquán Cónglún]] 林泉從倫 provides the *píngchàng* 評唱 commentary on the *sònggǔ* verses of the Northern-Sòng Cáodòng master [[子淳|Dānxiá Zǐchún]] 丹霞子淳 (1066–1119). The two pairs [[KR6q0251]] / [[KR6q0252]] together constitute Cónglún's systematic *píngchàng* on the two major Northern-Sòng Cáodòng *sònggǔ* corpora (Yìqīng and Zǐchún).

## About the work

A six-juan Cáodòng *sònggǔ* / *píngchàng* collaborative commentary, X67 n1304. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Parallel to [[KR6q0251]] in structure: Zǐchún's 100 *sònggǔ* verses (Northern Sòng) receive Cónglún's Yuán-era *píngchàng* running-commentary. Title: *Xūtáng jí* 虗堂集 ("Empty-Hall Collection"), distinct from [[KR6q0251]]'s *Kōnggǔ jí* ("Empty-Valley Collection") — Cónglún's twin-titled pair.

### Abstract

[[子淳|Dānxiá Zǐchún]] 丹霞子淳 (1066–1119/1120; died Xuānhé 1 / spring, per *Chándēng shìpǔ* 禪燈世譜 juan 9). Northern-Sòng Cáodòng master; dharma-heir of [[道楷|Fúróng Dàokǎi]] 芙蓉道楷 in the second major Cáodòng transmission-line. Major teacher of [[正覺|Hóngzhì Zhèngjué]] 宏智正覺 (1091–1157) whose own *sònggǔ* would become the basis of the *Cóngróng lù*.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100 (Zǐchún's original composition); *notAfter* c. 1290 (Cónglún's commentary completion).
