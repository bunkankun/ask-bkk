# Pǔān Yìnsù chánshī yǔlù 普菴印肅禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Pǔān Yìnsù* — a three-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Pǔān Yìnsù** 普菴印肅 (1115–1169), one of the most extraordinary figures in the Sòng Chán tradition — a Línjì-lineage master (via Mùān Shànguǒ 牧庵善果) famous for his miraculous-visionary experiences, his popular religious following, and his significant role in the popularisation of Chán-inflected Buddhist practice in rural China.

Three-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1356.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1150; *notAfter* c. 1200.
