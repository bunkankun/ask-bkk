# Zhīkōng Yùn chánshī yǔlù 知空蘊禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhī-kōng Yùn*
by [[學蘊]] (說), [[通來]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Zhīkōng Xuéyùn** [[學蘊]] 知空學蘊 (**1613-10-14 – 1689-07-14**, age 77, *sēnglà* 65), Línjì 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Kāifēng Mìxíng Jìrěn** 開峰密行寂忍 (Húguǎng Héngzhōu). A Yúnnán monastic — native of **Ěrhǎi** 洱海, ordained at age 14 at **Jīzúshān Jìguāngsì** 雞足寂光寺. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Tōnglái** [[通來]] *děng biān*. Held five abbacies across Yúnnán: **Kāifēng Nányún** 開峰南雲, **Chǔxióng Fúchéng** 楚雄福城, **Jiǔtái Fāngguǎng** 九臺方廣, **Guǎngtōng Xiānyáng** 廣通仙羊, and **Jìnglè** 淨樂. The opening *shàngtáng* is dated **辛亥年三月十五日** = **1671-04-23** at Fúchéngsì, where he received law-instruments forwarded by his teacher Mìxíng. *notBefore* = **1671** (Fúchéngsì opening *shàngtáng*), *notAfter* = **1689** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B400. Juan 1: *shàngtáng*, *xiǎocān*, *shìzhòng*, *cháhuà*, *xínglù*; juan 2: *fǎyǔ*, *jīyuán*, *zèngjì*, etc.
