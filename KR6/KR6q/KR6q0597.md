# Bóshān Sùrú Hàn chánshī yǔlù 博山粟如瀚禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Sù-rú Hàn of Bó-shān*
by [[弘瀚]] (說), [[傅鵬]] (編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Sùrú Hónghàn** [[弘瀚]] 粟如弘瀚 (**1630-03-16 – 1706-04-10**, age 77, *sēnglà* 54), **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Xuějiàn Dàofèng** 雪澗道奉 (1592–1670) at **Bóshān Néngrénsì** 博山能仁寺 — **Qīngyuán 33rd-generation** descendant; **6th-generation abbot of Bóshān** in the **Wúyì Yuánlái** 無異元來 (1576–1630) line. Native of **Yǒngfēng** 永豐 (Jiāngxī), lay surname **Yú** 余. Compiled by lay disciple **Fù Péng** [[傅鵬]] *biān*. *notBefore* = **1668** (*fùfǎ*); *notAfter* = **1706** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B490.

Documents teaching at three Jiāngxī abbacies: **Xìnzhōu Bóshān Néngrénsì** 信州博山能仁寺 (the Cáodòng mother-temple, held twice — *shàngtáng yī* and *zàizhù shàngtáng sān*); **Xìnzhōu Gāoquán** 信州高泉; **Xìnzhōu Yíngshān** 信州瀛山.

**Contents.** Juan 1: portrait + *zìzàn*; preface and post-preface; *zǒngmù*; *Bóshān shàngtáng yī*. Juan 2: *Bóshān shàngtáng èr*; *Gāoquán yǔlù*; *Yíngshān yǔlù*. Juan 3: *zàizhù Bóshān shàngtáng sān*. Juan 4: *xiǎocān yī*; *zàizhù Bóshān xiǎocān èr*. Juan 5: *shìzhòng yī*; *zàizhù shìzhòng èr*; *shǒuzuò liáo bǐngfú*; *rùshì jīyuán*. Juan 6: *niānsòng* (with the rare *èr shāngrén yú zhū* 二商人舁豬 *gōngàn*); closing *xíngzhuàng* + 〈青原三十三世博山第六代住持粟如瀚大師塔銘〉.

## Other points of interest

This is the principal **Bóshān Cáodòng** *yǔlù* of the late 17th century, continuing the lineage of **Wúyì Yuánlái** 無異元來 → **Xuějiàn Dàofèng** → Hónghàn — the same lineage out of which **Yǒngjué Yuánxián** 永覺元賢 split into the Gǔshān (Fújiàn) sub-line. The 6-generation succession at Bóshān is one of the most stable continuous Cáodòng abbacy-lines of the Qīng era, paralleling the contemporaneous **Tiānrán Hánshì** [[函昰]] line in Lǐngnán (cf. [[KR6q0526]]).
