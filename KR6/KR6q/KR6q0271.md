# Xuěfēng Yìcún chánshī yǔlù 雪峰義存禪師語錄 (Zhēnjué chánshī yǔlù 真覺禪師語錄)

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Xuěfēng Yìcún* — a two-juan Míng-edited *yǔlù* of the great late-Táng Chán master [[義存|Xuěfēng Yìcún]] 雪峰義存 (822–908), posthumous title *Zhēnjué chánshī* 真覺禪師. Compiled in the Míng by [[林弘衍|Lín Hóngyǎn]] 林弘衍.

## About the work

A two-juan Míng-edited *yǔlù* of a classical Táng master, X69 n1333. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

[[義存|Xuěfēng Yìcún]] 雪峰義存 (822–908): one of the most important late-Táng Chán masters; abbot of Xuěfēngsì 雪峰寺 in Fúzhōu. Dharma-heir of [[宣鑑|Déshān Xuānjiàn]] 德山宣鑑. Through his prominent dharma-heirs ([[文偃|Yúnmén Wényǎn]] 雲門文偃 the founder of Yúnmén-school; [[師備|Xuánshā Shībèi]] 玄沙師備 the ancestor of the Fǎyǎn-school), Xuěfēng's line produced two of the classical Chán Five Houses.

Compiler [[林弘衍|Lín Hóngyǎn]] 林弘衍: late-Míng lay Buddhist editor. Lifedates unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1600 (Míng Wànlì); *notAfter* 1644 (end of Míng).
