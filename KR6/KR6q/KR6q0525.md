# Pǔfēng Fǎzhù Dòng chánshī yǔlù 浦峰法柱棟禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fǎ-zhù Dòng of Pǔ-fēng*
by [[海棟]] (說), [[慧昇]] (集), [[慧渠]] (編), [[慧岱]] (錄)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Fǎzhù Hǎidòng** [[海棟]] 法柱海棟, Línjì-Yángqí 34th-generation dharma-heir of **Chángmíng Xìngjiǒng** [[性炅]] 長明性炅 (?–1668), itself the subject of [[KR6q0524]]. Native of **Lǐzhōu Shímén** 澧洲石門 (Húnán); lay surname **Chén** 陳; father **Sānguì** 三桂, mother née Zhào 趙. Parents both died when he was 20; left home at 25. Compiled jointly by three *fǎsì*: **Zōngqìng Huìshēng** [[慧昇]] *jí*, **Bìyán Huìqú** [[慧渠]] *biān*, **Zhōngshān Huìdài** [[慧岱]] *lù*. First abbacy at **Shàozhōu Jīnfēng Qīngliánān** 卲州金峰青蓮菴; succeeded his teacher at **Chénzhōufǔ Tiānyúnshān Pǔfēngsì** 辰州府天雲山浦峰寺 on **康熙辛亥 10/1** = **1671-11-02**, holding the seat for twenty years. *notBefore* = **1671** (Pǔfēng entry), *notAfter* = **1691** (block-printing date: **康熙辛未蒲月夏至前五日刊**, Kāngxī 30 / 1691, 5th lunar month). Printed at **Jiāxīngfǔ Léngyánsì** 嘉興府楞嚴寺 流通正藏 (cf. colophon "板存嘉興府楞嚴寺流通正藏"). Juan 1: *shàngtáng*, *shìzhòng*, *niāngǔ*, *sònggǔ*, ten oxherding *sòng* + *sìwēiyí*, twelve *liánfāngjì* (verse-bestowal poems for his twelve dharma-heirs); juan 2: *jīyuán*, *fǎyǔ*, *shìjì*, *zájì*, *xiàngzàn*, *shūxù*, *fóshì*, *fǎpài*, and the teacher's *xíngzhuàng* (biographical inscription). The 法派 transmission-verse runs **海慧明常淨通真達 本源道弘證佛果 紹繼祖燈** — Huì 慧 is the next generation, hence Huìshēng / Huìqú / Huìdài.
