# Shuàiān Fàncóng chánshī yǔlù 率菴梵琮禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Shuàiān Fàncóng* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Shuàiān Fàncóng** 率菴梵琮. Compiled by [[了見|Liǎojiàn]] 了見 (*xiǎo shī* junior-disciple) and [[文郁本空|Wényù Běnkōng]] 文郁本空 (*shìzhě* attendant). X69 n1364.

Dating: broad Southern-Sòng bracket (*notBefore* 1180, *notAfter* 1260).
