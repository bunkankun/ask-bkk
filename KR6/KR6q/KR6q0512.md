# Gǔsù Zūn chánshī yǔlù 古宿尊禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Gǔ-sù Zūn*
by [[尊]] (說), [[明圓]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Gǔsù Zūn** [[尊]] 古宿尊, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiāngyán Dàngshān Yuǎnwēng** 香巖宕山遠翁. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Bīnglún Míngyuán** [[明圓]] 冰輪明圓 *děng biān*. Native of Chéngdū; abbacies at **Dèngzhōu Dānxiá**, **Xiāngzhōu Dòngshān**, and Shǎnxī monasteries. *notBefore* / *notAfter*: c. 1660–1690. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B387.
