# Shāncí Jì chánshī yǔlù 山茨際禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shān-cí Jì*
by [[達尊]] (等編次), [[達謙]] (等編次)

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Shāncí Tōngjì** [[通際]] 山茨通際 (**1608-08-20 – 1645-03-05**, age 38, *zuòxià* 24 — the youngest death among the four founders of the **Qìngshān** 磬山 sub-branch). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635, cf. [[KR6q0603]]) — alongside **Yùlín Tōngxiù** [[通琇]] ([[KR6q0605]]), **Línggāo Tōngyù** 林臯通豫, and **Ruòān Tōngwèn** [[通問]]. Native of **Tōngzhōu** 通州 (Jiāngsū), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Ěrzhān Dázūn** [[達尊]] and **Xuáncí Dáqiān** [[達謙]] *děng biāncì*. Catalog source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** Vol. 157 No. 1649.

Documents teaching at three abbacies — concentrated in the dramatic decade 1635–1645 between the master's *fùfǎ* and his early death amid the dynastic transition: **Hángzhōufǔ Dōngmíng chánsì** 杭州府東明禪寺; **Nányuè Lǜluóān** 南嶽綠蘿菴 (Húnán); **Chángshāfǔ Liúyáng Nányuán chánsì** 長沙府瀏陽南源禪寺 (where his disciple [[達尊]] — at the same monastery — would later (1646–1663) restore the famous Shíshuāngsì 石霜寺 ancestral seat). *notBefore* = **1635** (post-Yuán-xiū-death *fùfǎ*); *notAfter* = **1645** (death). Catalog dynasty 清.

**Contents.** Juan 1: *Nányuè Shāncí Jì chánshī yǔlù xù*; *mùlù*; abbacy *yǔlù* records (Dōngmíngsì 1638+, Lǜluóān, Liúyáng Nányuánsì); *yīnshì*. Juan 2: *niāngǔ*; *dàigǔ*; *sònggǔ*. Juan 3: *jīyuán*; *fǎyǔ* (with disciples named: 示何二山居士, 示省指禪人, 示三學禪人, 示照初禪人, 示憩南楊居士, *shì nǐ Jìzǒng* 示尼繼總 — a nun-disciple, also addressed in the *Fúshī Jiēyuán Xínggāng* 行剛 *yǔlù* [[KR6q0428]]). Juan 4: closing materials.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0608]] (Shāncí Tōngjì) and [[KR6q0605]] (Yùlín Tōngxiù) are the two principal *yǔlù* of Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū's four heirs in the Qiánlóng-canon — Tōngjì's brief, intense career (38 years) producing the *Nányuè chándēnglù* (Húnán Chán biographical anthology) and three Hangzhou-Húnán abbacies; Tōngxiù's longer career producing imperial-court engagement and the *Guóshī* title. The dharma-heir transition from Tōngjì → Dázūn → Shíshuāngsì restoration (1646+) makes this one of the foundational lines of Qīng-era Húnán Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism — see [[KR6q0420]] 《石霜爾瞻尊禪師語錄》 for the next-generation continuation.
