# Shānxī Bǎishān Kǎi chánshī yǔlù 山西柏山楷禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bǎi-shān Kǎi of Shān-xī*
by [[德楷]] (說), [[行悟]] (等編次)

Five-juan *yǔlù* of **Bǎishān Dékǎi** [[德楷]] 柏山德楷 (**1629 – 1702-04-19**, age 74, *sēnglà* 42), **Cáodòng-school** 31st-generation dharma-heir of **Zhúlàng Tōngér** 竹浪通爾 at **Juéhuá** 覺華. Native of **Wéiyáng Tàixìng** 維揚泰興 (Yángzhōu), lay surname **Zhuāng** 莊. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Xíngwù** [[行悟]] *děng biāncì*. Three prefaces, one labeled **重編** *zhòngbiān*.

Documents Dékǎi's three Shānxī abbacies — an unusual northern-China Cáodòng circuit: **Shānxī Fēnzhōufǔ Fēnyángxiàn Pǔtōng chánsì** 山西汾州府汾陽縣普通禪寺; **Báiyún chánsì** 白雲禪寺; **Fēnzhōufǔ Xiàoyìxiàn Lóngshān Huáyán chányuàn** 汾州府孝義縣龍山華嚴禪院 (the title-throne for the **Cáodòng 31st-generation** lineage seat). *notBefore* ≈ **1670** (post-付法 abbacy career); *notAfter* = **1702** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B466.

**Contents.** Juan 1: three prefaces; *xíngshí*; *Pǔtōngsì yǔlù*; *Báiyúnsì yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Lóngshān Huáyányuàn yǔlù*. Juan 3: *xiǎocān*; *wǎncān*; *shìzhòng*. Juan 4: *pǔshuō*; *chóushì fǎyǔ*; *jīyuán*. Juan 5: *niāngǔ*; *sònggǔ*; *chuíshì*; *zuòmíng*; *xiàngzàn*.

## Other points of interest

A rare **northern-China Cáodòng** *yǔlù* in J39, with all three abbacies in **Fēnzhōufǔ** 汾州府 (Shānxī). Dékǎi's earlier formation in **Yángzhōu** Tàixìng (his birthplace) and Juéhuá (Cáodòng base) before migrating north establishes a distinct geographic-lineage trajectory. The *zhòngbiān* preface indicates a previously-circulated edition before this canonical version.
