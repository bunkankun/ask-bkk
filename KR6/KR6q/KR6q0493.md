# Yúnhuàn Chén chánshī yǔlù 雲幻宸禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yún-huàn Chén*
by [[印宸]] (說), [[發林]] (等編), [[王民皞]] (序), [[李道泰]] (塔記)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Yúnhuàn Yìnchén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623 – **1669-05-15**, *shìshòu* 47), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Héngxiù Fālín** [[發林]]. Front-preface by **Wáng Mínhào** [[王民皞]] (古臨弟), dated **康熙癸丑春 = 1673 spring** at Yúnnán Āmí. *Tǎjì* by **Lǐ Dàotài** [[李道泰]]. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B353.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Native of **Zhōngzhōu** 忠州 (Sìchuān). Sole abbacy at **Bāzhōu Lóngtán Zhōngfēng Chánsì** 巴州龍潭中峰禪寺 from **順治戊戌 = 1658** (entry-sermon).

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1658** (Lóngtán abbacy entry). *notAfter* = **1673** (Wáng Mínhào preface, four years post-decease).

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Yúnhuàn Yìnchén → Héngxiù Fālín. One of Pòshān's many heirs of Sìchuān provenance.
