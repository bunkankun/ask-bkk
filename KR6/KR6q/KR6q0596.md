# Dǒunán Wěi chánshī yǔlù 斗南暐禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dǒu-nán Wěi*
by [[暐]] (說), [[普潤]] (等錄)

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Dǒunán Wěi** [[暐]] 斗南暐 (lifedates unknown), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Biéān Xìngtǒng** 別庵性統 (1661–1717) of **Pǔtuóshān** — thus a co-disciple of **Cuìyá Bì** [[必]] (cf. [[KR6q0595]]). Recorded by attendant **Pǔrùn** [[普潤]] *děng lù* (with co-recorder **Fǎquán** 法權). Documents teaching at two Zhèjiāng / Jiāngsū abbacies: **Wēnzhōu Jiēdài chánsì** 溫州接待禪寺 and **Wúxī Cíyún chánsì** 無錫慈雲禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1700**; *notAfter* ≈ **1730**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B488.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *Wēnzhōu Jiēdàisì yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*; *bǐngfú*; *jīyuán*; *niān* on classic *gōngàn* (大事未明 / 大事已明 如喪考妣, 青林挑柴, 國師三喚侍者, 南泉住庵). Juan 2: *sònggǔ*. Juan 3: *jì* (eyes-ears-nose-tongue-body-mind 六首, *sìwēiyí*, *tí Shínǚ fūrén* 題石女夫人, *tí Dànhéshàng* 題淡和尚, *shìzhōng lǐchàn yǒu gǎn* 室中禮懺有感, *cǎibǎizǐ* 採柏子).

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0595]] ([[必]]) and [[KR6q0596]] ([[暐]]) are paired *yǔlù* of two co-disciples of **Biéān Xìngtǒng** 別庵性統 (the Pǔtuóshān LínjìYángqí mainstay), documenting the early-18th-century outflow from Pǔtuóshān into the Zhèjiāng / Jiāngsū / Beijing axis. Together with [[KR6q0209]] 《高峰三山來禪師年譜》 (Xìngtǒng's compilation of his teacher's chronology) and [[KR6q0203]] (Xìngtǒng's own *yǔlù*), the cluster captures three generations of the **Sānshān Dēnglái 三山燈來** → **Biéān Xìngtǒng** → second-generation lineage at the most prominent late-Qīng coastal Buddhist site.
