# Xiàngyái Tǐng chánshī yǔlù 象崖珽禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiàng-yái Tǐng*
by [[性珽]] (說), [[益聞]] (重刊)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Xiàngyái Xìngtǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (**1598 – 1651-08/09**), Línjì-Yángqí thirty-sixth-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). *Chóngkān* 重刊 (re-cut) by *fǎsūn* **Yìwén** [[益聞]]. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B308. Includes juan-4 *xíngzhuàng* + *tǎmíng*.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[性珽]] note: native of **Fúqīng** 福清 (Fúzhōu, Fújiàn), lay surname **Chén** 陳. Tonsured at 19 (1616) at **Chánglè Chángxī Fúgōng**. Multi-teacher Chán pilgrimage circuit: **Huángbò Zhōng** (3 years), **Gǔshān Wúyì** (no transmission), **Huángbò Mìyún Yuánwù** (full precepts), **Jīnsù Yuánwù** (no transmission). Final transmission from **Pòshān Hǎimíng** in c. 1640.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1640** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1651** (death). Six abbacies: **Lóngpán** → **Niānhuá** → **Bǎolín** → **Léngjiā** → **Huángbò** (twice) → **Yǒuyáng Huáyánsì** 酉陽華嚴寺 (death-seat at Sìchuān 酉陽 prefecture).

**Significance.** Among the Pòshān heirs, Xìngtǐng is distinctive for his Fújiàn provincial provenance (most Pòshān heirs were Sì-chuān-bred), for his successive Jiāngnán → Sìchuān trajectory (ending at the Yǒuyáng border with **Rǎn Yùcén** patron — the same lay-patron family later documented in [[KR6q0467]]'s 1671 *kāitáng* roster), and for training the young Liányuè Dàozhèng before Liányuè crossed to Pòshān himself (cf. [[KR6q0445]] juan 5 *xíngshí*).

**Heirs.** **Shígǔ Huì** 石谷慧, **Yúnfù Zhì** [[道智]] 雲腹智 (cf. [[KR6q0480]]), **Yúshān Ruì** 余山瑞.

**Re-cut status.** Catalog meta gives the editor function as *chóngkān* 重刊 — meaning Yìwén prepared a re-cut of an earlier recension. The Jiāxīng Canon J34 B308 imprint is the *chóngkān* version.

## Other points of interest

- **Fú-jiàn-bred Pòshān heir.** Xìngtǐng is a rare non-Sì-chuān Pòshān-line heir, reflecting the cross-regional reach of Pòshān's Sichuan-Jiāngnán-Fújiàn pilgrimage circuit.
- **Yǒuyáng patronage continuity.** The Rǎn Yùcén (酉陽 司主, *xuānwèisī*) lay-patron family appears continuously across two generations of Pòshān-line texts: Xìngtǐng's final abbacy patron in 1650, and the Bùlín → Lùnán Dōushuài patron in 1671 (cf. [[KR6q0467]]).
