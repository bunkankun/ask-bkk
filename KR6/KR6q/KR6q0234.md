# Zhèngjué Rùnguāng Zé chánshī zǎo xuě jí 正覺潤光澤禪師澡雪集

*Bathing-in-Snow Collection of Chán Master Rùnguāng Zé of Zhèngjué*

A one-juan Qīng Chán poetic-miscellany by [[澤|Rùnguāng Zé]] 潤光澤 (catalog single-character name: Zé 澤), *hào* Rùnwēng 潤翁, abbot of Zhèngjuésì 正覺寺. Re-edited (*chóng biān* 重編) by his dharma-heir [[照水|Zhàoshuǐ]] 照水 (spring-tide 春臺, referenced as a visiting-disciple in the preface); prefaced by [[陳聞道|Chén Wéndào]] 陳聞道.

## About the work

A brief one-juan poetic and literary collection, J39 B458. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. The title *Zǎo xuě jí* 澡雪集 ("Bathing in Snow") evokes a classical Chán-poetic trope of purification through extreme practice. [[陳聞道|Chén Wéndào]]'s preface was written after Zé's death, when his disciple Zhàoshuǐ came to Chén with the compiled manuscript seeking a preface.

Zé composed his poetry and prose *huò xíng yín zé pàn, huò wò bìng chuáng tóu* 或行吟澤畔或臥病床頭 ("sometimes reciting-verses by the marshes, sometimes lying on the sickbed") — a poetic self-description indicating a life-style mixing meditative-solitude retreats and physical frailty.

### Abstract

[[澤|Rùnguāng Zé]] 潤光澤: Qīng Chán master, abbot of Zhèngjuésì 正覺寺. Lifedates unrecorded. Based on the preface's *jiǎzǐ* 甲子 reference (likely Kāngxī 23 = 1684), Zé died by about 1684 with his poetic corpus being compiled by his disciple shortly thereafter.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1670 (Zé's mature productive period); *notAfter* c. 1700 (the publication-horizon).
