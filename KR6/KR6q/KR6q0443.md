# Jièān Jìn chánshī yǔlù 介菴進禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jiè-ān Jìn*
by [[悟進]] (說), [[真理]] (等編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Jièān Wùjìn** [[悟進]] 介菴悟進 (1612 – after 1680s) — principal dharma-heir of [[妙用|Yuānhú Miàoyòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (1587–1642, cf. [[KR6q0416]]) in the **Wúqù Rúkōng → Chēxī Wúhuàn → Nánmíng Zōngguǎng** Cáodòng sub-line. Also the editorial author of the *xíngzhuàng* preserved in [[KR6q0416]] juan 2 for his master. *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Wùjìn** 悟進, *hào* **Jièān** 介菴, late-life *hào* **Zhǐzū chánshī** 之祖禪師 / **Jièān Jìn** 介菴進 (per catalog). Lay surname **Zhāng** 張, of **Jiāxīng** 嘉興. Father **Zhāng Shùnfǔ** 張舜甫; mother **Hèshì** 賀氏 who dreamt of the sun-wheel at conception. Born **萬曆 40 壬子 10.9 辰時** = **~2 November 1612** Western. Principal abbot of **Jiāxīng Jīnmíngsì** 嘉興金明寺 from late 1640s onwards. Compiled by dharma-heir **Zhēnlǐ** [[真理]] 真理. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B233.

### Abstract

**Author.** See the extensive biographical frame established at [[KR6q0416]] (where Wùjìn authored the *xíngzhuàng* for his master Miàoyòng). The present *yǔlù* preserves Wùjìn's own juan 5 *xíngshí* 行實, delivering the following additional personal detail: tonsure at **age 23** (= 1634) at **Tóngyuèān** 桐月庵 under **Wànrú Tōngwēi** 萬如通微 (Miàoyòng's hermitage-successor; cf. [[KR6q0404]]); 1638 interview with **Mìyún Yuánwù**; decisive awakening on hearing the carpenter's axe-sound during an illness-period at Tóngyuèān. Miàoyòng passed the **源流衣拂** 源流衣拂 formal transmission to Wùjìn in **1642** at age 31. After Miàoyòng's 1642 October death, Wùjìn went to **Wǔlín Bǎoshòusì** 武林寶壽寺 in 1643 spring to meet **Shíyǔ Míngfāng** 石雨明方 (Miàoyòng's dharma-brother) to request the *tǎmíng* for Miàoyòng (later preserved at [[KR6q0416]]).

**Abbacies (1648+).** **順治戊子** 順治戊子 = **1648**: received an abbacy-invitation from Jiāhé protectors — most likely the **Jiāxīng Jīnmíngsì** 金明寺 seat that serves as his primary abbatial designation in subsequent cross-references. The preface signature to the present text identifies him as "**秀州金明寺嗣祖沙門悟進**" — the *zhǔchí* 住持 of **Xiùzhōu Jīnmíngsì** (秀州 = Jiāxīng).

**Compositional history.** The opening preface is **self-authored** (signed "*Xiùzhōu Jīnmíngsì sìzǔ shāmén Wùjìn shū* 秀州金明寺嗣祖沙門悟進書") — rare for a Chán *yǔlù* to open with a self-preface. The preface explicitly argues for the authenticity of the **源流法偈** lineage-transmission-verse genre against those who question it. Undated but consistent with a late-career Jīnmíngsì cutting c. 1670s–1690s. *notBefore* = **1648** (first Jiāxīng abbacy invitation); *notAfter* = **1690** conservatively.

**Contents by juan.** (j.1) 序 + 啟 + **shàngtáng** 上堂; (j.2) **shàngtáng** continued; (j.3) **shìzhòng** 示眾; (j.4) **xiǎocān** 小參; (j.5) **niāngǔ** 拈古 + **sònggǔ** 頌古 + **行實** 行實 (self-authored autobiography); (j.6) **jīyuán** 機緣 + **wèndá** 問答 + **chuíwèn** 垂問; (j.7) **xiàngzàn** 像贊 + **fǎyǔ** 法語 + **shūwèn** 書問; (j.8) **jìsòng** 偈頌; (j.9) **shījì** 詩偈; (j.10) **shūyǐn** 疏引 + **xùbá** 序跋 + **zhēnmíng** 箴銘.

## Other points of interest

- **Self-authored preface.** The opening preface signed by Wùjìn himself is the distinctive feature of this *yǔlù* — most Chán *yǔlù* open with lay-patron or senior-dharma-brother prefaces, rarely with a master-self-preface. Wùjìn's self-preface polemicizes against those who doubt the **源流法偈** lineage-verse genre, defending the authenticity of the Wúqù / Nánmíng / Miàoyòng transmission-tradition. This positions the present *yǔlù* as explicitly **self-defending lineage-authentication** — a late-career theological-polemical posture consistent with the Cáodòng-Línjì sectarian tensions of the mid-Kāng-xī era.
- **The Jīnmíngsì abbacy continuity.** Wùjìn's long tenure at **Jiāxīng Jīnmíngsì** (from c. 1648 through at least the 1680s, = c. 35+ years) is one of the longest single-seat Chán abbacies documented in the J28–J29 cluster. The stability of this tenure — unusual among the abbacy-hopping masters of the period — reflects Wùjìn's specific editorial-institutional focus on the master-Miào-yòng legacy-custodianship.
