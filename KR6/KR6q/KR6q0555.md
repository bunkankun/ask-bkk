# Gāofēng Qiáosōng Yì chánshī yǔlù 高峰喬松億禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qiáo-sōng Yì of Gāo-fēng [-yuàn]*
by [[玄億]] (說), [[燈洪]] (編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Qiáosōng Xuányì** [[玄億]] 喬松玄億 (also **Dēngyì** 燈億 in DILA's primary form) (b. **1619-12-11**, death year unknown — was 51 in 1669), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1608–1663), thus a co-disciple of **Héngshān Dēngbǐng** [[燈炳]] (cf. [[KR6q0554]]). Native of **Línshuǐ** 鄰水 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Féng** 馮. Compiled by his *shūjì* **Dēnghóng** [[燈洪]] *biān*.

The *yǔlù* documents three abbacies, two in Sìchuān and one east in Húběi: **Zhōngzhōu Bǎiyánsì** 忠州柏巖寺; **Kuízhōufǔ Liángshānxiàn Gāofēng chányuàn** 夔州府梁山縣高峰禪院 (the seat giving him his sobriquet); **Èzhōu Dàbiéshān Wòyún chányuàn** 鄂州大別山臥雲禪院 (Wǔhàn area). *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-Qìng-zhōng-flight era of 付法); *notAfter* ≈ **1675** (continuing abbacy at Wòyún after 1669, plus printing). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B440.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *mùcì*; *Liángshān Gāofēng yǔlù*; *Èzhōu Dàbiéshān Wòyún yǔlù*; *fǎyǔ* and *jì* including 示寧仙居士, 送大樹禪者行腳, 寄慈祥禪友, 示楊仁寰居士, 示純若禪者 etc., and 山居 mountain-dwelling poems. Juan 2: *xíngyóu* (autobiographical narration), continuing *jì* and *fóshì*.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0554]] and [[KR6q0555]] are paired witnesses to the **Sìchuān → Wǔ-Hàn** axis of the Línjì 大慧-school **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line: both Dēngbǐng (Héngshān) and Xuányì (Qiáosōng) were dharma-heirs of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī**, and both eventually moved east into Húběi (Mountain-East Wǔ-Hàn region) — Xuányì at the famous **Dàbiéshān** 大別山 directly across the river from Wǔchāng — illustrating how the post-Sānfān-luàn Sìchuān refugees consolidated downriver in eastern Húběi during the late Kāngxī period.
