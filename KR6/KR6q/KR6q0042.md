# Nán Sòng Yuán-Míng chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn 南宋元明禪林僧寶傳

*Biographies of the Treasure of the Saṅgha of the Chán Forest from the Southern Sòng through the Yuán and Míng*

by [[自融]] Zìróng (撰), supplemented (補輯) by [[性磊]] Xìnglěi, preface dated 1654 (Qīng *Shùnzhì* 11)

## About the work

A 15-juan early-Qīng continuation of the *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* tradition ([[KR6q0040]] + [[KR6q0041]]), covering Chán biographies from the Southern Sòng through the Yuán and Míng. Compiled by Zìróng with substantial supplementary material added by Xìnglěi.

### Abstract

The work completes the three-part *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* sub-tradition by adding the post-Northern-Sòng generations that [[慧洪]] Huìhóng's original ([[KR6q0040]]) and [[祖琇]] Zǔxiù's first continuation ([[KR6q0041]]) had not covered. Editorial method follows the established *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* literary-biographical format. Circulated in the Jiāxīng 嘉興 canon.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Discussed within scholarship on the *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* tradition.

## Other points of interest

Together with [[KR6q0040]] and [[KR6q0041]], this work forms the canonical three-part biographical compendium of Chán masters from the Táng through the Míng, in continuous literary-biographical (rather than lamp-record) format.
