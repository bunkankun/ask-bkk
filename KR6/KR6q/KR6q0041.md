# Sēngbǎo zhèngxù zhuàn 僧寶正續傳

*Orthodox Continuation of the Biographies of the Treasure of the Saṅgha*

by [[祖琇]] Zǔxiù (撰), Southern Sòng (composed ca. 1131–1162)

## About the work

A seven-juan supplement to [[慧洪]] Huìhóng's *Chánlín sēngbǎo zhuàn* ([[KR6q0040]]), by the Southern Sòng monk Zǔxiù. Adds biographies of further Chán masters from the Northern Sòng to the early Southern Sòng, in continuation of Huìhóng's biographical method.

### Abstract

Zǔxiù — the same Zǔxiù who compiled the *Lóngxīng biānnián tōnglùn* [[隆興編年通論]] of 1164 — extends Huìhóng's biographical compendium with later figures, particularly from the Yángqí 楊岐 line whose ascendancy in the early Southern Sòng was already evident. The work preserves Huìhóng's literary-narrative format and is treated as the canonical second member of the three-part *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* sub-tradition ([[KR6q0040]] + [[KR6q0041]] + [[KR6q0042]]).

## Translations and research

No English translation. Treated as part of the *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* tradition in studies of Sòng Chán biographical literature; no dedicated monograph.

## Other points of interest

The continuity of literary-biographical method from Huìhóng through Zǔxiù gives the *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* tradition an unusually consistent stylistic profile across its three constituent works.
