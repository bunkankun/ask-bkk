# Shíxī Xīnyuè chánshī zálù 石溪心月禪師雜錄

One-juan miscellany of **Shíxī Xīnyuè** [[心月]] 石溪心月 (d. 1254), *hào* Chuányī 傳衣 ("Robe-Transmitter"), posthumously **Fóhǎi chánshī**. *Xuzangjing* X71 n1406. The running banner reads *Chuányī Shíxī Fóhǎi chánshī zálù*. Occasional verse and prose not absorbed into the main *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]]): *hé* 和 (response-verse) to laymen, farewell poems for travelling monks, single-*shǒu* occasional pieces. Short but characterful — shares the hermit-poetic sensibility that is a signature of mid-Sòng Jìngshān Chán.
