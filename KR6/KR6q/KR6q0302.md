# Yǎnxī Guǎngwén chánshī yǔlù 偃溪廣聞禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Yǎnxī Guǎngwén* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Yǎnxī Guǎngwén** 偃溪廣聞 (1189–1263), Línjì-Yángqí master; dharma-heir of Pòān Zǔxiān 破菴祖先. Compiled by nine of his attendant-disciples.

Two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1368.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1200; *notAfter* c. 1270.
