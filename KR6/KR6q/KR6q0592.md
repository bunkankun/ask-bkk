# Zhúfēng Mǐn chánshī yǔlù 竺峰敏禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhú-fēng Mǐn*
by [[幻敏]] (說), [[性鉅]] (等編), [[性湛]] (等編), [[性珩]] (等編), [[性濟]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* (+ 2 juan *hòulù*) of **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] 竺峰幻敏 (**1639-01-18 – 1707-04-14**, age 70, *sēnglà* 65 — exceptionally long, having been tonsured at age 10), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668) in the **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line — thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]] ([[KR6q0554]]), [[玄億]] ([[KR6q0555]]), and [[至善]] ([[KR6q0556]]). Native of **Fēnglíng** 酆陵 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Xú** 徐. Compiled jointly by four *fǎsì*: **Xìngjù** [[性鉅]], **Xìngzhàn** [[性湛]], **Xìnghéng** [[性珩]], **Xìngjì** [[性濟]]. *notBefore* = **1668** (post-master-death succession at Qìngzhōng); *notAfter* = **1707** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B483.

The *yǔlù* documents teaching at four abbacies, with **Sìchuān Zhōngzhōu Chìjiàn Zhènzōng chánsì** 四川忠州敕建振宗禪寺 (the **imperially-bestowed** Sìchuān seat) as the principal preaching site (juan 1–2). Five *qǐngqǐ* invitation-petitions are preserved at the head of juan 1, signed by **Yáng** prefect (郡侯), **Liú** sub-prefect (副府), and the prefectural gentry — establishing the imperial-state-supported character of the Zhènzōng abbacy.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *mùlù*; five *qǐngqǐ*; *Zhènzōngsì shàngtáng*. Juan 2: continuing *Zhènzōng yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*. Juan 3: *shìzhòng*; *cháhuà*; *jīyuán*; *fóshì* (with the funeral cycle for **Qìngzhōng lǎorén** 慶忠老人 = Tiěbì Huìjī's **1668** death — *guàzhōng*, *rùguān*, *qǐkān*, *rùtǎ*). Juan 4–6: continuing materials including the closing *xíngzhuàng* and *fēndēng* listing thirty-three *fǎsì* and eighteen lay disciples.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0451]] (the master Tiěbì's 20-juan *yǔlù* re-edited by Huànmǐn) and [[KR6q0592]] (Huànmǐn's own 6-juan *yǔlù*) together form the principal Línjì-Dàhuì **Jùyún** 聚雲 / **Qìngzhōng** lineage textual record. Huànmǐn's exceptionally long *sēnglà* (65 years, tonsured at age 10) places him among the most prolific southwestern Línjì lineage-builders of the Qīng era. The *Lèngyánsì* 楞嚴寺 abbacy at Jiāxīng directly connects him to the Jiāxīng Canon publication operation.
