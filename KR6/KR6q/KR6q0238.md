# Tiānníng Fǎzhōu Jì chánshī shèng yǔ 天寧法舟濟禪師剩語

*Remaining Words of Chán Master Fǎzhōu Jì of Tiānníng* — a one-juan mid-Míng Chán *yǔlù* of [[道濟|Fǎzhōu Dàojì]] 法舟道濟 (1487–1560), abbot of Tiānníngsì 天寧寺 in Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). The title *shèng yǔ* 剩語 ("remaining words") is self-deprecating, suggesting what survived from the master's teaching. Compiled by his dharma-heir [[如淵|Rúyuān]] 如淵 et al.

## About the work

A one-juan mid-Míng Chán teaching-collection, J40 B491. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text preserves Fǎzhōu Dàojì's teachings from his productive Jiāxīng Tiānníngsì abbacy — a distinguishing work documenting mid-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán practice in the period before the late-Míng revival associated with Mìyún Yuánwù and his generation.

### Abstract

[[道濟|Fǎzhōu Dàojì]] 法舟道濟 (1487 – 1560, age 74). *Hào* Fǎzhōu 法舟 ("Dharma-Boat"). Native of Jiāxīng 嘉興 (Zhèjiāng). Abbot of Tiānníngsì 天寧寺. Principal dharma-heir: [[東谿方澤|Dōngxī Fāngzé]] 東谿方澤 (of Jīngyánsì 精嚴寺).

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1520 (Dàojì's mature productive period); *notAfter* 1560 (his death).
