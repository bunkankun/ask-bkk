# Sānfēng Zàng héshàng yǔlù 三峰藏和尚語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Reverend Sān-fēng Zàng*
by [[法藏]] (說), [[弘儲]] (記)

## About the work

Sixteen-juan *yǔlù* of **Hànyuè Sānfēng Fǎzàng** [[法藏]] 漢月三峰法藏 (1573-11-11 – 1635-09-03), late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of [[圓悟]] **Mìyún Yuánwù** (received *fùfǎ* in 1624) and the most theologically prominent — and ultimately most controversial — of Yuánwù's heirs. Recorded by his chief dharma-heir **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 (1605–1672). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B299. The j. 16 *niánpǔ* (year-chronology, 1573–1635) is the principal source for Fǎzàng's biography.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[法藏]] note: lay surname **Sū** 蘇, native of **Liángxī** 梁溪 (Wúxī, Jiāngsū). Lifedates **萬曆 1 / 10/7 = 1573-11-11** to **崇禎 8 / 7/22 = 1635-09-03**, age 63. *Zì* **Hànyuè** 漢月; *hào* **Sānfēng** 三峰 (after **Sānfēng Qīngliángyuàn** 三峰清涼院 at Yúshān 虞山 in Chángshú), also **Yúmì** 於密, **Tiānshān** 天山, **Xuánmù** 玄墓. Abbacies at **Sānfēng Qīngliáng**, **Xuánmù** (Dèngwèishān, Sūzhōu), **Tiānshān**, and **Wànfēng / Shèngēnsì** 萬峰 / 聖恩禪寺 at **Píngwàng** 平望.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1624** (post-*fùfǎ* receipt from Mìyún and beginning of independent preaching). *notAfter* = **1635** (death). The 16-juan corpus covers his complete public preaching career.

**Doctrinal centrality.** Author of [[KR6q0167]] 《五宗原》 (the 1628 theological defense of the Five Houses of Chán doctrine) which precipitated the major seventeenth-century Chán controversy. Mìyún Yuánwù wrote [[KR6q0168]] 《闢妄救略說》 against him; the dispute reached imperial attention with the Yōngzhèng emperor's 1733 《御製揀魔辨異錄》 declaring Fǎzàng a heretic and banning his writings. Despite this, the J34 B299 *yǔlù* survived in the Jiāxīng Canon imprint.

**Lineage.** Hànyuè Fǎzàng → **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] (continuator at Sūzhōu Língyánsì, [[KR6q0475]]) — the principal sub-lineage that became the target of the 1733 imperial proscription.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon Míng *yǔlù* (J34 B299). The juan-16 niánpǔ provides the principal documentation.*

## Other points of interest

- **Subject of the most consequential Chán doctrinal controversy of the seventeenth century.** The *Wǔzōng yuán* / *Pìwàng jiùlüèshuō* dispute, prolonged through the *Zhìfēi biàn* 智非辨 and other rejoinders, dominated mid-seventeenth-century Chán intellectual life and was suppressed by Yōngzhèng in 1733 with consequences for the Sānfēng line lasting into the modern era.
- **Survival despite proscription.** That this 16-juan *yǔlù* was preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon despite the 1733 imperial banning of Fǎzàng's writings reflects the limits of Yōngzhèng's suppression — the Jiāxīng Canon, having been printed through privately-funded subscription in the seventeenth century, was not under direct imperial editorial control.
