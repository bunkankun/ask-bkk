# Xièyún Jī chánshī Guóqīng Wúwèitáng yǔlù 燮雲璣禪師國清無畏堂語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiè-yún Jī at the Wú-wèi-táng of Guó-qīng-sì*
by [[濟璣]] (說), [[玄杲]] (錄), [[張有譽]] (序)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Xièyún Jìjī** [[濟璣]] 燮雲濟璣 (d. **1648**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hànyuè Sānfēng** sub-line. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Xuángǎo** [[玄杲]] *lù* at the **Wúwèitáng** 無畏堂 of **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** 天台國清寺 — the historic Tiāntái-school flagship monastery, an unusual seat for a Línjì abbot. Front-preface by **Zhāng Yǒuyù** [[張有譽]] *Dàyuán jūshì* 大圓居士 (1589–1669, the same Wúxī lay-Chán official documented as preface-writer for [[KR6q0204]] under Hóngchǔ's teaching). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B303.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA A015946 and 《五燈全書》 j. 84: native of **Tōngzhōu** 通州. Initial three-year training under **Sānfēng Fǎzàng** [[法藏]]; after Fǎzàng's 1635 death continued under heir **Língyán Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] at **Xiángfúsì** 祥符寺. Decisive *chèzhèng* 徹證 on the *Mìaoxǐ qǐngyì Fóguǒ yǒujù wújù* 妙喜請益佛果有句無句 *gōngàn*. Abbot at Tiāntái Guóqīngsì where he died (1648) and was interred in stūpa.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1640** (post-transmission abbacy at Guóqīng). *notAfter* = **1648** (death). Single-juan *yǔlù* reflects the relatively brief Guóqīng abbacy.

**Línjì at Guóqīng.** Jìjī's installation as Línjì-line abbot of the **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** is institutionally significant — the historic seat of **Zhìyǐ** 智顗 and Tiāntái-school doctrinal authority, but here held by a Línjì-Yángqí Sān-fēng-line monastic. The 無畏堂 *Wúwèitáng* hall-name suggests a specific preaching-hall within the larger Guóqīng compound.

**Lineage.** Mìyún Yuánwù → Hànyuè Fǎzàng → Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ → Xièyún Jìjī. The Sānfēng sub-line was suppressed by Yōngzhèng's 1733 proscription, but this 1648 *yǔlù* survived in the Jiāxīng Canon imprint.

## Other points of interest

- **Línjì abbacy of Tiāntái flagship.** Jìjī's seat at Guóqīngsì represents an instance of cross-tradition institutional capture — a Línjì-Yángqí Sān-fēng-line abbot at the Suí-era foundation of Chinese Tiāntái-school doctrinal practice. Such cross-tradition abbacies were common in the late-Míng / early-Qīng but particularly pointed at the Tiāntái flagship.
