# Tōngtiān Dànyá Yuán chánshī yǔlù 通天澹崖原禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dàn-yá Yuán of Tōng-tiān [-sì]*
by [[超原]] (說), [[明德]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Tōngtiān Dànyá Chāoyuán** [[超原]] 通天澹崖超原 (**1630-12-14 – 1698-05-27**, age 69, *sēnglà* 36), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tōngtiān Yìsǒu Xínggāo** [[行高]] (1616–1669, cf. [[KR6q0569]]). Native of **Xīnghuà Pútián** 興化莆田 (Fújiàn), lay surname **Zhāng** 張. Compiled by his dharma-son **Míngdé** [[明德]] *děng biān*. Documents Chāoyuán's one-year abbacy at **Púyáng Tōngtiān chánsì** 蒲陽通天禪寺 succeeding his teacher in **1669**, plus the subsequent retirement period during which he travelled the **Lúshān** 廬嶽 and **Jīnlíng** 金陵 monastic circuits. *notBefore* = **1669** (Tōngtiān succession); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-1698-death printing). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B456.

**Contents.** Two juan: *shàngtáng*, *xiǎocān*, *jīyuán*, *jì*, *fóshì*; closing *xíngshí* (autobiographical / hagiographical narration); *liánfāngjì* listing his thirteen *fǎsì*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔlù* completes the teacher-disciple pair with [[KR6q0569]] — together documenting two generations of **Púyáng Tōngtiānsì** Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism in Fújiàn under the **Wànrú Tōngwēi** branch. Chāoyuán is the same person (or possibly homonymous within the broader Mì-yún-line cohort) who authored the prefaces to several Sìchuān *yǔlù* in the J38–J39 cluster (cf. [[KR6q0554]], [[KR6q0557]], [[KR6q0559]] — all signed in 1695–1696, dated within his lifetime).
