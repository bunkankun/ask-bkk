# Hóngshān Yúzhāo Yǔnfén chánshī yǔlù 洪山俞昭允汾禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yú-zhāo Yǔn-fén of Hóng-shān*
by [[允汾]] (說), [[上睿]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Yúzhāo Yǔnfén** [[允汾]] 俞昭允汾 (**1622 – 1680-02-12**, *shìshòu* 59, *sēnglà* 38), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] in the Hànyuè Sānfēng line. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Chǔlín Shàngruì** [[上睿]] *děng biān*. Six abbacies: **西林乾明** → **潼泉法興** → **東巖廣慧** → **竟陵紫芝** → **寶通報國** → **天台赤城**. Title-form *Hóngshān* references his **Wǔchāng Hóngshān** abbacy. Juan 5 includes the *Xiàotáng héshàng tǎmíng*. *notBefore* = c. 1655 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1680 (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B389.
