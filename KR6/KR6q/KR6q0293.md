# Yīngān Tánhuá chánshī yǔlù 應菴曇華禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Yīngān Tánhuá* — a ten-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Yīngān Tánhuá** 應菴曇華 (1103–1163), a major figure in the Hǔqiū branch of the LínjìYángqí line — dharma-heir of Hǔqiū Shàolóng 虎丘紹隆 (see [[KR6q0292]]), and teacher of Mìān Xiánjié 密菴咸傑 (d. 1186), who in turn taught Pòān Zǔxiān 破菴祖先 and Fózhào Déguāng 佛照德光.

Ten-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1359. Compiled by dharma-heir [[守詮|Shǒuquán]] 守詮 et al.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1140; *notAfter* c. 1180.
