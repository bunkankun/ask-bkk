# Dàjué Pǔjì Néngrén Xiù Guóshī yǔlù 大覺普濟能仁琇國師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of the State Preceptor Xiù, Greatly-Awakened, Universally Saving, Bestower of Benevolence*
by [[行岳]] (編)

Seven-juan *yǔlù* of **Yùlín Tōngxiù** [[通琇]] 玉林通琇 (**1614-05-28 – 1675-09-28**, age 62), the **Shùnzhì-emperor's preceptor** and most prominent Línjì-Yángqí *guóshī* (state preceptor) of the early Qīng. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635, cf. [[KR6q0603]]) — one of the four founders of the **Qìngshān** 磬山 sub-branch. Native of **Jiāngyīn** 江陰. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Qīyún Xíngyuè** [[行岳]] (1614–1666) *biān*. The full title invokes Tōngxiù's three successive imperial titles bestowed by Shùnzhì: **Dàjué chánshī** 大覺禪師, **Dàjué Pǔjì chánshī**, and **Dàjué Pǔjì Néngrén Guóshī**.

*notBefore* ≈ **1640** (post-Yuán-xiū-1635-death, *fùfǎ* and abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1675** (death). Catalog dynasty 清; source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** Vol. 154 No. 1641.

**Contents.** Juan 1: title-frame as **Dàjué Pǔjì Néngrén Yùlín Xiù Guóshī yǔlù** 大覺普濟能仁玉琳琇國師語錄 (with the imperially-bestowed name **Yùlín 玉琳** in the imperial form rather than Yùlín 玉林); *mùlù* (introduced by **Jǐ Yī** 幾一); *shàngtáng* (one). Juan 2: *shàngtáng* (two); *shìzhòng*. Juans 3–4: *xiǎocān* (one and two — extensive). Juan 5: *fǎyǔ* (instructions to disciples by name — including *Quánān Jìn shàngzuò* 全菴進上座, *Dǐng shìsī* 嵿侍司, *Yuè shūzhuàng* 岳書狀 = the editor [[行岳]]). Juans 6–7: continuing materials.

## Other points of interest

The imperial title sequence is significant: the bestowal of *Guóshī* status — preceptorship to the empire — is unique in J40 to **Yùlín Tōngxiù** and his Tiāntóng cousin **Mùchén Dàomǐn** [[道忞]] (cf. [[KR6q0604]] / [[KR6q0607]]). Both were cultivated by the Shùnzhì emperor, in the early-Qīng court's strategic Buddhist legitimization. The phonetic glosses (*yīnshì* 音釋) at the end of each abbacy section indicate careful textual treatment for canonical inclusion.
