# Gǔlín Rú chánshī yǔlù 古林如禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Gǔ-lín Rú*
by [[機如]] (說), [[全威]] (等記錄)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Gǔlín Jīrú** [[機如]] 古林機如, Línjì-Yángqí Hǔ-qiū-school dharma-heir of **Zhúān Zhēnyǎn** [[真衍]] 竹庵真衍 in the Jīnmíng-Jièlǎo line. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Quánwēi** [[全威]] *děng jìlù*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B346. Lifedates not preserved.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[機如]] note: dharma-heir of Zhēnyǎn at the **Sūzhōu Zhúān** seat, with own subsequent abbacy at the **Gǔlín** 古林 site (whence the title-form 古林如). 

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession; Jīrú compiled [[KR6q0490]] for Zhēnyǎn). *notAfter* = c. **1690** as a conservative bound.

**Lineage.** Hǔqiū → Wúzhǔn → Duànqiáo Lún → Pǔmíng-Yòng → Jīnmíng-Jièlǎo → Zhúān-Zhēnyǎn → Gǔlín-Jīrú (32nd-generation Línjì in the Hǔ-qiū-school sub-line).
