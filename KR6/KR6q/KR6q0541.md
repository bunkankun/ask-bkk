# Línwǒ chánshī yǔlù 林我禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lín-wǒ*
by [[鑑]] (說), [[海錱]] (等編錄), [[海茲]] (等編錄)

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Línwǒ Jiàn** [[鑑|Línwǒ Jiàn]] 林我鑑 (also **Hǎibǎo Jiàn** 海寶鑑) (**1612-05-08 – 1679-03-17**, age 68, *sēnglà* 48), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yìān Yìnshī** 易菴印師. Native of **Chuānběi Lángzhōu** 川北閬州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Hóu** 侯, displaced to **Qín** 秦 (Shǎnxī) in **崇禎十七年** = **1644** by the Sìchuān civil war and based in the **Guānzhōng / Níngxià / Gānsù** northwest for the rest of his life. Compiled jointly by *fǎsì* **Hǎizhèn** [[海錱]] (= 愚參海金 of Shífāng, *xītáng*) and **Xuělín Hǎicí** [[海茲]] (of Shuòfāng / Níngxià, *zhīcáng*) *děng biānlù*. Printed at **Jiāxīng Léngyánsì** 嘉興楞嚴寺 (流通) in **康熙辛未歲臘月吉旦** = **1692-01** (Kāngxī 30, lunar 12, 1st day).

The *yǔlù* documents three principal abbacies in succession (titled in juan 1–2): **Guānzhōng Jīngyáng Wéntǎ Tiěfó chánsì** 關中涇陽文塔鐵佛禪寺 (opening sermon **康熙乙巳 10/15** = **1665-11-22**), **Chángān Dàxìngshàn chánsì** 長安大興善禪寺 (entered **康熙六年** = **1667**), and **Qínzhōng Níngxià Hǎibǎotǎsì** 秦中寧夏海寶塔寺. *notBefore* = **1665** (opening sermon at Tiěfó); *notAfter* = **1692** (printing). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B422.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *Tiěfósì yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Xīngshànsì yǔlù*; *Hǎibǎotǎsì yǔlù*. Juan 3: *shìzhòng*, *rùshì*, *jīyuán*, *niānsòng*, *sònggǔ*, the *liánfāngjì* listing four *fǎsì*, and *tízàn*. Juan 4: *xíngshí*, **林我禪師塔銘** (preserving birth and death dates), and the colophon.

## Other points of interest

This is one of the most northwestern Chán *yǔlù* in the Jiāxīng Canon, documenting the rebuilding of Línjì Buddhism in **Guānzhōng** (Wèi-river plain) and **Níngxià** during the post-1644 reconstruction. Línwǒ's pre-tonsure life included an unusually long period of Tiāntái-school *Fǎhuá* lecture-circuit attendance (five years) before he turned to Chán under **Bǎochí** at Dàsuísì — characteristic of the doctrine-followed-by-Chán arc favored in 17th-century Sìchuān monastic biography. The *Tǎmíng* notes a *fǎhuì* of his teacher **Yìān Yìnshī** traveling personally to Chángān in **己酉春** = **1669** to deliver his *fùfǎ*, an act of long-range succession-formalization unusual in the Yángqí line.
