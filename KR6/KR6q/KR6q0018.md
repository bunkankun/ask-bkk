# Wǔdēng yántǒng mùlù 五燈嚴統目錄

*Table of Contents to the Strict Orthodoxy of the Five Lamps*

compiled by [[通容]] (Tōngróng, sobriquet Fèiyǐn 費隱, 1593–1661), two-juan table of contents to the *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 ([[KR6q0019]])

## About the work

The two-juan table of contents to Fèiyǐn Tōngróng's 25-juan *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 ([[KR6q0019]]), the controversial 1653 Línjì-orthodox revision of the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* 五燈會元 ([[KR6q0012]]). Separately catalogued in the Xuzangjing.

### Abstract

Finding-aid for the parent work. See [[KR6q0019]] for its content and the controversy it generated.
