# Tiānwáng Shuǐjiàn Hǎi héshàng wǔhuì lù 天王水鑑海和尚五會錄
*Five-Assembly Record of Venerable Shuǐ-jiàn Hǎi of Tiān-wáng[-sì]*
by [[慧海]] (說), [[原澂]] (等編)

## About the work

Six-juan **five-assembly** (*wǔhuì* 五會) *yǔlù* of **Shuǐjiàn Huìhǎi** [[慧海]] 水鑑慧海 (**1626–1687**, DILA A001716) — the **earlier-edition** version of his *yǔlù*, covering five of the eventual six abbacies. Companion-volume to [[KR6q0440]] 《天王水鑑海和尚六會錄》 (10 juan, 1690 integrated edition). See the full biographical context at [[慧海]] (second entry) and at [[KR6q0440]] Abstract. Compiled by dharma-heir [[原澂|Fǎlán Yuánchéng]] 原澂. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B231.

### Abstract

**Relationship to [[KR6q0440]].** The present **五會錄** 五會錄 is the **1672 partial-edition** Huìhǎi *yǔlù* covering five abbacies. The 1690 edition ([[KR6q0440]] 六會錄) added the **Hǎiyán Jīnsù Guǎnghuìsì** 金粟廣慧寺 abbacy (1676 entry) and/or the **Qīxián Chánsì** 棲賢禪寺 abbacy as a sixth-assembly supplement. The five abbacies covered here are: (1) **Tiěfósì** 鐵佛寺 (from 1658); (2) **Jīngzhōu Tiānwáng Chánsì** 荊州天王禪寺 — the primary restoration-abbacy of the Táng-era **Tiānwáng Dàowù** 天王道悟 ancestral seat; (3) **Huánglóngsì** 黃龍寺 (**Èzhōu** 鄂州 = modern Wǔhàn); (4) **Dìzàngsì** 地藏寺 (**Yízhēn** 儀真 = modern Yízhēng in Jiāngsū, a seat also referenced as Huìhǎi's *kāishān chánzǔ* 開山禪祖 seat per the Xú Xíng preface); (5) **Hànyáng Qīxiánsì** 漢陽棲賢寺 — the Hàn-yáng-based seat on the opposite bank from Huánglóng. This differs from the six-abbacy enumeration at [[KR6q0440]] — the precise mapping between the two editions' abbacy-lists deserves philological collation.

**Compositional history.** Cut with three front-prefaces:

1. **Xú Xíng** 徐行 (**檗菴氏** Bòānshì), dated **康熙壬子 10.15** = **Kāngxī 11 / 1672 lunar 10.15** = ~**4 December 1672** Western. Xú's preface provides the key chronology: Huìhǎi had been teaching for "*èrshí nián lái* 二十年來" (twenty years) prior to 1672 — placing his first-teaching at c. 1652–1658 (matches the [[KR6q0440]] "戊戌 1658 Tiěfó" starting-date). "*Lì Wú rù Chǔ wǔ kāi cóngxí, qí jiān yī chuàng Tiānwáng, liǎng xīng Huánglóng, Dìzàng* 歷吳入楚五開叢席其間一刱天王兩興黃龍地藏" — the preface's own abbacy-summary differs slightly from the juan-structure and includes two Huánglóng residences.
2. **Yè Nánshēng** 葉南生 (Jīngzhōu tuīguān) — the same 1659 preface preserved in [[KR6q0440]].
3. A third preface by an undated author (possibly at 鄂州 = Wǔhàn context).

*notBefore* = **1658** (Tiěfó first-teaching); *notAfter* = **1672** (Xú Xíng preface; the 1672 cutting precedes the 1676 Jīnsù abbacy and the 1690 integrated edition).

**Contents by juan.** Six juan of *shàngtáng* + ancillary material from the five abbacies enumerated above.

## Other points of interest

- **Dual 五會錄 / 六會錄 editions.** The preservation of both a 1672 five-assembly edition ([[KR6q0441]]) and a 1690 six-assembly integrated edition ([[KR6q0440]]) in sequential Jiāxīng numbering (B230–B231) parallels the dual-edition preservation pattern visible at [[KR6q0418]] / [[KR6q0419]] (Xuěguān Zhìyín 13-juan / 6-juan) and [[KR6q0434]] / [[KR6q0435]] (Fǎxǐyìn 12-juan / 2-juan Léngyán continuation). These multi-edition preservations reflect **incremental Chán-master-corpus cutting** as part of the ongoing Jiāxīng Canon project's mid-Kāng-xī phase (1660s–90s).
- **Xú Xíng preface date as biographical anchor.** The 1672-10-15 date and the "*èrshí nián lái* 二十年來" (twenty-year career) datum together fix **1652–58** as the earliest-activity window for Huìhǎi's public teaching, before the formal 1658 Tiěfósì opening. This is a useful pre-1658 pre-abbacy biographical-chronology detail not preserved in [[KR6q0440]].
