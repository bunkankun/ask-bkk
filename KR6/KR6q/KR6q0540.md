# Hèfēng chánshī yǔlù 鶴峰禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hè-fēng*
by [[濟悟]] (說), [[上震]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Hèfēng Jìwù** [[濟悟]] 鶴峰濟悟 (**1626-11-08 – 1687-10-16**, age 62, *jièlà* 44), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Língyǐn Jùdé Hónglǐ** 靈隱具德弘禮 (1600–1667). Native of **Wúmén** 吳門 (Sūzhōu), lay surname **Fèi** 費. Compiled by his sole *fǎsì* **Shàngzhèn** [[上震]] *děng biān*. The *yǔlù* documents his teaching across six successive lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Lóngtán Fúshàn** 龍潭福善 (the seat that gave him his sobriquet **Lóngtán Hèfēng**), **Gūsū Bǎoshù** 姑蘇寶樹, **Dānghú Zhúyǐn** 當湖竺隱, **Tóngxī Jìzhào** 桐溪寂照, **Púshuǐ Fúshàn** 濮水福善, and **Fúshòu chánlín** 福壽禪林 (Tóngxiāng).

Three prefaces are preserved: by his co-disciple **Ānshān tóngméndì Jìrì** 安山同門弟濟日 dated **康熙壬申皋月之望** = **1692-06-29** (Kāngxī 31, lunar 5/15); by 法弟 **Zhòng Hóngdào** 仲弘道 dated **康熙壬申歲次十月** = **1692-11**; and by his *Língyǐn tóngméndì* (co-disciple at Língyǐn) **Huìlù** 慧輅 dated **康熙歲次乙亥季夏** = **1695** summer. *notBefore* ≈ **1670** (post-Hóng-lǐ-death abbacies began ca. 1668–1670); *notAfter* = **1695** (latest preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B421.

**Contents.** Juan 1: three prefaces, *mùcì*, *shēngzuò* (= *shàngtáng*), *xiǎocān*, *shìzhòng*, *jīyuán*, *niānsòng*. Juan 2: *fóshì* (a substantial collection of cremation/sealing-coffin/funerary verses for fellow monks and lay donors), *xíngzhuàng*, and *tǎmíng* (stupa-inscription) — the principal sources for his life.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J38 B421).*

## Other points of interest

The *fóshì* section in juan 2 is unusually rich — providing a full panorama of mid-Kāngxī-era Sūzhōu / Tóng-xiāng-area LínjìYángqí Buddhist mortuary practice across both clerical and lay sponsorship contexts.
