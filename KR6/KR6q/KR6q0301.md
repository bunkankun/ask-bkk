# Xiàoyǐn Dàxīn chánshī yǔlù 笑隱大訢禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Xiàoyǐn Dàxīn* — a four-juan Yuán *yǔlù* of **Xiàoyǐn Dàxīn** 笑隱大訢 (1284–1344), the dominant Yuán-period Chán master of the imperial court (*shì jiào zōng zhǔ* 釋教宗主 "Head of Buddhist Schools") and preface-writer for multiple major Yuán Chán texts (including [[KR6q0179]] *Zhèng dào gē zhù* by [[永盛|Yǒngshèng]]). Compiled by four of his *ménrén* 門人 disciples: [[延俊|Yánjùn]], [[慧曇|Huìtán]], [[中孚|Zhōngfú]], [[崇裕|Chóngyù]].

Four-juan Yuán Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1367.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1310; *notAfter* c. 1350.
