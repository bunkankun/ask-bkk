# Lángyá Huìjué chánshī yǔlù (xù) 瑯琊慧覺禪師語錄（序）

*Preface to the Sayings-Record of Chán Master Lángyá Huìjué* — a one-juan preface-only text for the *yǔlù* of the Northern-Sòng master [[慧覺|Lángyá Huìjué]] 瑯琊慧覺, one of the major contemporaries of Shíshuāng Chǔyuán in the Línjì line. Written by his disciple (*cānxué* 參學) [[用孫|Yòngsūn]] 用孫. The main body of the *yǔlù* proper is not preserved in this catalog-entry; only the preface.

## About the work

A one-juan preface-only text preserving a Northern-Sòng *yǔlù*-preface, X69 n1339. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

[[慧覺|Lángyá Huìjué]] 瑯琊慧覺: Northern-Sòng Línjì-lineage master. Lifedates unrecorded; dharma-heir of [[善昭|Fényáng Shànzhāo]] 汾陽善昭 (947–1024). One of the second-generation Línjì masters active between Shànzhāo and Shíshuāng Chǔyuán.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1050 (Huìjué's mature teaching-years); *notAfter* c. 1100.
