# Guāntāo Qí chánshī yǔlù 觀濤奇禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Guān-tāo Qí*
by [[大奇]] (說), [[興舒]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Guāntāo Dàqí** [[大奇]] 觀濤大奇 (**1625 – 1678-08-26**), Cáo-dòng-school (Shòu-chāng-branch) dharma-heir of **Tiānjiè Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] (1593–1659). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Xīngshū** [[興舒]] *děng biān*. Two abbacies: **Chóngxiān Xiǎnxiàosì** at Hángzhōu Gāotíngshān; **Xīnchéng Shòuchāngsì** (the Cáodòng ancestral seat). Juan 6 includes the *shòuzàng míng bìng xù* 壽藏銘并序 giving lifedates. *notBefore* = c. 1655 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1678 (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B362.
