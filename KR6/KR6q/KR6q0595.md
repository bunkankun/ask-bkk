# Cuìyá Bì chánshī yǔlù 翠崖必禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cuì-yá Bì*
by [[必]] (說), [[道通]] (錄)

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Cuìyá Bì** [[必]] 翠崖必 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Biéān Xìngtǒng** 別庵性統 (1661–1717) of **Pǔtuóshān**. Recorded by his *fǎsì* **Dàotōng** [[道通]] *lù*. Documents teaching at four abbacies, two in Hángzhōu / Jiāngxī and two in **Beijing** (京都): **Hángzhōufǔ Rénhéxiàn Yǒngshòu chánsì** 杭州府仁和縣永壽禪寺; **Jiāngxī Gàngzhōufǔ Chángníngxiàn Shīlín chányuàn** 江西戇州府長寧縣獅林禪院; **Jīngdū Guānyīn chánsì** 京都觀音禪寺; **Jīngdū Jiāxīng chánsì** 京都嘉興禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1700** (post-Xìng-tǒng-late-period *fùfǎ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1730**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B487.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *mùcì*; abbacy *yǔlù* records (the four seats listed above). Juan 2: *xiǎocān*; *jīyuán*; *fóshì* (with *Jìngshān sǎo Huìzǔ tǎ* 徑山掃慧祖塔, *Cáoxī lǐ Liùzǔ zhēn* 曹溪禮六祖真 — pilgrimages to the founder-stupas, plus mortuary rituals for various lay donors). Juan 3: closing materials.

## Other points of interest

The four-abbacy circuit including two **Beijing** seats (*Jīngdū Guānyīn* and *Jīngdū Jiāxīng*) marks Cuìyá Bì as one of the most centrally-positioned **Pǔtuóshān → Beijing** axis Línjì-Yángqí monks of the early-18th-century Kāngxī Yōngzhèng transition. The pilgrimage *fóshì* to the **Liùzǔ** stupa at **Cáoxī** indicates a continuing Lìngnán Cáodòng / Hǎiyún engagement.
