# Biémù Chún chánshī yǔlù 別牧純禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bié-mù Chún*
by [[純]] (說), [[福月]] (等編), [[慧輅]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Biémù Chún** [[純]] 別牧純 (b. **1638-07-01**, alive at age 60 in **1697**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jìngtíng Dézōng** [[德宗]] (1621–1684, cf. [[KR6q0587]]) at **Nányuán Hóngshèngsì** 南源洪聖寺 in Húnán. Native of **Sānwú** 三吾 (Húnán), lay name **Lǐ Shànbǎo** 李善寶. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Fúyuè** [[福月]] *děng biān*. Preface by **Dìhuī Huìlù** [[慧輅]] (1627–1725, the very long-lived Líng-yǐn-affiliated lay scholar-monk).

Documents teaching at two abbacies: **Quánzhōu Nányuán Hóngshèngsì** 全州南源洪聖寺 (succeeded his teacher); **Sānwú Cáoshān Jíshèngsì** 三吾曹山集聖寺. *notBefore* = **康熙丁巳** = **1677** (*fùfǎ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1697** (last attested year). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B476.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *Nányuánsì yǔlù*; *Cáoshān Jíshèngsì yǔlù*; *fēnzuò bǐngfú* 分座秉拂; *jīyuán*; *fóshì*; *sònggǔ*; *xíngshí* (the principal source for his life — including the cat-cry breakthrough at Yíshān).

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0587]] + [[KR6q0588]] form a complete teacher-disciple sequence within the **Yíshān → Nányuán** Húnán Línjì-Yángqí line. The *Nányuánsì* foundation in 1662 and its joint construction by master and student is recorded both here and in [[德宗]]'s *yǔlù*.
