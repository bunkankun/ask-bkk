# Shànquán Wèi chánshī yǔlù 善權位禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shàn-quán Wèi*
by [[達位]] (說), [[大悅]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Shànquán Dáwèi** [[達位]] 善權達位 (**1619-10-29 – 1684-10-01**, age 67, *sēnglà* 45), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo** 月幢徹了 (1614–1667). Native of **Chǔběi** 楚北 (Húběi), lay surname **Qú** 瞿; took refuge in Guìzhōu during the dynastic chaos. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Tiānyī Dàyuè** [[大悅]] *děng biān*.

Documents teaching at three Guìzhōu region abbacies: **Tàipíng Wànshòusì** 太平萬壽寺; **Sōngkuīshān Pǔguāngsì** 松巋普光寺 (the principal seat — gives him his sobriquet **Sōngkuī Shànquán**); **Cíyún Pǔxiángsì** 慈雲普祥寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-付法 abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1684** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B469.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; abbacy *yǔlù* (Sōngkuī, Tàipíng, Cíyún); *xiǎocān*; *fǎyǔ*. Juan 2: *jīyuán*; *niāngǔ*; *sònggǔ*; closing **塔銘**, **行實**, **分燈** (lineage-distribution listing his ten *fǎsì* and five lay disciples).

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0581]]–[[KR6q0582]] form a teacher-disciple sequence within the major **Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo** 月幢徹了 line of Línjì-Yángqí Guìzhōu Buddhism — a separate Sìchuān-Guìzhōu sub-line from the Pò-shān-line clusters in J38–J39 (cf. [[KR6q0564]], [[KR6q0567]], etc.). Dáwèi's disciple [[如純]] is the compiler of the major **《黔南會燈錄》** [[KR6q0036]], the principal Guìzhōu Chán biographical anthology.
