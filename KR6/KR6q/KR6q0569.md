# Tōngtiān Yìsǒu Gāo chánshī yǔlù 通天逸叟高禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yì-sǒu Gāo of Tōng-tiān [-sì]*
by [[行高]] (說), [[明德]] (重梓), [[超原]] (行略)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Tōngtiān Yìsǒu Xínggāo** [[行高]] 通天逸叟行高 (**1616 – 1669-04-12**, age 54, *sēnglà* 40), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Wànrú Tōngwēi** 萬如通微 (1594–1657). Native of **Xīnghuà Xiānyóu** 興化仙遊 (Fújiàn), lay surname **Chén** 陳. *Zhòngzǐ* (re-engraving) by his dharma-grandson **Míngdé** [[明德]]; **行略** (life-narrative) authored by his sole *fǎsì* **Dànyá Chāoyuán** [[超原]] (1630–1698). Documents teaching at his sole abbacy **Púyáng Tōngtiān chánsì** 蒲陽通天禪寺 (Fújiàn). The *xínglüè* records the formal *fùfǎ* transmission to Chāoyuán on **康熙己酉年正月元日** = **1669-02-01**, just two months before Yìsǒu's death. *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-Wàn-rú-death abbacy); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (the *zhòngzǐ* postdates Chāoyuán's 1698 death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B455.

**Contents.** Single juan: *mùlù*; *fǎyǔ* and verses including *yǔ Dànyá Yuán hòutáng* 與澹崖原後堂 (to his disciple [[超原]]) and *yǔ Huásī Yuán xītáng* 與華斯圓西堂; *jīyuán*; the closing *xínglüè* by 超原.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0569]] and the immediately following [[KR6q0570]] form a teacher-disciple sequence — both compiled / re-engraved by the same *fǎsūn* **Míngdé** [[明德]] in the late-1690s / early-1700s — capturing two generations of the **Púyáng Tōngtiān chánsì** abbacy (Yìsǒu d. 1669; Chāoyuán d. 1698) within the **Wànrú** branch of LínjìYángqí. The compactness of Yìsǒu's single juan reflects his short post-Wàn-rú-death career (only ~12 years).
