# Jìguāng Huò chánshī yǔlù 寂光豁禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jì-guāng Huò*
by [[豁]] (說), [[發育]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Jìguāng Yìnhuò** [[豁]] 寂光印豁 (b. **1619-12-29**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666); transmission **1659**. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Xiàocāng Fāyù** [[發育]] *děng biān*. Three abbacies: **Lóngyìn Fózǐsì**, **Suìníng Guǎngdésì**, **Xīchōng Zīfúsì** (all Sìchuān). Juan 4 includes *xíngshí*. Twelve named heirs. *notBefore* = **1659** (transmission), *notAfter* = c. **1675** as a conservative bound. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B367.
