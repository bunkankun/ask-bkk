# Chánglíng Shǒuzhuó chánshī yǔlù 長靈守卓禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Chánglíng Shǒuzhuó* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional Chán *yǔlù* of [[守卓|Chánglíng Shǒuzhuó]] 長靈守卓 (1065–1123), the Línjì-Yángqí master at Chánglíng 長靈. Compiled by his dharma-heir [[介諶|Jièchén]] 介諶.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1347. Shǒuzhuó's dates: 1065–1123; dharma-heir of [[克文|Bǎofēng Kèwén]] 寶峰克文.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100; *notAfter* c. 1160 (compilation-horizon).
