# Dōushuài Bùlín Jiān chánshī yǔlù 兜率不磷堅禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bù-lín Jiān of Dōu-shuài*
by [[宗堅]] (說), [[妙聖]] (等記)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Bùlín Zōngjiān** [[宗堅]] 不磷宗堅, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yězhú Fúhuì** [[福慧]] in the Pòshān → Zìshuǐ Yuánliáo → Shānhuī Bì → Yězhú → Bùlín Sìchuān-Yúnnán sub-line. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Miàoshèng** [[妙聖]] *děng*. Front-preface by lay scholar **Qián Guāngxiù Shèngyuè** 錢光繡聖月 *zhéān yěsǒu* of **Yín** 鄞 (Níngbō), dated **康熙癸丑除夕前三日 ≈ 1674-02-01 Gregorian**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B285. Title-form references his **Lùnánzhōu Dōushuài Chányuàn** 路南州兜率禪院 (Yúnnán) abbacy. Contents: *xù*; *shàng-juan* *shàngtáng*; *zhōng-juan* *xiǎocān* / *jīyuán*; *xià-juan* *fǎyǔ* / *jì*.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[宗堅]] note and the Qián Guāngxiù preface: native of **Qíyáng** 蘄陽 (Húběi); ministry in Yúnnán's **Nánzhào** 南詔 region. Eventually succeeded **Yězhú Fúhuì** at the **Yúnnánfǔ Sōngshān** 雲南府嵩山 seat (*jì xí Sōngshān* 繼席嵩山) — the senior Yúnnán Pòshān-line abbacy.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1665** (conservative bound corresponding to the 1665 cutoff of Fúhuì's *qiánlù* [[KR6q0438]] and the start of Bùlín's documented preaching career as Fúhuì's heir). *notAfter* = **1674** (Qián Guāngxiù preface). The volume covers the **Lùnán Dōushuài** abbacy (the Yúnnán Lùnánzhōu seat from which the title derives) and post-Fú-huì succession at Sōngshān.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Zìshuǐ Yuánliáo → Shānhuī Bì 山暉璧 → Yězhú Fúhuì → Bùlín Zōngjiān. The Qián Guāngxiù preface emphasizes Bùlín's claim to the broader Sòng-Míng Línjì pedigree: *fù Yángqí ér zǔ Shíshuāng* 父楊岐而祖石霜 (his "father" is Yángqí, his "grandfather" Shíshuāng) — invoking the two seminal Línjì-school figures Yángqí Fānghuì 楊岐方會 (992–1049) and Shíshuāng Chǔyuán 石霜楚圓 (986–1039) at the foundation of the Yángqí branch.

**Wú Sān-guì-period frontier ministry.** Bùlín's documented Yúnnán ministry coincides exactly with the **Wú Sānguì** 吳三桂 *Píngxī Wáng* administration in Yúnnán (1660–73), making the volume a witness to the late-1660s and early-1670s Yúnnán Chán mission-network on the eve of the **1673 Three-Feudatories rebellion** outbreak. Qián's preface, dated just one day before the lunar new year of **甲寅 = 1674**, was composed in the immediate aftermath of the rebellion's outbreak (1673/11) — making it one of very few Yún-nán-related prefaces composed during the early phase of the war.

- **Three-Feudatories-era Yúnnán Chán.** This and [[KR6q0466]] together preserve some of the most detailed Chán *yǔlù* documentation of Yúnnán Chán ministry under the 1660s–70s Wú Sānguì regime, on either side of the 1673 rebellion outbreak.
