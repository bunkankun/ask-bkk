# Xùdēng cúngǎo 續燈存稿

*Preserved Drafts of the Continuing Lamp*

edited (編定) by [[通問]] (Tōngwèn); compiled (彙集) by [[施沛]] (Shī Pèi), late Míng

## About the work

A 12-juan late-Míng Chán lamp-record continuation, assembled by the lay editor Shī Pèi and given its final editorial form by the monk Tōngwèn. Title — "Preserved Drafts of the Continuing Lamp" — modestly signals its provisional character: it aims to save useful material rather than to produce a definitive continuation.

### Abstract

The work is one of several competing late-Míng continuations of the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* ([[KR6q0012]]) and *Xù chuándēng lù* ([[KR6q0016]]) traditions. The collaboration of a literatus (Shī Pèi) as principal compiler with a monastic editor (Tōngwèn) is characteristic of the late-Míng lay-monastic Chán ecosystem. Circulation remained limited; the work was preserved in the Jiāxīng canon.

## Translations and research

No English translation. No dedicated monograph.

## Other points of interest

The compilation's modest title and lay-led editorial structure contrasts instructively with the polemically-titled contemporary works of Fèiyǐn [[通容|Tōngróng]] ([[KR6q0019]]).
