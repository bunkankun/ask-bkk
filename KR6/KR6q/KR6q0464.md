# Tánxī Zǐzhōu Chuán chánshī yǔlù 壇溪梓舟船禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tán-xī Zǐ-zhōu Chuán*
by [[船]] (說), [[明法]] (等編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Zǐzhōu Chāochuán** [[船]] 梓舟超船 (b. **1619-04-29**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Èryǐn Bǎoān Mì** 二隱寶安謐 at **Jīngxī Bǎoānsì** 荊溪保安寺. Compiled by *ménrén* **Míngfǎ** [[明法]] *děng*. Front-preface by **Zhāng Lián / Tōngshēng** 武張聯法名通陞 (Hú-guǎng-area lay-Chán figure who served as a military-administrative official supervising grain-transport on the Hànshuǐ corridor in the early 1660s). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B281. Contents: juan 1–2 *shàngtáng* / *xiǎocān* / *fǎyǔ*; juan 3 *jīyuán* / *jì* / **行實** (autobiographical narrative).

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the juan-3 *xíngshí* and DILA: native of **Tóngchuānzhōu** 童川州 (= **Chéngdūfǔ Tóngchuān** 成都府潼川, Sìchuān); lay surname **Hé** 何. Tonsured **1640** under **Yìngchūn** 映春 at Báiliánān; full precepts **1642** at Rónghuáshān with three-year *xiébùzhìxí* sūtra-prostration practice. Late-Míng warfare disruptions and famine drove him to *Guānyīn* invocation. **1645** onward: nine-year pilgrimage circuit through *virtually every major Mì-yún-line successor*: Cuìyán → **Wànrú Tōngwēi** at Yíxīng → **Yùlín Tōngxiù** at Húzhōu → **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** at Jìngshān → **Mùyún Tōngmén** at Tiāntóng → **Jùdé Hónglǐ** at Língyǐn → **Mùchén Dàomín** at Tiāntóng → **Zìxián** at Sāntǎ. Final teacher **Èryǐn Mì** at **Huátíng Chuánzǐ Fǎrěnsì** 華亭船子法忍寺 (whence the dharma-name 船 derives, in resonance with the Táng-era *chuánzǐ* boatman-master); 4 years of *tòngbàng*; 3-year *yǐnguān* at Gǔxīnān. **庚子 = 1660** transmission at Bǎoānsì on the *chūguān* "破" reply.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1670** as a conservative bound. The Zhāng Lián preface mentions a 1666 (丙午) meeting at **Wǔ-Hàn** during which Chuán was already in active ministry. The preface itself is undated but post-1666.

**Lineage and abbacies.** Two abbacies in Húběi border-region: **Xiāngzhōu Lùménsì** 襄州鹿門寺 (the *zìshān* 紫山 abbacy referenced in 鹿門 catalog-form, 2 years post-1660s) and **Tánxī gǔsì** 檀溪古寺 (Hànshuǐ valley historic temple, restored by Chuán; whence the title-form 壇溪 / 檀溪 / 鹿門梓舟船). Èryǐn → Chuán is one Línjì sub-line of the **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** 天隱圓修 → **Tōngshòu** / **Èryǐn Mì** Qìngshān succession (parallel to [[KR6q0463]]'s Yúnwài Xíngzé via Sōngjì Tōngshòu, but a distinct branch).

**Cross-school synthesis.** Zhāng Lián's preface emphasizes Chuán's **doctrinal range** spanning both Línjì and Cáodòng currents: *wèi chéng Cáodòng zhī mài ér jiān shàn qí miánmì wú yúxiān zhī piān; xiàn jiē Línjì zhī chuán ér dú dé qí zhíjié wú lǔmǎng zhī shī* 未承曹洞之脈而兼擅其綿密無連纖之偏現接臨濟之傳而獨得其直捷無魯莽之失.

## Other points of interest

- **Comprehensive nine-year pilgrimage circuit.** The 1645–1654 pilgrimage record visits **eight of the major contemporary Línjì masters** (Wànrú, Yùlín, Fèiyǐn, Mùyún, Jùdé, Mùchén, Cuìyán, Zìxián, Èryǐn) before final commitment to Èryǐn — making this volume a particularly informative witness to the mid-seventeenth-century cross-Línjì-circuit pilgrim practice. Compare [[KR6q0458]]'s Rúxǐ Cáodòng pilgrimage and [[KR6q0462]]'s Bùhuì cross-school circuit for parallel cases.
- **Boat / Chuánzǐ resonance.** The single-character dharma-name 船 (catalog-form) deliberately echoes the Táng-era **Chuánzǐ Déchéng** 船子德誠 ("Boatman" Déchéng), with the master taking up the seat at **Huátíng Chuánzǐ Fǎrěnsì** 華亭船子法忍寺 — the historical site of Chuánzǐ Déchéng's hermitage.
