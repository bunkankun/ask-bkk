# Wénmù Niàn chánshī yǔlù 文穆念禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Wén-mù Niàn*
by [[念]] (說), [[真慧]] (等編)

Five-juan *yǔlù* of **Wénmù Niàn** [[念]] 文穆念 (**1633-07-28 – 1678-01-05**, *shìshòu* 45), Línjì-line dharma-heir of **Lǎnfàng Zhìdà** 嬾放智大 of the **Qíshān Rénruìsì** 歧山仁瑞寺 line — a Míng-loyalist cross-lineage tradition with origins in the post-1644 refusal-to-surrender community. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhēnhuì** [[真慧]] *děng biān*. Three abbacies: **Fǎchuáng Shuānglín**, **Dānxiá Xiānxiá**, **Fúniú Yúnyán**. Juan 5 *xíngshí* dated post-1678. *notBefore* = c. 1660 (post-1660 pilgrimage and abbacy entries), *notAfter* = 1678 (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B363.
