# Nánhǎi Bǎoxiànglín Huìgōng Xiòng chánshī yǔlù 南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huì-gōng Xiòng of Bǎo-xiàng-lín, Nán-hǎi*
by [[開詗]] (說), [[傳一]] (等錄)

## About the work

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Huìgōng Kāixiòng** [[開詗]] 慧弓開詗 (**1634** – **1676/77**), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Zàishēn Hóngzàn** [[弘贊]] 在犙弘贊 (1611–1685) at **Dǐnghúshān Qìngyúnsì** 鼎湖山慶雲寺 in Zhàoqìng (Guǎngdōng). Recorded by *fǎsì* **Chuányī** [[傳一]] *děng lù*. Title-form references the **Nánhǎi Bǎoxiànglín** 南海寶象林 abbacy in Guǎngdōng. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J35 B336.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[開詗]] note: native of **Shùndé** 順德 (Guǎngdōng), lay surname **Zhāng** 張, lay-name **Zhāng Míngdá** 張明達. Tonsured at **17** (= **1650**) under Zàishēn Hóngzàn at Dǐnghúshān. Subsequent lifelong association with the Dǐnghú / Yúnmén Cáodòng line in southern Guǎngdōng.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1676/77** (death age 43). Six-juan extent reflects his relatively brief but intensive 1660s–1670s ministry.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school. Wúyì Yuánlái 無異元來 (1575–1630) → Yīngbēn Hóngjué 應本宏覺 → Zàishēn Hóngzàn (1611–1685) → Huìgōng Kāixiòng — the Bóshān → Dǐnghú line.

**Other works.** [[KR6q0132]] 《溈山警策句釋記》 collaborative commentary with his teacher Hóngzàn. Also editorial-compiler of *Xuěguān chánshī yǔlù* (a different volume, 13 juan).

**Guǎng-dōng-region significance.** Together with [[KR6q0132]], this volume establishes Huìgōng as one of the most prolific Guǎng-dōng-region Cáodòng *chánshī* of his generation, anchoring the **Dǐnghú / Yúnmén** sub-line's southern preaching presence.
