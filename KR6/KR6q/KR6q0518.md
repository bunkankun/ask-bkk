# Huáyán Háichū Fó chánshī yǔlù 華巖還初佛禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hái-chū Fó of Huá-yán*
by [[佛]] (說), [[通量]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Háichū Guāngfó** [[佛]] (= [[光佛]]) 還初光佛 (b. **1631**, alive 1685 at age 55), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] (1628–1701) at **Chóngqìng Huáyán Chányuàn** 重慶華巖禪院. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Xīlín Tōngliàng** [[通量]] *děng biān*. Same Guāngfó who compiled his master's [[KR6q0488]] *yǔlù*. *notBefore* = c. **1685** (post-master-decease succession; master died 1701 — but Guāngfó was already abbot for some time before). Actually master Déyù died **1701** so Guāngfó's own ministry mainly post-dates that; conservative *notBefore* = 1685 / *notAfter* = c. 1705 covers the active period. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B393.
