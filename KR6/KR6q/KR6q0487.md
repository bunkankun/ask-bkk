# Fóyǔ Yù chánshī yǔlù 佛語御禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fó-yǔ Yù*
by [[徹御]] (說), [[法傑]] (等編), [[法秀]] (等編), [[聖可]] (序)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Fóyǔ Chèyù** [[徹御]] 佛語徹御 (d. **1674-01-21**, age 40), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huìjué Zhàoyī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) at **Yúzhōu Xiāngguósì** 渝州香國寺 (Chóngqìng). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Fǎjié** [[法傑]] + **Yìtáng Fǎxiù** [[法秀]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Shèngkě Déyù** [[聖可]] / [[德玉]] (1628–1701) — Chèyù's dharma-uncle in the Pòshān-line. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J35 B341.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[徹御]] note: native of **Jiāzhōu** 嘉州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Fàn** 范. Tonsured at age 10 under Zhàoyī. Awakening on a corpse-meditation incident. Sole abbacy at **Chóngqìng Xiāngguósì**. Died **康熙12 12/15** = **1674-01-21 Gregorian**, age 40.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1665** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1674** (death). Brief 40-year life and one-juan extent reflect the brevity of his ministry.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Huìjué Zhàoyī → Fóyǔ Chèyù → Yìtáng Fǎxiù [[法秀]]. The Pòshān-line through Zhàoyī produced multiple short-lived but documented *yǔlù* — Chèyù at age 40, Fǎxiù succession at Xiāngguósì.
