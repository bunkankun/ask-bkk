# Yuèjiàn chánshī yǔlù 月磵禪師語錄

Two-juan late-Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Yuèjiàn Wénmíng** [[文明]] 月磵文明 (lifedates unrecorded; active from Jǐngdìng 5 / 1264), Yángqí-branch Línjì master, dharma-heir of [[了慧]] Xīyán Liǎohuì (1198–1262). *Xuzangjing* X70 n1392. Preface by Juéān Mèngzhēn 覺菴夢真 (abbot of Píngjiāng Shuāngé) dated Zhìyuán *jiǎshēn* autumn (1284) — two decades after Wénmíng's first abbacy. Records four abbacies: Xìnzhōu Éhú Rénshòu 鵝湖仁壽 (entered 1264), Ráozhōu Tiānníng 天寧, Qīxián Miàoguǒ 棲賢妙果, and Ráozhōu Jiànfú 薦福 (both first and second tenures), with *gàoxiāng pǔshuō*, *sòng*, *tíbá*, *xiǎo fóshì*, *zàn*, *zìzàn*, and closing *bá*. Per DILA A013871, Wénmíng (*hào* Yuèjiàn 月磵 "Moon-Ravine") received his education at Huánglóngsì and taught as abbot through the four sites above. His dharma-heir is Dōngshān Chóng 東山崇.
