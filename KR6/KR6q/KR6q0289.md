# Wúmén Huìkāi chánshī yǔlù 無門慧開禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Wúmén Huìkāi* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **[[慧開|Wúmén Huìkāi]]** 無門慧開 (1183–1260), the celebrated compiler of the famous **Wúmén guān** 無門關 (Japanese *Mumonkan*, compiled 1228) — one of the most important Chinese Chán *gōng'àn* collections. The present text preserves Huìkāi's own *yǔlù* — his teaching sermons, encounter-dialogues, and related material. Compiled by nine of his attendant-disciples.

Two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1355. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1220 (Huìkāi's mature teaching career); *notAfter* 1260 (his death).
