# Liánfēng chánshī yǔlù 蓮峰禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lián-fēng*
by [[素]] (說), [[性深]] (等編)

Substantial ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Liánfēng Sù** [[素]] 蓮峰素 (b. **1612**, still alive in 1691 at age 80, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎichī Xíngyuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662) in the **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 → **Tiāntóng / Jīngshān** 天童徑山 lineage. Native of **Pútián** 莆田 (Fújiàn), lay surname Kē 柯; his entire abbatial career was conducted within **Xīnghuàfǔ** 興化府 (Fújiàn). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Ruòhǎi Xìngshēn** [[性深]] *děng biān*. The first preface, by his Línjì-lineage *fǎdì* (younger dharma-brother) **Chāopéi** 超裴, is dated **丙午臘月望日** = **1666-12-31** (Kāngxī 5, 12th lunar month full moon) at **Yǎngtái shānfáng** 養苔山房 — by which time Liánfēng had retired to **Yúnfēng** 雲峰. *notBefore* = **順治十年癸巳 2/28** = **1653-03-27** (entered Gūshānsì, opening date of the recorded sermons); *notAfter* = **1666** (preface, capturing the corpus shortly after retirement). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B410.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces, *Xīnghuà Gūshān chánsì yǔlù* 福建興化孤山禪寺語錄 and *Jiǔzuòshān Xīngguó chánsì yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Xīnghuà Bàoēn chánsì yǔlù*. Juan 3: *Lónghuá Wànshòu chánsì yǔlù*, *Lúshān Tōngtiān chánsì yǔlù*, *Xīmíng chánsì yǔlù*. Juan 4: *xiǎocān*. Juan 5: *shìzhòng*, *niāngǔ*, *sònggǔ*, *dàigǔ*. Juan 6: *rùshì*, *kānbiàn jīyuán*, *fǎyǔ*, *zàn*, *zìtí*. Juan 7: *xù*, *gē*, *zhuàn*, *xíngshí*, *qǐ*, *yǐn*, *cí*, *míng*, *fóshì*. Juan 8–10: *wén*, *jì*, and three juan of *záyǒng* (miscellaneous poems).

## Other points of interest

A regional Fújiàn *yǔlù* — six abbacies all within Xīnghuàfǔ. The *záyǒng* fills three juan (8–10), unusually substantial for a *yǔlù*; combined with the more conventional *yǔlù* sections in juan 1–7, this gives Liánfēng's collection an almost *bièjí* 別集 character. The "原書闕" 原書闕 ("original gap") notation between the two prefaces and the table of contents in juan 1 indicates a known lacuna in the printed exemplar.
