# Wǔ jiā yǔlù (xuǎn lù) 五家語錄（選錄）

*Selected Five-House Sayings-Records* — a two-juan compact-selection version of the expanded Míng-era *Wǔ jiā yǔlù* 五家語錄 tradition, originally assembled in compact form by the Táng-era **[[慧然|Huìrán]]** 慧然 and re-edited (*chóng dìng* 重訂) by the Míng layman [[郭凝之|Guō Níngzhī]] 郭凝之.

Two-juan Táng-with-Ming-recension Chán lineage-*yǔlù* collection, J23 B134.

Dating: *notBefore* 850 (Táng Huìrán's compilation); *notAfter* 1644 (end of Míng; Guō Níngzhī's re-edition).
