# Xù chuándēng lù mùlù 續傳燈錄目錄

*Table of Contents to the Continued Record of the Transmission of the Lamp*

anonymous three-juan table of contents to the *Xù chuándēng lù* 續傳燈錄 ([[KR6q0016]])

## About the work

The three-juan general table of contents to the 36-juan *Xù chuándēng lù* 續傳燈錄 ([[KR6q0016]]), the early-Míng continuation of the *Jǐngdé chuándēng lù* 景德傳燈錄 ([[KR6q0003]]). Transmitted separately through the Japanese monastic editions that became the basis of the Xuzangjing.

### Abstract

Finding-aid for the parent work, enumerating lineage positions and entry headings. See [[KR6q0016]] for the parent text's history.
