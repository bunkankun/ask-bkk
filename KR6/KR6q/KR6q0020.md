# Wǔdēng yántǒng jiěhuò biān 五燈嚴統解惑編

*Resolution of Doubts about the Strict Orthodoxy of the Five Lamps*

by [[通容]] (Tōngróng), self-defence of the *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 ([[KR6q0019]]), 1654

## About the work

A one-juan polemical pamphlet by [[通容]] Fèiyǐn Tōngróng defending the lineage re-attributions in his *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 ([[KR6q0019]]) against the Cáodòng-line counter-attacks that the *Yántǒng* had immediately provoked on its 1653 publication. The title — "Resolution of Doubts about the *Wǔdēng yántǒng*" — frames Tōngróng's intervention as clarification rather than retreat.

### Abstract

Tōngróng works through the disputed lineage attributions one by one, citing earlier sources to justify his decisions in the *Yántǒng*. The principal Cáodòng-side respondents ([[為霖]] Wèilín et al.) had argued that Tōngróng's reattributions were Línjì-side pseudo-genealogy; Tōngróng counters that he is merely correcting accreted Cáodòng errors. The polemic is unresolvable on its own terms — both sides could and did adduce earlier-source quotations supporting their position — but the *Jiěhuò biān* is essential reading alongside the *Yántǒng* to understand the structure of the controversy.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Treated together with the *Yántǒng* in [[吳疆]] Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (OUP, 2008).

## Other points of interest

The pair *Wǔdēng yántǒng* + *Wǔdēng yántǒng jiěhuò biān* together constitute the principal documentation of the mid-seventeenth-century Línjì-Cáodòng lineage dispute as it was conducted in print.
