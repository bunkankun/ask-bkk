# Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Jī chánshī yǔlù 慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Jī*
by [[慧機]] (說), [[幻敏]] (重編)

## About the work

Twenty-juan definitive *zhènglù* 正錄 of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668), second-generation head of the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派 and chief Sìchuān Línjì dharma-heir of **Jùyún Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]]. Stands in definitive-edition relation to the 3-juan selective 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 at [[KR6q0450]] (J29 B240, early 1642 recension): this B241 is the full *chóngbiān* 重編 expansion covering the master's complete 32-year preaching career across ten abbacies, prepared after Tiěbì's 1668 death by his dharma-heir **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] 竺峰幻敏 (1638–1707). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B241. Opens with the same two prefaces as B240: the Xióng Rǔxué 熊汝學 (**崇禎壬午秋九日** = **1642/9**) and Móu Dàoxíng 牟道行 *zhēbiǎo* prefaces. Closes with juan 20 containing (i) the 《慶忠機和尚年譜序》 of **Lǐ Dàojì** 李道濟 (西江弟子) dated **康熙甲寅孟春上浣 = 1674/1**; (ii) the 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》 compiled by the other chief dharma-heir **Wànfēng Zhìshàn** 萬峰至善 (*qián zhù Tánzhōu Wànfēng sìfǎ ménrén* 前住潭州萬峰嗣法門人); and (iii) the 《行狀》 formal biography.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1639** (Tiěbì's **Chóngzhēn 12** accession to the Jùyún abbacy on Guǎngzhēn's death). *notAfter* = **1674** (Lǐ Dàojì's *Kāngxī 13* preface to the year-chronology of juan 20, the latest datable signature in the corpus). The overall compilation was completed in 1674 or shortly after, incorporating material post-dating Tiěbì's **Kāngxī 7 戊申 9/21 = 1668/10/26** death.

**Structural relation to [[KR6q0450]].** B240 (3 juan, 1642) captured only the opening three years of Tiěbì's Jùyún tenure with Xióng Rǔxué and Móu Dàoxíng as contemporary lay-disciple prefaces. B241 (20 juan) retains those two prefaces verbatim but expands the interior corpus to include (a) the later seven abbacies at **Dìzàngyuàn**, **Yínwēng**, **Yúnlái**, **Qìngzhōngsì**, **Yúnyán**, **Bǎoshèngsì**, and the final **Zhìpíng / Lóngchāng Chányuàn** 治平 / 龍昌 on the Bái Jūyì willow-site; (b) the 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》; (c) the 行狀.

**Year-chronology.** The 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》 by Wànfēng Zhìshàn is the single most detailed biographical witness to Tiěbì: it begins at **Wànlì 31 癸卯 10/20 寅時** = **1603/11/22** with the master's birth at **Yíngshān** 營山 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Luó** 羅 of a multi-*jìnshì* scholar-gentry household, and ends with the Kāngxī 7 death at Zhìpíng. Lǐ Dàojì's preface compares Zhìshàn's year-editing to Ānanda's sūtra-compilation: *tóngshī zhī biānnián, qí yóu Ānán zhī jiéjí yě zāi* 童師之編年其猶阿難之結集也哉.

**Lineage and heirs.** Twenty-three named dharma-heirs per the 《續燈正統》 j. 16 roster, of whom two figure prominently in the B241 apparatus: **Sānshān Dēnglái** [[燈來]] (whose *fùfǎ* certificate Tiěbì composed, but who does not compile this B241 — his parallel work is at [[KR6q0448]] / [[KR6q0449]] / [[KR6q0450]] and his own *yǔlù* at [[KR6q0454]]) and **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] (this volume's *chóngbiān* compiler, with his own *yǔlù* at J40 B483 outside KR).

## Other points of interest

- **Different compiler from B240.** Where B240 was edited by the dharma-grandfather-line heir Dēnglái in 1642 (alongside the Guǎngzhēn volumes), B241 is edited by the other chief heir Huànmǐn in 1674 (after Tiěbì's death). The *chóngbiān* 重編 designation in the catalog meta reflects Huànmǐn's comprehensive re-editing to include the *niánpǔ* and *xíngzhuàng* apparatus absent from B240.
- **Scholar-gentry lineage.** The Luó family of Yíngshān produced multiple *jìnshì* across generations — noted in the *niánpǔ* opening entry as *jiāshì yǐ yījīng chuán duō kējiǎ* 家世以一經傳多科甲 — making Tiěbì one of the few Sichuan Chán masters of the period from a continuous scholar-official pedigree.
