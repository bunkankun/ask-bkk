# Jiātài pǔdēng lù zǒngmùlù 嘉泰普燈錄總目錄

*General Table of Contents for the Jiātài-Era Universal Record of the Lamp*

compiled by [[正受]] (Zhèngshòu, fl. 1204), separately catalogued general table of contents to the *Jiātài pǔdēng lù* ([[KR6q0010]])

## About the work

The three-juan general table of contents (*zǒngmùlù* 總目錄) to the 30-juan *Jiātài pǔdēng lù* 嘉泰普燈錄 ([[KR6q0010]]), the fifth of the canonical Sòng Chán lamp records. Separately transmitted through the Japanese monastic editions that became the basis of the Xuzangjing, where it is catalogued at X79 n. 1558 distinct from the main text at X79 n. 1559.

### Abstract

A finding-aid for the parent work, enumerating the lineage positions, entry headings, and sub-heading categories. As with the other Sòng lamp-record *mùlù*, the independent transmission reflects Japanese editorial practice rather than distinct authorship or compilation.
