# Běijiàn Jūjiǎn chánshī yǔlù 北磵居簡禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Běijiàn Jūjiǎn* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Běijiàn Jūjiǎn** 北磵居簡 (1164–1246), Línjì-Yángqí master and notable Chán literary-cultural figure of Southern Sòng. Compiled by his disciple (*cānxué* 參學) [[大觀|Dàguān]] 大觀.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1365.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1180; *notAfter* c. 1250 (Jūjiǎn died in 1246).
