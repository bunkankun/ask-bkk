# Hānyú Xiān chánshī yǔlù 憨予暹禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hān-yú Xiān*
by [[洪暹]] (說), [[法雲]] (等編)

## About the work

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Hānyú Hóngxiān** [[洪暹]] 憨予洪暹 (= **憨予暹**, the catalog form), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zìxián Xíngjué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (1610–1662). Compiled by *ménrén* **Fǎyún** [[法雲]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Hú Shìān** 胡世安 (1593–1663) — *Tàizǐ tàibǎo bīngbù shàngshū Wǔyīngdiàn dàxuéshì Xīshǔ* (Crown-Prince Tutor, Minister of War, Wǔyīngdiàn Grand Secretary, of Western Sìchuān) — dated **順治己亥仲秋望日 = 1659/9/15 lunar = 1659-10-26 Gregorian**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B288.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[洪暹]] note: dharma-heir of Zìxián Xíngjué of the Mìyún → Línyě line; lead compiler of his master's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0469]]. Hóngxiān's own seat was at **Fényáng** 汾陽 (Shǎnxī / Shānxī) — significant for being the historic seat of **Fényáng Shànzhāo** 汾陽善昭 (947–1024), the founder of *gōngàn* literature. The Hú Shìān preface explicitly invokes this Fényáng pedigree: *Hānyú héshàng zhuōxí Fényáng, jiànchuí shīkū, chàngtí zhíqiē, jiē rìyuè dēng yú zhōngtiān, yìnzhèng dòngxuán* 憨予和尚卓錫汾陽鍵椎獅窟唱提直切揭日月燈於中天印證洞玄.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (conservative bound for Hóngxiān's first abbacy, the seat predating the 1659 publication). *notAfter* = **1659** (Hú Shìān preface). Hóngxiān's master Xíngjué was still alive at this point (until 1662), making this a *yǔlù* published while the master was still active — uncommon and signaling an exceptional lay-patronage commitment to printing the disciple's work.

**Hú Shìān as preface-writer.** Hú Shìān (1593–1663), the early-Qīng Wǔyīngdiàn grand secretary and a senior survivor of Míng-Qīng transition officialdom, was a Western-Sì-chuān native (西蜀). His preface here, dated 1659/9, falls in his late-life period (he died 1663). The Hú-Hóngxiān connection likely derives from shared Sìchuān provenance: Hóngxiān's master Xíngjué was a Sìchuān 合州 native, and Hú himself was 西蜀.

**Lineage.** Mìyún Yuánwù → Línyě Tōngqí → Zìxián Xíngjué → Hānyú Hóngxiān — a one-to-one Sì-chuān-line transmission within the broader Mìyún 32nd-generation Línjì.

## Other points of interest

- **Pre-master publication.** The 1659 publication date precedes the master Xíngjué's 1662 death — making this an unusual case of a disciple's *yǔlù* printed while the master was still active in ministry. Suggests strong lay-patronage initiative (likely Hú Shìān's own circle in West Sìchuān).
- **Fén-yáng-line claim.** The Fényáng abbacy positions Hóngxiān's preaching in deliberate continuity with the **Fényáng Shànzhāo** Sòng-era Línjì foundation — a programmatic claim by a Sì-chuān-bred master to the Héběi / Shǎnxī Línjì heartland traditional identity.
