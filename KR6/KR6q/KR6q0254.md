# Wànsōng lǎorén píngchàng Tiāntóng Jué héshàng niāngǔ qǐngyì lù 萬松老人評唱天童覺和尚拈古請益錄

*Old-Man Wànsōng's Evaluation-Singing on Master Tiāntóng Jué's Niāngǔ: Record of Requesting-Benefit* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng / Jīn / Yuán Cáodòng collaborative commentary, companion to the more famous *Cóngróng lù* [[KR6q0114]]. Where the *Cóngróng lù* has [[行秀|Wànsōng Xíngxiù]] 萬松行秀 (1166–1246) commenting on [[正覺|Hóngzhì Zhèngjué]]'s 宏智正覺 (1091–1157) *sònggǔ* (verse-commentary), this parallel text has the same Wànsōng commenting on Hóngzhì's *niāngǔ* (prose-commentary) — representing Wànsōng's companion-treatment of Hóngzhì's other major commentarial-corpus.

## About the work

A two-juan Cáodòng *niāngǔ* / *píngchàng* collaborative commentary, X67 n1307. Non-commentary on a single parent Buddhist text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text gathers cases where Hóngzhì's original *niāngǔ* prose-commentaries were given in response to disciple-questions (*qǐngyì* 請益 "requesting beneficial teaching") — whence the title's *Qǐngyì lù* 請益錄 ("Record of Requesting Beneficial [Teaching]"). Wànsōng's Yuán-era *píngchàng* adds running-commentary in the same style as his famous *Cóngróng lù*.
