# Jìngtíng Zōng chánshī yǔlù 逕庭宗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jìng-tíng Zōng*
by [[德宗]] (說), [[行謙]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Jìngtíng Dézōng** [[德宗]] 逕庭德宗 (**1621-09-19 – 1684-10-15**, age 64, *fǎlà* 35), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yíshān Wòzhōng Zhìfú** 嶷山握中智符 (1625–1688). Native of **Línglíng** 零陵 (Húnán), lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Wúzhèng Xíngqiān** [[行謙]] *děng biān*. *notBefore* = **1657-12-30** (*fùzhǔ* on Buddha's enlightenment day); *notAfter* = **1684** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B475.

Documents an extraordinary fourteen+ Húnán abbacies — a remarkable circuit-record. Principal seats: **Qīngxiāng Nányuán Hóngshèngsì** 清湘南源洪聖寺; **Yíshān Zhùshèngsì** 嶷山祝聖寺; **Cáoyuán Bǎojìngsì** 曹源寶鏡寺; **Wòyúnshān Zuìshèngsì** 臥雲山最勝寺. Plus smaller hermitages and chapels (Shòufóān, Xīnglóngān, Biéchénān, Bìluóān, Pílúān, Liánchíshān).

**Contents.** Juan 1: *yuánqǐ*; preface; *mùcì*; abbacy *yǔlù* (in chronological order across the Húnán circuit). Juan 2: *xiǎocān* and continuing abbacy materials; closing *xíngzhuàng* and **塔銘** (preserving birth and death dates).

## Other points of interest

The fourteen-plus abbacies in Húnán within a single career mark an exceptionally fluid pattern of monastic mobility, characteristic of mid-Qīng Húnán Línjì where monks often occupied multiple smaller seats simultaneously rather than committing to single life-long abbacies.
