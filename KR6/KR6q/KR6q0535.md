# Dàbēi Miàoyún chánshī yǔlù 大悲妙雲禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Miào-yún of Dà-bēi [-sì]*
by [[真雄]] (說), [[機德]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Miàoyún Zhēnxióng** [[真雄]] 妙雲真雄 (b. **1634-10-28**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jièān Wùjìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673), thus a co-disciple of [[圓法]] (cf. [[KR6q0532]]), [[真璨]] ([[KR6q0533]]), and [[真在]] ([[KR6q0534]]). Native of **Jīnhuá Wǔyì** 金華武義, lay surname **Xú** 徐, original monastic name **Shòufēng** 壽峰. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Tiānwéi Jīdé** [[機德]] *děng biān*. The *yǔlù* documents his teaching at three abbacies: **Shāndōng Línqīngzhōu Dàbēi chánsì** 山東臨清州大悲禪寺, then on his return south the prestigious **Zhèjiāng Jiāxīng Sāntǎ Jǐngdé chánsì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺 (the same seat formerly held by his elder co-disciple [[圓法]] until 1669), with the **Hǎihuì chányuàn** 海會禪院 also within the Sāntǎfǔ complex.

The *yǔlù* opens with three *qǐngqǐ* (invitation petitions): from the gentry of Línqīngzhōu (Shāndōng) and Jiāxīngfǔ, plus a third from the **Jǐngdé fǎzhí** 景德法姪 (younger dharma-nephew at Sāntǎsì); a *zhuàn* (biography) follows immediately. *notBefore* = **1672** (壬子, *fùfǎ* and the implied beginning of his preaching career — supported by the *xínglüè* note "師自壬子離金明"); *notAfter* = **1713** (printing date in the colophon: **康熙五十二年歲次癸巳九月吉旦**). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B415.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces, three *qǐng-qǐ*, *Dà-bēi Miào-yún chán-shī zhuàn*, *Lín-qīng-zhōu Dà-bēi yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Sān-tǎ Jǐng-dé Hǎi-huì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *niān-gǔ*. Juan 4: *sòng-gǔ*, *fǎ-yǔ* (示天惟德記錄, 示天嶧脩侍者, etc.), *xíng-lüè* (autobiographical narration). Juan 5: *jī-yuán*, *xiàng-zàn* (image-encomia for Avalokiteśvara, Bodhidharma, his teacher Jiè-ān, his elder co-disciple **Ān-huà Shì-gāo** 安化世高 [[本則]], and the lay patron **Mù-gōng Xiàng** 牧公項), and the *lián-fāng-jì* listing all twenty of his dharma-heirs. Juan 6: closing *jì*, *fó-shì*, and printing colophon.

## Other points of interest

The Sāntǎ Jǐngdé succession is significant: when **Zhǔfēng Yuánfǎ** [[圓法]] died in 1669 leaving only one *fǎsì* (the distant **Huáiān Xīnzōng Chéngrán**), the seat passed laterally to a younger dharma-nephew within the broader **Jīnmíng Jièān** 金明介菴 line — Miàoyún Zhēnxióng — rather than to one of Zhǔfēng's own disciples. This kind of intra-lineage abbatial sharing is a characteristic feature of the Jiā-xīng-area Línjì-Yángqí federation in the Kāngxī era. The 1713 print date — forty years after his teacher's 1673 death and four decades after his own *fùfǎ* — also suggests that Zhēnxióng lived into the 1690s and that the corpus accumulated steadily before being engraved by his disciples after his death.
