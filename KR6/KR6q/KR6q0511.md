# Yuǎnān Tǎn chánshī yǔlù 遠菴僼禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yuǎn-ān Tǎn*
by [[本僼]] (說), [[元視]] (等編)

Sixteen-juan *yǔlù* of **Yuǎnān Běntǎn** [[本僼]] 遠菴本僼 (**1622 – 1682-05/06**), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Mùchén Dàomín** [[道忞]] (1596–1674). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Yuánshì** [[元視]] *děng biān*. Five abbacies: **長沙神鼎** → **天童** (succession) → **瑞巖** (Níngbō, the title-form) → **平陽** → **白雲**. Same Yuǎnān héshàng who organized publication of [[KR6q0481]] 《雲叟住禪師語錄》 at Ruìyánsì for his dharma-brother Yúnsǒu Běnzhù. Heir: [[元揆]] Yuánkuí (cf. [[KR6q0513]]). *notBefore* = c. 1660 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1682 (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B386.
