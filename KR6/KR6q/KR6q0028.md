# Xùdēng zhèngtǒng 續燈正統

*Orthodox Succession Continuing the Lamp*

compiled by [[性統]] (Xìngtǒng), preface dated 1691 (Qīng *Kāngxī* 30)

## About the work

A 42-juan early-Qīng Chán lamp-record continuation, compiled by the Cáodòng-line monk Bié'ān Xìngtǒng 別庵性統 as a continuation of the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* ([[KR6q0012]]) with coverage of the Yuán, Míng, and early Qīng generations. The title — "Orthodox Succession Continuing the Lamp" — announces its claim to authoritative genealogical succession.

### Abstract

Xìngtǒng's compilation represents the Cáodòng response to Fèiyǐn [[通容|Tōngróng]]'s earlier *Wǔdēng yántǒng* ([[KR6q0019]]), restoring the Cáodòng-line attributions that Tōngróng had re-routed to Línjì ancestors. The work is accordingly one of the principal documents in the mid-Qīng phase of the Línjì-Cáodòng lineage controversy, alongside its Línjì rival [[超永|Chāoyǒng]]'s *Wǔdēng quánshū* ([[KR6q0022]]) of 1697. The two works — appearing within six years of each other — represent the two lineages' competing closings of the lamp-record tradition.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Treated in Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (OUP, 2008), as the principal Cáodòng-side compilation in the mid-Qīng lineage dispute.

## Other points of interest

The very close temporal proximity (1691 and 1697) of the competing Cáodòng-side *Xùdēng zhèngtǒng* and Línjì-side *Wǔdēng quánshū* makes the pair useful for understanding late-imperial Chán lineage politics in parallel.
