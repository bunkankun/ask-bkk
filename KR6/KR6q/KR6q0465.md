# Xūzhōu Shěng chánshī yǔlù 虛舟省禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xū-zhōu Shěng*
by [[行省]] (說), [[超直]] (等編)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Xūzhōu Xíngshěng** [[行省]] 虛舟行省 (**1600-02-06 – 1668-06-14**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Wènshí Chāozhí** [[超直]]. Two front-prefaces: (i) by **Yán Dàcān** [[嚴大參]] 𨍏轢大參 (= **Yán Diàoyù** 嚴調御, the prominent Jiāxīng lay-Chán figure and editor of [[KR6q0202]] etc.), dated **戊戌夏 = 1658 summer**, framing Xūzhōu as a *fǎxiōng* 法兄 (dharma-brother) sharing the same Fèiyǐn 同門 affiliation as Yán himself; (ii) posthumous preface by **Wǔlín fǎzhí** 武林法侄 **Zhāng Wénjiā** 張文嘉 (*Zhòngjiā fǔ* 仲嘉甫), undated but post-1668 (*shī jì shìjì* 師既示寂). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B282; original cutting at the **Hézhōng Léngyánsì** 禾中楞嚴 in Jiāxīng. Contents: juan 1 *shàngtáng* / *xiǎocān*; juan 2 *shìzhòng* / *shànggòng* / *jīyuán* / *jì* / *zàn*; juan 3 *niāngǔ* / *sònggǔ*; juan 4 *xíngzhuàng* + *tǎmíng* + appendices including 《筏喻初學》 and the separately-printed 《八識規矩頌註》 ([[KR6q0215]]).

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[行省]] note: lay native of **Cíxī** 慈谿, surname **Yáo** 姚, born 1600. Lay-Chán pilgrim under multiple Línjì masters (Yúnmén, Píngtáo, Yǔfēng, Lóngmén, Lǐān) and finally **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Jīnsù; received the lay-name *Tōngshěng* from Mìyún. **1644** (age 46) tonsured at **Xuědòu** in the Míng-Qīng warfare-disruption; transferred to **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** and renamed **Xíngshěng** 行省 (the standard *xíng*-generation form for Fèiyǐn heirs, paralleling [[KR6q0463]]'s **Xíngzé**). Transmission **1654** at *Yúyún Chāoguǒ*; abbacies 1655–59 at Xiǎnjìng, Hùshēng, Liúxī Chányuàn. The Yán Dàcān preface dates the corpus as already compiled by **1658** at the Hǒushān seat.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1654** (transmission and beginning of public preaching). *notAfter* = **1670** as a conservative bound — the volume was complete by **1658** per the Yán preface, with the Zhāng Wénjiā post-1668 preface added at the time of the Léngyánsì publication after Xūzhōu's death.

**Heirs.** Six *fǎsì* (Méiān, Wènshí [this volume's compiler], Kēxuě, Xuědìng) plus two ordained nuns (Mìqǐ, Juéwù) and two principal lay-heirs (Hézhuó Xuě Chāoxìn, Shěn Shàngxiān). Wènshí Chāozhí's reputation among the heirs centered on his fund-raising for the Léngyánsì publication: *jiélì mùzǐ, cángbǎn yú Hézhōng zhī Léngyán* 竭力募梓藏板於禾中之楞嚴.

**Doctrinal interest.** Xūzhōu's distinctive Yogācāra-Chán synthesis is preserved in the appended 《八識規矩頌註》 ([[KR6q0215]]) and referenced in the second preface as *biékè bāshí guījǔsòng zhù* 別刻八識規矩頌註. Few early-Qīng Chán masters produced Yogācāra commentaries at this scholastic depth.

## Other points of interest

- **Late-life monastic conversion.** Xūzhōu's transition from lay-Chán to ordained monastic at **age 46** (1644) — driven directly by the Míng-Qīng warfare-disruption and family-loss — preserves a documented life-transition triggered by the dynastic transition. Compare [[KR6q0464]] 's Chuán (also late-Míng-warfare-driven Chán entry) for parallel cases.
- **Yogācāra commentary.** The accompanying *Bāshí guījǔsòng zhù* (separately preserved at [[KR6q0215]]) makes Xūzhōu one of the few early-Qīng Chán masters to produce a substantial commentary on Xuánzàng's *Bāshí guījǔsòng* — a doctrinal scholastic engagement unusual for the Línjì preaching tradition.
