# Dàchuān Pǔjì chánshī yǔlù 大川普濟禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Dàchuān Pǔjì* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Dàchuān Pǔjì** 大川普濟 (1179–1253), the Línjì-Yángqí master who is better known as the compiler of the major *Wǔ dēng huì yuán* 五燈會元 (*Compendium of the Five Lamp Histories*, 1252) — one of the most important lamp-records of Southern-Sòng Chán. Compiled by his disciple (*mén rén* 門人) [[元愷|Yuánkǎi]] 元愷.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1369.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1200; *notAfter* 1260 (Pǔjì died 1253).
