# Yúnshān Yànjū Shēn chánshī yǔlù 雲山燕居申禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yàn-jū Shēn of Yún[-tiān]-shān*
by [[德申]] (說), [[合哲]] (等編)

Eight-juan *yǔlù* of **Yànjū Déshēn** [[德申]] 燕居德申 (**1610 – 1678-09-30**, age 69, *sēnglà* 50), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Native of **Xī Shǔ Zhōngnán** 西蜀忠南 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Hézhé** [[合哲]] *děng biān*.

Documents teaching at seven abbacies, principally **Guìzhōu Wēiqīng Máopōshān Yúntiān chánsì** 貴州威青茅坡山雲天禪寺 (the principal seat — Guìzhōu, gives him his sobriquet **Yúnshān**). Other seats include **Liúzhōng** 流中, **Wànxiàng** 萬項, **Dàxīng** 大興, **Tàipíng** 太平, **Guānyīn** 觀音, **Shòujiè** 受戒. *notBefore* ≈ **1645** (post-1643 *fùfǎ* + abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1678** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B478.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *shàngtáng* (Yúntiān seat). Juan 2–3: *xiǎocān*; *wǎncān*; *qǐngyì fǎyào* (lay-disciple Q&A — addressing **Jièfū Guō** 介夫郭居士, **Yúyě Wáng Hóngdì** 于野王居士, **Jùyún Shěn Jīng** 聚雲沈居士, **Jìmíng Lǐ** 繼明李居士, **Wánbì Liú Zhīyù** 完璧劉居士, **Sùmíng Qìngzhǐ** 素明座主, **Sùméi Chén Línfú** 素梅陳居士). Juan 3: *jīyuán*; *xíngshí*. Juan 4: *yuánliú zhuósòng* — verses on the lineage stream, originally composed in response to **Pòshān** 's *chuíwèn* during his apprenticeship and re-extracted by his teacher's command. Juan 5–8: continuing materials including extensive *sònggǔ* and *fǎyǔ* — the *Shīzǐlín xiǎocān* 獅子林小參 indicates a teaching residency at one of the famous Shīzǐlín gardens.

## Other points of interest

The 43 *fǎsì* — among the largest disciple-cohorts in the entire J40 sequence — places Déshēn alongside [[如相]] 敏樹 (24 *fǎsì*) and [[淨瑩]] 元潔 (40 *fǎsì*) as one of the major 32nd-generation Línjì-Yángqí lineage-builders. The *qǐngyì* sub-section in juan 2–3 is unusually detailed in named lay-recipient form, providing a substantial register of Sìchuān / Guìzhōu lay-Buddhist gentry contacts.
