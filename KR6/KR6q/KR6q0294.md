# Xiātáng Huìyuǎn chánshī guǎng lù 瞎堂慧遠禪師廣錄

*Extended Record of Chán Master Xiātáng Huìyuǎn* — a four-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Xiātáng Huìyuǎn** 瞎堂慧遠 (1103–1176), Línjì-Yángqí Hǔ-qiū-line master; dharma-heir of Yuánwù Kèqín. Compiled by four *cānxué* disciples: [[齊己|Qíjǐ]], [[如本|Rúběn]], [[祖淳|Zǔchún]], [[法慧|Fǎhuì]].

Four-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *guǎng lù*, X69 n1360.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1150; *notAfter* c. 1180.
