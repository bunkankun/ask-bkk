# Yùméi Liàng chánshī yǔlù 玉眉亮禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yù-méi Liàng*
by [[亮]] (說), [[空謐]] (編), [[張廷勱]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Yùméi Dēngliàng** [[亮]] 玉眉燈亮 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir primarily of **Ěrān Dēngsōng** 耳菴燈嵩, with secondary transmission also from **Gāofēng Sānshān Dēnglái** 三山燈來 [[燈來]] (1614–1685). Native of **Zhōngzhōu** 忠州 (Sìchuān). Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Kōngmì** [[空謐]] *biān*. Preface by lay disciple **Zhāng Tíngmài** [[張廷勱]] dated **康熙庚午秋九月上浣** = **1690** lunar 9.

Documents teaching at two abbacies: **Zhōngzhōu Wǔfēngshān Tiānníngsì** 忠州五峰山天寧寺 (entered **康熙八年九月十八日** = **1669-10-23**) and **Wànsōng Tiāntái** 萬松天台. *notBefore* = **1669** (Tiānníng entry); *notAfter* = **1690** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B443.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *fóshì* (rich set of mortuary verses for his teacher Sānshān, his teacher's stupa-cleaning, image-rituals, and others — including a lament for his "uncle" *Fǎnsù* 返素師伯 on hearing of his death; cremation verses for **Hèlín dàdé** 鶴林大德, **Yuánchū shuòdé** 圓初碩德, **Zhǎngān Zhànxiū qísù** 長安湛休耆宿, **Jiànxuán shuòtóu** 見玄碩頭); *jìzàn* (prescribing his disciple **Bīngxián shǒuzuò** 冰絃首座 + image-encomium for Bodhidharma); *sònggǔ* with *zhuóniān* (verses with marginal commentary on classic *gōngàn*); *shī* (poems including *Shàng ēnshī* 上恩師 — to his teacher).

## Other points of interest

This compact one-juan *yǔlù* is significant within the J39 sequence as one of the **Sānshān Dēnglái cohort** *yǔlù* (alongside [[KR6q0556]], [[KR6q0557]], etc.) — Yùméi Liàng was the *wéinuó* whom Dēnglái deputized on his deathbed, alongside specific oral-transmission instructions, and the Bīngxián line continues from there. The strikingly extensive *fóshì* section (occupying nearly half the juan) reflects Yùméi's institutional role as Dēnglái's mortuary-ritual coordinator within the larger Sìchuān cohort following the master's death in 1685.
