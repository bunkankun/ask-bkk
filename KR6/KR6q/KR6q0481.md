# Yúnsǒu Zhù chánshī yǔlù 雲叟住禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yún-sǒu Zhù*
by [[住]] (說), [[元一]] (記錄)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Yúnsǒu Běnzhù** [[住]] 雲叟本住 (1620 – **1676-09-17**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mùchén Shānwēng Dàomín** [[道忞]] (1596–1674) at **Tánzhōu Lóngyásì** 潭州龍牙寺. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Yuányī** [[元一]]. Front-preface by Běnzhù's dharma-brother **Ruì** 睿 of **Wòlóng** 臥龍, written at the request of **Yuǎnān héshàng** 遠菴和尚 of **Míngzhōu Ruìyán** 明州瑞巖. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B310.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[住]] note: native of **Ānlù** 安陸 (Húběi). Full precepts at **Dàwéishān** 大溈山, formal transmission from **Mùchén Dàomín** at **Qīngzhōu** 青州. Sole abbacy at the Tang-era **Lóngyá** 龍牙 site in Tánzhōu (Húnán) — the historic Caves of **Lóngyá Jūdùn**.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1676** (death). The Lóngyá monastic-supervisor **Huò Xuělín** 奯雪林 personally traveled the 3,000+ *li* from Húnán to Zhèjiāng to commission the publication after Běnzhù's 1676 death.

**Lineage.** Mìyún Yuánwù → Mùchén Shānwēng Dàomín → Yúnsǒu Běnzhù. Stands as a parallel sub-line to the [[KR6q0457]] **Dábiàn Xiǎnquán** 達變顯權 (Dàomín's other heir at Língfēng / Wǔlěishān).
