# Báiyún Shǒuduān chánshī yǔlù 白雲守端禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Báiyún Shǒuduān* — a two-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of [[守端|Báiyún Shǒuduān]] 白雲守端 (1025–1072), the important Yángqí Línjì-sub-lineage master; dharma-heir of [[方會|Yángqí Fānghuì]] 楊岐方會 (992–1049) and teacher of [[法演|Wǔzǔ Fǎyǎn]] 五祖法演 (through whom the Yángqí line extends to [[克勤|Yuánwù Kèqín]] and beyond).

Two-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1351. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1060; *notAfter* c. 1100.
