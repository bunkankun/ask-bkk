# Yìnxīn Fómǐn Nè chánshī yǔlù 印心佛敏訥禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fó-mǐn Nè of Yìn-xīn*
by [[寂訥]] (說), [[法棟]] (等錄), [[法通]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Fómǐn Yìnxīn Jìnè** [[寂訥]] (catalog 寂納 = 寂訥), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Nòuyún Zhēnshí** [[真實]] 耨雲真實 (b. 1626) in the Pòshān → Zhàngxuě → Cǎotáng sub-line. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Fǎdòng** [[法棟]] + **Fǎtōng** [[法通]] *děng lù/biān*. Title-form 印心 references the **Yìnxīn** seat. *notBefore* = c. **1680** (post-master-active period). *notAfter* = c. **1700** as conservative bound. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B372.
