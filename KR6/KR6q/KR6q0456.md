# Pǔguāng Tǎnān chánshī yǔlù 普光坦菴禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Pǔ-guāng Tǎn-ān*
by [[機峻]] (說), [[全弘]] (錄), [[羅開麟]] (序)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Tǎnān Jījùn** [[機峻]] 坦菴機峻, **32nd-generation Línjì** dharma-heir of **Píngshān Cìān Zhēnběn** [[真本]] 瓶山次菴真本 (= 項謙, the Gǔpíngshān 古瓶山 *Mùdàozhě* 牧道者) at his **Pǔguāngsì** 普光寺 abbacy in **Yǒngfēng** 永豐 (Jiāxīng prefecture) from **1670/5/19**. Compiled by *shìzhě* attendant **Quánhóng** [[全弘]]. Front-matter: (i) preface by **Kōngzhū Luó Zhēnxìng** 空諸羅真性, dated **康熙庚戌孟秋丙辰 = 1670/8/15 Gregorian** (the catalog meta credits a separate *Luó Kāilín* [[羅開麟]] — possibly a parallel signature, see below); (ii) three *qǐngqǐ* 請啟 invitation-memorials by lay-disciple groups: (a) *Jiāxīng zhūxiāngshēn qǐngqǐ* (22 named patrons including 張天植 and 曹溶); (b) *zhòng wénxué qǐngqǐ* (29 named patrons including [[施博]] Shī Bó and *Luó Kāilín*); (c) *Pǔguāng qǐngqǐ*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B247.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* / *notAfter* = **1670**. The first dated event is **康熙庚戌 5/19 = 1670/6/27 Gregorian** when Jījùn received the lay-patron invitation at **Jīngyánsì** 精嚴寺. Formal entry to Pǔguāngsì on **6/6 = 1670/7/13**. The Luó Zhēnxìng front-preface is dated **1670/8/15**; the volume was therefore cut within the calendar year of the abbacy-installation. The compact one-juan extent reflects that the *yǔlù* records only the opening months of Jījùn's Pǔguāng tenure.

**Lineage.** The juan-1 entry-sermon's incense-offering verse names *Línjì zhèngchuán sānshíèr shì Píngshāntáng shàng Cìān běnshī lǎorén* 臨濟正傳三十二世瓶山堂上次菴本師老人 — confirming Jījùn's status as a 32nd-generation Línjì heir of **Cìān Zhēnběn** at Píngshān, in the **Hǔqiū → Wúzhǔn Shīfàn → Duànqiáo Mìaolún → Jìngshān Chōng → Xìngshàn Guǎng → Pǔmíng Yòng → Jīnmíng Jièlǎo → Píngshān Cìān** sub-line. The Luó Zhēnxìng preface confirms this transmission as *zuìhòu zhì Píngshān ménxià, shuǐrǔ xiāngtóu, suì shòu jìbié* 最後至瓶山門下水乳相投遂受記莂 — i.e., that Jījùn had consulted multiple teachers before receiving formal certification at Píngshān.

**Background.** Per the *zhūxiāngshēn* invitation: native of **Jīnlíng** 金陵 (Nánjīng) — *Jīnlíng yùxiù, Zhōngfù chéngxiáng* 金陵毓秀鍾阜呈祥. Per the *Pǔguāng* invitation: previously preached in **Hǎimǐn** 海閩 / **Zhāngnán** 漳南 — i.e., the Fújiàn coastal region, with abbacies near *Cānglóngjī* 蒼龍磯 (in Zhāngzhōu prefecture). The Jiāxīng invitation thus represents an eastward / northward migration from the Fújiàn ministry to a Hǔ-qiū-school heartland in Zhèjiāng.

**Lay-patron rosters.** The three invitation-memorials together name about sixty Jiāxīng lay-Chán patrons, including: **Zhāng Tiānzhí** 張天植, **Cáo Róng** 曹溶, **Wáng Tíng** 王庭, **Zhū Màoshí** 朱茂時 (in the *xiāngshēn* roster), and [[施博]] **Shī Bó** (the same preface-writer of [[KR6q0432]] / [[KR6q0433]] / [[KR6q0443]] / [[KR6q0444]] — confirming Shī Bó's continued lay-Chán activity in 1670 Jiāxīng). The *Luó Kāilín* / 羅開驎 of the *zhòng wénxué* roster is presumably the *xù* author credited in the catalog meta.

**Catalog-vs-source tension.** Catalog meta gives 羅開麟 as preface-writer (序); the surviving signed preface in the source-text is by **Luó Zhēnxìng**. Both share the surname **Luó** 羅 and may belong to a single Jiāxīng Luó family-circle of lay-disciples; Luó Kāilín is one named signer of the *zhòng wénxué* invitation, suggesting a kinsman-of-the-preface-writer rather than the preface-writer himself.

## Other points of interest

- **Píngshān Zhēnběn lineage extension.** Where [[KR6q0202]] preserves Zhēnběn's own *Mùdàozhě* sermons and [[KR6q0455]] preserves a parallel Pǔmíng-Yòng heir's *yǔlù*, this volume is the documentary witness to **Zhēnběn as transmission-grandfather** to the next *jī*-generation through Jījùn at Pǔguāng. It is, with [[KR6q0455]], the second of two extant *jí*-generation works in the Hǔqiū → Pǔmíng-Yòng line preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon.
- **Sixty-patron Jiāxīng lay roster.** The combined *qǐngqǐ* signatures preserve a uniquely detailed snapshot of the **1670 Jiāxīng lay-Chán patronage network**, including senior officials (Zhāng Tiānzhí, Cáo Róng), local *xiāngshēn*, and the published-author lay disciple [[施博]] Shī Bó in his Jiāxīng activity post-dating his earlier Cáo-dòng-line preface-work.
