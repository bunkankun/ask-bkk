# Nányuè Jìqǐ héshàng yǔlù 南嶽繼起和尚語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Reverend Nán-yuè Jì-qǐ*
by [[弘儲]] (說), [[濟機]] (等編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 / **Jìqǐ Hóngchǔ** 繼起弘儲 (**1605-03-26 – 1672-11-16**), Línjì-Yángqí principal dharma-heir of **Hànyuè Sānfēng Fǎzàng** [[法藏]] (1573–1635) and the chief continuator of the controversial **Sānfēng** sub-line at **Sūzhōu Língyánsì** 蘇州靈巖寺. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Jìjī** [[濟機]] *děng biān*. Title-form *Nányuè Jìqǐ* references both the **Nányuè Huáiràng** Línjì origin-pedigree and Hóngchǔ's own *hào* **Jìqǐ** 繼起. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B301.

### Abstract

**Self-edited corpus.** The juan-1 *zìxù* by Hóngchǔ ("退翁自序"), dated **壬子九月霜降第一日 = 1672/10/22 Gregorian** (about three weeks before his **1672-11-16** death), explains the compilation: during his **庚戌長夏 = 1670 summer** sickbed retreat, Hóngchǔ realized that traveling pilgrims could not easily acquire his earlier voluminous *yǔlù* compilations (*Shùquán Bàocí*, *Jiǎchén Xiāngyúnguǎn sǎnlù*, *Yáofēng hòulù*); he therefore distilled from them a 10-juan condensed selection focusing on *yǔ zhī jiǎnzhí zhě* 語之簡直者 (passages of direct simplicity) for portable use. Jìjī finalized the editing.

**Authorship.** Per the [[弘儲]] note: lay name **Lǐ Jīn** 李金, *zì* **Zhònglián jūshì** 仲連居士. Trained under Hànyuè Fǎzàng with formal transmission. Carrier of the Sān-fēng-line lineage through Sūzhōu Língyánsì and Yáofēng abbacies. Recorder of his master's [[KR6q0473]] 《三峰藏和尚語錄》.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1640** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1672/10** (self-preface). The 10-juan compilation specifically distills material composed across the master's c. 1640–1672 preaching career.

**Lineage and significance.** Mìyún Yuánwù → Hànyuè Fǎzàng → Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ. The Sānfēng line was the principal target of the **Yōngzhèng emperor's 1733 御製揀魔辨異錄** declaring it heretical and ordering the dismissal of its dharma-heirs. That this volume survived in the Jiāxīng Canon (despite the imperial suppression) is significant — preserving Hóngchǔ's own selected condensation of his career-long preaching.

## Other points of interest

- **Self-curated farewell-volume.** The 1670 summer sickbed compilation followed by the 1672/10 self-preface make this volume distinctive as a master's own deliberate selection of his most important preaching, completed weeks before death. Few comparable Chán *yǔlù* survive in such a directly self-curated form.
- **Sūzhōu Língyánsì line.** Hóngchǔ's continuation of the Sānfēng line through Sūzhōu Língyánsì and the Yáofēng abbacy (whence the 堯封後錄 = 堯峰後錄 mentioned in the self-preface as one of the source-compilations) maintained the Hànyuè teaching against Mì-yún-line orthodox pressure even before the eventual 1733 imperial proscription.
