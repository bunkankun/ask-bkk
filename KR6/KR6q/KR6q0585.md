# Pánshān Lǎngkōng Shùn chánshī yǔlù 盤山朗空順禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lǎng-kōng Shùn of Pán-shān*
by [[順]] (說), [[實性]] (記錄)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Lǎngkōng Shùn** [[順]] 朗空順 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pánshān** 盤山 — thus a co-disciple of [[昊]] **Huīzhōu Hào** (cf. [[KR6q0573]]). Native of **Yǒngpíngfǔ Chānglíxiàn** 永平府昌黎縣 (Héběi), lay surname **Zhào** 趙. Recorded by his female attendant **Shíxìng** [[實性]] *jìlù*. *notBefore* = **1688** (master's death and Lǎngkōng's *fùzhǔ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1710**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B473.

**Contents.** Single juan: abbacy *yǔlù* at **Lèyì Yǒngshèngān** 樂邑永盛菴; *Liǎozōngjiànshī fǎsì* lineage notice; *Ruìtíng Shèng chánshī fǎsì* lineage notice — i.e. the volume includes brief lineage-records for both Lǎngkōng's master and a related Pán-shān-line monk.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J40 B473).*

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0573]] ([[昊]]) and [[KR6q0585]] ([[順]]) together document the two principal heirs of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** of **Pánshān** in the post-1688 generation — both based in the Héběi / Pánshān regional Línjì-Yángqí establishment. Ling-zōng's combined disciple-cohort (these two plus others) anchored Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism in the Héběi → Tiānjīn → Liáodōng frontier into the late Kāngxī era.
