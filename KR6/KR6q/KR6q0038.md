# Jìdēng lù 繼燈錄

*Continuing-Lamp Record*

compiled by [[元賢]] Yuánxián (sobriquet Yǒngjué 永覺, 1578–1657), late Míng / early Qīng

## About the work

A six-juan late-Míng / early-Qīng Cáodòng-line Chán lamp record by Yǒngjué Yuánxián, supplementing the existing *Wǔdēng huìyuán* ([[KR6q0011]]) and *Xù chuándēng lù* ([[KR6q0015]]) traditions with expanded coverage of the Cáodòng line through the Yuán and Míng. Title — "Continuing-Lamp Record" — frames it as continuation rather than revision.

### Abstract

Yuánxián was one of the two most important seventeenth-century Cáodòng-line masters (alongside [[無異元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái, 1575–1630), and his *Jìdēng lù* is the principal Cáodòng-side late-Míng contribution to the lamp-record literature, distinct from and in a certain counterpoint to the contemporary Línjì-line works by [[費隱通容]] Fèiyǐn Tōngróng. Unlike Tōngróng's polemical *Wǔdēng yántǒng* ([[KR6q0018]]), Yuánxián's compilation is restrained and scholarly; it restores the Cáodòng-line attributions while avoiding direct polemic against the Línjì.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Yǒngjué Yuánxián has received substantial Western scholarly attention: Jiang Wu, *Enlightenment in Dispute* (OUP, 2008), treats him and his Jǔngū-shān 鼓山 monastic community at length; see also [[廖肇亨]] Liào Zhàohēng's Chinese-language work on seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán.

## Other points of interest

Yuánxián's *Jìdēng lù* and the contemporary Línjì-side works together document the seventeenth-century intensification of Chán lineage consciousness that Jiang Wu calls "the reinvention of Chan Buddhism."
