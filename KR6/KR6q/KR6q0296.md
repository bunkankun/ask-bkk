# Pǔjué Zōnggǎo chánshī yǔlù 普覺宗杲禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Pǔjué Zōnggǎo* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **[[宗杲|Dàhuì Zōnggǎo]]** 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163), the dominant Southern-Sòng Chán master and originator of *kàn huà chán* 看話禪 method. *Pǔjué* 普覺 is Zōnggǎo's posthumous title. Compiled by his dharma-heirs [[法宏|Fǎhóng]] 法宏 and [[道謙|Dàoqiān]] 道謙.

Two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1362. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

This is a compact recension of Zōnggǎo's teachings, companion to the much larger *Dàhuì Pǔjué chánshī yǔlù* (T47 n1998A, 30 juan) — the principal canonical *yǔlù* of Zōnggǎo.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1130; *notAfter* 1190 (Jìngshān Zǔqìng's 1190 preface to the *Pǔjué Zōnggǎo chánshī yǔlù* provides a useful chronological anchor).
