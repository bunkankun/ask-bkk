# Zhìtán Zhèng chánshī yǔlù 智覃正禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhì-tán Zhèng*
by [[最正]] (說), [[尚能]] (等編), [[尚瑩]] (等編)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Zhìtán Zuìzhèng** [[最正]] 智覃最正, LínjìYángqí Línyě → Zìxián → Tiānmù → Xiùyě → Zhìtán fifth-generation heir. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Shàngnéng** [[尚能]] + **Shàngyíng** [[尚瑩]] *děng biān*. Together with [[KR6q0469]], [[KR6q0495]], [[KR6q0496]], [[KR6q0497]] forms a five-generation Lín-yě-line continuous *yǔlù* series — exceptional generational depth in the Jiāxīng Canon. *notBefore* = c. 1680, *notAfter* = c. 1700. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B358.
