# Chījué Dàochōng chánshī yǔlù 痴絕道冲禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Chījué Dàochōng* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Chījué Dàochōng** 痴絕道冲 (1169–1250), Línjì-Yángqí master. Compiled by five dharma-heir disciples led by [[智沂|Zhìyí]] 智沂 (plus 悟開, 行彌, 紹甄, 元樞).

Two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X70 n1376.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1200; *notAfter* c. 1280.
