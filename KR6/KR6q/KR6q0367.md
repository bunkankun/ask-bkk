# Yǒngjué Yuánxián chánshī guǎnglù 永覺元賢禪師廣錄

Thirty-juan "Extensive Record" (*guǎnglù* 廣錄) of **Yǒngjué Yuánxián** [[元賢]] 永覺元賢 (1578–1657), the Shòuchāng 壽昌 Cáodòng 曹洞 master whose 30-juan self-contained corpus — *yǔlù* proper plus *chányú nèiwài jí* 禪餘內外集, *wǎn lù* 晚錄, *zuìhòu yǔ* 最後語, *dòngshàng gǔzhé* 洞上古轍, *mǐyán* 䆿言 — together amount to the most substantial MíngQīng transitional Cáodòng corpus. *Xuzangjing* X72 no. 1437. Re-edited (*chóngbiān* 重編) by Yuánxián's principal dharma-heir [[道霈]] Wèilín Dàopèi 為霖道霈 (21 December 1615 – 27 October 1702) in the three years following the master's death (Shùnzhì 14 / 1657).

### Abstract

The collection opens with two prefatorial *zàn* 贊 — one by **[[元來]] Wúyì Yuánlái** ("吞却死猫頭，嚙盡生人膽…") dating from before Yuánlái's own death in 1630, and thus describing the young Yuánxián as a Shòuchāng brother-in-dharma — and one by the lay disciple **Zhèng Xuán** 鄭瑄 (*Zuófēi ān dìzǐ* 昨非菴弟子), praising Yuánxián as "the only such person in five hundred years" 五百年來僅此人. The principal preface, ***Yǒngjué héshàng guǎnglù xù*** 永覺和尚廣錄序, is by **Lín Zhīfān** 林之蕃 (a *jìnshì* *kǎogōng sī lángzhōng* 吏部考功司郎中 / *Púsàjiè dìzǐ*) who "had lived under the master's seat and received his teaching, from youth to old age, for more than a day"; Lín narrates the 30-juan compilation as the three-year labour of Dàopèi after the master's death, and calls it "the *tījīn* 梯金 platform for the new restoration of Shàolín / Cáodòng orthodoxy." Several further prefaces follow: to the *Chányú nèijí* 禪餘內集 by Cáo Gǔ 曹谷 (Chóngzhēn *Guǐwèi* 癸未 / 1643, during Yuánxián's Gǔshān years — thus a pre-death preface to one of the component collections, reused in the posthumous consolidation); to the *Chányú wàijí* 禪餘外集; and others.

The structure of the 30 juan spans all the classic genres — *shàngtáng*, *xiǎocān*, *wèndá*, *fǎyǔ*, *niāngǔ*, *sònggǔ*, *zàn*, *jìsòng*, *shū*, *wén*, *shū*, and the book-length sub-collections: ***Dòngshàng gǔzhé*** 洞上古轍 (a historical-doctrinal treatise on Cáodòng lineage and *wǔwèi* 五位 teaching — one of the chief Qīng-dynasty Cáodòng theoretical works); and the ***Mǐyán*** 䆿言 ("Sleep-Talk") miscellaneous doctrinal essays. The ***Zuìhòu yǔ*** 最後語 captures Yuánxián's last-year teachings of 1656–1657.

Yuánxián's career trajectory is set out in [[元賢]]'s person note; the *guǎnglù* covers it in full, from the first recorded abbacy at Ōuníng Jīnxiānān 金仙庵 in c. 1626 to the final teachings at Gǔshān in 1657. Lín Zhīfān also notes that Yuánxián's separately-circulating works — the *Léngyán* 楞嚴 and *Jīngāng* 金剛 sūtra commentaries, the *Xīnjīng zhǐzhǎng* 心經指掌, the *Sìfēn yuēyì* 四分約義 (*Vinaya* summary), the *Lǜxué fārèn* 律學發軔, the *Hóngshì lù* 弘釋錄, the *Jìdēng bǔdēng èr lù* 繼燈補燈二錄 ([[KR6q0038]]), the *Jìngcí yàoyǔ* 淨慈要語, the *Gǔshān zhì* 鼓山志, and the *Kāiyuán sì zhì* 開元寺志 — are not included in the *guǎnglù* but are preserved in separate Kanripo / Jiāxīng entries, several of them within KR6q.

Date bracket: c. 1626 (first abbacy) through c. 1660 (final editorial form of the 30-juan corpus, three years after Yuánxián's 1657 death).

## Translations and research

Yuán-xián is a central subject in Jiang Wu's *Enlightenment in Dispute* (OUP, 2008). The *Dòng-shàng gǔ-zhé* and *Mǐ-yán* are partially treated in Chinese-language 17th-century Cáo-dòng scholarship. No book-length Western-language study of the *guǎng-lù* proper.

## Other points of interest

Lín Zhīfān's preface, written at the height of Dàopèi's editorial work, anticipates that the *guǎnglù* will "one day, under a *fóxīn tiānzǐ* 佛心天子, be incorporated into the imperial canon alongside *Yǒngmíng zōngjìng* 永明宗鏡, *Míngjiào tánjīn* 明教鐔津, and other *zūnsù yǔlù* 尊宿語錄" — a specific political-cultural aspiration for the new Qīng regime which was, in the event, only partially fulfilled through the inclusion in the later Xuzangjing rather than the mainline Qīng Tripitaka.
