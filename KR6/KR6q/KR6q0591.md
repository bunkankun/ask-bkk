# Zhāojué Zhúfēng Xù chánshī yǔlù 昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhú-fēng Xù of Zhāo-jué [-sì]*
by [[真續]] (說), [[實悟]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Zhúfēng Zhēnxù** [[真續]] 竹峰真續 (b. **1660-05-16** on Buddha's Birthday), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Fóyuān Chègāng** 佛冤徹綱 (1626–?). Native of **Sōngzhōu** 松州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Shěn** 沈. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Déyún Shíwù** [[實悟]] *děng biān*.

Documents teaching at five Sìchuān seats: **Wēifèng Zhāojuésì** 威鳳昭覺寺 (the historic mother-temple); **Miàojué Chóngshàn** 妙覺崇善; **Xīlóng Dàbēi** 西龍大悲; **Xīchóng Wúliàng** 西崇無量; **Fènghuáng Fǎzàng** 鳳凰法藏. *notBefore* ≈ **1690** (post-付法 abbacy onset, after his time at Zhāojué under Fóyuān); *notAfter* ≈ **1720** (large *fǎsì* roster of 83 indicates a long career). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B479.

**Contents.** Juan 1: four prefaces; *mùcì*; *shàngtáng*. Juan 2–3: continuing abbacy *yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*. Juan 4: *sònggǔ* on classic *gōngàn* (明星悟道, 拈花微笑, 婆子燒菴, 南泉斬貓, 三玄三要, 馬祖三喝, 牧牛頌, 十無頌 with sub-verses on *wúgēnshù* 無根樹, *wúxiánqín* 無絃琴, *wúyuèsuǒ* 無鑰鎖). Juan 5: *xíngshí*; *fēndēng* listing the 83 *fǎsì*. Juan 6: closing *fóshì* and miscellaneous.

## Other points of interest

The eighty-three named *fǎsì* in juan 5 is one of the largest disciple-cohorts attested in any single Jiā-xīng-canon LínjìYángqí *yǔlù*, indicating Zhúfēng's institutional weight at **Zhāojuésì** 昭覺寺 — the principal mother-temple of Sìchuān LínjìYángqí Buddhism, whence the 18th-century revival of southwestern Buddhism radiated. The succession of Zhāojué abbacies from **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) through his second-generation heirs ([[徹生]] [[KR6q0544]]) and third-generation heir Zhúfēng (b. 1660, this text) marks the institutional continuity of the Sìchuān line into the 18th century.
