# Guóqīng Dàyǔ Tāo chánshī yǔlù 國清大庾韜禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dà-yǔ Tāo of Guó-qīng-sì*
by [[行韜]] (說), [[顯裕]] (等錄)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Dàyǔ Xíngtāo** [[行韜]] 大庾行韜 (d. **1652-08-28**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hànyuè Sānfēng** sub-line and *zhí* 姪 (nephew) of **Hànyuè Fǎzàng** [[法藏]]. Recorded by *shìzhě* **Xiǎnyù** [[顯裕]] *děng lù* at the **Tiāntái Guóqīngsì** 天台國清寺. Includes the *yībō tǎmíng* 衣缽塔銘 tomb-inscription. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B305.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[行韜]] note: native of **Wújiāng** 吳江, lay surname **Zhào** 趙. Studied under his uncle **Hànyuè Fǎzàng** in childhood — a rare family-line preliminary connection in the Sānfēng tradition. **1646**: retreated to Wǔyíshān. Full precepts under **Fúshí Tōngxián** at Wújiāng Bàoēnsì; final transmission under **Língyán Hóngchǔ**. **1650**: installed by Hóngchǔ at Guóqīngsì.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1650** (Guóqīng abbacy entry by Hóngchǔ's instruction). *notAfter* = **1652** (death). Two-year abbacy.

**Lineage and Guóqīng succession.** Mìyún Yuánwù → Hànyuè Fǎzàng → Tuìwēng Hóngchǔ → Dàyǔ Xíngtāo. Direct successor at Guóqīngsì to **Xièyún Jìjī** [[濟璣]] (d. 1648, [[KR6q0476]]) — both Hóngchǔ heirs at the Tiāntái flagship.

## Other points of interest

- **Family-line uncle-nephew transmission.** Xíngtāo's childhood study under his uncle **Hànyuè Fǎzàng** is a documented case of family-bound monastic learning subsequently regularized through Hóngchǔ's full transmission — uncommon in the broader Mì-yún-line and indicative of the Sān-fēng-line's localized Wújiāng / Sūzhōu kinship-network.
- **Continuous Sānfēng Guóqīng abbacy.** The two-abbacy sequence Jìjī (d. 1648) → Xíngtāo (1650–52) extends the Sān-fēng-line's claim on Tiāntái Guóqīngsì for at least 1640s–1652 — a sustained Línjì occupancy of the Tiāntái flagship in the late-Míng / early-Qīng transition window.
