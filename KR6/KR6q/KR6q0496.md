# Xiùyě Lín chánshī yǔlù 秀野林禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiù-yě Lín*
by [[明林]] (說), [[最正]] (等編)

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Xiùyě Mínglín** [[明林]] 秀野明林, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānmù / Qírán Chāozhì** [[超智]] (cf. [[KR6q0495]]). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìtán Zuìzhèng** [[最正]] *děng biān* (whose own *yǔlù* is at [[KR6q0497]]). Lineage: Línyě Tōngqí → Zìxián Xíngjué → Tiānmù Chāozhì → Xiùyě Mínglín → Zhìtán Zuìzhèng. *notBefore* = c. 1670, *notAfter* = c. 1695. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B357.
