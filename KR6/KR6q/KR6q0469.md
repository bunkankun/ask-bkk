# Zìxián Jué chánshī yǔlù 自閒覺禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zì-xián Jué*
by [[覺]] (說), [[洪暹]] (等編)

## About the work

Eight-juan *yǔlù* of **Zìxián Xíngjué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (**1610-09-23 – 1662-02-14**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) at **Tōngxuánsì** 通玄寺. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Hānyú Xiān** [[洪暹]] 憨予暹 *děng*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B287. Six-abbacy structure: juan 1 — Sūzhōu Huángxī Luóhànsì + Chóngchuān Cíjì + Fèngchuān Fǎhǎi *yǔlù*; juan 2 — Fèngchuān Yuèlín + Jiāxīng Lóngyuān Jǐngdé *yǔlù*; juan 3 — Jīngxī Fúróng *yǔlù*; juan 4 — *shìzhòng* / *xiǎocān* / *wǎncān* / *fǎyǔ* / *wèndá* / *shìzhōng kānbiàn*; juan 5 — *niānsòng* / *liánfāngjì* / **行實**; juan 6 — *shūwèn* / *fózǔzàn* / *zìzàn* / *fóshì*; juan 7–8 *xíngzhuàng* and additional matter.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA and the juan-5 *xíngshí* + juan-8 *xíngzhuàng*: native of **Hézhōu** 合州 (Chóngqìng, Sìchuān), lay surname **Yú** 余. Tonsured at age 8 at Mǎdāngshān under **Tàikōng**. **1635**: left Sìchuān; first contact with **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Tiāntóng on a *lùzhù* 露柱 collision triggering initial *xǐng*. Decisive transmission from **Tōngxuán Línyě Tōngqí**.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1648** (the 戊子 entry-sermon at Sūzhōu Huángxī Luóhànsì, the first of his six abbacies and the earliest dated material in the corpus). *notAfter* = **1665** as a conservative bound for the post-1662-decease compilation by Hóngxiān. The corpus must have been completed within a few years of the 1662 death since lay-patron memory of the master was still vivid.

**Six abbacies, six *yǔlù*.** The structural arrangement of juan 1–3 explicitly partitions the *yǔlù* by abbacy: **Huángxī Luóhàn 1648 → Cíjì 1649 → Fǎhǎi 1650 → Yuèlín 1651 → Lóngyuān Jǐngdé 1654 → Fúróng 1656**. The Fúróngsì at **Jīngxī** (Yíxīng region) was the death-seat and the source of the title-form 芙蓉自閒.

**Lineage.** Pòshān → Mìyún Yuánwù → Línyě Tōngqí → Zìxián Xíngjué — i.e., one Sì-chuān-born offshoot of the Mìyún main line. The 33 named *fǎsì* across six abbacies indicates a substantial preaching network. Notable heirs: **Tiānmù Zhì** 天目智 (a third-generation founder), **Cílǎng Jiù** 慈朗鷲, **Hànguān Yù** 漢關喻, **Hānyú Xiān** (this volume's compiler).

**Cross-reference with [[KR6q0464]].** The pilgrim **Zǐzhōu Chuán** [[船]] 梓舟超船 visited *Sāntǎ jiàn Zìxián* 三塔見自閒 in his 1645 pilgrimage circuit (*Sāntǎ* perhaps an early-1640s pre-abbacy seat); confirms Xíngjué's recognition as a notable teaching master well before his formal 1648 abbacy at Luóhànsì.

## Other points of interest

- **Zhèjiāng / Jiāngsū six-abbacy circuit.** The 1648–1656 abbacy-sequence traces a coherent **Jiāngnán mendicant circuit** through the Sūzhōu / Níngbō / Jiāxīng / Yíxīng cluster — distinct from the Sìchuān or Fújiàn-Yúnnán Pòshān-sub-line patterns and reflective of Línyě Tōngqí's Tōng-xuán-based Zhèjiāng establishment.
