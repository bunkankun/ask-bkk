# Chúnbèi Dé chánshī yǔlù 純備德禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chún-bèi Dé*
by [[廣德]] (說), [[智遠]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Chúnbèi Guǎngdé** [[廣德]] 純備廣德 (lifedates unknown, active **1666–1672+**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěrdú Dàotài** 耳毒道泰 — thus a co-disciple of **Huànzhù Míng** [[明|Huànzhù Míng]] (cf. [[KR6q0549]]). Native of **Shǔdōng Fēngdū** 蜀東鄷都 (= 酆都, Sìchuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn** [[智遠]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at two abbacies on the upper-Yangtze gorge corridor: **Yílíngzhōu Huánglíngshān Yuèxiá chányuàn** 彝陵州黃陵月峽禪院 (entered **康熙丙午** = **1666** spring) and **Sìguī Fóxīng Dàbēi chányuàn** 秭歸佛興大悲禪院 (founded by Guǎngdé in **康熙壬子** = **1672**). Preface dated **康熙庚戌三月上巳** = **1670-04-21** by **Gǔtíng Dàolóng Bódá** 古庭道隆博達 at the Qīnglín plum-pavilion. *notBefore* = **1666**; *notAfter* ≈ **1672+**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B433.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *Huánglíng Yuèxiáyuàn yǔlù*; *Dàbēiyuàn yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*; *fǎyǔ* (示方勝, 示乾齡王居士, 示歸源宋居士, 示弘玄道人, 示融一監院, 示疑悟禪人). Juan 2: continuing *fǎyǔ* and *jì*; *jīyuán*; *sònggǔ*; **聯芳** (lineage-distribution) listing the three *fǎsì*; *fóshì*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔlù* together with [[KR6q0551]] (the *yǔlù* of his disciple **Fǎchuáng Zhìyuǎn**) documents the late-Kāngxī Línjì-Yángqí presence on the **Yílíng** 彝陵 / **Sìguī** 秭歸 stretch of the Yangtze gorges — a corridor of strategic monastic geography that received heavy state patronage as part of post-Sānfān-luàn reconstruction. Guǎngdé's founding of **Fóxīng Dàbēi chányuàn** at Sìguī in 1672 is a primary record of that effort.
