# Mǐnshù chánshī yǔlù 敏樹禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mǐn-shù*
by [[如相]] (說), [[道崇]] (編), [[道領]] (錄)

Substantial ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** [[如相]] 敏樹如相 (**1603 – 1672-12-21**, age 70, *sēnglà* 45), LínjìYángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) and the foundational figure of the major **Mǐnshù** 敏樹 sub-line of southwestern LínjìYángqí Buddhism. Native of **Tóngchuānzhōu Línshuǐ** 潼川州鄰水 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Wáng** 王. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Tiānyǐn Dàochóng** [[道崇]] *biān* + recorded by his *fǎsì* **Chìsōng Dàolǐng** [[道領]] *lù* (whose own *yǔlù* is [[KR6q0565]]). Two prefaces dated **康熙戊辰蒲月** = **1688** lunar 5 by 貴陽黔靈山弘福寺.

The *yǔlù* documents seven successive abbacies across the southwestern frontier circuit: **Sìchuān Diànjiāngxiàn Bǎizhàng chányuàn** 四川墊江縣百丈禪院 (the principal seat); **Húguǎng Shīzhōuwèi Wànshòu chánsì** 湖廣施州衛萬壽禪寺; **Sìchuān Péngshuǐxiàn Tàipíng chánsì** 四川彭水縣太平禪寺; **Guìzhōu Shíqiānfǔ Sānmèi chányuàn** 貴州石阡府三昧禪院; **Sìchuān Zūnyìfǔ Hǎilóngshān Gǔlóng chányuàn** 四川遵義府海龍山古龍禪院; **Wànxiàn Cíyún chányuàn** 萬縣慈雲禪院 (re-entered); **Guìzhōu Dàxīng chánsì** 貴州大興禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1645** (post-*fùfǎ* abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1688** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B450.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces, *mùlù*, *Bǎizhàngyuàn yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Wànshòusì yǔlù*. Juan 3: *Tàipíngsì yǔlù*; *Sānmèiyuàn yǔlù*. Juan 4: *Gǔlóngyuàn yǔlù*; *Cíyúnyuàn yǔlù*; *Dàxīngsì yǔlù*. Juan 5: *xiǎocān*, *pǔshuō*, *cháhuà*. Juan 6–10: continuing materials including *jīyuán*, *fóshì*, the *xíngzhuàng* and *tǎmíng* (death **康熙壬子十一月初三日亥時** = **1672-12-21**), and the *fēndēng* listing twenty-four *fǎsì*.

## Other points of interest

The Mǐnshù sub-line founded by Rúxiāng is one of the largest LínjìYángqí lineage-clusters in the late-17th-century SìchuānGuìzhōuYúnnán region, parallel to the **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** 丈雪通醉 and **Shèngkě Déyù** 聖可德玉 sub-lines also under Pòshān Hǎimíng. Its 24 *fǎsì* span the entire southwestern frontier; many of these are independently documented in J38–J39 *yǔlù*: 耳毒泰 → [[明|Huànzhù Míng]] ([[KR6q0549]]), 耳毒泰 → [[廣德]] ([[KR6q0550]]), [[道領]] ([[KR6q0565]]), 穎秀 → [[性炅]] ([[KR6q0524]]), etc.
