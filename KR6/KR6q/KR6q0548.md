# Yúnfēng Tǐzōng Níng chánshī yǔlù 雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tǐ-zōng Níng of Yún-fēng [-sì]*
by [[寧]] (說), [[續清]] (等編), [[德玉]] (序), [[徹生]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Tǐzōng Dàoníng** [[寧]] 體宗道寧 (**1598-07-12 – 1669**, age 72, *sēnglà* 37), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Native of **Lúzhōu** 瀘州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Zhànyī Xùqīng** [[續清]] *děng biān*.

The *yǔlù* documents three Sìchuān abbacies: **Lúzhōu Fāngshān Yúnfēngsì** 瀘州方山雲峰寺 (the principal seat, entered **順治九年三月十三日** = **1652-04-21** on the invitation of *Chuānnándào* 川南道 official **Wú Dēng** 吳登 and the assembled prefectural gentry); **Jiāngānxiàn Pánlóngsì** 江安縣蟠龍寺; and **Nánxīxiàn Kāiyuánsì** 南溪縣開元寺. The Yúnfēngsì entry is the same monastery where **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] was ordained as a *shāmí* in **丁酉冬** = **1657** winter (cf. [[KR6q0544]]).

Two cross-lineage prefaces are preserved: by **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] (signed 華岩法弟德玉, "younger dharma-brother") dated **康熙乙丑孟夏浴佛日** = **1685-05-10** (Buddha's Birthday); and by **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] (signed 青城法姪徹生, "younger dharma-nephew") dated **康熙乙亥正陽佛誕之吉** = **1695-05-19**. *notBefore* = **1652** (Yúnfēngsì entry); *notAfter* = **1695** (latest preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B431.

**Contents.** Single juan: two prefaces; *Pánlóngsì yǔlù*; *Kāiyuánsì yǔlù*; *jīyuán*; *sònggǔ* on classic *gōngàn* (世尊初生, 雪山成道, 達磨見武帝, 丹霞燒木佛, 本來面目, 女子入定, 國師三喚侍者, 倩女離魂, plus two *Yuánjué* / *Léngyán* themes); *jì*; closing materials including the *xíngshí* and the *fēndēng* listing six dharma-heirs.

## Other points of interest

The text was published roughly 25 years after the master's death, with prefaces composed across a 10-year span (1685, 1695) by two younger Pò-shān-line cousins both based in major Sìchuān monastic centers (Huáyánsì and Qīngchéng Fènglínsì). This pattern of late-Kāngxī cross-monastery preface-exchange is characteristic of the Jiā-xīng-canon Sìchuān cluster (cf. [[KR6q0538]], [[KR6q0539]], [[KR6q0544]], [[KR6q0545]], [[KR6q0547]]) and reflects the post-Sānfān-luàn institutional consolidation of southwestern Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism.
