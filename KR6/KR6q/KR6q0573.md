# Luánzhōu Wànshàn Huīzhōu Hào chánshī yǔlù 灤州萬善暉州昊禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huī-zhōu Hào of Wàn-shàn [-sì] in Luán-zhōu*
by [[昊]] (說), [[性珍]] (等錄), [[寂寶]] (等錄)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Huīzhōu Hào** [[昊]] 暉洲昊 (lifedates unknown, active **1675–1699+**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎozōng Chāojiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pánshān** 盤山. Native of **Luánzhōu Lètíng** 灤州樂亭 (Héběi), lay surname **Liú** 劉. Recorded by his attendants **Xìngzhēn** [[性珍]] and **Jìbǎo** [[寂寶]] *děng lù*. Preface dated **康熙三十八年歲次己卯閏月** = **1699** leap-month.

The *yǔlù* documents an exceptionally extensive **thirteen-abbacy** circuit spanning the **Héběi → Tiānjīn → Liáodōng → Mukden** northern frontier — comparable in geographic scope only to **Qiānshān Shèngrén** [[函可]]'s [[KR6q0527]] and **Gǔlín Zhì** [[智|Gǔlín Zhì]]'s [[KR6q0546]]. Principal seats include **Luánzhōu Wànshànsì** 灤州萬善禪寺 (the title-seat); **Shèngjīng Fèngtiān Huáyánsì** 盛京奉天華嚴寺 (Mukden); **Pánshān Zhèngfǎ chánsì** 盤山正法禪寺; **Tiānjīnwèi** 天津衛 (twice — *Fàngshēngyuàn* and *Bōrěyuàn*). Other abbacies in **Yǒngpíngfǔ** 永平府, **Yùtiánxiàn** 玉田縣, **Liáodōng Cháoyángsì** 朝陽寺, **Níngyuǎnzhōu Dìzàngsì** 寧遠地藏寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1675** (post-付法 abbacy career under Liǎozōng who was active from the 1670s); *notAfter* = **1699** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B460.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *mùcì*; abbacy *yǔlù* materials. Juan 2–4: continuing abbacy *shàngtáng* records. Juan 5: *Lètíng Shèngyánsì yǔlù* (the final abbacy). Juan 6: *chuíwèn*; *rùshì jīyuán*; *wèndá jīyuán*; *qǐngyì*; *fùzhǔ* (entrusting Tiānxǐ Yìn 天璽印 with the lineage); *dàigǔ*; *biégǔ*; *niāngǔ*; *sònggǔ*; *fóshì*; the closing *lüèzhuàng* 略狀 (autobiographical summary).

## Other points of interest

This is the major Línjì-Yángqí *yǔlù* of post-1644 northern-frontier Buddhism after **Pòshān Hǎimíng**'s line passed through 函可 (Cáodòng, exiled to Liáodōng) and 古林智 (Línjì, integrated into the Mukden ecclesiastical establishment). Huīzhōu Hào represents the third-stage **institutional consolidation** of Manchurian-Hé-běi Buddhism: 13 simultaneously-held abbacies under formal Qīng patronage, with **Tiānjīn** as a key bridge-station between Héběi monastic centers and the Liáodōng frontier.
