# Dàbó Qián chánshī yǔlù 大博乾禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dà-bó Qián*
by [[乾]] (說), [[超宗]] (等錄)

Five-juan *yǔlù* of **Dàbó Xíngqián** [[乾]] 大博行乾 (**1602 – 1673-09-20**, age 72, *sēnglà* 59), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wànrú Tōngwēi** 萬如通微 (1594–1657, of Lóngchí). Native of **Sìchuān Dázhōu** 達州, lay surname **Hú** 胡. Recorded by his attendant **Chāozōng** [[超宗]] *děng lù*. *notBefore* = **順治壬辰** = **1652** autumn (received *jìbié*); *notAfter* = **1673** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B472.

Documents an extraordinary **eleven-abbacy** circuit, all in the **Héběi / Tiānjīn / suburban Beijing** region — among the most extensive northern-China abbatial itineraries in the Jiāxīng Canon: **Tiānjīn Dìzàngsì** 天津地藏寺, **Tiānjīn Rúláisì** 天津如來寺, **Xióngxiàn Chóngníngsì** 雄縣崇寧寺, **Yángcūn Bàochéngsì** 楊村報成寺, **Zhuōzhōu Pǔdùsì** 涿州普度寺, **Liángxiāng Shífāngsì** 良鄉十方寺, **Jīnmén Bōrěyuàn** 津門般若院, **Luánzhōu Wànshànsì** 灤州萬善寺, **Xīshān Tánzhèsì** 西山潭柘寺 (the famous suburban-Beijing temple), **Dūchéng Fǎtōngsì** 都城法通寺 (within Beijing), **Pánshān Zhōngpánsì** 盤山中盤寺 (the burial site).

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces. Juan 2–5: continuing material — full *shàngtáng* records, *xiǎocān*, *fǎyǔ*, *jīyuán*, *fóshì*, *xíngshí*.

## Other points of interest

The **Pánshān Zhōngpánsì** 盤山中盤寺 is one of the major imperial-Qīng Buddhist temples — the same Pánshān where [[昊|Huīzhōu Hào]] (cf. [[KR6q0573]]) of the next generation also held abbacy. **Tánzhèsì** 潭柘寺 in the western Beijing hills was historically one of the most prestigious abbacies in the empire ("先有潭柘寺，後有北京城" — "Tánzhèsì existed before Beijing was built"). The Manchu-court Buddhist patronage axis traceable through Dàbó (1652+) → Huīzhōu Hào (1675+) is among the most important documented in the Jiāxīng Canon.
