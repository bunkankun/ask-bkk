# Lǚyán Hé chánshī yǔlù 侶巖荷禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lǚ-yán Hé*
by [[荷]] (說), [[成純]] (等記錄)

Seven-juan *yǔlù* of **Lǚyán Hé** [[荷]] 侶巖荷 (lifedates unknown, active **1675–1685**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiānàn Běnshēng** 天岸本昇 (1620–1673) in the **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 → **Tiāntóng** 天童 line. Recorded by his attendant **Chéngchún** [[成純]] *děng jìlù*. Two prefaces dated **康熙乙丑** = **1685**: by his dharma-cousin **Tiāntóngshān Xiǎoxī Běnxī** 天童山曉皙本晳 (lunar 9), and by lay disciple **Chén Liàng** 陳量 (Buddha's Birthday).

Documents teaching at three lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Húzhōu Wūshān Lóngquánān** 湖州烏山龍泉菴 (the seat giving him his sobriquet **Lóngquán** 龍泉); **Tiáochéng Shīzǐlín** 苕城師子林; **Ānjízhōu Lóngshān Dōngchánsì** 安吉州龍山東禪寺. *notBefore* = **1675** (post-Běn-shēng-death abbacy onset, ca. 1675); *notAfter* = **1685** (prefaces). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B452.

**Contents.** Juan 1: two prefaces; *Lóngquánān yǔlù*; *Shīzǐlín yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Dōngchánsì yǔlù*. Juan 3: *shìzhōng jǔgǔ*; *sònggǔ*. Juan 4: *chuídài*. Juan 5: *jīyuán*. Juan 6–7: *jì* (poetry — including *kū běnshī héshàng* 哭本師和尚 mourning his teacher Běnshēng, *shòu* birthday-verses for fellow monks 法雷, 燈 etc., consolation verses, *shìtú* 示徒, *shì chánzhě zhùshān* 示禪者住山).
