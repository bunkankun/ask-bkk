# Jièshí Zhìpéng chánshī yǔlù 介石智朋禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Jièshí Zhìpéng* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Jièshí Zhìpéng** 介石智朋, Línjì-Yángqí master. Compiled by seven *cānxué* 參學 attendant-disciples led by [[正賢|Zhèngxián]] 正賢.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1371.

Dating: broad Southern-Sòng bracket.
