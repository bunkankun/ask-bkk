# Mìxíng Rěn chánshī yǔlù 密行忍禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mì-xíng Rěn*
by [[寂忍]] (說), [[明廣]] (等編), [[明珍]] (等編)

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Mìxíng Jìrěn** [[寂忍]] 密行寂忍 (DILA gives world-age 58 but no firm birth/death years), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Native of **Yúnnán Yíliáng** 宜良, lay surname **Gǔ** 谷. The most senior of Pòshān's Yún-nán-born heirs and the principal teacher whose lineage extended into Húguǎng / Héngzhōu, where his Yúnnán dharma-heir [[學蘊]] received final instructions from him at **Kāifēng Nányún chányuàn** 開峰南雲禪院 (cf. [[KR6q0523]]). Compiled by his *fǎsūn* (dharma-grandsons) **Míngguǎng** [[明廣]] and **Míngzhēn** [[明珍]] *děng biān*.

Five abbacies are documented: **Xùzhōu Xǐyù** 敘州喜獄, **Luòyuán Xīngguó** 雒源興國 (entered **順治丁酉** = **1657**, served as a *bìguān* 掩關 / sealed-room retreat), **Bǎoqìng Dàbēi** 寶慶大悲 (Húnán), **Héngyáng Kāifēng Nányún** 衡陽開峰南雲禪院 (Húguǎng), and a fifth implied. The principal preface is by **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] of Qīngchéngshān (cf. [[KR6q0544]]) signed at Jiāxīngfǔ in **康熙甲戌蒲月** = **1694** (Kāngxī 33, lunar 5) — composed during Chèshēng's eastern engraving expedition for the *Jǐnjiāng chándēng* 錦江禪燈. *notBefore* = **1657** (Luòyuán Zhōngxīng entry); *notAfter* = **1694** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B428.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface, *mùcì*, *shàngtáng*. Juan 2: *jīyuán*, *fǎyǔ* (示求戒諸禪者, 示禪者, 示鑿池諸禪者, 示映天飯頭, 示憨岳李居士, *zhíshìbǎng* 職事榜, *ānchánbǎng* 安禪榜). Juan 3: *sònggǔ* on classic *gōngàn* (拈花微笑, 打車打牛, 德山托缽, 百丈野狐, 文殊問菴提遮女, 南泉斬貓, etc.) and the closing **行實** (autobiographical / hagiographical narration).

## Other points of interest

The cross-regional preface authorship — **Sìchuān** Chèshēng for a **Yúnnán** master's *yǔlù*, written at **Jiāxīng** during a publishing expedition — is itself important documentation of how the Pòshān Hǎimíng network in the 1690s coordinated southwestern (Yúnnán-Sìchuān-Guìzhōu) Buddhist publishing through the eastern Jiāxīng Léngyánsì 流通 channel. The *yǔlù*'s 行實 in juan 3 is also a primary source for the early-Qīng Yúnnán Línjì-Yángqí monastic landscape (Zhúzǐshān, Yúntáishān), supplementing the [[KR6q0523]] 〈行錄〉 of his disciple [[學蘊]].
