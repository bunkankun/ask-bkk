# Wànfēng héshàng yǔlù 萬峰和尚語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Master Wàn-fēng*
by [[時蔚]] (說), [[普壽]] (集)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Wànfēng Shíwèi** [[時蔚]] 萬峰時蔚 (**1303-10-18 – 1381-03-03**, age 79), Yuán-Míng-transition Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qiānyán Yuáncháng** 千巖元長 (1284–1357). Native of **Lèqīng** 樂清 (Zhèjiāng), lay surname **Jīn** 金. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Pǔshòu** [[普壽]] *jí*.

The *yǔlù* documents Shíwèi's thirty-year career at **Sūzhōu Dèngwèishān Shèngēnsì** 蘇州鄧蔚山聖恩寺 (commonly called **Wànfēng** 萬峰), the late-Yuán / early-Míng Línjì-Yángqí center in the Sūzhōu region. *notBefore* = ca. **1351** (post-Qiān-yán-acquaintance opening of *fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1381** (death). Catalog dynasty 明 — but Shíwèi's career began in the Yuán and the canonical edition is a Míng reprint. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B492.

**Contents.** Single juan: *kān ... jiùxù* 刊...舊序 ("preface to the reprint of the old edition"); *Běnjìng jūshì rùshān qiúyǔ shìzhòng* (instruction to lay disciple Běnjìng coming up the mountain to seek the dharma); *sònggǔ*; an extensive *fǎyǔ* section addressed by name to many Yuán-Míng-transition Chán contemporaries — **Xuěshān Xī chánzhǎng** 雪山熙長老, **Yǎngshān Wúniàn Xué shǒuzuò** 仰山無念學首座 (his fǎsì), **Rìzhào Huì shǒuzuò** 日照慧首座, **Bǎofēng Zhēn shǒuzuò** 寶峰真首座, **Hǔqiū Yuán shǒuzuò** 虎丘圓首座, plus image-encomia and other materials.

## Translations and research

- 蕭志濱 etc.: studies on the **Qiān-yán Yuán-cháng** → **Wàn-fēng Shí-wèi** Yuán-Míng-transition lineage have been done in Chinese-language scholarship. The Wàn-fēng line is one of the foundational continuities from late-Yuán **Hǔ-pǎo** Línjì into the Míng-Qīng **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** revival, since Mì-yún himself traced lineage back to Wàn-fēng.

## Other points of interest

The *yǔlù* is significant within the Jiāxīng Canon as one of the few Yuán-Míng-transition Línjì-Yángqí *yǔlù* preserved alongside the predominantly post-1644 cluster. **Wànfēng Shíwèi** is the great-grandfather (in the lineage sense) of **Mìyún Yuánwù** 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642) — the foundational figure of the late-Míng Línjì revival; the unbroken line **Qiānyán Yuáncháng → Wànfēng Shíwèi → Bǎocáng Pǔchí → Dōngmíng Huìjǎn → Hǎizhōu Cí → Bàoēn Jìn → Tiānqí Běnruì → Wúwén Cōng → Xiàoyán Débǎo** continues to Mìyún and then through the entire late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí establishment whose *yǔlù* fills J27–J40 of the Jiāxīng Canon.
