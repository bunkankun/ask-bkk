# Xù gǔ zūnsù yǔyào mùlù 續古尊宿語要目錄

*Table of Contents for the Continued Essential Sayings of the Ancient Venerable Masters* — another of [[道忠|Mujaku Dōchū]]'s 無著道忠 table-of-contents scholarly works, this one for the continuation-anthology of the classical *Gǔ zūnsù yǔyào*. See [[KR6q0260]] and [[KR6q0262]] for context on Dōchū's *mùlù* scholarship. *NotBefore* 1680, *notAfter* 1744.
