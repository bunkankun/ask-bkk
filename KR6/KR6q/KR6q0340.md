# Xūzhōu Pǔdù chánshī yǔlù 虛舟普度禪師語錄

One-juan Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán *yǔlù* of **Xūzhōu Pǔdù** [[普度]] 虛舟普度 (1199 – Zhìyuán 17.4.24 / 31 May 1280, *shì* 82, *xià* 68), Yángqí-branch Línjì master, dharma-heir of Huázàng Wúdé Juétōng 華藏無得覺通 (the line descending from Mìān through a different branch than Wújùn's). *Xuzangjing* X71 n1407 (and Jiāxīng). Compiled by four dharma-heirs led by [[淨伏]] Jìngfú (Hǔyán Jìngfú 虎巖淨伏). Eight abbacy records: Jiànkāng Bànshān Bàoníng 半山報寧 (from Chúnyòu 1 / 1241), Zhènjiāng Jīnshān Lóngyóu 金山龍游, Tánzhōu Lùyuàn Bāozhōng 鹿苑褒忠, Fǔzhōu Shūshān Báiyún 踈山白雲, Píngjiāng Chéngtiān Néngrén 承天能仁, Lín'ān Zhōngtiānzhú Tiānníng Wànshòu 中天竺天寧萬壽 (from Jǐngdìng era), Lín'ān Língyǐn Jǐngdé 靈隱景德, and Lín'ān Jìngshān Xīngshèng Wànshòu 徑山興聖萬壽 (from Zhìyuán 14 / 1277). Plus *xiǎocān*, *jìsòng*, *zhēnzàn*, *zìzàn*, *xiǎo fóshì*, and closing *xíngzhuàng*. Per DILA: surname Shǐ 史, native of Wéiyáng Jiāngdū 維揚江都; tonsured at twelve at Tiānníngsì; five years with Lóngxī Zǔxìn 龍溪祖信; received dharma-transmission from Wúdé. Seven dharma-heirs named: Hǔyán Jìngfú, Yōngsǒu Shízhōng 庸叟時中, Zhúxī Miàotǎn 竺西妙坦, and four others.
