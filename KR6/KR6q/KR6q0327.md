# Duànqiáo Miàolún chánshī yǔlù 斷橋妙倫禪師語錄

Two-juan late-Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Duànqiáo Miàolún** [[妙倫]] 斷橋妙倫 (1201–1261), dharma-heir of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Compiled by the attendants [[文寶]] Wénbǎo and [[善靖]] Shànjìng. *Xuzangjing* X70 n1394. Preface by Lín Xīyì 林希逸 dated Xiánchún 1 *rénrì* (1265) describes the recovery of the text from worm-eaten fragments at Bòshān 檗山, then a completed copy. Four abbacy records: Táizhōu Ruìfēng Zhǐyuán 瑞峯祇園, Táizhōu Ruìyán Jìngtǔ 瑞巖淨土, Tiāntái Guóqīng Jiàozhōng 國清教忠, and Jìngcí Bàoēn Guāngxiào 淨慈報恩光孝 (Lín'ān). Juan 2 *fǎyǔ*, *fózǔ zàn*, *zìzàn*, *jìsòng*, *xiǎo fóshì*, and *xíngzhuàng*. Miàolún is the teacher of Gāofēng Yuánmiào 高峰原妙, whose line via Zhōngfēng Míngběn 中峰明本 dominates later Yuán-Míng Línjì.
