# Míngzhōu Tiāntóng Jǐngdé Chánsì Hóngzhì Jué chánshī yǔlù 明州天童景德禪寺宏智覺禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hóng-zhì Jué of Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé Chán-sì in Míng-zhōu*
by [[正覺]] (說), [[淨啟]] (重編)

## About the work

Four-juan abridged-and-reconstituted *yǔlù* of **Hóngzhì Zhèngjué** [[正覺]] 宏智正覺 (1091–1157), the Southern-Sòng Cáodòng patriarch and principal proponent of *mòzhào chán* 默照禪 (silent-illumination), abbot of **Tiāntóng Jǐngdé Chánsì** 天童景德禪寺 in **Míngzhōu** 明州 (= modern Níngbō) for nearly thirty years from c. 1131 to his **Shàoxīng 27 / 10/8 = 1157-11-18** death. *Chóngbiān* 重編 (re-edited) by the early-Qīng Cáodòng master **Jìngqǐ** [[淨啟]] (d. **1674-10-15**) — DILA-affiliated as 多福淨啟 / 妙叶淨啟 of Tiáoxī, dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂. Jiāxīng Canon J32 B272 (note: in *Jiāxīng* volume 32, distinct from the J29 cluster of contemporary 1660s–70s *yǔlù*). Front-preface by **Yìtíng jūshì** **Xú Jìēn** 逸亭居士徐繼恩 (Cíxī, Zhèjiāng), undated. Contents: juan 1 — *shàngtáng* / *wǎncān* / *shìzhòng*; juan 2 — *jǔgǔ*; juan 3 — *sònggǔ*; juan 4 — *jīyuán* / *tǎmíng* / *míng* / *jì* / *jì*.

### Abstract

**Status as recension.** Distinct from the canonical 9-juan 《宏智禪師廣錄》 ([[KR6q0070]] / T48 No. 2001), which preserves the integral Sòng-era recension compiled by Zhèngjué's chief dharma-heir [[宗法]] **Sìzōng** etc. The present 4-juan volume is a Qīng-period reconstitution by **Qíngyún fǎxiōng** 晴雲法兄 (likely Jìngqǐ in alternate-name guise; see [[淨啟]] note) of material that had become scattered (*sǎnwáng* 散亡) over the *shùbǎi nián* 數百年 since Shàoxīng. Xú Jìēn's preface explicitly frames the volume as a **gathering from miscellaneous external sources** rather than a fresh transmission of the canonical text: *Qíngyún fǎxiōng yú tā jiā zájiàn zhě, guǎng wéi sōují* 晴雲法兄於他家雜見者廣為搜緝.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1131** (Zhèngjué's accession to Tiāntóng); *notAfter* = **1157** (his death) — for the underlying speaker. The Qīng compiler [[淨啟]] died **1674**, providing a *terminus ante quem* for the Jiāxīng Canon imprint of c. 1670–73.

**Doctrinal significance.** Preserves Cáodòng silent-illumination *fǎyǔ* and *jǔgǔ / sònggǔ* material from the Tiāntóng *fǎxí*. The 4-juan structure (vs. the 9-juan canonical edition) emphasizes the *jǔgǔ* (juan 2) and *sònggǔ* (juan 3) selections — the two genres where Hóngzhì's verse-precedent set most influenced subsequent Cáodòng practice (the *Cóngróng lù* 從容錄 [[KR6q0079]] of Wànsōng Xíngxiù being the most prominent expansion of Hóngzhì's *sònggǔ bǎi zé*).

**Cáo-dòng-line significance.** Jìngqǐ's *zhòngbiān* sits within the broader early-Qīng Cáo-dòng-line revival of Sòng-era patriarchal materials by figures such as [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (Tiānjiè), [[元賢]] 永覺元賢 (Gǔshān), and others — a deliberate Cáodòng counter-movement to the Línjì-line publishing of the time. The Tiāntóngsì connection is particularly resonant: by the 1660s the seat had been held for several generations by the Mì-yún-line Línjì masters ([[圓悟]] Mìyún Yuánwù; [[道忞]] Mùchén Dàomín; cf. [[KR6q0457]]), and recovering Hóngzhì's Tiāntóng *yǔlù* served as a Cáodòng claim on the seat's deeper historical pedigree.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon Sòng-text imprint (J32 B272). The Xú Jìēn preface provides the principal documentation of the Qīng-era reconstitution.*

## Translations and research

- For Hóng-zhì's biography and doctrine: Morten Schlütter, *How Zen Became Zen* (Hawaii 2008) — the principal English-language treatment of the silent-illumination / *kàn-huà* dispute and Hóng-zhì's central role.
- Taigen Dan Leighton, *Cultivating the Empty Field: The Silent Illumination of Zen Master Hongzhi* (1st ed. 1991) — translation of selected *jì*, *míng*, and *fǎ-yǔ*.
- See also [[KR6q0070]] 《宏智禪師廣錄》 (the canonical 9-juan recension) and [[KR6q0079]] 《從容錄》 (the *sòng-gǔ bǎi zé* expansion).

## Other points of interest

- **Cross-referenced with [[KR6q0070]].** This is the second of two extant *yǔlù* recensions of Hóngzhì in the Kanripo corpus. The 9-juan T48 No. 2001 is canonical; this 4-juan J32 B272 is the abridged Qīng reconstitution. Material in this 4-juan volume is partly overlapping with, partly supplementary to, [[KR6q0070]].
- **Tiāntóng pedigree-claim.** Jìngqǐ's recovery of Hóngzhì at Tiāntóng can be read as a 17th-century Cáo-dòng-line **historical-pedigree claim** on the same Tiāntóngsì then under Línjì-line institutional control — particularly poignant given the Cáodòng / Línjì doctrinal disputes from Hóngzhì / Dàhuì Zōnggǎo's own time.
