# Cáoyuán Dàoshēng chánshī yǔlù 曹源道生禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Cáoyuán Dàoshēng* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Cáoyuán Dàoshēng** 曹源道生. Compiled by attendant (*shìzhě* 侍者) [[道冲|Dàochōng]] 道冲.

X70 n1375. Note: this Dàochōng is likely distinct from the Míng-Qīng transitional 道沖 who edited [[KR6q0206]] *Jī lèi jí*; the name-variant 冲/沖 and the lifetime-spread (Southern Sòng vs. Qīng) distinguish them.
