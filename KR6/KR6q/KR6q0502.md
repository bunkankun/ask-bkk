# Shípú Zhì chánshī yǔlù 石璞質禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shí-pú Zhì*
by [[超質]] (說), [[明嵩]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Shípú Chāozhì** [[超質]] 石璞超質, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fógǔ Xíngwén** [[行聞]] 佛古行聞 (1610–1666). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Míngsōng** [[明嵩]] *děng biān*. Documented seats: **Pínghú Cáoxī Chányuàn** 嘉興平湖曹溪禪院 (1681 entry); **Tàicāng Xiānglín Chányuàn** 姑蘇太倉香林禪院 (1682 entry) — succeeding to his master's two principal seats. *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B365.
