# Jīngnán Nèi Hùguósì Qǐzhēn Chéng chánshī yǔlù 荊南內護國寺啟真誠禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qǐ-zhēn Chéng of Nèi Hù-guó-sì in Jīng-nán*
by [[誠]] (說), [[振聞]] (編)

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Qǐzhēn Chéng** [[誠]] 啟真誠, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hùguó Biànzhī = Biéfēng Xiù** 別峰秀 in the Wǔ-zǔ-line Línjì sub-tradition. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhènwén** [[振聞]] *biān*. Sole abbacy at **Jīngnán Nèi Hùguósì** 荊南內護國寺. Juan 3 includes the *Ēnmǔ chuán* (mother-biography). *notBefore* / *notAfter*: c. 1670–1700. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B395.
