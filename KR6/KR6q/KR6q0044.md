# Jiànzhōu hóng shì lù 建州弘釋錄

*Record of Glorifying the Dharma in Jiànzhōu*

compiled by [[元賢]] Yuánxián (集, sobriquet Yǒngjué 永覺, 1578–1657), late Míng / early Qīng

## About the work

A two-juan regional Buddhist record covering eminent monks of the Jiànzhōu 建州 region (modern north-central Fújiàn 福建), compiled by Yǒngjué Yuánxián as a local-history complement to his lamp-record continuation *Jìdēng lù* ([[KR6q0038]]). Title — "Record of Glorifying the Dharma in Jiànzhōu" — frames it as a regional commemoration.

### Abstract

Yuánxián was abbot at Gǔshān Yǒngqiánsì 鼓山湧泉寺 in Fúzhōu and the central figure of seventeenth-century Cáodòng Chán in Fújiàn; the *Jiànzhōu hóng shì lù* gathers biographies and recorded sayings of Buddhist figures associated with the region, both Chán and other traditions. Useful as a primary source for late-Míng Fújiàn Buddhism.
