# Yīngníng Jìng chánshī yǔlù 攖寧靜禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yīng-níng Jìng*
by [[智靜]] (說), [[德亮]] (等編), [[德莪]] (等錄)

## About the work

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Yīngníng Zhìjìng** [[智靜]] 攖寧智靜, native of **Shānyīn** 山陰 (Shàoxīng). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Déliàng** [[德亮]] *děng biān*; recorded by **Déé** [[德莪]] *děng lù*. Front-matter has illustrations (*<img:>*) and prefaces (some images-only in the source-files). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B286.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA A019266 and the juan-1 *xù*: trained extensively under [[圓悟]] **Mìyún Yuánwù** at Tiāntóng and **Dōngshān Ěrmì** 東山爾密 (1590–1642). Decisive *xǐng* on the candle-extinguishment exchange with Ěrmì when an attendant accidentally put out a *liúlí dēng* 琉璃燈 lamp and Ěrmì cried *huǒ niè* 火聻 ("where is the fire?") with the attendant's reply *yǒu zài zhèlǐ* 有在這裡 ("right here"). Final transmission via **Miánshí Jìngyùn** 眠石淨蘊 at **Tiāntái Hùguósì** 天台護國寺 (the *chéng sì Hùguó* 承嗣護國 reference). Three documented abbacies: **Jíshān Jièzhūsì** 蕺山戒珠寺 in Shàoxīng (his native region); **Wújiāng Yùzhǐān** 吳江喻指菴 (Sūzhōu prefecture); **Líchuān Luóhànsì** 黎川羅漢寺 (Jiāngxī). Lifedates not preserved in DILA.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1635** (post-Mì-yún / Dōngshān Ěrmì training period; both teachers died 1642). *notAfter* = **1670** as a conservative bound for the post-decease compilation by Déliàng + Déé.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school heir of Miánshí Jìngyùn at Tiāntái Hùguó (a Cáodòng seat); the cross-school training with Línjì-line Mìyún and Ěrmì illustrates the cross-lineage practice common in the late-Míng / early-Qīng period.

## Other points of interest

- **Jíshān Jièzhūsì.** The Jíshān Jièzhūsì in Shàoxīng was a noted Wáng Xīzhī 王羲之 calligraphy site (the *jièzhū tàizǐ* 戒珠太子 stone). Zhìjìng's abbacy there continued the literati-Buddhist tradition of the Yánshān region.
- **Cross-school training pattern.** The Línjì → Cáodòng training-trajectory (Mìyún / Ěrmì → Miánshí Jìngyùn) parallels that documented elsewhere in this Jiāxīng cluster (cf. [[KR6q0455]], [[KR6q0464]]) — illustrating the porous late-Míng / early-Qīng cross-school pedagogy.
