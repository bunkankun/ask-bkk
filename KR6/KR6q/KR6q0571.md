# Báiyún Xiànglín Běnzhēn chánshī yǔlù 白雲象林本真禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn of Bái-yún [-sì]*
by [[本真]] (說), [[照水]] (重梓), [[妙心]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Xiànglín Běnzhēn** [[本真]] 象林本真 (**1620 – 1691** summer, age 72), Línjì-Yángqí lineage. Native of **Suízhōu** 隨州 (Húběi). *Zhòngzǐ* (re-engraved) by his sole *fǎsì* **Xǐxīn Zhàoshuǐ** [[照水]] (subject of [[KR6q0572]]); preface by lay disciple **Miàoxīn** [[妙心]] dated **順治巳亥歲佛歡喜日** = **1659-08-30** (Yúlánpén day).

Documents teaching at his principal seat **Qízhōu Zǐyúnshān Báiyún chánsì** 蘄州紫雲山白雲禪寺 (entered **順治甲午仲冬初** = **1654-12**). The compact one-juan extent likely represents an earlier curated selection rather than the full output of his 37-year preaching career. *notBefore* = **1654** (Báiyún entry); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-1691-death *zhòngzǐ*). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B457.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface (Miàoxīn, 1659); *niānsòng* on classic *gōngàn* (世尊初生, 女子出定, 世尊拈花, 殃崛救產難, 德山托缽, 百丈野狐, 南泉斬貓, 靈雲見桃花悟道因緣); *jì* including verses to and from his disciples (與洗心水西堂 to [[照水]]) and contemporary monks (秋浦, 雲衲明, 自覺, 此藥, 佛月照, 竹菴 lay-Zhào jūshì), plus *fóshì* and a closing *xíngshí*.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0571]] and the immediately following [[KR6q0572]] form a teacher-disciple pair (Běnzhēn → Zhàoshuǐ), parallel to the [[KR6q0569]]–[[KR6q0570]] pair (Xínggāo → Chāoyuán). The same disciple Zhàoshuǐ ([[照水]]) earlier re-edited his *tonsure-teacher's* collection [[KR6q0234]] 《正覺潤光澤禪師澡雪集》 — i.e. Zhàoshuǐ functioned as the textual-archivist for both his ordination-line and his transmission-line, an unusual editorial double role.
