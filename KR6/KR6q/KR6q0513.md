# Shéndǐng Yīkuí chánshī yǔlù 神鼎一揆禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shén-dǐng Yī-kuí*
by [[元揆]] (說), [[成炯]] (等編)

Twelve-juan *yǔlù* of **Yīkuí Yuánkuí** [[元揆]] 一揆元揆 (b. **1634-05-23**, alive 1697 age 64), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yuǎnān Běntǎn** [[本僼]] (1622–1682) at Tiāntóng. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Yùtáng Chéngjiǒng** [[成炯]] *děng biān*. Five abbacies: **月掌壽聖** → **京山永隆** → **神鼎資聖** → **瑞巖** (succession) → **天童** (succession). The title-form 神鼎 echoes the Sòng-era **Hóngyīn Yīngshàn** 紅印應禪 / **Shéndǐng Hóngyīn** 神鼎洪諲 lineage. *notBefore* = c. 1670 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1697 (last attestation alive). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B388.
