# Huànzhù Míng chánshī yǔlù 幻住明禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huàn-zhù Míng*
by [[明]] (說), [[清尚]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Huànzhù Míng** [[明|Huànzhù Míng]] 幻住明 (lifedates unknown, active **1660–1694**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěrdú Dàotài** 耳毒道泰 in the Pòshān Hǎimíng line. Native of **Lúzhōu** 瀘州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Zēng** 曾. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Qīngshàng** [[清尚]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at four Sìchuān (Dōng-Chuān region) abbacies: **Línshuǐxiàn Xiānrénshān Guānyīn chányuàn** 鄰水縣仙人山觀音禪院 (entered **順治庚子冬** = **1660** winter); **Dàzhúxiàn Wànfēngshān Tàipíng chánsì** 大竹縣萬峰山太平禪寺; **Wànxiàn Xīnglóng chánsì** 萬縣興隆禪寺; and **Shèhóng Huìlíngsì** 射洪會靈寺. Preface dated **康熙甲戌歲冬杪除日** = **1694-12** at **Fánlíng** 樊陵 (lay anchorite). *notBefore* = **1660**; *notAfter* = **1694**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B432.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface, *mùcì*, three abbacy *yǔlù* (Guānyīn 1660 winter; Tàipíng 1661 winter; Xīnglóng 1663 winter and 1664 mid-summer). Juan 2: *xiǎocān*; *cháhuà*; *shìzhòng*; *jīyuán*; *sònggǔ* on classic *gōngàn* (晦堂與黃山谷論吾無隱乎爾話, plus *sìwēiyí*); *liánfāngjì* (with the verse-bestowal poems for 天然 and 水映 *fǎsì*); *fǎyǔ*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔlù* extends the Pòshān → Ěrdú Dàotài branch of the Sìchuān Línjì-Yángqí lineage into a third generation; the four Dōng-Chuān (eastern Sìchuān) abbacies — Línshuǐ, Dàzhú, Wànxiàn, Shèhóng — fill in important monastic geography otherwise less well-attested in the Sì-chuān-cluster Jiā-xīng-canon entries.
