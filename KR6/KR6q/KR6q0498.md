# Bǎiyú chánshī yǔlù 百愚禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bǎi-yú*
by [[淨斯]] (說), [[智操]] (等編語錄), [[智海]] (等編語錄)

Twenty-juan *yǔlù* of **Bǎiyú Jìngsī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (**1610-07-11 – 1665-10-06**, *shìshòu* 56), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Ruìbái Míngxuě** [[明雪]] 瑞白明雪 (1584–1641). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìcāo** [[智操]] + **Zhìhǎi** [[智海]] *děng biānyǔlù*. Eight abbacies in Jiāngnán: **Jiādìng Zhāoqìng** → **Qīnglóng Lóngfú** → **Biànshān Lónghuá** → **Yuèzhōu Néngrén** → **Yúnmén Xiǎnshèng** (Cáodòng flagship) → **Gāotíng Fórì** → **Guóshān Shànquán** → **Jiāngdū Shànqìng**. 42 named heirs. Juan 20 includes the *tǎzhìmíng*. *notBefore* = c. 1640 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1665 (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B359.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J36 B359). Juan 20 *tǎzhìmíng* is the principal biographical witness.*
