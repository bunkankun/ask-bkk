# Pǔmén Xiǎn chánshī yǔlù 普門顯禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Pǔ-mén Xiǎn*
by [[燈顯]] (說), [[性胤]] (錄)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Pǔmén Dēngxiǎn** [[燈顯]] 普門燈顯 (lifedates unknown), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668) — thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]] ([[KR6q0554]]), [[玄億]] ([[KR6q0555]]), [[至善]] ([[KR6q0556]]), [[幻敏]] ([[KR6q0592]]) in the **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line. Native of **Fúlíng** 涪陵 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Xià** 夏. Recorded by his *fǎsì* **Xìngyìn** [[性胤]] *lù*. Documents teaching at **Fúlíng Huílóngsì** 涪陵迴龍寺 and **Tóngbǎi Tánhuásì** 桐柏曇華寺.

*notBefore* ≈ **1668** (post-Tiě-bì-death abbacy onset); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-printing). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B485.

**Contents.** Juan 1: *zhū-shān qǐng-qǐ* (multiple *qǐng-qǐ* invitations); *pǔ-shuō*; *xiǎo-cān*; *fǎ-yǔ*; *jī-yuán*. Juan 2: *fó-shì*; *zàn-jì* (image-encomia for Bodhidharma, Avalokiteśvara, his dharma-grandfather **Jù-yún Lǎo-rén** 聚雲師翁 = 聚雲吹萬, his teacher **Qìng-zhōng Lǎo-rén** 慶忠老人; landscape-image *xiàng-zàn* including 四景畫軸; *fǎ-yǔ* to disciples (示鎮九文學, 示舌端上座, 示睿珍上座, etc.); closing **塔銘** preserving lineage and abbacy details.
