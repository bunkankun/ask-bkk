# Yúnfù Zhì chánshī yǔlù 雲腹智禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yún-fù Zhì*
by [[道智]] (說), [[嶽賢]] (記錄), [[聯昇]] (記錄)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Yúnfù Dàozhì** [[道智]] 雲腹道智 (**1613 – 1673-07-13**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiàngyái Xìngtǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (1598–1651) in the Pòshān-Sìchuān sub-line. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Yuèxián** [[嶽賢]] + **Liánshēng** [[聯昇]]. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B309. Juan 2 includes the *xíngzhuàng* preserving birth and death dates.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[道智]] note: native of **Qúxiàn** 渠縣 (Shùnqìng, Sìchuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Conception preceded by mother's dream of a *yìsēng tuōbō* 異僧托缽 — a standard *xìngshēng* topos. Born 1613, died 1673 *zuòtuō* 端坐而逝.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1650** (post-transmission preaching, after his teacher Xìngtǐng died 1651). *notAfter* = **1673** (death). 

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Xiàngyái Xìngtǐng → Yúnfù Dàozhì. Heirs: **Huìyě Yìxǐng** 會也益惺; **Huìyuè Yìyǎn** 慧月益眼.
