# Yīchū Yuán chánshī yǔlù 一初元禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yī-chū Yuán*
by [[元]] (說), [[真開]] (等編), [[真智]] (等編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Yīchū Yuán** [[元]] 一初元 (= **Yīchū Wùyuán** 一初悟元, previously documented at [[KR6q0416]]) — dharma-brother of [[悟進|Jièān Wùjìn]] (at [[KR6q0443]]) and co-heir of [[妙用|Yuānhú Miàoyòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (cf. [[KR6q0416]]) in the Cáodòng Wúqù Rúkōng sub-line. Catalog-form **元** 元 (distinct from [[KR6q0421]] **Bǎichī Yuán** 百癡元 = the Fèiyǐn Línjì-line heir, same single-character *fǎhuì*). *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Wùyuán** 悟元, *hào* **Yīchū** 一初 ("First"). Held abbacy at **Púxī Yǒngzhèng Chánsì** 濮溪永正禪寺 from **戊子 1648**, parallel to dharma-brother Wùjìn's 1648 Jīnmíngsì entry. Compiled by dharma-heirs **Zhēnkāi** [[真開]] 真開 and **Zhēnzhì** [[真智]] 真智 — 真-generation heirs. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B234.

### Abstract

**Context.** [[KR6q0444]] is the companion-volume to [[KR6q0443]] 《介菴進禪師語錄》 — the two Miàoyòng dharma-heirs 介菴 and 一初 each produced their own *yǔlù* volumes, collected sequentially in Jiāxīng Canon J29 (B233 = Jièān's 10 juan; B234 = Yīchū's 2 juan). For the broader biographical context of the Cáodòng Wúqù / Nán-míng-line transmission, see [[KR6q0416]] and [[KR6q0443]]. Yī-chū-specific biographical material is thinner than his dharma-brother's.

**Abbacy.** **Púxī Yǒngzhèng Chánsì** 濮溪永正禪寺 from **戊子 = 1648** on lay-patron petition. The **Púxī** 濮溪 location is in the Jiāxīng area.

**Compositional history.** Opening preface by **Shībó** 施博 (*hòuxué* 後學; cf. [[KR6q0432]], [[KR6q0433]]) — undated, but consistent with a c. 1676–85 cutting-date. Shībó's preface positions the two dharma-brothers as parallel post-Miào-yòng inheritors: "*wǔ chuán zhì jīn zé yǒu Jièān Yīchū liǎng shī chū yān, Jièān héshàng zhǔfǎ Jīnmíng zhūfāng yǎngzhòng; Yīchū héshàng yú wùzǐ nián wèi tányuè qǐng jū Púxī Yǒngzhèng* 四傳至今則有介菴一初兩師出焉介菴和尚主法金明諸方仰重一初和尚於戊子年為檀越請居濮溪永正". The second preface (unread in my extract) continues this theme. *notBefore* = **1648** (Púxī abbacy); *notAfter* = **1685** conservatively.

**Contents.** Two juan of **shàngtáng + xiǎocān + jīyuán + niānsòng** 上堂 + 小參 + 機緣 + 拈頌 material.

## Other points of interest

- **Paired with [[KR6q0443]] as Miàoyòng's two-heir monuments.** [[KR6q0443]] (介菴悟進, 10 juan) + [[KR6q0444]] (一初悟元, 2 juan) preserve the matched *yǔlù* pair for Miàoyòng's two principal heirs, with Jièān's being substantially more extensive — reflecting Jièān's longer-tenure primary abbacy at Jīnmíng versus Yīchū's more modest Púxī tenure. Both 1648 abbacy-start dates and Jiāxīng Canon sequential placement reflect the editors' deliberate pairing.
- **名 disambiguation 元.** The catalog-form **元** in this entry refers to **Yīchū Wùyuán** (Cáodòng Wúqù sub-line), not to be confused with [[KR6q0421]]'s **Bǎichī Yuán** (Línjì Fèiyǐn sub-line). Two distinct Chán masters with the same single-character dharma-name in the same Jiāxīng Canon cluster.
