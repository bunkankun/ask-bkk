# Dábiàn Quán chánshī yǔlù 達變權禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dá-biàn Quán*
by [[顯權]] (說), [[海澂]] (錄)

## About the work

Five-juan *yǔlù* of **Dábiàn Xiǎnquán** [[顯權]] 達變顯權 (DILA standardized; *zì* Dábiàn, with the catalog form retaining the single character 權 from *Quán*), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Mùchén Shānwēng Dàomín** [[道忞]] 木陳山翁道忞 (1596–1674) at **Tiāntóngsì** 天童寺. Recorded by *shìzhě* attendant **Hǎichéng** [[海澂]]. Front-preface by *tóngmén fǎdì* **Bīngān Zhāng Lìlián** 冰菴張立廉 of **Lùchéng** 鹿城 (= Zhènhǎi 鎮海, modern Níngbō coastal area), undated. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B248. Contents: *xù*; juan 1 *zhù Míngzhōu Cíxīxiàn Wǔlěishān Língfēng Chánsì yǔlù* — entry-sermon at the Wǔlěishān **Língfēng Chánsì** 五磊山靈峰禪寺 abbacy in Cíxī (Míngzhōu, modern Níngbō prefecture); subsequent juan with *shàngtáng* / *xiǎocān* / *fǎyǔ* / *jīyuán* / *fóshì* arrangements, in standard Línjì *yǔlù* organization.

### Abstract

**Dating.** No precise dated entries in the corpus. *notBefore* = **1660** (conservative bound for Xiǎnquán's Língfēng abbacy, which post-dates his Tiāntóng *fēnzuò* role under Dàomín who returned from his **1659–60 imperial summons** to the Shùnzhì palace). *notAfter* = **1674** (Xiǎnquán is recorded as having predeceased Dàomín, who himself died **Kāngxī 13 = 1674**; the preface phrase *jí shī shùnshì ér jǐnrán shānghuái* 及師順世而盡然傷懷 confirms posthumous compilation). The Zhāng Lìlián preface refers to Dàomín's personal sponsorship of the publication after Xiǎnquán's death: *jīn fù shōushí yílù wéi shī diǎnchū mòhòu guāngmíng* 今復收拾遺錄為師點出末後光明 — a rare instance of a master compiling a deceased disciple's *yǔlù*.

**Authorship.** Per the Zhāng Lìlián preface and DILA authority A001978: native of **Fēngchéng** 豐城 (Jiāngxī), lay surname **Wáng** 王. First training at **Tiāntóngsì** under **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] before Yuánwù's 1642 death (*shǎo shì xiān Tiāntóng zhuīfú* 少侍先天童椎拂, where *xiān Tiāntóng* = "the late Tiāntóng [abbot]" = Yuánwù), then formal *fùfǎ* under Dàomín on Dàomín's succession to the Tiāntóng seat. *Shìshòu* **52**. Praised in the preface as *yányì fú wù lèi Xiù Tiěmiàn* 嚴毅服物類秀鐵面 (severe like Xiù Tiěmiàn), *tuōluò xínghái lèi Guǎng wúxīn* 脫落形骸類廣無心 (free of form like Guǎng Wúxīn), *cǎoyī fēishí qīngkǔ liànxíng lèi Xiànggǔ tóutuó* 草衣菲食清苦鍊行類象骨頭陀 (austere like Xiànggǔ Tóutuó), *diǎnsī zhòngwù wǎng xiè cānchéng lèi Shíshuāng jiānsì* 典司眾務罔懈參承類石霜監寺 (diligent in office like Shíshuāng's monastic-supervisor) — a four-fold encomium framing his ascetic and disciplinary character.

**Lineage and seat.** Xiǎnquán's Língfēngsì at **Wǔlěishān** 五磊山 in Cíxī is the same Cíxī monastery preserved in the contemporary 嘉興 publication network. Sole recorded dharma-heir: **Wǔlěi Língshān Tiānyīn Cháo** 五磊靈山天音朝.

## Other points of interest

- **Posthumous *yǔlù* compiled by the master.** The preface's note that **Dàomín** himself collected and sponsored the publication of his predeceased dharma-heir's *yílù* 遺錄 ("remaining records") is unusual — most Jiāxīng Canon *yǔlù* are compiled by surviving disciples. Dàomín's personal involvement (described in the preface as expressing *tiān sàng yǔ* 天喪予, the Confucian formula of master-mourning-disciple from *Lúnyǔ* 11.9) gives this volume a distinctive elegiac character.
- **Zhāng Lìlián as 同門.** The preface-writer's self-styling as *tóngmén fǎdì* 同門法弟 indicates Zhāng was also a Mùchén Dàomín disciple — though listed in the catalog as preface-writer rather than as a published Chán *yǔlù* author, suggesting lay-Chán affiliation rather than monastic ordination.
