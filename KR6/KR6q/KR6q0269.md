# Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi chánshī guǎnglù 百丈懷海禪師廣錄

*Extended Record of Chán Master Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi* — the expanded-recension *yǔlù* of [[懷海|Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi]] 百丈懷海 (720–814); parallel to [[KR6q0268]] *Bǎizhàng Huáihǎi chánshī yǔlù*. Constitutes juan 3 of the *Sìjiā yǔlù*.

## About the work

One-juan Táng Chán *guǎnglù* ("extended record"), X69 n1323. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The *guǎnglù* preserves a more expansive selection of Bǎizhàng's teachings than the companion *yǔlù* [[KR6q0268]]; see that entry for context.

Dating: *notBefore* 820; *notAfter* c. 900.
