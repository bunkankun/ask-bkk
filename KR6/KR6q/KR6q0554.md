# Héngshān chánshī yǔlù 衡山禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Héng-shān*
by [[燈炳]] (說), [[宗位]] (編), [[超源]] (序), [[藍理]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Héngshān Dēngbǐng** [[燈炳]] 衡山燈炳 (also **Qìngyún Bǐng** 慶雲炳) (**1611-05-22 – 1680-07-13**, age 70), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 — a senior figure in the Sìchuān **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line, dharma-grandson of **Jùyún Chuīwàn** 聚雲吹萬 of Bǎofēng. Native of **Héjiāng** 合江 (Sìchuān). Compiled by his *fǎsūn* (dharma-grandson) **Zōngwèi** [[宗位]] *biān*, with the manuscript carried south for engraving by his third-generation grandson **Jǐhuàn chánrén** 几幻禪人 with the support of his Pǔtuó dharma-cousin **Biéān** 別菴和尚.

Two prefaces are preserved in juan 1: by 龍池後裔閩莆 **Chāoyuán Dànyá** [[超源]] (Mǐnpǔ, of the Lóngchí Línjì line) dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; and by **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] (1649–1720), then *zhènshǒu Zhèjiāng děngchù dìfāng Dìnghǎi zǒngbīngguān zuǒ dūdū* (Naval Commander of Dìnghǎi, Zhèjiāng), dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1.

The first abbacy is dated explicitly: Dēngbǐng entered **Kuízhōufǔ Shízhù Jíxiáng chánsì** 夔州府石砫吉祥禪寺 on **順治壬辰七月望日** = **1652-08-19** on the invitation of **Sū Màoyǔ** 蘇茂宇 of 祇園檀越; later abbacy at **Qìngyún chánsì** 慶雲禪寺 (giving him his second sobriquet). *notBefore* = **1652** (Jíxiángsì entry); *notAfter* = **1696** (latest preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B438.

**Contents.** Single juan: two prefaces; *xiǎocān*; *shìzhòng*; *fǎyǔ*; *jīyuán*; *wèndá*; *fóshì* (cremation/funeral verses for **Jùyún Wànzǔ** 聚雲萬祖, lay-patron Sū Màoyǔ, fellow monk Sīxiū, *wéinuó* Xīnchí, etc.); *tízàn* (image-encomia for his teacher Chuīwàn 吹萬師翁, Bodhidharma 達磨渡江/隻履西歸, etc.); closing materials including the *tǎmíng* with his birth and death dates.

## Other points of interest

The *yǔlù*'s fifteen-year publishing delay (1680 death → 1695–1696 prefaces) reflects the post-Sānfān-luàn (1681) rebuilding of southwestern monastic networks; the manuscript was carried "**south**" by **Jǐhuàn** 几幻 (a third-generation descendant) to the major eastern publishing center, with **Pǔtuóshān** as the staging-ground via **Biéān**. The military-commander preface by **Lán Lǐ** is unusual within Buddhist *yǔlù*: by 1696 Lán Lǐ was famous for his conspicuous role in the 1683 Pénghú sea-battle that ended the Zhèngrégime in Tāiwān, and his preface is one of the rare points of contact between high Qīng military elites and a Sì-chuān-rooted Línjì-Dàhuì lineage *yǔlù*.
