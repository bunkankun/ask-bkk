# Chánmén zhū zǔshī jìsòng 禪門諸祖師偈頌

*Verses-and-Gathas of the Various Patriarch-Masters of the Chán Gate* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán verse-anthology gathering the transmission-verses (*jì* 偈 gathas; *sòng* 頌 verses) of the classical Chán patriarchs from the Indian through the Chinese lineages. Compiled by the Southern-Sòng Chán monks [[子昇|Zǐshēng]] 子昇 and [[如祐|Rúyòu]] 如祐.

## About the work

A two-juan classical Chán verse-anthology, X66 n1298. Non-commentary (an anthology-gathering rather than interpretive commentary); *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text assembles the canonical transmission-verses of the Chán lineage from the Indian patriarchs (beginning with Mahākāśyapa) through the Chinese Six Patriarchs and subsequent generations. The collection provides a compact reference-anthology of the verse-transmission tradition, useful for students needing the canonical lineage-verses in convenient form.

### Abstract

[[子昇|Zǐshēng]] 子昇 and [[如祐|Rúyòu]] 如祐: Southern-Sòng Chán monks, co-compilers. Lifedates unrecorded. Beyond their editorial role on this text, no substantial biographical material is preserved.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1150 (plausible anchor for Southern-Sòng compilation); *notAfter* c. 1250.
