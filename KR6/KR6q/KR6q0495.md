# Qírán Zhì chánshī yǔlù 奇然智禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qí-rán Zhì*
by [[超智]] (說), [[明林]] (等編), [[明燦]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Tiānmù / Qírán Chāozhì** [[超智]] 天目超智 (b. **1626**, alive in 1685), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zìxián Xíngjué** [[覺]] (1611–1662) in the Línyě → Zìxián line. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Xiùyě Mínglín** [[明林]] + **Míngcàn** [[明燦]] *děng biān*. Three abbacies: **Yǒngjiā Tiānwáng**, **Ruìān Xiānyán**, **Kūnyáng Bǎoxīng**. Catalog meta uses the *Qírán* 奇然 *hào* alongside the more common *Tiānmù* 天目 attestation. *notBefore* = c. 1660, *notAfter* = c. 1685. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B356.
