# Lǜluó Héngxiù Lín chánshī yǔlù 綠蘿恒秀林禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Héng-xiù Lín of Lǜ-luó*
by [[發林]] (說), [[光悠]] (等編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Héngxiù Fālín** [[發林]] 恒秀發林, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yúnhuàn Yìnchén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623–1669) in the Pòshān-Sìchuān sub-line. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Guāngyōu** [[光悠]] *děng biān*. Title-form *Lǜluó* 綠蘿 references the **Lǜluóshān** abbacy (location uncertain — likely Yúnnán region). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B355.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Heir of Yúnhuàn Yìnchén; previously compiled his master's [[KR6q0493]] *yǔlù*. Active in 1673 (per Wáng Mínhào reference at [[KR6q0493]]). Successive abbacies after master's 1669 death.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Yúnhuàn Yìnchén → Héngxiù Fālín — Sìchuān-Yúnnán Pòshān-line.
