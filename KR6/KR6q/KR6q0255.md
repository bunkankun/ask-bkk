# Jìngshí dīrǔ jí 徑石滴乳集

*Dripping-Milk Collection from Jìngshí* — a five-juan Qīng Chán-lineage biographical anthology, compiled by the Línjì-Yángqí master [[真在|Shānduó Zhēnzài]] 山鐸真在 (b. 1621), with subsequent continuation (*chóng xù* 重續) by [[機雲|Jīyún]] 機雲. The title's *Jìngshí* 徑石 is a compound name for Jìngshān 徑山 and Shílín 石林 — the two principal Southern-Sòng Chán monasteries in the Hángzhōu region.

## About the work

A five-juan Chán-lineage biographical-anthology, X67 n1308. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text's *dīrǔ* 滴乳 ("dripping milk") title evokes the classical Buddhist image of nurturing dharma-transmission as mother's milk nourishing offspring. The collection gathers biographical-narrative and teaching-excerpt material from multiple generations of Chán masters, focused on the Hángzhōu-area Jìngshān / Shílín lineage-traditions.

### Abstract

[[真在|Shānduó Zhēnzài]] 山鐸真在 (b. 1621/12/31 per his *yǔlù* juan 1 *xíngzhuàng*): Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Shānduó 山鐸 ("Mountain-Bell"), Yúnān 筠庵.

Continuer [[機雲|Jīyún]] 機雲: later compiler of additional entries; lifedates unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1670 (Zhēnzài's mature productive period); *notAfter* c. 1720 (Jīyún's continuation horizon).
