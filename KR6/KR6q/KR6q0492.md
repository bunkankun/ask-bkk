# Dōngshān Pòfēng Zhòng chánshī yǔlù 東山破峰重禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Pò-fēng Zhòng of Dōng-shān*
by [[普重]] (說), [[傳慧]] (等編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Pòfēng Pǔzhòng** [[普重]] 破峰普重, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huìjué Zhàoyī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) in the Pòshān-Sìchuān sub-line. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Chuánhuì** [[傳慧]]. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B352.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per [[普重]] note: heir of [[照衣]] at the Pòshān → Zhàoyī line, abbot at **Dōngshān** 東山. Lifedates not preserved.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1665** (conservative bound for post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound for post-master-decease compilation.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Huìjué Zhàoyī → Pòfēng Pǔzhòng. Among Zhàoyī's six named heirs ([[徹御]] Fóyǔ at [[KR6q0487]], Cuìé, Xiùé, Zhèngé, Qīngyuán), Pòfēng was listed first.
