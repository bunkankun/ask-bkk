# Guǎngfúshān Shèngjuésì Mìyìn chánshī yǔlù 廣福山勝覺寺密印禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mì-yìn at Shèng-jué-sì of Guǎng-fú-shān*
by [[真傳]] (說), [[如暐]] (等編)

## About the work

Twelve-juan *yǔlù* of **Mìyìn Zhēnchuán** [[真傳]] 密印真傳 (b. **1625-07-31**, alive in 1678 at age 54), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] via **Língyǐn Yìnwén** 靈隱印文. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Xiàofēng Rúwěi** [[如暐]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Zhuāng Lín** 莊鏻 (*jìnshì*, *Hénán ànchásī fùshǐ*). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J35 B343. Twelve-juan structure: *shàngtáng* (j. 1–3); *xiǎocān* (j. 4); *kāishì* (j. 5–6); *fǎyǔ* (j. 7); subsequent juan with various genres + **j. 12 *tǎmíng bēijì***.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[真傳]] note: native of **Xùzhōufǔ** 敘州府 (Sìchuān-Nán), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Tonsured at Guānyīndòng after parents' deaths; full precepts under **Yǔsōng** 語嵩; transmission from **Língyǐn Yìnwén**. Three abbacies: **Húguǎng Huìtóng Huílóng** 湖廣會同迴龍 → **Qíshān Yǒngxīng** 旗山永興 → **Qiányáng Guǎngfúshān Shèngjuésì** 黔陽廣福山勝覺寺 (the 30+ year long-tenure seat).

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound. Alive in 1678 per the *tǎmíng* dating; the volume reflects the 1660s–1680s preaching career.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎimíng → Língyǐn Yìnwén → Mìyìn Zhēnchuán — the Pòshān-line via the Yúnnán / Guìzhōu mission-circuit (Yìnwén had been active at **Yúnnán Yúnjí** 雲南雲集 and **Hóngjiāng Huílóng** 洪江迴龍 abbacies). Mìyìn's 30+ year tenure at Guǎngfúshān in **Qiányáng** (Húnán-Guìzhōu border) preserves an extended Pòshān-line frontier ministry distinct from the central Sìchuān cluster.

**Lay-patron preface.** **Zhuāng Lín** 莊鏻's preface frames the volume as a deliberate Confucian-Chán bridge (*míng xīn jiàn xìng sānjiào tóngyuán* 明心見性三教同原), invoking the *liǎng Lùn / liǎng Mèng* analogy and explicitly connecting Mìyìn's preaching to Pòshān's broader corpus.

- **30-year Guǎngfúshān ministry.** Among the longest documented Pòshān-line individual abbacies, with corresponding documentation depth. The Qiányáng border-region site puts the ministry at the political-geographical periphery between Húnán, Guìzhōu, and Sìchuān-Nán.
