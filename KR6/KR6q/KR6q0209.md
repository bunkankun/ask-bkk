# Gāofēng Sānshān Lái chánshī nián pǔ 高峰三山來禪師年譜

*Chronology of Chán Master Sānshān Lái of Gāofēng*

A one-juan year-by-year biographical chronology of the Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685), compiled by his dharma-heir [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統 (1661–1717). Prefaced by **[[至善|Zhìshàn]]** 至善, another of Dēnglái's dharma-heirs.

## About the work

A one-juan *nián pǔ* 年譜 (year-by-year chronological biography) of a Chán master, J29 B245. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The *nián pǔ* genre — a classical Chinese biographical form — presents its subject's life in strict annual sequence, with dated entries for ordination, teaching positions, notable encounters, poem-compositions, dharma-transmissions, pilgrimage-journeys, and death. Xìngtǒng's compilation of his master Dēnglái's *nián pǔ* provides a comprehensive primary-source for the mid-to-late 17th-century Sichuan / Zhèjiāng Línjì-Yángqí Chán network.

Key dates established by the *nián pǔ*:
- Birth: 1614/11/29 (Wànlì 42 / 10 / 28 *xū* hour) in Zhōngzhōu 忠州 (Sichuan).
- Ordination and Chán training under [[慧機|Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機.
- Abbacies at Gāofēng Kāixī 高峰開禧 (Sichuan) and Wǔyún 五雲 (Zhèjiāng).
- Death: 1685/8/17 (Kāngxī 24 / 7 / 18), age 72.
- Post-death institutional continuity through Xìngtǒng's own Shèngyīn jiēdàisì 聖因接待寺 abbacy.

### Abstract

See [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] and [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] person notes for details. The *nián pǔ* complements Xìngtǒng's other major editorial-compilational work — the systematic *Wǔ jiā zōng zhǐ zuǎn yào* [[KR6q0169]] — by providing the personal-biographical frame for Dēnglái's doctrinal output.

**[[至善|Zhìshàn]]** 至善: dharma-heir of Dēnglái; preface-writer for the *nián pǔ*. Lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* 1685 (immediately after Dēnglái's death, the moment at which *nián pǔ* compilation normally began); *notAfter* 1710 (well within Xìngtǒng's productive compilation period, which ended with his 1717 death). A plausible specific date-range: late 1680s to early 1700s.

## Other points of interest

The *nián pǔ* is the principal primary source for Dēnglái's life and career, much more detailed than the standard lamp-record biographies. As a self-documenting Chán-institutional product — compiled by Dēnglái's own dharma-heir for inclusion in the canonical collection — it represents a particularly rich witness to late-17th-century Chán monastic self-representation.

The text's compilation-context — Xìngtǒng's editorial programme that also produced the *Wǔ jiā zōng zhǐ zuǎn yào* [[KR6q0169]] and the *Xù dēng zhèng tǒng* 續燈正統 [[KR6q0028]] — demonstrates the systematic character of late-17th-century Línjì lineage-documentation: a dharma-heir's responsibility to preserve his master's life-record, teaching-collection, and lineage-historical position through multiple-genre canonical publication.
