# Fógǔ Wén chánshī yǔlù 佛古聞禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fó-gǔ Wén*
by [[行聞]] (說), [[超質]] (等編)

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Fógǔ Xíngwén** [[行聞]] 佛古行聞 (**1610-08-10 – 1666-12-18**, *shìshòu* 57), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Shípú Chāozhì** [[超質]] *děng biān*. Four abbacies: **Tàicāng Xiānglínsì** 太倉香林寺 → **Pínghú Cáoxīsì** 平湖曹溪寺 → **Yāndū Qiānfósì** 燕都千佛寺 (Beijing) → **Yānjīng Yuánníngsì** 燕京圓寧寺. Juan 4 includes the *Línjì sānshíèr shì Xiānglínsì Fógǔ Wén chánshī tǎmíng* 臨濟三十二世香林寺佛古聞禪師塔銘. Sixteen heirs in line including **Shípú Chāozhì** (this volume's compiler). *notBefore* = **1644** (post-transmission), *notAfter* = **1666** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B364.
