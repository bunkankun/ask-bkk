# Cǎotáng Nòuyún Shí chánshī yǔlù 草堂耨雲實禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cǎo-táng Nòu-yún Shí*
by [[真實]] (說), [[寂訥]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Nòuyún Zhēnshí** [[真實]] 耨雲真實 (b. **1626-07-08**, alive 1686), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Fómǐn Jìnè** [[寂訥]] *děng biān*. Continued at the **Cǎotáng** 草堂 succession-seat in Sìchuān (the same Cǎotáng that figures in Tōngzuì's six-abbacy career — see [[通醉]] note). *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1686** (master's age 61, alive at compilation). Juan 2 *xíngyóu* + *fēndēng*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B370.
