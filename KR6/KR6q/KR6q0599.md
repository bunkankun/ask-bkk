# Qìngshān Mùtíng Pǔfū Zhuō chánshī yǔlù 磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mù-tíng Pǔ-fū Zhuō of Qìng-shān*
by [[拙]] (說), [[光模]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Pǔfū Shízhuō** [[拙]] 樸夫實拙 (b. **1682**, age 56 in **1737**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔyún Pèi** 古雲沛. Lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Shítán Guāngmó** [[光模]] *děng biān*. Documents three abbacies and precept-conferral activity in the **Jiāngxī Póyáng** 鄱陽 / **Línchuān** 臨川 region: **Póyáng Jiànxī Yǒngzhèn chánsì** 鄱陽劍溪永鎮禪寺 (the principal seat); **Bàlíng Báilùdù Xītīngsì** 霸陵白鷺渡西汀寺; **Línchuān Qīngyún cǎoān** 臨川青雲艸菴. Plus precept-conferral at **Fúliáng Yuántōngān** 浮梁圓通菴 in **乾隆元年丙辰冬** = **1736** winter and **Chóngrén Tiānzhúsì** 崇仁天竺寺 in **1736** lunar 12. *notBefore* ≈ **1720**; *notAfter* ≈ **1740**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B493.

**Contents.** Juan 1: portrait + preface; *mùcì*; *Yǒngzhènsì yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Xītīngsì yǔlù*. Juan 3: precept-lectures at Yuántōng (1736) and Tiānzhú (1736). Juan 4: *Qīngyún cǎoān yǔlù*. Juan 5: *kānbiàn*; *jīyuán*; *fù Gāofēng dàshī liùwèn zhuóyǔ* 附高峰大師六問著語; *niāngǔ*; *sònggǔ*. Juan 6: *zhuàn* (the principal source for his life including age-evidence to 1737).

## Other points of interest

The **Qiánlóng-era** materials — precept-lectures dated **1736** and the *zhuàn* dating to **1737** — make [[KR6q0599]] among the latest imprints in the Jiā-xīng-canon supplement, extending the J40 record into the second decade of the Qiánlóng reign. The 附高峰大師六問著語 in juan 5 is a *zhuóyǔ* (commentary) on the famous **Gāofēng Yuánmiào** 高峰原妙 *liù wèn* — anchoring this Qiánlóng-era *yǔlù* in the late-Sòng / early-Yuán Línjì *gōngàn* repertoire.
