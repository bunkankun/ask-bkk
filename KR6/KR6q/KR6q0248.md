# Chánlín lèijù 禪林類聚

*Classified Gathering of the Chán Forest* — a twenty-juan Yuán-dynasty Chán classified-reference anthology, compiled by [[道泰|Yuán Dàotài]] 元道泰 (a Yuán-era monk; not the same as the earlier Liú-Sòng or Běi-Wèi monks of the same name; lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded).

## About the work

A twenty-juan Yuán-dynasty Chán anthology, X67 n1299. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The *lèijù* 類聚 ("classified gathering") genre: the text takes Chán-source-materials (*gōng'àn*, master-dialogues, *yǔlù* passages) and organises them by topical or thematic category rather than by lineage-chronology. This allows the reader to locate Chán materials on specific themes (doctrinal topics, encounter-moments, specific images or phrases) across the entire Sòng-Yuán *yǔlù* tradition.

### Abstract

[[道泰|Dàotài]] 道泰 (Yuán-dynasty Chán monk): the named compiler. Lifedates and biographical details unrecorded. Given the text's Yuán compilation, Dàotài was active in the late-13th or early-14th century. Not to be confused with two earlier monks of the same name.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1280 (reasonable start for Yuán Chán anthology-compilation); *notAfter* c. 1340 (approximate Yuán-era horizon).
