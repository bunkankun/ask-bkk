# Hǔqiū Shàolóng chánshī yǔlù 虎丘紹隆禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Hǔqiū Shàolóng* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional *yǔlù* of **Hǔqiū Shàolóng** 虎丘紹隆 (1077–1136), *shì* Dàhuì 大慧 (not to be confused with Dàhuì Zōnggǎo!). Line-parallel to Dàhuì Zōnggǎo: both dharma-heirs of Yuánwù [[克勤|Kèqín]] 圓悟克勤. Shàolóng founded the **Hǔqiūsì** 虎丘寺 branch of the Yángqí Línjì sub-lineage, while Zōnggǎo founded the Jìngzhì sub-branch.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1358. Compiled by disciple [[嗣端|Sìduān]] 嗣端 et al.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1120; *notAfter* c. 1160.
