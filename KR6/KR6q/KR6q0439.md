# Fǎlán Chéng chánshī yǔlù 法瀾澂禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fǎ-lán Chéng*
by [[原澂]] (說), [[清杲]] (編次)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Fǎlán Yuánchéng** [[原澂]] 法瀾原澂 — dharma-heir of **Shāwēng Hǎi** 沙翁海 (also **敬太海老和尚** 敬太海老和尚) in the Húběi Jīngzhōu regional Chán tradition, and the mid-Kāng-xī abbot of multiple **Jīngzhōufǔ** seats including the classical **Tiānwáng Chánsì** 荊州天王禪寺 (ancestral seat of the Táng-era master **Tiānwáng Dàowù** 天王道悟, 727–808). *Fǎhuì* 法諱 **Yuánchéng** 原澂 (catalog-form; short-form **Chéng** 澂 in the work-title), *hào* **Fǎlán** 法瀾 ("Dharma-Wave"). Compiled by dharma-heir **Qīnggǎo** [[清杲]] 清杲. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B229.

### Abstract

**Author.** Fǎlán Yuánchéng was dharma-heir of **Shāwēng Hǎi lǎohéshàng** 沙翁海老和尚 — described in the opening *shàngtáng* as "*zhōngxìng běnsì sì zuò dàochǎng běnshī* 中興本寺四坐道場本師" ("restorer-master of the four abbacies of this temple") — a Jīngzhōu regional master who revived the Tiānwáng Chánsì seat and three others. The *běnshī*'s alternative-name is **Jìngtài Hǎi** 敬太海 per the opening incense-line.

**Four Jīngzhōu abbacies (1670–1673+).** (1) **Jīngzhōu Tiānwáng Chánsì** 荊州天王禪寺 — first abbacy, entered **康熙 9 庚戌 2.1** = **21 March 1670**. (2) **Dāngyáng Dùmén Chánsì** 當陽度門禪寺 — the Dùménsì is the ancestral seat of **Shénxiù** 神秀 (early 8th-century Northern Chán founder) in Dāngyáng. (3) **Jīngzhōu Pútíchǎng** 荊州菩提場. (4) **Jīngzhōu Bàoēn Chánsì** 荊州報恩禪寺. The seat-sequence represents a substantial mid-Kāng-xī Chán recovery in the Jīngzhōu region — including the restoration of two classical ancestral seats (Tiānwángsì of the Táng-era Tiānwáng Dàowù, and Dùménsì of Shénxiù).

**Compositional history.** Single front-preface by **Wáng Cáidǐng** 王才鼎 (**鍾山** Zhōngshān lay-patron), dated **康熙十二年 歲枉癸丑 七月既望** = **Kāngxī 12 / 1673 lunar 7.16** = ~**26 August 1673** Western. The preface's notable feature is its strong **Confucian-Chán syncretic argument**: Wáng explicitly compares "*chéngyì zhèngxīn* 誠意正心" of Confucianism to "*míngxīn jiànxìng* 明心見性" of Chán, arguing that the two paths converge in unity — a Qīng-era lay-Confucian Chán-justification register typical of the Kāngxī period. *notBefore* = **1670** (Kāngxī 9.2.1 Tiānwáng entry); *notAfter* = **1673** (Wáng Cáidǐng preface).

**Contents by juan.** (j.1) front-preface + **Tiānwáng Chánsì** *shàngtáng* + **Dùmén Chánsì** *shàngtáng*; (j.2) **Pútíchǎng** + **Bàoēn Chánsì** *shàngtáng* + **jīyuán** 機緣 + **rùshì** 入室 + **jìsòng** 偈頌 + **fóshì** 佛事 + **塔記(附)** 塔記(附).

## Other points of interest

- **Tiānwángsì restoration.** The **Jīngzhōu Tiānwáng Chánsì** is the ancestral seat of the Táng-era master **Tiānwáng Dàowù** 天王道悟 (727–808), one of the famous **Mǎzǔ Dàoyī** 馬祖道一 successors and a founder-figure of the pre-Línjì Chán tradition. Yuánchéng's 1670 restoration-abbacy is a specific mid-Kāng-xī reset for this ancestral seat, and his inherited master **Shāwēng Hǎi**'s earlier four-abbacy restoration-program forms the immediate pre-history. For Jīngzhōu Chán institutional history, this *yǔlù* is a focused primary source.
- **Dùménsì and Shénxiù connection.** The **Dāngyáng Dùménsì** 當陽度門禪寺 is the seat of **Shénxiù** 神秀 (606?–706), the early-Táng founder of the **Northern Chán** tradition (displaced in orthodox historiography by Huìnéng's Southern Chán). Yuánchéng's Dùmén abbacy is a small but specific Qīng-era restoration of this canonically-Northern-Chán seat within a Southern-Chán Línjì-institutional framework — a syncretic-historical gesture worth noting for anyone tracing the reception of Northern Chán by post-Míng Línjì.
- **Wáng Cáidǐng's Confucian-Chán syncretism.** The 1673 preface's explicit **诚意正心 / 明心見性** dual-path synthesis is a prime example of **Kāng-xī-era lay-Confucian Chán-patronage rhetoric** — reflecting the Qīng-court-approved Confucian-Buddhist accommodation policy that Kāngxī would formalize later with his patronage of both traditions. For historians of Qīng religious-political discourse, the preface is a specific datum.
