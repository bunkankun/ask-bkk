# Zhuófēng Jué chánshī yǔlù 卓峰玨禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhuó-fēng Jué*
by [[性玨]] (說), [[宗位]] (編), [[超源]] (序), [[藍理]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Zhuófēng Xìngjué** [[性玨]] 卓峰性玨 (**1625-08-21 – 1685** autumn, age 61), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Héngshān Dēngbǐng** [[燈炳]] (1611–1680, cf. [[KR6q0554]]) in the Sìchuān **Jùyún** 聚雲 sub-line. Native of **Zhōngzhōu** 忠州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Chéng** 成. Compiled by his sole *fǎsì* **Zōngwèi** [[宗位]] *biān* (the same compiler of [[KR6q0554]]). Two prefaces: by **Chāoyuán Dànyá** [[超源]] dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; and by the military commander **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1 — i.e. the same two preface-authors as the parallel teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0554]]. *notBefore* = **1680** (Qìngyún succession at his teacher's death); *notAfter* = **1696** (latest preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B444.

**Contents.** Single juan: two prefaces; *Sìchuān Zhōngzhōu Qìngyún chánsì yǔlù*; *xiǎocān*; *fóshì* (a substantial sequence for his teacher Dēngbǐng's death in 1680: *ānzuò* 安座, *qǐkān* 起龕, *jǔhuǒ* 舉火, *shōulínggǔ* 收靈骨, *qǐlínggǔ* 起靈骨, *rùtǎ* 入塔, *shǒujiǔ* 首九, *bǎiqī niānxiāng* 百期拈香 — i.e. the standard mortuary rituals from cremation through hundredth-day commemoration); *jì*; closing materials including *tǎmíng* with birth and death dates.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J39 B444).*

## Other points of interest

The *fóshì* section preserves a complete liturgical record of the funerary cycle for **Héngshān Dēngbǐng** 衡山燈炳 in 1680, paralleling the analogous *fóshì* sequences in [[KR6q0557]] and [[KR6q0558]] for **Sānshān Dēnglái** 三山燈來 (1685). Together these provide a remarkable comparative dossier on late-17th-century Sìchuān **Jùyún 聚雲** Línjì-Dàhuì school mortuary ritual.
