# Xuěān Cóngjǐn chánshī sònggǔ 雪菴從瑾禪師頌古

*Verses-on-Ancient-Cases of Chán Master Xuěān Cóngjǐn* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *sònggǔ* collection by [[從瑾|Xuěān Cóngjǐn]] 雪菴從瑾. Lifedates unrecorded; Southern-Sòng Línjì-lineage master. One-juan, X69 n1348.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1150; *notAfter* c. 1250 (broad Southern-Sòng bracket).
