# Dàfāng chánshī yǔlù 大方禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dà-fāng*
by [[行海]] (說), [[超鳴]] (編)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Dàfāng Xínghǎi** [[行海]] 大方行海 (**1604-07-03 – 1670-02-24**, *shìshòu* 67), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fúshí Tōngxián** [[通賢]] 浮石通賢. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Chāomíng** [[超鳴]] *biān*. *notBefore* = c. **1650** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1670** (death). Juan 6 includes *xíngshí* and *tǎmíng* with lifedates. Sole abbacy at **Jiāngsū Lóujiāng Chándēngān** 江蘇婁江禪燈菴 from 1669; the *yǔlù* covers his decades of pre-abbacy practice and the brief Lóujiāng tenure. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B366.
