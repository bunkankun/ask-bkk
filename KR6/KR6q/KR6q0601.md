# Huànyǒu Chuán chánshī yǔlù 幻有傳禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huàn-yǒu Chuán*
by [[圓悟]] (等編), [[圓修]] (等編)

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Lóngchí Huànyǒu Zhèngchuán** [[正傳]] 龍池幻有正傳 (**1549 – 1614-03-21**, age 66), **the foundational late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí lineage-restorer** from whom the entire Mìyún → Línyě / Tiānyǐn / Wànrú establishment descends. Native of **Lìyáng** 溧陽 (Jiāngsū). Compiled jointly by his two greatest *fǎsì*: **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] (1566–1642) and **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635) *děng biān*. The catalog source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** of the canon (Vol. 153 No. 1637), but the work is also preserved in the Jiā-xīng-canon supplement.

*notBefore* ≈ **1580** (post-mature-abbacy); *notAfter* ≈ **1620** (post-Yuán-wù's editorial completion, several years after Huànyǒu's 1614 death). The compilation by Mìyún Yuánwù — soon to become the most influential Línjì-Yángqí abbot of his generation — makes this *yǔlù* the foundational textual document of the entire late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí revival. Catalog dynasty 明.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface (by **Shǐ Yī** 史一, possibly **Shǐ Sìmìng** 史孟麟); *mùcì*; *Nánlù* 南錄 (southern abbacy records). Juan 2: *Běilù* 北錄 (northern abbacy records). Juan 3–5: *Xiántán* 閒談 (idle conversations) — preceded by two prefaces by **Shǐ Sān** 史三 and a second-edition preface; followed by *Wǎnhuà* 晚話 (evening talks); the master's deathbed *mòhòu jì bìng yǔ* 末後偈并語; *rùtǎ yǔ* 入塔語 (stupa-burial sermon). Juan 6+: *jǔgǔ*, *fǎyǔ*, etc.

## Other points of interest

This is the **lineage-foundational** *yǔlù* for the entire late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí establishment that fills J27–J40 of the Jiāxīng Canon. Through Huànyǒu's three *fǎsì* — **Mìyún Yuánwù**, **Tiānyǐn Yuánxiū**, **Xuějiāo Yuánxìn** — the lineage extended to the famous Línjì revival figures **Mùchén Dàomǐn** 木陳道忞, **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容, **Shíchē Tōngchéng** 石車通乘, **Wànrú Tōngwēi** 萬如通微 (cf. [[KR6q0584]]), **Yùlín Tōngxiù** 玉林通琇, etc. — i.e. the entire Mìyún cohort.
