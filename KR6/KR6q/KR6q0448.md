# Jùyún Chuīwàn Zhēn chánshī yǔlù 聚雲吹萬真禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Zhēn*
by [[廣真]] (說), [[燈來]] (編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Jùyún Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639), late-Míng Sichuan Línjì-Yángqí master and founder of the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派 sub-lineage at **Zhōngzhōu Jùyún Chányuàn** 忠州聚雲禪院. Compiled by the dharma-grandson **Sānshān Dēnglái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685, heir-of-heir via [[慧機]] 鐵壁慧機), published in the Jiāxīng Canon as J29 B238. Front-preface by **Xióng Rǔxué** 熊汝學 (*dēngzhào* 燈炤), self-styled *Lúshān Qiānzhàngyán tóutuó* 廬山千丈巖頭陀, *shuǐbù shàngshūláng qián quèjīng shǐzhě* 水部尚書郎前榷荊使者, dated **崇禎壬午菊月 = 1642 intercalary 9th month**. Contents: *shàng-juan* — *shàngtáng* + *cháhuà*; *zhōng-juan* — *pǔshuō* + *xiǎocān* + *shìzhòng* + *fǎyǔ*; *xià-juan* — *rùtáng* + *rùshì* + *kānbiàn* + *wèndá* + *sònggǔ* + *záshù* + *tǎmíng*. The closing colophon notes *lìng kè zhènglù shí juan, guǎnglù sānshí zhǒng, gòng liùshí liù juan, bǎn cún Zhōngzhōu Zhìpíng Chányuàn* 另刻正錄十卷廣錄三十種共六十六卷板存忠州治平禪院 — the *zhènglù* referred to there is [[KR6q0449]] (20 juan, J29 B239, *Chuīwàn chánshī yǔlù*), and the 30-title *guǎnglù* was Guǎngzhēn's separately cut 50-juan collection (now only partially surviving, with individual treatises at [[KR6q0235]] 《一貫別傳》 and the *Shìjiào sānzìjīng* 釋教三字經).

### Abstract

**Dating.** Juan 1 opens with a **萬曆戊午上元日 = 1618 1/15** entry-sermon at **Héngzhōufǔ Xiāoxiāng Húdōng Chányuàn** 衡州府瀟湘湖東禪院 (Húnán). Subsequent *shàngtáng* juan follow Guǎngzhēn's Jiāngnán / Fújiàn / Guǎngdōng / Húxiāng circuit and eventual return to Sìchuān for the five-seat abbacy in Zhōngzhōu. Closing juan includes his death-verse *cháo dǎ sānqiān, mù dǎ bābǎi; yào jiàn Jùyún, méimáo chū xiě* 朝打三千暮打八百要見聚雲眉毛出血 and the Chóngzhēn 12/7/30 (= **1639 8/28**) death-scene. *notBefore* = **1618** (first abbacy); *notAfter* = **1642** (Xióng Rǔxué preface).

**Authorship and transmission.** Xióng Rǔxué's preface notes that the compilation was incomplete *several years after* Guǎngzhēn's death — *yuánjì shù nián ér yǔlù yóu wèi quán kè* 圓寂數年而語錄猶未全刻 — and that Xióng and his *tóngméng* partners personally organized the engraving. The preface distinguishes three layers: (i) this three-juan selective *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0448]]); (ii) a separately cut ten-juan *zhènglù* (the twenty-juan [[KR6q0449]] in its final expanded form); (iii) thirty separate *guǎnglù* titles totalling some fifty juan, chiefly doctrinal and literary rather than *yǔlù* proper.

**Doctrinal character.** Guǎngzhēn's Jùyún tradition was distinctively oriented toward **三教一貫** *sānjiào yīguàn* (the unity of the three teachings) — see [[KR6q0235]] for his principal systematic work — and his *yǔlù* deploys extensive Confucian-classical citation alongside Chán *gōngàn* treatment. Xióng's preface emphasizes this character: *jiē fāmíng sāndài zhī ào yǐ pángjué zhūzǐ bǎijiā, jí wú rú hàoshǒu qióngjīng shàng bù néng chuǎng qí lángwǔ* 皆發明三代之奧以旁抉諸子百家即吾儒皓首窮經尚不能闖其廊廡.

**Lineage.** Guǎngzhēn received his *yìnkě* from **Cháoyáng Yuèmíng Liánchí chánshī** 朝陽月明聯池禪師, a Sichuan-based Línjì master. Principal dharma-heirs are **Zhìpíng Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] 治平鐵壁慧機 (compiler-circle of [[KR6q0450]], [[KR6q0451]]), **Wànsōng Sānmù Huìzhī** 萬松三目慧芝, and **Bǎofēng Sānbāzhǎng Tiěméi Huìlì** 寶峰三巴掌鐵眉慧麗 (cf. [[KR6q0452]]).

## Other points of interest

- **Paired with [[KR6q0449]] as a single Guǎngzhēn corpus.** B238 (3 juan) is a selective *yǔlù* compilation; B239 (20 juan) is the full *zhènglù*. Both share the compiler **Dēnglái** [[燈來]]; the latter is explicitly marked *chóngbiān* 重編 in the catalog, indicating it is the definitive expansion of the selective volume.
- **Three-teachings-unity Chán.** The Jùyúnpài is distinctive within the Míng-Qīng Línjì field for its programmatic *sānjiào yīguàn* orientation and for producing catechism-style didactic texts ([[KR6q0235]], 釋教三字經) aimed at lay-scholar audiences — quite different in character from the contemporary Mìyún / Hànyuè disputation-literature of Jiāngnán.
