# Yùquán Qíbái Fù chánshī yǔlù 玉泉其白富禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qí-bái Fù of Yù-quán [-sì]*
by [[德富]] (說), [[圓頂]] (等編)

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Qíbái Défù** [[德富]] 其白德富 (**1628-02-04 – 1690-07-03**, age 64), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696), thus a co-disciple of **Zhúlàng Chèshēng** [[徹生]] (cf. [[KR6q0544]]). Native of **Shǔnán Méizhōu** 蜀南眉州 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Gōng** 龔. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Róngchè Yuándǐng** [[圓頂]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at his sole abbacy **Méizhōu Pánlóngshān Yùquán chánsì** 眉州蟠龍山玉泉禪寺 (Sìchuān), where he served until his death.

The *yǔlù* opens with a portrait-frontispiece, *zàn*, two prefaces, and a comprehensive *mùcì*. *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-*fùfǎ* abbacy onset, ca. 1659–1660 when Zhàngxuě sealed his dharma-heirs); *notAfter* = **1690** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B430.

**Contents.** Juan 1: portrait + *zàn*; *xù* (二); *mùcì*; *shàngtáng* (the principal preaching record at Yùquánsì). Juan 2: *fǎyǔ* (示融默監寺, 示秀雲, 示福田, 示融穆, 示吼月, 示文達, 示融之, 示性一, 示體空, 示了機, 示融徹首座, 示玟睿維那 etc. — addressing the master's principal disciples). Juan 3: closing *jīyuán*, *fóshì*, the *xíngshí* (autobiographical narration), the **塔銘** (stupa-inscription dating his death to 1690), and the **分燈** (lineage-distribution) listing his five dharma-heirs.

## Other points of interest

The Sìchuān cluster of Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì's heirs appears in J38 in close succession — [[KR6q0544]] (Chèshēng), [[KR6q0547]] (Défù) — both with abbacy-bases on or near famous Sìchuān Daoist-Buddhist mountains (Qīngchéngshān; Pánlóngshān). The internal lineage-economy is visible in the *fǎyǔ* section, where Défù addresses each of his five later *fǎsì* by name.
