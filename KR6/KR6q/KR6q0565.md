# Chìsōng Lǐng chánshī yǔlù 赤松領禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chì-sōng Lǐng*
by [[道領]] (說), [[寂源]] (錄)

Five-juan *yǔlù* of **Chìsōng Dàolǐng** [[道領]] 赤松道領 (b. **1634-05-20**, death year unknown — was 53 in **1686**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mǐnshù Rúxiāng** [[如相]] (1603–1672, cf. [[KR6q0564]]) and the principal Línjì-Yángqí *zǔshī* of **Guìyáng Qiánlíngshān Hóngfúsì** 貴陽黔靈山弘福寺 — the most important Buddhist monastery in Guìzhōu province under the Qīng. Native of **Tóngchuān** 潼川 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Hán** 韓; his mother carried him into Guìzhōu during the wartime chaos.

Recorded by his *fǎsì* **Qiányù Jìyuán** [[寂源]] *lù*. Preface dated **康熙辛未皋月午日** = **1691** lunar 5 written at **Gǔyúzhōu** 古渝州 (Chóngqìng). Plus a secondary preface dated **康熙二十年歲次癸亥桐月** (with a probable misprint in the era-year — 康熙二十年 = 1681 ≠ 癸亥 1683; likely intended is 康熙二十二年 = 1683 lunar 3). *notBefore* = **1667** (康熙丁未, **Báiyún** 白雲 invitation by 閫臺 **Zhāng Guānghuàn** 張光煥); *notAfter* = **1691** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B451.

**Contents.** Five juan: prefaces; abbacy *yǔlù* (Báiyún 1667; subsequent invitations from **Yúnnán** 1684 (康熙甲子秋, to assist with 九峰山開山靈和尚 sweeping); Hóngfúsì 1686 winter (大中丞慕公 = the *Xúnfǔ* of Guìzhōu); etc.); *xíngyóu* in juan 5; *fǎyǔ*, *jīyuán*, *fóshì*, *liánfāngjì*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔlù* is the most important documentary source for the founding of **Guìyáng Hóngfúsì** 貴陽弘福寺 — the leading Línjì-Yángqí monastery in late-Qīng Guìzhōu, which still stands. Dàolǐng's three-year *bìguān* preceded the monastery's institutional opening; the imperial-class lay-patron preface ("大中丞慕公" = the Guìzhōu Provincial Governor) at the **1686** invitation marks the formal state recognition of the foundation.
