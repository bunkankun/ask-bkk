# Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng shū miào zōng chāo 觀無量壽佛經疏妙宗鈔
*The Wonderful-Lineage Sub-Commentary on the Commentary on the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra*
by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ, 述)

## About the work

This 6-fascicle sub-commentary on [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ's [[KR6f0073|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng shū*]] (T1750) is by [[知禮]] Zhīlǐ (960–1028), the great Northern-Sòng Tiāntái-school revivalist. It is one of the principal documents of the Sòng *Shānjiā* 山家 (Mountain-Family) Tiāntái lineage's Pure Land synthesis.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1020 – 1028) reflects [[知禮]] Zhīlǐ's mature scholarly period.
