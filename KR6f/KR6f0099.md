# Hòu chū ā mí tuó fó jì 後出阿彌陀佛偈
*Late-Issued Verses on Amitābha Buddha*
by anonymous translator

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text preserves verses (*jì* 偈) on Amitābha Buddha. The "late-issued" (*hòu chū* 後出) of the title indicates that the verses postdate the principal Pure Land sūtras and constitute a derivative-devotional work.
