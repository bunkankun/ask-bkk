# Rú lái zhuāng yán zhì huì guāng míng rù yī qiè fó jìngjiè jīng 如來莊嚴智慧光明入一切佛境界經
*The Sūtra on the Tathāgata's Adornment of Wisdom-Light Entering All Buddha-Realms*
by [[曇摩流支]] (Dharmaruci, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[曇摩流支]] Dharmaruci treats the Mahāyāna doctrine of the *zhì huì guāng míng* (wisdom-light) of the *Tathāgata* entering all Buddha-realms. The work belongs to the broader *all-Buddha-realms* (一切佛境界) cycle of Mahāyāna materials.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[曇摩流支]] Dharmaruci's Northern-Wèi activity in Luòyáng (504 – 519 CE).

The Taishō text (T0357) is established on the standard apparatus.
