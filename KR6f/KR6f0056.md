## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng-preserved text is the *Śrīmālā Sūtra* with interlinear annotations (*xié zhù* 挾注 — "tucked-in annotations"). The format provides word-level glosses on the parent sūtra.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500 – 900) reflects the broad period of Dūnhuáng-fragment composition. The Taishō text (T2763) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript.
