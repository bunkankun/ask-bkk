# Fó shuō bǎo jī sānmèi wén-shū-shī-lì pútísà wèn fǎ shēn jīng 佛說寶積三昧文殊師利菩薩問法身經
*The Sūtra on the Treasure-Heap Samādhi as Asked by Bodhisattva Mañjuśrī Concerning the Dharma-Body*
by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text attributed to [[安世高]] Ān Shìgāo treats the Mahāyāna doctrine of the *bǎo jī sānmèi* (*Ratna-kūṭa-samādhi*) and the *fǎ shēn* (*dharmakāya*). Modern scholarship (Nattier 2008) is somewhat sceptical of the traditional ascription to Ān Shìgāo, given the Mahāyāna character of the text, but the attribution is preserved by the canonical tradition.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The traditional attribution to [[安世高]] Ān Shìgāo would place the translation in the late-Hàn period (148 – 170 CE).

The Taishō text (T0356) is established on the standard apparatus.
