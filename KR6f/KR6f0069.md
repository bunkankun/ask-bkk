# Wú liàng shòu jīng yì jì juàn xià 無量壽經義記卷下
*Notes on the Meaning of the Sūtra of Amitāyus, [Surviving] Lower Fascicle* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng manuscript fragment preserves only the lower fascicle of a 2-fascicle Pure Land commentary. Preserved in Taishō volume 85 — the *Dūnhuáng yíshū* 敦煌遺書 series.

### Prefaces

*No tiyao or preface in source.*

### Abstract

The fragment is anonymous and undated; conventional scholarship places it in the broad late-Northern-Dynasties / Suí / Tang period (500 – 900 CE) of Dūnhuáng manuscript production.

The Taishō text (T2759) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript.
