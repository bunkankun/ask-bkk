# Dà bǎo jī jīng lùn 大寶積經論
*Treatise on the Great Treasure-Heap Sūtra*
by [[菩提流支]] (Bodhiruci, 譯)

## About the work

This 4-fascicle text by [[菩提流支]] Bodhiruci is a *śāstra* (treatise) on the *Mahā-ratnakūṭa* tradition — i.e. on the *Bǎo jī*-cycle of Mahāyāna sūtras (cf. [[KR6f0001|the great compendium T0310]]), whose Indic original is attributed to Vasubandhu / Sthiramati. The treatise was translated into Chinese by Bodhiruci in the early Northern-Wèi period.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[菩提流支]] Bodhiruci's Northern-Wèi activity in Luòyáng (508 – 537 CE).

The Taishō text (T1523) is established on the standard apparatus.
