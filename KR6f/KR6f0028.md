# Xū mó tí jīng 須摩提經
*The Sumati Sūtra*
by [[菩提流志]] (Bodhiruci II, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci II's Tang version of the *Sumati-paripṛcchā*, the third parallel translation alongside [[KR6f0026|Zhú Fǎhù's T0334]] and [[KR6f0027|Kumārajīva's T0335]]. The work was incorporated as one of the constituent assemblies of the larger [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] (T0310).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci II's Cháng'ān period (706 – 713 CE), the years of his great *Bǎo jī* compilation project.

The Taishō text (T0336) is established on the standard apparatus.
