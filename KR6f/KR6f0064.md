# Fó shuō dà ā mí tuó jīng 佛說大阿彌陀經
*The Great Sūtra of Amitābha (Wáng Rì-xiū's composite recension)*
by [[王日休]] (Wáng Rì-xiū, 校輯 / collated and compiled)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[王日休]] Wáng Rì-xiū (d. 1173) is the famous Southern-Sòng *composite recension* of the *Larger Sukhāvatī-vyūha-sūtra*, synthesised from the four major existing Chinese translations ([[KR6f0060|T0360]], [[KR6f0061|T0361]], [[KR6f0062|T0362]], [[KR6f0063|T0363]]). The result is a unified Chinese Pure Land scripture that combines what Wáng Rì-xiū judged to be the best readings from each of the source-translations, producing a stylistically polished and devotionally accessible text. The work became one of the most widely-chanted versions of the *Sukhāvatī-vyūha* in late-imperial Chinese Pure Land practice.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1160 – 1173) reflects [[王日休]] Wáng Rì-xiū's mature Buddhist period leading up to his death.

The Taishō text (T0364) is established on the standard apparatus.

## Other points of interest

- Wáng Rì-xiū's editorial method — synthesising multiple Chinese translations of a single Indic original into one composite recension — is unusual in the Buddhist canon and is one of the most distinctive lay-Buddhist editorial achievements of the Sòng period.
