# Pí yē suō wèn jīng 毘耶娑問經
*The Sūtra of the Inquiry of Vyāsa*
by [[瞿曇般若流支]] (Gautama Prajñāruci, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[瞿曇般若流支]] Gautama Prajñāruci is a Mahāyāna sūtra featuring the legendary Indian sage *Pí yē suō* 毘耶娑 (Vyāsa, the redactor of the *Mahābhārata* and the *Vedas*) as the bodhisattva-inquirer. The Buddhist appropriation of Vyāsa as a Buddhist sage-figure is unusual and reflects the broader inter-religious dialogue between Buddhism and Brahmanical traditions in mid-first-millennium India.
