# Fó shuō dà chéng rù zhū fó jìngjiè zhì guāng míng zhuāng yán jīng 佛說大乘入諸佛境界智光明莊嚴經
*The Great-Vehicle Sūtra on Entering All Buddha-Realms with Wisdom-Light Adornment*
by [[法護]] (Fǎ-hù / Dharmapāla *et al.*, 等譯)

## About the work

This 5-fascicle text by [[法護]] Fǎ-hù (the Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau translator) and his team is the third Chinese version of the *Buddha-realm wisdom-light* material, after [[KR6f0049|T0357]] and [[KR6f0050|T0358]]. The 5-fascicle scope reflects an expanded recension of the underlying Indic text relative to the earlier Chinese versions.
