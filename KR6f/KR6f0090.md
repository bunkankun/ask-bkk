# Ā mí tuó jīng shū 阿彌陀經疏 (Wǒnhyo)
*Commentary on the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0082|*Smaller Sukhāvatī*]] is by the Silla master [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo. Distinct from [[KR6f0067|Wǒnhyo's *Two-Fascicle Larger Sukhāvatī Zōng yào*]] (T1747); the present commentary treats the *Smaller* parallel.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (660 – 686) reflects Wǒnhyo's mature Silla period.

The Taishō text (T1759) is established on the standard apparatus.
