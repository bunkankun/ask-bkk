# Fó shuō pǔ mén pǐn jīng 佛說普門品經 (recension b)
*The Sūtra of the Universal Gate Chapter (recension b)*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is the second Taishō recension (T0315b) of [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's *Pǔ mén pǐn jīng*, parallel to [[KR6f0006|T0315a]]. The two recensions preserve the same translation in slightly different states, with T0315b drawing on the Sòng (宋), Yuán (元), Palace (宮) and other witnesses rather than the Korean.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE). The two-recension preservation in the Taishō provides exceptional comparative-philological apparatus for studying the textual history of Zhú Fǎhù's translation work.

The Taishō text (T0315b) is established on the standard non-Korean witnesses.
