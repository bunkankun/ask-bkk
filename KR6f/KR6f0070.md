# Wú liàng shòu guān jīng yì jì 無量壽觀經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra* (anonymous Dūnhuáng fragment)
[anonymous]

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous Dūnhuáng-preserved text is a commentary on the [[KR6f0071|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng*]] (T0365), the *Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra* — one of the *Three Pure Land Sutras*. Preserved in Taishō volume 85 (the *Dūnhuáng yíshū*).

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500 – 900) reflects the broad Dūnhuáng-fragment composition period.

The Taishō text (T2760) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript.
