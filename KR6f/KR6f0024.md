# Fó shuō yōu tián wáng jīng 佛說優填王經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on King Udayana*
by [[法炬]] (Fǎ-jù, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[法炬]] Fǎ-jù is a Mahāyāna sūtra concerning King *Yōu Tián* 優填 (Udayana), the legendary Indian king famous in Buddhist hagiography for commissioning the first Buddha-image. The work treats the *King Udayana*'s relations with the Buddha and his patronage of the *saṅgha*.

### Abstract

[[法炬]] Fǎ-jù (active late 3rd / early 4th c.) was a Western-Jìn translator. The translation belongs to the period c. 290 – 306 CE per the *Chū sānzàng jì jí*. The bracket adopted here reflects this window.

The Taishō text (T0332) is established on the standard apparatus.
