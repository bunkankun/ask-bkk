# Liǎng juàn wú liàng shòu jīng zōng yào 兩卷無量壽經宗要
*Essential Doctrinal Lineage of the Two-Fascicle Sūtra of Amitāyus*
by [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo / Yuánxiǎo, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle compendium by [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo (617–686) treats the doctrinal essence (*zōng yào* 宗要) of the [[KR6f0060|2-fascicle *Wú liàng shòu jīng*]] (T0360). The work belongs to Wǒnhyo's vast commentarial corpus on the major Mahāyāna scriptures, including parallel *zōng yào* compendia on other major sūtras.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (660 – 686) reflects [[元曉]] Wǒnhyo's mature Silla period.

The Taishō text (T1747) is established on the standard apparatus.
