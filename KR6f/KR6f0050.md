# Dù yī qiè zhū fó jìngjiè zhì yán jīng 度一切諸佛境界智嚴經
*The Sūtra on the Wisdom-Adornment of Crossing Over All Buddha-Realms*
by [[僧伽婆羅]] (Saṃghabhara *et al.*, 等譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[僧伽婆羅]] Saṃghabhara and his Liáng-period team is parallel to [[KR6f0049|*Rú lái zhuāng yán zhì huì*]] (T0357) and [[KR6f0051|*Dà chéng rù zhū fó jìngjiè zhì guāng míng zhuāng yán jīng*]] (T0359) — three Chinese versions of the same Mahāyāna *Buddha-realm wisdom-light* material.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[僧伽婆羅]] Saṃghabhara's Liáng-period activity (506 – 524 CE).

The Taishō text (T0358) is established on the standard apparatus.
