# Guān wú liàng shòu jīng yì shū 觀無量壽經義疏 (Huì-yuǎn)
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra*
by [[慧遠]] (Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn / Suí Yuǎn, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0071|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng* T0365]] is by [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn* (523–592). It is the foundational Pure Land commentary in the Chinese tradition — the work studied by Tanaka 1990 as the doctrinal "dawn" of mature Chinese Pure Land Buddhism.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 592) reflects [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn*'s mature Suí period.

The Taishō text (T1749) is established on the standard apparatus.
