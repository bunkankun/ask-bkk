# Dà chéng xiǎn shí jīng 大乘顯識經
*The Great-Vehicle Sūtra Manifesting Consciousness*
by [[地婆訶羅]] (Divākara, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[地婆訶羅]] Divākara treats the doctrine of *xiǎn shí* 顯識 ("manifesting consciousness") — the Yogācāra-Mahāyāna doctrine of consciousness-types and their relation to phenomenal manifestation. The work was incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] tradition.
