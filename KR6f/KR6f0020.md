# Fó shuō xū lài jīng 佛說須賴經 (Bái-yán)
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Surata (Surata-paripṛcchā)*
by [[白延]] (Bái Yán, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[白延]] Bái Yán is the earliest Chinese version of the *Surata-paripṛcchā* — the Mahāyāna sūtra on the inquiry of the layman Surata. A parallel translation by [[KR6f0021|支施崙]] Zhī Shī-lún (T0329) was produced shortly afterwards in the Former-Liáng state. Together with [[KR6f0014|Ān Xuán's *Fǎ jìng jīng* / *Ugra-paripṛcchā*]], the *Surata-paripṛcchā* is one of the foundational *paripṛcchā*-genre Mahāyāna sūtras.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[白延]] Bái Yán's Cáo-Wèi period activity, c. 252 – 256 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The Taishō text (T0328) is established on the standard apparatus.
