# Fó shuō dà chéng shí fǎ jīng 佛說大乘十法經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Ten Dharmas of the Great Vehicle*
by [[僧伽婆羅]] (Saṃghabhara, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[僧伽婆羅]] Saṃghabhara (the great Liáng-dynasty Cambodian translator) treats the *shí fǎ* 十法 ("Ten Dharmas") of the Mahāyāna — i.e. the ten foundational doctrinal categories of the bodhisattva path. Later incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] as assembly 9 (*Dà chéng shí fǎ huì* 大乘十法會).

### Abstract

[[僧伽婆羅]] Saṃghabhara (Sēng-jiā-pó-luó 僧伽婆羅, fl. early 6th c., a Funanese / Cambodian translator at the Liáng 梁 court of Wǔ-dì 武帝) was a major translator of Yogācāra-Mahāyāna texts. The translation is conventionally dated to the period 506 – 524 CE, his Liáng-period activity. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The Taishō text (T0314) is established on the standard apparatus.
