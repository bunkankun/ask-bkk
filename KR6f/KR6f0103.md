# Mí lè pútísà suǒ wèn jīng lùn 彌勒菩薩所問經論
*Treatise on the Sūtra of the Inquiry of Bodhisattva Maitreya*
by [[菩提流支]] (Bodhiruci, 譯)

## About the work

This 9-fascicle text by [[菩提流支]] Bodhiruci is the Indian *śāstra* (treatise) on the *Maitreya-paripṛcchā* tradition (parent sūtra: [[KR6f0041|*Mí lè pútísà suǒ wèn běn yuàn jīng* T0349]] by Zhú Fǎhù). The Indic authorship is attributed to *Sthiramati* or another Yogācāra-school *ācārya*; Bodhiruci's translation provides the principal Chinese textual basis for the *Maitreya-paripṛcchā* commentarial tradition.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[菩提流支]] Bodhiruci's Northern-Wèi activity (508 – 537 CE). The 9-fascicle scope makes the work one of the most substantial Northern-Wèi *śāstra* translations.

The Taishō text (T1525) is established on the standard apparatus.
