# Fó shuō mó hē yǎn bǎo yán jīng 佛說摩訶衍寶嚴經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Mahāyāna Jewel-Adornment*
by anonymous translator

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text is one of the parallel translations of the *Kāśyapa-parivarta* (alongside [[KR6f0042|Lokakṣema's T0350]] and [[KR6f0044|Dānapāla's T0352]]). The four-translation tradition (T0350, T0351, T0352, plus the *Mahā-ratnakūṭa* assembly 43) makes the *Kāśyapa-parivarta* one of the most extensively re-translated Mahāyāna texts in the Chinese canon.
