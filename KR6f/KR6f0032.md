# Wén-shū-shī-lì suǒ shuō bù sī yì fó jìngjiè jīng 文殊師利所說不思議佛境界經
*The Sūtra Spoken by Mañjuśrī on the Inconceivable Buddha-Realm*
by [[菩提流志]] (Bodhiruci II, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci II is a Mahāyāna sūtra featuring Mañjuśrī as the principal speaker — paralleling but distinct from the Avataṃsaka-tradition material on the *bù sī yì fó jìngjiè* (cf. [[KR6e0049|T0300]] and [[KR6e0050|T0301]]).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[菩提流志]] Bodhiruci II's Cháng'ān period (706 – 713 CE), the years of his great *Bǎo jī* compilation project.

The Taishō text (T0340) is established on a particularly rich apparatus including the *Shèng* 聖, *Shèng-yǐ* 聖乙, and other witnesses.
