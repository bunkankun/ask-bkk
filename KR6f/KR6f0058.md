# Shèng mán jīng shù jì 勝鬘經述記
*Recorded Notes on the Śrīmālā Sūtra*
by [[窺基]] (Kuījī, 基說 / lectured) and [[義令]] (Yìlìng, 記 / recorded)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text is the Tang-period lecture-record on the [[KR6f0045|*Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra*]] by the great Yogācāra-school master [[窺基]] Kuī-jī (632–682), recorded by his disciple [[義令]] Yì-lìng. The catalog meta lists "大乘" as author, which appears to be a placeholder term referring to the *Mahāyāna* doctrinal tradition rather than a personal name; the actual authorial attribution is to Kuī-jī.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (660 – 682) reflects [[窺基]] Kuījī's mature scholarly period. The work documents Kuījī's Yogācāra-school reading of the *Śrīmālā* and its *tathāgatagarbha* doctrine — an unusual case of an explicitly Yogācāra master commenting on a foundational *tathāgatagarbha* sūtra.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X352) collection.
