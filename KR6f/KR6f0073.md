# Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng shū 觀無量壽佛經疏 (Zhì-yǐ)
*Commentary on the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra*
by [[智顗]] (Zhì-yǐ, 說 / lectured)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary by [[智顗]] Zhì-yǐ (538–597, the founder of the Tiāntái 天台 school) is the Tiāntái-school reading of the [[KR6f0071|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng* T0365]]. The work integrates Pure Land contemplative practice with the Tiāntái-school *zhǐ guān* (śamatha-vipaśyanā) framework.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 597) reflects [[智顗]] Zhì-yǐ's mature scholarly period at the imperial Suí court and at Tiāntái-shān 天台山.

The Taishō text (T1750) is established on the standard apparatus.
