# Shèng mán jīng shū 勝鬘經疏
*Commentary on the Śrīmālā Sūtra*
by [[昭法師]] (Zhāo fǎshī, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text is a Dūnhuáng-preserved Buddhist commentary on the [[KR6f0045|*Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra*]] by an unidentified *Zhāo fǎshī* ("Master Zhāo"). The work is one of several pre-Tang and Tang fragmentary *Śrīmālā* commentaries preserved at Dūnhuáng.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (500 – 900) reflects the broad pre-Tang to Tang period during which the work could have been composed.

The Taishō text (T2762) is established on the Dūnhuáng manuscript witness.
