# Ā mí tuó jīng yì shū 阿彌陀經義疏 (Yuánzhào)
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[元照]] (Yuánzhào, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0082|*Smaller Sukhāvatī*]] is by [[元照]] Yuánzhào (1048–1116), paralleling his commentary on the [[KR6f0077|*Visualization Sūtra*]] (T1754).

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1080 – 1116) reflects Yuánzhào's mature scholarly period.

The Taishō text (T1761) is established on the standard apparatus.
