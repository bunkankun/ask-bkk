# Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng yì shū 觀無量壽佛經義疏 (Yuán-zhào)
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Visualization-of-Amitāyus Sūtra*
by [[元照]] (Yuán-zhào, 述)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0071|*Guān wú liàng shòu fó jīng* T0365]] is by [[元照]] Yuán-zhào (1048–1116), the great Northern-Sòng Vinaya-school revivalist. The work integrates Pure Land devotional practice with the *Lǜ-zōng* 律宗 emphasis on monastic-discipline foundations.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1080 – 1116) reflects [[元照]] Yuán-zhào's mature scholarly period at the Líng-zhī-sì in Hángzhōu.

The Taishō text (T1754) is established on the standard apparatus.
