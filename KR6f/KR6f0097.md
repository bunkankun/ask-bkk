# Guān shì yīn pútísà shòu jì jīng 觀世音菩薩授記經
*The Sūtra on Avalokiteśvara's Receiving the Prediction*
by [[曇無竭]] (Dharmodgata, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[曇無竭]] Tán-wú-jié is a Mahāyāna sūtra recounting the future Buddhahood of *Guān shì yīn* 觀世音 (Avalokiteśvara) — i.e. the Buddha's prediction (*shòu jì* / *vyākaraṇa*) that this most-venerated of bodhisattvas will eventually attain Buddhahood as *Pǔ-guāng-gōng-dé Shān-wáng Tathāgata* 普光功德山王如來. The work is one of the principal scriptural foundations of the Avalokiteśvara cult in East Asian Buddhism.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (420 – 479) reflects the LiúSòng period of Tánwújié's activity.
