# Ā mí tuó jīng shū 阿彌陀經疏 (Zhì-yuán)
*Commentary on the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[智圓]] (Zhì-yuán, 述)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0082|*Smaller Sukhāvatī*]] is by [[智圓]] Zhì-yuán (976–1022), the great Northern-Sòng Tiāntái-school *Shānwài* 山外 ("Off-the-Mountain") master, presenting the *Shānwài* reading of the Pure Land sūtra in opposition to the *Shānjiā* (Mountain-Family) line of [[知禮]] Zhī-lǐ.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (990 – 1022) reflects [[智圓]] Zhì-yuán's mature scholarly period.

The Taishō text (T1760) is established on the standard apparatus.
