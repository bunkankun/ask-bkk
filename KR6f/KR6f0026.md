# Fó shuō xū mó tí pútísà jīng 佛說須摩提菩薩經 (Zhú Fǎhù)
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Bodhisattva Sumati*
by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù is the *Sumati-paripṛcchā* — the Mahāyāna sūtra on the bodhisattva-girl *Sumati* (須摩提, "Good-Mind"). The work has parallel translations: [[KR6f0027|Kumārajīva's T0335]] and [[KR6f0028|Bodhiruci's T0336]]. The work was incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] tradition.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[竺法護]] Zhú Fǎhù's Cháng'ān period (266 – 313 CE). The doctrinal substance — the Sumati narrative — is one of the principal Mahāyāna sūtras featuring a female bodhisattva-protagonist, and is therefore important for the study of gender in early Mahāyāna literature.

The Taishō text (T0334) is established on the standard apparatus.
