# Ā mí tuó gǔ yīn shēng wáng tuó luó ní jīng 阿彌陀鼓音聲王陀羅尼經
*The Dhāraṇī Sūtra of the Amitābha Drum-Voice King*
by anonymous translator

## About the work

This 1-fascicle anonymous text presents a Pure Land *dhāraṇī*-sūtra centred on Amitābha as the *Drum-Voice King* (鼓音聲王) — a cosmic-musical aspect of the Buddha-body. The text is part of the broader corpus of Pure Land *dhāraṇī* materials that grew up around the *Three Pure Land Sutras*.
