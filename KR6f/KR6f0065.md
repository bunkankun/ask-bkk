# Wú liàng shòu jīng yì shū 無量壽經義疏 (Huì-yuǎn)
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Sūtra of Amitāyus*
by [[慧遠]] (Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn / Suí Yuǎn, 撰)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0060|*Wú liàng shòu jīng* T0360]] is by [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn* (the Suí *Dìlùn*-school master, 523–592). One of the foundational Pure-Land commentaries in the Chinese tradition, it provided the Yogācāra-influenced doctrinal-interpretive framework that would be elaborated by later Pure-Land masters Tánluán 曇鸞, Dàochuò 道綽, and Shàndǎo 善導.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 592) reflects [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn*'s mature Suí period.

The Taishō text (T1745) is established on the standard apparatus.
