# Shèng mán jīng shū yì sī chāo 勝鬘經疏義私鈔
*The Sub-Commentary's Private Notes on the Commentary on the Śrīmālā Sūtra*
by [[聖德太子]] (Shōtoku Taishi, 疏 / commentary) and [[明空]] (Míngkōng, 私鈔 / sub-commentary)

## About the work

This 6-fascicle composite text combines [[聖德太子]] Prince Shōtoku's traditional *Shōman gisho* (the Japanese imperial commentary on the [[KR6f0045|*Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra*]]) with the Tang Chinese sub-commentary by [[明空]] Míng-kōng. The work documents the early Sino-Japanese intellectual exchange around the *Śrīmālā*-tradition.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The composite work spans Shōtoku's death (622, the *terminus post quem* for the parent commentary) to the late Tang (the bracket adopted here, 622 – 850). The Tang sub-commentary by Míngkōng provides important comparative material for Sino-Japanese Buddhist commentarial tradition.

The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X353) collection.
