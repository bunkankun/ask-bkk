# Fó shuō rú huàn sān mó dì wú liàng yìn fǎ mén jīng 佛說如幻三摩地無量印法門經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Boundless-Seal Dharma-Gate of the Illusion-Like Samādhi*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla *et al.*, 等譯)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text by [[施護]] Dānapāla and his Northern-Sòng team treats the *rú huàn sān mó dì* 如幻三摩地 ("illusion-like *samādhi*") and its associated dharmā-gates of *wú liàng yìn* (boundless seals).

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[施護]] Dānapāla's Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau activity (980 – 1017 CE).

The Taishō text (T0372) is established on the standard apparatus.
