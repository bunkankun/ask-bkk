# Shèng mán jīng yì jì 勝鬘經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Śrīmālā Sūtra*
by [[慧遠]] (Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn / Suí Yuǎn, 撰)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0045|*Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra*]] is by [[慧遠]] *Jìng-yǐng Huì-yuǎn* (the Suí *Dìlùn*-school master, 523–592, distinct from the much earlier Lúshān Huì-yuǎn of 334–416). The work belongs to Huì-yuǎn's vast commentarial corpus on the major Mahāyāna scriptures and represents the canonical Suí-period reading of the *Śrīmālā*.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 592) reflects [[慧遠]] *Jìngyǐng Huìyuǎn*'s mature scholarly period. The work is preserved in the Manji *Xù zàng jīng* (X351).
