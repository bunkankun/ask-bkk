# Fó shuō dà jiā yè wèn dà bǎo jī zhèng fǎ jīng 佛說大迦葉問大寶積正法經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Mahākāśyapa's Inquiry Concerning the True Dharma of the Great Treasure-Heap*
by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

This 5-fascicle text by [[施護]] Dānapāla is the Northern-Sòng version of the *Kāśyapa-parivarta*, paralleling the earlier [[KR6f0042|Lokakṣema T0350]] and [[KR6f0043|anonymous T0351]]. The 5-fascicle scope reflects an expanded recension of the underlying Indic text relative to the earlier Chinese versions.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[施護]] Dānapāla's Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau activity (980 – 1017 CE).

The Taishō text (T0352) is established on the standard apparatus.
