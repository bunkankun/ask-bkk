# Fù zǐ hé jí jīng 父子合集經
*The Father-and-Son Joint Compilation Sūtra (Pitāputra-samāgama)*
by [[日稱]] (Rì-chēng *et al.*, 等譯)

## About the work

This 20-fascicle text by [[日稱]] Rì-chēng and his Sòng imperial-bureau team is the *Pitāputra-samāgama-sūtra* — the Mahāyāna sūtra on the Buddha's reunion with his father King Śuddhodana. The text was incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] tradition and also circulated independently.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[日稱]] Rì-chēng's mid-11th-century Sòng-bureau activity, c. 1058 – 1078 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work is one of the substantial Northern-Sòng Mahāyāna translation projects.

The Taishō text (T0320) is established on the standard apparatus, including the *Shī-gǔ* 獅谷 witness.
