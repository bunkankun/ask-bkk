# Chēng zàn jìng tǔ fó shè shòu jīng 稱讚淨土佛攝受經
*The Sūtra Praising the Pure Land and the Buddha's Embrace*
by [[玄奘]] (Xuánzàng, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng is the second Chinese version of the *Smaller Sukhāvatīvyūhasūtra*, parallel to [[KR6f0082|Kumārajīva's T0366]]. Xuánzàng's translation, while more philologically faithful to the Sanskrit, never displaced Kumārajīva's older, stylistically more polished version in liturgical practice.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[玄奘]] Xuánzàng's Cháng'ān period (645 – 664 CE).

The Taishō text (T0367) is established on the standard apparatus.
