# Ā mí tuó jīng yào jiě 阿彌陀經要解
*Essential Explication of the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[智旭]] (Zhì-xù / Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù, 解)

## About the work

The *Yào jiě* in 1 fascicle is the late-Míng commentary on the [[KR6f0082|*Smaller Sukhāvatī*]] by [[智旭]] *Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù* 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), one of the *Four Great Masters of the Late Míng*. It is one of the most influential single Pure Land commentaries in late-imperial Chinese Buddhism and remained the standard introductory text in subsequent Chinese Pure Land study.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (1620 – 1655) reflects [[智旭]] Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù's mature scholarly period leading up to his death.

The Taishō text (T1762) is established on the standard apparatus.

## Other points of interest

- Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù's *Yào jiě* — a late-Míng synthetic Buddhist work — represents the maturation of the lay-monastic Pure Land syncretist tradition that would shape modern Chinese Buddhist revival in the 20th century under figures like Yìn-guāng 印光.
