# Fó shuō ā mí tuó sān yē sān fó sà lóu fó tán guò dù rén dào jīng 佛說阿彌陀三耶三佛薩樓佛檀過度人道經
*The Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Amitābha Samyak-saṃbuddha Surpassing-the-Path-of-Beings*
by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

This 2-fascicle text by [[支謙]] Zhī Qiān is the second early Chinese version of the *Sukhāvatī-vyūha-sūtra*, after [[KR6f0061|Lokakṣema's T0361]] but before [[KR6f0060|the canonical T0360]]. The unusually long, transcription-heavy title preserves much of the Sanskrit original phonologically and is characteristic of the early-Three-Kingdoms transcription style.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[支謙]] Zhī Qiān's Wú-period activity (222 – 253 CE).

The Taishō text (T0362) is established on the standard apparatus.
