# Rù fǎ jiè tǐ xìng jīng 入法界體性經
*The Sūtra on Entering the Body-Nature of the Dharma-Realm*
by [[闍那崛多]] (Jñānagupta, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[闍那崛多]] Jñānagupta is a Mahāyāna sūtra on the doctrine of *fǎ jiè tǐ xìng* 法界體性 (the *body-nature of the dharma-realm*) — i.e. the foundational nature of the *dharma-dhātu*. The work was incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] tradition.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[闍那崛多]] Jñānagupta's Suí-imperial-bureau activity (587 – 600 CE).

The Taishō text (T0355) is established on the standard apparatus.
