# Shèng mán bǎo kū 勝鬘寶窟
*The Treasure-Cave Commentary on the Śrīmālā Sūtra*
by [[吉藏]] (Jí-zàng, 撰)

## About the work

The *Shèng mán bǎo kū* in 3 fascicles is the great Suí-period commentary by [[吉藏]] Jí-zàng on the [[KR6f0045|*Śrīmālā-siṃha-nāda-sūtra*]] (T0353). One of Jí-zàng's most influential commentaries, it remained the standard authoritative reading of the *Śrīmālā* in East Asian Buddhist scholarship throughout the medieval period and was foundational for the *tathāgatagarbha* doctrinal tradition.

### Abstract

The work belongs to [[吉藏]] Jí-zàng's mature scholarly period at the imperial Suí court (595 – 623 CE).

The Taishō text (T1744) is established on the standard apparatus.
