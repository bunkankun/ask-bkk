# Fó shuō dà chéng wú liàng shòu zhuāng yán jīng 佛說大乘無量壽莊嚴經
*The Great-Vehicle Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on the Adornment of Amitāyus*
by [[法賢]] (Fǎxián / Devaśānti, 譯)

## About the work

This 3-fascicle text by [[法賢]] Fǎxián is the Northern-Sòng version of the *Sukhāvatī-vyūha-sūtra*. The five extant Chinese translations — [[KR6f0060|T0360]], [[KR6f0061|T0361]], [[KR6f0062|T0362]], the present T0363, and the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa* assembly 5]] — provide a uniquely rich comparative apparatus for the textual history of the *Larger Sukhāvatī*.

### Prefaces

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[法賢]] Fǎxián's Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau activity (982 – 1001 CE).

The Taishō text (T0363) is established on the standard apparatus.
