# Wú liàng shòu jīng yì shū 無量壽經義疏 (Jí-zàng)
*Commentary on the Meaning of the Sūtra of Amitāyus*
by [[吉藏]] (Jí-zàng, 撰)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6f0060|*Wú liàng shòu jīng* T0360]] is by [[吉藏]] Jí-zàng (the Sān-lùn-school master, 549–623). The work presents the Sān-lùn-school *Madhyamaka* reading of the Pure Land sūtra, distinct from the *Dìlùn*-Yogācāra reading of [[KR6f0065|the parallel Huì-yuǎn commentary]].

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (595 – 623) reflects [[吉藏]] Jí-zàng's mature scholarly period.

The Taishō text (T1746) is established on the standard apparatus.
