# Fó shuō dà chéng pútísà cáng zhèng fǎ jīng 佛說大乘菩薩藏正法經
*The Sūtra of the True Dharma of the Bodhisattva-Piṭaka of the Great Vehicle*
by [[法護]] (Fǎhù / Dharmapāla, *et al.*, 等譯)

## About the work

This 40-fascicle text by [[法護]] Fǎ-hù (Dharmapāla, the Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau translator) and his team is the *Bodhisattva-piṭaka* (菩薩藏) — the Mahāyāna canonical collection on bodhisattva-path doctrine. The text was incorporated into the [[KR6f0001|*Mahā-ratnakūṭa*]] tradition as one of its 49 assemblies and stands among the most substantial Northern-Sòng *Bǎo-jī*-tradition translation projects.

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[法護]] Fǎhù / Dharmapāla's Northern-Sòng imperial-bureau activity, c. 1004 – 1058 CE. The bracket adopted here reflects this window. At 40 fascicles, the work is one of the most substantial Sòng Buddhist translation projects, paralleling the great Tang [[KR6f0001|*Bǎo jī*]] and [[KR6e0010|*Huáyán*]] projects in scale.

The Taishō text (T0316) is established on the standard apparatus.
