# Ā mí tuó jīng yì jì 阿彌陀經義記
*Notes on the Meaning of the Amitābha Sūtra*
by [[智顗]] (Zhì-yǐ, 說 / lectured)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle commentary by [[智顗]] Zhì-yǐ on the [[KR6f0082|*Smaller Sukhāvatī*]] (T0366) provides the Tiāntái-school reading of the Pure Land devotional sūtra.

### Abstract

The bracket adopted here (580 – 597) reflects [[智顗]] Zhì-yǐ's mature scholarly period.
