# Fó shuō sān shí wǔ fó míng lǐ chàn wén 佛說三十五佛名禮懺文
*The Worship-Repentance Text of the Names of Thirty-Five Buddhas*
by [[不空]] (Amoghavajra, 譯)

## About the work

This 1-fascicle text by [[不空]] Amoghavajra is the *Sanskrit Triskandhaka* — the famous Buddhist confession-repentance text invoking the names of the *thirty-five Buddhas of confession* (三十五佛). The text became one of the most widely chanted Buddhist liturgical texts in East Asia and Tibet, used in monastic confession-rituals throughout the medieval period.

*No formal preface.*

### Abstract

The translation belongs to [[不空]] Amoghavajra's Cháng'ān period (746 – 774 CE). The bracket adopted here reflects this window. The work is one of the principal texts of the Esoteric-Buddhist devotional tradition that Amoghavajra established at the Tang court.

The Taishō text (T0326) is established on the standard apparatus.
