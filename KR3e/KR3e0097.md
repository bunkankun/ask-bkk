# Lán-tái guǐ-fàn 蘭臺軌範
*The Standards of the Orchid Terrace*
by [[徐大椿]] (Xú Dàchūn, *zì* Língtāi, *hào* Huí-xī lǎorén, 1693–1771, of Wú-jiāng, 清)

## About the work

Xú Dàchūn's comprehensive clinical-medicine reference, in 8 juan, completed Qián-lóng 29 (1764). The title's "Orchid Terrace" (Lán-tái 蘭臺) alludes to the imperial archives, signaling Xú's authoritative-canonical aspiration. The work covers all major disease-categories: ***Tōng-zhì fāng*** 通治方 (general-treatment prescriptions, juan 1); ***Fēng*** 風, ***Bì lì-jié*** 痺歷節, ***Wěi*** 痿, ***Jué*** 厥, ***Xū-láo*** 虛勞, ***Xiāo-zhèng*** 消症 (juan 2); ***Shānghán***, ***Bǎi-hé*** 百合, ***Gǎn-mào*** 感冒, ***Hán-rè***, ***Huò-luàn*** 霍亂, ***Chì*** 痓, ***Lóng-bì lì lín*** (juan 3); ***Shī*** 濕, ***Yē*** 暍, ***Jiē-nüè***, ***Lì*** 痢 (juan 4); etc. Each disease-gate provides discussion plus selected prescriptions drawn from Xú's careful evaluation of the medical canon. The work is one of the major Qīng-period systematic medical references.

### Tiyao

*[Source 000 file's *tíyào* truncated; Kyoto Zinbun 0214106.]*

The work organizes clinical medicine by disease-category, with each gate providing theoretical discussion plus curated prescriptions. The categorical sequence follows a careful ordering: general → wind / impediment / atrophy / collapse / deficiency / wasting → cold-damage / Bǎi-hé / cold-and-heat / convulsion / urinary-blockage → damp / heat-stroke / malaria / dysentery → other major specialty topics. The work is based on Xú's lifetime clinical experience and represents his mature medical-clinical synthesis.

### Abstract

Composition window: 1764/1764, the date of the *Lán-tái guǐ-fàn* completion (per Xú's preface).

The work's significance:

(a) **The major Qīng-period systematic clinical reference**: Xú's 8-juan systematic disease-treatment reference is one of the more authoritative Qīng clinical works, drawing on Xú's careful Confucian-philological method and lifetime clinical experience.

(b) **The "Orchid Terrace" framing**: the title's claim to imperial-archive authority signals Xú's confidence in the work's standard-setting role. Xú lived in retirement and refused official appointments, but the work claims for itself a quasi-official authority.

(c) **The integration of Xú's specialized works**: the *Lán-tái guǐ-fàn* synthesizes the doctrinal-and-prescriptive reasoning developed across Xú's specialty works (the *Bǎi-zhǒng lù* on materia medica, the *Lèi-fāng* on prescriptions, the *Yuán-liú lùn* on theory).

The catalog meta dynasty 清 is correct.
