# Shānghán lèi-fāng 傷寒類方
*The Cold-Damage Prescriptions Categorically Arranged*
by [[徐大椿]] (Xú Dàchūn, *zì* Língtāi, *hào* Huí-xī lǎorén, 1693–1771, 清)

## About the work

Xú Dàchūn's reorganization of Zhāng Jī's *Shānghán lùn* by **prescription-type** rather than by the conventional channel-or-syndrome organization. The work groups together all prescriptions derived from a base prescription — e.g., the *Guì-zhī tāng* category includes 19 prescriptions: *Guì-zhī tāng*, *Guì-zhī jiā fù-zǐ tāng*, *Guì-zhī jiā guì tāng*, *Guì-zhī qù sháo-yào tāng*, *Guì-zhī qù sháo-yào jiā fù-zǐ tāng*, *Guì-zhī jiā hòu-pò xìng-rén tāng*, *Xiǎo jiàn-zhōng tāng*, *Guì-zhī jiā sháo-yào shēng-jiāng rén-shēn xīn-jiā tāng*, *Guì-zhī gān-cǎo tāng*, *Fú-líng guì-zhī gān-cǎo dà-zǎo tāng*, *Guì-zhī má-huáng gè-bàn tāng*, *Guì-zhī èr má-huáng yī tāng*, etc. The categorical-by-prescription organization makes the *Shānghán lùn* more accessible for clinical-prescription study, complementing the conventional channel-by-channel organization. The work is one of the more clinically useful Qīng-period reorganizations of the *Shānghán* canon.

### Tiyao

The work organizes Zhāng Jī's prescriptions into categories, with each base-prescription as the head and its variants gathered beneath. This is one of the more clinically useful reorganizations of the *Shānghán lùn*'s prescription material.

### Abstract

Composition window: 1759/1759, the date of Xú's preface.

The work's significance:

(a) **The categorical-by-prescription reorganization**: a methodological innovation in *Shānghán* studies. Where conventional *Shānghán* literature organizes by channel-disease (Tài-yáng, Yáng-míng, etc.) or by symptom-pattern, Xú's prescription-categorical organization allows clinical study by base-prescription-and-variants — useful for prescription-formulation reasoning.

(b) **Clinical-pedagogical accessibility**: the work serves as a quick-reference for the relations among related prescriptions, allowing the practitioner to quickly understand the scope of *jiā-jiǎn* (additions-and-subtractions) variants from any given base.

(c) **Part of Xú's *Shānghán*-philological project**: alongside Xú's other *Shānghán*-related works, the *Lèi-fāng* contributes to the comprehensive Qīng-period systematic reorganization of the cold-damage canon.

The catalog meta dynasty 清 is correct.
