# Zhuànjì lèi 傳記類
*Histories — Biographical Writings*

## Scope and scholarly tradition

The *Zhuànjì lèi* is the seventh subdivision of the *Shǐbù* 史部 in the Imperial Library's fourfold scheme. It collects works of formal biography that fall outside the *lièzhuàn* 列傳 sections of the *Zhèngshǐ* 正史: free-standing chronological biographies (*niánpǔ* 年譜), single-life accounts (*xíngzhuàng* 行狀, *biézhuàn* 別傳), thematic biographical anthologies (women, recluses, eminent ministers, "talented men" of a dynasty, school-and-lineage compendia), prefectural and local *xiāngxián* collections, *xuéàn* 學案 (school cases) tracing Confucian transmission, eyewitness campaign and embassy records that double as biographies of their compilers, and (under the *Sìkù* editors' final rubric *biélùzhīshǔ* 別錄之屬) travel diaries with a strong autobiographical cast. As Wilkinson notes (§9, "Biographies and prosopography"), the term *zhuànjì* 傳記 displaces the older *zázhuàn* 雜傳 from the Sòng onward and becomes the standard cover-term for biography in the imperial bibliographic tradition; the genre's diversification — especially the rise of the *niánpǔ* in the Sòng and of the *xuéàn* in the late Míng / early Qīng — is what gives the *Sìkù*'s *Zhuànjì* division its distinctive shape.

The 61 works collected here span from the Western Hàn (Liú Xiàng's *Gǔ liènǚ zhuàn* 古列女傳, c. 16 BCE) through the high Qiánlóng (Lǐ Qīngfù's *Mǐnzhōng lǐxué yuānyuán kǎo* 閩中理學淵源考, c. 1755), with the heaviest representation from the Southern Sòng and the MíngQīng transition — the two periods in which biographical compilation and *Dàoxué* lineage history were most actively cultivated. The *Sìkù* editors organize the division along four traditional sub-categories: *shèngxiánzhīshǔ* 聖賢之屬 (sages, e.g. the Confucius cluster), *míngrénzhīshǔ* 名人之屬 (named individuals, dominated by *niánpǔ* and single-life *xíngzhuàng*), *zǒnglùzhīshǔ* 總錄之屬 (collective anthologies, the bulk of the division), and *biélùzhīshǔ* 別錄之屬 (separate records, where the campaign narratives and travel diaries are placed). Wilkinson observes (§9, Table 25) that perhaps 600,000 Chinese biographies survive, the vast majority in MíngQīng gazetteers; the *Sìkù*'s *Zhuànjì* selection is therefore a tightly filtered canonical core, with the *cúnmù* 存目 ledger catching everything else.

## Important texts and text clusters

- **The Liú Xiàng anchor.** *Gǔ liènǚ zhuàn* 古列女傳 [[KR2g0017]] is the foundational Hàn anthology of women's biography and the model for the entire *zǒnglù* sub-category. Liú Xiàng's editorial procedure — thematic categories, terminal four-character *sòng* 頌, illustrated folding-screen format — set a template that was followed continuously down to the Míng court compilation *Gǔjīn liènǚ zhuàn* 古今列女傳 [[KR2g0035]].

- **The Confucius / Kǒng-family cluster.** *Kǒngzǐ biānnián* 孔子編年 [[KR2g0001]], the *Sìkù* editors' chosen head of the division, is paired with the two great Kǒng-family gazetteer-genealogies, *Dōngjiā zájì* 東家雜記 [[KR2g0002]] (Southern Sòng) and *Kǒngshì zǔtíng guǎngjì* 孔氏祖庭廣記 [[KR2g0006]] (Jīn). *Yànzǐ chūnqiū* 晏子春秋 [[KR2g0003]] — formally a Warring-States compilation — is placed in the *shèngxián* sub-category alongside them.

- **The Sòng *niánpǔ* form.** *Dù gōngbù niánpǔ* 杜工部年譜 [[KR2g0007]] and *Dù gōngbù shī niánpǔ* 杜工部詩年譜 [[KR2g0008]], paired in the *Sìkù* edition, are early specimens of the genre Wilkinson identifies as the most important Sòng innovation in biographical form (§9.5); *Zhūzǐ niánpǔ* 朱子年譜 [[KR2g0016]] is the standard Qīng *niánpǔ* of Zhū Xī.

- **The *Yuè Fēi* documentary archive.** *Jīntuó cuìbiān* 金佗稡編 [[KR2g0010]] is the *locus classicus* for Yuè Fēi's posthumous rehabilitation and the documentary foundation of the entire Chinese "loyalist general" cult. Compiled in two stages (1218, 1228) by his grandson [[岳珂]], it sets the pattern for later imperially-sanctioned martyrology in the division — including *Zhōngzhēn lù* 忠貞錄 [[KR2g0013]] (the Hé Téngjiāo cult) and *Zhūgě zhōngwǔ shū* 諸葛忠武書 [[KR2g0014]].

- **The Sòng *Dàoxué* lineage project.** *YīLuò yuānyuán lù* 伊洛淵源錄 [[KR2g0023]] and *Sòng míngchén yánxíng lù qiánjí* 宋名臣言行錄前集 [[KR2g0024]] are Zhū Xī's twin prosopographical works that establish the *Cheng-Zhū* lineage and the *Bei-Sòng* ministerial canon respectively. [[杜大珪]]'s *Míngchén bēizhuàn wǎnyǎn zhī jí* 名臣碑傳琬琰之集 [[KR2g0025]] (1194) is the parallel stele-anthology, the principal working source for Sòng prosopography down to the present.

- **The Yuán prosopographies.** *Táng cáizǐ zhuàn* 唐才子傳 [[KR2g0032]] (1304) by [[辛文房]] is the standard collective biography of Táng poets, while *Yuáncháo míngchén shìlüè* 元朝名臣事略 [[KR2g0033]] (1329) by [[蘇天爵]] is the foundational document for Yuán-period prosopography, on whose scaffold the *Yuánshǐ* *lièzhuàn* were built.

- **The MíngQīng *xuéàn* / *Lǐxué* lineage compendia.** *Míngrú xuéàn* 明儒學案 [[KR2g0046]] is Huáng Zōngxī's foundational instance of the *xuéàn* sub-genre. Three early-Qīng counter-and-correctives stand alongside it: [[萬斯同]]'s *Rúlín zōngpài* 儒林宗派 [[KR2g0049]], [[沈佳]]'s *Cheng-Zhū*-leaning *Míngrú yánxíng lù* 明儒言行錄 [[KR2g0050]], and [[朱軾]]'s Yōngzhèng-court synthesis *Shǐzhuàn sān biān* 史傳三編 [[KR2g0051]]. [[李清馥]]'s *Mǐnzhōng lǐxué yuānyuán kǎo* 閩中理學淵源考 [[KR2g0052]] (c. 1742–1755) closes the sequence with the most extensive single account of the *Cheng-Zhū* lineage in Fújiàn.

- **The Qing imperial-Banner archive.** Four Qiánlóng *Qīndìng* 欽定 (imperially-fixed) compilations form a single documentary block on Manchu Banner and tributary-prince history: *Qīndìng Bāqí Mǎnzhōu shìzú tōngpǔ* 欽定八旗滿州氏族通譜 [[KR2g0044]] (1744), *Qīndìng shèngcháo xùnjié zhūchén lù* 欽定勝朝殉節諸臣錄 [[KR2g0045]] (1776), *Qīndìng wàifān Měnggǔ Huíbù wánggōng biǎozhuàn* 欽定外藩蒙古回部王公表傳 [[KR2g0043]] (1779), and *Qīndìng zōngshì wánggōng gōngjī biǎozhuàn* 欽定宗室王公功績表傳 [[KR2g0042]] (1781). They are the indispensable sources for Manchu Banner prosopography and the official *Sìkù*-period statement of Qiánlóng's reading of the Qīng founders' history.

- **The SòngYuán travel-diary cluster (*biélù*).** [[滕甫]]'s *Zhēngnán lù* 征南錄 [[KR2g0053]] (Nóng Zhìgāo campaign, 1052–54), [[陸游]]'s *Rùshǔ jì* 入蜀記 [[KR2g0056]] (1170), and [[范成大]]'s pair *Cānluán lù* 驂鸞錄 [[KR2g0054]] (1172–75) and *Wúchuán lù* 吳船錄 [[KR2g0055]] (1177) together constitute the classical Southern-Sòng corpus of the *rìjì*-style travel-diary as biographical form. *Xīshǐ jì* 西使記 [[KR2g0057]] is the principal Chinese eyewitness record of the Mongol western campaign of 1252–60 (Cháng Dé's mission to Hülegü), and the canonical Chinese source on the destruction of Alamut and Baghdad.

- **The MíngQīng provincial *xiāngxián*.** *Jīngkǒu qíjiù zhuàn* 京口耆舊傳 [[KR2g0029]] (Zhènjiāng), *Pǔyáng rénwù jì* 浦陽人物記 [[KR2g0034]] (Pǔyáng), *Bǎiyuè xiānxián zhì* 百越先賢志 [[KR2g0040]] (Guǎngdōng), and *Zhōngzhōu rénwù kǎo* 中州人物考 [[KR2g0047]] (Hénán) exemplify the *xiāngxián* genre, a sub-form to which the *Sìkù* editors were notably hospitable.

## Important persons

- [[劉向]] — Western Hàn imperial-library editor and author of the *Gǔ liènǚ zhuàn*, the prototype of the *zǒnglù* sub-category.
- [[皇甫謐]] — WèiJìn philologist whose *Gāoshì zhuàn* 高士傳 [[KR2g0018]] founded the *yǐnyì* 隱逸 collective-biography tradition.
- [[朱熹]] — author of the twin Sòng *Dàoxué* prosopographies *YīLuò yuānyuán lù* and *Sòng míngchén yánxíng lù*, the methodological foundation of the entire *xuéàn* lineage in this division.
- [[陸游]] and [[范成大]] — the two Southern-Sòng *shīrén*-historians whose joint travel-diary corpus established the *rìjì* as a major prose genre.
- [[岳珂]] — Yuè Fēi's grandson, whose *Jīntuó cuìbiān* is the documentary anchor of the Chinese loyalist-general cult.
- [[蘇天爵]] — leading Yuán *Hànrén* historian whose *Yuáncháo míngchén shìlüè* became the working scaffold for the *Yuánshǐ lièzhuàn*.
- [[辛文房]] — early-Yuán *Sèmù* compiler whose *Táng cáizǐ zhuàn* remains the foundational reference for the lives of Táng poets.
- [[杜大珪]] — Southern-Sòng *jìnshì* who compiled the largest Sòng stele-biography anthology and the principal working source for Sòng prosopography.
- [[黃宗羲]] — *Dōnglín*-affiliated Míng loyalist whose *Míngrú xuéàn* founded the *xuéàn* sub-genre and shaped all subsequent *Lǐxué* historiography.
- [[萬斯同]] — Huáng Zōngxī's leading pupil, principal private historiographer of the *Míngshǐ* project, and author of the methodologically corrective *Rúlín zōngpài*.
- [[王懋竑]] — Qīng *kǎojù* scholar whose *Zhūzǐ niánpǔ* is the standard *niánpǔ* of Zhū Xī.
- [[高宗弘曆]] — Qiánlóng emperor; *aedile commendantis* of the four *Qīndìng* Banner-and-prince *biǎozhuàn* that anchor the Qīng segment of the division.
- [[李清馥]] — Fújiàn *Cheng-Zhū* prosopographer whose *Mǐnzhōng lǐxué yuānyuán kǎo* closes the *xuéàn* tradition in the *Sìkù*.
- [[滕甫]] — Northern-Sòng official whose eyewitness *Zhēngnán lù* is the opening text in the division's *biélù* travel/campaign cluster.

## Topics

- **Moral exemplarity and its categories.** From Liú Xiàng onward, the *Zhuànjì* division is organized by moral category — *liènǚ* (chaste / exemplary women), *gāoshì* (eminent recluses), *liánlì* (incorrupt officials, see *Liánlì zhuàn* 廉吏傳 [[KR2g0021]]), *zhōngzhēn* (loyal-and-true), *míngrú* (eminent Confucians). The categories are not neutral filing-headers; they encode the work's normative argument.
- **The *niánpǔ* as biographical form.** A Sòng innovation (Wilkinson §9.5), applied first to the great Táng poets ([[KR2g0007]], [[KR2g0008]]) and to Confucius himself ([[KR2g0001]]), then to the Sòng *Dàoxué* masters ([[KR2g0016]]). The genre's premise — that a single life can be reconstructed year by year from documentary fragments — anticipates and trains the *kǎozhèng* 考證 sensibility.
- **The *xuéàn* and the historiography of Confucian transmission.** Zhū Xī's *YīLuò yuānyuán lù* [[KR2g0023]] is the methodological seed; Huáng Zōngxī's *Míngrú xuéàn* [[KR2g0046]] is the mature form; the early-Qīng quartet ([[KR2g0049]], [[KR2g0050]], [[KR2g0051]], [[KR2g0052]]) plays out the form's possibilities. The central debate is whether to organize by *xuépài* (school) or by chronology, and whether to admit *Yáojiāng* (Wáng Yángmíng) figures.
- **Sòng partisan documentation and the politics of rehabilitation.** *Jīntuó cuìbiān* [[KR2g0010]] and *Wèi Zhènggōng jiànlù* 魏鄭公諫錄 [[KR2g0004]] together with its Yuán continuation [[KR2g0012]] are documentary archives compiled to defend or restore a posthumous reputation; *Qìngyuán dǎngjìn* 慶元黨禁 [[KR2g0027]] is the partisan record of the 1196 *Dàoxué* proscription. The genre's premise is that historiography is itself a form of vindication.
- **The Qīng imperial reordering of dynastic memory.** The four Qiánlóng *Qīndìng* compilations [[KR2g0042]]–[[KR2g0045]] are not merely biographical; they are official re-readings — Dorgon rehabilitated, the Banner founders ennobled, the Míng martyrs canonized — that recast Qīng identity at the high point of the *Sìkù* project.
- **Travel-diary as autobiography.** The *biélù* sub-category at the end of the division ([[KR2g0053]]–[[KR2g0061]]) treats the eyewitness *rìjì* as a biographical form: the traveller's *kǎozhèng* notes, route observations, and self-positioning constitute a kind of indirect life-narrative. The Sòng pair Lù Yóu / Fàn Chéngdà is canonical; the Yuán *Xīshǐ jì* [[KR2g0057]] extends the form to inter-civilizational embassy.
- **Provincial *xiāngxián* compilation.** *Xiāngxián* (local-worthy) anthologies of a single prefecture or province become a recognizable sub-form from the Southern Sòng onward; they are the prefectural-gazetteer's biographical core extracted and re-circulated as free-standing texts.
