# Fǎhuá jīng shòu-shǒu (juàn-shǒu) 法華經授手〔卷首〕
*Hand-Bestowal Commentary on the Lotus Sūtra: Front Matter*
by [[智祥]] (Zhì-xiáng / Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng / Tīng-yún, 識 — labeller / identifier)

## About the work

The opening front-matter (*juàn-shǒu* 卷首) volume of Zhì-xiáng's three-part *Fǎhuá jīng shòu-shǒu* 法華經授手 commentarial complex: this work ([[KR6d0087]], the front matter), the *Shòu-shǒu kē* ([[KR6d0088]], the sectional analysis), and the *Shòu-shǒu* proper ([[KR6d0089]], the ten-juan main commentary). Compiled by Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng 頻吉智祥 (1637–1709), the early-Qīng Línjì Chán master.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0621 recension consists primarily of front-matter material — prefaces, structural overviews, and editorial framing — rather than substantive commentary. The body attribution names Zhì-xiáng as 識 (*shí*, "labeller / identifier"), indicating his editorial responsibility for the front-matter material specifically rather than for the substantive commentary itself.

### Abstract

The *Shòu-shǒu* 授手 (literally "hand-bestowal" or "passing-by-hand") title is drawn from the classical Chinese tutorial metaphor of the master directly transmitting knowledge from hand to hand to the student, and indicates a commentary intended for direct face-to-face pedagogical use. The three-part *Shòu-shǒu* complex — front matter, sectional analysis, and main commentary — is parallel to the *Dà-chéng* complex ([[KR6d0084]], [[KR6d0085]], [[KR6d0086]]) in providing complete navigational and substantive apparatus for systematic Lotus Sūtra study.

The composition is bracketed within Zhì-xiáng's productive period c. 1670–1709, with the *Shòu-shǒu* complex most plausibly placed in his mature productive years.
