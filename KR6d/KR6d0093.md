# Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí kē 法華經科拾科
*Sectional Analysis of the Sectional-Gleanings on the Lotus Sūtra*
sectional structure by [[佛閑]] (Fó-xián, 立科); re-redacted by [[智一]] (Zhì-yī, 重訂)

## About the work

The sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) volume of the three-text *Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí* commentarial complex by Fó-xián 佛閑 of Pǔ-dé-sì 普德寺 in Nán-jīng, with re-redaction (*chóng-dìng* 重訂) by Zhì-yī 智一. The work is the navigational apparatus to the *Kē-shí* main commentary ([[KR6d0094]]) and follows the standard Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng editorial pattern of providing structural-outline navigational aids for major commentarial productions.

### Prefaces

The text in the X33n0627 recension carries the standard front matter and consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kē-pàn* sectional division of the *Kē-shí* main commentary.

### Abstract

The *Kē-shí kē* belongs together with the *Kē-shí xuán-tán juàn-shǒu* ([[KR6d0092]]) as the auxiliary navigational apparatus to the *Kē-shí* main commentary ([[KR6d0094]]). The dating is securely fixed by Zhì-yī's 1705 preface (preserved in [[KR6d0092]]).
