# Shān-jiā yì-yuàn 山家義苑
*The Mountain-House Doctrinal Garden*
by [[可觀]] (Kěguān / Zhú'ān Kěguān, 述); verified by [[智增]] (Zhìzēng, 證)

## About the work

A two-*juan* Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* 山家 doctrinal compendium by **Zhú-ān Kě-guān** 竹菴可觀, with editorial verification (*zhèng* 證) by **Zhì-zēng** 智增. The title — *yì-yuàn* 義苑 ("doctrinal garden") — indicates a topical compilation gathering the principal *shān-jiā* doctrinal positions into a unified anthology.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Kě-guān's productive period c. 1140–1220.
