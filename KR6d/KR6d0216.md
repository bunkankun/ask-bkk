# Shǐ-zhōng xīn-yào zhù 始終心要注
*Annotation on the Beginning-and-End Heart-Essential*
by [[從義]] (Cóngyì / Shénzhì, 注)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng annotated commentary by **Cóngyì** 從義 (1042–1091) on Zhànrán's *Kē shǐ-zhōng xīn-yào* ([[KR6d0215]]). Companion to Cóngyì's other Sòng *shān-jiā* productions ([[KR6d0063]], [[KR6d0139]], [[KR6d0140]], [[KR6d0169]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Cóngyì's productive period c. 1062–1091.
