# Tiāntái Zhì-zhě dà-shī zhāi-jì lǐ-zàn wén 天台智者大師齋忌禮讚文
*Fast-Day Commemoration Worship-Encomium Text for the Tiāntái Great Master Zhìyǐ*
by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng commemorative liturgy by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032) for the periodic monastic commemoration of Zhìyǐ 智顗 (538–597), the founding patriarch of the Tiāntái school. The work belongs to the broader Tiāntái institutional commemorative tradition that developed around the school's founding figure.

### Abstract

The *Zhāi-jì lǐ-zàn wén* provides the standard Sòng-period Tiāntái liturgical material for the *zhāi-jì* 齋忌 (fast-day commemoration) services held annually on the anniversary of Zhìyǐ's death. The composition is bracketed within Zūnshì's productive period at Tiānzhú-sì in Háng-zhōu, c. 996–1032.
