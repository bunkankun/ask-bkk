# Tiāntái Zhìzhě dàshī zhāijì lǐzàn wén 天台智者大師齋忌禮讚文
*Fast-Day Commemoration Worship-Encomium Text for the Tiāntái Great Master Zhìyǐ*
by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng commemorative liturgy by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032) for the periodic monastic commemoration of [[智顗]] Zhìyǐ (538–597), the founding patriarch of the Tiāntái school. The work belongs to the broader Tiāntái institutional commemorative tradition that developed around the school's founding figure.

### Abstract

The *Zhāijì lǐzàn wén* provides the standard Sòng-period Tiāntái liturgical material for the *zhāijì* 齋忌 (fast-day commemoration) services held annually on the anniversary of Zhìyǐ's death. The composition is bracketed within Zūnshì's productive period at Tiānzhúsì in Hángzhōu, c. 996–1032.
