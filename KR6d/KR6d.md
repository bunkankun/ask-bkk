# Fǎhuá bù 法華部 — Saddharmapuṇḍarīka (Lotus) Section

## Scope and scholarly tradition

`KR6d` corresponds to the *Fǎhuá bù* 法華部 of the Taishō structural ordering — the Lotus Sūtra and its satellite scriptures in Taishō volume 9, the Lotus sub-commentary tradition in Taishō volumes 26 and 33–34, the Tiāntái 天台 doctrinal-and-meditation corpus in Taishō volume 46, the Lotus Dūnhuáng 敦煌 fragments in volume 85, and the very large Sòng–Qīng manuscript continuation of all these strands preserved in the *Xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏經. In Wilkinson's outline of the Taishō (§29.5.8), volume 9 is identified as "*Fǎhuá bù* (Lotus and other Mahāyāna sūtras)"; Kanripo's `KR6d` extends that vol. 9 nucleus outward to absorb all Chinese-language Lotus commentary and the entire Tiāntái scholastic apparatus that grows out of it, because in East-Asian classification the Tiāntái 天台 doctrinal tradition — including its meditation manuals, doctrinal-classification literature, and ritual / *chànyí* 懺儀 works — is canonically attached to the Lotus Sūtra rather than treated as an independent *zōng* 宗 section.

The principal scholarly tradition organising the division is therefore Tiāntái, whose founder Zhìyǐ 智顗 (538–597) was traditionally regarded as the patriarch and whose 593–597 lectures at Yùquánsì 玉泉寺, redacted by Guàndǐng 灌頂 (561–632), produced the *Tiāntái sāndàbù* 天台三大部 ("Three Great Treatises of Tiāntái") — *Fǎhuá xuányì* [[KR6d0006]], *Fǎhuá wénjù* [[KR6d0014]], and *Móhē zhǐguān* [[KR6d0130]] — around which most of the division is structured (Wilkinson §29.5.7.1 on Tiāntái; Hurvitz 1963 on Zhìyǐ; Lopez 2016 and Teiser & Stone 2009 on the Lotus Sūtra). Pre-Tiāntái Liáng exegesis (Fǎyún 法雲), the early Suí Sānlùn 三論 reading by Jízàng 吉藏, and the Táng Faxiang 法相 / Cí'ēn 慈恩 reading by Kuījī 窺基 are also represented; from the 8th century onward the division is dominated by Zhànrán 湛然 (711–782) and his Sòng-period successors of the Tiāntái revival, and from the 11th century by the long *shānjiā* / *shānwài* 山家／山外 controversy and its literature. The division extends through Yuán, Míng (with the Tiāntái revival under Zhìxù 智旭 1599–1655), and Qīng (under Língyào 靈耀 and Tōnglǐ 通理) to roughly the mid-18th century.

## Important texts and text clusters

The 249 per-text notes in `kb/KR6d/` organise into five recognisable clusters.

### The Lotus Sūtra itself and its parallel translations

- *Zhèng fǎhuá jīng* 正法華經 [[KR6d0002]] — Dharmarakṣa's 竺法護 translation of 286 CE, the earliest complete Chinese Lotus, surviving in 10 *juan*.
- *Miàofǎ liánhuá jīng* 妙法蓮華經 [[KR6d0001]] — Kumārajīva's 鳩摩羅什 translation of 406 CE, the dominant East-Asian recension in 7 *juan* and 28 chapters; the literary base for virtually all subsequent Lotus exegesis in Chinese.
- *Tiānpǐn miàofǎ liánhuá jīng* 添品妙法蓮華經 [[KR6d0003]] — the Suí (601) collation by Jñānagupta 闍那崛多 and Dharmagupta 笈多, incorporating the *Devadatta* chapter and the *Pǔmén pǐn* 普門品 verses.
- *Sàtán fēntuólì jīng* 薩曇分陀利經 [[KR6d0004]] — a Western Jìn anonymous partial translation of the *Devadatta* episode, preserving a textual stratum independent of the three full versions.

### Satellite Lotus-cycle sūtras (vol. 9)

- *Wúliàng yì jīng* 無量義經 [[KR6d0118]] — the Liáng-period "Sūtra of Innumerable Meanings," traditionally the prologue of the East-Asian "Threefold Lotus" 法華三部經.
- *Fóshuō Guān Pǔxián púsà xíngfǎ jīng* 佛說觀普賢菩薩行法經 [[KR6d0120]] — the Liú-Sòng-period "Sūtra of the Contemplation of Samantabhadra's Practice," the epilogue of the same triad.
- *Fóshuō fǎhuá sānmèi jīng* 佛說法華三昧經 [[KR6d0108]], *Bùtuìzhuǎn fǎlún jīng* 不退轉法輪經 [[KR6d0106]], *Dà fǎgǔ jīng* 大法鼓經 [[KR6d0109]], *Dà sàzhē Nígānzǐ suǒshuō jīng* 大薩遮尼乾子所說經 [[KR6d0111]] — Mahāyāna sūtras grouped with the Lotus on doctrinal and thematic grounds (one-vehicle teaching, irreversibility, the Tathāgata's hidden lifespan).

### The *Tiāntái sāndàbù* 天台三大部 and their commentaries

- *Miàofǎ liánhuá jīng xuányì* 妙法蓮華經玄義 [[KR6d0006]] — Zhìyǐ's 593 Yùquánsì lectures on the doctrinal meaning of the Lotus, recorded by Guàndǐng; the foundational systematic theology of Tiāntái, source for the *wǔshí bājiào* 五時八教 classification.
- *Miàofǎ liánhuá jīng wénjù* 妙法蓮華經文句 [[KR6d0014]] — Zhìyǐ's 587 phrase-by-phrase commentary on Kumārajīva's Lotus, recorded by Guàndǐng; pairs with the *Xuányì* as the doctrinal-plus-textual exegetical core.
- *Móhē zhǐguān* 摩訶止觀 [[KR6d0130]] — Zhìyǐ's 594 lectures on Tiāntái meditation, recorded by Guàndǐng; the most systematic *śamatha-vipaśyanā* manual in Chinese, organised on the *shíjìng × shíchéng* 十境十乘 matrix.
- *Fǎhuá xuányì shìqiān* 法華玄義釋籤 [[KR6d0007]], *Fǎhuá wénjù jì* 法華文句記 [[KR6d0015]], and *Zhǐguān fǔxíng zhuànhóngjué* 止觀輔行傳弘決 [[KR6d0131]] — Zhànrán's 湛然 (711–782) standard sub-commentaries on each member of the *sāndàbù*; the principal textual base of the Sòng Tiāntái revival.

### Alternative Lotus-commentary traditions

- *Fǎhuá jīng yìjì* 法華經義記 [[KR6d0005]] — Fǎyún's 法雲 Liáng-period exegesis, the principal southern pre-Tiāntái reading and the polemical target of Zhìyǐ's *Xuányì*.
- *Fǎhuá xuánlùn* 法華玄論 [[KR6d0023]], *Fǎhuá yìshū* 法華義疏 [[KR6d0024]], *Fǎhuá yóuyì* 法華遊意 [[KR6d0025]] — Jízàng's 吉藏 (549–623) Sānlùn 三論-school readings, doctrinally distinct from Tiāntái.
- *Miàofǎ liánhuá jīng xuánzàn* 妙法蓮華經玄贊 [[KR6d0026]] — Kuījī's 窺基 (632–682) Cí'ēn 慈恩 / Yogācāra reading of the Lotus, the principal Faxiang interpretation.
- *Fǎhuá zōngyào* 法華宗要 [[KR6d0032]] — the Sillan master Wǒnhyo's 元曉 short doctrinal synthesis, an important East-Asian non-Chinese voice.

### Tiāntái doctrinal, meditation, and ritual literature (vol. 46)

- *Sìjiào yì* 四教義 [[KR6d0166]] and *Tiāntái sìjiào yí* 天台四教儀 [[KR6d0168]] — Zhìyǐ's and the late-Táng / Korean Chégwan 諦觀's expositions of the four doctrinal teachings, the most-studied Tiāntái catechetical works.
- *Shí bùèr mén* 十不二門 [[KR6d0157]] and the very large body of commentary it provoked (e.g. *Shí bùèr mén zhǐyào chāo* [[KR6d0158]] by Zhīlǐ 知禮) — Zhànrán's 10-fold non-duality scheme, the textual flashpoint of the *shānjiā / shānwài* controversy.
- *Jīngāngbēi* 金剛錍 [[KR6d0175]] — Zhànrán's polemical defense of *wúqíng yǒu xìng* 無情有性 (Buddha-nature in insentient things), generative of a sub-commentary tradition into the Sòng.
- *Fǎhuá sānmèi chànyí* 法華三昧懺儀 [[KR6d0191]] and the related *chànyí* / *xíngfǎ* corpus — Zhìyǐ's Lotus-Samādhi liturgy and its Sòng elaborations (Zūnshì 遵式, Zhīlǐ 知禮), the principal Tiāntái ritual texts.
- *Sìmíng zūnzhě jiàoxíng lù* 四明尊者教行錄 [[KR6d0186]] — Zōngxiǎo's 宗曉 collected works of Zhīlǐ, the textual monument of the Sòng *shānjiā* school.
- *Jiàoguān gāngzōng* 教觀綱宗 [[KR6d0188]] — Zhìxù's 智旭 (1599–1655) Míng-period summary of Tiāntái doctrine, the principal late-imperial textbook.

## Important persons

- [[鳩摩羅什]] — translator of the standard Lotus recension (T262 = [[KR6d0001]]); the literary register of his translation supplied the canonical Sinitic vocabulary of all subsequent Lotus exegesis in this division.
- [[竺法護]] — translator of the earliest complete Chinese Lotus (T263 = [[KR6d0002]], 286) and of several satellite vol. 9 sūtras; his Lotus survives as the principal philological witness independent of Kumārajīva.
- [[闍那崛多]] — co-translator with Dharmagupta 笈多 of the Suí Lotus recension (T264 = [[KR6d0003]], 601), source of the textual additions to the received Kumārajīva Lotus.
- [[法雲]] — Liáng-period exegete of the Lotus ([[KR6d0005]]); the standard pre-Tiāntái southern reading, against which Zhìyǐ defined his own system.
- [[慧思]] — Tiāntái second patriarch and Zhìyǐ's teacher; author of the *Fǎhuá jīng ānlèxíng yì* [[KR6d0156]] and other proto-Tiāntái Lotus-meditation works.
- [[智顗]] — third Tiāntái patriarch and de facto founder of Chinese Tiāntái doctrine; lecturer of the *sāndàbù* and dozens of other works in this division (the most-cited person in `KR6d`, named in ≈ 25 per-text notes).
- [[灌頂]] — Zhìyǐ's principal disciple and the redactor / co-author of nearly the entire Zhìyǐ corpus; also author of the *Guóqīng bǎilù* [[KR6d0183]], the documentary record of the early Tiāntái community.
- [[吉藏]] — Sānlùn master whose Lotus commentaries ([[KR6d0023]]–[[KR6d0025]], [[KR6d0128]]) represent the principal alternative early-Suí exegetical line.
- [[窺基]] — Cí'ēn / Faxiang master who gave the Lotus a Yogācāra reading ([[KR6d0026]]); the source of the *xuánzàn* sub-commentary tradition.
- [[湛然]] — sixth Tiāntái patriarch and reviver of the school in the mid-Táng; author of the canonical sub-commentaries on the *sāndàbù* ([[KR6d0007]], [[KR6d0015]], [[KR6d0131]]) and of the doctrinally generative *Shí bùèr mén* [[KR6d0157]] and *Jīngāngbēi* [[KR6d0175]].
- [[空海]] — founder of Japanese Shingon and author of seven Lotus-related Heian works in this division (e.g. [[KR6d0036]]–[[KR6d0042]]), giving the Lotus its esoteric / *mìjiào* 密教 reading.
- [[知禮]] — leader of the Sòng *shānjiā* 山家 branch of Tiāntái and the dominant figure of the 11th-century revival; the second-most-cited person in `KR6d` (≈ 11 notes), with the *Shí bùèr mén zhǐyào chāo* [[KR6d0158]] and his liturgical *xíngfǎ* corpus.
- [[遵式]] — Zhīlǐ's contemporary and the principal Sòng ritual compiler; author of Tiāntái *chànyí* and devotional works (e.g. [[KR6d0195]], [[KR6d0199]]).
- [[仁岳]] — Zhīlǐ's disciple who later turned to the *shānwài* 山外 position; author of the *Sìmíng Rényuè yìshuō cóngshū* [[KR6d0224]], the principal *shānwài* counter-statement.
- [[從義]] — late-Sòng commentator on the *Móhē zhǐguān* and *Shí bùèr mén* whose works ([[KR6d0139]]–[[KR6d0141]]) preserve the post-Zhīlǐ scholastic apparatus.
- [[宗曉]] — Sòng compiler of the *Sìmíng zūnzhě jiàoxíng lù* [[KR6d0186]], the corpus of Zhīlǐ's writings; principal documentary editor of *shānjiā* Tiāntái.
- [[智旭]] — Míng-period reviver of Tiāntái and author of the *Jiàoguān gāngzōng* [[KR6d0188]]; the principal late-imperial systematiser whose works re-frame Tiāntái for the Míng-Qīng setting.
- [[靈耀]] — Qīng-period Tiāntái commentator on the *Móhē zhǐguān* and the *sāndàbù* ([[KR6d0142]] and related); his work is the principal Qīng Tiāntái scholastic continuation.

## Topics

- **The ekayāna 一乘 doctrine and *kāiquán xiǎnshí* 開權顯實.** The proclamation in chapters 2–9 of the Lotus that the *śrāvaka* / *pratyekabuddha* / *bodhisattva* three vehicles are provisional (*quán* 權) and the Buddha-vehicle alone is real (*shí* 實) is the central scriptural commitment around which the entire Tiāntái doctrinal apparatus is built, and almost every Sinitic Lotus exegete from Fǎyún onward addresses its hermeneutic status.
- **The *jìmén* / *běnmén* 跡門／本門 division.** The split of the Lotus into a "trace-gate" (chapters 1–14) and an "origin-gate" (15–28) is associated with Dàoxuān's preface to Kumārajīva ([[KR6d0001]]) and given systematic articulation in Zhìyǐ's *Xuányì* [[KR6d0006]]; it remains the standard Sinitic exegetical schema through the entire division.
- **The *wǔshí bājiào* 五時八教 classification.** Zhìyǐ's chronological-and-modal organisation of the Buddha's *kerygma* into five periods (*Huāyán*, *Lùyuàn / Āhán*, *Fāngděng*, *Bōrě*, *Fǎhuá-Nièpán*) and eight modes is developed in the *Xuányì*, summarised in [[KR6d0166]] and [[KR6d0167]], and given canonical compact form in Chégwan's *Tiāntái sìjiào yí* [[KR6d0168]]; it is the basic doctrinal-classification apparatus of the division.
- **Tiāntái meditation: *yīxīn sānguān* 一心三觀, *yīniàn sānqiān* 一念三千, *zhǐguān bùèr* 止觀不二.** Articulated principally in the *Móhē zhǐguān* [[KR6d0130]] and its commentaries; the most systematic Mahāyāna meditation theory in Chinese, organised on the *shíjìng × shíchéng* 十境十乘 matrix.
- **The *shānjiā / shānwài* 山家／山外 controversy.** The 11th-century Sòng-period split between Zhīlǐ's [[知禮]] "mountain-house" *shānjiā* — defending the Tiāntái doctrine of evil in the Buddha-nature (*xìng-è* 性惡) and Guàndǐng's role in the early canon — and the *shānwài* counter-position of Yuánqīng 源清, Zhìyuán 智圓, and (later) Rényuè 仁岳, particularly over the interpretation of Zhànrán's *Shí bùèr mén* [[KR6d0157]] and the status of Guàndǐng's *Guānyīn xuányì* commentary [[KR6d0046]]. The dispute generated the large mid-Sòng polemical literature visible in [[KR6d0184]]–[[KR6d0186]] and [[KR6d0220]]–[[KR6d0235]].
- **Buddha-nature in the insentient (*wúqíng yǒu xìng* 無情有性).** Zhànrán's claim in the *Jīngāngbēi* [[KR6d0175]] that even insentient things possess Buddha-nature; the Tiāntái counter-position to the Chán and Huāyán readings on the same question, generating the *Jīngāngbēi*-commentary sub-tradition ([[KR6d0176]]–[[KR6d0181]]).
- **The Lotus liturgy: *Fǎhuá sānmèi* 法華三昧 and the *chànyí* tradition.** Zhìyǐ's Lotus-Samādhi practice from the *Móhē zhǐguān* and the *Fǎhuá sānmèi chànyí* [[KR6d0191]] is the textual base for a Sòng-period ritual literature, principally elaborated by Zūnshì and Zhīlǐ, that fed back into ordinary monastic and lay practice and shaped the late-imperial Tiāntái devotional landscape.
- **The Guānyīn 觀音 sub-cult.** The detachment of the *Pǔmén pǐn* 普門品 (Lotus chapter 25) as an independent devotional unit, with its own commentarial corpus ([[KR6d0046]]–[[KR6d0057]]) — Zhìyǐ's *Guānyīn xuányì* and *yìshū* and their Sòng / Míng / Qīng commentaries — is one of the principal vehicles by which the Lotus's textual presence extended into popular ritual practice.
- **Japanese Lotus reception: Tendai 天台 and Shingon 真言 readings.** The Heian-period esoteric / *mìjiào* interpretations of the Lotus by Kūkai 空海 ([[KR6d0036]]–[[KR6d0044]]) and the early Japanese Tendai materials (e.g. [[KR6d0220]]) constitute a distinct Lotus reading transmitted within the same division.
- **The Míng–Qīng Tiāntái revival.** Zhìxù's [[智旭]] 17th-century systematisation, the Qīng-period Tiāntái commentaries by Língyào, Tōnglǐ, and others ([[KR6d0084]]–[[KR6d0098]], [[KR6d0142]]), and the late-imperial *Sìjiào yí* sub-commentaries ([[KR6d0169]]–[[KR6d0174]]) constitute the final phase of pre-modern Lotus scholarship represented in the division.
