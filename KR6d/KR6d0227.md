# Tiānzhú bié-jí 天竺別集
*The Tiānzhú Separate-Collection*
by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 述); re-edited by [[慧觀]] (Huìguān, 重編)

## About the work

A three-*juan* literary collection by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032), titled after his principal monastic residence at the Tiānzhú-sì 天竺寺 in Háng-zhōu. Companion to the *Jīn-yuán jí* ([[KR6d0226]]); together the two works constitute Zūnshì's principal collected literary writings, edited by the later monk Huìguān.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed with [[KR6d0226]] in Zūnshì's productive period c. 996–1032, with editorial finalisation extending into the Sòng-Yuán period.
