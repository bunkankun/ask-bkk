# Běifēng jiàoyì 北峯教義
*The Doctrinal Meanings of [Master] Běi-fēng*
by [[宗印]] (Zōngyìn / Běifēng Zōngyìn / Huìxíng fǎshī, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* doctrinal treatise by **Běifēng Zōngyìn** 北峰宗印 (1148–1213). Zōngyìn was the teacher of **Huìyán Fǎzhào** 晦巖法照 ([[法照]]) and of **Gǔyún Yuáncuì** 古雲元粹 ([[元粹]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zōngyìn's productive period c. 1180–1213.
