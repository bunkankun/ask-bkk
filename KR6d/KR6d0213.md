# Pǔxián púsà fāyuàn wén 普賢菩薩發願文
*Vow-Arousing Text of Bodhisattva Samantabhadra*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dàshī, 撰)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* devotional vow-text by Zhìyǐ 智顗, modelled on the *Samantabhadra*-vow tradition (the *Pǔxián xíng-yuàn pǐn* 普賢行願品 of the *Avataṃsaka-sūtra*).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
