# Zhù Fǎhuá běn-jì shí bù-èr mén 註法華本迹十不二門
*Annotated Commentary on the Lotus's Origin-and-Trace Ten Non-Dual Gates*
by [[宗翌]] (Zōng-yì, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng annotated commentary by Zōng-yì 宗翌 on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* ([[KR6d0157]], T1927). The composition is securely dated by Zōng-yì's own preface to Xián-píng 咸平 1 (= 998 CE).

### Abstract

The work belongs together with Yuán-qīng's *Shì-zhū zhǐ* ([[KR6d0160]]) as one of the very earliest Sòng commentaries on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén*, predating Zhīlǐ's foundational *Zhǐ-yào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]]) by three decades. The 998 dating places the work in the immediate aftermath of the Five Dynasties period and at the beginning of the Northern-Sòng Tiāntái revival.
