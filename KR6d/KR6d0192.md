# Fǎhuá sānmèi xíngshì yùnxiǎng bǔzhù yí 法華三昧行事運想補助儀
*Auxiliary Liturgical Apparatus for the Lotus Samādhi Practice and Visualisation*
by [[湛然]] (Zhànrán / Jīngxī Zhànrán, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Táng auxiliary liturgical apparatus by Zhànrán 湛然 (711–782) supplementing Zhìyǐ's *Fǎhuá sānmèi chànyí* ([[KR6d0191]], T1941). The work provides additional practical instruction for the *Fǎhuá sānmèi* practice, including detailed visualisation (*yùnxiǎng* 運想) procedures that Zhìyǐ's foundational text had left implicit.

### Abstract

Zhànrán's *Bǔzhù yí* belongs together with the brief *Lüè Fǎhuá sānmèi bǔzhù yí* ([[KR6d0193]]) and the *Lǐ Fǎhuájīng yíshì* ([[KR6d0194]]) as the principal Táng-period supplementary liturgical apparatus for the standard Tiāntái Lotus Sūtra repentance cycle. The composition is bracketed within Zhànrán's mature productive period c. 750–782.
