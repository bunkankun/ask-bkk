# Fǎhuá sān-mèi xíng-shì yùn-xiǎng bǔ-zhù yí 法華三昧行事運想補助儀
*Auxiliary Liturgical Apparatus for the Lotus Samādhi Practice and Visualisation*
by [[湛然]] (Zhànrán / Jīngxī Zhànrán, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Táng auxiliary liturgical apparatus by Zhànrán 湛然 (711–782) supplementing Zhìyǐ's *Fǎhuá sān-mèi chàn-yí* ([[KR6d0191]], T1941). The work provides additional practical instruction for the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* practice, including detailed visualisation (*yùn-xiǎng* 運想) procedures that Zhìyǐ's foundational text had left implicit.

### Abstract

Zhànrán's *Bǔ-zhù yí* belongs together with the brief *Lüè Fǎhuá sān-mèi bǔ-zhù yí* ([[KR6d0193]]) and the *Lǐ Fǎhuá-jīng yí-shì* ([[KR6d0194]]) as the principal Táng-period supplementary liturgical apparatus for the standard Tiāntái Lotus Sūtra repentance cycle. The composition is bracketed within Zhànrán's mature productive period c. 750–782.
