# Fāng-děng sān-mèi xíng-fǎ 方等三昧行法
*Method-Practice for the Vaipulya Samādhi*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dà-shī, 說)

## About the work

A single-*juan* meditation-liturgical manual by Zhìyǐ 智顗 on the *fāng-děng sān-mèi* 方等三昧 ("Vaipulya *samādhi*"), one of the **four kinds of *samādhi*** (*sì zhǒng sān-mèi* 四種三昧) systematised by Zhìyǐ in the *Móhē zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0130]]).

### Abstract

The *Fāng-děng sān-mèi xíng-fǎ* belongs together with the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi chàn-yí* ([[KR6d0191]]) and other Tiāntái meditation-liturgical manuals as the foundational liturgical apparatus of the Tiāntái meditative tradition. The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
