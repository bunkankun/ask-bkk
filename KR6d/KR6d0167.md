# Tiāntái bā-jiào dà-yì 天台八教大意
*The Great Purport of the Tiāntái Eight Doctrines*
by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng / Zhāng-ān Guàndǐng, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* synoptic exposition of the Tiāntái *bā-jiào* 八教 (eight doctrinal classifications) by Guàndǐng 灌頂 (561–632), Zhìyǐ's principal disciple and the redactor of his major treatises. The *bā-jiào* combines the Tiāntái *huà-yí sì-jiào* 化儀四教 (four modes of teaching: sudden / gradual / secret / indeterminate) and *huà-fǎ sì-jiào* 化法四教 (four doctrinal contents: *zàng* / *tōng* / *bié* / *yuán*) into the comprehensive eight-fold Tiāntái doctrinal-classification system.

### Abstract

Guàndǐng's *Bā-jiào dà-yì* is one of the principal early Tiāntái pedagogical productions, providing accessible synoptic introduction to the foundational *bā-jiào* doctrinal-classification framework. The work is parallel in genre to Dìguān's later *Sì-jiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931) but earlier and more extensive in doctrinal coverage.

The composition is bracketed within Guàndǐng's productive period after Zhìyǐ's death (597) and before his own death (632).
