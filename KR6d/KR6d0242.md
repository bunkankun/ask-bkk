# Tāi-zōng jīng-yīng jí 台宗精英集
*The Tiāntái School Essential-Heroes Collection*
compiled by [[普容]] (Pǔróng / Xiāndū Pǔróng, 集)

## About the work

A four-*juan* Sòng-period anthology by **Xiān-dū Pǔ-róng** 仙都普容 of essential Tiāntái doctrinal materials. Per DILA the work originally extended to five *juan*; the four-*juan* canonical recension may represent a partial transmission.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Pǔ-róng's productive period in the Sòng (c. 1100–1250).
