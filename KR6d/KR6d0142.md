# Móhē zhǐguān guàn-yì kē 摩訶止觀貫義科
*Section-Threading-Meaning Analysis of Mahā-Cessation-and-Contemplation*
by [[受登]] (Shòu-dēng / Tiān-xī Jǐng-chún, 說); editorial supplementation by [[靈耀]] (Líng-yào, 補定)

## About the work

A two-*juan* (上 / 下) early-Qīng sectional analysis (*kē* 科) of the *Móhē zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0130]], T1911) by Tiān-xī Shòu-dēng 天溪受登 (b. 1607), with editorial supplementation (*bǔ-dìng* 補定 — "supplementing-and-fixing") by Líng-yào 靈耀 (the early-Qīng Tiāntái master also responsible for [[KR6d0012]] and [[KR6d0057]]).

The genre title — *guàn-yì kē* 貫義科 ("threading-meaning sectional-analysis") — distinguishes the work from the standard *kē-wén* 科文 (purely structural analysis): rather than providing only the hierarchical structural-outline diagrams, the *guàn-yì kē* threads together the meaning-content of each sectional unit, providing a more discursive analytical apparatus.

### Prefaces

The text in the X56n0924 recension carries the standard front matter; the body proceeds with the *guàn-yì* sectional analysis.

### Abstract

The *Guàn-yì kē* is the principal early-Qīng sectional-analysis apparatus on the *Móhē zhǐguān*, complementing the existing Sòng-period *kē* productions in the canonical apparatus. Its production by Shòu-dēng and Líng-yào in the early-Qīng productive period demonstrates the institutional vitality of the Tiāntái scholastic tradition in the early-Qīng period, alongside the parallel productions of the early-Qīng Tiāntái revival in southern China.

The composition is bracketed within Shòu-dēng's productive period after his birth in 1607 and before the standard cutoff of the early-Qīng productive period c. 1700.
