# Shī-shí tōng-lǎn 施食通覽
*Comprehensive Overview of Food-Offering [Rituals]*
edited by [[宗曉]] (Zōngxiǎo / Shízhī Zōngxiǎo, 編)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng compilation by **Shí-zhī Zōng-xiǎo** 石芝宗曉 (1151–1214) on the *shī-shí* 施食 (food-offering) ritual tradition — including the *yú-jiā yàn-kǒu* 瑜伽燄口 (Yogācāra Fire-Mouth) ritual and other Mahāyāna preta-feeding ceremonies. Belongs to the substantial Sòng-period Buddhist liturgical-handbook tradition.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zōng-xiǎo's productive period c. 1180–1214.
