# Qiānshǒu qiānyǎn dàbēixīn zhòu xíngfǎ 千手千眼大悲心咒行法
*Practice-Method of the Thousand-Hand Thousand-Eye Great-Compassion-Heart Dhāraṇī* (early-Qīng recension)
originally compiled by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ / Sìmíng Zhīlǐ, 始集); re-compiled by [[讀體]] (Dútǐ / Jiànyuè / Bǎohuáshān master, 重纂)

## About the work

A single-*juan* early-Qīng re-compilation by **Jiàn-yuè Dú-tǐ** 見月讀體 (1601–1679) of Sì-míng Zhīlǐ's earlier Northern-Sòng *Qiān-shǒu yǎn dà-bēi-xīn zhòu xíng-fǎ* ([[KR6d0200]], T1950). The work preserves Zhīlǐ's foundational Avalokiteśvara *dhāraṇī* liturgy in early-Qīng recension, providing the standard institutional apparatus for the Bǎo-huá-shān *Vinaya*-school's continued use of the practice.

### Abstract

The two-stage editorial production — Zhīlǐ's Sòng original compilation and Dú-tǐ's early-Qīng re-compilation — demonstrates the continuous institutional vitality of the Avalokiteśvara *dhāraṇī* devotional tradition through more than six centuries of East-Asian Buddhist history.

The dating bracket (996–1679) covers the entire textual development from Zhīlǐ's original through Dútǐ's re-compilation.
