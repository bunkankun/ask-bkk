# Jīngāngbēi lùn shìwén 金剛錍論釋文
*Text-Interpretation of the Vajra-Lance Treatise*
by [[時舉]] (Shíjǔ, 釋); collated by [[海眼]] (Hǎiyǎn / Bìng tóutuó, 會)

## About the work

A three-*juan* Sòng-period running commentary on Zhànrán's *Jīn-gāng-bēi* ([[KR6d0175]], T1932) by the Tiāntái master **Shíjǔ** 時舉, with editorial collation (會 *huì*) by the late-Míng monk **Hǎiyǎn** 海眼 (the *Bìng tóu-tuó* "Sick *Dhutaṅga*"). The work belongs together with the other Sòng *Jīn-gāng-bēi* commentaries ([[KR6d0177]], [[KR6d0178]], [[KR6d0179]], [[KR6d0180]]) as part of the substantial Sòng commentarial corpus on Zhànrán's foundational doctrine.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the standard front matter and Shíjǔ's editorial framing referencing Zhīlǐ's *Sìmíng shíyì shū* ([[KR6d0185]]).

### Abstract

The composition of the original Shíjǔ commentary is bracketed within the Sòng productive period c. 1100–1300; the editorial finalisation by Hǎiyǎn extends the bracket into the late-Míng era (c. 1600). The work was preserved through the SòngMíng commentarial transmission and reprinted in the Manji-zoku canonical apparatus.
