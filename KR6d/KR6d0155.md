# Fǎ-jiè cì-dì chū-mén 法界次第初門
*Beginning-Gates of the Sequential Dharma-Realm*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dà-shī, 撰)

## About the work

A three-*juan* (上 / 中 / 下) Tiāntái doctrinal handbook by Zhìyǐ 智顗, providing systematic introductory exposition of the standard Buddhist doctrinal categories — *skandhas*, *dhātus*, *āyatanas*, *ārya-satya*, *pratītyasamutpāda*, etc. — organised in the Tiāntái sequential framework. The work is one of Zhìyǐ's principal pedagogical productions for elementary doctrinal study.

### Abstract

The *Fǎ-jiè cì-dì chū-mén* is a Tiāntái-style doctrinal lexicon-cum-handbook providing systematic introductory exposition of foundational Buddhist categories. The work belongs together with Zhìyǐ's other elementary pedagogical productions as the standard Tiāntái introductory apparatus for doctrinal study, complementing his more systematic mature works (the *Xuán-yì*, the *Wén-jù*, the *Móhē zhǐguān*).

The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
