# Xiū-chàn yào-zhǐ 修懺要旨
*Essential Purport of Cultivating Repentance*
by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ / Sì-míng Zhīlǐ / Fǎ-zhì dàshī, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng treatise by **Sì-míng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 (960–1028) on the essential principles of repentance practice. Preserved in the Northern Yongle Edition of the Canon as L167n1573, the work belongs to Zhīlǐ's broader liturgical-doctrinal corpus alongside his repentance liturgies ([[KR6d0196]], [[KR6d0200]]).

### Abstract

Zhīlǐ's *Yào-zhǐ* provides the doctrinal-theoretical framework underlying his various repentance liturgies, articulating the *shān-jiā* tradition's distinctive understanding of the relationship between repentance practice (*xiū-chàn* 修懺) and the broader Tiāntái meditative-doctrinal apparatus.

The composition is bracketed within Zhīlǐ's mature productive period c. 996–1028.
