# Jīn-gāng-bēi lùn yì-jiě 金剛錍論義解
*Doctrinal Interpretation of the Vajra-Lance Treatise*
by [[善月]] (Shànyuè / Bóting Shànyuè, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng commentary on Zhànrán's *Jīn-gāng-bēi* ([[KR6d0175]], T1932) by **Bóting Shànyuè** 柏庭善月 of Sì-míng. Body attribution: *Sì-míng shāmén Shàn-yuè shù* 四明沙門　善月　述. Companion to Shànyuè's other Lotus-tradition productions ([[KR6d0021]], [[KR6d0064]]).

### Abstract

Shànyuè's *Yì-jiě* belongs to the substantial Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* commentarial tradition on Zhànrán's *Jīn-gāng-bēi*, providing the orthodox *shān-jiā* interpretation of the doctrine of the buddha-nature of insentient beings against the *shān-wài* alternative interpretation preserved in Zhì-yuán's *Xiǎn-xìng lù* ([[KR6d0179]]).

The composition is bracketed within Shànyuè's productive period at the Yán-qìng-sì 延慶寺 in Sì-míng, c. 1180–1240.
