# Lüè Fǎhuá sān-mèi bǔ-zhù yí 略法華三昧補助儀
*Brief Auxiliary Apparatus for the Lotus Samādhi*
Anonymous (Táng to Northern Sòng).

## About the work

A short single-*juan* anonymous brief auxiliary liturgical apparatus for the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* tradition, complementing Zhànrán's *Bǔ-zhù yí* ([[KR6d0192]]) and Zhìyǐ's foundational *Chàn-yí* ([[KR6d0191]]). The work is one of the standard Tang-Sòng *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* liturgical apparatus.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed loosely within the post-Zhànrán productive period for *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* liturgical material (c. 750–1100). The work was preserved through the Tiāntái liturgical tradition and incorporated into the Taishō canonical apparatus alongside the other principal *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* texts.
