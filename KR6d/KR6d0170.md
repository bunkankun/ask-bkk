# Sì-jiào yí bèi-shì 四教儀備釋
*Comprehensive Interpretation of the Four-Doctrines Manual*
by [[元粹]] (Yuáncuì / Gǔyún Yuáncuì, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* (上 / 下) Yuán-period commentary by **Gǔ-yún Yuán-cuì** 古雲元粹 of Tiáo-xī (Húzhōu) on Dìguān's *Tiāntái sì-jiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931). Body attribution: *Tiáo-xī shāmén Yuán-cuì shù* 苕谿沙門　元粹　述. The composition is bracketed within Yuán-cuì's productive period c. 1214–1290 (extending the bracket beyond his documented productive years to allow for late activity).

### Abstract

Yuán-cuì's *Bèi-shì* belongs to the substantial Sòng-Yuán commentarial tradition on Dìguān's *Sì-jiào yí*. As a dharma-disciple of Běi-fēng Zōng-yìn 北峰宗印 (1148–1213), Yuán-cuì represents the late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái scholastic tradition's continued engagement with the Tiāntái doctrinal-classification framework.
