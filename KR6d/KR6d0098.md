# Fǎhuá jīng zhǐzhǎng shū shìyì 法華經指掌疏事義
*Matters-and-Meanings of the Pointing-the-Palm Subcommentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[通理]] Tōnglǐ (述)

## About the work

The matters-and-meanings glossary (*shìyì* 事義) volume of [[通理]] Tōnglǐ's four-text *Fǎhuá jīng zhǐzhǎng shū* commentarial complex (see [[KR6d0095]]). Provides the topical-encyclopedic apparatus accompanying the *Zhǐzhǎng shū* main commentary ([[KR6d0097]]).

### Prefaces

The text carries the standard front matter for the *shìyì* glossary apparatus.

### Abstract

The *Shìyì* completes the four-text *Zhǐzhǎng shū* complex by providing the topical-encyclopedic glossary of names, terms, and concepts referenced in the main commentary. Together, the four-text complex ([[KR6d0095]] *kēwén* / [[KR6d0096]] *xuánshì* / [[KR6d0097]] *Zhǐzhǎng shū* / [[KR6d0098]] *Shìyì*) provides the most fully-apparatus-equipped mid-Qīng Lotus Sūtra commentarial production, complete with sectional analysis, introductory exposition, main commentary, and topical glossary.

The dating is bracketed with the rest of the four-text complex within Tōnglǐ's mature productive period c. 1750–1782.
