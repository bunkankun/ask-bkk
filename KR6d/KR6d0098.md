# Fǎhuá jīng zhǐ-zhǎng shū shì-yì 法華經指掌疏事義
*Matters-and-Meanings of the Pointing-the-Palm Subcommentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[通理]] (Tōng-lǐ, 述)

## About the work

The matters-and-meanings glossary (*shì-yì* 事義) volume of Tōng-lǐ's four-text *Fǎhuá jīng zhǐ-zhǎng shū* commentarial complex (see [[KR6d0095]]). Provides the topical-encyclopedic apparatus accompanying the *Zhǐ-zhǎng shū* main commentary ([[KR6d0097]]).

### Prefaces

The text carries the standard front matter for the *shì-yì* glossary apparatus.

### Abstract

The *Shì-yì* completes the four-text *Zhǐ-zhǎng shū* complex by providing the topical-encyclopedic glossary of names, terms, and concepts referenced in the main commentary. Together, the four-text complex ([[KR6d0095]] *kē-wén* / [[KR6d0096]] *xuán-shì* / [[KR6d0097]] *Zhǐ-zhǎng shū* / [[KR6d0098]] *Shì-yì*) provides the most fully-apparatus-equipped mid-Qīng Lotus Sūtra commentarial production, complete with sectional analysis, introductory exposition, main commentary, and topical glossary.

The dating is bracketed with the rest of the four-text complex within Tōng-lǐ's mature productive period c. 1750–1782.
