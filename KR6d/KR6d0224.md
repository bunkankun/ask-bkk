# Sì-míng Rényuè yì-shuō cóng-shū 四明仁岳異說叢書
*Collected Series of the Differing Theories of Sì-míng [Zhīlǐ] and Rényuè*
compiled by [[繼忠]] (Jì-zhōng / Fú-zōng Jì-zhōng, 集)

## About the work

A seven-*juan* Northern-Sòng compilation by **Fú-zōng Jì-zhōng** 扶宗繼忠 (1012–1082) documenting the doctrinal differences between **Sì-míng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 (the *shān-jiā* tradition founder, [[知禮]]) and **Rényuè** 仁岳 (the principal *shān-wài* spokesperson, [[仁岳]]). The work is the most extensive single documentary record of the *shān-jiā* / *shān-wài* polemical exchange.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Jì-zhōng's productive period c. 1040–1082.
