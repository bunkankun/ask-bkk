# Sìmíng Rényuè yìshuō cóngshū 四明仁岳異說叢書
*Collected Series of the Differing Theories of Sì-míng [Zhīlǐ] and Rényuè*
compiled by [[繼忠]] (Jìzhōng / Fúzōng Jìzhōng, 集)

## About the work

A seven-*juan* Northern-Sòng compilation by **Fúzōng Jìzhōng** 扶宗繼忠 (1012–1082) documenting the doctrinal differences between **Sìmíng Zhīlǐ** 四明知禮 (the *shānjiā* tradition founder, [[知禮]]) and **Rényuè** 仁岳 (the principal *shānwài* spokesperson, [[仁岳]]). The work is the most extensive single documentary record of the *shānjiā* / *shānwài* polemical exchange.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Jìzhōng's productive period c. 1040–1082.
