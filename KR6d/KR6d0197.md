# Shìjiā rúlái nièpán lǐ-zàn wén 釋迦如來涅槃禮讚文
*Worship-Encomium Text for the Parinirvāṇa of Tathāgata Śākyamuni*
by [[仁岳]] (Rényuè / Jìngjué Rényuè, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng commemorative liturgical text (*lǐ-zàn wén* 禮讚文) by **Rényuè** 仁岳 — the *shān-wài* tradition spokesperson — for the commemoration of Śākyamuni Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* (passage into final nirvāṇa).

### Abstract

The work belongs to the substantial Sòng-period Buddhist commemorative-liturgical genre, providing standardised liturgical material for the periodic monastic commemoration of the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa*. The composition is bracketed within Rényuè's productive period c. 1010–1060.
