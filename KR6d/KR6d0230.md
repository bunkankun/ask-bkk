# Sān-qiān yǒu-mén sòng lüè-jiě 三千有門頌略解
*Brief Interpretation of the Three-Thousand-Existence-Gates Verses*
by [[真覺]] (Zhēnjué / Bǎisōng Zhēnjué, 略解); collated by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒu-yì Zhìxù, 較)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Míng commentary by **Bǎi-sōng Zhēn-jué** 百松真覺 on the *sān-qiān yǒu-mén sòng* 三千有門頌 (*Three-Thousand Existence-Gates Verses*) — verses summarising the Tiāntái *yī-niàn sān-qiān* 一念三千 (three-thousand realms in a single thought) cosmology. The work was collated (較 *jiào*) by **Ǒu-yì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), Bǎi-sōng's contemporary and one of the *Míng-mò sì-dà gāo-sēng* 明末四大高僧.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within the late-Wàn-lì through Chóngzhēn productive period c. 1573–1655.
