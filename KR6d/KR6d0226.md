# Jīn-yuán jí 金園集
*The Golden-Garden Collection*
by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 述); re-edited by [[慧觀]] (Huìguān, 重編)

## About the work

A three-*juan* literary collection by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032) — the Tiāntái-Pure Land master of Háng-zhōu Tiānzhú-sì — re-edited (重編 *chóng-biān*) by the later monk **Huìguān** 慧觀. Companion to Zūnshì's *Tiānzhú bié-jí* ([[KR6d0227]]); together the two works constitute Zūnshì's principal collected literary writings.

### Abstract

The composition of the original Zūnshì material is bracketed within his productive period c. 996–1032; the editorial finalisation by Huìguān extends the bracket to the late Sòng / Yuán period (c. 1300).
