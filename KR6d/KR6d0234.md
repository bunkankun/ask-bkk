# Yuándùn zōngyǎn 圓頓宗眼
*The Lineage-Eye of the Perfect-Sudden [Doctrine]*
by [[法登]] (Fǎdēng / Yìtáng Fǎdēng, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* essay by **Yìtáng Fǎdēng** 逸堂法登 on the Tiāntái *yuándùn* 圓頓 (perfect-sudden) doctrine, articulating the school's distinctive doctrinal-meditative commitment.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Fǎdēng's productive period c. 1150–1250.
