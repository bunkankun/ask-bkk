# Yuán-dùn zōng-yǎn 圓頓宗眼
*The Lineage-Eye of the Perfect-Sudden [Doctrine]*
by [[法登]] (Fǎdēng / Yìtáng Fǎdēng, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* essay by **Yì-táng Fǎ-dēng** 逸堂法登 on the Tiāntái *yuán-dùn* 圓頓 (perfect-sudden) doctrine, articulating the school's distinctive doctrinal-meditative commitment.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Fǎ-dēng's productive period c. 1150–1250.
