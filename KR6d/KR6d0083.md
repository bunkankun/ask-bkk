# Fǎhuá jīng zhuó-jiě 法華經卓解
*Distinguished Interpretation of the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[徐昌治]] (Xú Chāng-zhì / Jìn-zhōu / Wúyī dàorén, 著)

## About the work

A seven-*juan* late-Míng / early-Qīng Lotus Sūtra commentary by the lay Buddhist scholar **Xú Chāng-zhì** 徐昌治 (sobriquet **Wúyī dàorén** 無依道人, "Master Without-Refuge"). The work is preserved as X32n0617 in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* and represents the early-Qīng continuation of the late-Wàn-lì literati-Buddhist commentarial tradition.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0617 recension carries Xú Chāng-zhì's own framing of the *zhuó-jiě* 卓解 ("distinguished interpretation") genre, which indicates a distinctive personal-individual reading rather than a school-derivative exposition.

### Abstract

Xú Chāng-zhì's *Zhuó-jiě* belongs to the early-Qīng continuation of the late-Wàn-lì literati-Buddhist commentarial tradition, parallel to Jiāo Hóng's *Píng-lín* ([[KR6d0078]]) and the broader late-Míng tradition of distinctive personal commentaries on the Lotus Sūtra. The work's seven-*juan* extent and continuous-running structure place it among the more substantial late-imperial Lotus commentaries.

The dating is bracketed within the early-Qīng productive period c. 1644–1700.
