# Qǐng Guān-shì-yīn púsà xiāo-fú dú-hài tuó-luó-ní sān-mèi yí 請觀世音菩薩消伏毒害陀羅尼三昧儀
*Liturgy of the Samādhi-Dhāraṇī of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara for Eliminating Poisonous Harm*
compiled by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 集)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng *dhāraṇī*-liturgical apparatus by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032) for the *Qǐng Guān-shì-yīn púsà xiāo-fú dú-hài tuó-luó-ní jīng* 請觀世音菩薩消伏毒害陀羅尼經 (T1043, the *Sūtra Inviting Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara to Eliminate Poisonous Harm*). The work provides standardised liturgical procedure for the protective-*dhāraṇī* practice associated with Avalokiteśvara devotional cult.

### Abstract

Zūnshì's *Sān-mèi yí* belongs to his extensive Tiāntái-Pure Land devotional liturgical apparatus. The text editorial note records *Jīn-guāng-míng zuì-shèng chàn-yí tóng* 金光明最勝懺儀同 ("same as the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa zuì-shèng chàn-yí*") indicating structural parallel with [[KR6d0196]]. The composition is bracketed within Zūnshì's productive period at Tiānzhú-sì in Háng-zhōu, c. 996–1032.
