# Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí xuán-tán juàn-shǒu 法華經科拾懸談卷首
*Front-Matter Hanging-Discussion of the Sectional-Gleanings on the Lotus Sūtra*
sectional structure by [[佛閑]] (Fó-xián / Fó-xián Xù-yī, 立科); supplementary editing by [[智一]] (Zhì-yī / Xuě-dùn, 拾遺)

## About the work

The opening front-matter (*juàn-shǒu* 卷首) volume of the three-text *Fǎhuá jīng kē-shí* commentarial complex by Fó-xián 佛閑 (also referenced as **Fó-xián Xù-yī** 佛閑勗伊大師) of the **Pǔ-dé-sì** 普德寺 in Nán-jīng, with editorial supplementation by his disciple **Zhì-yī** 智一. The three-text complex — this work ([[KR6d0092]], front matter), the *Kē-shí kē* ([[KR6d0093]], sectional analysis), and the *Kē-shí* proper ([[KR6d0094]], main commentary, 7 *juan*) — represents the principal early-Kāngxī Nán-jīng Buddhist contribution to the Lotus Sūtra commentarial corpus.

### Prefaces

The text opens with Zhì-yī's preface dated Kāngxī *yǐ-yǒu* 康熙乙酉 (= 1705 CE). The *xuán-tán* 懸談 ("hanging-discussion") in the title is a technical Buddhist scholastic term for the introductory doctrinal-historical exposition that precedes the running commentary on a text — analogous to the Tiāntái *xuán-yì* 玄義 framework but more discursive and less systematically organized.

### Abstract

Fó-xián's *Kē-shí xuán-tán juàn-shǒu* establishes the doctrinal-historical framework for the entire *Kē-shí* complex: it provides the introductory exposition of the Lotus Sūtra's place in the Mahāyāna canonical tradition, the principal interpretive issues, and the editorial method to be employed in the subsequent main commentary. The work is structurally parallel to other three-text commentarial complexes of the early-Qīng period (Zhì-xiáng's *Shòu-shǒu* complex, [[KR6d0087]]–[[KR6d0089]]; Dà-yì's *Dà-chéng* complex, [[KR6d0084]]–[[KR6d0086]]).

The dating is securely fixed by Zhì-yī's 1705 preface, with composition presumably extending back through Fó-xián's late-Kāngxī productive period (c. 1700–1705).
