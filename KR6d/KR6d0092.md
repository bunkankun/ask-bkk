# Fǎhuá jīng kēshí xuántán juànshǒu 法華經科拾懸談卷首
*Front-Matter Hanging-Discussion of the Sectional-Gleanings on the Lotus Sūtra*
sectional structure by [[佛閑]] (Fóxián / Fóxián Xùyī, 立科); supplementary editing by [[智一]] (Zhìyī / Xuědùn, 拾遺)

## About the work

The opening front-matter (*juànshǒu* 卷首) volume of the three-text *Fǎhuá jīng kēshí* commentarial complex by Fóxián 佛閑 (also referenced as **Fóxián Xùyī** 佛閑勗伊大師) of the **Pǔdésì** 普德寺 in Nánjīng, with editorial supplementation by his disciple **Zhìyī** 智一. The three-text complex — this work ([[KR6d0092]], front matter), the *Kēshí kē* ([[KR6d0093]], sectional analysis), and the *Kēshí* proper ([[KR6d0094]], main commentary, 7 *juan*) — represents the principal early-Kāngxī Nánjīng Buddhist contribution to the Lotus Sūtra commentarial corpus.

### Prefaces

The text opens with Zhìyī's preface dated Kāngxī *yǐyǒu* 康熙乙酉 (= 1705 CE). The *xuántán* 懸談 ("hanging-discussion") in the title is a technical Buddhist scholastic term for the introductory doctrinal-historical exposition that precedes the running commentary on a text — analogous to the Tiāntái *xuányì* 玄義 framework but more discursive and less systematically organized.

### Abstract

Fóxián's *Kēshí xuántán juànshǒu* establishes the doctrinal-historical framework for the entire *Kēshí* complex: it provides the introductory exposition of the Lotus Sūtra's place in the Mahāyāna canonical tradition, the principal interpretive issues, and the editorial method to be employed in the subsequent main commentary. The work is structurally parallel to other three-text commentarial complexes of the early-Qīng period (Zhìxiáng's *Shòushǒu* complex, [[KR6d0087]]–[[KR6d0089]]; Dàyì's *Dàchéng* complex, [[KR6d0084]]–[[KR6d0086]]).

The dating is securely fixed by Zhìyī's 1705 preface, with composition presumably extending back through Fóxián's late-Kāngxī productive period (c. 1700–1705).
