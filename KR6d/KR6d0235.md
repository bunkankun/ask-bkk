# Yì zhōng-xīng jiào-guān 議中興教觀
*Discussion of the Mid-Revival of Doctrine-and-Contemplation*
by [[法登]] (Fǎdēng / Yìtáng Fǎdēng, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng essay by **Yì-táng Fǎ-dēng** 逸堂法登 on the *zhōng-xīng* 中興 ("mid-revival") of Tiāntái doctrine-and-contemplation. The work belongs together with [[KR6d0234]] as Fǎ-dēng's principal contributions to the Southern-Sòng *shān-jiā* tradition's institutional self-understanding.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Fǎ-dēng's productive period c. 1150–1250.
