# Fǎhuá sān-mèi chàn-yí 法華三昧懺儀
*Repentance Liturgy for the Lotus Sūtra Samādhi*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dà-shī, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* repentance liturgy (*chàn-yí* 懺儀) by Zhìyǐ 智顗 for the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* 法華三昧 ("Lotus Sūtra *samādhi*") practice. The work is the foundational liturgical text of the East-Asian Lotus Sūtra meditative-confessional tradition and the principal scriptural authority for the standard Tiāntái Lotus Sūtra repentance ritual cycle.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi chàn-yí kān-dìng yuán-běn xù* 法華三昧懺儀勘定元本序 ("Preface to the Original Text Examined-and-Fixed of the Lotus Sūtra Samādhi Repentance Liturgy"), providing editorial framing for the standard Tiāntái recension.

### Abstract

The *Fǎhuá sān-mèi chàn-yí* is one of the most institutionally significant Tiāntái productions: it provides the standard liturgical apparatus for the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* practice that was systematised by Zhìyǐ in the *Móhē zhǐguān* and that became the central meditative-devotional practice of the Tiāntái school. Through the work's institutional centrality, the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* practice became one of the principal forms of medieval East-Asian Buddhist devotional cultivation.

The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
