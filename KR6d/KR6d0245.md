# Tiāntái chuán Fó xīn-yìn jì zhù 天台傳佛心印記註
*Annotation on the Record of the Tiāntái Transmission of the Buddha's Heart-Seal*
by [[傳燈]] (Chuándēng / Yōu-xī Chuándēng, 註)

## About the work

A two-*juan* late-Míng commentary by **Yōu-xī Chuándēng** 幽溪傳燈 (1554–1628), the central figure of the late-Míng Tiāntái revival, on Huái-zé's *Tiāntái chuán Fó xīn-yìn jì* ([[KR6d0187]], T1938).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Chuándēng's productive period c. 1580–1628.
