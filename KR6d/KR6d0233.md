# Zhúān cǎolù 竹菴艸錄
*The Bamboo-Hermitage Grass-Records*
by [[可觀]] (Kěguān / Zhú'ān Kěguān, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* miscellaneous-writings collection by **Zhúān Kěguān** 竹菴可觀. The title — *cǎolù* 艸錄 ("grass-records," i.e., draft-writings) — indicates an informal collection of notes, essays, and miscellaneous writings, complementary to Kěguān's more systematic *Shānjiā yìyuàn* ([[KR6d0232]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Kěguān's productive period c. 1140–1220.
