# Zhú-ān cǎo-lù 竹菴艸錄
*The Bamboo-Hermitage Grass-Records*
by [[可觀]] (Kěguān / Zhú'ān Kěguān, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* miscellaneous-writings collection by **Zhú-ān Kě-guān** 竹菴可觀. The title — *cǎo-lù* 艸錄 ("grass-records," i.e., draft-writings) — indicates an informal collection of notes, essays, and miscellaneous writings, complementary to Kě-guān's more systematic *Shān-jiā yì-yuàn* ([[KR6d0232]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Kě-guān's productive period c. 1140–1220.
