# Jiào-guān gāng-zōng shì-yì 教觀綱宗釋義
*Interpretive Notes on the Outline-Guideline of Doctrine and Contemplation*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒu-yì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Míng companion-commentary by **Ǒu-yì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 on his own *Jiào-guān gāng-zōng* ([[KR6d0188]], T1939), providing additional interpretive notes on contested or obscure points in the main treatise. Together with the *Gāng-zōng* itself, it constitutes Zhìxù's two-text apparatus on the Tiāntái doctrinal-meditative system.

### Abstract

Zhìxù's *Shì-yì* belongs together with the *Gāng-zōng* as a coordinated two-text pedagogical apparatus, with the *Gāng-zōng* providing the main systematic exposition and the *Shì-yì* providing supplementary interpretive notes on selected passages. The composition is bracketed with the *Gāng-zōng* in Zhìxù's mature productive period c. 1641–1655.
