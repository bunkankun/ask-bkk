# Jiàoguān gāngzōng shìyì 教觀綱宗釋義
*Interpretive Notes on the Outline-Guideline of Doctrine and Contemplation*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Míng companion-commentary by **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 on his own *Jiàoguān gāngzōng* ([[KR6d0188]], T1939), providing additional interpretive notes on contested or obscure points in the main treatise. Together with the *Gāngzōng* itself, it constitutes Zhìxù's two-text apparatus on the Tiāntái doctrinal-meditative system.

### Abstract

Zhìxù's *Shìyì* belongs together with the *Gāngzōng* as a coordinated two-text pedagogical apparatus, with the *Gāngzōng* providing the main systematic exposition and the *Shìyì* providing supplementary interpretive notes on selected passages. The composition is bracketed with the *Gāngzōng* in Zhìxù's mature productive period c. 1641–1655.
