# Jiào-guān gāng-zōng 教觀綱宗
*The Outline-Guideline of Doctrine and Contemplation*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒu-yì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Míng synthetic introduction to the Tiāntái doctrinal-and-meditative system by **Ǒu-yì Zhìxù** 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), originally titled *Yī-dài shí-jiào quán-shí gāng-yào tú* 一代時教權實綱要圖 ("Outline-Diagram of the Provisional-and-Real of the Whole-Era Time-Teaching"). The Taishō editorial note explains the title change: *Cháng-fú nán-kàn jīn tiān sì-jiào gè shí-chéng guān gǎi-zuò shū-cè tí-míng* 長幅難看。今添四教各十乘觀。改作書冊題名 ("The original long-scroll [diagram] format was difficult to read; now [I have] added the four-doctrines' respective ten-vehicle contemplations and changed [it] to book-format with [a new] title"). Body attribution: *Běi-Tiānmù Ǒu-yì shāmén Zhìxù chóng-shù* 北天目蕅益沙門智旭重述.

### Prefaces

The text in the Taishō recension carries Zhìxù's own framing: "*The Buddha-patriarchs' essentials [are] doctrine-and-contemplation only. Contemplation without doctrine is not correct; doctrine without contemplation is not transmitted. With doctrine without contemplation [is] desolate; with contemplation without doctrine [is] dangerous. Yet generally discussing the time-teaching, the great outline has eight; relying on the doctrine [to] establish contemplation, the number is also briefly [the] same. The eight doctrines are: 1, sudden; 2, gradual; 3, secret; 4, indeterminate — named the four-doctrines of teaching-modes…*"

### Abstract

The *Jiào-guān gāng-zōng* is one of the most ambitious late-Míng pedagogical productions on the Tiāntái doctrinal-meditative tradition: it provides systematic compact synthesis of the entire Tiāntái system suitable for late-Míng monastic and lay pedagogical use. The work draws on the standard Tiāntái scholastic apparatus (Dìguān's *Sì-jiào yí*, Zhìyǐ's *Móhē zhǐguān*) and integrates these into Zhìxù's distinctive late-Míng Tiāntái-Pure Land synthesis.

The composition is bracketed within Zhìxù's mature productive period c. 1641–1655.

## Other points of interest

The *Jiào-guān gāng-zōng* became one of the most widely studied late-Míng / Qīng / Republican-period Tiāntái pedagogical texts and is one of the principal vehicles by which Zhìxù's late-Míng synthetic apparatus was transmitted to subsequent generations of Chinese Buddhist students.
