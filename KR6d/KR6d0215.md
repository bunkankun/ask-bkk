# Kē shǐ-zhōng xīn-yào 科始終心要
*Sectional Outline of the Beginning-and-End Heart-Essential*
by [[湛然]] (Zhànrán / Jīngxī Zhànrán, 述)

## About the work

A short single-*juan* late-Táng compact synoptic essay by Zhànrán 湛然 (711–782) on the *shǐ-zhōng xīn-yào* 始終心要 ("beginning-and-end heart-essential") — a concentrated formulation of Tiāntái doctrinal-meditative practice covering the entire path from initial arousal-of-bodhi through final Buddhahood.

### Abstract

The work belongs to Zhànrán's broader corpus of compact pedagogical productions ([[KR6d0157]] *Shí bù-èr mén*, [[KR6d0060]] *Fǎhuá jīng dà-yì*) and represents one of the most concentrated late-Táng Tiāntái doctrinal summaries. The composition is bracketed within Zhànrán's mature productive period c. 750–782.
