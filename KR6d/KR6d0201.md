# Chì-shèng-guāng dào-chǎng niàn-sòng yí 熾盛光道場念誦儀
*Recitation Liturgy for the Blazing-Light *Dào-chǎng**
by [[遵式]] (Zūnshì / Cíyún chànzhǔ, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng recitation liturgy by **Zūnshì** 遵式 (964–1032) for the **Chì-shèng-guāng** 熾盛光 ("Blazing-Light") *dào-chǎng* 道場 (ritual area) tradition — a Mahāyāna-Tantric apotropaic practice associated with the *Tejaprabha-Buddha* (the Buddha of Blazing Light) for protection against astral and karmic calamities.

### Abstract

Zūnshì's *Niàn-sòng yí* belongs to his comprehensive late-Northern-Sòng liturgical apparatus and demonstrates the integration of Mahāyāna-Tantric protective practices into the standard Tiāntái devotional tradition. The composition is bracketed within Zūnshì's productive period at Tiānzhú-sì in Háng-zhōu, c. 996–1032.
