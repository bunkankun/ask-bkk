# Suí-zì-yì sān-mèi 隨自意三昧
*The Self-Following Samādhi*
by [[慧思]] (Huì-sī / Nán-yuè Huì-sī, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Chén-period meditation manual by **Huì-sī** 慧思 (515–577), the second Tiāntái patriarch, on the *suí-zì-yì sān-mèi* 隨自意三昧 ("self-following *samādhi*") — a meditative practice that Huì-sī articulated alongside the *Fǎhuá sān-mèi* and the *Wú-zhèng sān-mèi*.

### Abstract

The *Suí-zì-yì sān-mèi* belongs together with Huì-sī's other major surviving works ([[KR6d0151]], [[KR6d0152]], [[KR6d0156]]) as the documentary record of his pre-Zhìyǐ Sinitic Mahāyāna meditative-doctrinal synthesis. The composition is bracketed within Huì-sī's productive period at Nán-yuè 南嶽 c. 560–577.
