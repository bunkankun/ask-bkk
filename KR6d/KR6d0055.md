# Guānyīn jīng yìshū jì huìběn 觀音經義疏記會本
*Combined Edition of the Guānyīn Sūtra Doctrinal Subcommentary*
spoken by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ, 說); subcommentary by [[知禮]] (Zhīlǐ of Sòng, 述)

## About the work

A four-*juan* combined-edition (*huìběn* 會本) interweaving Zhìyǐ's *Guānyīn yìshū* ([[KR6d0048]], T1728) with Zhīlǐ's *Guānyīn yìshū jì* ([[KR6d0049]], T1729) into a single readable text. The companion to the *Xuányì jì huìběn* of [[KR6d0052]]; together the two combined-editions complete Wáng Jìcháng's 王季常 late-Míng editorial project on the Tiāntái *Pǔmén* corpus, undertaken at Jīnlíng in Chóngzhēn 崇禎 1–2 (1628–1629).

### Prefaces

The text in the X35n0645 recension carries no separate translator's preface beyond what is preserved in the bracketed [...] sections drawn from the original Sòng *Yìshū jì* (see [[KR6d0049]]). The editorial method follows that of [[KR6d0052]]: each pericope of Zhìyǐ's *Yìshū* is presented in the smaller / distinguished typography, immediately followed by the corresponding section of Zhīlǐ's *Yìshū jì*, allowing the reader to follow the original Sui-period commentary and its Sòng subcommentary in parallel.

### Abstract

The combined-edition (*huìběn*) is a late-Míng editorial product designed to make Zhīlǐ's Sòng-period subcommentary on Zhìyǐ's *Guānyīn yìshū* accessible to a non-scholarly readership. Together with the parallel *Xuányì jì huìběn* ([[KR6d0052]]) and the four auxiliary navigational works ([[KR6d0050]], [[KR6d0051]], [[KR6d0053]], [[KR6d0054]]), it constitutes Wáng Jìcháng's complete late-Míng editorial reorganisation of the Tiāntái *Pǔmén* corpus into a unified accessible format.

The substantive content remains the Sui-period *Yìshū* of Zhìyǐ and the Sòng-period *Yìshū jì* of Zhīlǐ; for the doctrinal discussion of these works see [[KR6d0048]] and [[KR6d0049]] respectively. The *huìběn* itself is a late-Míng editorial production (1628–1629) and contributes no new doctrinal content beyond the editorial reorganisation.

## Other points of interest

The completion of both Wáng Jìcháng's combined-editions (this text and [[KR6d0052]]) provides the late-Míng monastic and lay reader with a single-volume readable apparatus for the entire Tiāntái *Pǔmén* commentarial corpus, demonstrating the editorial ambition and pedagogical thoroughness of the late-Míng Buddhist publishing enterprise. The reorganisation of dense Sòng-period scholastic material into accessible combined-edition formats was one of the principal late-Míng monastic-editorial contributions to the broader transmission of the medieval Buddhist heritage to early-modern East-Asian readership.
