# Suí-yuán jí 隨緣集
*The Following-Conditions Collection*
by [[靈耀]] (Língyào / Língyào Quánzhāng, 著)

## About the work

A four-*juan* Qing-period literary collection by **Líng-yào Quán-zhāng** 靈耀全彰 — the early-to-mid-Qīng Tiāntái master also responsible for [[KR6d0012]], [[KR6d0057]], [[KR6d0142]], and [[KR6d0173]]. The work is the most extensive of Líng-yào's literary productions and contains his collected miscellaneous writings on Tiāntái doctrine, Buddhist practice, and broader Buddhist intellectual culture. The title — *suí-yuán* 隨緣 ("following-conditions") — draws on the standard Mahāyāna doctrinal vocabulary of *yuán-qǐ* 緣起 (dependent-origination).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Líng-yào's productive period c. 1644–1796 (early-to-mid Qīng era). The work is one of the few substantial Qīng Tiāntái literary collections preserved in the canonical apparatus and is consequently of substantial value for the modern reconstruction of Qīng Tiāntái intellectual culture.
