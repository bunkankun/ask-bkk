# Fǎhuá jīng zhǐ-zhǎng shū xuán-shì 法華經指掌疏懸示
*Hanging-Display of the Pointing-the-Palm Subcommentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[通理]] (Tōng-lǐ, 述)

## About the work

The introductory exposition (*xuán-shì* 懸示, "hanging-display") volume of Tōng-lǐ's four-text *Fǎhuá jīng zhǐ-zhǎng shū* commentarial complex (see [[KR6d0095]]). Provides the doctrinal-historical introduction to the *Zhǐ-zhǎng shū* main commentary ([[KR6d0097]]).

### Prefaces

The text carries the standard front matter for the introductory exposition. The *xuán-shì* genre is parallel to the *xuán-tán* of [[KR6d0092]] and to the broader Tiāntái *xuán-yì* 玄義 framework — providing the systematic doctrinal-historical preface that orients the reader to the main commentary.

### Abstract

The *Xuán-shì* belongs together with [[KR6d0095]] (the *kē-wén*) as the principal navigational-introductory apparatus to Tōng-lǐ's *Zhǐ-zhǎng shū* main commentary ([[KR6d0097]]). The two together fulfill the function of the standard Tiāntái scholastic *Wǔ-zhòng xuán-yì* introductory framework, providing the structural and doctrinal orientation needed for productive engagement with the main commentary.
