# Tiāntái sì-jiào yí jí-zhù (xuǎn-lù "jí-zhù" běn-wén) 天台四教儀集註
*The Tiāntái Four-Doctrines Manual with Compiled Annotations* (Northern Yongle canon recension; with selected extracts from the *jí-zhù* base text)
by [[諦觀]] (Dìguān / Cheguan, 注), with Sòng-Yuán-Míng compiled commentaries

## About the work

A nine-*juan* expanded combined-edition of Dìguān's *Tiāntái sì-jiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931) with substantial Sòng-Yuán-Míng commentary integrated into a unified text, preserved in the Northern Yongle Edition of the Canon (永樂北藏 *Yǒnglè běi-zàng*, completed c. 1410–1440) as L189n1629.

### Abstract

The Yongle canon recension preserves Dìguān's original concise *Sì-jiào yí* expanded with substantial Sòng-Yuán-Míng commentary into a comprehensive combined-edition apparatus. The work belongs together with the parallel Yongle-canon recension of Jièhuán's *Yào-jiě* ([[KR6d0068]], L184n1617) as evidence for the Yongle-period editorial practice of producing expanded annotated recensions of major commentarial works.

The dating is bracketed loosely from Dìguān's original composition (c. 960) through the Yongle canon's completion (c. 1410).
