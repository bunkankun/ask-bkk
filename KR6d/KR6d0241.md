# Shānjiā xùyú jí 山家緒餘集
*The Mountain-House Continuing-Remainder Collection*
by [[善月]] (Shànyuè / Bóting Shànyuè, 述)

## About the work

A three-*juan* Southern-Sòng *shānjiā* miscellany-collection by **Bóting Shànyuè** 柏庭善月, gathering supplementary writings (*xùyú* 緒餘 — "continuing-remainder") on the Tiāntái doctrinal tradition.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Shànyuè's productive period c. 1180–1240.
