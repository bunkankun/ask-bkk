# Sì-jiào yí jí-zhù jié-yì 四教儀集註節義
*Selected-Meanings of the Compiled-Annotations on the Four-Doctrines Manual*
by [[靈耀]] (Líng-yào / Líng-yào Quán-zhāng, 節)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Qing-period selective-extract (節 *jié*) commentary by **Líng-yào Quán-zhāng** 靈耀全彰 on Méng-rùn's *Sì-jiào yí jí-zhù* — providing accessible Qing-period extracts of the Yuán combined-edition apparatus. Companion to Líng-yào's other Tiāntái productions ([[KR6d0012]], [[KR6d0057]], [[KR6d0142]]).

### Prefaces

The text opens with a *Sì-jiào jí-zhù jié-yì bìng kē xù* 四教集註節義并科序 (preface to the joint *jié-yì* and *kē*).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Líng-yào's productive period c. 1644–1796 (early-to-mid Qīng era).
