# Dà-shèng zhǐ-guān fǎ-mén zōng-yuán jì 大乘止觀法門宗圓記
*Lineage-Perfecting Notes on the Mahāyāna Cessation-and-Contemplation Dharma-Gates*
by [[了然]] (Liǎo-rán / Zhì-yǒng Liǎo-rán / Hǔ-xī zūn-zhě, 述)

## About the work

A five-*juan* Sòng-period subcommentary on Huì-sī's *Dà-shèng zhǐ-guān fǎ-mén* ([[KR6d0152]], T1924) by **Liǎo-rán** 了然 (1077–1141), the Northern-through-Southern Sòng Tiāntái master and *Vinaya* expert. The work is the principal Sòng-period subcommentary on Huì-sī's foundational pre-Tiāntái meditation manual.

### Prefaces

The text in the X55n0904 recension carries the standard front matter; the body proceeds with the running commentary.

### Abstract

Liǎo-rán's *Zōng-yuán jì* — its title literally "lineage-perfecting notes" — provides systematic Sòng *shān-jiā* 山家 commentary on Huì-sī's foundational meditation manual, integrating the work into the standard Tiāntái scholastic tradition. The composition is bracketed within Liǎo-rán's productive period c. 1095–1141.
