# Dàshèng zhǐguān fǎmén zōngyuán jì 大乘止觀法門宗圓記
*Lineage-Perfecting Notes on the Mahāyāna Cessation-and-Contemplation Dharma-Gates*
by [[了然]] (Liǎorán / Zhìyǒng Liǎorán / Hǔxī zūnzhě, 述)

## About the work

A five-*juan* Sòng-period subcommentary on Huìsī's *Dàshèng zhǐguān fǎmén* ([[KR6d0152]], T1924) by **Liǎorán** 了然 (1077–1141), the Northern-through-Southern Sòng Tiāntái master and *Vinaya* expert. The work is the principal Sòng-period subcommentary on Huìsī's foundational pre-Tiāntái meditation manual.

### Prefaces

The text in the X55n0904 recension carries the standard front matter; the body proceeds with the running commentary.

### Abstract

Liǎorán's *Zōngyuán jì* — its title literally "lineage-perfecting notes" — provides systematic Sòng *shānjiā* 山家 commentary on Huìsī's foundational meditation manual, integrating the work into the standard Tiāntái scholastic tradition. The composition is bracketed within Liǎorán's productive period c. 1095–1141.
