# Bié-chuán xīn-fǎ yì 別傳心法議
*Discussion of the Separately-Transmitted Heart-Dharma*
by [[戒珠]] (Jièzhū / Fēishān Jièzhū, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng polemical treatise by **Fēi-shān Jiè-zhū** 飛山戒珠 (b. 985) on the doctrine of the **separately-transmitted heart-dharma** (*bié-chuán xīn-fǎ* 別傳心法) — addressing the standard Chán-school claim to exclusive transmission of the Buddha's awakening outside the standard scriptural-doctrinal channels.

### Abstract

Jiè-zhū's *Yì* belongs to the substantial Sòng-period Tiāntái-Chán polemical literature, articulating the Tiāntái-tradition critique of the Chán-school's exclusive-transmission claim. The composition is bracketed within Jiè-zhū's productive period c. 1010–1080.
