# Shí bù-èr mén shū-yào 十不二門樞要
*Pivot-Essentials of the Ten Non-Dual Gates*
by [[了然]] (Liǎo-rán / Zhì-yǒng Liǎo-rán / Hǔ-xī zūn-zhě, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* Sòng-period commentary by **Liǎo-rán** 了然 (1077–1141) on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* ([[KR6d0157]]). Companion to Liǎo-rán's other major Sòng *shān-jiā* commentary, the *Dà-shèng zhǐ-guān fǎ-mén zōng-yuán jì* ([[KR6d0153]]) on Huì-sī's *Dà-shèng zhǐ-guān fǎ-mén*.

### Abstract

Liǎo-rán's *Shū-yào* belongs to the late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng productive period of the *shān-jiā* commentarial tradition. Together with the broader *Shí bù-èr mén* commentarial corpus ([[KR6d0158]]–[[KR6d0165]]), it documents the mature Sòng Tiāntái scholastic engagement with Zhànrán's foundational doctrinal apparatus.

The composition is bracketed within Liǎo-rán's productive period c. 1095–1141.
