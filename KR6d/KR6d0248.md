# Fǎ-jiè ān-lì tú 法界安立圖
*Diagrams of the Establishment of the Dharma-Realm*
compiled by [[仁潮]] (Réncháo / Bèilín Réncháo, 錄)

## About the work

A three-*juan* late-Míng systematic exposition by **Bèi-lín Rén-cháo** 貝琳仁潮 of the Buddhist *fǎ-jiè* 法界 (*dharmadhātu*) cosmological structure, with topographical-cosmological diagrams. The work belongs to the late-Míng Buddhist cosmographic tradition that systematised the foundational Buddhist cosmological materials (*Sumeru* cosmology, the *trichiliocosm*, the *six paths*, etc.) into accessible visual-textual form for late-imperial readership.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within the late-Míng productive period (1573–1644).
