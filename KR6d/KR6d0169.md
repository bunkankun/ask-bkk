# Sì-jiào yí jí-jiě 四教儀集解
*Compiled Interpretations of the Four-Doctrines Manual*
by [[從義]] (Cóngyì / Shénzhì, 撰)

## About the work

A three-*juan* Northern-Sòng compiled-interpretations commentary by **Cóngyì** 從義 (1042–1091) on Dìguān's *Tiāntái sì-jiào yí* ([[KR6d0168]], T1931). The work is the principal Sòng-period subcommentary on the standard Tiāntái introductory manual, and through its transmission the standard medieval pedagogical apparatus for the *Sì-jiào yí*.

### Abstract

Cóngyì's *Jí-jiě* draws together the various interpretations of Dìguān's *Sì-jiào yí* current in the mid-Northern-Sòng *shān-jiā* tradition, providing systematic commentary on each section of the manual. The work belongs together with Cóngyì's other major Sòng productions ([[KR6d0063]], [[KR6d0139]], [[KR6d0140]]) as the principal mid-Northern-Sòng *shān-jiā* scholastic apparatus.

The composition is bracketed within Cóngyì's productive period c. 1062–1091.
