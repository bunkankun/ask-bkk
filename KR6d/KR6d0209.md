# Sānguān yì 三觀義
*The Meaning of the Threefold Contemplation*
by [[智顗]] (Zhìyǐ / Tiāntái dàshī, 撰)

## About the work

A two-*juan* (上 / 下) Tiāntái doctrinal-meditative treatise by Zhìyǐ 智顗 on the **threefold contemplation** (*sānguān* 三觀) — the foundational Tiāntái meditative apparatus comprising contemplation of the empty (*kōng* 空), the provisional (*jiǎ* 假), and the middle (*zhōng* 中). The doctrine of the *sānguān* is central to the Tiāntái scholastic tradition and provides the meditative-doctrinal apparatus for the *yīxīn sānguān* 一心三觀 ("threefold contemplation in a single mind") that became the school's most distinctive meditative practice.

### Abstract

Zhìyǐ's *Sānguān yì* belongs together with the *Móhē zhǐguān* ([[KR6d0130]]) as the foundational textual authority for the Tiāntái threefold-contemplation tradition. The composition is bracketed within Zhìyǐ's productive period c. 575–597.
