# Bǎo-yún zhèn-zǔ jí 寶雲振祖集
*Collection of the Bǎo-yún Patriarchal-Promulgation*
edited by [[宗曉]] (Zōngxiǎo / Shízhī Zōngxiǎo, 編)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng compilation by **Shí-zhī Zōng-xiǎo** 石芝宗曉 (1151–1214) of materials documenting the **Bǎo-yún-sì** 寶雲寺 Tiāntái lineage — the institutional centre under **Bǎo-yún Yì-tōng** 寶雲義通 (927–988) at which Sì-míng Zhīlǐ received his Tiāntái formation. Companion to Yuán-wù's parallel *Luó-xī zhèn-zǔ jí* ([[KR6d0223]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zōng-xiǎo's productive period c. 1180–1214.
