# Shí bù-èr mén yì 十不二門義
*The Meaning of the Ten Non-Dual Gates*
extracted-and-published by [[道邃]] (Dào-suì / Xīngdào, 錄出)

## About the work

A single-*juan* late-Táng record of Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* ([[KR6d0157]], T1927), extracted-and-published (錄出 *lù-chū*) by **Dào-suì** 道邃 — the tenth Tiāntái patriarch and Saichō's Chinese teacher. The work preserves the *Shí bù-èr mén* in a slightly different recension or with editorial framing distinct from the Taishō T1927 recension, demonstrating the post-Zhànrán textual transmission at Tiāntái-shān.

### Prefaces

The text in the X56n0925 recension reproduces Zhànrán's text with editorial framing by Dào-suì.

### Abstract

Dào-suì's *Shí bù-èr mén yì* belongs together with his *Zhǐguān jì-zhōng yì-yì* ([[KR6d0136]]) as the principal documentary witnesses to Dào-suì's role as the post-Zhànrán Tiāntái scholastic transmitter at Tiāntái-shān. The work is consequently of substantial historiographical importance for the modern reconstruction of late-Táng Tiāntái scholastic activity.

The composition is bracketed within Dào-suì's productive period c. 800–850.
