# Fǎhuá jīng yǎnyì kē 法華經演義科
*Sectional Analysis of the Lotus Sūtra Expounded-Meaning Commentary*
by [[廣和]] (Guǎnghé, 標科 — labelled-sections compiler)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) navigational apparatus to [[一松]] Yīsōng's *Fǎhuá jīng yǎnyì* ([[KR6d0091]], X33n0625, 7 *juan*), compiled by [[廣和]] Guǎnghé — the early-Qīng monastic editor who also redacted Yīsōng's main commentary into its definitive form. The work is preserved as X33n0624 in the *Xùzàngjīng*.

### Prefaces

The text in the X33n0624 recension carries the standard front-matter material, which includes the *Xīnkè Fǎhuá yǎnyì xù* 新刻法華演義序 by [[曉柔]] Xiǎoróu — the principal source for Yīsōng's biographical obscurity (Xiǎoróu states: "the Master is not known of what country he was, nor is his era clearly understood").

### Abstract

The *Yǎnyì kē* provides the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Yīsōng's *Yǎnyì* main commentary, parallel to other Qīng-period *kē* productions on major commentarial works. Its compilation by Guǎnghé — the same editor responsible for the main commentary's final redaction — demonstrates the integrated editorial production typical of the late-imperial monastic publishing tradition.

The dating is bracketed within Guǎnghé's productive period in the early Qīng (Shùnzhì through Kāngxī eras, 1644–1722).
