# Fǎhuá jīng yǎn-yì kē 法華經演義科
*Sectional Analysis of the Lotus Sūtra Expounded-Meaning Commentary*
by [[廣和]] (Guǎng-hé, 標科 — labelled-sections compiler)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) navigational apparatus to **Yī-sōng** 一松's *Fǎhuá jīng yǎn-yì* ([[KR6d0091]], X33n0625, 7 *juan*), compiled by Guǎng-hé 廣和 — the early-Qīng monastic editor who also redacted Yī-sōng's main commentary into its definitive form. The work is preserved as X33n0624 in the *Xù-zàng-jīng*.

### Prefaces

The text in the X33n0624 recension carries the standard front-matter material, which includes the *Xīn-kè Fǎhuá yǎn-yì xù* 新刻法華演義序 by **Xiǎo-róu** 曉柔 — the principal source for Yī-sōng's biographical obscurity (Xiǎo-róu states: "the Master is not known of what country he was, nor is his era clearly understood").

### Abstract

The *Yǎn-yì kē* provides the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Yī-sōng's *Yǎn-yì* main commentary, parallel to other Qīng-period *kē* productions on major commentarial works. Its compilation by Guǎng-hé — the same editor responsible for the main commentary's final redaction — demonstrates the integrated editorial production typical of the late-imperial monastic publishing tradition.

The dating is bracketed within Guǎng-hé's productive period in the early Qīng (Shùn-zhì through Kāngxī eras, 1644–1722).
