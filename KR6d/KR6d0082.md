# Fǎhuá jīng huì-yì 法華經會義
*Synthetic-Meaning Commentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒu-yì Zhìxù, 述)

## About the work

A seven-*juan* late-Míng running commentary on the Lotus Sūtra by Ǒu-yì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655). The work is the longer companion to Zhìxù's synoptic *Lún-guàn* ([[KR6d0081]]): the *Lún-guàn* providing the unifying synoptic framework, the *Huì-yì* providing the comprehensive running commentary on the entire Lotus.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0616 recension carries Zhìxù's own framing of the *huì-yì* 會義 ("synthetic-meaning") genre: a commentary that synthesises the principal interpretive traditions into a unified expository treatment. The work belongs together with the *Lún-guàn*, the *Jié-yào* ([[KR6d0010]]), and the *Yú-jiā jí-yào* as Zhìxù's principal Tiāntái-school productions on the Lotus Sūtra and the broader Tiāntái triple-treatise corpus.

### Abstract

The *Huì-yì* is the principal late-Míng Tiāntái-school running commentary on the Lotus Sūtra. Where Zhìyǐ's *Wén-jù* ([[KR6d0014]]) and Zhànrán's *Wén-jù jì* ([[KR6d0015]]) had established the foundational Tiāntái commentarial apparatus, Zhìxù's *Huì-yì* provides the late-Míng synthetic re-presentation of that apparatus suitable for late-imperial monastic and lay readership. The work draws on (1) the foundational Tiāntái commentarial corpus; (2) the Sòng *shān-jiā* tradition through Sì-míng Zhīlǐ; (3) Hānshān Déqīng's Chán-Yogācāra synthetic apparatus; and (4) Zhìxù's own Pure Land devotional commitments.

The work's distinctive contribution is its harmonisation of the strict Tiāntái scholastic apparatus with the Pure Land devotional tradition that Zhìxù championed. The result is the principal late-Míng Tiāntái-Pure Land synthetic Lotus Sūtra commentary and one of the most influential late-imperial Buddhist commentaries on any Mahāyāna sūtra.

The composition is bracketed within Zhìxù's mature productive period after 1641, when he established his retreat at Mount Jiǔhuá 九華 and committed to systematic Tiāntái-Pure Land synthetic exposition. Most plausibly placed in the period 1645–1655.

## Other points of interest

The *Lún-guàn* + *Huì-yì* pair on the Lotus Sūtra is one of Zhìxù's most ambitious productive achievements and represents the late-Míng Tiāntái tradition's principal contribution to the Lotus Sūtra commentarial corpus. Together with his abridgement of the *Xuán-yì* ([[KR6d0010]]) and his various Tiāntái scholastic productions, the Lotus Sūtra apparatus completes Zhìxù's late-Míng pedagogical project of systematic synthesis of the Tiāntái scholastic tradition with the broader Mahāyāna devotional and meditative apparatus.
