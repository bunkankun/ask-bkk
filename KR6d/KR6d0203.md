# Déyù Lónghuá xiūzhèng chànyí 得遇龍華修證懺儀
*Repentance Liturgy for Cultivating-and-Verifying the Encounter with the Dragon-Flower [Maitreya]*
by [[如惺]] (Rúxīng, 撰)

## About the work

A four-*juan* late-Míng repentance liturgy by **Rúxīng** 如惺 for the Maitreya devotional cult — specifically for the practice of cultivating-and-verifying the *déyù Lónghuá* 得遇龍華 (encounter with the *Lónghuáshù* — the Dragon-Flower Tree under which Maitreya will become Buddha in the future age). The work belongs to the substantial late-Míng Maitreya devotional tradition and provides standardised liturgical apparatus for the practice.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within the late-Míng productive period (Wànlì through Chóngzhēn era, 1573–1644).
