# Luó-xī zhèn-zǔ jí 螺溪振祖集
*Collection of the Luó-xī Patriarchal-Promulgation*
edited by [[元悟]] (Yuán-wù / Luóxī Yuánwù, 編)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng compilation by **Luó-xī Yuán-wù** 螺溪元悟 of materials documenting the Tiāntái-shān **Luó-xī Chuán-jiào-yuàn** 螺溪傳教院 patriarchal lineage — the same institutional context at which Goryeo Dìguān had transmitted the recovered Tiāntái texts back to China. Companion to Zōng-xiǎo's parallel *Bǎo-yún zhèn-zǔ jí* ([[KR6d0222]]).

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Yuán-wù's productive period in the Southern Sòng (c. 1150–1250).
