# Shí bù-èr mén zhǐ-yào chāo xiáng-jiě 十不二門指要鈔詳解
*Detailed Interpretation of the Pointing-the-Essentials Notes on the Ten Non-Dual Gates*
by [[可度]] (Kě-dù / Wú-jí Kě-dù, 祥解); divided-and-collated by [[正謐]] (Zhèngmì / Yǐ-xuán, 分會)

## About the work

A two-*juan* late-Sòng / early-Yuán detailed commentary by **Wú-jí Kě-dù** 無極可度 on Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐ-yào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]], T1928), with editorial *fēn-huì* 分會 ("division-and-combination") work by the late-Míng monk **Yǐ-xuán Zhèng-mì** 以玄正謐 of Lín-hǎi 臨海. The work is the principal late-Sòng-Yuán-Míng subcommentary on Zhīlǐ's foundational *shān-jiā* polemical work.

### Abstract

Kě-dù's *Xiáng-jiě* provides systematic late-Sòng / early-Yuán Tiāntái commentary on Zhīlǐ's *Zhǐ-yào chāo*, integrating two centuries of accumulated scholastic interpretation of the work into a unified detailed exposition. The Míng editorial work by Zhèng-mì subsequently reorganised the text for late-imperial readership.

The dating of the original Kě-dù composition is bracketed within his productive period in the late Sòng / early Yuán transition (c. 1200–1300); the editorial finalisation by Zhèng-mì extends the bracket to the late-Míng period (c. 1400–1600).
