# Fǎhuá jīng dà-chéng kē 法華經大成科
*Sectional Analysis of the Lotus Sūtra Great Consummation*
by [[際慶]] (Jì-qìng, 排錄 — sequential arranger)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) navigational apparatus to **Dà-yì** 大義's *Fǎhuá jīng dà-chéng* ([[KR6d0085]], X32n0619, 10 *juan*), compiled by Jì-qìng 際慶 of the **Jìn-níng Guǎng-jì-sì** 晉寧廣濟寺 in modern Yúnnán. The preface by **Wéi-xuán** 惟鉉 dates the work to Kāngxī 丙戌 (= 1706 CE).

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0618 recension carries Wéi-xuán's preface dated Kāngxī 1706 (the *bǐng-xū* year). The work consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kē-pàn* sectional division of the *Dà-chéng*.

### Abstract

The *Dà-chéng kē* is the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Dà-yì's *Dà-chéng* ([[KR6d0085]]), produced concurrently with or shortly after the *Dà-chéng* itself for use in monastic and lay study. Its compilation in 1706 establishes a *terminus ante quem* of 1706 for the completion of Dà-yì's *Dà-chéng* — a useful constraint for dating Dà-yì's productive activity, which according to his own *Biān-jí shǐ-mò* (compilation history) extended through 1736 for the final consolidation of the *Dà-chéng*'s ten *juan*.

The pairing of [[KR6d0085]] (the *Dà-chéng*) with [[KR6d0084]] (its *kē*) and [[KR6d0086]] (its *yīn-yì*, phonetic-and-meaning glossary) demonstrates the systematic late-Kāngxī editorial practice of providing complete navigational apparatus for major commentarial productions — a continuation of the Sòng tradition observed in [[KR6d0061]] and elsewhere.

## Other points of interest

The Yúnnán institutional location of Jì-qìng's productive activity at the Guǎng-jì-sì in Jìn-níng demonstrates the geographical breadth of the early-Qīng Tiāntái scholastic network, extending to the southwestern frontier provinces. The substantial editorial apparatus produced at this institutional location (the *Dà-chéng kē* of 1706) attests to the institutional vitality of southwestern Chinese Buddhism in the high-Kāngxī period.
