# Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng kē 法華經大成科
*Sectional Analysis of the Lotus Sūtra Great Consummation*
by [[際慶]] (Jìqìng, 排錄 — sequential arranger)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) navigational apparatus to **Dàyì** 大義's *Fǎhuá jīng dàchéng* ([[KR6d0085]], X32n0619, 10 *juan*), compiled by Jìqìng 際慶 of the **Jìnníng Guǎngjìsì** 晉寧廣濟寺 in modern Yúnnán. The preface by **Wéixuán** 惟鉉 dates the work to Kāngxī 丙戌 (= 1706 CE).

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0618 recension carries Wéixuán's preface dated Kāngxī 1706 (the *bǐngxū* year). The work consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kēpàn* sectional division of the *Dàchéng*.

### Abstract

The *Dàchéng kē* is the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Dàyì's *Dàchéng* ([[KR6d0085]]), produced concurrently with or shortly after the *Dàchéng* itself for use in monastic and lay study. Its compilation in 1706 establishes a *terminus ante quem* of 1706 for the completion of Dàyì's *Dàchéng* — a useful constraint for dating Dàyì's productive activity, which according to his own *Biānjí shǐmò* (compilation history) extended through 1736 for the final consolidation of the *Dàchéng*'s ten *juan*.

The pairing of [[KR6d0085]] (the *Dàchéng*) with [[KR6d0084]] (its *kē*) and [[KR6d0086]] (its *yīnyì*, phonetic-and-meaning glossary) demonstrates the systematic late-Kāngxī editorial practice of providing complete navigational apparatus for major commentarial productions — a continuation of the Sòng tradition observed in [[KR6d0061]] and elsewhere.

## Other points of interest

The Yúnnán institutional location of Jìqìng's productive activity at the Guǎngjìsì in Jìnníng demonstrates the geographical breadth of the early-Qīng Tiāntái scholastic network, extending to the southwestern frontier provinces. The substantial editorial apparatus produced at this institutional location (the *Dàchéng kē* of 1706) attests to the institutional vitality of southwestern Chinese Buddhism in the high-Kāngxī period.
