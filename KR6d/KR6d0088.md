# Fǎhuá jīng shòu-shǒu kē 法華經授手科
*Sectional Analysis of the Hand-Bestowal Commentary on the Lotus Sūtra*
by [[智祥]] (Zhì-xiáng / Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng / Tīng-yún, 排 — sequential arranger)

## About the work

The sectional-analysis (*kē* 科) volume of Zhì-xiáng's three-part *Fǎhuá jīng shòu-shǒu* 法華經授手 commentarial complex (see [[KR6d0087]], [[KR6d0089]]). Compiled by Pín-jí Zhì-xiáng 頻吉智祥 (1637–1709) himself.

### Prefaces

The text in the X32n0622 recension carries Zhì-xiáng's own editorial framing of the sectional analysis. The work consists exclusively of hierarchical structural diagrams showing the *kē-pàn* sectional division of the *Shòu-shǒu* main commentary ([[KR6d0089]]).

### Abstract

The *Shòu-shǒu kē* provides the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Zhì-xiáng's *Shòu-shǒu* main commentary, parallel to [[KR6d0084]] (the *Dà-chéng kē*) on Dà-yì's *Dà-chéng*. The three-part Zhì-xiáng *Shòu-shǒu* complex (front matter, sectional analysis, main commentary) follows the standard Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng editorial pattern of providing complete navigational apparatus for major commentarial productions.
