# Fǎhuá shí miào bù-èr mén shì-zhū zhǐ 法華十妙不二門示珠指
*Showing-the-Pearl Indicators of the Lotus's Ten Wonders and Non-Dual Gates*
by [[源清]] (Yuán-qīng / Fèng-xiān Yuán-qīng, 述)

## About the work

A two-*juan* Northern-Sòng commentary by Yuán-qīng 源清 (d. 1000) on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* ([[KR6d0157]], T1927) integrated with the broader Lotus *shí miào* 十妙 ("ten wonders") doctrinal apparatus of Zhìyǐ's *Xuán-yì*. The work is the earliest of the substantial Sòng *Shí bù-èr mén* commentaries.

### Abstract

Yuán-qīng's *Shì-zhū zhǐ* belongs to the very early Northern-Sòng productive period (Yuán-qīng died in 1000, twenty-eight years before Zhīlǐ), making it the earliest substantial Sòng-period commentary on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* — predating Zhīlǐ's foundational *Zhǐ-yào chāo* ([[KR6d0158]]) by some quarter-century. The work is consequently of substantial historiographical interest as evidence for the pre-Zhīlǐ Sòng Tiāntái scholastic tradition.

The composition is bracketed within Yuán-qīng's productive period c. 980–1000.
