# Jīn-gāng-bēi kē 金剛錍科 (Rényuè recension)
*Sectional Analysis of the Vajra-Lance*
by [[仁岳]] (Rényuè / Jìngjué Rényuè, 撰)

## About the work

A single-*juan* sectional analysis (*kē* 科) of Zhànrán's *Jīn-gāng-bēi* ([[KR6d0175]], T1932) by **Rényuè** 仁岳 — the principal Northern-Sòng *shān-wài* spokesperson. Body attribution: *Sòng Yún-jiān shāmén Jìng-yuè zhuàn* 宋　雲間沙門　淨岳　撰 (note: 淨岳 vs the more standard form 仁岳; "Jìng-yuè" 淨岳 is an alternate name preserved in this attribution).

### Abstract

Rényuè's *Kē* provides the structural-outline navigational apparatus to Zhànrán's *Jīn-gāng-bēi* and belongs together with his parallel work on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* ([[KR6d0162]]). The composition is bracketed within Rényuè's productive period c. 1010–1060.
