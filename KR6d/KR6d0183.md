# Guóqīng bǎi-lù 國清百錄
*The Hundred Records of Guóqīng [-sì]*
compiled by [[灌頂]] (Guàndǐng / Zhāng-ān Guàndǐng, 纂)

## About the work

A four-*juan* compilation of administrative-historical records from the **Tiāntái-shān Guóqīng-sì** 國清寺 (the principal Tiāntái monastery established by Zhìyǐ at Tiāntái-shān), compiled by **Guàndǐng** 灌頂 (561–632). The work is the principal documentary source for the institutional history of the early Tiāntái establishment and contains essential biographical, administrative, and ritual materials.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the *Guóqīng bǎi-lù xù* 國清百錄序 by Guàndǐng. The preface narrates the textual situation: "*The deceased master [Zhìyǐ] in Chén Tài-jiàn 7, the year* yǐ-wèi *[= 575], first hid himself at Tiāntái. The peak where he stayed was anciently named Fó-lǒng [Buddha-Mound]. Inquiring of the local people, [they said] 'those who roam this mountain often see the Buddha-image; therefore [it] became known as such.' Reaching Tài-jiàn 10, the* wù-xū *year [= 578], imperial command from Chén Xuān-dì named [it] Xiū-chán-sì [Cultivation-of-Meditation Monastery]. The Department-of-Personnel Senior-Minister Máo Xǐ*…"

### Abstract

The *Guóqīng bǎi-lù* contains administrative-historical materials documenting the early Tiāntái establishment, including imperial edicts, monastic correspondence, ritual texts, and biographical records. It is the principal documentary source for the modern reconstruction of the early Tiāntái institutional history and for the relationship between the Tiāntái establishment and the imperial Suí court under Wén-dì 文帝 and Yáng-dì 煬帝.

The composition is dated to Guàndǐng's productive period after Zhìyǐ's death (597) and before his own death (632).
