# Jīn-gāng-bēi kē 金剛錍科 (Zhì-yuán recension)
*Sectional Analysis of the Vajra-Lance*
by [[智圓]] (Zhìyuán / Gūshān Zhìyuán / Wúwài, 集)

## About the work

A single-*juan* alternative sectional analysis (*kē* 科) of Zhànrán's *Jīn-gāng-bēi* ([[KR6d0175]], T1932) by **Gū-shān Zhì-yuán** 孤山智圓 (976–1022) — one of the principal *shān-wài* spokespersons alongside Rényuè ([[仁岳]]). The work is parallel to Rényuè's *Kē* ([[KR6d0177]]); together they constitute the principal *shān-wài* tradition's navigational apparatus on the *Jīn-gāng-bēi*.

### Abstract

Zhì-yuán's *Kē* and *Xiǎn-xìng lù* ([[KR6d0179]]) together constitute the principal *shān-wài* commentarial apparatus on the *Jīn-gāng-bēi*, providing the *shān-wài* alternative interpretation of Zhànrán's doctrine of the buddha-nature of insentient beings. The composition is bracketed within Zhì-yuán's productive period c. 996–1022.
