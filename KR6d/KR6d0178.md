# Jīngāngbēi kē 金剛錍科 (Zhìyuán recension)
*Sectional Analysis of the Vajra-Lance*
by [[智圓]] (Zhìyuán / Gūshān Zhìyuán / Wúwài, 集)

## About the work

A single-*juan* alternative sectional analysis (*kē* 科) of Zhànrán's *Jīngāngbēi* ([[KR6d0175]], T1932) by **Gūshān Zhìyuán** 孤山智圓 (976–1022) — one of the principal *shānwài* spokespersons alongside Rényuè ([[仁岳]]). The work is parallel to Rényuè's *Kē* ([[KR6d0177]]); together they constitute the principal *shānwài* tradition's navigational apparatus on the *Jīngāngbēi*.

### Abstract

Zhìyuán's *Kē* and *Xiǎnxìng lù* ([[KR6d0179]]) together constitute the principal *shānwài* commentarial apparatus on the *Jīngāngbēi*, providing the *shānwài* alternative interpretation of Zhànrán's doctrine of the buddha-nature of insentient beings. The composition is bracketed within Zhìyuán's productive period c. 996–1022.
