# Móhē zhǐguān yì-lì suí-shì 摩訶止觀義例隨釋
*Following-Explanation Subcommentary on the Examples-and-Cases of Mahā-Cessation-and-Contemplation*
by [[處元]] (Chǔyuán / Cǎotáng Chǔyuán, 述)

## About the work

A six-*juan* Northern-Sòng running subcommentary on Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān yì-lì* ([[KR6d0132]], T1913) by Chǔ-yuán 處元 (1030–1119) of the *shān-jiā* lineage. The genre — *suí-shì* 隨釋 ("following-explanation") — indicates a continuous commentary that follows the source-text *pari passu*, providing systematic phrase-by-phrase exposition. Parallel in genre and period to Cóngyì's *Yì-lì zuǎn-yào* ([[KR6d0139]]).

### Prefaces

The text in the X56n0923 recension carries the standard front matter; the body proceeds with the running commentary.

### Abstract

Chǔ-yuán's *Suí-shì* and Cóngyì's *Zuǎn-yào* together represent the two principal Northern-Sòng *shān-jiā* subcommentaries on Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān yì-lì* — both works compositionally located in the productive period 1060–1119. The two subcommentaries' parallel existence demonstrates the substantial Northern-Sòng *shān-jiā* scholastic engagement with the *Zhǐguān yì-lì*, parallel to the multi-commentary tradition on the *Móhē zhǐguān* itself ([[KR6d0131]], [[KR6d0137]], [[KR6d0138]]).

The composition is bracketed within Chǔ-yuán's productive period c. 1060–1119.
