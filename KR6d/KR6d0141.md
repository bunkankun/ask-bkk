# Móhē zhǐguān yìlì suíshì 摩訶止觀義例隨釋
*Following-Explanation Subcommentary on the Examples-and-Cases of Mahā-Cessation-and-Contemplation*
by [[處元]] (Chǔyuán / Cǎotáng Chǔyuán, 述)

## About the work

A six-*juan* Northern-Sòng running subcommentary on Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān yìlì* ([[KR6d0132]], T1913) by Chǔyuán 處元 (1030–1119) of the *shānjiā* lineage. The genre — *suíshì* 隨釋 ("following-explanation") — indicates a continuous commentary that follows the source-text *pari passu*, providing systematic phrase-by-phrase exposition. Parallel in genre and period to Cóngyì's *Yìlì zuǎnyào* ([[KR6d0139]]).

### Prefaces

The text in the X56n0923 recension carries the standard front matter; the body proceeds with the running commentary.

### Abstract

Chǔyuán's *Suíshì* and Cóngyì's *Zuǎnyào* together represent the two principal Northern-Sòng *shānjiā* subcommentaries on Zhànrán's *Zhǐguān yìlì* — both works compositionally located in the productive period 1060–1119. The two subcommentaries' parallel existence demonstrates the substantial Northern-Sòng *shānjiā* scholastic engagement with the *Zhǐguān yìlì*, parallel to the multi-commentary tradition on the *Móhē zhǐguān* itself ([[KR6d0131]], [[KR6d0137]], [[KR6d0138]]).

The composition is bracketed within Chǔyuán's productive period c. 1060–1119.
