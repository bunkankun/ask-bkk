# Sān-jiào chū-xīng sòng zhù 三教出興頌註
*Annotation on the Verses on the Emergence of the Three Teachings*
annotated by [[宗曉]] (Zōngxiǎo / Shízhī Zōngxiǎo, 注)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Southern-Sòng commentary by **Shí-zhī Zōng-xiǎo** 石芝宗曉 (1151–1214) on verses concerning the emergence of the three teachings (Buddhism, Confucianism, Daoism). Belongs to the broader Sòng-period inter-school harmonisation literature.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within Zōng-xiǎo's productive period c. 1180–1214.
