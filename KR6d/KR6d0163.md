# Fǎhuá xuán-jì shí bù-èr mén xiǎn-miào 法華玄記十不二門顯妙
*Manifesting-Wonder of the Lotus Profound-Notes Ten Non-Dual Gates*
by [[處謙]] (Chǔqiān / Shénwù Chǔqiān, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Northern-Sòng commentary by **Shén-wù Chǔ-qiān** 神悟處謙 (1011–1075) on Zhànrán's *Shí bù-èr mén* ([[KR6d0157]]). Belongs to the second-generation post-Zhīlǐ *shān-jiā* tradition.

### Abstract

The *Xiǎn-miào* belongs to the substantial mid-Northern-Sòng *Shí bù-èr mén* commentarial tradition that developed around Zhīlǐ's foundational *Zhǐ-yào chāo*. Chǔ-qiān's contribution provides additional doctrinal exposition focused on the *xiǎn-miào* (manifesting-wonder) aspect of the *Shí bù-èr mén* tradition.

The composition is bracketed within Chǔ-qiān's productive period c. 1040–1075.
