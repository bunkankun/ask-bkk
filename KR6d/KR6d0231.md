# Fùzōng jí 復宗集
*The Restoring-the-Lineage Collection*
by [[與咸]] (Yǔxián / Zéshān Yǔxián, 述)

## About the work

A single-*juan* Sòng Tiāntái-school polemical-doctrinal treatise by **Zéshān Yǔxián** 澤山與咸. The title — "*Restoring-the-Lineage*" — suggests a work concerned with the restoration of the orthodox Tiāntái transmission against perceived doctrinal deviation.

### Abstract

The composition is bracketed within the Sòng productive period c. 1010–1100.
