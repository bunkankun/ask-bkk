# Běncǎo Tōngxuán 本草通玄
*Penetrating the Mystery of the Materia Medica*
by [[李中梓]] (Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, *zì* Shìcái 士材, 1588–1655, 明)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo tōngxuán* is one of Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's principal pharmacological works, complementing his shorter *Běncǎo zhēngyào* ([[KR3ec027]]) and the processing-oriented *Léi Gōng páozhì yàoxìng jiě* ([[KR3ec029]]). The work in 2 *juǎn* gives substantive theoretical treatment of drug *qìwèi*, *guījīng*, and *bǔxiè* application, in the *Shìcái xuépài* clinical-tonification framework. It is part of the corpus of Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's *yīzōng* (medical-lineage) pedagogy. The title invokes the *Lǎozǐ*'s "*xuán zhī yòu xuán, zhòng miào zhī mén*" — pharmacology as a "mysterious gateway" to the structure of the body.

### Prefaces

The local repository contains the basic substance entries; the title of each entry is preceded by Lǐ's qìwèi summary followed by his theoretical exposition. No separate authorial preface is preserved in the local file. The work is dated, by analogy with Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's other writings, to the 1620s–1640s.

### Abstract

For Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's biographical background see [[KR3ec027]]. The *Tōngxuán* is the most theoretically articulated of his three pharmacological works, and is the principal source for understanding the *Shìcái xuépài* approach to drug-property analysis. It works through over 200 substances in 2 *juǎn*, with each entry developing a distinctive doctrinal point — typically the placement of the drug within the *bǔ pí shèn* (tonify spleen-and-kidney) framework that Lǐ inherited from his teacher Yáng Yīxù.

The work was widely studied in the late-Míng / Qīng through its inclusion in the *Lǐ Shìcái xiānshēng sān shū* 李士材先生三書 collection — alongside *Zhēngyào* (KR3ec027) and the *Léi Gōng páozhì yàoxìng jiě* (KR3ec029). Through this combined dissemination it was a principal pharmacological textbook for early-Qīng medical students.
