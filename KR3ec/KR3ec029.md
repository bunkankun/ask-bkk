# Léi Gōng Páozhì Yàoxìng Jiě 雷公炮製藥性解
*Léi Gōng's Drug-Processing Methods, with an Explanation of Drug Properties*
by [[李中梓]] (Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, *zì* Shìcái 士材, 1588–1655, 明), building on the legacy of [[雷敩]] (Léi Xiào, *fl.* 5th c., Liú-Sòng)

## About the work

The *Léi Gōng páozhì yàoxìng jiě* is Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's late-Míng synthesis of two complementary domains of pharmacological knowledge: (a) drug *processing* (*páozhì* 炮製) in the tradition of Léi Xiào 雷敩's lost *Léi Gōng páozhì lùn* 雷公炮炙論 of the Liú-Sòng (5th c.), and (b) the *Yìshuǐ-Dōngyuán* pharmacological theory of drug *properties* (*yàoxìng* 藥性). The work is in 6 *juǎn* and organises drugs by category, presenting for each substance: (i) Lǐ's brief discussion of its qì-wèi and channel-entry (歸經), (ii) the traditional Léi-Gōng processing method, (iii) Lǐ's annotations on clinical use.

The "Léi Gōng" of the title refers to the legendary Yellow-Emperor-era *Léi Gōng* 雷公 (the *Sùwèn*'s interlocutor with Huángdì), conventionally taken as the inventor of drug processing. The actual textual ancestor is the *Léi Gōng páozhì lùn* (雷公炮炙論) attributed to Léi Xiào of the Liú-Sòng — a 5th-century *páozhì* manual now surviving only in fragments preserved in the *Zhènglèi běncǎo* tradition. Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's *Yàoxìng jiě* synthesises this *páozhì* legacy with the *Yìshuǐ-Dōngyuán* doctrinal pharmacology of Lǐ Gǎo, Wáng Hàogǔ, and Lǐ's own teacher Yáng Yīxù 楊一隅.

### Prefaces

The 華夏文獻 transmitted text preserves Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's own preface, which dates the work's composition probably to the 1620s–1640s. He explains that *páozhì* (processing — including alcohol-roasting, vinegar-roasting, salt-roasting, honey-roasting, mineral-stewing, etc.) is essential to drug efficacy and that pharmacology without processing is incomplete; he therefore unites the two traditions.

### Abstract

For Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's biographical background see [[KR3ec027]]. The dating range in frontmatter (1622–1655) covers the plausible composition window from Lǐ's early career through to his death.

The work is significant as one of the principal late-Míng systematisations of the *páozhì* tradition, and as a clinical pharmacology paired with practical processing guidance. It served as a reference standard for Húīzhōu and Jiāngnán pharmacies through the late-Míng and Qīng. The 雷敩 attribution in title is conventional — the actual *páozhì* doctrines were updated by Lǐ to match Míng practice.
