# Shíwù Běncǎo Huì Zuǎn 食物本草會纂
*Compiled Materia Medica of Foodstuffs*
by [[沈李龍]] (Shěn Lǐlóng, *zì* Lóngyù 龍御, *hào* Yīshān shānrén 易山山人, *fl.* late 17th c., 清)

## About the work

The *Shíwù běncǎo huì zuǎn* is the principal Qīng-dynasty dietetic pharmacopoeia. Shěn Lǐlóng's 12-*juǎn* work was compiled in Kāngxī 28 (1689) and printed in Kāngxī 30 (1691). It builds directly on the late-Míng *Shíwù běncǎo* ([[KR3ec034]]) attributed to Xuē Jǐ, expanding and updating its content with Qīng botanical and ethnographic information. The work catalogues over 1,100 food substances organised by category (水部, 火部, 穀部, 菜部, 果部, 禽部, 獸部, 魚部, 味部 etc.) with each entry providing *xìngwèi*, primary effects, contraindications, and processing methods. Many entries include illustrations (woodblock, mid-quality).

### Prefaces

The local repository contains only a header file. The standard editions preserve Shěn's own preface (date Kāngxī 28 = 1689) and a preface by the well-known *yīzōng* publisher Wāng Áng [[汪昂]].

### Abstract

Shěn Lǐlóng (沈李龍), *zì* Lóngyù 龍御, *hào* Yīshān shānrén 易山山人, native of Sūzhōu 蘇州 (Jiāngsū), *fl.* late 17th c. (the Kāngxī era). He was a private scholar-physician embedded in the Sūzhōu commercial-publishing networks of the Yánbó (耳目) press tradition. The work was widely reprinted through the Qīng and into the early Mínguó.

The *Huì zuǎn* is significant as the principal Qīng dietetic reference and as a witness to the early-Qīng standardisation of food-as-medicine pharmacology. It is referenced widely in late-Qīng household manuals and in the modern *zhōngyī* dietetic literature.
