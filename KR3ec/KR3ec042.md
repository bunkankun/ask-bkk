# Běncǎo Xīn Biān 本草新編
*A New Compilation of Materia Medica*
by [[陳士鐸]] (Chén Shìduó, *zì* Jìngzhī 敬之, *hào* Yuǎngōng 遠公 / Zhū Huā zǐ 朱華子, *c.* 1627 – *c.* 1707, 清)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo xīn biān* is a distinctively idiosyncratic late-17th-century pharmacology by the prolific Sūzhōu / Shānyīn physician Chén Shìduó. Chén's medical career was unconventional: he claimed that his medical works were dictated to him by the immortals (most famously Lǚ Dòngbīn 呂洞賓 and Zhāng Zhòngjǐng 張仲景), a self-presentation that linked his medicine to *xiān-shù* (immortality arts) and located it in the late-Míng / early-Qīng Daoist medical-occult tradition. The *Běncǎo xīn biān* in 5 *juǎn* gives substance entries with extensive clinical comments grounded in Chén's distinctive theoretical doctrine — the *Qíxí* 棄習 system — which emphasises the rejection of conventional pharmacology in favour of "true" drug action revealed through immortal teaching.

### Prefaces

The local repository preserves the substance body. The preface and clinical narrative material in the standard editions includes Chén's self-presentation of the immortals' transmission.

### Abstract

Chén Shìduó (陳士鐸, *c.* 1627 – *c.* 1707, CBDB 572125), native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). He was a private scholar-physician with an unusual *xiān-shù* / shamanic self-presentation. His prolific output (allegedly comprising over 100 *juǎn*) includes:

1. *Biàn zhèng lù* 辨證錄 (1687) — the principal clinical-pattern manual;
2. *Shí shì mì lù* 石室秘錄 (1687);
3. *Dòng tiān ào zhǐ* 洞天奧旨 (1694) — on external medicine;
4. *Běncǎo xīn biān* 本草新編 (this work);
5. *Mài jué chàn wù* 脈訣闡微 — on pulse.

His works circulated widely in late-17th-century Jiāngnán and were influential in popular medical practice. Modern scholarship is divided on the question of authentic versus pseudepigraphic attribution within Chén's corpus; some works ascribed to him may be by his disciples writing in his name.
