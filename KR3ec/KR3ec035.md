# Běncǎo Xiángjié 本草詳節
*Detailed Notes on the Materia Medica*
attributed to [[薛己]] (Xuē Jǐ, here under his *hào* Lìzhāi 立齋, the catalog form reading "薛立齋", 1487–1559, 明)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo xiángjié* is one of the mid-Míng *Xuē-school* pharmacologies, a 4-*juǎn* digest of the *Gāngmù*-tradition substance corpus organised for clinical reference. The catalog meta gives the author as "薛立齋" — Xuē Jǐ's *hào* form — and dates the work to the Míng. The text is a relatively concise reference, providing for each substance the qì-wèi, *guī-jīng*, and main effects in summary form, without the lengthy commentary characteristic of Miù Xīyōng [[繆希雍]]'s *Běncǎo jīng shū* ([[KR3ec019]]) or Ní Zhūmó's *Huì yán* ([[KR3ec033]]).

The work is part of the broader *Xuē-school* publishing programme that includes *Shíwù běncǎo* ([[KR3ec034]]) and *Běncǎo yuē yán* ([[KR3ec036]]). The relation between these three is debated: they may be three separate Xuē family productions, or they may represent three editions / formats of an underlying single Xuē-school pharmacological digest, marketed under different titles by different publishers.

### Prefaces

The local repository preserves only the basic header (the directory has 12 small files but principal authorial paratext is not extracted in the local copy). Major Míng editions of the *Xiángjié* preserve a brief publisher's preface noting Xuē's authorship and the work's intended use as a "abbreviated reference" (*xiáng-jié* 詳節 — "detailed yet condensed").

### Abstract

Xuē Jǐ (薛己, 1487–1559) — see his existing person note. The dating range reflects his lifetime. The work's significance is mostly as a documentary witness to the *Xuē-school* medical publishing operation, one of the major Jiāngnán medical publishing networks of the mid-Míng.
