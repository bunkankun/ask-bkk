# Sòngcháo Shìshí 宋朝事實
*Facts about the Song*
by [[李攸]]

## About the work

A 20-*juàn* thematic miscellany (*leishu*-type compilation) covering the institutions, events, and anecdotes of the Northern Song dynasty, compiled by 李攸 Lǐ Yōu (fl. 1111–1134; CBDB id 33328). The work is organized thematically, drawing on court documents, memorials, and records not always found in the official histories. Wilkinson (*Chinese History: A New Manual*, §51190) describes it as "a *huìyào*-like collection of excerpts, some of which cannot be found elsewhere," making it a valuable supplementary source for Northern Song institutional history.

### Abstract

李攸 Lǐ Yōu (fl. 1111–1134; CBDB id 33328) compiled the *Sòngcháo Shìshí* during the Southern Song, drawing on archival materials from the Northern Song period. The source file confirms the work's 20-*juàn* structure, beginning with the Song imperial genealogy tracing the dynasty back to "Xīzǔ" 僖祖 (the dynastic ancestor several generations before the founder), consistent with the format of Song institutional histories.

The work falls between a *huìyào* 會要 (institutional compendium) and a *bǐjì* in character: it excerpts official documents, proclamations, and anecdotes, but without the systematic administrative-category organization of formal *huìyào*. Its most significant contribution, as Wilkinson notes, is preserving material "not found elsewhere" — that is, Northern Song records that were not incorporated into the *Sòng huìyào jígǎo* 宋會要輯稿 or the *Sòngshǐ* 宋史. For this reason it functions as an important supplementary source for Northern Song political and administrative history.
