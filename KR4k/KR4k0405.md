# Xiàozǐ Zhuàn 孝子傳 (anonymous II)
*Biographies of Filial Sons* (second anonymous collection)
anonymous

## About the work

*Xiàozǐ Zhuàn* 孝子傳 is a jíyìběn reconstruction of a second anonymous collection of filial son biographies, distinct from the smaller anonymous compilation at [[KR4k0397]]. At over 1100 lines, it is one of the more substantial *xiàozǐ zhuàn* reconstructions in this division, comparable in scale to the named collections of [[蕭廣濟]] ([[KR4k0396]]) and [[宋躬]] ([[KR4k0402]]).

### Abstract

This larger anonymous collection may represent fragments that circulated unattached to any specific author in the reference works that preserved them, or it may be the remnant of a named collection whose attribution was lost. The tales span the full range of filial piety tropes: miraculous responses to sincere devotion, extreme bodily sacrifice, recognition by the state, and the supernatural rewards that accrued to the filially virtuous. The fragments were recovered principally from the *Tàipíng Yùlǎn* 太平御覽 and the *Yìwén Lèijù* 藝文類聚.
