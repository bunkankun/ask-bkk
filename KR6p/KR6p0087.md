# Xī-fāng fā-yuàn wén zhù 西方發願文註
*Annotated Commentary on the [Yúnqī Zhūhóng] Vow-Text for the Western [Pure Land]*
by [[實賢]] (Xǐng'ān Shíxián, 註)

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* commentary on [[袾宏]] **Yúnqī Zhūhóng**'s *Xī-fāng yuàn-wén* [[KR6p0064]] — Yúnqī's standard Pure Land vow-text — composed by the early-Qīng Pure Land master [[實賢]] **Xǐng'ān Shíxián** 省庵實賢 (1685–1734), the **Eleventh Patriarch** of the orthodox Chinese Pure Land lineage. The work supplies a sustained doctrinal exposition of Yúnqī's vow-text, expanding on Yúnqī's own commentary and integrating it with the Qīng-period Pure Land doctrinal apparatus.

### Abstract

Yúnqī's *Xī-fāng yuàn-wén* circulates with Yúnqī's own *shì* 釋 (commentary), supplied as part of the original composition (T1160 / KR6p0064). Xǐng'ān's *Zhù* is therefore a **second-order commentary**: an annotation on Yúnqī's already-annotated vow-text, supplying further doctrinal cross-references, scriptural source-citations, and pastoral applications that Yúnqī's first-order commentary had not addressed. The structural division (*kē* 科) follows Yúnqī's text phrase by phrase; the annotation density is high.

The work belongs to the broader **Qīng-period Pure Land commentary tradition** in which the foundational texts of the late-Míng Pure Land synthesis — Yúnqī's writings, [[傳燈]] Chuándēng's *Lùn*, [[智旭]] Ǒuyì's *Yào-jiě* — received successive layers of sub-commentary from the seventeenth- and eighteenth-century Pure Land masters. Xǐng'ān's contribution to this tradition is substantial: in addition to the present *Yuàn-wén zhù*, he produced a separate *Shēng wú-shēng lùn zhù* on Chuándēng's *Lùn*, and his *Dōng-hǎi ruò jiě* [[KR6p0097]] continued the tradition into Confucian-Buddhist comparative scholarship. Together these works establish Xǐng'ān as the central early-Qīng commentator on the late-Míng Pure Land canon.

The text is preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* 卍續藏 (X1161). The dating bracket adopted (1715–1734) covers Xǐng'ān's mature period at Fèng-shān / Méi-fēng-sì up to his death.
