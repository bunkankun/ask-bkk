# Jìng-tǔ bì qiú 淨土必求
*The Pure Land One Must Seek*
by [[古崑]] (Gǔkūn lǎorén, 集)

## About the work

A short single-*juǎn* Pure Land devotional anthology gathered (*jí* 集) by the late-Qīng Hangzhou-area monk **[[古崑]] Gǔkūn lǎorén** 古崑老人. The title is taken from the *Wú-liàng-shòu jīng* 無量壽經 (the *Larger Sukhāvatī-vyūha*) — specifically from the famous passage in which the Buddha tells Maitreya that even if the *sān-qiān dà-qiān shì-jiè* 三千大千世界 were filled with great fire, one *must* still pass through it to hear this scripture and accept it: hence "*bì qiú*" 必求, "one must seek [it]."

### Abstract

Gǔkūn's own preface (*zì-xù* 自敘) opens with this passage from the *Larger Sukhāvatī-vyūha* and declares his motivation: in light of the immeasurable benefit promised by the Buddha to those who hear and rejoice in this teaching even for a single moment of mindfulness (*nǎi zhì yī niàn* 乃至一念), Gǔkūn has — "regardless of derision and slander" — gathered this anthology for the benefit of those with karmic affinity (*yǒu yuán* 有緣). The anthology contains extracts from the *Jìng-tǔ sān-jīng* core corpus together with Pure Land patristic passages and devotional verse, organised pastorally rather than systematically. The register is more compact and more devotional than Gǔkūn's *Jìng-tǔ suí xué* [[KR6p0106]]; the work is conceived as a portable encouragement for the lay-Buddhist practitioner.

Preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* 卍續藏 as X1188. The dating bracket adopted (1860–1890) covers Gǔkūn's principal active period.
