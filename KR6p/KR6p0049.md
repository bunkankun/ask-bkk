# Lè-bāng yí-gǎo 樂邦遺稿
*Remaining Fragments [for the Anthology] of the Land of Bliss*
by [[宗曉]] (Shí-zhī Zōngxiǎo, 編)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* supplement to [[宗曉]] Zōngxiǎo's *Lè-bāng wén-lèi* [[KR6p0048]], gathering further Pure Land textual material that came to the compiler's attention after the completion of the parent anthology. The author's preface explicitly invokes the Confucian *shí-yí* 拾遺 ("gathering of remainders") tradition: "I have followed the Confucian textual scholars' principle of gathering remainders, and have set down whatever further fragments I have seen or heard which may be of benefit to those undertaking the Pure Land practice." The preface is dated *jiǎ-zǐ* 甲子 spring (1204), suggesting that the *Yí-gǎo* was begun immediately after the *Wén-lèi*'s completion and continued to expand until Zōngxiǎo's death in 1214.

### Abstract

The *Yí-gǎo* preserves the same thematic categorisation as the *Wén-lèi* — scriptural passages, prefaces, biographies, doctrinal essays, liturgical fragments — but covers material the compiler had missed in the original work. Some of the most valuable items are: brief Pure Land biographical fragments otherwise unattested in surviving sources, miscellaneous prefaces to lost or partially preserved Pure Land works, and a number of personal letters and devotional verses by Sòng Pure Land practitioners. Like the parent *Wén-lèi*, the *Yí-gǎo* is in places the *only* surviving witness to the texts it includes.

The Taishō text is collated against the Korean canon. Dating bracket: 1204 (the *Yí-gǎo* preface) to 1214 (Zōngxiǎo's death). The two anthologies together — *Wén-lèi* in five *juǎn* plus *Yí-gǎo* in two *juǎn* — constitute Zōngxiǎo's single greatest contribution to Pure Land textual scholarship.
