### Scope and scholarly tradition

KR6p collects the Chinese-language compositions of the Pure Land school (*jìngtǔ zōng* 淨土宗) — the body of doctrinal treatises, sūtra-commentaries, liturgical *zàn* / *chàn* / *yí* texts, devotional anthologies, biographies, and devotional poetry that grew up around the three principal Pure Land sūtras: the *Larger Sukhāvatī-vyūha* (*Wúliángshòu jīng* 無量壽經), the *Smaller Sukhāvatī-vyūha* (*Āmítuó jīng* 阿彌陀經), and the apocryphal *Contemplation Sūtra* (*Guān wúliángshòu jīng* 觀無量壽經). The 140 works gathered here are drawn from three regions of the Sino-Japanese canon: (i) the Taishō *Zhū-zōng bù* 諸宗部 (vol. 47, nos. 1957–1984) — the principal classical Pure Land treatises from Tánluán through Sòng-period Tiantai; (ii) the supplementary *Xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏 (vols. 1, 2, 22, 61, 62, 74) — the great bulk of Sòng–Qīng commentaries, ritual handbooks, and lay-Buddhist anthologies that the early-twentieth-century Japanese editors recovered from Chinese monastic libraries; and (iii) the Taishō *Gǔyì bù* 古逸部 (vol. 85, nos. 2826–2830), preserving Dunhuang-recovered Pure Land liturgies. The corresponding Taishō structural division — `div31.xml` 諸宗部 — is the formal canonical container, but the Kanripo arrangement extracts the Pure Land subset and supplements it heavily from the *Xùzàngjīng*.

Chronologically the division spans c. 530 CE (Tánluán 曇鸞's foundational tracts) through the very end of the Qīng (c. 1900). The internal centre of gravity is unusually late: roughly two-thirds of the entries date from the Míng-Qīng period, reflecting the fact that Pure Land devotion became the dominant lay-monastic practice of late imperial China and produced an immense vernacular literature absent from earlier canonical strata. Three transmissional issues run through the division: the inflation of the *Wúliángshòu* sūtra-corpus through apocryphal "translations" preserved only in the *Xùzàngjīng* (KR6p0031–0035); the Tiantai monopoly on the *Guān-jīng* commentarial tradition via the 觀經疏妙宗鈔 chain initiated by Zhīlǐ 知禮; and the steady migration of Pure Land authorship from the cloister to the lay literati class, culminating in the Péng Shàoshēng circle of the eighteenth century and Wèi Yuán's *Huìyì* recension of 1854.

### Important texts and text clusters

- **Wúliángshòu jīng 無量壽經 (Larger Sukhāvatī-vyūha) corpus** — *Wúliángshòu jīng huìyì* 無量壽經會譯 [[KR6p0001]] (Wèi Yuán 魏源, 1854), *Wúliángshòu jīng jì* 無量壽經記 [[KR6p0002]] (Xuányī 玄一, Táng), *Wúliángshòu jīng qǐxìn lùn* 無量壽經起信論 [[KR6p0003]] (Péng Jìqīng 彭際清, late 18th c.), and Wáng Gēngxīn 王耕心's *Móhē Āmítuó jīng zhōnglùn* 摩訶阿彌陀經衷論 [[KR6p0004]]; together they constitute the most sustained East-Asian commentarial engagement with the *larger* Pure Land sūtra.
- **Guān wúliángshòu jīng 觀無量壽經 (Contemplation Sūtra) commentarial chain** — the apex is Zhìyǐ 智顗 (attr.) and Zhīlǐ 知禮's *Guān wúliángshòu fó jīng shū miàozōng chāo* 觀無量壽佛經疏妙宗鈔 [[KR6p0007]] (preface 1021), the canonical Tiantai reading of the sūtra, around which a dense Sòng-period sub-cluster forms — Zhīlǐ's own *Róngxīn jiě* 融心解 [[KR6p0008]], Jièdù 戒度's *Fúxīn lùn* 扶新論 [[KR6p0009]] and *Zhèngguān jì* 正觀記 [[KR6p0010]], Chuándēng 傳燈's *Túsòng* 圖頌 [[KR6p0011]], Xùfǎ 續法's *Zhízhǐ shū* 直指疏 [[KR6p0012]], and Péng Jìqīng's *Yuē lùn* 約論 [[KR6p0013]]; the *Kē* 妙宗鈔科 [[KR6p0006]] supplies the structural outline. Earlier strata are Fǎcōng 法聰's *Jì* 釋觀無量壽佛經記 [[KR6p0005]] (Táng).
- **Āmítuó jīng 阿彌陀經 (Smaller Sukhāvatī-vyūha) commentaries** — Yuánzhào 元照 and Jièdù 戒度's *Yìshū wénchí jì* 阿彌陀經義疏聞持記 [[KR6p0015]] (Sòng); Xìngchéng 性澄's Yuán-period *Jùjiě* 句解 [[KR6p0016]]; Dàyòu 大佑's *Lüèjiě* 略解 [[KR6p0017]] and its sub-commentary by Chuándēng [[KR6p0018]]; the magnum opus of the division, Yúnqī Zhūhóng 雲棲袾宏's *Āmítuó jīng shūchāo* 阿彌陀經疏鈔 [[KR6p0019]] (1584) with its companion *Shìyì* 事義 [[KR6p0020]] and *Wènbiàn* 問辯 [[KR6p0021]] and the great sub-commentary *Yǎnyì* 演義 [[KR6p0022]] by Gǔdé fǎshī 古德法師; the matching Tiantai reading is Zhìxù 智旭's *Yàojiě* 要解 (preserved here only with Dámò 達默's *Biànméng chāo* 便蒙鈔 [[KR6p0025]]); plus the *Āmítuó jīng yìběn* 阿彌陀經異本 [[KR6p0014]] (a variant text), Xùfǎ's *Lüèzhù* 略註 [[KR6p0026]], and a handful of Qīng glosses ([[KR6p0027]]–[[KR6p0030]]).
- **Pure-Land apocrypha and pseudepigraphic dhāraṇī** — *Fó shuō wúliángshòu fó mínghào lìyì dàshì yīnyuán jīng* 佛說無量壽佛名號利益大事因緣經 attributed to Kāng Sēngkǎi 康僧鎧 [[KR6p0031]], *Guānshìyīn púsà wǎngshēng jìngtǔ běnyuán jīng* 觀世音菩薩往生淨土本緣經 [[KR6p0032]], *Niànfó chāotuō lúnhuí jiéjìng jīng* 念佛超脫輪迴捷徑經 [[KR6p0033]], *Shí wǎngshēng Āmítuó fó guó jīng* 十往生阿彌陀佛國經 [[KR6p0034]], and *Fó shuō Āmítuó fó gēnběn mìmì shénzhòu jīng* 佛說阿彌陀佛根本祕密神咒經 attributed to Bodhiruci 菩提流支 [[KR6p0035]]. These are almost certainly Táng-Sòng Chinese compositions, transmitted only in the *Xùzàngjīng* supplement, and they are the principal evidence base for studying the textual inflation of the Pure Land sūtra-corpus in medieval China.
- **Tánluán–Dàochuò–Shàndǎo doctrinal foundation** — *Lüèlùn ānlè jìngtǔ yì* 略論安樂淨土義 [[KR6p0036]] and *Zàn Āmítuó fó jié* 讚阿彌陀佛偈 [[KR6p0073]] of Tánluán 曇鸞 (c. 530–542); *Ānlè jí* 安樂集 [[KR6p0037]] of Dàochuò 道綽 (c. 620–645); Shàndǎo 善導's *Guānniàn fǎmén* 觀念阿彌陀佛相海三昧功德法門 [[KR6p0038]] together with his liturgical trilogy *Fǎshì zàn* 法事讚 [[KR6p0074]], *Wǎngshēng lǐzàn jié* 往生禮讚偈 [[KR6p0075]], and *Bānzhōu sānmèi xíngdào wǎngshēng zàn* 般舟三昧行道往生讚 [[KR6p0076]]. These six works constitute the Indian-Chinese doctrinal patrimony that all later Pure Land literature retransmits.
- **Sui-Táng monastic theology** — *Jìngtǔ shíyí lùn* 淨土十疑論 [[KR6p0040]] and *Wǔ fāngbiàn niànfó mén* 五方便念佛門 [[KR6p0041]] attributed to Zhìyǐ 智顗; Huáigǎn 懷感's *Shì jìngtǔ qúnyí lùn* 釋淨土群疑論 [[KR6p0039]] (Táng), the most substantial late-seventh-century systematic defence of Pure Land practice against Yogācāra critique; Jiācái 迦才's *Jìngtǔ lùn* 淨土論 [[KR6p0042]]; Kuījī 窺基's *Xīfāng yàojué shìyí tōngguī* 西方要決釋疑通規 [[KR6p0043]]; Wǒnhyo 元曉's Korean-Silla *Yóuxīn ānlè dào* 遊心安樂道 [[KR6p0044]]; Dàojìng 道鏡 and Shàndào's *Niànfó jìng* 念佛鏡 [[KR6p0045]]; and Fēixī 飛錫's *Niànfó sānmèi bǎowáng lùn* 念佛三昧寶王論 [[KR6p0046]].
- **Sòng-Tiantai systematization** — *Wǎngshēng jìngtǔ juéyí xíngyuàn èrmén* 往生淨土決疑行願二門 [[KR6p0047]] and *Wǎngshēng jìngtǔ chànyuàn yí* 往生淨土懺願儀 [[KR6p0079]] of Cíyún Zūnshì 慈雲遵式 (1000–1032); Zōngxiǎo 宗曉's two great anthologies *Lèbāng wénlèi* 樂邦文類 [[KR6p0048]] and *Lèbāng yígǎo* 樂邦遺稿 [[KR6p0049]] (1200–1214), the principal Sòng-period repositories of Pure Land prefaces, epigraphy, and hagiography; Chéngyù 澄彧's *Zhù shíyí lùn* 注十疑論 [[KR6p0051]]; and Wáng Rìxiū 王日休's vastly influential lay manual *Lóngshū zēngguǎng jìngtǔ wén* 龍舒增廣淨土文 [[KR6p0050]] (c. 1160–1173).
- **Yuan-period synthesis** — Huáizé 懷則's *Jìngtǔ jìngguān yàomén* 淨土境觀要門 [[KR6p0052]]; Tiānrú Wéizé 天如惟則's *Jìngtǔ huòwèn* 淨土或問 [[KR6p0053]] (c. 1350), the model dialectical defence of Pure Land against Chán objections; Pǔdù 普度's *Lúshān liánzōng bǎojiàn* 廬山蓮宗寶鑑 [[KR6p0054]] (c. 1300), the canonical history of the "Lotus society" lineage; the Yánshòu-attributed *Sānshí xìniàn* 三時繫念佛事 [[KR6p0081]] and *Yífàn* 儀範 [[KR6p0082]]; and Wáng Zǐchéng 王子成's monumental *Lǐniàn Mítuó dàochǎng chànfǎ* 禮念彌陀道場懺法 [[KR6p0084]].
- **Late-Míng lay-monastic revival cluster** — Yúnqī Zhūhóng 雲棲袾宏's prolific oeuvre, including the *Shūchāo* (above), *Jìngtǔ yíbiàn* 淨土疑辨 [[KR6p0058]], *Dá sìshíbā wèn* 答四十八問 [[KR6p0063]], *Xīfāng yuànwén* 西方願文 [[KR6p0064]], and the encyclopaedic *Jìngtǔ zīliáng quánjí* 淨土資糧全集 [[KR6p0065]]; Chuándēng 傳燈's Tiantai-school *Jìngtǔ shēng-wúshēng lùn* 淨土生無生論 [[KR6p0056]] with its commentaries [[KR6p0070]]/[[KR6p0071]]; Yuán Hóngdào 袁宏道's Gōng'ān-school *Xīfāng hé lùn* 西方合論 [[KR6p0057]] (1599) and the Míng-Qīng commentary on it [[KR6p0068]]; Lǐ Zhì 李贄's *Jìngtǔ jué* 淨土決 [[KR6p0062]]; the *Jìngtǔ zhǐguī jí* 淨土指歸集 of Dàyòu 大佑 [[KR6p0059]]; Dàoyǎn 道衍's *Jìngtǔ jiǎnyào lù* 淨土簡要錄 [[KR6p0060]]; Zōngběn 宗本's *Guīyuán zhízhǐ jí* 歸元直指集 [[KR6p0061]]; and the apex anthology of late-Míng / early-Qīng Pure Land, Chéngshí 成時's *Jìngtǔ shíyào* 淨土十要 [[KR6p0067]] (1655–1678).
- **Qīng lay-Buddhist Pure Land movement** — built around Péng Shàoshēng / Péng Jìqīng 彭紹升 / 彭際清 (1740–1796), who is here represented by no fewer than nine works: doctrinal treatises *Wúliángshòu jīng qǐxìn lùn* [[KR6p0003]], *Guān-jīng yuē lùn* [[KR6p0013]], *Āmítuó jīng yuē lùn* [[KR6p0028]]; anthologies *Chóngdìng xīfāng gōngjù* 重訂西方公據 [[KR6p0099]] and *Niànfó jǐngcè* 念佛警策 [[KR6p0100]]; the *Shěng'ān fǎshī yǔlù* 省菴法師語錄 [[KR6p0098]]; and the poetic collections *Èrlín chànghé shī* 二林唱和詩 [[KR6p0128]], *Guānhé jí jiéchāo* 觀河集節鈔 [[KR6p0129]], *Cèhǎi jí jiéchāo* 測海集節鈔 [[KR6p0130]]. Around Péng cluster Shíxián 實賢 (Xǐng'ān 省菴), Xíngcè 行策 (*Jìngtǔ jǐngyǔ* [[KR6p0093]], *Qǐyī xīn jīngjìn niànfó qīqī guīshì* [[KR6p0094]]), Chèwù 徹悟 (*Chèwù chánshī yǔlù* [[KR6p0101]]), Zhōu Mèngyán 周夢顏's *Xīguī zhízhǐ* 西歸直指 [[KR6p0092]], Sōng Shàndēng 沈善登's *Bàoēn lùn* 報恩論 [[KR6p0124]], and Wèi Yuán's *Huìyì*. The late-Qīng strand of Hangzhou-area devotional anthologies by Gǔkūn 古崑 ([[KR6p0085]], [[KR6p0106]], [[KR6p0107]], [[KR6p0116]], [[KR6p0117]]) forms the closing wave of the division.
- **Liturgical / praise (zàn 讚 / chàn 懺 / yí 儀) corpus** — beyond Shàndǎo's three liturgies and Zūnshì's *Chànyuàn yí* above: Tánluán's *Zàn Āmítuó fó jié* [[KR6p0073]]; the Nāgārjuna *Shíèr lǐ* 十二禮 (tr. Jñānagupta 禪那崛多) [[KR6p0080]]; Zhìshēng 智昇's *Jí zhū jīng lǐchàn yí* 集諸經禮懺儀 [[KR6p0077]]; Fǎzhào 法照's *Jìngtǔ wǔhuì niànfó lüè fǎshì yízàn* 淨土五會念佛略法事儀讚 [[KR6p0078]] and *Wǔhuì niànfó sòngjīng guānxíng yí (juǎn zhōng, xià)* [[KR6p0136]]; Yuánzhào and Jièdù's *Wúliángshòu fó zàn zhù* 無量壽佛讚註 [[KR6p0083]]; and the Dunhuang stratum [[KR6p0137]]–[[KR6p0140]].
- **Dunhuang stratum (Taishō vol. 85)** — Huìrì 慧日's *Lüè zhū jīnglùn niànfó fǎmén wǎngshēng jìngtǔ jí (juǎn shàng)* 略諸經論念佛法門往生淨土集 [[KR6p0135]], the only surviving juǎn of Huìrì's three-juǎn defence of "*niànfó* by sūtra and treatise" composed before 748; Fǎzhào's *Wǔhuì* middle and lower fascicles [[KR6p0136]] (the upper juǎn survives independently as KR6p0078); and the anonymous *Dàshèng jìngtǔ zàn* 大乘淨土讚 [[KR6p0137]], *Chízhāi niànfó chànhuǐ lǐwén* 持齋念佛懺悔禮文 [[KR6p0138]], *Dào'ān fǎshī niànfó zàn* 道安法師念佛讚 [[KR6p0139]] / *zànwén* 道安法師念佛讚文 [[KR6p0140]] — the principal Northwest-Chinese witnesses to Pure-Land devotional practice independent of the central-Táng exegetical traditions.
- **Devotional poetry (jìngtǔ shī 淨土詩) cluster** — Guǎngguì 廣貴's *Liánbāng shīxuǎn* 蓮邦詩選 [[KR6p0072]] (late Míng); Péng Shàoshēng's *Èrlín chànghé shī* and the *Jiéchāo* pair (above); Táo Shàn 陶善's *Qiónglóu yíngǎo jiéchāo* 瓊樓吟稿節鈔 [[KR6p0131]]; Děrǔn 德潤's *Pǔnéng Sōng chánshī jìngtǔ shī* 普能嵩禪師淨土詩 [[KR6p0133]]; and Kuānliàng 寬量's *Jìngtǔ jiùshēngchuán shī* 淨土救生船詩 [[KR6p0134]] — a distinctive Qīng vernacular genre giving the division much of its mid-late character.

### Important persons

- [[曇鸞]] (Tánluán, c. 476–542) — the earliest Chinese Pure Land author preserved in the canon; the *Lüèlùn ānlè jìngtǔ yì* [[KR6p0036]] and the *Zàn Āmítuó fó jié* [[KR6p0073]] are the foundational texts of the *zìlì / tālì* (self-power / other-power) distinction.
- [[道綽]] (Dàochuò, 562–645) — second of the "three masters of Xuánzhōng-sì 玄中寺"; the *Ānlè jí* [[KR6p0037]] is the principal Sui-Táng systematization of *niànfó* doctrine.
- [[善導]] (Shàndǎo, 613–681) — the central figure of classical Pure Land; four works in the division ([[KR6p0038]], [[KR6p0074]]–[[KR6p0076]]) establishing both doctrinal and liturgical practice.
- [[智顗]] (Tiāntái Zhìyǐ, 538–597) — Sui Tiantai patriarch; *Jìngtǔ shíyí lùn* [[KR6p0040]] and *Wǔ fāngbiàn niànfó mén* [[KR6p0041]] traditionally attributed to him; the actual composition almost certainly Sòng-period under his name.
- [[懷感]] (Huáigǎn, fl. 690–705) — Xuánzàng's disciple turned Pure-Land apologist; *Shì jìngtǔ qúnyí lùn* [[KR6p0039]] is the most rigorous early scholastic defence of Pure-Land practice within Yogācāra doctrine.
- [[迦才]] (Jiācái, fl. 645–670) — author of the early-Táng *Jìngtǔ lùn* [[KR6p0042]], an alternative scholastic systematization parallel to Dàochuò.
- [[飛錫]] (Fēixī, fl. 742–779) — late-Táng author of *Niànfó sānmèi bǎowáng lùn* [[KR6p0046]], a key statement of the universal-scope reading of *niànfó*.
- [[元曉]] (Wǒnhyo, 617–686) — Silla scholar; the *Yóuxīn ānlè dào* [[KR6p0044]] is the Korean voice in the Táng Pure Land canon.
- [[慧日]] (Címǐn Huìrì, 680–748) — author of the Dunhuang *Lüè zhū jīnglùn niànfó fǎmén* [[KR6p0135]]; an opponent of Chán quietism who insisted on textual-scholastic foundations for Pure Land.
- [[法照]] (Fǎzhào, c. 745–805) — Wǔtáishān-based liturgist; creator of the *wǔhuì niànfó* 五會念佛 chanting system represented by KR6p0078 and KR6p0136.
- [[知禮]] (Sìmíng Zhīlǐ, 960–1028) — the architect of Sòng-Tiantai Pure-Land thought; the *Miàozōng chāo* [[KR6p0007]], *Róngxīn jiě* [[KR6p0008]], and *Kē* [[KR6p0006]] together define the Tiantai reading of the *Guān-jīng* down to the present.
- [[遵式]] (Cíyún Zūnshì, 964–1032) — Zhīlǐ's contemporary; the *Juéyí xíngyuàn èrmén* [[KR6p0047]] and *Chànyuàn yí* [[KR6p0079]] codified Pure Land ritual for the Hángzhōu-area Tiantai network.
- [[元照]] (Língzhī Yuánzhào, 1048–1116) — Vinaya master who turned to Pure Land; *Āmítuó jīng yìshū* and *Wúliángshòu fó zàn zhù* together with the Jièdù sub-commentaries form a distinct Sòng-Vinaya reading of Pure Land texts.
- [[戒度]] (Jièdù, fl. 1180–1240) — Yuánzhào's principal commentator; three works in the division ([[KR6p0009]], [[KR6p0010]], [[KR6p0015]]).
- [[宗曉]] (Zōngxiǎo, 1151–1214) — Sòng-period anthologist whose *Lèbāng wénlèi* / *Lèbāng yígǎo* preserve the bulk of the Sòng prefatory and hagiographic corpus.
- [[王日休]] (Wáng Rìxiū, d. 1173) — Sòng lay literatus; the *Lóngshū jìngtǔ wén* [[KR6p0050]] is the most widely circulated lay Pure-Land manual in pre-modern China.
- [[懷則]] (Huáizé, fl. 1290–1340) and [[天如則]] (Tiānrú Wéizé, c. 1286–1354) — Yuán Tiantai authors of, respectively, the *Jìngguān yàomén* and the *Jìngtǔ huòwèn*.
- [[普度]] (Yōután Pǔdù, d. 1330) — Yuán reformer of the White Lotus society; the *Lúshān liánzōng bǎojiàn* [[KR6p0054]] is the canonical lineage-history of the "Lotus society" tradition.
- [[袾宏]] (Yúnqī Zhūhóng, 1535–1615) — late-Míng master at Yúnqī-sì 雲棲寺; the dominant authorial presence in the division (eight works including the *Āmítuó jīng shūchāo*) and the architect of late-Míng Pure Land revival.
- [[傳燈]] (Yōuxī Chuándēng, 1554–1627) — Míng Tiantai exegete; commentator on both the *Guān-jīng* and the *Āmítuó jīng* tradition, author of the *Jìngtǔ shēng-wúshēng lùn*.
- [[大佑]] (Dàyòu, 1334–1407) — Yuán-Míng compiler bridging the two dynasties; three works in the division.
- [[袁宏道]] (Yuán Hóngdào, 1568–1610) — Gōng'ān-school literatus; *Xīfāng hé lùn* [[KR6p0057]] is the principal late-Míng literati statement of Pure Land doctrine.
- [[李贄]] (Lǐ Zhì, 1527–1602) — heterodox Míng literatus; *Jìngtǔ jué* [[KR6p0062]] places him in the Pure Land tradition.
- [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 1599–1655) — last of the "four great Míng masters"; his *Āmítuó jīng yàojiě* (transmitted here in [[KR6p0025]]) is the standard Tiantai reading of the smaller sūtra.
- [[成時]] (Chéngshí, 1618–1678) — Zhìxù's disciple; compiler of the *Jìngtǔ shíyào* [[KR6p0067]], the canonical late-Míng / early-Qīng Pure-Land anthology.
- [[彭際清]] (Péng Shàoshēng / Jìqīng, 1740–1796) — leading Qīng lay-Buddhist scholar; nine works represent his doctrinal, anthological, and poetic output and define eighteenth-century lay Pure Land literature.
- [[實賢]] (Xǐng'ān Shíxián, 1686–1734) — Qīng patriarch of the Pure Land lineage; subject of [[KR6p0098]] and annotator of [[KR6p0087]] and [[KR6p0097]].
- [[行策]] (Jiéliú Xíngcè, 1628–1682) — *Jìngtǔ jǐngyǔ* [[KR6p0093]] and *Niànfó qīqī guīshì* [[KR6p0094]]; founder of the *fó-qī* 佛七 seven-day intensive *niànfó* retreat as a standardized form.
- [[古崑]] (Gǔkūn, fl. 1860–1890) — late-Qīng Hangzhou-area anthologist; five works form the closing wave of the division.
- [[魏源]] (Wèi Yuán, 1794–1857) — late-Qīng statecraft scholar; the *Wúliángshòu jīng huìyì* [[KR6p0001]] is the first sustained Chinese attempt at a synthetic conflated recension of the larger sūtra.
- [[沈善登]] (Shěn Shàndēng, 1830–1902) — *Bàoēn lùn* [[KR6p0124]]; a late-Qīng intellectual systematization of Pure Land in dialogue with mid-nineteenth-century reformist Confucianism.

### Topics

- **The five Chinese versions of the *Wúliángshòu jīng* and the problem of a unified Larger Pure Land Sūtra** — running from the medieval translator-attributions through Yúnqī Zhūhóng's preference for the smaller sūtra to Wèi Yuán's nineteenth-century *huìyì* solution and Xià Liánjū's twentieth-century continuation; the principal philological problem of the division.
- **The status of the *Guān wúliángshòu jīng* as a Central-Asian / Chinese composition** — debate, beginning with Mochizuki Shinkō's mid-twentieth-century work and continuing into Fujita Kōtatsu and Stevenson's studies, over whether the sūtra is an Indian translation or a Sino-Central-Asian composition; the entire commentarial chain [[KR6p0005]]–[[KR6p0013]] presupposes it as canonical.
- **The Tiantai monopolization of *Guān-jīng* exegesis** — how Zhīlǐ's *Miàozōng chāo* established a doctrinal grid (*xìng-shàn xìng-è* 性善性惡, *yīniàn sānqiān* 一念三千) on top of the sūtra's contemplative scheme, and how this Tiantai reading survived as the only living *Guān-jīng* commentarial tradition through the Sòng, Yuán, and Míng periods.
- **The *niànfó sānmèi* debate** — whether *niànfó* is a propaedeutic ritual practice, a meditative *samādhi*, or an actualization of *zìxìng Mítuó wéixīn jìngtǔ* 自性彌陀唯心淨土 (the Amitābha-of-one's-own-nature, the Pure Land of mind-only); a debate that runs from Fēixī's *Bǎowáng lùn* [[KR6p0046]] through Yánshòu and Yuán Hóngdào to the late-Míng "*chán-jìng yī-zhì* 禪淨一致" (Chán–Pure-Land synthesis) of Yúnqī and Zhìxù.
- **Chán–Pure-Land synthesis (chán-jìng shuāngxiū 禪淨雙修)** — the Sòng-Míng-Qīng convergence of Chán practice and Pure Land devotion, most clearly thematized in Tiānrú Wéizé's *Huòwèn* [[KR6p0053]], Yuán Hóngdào's *Hé lùn* [[KR6p0057]], Jīhúng 濟能's *Jiǎohǔ jí* 角虎集 [[KR6p0096]], and Chèwù's *Yǔlù* [[KR6p0101]]; defined the dominant late-imperial monastic style.
- **Pure Land hagiography and the "Lotus society" lineage** — the construction of an unbroken lineage from Lúshān Huìyuǎn 廬山慧遠's 4th-c. society through the Táng masters to the present, codified by Pǔdù's *Liánzōng bǎojiàn* [[KR6p0054]] and Zōngxiǎo's anthologies, and disseminated as the model of Pure Land religious community.
- **The migration of authorship from cloister to lay literati** — most visible in the eighteenth-century Péng Shàoshēng circle (his nine works represent the apex), but already prepared in the Sòng with Wáng Rìxiū, in the Míng with Yuán Hóngdào and Lǐ Zhì, and continuing into the late-Qīng with Wèi Yuán, Shěn Shàndēng, and Wáng Gēngxīn.
- **Pure Land devotional poetry as canonical literature** — the unusual fact, distinctive to this division within the Kanripo Buddhist canon, that *jìngtǔ shī* 淨土詩 collections are treated as canonical religious texts and admitted to the *Xùzàngjīng*; a Qīng phenomenon documented in [[KR6p0072]], [[KR6p0128]]–[[KR6p0134]].
- **The *fó-qī* 佛七 (seven-day intensive *niànfó*) and Pure Land monastic regulation** — the standardization of ritual recitation regimes traceable from Zhìshēng's *Lǐchàn yí* [[KR6p0077]] and Fǎzhào's *Wǔhuì* [[KR6p0078]] through Xíngcè's *Qīqī guīshì* [[KR6p0094]] to nineteenth-century Hangzhou practice in Gǔkūn's manuals; the operational practice underlying the doctrinal literature.
- **The Dunhuang independent witness** — the *Gǔyì bù* texts [[KR6p0135]]–[[KR6p0140]] preserve a Pure-Land devotional tradition (Címǐn Huìrì, Fǎzhào, anonymous liturgies) at least partly independent of the Chang'an-Luòyáng centres, and their survival only at Dunhuang shows how much medieval Chinese Pure Land literature has been lost in central transmission.
