# Yúnqī jìng-tǔ huì-yǔ 雲棲淨土彙語
*Anthology of [Yúnqī Zhūhóng's] Sayings on the Pure Land*
by [[虞執西]] (Yú Zhí-xī, 錄) and [[嚴培西]] (Yán Péi-xī, 錄)

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* anthology of [[袾宏]] **Yúnqī Zhūhóng**'s Pure Land sayings (*jìng-tǔ yǔ* 淨土語) collated from across Yúnqī's prose corpus by his lay-Buddhist disciples [[虞執西]] **Yú Zhí-xī** and [[嚴培西]] **Yán Péi-xī** in the early seventeenth century, shortly after Yúnqī's death in 1615.

### Abstract

Yúnqī's Pure Land writings are scattered across many works — the *Ē-mí-tuó jīng shū-chāo* [[KR6p0028]], the *Jìng-tǔ yí-biàn* [[KR6p0058]], the *Dá sì-shí-bā wèn* [[KR6p0063]], the *Xī-fāng yuàn-wén* [[KR6p0064]], plus prefaces, letters, and miscellaneous prose pieces preserved in his collected works the *Yúnqī fǎ-huì* 雲棲法彙. The *Huì-yǔ* extracts and re-organises Yúnqī's most pointed and pastoral Pure Land sayings into a single accessible volume, organised thematically rather than by source-text: opening sections on the doctrinal foundations of Pure Land devotion; sections on practical *niàn-fó* practice; sections on the deathbed *zhì-niàn*; sections of pastoral counsel directed to lay-Buddhist householders and women; sections of polemical responses to Chán objections.

The work belongs to the **post-mortem consolidation of Yúnqī's intellectual legacy** carried out by his lay-Buddhist circle in the decades after his death. Together with the collected *Yúnqī fǎ-huì*, the *Wǎngshēng jí*, and the various individual Pure Land writings, the *Huì-yǔ* establishes Yúnqī as the **doctrinal centre of late-Míng Pure Land Buddhism** — a status that the subsequent Qīng-period Pure Land masters (Xǐng'ān Shíxián [[實賢]], Hóng-luó Dámò [[達默]], etc.) consistently reinforced.

The text is preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* 卍續藏 (X1170). No preface fixes a precise composition date; the bracket adopted (1620–1660) covers the early to mid seventeenth century during which the Yúnqī lay-Buddhist circle was consolidating his legacy.
