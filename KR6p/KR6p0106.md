# Jìngtǔ suí xué 淨土隨學
*Pure Land Studies, Followed Wherever One May Be*
by [[古崑]] (Gǔkūn lǎorén, 編)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* Pure Land devotional anthology compiled (*biān* 編) by the late-Qīng Hangzhou-area monk **[[古崑]] Gǔkūn lǎorén** 古崑老人 during the Tóngzhì 同治 / Guāngxù 光緒 reigns. The title — *suí xué* 隨學 ("studies pursued wherever one happens to be") — frames the work as a portable handbook for itinerant or busy practitioners, designed to support the practice of *niànfó* under any external circumstances.

### Abstract

The opening *tící* 題詞 (titular verse-encomium) summarises the editorial conception: *Pópó* 娑婆 (this Sahā-world) is a sea of suffering; the Buddha's *xīfāng* gate is the unique compassionate deliverance; the Lúshān tradition of [[慧遠]] **Huìyuǎn**'s *Báiliánshè* 白蓮社 is the source of the Chinese Pure Land lineage; the present *zhèngfǎ* decline (*língyí* 陵夷) has produced widespread confusion that mistakes *wéixīn jìngtǔ* 唯心淨土 ("Pure Land as mind-only") for an annulment of the Western *xīfāng tǔ* — and Gǔkūn's anthology is offered as a *fǎyào* 法藥 (dharma-medicine) directed precisely at that confusion. The title verse explicitly cross-references the *Jìngtǔ shénzhū* 淨土神珠 [[KR6p0117]] as Gǔkūn's prior compilation in the same series.

The body of the anthology comprises substantial extracts from the standard Pure Land patristic and pastoral literature, organised thematically rather than chronologically: doctrinal extracts from the *Jìngtǔ sānjīng* 淨土三經 corpus, Tiāntái Zhìyǐ 智顗's *Shíyí lùn*, the **Yúnqī Zhūhóng** [[袾宏]] / **Ǒuyì Zhìxù** late-Míng Pure Land tradition, the **Chèwù** [[徹悟]] / **Péng Jìqīng** [[彭際清]] late-Qīng synthesis, and devotional verse. The work is one of the four short Gǔkūn anthologies preserved in the *Xùzàngjīng* 卍續藏 — alongside *Jìngtǔ bìqiú* [[KR6p0107]] (X1188), *Liánzōng bìdú* [[KR6p0116]] (X1197), and *Jìngtǔ shénzhū* [[KR6p0117]] (X1198) — and shares with them the lay-pastoral, Hangzhou-regional register of late-Qīng popular Pure Land devotional scholarship.

The dating bracket adopted (1860–1890) covers Gǔkūn's principal active period.
