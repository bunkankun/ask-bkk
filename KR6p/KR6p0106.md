# Jìng-tǔ suí xué 淨土隨學
*Pure Land Studies, Followed Wherever One May Be*
by [[古崑]] (Gǔkūn lǎorén, 編)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* Pure Land devotional anthology compiled (*biān* 編) by the late-Qīng Hangzhou-area monk **[[古崑]] Gǔkūn lǎorén** 古崑老人 during the Tóngzhì 同治 / Guāngxù 光緒 reigns. The title — *suí xué* 隨學 ("studies pursued wherever one happens to be") — frames the work as a portable handbook for itinerant or busy practitioners, designed to support the practice of *niàn-fó* under any external circumstances.

### Abstract

The opening *tí-cí* 題詞 (titular verse-encomium) summarises the editorial conception: *Pó-pó* 娑婆 (this Sahā-world) is a sea of suffering; the Buddha's *xī-fāng* gate is the unique compassionate deliverance; the Lú-shān tradition of [[慧遠]] **Huì-yuǎn**'s *Bái-lián-shè* 白蓮社 is the source of the Chinese Pure Land lineage; the present *zhèng-fǎ* decline (*líng-yí* 陵夷) has produced widespread confusion that mistakes *wéi-xīn jìng-tǔ* 唯心淨土 ("Pure Land as mind-only") for an annulment of the Western *xī-fāng tǔ* — and Gǔkūn's anthology is offered as a *fǎ-yào* 法藥 (dharma-medicine) directed precisely at that confusion. The title verse explicitly cross-references the *Jìng-tǔ shén-zhū* 淨土神珠 [[KR6p0117]] as Gǔkūn's prior compilation in the same series.

The body of the anthology comprises substantial extracts from the standard Pure Land patristic and pastoral literature, organised thematically rather than chronologically: doctrinal extracts from the *Jìng-tǔ sān-jīng* 淨土三經 corpus, Tiāntái Zhì-yǐ 智顗's *Shí-yí lùn*, the **Yúnqī Zhū-hóng** [[袾宏]] / **Ǒu-yì Zhì-xù** late-Míng Pure Land tradition, the **Chè-wù** [[徹悟]] / **Péng Jì-qīng** [[彭際清]] late-Qīng synthesis, and devotional verse. The work is one of the four short Gǔkūn anthologies preserved in the *Xù-zàng-jīng* 卍續藏 — alongside *Jìng-tǔ bì-qiú* [[KR6p0107]] (X1188), *Lián-zōng bì-dú* [[KR6p0116]] (X1197), and *Jìng-tǔ shén-zhū* [[KR6p0117]] (X1198) — and shares with them the lay-pastoral, Hangzhou-regional register of late-Qīng popular Pure Land devotional scholarship.

The dating bracket adopted (1860–1890) covers Gǔkūn's principal active period.
