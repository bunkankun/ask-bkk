# Ē-mí-tuó jīng shū-chāo yǎn-yì 阿彌陀經疏鈔演義
*Explanatory Amplification of the* Shū-chāo *on the Smaller Amitābha-sūtra*
by [[古德法師]] (Gǔdé fǎshī, 演義) and [[慈航智願]] (Cíháng Zhìyuàn, 定本)

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* *yǎn-yì* 演義 ("explanatory amplification") of [[袾宏]] Yúnqī Zhūhóng's *Shū-chāo* [[KR6p0019]] by the late-Wànlì monk [[古德法師]] Gǔdé fǎshī of Yún-qī-sì, edited and finalised after his death by his disciple [[慈航智願]] Cíháng Zhìyuàn. The work is a sustained sub-commentary that proceeds through Zhūhóng's *Shū-chāo* paragraph by paragraph, expanding compressed argumentation, supplementing canonical citation, and addressing apparent ambiguities. It is the most extensive of the supplementary works on the *Shū-chāo* — the *Shì-yì* [[KR6p0020]] is a glossary, the *Wèn-biàn* [[KR6p0021]] is a defensive Q-and-A, but the *Yǎn-yì* is a full sub-commentary in the classical Chinese mode.

### Abstract

The *Yǎn-yì* preserves the doctrinal and pedagogical structure of the late-Wànlì Yún-qī-sì circle's reception of the *Shū-chāo* — the immediate post-Zhūhóng generation working out the implications of his synthetic Pure Land position. The text engages closely with [[傳燈]] Chuándēng's rival Tiāntái-school *Yuán-zhōng-chāo* [[KR6p0018]] and with Tiāntái critiques of Zhūhóng's *yī-xíng sì-mén* doctrine, defending the synthetic position with extensive doctrinal argument. The text adopts the standard format for the late-Míng sub-commentary: each main heading begins with a *biāo* 標 quoting the *Shū-chāo* lemma, then a *shì* 釋 (gloss), then a *zhèng* 證 (canonical attestation), then optional *biàn* 辯 (defence against possible objections).

The dating bracket (c. 1615–1640) is by inference: Zhūhóng died in 1615, and the *Yǎn-yì* presupposes both the *Shū-chāo* and the *Wèn-biàn* as canonical reference points. Cíháng Zhì-yuàn's editorial completion plausibly falls in the 1620s or 1630s; Wàn-lì → Tiānqǐ → Chóng-zhēn covers the relevant period.
