# Ē-mí-tuó jīng yuē-lùn 阿彌陀經約論
*Concise Treatise on the Smaller Amitābha-sūtra*
by [[彭際清]] (Péng Jì-qīng, 述)

## About the work

A short single-*juǎn* doctrinal essay by the late-Qīng lay-Buddhist [[彭際清]] Péng Jì-qīng (1740–1796) on the *Smaller Amitābha-sūtra*, completing his three-part Pure Land *yuē-lùn* trilogy alongside the *Wúliángshòu jīng qǐxìn lùn* [[KR6p0003]] and the *Guān-wúliángshòu fó jīng yuē-lùn* [[KR6p0013]]. The author's preface — signed *Pú-sà jiè dì-zǐ Péng Jì-qīng shù* 菩薩戒弟子彭際清述 — frames the work in four divisions: (1) the *yào-lǐng* 要領 ("essential points") of the sūtra, (2) the *wǎng-shēng lì-yì* 往生利益 ("benefits of rebirth"), (3) special Q-and-A (*biè shēn wèn-dá* 別申問答), and (4) closing dedication-verses (*jié-sòng* 偈) for the merit-transfer.

### Abstract

Péng's reading of the smaller sūtra is structured around five organising doctrinal pillars: *yī-xīn* 一心 (one-mind) as the doctrinal core, *chí-míng* 持名 (upholding the name) as the practice, *xìn-yuàn* 信願 (faith and aspiration) as the orienting condition, *bù tuì* 不退 (non-retrogression) as the soteriological signpost, and *ē-nòu-duō-luó sān-miǎo sān-pú-tí* 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提 (*anuttarā samyak-sambodhi*) as the ultimate goal. The text's distinctive doctrinal claim is the identification of the *Smaller Sukhāvatī-vyūma's* "one-pointed mind not distracted" (*yī-xīn bù luàn* 一心不亂) with the Huáyán doctrine of *yīxīn fǎ-jiè* 一心法界 — making the practice of *niàn-fó* a direct manifestation of the totalising Dharma-realm, and Pure Land devotion a route to *immediate* (rather than gradually progressed) realisation of bodhi.

The *Yuē-lùn* is more compact than Péng's earlier *Qǐxìn lùn* on the larger sūtra and shares the brevity of his *Yuē-lùn* on the *Guānjīng*. Together the three texts constitute the late-Qīng lay-Buddhist commentary apparatus for the three principal Pure Land sūtras, the precedent that [[魏源]] Wèi Yuán's *Jìngtǔ sì jīng* would build on a generation later. Dating bracket: Péng's mature period, c. 1781–1796.
