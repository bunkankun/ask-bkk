# Jiào sān dòng zhēn wén wǔ fǎ Zhèng yī méng wēi lù lì chéng yí 醮三洞真文五法正一盟威籙立成儀

*Established Liturgy for the Offering to the Three-Cavern Perfect-Scripts, Five Dharmas, and Zhèngyī Covenant-Authority Register*

by [[張萬福]] (Zhāng Wànfú) — Táng Daoist liturgiologist

A Táng Zhèng yī **ritual-liturgical compilation** for major offering rites, by the key early-8th-century liturgiologist Zhāng Wànfú. Preserved as **DZ 1212** / CT 1212 (正一部).

**Dating.** Early 8th century. Dynasty: 唐.
