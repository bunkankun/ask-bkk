# Liù shí jiǎ zǐ běn mìng yuán chén lì 六十甲子本命元辰曆

*Calendar of the Sexagenary Original-Destiny Original-Hours*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties

A Six-Dynasties Daoist **sexagenary-cycle horoscopic calendar** for determining a person's destiny-years. Preserved as **DZ 1289** / CT 1289 (正一部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 六朝.
