# Zhěn zhōng jīng 枕中經

*Scripture Kept under the Pillow*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng **secret-instructions scripture** (title evokes Sūn Sīmiǎo's *Zhěn zhōng fāng* 枕中方). Preserved as **DZ 1422** / CT 1422 (正一部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
