# Xù Dào zàng jīng mù lù 續道藏經目錄

*Catalogue of the Supplementary Dào zàng*

Anonymous; Míng

**The final text of the Dào zàng** — catalogue of the **Wàn lì xù Dào zàng** 萬曆續道藏 (Wànlì Supplementary Dào zàng) of 1607, the late-Míng supplement of 56 further texts added after the 1445 main canon. Preserved as **DZ 1431b** / CT 1431b (正一部).

**Dating.** 1607. Dynasty: 明.
