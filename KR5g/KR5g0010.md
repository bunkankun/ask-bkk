# Dào yào líng zhī shén guǐ pǐn jīng 道要靈衹神鬼品經

*Essential-Way Scripture on the Classes of Numinous Spirits, Gods, and Demons*

Anonymous; late Six Dynasties to Táng

A Daoist **systematic catalogue of the spirit-world**, classifying gods, numinous beings, and demons. Preserved as **DZ 1201** / CT 1201 (正一部).

**Dating.** c. 6th–7th century. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
