# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo kè zhōng fǎ 洞玄靈寶課中法

*Dòng-xuán Líng-bǎo Methods of Daily Offices*

Anonymous; early Táng

An early Táng **liturgical manual for daily Daoist offices** (*kè zhōng* 課中 — the "course of daily practice"). Preserved as **DZ 1246** / CT 1246 (正一部).

**Dating.** Early Táng. Dynasty: 唐初期.
