# Dào mén kē fàn dà quán jí 道門科範大全集

*Complete Collection of Ritual Rubrics of the Gate of the Way*

by [[杜光庭]] (Dù Guāngtíng, 850–933)

**A monumental Táng-Five-Dynasties ritual-rubric compendium** by **Dù Guāngtíng** — one of the most prolific Daoist liturgiologists of the period. Provides comprehensive ritual rubrics for Daoist services. Preserved as **DZ 1225** / CT 1225 (正一部) in 87 *juàn*.

**Dating.** Late Táng to early Five Dynasties. Dynasty: 唐-五代.
