# Dòng shén Bā dì yuán biàn jīng 洞神八帝元變經

*Dòngshén Scripture of the Original Transformations of the Eight Thearchs*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng scripture on the **Eight Thearchs** (*bā dì* 八帝) and their original transformations. Preserved as **DZ 1202** / CT 1202 (正一部).

**Dating.** 8th–9th century. Dynasty: 唐.
