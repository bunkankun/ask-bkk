# Shàng qīng míng jiàn yào jīng 上清明鑑要經

*Shàng-qīng Essential Scripture of the Luminous Mirror*

Anonymous; Táng

A Táng Shàng qīng scripture on the **luminous-mirror** (*míng jiàn* 明鑑) — a ritual implement used in exorcism and cosmic visualization. Preserved as **DZ 1206** / CT 1206 (正一部).

**Dating.** Táng. Dynasty: 唐.
