# Tài shàng Xuán tiān Zhēn wǔ wú shàng jiāng jūn lù 太上玄天真武無上將軍籙

*Most High Register of the Supreme General Zhēn-wǔ of the Dark Heaven*

Anonymous; Sòng-Míng

An ordination-register for officiating priests of the **Zhēn wǔ** 真武 cult. Preserved as **DZ 1213** / CT 1213 (正一部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Míng. Dynasty: 宋-明.
