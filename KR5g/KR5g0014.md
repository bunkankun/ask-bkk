# Sān tiān nèi jiě jīng 三天內解經

*Inner Explanation of the Three Heavens*

by [[徐氏]] (Xú shì) — Southern-Dynasties Zhèng yī author

**A crucial Southern-Dynasties Celestial-Masters historical-doctrinal text** — providing the Zhèng yī lineage's own self-understanding of the "Three Heavens" (*sān tiān* 三天) revelation, the history of Zhāng Dàolíng's covenant with Tài shàng Lǎo jūn, and the structure of the Daoist pantheon. A foundational primary source for early Celestial-Masters history. Preserved as **DZ 1205** / CT 1205 (正一部).

**Dating.** 5th century. Dynasty: 南朝.
