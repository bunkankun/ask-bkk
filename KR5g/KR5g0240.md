# Dào zàng quē jīng mù lù 道藏闕經目錄

*Catalogue of Scriptures Missing from the Dào zàng*

Anonymous; Míng

A **catalogue of scriptures known to have been in earlier versions of the Dào zàng but missing from the 1445 Zhèng tǒng compilation** — an indispensable index of what has been lost. Preserved as **DZ 1430** / CT 1430 (正一部).

**Dating.** 1444–1445. Dynasty: 明.
