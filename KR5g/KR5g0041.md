# Dào mén shí guī 道門十規

*Ten Regulations of the Gate of the Way*

by [[張宇初]] (Zhāng Yǔchū, 1359–1410) — 43rd Celestial Master

**The programmatic early-Míng regulations** issued by the 43rd Celestial Master for the reorganization of Daoist institutions under the new dynasty. Ten-point statement covering ordination, ritual, monastic discipline, Daoist-Confucian relations. Preserved as **DZ 1232** / CT 1232 (正一部).

**Dating.** c. 1400. Dynasty: 明.
