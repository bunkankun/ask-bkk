# Dà huì jìng cí miào lè tiān zūn shuō fú dé wǔ shèng jīng 大惠靜慈妙樂天尊說福德五聖經

*Scripture of the Five Saints of Blessings-and-Virtue as Expounded by the Great-Grace Still-Compassionate Wondrous-Joy Heaven-Worthy*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

The opening text of the **Zhèng yī bù** 正一部 (Orthodox Unity Section) — the largest of the four supplementary sections of the Zhèng tǒng Dào zàng, containing principally ritual, liturgical, and hagiographical materials of the Zhèng yī 正一 (Celestial-Masters) lineage and related traditions. This scripture expounds the **Five Saints of Blessings-and-Virtue** (*fú dé wǔ shèng* 福德五聖). Preserved as **DZ 1192** / CT 1192.

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
