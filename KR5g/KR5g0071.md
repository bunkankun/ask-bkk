# Yì lín 意林

*Forest of Meanings*

by [[馬總]] (Mǎ Zǒng, *d.* 823) — mid-Táng scholar-official

A mid-Táng **leishu-style anthology** of extracts from non-canonical philosophical works — a significant source for otherwise-lost Warring-States and Hàn writings. Catalogued here under KR5g but not preserved in the Dào zàng proper; the Kanseki catalog places the work in this division for classificatory reasons.

**Dating.** Late 8th / early 9th century. Dynasty: 唐.
