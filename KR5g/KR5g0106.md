# Dòng xuán Líng bǎo wǔ lǎo shè zhào Běi fēng guǐ mó chì shū yù jué 洞玄靈寶五老攝召北酆鬼魔赤書玉訣

*Dòngxuán Língbǎo Five-Elders' Red-Writ Jade-Instructions for Summoning the Demon-Ghosts of Northern Fēng*

Anonymous; Six Dynasties to Táng

A Líng bǎo **exorcistic talismanic scripture** summoning and controlling the demon-ghosts of the underworld (Běi fēng 北酆). Preserved as **DZ 1297** / CT 1297 (正一部).

**Dating.** Six Dynasties to Táng. Dynasty: 六朝-唐.
