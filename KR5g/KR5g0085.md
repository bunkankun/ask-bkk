# Pán tiān jīng 盤天經

*Plate-Heaven Scripture*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A Daoist **cosmological-divinatory scripture** on the heavenly plate (*pán tiān* 盤天 — perhaps the circumpolar sky). Preserved as **DZ 1276** / CT 1276 (正一部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
