# Tài shàng zhù guó jiù mín zǒng zhēn bì yào 太上助國救民總真祕要

*Most High Comprehensive-Perfected Secret Essentials for Aiding the State and Saving the People*

by [[元妙宗]] (Yuán Miàozōng)

A 10-*juàn* Northern-Sòng ritual manual for state-protection and people-saving rites, compiled under Huī-zōng's patronage. Preface 1116. Preserved as **DZ 1227** / CT 1227 (正一部).

**Dating.** 1116. Dynasty: 北宋.
