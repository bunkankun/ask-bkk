# Tài shàng Lǎo jūn qīng jìng xīn jīng 太上老君清靜心經

*Most High Lord Lǎo's Heart-Scripture of Purity and Stillness*

Anonymous; Táng-Sòng

A Daoist **heart-scripture** (*xīn jīng* 心經) — short meditation scripture in the tradition of the famous *Cháng qīng jìng jīng* ([[KR5c0252]]). Preserved as **DZ 1169** / CT 1169 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Táng-Sòng. Dynasty: 唐-宋.
