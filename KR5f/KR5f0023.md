# Yīn dān nèi piān 陰丹內篇

*Inner Chapter of the Yīn Elixir*

Anonymous; Sòng-Yuán

A short Daoist **inner-alchemical treatise** on the *yīn dān* 陰丹 — the female / passive aspect of the elixir work. Preserved as **DZ 1189** / CT 1189 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Sòng-Yuán. Dynasty: 宋-元.
