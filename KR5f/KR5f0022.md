# Tuó yuè zǐ 橐籥子

*Master Bellows*

by [[祝洞天]] (Zhù Dòngtiān)

A short Sòng Daoist philosophical treatise named after the *Dào dé jīng* ch. 5 image of the cosmos as a "bellows" (*tuó yuè* 橐籥). Preserved as **DZ 1188** / CT 1188 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Sòng. Dynasty: 宋.
