# Yù zǐ 鬻子

*Master Yù*

attributed to [[鬻熊]] (Yù Xióng) — legendary Zhōu counselor

A classical philosophical miscellany pseudepigraphically attributed to the Zhōu counselor Yù Xióng. Received text is a Hàn or Six-Dynasties compilation. Preserved as **DZ 1171** / CT 1171 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Hàn to Six Dynasties. Dynasty: 漢-六朝.
