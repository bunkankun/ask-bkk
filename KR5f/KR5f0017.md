# Jí zhù Tài xuán jīng 集註太玄經

*Collected-Commentary Edition of the Great-Mystery Scripture*

text by [[揚雄]] (Yáng Xióng, 53 BCE – 18 CE)

**The *Tài xuán jīng*** 太玄經 — Yáng Xióng's great Western-Hàn imitation of the *Yì jīng*, constructed on an 81-tetragram (rather than 64-hexagram) system. A major work of Hàn speculative philosophy. The Dào zàng edition (*Jí zhù*, collected commentary) presents the text with accumulated Hàn through Sòng commentaries. Preserved as **DZ 1183** / CT 1183 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Core text c. 1 CE; commentaries Hàn through Sòng. Dynasty: 漢-宋.

## Translations and research
