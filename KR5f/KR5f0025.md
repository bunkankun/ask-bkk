# Mì chuán Zhèng yáng zhēn rén Líng bǎo bì fǎ 祕傳正陽真人靈寶畢法

*Secretly-Transmitted Perfected-of-Pure-Yáng's Líng-bǎo Completed-Dharma*

attributed to [[鍾離權]] (Zhōnglí Quán, Zhèng yáng zhēn rén)

**The concluding text of the Tài qīng bù, and the last text of the Four Supplements.** A Northern-Sòng **inner-alchemical classic** attributed to **Zhōnglí Quán** (Zhèng yáng zhēn rén) — traditional teacher of Lǚ Dòngbīn and patriarch of the Zhōng-Lǚ 鍾呂 *nèi dān* transmission. One of the foundational texts of later inner-alchemical lineages (Wáng Chóngyáng traced his heritage through this tradition). Preserved as **DZ 1191** / CT 1191 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 北宋.
