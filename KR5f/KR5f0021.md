# Bào pǔ zǐ wài piān 抱朴子外篇

*Outer Chapters of the Master Embracing the Unhewn*

by [[葛洪]] (Gě Hóng, 283–343)

The ***Outer Chapters*** of the *Bào pǔ zǐ* — Gě Hóng's **socio-political-philosophical** companion to the *Inner Chapters* [[KR5f0019]], in 50 *juàn*. Where the *Inner Chapters* argue for immortality practices, the *Outer Chapters* engage with Confucian social-moral discourse: ruler-subject relations, scholarship, literature, honesty, friendship. Preserved as **DZ 1187** / CT 1187 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** *c.* 317–330. Dynasty: 東晉.
