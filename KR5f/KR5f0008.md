# Zǐ huá zǐ 子華子

*Master Zǐ-huá*

attributed to [[程本]] (Chéng Běn, Zǐ huá zǐ)

A **pseudepigraphical late-Northern-Sòng / early-Southern-Sòng philosophical miscellany** ascribed to the Spring-and-Autumn figure Chéng Běn (Zǐ huá zǐ). Preserved as **DZ 1174** / CT 1174 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Late 11th / early 12th century. Dynasty: 北宋後期.
