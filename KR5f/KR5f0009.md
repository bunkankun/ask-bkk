# Hé guān zǐ 鶡冠子

*Master Pheasant-Cap*

commentary by [[陸佃]] (Lù Diàn, 1042–1102)

The ***Hé guān zǐ*** is a late-Warring-States / early-Hàn **Huáng-Lǎo syncretic philosophical-strategic text**, traditionally associated with an anonymous recluse of Chǔ. Long considered a forgery, its authenticity was reaffirmed by the Mǎ wáng duī 馬王堆 discoveries. The received text in the Dào zàng is accompanied by **Lù Diàn's 陸佃 Northern-Sòng commentary**. Preserved as **DZ 1175** / CT 1175 (Tài qīng bù 太清部).

**Dating.** Core text late Warring-States / early Hàn; Lù Diàn commentary Northern Sòng. Dynasty: 戰國-北宋.
