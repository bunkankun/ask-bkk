# Dà-bó Qián chán-shī yǔ-lù 大博乾禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dà-bó Qián*
by [[乾]] (說), [[超宗]] (等錄)

Five-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Dà-bó Xíng-qián** [[乾]] 大博行乾 (**1602 – 1673-09-20**, age 72, *sēng-là* 59), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** 萬如通微 (1594–1657, of Lóng-chí). Native of **Sì-chuān Dá-zhōu** 達州, lay surname **Hú** 胡. Recorded by his attendant **Chāo-zōng** [[超宗]] *děng lù*. *notBefore* = **順治壬辰** = **1652** autumn (received *jì-bié*); *notAfter* = **1673** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B472.

Documents an extraordinary **eleven-abbacy** circuit, all in the **Hé-běi / Tiān-jīn / suburban Beijing** region — among the most extensive northern-China abbatial itineraries in the Jiā-xīng Canon: **Tiān-jīn Dì-zàng-sì** 天津地藏寺, **Tiān-jīn Rú-lái-sì** 天津如來寺, **Xióng-xiàn Chóng-níng-sì** 雄縣崇寧寺, **Yáng-cūn Bào-chéng-sì** 楊村報成寺, **Zhuō-zhōu Pǔ-dù-sì** 涿州普度寺, **Liáng-xiāng Shí-fāng-sì** 良鄉十方寺, **Jīn-mén Bō-rě-yuàn** 津門般若院, **Luán-zhōu Wàn-shàn-sì** 灤州萬善寺, **Xī-shān Tán-zhè-sì** 西山潭柘寺 (the famous suburban-Beijing temple), **Dū-chéng Fǎ-tōng-sì** 都城法通寺 (within Beijing), **Pán-shān Zhōng-pán-sì** 盤山中盤寺 (the burial site).

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces. Juan 2–5: continuing material — full *shàng-táng* records, *xiǎo-cān*, *fǎ-yǔ*, *jī-yuán*, *fó-shì*, *xíng-shí*.

## Other points of interest

The **Pán-shān Zhōng-pán-sì** 盤山中盤寺 is one of the major imperial-Qīng Buddhist temples — the same Pán-shān where [[昊|Huī-zhōu Hào]] (cf. [[KR6q0573]]) of the next generation also held abbacy. **Tán-zhè-sì** 潭柘寺 in the western Beijing hills was historically one of the most prestigious abbacies in the empire ("先有潭柘寺，後有北京城" — "Tán-zhè-sì existed before Beijing was built"). The Manchu-court Buddhist patronage axis traceable through Dà-bó (1652+) → Huī-zhōu Hào (1675+) is among the most important documented in the Jiā-xīng Canon.
