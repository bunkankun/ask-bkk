# Nán-yuè Jì-qǐ hé-shàng yǔ-lù 南嶽繼起和尚語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Reverend Nán-yuè Jì-qǐ*
by [[弘儲]] (說), [[濟機]] (等編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 / **Jì-qǐ Hóng-chǔ** 繼起弘儲 (**1605-03-26 – 1672-11-16**), Línjì-Yángqí principal dharma-heir of **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng Fǎ-zàng** [[法藏]] (1573–1635) and the chief continuator of the controversial **Sān-fēng** sub-line at **Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-sì** 蘇州靈巖寺. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Jì-jī** [[濟機]] *děng biān*. Title-form *Nán-yuè Jì-qǐ* references both the **Nán-yuè Huái-ràng** Línjì origin-pedigree and Hóng-chǔ's own *hào* **Jì-qǐ** 繼起. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B301.

### Abstract

**Self-edited corpus.** The juan-1 *zì-xù* by Hóng-chǔ ("退翁自序"), dated **壬子九月霜降第一日 = 1672/10/22 Gregorian** (about three weeks before his **1672-11-16** death), explains the compilation: during his **庚戌長夏 = 1670 summer** sickbed retreat, Hóng-chǔ realized that traveling pilgrims could not easily acquire his earlier voluminous *yǔ-lù* compilations (*Shù-quán Bào-cí*, *Jiǎ-chén Xiāng-yún-guǎn sǎn-lù*, *Yáo-fēng hòu-lù*); he therefore distilled from them a 10-juan condensed selection focusing on *yǔ zhī jiǎn-zhí zhě* 語之簡直者 (passages of direct simplicity) for portable use. Jì-jī finalized the editing.

**Authorship.** Per the [[弘儲]] note: lay name **Lǐ Jīn** 李金, *zì* **Zhòng-lián jū-shì** 仲連居士. Trained under Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng with formal transmission. Carrier of the Sān-fēng-line lineage through Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-sì and Yáo-fēng abbacies. Recorder of his master's [[KR6q0473]] 《三峰藏和尚語錄》.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1640** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1672/10** (self-preface). The 10-juan compilation specifically distills material composed across the master's c. 1640–1672 preaching career.

**Lineage and significance.** Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng → Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ. The Sān-fēng line was the principal target of the **Yōng-zhèng emperor's 1733 御製揀魔辨異錄** declaring it heretical and ordering the dismissal of its dharma-heirs. That this volume survived in the Jiā-xīng Canon (despite the imperial suppression) is significant — preserving Hóng-chǔ's own selected condensation of his career-long preaching.

## Other points of interest

- **Self-curated farewell-volume.** The 1670 summer sickbed compilation followed by the 1672/10 self-preface make this volume distinctive as a master's own deliberate selection of his most important preaching, completed weeks before death. Few comparable Chán *yǔ-lù* survive in such a directly self-curated form.
- **Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-sì line.** Hóng-chǔ's continuation of the Sān-fēng line through Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-sì and the Yáo-fēng abbacy (whence the 堯封後錄 = 堯峰後錄 mentioned in the self-preface as one of the source-compilations) maintained the Hàn-yuè teaching against Mì-yún-line orthodox pressure even before the eventual 1733 imperial proscription.
