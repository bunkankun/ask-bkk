# Hān-yú Xiān chán-shī yǔ-lù 憨予暹禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hān-yú Xiān*
by [[洪暹]] (說), [[法雲]] (等編)

## About the work

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hān-yú Hóng-xiān** [[洪暹]] 憨予洪暹 (= **憨予暹**, the catalog form), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zì-xián Xíng-jué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (1610–1662). Compiled by *mén-rén* **Fǎ-yún** [[法雲]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Hú Shì-ān** 胡世安 (1593–1663) — *Tài-zǐ tài-bǎo bīng-bù shàng-shū Wǔ-yīng-diàn dà-xué-shì Xī-shǔ* (Crown-Prince Tutor, Minister of War, Wǔ-yīng-diàn Grand Secretary, of Western Sì-chuān) — dated **順治己亥仲秋望日 = 1659/9/15 lunar = 1659-10-26 Gregorian**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B288.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[洪暹]] note: dharma-heir of Zì-xián Xíng-jué of the Mì-yún → Lín-yě line; lead compiler of his master's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0469]]. Hóng-xiān's own seat was at **Fén-yáng** 汾陽 (Shǎn-xī / Shān-xī) — significant for being the historic seat of **Fén-yáng Shàn-zhāo** 汾陽善昭 (947–1024), the founder of *gōng-àn* literature. The Hú Shì-ān preface explicitly invokes this Fén-yáng pedigree: *Hān-yú héshàng zhuō-xí Fén-yáng, jiàn-chuí shī-kū, chàng-tí zhí-qiē, jiē rì-yuè dēng yú zhōng-tiān, yìn-zhèng dòng-xuán* 憨予和尚卓錫汾陽鍵椎獅窟唱提直切揭日月燈於中天印證洞玄.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (conservative bound for Hóng-xiān's first abbacy, the seat predating the 1659 publication). *notAfter* = **1659** (Hú Shì-ān preface). Hóng-xiān's master Xíng-jué was still alive at this point (until 1662), making this a *yǔ-lù* published while the master was still active — uncommon and signaling an exceptional lay-patronage commitment to printing the disciple's work.

**Hú Shì-ān as preface-writer.** Hú Shì-ān (1593–1663), the early-Qīng Wǔ-yīng-diàn grand secretary and a senior survivor of Míng-Qīng transition officialdom, was a Western-Sì-chuān native (西蜀). His preface here, dated 1659/9, falls in his late-life period (he died 1663). The Hú-Hóng-xiān connection likely derives from shared Sì-chuān provenance: Hóng-xiān's master Xíng-jué was a Sì-chuān 合州 native, and Hú himself was 西蜀.

**Lineage.** Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Lín-yě Tōng-qí → Zì-xián Xíng-jué → Hān-yú Hóng-xiān — a one-to-one Sì-chuān-line transmission within the broader Mì-yún 32nd-generation Línjì.

## Other points of interest

- **Pre-master publication.** The 1659 publication date precedes the master Xíng-jué's 1662 death — making this an unusual case of a disciple's *yǔ-lù* printed while the master was still active in ministry. Suggests strong lay-patronage initiative (likely Hú Shì-ān's own circle in West Sì-chuān).
- **Fén-yáng-line claim.** The Fén-yáng abbacy positions Hóng-xiān's preaching in deliberate continuity with the **Fén-yáng Shàn-zhāo** Sòng-era Línjì foundation — a programmatic claim by a Sì-chuān-bred master to the Hé-běi / Shǎn-xī Línjì heartland traditional identity.
