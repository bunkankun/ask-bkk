# Luán-zhōu Wàn-shàn Huī-zhōu Hào chán-shī yǔ-lù 灤州萬善暉州昊禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huī-zhōu Hào of Wàn-shàn [-sì] in Luán-zhōu*
by [[昊]] (說), [[性珍]] (等錄), [[寂寶]] (等錄)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Huī-zhōu Hào** [[昊]] 暉洲昊 (lifedates unknown, active **1675–1699+**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pán-shān** 盤山. Native of **Luán-zhōu Lè-tíng** 灤州樂亭 (Hé-běi), lay surname **Liú** 劉. Recorded by his attendants **Xìng-zhēn** [[性珍]] and **Jì-bǎo** [[寂寶]] *děng lù*. Preface dated **康熙三十八年歲次己卯閏月** = **1699** leap-month.

The *yǔ-lù* documents an exceptionally extensive **thirteen-abbacy** circuit spanning the **Hé-běi → Tiān-jīn → Liáo-dōng → Mukden** northern frontier — comparable in geographic scope only to **Qiān-shān Shèng-rén** [[函可]]'s [[KR6q0527]] and **Gǔ-lín Zhì** [[智|Gǔ-lín Zhì]]'s [[KR6q0546]]. Principal seats include **Luán-zhōu Wàn-shàn-sì** 灤州萬善禪寺 (the title-seat); **Shèng-jīng Fèng-tiān Huá-yán-sì** 盛京奉天華嚴寺 (Mukden); **Pán-shān Zhèng-fǎ chán-sì** 盤山正法禪寺; **Tiān-jīn-wèi** 天津衛 (twice — *Fàng-shēng-yuàn* and *Bō-rě-yuàn*). Other abbacies in **Yǒng-píng-fǔ** 永平府, **Yù-tián-xiàn** 玉田縣, **Liáo-dōng Cháo-yáng-sì** 朝陽寺, **Níng-yuǎn-zhōu Dì-zàng-sì** 寧遠地藏寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1675** (post-付法 abbacy career under Liǎo-zōng who was active from the 1670s); *notAfter* = **1699** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B460.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *mù-cì*; abbacy *yǔ-lù* materials. Juan 2–4: continuing abbacy *shàng-táng* records. Juan 5: *Lè-tíng Shèng-yán-sì yǔ-lù* (the final abbacy). Juan 6: *chuí-wèn*; *rù-shì jī-yuán*; *wèn-dá jī-yuán*; *qǐng-yì*; *fù-zhǔ* (entrusting Tiān-xǐ Yìn 天璽印 with the lineage); *dài-gǔ*; *bié-gǔ*; *niān-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*; *fó-shì*; the closing *lüè-zhuàng* 略狀 (autobiographical summary).

## Other points of interest

This is the major Línjì-Yángqí *yǔ-lù* of post-1644 northern-frontier Buddhism after **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng**'s line passed through 函可 (Cáodòng, exiled to Liáo-dōng) and 古林智 (Línjì, integrated into the Mukden ecclesiastical establishment). Huī-zhōu Hào represents the third-stage **institutional consolidation** of Manchurian-Hé-běi Buddhism: 13 simultaneously-held abbacies under formal Qīng patronage, with **Tiān-jīn** as a key bridge-station between Hé-běi monastic centers and the Liáo-dōng frontier.
