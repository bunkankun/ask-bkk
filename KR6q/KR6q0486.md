# Huì-jué Yī chán-shī yǔ-lù 慧覺衣禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huì-jué Yī*
by [[照衣]] (說), [[徹御]] (等編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Huì-jué Zhào-yī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (**1605-04-08 – 1673-10-27**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chè-yù** [[徹御]] *děng biān*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B340. Juan 3 includes the *xíng-lù* 行錄 self-biographical record.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[照衣]] note: native of **Qí-jiāng-xiàn** 綦江縣 (Yú-zhōu, Sì-chuān), lay surname **Xióng** 熊. Late-life ordination at age 30 under **Cháng-bái** at Huá-yín-shān; eight years' refuge at **Yè-láng** during the 1644 dynastic transition. **1653/9**: transmission from Pòshān at Liáng-shān Jīn-chéng-sì + Shuāng-guì-sì.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1675** as a conservative bound for post-1673-decease compilation.

**Eight abbacies.** All Sì-chuān-region: Chóng-qìng Chóng-yīn-sì → Hú-shān Zhì-píng-sì → Tài-gōng-shān Shí-bǎo-sì → Jiāng-jīn Yuán-tōng-sì → Zhū-lín Hóng-fú-sì → Bā-xiàn Bǎo-lián-yuàn — a Sì-chuān-internal frontier-restoration ministry under the Pòshān-line aegis.

**Lineage.** Pò-shān Hǎi-míng → Huì-jué Zhào-yī → six named heirs including **Pò-fēng Zhòng** [[普重]] (cf. [[KR6q0492]]) and **Fó-yǔ Chè-yù** [[徹御]] (cf. [[KR6q0487]]).

## Other points of interest

- **Late-life monastic conversion at age 30.** Like several of his Sì-chuān contemporaries (cf. [[KR6q0464]]'s Chuán, [[KR6q0465]]'s Xíng-shěng), Zhào-yī's late-life ordination was triggered by the 1640s warfare-disruption rather than youthful religious aspiration — making the Pòshān-line Sì-chuān population partly composed of mature converts from the late-Míng warfare-collapse generation.
