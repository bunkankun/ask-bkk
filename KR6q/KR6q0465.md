# Xū-zhōu Shěng chán-shī yǔ-lù 虛舟省禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xū-zhōu Shěng*
by [[行省]] (說), [[超直]] (等編)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xū-zhōu Xíng-shěng** [[行省]] 虛舟行省 (**1600-02-06 – 1668-06-14**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Wèn-shí Chāo-zhí** [[超直]]. Two front-prefaces: (i) by **Yán Dà-cān** [[嚴大參]] 𨍏轢大參 (= **Yán Diào-yù** 嚴調御, the prominent Jiā-xīng lay-Chán figure and editor of [[KR6q0202]] etc.), dated **戊戌夏 = 1658 summer**, framing Xū-zhōu as a *fǎ-xiōng* 法兄 (dharma-brother) sharing the same Fèi-yǐn 同門 affiliation as Yán himself; (ii) posthumous preface by **Wǔ-lín fǎ-zhí** 武林法侄 **Zhāng Wén-jiā** 張文嘉 (*Zhòng-jiā fǔ* 仲嘉甫), undated but post-1668 (*shī jì shì-jì* 師既示寂). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B282; original cutting at the **Hé-zhōng Léng-yán-sì** 禾中楞嚴 in Jiā-xīng. Contents: juan 1 *shàng-táng* / *xiǎo-cān*; juan 2 *shì-zhòng* / *shàng-gòng* / *jī-yuán* / *jì* / *zàn*; juan 3 *niān-gǔ* / *sòng-gǔ*; juan 4 *xíng-zhuàng* + *tǎ-míng* + appendices including 《筏喻初學》 and the separately-printed 《八識規矩頌註》 ([[KR6q0215]]).

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[行省]] note: lay native of **Cí-xī** 慈谿, surname **Yáo** 姚, born 1600. Lay-Chán pilgrim under multiple Línjì masters (Yún-mén, Píng-táo, Yǔ-fēng, Lóng-mén, Lǐ-ān) and finally **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** at Jīn-sù; received the lay-name *Tōng-shěng* from Mì-yún. **1644** (age 46) tonsured at **Xuě-dòu** in the Míng-Qīng warfare-disruption; transferred to **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** and renamed **Xíng-shěng** 行省 (the standard *xíng*-generation form for Fèi-yǐn heirs, paralleling [[KR6q0463]]'s **Xíng-zé**). Transmission **1654** at *Yú-yún Chāo-guǒ*; abbacies 1655–59 at Xiǎn-jìng, Hù-shēng, Liú-xī Chán-yuàn. The Yán Dà-cān preface dates the corpus as already compiled by **1658** at the Hǒu-shān seat.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1654** (transmission and beginning of public preaching). *notAfter* = **1670** as a conservative bound — the volume was complete by **1658** per the Yán preface, with the Zhāng Wén-jiā post-1668 preface added at the time of the Léng-yán-sì publication after Xū-zhōu's death.

**Heirs.** Six *fǎ-sì* (Méi-ān, Wèn-shí [this volume's compiler], Kē-xuě, Xuě-dìng) plus two ordained nuns (Mì-qǐ, Jué-wù) and two principal lay-heirs (Hé-zhuó Xuě Chāo-xìn, Shěn Shàng-xiān). Wèn-shí Chāo-zhí's reputation among the heirs centered on his fund-raising for the Léng-yán-sì publication: *jié-lì mù-zǐ, cáng-bǎn yú Hé-zhōng zhī Léng-yán* 竭力募梓藏板於禾中之楞嚴.

**Doctrinal interest.** Xū-zhōu's distinctive Yogācāra-Chán synthesis is preserved in the appended 《八識規矩頌註》 ([[KR6q0215]]) and referenced in the second preface as *bié-kè bā-shí guī-jǔ-sòng zhù* 別刻八識規矩頌註. Few early-Qīng Chán masters produced Yogācāra commentaries at this scholastic depth.

## Other points of interest

- **Late-life monastic conversion.** Xū-zhōu's transition from lay-Chán to ordained monastic at **age 46** (1644) — driven directly by the Míng-Qīng warfare-disruption and family-loss — preserves a documented life-transition triggered by the dynastic transition. Compare [[KR6q0464]] 's Chuán (also late-Míng-warfare-driven Chán entry) for parallel cases.
- **Yogācāra commentary.** The accompanying *Bā-shí guī-jǔ-sòng zhù* (separately preserved at [[KR6q0215]]) makes Xū-zhōu one of the few early-Qīng Chán masters to produce a substantial commentary on Xuán-zàng's *Bā-shí guī-jǔ-sòng* — a doctrinal scholastic engagement unusual for the Línjì preaching tradition.
