# Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Jī chán-shī yǔ-lù 慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Jī*
by [[慧機]] (說), [[幻敏]] (重編)

## About the work

Twenty-juan definitive *zhèng-lù* 正錄 of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668), second-generation head of the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派 and chief Sì-chuān Línjì dharma-heir of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]]. Stands in definitive-edition relation to the 3-juan selective 《慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄》 at [[KR6q0450]] (J29 B240, early 1642 recension): this B241 is the full *chóng-biān* 重編 expansion covering the master's complete 32-year preaching career across ten abbacies, prepared after Tiě-bì's 1668 death by his dharma-heir **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] 竺峰幻敏 (1638–1707). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B241. Opens with the same two prefaces as B240: the Xióng Rǔ-xué 熊汝學 (**崇禎壬午秋九日** = **1642/9**) and Móu Dào-xíng 牟道行 *zhē-biǎo* prefaces. Closes with juan 20 containing (i) the 《慶忠機和尚年譜序》 of **Lǐ Dào-jì** 李道濟 (西江弟子) dated **康熙甲寅孟春上浣 = 1674/1**; (ii) the 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》 compiled by the other chief dharma-heir **Wàn-fēng Zhì-shàn** 萬峰至善 (*qián zhù Tán-zhōu Wàn-fēng sì-fǎ mén-rén* 前住潭州萬峰嗣法門人); and (iii) the 《行狀》 formal biography.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1639** (Tiě-bì's **Chóng-zhēn 12** accession to the Jù-yún abbacy on Guǎng-zhēn's death). *notAfter* = **1674** (Lǐ Dào-jì's *Kāng-xī 13* preface to the year-chronology of juan 20, the latest datable signature in the corpus). The overall compilation was completed in 1674 or shortly after, incorporating material post-dating Tiě-bì's **Kāng-xī 7 戊申 9/21 = 1668/10/26** death.

**Structural relation to [[KR6q0450]].** B240 (3 juan, 1642) captured only the opening three years of Tiě-bì's Jù-yún tenure with Xióng Rǔ-xué and Móu Dào-xíng as contemporary lay-disciple prefaces. B241 (20 juan) retains those two prefaces verbatim but expands the interior corpus to include (a) the later seven abbacies at **Dì-zàng-yuàn**, **Yín-wēng**, **Yún-lái**, **Qìng-zhōng-sì**, **Yún-yán**, **Bǎo-shèng-sì**, and the final **Zhì-píng / Lóng-chāng Chán-yuàn** 治平 / 龍昌 on the Bái Jū-yì willow-site; (b) the 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》; (c) the 行狀.

**Year-chronology.** The 《治平鐵壁機禪師年譜》 by Wàn-fēng Zhì-shàn is the single most detailed biographical witness to Tiě-bì: it begins at **Wàn-lì 31 癸卯 10/20 寅時** = **1603/11/22** with the master's birth at **Yíng-shān** 營山 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Luó** 羅 of a multi-*jìn-shì* scholar-gentry household, and ends with the Kāng-xī 7 death at Zhì-píng. Lǐ Dào-jì's preface compares Zhì-shàn's year-editing to Ānanda's sūtra-compilation: *tóng-shī zhī biān-nián, qí yóu Ānán zhī jié-jí yě zāi* 童師之編年其猶阿難之結集也哉.

**Lineage and heirs.** Twenty-three named dharma-heirs per the 《續燈正統》 j. 16 roster, of whom two figure prominently in the B241 apparatus: **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]] (whose *fù-fǎ* certificate Tiě-bì composed, but who does not compile this B241 — his parallel work is at [[KR6q0448]] / [[KR6q0449]] / [[KR6q0450]] and his own *yǔ-lù* at [[KR6q0454]]) and **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] (this volume's *chóng-biān* compiler, with his own *yǔ-lù* at J40 B483 outside KR).

## Other points of interest

- **Different compiler from B240.** Where B240 was edited by the dharma-grandfather-line heir Dēng-lái in 1642 (alongside the Guǎng-zhēn volumes), B241 is edited by the other chief heir Huàn-mǐn in 1674 (after Tiě-bì's death). The *chóng-biān* 重編 designation in the catalog meta reflects Huàn-mǐn's comprehensive re-editing to include the *nián-pǔ* and *xíng-zhuàng* apparatus absent from B240.
- **Scholar-gentry lineage.** The Luó family of Yíng-shān produced multiple *jìn-shì* across generations — noted in the *nián-pǔ* opening entry as *jiā-shì yǐ yī-jīng chuán duō kē-jiǎ* 家世以一經傳多科甲 — making Tiě-bì one of the few Sichuan Chán masters of the period from a continuous scholar-official pedigree.
