# Jīng-nán Nèi Hù-guó-sì Qǐ-zhēn Chéng chán-shī yǔ-lù 荊南內護國寺啟真誠禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qǐ-zhēn Chéng of Nèi Hù-guó-sì in Jīng-nán*
by [[誠]] (說), [[振聞]] (編)

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Qǐ-zhēn Chéng** [[誠]] 啟真誠, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Hù-guó Biàn-zhī = Bié-fēng Xiù** 別峰秀 in the Wǔ-zǔ-line Línjì sub-tradition. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhèn-wén** [[振聞]] *biān*. Sole abbacy at **Jīng-nán Nèi Hù-guó-sì** 荊南內護國寺. Juan 3 includes the *Ēn-mǔ chuán* (mother-biography). *notBefore* / *notAfter*: c. 1670–1700. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B395.
