# Xī-yán Liǎo-huì chánshī yǔlù 西巖了慧禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Xī-yán Liǎo-huì* — a two-juan late-Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Xī-yán Liǎo-huì** [[了慧]] 西巖了慧 (1198 – Jǐngdìng 3.3.22 / 19 April 1262, *shì* 65), dharma-heir of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Compiled by five *mén-rén shì-zhě* 門人侍者.

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* in *Xuzangjing* X70 n1391. Preface by Huái-hǎi Yuán-zhào 淮海元肇 (then abbot of Yù-wáng-shān) dated Jǐngdìng *guǐ-hài* mid-spring (early 1263). Five abbacy records: Píngjiāng Dìng-huì 定慧, Wēnzhōu Yànshān Néng-rén 鴈山能仁, Jiāng-zhōu Dōng-lín 東林, Qìngyuán Tài-bái-míng-shān Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé 太白名山天童景德, and Ruì-yán-shān Kāi-shàn 瑞巖開善. Followed in juan 2 by *fǎ-yǔ*, *bá-yǔ*, *jì-sòng*, *zàn fó-zǔ*, *zì-zàn*, *xiǎo fó-shì*, and the *xíng-zhuàng*.

### Abstract

Per DILA A009257 and the *xíng-zhuàng*: Liǎo-huì (*hào* Xī-yán 西巖 "Western Cliff"), surname Luó 羅, native of Péng-chí 蓬池 in Péng-zhōu 蓬州 (Shǔ). Tonsured at nineteen by Zǔ-dēng 祖燈 of Ān-guó-sì on Yù-zhǎng-shān; studied briefly the *Prajñāpāramitā* *niàn-fó sān-mèi* (not to his taste), then the *xìng-zōng* sūtra scholarship at Chéngdū. He eventually trained across a striking range of masters: Huài-ān Zhào 壞菴照 at Zhāo-jué, Zhè-wēng Rú-yǎn 浙翁如琰, Gāo-yuán Zǔ-quán 高原祖泉, Wújùn Shīfàn 無準師範 (his transmission-master), Shí-tián Fǎ-xūn 石田法薰, and Miào-fēng Zhī-shàn 妙峰之善. Five successive abbacies, ending at Tiān-tóng, after which he retired to the Huàn-zhì-ān 幻智庵; died at 65 with 47 summer-retreats to his credit. Principal dharma-heirs: Dōng-yán Jìng-rì 東巖淨日 and Yuè-jiàn Wén-míng 月磵文明 (author of [[KR6q0325]]).
