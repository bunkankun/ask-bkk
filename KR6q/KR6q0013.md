# Wǔdēng huìyuán xùlüè 五燈會元續略

compiled by [[淨柱]] (Jìngzhù), late Míng

## About the work

A four-juan late-Míng supplement to the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* 五燈會元 ([[KR6q0012]]), continuing the Chán lineage record from the Southern Sòng into the Yuán and Míng. Compiled by Jìngzhù, a Cáodòng-line monk, and completed in the closing years of the Chóngzhēn reign (the standard dating is 1640 – early 1640s, ambiguous in the sources).

### Tiyao


### Abstract

One of several seventeenth-century lamp-record continuations addressing the perceived need to update Pǔjì's 1252 synthesis for the YuánMíng generations. Jìngzhù's compilation is more compressed than the parallel efforts by Wénxiù ([[KR6q0017]]) and Tōngróng ([[KR6q0019]]), drawing primarily on Míng monastic biographical literature. The Cáodòng-line perspective gives the work a modest corrective emphasis against the Línjì-dominant *Wǔdēng yántǒng* compiled around the same time.

## Translations and research

No English translation. No dedicated monograph.

## Other points of interest

The work circulated in the Jiāxīng 嘉興 canon and thereby entered the Xuzangjing; its brevity relative to parallel Míng continuations helped it serve as a convenient reference but limited its long-term scholarly authority.
