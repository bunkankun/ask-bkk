# Xù gǔ zūn sù yǔ yào 續古尊宿語要

*Continued Essential Sayings of the Ancient Venerable Masters* — a six-juan Southern-Sòng Chán supplementary anthology by [[師明|Shīmíng]] 師明, continuing the *Gǔ zūn sù yǔ yào* tradition of the earlier [[賾藏|Zézàngzhǔ]] 賾藏主 compilation. Covered by [[道忠|Mujaku Dōchū]]'s table-of-contents [[KR6q0264]].

## About the work

A six-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù* supplementary anthology, X68 n1318. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1200 (Southern-Sòng mid-period); *notAfter* c. 1250.
