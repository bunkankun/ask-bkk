# Xiào-yǐn Dà-xīn chánshī yǔlù 笑隱大訢禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Xiào-yǐn Dà-xīn* — a four-juan Yuán *yǔlù* of **Xiào-yǐn Dà-xīn** 笑隱大訢 (1284–1344), the dominant Yuán-period Chán master of the imperial court (*shì jiào zōng zhǔ* 釋教宗主 "Head of Buddhist Schools") and preface-writer for multiple major Yuán Chán texts (including [[KR6q0179]] *Zhèng dào gē zhù* by [[永盛|Yǒngshèng]]). Compiled by four of his *mén-rén* 門人 disciples: [[延俊|Yán-jùn]], [[慧曇|Huì-tán]], [[中孚|Zhōng-fú]], [[崇裕|Chóng-yù]].

Four-juan Yuán Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1367.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1310; *notAfter* c. 1350.
