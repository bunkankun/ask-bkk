# Xuěfēng Huìkōng chánshī yǔlù 雪峰慧空禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Xuěfēng Huìkōng* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù* of **Xuěfēng Huìkōng** 雪峰慧空 (1096–1158). Compiled by his dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) [[慧弼|Huìbì]] 慧弼.

## About the work

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1346. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

**Huìkōng** 慧空: Northern-Sòng-to-Southern-Sòng transitional LínjìYángqí Chán master; abbot of Xuěfēngsì 雪峰寺 in Fúzhōu. Dharma-heir of Zhàntáng Wénzhǔn 湛堂文準 in the Línjì line.

**Compiler [[慧弼|Huìbì]]** 慧弼: Huìkōng's dharma-heir; editor of the *yǔlù*. Lifedates unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1120; *notAfter* c. 1200.
