# Xuě-fēng Yì-cún chánshī yǔlù 雪峰義存禪師語錄 (Zhēn-jué chánshī yǔlù 真覺禪師語錄)

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Xuě-fēng Yì-cún* — a two-juan Míng-edited *yǔlù* of the great late-Táng Chán master **Xuě-fēng Yì-cún** 雪峰義存 (822–908), posthumous title *Zhēn-jué chánshī* 真覺禪師. Compiled in the Míng by [[林弘衍|Lín Hóng-yǎn]] 林弘衍.

## About the work

A two-juan Míng-edited *yǔlù* of a classical Táng master, X69 n1333. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

**Xuě-fēng Yì-cún** 雪峰義存 (822–908): one of the most important late-Táng Chán masters; abbot of Xuě-fēng-sì 雪峰寺 in Fú-zhōu. Dharma-heir of Dé-shān Xuān-jiàn 德山宣鑑. Through his prominent dharma-heirs (Yún-mén Wén-yǎn 雲門文偃 the founder of Yúnmén-school; Xuán-shā Shī-bèi 玄沙師備 the ancestor of the Fǎyǎn-school), Xuě-fēng's line produced two of the classical Chán Five Houses.

**Compiler [[林弘衍|Lín Hóng-yǎn]]** 林弘衍: late-Míng lay Buddhist editor. Lifedates unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1600 (Míng Wànlì); *notAfter* 1644 (end of Míng).
