# Bǎi-zhàng Huái-hǎi chánshī guǎng lù 百丈懷海禪師廣錄

*Extended Record of Chán Master Bǎi-zhàng Huái-hǎi* — the expanded-recension *yǔlù* of **Bǎi-zhàng Huái-hǎi** 百丈懷海 (720–814); parallel to [[KR6q0268]] *Bǎi-zhàng Huái-hǎi chánshī yǔlù*. Constitutes juan 3 of the *Sì jiā yǔlù*.

## About the work

One-juan Táng Chán *guǎng lù* ("extended record"), X69 n1323. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The *guǎng lù* preserves a more expansive selection of Bǎi-zhàng's teachings than the companion *yǔlù* [[KR6q0268]]; see that entry for context.

Dating: *notBefore* 820; *notAfter* c. 900.
