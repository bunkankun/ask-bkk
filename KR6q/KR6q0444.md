# Yī-chū Yuán chán-shī yǔ-lù 一初元禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yī-chū Yuán*
by [[元]] (說), [[真開]] (等編), [[真智]] (等編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Yī-chū Yuán** [[元]] 一初元 (= **Yī-chū Wù-yuán** 一初悟元, previously documented at [[KR6q0416]]) — dharma-brother of [[悟進|Jiè-ān Wù-jìn]] (at [[KR6q0443]]) and co-heir of [[妙用|Yuān-hú Miào-yòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (cf. [[KR6q0416]]) in the Cáo-dòng Wú-qù Rú-kōng sub-line. Catalog-form **元** 元 (distinct from [[KR6q0421]] **Bǎi-chī Yuán** 百癡元 = the Fèi-yǐn Línjì-line heir, same single-character *fǎ-huì*). *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Wù-yuán** 悟元, *hào* **Yī-chū** 一初 ("First"). Held abbacy at **Pú-xī Yǒng-zhèng Chán-sì** 濮溪永正禪寺 from **戊子 1648**, parallel to dharma-brother Wù-jìn's 1648 Jīn-míng-sì entry. Compiled by dharma-heirs **Zhēn-kāi** [[真開]] 真開 and **Zhēn-zhì** [[真智]] 真智 — 真-generation heirs. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B234.

### Abstract

**Context.** [[KR6q0444]] is the companion-volume to [[KR6q0443]] 《介菴進禪師語錄》 — the two Miào-yòng dharma-heirs 介菴 and 一初 each produced their own *yǔlù* volumes, collected sequentially in Jiā-xīng Canon J29 (B233 = Jiè-ān's 10 juan; B234 = Yī-chū's 2 juan). For the broader biographical context of the Cáo-dòng Wú-qù / Nán-míng-line transmission, see [[KR6q0416]] and [[KR6q0443]]. Yī-chū-specific biographical material is thinner than his dharma-brother's.

**Abbacy.** **Pú-xī Yǒng-zhèng Chán-sì** 濮溪永正禪寺 from **戊子 = 1648** on lay-patron petition. The **Pú-xī** 濮溪 location is in the Jiā-xīng area.

**Compositional history.** Opening preface by **Shī-bó** 施博 (*hòu-xué* 後學; cf. [[KR6q0432]], [[KR6q0433]]) — undated, but consistent with a c. 1676–85 cutting-date. Shī-bó's preface positions the two dharma-brothers as parallel post-Miào-yòng inheritors: "*wǔ chuán zhì jīn zé yǒu Jiè-ān Yī-chū liǎng shī chū yān, Jiè-ān héshàng zhǔ-fǎ Jīn-míng zhū-fāng yǎng-zhòng; Yī-chū héshàng yú wù-zǐ nián wèi tán-yuè qǐng jū Pú-xī Yǒng-zhèng* 四傳至今則有介菴一初兩師出焉介菴和尚主法金明諸方仰重一初和尚於戊子年為檀越請居濮溪永正". The second preface (unread in my extract) continues this theme. *notBefore* = **1648** (Pú-xī abbacy); *notAfter* = **1685** conservatively.

**Contents.** Two juan of **shàng-táng + xiǎo-cān + jī-yuán + niān-sòng** 上堂 + 小參 + 機緣 + 拈頌 material.

## Other points of interest

- **Paired with [[KR6q0443]] as Miào-yòng's two-heir monuments.** [[KR6q0443]] (介菴悟進, 10 juan) + [[KR6q0444]] (一初悟元, 2 juan) preserve the matched *yǔlù* pair for Miào-yòng's two principal heirs, with Jiè-ān's being substantially more extensive — reflecting Jiè-ān's longer-tenure primary abbacy at Jīn-míng versus Yī-chū's more modest Pú-xī tenure. Both 1648 abbacy-start dates and Jiā-xīng Canon sequential placement reflect the editors' deliberate pairing.
- **名 disambiguation 元.** The catalog-form **元** in this entry refers to **Yī-chū Wù-yuán** (Cáo-dòng Wú-qù sub-line), not to be confused with [[KR6q0421]]'s **Bǎi-chī Yuán** (Línjì Fèi-yǐn sub-line). Two distinct Chán masters with the same single-character dharma-name in the same Jiā-xīng Canon cluster.
