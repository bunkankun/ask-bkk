# Hǔ-qiū Shào-lóng chánshī yǔlù 虎丘紹隆禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Hǔ-qiū Shào-lóng* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional *yǔlù* of **Hǔ-qiū Shào-lóng** 虎丘紹隆 (1077–1136), *shì* Dà-huì 大慧 (not to be confused with Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo!). Line-parallel to Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo: both dharma-heirs of Yuán-wù [[克勤|Kè-qín]] 圓悟克勤. Shào-lóng founded the **Hǔ-qiū-sì** 虎丘寺 branch of the Yángqí Línjì sub-lineage, while Zōng-gǎo founded the Jìngzhì sub-branch.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1358. Compiled by disciple [[嗣端|Sì-duān]] 嗣端 et al.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1120; *notAfter* c. 1160.
