# Dōng-shān Pò-fēng Zhòng chán-shī yǔ-lù 東山破峰重禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Pò-fēng Zhòng of Dōng-shān*
by [[普重]] (說), [[傳慧]] (等編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Pò-fēng Pǔ-zhòng** [[普重]] 破峰普重, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huì-jué Zhào-yī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chuán-huì** [[傳慧]]. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B352.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per [[普重]] note: heir of [[照衣]] at the Pòshān → Zhào-yī line, abbot at **Dōng-shān** 東山. Lifedates not preserved.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1665** (conservative bound for post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound for post-master-decease compilation.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎi-míng → Huì-jué Zhào-yī → Pò-fēng Pǔ-zhòng. Among Zhào-yī's six named heirs ([[徹御]] Fó-yǔ at [[KR6q0487]], Cuì-é, Xiù-é, Zhèng-é, Qīng-yuán), Pò-fēng was listed first.
