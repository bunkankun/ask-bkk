# Yì-zhōu Sōng-shān Yě-zhú chán-shī hòu-lù 益州嵩山野竹禪師後錄
*Supplementary Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yě-zhú of Sōng-shān, Yì-zhōu*
by [[福慧]] (說), [[洪希]] (等編)

## About the work

Eight-juan **supplementary** *hòu-lù* 後錄 of **Yě-zhú Fú-huì** [[福慧]] 嵩山野竹福慧 (Sì-chuān → Yún-nán mission-master, see person-note for full biography). Continues the 14-juan *qián-lù* [[KR6q0438]] 《嵩山野竹禪師錄》 (J29 B224, cut **1665**) which had covered Fú-huì's nine-abbacy Yún-nán mission through the mid-1660s. This 後錄 covers the post-1665 period and the additional **Yún-nán Dà-lǐ Bīn-chuān-zhōu Jī-zú-shān** 雞足山 ministry begun in **康熙十年 11/15 = 1671/12/15**. Compiled by *sì-fǎ mén-rén* **Hóng-xī** [[洪希]] (then abbot of **Jī-zú-shān Dà-jué-sì** 大覺寺) with co-compiler **Guǎng-zhì** 廣智 (abbot of **Jī-zú-shān Shí-zhōng-sì** 石鐘寺). Front-preface by the prominent late-Míng / early-Qīng official **Gōng Dǐng-zī** 龔鼎孳 (1615–1673), dated **康熙十一年壬子孟冬 = 1672/11**, with extensive titulary listing his offices through 戶刑二部尚書 / *都察院 zuǒ-dū yù-shǐ*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B284.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1666** (immediately post-the *qián-lù* cutoff at 乙巳 = 1665). *notAfter* = **1672** (Gōng Dǐng-zī preface). The earliest dated event in the text is the **康熙十年 11/15 = 1671/12/15** entry-sermon at **Jī-zú-shān Shí-zhōng-sì** 雞足山石鐘寺 — a famous Buddhist sacred site (the supposed final-residence of Mahā-Kāśyapa per the Yún-nán tradition). Earlier 1666–71 abbacies in central Yún-nán continue from the *qián-lù* sequence.

**Gōng Dǐng-zī's preface.** Gōng (1615–1673), one of the most prominent post-1644 Han officials at the Qīng court, writes from his perspective as having served in 順治 court alongside the Tiān-tóng abbot **Shān-xiǎo Běn-xī** 山曉本皙. The preface frames Yě-zhú's work as **continuing the broad Sì-chuān (巴渝) Línjì lineage** (*Mǎ-shī yǐ huán Dōng-shān fù-zǐ ér hòu zhì Wò-lóng Yǎng-shān jí Jīn-líng Yì-shàn Hǎi-zhōu Cí-gōng fán bā shì jiē zhōng Mín xiù* 馬師以還東山父子而後至臥龍仰山及金陵翼善海舟慈公凡八世皆鍾岷秀) — drawing the line from Mǎ-zǔ Dào-yī through the Sì-chuān Línjì series down to the Mìyún → Pòshān Sì-chuān-Yún-nán transmission of Fú-huì's own day. Mentions that Shān-xiǎo and Yě-zhú are 同邑 from Sì-chuān, and that **Shān-xiǎo personally brokered** the publication by sending the *hòu-lù* to Gōng for prefacing.

**Lineage and abbacy.** Per the [[福慧]] note: Pòshān Hǎi-míng → Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo → [[璧]] **Shān-huī Bì** 山暉璧 → **Yě-zhú Fú-huì**. The 後錄 documents Fú-huì's eastward mission-extension from the central-Yún-nán nine-abbacy network of the 前錄 to the **Jī-zú-shān** sacred-mountain cluster — bringing the Pòshān-line preaching to the Mahā-Kāśyapa pilgrimage site itself. The 1671 entry-sermon's incense-offering verse names *zǔ-shī Jiā-yè zūn-zhě* 祖師迦葉尊者 and *qián-zhù Jiāng-nán Sū-zhōu-fǔ Shuāng-tǎ Shòu-níng Zhuān-jìng lǎo-héshàng* 前住江南蘇州府雙塔壽寧磚鏡老和尚 — the *běn-shī* 本師 reference invoking **Zhuān-jìng** 磚鏡 (= 山暉Bì's *hào* in some sources, or a parallel teacher).

## Other points of interest

- **Gōng Dǐng-zī as preface-writer.** Gōng Dǐng-zī (1615–1673), one of the most prominent **post-1644 Han loyalist-turned-Qīng officials**, was a major late-Míng / early-Qīng cultural figure. His preface here, dated 1672/11 just before his 1673 death, is a late-life composition. Few Chán *yǔ-lù* prefaces of the early Qīng carry comparable official-cultural weight.
- **Jī-zú-shān as sacred Mahā-Kāśyapa site.** The 1671 abbacy entry into the Jī-zú-shān cluster (Shí-zhōng-sì, Dà-jué-sì) is significant: by tradition Jī-zú-shān is the site of Mahā-Kāśyapa's final samādhi-retreat. Yě-zhú's installation as abbot of the principal sacred-mountain monasteries makes him institutionally one of the most senior Chán teachers in southwestern China by the early 1670s.
- **Wú Sān-guì-period frontier ministry.** Yě-zhú's Yún-nán ministry operated under the **Wú Sān-guì** 吳三桂 *Píng-xī Wáng* administration (1660–73) — a politically delicate position making the 1672 Gōng Dǐng-zī (Beijing-based central-government official) preface particularly noteworthy as a Qīng-court endorsement of the frontier mission just before the 1673 outbreak of the Three-Feudatories rebellion.
