# Duàn-qiáo Miào-lún chánshī yǔlù 斷橋妙倫禪師語錄

Two-juan late-Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Duàn-qiáo Miào-lún** [[妙倫]] 斷橋妙倫 (1201–1261), dharma-heir of [[師範]] Wújùn Shīfàn. Compiled by the attendants [[文寶]] Wén-bǎo and [[善靖]] Shàn-jìng. *Xuzangjing* X70 n1394. Preface by Lín Xī-yì 林希逸 dated Xiánchún 1 *rén-rì* (1265) describes the recovery of the text from worm-eaten fragments at Bò-shān 檗山, then a completed copy. Four abbacy records: Táizhōu Ruì-fēng Zhǐ-yuán 瑞峯祇園, Táizhōu Ruì-yán Jìng-tǔ 瑞巖淨土, Tiāntái Guó-qīng Jiào-zhōng 國清教忠, and Jìng-cí Bào-ēn Guāng-xiào 淨慈報恩光孝 (Lín'ān). Juan 2 *fǎ-yǔ*, *fó-zǔ zàn*, *zì-zàn*, *jì-sòng*, *xiǎo fó-shì*, and *xíng-zhuàng*. Miào-lún is the teacher of Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào 高峰原妙, whose line via Zhōng-fēng Míng-běn 中峰明本 dominates later Yuán-Míng Línjì.
