# Yúnān Kèwén chánshī yǔlù (xù, sù, fù lù) 雲菴克文禪師語錄（敘．疏．附錄）

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Yúnān Kèwén — Preface, Memorial, Appendix* — paratexts and supplementary materials for the *yǔlù* of the Northern-Sòng Huánglóng-lineage Línjì master **Yúnān Zhēnjìng Kèwén** 雲庵真淨克文 (1025–1102) — teacher of the major Chán-literary figure Juéfàn Huìhóng 覺範慧洪 (see [[德洪]]). Compiled by his dharma-heir [[福深|Fúshēn]] 福深.

## About the work

A one-juan paratext-and-appendix collection for Kèwén's *yǔlù*, X69 n1342. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. The main body of the *yǔlù* proper is preserved separately; this text provides the front-and-back matter.

**Zhēnjìng Kèwén** 真淨克文 (1025–1102), *shì* Yúnān 雲庵: major Northern-Sòng Huánglóng-lineage Chán master; dharma-heir of Huánglóng Huìnán 黃龍慧南. Teacher of Juéfàn Huìhóng [[德洪]] and of multiple other prominent Northern-Sòng Línjì masters.

**Compiler [[福深|Fúshēn]]** 福深: dharma-heir (*sì fǎ* 嗣法) of Kèwén; compiler of the paratexts.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1095 (late Kèwén teaching-period); *notAfter* c. 1150 (publication-horizon).
