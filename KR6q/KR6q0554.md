# Héng-shān chán-shī yǔ-lù 衡山禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Héng-shān*
by [[燈炳]] (說), [[宗位]] (編), [[超源]] (序), [[藍理]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] 衡山燈炳 (also **Qìng-yún Bǐng** 慶雲炳) (**1611-05-22 – 1680-07-13**, age 70), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 — a senior figure in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line, dharma-grandson of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn** 聚雲吹萬 of Bǎo-fēng. Native of **Hé-jiāng** 合江 (Sì-chuān). Compiled by his *fǎ-sūn* (dharma-grandson) **Zōng-wèi** [[宗位]] *biān*, with the manuscript carried south for engraving by his third-generation grandson **Jǐ-huàn chán-rén** 几幻禪人 with the support of his Pǔ-tuó dharma-cousin **Bié-ān** 別菴和尚.

Two prefaces are preserved in juan 1: by 龍池後裔閩莆 **Chāo-yuán Dàn-yá** [[超源]] (Mǐn-pǔ, of the Lóng-chí Línjì line) dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; and by **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] (1649–1720), then *zhèn-shǒu Zhè-jiāng děng-chù dì-fāng Dìng-hǎi zǒng-bīng-guān zuǒ dū-dū* (Naval Commander of Dìng-hǎi, Zhè-jiāng), dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1.

The first abbacy is dated explicitly: Dēng-bǐng entered **Kuí-zhōu-fǔ Shí-zhù Jí-xiáng chán-sì** 夔州府石砫吉祥禪寺 on **順治壬辰七月望日** = **1652-08-19** on the invitation of **Sū Mào-yǔ** 蘇茂宇 of 祇園檀越; later abbacy at **Qìng-yún chán-sì** 慶雲禪寺 (giving him his second sobriquet). *notBefore* = **1652** (Jí-xiáng-sì entry); *notAfter* = **1696** (latest preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B438.

**Contents.** Single juan: two prefaces; *xiǎo-cān*; *shì-zhòng*; *fǎ-yǔ*; *jī-yuán*; *wèn-dá*; *fó-shì* (cremation/funeral verses for **Jù-yún Wàn-zǔ** 聚雲萬祖, lay-patron Sū Mào-yǔ, fellow monk Sī-xiū, *wéi-nuó* Xīn-chí, etc.); *tí-zàn* (image-encomia for his teacher Chuī-wàn 吹萬師翁, Bodhidharma 達磨渡江/隻履西歸, etc.); closing materials including the *tǎ-míng* with his birth and death dates.

## Other points of interest

The *yǔ-lù*'s fifteen-year publishing delay (1680 death → 1695–1696 prefaces) reflects the post-Sānfān-luàn (1681) rebuilding of southwestern monastic networks; the manuscript was carried "**south**" by **Jǐ-huàn** 几幻 (a third-generation descendant) to the major eastern publishing center, with **Pǔ-tuó-shān** as the staging-ground via **Bié-ān**. The military-commander preface by **Lán Lǐ** is unusual within Buddhist *yǔ-lù*: by 1696 Lán Lǐ was famous for his conspicuous role in the 1683 Pénghú sea-battle that ended the Zhèng-régime in Tāi-wān, and his preface is one of the rare points of contact between high Qīng military elites and a Sì-chuān-rooted Línjì-Dà-huì lineage *yǔ-lù*.
