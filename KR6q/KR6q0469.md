# Zì-xián Jué chán-shī yǔ-lù 自閒覺禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zì-xián Jué*
by [[覺]] (說), [[洪暹]] (等編)

## About the work

Eight-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zì-xián Xíng-jué** [[覺]] 自閒行覺 (**1610-09-23 – 1662-02-14**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652) at **Tōng-xuán-sì** 通玄寺. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hān-yú Xiān** [[洪暹]] 憨予暹 *děng*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B287. Six-abbacy structure: juan 1 — Sū-zhōu Huáng-xī Luó-hàn-sì + Chóng-chuān Cí-jì + Fèng-chuān Fǎ-hǎi *yǔ-lù*; juan 2 — Fèng-chuān Yuè-lín + Jiā-xīng Lóng-yuān Jǐng-dé *yǔ-lù*; juan 3 — Jīng-xī Fú-róng *yǔ-lù*; juan 4 — *shì-zhòng* / *xiǎo-cān* / *wǎn-cān* / *fǎ-yǔ* / *wèn-dá* / *shì-zhōng kān-biàn*; juan 5 — *niān-sòng* / *lián-fāng-jì* / **行實**; juan 6 — *shū-wèn* / *fó-zǔ-zàn* / *zì-zàn* / *fó-shì*; juan 7–8 *xíng-zhuàng* and additional matter.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA and the juan-5 *xíng-shí* + juan-8 *xíng-zhuàng*: native of **Hé-zhōu** 合州 (Chóng-qìng, Sì-chuān), lay surname **Yú** 余. Tonsured at age 8 at Mǎ-dāng-shān under **Tài-kōng**. **1635**: left Sì-chuān; first contact with **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** at Tiān-tóng on a *lù-zhù* 露柱 collision triggering initial *xǐng*. Decisive transmission from **Tōng-xuán Lín-yě Tōng-qí**.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1648** (the 戊子 entry-sermon at Sū-zhōu Huáng-xī Luó-hàn-sì, the first of his six abbacies and the earliest dated material in the corpus). *notAfter* = **1665** as a conservative bound for the post-1662-decease compilation by Hóng-xiān. The corpus must have been completed within a few years of the 1662 death since lay-patron memory of the master was still vivid.

**Six abbacies, six *yǔ-lù*.** The structural arrangement of juan 1–3 explicitly partitions the *yǔ-lù* by abbacy: **Huáng-xī Luó-hàn 1648 → Cí-jì 1649 → Fǎ-hǎi 1650 → Yuè-lín 1651 → Lóng-yuān Jǐng-dé 1654 → Fú-róng 1656**. The Fú-róng-sì at **Jīng-xī** (Yí-xīng region) was the death-seat and the source of the title-form 芙蓉自閒.

**Lineage.** Pòshān → Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Lín-yě Tōng-qí → Zì-xián Xíng-jué — i.e., one Sì-chuān-born offshoot of the Mì-yún main line. The 33 named *fǎ-sì* across six abbacies indicates a substantial preaching network. Notable heirs: **Tiān-mù Zhì** 天目智 (a third-generation founder), **Cí-lǎng Jiù** 慈朗鷲, **Hàn-guān Yù** 漢關喻, **Hān-yú Xiān** (this volume's compiler).

**Cross-reference with [[KR6q0464]].** The pilgrim **Zǐ-zhōu Chuán** [[船]] 梓舟超船 visited *Sān-tǎ jiàn Zì-xián* 三塔見自閒 in his 1645 pilgrimage circuit (*Sān-tǎ* perhaps an early-1640s pre-abbacy seat); confirms Xíng-jué's recognition as a notable teaching master well before his formal 1648 abbacy at Luó-hàn-sì.

## Other points of interest

- **Zhè-jiāng / Jiāng-sū six-abbacy circuit.** The 1648–1656 abbacy-sequence traces a coherent **Jiāng-nán mendicant circuit** through the Sū-zhōu / Níng-bō / Jiā-xīng / Yí-xīng cluster — distinct from the Sì-chuān or Fú-jiàn-Yún-nán Pòshān-sub-line patterns and reflective of Lín-yě Tōng-qí's Tōng-xuán-based Zhè-jiāng establishment.
