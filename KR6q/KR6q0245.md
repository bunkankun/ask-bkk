# Zōng mén niān gǔ huì jí 宗門拈古彙集

*Complete Collection of Sectarian-Gate Comments-on-Ancient-Cases* — a forty-five-juan Qīng Chán *niān gǔ* (prose-commentary on classical cases) comprehensive anthology, compiled by the Línjì-lineage master [[淨符|Wèi-zhōng Jìng-fú]] 位中淨符 (also Bái-yán Wèi-zhōng 白巖位中). Parallel to [[KR6q0243]] *Lián zhū tōng jí* but gathering *niān gǔ* 拈古 (prose-commentary) rather than *sòng gǔ* 頌古 (verse-commentary).

## About the work

A massive forty-five-juan Chán prose-commentary anthology, X66 n1296. Non-commentary on a single parent text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Structure: for each classical Chán *gōng'àn* case, the text presents the case followed by successive prose-commentary responses (*niān* 拈 "raising-up" — brief interpretive remarks) by various Chán masters across the Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng tradition. The 45-juan scope makes it one of the most comprehensive prose-commentary anthologies in the Chinese Chán canonical tradition.

### Abstract

[[淨符|Wèi-zhōng Jìng-fú]] 位中淨符 (DILA A001108): Qīng Línjì-lineage Chán master. *Hào* Wèi-zhōng 位中 ("Within-Position"), Bái-yán 白巖 ("White Cliff"), Ǒu dàorén 偶道人 ("Coincidental Daoist"). Native of Lú-líng 廬陵 (Jiāng-xī). Lifedates unrecorded; active late 17th to early 18th century.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1660 (Jìng-fú's mature productive period); *notAfter* c. 1710 (compilation-horizon).
