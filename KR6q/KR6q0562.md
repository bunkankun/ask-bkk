# Líng-jī chán-shī yǔ-lù 靈機禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Líng-jī*
by [[行觀]] (說), [[寂方]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zī-fú Líng-jī Xíng-guān** [[行觀]] 資福靈機行觀 (**1616-03-10 – 1681-11-08**, age 66, *sēng-là* 49), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661). Native of **Lóng-xī** 龍溪 (Fú-jiàn), lay surname **Zhōu** 周. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-qióng Jì-fāng** [[寂方]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at two abbacies: **Hú-zhōu Wǔ-kāng Xiáng-fèng-shān Xiǎn-zhōng Chóng-xiào Zī-fú chán-sì** 湖州武康翔鳳山顯忠崇孝資福禪寺 (**1654** abbacy / **1644** initial residence — the principal seat) and **Jiā-xīng-fǔ Shí-mén-xiàn Fú-yán chán-sì** 嘉興府石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙辛亥** = **1671**). *notBefore* = **1654** (formal abbacy onset); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (the canon-edition is a *zhòng-kè* 重刻 reissue with corrected materials, likely a couple of decades after the master's 1681 death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B448.

**Contents.** Juan 1: three prefaces (one labeled *zhòng-kè yuán-gǎo xù* 重刻原稿序, another *kè-zhèng yuán-gǎo xù* 刻正原稿序); *mù-lù*; *Zī-fú-sì yǔ-lù*; *Fú-yán-sì yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*; *shì-zhòng*. Juan 2: *jī-yuán*; *chuí-wèn wǔ zé* 垂問五則 (five suspended questions). Juan 3: *kān-biàn*; *zǎo-cān* 早參 (morning interviews); *wǎn-cān* 晚參 (evening interviews). Juan 4: *yuán-liú-sòng* 源流頌 (verses on the lineage stream). Juan 5: *sòng-gǔ*; *shū-wèn* (epistolary, including 復海鹽張禹侯居士書). Juan 6: closing materials including the master's *xíng-zhuàng* 行狀 (the principal source for his life) and *tǎ-míng*.

## Other points of interest

The double-preface format (*zhòng-kè* + *kè-zhèng*) suggests an iterative editorial history: an initial publication during the master's lifetime, then a posthumous corrected reissue. The 源流頌 in juan 4 — "verses on the lineage stream" — is an unusual genre marker, presenting a verse-form patriarchal genealogy in continuation of his teacher Fèi-yǐn's program of explicit Línjì-lineage articulation (cf. *Fèi-yǐn chán-shī jì-zǔ dào yǐng* 費隱禪師繼祖道影).
