# Tiānwáng Shuǐjiàn Hǎi héshàng zhù Jīnsù yǔlù 天王水鑑海和尚住金粟語錄
*Record of Venerable Shuǐ-jiàn Hǎi of Tiān-wáng[-sì] during his Jīn-sù abbacy*
by [[慧海]] (說), [[原澂]] (等編), [[原上]] (等編)

## About the work

Three-juan **Jīn-sù-specific** *yǔlù* of **Shuǐjiàn Huìhǎi** [[慧海]] 水鑑慧海 (1626–1687, cf. [[慧海]] second entry and [[KR6q0440]]), covering his **Hǎiyán Jīnsùshān Guǎnghuì Chánsì** 金粟山廣慧禪寺 abbacy (entered **丙辰 1676** on Zhèjiāng *fāntái* **Lǐ Shìzhēn** 李士楨 patronage). This is the **sixth abbacy** that was added to the [[KR6q0440]] six-assembly integrated *yǔlù* in 1690; the present volume [[KR6q0442]] is the standalone Jīn-sù-block, with additional biographical appendix (**行狀** 行狀 + **傳** 傳) preserved in juan 2. Compiled by dharma-heirs [[原澂|Fǎlán Yuánchéng]] 原澂 and **Yuánshàng** [[原上]] 原上. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B232.

### Abstract

**Context.** This volume is the **Jīnsù abbacy standalone record** for Huìhǎi, preserving the *shàngtáng* corpus from his 1676+ residence at the Mìyún ancestral seat **Hǎiyán Jīnsù Guǎnghuìsì**. Together with [[KR6q0440]] (10-juan integrated 1690 edition covering all six abbacies) and [[KR6q0441]] (6-juan 1672 five-abbacy edition), [[KR6q0442]] completes the **three-volume Huìhǎi corpus preserved in Jiāxīng Canon J29 (B230–B232)**. The sequential numbering of the three volumes reflects the editorial architecture: B230 = full integrated; B231 = 1672 partial; B232 = Jīn-sù-standalone supplement.

**Significance of the Jīnsù abbacy.** Huìhǎi's **1676 entry at Jīnsùshān Guǎnghuìsì** is historically significant as a **Kāng-xī-era Mìyún Yuánwù ancestral-seat recovery**. Jīnsù had been the primary abbacy of **Mìyún Yuánwù** in the 1630s, subsequently held by **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** (cf. [[KR6q0403]]) in the 1640s, and by **Bǎichī Yuán** (cf. [[KR6q0421]]) 1648–1653. Huìhǎi's 1676 tenure continues the Mìyún-ancestral-seat tradition, with the Qīng Zhèjiāng *fāntái* **Lǐ Shìzhēn** 李士楨 directly sponsoring the abbacy-entry through a formal *chuánxí* 傳檄 administrative directive.

**Compositional history.** The standalone Jīnsù cutting is likely contemporaneous with or slightly pre-dates the 1690 integrated [[KR6q0440]] edition. *notBefore* = **1676** (Jīnsù entry); *notAfter* = **1690** (with Huìhǎi's 1687 death as a firmer earlier limit for the cutting).

**Contents by juan.** (j.1) **Jīnsù shàngtáng** 金粟上堂 corpus; (j.2) **行狀** 行狀 + **傳** 傳 — the principal biographical appendix for Huìhǎi (not preserved in [[KR6q0440]] or [[KR6q0441]]); (j.3) **jì** 記 + **chǐdú** 尺牘 + **xiàngzàn** 象贊 + **zázhù** 雾著 + **zájì** 雜偈 + **fóshì** 佛事.

## Other points of interest

- **Three-volume Huìhǎi corpus architecture.** The Jiāxīng Canon preservation of three sequential volumes for Huìhǎi (B230 integrated / B231 five-abbacy / B232 Jīn-sù-standalone) with the biographical appendix specifically in the last volume, is an editorially-sophisticated layered-preservation pattern. Anyone working on Huìhǎi's biography should consult all three volumes in sequence: [[KR6q0442]] juan 2 for the *xíngzhuàng* + *zhuàn* biographical base, then [[KR6q0440]]'s integrated view, then [[KR6q0441]] for the 1672 intermediate.
- **Lǐ Shìzhēn and Jīnsù.** The 1676 imperial-administrative sponsorship of Huìhǎi's Jīnsù entry by **Lǐ Shìzhēn** 李士楨 (1619–1694) is the same directive that produced the 1676 Jiāxīng Canon submission of Huìhǎi's 10-juan *yǔlù*. Lǐ Shìzhēn's dual role as **abbacy-sponsor + *yǔlù* cutting-patron** for the same master-figure in the same year is a distinctive Qīng-era Buddhist-administrative moment.
