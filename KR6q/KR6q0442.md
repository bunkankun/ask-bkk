# Tiān-wáng Shuǐ-jiàn Hǎi héshàng zhù Jīn-sù yǔ-lù 天王水鑑海和尚住金粟語錄
*Record of Venerable Shuǐ-jiàn Hǎi of Tiān-wáng[-sì] during his Jīn-sù abbacy*
by [[慧海]] (說), [[原澂]] (等編), [[原上]] (等編)

## About the work

Three-juan **Jīn-sù-specific** *yǔlù* of **Shuǐ-jiàn Huì-hǎi** [[慧海]] 水鑑慧海 (1626–1687, cf. [[慧海]] second entry and [[KR6q0440]]), covering his **Hǎi-yán Jīn-sù-shān Guǎng-huì Chán-sì** 金粟山廣慧禪寺 abbacy (entered **丙辰 1676** on Zhè-jiāng *fān-tái* **Lǐ Shì-zhēn** 李士楨 patronage). This is the **sixth abbacy** that was added to the [[KR6q0440]] six-assembly integrated *yǔlù* in 1690; the present volume [[KR6q0442]] is the standalone Jīn-sù-block, with additional biographical appendix (**行狀** 行狀 + **傳** 傳) preserved in juan 2. Compiled by dharma-heirs [[原澂|Fǎ-lán Yuán-chéng]] 原澂 and **Yuán-shàng** [[原上]] 原上. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B232.

### Abstract

**Context.** This volume is the **Jīn-sù abbacy standalone record** for Huì-hǎi, preserving the *shàng-táng* corpus from his 1676+ residence at the Mìyún ancestral seat **Hǎi-yán Jīn-sù Guǎng-huì-sì**. Together with [[KR6q0440]] (10-juan integrated 1690 edition covering all six abbacies) and [[KR6q0441]] (6-juan 1672 five-abbacy edition), [[KR6q0442]] completes the **three-volume Huì-hǎi corpus preserved in Jiā-xīng Canon J29 (B230–B232)**. The sequential numbering of the three volumes reflects the editorial architecture: B230 = full integrated; B231 = 1672 partial; B232 = Jīn-sù-standalone supplement.

**Significance of the Jīn-sù abbacy.** Huì-hǎi's **1676 entry at Jīn-sù-shān Guǎng-huì-sì** is historically significant as a **Kāng-xī-era Mìyún Yuán-wù ancestral-seat recovery**. Jīn-sù had been the primary abbacy of **Mìyún Yuán-wù** in the 1630s, subsequently held by **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** (cf. [[KR6q0403]]) in the 1640s, and by **Bǎi-chī Yuán** (cf. [[KR6q0421]]) 1648–1653. Huì-hǎi's 1676 tenure continues the Mìyún-ancestral-seat tradition, with the Qīng Zhè-jiāng *fān-tái* **Lǐ Shì-zhēn** 李士楨 directly sponsoring the abbacy-entry through a formal *chuán-xí* 傳檄 administrative directive.

**Compositional history.** The standalone Jīn-sù cutting is likely contemporaneous with or slightly pre-dates the 1690 integrated [[KR6q0440]] edition. *notBefore* = **1676** (Jīn-sù entry); *notAfter* = **1690** (with Huì-hǎi's 1687 death as a firmer earlier limit for the cutting).

**Contents by juan.** (j.1) **Jīn-sù shàng-táng** 金粟上堂 corpus; (j.2) **行狀** 行狀 + **傳** 傳 — the principal biographical appendix for Huì-hǎi (not preserved in [[KR6q0440]] or [[KR6q0441]]); (j.3) **jì** 記 + **chǐ-dú** 尺牘 + **xiàng-zàn** 象贊 + **zá-zhù** 雾著 + **zá-jì** 雜偈 + **fó-shì** 佛事.

## Other points of interest

- **Three-volume Huì-hǎi corpus architecture.** The Jiā-xīng Canon preservation of three sequential volumes for Huì-hǎi (B230 integrated / B231 five-abbacy / B232 Jīn-sù-standalone) with the biographical appendix specifically in the last volume, is an editorially-sophisticated layered-preservation pattern. Anyone working on Huì-hǎi's biography should consult all three volumes in sequence: [[KR6q0442]] juan 2 for the *xíng-zhuàng* + *zhuàn* biographical base, then [[KR6q0440]]'s integrated view, then [[KR6q0441]] for the 1672 intermediate.
- **Lǐ Shì-zhēn and Jīn-sù.** The 1676 imperial-administrative sponsorship of Huì-hǎi's Jīn-sù entry by **Lǐ Shì-zhēn** 李士楨 (1619–1694) is the same directive that produced the 1676 Jiā-xīng Canon submission of Huì-hǎi's 10-juan *yǔlù*. Lǐ Shì-zhēn's dual role as **abbacy-sponsor + *yǔlù* cutting-patron** for the same master-figure in the same year is a distinctive Qīng-era Buddhist-administrative moment.
