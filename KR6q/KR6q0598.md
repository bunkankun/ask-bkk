# Wàn-fēng hé-shàng yǔ-lù 萬峰和尚語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Master Wàn-fēng*
by [[時蔚]] (說), [[普壽]] (集)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Wàn-fēng Shí-wèi** [[時蔚]] 萬峰時蔚 (**1303-10-18 – 1381-03-03**, age 79), Yuán-Míng-transition Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Qiān-yán Yuán-cháng** 千巖元長 (1284–1357). Native of **Lè-qīng** 樂清 (Zhèjiāng), lay surname **Jīn** 金. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Pǔ-shòu** [[普壽]] *jí*.

The *yǔ-lù* documents Shí-wèi's thirty-year career at **Sū-zhōu Dèng-wèi-shān Shèng-ēn-sì** 蘇州鄧蔚山聖恩寺 (commonly called **Wàn-fēng** 萬峰), the late-Yuán / early-Míng Línjì-Yángqí center in the Sū-zhōu region. *notBefore* = ca. **1351** (post-Qiān-yán-acquaintance opening of *fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1381** (death). Catalog dynasty 明 — but Shí-wèi's career began in the Yuán and the canonical edition is a Míng reprint. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B492.

**Contents.** Single juan: *kān ... jiù-xù* 刊...舊序 ("preface to the reprint of the old edition"); *Běn-jìng jū-shì rù-shān qiú-yǔ shì-zhòng* (instruction to lay disciple Běn-jìng coming up the mountain to seek the dharma); *sòng-gǔ*; an extensive *fǎ-yǔ* section addressed by name to many Yuán-Míng-transition Chán contemporaries — **Xuě-shān Xī chán-zhǎng** 雪山熙長老, **Yǎng-shān Wú-niàn Xué shǒu-zuò** 仰山無念學首座 (his fǎ-sì), **Rì-zhào Huì shǒu-zuò** 日照慧首座, **Bǎo-fēng Zhēn shǒu-zuò** 寶峰真首座, **Hǔ-qiū Yuán shǒu-zuò** 虎丘圓首座, plus image-encomia and other materials.

## Translations and research

- 蕭志濱 etc.: studies on the **Qiān-yán Yuán-cháng** → **Wàn-fēng Shí-wèi** Yuán-Míng-transition lineage have been done in Chinese-language scholarship. The Wàn-fēng line is one of the foundational continuities from late-Yuán **Hǔ-pǎo** Línjì into the Míng-Qīng **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** revival, since Mì-yún himself traced lineage back to Wàn-fēng.

## Other points of interest

The *yǔ-lù* is significant within the Jiā-xīng Canon as one of the few Yuán-Míng-transition Línjì-Yángqí *yǔ-lù* preserved alongside the predominantly post-1644 cluster. **Wàn-fēng Shí-wèi** is the great-grandfather (in the lineage sense) of **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 (1566–1642) — the foundational figure of the late-Míng Línjì revival; the unbroken line **Qiān-yán Yuán-cháng → Wàn-fēng Shí-wèi → Bǎo-cáng Pǔ-chí → Dōng-míng Huì-jǎn → Hǎi-zhōu Cí → Bào-ēn Jìn → Tiān-qí Běn-ruì → Wú-wén Cōng → Xiào-yán Dé-bǎo** continues to Mì-yún and then through the entire late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí establishment whose *yǔ-lù* fills J27–J40 of the Jiā-xīng Canon.
