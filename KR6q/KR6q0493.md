# Yún-huàn Chén chán-shī yǔ-lù 雲幻宸禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yún-huàn Chén*
by [[印宸]] (說), [[發林]] (等編), [[王民皞]] (序), [[李道泰]] (塔記)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yún-huàn Yìn-chén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623 – **1669-05-15**, *shì-shòu* 47), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Héng-xiù Fā-lín** [[發林]]. Front-preface by **Wáng Mín-hào** [[王民皞]] (古臨弟), dated **康熙癸丑春 = 1673 spring** at Yún-nán Ā-mí. *Tǎ-jì* by **Lǐ Dào-tài** [[李道泰]]. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B353.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān). Sole abbacy at **Bā-zhōu Lóng-tán Zhōng-fēng Chán-sì** 巴州龍潭中峰禪寺 from **順治戊戌 = 1658** (entry-sermon).

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1658** (Lóng-tán abbacy entry). *notAfter* = **1673** (Wáng Mín-hào preface, four years post-decease).

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎi-míng → Yún-huàn Yìn-chén → Héng-xiù Fā-lín. One of Pòshān's many heirs of Sì-chuān provenance.
