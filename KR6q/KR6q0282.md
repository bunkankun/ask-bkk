# Cháng-líng Shǒu-zhuó chánshī yǔlù 長靈守卓禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Cháng-líng Shǒu-zhuó* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng / Southern-Sòng transitional Chán *yǔlù* of **Cháng-líng Shǒu-zhuó** 長靈守卓 (1065–1123), the Línjì-Yángqí master at Cháng-líng 長靈. Compiled by his dharma-heir [[介諶|Jiè-chén]] 介諶.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1347. Shǒu-zhuó's dates: 1065–1123; dharma-heir of Bǎo-fēng Kè-wén 寶峰克文. See [[KR6q0282]] for catalog position.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100; *notAfter* c. 1160 (compilation-horizon).
