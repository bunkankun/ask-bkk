# Pǔ-guāng Tǎn-ān chán-shī yǔ-lù 普光坦菴禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Pǔ-guāng Tǎn-ān*
by [[機峻]] (說), [[全弘]] (錄), [[羅開麟]] (序)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tǎn-ān Jī-jùn** [[機峻]] 坦菴機峻, **32nd-generation Línjì** dharma-heir of **Píng-shān Cì-ān Zhēn-běn** [[真本]] 瓶山次菴真本 (= 項謙, the Gǔ-píng-shān 古瓶山 *Mù-dào-zhě* 牧道者) at his **Pǔ-guāng-sì** 普光寺 abbacy in **Yǒng-fēng** 永豐 (Jiā-xīng prefecture) from **1670/5/19**. Compiled by *shì-zhě* attendant **Quán-hóng** [[全弘]]. Front-matter: (i) preface by **Kōng-zhū Luó Zhēn-xìng** 空諸羅真性, dated **康熙庚戌孟秋丙辰 = 1670/8/15 Gregorian** (the catalog meta credits a separate *Luó Kāi-lín* [[羅開麟]] — possibly a parallel signature, see below); (ii) three *qǐng-qǐ* 請啟 invitation-memorials by lay-disciple groups: (a) *Jiā-xīng zhū-xiāng-shēn qǐng-qǐ* (22 named patrons including 張天植 and 曹溶); (b) *zhòng wén-xué qǐng-qǐ* (29 named patrons including [[施博]] Shī Bó and *Luó Kāi-lín*); (c) *Pǔ-guāng qǐng-qǐ*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B247.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* / *notAfter* = **1670**. The first dated event is **康熙庚戌 5/19 = 1670/6/27 Gregorian** when Jī-jùn received the lay-patron invitation at **Jīng-yán-sì** 精嚴寺. Formal entry to Pǔ-guāng-sì on **6/6 = 1670/7/13**. The Luó Zhēn-xìng front-preface is dated **1670/8/15**; the volume was therefore cut within the calendar year of the abbacy-installation. The compact one-juan extent reflects that the *yǔ-lù* records only the opening months of Jī-jùn's Pǔ-guāng tenure.

**Lineage.** The juan-1 entry-sermon's incense-offering verse names *Lín-jì zhèng-chuán sān-shí-èr shì Píng-shān-táng shàng Cì-ān běn-shī lǎo-rén* 臨濟正傳三十二世瓶山堂上次菴本師老人 — confirming Jī-jùn's status as a 32nd-generation Línjì heir of **Cì-ān Zhēn-běn** at Píng-shān, in the **Hǔ-qiū → Wú-zhǔn Shī-fàn → Duàn-qiáo Mìao-lún → Jìng-shān Chōng → Xìng-shàn Guǎng → Pǔ-míng Yòng → Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo → Píng-shān Cì-ān** sub-line. The Luó Zhēn-xìng preface confirms this transmission as *zuì-hòu zhì Píng-shān mén-xià, shuǐ-rǔ xiāng-tóu, suì shòu jì-bié* 最後至瓶山門下水乳相投遂受記莂 — i.e., that Jī-jùn had consulted multiple teachers before receiving formal certification at Píng-shān.

**Background.** Per the *zhū-xiāng-shēn* invitation: native of **Jīn-líng** 金陵 (Nán-jīng) — *Jīn-líng yù-xiù, Zhōng-fù chéng-xiáng* 金陵毓秀鍾阜呈祥. Per the *Pǔ-guāng* invitation: previously preached in **Hǎi-mǐn** 海閩 / **Zhāng-nán** 漳南 — i.e., the Fú-jiàn coastal region, with abbacies near *Cāng-lóng-jī* 蒼龍磯 (in Zhāng-zhōu prefecture). The Jiā-xīng invitation thus represents an eastward / northward migration from the Fú-jiàn ministry to a Hǔ-qiū-school heartland in Zhè-jiāng.

**Lay-patron rosters.** The three invitation-memorials together name about sixty Jiā-xīng lay-Chán patrons, including: **Zhāng Tiān-zhí** 張天植, **Cáo Róng** 曹溶, **Wáng Tíng** 王庭, **Zhū Mào-shí** 朱茂時 (in the *xiāng-shēn* roster), and [[施博]] **Shī Bó** (the same preface-writer of [[KR6q0432]] / [[KR6q0433]] / [[KR6q0443]] / [[KR6q0444]] — confirming Shī Bó's continued lay-Chán activity in 1670 Jiā-xīng). The *Luó Kāi-lín* / 羅開驎 of the *zhòng wén-xué* roster is presumably the *xù* author credited in the catalog meta.

**Catalog-vs-source tension.** Catalog meta gives 羅開麟 as preface-writer (序); the surviving signed preface in the source-text is by **Luó Zhēn-xìng**. Both share the surname **Luó** 羅 and may belong to a single Jiā-xīng Luó family-circle of lay-disciples; Luó Kāi-lín is one named signer of the *zhòng wén-xué* invitation, suggesting a kinsman-of-the-preface-writer rather than the preface-writer himself.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J29 B247). The Luó Zhēn-xìng preface, the three *qǐng-qǐ* invitation-memorials with their lay-patron rosters, and the entry-sermon dating-notice provide the principal documentation.*

## Other points of interest

- **Píng-shān Zhēn-běn lineage extension.** Where [[KR6q0202]] preserves Zhēn-běn's own *Mù-dào-zhě* sermons and [[KR6q0455]] preserves a parallel Pǔ-míng-Yòng heir's *yǔ-lù*, this volume is the documentary witness to **Zhēn-běn as transmission-grandfather** to the next *jī*-generation through Jī-jùn at Pǔ-guāng. It is, with [[KR6q0455]], the second of two extant *jí*-generation works in the Hǔ-qiū → Pǔ-míng-Yòng line preserved in the Jiā-xīng Canon.
- **Sixty-patron Jiā-xīng lay roster.** The combined *qǐng-qǐ* signatures preserve a uniquely detailed snapshot of the **1670 Jiā-xīng lay-Chán patronage network**, including senior officials (Zhāng Tiān-zhí, Cáo Róng), local *xiāng-shēn*, and the published-author lay disciple [[施博]] Shī Bó in his Jiā-xīng activity post-dating his earlier Cáo-dòng-line preface-work.
