# Jìng shí dī rǔ jí 徑石滴乳集

*Dripping-Milk Collection from Jìng-shí* — a five-juan Qīng Chán-lineage biographical anthology, compiled by the Línjì-Yángqí master [[真在|Shān-duó Zhēn-zài]] 山鐸真在 (b. 1621), with subsequent continuation (*chóng xù* 重續) by [[機雲|Jī-yún]] 機雲. The title's *Jìng-shí* 徑石 is a compound name for Jìng-shān 徑山 and Shí-lín 石林 — the two principal Southern-Sòng Chán monasteries in the Hángzhōu region.

## About the work

A five-juan Chán-lineage biographical-anthology, X67 n1308. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text's *dī rǔ* 滴乳 ("dripping milk") title evokes the classical Buddhist image of nurturing dharma-transmission as mother's milk nourishing offspring. The collection gathers biographical-narrative and teaching-excerpt material from multiple generations of Chán masters, focused on the Hángzhōu-area Jìng-shān / Shí-lín lineage-traditions.

### Abstract

[[真在|Shān-duó Zhēn-zài]] 山鐸真在 (DILA A000945, b. 1621/12/31 per his *yǔlù* juan 1 *xíng zhuàng*): Qīng Línjì-Yángqí Chán master. *Hào* Shān-duó 山鐸 ("Mountain-Bell"), Yún-ān 筠庵.

**Continuer [[機雲|Jī-yún]]** 機雲: later compiler of additional entries; lifedates unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1670 (Zhēn-zài's mature productive period); *notAfter* c. 1720 (Jī-yún's continuation horizon).
