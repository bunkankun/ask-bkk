# Yún-fù Zhì chán-shī yǔ-lù 雲腹智禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yún-fù Zhì*
by [[道智]] (說), [[嶽賢]] (記錄), [[聯昇]] (記錄)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yún-fù Dào-zhì** [[道智]] 雲腹道智 (**1613 – 1673-07-13**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiàng-yái Xìng-tǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (1598–1651) in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Yuè-xián** [[嶽賢]] + **Lián-shēng** [[聯昇]]. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B309. Juan 2 includes the *xíng-zhuàng* preserving birth and death dates.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[道智]] note: native of **Qú-xiàn** 渠縣 (Shùn-qìng, Sì-chuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Conception preceded by mother's dream of a *yì-sēng tuō-bō* 異僧托缽 — a standard *xìng-shēng* topos. Born 1613, died 1673 *zuò-tuō* 端坐而逝.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1650** (post-transmission preaching, after his teacher Xìng-tǐng died 1651). *notAfter* = **1673** (death). 

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎi-míng → Xiàng-yái Xìng-tǐng → Yún-fù Dào-zhì. Heirs: **Huì-yě Yì-xǐng** 會也益惺; **Huì-yuè Yì-yǎn** 慧月益眼.
