# Zhè-ān Fàn chán-shī yǔ-lù 蔗菴範禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhè-ān Fàn*
by [[淨範]] (說), [[智璋]] (等錄)

Thirty-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Wéi-zé / Zhè-ān Jìng-fàn** [[淨範]] 蔗菴淨範 (**1620 – 1692**, *shì-shòu* 73), Cáo-dòng-school principal dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665). Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-zhāng** [[智璋]] *děng lù*. The 30-juan extent reflects the long c. 1649–1692 preaching career across five major Zhè-jiāng Cáo-dòng abbacies. Juan 30 *zì-xù*. Stands alongside [[KR6q0459]], [[KR6q0471]], [[KR6q0472]] as the **fourth and most extensive** Sān-yí Míng-yú-line *yǔ-lù* in the Jiā-xīng Canon — Jìng-fàn was first-listed among Míng-yú's thirty heirs and lead compiler of his master's [[KR6q0411]] *yǔ-lù*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B369.
