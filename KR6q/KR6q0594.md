# Jiàn-táng Yī chán-shī yǔ-lù 鑑堂一禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jiàn-táng Yī*
by [[一]] (說), [[明滿]] (等錄), [[明慧]] (等錄), [[杜允貞]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Jiàn-táng Yī** [[一|Jiàn-táng Yī]] 鑑堂一 (lifedates unknown), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] (1639–1707, cf. [[KR6q0592]]) — **Nán-yuè 31st-generation** Línjì descendant. Recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Míng-mǎn** [[明滿]] and **Míng-huì** [[明慧]] *děng lù*; preface by lay scholar **Dù Yǔn-zhēn** [[杜允貞]]. Documents teaching at **Cháng-lè chán-sì** 常樂禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1700** (post-Huàn-mǐn-late-period *fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1730**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B486.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *mù-cì*; *Cháng-lè-sì shàng-táng*; *shì-zhòng*; *xiǎo-cān*; *fó-shì* (a substantial cremation/burial sequence — *rù-tǎ*, *yìng sān-jiǔ*, **體如和尚入塔** for *Tǐ-rú hé-shàng*, **勝泉禪人舉火** for Shèng-quán; *Jiàn-chū dà-dé* sealing/cremation; **性心尼下火** *Xìng-xīn-nǐ* — a rare cremation-record for a nun; **真敬沙彌起龕** *Zhēn-jìng shā-mí*).

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J40 B486).*

## Other points of interest

This third-generation **Jù-yún 聚雲** lineage *yǔ-lù* (Tiě-bì → Huàn-mǐn → Jiàn-táng Yī) extends the Sì-chuān Línjì-Dà-huì record into the early 18th century. The *fó-shì* section records mortuary rituals for fellow monks including the unusual **性心尼下火** (cremation of nun Xìng-xīn) — indicating Jiàn-táng's monastery hosted both monks and nuns.
