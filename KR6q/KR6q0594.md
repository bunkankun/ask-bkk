# Jiàntáng Yī chánshī yǔlù 鑑堂一禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jiàn-táng Yī*
by [[一]] (說), [[明滿]] (等錄), [[明慧]] (等錄), [[杜允貞]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Jiàntáng Yī** [[一|Jiàntáng Yī]] 鑑堂一 (lifedates unknown), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]] (1639–1707, cf. [[KR6q0592]]) — **Nányuè 31st-generation** Línjì descendant. Recorded by his *fǎsì* **Míngmǎn** [[明滿]] and **Mínghuì** [[明慧]] *děng lù*; preface by lay scholar **Dù Yǔnzhēn** [[杜允貞]]. Documents teaching at **Chánglè chánsì** 常樂禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1700** (post-Huàn-mǐn-late-period *fùfǎ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1730**. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J40 B486.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *mùcì*; *Chánglèsì shàngtáng*; *shìzhòng*; *xiǎocān*; *fóshì* (a substantial cremation/burial sequence — *rùtǎ*, *yìng sānjiǔ*, **體如和尚入塔** for *Tǐrú héshàng*, **勝泉禪人舉火** for Shèngquán; *Jiànchū dàdé* sealing/cremation; **性心尼下火** *Xìngxīnnǐ* — a rare cremation-record for a nun; **真敬沙彌起龕** *Zhēnjìng shāmí*).

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J40 B486).*

## Other points of interest

This third-generation **Jùyún 聚雲** lineage *yǔlù* (Tiěbì → Huànmǐn → Jiàntáng Yī) extends the Sìchuān LínjìDàhuì record into the early 18th century. The *fóshì* section records mortuary rituals for fellow monks including the unusual **性心尼下火** (cremation of nun Xìngxīn) — indicating Jiàntáng's monastery hosted both monks and nuns.
