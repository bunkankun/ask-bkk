# Bié-mù Chún chán-shī yǔ-lù 別牧純禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bié-mù Chún*
by [[純]] (說), [[福月]] (等編), [[慧輅]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Bié-mù Chún** [[純]] 別牧純 (b. **1638-07-01**, alive at age 60 in **1697**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jìng-tíng Dé-zōng** [[德宗]] (1621–1684, cf. [[KR6q0587]]) at **Nán-yuán Hóng-shèng-sì** 南源洪聖寺 in Húnán. Native of **Sān-wú** 三吾 (Húnán), lay name **Lǐ Shàn-bǎo** 李善寶. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Fú-yuè** [[福月]] *děng biān*. Preface by **Dì-huī Huì-lù** [[慧輅]] (1627–1725, the very long-lived Líng-yǐn-affiliated lay scholar-monk).

Documents teaching at two abbacies: **Quán-zhōu Nán-yuán Hóng-shèng-sì** 全州南源洪聖寺 (succeeded his teacher); **Sān-wú Cáo-shān Jí-shèng-sì** 三吾曹山集聖寺. *notBefore* = **康熙丁巳** = **1677** (*fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1697** (last attested year). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B476.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *Nán-yuán-sì yǔ-lù*; *Cáo-shān Jí-shèng-sì yǔ-lù*; *fēn-zuò bǐng-fú* 分座秉拂; *jī-yuán*; *fó-shì*; *sòng-gǔ*; *xíng-shí* (the principal source for his life — including the cat-cry breakthrough at Yí-shān).

## Other points of interest

KR6q0587 + KR6q0588 form a complete teacher-disciple sequence within the **Yí-shān → Nán-yuán** Húnán Línjì-Yángqí line. The *Nán-yuán-sì* foundation in 1662 and its joint construction by master and student is recorded both here and in [[德宗]]'s *yǔ-lù*.
