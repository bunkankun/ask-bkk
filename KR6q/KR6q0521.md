# Zhōng-nán-shān Pán-lóng Zǐ-sù chán-shī yǔ-lù 終南山蟠龍子肅禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zǐ-sù of Pán-lóng-sì on Zhōng-nán-shān*
by [[超遠]] (說), [[性明]] (編), [[海闊]] (等錄)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zǐ-sù Chāo-yuǎn** [[超遠]] 子肅超遠 (**1631-11-11 – 1689-12-15**, age 59), Línjì-Yángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Wén-ruò Xíng-yíng** 文弱行盈 (Lóng-chí line). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-míng** [[性明]] *biān* + **Hǎi-kuò** [[海闊]] *děng lù*. Sole abbacy at **Huá-zhōu Zhōng-nán-shān Pán-lóng-sì** 華州終南山蟠龍寺 from **1672/8/19**. *notBefore* = **1672** (abbacy entry), *notAfter* = **1689** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B396, also preserved in 卍續藏.
