# Bù-huì chán-shī yǔ-lù 不會禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bù-huì*
by [[法通]] (說), [[性靈]] (等編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Bù-huì Fǎ-tōng** [[法通]] 不會法通 (b. **1621-12-04**, DILA-standardized as **Tōng-fǎ** 通法), Línjì-Yángqí Pòshān-line dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-líng** [[性靈]] *děng*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J32 B276. Contents: front-matter (*xù*; *kāi-táng shū* 開堂疏); juan 1–3 *shàng-táng*; juan 4–5 *xiǎo-cān* + *fǎ-yǔ*; juan 6–7 with *xíng-shí* 行實 closing the latter; juan 8–10 *jì*, *jǔ-gǔ*, *sòng-gǔ*, etc.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the DILA authority A019261 and the juan-7 *xíng-shí*: native of **Nán-chōng** 南充 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Began Chán-training under **Tiān-yǐn dà-shī** 天隱大師 on the *wàn-fǎ guī-yī* 萬法歸一 *gōng-àn* in his teens. Tonsured at **26** (= **1646**) under **Xīng-chū** 惺初. Trained four years under **Shì-yě Jié hé-shàng** 侍埜詰 in Húběi; pilgrimage to **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** at Jìng-shān, **Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] at Tiān-jiè, **Mù-chén Dào-mín** [[道忞]] at Tiān-tóng. Final transmission from **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** at Shuāng-guì-sì in **1666**, the year of Pòshān's death.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1666** (the Qīng-guǒ retreat-residency). The two opening *kāi-táng shū* invitation-petitions in the front-matter are dated **康熙十年十月初一日** = **1671-11-02 Gregorian** (lay-official roster headed by 潘之彪, 沈燦, 蒲拱宸, 楊鼎, 譚性, 何寧, 王起蛟, 何其偉) and the second by *fǎ-dì-zǐ* lay disciples (陳盛, 陳昌, 陳潛思, 陳起祚, 王登程, 陳起裕). *notAfter* = **1675** as a conservative bound for the post-*kāi-táng* compilation. Bù-huì's death-date is not preserved in DILA but the volume must have been compiled before then.

**Lineage and heirs.** Pòshān → Bù-huì line; principal *fǎ-sì* heirs **Xìng-líng** [[性靈]] (this volume's compiler), **Tōng-dā** 通耷, **Cí-kūn Fǎ-yù** 慈昆法玉, **Qìng-fǎ** 慶法. The Bù-huì sub-line is one of several Pòshān-derived Sì-chuān Línjì sub-lineages, parallel to the [[通醉]] Zhàng-xuě line ([[KR6q0415]], [[KR6q0035]]) and the [[道正]] Lián-yuè line ([[KR6q0445]], [[KR6q0446]]). The 1671 Qīng-guǒ *kāi-táng* (10 years after Bù-huì's 1666 retreat-entry) shows that even his deliberate retreat at Shè-jiāng eventually turned into a major preaching seat under lay-official pressure.

**Cross-school pilgrimage record.** The juan-7 *xíng-shí* preserves a notably detailed cross-school pilgrimage record: from the Línjì-Yáng-qí Mì-yún line (Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng at Jìng-shān) to the Cáo-dòng line (Jué-làng Dào-shèng at Tiān-jiè) to the Mù-chén-line Tiān-tóng (Mù-chén Dào-mín) before the final commitment to the Sì-chuān Pòshān line. This reflects the still-fluid mid-seventeenth-century cross-lineage practice before the firmer post-1660 doctrinal hardening.

## Other points of interest

- **Pòshān's last principal heir.** Bù-huì's transmission in 1666, the year of Pòshān's death, makes him plausibly the last formal Pòshān heir. The detailed *xíng-shí* documenting his cross-school pilgrimage circuit before final Pòshān commitment is a distinctive feature.
- **1671 Qīng-guǒ lay-patron prosopography.** The two *kāi-táng shū* together preserve a roster of about thirteen lay-patrons of the Sì-chuān **Shè-jiāng** region in 1671, useful for prosopography of post-Zhāng-Xiàn-zhōng Sì-chuān Buddhist lay-patronage networks.
