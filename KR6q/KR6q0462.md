# Bùhuì chánshī yǔlù 不會禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bù-huì*
by [[法通]] (說), [[性靈]] (等編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Bùhuì Fǎtōng** [[法通]] 不會法通 (b. **1621-12-04**, DILA-standardized as **Tōngfǎ** 通法), LínjìYángqí Pòshān-line dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Xìnglíng** [[性靈]] *děng*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J32 B276. Contents: front-matter (*xù*; *kāitáng shū* 開堂疏); juan 1–3 *shàngtáng*; juan 4–5 *xiǎocān* + *fǎyǔ*; juan 6–7 with *xíngshí* 行實 closing the latter; juan 8–10 *jì*, *jǔgǔ*, *sònggǔ*, etc.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the DILA authority A019261 and the juan-7 *xíngshí*: native of **Nánchōng** 南充 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Began Chán-training under **Tiānyǐn dàshī** 天隱大師 on the *wànfǎ guīyī* 萬法歸一 *gōngàn* in his teens. Tonsured at **26** (= **1646**) under **Xīngchū** 惺初. Trained four years under **Shìyě Jié héshàng** 侍埜詰 in Húběi; pilgrimage to **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** at Jìngshān, **Juélàng Dàoshèng** [[道盛]] at Tiānjiè, **Mùchén Dàomín** [[道忞]] at Tiāntóng. Final transmission from **Pòshān Hǎimíng** at Shuāngguìsì in **1666**, the year of Pòshān's death.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1666** (the Qīngguǒ retreat-residency). The two opening *kāitáng shū* invitation-petitions in the front-matter are dated **康熙十年十月初一日** = **1671-11-02 Gregorian** (lay-official roster headed by 潘之彪, 沈燦, 蒲拱宸, 楊鼎, 譚性, 何寧, 王起蛟, 何其偉) and the second by *fǎdìzǐ* lay disciples (陳盛, 陳昌, 陳潛思, 陳起祚, 王登程, 陳起裕). *notAfter* = **1675** as a conservative bound for the post-*kāitáng* compilation. Bùhuì's death-date is not preserved in DILA but the volume must have been compiled before then.

**Lineage and heirs.** Pòshān → Bùhuì line; principal *fǎsì* heirs **Xìnglíng** [[性靈]] (this volume's compiler), **Tōngdā** 通耷, **Cíkūn Fǎyù** 慈昆法玉, **Qìngfǎ** 慶法. The Bùhuì sub-line is one of several Pòshān-derived Sìchuān Línjì sub-lineages, parallel to the [[通醉]] Zhàngxuě line ([[KR6q0415]], [[KR6q0035]]) and the [[道正]] Liányuè line ([[KR6q0445]], [[KR6q0446]]). The 1671 Qīngguǒ *kāitáng* (10 years after Bùhuì's 1666 retreat-entry) shows that even his deliberate retreat at Shèjiāng eventually turned into a major preaching seat under lay-official pressure.

**Cross-school pilgrimage record.** The juan-7 *xíngshí* preserves a notably detailed cross-school pilgrimage record: from the LínjìYángqí Mìyún line (Fèiyǐn Tōngróng at Jìngshān) to the Cáodòng line (Juélàng Dàoshèng at Tiānjiè) to the Mù-chén-line Tiāntóng (Mùchén Dàomín) before the final commitment to the Sìchuān Pòshān line. This reflects the still-fluid mid-seventeenth-century cross-lineage practice before the firmer post-1660 doctrinal hardening.

## Other points of interest

- **Pòshān's last principal heir.** Bùhuì's transmission in 1666, the year of Pòshān's death, makes him plausibly the last formal Pòshān heir. The detailed *xíngshí* documenting his cross-school pilgrimage circuit before final Pòshān commitment is a distinctive feature.
- **1671 Qīngguǒ lay-patron prosopography.** The two *kāitáng shū* together preserve a roster of about thirteen lay-patrons of the Sìchuān **Shèjiāng** region in 1671, useful for prosopography of post-Zhāng-Xiàn-zhōng Sìchuān Buddhist lay-patronage networks.
