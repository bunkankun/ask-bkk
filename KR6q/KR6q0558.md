# Yù-méi Liàng chán-shī yǔ-lù 玉眉亮禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yù-méi Liàng*
by [[亮]] (說), [[空謐]] (編), [[張廷勱]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yù-méi Dēng-liàng** [[亮]] 玉眉燈亮 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir primarily of **Ěr-ān Dēng-sōng** 耳菴燈嵩, with secondary transmission also from **Gāo-fēng Sān-shān Dēng-lái** 三山燈來 [[燈來]] (1614–1685). Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān). Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Kōng-mì** [[空謐]] *biān*. Preface by lay disciple **Zhāng Tíng-mài** [[張廷勱]] dated **康熙庚午秋九月上浣** = **1690** lunar 9.

Documents teaching at two abbacies: **Zhōng-zhōu Wǔ-fēng-shān Tiān-níng-sì** 忠州五峰山天寧寺 (entered **康熙八年九月十八日** = **1669-10-23**) and **Wàn-sōng Tiān-tái** 萬松天台. *notBefore* = **1669** (Tiān-níng entry); *notAfter* = **1690** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B443.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *fó-shì* (rich set of mortuary verses for his teacher Sān-shān, his teacher's stupa-cleaning, image-rituals, and others — including a lament for his "uncle" *Fǎn-sù* 返素師伯 on hearing of his death; cremation verses for **Hè-lín dà-dé** 鶴林大德, **Yuán-chū shuò-dé** 圓初碩德, **Zhǎng-ān Zhàn-xiū qí-sù** 長安湛休耆宿, **Jiàn-xuán shuò-tóu** 見玄碩頭); *jì-zàn* (prescribing his disciple **Bīng-xián shǒu-zuò** 冰絃首座 + image-encomium for Bodhidharma); *sòng-gǔ* with *zhuó-niān* (verses with marginal commentary on classic *gōng-àn*); *shī* (poems including *Shàng ēn-shī* 上恩師 — to his teacher).

## Other points of interest

This compact one-juan *yǔ-lù* is significant within the J39 sequence as one of the **Sān-shān Dēng-lái cohort** *yǔ-lù* (alongside [[KR6q0556]], [[KR6q0557]], etc.) — Yù-méi Liàng was the *wéi-nuó* whom Dēng-lái deputized on his deathbed, alongside specific oral-transmission instructions, and the Bīng-xián line continues from there. The strikingly extensive *fó-shì* section (occupying nearly half the juan) reflects Yù-méi's institutional role as Dēng-lái's mortuary-ritual coordinator within the larger Sì-chuān cohort following the master's death in 1685.
