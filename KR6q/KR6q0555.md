# Gāo-fēng Qiáo-sōng Yì chán-shī yǔ-lù 高峰喬松億禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qiáo-sōng Yì of Gāo-fēng [-yuàn]*
by [[玄億]] (說), [[燈洪]] (編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Qiáo-sōng Xuán-yì** [[玄億]] 喬松玄億 (also **Dēng-yì** 燈億 in DILA's primary form) (b. **1619-12-11**, death year unknown — was 51 in 1669), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1608–1663), thus a co-disciple of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] (cf. [[KR6q0554]]). Native of **Lín-shuǐ** 鄰水 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Féng** 馮. Compiled by his *shū-jì* **Dēng-hóng** [[燈洪]] *biān*.

The *yǔ-lù* documents three abbacies, two in Sì-chuān and one east in Húběi: **Zhōng-zhōu Bǎi-yán-sì** 忠州柏巖寺; **Kuí-zhōu-fǔ Liáng-shān-xiàn Gāo-fēng chán-yuàn** 夔州府梁山縣高峰禪院 (the seat giving him his sobriquet); **È-zhōu Dà-bié-shān Wò-yún chán-yuàn** 鄂州大別山臥雲禪院 (Wǔ-hàn area). *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-Qìng-zhōng-flight era of 付法); *notAfter* ≈ **1675** (continuing abbacy at Wò-yún after 1669, plus printing). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B440.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *mù-cì*; *Liáng-shān Gāo-fēng yǔ-lù*; *È-zhōu Dà-bié-shān Wò-yún yǔ-lù*; *fǎ-yǔ* and *jì* including 示寧仙居士, 送大樹禪者行腳, 寄慈祥禪友, 示楊仁寰居士, 示純若禪者 etc., and 山居 mountain-dwelling poems. Juan 2: *xíng-yóu* (autobiographical narration), continuing *jì* and *fó-shì*.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0554 and KR6q0555 are paired witnesses to the **Sì-chuān → Wǔ-Hàn** axis of the Línjì 大慧-school **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line: both Dēng-bǐng (Héng-shān) and Xuán-yì (Qiáo-sōng) were dharma-heirs of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī**, and both eventually moved east into Húběi (Mountain-East Wǔ-Hàn region) — Xuán-yì at the famous **Dà-bié-shān** 大別山 directly across the river from Wǔ-chāng — illustrating how the post-Sānfān-luàn Sì-chuān refugees consolidated downriver in eastern Húběi during the late Kāngxī period.
