# Yù-quán Qí-bái Fù chán-shī yǔ-lù 玉泉其白富禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qí-bái Fù of Yù-quán [-sì]*
by [[德富]] (說), [[圓頂]] (等編)

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Qí-bái Dé-fù** [[德富]] 其白德富 (**1628-02-04 – 1690-07-03**, age 64), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696), thus a co-disciple of **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] (cf. [[KR6q0544]]). Native of **Shǔ-nán Méi-zhōu** 蜀南眉州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Gōng** 龔. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Róng-chè Yuán-dǐng** [[圓頂]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at his sole abbacy **Méi-zhōu Pán-lóng-shān Yù-quán chán-sì** 眉州蟠龍山玉泉禪寺 (Sì-chuān), where he served until his death.

The *yǔ-lù* opens with a portrait-frontispiece, *zàn*, two prefaces, and a comprehensive *mù-cì*. *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-*fù-fǎ* abbacy onset, ca. 1659–1660 when Zhàng-xuě sealed his dharma-heirs); *notAfter* = **1690** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B430.

**Contents.** Juan 1: portrait + *zàn*; *xù* (二); *mù-cì*; *shàng-táng* (the principal preaching record at Yù-quán-sì). Juan 2: *fǎ-yǔ* (示融默監寺, 示秀雲, 示福田, 示融穆, 示吼月, 示文達, 示融之, 示性一, 示體空, 示了機, 示融徹首座, 示玟睿維那 etc. — addressing the master's principal disciples). Juan 3: closing *jī-yuán*, *fó-shì*, the *xíng-shí* (autobiographical narration), the **塔銘** (stupa-inscription dating his death to 1690), and the **分燈** (lineage-distribution) listing his five dharma-heirs.

## Other points of interest

The Sì-chuān cluster of Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì's heirs appears in J38 in close succession — [[KR6q0544]] (Chè-shēng), [[KR6q0547]] (Dé-fù) — both with abbacy-bases on or near famous Sì-chuān Daoist-Buddhist mountains (Qīng-chéng-shān; Pán-lóng-shān). The internal lineage-economy is visible in the *fǎ-yǔ* section, where Dé-fù addresses each of his five later *fǎ-sì* by name.
