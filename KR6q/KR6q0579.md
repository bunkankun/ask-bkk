# Shān-xī Bǎi-shān Kǎi chán-shī yǔ-lù 山西柏山楷禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bǎi-shān Kǎi of Shān-xī*
by [[德楷]] (說), [[行悟]] (等編次)

Five-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Bǎi-shān Dé-kǎi** [[德楷]] 柏山德楷 (**1629 – 1702-04-19**, age 74, *sēng-là* 42), **Cáodòng-school** 31st-generation dharma-heir of **Zhú-làng Tōng-ér** 竹浪通爾 at **Jué-huá** 覺華. Native of **Wéi-yáng Tài-xìng** 維揚泰興 (Yáng-zhōu), lay surname **Zhuāng** 莊. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Xíng-wù** [[行悟]] *děng biān-cì*. Three prefaces, one labeled **重編** *zhòng-biān*.

Documents Dé-kǎi's three Shān-xī abbacies — an unusual northern-China Cáodòng circuit: **Shān-xī Fēn-zhōu-fǔ Fēn-yáng-xiàn Pǔ-tōng chán-sì** 山西汾州府汾陽縣普通禪寺; **Bái-yún chán-sì** 白雲禪寺; **Fēn-zhōu-fǔ Xiào-yì-xiàn Lóng-shān Huá-yán chán-yuàn** 汾州府孝義縣龍山華嚴禪院 (the title-throne for the **Cáodòng 31st-generation** lineage seat). *notBefore* ≈ **1670** (post-付法 abbacy career); *notAfter* = **1702** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B466.

**Contents.** Juan 1: three prefaces; *xíng-shí*; *Pǔ-tōng-sì yǔ-lù*; *Bái-yún-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Lóng-shān Huá-yán-yuàn yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *xiǎo-cān*; *wǎn-cān*; *shì-zhòng*. Juan 4: *pǔ-shuō*; *chóu-shì fǎ-yǔ*; *jī-yuán*. Juan 5: *niān-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*; *chuí-shì*; *zuò-míng*; *xiàng-zàn*.

## Other points of interest

A rare **northern-China Cáodòng** *yǔ-lù* in J39, with all three abbacies in **Fēn-zhōu-fǔ** 汾州府 (Shānxī). Dé-kǎi's earlier formation in **Yáng-zhōu** Tài-xìng (his birthplace) and Jué-huá (Cáodòng base) before migrating north establishes a distinct geographic-lineage trajectory. The *zhòng-biān* preface indicates a previously-circulated edition before this canonical version.
