# Fǎ-chuáng Yuǎn chán-shī yǔ-lù 法幢遠禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fǎ-chuáng Yuǎn*
by [[智遠]] (說), [[通慧]] (等編), [[弘源]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** [[智遠]] 法幢智遠 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Chún-bèi Guǎng-dé** [[廣德]] (cf. [[KR6q0550]]), thus a *fǎ-sūn* of **Ěr-dú Dào-tài** in the Pò-shān Hǎi-míng line. Native of **Shǔ-dōng Wàn-xiàn** 蜀東萬縣 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Sòng** 宋. Compiled by his sole *fǎ-sì* **Ān-shí Tōng-huì** [[通慧]] *děng biān*. Preface by **Hān-táng Hóng-yuán** [[弘源]] dated **康熙三十四年四月佛誕日** = **1695-05-21** at **Yí-líng**. *notBefore* ≈ **1672** (post-master's Sì-guī Fó-xīng 1672 founding); *notAfter* = **1695** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B434.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *mù-cì*; *Huáng-líng-yuàn yǔ-lù* (the principal *shàng-táng* record at the same Yangtze-gorge seat his teacher had held); *xiǎo-cān*; *shì-zhòng*; *sòng-gǔ*; ten **牧頌** *mù-sòng* (oxherding poems: *wèi-mù*, *chū-tiáo*, *shòu-zhì*, *huí-shǒu*, *xún-fú*, *wú-ài*, *rèn-yùn*, *xiāng-wàng*, *dú-zhào*, *shuāng-mián*); *lián-fāng* (lineage-distribution).

## Other points of interest

The pairing of [[KR6q0550]] (Chún-bèi Guǎng-dé) and KR6q0551 (Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn) gives a teacher-disciple sequence of *yǔ-lù* documenting the Húběi Yangtze-gorge institutional rebuilding of late-Kāngxī Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism on the Pò-shān → Ěr-dú line. The *mù-sòng* sequence in Zhì-yuǎn's *yǔ-lù* uses a slightly non-standard ten-stage scheme (omitting the more familiar *kuò-niú* / *fǎn-běn* terminology in favor of *xún-fú* / *xiāng-wàng* / *dú-zhào* / *shuāng-mián*).
