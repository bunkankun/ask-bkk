# Fó-guǒ Kè-qín chánshī xīn yào 佛果克勤禪師心要

*Heart-Essentials of Chán Master Fó-guǒ Kè-qín* — a four-juan collection of letters and doctrinal-essays by [[克勤|Yuán-wù Kè-qín]] 圓悟克勤 (*shì* Fó-guǒ 佛果, 1063–1135), the compiler of the famous *Bì yán lù* and one of the major Northern-Sòng Línjì-Yángqí masters. Compiled by his dharma-heir [[子文|Zǐ-wén]] 子文.

Four-juan Northern-Sòng Chán doctrinal collection, X69 n1357. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The *xīn yào* 心要 ("heart-essentials") format emphasises the most important teaching-content of the master — typically his letters to disciples containing concise doctrinal instruction, a form Kè-qín was particularly prolific in.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1115; *notAfter* 1135 (Kè-qín's death).
