# Wù-chū Dà-guān chánshī yǔlù 物初大觀禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Wù-chū Dà-guān* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **[[大觀|Wù-chū Dà-guān]]** 物初大觀 (1201–1268), dharma-heir of Běi-jiàn Jū-jiǎn 北磵居簡 (see [[KR6q0299]]). Compiled by his dharma-heir disciple (*mén rén* 門人) [[德溥|Dé-pǔ]] 德溥 et al.

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1366.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1240 (Dà-guān's mature career); *notAfter* c. 1280.
