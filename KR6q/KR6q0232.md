# Gāofēng Sānshān Lái chánshī shū yǔ 高峰三山來禪師疏語

*Announcement-Speeches of Chán Master Sānshān Lái of Gāofēng*

A three-juan supplementary collection of [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] 三山燈來's (1614–1685) ceremonial-announcement speeches (*shū yǔ* 疏語: a specific genre of formal public announcements read at monastic ceremonies — precept-ordinations, merit-dedications, temple-openings, etc.). Compiled by his dharma-heir [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統 (1661–1717), complementing the *Wǔ jiā zōng zhǐ zuǎn yào* [[KR6q0169]] and the *nián pǔ* [[KR6q0209]] in Xìngtǒng's broader editorial project of preserving his master's corpus.

## About the work

A three-juan ceremonial-speech collection, J39 B439. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. The *shū yǔ* 疏語 genre occupies an unusual place in the monastic literary canon: these are not Chán doctrinal-teaching sermons but formal public-ceremonial addresses, delivered on occasions of monastic-institutional activity. The three-juan scale reflects Dēnglái's substantial institutional-ceremonial output across his 70-year career.

See [[KR6q0169]], [[KR6q0209]] for broader context on Dēnglái and Xìngtǒng.

Dating: *notBefore* 1685 (immediately after Dēnglái's death, the start of Xìngtǒng's editorial compilation); *notAfter* 1710 (during Xìngtǒng's productive editorial period).
