# Lián-fēng chán-shī yǔ-lù 蓮峰禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lián-fēng*
by [[素]] (說), [[性深]] (等編)

Substantial ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Lián-fēng Sù** [[素]] 蓮峰素 (b. **1612**, still alive in 1691 at age 80, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎi-chī Xíng-yuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662) in the **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 → **Tiān-tóng / Jīng-shān** 天童徑山 lineage. Native of **Pú-tián** 莆田 (Fú-jiàn), lay surname Kē 柯; his entire abbatial career was conducted within **Xīng-huà-fǔ** 興化府 (Fú-jiàn). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Ruò-hǎi Xìng-shēn** [[性深]] *děng biān*. The first preface, by his Línjì-lineage *fǎ-dì* (younger dharma-brother) **Chāo-péi** 超裴, is dated **丙午臘月望日** = **1666-12-31** (Kāngxī 5, 12th lunar month full moon) at **Yǎng-tái shān-fáng** 養苔山房 — by which time Lián-fēng had retired to **Yún-fēng** 雲峰. *notBefore* = **順治十年癸巳 2/28** = **1653-03-27** (entered Gū-shān-sì, opening date of the recorded sermons); *notAfter* = **1666** (preface, capturing the corpus shortly after retirement). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B410.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces, *Xīng-huà Gū-shān chán-sì yǔ-lù* 福建興化孤山禪寺語錄 and *Jiǔ-zuò-shān Xīng-guó chán-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Xīng-huà Bào-ēn chán-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *Lóng-huá Wàn-shòu chán-sì yǔ-lù*, *Lú-shān Tōng-tiān chán-sì yǔ-lù*, *Xī-míng chán-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 4: *xiǎo-cān*. Juan 5: *shì-zhòng*, *niān-gǔ*, *sòng-gǔ*, *dài-gǔ*. Juan 6: *rù-shì*, *kān-biàn jī-yuán*, *fǎ-yǔ*, *zàn*, *zì-tí*. Juan 7: *xù*, *gē*, *zhuàn*, *xíng-shí*, *qǐ*, *yǐn*, *cí*, *míng*, *fó-shì*. Juan 8–10: *wén*, *jì*, and three juan of *zá-yǒng* (miscellaneous poems).

## Other points of interest

A regional Fú-jiàn *yǔ-lù* — six abbacies all within Xīng-huà-fǔ. The *zá-yǒng* fills three juan (8–10), unusually substantial for a *yǔ-lù*; combined with the more conventional *yǔ-lù* sections in juan 1–7, this gives Lián-fēng's collection an almost *biè-jí* 別集 character. The "原書闕" 原書闕 ("original gap") notation between the two prefaces and the table of contents in juan 1 indicates a known lacuna in the printed exemplar.
