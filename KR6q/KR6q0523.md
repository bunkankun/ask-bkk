# Zhī-kōng Yùn chán-shī yǔ-lù 知空蘊禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhī-kōng Yùn*
by [[學蘊]] (說), [[通來]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zhī-kōng Xué-yùn** [[學蘊]] 知空學蘊 (**1613-10-14 – 1689-07-14**, age 77, *sēng-là* 65), Línjì 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Kāi-fēng Mì-xíng Jì-rěn** 開峰密行寂忍 (Húguǎng Héng-zhōu). A Yún-nán monastic — native of **Ěr-hǎi** 洱海, ordained at age 14 at **Jī-zú-shān Jì-guāng-sì** 雞足寂光寺. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Tōng-lái** [[通來]] *děng biān*. Held five abbacies across Yún-nán: **Kāi-fēng Nán-yún** 開峰南雲, **Chǔ-xióng Fú-chéng** 楚雄福城, **Jiǔ-tái Fāng-guǎng** 九臺方廣, **Guǎng-tōng Xiān-yáng** 廣通仙羊, and **Jìng-lè** 淨樂. The opening *shàng-táng* is dated **辛亥年三月十五日** = **1671-04-23** at Fú-chéng-sì, where he received law-instruments forwarded by his teacher Mì-xíng. *notBefore* = **1671** (Fú-chéng-sì opening *shàng-táng*), *notAfter* = **1689** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B400. Juan 1: *shàng-táng*, *xiǎo-cān*, *shì-zhòng*, *chá-huà*, *xíng-lù*; juan 2: *fǎ-yǔ*, *jī-yuán*, *zèng-jì*, etc.
