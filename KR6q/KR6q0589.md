# Tiān-yì Xiáng chán-shī yǔ-lù 天翼翔禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tiān-yì Xiáng*
by [[巨翔]] (說), [[寂乾]] (錄), [[際鋒]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tiān-yì Jù-xiáng** [[巨翔]] 天翼巨翔 (**1663-01-13 – 1695-06-20**, age only 34, *sēng-là* 14), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wéi-shān Gǔ-méi Dìng-liè** [[定冽]] (cf. [[KR6q0576]]). Native of **Shǎn-xī Líng-líng** 陵霄, lay surname **Zhāng** 張. Recorded by attendant **Jì-qián** [[寂乾]] *lù*; compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Cáng-ān Jì-fēng** [[際鋒]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at **Tán-zhōu Dà-Wéi-shān Mì-yìn chán-sì** 潭州大溈山密印禪寺 and **Tán-zhōu Fú-róng-shān Guǎng-huà-sì** 芙蓉山廣化寺 — the same Húnán seats his teacher had held. *notBefore* = **1691** (康熙三十年, formal abbacy at the Wéi-yǎng home-temple at age 28); *notAfter* = **1695** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B477.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *Mì-yìn-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Guǎng-huà-sì yǔ-lù*; *jī-yuán*; *jǔ-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*; **行實** (the principal source for his life — including the dramatic three-stage awakening: at 9 reading *Śūraṅgama*, at 24 entering Dìng-liè's gate, and at 27 in *bì-guān* at Zǐ-zhú-lín).

## Other points of interest

KR6q0576 ([[定冽]], 1669+ at Wéi-shān) and KR6q0589 (his disciple [[巨翔]], 1691+ at Wéi-shān) document a continuous Línjì-Yángqí presence at **Mì-yìn-sì** 密印寺 — the historic mother-temple of the **Wéi-yǎng** school — over more than two decades. Jù-xiáng's death at age 34 was unusually early; the manuscript-cluster including [[際鋒]]'s editorial work followed within months.
