# Hè-lín Tiān-shù Zhí chán-shī yǔ-lù 鶴林天樹植禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tiān-shù Zhí of Hè-lín*
by [[行植]] (說), [[湛祐]] (編)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tiān-shù Xíng-zhí** [[行植]] 天樹行植 (**1633-01-23 – 1680-11/12**, age 49), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mù-yún Tōng-mén** 牧雲通門 (1599–1671). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Tiān-fú Zhàn-yòu** [[湛祐]] *biān*. Sole abbacy at **Hè-lín-sì** 鶴林寺 (Jīng-kǒu / Zhèn-jiāng) from **1671** (succession to teacher Mù-yún). *notBefore* = **1671** (succession), *notAfter* = **1680** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B399.
