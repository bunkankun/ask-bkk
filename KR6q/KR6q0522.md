# Hèlín Tiānshù Zhí chánshī yǔlù 鶴林天樹植禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tiān-shù Zhí of Hè-lín*
by [[行植]] (說), [[湛祐]] (編)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Tiānshù Xíngzhí** [[行植]] 天樹行植 (**1633-01-23 – 1680-11/12**, age 49), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Mùyún Tōngmén** 牧雲通門 (1599–1671). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Tiānfú Zhànyòu** [[湛祐]] *biān*. Sole abbacy at **Hèlínsì** 鶴林寺 (Jīngkǒu / Zhènjiāng) from **1671** (succession to teacher Mùyún). *notBefore* = **1671** (succession), *notAfter* = **1680** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B399.
