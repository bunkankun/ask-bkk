# Chuī-wàn chán-shī yǔ-lù 吹萬禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chuī-wàn*
by [[廣真]] (說), [[燈來]] (重編)

## About the work

Twenty-juan *zhèng-lù* 正錄 of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639), the principal and most extensive recording of Guǎng-zhēn's preaching. Stands in contrast to the selective 3-juan **[[KR6q0448]]** 《聚雲吹萬真禪師語錄》 (same author, same Canon volume cluster, same preface-hand): the 3-juan B238 is the *xuǎn-biān* 選編 selection published first, while the 20-juan B239 is the definitive *chóng-biān* 重編 expansion. Both were compiled by the dharma-grandson **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685) through Guǎng-zhēn's chief heir **Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] 鐵壁慧機. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B239. Two prefaces: (i) **Xióng Rǔ-xué** 熊汝學 (*dēng-zhào* 燈照), **崇禎壬午菊月 = 1642/9 intercalary**, essentially identical to the B238 preface; (ii) **Shěn Yì-wěi** 沈奕瑋 of **Qián-nán** 黔南, *Lù-yuán xué-rén* 鹿園學人, **崇禎壬午小春既望 = 1642/10/16** on the *Píng-shān zhōu-cì* 平山舟次 (river-boat) — a poetically pungent preface insisting on the Línjì / Dà-huì lineage identity and praising the **Tiě-bì héshàng**'s role in the transmission of the Jù-yún teaching.

### Abstract

**Dating.** Same dating-window as [[KR6q0448]]: *notBefore* = **1618** (first abbacy at Xiāo-xiāng Hú-dōng Chán-yuàn); *notAfter* = **1642** (preface window). The 20-juan structure preserves the full biographical arc through the Jù-yún-sì 聚雲寺 founding and the Chóng-zhēn 12 (**1639/8/28**) death-scene at Zhōng-zhōu.

**Relation to the B238 selection.** The B238 close-colophon *lìng kè zhèng-lù shí juan, guǎng-lù sān-shí zhǒng* 另刻正錄十卷廣錄三十種 refers to an earlier (no-longer-surviving) ten-juan state of the *zhèng-lù*; B239's 20 juan represents the final *chóng-biān* 重編 expansion of that material. The catalog meta for KR6q0449 accordingly records the compiler-function as *chóng-biān* 重編, distinguishing it from B238 (*biān* 編).

**Shěn Yì-wěi's preface.** Shěn (a Guì-zhōu literatus) adds doctrinal emphases absent from the Xióng preface: (i) Guǎng-zhēn as the **Línjì / Dà-huì mid-stream** master of the late Míng, invoking Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo by name (*cǐ bù dú Lín-jì, Dà-huì qiān-nián yī-jù jí qǐ* 此不獨臨濟大慧千年一炬即起); (ii) the critical importance of Guǎng-zhēn's chief heir **Tiě-bì chán-shī** 鐵壁禪師 = [[慧機]] as the preservation-channel for the teaching (*bǎo-yuè yī-lún jìn zài Tiě-bì chán-shī máo-kǒng zhōng* 寶月一輪近在鐵壁禪師毛孔中); and (iii) the pressing need to print the corpus against corrupted and amateur Chán literature.

**Lineage.** Identical to [[KR6q0448]]. Guǎng-zhēn 廣真 (heir of **Cháo-yáng Yuè-míng Lián-chí** 朝陽月明聯池) → principal heirs **Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] (cf. [[KR6q0450]], [[KR6q0451]]), **Wàn-sōng Sān-mù Huì-zhī** 萬松三目慧芝, **Bǎo-fēng Sān-bā-zhǎng Tiě-méi Huì-lì** (cf. [[KR6q0452]]). The compiler **Dēng-lái** is an heir of Huì-jī, hence Guǎng-zhēn's dharma-grandson.

## Other points of interest

- **Definitive zhèng-lù of the Jù-yún corpus.** Where [[KR6q0448]] preserves a selective cross-section, this 20-juan recension is the definitive edition of Guǎng-zhēn's *yǔ-lù* and the basic text for any scholarly engagement with his preaching.
