# Chuīwàn chánshī yǔlù 吹萬禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chuī-wàn*
by [[廣真]] (說), [[燈來]] (重編)

## About the work

Twenty-juan *zhènglù* 正錄 of **Jùyún Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639), the principal and most extensive recording of Guǎngzhēn's preaching. Stands in contrast to the selective 3-juan **[[KR6q0448]]** 《聚雲吹萬真禪師語錄》 (same author, same Canon volume cluster, same preface-hand): the 3-juan B238 is the *xuǎnbiān* 選編 selection published first, while the 20-juan B239 is the definitive *chóngbiān* 重編 expansion. Both were compiled by the dharma-grandson **Sānshān Dēnglái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685) through Guǎngzhēn's chief heir **Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] 鐵壁慧機. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B239. Two prefaces: (i) **Xióng Rǔxué** 熊汝學 (*dēngzhào* 燈照), **崇禎壬午菊月 = 1642/9 intercalary**, essentially identical to the B238 preface; (ii) **Shěn Yìwěi** 沈奕瑋 of **Qiánnán** 黔南, *Lùyuán xuérén* 鹿園學人, **崇禎壬午小春既望 = 1642/10/16** on the *Píngshān zhōucì* 平山舟次 (river-boat) — a poetically pungent preface insisting on the Línjì / Dàhuì lineage identity and praising the **Tiěbì héshàng**'s role in the transmission of the Jùyún teaching.

### Abstract

**Dating.** Same dating-window as [[KR6q0448]]: *notBefore* = **1618** (first abbacy at Xiāoxiāng Húdōng Chányuàn); *notAfter* = **1642** (preface window). The 20-juan structure preserves the full biographical arc through the Jùyúnsì 聚雲寺 founding and the Chóngzhēn 12 (**1639/8/28**) death-scene at Zhōngzhōu.

**Relation to the B238 selection.** The B238 close-colophon *lìng kè zhènglù shí juan, guǎnglù sānshí zhǒng* 另刻正錄十卷廣錄三十種 refers to an earlier (no-longer-surviving) ten-juan state of the *zhènglù*; B239's 20 juan represents the final *chóngbiān* 重編 expansion of that material. The catalog meta for [[KR6q0449]] accordingly records the compiler-function as *chóngbiān* 重編, distinguishing it from B238 (*biān* 編).

**Shěn Yìwěi's preface.** Shěn (a Guìzhōu literatus) adds doctrinal emphases absent from the Xióng preface: (i) Guǎngzhēn as the **Línjì / Dàhuì mid-stream** master of the late Míng, invoking Dàhuì Zōnggǎo by name (*cǐ bù dú Línjì, Dàhuì qiānnián yījù jí qǐ* 此不獨臨濟大慧千年一炬即起); (ii) the critical importance of Guǎngzhēn's chief heir **Tiěbì chánshī** 鐵壁禪師 = [[慧機]] as the preservation-channel for the teaching (*bǎoyuè yīlún jìn zài Tiěbì chánshī máokǒng zhōng* 寶月一輪近在鐵壁禪師毛孔中); and (iii) the pressing need to print the corpus against corrupted and amateur Chán literature.

**Lineage.** Identical to [[KR6q0448]]. Guǎngzhēn 廣真 (heir of **Cháoyáng Yuèmíng Liánchí** 朝陽月明聯池) → principal heirs **Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] (cf. [[KR6q0450]], [[KR6q0451]]), **Wànsōng Sānmù Huìzhī** 萬松三目慧芝, **Bǎofēng Sānbāzhǎng Tiěméi Huìlì** (cf. [[KR6q0452]]). The compiler **Dēnglái** is an heir of Huìjī, hence Guǎngzhēn's dharma-grandson.

## Other points of interest

- **Definitive zhènglù of the Jùyún corpus.** Where [[KR6q0448]] preserves a selective cross-section, this 20-juan recension is the definitive edition of Guǎngzhēn's *yǔlù* and the basic text for any scholarly engagement with his preaching.
