# Shānān zálù 山菴雜錄

*Miscellaneous Records of the Mountain Hermitage*

by [[無慍]] Shùzhōng Wúyùn 恕中無慍 (1309–1386), late-Yuán / early-Míng Yángqí-branch Línjì master (see also [[KR6q0349]]; *zì* Shùzhōng 恕中, self-style Kōngshì dàorén 空室道人 — *not* "Héngshān," as an earlier stub erroneously recorded).

## About the work

A two-juan collection of miscellaneous Chán-related notes, anecdotes, and biographical fragments, by the early-Míng Línjì-line monk Wúyùn. Title — "Miscellaneous Records of the Mountain Hermitage" — frames it as informal reflection rather than systematic compilation.

### Abstract

A late-Yuán / early-Míng monastic miscellany in the genre between *yǔlù* (recorded sayings) and *bǐjì* 筆記 (literary jottings). Contains Wúyùn's notes on contemporaries, anecdotes about earlier masters, and brief reflections on Chán teaching. As a primary source it preserves casual material that more formal lamp records and biographical compendia exclude.

## Translations and research

No English translation. No dedicated monograph; cited in surveys of Yuán-Míng Chán miscellany literature.

## Other points of interest

The genre — informal monastic miscellany — is well represented in the late-imperial Chán corpus and worth attention as a counterweight to the more formal lamp-record and *yǔlù* genres.
