# Hán-sōng Cāo chán-shī yǔ-lù 寒松操禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hán-sōng Cāo*
by [[智操]] (說), [[德昊]] (編)

Twenty-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hán-sōng Zhì-cāo** [[智操]] 寒松智操 (**1626-10-07 – 1688-02/1689-01**, *shì-shòu* 63), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Bǎi-yú Jìng-sī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (1610–1665). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-hào** [[德昊]] *biān*. Same Zhì-cāo who co-compiled his master's 20-juan [[KR6q0498]]. Active at **Sōng-jiāng Lóng-fú** 松江隆福. Juan 17 *zì-xù*. *notBefore* = c. **1665** (post-master-decease succession), *notAfter* = **1688** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B392.
