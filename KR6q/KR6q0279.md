# Sǐ-xīn Wù-xīn chánshī yǔlù 死心悟新禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Sǐ-xīn Wù-xīn* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Huánglóng Sǐ-xīn Wù-xīn** 黃龍死心悟新 (1043–1114), dharma-heir of Huánglóng Zǔ-xīn (see [[KR6q0278]]); the third master in the Huánglóng sub-lineage. Constitutes the third of four *yǔlù* in the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù*.

## About the work

A one-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1344. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

**Sǐ-xīn Wù-xīn** 死心悟新 (1043–1114): Northern-Sòng Huánglóng master; the distinctive sobriquet *Sǐ-xīn* 死心 ("dead mind") reflects his teaching-emphasis on the extinction of discriminating mental-activity.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100; *notAfter* c. 1150.
