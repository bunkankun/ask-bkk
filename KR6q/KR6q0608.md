# Shān-cí Jì chán-shī yǔ-lù 山茨際禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shān-cí Jì*
by [[達尊]] (等編次), [[達謙]] (等編次)

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Shān-cí Tōng-jì** [[通際]] 山茨通際 (**1608-08-20 – 1645-03-05**, age 38, *zuò-xià* 24 — the youngest death among the four founders of the **Qìng-shān** 磬山 sub-branch). Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635, cf. [[KR6q0603]]) — alongside **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** [[通琇]] ([[KR6q0605]]), **Líng-gāo Tōng-yù** 林臯通豫, and **Ruò-ān Tōng-wèn** [[通問]]. Native of **Tōng-zhōu** 通州 (Jiāng-sū), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Ěr-zhān Dá-zūn** [[達尊]] and **Xuán-cí Dá-qiān** [[達謙]] *děng biān-cì*. Catalog source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** Vol. 157 No. 1649.

Documents teaching at three abbacies — concentrated in the dramatic decade 1635–1645 between the master's *fù-fǎ* and his early death amid the dynastic transition: **Háng-zhōu-fǔ Dōng-míng chán-sì** 杭州府東明禪寺; **Nán-yuè Lǜ-luó-ān** 南嶽綠蘿菴 (Hú-nán); **Cháng-shā-fǔ Liú-yáng Nán-yuán chán-sì** 長沙府瀏陽南源禪寺 (where his disciple [[達尊]] — at the same monastery — would later (1646–1663) restore the famous Shí-shuāng-sì 石霜寺 ancestral seat). *notBefore* = **1635** (post-Yuán-xiū-death *fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1645** (death). Catalog dynasty 清.

**Contents.** Juan 1: *Nán-yuè Shān-cí Jì chán-shī yǔ-lù xù*; *mù-lù*; abbacy *yǔ-lù* records (Dōng-míng-sì 1638+, Lǜ-luó-ān, Liú-yáng Nán-yuán-sì); *yīn-shì*. Juan 2: *niān-gǔ*; *dài-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*. Juan 3: *jī-yuán*; *fǎ-yǔ* (with disciples named: 示何二山居士, 示省指禪人, 示三學禪人, 示照初禪人, 示憩南楊居士, *shì nǐ Jì-zǒng* 示尼繼總 — a nun-disciple, also addressed in the *Fú-shī Jiē-yuán Xíng-gāng* 行剛 *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0428]]). Juan 4: closing materials.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0608 (Shān-cí Tōng-jì) and KR6q0605 (Yù-lín Tōng-xiù) are the two principal *yǔ-lù* of Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū's four heirs in the Qiánlóng-canon — Tōng-jì's brief, intense career (38 years) producing the *Nán-yuè chán-dēng-lù* (Húnán Chán biographical anthology) and three Hangzhou-Húnán abbacies; Tōng-xiù's longer career producing imperial-court engagement and the *Guó-shī* title. The dharma-heir transition from Tōng-jì → Dá-zūn → Shí-shuāng-sì restoration (1646+) makes this one of the foundational lines of Qīng-era Hú-nán Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism — see [[KR6q0420]] 《石霜爾瞻尊禪師語錄》 for the next-generation continuation.
