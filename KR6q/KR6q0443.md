# Jiè-ān Jìn chán-shī yǔ-lù 介菴進禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jiè-ān Jìn*
by [[悟進]] (說), [[真理]] (等編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn** [[悟進]] 介菴悟進 (1612 – after 1680s) — principal dharma-heir of [[妙用|Yuān-hú Miào-yòng]] 鴛湖妙用 (1587–1642, cf. [[KR6q0416]]) in the **Wú-qù Rú-kōng → Chē-xī Wú-huàn → Nán-míng Zōng-guǎng** Cáo-dòng sub-line. Also the editorial author of the *xíng-zhuàng* preserved in [[KR6q0416]] juan 2 for his master. *Fǎ-huì* 法諱 **Wù-jìn** 悟進, *hào* **Jiè-ān** 介菴, late-life *hào* **Zhǐ-zū chán-shī** 之祖禪師 / **Jiè-ān Jìn** 介菴進 (per catalog). Lay surname **Zhāng** 張, of **Jiā-xīng** 嘉興. Father **Zhāng Shùn-fǔ** 張舜甫; mother **Hè-shì** 賀氏 who dreamt of the sun-wheel at conception. Born **萬曆 40 壬子 10.9 辰時** = **~2 November 1612** Western. Principal abbot of **Jiā-xīng Jīn-míng-sì** 嘉興金明寺 from late 1640s onwards. Compiled by dharma-heir **Zhēn-lǐ** [[真理]] 真理. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B233.

### Abstract

**Author.** See the extensive biographical frame established at [[KR6q0416]] (where Wù-jìn authored the *xíng-zhuàng* for his master Miào-yòng). The present *yǔlù* preserves Wù-jìn's own juan 5 *xíng-shí* 行實, delivering the following additional personal detail: tonsure at **age 23** (= 1634) at **Tóng-yuè-ān** 桐月庵 under **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** 萬如通微 (Miào-yòng's hermitage-successor; cf. [[KR6q0404]]); 1638 interview with **Mìyún Yuán-wù**; decisive awakening on hearing the carpenter's axe-sound during an illness-period at Tóng-yuè-ān. Miào-yòng passed the **源流衣拂** 源流衣拂 formal transmission to Wù-jìn in **1642** at age 31. After Miào-yòng's 1642 October death, Wù-jìn went to **Wǔ-lín Bǎo-shòu-sì** 武林寶壽寺 in 1643 spring to meet **Shí-yǔ Míng-fāng** 石雨明方 (Miào-yòng's dharma-brother) to request the *tǎ-míng* for Miào-yòng (later preserved at [[KR6q0416]]).

**Abbacies (1648+).** **順治戊子** 順治戊子 = **1648**: received an abbacy-invitation from Jiā-hé protectors — most likely the **Jiā-xīng Jīn-míng-sì** 金明寺 seat that serves as his primary abbatial designation in subsequent cross-references. The preface signature to the present text identifies him as "**秀州金明寺嗣祖沙門悟進**" — the *zhǔ-chí* 住持 of **Xiù-zhōu Jīn-míng-sì** (秀州 = Jiā-xīng).

**Compositional history.** The opening preface is **self-authored** (signed "*Xiù-zhōu Jīn-míng-sì sì-zǔ shā-mén Wù-jìn shū* 秀州金明寺嗣祖沙門悟進書") — rare for a Chán *yǔlù* to open with a self-preface. The preface explicitly argues for the authenticity of the **源流法偈** lineage-transmission-verse genre against those who question it. Undated but consistent with a late-career Jīn-míng-sì cutting c. 1670s–1690s. *notBefore* = **1648** (first Jiā-xīng abbacy invitation); *notAfter* = **1690** conservatively.

**Contents by juan.** (j.1) 序 + 啟 + **shàng-táng** 上堂; (j.2) **shàng-táng** continued; (j.3) **shì-zhòng** 示眾; (j.4) **xiǎo-cān** 小參; (j.5) **niān-gǔ** 拈古 + **sòng-gǔ** 頌古 + **行實** 行實 (self-authored autobiography); (j.6) **jī-yuán** 機緣 + **wèn-dá** 問答 + **chuí-wèn** 垂問; (j.7) **xiàng-zàn** 像贊 + **fǎ-yǔ** 法語 + **shū-wèn** 書問; (j.8) **jì-sòng** 偈頌; (j.9) **shī-jì** 詩偈; (j.10) **shū-yǐn** 疏引 + **xù-bá** 序跋 + **zhēn-míng** 箴銘.

## Other points of interest

- **Self-authored preface.** The opening preface signed by Wù-jìn himself is the distinctive feature of this *yǔlù* — most Chán *yǔlù* open with lay-patron or senior-dharma-brother prefaces, rarely with a master-self-preface. Wù-jìn's self-preface polemicizes against those who doubt the **源流法偈** lineage-verse genre, defending the authenticity of the Wú-qù / Nán-míng / Miào-yòng transmission-tradition. This positions the present *yǔlù* as explicitly **self-defending lineage-authentication** — a late-career theological-polemical posture consistent with the Cáo-dòng-Línjì sectarian tensions of the mid-Kāng-xī era.
- **The Jīn-míng-sì abbacy continuity.** Wù-jìn's long tenure at **Jiā-xīng Jīn-míng-sì** (from c. 1648 through at least the 1680s, = c. 35+ years) is one of the longest single-seat Chán abbacies documented in the J28–J29 cluster. The stability of this tenure — unusual among the abbacy-hopping masters of the period — reflects Wù-jìn's specific editorial-institutional focus on the master-Miào-yòng legacy-custodianship.
