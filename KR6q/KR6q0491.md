# Gǔ-lín Rú chán-shī yǔ-lù 古林如禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Gǔ-lín Rú*
by [[機如]] (說), [[全威]] (等記錄)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Gǔ-lín Jī-rú** [[機如]] 古林機如, Línjì-Yángqí Hǔ-qiū-school dharma-heir of **Zhú-ān Zhēn-yǎn** [[真衍]] 竹庵真衍 in the Jīn-míng-Jiè-lǎo line. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Quán-wēi** [[全威]] *děng jì-lù*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B346. Lifedates not preserved.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[機如]] note: dharma-heir of Zhēn-yǎn at the **Sū-zhōu Zhú-ān** seat, with own subsequent abbacy at the **Gǔ-lín** 古林 site (whence the title-form 古林如). 

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession; Jī-rú compiled [[KR6q0490]] for Zhēn-yǎn). *notAfter* = c. **1690** as a conservative bound.

**Lineage.** Hǔ-qiū → Wú-zhǔn → Duàn-qiáo Lún → Pǔ-míng-Yòng → Jīn-míng-Jiè-lǎo → Zhú-ān-Zhēn-yǎn → Gǔ-lín-Jī-rú (32nd-generation Línjì in the Hǔ-qiū-school sub-line).
