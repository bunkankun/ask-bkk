# Fó-yuān chán-shī yǔ-lù 佛冤禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fó-yuān*
by [[徹綱]] (說), [[性純]] (等編)

Twelve-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Fó-yuān Chè-gāng** [[徹綱]] 佛冤徹綱, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696) in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-chún** [[性純]] *děng biān*. Same Chè-gāng who edited his master's 10-juan [[KR6q0415]] 《昭覺丈雪醉禪師語錄》. *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession). *notAfter* = c. **1690** as a conservative bound. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B371.
