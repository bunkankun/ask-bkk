# Lín-quán lǎo-rén píng chàng Dān-xiá Chún chánshī sòng gǔ Xū-táng jí 林泉老人評唱丹霞淳禪師頌古虗堂集

*Old-Man Lín-quán's Evaluation-Singing on Master Dān-xiá Chún's Sòng-gǔ: Empty-Hall Collection* — a six-juan companion-volume to [[KR6q0251]] *Kōng gǔ jí*, in which the Yuán Cáodòng master [[從倫|Lín-quán Cóng-lún]] 林泉從倫 provides the *píng chàng* 評唱 commentary on the *sòng gǔ* verses of the Northern-Sòng Cáodòng master [[子淳|Dān-xiá Zǐ-chún]] 丹霞子淳 (1066–1119). The two pairs [[KR6q0251]] / [[KR6q0252]] together constitute Cóng-lún's systematic *píng chàng* on the two major Northern-Sòng Cáodòng *sòng gǔ* corpora (Yì-qīng and Zǐ-chún).

## About the work

A six-juan Cáodòng *sòng gǔ* / *píng chàng* collaborative commentary, X67 n1304. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Parallel to [[KR6q0251]] in structure: Zǐ-chún's 100 *sòng gǔ* verses (Northern Sòng) receive Cóng-lún's Yuán-era *píng chàng* running-commentary. Title: *Xū-táng jí* 虗堂集 ("Empty-Hall Collection"), distinct from [[KR6q0251]]'s *Kōng gǔ jí* ("Empty-Valley Collection") — Cóng-lún's twin-titled pair.

### Abstract

**[[子淳|Dān-xiá Zǐ-chún]]** 丹霞子淳 (DILA A000084, 1066–1119/1120; died Xuān-hé 1 / spring, per *Chán dēng shì pǔ* 禪燈世譜 juan 9). Northern-Sòng Cáodòng master; dharma-heir of Fú-róng Dào-kǎi 芙蓉道楷 in the second major Cáodòng transmission-line. Major teacher of Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué 宏智正覺 (1091–1157) whose own *sòng gǔ* would become the basis of the *Cóng róng lù*.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100 (Zǐ-chún's original composition); *notAfter* c. 1290 (Cóng-lún's commentary completion).
