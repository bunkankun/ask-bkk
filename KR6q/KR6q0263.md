# Gǔ zūn sù yǔlù 古尊宿語錄 (Zhōnghuá Canon recension, 48 juan with Ming re-collation)

*Sayings of the Ancient Venerable Masters* (Zhōnghuá Canon recension) — an alternate 48-juan recension of [[賾藏|Zé-zàng-zhǔ]]'s 賾藏主 Southern-Sòng Chán-*yǔlù* anthology (see [[KR6q0261]]), preserved in the Zhōnghuá dà-zàng-jīng (vol. 77, no. 1710) with a Míng-era re-collation by [[淨戒|Jìng-jiè]] 淨戒.

## About the work

A 48-juan alternate-recension of [[KR6q0261]] with Ming-era re-collation, Zhōnghuá 77/1710. See [[KR6q0261]] for full content and context.

Dating: *notBefore* 1131 (original compilation); *notAfter* c. 1450 (Jìng-jiè's Míng re-collation horizon).
