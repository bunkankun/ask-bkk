# Dà-fāng chán-shī yǔ-lù 大方禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dà-fāng*
by [[行海]] (說), [[超鳴]] (編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Dà-fāng Xíng-hǎi** [[行海]] 大方行海 (**1604-07-03 – 1670-02-24**, *shì-shòu* 67), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fú-shí Tōng-xián** [[通賢]] 浮石通賢. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chāo-míng** [[超鳴]] *biān*. *notBefore* = c. **1650** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1670** (death). Juan 6 includes *xíng-shí* and *tǎ-míng* with lifedates. Sole abbacy at **Jiāng-sū Lóu-jiāng Chán-dēng-ān** 江蘇婁江禪燈菴 from 1669; the *yǔ-lù* covers his decades of pre-abbacy practice and the brief Lóu-jiāng tenure. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B366.
