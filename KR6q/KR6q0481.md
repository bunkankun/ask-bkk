# Yún-sǒu Zhù chán-shī yǔ-lù 雲叟住禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yún-sǒu Zhù*
by [[住]] (說), [[元一]] (記錄)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yún-sǒu Běn-zhù** [[住]] 雲叟本住 (1620 – **1676-09-17**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mù-chén Shān-wēng Dào-mín** [[道忞]] (1596–1674) at **Tán-zhōu Lóng-yá-sì** 潭州龍牙寺. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Yuán-yī** [[元一]]. Front-preface by Běn-zhù's dharma-brother **Ruì** 睿 of **Wò-lóng** 臥龍, written at the request of **Yuǎn-ān héshàng** 遠菴和尚 of **Míng-zhōu Ruì-yán** 明州瑞巖. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B310.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[住]] note: native of **Ān-lù** 安陸 (Hú-běi). Full precepts at **Dà-wéi-shān** 大溈山, formal transmission from **Mù-chén Dào-mín** at **Qīng-zhōu** 青州. Sole abbacy at the Tang-era **Lóng-yá** 龍牙 site in Tán-zhōu (Hú-nán) — the historic Caves of **Lóng-yá Jū-dùn**.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1676** (death). The Lóng-yá monastic-supervisor **Huò Xuě-lín** 奯雪林 personally traveled the 3,000+ *li* from Hú-nán to Zhè-jiāng to commission the publication after Běn-zhù's 1676 death.

**Lineage.** Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Mù-chén Shān-wēng Dào-mín → Yún-sǒu Běn-zhù. Stands as a parallel sub-line to the [[KR6q0457]] **Dá-biàn Xiǎn-quán** 達變顯權 (Dào-mín's other heir at Líng-fēng / Wǔ-lěi-shān).
