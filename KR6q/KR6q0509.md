# Yìn-xīn Fó-mǐn Nè chán-shī yǔ-lù 印心佛敏訥禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fó-mǐn Nè of Yìn-xīn*
by [[寂訥]] (說), [[法棟]] (等錄), [[法通]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Fó-mǐn Yìn-xīn Jì-nè** [[寂訥]] (catalog 寂納 = 寂訥), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Nòu-yún Zhēn-shí** [[真實]] 耨雲真實 (b. 1626) in the Pò-shān → Zhàng-xuě → Cǎo-táng sub-line. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Fǎ-dòng** [[法棟]] + **Fǎ-tōng** [[法通]] *děng lù/biān*. Title-form 印心 references the **Yìn-xīn** seat. *notBefore* = c. **1680** (post-master-active period). *notAfter* = c. **1700** as conservative bound. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B372.
