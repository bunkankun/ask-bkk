# Yǒng-jué Yuán-xián chánshī guǎng-lù 永覺元賢禪師廣錄

Thirty-juan "Extensive Record" (*guǎng-lù* 廣錄) of **Yǒng-jué Yuán-xián** [[元賢]] 永覺元賢 (1578–1657), the Shòu-chāng 壽昌 Cáo-dòng 曹洞 master whose 30-juan self-contained corpus — *yǔlù* proper plus *chán-yú nèi-wài jí* 禪餘內外集, *wǎn lù* 晚錄, *zuì-hòu yǔ* 最後語, *dòng-shàng gǔ-zhé* 洞上古轍, *mǐ-yán* 䆿言 — together amount to the most substantial Míng-Qīng transitional Cáo-dòng corpus. *Xuzangjing* X72 no. 1437. Re-edited (*chóng-biān* 重編) by Yuán-xián's principal dharma-heir [[道霈]] Wèi-lín Dào-pèi 為霖道霈 (21 December 1615 – 27 October 1702) in the three years following the master's death (Shùn-zhì 14 / 1657).

### Abstract

The collection opens with two prefatorial *zàn* 贊 — one by **[[元來]] Wú-yì Yuán-lái** ("吞却死猫頭，嚙盡生人膽…") dating from before Yuán-lái's own death in 1630, and thus describing the young Yuán-xián as a Shòu-chāng brother-in-dharma — and one by the lay disciple **Zhèng Xuán** 鄭瑄 (*Zuó-fēi ān dì-zǐ* 昨非菴弟子), praising Yuán-xián as "the only such person in five hundred years" 五百年來僅此人. The principal preface, ***Yǒng-jué hé-shàng guǎng-lù xù*** 永覺和尚廣錄序, is by **Lín Zhī-fān** 林之蕃 (a *jìn-shì* *kǎo-gōng sī láng-zhōng* 吏部考功司郎中 / *Pú-sà-jiè dì-zǐ*) who "had lived under the master's seat and received his teaching, from youth to old age, for more than a day"; Lín narrates the 30-juan compilation as the three-year labour of Dào-pèi after the master's death, and calls it "the *tī-jīn* 梯金 platform for the new restoration of Shào-lín / Cáo-dòng orthodoxy." Several further prefaces follow: to the *Chán-yú nèi-jí* 禪餘內集 by Cáo Gǔ 曹谷 (Chóng-zhēn *Guǐ-wèi* 癸未 / 1643, during Yuán-xián's Gǔ-shān years — thus a pre-death preface to one of the component collections, reused in the posthumous consolidation); to the *Chán-yú wài-jí* 禪餘外集; and others.

The structure of the 30 juan spans all the classic genres — *shàng-táng*, *xiǎo-cān*, *wèn-dá*, *fǎ-yǔ*, *niān-gǔ*, *sòng-gǔ*, *zàn*, *jì-sòng*, *shū*, *wén*, *shū*, and the book-length sub-collections: ***Dòng-shàng gǔ-zhé*** 洞上古轍 (a historical-doctrinal treatise on Cáo-dòng lineage and *wǔ-wèi* 五位 teaching — one of the chief Qīng-dynasty Cáo-dòng theoretical works); and the ***Mǐ-yán*** 䆿言 ("Sleep-Talk") miscellaneous doctrinal essays. The ***Zuì-hòu yǔ*** 最後語 captures Yuán-xián's last-year teachings of 1656–1657.

Yuán-xián's career trajectory is set out in [[元賢]]'s person note; the *guǎng-lù* covers it in full, from the first recorded abbacy at Ōu-níng Jīn-xiān-ān 金仙庵 in c. 1626 to the final teachings at Gǔ-shān in 1657. Lín Zhī-fān also notes that Yuán-xián's separately-circulating works — the *Léng-yán* 楞嚴 and *Jīn-gāng* 金剛 sūtra commentaries, the *Xīn-jīng zhǐ-zhǎng* 心經指掌, the *Sì-fēn yuē-yì* 四分約義 (*Vinaya* summary), the *Lǜ-xué fā-rèn* 律學發軔, the *Hóng-shì lù* 弘釋錄, the *Jì-dēng bǔ-dēng èr lù* 繼燈補燈二錄 ([[KR6q0038]]), the *Jìng-cí yào-yǔ* 淨慈要語, the *Gǔ-shān zhì* 鼓山志, and the *Kāi-yuán sì zhì* 開元寺志 — are not included in the *guǎng-lù* but are preserved in separate Kanripo / Jiāxīng entries, several of them within KR6q.

Date bracket: c. 1626 (first abbacy) through c. 1660 (final editorial form of the 30-juan corpus, three years after Yuán-xián's 1657 death).

## Translations and research

Yuán-xián is a central subject in Jiang Wu's *Enlightenment in Dispute* (OUP, 2008). The *Dòng-shàng gǔ-zhé* and *Mǐ-yán* are partially treated in Chinese-language 17th-century Cáo-dòng scholarship. No book-length Western-language study of the *guǎng-lù* proper.

## Other points of interest

Lín Zhī-fān's preface, written at the height of Dào-pèi's editorial work, anticipates that the *guǎng-lù* will "one day, under a *fó-xīn tiān-zǐ* 佛心天子, be incorporated into the imperial canon alongside *Yǒng-míng zōng-jìng* 永明宗鏡, *Míng-jiào tán-jīn* 明教鐔津, and other *zūn-sù yǔ-lù* 尊宿語錄" — a specific political-cultural aspiration for the new Qīng regime which was, in the event, only partially fulfilled through the inclusion in the later Xuzangjing rather than the mainline Qīng Tripitaka.
