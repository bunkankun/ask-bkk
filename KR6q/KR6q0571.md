# Bái-yún Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn chán-shī yǔ-lù 白雲象林本真禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn of Bái-yún [-sì]*
by [[本真]] (說), [[照水]] (重梓), [[妙心]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** [[本真]] 象林本真 (**1620 – 1691** summer, age 72), Línjì-Yángqí lineage. Native of **Suí-zhōu** 隨州 (Hú-běi). *Zhòng-zǐ* (re-engraved) by his sole *fǎ-sì* **Xǐ-xīn Zhào-shuǐ** [[照水]] (subject of [[KR6q0572]]); preface by lay disciple **Miào-xīn** [[妙心]] dated **順治巳亥歲佛歡喜日** = **1659-08-30** (Yú-lán-pén day).

Documents teaching at his principal seat **Qí-zhōu Zǐ-yún-shān Bái-yún chán-sì** 蘄州紫雲山白雲禪寺 (entered **順治甲午仲冬初** = **1654-12**). The compact one-juan extent likely represents an earlier curated selection rather than the full output of his 37-year preaching career. *notBefore* = **1654** (Bái-yún entry); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-1691-death *zhòng-zǐ*). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B457.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface (Miào-xīn, 1659); *niān-sòng* on classic *gōng-àn* (世尊初生, 女子出定, 世尊拈花, 殃崛救產難, 德山托缽, 百丈野狐, 南泉斬貓, 靈雲見桃花悟道因緣); *jì* including verses to and from his disciples (與洗心水西堂 to [[照水]]) and contemporary monks (秋浦, 雲衲明, 自覺, 此藥, 佛月照, 竹菴 lay-Zhào jūshì), plus *fó-shì* and a closing *xíng-shí*.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0571 and the immediately following [[KR6q0572]] form a teacher-disciple pair (Běn-zhēn → Zhào-shuǐ), parallel to the [[KR6q0569]]–[[KR6q0570]] pair (Xíng-gāo → Chāo-yuán). The same disciple Zhào-shuǐ ([[照水]]) earlier re-edited his *tonsure-teacher's* collection [[KR6q0234]] 《正覺潤光澤禪師澡雪集》 — i.e. Zhào-shuǐ functioned as the textual-archivist for both his ordination-line and his transmission-line, an unusual editorial double role.
