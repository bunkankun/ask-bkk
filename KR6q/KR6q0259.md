# Qīngzhōu bǎi wèn 青州百問

*Hundred Questions of Qīngzhōu* — a one-juan Yuán-dynasty Chán Q&A collection: 100 questions posed by [[一辯|Yībiàn]] 一辯 and answered by [[覺|Jué]] 覺. The Qīngzhōu 青州 of the title refers to the geographic locale (Qīngzhōu in Shāndōng, a northern Chán-regional centre).

## About the work

A one-juan Yuán Chán Q&A catechism, X67 n1313. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. Parallel in form to [[KR6q0258]] *Tōng xuán bǎi wèn* (the Wànsōng Xíngxiù Q&A collection) — representing the Yuán-era genre of 100-question Chán catechisms used for doctrinal-instructional purposes.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1250; *notAfter* c. 1340 (broad Yuán-dynasty bracket).
