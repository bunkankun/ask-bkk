# Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Zhēn chán-shī yǔ-lù 聚雲吹萬真禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Zhēn*
by [[廣真]] (說), [[燈來]] (編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639), late-Míng Sichuan Línjì-Yángqí master and founder of the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派 sub-lineage at **Zhōng-zhōu Jù-yún Chán-yuàn** 忠州聚雲禪院. Compiled by the dharma-grandson **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685, heir-of-heir via [[慧機]] 鐵壁慧機), published in the Jiā-xīng Canon as J29 B238. Front-preface by **Xióng Rǔ-xué** 熊汝學 (*dēng-zhào* 燈炤), self-styled *Lú-shān Qiān-zhàng-yán tóu-tuó* 廬山千丈巖頭陀, *shuǐ-bù shàng-shū-láng qián què-jīng shǐ-zhě* 水部尚書郎前榷荊使者, dated **崇禎壬午菊月 = 1642 intercalary 9th month**. Contents: *shàng-juan* — *shàng-táng* + *chá-huà*; *zhōng-juan* — *pǔ-shuō* + *xiǎo-cān* + *shì-zhòng* + *fǎ-yǔ*; *xià-juan* — *rù-táng* + *rù-shì* + *kān-biàn* + *wèn-dá* + *sòng-gǔ* + *zá-shù* + *tǎ-míng*. The closing colophon notes *lìng kè zhèng-lù shí juan, guǎng-lù sān-shí zhǒng, gòng liù-shí liù juan, bǎn cún Zhōng-zhōu Zhì-píng Chán-yuàn* 另刻正錄十卷廣錄三十種共六十六卷板存忠州治平禪院 — the *zhèng-lù* referred to there is [[KR6q0449]] (20 juan, J29 B239, *Chuī-wàn chán-shī yǔ-lù*), and the 30-title *guǎng-lù* was Guǎng-zhēn's separately cut 50-juan collection (now only partially surviving, with individual treatises at [[KR6q0235]] 《一貫別傳》 and the *Shì-jiào sān-zì-jīng* 釋教三字經).

### Abstract

**Dating.** Juan 1 opens with a **萬曆戊午上元日 = 1618 1/15** entry-sermon at **Héng-zhōu-fǔ Xiāo-xiāng Hú-dōng Chán-yuàn** 衡州府瀟湘湖東禪院 (Hú-nán). Subsequent *shàng-táng* juan follow Guǎng-zhēn's Jiāng-nán / Fú-jiàn / Guǎng-dōng / Hú-xiāng circuit and eventual return to Sì-chuān for the five-seat abbacy in Zhōng-zhōu. Closing juan includes his death-verse *cháo dǎ sān-qiān, mù dǎ bā-bǎi; yào jiàn Jù-yún, méi-máo chū xiě* 朝打三千暮打八百要見聚雲眉毛出血 and the Chóng-zhēn 12/7/30 (= **1639 8/28**) death-scene. *notBefore* = **1618** (first abbacy); *notAfter* = **1642** (Xióng Rǔ-xué preface).

**Authorship and transmission.** Xióng Rǔ-xué's preface notes that the compilation was incomplete *several years after* Guǎng-zhēn's death — *yuán-jì shù nián ér yǔ-lù yóu wèi quán kè* 圓寂數年而語錄猶未全刻 — and that Xióng and his *tóng-méng* partners personally organized the engraving. The preface distinguishes three layers: (i) this three-juan selective *yǔ-lù* (KR6q0448); (ii) a separately cut ten-juan *zhèng-lù* (the twenty-juan [[KR6q0449]] in its final expanded form); (iii) thirty separate *guǎng-lù* titles totalling some fifty juan, chiefly doctrinal and literary rather than *yǔ-lù* proper.

**Doctrinal character.** Guǎng-zhēn's Jù-yún tradition was distinctively oriented toward **三教一貫** *sān-jiào yī-guàn* (the unity of the three teachings) — see [[KR6q0235]] for his principal systematic work — and his *yǔ-lù* deploys extensive Confucian-classical citation alongside Chán *gōng-àn* treatment. Xióng's preface emphasizes this character: *jiē fā-míng sān-dài zhī ào yǐ páng-jué zhū-zǐ bǎi-jiā, jí wú rú hào-shǒu qióng-jīng shàng bù néng chuǎng qí láng-wǔ* 皆發明三代之奧以旁抉諸子百家即吾儒皓首窮經尚不能闖其廊廡.

**Lineage.** Guǎng-zhēn received his *yìn-kě* from **Cháo-yáng Yuè-míng Lián-chí chán-shī** 朝陽月明聯池禪師, a Sichuan-based Línjì master. Principal dharma-heirs are **Zhì-píng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] 治平鐵壁慧機 (compiler-circle of [[KR6q0450]], [[KR6q0451]]), **Wàn-sōng Sān-mù Huì-zhī** 萬松三目慧芝, and **Bǎo-fēng Sān-bā-zhǎng Tiě-méi Huì-lì** 寶峰三巴掌鐵眉慧麗 (cf. [[KR6q0452]]).

## Other points of interest

- **Paired with [[KR6q0449]] as a single Guǎng-zhēn corpus.** B238 (3 juan) is a selective *yǔ-lù* compilation; B239 (20 juan) is the full *zhèng-lù*. Both share the compiler **Dēng-lái** [[燈來]]; the latter is explicitly marked *chóng-biān* 重編 in the catalog, indicating it is the definitive expansion of the selective volume.
- **Three-teachings-unity Chán.** The Jù-yún-pài is distinctive within the Míng-Qīng Línjì field for its programmatic *sān-jiào yī-guàn* orientation and for producing catechism-style didactic texts ([[KR6q0235]], 釋教三字經) aimed at lay-scholar audiences — quite different in character from the contemporary Mì-yún / Hàn-yuè disputation-literature of Jiāng-nán.
