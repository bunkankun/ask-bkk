# Yún-fēng Tǐ-zōng Níng chán-shī yǔ-lù 雲峰體宗寧禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tǐ-zōng Níng of Yún-fēng [-sì]*
by [[寧]] (說), [[續清]] (等編), [[德玉]] (序), [[徹生]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tǐ-zōng Dào-níng** [[寧]] 體宗道寧 (**1598-07-12 – 1669**, age 72, *sēng-là* 37), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Native of **Lú-zhōu** 瀘州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Zhàn-yī Xù-qīng** [[續清]] *děng biān*.

The *yǔ-lù* documents three Sì-chuān abbacies: **Lú-zhōu Fāng-shān Yún-fēng-sì** 瀘州方山雲峰寺 (the principal seat, entered **順治九年三月十三日** = **1652-04-21** on the invitation of *Chuān-nán-dào* 川南道 official **Wú Dēng** 吳登 and the assembled prefectural gentry); **Jiāng-ān-xiàn Pán-lóng-sì** 江安縣蟠龍寺; and **Nán-xī-xiàn Kāi-yuán-sì** 南溪縣開元寺. The Yún-fēng-sì entry is the same monastery where **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] was ordained as a *shā-mí* in **丁酉冬** = **1657** winter (cf. [[KR6q0544]]).

Two cross-lineage prefaces are preserved: by **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] (signed 華岩法弟德玉, "younger dharma-brother") dated **康熙乙丑孟夏浴佛日** = **1685-05-10** (Buddha's Birthday); and by **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] (signed 青城法姪徹生, "younger dharma-nephew") dated **康熙乙亥正陽佛誕之吉** = **1695-05-19**. *notBefore* = **1652** (Yún-fēng-sì entry); *notAfter* = **1695** (latest preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B431.

**Contents.** Single juan: two prefaces; *Pán-lóng-sì yǔ-lù*; *Kāi-yuán-sì yǔ-lù*; *jī-yuán*; *sòng-gǔ* on classic *gōng-àn* (世尊初生, 雪山成道, 達磨見武帝, 丹霞燒木佛, 本來面目, 女子入定, 國師三喚侍者, 倩女離魂, plus two *Yuán-jué* / *Léng-yán* themes); *jì*; closing materials including the *xíng-shí* and the *fēn-dēng* listing six dharma-heirs.

## Other points of interest

The text was published roughly 25 years after the master's death, with prefaces composed across a 10-year span (1685, 1695) by two younger Pò-shān-line cousins both based in major Sì-chuān monastic centers (Huá-yán-sì and Qīng-chéng Fèng-lín-sì). This pattern of late-Kāngxī cross-monastery preface-exchange is characteristic of the Jiā-xīng-canon Sì-chuān cluster (cf. [[KR6q0538]], [[KR6q0539]], [[KR6q0544]], [[KR6q0545]], [[KR6q0547]]) and reflects the post-Sānfān-luàn institutional consolidation of southwestern Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism.
