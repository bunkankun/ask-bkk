# Dōu-shuài Bù-lín Jiān chán-shī yǔ-lù 兜率不磷堅禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bù-lín Jiān of Dōu-shuài*
by [[宗堅]] (說), [[妙聖]] (等記)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Bù-lín Zōng-jiān** [[宗堅]] 不磷宗堅, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yě-zhú Fú-huì** [[福慧]] in the Pòshān → Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo → Shān-huī Bì → Yě-zhú → Bù-lín Sì-chuān-Yún-nán sub-line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Miào-shèng** [[妙聖]] *děng*. Front-preface by lay scholar **Qián Guāng-xiù Shèng-yuè** 錢光繡聖月 *zhé-ān yě-sǒu* of **Yín** 鄞 (Níng-bō), dated **康熙癸丑除夕前三日 ≈ 1674-02-01 Gregorian**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B285. Title-form references his **Lù-nán-zhōu Dōu-shuài Chán-yuàn** 路南州兜率禪院 (Yún-nán) abbacy. Contents: *xù*; *shàng-juan* *shàng-táng*; *zhōng-juan* *xiǎo-cān* / *jī-yuán*; *xià-juan* *fǎ-yǔ* / *jì*.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[宗堅]] note and the Qián Guāng-xiù preface: native of **Qí-yáng** 蘄陽 (Hú-běi); ministry in Yún-nán's **Nán-zhào** 南詔 region. Eventually succeeded **Yě-zhú Fú-huì** at the **Yún-nán-fǔ Sōng-shān** 雲南府嵩山 seat (*jì xí Sōng-shān* 繼席嵩山) — the senior Yún-nán Pòshān-line abbacy.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1665** (conservative bound corresponding to the 1665 cutoff of Fú-huì's *qián-lù* [[KR6q0438]] and the start of Bù-lín's documented preaching career as Fú-huì's heir). *notAfter* = **1674** (Qián Guāng-xiù preface). The volume covers the **Lù-nán Dōu-shuài** abbacy (the Yún-nán Lù-nán-zhōu seat from which the title derives) and post-Fú-huì succession at Sōng-shān.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎi-míng → Zì-shuǐ Yuán-liáo → Shān-huī Bì 山暉璧 → Yě-zhú Fú-huì → Bù-lín Zōng-jiān. The Qián Guāng-xiù preface emphasizes Bù-lín's claim to the broader Sòng-Míng Línjì pedigree: *fù Yáng-qí ér zǔ Shí-shuāng* 父楊岐而祖石霜 (his "father" is Yáng-qí, his "grandfather" Shí-shuāng) — invoking the two seminal Línjì-school figures Yáng-qí Fāng-huì 楊岐方會 (992–1049) and Shí-shuāng Chǔ-yuán 石霜楚圓 (986–1039) at the foundation of the Yáng-qí branch.

**Wú Sān-guì-period frontier ministry.** Bù-lín's documented Yún-nán ministry coincides exactly with the **Wú Sān-guì** 吳三桂 *Píng-xī Wáng* administration in Yún-nán (1660–73), making the volume a witness to the late-1660s and early-1670s Yún-nán Chán mission-network on the eve of the **1673 Three-Feudatories rebellion** outbreak. Qián's preface, dated just one day before the lunar new year of **甲寅 = 1674**, was composed in the immediate aftermath of the rebellion's outbreak (1673/11) — making it one of very few Yún-nán-related prefaces composed during the early phase of the war.

- **Three-Feudatories-era Yún-nán Chán.** This and [[KR6q0466]] together preserve some of the most detailed Chán *yǔ-lù* documentation of Yún-nán Chán ministry under the 1660s–70s Wú Sān-guì regime, on either side of the 1673 rebellion outbreak.
