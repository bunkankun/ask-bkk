# Yuè-chuáng Liǎo chán-shī yǔ-lù 月幢了禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yuè-chuáng Liǎo*
by [[了]] (說), [[達最]] (等編)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo** [[了]] 月幢徹了 (1614–1667), Línjì *chán-shī* of the Pòshān → Zhàng-xuě "Shǔ-zōng" line, dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695) and — by imperial grace — bearer of the dharma-name **Zhēn-fó** 真佛 bestowed by the Southern-Míng **Yǒng-lì** emperor. Compiled by the dharma-heir **Zǔ-bí Dá-zuì** [[達最]] 祖鼻達最. Opens with a single preface by **Xú Shì-wěi** 徐士煒 of Gū-sū Hán-shān 姑蘇寒山, signed *xūn-mù shū* 薰沐書. Contents: juan 1 — *shàng-táng*; juan 2 — *xiǎo-cān* / *guǎng-lù* / *rù-shì jī-yuán* / *kān-yàn jī-yuán* / *wèn-dá jī-yuán* / *fǎ-yǔ* / **行實** *xíng-shí*; juan 3 — *sòng-gǔ* / *fēn-dēng* / *shū-wèn* / *zhū-zàn*; juan 4 — *jì* / *gē*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B237.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Juan-1 opens at **Yún-nán-fǔ Shí-bǎo Chán-yuàn** 雲南府石寶禪院 with a *gēng-zǐ 3/14* dating-notice — **1660/4/22 Gregorian** — when Yuè-chuáng was formally requested to take the abbacy by the Yún-nán lay patrons **Yǔ-hóng Wáng-gōng** 禹洪王公 and **Míng-yǔ Yáng-gōng** 明宇楊公. The juan-2 *xíng-shí*, narrated at **Ān-lóng Hú-shān** 安龍湖山 in **甲午 春 = 1654 spring** to the *guān-táng* disciple **Gū-dēng** 孤燈, gives the principal biographical record: born **甲寅 7/20 未時** = **1614-08-24** at **Jiāng-jīn** 江津 (Sì-chuān Chóng-qìng), lay surname **Máo** 毛 / **Táo** 陶 (mother); decisive *xǐng* under **Guī-yuán dà-shī** 歸源大師 on *wàn-fǎ guī-yī*; consolidation under **Líng-fá** 靈筏, **Xiàng-yái** 象崖, and finally **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** at **Yǔ-mén** 禹門 in 己丑 臘 = **1650 early**; formal transmission **辛卯 夏** (**1651**) with *yuán-liú fú-zǐ xìn-jīn* 源流拂子信金.

**Abbacies and dating.** The 行實 covers the period through 辛卯 1651 transmission; subsequent seats (1651–66) are documented in the juan-1 *shàng-táng* and juan-2 *xiǎo-cān*. The Shí-bǎo (Yún-nán-fǔ) abbacy begins **1660/3/14 lunar = 1660/4/22 Gregorian**. Prior to the 1660 Yún-nán-fǔ ministry, Yuè-chuáng had opened dharma at a smaller Shí-bǎo-yuàn in **癸巳 冬 = 1653** per the DILA authority note. Death date **Kāng-xī 丙午 季冬 12/22** = **1667-01-16** (*shì-shòu* 53), per the parallel DILA entry, gives *notAfter* = **1667**. The compiled corpus cannot post-date the master's death; the first datable event is **1654** at Ān-lóng (the 行實 narration), whence *notBefore* = **1654**.

**Imperial patronage.** The Southern-Míng **Yǒng-lì** emperor (reigned in Guì-zhōu / Yún-nán 1647–61), admiring Yuè-chuáng's reputation, requested dharma-instruction in verse, bestowed the name **Zhēn-fó** 真佛 and a *chěn-fú* robe, and made substantial treasury donations for state *zhù-xī* 祝釐. Two poetic memorials *biǎo-chéng jì-sòng* 表呈偈頌 are recorded. This is one of the rare direct Southern-Míng imperial patronage-episodes in Qīng-era Chán *yǔ-lù* literature.

**Lineage.** Yuè-chuáng stands in the Pòshān Hǎimíng → Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì → Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo transmission, part of the **Shǔ-zōng → Qián-nán** 蜀宗 → 黔南 displacement of the mid-seventeenth century as Sichuanese Línjì masters migrated into Guì-zhōu and Yún-nán under pressure of the 1644+ warfare. The principal *dá*-generation heirs **Jiàn-duān Dá-zǔ**, **Zǔ-bí Dá-zuì** (this volume's compiler), **Shàn-quán Dá-wèi**, **Jí-shèng Dào-zhēn**, and **Xiǎn-jīn Dá-gǔ** make Yuè-chuáng the principal origin-point of the early-Qīng **Qián-nán** Chán establishment.

## Other points of interest

- **Name disambiguation.** DILA authority standardizes the dharma-name as **徹了** with **月幢了** / **月幢** / **月幢徹了** / the lay name **毛應僧** and the imperially-bestowed **真佛** all attested. The Jiā-xīng Canon catalog form is the bare single-character **了**, followed here with the person-note at [[了]].
- **Southern-Míng imperial patronage.** Among early-Qīng Chán *yǔ-lù*, the volume is distinctive for recording a Yǒng-lì court imperial-summons episode with memorial verses — preserving material that would otherwise have been purged from a Qīng-era imprint had the text not been prepared for the Jiā-xīng Canon's relatively permissive editorial framework.
