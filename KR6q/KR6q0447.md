# Yuèchuáng Liǎo chánshī yǔlù 月幢了禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yuè-chuáng Liǎo*
by [[了]] (說), [[達最]] (等編)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔlù* of **Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo** [[了]] 月幢徹了 (1614–1667), Línjì *chánshī* of the Pòshān → Zhàngxuě "Shǔzōng" line, dharma-heir of **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1695) and — by imperial grace — bearer of the dharma-name **Zhēnfó** 真佛 bestowed by the Southern-Míng **Yǒnglì** emperor. Compiled by the dharma-heir **Zǔbí Dázuì** [[達最]] 祖鼻達最. Opens with a single preface by **Xú Shìwěi** 徐士煒 of Gūsū Hánshān 姑蘇寒山, signed *xūnmù shū* 薰沐書. Contents: juan 1 — *shàngtáng*; juan 2 — *xiǎocān* / *guǎnglù* / *rùshì jīyuán* / *kānyàn jīyuán* / *wèndá jīyuán* / *fǎyǔ* / **行實** *xíngshí*; juan 3 — *sònggǔ* / *fēndēng* / *shūwèn* / *zhūzàn*; juan 4 — *jì* / *gē*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B237.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Juan-1 opens at **Yúnnánfǔ Shíbǎo Chányuàn** 雲南府石寶禪院 with a *gēngzǐ 3/14* dating-notice — **1660/4/22 Gregorian** — when Yuèchuáng was formally requested to take the abbacy by the Yúnnán lay patrons **Yǔhóng Wánggōng** 禹洪王公 and **Míngyǔ Yánggōng** 明宇楊公. The juan-2 *xíngshí*, narrated at **Ānlóng Húshān** 安龍湖山 in **甲午 春 = 1654 spring** to the *guāntáng* disciple **Gūdēng** 孤燈, gives the principal biographical record: born **甲寅 7/20 未時** = **1614-08-24** at **Jiāngjīn** 江津 (Sìchuān Chóngqìng), lay surname **Máo** 毛 / **Táo** 陶 (mother); decisive *xǐng* under **Guīyuán dàshī** 歸源大師 on *wànfǎ guīyī*; consolidation under **Língfá** 靈筏, **Xiàngyái** 象崖, and finally **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** at **Yǔmén** 禹門 in 己丑 臘 = **1650 early**; formal transmission **辛卯 夏** (**1651**) with *yuánliú fúzǐ xìnjīn* 源流拂子信金.

**Abbacies and dating.** The 行實 covers the period through 辛卯 1651 transmission; subsequent seats (1651–66) are documented in the juan-1 *shàngtáng* and juan-2 *xiǎocān*. The Shíbǎo (Yúnnánfǔ) abbacy begins **1660/3/14 lunar = 1660/4/22 Gregorian**. Prior to the 1660 Yúnnánfǔ ministry, Yuèchuáng had opened dharma at a smaller Shíbǎoyuàn in **癸巳 冬 = 1653** per the DILA authority note. Death date **Kāngxī 丙午 季冬 12/22** = **1667-01-16** (*shìshòu* 53), per the parallel DILA entry, gives *notAfter* = **1667**. The compiled corpus cannot post-date the master's death; the first datable event is **1654** at Ānlóng (the 行實 narration), whence *notBefore* = **1654**.

**Imperial patronage.** The Southern-Míng **Yǒnglì** emperor (reigned in Guìzhōu / Yúnnán 1647–61), admiring Yuèchuáng's reputation, requested dharma-instruction in verse, bestowed the name **Zhēnfó** 真佛 and a *chěnfú* robe, and made substantial treasury donations for state *zhùxī* 祝釐. Two poetic memorials *biǎochéng jìsòng* 表呈偈頌 are recorded. This is one of the rare direct Southern-Míng imperial patronage-episodes in Qīng-era Chán *yǔlù* literature.

**Lineage.** Yuèchuáng stands in the Pòshān Hǎimíng → Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì → Yuèchuáng Chèliǎo transmission, part of the **Shǔzōng → Qiánnán** 蜀宗 → 黔南 displacement of the mid-seventeenth century as Sichuanese Línjì masters migrated into Guìzhōu and Yúnnán under pressure of the 1644+ warfare. The principal *dá*-generation heirs **Jiànduān Dázǔ**, **Zǔbí Dázuì** (this volume's compiler), **Shànquán Dáwèi**, **Jíshèng Dàozhēn**, and **Xiǎnjīn Dágǔ** make Yuèchuáng the principal origin-point of the early-Qīng **Qiánnán** Chán establishment.

## Other points of interest

- **Name disambiguation.** DILA authority standardizes the dharma-name as **徹了** with **月幢了** / **月幢** / **月幢徹了** / the lay name **毛應僧** and the imperially-bestowed **真佛** all attested. The Jiāxīng Canon catalog form is the bare single-character **了**, followed here with the person-note at [[了]].
- **Southern-Míng imperial patronage.** Among early-Qīng Chán *yǔlù*, the volume is distinctive for recording a Yǒnglì court imperial-summons episode with memorial verses — preserving material that would otherwise have been purged from a Qīng-era imprint had the text not been prepared for the Jiāxīng Canon's relatively permissive editorial framework.
