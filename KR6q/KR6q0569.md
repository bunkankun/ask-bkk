# Tōng-tiān Yì-sǒu Gāo chán-shī yǔ-lù 通天逸叟高禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yì-sǒu Gāo of Tōng-tiān [-sì]*
by [[行高]] (說), [[明德]] (重梓), [[超原]] (行略)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tōng-tiān Yì-sǒu Xíng-gāo** [[行高]] 通天逸叟行高 (**1616 – 1669-04-12**, age 54, *sēng-là* 40), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** 萬如通微 (1594–1657). Native of **Xīng-huà Xiān-yóu** 興化仙遊 (Fú-jiàn), lay surname **Chén** 陳. *Zhòng-zǐ* (re-engraving) by his dharma-grandson **Míng-dé** [[明德]]; **行略** (life-narrative) authored by his sole *fǎ-sì* **Dàn-yá Chāo-yuán** [[超原]] (1630–1698). Documents teaching at his sole abbacy **Pú-yáng Tōng-tiān chán-sì** 蒲陽通天禪寺 (Fú-jiàn). The *xíng-lüè* records the formal *fù-fǎ* transmission to Chāo-yuán on **康熙己酉年正月元日** = **1669-02-01**, just two months before Yì-sǒu's death. *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-Wàn-rú-death abbacy); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (the *zhòng-zǐ* postdates Chāo-yuán's 1698 death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B455.

**Contents.** Single juan: *mù-lù*; *fǎ-yǔ* and verses including *yǔ Dàn-yá Yuán hòu-táng* 與澹崖原後堂 (to his disciple [[超原]]) and *yǔ Huá-sī Yuán xī-táng* 與華斯圓西堂; *jī-yuán*; the closing *xíng-lüè* by 超原.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0569 and the immediately following [[KR6q0570]] form a teacher-disciple sequence — both compiled / re-engraved by the same *fǎ-sūn* **Míng-dé** [[明德]] in the late-1690s / early-1700s — capturing two generations of the **Pú-yáng Tōng-tiān chán-sì** abbacy (Yì-sǒu d. 1669; Chāo-yuán d. 1698) within the **Wàn-rú** branch of Línjì-Yángqí. The compactness of Yì-sǒu's single juan reflects his short post-Wàn-rú-death career (only ~12 years).
