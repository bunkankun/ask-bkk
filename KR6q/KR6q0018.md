# Wǔdēng yántǒng mùlù 五燈嚴統目錄

compiled by [[通容]] (Tōngróng, sobriquet Fèiyǐn 費隱, 1593–1661), two-juan table of contents to the *Wǔdēng yántǒng* ([[KR6q0019]])

## About the work

The two-juan table of contents to Fèiyǐn Tōngróng's 25-juan *Wǔdēng yántǒng* 五燈嚴統 ([[KR6q0019]]), the controversial 1653 Línjì-orthodox revision of the *Wǔdēng huìyuán*. Separately catalogued in the Xuzangjing.
