# Mǐn-shù chán-shī yǔ-lù 敏樹禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mǐn-shù*
by [[如相]] (說), [[道崇]] (編), [[道領]] (錄)

Substantial ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** [[如相]] 敏樹如相 (**1603 – 1672-12-21**, age 70, *sēng-là* 45), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) and the foundational figure of the major **Mǐn-shù** 敏樹 sub-line of southwestern Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism. Native of **Tóng-chuān-zhōu Lín-shuǐ** 潼川州鄰水 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Wáng** 王. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Tiān-yǐn Dào-chóng** [[道崇]] *biān* + recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Chì-sōng Dào-lǐng** [[道領]] *lù* (whose own *yǔ-lù* is [[KR6q0565]]). Two prefaces dated **康熙戊辰蒲月** = **1688** lunar 5 by 貴陽黔靈山弘福寺.

The *yǔ-lù* documents seven successive abbacies across the southwestern frontier circuit: **Sì-chuān Diàn-jiāng-xiàn Bǎi-zhàng chán-yuàn** 四川墊江縣百丈禪院 (the principal seat); **Hú-guǎng Shī-zhōu-wèi Wàn-shòu chán-sì** 湖廣施州衛萬壽禪寺; **Sì-chuān Péng-shuǐ-xiàn Tài-píng chán-sì** 四川彭水縣太平禪寺; **Guì-zhōu Shí-qiān-fǔ Sān-mèi chán-yuàn** 貴州石阡府三昧禪院; **Sì-chuān Zūn-yì-fǔ Hǎi-lóng-shān Gǔ-lóng chán-yuàn** 四川遵義府海龍山古龍禪院; **Wàn-xiàn Cí-yún chán-yuàn** 萬縣慈雲禪院 (re-entered); **Guì-zhōu Dà-xīng chán-sì** 貴州大興禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1645** (post-*fù-fǎ* abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1688** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B450.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces, *mù-lù*, *Bǎi-zhàng-yuàn yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Wàn-shòu-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *Tài-píng-sì yǔ-lù*; *Sān-mèi-yuàn yǔ-lù*. Juan 4: *Gǔ-lóng-yuàn yǔ-lù*; *Cí-yún-yuàn yǔ-lù*; *Dà-xīng-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 5: *xiǎo-cān*, *pǔ-shuō*, *chá-huà*. Juan 6–10: continuing materials including *jī-yuán*, *fó-shì*, the *xíng-zhuàng* and *tǎ-míng* (death **康熙壬子十一月初三日亥時** = **1672-12-21**), and the *fēn-dēng* listing twenty-four *fǎ-sì*.

## Other points of interest

The Mǐn-shù sub-line founded by Rú-xiāng is one of the largest Línjì-Yángqí lineage-clusters in the late-17th-century Sì-chuān-Guì-zhōu-Yún-nán region, parallel to the **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** 丈雪通醉 and **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** 聖可德玉 sub-lines also under Pò-shān Hǎi-míng. Its 24 *fǎ-sì* span the entire southwestern frontier; many of these are independently documented in J38–J39 *yǔ-lù*: 耳毒泰 → [[明|Huàn-zhù Míng]] (KR6q0549), 耳毒泰 → [[廣德]] (KR6q0550), [[道領]] (KR6q0565), 穎秀 → [[性炅]] (KR6q0524), etc.
