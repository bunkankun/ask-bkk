# Gǔ-sù Zūn chán-shī yǔ-lù 古宿尊禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Gǔ-sù Zūn*
by [[尊]] (說), [[明圓]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Gǔ-sù Zūn** [[尊]] 古宿尊, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiāng-yán Dàng-shān Yuǎn-wēng** 香巖宕山遠翁. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Bīng-lún Míng-yuán** [[明圓]] 冰輪明圓 *děng biān*. Native of Chéng-dū; abbacies at **Dèng-zhōu Dān-xiá**, **Xiāng-zhōu Dòng-shān**, and Shǎn-xī monasteries. *notBefore* / *notAfter*: c. 1660–1690. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B387.
