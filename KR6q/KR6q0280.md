# Chāo-zōng Huì-fāng chánshī yǔlù 超宗慧方禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Chāo-zōng Huì-fāng* — the fourth and final *yǔlù* in the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* 黃龍四家錄 series. One-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1345.

**Chāo-zōng Huì-fāng** 超宗慧方: Northern-Sòng Huánglóng Línjì-sub-lineage master; fourth master in the canonical Huánglóng-sub-lineage collection. Lifedates and full biographical details meagre.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100; *notAfter* c. 1150.
