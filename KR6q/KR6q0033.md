# Zhèngyuán lüèjí bǔyí 正源略集補遺

compiled by [[達珍]] (Dázhēn), one-juan supplement to the *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* ([[KR6q0032]])

## About the work

A single-juan supplement gathering material Dázhēn had omitted from, or come across after completing, the *Zhèngyuán lüèjí* ([[KR6q0032]]).

### Tiyao


### Abstract

Additional Chán figures and dialogues in the same anthology format as the parent work. Preserves its conservative editorial orientation.
