# Dǒu-nán Wěi chán-shī yǔ-lù 斗南暐禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dǒu-nán Wěi*
by [[暐]] (說), [[普潤]] (等錄)

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Dǒu-nán Wěi** [[暐]] 斗南暐 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bié-ān Xìng-tǒng** 別庵性統 (1661–1717) of **Pǔ-tuó-shān** — thus a co-disciple of **Cuì-yá Bì** [[必]] (cf. [[KR6q0595]]). Recorded by attendant **Pǔ-rùn** [[普潤]] *děng lù* (with co-recorder **Fǎ-quán** 法權). Documents teaching at two Zhè-jiāng / Jiāng-sū abbacies: **Wēn-zhōu Jiē-dài chán-sì** 溫州接待禪寺 and **Wú-xī Cí-yún chán-sì** 無錫慈雲禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1700**; *notAfter* ≈ **1730**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B488.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *Wēn-zhōu Jiē-dài-sì yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*; *bǐng-fú*; *jī-yuán*; *niān* on classic *gōng-àn* (大事未明 / 大事已明 如喪考妣, 青林挑柴, 國師三喚侍者, 南泉住庵). Juan 2: *sòng-gǔ*. Juan 3: *jì* (eyes-ears-nose-tongue-body-mind 六首, *sì-wēi-yí*, *tí Shí-nǚ fū-rén* 題石女夫人, *tí Dàn-hé-shàng* 題淡和尚, *shì-zhōng lǐ-chàn yǒu gǎn* 室中禮懺有感, *cǎi-bǎi-zǐ* 採柏子).

## Other points of interest

KR6q0595 ([[必]]) and KR6q0596 ([[暐]]) are paired *yǔ-lù* of two co-disciples of **Bié-ān Xìng-tǒng** 別庵性統 (the Pǔ-tuó-shān Línjì-Yángqí mainstay), documenting the early-18th-century outflow from Pǔ-tuó-shān into the Zhè-jiāng / Jiāng-sū / Beijing axis. Together with [[KR6q0209]] 《高峰三山來禪師年譜》 (Xìng-tǒng's compilation of his teacher's chronology) and [[KR6q0203]] (Xìng-tǒng's own *yǔ-lù*), the cluster captures three generations of the **Sān-shān Dēng-lái 三山燈來** → **Bié-ān Xìng-tǒng** → second-generation lineage at the most prominent late-Qīng coastal Buddhist site.
