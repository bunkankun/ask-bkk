# Pǔ-jué Zōng-gǎo chánshī yǔlù 普覺宗杲禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Pǔ-jué Zōng-gǎo* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **[[宗杲|Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo]]** 大慧宗杲 (1089–1163), the dominant Southern-Sòng Chán master and originator of *kàn huà chán* 看話禪 method. *Pǔ-jué* 普覺 is Zōng-gǎo's posthumous title. Compiled by his dharma-heirs [[法宏|Fǎ-hóng]] 法宏 and [[道謙|Dào-qiān]] 道謙.

Two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1362. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

This is a compact recension of Zōng-gǎo's teachings, companion to the much larger *Dà-huì Pǔ-jué chánshī yǔlù* (T47 n1998A, 30 juan) — the principal canonical *yǔlù* of Zōng-gǎo.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1130; *notAfter* 1190 (Jìng-shān Zǔ-qìng's 1190 preface to the *Pǔ-jué Zōng-gǎo chánshī yǔlù* provides a useful chronological anchor).
