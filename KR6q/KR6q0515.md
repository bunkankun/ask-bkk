# Běijīng Chǔlín chánshī yǔlù 北京楚林禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chǔ-lín of Beijing*
by [[上睿]] (說), [[照琮]] (等錄)

Six-juan *yǔlù* of **Chǔlín Shàngruì** [[上睿]] 楚林上睿 at **Jīngdū Bàoēnsì** 京都報恩寺 in Beijing, LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yúzhāo Yǔnfén** [[允汾]] 俞昭允汾 (1622–1680) in the Hànyuè Sānfēng → Hóngchǔ line. Recorded by *fǎsì* **Zhàocōng** [[照琮]] *děng lù*. Notable as one of the few documented Sān-fēng-line abbacies in the Beijing capital — bringing the line that would face Yōngzhèng's 1733 proscription into Qīng-court proximity. *notBefore* = c. **1680** (post-master-decease succession), *notAfter* = c. **1700** as conservative bound. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B390.
