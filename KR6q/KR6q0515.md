# Běi-jīng Chǔ-lín chán-shī yǔ-lù 北京楚林禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chǔ-lín of Beijing*
by [[上睿]] (說), [[照琮]] (等錄)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Chǔ-lín Shàng-ruì** [[上睿]] 楚林上睿 at **Jīng-dū Bào-ēn-sì** 京都報恩寺 in Beijing, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yú-zhāo Yǔn-fén** [[允汾]] 俞昭允汾 (1622–1680) in the Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng → Hóng-chǔ line. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Zhào-cōng** [[照琮]] *děng lù*. Notable as one of the few documented Sān-fēng-line abbacies in the Beijing capital — bringing the line that would face Yōng-zhèng's 1733 proscription into Qīng-court proximity. *notBefore* = c. **1680** (post-master-decease succession), *notAfter* = c. **1700** as conservative bound. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B390.
