# Bó-shān Sù-rú Hàn chán-shī yǔ-lù 博山粟如瀚禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Sù-rú Hàn of Bó-shān*
by [[弘瀚]] (說), [[傅鵬]] (編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Sù-rú Hóng-hàn** [[弘瀚]] 粟如弘瀚 (**1630-03-16 – 1706-04-10**, age 77, *sēng-là* 54), **Cáodòng-school** dharma-heir of **Xuě-jiàn Dào-fèng** 雪澗道奉 (1592–1670) at **Bó-shān Néng-rén-sì** 博山能仁寺 — **Qīng-yuán 33rd-generation** descendant; **6th-generation abbot of Bó-shān** in the **Wú-yì Yuán-lái** 無異元來 (1576–1630) line. Native of **Yǒng-fēng** 永豐 (Jiāng-xī), lay surname **Yú** 余. Compiled by lay disciple **Fù Péng** [[傅鵬]] *biān*. *notBefore* = **1668** (*fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1706** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B490.

Documents teaching at three Jiāng-xī abbacies: **Xìn-zhōu Bó-shān Néng-rén-sì** 信州博山能仁寺 (the Cáodòng mother-temple, held twice — *shàng-táng yī* and *zài-zhù shàng-táng sān*); **Xìn-zhōu Gāo-quán** 信州高泉; **Xìn-zhōu Yíng-shān** 信州瀛山.

**Contents.** Juan 1: portrait + *zì-zàn*; preface and post-preface; *zǒng-mù*; *Bó-shān shàng-táng yī*. Juan 2: *Bó-shān shàng-táng èr*; *Gāo-quán yǔ-lù*; *Yíng-shān yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *zài-zhù Bó-shān shàng-táng sān*. Juan 4: *xiǎo-cān yī*; *zài-zhù Bó-shān xiǎo-cān èr*. Juan 5: *shì-zhòng yī*; *zài-zhù shì-zhòng èr*; *shǒu-zuò liáo bǐng-fú*; *rù-shì jī-yuán*. Juan 6: *niān-sòng* (with the rare *èr shāng-rén yú zhū* 二商人舁豬 *gōng-àn*); closing *xíng-zhuàng* + 〈青原三十三世博山第六代住持粟如瀚大師塔銘〉.

## Other points of interest

This is the principal **Bó-shān Cáodòng** *yǔ-lù* of the late 17th century, continuing the lineage of **Wú-yì Yuán-lái** 無異元來 → **Xuě-jiàn Dào-fèng** → Hóng-hàn — the same lineage out of which **Yǒng-jué Yuán-xián** 永覺元賢 split into the Gǔ-shān (Fú-jiàn) sub-line. The 6-generation succession at Bó-shān is one of the most stable continuous Cáodòng abbacy-lines of the Qīng era, paralleling the contemporaneous **Tiān-rán Hán-shì** [[函昰]] line in Lǐng-nán (cf. [[KR6q0526]]).
