# Yě-yún Yìng chánshī zōng tǒng sòng 野雲映禪師宗統頌

*Lineage-Genealogy Verses of Chán Master Yě-yún Yìng* — a one-juan Qīng Chán-lineage verse-commentary by [[燈映|Yě-yún Dēng-yìng]] 野雲燈映, providing verse-responses (*sòng* 頌) to the transmission-encounters of each patriarch in the classical Línjì lineage descending from Nányuè Huáiràng. Compiled by his dharma-heir [[性燦|Xìng-cǎn]] 性燦.

## About the work

A one-juan lineage-genealogical verse-commentary, J40 B484. Non-commentary on a single parent text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text follows the *zōng tǒng sòng* 宗統頌 genre pattern: for each classical Línjì patriarch from Nányuè Huáiràng (the first post-Huìnéng generation) onward, the text quotes the standard transmission-exchange from the lamp-records and appends Yě-yún's responding verse-commentary. The approach parallels [[通門|Mù-yún Tōng-mén]]'s *Zōng běn tóu jī sòng* [[KR6q0213]] but at more modest scale.

### Abstract

[[燈映|Yě-yún Dēng-yìng]] 野雲燈映 (DILA likely unregistered): Qīng Chán master. *Hào* Yě-yún 野雲 ("Wild Cloud"). Lifedates unrecorded; active mid-to-late 17th century based on the text's canonical inclusion.

**Compiler [[性燦|Xìng-cǎn]]** 性燦: Yě-yún's dharma-heir. Lifedates unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1650; *notAfter* c. 1700 (broad Qīng bracket based on canonical-inclusion context).
