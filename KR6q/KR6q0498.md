# Bǎi-yú chán-shī yǔ-lù 百愚禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bǎi-yú*
by [[淨斯]] (說), [[智操]] (等編語錄), [[智海]] (等編語錄)

Twenty-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Bǎi-yú Jìng-sī** [[淨斯]] 百愚淨斯 (**1610-07-11 – 1665-10-06**, *shì-shòu* 56), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Ruì-bái Míng-xuě** [[明雪]] 瑞白明雪 (1584–1641). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-cāo** [[智操]] + **Zhì-hǎi** [[智海]] *děng biān-yǔ-lù*. Eight abbacies in Jiāng-nán: **Jiā-dìng Zhāo-qìng** → **Qīng-lóng Lóng-fú** → **Biàn-shān Lóng-huá** → **Yuè-zhōu Néng-rén** → **Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng** (Cáo-dòng flagship) → **Gāo-tíng Fó-rì** → **Guó-shān Shàn-quán** → **Jiāng-dū Shàn-qìng**. 42 named heirs. Juan 20 includes the *tǎ-zhì-míng*. *notBefore* = c. 1640 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1665 (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B359.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J36 B359). Juan 20 *tǎ-zhì-míng* is the principal biographical witness.*
