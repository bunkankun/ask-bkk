# Yǎn-xī Guǎng-wén chánshī yǔlù 偃溪廣聞禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Yǎn-xī Guǎng-wén* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Yǎn-xī Guǎng-wén** 偃溪廣聞 (1189–1263), Línjì-Yángqí master; dharma-heir of Pò-ān Zǔ-xiān 破菴祖先. Compiled by nine of his attendant-disciples.

Two-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1368.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1200; *notAfter* c. 1270.
