# Jiàowài biéchuán 教外別傳

*Transmission Outside the Teaching*

compiled by [[黎眉]] (Lí Méi) and others, late Míng

## About the work

A 16-juan late-Míng Chán anthology whose title — "Transmission Outside the Teaching" — invokes the standard Chán slogan (教外別傳，不立文字, "A transmission outside the scriptures; not dependent on words"). Compiled by Lí Méi together with other late-Míng monastic editors; emphasises the teaching-dialogues of the orthodox Línjì and Cáodòng lines.

### Abstract

One of the late-Míng Chán anthology compilations that flourished alongside the *Zhǐyuè lù* of [[瞿汝稷|Qú Rǔjī]] ([[KR6q0023]]) — the late sixteenth and early seventeenth centuries saw substantial lay and monastic interest in curated Chán readers, and the *Jiàowài biéchuán* is a representative exemplar. The compilation draws from the Sòng lamp records and from early-Míng recorded-sayings, selecting for pedagogical value rather than genealogical completeness.

## Translations and research

No English translation. No dedicated monograph. Discussed briefly in surveys of Míng Chán literature.

## Other points of interest

The title's programmatic Chán slogan captures the genre's aspiration: a teaching reader that bypasses doctrinal exposition in favour of direct recorded-sayings transmission.
