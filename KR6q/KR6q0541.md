# Lín-wǒ chán-shī yǔ-lù 林我禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lín-wǒ*
by [[鑑]] (說), [[海錱]] (等編錄), [[海茲]] (等編錄)

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Lín-wǒ Jiàn** [[鑑|Lín-wǒ Jiàn]] 林我鑑 (also **Hǎi-bǎo Jiàn** 海寶鑑) (**1612-05-08 – 1679-03-17**, age 68, *sēng-là* 48), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yì-ān Yìn-shī** 易菴印師. Native of **Chuān-běi Láng-zhōu** 川北閬州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Hóu** 侯, displaced to **Qín** 秦 (Shǎn-xī) in **崇禎十七年** = **1644** by the Sì-chuān civil war and based in the **Guān-zhōng / Níng-xià / Gān-sù** northwest for the rest of his life. Compiled jointly by *fǎ-sì* **Hǎi-zhèn** [[海錱]] (= 愚參海金 of Shí-fāng, *xī-táng*) and **Xuě-lín Hǎi-cí** [[海茲]] (of Shuò-fāng / Níng-xià, *zhī-cáng*) *děng biān-lù*. Printed at **Jiā-xīng Léng-yán-sì** 嘉興楞嚴寺 (流通) in **康熙辛未歲臘月吉旦** = **1692-01** (Kāngxī 30, lunar 12, 1st day).

The *yǔ-lù* documents three principal abbacies in succession (titled in juan 1–2): **Guān-zhōng Jīng-yáng Wén-tǎ Tiě-fó chán-sì** 關中涇陽文塔鐵佛禪寺 (opening sermon **康熙乙巳 10/15** = **1665-11-22**), **Cháng-ān Dà-xìng-shàn chán-sì** 長安大興善禪寺 (entered **康熙六年** = **1667**), and **Qín-zhōng Níng-xià Hǎi-bǎo-tǎ-sì** 秦中寧夏海寶塔寺. *notBefore* = **1665** (opening sermon at Tiě-fó); *notAfter* = **1692** (printing). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B422.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *Tiě-fó-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Xīng-shàn-sì yǔ-lù*; *Hǎi-bǎo-tǎ-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *shì-zhòng*, *rù-shì*, *jī-yuán*, *niān-sòng*, *sòng-gǔ*, the *lián-fāng-jì* listing four *fǎ-sì*, and *tí-zàn*. Juan 4: *xíng-shí*, **林我禪師塔銘** (preserving birth and death dates), and the colophon.

## Other points of interest

This is one of the most northwestern Chán *yǔ-lù* in the Jiā-xīng Canon, documenting the rebuilding of Línjì Buddhism in **Guān-zhōng** (Wèi-river plain) and **Níng-xià** during the post-1644 reconstruction. Lín-wǒ's pre-tonsure life included an unusually long period of Tiāntái-school *Fǎ-huá* lecture-circuit attendance (five years) before he turned to Chán under **Bǎo-chí** at Dà-suí-sì — characteristic of the doctrine-followed-by-Chán arc favored in 17th-century Sì-chuān monastic biography. The *Tǎ-míng* notes a *fǎ-huì* of his teacher **Yì-ān Yìn-shī** traveling personally to Cháng-ān in **己酉春** = **1669** to deliver his *fù-fǎ*, an act of long-range succession-formalization unusual in the Yángqí line.
