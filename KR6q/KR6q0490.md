# Sūzhōu Zhúān Yǎn chánshī yǔlù 蘇州竹庵衍禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhú-ān Yǎn of Sū-zhōu*
by [[真衍]] (說), [[機如]] (編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Ǒuān / Zhúān Zhēnyǎn** [[真衍]] 藕菴真衍 (= **竹庵衍**), LínjìYángqí Hǔ-qiū-school dharma-heir of **Jīnmíng Jièlǎo** 金明介老. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Gǔlín Jīrú** [[機如]]. Front-preface (preserved in juan 1) summarizes the lineage: 臨濟 → 15 → 斷橋 / 雪巖 → 斷橋 → 14 → 金明 → 真衍. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J36 B345.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Lineage-position established in the front-preface: 真衍 is *fǎzhí* / dharma-nephew of **Yǒngzhèng Yī** (cf. [[KR6q0455]]) and **Píngshān Zhēnběn** [[真本]] (cf. [[KR6q0202]]) — three parallel **真**-generation Hǔ-qiū-line heirs of Jīnmíng Jièlǎo. Lifedates not preserved.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (post-mid-century preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound.

**Lineage.** Hǔqiū → Wúzhǔn → Duànqiáo Lún (Sòng) → 14 generations → PǔmíngYòng → Jīnmíng Jièlǎo → 真衍 + heir Jīrú [[機如]] (whose own *yǔlù* is at [[KR6q0491]]).

## Other points of interest

- **Jīnmíng Jièlǎo cluster.** With [[KR6q0455]], [[KR6q0456]], [[KR6q0490]] together in the Jiāxīng cluster, the corpus preserves **three of Jīnmíng Jièlǎo's heirs' parallel Línjì sub-lineages** in the Hǔ-qiū-school — making the Jīnmíng circle one of the most extensively documented late-1660s / 1670s LínjìHǔqiū sub-lineage clusters.
