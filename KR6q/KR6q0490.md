# Sū-zhōu Zhú-ān Yǎn chán-shī yǔ-lù 蘇州竹庵衍禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhú-ān Yǎn of Sū-zhōu*
by [[真衍]] (說), [[機如]] (編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Ǒu-ān / Zhú-ān Zhēn-yǎn** [[真衍]] 藕菴真衍 (= **竹庵衍**), Línjì-Yángqí Hǔ-qiū-school dharma-heir of **Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo** 金明介老. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Gǔ-lín Jī-rú** [[機如]]. Front-preface (preserved in juan 1) summarizes the lineage: 臨濟 → 15 → 斷橋 / 雪巖 → 斷橋 → 14 → 金明 → 真衍. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B345.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Lineage-position established in the front-preface: 真衍 is *fǎ-zhí* / dharma-nephew of **Yǒng-zhèng Yī** (cf. [[KR6q0455]]) and **Píng-shān Zhēn-běn** [[真本]] (cf. [[KR6q0202]]) — three parallel **真**-generation Hǔ-qiū-line heirs of Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo. Lifedates not preserved.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (post-mid-century preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound.

**Lineage.** Hǔ-qiū → Wú-zhǔn → Duàn-qiáo Lún (Sòng) → 14 generations → Pǔ-míng-Yòng → Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo → 真衍 + heir Jī-rú [[機如]] (whose own *yǔ-lù* is at [[KR6q0491]]).

## Other points of interest

- **Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo cluster.** With [[KR6q0455]], [[KR6q0456]], [[KR6q0490]] together in the Jiā-xīng cluster, the corpus preserves **three of Jīn-míng Jiè-lǎo's heirs' parallel Línjì sub-lineages** in the Hǔ-qiū-school — making the Jīn-míng circle one of the most extensively documented late-1660s / 1670s Línjì-Hǔ-qiū sub-lineage clusters.
