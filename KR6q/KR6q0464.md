# Tán-xī Zǐ-zhōu Chuán chán-shī yǔ-lù 壇溪梓舟船禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tán-xī Zǐ-zhōu Chuán*
by [[船]] (說), [[明法]] (等編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zǐ-zhōu Chāo-chuán** [[船]] 梓舟超船 (b. **1619-04-29**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Èr-yǐn Bǎo-ān Mì** 二隱寶安謐 at **Jīng-xī Bǎo-ān-sì** 荊溪保安寺. Compiled by *mén-rén* **Míng-fǎ** [[明法]] *děng*. Front-preface by **Zhāng Lián / Tōng-shēng** 武張聯法名通陞 (Hú-guǎng-area lay-Chán figure who served as a military-administrative official supervising grain-transport on the Hàn-shuǐ corridor in the early 1660s). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B281. Contents: juan 1–2 *shàng-táng* / *xiǎo-cān* / *fǎ-yǔ*; juan 3 *jī-yuán* / *jì* / **行實** (autobiographical narrative).

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the juan-3 *xíng-shí* and DILA: native of **Tóng-chuān-zhōu** 童川州 (= **Chéng-dū-fǔ Tóng-chuān** 成都府潼川, Sì-chuān); lay surname **Hé** 何. Tonsured **1640** under **Yìng-chūn** 映春 at Bái-lián-ān; full precepts **1642** at Róng-huá-shān with three-year *xié-bù-zhì-xí* sūtra-prostration practice. Late-Míng warfare disruptions and famine drove him to *Guān-yīn* invocation. **1645** onward: nine-year pilgrimage circuit through *virtually every major Mì-yún-line successor*: Cuì-yán → **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** at Yí-xīng → **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** at Hú-zhōu → **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** at Jìng-shān → **Mù-yún Tōng-mén** at Tiān-tóng → **Jù-dé Hóng-lǐ** at Líng-yǐn → **Mù-chén Dào-mín** at Tiān-tóng → **Zì-xián** at Sān-tǎ. Final teacher **Èr-yǐn Mì** at **Huá-tíng Chuán-zǐ Fǎ-rěn-sì** 華亭船子法忍寺 (whence the dharma-name 船 derives, in resonance with the Táng-era *chuán-zǐ* boatman-master); 4 years of *tòng-bàng*; 3-year *yǐn-guān* at Gǔ-xīn-ān. **庚子 = 1660** transmission at Bǎo-ān-sì on the *chū-guān* "破" reply.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1670** as a conservative bound. The Zhāng Lián preface mentions a 1666 (丙午) meeting at **Wǔ-Hàn** during which Chuán was already in active ministry. The preface itself is undated but post-1666.

**Lineage and abbacies.** Two abbacies in Hú-běi border-region: **Xiāng-zhōu Lù-mén-sì** 襄州鹿門寺 (the *zì-shān* 紫山 abbacy referenced in 鹿門 catalog-form, 2 years post-1660s) and **Tán-xī gǔ-sì** 檀溪古寺 (Hàn-shuǐ valley historic temple, restored by Chuán; whence the title-form 壇溪 / 檀溪 / 鹿門梓舟船). Èr-yǐn → Chuán is one Línjì sub-line of the **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū** 天隱圓修 → **Tōng-shòu** / **Èr-yǐn Mì** Qìng-shān succession (parallel to [[KR6q0463]]'s Yún-wài Xíng-zé via Sōng-jì Tōng-shòu, but a distinct branch).

**Cross-school synthesis.** Zhāng Lián's preface emphasizes Chuán's **doctrinal range** spanning both Línjì and Cáo-dòng currents: *wèi chéng Cáo-dòng zhī mài ér jiān shàn qí mián-mì wú yú-xiān zhī piān; xiàn jiē Lín-jì zhī chuán ér dú dé qí zhí-jié wú lǔ-mǎng zhī shī* 未承曹洞之脈而兼擅其綿密無連纖之偏現接臨濟之傳而獨得其直捷無魯莽之失.

## Other points of interest

- **Comprehensive nine-year pilgrimage circuit.** The 1645–1654 pilgrimage record visits **eight of the major contemporary Línjì masters** (Wàn-rú, Yù-lín, Fèi-yǐn, Mù-yún, Jù-dé, Mù-chén, Cuì-yán, Zì-xián, Èr-yǐn) before final commitment to Èr-yǐn — making this volume a particularly informative witness to the mid-seventeenth-century cross-Línjì-circuit pilgrim practice. Compare [[KR6q0458]]'s Rú-xǐ Cáo-dòng pilgrimage and [[KR6q0462]]'s Bù-huì cross-school circuit for parallel cases.
- **Boat / Chuán-zǐ resonance.** The single-character dharma-name 船 (catalog-form) deliberately echoes the Táng-era **Chuán-zǐ Dé-chéng** 船子德誠 ("Boatman" Dé-chéng), with the master taking up the seat at **Huá-tíng Chuán-zǐ Fǎ-rěn-sì** 華亭船子法忍寺 — the historical site of Chuán-zǐ Dé-chéng's hermitage.
