# Yún-shān Yàn-jū Shēn chán-shī yǔ-lù 雲山燕居申禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yàn-jū Shēn of Yún[-tiān]-shān*
by [[德申]] (說), [[合哲]] (等編)

Eight-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yàn-jū Dé-shēn** [[德申]] 燕居德申 (**1610 – 1678-09-30**, age 69, *sēng-là* 50), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Native of **Xī-Shǔ Zhōng-nán** 西蜀忠南 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Hé-zhé** [[合哲]] *děng biān*.

Documents teaching at seven abbacies, principally **Guì-zhōu Wēi-qīng Máo-pō-shān Yún-tiān chán-sì** 貴州威青茅坡山雲天禪寺 (the principal seat — Guì-zhōu, gives him his sobriquet **Yún-shān**). Other seats include **Liú-zhōng** 流中, **Wàn-xiàng** 萬項, **Dà-xīng** 大興, **Tài-píng** 太平, **Guān-yīn** 觀音, **Shòu-jiè** 受戒. *notBefore* ≈ **1645** (post-1643 *fù-fǎ* + abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1678** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B478.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *shàng-táng* (Yún-tiān seat). Juan 2–3: *xiǎo-cān*; *wǎn-cān*; *qǐng-yì fǎ-yào* (lay-disciple Q&A — addressing **Jiè-fū Guō** 介夫郭居士, **Yú-yě Wáng Hóng-dì** 于野王居士, **Jù-yún Shěn Jīng** 聚雲沈居士, **Jì-míng Lǐ** 繼明李居士, **Wán-bì Liú Zhī-yù** 完璧劉居士, **Sù-míng Qìng-zhǐ** 素明座主, **Sù-méi Chén Lín-fú** 素梅陳居士). Juan 3: *jī-yuán*; *xíng-shí*. Juan 4: *yuán-liú zhuó-sòng* — verses on the lineage stream, originally composed in response to **Pò-shān** 's *chuí-wèn* during his apprenticeship and re-extracted by his teacher's command. Juan 5–8: continuing materials including extensive *sòng-gǔ* and *fǎ-yǔ* — the *Shī-zǐ-lín xiǎo-cān* 獅子林小參 indicates a teaching residency at one of the famous Shī-zǐ-lín gardens.

## Other points of interest

The 43 *fǎ-sì* — among the largest disciple-cohorts in the entire J40 sequence — places Dé-shēn alongside [[如相]] 敏樹 (24 *fǎ-sì*) and [[淨瑩]] 元潔 (40 *fǎ-sì*) as one of the major 32nd-generation Línjì-Yángqí lineage-builders. The *qǐng-yì* sub-section in juan 2–3 is unusually detailed in named lay-recipient form, providing a substantial register of Sì-chuān / Guì-zhōu lay-Buddhist gentry contacts.
