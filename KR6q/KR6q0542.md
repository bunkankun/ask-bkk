# Shí-guān chán-shī yǔ-lù 石關禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shí-guān*
by [[芳桂]] (等編)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Pǔ-míng Shí-guān Xíng-líng** [[行凌]] 普明石關行凌 (b. **1614**, death year unknown — last attested at age 60 in **1673**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 (1593–1661), thus a co-disciple of all Tōng-róng's principal heirs (Yǐn-yuán Lóng-qí 隱元隆琦, Mù-chén Dào-mǐn 木陳道忞, etc.). Native of **Jiā-xīng Xiù-shuǐ** 秀水, lay surname **Gù** 顧. Compiled by his sole *fǎ-sì* **Xiāng-yán Fāng-guì** [[芳桂]] (1631–1708) *děng biān*. The work is paired in J38 with Fāng-guì's own *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0543]] (J38 B424), giving a teacher-disciple sequence.

The *yǔ-lù* documents teaching at three abbacies: **Pán-lóng-sì** 蟠龍寺, **Kāi-fǎ-sì** 開法寺, and **Pǔ-míng-sì** 普明寺 (the latter the principal seat, succeeded by Fāng-guì in 1677). *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-*fù-fǎ* abbacy onset, since Fèi-yǐn died in 1661 and Shí-guān's recorded sermons begin around then); *notAfter* ≈ **1675** (Fāng-guì took over Pǔ-míng in 1677, so the *yǔ-lù* of his teacher closes around then). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B423.

**Contents.** Single juan: preface; *shàng-táng*; *xiǎo-cān*; *jī-yuán*; *zàn* (image-encomia for Śākyamuni, Maitreya, Amitābha, Mañjuśrī, Samantabhadra, plus two *zì-tí*); *niān* and *sòng* on classic *gōng-àn* (世尊拈花, 女子出定, 臨濟在黃檗喫三頓痛棒, etc.).

## Other points of interest

The *yǔ-lù* fits naturally with [[KR6q0543]] in the canon ordering. The *xíng-shí* in juan 1 contains the unusual detail that Shí-guān's awakening came when sitting under a *liú-lí* (glass) lamp, hearing the latch of the privy door open as a fellow monk emerged at night — typical of the Mì-yún → Fèi-yǐn pedagogy that emphasized sudden environmental triggers ("起發疑情" through banal sound or sight) over scriptural study.
