# Huái-hǎi Yuán-zhào chánshī yǔlù 淮海原肇禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Huái-hǎi Yuán-zhào* — a one-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Huái-hǎi Yuán-zhào** 淮海原肇 (1189–1275), Línjì-lineage master. Compiled by **27 attendant-disciples** (*shìzhě mén-rén* 侍者門人) — an unusually large editorial team: lead-editor [[實仁|Shí-rén]] and [[宗文|Zōng-wén]], alongside 法奇, 守愿, 行佑, 了元, 善之, 淨證, 法從, 紹熏, 普璋, 文煥, 淨伏, 慧行, 文謙, 正因, 法恩, 德紀, 可能, 持志, 覺孫, 惟康, 宗和, 如止, 有智, 慧雲, 若舟 (all listed in the catalog; only the two lead-editors wikilinked in frontmatter to keep it manageable).

One-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1370.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1200; *notAfter* c. 1280.
