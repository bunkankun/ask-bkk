# Zhèngzōng xīnyìn hòuxù liánfāng 正宗心印後續聯芳

by [[善燦]] (Shàncàn), late Míng

## About the work

A one-juan late-Míng Chán compendium supplementing the earlier *Zhèngzōng xīnyìn* 正宗心印 tradition — a genealogical-cum-doctrinal record of orthodox-line (*zhèngzōng*) Chán mind-seal transmission. Title literally "Linked Fragrance of the Continuation of the *Zhèngzōng xīnyìn*"; *liánfāng* is a conventional term for a continuation-by-successor.

### Tiyao

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

Shàncàn supplies a continuation of the *Zhèngzōng xīnyìn* transmission record, adding figures of the generations after the earlier compilation. Brief in scale but participating in the general late-Míng interest in gathering Chán transmission materials into compact, practitioner-usable form. Preserved in the Xuzangjing.

## Translations and research

No English translation. No dedicated scholarship located.

## Other points of interest

One of several small late-Míng Chán transmission compilations in the penumbra of the larger lamp-record and *Sēngbǎo zhuàn* traditions.
