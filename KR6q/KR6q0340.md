# Xū-zhōu Pǔ-dù chánshī yǔlù 虛舟普度禪師語錄

One-juan Southern-Sòng / early-Yuán *yǔlù* of **Xū-zhōu Pǔ-dù** [[普度]] 虛舟普度 (1199 – Zhìyuán 17.4.24 / 31 May 1280, *shì* 82, *xià* 68), Yángqí-branch Línjì master, dharma-heir of Huá-zàng Wú-dé Jué-tōng 華藏無得覺通 (the line descending from Mì-ān through a different branch than Wújùn's). *Xuzangjing* X71 n1407 (and Jiāxīng). Compiled by four dharma-heirs led by [[淨伏]] Jìng-fú (Hǔ-yán Jìng-fú 虎巖淨伏). Eight abbacy records: Jiàn-kāng Bàn-shān Bào-níng 半山報寧 (from Chúnyòu 1 / 1241), Zhèn-jiāng Jīn-shān Lóngyóu 金山龍游, Tán-zhōu Lù-yuàn Bāo-zhōng 鹿苑褒忠, Fǔ-zhōu Shū-shān Bái-yún 踈山白雲, Píngjiāng Chéng-tiān Néng-rén 承天能仁, Lín'ān Zhōng-tiān-zhú Tiān-níng Wàn-shòu 中天竺天寧萬壽 (from Jǐngdìng era), Lín'ān Líng-yǐn Jǐng-dé 靈隱景德, and Lín'ān Jìng-shān Xīng-shèng Wàn-shòu 徑山興聖萬壽 (from Zhìyuán 14 / 1277). Plus *xiǎo-cān*, *jì-sòng*, *zhēn-zàn*, *zì-zàn*, *xiǎo fó-shì*, and closing *xíng-zhuàng*. Per DILA: surname Shǐ 史, native of Wéi-yáng Jiāng-dū 維揚江都; tonsured at twelve at Tiān-níng-sì; five years with Lóng-xī Zǔ-xìn 龍溪祖信; received dharma-transmission from Wú-dé. Seven dharma-heirs named: Hǔ-yán Jìng-fú, Yōng-sǒu Shí-zhōng 庸叟時中, Zhú-xī Miào-tǎn 竺西妙坦, and four others.
