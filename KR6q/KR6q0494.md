# Lǜ-luó Héng-xiù Lín chán-shī yǔ-lù 綠蘿恒秀林禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Héng-xiù Lín of Lǜ-luó*
by [[發林]] (說), [[光悠]] (等編)

## About the work

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Héng-xiù Fā-lín** [[發林]] 恒秀發林, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yún-huàn Yìn-chén** [[印宸]] 雲幻印宸 (1623–1669) in the Pòshān-Sì-chuān sub-line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Guāng-yōu** [[光悠]] *děng biān*. Title-form *Lǜ-luó* 綠蘿 references the **Lǜ-luó-shān** abbacy (location uncertain — likely Yún-nán region). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B355.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Heir of Yún-huàn Yìn-chén; previously compiled his master's [[KR6q0493]] *yǔ-lù*. Active in 1673 (per Wáng Mín-hào reference at [[KR6q0493]]). Successive abbacies after master's 1669 death.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound.

**Lineage.** Pòshān Hǎi-míng → Yún-huàn Yìn-chén → Héng-xiù Fā-lín — Sì-chuān-Yún-nán Pòshān-line.
