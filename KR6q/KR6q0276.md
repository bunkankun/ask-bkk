# Huánglóng sì jiā lù (xù) 黃龍四家錄（序）

*Preface to the Four-House Record of the Huánglóng [Sub-lineage]* — a one-juan preface-only text for the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* 黃龍四家錄, a Northern-Sòng compilation of the *yǔlù* of the four major masters of the Huánglóng 黃龍 sub-lineage of the Línjì Chán school. Preface by [[秀人錢密|Qián Mì]] 錢密 (the *Xiù-rén* 秀人 designation — "Refined Person" — appears to be a sobriquet).

## About the work

A one-juan preface-only text, X69 n1340. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The parent *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* compiles the *yǔlù* of the four principal Huánglóng-line masters descending from Huánglóng Huì-nán (1002–1069), the founder of the Huánglóng sub-lineage. The Huánglóng lineage eventually died out by the mid-Sòng, with the Yángqí sub-lineage carrying the Línjì tradition forward.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1100 (Northern-Sòng Huánglóng peak); *notAfter* c. 1200.
