# Zhì-tán Zhèng chán-shī yǔ-lù 智覃正禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhì-tán Zhèng*
by [[最正]] (說), [[尚能]] (等編), [[尚瑩]] (等編)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zhì-tán Zuì-zhèng** [[最正]] 智覃最正, Línjì-Yángqí Lín-yě → Zì-xián → Tiān-mù → Xiù-yě → Zhì-tán fifth-generation heir. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Shàng-néng** [[尚能]] + **Shàng-yíng** [[尚瑩]] *děng biān*. Together with [[KR6q0469]], [[KR6q0495]], [[KR6q0496]], [[KR6q0497]] forms a five-generation Lín-yě-line continuous *yǔ-lù* series — exceptional generational depth in the Jiā-xīng Canon. *notBefore* = c. 1680, *notAfter* = c. 1700. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B358.
