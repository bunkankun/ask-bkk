# Yuè-jiàn chánshī yǔlù 月磵禪師語錄

Two-juan late-Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Yuè-jiàn Wén-míng** [[文明]] 月磵文明 (lifedates unrecorded; active from Jǐngdìng 5 / 1264), Yángqí-branch Línjì master, dharma-heir of [[了慧]] Xī-yán Liǎo-huì (1198–1262). *Xuzangjing* X70 n1392. Preface by Jué-ān Mèng-zhēn 覺菴夢真 (abbot of Píngjiāng Shuāng-é) dated Zhìyuán *jiǎ-shēn* autumn (1284) — two decades after Wén-míng's first abbacy. Records four abbacies: Xìn-zhōu É-hú Rén-shòu 鵝湖仁壽 (entered 1264), Ráo-zhōu Tiān-níng 天寧, Qī-xián Miào-guǒ 棲賢妙果, and Ráo-zhōu Jiàn-fú 薦福 (both first and second tenures), with *gào-xiāng pǔ-shuō*, *sòng*, *tí-bá*, *xiǎo fó-shì*, *zàn*, *zì-zàn*, and closing *bá*. Per DILA A013871, Wén-míng (*hào* Yuè-jiàn 月磵 "Moon-Ravine") received his education at Huáng-lóng-sì and taught as abbot through the four sites above. His dharma-heir is Dōng-shān Chóng 東山崇.
