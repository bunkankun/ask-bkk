# Míng-zhōu Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé Chán-sì Hóng-zhì Jué chán-shī yǔ-lù 明州天童景德禪寺宏智覺禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hóng-zhì Jué of Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé Chán-sì in Míng-zhōu*
by [[正覺]] (說), [[淨啟]] (重編)

## About the work

Four-juan abridged-and-reconstituted *yǔ-lù* of **Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué** [[正覺]] 宏智正覺 (1091–1157), the Southern-Sòng Cáo-dòng patriarch and principal proponent of *mò-zhào chán* 默照禪 (silent-illumination), abbot of **Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé Chán-sì** 天童景德禪寺 in **Míng-zhōu** 明州 (= modern Níng-bō) for nearly thirty years from c. 1131 to his **Shào-xīng 27 / 10/8 = 1157-11-18** death. *Chóng-biān* 重編 (re-edited) by the early-Qīng Cáo-dòng master **Jìng-qǐ** [[淨啟]] (d. **1674-10-15**) — DILA-affiliated as 多福淨啟 / 妙叶淨啟 of Tiáo-xī, dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂. Jiā-xīng Canon J32 B272 (note: in *Jiā-xīng* volume 32, distinct from the J29 cluster of contemporary 1660s–70s *yǔ-lù*). Front-preface by **Yì-tíng jū-shì** **Xú Jì-ēn** 逸亭居士徐繼恩 (Cí-xī, Zhè-jiāng), undated. Contents: juan 1 — *shàng-táng* / *wǎn-cān* / *shì-zhòng*; juan 2 — *jǔ-gǔ*; juan 3 — *sòng-gǔ*; juan 4 — *jī-yuán* / *tǎ-míng* / *míng* / *jì* / *jì*.

### Abstract

**Status as recension.** Distinct from the canonical 9-juan 《宏智禪師廣錄》 ([[KR6q0070]] / T48 No. 2001), which preserves the integral Sòng-era recension compiled by Zhèng-jué's chief dharma-heir [[宗法]] **Sì-zōng** etc. The present 4-juan volume is a Qīng-period reconstitution by **Qíng-yún fǎ-xiōng** 晴雲法兄 (likely Jìng-qǐ in alternate-name guise; see [[淨啟]] note) of material that had become scattered (*sǎn-wáng* 散亡) over the *shù-bǎi nián* 數百年 since Shào-xīng. Xú Jì-ēn's preface explicitly frames the volume as a **gathering from miscellaneous external sources** rather than a fresh transmission of the canonical text: *Qíng-yún fǎ-xiōng yú tā jiā zá-jiàn zhě, guǎng wéi sōu-jí* 晴雲法兄於他家雜見者廣為搜緝.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1131** (Zhèng-jué's accession to Tiān-tóng); *notAfter* = **1157** (his death) — for the underlying speaker. The Qīng compiler [[淨啟]] died **1674**, providing a *terminus ante quem* for the Jiā-xīng Canon imprint of c. 1670–73.

**Doctrinal significance.** Preserves Cáo-dòng silent-illumination *fǎ-yǔ* and *jǔ-gǔ / sòng-gǔ* material from the Tiān-tóng *fǎ-xí*. The 4-juan structure (vs. the 9-juan canonical edition) emphasizes the *jǔ-gǔ* (juan 2) and *sòng-gǔ* (juan 3) selections — the two genres where Hóng-zhì's verse-precedent set most influenced subsequent Cáo-dòng practice (the *Cóng-róng lù* 從容錄 [[KR6q0079]] of Wàn-sōng Xíng-xiù being the most prominent expansion of Hóng-zhì's *sòng-gǔ bǎi zé*).

**Cáo-dòng-line significance.** Jìng-qǐ's *zhòng-biān* sits within the broader early-Qīng Cáo-dòng-line revival of Sòng-era patriarchal materials by figures such as [[道盛]] 覺浪道盛 (Tiān-jiè), [[元賢]] 永覺元賢 (Gǔ-shān), and others — a deliberate Cáo-dòng counter-movement to the Línjì-line publishing of the time. The Tiān-tóng-sì connection is particularly resonant: by the 1660s the seat had been held for several generations by the Mì-yún-line Línjì masters ([[圓悟]] Mì-yún Yuán-wù; [[道忞]] Mù-chén Dào-mín; cf. [[KR6q0457]]), and recovering Hóng-zhì's Tiān-tóng *yǔ-lù* served as a Cáo-dòng claim on the seat's deeper historical pedigree.

## Translations and research

- For Hóng-zhì's biography and doctrine: Morten Schlütter, *How Zen Became Zen* (Hawaii 2008) — the principal English-language treatment of the silent-illumination / *kàn-huà* dispute and Hóng-zhì's central role.
- Taigen Dan Leighton, *Cultivating the Empty Field: The Silent Illumination of Zen Master Hongzhi* (1st ed. 1991) — translation of selected *jì*, *míng*, and *fǎ-yǔ*.
- See also [[KR6q0070]] 《宏智禪師廣錄》 (the canonical 9-juan recension) and [[KR6q0079]] 《從容錄》 (the *sòng-gǔ bǎi zé* expansion).

## Other points of interest

- **Cross-referenced with [[KR6q0070]].** This is the second of two extant *yǔ-lù* recensions of Hóng-zhì in the Kanripo corpus. The 9-juan T48 No. 2001 is canonical; this 4-juan J32 B272 is the abridged Qīng reconstitution. Material in this 4-juan volume is partly overlapping with, partly supplementary to, KR6q0070.
- **Tiān-tóng pedigree-claim.** Jìng-qǐ's recovery of Hóng-zhì at Tiān-tóng can be read as a 17th-century Cáo-dòng-line **historical-pedigree claim** on the same Tiān-tóng-sì then under Línjì-line institutional control — particularly poignant given the Cáo-dòng / Línjì doctrinal disputes from Hóng-zhì / Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo's own time.
