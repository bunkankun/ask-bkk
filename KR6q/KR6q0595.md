# Cuì-yá Bì chán-shī yǔ-lù 翠崖必禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cuì-yá Bì*
by [[必]] (說), [[道通]] (錄)

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Cuì-yá Bì** [[必]] 翠崖必 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bié-ān Xìng-tǒng** 別庵性統 (1661–1717) of **Pǔ-tuó-shān**. Recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Dào-tōng** [[道通]] *lù*. Documents teaching at four abbacies, two in Háng-zhōu / Jiāng-xī and two in **Beijing** (京都): **Háng-zhōu-fǔ Rén-hé-xiàn Yǒng-shòu chán-sì** 杭州府仁和縣永壽禪寺; **Jiāng-xī Gàng-zhōu-fǔ Cháng-níng-xiàn Shī-lín chán-yuàn** 江西戇州府長寧縣獅林禪院; **Jīng-dū Guān-yīn chán-sì** 京都觀音禪寺; **Jīng-dū Jiā-xīng chán-sì** 京都嘉興禪寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1700** (post-Xìng-tǒng-late-period *fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1730**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B487.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *mù-cì*; abbacy *yǔ-lù* records (the four seats listed above). Juan 2: *xiǎo-cān*; *jī-yuán*; *fó-shì* (with *Jìng-shān sǎo Huì-zǔ tǎ* 徑山掃慧祖塔, *Cáo-xī lǐ Liù-zǔ zhēn* 曹溪禮六祖真 — pilgrimages to the founder-stupas, plus mortuary rituals for various lay donors). Juan 3: closing materials.

## Other points of interest

The four-abbacy circuit including two **Beijing** seats (*Jīng-dū Guān-yīn* and *Jīng-dū Jiā-xīng*) marks Cuì-yá Bì as one of the most centrally-positioned **Pǔ-tuó-shān → Beijing** axis Línjì-Yángqí monks of the early-18th-century Kāngxī-Yōngzhèng transition. The pilgrimage *fó-shì* to the **Liù-zǔ** stupa at **Cáo-xī** indicates a continuing Lìng-nán Cáodòng / Hǎi-yún engagement.
