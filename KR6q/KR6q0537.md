# Huì-yuè Xù chán-shī yǔ-lù 晦嶽旭禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huì-yuè Xù*
by [[智旭]] (說), [[全琳]] (等錄)

Eight-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Huì-yuè Zhì-xù** 晦嶽智旭 (cf. the disambiguation block in [[智旭]]) — Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shān-duó Zhēn-zài** [[真在]] (1621–1672), thus a co-disciple of [[機能]] (cf. [[KR6q0536]]). **NOT** to be confused with the famous late-Míng integrative master Ŏuyì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), who shares the dharma-name. Native of **Qí-shuǐ** 蘄水 (Hú-běi), lay surname **Wáng** 王. Lifedates unknown — was active across multiple major Jiā-xīng / Zhèn-jiāng abbacies between 1683 and 1703. Compiled by his attendant **Yùn-shí Quán-lín** [[全琳]] *děng lù*.

The *yǔ-lù* is exceptional among the late J38 collections for its sweep of seven major abbacies, all in the lower Yangtze: **Jiā-xīng Jīn-míng chán-sì** 嘉興府金明禪寺 (entered **康熙癸亥 10/3** = **1683-11-21**, the seat of his teacher Shān-duó's lineage-grandfather **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn**), **Sān-tǎ Lóng-yuān Hǎi-huì Jǐng-dé chán-sì** 三塔龍淵海會景德禪寺 (Jiā-xīng — the same site held earlier by [[圓法]] and [[真雄]]), **Zhèn-jiāng Běi-gù-shān Gān-lù-sì Hǎi-mén chán-yuàn** 鎮江府北固山甘露寺海門禪院, **Zhèn-jiāng Gān-lù chán-sì** 鎮江府甘露禪寺 proper, **Jīng-kǒu Dà-jué chán-sì** 京口大覺禪寺, **Shèng-guǒ chán-sì** 勝果禪寺, then a return to **Jīn-míng**, and finally **Jiā-xīng Zhēn-rú chán-sì** 嘉興府真如禪寺. The principal preface is dated **康熙癸未花朝** = **1703** spring (Kāngxī 42, *huā-zhāo* = 2nd lunar month 15). *notBefore* = **1683** (Jīn-míng entry); *notAfter* = **1703** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B418.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *Jīn-míng yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Sān-tǎ Lóng-yuān Hǎi-huì Jǐng-dé yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *Gān-lù Hǎi-mén yǔ-lù*. Juan 4: *Gān-lù-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 5: *Dà-jué yǔ-lù* + *Shèng-guǒ yǔ-lù*. Juan 6: *Fù-zhù Jīn-míng yǔ-lù*. Juan 7: *Zhēn-rú yǔ-lù*. Juan 8: *niān-gǔ* recorded by *jì-shì* **Quán-běn** 全本 *jì-lù*.

## Other points of interest

The *yǔ-lù* is the largest single collection in the Jiè-ān / Shān-duó sub-lineage and traces the Jiā-xīng-Zhèn-jiāng axis of Línjì-Yángqí abbatial circulation in the late Kāngxī era — Huì-yuè Zhì-xù held more abbacies in succession than any of his cousin-monks documented in [[KR6q0532]]–[[KR6q0536]]. The succession at Sān-tǎ Lóng-yuān (Jiā-xīng) traces a multi-generational continuity: [[圓法]] (1663–1669) → [[真雄]] (post-1672) → 晦嶽智旭. The 1703 print date places the corpus among the later Jiā-xīng-canon supplemental imprints.
