# Huìyuè Xù chánshī yǔlù 晦嶽旭禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huì-yuè Xù*
by [[智旭]] (說), [[全琳]] (等錄)

Eight-juan *yǔlù* of **Huìyuè Zhìxù** 晦嶽智旭 (cf. the disambiguation block in [[智旭]]) — LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Shānduó Zhēnzài** [[真在]] (1621–1672), thus a co-disciple of [[機能]] (cf. [[KR6q0536]]). **NOT** to be confused with the famous late-Míng integrative master Ŏuyì Zhìxù 蕅益智旭 (1599–1655), who shares the dharma-name. Native of **Qíshuǐ** 蘄水 (Húběi), lay surname **Wáng** 王. Lifedates unknown — was active across multiple major Jiāxīng / Zhènjiāng abbacies between 1683 and 1703. Compiled by his attendant **Yùnshí Quánlín** [[全琳]] *děng lù*.

The *yǔlù* is exceptional among the late J38 collections for its sweep of seven major abbacies, all in the lower Yangtze: **Jiāxīng Jīnmíng chánsì** 嘉興府金明禪寺 (entered **康熙癸亥 10/3** = **1683-11-21**, the seat of his teacher Shānduó's lineage-grandfather **Jièān Wùjìn**), **Sāntǎ Lóngyuān Hǎihuì Jǐngdé chánsì** 三塔龍淵海會景德禪寺 (Jiāxīng — the same site held earlier by [[圓法]] and [[真雄]]), **Zhènjiāng Běigùshān Gānlùsì Hǎimén chányuàn** 鎮江府北固山甘露寺海門禪院, **Zhènjiāng Gānlù chánsì** 鎮江府甘露禪寺 proper, **Jīngkǒu Dàjué chánsì** 京口大覺禪寺, **Shèngguǒ chánsì** 勝果禪寺, then a return to **Jīnmíng**, and finally **Jiāxīng Zhēnrú chánsì** 嘉興府真如禪寺. The principal preface is dated **康熙癸未花朝** = **1703** spring (Kāngxī 42, *huāzhāo* = 2nd lunar month 15). *notBefore* = **1683** (Jīnmíng entry); *notAfter* = **1703** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B418.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *Jīnmíng yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Sāntǎ Lóngyuān Hǎihuì Jǐngdé yǔlù*. Juan 3: *Gānlù Hǎimén yǔlù*. Juan 4: *Gānlùsì yǔlù*. Juan 5: *Dàjué yǔlù* + *Shèngguǒ yǔlù*. Juan 6: *Fùzhù Jīnmíng yǔlù*. Juan 7: *Zhēnrú yǔlù*. Juan 8: *niāngǔ* recorded by *jìshì* **Quánběn** 全本 *jìlù*.

## Other points of interest

The *yǔlù* is the largest single collection in the Jièān / Shānduó sub-lineage and traces the JiāxīngZhènjiāng axis of LínjìYángqí abbatial circulation in the late Kāngxī era — Huìyuè Zhìxù held more abbacies in succession than any of his cousin-monks documented in [[KR6q0532]]–[[KR6q0536]]. The succession at Sāntǎ Lóngyuān (Jiāxīng) traces a multi-generational continuity: [[圓法]] (1663–1669) → [[真雄]] (post-1672) → 晦嶽智旭. The 1703 print date places the corpus among the later Jiā-xīng-canon supplemental imprints.
