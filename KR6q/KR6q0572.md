# Xiāng-yán Xǐ-xīn Shuǐ chán-shī yǔ-lù 香巖洗心水禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xǐ-xīn Shuǐ of Xiāng-yán [-sì]*
by [[照水]] (說), [[實雲]] (等編), [[海清]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xǐ-xīn Zhào-shuǐ** [[照水]] 洗心照水 (lifedates unknown — was 30 in **1683**, age 45 in **1698**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** [[本真]] (1620–1691, cf. [[KR6q0571]]). Native of **Jiàn-kāng Jiāng-níng** 建康江寧 (Nán-jīng), lay surname **Liú** 劉. Compiled by his attendants **Shí-yún** [[實雲]] (catalog form; the source itself reads 實雪 Shí-xuě) and **Hǎi-qīng** [[海清]] *děng biān*.

Three prefaces are preserved: by lay scholar **Zhào Yùn-xī** 趙運熙 (拔貢) dated **康熙丁卯歲春王上元日** = **1687-02-26** (lunar 1/15, *Yuán-xiāo*); a **康熙丙寅** = **1686** verse-text; and by a former *副總兵 zuǒ-dū-dū* of **Qú-xié** 衢協 region (Zhè-jiāng) dated **康熙乙亥秋九月** = **1695-10**. Documents teaching at four lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Huá-yán-sì** 華嚴寺 (entered **康熙癸酉中秋** = **1693-09**); **Bǎo-qìng** 寶慶; **Guān-yīn-sì** 觀音寺 (entered **康熙甲戌元宵** = **1694-02**); **Xiāng-yán-sì** 香巖寺 (the principal seat). *notBefore* = **1683** (癸亥 *fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1698** (last attested year). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B459.

**Contents.** Two juan: prefaces; *shàng-táng* records at the four abbacies; *xiǎo-cān*; *jī-yuán*; *fǎ-yǔ*; *jì*; *fó-shì*; *xíng-shí* (the autobiographical narration in juan 2 — primary source for his life).

## Other points of interest

The pairing with [[KR6q0571]] (Běn-zhēn) shows a complete teacher-disciple sequence within the Línjì-Yángqí 35-generation series: tonsure under **Zhèng-jué Rùn-guāng Zé** 潤光澤 (cf. [[KR6q0234]]) → *fù-fǎ* under **Bái-yún Xiàng-lín Běn-zhēn** [[本真]] in 1683 — Zhào-shuǐ's editorial work spans both lineage-lines. The 1695 preface by a Zhè-jiāng military commander indicates substantial provincial-elite patronage, perhaps building on Zhào-shuǐ's Jiāng-níng (Nán-jīng) lay-Buddhist family background.
