# Zhèngyuán lüèjí 正源略集

compiled (輯) by [[際源]] (Jìyuán) and [[了貞]] (Liǎozhēn); edited (編) by [[達珍]] (Dázhēn), preface dated 1820 (Qīng *Jiāqìng* 25)

## About the work

A 15-juan late-Qīng Chán lamp-record selection, compiled by three Qīng monks drawing on earlier lamp records and recorded-sayings literature to produce a more compact reference than the massive *Wǔdēng quánshū*. Title — "Abbreviated Collection of the Orthodox Source" — announces its compendium character.

### Tiyao


### Abstract

One of the later-Qīng attempts to produce a condensed, practically-usable Chán lamp record selecting the most important figures and dialogues from the sprawling *Wǔdēng quánshū* and its Cáodòng-side competitor *Xùdēng zhèngtǒng*. Relatively conservative in its lineage attributions; avoids the polemical edge of the seventeenth-century disputes.

## Translations and research

No English translation. No dedicated monograph.

## Other points of interest

A usefully compact late-Qīng Chán reference; its lineage framework is closer to the older *Wǔdēng huìyuán* than to the seventeenth-century sectarian revisions.
