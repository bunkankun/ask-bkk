# Guān-tāo Qí chán-shī yǔ-lù 觀濤奇禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Guān-tāo Qí*
by [[大奇]] (說), [[興舒]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Guān-tāo Dà-qí** [[大奇]] 觀濤大奇 (**1625 – 1678-08-26**), Cáo-dòng-school (Shòu-chāng-branch) dharma-heir of **Tiān-jiè Jué-làng Dào-shèng** [[道盛]] (1593–1659). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xīng-shū** [[興舒]] *děng biān*. Two abbacies: **Chóng-xiān Xiǎn-xiào-sì** at Háng-zhōu Gāo-tíng-shān; **Xīn-chéng Shòu-chāng-sì** (the Cáo-dòng ancestral seat). Juan 6 includes the *shòu-zàng míng bìng xù* 壽藏銘并序 giving lifedates. *notBefore* = c. 1655 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1678 (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B362.
