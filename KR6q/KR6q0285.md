# Bái-yún Shǒu-duān chánshī yǔlù 白雲守端禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Bái-yún Shǒu-duān* — a two-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Bái-yún Shǒu-duān** 白雲守端 (1025–1072), the important Yángqí Línjì-sub-lineage master; dharma-heir of Yángqí Fāng-huì 楊岐方會 (992–1049) and teacher of Wǔ-zǔ Fǎ-yǎn 五祖法演 (through whom the Yángqí line extends to Yuán-wù Kè-qín and beyond).

Two-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1351. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1060; *notAfter* c. 1100.
