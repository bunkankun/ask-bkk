# Línquán lǎorén píngchàng Tóuzǐ Qīng héshàng sònggǔ Kōnggǔ jí 林泉老人評唱投子青和尚頌古空谷集

*Old-Man Línquán's Evaluation-Singing on Master Tóuzǐ Qīng's Sònggǔ: Empty-Valley Collection* — a six-juan Chán commentary with dual-authorial structure: Sòng-era *sònggǔ* 頌古 verses by [[義青|Tóuzǐ Yìqīng]] 投子義青 (1032–1083) of the Cáodòng lineage, with Yuán-era *píngchàng* 評唱 ("evaluation-singing" commentary) by [[從倫|Línquán Cónglún]] 林泉從倫 (b. 1223, *hào* Línquán lǎorén 林泉老人 "Old Man of the Forest-Spring"). The Yuán-era commentary gives the text its present title. Parallel structure to the *Bìyán lù* (Kèqín on Xuědòu) and *Cóngróng lù* (Wànsōng on Hóngzhì) — the great Sòng-Yuán Chán *píngchàng* tradition.

## About the work

A six-juan Cáodòng Chán *sònggǔ* / *píngchàng* collaborative commentary, X67 n1303. Non-commentary on a single parent Buddhist text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

Yìqīng's 100 *sònggǔ* verses on classical *gōng'àn* cases circulated in the Cáodòng tradition from the late 11th century onward. Cónglún's Yuán-era *píngchàng* commentary — adding running-expository commentary to each case-and-verse — brings the material into the mature Yuán-Cáodòng lineage-teaching form.

### Abstract

[[義青|Tóuzǐ Yìqīng]] 投子義青 (1032–1083/5/29; age 52, per his *yǔlù* juan 2 biographical record). Major Northern-Sòng Cáodòng-lineage master. *Shì* Cíjì 慈濟, *Miàoxù dàshī* 妙續大師. Native of Shūzhōu 舒州 (Ānhuī); nicknamed "Qīng Huáyán" 青華嚴 ("Qīng the Huáyán Specialist"). Dharma-heir of [[景玄|Dàyáng Jǐngxuán]] 大陽景玄 in the Cáodòng line; abbot of Tóuzǐshān 投子山 in his native Shūzhōu.

[[從倫|Línquán Cónglún]] 林泉從倫 (b. 1223): Yuán Cáodòng-lineage master. *Hào* Línquán 林泉 ("Forest-Spring"), Línquán lǎorén 林泉老人. Also called Bàoēn Cónglún 報恩從倫.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1060 (Yìqīng's original composition of the *sònggǔ*); *notAfter* c. 1290 (Cónglún's productive period for the *píngchàng* — the received form reflects the Yuán Cónglún commentary's completion).
