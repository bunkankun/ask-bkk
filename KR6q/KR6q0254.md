# Wànsōng lǎorén píng chàng Tiāntóng Jué héshàng niān gǔ qǐng yì lù 萬松老人評唱天童覺和尚拈古請益錄

*Old-Man Wànsōng's Evaluation-Singing on Master Tiāntóng Jué's Niāngǔ: Record of Requesting-Benefit* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng / Jin / Yuán Cáodòng collaborative commentary, companion to the more famous *Cóng róng lù* [[KR6q0114]]. Where the *Cóng róng lù* has Wànsōng Xíngxiù 萬松行秀 (1166–1246) commenting on Hóngzhì Zhèngjué's 宏智正覺 (1091–1157) *sòng gǔ* (verse-commentary), this parallel text has the same Wànsōng commenting on Hóngzhì's *niān gǔ* (prose-commentary) — representing Wànsōng's companion-treatment of Hóngzhì's other major commentarial-corpus.

## About the work

A two-juan Cáodòng *niān gǔ* / *píng chàng* collaborative commentary, X67 n1307. Non-commentary on a single parent Buddhist text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text gathers cases where Hóngzhì's original *niān gǔ* prose-commentaries were given in response to disciple-questions (*qǐng yì* 請益 "requesting beneficial teaching") — whence the title's *Qǐng yì lù* 請益錄 ("Record of Requesting Beneficial [Teaching]"). Wànsōng's Yuán-era *píng chàng* adds running-commentary in the same style as his famous *Cóng róng lù*.
