# Wàn-sōng lǎo-rén píng chàng Tiān-tóng Jué héshàng niān gǔ qǐng yì lù 萬松老人評唱天童覺和尚拈古請益錄

*Old-Man Wàn-sōng's Evaluation-Singing on Master Tiān-tóng Jué's Niān-gǔ: Record of Requesting-Benefit* — a two-juan Southern-Sòng / Jin / Yuán Cáodòng collaborative commentary, companion to the more famous *Cóng róng lù* [[KR6q0114]]. Where the *Cóng róng lù* has Wàn-sōng Xíng-xiù 萬松行秀 (1166–1246) commenting on Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué's 宏智正覺 (1091–1157) *sòng gǔ* (verse-commentary), this parallel text has the same Wàn-sōng commenting on Hóng-zhì's *niān gǔ* (prose-commentary) — representing Wàn-sōng's companion-treatment of Hóng-zhì's other major commentarial-corpus.

## About the work

A two-juan Cáodòng *niān gǔ* / *píng chàng* collaborative commentary, X67 n1307. Non-commentary on a single parent Buddhist text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text gathers cases where Hóng-zhì's original *niān gǔ* prose-commentaries were given in response to disciple-questions (*qǐng yì* 請益 "requesting beneficial teaching") — whence the title's *Qǐng yì lù* 請益錄 ("Record of Requesting Beneficial [Teaching]"). Wàn-sōng's Yuán-era *píng chàng* adds running-commentary in the same style as his famous *Cóng róng lù*.
