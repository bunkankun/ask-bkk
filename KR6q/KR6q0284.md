# Bǎoníng Rényǒng chánshī yǔlù 保寧仁勇禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Bǎoníng Rényǒng* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of [[仁勇|Bǎoníng Rényǒng]] 保寧仁勇, Línjì-Yángqí lineage master active at Bǎoníngsì 保寧寺. Compiled by his disciples [[道務|Dàowù]] 道務 (*cānxué* 參學 student-disciple) and [[圓淨|Yuánjìng]] 圓淨 (*ménrén* 門人 formal disciple).

One-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1350. Rényǒng's lifedates unrecorded; dharma-heir of [[方會|Yángqí Fānghuì]] 楊岐方會 (992–1049), making him one of the founding figures of the Yángqí sub-lineage.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1060; *notAfter* c. 1100.
