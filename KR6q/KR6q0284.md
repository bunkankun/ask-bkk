# Bǎo-níng Rén-yǒng chánshī yǔlù 保寧仁勇禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Bǎo-níng Rén-yǒng* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Bǎo-níng [[仁勇|Rén-yǒng]]** 保寧仁勇, Línjì-Yángqí lineage master active at Bǎo-níng-sì 保寧寺. Compiled by his disciples [[道務|Dào-wù]] 道務 (*cān xué* 參學 student-disciple) and [[圓淨|Yuán-jìng]] 圓淨 (*mén rén* 門人 formal disciple).

One-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1350. Rén-yǒng's lifedates unrecorded; dharma-heir of Yángqí Fāng-huì 楊岐方會 (992–1049), making him one of the founding figures of the Yángqí sub-lineage.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1060; *notAfter* c. 1100.
