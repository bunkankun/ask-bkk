# Xiàng-yái Tǐng chán-shī yǔ-lù 象崖珽禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiàng-yái Tǐng*
by [[性珽]] (說), [[益聞]] (重刊)

## About the work

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xiàng-yái Xìng-tǐng** [[性珽]] 象崖性珽 (**1598 – 1651-08/09**), Línjì-Yángqí thirty-sixth-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] 破山海明 (1597–1666). *Chóng-kān* 重刊 (re-cut) by *fǎ-sūn* **Yì-wén** [[益聞]]. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B308. Includes juan-4 *xíng-zhuàng* + *tǎ-míng*.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[性珽]] note: native of **Fú-qīng** 福清 (Fú-zhōu, Fú-jiàn), lay surname **Chén** 陳. Tonsured at 19 (1616) at **Cháng-lè Cháng-xī Fú-gōng**. Multi-teacher Chán pilgrimage circuit: **Huáng-bò Zhōng** (3 years), **Gǔ-shān Wú-yì** (no transmission), **Huáng-bò Mì-yún Yuán-wù** (full precepts), **Jīn-sù Yuán-wù** (no transmission). Final transmission from **Pòshān Hǎi-míng** in c. 1640.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1640** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1651** (death). Six abbacies: **Lóng-pán** → **Niān-huá** → **Bǎo-lín** → **Léng-jiā** → **Huáng-bò** (twice) → **Yǒu-yáng Huá-yán-sì** 酉陽華嚴寺 (death-seat at Sì-chuān 酉陽 prefecture).

**Significance.** Among the Pòshān heirs, Xìng-tǐng is distinctive for his Fú-jiàn provincial provenance (most Pòshān heirs were Sì-chuān-bred), for his successive Jiāng-nán → Sì-chuān trajectory (ending at the Yǒu-yáng border with **Rǎn Yù-cén** patron — the same lay-patron family later documented in [[KR6q0467]]'s 1671 *kāi-táng* roster), and for training the young Lián-yuè Dào-zhèng before Lián-yuè crossed to Pòshān himself (cf. [[KR6q0445]] juan 5 *xíng-shí*).

**Heirs.** **Shí-gǔ Huì** 石谷慧, **Yún-fù Zhì** [[道智]] 雲腹智 (cf. [[KR6q0480]]), **Yú-shān Ruì** 余山瑞.

**Re-cut status.** Catalog meta gives the editor function as *chóng-kān* 重刊 — meaning Yì-wén prepared a re-cut of an earlier recension. The Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B308 imprint is the *chóng-kān* version.

## Other points of interest

- **Fú-jiàn-bred Pòshān heir.** Xìng-tǐng is a rare non-Sì-chuān Pòshān-line heir, reflecting the cross-regional reach of Pòshān's Sichuan-Jiāngnán-Fújiàn pilgrimage circuit.
- **Yǒu-yáng patronage continuity.** The Rǎn Yù-cén (酉陽 司主, *xuān-wèi-sī*) lay-patron family appears continuously across two generations of Pòshān-line texts: Xìng-tǐng's final abbacy patron in 1650, and the Bù-lín → Lù-nán Dōu-shuài patron in 1671 (cf. [[KR6q0467]]).
