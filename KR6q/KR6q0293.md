# Yīng-ān Tán-huá chánshī yǔlù 應菴曇華禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Yīng-ān Tán-huá* — a ten-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Yīng-ān Tán-huá** 應菴曇華 (1103–1163), a major figure in the Hǔ-qiū branch of the Línjì-Yángqí line — dharma-heir of Hǔ-qiū Shào-lóng 虎丘紹隆 (see [[KR6q0292]]), and teacher of Mì-ān Xián-jié 密菴咸傑 (d. 1186), who in turn taught Pò-ān Zǔ-xiān 破菴祖先 and Fó-zhào Dé-guāng 佛照德光.

Ten-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1359. Compiled by dharma-heir [[守詮|Shǒu-quán]] 守詮 et al.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1140; *notAfter* c. 1180.
