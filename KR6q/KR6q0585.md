# Pán-shān Lǎng-kōng Shùn chán-shī yǔ-lù 盤山朗空順禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lǎng-kōng Shùn of Pán-shān*
by [[順]] (說), [[實性]] (記錄)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Lǎng-kōng Shùn** [[順]] 朗空順 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** 了宗超見 (1614–1688) of **Pán-shān** 盤山 — thus a co-disciple of [[昊]] **Huī-zhōu Hào** (cf. [[KR6q0573]]). Native of **Yǒng-píng-fǔ Chāng-lí-xiàn** 永平府昌黎縣 (Hé-běi), lay surname **Zhào** 趙. Recorded by his female attendant **Shí-xìng** [[實性]] *jì-lù*. *notBefore* = **1688** (master's death and Lǎng-kōng's *fù-zhǔ*); *notAfter* ≈ **1710**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B473.

**Contents.** Single juan: abbacy *yǔ-lù* at **Lè-yì Yǒng-shèng-ān** 樂邑永盛菴; *Liǎo-zōng-jiàn-shī fǎ-sì* lineage notice; *Ruì-tíng Shèng chán-shī fǎ-sì* lineage notice — i.e. the volume includes brief lineage-records for both Lǎng-kōng's master and a related Pán-shān-line monk.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0573 ([[昊]]) and KR6q0585 ([[順]]) together document the two principal heirs of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** of **Pán-shān** in the post-1688 generation — both based in the Hé-běi / Pán-shān regional Línjì-Yángqí establishment. Ling-zōng's combined disciple-cohort (these two plus others) anchored Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism in the Hé-běi → Tiān-jīn → Liáo-dōng frontier into the late Kāngxī era.
