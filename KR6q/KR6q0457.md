# Dá-biàn Quán chán-shī yǔ-lù 達變權禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dá-biàn Quán*
by [[顯權]] (說), [[海澂]] (錄)

## About the work

Five-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Dá-biàn Xiǎn-quán** [[顯權]] 達變顯權 (DILA standardized; *zì* Dá-biàn, with the catalog form retaining the single character 權 from *Quán*), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mù-chén Shān-wēng Dào-mín** [[道忞]] 木陳山翁道忞 (1596–1674) at **Tiān-tóng-sì** 天童寺. Recorded by *shì-zhě* attendant **Hǎi-chéng** [[海澂]]. Front-preface by *tóng-mén fǎ-dì* **Bīng-ān Zhāng Lì-lián** 冰菴張立廉 of **Lù-chéng** 鹿城 (= Zhèn-hǎi 鎮海, modern Níng-bō coastal area), undated. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B248. Contents: *xù*; juan 1 *zhù Míng-zhōu Cí-xī-xiàn Wǔ-lěi-shān Líng-fēng Chán-sì yǔ-lù* — entry-sermon at the Wǔ-lěi-shān **Líng-fēng Chán-sì** 五磊山靈峰禪寺 abbacy in Cí-xī (Míng-zhōu, modern Níng-bō prefecture); subsequent juan with *shàng-táng* / *xiǎo-cān* / *fǎ-yǔ* / *jī-yuán* / *fó-shì* arrangements, in standard Línjì *yǔ-lù* organization.

### Abstract

**Dating.** No precise dated entries in the corpus. *notBefore* = **1660** (conservative bound for Xiǎn-quán's Líng-fēng abbacy, which post-dates his Tiān-tóng *fēn-zuò* role under Dào-mín who returned from his **1659–60 imperial summons** to the Shùn-zhì palace). *notAfter* = **1674** (Xiǎn-quán is recorded as having predeceased Dào-mín, who himself died **Kāng-xī 13 = 1674**; the preface phrase *jí shī shùn-shì ér jǐn-rán shāng-huái* 及師順世而盡然傷懷 confirms posthumous compilation). The Zhāng Lì-lián preface refers to Dào-mín's personal sponsorship of the publication after Xiǎn-quán's death: *jīn fù shōu-shí yí-lù wéi shī diǎn-chū mò-hòu guāng-míng* 今復收拾遺錄為師點出末後光明 — a rare instance of a master compiling a deceased disciple's *yǔ-lù*.

**Authorship.** Per the Zhāng Lì-lián preface and DILA authority A001978: native of **Fēng-chéng** 豐城 (Jiāng-xī), lay surname **Wáng** 王. First training at **Tiān-tóng-sì** under **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] before Yuán-wù's 1642 death (*shǎo shì xiān Tiān-tóng zhuī-fú* 少侍先天童椎拂, where *xiān Tiān-tóng* = "the late Tiāntóng [abbot]" = Yuán-wù), then formal *fù-fǎ* under Dào-mín on Dào-mín's succession to the Tiāntóng seat. *Shì-shòu* **52**. Praised in the preface as *yán-yì fú wù lèi Xiù Tiě-miàn* 嚴毅服物類秀鐵面 (severe like Xiù Tiě-miàn), *tuō-luò xíng-hái lèi Guǎng wú-xīn* 脫落形骸類廣無心 (free of form like Guǎng Wú-xīn), *cǎo-yī fēi-shí qīng-kǔ liàn-xíng lèi Xiàng-gǔ tóu-tuó* 草衣菲食清苦鍊行類象骨頭陀 (austere like Xiàng-gǔ Tóu-tuó), *diǎn-sī zhòng-wù wǎng xiè cān-chéng lèi Shí-shuāng jiān-sì* 典司眾務罔懈參承類石霜監寺 (diligent in office like Shí-shuāng's monastic-supervisor) — a four-fold encomium framing his ascetic and disciplinary character.

**Lineage and seat.** Xiǎn-quán's Líng-fēng-sì at **Wǔ-lěi-shān** 五磊山 in Cí-xī is the same Cí-xī monastery preserved in the contemporary 嘉興 publication network. Sole recorded dharma-heir: **Wǔ-lěi Líng-shān Tiān-yīn Cháo** 五磊靈山天音朝.

## Other points of interest

- **Posthumous *yǔ-lù* compiled by the master.** The preface's note that **Dào-mín** himself collected and sponsored the publication of his predeceased dharma-heir's *yí-lù* 遺錄 ("remaining records") is unusual — most Jiā-xīng Canon *yǔ-lù* are compiled by surviving disciples. Dào-mín's personal involvement (described in the preface as expressing *tiān sàng yǔ* 天喪予, the Confucian formula of master-mourning-disciple from *Lúnyǔ* 11.9) gives this volume a distinctive elegiac character.
- **Zhāng Lì-lián as 同門.** The preface-writer's self-styling as *tóng-mén fǎ-dì* 同門法弟 indicates Zhāng was also a Mù-chén Dào-mín disciple — though listed in the catalog as preface-writer rather than as a published Chán *yǔ-lù* author, suggesting lay-Chán affiliation rather than monastic ordination.
