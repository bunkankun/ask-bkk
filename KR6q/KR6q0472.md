# Shēn-fēng Xiàn chán-shī yǔ-lù 屾峰憲禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shēn-fēng Xiàn*
by [[憲]] (說), [[智質]] (編), [[智原]] (編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Shēn-fēng Xiàn** [[憲]] 屾峰憲, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-zhí** [[智質]] + **Zhì-yuán** [[智原]] *tóng biān* 同編. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B298 (note: in J34 cluster, distinct from the earlier Sān-yí Cáo-dòng publications [[KR6q0459]], [[KR6q0471]] in J32–J33). Five-abbacy structure across juan 1–5: **Zuì-lǐ Gǔ Zī-shèng-sì** 檇李古資聖寺, **Qián-xī Gǔ Qí-chāng-sì** 前溪古齊昌寺, **Dōng-shān Mìao-jué-yuàn** 東山妙覺院, **Guān-yīn-yuàn** 觀音院, **Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng-sì** 雲門顯聖寺.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA A001774 and the [[憲]] note: dharma-heir of Sān-yí Míng-yú at Yún-mén-sì, the same teacher as [[淨挺]] (at [[KR6q0471]]) and [[淨啟]] (at [[KR6q0459]]). The juan-9 *yú-ān lǎo-héshàng xiàng* 愚菴老和尚像 verse names Sān-yí (also styled *Yú-ān* 愚菴) as *wú shī* 吾師. Lifedates and native place not preserved.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (conservative bound for an active *yǔ-lù*-producing Cáo-dòng heir of Sān-yí, who died 1665). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound for the post-master compilation by Zhì-zhí + Zhì-yuán.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school via Sān-yí Míng-yú line. Stands alongside [[淨挺]] (also Sān-yí heir, eighteen-juan *yǔ-lù* + multiple scholarly works) and [[淨啟]] (Sān-yí heir, *zhòng-biān* of the Hóng-zhì *yǔ-lù*) as the **third extant Sān-yí-line *yǔ-lù*** preserved in the Jiā-xīng Canon. Together these three volumes form a coherent late-1660s / 1670s Cáo-dòng publishing program.

## Other points of interest

- **Five-abbacy Háng-zhōu / Jiā-xīng circuit.** Shēn-fēng Xiàn's five-seat sequence (Zuì-lǐ → Qián-xī → Dōng-shān → Guān-yīn-yuàn → Yún-mén) traces a coherent Háng-zhōu / Jiā-xīng / Sū-zhōu Cáo-dòng circuit, ending at Sān-yí Míng-yú's home seat at Yún-mén Xiǎn-shèng-sì — a deliberate succession-claim by the dharma-heir to the master's flagship abbacy.
