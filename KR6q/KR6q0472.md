# Shēnfēng Xiàn chánshī yǔlù 屾峰憲禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shēn-fēng Xiàn*
by [[憲]] (說), [[智質]] (編), [[智原]] (編)

## About the work

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Shēnfēng Xiàn** [[憲]] 屾峰憲, Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìzhí** [[智質]] + **Zhìyuán** [[智原]] *tóng biān* 同編. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J34 B298 (note: in J34 cluster, distinct from the earlier Sānyí Cáodòng publications [[KR6q0459]], [[KR6q0471]] in J32–J33). Five-abbacy structure across juan 1–5: **Zuìlǐ Gǔ Zīshèngsì** 檇李古資聖寺, **Qiánxī Gǔ Qíchāngsì** 前溪古齊昌寺, **Dōngshān Mìaojuéyuàn** 東山妙覺院, **Guānyīnyuàn** 觀音院, **Yúnmén Xiǎnshèngsì** 雲門顯聖寺.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA A001774 and the [[憲]] note: dharma-heir of Sānyí Míngyú at Yúnménsì, the same teacher as [[淨挺]] (at [[KR6q0471]]) and [[淨啟]] (at [[KR6q0459]]). The juan-9 *yúān lǎohéshàng xiàng* 愚菴老和尚像 verse names Sānyí (also styled *Yúān* 愚菴) as *wú shī* 吾師. Lifedates and native place not preserved.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (conservative bound for an active *yǔlù*-producing Cáodòng heir of Sānyí, who died 1665). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound for the post-master compilation by Zhìzhí + Zhìyuán.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school via Sānyí Míngyú line. Stands alongside [[淨挺]] (also Sānyí heir, eighteen-juan *yǔlù* + multiple scholarly works) and [[淨啟]] (Sānyí heir, *zhòngbiān* of the Hóngzhì *yǔlù*) as the **third extant Sān-yí-line *yǔlù*** preserved in the Jiāxīng Canon. Together these three volumes form a coherent late-1660s / 1670s Cáodòng publishing program.

## Other points of interest

- **Five-abbacy Hángzhōu / Jiāxīng circuit.** Shēnfēng Xiàn's five-seat sequence (Zuìlǐ → Qiánxī → Dōngshān → Guānyīnyuàn → Yúnmén) traces a coherent Hángzhōu / Jiāxīng / Sūzhōu Cáodòng circuit, ending at Sānyí Míngyú's home seat at Yúnmén Xiǎnshèngsì — a deliberate succession-claim by the dharma-heir to the master's flagship abbacy.
