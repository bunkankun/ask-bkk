# Tōng-tiān Dàn-yá Yuán chán-shī yǔ-lù 通天澹崖原禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dàn-yá Yuán of Tōng-tiān [-sì]*
by [[超原]] (說), [[明德]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tōng-tiān Dàn-yá Chāo-yuán** [[超原]] 通天澹崖超原 (**1630-12-14 – 1698-05-27**, age 69, *sēng-là* 36), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tōng-tiān Yì-sǒu Xíng-gāo** [[行高]] (1616–1669, cf. [[KR6q0569]]). Native of **Xīng-huà Pú-tián** 興化莆田 (Fú-jiàn), lay surname **Zhāng** 張. Compiled by his dharma-son **Míng-dé** [[明德]] *děng biān*. Documents Chāo-yuán's one-year abbacy at **Pú-yáng Tōng-tiān chán-sì** 蒲陽通天禪寺 succeeding his teacher in **1669**, plus the subsequent retirement period during which he travelled the **Lú-shān** 廬嶽 and **Jīn-líng** 金陵 monastic circuits. *notBefore* = **1669** (Tōng-tiān succession); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-1698-death printing). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B456.

**Contents.** Two juan: *shàng-táng*, *xiǎo-cān*, *jī-yuán*, *jì*, *fó-shì*; closing *xíng-shí* (autobiographical / hagiographical narration); *lián-fāng-jì* listing his thirteen *fǎ-sì*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔ-lù* completes the teacher-disciple pair with [[KR6q0569]] — together documenting two generations of **Pú-yáng Tōng-tiān-sì** Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism in Fú-jiàn under the **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** branch. Chāo-yuán is the same person (or possibly homonymous within the broader Mì-yún-line cohort) who authored the prefaces to several Sì-chuān *yǔ-lù* in the J38–J39 cluster (cf. [[KR6q0554]], [[KR6q0557]], [[KR6q0559]] — all signed in 1695–1696, dated within his lifetime).
