# Chánlín bǎoxùn bǐ shuō 禪林寶訓筆說

"*Bǐ shuō* [Brush-Talk] Commentary on the *Chánlín bǎoxùn*" — a three-juan Qing running-commentary on [[KR6q0099]] *Chánlín bǎoxùn*, composed (*shù* 述) by [[智祥]] Zhìxiáng

## About the work

A three-juan Qing running-commentary in the *bǐ shuō* 筆說 ("brush-talk") informal-prose commentary format. X64 n1266. *commentedTextid*: KR6q0099. One of the cluster of Qing-period commentaries on the *Chánlín bǎoxùn* ([[KR6q0153]], [[KR6q0154]], [[KR6q0155]], [[KR6q0156]], and this [[KR6q0157]]).

## Other points of interest

The cluster of five Qing commentaries on the *Chánlín bǎoxùn* preserved in *Xù zàng jīng* volume 64 ([[KR6q0153]] through [[KR6q0157]]) testifies to the continuing importance of the *Chánlín bǎoxùn* as pedagogical reference in the late-imperial Chinese monastic curriculum. Each commentator adopts a different editorial format (*yīn yì*, *hé zhù*, *niān sòng*, *shùn zhū*, *bǐ shuō*), providing a cross-section of the Chinese late-imperial commentarial genre-ecosystem as applied to a single parent text.
