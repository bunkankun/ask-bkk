# Shànhuì dàshì yǔlù 善慧大士語錄

*Sayings-Record of the Great Layman Shànhuì* — a four-juan Táng-compiled *yǔlù* of the famous **Shànhuì dàshì** 善慧大士 (i.e., **Fù dàshì** 傅大士, "Fù the Great Layman," 497–569), the Liáng-dynasty lay Buddhist and visionary who is traditionally credited with major innovations in Chinese Buddhist liturgical-ceremonial practice (including the design of the rotating canonical-library wheel, *lún zàng* 輪藏). Compiled by the Táng author [[樓頴|Lóu Yǐng]] 樓頴.

## About the work

A four-juan Táng compilation of Fùdàshì's teachings and associated materials, X69 n1335. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

**Fùdàshì** 傅大士 / Shànhuì 善慧 (497–569): Liáng-dynasty lay Buddhist; a visionary-eccentric figure who is traditionally regarded as an incarnation of Maitreya. Associated with the monastery Sōngshān 嵩山 and later Wǔdōngshān 雙林山. Credited with the invention of the *lún zàng* rotating-canon-library and with numerous miraculous and prophetic compositions.

**Compiler [[樓頴|Lóu Yǐng]]** 樓頴: Táng-era compiler; lifedates unrecorded. His editorial work assembled the Liáng-era Fùdàshì materials into canonical *yǔlù* form.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 780 (Táng compilation horizon); *notAfter* c. 830.
