# Mì-xíng Rěn chán-shī yǔ-lù 密行忍禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mì-xíng Rěn*
by [[寂忍]] (說), [[明廣]] (等編), [[明珍]] (等編)

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Mì-xíng Jì-rěn** [[寂忍]] 密行寂忍 (DILA gives world-age 58 but no firm birth/death years), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666). Native of **Yún-nán Yí-liáng** 宜良, lay surname **Gǔ** 谷. The most senior of Pò-shān's Yún-nán-born heirs and the principal teacher whose lineage extended into Hú-guǎng / Héng-zhōu, where his Yún-nán dharma-heir [[學蘊]] received final instructions from him at **Kāi-fēng Nán-yún chán-yuàn** 開峰南雲禪院 (cf. [[KR6q0523]]). Compiled by his *fǎ-sūn* (dharma-grandsons) **Míng-guǎng** [[明廣]] and **Míng-zhēn** [[明珍]] *děng biān*.

Five abbacies are documented: **Xù-zhōu Xǐ-yù** 敘州喜獄, **Luò-yuán Xīng-guó** 雒源興國 (entered **順治丁酉** = **1657**, served as a *bì-guān* 掩關 / sealed-room retreat), **Bǎo-qìng Dà-bēi** 寶慶大悲 (Húnán), **Héng-yáng Kāi-fēng Nán-yún** 衡陽開峰南雲禪院 (Hú-guǎng), and a fifth implied. The principal preface is by **Zhú-làng Chè-shēng** [[徹生]] of Qīng-chéng-shān (cf. [[KR6q0544]]) signed at Jiā-xīng-fǔ in **康熙甲戌蒲月** = **1694** (Kāngxī 33, lunar 5) — composed during Chè-shēng's eastern engraving expedition for the *Jǐn-jiāng chán-dēng* 錦江禪燈. *notBefore* = **1657** (Luò-yuán Zhōng-xīng entry); *notAfter* = **1694** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B428.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface, *mù-cì*, *shàng-táng*. Juan 2: *jī-yuán*, *fǎ-yǔ* (示求戒諸禪者, 示禪者, 示鑿池諸禪者, 示映天飯頭, 示憨岳李居士, *zhí-shì-bǎng* 職事榜, *ān-chán-bǎng* 安禪榜). Juan 3: *sòng-gǔ* on classic *gōng-àn* (拈花微笑, 打車打牛, 德山托缽, 百丈野狐, 文殊問菴提遮女, 南泉斬貓, etc.) and the closing **行實** (autobiographical / hagiographical narration).

## Other points of interest

The cross-regional preface authorship — **Sì-chuān** Chè-shēng for a **Yún-nán** master's *yǔ-lù*, written at **Jiā-xīng** during a publishing expedition — is itself important documentation of how the Pò-shān Hǎi-míng network in the 1690s coordinated southwestern (Yún-nán-Sì-chuān-Guì-zhōu) Buddhist publishing through the eastern Jiā-xīng Léng-yán-sì 流通 channel. The *yǔ-lù*'s 行實 in juan 3 is also a primary source for the early-Qīng Yún-nán Línjì-Yángqí monastic landscape (Zhú-zǐ-shān, Yún-tái-shān), supplementing the [[KR6q0523]] 〈行錄〉 of his disciple [[學蘊]].
