# Yīng-níng Jìng chán-shī yǔ-lù 攖寧靜禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yīng-níng Jìng*
by [[智靜]] (說), [[德亮]] (等編), [[德莪]] (等錄)

## About the work

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yīng-níng Zhì-jìng** [[智靜]] 攖寧智靜, native of **Shān-yīn** 山陰 (Shào-xīng). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Dé-liàng** [[德亮]] *děng biān*; recorded by **Dé-é** [[德莪]] *děng lù*. Front-matter has illustrations (*<img:>*) and prefaces (some images-only in the source-files). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B286.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA A019266 and the juan-1 *xù*: trained extensively under [[圓悟]] **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** at Tiān-tóng and **Dōng-shān Ěr-mì** 東山爾密 (1590–1642). Decisive *xǐng* on the candle-extinguishment exchange with Ěr-mì when an attendant accidentally put out a *liú-lí dēng* 琉璃燈 lamp and Ěr-mì cried *huǒ niè* 火聻 ("where is the fire?") with the attendant's reply *yǒu zài zhè-lǐ* 有在這裡 ("right here"). Final transmission via **Mián-shí Jìng-yùn** 眠石淨蘊 at **Tiān-tái Hù-guó-sì** 天台護國寺 (the *chéng sì Hù-guó* 承嗣護國 reference). Three documented abbacies: **Jí-shān Jiè-zhū-sì** 蕺山戒珠寺 in Shào-xīng (his native region); **Wú-jiāng Yù-zhǐ-ān** 吳江喻指菴 (Sū-zhōu prefecture); **Lí-chuān Luó-hàn-sì** 黎川羅漢寺 (Jiāng-xī). Lifedates not preserved in DILA.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1635** (post-Mì-yún / Dōng-shān Ěr-mì training period; both teachers died 1642). *notAfter* = **1670** as a conservative bound for the post-decease compilation by Dé-liàng + Dé-é.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school heir of Mián-shí Jìng-yùn at Tiān-tái Hù-guó (a Cáo-dòng seat); the cross-school training with Línjì-line Mì-yún and Ěr-mì illustrates the cross-lineage practice common in the late-Míng / early-Qīng period.

## Other points of interest

- **Jí-shān Jiè-zhū-sì.** The Jí-shān Jiè-zhū-sì in Shào-xīng was a noted Wáng Xī-zhī 王羲之 calligraphy site (the *jiè-zhū tài-zǐ* 戒珠太子 stone). Zhì-jìng's abbacy there continued the literati-Buddhist tradition of the Yán-shān region.
- **Cross-school training pattern.** The Línjì → Cáo-dòng training-trajectory (Mì-yún / Ěr-mì → Mián-shí Jìng-yùn) parallels that documented elsewhere in this Jiā-xīng cluster (cf. [[KR6q0455]], [[KR6q0464]]) — illustrating the porous late-Míng / early-Qīng cross-school pedagogy.
