# Shí-pú Zhì chán-shī yǔ-lù 石璞質禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shí-pú Zhì*
by [[超質]] (說), [[明嵩]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Shí-pú Chāo-zhì** [[超質]] 石璞超質, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fó-gǔ Xíng-wén** [[行聞]] 佛古行聞 (1610–1666). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Míng-sōng** [[明嵩]] *děng biān*. Documented seats: **Píng-hú Cáo-xī Chán-yuàn** 嘉興平湖曹溪禪院 (1681 entry); **Tài-cāng Xiāng-lín Chán-yuàn** 姑蘇太倉香林禪院 (1682 entry) — succeeding to his master's two principal seats. *notBefore* = c. **1670** (post-master-decease succession). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B365.
