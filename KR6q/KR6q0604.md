# Mìyún Yí chánshī yǔlù 密雲怡禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mì-yún Yí*
by [[道忞]] (上進)

Ten-juan *yǔlù* of **Mìyún Yuánwù** [[圓悟]] 密雲圓悟 (**1566 – 1642**, age 77 *suì*). The catalog title 密雲怡禪師 — the character 怡 (*yí*) for **悟** (*wù*) is a typesetting variant or substitution; the inner-text title gives 密雲悟禪師. **The single most influential LínjìYángqí Chán master of the late Míng** — primary *fǎsì* of **Lóngchí Huànyǒu Zhèngchuán** [[正傳]] (cf. [[KR6q0601]]) and the master from whom the entire post-1620 LínjìYángqí establishment descends.

The Qiánlóng-canon recension (Vol. 154 No. 1640) was **presented to the imperial court** (*shàngjìn* 上進) by his most senior *fǎsì* **Mùchén Dàomǐn** [[道忞]] (1596–1674) — who himself served as **Shùnzhì-emperor's preceptor** at the imperial court. The *jìn ... zòuzhāng* 進...奏章 (presentation memorial) at the head of juan 1 indicates this is the imperial-edition recension. *notBefore* ≈ **1610** (post-Mì-yún's mature abbacy); *notAfter* ≈ **1660** (post-Dào-mǐn's imperial-court service when the *shàngjìn* would have occurred). Catalog dynasty 明 (since Mìyún's career is Míng).

**Contents.** Juan 1: *jìn ... zòuzhāng*; preface (by **Shù Yī** 庶一); *mùlù*; *shàngtáng* (continued through juan 3) at six successive abbacies — **Chángzhōu Jīngxī Lóngchí Yǔményuàn** 常州荊溪龍池禹門禪院 (his teacher's monastery), **Tiāntāishān Tōngxuánsì** 天台山通玄寺, **Jiāxīng Hǎiyán Jīnsùshān Guǎnghuìsì** 嘉興海鹽金粟山廣慧禪寺, **Fúzhōu Huángbòshān Wànfúsì** 福州府黃檗山萬福禪寺, **Míngzhōu Yùwáng Guǎnglìsì** 明州育王廣利禪寺 (*Āyùwángsì*), and **Míngzhōu Tàibáishān Tiāntóng Jǐngdé chánsì** 明州太白山天童景德禪寺 (the famous Tiāntóngsì, his principal seat). Juan 4: *shàngtáng kāishì*; *xiǎocān*. Juans 5+: continuing materials.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0604]] is the **most important** late-Míng LínjìYángqí *yǔlù* in the canon — Mìyún Yuánwù's six-abbacy circuit (Lóngchí, Tiāntāi, Jīnsù, Huángbò, Yùwáng, Tiāntóng) defines the geographic backbone of the late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì revival. From these six seats — and especially **Tiāntóngsì** at Níngbō — the Mìyún cohort radiated outward across the entire J27–J40 corpus: heirs include **Mùchén Dàomǐn** (the imperial preceptor), **Shíchē Tōngchéng** 石車通乘, **Wànrú Tōngwēi** 萬如通微, **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容, **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]], **Línyě Tōngqí** 林野通奇, **Hànyuè Fǎzàng** 漢月法藏, etc. — i.e. virtually the entire late-Míng / early-Qīng LínjìYángqí establishment. The *Shàngjìn* (court-presentation) framing makes this recension the imperial-canonical edition, prepared by Dàomǐn during or after his time as Shùnzhì's preceptor.
