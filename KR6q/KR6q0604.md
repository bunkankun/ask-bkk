# Mì-yún Yí chán-shī yǔ-lù 密雲怡禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mì-yún Yí*
by [[道忞]] (上進)

Ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] 密雲圓悟 (**1566 – 1642**, age 77 *suì*). The catalog title 密雲怡禪師 — the character 怡 (*yí*) for **悟** (*wù*) is a typesetting variant or substitution; the inner-text title gives 密雲悟禪師. **The single most influential Línjì-Yángqí Chán master of the late Míng** — primary *fǎ-sì* of **Lóng-chí Huàn-yǒu Zhèng-chuán** [[正傳]] (cf. [[KR6q0601]]) and the master from whom the entire post-1620 Línjì-Yángqí establishment descends.

The Qiánlóng-canon recension (Vol. 154 No. 1640) was **presented to the imperial court** (*shàng-jìn* 上進) by his most senior *fǎ-sì* **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** [[道忞]] (1596–1674) — who himself served as **Shùnzhì-emperor's preceptor** at the imperial court. The *jìn ... zòu-zhāng* 進...奏章 (presentation memorial) at the head of juan 1 indicates this is the imperial-edition recension. *notBefore* ≈ **1610** (post-Mì-yún's mature abbacy); *notAfter* ≈ **1660** (post-Dào-mǐn's imperial-court service when the *shàng-jìn* would have occurred). Catalog dynasty 明 (since Mì-yún's career is Míng).

**Contents.** Juan 1: *jìn ... zòu-zhāng*; preface (by **Shù Yī** 庶一); *mù-lù*; *shàng-táng* (continued through juan 3) at six successive abbacies — **Cháng-zhōu Jīng-xī Lóng-chí Yǔ-mén-yuàn** 常州荊溪龍池禹門禪院 (his teacher's monastery), **Tiān-tāi-shān Tōng-xuán-sì** 天台山通玄寺, **Jiā-xīng Hǎi-yán Jīn-sù-shān Guǎng-huì-sì** 嘉興海鹽金粟山廣慧禪寺, **Fú-zhōu Huáng-bò-shān Wàn-fú-sì** 福州府黃檗山萬福禪寺, **Míng-zhōu Yù-wáng Guǎng-lì-sì** 明州育王廣利禪寺 (*Ā-yù-wáng-sì*), and **Míng-zhōu Tài-bái-shān Tiān-tóng Jǐng-dé chán-sì** 明州太白山天童景德禪寺 (the famous Tiān-tóng-sì, his principal seat). Juan 4: *shàng-táng kāi-shì*; *xiǎo-cān*. Juans 5+: continuing materials.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0604 is the **most important** late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí *yǔ-lù* in the canon — Mì-yún Yuán-wù's six-abbacy circuit (Lóng-chí, Tiān-tāi, Jīn-sù, Huáng-bò, Yù-wáng, Tiān-tóng) defines the geographic backbone of the late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì revival. From these six seats — and especially **Tiān-tóng-sì** at Níng-bō — the Mì-yún cohort radiated outward across the entire J27–J40 corpus: heirs include **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** (the imperial preceptor), **Shí-chē Tōng-chéng** 石車通乘, **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** 萬如通微, **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容, **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]], **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇, **Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** 漢月法藏, etc. — i.e. virtually the entire late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí establishment. The *Shàng-jìn* (court-presentation) framing makes this recension the imperial-canonical edition, prepared by Dào-mǐn during or after his time as Shùnzhì's preceptor.
