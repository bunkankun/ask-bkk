# Pǔ-mén Xiǎn chán-shī yǔ-lù 普門顯禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Pǔ-mén Xiǎn*
by [[燈顯]] (說), [[性胤]] (錄)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Pǔ-mén Dēng-xiǎn** [[燈顯]] 普門燈顯 (lifedates unknown), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668) — thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]] (KR6q0554), [[玄億]] (KR6q0555), [[至善]] (KR6q0556), [[幻敏]] (KR6q0592) in the **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line. Native of **Fú-líng** 涪陵 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Xià** 夏. Recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Xìng-yìn** [[性胤]] *lù*. Documents teaching at **Fú-líng Huí-lóng-sì** 涪陵迴龍寺 and **Tóng-bǎi Tán-huá-sì** 桐柏曇華寺.

*notBefore* ≈ **1668** (post-Tiě-bì-death abbacy onset); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-printing). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B485.

**Contents.** Juan 1: *zhū-shān qǐng-qǐ* (multiple *qǐng-qǐ* invitations); *pǔ-shuō*; *xiǎo-cān*; *fǎ-yǔ*; *jī-yuán*. Juan 2: *fó-shì*; *zàn-jì* (image-encomia for Bodhidharma, Avalokiteśvara, his dharma-grandfather **Jù-yún Lǎo-rén** 聚雲師翁 = 聚雲吹萬, his teacher **Qìng-zhōng Lǎo-rén** 慶忠老人; landscape-image *xiàng-zàn* including 四景畫軸; *fǎ-yǔ* to disciples (示鎮九文學, 示舌端上座, 示睿珍上座, etc.); closing **塔銘** preserving lineage and abbacy details.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0554 ([[燈炳]]), KR6q0555 ([[玄億]]), KR6q0556 ([[至善]]), KR6q0592 ([[幻敏]]), and KR6q0593 ([[燈顯]]) together form the most complete surviving lineage-record of the **Jù-yún 聚雲** sub-line of Sì-chuān Línjì-Dà-huì school in the second post-1644 generation — five major *yǔ-lù* by direct heirs of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī**.
