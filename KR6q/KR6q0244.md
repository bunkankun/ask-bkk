# Chán zōng sòng gǔ lián zhū tōng jí 禪宗頌古聯珠通集 (ZhonghuaCanon recension, 21 juan)

*Complete Collection of Linked-Pearl Verses-on-Ancient-Cases of the Chán School* — a twenty-one-juan alternate recension of the SòngYuán Chán *sòng gǔ* anthology originally compiled by [[法應|Fǎyìng]] 法應 (Sòng) and continued by [[普會|Pǔhuì]] 普會 (Yuán), here with a Ming-era re-collation (*chóng jiào* 重校) by [[淨戒|Jìngjiè]] 淨戒. Distinct from the 卍續藏 40-juan recension [[KR6q0243]].

## About the work

A twenty-one-juan SòngYuánMíng transitional Chán *sòng gǔ* anthology, preserved in the Zhōnghuá Canon (vol. 78, no. 1720). Non-commentary on a single parent text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The 21-juan scope represents a substantially abridged form of the original 40-juan anthology ([[KR6q0243]]), likely produced by Míng-era recension as a more compact reference-edition for practical monastic use. Jìngjiè's Ming-era re-collation preserves the core material while reducing the number of verse-responses per case.

See [[KR6q0243]] for broader context on Fǎyìng, Pǔhuì, and the original 40-juan compilation.

**[[淨戒|Jìngjiè]]** 淨戒: Ming-era re-collator of this 21-juan recension. Lifedates and specific biographical details unrecorded.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1175 (Fǎyìng's original compilation); *notAfter* c. 1450 (the approximate horizon for Jìngjiè's Míng-era re-collation). The received form thus represents nearly three centuries of cumulative editorial development.
