# Sān-fēng Zàng hé-shàng yǔ-lù 三峰藏和尚語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Reverend Sān-fēng Zàng*
by [[法藏]] (說), [[弘儲]] (記)

## About the work

Sixteen-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng Fǎ-zàng** [[法藏]] 漢月三峰法藏 (1573-11-11 – 1635-09-03), late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of [[圓悟]] **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** (received *fù-fǎ* in 1624) and the most theologically prominent — and ultimately most controversial — of Yuán-wù's heirs. Recorded by his chief dharma-heir **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] 退翁弘儲 (1605–1672). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B299. The j. 16 *nián-pǔ* (year-chronology, 1573–1635) is the principal source for Fǎ-zàng's biography.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[法藏]] note: lay surname **Sū** 蘇, native of **Liáng-xī** 梁溪 (Wú-xī, Jiāng-sū). Lifedates **萬曆 1 / 10/7 = 1573-11-11** to **崇禎 8 / 7/22 = 1635-09-03**, age 63. *Zì* **Hàn-yuè** 漢月; *hào* **Sān-fēng** 三峰 (after **Sān-fēng Qīng-liáng-yuàn** 三峰清涼院 at Yú-shān 虞山 in Cháng-shú), also **Yú-mì** 於密, **Tiān-shān** 天山, **Xuán-mù** 玄墓. Abbacies at **Sān-fēng Qīng-liáng**, **Xuán-mù** (Dèng-wèi-shān, Sū-zhōu), **Tiān-shān**, and **Wàn-fēng / Shèng-ēn-sì** 萬峰 / 聖恩禪寺 at **Píng-wàng** 平望.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1624** (post-*fù-fǎ* receipt from Mì-yún and beginning of independent preaching). *notAfter* = **1635** (death). The 16-juan corpus covers his complete public preaching career.

**Doctrinal centrality.** Author of [[KR6q0167]] 《五宗原》 (the 1628 theological defense of the Five Houses of Chán doctrine) which precipitated the major seventeenth-century Chán controversy. Mì-yún Yuán-wù wrote [[KR6q0168]] 《闢妄救略說》 against him; the dispute reached imperial attention with the Yōng-zhèng emperor's 1733 《御製揀魔辨異錄》 declaring Fǎ-zàng a heretic and banning his writings. Despite this, the J34 B299 *yǔ-lù* survived in the Jiā-xīng Canon imprint.

**Lineage.** Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng → **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] (continuator at Sū-zhōu Líng-yán-sì, [[KR6q0475]]) — the principal sub-lineage that became the target of the 1733 imperial proscription.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon Míng *yǔ-lù* (J34 B299). The juan-16 nián-pǔ provides the principal documentation.*

## Other points of interest

- **Subject of the most consequential Chán doctrinal controversy of the seventeenth century.** The *Wǔ-zōng yuán* / *Pì-wàng jiù-lüè-shuō* dispute, prolonged through the *Zhì-fēi biàn* 智非辨 and other rejoinders, dominated mid-seventeenth-century Chán intellectual life and was suppressed by Yōng-zhèng in 1733 with consequences for the Sān-fēng line lasting into the modern era.
- **Survival despite proscription.** That this 16-juan *yǔ-lù* was preserved in the Jiā-xīng Canon despite the 1733 imperial banning of Fǎ-zàng's writings reflects the limits of Yōng-zhèng's suppression — the Jiā-xīng Canon, having been printed through privately-funded subscription in the seventeenth century, was not under direct imperial editorial control.
