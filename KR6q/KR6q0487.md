# Fó-yǔ Yù chán-shī yǔ-lù 佛語御禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Fó-yǔ Yù*
by [[徹御]] (說), [[法傑]] (等編), [[法秀]] (等編), [[聖可]] (序)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Fó-yǔ Chè-yù** [[徹御]] 佛語徹御 (d. **1674-01-21**, age 40), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huì-jué Zhào-yī** [[照衣]] 慧覺照衣 (1605–1673) at **Yú-zhōu Xiāng-guó-sì** 渝州香國寺 (Chóng-qìng). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fǎ-jié** [[法傑]] + **Yì-táng Fǎ-xiù** [[法秀]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[聖可]] / [[德玉]] (1628–1701) — Chè-yù's dharma-uncle in the Pòshān-line. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B341.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[徹御]] note: native of **Jiā-zhōu** 嘉州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Fàn** 范. Tonsured at age 10 under Zhào-yī. Awakening on a corpse-meditation incident. Sole abbacy at **Chóng-qìng Xiāng-guó-sì**. Died **康熙12 12/15** = **1674-01-21 Gregorian**, age 40.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1665** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1674** (death). Brief 40-year life and one-juan extent reflect the brevity of his ministry.

**Lineage.** Pò-shān Hǎi-míng → Huì-jué Zhào-yī → Fó-yǔ Chè-yù → Yì-táng Fǎ-xiù [[法秀]]. The Pòshān-line through Zhào-yī produced multiple short-lived but documented *yǔ-lù* — Chè-yù at age 40, Fǎ-xiù succession at Xiāng-guó-sì.
