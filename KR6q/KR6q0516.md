# Cháng-mù Diàn chán-shī yǔ-lù 長目電禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cháng-mù Diàn*
by [[光電]] (說), [[悟本]] (編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Cháng-mù Guāng-diàn** [[光電]] (**1636-11-29 – 1685-08-27**, *shì-shòu* 50), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Héng-xiù Fā-lín** [[發林]] (cf. [[KR6q0494]]) in the Pòshān → Yún-huàn → Héng-xiù → Cháng-mù line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Wù-běn** [[悟本]]. *fǎ-fù* 1670; three Pòshān-line abbacies. *notBefore* = **1670** (transmission), *notAfter* = **1685** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B391.
