# Tōng xuán bǎi wèn 通玄百問

*Hundred Questions on Penetrating-Mystery* — a one-juan late-Jīn / early-Yuán Chán Q&A collection: 100 questions posed by the lay Buddhist [[圓通|Yuán-tōng]] 圓通 and answered (*yǎng dá* 仰答 "respectfully responded to") by [[行秀|Wàn-sōng Xíng-xiù]] 萬松行秀 (1166–1246).

## About the work

A one-juan Chán Q&A catechism, X67 n1312. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text's 100 questions systematically interrogate Wàn-sōng on the major Chán doctrinal and practical topics. Parallel in form to other Chán catechetical Q&A collections from the period.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1220 (Wàn-sōng's mature productive period); *notAfter* 1246 (Wàn-sōng's death).
