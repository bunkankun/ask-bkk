# Zhāo-jué Zhú-fēng Xù chán-shī yǔ-lù 昭覺竹峰續禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhú-fēng Xù of Zhāo-jué [-sì]*
by [[真續]] (說), [[實悟]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zhú-fēng Zhēn-xù** [[真續]] 竹峰真續 (b. **1660-05-16** on Buddha's Birthday), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Fó-yuān Chè-gāng** 佛冤徹綱 (1626–?). Native of **Sōng-zhōu** 松州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Shěn** 沈. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Dé-yún Shí-wù** [[實悟]] *děng biān*.

Documents teaching at five Sì-chuān seats: **Wēi-fèng Zhāo-jué-sì** 威鳳昭覺寺 (the historic mother-temple); **Miào-jué Chóng-shàn** 妙覺崇善; **Xī-lóng Dà-bēi** 西龍大悲; **Xī-chóng Wú-liàng** 西崇無量; **Fèng-huáng Fǎ-zàng** 鳳凰法藏. *notBefore* ≈ **1690** (post-付法 abbacy onset, after his time at Zhāo-jué under Fó-yuān); *notAfter* ≈ **1720** (large *fǎ-sì* roster of 83 indicates a long career). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B479.

**Contents.** Juan 1: four prefaces; *mù-cì*; *shàng-táng*. Juan 2–3: continuing abbacy *yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*. Juan 4: *sòng-gǔ* on classic *gōng-àn* (明星悟道, 拈花微笑, 婆子燒菴, 南泉斬貓, 三玄三要, 馬祖三喝, 牧牛頌, 十無頌 with sub-verses on *wú-gēn-shù* 無根樹, *wú-xián-qín* 無絃琴, *wú-yuè-suǒ* 無鑰鎖). Juan 5: *xíng-shí*; *fēn-dēng* listing the 83 *fǎ-sì*. Juan 6: closing *fó-shì* and miscellaneous.

## Other points of interest

The eighty-three named *fǎ-sì* in juan 5 is one of the largest disciple-cohorts attested in any single Jiā-xīng-canon Línjì-Yángqí *yǔ-lù*, indicating Zhú-fēng's institutional weight at **Zhāo-jué-sì** 昭覺寺 — the principal mother-temple of Sì-chuān Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism, whence the 18th-century revival of southwestern Buddhism radiated. The succession of Zhāo-jué abbacies from **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) through his second-generation heirs ([[徹生]] [[KR6q0544]]) and third-generation heir Zhú-fēng (b. 1660, this text) marks the institutional continuity of the Sì-chuān line into the 18th century.
