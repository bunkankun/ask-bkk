# Láng-yá Huì-jué chánshī yǔlù (xù) 瑯琊慧覺禪師語錄（序）

*Preface to the Sayings-Record of Chán Master Láng-yá Huì-jué* — a one-juan preface-only text for the *yǔlù* of the Northern-Sòng master **Láng-yá Huì-jué** 瑯琊慧覺, one of the major contemporaries of Shí-shuāng Chǔ-yuán in the Línjì line. Written by his disciple (*cān xué* 參學) [[用孫|Yòng-sūn]] 用孫. The main body of the *yǔlù* proper is not preserved in this catalog-entry; only the preface.

## About the work

A one-juan preface-only text preserving a Northern-Sòng *yǔlù*-preface, X69 n1339. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

**Láng-yá Huì-jué** 瑯琊慧覺: Northern-Sòng Línjì-lineage master. Lifedates unrecorded; dharma-heir of **Fén-yáng Shàn-zhāo** 汾陽善昭 (947–1024). One of the second-generation Línjì masters active between Shàn-zhāo and Shí-shuāng Chǔ-yuán.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1050 (Huì-jué's mature teaching-years); *notAfter* c. 1100.
