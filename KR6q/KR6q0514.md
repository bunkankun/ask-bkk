# Hóng-shān Yú-zhāo Yǔn-fén chán-shī yǔ-lù 洪山俞昭允汾禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Yú-zhāo Yǔn-fén of Hóng-shān*
by [[允汾]] (說), [[上睿]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yú-zhāo Yǔn-fén** [[允汾]] 俞昭允汾 (**1622 – 1680-02-12**, *shì-shòu* 59, *sēng-là* 38), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] in the Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chǔ-lín Shàng-ruì** [[上睿]] *děng biān*. Six abbacies: **西林乾明** → **潼泉法興** → **東巖廣慧** → **竟陵紫芝** → **寶通報國** → **天台赤城**. Title-form *Hóng-shān* references his **Wǔ-chāng Hóng-shān** abbacy. Juan 5 includes the *Xiào-táng héshàng tǎ-míng*. *notBefore* = c. 1655 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1680 (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B389.
