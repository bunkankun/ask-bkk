# Biéān chánshī tóng mén lù 別菴禪師同門錄

*Same-Gate [i.e., Fellow-Lineage] Record of Chán Master Biéān* — a three-juan Chán-lineage genealogical record by [[性統|Biéān Xìngtǒng]] 別庵性統 (1661–1717) himself, compiled by his dharma-heir [[弘秀|Hóng-xiù]] 弘秀. Documents the *tóng mén* 同門 ("same gate" / fellow dharma-brother) lineage-network of Xìngtǒng's own generation of Línjì Chán masters descending from [[燈來|Sānshān Dēnglái]] 三山燈來.

## About the work

A three-juan lineage-genealogical record, J39 B445. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The *tóng mén lù* 同門錄 genre documents the "brotherhood" (lineage-siblings) of a given master — recording the various dharma-heirs of a single teacher and their subsequent monastic careers. Xìngtǒng's three-juan compilation thus serves as a parallel-track documentation to the canonical lamp-records: rather than tracing the vertical descent of the Línjì dharma-transmission, the *tóng mén lù* records the horizontal spread of dharma-siblings within a single master-generation.

Xìngtǒng's broader editorial programme — of which this text is one component — comprehensively documents the Sānshān Dēnglái lineage-tradition through multiple genres: doctrinal synthesis [[KR6q0169]], biographical chronology [[KR6q0209]], ceremonial compositions [[KR6q0232]], and now fellow-disciple records [[KR6q0233]]. Together these constitute an unusually complete self-documenting Chán lineage-archive.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1690 (a reasonable anchor for Xìngtǒng's mature editorial productivity); *notAfter* 1717 (Xìngtǒng's death).
