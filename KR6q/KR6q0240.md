# Hè-shān chánshī zhí zhǒu jí 鶴山禪師執帚集

*Broom-Holding Collection of Chán Master Hè-shān* — a two-juan Qīng Chán *yǔlù*-style collection by [[鶴山|Hè-shān]] 鶴山 ("Crane Mountain"). Compiled by his disciple [[德敷|Dé-fū]] 德敷. The title's *zhí zhǒu* 執帚 ("holding a broom") is a classical self-deprecating monastic image, referring to the Chán master's humble ongoing sweeping of his own and others' delusion.

## About the work

A two-juan *yǔlù*-style collection, J40 B497. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. Preserves Hè-shān's teaching-voice: hall-sermons, *gōng'àn* commentary, letters, poetry.

### Abstract

[[鶴山|Hè-shān]]: Qīng Chán master. *Hào* only (dharma-name not identified in the catalog single-character). Lifedates unrecorded.

**Compiler [[德敷|Dé-fū]]** 德敷: Hè-shān's disciple.

Dating: broad Qīng bracket (*notBefore* c. 1660, *notAfter* c. 1720).
