# Zhuó-fēng Jué chán-shī yǔ-lù 卓峰玨禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhuó-fēng Jué*
by [[性玨]] (說), [[宗位]] (編), [[超源]] (序), [[藍理]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zhuó-fēng Xìng-jué** [[性玨]] 卓峰性玨 (**1625-08-21 – 1685** autumn, age 61), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** [[燈炳]] (1611–1680, cf. [[KR6q0554]]) in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line. Native of **Zhōng-zhōu** 忠州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Chéng** 成. Compiled by his sole *fǎ-sì* **Zōng-wèi** [[宗位]] *biān* (the same compiler of [[KR6q0554]]). Two prefaces: by **Chāo-yuán Dàn-yá** [[超源]] dated **康熙乙亥王春** = **1695** spring; and by the military commander **Lán Lǐ** [[藍理]] dated **康熙丙子孟春月上浣** = **1696** lunar 1 — i.e. the same two preface-authors as the parallel teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0554]]. *notBefore* = **1680** (Qìng-yún succession at his teacher's death); *notAfter* = **1696** (latest preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B444.

**Contents.** Single juan: two prefaces; *Sì-chuān Zhōng-zhōu Qìng-yún chán-sì yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*; *fó-shì* (a substantial sequence for his teacher Dēng-bǐng's death in 1680: *ān-zuò* 安座, *qǐ-kān* 起龕, *jǔ-huǒ* 舉火, *shōu-líng-gǔ* 收靈骨, *qǐ-líng-gǔ* 起靈骨, *rù-tǎ* 入塔, *shǒu-jiǔ* 首九, *bǎi-qī niān-xiāng* 百期拈香 — i.e. the standard mortuary rituals from cremation through hundredth-day commemoration); *jì*; closing materials including *tǎ-míng* with birth and death dates.

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J39 B444).*

## Other points of interest

The *fó-shì* section preserves a complete liturgical record of the funerary cycle for **Héng-shān Dēng-bǐng** 衡山燈炳 in 1680, paralleling the analogous *fó-shì* sequences in [[KR6q0557]] and [[KR6q0558]] for **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** 三山燈來 (1685). Together these provide a remarkable comparative dossier on late-17th-century Sì-chuān **Jù-yún 聚雲** Línjì-Dà-huì school mortuary ritual.
