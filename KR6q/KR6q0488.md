# Huáyán Shèngkě chánshī yǔlù 華嚴聖可禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huá-yán Shèng-kě*
by [[德玉]] (說), [[光佛]] (等編)

## About the work

Five-juan *yǔlù* of **Shèngkě Déyù** [[德玉]] 聖可德玉 (**1628-08-12 – 1701/02**, *shìshòu* 73), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Pòshān Hǎimíng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) at **Huáyánsì** 華嚴寺 in Sìchuān (whence his abbacy-form *Huáyán Shèngkě* / *Huáyán lǎorén*). Compiled by *fǎsì* **Guāngfó** [[光佛]] *děng biān*. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J35 B342.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[德玉]] note: native of **Yíngshān** 營山 (Sìchuān; same prefecture as [[慧機]] Tiěbì Huìjī). Born 1628; tonsured under Pòshān during the 1640s–50s; transmission within the Pòshān-line. Sole long-tenure abbacy at **Huáyánsì** in Sìchuān, where he served until his 1701 death.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1701** (death). The five-juan extent covers the long 1660s–1700 preaching career.

**Other works.** Apart from this *yǔlù*, Déyù was a noted *shùnzhū* commentary author: [[KR6q0156]] 《禪林寶訓順硃》 (commentary on *Chánlín bǎoxùn*) and [[KR6q0227]] 《道德經順硃》 (commentary on *Lǎozǐ*). Also wrote the front-preface to his dharma-nephew [[徹御]]'s *yǔlù* [[KR6q0487]].

**Lineage and significance.** Listed in the [[海明]] Pòshān-line note as one of the principal heirs (the "Huáyán line"), parallel to **Zhàngxuě Tōngzuì** [[通醉]] (Zhāojué line) and **Liányuè** [[道正]] (Yùquán line). His exceptional longevity (73 years, dying 1701) and combined Chán-and-classical-text-commentary output make him distinctive among the Pòshān heirs.

## Other points of interest

- **Multi-text-commentary Chán master.** Déyù's combination of *yǔlù* preaching and classical-text commentary across both Buddhist (*Chánlín bǎoxùn*) and Daoist (*Lǎozǐ*) traditions makes him an unusual hybrid figure within the Pòshān-line — paralleling the [[淨挺]] Liángtíng Cáo-dòng-side polymath model.
