# Huá-yán Shèng-kě chán-shī yǔ-lù 華嚴聖可禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huá-yán Shèng-kě*
by [[德玉]] (說), [[光佛]] (等編)

## About the work

Five-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] 聖可德玉 (**1628-08-12 – 1701/02**, *shì-shòu* 73), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666) at **Huá-yán-sì** 華嚴寺 in Sì-chuān (whence his abbacy-form *Huá-yán Shèng-kě* / *Huá-yán lǎo-rén*). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Guāng-fó** [[光佛]] *děng biān*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B342.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[德玉]] note: native of **Yíng-shān** 營山 (Sì-chuān; same prefecture as [[慧機]] Tiě-bì Huì-jī). Born 1628; tonsured under Pòshān during the 1640s–50s; transmission within the Pòshān-line. Sole long-tenure abbacy at **Huá-yán-sì** in Sì-chuān, where he served until his 1701 death.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1701** (death). The five-juan extent covers the long 1660s–1700 preaching career.

**Other works.** Apart from this *yǔ-lù*, Dé-yù was a noted *shùn-zhū* commentary author: [[KR6q0156]] 《禪林寶訓順硃》 (commentary on *Chánlín bǎoxùn*) and [[KR6q0227]] 《道德經順硃》 (commentary on *Lǎo-zǐ*). Also wrote the front-preface to his dharma-nephew [[徹御]]'s *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0487]].

**Lineage and significance.** Listed in the [[海明]] Pòshān-line note as one of the principal heirs (the "Huá-yán line"), parallel to **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] (Zhāo-jué line) and **Lián-yuè** [[道正]] (Yù-quán line). His exceptional longevity (73 years, dying 1701) and combined Chán-and-classical-text-commentary output make him distinctive among the Pòshān heirs.

## Other points of interest

- **Multi-text-commentary Chán master.** Dé-yù's combination of *yǔ-lù* preaching and classical-text commentary across both Buddhist (*Chánlín bǎoxùn*) and Daoist (*Lǎo-zǐ*) traditions makes him an unusual hybrid figure within the Pòshān-line — paralleling the [[淨挺]] Liáng-tíng Cáo-dòng-side polymath model.
