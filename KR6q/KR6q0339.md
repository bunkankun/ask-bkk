# Shí-xī Xīn-yuè chánshī zá-lù 石溪心月禪師雜錄

One-juan miscellany of **Shí-xī Xīn-yuè** [[心月]] 石溪心月 (d. 1254), *hào* Chuán-yī 傳衣 ("Robe-Transmitter"), posthumously **Fó-hǎi chánshī**. *Xuzangjing* X71 n1406. The running banner reads *Chuán-yī Shí-xī Fó-hǎi chánshī zá-lù*. Occasional verse and prose not absorbed into the main *yǔlù* ([[KR6q0338]]): *hé* 和 (response-verse) to laymen, farewell poems for travelling monks, single-*shǒu* occasional pieces. Short but characterful — shares the hermit-poetic sensibility that is a signature of mid-Sòng Jìng-shān Chán.
