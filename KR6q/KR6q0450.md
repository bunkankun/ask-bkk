# Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Jī chán-shī yǔ-lù 慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Jī*
by [[慧機]] (說), [[燈來]] (編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** [[慧機]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668), the principal Sì-chuān Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Jù-yún Chuī-wàn Guǎng-zhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639) and Huì-jī's second-generation head of the **Jù-yún-pài** 聚雲派 at **Jù-yún-sì** 聚雲寺 (Zhōng-zhōu, Sì-chuān). Compiled by the chief dharma-heir **Sān-shān Dēng-lái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685), who simultaneously edited the Guǎng-zhēn volumes [[KR6q0448]] and [[KR6q0449]]. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J29 B240. Contents: *shàng-juan* — *shàng-táng*; *zhōng-juan* — *pǔ-shuō* / *xiǎo-cān* / *shì-zhòng* / *fǎ-yǔ* / *biān-cè-yǔ* / *chá-huà*; *xià-juan* — *jī-yuán* / *wèn-dá* / *sòng-gǔ* / *fó-shì* / *shī-jì*. Two prefaces: (i) **Móu Dào-xíng** 牟道行 *hóng-wù* 弘悟 (*jìn-shì, lì-bù shàng-shū-láng qián Zǐ-wēi shě-rén, jīn pú-sà-jiè dì-zǐ Gǔ-róng bǐng-sù dào-rén* 賜進士出身吏部尚書郎前紫微舍人今菩薩戒弟子古戎秉素道人), undated, a *zhē-biǎo* 遮表 dialectical framing of Tiě-bì's preaching; (ii) **Xióng Rǔ-xué** 熊汝學 of **Lú-shān Qiān-zhàng-yán** (same preface-writer as [[KR6q0448]], [[KR6q0449]]), dated **崇禎壬午秋九日 = 1642 autumn 9th**, signing as *dì-zǐ* 弟子 — establishing Xióng's formal lay-disciple status under Tiě-bì.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1639** (Tiě-bì's succession to the Jù-yún abbacy on Guǎng-zhēn's death in **崇禎十二** / 1639) — the earliest possible start of any Tiě-bì *yǔ-lù* material. *notAfter* = **1642** (Xióng Rǔ-xué preface). This 3-juan selective compilation was cut together with the Guǎng-zhēn B238/B239 complex (same Zhōng-zhōu Zhì-píng printing-workshop, same preface-circle) in the 1642 Jù-yún-pài publication campaign.

**Relation to [[KR6q0451]].** This B240 (3 juan) is the early selective *yǔ-lù* covering Tiě-bì's first preaching-period after assuming the Jù-yún abbacy; [[KR6q0451]] (20 juan, J29 B241) is the definitive *chóng-biān* 重編 expansion prepared after Tiě-bì's 1668 death by his other chief heir **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]], and incorporates the later seven abbacies (Qìng-zhōng, Yún-yán, Bǎo-shèng, Zhì-píng) not yet open at the time of this B240 imprint.

**Móu Dào-xíng's preface.** Uses the classical *zhē-biǎo* 遮表 (concealing / revealing) antithesis as a framework for reading Tiě-bì's *yǔ-lù*: each utterance is analyzed as simultaneously *zhē* and *biǎo*, with Móu challenging the reader to locate which is which. An unusual critical preface emphasizing the formal problem of interpreting a *yǔ-lù* against contemporary "blind leading blind" (*yī-máng zhòng-máng* 一盲眾盲) Chán-reading tendencies.

**Xióng Rǔ-xué's preface.** Locates Tiě-bì's teaching in the **Sōng-shān** 嵩山 (Dà-mó) → Guǎng-zhēn → Tiě-bì transmission-line and presents Tiě-bì as the one who *continues-and-opens* (*jì-wǎng kāi-lái* 繼往開來) the Jù-yún revival of **Dà-huì Zōng-gǎo's** Línjì teaching. Xióng admits he never met Guǎng-zhēn (*yú hèn bù jí jiàn Jù-yún* 余恨不及見聚雲) and came to the lineage through Tiě-bì directly.

## Other points of interest

- **Paired with [[KR6q0451]] as a single Tiě-bì corpus.** B240 (3 juan, early recension, 1642) and B241 (20 juan, definitive *chóng-biān*, post-1668) together constitute the complete extant Tiě-bì *yǔ-lù* — the B240 covering only the opening 1639–42 window of the Jù-yún seat, B241 the full thirty-two-year preaching career.
- **Same compiler, same preface-circle.** Dēng-lái's four consecutive Jiā-xīng Canon entries [[KR6q0448]] / [[KR6q0449]] / [[KR6q0450]] / [[KR6q0451]] (J29 B238–B241) form a single publishing campaign of the Jù-yún-pài lineage: grandfather (Guǎng-zhēn, two volumes) + father (Tiě-bì Huì-jī, two volumes) compiled by the grandson (Dēng-lái), with Xióng Rǔ-xué as the common lay-patron preface-writer across all four.
