# Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Jī chánshī yǔlù 慶忠鐵壁機禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Jī*
by [[慧機]] (說), [[燈來]] (編)

## About the work

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Qìngzhōng Tiěbì Huìjī** [[慧機]] 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668), the principal Sìchuān LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Jùyún Chuīwàn Guǎngzhēn** [[廣真]] 聚雲吹萬廣真 (1582–1639) and Huìjī's second-generation head of the **Jùyúnpài** 聚雲派 at **Jùyúnsì** 聚雲寺 (Zhōngzhōu, Sìchuān). Compiled by the chief dharma-heir **Sānshān Dēnglái** [[燈來]] 三山燈來 (1614–1685), who simultaneously edited the Guǎngzhēn volumes [[KR6q0448]] and [[KR6q0449]]. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J29 B240. Contents: *shàng-juan* — *shàngtáng*; *zhōng-juan* — *pǔshuō* / *xiǎocān* / *shìzhòng* / *fǎyǔ* / *biāncèyǔ* / *cháhuà*; *xià-juan* — *jīyuán* / *wèndá* / *sònggǔ* / *fóshì* / *shījì*. Two prefaces: (i) **Móu Dàoxíng** 牟道行 *hóngwù* 弘悟 (*jìnshì, lìbù shàngshūláng qián Zǐwēi shěrén, jīn púsàjiè dìzǐ Gǔróng bǐngsù dàorén* 賜進士出身吏部尚書郎前紫微舍人今菩薩戒弟子古戎秉素道人), undated, a *zhēbiǎo* 遮表 dialectical framing of Tiěbì's preaching; (ii) **Xióng Rǔxué** 熊汝學 of **Lúshān Qiānzhàngyán** (same preface-writer as [[KR6q0448]], [[KR6q0449]]), dated **崇禎壬午秋九日 = 1642 autumn 9th**, signing as *dìzǐ* 弟子 — establishing Xióng's formal lay-disciple status under Tiěbì.

### Abstract

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1639** (Tiěbì's succession to the Jùyún abbacy on Guǎngzhēn's death in **崇禎十二** / 1639) — the earliest possible start of any Tiěbì *yǔlù* material. *notAfter* = **1642** (Xióng Rǔxué preface). This 3-juan selective compilation was cut together with the Guǎngzhēn B238/B239 complex (same Zhōngzhōu Zhìpíng printing-workshop, same preface-circle) in the 1642 Jùyúnpài publication campaign.

**Relation to [[KR6q0451]].** This B240 (3 juan) is the early selective *yǔlù* covering Tiěbì's first preaching-period after assuming the Jùyún abbacy; [[KR6q0451]] (20 juan, J29 B241) is the definitive *chóngbiān* 重編 expansion prepared after Tiěbì's 1668 death by his other chief heir **Zhúfēng Huànmǐn** [[幻敏]], and incorporates the later seven abbacies (Qìngzhōng, Yúnyán, Bǎoshèng, Zhìpíng) not yet open at the time of this B240 imprint.

**Móu Dàoxíng's preface.** Uses the classical *zhēbiǎo* 遮表 (concealing / revealing) antithesis as a framework for reading Tiěbì's *yǔlù*: each utterance is analyzed as simultaneously *zhē* and *biǎo*, with Móu challenging the reader to locate which is which. An unusual critical preface emphasizing the formal problem of interpreting a *yǔlù* against contemporary "blind leading blind" (*yīmáng zhòngmáng* 一盲眾盲) Chán-reading tendencies.

**Xióng Rǔxué's preface.** Locates Tiěbì's teaching in the **Sōngshān** 嵩山 (Dàmó) → Guǎngzhēn → Tiěbì transmission-line and presents Tiěbì as the one who *continues-and-opens* (*jìwǎng kāilái* 繼往開來) the Jùyún revival of **Dàhuì Zōnggǎo's** Línjì teaching. Xióng admits he never met Guǎngzhēn (*yú hèn bù jí jiàn Jùyún* 余恨不及見聚雲) and came to the lineage through Tiěbì directly.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J29 B240). The Móu Dàoxíng and Xióng Rǔxué prefaces provide the principal documentation.*

## Other points of interest

- **Paired with [[KR6q0451]] as a single Tiěbì corpus.** B240 (3 juan, early recension, 1642) and B241 (20 juan, definitive *chóngbiān*, post-1668) together constitute the complete extant Tiěbì *yǔlù* — the B240 covering only the opening 1639–42 window of the Jùyún seat, B241 the full thirty-two-year preaching career.
- **Same compiler, same preface-circle.** Dēnglái's four consecutive Jiāxīng Canon entries [[KR6q0448]] / [[KR6q0449]] / [[KR6q0450]] / [[KR6q0451]] (J29 B238–B241) form a single publishing campaign of the Jùyúnpài lineage: grandfather (Guǎngzhēn, two volumes) + father (Tiěbì Huìjī, two volumes) compiled by the grandson (Dēnglái), with Xióng Rǔxué as the common lay-patron preface-writer across all four.
