# Qí-rán Zhì chán-shī yǔ-lù 奇然智禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Qí-rán Zhì*
by [[超智]] (說), [[明林]] (等編), [[明燦]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Tiān-mù / Qí-rán Chāo-zhì** [[超智]] 天目超智 (b. **1626**, alive in 1685), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zì-xián Xíng-jué** [[覺]] (1611–1662) in the Lín-yě → Zì-xián line. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xiù-yě Míng-lín** [[明林]] + **Míng-càn** [[明燦]] *děng biān*. Three abbacies: **Yǒng-jiā Tiān-wáng**, **Ruì-ān Xiān-yán**, **Kūn-yáng Bǎo-xīng**. Catalog meta uses the *Qí-rán* 奇然 *hào* alongside the more common *Tiān-mù* 天目 attestation. *notBefore* = c. 1660, *notAfter* = c. 1685. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B356.
