# Chún-bèi Dé chán-shī yǔ-lù 純備德禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chún-bèi Dé*
by [[廣德]] (說), [[智遠]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Chún-bèi Guǎng-dé** [[廣德]] 純備廣德 (lifedates unknown, active **1666–1672+**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěr-dú Dào-tài** 耳毒道泰 — thus a co-disciple of **Huàn-zhù Míng** [[明|Huàn-zhù Míng]] (cf. [[KR6q0549]]). Native of **Shǔ-dōng Fēng-dū** 蜀東鄷都 (= 酆都, Sì-chuān), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn** [[智遠]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at two abbacies on the upper-Yangtze gorge corridor: **Yí-líng-zhōu Huáng-líng-shān Yuè-xiá chán-yuàn** 彝陵州黃陵月峽禪院 (entered **康熙丙午** = **1666** spring) and **Sì-guī Fó-xīng Dà-bēi chán-yuàn** 秭歸佛興大悲禪院 (founded by Guǎng-dé in **康熙壬子** = **1672**). Preface dated **康熙庚戌三月上巳** = **1670-04-21** by **Gǔ-tíng Dào-lóng Bó-dá** 古庭道隆博達 at the Qīng-lín plum-pavilion. *notBefore* = **1666**; *notAfter* ≈ **1672+**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B433.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *Huáng-líng Yuè-xiá-yuàn yǔ-lù*; *Dà-bēi-yuàn yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*; *fǎ-yǔ* (示方勝, 示乾齡王居士, 示歸源宋居士, 示弘玄道人, 示融一監院, 示疑悟禪人). Juan 2: continuing *fǎ-yǔ* and *jì*; *jī-yuán*; *sòng-gǔ*; **聯芳** (lineage-distribution) listing the three *fǎ-sì*; *fó-shì*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔ-lù* together with [[KR6q0551]] (the *yǔ-lù* of his disciple **Fǎ-chuáng Zhì-yuǎn**) documents the late-Kāngxī Línjì-Yángqí presence on the **Yí-líng** 彝陵 / **Sì-guī** 秭歸 stretch of the Yangtze gorges — a corridor of strategic monastic geography that received heavy state patronage as part of post-Sānfān-luàn reconstruction. Guǎng-dé's founding of **Fó-xīng Dà-bēi chán-yuàn** at Sì-guī in 1672 is a primary record of that effort.
