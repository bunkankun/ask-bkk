# Wéi-shān Gǔ-méi Liè chán-shī yǔ-lù 溈山古梅冽禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Gǔ-méi Liè of Wéi-shān*
by [[定冽]] (說), [[真謙]] (記錄)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Gǔ-méi Dìng-liè** [[定冽]] 古梅定冽 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Huì-shān Zhì-hǎi** 慧山智海. Native of **Shào-líng** 邵陵 (Húnán), lay surname **Wáng** 王. Recorded by his attendant **Zhēn-qiān** [[真謙]] *jì-lù*. Documents teaching at two Húnán abbacies: **Tán-zhōu Dà-Wéi-shān Mì-yìn chán-sì** 潭州大溈山密印禪寺 (entered **康熙己酉九月廿一日** = **1669-10-15**) and **Tán-zhōu Fú-róng-shān Guǎng-huà chán-sì** 潭州芙蓉山廣化禪寺. *notBefore* = **1669** (Mì-yìn-sì entry); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-career printing). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B463.

**Contents.** Juan 1: *Mì-yìn-sì yǔ-lù*; *Guǎng-huà-sì yǔ-lù*; *jǔ-gǔ*. Juan 2: *Mì-yìn-sì shǒu-zuò-liáo bǐng-fú yǔ-lù* 首座寮秉拂語錄 (head-monk hall whisk-holding records); *Guǎng-huà-sì shǒu-zuò-liáo bǐng-fú yǔ-lù*.

## Other points of interest

The **Mì-yìn-sì** 密印寺 on **Wéi-shān** is the historic mother-temple of the **Wéi-yǎng** 溈仰 school of Chán, founded under **Lìng-yòu** 靈祐 (771–853) and **Huì-jì** 慧寂 (807–883). By the late Kāngxī era it had been continuously occupied by Línjì-Yángqí abbots — Dìng-liè's 1669 succession is one such instance. The juan-2 *shǒu-zuò-liáo bǐng-fú yǔ-lù* (head-monk-hall whisk-holding records) is unusually preserved at length, providing a rare glimpse of Wéi-shān's internal pedagogical structure under post-Pò-shān Línjì administration.
