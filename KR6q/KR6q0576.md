# Wéishān Gǔméi Liè chánshī yǔlù 溈山古梅冽禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Gǔ-méi Liè of Wéi-shān*
by [[定冽]] (說), [[真謙]] (記錄)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Gǔméi Dìngliè** [[定冽]] 古梅定冽 (lifedates unknown), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Huìshān Zhìhǎi** 慧山智海. Native of **Shàolíng** 邵陵 (Húnán), lay surname **Wáng** 王. Recorded by his attendant **Zhēnqiān** [[真謙]] *jìlù*. Documents teaching at two Húnán abbacies: **Tánzhōu DàWéishān Mìyìn chánsì** 潭州大溈山密印禪寺 (entered **康熙己酉九月廿一日** = **1669-10-15**) and **Tánzhōu Fúróngshān Guǎnghuà chánsì** 潭州芙蓉山廣化禪寺. *notBefore* = **1669** (Mìyìnsì entry); *notAfter* ≈ **1700** (post-career printing). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B463.

**Contents.** Juan 1: *Mìyìnsì yǔlù*; *Guǎnghuàsì yǔlù*; *jǔgǔ*. Juan 2: *Mìyìnsì shǒuzuòliáo bǐngfú yǔlù* 首座寮秉拂語錄 (head-monk hall whisk-holding records); *Guǎnghuàsì shǒuzuòliáo bǐngfú yǔlù*.

## Other points of interest

The **Mìyìnsì** 密印寺 on **Wéishān** is the historic mother-temple of the **Wéiyǎng** 溈仰 school of Chán, founded under **Lìngyòu** 靈祐 (771–853) and **Huìjì** 慧寂 (807–883). By the late Kāngxī era it had been continuously occupied by LínjìYángqí abbots — Dìngliè's 1669 succession is one such instance. The juan-2 *shǒuzuòliáo bǐngfú yǔlù* (head-monk-hall whisk-holding records) is unusually preserved at length, providing a rare glimpse of Wéishān's internal pedagogical structure under post-Pò-shān Línjì administration.
