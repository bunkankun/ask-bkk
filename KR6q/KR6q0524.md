# Pǔ-fēng Cháng-míng Jiǒng chán-shī yǔ-lù 浦峰長明炅禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cháng-míng Jiǒng of Pǔ-fēng*
by [[性炅]] (說), [[海棟]] (編)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Cháng-míng Xìng-jiǒng** [[性炅]] 長明性炅 (?–**1668**), Línjì-Yángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Yǐng-xiù Zhēn-wù** 穎秀真悟 (Yǒu-yáng Lóng-shān Hǔ-xī-táng). Native of **Shǔ-dōng Fú-líng** 蜀東涪陵 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Shí** 石. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fǎ-zhù Hǎi-dòng** [[海棟]] *biān*. Held four abbacies: **Chén-zhōu Lóng-shān** 辰州龍山, **Chén-zhōu Pǔ-fēng** 辰州浦峰 (entered **康熙丁未 9/1** = **1667-10-17**), **Lǐ-zhōu Qī-xīng-shān Pú-tí chán-yuàn** 灃州七星山菩提禪院, and **Yuán-zhōu Míng-shān Tài-hé chán-yuàn** 沅州明山太和禪院. *notBefore* ≈ **1665** (earliest abbacies prior to Pǔ-fēng), *notAfter* = **1668** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B401. Contents: *shàng-táng*, *shì-zhòng*, *sòng-gǔ*, *mù-niú-sòng* (ten oxherding verses), *sì-wēi-yí*, *fǎ-pài*, *lián-fāng*, *fǎ-yǔ*, *shì-jì*, *zá-jì*, *fó-shì*, *tǎ-míng* (stūpa-inscription).
