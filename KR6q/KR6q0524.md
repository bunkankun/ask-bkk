# Pǔfēng Chángmíng Jiǒng chánshī yǔlù 浦峰長明炅禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cháng-míng Jiǒng of Pǔ-fēng*
by [[性炅]] (說), [[海棟]] (編)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Chángmíng Xìngjiǒng** [[性炅]] 長明性炅 (?–**1668**), LínjìYángqí 33rd-generation dharma-heir of **Yǐngxiù Zhēnwù** 穎秀真悟 (Yǒuyáng Lóngshān Hǔxītáng). Native of **Shǔdōng Fúlíng** 蜀東涪陵 (Sìchuān), lay surname **Shí** 石. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Fǎzhù Hǎidòng** [[海棟]] *biān*. Held four abbacies: **Chénzhōu Lóngshān** 辰州龍山, **Chénzhōu Pǔfēng** 辰州浦峰 (entered **康熙丁未 9/1** = **1667-10-17**), **Lǐzhōu Qīxīngshān Pútí chányuàn** 灃州七星山菩提禪院, and **Yuánzhōu Míngshān Tàihé chányuàn** 沅州明山太和禪院. *notBefore* ≈ **1665** (earliest abbacies prior to Pǔfēng), *notAfter* = **1668** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B401. Contents: *shàngtáng*, *shìzhòng*, *sònggǔ*, *mùniúsòng* (ten oxherding verses), *sìwēiyí*, *fǎpài*, *liánfāng*, *fǎyǔ*, *shìjì*, *zájì*, *fóshì*, *tǎmíng* (stūpa-inscription).
