# Cǎo-táng Nòu-yún Shí chán-shī yǔ-lù 草堂耨雲實禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Cǎo-táng Nòu-yún Shí*
by [[真實]] (說), [[寂訥]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Nòu-yún Zhēn-shí** [[真實]] 耨雲真實 (b. **1626-07-08**, alive 1686), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Zhàng-xuě Tōng-zuì** [[通醉]] 丈雪通醉 (1610–1696). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Fó-mǐn Jì-nè** [[寂訥]] *děng biān*. Continued at the **Cǎo-táng** 草堂 succession-seat in Sì-chuān (the same Cǎo-táng that figures in Tōng-zuì's six-abbacy career — see [[通醉]] note). *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1686** (master's age 61, alive at compilation). Juan 2 *xíng-yóu* + *fēn-dēng*. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B370.
