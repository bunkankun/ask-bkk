# Xiānjué jí 先覺集

compiled by [[陶明潛]] (Táo Míngqián), late Míng

## About the work

A one-juan late-Míng anthology of brief biographical notices and enlightenment-occasion records for laypeople of recognised Chán attainment — the "Collected [Records of the] Formerly-Awakened." Compiled by the lay editor Táo Míngqián.

### Tiyao


### Abstract

A compact lay-practitioner anthology in the same genre as Zhū Shí'ēn's *Jūshì fēndēng lù* ([[KR6q0039]]), selecting exemplary lay Chán figures from earlier lamp records and recorded-sayings literature and presenting them in condensed form for the late-Míng lay-Buddhist reading market.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Mentioned within scholarship on late-Míng lay Buddhism; no dedicated monograph.

## Other points of interest

Part of the broader late-Míng publishing phenomenon of compact lay-Chán readers, for which Qú Rǔjī's *Zhǐyuè lù* ([[KR6q0023]]) was the paradigm and chief market success.
