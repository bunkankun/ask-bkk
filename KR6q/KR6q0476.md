# Xiè-yún Jī chán-shī Guó-qīng Wú-wèi-táng yǔ-lù 燮雲璣禪師國清無畏堂語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiè-yún Jī at the Wú-wèi-táng of Guó-qīng-sì*
by [[濟璣]] (說), [[玄杲]] (錄), [[張有譽]] (序)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xiè-yún Jì-jī** [[濟璣]] 燮雲濟璣 (d. **1648**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng** sub-line. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Xuán-gǎo** [[玄杲]] *lù* at the **Wú-wèi-táng** 無畏堂 of **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** 天台國清寺 — the historic Tiāntái-school flagship monastery, an unusual seat for a Línjì abbot. Front-preface by **Zhāng Yǒu-yù** [[張有譽]] *Dà-yuán jū-shì* 大圓居士 (1589–1669, the same Wú-xī lay-Chán official documented as preface-writer for [[KR6q0204]] under Hóng-chǔ's teaching). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B303.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per DILA A015946 and 《五燈全書》 j. 84: native of **Tōng-zhōu** 通州. Initial three-year training under **Sān-fēng Fǎ-zàng** [[法藏]]; after Fǎ-zàng's 1635 death continued under heir **Líng-yán Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] at **Xiáng-fú-sì** 祥符寺. Decisive *chè-zhèng* 徹證 on the *Mìao-xǐ qǐng-yì Fó-guǒ yǒu-jù wú-jù* 妙喜請益佛果有句無句 *gōng-àn*. Abbot at Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì where he died (1648) and was interred in stūpa.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1640** (post-transmission abbacy at Guó-qīng). *notAfter* = **1648** (death). Single-juan *yǔ-lù* reflects the relatively brief Guó-qīng abbacy.

**Línjì at Guó-qīng.** Jì-jī's installation as Línjì-line abbot of the **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** is institutionally significant — the historic seat of **Zhì-yǐ** 智顗 and Tiāntái-school doctrinal authority, but here held by a Línjì-Yángqí Sān-fēng-line monastic. The 無畏堂 *Wú-wèi-táng* hall-name suggests a specific preaching-hall within the larger Guó-qīng compound.

**Lineage.** Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng → Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ → Xiè-yún Jì-jī. The Sān-fēng sub-line was suppressed by Yōng-zhèng's 1733 proscription, but this 1648 *yǔ-lù* survived in the Jiā-xīng Canon imprint.

## Other points of interest

- **Línjì abbacy of Tiāntái flagship.** Jì-jī's seat at Guó-qīng-sì represents an instance of cross-tradition institutional capture — a Línjì-Yángqí Sān-fēng-line abbot at the Suí-era foundation of Chinese Tiāntái-school doctrinal practice. Such cross-tradition abbacies were common in the late-Míng / early-Qīng but particularly pointed at the Tiāntái flagship.
