# Wàn-fēng Tóng-zhēn chán-shī yǔ-lù 萬峰童真禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Tóng-zhēn of Wàn-fēng*
by [[至善]] (說), [[德林]] (記)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* (DILA: 5 juan) of **Wàn-fēng Tóng-zhēn Zhì-shàn** [[至善]] 萬峰童真至善 (lifedates unknown, active **1654–1678+**), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1608–1663) — thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]] (cf. [[KR6q0554]]) and [[玄億]] (cf. [[KR6q0555]]) in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line. Native of **Yú-zhōu Jiāng-jīn** 渝州江津 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Jiāng** 江. Recorded by his attendant **Dé-lín** [[德林]] *jì*.

The *yǔ-lù* documents two principal Húběi abbacies: **Hú-guǎng Dé-ān-fǔ Xiào-gǎn-xiàn Jiǎn-táng chán-yuàn** 湖廣德安府孝感縣簡堂禪院 (Hú-běi); and **Dé-ān-fǔ Xiào-gǎn-xiàn Gāo-fēng chán-yuàn** 德安府孝感縣高峰禪院 (formal entry **康熙十七年戊午閏三月十二日** = **1678**). Earlier (**康熙七年** = **1668**) at **È-zhōu Dà-bié-shān Wò-yún-ān** 鄂州大別山臥雲庵 (Wǔ-Hàn region) — the same site contemporaneously held by [[玄億]]. *notBefore* = **1666** (康熙丙午, opening of *fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1678** (Gāo-fēng-yuàn entry; printing follows). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B441.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces (by **Lǐ Dào-jì** 李道濟 of Jí-zhōu dated **康熙丁未孟夏** = **1667** 4th lunar month, signed 屏山李道濟龍孫); *mù-cì*; *Jiǎn-táng yǔ-lù*; *Gāo-fēng yǔ-lù*; *fǎ-yǔ* (示雙林居士, 送卓雲上座參方, 示陳玉甫居士, 示彭君甫居士六十法語, 示程聖裔居士). Juan 2: *zhēn-zàn* (image-encomia for 出山文殊大士, 須菩提, 初祖, **趙松雪十六羅漢圖**, 藥王大士, **聚雲老和尚**, etc.). Juan 3–6: continuing materials.

## Other points of interest

[[KR6q0554]], [[KR6q0555]], and KR6q0556 form a triple set of *yǔ-lù* by three dharma-brothers under **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** in the Sì-chuān **Jù-yún** sub-line, all of whom moved east from Sì-chuān to Húběi during or after the post-Sānfān-luàn (after 1681) reconstruction era. The 趙松雪十六羅漢圖 *zhēn-zàn* in juan 2 is a notable instance of late-Yuán painter **Zhào Mèng-fǔ** 趙孟頫's famous *Sixteen Arhats* iconography being incorporated into mid-Qīng monastic image-encomia practice — Zhì-shàn must have had access to a copy of the original at one of his Húběi monasteries.
