# Xuě-jiāo Xìn chán-shī yǔ-lù 雪嶠信禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xuě-jiāo Xìn*
by [[弘歇]] (等編)

Ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xuě-jiāo Yuán-xìn** [[圓信]] 雪嶠圓信 (**1571 – 1647-09-24**, age 77). One of the **three principal *fǎ-sì*** of **Lóng-chí Huàn-yǒu Zhèng-chuán** [[正傳]] (cf. [[KR6q0601]]) — alongside [[圓悟]] **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** and [[圓修]] **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū**. *Bié-hào* **Yǔ-fēng lǎo-rén** 語風老人, **Qīng-shī-wēng** 青獅翁 ("Old Man of the Blue Lion"), **Xuě-shī-zǐ** 雪獅子 ("Snow Lion"), lay name **Zhū** 朱 (i.e. **Zhū Xuě-tíng** 朱雪庭). Native of **Yín-xiàn** 鄞縣 (Níng-bō, Zhèjiāng). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Hóng-xiē** [[弘歇]] (with **Hóng-zhū** 弘珠) *děng biān*.

The *yǔ-lù* documents Yuán-xìn's three principal abbacies and his late-life teaching. *notBefore* ≈ **1610** (post-mature-master period under Huàn-yǒu); *notAfter* = **1647** (death). Catalog source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** of the canon Vol. 153 No. 1638.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface (by **Yú Yī** 魚一); *zhòng hù-fǎ qǐng kāi-táng shū* 眾護法請開堂疏; *mù-lù*; *Dōng-tǎ chán-sì yǔ-lù* 住東塔禪寺語錄; *Yuè-zhōu Yún-mén-sì yǔ-lù* 越州雲門寺語錄. Juan 2: *Kāi-xiān chán-sì yǔ-lù*; *pǔ-shuō*. Juan 3: *xiǎo-cān*; *wǎn-cān*; *chá-huà*; *shì-zhòng*; *kāi-shì*. Juan 4: *fǎ-yǔ* (示錢相國塞菴居士, 示錢元冲居士, etc.). Juan 5–10: continuing materials, *jì-zàn*, *fó-shì*, *xíng-shí*. The frequent *yīn-shì* 音釋 (phonetic glosses) at chapter ends indicates careful textual treatment for canonical inclusion.

## Other points of interest

Xuě-jiāo Yuán-xìn is one of the most distinctive Línjì-Yángqí Chán figures of the late Míng — known for his unconventional, eccentric self-presentation (the **Xuě-shī-zǐ** "Snow Lion" persona), his *Qīng-shī-wēng* "Blue Lion Old Man" sobriquet, and his prominent role as teacher of **Tuì-ān Duàn-yú Zhì** [[智|Tuì-ān Duàn-yú Zhì]] (cf. [[KR6q0455]]). His Yún-mén-sì abbacy at Shào-xìng (越州) places him at one of the most prestigious Chán mountain-temples; his Kāi-xiān-sì 開先 abbacy at Lú-shān adds Jiāng-xī to his circulation. Together with [[KR6q0601]] (his teacher Huàn-yǒu) and [[KR6q0603]] / [[KR6q0604]] (his co-disciples Tiān-yǐn / Mì-yún), KR6q0602 forms the core textual evidence for the late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí lineage-restoration.
