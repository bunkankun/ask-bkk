# Guǎng-fú-shān Shèng-jué-sì Mì-yìn chán-shī yǔ-lù 廣福山勝覺寺密印禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mì-yìn at Shèng-jué-sì of Guǎng-fú-shān*
by [[真傳]] (說), [[如暐]] (等編)

## About the work

Twelve-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Mì-yìn Zhēn-chuán** [[真傳]] 密印真傳 (b. **1625-07-31**, alive in 1678 at age 54), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-grandson of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] via **Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén** 靈隱印文. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xiào-fēng Rú-wěi** [[如暐]] *děng biān*. Front-preface by **Zhuāng Lín** 莊鏻 (*jìn-shì*, *Hé-nán àn-chá-sī fù-shǐ*). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B343. Twelve-juan structure: *shàng-táng* (j. 1–3); *xiǎo-cān* (j. 4); *kāi-shì* (j. 5–6); *fǎ-yǔ* (j. 7); subsequent juan with various genres + **j. 12 *tǎ-míng bēi-jì***.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[真傳]] note: native of **Xù-zhōu-fǔ** 敘州府 (Sì-chuān-Nán), lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Tonsured at Guān-yīn-dòng after parents' deaths; full precepts under **Yǔ-sōng** 語嵩; transmission from **Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén**. Three abbacies: **Hú-guǎng Huì-tóng Huí-lóng** 湖廣會同迴龍 → **Qí-shān Yǒng-xīng** 旗山永興 → **Qián-yáng Guǎng-fú-shān Shèng-jué-sì** 黔陽廣福山勝覺寺 (the 30+ year long-tenure seat).

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1655** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = c. **1685** as a conservative bound. Alive in 1678 per the *tǎ-míng* dating; the volume reflects the 1660s–1680s preaching career.

**Lineage.** Pò-shān Hǎi-míng → Líng-yǐn Yìn-wén → Mì-yìn Zhēn-chuán — the Pòshān-line via the Yún-nán / Guì-zhōu mission-circuit (Yìn-wén had been active at **Yún-nán Yún-jí** 雲南雲集 and **Hóng-jiāng Huí-lóng** 洪江迴龍 abbacies). Mì-yìn's 30+ year tenure at Guǎng-fú-shān in **Qián-yáng** (Hú-nán-Guì-zhōu border) preserves an extended Pòshān-line frontier ministry distinct from the central Sì-chuān cluster.

**Lay-patron preface.** **Zhuāng Lín** 莊鏻's preface frames the volume as a deliberate Confucian-Chán bridge (*míng xīn jiàn xìng sān-jiào tóng-yuán* 明心見性三教同原), invoking the *liǎng Lùn / liǎng Mèng* analogy and explicitly connecting Mì-yìn's preaching to Pò-shān's broader corpus.

## Other points of interest

- **30-year Guǎng-fú-shān ministry.** Among the longest documented Pòshān-line individual abbacies, with corresponding documentation depth. The Qián-yáng border-region site puts the ministry at the political-geographical periphery between Hú-nán, Guì-zhōu, and Sì-chuān-Nán.
