# Zǔ-liàng Qǐ chán-shī yǔ-lù 祖亮啟禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zǔ-liàng Qǐ*
by [[超啟]] (說), [[廣和]] (記錄)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Zǔ-liàng Chāo-qǐ** [[超啟]] 祖亮超啟 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí monk in the **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容 line — successor at his teacher **Zī-fú Líng-jī Xíng-guān** [[行觀]] (1616–1681)'s principal abbacy at **Hú-zhōu Xiáng-fèng Zī-fú-sì** 翔鳳資福寺 (cf. [[KR6q0562]]). Recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Guǎng-hé** [[廣和]] *jì-lù*. Preface dated **康熙四十年孟夏蓂生莢之四日** = **1701** lunar 4 (Kāngxī 40) by 普明法弟 (a co-disciple).

Documents teaching at three lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Hú-zhōu Xiáng-fèng-shān Zī-fú chán-sì** 湖州翔鳳資福禪寺 (entered **康熙庚午二月十五日** = **1690-03-25** — nine years after his teacher's 1681 death); **Háng-zhōu Wū-shān Jìng-míng chán-sì** 杭州㞾山淨明禪寺 (entered **康熙三十二年三月初八日** = **1693-04-12**); **Jiā-xīng Shí-mén-xiàn Fú-yán chán-sì** 嘉興石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙三十三年九月二十一日** = **1694-11-08**). *notBefore* = **1690** (Zī-fú entry); *notAfter* = **1701** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B449.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *Zī-fú-sì yǔ-lù*; *Jìng-míng-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Fú-yán-sì yǔ-lù*; *duì-jī* (encounter-dialogues); *tí-zàn* (image-encomia for the First Patriarch, his teacher Qīng-shān, Lì-chán-hé-shàng, his own self-portrait, lay donor *xíng-lè-tú* 行樂圖, etc.); *fó-shì* including *shè xiān lǎo-hé-shàng tǎ-gōng* 設先老和尚塔供 (a stupa-offering to his late teacher).

## Other points of interest

This *yǔ-lù* completes a teacher-disciple-grand-disciple sequence within the Fèi-yǐn-line abbacy succession at the **Xiáng-fèng-shān Zī-fú-sì** ↔ **Shí-mén Fú-yán-sì** complex: [[行觀]] (1616–1681, [[KR6q0562]]) → 超啟 (entered Zī-fú-sì 1690, this text) → ... The 1701 preface by 普明法弟 places the publication exactly at the master's late-career consolidation; both abbacies (Zī-fú and Fú-yán) had originally been built up by 行觀 and were inherited by his Mì-yún-line successors.
