# Zǔliàng Qǐ chánshī yǔlù 祖亮啟禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zǔ-liàng Qǐ*
by [[超啟]] (說), [[廣和]] (記錄)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Zǔliàng Chāoqǐ** [[超啟]] 祖亮超啟 (lifedates unknown), LínjìYángqí monk in the **Fèiyǐn Tōngróng** 費隱通容 line — successor at his teacher **Zīfú Língjī Xíngguān** [[行觀]] (1616–1681)'s principal abbacy at **Húzhōu Xiángfèng Zīfúsì** 翔鳳資福寺 (cf. [[KR6q0562]]). Recorded by his *fǎsì* **Guǎnghé** [[廣和]] *jìlù*. Preface dated **康熙四十年孟夏蓂生莢之四日** = **1701** lunar 4 (Kāngxī 40) by 普明法弟 (a co-disciple).

Documents teaching at three lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Húzhōu Xiángfèngshān Zīfú chánsì** 湖州翔鳳資福禪寺 (entered **康熙庚午二月十五日** = **1690-03-25** — nine years after his teacher's 1681 death); **Hángzhōu Wūshān Jìngmíng chánsì** 杭州㞾山淨明禪寺 (entered **康熙三十二年三月初八日** = **1693-04-12**); **Jiāxīng Shíménxiàn Fúyán chánsì** 嘉興石門縣福嚴禪寺 (entered **康熙三十三年九月二十一日** = **1694-11-08**). *notBefore* = **1690** (Zīfú entry); *notAfter* = **1701** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B449.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *Zīfúsì yǔlù*; *Jìngmíngsì yǔlù*. Juan 2: *Fúyánsì yǔlù*; *duìjī* (encounter-dialogues); *tízàn* (image-encomia for the First Patriarch, his teacher Qīngshān, Lìchánhéshàng, his own self-portrait, lay donor *xínglètú* 行樂圖, etc.); *fóshì* including *shè xiān lǎohéshàng tǎgōng* 設先老和尚塔供 (a stupa-offering to his late teacher).

## Other points of interest

This *yǔlù* completes a teacher-disciple-grand-disciple sequence within the Fèi-yǐn-line abbacy succession at the **Xiángfèngshān Zīfúsì** ↔ **Shímén Fúyánsì** complex: [[行觀]] (1616–1681, [[KR6q0562]]) → 超啟 (entered Zīfúsì 1690, this text) → ... The 1701 preface by 普明法弟 places the publication exactly at the master's late-career consolidation; both abbacies (Zīfú and Fúyán) had originally been built up by 行觀 and were inherited by his Mì-yún-line successors.
