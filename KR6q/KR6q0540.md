# Hè-fēng chán-shī yǔ-lù 鶴峰禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hè-fēng*
by [[濟悟]] (說), [[上震]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hè-fēng Jì-wù** [[濟悟]] 鶴峰濟悟 (**1626-11-08 – 1687-10-16**, age 62, *jiè-là* 44), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Líng-yǐn Jù-dé Hóng-lǐ** 靈隱具德弘禮 (1600–1667). Native of **Wú-mén** 吳門 (Sūzhōu), lay surname **Fèi** 費. Compiled by his sole *fǎ-sì* **Shàng-zhèn** [[上震]] *děng biān*. The *yǔ-lù* documents his teaching across six successive lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Lóng-tán Fú-shàn** 龍潭福善 (the seat that gave him his sobriquet **Lóng-tán Hè-fēng**), **Gū-sū Bǎo-shù** 姑蘇寶樹, **Dāng-hú Zhú-yǐn** 當湖竺隱, **Tóng-xī Jì-zhào** 桐溪寂照, **Pú-shuǐ Fú-shàn** 濮水福善, and **Fú-shòu chán-lín** 福壽禪林 (Tóng-xiāng).

Three prefaces are preserved: by his co-disciple **Ān-shān tóng-mén-dì Jì-rì** 安山同門弟濟日 dated **康熙壬申皋月之望** = **1692-06-29** (Kāngxī 31, lunar 5/15); by 法弟 **Zhòng Hóng-dào** 仲弘道 dated **康熙壬申歲次十月** = **1692-11**; and by his *Líng-yǐn tóng-mén-dì* (co-disciple at Líng-yǐn) **Huì-lù** 慧輅 dated **康熙歲次乙亥季夏** = **1695** summer. *notBefore* ≈ **1670** (post-Hóng-lǐ-death abbacies began ca. 1668–1670); *notAfter* = **1695** (latest preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B421.

**Contents.** Juan 1: three prefaces, *mù-cì*, *shēng-zuò* (= *shàng-táng*), *xiǎo-cān*, *shì-zhòng*, *jī-yuán*, *niān-sòng*. Juan 2: *fó-shì* (a substantial collection of cremation/sealing-coffin/funerary verses for fellow monks and lay donors), *xíng-zhuàng*, and *tǎ-míng* (stupa-inscription) — the principal sources for his life.

### Tiyao

*Not applicable — Jiā-xīng-canon imprint (J38 B421).*

## Other points of interest

The *fó-shì* section in juan 2 is unusually rich — providing a full panorama of mid-Kāngxī-era Sūzhōu / Tóng-xiāng-area Línjì-Yángqí Buddhist mortuary practice across both clerical and lay sponsorship contexts.
