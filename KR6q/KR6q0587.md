# Jìng-tíng Zōng chán-shī yǔ-lù 逕庭宗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jìng-tíng Zōng*
by [[德宗]] (說), [[行謙]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Jìng-tíng Dé-zōng** [[德宗]] 逕庭德宗 (**1621-09-19 – 1684-10-15**, age 64, *fǎ-là* 35), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yí-shān Wò-zhōng Zhì-fú** 嶷山握中智符 (1625–1688). Native of **Líng-líng** 零陵 (Húnán), lay surname **Jiǎng** 蔣. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Wú-zhèng Xíng-qiān** [[行謙]] *děng biān*. *notBefore* = **1657-12-30** (*fù-zhǔ* on Buddha's enlightenment day); *notAfter* = **1684** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B475.

Documents an extraordinary fourteen+ Húnán abbacies — a remarkable circuit-record. Principal seats: **Qīng-xiāng Nán-yuán Hóng-shèng-sì** 清湘南源洪聖寺; **Yí-shān Zhù-shèng-sì** 嶷山祝聖寺; **Cáo-yuán Bǎo-jìng-sì** 曹源寶鏡寺; **Wò-yún-shān Zuì-shèng-sì** 臥雲山最勝寺. Plus smaller hermitages and chapels (Shòu-fó-ān, Xīng-lóng-ān, Bié-chén-ān, Bì-luó-ān, Pí-lú-ān, Lián-chí-shān).

**Contents.** Juan 1: *yuán-qǐ*; preface; *mù-cì*; abbacy *yǔ-lù* (in chronological order across the Húnán circuit). Juan 2: *xiǎo-cān* and continuing abbacy materials; closing *xíng-zhuàng* and **塔銘** (preserving birth and death dates).

## Other points of interest

The fourteen-plus abbacies in Húnán within a single career mark an exceptionally fluid pattern of monastic mobility, characteristic of mid-Qīng Húnán Línjì where monks often occupied multiple smaller seats simultaneously rather than committing to single life-long abbacies.
