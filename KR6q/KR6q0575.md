# Hé-yī Zì chán-shī yǔ-lù 何一自禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hé-yī Zì*
by [[超自]] (說), [[明修]] (等編), [[明惠]] (等編), [[明智]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hé-yī Chāo-zì** [[超自]] 何一超自 (lifedates unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Xìng-kōng Xíng-zhēn** [[行臻]] (1608–1678, cf. [[KR6q0574]]). Native of **Hé-nán Qǐ-xiàn** 河南杞縣, lay surname **Wáng** 王. Compiled jointly by three of his *fǎ-sì*: **Yuán-fēng Míng-xiū** [[明修]], **Kǒng-yì Míng-huì** [[明惠]], **Lǚ-ān Míng-zhì** [[明智]].

Three prefaces are preserved: by **Wáng Zé-hóng** 楚邾城王澤弘 (a Qīng official) in **Cháng-ān Sī-...** dated **康熙三十六年孟春** = **1697** lunar 1; by **Zhāng Kāi-zōng** 張開宗 dated **康熙丁丑夏** = **1697** summer; and by **Rǎn Jìn-zǔ** 牟陽冉覲祖 dated **康熙三十二年癸酉中秋** = **1693-09-25**. Documents teaching at two principal seats: **Běi-jīng Shùn-tiān-fǔ Wǎn-píng-xiàn Chóng-níng chán-yuàn** 北京順天府宛平縣崇寧禪院 (entered **康熙二十年** = **1681** — i.e. an abbacy in suburban Běijīng); and a later **京師** (capital) invitation **康熙三十七年戊寅** = **1698**. *notBefore* = **1681** (Chóng-níng entry); *notAfter* = **1698** (capital invitation; printing follows). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B462.

**Contents.** Two juan: prefaces; abbacy *yǔ-lù*; *xíng-shí* in juan 2 (the principal source for his life).

## Other points of interest

The Běi-jīng abbacy at Chóng-níng-yuàn under the Kāngxī court places this *yǔ-lù* in the imperial-monastic ambit of late-Kāngxī Beijing — a relatively rare orientation in the Jiā-xīng-canon Línjì-Yángqí cluster, which is dominated by lower-Yangtze and southwestern frontier centers. Together with [[KR6q0574]] (his teacher 行臻's earlier Sū-zhōu abbacy under Manchu *Zhèng-huáng-qí* banner patronage), the pair documents a sustained Manchu-state-elite Buddhist patronage axis spanning Sū-zhōu (1661+) → Běi-jīng (1681+) → Jīng-shī (1698) over four decades.
