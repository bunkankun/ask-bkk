# Héyī Zì chánshī yǔlù 何一自禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hé-yī Zì*
by [[超自]] (說), [[明修]] (等編), [[明惠]] (等編), [[明智]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔlù* of **Héyī Chāozì** [[超自]] 何一超自 (lifedates unknown), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Xìngkōng Xíngzhēn** [[行臻]] (1608–1678, cf. [[KR6q0574]]). Native of **Hénán Qǐxiàn** 河南杞縣, lay surname **Wáng** 王. Compiled jointly by three of his *fǎsì*: **Yuánfēng Míngxiū** [[明修]], **Kǒngyì Mínghuì** [[明惠]], **Lǚān Míngzhì** [[明智]].

Three prefaces are preserved: by **Wáng Zéhóng** 楚邾城王澤弘 (a Qīng official) in **Chángān Sī-...** dated **康熙三十六年孟春** = **1697** lunar 1; by **Zhāng Kāizōng** 張開宗 dated **康熙丁丑夏** = **1697** summer; and by **Rǎn Jìnzǔ** 牟陽冉覲祖 dated **康熙三十二年癸酉中秋** = **1693-09-25**. Documents teaching at two principal seats: **Běijīng Shùntiānfǔ Wǎnpíngxiàn Chóngníng chányuàn** 北京順天府宛平縣崇寧禪院 (entered **康熙二十年** = **1681** — i.e. an abbacy in suburban Běijīng); and a later **京師** (capital) invitation **康熙三十七年戊寅** = **1698**. *notBefore* = **1681** (Chóngníng entry); *notAfter* = **1698** (capital invitation; printing follows). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J39 B462.

**Contents.** Two juan: prefaces; abbacy *yǔlù*; *xíngshí* in juan 2 (the principal source for his life).

## Other points of interest

The Běijīng abbacy at Chóngníngyuàn under the Kāngxī court places this *yǔlù* in the imperial-monastic ambit of late-Kāngxī Beijing — a relatively rare orientation in the Jiā-xīng-canon LínjìYángqí cluster, which is dominated by lower-Yangtze and southwestern frontier centers. Together with [[KR6q0574]] (his teacher 行臻's earlier Sūzhōu abbacy under Manchu *Zhènghuángqí* banner patronage), the pair documents a sustained Manchu-state-elite Buddhist patronage axis spanning Sūzhōu (1661+) → Běijīng (1681+) → Jīngshī (1698) over four decades.
