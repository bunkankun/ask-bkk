# Tiān-níng Fǎ-zhōu Jì chánshī shèng yǔ 天寧法舟濟禪師剩語

*Remaining Words of Chán Master Fǎ-zhōu Jì of Tiān-níng* — a one-juan mid-Míng Chán *yǔlù* of [[道濟|Fǎ-zhōu Dào-jì]] 法舟道濟 (1487–1560), abbot of Tiān-níng-sì 天寧寺 in Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhè-jiāng). The title *shèng yǔ* 剩語 ("remaining words") is self-deprecating, suggesting what survived from the master's teaching. Compiled by his dharma-heir [[如淵|Rú-yuān]] 如淵 et al.

## About the work

A one-juan mid-Míng Chán teaching-collection, J40 B491. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

The text preserves Fǎ-zhōu Dào-jì's teachings from his productive Jiā-xīng Tiān-níng-sì abbacy — a distinguishing work documenting mid-Míng Línjì-Yángqí Chán practice in the period before the late-Míng revival associated with Mìyún Yuán-wù and his generation.

### Abstract

[[道濟|Fǎ-zhōu Dào-jì]] 法舟道濟 (DILA A001560, 1487 – 1560, age 74). *Hào* Fǎ-zhōu 法舟 ("Dharma-Boat"). Native of Jiā-xīng 嘉興 (Zhè-jiāng). Abbot of Tiān-níng-sì 天寧寺. Principal dharma-heir: Dōng-xī Fāng-zé 東谿方澤 (of Jīngyán-sì 精嚴寺).

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1520 (Dào-jì's mature productive period); *notAfter* 1560 (his death).
