# Hǎiyìn Zhāorú chánshī yǔlù 海印昭如禪師語錄

One-juan early-Yuán *yǔlù* of **Hǎiyìn Zhāorú** [[昭如]] 海印昭如, Yángqí-branch Línjì master, dharma-heir of [[祖欽]] Xuěyán Zǔqīn. *Xuzangjing* X70 n1398. Preface by Sūn Xùnshēn 曾巽申 (Hànlín Guóshǐyuàn biānxiū 翰林國史院編修) dated Yányòu 4.7 (August–September 1317) — the preface states that in Zhìdà *wùshēn* (1308) summer Zhāorú led a Yúlánpén offering for Xùnshēn's mother. Three abbacy records: Yuánzhōu Mùpíng Xīnghuà 木平興化, Línjiāng Ruìyúnshān Huìlì 瑞筠山慧力, and Ráozhōu Dōnghú Jiànsì 東湖薦[福] — followed by *sònggǔ*, *jìsòng*, *fózǔ zàn*, etc. Compiled by three dharma-heirs: [[行純]] Xíngchún, [[道彰]] Dàozhāng, and [[從心]] Cóngxīn. The preface compares Zhāorú's role to Zhàntáng Wénzhǔn 湛堂文準's at the close of the Northern Sòng — "my master today's Zhàntáng."
