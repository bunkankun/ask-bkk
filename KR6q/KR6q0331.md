# Hǎi-yìn Zhāo-rú chánshī yǔlù 海印昭如禪師語錄

One-juan early-Yuán *yǔlù* of **Hǎi-yìn Zhāo-rú** [[昭如]] 海印昭如, Yángqí-branch Línjì master, dharma-heir of [[祖欽]] Xuě-yán Zǔ-qīn. *Xuzangjing* X70 n1398. Preface by Sūn Xùn-shēn 曾巽申 (Hàn-lín Guóshǐ-yuàn biānxiū 翰林國史院編修) dated Yán-yòu 4.7 (August–September 1317) — the preface states that in Zhì-dà *wù-shēn* (1308) summer Zhāo-rú led a Yúlán-pén offering for Xùn-shēn's mother. Three abbacy records: Yuán-zhōu Mù-píng Xīng-huà 木平興化, Lín-jiāng Ruì-yún-shān Huì-lì 瑞筠山慧力, and Ráo-zhōu Dōng-hú Jiàn-sì 東湖薦[福] — followed by *sòng-gǔ*, *jì-sòng*, *fó-zǔ zàn*, etc. Compiled by three dharma-heirs: [[行純]] Xíng-chún, [[道彰]] Dào-zhāng, and [[從心]] Cóng-xīn. The preface compares Zhāo-rú's role to Zhàn-táng Wén-zhǔn 湛堂文準's at the close of the Northern Sòng — "my master today's Zhàn-táng."
