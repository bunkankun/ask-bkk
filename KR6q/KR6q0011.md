# Wǔdēng huìyuán mùlù 五燈會元目錄

compiled by [[普濟]] (Pǔjì, 1178–1253), separately catalogued two-juan table of contents to the *Wǔdēng huìyuán* ([[KR6q0012]])

## About the work

The two-juan table of contents to the 20-juan *Wǔdēng huìyuán* 五燈會元 ([[KR6q0012]]), the great Southern Sòng synthesis of the five preceding Chán lamp records. Separately transmitted through Japanese monastic channels and catalogued at X80 n. 1564 distinct from the main text at X80 n. 1565.

### Tiyao


### Abstract

A finding-aid for the parent work, giving lineage positions and entry headings across the 20 juan and the roughly 1,650 figures treated. As with comparable *mùlù* for earlier lamp records, its independent transmission reflects Japanese editorial practice rather than a distinct act of compilation.
