# Bǎo-jué Zǔ-xīn chánshī yǔlù 寶覺祖心禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Bǎo-jué Zǔ-xīn* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Huánglóng Zǔ-xīn** 黃龍祖心 (1025–1100), *shì* Bǎo-jué 寶覺, dharma-heir of Huánglóng Huì-nán; one of the major second-generation Huánglóng-sub-lineage masters. Constitutes the second of four *yǔlù* in the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* 黃龍四家錄. Recorded by attendant-disciple [[子和|Zǐ-hé]] 子和; re-edited by disciple [[仲介|Zhòng-jiè]] 仲介.

## About the work

A one-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1343. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. See [[KR6q0276]] for the preface to the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* series.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1090; *notAfter* c. 1150.
