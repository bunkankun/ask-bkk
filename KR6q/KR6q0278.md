# Bǎojué Zǔxīn chánshī yǔlù 寶覺祖心禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Bǎojué Zǔxīn* — a one-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Huánglóng Zǔxīn** 黃龍祖心 (1025–1100), *shì* Bǎojué 寶覺, dharma-heir of Huánglóng Huìnán; one of the major second-generation Huánglóng-sub-lineage masters. Constitutes the second of four *yǔlù* in the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* 黃龍四家錄. Recorded by attendant-disciple [[子和|Zǐhé]] 子和; re-edited by disciple [[仲介|Zhòngjiè]] 仲介.

## About the work

A one-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1343. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted. See [[KR6q0276]] for the preface to the *Huánglóng sì jiā lù* series.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1090; *notAfter* c. 1150.
