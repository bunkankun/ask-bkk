# Zhú-fēng Mǐn chán-shī yǔ-lù 竺峰敏禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Zhú-fēng Mǐn*
by [[幻敏]] (說), [[性鉅]] (等編), [[性湛]] (等編), [[性珩]] (等編), [[性濟]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* (+ 2 juan *hòu-lù*) of **Zhú-fēng Huàn-mǐn** [[幻敏]] 竺峰幻敏 (**1639-01-18 – 1707-04-14**, age 70, *sēng-là* 65 — exceptionally long, having been tonsured at age 10), Línjì 大慧 派 dharma-heir of **Qìng-zhōng Tiě-bì Huì-jī** 慶忠鐵壁慧機 (1603–1668) in the **Jù-yún** 聚雲 sub-line — thus a co-disciple of [[燈炳]] (KR6q0554), [[玄億]] (KR6q0555), and [[至善]] (KR6q0556). Native of **Fēng-líng** 酆陵 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Xú** 徐. Compiled jointly by four *fǎ-sì*: **Xìng-jù** [[性鉅]], **Xìng-zhàn** [[性湛]], **Xìng-héng** [[性珩]], **Xìng-jì** [[性濟]]. *notBefore* = **1668** (post-master-death succession at Qìng-zhōng); *notAfter* = **1707** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B483.

The *yǔ-lù* documents teaching at four abbacies, with **Sì-chuān Zhōng-zhōu Chì-jiàn Zhèn-zōng chán-sì** 四川忠州敕建振宗禪寺 (the **imperially-bestowed** Sì-chuān seat) as the principal preaching site (juan 1–2). Five *qǐng-qǐ* invitation-petitions are preserved at the head of juan 1, signed by **Yáng** prefect (郡侯), **Liú** sub-prefect (副府), and the prefectural gentry — establishing the imperial-state-supported character of the Zhèn-zōng abbacy.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface; *mù-lù*; five *qǐng-qǐ*; *Zhèn-zōng-sì shàng-táng*. Juan 2: continuing *Zhèn-zōng yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*. Juan 3: *shì-zhòng*; *chá-huà*; *jī-yuán*; *fó-shì* (with the funeral cycle for **Qìng-zhōng lǎo-rén** 慶忠老人 = Tiě-bì Huì-jī's **1668** death — *guà-zhōng*, *rù-guān*, *qǐ-kān*, *rù-tǎ*). Juan 4–6: continuing materials including the closing *xíng-zhuàng* and *fēn-dēng* listing thirty-three *fǎ-sì* and eighteen lay disciples.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0451 (the master Tiě-bì's 20-juan *yǔ-lù* re-edited by Huàn-mǐn) and KR6q0592 (Huàn-mǐn's own 6-juan *yǔ-lù*) together form the principal Línjì-Dà-huì **Jù-yún** 聚雲 / **Qìng-zhōng** lineage textual record. Huàn-mǐn's exceptionally long *sēng-là* (65 years, tonsured at age 10) places him among the most prolific southwestern Línjì lineage-builders of the Qīng era. The *Lèng-yán-sì* 楞嚴寺 abbacy at Jiā-xīng directly connects him to the Jiā-xīng Canon publication operation.
