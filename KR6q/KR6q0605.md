# Dà-jué Pǔ-jì Néng-rén Xiù Guó-shī yǔ-lù 大覺普濟能仁琇國師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of the State Preceptor Xiù, Greatly-Awakened, Universally Saving, Bestower of Benevolence*
by [[行岳]] (編)

Seven-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** [[通琇]] 玉林通琇 (**1614-05-28 – 1675-09-28**, age 62), the **Shùnzhì-emperor's preceptor** and most prominent Línjì-Yángqí *guó-shī* (state preceptor) of the early Qīng. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635, cf. [[KR6q0603]]) — one of the four founders of the **Qìng-shān** 磬山 sub-branch. Native of **Jiāng-yīn** 江陰. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Qī-yún Xíng-yuè** [[行岳]] (1614–1666) *biān*. The full title invokes Tōng-xiù's three successive imperial titles bestowed by Shùnzhì: **Dà-jué chán-shī** 大覺禪師, **Dà-jué Pǔ-jì chán-shī**, and **Dà-jué Pǔ-jì Néng-rén Guó-shī**.

*notBefore* ≈ **1640** (post-Yuán-xiū-1635-death, *fù-fǎ* and abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1675** (death). Catalog dynasty 清; source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** Vol. 154 No. 1641.

**Contents.** Juan 1: title-frame as **Dà-jué Pǔ-jì Néng-rén Yù-lín Xiù Guó-shī yǔ-lù** 大覺普濟能仁玉琳琇國師語錄 (with the imperially-bestowed name **Yù-lín 玉琳** in the imperial form rather than Yù-lín 玉林); *mù-lù* (introduced by **Jǐ Yī** 幾一); *shàng-táng* (one). Juan 2: *shàng-táng* (two); *shì-zhòng*. Juans 3–4: *xiǎo-cān* (one and two — extensive). Juan 5: *fǎ-yǔ* (instructions to disciples by name — including *Quán-ān Jìn shàng-zuò* 全菴進上座, *Dǐng shì-sī* 嵿侍司, *Yuè shū-zhuàng* 岳書狀 = the editor [[行岳]]). Juans 6–7: continuing materials.

## Other points of interest

The imperial title sequence is significant: the bestowal of *Guó-shī* status — preceptorship to the empire — is unique in J40 to **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** and his Tiān-tóng cousin **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** [[道忞]] (cf. [[KR6q0604]] / [[KR6q0607]]). Both were cultivated by the Shùnzhì emperor, in the early-Qīng court's strategic Buddhist legitimization. The phonetic glosses (*yīn-shì* 音釋) at the end of each abbacy section indicate careful textual treatment for canonical inclusion.
