# Zēngjí xù chuándēng lù 增集續傳燈錄

compiled by [[文琇]] (Wénxiù, sobriquet Nánshí 南石), Míng *Yǒnglè* 15 (1417)

## About the work

A six-juan supplemental continuation of the *Xù chuándēng lù* ([[KR6q0016]]), adding Chán masters of the late Yuán and early Míng that the anonymous *Xù chuándēng lù* had not covered or had covered only thinly. Compiled by Nánshí Wénxiù, a Línjì-line monk active in the early Míng.

### Tiyao

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

One of the early-fifteenth-century responses to the perceived inadequacy of the anonymous *Xù chuándēng lù*'s treatment of the most recent generations: Wénxiù supplements its coverage with fuller entries on the Yuán-to-early-Míng Línjì line, to which he himself belonged. Compact in scale compared to the *Wǔdēng huìyuán xùlüè* and *Wǔdēng yántǒng*, but earlier in date and preserved in the Jiāxīng 嘉興 canon.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Occasional citation in studies of early-Míng Chán; no dedicated monograph.

## Other points of interest

Wénxiù's work is a useful terminus for Yuán-Míng transition Chán genealogy, preceding the larger seventeenth-century continuations by nearly two centuries.
