# Huàn-zhù Míng chán-shī yǔ-lù 幻住明禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huàn-zhù Míng*
by [[明]] (說), [[清尚]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Huàn-zhù Míng** [[明|Huàn-zhù Míng]] 幻住明 (lifedates unknown, active **1660–1694**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Ěr-dú Dào-tài** 耳毒道泰 in the Pò-shān Hǎi-míng line. Native of **Lú-zhōu** 瀘州 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Zēng** 曾. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Qīng-shàng** [[清尚]] *děng biān*. Documents teaching at four Sì-chuān (Dōng-Chuān region) abbacies: **Lín-shuǐ-xiàn Xiān-rén-shān Guān-yīn chán-yuàn** 鄰水縣仙人山觀音禪院 (entered **順治庚子冬** = **1660** winter); **Dà-zhú-xiàn Wàn-fēng-shān Tài-píng chán-sì** 大竹縣萬峰山太平禪寺; **Wàn-xiàn Xīng-lóng chán-sì** 萬縣興隆禪寺; and **Shè-hóng Huì-líng-sì** 射洪會靈寺. Preface dated **康熙甲戌歲冬杪除日** = **1694-12** at **Fán-líng** 樊陵 (lay anchorite). *notBefore* = **1660**; *notAfter* = **1694**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B432.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface, *mù-cì*, three abbacy *yǔ-lù* (Guān-yīn 1660 winter; Tài-píng 1661 winter; Xīng-lóng 1663 winter and 1664 mid-summer). Juan 2: *xiǎo-cān*; *chá-huà*; *shì-zhòng*; *jī-yuán*; *sòng-gǔ* on classic *gōng-àn* (晦堂與黃山谷論吾無隱乎爾話, plus *sì-wēi-yí*); *lián-fāng-jì* (with the verse-bestowal poems for 天然 and 水映 *fǎ-sì*); *fǎ-yǔ*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔ-lù* extends the Pò-shān → Ěr-dú Dào-tài branch of the Sì-chuān Línjì-Yángqí lineage into a third generation; the four Dōng-Chuān (eastern Sì-chuān) abbacies — Lín-shuǐ, Dà-zhú, Wàn-xiàn, Shè-hóng — fill in important monastic geography otherwise less well-attested in the Sì-chuān-cluster Jiā-xīng-canon entries.
