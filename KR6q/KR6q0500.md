# Wén-mù Niàn chán-shī yǔ-lù 文穆念禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Wén-mù Niàn*
by [[念]] (說), [[真慧]] (等編)

Five-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Wén-mù Niàn** [[念]] 文穆念 (**1633-07-28 – 1678-01-05**, *shì-shòu* 45), Línjì-line dharma-heir of **Lǎn-fàng Zhì-dà** 嬾放智大 of the **Qí-shān Rén-ruì-sì** 歧山仁瑞寺 line — a Míng-loyalist cross-lineage tradition with origins in the post-1644 refusal-to-surrender community. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhēn-huì** [[真慧]] *děng biān*. Three abbacies: **Fǎ-chuáng Shuāng-lín**, **Dān-xiá Xiān-xiá**, **Fú-niú Yún-yán**. Juan 5 *xíng-shí* dated post-1678. *notBefore* = c. 1660 (post-1660 pilgrimage and abbacy entries), *notAfter* = 1678 (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B363.
