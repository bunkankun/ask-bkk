# Shàn-quán Wèi chán-shī yǔ-lù 善權位禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shàn-quán Wèi*
by [[達位]] (說), [[大悅]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Shàn-quán Dá-wèi** [[達位]] 善權達位 (**1619-10-29 – 1684-10-01**, age 67, *sēng-là* 45), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo** 月幢徹了 (1614–1667). Native of **Chǔ-běi** 楚北 (Húběi), lay surname **Qú** 瞿; took refuge in Guì-zhōu during the dynastic chaos. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Tiān-yī Dà-yuè** [[大悅]] *děng biān*.

Documents teaching at three Guì-zhōu region abbacies: **Tài-píng Wàn-shòu-sì** 太平萬壽寺; **Sōng-kuī-shān Pǔ-guāng-sì** 松巋普光寺 (the principal seat — gives him his sobriquet **Sōng-kuī Shàn-quán**); **Cí-yún Pǔ-xiáng-sì** 慈雲普祥寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1660** (post-付法 abbacy onset); *notAfter* = **1684** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B469.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; abbacy *yǔ-lù* (Sōng-kuī, Tài-píng, Cí-yún); *xiǎo-cān*; *fǎ-yǔ*. Juan 2: *jī-yuán*; *niān-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*; closing **塔銘**, **行實**, **分燈** (lineage-distribution listing his ten *fǎ-sì* and five lay disciples).

## Other points of interest

KR6q0581–KR6q0582 form a teacher-disciple sequence within the major **Yuè-chuáng Chè-liǎo** 月幢徹了 line of Línjì-Yángqí Guì-zhōu Buddhism — a separate Sì-chuān-Guì-zhōu sub-line from the Pò-shān-line clusters in J38–J39 (cf. [[KR6q0564]], [[KR6q0567]], etc.). Dá-wèi's disciple [[如純]] is the compiler of the major **《黔南會燈錄》** [[KR6q0036]], the principal Guì-zhōu Chán biographical anthology.
