# Sìjiā yǔlù (xù, yǐn, bá) 四家語錄（序．引．跋）

*Preface, Introduction, and Postface to the Four-House Sayings-Records* — a one-juan collection of paratextual materials (preface-introductions-postfaces) for the *Sìjiā yǔlù* 四家語錄 ("Four-House Sayings-Records"), written by [[鶴徵|Hèzhēng]] 鶴徵. The *Sìjiā yǔlù* is a Táng-era compilation of the *yǔlù* of four classical Chán masters; this text preserves only the front-and-back matter.

## About the work

A one-juan Táng collection of paratexts (prefaces, introductions, postfaces), X69 n1320. Non-commentary; *commentedTextid* omitted.

[[鶴徵|Hèzhēng]] 鶴徵: Táng figure; lifedates and full biographical details unrecorded.

Dating: broad Táng bracket (*notBefore* 800, *notAfter* 900).
