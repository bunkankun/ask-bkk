# Kāi-fú Dào-níng chánshī yǔlù 開福道寧禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Kāi-fú Dào-níng* — a two-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Kāi-fú Dào-níng** 開福道寧 (1053–1113), Línjì-Yángqí lineage master at Kāi-fú-sì 開福寺. Compiled by his dharma-heir *xiǎo shī* [[善果|Shàn-guǒ]] 善果.

Two-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1353. Dào-níng was a disciple of Wǔ-zǔ Fǎ-yǎn 五祖法演 in the Yángqí sub-lineage.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1090; *notAfter* c. 1150.
