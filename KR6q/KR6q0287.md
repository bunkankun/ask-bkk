# Kāifú Dàoníng chánshī yǔlù 開福道寧禪師語錄

*Sayings-Record of Chán Master Kāifú Dàoníng* — a two-juan Northern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Kāifú Dàoníng** 開福道寧 (1053–1113), LínjìYángqí lineage master at Kāifúsì 開福寺. Compiled by his dharma-heir *xiǎo shī* [[善果|Shànguǒ]] 善果.

Two-juan Northern-Sòng Chán *yǔlù*, X69 n1353. Dàoníng was a disciple of Wǔzǔ Fǎyǎn 五祖法演 in the Yángqí sub-lineage.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1090; *notAfter* c. 1150.
