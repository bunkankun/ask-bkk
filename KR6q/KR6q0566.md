# Lǚ-yán Hé chán-shī yǔ-lù 侶巖荷禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Lǚ-yán Hé*
by [[荷]] (說), [[成純]] (等記錄)

Seven-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Lǚ-yán Hé** [[荷]] 侶巖荷 (lifedates unknown, active **1675–1685**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-àn Běn-shēng** 天岸本昇 (1620–1673) in the **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** 密雲圓悟 → **Tiān-tóng** 天童 line. Recorded by his attendant **Chéng-chún** [[成純]] *děng jì-lù*. Two prefaces dated **康熙乙丑** = **1685**: by his dharma-cousin **Tiān-tóng-shān Xiǎo-xī Běn-xī** 天童山曉皙本晳 (lunar 9), and by lay disciple **Chén Liàng** 陳量 (Buddha's Birthday).

Documents teaching at three lower-Yangtze abbacies: **Hú-zhōu Wū-shān Lóng-quán-ān** 湖州烏山龍泉菴 (the seat giving him his sobriquet **Lóng-quán** 龍泉); **Tiáo-chéng Shī-zǐ-lín** 苕城師子林; **Ān-jí-zhōu Lóng-shān Dōng-chán-sì** 安吉州龍山東禪寺. *notBefore* = **1675** (post-Běn-shēng-death abbacy onset, ca. 1675); *notAfter* = **1685** (prefaces). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B452.

**Contents.** Juan 1: two prefaces; *Lóng-quán-ān yǔ-lù*; *Shī-zǐ-lín yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Dōng-chán-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *shì-zhōng jǔ-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*. Juan 4: *chuí-dài*. Juan 5: *jī-yuán*. Juan 6–7: *jì* (poetry — including *kū běn-shī hé-shàng* 哭本師和尚 mourning his teacher Běn-shēng, *shòu* birthday-verses for fellow monks 法雷, 燈 etc., consolation verses, *shì-tú* 示徒, *shì chán-zhě zhù-shān* 示禪者住山).
