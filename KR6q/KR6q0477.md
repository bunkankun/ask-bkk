# Guó-qīng Dà-yǔ Tāo chán-shī yǔ-lù 國清大庾韜禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dà-yǔ Tāo of Guó-qīng-sì*
by [[行韜]] (說), [[顯裕]] (等錄)

## About the work

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Dà-yǔ Xíng-tāo** [[行韜]] 大庾行韜 (d. **1652-08-28**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ** [[弘儲]] in the **Hàn-yuè Sān-fēng** sub-line and *zhí* 姪 (nephew) of **Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** [[法藏]]. Recorded by *shì-zhě* **Xiǎn-yù** [[顯裕]] *děng lù* at the **Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì** 天台國清寺. Includes the *yī-bō tǎ-míng* 衣缽塔銘 tomb-inscription. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J34 B305.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[行韜]] note: native of **Wú-jiāng** 吳江, lay surname **Zhào** 趙. Studied under his uncle **Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** in childhood — a rare family-line preliminary connection in the Sān-fēng tradition. **1646**: retreated to Wǔ-yí-shān. Full precepts under **Fú-shí Tōng-xián** at Wú-jiāng Bào-ēn-sì; final transmission under **Líng-yán Hóng-chǔ**. **1650**: installed by Hóng-chǔ at Guó-qīng-sì.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = **1650** (Guó-qīng abbacy entry by Hóng-chǔ's instruction). *notAfter* = **1652** (death). Two-year abbacy.

**Lineage and Guó-qīng succession.** Mì-yún Yuán-wù → Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng → Tuì-wēng Hóng-chǔ → Dà-yǔ Xíng-tāo. Direct successor at Guó-qīng-sì to **Xiè-yún Jì-jī** [[濟璣]] (d. 1648, [[KR6q0476]]) — both Hóng-chǔ heirs at the Tiāntái flagship.

## Other points of interest

- **Family-line uncle-nephew transmission.** Xíng-tāo's childhood study under his uncle **Hàn-yuè Fǎ-zàng** is a documented case of family-bound monastic learning subsequently regularized through Hóng-chǔ's full transmission — uncommon in the broader Mì-yún-line and indicative of the Sān-fēng-line's localized Wú-jiāng / Sū-zhōu kinship-network.
- **Continuous Sān-fēng Guó-qīng abbacy.** The two-abbacy sequence Jì-jī (d. 1648) → Xíng-tāo (1650–52) extends the Sān-fēng-line's claim on Tiān-tái Guó-qīng-sì for at least 1640s–1652 — a sustained Línjì occupancy of the Tiāntái flagship in the late-Míng / early-Qīng transition window.
