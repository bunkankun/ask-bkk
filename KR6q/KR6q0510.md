# Hānxiū chánshī yǔlù 憨休禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hān-xiū*
by [[如乾]] (說), [[繼堯]] (等編)

Twelve-juan *yǔlù* of **Hānxiū Rúqián** [[如乾]] 憨休如乾, LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Yúné Xǐ** 雲峨喜 (1613–1676) at **Fēngxuésì**. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Jìyáo** [[繼堯]] *děng biān*. Catalog meta omits the *speaker* function, crediting only Jìyáo as *děng biān*; the actual speaker is Rúqián / Hānxiū as established by the juan-1 biographical introduction (signed by **Hóngshàn** 弘善 of Jīnchéng).

Nine abbacies in Shǎnxī / SìchuānHénán region: **涇陽興福** (1672) → 廣教 → 清福 → 燉煌 → 金粟 → 興善 (1684) → 風穴 (1688) (succession to teacher's seat). Companion volume [[KR6q0229]] 《敲空遺響》 12 juan supplement. *notBefore* = **1672** (first abbacy), *notAfter* = c. **1700** as conservative bound. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J37 B383.
