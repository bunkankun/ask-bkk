# Huàn-yǒu Chuán chán-shī yǔ-lù 幻有傳禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huàn-yǒu Chuán*
by [[圓悟]] (等編), [[圓修]] (等編)

Ten-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Lóng-chí Huàn-yǒu Zhèng-chuán** [[正傳]] 龍池幻有正傳 (**1549 – 1614-03-21**, age 66), **the foundational late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí lineage-restorer** from whom the entire Mì-yún → Lín-yě / Tiān-yǐn / Wàn-rú establishment descends. Native of **Lì-yáng** 溧陽 (Jiāng-sū). Compiled jointly by his two greatest *fǎ-sì*: **Mì-yún Yuán-wù** [[圓悟]] (1566–1642) and **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū** [[圓修]] (1575–1635) *děng biān*. The catalog source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** of the canon (Vol. 153 No. 1637), but the work is also preserved in the Jiā-xīng-canon supplement.

*notBefore* ≈ **1580** (post-mature-abbacy); *notAfter* ≈ **1620** (post-Yuán-wù's editorial completion, several years after Huàn-yǒu's 1614 death). The compilation by Mì-yún Yuán-wù — soon to become the most influential Línjì-Yángqí abbot of his generation — makes this *yǔ-lù* the foundational textual document of the entire late-Míng Línjì-Yángqí revival. Catalog dynasty 明.

**Contents.** Juan 1: preface (by **Shǐ Yī** 史一, possibly **Shǐ Sì-mìng** 史孟麟); *mù-cì*; *Nán-lù* 南錄 (southern abbacy records). Juan 2: *Běi-lù* 北錄 (northern abbacy records). Juan 3–5: *Xián-tán* 閒談 (idle conversations) — preceded by two prefaces by **Shǐ Sān** 史三 and a second-edition preface; followed by *Wǎn-huà* 晚話 (evening talks); the master's deathbed *mò-hòu jì bìng yǔ* 末後偈并語; *rù-tǎ yǔ* 入塔語 (stupa-burial sermon). Juan 6+: *jǔ-gǔ*, *fǎ-yǔ*, etc.

## Other points of interest

This is the **lineage-foundational** *yǔ-lù* for the entire late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì-Yángqí establishment that fills J27–J40 of the Jiā-xīng Canon. Through Huàn-yǒu's three *fǎ-sì* — **Mì-yún Yuán-wù**, **Tiān-yǐn Yuán-xiū**, **Xuě-jiāo Yuán-xìn** — the lineage extended to the famous Línjì revival figures **Mù-chén Dào-mǐn** 木陳道忞, **Fèi-yǐn Tōng-róng** 費隱通容, **Shí-chē Tōng-chéng** 石車通乘, **Wàn-rú Tōng-wēi** 萬如通微 (cf. [[KR6q0584]]), **Yù-lín Tōng-xiù** 玉林通琇, etc. — i.e. the entire Mì-yún cohort.
