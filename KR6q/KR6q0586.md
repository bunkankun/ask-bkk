# Pán-shān Liǎo-zōng chán-shī yǔ-lù 盤山了宗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Liǎo-zōng of Pán-shān*
by [[超見]] (說), [[徹凡]] (等錄), [[心月]] (等錄)

Four-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** [[超見]] 了宗超見 (**1614-10-17 – 1688**, age 75), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Dà-bó Xíng-qián** [[乾]] (1602–1673, cf. [[KR6q0584]]). Native of **Shùn-tiān-fǔ Bǎo-dǐ-xiàn** 順天府寶坻縣 (Hé-běi), lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Chè-fán** [[徹凡]] and **Xīn-yuè** [[心月]] *děng lù*.

Documents Chāo-jiàn's eight major Hé-běi abbacies — most prominently the historic **Pán-shān Gǔ-zhōng-pán Zhèng-fǎ-sì** 盤山古中盤正法寺 from which his "Pán-shān" sobriquet derives. Other seats include **Nán-tāi** 南臺, **Lóng-yún** 隆阭, **Wàn-shàn-sì** 萬善寺, **Fàng-shēng-yuàn** 放生院, **Pǔ-dù-sì** 普渡寺, **Guān-yīn-sì** 觀音寺, **Rén-shèng-sì** 仁聖寺. *notBefore* ≈ **1675** (post-Dà-bó-death abbacy maturity); *notAfter* = **1688** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B474.

**Contents.** Juan 1–3: abbacy *yǔ-lù*; *xiǎo-cān*; *jī-yuán*; *fǎ-yǔ*; *fó-shì*. Juan 4: closing *xíng-shí* preserving birth and death dates.

## Other points of interest

KR6q0584 ([[乾]]), KR6q0586 (his *fǎ-sì* [[超見]]), KR6q0573 ([[昊]]), and KR6q0585 ([[順]]) form a complete three-generation Línjì-Yángqí northern Pán-shān lineage record: **Dà-bó Xíng-qián** (1602–1673) → **Liǎo-zōng Chāo-jiàn** (1614–1688) → **Huī-zhōu Hào** & **Lǎng-kōng Shùn**. Together they document the consolidation of the **Mì-yún → Wàn-rú → Pán-shān** Línjì-Yángqí northern-Hé-běi establishment over three generations, with **Pán-shān Zhèng-fǎ-sì** as the principal anchor and **Wàn-shàn-sì** + **Tān-zhè-sì** as the secondary rotating-abbacy posts.
