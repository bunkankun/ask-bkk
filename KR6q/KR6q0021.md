# Wǔdēng quánshū mùlù 五燈全書目錄

compiled by [[超永]] (Chāoyǒng), 16-juan table of contents to the *Wǔdēng quánshū* ([[KR6q0022]]), 1697

## About the work

The 16-juan general table of contents to the 120-juan *Wǔdēng quánshū* 五燈全書 ([[KR6q0022]]), the great early-Qīng synthesis. Separately catalogued in the Xuzangjing.

### Tiyao

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

Finding-aid for the parent work, in unusual extent (16 juan of *mùlù* alone) reflecting the parent's massive 120-juan scale. See [[KR6q0022]] for substantive content.
