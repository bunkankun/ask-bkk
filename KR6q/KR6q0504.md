# Jì-guāng Huò chán-shī yǔ-lù 寂光豁禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Jì-guāng Huò*
by [[豁]] (說), [[發育]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Jì-guāng Yìn-huò** [[豁]] 寂光印豁 (b. **1619-12-29**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pò-shān Hǎi-míng** [[海明]] (1597–1666); transmission **1659**. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xiào-cāng Fā-yù** [[發育]] *děng biān*. Three abbacies: **Lóng-yìn Fó-zǐ-sì**, **Suì-níng Guǎng-dé-sì**, **Xī-chōng Zī-fú-sì** (all Sì-chuān). Juan 4 includes *xíng-shí*. Twelve named heirs. *notBefore* = **1659** (transmission), *notAfter* = c. **1675** as a conservative bound. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B367.
