# Míng-jué Cōng chán-shī yǔ-lù 明覺聰禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of the "Bright-Awakened" Chán Master Cōng*
by [[寂空]] (等編次), [[方醒]] (等編次)

Twenty-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hān-pú Xìng-cōng** [[性聰]] 憨璞性聰 (**1611 – 1667-01-07**, age 57, *sēng-là* 39), imperially-titled **Míng-jué chán-shī** 明覺禪師 by the **Shùnzhì emperor** in 1657. Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Bǎi-chī Xíng-yuán** 百癡行元 (1611–1662) — thus a co-disciple of [[素]] **Lián-fēng Sù** ([[KR6q0530]]) and [[德風]] **Dé-fēng** ([[KR6q0531]]). Native of **Shào-wǔ** 邵武 (Fú-jiàn), lay surname **Lián** 連. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Jì-kōng** [[寂空]] and **Pǔ-yuè Fāng-xǐng** [[方醒]] *děng biān-cì*. Catalog source-edition is the **Qiánlóng Edition** Vol. 158 No. 1652.

Together with KR6q0604 (Mì-yún Yuán-wù), KR6q0605 (Yù-lín Tōng-xiù), KR6q0606 (Máo-xī Xíng-sēn), and KR6q0607 (Mù-chén Dào-mǐn), this is one of the **five great imperial-Manchu Línjì-Yángqí *yǔ-lù*** of the Shùnzhì-Kāngxī court canonized in the Qiánlóng Tripiṭaka. *notBefore* = **1657** (Shùnzhì invitation to Wàn-shàn-diàn — the principal preserved teaching content); *notAfter* = **1667** (death). Catalog dynasty 清.

**Contents.** Juan 1: imperial **chì-shū** 敕書; *biǎo* (memorials) — 謝恩表 (thanks-for-grace memorial), 進五燈表 (presentation of *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* memorial), 辭表 (farewell memorial); five prefaces; *mù-lù*; *yīn-shì* (phonetic glosses). Juan 2: **北京皇城內萬善殿語錄** 北京皇城內萬善殿語錄 — recordings from the **Forbidden City Wàn-shàn Hall** retreat with the Shùnzhì emperor, **1657** summer. Juans 2–4: **燕京順天府愍忠禪寺語錄** 燕京順天府愍忠禪寺語錄 — recordings from **Mǐn-zhōng-sì** in Beijing 1659–1660. Juans 5+: continuing materials including the seven southern abbacies' *yǔ-lù* and *fù-fǎ* records.

## Other points of interest

The **Wàn-shàn-diàn** 萬善殿 *yǔ-lù* in juan 2 is the most direct surviving record of the **Shùnzhì emperor's personal Buddhist instruction** — Xìng-cōng (along with Yù-lín Tōng-xiù, Mù-chén Dào-mǐn, and Máo-xī Xíng-sēn) was one of the four master Línjì-Yángqí teachers personally engaged by the young emperor. The **進五燈表** (presentation memorial for the *Wǔ-dēng quán-shū* 五燈全書) indicates Xìng-cōng's editorial oversight of the major **Wǔ-dēng quán-shū** lamp-history compilation — a primary monument of late-Míng / early-Qīng Línjì historiography. KR6q0609 is the closing entry of the J40 catalog sequence, marking the final imperial-canonical inclusion of the Shùnzhì-Kāngxī Buddhist establishment.
