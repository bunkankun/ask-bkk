# Jiànzhōng jìngguó xùdēng lù mùlù 建中靖國續燈錄目錄

compiled by [[惟白]] (Wéibái, fl. 1101), separately catalogued table of contents to the *Jiànzhōng jìngguó xùdēng lù* ([[KR6q0007]])

## About the work

The three-juan table of contents (*mùlù* 目錄) to the 30-juan *Jiànzhōng jìngguó xùdēng lù* 建中靖國續燈錄 ([[KR6q0007]]), the third of the Sòng Chán lamp records. Separately transmitted — and separately catalogued in the Xuzangjing (X78 n. 1555, distinct from n. 1556 which is the main text) — the *mùlù* is essentially a finding-aid for the 1,712 figures enumerated in the parent work.

### Tiyao

*No tiyao found in source.*

### Abstract

Presented together with the main work to Sòng Huīzōng 徽宗 in the first year of the *Jiànzhōng jìngguó* 建中靖國 reign period (1101). The existence of a separately-transmitted *mùlù* is not unique to this lamp record (cf. the juan-level tables of contents at the head of most Chán lamp records), but the 建中靖國 *mùlù* was transmitted as an independent three-juan text through the Japanese monastic editions that became the basis of the Xuzangjing, and is therefore catalogued separately in modern Chinese Buddhist bibliographies.

## Translations and research

No English translation. Scholarly interest is confined to philological notes within the larger literature on the *Jiànzhōng jìngguó xùdēng lù* itself; see the entry for [[KR6q0007]].

## Other points of interest

The separate transmission of this *mùlù* — and of comparable *mùlù* for other Sòng lamp records — is a minor but useful witness to Japanese editorial practice in shaping the Xuzangjing's treatment of Sòng Chán materials.
