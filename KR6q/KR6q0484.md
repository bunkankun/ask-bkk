# Nán-hǎi Bǎo-xiàng-lín Huì-gōng Xiòng chán-shī yǔ-lù 南海寶象林慧弓詗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Huì-gōng Xiòng of Bǎo-xiàng-lín, Nán-hǎi*
by [[開詗]] (說), [[傳一]] (等錄)

## About the work

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Huì-gōng Kāi-xiòng** [[開詗]] 慧弓開詗 (**1634** – **1676/77**), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Zài-shēn Hóng-zàn** [[弘贊]] 在犙弘贊 (1611–1685) at **Dǐng-hú-shān Qìng-yún-sì** 鼎湖山慶雲寺 in Zhào-qìng (Guǎng-dōng). Recorded by *fǎ-sì* **Chuán-yī** [[傳一]] *děng lù*. Title-form references the **Nán-hǎi Bǎo-xiàng-lín** 南海寶象林 abbacy in Guǎng-dōng. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J35 B336.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[開詗]] note: native of **Shùn-dé** 順德 (Guǎng-dōng), lay surname **Zhāng** 張, lay-name **Zhāng Míng-dá** 張明達. Tonsured at **17** (= **1650**) under Zài-shēn Hóng-zàn at Dǐng-hú-shān. Subsequent lifelong association with the Dǐng-hú / Yún-mén Cáo-dòng line in southern Guǎng-dōng.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1660** (post-transmission preaching). *notAfter* = **1676/77** (death age 43). Six-juan extent reflects his relatively brief but intensive 1660s–1670s ministry.

**Lineage.** Cáo-dòng-school. Wú-yì Yuán-lái 無異元來 (1575–1630) → Yīng-bēn Hóng-jué 應本宏覺 → Zài-shēn Hóng-zàn (1611–1685) → Huì-gōng Kāi-xiòng — the Bó-shān → Dǐng-hú line.

**Other works.** [[KR6q0132]] 《溈山警策句釋記》 collaborative commentary with his teacher Hóng-zàn. Also editorial-compiler of *Xuě-guān chán-shī yǔ-lù* (a different volume, 13 juan).

**Guǎng-dōng-region significance.** Together with [[KR6q0132]], this volume establishes Huì-gōng as one of the most prolific Guǎng-dōng-region Cáo-dòng *chán-shī* of his generation, anchoring the **Dǐng-hú / Yún-mén** sub-line's southern preaching presence.
