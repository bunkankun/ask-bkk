# Dà-bēi Miào-yún chán-shī yǔ-lù 大悲妙雲禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Miào-yún of Dà-bēi [-sì]*
by [[真雄]] (說), [[機德]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Miào-yún Zhēn-xióng** [[真雄]] 妙雲真雄 (b. **1634-10-28**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí 32nd-generation dharma-heir of **Jiè-ān Wù-jìn** 介菴悟進 (1612–1673), thus a co-disciple of [[圓法]] (cf. [[KR6q0532]]), [[真璨]] ([[KR6q0533]]), and [[真在]] ([[KR6q0534]]). Native of **Jīn-huá Wǔ-yì** 金華武義, lay surname **Xú** 徐, original monastic name **Shòu-fēng** 壽峰. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Tiān-wéi Jī-dé** [[機德]] *děng biān*. The *yǔ-lù* documents his teaching at three abbacies: **Shāndōng Lín-qīng-zhōu Dà-bēi chán-sì** 山東臨清州大悲禪寺, then on his return south the prestigious **Zhè-jiāng Jiā-xīng Sān-tǎ Jǐng-dé chán-sì** 嘉興三塔景德禪寺 (the same seat formerly held by his elder co-disciple [[圓法]] until 1669), with the **Hǎi-huì chán-yuàn** 海會禪院 also within the Sān-tǎ-fǔ complex.

The *yǔ-lù* opens with three *qǐng-qǐ* (invitation petitions): from the gentry of Lín-qīng-zhōu (Shāndōng) and Jiā-xīng-fǔ, plus a third from the **Jǐng-dé fǎ-zhí** 景德法姪 (younger dharma-nephew at Sān-tǎ-sì); a *zhuàn* (biography) follows immediately. *notBefore* = **1672** (壬子, *fù-fǎ* and the implied beginning of his preaching career — supported by the *xíng-lüè* note "師自壬子離金明"); *notAfter* = **1713** (printing date in the colophon: **康熙五十二年歲次癸巳九月吉旦**). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B415.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces, three *qǐng-qǐ*, *Dà-bēi Miào-yún chán-shī zhuàn*, *Lín-qīng-zhōu Dà-bēi yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Sān-tǎ Jǐng-dé Hǎi-huì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: *niān-gǔ*. Juan 4: *sòng-gǔ*, *fǎ-yǔ* (示天惟德記錄, 示天嶧脩侍者, etc.), *xíng-lüè* (autobiographical narration). Juan 5: *jī-yuán*, *xiàng-zàn* (image-encomia for Avalokiteśvara, Bodhidharma, his teacher Jiè-ān, his elder co-disciple **Ān-huà Shì-gāo** 安化世高 [[本則]], and the lay patron **Mù-gōng Xiàng** 牧公項), and the *lián-fāng-jì* listing all twenty of his dharma-heirs. Juan 6: closing *jì*, *fó-shì*, and printing colophon.

## Other points of interest

The Sān-tǎ Jǐng-dé succession is significant: when **Zhǔ-fēng Yuán-fǎ** [[圓法]] died in 1669 leaving only one *fǎ-sì* (the distant **Huái-ān Xīn-zōng Chéng-rán**), the seat passed laterally to a younger dharma-nephew within the broader **Jīn-míng Jiè-ān** 金明介菴 line — Miào-yún Zhēn-xióng — rather than to one of Zhǔ-fēng's own disciples. This kind of intra-lineage abbatial sharing is a characteristic feature of the Jiā-xīng-area Línjì-Yángqí federation in the Kāngxī era. The 1713 print date — forty years after his teacher's 1673 death and four decades after his own *fù-fǎ* — also suggests that Zhēn-xióng lived into the 1690s and that the corpus accumulated steadily before being engraved by his disciples after his death.
