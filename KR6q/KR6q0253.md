# Qióngjué lǎorén Tiānqí zhízhù Tiāntóng Jué héshàng sònggǔ 焭絕老人天奇直註天童覺和尚頌古

*Direct Commentary by Old-Man Qióngjué Tiānqí on the Sònggǔ Verses of Master Tiāntóng Jué* — a two-juan Míng Chán commentary by [[本瑞|Tiānqí Běnruì]] 天奇本瑞 (d. 1508) on the classical *sònggǔ* verses of the Southern-Sòng Cáodòng master [[正覺|Tiāntóng Hóngzhì Zhèngjué]] 天童宏智正覺 (1091–1157) — whose *sònggǔ* form the basis of the *Cóngróng lù* [[KR6q0114]]. Compiled by Běnruì's disciple [[性福|Xìngfú]] 性福.

Companion text to [[KR6q0250]] (*Běnruì's Xuědòu sònggǔ commentary*). Běnruì (a Línjì-lineage 26th-generation master) thus wrote commentaries on both the Línjì (Xuědòu) and Cáodòng (Hóngzhì) major *sònggǔ* corpora.

## About the work

A two-juan Míng direct-commentary (*zhízhù*), X67 n1306. Non-commentary on a single parent Buddhist text; *commentedTextid* omitted.

See [[KR6q0250]] for context on Běnruì's *zhízhù* method and the Míng-era plain-commentary approach.
