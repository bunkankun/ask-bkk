# Xiù-yě Lín chán-shī yǔ-lù 秀野林禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiù-yě Lín*
by [[明林]] (說), [[最正]] (等編)

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xiù-yě Míng-lín** [[明林]] 秀野明林, Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Tiān-mù / Qí-rán Chāo-zhì** [[超智]] (cf. [[KR6q0495]]). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-tán Zuì-zhèng** [[最正]] *děng biān* (whose own *yǔ-lù* is at [[KR6q0497]]). Lineage: Lín-yě Tōng-qí → Zì-xián Xíng-jué → Tiān-mù Chāo-zhì → Xiù-yě Míng-lín → Zhì-tán Zuì-zhèng. *notBefore* = c. 1670, *notAfter* = c. 1695. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B357.
