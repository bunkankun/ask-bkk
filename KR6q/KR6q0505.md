# Tiān-tái Tōng-xuán-sì Dú-lǎng chán-shī yǔ-lù 天台通玄寺獨朗禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Dú-lǎng of Tōng-xuán-sì on Tiān-tái-shān*
by [[行曰]] (說), [[超心]] (等編), [[超香]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Dú-lǎng Xíng-rì** [[行曰]] 獨朗行日 (lifedates not preserved; *shì-shòu* 66, *sēng-là* 43), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Lín-yě Tōng-qí** 林野通奇 (1596–1652). Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Chāo-xīn** [[超心]] + **Chāo-xiāng** [[超香]] *děng biān*. Two abbacies: **Yáo-jiāng Jī-qìng** + **Tiān-tái Tōng-xuán-sì** (death-seat). Juan 2 *tǎ-míng*. *notBefore* / *notAfter*: c. 1655–1685. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J36 B368.
