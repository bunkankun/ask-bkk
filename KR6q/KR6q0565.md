# Chì-sōng Lǐng chán-shī yǔ-lù 赤松領禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Chì-sōng Lǐng*
by [[道領]] (說), [[寂源]] (錄)

Five-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Chì-sōng Dào-lǐng** [[道領]] 赤松道領 (b. **1634-05-20**, death year unknown — was 53 in **1686**), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Mǐn-shù Rú-xiāng** [[如相]] (1603–1672, cf. [[KR6q0564]]) and the principal Línjì-Yángqí *zǔ-shī* of **Guì-yáng Qián-líng-shān Hóng-fú-sì** 貴陽黔靈山弘福寺 — the most important Buddhist monastery in Guì-zhōu province under the Qīng. Native of **Tóng-chuān** 潼川 (Sì-chuān), lay surname **Hán** 韓; his mother carried him into Guì-zhōu during the wartime chaos.

Recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Qián-yù Jì-yuán** [[寂源]] *lù*. Preface dated **康熙辛未皋月午日** = **1691** lunar 5 written at **Gǔ-yú-zhōu** 古渝州 (Chóng-qìng). Plus a secondary preface dated **康熙二十年歲次癸亥桐月** (with a probable misprint in the era-year — 康熙二十年 = 1681 ≠ 癸亥 1683; likely intended is 康熙二十二年 = 1683 lunar 3). *notBefore* = **1667** (康熙丁未, **Bái-yún** 白雲 invitation by 閫臺 **Zhāng Guāng-huàn** 張光煥); *notAfter* = **1691** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J39 B451.

**Contents.** Five juan: prefaces; abbacy *yǔ-lù* (Bái-yún 1667; subsequent invitations from **Yún-nán** 1684 (康熙甲子秋, to assist with 九峰山開山靈和尚 sweeping); Hóng-fú-sì 1686 winter (大中丞慕公 = the *Xún-fǔ* of Guì-zhōu); etc.); *xíng-yóu* in juan 5; *fǎ-yǔ*, *jī-yuán*, *fó-shì*, *lián-fāng-jì*.

## Other points of interest

This *yǔ-lù* is the most important documentary source for the founding of **Guì-yáng Hóng-fú-sì** 貴陽弘福寺 — the leading Línjì-Yángqí monastery in late-Qīng Guì-zhōu, which still stands. Dào-lǐng's three-year *bì-guān* preceded the monastery's institutional opening; the imperial-class lay-patron preface ("大中丞慕公" = the Guì-zhōu Provincial Governor) at the **1686** invitation marks the formal state recognition of the foundation.
