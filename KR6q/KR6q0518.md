# Huá-yán Hái-chū Fó chán-shī yǔ-lù 華巖還初佛禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Hái-chū Fó of Huá-yán*
by [[佛]] (說), [[通量]] (等編)

Two-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Hái-chū Guāng-fó** [[佛]] (= [[光佛]]) 還初光佛 (b. **1631**, alive 1685 at age 55), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** [[德玉]] (1628–1701) at **Chóng-qìng Huá-yán Chán-yuàn** 重慶華巖禪院. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Xī-lín Tōng-liàng** [[通量]] *děng biān*. Same Guāng-fó who compiled his master's [[KR6q0488]] *yǔ-lù*. *notBefore* = c. **1685** (post-master-decease succession; master died 1701 — but Guāng-fó was already abbot for some time before). Actually master Dé-yù died **1701** so Guāng-fó's own ministry mainly post-dates that; conservative *notBefore* = 1685 / *notAfter* = c. 1705 covers the active period. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B393.
