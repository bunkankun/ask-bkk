# Qìng-shān Mù-tíng Pǔ-fū Zhuō chán-shī yǔ-lù 磬山牧亭樸夫拙禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Mù-tíng Pǔ-fū Zhuō of Qìng-shān*
by [[拙]] (說), [[光模]] (等編)

Six-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Pǔ-fū Shí-zhuō** [[拙]] 樸夫實拙 (b. **1682**, age 56 in **1737**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Gǔ-yún Pèi** 古雲沛. Lay surname **Lǐ** 李. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Shí-tán Guāng-mó** [[光模]] *děng biān*. Documents three abbacies and precept-conferral activity in the **Jiāng-xī Pó-yáng** 鄱陽 / **Lín-chuān** 臨川 region: **Pó-yáng Jiàn-xī Yǒng-zhèn chán-sì** 鄱陽劍溪永鎮禪寺 (the principal seat); **Bà-líng Bái-lù-dù Xī-tīng-sì** 霸陵白鷺渡西汀寺; **Lín-chuān Qīng-yún cǎo-ān** 臨川青雲艸菴. Plus precept-conferral at **Fú-liáng Yuán-tōng-ān** 浮梁圓通菴 in **乾隆元年丙辰冬** = **1736** winter and **Chóng-rén Tiān-zhú-sì** 崇仁天竺寺 in **1736** lunar 12. *notBefore* ≈ **1720**; *notAfter* ≈ **1740**. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J40 B493.

**Contents.** Juan 1: portrait + preface; *mù-cì*; *Yǒng-zhèn-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *Xī-tīng-sì yǔ-lù*. Juan 3: precept-lectures at Yuán-tōng (1736) and Tiān-zhú (1736). Juan 4: *Qīng-yún cǎo-ān yǔ-lù*. Juan 5: *kān-biàn*; *jī-yuán*; *fù Gāo-fēng dà-shī liù-wèn zhuó-yǔ* 附高峰大師六問著語; *niān-gǔ*; *sòng-gǔ*. Juan 6: *zhuàn* (the principal source for his life including age-evidence to 1737).

## Other points of interest

The **Qiánlóng-era** materials — precept-lectures dated **1736** and the *zhuàn* dating to **1737** — make KR6q0599 among the latest imprints in the Jiā-xīng-canon supplement, extending the J40 record into the second decade of the Qiánlóng reign. The 附高峰大師六問著語 in juan 5 is a *zhuó-yǔ* (commentary) on the famous **Gāo-fēng Yuán-miào** 高峰原妙 *liù wèn* — anchoring this Qiánlóng-era *yǔ-lù* in the late-Sòng / early-Yuán Línjì *gōng-àn* repertoire.
