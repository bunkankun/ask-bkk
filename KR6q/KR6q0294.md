# Xiā-táng Huì-yuǎn chánshī guǎng lù 瞎堂慧遠禪師廣錄

*Extended Record of Chán Master Xiā-táng Huì-yuǎn* — a four-juan Southern-Sòng *yǔlù* of **Xiā-táng Huì-yuǎn** 瞎堂慧遠 (1103–1176), Línjì-Yángqí Hǔ-qiū-line master; dharma-heir of Yuán-wù Kè-qín. Compiled by four *cān-xué* disciples: [[齊己|Qí-jǐ]], [[如本|Rú-běn]], [[祖淳|Zǔ-chún]], [[法慧|Fǎ-huì]].

Four-juan Southern-Sòng Chán *guǎng lù*, X69 n1360.

Dating: *notBefore* c. 1150; *notAfter* c. 1180.
