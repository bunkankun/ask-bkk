# Shén-dǐng Yī-kuí chán-shī yǔ-lù 神鼎一揆禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Shén-dǐng Yī-kuí*
by [[元揆]] (說), [[成炯]] (等編)

Twelve-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Yī-kuí Yuán-kuí** [[元揆]] 一揆元揆 (b. **1634-05-23**, alive 1697 age 64), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Yuǎn-ān Běn-tǎn** [[本僼]] (1622–1682) at Tiān-tóng. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Yù-táng Chéng-jiǒng** [[成炯]] *děng biān*. Five abbacies: **月掌壽聖** → **京山永隆** → **神鼎資聖** → **瑞巖** (succession) → **天童** (succession). The title-form 神鼎 echoes the Sòng-era **Hóng-yīn Yīng-shàn** 紅印應禪 / **Shén-dǐng Hóng-yīn** 神鼎洪諲 lineage. *notBefore* = c. 1670 (post-transmission), *notAfter* = 1697 (last attestation alive). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J37 B388.
