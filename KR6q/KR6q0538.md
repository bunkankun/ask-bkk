# Huáyán BùyànLè chánshī yǔlù 華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bù-yàn-Lè of Huá-yán [-yuàn]*
by [[道樂]] (說), [[德普]] (記錄)

Three-juan *yǔlù* of **Bùyàn Dàolè** [[道樂]] 不厭道樂 (b. **1640-10-08**, death year unknown), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Shèngkě Déyù** 聖可德玉 (1628–1701), in the Guìzhōu / Sìchuān regional **Mǐnshù** 敏樹 sub-line of LínjìYángqí. Native of **Sīnán Yìnjiāng** 思南印江 (Guìzhōu), lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Recorded by his *fǎsì* **Cíjì Dépǔ** [[德普]] *jìlù*.

Dàolè's career was conducted entirely in the Guìzhōu / Sìchuān upper-Yangtze region — a far-southwest LínjìYángqí outpost relatively isolated from the eastern Jiā-xīng-axis collections that surround it in the Jiāxīng Canon. Three abbacies: **Xìnglóngwèi Lóngshānsì** 興隆衛龍山寺 (Guìzhōu, entered **康熙丙辰 9** = **1676-10**, held three years until war forced retreat); **Zūnyì Yùquánsì** 遵義玉泉寺 (Guìzhōu, entered **甲子春** = **1684**); and **Chóngqìng Huáyán chányuàn** 重慶華嚴禪院 (Sìchuān, post-**乙丑** = **1685** when he received *fùfǎ* from Shèngkě at Lúzhōu). The opening preface is by **Yuānshuǐ Zhòng Bìxiān Gěmín** 鴛水仲碧仙葛民 dated **康熙癸酉上巳日** = **1693-04-09** (Kāngxī 32, *shàngsì* festival). *notBefore* = **1676** (Lóngshān entry); *notAfter* = **1693** (preface). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B419.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *xíngyóu* (autobiographical sketch — primary source for his life); *Lóngshānsì yǔlù*; *Huáyán chányuàn yǔlù*. Juan 2: *xiǎocān*, *jīyuán*, *niānsòng*. Juan 3: *dānsòng* (single-verse responses to twelve classic *gōngàn* including 馬祖, 德山托缽, 靈雲見桃花); *yìnjì* listing eleven *fǎsì*; *fóshì*; closing with the master's *guàzhēn* (portrait-hanging *zàn*) and two memorial *jì* for an elder dharma-friend.

## Other points of interest

The *xíngyóu* in juan 1 is unusually candid about the master's pre-tonsure life: marriage at 13, widowhood from his father at 27, late tonsure at 31. The Chóngqìng Huáyányuàn was a major Sìchuān Línjì center re-developed under Shèngkě's patronage in the post-Sānfān-luàn (after 1681) reconstruction era; Dàolè's abbacy there represents the second-generation institutional consolidation of Mǐnshù LínjìYángqí Buddhism in the southwest.
