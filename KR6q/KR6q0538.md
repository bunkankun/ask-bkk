# Huá-yán Bù-yàn-Lè chán-shī yǔ-lù 華嚴不厭樂禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Bù-yàn-Lè of Huá-yán [-yuàn]*
by [[道樂]] (說), [[德普]] (記錄)

Three-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Bù-yàn Dào-lè** [[道樂]] 不厭道樂 (b. **1640-10-08**, death year unknown), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Shèng-kě Dé-yù** 聖可德玉 (1628–1701), in the Guì-zhōu / Sì-chuān regional **Mǐn-shù** 敏樹 sub-line of Línjì-Yángqí. Native of **Sī-nán Yìn-jiāng** 思南印江 (Guì-zhōu), lay surname **Yáng** 楊. Recorded by his *fǎ-sì* **Cí-jì Dé-pǔ** [[德普]] *jì-lù*.

Dào-lè's career was conducted entirely in the Guì-zhōu / Sì-chuān upper-Yangtze region — a far-southwest Línjì-Yángqí outpost relatively isolated from the eastern Jiā-xīng-axis collections that surround it in the Jiā-xīng Canon. Three abbacies: **Xìng-lóng-wèi Lóng-shān-sì** 興隆衛龍山寺 (Guì-zhōu, entered **康熙丙辰 9** = **1676-10**, held three years until war forced retreat); **Zūn-yì Yù-quán-sì** 遵義玉泉寺 (Guì-zhōu, entered **甲子春** = **1684**); and **Chóng-qìng Huá-yán chán-yuàn** 重慶華嚴禪院 (Sì-chuān, post-**乙丑** = **1685** when he received *fù-fǎ* from Shèng-kě at Lú-zhōu). The opening preface is by **Yuān-shuǐ Zhòng Bì-xiān Gě-mín** 鴛水仲碧仙葛民 dated **康熙癸酉上巳日** = **1693-04-09** (Kāngxī 32, *shàng-sì* festival). *notBefore* = **1676** (Lóng-shān entry); *notAfter* = **1693** (preface). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B419.

**Contents.** Juan 1: prefaces; *xíng-yóu* (autobiographical sketch — primary source for his life); *Lóng-shān-sì yǔ-lù*; *Huá-yán chán-yuàn yǔ-lù*. Juan 2: *xiǎo-cān*, *jī-yuán*, *niān-sòng*. Juan 3: *dān-sòng* (single-verse responses to twelve classic *gōng-àn* including 馬祖, 德山托缽, 靈雲見桃花); *yìn-jì* listing eleven *fǎ-sì*; *fó-shì*; closing with the master's *guà-zhēn* (portrait-hanging *zàn*) and two memorial *jì* for an elder dharma-friend.

## Other points of interest

The *xíng-yóu* in juan 1 is unusually candid about the master's pre-tonsure life: marriage at 13, widowhood from his father at 27, late tonsure at 31. The Chóng-qìng Huá-yán-yuàn was a major Sì-chuān Línjì center re-developed under Shèng-kě's patronage in the post-Sānfān-luàn (after 1681) reconstruction era; Dào-lè's abbacy there represents the second-generation institutional consolidation of Mǐn-shù Línjì-Yángqí Buddhism in the southwest.
