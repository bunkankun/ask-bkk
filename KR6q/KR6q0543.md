# Xiāngyán chánshī yǔlù 香嚴禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiāng-yán*
by [[明耀]] (等編錄), [[葉燮]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔlù* of **Xiāngyán Fāngguì** [[芳桂]] 香嚴芳桂 (**1631 – 1708-10-14**, age 78, *sēnglà* 62), LínjìYángqí dharma-heir of **Pǔmíng Shíguān Xínglíng** [[行凌]] (b. 1614) at **Pǔmíngsì** 普明寺 in Jiāxīng. Native of Jiāxīng, lay surname **Chén** 陳. Compiled by his *fǎsì* **Míngyào** [[明耀]] *děng biānlù*. The work is paired in J38 with his teacher's *yǔlù* [[KR6q0542]] 《石關禪師語錄》, giving a complete teacher-disciple sequence.

The principal preface is by **Yè Xiè** [[葉燮]] 橫山先生 (1627–1703), the major Qīng poetics theorist (author of 《原詩》 *Yuán shī*) and a fellow Jiāxīngfǔ compatriot — one of three prefatorial pieces in juan 1; another is dated **康熙辛酉季夏** = **1681** summer by 法弟 **Dúyán** 獨巖. The *xíngshí* in juan 1 records that Fāngguì received *fùfǎ* in **壬子** = **1672** and succeeded as abbot of Pǔmíngsì in **康熙十六/十七年戊午冬** = **1678** winter, after his teacher's *shǒukān* 守龕 (coffin-watching) period concluded. The closing notice records his death at age 78 on **康熙四十七年戊子 9/1** = **1708-10-14**. *notBefore* = **1672** (*fùfǎ*); *notAfter* = **1708** (death). Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J38 B424.

**Contents.** Single juan: prefaces (Yè Xiè + 獨巖); *shàngtáng*; *xiǎocān*; *jīyuán*; *fǎyǔ*; *zàn*; *niānsòng* on classic *gōngàn*; *xíngshí* (autobiographical narration); closing death-notice and *tǎmíng* details. The narration mentions a 1670 flood at the master's monastery and reconstruction work over multiple Kāngxī decades — a vivid record of Pǔmíngsì institutional history.

## Other points of interest

The **Yè Xiè** preface is the principal point of literary-cultural interest: it places this otherwise modest *yǔlù* within the social geography of late-Kāngxī Jiāxīng poetic and intellectual culture. Yè Xiè's most influential disciple, **Shěn Déqián** 沈德潛 (1673–1769), grew up within this same JiāxīngSūzhōu milieu — i.e. Fāngguì stood, indirectly, at the social center of the most important poetic-theoretical circle of his era. The *yǔlù*'s 1708-postface dating-evidence makes it among the latest Jiā-xīng-canon contributions chronicled in the J38 sequence.
