# Xiāng-yán chán-shī yǔ-lù 香嚴禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Xiāng-yán*
by [[明耀]] (等編錄), [[葉燮]] (序)

Single-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Xiāng-yán Fāng-guì** [[芳桂]] 香嚴芳桂 (**1631 – 1708-10-14**, age 78, *sēng-là* 62), Línjì-Yángqí dharma-heir of **Pǔ-míng Shí-guān Xíng-líng** [[行凌]] (b. 1614) at **Pǔ-míng-sì** 普明寺 in Jiā-xīng. Native of Jiā-xīng, lay surname **Chén** 陳. Compiled by his *fǎ-sì* **Míng-yào** [[明耀]] *děng biān-lù*. The work is paired in J38 with his teacher's *yǔ-lù* [[KR6q0542]] 《石關禪師語錄》, giving a complete teacher-disciple sequence.

The principal preface is by **Yè Xiè** [[葉燮]] 橫山先生 (1627–1703), the major Qīng poetics theorist (author of 《原詩》 *Yuán shī*) and a fellow Jiā-xīng-fǔ compatriot — one of three prefatorial pieces in juan 1; another is dated **康熙辛酉季夏** = **1681** summer by 法弟 **Dú-yán** 獨巖. The *xíng-shí* in juan 1 records that Fāng-guì received *fù-fǎ* in **壬子** = **1672** and succeeded as abbot of Pǔ-míng-sì in **康熙十六/十七年戊午冬** = **1678** winter, after his teacher's *shǒu-kān* 守龕 (coffin-watching) period concluded. The closing notice records his death at age 78 on **康熙四十七年戊子 9/1** = **1708-10-14**. *notBefore* = **1672** (*fù-fǎ*); *notAfter* = **1708** (death). Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J38 B424.

**Contents.** Single juan: prefaces (Yè Xiè + 獨巖); *shàng-táng*; *xiǎo-cān*; *jī-yuán*; *fǎ-yǔ*; *zàn*; *niān-sòng* on classic *gōng-àn*; *xíng-shí* (autobiographical narration); closing death-notice and *tǎ-míng* details. The narration mentions a 1670 flood at the master's monastery and reconstruction work over multiple Kāngxī decades — a vivid record of Pǔ-míng-sì institutional history.

## Other points of interest

The **Yè Xiè** preface is the principal point of literary-cultural interest: it places this otherwise modest *yǔ-lù* within the social geography of late-Kāngxī Jiā-xīng poetic and intellectual culture. Yè Xiè's most influential disciple, **Shěn Dé-qián** 沈德潛 (1673–1769), grew up within this same Jiā-xīng-Sūzhōu milieu — i.e. Fāng-guì stood, indirectly, at the social center of the most important poetic-theoretical circle of his era. The *yǔ-lù*'s 1708-postface dating-evidence makes it among the latest Jiā-xīng-canon contributions chronicled in the J38 sequence.
