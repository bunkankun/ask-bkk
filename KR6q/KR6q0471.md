# Yúnxī Liángtíng Tǐng chánshī yǔlù 雲溪俍亭挺禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Liáng-tíng Tǐng of Yún-xī*
by [[淨挺]] (說), [[智淙]] (等編)

## About the work

Eighteen-juan *yǔlù* of **Liángtíng Jìngtǐng** [[淨挺]] 俍亭淨挺 (**1615-11-24 – 1684-11-01**), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sānyí Míngyú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665) and one of the most prolific Chán-master authors of the early-Qīng Hángzhōu monastic-publishing community. Compiled by *fǎsì* **Zhìzōng** [[智淙]] (智琮) *děng biān*. The Yúnxī (= Yúnxī of Hángzhōu) seat references his **Cíyún** 慈雲 abbacy. Printed as Jiāxīng Canon J33 B294; also preserved in the 卍續藏 collection. Companion volumes by the same master: [[KR6q0218]] 《學佛考訓》 (1670, 10 juan encyclopedic reference) and a series of doctrinal essays at [[KR6q0219]] *passim*.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[淨挺]] note: lay native of **Qiántáng** 錢塘 (Hángzhōu); lay name **Xú Shìchén** 徐世臣 (huì 繼恩, *biézì* 逸亭). Late-Míng *míngjīng* graduate under the Hóngguāng court (**1645**); subsequently engaged with **Sānyí Míngyú** as a *jūshì* 居士 layman, with formal *jìbié* received at **Yúnménsì**. Tonsured at 46 (**1642**, after his parents' deaths). Three abbacies: **Cíyún** 慈雲 (Hángzhōu), **Fànshòu** 梵受, **Xiǎnshèng** 顯聖.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1645** (post-lay-*jìbié* preaching activity, with the Hóngguāng *míngjīng* in the same year as his Cáodòng *jìbié* under Sānyí). *notAfter* = **1684** (Jìngtǐng's death). Eighteen juan reflects the substantial documented preaching career across nearly forty years.

**Lineage and heirs.** Cáo-dòng-school via **Sānyí Míngyú** (1599–1665) line. Thirty named heirs including **Zhìzōng** [[智淙]] (this volume's compiler). The Cáodòng affiliation makes Jìngtǐng a contemporary parallel within Hángzhōu's Cáodòng establishment to **Jìngqǐ** [[淨啟]] (also a 三宜明盂 heir, the *zhòngbiān* of [[KR6q0459]]) — the two were dharma-brothers, with Jìngtǐng writing the front-preface to Jìngqǐ's [[KR6q0459]] under his lay-name *Yìtíng jūshì*.

**Comprehensive Buddhist literary production.** Jìngtǐng was distinctive for producing both monastic *yǔlù* (this volume) and substantial Buddhist scholarly work (the [[KR6q0218]] *Xuéfó kǎoxùn* encyclopedic reference, the [[KR6q0219]] series of essays). Yán Háng's preface characterizes him as *yú xué wú bù kuī, zhùshū mǎn jiā* 于學無不窺著書滿家 ("his learning omits no field, his books fill the house").

## Other points of interest

- **Lay-monastic dual identity.** Jìngtǐng continued to use his lay-name **Xú Jìēn** / **Yìtíng jūshì** for literary writing even after monastic ordination. This is documented in his front-preface to [[KR6q0459]] (the Hóngzhì Zhèngjué *zhòngbiān*) where he signs as a layman — providing a unique within-corpus cross-reference between his ordained and lay-literary identities.
- **Dharma-brothers as Cáo-dòng-line co-revivers.** Jìngtǐng and **Jìngqǐ** [[淨啟]] (compiler of [[KR6q0459]]) are dharma-brothers in the same Sānyí Míngyú line; their parallel publishing activity in the late 1660s and 1670s — Jìngqǐ recovering the Sòng-era Hóngzhì *yǔlù*, Jìngtǐng producing his own multi-genre Cáodòng output — represents a coordinated Cáo-dòng-line publishing program in early-Qīng Hángzhōu.
