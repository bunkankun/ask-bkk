# Yún-xī Liáng-tíng Tǐng chán-shī yǔ-lù 雲溪俍亭挺禪師語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Chán Master Liáng-tíng Tǐng of Yún-xī*
by [[淨挺]] (說), [[智淙]] (等編)

## About the work

Eighteen-juan *yǔ-lù* of **Liáng-tíng Jìng-tǐng** [[淨挺]] 俍亭淨挺 (**1615-11-24 – 1684-11-01**), Cáo-dòng-school dharma-heir of **Sān-yí Míng-yú** 三宜明盂 (1599–1665) and one of the most prolific Chán-master authors of the early-Qīng Háng-zhōu monastic-publishing community. Compiled by *fǎ-sì* **Zhì-zōng** [[智淙]] (智琮) *děng biān*. The Yún-xī (= Yún-xī of Háng-zhōu) seat references his **Cí-yún** 慈雲 abbacy. Printed as Jiā-xīng Canon J33 B294; also preserved in the 卍續藏 collection. Companion volumes by the same master: [[KR6q0218]] 《學佛考訓》 (1670, 10 juan encyclopedic reference) and a series of doctrinal essays at [[KR6q0219]] *passim*.

### Abstract

**Authorship.** Per the [[淨挺]] note: lay native of **Qián-táng** 錢塘 (Háng-zhōu); lay name **Xú Shì-chén** 徐世臣 (huì 繼恩, *bié-zì* 逸亭). Late-Míng *míng-jīng* graduate under the Hóng-guāng court (**1645**); subsequently engaged with **Sān-yí Míng-yú** as a *jū-shì* 居士 layman, with formal *jì-bié* received at **Yún-mén-sì**. Tonsured at 46 (**1642**, after his parents' deaths). Three abbacies: **Cí-yún** 慈雲 (Háng-zhōu), **Fàn-shòu** 梵受, **Xiǎn-shèng** 顯聖.

**Dating.** *notBefore* = c. **1645** (post-lay-*jì-bié* preaching activity, with the Hóng-guāng *míng-jīng* in the same year as his Cáo-dòng *jì-bié* under Sān-yí). *notAfter* = **1684** (Jìng-tǐng's death). Eighteen juan reflects the substantial documented preaching career across nearly forty years.

**Lineage and heirs.** Cáo-dòng-school via **Sān-yí Míng-yú** (1599–1665) line. Thirty named heirs including **Zhì-zōng** [[智淙]] (this volume's compiler). The Cáo-dòng affiliation makes Jìng-tǐng a contemporary parallel within Háng-zhōu's Cáo-dòng establishment to **Jìng-qǐ** [[淨啟]] (also a 三宜明盂 heir, the *zhòng-biān* of [[KR6q0459]]) — the two were dharma-brothers, with Jìng-tǐng writing the front-preface to Jìng-qǐ's [[KR6q0459]] under his lay-name *Yì-tíng jū-shì*.

**Comprehensive Buddhist literary production.** Jìng-tǐng was distinctive for producing both monastic *yǔ-lù* (this volume) and substantial Buddhist scholarly work (the [[KR6q0218]] *Xué-fó kǎo-xùn* encyclopedic reference, the [[KR6q0219]] series of essays). Yán Háng's preface characterizes him as *yú xué wú bù kuī, zhù-shū mǎn jiā* 于學無不窺著書滿家 ("his learning omits no field, his books fill the house").

## Other points of interest

- **Lay-monastic dual identity.** Jìng-tǐng continued to use his lay-name **Xú Jì-ēn** / **Yì-tíng jū-shì** for literary writing even after monastic ordination. This is documented in his front-preface to [[KR6q0459]] (the Hóng-zhì Zhèng-jué *zhòng-biān*) where he signs as a layman — providing a unique within-corpus cross-reference between his ordained and lay-literary identities.
- **Dharma-brothers as Cáo-dòng-line co-revivers.** Jìng-tǐng and **Jìng-qǐ** [[淨啟]] (compiler of [[KR6q0459]]) are dharma-brothers in the same Sān-yí Míng-yú line; their parallel publishing activity in the late 1660s and 1670s — Jìng-qǐ recovering the Sòng-era Hóng-zhì *yǔ-lù*, Jìng-tǐng producing his own multi-genre Cáo-dòng output — represents a coordinated Cáo-dòng-line publishing program in early-Qīng Háng-zhōu.
