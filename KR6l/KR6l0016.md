# Āpítán Xīn Lùnjīng 阿毘曇心論經
*Abhidharma-hṛdaya-sūtra-śāstra: Sūtra-Commentary on the Heart of the Abhidharma*
by [[法勝]] (論), [[優波扇多]] (釋), [[那連提耶舍]] (譯)

## About the work

The *Āpítán Xīn Lùnjīng* 阿毘曇心論經 (CBETA [[T28n1551]]) is a Northern Qi (北齊) dynasty translation of an expanded commentary on the *Abhidharma-hṛdaya-śāstra* ([[KR6l0015]], T1550), 6 juan. The text is attributed to the root text by Dharmaśreṣṭhin 法勝 ([[法勝]]) with a commentary by Upaśānta 優波扇多 ([[優波扇多]]); it was translated by Narendrayaśas 那連提耶舍 ([[那連提耶舍]]) under the Northern Qi (高齊). The Taisho header "No. 1551 [cf. Nos. 1550, 1552]" signals its place in the *Hṛdaya* transmission alongside T1550 and T1552.

### Prefaces

The opening of the text includes a brief framing that situates it as an explanatory expansion of the *Abhidharma-hṛdaya*: "Now, wishing to elucidate the *Abhidharma-hṛdaya* for the benefit of disciples..." (*Jīn yù jiě shì āpítán xīn lìyì dìzǐ gù* 今欲解釋阿毘曇心利益弟子故). The commentary distinguishes itself from earlier glosses as neither too broad nor too narrow (*lí yú guǎng-lüè* 離於廣略).

### Abstract

This text represents the second layer of the *Hṛdaya* commentary tradition: Dharmaśreṣṭhin's root verse (*kārikā*) text (T1550) received an extended commentary attributed to Upaśānta 優波扇多 (*Upaśānta*), another Sarvāstivāda master. Narendrayaśas 那連提耶舍 (Skt. *Narendrayaśas*, 517–589 CE), a North Indian monk who worked in the Northern Qi, Northern Zhou, and Sui courts and was the most prolific translator of his era, rendered it into Chinese. The 6-juan format represents a middle ground between the brief 4-juan T1550 and the more elaborate 11-juan T1552.

The Northern Qi patronage of translation (here by Narendrayaśas) is consistent with the court's support for Buddhism; Narendrayaśas also translated major sūtras (including the *Mahāratnakūṭa*) and is known for producing high-quality renderings.
