# Wǔshì Pípóshā Lùn 五事毘婆沙論
*Pañcavastu-vibhāṣā: Commentary on the Five Topics*
by [[法救]] (造), [[玄奘]] (譯)

## About the work

The *Wǔshì Pípóshā Lùn* 五事毘婆沙論 (Skt. *Pañcavastu-vibhāṣā*, CBETA [[T28n1555]]) is a Tang dynasty translation of a Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma treatise in 2 juan, attributed to the Venerable Dharmatrāta 尊者法救 ([[法救]]) and translated by Xuánzàng 玄奘 ([[玄奘]]). The text is a commentary on "five topics" (*wǔ shì* 五事) of Abhidharma doctrine, specifically five categories particularly debated within the Sarvāstivāda tradition: form (*rūpa*), feeling (*vedanā*), consciousness (*vijñāna*), and related categories.

### Abstract

The *Pañcavastu-vibhāṣā* is a brief but focused commentary attributed to Dharmatrāta 法救, one of the four major Kaśmīra Vaibhāṣika masters frequently cited in the *Mahāvibhāṣā* ([[KR6l0010]]). It treats five specific Abhidharma topics in vibhāṣā (exposition/commentary) style, providing definitions, analyses, and sub-distinctions. As a short specialist text, it complements the broader *Zá āpítán xīn lùn* ([[KR6l0017]]) also attributed to the same master.
