# Rù Āpídámó Lùn 入阿毘達磨論
*Abhidharma-avatāra-śāstra: Introduction to the Abhidharma*
by [[塞建陀羅]] (造), [[玄奘]] (譯)

## About the work

The *Rù Āpídámó Lùn* 入阿毘達磨論 (Skt. *Abhidharma-avatāra-śāstra* "Introduction to the Abhidharma", CBETA [[T28n1554]]) is a Tang dynasty translation of a Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma introductory manual, 2 juan, composed by the arhat Skandhila 塞建陀羅 ([[塞建陀羅]]) and translated by Xuánzàng 玄奘 ([[玄奘]]). The source text reads "塞建陀羅阿羅漢造，三藏法師玄奘奉詔譯."

### Prefaces

No independent preface survives in the received text.

### Abstract

The *Abhidharma-avatāra* ("entry into Abhidharma") is a concise Sarvāstivāda primer attributed to the arhat Skandhila 塞建陀羅 (*Skandhila*, also written 塞建地羅 in DILA). It functions as an introductory text (*avatāra* "entry/descent") to the more elaborate Abhidharma corpus, providing accessible definitions of fundamental categories. A Tibetan translation of the same text also survives in the Tengyur (D4100), attributed to *Skandhila*. The text is placed by modern scholars in approximately the 4th–6th century CE, likely in the Kaśmīra tradition of condensed Abhidharma manuals that parallels the *Hṛdaya* lineage ([[KR6l0015]]–[[KR6l0017]]).

Xuánzàng translated this text as part of his systematic rendering of Sarvāstivāda Abhidharma literature during the Táng.
