# Jùshě Lùn Sòng Shū Yì Chāo 俱舍論頌疏義鈔
*Doctrinal Extracts from the Commentary on the Abhidharmakośa Verses*
by [[慧暉]] (述)

## About the work

The *Jùshě Lùn Sòng Shū Yì Chāo* 俱舍論頌疏義鈔 (CBETA [[X53n839]]) is a Tang dynasty 3-juan sub-commentary (*chāo* 鈔) on 圓暉's *Jùshě lùn sòng shū* ([[KR6l0036]], T1823), composed by the monk Huìhuī 慧暉 ([[慧暉]], Tang), a student of 圓暉. The title *yì chāo* 義鈔 indicates "doctrinal extracts" — selected passages from 圓暉's text with additional explanation. The CBETA edition preserves the text in 3 juan with a table of contents.

### Prefaces

No independent preface in the surviving text. The opening consists of a table of contents listing the three fascicles by their chapter coverage.

### Abstract

Huìhuī 慧暉 (DILA A001732; fl. mid-to-late 8th century CE), styled Tang Abhidharma monk, was a student of 圓暉 ([[圓暉]]) who lived at Xīmíngsì 西明寺 in Chang'an. He composed this sub-commentary (*yì chāo*) as doctrinal annotations on 圓暉's commentary, focusing on clarifying the most difficult passages. Like his fellow student 遁麟's *Jùshě lùn sòng shū jì* ([[KR6l0041]]), this text belongs to the second-generation transmission of 圓暉's *Sòng Shū* commentary tradition.

This Tang Abhidharma master Huìhuī (DILA A001732) is entirely distinct from the Southern-Sòng Cáodòng Chán master of the same name 慧暉 (1097–1183; DILA A012808) represented in the existing *yǔlù* tradition.
