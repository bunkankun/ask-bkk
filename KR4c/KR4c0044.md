# Biéběn Hán wén kǎoyì 別本韓文考異
*Alternative Edition: Hán Yù Collection with Critical Notes*
by [[韓愈]] (撰), [[朱熹]] (考異), [[王伯大]] (重編)

## About the work
*Bié-běn Hán wén kǎo-yì* 別本韓文考異 in 40 *juǎn* — also widely transmitted as *Zhū Wén-gōng jiào Cháng-lí xiān-shēng wén jí* 朱文公校昌黎先生文集 (the SBCK title) — is the **consolidated** form of the Zhū Xī Hán-Yù critical apparatus: Wáng Bó-dà 王伯大's late-Sòng (*Chún-yòu* / *Bǎo-yòu* period, *ca.* 1234–1244) editorial digest in which Zhū Xī's [[朱熹]] *kǎo-yì* notes (originally separately printed as the 10-*juǎn* *Yuán-běn Hán jí kǎo-yì* = [[KR4c0043]]) are dispersed back into the body of the Hán Yù collection underneath the relevant lines. The 40-*juǎn* arrangement matches the standard Tang-period Lǐ Hàn 李漢 [[李漢]] table-of-contents form. Wáng's editorial consolidation made the *kǎo-yì* far more usable as a reading text and as a result became the dominant Sòng-Yuán-Míng circulation of the Hán-Yù corpus, almost completely displacing Zhū's separate *yuán-běn* (= [[KR4c0043]]) for several centuries.

The SBCK file opens with Zhū Xī's substantial *Hán wén kǎo-yì xù* 韓文考異序 — itself a major piece of Sòng critical bibliography, surveying the *Háng běn* 杭本 (*Xiáng-fú* period, *ca.* 1010), *Shǔ běn* 蜀本 (*Tiān-xǐ*, *ca.* 1020), and *Jiā-yòu Shǔ běn* 嘉祐蜀本 (1057), the *Guǎn-gé běn* 館閣本, and the principles by which Fāng Sōng-qīng's [[方崧卿]] *Jǔ-zhèng* should be retained or corrected.

### Tiyao
*No tíyào in source.* The KR4c0044 file is the SBCK base, which preserves Zhū Xī's preface but no Sìkù tíyào. The Sìkù WYG 40-*juǎn* tíyào (V1073.3) survives in the Zinbun digital *Sìkù tíyào*; it pairs the entry with [[KR4c0043]] and notes Wáng Bó-dà's editorial consolidation.

### Abstract
The Wáng Bó-dà recension is the principal pre-modern reading edition of Hán Yù — it consolidates the *jiào-kān* foundation of Fāng Sōng-qīng (= [[KR4c0042]]) and the critical refinement of Zhū Xī (= [[KR4c0043]]) into a single 40-*juǎn* working text, replicating the original Lǐ Hàn arrangement but enriched with Zhū's interlinear *kǎo-yì*. From the late Sòng through the Míng this was the standard form of Hán Yù's collection on the *kē-jǔ* curriculum and in private libraries.

The Sòng *Tángshū yì-wén zhì* records *Hán Cháng-lí jí* 韓昌黎集 in 40 *juǎn*; the 40-*juǎn* form is the original Lǐ Hàn (Hán Yù's son-in-law and his original editor in 824) configuration, preserved without break across the entire Sòng-Yuán-Míng-Qīng tradition.

Wáng Bó-dà (cbdbId 20099 in CBDB; meta gives no dates; CBDB has *fl.* 1214 and death 1253) was a *Wú-zhōu* 婺州 *Yì-wū* 義烏 native (modern *Yì-wū* in central Zhèjiāng); *jìn-shì* of *Jiā-tài* 2 (1202); successively *zhōng-shū shè-rén* 中書舍人 and *Hàn-lín xué-shì* 翰林學士 in the late Lǐ-zōng court. He died in 1253. The editorial consolidation of Zhū Xī's *kǎo-yì* into the Hán-Yù body text was undertaken in his middle years.

For Hán Yù himself, see the existing person note [[韓愈]]; for the collateral and downstream Hán-Yù commentary tradition see [[KR4c0042]], [[KR4c0043]], [[KR4c0045]], [[KR4c0046]], [[KR4c0047]].

## Other points of interest
The Sòng-Yuán dominance of Wáng Bó-dà's consolidated form is the principal reason Zhū Xī's separate *yuán-běn* fell out of circulation; the *Sìkù*-editorial decision to print both [[KR4c0043]] and [[KR4c0044]] is therefore unusual and reflects the editors' commitment to recovering lost original forms even where a downstream consolidation had been the practical reading text.
