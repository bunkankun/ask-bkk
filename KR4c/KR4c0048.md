# Liǔ Hédōng jí 柳河東集
*Collected Works of Liǔ of Hé-dōng (Liǔ Zōng-yuán)*
by [[柳宗元]] (撰), [[韓醇]] (詁訓)

## About the work
*Gǔ-xùn Liǔ xiān-shēng wén-jí* 詁訓柳先生文集 in 45 *juǎn* (with appended *Wài-jí* 外集 in 2 *juǎn* and *Xīn-biān wài-jí* 新編外集 in 1 *juǎn*) — generally known as *Liǔ Hé-dōng jí* 柳河東集 — is the foundational Sòng annotated edition of Liǔ Zōng-yuán [[柳宗元]]'s collected works, with running commentary (*gǔ-xùn* 詁訓 — glosses and explanations) by **Hán Chún** 韓醇 (*zì* Zhòng-shào 仲韶, *Lín-qióng* 臨邛 / Sì-chuān man, fl. *ca.* 1170–1180; not the same person as Hán Yù's son-in-law Hán Yù 韓郁). The 45-*juǎn* form descends from Liǔ Yǔ-xī's [[KR4c0051 author]] Liú Yǔ-xī 劉禹錫's original Tang-period editorial close (820s) — Liú Yǔ-xī, Liǔ Zōng-yuán's lifelong friend, edited the collection at his death in 819 and supplied the substantial preface preserved here. Of the four Sòng circulating recensions identified by the Sìkù tíyào (a 33-*juǎn* *Yuán-fú* period print, the *Zēng* prime-minister family copy, the *Yàn Yuán-xiàn* family copy, and the present 45-*juǎn* — derived from the *Mù Xiū* 穆脩 family copy and identified in the Sòng tradition as Liú Yǔ-xī's original 45-*juǎn*), the present version is the most authoritative.

### Tiyao
*Gǔ-xùn Liǔ xiān-shēng wén-jí* in 45 *juǎn*, with *Wài-jí* in 2 *juǎn* and *Xīn-biān wài-jí* in 1 *juǎn* — composed by Liǔ Zōng-yuán of the Táng, with *yīn-shì* (phonetic and meaning notes) by Hán Chún of the Sòng. Chún's *zì* was Zhòng-shào, a *Lín-qióng* man; his beginning and end are unknown. Zōng-yuán's collection was edited by Liú Yǔ-xī. Subsequent *juǎn-mù* additions and deletions by the Sòng produced four parallel recensions: (1) 33 *juǎn*, *Yuán-fú* period (1098–1100) capital print; (2) the Zēng [Gǒng?] family copy; (3) the Yàn Yuán-xiàn 晏元獻 (= Yàn Shū 晏殊) family copy; (4) the present 45-*juǎn* version, said to come from the Mù Xiū 穆脩 family and to be Liú Yǔ-xī's original recension.

Per Chén Zhèn-sūn's *Shū-lù jiě-tí*: Liú Yǔ-xī's preface says he edited the prose into 32 *tōng* (sections), with the [Hán Yù] *zhì* and *jì-wén* appended at the end of the first *tōng*; current circulating texts are all 45 *juǎn* and do not include Hán Yù's *zhì-wén* — therefore not the original. Examining the present text: Liú Yǔ-xī's preface here gives 45 *tōng* not 32 — disagreeing with Chén's account; either later editors revised the preface to match the 45-*juǎn* circulating version, or the figure is not certain. [Continued *tíyào*: discussion of Hán Chún's annotation method and its relationship to the parallel Sòng Liǔ-Zōng-yuán commentaries.]

### Abstract
The 45-*juǎn* form is the principal Sòng recension of the Liǔ Zōng-yuán corpus. Hán Chún's commentary represents the earlier of the two surviving major Sòng commentary traditions (the other being the *Liǔ Hé-dōng jí zhù* by Tóng Zōng-shuō [[童宗說]], Zhāng Dūn-yí 張敦頤, and Pān Wěi 潘緯 = [[KR4c0049]], and behind both, the *Wǔ-bǎi-jiā zhù* anthology of Wèi Zhòng-jǔ = [[KR4c0050]]). Hán Chún's notes are principally philological — *yīn-shì* phonetic glosses and source-tracing for allusions — without the philosophical interpretation found in the later Sòng *jí-zhù* tradition.

For Liǔ Zōng-yuán himself see the existing person note [[柳宗元]]; for the parallel Liǔ commentary editions see [[KR4c0049]] and [[KR4c0050]]; for the Hán Yù counterpart commentary tradition see [[KR4c0042]] – [[KR4c0047]].

## Other points of interest
Hán Chún was also responsible for an annotated edition of Hán Yù's collection (a *Hán Cháng-lí jí jiào-zhèng* mentioned in Sòng catalogs but not surviving in WYG); his pairing of the Hán-Liǔ commentary work places him in the broader Sòng *fù-gǔ* commemorative scholarship of the *Lóng-xìng* / *Qián-dào* generation.
