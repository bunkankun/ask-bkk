# Overview — KR3i 譜錄類 *Pǔlù lèi*

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3i corresponds to the *Sìkù quánshū* category 譜錄類 *pǔlù lèi*, the eighth division of the *Zǐbù* 子部 (Masters Branch), reserved for monographs that take a single physical object — an implement, a foodstuff, a plant, an animal, a mineral — as their subject and exhaust it from every angle: origin, manufacture, geography, taxonomy, anecdote, and poetry. As Wilkinson notes (§36.1, §36.20), the *Sìkù* editors created the category to gather the heterogeneous "miscellaneous handbooks" (his phrase) that had accumulated since the Tang and did not fit neatly into the older *zájiā* 雜家 or *nóngjiā* 農家 rubrics — works on tea, ink, inkstones, incense, flowers, fish, and crabs whose only common feature is the *single-object-monograph* form. The model is Lù Yǔ's 陸羽 *Chájīng* 茶經 (c. 760, [[KR3i0019]]), whose audacious claim to *jīng* (classic) status — a title-form reserved in the Han–Tang tradition for canonical scripture — was accepted by later writers and made the X-*jīng* / X-*pǔ* / X-*lù* / X-*shǐ* monograph a legitimate Chinese literary kind.

The chronological centre of gravity of KR3i sits firmly in the Sòng (especially Northern Sòng to mid-Southern Sòng, c. 980–1250), the period in which literati connoisseurship of the studio-objects, of tea, wine, and flowers, and of regional specialties such as lychee and citrus crystallised into a self-conscious genre. Sū Yìjiǎn's *Wénfáng sìpǔ* 文房四譜 of 986 ([[KR3i0002]]) is the foundational systematic *pǔ*, and Ōuyáng Xiū's *Luòyáng mǔdān jì* 洛陽牡丹記 of c. 1031–34 ([[KR3i0029]]) initiates the flower-treatise sub-tradition; the form is then taken up by Cài Xiāng, Mǐ Fú, Fàn Chéngdà, Hán Yànzhí, and many others. The division also collects a late-imperial layer of imperially-commissioned, court-printed compendia (*Qīndìng Xīqīng yànpǔ* of 1778 [[KR3i0009]]; *Qiánlù* of 1750 [[KR3i0014]]; *Yùdìng Pèiwénzhāi guǎng qúnfāng pǔ* of 1708 [[KR3i0044]]), in which the private literati genre is reabsorbed into Qīng imperial cultural policy. A handful of pre-Sòng works (Dài Kǎizhī's *Zhúpǔ* of c. 400–490 [[KR3i0040]], Biàn Bīn's *Qínshòu juélù* of c. 483–500 [[KR3i0051]], Lù Yǔ's *Chájīng*, and Zhāng Yòuxīn's *Jiānchá shuǐjì* [[KR3i0025]]) furnish the genre's prehistory, while the early-Qīng *Xù chájīng* 續茶經 ([[KR3i0024]]) and the polymathic *Yìyú túzàn* cluster ([[KR3i0048]]–[[KR3i0050]]) show its late afterlife. One conspicuous outlier — the *Qíqì túshuō* 奇器圖說 ([[KR3i0001]]) of 1627, the Jesuit Johann Schreck's collaboration with Wáng Zhēng on European mechanical engineering — sits within KR3i only by *Sìkù* default, lacking any other obvious home; it should be read as Jesuit-Chinese collaboration literature, not as *pǔlù* properly.

## Important texts and text clusters

The principal texts and recognised clusters within KR3i, in rough thematic order:

- **The foundational monographs.** *Chájīng* 茶經 by Lù Yǔ ([[KR3i0019]]) — the genre's seed and the model from which most later *pǔlù* derive. *Wénfáng sìpǔ* 文房四譜 by Sū Yìjiǎn ([[KR3i0002]]) — the first systematic monograph on the *wénfáng sìbǎo* 文房四寶 (brush, inkstone, paper, ink) and the structural template for later studio-object treatises.

- **The inkstone (硯) cluster.** *Yànshǐ* 硯史 by Mǐ Fú ([[KR3i0003]]) — the most influential single-author treatise on inkstones; *Shèzhōu yànpǔ* 歙州硯譜 ([[KR3i0004]]), *Shèyàn shuō* 歙硯說 ([[KR3i0005]]), *Duānxī yànpǔ* 端溪硯譜 ([[KR3i0006]]), *Yànpǔ* 硯譜 ([[KR3i0007]]), and *Yànjiān* 硯箋 by Gāo Sìsūn ([[KR3i0008]]) — a tight Sòng sub-tradition on the regional inkstones of Shèzhōu and Duānxī; *Qīndìng Xīqīng yànpǔ* 欽定西清硯譜 ([[KR3i0009]]) — the Qiānlóng court's lavish catalogue of the imperial inkstone collection, which closes the cluster from above.

- **The ink (墨) cluster.** *Mòpǔ fǎshì* 墨譜法式 by Lǐ Xiàoměi ([[KR3i0010]]), *Mòjīng* 墨經 by Cháo Guànyī ([[KR3i0011]]), *Mòshǐ* 墨史 by Lù Yǒu ([[KR3i0012]]), *Mòfǎ jíyào* 墨法集要 by Shěn Jìsūn ([[KR3i0013]]) — Sòng–Yuán–Míng treatises on ink-making (composition, soot-grading, glue, moulding) and on the canon of celebrated ink-makers.

- **The incense (香) cluster.** *Xiāngpǔ* 香譜 by Hóng Chú ([[KR3i0015]]), *Chénshì xiāngpǔ* 陳氏香譜 ([[KR3i0016]]), *Xiāngshèng* 香乘 by Zhōu Jiāzhòu ([[KR3i0017]]) — Sòng-to-Míng monographs on aromatic materials, blends, and burning customs.

- **The tea (茶) cluster.** *Chájīng* ([[KR3i0019]]), *Chálù* 茶錄 by Cài Xiāng ([[KR3i0020]]), *Pǐnchá yàolù* 品茶要錄 by Huáng Rú ([[KR3i0021]]), *Xuānhé Beìyuàn gòngchá lù* 宣和北苑貢茶錄 ([[KR3i0022]]), *Dōngxī shìchá lù* 東溪試茶錄 ([[KR3i0023]]), *Xù chájīng* 續茶經 by Lù Tíngcàn ([[KR3i0024]]), *Jiānchá shuǐjì* 煎茶水記 ([[KR3i0025]]) — together the densest sub-corpus of the division, covering tea-cultivation, manufacture, tribute geography (especially the Beìyuàn 北苑 plantations in Fújiàn), brewing-method, and water-evaluation.

- **The wine and sugar texts.** *Beìshān jiǔjīng* 北山酒經 by Zhū Yìzhōng ([[KR3i0026]]) — the most technically detailed Sòng treatise on rice-wine brewing; *Jiǔpǔ* 酒譜 by Dòu Píng ([[KR3i0027]]); *Tángshuāng pǔ* 糖霜譜 by Wáng Zhuó ([[KR3i0028]]) — the first systematic monograph on crystallised sugar manufacture.

- **The flower treatises.** *Luòyáng mǔdān jì* 洛陽牡丹記 by Ōuyáng Xiū ([[KR3i0029]]) — the genre-founding flower-treatise; *Yángzhōu sháoyào pǔ* 揚州芍藥譜 ([[KR3i0030]]); the chrysanthemum sequence *Liúshì júpǔ* ([[KR3i0031]]), *Shǐshì júpǔ* ([[KR3i0032]]), *Fàncūn júpǔ* ([[KR3i0034]]), *Bǎijú jípǔ* ([[KR3i0035]]); *Fàncūn méipǔ* 范村梅譜 ([[KR3i0033]]) on plums; *Jīnzhāng lánpǔ* 金漳蘭譜 ([[KR3i0036]]) on the Zhāngzhōu orchids; *Hǎitáng pǔ* 海棠譜 ([[KR3i0037]]) on crab-apple — a tightly-knit Sòng connoisseurship literature on garden flowers, mostly tied to specific regional cultivars.

- **The fruit and plant monographs.** *Lìzhī pǔ* 荔枝譜 by Cài Xiāng ([[KR3i0038]]) — the foundational lychee monograph; *Júlù* 橘錄 by Hán Yànzhí ([[KR3i0039]]) — the foundational citrus monograph (Yǒngjiā citrus varieties); *Zhúpǔ* 竹譜 by Dài Kǎizhī ([[KR3i0040]]) — the oldest text in the division, a Six-Dynasties verse-treatise on bamboo; *Sǔnpǔ* 筍譜 by the monk Zànníng ([[KR3i0041]]); *Jūnpǔ* 菌譜 ([[KR3i0042]]) on mushrooms.

- **The dietetic and imperial-flora compendia.** *Yǐnshàn zhèngyào* 飲膳正要 ([[KR3i0043]]) — Hū Sīhuì's 忽思慧 Yuán imperial dietetic manual, distinctive among KR3i for its Inner-Asian (Mongol/Uyghur/Islamic) culinary stratum, treated at length in Wilkinson §36.20 #9; *Yùdìng Pèiwénzhāi guǎng qúnfāng pǔ* 御定佩文齋廣羣芳譜 ([[KR3i0044]]) — the Kāngxī-era 100-*juàn* imperial flora-and-agriculture compendium, the most encyclopaedic single work in the division.

- **The zoological treatises.** *Qínjīng* 禽經 ([[KR3i0045]]) — bird monograph pseudonymously attributed to Shī Kuàng 師曠 with a Zhāng Huá 張華 commentary, in fact a Sòng compilation; *Xièpǔ* 蟹譜 by Fù Gōng ([[KR3i0046]]) and *Xièlüè* 蟹略 by Gāo Sìsūn ([[KR3i0047]]) — the two crab monographs; the *Yìyú túzàn* cluster — Yáng Shèn's *Yìyú túzàn* 異魚圖贊 ([[KR3i0048]]), Hú Shìān's 胡世安 *Yìyú túzàn jiān* ([[KR3i0049]]) and *Yìyú túzàn bǔ* ([[KR3i0050]]) — Míng-Qīng illustrated encomia on strange fishes, in verse form; and the outlier *Qínshòu juélù* 禽獸決錄 by Biàn Bīn ([[KR3i0051]]), a Southern-Qí allegorical-satirical *jué* on the moral natures of beasts, surviving in fragments.

- **The numismatic, lithological, and Jesuit outliers.** *Qiánlù* 錢錄 ([[KR3i0014]]) — Liáng Shīzhèng's 1750 Qiānlóng imperial catalogue of coinage; *Yúnlín shípǔ* 雲林石譜 by Dù Wǎn ([[KR3i0018]]) — the foundational monograph on ornamental scholar-stones; *Qíqì túshuō* 奇器圖說 ([[KR3i0001]]) — the Schreck-Wáng Jesuit treatise on European mechanical devices, classified as *pǔlù* by the *Sìkù* for want of a better category.

## Important persons

- [[陸羽]] (Lù Yǔ, 733–805) — author of the *Chájīng* ([[KR3i0019]]); the *cháshèng* 茶聖 (Sage of Tea), genre-founder of Chinese *pǔlù* literature.
- [[蘇易簡]] (Sū Yìjiǎn, 957–995) — Sòng Hànlín scholar; author of the *Wénfáng sìpǔ* ([[KR3i0002]]) and the immediate genre-founder for the Sòng object-treatise tradition.
- [[米芾]] (Mǐ Fú, 1051–1107) — Northern Sòng calligrapher, painter, and connoisseur; his *Yànshǐ* ([[KR3i0003]]) is the most influential single-author inkstone monograph.
- [[歐陽修]] (Ōuyáng Xiū, 1007–1072) — the dominant Northern Sòng literatus; his *Luòyáng mǔdān jì* ([[KR3i0029]]) inaugurates the flower-treatise sub-genre.
- [[蔡襄]] (Cài Xiāng, 1012–1067) — Northern Sòng calligrapher and Fújiàn official; double presence in the division as author of *Chálù* ([[KR3i0020]]) and *Lìzhī pǔ* ([[KR3i0038]]).
- [[范成大]] (Fàn Chéngdà, 1126–1193) — Southern Sòng poet and Sūzhōu-gardener; author of two flower-treatises on his own Fàncūn 范村 garden, *Fàncūn méipǔ* ([[KR3i0033]]) and *Fàncūn júpǔ* ([[KR3i0034]]).
- [[高似孫]] (Gāo Sìsūn, fl. 1184) — Southern Sòng polymath; author of *Yànjiān* ([[KR3i0008]]) and *Xièlüè* ([[KR3i0047]]), both representing the more philologically-erudite *jiān*/*lüè* sub-style of the genre.
- [[周嘉胄]] (Zhōu Jiāzhòu, d. c. 1660) — late-Míng compiler of *Xiāngshèng* ([[KR3i0017]]), the most comprehensive single Chinese treatise on incense.
- [[釋贊寧]] (Shì Zànníng, 918–999) — the eminent Sòng-Buddhist historian; author of *Sǔnpǔ* ([[KR3i0041]]), an unusual case of a Buddhist monk producing a secular *pǔlù*.
- [[戴凱之]] (Dài Kǎizhī, fl. 5th c.) — earliest *pǔlù* author preserved in the division; his *Zhúpǔ* ([[KR3i0040]]) is a Six-Dynasties verse-treatise antedating the rest of the corpus.
- [[忽思慧]] (Hū Sīhuì / Hoshoi, fl. 1314–1330) — Yuán court dietitian of probable Uyghur/Inner-Asian extraction; the *Yǐnshàn zhèngyào* ([[KR3i0043]]) introduces a substantial Mongol-Islamic dietetic stratum into the division.
- [[楊愼]] (Yáng Shèn, 1488–1559) — Míng polymath in Yúnnán exile; author of the *Yìyú túzàn* ([[KR3i0048]]), basis for the strange-fish cluster.
- [[胡世安]] (Hú Shìān, 1593–1663) — Míng-Qīng transition official; annotator and supplementer of Yáng Shèn's strange-fish encomia ([[KR3i0049]], [[KR3i0050]]).
- [[鄧玉函]] (Dèng Yùhán / Johann Schreck, 1576–1630) and [[王徵]] (Wáng Zhēng, 1571–1644) — co-authors of the *Qíqì túshuō* ([[KR3i0001]]); the only European author represented in the division.
- [[于敏中]] (Yú Mǐnzhōng, 1714–1779), [[梁詩正]] (Liáng Shīzhèng, 1697–1763), [[汪灝]] (Wāng Hào) — chief Qīng compilers of the three great court-sponsored compendia (*Xīqīng yànpǔ*, *Qiánlù*, *Pèiwénzhāi guǎng qúnfāng pǔ*) that close the division.

## Topics

The principal subjects and interpretive problems running through KR3i:

- **The *pǔlù* as a literati genre.** The single-object monograph is one of the most distinctive Chinese contributions to non-fictional prose, and the Sòng explosion of *pǔlù* writing (c. 980–1250) is bound up with the formation of the Sòng literatus's material-cultural identity (Bol, Egan). The genre is unintelligible apart from the practice of literati connoisseurship — the assertion that ordinary objects, properly attended to, are worthy of *jīng* (classic) treatment.

- **The *jīng* / *pǔ* / *lù* / *shǐ* / *jiān* / *lüè* terminological hierarchy.** Authors choose the title-form (X-*jīng* = the most ambitious, claiming classic status; X-*shǐ* = a history of an object; X-*pǔ* = a treatise / catalogue; X-*lù* = a record; X-*jiān* = annotated notes; X-*lüè* = a concise survey) as a deliberate generic positioning. The interplay of these terms across the division — *Chájīng* vs. *Sǔnpǔ*, *Yànshǐ* vs. *Yànjiān*, *Mòjīng* vs. *Mòpǔ* vs. *Mòshǐ* — is a major topic in its own right.

- **Tribute geography and regional specialisation.** A large proportion of the division concerns objects associated with specific regional production sites: Beìyuàn 北苑 tribute tea (Fújiàn), Duānxī 端溪 inkstones (Guǎngdōng), Shèzhōu 歙州 inkstones (Ānhuī), Fújiàn lychee, Wēnzhōu / Yǒngjiā citrus, Luòyáng peonies, Yángzhōu herbaceous peonies, Sìchuān crystallised sugar, Zhāngzhōu orchids. The *pǔlù* genre is in part the literary face of the Sòng-and-later tribute economy.

- **Manufacturing technique and *kējì* literature.** Several texts contain detailed technical descriptions: ink-making procedure (*Mòfǎ jíyào*), wine-brewing (*Beìshān jiǔjīng*), tea-cake processing (*Chájīng*, *Beìyuàn gòngchá lù*), sugar-frost crystallisation (*Tángshuāng pǔ*). They are primary sources for Chinese science-and-technology history of considerable importance (Needham *SCC* draws on them extensively).

- **The literatus garden.** The flower-treatise cluster (peony, chrysanthemum, plum, orchid, crab-apple) is bound up with the Sòng-and-later cultural form of the named literatus garden — Ōuyáng Xiū's Luòyáng, Fàn Chéngdà's Fàncūn (Stone-Lake), etc. — and with the practice of cultivar-naming and rank-ordering as a connoisseurship activity.

- **Imperial appropriation of literati genres.** The late-imperial layer of the division (Pèiwénzhāi guǎng qúnfāng pǔ, Xīqīng yànpǔ, Qiánlù) shows the Qīng court systematically taking over the previously-private literati object-treatise form for ideological / display purposes. The Kāngxī and Qiānlóng emperors are visible patrons of this absorption.

- **The Buddhist and the Inner-Asian voice.** Two anomalous texts — Zànníng's *Sǔnpǔ* (a Buddhist monk's secular *pǔ*) and Hū Sīhuì's *Yǐnshàn zhèngyào* (a Mongol-court dietitian's Inner-Asian-inflected cookbook) — show that the genre was capable of accommodating non-literati voices, with consequences for the cultural-historical interpretation of the genre.

- **Pseudepigraphy and recompilation.** The *Qínjīng* ([[KR3i0045]]) is a pseudonymous Sòng compilation falsely attributed to the Spring-and-Autumn musician Shī Kuàng with a falsely-attributed Zhāng Huá commentary; the *Qínshòu juélù* ([[KR3i0051]]) is a Qīng *jíyì* reconstruction of a Southern-Qí satirical work from quoted fragments. Both raise the standard textual-critical problems of the *pǔlù* corpus and of *Sìkù* recensions generally.

## Timeline

Works are listed at the date bracket of their composition / recension as given in each per-text note's frontmatter.

- **c. 400–490** — *Zhúpǔ* 竹譜 [[KR3i0040]] — the oldest text in the division; a Six-Dynasties bamboo-monograph in four-character verse, antedating the Tang–Sòng efflorescence.
- **c. 483–500** — *Qínshòu juélù* 禽獸決錄 [[KR3i0051]] — Southern-Qí satirical-allegorical *jué* by Biàn Bīn; survives only in fragments.
- **c. 760–780** — *Chájīng* 茶經 [[KR3i0019]] — Lù Yǔ's foundational tea-classic; the genre's seed.
- **c. 814–825** — *Jiānchá shuǐjì* 煎茶水記 [[KR3i0025]] — Zhāng Yòuxīn's late-Tang treatise on water for tea-brewing; bridge from Lù Yǔ to Sòng.
- **980–1001** — *Sǔnpǔ* 筍譜 [[KR3i0041]] — Zànníng's bamboo-shoot monograph; the genre's first Sòng appearance, by a Buddhist monk.
- **986** — *Wénfáng sìpǔ* 文房四譜 [[KR3i0002]] — Sū Yìjiǎn's foundational treatise on the studio's Four Treasures; structural template for later object-monographs.
- **c. 1031–1034** — *Luòyáng mǔdān jì* 洛陽牡丹記 [[KR3i0029]] — Ōuyáng Xiū's peony record; initiates the flower-treatise sub-genre.
- **1049–1064** — *Chálù* 茶錄 [[KR3i0020]] — Cài Xiāng's tea record, the first major Sòng tea-treatise.
- **1059** — *Lìzhī pǔ* 荔枝譜 [[KR3i0038]] — Cài Xiāng's foundational lychee monograph.

- **1064–1066** — *Dōngxī shìchá lù* 東溪試茶錄 [[KR3i0023]] — Sòng Zǐān's tea-testing notes from Dōngxī (Fújiàn).

- **c. 1250–1322** — *Chénshì xiāngpǔ* 陳氏香譜 [[KR3i0016]] — Chén Jìng's incense-treatise (recension partly Yuán).
- **c. 1259** — *Hǎitáng pǔ* 海棠譜 [[KR3i0037]] — Chén Sī's crab-apple monograph.
- **1330** — *Yǐnshàn zhèngyào* 飲膳正要 [[KR3i0043]] — Hū Sīhuì's Yuán imperial dietetic compendium, presented to Wénzōng; the principal Inner-Asian voice in the division.
- **c. 1330–1338** — *Mòshǐ* 墨史 [[KR3i0012]] — Lù Yǒu's history of ink-making and ink-makers.
- **1398** — *Mòfǎ jíyào* 墨法集要 [[KR3i0013]] — Shěn Jìsūn's essentials of ink-making; transition to the Míng technical tradition.
- **1544** — *Yìyú túzàn* 異魚圖贊 [[KR3i0048]] — Yáng Shèn's illustrated encomia on strange fishes, composed in Yúnnán exile.
- **c. 1618–1641** — *Xiāngshèng* 香乘 [[KR3i0017]] — Zhōu Jiāzhòu's late-Míng comprehensive incense-treatise, the largest single Chinese work on incense.
- **c. 1620–1625** — *Yìyú túzàn bǔ* 異魚圖贊補 [[KR3i0050]] — Hú Shìān's late-Míng supplement to Yáng Shèn.
- **1627** — *Qíqì túshuō* 奇器圖說 [[KR3i0001]] — Schreck and Wáng Zhēng's Jesuit-Chinese treatise on European mechanical engineering; categorial outlier.
- **1630** — *Yìyú túzàn jiān* 異魚圖贊箋 [[KR3i0049]] — Hú Shìān's annotated edition of Yáng Shèn.
- **1708** — *Yùdìng Pèiwénzhāi guǎng qúnfāng pǔ* 御定佩文齋廣羣芳譜 [[KR3i0044]] — Wāng Hào & Zhāng Yìshào's 100-*juàn* Kāngxī imperial flora; the largest single work in the division.
- **1717–1734** — *Xù chájīng* 續茶經 [[KR3i0024]] — Lù Tíngcàn's continuation of Lù Yǔ; integrates a millennium of post-Tang tea-literature.
- **1750–1751** — *Qiánlù* 錢錄 [[KR3i0014]] — Liáng Shīzhèng's Qiānlóng imperial coinage catalogue.
- **1778** — *Qīndìng Xīqīng yànpǔ* 欽定西清硯譜 [[KR3i0009]] — Yú Mǐnzhōng's Qiānlóng imperial inkstone catalogue; the latest work in the division.
