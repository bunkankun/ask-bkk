# Wǔ jīng zǒng yào 武經總要
*Comprehensive Essentials of Military Classics*
imperially commissioned; compiled by [[曾公亮]] (Zēng Gōngliàng, 998–1078, 宋, 奉敕撰) and [[丁度]] (Dīng Dù, 990–1053, 宋, 奉敕撰)

## About the work

A 40-juan Northern-Sòng comprehensive imperial military encyclopedia, commissioned by Rénzōng in *Bǎoyuán* 1 (1038) and completed *Qìnglì* 4 (1044). The work is the most comprehensive premodern Chinese military reference work — covering classical military theory, weapons technology, fortification, troop deployment, signals and communications, frontier strategy, supply systems, and astrological-divinatory military practice. Distinguished by extensive *tú* (illustrations) of military equipment, formations, and engineering — making the work one of the principal pre-modern Chinese illustrated technical references. Substantively the work synthesises the *Wǔ jīng qī shū* tradition (canonised under Shénzōng a generation later) with detailed practical-administrative material.

### Abstract

The *Wǔ jīng zǒng yào* is the comprehensive Northern-Sòng imperial military encyclopedia. Composition window: precisely datable. Commissioned 1038; completed 1044. The frontmatter brackets to 1038–1044.

The bibliographic record: *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*; *Wényuāngé shūmù*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Bīngjiā lèi*.
