# Hé bóshì bèi lùn 何博士備論
*Master Hé's Comprehensive Discourses*
by [[何去非]] (Hé Qùfēi, fl. 1082–1090, 宋)

## About the work

A two-juan Northern-Sòng military-historical essay-collection by Hé Qùfēi, taking specific historical military cases as occasions for strategic-doctrinal exposition. Composition: bracketed by Hé's *Wǔxué* tenure and Sū Shì's promotion-advocacy (1082–1090). The frontmatter brackets to 1082–1090.

### Abstract

The *Hé bóshì bèi lùn* is one of the principal Northern-Sòng military-historical works in the *jiào shǐ* (lessons-of-history) genre. The bibliographic record: *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Bīngjiā lèi*.
