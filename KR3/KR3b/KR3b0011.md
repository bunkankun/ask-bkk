# Tàibái yīn jīng 太白陰經
*The Hidden Classic of Venus (Great-White)*
by [[李筌]] (Lǐ Quán, fl. 759, 唐)

## About the work

An 8-juan Mid-Táng classical military-administrative treatise by Lǐ Quán, taking its title from the planet Venus (*Tàibái* — the Western military star). The work covers a wide range of military and administrative topics: from foundational *Lǐxué*-grounded military philosophy through specific tactical, logistical, and astrological-divination materials. Composition: shortly after the An-Lù-shān rebellion's suppression (763); the catalog meta gives *fl. 759*. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 759–770.

### Abstract

The *Tàibái yīn jīng* is one of the principal Mid-Táng military treatises and reflects the post-An-Lù-shān period's intensified concern with military reform and administrative reconstitution. The bibliographic record: *Xīn Táng shū yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Bīngjiā lèi*.
