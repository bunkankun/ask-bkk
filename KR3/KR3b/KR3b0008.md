# Sān lüè zhí jiě 三畧直解
*The Three Strategies, Directly Explained*
by [[劉寅]] (Liú Yín, 明)

## About the work

A 3-juan early-Míng commentary on the *Huángshígōng sān lüè* ([[KR3b0007]]), composed by Liú Yín in *Hóngwǔ* 4 (1371). The work provides phrase-by-phrase explanation in clear language, suitable for use as a military-classical textbook. Composition: precisely datable to 1371. The frontmatter brackets to 1371–1371.

### Abstract

The *Sān lüè zhí jiě* is the standard early-Míng commentary on the *Sān lüè*. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Bīngjiā lèi*.
