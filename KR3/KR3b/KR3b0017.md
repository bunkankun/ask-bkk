# Zhèn jì 陣紀
*Records of Battle Formations*
by [[何良臣]] (Hé Liángchén, *zì* Jìmíng 際明, 明)

## About the work

A 4-juan Late-Míng military-formations treatise by Hé Liángchén, in the *wōkòu* defence tradition of Táng Shùnzhī ([[KR3b0016]]) and Qī Jìguāng ([[KR3b0019]]). Composition: bracketed by the *wōkòu* crisis period (1550s–1590s). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1560–1600.

### Abstract

The *Zhèn jì* is one of the principal Late-Míng military-tactical treatises. The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Bīngjiā lèi*.
