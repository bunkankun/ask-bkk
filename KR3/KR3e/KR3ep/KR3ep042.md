# Chén Xīntián wàikē fāngàn 陳莘田外科方案
*Surgical Case-and-Prescription Records of Chén Xīntián*
by [[陳莘田]] Chén Xīntián 陳莘田 (mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgeon).

## About the work

A six-*juǎn* *wàikē* 外科 (external-medicine / surgical) casebook of the mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgical specialist Chén Xīntián. The opening of the hxwd `_000.txt` is a long mnemonic-verse compendium of *wàikē* prescriptions (*Wàikē bèiyòng zhūfāng* 外科備用諸方 — "all the prescriptions for surgical use") in seven-syllable rhyming couplets — a standard Qīng pedagogical format for committing prescription compositions to memory. The first prescription is *Zhēnrén huómìng yǐn* 真人活命飲 — the foundational late-Míng / Qīng surgical formula for *yōngjū* 癰疽 (abscesses and carbuncles) — with the mnemonic: *"Zhēnrén huómìng yòng yínhuā, Fángbái guī chén cǎojié jiā, bèimǔ tiānhuā jiā jiān rǔmò, chuānshān jiǎocì jiǔ jiān jiā"* (Real-person life-saving uses *jīnyínhuā*; add *fángfēng*, *báizhǐ*, *dāngguī*, *chénpí*, *gāncǎo jié*; *bèimǔ*, *tiānhuā fěn* added with *rǔxiāng* and *mòyào*; *chuānshānjiǎ*, *zàojiǎo cì*, wine-decocted, excellent). The verse-mnemonic format extends through more than thirty prescriptions covering the entire Qīng *wàikē* repertoire.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` opens directly with the *Wàikē bèiyòng zhūfāng* mnemonic-verse compendium; no standalone preface precedes it. *No formal preface in `_000.txt`.*

### Abstract

Chén Xīntián 陳莘田 — mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgeon, conventionally dated to the early-to-mid nineteenth century (Dàoguāng / Xiánfēng era). Biographical data are thin; he is treated in modern Chinese surveys as one of the principal Sūzhōu *wàikē* practitioners of his generation. CBDB has no entry. The composition window 1820–1880 reflects his conventional floruit.

The work is one of several mid-to-late Qīng Sūzhōu surgical casebooks — a regional specialism of the Sūzhōu medical tradition that descends from Chén Shígōng's 陳實功 Míng-period *Wàikē zhèngzōng* 外科正宗 (1617) and from the Qiánlóng-era imperially-commissioned *Wàikē xīnfǎ yàojué* 外科心法要訣 of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* 醫宗金鑑. The verse-mnemonic compendium at the head of the work is a pedagogical companion to the cases proper, allowing students to commit the standard formulas to memory.
