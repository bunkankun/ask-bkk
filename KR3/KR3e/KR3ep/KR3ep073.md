# Miù Sōngxīn yīàn 繆松心醫案
*Medical Case Records of Miù Sōngxīn*
by [[繆遵義]] Miù Zūnyì (Sōngxīn 松心, 1710–1793), of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* casebook of the mid-Qīng Sūzhōu physician Miù Zūnyì (*hào* Sōngxīn 松心), organised by syndrome. The text overlaps in clinical signature and patient-source-area with the closely related compilation [[KR3ep075]] *Sōngxīn yīàn bǐjì* and may represent an independent transcription of overlapping case-material.

### Abstract

Miù Zūnyì 繆遵義 (CBDB 91312, 1710–1793), *hào* Sōngxīn 松心 (also *Tiānshū* 天樞 in some sources), of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu, Jiāngsū). A *Qiánlóng yǐchǒu* (1745) *jìnshì*; held minor official posts before turning to medicine in middle age. He was a contemporary of Yè Tiānshì in Sūzhōu and is sometimes grouped with the other major Sūzhōu late-Yè-school masters.

The cases proceed through 喘 (gasping respiration), 不寐 (insomnia), and other internal-medicine categories. The prescribing signature — *Dàncài / Bāxìng / Kǎnqì / Màidōng / Chuānbèi*; *Báishā fāng* (white-sand formula of *Hǎigéké*, *Bái fúshí*, *Chántuì*, *Jìyúdǎn* with silver) — is highly distinctive and identifies Miù as a meticulous practitioner of the Sūzhōu Yè-school light-and-precise prescribing style. The opening *Chuǎn* case explicitly invokes the *Nèijīng* yángqiáo / *bùmèi* (yáng-vessel insomnia) doctrine — a textbook display of canonical-doctrinal anchoring of Yè-school clinical practice.

The composition window 1740–1793 reflects Miù's mature clinical decades through to his death.
