# Xù míngyī lèiàn (Sìkù běn) 續名醫類案（四庫本）
*Continuation of the Categorised Case Records of Famous Physicians — Imperial Library Edition*
by [[魏之琇]] Wèi Zhīxiù 魏之琇 (1722–1772).

## About the work

The Sìkù quánshū 四庫全書 recension of the *Xù míngyī lèiàn* — Wèi Zhīxiù's great mid-Qīng casebook anthology described in detail in [[KR3ep017]]. The *Sìkù* edition is shorter and more rigorously edited than the longer hxwd recension preserved as [[KR3ep017]]: it runs to 36 *juǎn* against the longer recension's 60, the editorial cuts effected by the imperial editors in the course of admission to the *Sìkù quánshū* in the 1770s–1780s (the *Sìkù* collection was completed in 1782). Editorial differences between the two recensions are extensively documented in modern Chinese reference works.

This entry is paired with [[KR3ep017]] (the longer non-*Sìkù* recension). The two are catalogued separately because they represent meaningfully different textual states of the work. The composition window 1745–1782 reflects Wèi's lifetime work-period as lower bound and the *Sìkù* completion date as upper bound.

### Abstract

For the substantive textual and biographical discussion see [[KR3ep017]]. The principal difference between the *Sìkù* recension and the longer hxwd recension is editorial scale: the imperial editors of the 1770s–1780s pruned the work to 36 *juǎn* and re-organised the categories, removing some cases altogether and condensing the editorial introductions to the syndrome-classes. The Wáng Mèngyīng 王孟英 = [[王士雄] mid-nineteenth-century edited version (the basis of most subsequent Qīng reprints, including those of the late-Qīng *Yītǒng zhèngmài quánshū* 醫統正脈全書 type) follows the longer pre-*Sìkù* recension.
