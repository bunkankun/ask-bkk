# Sōngxīn yīàn bǐjì 松心醫案筆記
*Notebook of Sōngxīn's Medical Case Records*
by [[繆遵義]] Miù Zūnyì (Sōngxīn 松心, 1710–1793), as transcribed by [[董受芝]] Dǒng Shòuzhī (Republican period).

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* manuscript transcription of further cases of [[繆遵義]] Miù Sōngxīn (1710–1793), recovered by the Jiāxīng 嘉禾 physician [[董受芝]] Dǒng Shòuzhī from a manuscript he obtained "from a Sūzhōu book-dealer," and prepared for publication with a preface by [[張龢芬]] Zhāng Héfēn of Cíxī 慈溪 dated *guǐchǒu dōngrì* (1913, winter).

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` carries a preface signed *Cíxī Zhāng Héfēn shū yú Hǎi-* (the rest cut off in the preview) dated *guǐchǒu dōngrì* (1913). The preface frames the work in the medical-historiographical lineage of [[古今醫案]] *Gǔjīn yīàn* (Yú Zhèn), [[名醫類案]] *Míngyī lèiàn* (Jiāng Guàn), [[葉氏指南]] *Yèshì zhǐnán* (Yè Tiānshì), *Wújí Sānjiā yīàn* (Wú Jīnshòu's compilation = [[KR3ep020]]), [[吳門治驗錄]] *Wúmén zhìyàn lù*, and the works of [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng of Qiánzhāi 潛齋 and Liǔ Bǎoyí 柳寶詒 of Jiāngyīn 江陰 ([[KR3ep021]], [[KR3ep022]]). Zhāng praises the recovered Miù manuscript as a "secret formula of the dragon palace" 龍宮秘方, with discussion as penetrating as Yù Jiāyán's and clinical agility as alive as Yè Tiānshì's, and notes that "the *àn* (cases) mention 'my own clan brother *Zhòngchún* 仲醇'," confirming the author's surname as **Miù** 繆 — the same family as the Míng physician Miù Xīyōng 繆希雍 (字 Zhòngchún 仲醇, 1546–1627).

### Abstract

The text is a Republican-period salvage publication of mid-Qīng case-material — Miù Zūnyì's clinical practice (c. 1740–1793) finally reaching print in 1913 through Dǒng Shòuzhī's manuscript acquisition. The composition window 1740–1913 brackets the original clinical period through the Republican-period editorial assembly.

Together with [[KR3ep073]] *Miù Sōngxīn yīàn*, the present work constitutes the principal documentary record of Miù Zūnyì's clinical practice. The two texts overlap in some cases but each preserves material not found in the other; collation of the two yields the most complete Miù Sōngxīn casebook.
