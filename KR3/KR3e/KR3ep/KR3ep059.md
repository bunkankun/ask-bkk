# Wáng Zhòngqí yīàn 王仲奇醫案
*Medical Case Records of Wáng Zhòngqí*
by [[王仲奇]] Wáng Zhòngqí (1881–1945), of Shèxiàn 歙縣 (Ānhuī).

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* casebook of the early-twentieth-century Xīnān 新安 school physician Wáng Zhòngqí — one of the most influential Shànghǎi practitioners of the 1920s–1930s. The 漢學文典 source preserves the case-records without preface, the patient-name and address given in canonical Shànghǎi street form (汪，唐家弄; 王，十六浦; etc.) — confirming the Shànghǎi setting.

### Abstract

Wáng Zhòngqí 王仲奇 (1881–1945), *míng* Jīnjié 金杰, of Shèxiàn 歙縣 (the Huīzhōu / Xīnān homeland in southern Ānhuī), descended from a long-established Xīnān 新安 medical lineage. He moved his practice to Shànghǎi in the early Republican period and built a major clinical reputation in the foreign-concession period, alongside [[丁甘仁]] Dīng Gānrén, [[陳蓮舫]] Chén Liánfǎng, [[費繩甫]] Fèi Shéngfǔ, and Cáo Yǐngfǔ [[曹穎甫]].

The casebook represents Wáng's mature clinical signature: **Yè-school light-and-pacifying** prescribing infused with the Xīnān school's characteristic attention to *gānyīn* / *shènyīn* (liver-yin / kidney-yin) deficiency. The opening cases address throat-pain with *xuányōng* descent (懸壅下垂) and liver-gall-bladder *qì*-transversal-blockage of stomach-descent, treated with light *hǎigéfěn / chuānbèimǔ / qīngdài / shíhú* combinations and *xuánfùhuā / dàizhěshí / fǎbànxià* for the *gānwèi* axis. This is a textbook Xīnān-meets-Yè-school clinical compendium.

The composition window 1900–1945 reflects Wáng's mature clinical decades through to his death. The cases were collected by his disciples; first major Republican printing in the early 1930s, later widely reproduced in twentieth-century Chinese-medical compilations.
