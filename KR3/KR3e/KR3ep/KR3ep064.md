# Línzhěn yīàn 臨診醫案
*Bedside Medical Case Records*
Anonymous compiler.

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* anonymous mid-Qīng casebook from the Wúsōng 吳淞 / Shànghǎi region. The cases are exactly dated; the opening case carries the precise date *Dàoguāng dīngyǒu shíyī yuè shísì rì* (Dàoguāng 17 = 14 November 1837) and is set on board the *Shī Chángshùn shāchuán* 施長順沙船 (a sand-junk in the Wúsōng / Yangtze-mouth coastal trade) — providing an unusually firm *terminus ante quem* and an unusual maritime clinical setting.

### Abstract

The casebook is set on the maritime trade route off Wúsōng — the patient-list includes 朱象兄 (a passenger / shāchuán client) at 35 *suì*, 浦大兄 of 吳淞南門外 (outside the south gate of Wúsōng), 浦良兄 of 吳淞 (Wúsōng), and 朱大兄 of 吳淞. The compiler is unnamed but is clearly a working clinician of the lower Yangtze coastal-trade community.

The clinical signature — careful pulse-and-tongue notation, light-aromatic-cleansing prescriptions for *dōngwēn* (冬溫, winter-warmth), *xiéshí mēngzǔ* (邪食蒙阻, blocked food-pathogens at middle-burner) — is characteristic of the late-Yè-school Jiāng-Zhè urban tradition. The 1837 date situates the text in the lower-Yangtze medical milieu of the Dàoguāng period, contemporary with [[李冠仙]] Lǐ Guànxiān ([[KR3ep060]]). The composition window 1837–1880 reflects an active clinical career from the dated opening case to a presumed mid-Tóngzhì / Guāngxù compilation.
