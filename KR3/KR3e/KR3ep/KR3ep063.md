# Yè Tiānshì Cáo Rénbó Hé Yuáncháng yīàn 葉天士曹仁伯何元長醫案
*Combined Medical Case Records of Yè Tiānshì, Cáo Rénbó, and Hé Yuáncháng*
by [[葉桂]] Yè Guì (Tiānshì 天士, 1666–1745), [[曹存心]] Cáo Cúnxīn (Rénbó 仁伯, 1767–1834), and [[何元長]] Hé Yuáncháng (1752–1806).

## About the work

A three-*juǎn* composite casebook that joins selections from the case material of three of the most important Qīng *wēnbìng* / *biànzhèng* physicians of the Sūzhōu region across more than a century: **Yè Tiānshì** (founder of the *wēnbìng* school proper), **Cáo Cúnxīn** (Rénbó, of Chángshú), and **Hé Yuáncháng** of Sūzhōu. The arrangement is by syndrome (春溫門, 三十三方; 風溫門; etc.).

### Abstract

The text belongs to a Qīng tradition of *sānjiā / sìjiā yīàn* (three-family / four-family casebook) compilations — most famously [[KR3ep020]] *Sānjiā yīàn hékè* of [[吳金壽]] Wú Jīnshòu (1830) — that thematically arrange syndrome-grouped selections of cases by major Sūzhōu-school masters for didactic purposes. The present compilation appears to be an anonymous nineteenth-century editorial selection (compiler not identified in the source) drawing from:

1. **Yè Guì 葉桂** (Yè Tiānshì, 1666–1745) — the founder of the *wēnbìng* school proper; cases drawn principally from the *Línzhèng zhǐnán yīàn* ([[KR3ep010]]) and related Yè-school sources.
2. **Cáo Cúnxīn 曹存心** (Rénbó, 1767–1834) — the leading Sūzhōu / Chángshú physician of the late-Qiánlóng to Jiāqìng period; cases drawn from [[KR3ep025]] *Cáo Rénbó yīàn lùn*, [[KR3ep069]] *Cáo Rénbó yīàn*, and related materials.
3. **Hé Yuáncháng 何元長** (1752–1806) — the leading early-Jiāqìng Sūzhōu physician of the Hé-family medical lineage; one of the "Hé-family three" of [[何琬亭]] Hé Wǎntíng, Hé Yuáncháng, and [[何書山]] Hé Shūshān, ancestors of the famous Ménghé Hé-family clinicians.

The composition window 1720–1880 reflects the combined active dates of the three physicians plus a probable mid-to-late-nineteenth-century editorial compilation. The *sānjiā* format makes the text a heavily-used didactic resource in Republican-era Chinese-medical schools.
