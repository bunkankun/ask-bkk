# Běncǎo Zhèngyì 本草正義
*The Correct Sense of the Materia Medica*
by [[張壽頤]] (Zhāng Shòuyí, *zì* Shānléi 山雷, 1873–1934, 民國)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo zhèngyì* is the principal Republican-era critical pharmacopoeia and the immediate predecessor of the modern PRC TCM materia medica curriculum. Zhāng Shòuyí composed it from his pharmacology lectures at the *Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānménxuéxiào* 蘭谿中醫專門學校 during the 1910s and 1920s; the work was printed in 1932, two years before his death. The title — *zhèng yì* "correct sense" — programmatically asserts that the centuries of late-imperial pharmacological commentary had introduced errors and inconsistencies, and that Zhāng's purpose is to restore the *correct* doctrine of each substance.

The work covers approximately 270 of the most clinically important substances. For each, Zhāng's method is critical-historical: he gives the *Běnjīng* core text, surveys the major commentaries from Táo Hóngjǐng [[陶弘景]] through the Sòng catalogers ([[KR3ec009]]) to the Qīng *jīngfāng* school (Zōu Shù [[鄒澍]], Zhōu Yán [[周巖]]), adjudicates the major disputes, and offers his own assessment grounded in *jīngfāng* clinical experience. The result is the most theoretically careful pharmacopoeia of its generation, and the principal pre-PRC bridge between the late-imperial and the modern TCM pharmacological literature.

### Abstract

Zhāng Shòuyí (張壽頤, 1873–1934, no confident CBDB id), *zì* Shānléi 山雷. Native of Jiādìng 嘉定 (now in Shànghǎi). He was a key figure in the early-Republican movement to establish formal Chinese-medicine colleges: he taught at the Lánxī school from c. 1916 onwards, training a generation of physicians who carried the *jīngfāng* tradition into the PRC era. He also published the *Zhōngfēng kǎo* 中風斠詮 (a major treatise on stroke pathology) and the *Shānléi yīxué cóngshū* 山雷醫學叢書 collection.

The *Zhèngyì*'s significance is as the principal Republican-era pharmacopoeia and the textual model for the standard PRC *Zhōngyào xué* 中藥學 college textbooks compiled from the 1950s onwards. The substance entries in the major modern textbooks (the various editions of the *Zhōngyào xué* national teaching plan) follow Zhāng's organisational principles and frequently cite his adjudications of doctrinal dispute.
