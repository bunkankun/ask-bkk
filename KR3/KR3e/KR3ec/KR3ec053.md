# Běncǎo Sībiàn Lù 本草思辨錄
*Record of Reflective Discussion on the Materia Medica*
by [[周巖]] (Zhōu Yán, *zì* Bódù 伯度, *hào* Lùqǐ Shānrén 鹿起山人, 1832 – early 20th c., 清)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo sībiàn lù* is the principal late-Qīng *jīngfāng* 經方 (classical-formula school) pharmacology, printed in 4 *juǎn* in 光緒三十年 (1904). Zhōu Yán wrote it as a substance-by-substance critical examination of the drugs used in Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Shānghán lùn* and *Jīnguì yàolüè* — the canonical *jīngfāng* corpus. His method is *sī biàn* 思辨 ("reflective discussion"): for each substance he canvasses the *Běnjīng* core text, the major commentarial positions ([[劉若金]] Liú Ruòjīn, Zōu Shù [[鄒澍]], Zhāng Zhìcōng [[張志聰]], Chén Niànzǔ [[陳念祖]] etc.), and then offers his own assessment grounded in Zhòngjǐng's actual formula usage.

The work covers approximately 130 substances — a deliberately compact selection limited to the *ShānghánJīnguì* core. This places the *Sī biàn lù* squarely in the Chángzhōu / Shàoxīng *jīngfāng* tradition that runs from [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng through [[柯琴]] Kē Qín, [[徐彬]] Xú Bīn, Zōu Shù, Yáng Shítài [[楊時泰]] ([[KR3ec052]]), and culminates in Zhōu Yán in the early 20th century. Its critical engagement with Zōu Shù's *Běnjīng shū zhèng* ([[KR3ec066]]) is particularly close — Zhōu Yán frequently agrees with Zōu in principle but corrects him on specific clinical readings.

### Prefaces

The local repository preserves the substance entries. Standard editions preserve Zhōu Yán's own preface (光緒甲辰 = 1904) explaining the *jīngfāng* programme, plus a disciple's postface.

### Abstract

Zhōu Yán (周巖, 1832 – early 20th c., no confident CBDB id), *zì* Bódù 伯度, *hào* Lùqǐ Shānrén 鹿起山人, native of Shānyīn 山陰 (Shàoxīng, Zhèjiāng). *Jǔrén*; held minor county-level posts in Húběi before withdrawing to clinical practice. He came to medicine relatively late, in his forties.

The *Sī biàn lù*'s significance is as the most theoretically careful late-Qīng *jīngfāng*-grounded pharmacology and as the principal bridge between the mid-19th-century Chángzhōu pharmacological school (Zōu Shù, Yáng Shítài) and the Republican-era *jīngfāng* revival led by [[曹穎甫]] Cáo Yǐngfǔ and Zhāng Shòuyí [[張壽頤]] ([[KR3ec063]]). The work has been continuously in print since 1904 and remains a standard reference in modern *jīngfāng* teaching curricula.
