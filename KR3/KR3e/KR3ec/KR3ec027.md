# Běncǎo Zhēngyào 本草徵要
*Materia Medica with Verification-of-Essentials*
by [[李中梓]] (Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, *zì* Shìcái 士材, *hào* Niànémǐ 念莪, 1588–1655, 明)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo zhēngyào* is one of the principal late-Míng pharmacological textbooks, written by Lǐ Zhōngzǐ — a leading clinician of the post-Miù-Xīyōng generation and a major figure in the consolidation of *yīnbǔ* (yin-tonification) clinical theory. The work, in 2 *juǎn*, gives concise drug-by-drug entries — abbreviated for clinical use — with each substance presented with its *xìngwèiguījīngzhǔzhì* (nature, taste, channel-entry, main effect) under a standardised heading. It is part of Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's larger pedagogical project, which also includes the *Yīzōng bì dú* 醫宗必讀, *Shìcái sān shū* 士材三書, and other texts.

The work was widely used in late-Míng / Qīng medical education and is the immediate ancestor of the Qīng-dynasty pocket-format pharmacological texts.

### Prefaces

The 漢學文典 transmitted text preserves Lǐ Zhōngzǐ's autograph preface, dating the composition to the early 1640s.

### Abstract

Lǐ Zhōngzǐ (李中梓), *zì* Shìcái 士材, *hào* Niànémǐ 念莪, 1588–1655, native of Yúnjiān 雲間 (modern Sōngjiāng 松江, Shànghǎi). He came from a literate Jiāngnán family — his father Lǐ Shàngyàng 李尚陽 was a Wànlì-era *jǔrén* — and trained in classical Confucian learning before turning to medicine after his elder brother's death from an iatrogenic error. He practised in Sōngjiāng and built a major reputation through his teaching at the Lóngfū 龍府 (Píngdōng wáng) Princely Court.

He is the master of the *Shìcái xuépài* 士材學派 — a clinical tradition emphasising *bǔ pí shèn* 補脾腎 (tonifying spleen and kidney) — and his disciples include the major early-Qīng physicians Shěn Lǎngzhōng 沈朗仲 and Mǎ Yuántái 馬元台. His works in this lineage include:

1. *Yīzōng bì dú* 醫宗必讀 (1637) — the foundational textbook;
2. *Zhěnjiā zhèng yǎn* 診家正眼 (on pulse);
3. *Běncǎo tōngxuán* 本草通玄 ([[KR3ec032]]) — a major pharmacology;
4. *Běncǎo zhēngyào* 本草徵要 ([[KR3ec027]]) — the pocket-format pharmacology;
5. *Léi gōng páozhì yào xìng jiě* 雷公炮製藥性解 ([[KR3ec029]]) — a processing-and-properties text;
6. *Nèi jīng zhī yào* 內經知要 — on the *Sùwèn*;
7. *Shāng hán kuò yào* 傷寒括要 — on the *Shānghán lùn*.

CBDB id for Lǐ Zhōngzǐ is not confidently assigned. The lifedates 1588–1655 are securely attested in Sōngjiāng local gazetteers and in the prefaces to his own works.

The *Zhēngyào* is significant as a clinically focused pharmacology that abstracted the *Gāngmù* corpus into a usable bedside reference. Like Wāng Áng's *Běncǎo bèiyào* ([[KR3ec038]]) two generations later, it served the practical need of clinicians for a shorter and more focused text than Lǐ Shízhēn's massive encyclopedia.
