# Yào Yào Fēn Jì 要藥分劑
*The Essential Drugs, Classified by Preparation*
by [[沈金鰲]] (Shěn Jīn'áo, *zì* Qínyuán 芹園, *hào* Zūnshēng lǎorén 尊生老人, 1717–1776, 清)

## About the work

The *Yào yào fēn jì* is one part of Shěn Jīn'áo's massive 70-*juǎn* *Shěn shì zūnshēng shū* 沈氏尊生書 (1773), a comprehensive medical encyclopedia. The pharmacological component covers 449 commonly-used drugs in 10 *juǎn*, organised by *jì* 劑 — preparation-type / therapeutic action — into ten major classes: 宣 (dispersing), 通 (unblocking), 補 (tonifying), 泄 (draining), 輕 (light/raising), 重 (heavy/anchoring), 澀 (astringing), 滑 (lubricating), 燥 (drying), 濕 (moistening). Each substance is presented under its principal therapeutic *jì* and discussed in relation to that action. This classification by *jì* is a distinctive contribution; most prior pharmacologies organise by botanical category or by *Běnjīng* three-grade structure.

The work was widely studied in the late Qīng for its clinical clarity and is referenced in Tāng Zōnghǎi [[唐宗海]]'s *Běncǎo wèn dá* ([[KR3ec056]]).

### Prefaces

The local repository preserves the substance entries. Shěn Jīn'áo's preface to the parent *Zūnshēng shū* (1773) is preserved in the standard edition.

### Abstract

Shěn Jīn'áo (沈金鰲, 1717–1776, CBDB 79676), *zì* Qínyuán 芹園, *hào* Zūnshēng lǎorén 尊生老人, native of Wúxī 無錫 (Jiāngsū). He was a *jǔrén* who served briefly as a local educational officer (*xùndǎo* 訓導) before retiring to medical practice. His *Shěn shì zūnshēng shū* (1773) is one of the most comprehensive late-Qīng medical syntheses, covering pharmacology (*Yào yào fēn jì*), gynaecology, paediatrics, internal medicine, *Shānghán*, and other domains in 72 *juǎn*. The pharmacological component is part of a larger pedagogical vision.

The work is significant for its therapeutic-action-based organisational principle, which anticipates the modern *zhōngyī* curriculum's reorganisation of pharmacology around clinical function.
