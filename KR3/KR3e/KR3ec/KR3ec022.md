# Zhèngzhì Běncǎo 證治本草
*Materia Medica for Diagnosis and Treatment*
by [[陸之柷]] (Lù Zhīzhù, *fl.* Míng, dates unknown)

## About the work

The *Zhèngzhì běncǎo* is a Míng-dynasty pharmacopoeia organised by clinical presentation (證, pattern; 治, treatment) rather than by substance category. The catalog assigns it to Lù Zhīzhù 陸之柷 and dates it 明. The work belongs to the genre of *zhèngzhì* (diagnosis-treatment) pharmacology that flourished in the late Míng — texts that re-arrange the substance-based corpus of the *Zhènglèi*/*Gāngmù* tradition by disease category, making them more accessible for bedside use.
