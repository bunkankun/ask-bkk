# Běncǎo Chóngyuán 本草崇原
*Revering the Origin in the Materia Medica*
begun by [[張志聰]] (Zhāng Zhìcōng, *zì* Yǐn'ān 隱菴, *c.* 1610 – *c.* 1695, 清); completed posthumously by his disciple [[高世栻]] (Gāo Shìshì, *zì* Shìyù 士御, *fl.* late 17th c., 清)

## About the work

The *Běncǎo chóngyuán* is the major pharmacological work of the late-17th-century Hángzhōu *Lúshān* 侶山 medical academy founded by Zhāng Zhìcōng — one of the principal centres of late-Míng / early-Qīng *Sùwèn-Língshū*-grounded clinical theory. Zhāng began the work but died before completing it; his leading disciple Gāo Shìshì took up the manuscript and brought it to completion in the late 17th century. The catalog meta correctly captures this: 著者 張志聰 歿而書未成，後由弟子 高世栻 續成 ("the author Zhāng Zhìcōng died before the work was finished, and it was subsequently completed by his disciple Gāo Shìshì").

The work in 3 *juǎn* organises substances by the *Běnjīng*'s original three-grade structure (上中下品) — *chóng yuán* 崇原 ("revering the origin") explicitly returns to the *Běnjīng*'s authority against the *Gāngmù*'s reorganisation. Each substance entry combines the *Běnjīng* core text with Zhāng's exegetical commentary grounded in *zàngfǔ* doctrine and Hangzhou-school theoretical principles.

### Prefaces

The local repository preserves the substance body. Standard editions preserve Gāo Shìshì's preface describing how he took up the master's incomplete manuscript and brought it to completion.

### Abstract

Zhāng Zhìcōng (張志聰, *c.* 1610 – *c.* 1695, no confident CBDB id), *zì* Yǐn'ān 隱菴, native of Qiántáng 錢唐 (Hángzhōu). He was the founder of the Lúshān 侶山 medical academy in Hángzhōu — one of the most intellectually serious early-Qīng medical teaching institutions. His students included Gāo Shìshì, Zhāng Lìfēng 張理風, Yè Zhēn 葉震, and a generation of Jiāngnán physicians.

Gāo Shìshì (高世栻, CBDB 438036 — no confident dates), *zì* Shìyù 士御, was Zhāng Zhìcōng's leading disciple and the principal Qīng exponent of the Lúshān doctrines. After Zhāng's death he completed multiple of his teacher's unfinished works, including the *Běncǎo chóngyuán* and the *Sùwèn jí zhù* 素問集注.

The work's significance is as the principal Lúshān pharmacological text. The Lúshān school's distinctive contribution to Qīng medicine is the integration of *Sùwèn-Língshū* theory with clinical practice via close substance-by-substance pharmacological exegesis. The *Chóngyuán* embodies this approach.
