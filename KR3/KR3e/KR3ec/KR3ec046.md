# Shénnóng Běncǎo Jīng Dú 神農本草經讀
*Reading the Shennong Materia Medica Classic*
by [[陳念祖]] (Chén Niànzǔ, *zì* Xiūyuán 修園, 1753–1823, 清)

## About the work

The *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng dú* is the influential late-Qīng *Běnjīng* commentary by Chén Niànzǔ — one of the most prolific medical popularizers of the Qiánlóng-Jiāqìng era and the founder of the *Xiūyuán* publishing school of *zhōngyī* textbooks. The work in 4 *juǎn* (completed 1803, Jiāqìng 8) gives the *Běnjīng* core text substance by substance and provides clear, pedagogical exegesis with Chén's own clinical comments. It is one of the principal early-19th-century pharmacologies and the immediate ancestor of much of the 20th-century *zhōngyī* curriculum.

Chén's pharmacology is distinctively classicist: he privileges the *Běnjīng* text over later accretions, and explicitly criticises both Wāng Áng [[汪昂]]'s *Bèiyào* tradition and the *kǎojù*-tradition of Sūn Xīngyǎn's [[孫星衍]] *Běnjīng* reconstruction for their respective failures. His own approach grounds pharmacology in the *Sùwèn-Língshū* framework while reading the *Běnjīng* as canonical scripture.

### Abstract

Chén Niànzǔ (陳念祖, 1753–1823, CBDB 81917), *zì* Xiūyuán 修園, native of Chánglè 長樂 (modern Fújiàn). He was a *jǔrén* who served briefly as Hébǎo zhīxiàn 河北知縣 before retiring to teach medicine at Fúzhōu. His pedagogical output is enormous — including the standard reading textbooks *Yīxué shí zài yì* 醫學實在易, *Shānghán lùn qiǎn zhù* 傷寒論淺注, *Yī yī ǒu cuì* 醫醫偶萃, *Niǔ wàn lùn* 女科要旨, *Yīxué cóng zhòng lù* 醫學從眾錄, and others — and his *Xiūyuán quánshū* 修園全書 served as the standard physician textbook for two centuries.

The *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng dú* is the principal element of Chén's pharmacology. It established the model of reading the *Běnjīng* itself as a canonical text rather than the modern *Gāngmù* corpus — a methodological move that anticipated the 20th-century *zhōngyī* curriculum's return to classical sources.
