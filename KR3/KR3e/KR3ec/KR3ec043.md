# Běn Jīng Féng Yuán 本經逢原
*Returning to the Source of the Original Canon*
by [[張璐]] (Zhāng Lù, *zì* Lùyǔ 路玉, *hào* Shíwán lǎorén 石頑老人, 1617–1700, 清)

## About the work

The *Běn jīng féng yuán* is the late-17th-century pharmacological exegesis by Zhāng Lù — one of the three founding fathers of the Qīng *Yīzōng* (medical-lineage) school alongside Yù Chāng 喻昌 and Lǐ Zhōngzǐ [[李中梓]]. Completed in Kāngxī 34 (1695), the work organises substances by the *Gāngmù*-derived classification but with a self-conscious return to *Běnjīng* (神農本草經) categories as the authoritative source — hence the title "Returning to the Origin of the Canon". Each substance entry gives the *Běnjīng* core text followed by Zhāng's clinical commentary integrating Lǐ Shízhēn [[李時珍]], Miù Xīyōng [[繆希雍]], the *Yìshuǐ-Dōngyuán* tradition, and Zhāng's own clinical observation.

The work was widely used as a Qīng clinical reference and is the immediate ancestor of Yáo Qiú's [[姚球]] *Běncǎo jīng jiě* ([[KR3ec044]]). Zhāng Lù's broader works include the *Yīzōng tì miàn* 醫宗弟子問 / *Yīzōng huì miàn* and the *Zhāng shì yī tōng* 張氏醫通, the latter being the most influential Qīng general internal-medicine reference.

### Prefaces

The transmitted text preserves Zhāng's autograph preface (date 康熙三十四年 = 1695) and editorial *fán lì*.

### Abstract

Zhāng Lù (張璐, 1617–1700) — see his existing person note. The *Běn jīng féng yuán* is part of Zhāng's program to *re-anchor* Qīng clinical practice in the canonical pre-Sòng pharmacology, against what he saw as the over-elaboration of *Gāngmù*-tradition citation pharmacology. It is the principal late-17th-century *kǎojù*-inclined pharmacology.
