### Abstract

Yáng Shìyíng 楊士瀛 (字 登父, *hào* Rénzhāi 仁齋) was a Húaí'ān 懷安 (福建) physician of the mid-Southern Sòng. His magnum opus is the *Rénzhāi zhí zhǐ fāng* 仁齋直指方 (twenty-six juan, 1264), one of the principal Sòng formulary-and-syndromic compendia, which integrates the Sòng-period clinical synthesis with the *Jú fāng* 局方 commercial-formulary heritage. The *Chá mài zǒng kuò* is the pulse component of Yáng's curriculum, smaller in scope than the *Zhí zhǐ fāng* and likely composed as a teaching extract. Because the jicheng.tw file is essentially empty, this entry is unable to engage with the textual evidence directly; the work is reprinted in modern editions of Yáng's collected medical works (e.g., *Yáng Rénzhāi yī xué quán shū* 楊仁齋醫學全書, Beijing: Zhōngguó zhōngyīyào, 2006).
