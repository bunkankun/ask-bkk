# Bīn Hú Mài Xué Mài Jué 瀕湖脈學脈訣
*Pulse Songs from the Lakeside Pulse Studies*
by [[李時珍]] (Lǐ Shízhēn, *zì* Dōngbì 東璧, *hào* Bīnhú shānrén 瀕湖山人, 1518–1593, 明)

## About the work

A satellite catalog-entry pointing to the **mnemonic-verse portion** of Lǐ Shízhēn's *Bīnhú mài xué* — the *tǐzhuàng shī* 體狀詩, *xiānglèi shī* 相類詩, and *zhǔbìng shī* 主病詩 sets that accompany each of the twenty-seven pulse-types in the main work. The jicheng.tw corpus duplicates the title in catalog metadata but the corresponding body file `KR3eb015_001.txt` is an empty stub. All textual content is contained in [[KR3eb014]] `KR3eb014_000.txt`; see that entry for the substantive description, dating, and bibliography.

### Prefaces

*No text in the jicheng.tw file.* The work-cluster's preface is the one preserved in [[KR3eb014]], dated *Míng Jiājìng jiǎzǐ shàngyuán rì* = first-month full-moon day of 1564.

### Abstract

This catalog entry exists in the jicheng.tw index as a structural pointer; the main text and the *mài jué* component are not separable in the original manuscript tradition. The reason for the doubled entry is the long-standing Chinese editorial convention of treating the pulse-verses as a teaching-extract suitable for independent recitation; some Qing prints separate them out as *Bīnhú mài jué* 瀕湖脈訣 (deliberately echoing the pseudo-Wáng-Shūhé title that Lǐ Shízhēn was at pains to displace). For all substantive content see [[KR3eb014]].
