# Mài Guàn 脈貫
*The Pulse Threaded Together*
by [[王賢]] (Wáng Xián, *zì* Xuéyuān 學淵, *fl.* Kāngxī, 清)

## About the work

A nine-juan early-Kāngxī pulse synthesis by Wáng Xián. The book title — *guàn* = "threaded through" — names its central project: integrating the entire received pulse-doctrinal heritage into a single continuous exposition. The juan structure proceeds from foundational definitions and the *Nèijīng* pulse-theoretic framework (juan 1), through pulse-position and method (juan 2–3), the twenty-eight pulse types (juan 4–5), pulse-by-disease correlations (juan 6–7), and pulse-prognosis (juan 8–9). The work cites — and where necessary corrects — Wáng Shūhé [[KR3eb011]], Cuī Jiāyàn [[KR3eb025]], Huá Shòu [[KR3eb023]], Lǐ Shízhēn [[KR3eb014]] / [[KR3eb019]], Lǐ Zhōngzǐ [[KR3eb020]], Lǐ Yángāng [[KR3eb018]], and Zhāng Lù [[KR3eb022]].

### Abstract

Wáng Xián 王賢 (字 學淵, 號 筆林 Bǐlín) was an early-Kāngxī physician of Yùtián 玉田 (Hèběi) — though some bibliographic records place him in Húběi. He is otherwise lightly documented; no CBDB record. The *Mài guàn* is his principal surviving work and one of the more comprehensive single-author early-Qing pulse syntheses, comparable in scope to Lǐ Yángāng's ten-juan [[KR3eb018]] *Mài jué huì biàn* (1664–66) but more conservative in its approach (where Lǐ Yángāng demolishes the pseudo-*Mài jué* line by line, Wáng Xián incorporates the polemic as background and focuses on positive exposition). The book was reprinted several times in the late Qing.

The conventional dating of 1711 follows the internal preface evidence; the catalog meta records 清 dynasty.
