# Mài Jiǎn Bǔ Yì 脈簡補義
*Supplementary Meaning to the Pulse Notes*
by [[周學海]] (Zhōu Xuéhǎi, 字澂之, 號健霞, 1856–1906, 清)

## About the work

A two-juan supplement to the *Mài yì jiǎn mó* [[KR3eb036]], extending Zhōu Xuéhǎi's earlier treatment with additional discussions and clinical observations drawn from his post-1891 practice. The work is conceived as an organic continuation: where the *Jiǎn mó* surveyed the received pulse-doctrinal literature, the *Bǔ yì* takes up the gaps and the new clinical evidence that the original survey had not been able to address. It is the second component of Zhōu's four-part pulse cycle.

The work is conventionally dated to **1893** on the basis of the *Zhōu shì yī xué cóng shū* publication record (the *Bǔ yì* appears in the second series of the *cóngshū*, which was printed in 1893).

### Abstract

Zhōu's *Mài jiǎn bǔ yì* documents the further development of his **form-vs-momentum** doctrine (*qìxuè xíngshì*; see [[KR3eb007]]), including the application of the distinction to a wider range of disease patterns and the elaboration of "*momentum disrupted in form* / *form disrupted in momentum*" sub-classes. The book is also the principal vehicle for Zhōu's polemic against the late-Qing Western-medical view that *cùnkǒu* pulse-diagnosis is unreliable: where the *Chóng dìng zhí jué* [[KR3eb007]] mentions this only in passing, the *Bǔ yì* discusses it at length.

For Zhōu's biographical context see the person note; for the broader cycle structure see [[KR3eb036]].
