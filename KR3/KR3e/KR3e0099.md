# Shānghán lèifāng 傷寒類方
*The Cold-Damage Prescriptions Categorically Arranged*
by [[徐大椿]] (Xú Dàchūn, *zì* Língtāi, *hào* Huíxī lǎorén, 1693–1771, 清)

## About the work

Xú Dàchūn's reorganization of Zhāng Jī's *Shānghán lùn* by **prescription-type** rather than by the conventional channel-or-syndrome organization. The work groups together all prescriptions derived from a base prescription — e.g., the *Guìzhī tāng* category includes 19 prescriptions: *Guìzhī tāng*, *Guìzhī jiā fùzǐ tāng*, *Guìzhī jiā guì tāng*, *Guìzhī qù sháoyào tāng*, *Guìzhī qù sháoyào jiā fùzǐ tāng*, *Guìzhī jiā hòupò xìngrén tāng*, *Xiǎo jiànzhōng tāng*, *Guìzhī jiā sháoyào shēngjiāng rénshēn xīnjiā tāng*, *Guìzhī gāncǎo tāng*, *Fúlíng guìzhī gāncǎo dàzǎo tāng*, *Guìzhī máhuáng gèbàn tāng*, *Guìzhī èr máhuáng yī tāng*, etc. The categorical-by-prescription organization makes the *Shānghán lùn* more accessible for clinical-prescription study, complementing the conventional channel-by-channel organization. The work is one of the more clinically useful Qīng-period reorganizations of the *Shānghán* canon.

### Tiyao

*[The SKQS tíyào is preserved at Kyoto Zinbun 0214108. The local source 000 file shows only the table of contents.]*

The work organizes Zhāng Jī's prescriptions into categories, with each base-prescription as the head and its variants gathered beneath. This is one of the more clinically useful reorganizations of the *Shānghán lùn*'s prescription material.

### Abstract

Composition window: 1759/1759, the date of Xú's preface.

The work's significance:

(a) **The categorical-by-prescription reorganization**: a methodological innovation in *Shānghán* studies. Where conventional *Shānghán* literature organizes by channel-disease (Tàiyáng, Yángmíng, etc.) or by symptom-pattern, Xú's prescription-categorical organization allows clinical study by base-prescription-and-variants — useful for prescription-formulation reasoning.

(b) **Clinical-pedagogical accessibility**: the work serves as a quick-reference for the relations among related prescriptions, allowing the practitioner to quickly understand the scope of *jiājiǎn* (additions-and-subtractions) variants from any given base.

(c) **Part of Xú's *Shānghán*-philological project**: alongside Xú's other *Shānghán*-related works, the *Lèifāng* contributes to the comprehensive Qīng-period systematic reorganization of the cold-damage canon.

The catalog meta dynasty 清 is correct.
