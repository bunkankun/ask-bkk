# Fúxié xīnshū 伏邪新書
*A New Book on Latent Pathogens*
by [[劉吉人]] (Liú Jírén, late 清)

## About the work

A late-Qīng treatise on **latent pathogens** (*fúxié* 伏邪) — the broad category encompassing all warm-disease conditions caused by pathogens that have laid dormant within the body for some period before manifesting clinically. The work is one of the principal late-Qīng monographs on the *fúqì* / *fúxié* doctrinal tradition that goes back to the *Nèijīng* dictum "winter injury by cold produces warm-disease in spring" 冬傷於寒，春必溫病, and that was developed in the Qīng period particularly by [[柳寶詒]] Liǔ Bǎoyí (*Wēnrè féngyuán* [[KR3eg003]]).

### Abstract

Liǔ Bǎoyí's *Wēnrè féngyuán* explicitly references and engages with Liú Jírén's *Fúxié piān* 伏邪篇 in its middle juǎn — confirming the work's late-Qīng circulation and its visibility within the latent-pathogen current. The text systematically distinguishes the principal sub-categories of latent-pathogen disease: cold-injured latent-pathogen emerging as warm-disease (spring); summer-heat latent-pathogen emerging as latent summer-heat (*fúshǔ* 伏暑) in autumn; damp latent-pathogen; and others. For each, Liú expounds the *Nèijīng* and *Shānghán* doctrinal background, the diagnostic markers, and the prescription apparatus.

The work was preserved in the *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 collectanea ([[裘慶元]] Qiū Qìngyún, 1924), which is the principal transmission channel for the cluster of late-Qīng latent-pathogen monographs (Liǔ Bǎoyí, Liú Jírén, [[蔣樹杞]] Jiǎng Shùqǐ).

Dating is approximate. Liú's lifedates are not preserved (see [[劉吉人]] person note); the work is conventionally dated to the late Qīng on textual grounds.

## Other points of interest

The *fúxié* doctrinal category is one of the more analytically distinctive contributions of the late-Qīng *wēnbìng* tradition and remains an important conceptual category in modern TCM warm-disease pedagogy.
