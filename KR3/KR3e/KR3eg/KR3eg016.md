# Wēnrè lùn jiānzhèng 溫熱論箋正
*Annotated Critical Edition of the Treatise on Warm-Heat Disorders*
by [[葉桂]] (Yè Guì, *zì* Tiānshì 天士, 1666–1745) — base text; annotation by [[陳光淞]] Chén Guāngsōng (late-Qīng / early Republican)

## About the work

The principal late-Qīng / early-Republican **critical-annotation edition** of [[葉桂]] Yè Tiānshì's *Wēnrè lùn* ([[KR3eg001]]). The base text is Yè's foundational warm-disease treatise; the annotation (*jiānzhèng* 箋正 = "annotate-and-correct") is by [[陳光淞]] Chén Guāngsōng, a late-Qīng physician active in the 1900s–1910s. The catalog meta lists only Yè as author, since the *jiānzhèng* genre conventionally subordinates the annotator's identity to the base text; in this collection's format the annotation is the principal new contribution.

### Abstract

Chén Guāngsōng's *jiānzhèng* is one of a cluster of late-Qīng / Republican critical-annotation editions of the *Wēnrè lùn*, a moment in which the doctrinal authority of the *Wēnrè lùn* had become canonical and the textual-critical work shifted toward establishing the most authentic version of Yè's words. The principal textual issues addressed by Chén are: (a) the choice between the [[唐大烈]] Táng Dàliè *Wúyī huìjiǎng* recension and the [[華岫雲]] Huá Xiùyún *Línzhèng zhǐnán yīàn* recension; (b) the doctrinal weight of disputed passages on *xiézàifèi* 邪在肺 versus *xiézàixīnbāo* 邪在心包; (c) the relationship of the *Wēnrè lùn* to the parallel *Sānshí zhǒng wàigǎn zhèng* 三十種外感證 text also ascribed to Yè.

The work falls within the broader Shàoxīng-school late-Qīng / Republican *wēnbìng* editorial project, in which [[何廉臣]] Hé Liánchén and his circle systematically re-edited the canonical *wēnbìng* texts. Dating is approximate; the work probably appeared in the late 1900s or early 1910s.

## Other points of interest

The *jiānzhèng* genre is characteristic of late-Qīng / Republican medical-philological work — a Chinese-medical adaptation of the *Sìkù* 提要-style critical-edition apparatus.
