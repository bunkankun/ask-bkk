# Xuē shì shīrè lùn gējué 薛氏濕熱論歌訣
*Versified Summary of Mr. Xuē's "Treatise on Damp-Heat Disorders"*
by [[王旭高]] (Wáng Xùgāo, 1798–1862)

## About the work

A mid-19th-century **versified mnemonic** of [[薛雪]] Xuē Shēngbái's *Shīrè tiáobiàn* ([[KR3eg020]]), composed by the Wúxī physician Wáng Xùgāo as part of his pedagogical *gējué* 歌訣 (rhymed-formula) project. Each numbered proposition of Xuē's original treatise is rendered as a four- or seven-character verse for memorisation, with prose annotations as needed.

### Abstract

The *gējué* genre — versified medical mnemonics — is a characteristic late-Qīng pedagogical form, particularly associated with the Wúxī-Sūzhōu medical schools. Wáng Xùgāo's *gējué* output is substantial; the *Shīrè lùn gējué* is the principal *wēnbìng*-tradition contribution. Its companion piece is the *Wú Yòukě wēnyì lùn gēkuò* 吳又可溫疫論歌括 ([[KR3eg024]]), versifying [[吳有性]] Wú Yǒuxìng's *Wēnyì lùn*; together they constitute Wáng's distillation of the canonical *wēnbìng* monographs into mnemonic form.

The dating bracket reflects Wáng's principal active period — the *gējué* are typically dated to the 1840s–1850s, with the collected works *Wáng Xùgāo yī shū liù zhǒng* 王旭高醫書六種 printed posthumously in 1862. No earlier preface specifically dates this *gējué*.

The text is doctrinally a faithful summary of Xuē's original; its independent contribution is pedagogical rather than substantive. It became part of the standard mid-Qīng *wēnbìng* curriculum in the Wúxī-Sūzhōu medical milieu and was widely reprinted.

## Other points of interest

The genre of versified medical mnemonics — *gējué* / *gēkuò* — flourished particularly in the mid-19th-century Sūzhōu-Wúxī medical world; Wáng Xùgāo is its principal *wēnbìng*-school exponent.
