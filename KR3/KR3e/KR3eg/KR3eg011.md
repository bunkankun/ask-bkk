# Wēnbìng tiáobiàn 溫病條辨 (alternate recension)
*Detailed Analysis of Warm Diseases*
by [[吳塘]] (Wú Táng, *hào* Jūtōng 鞠通, 1758–1836)

## About the work

A second catalog entry for Wú Jūtōng's *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*, parallel to [[KR3eg010]]. The catalog meta gives no author and no dynasty for KR3eg011, but the title is identical to KR3eg010 and the structure of the source suggests this is the same six-*juǎn* text in a different recension — most likely the late-Qīng or Mínguó-era re-edition with annotations (a common form is the [[王士雄]] Wáng Shìxióng / [[葉霖]] Yè Lín / [[雷豐]] Léi Shàoyì annotated editions that circulated widely in the 19th and early 20th centuries).

### Abstract

For the full doctrinal-historical treatment of the *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*, see [[KR3eg010]]. The present entry represents a parallel recension. Without distinct paratext, the two entries cannot be further individuated from the catalog metadata alone; readers seeking the substantive content should consult [[KR3eg010]].

## Other points of interest

The presence of two catalog entries under identical title in a single *cóngshū* division most likely reflects the editorial decision to preserve two distinct textual witnesses to the *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*, given the importance of the text — a parallel to the dual entries for the *Shānghán lùn* in the same collection.
