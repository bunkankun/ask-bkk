# Shǔzhèng fāyuán 暑症發原
*Tracing the Origins of Summer-Heat Disorders*
by [[李識候]] (Lǐ Shíhòu, 清)

## About the work

A late-Qīng monograph dedicated to **summer-heat disorder** (*shǔzhèng* 暑症 / *shǔbìng* 暑病), in 1 *juǎn*. The text takes its place in the cluster of Qīng *wēnbìng*-tradition monographs on summer-heat — alongside the earlier base-text [[張鳳逵]] Zhāng Fèngkuí's *Shāngshǔ quánshū* ([[KR3eg005]]) and the canonical treatment of *shǔwēn* in the *wēnbìng* synthesis (cf. *Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*, *Wēnrè jīngwěi*).

### Abstract

Summer-heat is one of the four principal warm-disease categories in the Qīng *wēnbìng* synthesis, alongside *fēngwēn* 風溫, *shīwēn* 濕溫, and *wēnyì* 溫疫. Lǐ Shíhòu's monograph systematically traces the doctrinal origins of summer-heat back through the canonical *Nèijīng* loci (*Sùwèn* "Rèlùn" 熱論 etc.) and provides a syndrome-discriminating clinical apparatus.

Dating and authorial detail are not preserved. The text is conventionally placed in the late Qīng on doctrinal grounds. No CBDB record exists for the author.
