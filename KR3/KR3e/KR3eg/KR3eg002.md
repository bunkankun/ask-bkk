# Shíbìng lùn 時病論
*Treatise on Seasonal Disorders*
by [[雷豐]] (Léi Fēng, *zì* Sōngcún 松存, *hào* Shàoyì 少逸, 1833–1888)

## About the work

Léi Fēng's *Shíbìng lùn* in 8 *juǎn*, completed Guāngxù 8 (1882) and printed the following year. A major late-Qīng *wēnbìng* monograph organising the externally-contracted seasonal disorders (*shíbìng* 時病) into a clear four-season framework, with the canonical *wēnbìng*-school distinction between *fúqì* 伏氣 (latent-qi) and *xīngǎn* 新感 (newly-contracted) varieties carefully maintained throughout. The text is one of the most clinically useful and pedagogically influential of the late-Qīng *wēnbìng* treatises and has remained a standard reference in modern TCM training.

### Abstract

The preface is signed "Guāngxù bā nián rénwǔ zhòngqiū qián yī rì, Sānqú Léi Fēng Shàoyì shì zhì yú Yǎnghè Shānfáng" 光緒八年壬午仲秋前一日，三衢雷豐少逸氏識於養鶴山房, fixing both date (1882) and place (Yǎnghè Shānfáng 養鶴山房, Léi's studio in Qúzhōu 衢州). Léi was a native of Sānqú 三衢 (Qúzhōu) and a third-generation physician — his father [[雷逸仙]] Léi Yìxiān had studied under [[程芝田]] Chéng Zhītián.

The work is organised by season: spring disorders (*chūnwēn* 春溫, *fēngwēn* 風溫, *wēnyì* 溫疫, *wēndú* 溫毒); summer disorders (*shǔwēn* 暑溫, *shīwēn* 濕溫, *xiàshǔ* 夏暑); autumn disorders (*qiūzào* 秋燥, *fúshǔ* 伏暑); winter disorders (*dōngwēn* 冬溫, *dōnghán* 冬寒, *tányǐn* 痰飲). For each disorder Léi presents the canonical *Nèijīng* / *Shānghán lùn* doctrinal background, the standard prescriptions, and a body of his own clinical cases. The clinical idiom is recognisably continuous with the southern (Sūzhōu–Hángzhōu) Yè–Wú–Wáng line of *wēnbìng* practice, but with Léi's own emphasis on rigorous seasonal classification.

The work was reprinted many times in the late Qīng and Republican periods and remains in print as a standard *wēnbìng* textbook.

## Other points of interest

The text is the principal exhibit for the late-Qīng "Zhèjiāng" wing of the *wēnbìng* tradition, distinguished from the Sūzhōu mainstream by an explicit emphasis on the *fúqì* / *xīngǎn* distinction and on the seasonal pathogenetic schema.
