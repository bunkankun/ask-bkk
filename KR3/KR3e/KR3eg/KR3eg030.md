# Wēnzhèng zhǐguī 溫證指歸
*Pointing to the Heart of Warm-Disease Syndromes*
by [[周魁]] (Zhōu Kuí, 清)

## About the work

A late-Qīng warm-disease pedagogical treatise organised around syndrome-differentiation and prescription, in the late-Qīng *wēnbìng*-mainstream idiom. The title — *zhǐguī* 指歸 ("pointing to the heart of") — locates the work in the broader late-Qīng pedagogical *wēnbìng* genre, alongside [[婁傑]] Lóu Jié's *Wēnbìng zhǐnán* ([[KR3eg007]]) and [[馬宗元]] Mǎ Zōngyuán's *Wēnbìng biànzhèng* ([[KR3eg019]]).

### Abstract

Zhōu Kuí's biographical record has not been preserved in standard reference works, and the *Wēnzhèng zhǐguī* lacks a securely dated preface in widely accessible editions. The text is conventionally dated to the late Qīng on grounds of doctrinal alignment — it engages closely with the [[吳塘]] Wú Jūtōng (*Wēnbìng tiáobiàn*) and [[王士雄]] Wáng Mèngyīng (*Wēnrè jīngwěi*) doctrinal apparatus, which fixes the *terminus a quo* at ca. 1852, with the late-Qīng terminus extending to the end of the dynasty.

The doctrinal substance is a systematic mid-late-Qīng *wēnbìng* syndrome-differentiation organised by the canonical four-fen and three-burner staging frames, with the standard prescription apparatus. The text's contribution is pedagogical rather than doctrinally innovative.
