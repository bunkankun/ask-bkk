# Kèchuāng ǒután 客窗偶談
*Casual Conversations at the Guest-Window*
by [[沈明宗]] Shěn Míngzōng (*zì* Mùcún 目存, *hào* Qiūchí 秋池, *fl.* late seventeenth / early eighteenth century, Kāngxī period).

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* clinical-theoretical miscellany by Shěn Míngzōng — the early-Qīng Jiāxīng 嘉興 (浙江) physician of the **Lǐ-Zhū** 李朱 (Lǐ Dōngyuán / Zhū Dānxī) tradition who also produced the *Shānghán Líushū chánjiě* 傷寒六書纏解, the *Jīnguì yàolüè biānzhù* 金匱要略編注 (1692), and the *Wēnrè bìng piān* 溫熱病篇. The *Kèchuāng ǒután* is the **theoretical-doctrinal companion** to Shěn's classical-text commentary work — preserving his short doctrinal essays on the structure of Chinese medical theory. The work opens with a Confucian student-master dialogue on the inadequacy of the standard *shíèr jīngluò* / *wǔzàngliùfǔ* clinical schema and the necessity of integrating it with the *tiānrén* 天人 *yùnqì* 五運六氣 cosmological framework — establishing Shěn's signature integrative-doctrinal position.

### Abstract

Shěn Míngzōng 沈明宗 (Mùcún / Qiūchí), early-Qīng Jiāxīng physician, is one of the most theoretically sophisticated physicians of the post-Lǐ Shízhēn / pre-Wú Jūtōng period — combining classical *Lǐ-Zhū* clinical doctrine with the *yùnqì* cosmological framework and the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* classical-formulary tradition. His major works are the *Shānghán Liùshū chánjiě* (a commentary on the 'six books' of *Shānghán* commentary), the *Jīnguì yàolüè biānzhù* 金匱要略編注 (1692, a major early-Qīng commentary on the *Jīnguì*), and the present *Kèchuāng ǒután* miscellany. The composition window 1670–1710 reflects his documented productive period in the Kāngxī era. The work entered Chinese circulation via the late-Qīng / Republican *Hǎiwài huíliú zhōngyī gǔjí* (hxwd) repatriation programme.

Historiographical significance: Shěn Míngzōng is one of the **early-Qīng theorist-physicians** between the Míng Lǐ-Zhū-school orthodoxy and the mature *Wēnbìng*-school of the Qiánlóng — Jiāqìng era. The *Kèchuāng ǒután*'s integrative doctrinal-cosmological position prefigures the **Mèng-hé-school** integrative method of the Dàoguāng — Xiánfēng period (cf. [[KR3eq049]]). Not in CBDB.
