# Yīxué yuánliú lùn 醫學源流論
*Essays on the Sources and Streams of Medical Learning*
by [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn (1693–1771).

## About the work

KR3eq012 is a **second jicheng.tw-tradition exemplar** of Xú Dàchūn's *Yīxué yuánliú lùn* (1757), the principal substantive entry for which is **[[KR3eq011]]**. The catalog records the title twice within the same KR3eq division because the jicheng.tw corpus received two distinct manuscript-or-print transmissions of the same work, and the cataloguers assigned a separate KR id to each transmission to preserve the textual-witness distinction. The substantive content — the 99-essay arrangement, the 1757 self-preface, the doctrinal positions on the *Jīngfāng* tradition and against the Jīn-Yuán Four Masters — is identical to KR3eq011 and is treated at length there.

### Prefaces

See [[KR3eq011]] for the dated 1757 self-preface signed at the Bànsōng shūwū 半松書屋 on Wúshān 吳山.

### Abstract

For the work's doctrinal significance — its founding role in the mid-to-late Qīng *kǎojù yīxué* movement, its programmatic re-canonisation of the Hàn-Táng *Jīngfāng* tradition, and its polemic against the Jīn-Yuán Four Masters — see the principal entry at [[KR3eq011]].

The present entry is preserved as a separate KR id only to register the textual-witness distinction within the jicheng.tw corpus; the date-bracket and authorship-attribution follow the principal entry. CBDB records Xú Dàchūn at id 61225 with lifedates 1693–1771.
