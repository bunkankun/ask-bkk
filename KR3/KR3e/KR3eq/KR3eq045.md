# Yùgāngzhāi yīxué bǐzhǔ 鬱岡齋醫學筆麈
*Medical "Talking-Whisk" from the Yù-Crag Studio*
by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng (*zì* Yǔtài 宇泰, 1549–1613), completed at the *Yùgāngzhāi* 鬱岡齋 in **Wànlì 30 (1602)**, with the modern editorial recension prepared by **[[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi** (1901–1970, *zì* Zhījì 之濟) in 1926.

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* condensation by Qín Bówèi (the early-Republican-era Shànghǎi medical educator) of the medical-theoretical material in Wáng Kěntáng's twelve-*juǎn* late-Míng *bǐjì* 筆麈 (talking-whisk-style miscellany) ***Yùgāngzhāi bǐzhǔ*** 鬱岡齋筆麈. The parent *bǐzhǔ* — covering literature, history, *yìjīng* 易經 studies, *yīnyáng wǔxíng* cosmology, and medical questions in twelve *juǎn* — was preserved in the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 and is cited by the *Sìkù tíyào* editors as among the finest late-Míng *bǐjì* productions. Qín Bówèi's editorial intervention extracts the medical content (approximately three-tenths of the parent work) and presents it as a self-contained two-*juǎn* medical compendium with section-headings and apparatus suited to early-Republican Shànghǎi medical-school curriculum. The work is methodologically interesting as **Wáng Kěntáng's pre-*Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* medical notes** — composed in 1602, two years before the 1604 completion of the *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng*'s [[KR3e0078]] cold-damage and surgical sections — and therefore preserves Wáng's medical thinking in its formative pre-encyclopaedic stage.

### Prefaces

The jicheng.tw text opens with the preface by **[[秦伯未]] Qín Bówèi** of Shànghǎi (signed *Bǐngyín bāyuè shàng Shànghǎi Qín Zhījì Bówèifǔ jì* 丙寅八月上海秦之濟伯未甫記 = eighth month of *bǐngyín* 丙寅 = 1926). Qín records the parent twelve-*juǎn* *bǐzhǔ*'s inclusion in the *Sìkù quánshū*, his discovery that approximately three-tenths of the parent work concerns medicine, his commissioning of his colleague [[錢季寅]] Qián Jìyín to extract the medical sections for separate publication, the addition of *jiàochóu* 校讎 (textual collation), small-section *xiǎomù* 小目 (sub-headings), and the rename to *Yīxué bǐzhǔ* 醫學筆麈. Qín further notes that the parent *bǐzhǔ* was completed in *Wànlì sānshínián rényín* 萬曆三十年壬寅 = 1602, while Wáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* was completed in *Wànlì sānshíèrnián jiǎchén* 萬曆三十二年甲辰 = 1604, so that the medical *bǐzhǔ* records "*[material] preserved separately as Wáng was compiling the Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng, with which it can be cross-referenced*".

### Abstract

The composition window 1602–1926 reflects the two-stage history: Wáng Kěntáng's original 1602 composition of the parent *bǐzhǔ* (at age 53, mid-career), and Qín Bówèi's 1926 editorial extraction of the medical material into the present two-*juǎn* form. The work is the most useful surviving witness to **Wáng Kěntáng's medical thinking in its pre-encyclopaedic form** — the doctrinal positions on the *xiānghuǒ* / *mìngmén* problem, the integration of *yìjīng* cosmology with clinical reasoning, and the engagement with the [[朱震亨]] Zhū Dānxī yīn-supplementation tradition were all worked out in the 1602 *bǐzhǔ* before being systematised in the 1604 *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng*.

CBDB records Wáng Kěntáng at 1549–1613. Qín Bówèi (1901–1970) is the early-Republican Shànghǎi medical educator best known for his role in the Shànghǎi *Zhōngyī xuéyuàn* 中醫學院 founding and the *Zhōngyī rùmén* 中醫入門 textbook tradition.
