# Júfāng fāhuī 局方發揮
*A Critical Elucidation of the [Héjì] Júfāng*
by [[朱震亨]] Zhū Zhènhēng (*zì* Yànxiū 彥修, *hào* Dānxī 丹溪, 1281–1358).

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* medical-doctrinal critique of the Sòng imperial pharmacy formulary *Tàipíng huìmín héjì jú fāng* 太平惠民和劑局方 (cf. [[KR3e0033]]) by the fourth of the JīnYuán Four Masters. The work is the doctrinal turning-point of the JīnYuán medical revolution: Zhū argues (i) that the *Júfāng*'s habit of supplying prescriptions without aetiological reasoning produces clinicians who mistake symptomatic match for true therapeutic match, and (ii) that the *Júfāng*'s heavy reliance on warming, drying, and fragrant-aromatic medicines (溫補、燥熱、香竄) is catastrophically wrong for the actual *yīn*-deficiency-fire clinical population of his time. The form is dialogic: Zhū's disciples raise particular *Júfāng* prescriptions and Zhū analyses each in turn. The present digitised exemplar is from jicheng.tw, collating a 1583 金陵周氏仁壽堂 Míng-Wànlì cut against the Wényuāngé Sìkùquánshū text. This is the same work catalogued in the Wényuāngé Sìkùquánshū as **[[KR3e0061]]**; the present entry records the jicheng.tw transmission, the principal substantive discussion is at the [[KR3e0061]] entry.

### Abstract

Composition window 1340–1358 reflects the late mature period of Zhū's career; the work is not precisely datable within this window. Its doctrinal significance — turning post-Yuán Chinese medicine away from formulary-based clinical practice toward aetiology-grounded reasoning — and the long-running [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn-vs-Zhū Zhènhēng controversy that it provoked are treated at length in the canonical [[KR3e0061]] entry; here it is noted only that the work's classification under the *yīhuà* subdivision KR3eq in the modern Kanripo arrangement reflects the late inclusion of Zhū's critique alongside Edo-Qīng *yīhuà* and *yīlùn*. For the textual analysis, the SKQS *tíyào*, and the principal scholarly literature, see [[KR3e0061]].
