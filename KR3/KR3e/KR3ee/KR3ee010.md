# Jīngluò huìbiān 經絡彙編
*Compendium of Channels and Collaterals*
by [[翟良]] Zhái Liáng (撰)

## About the work
A late-Míng monograph on the channel-collateral system (*jīngluò* 經絡), composed by the Shāndōng physician [[翟良]] Zhái Liáng (*hào* Yùhú 玉壺, late Wànlì to Chóngzhēn period). Zhái's polemic, set out in the opening *Jīngluò tǒngxù* 經絡統序, is that the channel-system is not the proprietary concern of acupuncturists but the foundation of all clinical reasoning, including pharmacotherapy: "Those who do not understand the twelve channels and collaterals — the moment they open their mouths or move their hands, they are already wrong" (不明十二經絡，開口動手便錯). The work develops the full *zǐwǔ liúzhù* mapping of the twelve channels onto the twelve double-hours (寅-肺, 卯-大腸, etc.), reproduces the *Língshū jīngmài* chapter point-trajectory with extensive annotation, and adds Zhái's own chapter on the origins of disease in terms of channel-impedance. The second juan turns to clinical applications: which channel is implicated in which kind of headache, eye-disease, leg-pain, etc., with annotated formulary.

### Abstract
The *Jīngluò huìbiān* is dated by internal evidence to the late Wànlì or Chóngzhēn period — the latest internal reference is to [[李杲]] Lǐ Dōngyuán's *zhōngyǎng lùn*, and the printing-style of the surviving editions is mid-17th-century. Zhái Liáng is documented in late-Míng / early-Qīng local gazetteers of Yìzhōu 益州 (Shāndōng) as a literatus-physician who repeatedly declined office. His *Jīngluò huìbiān* belongs to the same late-Míng wave of "channel-doctrine" treatises as [[沈子祿]] Shěn Zǐlù and [[徐師曾]] Xú Shīcēng's *Jīngluò quánshū* ([[KR3ee011]]), [[張三錫]] Zhāng Sānxī's *Jīngluò kǎo* ([[KR3ee025]]), and [[滑壽]] Huá Shòu's earlier *Shísì jīng fāhuī* ([[KR3ee026]]) — collectively, the genre that re-centered Chinese clinical reasoning on the *jīngluò* mapping after the heavy formulary-orientation of the Sòng. The work was published privately in a Shāndōng family-print edition and survives in late-Míng manuscript witnesses; it is not registered in the *Sìkù*.
