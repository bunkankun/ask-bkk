# Zhēnjiǔ féngyuán 針灸逢源
*Acupuncture and Moxibustion Encountering the Source*
by [[李學川]] Lǐ Xuéchuān (撰)

## About the work
A six-juan late-Qīng acupuncture compendium by [[李學川]] Lǐ Xuéchuān (*zì* Sānyuán 三源, *hào* Lǐchéng 鄰澄), of Wúxiàn 吳縣 (Sūzhōu), prefaced in Jiāqìng 20 (1815). The work opens with a "supplementary printing" of the *Sùwèn* with annotated commentary (續刻素問經文補遺), beginning with the *Màiyào jīngwēi lùn* 脈要精微論 — Lǐ inserts the omitted *Sùwèn* passages from later imperial editions and provides his own interlinear gloss. The body of the work treats the channel-trajectories, acupoints, indications, and Lǐ's clinical case-records, anchored throughout in the *Sùwèn-Língshū-Nànjīng* canon (the "Source" of the title). The *Zhēnjiǔ féngyuán* is the most theoretically rigorous of the Qīng acupuncture handbooks and is the principal late-Qīng source for the integrated channel-and-formulary clinical method that would feed into the Republican-period TCM reconstitution.

### Abstract
The *Zhēnjiǔ féngyuán* is one of the principal late-Qīng acupuncture works (alongside [[廖潤鴻]] Liào Rùnhóng's *Zhēnjiǔ jíchéng* [[KR3ee007]]) and was widely used in the Jiāqìng / Dàoguāng period. Lǐ Xuéchuān's editorial ambition is to restore the canonical *Sùwèn-Língshū-Nànjīng* root-doctrine that he sees as having decayed in the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* ([[KR3ee015]])-dominated Qīng curriculum; this is the "encountering the source" (逢源) of the title. The work consolidates 361 acupoints, distinguishes the 14 channels in the standard post-Huá Shòu form ([[KR3ee026]]), and integrates Lǐ's own pharmacological prescriptions for each clinical syndrome.
