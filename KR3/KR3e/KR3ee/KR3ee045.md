# Zǐwǔ liúzhù zhēn jīng 子午流注針經
*Midnight-Noon Flowing-Injection Needle Classic*
by [[閻明廣]] Yán Míngguǎng (撰)

## About the work
A three-juan Jīn-dynasty acupuncture treatise by [[閻明廣]] Yán Míngguǎng of Chángshān 常山 (modern Héběi); the foundational text of the *zǐwǔ liúzhù* 子午流注 chronoacupuncture system. Yán's preface — reconstructed from the Míng *Pǔjì fāng* ([[KR3ee013]]) — explains the work's genesis: Yán long-practiced medicine "loving to read the *Nànjīng* and *Sùwèn*; their phrasing and reasoning are subtle, their hidden gate profoundly obscure — difficult both anciently and now." Yán then describes the *Zhǐwēi lùn* 指微論 (3 juan) of "the recent Hé Gōng of Nán-Táng" (南唐何公) — likely the Five-Dynasties [[何若愚]] Hé Ruòyú — as the work that "exhausts the channel-and-collateral source, follows the principle of needle-stimulation, illuminates the clarity-and-turbidity of *yíng* and *wèi*, distinguishes the partitions of the acupoint-orifices" but had not circulated widely. Yán in Zhēnyuán *guǐyǒu* 貞元癸酉 (1153) obtained Hé Gōng's *Zhǐwēi zhēn fù* 指微針賦 — a rhapsodic version of the *Zhǐwēi lùn* doctrine. Yán annotated and expanded Hé's *fù* with hundreds of additional clinical-application chapters, then drew on the *Nànjīng-Sùwèn* and Jiǎshì 賈氏 *jǐngyíng liùshí shǒu* 井滎六十首 to compose the *Liúzhù jīngluò jǐngyíng tú gējué* 流注經絡井滎圖歌訣 — the systematic ten-celestial-stems / twelve-earthly-branches assignment of acupoints to clinical timing. The final result is the *Zǐwǔ liúzhù zhēn jīng*.

### Abstract
The *Zǐwǔ liúzhù zhēn jīng* is the foundational text of the *zǐwǔ liúzhù* chronoacupuncture system that governs the assignment of specific acupoints to specific hour-of-the-day and day-of-the-celestial-stem combinations — the "Midnight-Noon flowing-injection" doctrine. The system was systematized in the Míng by [[徐鳳]] Xú Fèng's *Zhēnjiǔ dàquán* ([[KR3ee002]], 1439) and became foundational for all subsequent East-Asian acupuncture chronotherapy. Yán Míngguǎng's 1153 date establishes the doctrine in the Jīn period; Hé Ruòyú's earlier 10th-century *Zhǐwēi lùn* is the deeper source. The work is registered in the *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào* under *Zǐbù · Yījiā lèi*. The text survives in the SKQS recension drawn from the *Pǔjì fāng* reproduction.
