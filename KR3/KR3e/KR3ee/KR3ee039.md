# Màidù yùnxíng kǎo 脈度運行考
*Investigation of the Measure and Circulation of the Channels*
by [[李彰五]] Lǐ Zhāngwǔ (撰)

## About the work
A single-juan late-Qīng technical-philological treatise by [[李彰五]] Lǐ Zhāngwǔ (fl. Guāngxù period), prefaced and printed in Guāngxù 24 (1898, *zhūyōng yānmào* 著雍閹茂 = the *wùxū* year). Lǐ's self-preface gives the genesis: in his nearly-forty years of medical practice he had completed several monographs — the *Bìngjiā xūzhī gējué* 病家須知歌訣, the annotated *Hánwēn tiáobiàn* 寒溫條辨, the *Zhòngjǐng Shānghán jízhù* 仲景傷寒輯注 (30 juan), and the *Zhòngjǐng màifǎ wàizhù* 仲景脈法外注 — but lost the original draft of the *Màidù yùnxíng kǎo* in the upheavals of his official career. The popular memory-verse "*Lung-Yín, large-intestine-Mǎo, stomach-Chén... gall-bladder-Zǐ-Chǒu-Liver*" — fitting the twelve channels onto the twelve double-hours — Lǐ holds is "all over the place but inaccurate" (人多宗之，然究屬參差，未敢沿誤), because the channels are of unequal length (the hand-yīn channels are each 3 *chǐ* 5 *cùn*, the hand-yáng 5 *chǐ*, the foot-yīn 6 *chǐ* 5 *cùn*, the foot-yáng 8 *chǐ*, the *rèn-dū* each 4 *chǐ* 5 *cùn*, totaling 16 *zhàng* 2 *chǐ*) — how can twelve equal-length double-hours be assigned to twelve unequal channels, with *rèn* and *dū* left unaccounted for? Lǐ recommissioned his wife to compute (按照《內經·營氣運行》諸冊，詳為覈計) the precise *chǐ-cùn-fēn* values of each channel at each *kè* 刻 (quarter-hour subdivision) according to the *Língshū* doctrine of *yíngqì* circulation, and to standardize them in tabular form.

### Abstract
The *Màidù yùnxíng kǎo* is one of the most technically rigorous late-Qīng treatises on the *Nèijīng* doctrine of *yíngqì* circulation. Lǐ Zhāngwǔ's polemical move — to discard the popular twelve-hour-twelve-channel mnemonic as a popular oversimplification, and to substitute a *chǐ-cùn-fēn*-resolved tabular system — exemplifies the *kǎozhèng* late-Qīng impulse to subject the medical canon to mathematical-philological scrutiny. The work is short (one juan) but theoretically dense; it is registered in the *Zhōngguó zhōngyī gǔjí zǒngmù*. The composition date 1898 is internal.
