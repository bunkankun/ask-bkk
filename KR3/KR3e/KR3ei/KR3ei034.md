# Fùkē bǎibiàn 婦科百辨
*A Hundred Discriminations in Gynecology*
by [[莊履嚴]] (Zhuāng Lǚyán, Qīng)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* gynecology in the *bǎibiàn* 百辨 (one-hundred-discriminations) format by the Qīng physician [[莊履嚴]] Zhuāng Lǚyán. The work covers women's medicine through a sequence of question-and-discrimination entries beginning with *zázhèng* 雜症 (miscellaneous diseases: women's stroke, *bànshēnbùsuì* 半身不遂, headache, etc.) and proceeding through the canonical gynecological topics. Each entry combines a clinical-question framing with discriminating clinical analysis (*xūshí hánrè* differential), citation of major authorities ([[李杲]] Lǐ Dōngyuán's *Bǔzhōng yìqì tāng*, Sòng *Xiǎoxùmìng tāng* 小續命湯), and prescribed formula.

### Abstract

Internal evidence: the work prescribes *Bǔzhōng yìqì tāng* with *Bèimǔ* 貝母 and *Huánglián* 黃連 additions (a late-Míng / Qīng formulation) and uses the canonical late-Míng *yǐqìzhìxuè* 以氣治血 *xuèqì duìzhì* 血氣對治 discriminative method. The work belongs squarely to the Qīng popular practitioner-tradition; `notBefore` 1700 / `notAfter` 1800 is a defensible bracket.

The work is closely related to the *Fùkē wèndá* 婦科問答 [[KR3ei004]] in didactic format and content, with which it shares the discrimination-by-question pedagogical method.
