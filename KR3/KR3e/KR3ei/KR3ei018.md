# Nǚkē jīngyào 女科精要
*The Refined Essentials of Women's Medicine*
by [[馮兆張]] (Féng Zhàozhāng, early Qīng)

## About the work

A three-*juǎn* systematic gynecology work by the early-Qīng physician Féng Zhàozhāng 馮兆張 (*zì* Chǔzhān 楚瞻), forming an integral section of his major medical encyclopedia [[馮氏錦囊秘錄]] *Féngshì jǐnnáng mìlù* (*The Secret Records from Féng's Brocade Bag*, 50 *juǎn*, completed 1702). The work is structured by topical *mén* 門: *yuèjīng mén* 月經門 (menstrual disorders), *jīngbìng mén* 經病門 (menstrual-disease commentary), *bēnglòu mén* 崩漏門, *dàixià mén* 帶下門, *qiúzǐ mén* 求子門, *tāiqián mén* 胎前門, *línchǎn mén* 臨產門, *chǎnhòu mén* 產後門. Each topical *mén* opens with a substantial *zhūlùn* 諸論 (collected discussions) gathering classical and Sòng-Yuán-Míng authorities (frequently citing the *Sùwèn* [[KR3e0001]], [[王冰]] Wáng Bīng, [[李東垣]] Lǐ Dōngyuán, [[朱丹溪]] Zhū Dānxī, and [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sīmiǎo) before proceeding to formulary practice.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension carries the body of the work but no separable preface; that material is associated with the parent *Jǐnnáng mìlù*. The opening of the *yuèjīng mén* offers a substantial doctrinal opening establishing the Sòng *fùrén yǐ xuè wéi běn* 婦人以血為本 framework with citations from the *Sùwèn*, Wáng Bīng's commentary, and Lǐ Dōngyuán's *Píwèi lùn* 脾胃論 ([[KR3e0053]]).

### Abstract

Féng Zhàozhāng 馮兆張 (*zì* Chǔzhān 楚瞻) was a Hǎiyán 海鹽 (Zhèjiāng) physician of the early Qīng who completed the *Féngshì jǐnnáng mìlù* 馮氏錦囊秘錄 in 50 *juǎn* in 1702 (*Kāngxī* 41). The *Jǐnnáng mìlù* is a major early-Qīng systematic medical encyclopedia, organised by sub-discipline: internal medicine, paediatrics, gynecology, *shānghán*, *wàikē* (external medicine), pulse-diagnosis, and *materia medica*. The *Nǚkē jīngyào* is the gynecology section, comprising approximately three *juǎn* of the parent work. The work survives both as part of the *Jǐnnáng mìlù* parent compilation and as separately-extracted standalone editions, the latter circulated as `Nǚkē jīngyào` from the late Kāngxī era onwards.

Doctrinally Féng combines the Lǐ-Zhū *bǔzhōng* / *zīyīn* synthesis with extensive engagement with the Sòng-Yuán canonical tradition, citing Wáng Bīng's *Sùwèn* commentary, Sūn Sīmiǎo's *Qiānjīn yàofāng* ([[KR3e0013]]), [[陳自明]] Chén Zìmíng's *Fùrén dàquán liángfāng* ([[KR3e0038]]), and the *Héjì júfāng* ([[KR3e0033]]) directly. The work's distinctive feature is its emphasis on early-life female constitutional development: Féng argues that constitutional weakness from improperly nourished early adolescence is the principal long-term cause of adult gynecological disorders. The opening of the *jīngbìng mén* offers detailed advice on the timing of marriage in relation to *tiānguǐ* 天癸 onset.

The *Jǐnnáng mìlù* was widely reprinted in the Qīng and Republican periods and remains a standard reference in modern PRC TCM curriculum. The *Nǚkē jīngyào* is one of its most-extracted sections.

## Other points of interest

Féng's *Jǐnnáng mìlù* is one of the earliest major Qīng systematic medical compilations and a key bridge between the late-Míng *gāngmù* synthesis ([[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng, [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng) and the imperially-sponsored *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* of 1742. The work's wide circulation in popular Qīng medical book markets made it one of the principal channels by which late-Míng gynecological doctrine reached 18th-century practitioners.
