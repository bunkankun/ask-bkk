# Fùkē bīngjiàn 婦科冰鑑
*The Crystal Mirror of Gynecology*
by [[柴得華]] Chái Déhuá (= Chái Huáshēng 柴華生, mid- to late Qīng)

## About the work

An eight-*juǎn* systematic gynecology by the mid-to-late-Qīng physician [[柴得華]] Chái Déhuá. The *bīngjiàn* 冰鑑 ("crystal mirror") title — invoking the metaphor of utterly clear reflection — declares the work's aspiration to lucid, unambiguous gynecological reasoning. The work covers the canonical topical sequence — *tiáojīng*, *bēnglòu*, *dàixià*, *qiúzǐ*, *tāiqián*, *línchǎn*, *chǎnhòu*, *fùrén zábìng* — with each entry presenting a *zhěnmài* 診脈 (pulse-examination) note, étiological discussion, *zhìfǎ* 治法 (therapeutic-method) statement, and prescribed formula.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` `_000.txt` carries the author's *zìxù* 自序 (self-preface). Chái relates: in his youth he had been gravely misdiagnosed with a *wēnbìng* 溫病 (warm-disease) condition and barely survived; this experience led him to abandon the literati track for medicine. After many years of close study he gained "*shǎo yǒu dé*" (a little understanding). He notes the proverbial difficulty of gynecology — *fùrén yǐ xuè wéi zhǔ* (women's medicine takes blood as principal), and "*qì gù bù yì lǐ, ér xuè yóu nán tiáoxiè yě*" (qì is already not easy to regulate, but blood is even harder to harmonise). He laments that the existing gynecological literature is "*lùn ér bù xiáng, yǔ ér bù chàng*" 論而不詳，語而不暢 (discussion not detailed, statement not fluent) and undertakes his own systematic exposition.

### Abstract

Chái Déhuá 柴得華 (also recorded as Chái Huáshēng 柴華生) is a mid-to-late-Qīng physician otherwise undocumented in standard biographical reference works. Internal evidence places the work in the mature Qīng systematic-gynecology tradition: extensive citation of [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng (1607 *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng*), [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng (1620 *[[濟陰綱目]]*), [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn (1624 / 1640 *[[婦人規]]*), and the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn·Fùkē* (1742). The catalog meta dynasty 淸 is correct; `notBefore` 1750 / `notAfter` 1850 brackets the working range.

The work belongs to the broader Qīng systematic-gynecology corpus, alongside the *Nǚkē jīnglún* of [[蕭塤]] Xiāo Xūn ([[KR3ei029]]), the *Nǚkē qièyào* of [[吳道源]] Wú Dàoyuán ([[KR3ei017]]), and the *Fùkē yùchǐ* of [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn'áo ([[KR3ei023]]).
