# Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng·Nǚkē 證治準繩·女科
*Standard for Diagnosis and Therapy: Women's Section*
by [[王肯堂]] (Wáng Kěntáng = Wáng Yǔtài = Niànxī jūshì, 1549–1613, late Míng)

## About the work

A five-*cè* gynecological section of the late-Míng comprehensive medical encyclopedia *Liùkē Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* 六科證治準繩 (or simply *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng*, [[KR3e0078]], 120 *juǎn*) by [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng (1549–1613). The *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* portion was completed in *Wànlì dīngwèi* (1607) along with the *Yòukē zhǔnshéng* (paediatrics). The work is one of the great late-Míng gynecological *summae*, providing comprehensive coverage of menstrual disorders, *bēnglòu*, *dàixià*, *qiúzǐ*, *tāiqián*, *línchǎn*, *chǎnhòu*, and women's miscellaneous diseases. Its *gāngmù* 綱目 (outline-and-detail) systematic organisation was the principal model adopted (with re-arrangement) by [[武之望]] Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jìyīn gāngmù* (1620; see [[KR3ei006]]).

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` `_000.txt` carries Wáng's *zìxù* 自序 (author's preface). Wáng narrates the historiographic context: gynecological specialisation begins with [[扁鵲]] Biǎnquè's *dàixià* medicine at Hándān; [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *[[傷寒雜病論]]* 傷寒雜病論 devoted one of three *juǎn* to women; [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sīmiǎo's *Qiānjīn fāng* gave priority of place to women's medicine. Wáng traces the line through [[昝殷]] Zàn Yīn's *Chǎnbǎo* 產寶 ([[KR3e0044]], Táng), [[李師聖]] Lǐ Shīshèng's *[[產論]]* 產論 and [[郭稽中]] Guō Jìzhōng's accompanying formulary, the *Chǎnyù bǎoqìng jí* 產育寶慶集 ([[KR3e0040]]) of [[杜荍]] Dù Qiú, and to [[陳自明]] Chén Zìmíng's *Fùrén dàquán liángfāng* 婦人大全良方 ([[KR3e0038]]) — the principal Sòng *fùkē* synthesis — which Wáng then takes as the foundation he organises systematically in his own work. The preface is one of the most detailed late-Míng historiographies of the *fùkē* sub-discipline available in any medical source.

### Abstract

Wáng Kěntáng (1549–1613, see [[王肯堂]]) was a late-Míng Confucian-medical (*rúyī* 儒醫) literatus-physician of Jīntán 金壇 (Jiāngsū). *Jìnshì* of 1589, with imperial-academy and provincial-administrative posts in Fujian, he turned to medicine after retirement. The *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng* (120 *juǎn*) was completed in stages over a decade and a half: *Zázhèng zhǔnshéng* and *Lèifāng* 1597–1598; *Shānghán zhǔnshéng* and *Yángyī zhǔnshéng* 1604; *Yòukē zhǔnshéng* and *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* 1607. The whole corpus is praised by the *Sìkù* editors as "*the medical school's standard reference*" (*yījiā zhī guīniè* 醫家之圭臬). The *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* — 1607, in five *cè* — is the gynecology section.

Doctrinally Wáng is balanced-comprehensive: neither over-cooling (against the Zhū Dānxī tradition) nor over-warming (against the Zhāng Jièbīn *wēnbǔ* counter-tradition). His method is to assemble citations from the major Sòng-Yuán-Míng authorities under each clinical heading with critical evaluative commentary, rather than to advance a partisan doctrinal position. This even-handed encyclopedic method made the work the principal late-Míng *standard reference* (*zhǔnshéng* 準繩 = "plumb-line-and-cord", i.e. standard of measurement) for gynecological practice. Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jìyīn gāngmù* (1620; see [[KR3ei006]]) is a critical re-organisation of Wáng's *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* into a tighter *gāngmù* hierarchical structure — Wǔ explicitly acknowledged Wáng as his principal source.

The work has been continuously in print since 1607 and remains in active scholarly use in modern PRC TCM gynecology research.

## Other points of interest

The *Nǚkē zhǔnshéng* is the **textual foundation** of both major late-Míng / early-Qīng systematic gynecologies: Wǔ Zhīwàng's *Jìyīn gāngmù* (1620; condensation and re-organisation) and [[蕭塤]] Xiāo Xūn's *Nǚkē jīnglún* (1684; citation-and-commentary expansion, see [[KR3ei029]]). Together these three works form the **trilogy of late-Míng / early-Qīng systematic gynecology** that shaped all subsequent imperial and Republican-era *fùkē* practice and teaching.
