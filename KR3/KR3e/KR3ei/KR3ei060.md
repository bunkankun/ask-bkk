# Chóngdìng chǎnyùn jí 重訂產孕集
*Revised Collection on Pregnancy and Childbirth*
by [[張曜孫]] (Zhāng Yàosūn, 1808–1863)

## About the work

Zhāng Yàosūn's own revised redaction of his earlier *Chǎnyùn jí* 產孕集 ([[KR3ei059]]), prepared in the late *Dàoguāng* / *Xiánfēng* period (1850s, before Zhāng's death in 1863). The revised edition incorporates clinical-experience additions from Zhāng's medical practice during his retreat at Wǔchāng during the Tàipíng disturbances, and reorganises the original tripartite *huáitāi / línchǎn / chǎnhòu* scheme with finer-grained subdivisions and a more extensive *jiājiǎn* (additions-and-subtractions) apparatus for each principal prescription.

### Abstract

The hxwd recension of KR3ei060 carries the base-edition indicator `BASEEDITION: T` (i.e. a *T-series* base) — i.e. derives from a Tóngzhì / Guāngxù era print rather than the earlier *Dàoguāng* witness. The catalog meta records only Zhāng Yàosūn as author, identifying the work explicitly as a revised redaction (*chóngdìng*) of [[KR3ei059]]. The composition window is bracketed to 1850–1863 — after the original *Chǎnyùn jí* and before Zhāng's death.

The relationship between the two recensions is that of author's working-copy and final author-revised text. Both are preserved in the Hǎiwài huíguī series. The textual relationship between them has not been the object of detailed Chinese-medicine philological study, and is one of several outstanding tasks in late-Qīng obstetrical-literature criticism.
