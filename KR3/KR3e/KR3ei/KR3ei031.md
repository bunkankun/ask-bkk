# Nǚkē bǎiwèn 女科百問
*A Hundred Questions on Women's Medicine*
by [[宋仲甫]] (Sòng Zhòngfǔ = [[齊仲甫]] Qí Zhòngfǔ, *fl.* 1220, Southern Sòng)

## About the work

A two-*juǎn* (*shàng* / *xià*) Southern-Sòng gynecology by [[宋仲甫]] Sòng Zhòngfǔ (= [[齊仲甫]] Qí Zhòngfǔ — name variant in the transmitted tradition), structured as a sequence of one hundred numbered *wèndá* 問答 (question-and-answer) entries covering the canonical *fùkē* topics: *jīngshuǐ* 經水 (menstruation), *bēnglòu* 崩漏, *dàixià* 帶下, *qiúzǐ* 求子, *tāiqián* 胎前, *línchǎn* 臨產, *chǎnhòu* 產後, and miscellaneous women's diseases. Each question articulates a clinical scenario and the corresponding answer provides étiological discussion, classical citation, and prescribed formula. The work opens with a remarkable opening question on "*jīngxuè yǐ fēn nánnǚ zhī běnyuán*" 精血以分男女之本源 ("*essence and blood as the root-source by which men and women are differentiated*") — a foundational doctrinal opening that sets the work's *jīngxuè* (essence-blood) framework.

### Abstract

Sòng Zhòngfǔ 宋仲甫 is identified in Sòng-Yuán medical-bibliographic literature as [[齊仲甫]] Qí Zhòngfǔ (the *Sòngshǐ yìwén zhì* and several early bibliographies use this form); the *Sòng/Qí* variant probably reflects scribal variation in the medieval transmission. He is a Southern-Sòng gynecologist active in the early 13th century. The *Nǚkē bǎiwèn* is one of the major Sòng-period gynecological works alongside [[陳自明]] Chén Zìmíng's *Fùrén dàquán liángfāng* 婦人大全良方 ([[KR3e0038]], 1237). The catalog's dating 南宋 is correct; the work is conventionally dated c. 1220–1230. The *bǎiwèn* 百問 catechetical format would later be replicated in numerous Qīng popular gynecologies (compare [[KR3ei004]] *Fùkē wèndá* and [[KR3ei034]] *Fùkē bǎibiàn*).

Doctrinally the work reflects the mature Northern-Song / early-Southern-Song synthesis: it integrates the *Sùwèn* / *Língshū* canonical framework with [[孫思邈]] Sūn Sīmiǎo's *Qiānjīn yàofāng* ([[KR3e0013]]), [[王燾]] Wáng Tāo's *Wàitái mìyào* ([[KR3e0015]]), [[陳言]] Chén Yán's *Sānyīn fāng* ([[KR3e0041]], 1174), and the *Héjì júfāng* ([[KR3e0033]]). It is a major secondary witness to Northern-Sòng gynecological doctrine. The work was widely cited by Yuán-Míng-Qīng gynecologists and remains in active use in modern PRC TCM scholarship.
