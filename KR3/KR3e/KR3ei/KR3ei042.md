# Fùshì nǚkē 傅氏女科
*The Fù-Family's Gynecology*
attributed to [[傅山]] Fù Shān (= Fù Qīngzhǔ, 1607–1684)

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* supplementary gynecological collection attributed to [[傅山]] Fù Shān, presented as a *gleaning* of *Nǚkē* materials not included in the main *FùQīngzhǔ nǚkē* [[KR3ei001]]. The text's first paragraph explicitly states: "*The Master originally had a Nǚkē circulating in the world; these few entries are not included in that Nǚkē, hence preserved here*" (女科（先生本有《女科》傳於世，此數條《女科》未載故存之）). The entries cover *chǎnhòu* 產後 (post-natal), *tāilòu tāidòng* 胎漏胎動 (pregnancy bleeding and threatened miscarriage), *zǐxuán* 子懸 (suspended foetus), *báidài* 白帶 (white vaginal discharge), and other specific clinical scenarios with the relevant formulas.

### Prefaces

The KR `hxwd` recension carries no separable preface; the work opens with the parenthetical editorial note quoted above identifying itself as a supplementary collection to the main *FùQīngzhǔ nǚkē*.

### Abstract

The work belongs to the broader FùQīngzhǔ pseudepigraphic medical corpus (see [[KR3ei001]] *FùQīngzhǔ nǚkē*; [[KR3ei021]] *FùQīngzhǔ nǚkē gēkuò*; [[KR3ed080]] *Dàxiǎo zhūzhèng fānglùn*; [[KR3ed047]] *Fùshì záfāng*). The catalog meta records no specific author and dynasty unspecified; internal evidence places it firmly in the post-1827 FùQīngzhǔ literary tradition. `notBefore` 1827 (= the date of the main *FùQīngzhǔ nǚkē* imprint) / `notAfter` 1900 brackets the range. For the complex authorship problems of the Fù-Shān-attributed medical corpus see [[KR3ei001]] and [[KR3ed080]].

The work is a brief supplement rather than a full systematic treatise. The prescribed formulas — *Bǔqì xuè jiājiǎn* 補氣血加減 modifications for post-partum conditions, *Bāzhēn tāng* 八珍湯 derivatives for *tāilòu*, and similar — fit recognisably within the FùQīngzhǔ formulary style.
