# Bìngjī shāzhuàn 病機沙篆
*Sand-Seal of Pathomechanism*
by [[李中梓]] (Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, *zì* Shìcái 士材, *hào* Niàn'é 念莪, 1588–1655) — *zhuàn* 撰
expanded by [[尤乘]] (Yóu Chéng, *zì* Shēngzhōu 生洲, *fl.* Kāngxī) — *zēngjí* 增輯

## About the work

A systematic exposition of pathomechanism (*bìngjī* 病機) for major internal-medicine syndromes, organised disease-by-disease with etiological discussion, differential diagnosis, and representative formulas. Lǐ Zhōngzǐ — one of the leading late-Míng systematisers and a doctrinal grandson of the Dānxī school via his teacher's lineage — composed the bulk of the text in the years before his death in 1655; his disciple Yóu Chéng later supplemented the prescriptions and case-notes (*zēngjí*) and printed the work as part of the *Shìcái sān shū* 士材三書 (the "Three Books of Shìcái," after Lǐ's *zì*) in *Kāngxī bǐngwǔ* = 1666.

### Abstract

The work is a substantial mid-Qīng example of the *bìngjī* 病機 (pathomechanism) genre — diagnostic monographs that work outwards from the pathophysiological mechanism of each syndrome to the corresponding therapeutic strategy, in the manner consolidated by [[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù in the *Sùwèn xuánjī yuánbìng shì* 素問玄機原病式. The Lǐ Zhōngzǐ–Yóu Chéng version is more clinically applied: each *bìngjī* discussion is paired with practical prescription policy and brief case material.

The compositional division of labour between Lǐ and Yóu is not always cleanly separable in surviving editions; Yóu's *zēngjí* additions include both supplementary formulas and editorial *àn* 按 commentary, and some passages may be Yóu's interpolations extending Lǐ's positions. The 1666 printed edition is the editio princeps; the work was reprinted in the *Shìcái sān shū* collected edition through the late Qīng. The standard editions are arranged in 4 *juǎn* (sometimes counted as 2 *juǎn* with 4 *zhāng* 章); the catalog records 2 *juǎn*, in line with this older counting.
