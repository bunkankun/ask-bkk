# Nèishāng jí yào 內傷集要
*Collected Essentials on Internal Damage*
by [[蔡貽績]] (Cài Yíjī, *zì* Nǎiān 乃庵, of Yōuxiàn 攸縣, Húnán)

## About the work

A 6-*juǎn* practical handbook on *nèi shāng* 內傷 (internal damage), printed in the early summer of Dàoguāng *guǐwèi* (1823) when the author was seventy-two. The work is the second half of a programmatic pair: the author had earlier (Jiāqìng *dīngchǒu* = 1817) printed [[傷寒瘟疫抉要]] *Shānghán wēnyì jué yào* on externally-contracted disease, and now adds [[虛勞失血集要]] *Xūláo shīxuè jí yào* on internal depletion and blood-loss, the two halves being recombined under the umbrella title *Nèishāng jí yào* and sponsored to print by friends [[劉錫祺]] Liú Xíqí, [[楊廣先]] Yáng Guǎngxiān, [[鍾昌言]] Zhōng Chāngyán, and [[蔡磻溪]] Cài Pánxī.

The work proceeds through fixed expository registers — 經旨 (classical principles), 脈法 (pulse signs), 病源 (etiology), 證治 (syndrome and treatment), 方法 (formulas) — applied successively to *xū sǔn* 虛損, 失血, and a battery of related complaints (盜汗, 遺精, 噎膈, 噫氣, 痰飲, 嘔吐, 腹滿, 反胃, 泄瀉, 瀉痢, 噯氣, 嘈雜, 黃疸, 水腫, 寒熱, 多寐, 不寐, 健忘, 怔忡, etc.). Marginal sigla key the reader: △ for pulse points, □ for symptoms, — for channels and collaterals.

### Abstract

The work's history is laid out in the *zì xù* 自序 of Dàoguāng 3 (1823). Cài Yíjī describes himself as having been a sickly child whom the physician [[陳學周]] Chén Xuézhōu repeatedly saved; on Chén's encouragement, after qualifying as a *dìzǐyuán* 弟子員 he turned to medical study, working through the *Líng shū* and *Sù wèn* and the *fāng shū* tradition. A first product was *Yī xué zhǐ yào* 醫學指要 (= [[KR3eb006]]), printed in Jiāqìng *rénshēn* (1812) with the patronage of one [[陳顧廬]] Chén Gùlú. A second project — outwardly-contracted and internally-caused disease, paired — was prepared next; the *Shānghán wēnyì jué yào* half was printed in Jiāqìng 22 (1817) with funds raised by [[易麗昭]] Yì Lìzhāo and others, but resources ran out and the *Xūláo shīxuè jí yào* half stayed in the author's box. In 1823 a fresh subscription enabled the unprinted half to be cut and bound with the first to form the present combined *Nèishāng jí yào*.

The 凡例 declares the work to be a focused exposition of *xū sǔn* and *shī xuè* extended by analogy to other inner-damage syndromes; it expressly refuses to separate male and female cases beyond the matter of parturition, on the grounds that "without distinction yet with distinction, with distinction yet without — the realization is not far". The treatment-philosophy follows the standard late-Míng / Qīng *wēnbǔ* mainstream ([[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ, [[張介賓]] Zhāng Jièbīn, [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, [[喻嘉言]] Yú Jiāyán are all quoted), with frequent appeal to [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng on pulse and the *Sù wèn* on etiology.

The catalog meta supplies no dates for the author. From his prefaces — born in the late Qiánlóng era, seventy-two *suì* in 1823 — his birth falls in the early 1750s; no death-date is recoverable from the work itself. No CBDB record. ## Other points of interest

Cài Yíjī's medical project is unusually well-anchored autobiographically: across the three prefaces in *Yī xué zhǐ yào*, *Shānghán wēnyì jué yào*, and the present *Nèishāng jí yào*, the funding subscribers are all named, making the work a small case-study in the patronage networks supporting medical printing in late-Qiānlóng / Jiāqìng / early-Dàoguāng Húnán.
