# Yǎngshēng yuèlǎn 養生月覽
*Monthly Compendium for Nourishing Life*
compiled by [[周守忠]] Zhōu Shǒuzhōng (*fl.* Southern Sòng, *Jiādìng* era).

## About the work

A two-juan **month-by-month** *yǎngshēng* anthology — the seasonal complement to the same author's much larger topical anthology *Yǎngshēng lèizuǎn* ([[KR3eo022]]). The text proceeds calendrically from the first month through the twelfth, each month subdivided into entries for specific dates (the New Year's day, the *yuánrì*, the *zǐ* hour of the first month, etc.) and seasonal observances. For each entry Zhōu cites a named source — *Yuèlìng tújīng* 月令圖經 ([[月令圖經]]), *Sìshí zuǎnyào* 四時纂要 ([[四時纂要]]), *Zá Wǔxíng shū* 雜五行書 ([[雜五行書]]), *Lǚgōng suìshí zájì* 呂公歲時雜記 ([[呂公歲時雜記]]), *Jīngchǔ suìshí jì* 荊楚歲時記 ([[KR2j0003]]), *Suǒsuìlù* 瑣碎錄 ([[瑣碎錄]]) of [[溫革]] Wēn Gé, *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽 ([[KR3k0012]]), *Zhōu Chǔ Fēngtǔ jì* 周處風土記 ([[周處風土記]]), *Cuīshí Sìmín yuèlìng* 崔實四民月令 ([[崔實四民月令]]), *Yùzhú bǎodiǎn* 玉燭寶典 ([[玉燭寶典]]) — many of these the principal pre-Sòng "seasonal almanac" (*yuèlìng*) sources, several preserved chiefly through Zhōu's anthology.

### Prefaces

The jicheng.tw reprint of the *Yuèlǎn* preserves no separate xù; the work opens directly with *juàn shàng zhèngyuè* 卷上正月 ("Upper Juan, First Month"). For the editorial *xù*-tradition see [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn's joint preface to the *Yǎngshēng lèizuǎn* ([[KR3eo022]]).

### Abstract

The *Yuèlǎn* is one of the most important Southern-Sòng witnesses to the **early-medieval seasonal-almanac tradition**. Many of the specific *yuèlìng* texts [[周守忠]] Zhōu Shǒuzhōng cites — particularly the *Yuèlìng tújīng* 月令圖經, the *Sìshí zuǎnyào* 四時纂要 (the Táng / Five-Dynasties almanac attributed to [[韓鄂]] Hán È), the *Lǚgōng suìshí zájì* 呂公歲時雜記, the *Suǒsuìlù* 瑣碎錄, the *Zá xiūyǎng shū* 雜修養書 ([[雜修養書]]) — are partly or wholly preserved through Zhōu's anthology, making the *Yuèlǎn* one of the principal modern sources for the reconstruction of these earlier traditions. The very first entry, citing the *Yuèlìng tújīng* on the burning of dung at the *zǐ*-*chǒu* hour of the first lunar day, is itself a major piece of evidence for the *Yuèlìng tújīng* — a text now lost except for fragments.

The work's organisation as a **regimen-by-date schedule** marks it as the prototype of the late-imperial popular almanac (*lìshū* 曆書) tradition, in which the *yǎngshēng* directives are tabulated against the days of the year. Zhōu's *Yuèlǎn* effectively codified the **classical "yǎngshēng calendar"** for subsequent Yuán-Míng compilers.

The dating bracket 1208–1220 reflects Zhōu's known active period in the *Jiādìng* era. The catalog meta records dynasty as 宋; the present record specifies 南宋.

## Other points of interest

The *Yuèlǎn* is the principal medieval-Chinese *yǎngshēng* almanac and the source of many of the calendrically-fixed observances (the *Túsū jiǔ* of the *yuánrì*, the *juéfǎn* 屠蘇酒 ritual of the New Year, the *zhúyì* 桃逸 talismanic procedures, the *jíbàozhú* 庭前爆竹 firecracker tradition) that became part of the late-imperial popular calendar. The [[胡文煥]] Hú Wénhuàn 1596 Qiántáng print made the work available again in late-Míng circulation, and the present jicheng.tw recension is the most accessible modern reprint.
