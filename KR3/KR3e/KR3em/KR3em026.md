# Yānhóu mìjí 咽喉秘集
*Secret Collection on Throat and Larynx*
attributed to "Zhāng Sòngliáng" 張宋良 (almost certainly a typographic slip for [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōngliáng of [[KR3em024]] *Hóukē zhǐzhǎng*) with a "Mistress / Master Wú" 吳氏 named jointly in the catalog.

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* Qīng laryngology compendium covering the standard mid-Qīng "Zhāng-school" doctrinal architecture: a *zǒnglùn* 總論 (general theory) restating the *yīyīn yīyáng jié ér wéi bì* 一陰一陽結而為痹 framework from the *Nèijīng*, a six-pulse diagnostic schema mapping the laryngology to the *cùnguānchǐ* 寸關尺 pulse-positions on both wrists, a *fēnjīng* 分經 anatomical division of the throat into channels, and the systematic application of *liùwèi tāng* 六味湯 加減 as universal base prescription. This is followed by a clinical typology of named conditions (single, double, rosary-string *rǔé* 乳蛾, *chánhóu fēng* 纏喉風, *hóubì* 喉閉, etc.) and a formulary of insufflation powders, decoctions, and external dressings — including the *jiédú zǐjīn dān* 結毒紫金丹, *bóhé tāng* 薄荷湯, *huíxuě fāng* 迴雪方, and the *yágān fāng* 牙疳方.

### Prefaces

The witness preserves no preface; the text opens directly with the *zǒnglùn*.

### Abstract

The catalog meta records two authors: 張宋良 and 吳氏 ("Mistress / Master Wú", anonymous within a clan). The orthography 張宋良 is preserved in the present frontmatter per cataloging convention, but is almost certainly a Qīng-period typographic slip for the well-attested [[張宗良]] Zhāng Zōngliáng (*zì* Liúxiān 留仙) of Sūzhōu, author of [[KR3em024]] *Hóukē zhǐzhǎng* (preface 1757). The internal doctrinal apparatus of the present text — the six-flavour-decoction architecture (六味湯加減), the right-hand-pulse mapping to *fèifēng* 肺風 / *fèifúhán* 肺伏寒, the left-hand-pulse mapping to *xīnhuǒ* 心火 / *shènxū huǒyán* 腎虛火炎, the explicit *liùwèi tāng* universal-base strategy — is essentially identical to that of *Hóukē zhǐzhǎng*, and modern Chinese specialist scholarship (cf. 耿鑒庭 et al.) treats the two works as products of the same author and lineage. The Wú-clan 吳氏 attribution may reflect a co-transmitter or family relative responsible for the redaction of the present recension; no individual biographical detail attaches to this co-attribution and, following standard convention, no separate person note has been created for the anonymous 吳氏 collaborator.

The composition window is set to 1700–1800 — broadly congruent with the 1757 preface of [[KR3em024]] and allowing for the *Yānhóu mìjí*'s recension to have been finalized within the same Qián-lóng-era working life of Zhāng Zōngliáng. The catalog meta gives only "清" without finer dating.

## Other points of interest

The doctrinal twinning of *Yānhóu mìjí* with [[KR3em024]] *Hóukē zhǐzhǎng* — same author (under orthographic variant), same therapeutic system, same characteristic phraseology — makes the pair an instructive specimen of late-imperial intra-lineage textual proliferation: one major systematic monograph (*Hóukē zhǐzhǎng*) and a one-*juǎn* compact handbook (*Yānhóu mìjí*) co-existing for different clinical and pedagogical uses within the same family practice.
