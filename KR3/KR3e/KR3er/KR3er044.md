# Dāntái yù àn 丹臺玉案
*The Jade-Tablet Cases of the Cinnabar Terrace*
by [[孫文胤]] Sūn Wényìn (*zì* Duìwēi 對薇, *hào* Zàixiá 在峽 / Zàipéng 在蓬, late-Míng).

## About the work

A six-*juǎn* late-Míng clinical compendium covering internal medicine, gynaecology, pediatrics, ophthalmology, and external medicine. The title metaphor *dāntái yùàn* — "the jade-tablet cases of the cinnabar terrace" — frames the work as a Daoist-resonant clinical reference (the *dāntái* is the alchemical altar of the inner-cultivation Daoist tradition; *yùàn* are the immortal's record-tablets). The work is methodologically distinctive in its self-conscious synthesis of the Sòng-Jīn-Yuán four-master tradition with the Lǐngshū / Sùwèn canonical literature, the *Huángtíng nèijǐng* 黃庭內景 Daoist physiological tradition, and the *Màijīng* 脈經 of Wáng Shūhé. Sūn Wényìn's clinical orientation reflects the late-Míng warming-tonifying ascendancy: characteristic formulae include the *Bǔzhōng yìqì tāng* and the *Shēngmài sǎn*, and the work consistently emphasises *yuánqì* 元氣 (primordial-qì) preservation as the therapeutic foundation.

### Prefaces

The hxwd `_000.txt` opens with a preface by an official who befriended Sūn Wényìn during a stationing at Dānghú 當湖 in *yǐchǒu* 乙丑 winter (= winter 1625). The preface narrates that Sūn was a Xīnān 新安 (Hīzhōu) native who had moved to Běigù 北固 (Zhènjiāng) and become the most-sought-after physician of the lower-Yáng-zǐ region; the prefacer met him at Dānghú and "from his conversation, every word was striking; from his clinical tests, every test was effective" (與語語輒奇，試技技輒效). The prefacer further reports that Sūn's training came from his careful study of the *Língshū*, *Huángtíng nèijǐng*, *Màijīng* of Wáng Shūhé, and the four Jīn–Yuán masters; he urges Sūn to write down his clinical doctrine, against the objection of the dictum *"yī, yán yì yě"* 醫，言意也 (medicine is intention, not amenable to verbal transmission). The work was therefore composed in response to this prefatory urging and brought to print in the late *Wànlì* / *Chóngzhēn* era.

### Abstract

Sūn Wényìn (Duìwēi) was a Xīn-ān-born late-Míng physician active in the Zhènjiāng 鎮江 region; his clinical practice spanned the lower Yangtze, with frequent consultative travel to Sūzhōu and Hángzhōu. The composition of *Dāntái yùàn* is conventionally bracketed at *Tiānqǐ* / early *Chóngzhēn* era (c. 1625–1636); the *yǐchǒu* (1625) preface date provides the secure *terminus post quem* and the standard Chinese-medicine reference works place the work's composition in this Wànlì-Chóngzhēn transitional window. The work was repeatedly reprinted through the Qīng and was particularly influential in the Hangzhou-Suzhou medical milieu. Preserved in the *Hǎiwài huíliú zhōngyī gǔjí cóngshū* through Japanese collections.

## Translations and research
