# Yījīng mìzhǐ 醫經秘旨
*The Hidden Purport of the Medical Classics*
nominally attributed to [[盛寅]] Shèng Yín (*zì* Qǐdōng 啟東, 1375–1441, Sūzhōu); **actual author** [[江之蘭]] Jiāng Zhīlán (*zì* Hánzhēng 含徵, early-Qīng, Shèxiàn 歙縣).

## About the work

A one-*juǎn* short treatise of about ten topical essays on *Nèijīng* therapeutic-principle passages — *fact-on-the-ground identical with the first ten essays of [[KR3er103]]* *Yījīn yī fá* 醫津一筏 (1662) by Jiāng Zhīlán of Shèxiàn. The traditional attribution to **Shèng Yín** 盛寅 (the early-Míng Imperial Physician of Sūzhōu, 1375–1441), purportedly composed at his *Dòngtíng* 洞庭 lakeside retreat and presented under the date *Yǒnglè shíyǒu liù nián mùchūn shànghuàn* 永樂十有六年暮春上浣 = *Yǒnglè* 16 / 1418, late spring third month — is a **textual fraud**. The text was extracted from Jiāng Zhīlán's *Yījīn yī fá* in the late Kāngxī or Yōngzhèng era and re-attributed to Shèng Yín. The substitution was unmasked by Gāo Sīqián 高思潛's 1925 *kǎo* (philological investigation) for the *Sānsān yīshū* 三三醫書 edition (cf. [[KR3er103]]).

The catalog acknowledges the suspect attribution by writing *yuántí Shèng Yín* 原題 盛寅 ("originally attributed to Shèng Yín"). The *Sìkù quánshū zǒngmù tíyào* committee had already noted irregularities in the attribution.

The content — the standard *Nèijīng* therapeutic-principle essay on *zhìbìng bì qiú qí běn* 治病必求其本, the eleven illustrative examples (*yǐ hán zhì rè, yǐ rè zhì hán, yǐ bǔ zhì jī, yǐ yìqì zhì mǎn*, etc., and the seven *fǎnzhì* 反治 paradoxical applications), and the analytic exposition — is unambiguously identifiable with Jiāng's known authentic work.

### Abstract

The actual composition date follows Jiāng Zhīlán's 1662 *Yījīn yī fá*. The fraudulent Shèng-Yín attribution dates from the early 18th c., circulated under the title *Yījīng mìzhǐ* in successive Qīng reprints (including the 1836 *Dàoguāng bǐngshēn* re-collation here represented), and was definitively unmasked only in 1925.

Shèng Yín (1375–1441), the early-Míng Sūzhōu Imperial Physician (*Tàiyī yuàn yùyī* 太醫院御醫) and a documented historical figure, did **not** compose this work; he was associated separately with the early-Míng court medical-academy curriculum. His being chosen as a plausible authorial cover suggests the forger had specific knowledge of Sūzhōu medical-historical reputation. (Note: the early-Míng Shèng Yín is distinct from the Yuán *Shèng Yín* of similar dates whom CBDB also has, and the *Gūsū Qǐdōng* signing position of the spurious preface explicitly identifies the early-Míng Sūzhōu figure.)

CBDB has Shèng Yín at c_personid 88775 (early-Míng, Sūzhōu).

## Translations and research

For Jiāng Zhī-lán's authentic *Yī-jīn yī fá* and its philological recovery see [[KR3er103]]. The fraudulent *Yī-jīng mì-zhǐ* attribution is itself a useful case-study in late-Imperial textual misattribution; it is briefly discussed in Wú Yī-lì 吳逸力 et al., *Xīn-ān yī-jí kǎo* 新安醫籍考 (1990s).

## Other points of interest

The fraudulent attribution to an early-Míng Imperial Physician (rather than to the obscure provincial physician Jiāng Zhīlán) doubled the work's commercial appeal in the 18th-c. medical-book market — a notable case of late-Imperial book-trade prestige-arbitrage. The work continued to circulate under the *Yījīng mìzhǐ* title (with the spurious Shèng Yín attribution) into the early Republican period, when Gāo Sīqián's 1925 *Sānsān yīshū* re-attribution finally restored Jiāng Zhīlán's authorship.
