# Yīxué yàozé 醫學要則
*The Principles of Medical Learning*
by [[沈懋言]] Shěn Màoyán (Qīng-era physician, dates uncertain).

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* mid-Qīng pedagogical primer in 24 *zé* 則 ("principles"), composed in 1743 and first printed in *Qiánlóng* 59 / 1794. The work covers: (i) *Nèijīng* doctrinal principles; (ii) materia medica properties (*běncǎo yàoxìng* 本草藥性); (iii) pulse-diagnosis and channel-doctrine (*màijīng jīngluò* 脈經經絡); (iv) treatment of miscellaneous disorders (*zábìng zhìfǎ* 雜病治法). Each *zé* anthologizes selected citations from the *Nèijīng* and post-Hàn medical authors with the compiler's interlinear annotations.

The work is a typical mid-Qīng didactic primer aimed at students entering medical practice. Later bibliographers have judged the arrangement uneven and the doctrinal emphasis diffuse — a characteristic limitation of the *yàozé* / *yàoyán* primer genre, which tended toward eclectic anthology rather than systematic exposition.

The author Shěn Màoyán 沈懋言 may be related to (but is distinct from) the Qián-lóng-era Shěn Màofā 沈懋發 ([[KR3er093]] *Kuàicán piān*, c. 1777) — the two names share the *Mào* 懋 generation-character but the relationship is not securely established.

### Abstract

The 1743 composition / 1794 print dating is from the Douban-recorded *Qiánlóng* 59 imprint (*Qīng Qiánlóng wǔshíjiǔ nián Yīxué yàozé*). The composition window of 1743–1794 is adopted here. Shěn Màoyán is poorly documented; not in CBDB.
