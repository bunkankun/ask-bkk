# Huángdì Nèijīng Sùwèn jiàoyì 黃帝內經素問校義
*Collation Notes on the Yellow Emperor's Inner Classic, Basic Questions*
by [[胡澍]] (Hú Shù, 1825–1872, 清) — author

## About the work

The *Sùwèn jiàoyì* is a short but philologically rigorous late-Qīng collation of the *Huángdì nèijīng Sùwèn* by **Hú Shù** 胡澍 (1825–1872), *zì* Hǎifǔ 荄甫, a scholar of phonology and the Hàn Confucian classics native to Jīxī 績溪 in Huīzhōu (Ānhuī). The text survives in only **one juan** (the project was left unfinished at the author's death) and treats roughly seventy difficult readings in the opening chapters of the *Sùwèn*. Hú collates the [[林億]] Lín Yì recension against the Yuán Xióng Zōnglì 熊宗立 print, the Míng *Dàozàng* recension ([[KR5d0040]]), and pre-Táng quotations preserved in encyclopaedias, applying the standard methodology of the late-Qīng *Hànxué* school: phonetic loan-character analysis, comparison of variant graphs, and recourse to early citations to recover archaic readings lost in [[王冰]] Wáng Bīng's interpretation.

### Prefaces

The opening (`KR3ea006_000.txt`) carries an anonymous third-person preface — almost certainly by [[俞樾]] Yú Yuè or a close associate, in characteristic Qián-Jiā style — locating Hú Shù in the lineage of Hàn-philological revivalism (中年多病，留心方書) and reviewing five of his most important readings as samples: (i) on 飲食有節起居有常不妄作勞 (1.1) Hú restores [[全元起]] Quán Yuánqǐ's reading 不妄不作 against Wáng Bīng's 不妄作勞, citing the *Yuè lìng* commentary in [[鄭玄]] Zhèng Xuán for 作 = 詐 and demonstrating the rhyme pattern (數度/俱去); (ii) on 不時御神 he restores 時 = 善 (citing the *Máo zhuàn* on the *Xiǎoyǎ Qí biàn* 頍弁); (iii) on 皆謂之 he reads 謂 as a loan for 為 (citing the [[王充]] Wáng Chōng *Lùnhéng Xiào lì piān* 效力篇 quotation of the *Shàngshū Zǐ cái* 梓材); (iv) on 唯聖人從之故身無奇病 he emends 奇 to 苛 (= 疴) on graphical and contextual grounds. These are representative of his method throughout.

### Abstract

Hú Shù was a leading member of the late-Qīng Wǎn-school (皖派) of philological exegesis, a friend of [[俞樾]] Yú Yuè, [[孫詒讓]] Sūn Yíràng, and [[章太炎]] Zhāng Tàiyán's teacher Yú Mǎngfán. His main contributions are to phonology (the *Shuōwén jiězì jiào yì* 說文解字校議) and to the philology of the *Yīng shī* and the *Mencius*. The *Sùwèn jiàoyì* was undertaken in the last decade of his life, when chronic illness drew him to medical literature; he died in 1872 before completing the project. The single-juan extant fragment was edited and printed posthumously by **Yú Yuè** ([[俞樾]]) in 1881 as part of the *Hú Shǎo zōngbó yízhù* 胡少宗伯遺著 collection, and reissued in modern collections such as the *Sānsānyī jí* 三三醫籍 (1924) and the People's Medical Publishing's *Lìdài Sùwèn yánjiū wénjí*.

[[俞樾]] Yú Yuè's own *Nèijīng biànyán* 內經辨言 ([[KR3ea034]]) is the philologically closest companion work; together with Hú's *Jiàoyì* they constitute the foundational late-Qīng philological corpus on the *Sùwèn* and are routinely cited together in modern critical apparatuses (e.g. in the Renmin Weisheng punctuated edition of the *Huángdì nèijīng*).
