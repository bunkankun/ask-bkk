# Nèijīng yàoyuè 內經藥瀹
*Pharmacopoeial Nourishment from the Inner Classic*
by [[張驥]] (Zhāng Jì, 1859–1924, 民國) — author

## About the work

The *Nèijīng yàoyuè* in ten juan is the late-Qīng / early-Republican (民國) *Nèijīng*-based pharmacopoeia of **Zhāng Jì** 張驥 (*zì* Yǐnlíng 隱陵, *hào* Zhìjì 治紀), a Republican-era Chéngdū physician active in the medical-publishing milieu of Sìchuān. The work systematically extracts every *Nèijīng* passage that mentions a drug, a flavour (酸辛苦鹹甘淡), or a thermic property (寒熱溫涼), arranges them under the standard *qìwèi* 氣味 system of materia medica, and produces a "*Nèijīng* pharmacopoeia" — anchored in the principle, stated in the author's preface, that *Nèijīng* speaks not of drugs as such but of climatic-thermic-flavour categories that constitute the **doctrinal foundation** for all subsequent materia medica.

### Prefaces

The author's preface (`KR3ea042_000.txt`) addresses an objection: the *Nèijīng*, unlike the *Shénnóng běncǎo jīng*, does not enumerate drugs by species and is therefore (it is said) "not in the same category" (不與本經同類) as a pharmacopoeia. Zhāng's answer: the *Nèijīng* names the *qì* and *wèi* of every substance the body encounters ("天食人以五氣，地食人以五味"), and these categories are themselves the pharmacopoeia in its theoretical foundation. The *Shénnóng běncǎo* details the *yàomíng* 藥名 (drug names) while the *Nèijīng* furnishes the *fán* 凡 (general principles) and the *lì* 例 (typical cases). The title 藥瀹 is glossed from the *Zǐhuázǐ yīdàopiān* 子華子醫道篇: *yào zhě, yuè yě; yuè zhě, yǎng yě* 藥者，瀹也；瀹者，養也 ("drug is *yuè*; *yuè* is nourishment").

### Abstract

Zhāng Jì was a major Chéngdū bibliophile-physician active in the early Republican era. He collected and reprinted a substantial corpus of Hàn-Sòng medical texts in his *Sānsānyī jí* 三三醫籍 cluster (the same series that printed [[胡澍]] Hú Shù's *Sùwèn jiàoyì*). His original works include the present *Nèijīng yàoyuè* (1924), a companion *Nèijīng fāng* 內經方 (extracted prescriptions from the *Nèijīng*), the *Nèijīng yùnqì zhìyào* 內經運氣治要, and the *Léigōngzhāng* 雷公章 commentary. He died in 1924, the year of this work's first print. The jicheng.tw catalog dynasty 清 should strictly be 民國 (early Republic); the discrepancy is noted in the frontmatter.

The work is the most systematic late-traditional attempt to read the *Nèijīng* as a complete pharmacopoeia and has become a primary reference for the modern PRC *Nèijīng* pharmacopoeia studies (e.g. Zhāng Cānjiǎ 張燦玾's *Nèijīng yàowù tōngjiě* 內經藥物通解, 1985).
