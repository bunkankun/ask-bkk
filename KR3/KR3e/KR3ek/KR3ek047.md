# Chúnyìlú jí (chuāngyáng) 純懿廬集（瘡瘍）
*Anthology of the Hall of Pure Virtue (Sores and Ulcers)*
anonymous; a chapter / fascicle extracted from a larger *Chúnyìlú* 純懿廬 collection of unknown overall provenance.

## About the work

A concise late-Qīng *wàikē* fascicle on common superficial skin sores, extracted from the larger anonymous *Chúnyìlú* (the *Chúnyìlú* being a private studio name; the larger collection's authorship has not been recovered). Internal evidence — the text cites the imperial *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* [[KR3ek009]] (1742) — fixes the composition as post-1742; the stylistic register is later-Qīng. Defensible composition bracket: 1742–1911. Listed in UNCERTAIN.md.

### Abstract

There is no preface in the source file; the text opens directly with the *zhèngwén* 正文. *No tíyào / preface located in source.*

The work is a tightly organised theoretical-clinical fascicle on superficial skin sores classified under four headings — *shī chuāng* 濕瘡 (damp sores), *shā chuāng* 砂瘡 (gritty sores), *nóngkē chuāng* 膿窠瘡 (boil-nest sores), *zuòbǎn chuāng* 坐板瘡 (sitting-board sores, i.e. perineal dermatitis) — with aetiology in liver-spleen disequilibrium and *fēngrè* / *shīrè* 風熱 / 濕熱 invasion. The author proceeds in *Nèijīng*-style aphoristic *wèndá* 問答 pairs, each clinical sub-pattern followed by a tailored formula: *jīngqiāng sǎn* 荊羌散, *bòhé kūcǎo tāng* 薄荷枯草湯, *cāngzhú dāngguī tāng* 蒼朮當歸湯, *cāngzhú yínhuā tāng* 蒼朮銀花湯, *shēngqí chuānxiōng tāng* 生耆川芎湯, *dúhuó shēngdì tāng* 獨活生地湯, *dǎncǎo shēngdì tāng* 膽草生地湯, *shúdì yínhuā tāng* 熟地銀花湯, *shúdì ròuguì tāng* 熟地肉桂湯, *rǔxiāng jièsuì tāng* 乳香芥穗湯, *yuánhuā rǔxiāng tāng* 芫花乳香湯, *kǔshēn dìyú tāng* 苦參地榆湯, and others. The text cites the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* on *zuòbǎn chuāng* = *fēng gān* 風疳.

The work is distinctive in its compression and its pulse-and-pattern-driven (rather than formulary-driven) presentation — characteristic of the Jiāng-Zhè scholar-physician strand of late-Qīng medicine. It is a useful concise late-Qīng *wàikē* monograph on common skin disorders, valuable for its tight pulse–pattern–formula trinity and for evidence of the integration of *zábìng* 雜病 / *shānghán* 傷寒 pulse logic into *wàikē*.

The *Chúnyìlú* collection is little studied and the work's authorship has not been recovered; further bibliographic work is needed. Authorial absence in catalog meta is preserved.
