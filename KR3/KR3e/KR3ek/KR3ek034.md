# Yángkē gāngyào 瘍科綱要
*A Framework for Ulcer Medicine*
by [[張壽頤]] (Zhāng Shòuyí, *hào* Shānléi 山雷, 1873–1934)

## About the work

One of the most influential Republican-era TCM textbooks for external medicine, in 2 *juǎn* (卷上 / 卷下). Zhāng Shòuyí (*hào* Shānléi 山雷) was a founding teacher of the modern Chinese-medicine school system; this work was used as classroom material at the Lánxī Zhōngyī Zhuānmén Xuéxiào 蘭谿中醫專門學校 (Zhèjiāng), one of the first formal TCM colleges. The work marks the modernisation of the *yángkē* genre — moving from miscellany-of-formulae to systematic clinical reasoning — and is one of the principal Republican-era responses to the perceived need to professionalise traditional surgery against Western competition. First published 1917; circulated in revised form into the late 1920s.

### Abstract

There is no *_000.txt* (no front-matter file) in the Kanripo digitisation; the WYG-editions field does not apply to this Republican text. The work opens directly with *juǎn* shàng 第一章 *Wàiyáng zǒng lùn* 外瘍總論, which functions as its programmatic preface. Zhāng polemically rejects Wáng Hóngxù [[王洪緒]] (cf. [[KR3ek017]] *Wàikē zhèngzhì quánshēng jí*) for equating *yōng* 癰 with yang and *jū* 疽 with yin, arguing instead that *yōng* simply means "blockage" (*yōng* 壅) and *jū* "obstruction" (*jǔ* 沮 / *zǔ* 阻), and that both must be analysed by careful application of yīnyáng along multiple axes simultaneously (location, depth, speed of progression, virulence, hardness, pain quality). He aligns himself doctrinally with the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Wàikē xīnfǎ yàojué* [[KR3ek009]] tradition against the *quánshēng pài*'s simpler colour-based dichotomy.

The two *juǎn* form a systematic *wàikē* handbook combining classical theory with modern textbook organisation. Topical sections cover the general theory of external lesions (yīnyáng, swelling, pain, itch, pus, fistula formation, sequelae), then specific lesion classes (*yōngjū* 癰疽, *liúzhù* 流注, *luǒlì* 瘰癧, *tánhé* 痰核, *yángméi* 楊梅, *dīngdú* 疔毒). Strong didactic and critical orientation throughout; explicitly written as classroom teaching material.

Zhāng Shòuyí (*hào* Shānléi 山雷; *zì* Shòufǔ 壽甫) was born in Jiādìng 嘉定 (modern Shànghǎi) in 1873 and died in 1934. After the 1911 revolution he taught at the Lánxī medical college, where he trained a generation of Mínguó physicians. His major works include the *Běncǎo zhèngyì* 本草正義 ([[KR3ec063]], printed 1932), the *Zhōngfēng kǎoquán* 中風斠詮, and the *Shānléi yīxué cóngshū* 山雷醫學叢書. The *Běncǎo zhèngyì* is the principal Republican-era critical pharmacopoeia and the immediate predecessor of the modern PRC TCM materia medica curriculum.

## Other points of interest

The work's explicit pedagogical orientation makes it one of the clearest documents of the early-Republican TCM-college transformation of *wàikē*: the lesion-by-lesion miscellany of late-imperial surgical handbooks gives way to a textbook structured around general theory, then disease classes, then differential and prescription. Compare *Tánlì fǎmén* [[KR3ek033]], a contemporary work that retains the older family-lineage *jiāchuán* form.
