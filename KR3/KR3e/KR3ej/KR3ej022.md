# Cíyòu xīnshū 慈幼新書
*A New Book of Compassionate Childcare*
by [[程雲鵬]] Chéng Yúnpéng (撰); preface by [[張希良]] Zhāng Xīliáng

## About the work

A fourteen-*juǎn* late-Míng / early-Qīng paediatric compendium by [[程雲鵬]] Chéng Yúnpéng (*zì* Fèngchú 鳳雛), a Húběi physician and *wénzhāng* essayist of the *Shùnzhì* — early *Kāngxī* generation. The catalog meta records the dynasty as 明; internal evidence (the recorded close association with the eminent Qīng scholar [[魏禧]] Wèi Xǐ, 1624–1681) confirms the work as in fact early Qīng (c. 1650–1700). The work covers paediatric medicine *guǎngbó jīngwēi* 廣博精微 (broadly and minutely) — from *tāichǎn* 胎產 (obstetrics) through *dòuzhěn* 痘疹 (smallpox-and-eruptive-fevers), *jīngxián* 驚癇 (convulsions and epilepsy), *hánrè* 寒熱 (cold-and-heat disorders), down to *ěrmù hóuchǐ* 耳目喉齒 (ear-eye-throat-teeth) and *chuāngjiē jièxuǎn* 瘡癤疥癬 (sores and skin disorders).

### Prefaces

**Preface** by [[張希良]] Zhāng Xīliáng of Huáng'ān 黃安. Zhāng frames the work in terms of Confucian *shì jūnzǐ* 士君子 social-ethical obligation: a gentleman serves the world in any capacity, *suī qióng ér zàixià* 雖窮而在下 (even if poor and in a lowly position); medicine is one such service. Zhāng records his prior knowledge of Chéng's reputation as a *wénzhāng jùzǐ* (literary giant) of the Chǔ region through [[蔣慎齋]] Jiǎng Shènzhāi's recommendation during Zhāng's tenure as *sīduó* 司鐸 (Provincial Education Officer) of Jiāngxià 江夏. He then records the famous Wèi-Xǐ-on-Chéng's-medicine anecdote ([[魏禧]] Wèi Xǐ's *Wǔhóu yòngbīng* 武侯用兵 praise, Chéng's *Dèng Yǔ wèicháng wàngshā yīrén* 鄧禹未嘗妄殺一人 deflection) and Chéng's parallel inner-alchemy training with the Yánlíng and Huáshān adepts. Zhāng closes with a self-deprecating contrast: as a former *Mùtiān* (Hanlin) academic with administrative responsibility over the Zhèjiāng *shìxí* 士習 (scholarly customs), he had been frustrated by his inability to alleviate the *chēxià chuāngyí* 車下創痍 (the wounds of the people beside the road); Chéng, by contrast, can through his book *jiē* (raise) the people *yú rénshòu zhī yù* 於仁壽之域 (into the region of humaneness and longevity).

### Abstract

The *Cíyòu xīnshū* is one of the more clinically grounded early-Qīng paediatric works, distinguished by its synthesis of mainstream paediatric *zàngfǔ* doctrine with the *Wǔhóu yòngbīng* ([[諸葛亮]] Zhūgě Liàng-as-strategist) metaphor of clinical reasoning — i.e. an emphasis on case-specific tactical adaptation against the prescription-formulary norm. The work circulated in the Húběi region in manuscript and early print, with an early-Qīng imprint preserved in the jicheng.tw recension. Its fourteen *juǎn* (ctext.org preserves *juǎn* 4 *dòuchuāng* 痘瘡 as a representative chapter) cover the standard paediatric disease range; the section structure follows the conventional Wàn-family / Xuē-family Míng paediatric template rather than the [[陳復正]] Chén Fùzhèng *jīng / chù* polemical re-classification. The work is among the principal Húběi-regional paediatric works of the seventeenth century, complementing the better-known *Yòuyòu jíchéng* (Lǐngnán, 1750, [[KR3ej013]]) and *Yīzōng jīnjiàn yòukē xīnfǎ* (imperial, 1742, [[KR3ej029]]).

## Other points of interest

The catalog meta's dynasty attribution to *Míng* is incorrect. The Zhāng Xīliáng preface's reference to Wèi Xǐ (1624–1681) makes the work early Qīng beyond doubt; a reasonable composition window is c. 1650–1700. The case-driven *Wǔhóu yòngbīng* metaphor of clinical reasoning is one of the most articulated statements of the case-specific-tactical position in early-Qīng paediatric writing.
