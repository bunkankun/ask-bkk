# Jīnguì qǐyuè · Yòukē 金匱啟鑰（幼科）
*Opening the Lock of the Golden Coffer — Paediatrics*
by [[黃朝坊]] Huáng Cháofāng (撰)

## About the work

The paediatric section of [[黃朝坊]] Huáng Cháofāng's (*fl. Dàoguāng* era) collected medical *cóngshū* the *Jīnguì qǐyuè* 金匱啟鑰 (Opening the Lock of the Golden Coffer, c. 1820–1850), a multi-work paediatric, gynaecological, and internal-medicine collection. Huáng was a Qīng paediatric specialist of the *Dàoguāng* generation; his collected *cóngshū* is one of the secondary Qīng-period regional-physician compendia. The paediatric volume covers the standard paediatric disease-range in six *juǎn* and is the principal modern witness to Huáng's paediatric doctrine.

### Abstract

The *Jīnguì qǐyuè · Yòukē* is the paediatric section of Huáng Cháofāng's *cóngshū*. The work's frame is the standard High-Qīng paediatric synthesis — [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ *zàngfǔ* doctrine, *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* imperial orthodoxy, integration of *mádòu* 麻痘 (measles-and-smallpox) chapters from the Wàn-family and [[陳文中]] Chén Wénzhōng traditions, and Sòng-Míng paediatric prescription practice. The six *juǎn* cover the conventional paediatric disease-range. The title *Jīnguì qǐyuè* 金匱啟鑰 (Opening the Lock of the Golden Coffer) evokes the [[張仲景]] Zhāng Zhòngjǐng *Jīnguì yàolüè* 金匱要略 canonical reference and frames the work as opening the inherited medical canon for paediatric application. The work circulated principally in regional Qīng paediatric practice and was preserved in late-Qīng *cóngshū* recensions.
