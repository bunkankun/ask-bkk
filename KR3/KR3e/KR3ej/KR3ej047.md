# Mákē huórén quánshū 麻科活人全書
*Comprehensive Book of Life-Saving Measles Medicine*
by [[謝玉瓊]] Xiè Yùqióng (撰)

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* mid-Qīng specialty treatise on measles (*mázhěn* 麻疹) by [[謝玉瓊]] Xiè Yùqióng, *fl. Qiánlóng — Jiāqìng* era. The work is one of the three principal Qīng-period measles references — alongside the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn yòukē xīnfǎ yàojué* 醫宗金鑑幼科心法要訣 measles chapter ([[KR3ej029]]) and [[張霖]] Zhāng Lín's *Mázhèng jíchéng* 麻症集成 — and is the most clinically organised of the three: Xiè codified the 72 measles symptom-patterns (the *qīshíèr zhèng* 七十二症) that became the standard nosological framework of late-Qīng measles paediatric practice.

### Prefaces

The received text opens with a *fùlù mázhěn lùn* 附錄麻疹論 (Appended Discourse on Measles), beginning *jīng yún: dòu bǐng yú yīn, ér chéng yú yáng; má bǐng yú yáng, ér chéng yú yīn* 經云：痘稟於陰，而成於陽。麻稟於陽，而成於陰 (the canon says: smallpox inherits from yin and completes in yang; measles inherits from yang and completes in yin). The discourse articulates the *yīnyáng hùgēn* 陰陽互根 (yin-yang mutual-rooting) doctrine as the foundation of measles pathology, identifies measles as *xiāntiān zhēnyáng zhōng zhī tāidú* 先天真陽中之胎毒 (a prenatal-heaven true-yang fetal-poison) that must engage yin (manifest as the eruptive sweat *zhézhé sì hàn* 漐漐似汗) to complete its physiological course. Treatment principle: *fán dòuzhèng zhòng zài yǎngjiāng, mázhèng zhǐ yào qímiáo* 凡痘症重在養漿，麻症只要齊苗 (for smallpox, the priority is nourishing the pustular fluid; for measles, the priority is ensuring even and complete eruption) — *miáo qí gōng jū bājiǔ* 苗齊功居八九 (when the eruption is complete, eight-or-nine-tenths of the cure is achieved).

### Abstract

The *Mákē huórén quánshū* is the most comprehensive Qīng-period specialty treatise on measles. Its four *juǎn* are organised around the 72 measles symptom-patterns: each pattern is identified by its clinical presentation, related to one of the principal pathological mechanisms (*biǎo* 表 / *lǐ* 裡 / *xū* 虛 / *shí* 實 / *fēng* 風 / *huǒ* 火 / *tán* 痰 / *shī* 濕), and matched with a specific prescription. The work also treats the principal measles complications: *hóutòng* 喉痛 (throat pain, with *Gānjié tāng* 甘桔湯 as base, modified with *Huángqín shēngdì niúbàng* 黃芩生地牛蒡 for *èrè kǒukě niàochì* presentations, *Bànxià* 半夏 for *bùèrè shétāi báihuá* presentations) and the post-recovery convalescence. The work is one of the few Qīng paediatric texts to specifically address regional variations in measles presentation. Date: conventionally placed in the second half of the eighteenth century (1748–1800), after the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* (1742) which the work assumes as background.
