# Mázhěn chǎnzhù 麻疹闡註
*Elucidatory Commentary on Measles*
by [[張霞谿]] Zhāng Xiáxī (撰); preface by [[徐漸逵]] Xú Jiànkuí (1848)

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* mid-Qīng commentary on the measles (*mázhěn* 麻疹) chapter of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* 醫宗金鑑 by [[張霞谿]] Zhāng Xiáxī (*míng* Lián 廉, *zì* Tōngyuán 通源) of Zhūjì 諸暨 (Zhèjiāng). The work was published with a preface by [[徐漸逵]] Xú Jiànkuí (*hào* Xùzhāi 煦齋) at the Lǐchéng *shìyù* 蠡城試寓 (Lǐchéng examination lodge) in *Dàoguāng* 28 *wùshēn* 戊申 *mèngxià* 1848 fourth lunar month — posthumous publication arranged by Zhāng's family (clan-nephew Jíyuán 輯園, nephew Huìpō 蕙坡, brother Zǐtíng 紫庭, clan-cousin Chónggāo 重皋) under the editorial supervision of [[酈非葊]] Lìzǐ Fēi'ān. The work's title *chǎnzhù* 闡註 (elucidatory annotation) signals its commentarial-pedagogical genre: it provides paragraph-by-paragraph annotation of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* measles chapter to render its compressed verse-mnemonic format accessible to clinical practitioners.

### Prefaces

**Preface** by [[徐漸逵]] Xú Jiànkuí (*hào* Xùzhāi 煦齋) at the Lǐchéng shìyù, *Dàoguāng* 28 *wùshēn mèngxià* (fourth lunar month of 1848). Xú frames Zhāng's editorial motivation: standard measles references — the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* 醫宗金鑑, Zhàoshì's *Huìbǔ* 匯補 commentary, and the Xièshì *qīshíèr zhèng* 七十二症 (Seventy-two Symptoms) work — should suffice, but Zhāng felt that the *Jīnjiàn*'s compressed *jiǎnyuē* 簡約 (concise) format, the *fánsuǒ* 煩瑣 (excessive) qualifications, and the variable *biànyì* 變易 (changes) of measles symptomatology rendered annotation necessary. The work also embeds a *xiān shì yǐ dúshū zhī fǎ* 先示以讀書之法 (a preliminary discussion of how to read [medical books]). Xú characterises village physicians as treating *zhèng zhī píngxiǎn* 症之平險 (mild and severe diseases) without distinction, *bùkěn yǐ bùzhī cí* 不肯以不知辭 (refusing to admit ignorance), and *rǔmǎng mièliè* 鹵莽滅裂 (treating recklessly); the *chǎnzhù* aims to remedy this through systematic explanation. Earlier Lì Fēi'ān had collated the work for publication but had not completed the print run; the publication was effectively completed by the Zhāng family during the *Dàoguāng* 28 spring-summer measles epidemic. **Author's preface** (敘張子, partial) characterises Zhāng as a *Zhūjì lǎosù* 諸暨老宿 (an old worthy of Zhūjì), *bótōng jīngshǐ, gōng jǔyè, shàn gǔwéncí* 博通經史，工舉業，善古文詞 (broadly conversant in canonical and historical literature, skilled in eight-legged-essay composition, accomplished in classical prose).

### Abstract

The *Mázhěn chǎnzhù* is a focused mid-Qīng paediatric specialty work — an annotation-commentary on the measles chapter of the standard imperial paediatric reference. The work treats: measles pathological doctrine (*má bǐng yú yáng ér chéng yú yīn* 麻稟於陽而成於陰 — measles inherits from yang but completes in yin), symptomatology, treatment by clinical phase (*chūrè* 初熱 initial-fever, *jiànmiáo* 見苗 eruption emerging, *qímiáo* 齊苗 eruption fully out, *huítā* 回塌 receding, *huíhòu* 回後 post-recovery), and the supplementary disorders (*hóutòng* 喉痛, *késòu* 咳嗽, *xièlì*, *yánhóu* 咽喉). The work's commentary supplements the *Jīnjiàn* verses with extensive prescription-modification and clinical-pulse discussion. The 1848 publication date and the 1848 measles epidemic that occasioned the publication are documentary evidence for the social context of mid-Qīng paediatric clinical practice.
