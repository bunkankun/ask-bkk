# Yòukē zhǐnán 幼科指南
*Compass for Paediatrics*
by [[周震]] Zhōu Zhèn (撰); 1925 reprint preface by [[蔬香居士]] Shūxiāng jūshì for [[衡廬主人]] Hénglú zhǔrén

## About the work

A four-*juǎn* early-Qīng paediatric handbook by [[周震]] Zhōu Zhèn (*hào* Shènzhāi 慎齋), composed in the *Shùnzhì* 18 (1661) — *Kāngxī* 18 (1679) range and first printed in 1789 by the Yíxīng Dàoshēngtáng. The received recension preserves a Republican-era 1925 reprinting with a fresh preface by [[蔬香居士]] Shūxiāng jūshì for the contemporary editor [[衡廬主人]] Hénglú zhǔrén who had paired the work with his own *Fùkē zhǐnán* 婦科指南 (gynaecology compass). The work covers paediatric four-diagnostic procedures (*sìzhěn* 四診), pulse and finger-vein diagnosis, the five-*zàng* meridian disorders, *jīngfēng* 驚風, and the principal miscellaneous paediatric disorders.

### Prefaces

The received front-matter carries the **1925 reprint preface** by [[蔬香居士]] Shūxiāng jūshì, dated 民國十四年歲次乙丑孟夏月 = 1925 fourth lunar month. It frames paediatric medicine in *Lúnyǔ* 論語 idiom (the *yǎng* 養 / *yī* 醫 of *nǚzǐ yǔ xiǎorén* 女子與小人 being notoriously difficult), and recounts the biography of [[衡廬主人]] Hénglú zhǔrén — twenty years of *fēngchén* 風塵 (dusty travels) in government administration and judiciary, frustrated ambition, retirement into medical study, composition of the *Fùkē zhǐnán* and *Yòukē zhǐnán*. The preface presents the *Yòukē zhǐnán* as the rediscovered and re-presented form of Zhōu Zhèn's Qīng original. After the preface, the body of the work opens with *Sìzhěn zǒngkuò* 四診總括 (general outline of the four diagnostic procedures), followed by separate sections on *chásè* 察色 (visual diagnosis from facial coloration), *tīngshēng* 聽聲 (auscultation of the five vocal sounds correlated with the five *zàng*), *shěnbìng / wèn* 審病問 (clinical questioning), and *qièmài* 切脈 (pulse-taking).

### Abstract

The *Yòukē zhǐnán* is one of the standard Qīng-period paediatric handbooks of the post-*Kāngxī* era. Its *juǎn* 1 contains paediatric *gēfù* 歌賦 (rhymed verses and rhapsodies) including the distinctive *Hǔkǒu màiwén gē* 虎口脈紋歌 (Verses on the Tiger's-mouth Pulse Pattern) — a supplement to the conventional *sānguān zhǐwén* 三關指紋 finger-vein diagnostic doctrine, identifying *qīngzǐ* (blue-purple) patterns as *xīngān* (heart-liver) disorders, *huángbái* (yellow-white) as *gānfèi* (liver-lung), and *hēi* (black) as *shèn* (kidney). *Juǎn* 2 covers miscellaneous paediatric disorders. *Juǎn* 3–4 cover the five-*zàng* meridian disorders (*xīn gān fèi pí shèn jīng* 心肝肺脾腎經) with case-histories appended. The work's *píngzhèng* (moderate, balanced) approach to prescription distinguishes it from the more polemical works of the Wàn-family, [[陳復正]] Chén Fùzhèng, and [[夏鼎]] Xià Dǐng paediatric lineages. Standard modern edition: *Yòukē yīxué zhǐnán* 幼科醫學指南 jiàozhù, ed. [[鄭春素]] Zhèng Chūnsù et al. (Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè, 2013, ISBN 9787513220149) based on the 1789 *Qiánlóng* Dàoshēngtáng base-text with collation against the 1789 Lìyáng Bǎochìtáng Yùshùtáng edition and the 1814 *Jiāqìng* 19 Lìyáng Bǎochìtáng edition.
