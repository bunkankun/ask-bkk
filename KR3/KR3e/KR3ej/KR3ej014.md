# Yòukē lèicuì 幼科類萃
*Distilled Categories of Paediatrics*
by [[王鑾]] Wáng Luán (撰)

## About the work

A 28-*juǎn* Míng-period paediatric encyclopaedia by [[王鑾]] Wáng Luán (*zì* Wénróng 文融, *hào* Rónghú 容湖) of Wūchéng 烏程 (modern Wúxīng 吳興, Zhèjiāng), bearer of a multi-generation family paediatric practice. The work was composed across the *Hóngzhì* to *Jiājìng* periods (c. 1502–1534) and published with a preface by [[李濂]] Lǐ Lián of Sōngzhǔ 嵩渚 dated *Jiājìng* 13 (1534). It is one of the principal mid-Míng paediatric works prior to the Wàn Quán family lineage. Its 28 *juǎn* are organised: *juǎn* 1–2 as general theoretical introduction (*zǒnglùn* 總論), *juǎn* 3–28 as 26 disease-categories — neonatal disorders, *jīngfēng* 驚風, the *gān* 疳 chronic malnutrition syndromes, *nüè* 瘧, *lì* 痢, *tùxiè* 吐瀉, *késòu* 咳嗽, *zhěn* 疹, *shuǐzhǒng* 水腫, *fùzhàng* 腹脹, *xián* 癇 epilepsy, *Shānghán* 傷寒 (incorporated into paediatrics, a methodological innovation later expanded by [[陳復正]] Chén Fùzhèng), *shāngshí* 傷食, *fùtòng* 腹痛, *jīshāng* 積傷, *pǐjī* 痞積, *lín* 淋, *dāndú* 丹毒 (erysipelas), *dǎn* 疸 jaundice, *zhū xuèzhèng* 諸血證, *hànzhèng* 汗證, *hóu chǐ shé* 喉齒舌, *ěr mù kǒu bí* 耳目口鼻, *zázhèng* 雜證, and *dòu* 痘 smallpox.

### Prefaces

The received front-matter carries an unsigned postface (*bá* 跋) — actually the closing prose of a senior official commending the project. The postface frames the work in the [[扁鵲]] Biǎn Què anecdote (specialisation by category) and contrasts the Confucian ideal of *shénshèng gōngqiǎo* 神聖工巧 medicine with contemporary practice degraded to *chuǎimò tānqǔ* 揣抹探取 (palpation-and-guesswork). It then describes the work's coverage — from *shòutāi* 受胎 (conception) through neonatal nourishment, constitutional assessment, pulse-and-fingerprint diagnosis, and the principal disease categories — and praises Wáng Luán's editorial method of distilling the *qúnshū yàofāng* 群書要方 (essential prescriptions of the corpus of medical works) into a single accessible synthesis. The postface closes by noting that the work was printed at the Kāifēng *gōngshǔ* 開封公署 (provincial office) at the direction of [[劉鬆石]] Liú Sōngshí, and notes that this same official had also commissioned the printing of the *Shānghán liùshū* 傷寒六書. The Lǐ Lián 1534 preface itself is not present in the front-matter here transmitted.

### Abstract

The *Yòukē lèicuì* is the principal Wūchéng-Wáng-family Míng-period paediatric work. Its therapeutic frame integrates [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ's *zàngfǔ* doctrine with the Jīn-Yuán four-master synthesis ([[劉完素]] Liú Wánsù, [[張從正]] Zhāng Cóngzhèng, [[李東垣]] Lǐ Dōngyuán, [[朱丹溪]] Zhū Dānxī) and especially with [[張元素]] Zhāng Yuánsù (*hào* Jiégǔ 潔古) prescription practice. It is notable for unifying acupuncture and decoction therapy under a single paediatric *biànzhèng* schema, and for an emphasis on the central role of *píwèi* 脾胃 (spleen-stomach, the post-natal *hòutiān* 後天 root) in paediatric pathology — a doctrine inherited from Lǐ Dōngyuán and from Qián Yǐ's *zàngfǔ* paediatric synthesis. The work was reprinted by Liú Sōngshí at the Kāifēng provincial office in the early-to-mid *Jiājìng* period and circulated widely in Míng paediatric pedagogy. The 1984 photographic reprint (Zhōngyī gǔjí chūbǎnshè) is from the Tiānjīn Health Vocational Medical College's Míng woodblock edition.

## Other points of interest

The postface's reference to Liú Sōngshí 劉鬆石 (a senior *Jiājìng*-era official) and the parallel printing of the *Shānghán liùshū* 傷寒六書 at the Kāifēng provincial yamen is documentary evidence for the role of provincial-office printing in the dissemination of Míng-period medical works — a complement to the better-known commercial *fāngkè* 坊刻 trade.
