# Rénduān lù 仁端錄
*Record of the Foundations of Humaneness*
by [[徐謙]] Xú Qiān (撰)

## About the work

A late-Míng smallpox-and-paediatric monograph in 16 *juǎn* by **Xú Qiān** 徐謙 (style **Yìngqiū** 應秋), of *Jiāshàn* 嘉善 (Zhèjiāng). The work is one of the major paediatric smallpox treatises of the Wànlì / Chóngzhēn era and is listed in the *Sìkù quánshū* in the *Yījiālèi* 醫家類 category of *zǐbù* 子部 (Masters Section). The *hxwd* source file *KR3ej071_001.txt* contains only the file header; substantive content must be consulted via the *Sìkù quánshū* edition or other surviving recensions.

### Tiyao

The *Sìkù tíyào* lists the work and notes that Xú Qiān composed it to systematise his clinical experience in paediatric smallpox treatment.

### Abstract

The *Rénduān lù* is a comprehensive 16-*juǎn* late-Míng paediatric monograph, organised systematically by phase of smallpox-eruption and by complication. The title *Rénduān lù* — "Record of the Foundations of Humaneness" — explicitly frames the work in the Confucian *rén* 仁 / *yī wéi rénshù* 醫為仁術 (medicine as the art of humaneness) tradition, presenting paediatric practice as a moral as well as technical undertaking. Xú Qiān was a Wànlì / Chóngzhēn era physician of *Jiāshàn* 嘉善 (Zhèjiāng), active in the late 16th and early 17th centuries.

The work is structured around the standard late-Míng smallpox-phase organization (pre-eruption fever, eruption, *guànjiāng* pus-filling, *shōuyè* scab-formation, complications), with extensive case-history sections drawing on Xú's own clinical practice. The work covers paediatric *dòuzhěn* (smallpox-eruption), *mázhěn* (measles), and ancillary paediatric disorders.

The work was sufficiently respected to be included in the *Sìkù quánshū* (1772–1782), where it is preserved with *tíyào* (descriptive notice). The dating range 1591–1645 reflects Xú Qiān's productive lifespan; specific composition date is not precisely fixed by the *Sìkù* sources but is conventionally placed in the late Wànlì / early Chóngzhēn era.

CBDB does not provide precise birth-death dates for this Xú Qiān; the conventional dating is *fl.* 1591–1645.

## Other points of interest

The work's title — *Rénduān lù* "Record of the Foundations of Humaneness" — places it explicitly within the Confucian *rén* / *yī wéi rénshù* (medicine as humaneness) tradition that dominated late-Míng medical writing. The *Sìkù* inclusion of this work reflects the high Qīng official endorsement of selected late-Míng paediatric authorities.
