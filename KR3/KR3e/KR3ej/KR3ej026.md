# Yīngtóng lèicuì 嬰童類萃
*Distilled Categories on Infants and Children*
by [[王大綸]] Wáng Dàlún (撰)

## About the work

A late-Míng paediatric treatise by [[王大綸]] Wáng Dàlún (*hào* Yígāng 怡岡), bearer of a nine-generation family medical lineage. The work was completed and woodblock-printed in *Tiānqǐ* 2 = 1622 *júyuè* (chrysanthemum month, ninth lunar month) as one component of Wáng's three-work *cóngshū* the *Wángshì jiāchāo* 王氏家抄 (Family Transcriptions of the Wáng), the others being *Dòushā xīnfǎ* 痘痧心法 (Smallpox-and-eruption Heart-Method) and an external-medicine prescription appendix. The jicheng.tw recension transmits the paediatric volume *Yīngtóng lèicuì* alone.

### Prefaces

The front-matter carries the author's own **postface** (*bá* 跋) dated *Tiānqǐ* 2 (1622) *júyuè* by *Yígāng* Wáng Dàlún. The postface opens with a vocabulary classification of the age categories — *yuènèi wéi qiǎngbǎo* 月內為襁褓 (newborn within first month: swaddled), *jí qī yuē yīng* 及期曰嬰 (first year: *yīng*), *sān suì wéi hái* 三歲為孩 (three years: *hái*), *qī tiáo bā chèn* 七齠八齔 (seven-and-eight tooth-shedding age), *shí suì zhìzǐ* 十歲稚子 (ten: *zhìzǐ*), *shíliù suì wéi tóng* 十六歲為童 (sixteen: *tóng*) — and articulates the *yǎkē* (silent department) problem: the infant cannot speak, even the older child cannot speak with diagnostic precision; "embryos and infants entrust their lives entirely to the physician, and the physician is the substitute mother of all his charges" (*yīngtóng dú tīngmìng yú yī, ér yī dú wéi zhòngyīng zhī mǔ* 嬰童獨聽命於醫，而醫獨為眾嬰之母). [[倉公]] Cāng-gōng's maxim *yī zhě yì yě* 醫者意也 (the physician is the *yì* — intuitive understanding) is invoked, with [[韓愈]] Hán Yù's *Yuándào* 原道 metaphor of *xīn jìn huǒ chuán* 薪盡火傳 (the firewood is consumed but the fire is transmitted) — i.e. medical knowledge transmitted through generations. Wáng then recounts his family's nine-generation medical lineage, and explains the three-work *jiāchāo* publication.

### Abstract

The *Yīngtóng lèicuì* is one of several Wànlì / *Tiānqǐ* late-Míng paediatric compendia (alongside [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng yòukē* of 1602, [[KR3ej037]], and [[秦昌遇]] Qín Chāngyù's *Yòukē zhézhōng* of c. 1641, [[KR3ej018]]). The work organises paediatric disorders into a *lèicuì* (categorial distillation) format derived from the earlier Wàn Quán family and [[李時珍]] Lǐ Shízhēn's *Běncǎo gāngmù* taxonomic models. The work circulated in late-Míng and early-Qīng Jiāngnán paediatric practice. The 1622 date is fixed by the author's own postface.
