# Tóngyuán yīshū · Xiǎo'érkē 彤園醫書·小兒科
*Tóngyuán Medical Books — Paediatrics*
by [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán (撰)

## About the work

The paediatric section of [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán's (*hào* Tóngyuán 彤園, *fl. Jiāqìng* era) collected medical *cóngshū* the *Tóngyuán yīshū* 彤園醫書 (Tóngyuán Medical Books, a multi-work corpus including paediatrics, gynaecology, and adult internal medicine, c. 1796–1820). Zhèng Yùtán was a Qīng paediatric and gynaecological specialist of the *Qiánlóng* — *Jiāqìng* generation; his collected *cóngshū* is one of the principal Qīng-period medical-publishing achievements of the regional physician-authors. The paediatric volume covers the standard paediatric disease-range in six *juǎn*.

### Abstract

The *Tóngyuán yīshū · Xiǎo'érkē* is the paediatric section of Zhèng Yùtán's collected medical *cóngshū*. Zhèng's writings exemplify the late-eighteenth-century / early-nineteenth-century *jiāfāng* 家方 (household-formulary) Qīng medical synthesis: prescriptions drawn from his Húnán-region clinical practice (Zhèng was of Húnán origin), framed in the conventional [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ paediatric *zàngfǔ* doctrine, but more inclined toward the *wēnbǔ* 溫補 (warming-supplementing) school of [[張景岳]] Zhāng Jǐngyuè and the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* imperial orthodoxy than toward the polemical [[陳復正]] Chén Fùzhèng *jīng / chù* paediatric reform. The six *juǎn* cover the standard paediatric disease-range: neonatal disorders, *jīngfēng* 驚風, *gān* 疳, *Shānghán* and the six external pathogens, *mádòu* 麻痘 (measles-and-smallpox), *xièlì* 瀉痢 (diarrhoea-dysentery), and miscellaneous disorders. Date bracket: 1796 (*Jiāqìng* 1, conventional *terminus a quo* for Zhèng's principal writing period) to 1820 (the *Tóngyuán* corpus dates to before *Dàoguāng*).
