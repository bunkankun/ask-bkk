# Yòukē huìjué zhíjiě 幼科匯訣直解
*Direct Explanations of Compiled Paediatric Discussions*
anonymous Qīng paediatric compilation

## About the work

A single-*juǎn* anonymous Qīng paediatric handbook of the *huìjué zhíjiě* 匯訣直解 (compiled-and-directly-explained) didactic genre. The catalog meta records no author and no dynasty; internal evidence (genre, didactic style, prescription vocabulary) places the work in the mid-to-late Qīng. The work belongs to the late-Qīng *xuéyī* 學醫 (medical-study) paediatric tutorial tradition alongside the better-known *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* paediatric verses ([[KR3ej029]]) and [[王錫鑫]] Wáng Xīxīn's *Yòukē qièyào* ([[KR3ej025]]).

### Prefaces

The received front-matter is empty. The work's substantive content is preserved beginning at the body of the work; no author's preface or table-of-contents has been recovered.

### Abstract

The *Yòukē huìjué zhíjiě* is a late-Qīng paediatric tutorial in the standard *jué* + *jiě* 訣解 format: the inherited paediatric *gējué* 歌訣 (mnemonic verses) are quoted, then directly explained (*zhíjiě* 直解) for the *xuézhě* 學者 (medical student). The work's source-corpus is the conventional Qīng paediatric verse-tradition descending from [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ through the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn yòukē*. Date bracket: c. 1700 (after the principal Qīng paediatric *jué* tradition is established) to 1911 (end of Qīng). The work is one of the rarer paediatric tutorials in the catalog; the author is unidentified.
