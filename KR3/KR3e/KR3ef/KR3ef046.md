# Zhōnghán lùn biànzhèng guǎng zhù 中寒論辯證廣註
*Discriminating-Syndrome Extensive Commentary on the Treatise on Central Cold*
by [[汪琥]] (Wāng Hù, *zì* Yìnxí 苓友, *fl.* late Kāngxī, 清)

## About the work

A three-juan companion to [[汪琥]] Wāng Hù's principal *Shānghán* commentary ([[KR3ef045]]), addressing the parallel "*zhōnghán*" 中寒 (central / true cold-damage) tradition. The work clarifies the distinction — central in Sòng-Jīn medical doctrine — between *shānghán* (external cold-damage, addressed by the Zhāng Jī canon) and *zhōnghán* (direct attack on the interior by cold, requiring different therapeutic principles).

### Abstract

Composition window 1680–1689 brackets Wāng's continuing editorial activity on the *Shānghán* and *zhōnghán* topic in the mid-Kāngxī period. The doctrinal distinction between *shānghán* and *zhōnghán* was a central late-imperial debate, with major disagreements between commentators about which canonical *Shānghán* prescriptions properly belong to which category. Wāng's treatise is one of the more systematic Qīng treatments.
