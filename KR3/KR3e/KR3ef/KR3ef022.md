# Yīzōng jīnjiàn — Dìngzhèng Zhòngjǐng quánshū Shānghán lùn zhù 醫宗金鑑·訂正仲景全書傷寒論註
*Golden Mirror of Medicine — Corrected Complete Works of Zhòngjǐng: Commentary on the Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[吳謙]] (Wú Qiān, *zì* Liùjí 六吉, *fl.* early Qiánlóng, 清) — chief editor; co-editor [[劉裕鐸]] Liú Yùduó; with the official Qiánlóng medical-college editorial committee

## About the work

The *Shānghán lùn* component of the great imperial Qīng medical encyclopedia, the *Yùzuǎn Yīzōng jīnjiàn* 御纂醫宗金鑑 (compiled 1740–1742, presented 1749, in 90 juan total). This component — 17 juan in extent — presents the Sòng-recension *Shānghán lùn* with a comprehensive imperial-commission commentary harmonizing the major Qīng commentary traditions ([[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ, [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí, [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng, [[柯琴]] Kē Qín, [[張璐]] Zhāng Lù, etc.). The work is the official medical-curriculum text of the Qīng imperial medical academy 太醫院 from 1749 onward.

### Abstract

The composition window of 1742–1749 reflects the imperial commission and final presentation to the throne. [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān, Qīng-period Tàiyīyuàn yuànpàn 太醫院院判 (Director of the Imperial Medical Academy), served as chief editor under imperial commission, with [[劉裕鐸]] Liú Yùduó as principal co-editor. The work's distinguishing methodology is the systematic comparison of competing commentary lines and the production of an official harmonization — a feature typical of mid-Qīng imperial editorial enterprises. The work is divided into "corrected" 訂正 portions (with rectified canonical text and authoritative commentary) and "supplementary" 補 portions (extended clinical applications). The parallel *Jīnguì yàoluè* component is [[KR3ef086]]; the broader *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* is at [[KR3e0090]] / similar.

The *Shānghán lùn zhù* section of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* is the most widely-circulated *Shānghán* commentary in modern Chinese-medicine pedagogy, surpassed in citation only by the canonical *Shānghán lùn* itself.
