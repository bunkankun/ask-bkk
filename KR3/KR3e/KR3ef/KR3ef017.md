# Shānghán lùn gāng mù 傷寒論綱目
*Outline of the Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[沈金鰲]] (Shěn Jīn'áo, *zì* Zhīwēi 芷薇, 1717–1776, 清)

## About the work

A sixteen-juan synthesis of *Shānghán lùn* doctrine prepared by the Wúxī polymath [[沈金鰲]] Shěn Jīn'áo (1717–1776), composed as part of his comprehensive medical *cóngshū* the *Shěn shì zùnshēng shū* 沈氏尊生書 (1773). The work assembles selected canonical passages of the *Shānghán* under topical "outline" 綱 headings, with quoted commentary from earlier authorities ([[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ, [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí, [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng, [[柯琴]] Kē Qín, etc.) under "subordinate" 目 sections, in the analytical style typical of Qiánlóng-period medical compilations.

### Abstract

The composition window of 1774–1775 reflects the publication of the *Zùnshēng shū* in 1773 followed by Shěn's continued editorial activity through 1776 when he died. Shěn was a 1750 *jǔrén* who turned to medicine after a frustrated examination career; his medical work is closer to literati-physician practice than to specialist clinical writing. The *Gāng mù* is one of seven main components of the *Zùnshēng shū*, alongside the more widely-cited *Zábìng yuánliú xīzhú* 雜病源流犀燭. The work's distinctive contribution is its synthetic presentation of multiple commentary lines (Chéng, Fāng, Yú, Kē) — a feature that made it widely useful in late-imperial medical pedagogy.
