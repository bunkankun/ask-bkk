# Shānghán lùn dú 傷寒論讀
*A Reading of the Treatise on Cold Damage*
by [[沈又彭]] (Shěn Yòupéng, *zì* Yāofēng 堯封, *fl.* mid-Qiánlóng, 清)

## About the work

A four-juan mid-Qiánlóng "reader" of the *Shānghán lùn* by the Sūzhōu physician [[沈又彭]] Shěn Yòupéng, prepared as a pedagogical re-arrangement of the canonical text by clinical pattern. The work is similar in conception to [[柯琴]] Kē Qín's *Lái sū jí* ([[KR3ef013]]), but more concise and explicitly didactic — preparing the *Shānghán* materials for student use rather than scholarly debate.

### Abstract

The composition window of ca. 1764–1796 brackets the Qiánlóng middle to late period during which Shěn was active. Shěn was also the author of a parallel *Nǚ kē jí yào* 女科輯要 (gynecology compendium) and miscellaneous case-records. He is a representative figure of the mid-Qiánlóng Sūzhōu pedagogical-medicine line, working in the tradition of [[尤怡]] Yóu Yí and [[徐大椿]] Xú Dàchūn.
