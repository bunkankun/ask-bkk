# Yī zōng jīn jiàn · Shānghán xīn fǎ yào jué 醫宗金鑑·傷寒心法要訣
*Imperial Compendium of Medicine — Essential Verses on the Heart-Method of Cold Damage*
by [[吳謙]] (Wú Qiān, *zì* Liùjí 六吉, *fl.* Qiánlóng, 清) and [[劉裕鐸]] (Liú Yùduó), co-chief-editors under imperial commission, with the imperial physicians' staff of the Tàiyīyuàn 太醫院

## About the work

A five-juan versified pedagogical digest of *Shānghán lùn* doctrine, forming the *Shānghán xīn fǎ yào jué* 傷寒心法要訣 section of the *Yī zōng jīn jiàn* 醫宗金鑑 ([[KR3e0090]]) — the comprehensive Qiánlóng-period imperial medical compendium, completed Qiánlóng 7 = 1742 and printed Qiánlóng 14 = 1749. The work was compiled at the Tàiyīyuàn under the editorial chairmanship of [[吳謙]] Wú Qiān (the institute's *yuànpàn* 院判) and [[劉裕鐸]] Liú Yùduó.

### Abstract

Composition date 1742 (Qiánlóng 7). The *Shānghán xīn fǎ yào jué* takes the form of *gē jué* 歌訣 — heptasyllabic mnemonic verses — followed by prose annotations, organized by the canonical six-conformation schema. The opening verse — "六經為病盡傷寒，氣同病異豈期然，推其形臟原非一，因從類化故多端" (The six conformations as disease are entirely cold-damage; same *qì* but differing diseases — how could this be predetermined? Inquiring into the constitution and viscera reveals each is not one; following along varieties of transformation, hence the many ramifications) — encapsulates the editorial program: the *Shānghán* canon as a unified clinical framework whose ramifications follow from the constitution-and-viscera variations of the patient.

The work was the imperial-curriculum *Shānghán* textbook from 1749 onward and the standard reference of Qīng imperial-medical practice. As a *gē jué* digest of canonical *Shānghán* doctrine, it occupies a similar pedagogical niche to [[許叔微]]'s *Shānghán bǎi zhèng gē* ([[KR3ef061]]) of seven centuries earlier — but with the prestige and authority of imperial commission.

The work is one of the principal Qīng *Shānghán* pedagogical texts and remains in continuous use in modern Chinese-medicine education.

## Other points of interest

The *Yī zōng jīn jiàn* is the single most-cited Qīng medical reference work and the principal monument of mid-Qiánlóng imperial-medical scholarship. Its *Shānghán xīn fǎ yào jué* section is one of the two principal *Shānghán* materials in the compendium (the other being the *Dìng zhèng Zhòngjǐng quán shū · Shānghán lùn zhù* 訂正仲景全書·傷寒論註, which is the textual-philological companion).
