# Shānghán zǒngbìng lùn 傷寒總病論
*Comprehensive Treatise on Cold Damage and All Diseases*
by [[龐安時]] (Páng Ānshí, *zì* Ānchéng 安常, ca. 1042–1099, 北宋) — the catalog meta gives 龐安石 which is a graphical variant for 龐安時

## About the work

A six-juan late-Northern-Sòng comprehensive commentary on the *Shānghán* canon by the Qízhōu 蘄州 (modern Húběi) physician [[龐安時]] Páng Ānshí (字 Ānchéng 安常, ca. 1042–1099). The work — preface by [[黃庭堅]] Huáng Tíngjiān — is the second great Northern-Sòng *Shānghán* commentary after [[韓祗和]] Hán Zhīhé's *Wēizhǐ lùn* ([[KR3ef008]]), and the principal vehicle for Páng's distinctive doctrinal synthesis.

### Abstract

The composition date 1100 is conventional (just before Páng's death; some authorities give 1099 or 1102). Páng — a contemporary of [[蘇軾]] Sū Shì and [[黃庭堅]] Huáng Tíngjiān, who were both his close friends and patients — was the most distinguished medical practitioner of the late Northern Sòng outside the校正醫書局 circle. Páng's distinctive doctrinal contribution is the integration of *Shānghán* doctrine with the doctrine of *yùn qì* 運氣 (climatic-influence) and *biāoběn* 標本 (branch and root), anticipating the integrative direction of JīnYuán Héjiān-school synthesis. The *Zǒngbìng lùn* circulated widely in the SòngYuánMíng and was a major source for later commentators.

The catalog meta name is 龐安石 — a graphical variant for 龐安時 (the standard Sòng form). The figure is the same.

## Other points of interest

[[黃庭堅]] Huáng Tíngjiān's preface to Páng's *Zǒngbìng lùn* is one of the most-cited Sòng literati endorsements of a medical text and one of the most important sources for Páng's biography. [[蘇軾]] Sū Shì wrote a letter on Páng's treatment of him for an eye condition (in Sū's *Yú lù* 語錄), which is another primary biographical witness.
