# Shānghán zhǐzhǎng 傷寒指掌
*Pointing-Palm [Handbook] of Cold Damage*
by [[吳坤安]] (Wú Kūn'ān, *zì* BìHuá 璧華, *fl.* late Qiánlóng, 1750s–1796, 清)

## About the work

A four-juan late-Qiánlóng (1796) pedagogical handbook of *Shānghán* clinical practice by the Tóngxiāng 桐鄉 (Zhèjiāng) physician [[吳坤安]] Wú Kūn'ān. The title's metaphor — "pointing-to-the-palm" 指掌 — refers to the work's purpose: presenting *Shānghán* clinical practice in a concise, accessible form that the student can grasp at a glance. The work systematically integrates the *Shānghán* and *wēnbìng* 溫病 traditions, an important late-Qiánlóng synthesis.

### Abstract

Composition date 1796 fixed by Wú's preface. The work circulated widely in the 19th century and was repeatedly reprinted; its clinical-pragmatic synthesis of *Shānghán* and *wēnbìng* lines made it useful in both Jiāngnán and northern medical curricula. Wú's other works include the *Lèi zhèng pǔ jì běn shì fāng* 類證普濟本事方. No CBDB record located.
