# Shānghán lèi shū huó rén zǒng kuò 傷寒類書活人總括
*General Compendium of the Cold-Damage Genre Books for Saving Life*
by [[楊士瀛]] (Yáng Shìyíng, *zì* Dēngfù 登父, *hào* Rénzhāi 仁齋, *fl.* mid-thirteenth century, 南宋)

## About the work

A seven-juan late-Southern-Sòng *Shānghán* compendium by the Fúzhōu physician [[楊士瀛]] Yáng Shìyíng (Rénzhāi 仁齋), the author of the better-known *Rénzhāi zhí zhǐ* 仁齋直指 ([[KR3e0045]]). The work is conventionally identified as the "*Shānghán lèi shū*" 傷寒類書 component of Yáng's broader oeuvre — a genre-organized compendium of cold-damage clinical material — combined with the *Huó rén zǒng kuò* 活人總括 ("General Compendium of *Huó rén shū*"), Yáng's earlier digest of [[朱肱]] Zhū Hóng's *Lèi zhèng huó rén shū* 類證活人書.

### Abstract

The Kanripo source file is effectively empty (header only) — body content has not been digitized into the jicheng.tw edition Kanripo carries. The work is bibliographically attested as part of Yáng's oeuvre and is sometimes catalogued as part of the *Rénzhāi zhí zhǐ* 仁齋直指 in extended recensions. Composition window 1250–1264 is bracketed by Yáng's productive period and the completion of his *Rénzhāi zhí zhǐ* in 1264.

The work is one of the principal Southern-Sòng *Shānghán* digests in the post-Zhū Hóng tradition — Zhū Hóng's *Huó rén shū* (preface 1107) being the dominant Northern-Sòng *Shānghán* clinical reference, and Yáng's *Zǒng kuò* the most authoritative thirteenth-century abridgement and updating of that material.
