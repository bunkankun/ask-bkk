# Shānghán zábìng lùn jí jiě 傷寒雜病論集解
*Collected Explanations on the Treatise on Cold Damage and Miscellaneous Disorders*
by [[張機]] (Zhāng Jī, *zì* Zhòngjǐng, 東漢) — original; [[林億]] (Lín Yì, *fl.* 1057–1068, 宋) and colleagues at the校正醫書局 — collected commentary

## About the work

A composite presentation of the *Shānghán zábìng lùn* — reunifying cold-damage and miscellaneous-disease portions into a single sixteen-juan whole, with the校正醫書局 (Bureau for the Correction of Medical Texts) collation framework. The "集解" (collected commentary) refers to the bureau's harmonization of the variant manuscript witnesses [[林億]] Lín Yì and colleagues had access to. The text restores the structural unity of Zhāng Jī's original 16-juan work which [[王叔和]] Wáng Shūhé had split into separate cold-damage and miscellaneous-disease lines.

### Abstract

The catalog meta gives 漢 dynasty and Lín Yì et al. as collectors of the explanations. The recension reflects the Sòng校正醫書局 understanding of the textual situation: the 16-juan original — divided since Wáng Shūhé's late-3rd-c. editorial intervention into the *Shānghán lùn* (cold-damage, 10 juan) and what survives as the *Jīnguì yàoluè* (miscellaneous diseases, 3 juan) — is here reunited as a single text with cross-edition collation. The bureau presented its full set of *Shānghán*-canon prints to the throne in 1065–1066; the *jí jiě* form represents one of the alternative editorial presentations from that working period.

This recension is rare and survives in the modern medical-philological literature chiefly through the 漢學文典 digital re-editing tradition. It is distinct from both the standard *Sòngběn* [[KR3ef001]] and the *Jīnguì yùhán jīng* [[KR3ef080]]; it should be regarded as a synthetic Sòng-bureau presentation of the materials rather than as an independent textual stratum.
