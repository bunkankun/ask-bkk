# Shānghán kuò yào 傷寒括要
*Concise Essentials of Cold Damage*
by [[李中梓]] (Lǐ Zhōngzǐ, *zì* Shìcái 士材, *hào* Niànzé 念莪, 1588–1655, 明)

## About the work

A two-juan late-Míng (1649) pedagogical compendium on *Shānghán* clinical practice by the influential Jiāngsū physician [[李中梓]] Lǐ Zhōngzǐ (字 Shìcái 士材, 1588–1655). The work is a concise practical guide rather than a textual commentary — Lǐ summarizes the *Shānghán* canon's clinical doctrine in a form suitable for student use, with selected formulas, prognostic indicators, and clinical adjustments. The catalog meta gives 明 dynasty (correctly: composed in the last decade of the Míng before the dynasty's fall).

### Abstract

Composition date 1649 is given by Lǐ's preface; the work was first printed in 1649 (Shùnzhì 6, in the early Qīng dynasty by which time the Míng had fallen). Lǐ is one of the most influential late-Míng pedagogical medical writers; his other works — the *Yī zōng bì dú* 醫宗必讀 (1637), the *Nèijīng zhī yào* 內經知要 ([[KR3ea033]]), the *Běncǎo tōng xuán* 本草通玄 — were repeatedly reprinted through the Qīng and shaped the early-Qīng pedagogical medicine landscape. The *Shānghán kuò yào* situates Lǐ's *Shānghán* thinking within his broader pedagogical program.
