# Shānghán dà bái 傷寒大白
*Plain Exposition of Cold Damage*
by [[秦之楨]] (Qín Zhīzhēn, *zì* Huángshì 皇士, *fl.* early Kāngxī, 清)

## About the work

A four-juan early-Kāngxī (1714) pedagogical commentary on the *Shānghán lùn* by the Sūzhōu physician [[秦之楨]] Qín Zhīzhēn (字 Huángshì 皇士). The title's metaphor — "great whiteness" 大白 = plainness / clarity — signals the author's intent to present the *Shānghán* canon in fully transparent form, without doctrinal accretions or polemical complications.

### Abstract

Composition date 1714 (Kāngxī 53) given by Qín's preface. Qín presents the *Shānghán* materials in a streamlined sequence, with clear syndrome-headers, brief commentary, and selected canonical formulas. The work was widely used pedagogically in the mid-Qīng and continued to be reprinted into the early Republican era. Qín is a representative figure of the moderate Kāngxī pedagogical tradition, situated between the strict revisionists ([[喻昌]] Yú Chāng) and the literati-physicians ([[尤怡]] Yóu Yí).
