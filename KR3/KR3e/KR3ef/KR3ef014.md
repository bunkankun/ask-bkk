# Shānghán lùn zhù lái sū jí 傷寒論註來蘇集
*Annotated Cold-Damage Treatise, Lái-sū Collection*
by [[柯琴]] (Kē Qín, *zì* Yùnbó 韻伯, *fl.* late 17th c., 清)

## About the work

The four-juan core commentary component of [[柯琴]] Kē Qín's larger *Láisū jí* — the actual annotation of the *Shānghán lùn* text, as distinct from the doctrinal essays of the *Yì* ([[KR3ef007]]). This recension presents the *Shānghán* materials reorganized by clinical syndrome and treatment pattern, with Kē's running commentary on each segment. In modern editions the *Lùn zhù* is the most heavily used component of the *Láisū jí* set.

### Abstract

Same composition window as [[KR3ef013]] (1669–1674). The *Lùn zhù*'s structural innovation is its segmentation of the *Shānghán* text by 證 (clinical syndrome) — a re-arrangement that pulls related sections together across Wáng Shūhé's original chapter-divisions, allowing the reader to see the full prescriptive logic for each syndrome in one place. Kē's commentary is concise, clinically-oriented, and explicitly polemical against both the [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ commentarial tradition and the [[方有執]] Fāng Yǒuzhí / [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng restoration line — Kē argues that his syndrome-based reorganization is the proper recovery of Zhāng Jī's clinical intent, against the Wáng Shūhé manuscript-sequence approach.

The text is commonly bound with the *Shānghán lùn yì* [[KR3ef007]] and the *Fù yì* [[KR3ef033]] as a unified set.
