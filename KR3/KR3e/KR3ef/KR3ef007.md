# Shānghán lùn yì 傷寒論翼
*Wings to the Treatise on Cold Damage Disorders*
by [[柯琴]] (Kē Qín, *zì* Yùnbó 韻伯, *fl.* Kāngxī 1660s–1680s, 清)

## About the work

A two-juan supplement and theoretical companion to [[柯琴]] Kē Qín's larger *Shānghán lái sū jí* 傷寒來蘇集 ([[KR3ef013]]) — Kē's general comprehensive commentary on the *Shānghán lùn*. The *Yì* (literally "wings") presents 13 systematic essays on key doctrinal problems in the cold-damage canon, including the six-channel framework, the *yīngwèi* 營衛 doctrine, the relation of *Shānghán* to *wēnbìng* 溫病, and the pharmacology of the principal canonical formulas. The work is widely read alongside the *Lái sū jí* as the doctrinal underpinning of Kē Qín's "channel-pattern" approach.

### Abstract

The composition date falls in the same Kāngxī period (ca. 1669–1674) that yielded the *Shānghán lái sū jí*. Kē Qín, a native of Cíxī 慈溪 (Zhèjiāng) who studied with the [[喻昌]] Yú Chāng tradition, was one of the most influential Qīng commentators on the *Shānghán* canon. His distinctive contribution is the systematic re-organization of the *Shānghán* materials by channel-pattern (證) rather than by [[王叔和]] Wáng Shūhé's manuscript-traditional sequence — an organizational principle that has dominated Chinese-medicine pedagogy ever since. The *Yì*'s essays argue for this re-organization on doctrinal grounds drawn from the *Sùwèn* "*Rè lùn*" 熱論 and from Yú Chāng's earlier doctrinal work, while distancing Kē Qín from Yú on key clinical questions (notably the indication for moxibustion).
