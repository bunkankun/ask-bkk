# Shānghán fā wēi lùn 傷寒發微論
*Treatise Bringing to Light the Subtleties of Cold Damage*
by [[許叔微]] (Xǔ Shūwēi, *zì* Zhīkě 知可, ca. 1080–ca. 1154, 南宋)

## About the work

A two-juan Southern-Sòng *Shānghán* essay-collection by [[許叔微]] Xǔ Shūwēi (Xǔ Xuéshì 許學士), companion to his *Shānghán bǎi zhèng gē* ([[KR3ef061]]). The work is also bibliographically attested under the alternative title *Yì Shānghán lùn* 翼傷寒論 (Wing to the *Shānghán lùn*). It consists of clinical essays on a range of *Shānghán* doctrinal and therapeutic points — pulse-and-syndrome differentiation, pathogen-progression, and the limits of canonical prescription application — taking up topics that the strict *zhèngfāng* (syndrome-prescription) format of the canonical text leaves under-developed.

### Abstract

Composition window 1133–1154 is bracketed by Xǔ's *Lèizhèng pǔjì běnshì fāng* ([[KR3e0032]], 1133) and his death. The work circulates with the *Bǎi zhèng gē* as a single set in the Yuán-edition reprintings under the binding title 《新編張仲景注解傷寒百證歌五卷·發微論二卷》 (preserved in the introductory matter of the received text). The bibliographic survey embedded in this introductory matter — itself a major early-modern *Shānghán* bibliographic source — names the principal Sòng–Yuán *Shānghán* commentary tradition ([[王叔和]] Wáng Shūhé, [[成無己]] Chéng Wúyǐ, [[李檉]] Lǐ Chēng, …) and identifies the *Fā wēi lùn* as the *Yì Shānghán lùn* of 《書錄解題》.

The Yuán-edition was rare in the Qīng — Qián Zūnwáng 錢遵王's *Dúshū mǐnqiú jì* is one of the few catalog entries — and the modern reprintings derive from the Yuán block in 錢's collection (later in the hands of the Hǎiyú 海虞 collector who wrote the bibliographic introduction).

The work, while bibliographically less famous than the *Bǎi zhèng gē*, is the more discursive of the two and is the principal vehicle for Xǔ Shūwēi's own doctrinal positions on the *Shānghán* tradition.

## Other points of interest

The 1820s-era bibliographic preface (preserved in Kanripo source under the title "重雕元刻傷寒百證歌發微論敘") is one of the most-cited early-modern records of Xǔ Shūwēi's lost works (the *Nǐ Shānghán gē*, *Zhìfǎ* 81 piān, *Sānshíliù tú*, *Yì Shānghán lùn*, *Biàn lèi*) and the textual transmission of the surviving pair.
