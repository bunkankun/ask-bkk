# Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Fùkē xīnfǎ yàojué 醫宗金鑑·婦科心法要訣
 *Heart-Method Essentials for Women's Medicine (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu021. The two listings reflect the *hxwd* reprint catalog's separation of the same 6-*juàn* imperial women's-medicine chapter of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016) into multiple sub-volume entries.

### Abstract

6 *juàn* covering women's medicine in the imperial mnemonic-verse format, organised by the eight categorical gates inherited from [[陳自明]] Chén Zìmíng via [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ and [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng. Composition window 1742–1749. For full description see [[KR3eu021]].
