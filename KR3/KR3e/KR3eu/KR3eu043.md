# Zhèngzhì zhāi yào 證治摘要
 *Selected Essentials of Pattern-and-Treatment*
by [[中川成章]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Zhèngzhì zhāi yào* 證治摘要 (Japanese *Shōchi tekiyō*; "Selected Essentials of Pattern-and-Treatment"), 2 *juàn*, is a clinical handbook by the late-Edo *kanpō* physician [[中川成章]] **Nakagawa Seishō** 中川成章. The title deliberately echoes — at one remove — the framework of the major literate-physician clinical compendia of the late Míng (Wáng Kěntáng's *Zhèngzhì zhǔnshéng*, KR3eu011) and Xuē Jǐ's *Nèikē zhāi yào* (KR3eu003), distilling the clinical *zhèngzhì* 證治 (pattern-and-treatment) tradition into a concise practitioner's reference suitable for Edo Japanese clinical use.

### Abstract

The composition window is the second quarter of the 19th century, the period of Nakagawa's mature practice. The work is one of many late-Edo private-publication *kanpō* clinical handbooks that distil the Chinese imperial-Qīng synthesis (Zhāng Jièbīn, the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn*, the Tamba philological tradition) for Japanese practitioner use. Its compact 2-*juàn* format and practical orientation place it in the same genre as [[湯本求真]] Yumoto Kyūshin's later *Kōyū dōshikaikō* and the broader Edo-Meiji *kanpō* handbook tradition.

Substantial Western-language scholarship is not available; the work has been studied chiefly in modern Japanese kanpō historiography.
