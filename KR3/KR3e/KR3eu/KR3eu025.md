# Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Wàikē xīnfǎ yàojué 醫宗金鑑·外科心法要訣
 *Heart-Method Essentials for External Medicine*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

The *Wàikē xīnfǎ yàojué* 外科心法要訣 (16 *juàn*) is the external-medicine (surgical, dermatological, ulcerative-disease) chapter of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016). It systematises the Míng-era *Wàikē zhèngzōng* 外科正宗 of Chén Shígōng 陳實功 (1617) and the *Wàikē dàchéng* 外科大成 of Qí Kūn 祁坤 (1665) into the imperial mnemonic-verse-and-commentary format, covering *yōngjū* 癰疽 (deep-seated abscesses and carbuncles) by anatomical region, *liúzhù* 流注 (migratory abscesses), *liútán* 流痰 (cold-abscesses, including tuberculous bone disease), *yáng* 瘍 (ulcers), *xuǎn* 癬 (tinea-like dermatoses), *yīnchuāng* 陰瘡 (genital ulcers), *gānchuāng* 疳瘡 (syphilitic ulcers), *màifēng* 麻風 (leprosy), and pediatric *yánmí* (eye-and-throat surface diseases).

### Abstract

The chapter is one of the most comprehensive Qīng-period external-medicine treatises and was the standard Qīng imperial-academy textbook of the specialty. The imperial editors integrate the late-Míng dual surgical tradition — Chén Shígōng's pragmatic *Wàikē zhèngzōng* and the doctrinal-integrative line of [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's external-medicine works (cf. KR3eu007) and Wáng Kěntáng's *Yángyī zhǔnshéng* — into a single coherent reference, with detailed anatomical illustrations of lesion-types by body region and the surgical instruments required.

Composition window 1742–1749. For the parent compendium see [[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016.
