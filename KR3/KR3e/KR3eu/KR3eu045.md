# Shānghán zhī yánjiū 傷寒之研究
 *A Study of the Shāng-hán Tradition*
by [[中西惟忠]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Shānghán zhī yánjiū* 傷寒之研究 (Japanese *Shōkan no kenkyū*), 10 *juàn*, is a major monograph on Zhāng Zhòngjǐng's *Shānghán lùn* by the Kyoto-based late-Edo physician [[中西惟忠]] **Nakanishi Iyū** 中西惟忠 (1724–1803), a second-generation disciple of [[吉益東洞]] **Yoshimasu Tōdō** 吉益東洞 and a senior figure of the *koihō* 古醫方 (Ancient-Formula School) of Kyōto medicine. The work belongs to the philological-and-clinical recovery programme of late-18th-century Edo *koihō*: rejecting Sòng *zàngfǔ* / *qì*-physiological accretions and Jīn-Yuán doctrinal speculation, returning to the formula-and-symptom-pattern reasoning of the *Shānghán* / *Jīnguì* texts themselves. The same work is independently catalogued as [[KR3ef029]] in the present knowledgebase.

### Abstract

Composition is securely 1789 (preface dated). The work is one of the longest and most thoroughgoing Edo *koihō*-school *Shānghán* studies, working systematically through the channel-and-syndrome chapters with detailed clinical commentary on prescription application. The *koihō* school's distinctive *fùzhèng* 腹證 (abdominal-palpation diagnostics) is here developed as the central diagnostic technique — Yoshimasu Tōdō's signature contribution — and the work is one of the principal late-Edo elaborations of the *fùzhèng* method.

The *koihō* school's pragmatic clinical orientation — distinguishing classical-formula prescription (*kohō* 古方) from contemporary-formula prescription (*kōhō* 後方 / *jihō*) and insisting on the demonstrable effectiveness of the former — became one of the most influential currents in modern Japanese kanpō and a major influence on the late-Qīng Chinese *jīngfāngpài* 經方派 revival via Yáng Shǒujìng's 1880s importation of Edo medical literature.

For Nakanishi's biographical context and his *Yào zhēng zhì jí* 藥徵績集 see his person note.
