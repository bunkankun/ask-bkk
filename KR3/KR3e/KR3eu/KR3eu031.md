# Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Zábìng xīnfǎ 醫宗金鑑·雜病心法
 *Heart-Method for Miscellaneous Diseases (alternate listing)*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

This entry duplicates KR3eu020 (the *Zábìng xīnfǎ yàojué* 雜病心法要訣) under the slightly truncated title *Zábìng xīnfǎ* 雜病心法. The chapter is the internal-medicine *zábìng* section of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016).

### Abstract

5 *juàn* covering the principal internal-medicine clinical categories — *fēng* / *hán* / *shǔ* / *shī* / *zào* / *huǒ*, heart-and-spleen conditions, *qì*-and-blood deficiency, the major emotional-pattern syndromes, accumulations, digestive obstructions, sweating, oedema, jaundice — in seven-character verse mnemonic form with prose commentary. Composition window 1742–1749. The doctrinal framework is the imperial-orthodoxy balanced clinical reasoning of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* as a whole.
