# Bǎo yīng cuì yào 保嬰粹要
 *Distilled Essentials for the Protection of Infants*
by [[薛己]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Bǎo yīng cuì yào* 保嬰粹要 ("Distilled Essentials for the Protection of Infants"), 1 *juàn*, is one of [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ's short pediatric treatises included in his *Xuēshì yīàn* corpus ([[KR3e0070]]). The work distils the principal SòngMíng pediatric doctrines — particularly the five-organ *gān* 疳 taxonomy and *jīngfēng* 驚風 (convulsion) typology developed by [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ — into a single condensed manual oriented at general (rather than specialist) clinical use. It is the shortest member of the Xuē pediatric trilogy that further includes the *Bǎo yīng jīnjìng lù* 保嬰金鏡錄 (also by Xuē) and the larger *Bǎo yīng cuō yào* 保嬰撮要 (whose authorship between Xuē and his father Xuē Kái 薛鎧 the Sìkù *tíyào* notes is disputed).

### Abstract

The *Bǎo yīng cuì yào* presents Xuē's selection from earlier pediatric literature, condensed by symptom and presented with the characteristic Xuē-school annotation — interpolated case records and a marked preference for warming-tonifying intervention (the *Liùwèi wán* and *Bāwèi yuán* combination, with *Bǔzhōng yìqì tāng* for centre deficiency) over the cold-cooling regimens that the Dānxī school had popularized for pediatric *gān* and convulsion. As with all of Xuē's pediatric works, the doctrinal foundation is [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ's *Xiǎo'ér yào zhèng zhí jué* (cf. KR3eu009 = *Qiánshì xiǎo'ér zhí jué*), whose categorical scheme of five-organ *gān*, the eight kinds of childhood epilepsy, and the *fēngguān / qìguān / mìngguān* index-finger-vein diagnostics underlies the chapter.

Composition window 1510–1559 follows Xuē's mature career; for the parent compendium and overall transmission see [[KR3e0070]].
