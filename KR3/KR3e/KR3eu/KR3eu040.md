# Jīnguì qǐyuè (Yǎnkē) 金匱啟鑰（眼科）
 *Opening the Lock of the Golden Cabinet — Ophthalmology*
by [[黃朝坊]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Yǎnkē* 眼科 (Ophthalmology) section of [[黃朝坊]] Huáng Cháofāng's *Jīnguì qǐyuè* 金匱啟鑰 ("Opening the Lock of the Golden Cabinet") — the companion volume to the women's-medicine section (KR3eu039) — is the late-Qīng ophthalmology volume of Huáng's classical-formula-school (*jīngfāngpài* 經方派) compendium. The work belongs to the conservative literate-physician ophthalmological tradition initiated by [[倪維德]] Ní Wéidé's *Yuánjī qǐwēi* (KR3eu005) and codified by the imperial *Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Yǎnkē xīnfǎ yàojué* (KR3eu023), against the more popular *wǔlún bākuò* 五輪八廓 craft-practitioner schema.

### Abstract

Composition window late 19th century; a more precise dating is not available without the work's preface. The volume covers the standard premodern Chinese disease-categories of ophthalmology — corneal opacities, glaucoma / *qīngmáng* 青盲, the inflammatory presentations, dry-eye, lacrimation, and *jīnzhēn bōzhàng* 金針撥障 (cataract couching) — with the warming-tonifying treatment orientation that characterises Huáng's compendium as a whole. The work has not received substantial Western-language scholarship.
