## About the work

This entry duplicates the parallel KR3eu016 listing for the Qīng imperial-commission medical compendium *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* — the same 90-*juàn* work, here entered separately in the *hxwd* reprint catalog. The structural plan (seven divisions: *Dìngzhèng Shānghán lùn zhù* 17 j., *Dìngzhèng Jīnguì zhù* 8 j., *Shānbǔ míngyī fānglùn* 8 j., *Sìzhěn xīnfǎ yàojué* 1 j., *Yùnqì yàojué* 1 j., *Zhūkē xīnfǎ yàojué* 54 j., *Zhènggǔ xīnfǎ yàozhǐ* 5 j.), the editorial history (commissioned Qiánlóng 7 = 1742, completed Qiánlóng 14 = 1749) and the doctrinal stance are described in detail in [[KR3eu016]] and in the canonical Sìkù entry [[KR3e0090]].

### Abstract

The present recension is an alternate `hxwd` reprint volume of the same imperial Qīng-period medical compendium documented at [[KR3eu016]] and [[KR3e0090]]. The work is the foundational Qīng imperial-commission medical-curriculum text — used continuously by the Qīng imperial *Tàiyīyuàn* from 1749 to 1911 — and remains a major reference in modern Chinese medicine.

For full provenance, structure, dating, transmission and significance see [[KR3e0090]] (the canonical Sìkù entry) and [[KR3eu016]] (the first reprint listing in the present `hxwd` series).
