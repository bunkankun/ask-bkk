# Qiánshì xiǎo'ér zhí jué 錢氏小兒直訣
 *Master Qián's Direct Mnemonics on Paediatrics*
by [[錢乙]] (撰), edited by [[閻孝忠]] (編) — annotated by [[薛己]] (校註)

## About the work

This is the standard Míng-era recension, edited and annotated by [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ, of the foundational Chinese paediatric work originally compiled in Xuānhé 1 (1119) by [[閻孝忠]] Yán Xiàozhōng from the clinical teachings of his master [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ (1032–1113). The original work, more commonly transmitted as *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhí jué* 小兒藥證直訣 ([[KR3ej020]] in the standard Sìkù form), is in three *juàn* — doctrine, case records, and prescriptions — and is the founding synthesis of the post-classical Chinese paediatric tradition: the doctrine of paediatric *chúnyáng* 純陽 (pure-yáng) constitution, the systematic application of the five-organ scheme adjusted for paediatric physiology (*gān* 肝 always over-vigorous, *pí* 脾 always weak, *shèn* 腎 always deficient), and the canonical prescriptions *Liùwèi dìhuáng wán* 六味地黃丸, *Dǎochì sǎn* 導赤散, *Xièbái sǎn* 瀉白散, *Yìhuáng sǎn* 益黃散, the paediatric *Sìjūnzǐ tāng*, etc. Xuē Jǐ's annotated edition is 4 *juàn* (the standard Sòng three plus Xuē's case-and-commentary appendix) and is the form in which the text was transmitted into the Míng-Qīng tradition; it is the fourth of the six earlier-authority works incorporated into the *Xuēshì yīàn* corpus [[KR3e0070]].

### Abstract

Authorship and date: the work is securely the posthumous compilation of Qián Yǐ's clinical doctrine by his pupil Yán Xiàozhōng, dated Xuānhé 1 (1119) by Yán's preface; this is the date adopted here for the composition of the original. Qián Yǐ himself (1032–1113) was the founding figure of post-classical Chinese paediatrics — recognised by the Sòng imperial court after his successful treatment of a Yáng-family imperial niece and appointed to the *Tàiyī jú* 太醫局 (Imperial Medical Bureau); his biography is in *Sòngshǐ* 462. Xuē's annotation belongs to the second quarter of the 16th century, within his standard programme of collating earlier paediatric authorities.

Through Qián Yǐ's *Zhí jué* — and via Xuē's revision — Chinese paediatric medicine acquired its post-classical canonical form. The five-phase paediatric pathology, the *gānfēng* / *jīngfēng* 驚風 classification of childhood convulsion, the five-organ *gān* 疳 (chronic-malnutrition) taxonomy, and the *kǔkǒu* 苦口 oral-medication-acceptance practice are all securely traceable to this text. The *Pǔjì fāng · Yīnghái mén* ([[KR3eu001]]), Xuē's other paediatric works (KR3eu004 etc.), Wáng Kěntáng's *Yòukē zhǔnshéng* ([[KR3eu015]]) and the Qīng imperial pediatric chapters all derive ultimately from this foundation.

For the canonical Sìkù form of the original work see [[KR3ej020]] *Xiǎo'ér yàozhèng zhí jué*.
