# Tóngyuán yīshū · Xiǎo'érkē 彤園醫書·小兒科
 *Vermillion-Garden Medical Writings — Paediatrics*
by [[鄭玉壇]] (撰)

## About the work

The *Xiǎo'érkē* 小兒科 (Paediatrics) section of the *Tóngyuán yīshū* 彤園醫書 ("Vermillion-Garden Medical Writings") is the paediatric volume of the mid-Qīng comprehensive medical compendium issued by [[鄭玉壇]] Zhèng Yùtán (*hào* Tóngyuán zhǔrén 彤園主人, *fl.* late Qiánlóng / early Jiāqìng). The compendium is one of the standard mid-Qīng *zhézhōng* 折衷 (compromise-synthesis) reference works; its women's-medicine section is [[KR3ei037]] in the present knowledgebase. The paediatric section presents the post-Yīzōng-jīn-jiàn settled doctrine of Qīng paediatric practice: [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ's foundational five-organ paediatric pathology, the standard *jīngfēng* 驚風 and *gān* 疳 typologies, and the smallpox / *dòuzhěn* paediatric specialist content.

### Abstract

Composition window 1796–1820 is inferred from Zhèng's biographical placement in the late-Qián-lóng / early-Jiā-qìng period and the work's stylistic and doctrinal alignment with the post-*Yīzōng jīnjiàn* mid-Qīng consensus. The *Tóngyuán yīshū* is one of the principal mid-Qīng private-publication medical compendia that consolidated and transmitted the official *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* synthesis to a broader literate-medical audience; its accessible format and *zhézhōng* doctrinal stance made it widely cited.

The work has not received substantial Western-language scholarship; modern reprint editions in Chinese collectanea (including the present *hxwd* reprint) are the principal channel through which it is now read.
