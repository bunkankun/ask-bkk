# Yīzōng jīnjiàn · Yòukē zábìng xīnfǎ 醫宗金鑑·幼科雜病心法
 *Heart-Method for Paediatric Miscellaneous Diseases*
by [[吳謙]] (奉敕撰) and [[劉裕鐸]] (奉敕撰), under imperial commission

## About the work

The *Yòukē zábìng xīnfǎ yàojué* 幼科雜病心法要訣 (6 *juàn*) is the paediatric miscellaneous-diseases chapter of the *Yīzōng jīnjiàn* compendium ([[KR3e0090]] / KR3eu016). It is the paediatric companion to the adult internal-medicine *Zábìng xīnfǎ yàojué* (KR3eu020) and complements the *Dòuzhěn xīnfǎ yàojué* (KR3eu022) which separately covers smallpox and measles.

### Abstract

The chapter integrates the foundational paediatric doctrine of [[錢乙]] Qián Yǐ (cf. KR3eu009) — the five-organ *gān* 疳 taxonomy, the eight-types childhood-epilepsy classification, the acute / chronic *jīngfēng* 驚風 (convulsion) doctrine, the *chúnyáng* paediatric-physiology concept, the index-finger-vein diagnostics — with the late-Míng synthesis of [[薛己]] Xuē Jǐ (KR3eu004) and [[王肯堂]] Wáng Kěntáng's *Yòukē zhǔnshéng* (KR3eu015), presented in the imperial mnemonic-verse-and-commentary format. Six juan cover: paediatric pulse-and-symptom diagnostics, neonatal conditions, the *jīngfēng* / *xián* (convulsion / epilepsy) cluster, the five-organ *gān* (chronic-malnutrition) cluster, the digestive disorders, and the standard paediatric prescription regimens. Composition window 1742–1749.
