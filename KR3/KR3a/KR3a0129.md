# Wángzǐ zhènglùn 王子正論
*The Correct Discourses of Master Wáng*
by [[王肅]] (Wáng Sù, 195–256, 魏)

## About the work

A lost Cáo-Wèi political-philosophical treatise attributed to Wáng Sù 王肅. Reconstructed from quotations in 《三國魏志本傳》 (Wáng Sù's own *Sānguó zhì* biography), 《太平御覽》, 《尚書》 sub-commentary, and *Zhōu lǐ* / *Zhōu guān* exegetical citations. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1387).

### Abstract

The *Zhènglùn* is a programmatic political-classical treatise — to be distinguished from Wáng Sù's [[KR3a0121]] *Xīnshū* and his polemical *Shèngzhèng lùn* 聖證論 against Zhèng Xuán 鄭玄. The reconstructed fragments are substantially fuller than for the *Xīnshū* and include passages on ritual music (*Bā yì* 八佾, *Xián chí* 咸池, *Dà jūn* 太均), the calendar of mourning, *Zhōu lǐ* offices, and statecraft. The work is repeatedly cited under variant titles (*Zhèng lùn* 政論, *Wángshǐ zhèng lùn* 王氏正論) in Táng sub-commentaries and leishu. Wáng Sù's career and his position as anti-Zhèng-Xuán classical authority are treated in [[王肅]] and on [[KR3a0001]]. The composition window is bracketed by his life. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
