# Rénzǐ dàolùn 任子道論
*On the Way, by Master Rén*
by [[任嘏]] (Rén Gǔ, *fl.* mid 3rd century, 魏)

## About the work

A lost Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 *zǐbù* treatise by Rén Gǔ 任嘏. Reconstructed from quotations in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, 《北堂書鈔》, 《初學記》. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1452).

### Abstract

Rén Gǔ — a native of Lèān 樂安 and a scholar-official of the Wèi court — composed the *Dàolùn* as a substantial *zǐ*-house treatise on the Way: surviving fragments are aphoristic and read in the same broad ethical-political register as the more famous Wèi treatises by Wáng Sù 王肅 and Dù Shù 杜恕. The text is mentioned by name in the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 in 10 (or by some manuscript variants, 8) *juàn*; only fragments survive. He is noted in *Sānguó zhì* commentary as one of the *Wéishì* 魏氏 "twenty-four worthies" of the Héjiān 河間 area, and the *Dàolùn* itself is referenced as a youthful work of his maturity. The composition window is bracketed by the Wèi period (220–265); the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
