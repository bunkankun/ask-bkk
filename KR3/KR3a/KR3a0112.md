# Zhèng xué yú jiàn shù 正學隅見述
*A Narration of My Cornered Glimpses of Orthodox Learning*
by [[王宏]] (Wáng Hóng, 清)

## About the work

A one-juan Mid-Qīng *Lǐxué*-orthodox *biji* by Wáng Hóng. The title — *yú jiàn* "cornered glimpses" — articulates a humble-narration mode, drawing on Confucian humility-conventions. Provenance and composition date unrecorded; the catalog meta gives no dates.

### Abstract

The *Zhèng xué yú jiàn shù* is a comparatively obscure Mid-Qīng *Lǐxué* work. Composition window unrecoverable; the frontmatter brackets broadly to the Mid-Qīng (ca. 1700–1750).

The substantive content is conventional *Zhūzǐxué* loyalist *biji*-style reflection, methodologically aligned with the broader Mid-Qīng anti-Yáng-míng line.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi*.
