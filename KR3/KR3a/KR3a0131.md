# Gǔjīn tōnglùn 古今通論
*General Discourse on Ancient and Modern (Times)*
by [[王嬰]] (Wáng Yīng, *fl.* Jìn, dates obscure)

## About the work

A lost Jìn 晉 historical-philosophical compilation by Wáng Yīng 王嬰. Reconstructed from quotations in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, and from Qútán Xīdá's 瞿曇悉達 *Kāiyuán zhàn jīng* 開元占經 (the early Táng astronomical-omenological compendium, which preserves astronomical/cosmological passages). Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1390).

### Abstract

The *Gǔjīn tōnglùn* — also cited in some sources as *Gǔjīn tōngyǔ* 古今通語 — is one of several Jìn-period works that survived only through citation in Mǎ Zǒng's 馬總 Táng-era *Yìlín* 意林. The fragments are eclectic: a substantial fraction are astronomical-cosmological (cited by *Kāiyuán zhàn jīng* for early imperial speculation on planetary motion and yīnyáng correlations); others are aphorisms on the rise and fall of dynasties — the *gǔjīn* 古今 frame in the title — and a few on moral psychology. The author Wáng Yīng is otherwise unrecorded in the standard histories; some Sòng catalogs treat him as a Wèi-Jìn figure, others assign the title to a southern Jìn (Sòng-Qí) author of the same name. The date-bracket here is conservative — the Jìn-period spread — pending more secure attribution. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
