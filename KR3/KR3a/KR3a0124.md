# Yuánzǐ zhèngshū 袁子正書
*The Correct Writings of Master Yuán*
by [[袁準]] (Yuán Zhǔn, *fl.* mid–late 3rd century, 晉)

## About the work

A lost Western Jìn 西晉 *zǐbù* treatise by Yuán Zhǔn 袁準 (son of Yuán Huàn 袁渙, the famous Wèi-period official). Reconstructed from quotations in 《初學記》, 《北堂書鈔》, 《太平御覽》, 《字林》, 《文選注》 (Li Shàn's notes on Rèn Fǎng's poems and edicts), and other Táng leishu. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1380).

### Abstract

Yuán Zhǔn's two surviving *zǐ*-house works — the *Zhèngshū* 正書 (this entry) and the *Zhènglùn* 正論 (cf. [[KR3a0130]]) — are commonly treated as a pair in the bibliographic record; some Sòng catalogs collapse them. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 lists a *Yuánzǐ* 袁子 in 19 *juàn* attributed to Yuán Zhǔn, of which only fragments survive. The reconstructed *Zhèngshū* covers ritual and institutional discussion (especially the calendar of mourning, the *xiǎogōng* 小功 grade, and other ritual topics), classical exegesis (notes on the *Chūnqiū*), and short essays on government. As a son of Yuán Huàn — and a contemporary of the late Wèi and early Jìn courts — Yuán Zhǔn is regularly cited by Péi Sōngzhī in the *Sānguó zhì* commentary on Wèi history. The date-bracket here is taken as the Western Jìn period (265–316); the *Sānguó zhì* commentary and *Jìn shū* notices suggest he was active in the Tàishǐ 泰始 (265–274) and following reigns.
