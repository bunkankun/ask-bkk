# Zhōushēngzǐ yàolùn 周生子要論
*Essential Discussions of Master Zhōushēng*
by [[周生烈]] (Zhōushēng Liè, *fl.* mid-3rd century, 魏)

## About the work

A lost Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 *Rújiā* 儒家 treatise transmitted only in fragments. The received text is a Qing-era *jíyì* 輯佚 reconstruction assembled from quotations preserved in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, 《北堂書鈔》, 《藝文類聚》, 《白居易六帖》 and related leishu. It is not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; the Kanripo entry derives from the CHANT corpus (CH2a1372).

### Abstract

The *Zhōushēngzǐ yàolùn* is attributed to Zhōushēng Liè 周生烈 (compound surname 周生, *míng* Liè), a Wèi-period classicist whose biographical record is thin. The *Sānguó zhì*, *Wèi shū* j. 13 mentions him only obliquely; the *Suí shū* 經籍志 lists him as the author of a *Lúnyǔ yìshuō* 論語義說 and several other classical and *zǐbù* titles, of which only this *Yàolùn* survives as a reconstructed compilation. The fragments are aphoristic and moralistic, framed around statecraft maxims (御天下失節 四海失墜), the reading of men (賢哲不可以色貌誘之), and conventional Confucian themes of governance and litigation (聽訟…). Many of the longer passages are recovered from 《意林》 j. 5 — Mǎ Zǒng's 馬總 Táng-era epitome — supplemented by short citations from 《太平御覽》 and 《北堂書鈔》. The composition window is bracketed to the author's plausible career under Wèi (220–265); the *received recension* is a 19th-century *jíyì*.

## Translations and research

*No substantial secondary literature located.* The fragmentary survival has attracted occasional notice in Wèi-Jìn intellectual histories but no dedicated monograph or critical edition outside of Mǎ Guóhàn's 馬國翰 *Yùhán shānfáng jíyì shū* 玉函山房輯佚書 collection.
