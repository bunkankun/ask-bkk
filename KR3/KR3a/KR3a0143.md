# Chénzǐ yàoyán 陳子要言
*The Essential Sayings of Master Chén*
by [[陳融]] (Chén Róng, *fl.* 3rd century, 吳)

## About the work

A very short SūnWú 孫吳 *zǐbù* fragment-compilation, surviving only as a small handful of citations in 《太平御覽》. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1466).

### Abstract

Chén Róng 陳融 is barely attested in the standard histories. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書經籍志 records his *Yàoyán* 要言 under the Wú period without further data, and the Sòng catalog of the *Chóngwén zǒngmù* 崇文總目 lists it as already very fragmentary. The surviving citations — perhaps a dozen lines at most — preserve only short moral sayings and one or two political aphorisms; no programmatic structure can be reconstructed. The date-bracket follows the Wú period (222–280); the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
