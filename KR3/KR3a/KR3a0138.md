# Sūzǐ 蘇子
*Master Sū*
by [[蘇彥]] (Sū Yàn, *fl.* 4th century, 晉)

## About the work

A lost Eastern Jìn 東晉 *zǐbù* treatise by Sū Yàn 蘇彥. Reconstructed from quotations in 《初學記》, 《太平御覽》, 《史記·李斯傳》《索隱》, 《後漢書·王符傳》, 《文選·陸士衡長安有狹邪行》注 and Lǐ Shàn's 《文選》 commentary on Lù Jī 陸機. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1458).

### Abstract

Sū Yàn was a 4th-century Jìn literatus, best known for his *Bù qián tiān* 步天歌 (astronomical-mnemonic verse) and the *Sūzǐ* — a *zǐ*-house compilation. The reconstructed fragments are heterogeneous: aphoristic moral sayings, classical commentary, observations on the *Lǎozǐ* and *Shàng shū*, and historical reflection (the *Shǐ jì·Lǐ Sī zhuàn* citation is particularly notable). Some Sòng catalogs treat the *Sūzǐ* as the *Yǎnshì lùn* 演史論; the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* preserves the title without further data. The author's exact dates are not preserved; the date-bracket here is the Eastern Jìn period (317–420). The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
