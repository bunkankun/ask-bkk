# Dú shū ǒu jì 讀書偶記
*Occasional Reading-Notes*
by [[雷鈜]] (Léi Hóng, 1697–1760, 清)

## About the work

A 3-juan Mid-Qīng *Lǐxué*-aligned reading-notebook by Léi Hóng. The composition window is Léi's working life (1720s–1760). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1720–1760.

### Abstract

The *Dú shū ǒu jì* is the closing work in the SKQS *Rújiā* sequence of KR3a — the work of a Mid-Qīng *Lǐxué* writer of moderate reputation. Substantively conventional Mid-Qīng *Zhūzǐxué* loyalist *biji*-style work.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi*.
