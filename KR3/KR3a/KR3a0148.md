# Shìwěi 士緯
*The Woof-Threads of the Officer*
by [[姚信]] (Yáo Xìn, *fl.* mid 3rd century, 吳)

## About the work

A lost Sūn-Wú 孫吳 *zǐbù* treatise on personnel evaluation (*rénwù* 人物) by Yáo Xìn 姚信. Reconstructed from quotations in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, 《藝文類聚》. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1473). The title-list (Kanripo and CHANT) gives Yáo Xìn as a Shǔ-Hàn figure, but the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* and standard biographical reference works place him under Sūn-Wú; the dynasty here follows the *Suí shū*.

### Abstract

The *Shìwěi* — the title combines *shì* 士 (officer / scholar-aspirant) with *wěi* 緯 (the woof-threads of a fabric, by extension the apocryphal weave underlying the *jīng* 經 warp) — is a *rénwù* treatise in the same broad genre as the more famous *Rénwù zhì* 人物志 of Liú Shào 劉劭. The surviving fragments engage with the standard topics of mid-3rd-century *rénwù lùn*: the discrimination of talent, the relation of inner character to outward expression, the management of recommendation systems, and the moral psychology of office-holding. Yáo Xìn was also the author of an important *Zhōu Yì zhù* 周易注 — Wú-period *Yì* commentary that is preserved in fragments in Lǐ Dǐngzuò's 李鼎祚 *Zhōu Yì jí jiě* 周易集解 of the Táng — but the *Shìwěi* is his major *zǐbù* contribution. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists it in 10 *juàn*. The composition window follows the Wú period; the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
