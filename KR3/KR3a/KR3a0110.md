# Yù lǎn jīngshǐ jiǎng yì 御覽經史講義
*Imperial-Reading Lectures on Classics and Histories*
imperially commissioned; compiled by [[蔣溥]] (Jiǎng Pǔ, 1708–1761, 清, 奉敕編) and [[劉統勳]] (Liú Tǒngxūn, 1700–1773, 清, 奉敕編)

## About the work

A 30-juan imperial-pedagogical anthology of classical and historical lectures, compiled under Qiánlóng's commission by Jiǎng Pǔ and Liú Tǒngxūn — both senior Mid-Qiánlóng *dàxuéshì*. The work was commissioned for imperial reading-and-discussion (*yù lǎn*) and provides the official imperial *jīngyán* curriculum for the early-Qiánlóng court. Composition window: bracketed by Qiánlóng's accession (1736) and Jiǎng Pǔ's death (1761). Most likely composed in the 1740s–1750s.

### Abstract

The *Yù lǎn jīngshǐ jiǎng yì* is the major Mid-Qiánlóng imperial-pedagogical anthology. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1736–1761.

The substantive structure: classical and historical lectures organised for imperial use, drawing on the standard pre-Qīng *jīngyán* tradition and adapted to Qiánlóng-court conventions.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi* (across V722–V723).
