# Qù fá lùn 去伐論
*Discourse on Eschewing Self-Boasting*
by [[孫毓]] (Sūn Yù, *fl.* late 3rd century, 晉)

## About the work

A very short Western Jìn 西晉 moral-philosophical essay by the ritualist and classicist Sūn Yù 孫毓 (governor of Chángshā 長沙太守 under the Jìn). Preserved as a single brief fragment in 《藝文類聚》. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1384).

### Abstract

The *Qù fá lùn* survives only as an *Yìwén lèijù* extract of about fifty lines, but the topic is conventional: an admonition against the cardinal Confucian fault of self-praise (*fá* 伐 — "to vaunt one's merit"), framed by appeals to the *Lúnyǔ* and the *Lǐjì*. Sūn Yù was a substantial classicist — the *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* records his *Máo shī yì wèn* 毛詩異問 and *Lǐ jì zhù* 禮記注 — and is regularly cited in *Tōng diǎn* 通典 for ritual opinion on mourning grades. The composition window is bracketed by his Western Jìn career. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
