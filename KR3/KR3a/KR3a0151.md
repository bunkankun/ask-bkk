# Shíwù lùn 時務論
*On the Pressing Tasks of the Time*
by [[楊偉]] (Yáng Wěi, *fl.* mid 3rd century, 魏)

## About the work

A very short Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 political essay by Yáng Wěi 楊偉 — best known as the Wèi-period astronomer who compiled the *Jǐngchū lì* 景初曆 (the calendar promulgated in 237 under Cáo Ruì). Surviving only as a brief cluster of citations in 《太平御覽》. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1476).

### Abstract

Yáng Wěi was Director of the *Tàishǐ* 太史 (Grand Astronomer's Office) under Wèi and presented the *Jǐngchū lì* 景初曆 — a major Wèi-period reform of the official calendar, promulgated in 237 and used through the Wèi-Jìn period. He is known primarily as a *lìfǎ* 曆法 (calendrical-astronomical) specialist. The *Shíwù lùn* — preserved only as a fragment — is a short political essay rather than an astronomical text, on the duties of the ruler in addressing the pressing administrative tasks of the day (*shíwù* 時務). The single longer extract in *Tàipíng yùlǎn* is the only sustained passage; no programmatic structure can be reconstructed. The date-bracket follows Yáng Wěi's Wèi-period career. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
