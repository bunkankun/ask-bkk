# Shìyào lùn 世要論
*Discourse on the Essentials of the Age*
by [[桓範]] (Huán Fàn, ?–249, 魏)

## About the work

A lost Cáo-Wèi 曹魏 political-philosophical treatise by Huán Fàn 桓範 (also cited as *Shì lùn* 世論 or, in some early Táng sources, *Huánfàn shìyào lùn*). Reconstructed from quotations in 《意林》, 《太平御覽》, 《北堂書鈔》, 《初學記》 and Lǐ Shàn's 《文選》 commentary on Liú Xiàobiāo's *Biànmìng lùn* 辨命論, Wáng Sēngdá's 王僧達 *Hé Lángyé Wáng yī gǔ shī*, and Lù Jī's *Jié kè shào nián chǎng xíng* 結客少年場行. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1465).

### Abstract

Huán Fàn was a senior Wèi statesman who served Cáo Cāo and his Wèi successors as Court Gentleman (尚書), Cavalier Attendant-in-Ordinary (散騎常侍), and Grand Master of Splendid Happiness (大鴻臚). His biography is in *Sānguó zhì* 三國志 j. 9 (曹爽傳附桓範傳, with Péi Sōngzhī's substantial *zhù*) — and his death in 249 is among the most famous events of the Wèi-Jìn transition: he was executed with Cáo Shuǎng 曹爽 by the Sīmǎ faction in the coup of that year. The *Shìyào lùn* was his major prose work — a comprehensive Wèi-period treatise on rulership and governance, presented as a series of *lùn* on specific topics. The *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* lists it in 12 *juàn*; only fragments survive. The surviving content covers the duties of the ruler, the qualities of ministers, the discrimination of talent, the deployment of *xíng* 刑 and *dé* 德, and military strategy. It is one of the most substantively political *zǐ*-house texts of the Wèi period. The death date of 249 anchors the upper end of the composition window; the received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.

## Translations and research

*No substantial dedicated secondary literature located* in Western languages; Andrew Chittick's work on the Wèi-Jìn aristocracy and Howard L. Goodman's *Ts'ao P'i Transcendent* mention Huán Fàn in passing. Standard fragment-compilation: 嚴可均 *Quán sān guó wén* 全三國文 j. 37.
