# Qīn dìng zhí zhōng chéng xiàn 欽定執中成憲
*Imperial-Sanctioned Established Standards for Holding the Mean*
imperially commissioned by [[雍正]] (Yōngzhèng, 1678–1735, 清, 敕撰)

## About the work

An 8-juan imperial pedagogical compendium imperially commissioned by Yōngzhèng in *Yōngzhèng* 6 (1728). The title — *zhí zhōng chéng xiàn* "established standards for holding the mean" — draws on the *Shàng shū* "Dà Yǔ mò" 大禹謨's "*yǔn zhí jué zhōng*" (firmly hold the mean) and articulates the work's project as imperial-pedagogical concentration on this canonical principle. The work draws Confucian classical materials and historical exemplars on the topic of *zhí zhōng* (holding the mean) and pairs in tradition with Yōngzhèng's other imperial-pedagogical work the *Shèng yù guǎng xùn* ([[KR3a0102]]).

### Abstract

The *Qīn dìng zhí zhōng chéng xiàn* is one of the principal Yōngzhèng-period imperial-pedagogical works. Composition: precisely datable to *Yōngzhèng* 6 (1728). The frontmatter brackets to 1728–1728.

The substantive content draws together classical citation and imperial elaboration on *zhí zhōng* — articulating Yōngzhèng's *Lǐxué* position with the precision typical of his administrative style.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi*; *Wényuāngé shūmù*.
