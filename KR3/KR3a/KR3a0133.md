# Lì xué 厲學
*On Diligent Study*
by [[庾闡]] (Yǔ Chǎn, c. 286–339, 晉)

## About the work

A short Eastern Jìn 東晉 essay on the discipline of scholarship by Yǔ Chǎn 庾闡. Preserved chiefly in the context of Yú Pǔ's 虞溥 biography in *Jìn shū* 晉書 j. 82, where Yǔ Chǎn's brief piece is embedded; the *jíyì* compilation here adds short ancillary citations. Not in the *Sìkù quánshū*; sourced from CHANT (CH2a1395).

### Abstract

The *Lì xué* is a brief admonitory essay urging persistent application in classical study — a topos as old as the *Xúnzǐ* 《勸學》 chapter, but recast in the rhythmical parallel-prose 駢文 idiom that was crystallising in the early Eastern Jìn. The piece is preserved primarily as a quotation in the *Jìn shū* biography of Yú Pǔ 虞溥 (the noted Eastern Jìn classicist and prefect of Póyáng 鄱陽), where it is cited as the rhetorical model that Yú Pǔ adopted in instructing his prefectural school. Yǔ Chǎn's longer *zǐ*-house treatise *Wén shì* 文釋 is separately preserved (cf. [[KR3a0156]]). The composition window is bracketed by his Eastern Jìn floruit; he was active from the Tàixìng 太興 reign onward. The received recension is a 19th-century *jíyì*.
