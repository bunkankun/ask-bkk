# Róngcūn yǔlù 榕村語錄
*Recorded Sayings of Master Róng-cūn*
sayings of [[李光地]] (Lǐ Guāngdì, *hào* Róngcūn 榕村, 1642–1718, 清, 撰); compiled by [[徐用錫]] (Xú Yòngxī, 1656–1737, 清, 輯) and [[李清植]] (Lǐ Qīngzhí, 1690–1744, 清, 輯)

## About the work

A 30-juan posthumous *yǔlù* of Lǐ Guāngdì — the leading Kāngxī-court *Lǐxué* official — compiled by his pupil Xú Yòngxī and his grandson Lǐ Qīngzhí. The work records Lǐ's *jiǎngxué* discussions across his career and is the principal source for Lǐ's mature philosophical-political positions outside his imperially-commissioned compilations (the *Yù zuǎn xìnglǐ jīng yì* [[KR3a0107]] and *Zhūzǐ quán shū* [[KR3a0108]]). The composition window: bracketed by Lǐ's mature lecturing-career (post-1670) and the editorial work by his successors (likely 1720s–1740s, before Lǐ Qīngzhí's 1744 death). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1670–1740.

### Abstract

The *Róngcūn yǔlù* is the principal source for Lǐ Guāngdì's mature thought outside his imperial commissions. Substantively the work establishes Lǐ's distinct positions on *Lǐxué* doctrinal questions, including his sober but firm Zhū-Xī-loyalist response to the Wáng Yángmíng tradition.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi*.
