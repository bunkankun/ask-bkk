# Sī biàn lù jí yào 思辨錄輯要
*The Essential Selection from the Records of Thought-and-Discrimination*
parent work by [[陸世儀]] (Lù Shìyí, *hào* Fútíng 桴亭, 1611–1672, 清, 撰); abridged-and-arranged by [[張伯行]] (Zhāng Bóxíng, 1651–1725, 清, 編次)

## About the work

A 35-juan abridged-and-arranged version (by the early-Qīng publisher-scholar Zhāng Bóxíng of the *Zhèng yì táng*) of Lù Shìyí's *Sī biàn lù* 思辨錄 — Lù's foundational *yǔlù*-style work of moral-philosophical reflection. Lù was one of the *Sān dà rú* (Three Great Confucians) of the early-Qīng *yímín* generation, alongside Lǐ Yóng and Sūn Qíféng; his *Sī biàn lù* was composed during his Míng-loyalist retreat. Zhāng Bóxíng's editorial work — undertaken at the *Zhèng yì táng* publishing-house — selected and re-arranged Lù's materials into the present 35-juan systematic form, which became the standard transmission. The original *Sī biàn lù* was longer and less systematically organised.

### Abstract

The *Sī biàn lù jí yào* is the principal Qīng-period preservation of Lù Shìyí's mature thought. The composition window has two layers: (i) Lù Shìyí's original composition spanning his retired-life period from the late Míng / early Qīng (ca. 1640–1672); (ii) Zhāng Bóxíng's editorial arrangement at the *Zhèng yì táng* in his Sūzhōu period (ca. 1707). The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1640–1707.

The substantive content places Lù as a major *yímín* generation Confucian, with positions overlapping but distinct from the contemporary Sūn Qíféng ([[KR3a0023]] editor) and Lǐ Yóng. Zhāng Bóxíng's editorial role made the work standard reading in the early-Qīng *Zhūzǐxué* curriculum.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi*; *Zhèng yì táng quán shū* 正誼堂全書 (Zhāng Bóxíng's collected publications).
