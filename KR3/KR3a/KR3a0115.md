# Dú Zhū suí bǐ 讀朱隨筆
*Random Notes on Reading Zhū-zǐ*
by [[陸隴其]] (Lù Lǒngqí, *hào* Sānyú 三魚, 1630–1692, 清)

## About the work

A 4-juan *biji* of Lù Lǒngqí's notes on reading Zhū Xī's writings — methodologically a Mid-Qīng equivalent of Hú Jūrén's *Jū yè lù* ([[KR3a0081]]) but focused specifically on Zhū Xī reading. Lù was the foundational early-Qīng Zhū-Xī-purist; this work documents his close reading of Zhū's corpus item-by-item. Composition window: bracketed by Lù's working life (post-1670 *jìnshì*) through to his 1692 death. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1670–1692.

### Abstract

The *Dú Zhū suí bǐ* is Lù Lǒngqí's principal close-reading record of Zhū Xī. The substantive position is uniformly Zhū-Xī-loyalist, with sharp critique of any drift toward Lù Wáng *xīnxué*.

The bibliographic record: SKQS *Zǐbù — Rújiā lèi*.
