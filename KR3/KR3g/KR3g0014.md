# Dàyǎn suǒyǐn 大衍索隱
*Index-Searching of the Great Expansion*
by [[丁易東]] (Dīng Yìdōng, late Sòng / early Yuán Sòng-loyalist, 宋, *zhuàn* 撰)

## About the work

Dīng Yìdōng's 3-juan systematic explication of the *dàyǎn zhī shù* 大衍之數 (great-expansion number = 50) of the *Yìjīng*'s *Xìcí* — the foundational Confucian numerological-divinatory parameter. Dīng Yìdōng was a late-Sòng *jìnshì*, *Cháofèng dàfū Tàifǔsì zhǔbù* (Court Gentleman / Imperial Stables Records-Keeper), and *Zhěnqián cōngbīngsuǒ biānxiūguān* (Privy Council Editor); refused service under the Yuán; built a private academy and taught students; later served as *Yuányáng shūyuàn shāncháng* (Director of the Yuányáng Academy).

Structure (per the 提要):

- *Yuányǎn* 原衍 (Original-Expansion) — 36 figures expounding the meaning of *dàyǎn zhī shù wǔshí, qí yòng sìshíjiǔ* "the great-expansion number is 50, [we] use 49"
- *Yìyǎn* 翼衍 (Wing-Expansion) — 29 figures elaborating the *Hétú* and related diagrams
- *Jīyǎn* 稽衍 (Examination-Expansion) — comparative collation of 57 different schools' *dàyǎn* interpretations from the *Yì wěi Qiánzáo dù* onwards, with Dīng Yìdōng's critical assessment

The work was nearly lost: alternative recensions had dropped the *Jīyǎn* section entirely; the *Sìkù* editors recovered it from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* and reorganized the material per the original table-of-contents. The work is one of the principal late-Sòng *Yìjīng*-numerological compendia.

For the broader *Yìjīng*-numerological tradition, see [[KR3g0001]], [[KR3g0005]], [[KR3g0007]], [[KR3g0013]], [[KR3g0015]].
