# Wǔjīng suànshù 五經算術
*The Five Classics' Mathematical Methods*
by [[甄鸞]] (Zhēn Luán, Northern Zhōu, *zhuàn* 撰); imperially commissioned annotation by [[李淳風]] (Lǐ Chúnfēng, Tang, *zhù* 注)

## About the work

Zhēn Luán's 2-juan systematic mathematical commentary on the calculatable passages of the Five Classics — the foundational Confucian canon of *Shūjīng*, *Shījīng*, *Yìjīng*, *Lùnyǔ* (and the related *Xiàojīng*), *Sānlǐ* (the three Ritual Classics), *Lǐjì*, and *Chūnqiū* (with its *Zuǒzhuàn* commentary). For each passage in the classics that involves quantitative-and-computational reasoning, Zhēn Luán supplies the mathematical apparatus needed to verify or interpret the classical statement.

The work is structured topically by source-classic and by computational-problem-type:

**Upper *juàn***: solar-and-lunar conjunction methods (*tuī rìyuè hésù fǎ*); 19-year intercalary cycle (*qiú shíjiǔ nián qī rùn fǎ*); the *Shàngshū* / *Xiàojīng* "myriad people" notation method (*zhàomín zhù shù yuè cì fǎ*); the *Shī Fátán* differing Máo and Zhèng commentaries; the *Shī Fēngnián* notation method; the *Zhōuyì* yarrow-stalk numbers; the *Lùnyǔ* "thousand-chariot state" calculation; the *Zhōuguān* chariot-cover method; the *Yílǐ* mourning-garment/cord measurements; the *Lǐjì Wángzhì* "ancient hundred-*lǐ* equals present 121-*lǐ* 60-*bù* 4-*chǐ* 2-*cùn* 2-*fēn*" method (and similar Zhèng-Xuán commentary verifications).

**Lower *juàn***: *Lǐjì Yuèlìng* *Huángzhōng* pitch-pipe methods; *Lǐjì Lǐyùn* commentary methods; the *Hànshū* terminus-at-*Nánshì* method; *Lǐjì Tóuhú* (pitch-pot game) calculation; *Chūnqiū Lǔ Xīgōng* 5th-year *zhèngyuè xīnhài shuò* (1st-month new-moon date) method; the related "next-month new-moon" derivation; the *Lǔ Xīgōng* 5th-year winter-solstice computation; the related "next *qì*" derivation; *Lǔ Wéngōng* 1st-year (*sìwèi* / *yǐwèi*) intercalary-month placement reconstruction; *Lǔ Wéngōng* 6th-year intercalary-month problem; *Lǔ Xiānggōng* 27th-year intercalary problem; the *Jiàngxiàn* (Jìn) old man's age-of-450-*jiǎzǐ* problem; *Wéngōng* 11th-year *Jiàngxiàn* old-man's-birth-month problem; *Lǔ Zhāogōng* 19th-year intercalary-month placement; the related *jírì* derivation; *Lǔ Zhāogōng* 19th-year (*wùyín*) intercalary placement; *Lǔ Zhāogōng* 19th and 20th year monthly-new-moon and winter-solstice problems.

The work is therefore both a mathematical handbook and a Confucian-classical commentary; its purpose is to demonstrate the mathematical-arithmetic skills required for proper exegesis of the Five Classics, and conversely to demonstrate the canonical-textual grounding of the *suànshù* (computational arts) tradition. The Sìkù 提要 makes the explicit point that the work is "*not only useful to the mathematical-arts family — it is also sufficient to clarify the Classics-and-Histories and to verify-and-correct doubtful meanings; for the *kǎojù* (evidential research) study it is especially meritorious*". This is one of the most explicit *Sìkù* editorial endorsements of the integration of mathematical-and-classical scholarship.

The Sìkù recension is recovered from the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* and reorganized into 2 *juàn* per the Tang *Yìwén zhì*'s recorded juan-count.

### Abstract

Composition window: c. 560–575 CE. Zhēn Luán was active under the Northern-Zhōu *Wǔdì* (560-578); his death is dated c. 575. The work was composed during this productive Northern-Zhōu period, before Lǐ Chúnfēng's Tang-imperial annotation (c. 656).

The work's significance:

(a) **Mathematical-Confucian classical synthesis**: the *Wǔjīng suànshù* is the principal pre-modern Chinese work that systematically applies mathematical analysis to the Confucian classical canon. By demonstrating that proper classical exegesis requires mathematical apparatus, the work positions mathematics as an essential component of Confucian scholarship rather than as a separate technical specialty. This integration of mathematics-and-classical-scholarship would shape Chinese mathematical practice through the late imperial period.

(b) **Pitch-pipe and calendrical material as classical exegesis**: the work's substantial treatment of pitch-pipe (*lǜlǚ*) and calendrical (*lì*) computations demonstrates that these specialist domains are direct extensions of the canonical Confucian classical inheritance — the *Lǐjì Yuèlìng* and the *Chūnqiū* are the principal sources for both. This framing establishes the *lǜlì* tradition as part of mainstream Confucian scholarship, not a separate technical concern.

(c) **The Sìkù editorial endorsement**: the 提要's strong statement that the work is "*especially meritorious for kǎojù study*" — citing the work's preservation of pre-Tang readings of the *Hòu-Hànzhì* and *Lǐjì yìshū* that have been corrupted in present-day transmission — places the *Wǔjīng suànshù* within the high-Qīng *kǎojù* (evidential research) project. This editorial framing is one of the more explicit endorsements of the integration of mathematical and philological scholarship in the *Sìkù tíyào* corpus.
