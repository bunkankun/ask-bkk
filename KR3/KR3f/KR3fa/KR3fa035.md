# Xuānxī tōng 宣西通
 *A Comprehensive Connection of the [Hóngfàn] Xuán and the West (Digest Version)*
by [[許桂林]] (撰)

## About the work

This catalog entry is the single-*juǎn* digest companion to [[KR3fa034]] *Xuānxī tōng* (4 *juǎn*), both by [[許桂林]] Xǔ Guìlín (1779–1821). The two together form a paired synthesist monograph and its précis: the longer four-*juǎn* version is the full philological-and-astronomical argument that the *Hóngfàn* 洪範 *xuánjī yùhéng* contains the framework of the Jesuit-introduced astronomy; the present single-*juǎn* version is its condensation into a usable summary. The catalog gives them separate reference numbers (`KX03-07-030` for the 4-*juǎn* version, `KXDLJ0031311` for the present 1-*juǎn* version) but they are the same author's work.

### Abstract

Composition window: Xǔ's mature scholarly years c. 1810–1821; the digest is plausibly a slightly later compression of the longer version, but no internal evidence allows narrower dating. See [[KR3fa034]] for the full treatment of authorship, content and significance. The digest is preserved in the *Sìkù wèishōu shū jíkān* and in the *Zhōngguó kēxué jìshù diǎnjí tōnghuì* under the separate KXDLJ identifier `KXDLJ0031311`, distinct from the four-*juǎn* version's KX-identifier; this is the standard editorial convention of the modern reprint series, which preserves separate textual witnesses with separate identifiers.

The digest summarises the four-fold argument structure of the longer work: (a) the *xuánjī yùhéng* and the equatorial-celestial reference system; (b) the *Yáo diǎn* solstitial-equinoctial framework and the precession problem; (c) the *qīzhèng* planetary system as anticipated in the classical *Yuèlìng* and *Lǚshì chūnqiū* tradition; (d) the *Hóngfàn jiǔchóu* as the master frame.
