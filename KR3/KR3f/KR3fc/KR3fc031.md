# Dùsuàn jiě 度算解
 *Exposition of Surveying Calculations*
by [[陳藎謨]] (撰)

## About the work

[[陳藎謨]] Chén Jìnmó's supplementary commentary on the computational procedures of his [[KR3fc030]] *Dùcè*, with particular focus on the use of the proportional-divider (*bǐlìguī* 比例規) and the related calculation tables. The work is presented as the *jiě* 解 (exposition) of the parent treatise's *suàn* 算 (calculation) procedures.

### Abstract

The *Dùsuàn jiě* extends the *Dùcè* by supplying detailed worked examples of the surveying-and-measurement procedures introduced there. Each procedure of the parent text is restated, with explicit step-by-step computation and (where applicable) instrument-manipulation instructions for the proportional-divider. The work also includes auxiliary tables — chord-and-sine tables, multiplication tables for instrument-graduation, and the standard *bǐlìfèncùn* 比例分寸 (proportional-graduation) reference values — that were not included in the parent text.

The work is a key documentary record of mid-Chóng-zhēn-era integrated practical mathematics. Together with the *Dùcè*, it shows the level of integrated Sino-European surveying-mathematical practice achievable in the closing decades of the Míng — distinct from but comparable to the contemporaneous work of [[徐光啟]] Xú Guāngqǐ in Beijing.

For the substantive discussion of Chén Jìnmó's surveying methodology see [[KR3fc030]]; for biographical context see the Person note at [[陳藎謨]].

Dating: *NotBefore* / *notAfter* bracketed at 1620–1650 as for the parent work, with the supplementary commentary plausibly completed in the same productive period.
