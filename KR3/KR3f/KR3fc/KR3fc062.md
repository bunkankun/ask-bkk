# Liùjiǔ xuān suànshū 六九軒筭書
 *The* Liù-jiǔ xuān *Mathematical Book*
by [[劉衡]] (撰)

## About the work

[[劉衡]] Liú Héng's (1776–1841) general collection of mathematical investigations in 3 *juàn*, gathered under the studio name *Liùjiǔ xuān* 六九軒 ("Six-nine Studio"). The collection is the most general-purpose of Liú Héng's mathematical works, presenting investigations across multiple branches of the mid-Qīng mathematical syllabus.

### Abstract

The *Liùjiǔ xuān suànshū* is the general-collection counterpart of Liú Héng's more specialised treatises ([[KR3fc063]] on sundials, [[KR3fc064]] on surveying with the right-triangle sighting-rod, [[KR3fc065]] on tabular root-extraction, [[KR3fc066]] on the *jiègēnfāng* algebraic equation, [[KR3fc067]] on the four-term proportion). Where those works treat single problem-types in depth, the *Liùjiǔ xuān* presents shorter investigations across multiple topics — typically problems that interested Liú Héng but did not merit a self-contained treatise.

The 3-*juàn* organisation broadly follows the standard mid-Qīng mathematical syllabus: arithmetic-and-algebraic procedures in *juàn* 1, geometric-and-surveying procedures in *juàn* 2, and miscellaneous problems including computational shortcuts in *juàn* 3. The work as a whole shows Liú Héng's working method: a procedural mathematician whose interest is in efficient computation rather than theoretical reformulation, in the lineage of [[楊輝]] Yáng Huī's practical-arithmetic tradition.

Liú Héng is associated with the mid-Jiā-qìng to Dàoguāng generation working largely outside the metropolitan Yángzhōu / Sūzhōu circles centred on [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán. His mathematical output is unusually voluminous for a provincial scholar; the *Liùjiǔ xuān suànshū* preserves the mid-Qīng practical-arithmetic tradition that is otherwise underrepresented in the surviving Qīng mathematical literature.

Dating: Liú Héng lived 1776–1841. *notBefore* 1800 (early mature work, age 24); *notAfter* 1841 (death year). The collection-format implies extended composition rather than a single dated compilation.
