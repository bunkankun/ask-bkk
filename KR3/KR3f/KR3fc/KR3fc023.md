# Jiǔzhāng xiángzhù bǐlèi suànfǎ dàquán 九章詳註比類算法大全
 *Comprehensive Compendium of Detailed Annotation and Categorical-Comparative Mathematical Methods on the Nine Chapters*
by [[吳敬]] (撰)

## About the work

[[吳敬]] Wú Jìng's 1450 mid-Míng practical-mathematical compendium in 12 *juàn*, organized under the nine-chapter framework of the [[KR3f0032]] *Jiǔzhāng suànshù* but with substantial expansion. The work also carries a sub-title *Chéngchú kāifāng qǐlì* 乘除開方起例 (Introductory Examples for Multiplication-Division-and-Root-Extraction) referring to the introductory *juàn*. The work is one of the principal mid-Míng abacus-arithmetic textbooks.

### Abstract

Wú Jìng's *Dàquán* contains some 1300 problems-with-solutions, expanding the *Jiǔzhāng*'s 246 by absorbing substantial Yuán-Míng practical material on commercial calculation, taxation, surveying, currency exchange, and the standard abacus-arithmetic mnemonics. The organizational principle is the *bǐlèi* 比類 method: problems are grouped by analogical pattern within each of the nine traditional chapters, with the simplest cases first and progressively more elaborate cases working out the same pattern. This pedagogical approach was Wú Jìng's principal contribution and would become the standard organizational principle of late-Míng / early-Qīng practical-mathematical writing.

The work also includes a substantial introductory *juàn* (the *Chéngchú kāifāng qǐlì*) covering the basic arithmetic operations on the abacus: the *jiǔjiǔ* multiplication-table, the multiplication-division shortcut mnemonics, the addition-and-subtraction methods, and the square-and-cube-root extraction procedures adapted to the abacus from the Sòng-Yuán counting-rod tradition. This introductory material is among the most systematic pre-Chéng Dàwèi accounts of the abacus operations and was a principal source for [[程大位]] Chéng Dàwèi's [[KR3fc027]] *Suànfǎ tǒngzōng* (1592).

The work's substantive significance beyond pedagogy lies in its preservation of significant amounts of Sòng-Yuán mathematical material. Wú Jìng's source-citations include problem-types from the lost [[楊輝]] *Rìyòng suànfǎ*, the *Yǎnggǔ jí* 養古集 of Lǐ Sī 李思 (otherwise lost), and citations of [[朱世傑]] Zhū Shìjié's *Suànxué qǐméng* by problem-type if not by direct quotation. The Sòng-Yuán *tiānyuányī* algebraic method is, however, absent from Wú Jìng's *Dàquán* — the early-Míng forgetting of Sòng-Yuán algebra is essentially complete in his work, foreshadowing the full late-Míng forgetting documented in [[顧應祥]]'s [[KR3f0043]].

Dating: 1450 per Wú Jìng's own preface (preserved in the work's opening).
