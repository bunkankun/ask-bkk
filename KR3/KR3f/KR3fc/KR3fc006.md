# Jígǔ suànjīng xìcǎo 緝古算經細草
 *Detailed Procedural Exposition of the Continuing-the-Ancients Mathematical Canon*
by [[張敦仁]] (細草)

## About the work

Zhāng Dūnrén 張敦仁's *xìcǎo* (detailed-procedure exposition) of [[王孝通]] Wáng Xiàotōng's Táng *Jígǔ suànjīng* 緝古算經 ([[KR3f0040]]), the principal cubic-equation classic of pre-modern Chinese mathematics. Composed in the closing years of the eighteenth century or early Jiāqìng, parallel to but independent of [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's [[KR3fc005]] *Jígǔ suànjīng kǎozhù*. The catalog records the entry under the title *Jígǔ suànjīng* (the parent-text title); the KX edition reproduces the parent text with Zhāng's interlinear and appended *xìcǎo* working.

### Abstract

Zhāng Dūnrén 張敦仁 (1754–1834; CBDB id 80926) was among the principal mathematical philologists of the Jiā-Dào-period scholarly circle at Yángzhōu, working alongside [[焦循]] Jiāo Xún (1763–1820) and [[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì (1768–1817). His *Qiúyī suànshù* ([[KR3fc041]]) had recovered the procedural specifics of [[秦九韶]] Qín Jiǔsháo's *Dàyǎn qiúyī shù* (the Chinese Remainder Theorem); the present *xìcǎo* extended the same recovery method to Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic algorithms.

Zhāng's exposition differs methodologically from Lǐ Huáng's parallel project in two respects. First, Zhāng's *xìcǎo* is more compact: rather than reconstructing every implicit geometric reduction and supplying full *túshuō* (figure-and-explanation), Zhāng concentrates on the numerical computation, presenting the cube-root extraction step-by-step in counting-rod or column-form notation. Second, where Lǐ Huáng tends to defer to the received text and emend only where the procedure-logic forces it, Zhāng more readily proposes textual corrections based on his own computational reconstruction.

The two Qīng *Jígǔ* expositions are conventionally read together: Lǐ Huáng for the geometric reduction and Zhāng Dūnrén for the computational *xìcǎo*. Together they made Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic methods fully accessible to mid-Qīng working mathematicians for the first time since the late Sòng. The work is also cited under the variant title *Jígǔ suànjīng xìcǎo* 緝古算經細草 in the bibliographies. Liú Héng 劉衡 ([[KR3fc007]]) subsequently added a *bǔzhù* 補注 to the work, extending Zhāng's reconstructions.

The catalog records the work without specified *juàn* count; the KX-imprint preserves it as 1 *juàn*. *NotBefore* is set conservatively at 1800 (allowing for the work to have been completed in the same decade as the *Qiúyī suànshù*); *notAfter* at Zhāng's death-year 1834.
