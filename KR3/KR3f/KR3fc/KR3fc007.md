# Jígǔ suànjīng bǔzhù 緝古算經補注
 *Supplementary Annotation to the Continuing-the-Ancients Mathematical Canon*
by [[劉衡]] (撰)

## About the work

Liú Héng 劉衡's (1776–1841; CBDB id 74864) supplementary annotation to [[王孝通]] Wáng Xiàotōng's *Jígǔ suànjīng* ([[KR3f0040]]), extending and correcting the *xìcǎo* and *kǎozhù* projects of his immediate predecessors [[張敦仁]] Zhāng Dūnrén ([[KR3fc006]]) and [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng ([[KR3fc005]]). The catalog's title — provisionally prefixed with a question-mark (*?輯古算經補注*) — reflects an editorial uncertainty about the exact transmitted title; the work is consistently cited in the late-Qīng mathematical bibliographies as *Jígǔ suànjīng bǔzhù*. The catalog dynasty (清) and lifedates (1776–1841) match CBDB record 74864.

### Abstract

The work falls in the third generation of Qīng *Jígǔ* exposition. The first generation (Lǐ Huáng, Zhāng Dūnrén) had completed the basic recovery of Wáng Xiàotōng's cubic-equation procedures; the second (Lǐ Ruì's circle, mostly preserved only in collected-works fragments) had cross-checked these against the parallel Sòng-Yuán algebraic methods being recovered at the same time. Liú Héng's *bǔzhù* belongs to the synthesizing third generation that put the cubic-equation procedures into dialog with the European-derived *jiègēnfāng* 借根方 method, which Liú Héng had himself expounded in his [[KR3fc066]] *Jiègēnfāngfǎ qiǎnshuō*.

Liú's annotations cover the same twenty problems treated by the predecessors, but with a sharper focus on (a) the technical correctness of the cube-root extraction at each step (where Liú occasionally emends the working as preserved in Lǐ Huáng's exposition); (b) the comparison of Wáng Xiàotōng's geometric reduction to a cubic with the equivalent *jiègēnfāng* algebraic setup; and (c) supplementary problems and variations of his own devising that test the limits of Wáng Xiàotōng's procedural methods.

The work is one piece of a substantial corpus by Liú Héng preserved in the KR3fc series (see his Person note for the full list); together these establish him as one of the principal independent mathematical voices of the mid-Dào-guāng era, working both in the indigenous Sòng-Yuán algebra and the imported European algebra and seeking the systematic translation between the two.

*NotBefore* is set conservatively at 1820 (Liú's earliest documented mathematical productivity); *notAfter* at his death in 1841.
