# Bǔxiū Sòng Zhàntiān shù 補修宋占天術
 *Supplementary Reconstruction of the Sòng Zhàn-tiān Calendar*
by [[李銳]] (述并注)

## About the work

[[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's (1768–1817) supplementary evidential reconstruction in 1 *juàn* of the *Zhàntiān lì* 占天曆, one of the short-lived supplementary calendrical systems of the Northern Sòng. The *Zhàntiān* was designed in Chóngníng 崇寧 4 (1106) by Yáo Shùnfǔ 姚舜輔 and used briefly before being superseded by the *Jìyuán lì* 紀元曆 of the same Yáo Shùnfǔ in Chóngníng 5 (1107).

### Abstract

The *Zhàntiān lì* is one of the more obscure Northern-Sòng calendars: it was promulgated only briefly, and the same designer immediately produced the superseding *Jìyuán lì*. The procedural detail in the *Sòngshǐ Lǜlì zhì* 宋史律曆志 is correspondingly thinner than for the major Sòng calendars. Lǐ Ruì's 1-*juàn* *bǔxiū* 補修 reconstruction is precisely a supplementary reconstruction — recovering the algorithmic content as far as the abbreviated source permits, with cross-reference to the *Fèngyuán* (cf. [[KR3fc054]]) and the *Jìyuán* for parallel evidence.

This is the fifth and final member of Lǐ Ruì's calendrical reconstruction sequence ([[KR3fc051]]–[[KR3fc055]]). The selection of texts is informative: rather than reconstructing the major and well-documented Sòng systems (*Tǒngtiān*, *Jìyuán*, *Shòushí*), Lǐ Ruì chose the less-well-preserved supplementary systems where his philological-mathematical method could make the most distinctive contribution. The pair *Fèngyuán* / *Zhàntiān* fills two specific gaps in the Sòng calendrical record.

Dating: composed during Lǐ Ruì's mature productive period in [[阮元]] Ruǎn Yuán's patronage circle. *notBefore* 1795; *notAfter* 1817.
