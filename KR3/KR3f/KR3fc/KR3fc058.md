# Gōugǔ suànshù xìcǎo 勾股算術細草
 *Detailed Working of Right-Triangle Arithmetic*
by [[李銳]] (撰)

## About the work

[[李銳]] Lǐ Ruì's (1768–1817) procedural treatise in 2 *juàn* on the *gōugǔ* 勾股 (right-triangle) problem domain — the subject matter of the *Jiǔzhāng suànshù* 九章算術 Chapter 9 *Gōugǔ* and the closely-related *Zhōubì suànjīng* 周髀算經 ([[KR3fc001]]'s companion classic). The title's *xìcǎo* 細草 indicates the genre — worked-example exposition with step-by-step computational procedure.

### Abstract

The *gōugǔ* (literally "leg and thigh," i.e. the two legs of a right triangle, with the hypotenuse *xián* 弦 understood) is the indigenous Chinese formulation of the Pythagorean theorem and its associated problem-types: given two of {leg, leg, hypotenuse, perimeter, area, sum of two sides, difference of two sides}, find the remaining elements. The *Jiǔzhāng* Chapter 9 presents the basic procedures and a representative selection of problem-types; the *Zhōubì suànjīng* embeds the right-triangle theorem in an astronomical-cosmological framework as the basis of *gàitiān* 蓋天 ("heaven-as-cover") cosmography.

Lǐ Ruì's 2-*juàn* *xìcǎo* presents a modernised, systematic exposition of the *gōugǔ* problem domain. The first *juàn* presents the basic procedures with worked examples; the second extends to harder problem-types (involving simultaneous use of multiple *gōugǔ* relations, or the use of *gōugǔ* in combination with the area / perimeter / circular-segment apparatus). Where the *Jiǔzhāng* Chapter 9 presents the procedures tersely, Lǐ Ruì's exposition supplies the intermediate steps, the algebraic derivations of the procedures from the basic Pythagorean identity, and the geometric figures clarifying the configurations.

The treatise is the central member of Lǐ Ruì's three-work procedural-exposition sequence ([[KR3fc057]] *Fāngchéng xīnshù cǎo* — linear systems; the present text — right triangles; [[KR3fc059]] *Húshǐ suànshù xìcǎo* — arcs and versed-sines). Together they constitute Lǐ Ruì's pedagogical project of making the recovered *Jiǔzhāng*-tradition procedures accessible to the educated mathematical reader of the Jiā-Dào era.

Dating: composed during Lǐ Ruì's mature productive period. *notBefore* 1795; *notAfter* 1817.
