# Gōugǔ chǐ cèliáng xīnfǎ 勾股尺測量新法
 *New Methods of Surveying with the Right-Triangle Sighting-Rod*
by [[劉衡]] (撰)

## About the work

[[劉衡]] Liú Héng's (1776–1841) treatise in 2 *juàn* on practical surveying using the *gōugǔ chǐ* 勾股尺 — a right-angled sighting-rod calibrated with arithmetic scales for the rapid graphical computation of the *gōugǔ* 勾股 (right-triangle) problem-types that occur in surveying.

### Abstract

The instrument here called *gōugǔ chǐ* 勾股尺 is the right-triangle sighting-rod — an L-shaped graduated instrument used in indigenous Chinese surveying to determine distances and heights by sightings combined with similar-triangle proportions. The fundamental surveying procedure is the *zhòngchā* 重差 ("repeated difference") method codified in the *Hǎidǎo suànjīng* 海島算經 of [[劉徽]] Liú Huī ([[KR3fc003]]'s parent text): two sightings at known horizontal separation give two similar right triangles, from which the unknown height or distance can be computed.

[[劉衡]] Liú Héng's 2-*juàn* treatise presents systematic procedures for surveying using a properly-graduated *gōugǔ chǐ*, with worked examples covering the standard problem-types: distance to an inaccessible object, height of a remote feature, depth of a valley, breadth of a river. The title's *xīnfǎ* 新法 ("new methods") indicates that the treatise presents Liú Héng's own systematic working — likely an improvement on the indigenous *zhòngchā* procedures by incorporating scales calibrated for direct readings rather than computational reductions.

The work belongs to the active mid-Qīng tradition of practical surveying mathematics — alongside [[李潢]] Lǐ Huáng's [[KR3fc003]] *Hǎidǎo suànjīng xìcǎo túshuō* and the various Méi-school treatments. It is closely paired with the companion treatise [[KR3fc063]] (sundial-laying with the *chǐsuàn*) as Liú Héng's two principal works on practical instrument-mathematics.

Dating: Liú Héng lived 1776–1841. *notBefore* 1800; *notAfter* 1841.
