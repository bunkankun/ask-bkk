# KR3fb 曆法 — Lìfǎ (Calendrical Method)

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3fb 曆法 (*Lìfǎ*) is the calendrical-method sub-division of KR3f 天文算法類 (*Tiānwén suànfǎ lèi*), parallel to the asterism-and-observational sub-division [[KR3fa]] 天文. Wilkinson is emphatic that *lì* 曆 means not "calendar" in the modern sense but "mathematical astronomy" — the computational apparatus whose practical output was the imperial almanac and whose periodic reform (*gǎilì* 改曆) was a recurring task of the Imperial Astronomical Bureau (*Qīntiānjiān* 欽天監) from the Hàn down to 1911 (Wilkinson §39.6, Box 219). The thirty-and-more *gǎilì* episodes of pre-modern Chinese history each generated a body of procedural manuals, polemical defences, evidentiary reconstructions and hemerological commentaries; KR3fb is the Kyoto-University supplementary collection of these texts (refid prefixes `KX03-07-035` through `KX03-07-056`, plus two `KXDLJ003` items), drawn from the *Zhōngguó kēxué jìshù diǎnjí tōnghuì* 中國科學技術典籍通匯 and cognate reprint series, gathering the procedural-astronomical literature that the *Sìkù* editors excluded or that survived only in manuscript or rare-print form.

The chronological span runs from the Hóng-wǔ-period (1383) Chinese translation of the Khwarezmian *Huíhuí lì* 回回曆 manual — preserved through Bèi Lín's 貝琳 1477 Nánjīng *Qīntiānjiān* recension [[KR3fb003]] and its companion worked-examples volume [[KR3fb004]] — through the dense late-Míng calendar crisis around the failing *Dàtǒng lì* 大統曆, the Jesuit-led *Chóngzhēn lìshū* 崇禎曆書 reform of 1629–35 and its 1645 promulgation as the Qīng *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 (Wilkinson §39.6.2), the dual Qīng tradition of (a) operational manuals for the Schall–Verbiest *Shíxiàn* system and (b) Chinese-scholarly defences of pre-Jesuit native methods, and culminating in the great 19th-century *kǎozhèng* 考證 reconstructions of the lost ancient astronomical systems — the Qín *Zhuānxū lì* 顓頊曆, the Hàn *Tàichū* 太初 / *Sāntǒng* 三統, the Yuán *Shòushí lì* 授時曆 — and Wāng Yuēzhēn's 汪曰楨 standard *shuòrùn* 朔閏 chronological apparatus for the entire pre-modern span. Unlike [[KR3fa]] (which contains a single Hàn root-text and otherwise spans Hàn-to-Qīng broadly), KR3fb is almost entirely a MíngQīng corpus: it documents the long internal Chinese struggle to maintain, reform, and finally replace the *Dàtǒng* / *Shòushí* mathematical-astronomical system in the face of Khwarezmian and then European competing systems (Wilkinson §39.6.2, §39.17.3.2).

## Important texts and text clusters

The 24 cataloged works fall into seven readily recognisable clusters.

**Islamic-calendar (*Huíhuí lì*) cluster.** Two works preserve the principal MíngQīng vehicle by which Khwarezmian Islamic astronomy entered Chinese practice, in parallel to the more theoretically ambitious *Qīzhèng tuībù* 七政推步 [[KR3f0006]] in the SKQS proper.
- *Huíhuí lìfǎ* 回回曆法 [[KR3fb003]] — Bèi Lín's 貝琳 1477 collated and printed recension of the Hóngwǔ 16 (1383) court translation of the Islamic ephemeris-and-procedural manual by Mǎshā Yīhēi 馬沙亦黑, Wú Bózōng 吳伯宗 and Lǐ Chōng 李翀; the Bureau's parallel "West-of-the-Western-Borders calendar" (*Xīyù lì* 西域曆) used alongside the *Dàtǒng* for eclipse cross-checking.
- *Huíhuí lìfǎ shìlì* 回回曆法釋例 [[KR3fb004]] — Bèi Lín's companion worked-examples volume.

**Late-Míng *Dàtǒng* operational and crisis cluster.** Six works document the operating state and progressive failure of the official *Dàtǒng lì* 大統曆 (the Míng renaming of Guō Shǒujìng's 郭守敬 Yuán *Shòushí*) in the century before the Jesuit reform.
- *Dàtǒng lì zhù* 大統歷注 [[KR3fb006]] — anonymous twelve-*juàn* practical hemerological commentary (*lìzhù*) on the official almanac, in the *xuǎnzé* 選擇 day-choosing genre.
- *Xīnbiān zūnyī Sītiāntái jīngwěi lìshū* 新編遵依司天臺經緯歷書 [[KR3fb007]] — Lù Wèi's 陸位 Wànlì private recension of an Imperial Astronomical Bureau ephemeris of the Seven Governors.
- *Shéndào dàbiān lìzōng tōngyì* 神道大編曆宗通議 [[KR3fb005]] — Zhōu Shùxué's 周述學 mid-Míng commoner-scholar attempt (1545–1582) to recover the spherical-trigonometric core of the lost Yuán *Shòushí* method, fused with *Huíhuí* longitude-latitude procedures.
- *Zhézhōng lìfǎ* 折衷曆法 [[KR3fb009]] — Zhū Zhòngfú's 朱仲福 sixteen-*juàn* "eclectic" reform attempt, framed by a sustained defence of private commoner mathematical-astronomical learning.
- *Wànnián lì bèikǎo* 萬年曆備考 [[KR3fb010]] — Zhū Zàiyù's 朱載堉 (1536–1611) supplementary evidentiary apparatus to his *Shèngshòu wànnián lì* 聖壽萬年曆 [[KR3f0007]] reform proposal of 1595; a comparative survey of fifty principal historical calendar systems.
- *Wùshēn lìchūn kǎozhèng* 戊申立春考證 [[KR3fb012]] — Xíng Yúnlù's 邢雲路 focused 1608 empirical demonstration that the *Dàtǒng*'s predicted *lìchūn* node was hours away from the gnomon-shadow observation; the empirical centrepiece of the larger critique in his *Gǔjīn lǜlì kǎo* [[KR3f0008]].
- *Lì zhì* 歷誌 [[KR3fb008]] — anonymous fifteen-*juàn* late-Míng textbook of plane and spherical trigonometry adapted to astronomical computation, asserting that "trigonometry is the ridgepole of calendrical computation."

**Conservative anti-Jesuit (*Dōngjú*) cluster.** Two works mount the principal Chinese-scholarly defence of native calendrical methods against the Jesuit reform.
- *Lì cè* 曆測 [[KR3fb011]] — Wèi Wénkuí's 魏文魁 surviving five-*juàn* theoretical writing (1628–1635), supplying the intellectual basis for his *Dōngjú* 東局 ("Eastern Bureau") rival reform programme established at court against Xú Guāngqǐ's 徐光啟 and Lǐ Tiānjīng's 李天經 *Xījú* 西局.
- *Dìnglì yùhéng* 定歷玉衡 [[KR3fb021]] — Zhāng Yōngjìng's 張雍敬 late-Kāng-xī twenty-one-*juàn* programmatic defence of native methods against the *Shíxiàn lì*, surveying fifty-six historical schools and identifying forty-four specific errors as the corrective "jade balance" of the title.

**Jesuit-era *Shíxiàn lì* operational and explanatory cluster.** Seven works document the imperial-Qīng Tychonic-Keplerian apparatus introduced by Adam Schall von Bell and Ferdinand Verbiest as the *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 of 1645 (Wilkinson §39.6.2, §39.6.3) — the calendar that ran the Qīng empire from 1645 to 1911.
- *Mínlì pùzhù jiěhuò* 民歷鋪註解惑 [[KR3fb013]] — [[湯若望]] Schall's three-*juàn* defensive apologia (1645–1666) for the editorial procedure he applied to the *pùzhù* 鋪註 hemerological glosses of the reformed civil almanac, cast as a dialogue answering critics among the Yíxīng 宜興 faction that would coalesce in the Yáng Guāngxiān 楊光先 anti-Western prosecution of 1664–65 (see [[KR3fa028]] *Bùdé yǐ*).
- *Jiāoshí lìshū* 交食曆書 [[KR3fb016]] — [[南懷仁]] Verbiest's 1683 single-*juàn* technical monograph on the *huángdào jiǔshí dù xiàn* 黃道九十度限 (the ninety-degree point of the ecliptic above the horizon, essential for solar-eclipse prediction), simplified from his *Jiāoshí lìzhǐ* 交食曆指 for working astronomers at the Bureau's Shèngjīng (Mukden) sub-station.
- *Lìxiàng běnyào* 曆象本要 [[KR3fb017]] — Yáng Wényán's 楊文言 two-*juàn* Qīng primer of post-*Chóngzhēn* / *Shíxiàn* Western-style astronomy, structured as a contrastive glossary of "new calendrical terms" (*xīn lìfǎ míng* 新曆法名).
- *Qīzhèng tái lì* 七政臺曆 [[KR3fb018]] — anonymous Qīng *Qīntiānjiān* working planetary almanac of the Seven Governors (*qīzhèng* 七政: sun, moon, five visible planets) in the post-*Shíxiàn* tradition.
- *Suàn qīzhèng jiāoshí língfàn fǎ* 算七政交食凌犯法 [[KR3fb019]] — anonymous Qīng practitioner's recipe-book for syzygies and planetary conjunctions/transits using the *Shíxiàn*-era tabular apparatus.
- *DàQīng Shíxiànshū jiānshì* 大清時憲書箋釋 [[KR3fb022]] — Miù Zhījìn's 繆之晉 three-*juàn* annotated commentary positioning itself between the official almanac and the popular *tōngshū* 通書 hemerological handbooks.
- *Xiāoxiāozǐ lì jìng* 囂囂子曆鏡 [[KR3fb020]] — Hú Xícān's 胡襲參 three-*juàn* diachronic "mirror" of historical calendrical systems for the use of contemporary post-Jesuit-era practitioners.

**Ming-loyalist *Shòushí* historical-philological cluster.** Two paired works recover the rationale of the Yuán system displaced by the Jesuit reform.
- *Shòushí lì gù* 授時曆故 [[KR3fb014]] — [[黃宗羲]] Huáng Zōngxī's five-*juàn* exegesis of the technical procedures of the Yuán *Shòushí lì* (1280) of Wáng Xún 王恂 and [[郭守敬]] Guō Shǒujìng — the level/standing/fixed-difference (*pínglìdìng sānchā* 平立定三差) interpolation and the *húshǐ gēyuán* 弧矢割圓 spherical computations — recovered both for intrinsic value and as a Ming-loyalist scholarly defence against the Jesuit narrative of Chinese decline.
- *Lìxué jiǎrú* 曆學假如 [[KR3fb015]] — Huáng's three-*juàn* pedagogical companion of model problems and worked examples, intended to break the *Shòushí* out of its closed-craft transmission and place it within the reach of literate-elite study.

**Qīng *kǎozhèng* reconstructive cluster.** Four 19th-century evidential-philological reconstructions of lost or imperfectly transmitted ancient systems, exemplifying the high *kǎozhèng* programme applied to mathematical astronomy.
- *Zhuānxū lì kǎo* 顓頊曆考 [[KR3fb001]] — [[鄒漢勛]] Zōu Hànxūn's two-*juàn* reconstruction (1840–1853) of the Qín / early-Western-Hàn *Zhuānxū lì* 顓頊曆 (in use c. 246–104 BCE), recovered by working backwards from dated histories using modern computation; consciously modelled on Dù Yù's 杜預 *Chūnqiū chánglì* and intended as the first instalment of a planned continuation through to the Míng *Dàtǒng*.
- *Hàn Tàichū lì kǎo* 漢太初曆考 [[KR3fb002]] — Chéng Róngjìng's 成蓉鏡 two-*juàn* evidentiary study (1840s) arguing that the *Lìshù jiǎzǐ piān* 厤術甲子篇 of *Shǐjì* "Lìshū" is a tabular embodiment of the *Tàichū lì* 太初曆 of 104 BCE rather than the lost *Yīn lì* 殷曆, with corrupt readings to be emended against Liú Xīn's 劉歆 *Sāntǒng lì* 三統曆.
- *Sāntǒngshù xiángshuō* 三統術詳說 [[KR3fb024]] — Chén Lǐ's 陳澧 five-*juàn* late-Qīng exegesis of Liú Xīn's *Sāntǒng* system as transmitted in the *Hàn shū "Lǜlì zhì"* 律曆志, in dialogue with Liú Bān's 劉攽 Sòng commentary; the canonical reference work for the system that Cullen (2017) takes as one of the three foundation-systems of Chinese mathematical astronomy.
- *Lìdài chángshù jíyào* 歷代長術輯要 [[KR3fb023]] — Wāng Yuēzhēn's 汪曰楨 fourteen-*juàn* standard 19th-century chronological apparatus: a year-by-year *shuòrùn* 朔閏 reference from the 841 BCE *Gònghé* 共和 regency to the end of the Míng, computed using the calendrical system actually in use in each period; the abridged form of his lost fifty-three-*juàn* *Èrshísì shǐ yuèrì kǎo* 二十四史月日考.

## Important persons

- [[貝琳]] (Bèi Lín, fl. 1477) — Nánjīng *Qīntiānjiān* astronomer whose collated 1477 printed recension of the Hóngwǔ *Huíhuí lì* translation [[KR3fb003]] [[KR3fb004]] is the principal Chinese vehicle of Khwarezmian-tradition Islamic astronomy in the Míng.
- [[周述學]] (Zhōu Shùxué, fl. 1545–1582) — Mid-Míng independent commoner-scholar whose *Lìzōng tōngyì* [[KR3fb005]] is the most substantial Míng attempt to recover the Yuán *Shòushí* spherical-trigonometric techniques outside the imperial Bureau.
- [[朱載堉]] (Zhū Zàiyù, 1536–1611) — Míng prince-scholar; his late corpus on mathematical harmonics and calendrics, of which the *Wànnián lì bèikǎo* [[KR3fb010]] is the evidentiary apparatus, frames the most ambitious commoner-elite reform proposal of the Wànlì period.
- [[邢雲路]] (Xíng Yúnlù, *jìnshì* 1580) — Late-Wàn-lì commoner-astronomer whose 1608 empirical demonstration [[KR3fb012]] of *Dàtǒng* failure is the immediate trigger for the late-Míng reform campaign that culminated in the Jesuit-led *Chóngzhēn lìshū*.
- [[魏文魁]] (Wèi Wénkuí, fl. 1628–1635) — Mánchéng commoner-astronomer, leader of the conservative *Dōngjú* 東局 ("Eastern Bureau") rival programme to the Jesuit reform; his *Lì cè* [[KR3fb011]] is the principal surviving theoretical statement of the anti-reform position in this division.
- [[湯若望]] (Adam Schall von Bell, 1591/2–1666) — German Jesuit, Director of the Imperial Astronomical Bureau 1645–1664 (Wilkinson §39.6.2); architect of the *Shíxiàn lì* 時憲曆 itself, repeatedly invoked across the Qīng cluster as the originator of the imperial-Chinese Jesuit astronomy. His *Mínlì pùzhù jiěhuò* [[KR3fb013]] is his own defence of his hemerological editorial procedure.
- [[南懷仁]] (Ferdinand Verbiest, 1623–1688) — Flemish Jesuit, Schall's successor as Bureau director (1674–1688); his *Jiāoshí lìshū* [[KR3fb016]] supplies one of the few focused operational eclipse-computation monographs of the post-Schall imperial corpus.
- [[黃宗羲]] (Huáng Zōngxī, 1610–1695) — The great Míng-loyalist scholar-philosopher; the paired *Shòushí lì gù* [[KR3fb014]] and *Lìxué jiǎrú* [[KR3fb015]] are the most influential early-Qīng historical-philological recovery of the Yuán *Shòushí* system displaced by the *Shíxiàn* reform.
- [[張雍敬]] (Zhāng Yōngjìng, fl. 1690–1709) — Late-Kāng-xī Xīnchéng scholar whose *Dìnglì yùhéng* [[KR3fb021]] is the most exhaustive Chinese-scholarly defence of native calendrical methods against the Jesuit *Shíxiàn lì*.
- [[鄒漢勛]] (Zōu Hànxūn, 1805–1853) — Xīnhuà *kǎozhèng* scholar whose *Zhuānxū lì kǎo* [[KR3fb001]] is the foundational 19th-century reconstruction of the lost Qín / early-Hàn calendar; killed on the Tàipíng front before his planned diachronic continuation could be completed.
- [[陳澧]] (Chén Lǐ, 1810–1882) — Pānyú Cantonese evidential scholar of the *Xuéhǎitáng* 學海堂; his *Sāntǒngshù xiángshuō* [[KR3fb024]] is the standard late-Qīng exegesis of the Hàn *Sāntǒng* system.
- [[汪曰楨]] (Wāng Yuēzhēn, 1813–1881) — Wūchéng historian-chronologist (Wilkinson at 9643 notes his sobriquet *Gāngmù* 剛木); his *Lìdài chángshù jíyào* [[KR3fb023]] is the standard nineteenth-century *shuòrùn* reference for the entire pre-modern span.
- [[繆之晉]] (Miù Zhījìn, fl. Qīng) — Compiler of the *DàQīng Shíxiànshū jiānshì* [[KR3fb022]], the principal mid-Qīng commentarial bridge between the official *Shíxiàn* almanac and popular *tōngshū* hemerological practice.

## Topics

- **Reform of the astronomical system (*gǎilì* 改曆).** Wilkinson §39.6 / Box 219 emphasises that *gǎilì* refers not to "calendar reform" in the modern sense but to reform of the underlying mathematical-astronomical system. KR3fb is the corpus through which the late-imperial *gǎilì* cycle is most directly documented: the late-Míng failure of the *Dàtǒng* ([[KR3fb010]], [[KR3fb012]]), the Jesuit reform that produced the *Shíxiàn* ([[KR3fb013]], [[KR3fb016]], [[KR3fb017]]), the conservative *Dōngjú* response ([[KR3fb011]], [[KR3fb021]]), and the *kǎozhèng* recovery of earlier displaced systems ([[KR3fb001]], [[KR3fb002]], [[KR3fb014]], [[KR3fb024]]).
- **The *Shòushí* / *Dàtǒng* mathematical core.** The level-standing-fixed-difference interpolation and spherical *húshǐ gēyuán* arc-and-sagitta computations of Wáng Xún and [[郭守敬]] Guō Shǒujìng (Sivin 2009, cited in Wilkinson §39.17.2.1) run as a subterranean thread through the division: as living-but-decaying Bureau practice in the late Míng ([[KR3fb006]], [[KR3fb007]]), as the object of commoner-scholarly recovery efforts ([[KR3fb005]], [[KR3fb009]]), as the displaced ancestor of the *Shíxiàn* whose technical rationale Huáng Zōngxī sought to preserve ([[KR3fb014]], [[KR3fb015]]), and as the implicit comparand against which conservative anti-Jesuit polemic measured the reformed system ([[KR3fb011]], [[KR3fb021]]).
- **The three-cornered confrontation: native, *Huíhuí*, and Western methods.** The division provides documentary evidence for all three poles of the late-Míng / Qīng astronomical pluralism: the *Huíhuí lì* of [[KR3fb003]] / [[KR3fb004]] inherited from the Yuán Muslim Bureau (Wilkinson §39.6.2); the Jesuit *Shíxiàn* of [[KR3fb013]] / [[KR3fb016]] / [[KR3fb022]]; and the native programme defended in [[KR3fb011]] / [[KR3fb021]] and recovered in [[KR3fb014]] / [[KR3fb015]].
- ***Pùzhù* 鋪註 and hemerology.** The dense day-by-day glosses on the official almanac — auspicious / inauspicious activities, *jiànchú* 建除, *qíshén fāngwèi* 奇神方位 (Wilkinson §39.16) — are themselves a contested object: Schall's apologia for his redaction of them [[KR3fb013]], the anonymous Míng *Dàtǒng lì zhù* [[KR3fb006]] continuing the inherited *xuǎnzé* tradition, and Miù Zhījìn's [[KR3fb022]] positioning between official almanac and popular *tōngshū*, all bear on the same question.
- **The Qīng *kǎozhèng* reconstruction of ancient mathematical-astronomical systems.** The 19th-century texts in the division ([[KR3fb001]], [[KR3fb002]], [[KR3fb023]], [[KR3fb024]]) embody a coherent evidential-philological programme: recover the procedures of the *Zhuānxū*, *Tàichū*, *Sāntǒng* and *Shòushí* systems from the histories' fragmentary procedural specifications and the dated entries of the standard histories, computing back from modern *mìshù* 密術 precise methods to identify both the constants in use and the textual corruptions in transmission. The systems concerned are precisely those that Cullen (2017) translates and Sivin (2009) studies as the foundation-corpus of Chinese mathematical astronomy.
- **Commoner-elite versus imperial-Bureau learning.** A striking proportion of the division's authors are scholars writing outside the *Qīntiānjiān*: Zhōu Shùxué [[KR3fb005]], Zhū Zhòngfú [[KR3fb009]], Xíng Yúnlù [[KR3fb012]], Wèi Wénkuí [[KR3fb011]], Huáng Zōngxī [[KR3fb014]] / [[KR3fb015]], Zhāng Yōngjìng [[KR3fb021]], Zōu Hànxūn [[KR3fb001]], Chéng Róngjìng [[KR3fb002]], Chén Lǐ [[KR3fb024]] and Wāng Yuēzhēn [[KR3fb023]] are all commoner or local-elite scholars; their writings collectively form the principal extra-Bureau body of mathematical-astronomical scholarship, in tension with the imperial monopoly that Wilkinson §39.6.3 documents.
- **Chronological apparatus and the use of past calendars.** The practical task of converting between the calendrical systems actually in use across two millennia — essential for reading the dated material in the standard histories — culminates in Wāng Yuēzhēn's [[KR3fb023]] standard *shuòrùn* reference, the canonical 19th-century answer to a problem implicit in the entire pre-modern Chinese historiographical apparatus.
