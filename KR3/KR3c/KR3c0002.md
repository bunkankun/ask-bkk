# Guǎnzǐ bǔ zhù 管子補注
*Supplementary Commentary on the* Guǎnzǐ
by [[劉績]] (Liú Jì, b. 1468, 明)

## About the work

A 24-juan Mid-Míng supplementary commentary on the *Guǎnzǐ* ([[KR3c0001]]), composed by Liú Jì in 1490. The work supplements Fáng Xuánlíng's Táng-period commentary with additional glossing, textual collation, and explanatory remarks. Composition: precisely datable to 1490. The frontmatter brackets to 1490–1490.

### Abstract

The *Guǎnzǐ bǔ zhù* is the principal Mid-Míng *Guǎnzǐ* commentary, complementing Fáng Xuánlíng's Táng layer. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Fǎjiā lèi*.
