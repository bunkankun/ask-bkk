# Zhé yù guī jiàn 折獄龜鑑
*Tortoise-Mirror for Adjudicating Cases*
by [[鄭克]] (Zhèng Kè, 12th c., 宋)

## About the work

An 8-juan Southern Sòng judicial-decision casebook, expanding Hé Níng's *Yí yù jí* ([[KR3c0006]]) and providing the standard pre-Yuán *yí yù*-genre reference. Composition: bracketed by Zhèng's mid-12th-century working life. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1131–1180.

### Abstract

The *Zhé yù guī jiàn* is one of the principal Sòng-period legal-historical reference works. The bibliographic record: *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Fǎjiā lèi*.
