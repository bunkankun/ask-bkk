# Xiǎozì lù 小字錄
*Register of Childhood Names*

by [[陳思]] (Chén Sī, late Southern Sòng, 撰).

## About the work

A late-Southern-Sòng update of Lù Guīméng's Táng-period *Xiǎomíng lù* ([[KR3k0009]]) covering the *xiǎozì* 小字 (childhood / familiar names) of historical figures of the post-Hàn period — particularly the Wèi, Jìn, Northern and Southern Dynasties, Suí, Táng, and Sòng. Compiled by Chén Sī 陳思 (*fl.* 1259) of the late Southern Sòng. Each entry consists of the *xiǎozì* as headword, followed by a short biographical note with the figure's adult name, *zì*, dynasty, and source citation — the latter typically a *běnjì* (basic annal) of the relevant dynastic history. The work organizes entries by category — *Lìdài dìwáng* (sovereigns through the dynasties), *Hòufēi* (consorts), then by general historical period. The work was a routine reference for late-Sòng and Yuán-Míng *zhèngshǐ* readers, since *xiǎozì* are systematically present in the *běnjì* but scattered across many *juan*.

### Tiyao (concise)

The *Xiǎozì lù* in 1 *juan* by Chén Sī of the Sòng. Sī flourished in *Bǎoyòu* 1259 by his own colophon evidence; native place not clearly recorded but he was the chief Sòng cataloguer of calligraphic *shūyuàn* (calligraphy collections) and the author of *Shū yuàn jīnghuá* 書苑菁華 and *Bǎozhāng dàifǎng lù* 寳章待訪錄. The book is a continuation and supplement of Lù Guīméng's *Xiǎomíng lù* of the Táng, extending the *xiǎozì* records through the Wèi, Jìn, North-South Dynasties, Suí, Táng, and Sòng. Each entry gives the *xiǎozì*, the figure's adult name and *zì*, the dynasty, and a brief notice with source.

### Abstract

The *Xiǎozì lù* is the standard pre-modern reference catalog of historical *xiǎozì* (childhood / familiar names) from the Wèi (3rd c.) through the Southern Sòng. The compiler Chén Sī 陳思 (fl. ca. 1259) was a Línān 臨安 *shūpù* (book-shop) proprietor and author of several important Southern-Sòng calligraphy-history works (the *Shū yuàn jīnghuá* 書苑菁華, *Bǎozhāng dàifǎng lù* 寳章待訪錄, *Hàn shǐpǔ* 翰史譜); his Línān *shūpù* was one of the principal sources of Southern-Sòng surviving *shūfǎ* (calligraphy) prints. He is unrelated to the early-Tang Buddhist *zhìguǎn* exegete Chén Sī of the same name.

The work is a methodical and useful supplement to Lù Guīméng's earlier *Xiǎomíng lù* ([[KR3k0009]]), covering the post-Táng periods that Lù's work did not reach. Each entry's brief biographical sketch, with source citation back to the *zhèngshǐ běnjì*, makes the work a quick reference for cross-canonical *xiǎozì* identification. The work is one of the small-scale specialized *lèishū* preserved among the larger compendia in the *Sìkù*'s *Lèishū lèi*. The standard modern edition is the Zhōnghuá shūjú photo-reprint of the *Sìkù* recension.
