# Qúnshū zhìyào 群書治要
*Essentials of Governance Drawn from the Books*

by [[魏徵]] (Wèi Zhēng, 580–643, 等奉敕撰); with Yú Shìnán 虞世南, Chǔ Liàng 褚亮, Xiāo Déyán 蕭德言 (et al.)

## About the work

A 50-*juan* Táng-imperial statecraft anthology compiled by [[魏徵]] and colleagues by imperial command of Táng Tàizōng 唐太宗, completed in Zhēnguān 5 (貞觀五年, 631). The work extracts politically relevant passages from the classics, histories, and philosophical masters to provide a portable curriculum in good governance. See the primary catalog entry at [[KR3j0082]] for full description.

**This Kanripo record (KR3k0078) represents a distinct textual witness** from the SBCK edition cataloged under KR3j0082. The KR3k0078 file preserves a Japanese printed edition from the *Tenmei* 天明 era (1781–1789), as evidenced by its multiple Japanese prefaces and editorial postfaces:

1. **考例** (Kōrei / Examination of Textual Rules): a collation note by Hosoi Tokutami 細井德民 dated *Tenmei* 5 (天明五年, 1785), describing the *Owari-han* 尾張藩 collation project.
2. **Wèi Zhēng's original preface** (魏徵序): the autograph preface explaining the rationale for compilation.
3. **Two Japanese prefaces** (*Inaba nobunori* 林信敬, dated *Tenmei* 7 / 丁未 April 1787): summarizing the transmission history and endorsing the *Owari* project.

The *Owari-han* 尾張藩 (one of the *go-sanke* 御三家 domains, descended from Tokugawa Ieyasu's ninth son) compiled this edition from multiple Japanese manuscript copies, supplementing against the original sources where possible, and had the text carved in 1786–87. The *Tenmei* reprint is the conduit through which the *Qúnshū zhìyào* re-entered Chinese circulation in the 19th century.

### Prefaces

See content summary above. The collation note (考例) records the chain of transmission from the *Kanazawa Bunko* 金澤文庫 (Hōjō Sanetoki 北條實時, late 13th c.) through Tokugawa Ieyasu's acquisition, to the *Kō-katsu-ji-ban* movable-type imperial reprint, and finally to the *Owari Meirin-dō* 明倫堂 academy edition.
