# Biānzhū 編珠
*Strung Pearls*

by [[杜公瞻]] (Dù Gōngzhān, Suí, 奉敕撰)

## About the work

A 4-*juan* Suí-period classified literary-allusion treasury compiled by [[杜公瞻]] by imperial command of Suí Yángdì 隋煬帝, presented in the first month of Dàyè 7 (大業七年正月, 611). For the full description, see the primary catalog entry [[KR3k0002]].

**This Kanripo record (KR3k0079) represents a distinct textual witness** from the WYG edition cataloged at [[KR3k0002]], which preserves only the 2-*juan* core in the recension transmitted via Gāo Shìqí 高士奇 (Kāngxī era). The KR3k0079 file opens directly with [[杜公瞻]]'s original preface — signed "著作佐郎兼散騎侍郎臣杜公膽謹序" (note: the character 膽 for 瞻 is a textual variant) — and proceeds through the 4-*juan* text in what appears to be a fuller or differently-based transmission. The preface explicitly states: "帝在江都日，好為雜詠及新體詩…爰命微臣編錄，得窺書囿，故目之曰『編珠』。其朱書者故實，墨書者正義。時大業七年正月，奉敕撰進，勒成四卷" (the emperor, residing at Jiāngdū, liked to compose *záyǒng* and new-style poetry; he commanded his minister to compile this text to assist the search; red entries are *gùshí* [allusions], black are *zhèngyì* [canonical citation]; presented to court in the first month of Dàyè 7, in 4 *juan*).

### Prefaces

**Dù Gōngzhān's preface** (dated Dàyè 7 / 611): States that the emperor at Jiāngdū frequently composed verse and wanted a handy compendium of allusive parallels. The 4-*juan* work covers 14 subject categories in the format: allusion (red, *gùshí* 故實) / source (black, *zhèngyì* 正義). The categories include Heaven, Earth, Mountains and Rivers, Dwellings, Ceremonial Guards, Music, Garments and Adornments, Pearls and Treasures, Silks, Wine and Banquets, Grain, Vegetables, Fruits, Carriages and Oars.

### Abstract

See [[KR3k0002]] for full description of the *Biānzhū* and its complex transmission history (including the *Sìkù* editors' reservations about authenticity). The present KR3k0079 file appears to represent either:
1. The full 4-*juan* original (rather than the 2-*juan* partial recension transmitted through Gāo Shìqí's Kāngxī-era recovery), or
2. A differently constituted edition based on a Japanese manuscript transmission parallel to the KR3j0082 *Qúnshū zhìyào* pattern.

The variant character 膽 for 瞻 in the preface signature is a scribal variant (both are *shěng xíng* of 瞻 in different manuscript traditions) and does not affect the attribution.
