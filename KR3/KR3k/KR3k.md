# Lèishū lèi 類書類

## Scope and scholarly tradition

KR3k collects the *lèishū* 類書 — literally "books arranged by category" — as defined and bounded by the *Sìkù quánshū* 四庫全書 editors of the 1780s. *Lèishū* are encyclopedic anthologies of excerpts from previous works, arranged under topical, prosopographical, or rhyme-based headings to be consulted by an emperor, an official, an examination candidate, or a literary writer searching for a precedent or a turn of phrase. The *Sìkù* placed them in the third (子, *Masters*) branch following the choice already made by the *Jiù Tángzhì* 舊唐志 (945), and split off the rhyme-organised lexical *lèishū* into the 小學 (*xiǎoxué*) category of the Classics branch — a division Wilkinson, *Chinese History: A New Manual* §72.1.1 follows and which is reflected here: KR3k contains the topical and prosopographical *lèishū* plus the major Qīng rhyme-treasuries that were nevertheless catalogued in 子部 (*Pèiwén yùnfǔ*, *Yùnfǔ qúnyù*).

The tradition runs from the lost early-3rd-century *Huánglǎn* 皇覽 commissioned for Wèi Wéndì 魏文帝 — conventionally the genre's founder — through to the great Kāngxī–Yōngzhèng imperial compendia of the early 18th century. Its chronological centre of gravity is the Sòng (works from 977 to 1296 dominate the catalog by both bulk and influence), but the surviving Suí–Táng nucleus ([[KR3k0002]], 0003, 0004, 0006) preserves citations from a thousand-plus pre-7th-century works, of which most no longer exist independently — so KR3k is also the largest single conduit by which the fragmentary pre-Táng book-world reaches the modern reader. Wilkinson §72.1.1 estimates that of the 1,400 books quoted in the *Yìwén lèijù* 藝文類聚 alone, roughly 90 % are otherwise lost. KR3k is therefore not just a genre-bin: it is, with KR1 (Classics) and KR2 (Histories), one of the principal evidence-bases for Chinese textual archaeology.

The genre splits into several distinct sub-traditions. Wilkinson §72.1.2 organises the canonical general *lèishū* by dynasty (Suí–Táng, Sòng–Yuán, Míng, Qīng); cross-cutting that chronological grid are the imperial-court mega-compendia ("the four great books of the Sòng" 宋四大書, of which three survive in KR3k); the literary-composition *lèishū* (allusion- and parallel-prose primers); the surname-and-pedigree (*xìngshì*) lineage; the *xiǎomíng* / *biéhào* prosopographical line founded by Liáng Yuándì 梁元帝; the *méngqiú* 蒙求 examination-and-children's primer line; the Southern-Sòng *cèyì* / *kǎojù* statecraft-crammer cluster (Yǒngjiā / Wùxué); the institutional-origins (*qǐyuán*) tradition; and the late-Míng / early-Qīng evidential and material-culture compendia. The *Sìkù* preface to the section warns explicitly that *lèishū* must be used with care because they typically quote from earlier *lèishū* rather than from the originals — a critique made famously caustic in Gù Yánwǔ's 顧炎武 dictum *gāotán ér bù gēn* 高談而不根 ("high talk without roots"; Wilkinson §72.1.1). The tension between *lèishū* as indispensable archive and *lèishū* as transcriptional sediment is the interpretive frame for the whole division.

## Important texts and text clusters

### Founders of the genre (6th–7th c.)

- *Gǔjīn tóngxìngmíng lù* 古今同姓名錄 [[KR3k0001]] — Liáng Yuándì 梁元帝's homonymic-prosopography compendium, the *Sìkù* editors' candidate for the oldest extant categorised *lèishū*; founder of a sub-genre that runs through to [[KR3k0035]], 0042, 0047, 0064 in this catalog.
- *Biānzhū* 編珠 [[KR3k0002]] — Suí Yáng-dì-period allusion-pair handbook attributed to Dù Gōngzhān 杜公瞻; the earliest surviving *lèishū* explicitly designed for the composition of *fù* 賦.
- *Běitáng shūchāo* 北堂書鈔 [[KR3k0004]] — Yú Shìnán's 虞世南 early-Táng compilation, conventionally the **earliest extant general *lèishū*** (Wilkinson §72.1.2.1); preserves a great many pre-Suí citations.

### The Táng "four great *lèishū*" 唐代四大類書

The four canonical Táng general *lèishū* listed together in Wilkinson §72.1.2.1 are all present in KR3k:

- *Yìwén lèijù* 藝文類聚 [[KR3k0003]] — Ōuyáng Xún 歐陽詢, 624; arranged in 46 *bù* / 727 *mù*; landmark in interleaving facts (*shì*) with literary excerpts (*wén*).
- *Běitáng shūchāo* [[KR3k0004]] — see above.
- *Chūxué jì* 初學記 [[KR3k0006]] — Xú Jiān 徐堅 et al., compiled for the Tang Xuánzōng 唐玄宗's princes; introduces the tripartite *xùshì* / *shìduì* / *shīwén* internal layout.
- *Bái Kǒng liùtiē* 白孔六帖 [[KR3k0008]] — Bái Jūyì 白居易's *Liùtiē*, with Kǒng Chuán's 孔傳 Northern-Sòng continuation; the prototypical examination-preparation *lèishū*. Wilkinson §72.1.2.1 cites it for the famous anecdote of Bái assembling entries in jars on a seven-tiered rack.

Around this nucleus belong *Lóngjīn fèngsuǐ pàn* 龍筋鳳髓判 [[KR3k0005]] — Zhāng Zhuó's 張鷟 model-*pàn* 判 anthology for the Táng *shēnyánshūpàn* 身言書判 examination — and the surviving fragments of two specifically examination-oriented texts recovered from Dùnhuáng and Dazaifu, *Tùyuán cèfǔ* 兔園策府 [[KR3k0069]] and *Hànyuàn* 翰苑 [[KR3k0070]]. These fragmentary witnesses are textually marginal but historically central as the only direct evidence for early-Táng *cèwén* pedagogy.

### The "four great books of the Sòng" (Sòng sì dà shū 宋四大書) — the three in KR3k

Of the four court-commissioned mega-compendia of early Northern Sòng (the fourth being *Tàipíng guǎngjì* 太平廣記, classified under *xiǎoshuō* and not in KR3k), three are here:

- *Tàipíng yùlǎn* 太平御覽 [[KR3k0012]] — Lǐ Fǎng 李昉 et al., 977–983, in 1000 *juan*; arranged in 55 *bù* / 5,363 *lèi*; quotes from over 2,000 sources, ~70–80 % otherwise lost (Wilkinson §72.1.2.2).
- *Cèfǔ yuánguī* 册府元龜 [[KR3k0013]] — Wáng Qīnruò 王欽若, Yáng Yì 楊億 et al., completed 1013, in 1000 *juan*; restricted to *zhèngshǐ* extracts; the principal Northern-Sòng compendium of imperial and bureaucratic precedent.
- *Yùhǎi* 玉海 [[KR3k0032]] — Wáng Yīnglín 王應麟, late 13th c., in 200 *juan*; technically the latest of this group but functionally the most intellectually rigorous, and the most reliable Sòng *lèishū* for source-citation (compiled as preparation for the *Bóxué hóngcí* 博學宏詞 examination, which Wáng passed in 1255).

### The Wáng Yīnglín cluster

Wáng Yīnglín alone accounts for four KR3k titles: the *Yùhǎi* [[KR3k0032]], the numerical-categories primer *Xiǎoxué gànzhū* 小學紺珠 [[KR3k0033]], the surname mnemonic *Xìngshì jíjiù piān* 姓氏急就篇 [[KR3k0034]], and (associated) the *Liùtiē bǔ* 六帖補 [[KR3k0037]] supplement of his student Yáng Bóyán 楊伯喦. This cluster is the high-water mark of late-Sòng *kǎojù*-style *lèishū* scholarship: every excerpt in *Yùhǎi* is source-tagged, an editorial standard not matched again until the *Sìkù*.

### Southern-Sòng statecraft / examination-crammer cluster

Driven by the late-12th- and 13th-century *cèyì* 策議 and *cèlùn* 策論 examination format, a recognisable cluster of policy-essay reference works was produced largely from the Yǒngjiā 永嘉 and Wùxué 婺學 milieu:

- *Lìdài zhìdù xiángshuō* 歷代制度詳說 [[KR3k0021]] — Lǚ Zǔqiān 呂祖謙, the Wùxué patriarch.
- *Bāmiàn fēng* 八面鋒 [[KR3k0022]] — Chén Fùliáng 陳傅良, in 93 aphoristic *gāng* 綱.
- *Qúnshū kǎosuǒ* 羣書考索 [[KR3k0029]] — Zhāng Rúyú 章如愚, in 4 *jí* and 212 *juan*, the largest of the cluster; central to Tillman 1990–92 (cited in Wilkinson §72.1.2.2) on Sòng polymath encyclopaedism.
- *Gǔjīn yuánliú zhìlùn* 古今源流至論 [[KR3k0031]] — Lín Jiōng 林駉 and Huáng Lǚwēng 黃履翁.
- *Qúnshū huìyuán jiéjiāng wǎng* 羣書會元截江網 [[KR3k0027]] — anonymous, a representative *fāngběn* 坊本 commercial crammer for Tàixué 太學 students.

### Surname-and-pedigree (*xìngshì*) tradition

A distinct *lèishū* sub-genre indexing the historical *jùnwàng* 郡望 (territorial branches of surname-lineages):

- *Yuánhé xìngzuǎn* 元和姓纂 [[KR3k0007]] — Lín Bǎo 林寶, 812; foundational Táng manual originally for fief-bestowal.
- *Gǔjīn xìngshì shū biànzhèng* 古今姓氏書辯證 [[KR3k0018]] — Dèng Míngshì 鄧名世, Southern Sòng critical revision of the *Yuánhé xìngzuǎn*.
- *Míngxián shìzú yánxíng lèigǎo* 名賢氏族言行類稿 [[KR3k0026]] — Zhāng Dìng 章定, hybrid of surname-pedigree and biographical-anecdote anthology, covering 1,189 surnames.
- *Shìzú dàquán* 氏族大全 [[KR3k0041]] — anonymous Yuán Máshā 麻沙 commercial expansion.
- *Wànxìng tǒngpǔ* 萬姓統譜 [[KR3k0044]] — Líng Dízhī 凌迪知, the bulk culmination of the Míng tradition (140 *juan*).

### Homonymic / sobriquet / childhood-name prosopography

The line founded by [[KR3k0001]] (above) continues with *Xiǎomíng lù* 小名錄 [[KR3k0009]] (Lù Guīméng 陸龜蒙, late Táng); *Xiǎozì lù* 小字錄 [[KR3k0035]] (Chén Sī 陳思, late Sòng); *Míngyí* 名疑 [[KR3k0042]] (Chén Shìyuán 陳士元, Míng); *Tóngxìngmíng lù* 同姓名錄 [[KR3k0047]] (Yú Yín 余寅, late Míng, the 12-*juan* expansion explicitly named in the *Sìkù tíyào* to [[KR3k0001]] as the 21-*juan* descendant); and the rhyme-organised *Bié hào lù* 別號錄 [[KR3k0064]] (Gě Wànlǐ 葛萬里, Qīng), which inverts the relationship and indexes from sobriquet back to personal name.

### Phraseological and rhyme-treasury *lèishū* (the Qīng imperial trilogy)

The Kāngxī–Yōngzhèng court produced an unparalleled cluster of phraseological reference works, all sponsored by the throne and printed at the Wǔyīngdiàn 武英殿:

- *Yùnfǔ qúnyù* 韻府群玉 [[KR3k0039]] — Yīn Jìnxián 陰勁弦 et al., late Yuán; the founding *yùnfǔ* 韻府 to which the entire Qīng imperial cluster responds. Cited by the Yǒnglè emperor in his commission of the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* (Wilkinson §72.1.2.3) as one of his two model works.
- *Yùdìng Pèiwén yùnfǔ* 御定佩文韻府 [[KR3k0059]] — Zhāng Yùshū 張玉書, Chén Tíngjìng 陳廷敬 et al., Kāngxī commission, 444 *juan*; "still has no equal among modern dictionaries for amount of lexical data" (Wilkinson §72.1.1).
- *Yùdìng Yùnfǔ shíyí* 御定韻府拾遺 [[KR3k0060]] — Kāngxī's own supplement to the *Pèiwén yùnfǔ*.
- *Yùdìng Piánzì lèibiān* 御定駢字類編 [[KR3k0056]] — Wú Shìyù 吳士玉, Shěn Zōngjìng 沈宗敬, 240 *juan*; complementary indexing-by-head-character to the *Pèiwén yùnfǔ*'s indexing-by-tail-rhyme.
- *Yùdìng Fēnlèi zìjǐn* 御定分類字錦 [[KR3k0057]] — Hé Chuò 何焯, Chén Péngnián 陳鵬年; Kāngxī matched-pair *duìǒu* 對偶 phraseological compendium.
- *Yùdìng Zǐshǐ jīnghuá* 御定子史精華 [[KR3k0058]] — Wú Shìyù, Wú Xiāng 吳襄; Yōngzhèng-period topical anthology of extracts from the 子 and 史 branches.

### General Qīng imperial *lèishū*

- *Yùdìng Yuānjiàn lèihán* 御定淵鑑類函 [[KR3k0055]] — Zhāng Yīng 張英, Wáng Shìzhēn 王士禛, completed 1702, printed 1710, 450 *juan*; a Kāngxī rework of the late-Míng *Táng lèihán* 唐類函 with later citations marked *zēng* 增 (Wilkinson §72.1.2.4); the principal Qīng general *lèishū* in KR3k. (The much larger *Gǔjīn túshū jíchéng* 古今圖書集成, 1726–28, was not included in the *Sìkù* and is therefore absent from KR3k.)

### Material-culture and *géwù* (*qǐyuán*) line

- *Shìwù jìyuán* 事物紀原 [[KR3k0014]] — Gāo Chéng 高承, Northern Sòng; the founding "origins-of-things" *lèishū*.
- *Gézhì jìngyuán* 格致鏡原 [[KR3k0061]] — Chén Yuánlóng 陳元龍, Qīng; the genre's mature *géwù* synthesis (cited in Wilkinson §72.1.2.4).
- *Huā mù niǎo shòu jí lèi* 花木鳥獸集類 [[KR3k0063]] — Wú Bǎozhī 吳寶芝, small Qīng natural-world allusion compendium.

### Late-Míng general / private / botanical

- *Tiānzhōng jì* 天中記 [[KR3k0049]] — Chén Yàowén 陳耀文, mid-Míng general *lèishū* with proto-*kǎojù* source citation.
- *Shāntáng sìkǎo* 山堂肆考 [[KR3k0052]] — Péng Dàyì 彭大翼, large late-Míng general *lèishū* by a private *zhūshēng* 諸生 compiler.
- *Túshū biān* 圖書編 [[KR3k0050]] — Zhāng Huáng 章潢, massive 127-*juan* late-Míng *túpǔ* 圖譜 (charts-and-tables) encyclopedia from the Báilùdòng 白鹿洞 milieu.
- *Quánfāng bèizǔ jí* 全芳備祖集 [[KR3k0028]] — Chén Jǐngyí 陳景沂, late-Sòng botanical *lèishū* of plants, flowers, fruits, vegetables, and herbs.

### *Méngqiú* primers

- *Méngqiú jízhù* 蒙求集註 [[KR3k0010]] — Sòng commentary by Xú Zǐguāng 徐子光 on Lǐ Hàn 李瀚's Táng *Méngqiú* (rhymed historical-anecdote primer for children).
- *Chúnzhèng méngqiú* 純正蒙求 [[KR3k0040]] — Hú Bǐngwén 胡炳文, Yuán Zhū Xī school orthodox primer.

- [[聖祖玄燁]] (the Kāngxī emperor 玄燁, 1654–1722) — personal patron and *yùdìng* 御定 sponsor of [[KR3k0055]], [[KR3k0059]], [[KR3k0060]] (and indirect sponsor of [[KR3k0056]], [[KR3k0057]], [[KR3k0058]]); single most important imperial actor in the division's Qīng-period output.
- [[張英]] (Zhāng Yīng, 1637–1708), [[王士禛]] (Wáng Shìzhēn, 1634–1711), [[張玉書]] (Zhāng Yùshū, 1642–1711), [[陳廷敬]] (Chén Tíngjìng, 1639–1712) — Hànlín officials who carried the Kāngxī commissions and whose names recur across the imperial cluster.
- [[陳元龍]] (Chén Yuánlóng, 1652–1736) — *Gézhì jìngyuán* [[KR3k0061]], the Qīng *géwù* compendium.

## Topics

- **The *lèishū* as conduit for lost books.** From the Táng *Yìwén lèijù* through the *Tàipíng yùlǎn* to the *Yǒnglè dàdiǎn* — itself the source-base for the *Sìkù* recovery of [[KR3k0001]] — KR3k is the single largest collection of pre-Táng citations preserved by quotation. The methodological caution (Wilkinson §72.1.1; Gù Yánwǔ's *gāotán ér bù gēn* 高談而不根) is that *lèishū* almost always quote a previous *lèishū* rather than the original; verification against the surviving original — when one exists — is therefore mandatory.

- **Imperial sponsorship and dynastic legitimation.** Wilkinson §72.1.1 emphasises the timing of the major imperial commissions (Sòng Tàizōng's *Tàipíng yùlǎn*; Yǒnglè's *Dàdiǎn*; Kāngxī's *Yuānjiàn lèihán* and *Pèiwén yùnfǔ*) at the start of new dynasties or after disputed accessions: the *lèishū* simultaneously displayed the new throne's literary stature, kept restive scholar-officials usefully employed, and let the new regime decide what counted as transmissible knowledge.

- **Classification systems.** The genre's organising matrices — heaven / earth / man / events / things, with the deeper *bù* / *lèi* / *mù* / *shǔ* hierarchy — set the template for many *bǐjì* and reference works outside the *lèishū* genre proper (Wilkinson §72.1.1.2). Departures from this matrix — Wáng Yīnglín's numerical-set organisation in *Xiǎoxué gànzhū* [[KR3k0033]], the rhyme-organisation of the *yùnfǔ* line, the surname-as-key indexing of *xìngshì* compendia — are the genre's most consequential structural innovations.

- **The Sòng *cèyì* / *kǎojù* statecraft *lèishū*.** A distinct Southern-Sòng cluster (Yǒngjiā / Wùxué) used the *lèishū* form not as a literary aid but as a policy-essay crammer for the examinations and as a vehicle for institutional history. Tillman 1990–92 (Wilkinson §72.1.2.2) treats Zhèng Qiáo 鄭樵, Zhāng Rúyú [[KR3k0029]], and Wáng Yīnglín together as a Sòng polymath-encyclopaedist tradition.

- **Phraseological *lèishū* and the Qīng court.** From the Sòng *Shìlèi fù* [[KR3k0011]] through to the Kāngxī rhyme-treasuries, a steadily intensifying technical machinery for the manufacture of parallel-prose and regulated verse: indexed by rhyme (*Pèiwén yùnfǔ*, *Yùnfǔ shíyí*), by head-character (*Piánzì lèibiān*), by matched pair (*Fēnlèi zìjǐn*), by topic-and-allusion (*Yuānjiàn lèihán*). For the user, these compete and complement; for the Qīng court, the entire suite functioned as instruments of cultural standardisation.

- **The surname-and-prosopography line.** A historiographically distinct *lèishū* tradition concerned with disambiguating persons rather than topicalising knowledge: from the Liáng *tóngxìngmíng* compendium [[KR3k0001]] through the Táng *Yuánhé xìngzuǎn* [[KR3k0007]] to the Qīng *Bié hào lù* [[KR3k0064]]. This line bridges KR3k and the genealogical interests of KR2 (Histories) more than any other cluster in the division.

- **The boundary problem.** The *Sìkù* editors deliberately excluded both the Yǒnglè *Dàdiǎn* (lost in 1900 in the Hànlín fire) and the *Gǔjīn túshū jíchéng* (printed by Yōngzhèng but not yet in the *Sìkù* canon when KR3k was constituted) from the category. Conversely, they extracted [[KR3k0001]] from the *Dàdiǎn* and re-issued it. The category as we receive it through KR3k is therefore a *Sìkù*-mediated and *Sìkù*-curated selection — not an exhaustive *lèishū* archive.

## Timeline

- 552–555 — *Gǔjīn tóngxìngmíng lù* 古今同姓名錄 [[KR3k0001]] — earliest extant *lèishū* by the *Sìkù* editors' verdict; founder of the homonymic-prosopography line.
- 605–618 — *Běitáng shūchāo* 北堂書鈔 [[KR3k0004]] — earliest extant general *lèishū*.
- 611 — *Biānzhū* 編珠 [[KR3k0002]] — earliest *fù*-composition allusion-pair handbook.
- 622–624 — *Yìwén lèijù* 藝文類聚 [[KR3k0003]] — first of the Táng "four great *lèishū*" by influence.
- c. 650–660 — *Hànyuàn* 翰苑 [[KR3k0070]] — Dazaifu-preserved fragment, only direct witness to the early-Táng *cèwén* primer line.
- c. 650–674 — *Tùyuán cèfǔ* 兔園策府 [[KR3k0069]] — Dùnhuáng-preserved early-Táng examination primer fragment.
- c. 685–730 — *Lóngjīn fèngsuǐ pàn* 龍筋鳳髓判 [[KR3k0005]] — model-*pàn* anthology for the *shēnyánshūpàn* examination.
