# Qí mín yào shù 齊民要術
*Essential Techniques for the Common People*
by [[賈思勰]] (Jiǎ Sīxié, 6th c., 後魏)

## About the work

A 10-juan agricultural encyclopedia by Jiǎ Sīxié, composed under Northern Wèi (the Kanripo catalog form 後魏 = Northern Wèi), dated to ca. 533–544 CE on internal evidence. The work is the foundational pre-modern Chinese agricultural reference and one of the most consequential pre-modern Chinese technical works. Coverage includes: cultivation of grains, vegetables, and fruits; sericulture; animal husbandry; food preparation (vinegar, soy, alcohol, preserved meats and vegetables); plus a closing 1-juan *zá shuō* (miscellaneous discussions) appendix. The work draws on prior agricultural literature (now mostly lost) and on Jiǎ's own experience as Tàishǒu of Gāoyángjùn. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 533–544.

### Abstract

The *Qí mín yào shù* is the foundational pre-modern Chinese agricultural encyclopedia. The bibliographic record: *Suí shū jīngjí zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Nóngjiā lèi*. The work has had unbroken East Asian reception (Korean, Japanese) and is the principal source for pre-modern Chinese agricultural technique.
