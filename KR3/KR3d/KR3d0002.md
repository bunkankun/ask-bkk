# Nóng shū 農書
*The Agricultural Book*
by [[陳旉]] (Chén Fū, 1076–1154, 宋)

## About the work

A 3-juan Southern Sòng agricultural manual by Chén Fū, completed *Shàoxīng* 19 (1149). Distinguished from the parallel northern-Chinese-focused tradition (*Qí mín yào shù*) by its Lower-Yangzi rice-cultivation focus. The frontmatter brackets to 1149.

### Abstract

The *Nóng shū* (Chén Fū) is the principal Southern-Sòng agricultural manual and the earliest substantial pre-modern Chinese rice-cultivation treatise. The bibliographic record: *Sòng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Nóngjiā lèi*.
