# Yě cài bó lù 野菜博錄
*Comprehensive Records of Wild Vegetables*
by [[鮑山]] (Bào Shān, 明)

## About the work

A 4-juan Late-Míng wild-edible-plants reference by Bào Shān, extending the *Jiù huāng běn cǎo* ([[KR3d0006]]) tradition of Zhū Sù. Composition: bracketed to ca. 1622 (the latest reasonable Late-Míng dating). The frontmatter brackets to 1622–1622.

### Abstract

The *Yě cài bó lù* is one of the principal Late-Míng applied-botanical works in the famine-relief edible-plants tradition. The bibliographic record: *Míng shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Nóngjiā lèi*.
