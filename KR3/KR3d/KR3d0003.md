# Nóngsāng jí yào 農桑輯要
*Essentials of Agriculture and Sericulture*
imperially commissioned and compiled by [[司農司]] (Sīnóngsī, the Yuán Office of Agricultural Affairs)

## About the work

A 7-juan Yuán imperial agricultural manual, commissioned by Khubilai Khan and prepared by the *Sīnóngsī* (Office of Agricultural Affairs). The work was distributed for use by local officials throughout the empire from *Zhìyuán* 10 (1273) onward; the standard recension dates to *Zhìyuán* 23 (1286). The work synthesises the *Qí mín yào shù* ([[KR3d0001]]) and Sòng-period agricultural literature with Yuán-period field experience. The frontmatter brackets to 1273–1286.

### Abstract

The *Nóngsāng jí yào* is the principal Yuán-imperial agricultural manual. The bibliographic record: *Yuán shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Nóngjiā lèi*.
