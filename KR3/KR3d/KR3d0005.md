# Nóng shū 農書 (Wáng Zhēn)
*The Agricultural Book*
by [[王禎]] (Wáng Zhēn, *zì* Bóshàn 伯善, fl. 1295–1330s, 元)

## About the work

A 22-juan Yuán-period agricultural encyclopedia by Wáng Zhēn, the major work of pre-modern Chinese agricultural literature alongside the *Qí mín yào shù* ([[KR3d0001]]). Completed in *Huángqìng* 2 (1313). The work has three major parts: (i) *Nóngsāng tōng jué* 農桑通訣 (general agricultural-sericultural principles); (ii) *Bǎigǔ pǔ* 百穀譜 (catalog of crops); (iii) *Nóng qì tú pǔ* 農器圖譜 (illustrated catalog of agricultural implements — the most extensive pre-modern Chinese illustrated technical reference for agricultural tools). Distinguished from Chén Fū's earlier *Nóng shū* ([[KR3d0002]]) by its much greater scope and its substantial illustrations. Companion to Wáng Zhēn's celebrated *Zào huó zì yìn shū fǎ* — one of the earliest pre-modern Chinese descriptions of wooden movable-type printing technology. The frontmatter brackets to ca. 1295–1313.

### Abstract

The Wáng Zhēn *Nóng shū* is the principal Yuán-period agricultural encyclopedia and one of the most consequential pre-modern Chinese technical works. The bibliographic record: *Yuán shǐ yìwén zhì*; SKQS *Zǐbù — Nóngjiā lèi*.
