# Liù-shū zhèng-é 六書正譌
*Correcting the Errors of the Six-Script Doctrine*
by [[周伯琦]] (Zhōu Bó-qí, 撰)

## About the work
A five-*juàn* orthographic dictionary by Zhōu Bó-qí 周伯琦 (1298–1369), companion to his *Shuōwén zì-yuán* [[KR1j0040]] in 1 *juàn*. Internally arranged by *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* 206 rhyme classes, with *xiǎo-zhuàn* small-seal as head graph; first the structural rationale of the graph is given, and below "*lì-zuò X-X, sú-zuò X-X*" (clerical writes X-X, vulgar writes X-X) for distinguishing — broadly modeled on Zhāng Yǒu's [[張有]] *Fù-gǔ biān* [[KR1j0031]].

### Abstract
The *Liù-shū zhèng-é* is the second of Zhōu Bó-qí's two paired late-Yuán paleographic monuments. Where the *Shuōwén zì-yuán* re-arranges the 540 *Shuōwén* radicals to bring cognates into adjacency, the *Liù-shū zhèng-é* re-organizes the actual graph corpus by rhyme class, in the manner of Zhāng Yǒu's *Fù-gǔ biān*. The work distinguishes *zhèng-tǐ*, *lì*-script, and *sú-tǐ* forms for each lemma. Sìkù compilers' overall judgment: more precise than Yáng Huán [[楊桓]] but less so than Zhāng Yǒu.
