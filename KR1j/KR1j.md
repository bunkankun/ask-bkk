### Scope and scholarly tradition

*Xiǎoxué* 小學 — literally "lesser learning," but in bibliographic usage the *study of language and the characters* — is the last of the ten sub-categories of the Sìkù quánshū's *Jīng-bù* 經部 (`KR1`). From the Hàn down to the early twentieth century, every work on script, lexicon, dialect, or rhyme was classed here rather than with secular philology, on the axiomatic grounds that scrupulous understanding of the *Wǔ-jīng* 五經 depended on a correct grasp of the words and graphs in which the Classics were written (Wilkinson § 6.2.1). The catalog therefore gathers together what a modern reader would call dictionaries, paleographic studies, dialect glossaries, treatises on the *liù-shū* 六書, rhyme tables, and reconstructions of archaic pronunciation, and treats them as a single ancillary discipline serving classical exegesis.

The division falls naturally into three traditional clusters, in the order the Sìkù editors themselves preserved: (1) **訓詁** *xùn-gǔ* — the *Ěryǎ* 爾雅 lineage of glossaries and synonymicons (KR1j0002–KR1j0016); (2) **字書** *zì-shū* — the *Shuōwén* 說文 / *Yùpiān* 玉篇 / *Kāngxī zìdiǎn* 康熙字典 lineage of character dictionaries, *liù-shū* analyses, and clerical- and seal-script studies (KR1j0017–KR1j0053); and (3) **韻書** *yùn-shū* — the *Qièyùn* 切韻 → *Guǎngyùn* 廣韻 → *Jíyùn* 集韻 line of rhyme books, the *děng-yùn* 等韻 rhyme-table tradition, and the Míng–Qīng *gǔyīn-xué* 古音學 reconstruction of archaic phonology (KR1j0054–KR1j0087). The works span roughly two millennia, from the late Western Hàn *Jí-jiù piān* 急就篇 of Shǐ Yóu 史遊 (c. -33) to the late-Qiánlóng imperial compilations of 1773–1786; the great inflection points are the late Hàn (Xǔ Shèn 許慎, Liú Xī 劉熙), the late Tángliutu·early Sòng restoration (the Xú brothers 徐鉉/徐鍇, *Guǎngyùn*, *Jíyùn*), and the Qīng *kǎo-jù* 考據 reconstruction of Old Chinese phonology associated with Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武 and Jiāng Yǒng 江永.

### Important texts and text clusters

**The *Ěryǎ* 爾雅 cluster (訓詁).** The pre-Hàn synonym-thesaurus *Ěryǎ* 爾雅 [[KR1j0002]] — a Classic in its own right since the Kāichéng 開成 stone *Jīng* of 837 (Wilkinson § 6.2.1.2) — anchors the entire division. The Sòng *zhèng-yì* 正義 lineage is represented by Xíng Bǐng's 邢昺 *Ěryǎ shū* 爾雅疏 [[KR1j0003]] and the composite *Ěryǎ zhù-shū* 爾雅注疏 [[KR1j0004]] coupling Guō Pú's 郭璞 (276–324) Jìn-era notes to Xíng Bǐng's sub-commentary; Zhèng Qiáo's 鄭樵 own *Ěryǎ zhù* 爾雅註 [[KR1j0005]] supplies a more independent Southern Sòng reading. Yáng Xióng's 揚雄 *Fāngyán* 方言 [[KR1j0006]] (full title *Yóuxuān shǐzhě jué-dài yǔ-shì bié-guó fāngyán* 輶軒使者絕代語釋別國方言) extends the *Ěryǎ* method to regional dialect, and Liú Xī's 劉熙 *Shìmíng* 釋名 [[KR1j0007]] (Wilkinson § 6.2.1.4) turns it toward etymology by rhyming pun. Zhāng Yī's 張揖 Wèi-period *Guǎngyǎ* 廣雅 [[KR1j0008]] is the standard expansion. Sòng additions to the genre — Lù Diàn's 陸佃 *Píyǎ* 埤雅 [[KR1j0011]] and Luó Yuàn's 羅願 *Ěryǎ yì* 爾雅翼 [[KR1j0012]] — concentrate on natural-history terminology. The Míng *Piányǎ* 駢雅 [[KR1j0013]] of Zhū Móuwěi 朱謀㙔 and Qīng *Xù Fāngyán* 續方言 [[KR1j0015]], *Biéyǎ* 別雅 [[KR1j0016]], and *Zì-gǔ* 字詁 [[KR1j0014]] are the late-imperial supplements.

**The *Shuōwén* 說文 cluster (字書, Hàn–Sòng).** Xǔ Shèn's 許慎 *Shuōwén jiězì* 說文解字 [[KR1j0018]] of 121 CE (Wilkinson § 6.2.1.3) is the foundational Chinese character dictionary: 9,353 entries (in the postface count) under 540 *bù* 部 classifiers, organized by graphic structure rather than by sound or meaning, and the perennial source for *liù-shū* analysis. The Southern-Táng *Xú* brothers preserve the work in two recensions — Xú Kǎi's 徐鍇 *Shuōwén xì-zhuàn* 說文繫傳 [[KR1j0019]] (the *Xiǎo Xú běn* 小徐本) and its *Zhuàn-yùn-pǔ* 篆韻譜 [[KR1j0021]]. Imperially-commissioned Sòng character-books continue the lineage: Sīmǎ Guāng's 司馬光 *Lèi-piān* 類篇 [[KR1j0029]], the *Yùpiān*-based *Chóng-xiū Yùpiān* 重修玉篇 [[KR1j0022]] and *Dà-guǎng yì-huì Yùpiān* 大廣益會玉篇 [[KR1j0056]] of Chén Péngnián 陳彭年, and Jiǎ Chāngcháo's 賈昌朝 *Qún-jīng yīn-biàn* 羣經音辨 [[KR1j0010]] for variant readings across the Classics.

**Standard-form and clerical-script handbooks (Táng–Sòng).** Yán Yuánsūn's 顏元孫 *Gān-lù zì-shū* 干祿字書 [[KR1j0023]], Zhāng Cān's 張參 *Wǔ-jīng wén-zì* 五經文字 [[KR1j0024]] and Táng Xuándù's 唐玄度 *Jiǔ-jīng zì-yàng* 九經字樣 [[KR1j0025]] regulate the orthography of Classical citations, in the context of the Kāichéng stone *Jīng* project. Guō Zhōngshù's 郭忠恕 *Hàn-jiǎn* 汗簡 [[KR1j0026]] and *Pèi-guī* 佩觽 [[KR1j0027]] and Xià Sǒng's 夏竦 *Gǔwén sì-shēng yùn* 古文四聲韻 [[KR1j0028]] are the principal Sòng compilations of pre-Qín *gǔwén* 古文 graph forms. Zhāng Yǒu's 張有 *Fù-gǔ biān* 復古編 [[KR1j0031]], Lóu Jī's 婁機 *Bān-Mǎ zì-lèi* 班馬字類 [[KR1j0032]] and *Hàn-lì zì-yuán* 漢隸字源 [[KR1j0033]], Xuē Shànggōng's 薛尚功 *Lì-dài zhōng-dǐng yí-qì kuǎn-shì fǎ-tiē* 歷代鐘鼎彝器款識法帖 [[KR1j0030]], and Lǐ Cóngzhōu's 李從周 *Zì-tōng* 字通 [[KR1j0035]] together constitute the Sòng paleographic / epigraphic strand. The Liáo Buddhist lexicon *Lóng-kān shǒu-jiàn* 龍龕手鑑 [[KR1j0036]] of Shì Xíngjūn 釋行均, included in the WYG over Qiánlóng objections, preserves the most extensive collection of variant graphs of its time.

***Liù-shū* 六書 theory (Sòng–Míng).** A specifically theoretical sub-strand grows out of the *Shuōwén* — Dài Tóng's 戴侗 *Liù-shū gù* 六書故 [[KR1j0034]], Yáng Huán's 楊桓 *Liù-shū tǒng* 六書統 [[KR1j0037]], Zhōu Bóqí's 周伯琦 *Liù-shū zhèng-é* 六書正譌 [[KR1j0041]] and *Shuōwén zì-yuán* 說文字原 [[KR1j0040]], Lǐ Wénzhòng's 李文仲 *Zì-jiàn* 字鑑 [[KR1j0039]], Zhào Huīqiān's 趙撝謙 *Liù-shū běn-yì* 六書本義 [[KR1j0043]], and Yè Bǐngjìng's 葉秉敬 *Zì-luán* 字㝈 [[KR1j0047]]. Wú Qiūyǎn's 吾丘衍 *Zhōu-Qín kè-shí shì-yīn* 周秦刻石釋音 [[KR1j0038]] applies the same analytic apparatus to bronze and stone inscriptions.

**The *Qièyùn* 切韻 → *Guǎngyùn* 廣韻 line (韻書, Sòng).** The unmodified pre-imperial Northern Sòng *Yuán-běn Guǎng-yùn* 原本廣韻 [[KR1j0054]] and the 1011 imperially-revised *Chóng-xiū Guǎng-yùn* 重修廣韻 [[KR1j0055]] of Chén Péngnián 陳彭年 and Qiū Yōng 丘雍 transmit the 206-rhyme system descending from Lù Fǎyán's 陸法言 *Qièyùn* 切韻 (601) and the Táng *Tángyùn* 唐韻 (Wilkinson § 1.2.2). Dīng Dù's 丁度 *Jíyùn* 集韻 [[KR1j0057]] (1067), with Sīmǎ Guāng, expands the rhyme dictionary to over 53,000 lemmas; his *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* 禮部韻略 in its later glossed redactions — *Fù shì-wén hù-zhù Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* 附釋文互註禮部韻略 [[KR1j0060]], the Máo Huǎng / Máo Jūzhèng 毛晃/毛居正 *Zēng-xiū hù-zhù Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè* 增修互註禮部韻略 [[KR1j0061]], Ōuyáng Délóng 歐陽德隆 and Guō Shǒuzhèng's *Yā-yùn shì-yí* 增修校正押韻釋疑 [[KR1j0062]] and Yáng Bóyán's 楊伯嵒 *Jiǔ-jīng bǔ-yùn* 九經補韻 [[KR1j0063]] — is the rhyme handbook of the Sòng examination system. Sīmǎ Guāng's *Qiè-yùn zhǐ-zhǎng-tú* 切韻指掌圖 [[KR1j0058]] is the canonical Northern Sòng *děng-yùn* 等韻 rhyme table.

**Jīn–Yuán–Míng rhyme books.** Hán Dàozhāo's 韓道昭 Jīn-period *Wǔ-yīn jí-yùn* 五音集韻 [[KR1j0064]] reorganizes the *Guǎngyùn* by initial-tone phonetics; Huáng Gōngshào 黃公紹 and Xióng Zhōng's 熊忠 *Gǔ-jīn yùn-huì jǔ-yào* 古今韻會擧要 [[KR1j0065]] is the principal Yuán synthesis. The anonymous *Sì-shēng quán-xíng děng-zǐ* 四聲全形等子 [[KR1j0066]] and Liú Jiàn's 劉鑑 *Jīng-shǐ zhèng-yīn qiè-yùn zhǐ-nán* 經史正音切韻指南 [[KR1j0067]] continue the rhyme-table tradition. Yuè Sháofèng 樂韶鳳 and Sòng Lián's 宋濂 *Hóng-wǔ zhèng-yùn* 洪武正韻 [[KR1j0068]] is the Hóngwǔ-imperial standard.

**Míng–Qīng *gǔyīn-xué* 古音學 (archaic phonology).** Yáng Shèn 楊愼 led the first systematic Míng reconstruction effort with *Gǔ-yīn cóng-mù* 古音叢目 [[KR1j0069]], *Gǔ-yīn lüè-lì* 古音略例 [[KR1j0070]], *Zhuǎn-zhù gǔ-yīn lüè* 轉注古音略 [[KR1j0071]], *Qí-zì yùn* 奇字韻 [[KR1j0044]] and *Gǔ-yīn pián-zì* 古音駢字 [[KR1j0045]]. Chén Dì's 陳第 *Qū-Sòng gǔ-yīn yì* 屈宋古音義 [[KR1j0073]] decisively rejected the *xié-yùn* 叶韻 doctrine of accommodation. The early-Qīng monument is the five-treatise *Yīn-xué wǔ-shū* 音學五書 of Gù Yánwǔ 顧炎武: *Yīn lùn* 音論 [[KR1j0078]], *Shī běn-yīn* 詩本音 [[KR1j0079]], *Yì yīn* 易音 [[KR1j0080]], *Táng-yùn zhèng* 唐韻正 [[KR1j0081]] and *Gǔ-yīn biǎo* 古音表 [[KR1j0082]], with the supplementary *Yùn bǔ zhèng* 韻補正 [[KR1j0083]]. Máo Qíling's 毛奇齡 *Gǔ-jīn tōng-yùn* 古今通韻 [[KR1j0084]] and *Yì yùn* 易韻 [[KR1j0085]] and Jì Róngshū's 紀容舒 *Sūn-shì Táng-yùn kǎo* 孫氏唐韻考 [[KR1j0086]] are the principal mid-Qīng reactions to and corrections of Gù; Jiāng Yǒng's 江永 *Gǔ-yùn biāo-zhǔn* 古韻標準 [[KR1j0087]] of 1759 represents the next refinement and the bridge to Duàn Yùcái 段玉裁.

**Qīng imperial *xiǎoxué* compilations.** The *Yù-dìng Kāngxī zì-diǎn* 御定康熙字典 [[KR1j0048]] of 1716 (Wilkinson § 6.2.1.5), under the nominal headship of Zhāng Yùshū 張玉書 and Chén Tíngjìng 陳廷敬, contains 47,043 head-characters arranged by the 214-classifier system inherited from the *Zì-huì* 字彙, and remained the authoritative character dictionary until the twentieth century. Lǐ Guāngdì's 李光地 *Yù-dìng yīn-yùn chǎn-wēi* 御定音韻闡微 [[KR1j0074]] (1726) is the matching imperial rhyme-book. The Qiánlóng-era *Qīn-dìng tóng-wén yùn-tǒng* 欽定同文韻統 [[KR1j0075]], *Qīn-dìng xié-yùn huì-jí* 欽定叶韻彙輯 [[KR1j0076]] and *Qīn-dìng yīn-yùn shù-wēi* 欽定音韻述微 [[KR1j0077]] complete the suite of state-sponsored phonological references. Gù Àijí's 顧藹吉 *Lì biàn* 隸辨 [[KR1j0052]] and Zhōu Jìng's 周靖 *Zhuàn-lì kǎo-yì* 篆隸考異 [[KR1j0053]] are the classic Kāngxī-era clerical-script reference works.

**Other notable items.** The Hàn primer *Jí-jiù piān* 急就篇 [[KR1j0017]] of Shǐ Yóu 史遊, transmitted with Yán Shīgǔ's 顏師古 Táng notes and Wáng Yìnglín's 王應麟 Sòng *bǔ-zhù* 補注, is the earliest character primer preserved entire and an important source for Hàn lexicon. Yán Shīgǔ's *Kuāng-miù zhèng-sú* 匡謬正俗 [[KR1j0009]] is a unique Táng compendium of philological corrections. Shū Tiānmín's 舒天民 Yuán-period *Liù-yì gāng-mù* 六藝綱目 [[KR1j0088]] is a primer that summarizes the *liù-yì* 六藝 (the six elementary arts) and is here grouped with *xiǎoxué* by association with the *Zhōu-lǐ* curriculum.

### Important persons

- [[許愼]] — Eastern Hàn lexicographer of the *Wǔ-jīng*; compiler of the *Shuōwén jiězì* 說文解字, the structural foundation of all subsequent Chinese character lexicography.
- [[揚雄]] — Western Hàn polymath whose *Fāngyán* 方言 inaugurated dialect lexicography in China and remained the only such work for over a millennium.
- [[劉熙]] — Late Hàn scholar of the *Bèi-hǎi* 北海 circle; author of the *Shìmíng*, the earliest sustained etymological project working through phonetic affinity (*shēng-xùn* 聲訓).
- [[郭璞]] — Western Jìn polymath whose annotations to the *Ěryǎ*, *Fāngyán* and *Shānhǎijīng* defined the medieval reception of all three.
- [[顏師古]] — Táng exegete whose annotations dominate the transmission of the *Hàn-shū* and supply the standard medieval gloss on the *Jí-jiù piān* and on textual variants generally.
- [[徐鉉]], [[徐鍇]] — The Xú brothers of the Southern Táng who rescued the *Shuōwén* from textual collapse and produced the two recensions (*Dà Xú* and *Xiǎo Xú*) on which all later editions depend.
- [[邢昺]] — Northern Sòng compiler of the official *Ěryǎ shū*, fixing the Sòng *zhèng-yì* form of the work.
- [[陳彭年]], [[丘雍]] — Lead editors of the 1011 imperial *Chóng-xiū Guǎng-yùn* and of the 1013 *Chóng-xiū Yùpiān*, the foundational Sòng rhyme- and character-books.
- [[丁度]] — Compiler of the *Jíyùn* and *Lǐ-bù yùn-lüè*, which fixed the rhyme system of Sòng examination poetry.
- [[司馬光]] — Took over the *Lèi-piān* and the *Jíyùn* after Dīng Dù's death and contributed the *Qiè-yùn zhǐ-zhǎng-tú*, bridging dictionary and rhyme-table traditions.
- [[鄭樵]] — Southern Sòng historian whose theoretical interventions on *wén* 文 and *zì* 字 reshaped how the *Shuōwén* was read, including by Duàn Yùcái.
- [[戴侗]], [[周伯琦]], [[趙撝謙]] — The principal Sòng–Míng *liù-shū* theorists.
- [[楊愼]] — Míng scholar in exile in Yúnnán who produced the first sustained corpus of works on archaic rhyme, framing the eventual Qīng programme.
- [[陳第]] — Late-Míng author of the *Máo-shī gǔ-yīn kǎo* and *Qū-Sòng gǔ-yīn yì*; rejected the *xié-yùn* doctrine and inaugurated the rigorous reconstruction of pre-Qín phonology.
- [[顧炎武]] — Early-Qīng polymath; the *Yīn-xué wǔ-shū* 音學五書 is the founding monument of Qīng *gǔyīn-xué*.
- [[毛奇齡]] — Vigorous critic of Gù Yánwǔ; preserved an alternative reading of Old Chinese rhyme that fed into eighteenth-century debate.
- [[江永]] — Anhuī-Wǎn 皖 school philologist who refined Gù's rhyme groups and trained Dài Zhèn 戴震; *Gǔ-yùn biāo-zhǔn* is the immediate predecessor of Duàn Yùcái's seventeen-rhyme system.
- [[張玉書]], [[陳廷敬]] — Nominal supervisors of the *Kāngxī zì-diǎn* compilation (1710–1716).
- [[李光地]] — Compiler of the *Yīn-yùn chǎn-wēi*, the official Kāngxī rhyme-book companion to the *Zìdiǎn*.

### Topics

- **The unity of *xiǎoxué* with classical exegesis.** Throughout the division, no work is offered as a study of language for its own sake; the *Ěryǎ* and *Shuōwén* are framed as instruments for understanding the *Wǔ-jīng* and *Shàng-shū*, and the rhyme books as aids for the examination essay and the recitation of the *Shī*. This is the bibliographic premise that holds the category together (Wilkinson § 6.2.1).
- **訓詁 vs. 字書 vs. 韻書.** The three sub-genres correspond to the three faces of the Chinese sign: meaning (*yì* 義), graph (*xíng* 形), and sound (*yīn* 音). Each generates its own canonical chain — *Ěryǎ* → *Fāngyán* → *Guǎngyǎ*; *Shuōwén* → *Yùpiān* → *Lèi-piān* → *Kāngxī zìdiǎn*; *Qièyùn* → *Guǎngyùn* → *Jíyùn* → *Hóng-wǔ zhèng-yùn* — and the dialectic between them is one of the principal interpretive problems of the field.
- **The *liù-shū* 六書.** Xǔ Shèn's six analytic categories (*xiàng-xíng* 象形, *zhǐ-shì* 指事, *huì-yì* 會意, *xíng-shēng* 形聲, *zhuǎn-zhù* 轉注, *jiǎ-jiè* 假借) become the master grid for all later character analysis (Wilkinson § 2.4); their interpretation, especially of *zhuǎn-zhù* and *jiǎ-jiè*, is contested from Dài Tóng through Zhōu Bóqí to the Qīng Shuōwén scholars.
- **The 540-*bù* vs. 214-classifier systems.** The reorganization of the character lexicon away from Xǔ Shèn's 540 *Shuōwén* classifiers toward the 214-classifier scheme stabilized by the *Zì-huì* 字彙 (Wàn-lì era) and inherited by the *Kāngxī zìdiǎn* is the principal structural transformation of the *zì-shū* tradition.
- **The *Qièyùn* / *Guǎngyùn* phonological system.** The 206-rhyme system of the *Qièyùn* / *Guǎngyùn*, later collapsed into the 106 *Píngshuǐ* 平水 rhymes for examination use, supplies the Middle-Chinese reference grid against which Old Chinese is later reconstructed (Wilkinson § 1.2.2).
- ***Xié-yùn* 叶韻 vs. *gǔ-yīn* 古音.** Sòng scholars (following Wú Yù 吳棫, [[KR1j0059]]) treated mismatches between the rhymes of the *Shī* and contemporary pronunciation as ad-hoc *xié-yùn* accommodations. Chén Dì rejected this in principle, arguing for systematic phonological change over time; Gù Yánwǔ converted this into a research programme. The Qīng *gǔyīn-xué* tradition develops from this rejection.
- **Imperial standardization.** From the Kāichéng stone *Jīng* through the *Hóng-wǔ zhèng-yùn* to the Kāngxī- and Qiánlóng-era imperial dictionaries, the state repeatedly intervenes in *xiǎoxué* to fix examination orthography and pronunciation; *昭同文之治* (Wilkinson § 6.2.1.5) is an explicit imperial frame for the *Kāngxī zìdiǎn*.
- **Paleography and inscriptional evidence.** Sòng *jīn-shí-xué* 金石學 (Xuē Shànggōng, Guō Zhōngshù) and Yuán *gǔwén* analysis (Wú Qiūyǎn) extend *xiǎoxué* to bronze and stone inscriptions, prefiguring the much fuller integration of oracle-bone and bronze evidence after 1899.
- **Dialect and the spoken vernacular.** Outside Yáng Xióng's *Fāngyán* and its Qīng *Xù Fāngyán*, the spoken language is largely absent from the division — a structural consequence of the classical-text orientation of *xiǎoxué* (Wilkinson § 6.2.1).
- **Clerical and seal scripts.** Lóu Jī's 漢隸 studies, Wú Qiūyǎn's *Zhōu-Qín kè-shí*, Gù Àijí's *Lì biàn* and Zhōu Jìng's *Zhuàn-lì kǎo-yì* form a distinct connoisseurial sub-tradition focused on the visual history of the script.

### Timeline

- c. -48 – -33 — *Jí-jiù piān* 急就篇 [[KR1j0017]] — Shǐ Yóu's Hàn primer, the earliest character textbook preserved entire.
- c. -200 – 100 — *Ěryǎ* 爾雅 [[KR1j0002]] — pre-Hàn glossary canonized as a Classic in 837.
- c. -10 – 18 — *Fāngyán* 方言 [[KR1j0006]] — Yáng Xióng's dialect survey; first dialect lexicon in any language.
- 100 – 121 — *Shuōwén jiězì* 說文解字 [[KR1j0018]] — Xǔ Shèn's structural dictionary; foundation of all later Chinese lexicography.
- c. 196 – 220 — *Shìmíng* 釋名 [[KR1j0007]] — Liú Xī's etymological dictionary by phonetic affinity.
- c. 227 – 232 — *Guǎngyǎ* 廣雅 [[KR1j0008]] — Zhāng Yī's Wèi-period expansion of the *Ěryǎ*.
- c. 630 – 645 — *Kuāng-miù zhèng-sú* 匡謬正俗 [[KR1j0009]] — Yán Shīgǔ's miscellany of philological corrections.
