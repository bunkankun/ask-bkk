# Dà-guǎng yì-huì Yùpiān 大廣益會玉篇
*The Greatly-Expanded* Yùpiān
by [[陳彭年]] (Chén Péng-nián, 961–1017) et al., by imperial command, Sòng Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 6 (1013)

## About the work
The *Sì-bù cóng-kān* (SBCK) reprint of the imperially-revised Northern-Sòng *Yùpiān* — i.e., the same imperial recension treated under [[KR1j0022]] (*Chóng-xiū Yùpiān*, WYG version) but bearing its alternative Sòng-period title *Dà-guǎng yì-huì Yùpiān*. The dictionary covers 30 *juàn* under 540 *bù-shǒu*, transmitting the Liáng-period *Yùpiān* (543) of Gù Yěwáng [[顧野王]] in the Northern-Sòng recension produced by Chén Péng-nián, Wú Ruì 吳銳, Qiū Yōng [[丘雍]] and others on imperial command (Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 6 / 1013). The SBCK base is the Yuán-Míng print line preserving the Sòng *Dà-guǎng yì-huì* title — distinct from Zhāng Shì-jùn's later print which (per the Sìkù tíyào discussion under [[KR1j0022]]) wrongly claimed *Shàng-yuán*-edition status while in fact transmitting the same Sòng *chóng-xiū* text. The work is the second great Chinese character dictionary after the *Shuōwén jiězì*; for the full bibliographic entry — provenance, Liáng/Táng/Sòng layered transmission, Shén Gǒng's *Wǔ-yīn shēng-lùn* / *fǎn-niǔ-tú* appendices, modern critical editions — see [[KR1j0022]].

### Tiyao
*No tiyao in source.* The SBCK transmits the Sòng-period *Yùpiān* without the Sìkù imperial *tíyào* (the imperial preface to the Sòng work itself, by Chén Péng-nián, is preserved). For the Sìkù tíyào on this work, see the WYG version [[KR1j0022]].

### Abstract
This entry is the SBCK transmission of the same *Yùpiān* recension treated more fully at [[KR1j0022]]. The SBCK base text descends from the Yuán-Míng print line which preserved the Northern-Sòng imperial title *Dà-guǎng yì-huì Yùpiān* — referring to Chén Péng-nián's [[陳彭年]] re-edition of Dà-zhōng-xiáng-fú 6 (1013) which substantially expanded the Liáng-period text of Gù Yěwáng [[顧野王]] (presented 543) and the Táng-period augmentation by Sūn Qiáng (Shàng-yuán 1, 674). The Sìkù imperial editors recognised this version as the one preserved in the *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* and used by Yuán-period scholars. *notBefore* = *notAfter* = 1013, the year of Chén Péng-nián's revision.

## Translations and research
For research literature see [[KR1j0022]]. The principal modern reference for the Sòng *Yùpiān* line is Liú Yèqiū 劉葉秋's *Zhōngguó zì-diǎn shǐ-lüè* (1983) and the Wú Yú 吳玉's *Yùpiān jiào-shì* 玉篇校釋 (Shanghai: Shanghai gǔjí, 1989).

## Other points of interest
The presence of the Sòng-period title *Dà-guǎng yì-huì* on the SBCK frontispiece is the signal that this is the post-1013 imperial revision — distinct from the lost original Liáng *Yùpiān* (only fragments of which survive in *Yǒng-lè dà-diǎn* citations and a sixth-century manuscript fragment from the Yùnzhèng-yuàn 元正院 in Japan, the *Wǎn-juàn-zǐ-běn Yùpiān* 篇玉殘卷, edited by Hú Jí 胡吉宣 1989).
