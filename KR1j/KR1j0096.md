# Ěryǎ Xiè-shì yīn 爾雅謝氏音
*Master Xiè's Phonological Commentary on the* Ěryǎ
by [[謝嶠]] (撰)

## About the work

A modern reconstruction of [[謝嶠]] Xiè Qiáo's lost *Ěryǎ yīn* 爾雅音 — the third of the Liáng triplet of *Ěryǎ* phonological commentaries, with Gù Yěwáng [[KR1j0094]] and Shī Qián [[KR1j0095]]. *Suí shū jīngjí zhì* 隋書‧經籍志 records the work in two *juàn*; lost by the Sòng. CHANT reconstruction CH2f1206 draws from Lù Démíng's *Jīngdiǎn shìwén* 經典釋文.

### Abstract

The Xiè-shì *yīn* is the lightest-attested of the three Liáng *Ěryǎ* phonological commentaries; the surviving fragments are short *fǎn-qiè* spellings, often cited by Lù Démíng jointly with one or both of the other Liáng commentators. The first preserved entries from 〈Shìgǔ 釋詁〉 include: *yáng-guān* 洋、觀 → *yīn guān* 音官 (cited "*Shìwén* refers to Gù and Xiè"); *ní* 尼 → *dìng* 定; *ní* 尼 → *yáng-ér fǎn* 羊而反.

The frequency of joint-citation (*Gù-Xiè* 顧、謝; *Shī-Xiè* 施、謝) suggests that the three Liáng *yīn* — though independently composed — circulated together as a paired reference set, in the same way that *Sūn-Guō* 孫郭 (Sūn Yán + Guō Pú) functioned for the earlier Wèi-Jìn layer.

Dating bracket (500–557) follows the Liáng-dynasty span; no internal dating evidence in the surviving fragments.

## Other points of interest

The Liáng triplet *Gù-Shī-Xiè* — coordinated phonological re-reading of the *Ěryǎ* in the mid-sixth century — is one of the principal lost sources behind Lù Démíng's *Ěryǎ shìwén* and so a critical filter for any reconstruction of the *Ěryǎ* reading-tradition before the Suí *Qièyùn* synthesis.
