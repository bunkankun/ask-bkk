# È-guǐ bào-yìng jīng 餓鬼報應經
*Sūtra on the Karmic Recompense [Leading to] Rebirth as a Hungry Ghost* (*Preta-vipāka-sūtra*?)
translator unknown ([[失譯]], 譯)

## About the work

T746 in one fascicle is an anonymous Chinese sūtra on the karmic recompense leading to rebirth as a *preta* (餓鬼 *è-guǐ*, "hungry ghost"). The translator is unknown; the text is dated by linguistic register to the broad Han-Six Dynasties translation period.

### Abstract

The text is structurally similar to [[KR6i0428]] / T734 (*Fó shuō guǐ-wèn-mù-lián jīng*) — a series of *preta*-confessions and karmic case-studies — but anonymous and likely later in date. The narrative format presents *preta* describing their suffering (the canonical *preta* phenomenology of unrelievable hunger and thirst, food-that-turns-to-fire, etc.) and recounting the prior-life unwholesome actions — typically miserliness, refusal of charity, hoarding of food — that led to the rebirth.

The genre and content are continuous with the broader Chinese Buddhist *preta*-tradition that culminates in the Tang-Sòng *Yúlán-pén* 盂蘭盆 ritual cycle. The anonymous transmission and the late-Six Dynasties / early Tang catalogue placement suggest the text may have been composed or translated during the period when the *preta*-and-merit-transfer cluster was crystallizing in East Asian Buddhism, providing scriptural support for the popular Buddhist liturgy of the Ghost Festival.
