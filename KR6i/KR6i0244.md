# Dàodì jīng 道地經
*Sūtra on the Stages of the Path (partial Saṅgharakṣa Yogācārabhūmi)*
composed by [[僧伽羅剎]] (Sēngqié Luóchà / Saṅgharakṣa, 造); translated by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

The *Dàodì jīng* (T607) is Ān Shìgāo's 安世高 ([[安世高]]) one-fascicle partial Hàn-period translation of Saṅgharakṣa's 僧伽羅剎 ([[僧伽羅剎]]) Sarvāstivāda *Yogācārabhūmi* — the same Indic original of which Dharmarakṣa later produced the much fuller seven-fascicle [[KR6i0243]] *Xiūxíng dàodì jīng* 修行道地經 (T606).

### Prefaces

The *Chū sānzàng jì jí* preserves a preface by Dào'ān 道安. The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A small early-Hàn rendering of selected sections of Saṅgharakṣa's *Yogācārabhūmi*, of capital importance both for the early study of Buddhist meditation theory in China and for the historical-philological reconstruction of the Indic original. Datable to Ān Shìgāo's Luòyáng activity (148–170 CE), making it among the earliest Chinese Buddhist translations of a *śāstra* form.
