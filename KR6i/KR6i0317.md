# Jīnguāngmíng jīng kēzhù 金光明經科註
*Topical Annotation of the Sūtra of Golden Light*
compiled by [[受汰]] (Shòutài, 集)

## About the work

X362 is [[受汰]]'s four-fascicle Ming-period topical-with-annotation (科註) edition of [[KR6i0301|*Jīnguāngmíng jīng* 金光明經]] (T663). The genre combines a *kē* 科 (structural outline) with a *zhù* 註 (running annotation): the sūtra is laid out under nested topical headings, each followed by an exegetical note. The *Jiāxìng* 嘉興 edition is the textual basis preserved in the *Xuzangjing*; the editing brings together the Tiāntái commentarial line.

### Prefaces

The work opens with [[受汰]]'s own *xù* 敘, which lays out the genre-history with admirable clarity: the *xuán-yì* 玄義 explicates the title, the *wén-jù* 文句 divides the chapters and sections; "from the *Lotus* downward, only the present sūtra is read in this paired manner" (法華而下惟今經例之), thanks to "Tiāntái Zhì-zhě dà-shī" (= [[智顗]]) — by which [[受汰]] means precisely the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* commentaries [[KR6i0304|T1783]] and [[KR6i0306|T1785]]. He continues that "Master Zhāng-ān (= [[灌頂]]) recorded what was spoken, transmitting it to later students; coming down to the Sòng, the Venerable of Sì-míng (= [[知禮]]) firmly took up the [Tiāntái] revival as his personal task… he composed *Jì* (i.e. T1784 and T1786) and elaborated their meaning."

### Abstract

The work is therefore a synthetic Tiāntái commentary: a Ming-period assemblage of the school's complete commentarial position on [[KR6i0301|T663]], drawing from [[KR6i0304|T1783]], [[KR6i0306|T1785]], [[KR6i0305|T1784]], and [[KR6i0307|T1786]] under [[受汰]]'s editorial frame. Date bracket conservatively follows the generic "Ming dynasty" ascription of [[受汰]] in the DILA Authority record (A000553). [[受汰]] was a *bhikṣu* of the Ān-guó-sì 安國寺 in Yán-guān 鹽官 (modern Hǎi-níng) and a disciple of Tóng-xī Héshàng 桐谿和尚.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0301]] (金光明經 / T663); preceding Tiāntái commentaries [[KR6i0304]], [[KR6i0306]] and their subcommentaries [[KR6i0305]], [[KR6i0307]].
