# Fóshuō Wèishēngyuàn jīng 佛說未生冤經
*Sūtra of [the King] Yet-To-Be-Born-Foe (Ajātaśatru)*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Wèishēngyuàn jīng* (T507) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). The protagonist *Wèishēngyuàn* 未生冤 is a Chinese semantic translation of *Ajātaśatru* — "Foe-Yet-Unborn" — the famous king of Magadha who imprisoned his father Bimbisāra and converted to Buddhism after the Buddha's death. This is one of the earliest Chinese sūtras specifically about Ajātaśatru.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

The Ajātaśatru narrative is one of the most important historical-mythical episodes in the Buddhist canon, treated extensively in the Pāli *Sāmaññaphala-sutta* and many Mahāyāna sources. Zhī Qiān's translation is dated to his Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
