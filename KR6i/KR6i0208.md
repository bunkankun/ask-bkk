# Fóshuō Zhuǎnyǒu jīng 佛說轉有經
*Sūtra on the Transition of Existence (Bhava-saṅkrānti)*
translated by [[佛陀扇多]] (Fótuóshànduō / Buddhaśānta, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Zhuǎnyǒu jīng* (T576, *Bhava-saṅkrānti-sūtra*) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra translated by Buddhaśānta 佛陀扇多 ([[佛陀扇多]]). The title rendered literally is "Sūtra on Transmigrating Existence"; *zhuǎnyǒu* 轉有 = *bhava-saṅkrānti*, the passage of *bhava* (existence) from one life to the next, treated here through the simile of a reflection (*pratibimba*).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A brief Mahāyāna treatise in sūtra form on consciousness, rebirth, and the absence of a transmigrating self, datable to Buddhaśānta's translation activity at Luòyáng and Yè under the Northern Wèi (525–539). It is a parallel to the [[KR6i0207]] 大方等修多羅王經 by Bodhiruci I and to a Tibetan version preserved as *Srid pa 'pho ba'i mdo*.
