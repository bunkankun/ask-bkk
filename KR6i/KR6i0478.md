# Bā dà-rén jué jīng shū 八大人覺經疏
*A Subcommentary on the Sūtra of the Eight Great-Person Awakenings*
compiled by [[續法]] (Xù-fǎ = Bǎi-tíng, 集)

## About the work

A one-fascicle commentary on the [[KR6i0476|*Bā dà-rén jué jīng* 八大人覺經]] (T779) by the Qīng-dynasty Huáyán scholar-monk [[續法]] (Xù-fǎ = Bǎi-tíng 柏亭, 1641–1728), one of the principal late-Qīng commentators on Huáyán-school texts. The work is preserved in the Manji *Zoku-zōkyō* (CBETA X37 No. 673).

### Abstract

Xùfǎ's *shū* (subcommentary / extended exegesis) is markedly fuller than [[智旭]]'s shorter [[KR6i0477|*Lüè-jiě*]] and reflects the Qīng-period scholastic tendency toward systematic exposition with doctrinal cross-references. Xùfǎ approaches the sūtra through Huáyán-school doctrinal categories: he reads the eight awakenings as a graded sequence of contemplative practices culminating in the bodhisattva's universal vow, and he organises the exposition around the Huáyán *yī-zhēn-fǎ-jiè* (one-true-Dharma-realm) doctrine. The work draws on a wide range of canonical and exegetical sources, including the *Avataṃsaka-sūtra*, the *Mahā-prajñā-pāramitā-śāstra*, and the standard Tiāntái *zhì-guān* texts, providing the reader with comprehensive doctrinal context for each of the eight contemplations.

Xùfǎ also produced [[KR6i0487|a much fuller five-fascicle subcommentary on the *Sì-shí'èr-zhāng jīng* 四十二章經疏鈔]], reflecting his programmatic engagement with the "Three Sūtras of the Buddha-Patriarchs" — the *Sì-shí'èr-zhāng jīng*, the *Bā dà-rén jué jīng*, and the *Yí-jiào jīng* — as foundational monastic curriculum texts.
