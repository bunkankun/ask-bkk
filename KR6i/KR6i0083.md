# Jìngmíng xuán lùn 淨名玄論
*Treatise on the Profound Meaning of [the Sūtra of] Pure Name [Vimalakīrti]*
by [[吉藏]] (Jízàng, 造)

## About the work

The *Jìngmíng xuán lùn* (T1780) is an eight-fascicle treatise on the *Vimalakīrti-nirdeśa-sūtra* ([[KR6i0076]] = T475) by the Sānlùn 三論 (Three-Treatise / Madhyamaka) master Jízàng 吉藏 ([[吉藏]]; 549–623 CE). It functions as a prolegomenon to the verse-by-verse commentary [[KR6i0084]] *Wéimó jīng yìshū* (T1781). *Jìngmíng* 淨名 ("Pure Name") is a Chinese semantic translation of *Vimalakīrti* (parallel to Xuánzàng's later *Wúgòuchēng* 無垢稱). The Taishō header cross-references Nos. 475 and 1781.

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the doxographical exposition. No separate authorial preface survives in the Taishō witnesses.

### Abstract

Jízàng was the systematizer and final great representative of the Sānlùn school, the Chinese Madhyamaka tradition descended from Kumārajīva via Sēngzhào 僧肇 ([[僧肇]]) and Falang 法朗. The *Jìngmíng xuán lùn* is one of three major Vimalakīrti texts by Jízàng (the others being [[KR6i0084]] *Wéimó jīng yìshū* and the lost *Wéimó luè shū*). Together they constitute the most extensive Sānlùn-school engagement with the *Vimalakīrti*.

The *Xuán lùn* (a *xuán*-genre treatise rather than a verse-by-verse *shū*) addresses the *Vimalakīrti*'s philosophical core: the doctrine of non-duality (*bùèr* 不二), the bodhisattva *upāya* (skillful means), the relation of *saṃsāra* and *nirvāṇa*, and the bodhisattva's stance toward *bodhi*. Jízàng deploys his characteristic *liángbiǎn* 兩遣 (refutation of both extremes) hermeneutic to argue that the *Vimalakīrti*'s teaching is the perfect Madhyamaka realization of *prajñā*.

Jízàng spent his late career at Yángzhōu (605–615 CE) under the Sui *Jiāngdū* 江都 establishment, before relocating to Cháng'ān at the start of the Tang. The *Xuán lùn* and *Yìshū* belong to this productive Yángzhōu period.
