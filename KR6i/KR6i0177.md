# Fóshuō Jīnyào tóngzǐ jīng 佛說金耀童子經
*Sūtra of the Boy Gold-Brilliance*
translated by [[天息災]] (Tiānxīzāi / Devaśāntika, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Jīnyào tóngzǐ jīng* (T546) is a one-fascicle Sòng sūtra by Tiānxīzāi 天息災 ([[天息災]]). The protagonist *Jīnyào tóngzǐ* 金耀童子 ("Boy Gold-Brilliance") is a child-bodhisattva figure.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna text in the child-bodhisattva genre. Translation dated to Tiānxīzāi's pre-name-change period (980–987 CE) or his post-name-change Fǎxián period (987–1000 CE) — the catalog meta gives 天息災, suggesting pre-987. Bracket: 980–1000 CE.
