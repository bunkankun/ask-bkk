# Fóshuō lǎo nǚrén jīng 佛說老女人經
*Sūtra of the Old Woman*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō lǎo nǚrén jīng* (T559) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). It opens a small "old woman" cluster (KR6i0191–0193) in which an aged woman becomes the Buddha's interlocutor on questions of life, death, and aging.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short sūtra in which an old woman queries the Buddha about the four sufferings (birth, aging, illness, death). Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
