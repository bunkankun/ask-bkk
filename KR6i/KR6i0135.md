# Fóshuō Suíyǒng zūnzhě jīng 佛說隨勇尊者經
*Sūtra of the Venerable Anuruddha-the-Brave*
translated by [[施護]] (Shīhù / Dānapāla, 等譯) et al.

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Suíyǒng zūnzhě jīng* (T505) is a one-fascicle Sòng-period sūtra by Shīhù 施護 ([[施護]]) and his team. The protagonist *Suíyǒng* 隨勇 ("the Brave / Following Bravery") may be a Sòng-era rendering of Sanskrit *Anuruddha* (one of the Buddha's foremost disciples).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Sòng translation on the merits and conduct of an arhat-disciple. Translation dated to Shīhù's productive period (989–1017 CE).
