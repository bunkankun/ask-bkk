# Fóshuō Hēdiāo Ānáhán jīng 佛說呵雕阿那鋡經
*Sūtra of Hēdiāo Ānáhán*
translated by [[竺曇無蘭]] (Zhú Tánwúlán, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Hēdiāo Ānáhán jīng* (T538) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Zhú Tánwúlán 竺曇無蘭 ([[竺曇無蘭]]). The names *Hēdiāo* 呵雕 and *Ānáhán* 阿那鋡 are phonetic transliterations.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative. Translation dated to Tánwúlán's Eastern Jìn period (381–395 CE).
