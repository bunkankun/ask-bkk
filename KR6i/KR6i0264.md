# Wénshūzhīlì pǔchāo sānmèi jīng 文殊支利普超三昧經
*Sūtra on the Universal-Transcending Samādhi of Mañjuśrī*
translated by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

T627 (three fascicles; Sanskrit *Ajātaśatrukaukṛtyavinodana-sūtra*) is [[竺法護]]'s Western Jin retranslation of the same Indic *sūtra* underlying [[KR6i0263|T626]] (Lokakṣema's *Āshéshì-wáng jīng*). Dharmarakṣa's title emphasizes [[文殊|Mañjuśrī]]'s "universal-transcending samādhi" (普超三昧 *pǔchāo sānmèi*), which the Buddha invokes as the vehicle for resolving Ajātaśatru's existential remorse over patricide. The text is a key source for the Mahāyāna doctrine that absolute *prajñā* dissolves the apparent reality of moral transgression.

### Structural Division

T627 is divided into thirteen chapters:

1. 正士品 (Zhèngshì pǐn) — *Chapter on the True Beings*
2. 化佛品 (Huàfó pǐn) — *Chapter on the Conjured Buddhas*
3. 舉缽品 (Jǔbō pǐn) — *Chapter on the Raising of the Bowl*
4. 幼童品 (Yòutóng pǐn) — *Chapter on the Young Boy*
5. 無吾我品 (Wúwúwǒ pǐn) — *Chapter on No-Self*
6. 總持品 (Zǒng-chí pǐn) — *Chapter on Dhāraṇī*
7. 三藏品 (Sānzàng pǐn) — *Chapter on the Three Baskets*
8. 不退轉輪品 (Bùtuìzhuǎnlún pǐn) — *Chapter on the Irreversible Wheel*
9. 變動品 (Biàndòng pǐn) — *Chapter on Transformation*
10. 決疑品 (Juéyí pǐn) — *Chapter on the Resolution of Doubts*
11. 心本淨品 (Xīnběnjìng pǐn) — *Chapter on the Originally Pure Mind*
12. 月首受決品 (Yuèshǒu shòujué pǐn) — *Chapter on the Prediction Received by Yuèshǒu*
13. 囑累品 (Zhǔlèi pǐn) — *Chapter of Entrustment*

Related texts in the canon: [[KR6i0263|T626]] (*Fóshuō Āshéshìwáng jīng*), [[KR6i0265|T628]] (*Fóshuō wèicéngyǒu zhèngfǎ jīng*), and [[KR6i0266|T629]] (*Fóshuō fàngbō jīng*).

### Abstract

The text is unanimously ascribed to [[竺法護]] in the canonical bibliographies; the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145, juan 2) places its translation in Tài-kāng 太康 8 (287 CE). The bracket given here follows the *Chū sānzàng jì jí*'s precise dating, which is generally accepted by modern scholarship (Boucher 2008). T627 is one of Dharmarakṣa's larger Mahāyāna translations and reflects his mature translation style; the chapter structure is more elaborate than [[KR6i0263|T626]]'s, and includes a major treatment of the *xīn běn jìng* — "the originally pure mind" — that became one of the most-cited passages in later East Asian *tathāgatagarbha* and Chán exegesis.
