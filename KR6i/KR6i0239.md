# Fóshuō Dà-ānbān shǒuyì jīng 佛說大安般守意經
*Sūtra of Great Mindfulness on Inhalation and Exhalation (Mahā-ānāpānasmṛti-sūtra)*
attributed to [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

The *Dà-ānbān shǒuyì jīng* (T602) is a two-fascicle text of immense significance in early Chinese Buddhism — for centuries the principal Chinese exposition of *ānāpānasmṛti* (mindfulness of breathing) and the foundational text of the *chán* (*dhyāna*) movement of the Eastern Hàn 後漢 and Three Kingdoms. The transmitted text, however, is universally recognized in modern scholarship to be a heavily commentated composite, with Ān Shìgāo's underlying translation overlaid by interlinear and embedded commentary by Kāng Sēnghuì 康僧會 (and possibly Zhī Dùn 支遁) that has become inseparable from the root text.

### Prefaces

Sēngyòu's *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145, ch. 6) preserves a famous preface by Kāng Sēnghuì describing the lineage of the meditation practice from Ān Shìgāo through his disciples Hán Lín 韓林, Pí Yè 皮業, and Chén Huì 陳慧, whose oral commentary Sēnghuì then incorporated. A second preface by Xiè Fù 謝敷 is also preserved.

### Abstract

A composite-witness text that reflects the very earliest layer of *dhyāna* practice in China. Modern Jan Nattier (2008, 2014) and Stefano Zacchetti (2002, 2010) have shown that Kongō-ji 金剛寺 (Japan) preserves an alternative manuscript redaction without commentary that is much closer to Ān Shìgāo's translation; T602 should therefore be read as a hybrid commentary-text, not a pure Hàn translation. The bracket 148–170 CE corresponds to Ān Shìgāo's documented translation activity at Luòyáng under Hàn Huándì 漢桓帝.
