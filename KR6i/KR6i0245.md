# Xiǎo-dàodì jīng 小道地經
*Lesser Sūtra on the Stages of the Path*
translated by [[支曜]] (Zhī Yào, 譯)

## About the work

The *Xiǎo-dàodì jīng* (T608) is a one-fascicle short meditation treatise translated by Zhī Yào 支曜 ([[支曜]]). The title parallels [[KR6i0244]] *Dàodì jīng* 道地經 (T607) and likely represents an even smaller selection / digest of the same Saṅgharakṣa *Yogācārabhūmi* source corpus, or a related minor *yoga*-treatise.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A miniature meditation handbook from the second-century CE, datable to Zhī Yào's documented translation activity (Zhōngpíng 中平 era, ca. 185 CE) at Luòyáng. Together with [[KR6i0239]] *Dà-ānbān shǒuyì jīng*, [[KR6i0240]] *Yīn-chí-rù jīng*, and [[KR6i0244]] *Dàodì jīng*, it forms part of the early Hàn corpus of Chinese *chán*-meditation literature.
