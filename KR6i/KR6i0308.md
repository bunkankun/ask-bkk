# Jīn-guāngmíng jīng shū 金光明經疏
*Commentary on the Sūtra of Golden Light*
composed by [[吉藏]] (Jí-zàng, 撰)

## About the work

T1787 is [[吉藏]]'s single-fascicle commentary on [[KR6i0301|*Jīn-guāngmíng jīng* 金光明經]] (T663) — a relatively short text by the standards of the Sān-lùn master's prolific commentarial corpus, but historically important as the only major non-Tiāntái Suí commentary on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* and as evidence of how Madhyamaka 三論 doctrinal categories were applied to a sūtra normally read through Tiāntái lenses.

### Abstract

The work bears the clean attribution 沙門吉藏撰 in the Taishō witness. Date bracket follows [[吉藏]]'s mature productive period at Jiā-xiáng-sì 嘉祥寺 in Kuàijī and subsequently at the Suí imperial chapels (590–623); the commentary is generally placed in the late Suí or very early Táng on the basis of doctrinal vocabulary. The opening lays out the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* programme in characteristically Sān-lùn terms: the Buddha's *fǎ-shēn* 法身 (Dharma-body) is "obscure and quiet, with no long or short lifespan" (法身幽寂壽無長短); compassion responds to the conditions of beings (至人絕慮有感必通); skilful means (大權方便) takes shape in accordance with the capacities of the audience. This framing positions T1787 as a Madhyamaka rebuttal of any reified reading of the Buddha's *Shòuliàng* (lifespan) doctrine — exactly the doctrinal point on which the Tiāntái commentaries (T1783, T1785) build their distinctive position.

The text is presumably an abridgement of a longer earlier work; cross-references in [[吉藏]]'s other commentaries indicate that he had also composed a more extensive *Sān-lùn xuán-yì* on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* that is now lost. T1787 is therefore best treated as a digest, but a doctrinally coherent one. It was little used in subsequent Chinese commentarial transmission, but is a key witness to early Suí *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* exegesis outside the Tiāntái lineage.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0301]] (金光明經 / T663).
