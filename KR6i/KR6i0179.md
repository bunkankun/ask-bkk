# Fóshuō Jīnguāngwáng tóngzǐ jīng 佛說金光王童子經
*Sūtra of the Boy Golden-Light-King*
translated by [[法賢]] (Fǎxián / Dharmabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Jīnguāngwáng tóngzǐ jīng* (T548) is a one-fascicle Sòng sūtra by Fǎxián 法賢 ([[法賢]]). The protagonist *Jīnguāngwáng tóngzǐ* 金光王童子 ("Boy Golden-Light-King") is a child-bodhisattva.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna text in the child-bodhisattva genre, paralleling [[KR6i0177]] *Jīnyào tóngzǐ jīng*. Translation dated to Fǎxián's post-987 productive period (989–1000 CE).
