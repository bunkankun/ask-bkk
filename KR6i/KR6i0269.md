# Fó-shuō huìyìn sānmèi jīng 佛說慧印三昧經
*Sūtra on the Samādhi of the Wisdom-Seal as Spoken by the Buddha*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

T632 (one fascicle, alt. title 慧印經; provisional Sanskrit *Tathāgatajñānamudrāsamādhi-sūtra*) is a short Mahāyāna *samādhi*-sūtra translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, fl. 222–253) at Jiànyè under the Wu. The text expounds the *huìyìn sānmèi* — a samādhi in which the meditator becomes "sealed" by the wisdom of the Tathāgatas, such that all *dharma*s are perceived as already containing the buddha-mark. It is the first of three Chinese versions: [[KR6i0270|T633]] is an anonymous later rendering, and [[KR6i0271|T634]] is a much-expanded Northern Song retranslation by [[智吉祥]] and his colleagues.

### Abstract

T632 is unanimously ascribed in the canonical bibliographies to [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān); the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145, juan 2) lists it among his works. Date bracket follows his productive period in Wu (222–253). As with [[KR6i0268|T631]], the CANWWW database erroneously assigns T632 to [[支曜]] (a data error in the AUT00021 key); the source colophon and all Chinese catalogs agree on Zhī Qiān. The text is an important early statement of the *jñāna-mudrā* (wisdom-seal) theme that was later expanded in tantric Buddhism. The "wisdom-seal" doctrine here developed influenced later Chinese exegesis on the relation between *prajñā* and *upāya*.
