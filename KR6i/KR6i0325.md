# Fóshuō bùzēng bùjiǎn jīng 佛說不增不減經
*Sūtra Spoken by the Buddha on Neither Increase nor Decrease*
translated by [[菩提流支]] (Pútíliúzhī / Bodhiruci, 譯)

## About the work

T668 in one fascicle is one of the principal short *tathāgatagarbha* sūtras, transmitted into Chinese by [[菩提流支]] of the Northern Wèi. The text takes up the question of whether the realm of beings (*sattvadhātu* 眾生界) increases or decreases over time — i.e., whether buddhahood removes beings from the round of saṃsāra. The Buddha's reply is "neither increase nor decrease" (不增不減): the realm of beings, the *dharma-dhātu* and the *tathāgata-garbha* are non-different in nature; what looks like decrease through liberation is in fact the manifestation of the originally pure tathāgatagarbha, which neither increases nor decreases.

### Abstract

The attribution to [[菩提流支]] (Bodhiruci, d. c. 527) is unanimous in the Chinese catalogues (*Lìdài sānbǎo jì*, *Kāiyuán lù*). Date bracket follows Bodhiruci's documented translation activity at the Northern Wèi capital Luòyáng (508–527). The Sanskrit original is lost, but a Tibetan recension exists that confirms the doctrinal coherence of Bodhiruci's translation.

T668 is a key witness to the early-sixth-century stage of *tathāgatagarbha* doctrine: the identification of *tathāgatagarbha* with *dharma-dhātu* anticipates the central thesis of the *Ratnagotravibhāga* (taken into Chinese as the *Bǎoxìng lùn* 寶性論, T1611). The text is brief — about 13 columns of Taishō text — but doctrinally dense, and it was extensively cited by later Chinese *tathāgatagarbha* exegetes including [[法藏]] and the early Chán tradition.

Related canonical texts: T1611 (寶性論 / *Ratnagotravibhāga*), with which T668 shares its conceptual core; [[KR6i0323]] (大方等如來藏經 / T666) and [[KR6i0326]] (佛說無上依經 / T669), the cognate *tathāgatagarbha* sūtras.
