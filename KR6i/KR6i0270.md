# Fó-shuō rúlái zhìyìn jīng 佛說如來智印經
*Sūtra on the Wisdom-Seal of the Tathāgata as Spoken by the Buddha*
translator unknown ([[失譯]])

## About the work

T633 (one fascicle, alt. title 諸佛慧法身經; provisional Sanskrit *Tathāgatajñānamudrāsamādhi-sūtra*) is an anonymously transmitted Chinese version of the same Indic *sūtra* underlying [[KR6i0269|T632]] (Zhī Qiān's *Huì-yìn sānmèi*) and [[KR6i0271|T634]] (the Northern Song *Dàshèng zhì-yìn jīng*). The text is conventionally placed in the Liú-Sòng (420–479) on stylistic grounds, but the translator is already lost in the *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145).

### Abstract

T633 has the alternate title 諸佛慧法身經 (*Sūtra on the Wisdom-Dharmakāya of All Buddhas*), recorded in the Sengyou catalog. The translator is anonymous; both Sengyou and the Tang catalogs (*Lìdài sānbǎo jì*, *Kāiyuán*) list the text as 失譯 ("lost translator"). Modern scholarship has generally placed it on stylistic grounds in the southern Liú-Sòng period, but no firmer dating is possible. The text is shorter and more compact than [[KR6i0269|T632]] and may represent a separate Indic recension rather than a retranslation of the same source. Together with T632 and [[KR6i0271|T634]], T633 forms the Chinese tradition of the "wisdom-seal samādhi" — a doctrinal motif that was later absorbed into tantric Buddhism's mudrā theory.
