# Yuánjué jīng chāo biàn yí-wù 圓覺經鈔辨疑誤
*Disambiguation of Doubtful Points and Errors in the Subcommentary to the Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment*
by [[觀復]] (Guānfù, 撰)

## About the work

A 2-fascicle critical-commentary text by the southern-Sòng Huáyán scholar-monk [[觀復]] Guānfù 觀復 (*hào* Xiào-ān 笑庵, active in the late twelfth century). The work is a *biàn yí-wù* 辨疑誤 — literally a "disambiguation of doubts and errors" — directed at the *Yuánjué jīng dàshū chāo* 圓覺經大疏鈔 of [[宗密]] Guīfēng Zōngmì ([[KR6i0557]]). It is therefore not a primary commentary on the *Yuánjué jīng* itself ([[KR6i0551]]) but a supercommentary that takes Zōngmì's *Dàshū chāo* as its working text and adjudicates apparent contradictions, philological slips, doctrinal mis-statements, and corrupt readings. As such it stands among the principal Sòng-era critical responses to the Zōngmì commentary tradition and a primary witness to the active reading of Zōngmì in southern-Sòng Huáyán circles.

### Abstract

Guānfù's authorial method is conservative-philological: where the *Dàshū chāo* gives a doctrinal classification or a citation that is suspicious, he typically supplies the parallel passage from the source, identifies the slip, and proposes a corrected reading. The work is therefore an indirect witness to the textual condition of the *Dàshū chāo* in the southern Sòng — Guānfù's text has occasionally been used by modern editors of Zōngmì to reconstruct readings now lost from the surviving *Dàshū chāo* recension. The composition window of *circa* 1143–1180 is inferred from Guānfù's other works (especially his *Yí jiào jīng lùn jì* 遺教經論記, also preserved in the Xù-zàng): the *biàn yí-wù* is generally taken to date to the same active middle period of his career, but a precise dating is not preserved. Guānfù's birth and death dates are not securely recorded in any surviving biographical source.
