# Xiànzài xiánjié qiān fómíng jīng 現在賢劫千佛名經
*Sūtra of the Names of the Thousand Buddhas of the Present Fortunate Aeon* (recension b)
Translator unknown (失譯)

## About the work

This is the second recension (T447b) of the present-aeon thousand-Buddha-names text, preserved only in the CBETA edition without additional canon witnesses. It carries the same alternative title as T446b: 現在賢劫千佛名經(一名「集諸佛大功德山」). The minimal canon attestation (CBETA only, no 【大→宋/元/明】 variants) suggests this recension represents a less widely circulated textual tradition than T447a ([[KR6i0024]]).

### Abstract

T447b is the less-attested recension of the present-aeon thousand-Buddhas list. Its parallel structure with T446b ([[KR6i0023]]) — same alternative title 集諸佛大功德山, same "orig/CBETA only" attestation — suggests that the "b" recensions of all three aeons (T446b, T447b, T448b) represent a coherent secondary transmission stream, possibly manuscript-based and distinct from the major printed-canon tradition represented by the "a" recensions. See [[KR6i0022]]–[[KR6i0027]] for the complete group.
