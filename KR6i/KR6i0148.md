# Fóshuō Zhāntuóyuè guówáng jīng 佛說旃陀越國王經
*Sūtra of the King of Caṇḍrāvatī*
translated by [[沮渠京聲]] (Jǔqú Jīngshēng, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Zhāntuóyuè guówáng jīng* (T518) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Jǔqú Jīngshēng 沮渠京聲 ([[沮渠京聲]]). The protagonist *Zhāntuóyuè* 旃陀越 (also written 旃陀掘) is a phonetic transliteration possibly of *Caṇḍrāvatī* or another Indic toponym.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative on a king's encounter with the Buddha. Translation dated to Jǔqú Jīngshēng's productive period (455–464 CE).
