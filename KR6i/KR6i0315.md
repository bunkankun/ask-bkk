# Jīn-guāngmíng jīng wén-jù xīn-jì 金光明經文句新記
*New Notes on the* Phrase-and-Sentence Commentary on the Sūtra of Golden Light
composed by [[從義]] (Cóngyì, 撰)

## About the work

X360 is [[從義]]'s seven-fascicle Northern-Sòng subcommentary on Zhìyǐ's [[KR6i0306|*Wén-jù* 文句]] (T1785), the *wén-jù* counterpart to his [[KR6i0314|*Shùn-zhèng jì* 順正記]] (X359). The title's *xīn-jì* 新記 ("new notes") is a programmatic statement of the work's ambition: a *new* annotation alongside [[知禮]]'s established [[KR6i0307|*Wén-jù jì* 文句記]] (T1786), with [[從義]]'s own emendations at points of disagreement.

### Abstract

The work is more philologically detailed than [[KR6i0314|X359]] (the *xuán-yì* counterpart), as the *wén-jù* genre demands. Date bracket again follows [[從義]]'s mature productive period (1070–1091). Together with X359, X360 is one of the two principal post-[[知禮]] Sòng subcommentaries on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* commentarial doublet, and like X359 it is intra-*shān-jiā* polemic that broadly accepts [[知禮]]'s framework while challenging him on specific philological and sectional readings.

[[從義]]'s positions did not displace [[知禮]]'s as the school's standard reading of the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa*; the *Sòng-Yuán-Míng* Tiāntái mainstream continued to prefer [[知禮]]. But X359 / X360 form a coherent rival corpus that was kept in print and in scholarly circulation, and is the principal evidence for the survival of internal critical discussion within the *shān-jiā* lineage in the post-[[知禮]] generation.

Related canonical texts: parent commentary [[KR6i0306]] (金光明經文句 / T1785); paired commentary [[KR6i0314]] (金光明經玄義順正記 / X359); rival/predecessor [[KR6i0307]] (金光明經文句記 / T1786).
