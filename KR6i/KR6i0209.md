# Fóshuō Dàshèng liúzhuǎn zhūyǒu jīng 佛說大乘流轉諸有經
*Mahāyāna Sūtra on Streaming through the [Three] Existences*
translated by [[義淨]] (Yìjìng, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Dàshèng liúzhuǎn zhūyǒu jīng* (T577) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra translated by Yìjìng 義淨 ([[義淨]]). It is another rendering of the *Bhava-saṅkrānti* corpus already represented at [[KR6i0207]] (Bodhiruci I) and [[KR6i0208]] (Buddhaśānta). Yìjìng's version is later, more idiomatically Tang-dynasty Buddhist Chinese, and reflects his characteristic terminological choices for *bhava*, *vijñāna*, and *pratibimba*.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short doctrinal sūtra on the transmigration of consciousness (*vijñāna*) through the three realms of existence (*tribhava*), explained by the simile of a reflection that transmits without substantial transfer. Translation datable within Yìjìng's main Cháng'ān translation period 700–711 CE (after his return from India in 695 and before his death in 713).
