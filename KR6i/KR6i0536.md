# Dà-shèng biàn-zhào guāng-míng zàng wú-zì fǎ-mén jīng 大乘遍照光明藏無字法門經
*The Mahāyāna Sūtra of the Letterless Dharma-Gate of the Universally-Illuminating-Radiance Treasury* (alt. *Wú-zì fǎ-mén jīng* 無字法門經; *Biàn-zhào guāng-míng zàng wú-zì fǎ-mén jīng* 遍照光明藏無字法門經)
re-translated by [[地婆訶羅]] (Dì-pó-hē-luó, *Divākara*, 再譯)

## About the work

T830 in one fascicle is Divākara's **second translation** (再譯 *zài-yì*) of the *Anakṣara-karaṇḍaka-vairocana-garbha-sūtra*, the same source-text as [[KR6i0534|*Wú-zì bǎo-qiè jīng* 無字寶篋經 (T828)]] by Bodhiruci and his own [[KR6i0535|*Dà-shèng lí wén-zì pǔ-guāng-míng zàng jīng* (T829)]]. The fact that both T829 and T830 are by Divākara — explicitly marked 再譯 ("re-translated") — reflects the late-Táng practice of producing successive refinements of the same source within a single translator's career. The Taishō editors retained both versions side-by-side. CANWWW gives the alternate Sanskrit title *Anakṣara-granthaka-garbha-sūtra*.

### Abstract

The narrative frame and doctrinal content are identical to those of [[KR6i0534|T828]] and [[KR6i0535|T829]]: the assembly at Mt Gṛdhrakūṭa; the bodhisattva Shèng-sī-wéi's two questions; the Buddha's exposition of the eight (or nine) *kleśa*s the *bodhisattva* must extinguish; the Buddha's exposition of the *anakṣara-dharma*-ground that the Tathāgata has awakened to. See [[KR6i0534]] for the full doctrinal summary.

The chief differences between T829 and T830 — both by Divākara — are at the level of phrasing and bodhisattva-name selection: T830 renders the title as *Biàn-zhào guāng-míng zàng* (the more etymologically transparent rendering of *vairocana-garbha*), where T829 had *Pǔ-guāng-míng zàng*. The doctrinal content is unchanged. The two-translation situation within Divākara's own career is one of the few preserved instances in the Chinese canon of an intra-translator revision.
