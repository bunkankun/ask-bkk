# Fóshuō Mòluó wáng jīng 佛說末羅王經
*Sūtra of the Mallaka King*
translated by [[沮渠京聲]] (Jǔqú Jīngshēng, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Mòluó wáng jīng* (T517) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Jǔqú Jīngshēng 沮渠京聲 ([[沮渠京聲]]). The protagonist *Mòluó wáng* 末羅王 may render Sanskrit *Malla-rāja* (king of the Malla clan, who hosted the Buddha's *parinirvāṇa* at Kuśinagara).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative on a Mallaka king's encounter with the Buddha. Translation dated to Jǔqú Jīngshēng's productive period (455–464 CE).
