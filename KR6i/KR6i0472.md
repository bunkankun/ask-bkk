# Fó shuō sì wúsuǒwèi jīng 佛說四無所畏經
*The Buddha Speaks: The Sūtra of the Four Fearlessnesses* (Skt. *Catur-vaiśāradya-sūtra*)
translated by [[施護]] (Shīhù = Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

T775 in one fascicle is a brief sūtra-translation by [[施護]] (Dānapāla, fl. 980–1017) at the Northern Sòng Translation Bureau in Kāifēng. The title 四無所畏 (*sì wú-suǒ-wèi*) translates Skt. *catur-vaiśāradya* — the four "fearlessnesses" or "self-confidences" of a *Tathāgata* — one of the standard *abhidharma* enumerations of the Buddha's exclusive epistemic and ethical perfections.

### Abstract

The four *vaiśāradya* of a buddha, as enumerated in the standard *abhidharma* tradition (cf. [[KR6n0023|*Abhidharma-kośa*]] book 7) and in the *prajñāpāramitā* and *bodhisattva-piṭaka* literature, are: (1) confidence in proclaiming *full and perfect awakening* — i.e. that he has attained complete *bodhi* and that no opponent can rightly contradict the claim; (2) confidence in proclaiming *the destruction of all defilements* — that all *āsravas* are extinguished in him; (3) confidence in *teaching the path* — that the path he expounds leads to the cessation of suffering for those who practise it; (4) confidence in *teaching the obstacles* — that the *dharmas* he identifies as obstacles to the path are indeed obstacles. The Buddha's four *vaiśāradya* are paired in scholastic literature with the *daśa-bala* (ten powers — see [[KR6i0479]] and [[KR6i0480]]) as the basic enumeration of the exclusive properties of buddhahood.

Dānapāla's brief Chinese rendering is a free-standing exposition of the *catur-vaiśāradya* doctrine in *sūtra*-frame, suitable for recitation and study. The *vaiśāradya* doctrine is given fuller treatment in the [[KR6q0019|*Mahā-prajñā-pāramitā-śāstra* (T1509)]] of [[龍樹|Nāgārjuna]] and in the [[KR6q0006|*Daśabhūmika-vyākhyāna*]] of [[世親|Vasubandhu]]; the Sòng Bureau's stand-alone sūtra-translation provides Chinese audiences with the doctrine in its short narrative form.
