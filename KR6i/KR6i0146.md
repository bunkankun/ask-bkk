# Fóshuō shèngjūn wáng suǒwèn jīng 佛說勝軍王所問經
*Sūtra of King Prasenajit's Questions*
translated by [[施護]] (Shīhù / Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō shèngjūn wáng suǒwèn jīng* (T516) is Shīhù's 施護 ([[施護]]) Sòng translation parallel to Xuánzàng's [[KR6i0145]] (T515). Both render the same Indic *Prasenajit-paripṛcchā-sūtra*.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

This is the second Chinese translation of the *Prasenajit-paripṛcchā*. Comparison with Xuánzàng's earlier translation provides another window into the evolution of Buddhist Chinese idiom across three centuries. Translation dated to Shīhù's productive period (989–1017 CE).
