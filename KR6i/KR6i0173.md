# Fóshuō Yéqí jīng 佛說耶祇經
*Sūtra of Yéqí*
translated by [[沮渠京聲]] (Jǔqú Jīngshēng, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Yéqí jīng* (T542) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Jǔqú Jīngshēng 沮渠京聲 ([[沮渠京聲]]). *Yéqí* 耶祇 is a phonetic transliteration possibly of *Yajña* or another Indic name.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative. Translation dated to Jǔqú Jīngshēng's productive period (455–464 CE).
