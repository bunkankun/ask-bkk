# Fóshuō Shēnrì jīng 佛說申日經
*Sūtra of Sundaya / Shēnrì*
translated by [[竺法護]] (Zhú Fǎhù / Dharmarakṣa, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Shēnrì jīng* (T535) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra by Dharmarakṣa 竺法護 ([[竺法護]]). *Shēnrì* 申日 may transliterate Sanskrit *Sundaya* or another personal name. The text is closely related to [[KR6i0164]] *Yuèguāng tóngzǐ jīng* — Shēnrì is the elder, and Yuèguāng is his son.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

The Shēnrì-Yuèguāng narrative pair (T535 + T534) belongs to the same Indic source-tradition. The doctrinal kernel is the Candraprabha-kumāra prophecy. Translation dated to Dharmarakṣa's productive period (266–308 CE).
