# Wǔmǔ zǐ jīng 五母子經
*Sūtra of the Five Mothers and the Son (Recension b)*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Wǔmǔ zǐ jīng* (T555b) is the "b" recension of [[KR6i0186]] (T555a). Both are attributed to Zhī Qiān.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A parallel recension to [[KR6i0186]] of the Five-Mothers narrative. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
