# Léngqié jīng cān-dìng shū 楞伽經參訂疏
*Comparative-Collation Commentary on the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
collated and annotated by [[廣莫]] (Guǎng-mò, 參訂)

## About the work

X332 in eight fascicles is [[廣莫]]'s late-Míng comparative-collation (*cān-dìng* 參訂) commentary on [[KR6i0327|*Léngqié-ābá-duōluó bǎo jīng* 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經]] (T670). The genre is *cān-dìng* — collating earlier annotations and emending the textual corruption — combined with *shū* — a doctrinal commentary. The work is one of the late-Míng monastic productions on the *Laṅkāvatāra*, contemporary with [[KR6i0343|X326]] / [[KR6i0345|X328]] / [[KR6i0346|X329]] / [[KR6i0347|X330]] / [[KR6i0348|X331]].

### Prefaces

The Taishō / *Xuzangjing* witness preserves at the head a preface (Léngqié jīng cān-dìng shū xù 楞伽經參訂疏序) by an unnamed contemporary, identifying the editor as "the *bhikṣu* of West-Wú [西吳] Shì Guǎng-mò 釋廣莫"; the preface frames the *cān-dìng* project explicitly as an effort to "raise up the dark and correct the erroneous, with the aim of supporting the sūtra and rectifying mind-learning" (標玄訂謬，將以翼經而正心學). The reference to *xīn-xué* 心學 ("mind-learning") is doubly apt: it is the late-Míng Wáng Yáng-míng-school philosophical idiom *and* the Chán-school self-designation of its lineage as the "school of the seal of mind" (心印宗門). The preface continues to draw a parallel with the Confucian *xīn* tradition, citing Yáo and Shùn's "the human-mind is precarious, the dao-mind is subtle" — a striking inter-textual move characteristic of late-Míng three-teachings discourse.

### Abstract

[[廣莫]] (DILA Authority A001663) is otherwise primarily known as a Vinaya specialist — author of the *Sìfēn jiè-běn yuán-qǐ shì-yì* ([[KR6k0148]]). The *Léngqié cān-dìng shū* is therefore one of the few non-Vinaya works in the [[廣莫]] corpus, and gives a different cross-section of his productive activity. Date bracket conservatively follows the late-Míng span (1580–1644); doctrinal vocabulary is consistent with a Wàn-lì–Chóng-zhēn date.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670); contemporaneous late-Míng commentaries [[KR6i0343]], [[KR6i0345]], [[KR6i0346]], [[KR6i0347]], [[KR6i0348]].
