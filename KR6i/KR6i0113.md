# Bùsīyì guāng púsà suǒshuō jīng 不思議光菩薩所說經
*Sūtra Spoken by the Bodhisattva Inconceivable Light*
translated by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Kumārajīva, 譯)

## About the work

The *Bùsīyì guāng púsà suǒshuō jīng* (T484) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra translated by Kumārajīva 鳩摩羅什 ([[鳩摩羅什]]) at Cháng'ān. The protagonist is the bodhisattva *Bùsīyì guāng* 不思議光 ("Inconceivable Light"; Sanskrit *Acintyaprabhāsa*).

### Prefaces

The text opens with *rúshì wǒwén*. The colophon attributes the translation to Kumārajīva.

### Abstract

This is a short Mahāyāna text on the bodhisattva *Acintyaprabhāsa*'s exposition of the perfections (*pāramitā*) and the bodhisattva path. The translation falls within Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān productivity (402–413 CE).
