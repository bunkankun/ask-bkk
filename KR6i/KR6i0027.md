# Wèilái xīngxiù jié qiān fómíng jīng 未來星宿劫千佛名經
*Sūtra of the Names of the Thousand Buddhas of the Future Starry Aeon* (recension b)
Translator unknown (失譯)

## About the work

The second recension (T448b) of the future-aeon thousand-Buddha-names text, preserved only in the CBETA edition. The source text carries the alternative title 未來星宿劫千佛名經(一名集諸佛大功德山) and the colophon note 開元拾遺附梁錄 ("appended to the Liang records in the Kaiyuan supplement"), placing it bibliographically within the *Kāiyuán shìjiào lù*'s supplementary appendix. Together with T448a ([[KR6i0026]]), T446a/b ([[KR6i0022]]–[[KR6i0023]]), and T447a/b ([[KR6i0024]]–[[KR6i0025]]), this text completes the "Three Aeons, Three Thousand Buddhas" system used in Chinese Buddhist liturgical confession.

### Prefaces

The colophon note 開元拾遺附梁錄 suggests the Kaiyuan catalog (730 CE) assigned this as a supplement to the Liang-period records, reflecting uncertain earlier transmission.

### Abstract

T448b is the less-attested recension of the future-aeon list; its preservation only in CBETA (without major canon edition witnesses) parallels T447b and T446b, suggesting a coherent "b" transmission stream separate from the major printed-canon "a" recensions. See [[KR6i0022]]–[[KR6i0026]] for context.
