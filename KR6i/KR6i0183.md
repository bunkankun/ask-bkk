# Fóshuō Módēng nǚ jiěxíng zhōng liùshì jīng 佛說摩登女解形中六事經
*Sūtra of the Mātaṅga Woman Resolving Six Matters in the Body*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Módēng nǚ jiěxíng zhōng liùshì jīng* (T552) is a one-fascicle anonymous short sūtra parallel to [[KR6i0182]] (T551) by Ān Shìgāo. The two preserve different recensions of the same Mātaṅga narrative.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. There is no surviving translator attribution.

### Abstract

A parallel recension to [[KR6i0182]]. The "six matters in the body" of the title refers to a Buddhist contemplative analysis of the body's constituents that the Buddha uses to redirect the Mātaṅga woman's attachment to Ānanda's body. Standard pre-Sui anonymous *shīyì* attribution.
