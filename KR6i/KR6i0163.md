# Púsà shēngdì jīng 菩薩生地經
*Sūtra of the Bodhisattva's Place of Birth*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Púsà shēngdì jīng* (T533) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). *Shēngdì* 生地 ("place of birth"; Sanskrit *jātabhūmi*) is the metaphysical doctrine of where bodhisattvas are reborn — into the *kuśala-mūla* (wholesome roots) of buddha-fields rather than into the mundane realms.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna text on the bodhisattva's place of rebirth, articulating the doctrine that bodhisattvas voluntarily take birth in difficult realms to liberate beings. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
