# Pǔdá wáng jīng 普達王經
*Sūtra of King Pǔdá*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Pǔdá wáng jīng* (T522) is a one-fascicle anonymous short sūtra. *Pǔdá* 普達 ("Universally-Comprehending") may be a Chinese semantic translation rather than a transliteration.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. There is no translator attribution.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative on a king's encounter with the Buddha. Standard pre-Sui anonymous *shīyì* attribution.
