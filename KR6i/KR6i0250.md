# Chán-mìyào fǎ jīng 禪祕要法經
*Sūtra of the Secret Essential Methods of Meditation*
translated by [[鳩摩羅什]] (Jiūmóluóshí / Kumārajīva, 等譯)

## About the work

The *Chán-mìyào fǎ jīng* (T613) is a three-fascicle Mahāyāna meditation manual ascribed in the canonical bibliography to Kumārajīva 鳩摩羅什 ([[鳩摩羅什]]) and his Cháng'ān team. It presents a sequenced program of *kasiṇa* / impurity / loving-kindness / mindfulness-of-the-buddha *dhyāna* leading toward the visualization of the Buddha — overlapping in part with the [[KR6i0251]] *Zuòchán sānmèi jīng* 坐禪三昧經 (T614) and the *Pratyutpanna-samādhi* tradition.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. The catalogs ascribe the text to Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān workshop ("等譯" — "translated together with [his team]").

### Abstract

A central work of early-fifth-century Chinese *chán* practice, datable to Kumārajīva's Cháng'ān period (401–413 CE). Modern scholarship — notably Yamabe Nobuyoshi (1999) — has shown that T613 contains substantial Chinese-composed elements alongside Indic source material; it should be read as a compilation/redaction by Kumārajīva's circle of older Sarvāstivāda *yoga* sources combined with vivid visualization material that may be partly Chinese in origin. Together with [[KR6i0251]], [[KR6i0252]], [[KR6i0253]], [[KR6i0254]] it forms the core Kumārajīva-period meditation corpus.
