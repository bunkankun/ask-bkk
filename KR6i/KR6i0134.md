# Bǐqiū tīngshī jīng 比丘聽施經
*Sūtra of the Monk Heard-of-Giving*
translated by [[曇無蘭]] (Tánwúlán / Dharmaratna, 譯)

## About the work

The *Bǐqiū tīngshī jīng* (T504) is a one-fascicle short sūtra translated by Tánwúlán 曇無蘭 ([[曇無蘭]]) — also written as 竺曇無蘭 ([[KR6i0124]] uses the longer form). The protagonist *Tīngshī bǐqiū* 聽施比丘 is a monk whose name means "Heard-of-Giving" (Sanskrit *Śrutadāna*).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Hīnayāna avadāna sūtra on a monk who exemplifies the perfection of giving. Translation falls within Tánwúlán's Eastern Jìn period (381–395 CE).
