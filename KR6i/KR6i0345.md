# Léngqié jīng xuán-yì 楞伽經玄義
*Profound Meaning of the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
composed by [[智旭]] (Zhìxù / Ǒuyì, 撰述)

## About the work

X328 in one fascicle is [[智旭]] (Ǒuyì Zhìxù, 1599–1655)'s *xuán-yì* on [[KR6i0327|*Léngqié-ābá-duōluó bǎo jīng* 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經]] (T670). The work is paired with [[KR6i0346|X329]] (the *yì-shū*) — together constituting [[智旭]]'s mature *Laṅkāvatāra* commentary in the Tiāntái commentarial pair-genre of *xuán-yì* + *wén-jù* / *yì-shū*.

### Abstract

The Taishō / *Xuzangjing* witness preserves the head-line: 支那藕益沙門　釋智旭　撰述 ("composed by the Chinese Ǒu-yì *bhikṣu* Shì Zhìxù"). Date bracket follows [[智旭]]'s late productive period at Língfēng-sì 靈峯寺 (1635–1655). The opening of the work captures the doctrinal programme tightly: "the sage's Self-Awakening Wisdom (自覺聖智), the Mind of First-Truth (第一義心), is not communicated by names and forms; how could it be entered through speculation? Depending on defilement-and-purity, [the sūtra] divides the *five dharmas*; *dharma* by *dharma* is fundamentally true. By delusion-and-awakening, [it] discriminates the *three natures*; nature by nature is essentially without nature. From the differentiation of substance-and-function in delusion, the *eight kinds of consciousness* are established — but consciousness is like a sky-flower. Where understanding manifests partial-and-perfect principle, the *twofold no-self* is named — but [no-]self is like the rabbit's horn. Therefore: all Buddha-speech transmits only this Mind; all Buddha-Mind seals only this speech."

This is a classic statement of [[智旭]]'s integrative doctrinal position: Yogācāra (the eight-consciousness scheme), Madhyamaka (the analogies of sky-flower and rabbit's horn for the unreality of conventional designations), Tathāgatagarbha (the *zì-jué shèng-zhì* / *Self-Awakening Wisdom*), and Chán-school (mind-seal) all read together through the sūtra's own structure. As a programmatic *xuán-yì* it shapes the reading of the parent sūtra in [[KR6i0346|X329]] and beyond.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670); paired commentary [[KR6i0346]] (楞伽經義疏 / X329).
