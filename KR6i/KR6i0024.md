# Xiànzài xiánjié qiān fómíng jīng 現在賢劫千佛名經
*Sūtra of the Names of the Thousand Buddhas of the Present Fortunate Aeon* (recension a)
Translator unknown (失譯)

## About the work

The *Xiànzài xiánjié qiān fómíng jīng* is the "present aeon" counterpart to the past- and future-aeon thousand-Buddha-names texts ([[KR6i0022]]–[[KR6i0023]] and [[KR6i0026]]–[[KR6i0027]]). It enumerates the thousand Buddhas of the present bhadrakalpa ("fortunate aeon"), the same cosmic period whose cosmological framework is elaborated in the *Xiánjié jīng* [[KR6i0001]]. Two recensions are preserved in the Taishō: T447a (this text) and T447b ([[KR6i0025]]). The anonymous translator and undated status are characteristic of the entire group.

### Abstract

T447a (現在賢劫千佛名經, recension a) is the principal Taishō recension of the present-aeon thousand-Buddhas list and is supported by the major canon editions (Sòng, Yuán, Míng, Gōng, Koryŏ). The present-aeon list most directly corresponds to the *Xiánjié jīng* ([[KR6i0001]]) compiled by Dharmarakṣa; together with the past-aeon and future-aeon lists, it forms the "three aeons, three thousand Buddhas" liturgical system used in the *Cíbēi sānmèi shuǐchàn* 慈悲三昧水懺 and other Chan and Tiantai confession rituals. The thousand names in T447a may derive from or share material with chapter 20 (*Qiān fó míngháo pǐn* 千佛名號品) of the *Xiánjié jīng*, though their relationship is one of belonging to the same tradition rather than direct textual derivation.
