# Léngqié jīng tōngyì 楞伽經通義
*Penetrating Meaning of the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
composed by [[善月]] (Shànyuè, 述)

## About the work

X323 in six fascicles is [[善月]]'s Southern-Sòng Tiāntái-school commentary on [[KR6i0327|*Léngqiéābáduōluó bǎo jīng* 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經]] (T670). The genre is *tōngyì* 通義 ("penetrating meaning"): a programmatic exposition of doctrinal content rather than a phrase-by-phrase gloss, organising the parent sūtra's teachings under thematic rubrics drawn from the late-Sòng Tiāntái scholastic apparatus.

### Prefaces

The Taishō / *Xuzangjing* witness preserves at the head a Sòng-period preface by Jiǎng Zhī-qí 蔣之奇 (1031–1104), the same preface that prefaces [[KR6i0327|T670]] in the Sòng print transmission. Jiǎng Zhī-qí's preface is dated to the late 1080s or 1090s; it forms a frontispiece to the Sòng *Laṅkāvatāra* edition that [[善月]]'s commentary engages.

### Abstract

[[善月]] (1149–1241), Sòng Tiāntái-school master sobriquet *Bó-tíng* 柏庭, native of Dìng-hǎi 定海, was successively abbot of major Tiāntái scholastic institutions (Dōng-hú Biàn-lì-sì 1180, Nán-hú 1192, Shàng-tiānzhú-sì 1213), and a prolific commentator on the major Mahāyāna sūtras. The *Léngqié jīng tōng-yì* is one of his mature works; date bracket follows his post-1180 productive period. As a Tiāntái commentary on a sūtra normally read through Yogācāra and Chán lenses, the *Tōng-yì* is doctrinally distinctive: [[善月]] reads the *Laṅkāvatāra*'s eight-consciousness scheme through the Tiāntái *xìng-jù* 性具 framework, and treats the *meat-prohibition* chapter as evidence for the Tiāntái precept-corpus.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670).
