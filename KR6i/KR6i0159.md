# Fóshuō Ājiūliú jīng 佛說阿鳩留經
*Sūtra of Ājiūliú*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Ājiūliú jīng* (T529) is a one-fascicle anonymous short sūtra. *Ājiūliú* 阿鳩留 is a phonetic transliteration possibly of *Akilu* or another Indic personal name.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative. Standard pre-Sui anonymous *shīyì* attribution.
