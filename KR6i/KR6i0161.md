# Fóshuō zhǎngzhě Yīnyuè jīng 佛說長者音悅經
*Sūtra of the Elder Yīnyuè*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō zhǎngzhě Yīnyuè jīng* (T531) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). *Yīnyuè* 音悅 may be a Chinese semantic translation rather than transliteration.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna* on an elder named Yīnyuè. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
