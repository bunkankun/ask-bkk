# Jìngmíng jīng Guānzhōng shì chāo 淨名經關中釋抄
*Guānzhōng Excerpt-Explanation of the Vimalakīrti Sūtra*
by [[道掖]] (Dào Yè, 撰)

## About the work

The *Jìngmíng jīng Guānzhōng shì chāo* (T2778) is a two-fascicle companion text to [[KR6i0096]] (T2777) by Dào Yè 道掖 ([[道掖]]). The Taishō header explicitly cross-references No. 2777 — these two texts circulated as a paired Guānzhōng commentary on the Vimalakīrti, with T2777 providing the main commentary and T2778 providing the *shì chāo* 釋抄 (excerpt-explanation) of selected passages.

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the excerpt-explanation format.

### Abstract

The pairing of T2777 (main commentary) with T2778 (excerpt-explanation digest) reflects a standard Tang commentarial practice — a major commentary accompanied by a more concise excerpt-version for ease of reference and teaching. Both texts preserve only at Dunhuang. Dào Yè's *Guānzhōng* commentary system is significant for transmitting the Cháng'ān-region commentarial synthesis to the provincial Hexi monasteries.

The dating bracket (700–800 CE) reflects the manuscript paleography and the close relationship to the parent commentary T2777. The *shì chāo* genre is characteristic of mid-Tang Buddhist scholasticism.
