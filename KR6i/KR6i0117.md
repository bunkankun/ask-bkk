# Bǎoshòu púsà pútí xíng jīng 寶授菩薩菩提行經
*Sūtra of the Bodhisattva Treasure-Conferral's Bodhisattva Practice*
translated by [[法賢]] (Fǎxián / Dharmabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

The *Bǎoshòu púsà pútí xíng jīng* (T488) is a one-fascicle late Mahāyāna sūtra translated by Fǎxián 法賢 ([[法賢]]; the Sòng imperial-renamed Tiānxīzāi 天息災, d. 1000 CE) at the Sòng *Yìjīng yuàn* 譯經院. The protagonist *Bǎoshòu* 寶授 ("Treasure-Conferral"; Sanskrit *Ratnadatta*) is the bodhisattva interlocutor.

### Prefaces

The text opens with *rúshì wǒwén*. The colophon attributes the translation to Fǎxián of the Sòng.

### Abstract

This is a brief Sòng-period Mahāyāna text on the bodhisattva's practice (*bodhicaryā*). Fǎxián's translation activity post-987 CE (after his name change) is dated 989–1000 CE. The text is structured as a dialogue between the bodhisattva *Ratnadatta* and the Buddha on the proper conduct of the bodhisattva path.
