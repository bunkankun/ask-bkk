# Fóshuō Shùtíjiā jīng 佛說樹提伽經
*Sūtra of Jyotiṣka (Variant Recension)*
translated by [[求那跋陀羅]] (Guṇabhadra, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Shùtíjiā jīng* (T540b) is the "b" recension of the *Jyotiṣka* sūtra (cf. [[KR6i0170]] = T540a). Both are attributed to Guṇabhadra.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

The "a" and "b" recensions of T540 represent variant Chinese textual transmissions of the same Indic source, both attributed to Guṇabhadra. Their preservation as separate texts in the Taishō reflects pre-Sui canonical instability for this short-text family. Translation dated to Guṇabhadra's productive period (435–468 CE).
