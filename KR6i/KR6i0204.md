# Fóshuō Zhǎngzhě Fǎzhì qī jīng 佛說長者法志妻經
*Sūtra of the Wife of Elder Fǎzhì*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Zhǎngzhě Fǎzhì qī jīng* (T572) is a one-fascicle short sūtra of unknown translation attribution. The protagonist is the wife of the elder *Fǎzhì* 法志 (\*Dharmaprasāda or similar). CANWWW alt. title 法志妻經.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. No translator attribution survives.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna *avadāna* in the wise-women cluster (alongside T567–T571). The catalog assigns no dynasty; the text is anonymous and listed among the Eastern Jìn *shīyì* corpus by *Chū sānzàng jì jí* (T2145), whence the conservative bracket of 317–420 CE used here.
