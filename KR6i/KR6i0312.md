# Jīnguāngmíng jīng wénjù kē 金光明經文句科
*Topical Outline of the* Phrase-and-Sentence Commentary on the Sūtra of Golden Light
arranged by [[明得]] (Míngdé, 排定)

## About the work

X357 is the *kē* 科 (topical outline) for [[KR6i0306|*Jīnguāngmíng jīng wénjù* 金光明經文句]] (T1785) — the structural-divisions chart of the *wénjù* leg of the Tiāntái commentarial doublet, paired with [[KR6i0310]] (X355) on the *xuányì* leg. Single fascicle, attributed in the witness to 雙徑沙門明得排定.

### Abstract

One of [[明得]]'s four ancillary Tiāntái treatises on the *Suvarṇaprabhāsa* (cf. 明得's DILA biography A008323). Like [[KR6i0310]] (X355), the work consists of nested topical headings drawn from T1785, organised hierarchically as a study aid. Date bracket follows 明得's mature productive period (c. 1560–1588) on Mt. Jīngshān 徑山 and at Jiāxīng. Of pedagogical rather than doctrinal interest, but historically valuable as documentation of late-Míng Tiāntái scholastic practice.

Related canonical texts: parent commentary [[KR6i0306]] (金光明經文句 / T1785); paired *kē* [[KR6i0310]] (金光明經玄義科 / X355).
