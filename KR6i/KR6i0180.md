# Fóshuō guāngmíng tóngzǐ yīnyuán jīng 佛說光明童子因緣經
*Sūtra of the Causes and Conditions of the Boy Light-Brilliance*
translated by [[施護]] (Shīhù / Dānapāla, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō guāngmíng tóngzǐ yīnyuán jīng* (T549) is a four-fascicle Sòng sūtra translated by Shīhù 施護 ([[施護]]). The protagonist *Guāngmíng tóngzǐ* 光明童子 ("Boy Light-Brilliance") is the central figure of an *avadāna* tracing his karmic causes leading to encounter with the Buddha.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A four-fascicle Mahāyāna *avadāna* on the child-bodhisattva *Prabhā-kumāra*. Translation dated to Shīhù's productive period (989–1017 CE).
