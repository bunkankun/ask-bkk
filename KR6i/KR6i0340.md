# Léngqié jīng jí-zhù 楞伽經集註
*Collected Annotations on the Sūtra of Laṅkā*
compiled by [[正受]] (Zhèngshòu, 集記)

## About the work

X324 in four fascicles is [[正受]]'s Southern-Sòng *jí-zhù* 集註 ("collected annotations") on [[KR6i0327|*Léngqié-ābá-duōluó bǎo jīng* 楞伽阿跋多羅寶經]] (T670). The genre is anthological: rather than offering original commentary, the work assembles glosses from earlier *Laṅkāvatāra* commentators — [[寶臣]]'s [[KR6i0334|T1791]] is heavily drawn upon — into a single working text, with [[正受]]'s own editorial frame supplying continuity and a Chán reading-lens.

### Prefaces

The Taishō witness preserves at the head a preface by Shěn Yíng 沈瀛 (sobriquet *Zhú-zhāi* 竹齋), Cháo-yì-dàfū and *Xīn Huái-dōng ān-fǔ-sī cān-yì-guān* 新淮東安撫司參議官. Shěn Yíng (DILA records him as a layman) frames the *Laṅkāvatāra* as a sūtra spoken by the Buddha for those of intermediate capacity (中根), in contrast to the *Śūraṅgama* and *Yuán-jué* spoken for those of the highest (上根). The preface gives no precise date, but Shěn Yíng's career places it in the late twelfth or very early thirteenth century — consistent with [[正受]]'s productive period.

### Abstract

[[正受]] (sobriquet *Léi-ān* 雷庵), Línjì-school dharma-heir of Zhuó-ān Déguāng 拙庵德光 (1121–1203), is best known as the compiler of the [[KR6q0010|*Jiā-tài pǔ-dēng lù* 嘉泰普燈錄]] (the fifth of the great Sòng Chán lamp histories, completed 1204). The *Léngqié jí-zhù* is his single major scholastic-commentarial production. As a Chán-school *jí-zhù* on a sūtra that the Chán tradition claims for itself (since Bodhidharma's transmission of T670 to Huìkě), the work represents the Sòng Chán reading of T670 *qua* sūtra rather than *qua* lineage-credential. Date bracket follows [[正受]]'s mature productive period (post-1180, pre-*Pǔ-dēng-lù* compilation 1204).

X324 was an influential late-Sòng / early-Yuán reference work and was the principal Chán-side complement to [[寶臣]]'s [[KR6i0334|T1791]] in the medieval Chinese commentarial transmission of the *Laṅkāvatāra*.

Related canonical texts: parent sūtra [[KR6i0327]] (楞伽阿跋多羅寶經 / T670); doctrinal predecessor [[KR6i0334]] (注大乘入楞伽經 / T1791); [[正受]]'s lamp-record [[KR6q0010]] (嘉泰普燈錄).
