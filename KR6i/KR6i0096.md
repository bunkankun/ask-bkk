# Jìngmíng jīng jíjiě Guānzhōng shū 淨名經集解關中疏
*Guānzhōng Collected-Explanations Commentary on the Vimalakīrti Sūtra*
by [[道掖]] (Dào Yè, 撰)

## About the work

The *Jìngmíng jīng jíjiě Guānzhōng shū* (T2777) is a two-fascicle commentary on Kumārajīva's *Vimalakīrti* ([[KR6i0076]] = T475) by the Tang Cháng'ān monk Dào Yè 道掖 ([[道掖]]). The title's *Guānzhōng* 關中 ("within the passes" — i.e., Cháng'ān region) signals the regional commentarial tradition; *jíjiě* 集解 ("collected explanations") indicates that the work is a digest gathering interpretations from earlier commentators (Sēngzhào, Huìyuǎn, Jízàng, Kuījī, etc.).

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the commentary's analytical framework. Dào Yè's preface — referenced in [[KR6i0094]] (T2775, *Wéimó shū shìqián xiǎo xù chāo*) — set out the work's purpose as a unified Cháng'ān-tradition reading.

### Abstract

Dào Yè (otherwise *Dào Yè* 道液 or *Dào Yè* in some witnesses) was a monk of mid-Tang Cháng'ān whose commentarial work survives only at Dunhuang. The *Jíjiě Guānzhōng shū* is a representative of the *Guānzhōng* commentarial style — synthetic, drawing on multiple earlier traditions, organized for monastic teaching. The work was authoritative enough to generate the sub-commentaries [[KR6i0094]] (T2775) and [[KR6i0097]] (T2778, the *Jìngmíng jīng Guānzhōng shìchāo*).

The Tang dating (ca. 700–750 CE) is established on the work's reception by the slightly later sub-commentaries and on paleographic grounds. Dào Yè's place in the broader Tang Vimalakīrti exegetical tradition is significant for showing how a regional Cháng'ān synthesis emerged alongside the major sectarian commentaries of Tiantai (Zhīyǐ) and Cí'ēn (Kuījī).
