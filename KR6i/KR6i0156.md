# Fóshuō zhǎngzhě zǐ Zhì jīng 佛說長者子制經
*Sūtra of the Elder's Son Zhì*
translated by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō zhǎngzhě zǐ Zhì jīng* (T526) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Ān Shìgāo 安世高 ([[安世高]]). The protagonist's name is *Zhì* 制 (Sanskrit unclear).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative on an elder's son named Zhì. Translation falls within Ān Shìgāo's productive period (148–170 CE).
