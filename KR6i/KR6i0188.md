# Fóshuō qīnǚ jīng 佛說七女經
*Sūtra of the Seven Women*
translated by [[支謙]] (Zhī Qiān, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō qīnǚ jīng* (T556) is a one-fascicle Mahāyāna sūtra by Zhī Qiān 支謙 ([[支謙]]). Seven women — daughters of a king — debate Buddhist doctrine and demonstrate exceptional bodhisattva-realization, in the same genre as the Candrottarā ([[KR6i0109]]) and other "wise women" texts.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short Mahāyāna text in the wise-women genre, demonstrating female bodhisattva-capacity. Translation dated to Zhī Qiān's Wú productive period (222–252 CE).
