# Fóshuō Nàinǚ Qípó jīng 佛說奈女耆婆經
*Sūtra of the Mango-Maiden and Jīvaka*
translated by [[安世高]] (Ān Shìgāo, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Nàinǚ Qípó jīng* (T554) is a one-fascicle parallel of [[KR6i0184]] (T553), both attributed to Ān Shìgāo. *Nàinǚ* 奈女 = 㮈女 (Āmrapālī, Mango-Maiden); *Qípó* 耆婆 = 祇域 (Jīvaka). The two transliterations of *Jīvaka* differ slightly.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A parallel recension to [[KR6i0184]] of the Āmrapālī-Jīvaka narrative. Translation falls within Ān Shìgāo's productive period (148–170 CE).
