# Yōupóyí jìngxíng fǎmén jīng 優婆夷淨行法門經
*Sūtra of the Pure Practice Dharma-Gate for Upāsikās*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Yōupóyí jìngxíng fǎmén jīng* (T579) is a two-fascicle anonymous Mahāyāna sūtra detailing the *jìngxíng fǎmén* 淨行法門, the "pure-practice dharma-gate" of the *upāsikā* (laywoman). It functions as a small lay-practice manual structured around questions put to the Buddha.

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula. No translator preface or attribution survives.

### Abstract

A short two-fascicle compilation outlining lay observance — refuges, precepts, basic meditative dispositions — addressed to female lay practitioners. Catalogue assignment is to the post-Han / pre-Tang anonymous (*shīyì*) corpus, hence the conservative bracket 317–600. Style and lexicon are consistent with the late Northern-and-Southern-dynasties devotional sūtra production.
