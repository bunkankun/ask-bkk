# Fóshuō Nèishēnguān zhāngjù jīng 佛說內身觀章句經
*Sūtra of Verses on Inner Body-Contemplation*
(translator unknown)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Nèishēn-guān zhāngjù jīng* (T610) is a one-fascicle anonymous text on the contemplation of the body (*kāyānupaśyanā* / *aśubha-bhāvanā*), in *zhāngjù* 章句 (verse-stanza) form.

### Prefaces

No translator preface survives.

### Abstract

A short anonymous meditation text consisting of mnemonic verses on body-contemplation, addressing the constituent impurities of the body in the standard *aśubha* enumeration. Catalogue assignment is to the post-Han / pre-Tang anonymous *shīyì* corpus, hence the conservative bracket of 317–600 CE.
