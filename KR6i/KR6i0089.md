# Wéimó jīng shū 維摩經疏
*Commentary on the Vimalakīrti Sūtra*
(anonymous Dunhuang manuscript commentary)

## About the work

The *Wéimó jīng shū* (T2770) is a one-fascicle anonymous Vimalakīrti commentary preserved in the Dunhuang manuscript corpus and edited into Taishō volume 85. The Taishō header cross-references No. 475 (Kumārajīva's translation). Together with [[KR6i0087]], [[KR6i0088]], [[KR6i0090]], and others, it forms the Dunhuang Vimalakīrti commentary cluster (T2768–T2778) — provincial Tang exegetical literature preserved only on the Hexi corridor.

### Prefaces

The text opens directly with the commentary; the manuscript is fragmentary and lacks a preface or colophon.

### Abstract

The *shū* genre title (commentary, gloss) distinguishes this manuscript from the *yìjì* genre of [[KR6i0087]]–[[KR6i0088]] and the *chāo* genre of [[KR6i0092]] and [[KR6i0093]]. The doctrinal content of T2770 reflects an early-to-mid Tang exegetical tradition with traces of Sānlùn (Three-Treatise) influence, suggesting an author trained in the Jízàng-school commentarial tradition. The manuscript witnesses are part of the Stein and Pelliot Dunhuang collections.
