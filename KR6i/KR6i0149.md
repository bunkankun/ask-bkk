# Fóshuō Módá guówáng jīng 佛說摩達國王經
*Sūtra of the King of Madra*
translated by [[沮渠京聲]] (Jǔqú Jīngshēng, 譯)

## About the work

The *Fóshuō Módá guówáng jīng* (T519) is a one-fascicle short sūtra by Jǔqú Jīngshēng 沮渠京聲 ([[沮渠京聲]]). *Módá* 摩達 is likely a transcription of *Madra* (a region in northwest India).

### Prefaces

The text opens with the canonical formula.

### Abstract

A short *avadāna*-style narrative. Translation dated to Jǔqú Jīngshēng's productive period (455–464 CE). The "kings" sub-cycle (KR6i0136–0154) preserved a remarkable concentration of these short royal narratives in Chinese transmission.
